# FrankenRobotics breakthrough pass

**Codex slate · 30 candidates, 10 ranked proposals, 3 Brenner alternatives · Q-001**

The highest-leverage change is to make a robot design an incrementally executable family of mechanisms, controllers, and experiments. A local edit should preserve every result whose assumptions still hold. A structural edit should fit into an already-running accelerator batch. An impossible mission should return a geometric, algebraic, or dynamical reason before it consumes a training run. The first test is whether a new design earns independent acceptance in one tenth the time, measured from edit through evaluation.

This slate extends the [MOR decision contract](../mor/MOR_CASE.md), the [question of record](../brenner/intake/question_of_record.md), and the [NEON plan](../../COMPREHENSIVE_PLAN_FOR_FRANKEN_ROBOTICS__NEON.md). NEON already proposes topology-specific codegen, power ports, symbolic preflight, equivariant policies, short-horizon adjoints, world snapshots, and CrashPacks. The entries below change the unit of compilation, proof, training, or experimentation. Ranking favors work eliminated at the design-loop level, an early decisive test, an executable first slice, and reuse across morphology families. This is the Codex contribution to the duel. Module and file-format names below are proposed additions to NEON. Ratios, budgets, and gates are prospective.

### #1 · Compose robot modules with machine-checkable substitution contracts · W01

Orient each physical port, reject ambiguous or ill-posed algebraic loops, and discharge endpoint compatibility and bounds on the affected part of the composition tree. Reuse the remaining leaf proof terms and compiled kernels. Begin with one two-sided actuator module, repeated in a family of framed wheeled carts and wheel-leg variants. Producing an executable replacement plus an independently checked receipt for which prior model obligations survive requires the substitution algebra and dependency graph. **Leverage.** Use FrankenSim `fs-ga`, `fs-phs` and `fs-mbd` for coordinate transforms, energy terms, and articulated dynamics. FrankenSymPy derives the local elimination and sparse derivatives. **Breakpoint, scale, and kill.** On a locked 64-edit sequence over 32 modules, include parameter edits, limb swaps, and a global bus edit.
