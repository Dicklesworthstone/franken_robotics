## Detailed Per-Item Evaluations

### W01 · Compose Robot Modules With Machine-Checkable Substitution Contracts — **915 / 1000**
- **Strengths:** Best-framed compositional algebra across the duel. Using structured/decorated cospans (disjoint union + pushout over typed port sets) with explicit into-module power sign conventions, algebraic-loop rejection, and incremental invalidation (specifically noting that shared battery/thermal bus edits trigger global invalidation — huge intellectual honesty!) directly solves the edit-verify-compile bottleneck. The first slice (two-sided actuator module across wheeled carts and wheel-legs) is dead simple to ship in M1.
- **Critique / Gap:** Leaves two subtle robotics wrinkles slightly implicit: (1) cross-branch closed kinematic loops (e.g., dual arms grasping one payload, parallel 4-bar legs) break pure tree-pushout local elimination and need explicit sub-PROP escalation; (2) for GPU execution, module substitution needs a crisp lowering contract into Featherstone's Schur-complement Port-ABI or MorphoTable rows so incremental proofs also mean incremental zero-stall GPU updates.
- **Net:** Mandatory cornerstone of v2.0.

### W02 · Execute Mixed Morphologies by Batching Operations Across Robots — **860 / 1000**
- **Strengths:** Tackles Paradox 2 from the purest systems-engineering angle: stable `EnvId`, segmented SoA tables, depth-ordered FK/leaf-to-root elimination records, connected-component contact islands compacted into homogeneous work queues, and explicit atomic tick-boundary layout swaps. Correctly calls out the 36 KiB 32-contact FP32 Delassus budget (which v1 got wrong by 9×!) and routes overflow/tails to matrix-free operator applications.
- **Critique / Gap:** In typical Studio co-design and e-process racing, every candidate morphology $m$ ALREADY runs $K \ge 32$ Common-Random-Number (CRN) episodes! Batching arbitrary unrelated robots into fine-grained operation queues adds scatter-gather indexing, dynamic queue compaction, and inter-record synchronization barriers that are *unnecessary* when 1 full 32-lane warp can simply hold 32 CRN envs of the SAME morphology!
- **Synthesis Opportunity:** Combine **warp-coherent morphology packing** (when $K \ge 32$ episodes/robot — zero scatter/compaction overhead) with **W02's ragged island work-queues** for dynamic contact-island sizing (`m_c <= 10` / `11..16` / `>16`) and $K < 32$ single-robot heterogeneous fleets!

### W03 · Transport Skills Through Physical Capability Maps (`.fskill`) — **875 / 1000**
- **Strengths:** One of the sharpest ideas in the entire duel. Instead of betting everything on a black-box neural network magically extrapolating across morphologies, W03 factors control into a task-frame `.fskill` + exact local actuation-to-wrench map $w = w_0 + B_m(q) u$ + bounded allocation QP with electrical/thermal secondary costs + a frozen morphology-conditioned residual. Even better: when a requested task wrench lies outside the new robot's attainable set, it returns an **exact separating infeasibility witness** BEFORE wasting a single training rollout!
- **Critique / Gap:** Affine instantaneous wrench allocation $w = w_0 + B_m(q) u$ captures instantaneous quasi-static / operational-space feasibility beautifully, but cannot by itself certify *multi-step dynamic* feasibility (e.g., underactuated bipedal capture-point recovery or momentum-assisted stair jumps) where feasibility spans a whole horizon. Also, pretraining denominator accounting (correctly flagged by COD!) matters heavily.
- **Net:** Pairing W03's physics-exact capability-map pullback (`frob-control::skill_transport`) with a Buckingham-$\Pi$ dimensionless equivariant graph policy (`FiberMorph`) creates an unbeatable two-layer zero-shot transfer engine!

### W04 · Compile Hybrid Safety as Reachable-Tube Composition (`.ftube`) — **765 / 1000**
- **Strengths:** Brutally honest about the hardest problem in hybrid safety: *ambiguous contact guards under bounded sensing error* ($R_{mn}((K_m \oplus E) \cap G_{mn}, D) \subseteq K_n$), explicit chatter handling, actuation latency, and verified backup domains. Quantifying all enabled reset successors under a common action is genuinely beyond Drake's smooth SOS or single-outcome numerical stepping.
- **Critique / Gap:** Cell-based backward viability pruning (`8 modes × 16,384 cells = 16 MiB`) works cleanly for 4D reduced systems (tray cart: position, velocity, tray angle, rate), but suffers exponential grid growth at 6–8D (3D humanoid capture-point + angular momentum + swing-foot clearance). Furthermore, 0.15 ms lookup is great on CPU/MCU but slower than a 0.3 µs register-resident closed-form KKT barrier inside a 32,768-env GPU RL step.
- **Net:** Adopt `.ftube` specifically for **low-dimensional (≤4D) nonsmooth guard-ambiguous safety kernels** (thresholds, braking, tray stabilization, door-pinch zones), paired alongside closed-form compiled SOS/CBF KKT shields for high-DoF per-joint/thermal/centroidal GPU RL exploration!

### W05 · Branch a Living World at the Intervention, With Causal Recomputation — **840 / 1000**
- **Strengths:** Converts M5 Ultra's 256 GiB unified memory from a passive "big scene bucket" into a **content-addressed causal spacetime tile graph**. Tracking exact downstream dependencies — including *swept entry into empty tiles* and *ray traversal through empty tiles* — catches subtle causal bugs that naive spatial bounding boxes miss, and honestly proves the exact Amdahl threshold (`dirty_work <= 4.21%` for 10×!).
- **Critique / Gap:** In tight indoor kitchens/apartments (6 m × 6 m), a closed-loop robot policy's observation changes on the first perturbed camera/LiDAR ray, which alters actions, which alters robot trajectory, quickly making the robot's local tile swath dirty; furthermore, a 96 GiB + 8 GiB + 32 GiB = 136 GiB base footprint is tight if combined with 64-building training banks.
- **Net:** Enormous win for **short-horizon (0.3–2.0 s) counterfactual failure debugging** (`frob-timetravel`), where interventions occur near $t_{\mathrm{fail}}$ so the causal cone stays small (<4% of world tiles) and 1,024 parallel `do(...)` forks share 96%+ of the living world untouched!

### W06 · Synthesize Closed-Loop Linkages From Task Geometry (`.flinkage`) — **830 / 1000**
- **Strengths:** Pragmatic masterclass in avoiding Gröbner foot-guns: starts with exact rational 2×2 elimination for 3-pose planar dyads, enforces explicit determinant-rank degeneracy partitioning, saturates denominators via Rabinowitsch trick ($sd - 1 = 0$), certifies a single continuous branch via half-angle polynomial root isolation + interval contraction, and ONLY graduates finite 0-D higher-order templates to `fsym-solvers::real_rur` (100% respecting `E023`'s `MAX_VARIABLES = 8`, `MAX_BITS = 4096` caps!).
- **Critique / Gap:** 3-pose synthesis leaves free choices (hence catalog sweeping 240 templates); for harder 5-pose Burmester or 9-point path synthesis (4,326 solutions) and spatial parallel ankles/Stewart platforms, pairing W06's exact rational/RUR lane with GPU parameter homotopy continuation yields complete coverage from 3-pose instant synthesis up to degree-40 spatial mechanisms.
- **Net:** Adopt W06 directly as M1's exact linkage synthesis spine (`frob-symbolic::linkage_synth`).

### W07 · Differentiate the Settled Gait Through Event Time and Impacts (`.fgait`) — **890 / 1000**
- **Strengths:** Mathematically immaculate. Correctly uses the autonomous saltation matrix $S = D_x R + \frac{(f^+ - D_x R f^-)\nabla g^T}{\nabla g^T f^-}$, augments the state with constant morphology parameters so guard/reset parameter sensitivities propagate automatically without fragile custom hand-code, solves the compact settled-cycle adjoint $(I - D_x P_\theta)^T \lambda = \nabla_x J$, and explicitly routes grazing, simultaneous impacts, and mode switches to separate branch/DFO search instead of pretending saltation handles them!
- **Critique / Gap:** Does not exploit morphological symmetry $\Gamma$ / H-K subgroup reduction ($[0, T/m]$ twisted boundary shooting in $\mathrm{Fix}(K)$) or `frankensim::fs-spectral::floquet` transient-growth diagnostics, which cut tangent integration cost by another 2×–4× and catch nonnormal fragile orbits.
- **Net:** Core winner; fuse W07's augmented-state settled adjoint with `fs-spectral::floquet` and symmetry-reduced shooting!

### W08 · Certify Morphologies Against Configuration-Space Bottlenecks (`.fclear`) — **820 / 1000**
- **Strengths:** Directly fixes v1's critical `G-WORLD-1` flaw (scalar ball clearance fails for rectangular/articulated robots!) with **two-sided inner/outer $(x,y,\psi)$ cubical complexes** that simultaneously certify *continuous geometric feasibility* (inner connected path) AND *mathematical impossibility* (outer disconnection cut) across ordered robot-width families! The scale check (4.69 MiB per fixture mode, 74 mm half-bin discretization margin honesty, 14.69× query speedup across 160 widths) is textbook rigor.
- **Critique / Gap:** Assumes wheeled base or rigidly folded mobile manipulator; stair risers, vertical overhead pallet-rack beams, and dynamic articulated-leg foot placement need an explicit `MorphologyNavEnvelope` height/riser filter layered onto the $(x,y,\psi)$ complex.
- **Net:** Adopt directly into `frob-worldgen::clearance_complex` as the upgraded `G-WORLD-1` gate!

### W09 · Turn a Mission Into a Counterexample-Guided Build Packet (`.fbuild`) — **850 / 1000**
- **Strengths:** Unifies requirements, finite COTS catalog join, CEGIS counterexample pruning (**with the razor-sharp epistemic rule that a failed simulation ONLY emits a pruning cut when a provable lower-vs-upper bound generalizes across the excluded family!**), parametric CAD, BOM, wiring/connector netlist, thermal/electrical budgets, firmware test vectors, proof digests, and offline 3D replay in ONE design transaction.
- **Critique / Gap:** Could explicitly harvest `frankensim`'s existing `fs-geocon`, `fs-fab`, `fs-toleralloc`, `fs-io` (STEP/STL), and `fs-matdb` (`E022`) to eliminate 70% of greenfield CAD/GD&T engineering cost.
- **Net:** High-impact Studio flagship deliverable; merge with `frob-mfg` (`fs-geocon`/`fs-fab`/`fs-toleralloc`) and `#![no_std]` firmware lowering.

### W10 · Factor a Contact System Once for Dozens of Controlled Interventions (`response_batch`) — **790 / 1000**
- **Strengths:** The most mathematically disciplined Tensor-Core physics proposal in the duel. Instead of making reckless single-RHS FP16 claims, W10 cleanly isolates the exact regime where dense matrix hardware wins: **factor $A = J M^{-1} J^T + D$ once, solve $AX = B$ for 24–64 controlled impulse/wrench interventions (or morphology adjoint columns!)**, certify every column with $\|b - A\hat{x}\|_2 / \underline{\lambda}$ and active-mode/cone-slack predicates, and route any mode-switching column to full contact fallback!
- **Critique / Gap:** As W10 itself honestly computes via Amdahl's law, a 13.6× linear-solve speedup only yields 10× end-to-end if contact solves are 97.1% of runtime (rare outside specialized impulse-response / adjoint sweeps). It is a **killer subsystem primitive** for `frob-adjoint` multi-RHS sensitivity solves and 1-tick counterfactual intervention screening, rather than a standalone 10× headline for generic RL rollouts.
- **Net:** Adopt cleanly as the exact, rigorously guarded Tensor-Core / `simdgroup_matrix` contact primitive!

### Brenner Forced Third Alternatives (`COD`)
- **Paradox 1 — Compile the Quotient of the Design Space (`.fquot`): `845 / 1000`.** Truly original and non-obvious! While everyone else focused on speeding up per-design compilation/execution, COD observed that mission-observable equivalence ($\pi(d_1) = \pi(d_2)$ over mass moments, collision charts, exposed sensor/actuation laws) lets us **not even run 29 out of 30 equivalent designs**! Its ballast-placement exclusion test (735,471 subsets → 24,546 distinct classes = `29.96×`, with thermal/harness observables cleanly splitting classes when relevant) is textbook Brenner science. Narrower when every design has unique external geometry, but **completely orthogonal** to Staged Algebraic Erasure & `.fmodule` — stacking them multiplies gains!
- **Paradox 2 — Schedule by the Earliest Possible Interaction (`interaction_calendar`): `685 / 1000`.** Conceptually elegant (asynchronous time-tiling of causally isolated islands), but hits severe practical friction in GPU robotics RL: (1) every 50–100 Hz closed-loop RL policy tick and every GPU ray-traced LiDAR/RGB-D frame acts as a mandatory external observation fence (capping lookahead $L$ to 5–20 substeps, not 64!), (2) dynamic moving clutter/humans/doors in tight indoor rooms shrink swept-bound horizons rapidly, and (3) asynchronous variable-clock GPU work queues complicate CUDA Graph capture and deterministic cross-env PPO rollout buffers. Keep strictly as an **M6 sparse multi-robot / large-warehouse research lane** rather than the M2 core engine (where warp-coherent MorphoJIT + ragged island compaction wins decisively).
- **Paradox 3 — Certify an Observable Energy Budget at a Physical Boundary (`.fenergy`): `870 / 1000`.** Pure Brenner gold! Instead of pretending to prove exact contact-sequence trajectories through nonsmooth impact chaos, `.fenergy` shifts the specification to a **physically measurable, contact-sequence-invariant energy inequality** (kinetic + drive work through measured cutoff latency + stored elastic/grav + bounded external work $\le$ independently measured lower work envelope of a guided compliant stop), verified in fixed-point integer arithmetic in `fln-kernel`! Zero claim laundering, 100% hardware-auditable, and complements compiled KKT CBF shields seamlessly!
