## #1 · Operadic mechanism algebra and heterogeneous execution · 803

**The breakthrough.** A typed module boundary makes a limb swap a local design transaction. An engineer can see which constraints survived, and a Studio agent can compose parts and run the edited graph without paying to rederive its unmodified subtrees. ABA and mapped power-port interconnection give a substantial starting point. Compile reusable primitives, record dependency summaries, and test a tree of rigid modules first. E021’s deferred constrained Dirac/DAE wiring and E022’s nightly-bound multibody dependencies are real integration costs.

**What breaks.** The ABA Schur update after eliminating a free joint is only positive semidefinite in general. With unit inertia and joint screw $e_1$, the update is $\operatorname{diag}(0,1,1,1,1,1)$. A 108 B FP32 inertia/bias packet represents the instantaneous response under a fixed boundary contract. It omits internal recovery data, contact and loop coupling, and thermal/electrical state. A single accumulator covers a star case. A nested tree needs intermediate records or recomputation. An $O(|E|)$ wiring walk also says little about proof-normalization time. Same topology eliminates schedule divergence from topology alone. Contacts and iteration counts still split lanes. At one warp per topology, 512 topologies over 256 SMs launch two warps per SM on average. Even 12 resident warps are only 25% of Ada’s 48-warp limit. A 1 MB table fitting in L2 establishes capacity, while residency and indirect gather traffic require measurement. [Ada tuning guide](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/13.4.2/ada-tuning-guide/index.html)

**10× test.** Measure edit-to-qualified rollout for precompiled limb swaps against warm topology buckets, a strong ragged operator queue, and equivalent incumbent scene construction. Count invalidation, remaps, contacts and cold cases. Give the operad credit for reusable checked boundaries even if the fused scheduler loses. The market claim rests on qualified structural edit cadence, which the 70% homogeneous-throughput gate alone cannot establish.

## #2 · Dimensionless morphology policy and symbolic pullback · 646

**The breakthrough.** A stable task-space interface can make a new chassis testable with frozen policy weights. Separate the task command from constrained actuation and tell the agent which goals the new body cannot realize. That is more distinctive than another morphology-conditioned network. MetaMorph already demonstrates universal controllers over a modular design space, and Drake and Isaac have strong controller building blocks. [MetaMorph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11931), [Drake controllers](https://drake.mit.edu/pydrake/pydrake.systems.controllers.html), [Isaac controllers](https://isaac-sim.github.io/IsaacLab/main/source/concepts/motion_generators.html)

**What breaks.** The quoted momentum scale is for angular momentum. Linear momentum needs $m_0\sqrt{g\ell_0}$. Buckingham normalization leaves independent contact, actuator and thermal time ratios. Frame covariance also leaves graph automorphism, environmental symmetry and achievable actions as separate obligations. $J_iM^{-1}J_i^\top$ can be singular. A damped inverse changes the commanded response, while independent limb inverses miss off-diagonal task coupling, support restrictions and saturation. Begin with a constrained allocator. E025’s `DenseTensor`, `ft-nn` and `ft-autograd` are CPU-backed. Resident segmented forward, backward and optimizer kernels on both vendors are substantial new work. The cited 660 TFLOP/s is the sparse dual-card BF16/f32 accumulation figure. The dense peak is about 330.4, giving about 0.25 ms for 28.7 GFLOPs at the assumed 35%, before graph gathers. The 360 KB sync estimate covers roughly 14.4 μs of ideal one-way payload at 25 GB/s, with collective latency, staging and update precision still to charge. [NVIDIA Ada Appendix A](https://images.nvidia.com/aem-dam/Solutions/geforce/ada/nvidia-ada-gpu-architecture.pdf)

**10× test.** Compare equal-quality first policies on held-out bodies against a frozen graph baseline and warm-start control/RL. Score refusals, training amortization, adaptation and finalist runs. The 16.4 s screening arithmetic uses a throughput assumption and is a different outcome from 102.4 hours of training to specialist quality.

## #3 · Hybrid barrier tubes and fused safety shields · 478

**The breakthrough.** A model-scoped certificate that admits a control and explains its domain would help engineers review controller limits and agents avoid repeatedly proposing the same invalid actions. Mode-indexed flows and reset checks are the correct objects to prototype. Drake already gives a strong SOS/optimization baseline. The proposed product delta is an independently checked, portable filter plus a usable domain witness. [Drake MathematicalProgram](https://drake.mit.edu/tutorials/mathematical_program.html)

**What breaks.** The printed $\forall d\,\sup_u$ condition permits a different input for every hidden disturbance. If safety requires $u=d$ and $d\in\{-1,1\}$, both pointwise problems are feasible and there is no shared safe input. With latent disturbance the needed condition is a common action over the entire observation set and active constraint intersection. A centroidal projection plus minimum capsule distance is generally not a closed eight-state system. Establish a simulation relation and discrepancy bound or restrict the plant. Include inter-sample evolution, delay, actuator feasibility and all enabled resets. Four barrier rows already generate up to 16 active sets before actuator boxes, genuine cone constraints and rank degeneracy. Top-four dropping can remove a safety-critical row. KKT sensitivities are piecewise and may fail at active-set changes. E021 provides candidate SOS and interval checks. E023’s univariate integer factor-product profiles cannot project these multivariate certificates, and E024 has no ready `norm_num`/e-graph tactic or reflective checker for this claim. Passivity of a fallback cannot establish collision, fall or slip avoidance.

**10× test.** First require a two-mode fixture with one common sampled-data action, a checked reset and a verified backup domain. Compare accepted decisions against tuned batched robust filtering at matched coverage. Measure the emitted arithmetic and its certificate separately. The 110-FMA count omits coefficient assembly and branch/rank handling. On the cited 25 μs step, 8% is 2 μs, so the quoted 0.5 μs is a separate stricter gate. A statistical detection rate is neither proof of the antecedent at the time of action nor a physical calibration receipt.

## #4 · Tensor-core Delassus and InEKF · 444

**The breakthrough.** Reuse a verified factor across genuinely shared contact responses. Dense covariance or batched multi-RHS work is a credible use for matrix units. It would help agents prioritize many small disturbances from one contact anchor. The single-RHS factorization in AGY deserves a measurement of its own because factorization and matrix-vector application are different workloads.

**What breaks.** For $n=48$ and rank-one PSD $W$, AGY’s $\rho=.01\,\mathrm{tr}(W)/n$ gives $\kappa(W+\rho I)=1+100n=4{,}801$. This refutes the claimed bound of 100. At that condition number $\kappa u_{\rm FP16}\approx2.34$, so the claimed fixed contraction does not follow. Even three factors of 0.049 give about $1.18\times10^{-4}$ times the starting error. TF32 has greater exponent range and comparable significand precision, so it does not repair this conditioning on its own. FP32 residuals do not inherit a three-precision guarantee. Metal lacks native f64. The audited E025 kernel is GEMM, not a contact solver. A single materialized FP16 read of the 48×48 matrix across 16,384 environments moves 75.5 MB, giving an ideal 37.4 μs bandwidth floor on the dual cards or 94.4 μs at the assumed Apple bandwidth. A fully fused on-chip implementation can avoid this read but must pay assembly, scratch and register costs. The 660 TFLOP/s bound requires structured sparsity for FP32 accumulation. [Independent audit C009](../mor/.mor/run-r1/audit/AUD01-C001-C016.jsonl), [Carson–Higham](https://eprints.maths.manchester.ac.uk/2629/), [NVIDIA Ada Appendix A](https://images.nvidia.com/aem-dam/Solutions/geforce/ada/nvidia-ada-gpu-architecture.pdf)

**10× test.** Compare untiled/blocked FP32, guarded single-RHS tensor Cholesky after valid shifting, and shared-anchor multi-RHS on both vendors. The 48×48 × 32-RHS FP32 multiply is 6.86 FLOP/B counting input and output traffic once. Reuse can improve that figure. Score total step time including Delassus assembly, all outer iterations, fallbacks, original cone/complementarity residual and f64 impulse error. A different contact law or a slower incumbent PGS configuration gives a weak denominator. Even AGY’s claimed 4–8× for the solver would fall short of a 10× end-to-end physics result.

## #5 · Unified-memory atlas and replay · 685

**The breakthrough.** A shared atlas and fenced snapshots can make model inspection, repair and training coexist on a large Mac. The engineer and Studio agent benefit most when a failure freezes a dependable view of the exact input and state. This is a real advantage over a 24 GiB working set for a workload that revisits many large assets. Genesis already runs on Metal, and a well-designed discrete implementation can cache a working set and prefetch tiles. [Genesis backends](https://genesis-world.readthedocs.io/en/v1.4.5/user_guide/overview/installation.html)

**What breaks.** E025’s host-backed `DenseTensor` cannot alias a device resource merely because both use UMA. The legacy gateway waits synchronously and binds at zero offset. Persistent leases, command completion, ownership and backpressure need implementation. Epoch counters alone do not prevent CPU reads of uncompleted or reused storage. The replay estimate of $50\mathrm{M}\times1{,}280$ B is 64 GB or about 59.6 GiB, leaving modest room inside the 64 GiB reservation for metadata and variability. It still requires a measured sensor bitrate, compression latency and a risk budget for concurrent working sets. 5 mm grid spacing says nothing about scan fidelity. Remote CUDA jobs also need scene assets and state, with cache misses charged against the network. [Apple resource synchronization](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metal/resource-synchronization), [Apple working-set limits](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metal/mtldevice/recommendedmaxworkingsetsize)

**10× test.** A 1.5 GiB cold transfer at 25 GB/s takes about 64 ms. Comparing that with a resident pointer switch credits warm-versus-cold placement. Compare equal access patterns against Genesis Metal, resident tiled CUDA/MJWarp or Isaac assets, and an equivalent CPU inspection path. Track completed scene queries, end-to-end inspection stalls and admitted cache misses with both CPU diagnostics and physics running. A no-drop high-capacity workflow is credible. Its training generalization and 10× iteration gain are open.

## #6 · Floquet–Poincaré continuation · 835

**The breakthrough.** This replaces re-optimizing a gait for every small edit with a sensitivity of the *settled* gait. A Studio agent can ask which gear ratio or leg length improves energy while retaining stability margin, then verify that prediction on a fresh orbit. E021’s `fs-spectral::floquet` and transient analysis already consume a monodromy. Start with that CPU oracle and one periodically operated robot. A GPU atlas is a later speed test.

**What breaks.** The saltation formula assumes an isolated transverse event and differentiable reset on a fixed branch. Differentiate the actual discrete integrator when impact timing is quantized or complementarity admits multiple branches. The fixed-point solve needs phase and any valid symmetry directions removed and $I-DP$ invertible. A simple eigenvalue derivative needs normalized left/right eigenvectors and the total derivative along the continued fixed point, including any changing control. Multipliers at unit modulus are inconclusive for ordinary local stability. Classifying a bifurcation needs crossing and nondegeneracy checks. LGVI alone establishes neither correct impact Jacobians nor physical fidelity. The $11.3$ GFLOP tangent estimate per 256-branch iteration checks out. It omits primal contact, guard location, retries, eigensolve conditioning and data movement. The supplied 37 ms estimate itself uses dual-CUDA timings. [Saltation matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06862), [FrankenSim Floquet evaluator](../../../frankensim/crates/fs-spectral/src/floquet.rs)

**10× test.** Freeze a sequence of small edits with unchanged contact order, plus grazing, unstable and mode-order-switch controls. Compare accepted periodic designs at matched cost-of-transport, residual and stability against warm-start direct shooting, Drake phase-constrained optimization and tuned RL as relevant. Separate orbit discovery from steady continuation and charge rejected steps. This can plausibly produce 10× in accepted periodic edits even at the proposal’s slower 500 ms/1.5 s gate. Comparing a single tangent solve with a cold PPO run cannot establish the published multiplier.

## #7 · Inverse linkage synthesis and feasible design regions · 746

**The breakthrough.** Solve for a dimensioned linkage and a continuous assembly witness before invoking a simulator. This gives a human editable pivots and an agent a typed design or a reason to try a different template. Begin with five rational planar poses and a short, independently checked mechanism sweep. Study coordinates are useful for a separately bounded spatial lane. The differentiation is constructive output with a witness that survives propagation to the CAD datums. Drake optimization and classical Burmester construction are appropriate references. [Burmester five-pose synthesis](https://ecommons.udayton.edu/mee_fac_pub/192/), [Drake multibody optimization](https://drake.mit.edu/tutorials/mathematical_program_multibody_plant.html)

**What breaks.** E023 `real_rur` is a bounded zero-dimensional solver with eight variables, 64 equations, degree 256, 4,096-bit coefficient and 200M-work limits. Its finite shape/shear search can return inconclusive. Six independent quartics with four distinct real roots each already give 4,096 isolated configurations. Six variables and degree four alone imply neither an eliminant of degree ≤24 nor 100% coverage of the named mechanisms. Float PGA motors are not exact rational input data. Isolated real roots still need circuit, order, singularity and clearance checks. E023’s Gröbner checker and univariate Lean bridge do not supply the proposed Positivstellensatz bridge. For a generic quartic SOS in 12 variables the degree-two monomial basis has 91 entries, giving 4,186 independent slots per dense Gram block. The 13×13 estimate requires a restricted structure or degree. A single inner ellipsoid can miss disconnected feasible islands. Multiplying twelve independent 70% pass rates assumes a distribution and independence that the proposal hasn’t established.

**10× test.** Compare accepted, branch-checked linkages/hour against an analytic planar generator and warm-start constrained synthesis with the same output tolerances. Count unresolved, degenerate and near-toggle cases. Ship certificate-backed local boxes before trying joint maximum-volume SOS synthesis. Measure feasible search yield separately from design coverage. A 95% pass rate inside a tiny region is easy to obtain and can lower the chance of finding a good design.

## #8 · Adversarial scene grammar · 721

**The breakthrough.** Turn a plausible scene change into a replayable regression. A human can inspect why a door and mat combination matters, and an agent can rerun the minimal failing fixture before accepting a repair. E003 already provides cubical topology, and E026 provides scan uncertainty. Use those for admission and prioritization. The novelty over the incumbent workflow is a checked failure artifact that connects search, replay and redesign.

**What breaks.** A connected voxel clearance region certifies occupancy connectivity under its geometric encoding. It leaves steering, fixture operation, traction, reach, actuation and perception to independent task witnesses. An arbitrary legal mixed discrete/continuous grammar has no cheap smooth projection. Contact modes and ray occlusion also invalidate a global exact gradient. Use same-branch numerical gradient checks and compare surrogate search plus discrete mutations against tuned CMA-ES or cross-entropy search. AGY’s own 30 iterations × 1,024 environments × 64 steps are 1.97M forward steps before reverse work and checkpoint recomputation. The stated five-million-step random baseline is then only 2.54× larger, not 150×. At a fixed $p=10^{-4}$, 10,000 independent draws find at least one instance about 63% of the time. A 352 B robot snapshot excludes the world, sensors and adjoint state that must be retained or recomputed. Charge CPU–GPU contention on M5 and data/parameter versioning across the 4090 shards. Found adversarial cases give no real-world occurrence rate without a sampling law and field calibration.

**10× test.** Measure time to five distinct independently admitted, reproducible failures at fixed compute and fidelity, with held-out buildings and no optimizer access to qualification seeds. Run the same grammar and witness on optimized incumbent scenes. Charge rejected scenes, path witnesses, ray costs and backward passes. A faster regression generator could clear 10× even if gradients lose. Check robustness after hardening as a separate outcome.

## #9 · Sparse living multiphysics islands · 614

**The breakthrough.** Localize fidelity to the interaction that changes the design choice. A tray’s slosh load, cord snag, or wet friction transition can yield specific repair guidance on mount, posture or clearance. E021/E022/E026 provide fluid, solid, tribology, power-port and IPC reference pieces. Genesis has coupled IPC and particle methods, and Isaac Lab has Newton VBD and experimental cables. The candidate must earn its gain through calibrated local activation and equal-quality interaction outcomes. [Genesis coupling](https://genesis-world.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user_guide/theory/coupling/index.html), [Isaac Lab deformables](https://isaac-sim.github.io/IsaacLab/v3.0.0/source/concepts/deformables.html)

**What breaks.** 1.245 MB covers one D3Q19 population field. A conventional two-buffer scheme needs 2.49 MB for populations and about 2.65 MB/env with listed rug and steam fields, roughly 2.71 GB at 1,024 envs before halos, CCD pairs, solver matrices, checkpoint deltas and other state. The claimed 50 Hz fluid cadence must distinguish exchange epochs from lattice substeps. At 2 mm resolution and 0.3 m/s physical flow, a 20 ms lattice step implies lattice Mach about 5.2. Staying below 0.25 needs a step under 0.97 ms, or around 21 substeps per exchange. Streaming every such substep would exceed the quoted bandwidth on either pole without locality or fewer active tiles. A power identity in continuous time does not prove discrete energy/momentum balance under asynchronous exchange, port lag or tile birth/death. Source lifts such as `fs-lbm`, `fs-solid` and `fs-contact` carry nightly dependency work. IPC and non-tunneling assurances need a feasible initial state, complete collision candidates, and successful CCD/convergence checks.

**10× test.** Pick one slosh fixture. Compare a calibrated low-order mechanical model, a local refined solver and an eligible existing continuum coupling at matched force/phase accuracy. Log actual activation, stable timestep, local transfers, flux/work residuals and hardware throughput. A slosh frequency match is a useful numerical check, with physical fidelity assessed against measured loads. A 10× speed claim needs accepted interaction cases per hour at matched error. Add cords, rugs and optical plumes after an isolated interaction changes design decisions at affordable cost.

## #10 · Counterfactual replay and offline dossier · 818

**The breakthrough.** This is the strongest Studio-facing proposal. An engineer can watch the same failure under one changed input, and an agent can commit a repair with a replayable intervention. NEON already has CrashPacks, and Genesis has rewind/reset. The increment is a first-class *paired intervention* with preconditions, outcome deltas, actual state lineage and an inspectable artifact. A native replay harness can deliver it well before large batches or the browser compiler. [NEON](../../COMPREHENSIVE_PLAN_FOR_FRANKEN_ROBOTICS__NEON.md), [Genesis checkpoints](https://genesis-world.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user_guide/configuration/checkpoints.html)

**What breaks.** Ten binary factors permit 1,024 exhaustive coalitions for a fixed deterministic value table. Monte Carlo permutations estimate attributions over their sampling law. Shapley contributions can be negative, and top-1 attribution need not recover an injected factor in an interacting or redundant system. An observed successful counterfactual is also insufficient evidence of a minimal repair. Replay must capture all influential world, sensor, estimator, policy, solver and RNG state. A morphology edit needs a checked mapping or a new initial-state draw. E025 and NEON’s determinism classes give no automatic cross-device bit identity. The proposal’s own M5 forecast is 1.024 s for the full batch, before overhead. The 368 MB ring estimate assumes 4.5 KB complete snapshots. The predicted HTML size also excludes some embedding overhead. A 6.2 MB binary payload encoded entirely as Base64 would take roughly 8.27 MB before HTML. E008’s GPU/compiler crates are planned, and offline WebGPU still requires a supported secure origin and device. [FrankenThreeD manifest](../../../franken_threed/Cargo.toml), [WebGPU initialization](https://www.w3.org/TR/webgpu/#navigatorgpu)

**10× test.** Start with bounded paired and small interaction ablations, a compact replay manifest, and a minimal viewer. Time human and agent repairs against optimized checkpoint/reset scripts and NEON’s diagnostic workflow, with hidden multi-cause cases and independent verification of fixes. Report time-to-repair and reproducibility separately from browser frame rate. A 10× win is plausible for reproducible diagnosis and repair in a targeted workflow. It would take more than a fast rollout burst to prove it.

## Brenner P1 · Staged algebraic erasure · 754

**What survives.** Checked compile-time algebra with a small packed runtime data plane is the right separation. This alternative contributes a testable erasure contract, particularly if each rewrite emits its dependencies, certificate and guard. E011/NEON §12 already proposes symbolic CSE and three-target lowering. The increment must be demonstrated through measured compiler reuse, elimination of runtime work, or a verified rewrite that the baseline cannot cheaply express.

**What breaks.** Lean proof erasure does not remove dynamic validity checks or establish floating-point equivalence of separately emitted shader code. A 512-topology schedule still needs loads, even if every loaded index is a small integer. `4,800 FMAs` equals 9,600 FLOPs under standard accounting, so the stated arithmetic reduction from 18,000 FLOPs is 1.875×. At 64 registers/thread, 64K registers give a ceiling of 32 warps before allocation effects, at most two thirds of Ada’s limit. A 1.4 s build is 14% of the first 10 s evaluation, prior to amortization. [Ada tuning guide](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/13.4.2/ada-tuning-guide/index.html)

**Exclusion test.** Keep AGY’s paired hardware protocol, but make codegen, equivariance and shielding separate treatments before testing the combined workflow. Compare equal kernels for numerical/performance claims and equal acceptance evidence for time-to-verified-policy. Include warm caches, proof-generation failures and unseen edits. Its 1.8× and 5× gates are reasonable evidence of an improvement if met. A 10× result would still require a stronger end-to-end gate.

## Brenner P2 · Warp-cohomogeneous port-graph batching · 551

**What survives.** Coalesced episode-lane SoA and shared topology dispatch are a sensible dense-cohort fast path. The proposal shares the #1 executable mechanism. A small scheduler benchmark can resolve when to use this path before the team invests in a permanent megakernel.

**What breaks.** Common random numbers pair inputs across comparisons and impose no 32-episode minimum. One warp per 512 topologies over 256 SMs gives two launched warps per SM on average. Even 2,048 topologies give eight per SM, below the claim’s illustrative 12-warps budget. Block placement can further reduce coverage. Identical schedules do not equal identical contact branches or load addresses. Metal threadgroup occupancy depends on actual pipeline limits and resource use. The 108 B port packet and scratch calculation apply to a narrow contraction, with schedule and intermediate-data costs still to measure. GPU launches, graph walks and topology mutation data publication still have cost even for cached shaders. [Ada tuning guide](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/13.4.2/ada-tuning-guide/index.html)

**Exclusion test.** Compare warm topology bins, padded variants, homogeneous operations compacted across unrelated robots, and an adaptive hybrid. Sweep $K=1,8,32,128$, balanced and skewed topology mixes, plus light and heavy contacts. Record useful lane work, memory transactions, graph rebuilds and qualified outcomes by cohort. Treat 4× over a tuned interpreter and 70% of homogeneous throughput as distinct local results. A 10× design-loop claim requires novel-edit-to-completion gains after all routing costs.

## Brenner P3 · Quotient verification and conformal-e guarding · 369

**What survives.** Publishing the domain of every proof and treating a conservative exit as an explicit outcome would improve Studio decisions. That idea has value when the controller can check its assumptions before using the certificate. The alternative inherits #3’s model, Lean-bridge and feasibility gaps and then relies more heavily on the statistical monitor.

**What breaks.** An anytime-valid e-process controls a false-rejection probability under a specified null. It supplies no universal bound on the detection time or missed shifts. Two friction regimes can produce identical observations up to first slip, so no innovation monitor can reliably distinguish them before that step. Revoking an applicability badge after detection does not preserve forward invariance through those missing milliseconds. A dissipative potential-to-kinetic transition can still cause a collision. Saltation gives perturbation transport. Jump safety needs the actual reset enclosure. Runtime state is $18\times4\times8{,}192=589{,}824$ B, about 576 KiB, four times the stated byte count. A full uncached read/write is already about 1.47 μs at 800 GB/s, before synchronization and compute. Fusing state may reduce traffic and raises the register budget.
