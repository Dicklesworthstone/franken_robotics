## Specific instructions for this unit

Your chapter is the mathematical spine: forty constructions. The subsystem chapters implement them, and their drafters write the mechanism beads. Your job is the **specification beads**. For each construction C-nn in your range write one bead with key `SPEC-C-nn` (two digits, for example `SPEC-C-33`), type `task`, labels `spine`, `spec`, `lemma`, the milestone label and the tier. A specification bead is real work with concrete deliverables: (1) `docs/math/C-nn.md`, the construction written as a contract: definitions, every statement with its proof class (Proved, Certified, Tested or Estimated), every formula with all symbols defined, the conventions it fixes, its failure cases and what it does not claim; (2) the construction's law tests: property tests and known answers in the owning crate's test suite, each with a negative control; (3) its registry entries: claims, theorem cards, known answers. The body must carry the **complete** mathematical content of the construction: do not summarise away any statement, formula, table or worked number. It also says which crates implement the construction, which gates measure it, and which theorem cards (T-nn) and known answers (KA-nnn) belong to it. Parent: the work package that owns the implementing crate (see `wp_index.md`). Milestone: that of the first gate that needs the construction. Where a construction is long, split it: `SPEC-C-nn` for the core and further beads with your prefix for its parts, each complete, depending on the core. Dependencies: the seed beads that create the crate the law tests live in (see `seed_index.md`).
