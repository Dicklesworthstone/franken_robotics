2. One exit-review bead per milestone, unless one of your seed beads is that review: a fresh-context review that regenerates the gate report, checks that every gate of the milestone has a measured value and a caught negative control, and records a disposition for each. 5. §40.4 kill gates: the fallback ledger: one registry bead (or several) with every kill gate, its trigger, the milestone that decides it, and its fallback, so that the decision beads the chapter units write can be checked against it. §43.12 (no milestone is blocked by one failed bet) belongs here.
