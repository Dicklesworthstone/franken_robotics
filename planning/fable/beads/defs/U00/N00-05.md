## Background

A reader who must decide how much to trust a specification needs to know how it was made: what was examined, by
what method, what was found and what changed as a result, and above all what has not been done. This record is
the provenance of the specification the beads were drafted from. It is carried into the repository so that the
beads stand without the source document, and it is append-only so that later review rounds extend it instead of
rewriting history. The delivery log of implementation work is a different thing and lives in `docs/delivery/`.

**How the specification was produced (FACT, five stages).**

1. *Study of exemplars.* The plan documents of sibling projects were read and their structural devices reused on
   purpose: evidence labels and a definition of "implemented"; claims that are never conflated; ambition tiers
   and layer discipline; kill gates with fallbacks named in advance; a first implementation session and a
   definition of done; an idea-pass appendix.
2. *Audits of the foundations.* Seven code audits reported what the sibling projects contain in code as opposed
   to in plans; load-bearing facts were then checked directly in source; a public-source pass covered GPU
   simulators, Rust-to-GPU routes, geometric robotics, scene assets and benchmarks.
3. *A first complete version and an ambition pass.* A first plan of 3,666 lines with thirty ideas winnowed to
   fifteen; then five exercises after the owner asked for a ten-fold advance: an analysis by distinct modes of
   reasoning (94 atomic claims, 76 audited against primary sources and by recomputation); an adversarial idea
   duel among three agents; a hypothesis session with a question of record, twenty-four hypotheses, refuter-first
   investigation, a verdict register, a challenge round and an adjudication that changed sixteen rows; a harvest
   of neglected computer-science primitives against thirteen anticipated hot spots; a compilation of eleven
   decision problems into mathematical families with runtime artifacts, proof obligations and fallbacks.
4. *The blend.* Independently written complete plans of the same project were compared without sparing any of
   them; a catalog merged 112 distinct ideas with the calculations that killed some; an integration charter fixed
   names, tiers and sixteen binding decisions; the specification was written chapter by chapter against it. The
   owner's instruction was maximalist: include every sound mechanism at the tier its evidence supports.
5. *Review of the blend.* A mathematical review, a coherence review, two completeness audits, a reconciliation
   pass and a final delta audit.

**What has not happened (FACT).** No FrankenRobotics code exists. Nothing was compiled, run or measured as
FrankenRobotics. No incumbent simulator was run by this program: every statement about one is a documentation
finding at a pinned version or a published figure. The external review rounds the planning method calls for
have not been run on the current version (N00-30). Paper-scale pilots were run at plan time and none used
FrankenSim dynamics, contact or a real sensor pipeline (N00-06).

**Limits of the record (FACT).** Eighteen audited-claim candidates were never audited (N00-31). The idea duel's
synthesis was compiled by an agent of the same model family as one duellist. The first verdict register was
adjudicated from evidence gathered by investigators of one model family; the challenge round is what corrected
it. Web sources were often read through a summarizing fetch, so quoted figures must be re-read from the primary
source before a gate cites them (N00-34). The reviews of rounds 9 and 10 covered the chapters as they stood
before rounds 11 and 12 inserted 66 subsections; those insertions have had no independent review (N00-32).

## Scope

In: `docs/provenance/REVIEW_LOG.md` (the stages, the thirteen rounds, the two lists above), its data twin
`docs/provenance/review_log.toml`, and the append-only check. Out: the artifacts of the exercises themselves
(they stay under `planning/`); the plan-time numerical checks (N00-06); the hypothesis register (N00-10).

## Technical approach

1. Write `review_log.toml` with one `[[round]]` per row (`n`, `examined`, `by`, `findings`, `changed`, `date`)
   and the lists `not_happened` and `limits`, each item with the key of the bead that discharges it.
2. Generate `REVIEW_LOG.md` from it (`xtask check-generated` covers the pair).
3. **Append-only check** (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/review_log.rs`): compared with the merge base, existing
   rounds are byte-identical; new rounds have `n` greater than every existing one; an item leaves `not_happened`
   or `limits` only by gaining a `discharged_by` record that names a closed bead and the artifact it produced.
4. Later review rounds (N00-30, N00-32) append rounds 14 onward in the same format: what was examined, by whom,
   counts of findings by severity, and what changed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The log holds thirteen rounds with the counts above; the two lists hold five items each.
- [ ] Editing a recorded round fails the check; appending round 14 passes.
- [ ] Removing a `not_happened` item without a discharging record fails the check.
- [ ] Each of the five limit items names the bead that addresses it or is marked `standing` with a reason (the
      two same-family limits are standing: they are disclosed, and N00-30 is the mitigation).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`review_log.rs`: `thirteen_rounds_loaded`; `edit_of_round_9_rejected`; `append_round_14_accepted`;
`discharge_requires_closed_bead_and_artifact`; `generated_markdown_matches_data`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/review_log.sh` runs the test on the tree and on a scratch copy with a planted edit of round 3's
counts, asserting failure. Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code, the round touched, and a `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls
Counts to pin: round 3 audited 76 of 94 claims (31 + 39 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 76; 18 not reached); round 5's challenge
had 27 contests (11 upheld + 16 partly upheld); round 11 had 315 rows (260 + 33 + 19 + 3). Negative control: a
log in which round 3 reads "all 94 audited" must fail a consistency assertion (76 + 18 = 94).

## Considerations

- The record names methods and counts, not authors' reputations; a finding stands on its evidence.
- A later revision of the independently written plans is not reflected here; the diff is bead N00-36.
- This is a record with two readers (the generated document and the exit review, which checks that every limit
  has an owner); if neither reads it, it is deleted.
