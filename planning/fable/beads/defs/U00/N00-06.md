## Background

Several statements in the bead graph rest on small programs that were run while the specification was written:
static, scalar or toy-dynamics checks with expectations registered before they ran. They are FACT about those
small programs, not about FrankenRobotics. They were run outside this repository, their receipts sit in a
planning reproduction bundle, and this program re-derived several values by hand or by script without
re-executing the bundle. **None of them uses FrankenSim dynamics, contact or a real sensor pipeline; the
integrated system remains a hypothesis.** Two things follow: each check must be re-run inside this repository
before any gate cites it, and every sentence that leans on one must be traceable to its row.

The nine recorded checks (FACT, as recorded; to be reproduced):

| Check | Result as recorded | What it supports | Re-run by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stance family | 11,520 of 11,520 classifications agree with the exact classifier; minimum half-width 3/14 m | The family-answer fixture of the mission engine | BD-042 |
| Material probe | 675 of 675 correct in class; 225 of 225 reversed under a delayed law; direct dwell measurement 900 of 900 | A probe that is perfect in class can reverse on held-out cases | BD-043 |
| Sensor alias | Exact Bayes success 27 of 32, 459 of 512, 3,807 of 4,096 with repeated reads; the common-mode case stays at 0.75 | Observation aliasing bounds what a better body can do | BD-043 |
| Docking | Feasible if and only if ε < (U + 1/2)/4; 561 of 561 cells agree with a linear program | The docking fixture | BD-041 |
| Docking with three gains | Exact docking unsatisfiable; 34,590 of 34,590 labels reverse | A finite tolerance is part of the mission | BD-041 |
| Solver pilot (pusher) | 20 of 20 correct on both arms; 1.345 times warm and 1.088 times cold; a residual of 575.7 µs the solver does not touch; ceiling about 1.46 | Compute the ceiling before a speed test | BD-043 |
| Sill, planar and static | 94 of 156 agreement; a change of winding law flips 24 of 156 | Model form changes rankings | BD-043 |
| Reuse and deadline | A correctly keyed dependency graph ties a semantic receipt on these cases | Memoization is hygiene, not a lever | BD-043 |
| Integer predicates on devices | Identical labels on an M5 and on two RTX 4090; the host integer path was fastest | Exact-integer kernels for strict predicates | BD-030 |

Three further checks were made by this program's own investigators with scripts that were **not retained**; they
must be re-run before a gate cites them (bead N00-35): CPU reference rates for a humanoid in double precision on
the Mac; condition numbers of sampled contact Hessians against half-precision round-off (the refutation of
half-precision solves); the Metal working-set limit and largest single buffer.

Two more plan-time results are cited by the thesis and are re-run with the M0 artifact list: the reachable
verdicts of the frozen fixed-n rule (at n = 64: 2,145 count pairs, 521 prefer, 521 inferior, 0 ties, 1,103
inconclusive; a tie first reachable at n = 86), and the power of the draft ten-fold decision rule (a rule
requiring a point estimate of at least 10 on every brief confirms a true ratio of 10 in at most 18% of simulated
campaigns).

What the checks did not show is part of the record: every strong baseline tied or won (a lifted linear program
tied the projection; a programmed expert tied the probe generator; exact Bayes tied the sensor calculation; a
scenario tree tied the docking family; a dependency graph tied the receipt; quantifier elimination produced the
pusher answers). What survives is an interface and a discipline, not a speed claim.

## Scope

In: the ledger file with one record per check, the re-run status of each, the consistency assertions among the
recorded numbers, and the citation rule. Out: the re-runs themselves (BD-030, BD-041, BD-042, BD-043, N00-35);
the pilots still to run for Frontier and Moonshot mechanisms (N00-07).

## Technical approach

1. **Ledger.** `crates/frob-conformance/registry/plan_time_checks.toml`, one `[[check]]` per row above plus the
   three unretained and the two thesis results (fourteen records): `id` (`PTC-01` …), `name`, `recorded` (the
   counts and ratios as strings and as typed values), `supports`, `bundle_receipt` (digest in the reproduction
   bundle, or `none: script not retained`), `rerun` (`not-run` | `reproduced` | `differs`), `rerun_record`
   (ledger reference, commit, hardware fingerprint, seed), `rerun_bead`.
2. **Reproduction rule.** A count reproduces only if exactly equal; a timing ratio reproduces if inside its
   recorded band (the band is written before the re-run: for the pusher, warm 1.345 and cold 1.088 are point
   records, so the band is declared as ±15% of each, DESIGN, with the ceiling recomputed from the re-run's own
   residual). A check that differs is recorded as `differs` with both values; the statements that cite it are
   listed for review and the hypothesis register row it supports is reopened.
3. **Citation rule.** A scan of `registries/gates.toml`, contracts and bead-close records for the phrase "FACT,
   plan-time" or a `PTC-` identifier: each must resolve to a ledger record; a gate record may cite only checks
   with `rerun = "reproduced"`.
4. **Consistency assertions** on recorded values (exact): 675 + 225 = 900 probe cases; 27/32 = 0.84375,
   459/512 = 0.896484375, 3807/4096 = 0.929443359375 (monotone in reads); 842.8 ÷ 575.7 = 1.4639…; docking
   boundary at U = 3/4 is 5/16; 521 + 521 + 0 + 1,103 = 2,145.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ledger holds fourteen records; each names its re-run bead and its bundle receipt or "not retained".
- [ ] After BD-030, BD-041, BD-042 and BD-043 close, the nine recorded checks are `reproduced` (every count
      exact, every timing ratio inside its band) or `differs` with the consequences listed.
- [ ] A gate record citing a check whose status is `not-run` is rejected by the citation scan.
- [ ] GATE-GAUNT-08's re-run clause ("the plan-time pilots re-run at M0 reproduce every count exactly and every
      timing ratio inside its recorded band") can be evaluated from this ledger alone.
- [ ] The statement "none uses FrankenSim dynamics, contact or a real sensor pipeline" is printed in the header
      of every report generated from the ledger.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/plan_time_checks.rs`: `fourteen_records`; `counts_reproduce_only_if_equal`
(11,519 of 11,520 → `differs`); `timing_band_accepts_1_30_rejects_1_60` (warm ratio against 1.345 ± 15%);
`ceiling_recomputed_from_rerun_residual`; `gate_citing_unrerun_check_rejected`; `arithmetic_consistency` (the
five assertions of step 4).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/plan_time_checks.sh` runs the re-run commands of the owning beads where available, updates the
ledger in a scratch copy and runs the scan. Logged per STD-E2E: per check a `metric` line with recorded value,
re-run value, band and verdict; commit, fingerprint and seed; `summary` with reproduced, differing and not-run
counts and gate GATE-GAUNT-08.

### Known answers and negative controls
The recorded counts above are the known answers (KA-001, KA-002, KA-017, KA-018 and the fixture rows KA-019 to
KA-026 draw on them). Negative controls: a re-run that reports 560 of 561 docking cells must be recorded as
`differs`, not rounded to agreement; a ledger whose pusher record cites a ceiling of 1.43 (the withdrawn
"shared share" derivation, 1.345 ÷ 0.94) must fail the consistency assertion.

## Considerations

- A re-run that differs is a result, not an embarrassment: it changes a hypothesis verdict through the
  append-only register (N00-10), never by editing the old record.
- The integer-predicate check ran on the three GPUs; its re-run needs both machines and is the only row here
  that does.
- The bundle is an external artifact; if it cannot be located, the affected rows are re-derived from first
  principles by their re-run beads and the ledger says `bundle_receipt = "unavailable"`.
