## Background

A work package can be funded for several honest reasons: it is needed for correctness, for trust, for coverage
of a mission class, for parity with incumbents, or because it is expected to shorten the time to an accepted
answer. Only the last is a *speed lever*, and the planning evidence closed several tempting speed arguments
before any code existed. This bead turns that evidence into a record that must exist before a package is funded
and a check that refuses the arguments already closed.

**What will not deliver ten-fold (stated plainly so that no package is funded on a hope the evidence closed).**

1. *A faster step.* Engine throughput is a parity requirement. The design must match incumbents closely enough
   that the levers are not cancelled; it does not look for the result there. One engine-internal question is
   open: structure-exploiting constraint assembly and solve, whose ceiling on an incumbent's trace is 2.6×; it
   is measured at M2 and is not counted in the thesis until then.
2. *Per-design kernel specialization.* Specialization of the recursions is capped near 1.2×. It is an
   optimization for survivors, and only if our own profile shows the recursion share justifies it.
3. *Half precision or tensor cores for contact.* Refuted. Not built.
4. *Sampling control as a stand-in evaluator.* Even the most sample-efficient published controller costs about
   4 × 10³ times a policy rollout per simulated second: a battery of 256 twenty-second episodes takes about 86
   minutes of a device against about 1.3 s for a policy. It is a screening rung, a fallback and a source of
   demonstrations; its episodes are acceptance evidence only for itself, where it is the delivered controller.
5. *One universal policy.* Out of reach at an affordable pretraining budget.
6. *Static checks alone.* Pruning trivial failures caps near 1.19× on trainings. Their value is feedback in
   seconds and typed repairs, not pruning.
7. *Reusing rollouts across edits.* Exact reuse is at most about 10% of compute.
8. *A faster solver for family queries.* A specialized solver measured 1.345× warm and 1.088× cold against a
   composed one, under a ceiling of about 1.46× set by a residual the solver does not touch.
9. *Certificates, full-room multiphysics and generative structure.* Certificates change what can be trusted,
   coupled multiphysics changes which failures are visible, structure optimization improves the built object.
   None shortens the time to a decision, and none is counted in the thesis.
10. *Any published ten-fold.* None was found for time to a qualified legged-robot design.

**What the program does first because of this (the order of first evidence).**

1. *Measure the rival before building against it.* At M0 and M1 the incumbent loop is timed by phase on host
   `trj`, starting with what needs no agent: cold start, a topology edit to the first step, and training to
   threshold on one and two cards. Versions and warm configurations are pinned and frozen first.
2. *Run the rank-agreement experiment early.* Whether cheap rungs order designs as the trained,
   referee-promoted rung does decides the "train few" lever and half of the "spend where it matters" lever.
   About forty per-design trainings (about 5 GPU-hours where specialists are reused, about 45 otherwise) need
   the owner's approval because they exceed the default compute envelope.
3. *Put the first mission slice on the CPU at M1.* The docking fixture exercises the mission query, the clock
   contract, the certificate bundle and the auditor before any GPU work exists.
4. *Hold a partial gate at M3* on legged and wheeled briefs, with a written rule for each outcome, and the
   tournament at M7.

Three hygiene rules complete the basis: the incumbent is measured before a mechanism is funded against it; the
ceiling is computed before any speed test; a frozen decision rule is enumerated before sampling.

## Scope

In: the funding record, its admission check, the closed-hope list as data, and the ordering assertions on the
bead graph. Out: computing a ceiling (N00-17); the pilot ledger (N00-07); the measurements themselves (BD-040,
BD-143); the program-wide gate record (the ten-fold unit).

## Technical approach

1. **Record.** `crates/frob-conformance/registry/funding.toml`, one `[[funding]]` per work package at the moment
   it is funded (schema `frob.funding/1`, DESIGN):
   `work_package`; `milestone`; `justification` ∈ {`correctness`, `trust`, `coverage`, `parity`, `speed-lever`};
   `reason` (one sentence); for `speed-lever`: `lever` (1 to 8, the lever register of N00-20), `ledger_phase`
   (the row of the measured incumbent phase ledger it attacks), `baseline_record` (ledger reference of the
   measured rival run, never a published figure), `removable_share` (measured fraction of the rival's loop the
   mechanism can remove), `ceiling` (from N00-17 with its inputs f, r, h), `claimed` (the ratio it sets out to
   show); for upper tiers: `pilot` (identifier in the pilot ledger); `envelope` (default, E1, E2, E3) with the
   approval reference when above default; `decided_by`, `date`.
2. **Admission check** `fn admit(record) -> Result<(), Refusal>`:
   a. `speed-lever` with no `baseline_record`, or one that points at a published figure, is refused ("measure
      the rival first");
   b. `speed-lever` with `ceiling < claimed` is refused with FROB-REF-866 (a speed experiment requested without
      its computed ceiling, or with a ceiling below its goal);
   c. `speed-lever` with `removable_share < 0.10` is refused under KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER: the package may be
      re-registered under another justification with that reason written and no speed figure;
   d. a `reason` that matches the closed-hope list (items 1 to 10 above, stored as patterns with their
      refutations in `closed_hopes.toml`) is refused with the item's refutation quoted; item 1's open exception
      (structure-exploiting solve) is admitted only as `parity` until the M2 profile exists;
   e. an upper-tier package is passed to `may_fund` of the pilot ledger;
   f. an envelope above default with no approval reference is refused, and the package's default-envelope
      variant is named.
3. **Ordering assertions on the bead graph** (run against the tracker export): every bead labelled as a speed
   lever at M2 or later depends, directly or transitively, on the incumbent phase-ledger beads (BD-040, BD-143);
   the rank-agreement pre-registration (BD-145) precedes any bead that eliminates designs on a cheap rung; the
   first mission slice (BD-140) has no dependency on any device bead; the partial-gate protocol (BD-314) precedes
   its arms.
4. **Re-reading.** At every milestone exit the check is re-run with the measured values then available (our own
   profile from M2); a record whose ceiling fell below its claim is flagged and its package loses its speed
   justification.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every work package funded from M1 onward has a funding record admitted by the check.
- [ ] The six refusals of step 2 each fire on their planted record (test plan).
- [ ] No package is recorded as a speed lever against a published denominator once the measured phase ledger
      exists (GATE-TENFOLD-02's consequence: the published table may not be cited as a denominator).
- [ ] The four ordering assertions hold on the bead graph, and a planted edge that lets a device bead block the
      first mission slice is reported.
- [ ] GATE-TENFOLD-09 can read, for every registered speed comparison, the ceiling and the claim from this
      record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/funding.rs`: `published_denominator_refused`; `ceiling_below_claim_refused_866`
(f = 0.85, r = ∞, h = 0, ceiling 20/3, claim 10); `removable_share_0_08_refused_as_lever` and
`…_readmitted_as_trust_without_speed_figure`; `closed_hope_static_pruning_refused` (reason "static checks will
prune enough designs for ten-fold" → item 6 with 1.19× quoted); `closed_hope_faster_step_refused` (→ item 1,
1/(1 − 0.87) = 7.7); `frontier_package_without_pilot_refused`; `e2_envelope_without_approval_refused`;
`ordering_device_bead_blocks_mission_slice_reported`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/funding_check.sh` registers the planted records and one sound record through `cargo run -p
frob-conformance -- funding admit --json`, then runs the ordering assertions on the tracker export. Logged per
STD-E2E: per record the justification, f, r, h, ceiling, claim, the refusal code or `admitted`; `summary` with
admitted and refused counts and gate GATE-TENFOLD-09.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-010 (1/(1 − s)), KA-013 (410/38 ≈ 10.8), KA-014 (general bound), KA-015 (1.19×); CX-001, CX-004, CX-006,
CX-007, CX-009. Negative controls: the six planted records; a mutant check that compares the claim with the
measured ratio instead of the ceiling.

## Considerations

- The record is deliberately small and is read by the milestone entry review and by GATE-TENFOLD-09; it is not
  a project-management system.
- A package refused as a speed lever is usually still worth building for another reason; the refusal changes
  what may be said about it, not whether it exists.
- If the measured M0 ledger puts every ceiling under 3 for every mission class, the thesis statement itself is
  rewritten before any work is funded against it.
