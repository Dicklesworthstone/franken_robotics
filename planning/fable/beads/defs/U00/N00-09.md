## Background

A work package can be funded for several honest reasons: it is needed for correctness, for trust, for coverage
of a mission class, for parity with incumbents, or because it is expected to shorten the time to an accepted
answer. Only the last is a *speed lever*, and the planning evidence closed several tempting speed arguments
before any code existed. This bead turns that evidence into a record that must exist before a package is funded
and a check that refuses the arguments already closed.

**What will not deliver ten-fold (stated plainly so that no package is funded on a hope the evidence closed).**

1. *A faster step.* Engine throughput is a parity requirement. The design must match incumbents closely enough
   that the levers are not cancelled; it does not look for the result there. One engine-internal question is
   open: structure-exploiting constraint assembly and solve, whose ceiling on an incumbent's trace is 2.6×; it
   is measured at M2 and is not counted in the thesis until then.
2. *Per-design kernel specialization.* Specialization of the recursions is capped near 1.2×. It is an
   optimization for survivors, and only if our own profile shows the recursion share justifies it.
3. *Half precision or tensor cores for contact.* Refuted. Not built.
4. *Sampling control as a stand-in evaluator.* Even the most sample-efficient published controller costs about
   4 × 10³ times a policy rollout per simulated second: a battery of 256 twenty-second episodes takes about 86
   minutes of a device against about 1.3 s for a policy. It is a screening rung, a fallback and a source of
   demonstrations; its episodes are acceptance evidence only for itself, where it is the delivered controller.
5. *One universal policy.* Out of reach at an affordable pretraining budget.
6. *Static checks alone.* Pruning trivial failures caps near 1.19× on trainings. Their value is feedback in
   seconds and typed repairs, not pruning.
7. *Reusing rollouts across edits.* Exact reuse is at most about 10% of compute.
8. *A faster solver for family queries.* A specialized solver measured 1.345× warm and 1.088× cold against a
   composed one, under a ceiling of about 1.46× set by a residual the solver does not touch.
9. *Certificates, full-room multiphysics and generative structure.* Certificates change what can be trusted,
   coupled multiphysics changes which failures are visible, structure optimization improves the built object.
   None shortens the time to a decision, and none is counted in the thesis.
10. *Any published ten-fold.* None was found for time to a qualified legged-robot design.

**What the program does first because of this (the order of first evidence).**

1. *Measure the rival before building against it.* At M0 and M1 the incumbent loop is timed by phase on host
   `trj`, starting with what needs no agent: cold start, a topology edit to the first step, and training to
   threshold on one and two cards. Versions and warm configurations are pinned and frozen first.
2. *Run the rank-agreement experiment early.* Whether cheap rungs order designs as the trained,
   referee-promoted rung does decides the "train few" lever and half of the "spend where it matters" lever.
   About forty per-design trainings (about 5 GPU-hours where specialists are reused, about 45 otherwise) need
   the owner's approval because they exceed the default compute envelope.
3. *Put the first mission slice on the CPU at M1.* The docking fixture exercises the mission query, the clock
   contract, the certificate bundle and the auditor before any GPU work exists.
4. *Hold a partial gate at M3* on legged and wheeled briefs, with a written rule for each outcome, and the
   tournament at M7.

Three hygiene rules complete the basis: the incumbent is measured before a mechanism is funded against it; the
ceiling is computed before any speed test; a frozen decision rule is enumerated before sampling.

## Scope

In: the funding record (file, schema, justification values), its admission check applied when one record is
registered, the closed-hope list as data, and the ordering assertions on the bead graph.

Out, with the bead that has it: computing a ceiling (N00-17); the code FROB-REF-866 itself, its comparison of
ceiling with goal and its ranked repairs (N36-05's `admit_experiment`, the code's one home; the row is N35-REF's);
the hypothesis register's scope rule and its function `hypotheses::scope` (N00-10), which `admit` and the
ordering assertions call; the pilot ledger (N00-07); the measurements themselves (BD-040, BD-143); the measurement of GATE-TENFOLD-09 (N39a-38, which depends on this bead: its
funding-rule check sweeps every admitted record against the shares the closed ledgers show, and it holds the
ceiling attachment of a registered speed comparison and the order rule for products of levers); the gate's
register row, its part at every exit and the verdict (N40-18, which also depends on this bead); the decision
KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER (N39a-39); the refusal of funding records for M2 work packages while the schedule gate's
re-plan is pending (BET-12, which adds that rule to `admit`). This bead measures no gate and writes no gate record:
it supplies the record those beads read, and the refusal of a bad record at the door.

## Technical approach

1. **Record.** `crates/frob-conformance/registry/funding.toml`, one `[[funding]]` per work package at the moment
   it is funded (schema `frob.funding/1`, DESIGN):
   `work_package`; `milestone`; `justification` ∈ {`correctness`, `trust`, `coverage`, `parity`, `speed-lever`};
   `reason` (one sentence); for `speed-lever`: `lever` (1 to 8, the lever register of N00-20), `ledger_phase`
   (the row of the measured incumbent phase ledger it attacks), `baseline_record` (ledger reference of the
   measured rival run, never a published figure), `removable_share` (measured fraction of the rival's loop the
   mechanism can remove), `ceiling` (from N00-17 with its inputs f, r, h), `claimed` (the ratio it sets out to
   show), and optionally `classes` (the mission classes, by the stratum identifiers of the rival's ledgers such as
   `legged-quad` and `wheeled`, for which the speed justification is claimed; absent means every class with a
   measured closed ledger); for upper tiers: `pilot` (identifier in the pilot ledger); `envelope` (default, E1,
   E2, E3) with the approval reference when above default; `decided_by`, `date`. DESIGN, the reason for
   `classes`: the decision KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER (N39a-39) strikes a lever per class and keeps it for the others, and
   without the field that restriction could live only in the prose of `reason`, which no check can read.
2. **Admission check** `fn admit(record) -> Result<(), Refusal>`:
   a. `speed-lever` with no `baseline_record`, or one that points at a published figure, or one whose run's
      phase ledger is not `ledger-closed` (its exclusive phases sum to the run's time within 2%; a run with more
      than 2% `unattributed` is flagged `ledger-open` and excluded from ceiling arithmetic, per the closure rule of
      N39a-24; GATE-TENFOLD-09 counts removable shares only on `ledger-closed` runs of the strong baseline), is
      refused ("measure the rival first");
   b. `speed-lever` with no `ceiling`, or with `ceiling < claimed`, is refused with FROB-REF-866 (a speed
      experiment requested without its computed ceiling, or with a ceiling below its goal; exit class 3). `admit`
      does not build this refusal: it builds a ceiling record `frob.perf.ceiling/1` from the funding record
      (`experiment` = the work package, `goal` = `claimed`, `baseline.profile` = the `ledger-closed` phase ledger
      of `baseline_record` with its cache state and fingerprint, `f`, `r`, `h` and `ceiling` from N00-17's
      calculator) and calls N36-05's `admit_experiment`, which raises the code with the four ranked repairs of
      N35-REF's row in order (compute the ceiling from the current profile; lower the goal to at most the
      ceiling; choose a target with a larger share of the baseline; fund the work for a reason other than speed,
      with no speed figure). `admit` passes that refusal through unchanged and holds no second comparison of
      ceiling with claim and no second list of repairs; N36-REF's matrix lists this check among the raising
      sites outside the performance program;
   c. `speed-lever` with `removable_share < 0.10` is refused under KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER: the package may be
      re-registered under another justification with that reason written and no speed figure;
   d. a `reason` that matches the closed-hope list (items 1 to 10 above, stored as patterns with their
      refutations in `closed_hopes.toml`) is refused with the item's refutation quoted; item 1's open exception
      (structure-exploiting solve) is admitted only as `parity` until the M2 profile exists;
   e. an upper-tier package is passed to `may_fund` of the pilot ledger;
   f. an envelope above default with no approval reference is refused, and the package's default-envelope
      variant is named;
   g. `speed-lever` with a `classes` entry that has no `ledger-closed` baseline run of its own is refused ("measure
      the rival first", naming the class); and once a KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER decision record exists for the record's
      lever, a listed class on which that record struck the lever is refused with the decision record's hash
      quoted, while the other listed classes are unaffected (the package is re-registered with the struck class
      removed from `classes`). `classes` on a record that is not `speed-lever` is refused as meaningless;
   h. a `reason` for which N00-10's `hypotheses::scope` returns `hyp.refuted-bound` (it states the strong form of a
      REFUTED hypothesis whose reversal condition has no history entry) is refused with the row id, the matched
      pattern and the recorded reversal condition quoted (for a proposal-only row, its `reversal_proposed`, which
      the owner must first adopt); `admit` holds no pattern list of its own.
   The function and the funding sweep of N39a-38 read `classes` (check g) the same way: the sweep checks the cited share
   class by class for the classes listed (all measured classes when absent).
3. **Ordering assertions on the bead graph** (run against the tracker export): every bead labelled as a speed
   lever at M2 or later depends, directly or transitively, on the incumbent phase-ledger beads (BD-040, BD-143);
   the rank-agreement pre-registration (BD-145) precedes any bead that eliminates designs on a cheap rung; the
   first mission slice (BD-140) has no dependency on any device bead; the partial-gate protocol (BD-314) precedes
   its arms; and every funded work package for which `hypotheses::scope`, given its record's `reason` and the
   gates its beads list in the export, returns `hyp.active-unsettled` has a bead that depends, directly or
   transitively, on a bead whose `gates` field lists that row's `settled_by` test (N00-10 step 4: work bound by
   an ACTIVE hypothesis waits on the test that settles it).
4. **Re-reading.** At every milestone exit the check is re-run with the measured values then available (our own
   profile from M2); a record whose ceiling fell below its claim is flagged and its package loses its speed
   justification. The re-run is started by the gate's recurring part (N40-18) through the sweep of N39a-38; what
   this bead supplies for it is that `admit` is a pure function of a stored record and the measured values, so
   that it can be re-applied, and the field `speed_justification_lost` (with the exit and the reason) that the
   sweep sets on a flagged record.
5. **Command (DESIGN name, an example target under BD-025's rule that the crate has no default binary).**
   `crates/frob-conformance/examples/funding.rs`, declared by this bead in the crate's `CONTRACT.md` row of
   examples: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example funding -- admit --record <path> --json` applies `admit` to
   one record file against the committed `funding.toml`, the closed ledgers and the decision records, and prints
   one JSON object (`work_package`, `outcome` `admitted` or `refused`, and for a refusal the code or reason, the
   pointer and the repairs); it exits 0 when the record is admitted and 3 when it is refused.
   `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example funding -- order --export <tracker JSONL> --json` runs the five
   ordering assertions of step 3 and prints each with its offending edges; exit 0 when all hold. Neither
   subcommand writes `funding.toml` or any gate row: an admitted record is committed by the funding change, and
   `committed_records_admitted` re-applies `admit` to every committed record.
6. **One vocabulary with the gate (DESIGN).** The justification values are the five above; the kill rule
   KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER re-justifies a package as `trust`, `coverage` or `correctness`, and `parity` is the value
   for work needed to match the incumbent (item 1 of the closed hopes); N39a-38 and N40-18 read these values and
   define none. The field `claimed` is the ratio a work package sets out to show; a registered speed comparison
   has its own field for the same idea, `goal_ratio`, in the attachment N39a-38 defines. Both refusals are the
   same product code, FROB-REF-866.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every work package funded from M1 onward has a funding record admitted by the check.
- [ ] The eight refusals of step 2 (a to h) each fire on their planted record (test plan).
- [ ] A speed-lever record restricted by `classes` is admitted for its unstruck classes and refused, with the
      decision record's hash, for a class on which KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER struck its lever; a record without
      `classes` covers every measured class.
- [ ] No package is recorded as a speed lever against a published denominator once the measured phase ledger
      exists (GATE-TENFOLD-02's consequence: the published table may not be cited as a denominator).
- [ ] The five ordering assertions hold on the bead graph, and a planted edge that lets a device bead block the
      first mission slice is reported, as is a funded package bound by an ACTIVE hypothesis with no path to its
      settling test.
- [ ] GATE-TENFOLD-09 can read, for every registered speed comparison, the ceiling and the claim from this
      record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/funding.rs`: `committed_records_admitted` (every record of `funding.toml` passes
`admit`; a planted committed record with `ceiling < claimed` fails the test); `published_denominator_refused`; `open_ledger_baseline_refused` (a
baseline run whose ledger is not `ledger-closed`); `ceiling_below_claim_refused_866`
(f = 0.85, r = ∞, h = 0, ceiling 20/3, claim 10); `ceiling_refusal_raised_by_admit_experiment` (the same
record's refusal carries pointer `goal` and the four repairs of the N35-REF row of 866 in their order, read from
the row; a record with no `ceiling` → 866 with pointer `ceiling`); `removable_share_0_08_refused_as_lever` and
`…_readmitted_as_trust_without_speed_figure`; `closed_hope_static_pruning_refused` (reason "static checks will
prune enough designs for ten-fold" → item 6 with 1.19× quoted); `closed_hope_faster_step_refused` (→ item 1,
1/(1 − 0.87) = 7.7); `frontier_package_without_pilot_refused`; `e2_envelope_without_approval_refused`;
`ordering_device_bead_blocks_mission_slice_reported`; `classes_absent_means_all_measured` (closed ledgers for
`legged-quad` and `wheeled`, no `classes` → the record covers both); `class_without_closed_ledger_refused`
(`classes = ["wheeled"]` with only a `ledger-open` wheeled run → refused, class named);
`struck_class_refused_other_admitted` (lever 2 struck on a `synthetic` class at 6% and kept on `legged-quad` at
54.67% → `classes = ["legged-quad"]` admitted, `classes = ["legged-quad", "synthetic"]` refused naming
`synthetic`); `classes_on_non_speed_refused`; `refuted_strong_form_reason_refused` (reason "per-design kernel
specialization is the default" → refused naming H-04 and its reversal condition; admitted for a `synthetic`
register whose H-04 history records the condition met); `active_bound_package_without_settling_dependency_reported`
(a `synthetic` export whose package lists a gate in H-04b's `binds` and has no path to its `settled_by` test).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/funding_check.sh` submits each planted record (refusals a to h) and one sound record through
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example funding -- admit --record <path> --json`, expecting exit 3 with the
planted refusal for each planted record and exit 0 for the sound one, then runs `cargo run -p frob-conformance
--example funding -- order --export <tracker JSONL> --json` on the live export and on a scratch copy with the
planted device edge into the first mission slice (expected: reported). Logged per STD-E2E: per record the exact
command line, exit code and wall time, the justification, f, r, h, ceiling, claim, the refusal code or reason
with its pointer and repairs, or `admitted`; per ordering assertion the edges checked and any offending edge;
`summary` with admitted and refused counts and gate GATE-TENFOLD-09.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-010 (1/(1 − s)), KA-013 (410/38 ≈ 10.8), KA-014 (general bound), KA-015 (1.19×); CX-001, CX-004, CX-006,
CX-007, CX-009. Negative controls: the planted records of refusals a to h; a mutant check that compares the claim
with the measured ratio instead of the ceiling; a mutant `admit` that compares ceiling with claim itself and
builds its own 866 without calling `admit_experiment` (caught by `ceiling_refusal_raised_by_admit_experiment`,
since its repairs are not the row's); a mutant `admit` that ignores `classes` (caught by
`struck_class_refused_other_admitted`).

## Considerations

- The record is deliberately small and is read by the milestone entry review and by GATE-TENFOLD-09; it is not
  a project-management system.
- A package refused as a speed lever is usually still worth building for another reason; the refusal changes
  what may be said about it, not whether it exists.
- If the measured M0 ledger puts every ceiling under 3 for every mission class, the thesis statement itself is
  rewritten before any work is funded against it.
