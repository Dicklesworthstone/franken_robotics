## Background

Second half of the hypothesis verdict register (rules in N00-10; first half in N00-11). States: ACTIVE
(unrefuted and untested), NARROWED (a weaker claim survives), REFUTED (not built in the strong form; reopened
only by its reversal condition), DEFERRED. Every number is a range read from published work or computed from
it: a FACT about its source and a HYPOTHESIS about this system until a gate records a measurement. H-18, H-19
and H-22a are the rows on which the ten-fold thesis turns.

## Scope

In: eighteen `[[hypothesis]]` records (H-13 to H-21, H-22a, H-22b, H-23 to H-29). Out: the register's rules
(N00-10).

## Technical approach

Records are appended to `crates/frob-conformance/registry/hypotheses.toml`, each with an initial history entry
dated 2026-11-21.

| ID | Hypothesis | State | What survives | Where it binds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-13 | Requirements replace rewards | NARROWED | Bounded-time requirements as the acceptance metric and as a falsification score. Not a replacement for shaped rewards | Missions are requirements; training keeps reward terms (BET-24) |
| H-14 | Conformal reality bands | NARROWED | Coverage within one calibrated robot and task family, varying by about six points at n = 20. Nothing for designs that were never built | Ground issues bands per calibrated family and refuses elsewhere (BET-28) |
| H-15 | Half precision | REFUTED | The condition number times half-precision round-off is at or above one in 37–64% of sampled contact states, and refinement alone costs more than the single-precision factorization | Not built. Single precision with diagonal scaling (BET-08) |
| H-16 | Living multiphysics in every world | REFUTED as stated | Full-room fluid is out of memory by orders of magnitude, and coupled cloth and cable at batch speed are out of reach on present foundations. A contact-level film-friction law and rigid proxies are the Solid substitutes; whether they reproduce the failures of coupled models is untested | The film law and the proxies are Solid. The proxy-adequacy pilot decides the proxies' claim and whether a coupled island is ever funded; islands are Moonshot (BET-36) |
| H-17 | From mission to design by solving | NARROWED | Trajectory optimization with design variables as local refinement (about 12 degrees of freedom, up to 17 parameters, 4–10 minutes). Polynomial synthesis only inside the symbolic library's caps. Monotone catalog selection of actuators, batteries and compute, with no measured timing. The pass rate of synthesized designs under independent closed-loop evaluation is unknown | A bounded exact synthesis lane, a refinement lane and catalog selection; continuation is Moonshot, on the CPU; every synthesized candidate passes the ordinary ladder |
| H-18 | Information, not the engine, is the lever | ACTIVE | No instrumented design session exists. Where every candidate is trained, training is about 96% of an illustrative campaign and information features that do not remove training cap near 1.04×. Once controller acquisition is amortized, agent turns are about two thirds of elapsed time. Which regime a mission is in is a measurement | The ten-fold test measures decisions, with a phase ledger and three single-change ablations |
| H-19 | A thin layer over an incumbent would do | ACTIVE | Decided by a measured recovery ratio, not by a count of gaps. Documented engine-internal gaps: a mixed-topology batch with run-to-run replay; a Metal tier. Unconfirmed gaps, to be checked before they are relied on: replay of one world outside its batch; solver-internal certificates. In the incumbent documents read, run-to-run determinism, heterogeneous worlds and the fastest solver sit on different solver paths, so each is available and their conjunction is not shown | The ten-fold test has three arms and reports how much of the gain a composed incumbent recovers; the design says what it does if that arm ties (BET-14) |
| H-20 | Proof-guided control | NARROWED | Reduced-order barrier shields with a statistically measured margin. No full-order certificates | An energy-budget tier and runtime filters, classed Tested (BET-25) |
| H-21 | Policy inheritance | NARROWED | No published figure measures a small typed parameter edit from parent to child. Transfer from a multi-embodiment prior to a similar body costs about 2–25% of a from-scratch run stopped at matched return; the one parent-to-child figure is about 50%. Single-limb topology edits: 33–100%. Chained inheritance risks loss of plasticity and narrows morphological diversity | Inheritance for parameter edits must beat an early-stopped from-scratch control per edit class before it is the default. Resets follow a measured regression of an audit twin, with a depth schedule as fallback (BET-16) |
| H-22a | Rank fidelity between rungs | ACTIVE | No published rank agreement between a shared policy and specialists was found. The nearest figure, 0.40, compares a critic with its own policy. A high rank correlation does not protect the winner: with 40 designs and the true best at cheap rank 3, τ is 0.91 on the top ten and a cut to two discards it | The rank-agreement experiment is the decisive early experiment of the program, and it reports containment and regret beside τ (BET-17) |
| H-22b | The fast tier as a paired control | ACTIVE | A paired correction gains 1/(1 − ρ²) before cost: 2.0× at 0.7, 2.8× at 0.8, 5.3× at 0.9. The correlation is unmeasured and is reported separately for continuous metrics and for binary outcomes | The tier-correction ledger |
| H-23 | A family per dispatch | NARROWED | Fewer rounds are plausible; measured compute gains in published population methods are about 3–4×. Paired finite-difference gradients are unproven over long contact-rich horizons | Families per dispatch, with short-horizon paired differences |
| H-24 | A geometric-algebra tree policy | NARROWED | Invariance to heading and to description conventions is necessary, and augmentation recovers most of it. There is no locomotion evidence for the geometric-algebra specifics: expect 0–1.3× | A Moonshot experiment with a plain morphology transformer as the control arm (BET-30) |
| H-25 | Brackets on what a body can achieve | ACTIVE | A controller-independent upper bound and an achieved lower bound on task utility; controller effort stops only when a checked upper bound falls below a competitor's lower bound. The bound is often vacuous for contact-rich full-order tasks | Utility brackets and the dominance stop in mission queries and the studio |
| H-26 | Common randomness compiled from the task | ACTIVE | Separate address domains for world fields, absolute time and task-triggered events keep paired draws paired across retiming and topology changes. A paired contrast's variance scales with 1 − ρ. Any change in a marginal distribution is a failure of the mechanism | Exposure domains and the coupling test in the task layer; randomness by address |
| H-27 | Step adjoints with short-horizon joint training | ACTIVE | Implicit-function adjoints through the converged contact solve are exact for a fixed active set and cheap. Several incumbents differentiate their step, so this is no differentiator. Gradients say nothing about outcomes decided by a change of contact sequence | Moonshot, off the critical path |
| H-28 | Placement and sizing along a prescribed motion | ACTIVE | Inverse dynamics is linear in inertial parameters and contact forces for a fixed motion, so placement and sizing is one cone program. Its certificate excludes a motion, not a design; gear ratios are enumerated; link lengths are not variables | The prescribed-motion sizing query of the mission engine |
| H-29 | Visibility and observability in sensor placement | ACTIVE | Visibility along a motion with the robot's own limbs and payload as occluders is a cheap, missing analysis. The observability index is exact only without bias states. The share of estimated-state failures it explains is unknown | Visibility under self-occlusion, and sensor mounts as design variables |

**Banned wording patterns (DESIGN).** H-13: "requirements replace rewards". H-14: a reality band or any "sim-to-real"
figure for a design that was never built. H-15: half precision, 16-bit floats or tensor cores for contact
solves. H-16: "full multiphysics in every world", "fluid in every training world". H-17: "mission to design by
solving" as a general capability. H-20: "provably safe", "certified controller", full-order safety
certificates. H-24: geometric-algebra policies described as an advantage without the control-arm result. H-27:
differentiability or gradients through contact as a differentiator.

**Reversal conditions for the REFUTED rows.** None is printed with H-15 or H-16; the register records, as
DESIGN defaults to be confirmed by the owner: H-15 is reopened only by a measurement on our own contact battery
showing κ·u < 1 in at least 99% of states after scaling; H-16 is reopened only by a byte model and a measured
step cost showing a full-room field fits the device budget at batch size. Until confirmed, both rows are
"no reversal recorded: not reopened". Encoding (the field of N00-10): each row leaves `reversal` absent and
holds its proposal in `reversal_proposed`; the change rule never treats a proposal as a recorded condition, so
neither row can move until the owner's decision sets `reversal`. H-09 in N00-11 is entered the same way.

**Settling tests.** H-13: the requirement-conformance and reward-exploit gates. H-14: the conformal-coverage
gate (mean in-cell coverage within 0.01 of 19/21 at n = 20). H-17: the synthesis-lane gates under independent
closed-loop evaluation. H-18: the measured incumbent phase ledger at M0 and M1 and the three ablations at M3.
H-19: the recovery ratios r_T and r_C of the ten-fold test at M3 and M7. H-20: the shield gates (return kept at
least 85%, step cost at most 5%, measured margin). H-21: the inheritance gate on 12 parent-child edit pairs.
H-22a: the rank-agreement experiment (thresholds 0.7 and 0.5 fixed before data). H-22b: the tier-correction
ledger's measured ρ on 200 paired addresses. H-23: the families-per-dispatch gate and the finite-difference
gradient pilot. H-24: the three-arm tree-policy experiment. H-25: the utility-bracket gate. H-26: the coupling
test. H-27: the step-adjoint gate. H-28: the prescribed-motion sizing gate. H-29: the visibility gates.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/hypotheses_rows_2.rs`: `eighteen_rows_states`; `h18_information_cap`
(training share 0.96 → features that remove no training cap at 1/(1 − 0.04) = 1.04);
`h22a_winner_lost_example` (40 designs, true best at cheap rank 3, a cut to two discards it; the test builds
the ranking whose cheap top ten carry the true ranks (2, 3, 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10): two discordant pairs of 45,
so Kendall τ on the top ten is (43 − 2)/45 = 41/45 = 0.911, and asserts τ ≥ 0.9 while containment of the best in
the top two is false);
`h22b_gain_table` (ρ = 0.7 → 1.96; 0.8 → 2.78; 0.9 → 5.26); `h14_coverage_19_over_21` (the n = 20 conformal
index gives nominal coverage 19/21 = 0.905 for a 0.90 target); `refuted_rows_have_proposal_only` (H-15 and H-16
load with `reversal_proposed` and no `reversal`, and a history entry that tries to move either is rejected).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/evidence_ledger.sh` (owned by N00-10, a dependency; this bead adds its stage `rows_h13_h29`) runs
`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test hypotheses_rows_2`, prints these rows and runs the wording lint on a
scratch clone with one planted sentence for each of the eight rows that carry banned wording (H-13, H-14, H-15,
H-16, H-17, H-20, H-24, H-27; each caught as `hyp.banned-wording`, H-27's as an ACTIVE row's pattern). Logged per
STD-E2E: row id, state, each finding with file and line, and a `summary` with plants caught out of eight.

### Known answers and negative controls
The arithmetic above. Negative controls: a planted sentence "contact solves run in half precision on tensor
cores"; a planted reality band for an unbuilt design in a report template; a register copy in which H-19 leaves
ACTIVE with no history entry citing a gate record (rejected by N00-10's change rule).
