## Background

The project's claims are comparative, so its first obligation is an honest account of what the strongest
existing systems already do. That account was made before any design decision: the incumbents were read in their
own files at pinned versions (MuJoCo and MuJoCo Warp 4.0.1, Newton 1.8.0, Warp 1.19.0, Isaac Lab 3.0.0 with
Isaac Sim 6.2.0, Genesis 1.4.7, Drake 1.58.0, ManiSkill 3.0.1). **No incumbent GPU simulator was run** (the one
executed incumbent measurement is CPU MuJoCo on the Mac): a capability listed here is documented, not
demonstrated, and where a search found nothing the text says "not found", which is absence of evidence and not
evidence of absence. The audit is repeated with execution at M0, when the incumbents are installed on host `trj`
and their strongest warm configurations are frozen (N00-14 carries the re-audit).

This bead carries the audit as data, so that the differentiator wordings (N00-15), the parity targets and the
ten-fold denominators can cite a row, and so that a row can be corrected when the M0 execution contradicts the
documentation. Every row is a FACT about its source; the third column is the consequence this project draws
(DESIGN).

A default rough-terrain humanoid run takes about 1 h on one RTX 4090 (derived from a published configuration and frame rate) | The training rung is a parity requirement. What is already solved elsewhere, and is consumed or matched rather than claimed: linear-time articulated-body
dynamics in spatial algebra and its Lie-group formulation; convex compliant contact that scales to thousands of
GPU worlds; Lie-group and variational integrators with discrete conservation laws; invariant filtering for
legged state estimation; morphological symmetry groups, equivariant policies and the symmetry classification of
gaits; monotone co-design with guaranteed fixed points, grammar-based robot search and trajectory optimization
with design variables; anytime-valid sequential inference; sampling-based predictive control of legged robots;
one policy across a class of bodies and policy sharing across candidates; certified collision-free regions and
support-region and wrench-cone feasibility as linear programs; exact family answers on small plants by standard
means (a lifted robust linear program, a projection, a satisfiability solver and quantifier elimination each
returned the same complete family answer as a specialized method on every fixture tried, FACT from the
plan-time pilots); adaptive stress testing, importance sampling for rare failures, delta debugging, conformal
prediction and topology optimization.

## Scope

In: the 21 rows as `[[incumbent_capability]]` records with source keys, pinned versions, `how_read`, the
consequence, and a `status` that the M0 execution updates. Out: executing incumbents (BD-039, BD-040, BD-142);
the documented-absence table and the not-found list (N00-14); the permitted wordings (N00-15).

## Technical approach

1. File `crates/frob-conformance/registry/incumbent_audit.toml`. Record fields: `id` (`IA-01` … `IA-21`),
   `capability`, `documented` (text), `figures` (typed list: value, unit, hardware, source key),
   `sources` (keys into `registries/sources.toml`), `versions`, `how_read` (`raw-text` | `figure-by-eye` |
   `derived`), `consequence`, `binds` (differentiator, bet or goal identifiers),
   `status` = `documented` | `reproduced-at-M0` | `contradicted-at-M0` | `not-executable`, `m0_record`.
2. The M0 re-audit (N00-14, with BD-039's harness) sets `status` and `m0_record` per row. A `contradicted` row
   triggers a review of every identifier in `binds`: the wording registry and parity targets are corrected in
   the same change, through the append-only rule for claims (N00-15).
3. Consistency test: every `figure` cited by a differentiator wording, a parity gate threshold or the Amdahl
   ledger (N00-18) resolves to exactly one record here; a figure read `figure-by-eye` carries its stated
   uncertainty (± 20 s for the 410 s row).
4. The list of "already solved elsewhere" is stored as `[[prior_art]]` records (topic, source keys) and is read
   by the claims lint: a document that presents one of these topics as new fails.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 21 capability records and the prior-art list load and every source key resolves.
- [ ] Each record names what it binds; N1, N3, N4, N5, N9, N10, N11, N12 and BET-06, BET-09, BET-15, BET-27
      each appear in at least one `binds`.
- [ ] After the M0 execution every record has a status other than `documented`, or `not-executable` with the
      reason (for example, hardware not available: the RTX 6000 Ada and RTX 5090 rows are re-measured on the RTX
      4090 of `trj` and recorded as such).
- [ ] The consistency test fails if a parity threshold cites a figure with no record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/incumbent_audit.rs`: `twenty_one_rows`; `sources_resolve`;
`figure_by_eye_has_uncertainty`; `mixed_batch_ratio_is_0_86` (the record stores 86 k samples/s and the ratio
0.86 to the harmonic mean of the homogeneous rates); `codesign_saving_67_4_percent_is_3_07x`
(1/(1 − 0.674) = 3.07, printed "about 3.1×"); `contradicted_row_lists_bound_identifiers`;
`prior_art_topic_presented_as_new_is_flagged`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/incumbent_audit.sh` loads the register, prints the table with statuses, and (once BD-039 exists)
joins each row to its M0 execution record. Logged per STD-E2E: row id, status, figures with units and source
keys, the M0 record reference; `summary` with counts by status and gate GATE-TENFOLD-01.

### Known answers and negative controls
Arithmetic pins: 1/(1 − 0.674) ≈ 3.07; 16 candidates per round at about 1 h on eight GPUs gives 30 GPU-minutes
per candidate (8 × 60 ÷ 16), the top of the 7.5–30 range. Negative controls: a differentiator wording citing an
unregistered figure; a row whose status was set to `reproduced-at-M0` with no execution record.

## Considerations

- Versions move quickly; a row is a statement about a pin. When an incumbent releases a new version the row is
  re-read and a new record appended, never edited in place.
- "Not found" is never upgraded to "does not exist" by this register.
- The register is read by the claims lint, the parity gates and the M0 re-audit.
