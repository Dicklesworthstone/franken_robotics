## Background

FrankenRobotics is built on a suite of sibling projects owned by the same owner. The design's architecture
follows from specific facts about what those siblings contain in code today, as opposed to in their plans. Each
fact was observed on 2026-11-21 (FrankenSim at commit `483d09c682f2`) and is cited with a path. Sibling code
moves daily, so a fact with a path is only as good as its last reading. This bead keeps the facts as data and
re-reads them mechanically at the commits pinned in the stack lock, so that a moved file, a changed constant or
a fixed defect is noticed when the pin moves and not when a design assumption quietly fails.

**FACT: FrankenSim already contains the hard referee-grade pieces.** SE(3) and SO(3) with exponential,
logarithm, adjoint, coadjoint and Jacobians (`frankensim/crates/fs-ga/src/lie.rs`); Featherstone forward and
inverse dynamics for floating-base trees (`fs-mbd/src/articulated.rs`); a 29-degree-of-freedom Unitree G1 model
with provenance (`fs-mbd/src/robot_models.rs`); a semismooth-Newton Signorini–Coulomb contact solve and
certified continuous collision detection (`fs-contact`); a discrete Euler–Poincaré step on SE(3) with its
adjoint (`fs-time`); CMA-ES, Bayesian optimization and MAP-Elites (`fs-dfo`, `fs-bo`, `fs-archive`); max-plus
algebra (`fs-tropical`); anytime-valid racing and e-processes (`fs-race`, `fs-eproc`); units, intervals,
evidence and a ledger.

**FACT: it also contains pieces an earlier draft did not use.** Multilevel Monte Carlo, control variates,
Gaussian importance sampling and Shapley effects (`fs-uq`); value-of-information ranking (`fs-voi`);
sum-of-squares programs and interval verification (`fs-sos`); dimensional-analysis regimes (`fs-regime`); an
out-of-process package checker (`fs-checker`); Floquet analysis of a supplied monodromy
(`fs-spectral/src/floquet.rs`); topology optimization and tolerance allocation (`fs-topopt`, `fs-toleralloc`);
rods and fatigue (`fs-solid`, with the rainflow counter in `fs-material`); port-Hamiltonian interconnection
(`fs-phs`); film and tribology models (`fs-tribo`, `fs-lbm`). No splitting or subset-simulation code exists
there.

**FACT: FrankenSim does not contain what a gym needs.** No batched environments, no GPU path in its workspace,
no terrain or interior scene representation for robots, no robot sensors, no MCP server. Its articulated contact
step refuses more than 21 contacts (`fs-contact/src/sustained/solve.rs`: `MAX_CONTACTS = 21`) and it has no
complete persistent contact time stepper (`fs-contact/CONTRACT.md`). Its accelerator doctrine says "FrankenSim
does not ship an accelerator backend" and "The CPU reference is permanent"
(`frankensim/docs/ACCELERATOR_DOCTRINE.md`).

**FACT: the suite has proven the GPU pattern outside FrankenSim's workspace.** One kernel source compiled for
CUDA through `cudarc` and NVRTC and for Metal through a prelude of macros, checked against a CPU emulation
(`frankensim/apps/instrument-lab/render-gpu-piano/src/{kernel.h,cuda.rs,metal.rs,emulate.rs}`), and a generic
Metal compute gateway whose documentation names "a physics integrator" as an intended consumer
(`frankentorch/crates/ft-kernel-metal/src/compute.rs`); the gateway accepts at most 31 buffer arguments
(`MAX_ARGUMENTS = 31`).

**FACT: the MCP substrate exists.** `fastmcp_rust` is a cancel-aware MCP framework built on asupersync contexts
and checkpoints, with tool, resource and prompt surfaces (`fastmcp_rust/crates/fastmcp/src/lib.rs`). Its own
documentation says support for the 2026-07-28 protocol revision is under implementation and not yet claimed, so
`frob-mcp` is admitted through INT-FMCP-01, and KILL-MCP names its fallbacks, the last of which is the CLI alone
(this is the row's `consequence`).

**FACT: the limits of the siblings are known and shape the design.** FrankenTorch tensors are host memory and
its device tags are metadata, so the learner runs in this project's own device kernels. `fs-qty` needs the
nightly feature `generic_const_exprs` (`frankensim/crates/fs-qty/src/lib.rs`), and most FrankenSim physics
crates depend on it, which ties the toolchain to nightlies on which that feature compiles: a named risk tested at
INT-TOOLCHAIN-01, with a small stable `frob-units` crate as the documented escape hatch. The siblings are on
four different pinned nightlies (`2026-07-06`, `2026-08-20`, `2026-08-25`, `2026-08-31`): the starting state,
from which one toolchain T is chosen and the others are moved to it in their own repositories. FrankenLean's
tactic crate (`franken_lean/crates/fln-anvil`, 1,423 lines) implements a discrimination tree and the `omega`
procedure, and its other tactics are stubs; FrankenSymPy's real-root machinery is capped (8 variables, degree
256, 4,096 bits, zero-dimensional ideals) and it has no homotopy solver. Proof routes and synthesis lanes are
sized to those caps.

**FACT (to recheck): five reported defects or limits**, reported by review and not yet reproduced by this
program at a pinned commit; each becomes an admission test and an upstream work item before anything depends on
the component:

1. Three errors in `fs_g1_train::ppo_update` (`frankensim/crates/fs-g1-train/src/ppo.rs`): for a negative
   advantage the clipped surrogate is applied on the wrong side of the ratio; the critic gradient into the
   shared bias is zero where the squared-error objective gives a non-zero value; concatenating two independently
   reset one-step episodes moves a frozen policy's second ratio from 1 to 1.582459.
2. A wrong joint law in the action sampler of the same file (`fs_g1_train::gaussian_action`): each marginal is
   correct, and adjacent channels agree in sign with probability 0.6655 where independence gives one half.
3. A near-straight integration defect in `fs-mbd`'s planar drive
   (`frankensim/crates/fs-mbd/src/planar_drive.rs`): at a heading of 32 rad with wheel speeds that differ by
   6 × 10⁻¹³ m/s the routine returns zero translation where the true one-second displacement is about
   (0.834, 0.551) m. The row's `consequence`: the routine is qualified against an independent SE(2)
   exponential before it is used as an oracle.
4. A stride-convention trap between `franken_numpy` and `frankentorch`: an unconverted stride reads 12 where 3
   is correct.
5. A fixed-witness assumption in FrankenSim's sustained articulated contact path: it assumes fixed material
   witnesses and a fixed frame, and rolling around a sharp lip changes both; the referee's transactional step
   therefore migrates witnesses.

**One convention hazard, confirmed by reading:** `fs-mbd::Wrench` holds a world-frame force and a body-frame
torque in one value, unlike this project's convention, and crosses the airlock only through a typed conversion.

**Six further facts found later**, each with its own admission test: two cases in which the bound verifier of
`fs-sos` returns a value that is not a bound; welded masses omitted by the suite's G1 catalog builder; a series
limit that refuses the differential of the SE(3) exponential beyond 32 m of translation; a swept-sphere helper
that covers straight motion only; and two more convention hazards, the sign of the legacy transformer in
`fs-phs` and a ray direction renormalized under a scaled instance in `f3d-math`.

Consequence (DESIGN): FrankenRobotics is the missing product on top of a mostly present foundation. FrankenSim
is the referee and the bedrock, consumed by path behind one airlock crate and never forked. This project owns
the batched Lie-algebraic rollout engine, the scenes, the sensors, the learning loop, the mission layer, the
adversary, the record and the agent-facing studio.

## Scope

In: the foundation fact sheet as data, that is, one row for every checkable statement of the Background (about
forty), entered in the one registry of sibling facts under `sheet = "foundation"`; the links from the eleven
behaviour facts to their admission tests; the `consequence` of every row; the wrapper script
`tools/oracle/recheck_foundation_facts.sh`, which prints this sheet, and the example `sibling_facts` it calls;
the list of consequences to watch.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- the registry and its mechanism: the file `crates/frob-conformance/registry/sibling_facts.toml`, its schema
  `frob.sibling-facts/1`, the probe kinds, the runner `frob_conformance::sibling_facts::probe_all`, reading at
  the pin, the result vocabulary, the report `frob.sibling-facts.report/1`, the `stale-fact` marking and the
  disposition rule at a pin move: N33a-09, a dependency. This bead defines no registry file, no probe and no
  report of its own; it adds rows and a wrapper. The rows of the suite chapter are N33a-09's.
- the admission tests themselves (BD-032 to BD-036, BD-093 and the beads for the six later facts); the choice
  of toolchain (BD-031); upstream fixes.

## Technical approach

1. **Data.** Rows of `crates/frob-conformance/registry/sibling_facts.toml` (the one registry, N33a-09), one
   `[[fact]]` per checkable statement (about 40 records from the paragraphs above), each with
   `sheet = "foundation"`. In that schema: `id` (`SF-<REPO>-<nnn>`, append-only), `repo`, `path`, `kind`
   (the kinds this sheet uses are `file-exists`, `symbol-exists`, `constant`, `text-present`, `text-absent`,
   `line-count` and `behaviour`), `pattern` and `read` (the value as read, its date and revision: for example
   `MAX_CONTACTS` = 21; `MAX_ARGUMENTS` = 31; the phrase "does not ship an accelerator backend"; the feature
   gate `generic_const_exprs`; 1,423 lines for `fln-anvil`, with `tolerance = 0.10` on that row because the
   crate is expected to grow), `label` (`FACT` or `FACT-to-recheck`), `consequence` (the design decision that
   leans on it and the beads to notify), `cited_by`, and `admission` (the `INT-…` gate) for behaviour facts.
   A fact this sheet shares with the suite chapter's list (the contact capacity of 21, the nightly feature in
   the units crate) is one row, not two: the row already entered by N33a-09 gains this sheet's `consequence`
   text, and the sheet lists it by identifier.
2. **Recheck script** `tools/oracle/recheck_foundation_facts.sh`: a thin wrapper (plain shell) that calls the
   runner of N33a-09 with the sheet filter `foundation` against the commits of `franken-stack.lock` and prints
   the sheet as a human table. It contains no probe logic. It reaches the runner through the example target
   this bead declares, `examples/sibling_facts.rs` (DESIGN: BD-025 admits library code to a script only through
   a declared example, and the crate has no default binary): a thin argument parser over `probe_all`,
   `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example sibling_facts -- report [--sheet <name>] [--lock <path>]`, which
   prints the report as one JSON object on standard output and exits non-zero only on a load error (a `changed`
   or `moved` fact is information and exits 0). It serves every sheet; the wrapper passes `--sheet foundation
   --lock franken-stack.lock`.
   a. for the probed kinds the runner writes `unchanged` (a fact that holds), `moved` (the symbol is found
      elsewhere; the new path is recorded) or `changed` (with the new value), or `unreadable`;
   b. for `behaviour` facts (the five reported defects and the six later facts) nothing is judged: the row
      links to the admission test's latest record and the table prints `defect-present`, `defect-fixed` or
      `not-run`;
   c. the output is the runner's report (schema `frob.sibling-facts.report/1`) filtered to this sheet, written
      as `target/foundation_facts/<commit>.json`, and the human table.
   The wrapper never refuses because a sibling's checkout is off its pin: the runner reads the pinned commit
   through git (N33a-09's rule).
3. **Hook.** The compatibility train (moving a pin) runs the probe before and after; any `changed` or `moved`
   fact lists the beads whose `consequence` field names it, and the pin move is not complete until each has a
   disposition (design unaffected; design updated; fallback adopted). The rule is N33a-09's and applies to
   every sheet; what this bead supplies for it is a complete `consequence` on each of its rows.
4. **Notable consequences to watch.** `MAX_CONTACTS` rising above 21 changes the referee-coverage bet;
   `fs-qty` no longer needing the nightly feature removes the `frob-units` escape hatch; the Metal gateway's
   argument cap rising above 31 relaxes the packed-argument design; fastmcp_rust claiming the newer protocol
   revision changes the admission test's scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The fact sheet loads as rows of the one registry; every statement in the Background that names a path or
      a constant has a record; no file `foundation_facts.toml` and no second runner exist.
- [ ] At the commits pinned at M0 the script reports each record as `unchanged`, `moved`, `changed` or
      `unreadable`, and the eleven behaviour facts as linked to their admission records.
- [ ] `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example sibling_facts -- report --sheet foundation` prints one
      `frob.sibling-facts.report/1` object holding only `sheet = "foundation"` rows, and passes BD-025's
      `no_conformance_command` and `binaries_declared` checks.
- [ ] A planted change (a scratch repository whose pinned commit has `MAX_CONTACTS = 64`) is reported as
      `changed` with the two beads its row's `consequence` names: N33a-14 (the capacity field of the airlock's
      `RefereeContact`) and BET-34 (the referee-coverage bet).
- [ ] No design document cites a sibling fact without a path (label lint N00-01, rule for FACT).
- [ ] The report is attached to the M0 admission record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/foundation_facts.rs` (on synthetic sibling trees under `tests/fixtures/`), tests
of this sheet's rows: `foundation_rows_cover_the_sheet` (every path and constant named in the Background has a
row; about forty); `foundation_constant_change_names_its_beads` (21 → 64 on the capacity row lists exactly
N33a-14 and BET-34, the beads of its `consequence`); `foundation_line_count_within_ten_percent` (the `fln-anvil` row with its declared
tolerance); `eleven_behaviour_rows_link_admission_records`; `shared_facts_are_one_row` (the capacity and the
nightly-feature facts appear once in the registry). The tests of the probe kinds themselves
(`symbol_moved_is_reported_with_new_path`, `text_absent_fact_fails_when_text_appears` with the file
`subset_sim.rs`, `behaviour_fact_links_admission_record`, `every_fact_has_consequence`, the change of a
constant) are in N33a-09, where the kinds are implemented.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundation_facts.sh` runs the recheck script against the real sibling checkouts at the stack
lock's commits and against the planted scratch checkout. Logged per STD-E2E: fact id, sibling, commit, kind,
expected and observed values, result; `summary` with unchanged, moved, changed, unreadable and not-run counts.

### Known answers and negative controls
Expected at commit `483d09c682f2` of FrankenSim: `MAX_CONTACTS = 21`; in FrankenTorch: `MAX_ARGUMENTS = 31`.
Sibling-defect known answers KA-003 to KA-008 are checked by their admission beads. Negative controls: the
planted constant change; a fact record with an empty `consequence`; a recheck run while a sibling's checkout
is on another commit than the stack lock names (the report must describe the pinned commit, read through git,
and say so; it must not describe the checkout, and it does not refuse).

## Considerations

- The script reads; it never edits a sibling. Fixes are upstream work items in the sibling's own repository.
- Other agents work in the siblings concurrently; a fact may change between reading and use, which is why the
  commit is part of every record.
- Facts about what is absent are the weakest kind; they are checked by search and can only ever be reported as
  "not found at this commit".
