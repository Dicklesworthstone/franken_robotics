## Background

No single mechanism delivers a ten-fold advance, and no conjunction has been shown to. The candidate thesis is a
conjunction of eight levers, each attacking a different row of the design-loop ledger. Seven of the eight are
methods a composed rival can also host. The levers multiply only where they act on different time: levers 1 and
2 act on the two largest rows and carry most of the thesis; levers 3, 4 and 7 reduce the number of episodes and
of agent turns; lever 5 changes what an answer is; levers 6 and 8 are hygiene that keeps the others from being
eaten by overhead. Every "surviving number" is a HYPOTHESIS about this system; published figures behind it are
FACTs about their sources.

Against the strongest composed rival, which may host every portable lever, the expected ratio on fixed-topology
CUDA missions is near one unless an engine-internal property binds, and no range is offered. On quadruped
missions the range tops out at ten, and reaching the top needs every lever to work at once, including the two
least measured (family policies and rank agreement). The denominator is an accepted decision and not a training
run: a 1% failure bound at 95% confidence costs 299 failure-free episodes per accepted design in every arm. If,
on any stratum, a short robust program over an incumbent engine reaches the same accepted answers in comparable
time, the report says so, the mechanisms ship as a layer over that engine for that stratum, and engine work
continues only for the gaps internal to an engine.

removable-share bound p_max per lever and for a set of levers (the union of their phases, each counted once),

## Technical approach

1. **File.** `crates/frob-conformance/registry/levers.toml`: `[[lever]]` (`n`, `name`, `mechanism`,
   `ledger_phases` = the rows of the Amdahl ledger (N00-18) it attacks, `tenfold_phases` = the phases of the
   ten-fold phase ledger it may be said to act on (below), `surviving` text and typed ranges,
   `tier`, `gates`, `hypotheses` = rows of the hypothesis register, `portable` = whether a composed rival can
   host it (true for seven; false only where an engine-internal property is required, which is lever 1, DESIGN:
   its mixed-topology batch with run-to-run replay is one of the two engine-internal gaps H-19 documents, and
   arm W of the ten-fold test hosts only its portable form, per-topology buckets or the engine's own
   heterogeneous solver without replay (N39b-43));
   `[[expected_range]]` (`class`, `denominator`, `ceiling`, `low`, `high`, `confidence`, `conditions`,
   `registered` date and ledger reference, `scored` records).
   `tenfold_phases` (DESIGN; the one statement of which phases each lever acts on is N39a-26's
   `lever_phases.toml`, `[[lever]]` with `n`, `name`, `tenfold_phases`; this register's field is a reference
   to that file's record for the same `n`, resolved at load time, and a load whose resolved set differs from
   the set printed below, or that names a lever missing from that file, fails): lever 1: `propose` (through
   the number of turns), `screen`; lever 2: `train`; lever 3: `screen`, `evaluate`; lever 4: `diagnose`; lever 5: `certify`,
   `propose`; lever 6: `check`, `compile`, `evaluate`; lever 7: `diagnose`; lever 8: `compile`, `author`. The
   names are those of the thirteen-phase ledger of the ten-fold test (N39a-24). Levers share phases (`propose`:
   1 and 5; `screen`: 1 and 3; `evaluate`: 3 and 6; `compile`: 6 and 8; `diagnose`: 4 and 7), which is why
   no figure is ever obtained by adding over levers. The mapping bounds what may be claimed for a lever; a
   mechanism can act outside its listed phases, and the saving table shows what happened.
2. **Single counting.** Every measured gain reported in a campaign report or in the ten-fold phase ledger names
   exactly one lever and one ledger phase. The check sums attributed time removed per phase and fails if it
   exceeds the phase's measured duration, if the same avoided training appears under lever 2 and lever 6, or if
   two levers' factors are multiplied where they act on the same time (the `compose` order rule of N00-17).
   For a cell of the ten-fold test the "measured duration" of a phase is taken from the saving table of
   N39a-26 (the seconds the rival spent in the phase, and the seconds saved between the arms): the sum of the
   gains booked to a phase may not exceed saved(phase), and a gain booked to a lever in a phase outside that
   lever's `tenfold_phases` is reported for review. Three single-counting rules exist in the program and each
   has one home: the booking of a measured gain to exactly one lever and one phase, here; the bound on a
   registered removable share and on a set of levers, in N39a-26; the order rule for a product of lever
   effects, in N39a-38.
3. **Pre-registration.** At M1 exit the six expected ranges are written to the ledger as registered predictions
   (a content-addressed record with date), with the measured M0 and M1 denominators substituted where they
   exist. They cannot be edited afterwards; a revision is a new record that cites the measurement prompting it.
4. **Scoring.** After the partial gate (legged and wheeled briefs) and after the tournament, each class is
   scored: measured pooled ratio and interval against per-design training and against the composed rival;
   `inside`, `below` or `above` the registered range; the conditions column re-read against what was measured
   (trained fraction, designs per turn, rank agreement). The score is printed in the report whichever way it
   falls. The measured inputs are read, not recomputed: the pooled ratio and interval from the gate's scorer,
   and the per-phase saving table and lever view of the cell from N39a-26, which are printed beside the score
   so that a ratio inside its range can be seen to come from the phases the levers were said to act on.
5. **The 299-episode constant.** `fn failure_free_episodes(p, confidence)` = ⌈ln(1 − confidence) ÷ ln(1 − p)⌉:
   at p = 0.01 and 95% it returns 299; the cost is charged to every arm's accepted decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Eight lever records and six expected-range records load; every lever names its ledger phases, hypotheses
      and gates, and every gate exists in the registry.
- [ ] The single-counting check rejects a report that books the same avoided training under levers 2 and 6, and
      one whose attributed savings exceed a phase's measured time.
- [ ] The ranges are registered before any M2 work package is funded as a speed lever, and the registration
      record's date precedes the first partial-gate run.
- [ ] After M3 the legged and wheeled classes carry a score; after M7 every class run carries one; classes not
      run say `not run` with the reason.
- [ ] No document states an expected range as a result (wording lint: these are HYPOTHESIS).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/levers.rs`: `eight_levers_six_ranges`; `double_counted_training_rejected`;
`savings_exceeding_phase_rejected` (phase 300 s, attributed 340 s); `seven_levers_portable` (lever 1 alone has `portable = false`);
`tenfold_phases_of_eight_levers` (the eight phase sets above, resolved from `lever_phases.toml` and equal to
it set for set; every name is one of the thirteen phases; a scratch copy of that file in which lever 2 also
lists `screen` makes the load fail with the lever and the differing phase named);
`gain_outside_lever_phases_reported` (a gain booked to lever 2 in `compile`);
`failure_free_episodes_299` (p = 1/100, 95% → 299; p = 10⁻⁴, 95% → 29,956); `range_edit_after_registration_rejected`;
`score_inside_below_above` (registered 3–10: measured 2.4 → `below`; 6.1 → `inside`; 12 → `above`).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/lever_register.sh` loads the register, registers the ranges in a scratch ledger, replays a
synthetic partial-gate record (labelled `synthetic`) and prints the scores and the attribution check. Logged
per STD-E2E: lever, phase, attributed and available microseconds, class, registered range, measured ratio and
interval, score; `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-012 (turn cap), KA-013 (compute per decision), KA-016 (paired gain); ⌈ln 0.05 ÷ ln 0.99⌉ = 299. CX-005,
CX-006, CX-007. Negative controls: the two planted reports; a range edited after registration.

## Considerations

- The ranges are deliberately not forecasts with probabilities; scoring them is for calibration of the planning
  method, not for a claim.
- Lever 8's value depends on how often the rival recompiles; that is measured in the incumbent ledger.
- A lever that a composed rival hosts as well yields no ratio against that rival; the recovery ratio is what
  shows this, and the plan's response is to ship the layer.
