## Background

The specification behind these beads went through thirteen internal review rounds (recorded in N00-05), and two
limits of that record are stated openly: the external review rounds that the planning method calls for have not
been run on the current version, and parts of the internal record were produced inside one family of reviewers
(the idea duel's synthesis was compiled by an agent of the same model family as one duellist; the first
hypothesis verdict register was adjudicated from evidence gathered by investigators of one model family, and
only a challenge round by others corrected it, changing sixteen rows). The owner's stated next step, before
implementation scope is opened, is external review. This bead is that review.

"External" means: the reviewer did not write the text under review, starts in a fresh context with only the
material under review and the questions, and, where the review is of a judgement rather than of arithmetic,
comes from a different model family or is a human. The earlier rounds show why this matters: round 9 found two
critical mathematical errors, one of them in the plan's own negative control; round 10 found nine places where
two binding texts could not both be implemented.

## Scope

In: at least three review rounds with distinct charters, their records appended to the review log, and a
disposition for every finding. Out: the audit of the eighteen unaudited claims (N00-31) and the review of the
sixty-six late subsections (N00-32), which are separate rounds with their own beads; applying accepted findings
to beads (done through the tracker by the lead, one change per finding).

## Technical approach

1. **Round A: adversarial reading of the thesis** (what could make the ten-fold claim wrong or unfalsifiable).
   Material: the root epic, the bet beads BET-13, BET-14, BET-17, the thesis arithmetic (N00-17), the Amdahl
   ledger (N00-18), the lever register (N00-20), the metric contract (N00-19). Questions: is each falsifier
   reachable; is any ceiling miscomputed; does any bet lack a competing arm; could the decision rule confirm a
   false ten-fold or miss a true one; which lever is double-counted.
2. **Round B: mathematical review** of the bead texts that carry formulas (specification beads of the
   constructions, theorem cards, known-answer registries). Questions: is every symbol defined; does every
   printed value follow from its formula (recompute each); does every known answer have a control that can
   fail; are conventions (frames, signs, units, trivializations) consistent across beads.
3. **Round C: implementability review**: a fresh reviewer picks twenty beads at random, stratified by milestone
   and unit, and for each answers: could I implement this without the source document; what is undefined; what
   contradicts a dependency; is the test plan executable as written; is any acceptance criterion uncheckable.
4. **Round D (optional, owner's choice): re-adjudication** of the hypothesis verdict register by reviewers of a
   different family from the original investigators, limited to the eleven ACTIVE rows and the four REFUTED
   rows, using the change rule of N00-10.
5. **Record per round** (appended to `docs/provenance/review_log.toml` as rounds 14 onward): what was examined
   (bead keys and digests), by whom (role and independence statement, not a name), the charter questions,
   findings as a list of records `{id, severity: critical | major | minor, location: bead key and section,
   statement, evidence or recomputation, proposed replacement}`, counts by severity.
6. **Disposition.** Every finding gets one of `applied` (with the tracker change reference), `rejected` (with
   the reason and, for a mathematical finding, the counter-derivation), `deferred` (with the bead that carries
   it and its milestone). No finding is left without one. A critical finding blocks the change of scope to
   implementation until it is `applied` or `rejected` with the owner's sign-off.
7. **Stop rule.** A round is repeated on the changed text if it produced a critical finding; review stops when
   one full round produces no critical and at most three major findings (DESIGN; the owner may tighten it).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Rounds A, B and C are recorded in the review log with their material digests, charters and findings.
- [ ] Every finding has a disposition; zero critical findings are open.
- [ ] Each round's independence statement says who wrote the reviewed text and that the reviewer did not.
- [ ] The two same-family limits of the earlier record are either addressed by round D or restated as standing
      in the log with the owner's decision.
- [ ] The final round met the stop rule, or the owner recorded a decision to proceed with named open findings.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/review_log.rs` (shared with N00-05): `round_has_material_digests`;
`finding_without_disposition_fails`; `critical_finding_open_blocks` (a synthetic log with one open critical
finding makes `review_complete()` false); `applied_finding_needs_change_reference`;
`independence_statement_present`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/review_rounds.sh` loads the log, recomputes the digests of the reviewed bead files at the recorded
commit, and prints per round the counts by severity and by disposition. Logged per STD-E2E: round, material
count, findings by severity, open findings; `summary` with rounds recorded and critical findings open.

### Known answers and negative controls
Round B is itself a recomputation of known answers: every value it recomputes is compared with the registry
(for this unit's rows: KA-001 to KA-032). Negative control of the process: before round B starts, the lead
plants three known errors in a scratch copy of the material (a wrong constant, an undefined symbol, a
contradicted dependency); a round that does not report at least two of the three is repeated with a different
reviewer.

## Considerations

- Review cost is bounded by sampling (round C) and by restricting round D to the rows that matter most.
- Findings are evidence, not authority: a finding is applied because its recomputation or citation holds, not
  because of who made it.
- The repository is at the planning stage; this bead is executable now, without any change of scope.
