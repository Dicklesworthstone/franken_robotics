## Background

One of the planning exercises produced 94 atomic claims about where design time goes, what incumbents do, which
mechanisms transfer from other fields, what the hardware can hold, and what to reject. Two independent auditors
re-read primary sources and redid arithmetic for 76 of them (31 confirmed, 39 partially supported with corrected
statements, 2 hypothesis-grade, 2 rejected, 2 non-material). **Eighteen were never reached.** Some of those
eighteen nonetheless shaped the design (a storage constraint on scene fields, the diagonal scaling of the
single-precision contact solve, several rejections), so each must be audited before a gate or a bead leans on
it. The claim records are in the repository at `planning/fable/mor/MOR_CLAIMS_AUDITED.jsonl` (field
`audit_coverage = "NOT_AUDITED"`); this bead carries their statements so that it stands alone.

The eighteen claims, the check each needs, and what would refute it:

| C046 | Add adaptive splitting over worlds through snapshot and restore, ranked by the smallest telemetry margin (friction utilization, torque headroom, support margin), to estimate finalist fall rates and harvest failing worlds | Check whether per-world randomness can be redrawn after a branch; re-read the two cited papers for their gain conditions | Branches are identical copies under the randomness model; or finalist failure probabilities are not small enough for splitting to gain |

## Scope

In: an audit record per claim, corrected statements where needed, and the list of beads each outcome touches.
Out: the experiments some refutation conditions name (rank agreement of the hard set, the ten-body test, the
monotonicity sweep); for those the audit records the claim as `hypothesis-grade` and names the bead that runs
the experiment.

## Technical approach

1. **Method per claim** (the method of the first 76): re-open every cited source at a pinned version and record
   how it was read; redo every arithmetic step in exact or double-precision arithmetic with the script kept
   under `tools/oracle/audit_claims/`; inspect sibling code where the claim cites it; then assign one of
   `confirmed`, `partially-supported` (with the corrected statement), `hypothesis-grade` (needs an experiment;
   name it), `rejected`, `non-material`.
2. **Arithmetic to redo** (expected values, to be confirmed): C053: 2 bytes × 300 m³ ÷ (0.02 m)³ = 75 MB;
   × 8,192 = 614 GB. C055: 20 m ÷ 65,536 = 0.305 mm; × 10⁵ N/m = 30.5 N. C089: 1 ÷ (0.8 + 0.2 ÷ 1.5) = 1.071.
   C054: warehouse of 60 m × 40 m × 8 m at 2 cm dense, 2 bytes per sample = 4.8 GB; at 1 cm = 38.4 GB. C060:
   κ₂ × u with single-precision unit round-off u = 2⁻²⁴ ≈ 6 × 10⁻⁸: 2–3 × 10⁶ → 0.12–0.18 raw; 2–5 × 10⁵ →
   0.012–0.03 scaled (the claim printed 0.14–0.19 raw; the difference is recorded, not silently adopted). C045:
   4,096 worlds × (10 s ÷ 2 ms) = 2.048 × 10⁷ world-steps per full battery. C040: the coherence bound is the
   composite quadruped iteration of the Amdahl ledger (N00-18): deleting training and compilation outright gives
   354 s ÷ 54 s ≈ 6.6×, so a forecast of at least 10× from inheritance on quadruped-class briefs needs a second
   lever acting on the remaining 54 s.
   C061 is not recomputed here: the conditioning check is run once, by N38a-11 (row 20 of the plan-time pilot
   ledger, script `tools/oracle/rerun/contact_conditioning.py`), whose result carries `within_factor_10`, the
   refutation condition of C061. If that result is on file when this audit closes, the C061 record cites it by
   hash and takes its outcome from it; if not, C061 is recorded `hypothesis-grade` naming N38a-11, like the
   other claims whose check is an experiment, so that this audit does not wait on a dynamics run.
3. **Binding.** For each claim list the beads and registry rows that lean on it, and act on the outcome:
   C045 → the hard-world set; C046 → the exploration lane of rare-failure estimation; C047 → labelled
   derivatives through events; C049 → the hypothesis row on exact reuse (already REFUTED as a lever; the audit
   confirms or reopens it by the change rule); C050 → the sampling-controller hypothesis row; C053, C054, C055 →
   the scene-pack format, the brick pool and the world bank; C060, C061 → diagonal scaling, the conditioning
   certificate and the half-precision refutation; C087 → the anti-goal on learned surrogates; C089 → the
   refutation of recursion-only specialization; C090 → any corner-based evaluation; C035, C040, C094 → the
   funding checklist and the lever register.
4. **Record.** Append the audited records to a new file `planning/fable/mor/MOR_CLAIMS_AUDIT_ROUND_2.jsonl`
   (the first-round file is not edited), and append a round to the review log with the counts by outcome.
5. **Consequences.** A `rejected` or `partially-supported` outcome is carried into the bound beads in the same
   change set, through their staging files (`planning/fable/beads/defs/<unit>/<key>.md`, synced to the tracker by
   `planning/fable/beads/tools/sync_beads.py`; an edit made with `br` alone is overwritten by the next sync), and
   into the hypothesis register through its append-only history.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Eighteen audit records exist, each with sources reopened, arithmetic redone, an outcome and, where
      corrected, the corrected statement.
- [ ] Every outcome other than `confirmed` or `non-material` has a list of affected beads and a change
      reference for each.
- [ ] Zero claims remain with coverage `NOT_AUDITED`; claims that need an experiment are `hypothesis-grade` with
      the experiment's bead named.
- [ ] The review log has a new round with the counts by outcome (they sum to 18).
- [ ] The audit scripts reproduce every recomputed number from a clean checkout.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`tools/oracle/audit_claims/test_arithmetic.py` (run by `scripts/e2e/claim_audit.sh`): `c053_scene_bytes`
(75 MB; 614 GB); `c055_resolution_and_force` (0.305 mm; 30.5 N); `c089_overall_gain` (1.071);
`c054_warehouse_bytes` (4.8 GB; 38.4 GB); `c060_kappa_times_u` (ranges above); `c045_world_steps`
(2.048 × 10⁷); `c040_quadruped_cap` (354 ÷ 54 = 6.56); `c061_reads_conditioning_result` (the C061 record cites
N38a-11's result by hash and copies its `within_factor_10`, or is `hypothesis-grade` naming N38a-11 when no
result is on file); `counts_sum_to_18`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/claim_audit.sh` runs the arithmetic scripts, validates the round-2 file against the claim schema,
checks that the first-round file is byte-identical to its committed digest, and prints the outcome table.
Logged per STD-E2E: claim id, outcome, each recomputed value with its expected value, sources reopened;
`summary` with counts by outcome.

### Known answers and negative controls
The expected values of step 2. Negative controls: an audit record with an outcome and no reopened source or
arithmetic (rejected by the schema check); a record that edits the first-round file; a planted wrong
recomputation (C053 computed with 1 byte per sample → 37.5 MB) that the arithmetic test must catch.

## Considerations

- Several of these claims are rejections; auditing a rejection means looking for the counterexample that would
  make the rejected idea worth reopening.
- C061's recomputation needs the G1 description and a contact-row model; it is the conditioning check of the
  plan-time pilots, run once by N38a-11, whose result this audit and N00-35 both cite by hash.
- No product code is needed: the audit can run from the first day of implementation, before the beads that
  lean on these claims are built.
