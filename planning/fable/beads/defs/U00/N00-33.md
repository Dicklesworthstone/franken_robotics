## Background

A known answer that nothing can fail validates nothing. Across the whole known-answer suite 325 rows state no
negative control and 133 state no tolerance, or only part of one, for a real-valued truth; such rows are
obligations, not tests, and may not be counted toward a gate (the count of uncounted rows must be zero by M3
for rows due by M3). Each owning unit closes its own. Among the 32 rows of document control, the audit, the
thesis, the bets, the North-Star scenarios and the claim product, fourteen state no control (seven thesis
ceilings and seven North-Star fixture rows) and four state no tolerance. This bead supplies the seven thesis
controls and the four tolerances, at M0, where the calculators they mutate (N00-17) exist. The seven North-Star
fixture controls mutate the stance classifier, the docking solver, the late-packet witness, the amendment
handler and the offline auditor's mission proof kinds, all of which arrive at M1, and the gates they register
against (GATE-AUGUR-02, GATE-AUGUR-03, GATE-LEMMA-03, GATE-LEMMA-04) are M1 gates; they are carried by N00p1-13
(split from this bead so that no M0 bead waits on M1 code).

Rule for a control (DESIGN): it is a defect, not a boundary. "The request is refused at the limit" tests the
limit; the control is the build or input in which the limit was removed or the formula was wrong. Each control
below is a mutation of the implementation or a planted input that makes the row's own test fail.

## Scope

In: seven control records (KA-009 to KA-013, KA-015, KA-016), four tolerance decisions (KA-001, KA-003, KA-004,
KA-006), and the test run that shows each control caught. These seven controls are the registered controls of
their rows: the ones the registry counts.

Out: the seven North-Star fixture controls (KA-019 to KA-021, KA-023 to KA-026), with their recorded runs:
N00p1-13 at M1, which follows this bead's record format and test-naming convention.

Out, with the bead that has it: the rule every control must satisfy (a defect and not a boundary; one of the five
kinds `mutation`, `planted-input`, `planted-design`, `planted-world`, `a-a`; a stated wrong value outside the
row's tolerance and not on the safe side; a named gate), its enforcement by the control loader and the meta-test
that counts rows without a control are the decision on OPEN-GAUNT-3, N38b-28; this bead depends on it and every
record here is written to pass that rule. N38b-28 also printed proposals for the same fourteen rows: six are the
same defect as the control here or in N00p1-13 (KA-010, 012, 016 here; KA-020, 024, 025 there) and eight
differ; they are alternates that the owner of each bead may accept as second controls, and they replace
nothing. Tolerances set by rule for
other rows (N38b-27). Controls for rows that already state one (they are registered by N00-KA and N00-CX); the
other units' rows.

## Technical approach

**Controls for the seven thesis rows** (mutants of the calculators of N00-17; kind `mutation`; each a
`cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch at its calculator in `crates/frob-conformance/src/thesis/calc.rs`, the
feature declared in `frob-conformance`'s manifest and named in the record's `feature` field, the identifier the
next one of the control registry's append-only sequence at entry; each registered against GATE-TENFOLD-09 and
GATE-GAUNT-02; run by the control runner of N38b-04, one control per build, as
`FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features
gauntlet-mutations,frob-conformance/nc-<nnnn> -- KA-0nn`, beside the clean
`FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- KA-0nn`):

| Row | Truth | Control (the mutant) | Why it is caught |
|---|---|---|---|
| KA-009 | R ≤ 1/q; 25/3 at q = 12% | A `restricted_mean` (N00-17) that drops unresolved missions from both arms before the ratio of the two arms is taken | On the fixture (candidate resolves 22 of 25 at 1/10 of the rival's time, rival resolves all) it reports 10, above the cap 25/3 |
| KA-010 | 1/(1 − s); 100/13 at s = 0.87 | A calculator that returns 1/s | Returns 1.149 at s = 0.87, not 7.69 |
| KA-011 | 3.0–3.5× and about 15× | A cycle ratio that omits the agent turn from the incumbent cycle (uses 300 s for both ends) | Returns 2.5 at both ends, not 3.0 and 3.5 |
| KA-012 | (T_tr + t)/t; 6 and 3.5 at 300 s | A turn cap that omits t in the numerator (T_tr/t) | Returns 5 and 2.5 |
| KA-013 | C ÷ (c + c_T·f); 205/19 at f = 0; f ≤ 3/410 for ten | A ratio that ignores the cheap rung (C ÷ (c_T·f)) | Unbounded at f = 0 (division by zero must be a refusal, and the mutant instead reports a huge number); reports f ≤ 10% for ten |
| KA-015 | 1/(1 − p); 25/21 at p = 16% | A pruning cap that returns 1 + p | Returns 1.16; the exact comparison with 25/21 fails |
| KA-016 | 1/(1 − ρ²) | A gain that uses 1/(1 − ρ) | Returns 3.33, 5.56 and 20 at ρ = 0.7, 0.82, 0.95, not 1.96, 3.05 and 10.26 |

**Controls for the seven North-Star fixture rows** (KA-019 to KA-021, KA-023 to KA-026): N00p1-13, at M1. Until
it closes, the registry printout of this bead's e2e script lists exactly those seven rows as owed, with the
bead that owes them and their first milestone M1, and GATE-GAUNT-02 does not count them (N00-KA's rule).

**Tolerances for the four rows printed `to set`** (DESIGN decisions, each with its reason):

| Row | Truth | Tolerance set | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| KA-001 | Ceiling 842.8 ÷ 575.7 | Truth the exact rational 8428/5757, compared exactly; tolerance 0.005 on the printed "about 1.46" (half a unit of its last digit, rule 2 of N38b-27), the same settlement as REC-01 of the reconciliation register (N38b-50), which this record and that one must agree on | The inputs are recorded to 0.1 µs, so the quotient is an exact rational of the record; the printed figure is a rounding of it |
| KA-003 | Displacement about (0.834, 0.551) m | Absolute 1 × 10⁻⁹ m per component against the independent SE(2) exponential evaluated in double precision at the same inputs; the printed three-digit pair is a sanity bound of ± 5 × 10⁻⁴ m | Both sides are double-precision evaluations of the same closed form; the chord formula near a straight path is well conditioned once the series is used |
| KA-004 | Second probability ratio stays 1 | Exact 1.0 in the tensor's precision (bitwise) | A frozen policy evaluated on identical observations must reproduce its own log-probability; any deviation is the defect |
| KA-006 | Critic gradient into the shared bias equals the gradient of the squared-error objective | Relative 1 × 10⁻¹² in double precision against a central finite difference with step 10⁻⁶, and non-zero | The reported defect is an exactly zero gradient; a finite-difference oracle separates zero from the true value by many orders |

Each control becomes a `[[control]]` record (`id`, `seed` = the KA number, `gate`, `kind`, `target`, `defect`,
`expect`, `feature`) and each tolerance a field update of its known-answer row with a dated note. A control is accepted
only after a recorded run in which the mutant build or planted input made the row's test fail and the clean
build passed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seven control records exist (KA-009 to KA-013, KA-015, KA-016), each with a recorded run showing
      `caught = true` and a clean run showing a pass.
- [ ] The four tolerances are written to the registry with their reasons; no row of KA-001 to KA-016 remains
      `to set` or `none stated`.
- [ ] The registry's obligation counters for this unit's rows read exactly seven, all of them the North-Star
      fixture rows owed by N00p1-13 at M1 (zero once it closes), so that every row of KA-001 to KA-016 can be
      counted by GATE-GAUNT-02 when its test passes.
- [ ] Each mutant is an `nc-<nnnn>` feature branch of `frob-conformance`, never in the `gauntlet-mutations`
      umbrella; a build with it and without the umbrella, or under the release profile, is a compile error
      (N38b-04's guard), and `cargo tree -e features` shows no `nc-*` feature in any product binary.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/ka_controls_part0_8.rs`, one test per control:
`control_ka009_dropped_censoring_is_caught`, `control_ka010_reciprocal_share_is_caught`,
`control_ka011_turn_omitted_is_caught`, `control_ka012_numerator_without_t_is_caught`,
`control_ka013_cheap_rung_ignored_is_caught` (also: f = 0 on the clean build is a refusal, not a huge number),
`control_ka015_one_plus_p_is_caught`, `control_ka016_linear_rho_is_caught`; each calls the control runner
(N38b-04) on its record, which rebuilds the tier target with `--features
gauntlet-mutations,frob-conformance/nc-<nnnn>`, runs the row's own assertion and requires it to fail with the
value in the "why it is caught" column (outcome `caught`, not `caught-unexpected`); the clean build of the same
row must pass. `seven_records_validate` (each record passes the loader of BD-025 and N38b-28's rule, and its
`feature` is declared in the manifest).
`tolerances_set_for_001_003_004_006` (KA-001 compared as the rational 8428/5757, its printed 1.46 within 0.005
and the 1.43 of the withdrawn derivation outside it, and its truth and tolerance equal to REC-01's settled
values when the reconciliation register is present; KA-003 at 1 × 10⁻⁹ m against
the independent SE(2) exponential; KA-004 bitwise; KA-006 relative 1 × 10⁻¹² against the central difference,
and a planted zero gradient fails); `no_obligation_rows_left_in_001_to_016`;
`north_star_rows_owed_by_n00p1_13` (the obligation list is exactly KA-019 to KA-021 and KA-023 to KA-026, each
naming N00p1-13 and M1).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ka_controls_part0_8.sh` runs the clean `g1_known_answers` target on the eleven rows, then the
control runner once per control (seven builds, each with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-conformance/nc-<nnnn>`),
then prints the registry's obligation list. Logged per STD-E2E: per control an `assert` line with the row, the
mutant, the expected truth, the mutant's value, the clean value and `caught`; per tolerance a `metric` line with
the row, the tolerance, the observed error and the oracle; one `obligation` line per owed row with the owing
bead and milestone; a `cargo tree -e features` line for the product binaries (`nc-*` features found: 0);
`summary` with seven of
seven caught, four tolerances set, seven rows owed, and gates GATE-GAUNT-01 and GATE-GAUNT-02.

### Known answers and negative controls
The mutant values above are themselves hand-checkable: 1/0.87 = 1.149; 300/120 = 2.5; 300/60 = 5;
1 + 0.16 = 1.16; 1/(1 − 0.7) = 3.33, 1/(1 − 0.82) = 5.56, 1/(1 − 0.95) = 20. A control that cannot be caught
(for example a planted defect that errs on the safe side at the fixture's values) is rejected and replaced; the
suite's own record notes one such case elsewhere (an episode count rounded down that still exceeds the exact
count), which is the pattern to avoid.

## Considerations

- The North-Star fixture controls are N00p1-13's; their overlap with the proof subsystem's mutation corpus is
  handled there.
- Tolerances for sibling-defect rows are tolerances of this repository's admission tests, not statements about
  the sibling's own test suite.
- If a mutant turns out to be indistinguishable on the fixture, the fixture is extended, never the truth
  loosened.
