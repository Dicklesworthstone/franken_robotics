## Background

Every number that comes from outside the repository is a FACT about its source, not about this system, and is
registered with how it was read. The record of the planning work states a limit plainly: web sources were often
read through a summarizing fetch, and several figures were read from a rendered chart by eye or derived from
published inputs. Such figures are good enough to plan with and not good enough to gate on. The rule is that a
figure is re-read from its primary source, at a pinned version, before any gate cites it. This bead does the
re-reading for the figures that the thesis, the incumbent audit, the Amdahl ledger, the hypothesis register and
the bets depend on, since those decide what gets funded.

## Scope

In: a re-read record per source, a corrected figure where the primary source differs, the propagation of each
correction to the registries and beads that use it, and the clearing of the `reread_before_gate` flag. Out:
measuring incumbents (that replaces published figures as denominators and is done by the baseline beads);
figures used only by other units' chapters (their units re-read their own).

## Technical approach

1. **Enumerate.** Query the registries for every `figures` entry whose source has `reread_before_gate = true`
   or `how_read` in {`summary-fetch`, `figure-by-eye`, `derived`}; write the worklist with the using records.
2. **Re-read.** For each source: fetch the primary artifact itself (the paper's PDF at a fixed version
   identifier, the documentation page at the pinned release tag, the benchmark page with its date), store its
   digest and retrieval date under `tools/oracle/sources/` (the artifact itself only where its licence permits;
   otherwise the digest and a locator), and transcribe the figure with page, table or figure number. For a
   figure read from a chart, extract it with a plot digitizer and record the resolution; for a derived figure,
   keep the derivation script and its inputs.
3. **Compare.** Classify each figure: `confirmed` (same value), `refined` (same within the stated uncertainty,
   now with a tighter value), `corrected` (differs), `unlocatable` (the primary source does not contain it).
4. **Propagate.** A `corrected` or `unlocatable` figure triggers, in the same change: the update of the source
   register and of every registry record that uses it (append-only where the registry requires it); a re-run
   of the thesis arithmetic (N00-17) with the new input and a diff of every ceiling; a note on each bead whose
   threshold or background quotes the figure; and, where a hypothesis row's surviving range changes, a history
   entry of kind `primary-source-reread` in the hypothesis register.
5. **Clear the flag** only for `confirmed`, `refined` and `corrected` figures; an `unlocatable` figure stays
   flagged and may never be cited by a gate.
6. **Record** the round in the review log: sources re-read, counts by class, ceilings that moved and by how
   much.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every source used by the registries of N00-11, N00-12, N00-13 and N00-14, and every source of the
      published phase figures in the table above (the Amdahl ledger's published rows), has a re-read record
      with a digest, a date and a locator.
- [ ] Every figure is classified; every `corrected` figure has its propagation change set.
- [ ] After the round, no gate row cites a flagged source (the citation lint passes).
- [ ] The thesis ceilings are recomputed and the diff is recorded (for example, if the 410 s figure refines to
      another value T, ceilings 4 and 5 are restated as (T + t)/t and T ÷ (c + c_T·f)).
- [ ] The review log has the round with counts that sum to the number of figures on the worklist.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`tools/oracle/sources/test_reread.py`: `every_flagged_source_has_record`; `record_has_digest_date_locator`;
`corrected_figure_has_propagation_refs`; `unlocatable_stays_flagged`; `derived_figure_script_reproduces_value`
(for the 1 h rough-terrain run and the 4 × 10³ cost ratio); `counts_sum`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/source_reread.sh` validates the records, re-runs each derivation script, runs the citation lint
over the gate registry and re-runs the thesis ceilings on the published-figure ledger before and after the
update (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example thesis -- ceilings --ledger <published-figure ledger> --json`,
the example target N00-17 declares).
Logged per STD-E2E: source key, figure, old and new value with units, how it was read before and after, class,
the artifact digest and locator, the derivation script and its output for a derived figure, each ceiling before
and after with the diff; on a failed check the record and the rule it broke; `summary` with counts by class,
flags cleared, flags left, and ceilings moved.

### Known answers and negative controls
Derived values to reproduce: 8 GPUs × 60 min ÷ 16 candidates = 30 GPU-minutes; 1/(1 − 0.674) = 3.07; a battery of
256 twenty-second episodes at about 4 × 10³ times the policy cost: 86 minutes against 1.3 s (ratio ≈ 3,970).
Negative controls: a record that cites a summary page instead of the primary artifact (rejected: the locator
must be a page, table or figure of the primary document); a flag cleared for an `unlocatable` figure; a
corrected figure with no propagation.

## Considerations

- Some primary sources are living pages (nightly benchmarks); the record stores the dated snapshot and the
  figure is re-read again when a pin moves.
- The by-eye figure of 410 s matters most: several ceilings scale with it. The M0 measurement on `trj`
  supersedes it as a denominator; the re-read keeps the published value honest as context.
- Executable at the planning stage, and does not send any private project detail to an outside service.
