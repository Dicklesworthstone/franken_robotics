## Background

Three numerical checks made by this program's own investigators during the hypothesis session shaped design
decisions, and their scripts were not kept. A result that cannot be re-run is not evidence the project may cite:
the record says each must be re-run before a gate cites it. All three are re-run with scripts committed under
`tools/oracle/rerun/`. This bead re-runs the first and the third. The second is rows 19 and 20 of the plan-time
pilot ledger and is re-run once, by N38a-11, with the program `tools/oracle/rerun/contact_conditioning.py`.
This bead appends its two results to the supplementary records `PILOT-PT-S1` and `PILOT-PT-S2` of the plan-time
pilot ledger in the pilot register (N38a-10; the separate check ledger that N00-06 had drafted was withdrawn when
N00-06 was merged into N38a-10), reads the result that N38a-11 appends to rows 19 and 20, and updates the
hypothesis rows that rest on the three.

1. **CPU reference rates for a humanoid in double precision on the Mac.** Used for: the claim that a CPU referee
   at 0.2–1.0× real time per core on 24 cores can shadow about 0.1–2.4% of GPU world-seconds, which is why
   referee work is sampled and never always-on (hypothesis H-11, NARROWED), and the referee-coverage bet. The
   ledger record `PILOT-PT-S1` stores two wordings of the same check: rates "for a humanoid" with r in 0.2–1.0
   times real time per core, and about 410,000 steps/s for a reference quadruped at a 2 ms step on 36 threads
   under background load; the re-run measures both and records which model gave which figure.
2. **Condition numbers of sampled contact Hessians against half-precision round-off.** Used for: the refutation
   of half-precision contact solves (H-15, REFUTED: condition number times half-precision round-off is at or
   above one in 37–64% of sampled contact states, and refinement fails outright in 9–18%), and for the
   requirement of diagonal scaling in single precision. Recorded humanoid figures: κ₂(H) of 3 × 10⁴ to 1 × 10⁵
   raw and 2 × 10³ to 9 × 10³ scaled at default impedance; 2 × 10⁶ to 3 × 10⁶ raw and 2 × 10⁵ to 5 × 10⁵ scaled
   near-rigid, over 201 poses with simplified contact rows.
3. **The Metal working-set limit and largest single buffer on the Mac.** Recorded: a working-set limit of about
   222.7 GiB and a largest single buffer of about 167.0 GiB on the M5 Ultra with 256 GB of unified memory (the
   plan also rounds them to 222 and 167 GiB; `PILOT-PT-S2` stores 222.7 and 167.0). Used for:
   the device memory budget, the world bank's resident set, and the division of labour between the two machines.

## Scope

In: the retained scripts of the CPU reference rate and of the Metal memory limits and their runs on the Mac;
the two results appended to `PILOT-PT-S1` and `PILOT-PT-S2`; the reading of the conditioning result on rows 19
and 20 by its receipt hash; the consequences where any of the three differs, including those of the
conditioning check for hypothesis H-15 and for the scaling requirement.

Out, with the bead that has it: the conditioning program, its run, its tests, its negative control and its
verdict against the recorded ranges (N38a-11, rows 19 and 20; this bead depends on it and re-runs nothing of
it); the ledger's records, their expected values and the derivation of `reproduced` or `differs` (N38a-10;
this bead never sets a status); the precision battery and the conditioning certificate of the product (their
own beads); the sampled referee itself; throughput parity measurements.

## Technical approach

1. **Humanoid CPU reference rate** (`tools/oracle/rerun/cpu_reference_rate.sh`).
   a. Workload: the built-in 29-degree-of-freedom G1 on flat ground under the referee tier, double precision,
      standing and a scripted walking sequence, 2 s and 20 s runs; the command is `frob sim run g1 --tier referee
      --seconds <s> --key rate-<threads>-<rep> --json` once the M0 smoke exists (BD-024; a run verb takes an
      idempotency key, one per repetition so that no run is answered from a previous one). Before that the
      script may use the suite's own
      articulated dynamics example at the pinned commit, labelled `pre-airlock`.
   b. Measure: simulated seconds per wall second on one core, at 1, 12, 24 and 36 threads running independent
      worlds, with the machine's fingerprint, power state and thermal state recorded; ten repetitions; report
      the median and the interquartile range.
   c. Derive: referee real-time factor per core r; coverage c = (cores × r) ÷ (GPU world-seconds per wall
      second) for the reference GPU workloads (the GPU rate is a TARGET until measured; the script prints c for
      the published incumbent rates as context and leaves the project's own cell empty until M2).
   d. Compare with the recorded band (r in 0.2–1.0; c in 0.1–2.4% at 24 cores).
   e. The quadruped wording: steps per second of a reference quadruped at a 2 ms step on 36 threads running
      independent worlds, through the same path as (a) (a FrankenSim catalog quadruped at the pin), with the
      machine's background load recorded; compared with about 410,000 steps/s. If the pinned catalog has no
      quadruped, this figure is recorded `not-run` with that reason, which is not a reproduction.
2. **Contact-Hessian conditioning: read here, run in N38a-11.** The program
   `tools/oracle/rerun/contact_conditioning.py`, its inputs (the G1 description and two further reference
   robots, 201 poses per robot from a fixed seed, default and near-rigid impedance), the matrix
   H = M + Jᵀ·D·J, the unit-diagonal scaling and the run are specified in N38a-11 and executed there once.
   This bead reads that result from rows 19 and 20 of the pilot register, checks it against its receipt hash,
   and uses these fields of it (it appends nothing to rows 19 and 20):
   a. the range of κ₂ raw and scaled per robot and impedance, and the field `within_factor_10` (both ends
      within a factor of 10 of the recorded range, the refutation condition used in the unaudited claim C061);
   b. the share of states with κ₂ × u ≥ 1 for half precision (u = 2⁻¹¹), single precision (u = 2⁻²⁴) and
      double precision (u = 2⁻⁵³), raw and scaled;
   c. the share in which refinement from a half-precision factor fails outright, and the stricter count
      `not_converged_in_5`. DESIGN, stated identically in N38a-11: the figure compared with the recorded
      9–18% is "fails outright" (the factorization breaks down, or the residual does not fall below its
      starting value in 10 refinement steps). This bead's draft had used "fails to converge in 5 iterations",
      which is kept as the second count. Reason: the record says "fails outright" and gives no tolerance.
   d. the verdict. DESIGN, stated identically in N38a-11: the status is by containment in the recorded ranges
      (half-precision share in 37–64%; outright failure in 9–18%; the four κ₂ ranges). This bead's draft had
      accepted κ₂ within a factor of 10; that test is kept as the recorded field of (a) and is what step 4
      uses. Reason: one status rule for every row of the pilot ledger, with the looser test still visible.
3. **Metal memory limits** (`tools/oracle/rerun/metal_limits.swift` or the probe verb once it exists).
   a. Query the device's recommended maximum working-set size and maximum buffer length; then confirm by
      allocation: bisect the largest single shared-storage buffer that can be allocated and written end to end;
      then allocate buffers of 1 GiB until the working-set limit is reached, touching every page.
   b. Record with the OS version, the machine's memory pressure state and other resident processes; three runs.
   c. Compare with the recorded 222.7 GiB and 167.0 GiB; state the unit explicitly (GiB = 2³⁰ bytes; an earlier
      text mixed gigabytes and gibibytes: 85% of 222.7 GiB is 189.3 GiB, which is 203.3 GB, the plan's "about
      203 GB (189 GiB)").
4. **Record and consequences.** Each of the two results is appended to its record (`PILOT-PT-S1`,
   `PILOT-PT-S2`) with the commit, the hardware fingerprint, the seed, the command, the environment and the
   values with units; the ledger derives `reproduced` (inside the recorded band) or `differs` (with both values
   kept) by N38a-10's rule. The scripts reach the register only through the two subcommands N38a-10 declares on
   the `pilots` example target (BD-025: no default binary), the same path N38a-11 appends through:
   `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example pilots -- append-result <record> --result <file.json> [--registry
   <path>]` and `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example pilots -- rerun-status <record> --json [--registry
   <path>]`. The result document names its `bead` (`N00-35`, the records' `rerun_owner`) and carries no status
   (one that states `reproduced` or `differs` is rejected; the append path derives it). The real file is appended
   once, when the bead's runs are done; `--registry` names a scratch copy, which tests and the end-to-end script
   use. If (1) differs, the coverage band in hypothesis H-11 gets a
   history entry and the sampled-referee coverage target is re-read. If (2) differs (status `differs` in the
   result of N38a-11), the consequence is decided on its recorded fields: if half precision is adequate
   (κ₂ × u < 1 in at least 99% of scaled states), H-15's row is flagged for the owner as a candidate
   reversal; if single precision without scaling is adequate (κ₂ × 2⁻²⁴ < 1 in at least 99% of raw states), the
   scaling requirement is reviewed; if `within_factor_10` is true and neither holds, H-15 keeps its verdict and
   the history entry records the new ranges. If (3)
   differs, the device budget and every scene-capacity figure derived from it are recomputed.

- [ ] No gate cites the old, unretained values after this bead closes.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
For the rate script: `coverage_formula` (24 cores × 0.2 ÷ 4,800 GPU world-seconds per second = 0.1%;
24 × 1.0 ÷ 1,000 = 2.4%). For the memory script: `gib_gb_conversion` (222.7 GiB × 0.85 = 189.3 GiB =
203.3 GB). For the record step: `results_appended_to_s1_s2` (each result carries commit, fingerprint, seed,
command and environment, and a result missing one is rejected by the ledger's check);
`conditioning_read_by_hash` (the consequence step reads rows 19 and 20 through their receipt hash; a result
edited after it was appended is detected by the hash; nothing is appended to rows 19 and 20);
`h15_consequence_rule` (a synthetic result with 99.5% of scaled states below one at half precision flags the
candidate reversal; one with 60% does not). The tests of the conditioning program itself
(`tools/oracle/rerun/test_conditioning.py`: `diag_case_scales_to_one`,
`scaling_never_increases_kappa_on_spd_2x2`, `half_precision_unit_roundoff_is_2_pow_minus_11`,
`share_counts_sum_to_poses`) moved with the program to N38a-11.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/rerun_unretained.sh` runs the rate script and the memory script on the Mac, reads the conditioning
result of N38a-11 from the pilot register (a missing result is a failure of this script, not a skip), appends
the two results to `PILOT-PT-S1` and `PILOT-PT-S2` of a scratch copy of the register in its `work/` directory
(`--example pilots -- append-result <record> --result <file.json> --registry <scratch copy>`; the register of
record is never edited by a script, STD-E2E), and logs the derived status of S1 and S2 in the scratch copy and
of rows 19 and 20 in the register of record (`--example pilots -- rerun-status <record> --json`). Logged per
STD-E2E: check id, command, wall time, fingerprint, seed, each measured value with unit and the recorded band,
verdict; `summary` with reproduced and differing counts.

### Known answers and negative controls
The hand cases above. Negative controls: a conditioning result whose content no longer matches its receipt hash
(the consequence step must refuse it; the control "scaling skipped" on the program itself is N38a-11's); an
allocation probe that reports the queried limit without
allocating (the record must contain the allocated byte count and a checksum of the touched pages); a rate
measured with a debug build (the record carries the build profile and is rejected unless release).

## Considerations

- The original robots and poses of check (2) may not be recoverable; the re-run (N38a-11) states its sample
  openly and the comparison is by range, not by pose. One ledger holds this check: rows 19 and 20 of the pilot
  register carry N38a-11's result, and this bead only reads it.
- Check (3) stresses the machine; it is run with other jobs paused and never during another measurement.
- These are measurements about a machine and about arithmetic, not about FrankenRobotics' speed; they carry no
  performance claim for the product.
