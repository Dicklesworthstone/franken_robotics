## Background

The specification behind these beads was blended from several independently written complete plans of the same
project. Two of them remain in the repository as separate documents and were still being revised by their
authors while the blend was written. The record states exactly which revision of each was integrated, and warns
that a later revision is not reflected and should be diffed against those commits:

| Document (repository root) | Last revision integrated | Changes after the first digest that were taken |
|---|---|---|
| `COMPREHENSIVE_PLAN_FOR_FRANKEN_ROBOTICS__ASTRA.md` | Commit `47e8e89`, 2026-11-21 22:07 | A fifth foundation defect (the joint law of sampled actions in the suite's trainer); two corrections to the ten-fold statistics; the withdrawal of one overhead attribution; controller class as part of a family answer; finite-tolerance docking over a gain interval; channel laws; sampled damping and the work of a held command; free-period orbits; further sibling facts |
| `COMPREHENSIVE_PLAN_FOR_FRANKEN_ROBOTICS__NEON.md` | Commit `1f5703d`, 2026-11-21 21:55 | The absence of 64-bit atomics on Metal and the overflow rule it requires; conflicting exact pins of a JSON crate across siblings; a mixed-frame wrench type in a sibling; corrected known answers; four killed ideas |

The earlier delta audit shows why a later diff is worth doing: of 54 changes it examined, 21 were covered more
weakly in the blend and 13 were missing, and they produced 34 corrections and 18 further subsections, several of
them corrections to known answers. A change made after the integrated commits could likewise correct a fixture
that beads now carry.

This bead is a bounded maintenance task: look for later revisions; if there are none, record that; if there
are, classify each change and carry the ones worth taking into the bead graph. It does not reopen the blend.

## Scope

In: the diff of each document from its integrated commit to its current head; a classification of every
change; findings routed to the owning units' beads; a record in the review log. Out: adopting new mechanisms
wholesale (a change that proposes a new mechanism is recorded as an idea for the owner, with its evidence, and
is not turned into beads here); any edit to the two documents.

## Technical approach

1. **Detect.** For each document run `git log --oneline 47e8e89..HEAD -- <file>` (respectively `1f5703d..HEAD`).
   If both are empty, write a review-log entry "no later revision as of <date>, head <commit>" and close.
2. **Diff.** Otherwise produce a word-level diff per document (`git diff --word-diff <integrated>..HEAD --
   <file>`), split into hunks by section, and for each hunk record: section, a one-sentence statement of what
   changed, and its kind: `correction-to-number` (a value, tolerance, count or threshold changed),
   `correction-to-statement` (a claim narrowed, withdrawn or reversed), `new-fact-about-sibling`,
   `new-counterexample-or-known-answer`, `new-mechanism`, `killed-idea`, `editorial`.
3. **Classify against the bead set.** For each non-editorial hunk decide: `already-covered` (cite the bead and
   section that carries it), `weaker-here` (the bead carries it less precisely: propose the exact replacement
   text), `missing` (propose the bead it belongs in, or a new bead with its owner), `not-taken` (with the
   reason: contradicted by evidence on record, outside scope, or refuted by a calculation that is shown).
4. **Recompute before carrying.** A `correction-to-number` is recomputed independently from its own formula
   before it is proposed; a corrected known answer that does not recompute is recorded as a disagreement with
   both values and goes to the mathematical review (N00-30 round B) instead of into a bead.
5. **Route.** Findings classified `weaker-here` or `missing` are applied by the lead to the owning unit's beads
   in their staging files (`planning/fable/beads/defs/<unit>/<key>.md`, synced to the tracker by
   `planning/fable/beads/tools/sync_beads.py`; an edit made with `br` alone is overwritten by the next sync), one
   change per finding; corrections to sibling facts update the foundation fact sheet
   (N00-16); corrections to hypothesis evidence go through the hypothesis register's change rule (N00-10);
   new counterexamples and known answers are appended to the registries by their owning units.
6. **Record.** Append a round to the review log: documents and commit ranges examined, the count of hunks by
   kind and by classification, and the list of findings with their dispositions. Update the two "last revision
   integrated" entries in the provenance record to the new heads.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The review log has an entry naming, for each document, the commit range examined and the head commit.
- [ ] If later revisions exist: every non-editorial hunk has a kind and a classification, and every
      `weaker-here` or `missing` finding has a disposition (`applied` with a change reference, `rejected` with a
      reason, `deferred` with a bead).
- [ ] No corrected number is carried into a bead without an independent recomputation on file.
- [ ] The provenance record's integrated-revision table is updated to the heads examined.
- [ ] If no later revision exists, the entry says so with the date and head, and the bead closes with that
      record as its evidence.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/review_log.rs` (shared): `delta_round_names_commit_ranges`;
`hunk_counts_sum_by_kind_and_classification`; `number_correction_requires_recompute_record`;
`integrated_revision_table_matches_last_delta_round`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/plan_delta.sh` runs the two `git log` range queries and, if non-empty, checks that the number of
diff hunks equals the number of classified records. Logged per STD-E2E: document, range, commits found, hunks,
counts by kind and classification, open findings; `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls
At the time of conversion the heads of both documents are the integrated commits (`47e8e89` and `1f5703d`), so
the expected first result is "no later revision". Negative control: a scratch branch with one planted change to
a number in one document; the script must report one commit, one hunk of kind `correction-to-number`, and the
check must fail until the hunk is classified and recomputed.

## Considerations

- The task repeats whenever either document moves; it is cheap when nothing changed and bounded when something
  did.
- Findings are adopted on their evidence. A change in another document is a prompt to recompute or re-read, not
  an authority.
- Needs no product code; touches only records and, through the lead, bead staging files.
