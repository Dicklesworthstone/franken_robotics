## Background

**The bet and its rider.** BET-30 (Frontier) says that the right group for an equivariant policy is Γ_obs, the
subgroup of the robot's mechanical symmetry group Γ_mech that also preserves sensors, payload and task. Its row
in the bet register carries a Moonshot rider from the hypothesis register (H-24, narrowed): a *geometric-algebra
tree policy*, a family-policy network whose link tokens carry multivector features (scalar, vector, bivector and
trivector parts of the geometric algebra of three-dimensional space) and whose layers are equivariant under
relabelling of links and under the rigid motions that preserve gravity, would learn faster than a *plain
morphology transformer* (the family policy's network: one token per link, heading normalization and convention
augmentation). The evidence: invariance to heading and to description conventions is necessary, augmentation
recovers most of it, and there is no locomotion evidence for the geometric-algebra specifics, so the expected
gain is 0 to 1.3 times. The falsifier, as registered with the bet: **the tree policy is falsified if it does not
reach the return threshold in 1/1.3 of the environment steps of the plain morphology transformer.**

**Why this bead exists.** The register reads BET-30 at two milestones, M4 and M8 (the bet register N00-28 and its
table of deciding gates; the gate register's view by milestone in N40-31 counts BET-30 once at M8). The bet
register's rule is that when a bet's verdict becomes `Held` or `Falsified` at one of its milestones, its bead
writes `docs/adr/bet-<nn>-<milestone>.md`. The bead BET-30 decides the M4 half and closes at M4, recording the
rider `pending M8`; the measurement at M8 (N23bx-02) writes the gate receipt and the decision of the kill gate,
not the bet's verdict. This bead is the M8 reading and the record `docs/adr/bet-30-m8.md`. Tier of what it reads:
Moonshot. Nothing on a critical path depends on it.

**The gate and kill gate it reads.** GATE-CORTEX-17 (Design comparison, M8, TARGET): arm B (the plain transformer
with a geometric-algebra encoder and decoder only) or arm C (full geometric-algebra message passing on the
kinematic tree) is at least 1.3 times faster than arm A (the plain transformer) to the return threshold at equal
wall-clock, or better with no adaptation beyond seed spread. KILL-GATREE: trigger "GATE-CORTEX-17 fails";
fallback "the plain morphology transformer remains; `fs-ga` features are not used in policies". The gate is read
twice: a pilot on tools outside the workspace (N23b-01) taken by the first reading of the kill gate (N23b-02),
then, only if that reading is `not-falsified` and a compute envelope is approved, the measurement on our engine
and the final reading (N23bx-02).

## Scope

In: the reading of the bet's step clause from the GATE-CORTEX-17 receipt; the combination of that clause with
the KILL-GATREE decisions on record; the bet's verdict on the rider and on the whole row at M8; the record
`docs/adr/bet-30-m8.md`; the register's `status` and `decision_record` fields for BET-30, updated through the bet
register's API in the same change; the end-to-end script that recomputes the reading.

Out, with the beads that have them: the pilot and the first reading of KILL-GATREE (N23b-01, N23b-02); the arms
and their layer laws (N23bx-01, SPEC-C-24); the nine training runs, the gate receipt, the gate's own reading rule
on wall-clock, the final reading of KILL-GATREE and its downstream changes (N23bx-02); the M4 half and its record
`docs/adr/bet-30-m4.md` (BET-30, which this bead never edits); the verdict function (N00-28); the exit state and
the exit check (N40-31). This bead applies no kill trigger and writes no kill-gate record.

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs, each cited by identifier and hash.** `docs/adr/bet-30-m4.md` (the M4 verdict, with the rider
   `pending M8`); the receipt `target/gates/GATE-CORTEX-17.json` and its row in `registries/gates.toml`; the
   KILL-GATREE decision records of N23b-02 and, if it ran, N23bx-02. A missing input other than a recorded
   `not run` stops the bead: the verdict is not filed.
2. **The step clause.** The bet counts environment steps; the gate counts elapsed time at equal wall-clock. Both
   are read from the same nine runs (arms A, B, C × seeds 2416, 2417, 2418). For arm X ∈ {B, C} and seed pair i,
   let S_A,i and S_X,i be the environment steps at the attainment look (the look at which the gate's attainment
   rule, with level αₖ = α/[k·(k + 1)] at look k, first established the return threshold; never the first
   crossing). With ℓᵢ = ln(S_A,i / S_X,i), mean ℓ̄ and sample standard deviation s over the n = 3 pairs, the
   one-sided 95% bounds of the step ratio ρˢ_X = S_A/S_X are exp(ℓ̄ ∓ t·s/√n), t = 2.920 (Student, 2 degrees of
   freedom), the same statistic N23bx-02 uses on times.
   - Clause **false** (the rider reaches threshold in 1/1.3 of the steps): for some X the lower bound of ρˢ_X
     is at least 1.3.
   - Clause **true** (rider falsified): for both B and C the upper bound of ρˢ_X is below 1.3.
   - Otherwise **unmeasured**, with both intervals printed.
   DESIGN: a seed on which X does not attain within the budget counts as X not reaching the threshold (the clause
   cannot be false on X); a seed on which A alone does not attain makes X's ratio unmeasured. Reason: fail-closed
   for a Moonshot, and no extrapolation of a censored run.
   Source of S: the receipt's per-run environment-step count at the attainment look, which N23bx-02 records
   (`not-attained` with the steps at the budget's end for a run that does not attain). Only for a receipt that
   lacks it (one written before that field) is S the product of the elapsed time to attainment and the arm's
   steps per second, labelled `derived` in the record.
3. **Verdict on the rider** (one of four, enumerated before the reading):
   - `Falsified: KILL-GATREE fired` when either reading of the kill gate is on record as fired (the first
     reading `falsified`; the final reading `failed`; or `not-passed: undetermined` with no approved repeat),
     whatever the step clause reads.
   - `Held { through: M8 }` when KILL-GATREE has not fired, the gate passed with its controls caught (the
     handicapped arm A′ detected, the A/A pair below 1.02, the identity-message arm C reading as a failure, the
     zero-weight and random policies missing the threshold), and the step clause is false.
   - `Falsified: step clause` with the gate passed when KILL-GATREE has not fired but the step clause is true
     (an arm can pass the gate through "better with no adaptation beyond seed spread" with a step ratio of 1.1).
     No kill gate has fired, so the bet register's verdict is `Falsified` and the exit state is `open` until the
     owner rules: either the kill gate's fallback is adopted by an amendment of N23bx-02's decision, or the bet's
     row is amended with a dated reason. This bead records the case as "passed its gate, falsified by its step
     clause" and asks for the ruling; it adopts no fallback itself. This is the reading BET-07 uses for its second
     clause.
   - `Untested: not run` when the gate was not measured on our engine (a precondition of N23bx-02 failed, or the
     first reading was `undetermined` and nothing was funded) and KILL-GATREE has not fired; the reason is
     printed.
   A gate pass without its controls caught is not a pass (bet-register rule).
4. **Verdict on the row.** The M4 record's verdict on clauses (a) and (b) is carried unchanged; the rider's
   verdict is added. Where KILL-GATREE fired and KILL-EQUIV and KILL-MARROW-BLOCKS did not, the row is partly
   falsified; that exit state is computed by N40-31, not here.
5. **Record.** `docs/adr/bet-30-m8.md`: the rider's statement and falsifier; S per arm and seed with its source
   (`receipt` or `derived`); ρˢ_B and ρˢ_C with both bounds against 1.3; the gate's own wall-clock ratios and
   zero-shot comparison as recorded (not recomputed); the controls caught; both KILL-GATREE records by hash; the
   M4 record by hash; the verdict on the rider and the row; if falsified, the fallback in force ("the plain
   morphology transformer"), and the wording changes made through N00-15 and N00-22 (no report calls a policy a
   geometric-algebra tree policy outside the Moonshot label). The bet register's `status` and `decision_record`
   for BET-30 are updated in the same commit; `bet-30-m4.md` is not edited (its digest is checked before and
   after).

- `bet30m8_first_reading_falsified_not_run`: N23b-02's record `falsified`, N23bx-02 `not run` →
  `Falsified: KILL-GATREE fired`, the step clause `not run`.
- `bet30m8_undetermined_unfunded_is_untested`: first reading `undetermined`, nothing funded, kill gate not

- `bet30m8_x_seed_not_attained`: one seed of C not attaining, B unmeasured → C counts as not reaching; clause

- `bet30m8_pass_without_control_not_counted`: the gate `passed` but the handicapped arm A′ not detected → not

- `bet30m8_m4_record_unchanged`: writing the M8 record leaves the digest of `bet-30-m4.md` unchanged.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/bet_30_m8_decide.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; read-only on the gate records): reads the receipt
and the KILL-GATREE records, prints `frob train status <job> --json` for each of the nine runs named in the
receipt to take the attainment look and its step count, recomputes ρˢ_B and ρˢ_C and the rider's verdict, runs
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example bets -- status --json`, and asserts the recorded values equal the recomputed ones
and the M4 record's digest is unchanged. Logged per STD-E2E, one NDJSON line per step: timestamp, script, bead,
step, event, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim Design comparison, the
Moonshot label, class, seeds), S per arm and seed with its source, both bounds of each ratio against 1.3, the
KILL-GATREE statuses with record hashes, the verdict; on failure the first differing value and the paths of the
receipt and records; a final `summary` line naming GATE-CORTEX-17, KILL-GATREE and BET-30.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the bounds above (1.3947 and 1.4635; 1.091 and 1.132; 0.953 and 1.598), which equal the arithmetic
of N23bx-02's reading rule on times when the same numbers are steps; t = 2.920 at 2 degrees of freedom; the
expected gain of H-24, 0 to 1.3 times. Negative controls: a receipt whose step count was edited after its hash
was taken (refused, hash mismatch); a KILL-GATREE record missing (no verdict filed); an attempt to rewrite
`bet-30-m4.md` (the digest check fails); a gate pass with the handicapped arm undetected (not counted).

## Considerations

- Steps and wall-clock can disagree: geometric-algebra layers ran 1.4 to 7.5 times slower per epoch in published
  work, so an arm can reach the threshold in fewer steps and still lose on time. The record prints both; the
  kill gate decides on time, the bet's clause on steps, and the verdict rule of step 3 says which governs.
- Three seeds are weak evidence; every one of the nine values is printed so a reader can judge.
- Kill gate: KILL-GATREE (trigger: GATE-CORTEX-17 fails; fallback: the plain morphology transformer remains and
  `fs-ga` features are not used in policies). At most four Moonshot items are active at once (pilot register
  N38a-09); if the rider never gets its turn the reading is `Untested: not run`, which is a recorded state and
  not a pass.
- What would make us revisit: a registered amendment of the bet's row that restates the rider on wall-clock, made
  before the measurement.
