## Background

**The bet.** BET-36 says that worlds which change (a wet patch, a rug that slides, a cord across a doorway, a
spill the robot's own wheels spread) can be simulated well enough to change design decisions without coupled
fluid, cloth and cable in every training world. Full-room fluid is out of memory by orders of magnitude, and
coupled cloth and cable at batch speed are out of reach on present foundations (H-16, refuted as stated). The
substitutes are a contact-level film-friction law and *proxies*: a rug as a free slab or hinged plates, a cord as
a fixed ridge, a free rod or a chain of links, a spill as a friction patch. The bet has three falsifier clauses,
all registered before M3:

- (a) Solid, M3: the film law misses its known answers, changes fewer than 10% of episode outcomes on the
  wet-floor battery, or adds more than 5% to step time. Read by BET-36 at M3.
- (b) Pilot, M6, before the island work package: the proxies reproduce fewer than 90% of the coupled model's
  failures in the same class; the proxy claim is then withdrawn for that class and the coupled island stays as
  an escalation.
- (c) Frontier, M6: films advected by the robot leave design rankings on the spill battery at a rank correlation
  of 0.95 or above with static patches, or cost more than 15% of the step.

**Why this bead exists.** The register reads BET-36 at M3 and M6 (bet register N00-28; N40-31 counts it once at
M6). The bead BET-36 decides clause (a) and closes at M3, recording (b) and (c) `pending`. The bet register's rule
is that the bet's bead writes `docs/adr/bet-<nn>-<milestone>.md` when a verdict is reached at a milestone; the M6
beads (the pilot, the two kill-gate decisions, the advected-film gate) write their own records, not the bet's.
This bead is the M6 reading and `docs/adr/bet-36-m6.md`.

**Terms.** A full-model failure is *reproduced* by a proxy when the proxy's episode with the same seed fails in
the same failure class of the taxonomy within one second of the full model's failure time (the pilot's
scoring). An *advected film* is a per-world film-thickness field that the robot's wheels and feet pick up,
carry and redeposit, as opposed to a static patch whose thickness never moves. τ-b is Kendall's rank correlation
with ties.

## Scope

In: the reading of clauses (b) and (c) from the records of the M6 beads; their combination with KILL-LIVING and
KILL-ADVECT; the bet's per-class verdict at M6 and on the whole row (carrying the M3 verdict on (a) unchanged);
`docs/adr/bet-36-m6.md`; the bet register's `status` and `decision_record` for BET-36, updated through its API in
the same change; the end-to-end script that recomputes the reading.

Out, with the beads that have them: the proxy-adequacy pilot, its scoring and the GATE-HAB-09 row (N19b-16); the
decision KILL-LIVING, its ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-LIVING-M6.md` and its downstream changes (N19b-20); the
advected film and its gate GATE-HAB-07 (BD-413, measured by N19b-10); the cost and ranking measurements and the
decision KILL-ADVECT with its ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-ADVECT-M6.md` (N19b-11); articulated proxies
(BD-415) and coupled islands (BD-416); clause (a), KILL-FILM and `docs/adr/bet-36-m3.md` (BET-36, which this bead
never edits); the exit state (N40-31). This bead applies no kill trigger and writes no kill-gate record.

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs, each cited by identifier and hash.** `docs/adr/bet-36-m3.md`; the GATE-HAB-09 row; the GATE-HAB-07
   row and receipt; the KILL-LIVING and KILL-ADVECT ADRs. A missing input stops the bead (no verdict filed),
   except a gate or decision on record as `not run`, which gives `Untested` with its reason.
2. **Clause (b), per class.** DESIGN: the classes are the three kinds of matter of the pilot: cord (rod), rug
   (cloth), oil (fluid). Reason: a proxy claim is made per kind of matter, and KILL-LIVING records per-kind
   verdicts where its pooled reading does not fire, so the bet's withdrawal and the kill gate speak about the same
   partition. For each kind k, read from the GATE-HAB-09 row the share S_F,k of full-model failures that the
   best proxy of that kind reproduces (pooled over the wheeled and the legged robot) with its exact binomial
   interval.
   - Clause (b) **true for k** (proxy claim withdrawn for k): S_F,k < 0.90.
   - **False for k**: S_F,k ≥ 0.90.
   - **Unmeasured for k**: the pilot reported the scene `uninformative` (fewer than 30 full-model failures).
   The point estimate is compared with 0.90, the reading KILL-LIVING freezes for the same share, so that
   "proxies suffice" and "proxy claim withdrawn" are complements for each kind and never both. Where the pooled
   share fires KILL-LIVING (S_F ≥ 0.90, islands not built) while one kind is below 0.90, the record prints both:
   islands are not built, and the proxy claim for that kind is withdrawn, so reports involving it say that the
   proxy is not shown to reproduce the coupled model's failures.
3. **Clause (c).** Read from N19b-11's measurements: the added step cost of the advected film in percent with its
   paired 95% interval per workload and device pair, and τ-b between the design rankings with static patches and
   with advection on the spill battery (multi-pass low-friction crossings), with its bootstrap interval over world
   addresses.
   - Clause (c) **true**: τ-b ≥ 0.95; or the lower end of the cost interval is above 15% on a workload-device pair
     a training batch would use.
   - **False**: τ-b < 0.95 and every such cost interval lies below 15%.
   - **Unmeasured**: a cost interval still straddles 15% at the end of its registered budget (N19b-11's
     `unresolved`).
   The same operational reading as KILL-ADVECT, without its mass trigger, which is a deciding gate (GATE-HAB-07)
   and not a falsifier clause.
4. **Verdict** (`HeldInPart`, a map, as the bet register defines for BET-36):
   - Kinds under (b): `Falsified: proxy claim withdrawn` where the clause is true; `Held { through: M6 }` where it
     is false; `Untested: uninformative` where unmeasured.
   - Advection under (c): `Falsified: KILL-ADVECT fired` when N19b-11's ADR reads `fired` (mass, cost or no ranking
     change), whatever the clause reads; `Held { through: M6 }` when the clause is false, GATE-HAB-07 passed with
     its non-conservative control caught, and the kill gate did not fire; `Untested` when unmeasured.
   - Clause (a) and the KILL-FILM decision: carried from the M3 record.
   - KILL-LIVING is recorded beside the verdict, one `decision` entry per kind (`kind:fluid`, `kind:cloth`,
     `kind:rod`, N19b-20) in N40-03's outcome names; it decides whether islands are funded, not whether the bet holds.
     When it fires the bet's fallback for islands is in force ("deformable matter is represented by proxy").
5. **Record.** `docs/adr/bet-36-m6.md`: the three clauses with their thresholds; S_F,k per kind with counts and
   intervals, and the pooled S_F; τ-b with its interval; cost per workload and device pair with intervals; the
   GATE-HAB-07 values (relative volume residual against 10⁻⁶ over 10⁵ steps, spreading exponent against 1/8 within
   2%, non-conservative control, cross-world leak count over 10⁶ world-episodes); both kill decisions cited by
   hash; the M3 record by hash; the verdict map; the fallbacks now in force and the wording changes made through
   N00-15 and N00-22; the beads that stop or start (from the kill ADRs, not re-decided). The digest of
   `bet-36-m3.md` is unchanged.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance bets::bet36m6`, on `synthetic` records:

- `bet36m6_rug_0_84_withdraws_rug_only`: cord 140/150 = 0.933, rug 126/150 = 0.840, oil 171/180 = 0.950; pooled
  437/480 = 0.910 ≥ 0.90 (KILL-LIVING fired: proxies suffice) → map {cord: held, rug: falsified (claim withdrawn),
  oil: held}, with the island fallback recorded.
- `bet36m6_all_kinds_above_0_9_holds_b`: 140/150, 136/150 = 0.907, 171/180 → all held.
- `bet36m6_uninformative_scene_untested`: oil with 24 full-model failures → oil `Untested: uninformative`.
- `bet36m6_tau_0_97_falsifies_c`: τ-b = 0.97 with N19b-11's ADR `fired: no ranking change` →
  `Falsified: KILL-ADVECT fired`.
- `bet36m6_cost_16_to_20_falsifies_c`: cost interval [16.2%, 19.8%] on the reference quadruped batch → clause true.
- `bet36m6_cost_straddle_unmeasured`: cost interval [13.0%, 18.0%] recorded `unresolved` → `Untested`.
- `bet36m6_tau_0_81_cost_9_holds_c`: τ-b = 0.81 with interval [0.74, 0.87], cost [7.5%, 10.1%], GATE-HAB-07 passed
  with its control caught, KILL-ADVECT not fired → held.
- `bet36m6_mass_gate_failed_twice`: GATE-HAB-07 failed after one repair cycle, N19b-11 `fired: mass` →
  `Falsified: KILL-ADVECT fired` though τ-b = 0.81.
- `bet36m6_m3_record_unchanged`: the digest of `bet-36-m3.md` is equal before and after.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/bet_36_m6_decide.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; read-only on the gate records): reads the GATE-HAB-09
and GATE-HAB-07 rows and both kill ADRs, recomputes S_F,k from the pilot's per-episode failure table (counts and
exact intervals), τ-b from the two ranking tables of the spill battery and the cost intervals from N19b-11's paired
runs, recomputes the verdict map, runs `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example bets -- status --json`, and asserts the
record equals the recomputation and the M3 record's digest is unchanged. Logged per STD-E2E, one NDJSON line per
step: timestamp, script, bead, step, event, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim
Fidelity for the pilot shares, class `Tested`, the instrument pin; Design comparison for the ranking), shares with
counts against 0.90, τ-b against 0.95, cost against 15%, the GATE-HAB-07 values against their bounds, kill-gate
statuses with ADR hashes, the verdict map; on failure the first differing value and the paths of the tables and
records; a final `summary` line naming GATE-HAB-07, GATE-HAB-09, KILL-ADVECT, KILL-LIVING and BET-36.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the shares above (0.933, 0.840, 0.950, pooled 0.910); the island cost bound, 0.95 + 0.05·c ≤ 10 so
c ≤ 181 rigid worlds per coupled world at 5% activity (KILL-LIVING's second trigger); KA-443, the radius of a
constant volume spreading on a flat floor grows as t^(1/8) in the axisymmetric case (front ∝ t^(1/5) planar), and
removing the h³ factor of the thin-film flux changes the exponent. Negative controls: a pilot table edited after
its hash was taken (refused); a pooled share used for a kind that has its own (the test asserts the per-kind
reading); a GATE-HAB-07 pass whose non-conservative control was not caught (not counted as a pass); a kill ADR
missing (no verdict filed); an attempt to rewrite `bet-36-m3.md` (the digest check fails).

## Considerations

- The pilot compares model forms inside one incumbent engine; it says nothing about whether that engine's cable,
  cloth or fluid is right, nor about real floors. Its shares are Fidelity between model forms, class `Tested`.
- Kill gates and fallbacks: KILL-ADVECT (the advected film fails its mass gate after one repair cycle, costs more
  than 15% of the step, or rankings agree with static patches at τ-b ≥ 0.95; fallback: static film-friction
  patches, advection not built); KILL-LIVING (proxies reproduce at least 90% of full-model failures, or an island
  costs more than 181 rigid worlds at 5% activity with no route to less; fallback: the Solid tier and the Frontier
  proxies are the product, coupled islands are not built, reports say deformable matter is represented by
  proxy). The bet's own fallback: static friction maps with wet patches.
- None of this is on the critical path of the ten-fold test and none is counted in the thesis.
- What would make us revisit: a coupled-model failure class that no proxy can produce in principle (for example
  a cord wrapping a wheel axle), which would argue for a per-failure-class withdrawal beside the per-kind one;
  proposed only as a dated amendment of the bet row before a new pilot.
