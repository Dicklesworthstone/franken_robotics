## Background

**Before.** An agent edits a robot description, launches training, and learns minutes to about an hour later
that the knees saturate (published training takes about seven minutes for a flat-ground quadruped task and
about an hour for a default rough-terrain humanoid run; FACT about sources).

**After.** `frob design check` returns in under two seconds: the mass spiral closes at 14.2 kg; peak knee torque
demand exceeds the selected actuator's continuous rating by 31%; the design has mechanical symmetry group
C₂ × C₂, of which its front-only depth camera preserves C₂; and three ranked repairs are offered as typed
edits. The numbers 14.2 kg and 31% are illustrative values of the story's fixture; "under two seconds" is a
TARGET owned by the analysis gate.

**What it demonstrates.** The first principle of the studio: check before you simulate. "Mass spiral" names the
co-design closure: heavier links need bigger actuators, which add mass, which needs bigger actuators; the
closure analysis reports whether that loop converges and at what mass. The symmetry report shows the algebra
doing work: the mechanical group is computed from the design, and the subgroup the sensors preserve is what a
policy may later be constrained to. Repairs come back as typed edits an agent can apply without parsing prose.
This is one of the three scenarios that define the M1 exit and must be driven by a fresh agent.

## Scope

In: the demo inputs, `demos/ns-01/demo.yaml`, the end-to-end script and its assertions. Out: the analyses, the
archetype, the edit machinery (their own beads).

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** A design agent holding only the generated agent guide.
2. **Inputs** (`demos/ns-01/inputs/`): `quad-a.fbot`, a `quad-mammal` archetype instance with four legs of
   three joints, a front-mounted depth camera, and a knee actuator card chosen one size too small. DESIGN: the
   fixture's parameters are fixed so that the mass roll-up closes at 14.2 kg and the knee's continuous-torque
   demand in the crouch load case exceeds the card's rating by 31% (derivation in
   `docs/derivations/ns-01-fixture.md`; tolerances ± 0.05 kg and ± 1 percentage point).
3. **Steps and what the agent sees.**
   a. `frob design check demos/ns-01/inputs/quad-a.fbot --json` → exit code 2 (completed with findings), one
      envelope: `result.closure = "closed"`, `result.mass_kg ≈ 14.2`, `result.symmetry = "C2xC2"`, a finding
      with code `A-11.knee.continuous`, severity `high`, a message naming the 31% exceedance and the load case,
      and at least three `repairs`, each `{edit, args, predicted}` (for example `swap-actuator` on `*.knee` to
      the next card with predicted mass +0.9 kg and margin +46%; `regear` on `*.knee`); `evidence` with class
      Tested, tier `static`, determinism `exact`, `reality: no-claim`; `next_actions[0]` is the command that
      applies the first repair.
   b. `frob analyze symmetry quad-a --json` → Γ_mech = C₂ × C₂ with its generators (left-right mirror,
      front-back mirror), Γ_obs = C₂, and the element that breaks the excluded generator (the camera mount).
   c. Apply `next_actions[0]` verbatim (`frob design edit quad-a swap-actuator --joints '*.knee' --to <card>`)
      → a new `DesignId`, the declared symmetry effect (none) and the invalidation set.
   d. `frob design check` on the new design → the knee finding is gone; the mass has risen by the predicted
      amount within tolerance; exit code 0 or 2 with no `high` finding.
4. **Demo file.** `demo_id: ns-01`; claims: the claim identifiers of GATE-ATEL-02 and GATE-SKEL-04; lane `cpu`;
   the four commands with `expect_exit` and expected JSON subsets; `fresh_agent: true`.
5. **Timing.** The script measures wall time of step (a) over 20 repetitions on a warm store and reports the
   95th percentile against 2 s; the demo asserts the single-run time under 2 s on the CPU tier.
6. **MCP parity.** Steps (a) to (d) are repeated through `frob-mcp` in process; result digests must equal the
   CLI's.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] From a clean checkout, `scripts/e2e/ns_01.sh` passes: exit codes, the expected JSON subsets, the claim
      product fields, and the before-and-after finding lists.
- [ ] The check returns in under 2 s at the 95th percentile on the archetype (GATE-ATEL-02's timing clause,
      measured value recorded with the TARGET label).
- [ ] The first repair, applied verbatim from `next_actions`, clears the finding, and the predicted effect
      matches the re-check within the stated tolerance.
- [ ] CLI and MCP result digests agree for all four steps.
- [ ] A fresh agent reaches step (d) from the one-paragraph brief "make this quadruped's actuators adequate"
      without further instruction; the transcript is stored with the M1 exit record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/demos.rs`: `ns01_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns01_fixture_mass_is_14_2` (roll-up of the
fixture's bodies and cards: 14.2 ± 0.05 kg); `ns01_fixture_knee_exceedance_is_31_percent` (demand ÷ rating =
1.31 ± 0.01 in the crouch case, recomputed from the fixture's own numbers); `ns01_expected_subset_matches_schema`
(the expected fragment validates against the `design.check` result schema).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_01.sh`, built on the North-Star skeleton: the four commands on CLI and MCP. Logged per STD-E2E:
each command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
identities, seed), `metric` lines for mass (kg), exceedance (%), check time (ms, threshold 2,000, TARGET),
`assert` lines for closure, symmetry groups, repair count and the cleared finding; `summary` naming
GATE-ATEL-02 and GATE-SKEL-04.

### Known answers and negative controls
The fixture's two numbers and the groups (C₂ × C₂ of order 4; Γ_obs = C₂ of order 2). Negative controls: the
same design with the correct actuator card (no knee finding: the check must not cry wolf); the design with a
second camera at the rear (Γ_obs must become the full C₂ × C₂); a repair applied to a stale parent (refused
with the stale-parent code, exit 7). Determinism: class D0; identical envelopes twice on one machine and on
Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- A demo shows the path runs; the check's predictive value is the business of the static-analysis bet, not of
  this scenario.
- Two seconds is measured on archetype templates; an unusually large design may take longer and says so.
