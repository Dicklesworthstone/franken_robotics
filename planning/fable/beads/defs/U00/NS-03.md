## Background

**Before.** Two designs are compared on mean reward over an arbitrary number of episodes; the comparison is
stopped when it looks convincing, which invalidates an ordinary significance test.

**After.** `frob eval compare A B --task warehouse-tote` runs both designs on common world addresses under a
declared anytime-valid study, stops when the evidence process crosses its threshold, and reports "B beats A on
energy per metre, evidence 240 to 1, valid at any stopping time". It reports separately whether each design
meets the mission's hard limits, because a paired win establishes neither. The figure 240 to 1 is illustrative.

**What it demonstrates.** Pairing on common world addresses removes world-to-world variance. An *e-process* is a
running evidence ratio that stays valid however often it is inspected: under the null hypothesis the chance it
ever exceeds 1/α is at most α, so stopping when it does is legitimate. A study declares its type (anytime-valid
or fixed-n exact) before data; the two are never mixed. The published tests are applied here to paired design
comparison; that application is the only novelty claimed.

## Scope

In: demo inputs, `demos/ns-03/demo.yaml`, the script. Out: the study machinery, the task family, the scene.

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** A design agent choosing between two candidate designs.
2. **Inputs.** Two wheeled carrier designs A and B (B has a lower-loss drivetrain); the task `warehouse-tote`
   (carry a tote along a generated warehouse aisle) with a metric `energy_per_metre` and two hard limits
   (completion within the time limit; peak winding temperature below the card's limit); a third design A′
   identical to A for the null check.
3. **Steps and what the agent sees.**
   a. `frob study plan --type anytime-valid --alpha 0.05 --metric energy_per_metre --design A --design B
      --task warehouse-tote --json` → the plan address, the cost estimate, and the budget at which the e-process
      can cross under the declared alternative.
   b. `frob eval compare A B --task warehouse-tote --plan <address> --json` → runs pairs on common world
      addresses until the e-process crosses 1/α = 20 or the budget ends; the envelope gives the verdict
      `settled: B`, the e-value at stopping, the exact pair count, the realized pair correlation, the claim
      (kind Design comparison, class Tested or Estimated, tier, determinism class), and, in the same result,
      `hard_limits: {A: {...}, B: {...}}` with each design's own verdicts.
   c. The human summary line is the permitted wording: "B beats A on energy per metre, evidence ⟨e⟩ to 1, study
      ⟨id⟩", followed by the hard-limit lines.
   d. `frob eval compare A A′ …` → `inconclusive` at budget end; the e-process never crosses.
   e. A request that mixes study types, or pairs arms in an uncontrolled determinism class, is refused.
4. **Lanes.** `cpu` (reduced): the same study on `batch-cpu` with a small budget and a larger planted
   efficiency gap so that it settles within 60 s. `device`: on the GPU tier, where the result is `provisional`
   until promoted.
5. **Demo file.** Claims: the claim identifier of GATE-ATEL-04; commands of the reduced lane.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The A-against-B study settles for B with an e-value at or above 20 and reports the exact pair count.
- [ ] The result carries both designs' hard-limit verdicts; a variant in which B wins on energy but violates the
      temperature limit reports the win and the violation together.
- [ ] The A-against-A′ study does not settle.
- [ ] Mixed study types and uncontrolled determinism classes are refused with their codes.
- [ ] CLI and MCP agree; the summary line is a rendering of the permitted Design-comparison wording.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`demos.rs`: `ns03_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns03_threshold_is_one_over_alpha` (α = 0.05 → 20);
`ns03_expected_fragment_has_hard_limits_for_both`; `ns03_null_pair_expected_inconclusive`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_03.sh`. Logged per STD-E2E: plan address, pair count, the e-value trajectory at each look, the
stopping time, the pair correlation, both hard-limit verdict sets, the evidence block; `summary` naming
GATE-ATEL-04.

### Known answers and negative controls
Gate threshold (TARGET): comparison type-I error at or below α under optional stopping over 10⁴ null
comparisons. A hand-checkable evidence value: a likelihood-ratio e-process with per-pair factors of 1.2 crosses
20 after ⌈ln 20 ÷ ln 1.2⌉ = 17 pairs. Negative controls: the null pair (must not settle); a peeking fixed-n
test presented as anytime-valid (refused); a report that prints the win without the hard-limit verdicts
(rejected by the claim-rule battery).

## Considerations

- A paired win is relative: it says nothing about either design meeting the mission, which is why the
  hard-limit verdicts are part of the same result.
- On the GPU tier the verdict is provisional until promotion; the scenario's wording carries that marker.
