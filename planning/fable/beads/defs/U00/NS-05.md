## Background

**The story.** The studio reports the symmetry group of a hexapod and the subgroup that its sensors, payload
and task preserve, lists the gaits that subgroup permits, and trains a policy equivariant under it that reaches
the target return in at most 0.7× the steps of an unconstrained one. The factor 0.7 is a TARGET owned by the
equivariance gate.

**What it demonstrates.** Symmetry is computed, not declared. A reference library elsewhere takes each robot's
symmetry group from a per-robot configuration file; here the group Γ_mech is derived from the design term (its
bodies, joints, inertias and actuator cards), and the observation-preserving subgroup Γ_obs from sensors,
payload and task. From the group follow, for free: the list of gaits it permits, as pairs (H, K) of a
spatio-temporal symmetry group H and its purely spatial subgroup K with their phase lags (a tripod gait, a
metachronal wave); a block-diagonal structure for linear maps that an equivariant policy uses; and metamorphic
tests. A declared symmetry is an assertion that is checked against the computed one.

## Scope

In: demo inputs, `demos/ns-05/demo.yaml`, the script. Out: the symmetry analysis, the gait atlas, equivariant
layers, training.

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** A design agent, then a training agent.
2. **Inputs.** A hexapod archetype instance with left-right and three-fold leg-pair regularity; a variant with a
   one-sided payload; a flat-ground velocity task.
3. **Steps and what the agent sees.**
   a. `frob analyze symmetry hexapod-a --json` → Γ_mech as generators with its order, Γ_obs for the stated
      sensors and task, and for each generator of Γ_mech excluded from Γ_obs the element that breaks it.
   b. The same on the payload variant → a strictly smaller Γ_obs with the payload named as the breaker.
   c. `frob algebra irreps --design hexapod-a --task flat-velocity --rep permutation:6 --json` (the verb's
      design form: the group is the stored Γ_obs of the robot-task pair, so the agent never types it; the
      representation is its action on the six legs) → the isotypic decomposition, the block types (real or
      complex) and the free-parameter count used by equivariant layers, with `group_kind: obs`.
   d. `frob gait atlas hexapod-a --json` → the gaits Γ_obs permits as (H, K) pairs with phase lags, including
      the alternating tripod (legs in two groups of three, half a period apart).
   e. For each of ten paired seeds s, `frob train start hexapod-a --task flat-velocity --tying equivariant
      --seed <s> --key ns05-eq-<s> --json` (the trainer's tying option, BD-326, N23a-31, N23a-33), with the
      augmented arm `--tying augmentation` (key `ns05-aug-<s>`) and the unconstrained arm without `--tying`
      (key `ns05-free-<s>`) → job handles; `frob train status <job> --json` until the return threshold.
   f. A report with the paired step ratio (equivariant ÷ unconstrained) and its bounds, final returns, and the
      augmented arm's result, whichever wins.
   g. `frob train start hexapod-a --task patch-velocity --tying equivariant --dry-run --key ns05-patch --json`,
      on a task with a one-sided friction patch that breaks the left-right mirror (Γ_obs becomes trivial for
      this robot-task pair), is refused, naming the broken generator (nothing to tie; repair `--tying
      augmentation` or none); a tying request that names a group outside Γ_obs, `frob train start <payload
      variant> --task flat-velocity --tying equivariant --group mech --dry-run --key ns05-mech --json` (Γ_mech on
      the payload variant of step b), is refused with FROB-REF-161, exit 3, its payload naming the generator that
      moves the payload (N23a-31 is the raising site at `train.start` and owns its `--group obs|mech` option).
4. **Lanes.** `cpu`: steps (a) to (d) and (g) only, which are exact and fast; they are the demo file's
   commands. `long`: the three-arm training on a GPU with ten paired seeds.
5. **If the equivariance kill gate fired**, steps (e) and (f) are `not demonstrated` and the scenario is
   printed as "symmetry report and gait list" (augmentation and metamorphic testing remain); the script then
   checks that `--tying equivariant` is refused as not admitted (exit 5, naming the KILL-EQUIV record, N23a-33)
   and that `--tying augmentation` still runs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Γ_mech and Γ_obs equal the groups on file for the hexapod and its payload variant, with the correct
      breaker named.
- [ ] The gait list contains the alternating tripod with phase lag one half, and no gait the group forbids.
- [ ] The symmetry-breaking task refuses the constraint.
- [ ] Long lane: the paired step ratio and its bounds are recorded against 0.7 with no loss of final return;
      the comparison with the augmented arm is reported.
- [ ] Steps (a) to (d) are class D0 and byte-identical on both platforms.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`demos.rs`: `ns05_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns05_expected_group_orders` (the orders of Γ_mech and Γ_obs on file for
the fixture and its variant); `ns05_tripod_in_expected_gaits` (two groups of three legs, lag 1/2);
`ns05_refusal_expected_on_patch_task`; `ns05_refusal_161_expected_on_group_mech`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_05.sh --lane cpu|long`. Logged per STD-E2E: groups as generator lists with orders, breakers,
block types, gaits with phase lags, per-seed steps and returns per arm, the ratio and bounds against 0.7, the
refusal code of step (g); `summary` naming GATE-MARROW-03, GATE-CORTEX-03 and GATE-ATEL-02.

### Known answers and negative controls
Group facts: a body with one mirror plane has Γ = C₂ (order 2); with two orthogonal mirror planes C₂ × C₂
(order 4); the count of gaits follows from the subgroup lattice of the group acting on the legs. Negative
controls: a moved sensor, a one-sided payload, a changed sole material and a reversed gear (each must shrink
the group and be named as the breaker); a declared symmetry that differs from the computed one (refused);
zero-weight and random policies failing the return threshold in the long lane (the behaviour-not-plumbing
rule; a shuffled-label student applies only to distillation).

## Considerations

- Training curves from a nondeterministic profile are not evidence; steps-to-return come from re-evaluation
  under a replayable profile.
- Whether the constraint beats plain augmentation is the symmetry bet's question; this scenario reports the
  comparison either way.
