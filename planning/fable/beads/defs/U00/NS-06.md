## Background

**The story.** An agent runs thirty design-check-simulate-explain-edit cycles on a wheel-leg hybrid in one hour
on the two-card Linux host. Each cycle dispatches a family of at least ten variants in one batch, so at least
three hundred designs are decided in the hour. Every design version is content-addressed, every comparison is a
declared study, and `frob design bisect` finds the edit that broke stair climbing. Thirty cycles, ten variants
and one hour are TARGETs.

**Why the unit is designs decided per hour.** The cycle count alone is worth only 3.0–3.5× against an incumbent
quadruped loop: a 120 s cycle with no training in it against an incumbent cycle of 360–420 s (about 300 s of
training plus an agent turn of 60–120 s). With the same agent latency t in both arms, deciding one design per
turn caps at (300 + t)/t, 3.5–6×. Deciding a *family* per turn is what removes that cap, and it is only valid
if the per-turn evaluation needs no per-design training, which is the business of the rank-agreement bet.

## Scope

In: demo inputs, `demos/ns-06/demo.yaml`, the scripted-agent session and its accounting. Out: mixed batches,
the daemon's slot dispatch, optimization verbs, lineage and bisect, the hybrid's contact laws.

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** A design agent with the full studio surface over MCP; `frobd` owning the devices.
2. **Inputs.** A wheel-leg hybrid archetype instance; a stair-and-flat task with a requirement; a starting
   lineage of five versions in which version 3 carries a **planted** regression (a gear ratio edit that
   prevents stair climbing).
3. **One cycle** (target 120 s of wall time including the agent's turn):
   a. `frob design check <parent> --json` → findings with ranked repairs.
   b. The agent proposes at least ten variants as typed edits, each `frob design patch <parent> --file
      <variant>.patch.json --key ns06-pat-<cycle>-<i> --json` against the parent (the registered form).
   c. `frob phylum sweep --designs <the variants> --task stairs-flat --worlds-per-design 16 --tier <tier> --key
      ns06-sw-<cycle> --json` → one dispatch steps all variants in one batch (different link lengths, gear
      ratios, wheel radii; a topology variant where the family includes one), each with its worlds; a
      provisional scorecard per design comes back. When the variants are one parameter family, the same cycle
      may use `frob opt start --design <parent> --family sweep --sweep <lever>=<lo>:<hi>:<n> --task stairs-flat
      --key ns06-opt-<cycle> --json` (the design named by `--design`, as N28b-09 spells it), which returns the
      family table and the designs decided per dispatch. That form is used only where N28b-09 has landed
      (it is not a dependency: the `cpu` lane uses `phylum sweep` alone).
   d. `frob eval compare <best> <parent> --task stairs-flat --key ns06-cmp-<cycle> --json` stores the paired
      comparison, and `frob explain compare <comparison> --json` (the verb reads the two designs, the task and
      the world addresses from the stored comparison) → why they differ on the task: first divergent event and
      binding atoms.
   e. The agent picks the next parent; every version has a `DesignId` that is a content hash.
4. **The hour.** Thirty cycles; the script counts designs with a recorded decision (kept, discarded with the
   reason, or shortlisted) and reports designs decided per hour, with compute used and tokens spent.
5. **Bisect.** `frob design bisect --good <v1> --bad <v5> --predicate succeeds --task stairs-flat --level 0.05
   --key ns06-bis-1 --json` (BD-343's spellings, under N28b-02's honesty rules) → the planted edit (the gear ratio change of
   version 3), labelled `a-change` or `first-change`, found by an anytime-valid comparison at each step at level
   α/P (P = ⌈log₂ 4⌉ + 1 = 3 probes for the four edits when v5 has no stored outcome), with the evidence at each
   step.
6. **Lanes.** `cpu` (reduced, the demo file): three cycles of four variants on `batch-cpu` with a scripted
   agent (fixed proposals), plus the bisect on the five-version lineage. `device`: the full hour on the Linux
   host with a live agent in a fresh context.
7. **Under fallbacks.** If mixed-topology batches were killed, families are parameter variants within one
   topology and the scenario says so; if the baseline rung may not rank a class (no rank-agreement record), the
   per-design decisions are labelled `screening only`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Full lane: at least thirty cycles and at least three hundred designs with recorded decisions inside one
      hour of wall time, with the phase ledger of the session summing to wall-clock.
- [ ] Every design version in the session resolves by content address; every comparison names its study.
- [ ] `design bisect` returns the planted edit (gate TARGET: at least 95 of 100 trials on planted edits).
- [ ] Zero kernel compiles occur after warm-up across the session's variants.
- [ ] The report states designs decided per hour and the cycle-only ceiling (3.0–3.5×) side by side, and labels
      per-design decisions with the rung that made them.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`demos.rs`: `ns06_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns06_lineage_fixture_has_planted_edit_at_v3`;
`ns06_cycle_ceiling_arithmetic` (360/120 = 3.0, 420/120 = 3.5; (300 + 60)/60 = 6, (300 + 120)/120 = 3.5);
`ns06_decision_record_fields` (design id, decision, rung, reason).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_06.sh --lane cpu|device`. Logged per STD-E2E: per cycle the commands with exit codes, wall time by phase
(agent turn, check, dispatch, explain), variants dispatched, compiles (must be 0 after warm-up), decisions; at
the end designs decided, cycles, wall time, GPU-seconds, tokens; the bisect's steps with evidence; `summary`
naming GATE-ATEL-04, GATE-TETHER-13 and GATE-SPARK-10.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-011 and KA-012 (the cycle and turn ceilings). Negative controls: a session in which each dispatch carries
one design (must report 30 designs and fail the 300 threshold); a lineage with no regression (bisect must report
that good and bad do not differ, label `no-change` with exit 2, under a key of its own `ns06-bis-2`, not invent
an edit); a design edited outside the patch verb (its hash does not resolve in the lineage: refused).

## Considerations

- A decision here is a studio decision at a declared rung, not a qualified result; shortlisted designs still go
  to training and qualification.
- The host is often heavily loaded; the disclosure block records the load, and the hour is wall time under it.
