## Background

**The story.** A tracked robot climbs stairs and crosses rubble; on soft soil the sinkage model is active and
labelled Frontier in the evidence.

**What it demonstrates.** Coverage beyond legs, and honesty about tier. Tracked, wheeled and hybrid robots have
no published agent-driven design loop to compare with, and the planning evidence found no sampling-control or
baseline results for tracked robots at all. A track is a moving belt with many simultaneous contacts, modelled
by a belt contact law; rubble is many rigid bodies; soft soil is terramechanics: a plate sinks to a depth set
by a pressure-sinkage law, and the thrust a track can develop is bounded by the soil's shear strength, with
ruts left as per-world mutable state. Rigid-ground traversal is Solid; the soil model is Frontier, and every
result that used it must say so in its evidence so that nobody mistakes a soft-ground figure for an
established one.

## Scope

In: demo inputs, `demos/ns-08/demo.yaml`, the script. Out: belt and tyre laws, soil mechanics, terrain
generators, the tracked baseline controller, the task family.

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** A design agent evaluating a tracked platform.
2. **Inputs.** The tracked archetype instance; three scenes from the terrain generators at a stated difficulty:
   a stair flight, a rubble field, a soft-soil plot with a soil card; three tasks from the M4 families (stair
   climbing for tracks, rubble crossing, straight traverse on soil) with requirements.
3. **Steps and what the agent sees.**
   a. `frob scene gen --family stairs --difficulty 0.6 --seed 2 --json` (and `rubble`, `soil-plot`) → scene
      packs.
   b. `frob control synth tracked-a --json` → the baseline controller for the tracked class, or
      `not-synthesizable` with the route to the sampling fallback.
   c. `frob sim run --design tracked-a --scene <stairs> --task stair-climb --json` → verdicts per world;
      `frob telemetry summary` with track slip, belt tension proxy and pitch margin.
   d. The same on rubble → verdicts, with the contact count per world and any capacity flags (never a silent
      drop of contacts).
   e. `frob contact soil --card <soil-card> --plate 0.3x0.1 --load 400 --json` → sinkage and available thrust
      on the card, with the law's closed-form values.
   f. `frob sim run … --scene <soil-plot> --task soil-traverse --json` → verdicts with sinkage, drawbar pull
      and rut depth; the envelope's evidence carries `tier_tag: frontier` for the soil model and names the soil
      card's provenance; if any card field is `assumed` the class is Estimated.
   g. Reset check: after an episode the rut state of each world is cleared (no leak into the next episode).
4. **Lanes.** `cpu` (reduced): four worlds each on `batch-cpu`, a three-step stair and a small rubble patch;
   step (e) is exact and fast. `device`: full scenes on a GPU.
5. **Under the soil kill gate** (the soil gate fails or no card with provenance exists): a rigid floor with
   rolling resistance and a drag card labelled `assumed`; soft-ground results labelled exploratory, and the
   scenario is printed under that name.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The tracked robot completes the stair and rubble tasks under its baseline controller in the reference
      worlds (verdicts recorded with their requirement margins).
- [ ] The soil run's evidence carries the Frontier tag and the soil card's provenance; no soft-ground figure is
      printed without them.
- [ ] `contact.soil` matches the closed forms within 10⁻⁶ in double precision; simulated plate sinkage and
      drawbar pull are within 2% of them (gate TARGETs).
- [ ] Rut leak is zero over the resets of the run.
- [ ] Capacity overflow, if any, is flagged on the world and never dropped silently.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`demos.rs`: `ns08_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns08_expected_evidence_has_frontier_tag_on_soil_run`;
`ns08_soil_fixture_closed_form` (for the fixture plate and load the expected sinkage is recomputed from the
soil card's own pressure-sinkage law and compared with the expected fragment; in the common power-law form
p = k·zⁿ this is z = (p/k)^(1/n)); `ns08_reset_expected_rut_zero`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_08.sh --lane cpu|device`. Logged per STD-E2E: scene identifiers, controller outcome, verdicts
and margins, contact counts and capacity flags, sinkage (m), drawbar pull (N), rut depth (m), the evidence
block with tier tag and card provenance; `summary` naming GATE-TRAC-08 and GATE-REFLEX-01.

### Known answers and negative controls
For a soil card in the common power-law form p = k·zⁿ (p ground pressure, z sinkage, k and n from the card;
the soil-mechanics bead is the authority for the exact law): doubling the load on the same plate multiplies
sinkage by 2^(1/n). Negative controls: a soil run whose evidence lacks the
Frontier tag (the claim-rule sweep rejects it); a rut state carried across a reset; the rubble scene with
contact capacity set too low (must raise the flag, not drop contacts).

## Considerations

- A tracked or hybrid class may use its baseline controller for ranking only where rank agreement has been
  measured for it; that measurement is due at M4 for these classes.
- Stair geometry and rubble size come from the hazard registries with provenance or are labelled `assumed`.
