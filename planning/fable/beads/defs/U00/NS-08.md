## Background

**The story.** A tracked robot climbs stairs and crosses rubble; on soft soil the sinkage model is active and
labelled Frontier in the evidence.

**What it demonstrates.** Coverage beyond legs, and honesty about tier. Tracked, wheeled and hybrid robots have
no published agent-driven design loop to compare with, and the planning evidence found no sampling-control or
baseline results for tracked robots at all. A track is a moving belt with many simultaneous contacts, modelled
by a belt contact law; rubble is many rigid bodies; soft soil is terramechanics: a plate sinks to a depth set
by a pressure-sinkage law, and the thrust a track can develop is bounded by the soil's shear strength, with
ruts left as per-world mutable state. Contact on rigid ground is Solid. Two models in this scenario are
Frontier: the belt law of the tracks, which every stair and rubble run uses, and the soil model. Every result
that used either must say so in its evidence, so that nobody mistakes a tracked or a soft-ground figure for an
established one.

## Scope

In: demo inputs, `demos/ns-08/demo.yaml` and the script `scripts/e2e/ns_08.sh`, which is the one implementation
of the scenario's steps and assertions on the stair scene, the rubble scene and the soft-soil plot, in both
lanes.

Out, and which bead has it:

- The law-level end-to-end checks of wheels, tyres, belts and hybrids (rolling, cornering force, scrub work,
  capacity refusals): N18b-09 (`scripts/e2e/wheels_tracks_ns08.sh`). That script's last stage calls this bead's
  script and holds no copy of the scenario's commands; it depends on this bead.
- The soil verb's own end-to-end script, in kept and fallback modes: N18b-19.
- Belt and tyre laws (BD-365, which names this scenario's rigid-ground legs in its done-condition), soil
  mechanics (BD-366), terrain generators, the tracked baseline controller, the task family.

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** A design agent evaluating a tracked platform.
2. **Inputs.** The tracked archetype instance `tracked-a` (an instance of the `tracked-flipper` archetype); three
   scenes: a stair flight and a rubble field from the terrain generators (families `terrain/stairs` and
   `terrain/rubble`, two of the ten of N19a-28) at a stated difficulty, and a soft-soil plot, which is not a
   terrain family: it is the flat drawbar scene of the soil verb's script (N18b-19), ground of family
   `terrain/flat` whose traverse region carries the soil card; three tasks from the M4 families (stair
   climbing for tracks, rubble crossing, straight traverse on soil) with requirements.
3. **Steps and what the agent sees.**
   a. `frob scene gen --family terrain/stairs --difficulty 0.6 --seed 2 --key ns08-sg-1 --json` and the same
      for `--family terrain/rubble` (key `ns08-sg-2`) → scene packs (the family is passed with `--family`, as
      BD-165 defines `scene.gen`); the soil plot is the drawbar scene of N18b-19 with the soil card assigned to
      its region.
   b. `frob control synth tracked-a --key ns08-ctl-1 --json` → the baseline controller for the tracked class,
      or `not-synthesizable` with the route to the sampling fallback.
   c. `frob sim run tracked-a --scene <stairs> --worlds <n> --seconds <s> --key ns08-run-1 --json`, then `frob
      task verdict <run> --task stair-climb --json` → verdicts per world; `frob telemetry summary <run> --json`
      with track slip, belt tension proxy and pitch margin. Asserted on this run and
      on (d): task success; a non-zero scrub energy on the turn segments; the belt law named with tier label
      Frontier in the `evidence` block; the `Degraded` share printed; and, for a sample of episodes, promotion
      or labelling against `strict`.
   d. The same on rubble (key `ns08-run-2`, task `rubble-cross`) → verdicts, with the contact count per world
      and any capacity flags (never a silent drop of contacts).
   e. `frob contact soil --soil <soil-card> --design tracked-a --end-effector <left track> --slip 0.2 --json`
      (the design input form of `contact.soil` with the spellings BD-366 fixes: the track's patch geometry and
      static load share read from the compiled model) → sinkage, the thrust at 20% slip and the thrust limit on
      the card, with the law's closed-form values.
   f. `frob sim run tracked-a --scene <soil plot> --worlds <n> --seconds <s> --key ns08-run-3 --json`, then
      `frob task verdict <run> --task soil-traverse --json` → verdicts with sinkage, drawbar pull
      and rut depth; the envelope's evidence carries `tier_tag: frontier` for the soil model and names the soil
      card's provenance; if any card field is `assumed` the class is Estimated. Asserted: the sinkage model is
      active (non-zero sinkage reported in telemetry), and thrust falls with slip as the shear law predicts:
      on `--profile strict` the drawbar pull at 20% slip is within 2% of the closed form.
   g. Reset check: after an episode each world's next episode starts from a state digest equal to a fresh
      world's (no terrain state leaks into the next episode). While the rut row of GATE-TRAC-08 reads the
      fallback "off" this checks that terrain resets each episode; once N18b-17 has landed, the same digest
      equality covers the rut field, which the digest includes. The check needs no rut-specific command.
   h. Determinism: the stair run of (c) repeated (key `ns08-run-1-repeat`) gives identical digests, and so does
      a second worker count (`--workers 8`, key `ns08-run-1-w8`).
4. **Lanes.** `cpu` (reduced): four worlds each on `batch-cpu`, a three-step stair and a small rubble patch;
   step (e) is exact and fast. `device`: full scenes on each device tier present. A step whose law fell back at
   its pilot runs against the fallback and asserts the fallback's name in the evidence.
5. **Under the soil kill gate** (the soil gate fails or no card with provenance exists): a rigid floor with
   rolling resistance and a drag card labelled `assumed`; soft-ground results labelled exploratory, and the
   scenario is printed under that name.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The tracked robot completes the stair and rubble tasks under its baseline controller in the reference
      worlds (verdicts recorded with their requirement margins); scrub energy on the turn segments is non-zero,
      the `Degraded` share is printed, and a sample of episodes is promoted or labelled against `strict`.
- [ ] The evidence of every stair and rubble run names the belt law with the tier label Frontier.
- [ ] The soil run's evidence carries the Frontier tag and the soil card's provenance; no soft-ground figure is
      printed without them.
- [ ] `contact.soil` matches the closed forms within 10⁻⁶ in double precision; simulated plate sinkage and
      drawbar pull are within 2% of them (gate TARGETs).
- [ ] No terrain state leaks over the resets of the run: every next episode starts from a fresh world's digest
      (ruts included once the rut field exists; the mode in force is logged).
- [ ] The stair run repeated, and run on a second worker count, gives identical digests.
- [ ] Capacity overflow, if any, is flagged on the world and never dropped silently.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`demos.rs`: `ns08_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns08_expected_evidence_has_frontier_tag_on_soil_run`;
`ns08_soil_fixture_closed_form` (for the track of `tracked-a` and its static load share the expected sinkage
is recomputed from the soil card's own pressure-sinkage law and compared with the expected fragment; in the
common power-law form p = k·zⁿ this is z = (p/k)^(1/n)); `ns08_reset_expected_fresh_digest`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_08.sh --lane cpu|device`. Logged per STD-E2E (every line `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`NS-08`),
`step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time): scene identifiers, controller outcome, verdicts
and margins, scrub work (J), the `Degraded` share, contact counts and capacity flags, sinkage (m), drawbar pull
(N), rut depth (m), digests where runs are compared, the evidence block with tier tags and card provenance;
`summary` naming GATE-TRAC-08 and GATE-REFLEX-01. The capability script of N18b-09 runs this script as its last
stage and reads these lines.

### Known answers and negative controls
For a soil card in the common power-law form p = k·zⁿ (p ground pressure, z sinkage, k and n from the card;
the soil-mechanics bead is the authority for the exact law): doubling the load on the same plate multiplies
sinkage by 2^(1/n). KA-416: track thrust at 20% slip, 293 N for the illustrative card. Negative controls: a
stair run whose evidence does not name the belt law as Frontier; a soil run whose evidence lacks the
Frontier tag (the claim-rule sweep rejects it); terrain state carried across a reset; the rubble scene with
contact capacity set too low (must raise the flag, not drop contacts).

## Considerations

- A tracked or hybrid class may use its baseline controller for ranking only where rank agreement has been
  measured for it; that measurement is due at M4 for these classes.
- Stair geometry and rubble size come from the hazard registries with provenance or are labelled `assumed`.
- The stair and rubble scenes depend on scene generators and on the tracked baseline controller; a failure
  there is reported with its first cause so that a controller defect is not booked as a contact defect.
- The CPU lane uses the `smoke` episode count and is not a statistical claim.
