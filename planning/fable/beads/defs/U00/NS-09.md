## Background

**The story.** A policy that scores well by exploiting contact compliance is re-evaluated on the referee tier;
the score falls outside the promotion envelope and the result is labelled `provisional: tier-sensitive`. A
design whose rank among finalists changes under another admissible contact law is labelled model-conditional
and may not spend prototype budget.

**What it demonstrates.** The trust ladder. Learned policies are optimizers, and an optimizer finds whatever
the simulator gets wrong: a compliant contact model that lets a foot sink and spring back can be mined for free
energy or free propulsion. The defence has three layers. *Promotion*: a fast-tier result is provisional until
it has been re-evaluated on the referee and the difference lies inside a frozen envelope. *Detectors*: the ten
exploitation detectors of `eval.detect` (BD-267), re-evaluations under perturbations that should not matter; the
one that catches this exploit is the per-step work-energy audit (`energy-created`: penetration recovery pumping
energy into the body). *The model-form rank check*: finalists are re-ranked under other admissible model forms (a
hard-contact law, another friction cone shape, stiffness halved and doubled, a half step), and a ranking that
flips is labelled model-conditional. Engines with several solvers exist; none was found with a promotion rule,
which is the one thing claimed here.

## Scope

In: `demos/ns-09/demo.yaml` (which names the inputs below), the script. Out: the promotion rule (BD-263), the
detectors (BD-267, N23b-05, N23b-06), the model-form check and FROB-REF-830 (N23b-08, BD-266), the energy audit
(BD-208), and the planted inputs themselves: the tier-sensitive policy and its honest control (N16b-06), the
planted box-cone ranking (N23b-08, over N23b-07's diagonal-traction exploit). The demo builds no plant of its
own, so that the story runs on the same artifacts the gates run on.

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** A training agent whose policy looks too good.
2. **Inputs.** A reference quadruped task; three inputs: `honest` (N16b-06's honest control, the baseline
   legged controller on the same task and addresses), `exploit-compliance` (N16b-06's **planted** plant A,
   `fixture:tier-sensitive-hopper`: a periodic leg-stroke controller tuned on a fast configuration whose
   penetration recovery hands the body d/h of velocity from nothing, so it gains speed by bouncing; conformance
   build), and `pyramid-winner` (the planted winner of N23b-08's `ranking-box-cone`, a design-and-policy pair
   that wins only under a pyramidal friction cone).
3. **Steps and what the agent sees.**
   a. `frob eval run fixture:tier-sensitive-hopper --battery <task battery> --tier <the lane's fast tier> --key
      ns09-ev-1 --json` → a high fast-tier score, marked `provisional`.
   b. `frob eval detect <result> --key ns09-det-1 --json` → the detectors' findings, exit 2: `energy-created`
      flagged with the first offending window, its contact mode and the created joules (the penetration
      recovery), and `tier-sensitive` from the promotion rule.
   c. `frob eval promote <result> --tier referee --key ns09-pro-1 --json` (each of these three is a run verb and
      carries its own idempotency key) → re-evaluation on the referee (or the strict CPU profile where the
      referee cannot complete the scene, named as adjudicator); the difference per quantity against the frozen
      envelope; verdict `not promoted`, label `provisional: tier-sensitive`; exit code 2.
   d. The same three commands on `honest` (keys `ns09-ev-2`, `ns09-det-2`, `ns09-pro-2`) → no detector fires;
      `established`.
   e. `frob fidelity modelform --ranking <ranking-box-cone> --k 8 --budget-share 0.10 --key ns09-mf-1 --json` →
      each finalist labelled `model-robust` or `model-conditional`; the ranking is `model-conditional` with the
      variant (the circular cone) under which `pyramid-winner`'s rank changed; the genuine ranking of N23b-08
      under key `ns09-mf-2` is `model-robust`.
   f. An attempt to promote the model-conditional ranking, `frob eval promote <ranking-box-cone> --key ns09-pro-3
      --json`, is refused with FROB-REF-830, exit 3, naming the flipping variant (N23b-08's
      `check_spend_or_promote`, the same function the build-packet and prototype-budget sites call when those
      exist); the flag is still present on every result derived from it.
   g. Citing the exploit's provisional result as established evidence, `frob report run <the exploit's run>
      --cite <its result> --json` (BD-302), is refused with FROB-REF-820, exit 3.
4. **Lanes.** `cpu` (reduced): the fast tier is `batch-cpu` in the lockstep profile with 16 worlds, the higher
   tier the strict profile. `device`: GPU against the referee.
5. **Demo file.** Claims: the claim identifiers of GATE-FID-02, GATE-FID-05 and GATE-CORTEX-04.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The planted exploit policy is not promoted and carries `provisional: tier-sensitive`; the honest policy is
      promoted.
- [ ] At least one detector flags the exploit and none flags the honest policy.
- [ ] The planted pyramid winner is `model-conditional`; a genuine winner in the same set is `model-robust`;
      the check costs at most 10% of the campaign's compute (gate TARGET).
- [ ] The promotion of the model-conditional ranking is refused with FROB-REF-830, and no downstream result has
      lost the flag.
- [ ] No report cites the provisional result as established: the citation is refused with FROB-REF-820.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/ns_09.sh --lane cpu` passes on a machine with no GPU; `--lane device` passes where a device
      exists and logs `not run` (never passed) where none does.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`demos.rs`: `ns09_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns09_expected_labels` (exploit → `provisional: tier-sensitive`; honest →
`established`; pyramid → `model-conditional`); `ns09_refusals_expected` (the model-conditional promotion → 830;
the provisional citation → 820);
`ns09_flag_union_survives_combination` (a product combined from a flagged and an unflagged input keeps the
flag).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_09.sh --lane cpu|device` (header `bead=NS-09 milestone=M3 labels=north-star,trust-ladder,
verb:eval.run,verb:eval.detect,verb:eval.promote,verb:fidelity.modelform,verb:report.run seed=17
needs=cpu|device plants=tier-sensitive-hopper,ranking-box-cone`) runs steps a to g with their keys. Logged per
STD-E2E to `target/e2e-logs/ns_09/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, each
command line with its key, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block; fast-tier and higher-tier
scores with units, per-quantity differences against the envelope (base displacement in m, energy per metre in
J/m, stance impulse in N·s) with the envelope hash; detector states with pairs and thresholds; energy created
(J) and its first offending window; model-form labels with `top_k_overlap` per variant and the cost against its
10% share; refusal codes with repairs; on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths; `summary`
naming GATE-FID-02, GATE-FID-05 and GATE-CORTEX-04.

### Known answers and negative controls
A hand-checkable energy audit: a ball dropped from 1 m on a penalty contact that returns it to 1.05 m has had
5% of m·g·h created by the simulator; the audit must raise `energy-created`. Negative controls: the honest
policy (no false alarm); a promotion rule mutant that compares against a re-baselined envelope (rejected: the
envelope is frozen); a consumer that drops the `model-conditional` flag.

## Considerations

- Promotion says the fast tier tracks the higher tier on this result; it does not say either is right.
- A new exploit found in use becomes a permanent detector fixture and negative control.
