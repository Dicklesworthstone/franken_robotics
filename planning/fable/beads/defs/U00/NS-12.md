## Background

**The story.** A walkthrough video becomes a private scene pack through the owner's existing reconstruction
pipeline, and a candidate home robot is evaluated in it. Scale is labelled `Estimated` unless anchored,
surfaces the scan never saw are treated conservatively and listed as a re-scan request, and the scan never
leaves the machine.

**What it demonstrates.** Real places as worlds, with three kinds of honesty. *Scale*: a reconstruction from
video has no absolute scale unless something of known size anchors it; an unanchored pack carries scale class
Estimated and every clearance computed in it inherits that. *Unknown regions*: a scan sees only what the camera
saw; unseen space is neither free nor solid, and the pack carries an unknown mask that analyses must treat
conservatively (a route through unknown space is not a route), with a *probe request* listing the measurements
or views that would settle an unresolved answer. *Privacy*: a scan of someone's home is private data; the pack
and everything derived from it carry a private mark, exports are refused without an explicit flag, logs carry
no private-scene data, and a purge removes the pack and the lineage closure of everything derived from it.

## Scope

In: demo inputs (a synthetic capture for the CPU lane; a real capture only on the owner's machine),
`demos/ns-12/demo.yaml`, the script. Out: scan import, scene fitting, clearance complexes, the privacy
machinery.

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** The owner (or an agent on the owner's machine); a design agent evaluating a home robot.
2. **Inputs.** CPU lane: a **synthetic** capture rendered from a generated kitchen with a region deliberately
   never viewed and no scale anchor (labelled `synthetic` in every output). Real lane: the reconstruction
   pipeline's output for a real walkthrough, private, never committed.
3. **Steps and what the user sees.**
   a. In a session opened with the private scope (`frob session open --scopes
      scene:import,scene:read,scene:run,scene:admin,sim:run,task:read,artifact:run,export:report,private:read,private:export --key
      ns12-s-1 --json`; holding `private:export` is not enough to export, the per-call private flag is also
      required), `frob scene import --kind place <reconstruction> --name my-kitchen --key ns12-si-1 --json` (a
      real-place import is private by construction, BD-398, and written beneath the owner-held `roots.private`,
      so `--private`, which only raises a mark, is not needed; there is no per-call root) → a scene pack with a loss report, a scale label
      (`estimated` from the pipeline's own scale prior with its interval, `relative-only` if it states none, or
      `witnessed` when an anchor of known size was supplied; N19c-02), an unknown mask with its volume share, and
      the private mark.
   b. `frob scene show <pack> --json` → the manifest, unsupported properties, the scale label and the mark.
   c. `frob scene fit --design home-robot --scene <pack> --route door-to-sink --key ns12-fit-1 --json` (the
      registered form: the robot's footprint family is read from the design) → a feasibility witness, an
      impossibility cut, or `unresolved` where the route would cross unknown space or where the scale
      uncertainty spans the clearance.
   d. `frob scene probe-request <answer of step c> --json` (the verb takes the unresolved answer, optionally
      `--cell`) → the re-scan request: the unseen surfaces and the one
      measurement (for example the doorway width, with a tape) that would settle the unresolved answer.
   e. Supply the anchor in the import's operator declaration (the doorway width, 0.82 m ± 2 mm between the two
      named jambs, a measured baseline and so a scale witness; it also answers the probe request of step d) and
      re-import (`frob scene import --kind place <reconstruction> --declare ns12-anchor.toml --name my-kitchen
      --key ns12-si-2 --json`) → a new pack with scale `witnessed`, the request of step d closed; step (c), re-run
      on the new pack with key `ns12-fit-2`, now resolves.
   f. `frob sim run home-robot --scene <pack> --worlds <n> --seconds <s> --key ns12-run-1 --json`, then `frob
      task verdict <run> --task fetch --json` → verdicts; every derived artifact
      carries the private mark; `reality: no-claim`.
   g. `frob artifact export <run artifact> --out exports/ --key ns12-ax-1 --json` → refused without the
      per-call private-export flag (FROB-REF-731 with `observed = missing-flag`, a private asset, raised by the
      export door; exit 5); with it (`--allow-private-export`, BD-305's spelling, key `ns12-ax-2`), the export is
      written under the session's export root, still marked, and recorded in the ledger's `export` table.
   h. `frob scene purge <pack> --json` (a dry run, the verb's default: the closure and its digest, nothing
      removed), then `frob scene purge <pack> --dry-run=false --confirm <closure digest> --json` (the `admin`
      form N37-19 fixes: no idempotency key, the confirm digest is the guard) → the pack and its lineage closure
      are removed with a purge receipt; no digest of the closure remains in the store, and the receipt lists the
      private export of step (g).
4. **Demo file.** Claims: the claim identifiers of GATE-HAB-03 and GATE-SAFE-04; CPU-lane commands on the
   synthetic capture only.
5. **Under the ingestion kill gate** the place is authored in the interior grammar from a tape-measure survey
   and the pack says it is a proxy.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The imported pack carries its scale label and unknown mask; an unanchored pack never reports a clearance
      verdict as resolved where scale uncertainty spans it.
- [ ] At least one probe request is emitted for the unseen region; supplying the anchor changes the label and
      resolves the fit.
- [ ] Every derived artifact kind carries the private mark; an export without the flag is refused; after the
      purge no digest of the closure remains.
- [ ] The end-to-end logs contain no private-scene data (a scan of the log files for scene bytes, file names
      inside the capture and thumbnails finds none).
- [ ] On the CPU lane (a synthetic capture) the demo's lines for GATE-HAB-03 read `no-claim`, never a pass; the gate's own record is N19c-07's.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`demos.rs`: `ns12_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns12_cpu_lane_inputs_are_synthetic`; `ns12_expected_scale_label_estimated`;
`ns12_expected_probe_request_nonempty`; `ns12_log_redaction_fixture` (a log line containing a path inside the
capture directory is rejected by the helper).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_12.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; header `bead=NS-12 milestone=M5 gates=GATE-HAB-03,GATE-SAFE-04
plants=reflected-reconstruction,unknown-route-witness,side-door-export`; `roots.private` of the scratch state and
`FROB_PRIVATE_ROOT` point at a temporary directory outside the repository, and the scratch safety configuration
sets the owner-held `private.export_allowed = true` so that step (g) tests the flag, not the owner's switch; the
CPU lane runs steps a to h on the
synthetic capture with the keys above, each compared run under its own key). Every NDJSON line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the command line with its key, exit code and wall time (paths inside the capture and
beneath the private root are masked by the helper and replaced by the pack digest), pack digest (never contents),
scale label, unknown-volume share, fit verdicts before and after the anchor, probe-request items and the closed
request's link, the refusal code and exit code of the unflagged export, the purge's count of removed objects; on
failure the first differing value; `summary` naming GATE-HAB-03 and GATE-SAFE-04.

### Known answers and negative controls
Scale arithmetic: a doorway reconstructed at 0.90 units and anchored at 0.82 m gives a scale of 0.911 m per
unit; a 0.80 m robot footprint that "fits" at the unanchored unit scale (0.90 > 0.80) has only 20 mm clearance
after anchoring. Negative controls: a reflected reconstruction (must be rejected: the transform class is not
established); a route through unknown space reported as a witness; an export attempted through a different
verb (every exporting verb must refuse).

## Considerations

- No private project or scene detail is sent to any outside service at any step; reconstruction runs locally.
- A real place evaluated this way is still simulation: nothing here is a Reality claim.
