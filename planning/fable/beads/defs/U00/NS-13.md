## Background

**Before.** An agent evaluates candidate bodies one at a time and reports the best score. Nothing says whether
an untried body would also work, or why a failed one cannot.

**After.** The mission is a query with a free parameter. A robot carries 400 N on four point feet at (±a, ±b)
with a = 1/3 m; its centre of mass may lie anywhere in a rectangle of half-dimensions 150 mm (along a) by 75 mm
(along b); each foot delivers a commanded normal force in [0, 160] N; the half-width b is free.
`frob mission synthesize` returns sets I ⊆ F ⊆ O (I the inner set, certified feasible; F the true feasible
set; O the outer set, not excluded). Every b ≥ 3/14 m is in the inner set, with the corner allocation
(160, 130, 110, 0) N as its witness. Every b < 3/14 m is excluded, with a dual vector as the impossibility
witness: at b = 0.20 m one foot would have to carry 165 N. The answer says that the boundary has zero reserve on
two constraints. `frob-verify` re-checks the bundle offline in exact rational arithmetic. When the mission is
amended to a passive equal-spring suspension, revalidation withdraws the earlier inner set and says what must
be re-solved: at b = 1/4 m, which had been admitted with the allocation (155, 135, 105, 5) N, the springs
deliver (175, 115, 85, 25) N against the 160 N rating. **These numbers are known answers, not targets.**

**Why the numbers are what they are** (so the bead stands alone). Write the four foot forces as
f = 100 + α·s_x + β·s_y + γ·s_x·s_y with s_x, s_y = ±1 the foot's signs. Force balance gives the 100 N mean;
moment balance at the worst corner of the centre-of-mass rectangle gives α = 100 × 0.15 ÷ (1/3) = 45 N and
β = 100 × 0.075 ÷ b = 7.5/b N; γ is the one free internal load. The bounds 0 ≤ f ≤ 160 at the heaviest foot
(100 + 45 + β + γ ≤ 160) and the lightest (100 − 45 − β + γ ≥ 0) require β − 55 ≤ γ ≤ 15 − β, feasible exactly
when β ≤ 35, that is b ≥ 7.5/35 = 3/14 m. At b = 3/14: β = 35, γ = −20, forces (160, 130, 110, 0). At b = 0.20:
β = 37.5, and keeping the lightest foot non-negative forces the heaviest to 165 N. At b = 1/4: β = 30, γ = −20
gives (155, 135, 105, 5); passive equal springs cannot apply an internal load, so γ = 0 and the forces are
(175, 115, 85, 25).

## Scope

In: demo inputs, `demos/ns-13/demo.yaml`, the CPU script (M1) and the at-scale addendum (M4). Out: the mission
engine, checkers, bundle writer, auditor, revalidation.

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** A mission author; an auditor on a second machine.
2. **Inputs.** `stance.fmission`: the family (b ∈ [0.10, 0.40] m), the load, the centre-of-mass rectangle, the
   force bounds, the authority model `commanded`, and the quantifier prefix "for every centre-of-mass position
   there exist foot forces"; an amendment file changing the authority model to `passive-equal-springs`.
3. **Steps and what the author sees.**
   a. `frob mission check stance.fmission --json` → admitted, with the quantifier order echoed.
   b. `frob mission synthesize stance.fmission --json` → `inner: b ≥ 3/14` with four vertex witnesses (one per
      corner of the centre-of-mass rectangle; the worst corner's allocation is (160, 130, 110, 0) N);
      `excluded: b < 3/14` with the dual multipliers; `unresolved: ∅`; `reserve: 0` on two named constraints at
      the boundary; class Certified for both cells.
   c. `frob mission show <answer> --set excluded --at b=0.20 --json` → the witness: one foot would need 165 N.
   d. `frob prove bundle <answer> --out stance.frobcert`; on a host with no GPU and no project checkout:
      `frob-verify stance.frobcert` → accepted in exact rational arithmetic; flags false.
   e. Apply the amendment and revalidate → the inner cell is withdrawn; at b = 1/4 m the report shows the
      spring forces (175, 115, 85, 25) N against 160 N, and lists what must be re-solved with its cost.
   f. `frob mission synthesize` on the amended mission → the new boundary for passive springs (the heaviest
      foot carries 100 + 45 + β ≤ 160, so β ≤ 15 and b ≥ 1/2 m: no design in the family [0.10, 0.40] qualifies,
      and the answer is an exclusion with its witness).
4. **Lanes.** `cpu` is the whole M1 scenario (exact arithmetic, seconds). The M4 addendum (non-blocking,
   recorded when the scale beads close): the same query as one 1,024-design family batch with labels identical
   on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal, and bundles re-checked on a second machine.
5. **Demo file.** Claims: the claim identifiers of GATE-AUGUR-02, GATE-AUGUR-05 and GATE-LEMMA-04;
   `fresh_agent: true` (the amendment task is among the scripted fresh-agent tasks).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The boundary is exactly 3/14 m; the witnesses and the excluded cell's dual are accepted by `frob-verify`
      built from an empty environment on a host with no GPU; bundle digests are identical on two hosts.
- [ ] At b = 0.20 m the exclusion witness shows 165 N; at b = 1/4 m the commanded allocation is
      (155, 135, 105, 5) N.
- [ ] The passive-spring amendment withdraws the inner set and reports (175, 115, 85, 25) N at b = 1/4 m; no
      inner cell is carried across the amendment.
- [ ] 11,520 of 11,520 grid states agree with an independent classifier (gate TARGET), and lifted and projected
      forms agree.
- [ ] An unresolved cell is never reported as a verdict; CLI and MCP agree.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`demos.rs`: `ns13_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns13_boundary_is_3_over_14` (exact rational); `ns13_corner_witness`
((160, 130, 110, 0) sums to 400 and balances both moments at b = 3/14); `ns13_b_0_20_needs_165`;
`ns13_b_quarter_commanded_and_passive` ((155, 135, 105, 5); (175, 115, 85, 25));
`ns13_passive_boundary_is_one_half`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_13.sh`. Logged per STD-E2E: mission digest, quantifier prefix, cells with exact rational bounds,
witness vectors, dual multipliers, bundle digest, auditor verdict and check time, the withdrawn cells after the
amendment with the reason, the evidence block; `summary` naming GATE-AUGUR-02, -05, -07 and GATE-LEMMA-04.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-019, KA-020, KA-021, KA-022. Negative controls (registered by N00-33): a witness (160, 130, 105, 5) that
breaks moment balance; a perturbed dual multiplier; b = 3/14 − 1/1000 offered as inner; an inner set carried
across the amendment; a bundle with its assumption list removed or its quantifier changed. Determinism: class
D0; identical bundle bytes twice on one machine and across platforms.

## Considerations

- On this small plant a standard lifted linear program returns the same answer in one solve; the scenario
  claims a kind of answer and its audit trail, not solve speed.
- The authority model is part of the claim: the same body is feasible under commanded forces and infeasible
  under passive springs.
