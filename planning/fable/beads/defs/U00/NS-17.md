## Background

**Before.** An agent submits fifty candidates against the same held-out scenes until one passes. Each
comparison is valid alone and the campaign is not.

**After.** `frob eval qualify` pre-commits the candidate, the baseline, the mission and the metrics. Submission
i runs on fresh sealed world clusters at level α_i = α / [i·(i + 1)], a series that sums to α. The discovery
lane cannot read those worlds, and only the decision and an aggregate receipt come back until the campaign
closes. The gate behind the story drives the lane with 500 adaptive retries on worlds that carry no signal and
requires a false-promotion rate of at most α, with power preserved on planted gains.

**What it demonstrates.** Discovery cannot read confirmation worlds. Two lanes exist: an open lane for search,
tuning and training, and a sealed lane for confirmation. Three mechanisms make the sealed lane's error rate
hold under adaptive retries: *pre-commitment* (the request is hashed before any sealed world is drawn, so the
question cannot be changed after seeing an answer); *fresh clusters per submission* (no sealed world is ever
used twice, so nothing can be learned about them); and a *shrinking error budget* (the levels α/2, α/6, α/12, …
sum to α·m/(m + 1) after m submissions, always below α). A capability firewall keeps sealed fields out of every
verb's output.

## Scope

In: demo inputs, `demos/ns-17/demo.yaml`, the script (a short adversarial campaign through the real verb). Out:
the lane, the firewall, the 2,000-campaign gate simulation.

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** A design agent (untrusted, adaptive); a lane administrator holding the `qual:admin` scope.
2. **Inputs.** A no-signal pair (candidate identical in distribution to the baseline) and a planted-gain pair
   (a true improvement of the declared size); a mission with metrics; α = 0.05; a scripted adversary that reads
   every receipt and all discovery data and retries with whatever looks best; for the control of step (h), the
   absolute no-signal world of the gate (`synthetic:null-absolute/1`: every candidate fails with probability
   0.055 against a claim of at most 0.05), the world on which an unblinded lane is caught (N24b-18).
3. **Steps and what the agent sees.**
   a0. The administrator opens the campaign: `frob eval ledger --action open --task M --baseline-class <class of
      B> --world-family <the sealed family> --json` (N24b-15's `admin` action, selected only with `--action` and
      carrying no idempotency key: `--key` on this verb is FROB-REF-802; the agent's own attempt is refused with
      FROB-REF-720, exit code 5).
   a. `frob session open --scopes qual:run --key ns17-s-1 --json` → a session without `qual:admin`.
   b. `frob eval qualify C1 --task M --claim '<the claim on energy_per_metre and success>' --baseline B --key
      ns17-q-1 --json` (the registered form: the design positional, resolved to the candidate with its frozen
      controller; task, claim, optional baseline, key) →
      a receipt with BD-259's fields and no others: the campaign, the submission index i = 1, the level
      α₁ = α/2 (`0.05/2`), the spend `0.05*1/2`, the decision (`promoted`, `not-promoted` or `inconclusive`), the
      units used, the list of what stays sealed, the receipt hash (which covers the request's commitment hash and
      the Merkle root of the clusters, so no cluster is named) and the three authorization flags, all false; no
      count, interval or e-value.
   c. `frob eval ledger <campaign> --json` → the index, levels spent (cumulative α·i/(i + 1)) and
      receipts; nothing sealed.
   d. The adversary retries 500 times on the no-signal pair with adaptively chosen variants (one key per
      request, `ns17-adv-<n>`) → the script counts
      promotions (expected: none or almost none in one campaign; the gate's statistical statement needs 2,000
      campaigns and is not made here).
   e. On the planted-gain pair → promoted within 10 requests (the gate requires this in at least 80% of
      campaigns).
   f. Attempts to read sealed material: `frob telemetry query <sealed run> --stream contact --json`, `frob
      artifact read <sealed cluster> --json`, `frob sim replay --run <sealed run> --world <w> --key ns17-rp-1
      --json` → each refused (FROB-REF-840 where the scope cannot read sealed clusters before the campaign
      closes); the firewall audit lists the
      attempts.
   g. Changing the candidate after commitment and resubmitting under the same idempotency key → refused as a
      conflict (FROB-REF-803, exit code 7); executing a request whose bytes differ from its commitment →
      FROB-REF-841.
   h. The control: the same adversary against an **unblinded** harness (the same held-out worlds reused at a
      constant level: MR-13's mutation, built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-arena/nc-<nnnn>`, never a
      product build) on `synthetic:null-absolute/1` → a false promotion appears quickly (each retry is a fresh
      test of size 0.0132, so the first false promotion comes near request 76 on average and within 500 with
      probability 0.9987); the script prints both outcomes side by side. (On a paired no-signal world the same
      harness is caught far less often, about 3% of 500-retry campaigns on the gate's `paired-equal` world at 64
      clusters, because the paired rule is conservative and the reused baseline outcomes are frozen; that is why
      the control runs on the absolute world.)
   i. The administrator closes the campaign (`frob eval ledger <campaign> --action close --json`, no key): the
      key is revealed, the read attempts of (f) now succeed on the revealed data, and the family is retired.
4. **Lanes.** `cpu` (reduced, the demo file): steps (a0) to (c), (f), (g) and (i) with small clusters on
   `batch-cpu`, and 20 retries. `long`: 500 retries and the control.
5. **Under the fallback** (the lane fails its gate): confirmation is fixed-n exact with a hard cap on
   submissions, and nothing is called qualified.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Receipts carry only the listed fields; the level of submission i equals α / [i·(i + 1)] and the ledger's
      cumulative level equals α·i/(i + 1).
- [ ] No sealed field is returned by any verb in the scripted read attempts; each attempt is refused and
      audited.
- [ ] The planted gain is promoted within 10 requests in the long lane; the unblinded control shows a false
      promotion on the absolute no-signal world, and its first false-promotion index is printed.
- [ ] A changed request under a used idempotency key is refused (FROB-REF-803, exit code 7); a request that
      differs from its commitment is refused (FROB-REF-841).
- [ ] The campaign is opened and closed only by the administrator's session; after the close the read attempts
      of step (f) succeed on the revealed data.
- [ ] The words "qualified at level α_i on sealed clusters ⟨identifier⟩" appear only for promoted requests.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`demos.rs`: `ns17_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns17_level_series` (α = 1/20: 1/40, 1/120, 1/240; cumulative after 3
submissions 3/80); `ns17_receipt_expected_fields_only`; `ns17_expected_refusals_for_sealed_reads`;
`ns17_control_expectation` (1 − (1 − 0.01319)⁵⁰⁰ = 0.9987 and mean first index 1/0.01319 ≈ 76 on the absolute
world; 0.034 for the paired no-signal world at 64 clusters, below what a demo can show);
`ns17_open_close_admin_only` (the agent's open is FROB-REF-720).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_17.sh --lane cpu|long`. Logged per STD-E2E: each command line with its key, exit code and wall
time; per submission the index, level, receipt hash,
decision and units used (never per-world data, and no count the receipt does not carry); cumulative level; refused read attempts with their
codes; the control's build features, world and first false promotion index; the open and close records; the
post-close reads; `summary` naming GATE-QUAL-01 and GATE-QUAL-02.

### Known answers and negative controls
Σ_{i=1..m} α/[i·(i + 1)] = α·m/(m + 1). Gate thresholds (TARGETs): over 2,000 no-signal campaigns with 500
retries each, a false promotion in at most 123; a planted gain promoted within 10 requests in at least 80%;
10⁴ fuzzed read attempts return no sealed field. Negative controls: the unblinded procedure; resubmission to
reused clusters; a receipt with an extra per-world field; a planted leak path that the audit must find.

## Considerations

- Late submissions are expensive by design (the level at i = 10 is α/110); the receipt prints the cost of the
  next request so an agent can decide whether to spend it.
- A qualification campaign can be replayed by the offline auditor (levels, commitments, cluster derivation) at
  M5.
