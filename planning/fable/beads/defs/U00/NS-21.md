## Background

**Before.** Every report says `reality: no-claim`.

**After.** Bench measurements of one quadruped on one floor are imported with their provenance. Predictions for
the held-out episodes are frozen before the comparison. For that robot and task family, Ground reports a cost
of transport of 0.62 with a band of [0.55, 0.71] at 90% nominal coverage from 20 calibration episodes, and
states that coverage at this sample size varies by about six points. The same question about a design that was
never built returns a refusal that names the missing calibrated family. The numbers 0.62 and [0.55, 0.71] are
illustrative.

**What it demonstrates.** The one legitimate way to say something about reality. Agreement with another
simulator or with a published figure is Parity. A Reality claim needs imported measurements with their own
provenance, a prediction whose digest predates the opening of the data, and a *calibrated family*: one built
robot, one task family, one set of conditions. Inside it a *conformal band* is issued: from n calibration
episodes, the interval whose half-width is the ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉-th smallest absolute residual covers a new
exchangeable episode with probability at least 1 − α on average (for n = 20 and 90%: the 19th of 20 residuals,
nominal coverage 19/21). Outside the family the answer is a refusal.

## Scope

In: demo inputs (the synthetic rehearsal; real data only when admitted), `demos/ns-21/demo.yaml`, the script.
Out: ingestion, clock fitting, identification, frozen predictions, bands, drift detection.

## Technical approach

1. **Actors.** A bench engineer with logs; an agent asking how far to trust a simulated figure.
2. **Inputs.** CPU lane: a **synthetic** dataset generated by the referee under a pinned seed with planted
   parameters and noise (labelled `synthetic` everywhere; it supports no Reality claim); 20 calibration episodes
   (sealed as the `validation` part: they supply the band's residuals) and 10 held-out episodes (the `holdout`
   part) of a quadruped walking on one floor; a second, unbuilt design. Real lane: an admitted
   dataset with its manifest.
3. **Steps and what the engineer sees.**
   a. `frob ground ingest <dataset> --design <built design> --task-family <walk-flat family> --key ns21-gin-1
      --json` (BD-387's form: the design and task family with the manifest determine the cell key, and a call
      that leaves a component undetermined is refused under FROB-REF-873) → admission: the manifest (robot,
      floor, instruments, units), the cell, fitted clocks, the calibration, validation and sealed holdout splits,
      and the provenance of every channel.
   b. `frob ground predict --dataset <dataset> --part validation --cell <walk-flat cell of the built design>
      --model <ModelId> --scene <floor scene> --task walk-flat --metric cost_of_transport:0.90 --decision <spec>
      --study fixed-n:20 --key ns21-gp-0 --json` for the 20 calibration episodes, and the same with
      `--part holdout --study fixed-n:10 --key ns21-gp-1` for the 10 held-out episodes (the form of BD-392; a
      mutate verb) → two frozen predictions, each with a digest and timestamp; both parts are still sealed. (A
      band's calibration set holds only residuals emitted by scoring frozen predictions, N30a-36, so the 20
      calibration episodes are predicted and scored like any sealed data.)
   c. `frob ground score <validation prediction> --key ns21-gs-0 --json` → opens the 20 calibration episodes
      once, scores them, puts their 20 residuals in the cell's pool, and records that the prediction's digest
      predates the opening.
   d. `frob ground band --cell <walk-flat cell of the built design> --metric cost_of_transport --level 0.90
      --json` (the form of BD-393) → the point prediction, the band at 90% nominal coverage from n = 20, the
      statement that coverage at this n varies by about six points, the calibrated cell's identity and the
      monitor states (exchangeability alarm quiet).
   e. Every report on this design and family now prints the band's reality line: on the real lane
      `reality: calibrated [cell=… n=20 level=0.90 band=… dataset=…]`, on the CPU lane
      `reality: synthetic [dataset=…]` (the grammar of BD-139); every other report still prints
      `reality: no-claim`.
   f. `frob ground score <holdout prediction> --key ns21-gs-1 --json` → the 10 held-out episodes scored against
      their frozen intervals and the holdout retired; the log states how many of the 10 fall inside the band of
      step d (about 9 expected; ten episodes are not asserted against a rate).
   g. `frob ground band --cell <the same cell key with the unbuilt design> --metric cost_of_transport --level
      0.90 --json` → refused with FROB-REF-895 (a band for a design that was never built), naming the missing
      calibrated family and carrying the simulation's own interval (exit code 3).
   h. Episodes on a floor condition the calibration never saw (recorded conditions outside the cell's
      envelope), ingested with `frob ground ingest <out-of-envelope episodes> --design <built design>
      --task-family <walk-flat family> --key ns21-gin-2 --json` → admitted, and the scope check retires the calibration at once (`ScopeChange(Envelope)`,
      N30a-40: deterministic, not left to the monitors, which at n = 20 see only gross shifts); the next band
      request is refused with FROB-REF-897 and no band is issued.
   i. A second `frob ground score <holdout prediction> --key ns21-gs-2 --json` (a fresh key, so the stored
      answer of step f is not simply returned) on the retired holdout → refused (FROB-REF-886, reuse of a spent
      holdout).
4. **Lanes.** `cpu`: the synthetic rehearsal end to end (the demo file). The real lane runs only on admitted
   data and is the import lane's record.
5. **Without admitted data** the scenario is demonstrated on the rehearsal and labelled so; the Reality gate
   rests at `no-claim`, recorded as the state and not as a pass. Under the band kill gate, reports print the
   raw residual table with `reality: no-claim`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each prediction digest's timestamp precedes the opening of the sealed data it predicts, and the record
      proves it.
- [ ] The band is issued for the built design in its family with n = 20 (the residuals of step c), nominal
      coverage and the finite-sample caveat; on the rehearsal, mean coverage is within 0.01 of 19/21 (gate
      TARGET, measured by the band battery N30a-39).
- [ ] The unbuilt design (895), the out-of-envelope episodes (retirement, then 897) and the retired holdout
      (886) each produce a refusal or a retirement, never a band.
- [ ] Every report prints its `reality:` line; with the synthetic dataset the lines on this design and family
      read `synthetic [dataset=…]` and every other line `no-claim`; neither is a Reality claim.
- [ ] Logs contain digests and aggregates only, no measurement data.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`demos.rs`: `ns21_demo_yaml_valid`; `ns21_conformal_rank_19_of_20` (n = 20, α = 0.1 → ⌈21 × 0.9⌉ = 19; nominal
19/21 = 0.905); `ns21_band_from_fixture_residuals` (sorted absolute residuals whose 19th value is 0.08 give a
half-width of 0.08); `ns21_band_counts_only_scored_residuals` (the pool holds the 20 residuals of the scored
validation prediction; before step c the band request is refused with 889, 0 of 19);
`ns21_expected_refusal_for_unbuilt_design` (895); `ns21_out_of_envelope_retires` (897 on the next request);
`ns21_synthetic_label_required`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ns_21.sh`. Logged per STD-E2E: each command line with its key, exit code and wall time, dataset
digest and provenance summary, split sizes, prediction
digest and its timestamp against the data-opening time, residual quantiles, the band with n and nominal
coverage, alarm states, refusal codes, the `reality:` line of each report; `summary` naming GATE-GROUND-01, -06
and -10.

### Known answers and negative controls
19/21 = 0.9048; the pilot's probe that was right in 675 of 675 in-class cases and in 0 of 225 on an omitted law
(the reason a condition outside the calibrated family gets no band, step h). Negative controls: a prediction frozen after opening; a refit with no version change;
holdout reuse; a band requested for a pooled, shifted regime (must be flagged); a Reality line resting on a
card field marked `assumed` (refused).

## Considerations

- A band is a statement about one calibrated family at one sample size; it does not transfer to a sibling
  design, another floor or another gait.
- Bench data may be private; nothing leaves the machine and logs carry no measurements.
