## (e) Contradictions, gaps and what was done

1. **Pilot ledger path.** The drafting brief names `registries/pilots.toml`; the plan (§38.5) places the pilot
   register at `frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`. N00-07 uses the plan's location and says the short
   spelling refers to the same file. The lead should make sure a Gauntlet-unit bead does not create a second
   file.
2. **Reversal conditions of REFUTED hypotheses.** §0.8 says a REFUTED row is reopened only by the reversal
   condition recorded with the verdict, but prints one only for H-04. N00-11 and N00-12 record proposed
   defaults for H-09, H-15 and H-16, labelled as proposals for the owner; until confirmed the rows read "no
   reversal recorded: not reopened".
3. **BET-11 against KILL-LEMMA.** The bet's falsifier is coverage below 80%; the kill gate fires below 50% after
   one repair cycle. No rule is stated for 50% to 80%. BET-11 reads the band as "falsified, kill gate not
   fired: certified kernels keep certificates, the rest carry randomized tests", flagged as a reading. The same
   pattern (bet threshold tighter than kill threshold) occurs for BET-16 (20% against 60%) and BET-18 (0.70
   against 0.5) and is handled the same way.
4. **BET-10 wording.** The falsifier says "at most half the restricted mean time" and, in the same sentence, a
   one-sided 95% lower bound on the ratio above 1.5; GATE-ATEL-03 says a factor of at least 1.5. BET-10 uses
   the lower bound above 1.5 as the test.
5. **Unmeasured rungs.** One passage (§38.12) says an unmeasured stratum is `discard-only`, another (§43.7)
   says `uninformative` until a record exists, and GATE-CORTEX-12 says a rung without a record is refused.
   BET-17 uses the fail-closed reading (`uninformative`) and flags it.
6. **"Comparable cost" in BET-19** has no number in §4. BET-19 registers "within a factor of 1.5 at equal
   checked scope", borrowed from the tie rule of BET-14; a DESIGN choice to confirm.
7. **Bets read at an off-critical-path milestone.** §40.5 lists BET-30 at "M4, M8" and BET-36 at "M3, M6".
   Each has one bead at the critical-path milestone (M4, M3); the later reading (the Moonshot tree policy; the
   proxy pilot and advected films) is a non-blocking addendum through `related`, so that no bead depends on a
   later milestone's bead. Likewise NS-13 is an M1 bead with its M4 at-scale reading as an addendum, and BET-20
   is at M3 (as §40.5 has it) with the gate's M4 part as an addendum.
8. **Bead milestones for bets with several readings** (BET-03, -10, -13, -14, -17, -19, -23, -24, -25, -33,
   -34): the bead sits at the last critical-path reading and its acceptance lists the earlier ones.
9. **Flag and file spellings.** Feature-flag names in N00-23, several command-line flags in the North-Star
   beads, registry file names under `registries/` and `crates/frob-conformance/registry/`, and the test names
   are DESIGN choices of the conversion; the verb registry and the workspace's feature table are the
   authority, and each bead says so where it matters.
10. **Derived arithmetic added by the conversion** (labelled in the beads): the derivations of the stance and
    docking numbers (NS-13, NS-22, N00-33) reproduce every printed value; the passive-spring boundary b ≥ 1/2 m
    and the family range [0.10, 0.40] m in NS-13 are additions; the composite split 300 s of 354 s in N00-18 is
    a reading of "about 85% of 5.9 minutes". The first-request episode count in §26.9's table (29,956) is at
    level 0.05, not at α₁ = α/2; BET-21 states both figures with their levels.
11. **Fixture details the chapters leave open**: the third-gain docking statement ("aliases at an error radius
    of 1/64 … every label that was feasible reverses") depends on the grid's cell definitions, which belong to
    the docking-family bead; NS-22 quotes the fact and does not fix the grid.
12. **"No attribution to other plans"** (charter §5) against Appendix K.2, whose owed item is a diff of two
    named plan documents. N00-36 names the two files and commits because the work cannot be done without them,
    and attributes no idea to them. N00-05 keeps the review log's counts and methods without naming authors.
13. **Planning-stage review beads** (N00-30, -31, -32, -34, -36) do not depend on `STD-E2E`, so that they can
    run before any change of scope; every other bead with a script depends on it.
14. **Size.** 100 beads is above the typical 20 to 45 because the brief fixes 62 keyed beads (one per bet, one
    per scenario, the root epic) before any registry or rule bead.
