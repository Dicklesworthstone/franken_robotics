## Background

The known-answer suite lists cases whose truth a test can compare against. The chapter of mathematical
constructions contributes 154 rows (KA-037 to KA-190), of which 47 were printed with tolerance `to set` and 72
with negative control `none stated`; a row in either state is an obligation and cannot support a gate. This
bead is the registry for the first fifteen constructions, C-01 to C-15: 55 rows, KA-037 to KA-091. Parts 2
and 3 (N10-KA2, N10-KA3) cover C-16 to C-27 and C-28 to C-40.

For these 55 rows the missing tolerances and controls are **set here**, in agreement with the specification
beads `SPEC-C-01` … `SPEC-C-15` (and N10a-10, N10a-11), each of which carries the derivation. A cell marked
"(set)" was printed `to set` or `none stated` in the suite and is filled by this unit; every such value is a
DESIGN proposal with status TARGET until the owning gate records a measurement.

Rules every row obeys: a derivation file on record under `docs/derivations/`; a tolerance with a reason
(`exact` for integers, rationals, Booleans, verdicts and digests); a negative control that is a mutation or a
planted failure, never a boundary behaviour; a tier and a site, the row running first on the weakest site
that can decide it; values carried as printed and never improved in transit. A case stated by a construction
and again by the subsystem that implements it is one fixture with two rows: this one tests the mathematics on
the host, the other tests the subsystem on its tiers, and they share one derivation file.

Sites: `host` (exact or double-precision arithmetic in a test), `auditor` (the offline checker), `referee`,
`lockstep` (the throughput tiers), `all tiers`. Tiers: G0 laws, G1 known answers, G2 parity, G3 metamorphic,
G5 determinism.

## Scope

In: the 55 records in `frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml` with every field; tolerances and controls
for every row printed without one; the derivation-file list; the map from rows to owning specification beads
and gates.

Out: the tests themselves (each specification bead's law suite); the registry schema and runner (Gauntlet
beads); rows of other constructions.

## Technical approach

Each row becomes one record `{id, where, case, truth, tolerance, tier, site, control[], derivation, owner,
due}`; `owner` is the specification bead that carries the derivation and the test. Ulp figures below are the
law gate's: at most 64 ulp in `f64` and 16 ulp in `f32` for single operations.

      control, derivation, owner and due milestone.

      printed value was altered.

      at the milestone against its truth with its control applied and caught; rows not yet due print as
      pending with their milestone.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `ka1_records_complete`: 55 identifiers, contiguous, unique; fields non-empty.
- `ka1_no_open_obligations`: zero `to-set`, zero empty controls in this range.
- `ka1_controls_resolve`: each control identifier resolves and names at least one gate.
- `ka1_printed_values_unchanged`: the truth text of each row equals the committed baseline hash of the suite's
  printed form (values are not improved in transit).
- `ka1_shared_fixtures`: rows marked as sharing a fixture point to one derivation file.
- The numeric assertions themselves live in the law suites of the owning specification beads; this bead's
  tests are on the registry.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ka_constructions_part1.sh`. Sources the STD-E2E helper. Runs the registry tests, then the G0 and
G1 runners filtered to C-01 … C-15, each row with its control. Log
`target/e2e-logs/ka_constructions_part1/<utc>.ndjson` with transcript: per row the command, exit code, wall

## Considerations

- The values set here are proposals. A specification bead may tighten or loosen one with a reason in its
  derivation file; the registry then follows the derivation file, which is the authority for a row's
  tolerance.
- A control that has never failed its gate in any recorded run is suspect and is reviewed at each milestone
  exit.
- Rows KA-085 and KA-066 run on devices and depend on the determinism class each device's arithmetic probe
  assigns; their tolerances are stated per class.
