## Background

The known-answer suite lists cases whose truth a test can compare against; each row has a case, a truth, a
tolerance, a tier and execution site, and a negative control that must make it fail. The registry file is the
authority; numbers are never reused or renumbered. The chapter of mathematical constructions contributes 154
rows; this bead is the registry for constructions C-16 to C-27: 50 rows, KA-092 to KA-141. Part 1 (N10-KA1)
covers C-01 to C-15 and part 3 (N10-KA3) covers C-28 to C-40.

The constructions of this part: C-16 a gait as a hybrid orbit (saltation, return maps, labeled derivatives);
C-17 a mission as a quantified set of designs; C-18 the assumption lattice and verdict polarity; C-19
information with a clock (causal policies and indistinguishability); C-20 observability as a Gramian modulo a
gauge; C-21 designs canonical up to the similarity group; C-22 a campaign quotiented by symmetry orbits; C-23
a family policy as a section, transported by edits; C-24 a geometric-algebra policy on the kinematic tree;
C-25 fidelity tiers as coupled estimators; C-26 rungs trusted by rank agreement and conformal margins; C-27
rare failures (bounded weights certify, splitting explores).

A row printed with tolerance `to set` or negative control `none stated` is an obligation, not a test: it
cannot support a gate until the missing part is registered. For this part every such cell is set by the
specification bead of the construction or by one of its part beads, and this registry carries the owner's value
marked "(set: <bead>)"; a row with an open cell is not counted as passing and supports no gate (the
fail-closed default).

Rules every row obeys: a derivation file under `docs/derivations/`; a tolerance with a reason (`exact` for
integers, rationals, Booleans, verdicts and digests); a control that is a mutation or planted failure, never a
boundary behaviour; a tier and a site; values carried as printed and not improved in transit; one fixture
identity per physical case (a case stated by a construction and again by its subsystem is one fixture with
two rows and one derivation file).

Sites: `host`, `auditor` (the offline checker), `referee`, `all tiers`. Tiers: G0 laws, G1 known answers, G3
metamorphic, G5 determinism.

## Scope

In: the 50 records with every printed field; the owner-set cells with the bead that set each; KA-096's entry in
the shared derivation file `docs/derivations/contact-strata.md` and KA-838's `same_as` (below); the registry
tests.

Out: derivations, tests and the setting of open tolerances and controls (the specification beads of C-16 to
C-27 and their part beads N10b-02, N10b-06, N10b-08, N10b-09 and N10b-14); the registry schema and runners
(Gauntlet beads).

## Technical approach

Each row becomes one `[[ka]]` row of `crates/frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml` in the row schema
that N38a-12 owns (`id`, `section`, `fixture`, `same_as`, `case`, `truth`, `tolerance`, `tier`, `sites`,
`control`, `derivation`, `label`, `due`, `printed`), read by the test kit's one parser. The columns map as: "C"
→ `section`; "Tier · site" → `tier`, `sites`; "Negative control" → `control.text` (the owner's text for a cell
marked "(set: <bead>)"; empty only for a cell printed `none stated` and not set, of which this part has none),
whose `nc` list N38b-05's seeding fills; a tolerance printed `to set` and not set by its owner → `to-set` (none in
this part). The owning
specification bead `SPEC-C-nn` is named, with the test's crate and name, in the front matter of the row's
derivation file, which lists the KA ids it serves (N38a-12's rule 1); the row's own `owner` is the gate that
owns it. An open row is reported by N38a-12's computed eligibility (`obligation: tolerance` or
`obligation: control`), never by a status field of the row. DESIGN, correcting an earlier draft that used a
record of its own (`where`, `site`, `control[]`, `owner = SPEC-C-nn`).
