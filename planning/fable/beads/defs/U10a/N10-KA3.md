## Background

The known-answer suite lists cases whose truth a test can compare against; each row has a case, a truth, a
tolerance, a tier and execution site, and a negative control that must make it fail. The registry file is the
authority; numbers are never reused or renumbered. The chapter of mathematical constructions contributes 154
rows; this bead is the registry for constructions C-28 to C-40: 49 rows, KA-142 to KA-190. Parts 1 and 2
(N10-KA1, N10-KA2) cover C-01 to C-15 and C-16 to C-27.

The constructions of this part: C-28 a failure's minimal counterexample world; C-29 a deterministic forkable
simulator as a causal model; C-30 requirements as temporal logic over unit-normalized predicates; C-31 safety
as an energy budget first and a filter second; C-32 region certificates and dual witnesses of infeasibility;
C-33 clearance certified from both sides; C-34 linkage synthesis (an exact bounded lane and a continuation
lane); C-35 structure and life (topology, fatigue, tolerance); C-36 the trapezoidal variational step and the
discrete nonholonomic step; C-37 the four-dimensional contact cone and joint-level rows; C-38 actuators as
port-Hamiltonian components with a thermal node; C-39 the value of an experiment and the design of a probe;
C-40 dominance as unreachability in a product system.

A row printed with tolerance `to set` or negative control `none stated` is an obligation, not a test. For
this part those cells are carried exactly as printed and assigned to the specification bead of their
construction; the fail-closed default is that the row is not counted as passing and supports no gate.

Rules every row obeys: a derivation file under `docs/derivations/`; a tolerance with a reason; a control that
is a mutation or planted failure, never a boundary behaviour; a tier and a site; values carried as printed;
one fixture identity per physical case.

Sites: `host`, `auditor` (the offline checker), `referee`, `strict` (the batched CPU tier under its strict
profile), `all tiers`. Tiers: G0 laws, G1 known answers, G3 metamorphic, G5 determinism.

## Scope

In: the 49 records with every printed field; the list of open obligations with their owners; the registry
tests.

Out: derivations, tests and the setting of open tolerances and controls (the specification beads of C-28 to
C-40); the registry schema and runners (Gauntlet beads).

## Technical approach

Each row becomes one record `{id, where, case, truth, tolerance, tier, site, control[], derivation, owner,
due}` in `frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml`, with `owner = SPEC-C-nn`.
