## Background

Two doctrine rules govern when work may be skipped. Both exist because skipping is where a fast system lies.

- **D-11, a hash identifies; only an implication licenses skipping a recheck.** A content hash says that two
  artifacts are the same bytes. It never says that a verdict about one transfers to a different one. A
  verdict is reused after a change only under a recorded implication: polarity in the assumption lattice, a
  monotonicity argument, a symmetry together with its stabilizer check, or equality of every dependency. A
  checker rejects an unknown proof kind or a changed quantifier even when every hash is consistent.
- **D-14, symmetry is used only where it holds.** A symmetry of the mechanism is intersected with the
  stabilizers of payload, gravity, task, observation and contact mode before it reduces any calculation.
  Augmenting data with a transformation that preserves a distribution and reducing one world by a
  transformation that preserves that world are different operations with different preconditions.

Terms. An identity (design, model, scene, kernel, policy, episode) is a content hash that names bytes and
carries no verdict. Polarity: an inclusion (a design proved feasible) survives the world set shrinking, the
policy class growing and the acceptance condition weakening, and must be rechecked on the opposite moves; an
exclusion is the reverse. The mechanical symmetry group preserves the body; the observation-preserving
subgroup also preserves sensors, payload, gravity and every task term; the stabilizer of a context also fixes
one particular world's terrain, material field, boundary conditions and current contact mode.

Tier: Solid. The mechanisms (memo graph, revalidation, symmetry computation, certificate checkers) are owned
elsewhere; this bead is the cross-cutting battery at M1, repeated when the later mechanisms land.

## Scope

In: the implication-record check on every reuse path (`d11_*`); the auditor's unknown-kind and
changed-quantifier rejections re-asserted (`d11_checker_*`); the group-requirement check on every consumer of
symmetry (`d14_*`); planted violations.

Out: the memo graph and shadow recompute (BD-111); the invalidation contract of module substitution (BD-077,
specified in N10a-11); the revalidation engine (BD-292); the symmetry computation (BD-102); campaign
quotienting (BD-285).

## Technical approach

**D-11: the implication record.**

1. Closed list of implication kinds (DESIGN names): `dependency-equality` (every dependency of the result,
   by its influence record, is byte-equal); `polarity` (the change moves an assumption in the direction its
   `survives` tag allows); `monotonicity` (a declared monotone relation with the direction of the change);
   `symmetry` (a group element maps the old problem to the new one, with the stabilizer check recorded). An
   unknown kind is rejected.
2. Every code path that returns a previously computed verdict for an input that is not byte-identical to the
   original must attach an implication record `{kind, from, to, evidence}`. The check enumerates the reuse
   call sites through the public reuse interface (there is one: a function that takes the record as a
   required argument, so reuse without a record does not compile) and tests each kind with a fixture.
3. Shadow recompute: on a sampled share of reuses the result is recomputed and compared; a disagreement is a
   failure of the implication, is reported with both results, and becomes a permanent control.
4. Refusals: FROB-REF-835, a verdict is reused after a change without a recorded implication (repairs:
   recompute; or supply the implication with its evidence). In the family dispatch layer, FROB-REF-162, a
   cached result has no recorded dependency digest, or the shadow recompute disagreed.
5. The auditor: a bundle with an unknown proof kind and consistent hashes is rejected; a bundle with a changed
   quantifier and consistent hashes is rejected. The registry of proof kinds is closed and versioned
   (decision ADR-LEMMA-04: consistent hashes do not make an unknown argument valid).

**D-14: the group a consumer may use.** Every consumer of a symmetry declares its use, and the check compares
the group it was given with the group its use requires:

| Use | Group required |
|---|---|
| Metamorphic test of the simulator | the mechanical group, with the world transformed along with the robot |
| Distributional augmentation; weight tying in policies and critics | the observation-preserving subgroup, with the task's world and command distributions invariant in law |
| Deduplicating candidate designs before a sweep | the observation-preserving subgroup of the slot arrangement |
| Gait enumeration | the observation-preserving subgroup intersected with the stabilizer of the command |
| Exact reduction inside one world (invariant critics, symmetric orbit search, merging two worlds, closed-loop block-diagonal linearization) | the stabilizer of the context |
| Block-diagonal mass and stiffness matrices at a symmetric stance | the mechanical group intersected with the stabilizer of the configuration |

The check: (a) the symmetry interface hands out groups as distinct types per requirement, so a consumer
written for one cannot be given another (compile-time); (b) at run time each reduction records the group
element used and the stabilizer check that admitted it; (c) when an edit or a world change removes an element
from the required group, the reduced calculation is replaced by the full one without an author's edit, and
the report names the breaker; (d) a quotient or a weight tie requested under a group that moves a sensor, the
payload or the task is refused: FROB-REF-161, group not observation-preserving (repairs: use the
observation-preserving subgroup the analysis reports; or remove the breaker it names).

Determinism: D0 for the record and group checks. Receipts as for every Gauntlet target.

- [ ] `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0_doctrine -- d11 d14` passes at M1 and is re-run at M3 and M4
      when revalidation, quotienting and symmetric controllers land.
- [ ] Reuse without an implication record does not compile; each of the four kinds has a passing fixture and
      a failing counterpart.
- [ ] The auditor rejects the unknown-kind bundle and the changed-quantifier bundle.
- [ ] Each symmetry consumer present at the milestone declares its use and receives the required group type.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: a memo entry served on hash match with no implication; a dependency graph
      with one edge omitted, which returns a promotion that fresh replay contradicts; an inclusion carried
      across an enlarged world set; an edit that touches a shared bus served a stale thermal product;
      weights tied under the mechanical group of a quadruped with a front-only lidar; a reduced calculation
      kept after a one-sided payload is added; a reduction inside a world with a one-sided slippery patch.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `d11_reuse_requires_record` (compile-fail): calling the reuse interface without a record.
- `d11_dependency_equality`: a result reused when every field in its influence record is unchanged; the same
  with one field changed is recomputed.
- `d11_polarity`: on the docking family, where a robust policy exists exactly when the sensor error ε is
  below (U + ½)/4 for actuator bound U: an inclusion at (U, ε) = (¾, ¼) is reused when ε falls to 1/8 and
  when U rises to 1; an exclusion at (¾, 3/8) is reused when ε rises and when U falls; each of the four
  opposite moves demands a recheck.
- `d11_world_set_enlarged`: adding one hidden gain 33/32 keeps every exclusion and rechecks every inclusion;
  the inclusion at (¾, 1/64) reverses (the reading (65/64)·p is possible under gains 1 and 33/32 for every
  first command p).
- `d11_stale_edge`: the dependency graph with one edge omitted is caught by the shadow recompute.
- `d11_checker_unknown_kind`, `d11_checker_changed_quantifier`: planted bundles through the auditor.
- `d14_group_types` (compile-fail): passing the mechanical group to a weight-tying consumer.
- `d14_front_lidar`: mechanical group C₂ × C₂, observation-preserving subgroup C₂; a tie under the larger
  group is refused with FROB-REF-161 and the lidar mount named as the breaker.
- `d14_payload_breaks_reduction`: adding a one-sided payload switches the consumer to the full calculation.
- `d14_augmentation_vs_reduction`: a world distribution that is mirror-invariant in law permits augmentation;
  a single world with a one-sided patch refuses reduction inside it.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/doctrine_licences.sh`. Sources the STD-E2E helper. Steps: the two families; then through the real
binary: `frob analyze symmetry <front-lidar quadruped> --json` (asserting both groups and the breaker);
`frob module substitute <design> --at <leaf path> --with <module@hash> --expected-parent <DesignId> --key
dl-mirror --json` for a mirror swap (reuse reported with implication `symmetry`) and the same verb with its own
key `dl-bus` for a bus edit (global invalidation): `module.substitute` is a `mutate` verb, so each call carries
its key and the expected parent (VERBS-module); `frob-verify --json <bundle>` under `env -i` on the two planted
bundles (both rejected). Log
`target/e2e-logs/doctrine_licences/<utc>.ndjson` with transcript: commands, exit codes, wall times, the
implication records seen, group orders, refusal codes with repairs, evidence blocks, digests of reused and
recomputed results, and on failure the reuse site or consumer at fault. Final `summary` line.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-079 (a mirror swap of a leaf reuses its siblings' results; a shared-bus edit invalidates every global
  result); control: one stale result after an edit.
- KA-103 (polarity on the docking family, values above) and KA-104 (world set enlarged by gain 33/32:
  exclusions kept, inclusions rechecked, the inclusion at (¾, 1/64) reverses; the least worst-case terminal
  error becomes 1/65 m); control: an inclusion carried across the enlargement.
- KA-106 (stale-verdict control: a dependency graph with one edge omitted returns a promotion that fresh
  replay contradicts, and the check catches it).
- KA-070 (front-lidar quadruped: C₂ × C₂ and C₂) and KA-071 (a one-sided payload removes the reflection and
  the reduced calculation is replaced by the full one).
- CX-031, CX-037, CX-049, CX-050.
- Metamorphic relations: an edit followed by its inverse restores every verdict with zero evaluations (the
  mutation that must break it: a memo key without delivery time); an inclusion survives the world set
  shrinking, the policy class growing and assumptions weakening, and an exclusion the three opposite moves
  (mutation: hash match taken as verdict transfer).

## Considerations

- A correctly keyed dependency graph is itself a sufficient licence (`dependency-equality`); a planning pilot
  found no reuse advantage of a richer receipt over such a graph, so the richer kinds are funded only where
  they license reuse that equality cannot (polarity, symmetry).
- The reuse interface is the choke point; a second path that bypasses it would defeat the check, so the exit
  review searches for caches outside it.
- Symmetry by tolerance (imported designs) is classed `Tested`; exact reduction under a `Tested` symmetry
  inherits that class.
- The later repetitions (revalidation at M3, symmetric controllers at M4) add fixtures to the same test
  families; they do not add new rules.
