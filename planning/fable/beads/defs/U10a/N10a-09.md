## Background

Doctrine rule **D-16, exploration and certification are separate lanes**: a method that finds failures
quickly (multilevel splitting, adaptive search, a discovery run) issues no bound. A bound comes from a lane
whose validity does not depend on where the search looked: crude sampling, bounded-weight importance
sampling, or a sealed confirmation run that discovery could not read.

Why this needs a check. Adversarial search is the efficient way to find a robot's failures, and it is
tempting to reuse its samples to state a failure rate, or to claim that because a clever sampler found
nothing, failures are rare. Both are invalid: an adaptive search's samples are not draws from the nominal
world distribution, and no sampler discounts the price of certifying absence. The rule separates two kinds
of output by type so that one cannot be passed off as the other.

The arithmetic that makes the rule concrete:

- *Zero-failure bound.* n independent nominal episodes with zero failures give p ≤ 1 − δ^(1/n) ≤ ln(1/δ)/n at
  confidence 1 − δ. With δ = 0.05, certifying p* = 10⁻⁴ needs n = ⌈ln 20/p*⌉, about 3·10⁴ episodes.
- *Bounded-weight importance sampling.* The estimate n⁻¹·Σ wᵢ·1[fail] with w = dp/dq ≤ w_max is unbiased with
  terms in [0, w_max]. With a defensive mixture q = β·p + (1 − β)·q′ the weights are at most 1/β, and zero
  observed failures certify only p ≤ ln(1/δ)/(β·n): never better than crude sampling on the nominal share.
- *Splitting.* With L levels of conditional probability p₀ and N trials per level, the product of
  conditional fractions has squared coefficient of variation near L·(1 − p₀)/(p₀·N); for L = 6, p₀ = 0.1,
  N = 10⁴ that is a coefficient of variation of about 7%. The trials are dependent, so the rate is
  `Estimated` only.
- *Unseen-mode mass* (Good–Turing: failure modes seen once divided by failures seen) is a stopping signal for
  exploration, not a bound.

Tier: Solid for the rule and the certification lanes; the mechanisms are owned by the adversarial-worlds
subsystem and the qualification lane. This bead is the cross-cutting check.

## Scope

In: the type-level separation check (`d16_*`); the lane-provenance check on every output that states a bound;

## Technical approach

1. **Two output types** (DESIGN). `Bound { value, confidence, lane: CertLane, receipts }` with
   `CertLane ∈ { CrudeNominal, BoundedWeight { w_max }, SealedConfirmation { request } }`, and
   `ExplorationFinding { failures, modes, estimate: Option<Estimated>, signal: StoppingSignal }`. There is no
   conversion from `ExplorationFinding` to `Bound`; a compile-fail test pins it. `Bound` has no constructor
   that accepts samples whose proposal distribution was adapted using outcomes of the same sample set.
2. **Lane provenance.** Every verb output that contains a field of kind `bound`, `upper-bound`,
   `failure-rate` with a confidence, or `certified-rate` must name its lane and carry the receipts that
   establish it: for `CrudeNominal`, the nominal draw addresses; for `BoundedWeight`, the proposal's
   definition frozen before sampling, the maximum weight and the exact weights computed from addressed
   draws; for `SealedConfirmation`, the pre-committed request and the sealed cluster identifiers.
3. **Estimates from exploration** may be reported, but only with evidence class `Estimated`, the label
   `exploration`, and no confidence statement of the certification kind.
4. **Read isolation.** A sealed confirmation run draws worlds from clusters the discovery phase could not
   read; the check asserts, on the capability scopes of the two phases, that no discovery-phase principal
   holds a read scope on a sealed cluster, and that a fuzzed set of read attempts returns no sealed field.
5. **Fresh randomness.** A fork at a splitting level must have its own world address and therefore fresh
   random streams; children that share the parent's streams reproduce the parent bit for bit and add nothing,
   and are detected as duplicates and refused.
6. **Arithmetic helpers with tests**: `zero_failure_bound(n, δ) = 1 − δ^(1/n)`;
   `episodes_for_linear_bound(p*, δ) = ⌈ln(1/δ)/p*⌉`; `mixture_bound(n, δ, β) = ln(1/δ)/(β·n)`;
   `splitting_cv2(L, p₀, N) = L·(1 − p₀)/(p₀·N)`. The second helper is the sufficient count from the linear
   bound ln(1/δ)/n, the form in which the doctrine states the price (29,958 at p* = 10⁻⁴, δ = 0.05). It is not
   the exact count, and it is named so that nobody takes it for one: the exact count ⌈ln δ/ln(1 − p*)⌉ = 29,956
   has one product function, `rare::zero_failure::required` (N26a-21), and one conformance oracle,
   `zero_failure_episodes` (N38b-56); this bead adds no third function for it (its test meets 29,956 only as
   the first n at which `zero_failure_bound` falls to 10⁻⁴).

Determinism: the type and provenance checks are D0; the calibration test below is seeded.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0_doctrine -- d16` passes at M3 on the adversarial-worlds verbs.
- [ ] No conversion from an exploration finding to a bound compiles.
- [ ] Every bound-bearing output names one of the three lanes with its receipts; an exploration estimate is
      classed `Estimated` and labelled `exploration`.
- [ ] Arithmetic: δ = 0.05 and p* = 10⁻⁴ give n = ⌈ln 20/p*⌉ = 29,958 episodes (about 3·10⁴); the mixture with β = ½ needs
      twice that; splitting with L = 6, p₀ = 0.1, N = 10⁴ gives a squared coefficient of variation of 0.0054
      and a coefficient of variation of 7.3%.
- [ ] Calibration: on a random walk with a closed-form hitting probability, the crude and bounded-weight
      bounds contain the truth in at least 1 − δ of 10⁴ repetitions.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: a bound constructed from splitting samples; a bound from an adaptive search's
      own samples; a "certified absence" issued by a defensive mixture at the crude-sampling price (claiming
      p ≤ ln(1/δ)/n instead of ln(1/δ)/(β·n)); a fork whose children share the parent's streams; a discovery
      principal holding a read scope on a sealed cluster; an exploration estimate printed with a confidence
      level.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `d16_no_conversion` (compile-fail).
- `d16_lane_required`: synthetic output schemas (labelled `synthetic`) with a bound and no lane fail.
- `d16_zero_failure_arithmetic`: for δ = 0.05 and p* = 10⁻⁴, ln(1/δ)/n ≤ 10⁻⁴ first holds at n = 29,958
  (ln 20 = 2.995732) and fails at n − 1; the exact bound 1 − δ^(1/n) is always below the linear one and first
  falls to 10⁻⁴ at n = 29,956.
- `d16_mixture_price`: β = ½ and zero failures over n episodes certify 2·ln(20)/n.
- `d16_splitting_variance`: 6 × 0.9/(0.1 × 10⁴) = 0.0054.
- `d16_calibration_random_walk`: a simple symmetric random walk started at 1 that must reach 10 before 0 has
  hitting probability 1/10 exactly; crude sampling with n = 1,000 and a bounded-weight scheme with w_max = 4
  give intervals containing 1/10 in at least 95% of 10⁴ repetitions.
- `d16_duplicate_children`: children created without fresh addresses are detected as duplicates.
- `d16_unseen_mode_is_signal`: the Good–Turing figure is exposed only inside an exploration finding.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/doctrine_lanes.sh`. Sources the STD-E2E helper. Steps: the test family; then through the real
binary on a small planted-failure fixture: `frob --json nemesis search … --key d16-s` (asserting the output is
an exploration finding with no bound field), `frob --json nemesis bound … --mode estimate --shift … --epsilon
1/10 --key d16-b` (asserting a bound with lane `BoundedWeight`, the frozen proposal hash, the maximum weight
and the confidence), and an attempt to request a bound from the search's own episodes, `frob --json nemesis
bound … --mode certify --episodes <the search's run set> --key d16-x`, which must be refused with
FROB-REF-560 (exit 3; raised by the lane firewall N26a-23) and draw nothing. Each `…` stands for the inputs the
verb's row requires, written out in the script: the candidate and `--task` for both verbs, `--legality` and
`--budget` for `nemesis search`, and `--tier batch-cpu`, `--target 1e-4` and `--finalist` for `nemesis bound`
(`--tier` has no default; the planted-failure fixture is no finalist of a recorded campaign, so without the
recorded attestation `--finalist` both bound calls would be refused by the finalists-only rule of N26a-26,
`rare-bound-not-finalist`, before the lane firewall is reached). The three are run verbs and each carries its
own idempotency key. Log
`target/e2e-logs/doctrine_lanes/<utc>.ndjson` with transcript: commands, exit codes, wall times, the lane of
every bound, weights against w_max, bounds with their confidence, the evidence block (claim, class, tier,
determinism, seed), and on failure the output that carried a bound without a valid lane. Final `summary`
line.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-138 (zero-failure bound: p ≤ 1 − δ^(1/n) ≤ ln(1/δ)/n; about 3·10⁴ episodes for p* = 10⁻⁴ at δ = 0.05).
- KA-139 (defensive mixture: weights at most 1/β; zero failures certify only p ≤ ln(1/δ)/(β·n)).
- KA-140 (splitting with L = 6, p₀ = 0.1, N = 10⁴: coefficient of variation about 7%).
- KA-141 (random walk with a closed-form hitting probability: the crude and bounded-weight bounds bracket the
  truth in at least 1 − δ of 10⁴ repetitions; control: children that share the parent's streams are detected
  as duplicates).
- CX-065 (a defensive mixture does not make certifying absence cheaper); CX-066 (a fork without fresh
  randomness multiplies nothing).
- Determinism: seeded repetitions reproduce their coverage count exactly.

## Considerations

- OPEN-NEMESIS-3 (owner): a certified failure rate of 10⁻⁶ exceeds the default compute envelope; the
  fail-closed default is to certify to 10⁻⁵ and report 10⁻⁶ as `Estimated`. The check enforces that such a
  figure carries the `Estimated` class.
- The rule also covers studies: a discovery batch that suggested a design keeps its original analysis and is
  not counted again in the confirmatory request.
- Found failures are replayed one tier up before they count; a bound is scoped to its tier.
- Risk: a "hybrid" estimator that looks like bounded-weight sampling but adapts its proposal using the same
  samples. The constructor's precondition (proposal frozen before sampling, by hash and timestamp) is the
  guard.
