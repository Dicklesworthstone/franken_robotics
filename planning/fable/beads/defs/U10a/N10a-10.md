## Background

Construction C-09 gives a robot two finite symmetry groups (the mechanical group Γ_mech and the
observation-preserving subgroup Γ_obs, specified in SPEC-C-09). This bead specifies the second half of the
construction: how those groups act linearly, and how the space of linear maps that commute with the action is
parameterized. This is what turns "the robot is symmetric" into (a) block-diagonal linearized dynamics for the
modes analysis, (b) equivariant layers whose free parameters are exactly the commutant, and (c) a
free-parameter count that is reported to an agent before training.

Decision ADR-MARROW-07: equivariant linear maps are parameterized by real and complex irreducible blocks, and
the Reynolds operator is their oracle. The blocks give exactly the free parameters of the commutant. Reynolds
averaging gives the same subspace and is exact; it is kept as the test, not replaced as an error. A
representation of quaternionic type is refused: no group that arises here has one, so meeting one means the
group computation is wrong. Rejected: averaging after every optimizer step as the production
parameterization (it optimizes in a redundant space and reports no parameter count); a quaternionic-type
implementation (no group in scope needs it; it stays in the rejected register).

Tier: Solid for the decomposition; Frontier for its payoff in learning (a bet tested by the three-arm
experiment). Kill gate for the block parameterization, named in advance: KILL-MARROW-BLOCKS. Trigger: the
block-and-mutation gate fails after one repair cycle, or exact field arithmetic for n ≤ 12 exceeds its bit
budget. Fallback: equivariant layers by Reynolds projection in `f64` with an idempotence test, classed
`Tested`, the free-parameter count read from the projection's rank.

Foundations (FACT, 2026-11-21): no group theory in FrankenSymPy; none of Mathlib's 43 representation-theory
modules accepted by FrankenLean's re-check; `fs-symmetry` covers cyclic groups in about 480 lines. Everything
here is new code in `frob-algebra`.

## Technical approach

**Setting.** Γ a finite group; ρ a real linear representation on ℝᵈ. For the groups of this project every ρ
on a channel space is a signed permutation (one index and one sign per channel per element), so all
arithmetic below is exact over ℚ, or over a real cyclotomic subfield for n-fold rotations.

**The objects.**

- *Isotypic components.* Each representation splits into isotypic components, one per irreducible
  representation that occurs.
- *Isotypic projector.* For an irreducible complex character χ of degree d_χ:
  P_χ = (d_χ/|Γ|)·Σ_g χ(g)*·ρ(g), with * the complex conjugate; for a complex-type character the projector of
  the real irreducible is the sum of P_χ and its conjugate.
- *Reynolds operator.* W ↦ |Γ|⁻¹·Σ_g ρ_out(g)·W·ρ_in(g)⁻¹ projects any linear map onto the equivariant ones
  exactly. With signed permutations it costs |Γ|·d_out·d_in operations.
- *Equivariant maps.* The W with ρ_out(g)·W = W·ρ_in(g) for all g: the commutant.
- *Real types.* Over the reals an irreducible representation has one of three types, read from the
  Frobenius–Schur indicator ι(χ) = |Γ|⁻¹·Σ_g χ(g²):

| ι(χ) | Real type | Real irreducible built from χ | Equivariant maps between m_in and m_out copies | Free parameters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| +1 | Real | χ itself, dimension d_χ | Θ ⊗ I with Θ any real m_out × m_in matrix | m_out·m_in |
| 0 | Complex | χ and its conjugate together, dimension 2·d_χ | blocks [[a, −b], [b, a]] ⊗ I: a complex m_out × m_in matrix | 2·m_out·m_in |
| −1 | Quaternionic | — | refused | — |

- *Dimension formula.* dim Hom_Γ(V_in, V_out) = Σᵢ cᵢ·mᵢ^out·mᵢ^in with cᵢ = 1 for real type and 2 for complex
  type.
- *Which groups have which types.* The groups that occur are cyclic, dihedral, their products and wreath
  products, and their real irreducible representations are of real type or of complex type. Every irreducible
  representation of C₂, C₂ × C₂, the dihedral groups and S₄ is of real type. Complex type occurs for the cyclic groups Cₙ with n ≥ 3 when no mirror is present, the
  symmetry groups of radial designs without a mirror plane (a tri-wheel cluster with handed wheels, a hexapod
  whose legs are all the same chirality). A real-type block is a scalar per copy pair; a complex-type pair is
  parameterized by 2×2 blocks [[a, −b], [b, a]], which is cheaper than averaging and equal to it. No symmetry
  group of a robot in this project has an irreducible of quaternionic type, and none is implemented.

**Algorithm fixed by the contract.**

1. From the character table compute ι for each character and pair each complex-type character with its
   conjugate.
2. For each real irreducible, build a symmetry-adapted orthonormal basis of its isotypic component in the
   input and output spaces by applying the projector (summed with its conjugate for complex type) and the
   transfer operators that align the copies with one another.
3. Store the change-of-basis matrices Q_in and Q_out and the block list. A map is W = Q_out·(⊕ᵢ Θᵢ)·Q_inᵀ with
   Θᵢ the free block of constituent i.
4. Biases are restricted to the fixed subspace of ρ_out. Hidden layers carry copies of the regular
   representation, on which pointwise nonlinearities commute with the group.

Cost: O(d³) once per model and channel space, cached by model identity; at run time a layer is applied in the
block basis or expanded once per optimizer step into a dense W, so inference pays nothing.

**Statements.**

**Fixtures** (exact; hand-checkable).

- *C₃ on three legs.* C₃ acts on ℝ³ by cyclic permutation. The equivariant maps ℝ³ → ℝ³ are the circulant
  matrices, with three free parameters. The space splits as the trivial representation plus one
  two-dimensional real irreducible of complex type, so the formula gives 1 + 2 = 3. A parameterization that
  gives the two-dimensional block a single scalar yields two parameters and spans only the symmetric
  circulants, which are exactly the maps equivariant under D₃: the error silently imposes a mirror symmetry
  the robot does not have, and the map x ↦ (x₂ − x₃, x₃ − x₁, x₁ − x₂), which is equivariant under C₃,
  becomes unrepresentable.
- *Regular representation of C₂ × C₂, 64 copies in and out* (width 256): 64·64·4 = 16,384 free weights against
  65,536 unconstrained.
- *D₆ on six legs* (permutation representation of a radial hexapod): the commutant dimension equals the number
  of orbits of D₆ on ordered pairs of legs, which is 4 (same leg, adjacent, next-adjacent, opposite); the
  decomposition is two one-dimensional and two two-dimensional real-type irreducibles, 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 4.
- *C₂ on a biped's two legs*: commutant dimension 2 (symmetric and antisymmetric).
- *Frobenius–Schur.* For C₃ the two non-trivial characters have ι = 0; for D₃ all have ι = +1; for Q₈ the
  two-dimensional character has ι = −1.

**Refusal.** FROB-REF-214: a representation with a constituent of quaternionic type, or a symmetry group
outside the supported families; returned with the character and its indicator, the group's order and
generators. Repair: none on the consumer's side; the group computation is examined.

**What it does not claim.** The blocks are a parameterization, not a guarantee of sample efficiency; the
learning payoff is a separate bet. An equivariant nonlinear map need not decouple into independent nonlinear
blocks.

**Implementation map.** Crate `frob-algebra::irreps`; verb `algebra.irreps` (reports the decomposition, the
type of each constituent and the free-parameter count for a pair of channel spaces). Gates: GATE-MARROW-02
(TARGET, M1: character tables and isotypic projectors correct for Cₙ and Dₙ with n ≤ 12, their products with
C₂, and S₄, against tables on file); GATE-MARROW-04 (TARGET, M4: the commutant statement holds exactly for
every one of those groups on 100 random pairs of channel spaces each; each of the eight planted single-sign or
single-transpose mutations of the algebraic core's laws is caught by at least three independent fixtures).
The eight are: reversed ad*; Ad* and its inverse exchanged; a pull-back in place of a push-forward; a missing
parity sign on a mirrored axis; linear and angular parts of a twist exchanged; the bracket's arguments
exchanged; the curvature bracket sign reversed; a conjugate missing in the projector. The harness that plants
all eight and counts the fixtures that catch each is N13-18; the first seven are stated by the law suites of
SPEC-C-02, SPEC-C-04, SPEC-C-08 and SPEC-C-09's group part; the eighth's fixtures are specified here. It is
subtle: without the conjugate the code computes
(d_χ/|Γ|)·Σ_g χ(g)·ρ(g), which is exactly the projector of the conjugate character χ*, so it is still
idempotent, still orthogonal to the others and the set of projectors still sums to the identity; R3's first
clauses cannot catch it, and it has no effect on a real-valued character. It is caught only on groups with
non-real characters, by three independent fixtures: the restriction-character clause of R3 on C₃, C₅ and C₇;
the eigenvector fixture (for the generator r of Cₙ and v_j with ρ(r)·v_j = ζʲ·v_j, ζ = e^(2πi/n), P_{χ_j}·v_j =
v_j, where the mutant returns 0); and the comparison of each labelled projector against the oracle table on
file.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `r1_homomorphism`: every pair of elements of C₂ × C₂, D₆ and C₃ acting on joint channels.
- `r2_character_orthonormality`: Cₙ and Dₙ for n ≤ 12, C₂ products, S₄.
- `r3_projectors`: P_χ² = P_χ, P_χ·P_ψ = 0, ρ(g)·P_χ = P_χ·ρ(g), Σ P_χ = I, on permutation and regular
  representations.
- `r3_restriction_character`: tr(ρ(g)·P_χ) = m_χ·χ(g) for every g and χ on the regular representations of
  C₃, C₅ and C₇ (m_χ = 1) and on the permutation representation of C₆ on six legs; `r3_eigenvector`: for
  ρ(r)·v_j = ζʲ·v_j, P_{χ_j}·v_j = v_j for every j on C₃, C₅, C₇. The conjugate-missing mutant passes
  `r3_projectors` and fails both of these and the oracle-table comparison (three fixtures).
- `r4_reynolds_projection`: 100 random rational W per group.
- `r5_commutant_dimension`: exact rank against Σ cᵢ·mᵢ^out·mᵢ^in on 100 random pairs of channel spaces per
  group; containment both ways.
- `r6_complex_block_equals_average`: C₃; for random (a, b) the block map equals the Reynolds average of its
  dense form, and every circulant is reached.
- `r7_quaternionic_refused`: Q₈.
- `r8_block_diagonal`: a random matrix averaged by Reynolds has zero entries between different isotypic
  components in the adapted basis.
- `hand_pilot`: the four commutant dimensions above from the formula against the exact rank.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-067 representation identities: exact; G0 · host. Control (set here): a conjugate missing in the
  projector. The mutant is the projector of the conjugate character, so it stays idempotent and the set still
  sums to the identity; it is caught on C₃ (and C₅, C₇) by the restriction-character clause
  tr(ρ(g)·P_χ) = m_χ·χ(g), by the eigenvector fixture and by the oracle-table comparison.
- Controls from the fixtures: a complex-type block parameterized as real (C₃ loses the antisymmetric
  circulant; dimension 2 instead of 3); a miscounted commutant for the regular representation; Reynolds
  averaging with ρ_in(g) in place of ρ_in(g)⁻¹ (wrong whenever some element is not an involution: caught on C₃
  and on D₆).
- Determinism: exact arithmetic, D0, identical on every platform.

## Considerations

- Bit budget: cyclotomic arithmetic for n ≤ 12 is small; exceeding the budget is a trigger of the kill gate,
  not a reason to drop to floating point silently (FROB-REF-215 names the overflow).
- Ceiling stated before any measurement: a factor equal to the order of Γ_obs in free parameters per layer and
  in trajectories per rollout (2 for a robot with a forward sensor suite, 4 for a blind fore-aft-symmetric
  quadruped, 12 for a blind radial hexapod); augmentation already recovers much of this, so the gain over
  augmentation may be nil (HYPOTHESIS).
- The same bases block-diagonalize the linearized dynamics for the modes analysis; a defect here shows up
  there as spurious couplings.
