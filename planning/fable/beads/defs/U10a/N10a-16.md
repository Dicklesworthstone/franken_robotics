## Background

Construction C-08 computes the displacement a body achieves per cycle of shape change as the holonomy of a
connection: with body velocity ξ and shape s, ξ = −𝔸(s)·ṡ at zero momentum; for a closed loop in shape space
the net displacement is the path-ordered exponential of −∮𝔸·ds, which to leading order is minus the integral
of the curvature (D𝔸)ᵢⱼ = ∂ᵢ𝔸ⱼ − ∂ⱼ𝔸ᵢ − [𝔸ᵢ, 𝔸ⱼ] over the enclosed surface. The construction is Frontier: it
is exact for principally kinematic systems (kinematic wheeled bases, snake-like crawlers, zero-momentum free
flight) and only a diagnostic for dynamic walking. The claim to be piloted is that the curvature picture can
**rank and propose gaits without rollouts** for kinematic locomotors, against the generic method, direct
collocation over the same loops.

The pilot gates the connection-curvature analysis (analysis A-09) and, in a second stage, sets the condition
under which the Moonshot shape-space atlas may be funded.

## Technical approach

**Protocol (frozen before the first run).**

1. Fixtures. Shape coordinates are in the chain convention of the specification SPEC-C-08, αᵢ = θᵢ₊₁ − θᵢ
   with θ₁, θ₂, θ₃ the segment headings from one end. (a) *Planar three-link floating body*: three identical
   uniform slender links, 1 kg and 1 m each, two revolute joints, zero linear and angular momentum; group
   SO(2) (orientation), shape (α₁, α₂). (b) *Wheeled snake*: three rigid segments joined by two actuated
   revolute joints, each segment carrying one passive wheel pair at its centre that forbids lateral slip (a
   Pfaffian constraint per segment); group SE(2), the pose of the middle segment; shape (α₁, α₂); segment
   length ℓ = 0.3 m. With three constraints and a three-dimensional group the connection is 𝔸 = W_g⁻¹·W_s
   wherever W_g is invertible. With unit-normal rows, det W_g = ± 2·ℓ·sin((α₁ − α₂)/2)·cos(α₁/2)·cos(α₂/2)
   (the sign follows the order of the rows), so for abs(αᵢ) < π the connection is singular exactly on the
   line α₁ = α₂: every circular arc, where the three axles meet at the arc's centre, and the straight body,
   where they are parallel. It is regular on S-shapes; at (0.5, −0.5) rad, abs(det W_g) = 0.2700 m.
2. Training loops and held-out loops. Thirty closed loops in shape space per fixture (circles and ellipses of
   five sizes and six centres, fixed in the protocol), split 20 for fitting any free choice (body frame,
   weights) and 10 held out. Two constraints on the centres, so that every loop measures something: for the
   snake no loop meets the singular line α₁ = α₂ (centres on S-shapes such as (0.5, −0.5) rad); for the
   three-link body no centre lies on the line α₁ = −α₂ of point-symmetric S-shapes, where the rotational
   curvature vanishes by the end-to-end symmetry of the chain and a small loop turns the body by nothing
   (centres on arcs such as (0.5, 0.5) rad).
3. Holonomy arm. Optimize the body frame toward minimum-perturbation coordinates on the training loops; for
   each loop compute the displacement per cycle from the curvature integral (small loops) and from the
   ordered exponential (all loops); actuator cost as the path integral of ‖ṡ‖² at unit cycle time.
4. Collocation arm (the generic comparator). Direct collocation of the same loop with the full kinematic
   equations, giving displacement per cycle and actuator cost.
5. Measures on the held-out loops: ranking of loops by cycles per metre (snake) or cycles per radian (three
   link), Kendall τ between arms; drift error, the difference between predicted and collocated displacement
   per cycle relative to the collocated value.
6. Budgets fixed before running: τ ≥ 0.8 on held-out loops; drift error at most 10% for loops of radius up to
   0.4 rad (TARGET).

**Falsifier.** Held-out loop ranking or drift error outside its budget.

**Second stage (the atlas condition).** A shape-space atlas is funded only after (i) an independent full
rollout admits at least one newly proposed gait (a loop the holonomy arm proposes that is not among the thirty
and whose rollout displacement is within 10% of the prediction) and (ii) a changed-contact test withdraws a
formerly valid one (a loop that lifts or slips one wheel pair must be refused by the method, because a
connection belongs to one contact stratum).

**Ceiling, stated before any measurement.** The method replaces gait search for kinematic locomotors only; for
dynamic walking it is a diagnostic. No speed claim beyond avoiding rollouts for this class.

**Fallback.** Direct collocation over the same loops.

**Where it runs.** `pilots/c08-holonomy/`, outside the workspace, on the CPU in double precision; an
off-the-shelf collocation setup with its pin recorded. Cost: about an hour on one core.

**Record** (identifier `PILOT-C-08`, the value of the `pilot` field in row C-08 of the
construction register). `frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`: protocol hash, falsifier, ceiling, fallback, cost, pins,
τ and drift tables for both fixtures, the two second-stage outcomes and the decision.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `beanie_reference`: two coaxial bodies with inertias 3 and 1 kg·m²: a relative turn of 2π rotates body 1 by
  −π/2.
- `snake_connection_defined`: abs(det W_g) = 0.2700 m at the S-shape (0.5, −0.5) rad and equals the closed
  form of protocol step 1 at 10³ random shapes; W_g is singular at (0, 0) and at (0.5, 0.5) rad (the straight
  body and an arc, where the three wheel axles are parallel or meet at a point); the singular set is reported.
- `ordered_exponential_small_loop`: on the wheeled snake, for a circle of radius 0.05 rad centred at
  (0.5, −0.5) rad, the translation from the ordered exponential and from the curvature integral agree within
  5%; on the three-link body, for a circle of the same radius centred at (0.5, 0.5) rad, the two rotations
  agree to 10⁻⁹ relative (the group is abelian, so the curvature integral is exact there).
- `stratum_refusal`: a loop annotated with a lifted wheel pair is refused.
- `kendall_tau_implementation`: known rankings.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_c08.sh` drives the pilot binary, sources the STD-E2E helper and logs to
`target/e2e-logs/pilot_c08/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: per fixture and loop the command, exit code, wall
time, predicted and collocated displacement with units, drift error against 10%, τ against 0.8, the sign and
chart controls' outcomes, the protocol hash, and on failure the loop whose prediction left its budget. The
`summary` line carries the decision and the second-stage outcomes.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: KA-064 (the beanie ratio −I₂/(I₁ + I₂)); KA-065 (three-link holonomy against direct
integration). Negative controls: the flipped bracket sign (CX-030); a curvature map computed in a deliberately
poor body frame (attached to an end segment) must show larger drift error on large loops than the optimized
frame, demonstrating that the coordinate optimization matters; an A/A comparison of collocation against
itself gives τ = 1.

## Considerations

- For the abelian group SO(2) of the three-link body the bracket term vanishes, so the sign control must use
  the wheeled snake, whose group SE(2) is not abelian. For the same reason the curvature integral on the
  three-link body is exact at every loop size (Stokes' theorem), so its drift error measures quadrature and
  collocation error only: the drift clause of the falsifier is decided on the wheeled snake, and the
  three-link body contributes its ranking by cycles per radian and a check of the collocation setup.
- Orientation values from an independent `f64` computation made in review (the pilot reproduces them and does
  not copy them): on the snake, for circles centred at (0.5, −0.5) rad, the curvature integral is off in
  translation by 1.3% at radius 0.05 rad, 2.7% at 0.1, 5.4% at 0.2 and 10% at 0.4, and with the bracket sign
  flipped by about half the displacement at each of these radii; on the three-link body the rotational
  curvature at (0.5, 0.5) rad is 0.0246, so a circle of radius 0.1 rad turns it by 7.7·10⁻⁴ rad.
- Collocation is the comparator and also the truth here; if the two arms disagree on small loops the
  collocation setup is checked first against direct integration.
- If only the first stage passes, the Moonshot atlas stays unfunded and waits in the moonshot queue. If both
  stages pass here, the atlas is still not funded by this record: the funding rule is recorded as met only by
  N25c-46, after the M6 pilot (N25c-42) and its gate have repeated the admitted gait and the withdrawal on the
  referee.
