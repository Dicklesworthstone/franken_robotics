## Background

Construction C-17 (a mission is a quantified set of designs; specified by SPEC-C-17 and N10b-04) is Frontier.
Its claim is not speed: it is that a causal mission and a morphology can be mapped to sound small sub-problems
whose answers are checkable certificates, and that this decides cells that matter. Doctrine requires a pilot
with a falsifier before further work is funded, and requires each specialized method to name the generic
method it must beat; if the generic method ties, the project says so and ships the generic method. FACT
(planning pilots, to be re-run in this repository): on a static stance family a lifted robust linear program
and the specialized projection each reached the optimum in one solve, and a scenario tree matched the docking
family, so a tie is the expected outcome.

This bead is the pilot. It runs on the first mission slice (milestone M1, CPU only) and evaluates three
families, each against its generic comparator. Its result gates the *later* mission work (amendments,
separation and probes at M3; queries at scale and region certificates at M4), and feeds the kill gate
KILL-FAMILYCERT.

Terms. *Family*: a parameterized set of designs with one mission. *Cell decided*: a cell with an inner
witness or an exclusion accepted by the independent auditor. *Generic comparator*: an off-the-shelf method
that answers the same question (a lifted robust linear program, a scenario-tree linear program, an interval or
satisfiability tool). *False certificate*: a bundle the auditor accepts that is shown false within its scope.

## Scope

In: the frozen protocol over the three families; the falsifier evaluation; the ceiling statement; the record
`PILOT-C-17`.

Out: building the families (BD-129 docking, BD-130 stance, the energy exclusion named in `needs`); the mutation
corpus (BD-136); the fresh-agent session (BD-140); the kill-gate decision itself, which is taken at M3 on the
partial ten-fold gate.

## Technical approach

**Protocol (frozen before execution).**

| Family | Mission and cells | Specialized arm | Generic comparator | Known answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Static (stance; construction C-32) | 400 N on four point feet at (±a, ±b), a = 1/3 m; centre of mass anywhere in a rectangle of half-sizes 150 mm and 75 mm; each foot commanding any force in [0, 160] N; unknown: the half-width b | The projected stance family with vertex witnesses and dual exclusions | A lifted robust linear program | Minimum b = 3/14 m with forces (160, 130, 110, 0) N at the binding corner; at b = 1/5 m the dual witness gives f₊₊ − f₋₋ = 165 N against a cap of 160 N |
| Timing (docking; construction C-19) | Unit mass, hidden gain g ∈ {1, 2}, three ticks, command bound U, read error ε; the 561-cell grid U = ½ + i/32, ε = j/64, with the packet on time and late | The finite game over causal histories | A scenario-tree linear program | Admitted exactly when ε < (U + ½)/4: 344 admitted, 217 excluded, 9 on the boundary; all 561 excluded when late |
| Energy (construction C-31) | Lifting 8 kg over a 12 mm sill on a 0.90 J budget, for every wheel radius | The energy-budget exclusion | The same inequality posed to a conventional interval or satisfiability tool | 8 × 9.81 × 0.012 = 0.94176 J is needed, above the budget for every wheel radius; 0.05 J of approach kinetic energy removes the exclusion |

For each family, per cell: verdict of each arm, the bundle hash and the auditor's verdict for the specialized
arm, and the wall time of each arm from request to checked answer (the same phases timed in both arms: parse,
assemble, solve, check, serialize).

**Falsifier.** Any false certificate, or no cell decided that the comparator leaves undecided at equal cost.

**Ceiling (stated before the run).** The gain is in designs decided per agent turn and trainings avoided,
measured by the ten-fold test at the tournament; it is not a solver speed-up. If a fraction f of matched wall
time is improved by a factor r with overhead h, the speed-up is at most 1/(1 − f + f/r + h); the plan-time
pusher pilot showed why this is computed first: a residual of 575.7 µs that the solver does not touch capped a
zero-cost solver at about 1.46, and the measured 1.345 could not have confirmed its claim.

**Outcome rule.** A tie with the generic comparator is an acceptable outcome that ships the generic method
behind the same verbs, schema and checker. The pilot's verdicts are: `funded` (no false certificate and at
least one cell decided that the comparator leaves undecided at equal cost), `tie` (no false certificate; every
cell decided by both at comparable cost: later work is funded for the interface and the checker, and any claim
of exclusive expressivity on these families is retired), `fallback` (a false certificate: the procedure for a
false certificate applies, and if the cause is the rule and not an implementation defect the proof kind is
removed and its cells become `Tested`).

**Kill gates this pilot feeds.** KILL-FAMILYCERT (decided at M3; trigger: at the partial ten-fold gate no
family certificate has decided a cell that the generic comparator leaves undecided at equal cost; fallback:
mission answers are produced by the generic solvers alone and still ship in the bundle format, and the
specialized projections are removed). KILL-AUGUR-TIE (decided at M3; a short robust program over a
conventional solver reaches the same checked scope at comparable turnaround; fallback: ship that program
behind the same verbs). KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT (standing from M1).

**The six parts of a method experiment.** Ceiling (above); competing arms (specialized, generic, fallback);
potency control (a planted lowering defect, a swapped foot order with unchanged forces, must be rejected by
the auditor, and an A/A run of the comparator against itself must report zero differences); paired comparison
(cells paired by identity; times as a paired lower bound on the ratio over at least ten repetitions, never a
ratio of means); pre-declared outcome rule (above); reachability (all three verdicts are reachable on the
frozen cells).

**Record.** `PILOT-C-17` in `frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` with protocol hash, falsifier, ceiling,
outcome rule, kill gates, cost, tools and pins (both linear-program solvers, the auditor build), then the
result. Evidence class: `Certified` for the cells the auditor accepts, `Tested` for the comparison.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The record's protocol, falsifier, ceiling and outcome rule are committed before its result.
- [ ] Per family, both arms return the known answer exactly (3/14 m and the dual at 1/5 m; 344, 217, 9 and 561;
      0.94176 J against 0.90 J and the reversal at 0.95 J).
- [ ] Every bundle of the specialized arm is accepted by `frob-verify` built from an empty environment; the
      planted swapped-foot-order bundle is rejected.
- [ ] The count of false certificates is 0 (TARGET), checked against the seeded-defect corpus of BD-136.
- [ ] The count of cells decided by the specialized arm and left undecided by the comparator at equal cost is
      recorded (it may be 0), with the per-arm phase times and the computed ceiling.
- [ ] The verdict `funded`, `tie` or `fallback` is recorded, and the later mission work packages do not start
      before it (GATE-GAUNT-08).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In the pilot's harness (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/pilot_c17.rs`):
- `outcome_rule`: synthetic result tables (labelled `synthetic`): zero false certificates and one extra cell →
  `funded`; zero and zero → `tie`; one false certificate → `fallback` whatever else.
- `equal_cost_definition`: a cell counts as "left undecided at equal cost" only if the comparator's budget was
  at least the specialized arm's measured time; a comparator stopped early does not count.
- `phase_ledger_sums`: per arm, the five phase times sum to the wall time within 1%.
- `stance_known_answer`, `docking_known_answer`, `energy_known_answer`: the exact values above from each arm.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_c17.sh` (idempotent; refuses to overwrite a record with a result): for each family runs
`frob --json mission synthesize` and `frob --json mission refute` on the frozen `.fmission` documents, then
`frob-verify` on every bundle, then the comparator programs; validates the record. Sources the STD-E2E helper;
writes `target/e2e-logs/pilot_c17/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript; per cell one `metric` line (family, cell,
both verdicts, bundle hash, auditor verdict, phase times with units); `assert` lines for the known answers,
the planted bundle and the falsifier clauses; each line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N10b-05`), `step`,
`event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier,
determinism, identities, seed); on failure the first cell where an arm departs from the known answer. Final
`summary`: counts, verdict, gate GATE-GAUNT-08.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the stance values (KA-157, KA-158), the docking values (KA-108 to KA-111), the energy exclusion
(KA-154), quantifier order and strictness (KA-098, KA-099). Negative controls: the four stance mutations (a
swapped foot order with unchanged forces; an unreported positive force floor; a passive-authority tag; (60, 30)
passed as the torque), each rejected by the auditor; the energy exclusion issued with the approach-energy term
omitted; a docking bundle with its quantifiers reordered. Determinism: bundle bytes identical on two hosts;
exact arithmetic on both arms; the comparison runs twice with identical verdict tables.

## Considerations

- The honest prior is a tie. The pilot is designed so that a tie is informative and has a defined consequence.
- "Equal cost" must be fixed before the run, or the falsifier's second clause can be argued either way.
- If the energy family is not yet available as a mission exclusion at M1, the pilot runs on two families and is
  recorded `partial`; the missing family is added before the kill-gate decision at M3.
- Open question carried: whether certificates change any design decision. It is counted in the phase ledger of
  the ten-fold test; if the count is zero, certificates are evidence quality only and stay off the critical
  path.
