## Background

Construction C-18 (specified by SPEC-C-18) says that every mission certificate has a polarity: an inner
certificate survives when the world set shrinks, the policy class grows or the acceptance predicate weakens,
and must be rechecked on the opposite moves; an exclusion is the reverse. Reuse of verdicts under amendments is
Frontier, and before the revalidation engine is funded a paper-scale pilot must show that reading the table
never keeps a verdict that fresh evaluation contradicts. One false survivor is a stale verdict presented as
evidence, the worst failure this mechanism can have. This bead is that pilot. It runs on the certificates
produced by the pilot of construction C-17 (N10b-05: a static stance family, a timing docking family and an
energy exclusion on the first mission slice).

Terms. *Amendment*: a change to the world set W, the policy class Π or the acceptance predicate A of a mission.
*Survivor*: a certificate the table marks as surviving an amendment. *Fresh evaluation*: answering the amended
query from scratch, with no reuse. *Dependency closure*: everything a result was computed from.

## Scope

In: the frozen protocol with twelve scripted amendments; the falsifier evaluation; the ceiling; the record
`PILOT-C-18`.

Out: the revalidation engine and its verbs (BD-292, BD-347); the frozen 30-amendment campaign of the later
gates; the memo graph (BD-111).

## Technical approach

**Protocol (frozen before execution).**

1. *Certificates.* Every inner certificate and every exclusion of the three families of N10b-05, each carrying
   its assumption set with the survival direction per assumption (world set, authority and policy class,
   acceptance predicate).
2. *Twelve scripted amendments*, two per row of the polarity table, spread over the families (DESIGN, fixed
   before the run):

   | # | Axis and direction | Family | Amendment |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | W shrinks | docking | read error ε halved on every cell |
   | 2 | W shrinks | stance | centre-of-mass rectangle reduced from 150 × 75 mm to 100 × 50 mm |
   | 3 | W grows | docking | one hidden gain 33/32 admitted |
   | 4 | W grows | stance | rectangle enlarged to 200 × 100 mm |
   | 5 | Π grows | docking | command bound U raised by 1/32 on every cell |
   | 6 | Π grows | stance | per-foot cap raised from 160 N to 170 N |
   | 7 | Π shrinks | docking | the packet delivered late |
   | 8 | Π shrinks | stance | independent foot forces replaced by equal passive springs |
   | 9 | A weakens | docking | only v₃ = 0 required |
   | 10 | A weakens | energy | budget raised from 0.90 J to 0.95 J |
   | 11 | A strengthens | docking | no overshoot: x_k ≤ 1 at every tick |
   | 12 | A strengthens | stance | every foot force at least 10 N |

3. *Arms.* The mechanism (read the table: `survives` or `recheck` per certificate); the conventional
   alternative and reference (recompute every amended query from its dependency closure); the fallback is the
   reference itself.
4. *Measures.* For each (certificate, amendment): the table's entry and the fresh verdict. Counts: survivors,
   survivors contradicted, rechecks that confirmed, rechecks that reversed; evaluations saved.

**Falsifier.** One survivor that fresh evaluation contradicts.

**Ceiling (stated before the run).** The reuse share of compute: FACT (source register, to be re-read) exact
reuse of rollouts after a mass or geometry edit is at most about 10% of compute; FACT (planning pilot, to be
re-run) a semantic receipt and a correctly keyed dependency graph did identical work on a four-state edit
sequence. The benefit claimed is fewer wrong reuses, not time.

**Fallback and kill gate.** Every amendment recomputes from its dependency closure. Kill gate KILL-AUGUR-REUSE
(standing from M3): one incorrectly inherited verdict from an implication rule removes that rule, and reuse
falls back to the dependency-keyed memo graph.

**Expected results worth checking by hand** (all exact): amendment 3 keeps every docking exclusion (217 on the
grid), marks all 344 inclusions for recheck and reverses the inclusion at (U, ε) = (¾, 1/64), with a worst
terminal error of 1/65 m; amendment 7 excludes all 561 cells; amendment 8 withdraws the stance inclusion at
b = ¼ m, where active allocation gives (155, 135, 105, 5) N and equal passive springs give (175, 115, 85, 25) N
against the 160 N cap; amendment 10 removes the energy exclusion (0.94176 J needed).

**The six parts of a method experiment.** Ceiling (above); competing arms (above); potency control (a planted
table with the inclusion and exclusion columns exchanged must produce at least one contradicted survivor on
amendment 3, and an A/A comparison of fresh evaluation with itself must produce none); paired comparison
(pairs are (certificate, amendment); the reported quantity is a count with exact arithmetic on both sides);
pre-declared fallback (above); reachability (a single contradicted survivor falsifies; both verdicts are
reachable).

**Record.** `PILOT-C-18` in `frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`: protocol hash, falsifier, ceiling,
fallback, kill gate, cost, tools and pins, then the result. Evidence class `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The record's protocol, falsifier, ceiling and fallback are committed before its result.
- [ ] All twelve amendments ran against every certificate of the three families; the four counts are recorded.
- [ ] Survivors contradicted by fresh evaluation: 0 (TARGET). Any other value records the verdict `fallback`.
- [ ] The hand-checkable results above are reproduced exactly.
- [ ] The potency control produced a contradicted survivor and the A/A comparison produced none.
- [ ] The revalidation engine's beads do not start before the record has a result (GATE-GAUNT-08).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In the pilot's harness (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/pilot_c18.rs`):
- `table_entry_for_each_axis`: the six rows for both certificate kinds return the normative entries.
- `falsifier_evaluator`: synthetic tables (labelled `synthetic`): zero contradicted survivors → `funded`; one →
  `fallback`.
- `unordered_amendment`: an amendment that is neither a shrink nor a growth of W (one gain removed, another
  added) is `recheck` for both kinds and is never counted as a survivor.
- `saved_evaluations_count`: the count of evaluations saved equals the number of survivors.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_c18.sh` (idempotent; refuses to overwrite a record with a result): for each amendment
writes the amended `.fmission`, calls `frob --json mission revalidate` when the verb exists and otherwise the
reference table reader, then `frob --json mission synthesize` on the amended query for the fresh verdict, and
compares. Sources the STD-E2E helper; writes `target/e2e-logs/pilot_c18/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript; one
`metric` line per (certificate, amendment) with the table entry, the fresh verdict and the bundle hash;
`assert` lines for the falsifier, the hand-checkable results and the potency control; each line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N10b-07`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's
evidence block; on failure the first contradicted survivor with its certificate and amendment. Final
`summary`: the four counts, the verdict and gate GATE-GAUNT-08.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: KA-103 (polarity on the docking family), KA-104 (the 33/32 gain: 1/65 m), KA-105, KA-224 (the
twelve moves per fixture), the stance values KA-157 to KA-159, the energy value KA-154. Negative controls: the
exchanged-columns table; a certificate stripped of its assumption set (it must be `recheck` under every
amendment, never a survivor); a stale dependency key that omits delivery time (it inherits a promotion that
amendment 7 contradicts). Determinism: exact arithmetic; the script runs twice with identical tables; bundle
hashes equal across two hosts.

## Considerations

- Twelve amendments are a pilot, not coverage. The later gates run a frozen 30-amendment campaign and require
  every decision to equal full recomputation with unnecessary invalidations at most 20% of unaffected verdicts.
- Unnecessary rechecks are a cost, not an error; the pilot records them so that the later gate's 20% figure has
  a baseline.
- If the revalidation verb does not exist when the pilot runs, the table reader of the reference model is used
  and the record says so; the verdict then funds the engine's work, which re-runs the pilot through the verb.
