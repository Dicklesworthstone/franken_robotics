## Background

Construction C-23 claims that the policy of an edited design can start from its parent's policy through a
transport defined by the edit's declared correspondences (specified by N10b-14), and that this start saves
most of the training a child would otherwise need. The published evidence is thin: FACT (source register, to
be re-read) inheritance from a class prior costs about 2 to 25% of a from-scratch run, the one published
parent-to-child figure is about 50%, no source measures a small typed parameter edit, and single-limb topology
edits cost 33 to 100%. The mechanism is Frontier, so a paper-scale pilot with a written falsifier runs before
the inheritance work is funded. This bead is the second pilot of the construction (the first is the
forty-design family-policy experiment, shared with the rank-agreement construction and run by other beads).

Terms. *Parent–child edit pair*: a design and the design one typed edit away. *Adaptation samples*: the
environment steps a child policy needs, from its initialization, to reach a fixed return threshold (95% of the
final return of that child's own from-scratch specialist). *Blind pair*: a pair whose edit is chosen by a
party who does not see the parent's training and is revealed only after the protocol is frozen. *Raw-checkpoint
warm start*: the parent's weights copied by index into the child's network.

## Scope

In: the frozen protocol; the twelve pairs; the falsifier evaluation; the ceiling; the record `PILOT-C-23`
(part 2; part 1 is recorded by the beads that run the forty-design experiment).

Out: inheritance as a product feature and its gate (BD-322); the family policy (BD-320); the transport

## Technical approach

**Protocol (frozen before execution).**

1. *Pairs.* Twelve parent–child pairs in one morphology class (the reference quadruped), 4 of them blind.
   DESIGN composition, fixed before the run: 6 parameter edits (link length ±10%, mass ±15%, gear ratio one
   catalogue step, foot friction class, motor card swap, hip offset), 3 structural-light edits (add a sensor
   channel, add a passive tail link, lock a joint), 3 edits chosen blind. Edits that change support
   discontinuously (a mirrored pair, a removed limb, a wheel exchanged for a foot) are excluded: they have
   their own gate.
2. *Arms*, all with the same learner, budget cap, seeds (three) and task (the flat velocity task):
   arm T (transport, N10b-14); arm R (raw-checkpoint warm start, the conventional alternative); arm S (from
   scratch, the baseline and the source of the threshold); the fallback is arm S.
3. *Measure.* Adaptation samples to the threshold per pair, seed and arm; a run that does not reach the
   threshold within the cap is recorded at the cap (censored), and medians are taken over pairs.
4. *Host.* The product's own training path on one device where available (the PPO task of BD-198); otherwise
   the development oracle (an incumbent stack at a pinned release, out of process), with results labelled
   `development-oracle` and re-run on the product when it exists.

**Falsifier (TARGET).** Transport fails to halve the median adaptation samples: median(T)/median(S) > ½ over
the twelve pairs.

**Ceiling (stated before the run).** The gain on the training phase of a child is at most 1/f with f the
adaptation fraction; on the design loop it is bounded by 1/(1 − φ + φ·f + h), with φ the training share of
matched wall time and h the overhead of building the mapping. If training is 80% of a loop and transport
reaches f = 0.2, the loop gains at most 1/(0.2 + 0.16) ≈ 2.8.

**Fallback and kill gate.** Per-design training, with inheritance kept for parameter edits if those alone pass.
Kill gate KILL-INHERIT (decided at M4): median parameter-edit cost above 60% of from-scratch steps, or the
inherited arm more than 5% worse in over 20% of edits; fallback: every child trains from the class prior or
from scratch, and the mapping table is kept for loading policies.

**The six parts of a method experiment.** Ceiling (above); competing arms (T, R, S); potency control (a zero
edit must give arm T zero adaptation samples, since the child's first action equals the parent's; a
shuffled-correspondence transport must do no better than arm S; an A/A run of arm S against itself must give a
ratio whose 95% bound includes 1); paired lower bound (pairs by edit pair and seed; the reported quantity is a
one-sided 95% bound on the ratio of medians by a paired bootstrap, never a ratio of means); pre-declared
fallback (above); reachability (with twelve pairs and three seeds the smallest ratio distinguishable from ½ is
computed before the run from the from-scratch arm's spread; if the design cannot separate 0.5 from 0.6 the
record says `underpowered` and the pilot decides nothing).

**Record.** `PILOT-C-23` (part 2) in `frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`: protocol hash, falsifier,
ceiling, fallback, kill gate, cost (GPU-hours inside the approved envelope; 12 pairs × 3 arms × 3 seeds = 108
runs plus 12 parent specialists), tool and pin, then the result. Evidence class `Estimated`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The record's protocol, falsifier, ceiling and fallback are committed before its result; the blind edits
      are revealed after that commit and their reveal is logged.
- [ ] All 108 adaptation runs and the 12 parent runs completed or were censored at the cap; none was dropped.
- [ ] The ratio of medians T/S with its bound, the ratio R/S, and the count of pairs in which the inherited
      child is more than 5% worse at the cap are recorded, separately for parameter edits and the others.
- [ ] The potency controls hold (zero edit: zero samples; shuffled correspondences: no better than scratch).
- [ ] The verdict is one of `funded`, `funded for parameter edits only`, `fallback`, `underpowered`; the
      inheritance bead does not start before it (GATE-GAUNT-08).
- [ ] No compute outside the owner-approved envelope; without approval the record says `not run`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In the pilot's harness (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/pilot_c23.rs`):
- `adaptation_samples_definition`: a synthetic learning curve (labelled `synthetic`) crossing the threshold at
  step 12,000 returns 12,000; a curve that never crosses returns the cap and the flag `censored`.
- `ratio_of_medians_bootstrap`: twelve synthetic pairs with constant ratio 0.4 return a bound of 0.4.
- `falsifier_evaluator`: ratio 0.45 → `funded`; 0.55 → `fallback`; parameter edits 0.4 and others 0.9 →
  `funded for parameter edits only`.
- `blind_pairs_sealed`: the hash of the four blind edits is in the protocol commit and matches at reveal.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_c23.sh` (idempotent; seeds 2315, 2316, 2317 printed; refuses to overwrite a record with a
result): for each pair `frob --json design edit`; arm S by `frob --json train start`; arm T by the pilot's own transport
tool (`tools/pilots/c23/transport`, built from the reference transport of the specification, which writes the
child's initial checkpoint; the product verb `train.inherit` is the mechanism this pilot gates and is not
used) followed by `frob --json train start` from that checkpoint; arm R by a raw copy of the parent
checkpoint; polling `frob --json train status`; computes the measures; validates the record. Sources the STD-E2E helper; writes `target/e2e-logs/pilot_c23/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript; one
`metric` line per (pair, arm, seed) with adaptation samples, the threshold and the censoring flag; `assert`
lines for the potency controls and the falsifier; each line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N10b-15`), `step`,
`event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block (claim Design
comparison, class Estimated, tier, determinism class, policy and design identities, seed); on failure the
first pair whose run violated the protocol. Final `summary`: the ratios, the verdict, gate GATE-GAUNT-08.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: KA-129 (a zero-parameter edit gives a child whose first action equals the parent's), KA-130
(commuting edits give the same initialization). Negative controls: the shuffled-correspondence transport;
zero-weight, random and shuffled-label policies must fail the return threshold (a learning comparison needs
failing controls); a threshold edited after the first result must be refused by the study record.
Determinism: training carries its determinism class; a class that does not support paired statistics causes
the comparison to be refused.

## Considerations

- Twelve pairs cannot establish a general law; the pilot can only fail to falsify. The gate at M4 repeats the
  design with the full acceptance numbers (20% of from-scratch steps; a ten-edit chain with scheduled resets).
- Censoring matters: a transport that never reaches the threshold must count against it, at the cap.
- The thresholds 0.5 here (pilot falsifier), 0.2 (gate) and 0.6 (kill gate) are three different statements and
  are kept apart in the record.
