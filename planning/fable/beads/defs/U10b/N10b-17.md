## Background

Construction C-25 (specified by SPEC-C-25) gives estimators that use the coupling between a fast tier and the
referee: a control variate and a doubly robust audit correction. As mathematics they are Solid. Their *use*
across contact-rich tiers is Frontier, because everything depends on one number that nobody has measured: the
correlation ρ between the outcomes of the same world on two tiers. The saving is 1/(1 − ρ²) at best: about 2
at ρ = 0.7, 5 at 0.894, 10 at 0.949. Chaotic contact decorrelates paired worlds over long episodes, so ρ may
well be low. Before the tier-correction ledger is funded, a paper-scale pilot measures ρ and checks whether
the correction beats evaluating on the referee alone. It is cheap by design: it uses paired data that the
fidelity gate already pays for.

Terms. *World address*: (run seed, world, episode); the same address on two tiers is the same scenario.
*Paired data of the fidelity gate*: the standard battery run on the fast tiers and on the referee to freeze
the fidelity envelopes. *ρ_ep*: the per-episode correlation of the promoted quantity between tiers.
*High-agreement stratum*: a stratum of the battery (task family, terrain class) whose measured ρ_ep is at
least 0.7. *Referee-only*: estimating the high-tier mean from referee episodes alone.

## Scope

In: the frozen protocol; the measurement of ρ; the comparison against referee-only; the kill-rule evaluation;
the ceiling; the record `PILOT-C-25`.

Out: the ledger, propensity logging and promotion verbs as product features (BD-263, BD-264); the rank
agreement between tiers (BD-265), which is a different quantity (order of designs, not correlation of
episodes); the estimators' law suite (SPEC-C-25).

## Technical approach

**Protocol (frozen before execution).**

1. *Data.* 200 world addresses drawn by a keyed stream from the frozen standard battery, stratified by task
   family and terrain class (strata and the draw key are part of the record). Each address has its outcome on
   the fast tier (`batch-cpu` in `f32`, and a GPU tier where available) and on the referee, from the paired
   runs of the fidelity gate. Quantities: the promoted quantities of the trust ladder (the requirement verdict
   as a bit, and its robustness margin as a real number).
2. *Measure 1: ρ.* Per stratum and overall, ρ_ep with a 95% bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples of
   addresses), for the bit and for the margin, on whole episodes.
3. *Measure 2: the estimator against referee-only.* On each high-agreement stratum, replay the audit
   procedure off-line: fit the control-variate coefficient on a separate half of the addresses and freeze it;
   on the other half form Zᵢ = mᵢ + Aᵢ·(Yᵢ − mᵢ)/pᵢ with logged pᵢ ≥ p_min (DESIGN: p_min = 0.1 and p = 0.25
   by default) and audit bits drawn before outcomes are read; compare the variance of the corrected estimate
   per referee episode used with the variance of the referee-only mean. Repeat over 1,000 keyed re-draws of the
   audit bits.
4. *Arms.* The mechanism (frozen control variate with doubly robust audit correction); the conventional
   alternative and fallback (fixed escalation of finalists to the referee: referee-only).

**Kill rule (TARGET).** ρ below 0.7, or failure to beat referee-only by a factor of 2 on high-agreement
strata. In the terms of the gate register, which governs: ρ_ep below 0.7 on 200 paired addresses in every
stratum tested.

**Ceiling (stated before the run).** 1/(1 − ρ²) on the referee share of a decision: if a fraction f of a
promotion's wall time is referee episodes and the correction divides them by r, the promotion gains at most
1/(1 − f + f/r + h), with h the overhead of the audit bookkeeping.

**Fallback and kill gate.** Fixed escalation of finalists to the referee. Kill gate KILL-DR (decided at M3;
trigger: ρ_ep below 0.7 on 200 paired addresses in every stratum tested, or the coverage of the tier-correction
gate fails after one repair cycle; fallback: promotion reports the label only, from referee episodes alone,
and the ledger keeps recording ρ_ep). Segment mode (re-simulating short segments from snapshots) remains an
option inside that fallback, and estimates a different quantity that the report names.

**The six parts of a method experiment.** Ceiling (above); competing arms (above); potency control (a planted
pair of tiers with known ρ = 0.9, made by adding independent noise of known variance to referee outcomes, must
return ρ inside its interval and a variance ratio near 1/(1 − 0.81·(1 − n/N)); an A/A comparison of the
referee with itself must return ρ = 1 and no gain from auditing); paired lower bound (the variance ratio is
reported as a 95% lower bound over the audit re-draws, never as a ratio of means); pre-declared fallback
(above); reachability (with 200 addresses the half-width of the interval on ρ near 0.7 is about 0.07, so
values between 0.63 and 0.77 are recorded as `inconclusive` for that stratum, and the kill rule is read on the
interval's upper end).

**Record.** `PILOT-C-25` in `frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`: protocol hash, kill rule, ceiling,
fallback, kill gate, cost (no new referee episodes; CPU minutes for the analysis), tool and pin, then the
result. Evidence class `Estimated`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The record's protocol, kill rule, ceiling and fallback are committed before its result.
- [ ] ρ_ep is recorded per stratum and overall with its interval, for the bit and the margin; the strata are
      labelled high-agreement, low-agreement or inconclusive.
- [ ] On every high-agreement stratum the lower bound on the variance ratio against referee-only is recorded
      (TARGET at least 2).
- [ ] The potency control recovers ρ = 0.9 within its interval; the A/A control returns ρ = 1.
- [ ] Verdict `funded`, `fallback` or `inconclusive`; the tier-correction ledger does not start before it
      (GATE-GAUNT-08).
- [ ] No address outside the keyed draw was used, and no audit bit was drawn after its outcome was read.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In the pilot's harness (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/pilot_c25.rs`):
- `rho_known_answer`: synthetic pairs (labelled `synthetic`) with ρ = 0.9 by construction, 200 addresses:
  the estimate's interval contains 0.9.
- `variance_ratio_known_answer`: for the same pairs with n = 64 audited of N = 6,400, the control-variate
  variance ratio is 0.198 within 5% over 2,000 replications.
- `kill_rule_evaluator`: all strata with upper interval end below 0.7 → `fallback`; one stratum with lower end
  above 0.7 and ratio bound 2.3 → `funded`; all strata straddling 0.7 → `inconclusive`.
- `audit_bits_before_outcomes`: the harness refuses a run in which the outcome column was read before the
  audit bits were written.
- `dr_term_unbiased`: for Y ∈ {0, 1}, constant m and p = 0.1, the mean of Z over all audit outcomes equals the
  mean of Y exactly.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_c25.sh` (idempotent; draw key printed; refuses to overwrite a record with a result): reads
the paired runs through `frob --json run list` and `frob --json telemetry summary` for the 200 addresses on
each tier, computes the measures, validates the record. Sources the STD-E2E helper; writes
`target/e2e-logs/pilot_c25/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript; one `metric` line per stratum with ρ_ep, its
interval, the label and the variance-ratio bound; `assert` lines for the controls and the kill rule; each line
has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N10b-17`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the
envelope's evidence block (claim Fidelity, class Estimated, tier pair, determinism class, battery identity,
seed); on failure the first address missing from a tier. Final `summary`: the strata table, the verdict, gate
GATE-GAUNT-08.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: KA-132 (variance at the optimal β; thresholds 0.894 and 0.949), KA-133 (unbiasedness by
enumeration), KA-134 (added variance 2.25 for a prediction wrong by ½ at p = 0.1). Negative controls: audit
bits chosen after the outcomes (the estimate becomes biased and the harness must flag it); a unit coefficient
in place of the fitted one (the variance ratio departs from the formula); tiers paired by seed and tick
instead of by world address (ρ collapses; the harness must report the pairing key). Determinism: the analysis
is seeded and bit-identical twice on one machine; paired statistics are refused under an uncontrolled
determinism class.

## Considerations

- A low ρ is a result, not a failure of the pilot: it sends promotion back to the plain paired procedure and
  saves the cost of building a ledger nobody would use.
- ρ on the verdict bit and ρ on the margin can differ widely; both are recorded, and the kill rule is read on
  the quantity that promotion actually uses.
- Long contact-rich episodes are where ρ falls. The record reports ρ against episode length so that a
  horizon-dependent rule is possible later, without changing this pilot's verdict.
