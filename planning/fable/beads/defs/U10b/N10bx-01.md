## Background

A mission query asks for the set of designs that succeed for every world of a declared set, and answers with an
inner set (designs certified to succeed), an outer set (designs not yet excluded) and what is unresolved. Proof
rule 1, the necessary-condition exclusion, removes designs: relax the controller and the dynamics (any contact
force inside an outer cone, any torque under an upper envelope, any observation), and if even the relaxation is
infeasible, every realization it covers fails. The rule has a limit that this bead exists to enforce: the
relaxation must enclose every admissible state and energy source.

The energy budget of construction C-31, read as an exclusion, is the simplest instance: if a mandatory crossing
needs a potential rise greater than the initial energy plus all allowed external work, no controller succeeds,
clairvoyant or not. The specimen used throughout the specification: lifting an 8 kg body over a 12 mm sill needs
8 × 9.81 × 0.012 = 0.94176 J, above a 0.90 J store, for every wheel radius; 0.05 J of approach kinetic energy
(0.95 J in total) removes the exclusion, so approach energy is a term of the budget and not a footnote.

The pilot of construction C-17 (PILOT-C-17, bead N10b-05, at M1) compares three specialized families with a
generic comparator each: static (stance, BD-130), timing (docking, BD-129) and **energy**. The first two have
family beads. The third had none at M1: the proof kind `energy-budget` has its writer (N22b-14) and checker
(N12-17) at M4, and the analysis A-20 (N28a-07) gives an analysis verdict, not a mission certificate. Without
this bead the pilot runs on two families and is recorded `partial`, and the kill gate KILL-FAMILYCERT is decided
at M3 on two thirds of its evidence.

DESIGN (the choice this bead makes, with its reason). At M1 the exclusion is issued under rule 1 as a certificate
of kind `lp-farkas` on a one-row relaxation, with an inclusion record that lists every energy source. Reason: the
inequality is linear with exact rational data; `lp-farkas` is one of the seven mission kinds whose checker exists
at M1 (BD-132), so the offline auditor can re-check the bundle now; and the rule-1 object of the mission core
(BD-128) already has exactly this shape. Rejected: bringing a subset of the `energy-budget` kind forward to M1
(two beads would then specify one payload); using the A-20 verdict as the pilot's specialized arm (an analysis
finding is not a bundle the auditor can check, and the pilot's metric is time to a checked answer). From M4 the
same exclusion is also written as kind `energy-budget`; the two encodings must give the same verdict.

Tier: Frontier (the mission slice); the inequality itself is Solid mathematics.

## Technical approach

**1. The fixture mission** (exact decimals as rationals; g = 981/100 m/s² as declared).

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Body mass m | 8 kg, an interval [m_lo, m_hi] = [8, 8] in the base query |
| Sill rise h | 12 mm = 3/250 m |
| Free design parameter | wheel radius r ∈ [0.03, 0.15] m (DESIGN: wide enough to hold any wheel a cart of this size carries) |
| Stored energy available to the crossing | E_store = 0.90 J = 9/10 J |
| Approach kinetic energy | 0 J (declared: the cart starts the segment at rest against the sill) |
| Requirement | the body ends the segment with every wheel on the upper level |
| Amended form A | approach kinetic energy up to 0.05 J declared (store unchanged) |
| Amended form B | store raised to 0.95 J (the amendment the polarity pilot uses) |

**2. Applicability.** `MandatoryWorkLowering::applies` requires: a segment with a mandatory rise (a lower bound
h_lo > 0 on the rise of the centre of mass that every successful trace must make, derived from the scene and the
requirement: with level floors on both sides and all wheels required on the upper level, the rise of the centre
of mass of a rigid body equals the sill height, whatever the wheel radius); a declared energy block in the
mission; mass bounds. A mission whose rise depends on posture (an articulated body that can lower its centre of
mass) does not qualify unless the mission supplies h_lo with its own proof; the lowering then leaves the cell to
other rules.

**3. The relaxation.** Take the energy balance from the start of the segment to the first instant the centre of
mass is at its high point: m·g·h = (T₀ − T₁) + W_in − D, with T₀ the kinetic energy at the start (translation
and wheel and rotor spin), T₁ ≥ 0 the kinetic energy at the high point, W_in the work delivered by all sources,
D ≥ 0 the dissipation. Dropping T₁ and D (both of the sign that helps the design) and letting each source j
deliver any amount eⱼ in [0, cⱼ] gives the relaxed system

  Σⱼ eⱼ ≥ m_lo·g·h_lo,  0 ≤ eⱼ ≤ cⱼ,

which every real crossing satisfies. Sources and caps cⱼ (each an upper bound) are taken from the enclosure
check of A-20: stored electrical energy times the best path efficiency η_max ≤ 1 (1 when unknown), injected
electrical energy, elastic storage, kinetic energy at the start ½·m_hi·v_hi² + Σ ½·J_k·ω_k,hi² over the admitted
approach speeds, gravitational drop on the path before the rise, and declared external work.

**4. The dual and the cell-wide proof.** In the rule-1 form of the mission core (a system A·x ≥ w, 0 ≤ x ≤ c is
excluded by a dual y ≥ 0 when yᵀw > Σᵢ max(0, (Aᵀy)ᵢ)·cᵢ, strictly), A is the single row of ones, w = m_lo·g·h_lo
and y = 1, so the certificate is the strict inequality m_lo·g·h_lo > Σⱼ cⱼ. For the fixture:
m·g·h = 8 × 981/100 × 3/250 = 2943/3125 J = 0.94176 J; Σ c = 9/10 J; margin 261/6250 J = 0.04176 J > 0.
The margin does not contain r, so it is affine (constant) in the cell parameter and positive at both vertices of
[0.03, 0.15]: the whole cell is excluded, which is what "for every wheel radius" means. Infeasible sample points
never exclude a cell: where the margin depends on a cell parameter, the lowering evaluates it exactly at the
cell's vertices and excludes only when it is affine in each parameter and positive at all of them; otherwise it
splits at the exact root. For a free sill height the root is h* = (9/10)/(8 × 981/100) = 5/436 m ≈ 11.468 mm.

**5. The inclusion record (the side conditions the checker reads).** (a) the source list with, per source, its
cap, its unit, its provenance and the statement "upper bound"; (b) the list of energy sources the mission
declares, which must be a subset of (a); (c) h_lo and m_lo with the statement "lower bound over the cell" and
their derivation; (d) the dropped terms T₁ and D with their sign. If the mission declares a source that the
relaxation does not carry, or approach motion with no bound on speed, no exclusion is issued: refusal
FROB-REF-829 (a side condition of a proof rule is missing), repairs ranked: bind the missing source with a cap;
bind the approach-speed interval; accept `unresolved`. (The analysis-level form of the same defect is A-20's
FROB-REF-551; a mission answer reports 829 and cites the 551 finding when A-20 raised it.)

**6. Verdicts.** Base query: the cell is excluded, type `outer`-removed, rule `rule-1`, kind `lp-farkas`,
margin 261/6250 J. Amended form A: Σ c = 9/10 + 1/20 = 19/20 J ≥ 2943/3125 J, slack 103/12500 J = 0.00824 J: no
exclusion; the cell is `unresolved` (a necessary condition holds; nothing is proved feasible; this lowering
never returns `inner`). Amended form B: the same numbers through the store. Polarity tags on the certificate's
assumptions: every source cap is tagged "raising it weakens the exclusion", m_lo and h_lo "lowering it weakens
the exclusion", so reuse after an amendment is decided by implication and not by a matching hash.

**7. Class.** Exact arithmetic over the mission's declared numbers: `Certified`, capped by its inputs (a cap read
from a card field of provenance `assumed` caps the class at `Estimated`). Determinism D0. The answer returns only
after the checker has accepted the bundle (the rule of the bundle writer).

**8. From M4.** When the writer and checker of kind `energy-budget` exist, the lowering emits that kind as well.
A conformance test asserts that both encodings exclude the base query and that neither excludes the amended
forms; a disagreement is a defect in one of them, not a preference. The `lp-farkas` encoding is kept, because the
first mission slice's goldens cite it.

**9. Files.** `crates/frob-mission/src/lowering/mandatory_work.rs`; fixtures under `fixtures/mission/`; the
derivation in the crate documentation; goldens `fixtures/mission/golden/sill-energy.*.json`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob mission synthesize fixtures/mission/sill-energy.fmission --key sill-base --json` returns the wheel-radius cell
      excluded under rule 1 with an `lp-farkas` bundle; the printed required work is 0.94176 J, the available
      energy 0.90 J and the margin 0.04176 J (exactly 261/6250 J); `frob-verify` accepts the bundle.
- [ ] Amended form A (0.05 J of approach kinetic energy) and form B (store 0.95 J): no exclusion; the cell is
      `unresolved` with slack 0.00824 J (exactly 103/12500 J); no bundle claims an exclusion.
- [ ] The base query with approach motion declared and no speed bound, and the base query declaring an elastic
      source that the relaxation omits (planted), are refused with FROB-REF-829 and the ranked repairs.
- [ ] A bundle whose inclusion record omits a declared source is rejected by the checker although its arithmetic
      holds (KA-154's control: an exclusion issued with the approach-energy term omitted).
- [ ] With the sill height free in [10, 12] mm the answer splits at exactly 5/436 m: excluded above, unresolved
      at and below; a build that excludes the whole cell from its infeasible upper vertex is caught.
- [ ] With mass free in [7.5, 8] kg (7.5 × 9.81 × 0.012 = 0.8829 J < 0.90 J) the cell is not excluded as a
      whole; the lowering uses m_lo.
- [ ] The lowering is registered with the synthesis driver, and PILOT-C-17's energy family runs on it (the
      pilot's own acceptance; here: the fixture and the bundle hash are what the pilot record cites).
- [ ] The M4 cross-check test exists, marked `pending` until N22b-14 and N12-17 land.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-mission` lists the lowering: validity domain, side conditions, class, refusal.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/mandatory_work.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-mission mandatory_work`. Real core, real
checker, no mocks.
- `sill_exclusion_exact`: margin equals 261/6250 as a rational; dual y = 1; the bundle round-trips.
- `approach_energy_removes_exclusion`: 19/20 ≥ 2943/3125; verdict `unresolved`; slack 103/12500.
- `store_raise_removes_exclusion`: form B.
- `omitted_source_refused_829`, `unbound_approach_refused_829`.
- `cell_wide_constant_margin`: the margin has no dependence on r; both vertices checked.
- `free_height_splits_at_5_over_436`; `mass_interval_uses_lower_bound`.
- `never_inner`: property test over random rational missions (mass, rise, sources): the lowering returns only
  excluded or unresolved.
- `monotone_in_sources`: property test: adding a source or raising a cap never turns `unresolved` into excluded.
- `efficiency_unknown_is_one`: with no efficiency declared, η_max = 1 and the bound stays sound.
- `posture_dependent_rise_not_applicable`: an articulated body without a supplied h_lo: `applies` is false.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_sill_energy.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/mission_sill_energy/<utc>.ndjson`, transcript beside it). Steps: `frob --json mission check
<fixture>` and `frob --json mission synthesize <fixture> --key sill-<form>` on the base fixture (`sill-base`);
`frob-verify` on the emitted bundle; the same on forms A and B (`sill-a`, `sill-b`); the two refused variants
(`sill-unbound`, `sill-omitted`); the tampered bundle (inclusion record with one source removed). Each
synthesis carries its own key, so that no form returns another's stored receipt (convention 5; the verb is a
run verb); the determinism lane's second run uses the same keys suffixed `-r2`. Every line
carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's
evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities); `metric` lines give required work, available
energy and margin in joules against the exact values; `refusal` lines give the code and the first repair; on
failure the first differing value and the paths of the mission file and the bundle. The `summary` line gives
assertions passed and failed and `gates: []` (this family is not one of the cells of GATE-AUGUR-02 to 04; the
pilot record PILOT-C-17 cites the bundle hash logged here).

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-154 (C-31), KA-632 (mission rules) and KA-684 (A-20), one fact in three registries: 8 × 9.81 × 0.012 =
  0.94176 J against 0.90 J excludes for every wheel radius; 0.05 J of approach kinetic energy removes the
  exclusion. Exact to the printed digits.
- CX-076 (an energy budget may leave out small sources) and CX-306 (a quasi-static energy exclusion needs no
  approach energy): the tempting shortcut is the omitted term; the planted bundle without it must be rejected.
- Derived here (DESIGN, from the same inequality): the split at 5/436 m; the mass-interval control at 0.8829 J.
- Negative controls: the omitted source; the unbound approach speed; exclusion from sample points; m_hi used
  where m_lo is required (excludes [7.5, 8] kg wrongly); a non-strict comparison (at Σ c = 2943/3125 J exactly
  the exclusion must not issue).
- Metamorphic: scaling mass, store and approach energy by a common factor leaves the verdict unchanged;
  changing r changes nothing.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine, debug and release, both host platforms: identical bundle bytes.

## Considerations

- The margin is thin by construction (0.04176 J: 4.4% of the required work, 4.6% of the store). A wrong g, a rounded 0.94 J, or a rise measured to the
  wheel axle instead of the centre of mass flips the verdict; the values are exact rationals for that reason.
- A static rejection is a candidate for a dynamic test until its model contains the dynamics' resources. The
  refusal on an unbound source is the mechanism; it must not be softened to a warning.
- If KILL-FAMILYCERT fires at M3 (family certificates do not beat the generic comparator at equal cost), this
  lowering remains as a correct rule-1 instance; only the investment in further families stops.
- The cart fixture of the Arena chapter (the 12 mm sill of `fixture:cart-sill-dock`, BD-123) shares the
  geometry; this bead's mission file names its own numbers and does not import that fixture, so that a change
  to one does not silently move the other. A conformance row asserts the two sill heights agree.
