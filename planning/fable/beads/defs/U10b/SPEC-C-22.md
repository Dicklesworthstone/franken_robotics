## Background

A design campaign enumerates assignments of module types to slots: legs to hips, wheels to corners, actuators
to joints. The naive campaign dispatches one evaluation per enumerated design. But two assignments that differ
only by a symmetry of the chassis and task are the same closed loop, so most of a symmetric sweep is
duplicates. Construction C-22 removes them exactly and before any simulation: it counts the distinct designs
with the orbit-counting lemma, generates one canonical representative per orbit, and records for every member
the group element that maps it to the representative so that results are transported back correctly. Algebra
is spent at campaign time, where it deletes simulations, and not in the per-step loop.

Tier: Solid [S] for orbit enumeration and counting; Frontier [F] for wreath products (this bead) and for the
mission-observable projection quotient (N10b-12, with its pilot N10b-13). Owning chapters: the algebraic core
(crate `frob-algebra`, verb `algebra.orbits`) and design families (crate `frob-phylum`, verb
`phylum.quotient`). It serves BET-31 (campaign quotienting).

Terms. *Slots* X: the positions modules are assigned to. *Assignment*: a map from slots to module types.
*Γ_mech*: the symmetry group of the mechanism. *Γ_obs*: the subgroup of Γ_mech that also preserves every
sensor pose and field of view, the payload, the task's goal orientation and the terrain's symmetry (the
observation-preserving subgroup). *Orbit*: the set of images of an assignment under the group. *c(g)*: the
number of cycles of group element g acting on X. *ρ(g)*: the action of g on results (signed permutation of
channels). *Wreath product* K ≀ H: the group generated by a group K acting independently inside each slot and
a group H permuting the slots.

Rejected alternatives: one dispatch per enumerated design (the baseline it replaces); quotienting by the
mechanical group Γ_mech (wrong when a sensor breaks the symmetry: it merges designs the closed loop
distinguishes); hashing a canonical string without recording the mapping element (results could not be
transported back with the right signs).

### 2. Algorithms (numbered steps; DESIGN where the source leaves a choice)

**Count.** (1) Represent each g ∈ Γ as a permutation of X. (2) Compute c(g). (3) Sum k^c(g) in exact integers
and divide by |Γ|; a non-integer quotient is an error in the cycle index.

**Canonical representative with its mapping element.** (1) Fix an order on types and on slots. (2) For an
assignment a, compute the images g·a for all g ∈ Γ (the groups here have at most a few dozen elements). (3) The
representative is the lexicographically least image. (4) Record the least g (in a fixed order on Γ) with
g·a = rep. (5) Enumerate representatives directly by generating assignments in lexicographic order and keeping
those that are their own representative.

**Transport of a result.** A distributional result (a scorecard, a verdict over a world distribution that the
group preserves) is shared across the orbit. A result tied to an address is shared only after the address is
mapped through the group action; a comparison paired by address against a third design is re-run. Channel
values are mapped by ρ(g) (a signed permutation; axial quantities change sign under a mirror).

**Wreath product.** Elements are (k₁, …, k_|X|; h) with kᵢ ∈ K and h ∈ H; the action on an assignment of
(type, orientation) pairs applies h to the slots and kᵢ inside slot i. Precondition checks: every element of K
preserves the module's attachment-port invariants (port positions, port types, power signs); every element of H
preserves the decorated ports of the hub. A failed check refuses the group.

### 3. Worked fixtures (all exact)

- **Hexapod, full symmetry (KA-123).** Ten leg types on the six slots of a hexapod with dihedral symmetry of
  order 12. Cycle types: the identity (6 cycles); two rotations by ±60° (1 cycle each); two by ±120° (2 cycles
  each); the half turn (3 cycles); three mirrors through opposite slots (4 cycles each); three mirrors through
  opposite edges (3 cycles each). Count: (10⁶ + 2·10 + 2·10² + 10³ + 3·10⁴ + 3·10³)/12 = 1,034,220/12 = 86,185,
  a factor of 11.6 below 10⁶.
- **Sagittal mirror only (KA-124).** If a sensor leaves only the sagittal mirror (3 cycles): (10⁶ + 10³)/2 =
  500,500, a factor of 2.0.
- **Three types (KA-125).** (729 + 6 + 18 + 27 + 243 + 81)/12 = 1,104/12 = 92, which brute-force enumeration of
  the 3⁶ = 729 assignments confirms.
- **Wreath product (KA-126).** Two slots swapped by the hub, each holding one of k module types in one of two
  mounting orientations that the module's own half-turn symmetry identifies. Raw assignments: (2k)² = 4k². The
  group C₂ ≀ C₂ has order 2²·2 = 8. Fixed points: the identity fixes 4k²; the three elements that flip an
  orientation without swapping fix none; of the four elements that swap the slots, the two with equal
  orientation flips fix 2k each. Count: (4k² + 4k)/8 = (k² + k)/2, which is 6 for k = 3 against 36 raw
  assignments.
- **Contact strata (shared fixture with construction C-16).** Four feet with states open, stick, slip under
  the leg-permutation group C₂ × C₂: (3⁴ + 3·3²)/4 = 27.
- **Counterexample (CX-058).** Counting with Γ_mech when a front-only lidar is fitted merges designs that the
  closed loop distinguishes: 86,185 classes are reported where 500,500 are right.

### 4. Honest limits, refusals, foundations

- The factor is at most the group order; none of this is a source of large factors except on fixtures built to
  have them.
- A request to quotient under a group that is not observation-preserving is refused: FROB-REF-161 (a quotient
  or a weight tie is requested under a group that moves a sensor, the payload or the task; exit code 3;
  repairs: use Γ_obs as computed by the symmetry analysis, or remove the breaker named in the reply).
- Open question carried: whether the Mathlib module holding the orbit-counting lemma re-checks in the project's
  independent Lean checker at the pinned commit (OPEN-LEMMA-6; owner: the Lemma lead). Fail-closed default: the
  lemma is cited only after a recorded re-check; until then the count is `Tested`.
- Foundations (FACT, observed 2026-11-21 at FrankenSim `483d09c682f2`): none. `fs-symmetry` covers cyclic
  groups only.

## Considerations

- The acting group is an input computed elsewhere (the symmetry analysis). This construction is only as right
  as that group; hence the refusal for a group that is not observation-preserving and the lidar control.
- Orbit members share distributions, not per-address outcomes. Any consumer that pairs by address must map
  addresses or re-run; the contract states this in its first section.
- A design generator must not let an orbit straddle a train and test split; that rule belongs to the generator
  bead and relies on the representatives defined here.
- The numbers 430 and 130 used in tests are derived in the derivation file from the same cycle index
  ((4⁶ + 2·4 + 2·4² + 4³ + 3·4⁴ + 3·4³)/12 = 430; rotations only for k = 3: (729 + 6 + 18 + 27)/6 = 130).
