## Background

Construction C-29 treats a deterministic forkable simulator as a structural causal model. Its core (bead
SPEC-C-29) fixes what a fork and an intervention are. This bead specifies the second half: how interventions become
attributions. It extends the same contract file `docs/math/C-29.md` and the same law-suite scaffold, which is why
it depends on the core bead. Tier Solid [S] for interventional contrasts with a placebo-referenced stability
report; the Shapley summary is a budgeted mode. Owner: the Autopsy subsystem, crate `frob-autopsy`.

The problem: in a chaotic system any touch changes some outcomes. An intervention that "rescues" a failing episode
may have done so only because the fork perturbed the trajectory. C-29 therefore measures that floor with a
*placebo fork* and names a cause only when its effect beats the floor under a sequential test. Rejected
alternatives: (1) earliest-anomaly ordering, which names whatever went wrong first; (2) Shapley values as the
primary attribution: they average over coalitions, can be negative and cannot tell two alternative causes from one
joint cause; (3) the sibling's variance-based Shapley estimator: FACT
(`frankensim/crates/fs-uq/src/product_shapley.rs`) it says of itself that its effects "are not causal effects".

Terms. *Factor*: a declared mechanism path that an intervention do(factor := value) changes. *Replicates*: the
same intervention with independent tiny perturbations. *Failing worlds*: the set of recorded episodes that violate
the requirement. *Requirement*: the acceptance formula of the task (construction C-30). Proof classes: Proved (a
sealed Lean capsule), Certified (an instance certificate the offline auditor re-checks in exact arithmetic), Tested (a
property or known-answer test with a negative control), Estimated (depends on a card value, a fit or a sample).
Status of everything today: HYPOTHESIS.

## Technical approach

### 1. Definitions

- **Placebo fork.** An intervention with no mechanistic route to the outcome: one state coordinate perturbed in
  its last place (a relative 10⁻¹²). The placebo flip rate ρ₀ is the fraction of placebo forks whose verdict
  differs from the parent's. It measures how often chaos alone changes the outcome.
- **Replicates.** The same change applied with independent perturbations of the same size. A second series at a
  relative 10⁻⁷, the scale of single-precision round-off, is run for every intervention that changes the outcome.
- **Rescue rate** of an intervention c with R replicates: the fraction of replicates of do(c) in which the
  requirement is not violated. **Contrast**: the rescue rate minus ρ₀. **Flip rate under perturbation**: the
  fraction of replicates whose verdict differs from the unperturbed fork of the same intervention, reported at
  10⁻¹² and at 10⁻⁷.
- **Probability of necessity.** PN(c) = P(no failure under do(¬c) | failure), estimated over the failing worlds by
  the rescue rate.
- **Coalition value.** For a factor list N of size n and S ⊆ N, v(S) is the fraction of failures that applying all
  of S together prevents. The pair interaction is v({i, j}) − v({i}) − v({j}) + v(∅).

### 2. Statements with proof classes

| Id | Statement | Class |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | A candidate cause c is named only when PN(c) exceeds ρ₀ under a sequential test: its contrast is tested above zero against the placebo forks, by an exact one-sided test at a fixed replicate count or by an e-process (construction C-15) when forks are bought one at a time, with the level divided by the number of factors. | Tested (null calibration); the attribution itself is Estimated |
| A2 | Labels (TARGET thresholds): `but-for`, one factor with contrast at least 0.5, tested above the placebo, and flip rate at most 0.1 at 10⁻¹²; `contributing`, tested above the placebo with a smaller contrast; `joint`, no single factor rescues and a coalition does; `alternative`, several single factors each rescue; `precedes-snapshot`, no intervention at the fork tick rescues and the same change applied from the episode start does; `precision-sensitive`, the failure is not reproduced in double precision and is not admitted to the single-precision lane; `chaotic: unattributable`, ρ₀ above 0.2, and then no factor is named. | Tested |
| A3 | Shapley summary over n interventions: φᵢ = Σ over S ⊆ N∖{i} of \|S\|!·(n − \|S\| − 1)!/n!·(v(S ∪ {i}) − v(S)). It needs 2ⁿ coalitions and is computed only when no single intervention and no pair flips the outcome; exactly for n ≤ 5 (32 coalitions), by sampled permutations with an interval otherwise. | Tested (exact rational identities) |
| A4 | Σᵢ φᵢ = v(N) − v(∅); a factor that changes no coalition's value has φ = 0; factors that are interchangeable in v have equal φ. | Tested (exact) |
| A5 | Shapley values can be negative and do not identify necessary causes: the two-case fixture and the negative-value fixture below. | Tested (counterexamples) |
| A6 | Minimal repair sets are found by the 1-minimal search of construction C-28 run over interventions, with "the failure is prevented" as the test relation. | Tested |
| A7 | Budget (DESIGN): at most 12 factors, all singles and all pairs, 78 coalitions; with 8 replicates each, 64 placebo forks and the null fork that is 78·8 + 64 + 1 = 689 forks; the 10⁻⁷ series adds 8 forks per rescuing coalition. Exact Shapley over ten factors would need 2¹⁰ = 1,024 coalitions and leave no budget for one replicate. | Arithmetic, Tested |

Worked numbers (illustrative, hand-checkable): of 200 failing worlds re-run from their forks, removing the torque
limit prevents the failure in 164, nominal friction in 38 and a placebo in 12. Rescue rates 0.82, 0.19 and 0.06;
contrasts 0.76 and 0.13; the first is `but-for`, the second `contributing`. A placebo rate near a factor's rescue
rate, 30 of 200 against 38, leaves nothing to attribute.

### 3. Conventions, failure cases and non-claims

- The exact one-sided test at fixed counts is, by DESIGN of this contract, Fisher's exact test on the 2×2 table of
  (rescued, not rescued) by (intervention, placebo), at level α/m for m factors. The Autopsy chapter states "an
  exact one-sided test" without naming it; the choice is recorded here and is the Autopsy lead's to change.
- The construction names three labels (`but-for`, `joint`, `chaotic: unattributable`); the owning chapter's table
  has seven. The contract adopts the seven. In the first Shapley case below each factor is individually `but-for`
  with PN = 1 and the set is `alternative`; in the second the set is `joint`.
- An attribution has schema `frob.autopsy.attribution/1` and class Estimated: a cause in the model at this tier,
  among the listed factors. Type sketch (DESIGN): `Contrast { intervention, rescued: (u32, u32), contrast,
  flip_rate /* at 10⁻¹² and at 10⁻⁷ */, label }`; `CauseLabel { ButFor, Contributing, Joint, Alternative,
  PrecedesSnapshot, PrecisionSensitive, ChaoticUnattributable, None }`.
- Not claimed: that a successful counterfactual is a minimal repair; that a local rescue agrees with the global
  outcome; anything for a marginal failure or interacting causes beyond a set of factors.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `ka_146_shapley_two_cases`: v(∅) = 0. Case 1: v({A}) = v({B}) = v({A, B}) = 1. Case 2: v({A}) = v({B}) = 0,
  v({A, B}) = 1. Both give φ = (½, ½) exactly; case 1 labels each factor `but-for` and the set `alternative`,
  case 2 labels the set `joint`; the reports differ although φ does not.
- `shapley_negative_value`: v(∅) = 0, v({1}) = 1, v({2}) = 0, v({1, 2}) = 0 gives (½, −½), summing to 0.
- `shapley_identities`: 10⁴ seeded random v with n ≤ 5 in rationals: efficiency, null factor, symmetry.
- `shapley_gated`: when a single or a pair flips the outcome the summary is absent, with the reason.
- `budget_arithmetic`: 12 factors give 78 coalitions and 689 forks; ten factors give 1,024 coalitions.
- `contrast_and_labels_worked`: counts 164, 38 and 12 of 200 give 0.82, 0.19, 0.06 and contrasts 0.76, 0.13;
  with flip rate 0.02 the labels are `but-for` and `contributing`; 38 against a placebo of 30 names nothing.
- `level_split`: with 12 factors each test runs at α/12.
- `ka_147_placebo_chaotic`: on the synthetic model whose verdict is the parity of the last-place bits of the
  state, every one-coordinate nudge flips the verdict, ρ₀ = 1, and the report is `chaotic: unattributable` with no
  factor named even for an intervention that rescues every replicate.
- `ka_148_planted_cause_ranks_first`: 200 planted failures on the synthetic model; the planted mechanism has the
  largest contrast in at least 180 (TARGET at least 90%).
- `no_cause_names_nothing`: a failure with no planted cause names no factor.
- `flip_rate_blocks_but_for`: contrast 0.8 with flip rate 0.3 at 10⁻¹² is not labelled `but-for`.
- `null_calibration`: 10⁴ simulated studies in which the intervention equals a placebo name a factor in at most
  a fraction α of studies.
- `repair_set_is_one_minimal`: with interventions {A, B, C} and the failure prevented exactly when A and B are
  both applied, the repair set is {A, B}.
