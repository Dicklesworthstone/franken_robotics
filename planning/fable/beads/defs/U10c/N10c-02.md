## Background

Construction C-31 separates safety into three tiers (bead SPEC-C-31 holds the separation and tier 1, the energy
budget). This bead specifies tier 3: runtime filters for the whole body, also called shields. A shield is a stage
between the policy and the actuator limiter that replaces the proposed command u_π by the nearest command that
keeps a set of barrier rows satisfied. It removes model-level barrier violations during exploration at a bounded
cost per step. Tier Frontier [F]; its results are class Tested and nothing converts them into a proof. Owner: the
Reflex subsystem, crate `frob-shield`. The bead extends `docs/math/C-31.md` (part D) created by SPEC-C-31.

Design reasoning, with the rejected shortcuts. (1) Projecting onto the row and then clipping to the actuator box
is wrong: the hand-checkable case below leaves the box, and clipping it violates the row; the one-row problem is
therefore solved exactly inside the box. (2) A condition "for every disturbance there is a safe action" lets the
action be chosen after the disturbance is seen, which no controller can do; the quantifier order is ∃u ∀(w, e).
(3) Taking an infimum over the smaller friction cone for forces the environment chooses is optimistic; cones are
split. (4) A full-order certificate is not attempted: FACT (source register) sum-of-squares methods reach about
five to ten states; FACT (`frankensim/crates/fs-sos/src/roa.rs`) the sibling's region-of-attraction routine uses a
fixed quadratic Lyapunov function with a largest example of two states. The obligation "∃u ∀(w, e): a barrier
condition holds for a full-order robot" was a candidate theorem card and was rejected because it is quantified
nonlinear real arithmetic at thirty or more states; it is instead a KKT check at every step, assumption monitors
and a Tested rate.

Terms. *Barrier row*: a linear condition aᵀu ≥ b on the command, the linear-in-u form of ḣ + α(h) ≥ 0 for a barrier
function h ≥ 0 on a reduced-order template (capture point, joint limits, capsule distances; 2 to 12 states).
*Actuator box*: lo ≤ u ≤ hi. *Breach*: an assumption of the filter is false at some tick. *Alarm*: a monitor fires.
*Violation*: the safety requirement is violated in the episode. Proof classes: Proved (a sealed Lean capsule),
Certified (an instance certificate the offline auditor re-checks in exact arithmetic), Tested (a property or
known-answer test with a negative control), Estimated (depends on a card value, a fit or a sample). Status of
everything today: HYPOTHESIS.

## Technical approach

### 1. The filter

Minimize ‖u − u_π‖² in a metric R subject to rows aᵢᵀu ≥ bᵢ + δᵢ and lo ≤ u ≤ hi. The margin δ has three declared
parts: an inter-sample term L·Δt, where L is a Lipschitz bound on the time derivative of the row's left side over
a control period Δt; an estimation-error term; and the measured template-to-full term.

| Id | Statement | Class |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Quantifier order: there exists one command, chosen from delivered information, that satisfies the rows for every admitted disturbance w and estimation error e: ∃u ∀(w, e). A certificate proved in the order ∀w ∃u is rejected. | Tested (auditor rule on the quantifier prefix) |
| D2 | Split cones: forces the controller commands are restricted to the inner cone 𝒦(μ_min); forces the environment may apply range over the outer cone 𝒦(μ_max). | Tested |
| D3 | One row with a diagonal metric R is solved exactly: u(λ) = clip(u_π + λ·R⁻¹·a, lo, hi) is piecewise linear in λ with at most 2n breakpoints, aᵀu(λ) is nondecreasing, and the answer is u(λ*) for the least λ* ≥ 0 with aᵀu(λ*) ≥ b + δ, found by sorting the breakpoints. If the maximum of aᵀu over the box is below b + δ the row cannot be met within the actuators and the backup runs. | Tested against exhaustive active-set enumeration in exact rationals |
| D4 | Two to four rows take a fixed number of projected Newton steps followed by a mandatory KKT check: primal feasibility of rows and box, nonnegative multipliers, complementarity and stationarity, each within a stated tolerance. If any part fails, or the rows conflict, the step invokes a passive braking controller atomically; a partially converged iterate never reaches a driver. More than four coupled rows are refused when the shield is fitted. | Tested |
| D5 | Residual risk per episode: P(violation) ≤ E[N_breach]·δ₁ + P(N_alarm ≥ 1)·δ₂ + δ₃, with δ₁ = E[N_silent]/E[N_breach], the expected share of breaches on which every monitor stays silent (zero when no breach is expected); δ₂ = P(violation in the episode \| N_alarm ≥ 1); δ₃ = P(violation with neither a breach nor an alarm), the gap between template and body. The first term is Markov's inequality on the number of silent breaches and the second a conditional probability, so no term grows with the number of alarms. | The inequality is elementary; each δ is Estimated, with an interval |
| D6 | δ₁ must be estimated on independent hidden-state labels from the simulator, never from the monitor's own output: a monitor that is silent on every breach it cannot see reports δ₁ = 0 from its own log. | Tested (control) |
| D7 | The three numbers are estimated for one deployed policy and again when the policy changes. The bound is not claimed under a shift of the world distribution, because a shift moves δ₁ itself; shifted worlds are a separate row. | Rule, recorded |
| D8 | A sampled-and-held damping backup u = −K_d·y keeps stored energy from increasing at the ticks only under the gain bound 2·M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0 on the reflected inertia M̄ for hold period h. | Tested (hand case) |

### 2. The one-row algorithm (D3) as numbered steps

1. If aᵀ·clip(u_π) ≥ b + δ, return clip(u_π) with λ = 0.
2. For each coordinate j with a_j ≠ 0, compute the values of λ at which u_π,j + λ·a_j/R_jj meets lo_j or hi_j; keep
   those at or above 0. There are at most 2n.
3. Sort them. On each interval between consecutive breakpoints aᵀu(λ) is affine with slope Σ a_j²/R_jj over the
   coordinates not at a bound.
4. Walk the intervals from λ = 0 and return the first λ* at which aᵀu(λ*) = b + δ.
5. If the last interval ends below b + δ, report the row as infeasible in the box and invoke the backup.

### 3. Conventions, failure cases and non-claims

- Only a checked command passes on (DESIGN type `Checked<Command>`); events are `Projected { rows }`,
  `Alarm(monitor)`, `Backup(kind)`, `Uncovered`, `MarginRefused`.
- Monitors watch the assumptions: a friction estimate outside [μ_min, μ_max], innovation spikes in the estimator,
  unmodelled impact impulses, tracking error above the calibrated margin, actuator saturation. An alarm also
  triggers the backup.
- Tier 3 is Tested: no full-order certificate exists; sum-of-squares certificates are confined to reduced-order
  templates; passivity establishes neither collision avoidance nor fall avoidance.
- The template-to-full margin is measured, not computed: ε̂ is the ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉-th smallest calibration score,
  keyed by design class, policy identity, task family and tier; a shield refuses to run on a borrowed margin. When
  the index exceeds n no margin exists and the fit is refused: at the default α = 10⁻³ (OPEN-SHIELD-2) at least
  n = 999 calibration episodes are needed (⌈1000 × 0.999⌉ = 999 ≤ 999; for n = 998 the index is ⌈998.001⌉ = 999 > 998).
- Invariants and obligations carried. INV-SHIELD-01: a filtered command satisfies the actuator box exactly and the
  active rows within tolerance, or the backup controller is running. INV-SHIELD-02: a shield result is a claim
  product of class Tested; no conversion to Proved or Certified exists in the type. PO-SHIELD-02: the single-row
  projection equals the exact minimizer and never leaves the box (rational reference on 10⁶ instances). PO-SHIELD-03:
  no command leaves the filter without passing the optimality check or coming from the backup (type-state in the
  API, a compile-fail test, a planted non-convergent instance). PO-SHIELD-04: under damping injection the port
  model's stored energy is non-increasing, in continuous time for every K_d ⪰ 0 and at the ticks of a sampled, held
  command when 2·M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0.
- Refusals this contract relies on (registry N22-REF and the certificate registry; exit codes from those registries):

  | Code | Refused | Ranked repairs |
  |---|---|---|
  | FROB-REF-454 | A shield's rows have no common admissible command on the calibration set, or more than four are coupled | Reduce the row set; widen the actuator box; lower the speed limit |
  | FROB-REF-457 | A shield margin is used outside its scope key (new policy, design outside the class, other task family or tier) | Recalibrate for the new policy, class, task family or tier |
  | FROB-REF-826 | The quantifier prefix, domain or assumption set of a certificate does not match the query it is offered for (the ∀w ∃u certificate offered for ∃u ∀(w, e)) | Re-prove in the required order; narrow the query to what was proved |

### 4. Law-test layout, registry, implementers and gates

`crates/frob-shield/tests/laws_c31_filter.rs` with `tests/support/c31d_ref.rs`: the breakpoint method in exact
rationals; an exhaustive active-set solver for the same quadratic program (every subset of coordinates at lo or
hi), used as the independent oracle; a KKT checker; the risk-bound arithmetic. Generic over a trait
`C31FilterSubject`; mutants in `tests/support/c31d_mutants.rs`. Registry: KA-155 and KA-156 with derivation files;
controls seeded from CX-074 and CX-075.

Pilot (run by BD-338 and BD-339): one planar balance template with planted breaches. Falsifier (TARGET): any filter
output that fails its KKT check without invoking the fallback. Ceiling: safety evidence; no speed is claimed.
Fallback: tier 3 drops to monitoring only and tier 1 stands on its own. Gates: GATE-SHIELD-02 (the exactness
obligation holds with zero box violations; the worked example gives (1, 0.5); a planted non-convergent multi-row
instance invokes the backup); GATE-SHIELD-03 (5,000 in-envelope and 5,000 shifted-world episodes with independent
breach labels: in-envelope violations within the stated bound; shifted results reported separately; at least 85%
of unshielded return; at most 5% added step cost). Kill gate KILL-SHIELD (M4): GATE-SHIELD-03 fails after one
repair cycle; then the energy tier stays, barrier rows become reward penalties and alarms, and nothing is called a
shield.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `ka_155_one_row_worked`: R = I, u_π = (1, 0), box [0, 1]², row u₁ + u₂ ≥ 1.5: λ* = 0.5 and u = (1, 0.5).
- `project_then_clip_is_wrong`: the unboxed projection is (1.25, 0.25), outside the box; clipping it gives
  (1, 0.25), whose row value 1.25 is below 1.5.
- `breakpoint_equals_active_set_oracle`: 10⁴ seeded instances with n ≤ 6, diagonal R, random boxes, rows and
  margins, in rationals: the result equals the oracle's minimizer, satisfies KKT exactly, lies in the box, and uses
  at most 2n breakpoints; aᵀu(λ) is nondecreasing.
- `po_shield_02_million` (PO-SHIELD-02, G1 tier, `--ignored` in the fast run and executed by the e2e script):
  10⁶ seeded instances in rationals, each checked by the exact KKT conditions (which certify the unique minimizer of
  this strictly convex program) instead of the enumeration oracle; zero box violations, zero KKT failures.
- `margin_index_and_minimum_n`: with α = 10⁻³, n = 999 gives index 999 (the largest score) and n = 998 has no
  margin (index 999 > 998) and the fit is refused; with α = 0.05 and n = 39 the index is 38.
- `borrowed_margin_refused`: a margin keyed (class Q, policy P1, family F, tier T) offered for policy P2 refuses
  FROB-REF-457; the same key accepts.
- `result_class_is_tested` (INV-SHIELD-02): the filter's result type exposes class Tested and offers no constructor
  or conversion to Proved or Certified (a compile-fail case in `tests/ui/`).
- `checked_command_only` (PO-SHIELD-03, INV-SHIELD-01): a compile-fail case in which an unchecked iterate is passed
  to the driver interface does not compile; owed at the acceptance of N22b-23 (logged `pending` here until then),
  with the reference model's own type-state checked here.
- `infeasible_row_invokes_backup`: box [0, 1]², row u₁ + u₂ ≥ 2.5: backup, no command.
- `multi_row_kkt_failure_invokes_backup`: rows u₁ ≥ 0.8 and −u₁ ≥ −0.2 conflict: backup; no partial iterate is
  returned.
- `five_rows_refused`: fitting a shield with five coupled rows is refused with FROB-REF-454; four rows fit.
- `inter_sample_margin`: L = 2 per second and Δt = 0.01 s tighten b by 0.02.
- `split_cones`: with μ_min = 0.3 and μ_max = 0.8 a command that needs tangential force 0.5·N is rejected, and an
  environment force of 0.8·N is admitted into the worst case.
- `ka_156_quantifier_order`: toy with w in {−1, +1}, u in [−1, 1], safe exactly when u·w ≥ ½: for every w some u
  is safe, and no single u is safe for both; a certificate with prefix (for all w, exists u) is rejected with
  FROB-REF-826 for a claim whose prefix is (exists u, for all (w, e)).
- `risk_bound_arithmetic`: E[N_breach] = 0.2, δ₁ = 0.05, P(N_alarm ≥ 1) = 0.1, δ₂ = 0.02, δ₃ = 0.001 give 0.013.
- `self_labelling_reports_zero`: on a fixture with 40 breaches of which 10 are silent, the monitor's own log gives
  δ₁ = 0 and the hidden-state labels give 0.25.
- `sampled_damping_gain_bound` (PO-SHIELD-04): a free 1 kg slider at +1 m/s with 300 N·s/m held for 10 ms ends at −2 m/s with
  1.5 J added (2·1 − 0.01·300 < 0); with 100 N·s/m it ends at 0 m/s (2·1 − 0.01·100 ≥ 0).

## Considerations

- Open question OPEN-SHIELD-2 (the calibration level α and the per-episode violation budget): default α = 10⁻³ and
  a budget of 10⁻³ per episode; owner the project owner; it blocks GATE-SHIELD-03 and -04, not this bead.
- Cost envelope (TARGET of the owning chapter): at most 5% added step cost and at least 85% of unshielded return.
- A shield that intervenes on more than a declared fraction of ticks is flagged `shield-dominated`; the learner's
  sampled action is the command before the shield, and the applied command is logged beside it.
- Every adaptive element (the margin calibrator, the drift alarm, the alarm thresholds) ships with a replay
  scenario, evidence records, a calibration report and a demonstrated fallback, or it does not ship.
- What would make us revisit: a tier-3 result presented as Certified anywhere; a monitor estimating its own δ₁.
