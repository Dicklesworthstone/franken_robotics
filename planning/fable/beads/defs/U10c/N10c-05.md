## Background

Construction C-34 has two lanes for linkage synthesis. Bead SPEC-C-34 specifies lane 1 (exact, bounded by the caps
of the symbolic solver). This bead specifies lane 2: numerical continuation on the CPU in double precision, for
mechanism families whose synthesis or kinematics systems exceed those caps (four- and six-bar path synthesis,
Stewart–Gough forward kinematics, 6R inverse kinematics, parallel ankles). Tier Moonshot [M], at M8; nothing on
the critical path needs it. Owner: the Augur subsystem's constructive lanes, crate `frob-synth`. The bead extends
`docs/math/C-34.md` (part B).

The idea: solve one generic complex member of a family once and store it; then treat each new specification as a
parameter homotopy and track only the solution paths. Every claimed root is then certified outside the tracker,
in exact rational arithmetic. Decisions and their reasons: CPU and double precision, because path tracking needs
`f64` and the Metal backend has none, so there is no GPU lane; start systems by monodromy with a trace test, not
by a polyhedral start system, whose one-time cost for large families is unpriced; certification outside any proof
kernel, by an exact rational checker, with Smale's theorem as a named assumption of the bundle rather than a
re-proved result. FACT (observed 2026-11-21): no sibling has a homotopy solver, so this lane is new code.

The one honest sentence that governs the lane: an α-test proves a nearby nonsingular root and nothing about roots
that were not found. Hence the labels `Complete` and `Partial`.

Terms. For a polynomial system f and a point z with Df(z) invertible: β(f, z) = ‖Df(z)⁻¹·f(z)‖, the length of
the Newton step; γ(f, z) = sup over k ≥ 2 of ‖Df(z)⁻¹·Dᵏf(z)/k!‖^(1/(k−1)); α(f, z) = β(f, z)·γ(f, z).
*Monodromy*: finding all roots of a generic member by tracking loops in parameter space. *Trace test*: an
independent check that a root set is complete. *Lost path*: a tracked path that did not end at a certified root.
Proof classes: Proved, Certified (re-checked by the offline auditor in exact rationals), Tested, Estimated; status
HYPOTHESIS.

## Technical approach

### 1. Statements of lane 2

| Id | Statement | Class |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | A generic member of a mechanism family is solved once, by monodromy with a trace test, and stored as a ledgered, cached artifact. | Tested on the pilot family |
| N2 | A new task is the parameter homotopy H(z, t) = F(z; (1 − t)·p₀ + t·p₁), tracked from the stored solutions by a predictor–corrector with endgames, with recovery of ill-conditioned paths in extended precision. | Tested |
| N3 | Smale's α-test: α(f, z) < (13 − 3√17)/4 ≈ 0.157671 proves that Newton's iteration from z converges quadratically to a root within twice the Newton step, 2·β(f, z). | The theorem is a named assumption; each instance is Certified (theorem card T-34, proof kind `alpha-root`): for the given rational point the computed upper bound on α is below the threshold |
| N4 | Distinctness of two certified roots follows from disjoint balls of radius 2·β around their points. | Certified per pair |
| N5 | Non-reality follows from disjointness of the ball with its complex-conjugate ball. | Certified per root |
| N6 | Reality follows from the robust test α(f, z) < 0.03 together with ‖z − Re z‖ < 1/(20·γ(f, z)), which shows that z and its conjugate converge to one root. | Certified per root |
| N7 | A result is `Complete` only when the certified count equals an independent generic count with no lost path (and no positive-dimensional component); otherwise it is `Partial(certified, lost)`. A `Partial` result can support inclusion of the designs it found and can never support an exclusion. | Tested (label logic) |

Exact comparison for N3 without a square root (to be used by the checker): for α < 13/4, α < (13 − 3√17)/4 holds
exactly when (13 − 4·α)² > 153.

### 2. Known counts (FACT, literature, source register; to be re-read before a gate cites them)

At most 4 Burmester point pairs for five poses; 16 solutions of general 6R inverse kinematics; 40 of the
Stewart–Gough forward problem; 4,326 four-bars through nine points (1,442 cognate triples), against 286,720 paths
for the best multihomogeneous homotopy; a six-bar case in the literature needed a solution set of about 1.5
million. The generic solve for the nine-point problem is a very large one-time computation.

### 3. Hand-checkable cases

- z² − 2 at z = 3/2: β = 1/12, γ = 1/3, α = 1/36 ≈ 0.0278, certified; the root √2 is within 1/6 of 3/2. At z = 3:
  β = 7/6, γ = 1/6, α = 7/36 ≈ 0.194, not certified.
- Reality: f(z) = z³ + z at z = 0.001 + 1.001·i. α(f, z) = 0.0021 certifies a root near z, which is +i.
  α(f, Re z) = 0.0010 certifies a root near Re z, which is 0, a different root. ‖z − Re z‖ = 1.001 exceeds
  1/(20·γ(f, z)) = 0.033, so the robust test does not call the root real. A test that infers reality from
  α(f, Re z) alone calls +i real.

### 4. Conventions, failure cases and non-claims

- The checker evaluates β, γ and α as rational upper bounds at a rational (Gaussian-rational) point; the tracker's
  floating-point values are never trusted.
- Not claimed: completeness from α-tests; any GPU lane; any result for positive-dimensional solution sets.
- The fallback when the lane is withdrawn: lane 1 with multistart recovery on the CPU, every result `Partial`.

### 5. Law-test layout, registry, pilot and gate

`crates/frob-synth/tests/laws_c34_continuation.rs` with `tests/support/c34b_ref.rs`: exact β, γ and α for small
polynomial systems over Gaussian rationals; ball-disjointness tests; the label logic; a *synthetic* one-parameter
family F(z; p) = z² − p tracked by a simple predictor–corrector for the label tests. Generic over a trait
`C34ContinuationSubject`; mutants in `tests/support/c34b_mutants.rs`. Registry: KA-166 and KA-167 with derivation
files; the reality row (it has no printed KA number and is appended); the control from CX-083.

Pilot as the construction states it: the Stewart–Gough forward problem on the CPU; kill (TARGET): more than 0.1%
of paths lost, or a certified count below 40 on a generic instance. The owning chapter adds: 40 certified roots
for ten generic parameter sets by parameter homotopy from one monodromy solve; falsifier: any lost path the trace
test does not flag, or a `Complete` label on a run that missed a root. Ceiling: all isolated solutions for one
family at a time. Gate GATE-AUGUR-22 at M8 (40 certified Stewart–Gough roots on 10 generic parameter sets;
`Complete` issued only when the trace test agrees; lost paths at most 0.1% and every one flagged). Kill gate
KILL-SYNTH-CONT (M8): more than 0.1% lost paths, an unflagged lost path, or a missed known count.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `ka_167_alpha_threshold`: (13 − 3√17)/4 evaluates to 0.157671 to six digits; with the exact comparison α = 157/1000
  is accepted ((13 − 0.628)² = 153.066 > 153) and α = 158/1000 is rejected ((13 − 0.632)² = 152.967 < 153).
- `alpha_univariate`: z² − 2 at 3/2 gives (β, γ, α) = (1/12, 1/3, 1/36), certified; at 3 gives (7/6, 1/6, 7/36),
  not certified.
- `root_within_twice_newton_step`: 10⁴ seeded univariate cubics with a certified point: the true root (from
  exact isolation) lies within 2·β of the point.
- `distinct_by_disjoint_balls`: z² − 2 at ±3/2: balls of radius 1/6 are disjoint, two roots.
- `nonreal_by_conjugate_ball`: z² + 1 at z = 1.01·i: the ball and its conjugate are disjoint, so the root is not
  real.
- `reality_robust_test`: the z³ + z case: α(f, z) = 0.0021 and 1/(20·γ) = 0.033 to the printed digits, and the
  root is not called real; z² − 2 at 1.4142 + 0.0001·i passes both conditions and is called real.
- `labels`: certified 40, generic 40, lost 0 gives `Complete`; certified 39 gives `Partial(39, 1)`; certified 40
  with one lost path recorded gives `Partial(40, 1)`; an exclusion requested from a `Partial` result is refused.
- `toy_parameter_homotopy`: z² − p from p₀ = 1 to p₁ = 4: the two paths end at ±2, both certified, generic count
  2, `Complete`; with one path dropped the result is `Partial(1, 1)`.
- `ka_166_counts_table`: the registry rows carry 4, 16, 40 and 4,326 with class FACT and the re-read flag;
  3 × 1,442 = 4,326.
