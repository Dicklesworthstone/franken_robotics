## Background

Construction C-35 turns simulated loads into a part by three computations; bead SPEC-C-35 holds the frame and the
first (topology optimization). This bead specifies the second: cumulative fatigue damage. Stress histories at
gauges are reduced to cycles by rainflow counting, and damage accumulates by the linear rule over the lives the
material's stress–life curve assigns to those cycles. Tier Frontier [F]. Owner: the Foundry subsystem, crate
`frob-foundry`. The bead extends `docs/math/C-35.md` (part C), which is why it depends on the core bead.

Why it matters and what goes wrong. Rare overload cycles dominate life: in the worked case below 0.1% of the
cycles carry 11% of the damage, so a campaign that never contains them certifies nothing, and their rates come
from the adversarial-world subsystem. And a stress history arrives in windows (episodes), so a counter that
closes its books at every window end loses the cycles that span windows: the residue of open half-cycles must be
carried between windows and closed once. FACT (FrankenSim `483d09c682f2`, observed 2026-11-21): the sibling counter
`RainflowMiner` of `fs-material` retains rainflow residues across calls and warns that short windows do not prove
stabilization. Rejected shortcuts: counting window by window; dropping rare cycles from a history; feeding an
unsigned stress measure (von Mises) to a cycle counter.

What is not claimed: fatigue and wear laws are empirical, so every life figure is Estimated, and the linear rule
ignores load order.

Terms. *Gauge*: a location and plane at which signed stress is recorded. *Turning point*: a local peak or valley
of the history. *Range*: the absolute difference of two consecutive turning points. *Cycle* and *half-cycle*: the
units a rainflow count produces. *Residue*: the turning points still open when a window ends. *S–N curve*: life
N(S) in cycles at stress amplitude S. Proof classes: Proved, Certified, Tested, Estimated; status HYPOTHESIS.

## Technical approach

### 1. Statements

| Id | Statement | Class |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Stress histories at gauges are reduced to cycles by rainflow counting on turning points. | Tested against a brute-force reference on short histories |
| F2 | Damage accumulates by the linear rule D = Σᵢ nᵢ/N(Sᵢ) over counted cycles, with nᵢ the number of cycles at amplitude Sᵢ and a half-cycle counting one half; failure is predicted at D = 1. | Definition; arithmetic Tested exactly |
| F3 | Basquin's curve: N(S) = (S/S₀)⁻ᵐ, so multiplying the amplitude by a factor q divides the life by qᵐ. | Definition; Tested |
| F4 | The residue of open half-cycles is carried between windows: counting in windows with carried residues and one final close equals the whole-history count (relation MR-34). | Tested (exact equality) |
| F5 | The linear rule ignores load order: D is unchanged when blocks of cycles are permuted. | Tested; recorded as a limit, not a virtue |
| F6 | Every life figure is Estimated. A curve is valid only for the material state, the process and the surface finish it was measured for; otherwise the result is `fatigue: no-claim`. | Rule of the claim product |

### 2. The counting procedure the contract fixes (three-point rainflow)

1. Reduce the history to turning points. Keep a stack and a starting point S (the first point).
2. Read the next turning point onto the stack; if none remains go to step 6.
3. If the stack holds fewer than three points go to step 2. Let X be the range of the two most recent points and
   Y the range of the two before them.
4. If X < Y go to step 2.
5. If Y does not contain S, count Y as one cycle, remove its two points and go to step 3. If Y contains S, count Y
   as one half-cycle, remove its first point, move S to its second point and go to step 3.
6. Close: count each remaining range as one half-cycle.

Windowed operation: the stack and S are the residue. At a window end nothing is counted and the residue is kept;
the next window continues at step 2; step 6 runs once, at the end of the campaign.

### 3. Worked cases (hand-checkable)

- Damage: with m = 3, 10⁶ cycles at an amplitude of life 2·10⁶ give D = 0.5. A further 10³ cycles at five times
  the amplitude, of life 2·10⁶/5³ = 16,000, add 0.0625. So 10³ of 1,001,000 cycles (0.1%) carry 0.0625/0.5625 =
  11% of the damage.
- Residue: the history 0, 10, 0 split into the windows [0, 10] and [10, 0] holds one full cycle of range 10 (two
  half-cycles); a counter that drops residues at window ends counts none.
- Window close: the history 0, 10, 5, 8, 0 counts one cycle of range 3 and two half-cycles of range 10; with m = 3
  the damage is proportional to 27 + 1,000 = 1,027. Closing the books after the window (0, 10, 5) and again after
  (5, 8, 0) counts half-cycles of ranges 10, 5, 3 and 8, proportional to (1,000 + 125 + 27 + 512)/2 = 832, which
  is 19% low.

### 4. Conventions, failure cases and non-claims

- Signed stress only. The mean-stress policy is named in every result. Cycles that span tasks (a loaded leg, then
  an unloaded one) are counted, because the campaign's duty sequence is part of the history.
- Utilization for a service duration H from damage D accumulated over H_c campaign hours is U_f = (D/H_c)·H,
  against a limit of 1 divided by the card's scatter factor (carried from the owning chapter).
- A damage figure that changes by more than 5% when the window length is halved fails the construction's pilot.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `ka_169_linear_damage`: the damage case above in exact rationals: 1/2, then 1/16 more, total 9/16; the overload
  share is 1/9.
- `basquin_scaling`: with m = 3 an amplitude factor 5 gives a life factor 1/125; with m = 5 a factor 2 gives 1/32.
- `ka_170_residue_carried`: windows [0, 10] and [10, 0] with carried residue give one full cycle of range 10.
- `windowed_equals_whole` (MR-34): 10⁴ seeded integer histories of up to 200 turning points, split at random
  window boundaries: carried-residue counting equals the whole-history count exactly.
- `close_per_window_is_wrong`: the history 0, 10, 5, 8, 0 gives 1,027 whole and 832 when closed per window.
- `counts_sum_property`: the number of half-cycle equivalents equals the number of ranges in the turning-point
  sequence.
- `load_order_blindness`: two blocks of cycles separated by a return to zero give the same D in either order.
- `turning_point_reduction`: plateaus and monotone runs collapse to their end points.
- `no_claim_without_valid_curve`: a curve whose process tag differs from the part's gives `fatigue: no-claim`.
- `half_window_stability`: on a stationary synthetic history the damage rate changes by under 5% when the window
  length is halved with carried residues, and by more than 5% for the mutant that closes per window.
