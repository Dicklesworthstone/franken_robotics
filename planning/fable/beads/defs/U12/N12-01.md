## Background

Lemma decides how a statement becomes `Certified` or `Proved`. That decision rests on what the two proof siblings
can actually check, and those capabilities are facts with hard caps, observed by audit of `franken_lean` at
`4426f8ea7` and `frankensympy` at `29f847c1` (FrankenSim at `483d09c682f2` adds `fs-ivl`, `fs-sos` and
`fs-checker`). If the producer does not know the caps, it either fails late inside a tool or, worse, presents a
statement under a route that never decided it. This bead puts the capability table into a registry that the
producer reads, and implements the route chooser that the rest of Lemma uses.

Three consequences of the facts are architecture decisions:

- ADR-LEMMA-01, the algebraic route first: what is certified is formulated as a polynomial or rational identity
  (Cayley maps, quaternion norms, adjoint matrices), an exact rational inequality or an interval enclosure.
  Analytic statements about exp, log and manifolds wait. Reason: it is what both proof tools can check today.
  Rejected: stating identities through the matrix exponential (the Mathlib modules are blocked in FrankenLean and
  FrankenSymPy has no Lie-algebra or quaternion module in Rust).
- ADR-LEMMA-02, Lean is a second opinion, not a prover we drive: theorems are written in stock Lean with Mathlib
  and FrankenLean re-checks the compiled result when the dependency closure avoids the blocked modules. Reason:
  FrankenLean does not elaborate tactic proofs today. Rejected: generating tactic scripts for FrankenLean.
- Reflection is the one route from an instance to a proof, Frontier, measured before it is relied on (BD-382).

Tier: Solid (a registry and a pure function). A foundation that exists is not a foundation that is admitted:
admission is the job of the suite-consumption gates (INT-FSYM-01, INT-FLN-01).

## Scope

In: `registries/prooftools.toml` with every capability row and cap; a load-time check that the pins in the registry
equal the pins of the stack lock; the `Route` type and `choose_route`; budget pre-checks that refuse or downgrade
before a tool is invoked; the re-audit test that re-observes the capabilities at the pinned commits.
Out: invoking the tools (BD-019, BD-021, BD-380); the theorem-card registry (N12-06); the budget pilot (N12-22).

## Technical approach

1. Registry rows (FACT, each row carries `tool`, `pin`, `capability`, `status`, `caps`, `source_path`):

| Capability | FrankenSymPy | FrankenLean |
|---|---|---|
| Exact polynomial identity checking with an offline verifier | Yes (`fsym-proof-kernel`, `fsym-capsule-consumer`) | Not natively |
| Interval certificates: roots in a box, enclosures, sign over a box | Yes, up to 16 coordinates and 1,024 parameter cells | No |
| Exact rational linear algebra with certificates (LU, QR, LDLᵀ) | Yes | No |
| Gröbner bases, ideal membership | Buchberger over ℚ and 𝔽ₚ, cap of 1,024 basis polynomials; no F4 | No |
| Real solutions of polynomial systems | `real_rur`: at most 8 variables, 64 equations, degree 256, 4,096-bit coefficients; zero-dimensional ideals only; no homotopy solver | No |
| Lie algebras, quaternions, noncommutative symbols, group theory | None in Rust | not applicable |
| Translating a symbolic certificate into a Lean statement | `fsym-formal`: two univariate integer factor-product profiles (64-bit, degree at most 64) | not applicable |
| Elaborating Lean source with Mathlib tactics | not applicable | No: `MetaM`/`TacticM` are not wired; no Mathlib tactic has run |
| Tactic automation of its own | not applicable | `fln-anvil`: a decision-tree procedure and `omega` (integer linear arithmetic), 1,423 lines; other tactic names are type stubs |
| Re-checking compiled `.olean` files with an independent kernel | not applicable | Yes: Lean stdlib 2,433 of 2,433 modules; Mathlib 9,131 of 11,083 accepted, 0 failed |
| Mathlib modules a robotics proof would want | not applicable | Accepted: `Algebra.Lie.Basic`, `Algebra.Lie.OfAssociative`, `Algebra.Quaternion`, special linear and unitary groups, permutation groups, `Order.FixedPoints`. Blocked: `Geometry.Manifold.Algebra.LieGroup`, `Analysis.Normed.Algebra.MatrixExponential`, `Analysis.Quaternion`, all representation theory |
| Sealed offline proof bundles | Proof capsules | `fln seal-capsule` / `verify-capsule --require-semantic` |
| Bit-vector decisions with checked proofs | not applicable | Yes (`Engine::decide_bv`) |

   Further caps recorded: FrankenSymPy's bytecode interpreter caps an expression at 8,192 operations;
   `CertifyRootBox` handles at most 16 coordinates; FrankenLean's suite lock certifies only
   `x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`; a full Mathlib re-check peaked at 92.8 GiB. FrankenSim: `fs-ivl` (outward-rounded
   intervals, affine forms, Taylor models, interval Newton and Krawczyk), `fs-sos`, `fs-checker` (checks packages
   outside the stepping process).
2. `Route` (DESIGN): `PolyIdentity`, `IdealMembership { side_relations }`, `RationalLinearAlgebra`,
   `ExactInequality`, `IntervalEnclosure`, `ExhaustiveEnumeration`, `TraceReplay`, `BitVector`,
   `LeanCapsule { closure_ok }`, `Reflected { reflector }`, `RandomizedIdentityTest` (class `Tested`),
   `NotDecidable { instead }`.
3. `choose_route(statement_shape, sizes) -> Result<Route, Refusal>` is a pure table lookup over the statement's
   shape (polynomial identity, identity modulo side relations, rational inequality, enclosure over a box of d
   coordinates, finite enumeration, Lean general statement with import list) and its sizes. Rules: a polynomial
   expression above 8,192 operations is split or routed to `RandomizedIdentityTest`; an ideal-membership problem
   whose basis estimate exceeds 1,024 polynomials is `budget-exceeded`; a box above 16 coordinates or 1,024 cells
   is refused for `IntervalEnclosure`; a Lean statement whose import closure meets a blocked module returns
   `LeanCapsule { closure_ok: false }` and the card stays at its instance class; a statement about exp, log or a
   manifold returns `NotDecidable`.
4. Every produced certificate records the route and the registry revision in its receipts, so a later change of a
   cap is visible in the artifact graph.
5. Re-audit: a script in `tools/oracle/prooftools_audit.sh` re-observes each capability at the pinned commits
   (builds the named crates, runs their own self-tests, counts accepted modules from FrankenLean's ledger) and
   diffs the result against the registry. A drift is an error until the registry is updated by a dated edit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `registries/prooftools.toml` holds every row and cap above, each with a pin and a source path; it is
      schema-valid and loaded by `frob-lemma` at start-up; a pin that differs from the stack lock is a load error.
- [ ] `choose_route` returns the stated route for each of the 35 theorem cards' first route and refuses the twelve
      non-decidable obligations with `NotDecidable`. The card shapes come from a fixture,
      `crates/frob-lemma/tests/fixtures/card_shapes.toml` (DESIGN: card, statement shape, sizes, expected route,
      transcribed from §12.3), because the card registry (N12-06) depends on this bead; the twelve obligations come
      from the table "Obligations that no checker decides" of §12.3, each with the text of its "what it is instead".
- [ ] A statement over the caps is refused or downgraded before any tool process starts, with the cap named.
- [ ] The re-audit script reproduces the registry at the pins, or fails naming the row that drifted.
- [ ] `choose_route` and the registry revision are exposed for the card registry: N12-06 (which depends on this
      bead) fills the route and revision columns of `frob prove list --json` from them, and that output is
      observed there.
- [ ] `frob-lemma`'s `CONTRACT.md` (ten sections) states the caps under "validity domain" and "known gaps".
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/lemma_prooftools.sh` passes and its log ends with a `summary` line.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-lemma/tests/route.rs`:
- `route_poly_identity_small`: a 500-operation identity routes to `PolyIdentity`.
- `route_poly_identity_over_cap`: 8,193 operations is not `PolyIdentity`.
- `route_interval_17_coordinates_refused`; `route_interval_1025_cells_refused`.
- `route_groebner_basis_estimate_over_1024_budget_exceeded`.
- `route_lean_blocked_import`: an import list containing `Mathlib.Analysis.Normed.Algebra.MatrixExponential`
  gives `closure_ok: false`; one containing only `Mathlib.Order.FixedPoints` gives `closure_ok: true`.
- `route_analytic_statement_not_decidable`: "exp(x)·exp(−x) = I" is `NotDecidable`.
- `registry_pin_mismatch_is_load_error` (negative control: edit one pin).
- `route_table_matches_card_fixture`: for each of the 35 rows of `card_shapes.toml`, `choose_route` returns the
  expected first route (for example T-01 `PolyIdentity`, T-19 `RationalLinearAlgebra`, T-24 and T-26
  `ExhaustiveEnumeration`, T-27 `TraceReplay`, T-35 `LeanCapsule`); a mismatch prints card, shape and both
  routes.
- `twelve_obligations_not_decidable`: each of the twelve obligations returns `NotDecidable` with its `instead`
  text; none returns a route that could issue `Certified` or `Proved`.
- `cap_refused_before_spawn`: a planted 9,000-operation expression is refused or downgraded with the cap named
  (8,192) and the tool-process spawn counter stays at zero; likewise a 17-coordinate box.
- `receipt_records_route_and_revision`: a certificate produced through the chooser carries the route and the
  registry revision in its receipts.
- `choose_route_is_pure`: the same inputs give byte-identical serialized routes across 1,000 calls and in a
  second process.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead's own script, `scripts/e2e/lemma_prooftools.sh` (header `bead=N12-01 milestone=M0
labels=lemma,frob-lemma seed=0 needs=cpu`; sources the STD-E2E helper, idempotent, writes only under `target/`;
NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/lemma_prooftools/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it), built on this bead's
dependencies only: `audit`, `tools/oracle/prooftools_audit.sh` (this bead's re-audit), one `metric` line per
capability row with observed and registered values; `routes`, `cargo run -p frob-lemma --example choose_route --
--json` (an example target of this bead: it loads the registry, runs `choose_route` over the 35 rows of
`card_shapes.toml` and the twelve obligations, and prints per row the shape, the sizes, the route or the refusal
with the cap named, and the registry revision), asserting the 35 expected first routes and twelve `NotDecidable`
answers with their `instead` text; `controls`, each through `e2e_expect_caught`: the example with `--registry
<scratch copy>` whose one pin is edited (a load error), the re-audit against a scratch registry claiming a blocked
module accepted (fails naming the row), and the 9,000-operation and 17-coordinate shapes (refused or downgraded
with the cap named, tool-process spawn counter zero). `scripts/e2e/lemma_kernel_cert.sh` (bead N12-33, which
depends on this bead) later prints the route of each M0 certificate. Every NDJSON line carries `ts`, `script`,
`bead` ("N12-01"), `step` (`<tool>/<capability>`, `route/<card-or-obligation>` or `control/<name>`), `event`, the
exact command line (the crate build or self-test invoked, the ledger read, or the example run), exit code, wall
time, the pin observed and the pin in the stack lock, the registered and observed values of the row with units
(operations, coordinates, cells, polynomials, modules), the expected and observed route, the registry revision
and its BLAKE3, and on a drift or mismatch the first differing value with the path to the observation; the final
`summary` line gives rows checked, rows drifted, routes matched, controls caught and the overall verdict.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers are the counts in the table (2,433 of 2,433; 9,131 of 11,083; 1,024; 8,192; 16; 8, 64, 256, 4,096).
Negative controls: a registry edited to claim a blocked module is accepted must fail the re-audit; a route that
skips the cap check on a planted 9,000-operation expression must be caught by `route_poly_identity_over_cap`.
Determinism: `choose_route` is a pure function; identical output on every platform.

## Considerations

- The facts are dated; every one is re-read at the pins before a gate cites it. RISK-07 (FrankenSymPy budgets),
  RISK-16 (Lean needs a large-memory Linux host), RISK-11 ("certified" as a marketing word while the tools stay
  immature).
- Upstream items that would move rows: FD-FSYM-01 to -05 (code generation and the operation cap, a Lie-algebra
  module, permutation groups and character tables, certificate families for matrix identities and rational Gram
  matrices, faster Gröbner bases), FD-FLN-01 (unblock the Mathlib roots), FD-FLN-02 (macOS aarch64 lock). Each has
  an interim posture: the algebraic route with explicit matrices, small-group code in `frob-algebra`, entry-wise
  identities, Lean work on a Linux host.
- Process is never the product: the registry exists because `choose_route` reads it and the re-audit checks it.
