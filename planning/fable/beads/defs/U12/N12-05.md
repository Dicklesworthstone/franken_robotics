## Background

A mission answer is certified cell by cell. A family certificate is `Certified` evidence about a set of designs:
either a cell of the inner set (every design in it meets the mission) or a cell removed from the outer set (no
design in it does). Beyond what a kernel certificate carries, it has a quantifier prefix, a domain with its chart
and units, an assumption set with polarity, a proof kind from the closed registry, and a witness. It is a
statement about the declared model, world set and policy class, and its class is capped by its inputs: an exact
argument over an `Estimated` material card is `Estimated`.

Polarity is what makes a certificate reusable after an amendment without recomputing it. Write F(W, Π, A) for the
family of designs for which some policy in the class Π succeeds in every world of W under acceptance predicate A.
F is antitone in W, monotone in Π (more information, earlier packets, more actuator authority) and monotone in A
ordered by implication. Hence:

| Amendment | Inner certificate | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| W shrinks | survives | must be rechecked |
| W grows | must be rechecked | survives |
| Π grows | survives | must be rechecked |
| Π shrinks | must be rechecked | survives |
| A weakens | survives | must be rechecked |
| A strengthens | must be rechecked | survives |

Doctrine rule D-11 governs reuse: a hash identifies bytes; only an implication licenses skipping a recheck. A
verdict is reused after a change only under a recorded implication: polarity in the assumption lattice, a
monotonicity argument, a symmetry together with its stabilizer check, or equality of every dependency. Tier:
Frontier (family certificates are adopted with kill gates KILL-FAMILYCERT and KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT). Rejected
alternative: reusing a verdict because the sub-term's hash still matches (CX-037: an edit to a shared bus or a
base-coupled contact invalidates results whose own hashes are unchanged).

## Technical approach

```rust
// DESIGN: fixes intent, not signatures.
pub struct FamilyCertificate {
    claim: FamilyClaim,               // Inclusion | Exclusion | Partition
    prefix: Vec<Binder>,              // ordered quantifiers; part of the statement
    domain: Domain,                   // chart, units, cell with open or closed ends
    assumptions: Vec<Assumption>,     // id, value or digest, axis (World | Policy | Acceptance), survives: Grow | Shrink
    kind: ProofKind,                  // closed registry frob.cert.kinds/1
    witness: WitnessPayload,
    evidence: Evidence<Proof>,        // class set here, by the in-tree check, never by the caller
}
```

1. Construction: `FamilyCertificate::from_proof_object(obj, query, inputs)` runs the in-tree check, then sets
   `class = min(kind cap, min over inputs' classes, min over assumptions' classes)`. There is no constructor that
   takes a class. An input is every artifact the argument reads: actuator, interface and material card fields
   (one evidence leaf per field, by that field's provenance), the model, the world-set digest, sub-certificates.
2. Polarity assignment: for each assumption, the axis and the claim decide `survives`. For an inclusion: World →
   `shrink`, Policy → `grow`, Acceptance → `weaken` (serialized as `grow` of the accepted set). For an exclusion
   the opposite. The specimen's two assumptions follow: `authority: independent normal forces` is a Policy
   assumption of an inclusion, `survives: grow`; `world-set` is a World assumption, `survives: shrink`.
3. Reuse rule: `attach_existing(cert, changed_query)` succeeds only with a recorded implication, one of
   `Polarity { axis, direction }`, `Monotone { proof }` (a `monotone-transport` sub-certificate),
   `Symmetry { element, stabilizer_check }` (a `symmetry-transport` sub-certificate), or `SameDependencies`
   (equality of every dependency digest). Otherwise it refuses with FROB-REF-835 (a verdict is reused after a
   change without a recorded implication; repairs, ranked: record the implication that licenses the reuse;
   re-derive the certificate for the changed query; drop the claim to unresolved).
4. Presenting a result above the class of its weakest input, or as `Proved` or `Certified` without a certificate
   hash and a checker identity, is refused with FROB-REF-834 (repair: restate at the weakest input's class and
   name that input; the first next action offered is the measurement that would raise it).
5. Scope text: every family certificate serializes the sentence that it is about the declared model, world set
   and policy class, and carries the three no-authorization flags, false.
6. Ledger: the artifact graph records, per certificate, the assumption set with polarity, the inputs with their
   classes, and the implication used at each reuse.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `FamilyCertificate` cannot be built with a caller-chosen class (compile-fail test) and its class equals the
      minimum over kind cap, inputs and assumptions on the fixtures.
- [ ] An exact stance inclusion whose capacity field comes from a card field marked `assumed` has class
      `Estimated` and names that field as the weakest input (CX-109).
- [ ] PO-LEMMA-03: each row of the polarity table gives the stated survival on the docking and stance fixtures,
      by fresh evaluation of all twelve moves per fixture (six amendments × inclusion and exclusion); zero
      survivors contradicted by fresh evaluation.
- [ ] Reuse without a recorded implication is refused with FROB-REF-835; reuse with a polarity implication in the
      wrong direction is refused.
- [ ] The serialized certificate passes the schema validator and the auditor on the two fixtures.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/lemma_family_polarity.sh` passes and its log ends with a `summary` line counting zero
      survivors contradicted by fresh evaluation.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-lemma/tests/family.rs`:
- `class_cap_estimated_card` (expects `Estimated`, weakest input = the card field path).
- `polarity_docking_inclusion`: the inclusion at (U, ε) = (¾, ¼) (bound (U + ½)/4 = 5/16) survives ε falling to
  1/8 and U rising to 1; fresh evaluation confirms both; the opposite moves (ε rising to 3/8, U falling to ½, where
  the bound is ¼ and ε = ¼ is no longer strictly below it) are marked `recheck`, and fresh evaluation shows that
  both fail.
- `polarity_docking_exclusion`: the exclusion at (¾, 3/8) survives ε rising and U falling; ε falling to ¼ and U
  rising to 1 are marked `recheck`. Fresh evaluation: at ε = ¼ the cell is admitted (¼ < 5/16), so the exclusion
  is withdrawn; at U = 1 the bound is 3/8 and ε = 3/8 is at equality, still excluded, so that recheck was
  conservative and the exclusion is re-issued by the fresh solve, never by reuse.
- `polarity_world_grows_gain`: adding the hidden gain 33/32 keeps every exclusion and marks every inclusion
  `recheck`; the inclusion at (¾, 1/64) reverses, the least worst-case terminal error becoming 1/65 m.
- `polarity_stance_policy_shrinks`: the inclusion at b = ¼ m with allocation (155, 135, 105, 5) N is marked
  `recheck` when the authority changes to passive equal springs; fresh evaluation gives (175, 115, 85, 25) N,

- `polarity_twelve_moves_table`: a table-driven test over both fixtures and all twelve moves compares the

- `reuse_without_implication_refused` (FROB-REF-835); `reuse_same_dependencies_ok`;

- `class_not_settable` (compile-fail: no public constructor or setter takes a class);
  `class_is_minimum_on_fixtures` (kind cap, inputs and assumptions each made the weakest in turn).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead's own script, `scripts/e2e/lemma_family_polarity.sh` (header `bead=N12-05 milestone=M1
labels=lemma,frob-lemma seed=0 needs=cpu`; sources the STD-E2E helper, idempotent, writes only under `target/`).
The verbs that expose family certificates (`prove.bundle`, BD-133; `mission.synthesize`, BD-135) depend on this
bead, so the script drives the crate from outside through one example program, `cargo run -p frob-lemma
--example family_polarity -- <args> --json` (DESIGN: an example built on this bead and its dependencies only,
printing JSON). Steps: (1) `start`: commit, toolchain, host fingerprint; (2) `moves`: `--fixture docking --moves
all` and `--fixture stance --moves all`, the twelve moves per fixture (six amendments × inclusion and exclusion),
each with its recorded polarity, predicted survival and fresh verdict, asserting zero survivors contradicted;
(3) `reuse`: `--probe reuse-none`, `--probe reuse-wrong-direction` (each FROB-REF-835 with its first repair) and
`--probe reuse-same-dependencies` (reused); (4) `class`: `--probe assumed-capacity`, asserting class `Estimated`
and the card field path as the weakest input; (5) `serialize`: the two fixtures' certificates written to
`target/e2e-scratch/lemma_family_polarity/`, then `frob-verify --json` (BD-132's checkers) under `env -i` on each,
asserting `accept`, the scope sentence and the three false flags; (6) `control`: `--probe stale-verdict` (one
dependency edge omitted) through `e2e_expect_caught`, the reuse contradicted by fresh replay. Every NDJSON line
(`target/e2e-logs/lemma_family_polarity/<utc>.ndjson`, transcript beside it) carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`
("N12-05"), `step` (`<stage>/<fixture>/<move-or-probe>`), `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time,
the certificate hash, the assumption changed with its axis and `survives` value, the implication recorded (or the
FROB-REF-835 refusal), the class with its weakest input, the predicted and fresh verdicts, the auditor's verdict
where step 5 ran, and on a contradiction the certificate, the move and the path to reproduce; the `summary` line
counts survivors contradicted by fresh evaluation (must be zero) and assertions passed and failed.
`scripts/e2e/lemma_second_machine.sh` (bead N12-35, which depends on this bead) may run this script as a stage
of its amendment step (step 8), which repeats the docking read-error and stance-authority amendments through
`frob mission synthesize --from … --set …` and logs its lines under the same `bead` value.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-221 (class of a combined result is the minimum; an exact argument over an `Estimated` card is `Estimated`),
KA-224 (polarity table on the docking and stance fixtures). CX-109, CX-037, CX-045 (one policy per world offered
for one policy for all worlds). Metamorphic: swapping the order of two independent amendments does not change
which certificates survive. Determinism: D0 for the bookkeeping.

## Considerations

- For designs with an existential controller the per-world answers do not intersect to the joint answer:
  F(V) ⊆ ⋂ F({w}), strictly where the controller cannot observe in time which world it is in. Polarity never
  licenses intersecting per-world answers.
- Polarity is an evidence rule, not a speed lever: the measured reuse share of compute is small.
- KILL-FAMILYCERT (M3) and KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT govern the mechanism; on the fallback branch the type still
  carries generic-solver answers.
- An inclusion proved for independently commanded forces is false for a passive suspension; the authority model
  is therefore a typed assumption, never a comment.
