## Background

Every result of the system is proved, certified, tested or estimated, and says which. The four classes:

| Class | What it means | Produced by |
|---|---|---|
| `Proved` | A general statement accepted by a Lean kernel, and re-checked by FrankenLean when its closure permits. Also an instance evaluated by the kernel through a reflected checker whose soundness theorem is `Proved`. | `lean/` plus `fln verify-capsule` |
| `Certified` | An instance-level statement (this kernel, this model, this box, this cell of a design family) accepted by a checker with an offline-verifiable certificate. | FrankenSymPy proof kernel, interval certificates, exact rational certificates, the kernel-IR dimension checker, `frob-verify` |
| `Tested` | A property held on a stated set of cases. | The verification tiers G0 to G5 |
| `Estimated` | A number from a model or a statistical procedure, with its uncertainty. | Everything else |

Two rules follow. A report never prints `Proved` or `Certified` without the certificate's content hash and the
checker's identity (INV-LEMMA-01). "Verified" is not a class and the word is not used for results, anywhere in the
product's output. The reason for both is the same: a word that sounds like assurance and carries no receipt is how
"certified" becomes a marketing word while the proof tools stay immature (the pre-mortem's failure 11).

Lemma also has a fixed list of things it does not claim, and prove-cluster output must say so where a reader would
otherwise infer more. Tier: Solid. Rejected alternative: a style guide (a rule that lives in a style guide is a
path around the rule; the checks here run on generated output).

## Scope

In: the serialization of classes and of the Proof wording in `frob-lemma` output, with its single formatter; the
export check for INV-LEMMA-01; the non-claims scope block, its wording and where it is printed; the dynamic scan
for the banned word over the output captured by the Lemma end-to-end scripts.

Out, and where it is:
- the static scan for the banned word over every string the product can emit that is known before run time
  (verb help and descriptions, output schemas, refusal texts, `robot-docs`, report templates, the help of
  `frob-verify`), its pattern, its exemptions and its planted-word control: lint L-VERIFIED of the
  documentation lints (N00-01, test `doc_lints`). This bead depends on it, adds no second pattern and no second
  static test, and uses its exported matcher for the dynamic scan;
- the claim-product type (BD-005); the boundary checks of the claim product in the safety chapter (BD-308); the
  `reality:` line (BD-139); the non-decidable registry (N12-07).

## Technical approach

1. Proof wording, the only two forms a report may use:
   `proved: ⟨statement⟩; checker ⟨name and build⟩; certificate ⟨hash⟩` and
   `certified: ⟨statement⟩; auditor frob-verify ⟨build⟩; bundle ⟨hash⟩`.
   A formatter in `frob-lemma` (`fmt_proof_claim(evidence) -> String`) is the single producer of these strings; it
   takes `Evidence<Proof>` and reads the hash and checker from its receipts, so a string without them cannot be
   formed.
2. INV-LEMMA-01 as a schema check on every export: in every output schema of the prove cluster and in the
   envelope's `evidence` block, the fields `certificate` (or `bundle`) and `checker` are required whenever `class`
   is `Proved` or `Certified`. The exporter validates before writing; a violation is refused with FROB-REF-834
   (a result is presented above the class of its weakest input, or as `Proved` or `Certified` without a
   certificate hash and a checker identity; repair: attach the receipt or restate the class).
3. Banned word. The static scan is not built here: lint L-VERIFIED (N00-01) scans every string the product can
   emit that is known statically (verb help and descriptions generated from `registries/verbs.toml`, every
   output schema's descriptions and examples, refusal texts in the refusal registry, `robot-docs` output, report
   templates, and the help of `frob-verify`). Its pattern is the stem `verif` used of a result, case-insensitive
   ("verified", "verification passed", "verifies that"); in product strings the word has no permitted
   occurrence and no allow-list entry; the identifiers `frob-verify`, `prove.verify` and `verify-capsule` are
   different tokens and pass. What this bead adds is the dynamic check: `frob-conformance` exposes
   `banned_word::scan_dir(dir) -> Vec<Match>`, which applies that same matcher (`banned_word::find`, exported
   by N00-01) to every transcript and captured envelope under a directory of end-to-end logs, and the Lemma
   scripts call it on their own output. (An earlier draft of this bead specified a separate static test matching
   only the whole word "verified"; it is withdrawn in favour of the one pattern, DESIGN: the whole-word form
   misses "verification passed".)
4. Scope block. `prove.kernel`, `prove.list` (one array per listing, not per row), `prove.bundle` and report
   sections that cite a certificate print it (JSON field `not_claimed`, an array of fixed strings; text form one
   line each). The block comes from one function, `lemma::not_claimed()`; this bead wires it into the two verbs of
   BD-027 (`prove.kernel`, `prove.list`), and `prove.bundle` (BD-133, which builds on this bead) prints it through
   the same function. The strings, in order:
   - It is not claimed that the simulator as a whole is correct. Named kernels satisfy named identities, and named
     cells of a design family satisfy named inequalities under a declared model.
   - It is not claimed that floating-point results satisfy the identities exactly. A certificate is about the
     straight-line program over the rationals with square roots; the floating-point gap is measured by tests, not
     proved.
   - It is not claimed that any Mathlib result about Lie groups, matrix exponentials or representation theory has
     been re-checked by FrankenLean; those modules are blocked today.
   - Nothing is claimed about a physical robot. A family certificate is about the declared model, world set and
     policy class, and every bundle carries the three no-authorization flags.
   - A statistical receipt is not a proof. The auditor recomputes the arithmetic of a study; it cannot attest
     that the episodes ran.
   - It is not claimed that certificates change a design decision; whether they do is measured (OPEN-LEMMA-5).
   The first non-claim is, in substance, "Lemma does not claim the simulator is verified"; the output wording
   above avoids the banned word even in a negation, so the scan stays strict with no allow-list (DESIGN).
5. Lower classes: `Tested` output names the tier and the case set; `Estimated` output carries its uncertainty.
   Only a Proof claim can be `Proved` or `Certified`; the other six claim kinds are `Tested` or `Estimated`
   however much evidence they rest on.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every prove-cluster output with class `Proved` or `Certified` carries a certificate or bundle hash and a
      checker identity; a fixture exporter that omits either is refused with FROB-REF-834.
- [ ] Lint L-VERIFIED (N00-01) passes over the prove cluster's verbs, schemas, refusals and `frob-verify --help`
      with no allow-list entry; this bead cites that test and does not repeat it.
- [ ] The dynamic scan over all end-to-end transcripts of the Lemma scripts finds zero occurrences, using the
      matcher of N00-01; a transcript with a planted "verification passed" is caught (negative control).
- [ ] `frob prove kernel K-cay-step --json` contains the six `not_claimed` strings, and `frob prove list --json`
      contains them once per listing.
- [ ] `fmt_proof_claim` is the only function producing a string beginning `proved:` or `certified:` (a source scan
      asserts it).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-lemma/tests/wording.rs`: `proved_string_has_checker_and_hash`; `certified_string_has_auditor_and_bundle`;
`cannot_format_without_receipt` (compile-fail: no constructor of the formatter's input without receipts);
`export_check_refuses_missing_checker`; `not_claimed_has_six_entries`; `tested_names_tier_and_cases`;
`estimated_carries_uncertainty`; `non_proof_claim_capped_at_tested` (a Known-answer or Fidelity claim resting on
a certificate still serializes as `Tested` or `Estimated`, step 5); `sole_producer_of_proof_strings` (a source scan
of the workspace finds the literals `proved:` and `certified:` only inside `fmt_proof_claim` and its tests);
`prove_kernel_json_has_six_not_claimed` (`frob prove kernel K-cay-step --json`: the six fixed strings, in order,
none containing the banned stem); `prove_list_json_has_one_not_claimed_block` (`frob prove list --json`: one
array of the six strings at the top of the result, none inside a row).
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/banned_word_dynamic.rs`: `scan_dir_clean_on_recorded_transcripts`;
`planted_phrase_in_transcript_is_caught` ("verification passed" in a captured envelope, labelled `synthetic`);
`identifier_frob_verify_allowed_in_transcript` (a logged command line `frob-verify bundle.frobcert` is not a
match); `same_matcher_as_static_lint` (the function called is `banned_word::find`). The static tests
(`verified_in_report_template`, `planted_word_in_refusal_text_is_caught`, `frob_verify_binary_name_exempt`) are
in `doc_lints.rs` of N00-01.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead adds steps to `scripts/e2e/lemma_flags_claims.sh` (bead N12-11, the M0 gate script, a dependency) that run
`banned_word::scan_dir` over the script's own transcript and every captured envelope and assert the receipt fields
on every `Proved` or `Certified` result; it logs the
count of results by class and the count of banned-word matches (threshold 0). Its NDJSON lines for these checks
(STD-E2E helper) carry `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N12-08"), `step` (`wording/receipts`, `wording/banned-word`,
`wording/not-claimed`), `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the number of files and envelopes
scanned, matches found (with file, line and the matched text for each), results per class with the count missing
a certificate hash or checker identity (threshold 0), whether the six `not_claimed` strings are present, and on a
failure the first offending envelope's path for reproduction.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-221 (a result presented above the class of its weakest input is refused, FROB-REF-834). CX-110 (a study
receipt in a bundle does not prove the study's conclusion), CX-112. Negative controls: the phrase planted in a
transcript (the word planted in a refusal text is the static control of N00-01); the fixture exporter without a
checker identity. Determinism: not applicable.

## Considerations

- The rule binds reports, documentation and tool output; plan and bead text are outside the scan.
- Translating or paraphrasing layers (dashboards, HTML replays) must reuse the formatter's strings; a visible
  banner in rendered forms repeats the no-authorization flags.
- RISK-11 is the risk this bead mitigates; its early signal is "certificates without replay hashes; the word in
  any output".
