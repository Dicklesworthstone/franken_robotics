## Background

Statistical evidence travels in certificate bundles next to proofs: a qualification receipt, an empirical bound on
a failure rate, the e-value of a paired comparison. ADR-LEMMA-06 fixes its status: statistical receipts travel in
bundles and are never proofs, because an auditor can recompute arithmetic and cannot attest that episodes ran. The
proof kind for this is `sample-coverage`: the auditor recomputes the arithmetic of a recorded study (counts,
e-values, spent α), and the class of such a claim is at most `Tested` (an empirical bound inside a mission answer
is `Estimated`). The shortcut it blocks (CX-110): "a study receipt in a bundle proves the study's conclusion".

The kind is needed when the blinded qualification lane exists (M3): a result is `qualified` only with a receipt
whose decision is `promoted`, and receipts, the ledger of levels and, after a campaign closes, the cluster
derivation are part of the bundle and are replayed by `frob-verify`. This bead is the arithmetic checker. The full
campaign replay with commitments and cluster derivation is a later bead (BD-383, GATE-QUAL-03 at M5). Tier: Solid.
Rejected alternative: floating-point recomputation of bounds in the auditor (the auditor has exact integers and
rationals only, and a strict decision is computed exactly).

## Scope

In: the payload reader and checking rules of `sample-coverage` in `crates/frob-cert/src/kinds/sample_coverage.rs`;
the class cap; specimens and at least three mutations; the bound-from-counts rule in exact rationals; the e-value
table rule; the level-spend rule; the ledger change `sample-coverage-enable` that enables the row; this bead's
end-to-end script.
Out: running studies (BD-124, BD-259, BD-262); replay of commitments and sealed-cluster derivation (BD-383);
the reachability pre-analysis of a fixed-n rule (card T-26).

## Technical approach

Payload (DESIGN; all numbers exact rationals or integers): `study_type` (`zero-failure` | `binomial-bound` |
`bounded-weight` | `e-process-table` | `qualification-receipt`), `population` (identifier of the declared world
population or sealed family), `method`, `counts` (`n`, `failures` or `k`), `level` (α or δ), `claimed_bound`,
`alpha_total`, `index`, `alpha_i`, `alpha_spent`, `decision`, and for the e-table `p0`, `p1`, `alpha_plus`,
`alpha_minus`.

Rules, each exact:
1. Binomial upper bound. For n units with x failures and level α, a claimed upper bound p̄ on the failure
   probability is accepted when Σ_{k=0..x} C(n, k)·p̄ᵏ·(1 − p̄)ⁿ⁻ᵏ ≤ α. The sum is evaluated in exact rationals with
   big integers; the tail is decreasing in p, so checking at the claimed p̄ suffices and no root is solved. The
   auditor does not check tightness; it checks validity. Consequence for producers (DESIGN): a claimed bound
   written with a few decimal digits must be rounded upward, never to nearest. The printed table of KA-762
   (n = 200, α = 0.025: 0.0183, 0.0275, 0.0357, 0.0432, 0.0504 for 0 to 4 failures) is rounded to nearest; the
   exact bounds are 0.018275…, 0.027542…, 0.035655…, 0.043208…, 0.050414…, so 0.0275, 0.0432 and 0.0504 are
   below the true bound and are correctly rejected as claims, while 0.0276, 0.0433 and 0.0505 are accepted.
2. Zero-failure bound. n independent nominal episodes without failure give p ≤ 1 − δ^(1/n) ≤ ln(1/δ)/n at
   confidence 1 − δ. The checker accepts a claimed p̄ when (1 − p̄)ⁿ ≤ δ, which is rule 1 with x = 0, and never
   evaluates a logarithm.
3. Bounded-weight importance sampling with a defensive mixture of weight β: zero failures certify only
   p ≤ ln(1/δ)/(β·n); the checker accepts p̄ when (1 − β·p̄)ⁿ ≤ δ and rejects a claim that drops the factor β.
4. One-bit e-process table. Rates p₀ < p₁ are fixed in advance; each audited pair yields one bit (exceedance or
   not); n pairs, k exceedances. Two e-processes run on the bits. E⁺ tests "the exceedance rate is at least p₁"
   and promotes when E⁺ ≥ 1/α⁺, with E⁺ = ((1 − p₀)/(1 − p₁))^(n−k)·(p₀/p₁)ᵏ. E⁻ tests "the rate is at most p₀"
   and rejects (labels the result `tier-sensitive` in the tier-audit use) when E⁻ ≥ 1/α⁻, with
   E⁻ = ((1 − p₁)/(1 − p₀))^(n−k)·(p₁/p₀)ᵏ. Otherwise the decision is `continue`. The checker computes both as
   exact rationals (no logarithm) and recomputes the decision; the producer's integer table (n, k) → promote,
   reject or continue is checked row by row when it is carried.
5. Level spend. For request i of a campaign with total α: α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)], and after m requests the spent
   level is α·m/(m + 1) (the series telescopes because 1/[i·(i + 1)] = 1/i − 1/(i + 1)). The checker recomputes
   α_i and α_spent from `alpha_total` and `index`, and rejects a receipt whose level differs, whose index sequence
   has a gap among the receipts supplied, or whose spent level exceeds α.
6. Decision consistency: the recorded decision equals the decision the rule gives on the recorded counts at the
   recorded level.
7. Class cap: `claim.class` must be `Tested` or `Estimated`; `Certified` or `Proved` is rejected (FROB-REF-834).
8. Population: the bundle must name the population; a bound with no named population is rejected as a missing
   side condition (FROB-REF-829).
9. The pre-close form of `qualification-receipt`. A receipt bundled while its campaign is open (N24b-19) carries
   the level fields, the request and receipt hashes, the decision and the population, and no `counts`, which
   stay sealed until close. Rules 1 and 6 then do not apply: the checker applies rules 5, 7 and 8 and accepts with
   the note "decision not recomputed: counts sealed until the campaign closes". An entry that carries counts
   while the bundled campaign record's state is `open` is rejected (FROB-REF-829: a field the open state does not
   allow; the firewall's rule that no per-cluster datum leaves before close). Re-bundled after close, the same
   receipt carries its counts and every rule applies.
The report for this kind always prints: "arithmetic recomputed; that the episodes ran is not checked".

Enabling the row. The baseline row of `sample-coverage` (N12-02) is `not-implemented`; it becomes `enabled` only
by a change of kind `enable` in the revision ledger `registries/cert-kinds-revisions.toml` (N12x-08), slug
`sample-coverage-enable`, which carries the six admission items: (1) the payload schema above and the sentence of
what is recomputed; (2) cap `Tested`, scopes `mission` and `study` (no proof argument is owed, because the cap is
below `Certified`); (3) the accepted specimens and the six named mutants below, each with its rule; (4) not owed
(the 200-mutant campaign is required only of kinds capped at `Certified`); (5) `cargo tree -p frob-cert` prints
one line and no source file is shared with the receipt writer of BD-259; (6) the review of the checker by a
session that did not write it, whose identity the row records. The registry constant and the ledger row land in
one change; either alone fails `cargo xtask check-generated`.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead adds the corpus directory `crates/frob-cert/tests/specimens/sample-coverage/` (the receipt specimen
e6 of the worked artifacts as `accept-qualification-receipt.frobcert`, its pre-close form as
`accept-qualification-receipt-open.frobcert`, one accepted file per study type, and the mutants
`reject-bound.tail`, `reject-level.alpha-i`, `reject-class.certified`, `reject-side.population`,
`reject-decision.inconsistent`, `reject-open.counts`) with its `expected.toml`, and flips the kind to `enabled`
through the ledger change. This bead's own script, `scripts/e2e/lemma_kind_sample_coverage.sh` (header
`bead=N12-13 milestone=M3 labels=lemma,frob-cert gates=GATE-LEMMA-03 seed=0 needs=cpu`; sources the STD-E2E
helper, idempotent, writes only under `target/`): (1) `start`: commit, host fingerprint, the registry revision and
the kind's status; (2) `cargo build -p frob-cert --release`; (3) `frob-verify --json` under `env -i` on every file
of the directory, asserting the verdict, the first failing rule and the refusal code against `expected.toml`, one
accept and at least three rejects; (4) `frob prove verify <file> --json` on the e6 bundle and its pre-close form,
asserting the same verdict and an `evidence` block of class `Tested`; (5) `cargo xtask check-generated`, exit 0.
Each NDJSON line (`target/e2e-logs/lemma_kind_sample_coverage/<utc>.ndjson`, transcript beside it) carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead` ("N12-13"), `step` (`sample-coverage/<file>`), `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall
time, the in-tree envelope's `evidence` block where step 4 ran, the recomputed tail as an exact fraction against
the level, the level arithmetic (α_i, α_spent, α), the expected and observed first failing rule and refusal code,
the report sentence that the episodes are not attested, the bundle digest, and on a mismatch the first differing
value and the file to reproduce; the `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and the per-kind counts.
The directory-driven script of N12-34 then runs the same directory unchanged and re-records GATE-LEMMA-03's
per-kind counts for M3.

## Considerations

- Exact binomial tails with n in the thousands and rational p̄ produce large integers; the size limits of the
  auditor apply and a receipt beyond them is refused, not approximated. Claimed bounds should be written with
  short denominators (four decimal digits) so the powers stay tractable.
- Splitting estimates are dependent trials and `Estimated` only; the kind does not accept them as bounds.
- Statistical validity (independence of units, freshness of clusters, exchangeability) is outside the auditor;
  the null-calibration tests and the qualification gates carry it (RISK-34).
- A chance claim marked `universal`, or transported without a coupling budget, is rejected by the mission-side
  rules, not by this kind.
