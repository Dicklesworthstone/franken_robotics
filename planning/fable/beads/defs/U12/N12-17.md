## Background

Two proof kinds of the closed registry carry arguments whose danger lies in what they leave out.

`energy-budget`. Where a guide puts an absorber on every forward path (a stop, a bumper, a door, a drawer, a
lift), the energy that can reach the absorber is bounded by its sources, whatever the contact sequence:

  ½·m_cap·v² + ∫ max(P_elec, 0) dt + E_stored + E_gravity + E_other + E_reserve ≤ W_abs,min

m_cap is an upper bound on the effective moving mass including the reflected inertia of rotors, gears and wheels
(N²·J_r/r² at a wheel of radius r); v bounds its speed; the integral covers the worst-case drive input until the
cutoff acts; E_stored covers springs and compliance; E_gravity the weight over the absorber stroke and any drop on
the path; E_other every other actuator on the load path; E_reserve a declared margin (zero unless stated);
W_abs,min the lower work envelope of the absorber. Passive contact with any friction coefficient μ ≥ 0 only
dissipates, so the contact model drops out. Read as an exclusion, the same inequality removes a design family: if
a mandatory crossing needs a potential rise greater than the initial energy plus all allowed external work, no
controller succeeds. The failure mode is an omitted source. The auditor recomputes "the inequality with its error
bounds and its list of sources".

`product-unreachable`. To clear the replacement of design A by design B without either having a guarantee, both
plants run on the same exogenous scene, payload and clock law, with a joint monitor on the pair of outcomes; if the
pair (A succeeds, B fails) is unreachable over an enclosure of every admitted joint execution, B dominates A on
that cell. The failure modes are a coverage gap (a product whose internal branches are wrongly tied reaches only
(0, 0) and (1, 1) and hides a reachable (1, 0)) and outcomes enclosed separately (which admits the impossible
pair). The auditor recomputes "that the enclosure covers every admitted joint branch and excludes (1, 0)".

Tier: the energy inequality is Solid mathematics; both tiers that use the kinds are Frontier. Rejected
alternative: checking only the arithmetic (an inequality with a missing term is arithmetically true and
physically false).

## Scope

In: the two checkers in `crates/frob-cert/src/kinds/`, their payloads, structural checks, specimens and
mutations; registry rows enabled.
Out: the `.fenergy` artifact, its runtime monitor and the producer (BD-337); the product-system construction and
the witness search (BD-359); the cards' registry rows (CARD-T-28, CARD-T-32).

## Technical approach

1. `energy-budget` payload (DESIGN): `load_path`, a small port graph listing every element between the drive and
   the absorber with its kind (`mass`, `rotor`, `drive`, `spring`, `gravity-rise`, `gravity-drop`, `actuator`,
   `absorber`) and its parameters with units; `terms`, one entry per budget term with the element it accounts for,
   its value as a rational or an outward-rounded interval in joules, and its formula tag (`kinetic`, `electrical`,
   `stored`, `gravity`, `other`, `reserve`); `absorber_work_min`; `claim` (`holds` or `excludes`); the unit
   scales; the evidence class of each input.
2. `energy-budget` checks: (a) structural: every element of the load path whose kind can supply energy (`mass`,
   `rotor`, `drive`, `spring`, `gravity-rise`, `gravity-drop`, `actuator`) is accounted for by a term, and every
   term names an element of the path (set equality; a path without a gravity element must carry an explicit
   `level: true` declaration, otherwise the gravity term is required); (b) each term is recomputed from its
   element's parameters by its formula: ½·m·v² with m including N²·J_r/r² for every rotor on the path; P·t for a
   drive of power bound P over cutoff time t; m·g·Δh for gravity over the stroke and drops; the stored value for
   a spring; (c) the sum of upper ends is compared with the lower end of the absorber's work: `holds` requires
   sum ≤ W_abs,min; `excludes` requires the lower end of the required potential rise to be strictly greater than
   the upper end of the initial energy plus all allowed external work;
   (d) units: joules throughout, checked by the typed fields; (e) class: the claim's class is the minimum of
   `Certified` and its inputs' classes (in simulation the inputs are `Estimated`, so the claim is `Estimated`;
   FROB-REF-834 if presented higher).
3. `product-unreachable` payload: the shared inputs with their domains (intervals or boxes, exact rationals) and
   the declaration of which inputs are shared; the independent branch variables of each plant with their finite
   domains; a list of pieces, each a cell of the shared domain together with one combination of branch values,
   and for each piece the outcome maps of A and of B as rational affine predicates (success when an affine
   function is ≥ 0) or, for a non-affine piece, an interval enclosure of each margin.
4. `product-unreachable` checks: (a) joint coverage: the pieces partition the full product of the shared domain
   and all branch domains of both plants (the `partition` arithmetic over the shared coordinates, and exhaustive
   enumeration over the finite branch combinations; a combination that appears for A only together with a
   specific combination of B is a tied branch and is rejected unless declared shared); (b) on each piece the
   outcome pair is decided exactly: for affine predicates by their signs at the piece's vertices (a predicate
   that changes sign inside a piece means the piece must be split and is rejected), for enclosed margins by the
   interval ends; (c) no piece has outcome (A succeeds, B fails); a piece whose enclosure cannot exclude it is
   rejected; (d) progress and nonempty executions are declared per piece; (e) the bundle carries the paired
   verdict and each design's absolute verdict separately.
5. Both: caps on pieces and terms; the first failing rule is named; registry rows `enabled`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `energy-budget` specimen accepted: 8 kg at 0.4 m/s is 0.64 J; a 20 W drive over a 25 ms cutoff adds 0.50 J;
      gravity on an 8% slope over the 40 mm stroke is 8 × 9.81 × 0.040 × 0.08 = 0.251136 J (0.25 J rounded
      outward to 0.26 J in the bundle); a series spring stores 0.10 J; total at most 1.50 J against an absorber of
      50 N over 40 mm, 2.00 J.
- [ ] The same budget without its gravity term is rejected (rule `energy.source-missing`), although its
      arithmetic holds.
- [ ] Further rejected mutations: a rotor on the path with no reflected-inertia contribution; a cutoff delay of
      60 ms (0.64 + 1.20 + 0.25 + 0.10 = 2.19 J > 2.00 J); a class of `Certified` over `Estimated` inputs.
- [ ] Exclusion specimen accepted: lifting 8 kg over a 12 mm sill needs 8 × 9.81 × 0.012 = 0.94176 J, above a
      0.90 J budget; the same exclusion with 0.05 J of approach kinetic energy added to the budget is rejected
      (0.95 J ≥ 0.94176 J).
- [ ] `product-unreachable` specimen accepted: z ∈ [0, 1] shared; A succeeds when z ≥ 3/5, B when z ≥ 2/5; pieces
      [0, 2/5), [2/5, 3/5), [3/5, 1] give outcomes (0, 0), (0, 1), (1, 1); (1, 0) does not occur.
- [ ] Rejected mutations: z declared not shared (the product then contains (1, 0)); a missing piece; pieces only
      for the tied outcomes (0, 0) and (1, 1) with the middle interval absent; a piece on which A's predicate
      changes sign.
- [ ] GATE-LEMMA-03's per-kind condition is recorded for both kinds.

## Considerations

- The budget covers stops and guided contacts, not tip-over or loss of balance; it needs the physical guide. Its
  margin can be thin (1.49 J against 2.00 J). Its inputs are measurements a simulator cannot make, so a claim
  about a physical robot is a Reality claim with a recorded no-claim until bench data are imported.
- The structural check can only be as complete as the declared load path; the producer derives the path from the
  design's port graph, and the bundle embeds the path so a reviewer can read it.
- One mislabeled branch invalidates a dominance certificate; a conventional reachability tool on the same product
  model is the comparator, and the only advantage claimed is the lowering that keeps spatially coupled
  disturbances coupled.
- KILL-ENERGY and the dominance kill gate are owned by the shield and mission work.
