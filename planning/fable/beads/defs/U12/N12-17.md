## Background

Two proof kinds of the closed registry carry arguments whose danger lies in what they leave out.

`energy-budget`. Where a guide puts an absorber on every forward path (a stop, a bumper, a door, a drawer, a
lift), the energy that can reach the absorber is bounded by its sources, whatever the contact sequence:

  ½·m_cap·v² + ∫ max(P_elec, 0) dt + E_stored + E_gravity + E_other + E_reserve ≤ W_abs,min

m_cap is an upper bound on the effective moving mass including the reflected inertia of rotors, gears and wheels
(N²·J_r/r² at a wheel of radius r); v bounds its speed; the integral covers the worst-case drive input until the
cutoff acts; E_stored covers springs and compliance; E_gravity the weight over the absorber stroke and any drop on
the path; E_other every other actuator on the load path; E_reserve a declared margin (zero unless stated);
W_abs,min the lower work envelope of the absorber. Passive contact with any friction coefficient μ ≥ 0 only
dissipates, so the contact model drops out. Read as an exclusion, the same inequality removes a design family: if
a mandatory crossing needs a potential rise greater than the initial energy plus all allowed external work, no
controller succeeds. The failure mode is an omitted source. The auditor recomputes "the inequality with its error
bounds and its list of sources".

`product-unreachable`. To clear the replacement of design A by design B without either having a guarantee, both
plants run on the same exogenous scene, payload and clock law, with a joint monitor on the pair of outcomes; if the
pair (A succeeds, B fails) is unreachable over an enclosure of every admitted joint execution, B dominates A on
that cell. The failure modes are a coverage gap (a product whose internal branches are wrongly tied reaches only
(0, 0) and (1, 1) and hides a reachable (1, 0)) and outcomes enclosed separately (which admits the impossible
pair). The auditor recomputes "that the enclosure covers every admitted joint branch and excludes (1, 0)".

Tier: the energy inequality is Solid mathematics; both tiers that use the kinds are Frontier. Rejected
alternative: checking only the arithmetic (an inequality with a missing term is arithmetically true and
physically false).

## Technical approach

1. `energy-budget` payload (DESIGN; the one payload of the kind). The bundle embeds the `.fenergy` document
   (schema `frob.energy/1`, the plan's artifact), which already carries every term, the unit scales, the
   absorber and the claim, and adds the list of sources that the structural check needs:

   ```json
   { "kind": "energy-budget",
     "statement": "E_deliv <= W_abs at boundary dock_stop_A for readings in [0, r_max]",
     "primitive_equations": ["E_deliv = T_ub + E_drive + E_ext + E_stored + E_reserve",
                             "T_ub = ceil(1/2 * v_hat^T * M_cap * v_hat)", "v_hat = |r| + err"],
     "payload": { "reading": "holds",
                  "budget": { "...": "the full .fenergy document" },
                  "sources": [ { "component": "drive_left", "port": "shaft", "class": "drive",
                                 "covered_by": "terms.drive" },
                               { "component": "chassis", "port": "gravity", "class": "gravity",
                                 "covered_by": "terms.external[0]",
                                 "basis": { "mass_cap_kg": "8", "g_mps2": "9.81", "rise_m": "0.0032" } },
                               "..." ],
                  "level": false,
                  "port_graph_digest": "b3:…",
                  "reading_domain": { "r_max": "0.39" },
                  "absorber_card": { "hash": "b3:…",
                                     "bytes": "<canonical .fcard bytes of kind absorber (N22bx-01)>" },
                  "run": null },
     "assumptions": [ { "input": "absorber.w_abs_j", "provenance": "assumed", "class": "Estimated" }, "..." ] }
   ```

   Source classes and the term each must be covered by: `kinetic` (a mass or a rotor on the load path) by
   `terms.kinetic_ub`, which for a rotor must state `includes_reflected_inertia: true`; `drive` by
   `terms.drive`; `stored` (a spring or compliance) by an entry of `terms.stored`; `gravity` (a rise or a drop
   on the path) and `actuator` (any other actuator on the load path) by an entry of `terms.external`.
   `terms.reserve_j` is a declared margin and covers no source. `reading` is `holds` or `excludes`; for
   `excludes` the embedded document lists the available energy of the crossing (initial energy and every
   allowed external work), a member `required_rise` gives the mass lower bound, g and the rise lower bound,
   and the absorber is not read. `absorber_card` (optional) carries the canonical bytes of the absorber's
   `.fcard` of kind `absorber` (N22bx-01) and their BLAKE3; its check belongs to N22b-14 (step 3a below).
   `run` is null for a static budget; its content and its check belong to N22b-14. An earlier draft of this bead described the load path and the terms a second time in a vocabulary
   of its own (element kinds `mass`, `rotor`, `spring`, `gravity-rise`, `gravity-drop`; formula tags
   `kinetic`, `electrical`, `stored`, `gravity`, `other`, `reserve`) while the shield chapter's draft embedded
   the document; two payloads for one kind cannot both be checked, the document is what the producer writes,
   and so it is embedded. What that draft had and the document lacks, the recomputation of a gravity term
   from its parameters and the `level` declaration, is kept as the `basis` member and the `level` member.
2. `energy-budget` checks, in this order, naming the first rule that fails. The identifiers are stable and
   are the only rule vocabulary of the kind (N22b-14's mutation corpus names them):
   1. `energy.parse`: the embedded budget is parsed with the auditor's own parser, decimal strings to exact
      rationals; a float literal or an unknown field is rejected. `energy.flags`: an authorization flag
      missing or `true`.
   2. Structural (set equality between sources and terms). `energy.source-missing`: an entry of `sources`
      whose `covered_by` does not point at an existing term of the budget; a rotor or mass on the path with
      `includes_reflected_inertia` false is a kinetic source without a term. `energy.term-without-source`: a
      term of the budget that no source names. `energy.digest-mismatch`: `port_graph_digest` differs from the
      document's `premises.sources_complete`. `energy.gravity-undeclared`: no source of class `gravity` and
      `level` not `true` (a path without a gravity element must carry the explicit declaration, otherwise the
      gravity term is required).
   3. Terms. Each integer term is recomputed from the decimal strings with directed rounding (the left side
      up, W_abs down) at the declared scales, for the largest reading in `reading_domain` (T_ub is monotone in
      |r|, so the largest reading is the worst case): ½·m_cap·v̂² with m_cap including N²·J_r/r² for every
      rotor on the path; p_max·t for a drive of power bound p_max over cutoff time t; the stored and external
      bounds as written. `energy.term-mismatch`: a stated integer differs from the recomputed one.
      `energy.term-below-basis`: where a source carries a `basis`, its bound is recomputed by the formula of
      its class (m·g·Δh for gravity over the stroke and over drops) and the term must be at least that value
      rounded up at the scale. A source with no `basis` is a declared bound: it must appear in `assumptions`
      and it caps the claim at `Estimated`.
   3a. Absorber, when `absorber_card` is present: `energy.absorber-mismatch`, implemented by N22b-14. The card
      is parsed with the auditor's own canonical reader; its BLAKE3 must equal both `absorber_card.hash` and
      the hash in the embedded budget's `absorber.card`; W_abs is recomputed from the card's lower envelope
      as N22bx-01 defines it (trapezoids over the decimal knots up to x_lim, each knot's force and the stroke
      reduced by their stated uncertainties, exact rationals, rounded down to the energy step); a budget whose
      W_abs integer exceeds the recomputed one, or whose card hash differs, is rejected. Without this step an
      inflated `w_abs_j` in a bundle re-hashed consistently would be accepted (N22b-14's mutant 16). A bundle
      without the member, or any bundle before the step exists, is checked by the other steps with W_abs as
      written, its report states "absorber not recomputed", and its class is at most `Estimated`.
   4. `energy.inequality`: `holds` requires the sum of the upper ends to be at most the lower end of the
      absorber's work, W_abs,min, as integers; `excludes` requires the lower end of the required potential
      rise to be strictly greater than the upper end of the initial energy plus all allowed external work.
   5. `energy.class-above-inputs`: the claim's class is the minimum of `Certified` and its inputs' classes (in
      simulation the inputs are `Estimated`, so the claim is `Estimated`); a higher class is the condition of
      FROB-REF-834.
   6. Run replay, when `run` is not null: `energy.tick-mismatch` and `energy.cut-latch`, implemented by
      N22b-14. Until that step exists a bundle with a run is `unsupported`, never `accept`.
   7. `energy.domain`: the quantifier and domain of the statement against the query it is offered for (a
      mismatch is the condition of FROB-REF-826).
   Units are joules throughout, by the typed fields and the declared scales. The checker claims nothing
   about whether the port graph is the design's true graph: it recomputes arithmetic and structure, and its
   report states its trusted base.

**Three readings of the drop-stop card's gravity term (DESIGN; the same statement is in the pilot N22b-09, the
card GB-26 and the writer-and-corpus bead N22b-14, so that no two beads give "the card" different totals).** The plan prints
8 × 9.81 × 0.040 × 0.08 = 0.25 J and derives 1.49 J, 50.5 ms and 45.5 ms from it; the product is 0.251136 J.
(1) *Printed*: the term entered as the declared 0.25 J with no structured basis (250,000 in units of 10⁻⁶ J):
total 1,490,000, margin 510,000, longest cutoff delay 50,500 µs, or 45,500 µs with the reserve; KA-153 and
KA-568 are asserted on it to the printed digits. (2) *Product*: the term as the product its basis gives,
251,136: total 1,491,136, margin 508,864, delay 50,443.2 µs, or 45,443.2 µs with the reserve; this is the
governing reading, because a bound may not be rounded down. (3) *Outward*: written with fewer digits than its
basis the term is rounded up: 0.26 J at two decimals (total 1.50 J, delay 50.0 ms). The checker accepts all
three and rejects a fourth specimen in which 0.25 J is offered together with the basis (8 kg, 9.81 m/s²,
0.0032 m of rise) that it falls short of.

3. `product-unreachable` payload: the shared inputs with their domains (intervals or boxes, exact rationals) and
   the declaration of which inputs are shared; the independent branch variables of each plant with their finite
   domains; a list of pieces, each a cell of the shared domain together with one combination of branch values,
   and for each piece the outcome maps of A and of B as rational affine predicates (success when an affine
   function is ≥ 0) or, for a non-affine piece, an interval enclosure of each margin.
4. `product-unreachable` checks: (a) joint coverage: the pieces partition the full product of the shared domain
   and all branch domains of both plants (the `partition` arithmetic over the shared coordinates, and exhaustive
   enumeration over the finite branch combinations; a combination that appears for A only together with a
   specific combination of B is a tied branch and is rejected unless declared shared); (b) on each piece the
   outcome pair is decided exactly: for affine predicates by their signs at the piece's vertices (a predicate
   that changes sign inside a piece means the piece must be split and is rejected), for enclosed margins by the
   interval ends; (c) no piece has outcome (A succeeds, B fails); a piece whose enclosure cannot exclude it is
   rejected; (d) progress and nonempty executions are declared per piece; (e) the bundle carries the paired
   verdict and each design's absolute verdict separately.
5. Both: caps on pieces and terms; the first failing rule is named; registry rows `enabled` through the two
   ledger changes (an auditor at the revision before them returns `Unsupported` for the kind, never `accept`).
   Both kinds are capped at `Certified`, so item 4 of each change is owed: a seeded-fault campaign of at least
   200 mutated witnesses per kind with zero false accepts, run with BD-136's one generator on this bead's
   accepted specimens, the candidate rows enabled under the test feature `registry-candidate`, with this bead's
   operators added to `crates/frob-conformance/src/mutate/operators.rs` (`energy-budget`: drop one source, point
   one `covered_by` at a missing term, set `includes_reflected_inertia` to false, lower one term integer by one
   unit, lower a basis-carrying term below its basis, remove the gravity source without `level: true`, raise
   W_abs by one unit, raise the class; `product-unreachable`: remove one piece, duplicate one piece, declare a
   shared input unshared, tie a branch of A to one of B, move a piece boundary so a predicate changes sign
   inside it, flip one outcome to (1, 0)). The campaign is not N22b-14's hand corpus for BET-25, which stays
   N22b-14's. Item 5: no source file shared with the `.fenergy` producer (N22b-10 to N22b-13) or the
   product-system construction (BD-359). Item 6: review by a session that did not write the checker, its
   identity in the row. The constant and the ledger rows land in one change; either alone fails
   `cargo xtask check-generated`.

## Considerations

- The budget covers stops and guided contacts, not tip-over or loss of balance; it needs the physical guide. Its
  margin can be thin (1.49 J against 2.00 J). Its inputs are measurements a simulator cannot make, so a claim
  about a physical robot is a Reality claim with a recorded no-claim until bench data are imported.
- The structural check can only be as complete as the declared load path; the producer derives the path from the
  design's port graph, and the bundle embeds the path so a reviewer can read it.
- One mislabeled branch invalidates a dominance certificate; a conventional reachability tool on the same product
  model is the comparator, and the only advantage claimed is the lowering that keeps spatially coupled
  disturbances coupled.
- KILL-ENERGY and the dominance kill gate are owned by the shield and mission work.
