## Background

Two kinds of the closed proof-kind registry certify finite group-theoretic facts that design campaigns and the
gait atlas rely on. Each is re-derived by the offline auditor, not trusted from the producer. (This bead first
also carried the `rewrite-trace` checker of card T-27; the kernels derived at M2 ship its certificates, so it is
split out as N12p2-02 at M2, and this bead keeps the two kinds whose consumers arrive at M4.)

`orbit-count` (card T-24). A design campaign enumerates assignments of k module types to slots X; two
assignments in one orbit of the acting group Γ are the same closed loop. The number of distinct designs is
|Γ|⁻¹·Σ_{g ∈ Γ} k^c(g), with c(g) the number of cycles of g on X (the orbit-counting lemma). The acting group is
the observation-preserving group, never the mechanical group alone. The campaign generates one canonical
representative per orbit, the lexicographically least assignment.

`group-enumeration` (card T-25). A periodic gait of a symmetric system has a group H of spatio-temporal
symmetries and a normal subgroup K of purely spatial ones, with H/K cyclic. The gait atlas lists exactly the
pairs (H, K) of subgroups of the ambient group with K normal in H and H/K cyclic; completeness of that list is
decided by exhaustive enumeration of the subgroup lattice, re-derived by the checker.

Tier: `orbit-count` Solid; `group-enumeration` Frontier. Rejected alternative: accepting a count from the formula
without enumeration where enumeration is feasible.

## Scope

In: the two checkers, their payloads, a small permutation-group engine inside the auditor (generators as
permutations, closure by breadth-first multiplication, orders up to a declared cap), specimens and mutations,
the registry rows' `enable` changes in the revision ledger, and the end-to-end script
`scripts/e2e/lemma_kinds_combinatorial.sh`.
Out: the `rewrite-trace` checker (N12p2-02); generating campaigns (Phylum), building the gait atlas (Reflex); the
cards' rows (CARD-T-24, CARD-T-25); the ledger's mechanics (N12x-08).

## Technical approach

1. `orbit-count`. Payload: the slot set size n, generators of Γ as permutations of the slots (extended for a
   wreath product K ≀ H by the module group's action on per-slot orientations), the number of types k, the
   claimed count, and optionally the list of canonical representatives. The checker closes the generators into
   the group (cap on order, DESIGN: 2¹⁶), computes c(g) for every element, evaluates |Γ|⁻¹·Σ k^c(g) in exact
   integers (the sum must be divisible by |Γ|), and compares with the claim. For a wreath product the per-slot
   state is a pair (type, orientation) and an element is a slot permutation together with an orientation action
   per slot; the number of assignments fixed by g is then Π over the cycles C of g on the slots of f(C), where
   f(C) is k times the number of orientations fixed by the composite of the orientation actions around C (for a
   plain colouring f(C) = k and the product is k^c(g)). The checker evaluates this product form, never k^c(g),
   when the payload declares orientations. When kⁿ is within the enumeration
   cap (DESIGN: 2²⁴ assignments) it also enumerates all assignments, computes each orbit's lexicographically
   least member, and checks that the representative list is exactly that set; above the cap the representative
   check is `unsupported` and the count alone is certified by the formula evaluation.
2. `group-enumeration`. Payload: generators of the ambient group as permutations (of legs for a body group; of
   network cells for a declared clock-network group, the declaration carried as an assumption of the bundle);
   the pair convention, `all` (every pair listed) or `up-to-conjugacy` (one pair per class under conjugation by
   the ambient group); and the claimed list of pairs (H, K), each subgroup given by generators, with the
   generator h of the cyclic quotient H/K and the listed phase lags. The checker closes the ambient group (cap on
   order, DESIGN: 2¹⁰, since the lattice is enumerated exhaustively), enumerates all subgroups by closing every
   subset of a generating search (cyclic subgroups first, then joins, until closed under join), and for each
   ordered pair K ≤ H tests normality (g·K·g⁻¹ = K for every generator g of H; a failure names the generator
   pair) and cyclicity of H/K (some coset generates the quotient). Under `up-to-conjugacy` it merges the derived
   pairs into classes under (g·H·g⁻¹, g·K·g⁻¹) and checks that the claimed list holds exactly one member of each
   class. It accepts only if the set of pairs so obtained equals the claimed set exactly: no pair missing, none
   extra. Phase lags (as CARD-T-25 fixes them): for a pair with |H/K| = m and listed generator h, the checker
   checks that h·K generates H/K, and recomputes each leg's offset from the orbit of the leg under powers of h
   (h^j carries a leg to the leg offset by j/m of a period); the listed lags must equal these as exact
   rationals in [0, 1). Rule identifiers: `group.not-permutation`, `group.order-cap`, `group.pair-missing`,
   `group.pair-extra`, `group.not-normal`, `group.quotient-not-cyclic`, `group.convention`, `group.phase-lag`;
   for `orbit-count`: `orbit.not-permutation`, `orbit.order-cap`, `orbit.not-divisible`, `orbit.count`,
   `orbit.representative`.
3. Class: `Certified` for both, within the caps. A bundle whose group order exceeds the cap is refused
   (`unsupported`, never `accept`); above the assignment cap only the representative check is `unsupported` and
   the count stays `Certified` by the formula. A producer whose count rests on sampling records it as `Tested`
   and does not use these kinds.
4. Registry rows. Both rows are listed `not-implemented` in the baseline (N12-02) and go to `enabled` through
   `enable` changes of the revision ledger (N12x-08), slugs `orbit-count-enable` and `group-enumeration-enable`,
   each with the six admission items; an auditor built before that revision answers `Unsupported` for the kind,
   never `accept`. Item 4 (both caps are `Certified`): a seeded-fault campaign of at least 200 mutated witnesses
   per kind with zero false accepts, seed recorded, run with BD-136's one generator of certificate mutants on this
   bead's accepted specimens, the candidate rows enabled under the test feature `registry-candidate`, with this
   bead's operators added to `crates/frob-conformance/src/mutate/operators.rs` (`orbit-count`: claim the count
   off by one, compose one generator wrongly (one image changed so it stays a permutation of another group),
   replace a representative by a rotated member of its orbit, drop one generator, drop the orientation action
   from a wreath payload; `group-enumeration`: drop one pair, add one pair, shift one phase lag, replace the
   quotient generator h by a non-generating coset, switch the pair convention).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `orbit-count`: ten leg types on the six slots of a hexapod under the dihedral group of order 12 give
      (10⁶ + 2·10 + 2·10² + 10³ + 3·10⁴ + 3·10³)/12 = 86,185; under the sagittal mirror alone
      (10⁶ + 10³)/2 = 500,500; three types give 92, and enumeration of the 729 assignments confirms 92
      representatives. A claimed count of 86,186 is rejected; a generator that is not a permutation is rejected;
      a representative list with a non-minimal member is rejected.
- [ ] Wreath product: two slots swapped by the hub, each holding one of k types in one of two orientations that
      the module's half-turn identifies; the group has order 8 and the count is (4k² + 4k)/8 = (k² + k)/2, which
      is 6 for k = 3 against 36 raw assignments (fixed counts: the identity 4k²; the swap with equal flips on
      both slots, two elements, 2k each; the other five elements 0). A checker that applies k^c(g) to this
      payload with the 2k per-slot states as colours claims (4·36 + 4·6)/8 = 21 for k = 3 and is caught by this
      specimen (with the k types as colours the plain formula happens to give 6 here, which is why the specimen
      states the orientations and the checker must use the product form).
- [ ] `group-enumeration`: for the ambient group C₂ the pairs are exactly (C₂, C₂), (C₂, 1), (1, 1), and a list
      missing (C₂, 1) is rejected (`group.pair-missing`). For ℤ₄ × ℤ₂ (order 8) the checker finds eight
      subgroups and 23 pairs; the same list with the extra pair (ℤ₄ × ℤ₂, 1), whose quotient is not cyclic, is
      rejected (`group.pair-extra`). For D₄ acting on the four legs of a square body, a list containing
      (D₄, ⟨s⟩) with s a reflection is rejected (`group.not-normal`, naming r and s: r·s·r⁻¹ ∉ ⟨s⟩). A trot whose
      diagonal legs are listed half a period apart is rejected (`group.phase-lag`).
- [ ] The pinned corpus holds `crates/frob-cert/tests/specimens/orbit-count/` and `.../group-enumeration/`, each
      with its accepted specimens and at least three `reject-<rule>-*` files in `expected.toml`; the card mutants
      that N12-34's step 6a asserts by name are present with their card tags and `log_fields`: T-24
      `non_least_representative_rejects` (claimed and recomputed counts, whether enumeration ran) and T-25
      `non_normal_pair_rejects` (ambient order, subgroup count, claimed and recomputed pair counts).
- [ ] Registry rows `enabled` through the ledger changes `orbit-count-enable` and `group-enumeration-enable`,
      each with the six admission items (the item-4 campaign record cited: at least 200 invalid mutants per
      kind, zero false accepts); GATE-LEMMA-03's per-kind condition holds for both in this bead's script and its
      receipt, and the gate's M4 value is recorded by N12-34's re-run, not here.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/lemma_kinds_combinatorial.sh` exits 0 on the corpus and non-zero on the planted mutants of
      its self-test.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead's own script, `scripts/e2e/lemma_kinds_combinatorial.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper, seed-explicit,
idempotent, writes only under `target/`): (1) `start`: commit, host fingerprint, the registry revision and the
status of both rows; (2) `frob-verify` under `env -i`, in an empty working directory, on every file of the two
corpus directories, asserting the verdict and, for rejections, the first failing rule against `expected.toml`;
(3) `frob prove verify <files> --json` on the same files, asserting identical verdicts and rules; (4) the
auditor at the registry revision before the two `enable` changes, asserting `unsupported` for both kinds; (5) the
item-4 campaign record read back, asserting at least 200 invalid mutants per kind and 0 false accepts, logged as
`metric` lines against 200 and 0. NDJSON
to `target/e2e-logs/lemma_kinds_combinatorial/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line carries
`ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N12-18"), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, and the
envelope's `evidence` block for step (3); per bundle its BLAKE3, kind, group order, the cycle-index sum and the
claimed and recomputed counts (orbit-count) or the subgroup count and the claimed and recomputed pair counts
(group-enumeration), whether enumeration ran, the expected and observed verdict and first failing rule; on
failure the first missing, extra or wrong pair (or count) and the path of the file; a `summary` with specimens
accepted, mutants rejected by the expected rule and false accepts against 0. The same files are also picked up
by `scripts/e2e/lemma_auditor_mutations.sh` (owned by N12-34) for GATE-LEMMA-03, which logs the card-tagged
lines under `CARD-T-24` and `CARD-T-25`.

### Known answers and negative controls

The orbit counts 86,185, 500,500 and 92; the wreath count 6; the pair counts 3 (C₂), 11 (S₃) and 23 (ℤ₄ × ℤ₂,
eight subgroups). Negative control for counting with the
wrong group: counting with the mechanical group when a front-only lidar is fitted merges designs the closed loop
distinguishes; the specimen with the larger group against a query that declares the lidar is rejected by the
query-match rule. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- The auditor's small group engine must stay small (the size cap of 12,000 lines covers everything); orders are
  at most a few dozen for the bodies in scope, and the caps refuse anything larger.
- T-24's general counting lemma may be cited from Mathlib only if its module re-checks in FrankenLean
  (OPEN-LEMMA-6); this kind certifies instances by direct computation and does not depend on that.
- The enumeration lists admissible symmetry types, not realized gaits.
