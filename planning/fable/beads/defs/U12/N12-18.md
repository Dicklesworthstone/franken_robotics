## Background

Three kinds of the closed proof-kind registry certify finite combinatorial facts that kernels and analyses rely
on. Each is re-derived by the offline auditor, not trusted from the producer.

`rewrite-trace` (card T-27). A kernel's arithmetic is a straight-line program. A rewrite of it belongs to one of
two classes. Class E changes no bit under round-to-nearest with contraction off: commutation,
common-subexpression elimination, dead-code elimination, scaling by a power of two away from overflow and
underflow, and scheduling. Class R is equal over the reals only: reassociation, distribution, fused multiply-add.
A class-E rewrite ships a certificate (source hash, target hash, rule trace) that an independent checker replays;
a class-R rewrite makes a different kernel with a new identity, an error bound and a fidelity run. The rule table
is closed. A silent bit change would invalidate replay and both evidence ledgers, which is why the trace is
checked and not believed.

`orbit-count` (card T-24). A design campaign enumerates assignments of k module types to slots X; two
assignments in one orbit of the acting group Γ are the same closed loop. The number of distinct designs is
|Γ|⁻¹·Σ_{g ∈ Γ} k^c(g), with c(g) the number of cycles of g on X (the orbit-counting lemma). The acting group is
the observation-preserving group, never the mechanical group alone. The campaign generates one canonical
representative per orbit, the lexicographically least assignment.

`group-enumeration` (card T-25). A periodic gait of a symmetric system has a group H of spatio-temporal
symmetries and a normal subgroup K of purely spatial ones, with H/K cyclic. The gait atlas lists exactly the
pairs (H, K) of subgroups of the ambient group with K normal in H and H/K cyclic; completeness of that list is
decided by exhaustive enumeration of the subgroup lattice, re-derived by the checker.

Tier: `orbit-count` Solid; the other two Frontier. Rejected alternatives: accepting a rewrite because outputs
matched on test vectors; accepting a count from the formula without enumeration where enumeration is feasible.

## Scope

In: the three checkers, their payloads, a small permutation-group engine inside the auditor (generators as
permutations, closure by breadth-first multiplication, orders up to a declared cap), the class-E rule
interpreter over straight-line programs, specimens and mutations, registry rows enabled.
Out: finding rewrites (kernel work), generating campaigns (Phylum), building the gait atlas (Reflex); the Lean
reflector for trace replay (N12-28); the cards' rows (CARD-T-24, -25, -27).

## Technical approach

1. `rewrite-trace`. Payload: the source and target straight-line programs in the canonical text of the kernel IR
   (single assignment over add, sub, mul, div, sqrt, neg, abs, min, max, select, compare and literals, with
   bounded loops already unrolled or carried as loop blocks), their BLAKE3 hashes, and the trace: a list of
   steps `(rule, location, parameters)`. Rule table (closed, version-pinned in the registry):
   `commute-add`, `commute-mul`, `cse` (merge two identical expressions), `dce` (delete an unused assignment),
   `scale-pow2` (multiply by 2ᵏ and divide the consumer by 2ᵏ, with the declared range excluding overflow and
   underflow), `schedule` (reorder two adjacent independent assignments), and the exact simplifications `x·1`,
   `x + (−0)`, double negation, select with a constant predicate, and folding of exact constant operations.
   Not in the table and therefore rejected wherever they appear: reassociation, distribution, fused
   multiply-add, reciprocal substitution. The checker applies each step to the current program, checking the
   rule's precondition syntactically at the stated location, and accepts only if the final program's canonical
   text hashes to the target hash. A step whose rule is not in the table is `rewrite.rule-not-class-e`.
2. `orbit-count`. Payload: the slot set size n, generators of Γ as permutations of the slots (extended for a
   wreath product K ≀ H by the module group's action on per-slot orientations), the number of types k, the
   claimed count, and optionally the list of canonical representatives. The checker closes the generators into
   the group (cap on order, DESIGN: 2¹⁶), computes c(g) for every element, evaluates |Γ|⁻¹·Σ k^c(g) in exact
   integers (the sum must be divisible by |Γ|), and compares with the claim. When kⁿ is within the enumeration
   cap (DESIGN: 2²⁴ assignments) it also enumerates all assignments, computes each orbit's lexicographically
   least member, and checks that the representative list is exactly that set; above the cap the representative
   check is `unsupported` and the count alone is certified by the formula evaluation.
3. `group-enumeration`. Payload: generators of the ambient group as permutations, and the claimed list of pairs
   (H, K), each subgroup given by generators. The checker closes the ambient group (cap on order, DESIGN: 2¹⁰,
   since the lattice is enumerated exhaustively), enumerates all subgroups by closing every subset of a
   generating search (cyclic subgroups first, then joins, until closed under join), and for each ordered pair
   K ≤ H tests normality (g·K·g⁻¹ = K for every generator g of H) and cyclicity of H/K (some coset generates the
   quotient). It accepts only if the set of pairs so obtained equals the claimed set exactly: no pair missing,
   none extra.
4. Class: `Certified` for all three. The count is `Tested` instead when the group order or assignment count
   exceeds the caps and only sampling was possible.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `rewrite-trace`: a specimen trace of commutation, common-subexpression elimination and power-of-two scaling
      is accepted; a trace containing a reassociation filed as class E is rejected with rule
      `rewrite.rule-not-class-e`; a trace whose final program does not hash to the target is rejected; a
      `scale-pow2` step whose declared range admits overflow is rejected.
- [ ] `orbit-count`: ten leg types on the six slots of a hexapod under the dihedral group of order 12 give
      (10⁶ + 2·10 + 2·10² + 10³ + 3·10⁴ + 3·10³)/12 = 86,185; under the sagittal mirror alone
      (10⁶ + 10³)/2 = 500,500; three types give 92, and enumeration of the 729 assignments confirms 92
      representatives. A claimed count of 86,186 is rejected; a generator that is not a permutation is rejected;
      a representative list with a non-minimal member is rejected.
- [ ] Wreath product: two slots swapped by the hub, each holding one of k types in one of two orientations that
      the module's half-turn identifies; the group has order 8 and the count is (4k² + 4k)/8 = (k² + k)/2, which
      is 6 for k = 3 against 36 raw assignments.
- [ ] `group-enumeration`: for the ambient group C₂ the pairs are exactly (C₂, C₂), (C₂, 1), (1, 1); a list
      missing (C₂, 1) is rejected; a list containing a pair with K not normal in H is rejected.
- [ ] Registry rows `enabled`; GATE-LEMMA-03's per-kind condition recorded for all three.

`rewrite_scale_pow2_overflow_rejects`; `rewrite_fma_rejects`; `orbit_hexapod_86185`; `orbit_mirror_500500`;

`scripts/e2e/lemma_auditor_mutations.sh` (bead N12-34) includes specimens and mutants; it logs the group order,
the cycle-index sum, the count, the number of subgroups and pairs found, and for a trace the number of steps and
the first non-class-E rule.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-212 (rewrite trace with a reassociation filed as class E → `reject`); the orbit counts 86,185, 500,500 and 92;
the wreath count 6. CX-111 (a reassociation is not a harmless rewrite). Negative control for counting with the
wrong group: counting with the mechanical group when a front-only lidar is fitted merges designs the closed loop
distinguishes; the specimen with the larger group against a query that declares the lidar is rejected by the
query-match rule. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- Equality over the reals is what a class-E trace establishes as a theorem; bit-exactness in floating point is a
  test on a pinned corpus on every backend, not this certificate.
- The auditor's small group engine must stay small (the size cap of 12,000 lines covers everything); orders are
  at most a few dozen for the bodies in scope, and the caps refuse anything larger.
- T-24's general counting lemma may be cited from Mathlib only if its module re-checks in FrankenLean
  (OPEN-LEMMA-6); this kind certifies instances by direct computation and does not depend on that.
- The enumeration lists admissible symmetry types, not realized gaits.
