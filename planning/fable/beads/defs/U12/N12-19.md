## Background

Two rows of the closed proof-kind registry belong to Moonshot mechanisms. They are listed in the registry from
the start (status `not-implemented`, dispatching to `unsupported`) so that the registry is complete, and their
checkers are built only if the mechanisms that would emit them are funded after their pilots.

`tube-inclusion`. For a guard-ambiguous subsystem of at most four reduced states with a bounded sensing error set
E (a wheel at a threshold, a tray about to tip, braking across two floor materials), a table over at most 8 modes
× 16,384 cells is built by backward viability pruning. A cell K_m keeps a common safe action only if

  R_mn((K_m ⊕ E) ∩ G_mn, D) ⊆ K_n ⊖ (−E)   for every guard G_mn that may be enabled,

where cells hold measured states, the true state is the measured state plus an error in E, R_mn is the reset, D
the disturbance set, and A ⊖ B = {x : x + B ⊆ A}. The erosion is by −E, which equals E only for a centrally
symmetric error set. Where cells hold true states and the whole belief set must stay in one cell, the condition is
R_mn(K_m ∩ G_mn, D) ⊆ K_n ⊖ (E ⊕ (−E)). Inter-sample sweep, latency and chatter are accounted for. The auditor
recomputes the inclusion for every cell and enabled guard of a table. The reduction of a robot to four states is
an assumption that monitors watch; it is not proved, and no certificate of this kind says otherwise.

`alpha-root` (card T-34). In the continuation lane of linkage synthesis each root found by path tracking is
certified by Smale's α-test in exact rational arithmetic: α(f, z) < (13 − 3√17)/4 ≈ 0.157671 proves that Newton's
iteration from z converges quadratically to a root within twice the Newton step. Distinctness follows from
disjoint balls, non-reality from disjointness with the conjugate ball, and reality from the robust test
α(f, z) < 0.03 with ‖z − Re z‖ < 1/(20·γ(f, z)). The auditor recomputes the α bound of a rational point, with
Smale's theorem as a named assumption of the bundle, not a re-proved result. An α-test proves a nearby
nonsingular root and nothing about completeness.

Tier: Moonshot for both. Ceiling: evidence quality for one mechanism family at a time; neither is on the critical
path. Rejected alternative: checking α in floating point (the threshold comparison is a strict decision).

## Scope

In: the two checkers, built only on the funded branch; their payloads; specimens and mutations; the registry
status change from `not-implemented` to `enabled`.
Out: building tables (BD-440) and tracking paths (BD-437); the pilots of those mechanisms; the card row
(CARD-T-34).

## Technical approach

1. `tube-inclusion` payload: the reduced state dimension (≤ 4), the mode list (≤ 8), the grid of cells per mode
   as boxes with rational ends, the sets E and D as boxes or zonotopes with rational generators, each guard
   G_mn as affine inequalities, each reset R_mn as a rational affine map (or an interval enclosure of a
   nonlinear reset with its expression DAG), the convention (cells hold measured or true states), and for each
   kept cell its safe action and the list of target cells per enabled guard.
2. `tube-inclusion` checks, per kept cell and per guard that may be enabled from it: compute (K_m ⊕ E) ∩ G_mn as
   a polytope or an outward box; apply R_mn with D by interval or exact affine arithmetic to get an outer
   enclosure of the image; compute the eroded target K_n ⊖ (−E) exactly for boxes (shrink each face by the
   support of −E in that direction); accept the cell only if the image enclosure lies inside the union of the
   listed eroded targets. A cell whose guard list omits a guard that the cell's box can intersect is rejected
   (`tube.guard-missing`). The inter-sample sweep and latency margins must appear as declared inflations of
   K_m ⊕ E; their absence is a missing side condition (FROB-REF-829). Caps: 8 modes × 16,384 cells.
3. `alpha-root` payload: the polynomial system f with rational coefficients (n equations, n unknowns), the
   rational point z (real and imaginary parts as rationals), a rational matrix B claimed to approximate Df(z)⁻¹,
   and rational bounds β̄ and γ̄.
4. `alpha-root` checks, all in exact rationals with squared norms so that no root is taken: (a) evaluate f(z)
   and Df(z) exactly; (b) bound the defect of B: with R = I − B·Df(z) and ‖R‖_F² < 1, Df(z) is invertible and
   ‖Df(z)⁻¹·v‖ ≤ ‖B·v‖/(1 − ‖R‖_F); the checker verifies the claimed bounds by squaring both sides; (c) β̄ bounds
   ‖Df(z)⁻¹·f(z)‖; (d) for each k from 2 to the degree d, γ̄^(k−1) bounds ‖Df(z)⁻¹·Dᵏf(z)/k!‖, with the tensor
   norm bounded by a Frobenius-type bound computed exactly; (e) ᾱ = β̄·γ̄ and the threshold test
   ᾱ < (13 − 3√17)/4, decided exactly as 13 − 4·ᾱ > 0 and (13 − 4·ᾱ)² > 153; (f) for a reality claim the robust
   test ᾱ < 3/100 and ‖z − Re z‖² < 1/(400·γ̄²). The bundle's assumptions must list `smale-alpha-theorem`; without
   it the bundle is rejected (a missing assumption).
5. Completeness is outside the kind: a result is `Complete` only when the certified count equals an independent
   generic count with no lost path; that comparison is the producer's and is reported, not certified here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] While unfunded, both kinds dispatch to `unsupported` and no bundle of either kind can be accepted.
- [ ] `alpha-root` specimen accepted: f(x) = x² − 2 at z = 17/12 (f(z) = 1/144, Df = 17/6, β = 1/408, γ = 6/17,
      α = 1/1156 ≈ 0.000865 < 0.157671, and also below 3/100 for the reality test with z real).
- [ ] `alpha-root` mutations rejected: z = 1 (β = ½, γ = ½, α = ¼ ≥ 0.157671); a γ̄ below the true value; the
      assumption `smale-alpha-theorem` missing; a threshold comparison at exactly the bound.
- [ ] `tube-inclusion` specimen accepted on a one-dimensional synthetic threshold-crossing table; mutations
      rejected: an enabled guard omitted; erosion by E where −E is required for an asymmetric error set; a target
      cell list that does not cover the image; a missing latency inflation.
- [ ] GATE-LEMMA-03's per-kind condition (one accepted specimen, at least three rejected mutations) is recorded
      before either registry row is set to `enabled`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-cert/tests/kinds_moonshot.rs`: `unfunded_kinds_are_unsupported`; `alpha_sqrt2_at_17_over_12_accepts`;
`alpha_at_1_rejects`; `alpha_threshold_exact_square_test` (the comparison (13 − 4·ᾱ)² > 153 agrees with a
high-precision evaluation on 1,000 random rationals near the threshold); `alpha_missing_assumption_rejects`;
`alpha_reality_robust_test`; `tube_threshold_table_accepts`; `tube_guard_missing_rejects`;
`tube_asymmetric_error_erosion_sign` (E = [0, 1/10]: eroding by E instead of −E shrinks the wrong face and is
rejected); `tube_image_not_covered_rejects`; `tube_caps_refused` (9 modes).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/lemma_auditor_mutations.sh` (bead N12-34) asserts `unsupported` for both kinds until they are
enabled, and afterwards runs their specimens and mutants like any other kind.

### Known answers and negative controls

The hand case for α: at z = 17/12, Newton's next iterate is 577/408 and the root √2 lies within twice the Newton
step 1/408 of z. Known counts the continuation lane compares against (literature facts, to be re-read from the
primary sources before a gate cites them): at most 4 Burmester point pairs for five poses, 16 solutions of
general 6R inverse kinematics, 40 of the Stewart–Gough forward problem, 4,326 four-bars through nine points.
Negative controls are the mutations. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- Kill gates named in advance by the owning mechanisms: KILL-TUBE (tables) and KILL-SYNTH-CONT (continuation);
  if either fires, its kind stays `not-implemented` permanently and this bead closes for that half with the
  fallback's name.
- Smale's theorem is an assumption of the bundle: a report on this kind prints it in the trusted-base block.
- Tensor norm bounds for γ can be loose; a loose bound only makes the test conservative.
- The tube table's soundness depends on the reset and disturbance enclosures; those are inputs with their own
  classes, and the model-order reduction is a monitored assumption.
