## Background

Two rows of the closed proof-kind registry belong to Moonshot mechanisms, `tube-inclusion` (this bead) and
`alpha-root` (N12p1-01, split from this bead in polishing round 1 because the two checkers serve unrelated
mechanisms with separate pilots and kill gates). Both are listed in the registry from the start (status
`not-implemented`, dispatching to `unsupported`) so that the registry is complete, and each checker is built only
if the mechanism that would emit it is funded after its pilot.

`tube-inclusion`. For a guard-ambiguous subsystem of at most four reduced states with a bounded sensing error set
E (a wheel at a threshold, a tray about to tip, braking across two floor materials), a table over at most 8 modes
× 16,384 cells is built by backward viability pruning. A cell K_m keeps a common safe action only if

  R_mn((K_m ⊕ E) ∩ G_mn, D) ⊆ K_n ⊖ (−E)   for every guard G_mn that may be enabled,

where cells hold measured states, the true state is the measured state plus an error in E, R_mn is the reset, D
the disturbance set, and A ⊖ B = {x : x + B ⊆ A}. The erosion is by −E, which equals E only for a centrally
symmetric error set. Where cells hold true states and the whole belief set must stay in one cell, the condition is
R_mn(K_m ∩ G_mn, D) ⊆ K_n ⊖ (E ⊕ (−E)). Inter-sample sweep, latency and chatter are accounted for. The auditor
recomputes the inclusion for every cell and enabled guard of a table. The reduction of a robot to four states is
an assumption that monitors watch; it is not proved, and no certificate of this kind says otherwise.

Tier: Moonshot. Ceiling: evidence quality for the shield's reachable-set tables; not on the critical path.
Rejected alternative: checking the inclusions in floating point or on sampled states (an inclusion is a strict
set statement and is decided with outward-rounded or exact arithmetic).

## Scope

In: the `tube-inclusion` checker, built only on the funded branch; its payload; specimens and mutations; the
registry status change from `not-implemented` to `enabled`. This bead is the one specification of the payload
the auditor reads and of every check it makes. The shield unit's bead N22b-35 also specified a
checker for the same kind; the two texts are merged here (items 1 and 2), and N22b-35 depends on this bead.
Out: building tables (the builder N22b-34, under the epic BD-440); the pilot (N22b-33); the `alpha-root`
checker (N12p1-01); everything on the producing side and at run time (N22b-35): the belief-set lookup in the
shield, the `Uncovered` event and hand-over, the table as a payload of the shield record, the writer that turns a built table into a bundle of this kind, the braking fixture's
bundle and its mutants as registered specimens of the kind, and the campaign of GATE-SHIELD-05.

## Technical approach

1. `tube-inclusion` payload (one format, written by N22b-35's bundle writer from a table of N22b-34 and read
   by this checker): the reduced state dimension (≤ 4), the mode list (≤ 8), the grid of cells per mode as
   boxes with rational ends, the flow of each mode as a polynomial or rational one-tick map with its primitive
   equations, the action set, the sets E and D as boxes or zonotopes with rational generators, each guard
   G_mn as affine inequalities, each reset R_mn as a rational affine map (or an interval enclosure of a
   nonlinear reset with its expression DAG), the tick, the latency and the switch-count rule, the unsafe set,
   the convention (cells hold measured or true states), the kept set, and for each kept cell its mask of
   surviving actions, its designated common safe action and the list of target cells per enabled guard. (The
   first draft of this bead carried one safe action per cell and no flow, mask, unsafe set, tick or latency;
   the builder stores a mask and a designated action per cell, and the run-time lookup intersects masks over
   the modes of a belief, so the certificate has to cover every action in a mask.)
2. `tube-inclusion` checks. The checker parses the payload and rejects a description beyond the caps (4
   states, 8 modes, 16,384 cells per mode), a missing side condition, a missing or `true` no-authorization
   flag and an unknown kind. Then, for every kept cell, every action in its mask and every guard that may be
   enabled from the cell:
   a. compute (K_m ⊕ E) ∩ G_mn as a polytope or an outward box, with the inter-sample sweep and the latency
      margin applied as the declared inflations of K_m ⊕ E; their absence is a missing side condition
      (FROB-REF-829);
   b. recompute, with outward-rounded intervals on integers or exact affine arithmetic, an enclosure of the
      tube swept during the tick under the cell's flow and the action, and of the end set after the reset
      R_mn with D, under the latency and switch-count rules stated in the bundle;
   c. verify that the swept tube avoids the unsafe set;
   d. verify the inclusion. `Measured` semantics: the end-set enclosure, inflated by −E, lies inside the union
      of the listed target cells, all of which are kept; this is the plan's R_mn((K_m ⊕ E) ∩ G_mn, D) ⊆
      K_n ⊖ (−E) with K_n the kept region reached in mode n, and for a single target box it is the same as
      shrinking each face of the box by the support of −E in that direction. `True` semantics: the end-set
      enclosure, inflated by E ⊕ (−E), lies inside one kept cell. (Corrected on merging: the first draft eroded
      each listed target cell by itself and took the union, which leaves a gap at every face shared by two
      target cells and rejects an image that straddles two kept cells, although every reading of it lands in a
      kept cell. The erosion is of the union.)
   e. verify that the cell's designated action is in its mask.
   A cell whose guard list omits a guard that the cell's box can intersect is rejected (`tube.guard-missing`).
   The checker returns `accept`, or `reject` with the first failing cell, action and guard. A flow it cannot
   enclose (not polynomial or rational in its inputs) returns `unsupported`, never `accept`.

   The statement a bundle of this kind certifies: "for the reduced model in this bundle, from every kept cell
   every action in the cell's mask keeps every admitted true state out of the unsafe set for one tick and
   returns every admitted reading to a kept cell, for every guard that may be enabled". By induction over ticks
   this is invariance of the kept set for the reduced model. It is a Proof claim of class `Certified` about
   that model, and it says nothing about the robot beyond what the tracking margin and the monitors support.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] While unfunded, the kind dispatches to `unsupported` and no bundle of it can be accepted.
- [ ] `tube-inclusion` specimen accepted on a one-dimensional synthetic threshold-crossing table; mutations
      rejected: an enabled guard omitted; erosion by E where −E is required for an asymmetric error set; a target
      cell list that does not cover the image; a missing latency inflation; an action added to a mask that does
      not satisfy the inclusion (rejected at that cell and action); a tube that touches the unsafe set although
      its end set is admissible.
- [ ] An image that straddles two adjacent kept target cells is accepted under `Measured` semantics; the same
      image with one of the two cells not kept is rejected.
- [ ] A flow with a transcendental function returns `unsupported`; a bundle with a no-authorization flag set
      to `true` is rejected.
- [ ] The corpus directory `crates/frob-cert/tests/specimens/tube-inclusion/` takes further specimens and
      mutations by adding files with `expected.toml` and no change to the checker; this is shown with a second
      hand specimen (two modes, one guard) added after the first. (The braking bundle and its mutants, written by
      N22b-35's bundle writer, are registered there and pass through this checker as an acceptance line of
      N22b-35, which depends on this bead.)
- [ ] GATE-LEMMA-03's per-kind condition (one accepted specimen, at least three rejected mutations) is recorded
      before the registry row is set to `enabled`, and the row is enabled only by the revision ledger's change
      `tube-inclusion-enable` (N12x-08) with its six admission items: payload schema and what is recomputed;
      cap `Certified` with its reason; specimen and mutants by named rule; a seeded-fault campaign of at least
      200 mutated tables with zero false accepts; the standard-library-only closure with no source shared with
      the builder N22b-34; review by a session that did not write the checker.
- [ ] If KILL-TUBE fires, the row stays `not-implemented` permanently and this bead closes with the fallback's
      name (reduced-order barriers cover small subsystems).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-cert/tests/kind_tube_inclusion.rs`: `unfunded_kind_is_unsupported`; `tube_threshold_table_accepts`;
`tube_guard_missing_rejects`;
`tube_asymmetric_error_erosion_sign` (E = [0, 1/10]: eroding by E instead of −E shrinks the wrong face and is
rejected); `tube_image_not_covered_rejects`; `tube_caps_refused` (9 modes); `tube_union_erosion` (targets
[0, 1] and [1, 2], E = [0, 1/10], image [0.95, 1.05]: every reading lies in [0.85, 1.05], inside the union, so
the cell is accepted; with the second target not kept it is rejected); `tube_every_action_in_mask_checked`
(the hand table of the builder bead: adding action −1 to cell 3's mask is rejected at cell 3, action −1);
`tube_swept_tube_hits_unsafe_set_rejects` (a checker that verified end sets only would accept it);
`tube_designated_action_outside_mask_rejects`; `tube_unsupported_flow` (a transcendental flow returns
`unsupported`); `tube_flag_true_rejects`; `tube_seeded_fault_campaign` (200 mutated tables, seed recorded: a
bound moved, a guard dropped, an action added to a mask, an error set mirrored; zero accepted);
`tube_unsupported_before_enable` (the auditor at the revision before `tube-inclusion-enable` returns
`unsupported` for a valid specimen).

### End-to-end script and logging

The corpus directory `crates/frob-cert/tests/specimens/tube-inclusion/` (accept and reject files with
`expected.toml`; this bead's hand specimens and mutations, and later the braking bundle and its mutants that
N22b-35 registers) is run by `scripts/e2e/lemma_auditor_mutations.sh` (bead N12-34, a dependency), which asserts `unsupported` for the kind until it is
enabled and afterwards one accept and at least three rejects; it logs per file the number of kept cells, actions
and guards checked, the first failing cell, action and guard with the enclosure that left the target, and the
rule. Its NDJSON lines for this kind (STD-E2E helper, `target/e2e-logs/lemma_auditor_mutations/<utc>.ndjson`
with a transcript) carry `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N12-19`), `step` (`tube-inclusion/<specimen-or-mutation>`),
`event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the registry status of the kind, the expected and observed
verdict, the bundle's BLAKE3, and on a mismatch the first failing rule with the path to reproduce. On the funded
branch the shield's gate campaign (N22b-35, GATE-SHIELD-05 on braking) sends its real bundles through this
checker; that run is N22b-35's.

### Known answers and negative controls

The one-sided erosion: for an asymmetric error set E = [0, 1/10], the erosion is by −E; eroding by E shrinks the
wrong face and is caught (`tube_asymmetric_error_erosion_sign`). The union erosion of `tube_union_erosion`.
Negative controls are the mutations. Determinism: D0; identical verdicts twice on one machine, on a second
machine, and in debug and release.

## Considerations

- Kill gate named in advance by the owning mechanism: KILL-TUBE (N22b-36, M8; fallback: reduced-order barriers
  cover small subsystems). If it fires, this kind stays `not-implemented` permanently.
- The tube table's soundness depends on the reset and disturbance enclosures; those are inputs with their own
  classes, and the model-order reduction to at most four states is a monitored assumption.
- Size: up to 8 modes × 16,384 cells, each with a mask of actions and a list of guards; the checker is linear in
  cells × actions × guards, and a bundle beyond the caps is refused, never approximated.
- The auditor's 12,000-line cap: polytope and zonotope inclusion and the tick enclosure are the largest parts; if
  they threaten the cap the kind stays `not-implemented` and the shield's tables ship as `Tested`.
