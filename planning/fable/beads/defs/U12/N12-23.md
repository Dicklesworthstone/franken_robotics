## Background

OPEN-LEMMA-1 asks: what are the memory and the time needed to re-check one theorem's dependency closure with
FrankenLean? Its fail-closed default is "measure before any claim", it is owned by the Lemma lead, and it blocks
the admission gate INT-FLN-01. The facts behind the question (FrankenLean at `4426f8ea7`): the suite lock
certifies only `x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`; the development Mac has no Lean 4.32.0 toolchain installed, so Lean
work runs on a Linux host; a full Mathlib re-check peaked at 92.8 GiB. A single theorem's closure is far smaller,
but how much smaller is not known, and it decides which host the Lean lane needs (the risk register's entry: Lean
work needs a large-memory Linux host; early signal, the closure size measured at INT-FLN-01).

The Lean routes of the theorem cards are Frontier, so they also need a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier before
their work is funded. This bead is both: it measures the open question on one representative theorem per closure
family and reports whether the Lean lane is affordable on the host the program has. Ceiling, stated first: a Lean
capsule raises a card from `Certified` to `Proved` (a second, independent kernel's opinion); it changes no design
decision and no speed.

On ordering: INT-FLN-01 itself (bead BD-380) re-checks and seals one trivial theorem, whose closure is the Lean
core only; that first measurement is taken inside BD-380 and satisfies "measure before any claim" for the
admission gate. This bead extends the measurement to the closures the real cards need, and it must be on record
before any card's Lean route leaves `hypothesis` (BD-381 depends on it).

## Scope

In: registration (the protocol file `docs/pilots/lean-closure.md` and one `[[pilot]]` row of
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`, schema `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`); the measurement harness around
`lake build`, `fln check-olean`, `fln seal-capsule` and `fln verify-capsule --require-semantic`; five probe
theorems (the baseline P0 and the four family probes P1 to P4); the closure listing against the blocked-module
list; the record; the recommendation (host class, which cards' Lean routes are affordable); the closing or
re-opening of OPEN-LEMMA-1's row in `registries/open.toml` (N41-OPEN1).
Out: writing the cards' real proofs (BD-381, N12-39, the card beads); reflection closures (N12-26); upstream
work on FrankenLean.

## Technical approach

1. Probe theorems (DESIGN), chosen to span the closure families the cards use:
   - P0: a trivial arithmetic fact over ℕ (core only; the INT-FLN-01 baseline, re-measured for comparison).
   - P1 (ring arithmetic over a commutative ring; the family of T-15, T-01's general statement, T-03 through
     `Mathlib.Algebra.Quaternion`): e₁·f₁ + e₂·f₂ = 0 from f₂ = r·f₁ and e₁ = −r·e₂.
   - P2 (order theory; the family of T-12): a monotone map on a finite partial order with a least element has a
     least fixed point reached by iteration, importing `Mathlib.Order.FixedPoints`.
   - P3 (ordered fields and matrices; the family of T-21, T-23 and the reflectors): a two-variable Farkas
     instance over a linearly ordered field, importing the matrix and ordered-field modules only.
   - P4 (finite sums over ℚ; the family of T-26): Σ_{i ≤ n} 1/[i·(i + 1)] = n/(n + 1).
2. For each probe record: the transitive import closure (module count, total `.olean` bytes, and the list
   intersected with the blocked modules: `Geometry.Manifold.Algebra.LieGroup`,
   `Analysis.Normed.Algebra.MatrixExponential`, `Analysis.Quaternion`, every representation-theory module);
   stock Lean build wall time and peak resident memory; `fln check-olean` wall time and peak resident memory
   over the closure; capsule size; `verify-capsule --require-semantic` wall time and peak memory in an empty
   environment.
3. Measurement method: each step runs under a wrapper that samples resident memory (peak RSS from the kernel's
   accounting) and wall time; three repetitions; the host fingerprint and the pins (Lean v4.32.0, the Mathlib
   commit of FrankenLean's lock, the `fln` build) are recorded.
4. Falsifier (registered before the run; TARGET): any of P1 to P4 has a closure that meets a blocked module with
   no narrower import available; or the `fln` re-check of any probe's closure exceeds the memory of the Linux
   host available to the program (the host's installed memory is recorded at registration; the full-Mathlib
   figure of 92.8 GiB is the reference point).
5. Outcome table → decisions: per closure family, `affordable` (cards in the family may proceed), `needs larger
   host` (the cards stay `Certified` until a host is provided; owner input), or `blocked` (the family's Lean
   routes are parked with the blocking module named, pending upstream item FD-FLN-01).
6. The numbers are written into the proof-tool registry (N12-01) as measured facts with their date and pins, and
   into the review log.
7. Pilot register row (N38a-09's record, registered before the first run): `id = "PILOT-LEMMA-02"`, `mechanism` =
   "Lean routes of theorem cards re-checked and sealed by `fln` on the program's Linux host", `tier = "F"`,
   `owner = "§12.8"`, `bead = "N12-23"`, `work_package = "WP-31"`, `gated_beads = ["BD-381", "N12-24",
   "N12-39"]` (the Lean-lane beads; WP-31's other upper-tier mechanism, the certificate pipeline, is gated by
   PILOT-LEMMA-01 of N12-22, so neither record holds the other's beads back), `protocol` = `docs/pilots/lean-closure.md`
   with its BLAKE3, freeze date and ledger sequence, `falsifier` = the text of step 4 with the host's installed
   memory in GiB and the blocked-module intersection threshold 0, `ceiling` = `none on speed` with the reason "a
   capsule raises a card from Certified to Proved and changes no decision", `fallback` = { ships = "stock Lean
   alone, recorded as a single-kernel check", kill_gate = "to-register" } with the proposed wording "trigger: no
   closure family of P1 to P4 is `affordable` on the program's Linux host; fallback: stock Lean alone, recorded as
   a single-kernel check, no capsule, GATE-LEMMA-02 not run" (no kill gate of §12.6 names the Lean lane, and the
   fallback is the one the foundation row FD-FLN already states; N38a-09 accepts `to-register` at registration
   and blocks funding of the Lean-lane mechanism until the lead registers the gate, which is flagged to the lead
   in the review log), `tool` = `fln` with its build hash, site `host`. The run appends one `[[pilot.result]]`
   per repetition set with the per-probe values and the verdict.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot is registered with probes, method, host memory and falsifier before the first run; `PILOT-LEMMA-02`
      validates in `pilots.toml` under `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`, and the appended result's `protocol_blake3`
      equals the registered hash.
- [ ] For P0 to P4 the closure size (modules, bytes), the blocked-module intersection, and the time and peak
      memory of build, re-check, seal and verify are recorded, three repetitions each.
- [ ] The falsifier is evaluated and stated; each closure family has one of the three outcomes.
- [ ] OPEN-LEMMA-1 is closed in the open-question register with the measured figures, or re-opened with the
      reason (for example, no Linux host with enough memory).
- [ ] Negative control of the measurement: a probe that imports `Mathlib.Analysis.Normed.Algebra.MatrixExponential`
      is reported `blocked` with that module named; a probe run under a 1 GiB memory limit reports the limit hit
      rather than a pass.
- [ ] No card's Lean route is marked `checked` in the theorem-card registry before this record exists.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`lean/tests/closure/`: `closure_lists_transitive_imports` (a synthetic three-module project gives three modules);
`blocked_intersection_detects` (the planted import is found); `peak_memory_wrapper_reports` (a helper that
allocates a known 512 MiB is measured within 10%); `repetitions_are_three`; `pilot_row_validates`
(`PILOT-LEMMA-02` loads under `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`; with `ceiling` removed it is rejected; its `gated_beads`
are exactly BD-381, N12-24 and N12-39).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_lean_closure.sh`, on the Linux host (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON at
`target/e2e-logs/pilot_lean_closure/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it): checks the protocol
hash against the register, builds, re-checks, seals and verifies each probe three times, includes the two
negative controls (the `MatrixExponential` probe; the 1 GiB limit), appends the result, and runs `cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example pilots -- fundable --bead BD-381 --json`, asserting the answer the verdict and the
kill-gate field imply (`Blocked` with `kill-gate-to-register` while the gate row is unregistered, whatever the
verdict). Every line carries
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N12-23`), `step` (`P<n>/<stage>/rep<k>` with stage one of `build`, `check-olean`,
`seal`, `verify-capsule`), `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, peak resident memory in GiB,
the Lean, Mathlib and `fln` identities and the host fingerprint with its installed memory; per probe `metric`
lines for module count, closure bytes, blocked modules (threshold 0), wall time and peak memory of each step
against the host's memory; on a failure the `fln` diagnostic and the probe source path; and a `summary` with the
outcome per family, whether the falsifier was met and the verdict appended to `PILOT-LEMMA-02`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Reference facts to compare against (dated, at the pins): Lean standard library 2,433 of 2,433 modules re-checked;
Mathlib 9,131 of 11,083 accepted, 0 failed; a full Mathlib re-check peaked at 92.8 GiB. The two negative controls
are in the acceptance list. Determinism: closure listings are exact and reproducible; timings are per host.

## Considerations

- RISK-16 (Lean work needs a large-memory Linux host): mitigations are to measure first (this bead) and to state
  specifications in elementary form (OPEN-LEMMA-2).
- FD-FLN-02 (certify FrankenLean's suite lock on macOS aarch64, P3): until it lands the Lean lane has no path on
  the development Mac.
- If the pilot's outcome is `blocked` or unaffordable for every family, the fallback for the Lean lane is stock
  Lean alone, recorded as a single-kernel check: as in BD-381, the registry row says `proved (single kernel)` and
  the class is `Proved` only under that printed qualification (the class definition says "re-checked by
  FrankenLean when its closure permits"). Such a theorem cannot be sealed as a capsule, does not count toward
  GATE-LEMMA-02, and the instance certificates of the same cards stay `Certified`.
- Build outputs are not committed; only the measured figures and the closure lists are.
