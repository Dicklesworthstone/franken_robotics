## Background

The mathematics compendium is the single list of the formulas the system relies on: every row is a mathematical
statement, constants inside a formula are exact, a value that is a choice is marked DESIGN and a threshold a gate
will test is marked TARGET. The constructions specify the mathematics in context; the compendium states each
identity once, with when it holds and when it is known to fail. This bead turns section D.1 into an oracle
library: an independent, exact reference implementation of each row in the conformance crate, with a law test,
a known answer and a negative control per row. Implementations in the algebra and dynamics crates are tested
against these oracles and never regenerate them; that is the rule that a known answer is never regenerated from
the candidate. Rows that a construction's specification bead also carries are marked; rows with no construction
are carried only here.

Conventions used by every row (they are part of the statements). g = (R, p) = `a_from_b` is a rigid transform
acting as x_a = R·x_b + p; ξ = (ω, v) is a twist, angular part first, body-frame by default; w = (τ, f) is a
wrench, torque first; μ = (π, ℓ) is a momentum. The algebra coadjoint is the dual map ad*_ξ = ad_ξᵀ, with no minus
sign; the group coadjoint is the inverse transpose Ad_g⁻ᵀ; the Euler–Poincaré equation of a left-invariant
system in body velocity reads μ̇ = ad*_ξ μ + w. The map ξ ↦ −ad_ξᵀ is never written ad*. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: `registries/compendium/d1.toml` (one record per row), `crates/frob-conformance/src/compendium/d1.rs` (exact
rational oracles; rotations from rational unit quaternions or Cayley parameters so that no square root is
needed), `crates/frob-conformance/tests/compendium_d1.rs`, the compendium registry check
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/compendium_registry.rs` and the example `compendium_doc` shared by the ten
sections, the script's mutant feature `plant-coadjoint-minus`, and the generated `docs/math/compendium.md` section.
Out: the production implementations (algebra, model and dynamics crates); certificates (the theorem cards).

## Technical approach

| Bracket on 𝔰𝔢(3) | [ξ₁, ξ₂] = (ω₁ × ω₂, ω₁ × v₂ − ω₂ × v₁) | Angular part first | Antisymmetry and the Jacobi identity on rational triples. KA: [(e_x, 0), (e_y, 0)] = (e_z, 0). NC: the second term with a plus sign fails Jacobi | SPEC-C-01, SPEC-C-07 |
| Adjoint | Ad_g(ω, v) = (R·ω, R·v + p × R·ω); Ad(g·h) = Ad(g)·Ad(h) | g maps the source frame b to the target frame a | Homomorphism on rational poses; Ad preserves the bracket. NC: Ad(h)·Ad(g) | SPEC-C-02, SPEC-C-04 |

| Algebra adjoint and coadjoint | ad_ξ η = [ξ, η] = (ω × η_ω, ω × η_v + v × η_ω); ad*_ξ μ = ad_ξᵀ μ = (π × ω + ℓ × v, ℓ × ω), defined by ⟨ad*_ξ μ, η⟩ = ⟨μ, [ξ, η]⟩ | The transpose, with no minus sign; −ad_ξᵀ is written out where needed | The defining pairing identity in eighteen variables. NC: −ad_ξᵀ fails it by a factor −1 | SPEC-C-04 |
| Three typed frame maps | Twist map Ad_g; wrench pushforward Ad_g⁻ᵀ; wrench pullback Ad_gᵀ, the inverse of the pushforward; ⟨Ad_g⁻ᵀ w, Ad_g ξ⟩ = ⟨w, ξ⟩ | A Jacobian transpose is a pullback; moving a wrench with the twist map loses the moment arm | Pullback ∘ pushforward = identity. NC: the twist map applied to the wrench of the KA above gives zero torque | SPEC-C-02 |
| Power pairing | ⟨w, ξ⟩ = τ·ω + f·v, invariant under a change of frame | Twist and wrench expressed in the same frame | Invariance on rational poses. KA: planar two-link arm, power −0.3 W by f·v and by τ·q̇ | SPEC-C-02 |
| Reflection parity | Under x ↦ P·x + t with det P = −1: polar vectors map by P, axial vectors by det(P)·P; twists and wrenches by T(P) = diag(det(P)·P, P) | T(P) preserves the pairing and the bracket because det(P)² = 1 | Both preservations for P = diag(1, −1, 1) times a rational rotation. KA: e_y ↦ e_y, e_x ↦ −e_x, e_z ↦ −e_z for axes. NC: axial vectors mapped by P break the bracket | only here (with SPEC-C-09) |
| Euler–Poincaré, free body | μ̇ = ad*_ξ μ + w. With μ = (π, ℓ): π̇ = π × ω + ℓ × v + τ, ℓ̇ = ℓ × ω + f | Body frame, left-invariant kinetic energy | With π = I·ω, ℓ = m·v, w = 0: d(R·v)/dt = R·(ω × v + v̇) = 0. NC: the opposite sign gives 2R·(ω × v). KA: inertia diag(1, 2, 3), ω = (1, 1, 0): π̇ = (0, 0, −1) | SPEC-C-04 |
| Momentum map | μ = 𝕀(s)·(ξ + 𝔸(s)·ṡ) | 𝕀 is the locked (composite rigid-body) inertia at the base | Consistency with the block form of kinetic energy on a two-body fixture: 𝔸 = 𝕀⁻¹·𝕀_BS | SPEC-C-04, SPEC-C-08 |
| Reconstruction | ξ = −𝔸(s)·ṡ + 𝕀(s)⁻¹·p; at zero momentum ξ = −𝔸(s)·ṡ | p is the group momentum; the second term is absent for principally kinematic systems and zero-momentum free flight; a connection belongs to one contact stratum | KA (two coaxial bodies with inertias I₁, I₂, zero momentum): a relative rotation Δψ rotates body 1 by −I₂/(I₁ + I₂)·Δψ | SPEC-C-07, SPEC-C-08 |
| Curvature of the connection | (D𝔸)ᵢⱼ = ∂ᵢ𝔸ⱼ − ∂ⱼ𝔸ᵢ − [𝔸ᵢ, 𝔸ⱼ]; a small loop of area da in the (i, j) plane gives g ≈ exp(−(D𝔸)ᵢⱼ·da) | Leading order in loop size; large loops keep the ordered product | KA: 𝔸₁ = e_x, 𝔸₂ = e_y on SO(3) gives (D𝔸)₁₂ = −e_z and a rotation of +da about z. NC: +[𝔸ᵢ, 𝔸ⱼ] is wrong by twice the answer | SPEC-C-08 |
| Cayley map | cay(x) = (I − x/2)⁻¹·(I + x/2). On SO(3): R = I + 4/(4 + ‖ω‖²)·(ω̂ + ω̂²/2). On SE(3) the translation is (I − ω̂/2)⁻¹·v | Rational; lands on the group exactly; agrees with exp to second order | RᵀR = I and det R = 1 exactly for rational ω. KA: ω = (0, 0, 2) gives the quarter turn about z; (I − ω̂/2)⁻¹ = I + (2ω̂ + ω̂²)/(4 + ‖ω‖²) | SPEC-C-05 |
| Cayley angle | A Cayley step rotates by 2·atan(‖ω‖·h/2); the relative error is about (‖ω‖·h)²/12 | Use bounded by ‖ω‖·h ≤ 0.2 per substep (DESIGN), refused beyond | KA: at ‖ω‖·h = 0.2 the angle is 2·atan(0.1) = 0.199337 rad, relative error 0.00331 against 0.04/12 = 0.00333. NC: a step at ‖ω‖·h = 0.5 is refused, not silently taken | SPEC-C-05 |
| Planar Cayley | z₊ = z·((1 − a²/4) + i·a)/(1 + a²/4), a = ω·h | Unit complex z; the revolute coordinate is (c, s) plus a turn count | |z₊| = |z| exactly, since (1 − a²/4)² + a² = (1 + a²/4)². KA: a = 2 gives multiplication by i (a quarter turn = 2·atan(1)). NC: additive update z + i·a·z changes the modulus | only here |
| Inverse Cayley differential | (Dcay_x)⁻¹ = [[I + ½·ω̂ + ¼·ω·ωᵀ, 0], [½·v̂ + ¼·ω̂·v̂, I + ½·ω̂]] for x = (ω, v); its rotation block inverts to 2/(4 + ‖ω‖²)·(2I − ω̂) | Left-trivialized: cay(x)⁻¹·δcay(x) = Dcay_x(δx) | (I + ½ω̂ + ¼ω·ωᵀ)·(2/(4 + ‖ω‖²))·(2I − ω̂) = I exactly; the matrix times a finite-difference left-trivialized differential is the identity. NC: the right-trivialized form keeps momentum and destroys the energy bound | SPEC-C-36 |
| Pseudo-inertia | 𝖯 = [[Σ, h], [hᵀ, m]] with Σ = ∫ x·xᵀ dm = ½·tr(Ī)·I − Ī and h = m·c; consistent exactly when 𝖯 ≻ 0 | Ī is the rotational inertia about the frame origin | Round trip Ī ↔ Σ. NC: principal moments (1, 1, 3) give Σ = diag(3/2, 3/2, −1/2), not positive definite | SPEC-C-03 |
| Shell rule | 𝖯_shell = 𝖯(Ω, ρ) − 𝖯(Ω_t, ρ) for outer region Ω and inward offset Ω_t; a thin spherical shell tends to I = (2/3)·m·r² | Same frame and density; computed in exact rationals so the subtraction loses nothing | Spherical shell of thickness t: I/(m·r²) → 2/3 as t → 0 (exact rational formula (2/5)·(r⁵ − rᵢ⁵)/(r³ − rᵢ³)/r²). KA: aluminium box 0.4 × 0.3 × 0.2 m at 2,700 kg/m³: 64.8 kg solid, 3.45 kg with a 2.5 mm wall, ratio 18.8. NC: solid default for the enclosure | only here |
| Mass-support bound | ‖c − o‖² + tr(I_C)/(2m) ≤ ρ² for mass inside a ball of centre o and radius ρ | Applied to a declared mass envelope, never to a collision proxy | KA: centred 1 kg part with three principal moments 0.008 kg·m² needs 0.012 m² against 0.010 m² for ρ = 0.10 m (fails); a thin shell with moments 1/150 kg·m² reaches equality | SPEC-C-03 |
| Mobility count | M = 6·(n − 1 − j) + Σ fᵢ | Wrong for over-constrained linkages | KA: Sarrus (6 links, 6 revolutes): count 0, mobility 1; Bennett (4 links, 4 revolutes): count −2, mobility 1. The oracle returns the count and flags it as a count, not a mobility | SPEC-C-01 |
| Product of exponentials | g(θ) = exp(θ₁·Ŝ₁)·…·exp(θₙ·Ŝₙ)·g₀ | Screws in the base frame; polynomial in (cᵢ, sᵢ) with cᵢ² + sᵢ² = 1 | KA: planar two-link chain (0.4 m, 0.3 m; angles 0 and π/2): tool point (0.4, 0.3) m, point Jacobian [[−0.3, −0.3], [0.4, 0.0]] m/rad. NC: lower three rows of the space Jacobian used as the point Jacobian give torques (0, 0) | SPEC-C-11, SPEC-C-04 |
| Constant-twist chord | Δp = v·Δt·sinc(δ/2)·(cos(θ + δ/2), sin(θ + δ/2)) with δ = ω·Δt and sinc(0) = 1 | Planar motion at constant twist; exact for straight travel | KA: a unit-speed half turn in one second gives the chord (0, 2/π) m. NC: heading 32 rad, wheel speeds 1 and 1 + 6·10⁻¹³ m/s on a 0.5 m track, 400 steps of 1/400 s: a formula that divides by ω returns zero translation where the limit is about (0.8342, 0.5514) m | SPEC-C-07 |

Each record in `d1.toml` has `name`, `statement`, `validity`, `used_in`, `oracle` (function path), `tests`,
`negative_control`, `carried_by` (the specification beads, or `compendium-only`). The registry check
(`crates/frob-conformance/tests/compendium_registry.rs`, over every section file present) fails if a row has no
oracle, no test or no negative control where one is listed. The document `docs/math/compendium.md` is rendered
from the section files by the example target `compendium_doc`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All twenty-one rows of D.1 are present in the registry with an oracle function and at least one law test;
      every listed negative control is a test that fails when the wrong formula is substituted.
- [ ] The oracles use exact rational arithmetic wherever the statement is rational, and declare their tolerance
      with a dimension where it is not (the Cayley angle, the chord with sinc).
- [ ] The generated document matches the registry; rows marked `compendium-only` (reflection parity, planar
      Cayley, shell rule) are listed as such so that no specification bead is assumed to carry them.
- [ ] No production crate is a dependency of the oracle module, and no oracle value is generated by the code it
      will later check.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/compendium_d1.rs`, one test per row, named `d1_<row>`; for example
`d1_bracket_jacobi`, `d1_adjoint_homomorphism`, `d1_coadjoint_inverse_transpose`, `d1_ad_star_pairing`,
`d1_frame_maps_inverse`, `d1_pairing_invariant`, `d1_parity_preserves_bracket`, `d1_free_body_velocity_constant`,
`d1_beanie_reconstruction`, `d1_curvature_so3_fixture`, `d1_cayley_on_group`, `d1_cayley_angle_bound`,
`d1_planar_cayley_modulus`, `d1_inverse_cayley_differential`, `d1_pseudo_inertia_round_trip`,
`d1_shell_limit_two_thirds`, `d1_mass_support_fixture`, `d1_mobility_count_sarrus_bennett`, `d1_poe_planar_2r`,
`d1_chord_half_turn`, `d1_chord_near_straight`; plus one `d1_nc_<row>` per negative control.
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/compendium_registry.rs`: `rows_resolve_oracle_test_and_control` (every row of the
section files present); `row_without_listed_control_fails` and `unresolved_oracle_path_fails` (scratch copies of
`d1.toml` with the defect planted); `doc_matches_registry` (the rendered document equals the committed one).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/compendium_laws.sh`, written by this bead and shared by the ten section beads (header `bead=N12-41`,
`milestone=M1`, `plants=coadjoint-minus,doc-drift`; sources the STD-E2E helper `scripts/e2e/lib.sh`). The
compendium has no `frob` verb and none is added: the Gauntlet's oracles are Cargo test targets, never a verb
(ADR-GAUNT-01, BD-025), so the script drives the test targets through the build-offload shim. Steps:
1. `registry`: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test registry_check` and the compendium registry check `cargo
   test -p frob-conformance --test compendium_registry` (every row of every `registries/compendium/d*.toml`
   present has an oracle path that resolves, at least one test and, where listed, a negative control); logs the
   row count per section against the plan's counts (21, 12, 10, 14, 10, 15, 7, 19, 14, 4); a section whose bead
   has not landed is logged as `skip`, never as a pass.
2. `laws`: for each section target present, `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test compendium_dN` with
   `FROB_TEST_EVIDENCE_DIR` set; every `frob.testevidence/1` line becomes one `assert` line (row name, oracle
   path, test name, observed, expected, tolerance with its unit, status, seed for simulated rows) and every
   negative control one `assert` with `kind: "negative-control"` and `caught`.
3. `control` and plants. The compendium is a reference and records no gate, so it registers nothing in
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` (a control record must name a gate and a seed of an admitted
   form, BD-025, and `nc-<nnnn>` features belong to registered controls): the rows' negative controls are the
   `d<N>_nc_<row>` tests that step 2 runs, and the script's own mutant is a test-level control. This bead declares
   the feature `plant-coadjoint-minus = ["gauntlet-mutations"]` in the manifest of `frob-conformance` (never a
   default and never listed inside the umbrella; because it implies the umbrella it does not compile under the
   release profile), with a `cfg(feature = "plant-coadjoint-minus")` branch in `compendium::d1` that substitutes
   −ad_ξᵀ for ad*_ξ in the oracle. Every run includes `e2e_expect_caught coadjoint-minus -- cargo test -p
   frob-conformance --features plant-coadjoint-minus --test compendium_d1 -- d1_ad_star_pairing`, which must
   fail. The plants are the script's own negative controls (STD-E2E: with `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE=<plant>` the
   script must exit 1 with a failed assertion naming the plant, as `run-all.sh --verify-plants` checks): with
   `coadjoint-minus` step 2 builds the D.1 target with that feature, so `d1_ad_star_pairing` fails and the script
   logs the failed `assert` `coadjoint-minus:d1_ad_star_pairing`; with `doc-drift` a one-character edit of the
   regenerated document fails step 4's assertion `doc-drift:compendium-bytes`.
4. `doc`: regenerate the document into the scratch directory with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
   compendium_doc -- --out <scratch>/compendium.md` (this bead's example target, which renders every
   `registries/compendium/d*.toml` present) and assert byte equality with the committed `docs/math/compendium.md`
   (both digests logged).
5. `summary`: rows covered and controls caught per section, sections skipped, the gates touched (none: the
   compendium is a reference, not a gate).
Log: `target/e2e-logs/compendium_laws/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, and on failure the first
differing value with the row name and the path of the evidence file.

### Known answers and negative controls

As listed per row. Related suite rows restated here: the shell limit (2/3 against 2/5), the pseudo-inertia round
trip, the mass-support fixture, the curvature fixture, the planar two-link arm. Determinism: exact rows are
bit-reproducible on every platform; tolerance rows state their tolerance.

## Considerations

- The compendium does not replace the constructions' specifications; where both exist they must agree, and a
  disagreement is a defect in one of them, resolved by a dated edit.
- A wrong convention that is self-consistent passes law tests; the known answers with a moment arm, a sign and a
  mirror exist to break that symmetry.
