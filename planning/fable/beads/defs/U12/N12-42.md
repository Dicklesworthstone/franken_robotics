## Background

The mathematics compendium states each formula the system relies on once, with its validity and its known
failure. This bead turns section D.2 (time stepping and hybrid events) into oracles in the conformance crate:
independent reference implementations with a law test, a known answer and a negative control per row, against
which the dynamics, gait, clock and numerics code is tested and which that code never regenerates. Conventions
are those of section D.1: g = (R, p), twists (ω, v) angular first, wrenches (τ, f) torque first, ad*_ξ = ad_ξᵀ.
Rows also carried by a construction's specification bead are marked; four rows (rational clocks, exposure
average, single-precision spacing, two-sided scaling of a Cholesky solve) belong to no construction and are
carried only here. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: `registries/compendium/d2.toml`, `crates/frob-conformance/src/compendium/d2.rs`, the test target
`compendium_d2`, the generated document section. Out: the integrators, the event locator, the clock contract and
the scaling code themselves.

## Technical approach

NC: the differential evaluated at +h·ξ_k leaves momentum exact and destroys the energy bound, so it must be caught by energy and reversibility | SPEC-C-36 |

KA: four fixed wheels as ideal rows return ω = 0 under any command. NC: Euclidean projection (M replaced by I) raises kinetic energy on a fixture with unequal masses | SPEC-C-36, SPEC-C-07 |

NC: a marginal orbit (multiplier 1) is refused, not solved | SPEC-C-16 |

NC: unequal restitutions violate it by terms proportional to r_L − r_R | SPEC-C-16 |
| Rational clocks | Base unit 1/L s with L the least common multiple of the period denominators; process i fires when n ≡ φᵢ (mod Nᵢ) with Nᵢ = Tᵢ·L; firings up to clock n number ⌊(n − φᵢ)/Nᵢ⌋ + 1 | No floating-point time is compared; a model whose base unit is finer than a declared limit is refused | KA: physics at 400 Hz, an IMU at 100 Hz and a policy at 30 Hz give L = 1,200 and periods of 3, 12 and 40 base units; the policy tick at unit 40 falls one unit after the physics boundary at 39 and two before the one at 42; the pattern repeats every 120 units, 0.1 s. NC: running the policy every thirteenth physics step gives 39 units, a period of 32.5 ms instead of 33⅓ ms | only here |
| Exposure average | A sensor reading is a functional of the history over its window (a, b]; an impulse J inside an exposure of length T contributes J/T to that window and nothing to its neighbour | Half-open windows: an event at b belongs to the window ending at b. NC: a closed-closed window double-counts the boundary impulse; an open-open window loses it | only here |

NC: D with a non-power-of-two entry (3) breaks bitwise equality on at least one matrix of the same seeded sample (the test asserts that a difference exists, not that every matrix differs) | only here |

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All twelve rows of D.2 are present with an oracle and a law test; each listed negative control fails when
      the wrong formula or rule is substituted.
- [ ] The rational-clock oracle uses integers only; the exposure oracle uses half-open windows on the side ruled
      by N20a-49; both are tested at boundary instants, and the exposure oracle's own window-membership test is
      written independently of `frob-sense`'s `window_contains` (it is the allow-listed independent oracle of
      that bead's single-implementation lint, never a call into it).
- [ ] The floating-point rows (spacing; two-sided scaling) are tested bitwise on the host in single and double
      precision and state that they hold absent overflow and underflow.
- [ ] The four `compendium-only` rows are marked as such in the registry and the generated document.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/compendium_laws.sh` (defined with the D.1 bead) runs this target and logs one `assert` line per row
and per negative control.

### Known answers and negative controls

As listed per row. Suite rows restated: the clock fixture (L = 1,200; 3, 12, 40; repeat 120), the firing count
⌊(n − φᵢ)/Nᵢ⌋ + 1, the 20 N exposure average, the bouncing-ball saltation matrix, the rimless-wheel multiplier.
Determinism: integer and rational rows are exact; floating rows are bitwise on one platform and compared across
platforms under the IEEE probe.

## Considerations

- The variational-step oracle is for one rigid body; using it as a reference for an articulated tree would be a
  category error, and the oracle refuses a mass matrix that depends on configuration.
- The exposure row's boundary side is an open decision (N20a-49) because the plan states it twice, as (a, b] in
  the conventions chapter and in this appendix and as [open, close) in the Sensorium chapter. The oracle follows
  the ruling; if the ruling is `OpensAt`, this row's statement and its document line are amended to [a, b) by
  the same dated edit that amends the conventions rows, and the interior known answer (20 N) is unchanged.
- The Cholesky row assumes round-to-nearest with contraction off; a backend that contracts multiply-adds does
  not satisfy it, which is what the probe kernels check.
