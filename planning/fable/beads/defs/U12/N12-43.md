## Background

The mathematics compendium states each formula once, with its validity and known failure. This bead turns
section D.3 (contact, wheels and terrain) into oracles in the conformance crate, each with a law test, a known
answer and a negative control, as the fixed reference for the contact, wheel, film, soil and clearance code.
Two rows (squeeze film; pressure–sinkage and shear) are semi-empirical laws that belong to no construction and
are carried only here; their oracle is the closed form, and their class in any result is `Estimated`. Tier:
Solid for the mathematics.

## Scope

In: `registries/compendium/d3.toml`, `crates/frob-conformance/src/compendium/d3.rs`, the test target
`compendium_d3`, the generated document section. Out: the contact solver, the cone kernels, the film and soil
laws and the clearance complexes themselves.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All ten rows of D.3 are present with an oracle and a law test; each listed negative control fails when the
      wrong law is substituted.
- [ ] The two semi-empirical rows are marked `compendium-only` and `Estimated`; their oracles check the closed
      form, monotonicity, limits and dimensional homogeneity, and make no claim about soil or film physics.
- [ ] The cone rows are tested in exact rationals where the data are rational (the projection example; duality
      on Pythagorean points) and with a stated dimensionless tolerance elsewhere.
- [ ] The generated document matches the registry.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/compendium_laws.sh` runs this target and logs one `assert` line per row and per negative control.

### Known answers and negative controls

As listed per row. Suite rows restated: the √2 box excess, the coupling value μ·λ_n/√2, the pure-spin moment
(2/3)·μ·N·R, the ladder bound 2√2·a, the growth vectors. Determinism: exact rows bit-reproducible; tolerance rows
state tolerance and dimension.

## Considerations

- Rolling resistance and tactile micro-patches are separate rows of the contact solve and are not part of the
  four-dimensional cone; the oracle does not model them.
- The effective radius r is a card parameter, `Estimated` unless calibrated; the oracle checks the two closed
  forms for r and nothing about which applies to a real foot.
