## Background

The mathematics compendium states each formula once, with its validity and known failure. This bead turns
section D.4 (actuators and power) into oracles in the conformance crate, each with a law test, a known answer
and a negative control: the fixed reference for the actuator levels, the thermal network, the battery and bus,
the transmissions and the energy ledger. Most rows belong to the port-Hamiltonian actuator construction; seven
rows belong to no construction (motor envelope, voltage limit and field weakening, two-RC battery, bus power
ceiling, four-quadrant transmission, cost of transport, Froude number) and are carried only here. Tier: Solid.
Lumped thermal and electrical parameters are card values, `Estimated` unless calibrated; the oracles check the
formulas, not the cards.

## Scope

In: `registries/compendium/d4.toml`, `crates/frob-conformance/src/compendium/d4.rs`, the test target
`compendium_d4`, the generated document section. Out: actuator, battery and transmission models and cards; the
energy ledger implementation.

## Technical approach

| Row | Statement | Holds when; known failure | Law test, known answer (KA), negative control (NC) | Also specified by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Port-Hamiltonian form | ẋ = (J(x) − R(x))·∇H(x) + G(x)·u, y = G(x)ᵀ·∇H(x), so Ḣ = uᵀy − ∇HᵀR∇H | J skew, R positive semidefinite | Power balance identity on random J, R, quadratic H in exact rationals. NC: a J with a symmetric part creates energy | SPEC-C-38, SPEC-C-12 |
| Motor axis | L·di/dt = V − R_w·i − k·ω; J_r·ω̇ = k·i − b·ω − τ_load; locked-rotor current (V/R_w)·(1 − e^(−t/τ)) with τ = L/R_w | k_t = k_e = k in SI units | KA: R_w = 1 Ω, L = 10 mH give τ = 10 ms. Law: the two equations are the port-Hamiltonian form with H = φ²/(2L) + p²/(2J_r), a gyrator of modulus k, and dissipation R_w·i² + b·ω². NC: k_t ≠ k_e makes the gyrator create or lose power | SPEC-C-38 |
| Motor envelope | i ≤ min(I_max, (V_bus − k_e·N·ω)/R); τ = η·N·k_t·i − τ_friction | Steady-state electrical model at level L1 | No-load speed where the voltage-limited current reaches zero: ω = V_bus/(k_e·N); stall torque at ω = 0. NC: omitting the back-EMF term gives torque at any speed | only here |
| Voltage limit and field weakening | (λ + L_d·i_d)² + (L_q·i_q)² ≤ (V_lim/ω_e)² with V_lim = V_bus/√2; base speed V_lim/√(λ² + (L·I_max)²); top speed V_lim/(λ − L·I_max) when λ > L·I_max | Power-invariant dq coordinates, resistance neglected, non-salient for the closed forms; the inscribed circle is conservative by at most 1 − √3/2 of voltage | At base speed with i_d = 0, i_q = I_max the limit is met with equality; at top speed with i_d = −I_max, i_q = 0 likewise. KA: 1 − √3/2 ≈ 0.134. NC: when λ ≤ L·I_max the top-speed formula does not apply (no finite top speed from this limit) and the oracle says so | only here |
| Thermal node | C·Ṫ_w = P_loss − G_th·(T_w − T_amb), with copper loss i²·R₀·(1 + α·(T_w − T₀)) | One lumped node; card values are `Estimated` unless calibrated | At constant current the node is linear, Ṫ_w = a·T_w + c with a = (i²·R₀·α − G_th)/C. Law: closed-form exponential approach matches numerical integration | SPEC-C-38 |
| Thermal runaway condition | With s = i²·R₀·α: a stable equilibrium T_amb + P_amb/(G − s) exists exactly when s < G; runaway begins at I_crit = √(G/(R₀·α)). In general an equilibrium is stable where dP/dT < G | Constant current. Under torque control the loss curve is convex and the equilibrium vanishes at a tangency below the slope estimate | KA: R₀ = 0.1 Ω, α = 0.0039 K⁻¹, G_th = 0.5 W/K give I_crit = √(0.5/0.00039) ≈ 35.8 A; below it the temperature settles at T_amb + i²·R₀·(1 + α·(T_amb − T₀))/(G_th − i²·R₀·α), above it it grows exponentially. NC: a constant-resistance model never runs away | SPEC-C-38 |
| Entropy production | σ = P_loss/T_w + G_th·(T_w − T_amb)²/(T_w·T_amb) ≥ 0 whenever P_loss ≥ 0 | A sign check on each assigned loss; it cannot fail for a correct model | Nonnegativity for positive absolute temperatures. NC: a regenerative mode with a negated copper loss gives σ < 0 | SPEC-C-38 |
| Two-RC battery | V = OCV(z) − R₀·I − V₁ − V₂; dVₖ/dt = −Vₖ/(Rₖ·Cₖ) + I/Cₖ; dz/dt = −I/Q | I positive on discharge; R₀ depends on cell temperature; ageing advances between episodes only | Step response: under constant current each Vₖ approaches I·Rₖ with time constant Rₖ·Cₖ; charge balance z(t) = z₀ − I·t/Q. NC: the sign of I reversed charges on discharge | only here |
| Bus power ceiling | A source E with series resistance R delivers P only if P ≤ E²/(4·R), at V = [E + √(E² − 4·R·P)]/2 | Constant-power loads; above the ceiling there is no operating point and the step records a brownout | KA: E = 48 V, R = 0.1 Ω: ceiling 5,760 W at V = 24 V; at P = 2,880 W, V = 24·(1 + √½) ≈ 40.97 V. NC: a load of 6,000 W returns a brownout, not a voltage | only here |
| Four-quadrant transmission | Motoring: τ_joint = η_f·N·τ_motor. Back-driven: τ_motor = η_b·τ_joint/N. A screw with lead angle a and friction angle φ has η_f = tan a/tan(a + φ), η_b = tan(a − φ)/tan a, and locks when φ ≥ a | One multiplier in all quadrants creates energy | Round trip motoring then back-driving returns η_f·η_b < 1 of the power. KA: a = 10°, φ = 5°: η_f = tan 10°/tan 15° ≈ 0.658, η_b = tan 5°/tan 10° ≈ 0.496; φ ≥ a locks. NC: the same η in both directions lets a back-driven joint return more than was put in | only here |
| Rational gear law | From f₂ = r·f₁ and e₁ = −r·e₂ it follows that e₁·f₁ + e₂·f₂ = 0 | Ideal transformer; a composition of power-preserving maps preserves power | Polynomial identity; series composition gives ratio r·s. NC: e₁ = r·e₂ leaves 2·r·e₂·f₁ | SPEC-C-12 |
| Energy balance | Supplied = change in stored + dissipated + exported, per mechanism and per step, with the numerical residual on its own line | Heat held in a thermal node is stored energy, not loss | A motor–thermal fixture integrated exactly for one step closes with zero residual in rationals. NC: a ledger that books heat entering the node as both stored and lost shows a deficit equal to that heat | SPEC-C-12, SPEC-C-38 |
| Cost of transport | E/(m·g·d) | Stated separately for mechanical and electrical energy | Dimensionless under the similarity rescaling; invariant under power-of-two unit changes. NC: mixing mechanical energy in the numerator with an electrical claim | only here |
| Froude number | v²/(g·L) | L is leg length, or ℓ₀ in canonical form | Dimensionless; equal for dynamically similar gaits (v ∝ √L). NC: v/(g·L) is not dimensionless and fails the dimension check | only here (with SPEC-C-21) |

### Known answers and negative controls

As listed per row. Suite rows restated: τ = 10 ms for R_w = 1 Ω, L = 10 mH; I_crit ≈ 35.8 A; the bus ceiling
E²/(4·R). Determinism: rational rows exact; rows with exponentials and square roots state a tolerance with its
dimension.

## Considerations

- The thermal runaway condition is for constant current; under torque control the equilibrium vanishes at a
  tangency below the slope estimate, and the oracle exposes both cases.
- The second-law check has content only as a check on the sign of each assigned loss.
