## Background

The mathematics compendium states each formula once, with its validity and known failure. This bead turns
section D.5 (symmetry, similarity and counting) into oracles in the conformance crate, each with a law test, a
known answer and a negative control: the fixed reference for the finite-group code, the equivariant layers, the
gait atlas, the similarity canonical form, campaign quotienting and Kleene iteration. Every row of this section
is also carried by a construction's specification bead; the compendium's job here is the independent oracle and
the cross-check that the two statements agree. All the groups in scope have at most a few dozen elements, so the
oracles work by exhaustive enumeration in exact arithmetic. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: `registries/compendium/d5.toml`, `crates/frob-conformance/src/compendium/d5.rs` (a small brute-force group
engine over permutations and signed permutation matrices with rational or cyclotomic-free real arithmetic), the
test target `compendium_d5`, the generated document section. Out: the production group, character and poset
code; equivariant networks; campaign generation.

- [ ] All ten rows of D.5 are present with an oracle and a law test; each listed negative control fails when the

- [ ] The similarity oracle computes the number of dimensionless groups as n − r from the exponent matrix by
      exact rational rank.
- [ ] The generated document matches the registry.

## Considerations

- Linearized operators that commute with the action preserve isotypic blocks; an equivariant nonlinear map need
  not decouple. The oracles state the linear facts only.
- Similarity is exact mathematics and rare physics: material strength, motor technology, sensor noise and
  latency do not scale with a robot; the oracle reports the named groups that differ.
