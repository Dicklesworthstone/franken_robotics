## Background

The mathematics compendium states each formula once, with its validity and known failure. This bead turns
section D.6 (missions, information and certificates) into oracles in the conformance crate: small, exact,
independent reference solvers for the hand-checkable fixtures on which the mission subsystem, the sensing
analyses and the certificate checkers are judged. Every row of the section is also carried by a construction's
specification bead; the compendium supplies the independent oracle and the negative control. The fixtures here
are deliberately tiny (a scalar docking game, a four-foot stance, a one-parameter product system), so that the
oracle can enumerate or solve them exactly and a reader can check the numbers by hand. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: `registries/compendium/d6.toml`, `crates/frob-conformance/src/compendium/d6.rs` (exact rational enumeration
and small linear feasibility by vertex enumeration, written independently of the mission crate and of the
auditor), the test target `compendium_d6`, the generated document section. Out: the mission solvers, the
checkers, and every production analysis.

### Known answers and negative controls

As listed per row. Midpoint arithmetic for the record: at (e, W) = (0.45, 1.45) the positive-x pair must supply
1.45 × 1.45/2 = 841/800 = 1.05125 N against a cap of 1 N. Determinism: exact.

## Considerations

- These oracles are the only place where the family fixtures are solved by brute force; they must stay simple
  enough to audit by reading, and they must not grow into a second mission solver.
- A lifted linear program, a scenario tree and quantifier elimination are the generic comparators on the mission
  side; the oracles here are smaller than any of them.
