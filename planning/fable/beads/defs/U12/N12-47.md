## Background

The mathematics compendium states each formula once, with its validity and known failure. This bead turns
section D.7 (estimation, control and safety) into oracles in the conformance crate, each with a law test, a known
answer and a negative control: the fixed reference for the invariant filter, the geometric controllers, the
requirement language's robustness semantics, the energy-budget tier, tube tables and the runtime shield. Every
row is also carried by a construction's specification bead; the compendium supplies the independent oracle.
Tier: Solid for the mathematics; several consumers are Frontier or Moonshot, which does not change the
arithmetic.

## Scope

In: `registries/compendium/d7.toml`, `crates/frob-conformance/src/compendium/d7.rs`, the test target
`compendium_d7`, the generated document section. Out: the filter, controllers, monitors, shield and requirement
evaluators themselves.

## Technical approach

KA: θ = π/2 gives ‖e_R‖ = 1; θ = π gives 0, which is the excluded case. NC: using the error near θ = π as a measure of closeness | SPEC-C-13 |
| Requirement robustness | An atom ψ(x) ≥ 0 with declared scale s has ρ = ψ/s; conjunction is min, disjunction max, negation −ρ; G_[0,T] φ at time 0 equals H_[0,T] φ at time T | ρ > 0 implies satisfaction, ρ < 0 violation, ρ = 0 decides nothing; an uncovered window yields `undetermined` | Soundness on random signals against a Boolean evaluator; the streaming monotonic-deque window minimum equals the brute-force minimum. KA (units): a clearance of 0.02 m and a thermal margin of 15 K have minimum 0.02 in metres and kelvin and 15 in millimetres and kelvin; with scales 0.10 m and 10 K the values are 0.2 and 1.5 and clearance binds in every unit system. | Energy budget | ½·m_cap·v² + ∫ max(P_elec, 0) dt + E_stored + E_gravity + E_other + E_reserve ≤ W_abs,min | Needs a physical guide with an absorber on every forward path; passive contact with any μ ≥ 0 only dissipates. Covers stops and guided contacts, not balance | KA: 8 kg at 0.4 m/s is 0.64 J; a 20 W drive over a 25 ms cutoff adds 0.50 J; gravity on an 8% slope over a 40 mm stroke adds 8 × 9.81 × 0.040 × 0.08 = 0.25 J; a spring stores 0.10 J; total 1.49 J against 50 N over 40 mm = 2.00 J; largest admissible cutoff (2.00 − 0.64 − 0.25 − 0.10)/20 s = 50.5 ms, or 45.5 ms with 0.10 J of reserve. Exclusion: 8 kg over a 12 mm sill needs 0.94176 J against 0.90 J. NC: the budget without its gravity term | SPEC-C-31 |

NC: for K_n = [0, 6/5] the correct erosion is [1/10, 6/5] and the inclusion fails (a measured value of −1/10 is possible), while eroding by E instead of −E gives [0, 11/10] and wrongly accepts the unsafe table | SPEC-C-31 |
| Single-row shield | u(λ) = clip(u_π + λ·R⁻¹·a, lo, hi) is piecewise linear in λ with at most 2n breakpoints; the answer is the least λ ≥ 0 with aᵀu(λ) ≥ b | Diagonal metric R; quantifier order ∃u ∀(w, e). Projecting and then clipping is wrong | KA: R = I, u_π = (1, 0), box [0, 1]², row u₁ + u₂ ≥ 1.5: the breakpoint method gives (1, 0.5). NC: the projection that ignores the box gives (1.25, 0.25), which leaves the box, and clipping it gives (1, 0.25), which violates the row. Law: aᵀu(λ) is non-decreasing in λ; the result satisfies the KKT conditions of the box-constrained projection | SPEC-C-31 |
| Residual risk of a shield | Per episode at most E[N_breach]·δ₁ + P(N_alarm ≥ 1)·δ₂ + δ₃, with δ₂ the probability that an episode with at least one alarm ends in a violation | δ₁ needs independent hidden-state labels; the bound does not survive a shift that moves δ₁ | Exhaustive check on a finite episode model with planted breach, alarm and violation probabilities: the true violation probability never exceeds the bound; δ₁ is the share of breaches on which every monitor stays silent and δ₃ the probability of a violation with neither a breach nor an alarm. NC: estimating δ₁ from the monitor's own alarms (it cannot label its silent breaches) gives 0 and an invalid bound | SPEC-C-31 |

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All seven rows of D.7 are present with an oracle and a law test; each listed negative control fails when
      the wrong rule is substituted.
- [ ] The robustness oracle implements both the offline window semantics and the streaming deque and tests their
      equality; it returns `undetermined` for uncovered windows.
- [ ] The shield oracle returns the least λ by sorting breakpoints and checks KKT; it is tested on the stated
      fixture in exact rationals.
- [ ] The generated document matches the registry.

the attitude rows state an angular tolerance.

## Considerations

- A 600 s future window at 50 Hz needs 30,000 samples of memory; the oracle's deque is bounded by the window
  length and reports its size.
- The quantifier order ∃u ∀(w, e) is part of the shield row: a certificate proved in the order ∀w ∃u is rejected
  wherever it is offered.
