## Background

The theorem-card table has thirty-five rows, T-01 to T-35. A card is a statement the project wants, with its
route and tier, and a card is admitted only if a checker can decide it; an obligation that fails that test is a
test, a monitored assumption or a separate claim, and no bundle may present it as `Proved` or `Certified`. Nine
proof obligations of other chapters are written as "a candidate theorem card" for Lemma, and the table gives no
number to any of them. Until someone decides, each owning bead carries its obligation as `Tested` (that is the
fail-closed default every one of them states), and each waits for an answer nobody is assigned to give. This bead
is that answer: one owner decision by the Lemma lead, recorded as an ADR, that takes the nine candidates through
the admission rule of the card registry and either maps a candidate to an existing card, admits it under a new
number, or files it with the obligations that no checker decides.

Tier: Solid (a decision and registry rows). Rejected alternatives: letting each chapter number its own cards (two
chapters would pick the same number, and a card's class could be printed without Lemma's admission test);
admitting every candidate (several are analytic statements that no checker here decides, and a row with no
decidable route is exactly what the admission rule forbids); deciding candidate by candidate at each milestone
with no standing rule (the same question would be reopened nine times).

## Scope

In: the admission test applied to the nine candidates; the ADR; new registry rows from T-36 for admitted
candidates (status `hypothesis`, route and tier named); rows in the registry of obligations no checker decides
for the others; the standing rule for candidates raised later; notice to the owning beads.
Out: proving or certifying any admitted card (a card bead is opened for each admitted candidate by the decision,
with its own milestone); the proof obligations' own tests, which stay with their chapters; the thirty-five
existing cards; the registry mechanics (N12-06) and the non-decidable registry (N12-07).

## Technical approach

1. The admission test (the rule of the card registry, applied here by hand before the registry applies it by
   code): a candidate is admissible if its statement is about a finite algebraic object and a route exists whose
   kind is in the closed registry `frob.cert.kinds/1` or which is one of the named decision procedures
   (`kir-typecheck`, `exhaustive-enumeration`, `bit-vector`); a general statement is admissible only through
   `lean-capsule`, and then only if its closure can avoid the blocked Mathlib modules (analysis, measure theory).
2. The nine candidates, with the recommended disposition (DESIGN; the owner may decide otherwise, and the ADR
   records the reasons either way):

3. Numbering. Identifiers are append-only: admitted candidates take T-36 onward in the order of the table above
   (two for PO-SENS-07, then PO-SINEW-01, PO-SHIELD-04, PO-AUGUR-06, PO-NEMESIS-04, so T-36 to T-41 under the
   recommended dispositions). A number is assigned once and never reused; a candidate not admitted gets no number.
4. Registry effects. For each admitted candidate: a row in `registries/theorems.toml` with statement, tier,
   route, status `hypothesis`, the constructions and consumers, and the owning obligation; a card bead opened
   with its milestone (the mechanism's milestone for instance routes, M5 for Lean routes). For each candidate not
   admitted: a row in `registries/not-decidable.toml` with the reason and the substitute test, so that the guard
   refuses any claim that presents it as `Proved` or `Certified`.
5. Standing rule for later candidates: a chapter that wants a card files the statement, the proposed route and
   the consumer with the Lemma lead; the admission test above is applied; the outcome is a dated amendment to
   this ADR and a registry row. No chapter prints a card number that the registry does not hold.
6. Fail-closed default until the ADR exists: none of the nine is a card beyond what T-15, T-26 and T-30 already
   say; each obligation stays at the class its own chapter states (`Tested`, or a written proof on file).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An ADR records, for each of the nine candidates, the admission test, the disposition and the reason, signed
      by the Lemma lead.
- [ ] `registries/theorems.toml` holds one new row per admitted candidate, contiguous from T-36, schema-valid,
      status `hypothesis`; `frob prove list --json` prints them with no class.
- [ ] `registries/not-decidable.toml` holds one row per candidate not admitted, and the guard refuses a claim
      whose subject matches it (negative control: a bundle presenting the clearance Lipschitz bound as
      `Certified` is rejected).
- [ ] A row added for a candidate whose route is outside the closed registry fails the registry check (negative
      control: the infinite sum of PO-ARENA-02 offered with route "analysis").
- [ ] Each owning obligation's bead is told the outcome (card number or registry row), and a card bead exists
      for every admitted candidate.
- [ ] The standing rule is in the ADR, and no document or registry outside `registries/theorems.toml` carries a
      card number above T-35 that the registry lacks.

`crates/frob-lemma/tests/card_registry.rs` gains: `candidates_admitted_are_contiguous_from_t36`;

two candidates already certified through existing cards. Negative controls as listed in the acceptance criteria.
Determinism: the registry listing is sorted by id and identical across machines.

## Considerations

- A card that is admitted and never certified is honest (`hypothesis` prints no class); a candidate left
  undecided is not, because chapters would go on calling it a card.
- Admitting a Lean card adds to the closure question of OPEN-LEMMA-1; the Lean candidates are admitted as rows
  and scheduled only after the closure measurement.
- The exact comparison (1 − p⋆)ⁿ < δ needs big rationals (300 factors of 99/100); it stays inside the auditor's
  exact arithmetic and its size cap.
- If the owner admits fewer candidates, the numbering closes up at decision time; after the ADR it never moves.
