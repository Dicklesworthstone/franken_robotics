## Background

The actuator chapter models every element of a drive (winding, electromechanical conversion, rotor, gear,
coupling transmission, spring, thermal node, battery, bus, driver) as a port-Hamiltonian component: a stored
energy H(x), a structure matrix J, a dissipation matrix R and ports with collocated inputs u and outputs y, so
that ẋ = (J − R)·∇H + G·u and y = Gᵀ·∇H. Its proof obligation PO-SINEW-01 reads: "the composite of passive
elements through power-conserving junctions is passive", with the evidence "structural: skew-symmetry of J and
semidefiniteness of R per element, checked exactly on card rationals; a candidate theorem card". The bead that
runs those exact checks (N15b-09) files the candidate and leaves adoption to the proof subsystem.

The proof chapter's decision bead on candidate cards (N12-52) takes the nine candidates of other chapters
through the admission rule. Its recommended disposition for this one: "admit one instance card, tier Solid,
beside T-29 (wiring conserves power); the general composition theorem is not needed for the instance and is not
admitted now". It also says that a card bead is opened for each admitted candidate, and none had been written.
This is that card bead. It does not rule; it carries out the ruling.

**Admission, restated so that the bead stands alone.** A card is admitted only if a checker can decide it. For
an instance (a concrete card or a concrete composite with rational entries) the statement reduces to three
finite algebraic facts: J + Jᵀ = 0, the junction matrix W satisfies W + Wᵀ = 0, and R = L·D·Lᵀ over ℚ with D
diagonal and positive (a reduced factorization: L has one column per nonzero pivot, so a semidefinite R of rank
r has an r×r D). The first two are linear identities (proof kind `poly-identity`); the third is an identity of
the same kind for the factorization together with a `rational-ldlt` bundle on D, because that kind accepts
positive pivots only (BD-027) and a semidefinite R has zero pivots. Both kinds are in the closed registry
`frob.cert.kinds/1` and have checkers from M0. Class `Certified`, tier Solid.

**Number.** Candidate cards are numbered by N12-52, append-only from T-36 in the order of its table (two for
PO-SENS-07, then PO-SINEW-01, PO-SHIELD-04, PO-AUGUR-06, PO-NEMESIS-04). Under its recommended dispositions
this card is **T-38**. If the owner admits fewer candidates the numbering closes up at decision time; the
registry's number governs and the file names below follow it. No document outside the registry prints a card
number the registry does not hold.

Statement (instance form). Let components i = 1..k of a concrete design have Jᵢ = −Jᵢᵀ, Rᵢ = Rᵢᵀ ⪰ 0 and Hᵢ
bounded below, and let their internal ports be connected by u_int = W·y_int with W = −Wᵀ (a power-conserving
relation with no algebraic loop). Then with H = Σ Hᵢ the composite satisfies
Ḣ = −Σ ∇Hᵢᵀ·Rᵢ·∇Hᵢ + y_extᵀ·u_ext ≤ y_extᵀ·u_ext.

Why the three facts suffice (kept in the card's document as explanation, not as a card route). For each
component ∇Hᵢᵀ·Jᵢ·∇Hᵢ = ½·∇Hᵢᵀ·(Jᵢ + Jᵢᵀ)·∇Hᵢ = 0, so Ḣᵢ = −∇Hᵢᵀ·Rᵢ·∇Hᵢ + yᵢᵀ·uᵢ. Summing, the internal port power
is y_intᵀ·W·y_int = 0 by the same identity, and ∇Hᵢᵀ·Rᵢ·∇Hᵢ = Σⱼ dⱼ·(Lᵀ·∇Hᵢ)ⱼ² ≥ 0.

Scope of validity: linear dissipation with a state-dependent but pointwise semidefinite R. Set-valued monotone
elements (stiction) and impact laws (backlash on the referee) are passive by monotonicity or by a restitution
in [0, 1]; they are checked by N15b-09's own rules and are outside this card. Rejected alternative: leaving
the obligation `Tested` by simulation (energy that appears only in a rare quadrant is exactly what a sampled
test misses).

## Technical approach

1. Certificate recipe for an element with rational (J, R): three bundles, or two when R = 0.
   (i) Skew part: a `poly-identity` bundle for eᵀ·J·e = 0 in n free symbols e, which holds as a polynomial
   identity exactly when J + Jᵀ = 0 (the kind's payload is one pair of scalar expressions, so the matrix
   statement is carried by its quadratic form; entries are rational functions of the declared state where a
   value depends on temperature).
   (ii) Dissipation: the producer factors R exactly with symmetric pivoting (every pivot nonnegative, a zero
   pivot only with a zero remaining row: the semidefinite condition of N15b-09's check; when it fails R is not
   semidefinite and no bundle is written), drops the zero pivots, and writes a `poly-identity` bundle for
   eᵀ·R·e = Σ_{k=1..r} d_k·(ℓ_kᵀ·e)², with the nonzero pivots d_k and the matching columns ℓ_k of the permuted
   factor as exact constants, and a `rational-ldlt` bundle on the r×r matrix diag(d₁, …, d_r), which the M0
   checker accepts exactly when every d_k > 0. For R = 0 (a lossless element) r = 0 and the identity
   eᵀ·R·e = 0 stands alone. The quadratic form sees only the symmetric part of R, which is the part the
   dissipation term uses.
   DESIGN, with the reason: `rational-ldlt` is "symmetric with positive pivots", strict and with no pivoting
   (BD-027 and the kind registry; the reflector `ldlt-pos` and card T-19 rest on that one meaning), so a
   semidefinite R, whose own elimination meets a zero pivot, is never offered to it whole. The factorization
   identity carries the semidefinite case and the kind keeps its single acceptance condition. Rejected: a
   second, semidefinite acceptance condition inside `rational-ldlt` (a revision of an M0 kind, and a
   certificate that the `ldlt-pos` reflector could not promote).
2. Recipe for a composite: J_c = blockdiag(Jᵢ) + G_int·W·G_intᵀ and R_c = blockdiag(Rᵢ); the same bundles on
   (J_c, R_c), plus a `poly-identity` bundle for yᵀ·W·y = 0 in free symbols y (W + Wᵀ = 0). Lossless two-ports
   (gyrator, transformer, coupling matrix) contribute to W; their identities are those of T-15.
3. Checker: `frob-verify` offline; at build time the proof kernel of the symbolic sibling for parametric
   entries.
4. The general composition theorem is not a route of this card. If the Lemma lead later wants it, the
   decision's standing rule applies: the statement, a route and a consumer are filed, the admission test is
   run, and the outcome is a dated amendment with its own row.
5. Specimen (a motor axis; exact decimals): torque constant k = 1/10 N·m/A, winding resistance 1/2 Ω, viscous
   friction 1/1000 N·m·s/rad; J = [[0, −k], [k, 0]], R = diag(1/2, 1/1000). At ∇H = (i, ω) = (2 A, 50 rad/s):
   ∇Hᵀ·J·∇H = 0; dissipation 0.5 × 4 + 0.001 × 2,500 = 4.5 W; with 10 V applied and a load torque τ_load =
   0.1 N·m opposing the motion (in the motor-axis convention J_r·ω̇ = k·i − b·ω − τ_load, so the port input is
   u = (V, −τ_load) = (10 V, −0.1 N·m) against y = (i, ω)) the port power is yᵀ·u = 20 − 5 = 15 W and
   Ḣ = 10.5 W ≤ 15 W. Cross-check from the equations of motion with L = 1 mH and rotor inertia 10⁻⁴ kg·m²:
   L·di/dt = 10 − 1 − 5 = 4 V, so 2 A × 4 V = 8 W; J_r·dω/dt = 0.2 − 0.05 − 0.1 = 0.05 N·m, so 50 × 0.05 = 2.5 W;
   total 10.5 W.
6. Registry row (`registries/theorems.toml`, written by the decision as `hypothesis`; this bead supplies its
   content, in the row schema of N12-06): the number the decision assigns; `source = "PO-SINEW-01"`; tier [S];
   one route of kind `poly-identity`, class `Certified` (an instance's receipt is the set of its bundles, the
   `rational-ldlt` bundle of its pivot block among them); `relies_on = ["T-15", "T-29"]`; consumers:
   `actuator.check`, the module-level certificate "passivity of every element", the energy ledger; constructions
   C-38, C-12.
7. Artifacts: the document; the row's content; under `crates/frob-cert/tests/specimens/`,
   `poly-identity/accept-T-38-J.frobcert` (the skew form of the specimen's J),
   `poly-identity/accept-T-38-R.frobcert` (the factorization identity of R = diag(1/2, 1/1000): d = (1/2, 1/1000),
   ℓ₁ = (1, 0), ℓ₂ = (0, 1)), `rational-ldlt/accept-T-38-D.frobcert` (its pivot block) and the mutants below as
   `reject-<rule>-T-38-*.frobcert`, each file with its row in the directory's `expected.toml` (verdict, first
   failing rule, refusal code and `card = "T-38"`: the corpus layout that the directory-driven scripts of BD-132
   and N12-34 read, which fail on a file the manifest does not list).

      skew form leaves (11/100 − 1/10)·e₁·e₂, that is 0.01 × 2 × 50 = 1 W created at the specimen's point); a
      dissipation matrix with one negative entry (−1/1000: its pivot block fails `rational-ldlt`); the indefinite
      matrix [[0, 1], [1, 1]] presented with the factor of its lower-right entry alone (d = (1), ℓ = (0, 1): a
      zero pivot with a nonzero remaining row; the factorization identity fails by the term 2·e₁·e₂); a junction
      W with one sign flipped.

      eᵀ·R·e = (e₁ − e₂)²) and rejected when offered whole to `rational-ldlt` (its second pivot is 0); R = 0 is
      accepted on its identity alone.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-lemma/tests/cards/po_sinew_01.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-lemma --test cards -- po_sinew_01`.
- `skew_form_vanishes`: eᵀ·J·e = 0 for the specimen J at 10³ rational e.
- `semidefinite_by_reduced_factor`: diag(1/2, 1/1000) (r = 2); the rank-deficient thermal conductance
  [[1, −1], [−1, 1]] (r = 1, d = (1), ℓ = (1, −1)); R = 0 (r = 0, the identity alone): each recipe is accepted.
- `whole_singular_matrix_rejected_by_ldlt`: [[1, −1], [−1, 1]] offered whole to `rational-ldlt` is rejected at
  its second pivot, which is why the factored form is the recipe.
- `pivot_block_matches_identity`: the diagonal of the `rational-ldlt` bundle is the list of d_k of its identity
  bundle, in order; a pair in which they differ fails the card's check.
- `composite_blockdiag`: two motor axes on one bus node; J_c + J_cᵀ = 0 and R_c ⪰ 0 by the recipe.
- `specimen_numbers`: 4.5 W, 15 W, 10.5 W, and the cross-check 8 W + 2.5 W.
- `unequal_gyrator_rejects`, `negative_conductance_rejects`, `zero_pivot_nonzero_row_rejects`,
  `flipped_junction_rejects`.
- `registry_row_matches_decision`: the row's number, tier and single route equal the decision's record; a

## Considerations

- The identity says nothing about how much is dissipated, only the sign. Loss values are `Estimated` card
  fields.
- A state-dependent R must be semidefinite for every admitted state; for a rational function of temperature
  the certificate covers the declared temperature range by a sign proof of each pivot, or the card is checked
  at its range ends when the pivot is monotone (stated in the bundle).
- The general theorem is deliberately not a card: every consumer holds a concrete design, and an instance
  certificate is what the auditor can re-check.
- The card for PO-SHIELD-04 (bead N12x-04) relies on this one for J skew and R ⪰ 0 of a design's port model.
