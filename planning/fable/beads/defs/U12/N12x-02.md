## Background

A clearance complex answers "can this body get from here to there in this place" from both sides. It is a
cubical subdivision of the planar configuration space (x, y, ψ) of a footprint F₀ in one height slab of a scene.
The clearance c(q) of a configuration q is the distance from F₀ placed at q to the nearest obstacle, negative
inside an obstacle. Each cell is a closed box with centre q₀, translation edge Δ and heading width δ, and stores
two one-sided values c_lo = c(q₀) − e and c_hi = c(q₀) + e, with

  e = (Δ/2)·√2 + ρ·(δ/2) + ε_field + u_reg,

where ρ is the footprint's reference radius (the largest distance of a footprint point from its reference
point), ε_field the bound on the error of the obstacle geometry as evaluated, and u_reg the registration
uncertainty of an ingested scene. Cells with c_lo above a margin w form the inner complex (every configuration
in them is free) and cells with c_hi above w the outer complex (every free configuration lies in one of them).
Both verdicts, the route witness and the impossibility cut, are sound only if e really bounds the variation of
clearance over a cell. That is proof obligation PO-HAB-02: "over a cell of a clearance complex, clearance varies
from its centre value by at most e", with the evidence "a written proof from the two Lipschitz facts, as a
theorem card; a randomized check". The scene beads keep the written proof and the randomized check and report
the card as pending, because no card T-01 to T-35 states it. Card T-31 covers only the step after it (a verdict
follows from the stored complexes).

**Admission ruling.** A card is admitted only if a checker can decide it. The obligation has two layers.
- The *general lemma* (for every closed obstacle set and every q in the cell, |c(q) − c(q₀)| ≤ (Δ/2)·√2 +
  ρ·(δ/2)) is a statement of metric geometry, not about a finite algebraic object. No kind of the closed registry
  decides it. It is carried as a written proof and as two **named assumptions** of any bundle that relies on it,
  the same treatment card T-34 gives Smale's theorem; a Lean route is registered and is `parked` until the
  closure question for the needed modules is answered.
- The *instance statement* is decidable: for a stored complex, the error term recorded in its header dominates
  the formula for the stored Δ, δ, ρ, ε_field and u_reg, and every cell's c_lo and c_hi are the outward
  millimetre roundings of its recorded centre value minus and plus that term. This is exact rational arithmetic
  (√2 is handled by squaring), checked as a typed side statement of a `complex-connectivity` bundle.
Ruling: **admitted** as card **T-37**, class `Certified` for the instance statement with the two named
assumptions printed beside it. A bundle that hides the assumptions is rejected. Rejected alternatives: admitting
the general lemma as `Certified` on the strength of the randomized check (a test is not a proof); leaving the
arithmetic unchecked (then a header with e understated by a factor of two would pass the connectivity checker).

Tier: Solid for the instance route; Frontier for the parked Lean route.

## Technical approach

1. **Statement (T-37).** Let c be the signed distance from the placed footprint to a closed obstacle set, q₀ the
   centre of a cell with translation edge Δ and heading width δ, and ρ the reference radius. Then for every q in
   the cell |c(q) − c(q₀)| ≤ (Δ/2)·√2 + ρ·(δ/2). With a field that is evaluated to within ε_field and a
   registration known to within u_reg, the recorded centre value c̃(q₀) satisfies
   c̃(q₀) − e ≤ c(q) ≤ c̃(q₀) + e for every q in the cell.
2. **Written proof.** (A1) A distance function to a set has Lipschitz constant one, and so has the distance
   between a moving compact set and a fixed set as a function of the largest displacement of the moving set's
   points. (A2) Under a rotation by Δψ about the reference point a point at radius r ≤ ρ moves by the chord
   2·r·sin(|Δψ|/2) ≤ ρ·|Δψ|. From q₀ to q every footprint point moves by at most the translation
   ‖(Δx, Δy)‖ ≤ √((Δ/2)² + (Δ/2)²) = (Δ/2)·√2 plus the rotation term ρ·(δ/2); by (A1) the clearance changes by at
   most that. The sign convention inside obstacles does not matter, because the signed distance is also
   1-Lipschitz. The bound is not tight (the chord is shorter than the arc); it is safe.
3. **Instance rule `clear.cell-bound`** (exact rationals; lengths in millimetres as integers or reduced
   fractions; δ as a rational multiple of π is not used: the header stores δ in radians as a rational upper
   bound δ_ub ≥ δ, for 32 headings δ = 2π/32 and δ_ub = 19635/100000). The checker verifies:
   (i) t = e_hdr − ρ·δ_ub/2 − ε_field − u_reg ≥ 0 and 2·t² ≥ Δ² (which is t ≥ (Δ/2)·√2 without a square root);
   (ii) for every cell, c_lo ≤ ⌊c̃(q₀) − e_hdr⌋ and c_hi ≥ ⌈c̃(q₀) + e_hdr⌉ in millimetres;
   (iii) the bundle's `assumptions` list contains `distance-1-lipschitz` and `rotation-chord-le-arc` with
   polarity "survives: none" (they are mathematical, not about the scene);
   (iv) ρ in the header is at least the largest vertex radius of the footprint polygon recorded in the bundle
   (exact comparison of squared lengths).
4. **Worked numbers** (the default lattice): Δ = 50 mm, 32 headings, ρ = 400 mm: (Δ/2)·√2 = 35.36 mm and
   ρ·δ/2 = 400 × 0.098175 = 39.27 mm, sum 74.6 mm before field and registration error. Refined lattices:
   Δ = 12.5 mm with 128 headings gives 8.84 + 9.82 = 18.7 mm; Δ = 5 mm with 512 headings gives 3.54 + 2.45 =
   6.0 mm. A doorway fit whose total margin is under 2·e is `unresolved` at that lattice (149 mm at the default).
5. **Registry row.** id T-37; source PO-HAB-02; routes: { kind `complex-connectivity` (side statement
   `clear.cell-bound`), class `Certified`, named assumptions A1 and A2 } and { kind `lean-capsule`, class
   `Proved`, status `parked`, unblock condition: the closure of the metric lemmas avoids the blocked analysis
   modules }; constructions C-33; consumers `.fclear`, `scene.fit`, the clearance analysis.
6. **Artifacts.** The document; the row; `specimens/complex-connectivity/accept-T-37.frobcert` (a 4 × 4 × 8
   lattice around one rectangular obstacle) and its mutants.

## Considerations

- The card certifies the arithmetic of a stored complex, not the obstacle geometry: ε_field and u_reg are inputs
  with their own provenance, and a scan with a large u_reg correctly yields `unresolved` and a measurement
  request.
- The heading term uses ρ, the worst point of the footprint. A long body pays for its length in every cell;
  local refinement in ψ is the remedy, not a smaller ρ.
- If the Lean route is ever sealed, the named assumptions are replaced by the capsule hash in new bundles; old
  bundles stay valid as issued.
