## Background

A scene imported from outside (a scan, a mesh from another tool) must be registered to the workspace's frame,
and the import is refused unless its transform class is established: a proper rigid motion with a uniform
scale, not a reflection and not a general affine map (refusal FROB-REF-302). Three labelled landmarks do not
establish it. With A = (0, 0, 0), B = (1, 0, 0), C = (0, 1, 0), the reflection (x, y, z) ↦ (x, y, −z) fixes all
three, so a mirrored room registers with zero residual; a fourth landmark D = (0, 0, 1) maps to (0, 0, −1) and
shows a residual of 2 m. The registration bead (N19a-21) therefore requires four non-coplanar correspondences,
solves L = T·S⁻¹ from the difference matrices, and tests LᵀL = c²·I and det L > 0. Its soundness rests on proof
obligation PO-HAB-07: "an affine map that fixes four non-coplanar points is the identity, so four
correspondences determine the registration", evidence "linear-algebra card". No card T-01 to T-35 states it; the
scene beads report "card pending".

How candidates become cards. The proof chapter's decision bead (N12-52) took nine candidate cards of other
chapters through the admission rule and numbers the admitted ones from T-36. PO-HAB-07 is not among its nine
(its sibling PO-HAB-02, the Lipschitz bound on clearance over a cell, is: the decision files it with the
obligations that no checker decides, because it is an analytic statement with no finite witness, and it gets
no card). For a candidate raised later the decision sets a standing rule: the chapter files the statement, the
proposed route and the consumer with the Lemma lead; the admission test is applied; the outcome is a dated
amendment of the decision's record and a registry row; and no chapter prints a card number that the registry
does not hold. This bead is that filing for PO-HAB-07, and, once the amendment admits it, its card bead. The
card number is the next free one at the amendment (T-42 if the decision's recommended dispositions stand);
until then the files below use the name `po-hab-07`.

**The admission test, applied.** The statement is about a finite algebraic object and reduces to one polynomial identity.
Write f(x) = L·x + d, the four points p₀..p₃, and P = [p₁ − p₀, p₂ − p₀, p₃ − p₀] (3 × 3). If f fixes the four
points then (L − I)·P = 0. For every 3 × 3 matrix, P·adj(P) = det(P)·I, hence

  det(P)·(L − I) = ((L − I)·P)·adj(P),

an identity between polynomials in the nine entries of L and the nine entries of P. Its right side vanishes
under the hypothesis, and det(P) ≠ 0 is exactly non-coplanarity, so L = I, and then d = p₀ − L·p₀ = 0. The
identity is of proof kind `poly-identity`, which is in the closed registry with a checker from M0; the last step
is division by a non-zero element of a field. So the candidate passes the test, and the recommended
disposition filed with the Lemma lead is: **admit one card**, tier Solid, with one route, `poly-identity`: the
general statement by the symbolic identity, class `Certified`, and an instance (four given rational landmarks)
by the same identity with numbers and the exact inequality det(P) ≠ 0. No Lean route is proposed: the symbolic
identity already covers every instance. Fail-closed default until the amendment exists: PO-HAB-07 is a written
proof with a property test, the scene beads keep reporting "card pending", and nothing presents it as
`Certified`. Rejected alternative: a property test over random quadruples as the only evidence (it is kept, as
a control, in N19a-21).

Corollary used by the importer: two affine maps that agree on four non-coplanar points are equal (apply the
card to g⁻¹∘f when g is invertible, or directly to the difference: (L_f − L_g)·P = 0 gives L_f = L_g and then
d_f = d_g). So the registration computed from four correspondences is the only affine map consistent with
them, and its class (rotation with uniform scale, or not) is a property of the data and not of the solver.

## Technical approach

1. **Symbolic certificate (general statement).** A `poly-identity` bundle with left side det(P)·(L − I) and
   right side ((L − I)·P)·adj(P), entry by entry: nine identities in eighteen indeterminates, each of degree
   four. The auditor expands both sides to normal form over ℚ and compares.
2. **Second identity for the translation.** With L = I, f(p₀) = p₀ gives d = 0; as an identity:
   d = (f(p₀) − p₀) − (L − I)·p₀. A `poly-identity` bundle in the entries of L, d and p₀.
3. **Instance recipe (per import).** The bundle records the four source landmarks as exact rationals (decimal
   inputs are exact decimals), P, and det(P) as a reduced rational with the side statement det(P) ≠ 0.
   The importer's own non-degeneracy rule (a tetrahedron volume |det(P)|/6 of at least 1% of the cube of the
   largest separation between landmarks) is checked by the importer; the card's instance statement needs only
   det(P) ≠ 0.
4. **Corollary certificate.** The same identity with L replaced by L_f − L_g + I.
5. **Filing.** One page for the Lemma lead under the standing rule: the statement; the route (`poly-identity`,
   general and instance); the consumer (scene import and registration, refusal FROB-REF-302, gate
   GATE-HAB-04); the admission test as applied above; the request for a dated amendment of the decision's
   record. The specimens of item 8 are attached so that the test can be re-run.
6. **Registry row** (written when the amendment admits it; before that, no row and no number): the number
   the amendment assigns; source PO-HAB-07; tier [S]; one route, `poly-identity`, class `Certified`, status
   `hypothesis` until the first receipt; consumers: scene import and registration. If the amendment does not
   admit it, the obligation is entered in the registry of obligations that no checker decides, with the
   property test as its substitute, and the specimens stay as tests.
7. **Specimen numbers.** Points (0, 0, 0), (1, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0), (0, 0, 1): P = I, det = 1, volume 1/6. The
   reflection (x, y, z) ↦ (x, y, −z) has (L − I)·P = diag(0, 0, −2) ≠ 0: it does not fix the fourth point
   (residual 2 m), as the card requires. With the fourth point replaced by the coplanar (1, 1, 0), det(P) = 0
   and the hypothesis of the card fails: the reflection fixes all four.
8. **Artifacts.** The filing; the document; the row's content;
   `specimens/poly-identity/accept-po-hab-07.frobcert`, `accept-po-hab-07-translation.frobcert`, the instance
   specimen and the mutants (renamed to the card number at admission).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The filing is on record with the Lemma lead and the amendment of N12-52's record states the outcome;
      before it, `frob prove list --json` shows no row for PO-HAB-07 and no document prints a card number
      for it.
- [ ] `frob-verify` accepts the two symbolic bundles and the instance specimen.
- [ ] Rejected mutants: the identity with adj(P) replaced by its transpose (the cofactor matrix); the identity
      with det(P) replaced by the product of the diagonal of P; an instance bundle with four coplanar points
      (det(P) = 0) presented with the side statement det(P) ≠ 0; an instance bundle whose landmark order
      differs from the typed order in its definitions.
- [ ] After admission, `frob prove list --json` shows the card under the number the registry holds, with
      source PO-HAB-07 and its one route; the registry's admission check passes.
- [ ] The document states what the card does not say: it does not say the registration is rigid (that is the
      test LᵀL = c²·I with det L > 0), and it does not bound the effect of landmark noise.
- [ ] N19a-21's line "PO-HAB-07 card is on file with its symbolic check" can cite the registry number once it exists; N19c-27's audit
      replaces "card pending".

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-lemma/tests/cards/po_hab_07.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-lemma po_hab_07`.
- `adjugate_identity_expands`: P·adj(P) − det(P)·I = 0 in nine symbols.
- `main_identity_expands`: the nine degree-four identities.
- `unit_tetrahedron_instance`: det = 1; the identity map is the only fixed affine map (solve returns L = I,
  d = 0 exactly).
- `reflection_moves_fourth_point`: residual exactly 2 on D = (0, 0, 1) (KA-429).
- `coplanar_quadruple_fails_hypothesis`: det = 0 for (0,0,0), (1,0,0), (0,1,0), (1,1,0); the reflection fixes
  all four (CX-184's point: three, or four coplanar, do not suffice).
- `vertical_plane_gravity_case`: landmarks in the plane y = 0 plus a gravity direction (0, 0, −1): the
  reflection through that plane preserves all of them (CX-185); a fourth non-coplanar point breaks it.
- `two_maps_agree_then_equal`: property test over random rational affine maps and non-coplanar quadruples.
- `cofactor_mutant_rejects`, `diagonal_det_mutant_rejects`.

## Considerations

- Non-coplanarity in exact arithmetic is det(P) ≠ 0; with measured landmarks it must hold with margin, which is
  why the importer demands a tetrahedron volume of at least 1% of the cube of the largest separation (TARGET)
  before it trusts L. That rule belongs to the importer and its uncertainty model, not to this card.
- Symbolic expansion of nine degree-four identities in eighteen indeterminates is small (a few thousand terms);
  if the proof kernel's cap is hit, the identity is certified column by column.
