## Background

When a runtime shield's check fails, the controller falls back to a backup with a declared domain. For wheeled
and tracked bases the first backup is **damping injection** on the drive outputs: the command is u = −K_d·y,
with y the collocated outputs (the velocities conjugate to the actuated inputs) and K_d positive semidefinite.
In the port model of a design (each component with a stored energy H, a skew-symmetric structure matrix J, a
positive semidefinite dissipation matrix R, and ports with inputs u and outputs y = Gᵀ·∇H, so that
ẋ = (J − R)·∇H + G·u) this command can only remove energy in continuous time: dH/dt ≤ −yᵀ·K_d·y ≤ 0.

Proof obligation PO-SHIELD-04 of the control chapter has two parts: "under damping injection the port model's
stored energy is non-increasing: in continuous time for every K_d ⪰ 0, and at the ticks of a sampled, held
command when 2·M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0 (with actuation latency, when the exact one-tick map of analysis A-22 contracts in
the energy norm)", with the evidence "a polynomial identity for Lemma (a theorem card) for the continuous
statement; an exact rational check of the gain bound per fitted backup; the ledger's monotonicity on replay".
The gate GATE-SHIELD-07 repeats: "the continuous-time identity remains a theorem card". The sampled bound has
its mechanism bead (N22c-44), the backups theirs (N22b-24), the invariant battery its own (N22c-03); each
notes that no numbered card carries the identity.

The proof chapter's decision on candidate cards (N12-52) recommends: "admit one card, tier Frontier like its
mechanism: the identity as its general part, the gain bound as instance certificates. With latency the claim
stays `Tested`." It opens a card bead for each admitted candidate. This is that card bead; it carries out the
ruling and does not make it.

**Number.** Candidate cards are numbered by N12-52, append-only from T-36 in the order of its table; under its
recommended dispositions this card is **T-39**. The registry's number governs if the decision closes the
numbering up.

**The two parts as checkable statements.**
- General part (kind `poly-identity`, symbolic):
  ∇Hᵀ·[(J − R) − G·K_d·Gᵀ]·∇H = −∇Hᵀ·R·∇H − (Gᵀ·∇H)ᵀ·K_d·(Gᵀ·∇H) whenever J + Jᵀ = 0.
  With R ⪰ 0 and sym(K_d) ⪰ 0 this gives dH/dt ≤ −yᵀ·K_d·y ≤ 0.
- Instance part (kind `rational-ldlt`, per fitted backup): sym(K_d) ⪰ 0, and for the sampled, held command
  with no latency and no other force during the hold, 2·M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0, with M̄ the reflected inertia at the
  collocated outputs (its least value over the design's mass ranges) and h the control period. One tick maps
  the output rate by I − h·M̄⁻¹·K_d and changes the stored energy by −(h/2)·yᵀ·(2·K_d − h·K_d·M̄⁻¹·K_d)·y, which is
  non-positive exactly under that bound.

Why the sampled part cannot be skipped. A free 1 kg slider at +1 m/s under a sampled damper of 300 N·s/m that
holds −300 N for 10 ms ends at −2 m/s; its kinetic energy rises from 0.5 J to 2 J; the damper has done +1.5 J
of work, although the continuous identity holds for that gain. The bound 2·M̄ − h·K_d = 2 − 3 < 0 says so;
100 N·s/m gives 2 − 1 ≥ 0 and takes the speed from 1 m/s to 0.

## Technical approach

1. **General part, proof.** dH/dt = ∇Hᵀ·ẋ = ∇Hᵀ·(J − R)·∇H + ∇Hᵀ·G·u. The J term vanishes because
   ∇Hᵀ·J·∇H = ½·∇Hᵀ·(J + Jᵀ)·∇H. Substituting u = −K_d·y + u_ext gives −yᵀ·K_d·y + yᵀ·u_ext, and
   yᵀ·K_d·y = yᵀ·sym(K_d)·y ≥ 0.
2. **Symbolic certificate.** A `poly-identity` bundle in the entries of ∇H (n symbols), the strictly upper
   entries of J (the skew structure built in), the entries of R, G and K_d. Certified for the sizes the
   backups use (n ≤ 6 states per drive group, m ≤ 4 outputs), one bundle per (n, m); beyond that the identity
   is certified per instance.
3. **Instance certificates (a fitted backup).** The bundle carries the design's port-model matrices at the
   fitted operating set as rationals (from card values), and:
   (i) J + Jᵀ = 0 and R ⪰ 0 by reference to the design's certificates under the card for PO-SINEW-01
   (bead N12x-01);
   (ii) a `rational-ldlt` certificate for sym(K_d), semidefinite acceptance condition;
   (iii) the collocation check G_u = G_y (the outputs the backup reads are the conjugates of the inputs it
   drives) as a `poly-identity` on the two matrices; a backup whose outputs are not collocated is outside the
   card and its bundle is rejected;
   (iv) for a backup whose card says `damper: sampled`, a `rational-ldlt` certificate for 2·M̄ − h·K_d with
   the side conditions `latency: none` and `no other force during the hold` typed in the bundle; a backup
   with one tick of latency or more has no such certificate, and its claim is `Tested`.
4. **Registry row** (content for the row the decision writes): the number the decision assigns; source
   PO-SHIELD-04; tier [F]; routes `poly-identity` (general part) and `rational-ldlt` (instance part); relies
   on the card for PO-SINEW-01; consumers: the damping-injection backup, `shield.fit`, `shield.check`;
   constructions C-12, C-31.
5. **Specimens** (exact decimals). Continuous: a 1 kg slider with viscous friction 2 N·s/m and
   K_d = 300 N·s/m at v = 1 m/s: H = 0.5 J; dH/dt = −2 − 300 = −302 W ≤ −300 W. Sampled, h = 10 ms, M̄ = 1 kg:
   K_d = 100 gives 2·M̄ − h·K_d = 1 (accepted); K_d = 200 gives 0 (accepted: semidefinite; the speed is exactly
   reversed, +1 m/s to −1 m/s, and the energy is unchanged); K_d = 300 gives −1 (rejected; +1.5 J).
6. **Artifacts.** The document; `specimens/poly-identity/accept-T-39-n2m1.frobcert` and sizes up to (6, 4);
   `specimens/rational-ldlt/accept-T-39-Kd.frobcert`, `accept-T-39-gain-100.frobcert`,
   `accept-T-39-gain-200.frobcert`; mutants.

## Considerations

- The identity is easy; its value is that the backup's safety argument has a checked link instead of a
  sentence, and that the card states in one place where the continuous argument stops and the sampled bound
  begins.
- Holding on a slope needs the brake's holding torque; damping injection brings the rate to zero and does not
  hold a position. The backup's declared domain says so; the card does not.
- The reflected inertia in the bound is the least over the design's mass ranges; a payload removed after the
  fit lowers it, which is why the bundle binds the mass range it used.
- The symbolic bundle grows with (n, m); sizes above (6, 4) are certified per instance to stay inside the
  proof kernel's cap.
