## Background
A simulator advances a pose by g₊ = g·τ(h·ξ), where τ maps the Lie algebra to the group (a retraction), ξ is the body twist and h the step. Two retractions are supported everywhere: the exponential, and the Cayley map cay(x) = (I − x/2)⁻¹·(I + x/2). The Cayley map is rational, lands exactly on SO(3) and SE(3) in exact arithmetic, and uses only +, −, ×, ÷ and √, which IEEE 754 rounds correctly; that makes it a candidate for bit-identical execution on CPU, CUDA and Metal, and an object a polynomial-identity checker can certify (theorem cards T-01, T-02, T-03).

ADR-MARROW-02: Cayley on the batched tiers, exponential on the referee; both exist on every tier and the choice is part of `ModelId`. Reason: the Cayley step costs second-order phase error (it rotates by 2·atan(‖ω‖h/2) where the exponential rotates by ‖ω‖h; relative shortfall (‖ω‖h)²/12) in exchange for no transcendental call in a batched kernel; the referee can afford deterministic elementary functions. Rejected: adding a quaternion increment and renormalizing (an order-h² projection every step instead of a round-off-level one); Runge–Kutta on Euler angles (chart singularities).

ADR-MARROW-05: there are two coadjoints, each with its own name, type and sign. The group coadjoint Ad*_g = (Ad_g)⁻ᵀ moves wrenches between frames. The infinitesimal coadjoint ad*_ξ = (ad_ξ)ᵀ appears in the Euler–Poincaré equation μ̇ = ad*_ξ μ + w. Reason: an earlier wording called ad* "the negative transpose", which is true only of the generator of the group coadjoint, d/dt Ad*_{exp(tξ)} at 0 = −ad*_ξ; read that way a free body would obey μ̇ = −(ad_ξ)ᵀ μ, the wrong sign. The danger is specific: **energy conservation does not detect the error** (worked case below), so a suite that checks energy alone passes a simulator with this defect.

This bead implements the retractions and their differentials for SO(3) and SE(3), their refusals, and the laws that close the M0 rows of GATE-MARROW-01. Tier: Solid.

## Technical approach
Notation: x = (ω, v) ∈ 𝔰𝔢(3), θ = ‖ω‖, ω̂ the cross-product matrix, coefficient functions A = sin θ/θ, B = (1 − cos θ)/θ², C = (θ − sin θ)/θ³.

**Exponential.** SO(3): R = I + A·ω̂ + B·ω̂²; as a quaternion (cos(θ/2), (sin(θ/2)/θ)·ω). SE(3): exp(ω, v) = (exp ω̂, V·v) with V = I + B·ω̂ + C·ω̂². The `f64` host path calls `fs_ga` (`Se3::exp`, FACT: `fs-ga/src/lie.rs:766–776`), so it inherits `fs_math::det` and bit parity; the generic reference path implements the same formulas.
**Logarithm.** SO(3): from the unit quaternion (w, u), flipped if w < 0, θ = 2·atan2(‖u‖, w) and ω = θ·u/‖u‖ (ω = 2u for ‖u‖ below the small-angle threshold). SE(3): v = V⁻¹·p with V⁻¹ = I − ½·ω̂ + D·ω̂², D = 1/θ² − (1 + cos θ)/(2θ·sin θ).
**Cayley.** SO(3): the quaternion (1, ω/2)/√(1 + θ²/4); equivalently R = I + 4/(4 + θ²)·(ω̂ + ω̂²/2). SE(3): cay(ω, v) = (cay ω̂, (I − ω̂/2)⁻¹·v) with (I − ω̂/2)⁻¹ = (4I + 2ω̂ + ω·ωᵀ)/(4 + θ²). Inverse: ω = 2u/w from the unit quaternion, v = (I − ω̂/2)·p.
**Small angles (exponential lane, referee only).** A, B, C, D and the half-angle coefficient are evaluated by even polynomials in θ² below a threshold and by deterministic elementary functions above it, the two arms joined by a select (DESIGN: this removes the 0/0 forms without a branch). Degree and threshold are chosen so the truncation error is below half an ulp, and both are recorded in the kernel manifest. Initial values (DESIGN, confirmed by the derivation): `f64`, θ² < 2⁻⁶ with four terms in θ² (for A: 1 − θ²/6 + θ⁴/120 − θ⁶/5040 + θ⁸/362880, next term below 2.4 × 10⁻¹⁷); `f32`, θ² < 2⁻⁶ with two terms. The Cayley lane needs no such rule.
**Chart cuts.** `log`, `cay_inv` and the inverse differentials are singular at rotation by π (w = 0). A request within the stated margin of the cut returns FROB-REF-213 with the element, the margin and the charts that cover it, unless the caller supplies a second chart; no value is returned from the wrong side of a cut. Charts (DESIGN): chart_b(g) = log(b⁻¹·g) for b in {identity, the rotations by π about e_x, e_y, e_z}; the chart whose base corresponds to the largest-magnitude quaternion component has rotation angle at most 2π/3, so the four cover SO(3). Margins (DESIGN, set by measurement at the gate): 2⁻²⁰ rad for `log` and `cay_inv`, 2⁻¹⁰ rad for inverse differentials, recorded in the manifest.
**Cayley admission.** `cay_step(g, ξ, h)` refuses with FROB-REF-212 when ‖ω‖·h > 0.2 in one substep, tested without a square root as (ω·ω)·h² ≤ 0.04; the refusal returns the substep count that admits the step, ⌈‖ω‖·h/0.2⌉. At the bound the angle error is 0.33%; at ω = 20 rad/s, h = 2 ms it is 1.33 × 10⁻⁴ (0.0027 rad/s of lag). The bare map `cay(x)` is total and is what kernels and certificates use.
**Differentials.** Convention: left-trivialized, τ(x)⁻¹·Dτ(x)[δx] = dτ_x·δx, which matches right perturbations g·exp(δ); the right-trivialized form is dτ_{−x}.
- SO(3): dexp_ω = I − B·ω̂ + C·ω̂², inverse I + ½·ω̂ + D·ω̂²; dcay_ω = 2/(4 + θ²)·(2I − ω̂), inverse I + ½·ω̂ + ¼·ω·ωᵀ.
- SE(3) Cayley: (dcay_x)⁻¹ = [[I + ½·ω̂ + ¼·ω·ωᵀ, 0], [½·v̂ + ¼·ω̂·v̂, I + ½·ω̂]] in (angular, linear) block order; dcay_x is its inverse.
- SE(3) exponential: dexp_x = Σ_{n ≥ 0} (−1)ⁿ·Xⁿ/(n + 1)! with X = ad_x. Closed form (DESIGN; the Jacobian law is its arbiter): dexp_x = I − a₁·X + a₂·X² − a₃·X³ + a₄·X⁴, a₁ = (4 − θ·sin θ − 4·cos θ)/(2θ²), a₂ = (4θ − 5·sin θ + θ·cos θ)/(2θ³), a₃ = (2 − θ·sin θ − 2·cos θ)/(2θ⁴), a₄ = (2θ − 3·sin θ + θ·cos θ)/(2θ⁵), with limits ½, 1/6, 1/24, 1/120 as θ → 0 and the small-angle rule above. For a pure translation X² = 0 and the two differentials are exactly I − X/2 and I + X/2. `frob-lie` owns these in closed form because `fs_ga::Se3::left_jacobian` refuses when ‖ad‖_∞ > 32 (FACT), so a pure translation of 33 m, a regular pose, returns `IllConditioned` there; here it must return a value, and a genuine limit of the implementation is reported as `unsupported`, never as a singular pose.

**Refusals** (both exit code 3, family 2xx):
| Code | Trigger | Returned | Ranked repairs |
|---|---|---|---|
| FROB-REF-212 | ‖ω‖·h > 0.2 in one Cayley substep | The substep count that admits it | 1. substep by that count; 2. use the exponential on a tier that has it |
| FROB-REF-213 | `log`, `cay_inv` or an inverse differential inside the margin of a chart cut, no second chart supplied | The element, the margin, the covering charts | 1. supply the covering chart named in the reply; 2. reformulate with the group element instead of its logarithm |

**The coadjoint sign, worked.** Free body, rotational inertia diag(1, 2, 3) kg·m², ω = (1, 1, 0) rad/s, so π = (1, 2, 0). Correct law: π̇ = π × ω = (0, 0, −1). Reversed sign: (0, 0, +1). Both give ω·π̇ = 0, so kinetic energy is conserved either way. Only the correct one conserves spatial angular momentum: the body-frame rate π̇ + ω × π is (0, 0, 0) with the correct sign and (0, 0, 2) with the wrong one. Second check: with the origin at the centre of mass, π = I·ω and ℓ = m·v give v̇ = v × ω, so d(R·v)/dt = R·(ω × v + v̇) = 0; with the opposite sign it is 2R·(ω × v), a free body changing velocity with no force.

**Determinism and evidence.** Host `f64`, class D2 per platform, deterministic elementary functions on the exponential lane. Law results are tier G0; the gate is a Known answer claim. The certificates for T-01 (cay(Ω)ᵀ·cay(Ω) = I, det = 1), T-03 (‖q·c‖² = ‖q‖²·‖c‖²) and T-04 (Ad(gh) = Ad(g)·Ad(h); the group coadjoint is the inverse transpose) come from BD-021 and are cited, not produced, here.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Laws for SO(3) and SE(3), each on 10⁶ cases through the harness, at most 64 ulp in `f64` and 16 ulp in `f32` for single operations (TARGET): group axioms; retraction (τ(0) = e, dτ(0) = I, τ(x)·τ(−x) = e, for cay and exp); round trip (log(exp x) = x inside the injectivity radius, cay⁻¹(cay x) = x); adjoint (Ad(gh) = Ad(g)·Ad(h); Ad* = (Ad)⁻ᵀ; Ad(exp x) = exp(ad x) to a tolerance recorded with the gate, initial TARGET 1,024 ulp because the series reference accumulates error); coadjoint pair; pairing; free body; Jacobians.
- [ ] GATE-MARROW-01, M0 rows, recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with measured worst residuals, case counts, seed, commit and fingerprint; the record cites the T-01, T-03, T-04 certificates (GATE-LEMMA-00) and the OPEN-MARROW-1 ADR.
- [ ] A planted reversed ad* fails the free-body known answer π̇ = (0, 0, −1) and at least two further fixtures (coadjoint-pair law; free-body law), and passes an energy-only check (asserted, to document why energy is not the test).
- [ ] `cay_step` at ‖ω‖·h = 0.25 returns FROB-REF-212 with substep count 2, and at 0.1875 it succeeds; at the boundary the outcome is the exact sign of (ω·ω)·h² − 0.04 on the stored operands (the stored constant nearest 0.04), never a rounded comparison (guarded form in N13-11).
- [ ] `log` of a rotation by π − 2⁻²¹ rad returns FROB-REF-213 naming a covering chart; with that chart the round trip is within tolerance.
- [ ] dexp at a pure translation of 33 m with unit rotation rate about +z returns linear y components −16.5 m and +16.5 m in the two trivializations; no refusal.
- [ ] Group-membership residual of repeated `cay` steps stays within 4 ulp over 10⁷ steps with the per-step normalization on, and grows no faster than a random walk with it off.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` lists the two refusals, the margins, thresholds and the validity domain (‖ω‖·h ≤ 0.2 for stepping).

## Test plan
### Unit tests
- `free_body_coad_known_answer` (KA-229): values above; the mutant gives (0, 0, +1) and spatial rate (0, 0, 2).
- `euler_poincare_world_velocity_constant` (KA-191): random R, ω, v; R·(ω × v + v × ω) = 0 exactly in structure; with the reversed sign the residual equals 2R·(ω × v).
- `coad_dual_identity_18_variables` (KA-192): integer ξ, η, μ in [−8, 8]; equality exact.
- `generator_of_group_coadjoint_is_minus_coad`: derivative by `fs-ad` duals of t ↦ Ad*_{exp(tξ)} μ at 0 equals −ad*_ξ μ.
- `cayley_step_angle` (KA-237, KA-052): rotation angle of cay(ω·h) equals 2·atan(‖ω‖h/2); relative shortfall 1.333 × 10⁻⁴ at ‖ω‖h = 0.04 and 3.33 × 10⁻³ at 0.2.
- `cay_on_group_exact`: cay(Ω)ᵀ·cay(Ω) − I and det − 1 within 4 ulp on 10⁶ cases; SE(3) translation equals (I − ω̂/2)⁻¹·v.
- `dexp_matches_duals`, `dcay_matches_duals`, `dcay_inverse_closed_form` (KA-246, KA-172): closed forms against dual-number derivatives of the reference retractions and against the stated inverse matrices.

## Considerations
- The M0 exit needs only SO(3) and SE(3); the complete law table closes at M1 (N13-19).
- If the closed form for SE(3) dexp fails the Jacobian law near θ = π or for large translations, fall back to the block form [[J, 0], [Q, J]] with the same coefficient functions; the law, not the formula, is normative.
- The `f32` rows are measured on the generic reference code and on emitted kernels, never on a hand-written single-precision struct (ADR-MARROW-04).
- A requirement below 10⁻⁵ relative angle error needs ‖ω‖h < 0.011: substep, or use the exponential.
- No symplectic or energy claim follows from group membership; those are separate properties with separate tests (§16).
