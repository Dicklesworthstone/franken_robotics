## Background
`frob-lie` owns the groups every other crate speaks. After SO(3), SE(3) (BD-010, BD-011) and SO(2) (BD-012), four more are needed, each with a named consumer:
- **SE(2)**: planar bases. Wheeled-base kinematics, clearance complexes over (x, y, ψ) and the known answers for differential drives use the closed-form SE(2) exponential, deliberately not FrankenSim's `fs-mbd::planar_drive`, which has a reported defect near straight-line motion (FACT to recheck, admitted by BD-033).
- **SE₂(3)**: the floating-base state (rotation, velocity, position) as one group element. IMU dynamics are group-affine on it, so the invariant error of the estimator obeys an exactly linear equation (C-13).
- **SE₂₊ₖ(3)**: SE₂(3) extended by k contact or landmark points, the state of the contact-aided invariant filter (BD-114).
- **Product groups**: a floating-base robot's configuration space is Q = SE(3) × G₁ × … × Gₙ (C-01), with joint j contributing a subgroup; state updates, logarithms and adjoints act componentwise.
Tier: Solid. Design choice (DESIGN): the extended pose groups are one type generic in the number of vector columns, so SE(3) (one column), SE₂(3) (two) and SE₂₊ₖ(3) (2 + k) share code and laws; rejected: three hand-written groups, which is three places for one sign error.

## Scope
In: the four group families with `LieGroup`/`LieAlgebra` implementations, cay/exp/log and differentials, Ad, bracket, coad, pairing; their rows of every Lie law; the planted Jacobian control.
Out: frame typing (BD-047); joint subgroups (BD-046); the estimator (BD-114); the full-table gate record (N13-19); device layouts (N13-12).

## Technical approach
**SE(2).** Element (z, p): z a unit complex number (the type of BD-012 without the turn count), p ∈ ℝ²; action x_a = R(z)·x_b + p. Algebra (ω, v) with ω scalar first, v ∈ ℝ². J = [[0, −1], [1, 0]].
- Compose (z, p)·(z′, p′) = (z·z′, R(z)·p′ + p); inverse (z̄, −R(z)ᵀ·p).
- exp(ω, v) = (e^{iω}, V·v), V = [[sin ω/ω, −(1 − cos ω)/ω], [(1 − cos ω)/ω, sin ω/ω]]; small-angle polynomials as in BD-011. Check (constant-twist chord): forward speed u for time Δt with δ = ω·Δt from heading θ gives Δp = u·Δt·sinc(δ/2)·(cos(θ + δ/2), sin(θ + δ/2)), sinc(x) = sin x/x, sinc(0) = 1.
- cay(ω, v) = (((1 − ω²/4) + i·ω)/(1 + ω²/4), (I + ω·J/2)·v/(1 + ω²/4)).
- Ad_g(ω, v) = (ω, R·v − ω·J·p); group coadjoint Ad*_g(τ, f) = (τ + p × R·f, R·f) with the planar cross product a × b = a_x·b_y − a_y·b_x.
- Bracket [(ω₁, v₁), (ω₂, v₂)] = (0, ω₁·J·v₂ − ω₂·J·v₁); ad_ξ = [[0, 0], [−J·v, ω·J]]; ad*_ξ(π, ℓ) = (ℓ × v, −ω·J·ℓ).
- Differential (left-trivialized, as in BD-011): because X = ad_ξ satisfies X³ = −ω²·X, dexp_ξ = I − B·X + C·X² with B = (1 − cos ω)/ω², C = (ω − sin ω)/ω³; dcay from the matrix Cayley map, verified against dual numbers.
**Extended pose groups** `ExtendedPose<const N: usize>`: element (R, x₁, …, x_N), the matrix [[R, x₁ … x_N], [0, I_N]]. SE₂(3) is N = 2 with (x₁, x₂) = (velocity, position); SE₂₊ₖ(3) is N = 2 + k.
- Compose (R, xᵢ)·(R′, xᵢ′) = (R·R′, R·xᵢ′ + xᵢ); inverse (Rᵀ, −Rᵀ·xᵢ). Dimension 3 + 3N (9 for SE₂(3), 9 + 3k for SE₂₊ₖ(3)).
- Algebra (ω, u₁, …, u_N). exp = (exp ω̂, V(ω)·uᵢ) with V = I + B·ω̂ + C·ω̂²; cay = (cay ω̂, (I − ω̂/2)⁻¹·uᵢ); log and cay⁻¹ by the inverses of BD-011, with the same chart-cut refusal FROB-REF-213.
- Ad in (ω, u₁ … u_N) order: first block row (R, 0, …), row i: (x̂ᵢ·R, …, R at column i, …). Bracket: (ω₁ × ω₂, ω₁ × u₂ᵢ − ω₂ × u₁ᵢ). coad is the transpose of ad, written out as (π × ω + Σᵢ ℓᵢ × uᵢ, ℓᵢ × ω).
- A test asserts that N = 1 reproduces the `fs-ga`-backed SE(3) of BD-010 within the ulp bound, so the generic code cannot drift from the bit-parity path.
**Product groups.** `Product<A, B>` (static, nestable) and `DynProduct` (a runtime list of group kinds for model state, host only): compose, inverse, exp, log, cay, cay⁻¹ componentwise; Ad block-diagonal; bracket, coad, pairing componentwise and summed; `DIM` the sum. Refusals of a component (chart cut, Cayley bound) propagate with the component index.
**Laws.** Each group registers with the harness (N13-08) for: group axioms; retraction; round trip; adjoint; coadjoint pair; pairing; free body; Jacobians. Budget 10⁶ cases each, at most 64 ulp in `f64`, at most 16 ulp in `f32` for single operations (compose, inverse, action, cay) measured on the generic reference code.
**Determinism.** Host `f64`, D2 per platform; the Cayley lanes use only +, −, ×, ÷, √.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] SE(2), `ExtendedPose<2>`, `ExtendedPose<2 + k>` for k ∈ {1, 2, 4}, `Product<Se3, So2>` and a `DynProduct` of SE(3) with twelve SO(2) factors pass all eight laws on 10⁶ cases each inside the ulp bounds; their rows are appended to the GATE-MARROW-01 ledger (the complete gate is recorded by N13-19).
- [ ] A planted error in the SE(2) exponential's Jacobian (the sign of the −J·v column of ad inside dexp) is caught by the Jacobian law and by at least one other fixture (the seed bead's done-condition); the control is registered.
- [ ] The SE(2) exponential reproduces the chord known answers: a unit-speed half turn in one second gives (0, 2/π) m; heading 32 rad, unit speed, turn rate 6 × 10⁻¹³ rad/s, one second gives about (0.834, 0.551) m with no loss of the translation.
- [ ] `ExtendedPose<1>` agrees with the SE(3) of BD-010 within 64 ulp on 10⁶ cases.
- [ ] Product refusals carry the index of the refusing component.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` updated with the groups, their dimensions and validity domains.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
- `se2_laws_*`, `extended_pose_laws_*`, `product_laws_*`: the eight laws per group through `run_law`.
- `se2_chord_half_turn`: (ω, v) = (π, (1, 0)) for 1 s from θ = 0 gives p = (0, 2/π) = (0, 0.636620) m.
- `se2_near_straight`: θ₀ = 32 rad, v = (1, 0), ω = 6 × 10⁻¹³ gives p = (cos 32, sin 32) = (0.834223, 0.551427) m within 10⁻¹² (the admission case on which the sibling routine is reported to return zero translation).
- `se2_dexp_closed_form_vs_series`: I − B·X + C·X² against the 30-term series at ω ∈ {10⁻⁹, 0.5, 3}.
- `se2_dexp_planted_sign_is_caught`: with the mutation active the Jacobian law fails; the test asserts failure.
- `extended_pose_n1_matches_se3`, `extended_pose_adjoint_blocks`: Ad for N = 2 applied to (0, a, 0) returns (0, R·a, 0).
- `product_refusal_carries_index`: a `DynProduct` whose third factor is at a chart cut returns FROB-REF-213 with index 2.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/marrow_laws.sh --milestone m1 --groups se2,se23,sek3,product`: full-budget law runs; `STD-E2E` NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/marrow_laws/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript, with a `metric` line per law and group (worst ulp, threshold, cases, seed), an `assert` line for the planted Jacobian control, and on failure the first failing case in hex with the ledger path; summary with counts and GATE-MARROW-01.
### Known answers and negative controls
KA-239 to KA-246 for these groups; the chord answers (0, 2/π) m and (0.834, 0.551) m (KA-003 is the sibling admission case). Controls: the planted SE(2) Jacobian sign; exchanged angular and linear parts in the SE(2) twist; a transposed block in the extended adjoint. Metamorphic: a product law equals the conjunction of its factors' laws (a product of passing factors cannot fail). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine, 1 and 8 threads, debug and release.

## Considerations
- The seed done-condition reads "GATE-MARROW-01 complete". This bead completes the gate's Lie-group rows (every group, eight laws, 10⁶ cases, the ulp bounds, the planted Jacobian control). The rows of the law table that belong to other mechanisms (subgroups, reflections, representations, nested groups, orbits, curvature sign, cones) and the single gate record are N13-19, which depends on this bead.
- k is a compile-time constant here; a filter whose contact set changes size at run time uses the largest k with masked columns (the estimator's decision, BD-114).
- The extended groups do not exist in `fs-ga`; they are new code, so they have no bit-parity oracle and rely on laws plus the N = 1 cross-check.
- Line budget: these groups share the coefficient functions and small-angle code of BD-011; no second copy is allowed.
