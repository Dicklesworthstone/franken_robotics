## Background
A policy or critic for a symmetric robot can have its linear layers constrained to commute with the symmetry group, which divides both the parameters to learn and the data needed to learn them by at most the group's order (bet BET-07; the right group is the observation-preserving subgroup Γ_obs, bet BET-30). For real representations ρ_in on ℝ^{d_in} and ρ_out on ℝ^{d_out} of a finite group, the equivariant linear maps are the W with ρ_out(g)·W = W·ρ_in(g) for all g.

ADR-MARROW-07: equivariant linear maps are parameterized by real and complex irreducible blocks, and the Reynolds operator is their oracle. The blocks give exactly the free parameters of the commutant. Reynolds averaging, W ↦ |Γ|⁻¹·Σ_g ρ_out(g)·W·ρ_in(g)⁻¹, gives the same subspace and is exact for every type; it is kept as the test, not replaced as an error. A representation of quaternionic type is refused (FROB-REF-214). The blocks are a parameterization, and they earn their place for three reasons: optimization runs on exactly the free parameters instead of projecting after every step; the free-parameter count is reported to the agent before training; and the same bases block-diagonalize the linearized dynamics for the modal analysis. Rejected: projecting after every optimizer step as the primary mechanism (it is the fallback if this bead's kill gate fires); treating every block as real (counterexample below).

Counterexample (treating a complex-type block as real). C₃ acts on ℝ³ by cyclic permutation of three legs. The equivariant maps ℝ³ → ℝ³ are the circulant matrices, with three free parameters. The space splits as the trivial representation plus one two-dimensional real irreducible of complex type, so the formula gives 1 + 2 = 3. A parameterization that gives the two-dimensional block a single scalar yields two parameters and spans only the symmetric circulants. Those are exactly the maps equivariant under D₃: the error silently imposes a mirror symmetry the robot does not have, and the map x ↦ (x₂ − x₃, x₃ − x₁, x₁ − x₂), which is equivariant under C₃, becomes unrepresentable.

Tier: the decomposition is Solid; the layer and its payoff are Frontier, so this bead depends on its paper-scale pilot (N13-23) and carries kill gate KILL-MARROW-BLOCKS (decided in N13-24). Ceiling of the benefit, stated before any measurement: a factor equal to the order of Γ_obs in free parameters per layer and in trajectories per rollout: 2 for a robot with a forward sensor suite, 4 for a blind fore-aft-symmetric quadruped, 12 for a blind radial hexapod. Augmentation already recovers much of this, so the gain over augmentation may be nil (HYPOTHESIS; settled by the three-arm experiment of the learning chapter).

## Technical approach
Feature `frontier-blocks` (off by default). Inputs: an `ObsGroup` (the type barrier of N13-16: a mechanical group cannot be passed) and two channel spaces with their signed-permutation representations.
**Algorithm.**
1. From the character table, compute the Frobenius–Schur indicator ι for each character and pair each complex-type character with its conjugate (N13-14).
2. For each real irreducible, build a symmetry-adapted orthonormal basis of its isotypic component in the input and output spaces by applying the projector P_χ = (d_χ/|Γ|)·Σ_g χ(g)*·ρ(g) (summed with its conjugate for complex type) and the transfer operators that align the copies with one another. Construction (DESIGN, exact):
   a. P = the real isotypic projector of the constituent; V = its image, of dimension c·d_χ·m (c = 1 real type, 2 complex type; m the multiplicity).
   b. Real type: choose once per (group, χ) a subgroup H with exactly one copy of the trivial character in χ restricted to H (searched in the subgroup lattice, smallest first; H = {e} for degree one). E = P·R_H, with R_H the average of ρ over H, has rank m; take an exact orthogonal basis u₁ … u_m of its image (Gram–Schmidt without normalization). Fix once per (group, χ) transfer elements τ₁ = e, τ₂, …, τ_{d_χ} of the group algebra such that τ₁u, …, τ_{d_χ}u is an orthogonal basis of the irreducible generated by u (found on the first copy and reused for every copy and every space). The basis of copy k is {τ_j·u_k}. Such an H exists for every real-type character of the gate set (H = ⟨s⟩ for the degree-two characters of Dₙ and Dₙ × C₂; ⟨(0 1)⟩, S₃ or C₄ for the degree-two and degree-three characters of S₄); a character for which no subgroup qualifies is refused with FROB-REF-214 as outside the supported families, never given a guessed basis (fail closed).
   c. Complex type: the real operator J = i·(P_χ − P_χ̄) commutes with the action and satisfies J² = −P on V; it is the complex structure. Choose u₁ … u_m independent over that structure (orthogonalize with respect to both ⟨·,·⟩ and ⟨·, J·⟩); the basis of copy k is {τ_j·u_k, J·τ_j·u_k}.
   d. Because the action is orthogonal, Schur's lemma makes copies mutually orthogonal and gives every copy the same within-copy Gram matrix up to the scalar ‖u_k‖²; the columns are stored exactly with their squared norms and are normalized in floating point only when a dense matrix is expanded.
3. Store the two change-of-basis matrices Q_in and Q_out and the block list. A map is then W = Q_out·(⊕ᵢ Θᵢ)·Q_inᵀ, where Θᵢ is the free block of constituent i: Θ ⊗ I_{d_χ} with Θ any real m_out × m_in matrix for real type (m_out·m_in parameters); for complex type the blocks [[a, −b], [b, a]] ⊗ I_{d_χ}, a complex m_out × m_in matrix (2·m_out·m_in parameters).
4. Biases are restricted to the fixed subspace of ρ_out. Hidden layers carry copies of the regular representation, on which pointwise nonlinearities commute with the group.
**Layer object (DESIGN).** `EquivariantLinear { q_in, q_out, blocks, bias_basis }` with `num_params() = Σᵢ cᵢ·mᵢ^out·mᵢ^in + bias count`, `expand(θ) -> dense W` and its transpose map for gradients, and `apply_blocks(θ, x)`. The decomposition costs O(d³) once per model and channel space and is cached by `ModelId`. At run time a layer is either applied in the block basis or expanded once per optimizer step into a dense W, so inference pays nothing.
**Reynolds reference path.** `reynolds_layer(W)` projects a dense matrix exactly (rational) or in `f64` with an idempotence check; it is the oracle of every test here and the fallback mechanism if the kill gate fires.
**Proof obligation PO-MARROW-05:** the block parameterization spans exactly the commutant. Verification: exact rank of the Reynolds projection against the dimension formula, and containment both ways, per group on file: (⊆) every block basis map (one unit entry of one Θᵢ) is fixed by the Reynolds operator exactly; (⊇) the exact rank of the span of the block basis maps equals Σ cᵢ·mᵢ^out·mᵢ^in, which equals the exact rank of the Reynolds projection on Hom(ℝ^{d_in}, ℝ^{d_out}).
**GATE-MARROW-04** (Known answer; M4; TARGET): PO-MARROW-05 holds exactly for every group of GATE-MARROW-02 (Cₙ and Dₙ with n ≤ 12, their products with C₂, and S₄) on 100 random pairs of channel spaces each; all eight Marrow mutations are caught by at least three fixtures each (re-run of N13-18's matrix at this commit).
**Refusals.** FROB-REF-214 for a quaternionic constituent or an unsupported group; FROB-REF-215 if exact arithmetic exceeds its declared size (which is also a trigger of the kill gate).
**Determinism and evidence.** Construction D0; expansion in floating point D2. Outputs carry `Certified` when the PO-MARROW-05 check is attached as a certificate and `Tested` otherwise; a layer built under the fallback is always `Tested` and named by the fallback.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] GATE-MARROW-04 is measured by the gate's tier run and recorded from its receipt with measured values: for each of the 49 gate presentations, 100 random pairs of signed-permutation channel spaces (dimensions 1 to 24, random signs, random multiplicities): containment both ways exact, rank equal to the formula; zero failures; and the mutation matrix shows at least three independent catches for each of the eight mutations, with N13-18's eight mutation controls caught in the same run (the seed bead's done-condition, first half). The row is entered with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-MARROW-04 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19, the one writer of gate rows) by a session that did not implement this bead, N13-18 or BD-048; the script never writes it.
- [ ] The inputs of KILL-MARROW-BLOCKS are recorded: whether the gate passed, whether a repair cycle was used, and the peak exact-arithmetic bit length against its budget for every n ≤ 12; the decision itself is recorded as an ADR by N13-24, which depends on this bead (the seed bead's done-condition, second half).
- [ ] C₃ on three legs: three parameters; the antisymmetric circulant [[0, 1, −1], [−1, 0, 1], [1, −1, 0]] is represented (its block coordinates are zero on the trivial block and non-zero on the b coordinate of the complex block); D₃ on the same space gives two parameters and cannot represent it.
- [ ] C₂ × C₂ regular representation, 64 copies in and out: 16,384 parameters; expanding random θ gives W with ρ_out(g)·W − W·ρ_in(g) = 0 within 4 ulp for all g.
- [ ] Biases: the bias space has dimension equal to the multiplicity of the trivial character in ρ_out, and every bias is fixed by ρ_out.
- [ ] `expand` and its transpose are adjoint: ⟨expand(θ), G⟩ = ⟨θ, expandᵀ(G)⟩ within 64 ulp on random inputs (gradients are correct).
- [ ] A `MechGroup` cannot be passed (compile-fail); a quaternionic constituent is refused.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: (i) the complex block parameterized as real (C₃: rank 2 against 3); (ii) misaligned copies (transfer elements chosen per copy instead of once) break containment for a degree-two constituent of D₅ with multiplicity two; (iii) a conjugate missing in the projector; (iv) biases not restricted to the fixed subspace fail the bias check.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-231, KA-232, KA-251; CX-122. Controls (i) to (iv) and the eight mutations. Metamorphic: conjugating both representations by a group element leaves the span unchanged; the count is additive over direct sums, dim Hom(A ⊕ B, C) = dim Hom(A, C) + dim Hom(B, C), and between k and l copies of the regular representation it is k·l·|Γ|. Determinism: exact parts identical on every platform; expansions bit-identical per platform.

## Considerations
- Kill gate KILL-MARROW-BLOCKS. Trigger: GATE-MARROW-04 fails after one repair cycle, or the exact field arithmetic for n ≤ 12 exceeds its bit budget. Fallback: equivariant layers are produced by Reynolds projection in `f64` with an idempotence test, classed `Tested`; the free-parameter count is reported from the projection's rank. A capability that fell back is described by the name of the fallback.
- The learning-side kill gate (symmetry used for augmentation and metamorphic testing only, if equivariant policies do not beat augmentation) is separate and belongs to the three-arm experiment; this bead claims only that the parameterization is correct.
- The modal analysis at M1 needs only isotypic projectors (BD-048) to block-diagonalize mass and stiffness matrices; aligned copies add finer blocks and arrive here.
- Honest limit: a linearized operator that commutes with the action preserves the isotypic blocks; an equivariant nonlinear map need not decouple into independent nonlinear blocks.
- Reported fact to re-read from its source before any claim cites it: augmentation makes an ordinary network about as data-efficient as an equivariant one, and hard constraints are more fragile under distribution shift.
