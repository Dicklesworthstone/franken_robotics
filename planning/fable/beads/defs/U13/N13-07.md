## Background
The conventions table has 21 rows; one convention per concept, converted at exactly one boundary each. N13-01 created the normative document and the battery skeleton and passed the rows Marrow implements at M0; N13-06 passed the time rows on the referee at M1. At M2 the batch exists on four tiers (`referee`, `batch-cpu`, `gpu-cuda`, `gpu-metal`), and a convention that holds on the host and is silently different in a generated kernel (a twist stored linear-first in a device buffer, a raw angle in device state, a closed-closed window in a device sensor) would be invisible to host tests. This bead is the end-to-end closure: every row has a passing test, and the rows that have a device form pass on every tier. Tier: Solid. It is a test bead; it adds tests for the rows no other bead tests as conventions.

## Scope
In: the remaining row tests (1, 9, 10, 11, 12, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21); the per-tier forms of rows 3, 4, 8, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18; the end-to-end script; registration of every row's negative control.
Out: the mechanisms (owners below); gates of the owners.

## Technical approach
Each row keeps its test name `conv_NN_*` in `crates/frob-conformance/tests/conventions.rs` (host) and gains, where marked, a tier form run through the `frob` binary. Row, assertion, owner of the mechanism:
1. **World frame** (frob-io, frob-scene). A body released at rest falls along −z: after 1 s its z decreases by ½·g·t² = 4.903325 m with g = 9.80665 m/s² (to the integrator's stated order: TARGET relative 10⁻³ at the default step on throughput tiers, 10⁻⁹ on the referee), with x and y unchanged to round-off. A passive change of basis (yaw by 90° and a horizontal shift applied to scene, gravity, robot and every extrinsic) leaves every invariant observation unchanged. An imported y-up model is converted once, at import; its loss report names the conversion.
2. to 8. Already registered (N13-01, N13-02). Tier forms: the device buffer schema lists twist components in (angular, linear) order and wrench components in (torque, force) order; device state for a revolute joint holds (c, s) and no angle field, and a joint with turn state (one read by a lifted law, the flag `needs_turn_count`) also holds its integer turn count k in the `i32` turn slab that N13-37 adds to the device layout; a joint without turn state has no k entry, and the test fails a k slab on such a joint as well as a missing one.
9. **Link numbering** (frob-model, BD-148). For every archetype model compiled: λ(i) < i for all links; depth(i) = depth(λ(i)) + 1; the depth-level table partitions the links.
10. **Inertia** (N13-04). Stored pseudo-inertia is about the body-frame origin: moving the body frame by G changes the stored matrix to G·𝖯·Gᵀ and leaves the physics digest of a free-fall run unchanged.
11. **Connection** (N13-05). The SO(3) constant-connection loop gives +da about z.
12. **Units** (frob-core, frob-kir). Every buffer in every kernel manifest has a dimension in its schema; a kernel with a seeded dimension violation is rejected at build; a tolerance declared without a dimension is rejected; no public signature above layer 1 takes a bare `f64` physical quantity (API scan). One unitless epsilon applied to an angle and an impulse is refused.
13. to 15. **Time, instants, windows** (N13-06, BD-189). The 400/100/30 schedule (L = 1,200; periods 3, 12, 40; hyperperiod 120), the firing-count formula, the three-instant checkpoint replay and the 20 N exposure average, on all four tiers with identical packet-history digests; a throughput tier serving a non-multiple period by sample-and-hold exposes the age of the held value. The checkpoint replay runs in two forms: the M1 form of N13-06 (the force-torque exposure as the opened shutter) and the camera form that M1 could not run (a depth camera with a 10 ms exposure and a rolling shutter, BD-187 and BD-188, checkpointed just after its shutter opens, with the row position in the checkpoint); each restore goes through the user-facing verb `frob sim replay` (BD-203), which did not exist at M1.
16. **Comparators** (frob-spec, N13-10). A predicate declared strict is false at equality, a non-strict one true, and both are evaluated exactly: x = 16,777,217 against the bound 16,777,216 (not representable distinctly in `f32`) is decided correctly on every tier; the docking boundary case U = 3/4, ε = 5/16 is not admitted (strict).
17. **World address** (frob-kernels, BD-180). A draw depends on (address, step, stream) only: adding a stream changes no other stream; permuting slots leaves each world's draws unchanged; a fork's child has its own address and fresh streams; sensor noise is keyed by logical sensor, episode, sample and component.
18. **Anchored position** (BD-179). A shift by one cell (8 m) in either direction is exact for offsets in [−4, 4) m; the spacing of offsets is at most 2⁻²² m.
19. **Tier names** (frob-core). Exactly `referee`, `batch-cpu`, `gpu-cuda`, `gpu-metal` are accepted by `--tier`; `gpu`, `cuda` and `cpu` are refused as protocol errors with the list of valid names.
20. **Identities** (frob-core). `DesignId`, `ModelId`, `SceneId`, `KernelId`, `PolicyId`, `EpisodeId` equal the BLAKE3 hash of their canonical bytes (recomputed by the script with an independent BLAKE3); no identity type has a verdict field, and equal identities are never offered as equal verdicts.
21. **Exact numbers in artifacts** (frob-core, frob-cert). The canonical writer emits rationals reduced with a positive denominator; the reader rejects "2/4" and "1/−3"; 9,007,199,254,740,993 travels as a string and round-trips; an interval carries its implementation and precision and is outward-rounded (the interval of 1/10 contains 1/10 exactly).
**Meta-assertions.** The battery report shows 21 rows `pass` and none `not run`; every row has at least one negative control in the registry; the single-boundary scan of N13-01 extends to all crates.
**Determinism and evidence.** Rows 9, 12 and 13 to 21 are integer or exact and are class D0; the tier forms of rows 1 and 10 are compared under each tier's determinism class.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
The `conv_NN_*` tests for rows 1, 9, 10, 11, 12, 16 to 21 with the inputs and expected values listed; `conventions_all_rows_have_controls`; `conventions_document_matches_battery`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/conventions.sh --milestone m2`, plain shell driving the real binaries: `frob doctor --json` (tiers present); the host battery; then per tier `frob sim run --fixture conventions-freefall --tier <t> --seconds 1 --seed 7 --key conv-01-<t>-<run-id> --json` (row 1), `frob sense schedule --clocks physics=400Hz,imu=100Hz,policy=30Hz --tier <t> --json` and `frob sim run --fixture conventions-clock --tier <t> --seed 7 --key conv-13-<t>-<run-id> --json` (the fixture N13-06 registers) with `frob sim replay --run <r> --world 0 --from-snapshot <hash> --key conv-13-replay-<t>-<i>-<run-id> --json` from each of the three checkpoints, once with the force-torque exposure and once with the camera (rows 13 to 15), `frob sim run --fixture conventions-anchor --tier <t> --key conv-18-<t>-<run-id> --json` (row 18), a paired run with one extra noise stream under two distinct keys (row 17), and negative runs `frob sim run --fixture conventions-freefall --tier gpu --key conv-19-<name>-<run-id> --json` and the same with `cuda` and `cpu` (row 19, each refused with 802 and the list of valid names). Rows 3, 4, 8 and 12: the host battery reads the committed generated kernel manifests (kept current by `xtask check-generated`) for slot order, the absence of an angle field, the k slab and the dimensions, and `frob spark check --kernel <k> --backend <b> --key conv-check-<k>-<b>-<run-id> --json` for each kernel carrying a twist, wrench or revolute slab on each device backend confirms that the device runs that kernel within its class. This bead registers `conventions-freefall` and `conventions-anchor` in the fixture registry of N24b-03. Every run verb carries its own key, so a repeated call never returns a stored receipt in place of a run. Idempotent and seed-explicit. `STD-E2E` NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/conventions/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: every `cmd` with its exact command line, exit code and wall time; the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` lines with units and thresholds (4.903325 m, L = 1,200, 20 N, 2⁻²² m); digests wherever state is compared, with the first differing value and the artifact paths on failure; `refusal` lines for the negative runs; a final summary with assertions passed and failed per row and per tier.
### Known answers and negative controls
KA-191 to KA-206 as carried by the rows; CX-098 to CX-105. Controls as listed. Metamorphic relations exercised: passive change of world frame; slot permutation; cell shift exactness; re-phasing. Determinism lanes: the script twice on one machine gives identical digests; the D0 rows are identical across tiers and platforms.

## Considerations
- This bead depends on mechanisms from several subsystems; if one is late, its row stays `not run` and the bead stays open: the battery is never closed by removing a row.
- On a machine with one device family the other family's rows are recorded from the other machine; the summary names the fingerprint of each.
- Under the no-GPU variant of M2 (N40-33: no admitted, ratified device family) the `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal` forms are recorded `not run (variant: no-GPU)` in the battery report, the bead closes on the `referee` and `batch-cpu` forms with the device forms listed as open, and those forms are run when a device family is admitted; they are never counted as passing in the meantime.
- The battery is cheap (seconds per tier) and joins the smoke battery so a convention cannot regress unnoticed.
