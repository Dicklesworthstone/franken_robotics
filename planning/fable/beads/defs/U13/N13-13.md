## Background
`frob-algebra` is Marrow's second crate. It owns the algebra that is not Lie theory: finite groups as signed permutations with closure, conjugacy classes, character tables, isotypic projectors and the Reynolds operator; the `Cone` trait with the nonnegative orthant, second-order and positive-semidefinite cones; posets and antichains for co-design; the pseudo-inertia cone with its affine-invariant operations; max-plus matrices taken from FrankenSim's `fs-tropical` through the airlock. To these it adds five things: the computation of a design's mechanical symmetry group and of its observation-preserving subgroup; real and complex irreducible blocks as a parameterization of equivariant linear maps; subgroup lattices, orbit counting and orbit representatives; the four-dimensional contact cone as one more linear image of the second-order cone; and the mass-support predicate on the inertia cone. Both Marrow crates sit in layer 1. Neither knows what a robot is: they receive trees, screws, matrices and channel lists, and return groups, classes, projectors, signs and refusals.

Group computations here are exact, never floating point. A symmetry can be established by three routes: (i) by construction, when limbs are instances of one subterm under the `mirror` or `radial` constructors; (ii) by exact field arithmetic, with entries in ℚ for mirrors and in the real subfield of the cyclotomic field of order lcm(n, 4) for n-fold rotations, n ≤ 12; (iii) by tolerance, for imported designs, classed `Tested`. Route (ii), and exact character tables (the characters of a cyclic group are roots of unity), need a small exact field library. Why lcm(n, 4): cos(2π/n) lies in the n-th cyclotomic field, but sin(2π/n) is in the n-th cyclotomic field only when 4 divides n; for n = 3 the real subfield of ℚ(ζ₃) is ℚ and sin(2π/3) = √3/2 is outside it. Adjoining i fixes that. This bead creates the crate and that library. Tier: Solid.

Rejected: floating-point group computation with tolerances (a tolerance decides closure and equality of characters, which are discrete outcomes); linking FrankenSymPy at run time (it is a build-time tool confined to the derivation annex, and FACT: it has no group theory in Rust; an upstream work item, FD-FSYM-03, asks for permutation groups and character tables there, with the small-group code here as the interim posture and cross-checked tables as the switch-over); a general algebraic-number package (unneeded: every number that occurs is cyclotomic).

## Scope
In: the crate skeleton with contract and feature flags; `Cyclotomic<M>` field elements with arithmetic, conjugation, the real-subfield view and exact sign; trigonometric constants for rotations by 2π/n; exact isometry matrices over these fields; size budgets and the refusal on overflow.
Out: finite groups, characters, projectors (BD-048); symmetry detection (N13-15, N13-16); orbits and lattices (BD-049); cones (BD-050); inertia cone (BD-051); posets (BD-052); the verb (N13-14).

## Technical approach
**Crate.** `crates/frob-algebra/`, `[package.metadata.frob] layer = 1, rank = 2`, `unsafe_code = "deny"`; dependencies `frob-core`, `frob-lie` (for `exact::{Int, Rational, linalg}`; an earlier row of the same layer, which the layer rule permits), and the airlock crate for `fs-tropical`. `CONTRACT.md` with the ten sections, `contract.toml`, `tests/conformance.rs`. Modules (DESIGN): `field.rs`, `group.rs`, `characters.rs`, `projectors.rs`, `irreps.rs`, `symmetry/`, `orbits.rs`, `cone.rs`, `inertia.rs`, `poset.rs`, `tropical.rs`. Frontier mechanisms are behind features that are off by default: `frontier-blocks`, `frontier-wreath`, `frontier-affine-inertia`.
**Cyclotomic elements.** `Cyclotomic<const M: u32>`: an element of ℚ(ζ_M), stored as a polynomial in ζ of degree below φ(M) with `Rational` coefficients, reduced modulo the cyclotomic polynomial Φ_M. Operations: +, −, ×, inverse (extended Euclid modulo Φ_M), complex conjugation (ζ ↦ ζ⁻¹), exact equality, `is_rational`, `is_real` (equal to its conjugate), embedding into a common field ℚ(ζ_lcm) for mixed operations, and an outward-rounded complex interval for display and for sign decisions.
**Orders that occur.** For an n-fold rotation the working order is m = lcm(n, 4):
| n | m | φ(m) | degree of the real subfield | minimal polynomial of 2·cos(2π/m) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1, 2, 4 | 4 | 2 | 1 | x |
| 8 | 8 | 4 | 2 | x² − 2 |
| 3, 6, 12 | 12 | 4 | 2 | x² − 3 |
| 5, 10 | 20 | 8 | 4 | x⁴ − 5x² + 5 |
| 7 | 28 | 12 | 6 | x⁶ − 7x⁴ + 14x² − 7 |
| 9 | 36 | 12 | 6 | x⁶ − 6x⁴ + 9x² − 3 |
| 11 | 44 | 20 | 10 | x¹⁰ − 11x⁸ + 44x⁶ − 77x⁴ + 55x² − 11 |
(The polynomials were checked numerically for this bead; the implementation derives Φ_M and the minimal polynomials itself and the table is a test oracle.)
**Trigonometric constants.** With ζ = ζ_m, r = m/n and i = ζ^(m/4): cos(2π/n) = (ζ^r + ζ^(−r))/2 and sin(2π/n) = −i·(ζ^r − ζ^(−r))/2. Both are real elements of ℚ(ζ_m). `rotation_z(n) -> [[Cyclotomic; 3]; 3]` and rotations about the other coordinate axes are built from them.
**Exact sign of a real element.** Zero is decided exactly (all coefficients zero). Otherwise the element is evaluated in outward-rounded interval arithmetic with rational bounds on cos(2πk/M) and sin(2πk/M) (Taylor polynomials with remainder), doubling the working precision until the interval excludes zero; termination is guaranteed because the element is non-zero. Used for canonical orderings, never for equality.
**Exact isometries.** `ExactIsometry<F> { p: [[F; 3]; 3], t: [F; 3], det: i8 }` with F = `Rational` or `Cyclotomic`; `is_orthogonal()` checks PᵀP = I as an exact identity; the determinant is computed exactly and must be ±1. The parity rule (polar by P, axial by det(P)·P) is implemented on exact entries and cross-checked against `frob-lie`'s `Isometry` on rational cases.
**Budgets.** Every field computation runs under the declared rational size of N13-10 (DESIGN default 4,096 bits per numerator or denominator); exceeding it returns FROB-REF-215 with the expression, the inputs and the budget, never a rounded value. The peak size observed is recorded, because one trigger of the kill gate on irreducible blocks is "the exact field arithmetic for n ≤ 12 exceeds its bit budget".
**Unsupported orders.** A request for n > 12 on the exact route is refused by the symmetry code (N13-15) with FROB-REF-214, whose second condition is "a symmetry group outside the supported families"; this library itself supports any M, and the limit is policy (OPEN-MARROW-2).
**Determinism and evidence.** Everything is D0. Results that depend only on this arithmetic are `Certified` when their certificate is emitted and `Tested` otherwise.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The crate builds at layer 1, rank 2; `check-layers`, `check-deps` and `check-contracts` pass; no default feature enables a Frontier mechanism.
- [ ] Field laws hold exactly on 10⁵ random elements per order in the table: associativity, commutativity, distributivity, x·x⁻¹ = 1 for x ≠ 0, conj(conj(x)) = x, conj(x·y) = conj(x)·conj(y).
- [ ] For every n from 1 to 12: cos² + sin² = 1 exactly; `rotation_z(n)` is exactly orthogonal with determinant 1 and its n-th power is exactly the identity, while no smaller positive power is.
- [ ] Spot values: sin(2π/3)² = 3/4 and sin(2π/3) is not rational; 2·cos(2π/5) is a root of x² + x − 1; 2·cos(2π/8) squared equals 2; ζ_M^M = 1 and 1 + ζ + … + ζ^(M−1) = 0 for every M in the table.
- [ ] The minimal polynomials derived by the code equal the table.
- [ ] Exact sign: 1,000 random real elements per order agree in sign with a 200-bit numerical evaluation; elements constructed to be within 10⁻⁶⁰ of zero but non-zero are signed correctly; exact zeros are reported zero.
- [ ] A computation forced over its size budget returns FROB-REF-215.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: (i) reduction modulo x^M − 1 instead of Φ_M makes 1 + ζ + … + ζ^(M−1) non-zero and fails; (ii) sin(2π/n) built in ℚ(ζ_n) for n = 3 (without adjoining i) cannot represent √3/2 and the constructor refuses; (iii) a conjugation that maps ζ to ζ instead of ζ⁻¹ fails the conj laws on a non-real element; (iv) equality decided by floating evaluation with tolerance 10⁻¹² equates two distinct elements 10⁻²⁰ apart and fails.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`cyclotomic_field_laws_{4,8,12,20,28,36,44}`; `roots_of_unity_identities`; `trig_constants_exact_n1_to_12`; `rotation_order_exact`; `min_poly_table`; `real_sign_refinement`; `exact_isometry_orthogonality` (mirror diag(1, −1, 1); rotation by 2π/7 about z; a non-orthogonal matrix with one entry off by 10⁻³⁰ is rejected); `parity_rule_matches_frob_lie` (1,000 rational isometries); `size_budget_refusal_215`; `wrong_modulus_mutant_fails`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/marrow_algebra_field.sh`: runs the field suite and prints, per order, the laws passed, the peak numerator and denominator bit lengths against the budget and the wall time; then `frob capabilities --json` to assert the crate reports its exact-field route. `STD-E2E` NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/marrow_algebra_field/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `metric` lines (cases, failures against threshold 0, peak bits against budget), `refusal` line for the forced FROB-REF-215 with payload, `assert` lines for controls, seed, commit; on failure the two elements as exact coefficient lists; summary with counts.
### Known answers and negative controls
Algebraic identities above (exact). Controls (i) to (iv), registered against GATE-MARROW-02, whose character tables depend on this arithmetic. Determinism: D0, byte-identical results on every platform, thread count and build profile.

## Considerations
- OPEN-MARROW-2: whether exact field arithmetic should extend to radial designs with n above 12. Default: no; such designs are symmetric by construction through `radial`, and imported ones use the tolerance route with a `Tested` label. Owner: Marrow lead. Blocks nothing. The library needs no change either way; only the policy check in the symmetry code moves.
- The intra-layer dependency on `frob-lie` for exact integers and rationals is a DESIGN reading of the layer rule (same layer, earlier row); if the workspace owners want Marrow's crates to depend on `frob-core` alone, the exact kernel moves there.
- Degree 20 over ℚ (n = 11) makes inverses the most expensive operation; group computations use them rarely (orthogonality and closure need only products).
- `fs-symmetry` in FrankenSim covers cyclic groups only (about 480 lines, FACT) and is not used.
