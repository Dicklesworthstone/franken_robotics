## Background
Construction C-09: a morphology has two symmetry groups, and both are computed from the design, never trusted from a declaration (a declared symmetry is an assertion the compiler checks, refusing a mismatch). ADR-MARROW-06: two groups are computed and stored for every design in every task. The mechanical group Γ_mech preserves the body; its subgroup Γ_obs also preserves everything the closed loop can see or is asked to do. This bead computes the first; N13-16 computes the second.

**Γ_mech** is the set of automorphisms of the labelled kinematic tree that are realized by an isometry of the body at its reference configuration and that preserve link Regions, fills and materials (hence masses and pseudo-inertias), joint screw bases under the parity rule, joint limits, transmissions with their signed ratios, actuator cards, end-effector contact laws with their interface cards, and the topology of the power bus. Typical results: C₂ for a biped; C₂ × C₂ for a quadruped built symmetric fore-aft and left-right; a dihedral group for a radially symmetric hexapod, cyclic if its legs are handed.

The owner is `frob-algebra::symmetry`, determinism class D0, and the user-facing verb is `analyze.symmetry` (analysis A-03, built in BD-102 on top of this). `frob-algebra` does not know what a robot is: it receives a tree with exact numbers and opaque digests and returns a group with permutations, signs and reasons. Tier: Solid for the computation.

Key design decision: **enumerate candidate isometries, not candidate permutations.** The abstract automorphism group of a tree can be large (a robot with twelve identical bolts has 12! relabellings); the isometries that realize it are few. Rejected: graph-automorphism search followed by geometric filtering (combinatorial blow-up, and it needs the geometry check anyway); numerical matching with a tolerance as the only route (a tolerance decides a discrete fact; kept only as the labelled third route for imported designs).

## Scope
In: the input record; colouring; canonical form; candidate isometries; the verifying descent with first-mismatch records; closure into a group; the three routes to exactness and their evidence classes; the continuous-stabilizer report.
Out: induced representations, Γ_obs, breakers, stabilizers of a context (N13-16); the verb, the near-symmetry report text and repairs as typed edits (BD-102); exact field arithmetic (N13-13); group tables and characters (BD-048); the `mirror` and `radial` constructors (BD-056).

## Technical approach
**Input (DESIGN).** `SymmetryInput { bodies: [BodyRec], joints: [JointRec], bus: Graph, constructors: [DeclaredGenerator], reference: exact poses }`. A `BodyRec` has the parent index, the digest of its Region, fill and material, its pseudo-inertia in exact rationals, its contact law and interface-card digests; a `JointRec` has its class, exact screw basis in the parent frame, pitch, limit interval, transmission kind and signed ratio, and actuator-card digest. Exact numbers are rationals or cyclotomic elements; an input with floating numbers takes the tolerance route.
**Algorithm.**
1. *Colour* every node of the tree with an isometry-invariant key: the canonical hash of its Region, fill and material; the coefficients of the characteristic polynomial of its pseudo-inertia (exact rationals, so no eigenvalues are sorted); joint class, pitch and limit widths; actuator card, absolute gear ratio and transmission kind; contact law and interface card; and the multiset of its children's keys. DESIGN clarification: the characteristic polynomial is taken of the central form (the mass m and the second moment about the centre of mass, Σ_C = Σ − h·hᵀ/m), because the origin-based 4×4 matrix changes under a translation of the body frame and would not be an invariant.
2. *Canonical form* of the coloured rooted tree, bottom-up (each node's code is its key with the sorted list of its children's codes), which identifies isomorphic subtrees in O(n log n) for n bodies.
3. *Candidate isometries.* With three or more non-collinear attachment points in one class (children with equal codes) on the root body, an isometry is fixed, up to one reflection through their plane, by the images of three of them. That gives at most 2·m·(m − 1)·(m − 2) candidates for m such points (48 for four legs, 240 for six). Each comes from a 3×3 linear system solved exactly: for a base triple (a₁, a₂, a₃) and an ordered image triple (b₁, b₂, b₃) with equal Gram matrices, P = B·A⁻¹ with A = [a₂ − a₁, a₃ − a₁, n_a], B = [b₂ − b₁, b₃ − b₁, ±n_b], n the cross product of the two difference vectors, and t = b₁ − P·a₁; the candidate is kept only if PᵀP = I holds exactly. With fewer points (a biped's two hips, a differential drive's two wheels) the candidates are the signed axis permutations of the root's principal frame (48 matrices) together with the generators the design's `mirror` and `radial` constructors declare. A continuous stabilizer (a body of revolution with a single axial limb) is reported as such, and only its constructor-generated finite subgroup is used.
4. *Verify* each candidate by descending the tree: every body, screw basis (with parity), limit interval, signed ratio, card and contact law must map to its image. Per body: the image of its pseudo-inertia, G·𝖯·Gᵀ with G = [[P, t], [0, 1]], equals the pseudo-inertia of the image body in the reference frame; digests are equal. Per joint: the image of each screw under the parity rule (angular part by det(P)·P, linear part by P, with the translation term) spans the image joint's screw space; for a one-coordinate joint the coordinate sign ε is +1 if the image screw equals the image joint's declared screw and −1 if it equals its negative; a multi-coordinate joint must map by a signed permutation of its coordinates or the candidate fails; the limit interval maps to itself (ε = +1) or to its negated reverse (ε = −1) and must equal the image joint's; the signed transmission ratio must agree after applying ε; the bus graph must be preserved by the body permutation. The descent yields the body permutation σ and the sign vector ε on joint coordinates. The first mismatch ends the descent and is recorded, with its field path and its size, for the near-symmetry report.
5. *Close* the survivors under composition, check closure, and build the multiplication table, conjugacy classes and character table (BD-048).
**Data layout.** As in BD-048: order, generators, one record per element (body permutation as `u16` indices, coordinate signs as `i8`, the isometry in exact form), a multiplication table of |Γ|² bytes, a class map and a character table in exact field elements. Nothing here is floating point.
**Three routes to exactness.** A symmetry is established (i) by construction, when the limbs are instances of one subterm under `mirror` or `radial` and the check reduces to term equality plus integer arithmetic on mount indices modulo n; (ii) by exact field arithmetic, with entries in ℚ for mirrors and in the real subfield of the cyclotomic field of order lcm(n, 4) for n-fold rotations, n ≤ 12; or (iii) by tolerance, for imported designs, in which case the result is classed `Tested`, the tolerance is recorded, and the near-symmetry report says how far each failing field is from symmetric. Routes (i) and (ii) are D0 and classed `Certified` when the certificate is emitted. Each element records its route; a group's route is the weakest of its elements'.
**Output.** `MechGroup` (a distinct type, see N13-16): the group, per element (σ, ε, exact isometry, route), the list of rejected candidates with their first mismatch (field path, observed, required, size), the continuous-stabilizer note if any, and the evidence class.
**Refusals.** FROB-REF-214 when the closed group is outside the supported families (order above 256, or an n-fold rotation with n above 12 on the exact route), returning the order and generators, with repairs: 1. declare the symmetry by construction through `radial`; 2. take the tolerance route, labelled `Tested`. FROB-REF-215 when exact arithmetic exceeds its declared size.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] On synthetic exact trees: a quadruped skeleton (torso with four identical three-joint legs at (±a, ±b, 0), mirrored joints written in the axial-image form) gives Γ_mech of order 4 isomorphic to C₂ × C₂, with the three non-identity isometries diag(−1, 1, 1), diag(1, −1, 1) and diag(−1, −1, 1); a radial hexapod gives D₆ (order 12); the same hexapod with handed legs gives C₆ (order 6); a biped gives C₂; a tree with one leg lengthened by 1/1000 gives the trivial group.
- [ ] For the quadruped, the element diag(1, −1, 1) (left-right mirror) carries joint signs +1 on pitch joints and −1 on roll and yaw joints written with unmirrored axes, and +1 on all joints written in the axial-image form.
- [ ] Candidate counts match the bound: 48 candidates for four attachment points, 240 for six; every surviving candidate satisfies PᵀP = I exactly.
- [ ] Each change below removes exactly the expected elements and records a first mismatch at the stated field: one foot's sole material (interface card of that foot); one reversed gear (signed ratio); one limit interval narrowed on one side; one body's mass changed by 1 part in 10⁶ (pseudo-inertia).
- [ ] A body of revolution with a single axial limb reports a continuous stabilizer and returns only the constructor-declared finite subgroup.
- [ ] Routes: a design built with `mirror` reports route (i); the same geometry given by exact coordinates without constructors reports route (ii) with the same group; the same geometry with floating coordinates reports route (iii), class `Tested`, with the tolerance.
- [ ] A 13-fold radial design on the exact route returns FROB-REF-214 with the two repairs.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: (i) the parity sign omitted on axial vectors gives joint signs opposite to the truth under both mirrors, and with the asymmetric knee limits of the fixture it rejects both mirrors, so the group collapses to the half-turn alone; the test detects the wrong signs and the wrong order; (ii) colouring with the origin-based characteristic polynomial splits identical legs mounted at different offsets into different classes and loses the group; (iii) accepting PᵀP = I within 10⁻⁹ instead of exactly admits the lengthened-leg design.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-230 (its mechanical half: C₂ × C₂ of order 4), KA-270 (Γ_mech as built for the archetypes, exercised later on real archetypes), KA-250 (nested groups, with N13-16). CX-121 (a material edit breaks the element although the encoder permutation still exists), CX-124 (mirrored joint written with P·a). Controls (i) to (iii), registered against GATE-MARROW-03. Metamorphic: relabelling bodies, reordering children, or moving every body frame by its own rigid transform leaves the group unchanged up to isomorphism and transports the permutations. Determinism: D0; identical bytes on every platform.

## Considerations
- The planted asymmetries of the gate (a moved sensor, a one-sided payload, a changed sole material, a reversed gear) include two that are mechanical and two that concern Γ_obs; this bead must name the right field for the mechanical ones.
- OPEN-MARROW-2 (exact route beyond n = 12) defaults to no.
- Cost: bodies up to a few dozen, candidates up to a few hundred, each descent linear in the tree; the computation is milliseconds and is cached by design identity by the model compiler.
- A closed kinematic loop is handled on its spanning tree with the closure constraint treated as one more field to preserve.
