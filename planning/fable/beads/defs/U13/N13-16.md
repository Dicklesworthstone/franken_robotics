## Background
A robot with a forward-facing sensor is mechanically symmetric fore and aft and is not symmetric in closed loop: an obstacle ahead is seen, an obstacle behind is not. Tying policy weights under the mechanical group would constrain the policy to something false: it would have to walk backwards as the mirror image of walking forwards while seeing only forwards. ADR-MARROW-06 therefore stores two groups for every design in every task: the mechanical group Γ_mech (N13-15), and its subgroup Γ_obs that also preserves everything the closed loop can see or is asked to do. **Only Γ_obs may tie policy weights, augment data, deduplicate candidates or enumerate gaits.**

Definitions.
- Γ_obs is the subgroup of Γ_mech whose elements also preserve: the sensor suite as a set (mount, field of view, scan pattern, rate, clock and noise card of each sensor); every payload; the direction of gravity at the reference configuration; and every observation, action, reward and requirement term of the task, each of which must declare how it transforms. An element under which some term has no declared transformation is excluded.
- For one particular world there is a third, smaller object: the stabilizer of the context, Stab(context) ≤ Γ_obs, whose elements also fix that world's terrain, material field, boundary conditions and current contact mode.

The three have different uses and they are not interchangeable:

| Use | Group required | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Block-diagonal mass and stiffness matrices at a symmetric stance | Γ_mech ∩ Stab(configuration) | Mechanics only; sensors are irrelevant |
| Weight tying in policies and critics; data augmentation | Γ_obs, with the task's world and command distributions invariant in law | The closed loop must be symmetric, not only the body |
| Deduplicating candidate designs before a sweep | Γ_obs of the slot arrangement | Two candidates are the same experiment only if the closed loop cannot tell them apart |
| Gait enumeration by (H, K) pairs | Γ_obs ∩ Stab(command) for the body; the declared clock-network group (ℤ₄ × ℤ₂ for a quadruped) for the phase patterns | A gait is a closed-loop object; the body group alone does not separate the six primary quadruped gaits |
| Exact reduction inside one world: symmetric orbit search, merging two worlds | Stab(context) | Augmentation may pair two different worlds; reduction needs the same world |
| Metamorphic test of the simulator | Γ_mech with the world transformed along with the robot | The test transforms everything, so nothing is broken |

Tier: Solid for the computation; the use in equivariant networks is Frontier (BD-325 and the learning chapter's experiment). The sensor-side predicates with timing (a spinning lidar's beam order under a mirror) are refined later by the sensing work (BD-241); this bead provides the group operations and the generic filter they plug into.

## Technical approach
**Step 6: induce the representations** (all integer signed permutations: one `u16` index and one `i8` sign per channel per element).
- Joint positions and velocities: the body permutation with the coordinate signs ε from the verifying descent.
- Actuator channels: the same permutation with ε multiplied by the gear-direction sign (the actuator sign includes gear direction).
- The floating base: the twist rule of the parity table, (ω, v) ↦ (det(P)·P·ω, P·v + t × det(P)·P·ω), stored as the exact 6×6 matrix.
- Each observation, action, reward and requirement term, by its declared kind: invariant scalar (trivial); polar vector (P); axial vector (det(P)·P); joint-indexed (the joint representation); foot-indexed (the foot permutation); sensor-indexed (the sensor permutation); a scan with a ray permutation (the declared permutation of rays, with signs where a ray quantity is axial). A term with no declared kind has no representation.
**Step 7: filter to Γ_obs.** An element of Γ_mech survives if it (a) maps the sensor suite to itself with matching cards and scan patterns (each sensor's mount, an exact isometry, maps to the mount of a sensor with an identical card digest, field of view, rate, clock and noise card; the pattern maps to itself); (b) maps every payload to a payload with equal pseudo-inertia and attachment; (c) fixes the direction of gravity at the reference configuration (P·ĝ = ĝ for the polar vector ĝ); (d) has a representation on every task term. The first field that excludes an element is recorded as its breaker (field path, observed image, what was required). The survivors are closed under composition by construction (elements that preserve a structure form a subgroup); the code asserts closure and fails loudly otherwise.
**Step 8: return** both groups, the representations, the breakers, and for each breaker the smallest change that would restore the element, as data: {field path, current value, the value or added item that restores invariance}, ranked by the number of fields changed (for a forward lidar: add the mirrored sensor; or move the mount onto the element's fixed set; or remove the sensor). The design layer turns these into typed edits.
**Group-kind types (DESIGN).** `MechGroup`, `ObsGroup` and `ContextStabilizer` are distinct types. `ObsGroup` can only be produced by the filter; `ContextStabilizer` only by `stabilizer(&ObsGroup, &Context)`; functions that tie weights, augment, deduplicate or enumerate gaits take `&ObsGroup` (or a stabilizer), and a `MechGroup` cannot be passed (compile-fail test). `MechGroup::for_metamorphic_test()` is the sanctioned use of the larger group. Every exported group carries its kind in the artifact (`group_kind`).
**Stabilizers.** `stabilizer(group, context)` returns the elements that also fix a supplied context: a configuration (joint vector and base pose, compared exactly or within a recorded tolerance, labelled), a command (a twist or goal, through its representation), a contact mode (a set of feet in states open, stick or slip, through the foot permutation), terrain and material field descriptors (through supplied invariance predicates). Intersections are computed element-wise on the stored tables.
**Fixed subspaces.** For any element or subgroup and any representation, the exact fixed subspace (from BD-048), so the symmetry analysis can list the observations at which tying forces tied actions.
**Counterexamples that fix the rules (fixtures).**
- *A forward sensor.* A quadruped built fore-aft and left-right symmetric has Γ_mech = C₂ × C₂ of order 4. Add one forward-facing lidar on the torso. The left-right mirror maps the lidar to itself; the fore-aft mirror and the half-turn do not. So Γ_obs = C₂ of order 2, and the breaker for the other two elements is the lidar mount.
- *A fixed observation forces a fixed action.* Two identical latches, of which exactly one must be closed. At an observation fixed by the mirror, a deterministic equivariant controller must issue an action fixed by the mirror, so it commands both latches alike, and both matching commands fail. A controller that draws one latch at random is equivariant in law and succeeds. A failed search over tied deterministic policies therefore excludes only that policy class. The fixture checks that the fixed subspace of the mirror on the two-latch action space is the diagonal (dimension 1), which is what the analysis reports.
- *A world that is not symmetric.* A quadruped crossing a patch that is slippery on one side only. The task's world distribution may still be mirror-invariant in law, which licenses augmentation, while no single world is mirror-invariant, which forbids reduction inside it: `stabilizer(Γ_obs, that world)` is trivial while Γ_obs is not.
- *A material edit.* Changing one foot's sole material leaves the encoder permutation intact and breaks the transition symmetry. Both facts are recorded: the representation still exists, the group element does not.
**Proof obligation PO-MARROW-04.** Every element of Γ_obs fixes the sensor suite, the payloads, gravity and every task term; every element of Γ_mech outside Γ_obs has a recorded breaker. Verification: an exact check per generator; each breaker is replayed as a failing equality (the recorded field, transformed by the element, is compared with the original and must differ).
**Determinism and evidence.** D0. Evidence class as in N13-15 (the weakest route among the elements and the inputs).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Forward-lidar fixture: Γ_mech = C₂ × C₂ (order 4), Γ_obs = C₂ (order 2), breaker of the fore-aft mirror and of the half-turn = the lidar mount; the first-ranked repair is "add the mirrored sensor".
- [ ] Nested-groups law, exact, on 1,000 random synthetic designs with random sensor, payload and task-term sets: Γ_obs is a subgroup of Γ_mech; every element of Γ_obs has a representation on every task term; removing every sensor, payload and task term gives Γ_obs = Γ_mech.
- [ ] PO-MARROW-04 holds on every fixture: each generator of Γ_obs passes the four exact checks; each excluded element's breaker replays as a failing equality.
- [ ] Induced representations are homomorphisms: ρ(gh) = ρ(g)·ρ(h) exactly for joint, actuator, base-twist and every term representation.
- [ ] The actuation map intertwines: ρ_joint(g)·R = R·ρ_act(g) exactly for every element, with R the diagonal of signed transmission ratios (joint torque = R·actuator command), on every fixture including the compensated-gear quadruped.
- [ ] A task term with no declared transformation excludes every non-identity element, and the breaker names that term.
- [ ] Type barrier: passing a `MechGroup` to the tying, augmentation or deduplication entry points does not compile.
- [ ] Stabilizers: the one-sided slippery world gives a trivial stabilizer with Γ_obs of order 2; the symmetric stance of the quadruped gives Γ_mech ∩ Stab(configuration) of order 4; a forward command gives Γ_obs ∩ Stab(command) equal to the left-right mirror group.
- [ ] Two-latch fixture: the fixed subspace of the mirror on the action space has dimension 1 and is the diagonal.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: (i) a filter that returns Γ_mech unfiltered fails the lidar fixture (order 4 instead of 2); (ii) an actuator representation that ignores gear direction fails the intertwining check of the actuation map on the compensated-gear fixture (a quadruped whose mirrored knees are written in the polar form, ε = −1, with the gear reversed on the mirrored side so that the signed ratio agrees after ε and the element survives N13-15's descent; the true actuator sign is ε·sign(r′/r) = +1, the mutant gives −1); a homomorphism check cannot catch it, because the mutant is still a representation; (iii) a breaker recorded for the wrong field fails its replay; (iv) a one-sided payload that is ignored by the filter leaves the mirror in Γ_obs and fails.

## Considerations
- A false declaration of symmetry in a design is refused by the model compiler with FROB-REF-109 (declared symmetry differs from computed symmetry); this bead supplies the computed side.
- Augmentation needs the world and command distributions to be invariant in law; that is a statement about the task's randomization, declared by the task and checked by the sensing and arena work, not derivable from the body.
- Reported fact to keep in view (source register, to be re-read): augmentation makes an ordinary network about as data-efficient as an equivariant one, and hard constraints are more fragile under distribution shift; hence the type barrier and the conservative default of the smaller group.
- Retirement rule: when a physical or sensing asymmetry leaves Γ_obs trivial for a design and task, tying is off and augmentation alone remains; the reduced calculation is replaced by the full one without an author's edit.
