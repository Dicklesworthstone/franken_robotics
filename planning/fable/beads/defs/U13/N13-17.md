## Background
Symmetry is spent at campaign time, where it deletes simulations. Before a combinatorial sweep that assigns one of k module types to each of n slots (legs to hips, wheels to corners), two assignments in one orbit of the symmetry group are the same closed loop and need one evaluation. The mechanism lives in `frob-algebra::orbits` and is implemented by BD-049 with its verb `algebra.orbits` (read; M1: orbit counts and one canonical representative per orbit). This bead is the conformance side of that subsection: it states the specification the implementation must meet, as known answers, a law and negative controls, and it owns the subsection's end-to-end script, which runs the verb's own script (BD-049's) as a stage and adds the design-bound step. Tier: Solid for counting, representatives, strata and lattices; the wreath-product use is Frontier (pilot N13-25, mechanism N13-26). It is a test bead.

**Specification under test.**
- *Orbit counting and representatives.* `frob-algebra::orbits` computes the cycle index of the slot action of Γ_obs, counts the orbits, and generates one canonical representative per orbit. By the orbit-counting lemma (Burnside) the number of distinct assignments of k types to slots X under Γ is |Γ|⁻¹·Σ_{g ∈ Γ} k^c(g), with c(g) the number of cycles of g on X. The acting group is Γ_obs, never Γ_mech alone. The canonical representative is the lexicographically least assignment of its orbit, and for every member the group element that maps it to the representative is recorded, so results are transported back with the correct representation ρ(g). The saving is bounded by |Γ_obs| and is a factor of two for most sensed robots; it is stated before any sweep is priced.
- *Contact strata.* The same counter sizes mode enumerations: four feet, each open, sticking or slipping, under C₂ × C₂.
- *Subgroup lattice and (H, K) pairs.* All subgroups up to conjugacy, their normalizers, and the pairs K ⊴ H with H/K cyclic that gait enumeration needs. The groups here have at most a few dozen elements, so enumeration is by closure of generating sets.
- *Wreath products.* When each limb has an internal symmetry group K of its own and the hub permutes the limbs by H, the design's group contains K ≀ H = K^|X| ⋊ H, of order |K|^|X|·|H|, provided every limb automorphism preserves the limb's attachment port and the hub action preserves the decorated ports. Both conditions are checked before nested tying is offered. The use is narrow: steer-and-drive modules on a swerve base, symmetric grippers on a symmetric arm.

## Scope
In: the battery `crates/frob-algebra/tests/orbits_conformance.rs`; the Orbits law; the known answers; the lattice and (H, K) checks; the wreath-condition checks at the level BD-049 exposes; `scripts/e2e/algebra_orbits.sh` with its design-bound step and battery stage.
Out: the implementation (BD-049) and the verb's own script `scripts/e2e/algebra_orbits_verb.sh` (BD-049's, run here as a stage); campaign quotienting by canonical forms and the mission-observable digest (the families chapter); the gait atlas (the control chapter); nested weight tying (N13-26).

## Technical approach
**Known answers (exact integers).**
1. Ten module types on the six slots of a radially symmetric hexapod under D₆: (10⁶ + 2·10 + 2·10² + 10³ + 3·10⁴ + 3·10³)/12 = 86,185 orbits out of 10⁶ assignments (a factor of 11.6). The terms are: the identity (six cycles); the two rotations by ±60° (one cycle); the two by ±120° (two cycles); the half-turn (three cycles); the three reflections through opposite slots (four cycles); the three reflections through opposite edges (three cycles).
2. The same sweep when the sensors leave only the sagittal mirror: (10⁶ + 10³)/2 = 500,500 (a factor of 2.0).
3. With three types under D₆ the formula gives (729 + 6 + 18 + 27 + 243 + 81)/12 = 92, which brute-force enumeration confirms.
4. Contact strata: four feet, each open, sticking or slipping, under C₂ × C₂ give (3⁴ + 3·3²)/4 = 27 strata, with 162 one-foot transitions (648 directed transitions divided by four). Under the two-element subgroup that a forward-facing sensor leaves: (3⁴ + 3²)/2 = 45 strata and 648/2 = 324 transitions.
5. Wreath product: two slots swapped by the hub, each holding one of k module types in one of two mounting orientations that the module's own half-turn symmetry identifies; the group C₂ ≀ C₂ has order 8 and the count is (4k² + 4k)/8 = (k² + k)/2, which is 6 for k = 3 against 36 raw assignments.
6. Lattices (computed for this bead by exhaustive closure; DESIGN arithmetic): C₂ has 2 subgroups and 3 pairs (H, K) with K normal in H and H/K cyclic, namely (1, 1), (C₂, C₂), (C₂, 1); C₂ × C₂ has 5 subgroups and 11 such pairs (every K ≤ H except (C₂ × C₂, 1)); ℤ₄ × ℤ₂ has 8 subgroups (orders 1, 2, 2, 2, 4, 4, 4, 8) and 23 such pairs; D₆ has 16 subgroups in 10 conjugacy classes and 41 such pairs (the same reference list BD-049 asserts; recomputed by brute force for this round).
**The Orbits law.** The cycle-index count equals a brute-force count for every case with at most 10⁶ assignments: for each gate group acting on n slots with k types such that kⁿ ≤ 10⁶, enumerate all assignments, canonicalize each by applying every group element, count distinct canonical forms, and compare with the formula and with the number of representatives the generator emits. Also asserted: every emitted representative is the lexicographic minimum of its orbit; the recorded group element maps each member to its representative; orbit sizes sum to kⁿ; each orbit size divides |Γ|.
**Lattice checks.** Subgroups found by closure of generating sets are exactly the subgroups (cross-check: every subset closed under the table that contains the identity is in the list, by brute force for |Γ| ≤ 16); conjugacy classes of subgroups and normalizers satisfy |class| = |Γ|/|N(H)|; the (H, K) list is exactly the pairs with K ⊴ H and H/K cyclic (re-derived independently in the test by testing each pair).
**Wreath conditions.** With a limb group K and hub group H supplied as data with port descriptors: the constructor returns the group of order |K|^|X|·|H| only if both conditions hold; a limb automorphism that moves the attachment port, or a hub element that does not preserve a decorated port, makes it return the flat group with the failed condition named.
**End-to-end.** `scripts/e2e/algebra_orbits.sh` has three stages:
- Stage `verb`: runs BD-049's own script `scripts/e2e/algebra_orbits_verb.sh` (owned by BD-049, built on its dependencies) and fails if it fails. That script drives `frob algebra orbits` on the counts and refusals of the verb: `--family D6 --slots 6 --types 10` → 86,185 of 1,000,000 with the factor and `group_kind`, and with `--representatives --limit 5` the first five canonical representatives in lexicographic order with their orbit sizes; `--family C2 --action sagittal --slots 6 --types 10` → 500,500; `--family C2xC2 --slots 4 --types 3` → 27 (the standard action of C₂ × C₂ on four slots is regular); `--family C2 --action sagittal --slots 4 --types 3` → 45 (two 2-cycles); `--family D6 --slots 6 --types 3 --representatives` → 92 representatives, paginated and never cut; the order-300 group of `tests/fixtures/orbits/order300.json` (a 15-cycle and a disjoint 20-cycle on 35 points) → FROB-REF-214, exit 3, with the order reached and the generators; `--family C2 --slots 20000 --types 10` → FROB-REF-215, exit 3, before any arithmetic, with the expression, n, k, |Γ| and the budget; and the registry's own example for `algebra.orbits`, read from `registries/verbs.toml`, not hard-coded. This script asserts the stage's summary line and copies its counts into its own log.
- Stage `battery`: `cargo test -p frob-algebra --test orbits_conformance -- --nocapture` (known answers 1 to 6, the Orbits law, the lattice and wreath checks, the controls), one `assert` line per test.
- Stage `design`, the step that needs `design.new` (N14a-21, a dependency of this bead and not of BD-049): a design-bound call `--design <quad with forward lidar> --task <task> --set feet:4 --types 3` (the named-slot-set form of the verb registry; `--slots` takes a count only) → uses Γ_obs (order 2): 45, with a warning line that the mechanical group would have given 27. The design is created by the script from the fixture file `tests/fixtures/symmetry/quad_forward_lidar.fbot` with `frob design new quad-fwd-lidar --file tests/fixtures/symmetry/quad_forward_lidar.fbot --key ao-design-<run-id> --json` (N14a-21), and its identifier is logged; the task is the fixture task file beside it.
FROB-REF-214 and FROB-REF-215 are the two chapter codes the verb registry lists for `algebra.orbits` (the numerator Σ_g k^c(g) of the oversize count would need more than its declared 65,536 bits), so the stage `verb` observes both; running the registry's published example there keeps that example and the input forms used here from drifting apart unnoticed.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Known answers 1 to 6 hold exactly through the library API.
- [ ] The Orbits law passes for every group of GATE-MARROW-02 on every (n, k) with kⁿ ≤ 10⁶ and n ≤ 12 slots in the group's natural action: formula = brute force = number of emitted representatives; row appended to the GATE-MARROW-01 ledger as an `exact` law.
- [ ] The end-to-end script exits 0: its stage `verb` (BD-049's `algebra_orbits_verb.sh`) reports the counts 86,185, 500,500, 27, 45 and 92 read from verb envelopes, both refusals observed (FROB-REF-214 for the order-300 group, FROB-REF-215 for the oversize count) and the registry's published example running cleanly; its stage `battery` passes; its stage `design` gives 45 under Γ_obs with the warning.
- [ ] A design-bound sweep uses Γ_obs and says so; counting under Γ_mech when a front-only lidar is fitted is reported as the counterexample it is (27 against the correct 45).
- [ ] Negative controls caught: (i) a wrong cycle index (reflections through opposite slots counted with three cycles instead of four) gives 83,935 instead of 86,185 and fails; (ii) a representative generator that keeps the first-seen member instead of the lexicographic minimum fails the minimality assertion; (iii) the transport element recorded as its inverse fails the member-to-representative check on a non-involution; (iv) an (H, K) list that omits the normality test admits a non-normal K in D₆ and fails; (v) a wreath constructor that skips the port check returns order 8 for a limb whose half-turn moves its attachment port and fails.
- [ ] The count and the saving factor are printed before any sweep is priced: the envelope of `algebra.orbits` carries `raw`, `orbits`, `factor` and `bound` (= |Γ_obs|).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/algebra_orbits.sh`, plain shell, idempotent, no seed needed (exact). `STD-E2E` NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/algebra_orbits/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: each `cmd` with the exact command line, exit code and wall time; the envelope's evidence block (class, tier `static`, determinism `exact`, identities of design and task where bound); `metric` lines for each count against its known answer; a `refusal` line for each of the two refusals with code and payload; on failure the first differing count and the envelope saved as an artifact; a summary line with assertions passed and failed and the gates touched.
### Known answers and negative controls
KA-233, KA-234, KA-235, KA-252; the corrected fixture of the verification chapter (45 strata, 324 transitions under the observation subgroup); C-22's 92 and wreath count. Counterexample: counting with Γ_mech when a front-only lidar is fitted merges designs that the closed loop distinguishes. Controls (i) to (v), registered. Metamorphic: evaluating any member of an orbit and transporting the result equals evaluating the representative; relabelling slots permutes representatives and preserves counts. Determinism: D0; identical envelope bytes on every platform.

## Considerations
- The factor is at most the group order, between 2 and about 12 for the bodies on file; the battery asserts the envelope states the bound so that no report multiplies it by a speed-up measured elsewhere.
- Mode strata are counted only up to symmetry orbits; enumeration by hyperplane arrangements was considered and parked.
- The second, independent quotient of a campaign (designs whose mission-observable projections are equal are evaluated once; 735,471 ballast placements fall into 24,546 classes) is not part of `frob-algebra` and is tested by the families work.
- The lattice numbers in item 6 were computed for this bead; if BD-049's implementation disagrees, the independent brute-force check inside the test decides.
