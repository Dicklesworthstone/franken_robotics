## Background
GATE-MARROW-01 (claim: Known answer; earliest milestone M0; TARGET): all laws pass on 10⁶ cases each, with at most 64 ulp in `f64` and at most 16 ulp in `f32` for single operations; theorem cards T-01, T-03 and T-04 certified (GATE-LEMMA-00). At M0 the gate is recorded for SO(3) and SE(3) only (BD-011). At M1 it must be complete: every group, every law whose mechanism exists. The laws are spread over a dozen beads; this bead is the single place where the whole table is run under one procedure and one record, by a session that did not implement the mechanisms. Tier: Solid. It is a gate bead: it adds no mechanism.

Each law is a G0 property test over random inputs and a boundary set (identity, rotations by π, tiny angles, large translations, sign-equivalent quaternions, rotations approaching π from both sides). Tolerances are in units in the last place.

## Scope
In: running the complete law table; the per-law, per-group ledger; the gate record; the list of laws deferred to their own gates with the reason; the fresh-context review.
Out: the laws' implementations (owners below); the device gates (GATE-MARROW-05) and the block gate (GATE-MARROW-04).

**Procedure.**
1. Check out a clean tree at the candidate commit; record the commit, toolchain, hardware fingerprint and seed.
2. Run every registered law through the harness (N13-08) with the full budget, as the Gauntlet tier run `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0_laws -- GATE-MARROW-01` (the law rows KA-239 to KA-255 are G0 rows; the run writes one receipt with the ledger lines): 10⁶ cases per (law, group) for the ulp laws including the boundary set; the stated exhaustive or exact budgets for the exact laws. No `partial` ledger line is admissible.
3. Run the `f32` single-operation rows (compose, inverse, action, cay, the unit-complex update) on the generic reference code and, where a generated kernel exists, on `batch-cpu`.
4. Run the mutation harness (N13-18), and the same tier run with `--features gauntlet-mutations`, and require that every registered negative control of this gate is caught in that receipt; a control not caught fails the gate whatever the laws say.
5. Verify the three certificates through `frob prove verify <T-01> <T-03> <T-04> --json` (the certificate paths their card registry rows name; T-01: cay(Ω)ᵀ·cay(Ω) = I and det cay(Ω) = 1; T-03: ‖q·c‖² = ‖q‖²·‖c‖²; T-04: Ad(gh) = Ad(g)·Ad(h) and the group coadjoint is the inverse transpose) and cite their hashes.
6. Confirm the ADR for OPEN-MARROW-1 exists (N13-09) and cite it.
7. Record the M1 part of the row in `registries/gates.toml` from the receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-MARROW-01 --receipt <hash> --part M1` (the one writer of gate rows; the script never writes one): status, measured worst residual per law and group in ulp against 64 and 16, case counts (with the boundary cases that refused as declared, such as the 33 m `IllConditioned` Jacobian case, counted separately under `refused_expected` as the harness of N13-08 records them; an undeclared refusal is a failure), the recorded tolerance of the two non-ulp clauses, controls caught, certificates, deferred rows with reasons, commit, fingerprint, seed, command, receipt hash.
8. A fresh-context session that did not implement the laws performs steps 1 to 7 and signs the record.
**Determinism and evidence.** Claim kind: Known answer. Class: `Tested` for the floating laws, `Certified` for the three identities. Determinism: host D2; exact laws D0.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Every (law, group) cell marked "measured here" has a ledger line with the full budget and a worst residual at or below its bound: 64 ulp in `f64`, 16 ulp in `f32` for single operations; exact laws have zero failures.
- [ ] `registries/gates.toml` holds GATE-MARROW-01 with status `pass` and measured values (not the targets), citing the commit, fingerprint, seed, command and receipt hash, and resolving in the artifact graph.
- [ ] T-01, T-03 and T-04 are certified and their certificates replay offline; a mutated coefficient is rejected.
- [ ] Every negative control registered for GATE-MARROW-01 is caught in this run (the mutation matrix shows at least three catches for M1 to M7).
- [ ] The deferred rows are listed in the record with their gates: Blocks → GATE-MARROW-04 (M4); Predicates on devices → GATE-MARROW-05 (M2); the four-dimensional cone row → addendum at M2.
- [ ] The record is written by a session that did not implement the mechanisms; the bead closes with a typed reason citing this bead's script and its log.
- [ ] If any cell fails, the gate status is `fail` with the first failing case in the record; the tolerance is not widened and no cell is removed.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
None of its own beyond a meta-test `law_table_is_total`: every `LawId` of the harness registry has an owner, at least one registered (law, group) cell or an explicit deferral, and at least one negative control.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/marrow_laws.sh --milestone m1 --gate` (the shared script owned by N13-08; this bead adds its `--gate` stage): runs steps 2 to 6 from a clean build, then `frob prove verify <T-01> <T-03> <T-04> --json` and `frob capabilities --json`, and ends by printing the receipt hash that step 7 records (the Marrow capability rows must not say `implemented` unless this gate row exists). `STD-E2E` NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/marrow_laws/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: a `metric` line per cell (law, group, scalar, cases, boundary cases, worst ulp, threshold), `assert` lines per negative control (caught, by which fixtures), the evidence block of `prove verify` (claim Proof, class `Certified`, identities), seed, commit, fingerprint, wall time per law; on failure the first failing case with hex inputs, both sides and the ledger path; a final summary with cells passed and failed, controls caught and the gate touched. The script is idempotent and seed-explicit and exits non-zero on any failure.
### Known answers and negative controls
KA-239 to KA-255 (the law rows), KA-220 (the certificates), KA-256 and KA-257 as prerequisites already recorded. Controls: the eight Marrow mutations. Determinism lanes recorded with the gate: the run twice on one machine; 1 and 8 worker threads; debug and release; macOS and Linux for the Cayley lanes (bit equality expected and recorded if not attained).

## Considerations
- The gate's milestone in the register is M0; the M0 record (SO(3), SE(3)) and this M1 record are two rows of one gate, and the M1 exit review reads this one.
- Budget: the full table is of order 10⁸ evaluations and must fit the machine envelope (8 cores, 16 GiB, one hour); the script runs under a timeout and reports per-law wall time so that a slow law is visible.
- A law that cannot meet 64 ulp for a stated numerical reason is not waived: its owner proposes a law-specific bound by a dated amendment to the gate register before the measurement it governs.
