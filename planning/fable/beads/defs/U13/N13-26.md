## Background
Flat weight tying uses the observation-preserving group Γ_obs acting on a robot's channels. Some designs have more structure: each limb (module) has an internal symmetry group K of its own, and the hub permutes the limbs by a group H. Then the design's group contains the wreath product K ≀ H = K^|X| ⋊ H, of order |K|^|X|·|H|, where X is the set of slots: each limb may be transformed by its own element of K independently, and the hub then permutes limbs. The condition: every limb automorphism preserves the limb's attachment port, and the hub action preserves the decorated ports. Both conditions are checked before nested tying is offered. The use is narrow: steer-and-drive modules on a swerve base, symmetric grippers on a symmetric arm.

Tier: Frontier. Ceiling stated before measurement: a further factor equal to the order of a limb's own symmetry group, 1 or 2 for the archetypes on file (the pilot N13-25 measures the factor in free parameters on odd and even channel spaces and may revise this line). Pilot: N13-25. Kill and fallback: if the pilot is falsified (a nested element fails the equivariance law) or this bead's equivariance test fails after one repair cycle, the mechanism is dropped without replacement and flat Γ_obs tying remains. There is no separate kill-gate identifier; the decision is recorded in the review log with the pilot's record.

Design choice (DESIGN): work from generators, never enumerate the group. C₂ ≀ D₄ has 128 elements and C₂ ≀ D₆ has 768, above the 256-element cap of the finite-group type; but a linear map commutes with a group exactly when it commutes with its generators, so the equivariant subspace is an exact nullspace computation over a handful of generators. Rejected: enumerating the wreath product (cap, cost); averaging over it (the Reynolds sum has |K|^|X|·|H| terms).

## Scope
In: the port-condition checks; the nested generator set; the nested equivariant subspace by generator commutation; the nested layer on top of BD-325's layer object (or on the Reynolds path if the block kill gate fired); the equivariance test on the simulator; counts reported through the irreps verb.
Out: the orbit-counting use of wreath products in campaign deduplication (BD-049 and the families chapter); the pilot (N13-25); learning experiments.

## Technical approach
Feature `frontier-wreath` (off by default).
1. **Inputs.** An `ObsGroup` H acting on slots X (from N13-16); for each slot orbit, the limb's own group K given by generators with their signed-permutation action on the limb's channels (computed by running the symmetry computation of N13-15 and N13-16 on the limb as a design of its own, rooted at its attachment port); the limb's port record (port frame, mechanical interface, electrical and signal ports with their decorations) and the hub's decorated ports.
2. **Condition 1: every limb automorphism preserves the limb's attachment port.** For each generator k of K: its exact isometry fixes the port frame (as an isometry of the limb), and maps each decorated port item (keyed connector orientation, pin order, signal port) to an item with an identical record. A generator that fails is removed from K; the failure is reported with its field path.
3. **Condition 2: the hub action preserves the decorated ports.** For each generator h of H with slot permutation σ: the isometry of h maps the decorated port at slot x onto the decorated port at slot σ(x) including decoration. If any generator fails, nested tying is not offered and the flat group is returned with the failed condition named.
4. **Nested generators.** For each orbit of slots choose one slot x₀; the generator set is {k acting at x₀ : k a generator of K} ∪ {generators of H acting on slots and carrying limb channels along}. Because H is transitive on the orbit, these generate K ≀ H on that orbit. Orders are reported by the formula |K|^|X|·|H|, not by enumeration.
5. **Nested equivariant subspace.** Solve, exactly, ρ_out(g)·W = W·ρ_in(g) for every nested generator g: a linear system in the entries of W whose nullspace is the nested commutant (exact rational linear algebra). If the block kill gate did not fire, the nested layer is expressed as a linear subspace of BD-325's block coordinates for the flat group H (the constraints from K cut blocks down or zero them); otherwise it is a projector onto the nullspace applied after each optimizer step, with idempotence and residual tests as in the fallback.
6. **Reporting.** `algebra.irreps --nested` reports the flat count, the nested count, their ratio, and the two port conditions with pass or fail and reasons.
7. **Equivariance test (the law the pilot's falsifier names).** For each nested generator g and the compiled model on `batch-cpu`: on 10³ random admissible states and actions, step(ρ(g)·x, ρ(g)·u) = ρ(g)·step(x, u) to a round-off-level residual (TARGET: the residual bound used by the step-equivariance metamorphic test for flat generators). A generator that fails is not a symmetry of the simulator's model, whatever the port records say; nested tying for that limb is withdrawn and the failure is reported.
**Determinism and evidence.** Group-side computations D0. A nested symmetry is `Tested` (the simulator residual test) unless established by construction with a certificate.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Swerve base, zero caster trail: both port conditions pass; reported order 2⁴·8 = 128; commutant dimensions on the three channel spaces of the pilot are 1 against 3 (odd channel), 3 against 3 (even), 4 against 12 (odd and even).
- [ ] Swerve base with caster trail, or with a keyed off-axis connector: condition 1 fails for the half-turn and the reported group is flat D₄, with the field path of the failure.
- [ ] Hub with all port keys along +x: condition 2 fails; flat group returned.
- [ ] A symmetric two-finger gripper on a mirror-symmetric arm: K = C₂ (finger swap), H = C₂: nested order 2²·2 = 8; counts match exact nullspace computation by an independent brute-force check that enumerates all eight elements.
- [ ] The simulator equivariance test passes for every nested generator offered, and fails for the trail variant if tying is forced.
- [ ] No code path enumerates more than 256 group elements; C₂ ≀ D₆ (768 elements) is handled from generators.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: (i) skipping condition 1 offers nested tying on the trail variant and the simulator test fails; (ii) nested generators built without carrying limb channels under the hub permutation give a commutant that is too large; (iii) a nested "symmetry" with a wrong sign on the drive channel fails the simulator test.
- [ ] If the pilot or this test falsifies the mechanism: the bead closes with the typed reason "dropped without replacement", flat tying is unaffected, and the review log says so.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`port_condition_1_passes_and_fails`; `port_condition_2_passes_and_fails`; `nested_generators_generate_expected_order` (brute force for |K ≀ H| ≤ 128); `nested_commutant_dims_swerve`; `nested_commutant_equals_bruteforce_small`; `nested_layer_within_flat_blocks` (every nested map is a flat-equivariant map); `no_enumeration_above_cap`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/marrow_wreath.sh`: `frob design new --template swerve-4`; `frob analyze symmetry <id> --json` (hub D₄ and module symmetry); `frob algebra irreps --design <id> --task <task> --in actions --out actions --nested --json` (flat and nested counts, port conditions); `frob sim run` twice on `batch-cpu` with and without the nested generator applied to initial state and actions, comparing transported state digests; the same on the trail variant, expecting refusal of nested tying. `STD-E2E` NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/marrow_wreath/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `cmd` lines with exit codes and wall time, envelope evidence blocks (class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), `metric` lines for counts and for the equivariance residual against its bound, `assert` lines for port conditions and controls; on failure the generator, the field path or the first differing state component; summary with counts.
### Known answers and negative controls
The pilot's numbers (3 and 1; 3 and 3; 12 and 4); the orbit-count form of the same group structure, (k² + k)/2 for C₂ ≀ C₂. Controls (i) to (iii). Metamorphic: applying a nested generator to a whole run permutes and signs the result and changes nothing else. Determinism: exact on the group side; the simulator test under the tier's class.

## Considerations
- Whether nested tying is worth offering is decided by the pilot's measured factors and by whether any archetype on file passes both port conditions as built.
- Nested symmetry interacts with Γ_obs: a sensor on one module (a wheel encoder fault flag, a camera on one finger) breaks K at that slot; the symmetry computation on the limb with its sensors decides, not the limb's mechanics alone.
- The wreath product in campaign deduplication (counting module assignments) has the same two conditions and is checked by the orbits conformance battery.
