## Background
The mobility analysis (A-01) answers "how many degrees of freedom does this mechanism really have, and is any loop over-constrained?" Its Solid method is the rank of the constraint Jacobian at exact rational sample points. Construction C-01 adds a structural method: joints whose screw bases are bracket-closed are connected subgroups of SE(3) in one of twelve Hervé classes (BD-046), and mobility analysis can use intersections and products in the subgroup lattice. That gives the right answer for the exceptional over-constrained linkages whose mobility follows from subgroup structure (Sarrus: count 0, mobility 1) and returns no verdict for paradoxical ones (Bennett, Bricard), whose mobility rests on metric conditions and is decided by the rank test alone; both kinds are linkages where the Grübler–Kutzbach count is wrong.

Tier: Frontier. Ceiling, stated before measurement: correct mobility for exceptional over-constrained linkages and an explicit `undecided` for paradoxical ones; no speed benefit. What the lattice adds over the rank is an explanation (which subgroups intersect in which motion) and a second, independent route to the same number. Pilot: N13-27 (must pass before this bead starts). Falsifier: a lattice answer that disagrees with the exact rank of A-01. Kill and fallback: the rank at exact rational sample points is the only mobility method; this bead is then closed unbuilt or its code removed, and A-01 loses nothing it promised at M1.

Rejected alternative: making the lattice the primary method with the rank as a check. The lattice is silent or wrong-by-omission on metric-dependent linkages, so it can never be the arbiter.

## Scope
In: lattice operations on `JointSubgroup` (exact); the kinematic bond of a serial limb; single-loop and parallel-limb lattice mobility with `Decided` or `Undecided`; the integration point in A-01 with the agreement rule; tests.
Out: Hervé classification itself (BD-046); the rank method and the analysis verb (BD-101); multi-loop linkages beyond the parallel-limb pattern (reported `undecided`).

## Technical approach
Feature `frontier-lattice-mobility` in `frob-lie` (off by default); the analysis crate calls it when the feature is on.
**Lattice operations (exact rationals, class `Exact`, determinism D0).** For connected subgroups A, B given by their Lie algebras 𝔞, 𝔟 ⊂ 𝔰𝔢(3) (reduced screw bases):
- `meet(A, B)`: the subgroup with algebra 𝔞 ∩ 𝔟 (an exact subspace intersection; an intersection of subalgebras is a subalgebra), classified by BD-046.
- `join(A, B)`: the subgroup generated by A and B: the bracket closure of 𝔞 + 𝔟 (add brackets until the rank stops growing).
- `product_is_subgroup(A, B)`: true exactly when 𝔞 + 𝔟 is already bracket-closed; then the kinematic bond of the serial chain A then B is `join(A, B)` with dimension dim 𝔞 + dim 𝔟 − dim(𝔞 ∩ 𝔟). When false, the product is a manifold that is not a subgroup, and the lattice has no verdict about it.
- The partial order (inclusion) and the class of every result, so reports can say "planar gliding ∩ planar gliding = line translation".
**Bond of a serial limb.** For joints J₁ … J_k in series, each a one-parameter subgroup or a classified joint subgroup, all expressed in one frame at the reference configuration: fold with `product_is_subgroup`; the limb's bond is `Subgroup(B_limb)` if every partial product is a subgroup, else `NotASubgroup`. Idle mobility of the limb is (number of joint coordinates) − dim B_limb when the bond is a subgroup.
**Lattice mobility of a platform connected to a base by limbs L₁ … L_m** (a single loop is the case m = 2):
1. If any limb's bond is `NotASubgroup`: `Undecided { reason: limb i is not a subgroup }`.
2. Otherwise the platform's motion subgroup is M = meet over limbs of B_i, and the mobility is dim M + Σᵢ (idle mobility of limb i).
3. Validity note carried in the result: the argument assumes the bonds are configuration-independent subgroups; for limbs whose subgroup is conjugated as the mechanism moves (the class is constant and the instance changes), the verdict holds at the reference configuration and is labelled `Decided(reference)`; A-01 confirms it by rank at several sample points.
**Integration with A-01 (agreement rule).** A-01 always computes the rank mobility. If the lattice is `Decided(n)` and n differs from the rank mobility at the sample points, that is an internal error: the analysis fails closed (exit 9 class: an integrity fault, never a reported number) and the case is added to the negative-control registry. If the lattice is `Undecided`, the report says so and gives the rank result alone. The report prints the Grübler–Kutzbach count beside both, labelled as a count that is wrong for over-constrained linkages.
**Known answers the code must reproduce** (from the pilot): Sarrus: two limbs with bonds planar gliding G(u₁), G(u₂); meet = line translation along u₁ × u₂; mobility 1 where the count gives 0. Bennett: `Undecided`; rank 1 where the count gives −2. Planar four-bar in space: every limb inside G(u); mobility 1 where the spatial count gives −2. A spherical four-bar (four revolute axes through one point): all joints in the spherical subgroup SO(3) about that point; lattice mobility 1 (inside SO(3) the count is 3·(4 − 1 − 4) + 4 = 1).
**Evidence.** `Exact` computations; the verdict is `Certified` when the bracket-closure certificates (theorem card T-11's route) are attached, else `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `meet`, `join` and `product_is_subgroup` are exact and correct on all pairs drawn from a catalogue of 40 subgroups (the twelve classes in several poses): `meet` is the greatest lower bound and `join` the least upper bound under inclusion (checked by brute-force subspace arithmetic).
- [ ] Sarrus returns `Decided(1)`; Bennett returns `Undecided`; the planar four-bar in space and the spherical four-bar return `Decided(1)`; in each decided case the rank mobility at three rational sample points agrees.
- [ ] A-01's report shows count, lattice verdict and rank for these linkages: (0, 1, 1), (−2, undecided, 1), (−2, 1, 1), (−2, 1, 1).
- [ ] The agreement rule is enforced: a planted lattice defect (meet replaced by join) makes Sarrus report a lattice value that differs from the rank, and the analysis fails closed instead of printing it.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: (i) treating a non-subgroup product as its generated subgroup gives Bennett a decided, wrong mobility and is caught by the agreement rule; (ii) intersecting screw spans without first expressing them in one frame gives a wrong meet for Sarrus and fails; (iii) a floating-point rank in the intersection misses the one-dimensional meet when the two axis directions are nearly parallel (angle 10⁻⁹) and fails the exact fixture.
- [ ] The feature is off by default; with it off A-01's output is unchanged from M1.
- [ ] If the pilot failed or the agreement rule is violated on the design corpus after one repair cycle, the bead is closed with the typed reason "fallback: rank only" and the record says so.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`lattice_meet_join_catalogue`; `product_is_subgroup_cases` (two parallel rotations with a perpendicular translation: true, planar gliding; two skew rotations: false; rotation and translation along its axis: true, cylindrical); `limb_bond_three_parallel_revolutes` (planar gliding, idle mobility 0); `sarrus_lattice_mobility_1`; `bennett_undecided`; `planar_fourbar_in_space_1`; `spherical_fourbar_1`; `agreement_rule_fails_closed`; `near_parallel_axes_exact_meet`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/marrow_lattice_mobility.sh`: builds the four linkage fixtures as designs (`frob design new` from fixture files with `closure` constructors), runs `frob analyze mobility <id> --json` with the feature on, and asserts the triple (count, lattice, rank) for each; runs the same with the feature off and asserts the rank alone. `STD-E2E` NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/marrow_lattice_mobility/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `cmd` lines with exit codes and wall time, envelope evidence blocks (class, tier `static`, determinism `exact`, identities), `metric` lines for the three numbers per linkage against the known answers, `assert` lines for the planted defect (analysis fails closed) and controls; on failure the subgroups involved in exact form; summary with counts.
### Known answers and negative controls
The mobility-count row and C-01's test: Sarrus mobility 1 where the count gives 0; Bennett mobility 1 where the count gives −2. KA-247 for the closure facts used. Controls (i) to (iii). Metamorphic: conjugating the whole linkage by a rational rigid transform changes no verdict; reordering limbs changes nothing. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations
- The verdict `Undecided` is a feature: paradoxical linkages are exactly where a structural method must not guess.
- Mobility is a property of a configuration; singular poses, unilateral contacts and mode changes need the stratified description used elsewhere, and A-01 samples several poses.
- The design corpus at M3 includes linkages with known mobility; the validation of static analyses on the corpus is where a disagreement would first show at scale.
