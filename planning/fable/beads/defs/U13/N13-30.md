## Background
Construction C-03: a rigid body's ten inertial parameters are equivalent to the 4×4 pseudo-inertia matrix 𝖯 = [[Σ, h], [hᵀ, m]] (Σ the second moment of mass, h the first moment, m the mass), and the parameters are physically consistent exactly when 𝖯 is positive definite. The cone of such matrices with its affine-invariant metric is a Riemannian symmetric space; the log-Euclidean parametrization S = log 𝖯 is an equivalent chart for optimizers and either may be used. Payoff: optimization and identification steps follow geodesics and cannot leave the feasible set, which replaces "ten free floats" and the projection-after-the-fact that an unconstrained fit needs. `frob-algebra` owns the pseudo-inertia cone with its affine-invariant operations; the cone type, validation and the mass-support predicate are Solid (BD-051); the Riemannian operations are Frontier and are this bead. Their consumer is identification in the calibration lane at M5 (the fit of inertial parameters from logged excitation).

Tier: Frontier. Ceiling, stated before measurement: identification steps that cannot leave the feasible set; no speed benefit. Pilot: N13-29 (the geodesic between two consistent pseudo-inertias stays positive definite; falsifier: an indefinite intermediate). The construction's own pilot for the use in identification, run with the calibration lane: recovery of planted inertial parameters of one link from simulated excitation against a projected Euclidean fit; falsifier: no fewer infeasible iterates and no better recovery at equal data; fallback: a Euclidean fit projected onto the cone. Kill and fallback of this mechanism: log-Cholesky parameterization with a positivity test (no separate kill-gate identifier; the decision is recorded with the pilots' records).

Rejected: unconstrained steps with rejection of infeasible iterates (wastes evaluations and biases the search near the boundary); eigenvalue clipping (changes the mass and the centre of mass silently).

## Scope
In: the metric, exponential and logarithm maps, geodesic, distance and Riemannian gradient on 4×4 symmetric positive-definite matrices; a deterministic small symmetric eigen-solver they need; the step API; re-checks after a step (positivity certificate hook, mass support); the log-Euclidean chart; the log-Cholesky fallback behind the same API; laws and tests.
Out: the cone type, validation and mass support (BD-051); the identification procedure and its excitation design (the calibration lane); the fill rule (frob-model).

## Technical approach
Feature `frontier-affine-inertia` (off by default). All matrices are 4×4 symmetric; P ≻ 0.
**Operations.**
- Metric at P: ⟨U, V⟩_P = tr(P⁻¹·U·P⁻¹·V) for symmetric tangent matrices U, V. It is invariant under congruence P ↦ G·P·Gᵀ for any invertible G, in particular under a change of body frame G = [[R, p], [0, 1]], so the geometry does not depend on where the body frame was put.
- Exponential map: Exp_P(U) = P^{1/2}·exp(P^{−1/2}·U·P^{−1/2})·P^{1/2}, positive definite for every symmetric U.
- Logarithm map: Log_P(Q) = P^{1/2}·log(P^{−1/2}·Q·P^{−1/2})·P^{1/2}.
- Geodesic: γ(t) = P^{1/2}·(P^{−1/2}·Q·P^{−1/2})^t·P^{1/2}; distance d(P, Q) = ‖log(P^{−1/2}·Q·P^{−1/2})‖_F.
- Riemannian gradient of a function f with Euclidean gradient ∇f (symmetrized): grad f = P·∇f·P. A descent step: P₊ = Exp_P(−η·grad f).
- Log-Euclidean chart: S = log P, P = exp S, with steps taken as S₊ = S − η·∇_S f; offered as the equivalent chart.
**Numerics (DESIGN).** Matrix square roots, logarithms and exponentials of 4×4 symmetric matrices use a cyclic Jacobi eigen-decomposition with a fixed sweep order and a fixed number of sweeps (deterministic; no external linear-algebra library; scalar functions through the deterministic elementary functions). Results are symmetrized. The implementation is host `f64`, determinism class D2 per platform.
**Step API.** `InertiaStep { chart: AffineInvariant | LogEuclidean | LogCholesky }::apply(P, direction, η) -> Result<P₊, Refusal>`. After every step: (1) positivity is re-verified by an LDLᵀ with positive pivots (in `f64`; an exact rational re-check is available when the result is rounded to rationals for a certificate, the route of theorem card T-19); (2) if the body has a declared mass envelope, the mass-support bound ‖c − o‖² + tr(I_C)/(2m) ≤ ρ² is re-checked, because a geodesic step preserves positivity and not the envelope; a violation returns the cone's refusal for mass support and the largest admissible η found by bisection as a repair.
**Fallback chart.** Log-Cholesky: P = L·Lᵀ with L lower triangular and positive diagonal, parameterized by the logarithms of its diagonal and its strict lower entries (ten parameters); any parameter vector gives P ≻ 0. It implements the same `InertiaStep` trait with a positivity test, so the consumer does not change if the fallback is adopted.
**Laws (property tests, 10⁶ cases, tolerances in ulp of the matrix scale).** Exp_P(Log_P(Q)) = Q; Log_P(Exp_P(U)) = U; γ(0) = P, γ(1) = Q; d(G·P·Gᵀ, G·Q·Gᵀ) = d(P, Q) for random invertible G (affine invariance); d is symmetric and satisfies the triangle inequality on random triples; every Exp result has positive LDLᵀ pivots; the metric is positive definite on symmetric tangents.
**Evidence.** Outputs are floating results with a positivity re-check; identification results that use them are `Estimated` (they fit data), never `Certified`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The operations exist behind the feature and satisfy the laws on 10⁶ random cases (condition numbers of P up to 10⁸), within recorded tolerances.
- [ ] The pilot's values are reproduced: for 𝖯₁ = diag(1/300, 1/1200, 1/1200, 1) and 𝖯₂ = diag(1/300, 1/75, 1/75, 4) the midpoint is diag(1/300, 1/300, 1/300, 2) within 4 ulp; the geodesic continued to t = 3 is positive definite; the Euclidean extrapolation from 𝖯₂ toward 𝖯₁ is indefinite beyond t = 16/15.
- [ ] On 10⁶ random (P, direction, η) with large steps, geodesic and log-Cholesky steps never leave the cone, while Euclidean steps of the same Euclidean length leave it in a recorded, non-zero fraction (the control showing the test can fail).
- [ ] Frame independence: stepping in a shifted body frame and transporting back equals stepping in the original frame within tolerance.
- [ ] A step that violates a declared mass envelope is refused with the admissible step length; a step with no envelope declared is not checked against any collision proxy.
- [ ] The eigen-solver is deterministic: identical bits twice on one machine and across thread counts; its residual ‖A − V·D·Vᵀ‖ is within 16 ulp of ‖A‖ on 10⁶ matrices.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: (i) a Riemannian gradient computed as ∇f without the P·∇f·P factor breaks frame independence; (ii) Exp implemented as P + U leaves the cone; (iii) an asymmetric intermediate (missing symmetrization) fails the symmetry assertion; (iv) the envelope re-check omitted lets a step leave a declared ball.
- [ ] If the pilots' outcomes select the fallback (N13-29's falsifier holds, or N10a-13 finds no fewer infeasible iterates and no better recovery than the projected Euclidean fit), the default chart is log-Cholesky and the capability is described by that name; the decision cites both pilots' `[[pilot.result]]` records in `pilots.toml`.

## Considerations
- The benefit is feasibility of iterates, not speed; if the calibration lane's pilot shows no fewer infeasible iterates and no better recovery than a projected Euclidean fit at equal data, the Frontier claim is withdrawn and the fallback (or the projected fit) stays.
- Near-singular P (a thin plate or a rod has a nearly rank-deficient second moment) makes the metric stiff; the step API reports the condition number and refuses beyond a declared limit instead of returning noise.
- Identified inertias are `Estimated` and enter cards with provenance; nothing here upgrades evidence.
