## Background
GATE-MARROW-06 (claim: Known answer; M1; defined with the periodic-and-lifted rule, which was added after the gate tables were compiled and is binding all the same): the spring fixture is exact to 10⁻¹² in double precision on the referee and within 10⁻⁵ relative on device tiers; snapshot, restore and teleport preserve k on 10⁴ random states; the mutant that omits k from the snapshot is caught by an energy jump of π² J; `sense.alias` returns the witness for a single-turn encoder and none for a multi-turn encoder.

The rule behind the gate: a revolute coordinate is a unit complex number plus an integer turn count k; a law is `periodic` if it reads only the unit complex number and `lifted` if it reads the angle θ = atan2(s, c) + 2π·k; a lifted law on a state without k loses state. The fixture: a joint with a linear torsion spring of 0.5 N·m/rad and rest angle 0, held at θ = 0 and at θ = 2π: identical link poses, zero velocity, and energies 0 and π² J (9.8696 J), torques 0 and −π N·m. An encoder that reports the angle modulo one turn does not observe k, so the two worlds deliver identical observations and need holding torques that differ by π N·m: an observation alias, whose remedies are a multi-turn encoder, a homing move and a torque reading.

This bead is the M1 end-to-end measurement on the referee tier: every clause of the gate that does not need a device tier or teleport. The device and teleport clauses are N13-37 at M2, and the gate row is complete only then. Tier: Solid. It is a test bead run for the record by a session that did not implement the mechanisms.

## Technical approach
**Fixtures** (`tests/fixtures/turn/`): `turn-spring.fbot`: a base, one link on a revolute joint about +z with rotational inertia 0.5 kg·m² about the axis, no gravity torque (axis vertical), a `TorsionSpring` of 0.5 N·m/rad with rest angle 0 declared lifted, and a position-hold actuator; variants with a single-turn absolute encoder card and with a multi-turn encoder card. `turn-wheel.fbot`: the same link as a free wheel with only periodic laws (viscous friction, a cogging torque periodic in the angle).
**Script stages** (plain shell driving `frob`; exact seeds; exits non-zero on failure).
1. *Declarations* (N13-34): `frob design check turn-spring` passes with turn state derived; the `untracked` variant is refused at stage 1 with the lifted-law refusal.
2. *Static known answers on the referee.* Two runs with the joint held stationary by the actuator: world A at θ = 0 (k = 0) and world B at θ = 2π ((c, s) = (1, 0), k = 1). `frob sim run turn-spring --tier referee --init <A|B> --seconds 0.1 --seed 1 --json`, then `frob telemetry energy <run> --json`. Assert: spring energy 0 J and π² = 9.869604401089358 J; spring torque 0 N·m and −π = −3.141592653589793 N·m; holding torque of the actuator 0 and +π N·m; each to 10⁻¹² relative (absolute 10⁻¹² for the zeros); link pose digests of A and B equal; state digests of A and B different.
3. *Dynamic check.* Release world B from rest: with inertia 0.5 kg·m² and stiffness 0.5 N·m/rad the natural frequency is 1 rad/s and θ(t) = 2π·cos t. At t = π/2 the joint passes θ = 0 with rate −2π rad/s and kinetic energy ½ × 0.5 × (2π)² = π² J; k has gone from 1 to 0 exactly once; total energy is conserved within the referee's stated tolerance for the step used (recorded); the turn count is the same whatever the step size among 2 ms, 1 ms and 0.5 ms.
4. *Snapshot and restore preserve k.* On 10⁴ random states of a twelve-joint variant (random k in [−1,000, 1,000], random (c, s), 5% of states within 10⁻⁹ of the cut and 1% exactly on it): snapshot, restore, compare: byte-equal state and equal digest (through the referee's snapshot and restore interface in a test binary, since user-level replay verbs arrive at M2).
5. *The omitted-k mutant.* A build of the snapshot path with k omitted (planted through the conformance registry's control mechanism): restore world B and read the energy ledger: stored energy jumps from π² J to 0, a jump of 9.8696 J reported at the restore with the spring as the first offending element. The script asserts the jump is detected and equals π² J within 10⁻⁹ relative.
6. *The periodic control.* `turn-wheel`: run 10 s through several turns; snapshot mid-run; strip k; restore; continue: the remaining run's digests equal the uninterrupted run's bit for bit. This shows the rule binds only lifted laws.
7. *Alias witness.* `frob sense alias turn-spring --worlds A,B --encoder single-turn --json`: returns an alias witness: the two worlds give identical observation histories under the hold command and require holding torques that differ by π N·m; the listed remedies include a multi-turn encoder, a homing move and a torque reading. With `--encoder multi-turn`: none found (the encoder reads k).
8. *Record.* Write the referee rows of GATE-MARROW-06 with measured values; the gate's status remains `not run` until the device rows exist.
**Evidence.** Claim: Known answer. Tier `referee`; determinism class of the referee; identities of design, model and runs in every envelope.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Stage 2: energies 0 and π² J, torques 0 and −π N·m, to 10⁻¹² in double precision on the referee; equal pose digests, different state digests.
- [ ] Stage 3: kinetic energy π² J at θ = 0 within the recorded integrator tolerance; exactly one decrement of k; the same k history at the three step sizes.
- [ ] Stage 4: 10⁴ of 10⁴ states preserve k through snapshot and restore.
- [ ] Stage 5: the mutant is caught by an energy jump of π² J.
- [ ] Stage 6: the periodic wheel replays bit-identically with k dropped.
- [ ] Stage 7: an alias witness for the single-turn encoder with the three remedies; none for the multi-turn encoder.
- [ ] The partial gate row is recorded with measured values, commit, fingerprint, seed, command and receipt hash; the log meets the logging standard.
- [ ] Negative controls caught in addition to the mutant: a spring that reads only the branch angle fails stage 2 (reads 0 J in world B); an alias search that compares link poses instead of observation histories and required actions reports "none" for the single-turn case and fails stage 7.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
A dry-run test of the script; a fixture test that `turn-spring.fbot` and `turn-wheel.fbot` compile and that their declared access kinds are lifted and periodic respectively.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/turn_count.sh --tier referee`, sourcing the `STD-E2E` helper. NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/turn_count/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Each `cmd` line has the exact command, exit code and wall time; each envelope's evidence block is copied (claim, class, tier `referee`, determinism, `DesignId`, `ModelId`, run identity, seed); `metric` lines carry measured energies and torques with units against π² J and π N·m and the tolerance 10⁻¹², the count of states preserved against 10⁴, the mutant's energy jump, the k history per step size; digests are logged wherever state is compared, with the first differing field and the snapshot artifact path on failure; `refusal` lines for stage 1; the alias witness is logged as its two world identities and the differing required torque, not as raw scene data; the final `summary` gives assertions passed and failed and the gate touched (GATE-MARROW-06, referee rows).
### Known answers and negative controls
Energy 0 and π² J; torque 0 and −π N·m; kinetic energy π² J at the zero crossing; the periodic wheel. Controls: the omitted-k mutant, the branch-angle spring, the pose-comparing alias search. Metamorphic: adding 2π·n to the initial angle of the wheel fixture changes nothing; adding it to the spring fixture changes energy by ½·κ·((θ + 2π·n)² − θ²). Determinism lanes: the script twice on one machine gives identical digests; debug and release agree.

## Considerations
