## Background
A known answer is an analytic or hand-checkable truth with a number, a tolerance, an execution tier and, where one exists, a negative control that must make its test fail. The known-answer suite is tier G1 of the verification program (G0 for laws and exact identities), registered in `frob-conformance`; a capability counts as implemented only when its known-answer tests pass against a pinned oracle that was never regenerated from the candidate.

This bead is the registry for the algebraic core: the thirty-three rows KA-228 to KA-260, plus the known answers of the periodic-and-lifted rule, which were stated with their subsection after the suite's table was compiled and have no number yet. Each row carries its case, its truth with formula or value, tolerance, tier and site, negative control, and the bead whose test implements it. Mechanism beads cite these numbers and restate the values they need inline; this bead makes the list itself an artifact and checks that every row has a live test. Tier of the content: Solid.

## Scope
In: the registry rows and their derivation files; the completeness check; the request for the numbers of the unnumbered turn-count answers and their mapping; the plants named in the control texts of the rows that seed N13-18's mutation records; tracking of tolerances marked `to set`.
Out: the row schema, loader, five rules and printout (N38a-12); running rows on tiers and the gate's accounting (N38a-13); the number blocks (N38a-40); the seeding of controls from the stated control cells, which writes `controls.toml` and the rows' `control.nc` (N38b-05); the tests (owners in the last column); the counterexample registry (N13-CX); the second controls that the suite's obligations bead proposes for the six rows printed with "none stated" (N38b-30, which depends on this bead, checks every control of these rows against the binding rule for controls and registers its proposals as further control records with the row's number as `seed`; it edits no row).

## Technical approach
**Registry form.** One `[[ka]]` row per KA in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml`, in the row schema that N38a-12 owns with its loader and five rules: `id`, `section` (with `owner` = the gate or obligation that owns the row), `fixture`, `same_as`, `case`, `truth`, `tolerance` (`exact`, `abs`, `rel`, `printed-digits`, `gate` or `to-set`), `tier`, `sites`, `control` (`text` and the `nc` list of the negative-control registry, filled by the seeding tool of N38b-05), `derivation` (`docs/derivations/<fixture>.md`), `label` (DESIGN for hand derivations), `due`, `printed`. The columns below map as: "Tier · site" → `tier`, `sites`; "Negative control" → `control.text`; "Test owner" → the derivation file, whose front matter lists the KA ids it serves and, for each, the test that checks it (crate and test name) and the owning bead. KA-238 is written with `same_as = "KA-044"` and the same fixture. Two more rows test a physical case that the constructions' registry already carries and share its `fixture` and derivation file, without `same_as` because the plan prints them as separate answers: KA-228 with KA-049 (the planar two-link chain) and KA-230 with KA-070 (the quadruped with a front-only lidar); N10-KA1 marks the same three fixtures from its side. This bead writes the rows and their derivation files and never a row of `controls.toml`. A row is `eligible` only with a derivation, a tolerance and a registered control (N38a-12); a `to-set` row stays `obligation: tolerance` until its value is recorded. Results are reported per site by the runner (N38a-13), not stored in the row. A meta-test fails if a derivation file names a test that does not exist once its owning bead has closed (before that the row is listed as pending on that bead), if a test tagged with one of these numbers has no row, or if a tolerance is still `to-set` when its owning gate is recorded.
**Plants in control texts (for the mutation records of N13-18).** A control text that names one of the eight Marrow mutations names it by its `MutationId` (M1 to M8 of N13-18's operations table), so that the seeding tool resolves it to one control per plant. In seeding order: KA-243 names M1 (CX-116's control) and M3, and is the row from which M3 (a pull-back in place of a push-forward at a use site, which turns the planar 2R fixture's torques (−3, −3) N·m into (+3, +3) N·m) is seeded, with seed `KA-243`; KA-244 names M3 (that record, reused) and M5, and is the row from which M5 (linear and angular parts exchanged in a twist), which no catalogue entry states, is seeded; KA-247 seeds M6 (the bracket's arguments exchanged); KA-249 seeds M8 (a conjugate missing in the projector); KA-229 and KA-245 name M1, KA-242 names M2 (CX-020's control), KA-248 names M4 (CX-100's control), KA-254 names M7 (CX-102's control), KA-258 names M8 (KA-249's control); KA-239 and KA-256 ("the eight planted mutations") seed none. CX-117's shortcut (the lower three rows of a space screw Jacobian taken as the point Jacobian, torques (0, 0) on the same 2R fixture) is a different defect from M3: it keeps its own use-site plant, which BD-047's `planar_2r_lower_rows_shortcut_is_wrong` builds, and it is KA-228's control. KA-228's control text names it by that plant, `BD-047:cx-117` (N13-CX), as the cells of the eight mutations name their `MutationId`, so that the seeding tool, which matches by plant (N38b-05 step 3), reuses CX-117's control and allocates no second one for the same plant. Their printed cell is GATE-MARROW-04's clause about the whole battery (each mutation caught by at least three fixtures), and the binding rule for controls (N38b-28) admits to a row's `control.nc` only a control whose planting makes that row fail. Checked against N13-18's operations table: the bit-parity fixture of BD-010 is a catching fixture for M2, M5 and M6 only, so KA-256's `control.nc` lists those three, plus BD-010's own control (ii) (Ad_g replaced by Ad_{g⁻¹}), which BD-010 registers with its fixture test; none of the eight lists a group-axiom law among its fixtures, so KA-239 carries the printed text and admits none of them, and its control is N38b-30's record: the SE(3) inverse with its translation not rotated, (Rᵀ, −p) in place of (Rᵀ, −Rᵀ·p), so that g·g⁻¹ has translation p − R·p = (1, −1, 0) m for R a rotation by π/2 about z and p = (1, 0, 0) m; while no admitted control is registered, N38a-12's loader reports KA-239 `obligation: control`. The eight records attach to GATE-MARROW-04 through their seeds, which is where the printed cell's claim is checked. A cell that names several plants is resolved by N38b-05's seeding (its step 3) one plant at a time, reusing a control already seeded with the same plant and the same target, whether its seed is a CX entry or an earlier KA row.
