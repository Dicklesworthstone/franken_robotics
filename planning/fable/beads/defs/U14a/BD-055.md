## Background

A design (`frob.design/1`, extension `.fbot`) is a canonical JSON term (BD-054). The body, joint and
end-effector blocks are N14a-03 to N14a-05. This bead writes the remaining blocks of the format's field
reference: the `fill` of a body, and the whole-robot blocks `power_bus`, `sensors`, `payloads`, `budgets`,
`levers`, `locks` and `symmetry`. Every field the compiler reads is listed; a field not listed is refused
(`FROB-REF-103`).

Four architecture decisions are fixed in these blocks.

- ADR-SKEL-03: no physics field has a silent default. Fill, material, contact law, joint limits and an
  actuator's current convention must be written. Reason: each default yields a plausible wrong robot. The
  mass case: a torso box of 0.42 × 0.20 × 0.10 m in an alloy at 2,700 kg/m³ weighs 22.7 kg read as a solid
  block and 1.92 kg read as a 2.5 mm shell, a factor of 11.8. A default of `solid` would pass every check and
  size every actuator for a robot that does not exist. Rejected alternative: default to solid and warn; a
  warning in a report is not read by an optimizer.
- ADR-SKEL-06: shared resources are parts of the design. The power bus and the shared thermal sink are
  written in the term, because they couple limbs that the kinematic tree separates.
- ADR-SKEL-07: optimizers move levers only. Continuous optimization and co-design may change a parameter only
  if the design declares it as a lever with bounds and a manifold kind; agents may make any typed edit.
  Reason: an optimizer that can touch every number finds the numbers nobody meant to free.
- ADR-SKEL-04: symmetry is computed, and a declaration is an assertion. The `symmetry` block states the
  groups the designer expects; the compiler computes them and refuses on a mismatch; the declared value never
  overrides the computed one. Reason: a trusted declaration is a way to tie policy weights under a false group.

## Scope

In: schema, typing, canonical defaults and stage-1 refusals of `fill`, `power_bus`, `sensors`, `payloads`,
`budgets`, `levers`, `locks`, `symmetry`.

Out: the moments each fill produces (BD-062); declared and massless bodies at stage 2 (N14a-13); the lever
interface for optimizers and `unlock` (N14a-06); the comparison of a symmetry assertion with the computed
groups (N14a-11); budget sums and the stage-6 check (N14a-10 and the mass roll-up analysis); bus physics
(BD-075); sensor models and clocks (the sensing chapter's beads).

## Technical approach

Layout (DESIGN): `crates/frob-design/src/blocks/{fill.rs, power.rs, sensors.rs, payloads.rs, budgets.rs,
levers.rs, symmetry.rs}`.

**Fill.** How the Region is occupied. There is no default.

| Fill | Mass and inertia | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `solid` | Density times the Region's volume moments | Wheels, feet, small brackets, counterweights |
| `{solid: {intended: true}}` | As `solid` | A solid with a statement of intent: the way past the solid-fill plausibility refusal (`FROB-REF-168`, N13-04, under the default option B of decision N13-03, whose ADR may respell it). A distinct canonical form, so it has its own `DesignId` |
| `{shell: t}` | Density times the moments of the Region minus those of its inward offset by t | Torsos, housings, tubes, limb segments |
| `{lattice: φ}` | A fraction φ of the solid moments | Printed infill; a first-order model that states its isotropy assumption |
| `declared` | Given mass properties with provenance; the Region is the collision default and the mass support | Imported robots; parts with measured mass |
| `massless` | None | Frames and markers only |

Fill rules at typing: a body with no `fill` member refuses (`FROB-REF-105`). In the intent form, `intended` must
be the literal `true` (anything else is `FROB-REF-103`). `t` is a positive length; a shell
thicker than half the Region's smallest dimension d_min (N14a-03) refuses as a solid written as a shell
(`FROB-REF-105`): the test is t > d_min/2 in exact rational comparison. φ is a dimensionless exact number in
(0, 1].

**Whole-robot blocks.**

| Block | Fields | Required | Meaning and rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | `power_bus`: `id`, `battery`, `nominal`, `current_max`, `regen`, `harness_resistance`, `brownout`, `sink`, `members` | If any actuator exists | The shared electrical bus and the shared thermal sink. `battery` names a battery card; `nominal` is a voltage; `current_max` a current; `regen` is one of `to-battery`, `shunt`, `clip`; `sink` is `{"conductance": W/K}`; `members` defaults to every actuator and is written out (as the selector `"*"`) |
| Sensing | `sensors[]`: `id`, `card`, `parent`, `mount`, `rate`, `clock`, `fov`, `latency` | No | `parent` is a body or a pattern (`"joints:*"`, `"feet:*"`); `mount` a frame in the parent; rates are rationals in hertz. A sensor without a parent frame, a rate or a clock refuses (`FROB-REF-126`). Sensors enter the observation-preserving symmetry group |
| Payload | `payloads[]`: body fields, `mount`, `removable` | No | Bodies a task may add or remove. A payload enters the mass roll-up and both symmetry groups |
| Budgets | `budgets`: `mass_max`, `cost_max` with its currency, `power_peak_max`, `energy_min`, `envelope_max`, `payload_min` | No | Limits checked at preflight stage 6 (`FROB-REF-115`). Cost is summed from card prices, each with its provenance |
| Levers | `levers[]`: `id`, `path`, `bounds` or `choices`, `step`, `manifold`, `prior`, `locked` | No | The parameters an optimizer may move. `manifold` is one of `R`, `R+`, `S1`, `S2`, `SE3`, `inertia-cone`, `finite`. A value outside its bounds refuses (`FROB-REF-111`) |
| Locks | `locks` | No | Paths or block names that no edit may change without an explicit `unlock` (`FROB-REF-111`) |
| Symmetry | `symmetry`: `assert_mech`, `assert_obs` | No | Group names (`"C2xC2"`, `"C2"`, `"D6"`) or generator lists. An assertion; a mismatch refuses with the breaker (`FROB-REF-109`, N14a-11) |

Typing steps beyond the table:

1. Bus. If the term contains any `actuator` and no `power_bus`, refuse with `FROB-REF-103` naming the missing
   block (DESIGN: the field reference makes the block required whenever an actuator exists but names no code
   for a missing required block; 103 is the code of the field set, and the repair adds the block with the
   battery card left for the caller to choose, never filled in). Several buses are written as an array with distinct `id`s; then every bus writes `members` and the
   members partition the actuators (an actuator on two buses is the copied power port of N14a-02).
2. Sensors. `id` unique; `rate` parsed as an exact rational in hertz (`"1000 Hz"`); `clock` an identifier.
   Missing `parent`, `rate` or `clock`: `FROB-REF-126`, with the missing member named.
3. Payloads. Typed as bodies (N14a-03, with their own fill); `mount` names a mechanical port;
   `removable` defaults to `false`, written out with provenance `default`.
4. Budgets. Each limit has its dimension (mass, currency, power, energy, a length triple, mass).
5. Levers. `path` must resolve to a parameter of the term; an unresolved path refuses with `FROB-REF-103`
   (it names a field that does not exist). Exactly one of `bounds` and `choices`. Bounds have the parameter's
   dimension and lo < hi. The parameter's current value lies inside the bounds, or is one of the choices;
   otherwise `FROB-REF-111` with the nearest admissible value in `required`. `manifold` agrees with the
   field: `finite` if and only if `choices`; `R+` requires lo > 0. `locked` defaults to `false`, written out.
   Codes of an inadmissible declaration (DESIGN: the field reference names none; each follows the meaning of
   the code): both or neither of `bounds` and `choices` is `FROB-REF-103` (the field set); bounds of another
   dimension than the parameter's are `FROB-REF-102`; lo ≥ hi, `R+` with lo ≤ 0, and a manifold that disagrees
   with the field (`finite` with `bounds`, `choices` with another manifold) are `FROB-REF-111`, pointer the
   lever's `bounds` or `manifold`, first repair `declare-lever` with an admissible declaration.
6. Locks. Each entry is a path or a block name (`"power_bus"`, `"payloads"`, `"budgets/envelope_max"`) that
   resolves; unresolved entries refuse with `FROB-REF-103`.
7. Symmetry. Each assertion is a group name from the registered list or a list of generators (isometries in
   exact form). The block is stored and passed to N14a-11; nothing else may read it.

Refusals issued here (exit code 3; ranked repairs as typed edits):

| Code | Meaning | Triggers here | Ranked repairs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FROB-REF-105` | A body without a fill; a solid written as a shell | No `fill`; shell thickness above d_min/2; non-positive thickness; φ outside (0, 1] | Generated by BD-064's lints (`fill-missing`, `shell-as-solid`) and their ranking rule: 1. `set-fill {shell: t₀}`, t₀ the largest of {1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3} mm not above d_min/4, offered only as a suggestion to accept; 2. `set-fill solid`; 3. `set-fill declared`; for an over-thick shell: `set-fill solid` first, then `set-fill {shell: t₀}` |
| `FROB-REF-111` | A lever outside its bounds; an edit to a locked field | A lever whose current value is outside its bounds or not among its choices; an inadmissible lever declaration (step 5) | 1. `set-lever` to the nearest bound; 2. `declare-lever` with widened bounds (for an inadmissible declaration, `declare-lever` with an admissible one first) |
| `FROB-REF-126` | A sensor without a parent frame, a rate or a clock | A missing member of a sensor entry | `add-sensor` with the missing member filled from the sensor card's nominal value, listed for acceptance |
| `FROB-REF-103` | Unknown, misspelt or duplicate field | An unresolved lever or lock path; a missing bus block; an unknown member | `rename`; remove; add the block |

Determinism class D0. Every written-out default carries provenance `default` (canonical-form rule 6).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed condition: a body with no fill refuses with `FROB-REF-105`.
- [ ] Seed condition: a shell thicker than half the Region's smallest dimension refuses with `FROB-REF-105`:
      on the worked torso (d_min = 0.10 m), `{shell: "51 mm"}` refuses and `{shell: "50 mm"}` and
      `{shell: "2.5 mm"}` are admitted.
- [ ] Seed condition: a lever outside its bounds refuses with `FROB-REF-111`: the lever `thigh` with bounds
      [0.15, 0.30] m on a thigh length of 0.32 m refuses and names 0.30 m.
- [ ] The worked design's whole-robot blocks parse and canonicalize: bus `main` (22.2 V, 60 A, `to-battery`,
      sink 1.2 W/K) with `"members":"*"` written out; four sensors with rates 500, 1000, 500 and 10 Hz;
      budgets 14 kg, 4000 USD, 900 W and the envelope triple; three levers; assertions `C2xC2` and `C2`.
- [ ] A sensor entry with no `clock` refuses with `FROB-REF-126`; with no `rate` likewise.
- [ ] A design with actuators and no `power_bus` refuses, naming the block.
- [ ] A lever whose `path` names no parameter, and a lock that names no field, each refuse with `FROB-REF-103`.
- [ ] A `finite` lever without `choices`, an `R+` lever with a lower bound of 0, and a lever with lo ≥ hi each
      refuse with `FROB-REF-111`; a lever with both `bounds` and `choices` refuses with `FROB-REF-103`; bounds
      in kilograms on a length refuse with `FROB-REF-102`.
- [ ] Removing any written-out default from a canonical file and re-canonicalizing restores the same bytes.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-263: a shell thicker than half the Region's smallest dimension is refused as a solid written as a shell
  (`FROB-REF-105`); exact verdict. Control added here: a check that compares against the largest dimension
  must fail the 51 mm fixture.
- KA-284 (first rejection fixture of the sensor family): measurements with no timestamp or an unknown frame
  are refused at stage 1 (`FROB-REF-126`); here a sensor with no clock or no parent.
- Counterexample CX-125 (a body with no stated fill can default to solid): the torso weighs 22.7 kg solid and
  1.92 kg as a 2.5 mm shell; the planted mutant that defaults to solid must fail `missing_fill_refuses`.
- Negative control (planted): lever bounds compared in binary64. The exact boundary: 0.30 m admitted and
  `"0.30000000000000001 m"` refused. That value rounds to the same binary64 as 0.3 (the nearest doubles are
  0.29999999999999998890 and 0.30000000000000004441), so the mutant admits it and must fail the test; a value
  such as 0.3000000001 m would not catch the mutant, because binary64 still separates it from 0.3.
- Determinism lanes: identical bytes and refusals twice, debug and release, Linux and macOS (class D0).

## Considerations

- The repair for a missing fill never applies itself. It proposes a shell with the deterministic wall t₀ of
  BD-064 (no part-class table exists; N13-03 records that), and the designer or agent must accept it as a typed
  edit; applying it silently would be the default the format forbids.
- A solid-filled body that alone exceeds one quarter of `mass_max`: the design language's advisory is BD-064's
  `solid-heavy` lint (stage 1, on primitive Regions); under decision N13-03's default option B the same body
  written `solid` is refused at stage 2 with `FROB-REF-168` (N13-04) unless written in the intent form. Both need
  the computed mass, so neither is raised by this bead's typing.
- OPEN-SKEL-2 (where card prices come from and under what licence): prices are `assumed`, the cost budget is
  advisory, and `--strict-data` refuses a cost verdict. Owner: project lead. It blocks nothing before the
  build-packet work.
- OPEN-SKEL-4 (whether the `lattice` fill needs anisotropy): isotropic fraction only, with the assumption
  printed in the check report. Owner: Foundry lead.
- A design may assert only `assert_mech`, only `assert_obs`, or neither; absence asserts nothing and the
  computed groups are still reported.
