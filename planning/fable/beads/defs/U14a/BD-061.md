## Background

`frob-model` (layer 2, tier Solid) is the compiler of Skeleton. Its pipeline: Design → elaborate → validate →
five functors → `RobotModel` → lowerings. Construction C-12 (a robot is a composition over typed ports) gives
a design its meaning through five functors, structure-preserving maps from design terms to: frames
(kinematic), collision Regions and inertia (geometric), actuator and sensor channels (binding), resource
relations (budget), and groups (symmetry). This bead builds the first functor, the container all five fill,
and the first of the model's four lowerings.

The kinematic functor maps the expanded tree to the frame groupoid: a topologically numbered tree of bodies,
a screw basis and a reference transform for every joint, a table of named frames, and the closure pairs of
kinematic loops. Its memo key is the hash of the subtree's mechanical fields, so that renaming a body or
changing a fill recomputes no kinematics.

`RobotModel` is the compiled robot: immutable, with an identity `ModelId`. INV-SKEL-01: a `RobotModel` is
immutable and fully determined by its `DesignId`, the compiler version and the card versions it references.
The model lowers four ways: (1) to an articulated model of the referee tier, through the airlock; (2) to
packed structure-of-arrays buffers for the batched tiers; (3) to analysis views; (4) to a variant record
inside a size bucket. This bead does (1). The referee is the most trusted simulation tier: FrankenSim's
articulated dynamics in double precision, reached only through the airlock crate `frob-fsim`, which is the
one crate allowed to name FrankenSim's volatile types.

DESIGN reasons. Frames are typed (`a_from_b` composes only with `b_from_c`), so a transform cannot be
composed in the wrong order silently. The model is immutable so that a result can cite it by identity.
The referee is reached through an adapter so that a change of FrankenSim's API costs one crate.

## Scope

In: the kinematic functor; the `RobotModel` container and its assembly; `ModelId`; INV-SKEL-01; host-side
forward kinematics as the reference; the referee lowering and a forward-kinematics call through it.

Out: geometry and inertia (BD-062), which fills the model's inertial section and the referee's inertias;
binding, budget, symmetry functors (N14a-09 to N14a-11); analysis views and the variant record (N14a-12);
packed lowering (BD-148); memoization and invalidation (BD-063); mobility of closures (the mobility
analysis); contact sets on the referee (BD-079).

## Technical approach

Layout (DESIGN): `crates/frob-model/src/{kin/{tree.rs, joint.rs, frames.rs, fk.rs}, model.rs, id.rs,
lower/referee.rs}`.

```rust
// DESIGN sketch: intent, not signatures.
pub struct KinTree { bodies: Vec<BodyNode>,             // index i has parent λ(i) < i; body 0 is the root
                     joints: Vec<JointNode>,            // joint i connects λ(i) to i
                     levels: Vec<Range<u32>>,           // bodies grouped by depth, for level-parallel kernels
                     frames: FrameTable,                // named frames: ports, sensor mounts, end effectors
                     closures: Vec<Closure>, n_q: u32, n_v: u32 }
pub struct JointNode { subgroup: JointSubgroup,         // Hervé class and screw basis (from the Lie library)
                       parent_from_joint: ExactIsometry,// the joint's `origin`, at the reference configuration
                       limits: Limits, home: Vec<Qty>, coord: CoordLayout, parity: i8 }
pub struct RobotModel { id: ModelId, compiler: CompilerVersion, cards: Vec<CardVersion>,
                        kin: KinTree, /* inertial, binding, budget, symmetry sections: other beads */ }
```

The kinematic functor, as numbered steps:

1. Read the expanded tree (N14a-07) through the tracked view only. Keep its instance order: parents before
   children, so the parent index λ(i) is less than i. Precompute depth levels.
2. Root. The root body is attached to the world by a `free` joint (a floating base, six velocity
   coordinates). The world frame is right-handed, z up, metres; gravity points along −z.
3. For each non-root instance build a `JointNode` from its Joint block (N14a-04): the screw basis and Hervé
   class through the classifier of the Lie library; `parent_from_joint` from `origin` composed with the
   constructor placement; limits and home. An end effector or payload written without a joint hangs on a
   `fixed` joint. By convention the child's body frame is the joint frame at the reference configuration.
4. Mirror images carry `parity = −1`. Their rotation axes were already mapped as axial vectors at
   elaboration; the functor records the sign so that later stages know which coordinates, limits and gear
   signs are mirrored.
5. Coordinates. A revolute coordinate is laid out as a unit complex number plus an integer turn count; a
   prismatic one as a length; `n_q` and `n_v` are the totals. No raw angle is a state coordinate.
6. Frames. Enter every named frame (body origins, mechanical ports, sensor mounts, end-effector frames) into
   the frame table as (body, body_from_frame), exact. Model-indexed frames are checked at model compile time:
   every transform composition the model stores cancels inner names.
7. Closures. Each `closure(port_a, port_b)` becomes a `Closure` between two frames with the subgroup the
   closing joint allows. Closures are recorded, never solved here.
8. Forward kinematics (host reference). For joint coordinates θ, with world_from_body(0) the base pose,
   world_from_body(i) = world_from_body(λ(i)) · parent_from_joint(i) · ∏ₖ exp(θᵢₖ·Sᵢₖ). A pose is
   g = (R, p) and acts as x_a = R·x_b + p. Evaluated in double precision with the deterministic elementary
   functions; where every angle is a multiple of a quarter turn it is also evaluated exactly in rationals.
9. Store with the product its digest, its memo key (hash of the subtree's mechanical fields: joint class,
   screws, origin, limits, home, constructor placements) and its read set.

`ModelId` (DESIGN). The BLAKE3 hash, under the domain string `frob.model/1`, of: the digests of the model's
mechanical sections in a fixed order, the compiler version, and the sorted card versions. Section digests are
taken over a defined byte encoding of each product (exact rationals as reduced integer pairs, fixed field
order), never over a struct's memory. Names and the identifier map are outside the hash: no mechanical product
reads them, so two designs with different `DesignId` can share a `ModelId`, and INV-SKEL-01 still holds.

The referee lowering, as numbered steps:

1. Build a FrankenRobotics-native `RefereeModelSpec` (links with parent indices, one-axis joint kinds,
   axes, origins, and inertias when BD-062 has filled them) and hand it to `frob_fsim::RefereeDynamics`,
   which constructs FrankenSim's `fs_mbd::articulated::ArticulatedModel`. No FrankenSim type appears in
   `frob-model`.
2. Joint classes. FACT (to be re-checked by the admission test): the referee's foundation has joint kinds
   fixed, revolute, prismatic and helical only. `fixed`, `R`, `P`, `H` lower one to one. `C` lowers to a
   prismatic and a revolute on one line; `E` to two prismatics and a revolute; `S` to three revolutes with
   concurrent orthogonal axes; a non-root `free` to three prismatics and three revolutes. Intermediate links
   of a product are massless. This is the interim posture until the upstream work item FD-FSIM-01 (spherical,
   universal, planar and cylindrical joints as native kinds, M2) lands in FrankenSim's repository under its
   rules; the switch-over
   rule is to re-run the kinematics and dynamics parity gates and delete the products.
3. Record the coordinate map from model coordinates to referee coordinates (index and sign), so that states
   and telemetry convert at exactly one boundary.
4. Convert exact frames to double precision once, here. Conventions cross unchanged: twists are (ω, v) with
   the angular part first, wrenches (τ, f), transforms `a_from_b`.
5. The adapter converts and never reinterprets: a referee refusal becomes a typed refusal carrying the
   upstream reason and is never retried with weakened inputs. A memoized referee product carries the
   stack-lock digest in its key.

Determinism: the functor and `ModelId` are class D0 (exact). Forward kinematics in double precision is
bit-identical run to run on one platform; the referee claims same-platform determinism only. Evidence class
of outputs: `Tested`. Ledger: a compile record with the keys served and computed (BD-063's record).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed condition: forward kinematics of a template quadruped (the worked design `quad-a`, 12 actuated
      coordinates on a floating base, 18 velocity coordinates) runs on the referee through the airlock and
      agrees with the host reference within 10⁻¹² m and 10⁻¹² rad (TARGET) at 100 seeded configurations
      inside the joint limits.
- [ ] The three-revolute leg fixture gives its hand values (below) exactly at quarter turns and within
      10⁻¹² m otherwise.
- [ ] Body numbering is topological (λ(i) < i) and depth levels partition the bodies, on the worked designs
      and 10³ random designs.
- [ ] INV-SKEL-01: the same `DesignId`, compiler version and card versions give the same `ModelId` and
      bit-identical sections, twice, in debug and release, on Linux and macOS.
- [ ] A `rename` of a body leaves `ModelId` unchanged; a change of any joint origin, axis or limit changes it.
- [ ] `C`, `E` and `S` joints lower to products with a recorded coordinate map, or refuse with a typed
      referee refusal that names the upstream reason; nothing is silently approximated.
- [ ] No type from a FrankenSim crate appears in a public signature of `frob-model` (`xtask check-layers`).
- [ ] Cancellation of a compile leaves no partial model (request, drain, finalize).
- [ ] World poses of named frames are returned by the crate API (`RobotModel::frame_poses(q)`) on the worked
      designs; their reachability through `frob design show --frames --json` is asserted by N14a-21, which
      depends on this bead.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-model -- kin model lower` (several filters go after `--`).

- `planar_2r_tool_position`: links 0.4 m and 0.3 m, angles 0 and 1/4 turn; expected tool at (0.4, 0.3, 0) m.
- `leg_3r_quarter_turns_exact`: thigh and shank 0.21 m along −z, hip pitch 1/4 turn, knee −1/4 turn, both
  about +y; expected foot at (−0.21, 0, −0.21) m relative to the hip, exactly.
- `leg_3r_generic`: hip pitch 1/8 turn, knee −1/4 turn; expected foot at (0, 0, −0.21·√2) =
  (0, 0, −0.296985) m, within 10⁻¹² m.
- `quad_a_feet_mirror_images`: at a configuration mirrored across both planes the four feet are images of
  one another under the two reflections, within 10⁻¹² m.
- `topological_numbering_and_levels`.
- `model_id_stable_and_name_free`: INV-SKEL-01 and the rename case.
- `referee_fk_matches_host`: the seed condition, on the referee through `frob-fsim`.
- `spherical_joint_lowering_map`: an `S` joint lowers to three revolutes; the coordinate map round-trips a
  rotation away from the chart's singularity.
- `frame_table_compositions_cancel`: every stored composition has matching inner frame names.
- `prop_fk_composition` (10³ `synthetic` trees): world_from_body(i) equals the product along the path to the
  root computed independently from the frame table.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_author_compile.sh` (N14a-27): `frob design show quad-a --frames --at home --tier referee
--json` and the same with `--tier host`; asserts agreement of every named frame within the tolerance, and the
hand values of the leg fixture. NDJSON lines carry the command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (class, tier, determinism, `model_id`), the largest position and angle difference with its
threshold, and on failure the first frame that differs with both poses.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answer (hand-checkable): rotation about +y by θ maps the direction (0, 0, −1) to
  (−sin θ, 0, −cos θ). With θ₂ = 45° and θ₂ + θ₃ = −45° the two link directions are
  (−0.70711, 0, −0.70711) and (0.70711, 0, −0.70711), and the foot is 0.21 × their sum = (0, 0, −0.29698) m.
- Known answer shared with the Lie library: the planar two-link arm with links 0.4 m and 0.3 m at angles 0
  and π/2 has its tool at (0.4, 0.3) m.
- Negative control 1 (planted): transforms composed in the reverse order. The 2R fixture gives (0.3, 0.4).
- Negative control 2 (planted): a mirrored axis left unmapped (polar instead of axial). The mirror-image
  test fails on the hip-abduction joint.
- Negative control 3 (planted): `ModelId` hashed over a struct's memory. It differs between debug and
  release and the INV-SKEL-01 test fails.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; 1 and 36 threads; debug and release; Linux and macOS, each
  against its class (exact products D0; referee values same-platform only).

## Considerations

- The referee improves as FrankenSim lands its changes (more joint kinds, loop constraints); each is an
  upstream work item with an interim posture. Until loop constraints exist there, closures are compliant
  equality rows on the fast tiers and are not exact on the referee.
- A fixed-base mechanism (an arm bolted to a bench) is not expressible by a field of the design format; it is
  anchored by the task or scene. If a design-level field is wanted it needs a schema decision.
- Three orthogonal revolutes for `S` have a chart singularity; a design whose spherical joint works near it
  is reported by the lowering, and the batched tiers use the group directly.
- Scope cap of `frob-model`: 16 thousand lines including tests; the functor and the model container are
  expected to be a small part of it.
