## Background

A design (`frob.design/1`, extension `.fbot`) is a canonical JSON term. The term model and its canonical form
are BD-054; this bead writes the schema of two blocks of the format's field reference: the top-level block and
the Body block. The rule that governs every block: every field the compiler reads is listed, and a field not
listed is refused (`FROB-REF-103`). A misspelt field is therefore never ignored.

The Body block is where the system's position on mass is fixed. By default inertia is not a parameter of a
design. It is computed from a Region (a shape), a fill (how the shape is occupied) and a material (a density),
so that an agent designs shapes and materials and cannot write an inertia no body could have. The Region is
the mass support: all of the body's mass lies inside it. Collision and visual geometry are kept apart from
it, because a padded collision proxy or a decorative mesh must not change the mass.

DESIGN reasons. Three separate geometry fields (`region`, `collision`, `visual`) replace the one mesh that
converters conventionally use for all three purposes; with one mesh, simplifying the collision shape silently
changes mass, and a visual shell silently becomes a solid block. The identifier of a body is unique in its
scope and constructors derive instance identifiers from it (`leg.fl.thigh`), so that an edit, a refusal
pointer and a telemetry channel can all name the same instance.

## Scope

In: the schema, typing and canonical defaults of the top-level fields `schema`, `name`, `meta`, and of the Body
fields `body`, `region`, `material`, `mass_properties`, `collision`, `visual`, `ports`; the Region grammar and
its dimension queries; frames written as exact transforms.

Out: `fill` and its refusals (BD-055); the moments of a Region and shells (BD-062); checking declared mass
properties against the inertia cone and the mass-support bound (N14a-13); moments and offsets of non-primitive
Regions and collision fitting (N14a-14); joints and actuation (N14a-04); end effectors (N14a-05).

## Technical approach

Layout (DESIGN): `crates/frob-design/src/blocks/{top.rs, body.rs, region.rs, frame.rs}`.

Field reference for the two blocks:

| Block | Field | Required | Meaning and rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top | `schema` | Yes | `frob.design/1`. An unknown major version refuses (`FROB-REF-125`) and names the migration |
| Top | `name` | Yes | Display name. No mechanical compile product reads it |
| Top | `meta` | No | `author`, `licence`, `source`. No mechanical compile product reads it |
| Body | `body` | Yes | An identifier unique in its scope. Constructors derive instance identifiers such as `leg.fl.thigh` |
| Body | `region` | Yes | A primitive (`box`, `cylinder`, `capsule`, `sphere`, `ellipsoid`), a union or difference of primitives, or a mesh by content hash. The Region is the mass support |
| Body | `fill` | Yes | `solid`, `{shell: thickness}`, `{lattice: fraction}`, `declared` or `massless`. No default (BD-055) |
| Body | `material` | Unless the fill is `declared` or `massless` | A material card with a density and its provenance, from `fs-matdb` through the airlock or from a local `.fcard` |
| Body | `mass_properties` | With `declared` | Mass, centre of mass and inertia, each with provenance. Checked against the inertia cone and the mass-support bound, and compared with the geometry-derived values whenever both exist (N14a-13) |
| Body | `collision`, `visual` | No | Collision proxy and visual mesh, kept apart from the mass Region. The collision proxy defaults to the Region, and the canonical form writes that out |
| Body | `ports` | No | Named mount frames relative to the body, each a mechanical port |

Region grammar (DESIGN; spellings follow the format's worked example, where `{"capsule": ["0.03 m", "0.06 m"]}`
has the radius first and the lever path `…/capsule/1` addresses the length):

```
region  := {"box": [x, y, z]}                 full edge lengths, centred on the region frame
         | {"cylinder": [radius, length]}     axis along the region frame's z, centred
         | {"capsule": [radius, length]}      length is the distance between the two cap centres, along z
         | {"sphere": radius}
         | {"ellipsoid": [a, b, c]}           semi-axes
         | {"union": [placed, …]} | {"difference": [placed, placed, …]}   the first minus the rest
         | {"mesh": "b3:<64 hex>", "unit": "m"}                             a closed, oriented surface
placed  := {"of": region, "at": frame}
frame   := {"xyz": [x, y, z], "rot": {"axis": [i, j, k], "angle": turn-or-radian}}   rot absent = identity
```

All lengths are quantities (N14a-01). A length that is zero or negative refuses with `FROB-REF-102` (a
dimension that cannot be a size). A frame's rotation is written as an axis with exact rational components and
an angle; an angle given as a rational turn about a coordinate axis yields an exact rational rotation, which
the exact symmetry computation needs for mirrored and radial limbs.

Dimension queries each Region answers exactly (used by the shell rule of BD-055 and by lints):

| Region | Smallest dimension d_min | Bounding box |
|---|---|---|
| `box` | min(x, y, z) | x, y, z |
| `cylinder` | min(2·radius, length) | 2r, 2r, length |
| `capsule` | 2·radius | 2r, 2r, length + 2r |
| `sphere` | 2·radius | 2r in each direction |
| `ellipsoid` | 2·min(a, b, c) | 2a, 2b, 2c |
| union, difference, mesh | Twice the inradius, from the distance field, with its error bound (N14a-14) | From the parts or the vertices |

Typing steps for a Body:

1. `body`: an identifier of printable characters, unique among its siblings; a duplicate refuses with
   `FROB-REF-103` (a duplicate name is a duplicate field of the scope).
2. `region`: parse by the grammar; exactly one shape key.
3. `material`: resolve the identifier to a card of kind `material`, first in the workspace's card catalog, then
   in `fs-matdb` through the airlock. Record the card's content hash; the canonical form keeps the identifier,
   and the compile key of the body includes the card's version, so a new card revision recompiles the body.
   A material is required unless the fill is `declared` or `massless`; with those fills a `material` member is
   legal and unused by the mass computation.
4. `mass_properties`: required exactly when the fill is `declared`: `mass`, `com` (a point in the body frame),
   `inertia` (six entries about the centre of mass: xx, yy, zz, xy, xz, yz), each with provenance. Present
   with any other fill, it refuses with `FROB-REF-103` (a field the compiler would not read is not accepted).
5. `collision`: absent becomes the token `"region"` in the canonical form (rule 6: non-physics default written
   out). Otherwise a Region of its own, used only for contact.
6. `visual`: a mesh by content hash; never read by physics or sensors.
7. `ports`: a map from a port identifier to a frame in the body frame. Each is a mechanical port (N14a-02);
   instance identifiers are `<body>.<port>` (`torso.hip_mount`).

Refusals issued here (exit code 3): `FROB-REF-103` (unknown, misspelt or duplicate field; a field illegal for
the chosen fill), `FROB-REF-102` (a non-positive size; a size with a non-length unit), `FROB-REF-125` (schema
version). Repairs are typed edits: `rename` for a misspelling, `reshape` with the nearest legal Region form,
`set-material` with the catalog's nearest identifiers.

Determinism class D0. Provenance: every quantity carries one (a bare string is `designed`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The torso of the worked design parses: `{"box": ["0.42 m", "0.20 m", "0.10 m"]}`, material
      `al-6061-t6`, port `hip_mount` at (0.19, 0.08, 0) m; its canonical form contains `"collision":"region"`
      and the port frame with the identity rotation written out.
- [ ] `d_min` of that box is exactly 1/10 m; of the capsule `["0.025 m", "0.21 m"]` exactly 1/20 m.
- [ ] `"regoin"` in place of `"region"` refuses with `FROB-REF-103` and offers `rename` to `region`.
- [ ] A body with `fill: solid` and a `mass_properties` member refuses with `FROB-REF-103`; a body with
      `fill: declared` and no `mass_properties` refuses, naming the missing member.
- [ ] A body with `fill: solid` and no `material` refuses; with `fill: massless` and no `material` it parses.
- [ ] Two sibling bodies named `thigh` refuse with `FROB-REF-103`.
- [ ] A box edge of `"0 m"` or `"-1 mm"` refuses with `FROB-REF-102`.
- [ ] A mesh Region is accepted only as a `b3:` hash; a file path refuses (a manifest never embeds a path).
- [ ] Changing `name` or `meta` changes `DesignId` and no subterm hash of `root` or `limbs`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-design blocks::body blocks::top`.

- `torso_parses_and_canonicalizes`: the worked torso; asserts the canonical bytes of the block.
- `region_grammar_each_form`: one fixture per Region form, including a union of two placed boxes and a
  difference (a box minus a cylinder bore).
- `min_dimension_exact`: the table above on six fixtures, compared as rationals.
- `misspelt_field_refuses`, `duplicate_body_name_refuses`, `nonpositive_size_refuses`.
- `fill_field_coupling`: the four combinations of fill and `mass_properties`/`material` in the acceptance list.
- `frame_rotation_exact`: `{"axis": [0,0,1], "angle": "1/4 turn"}` yields the exact rotation
  [[0, −1, 0], [1, 0, 0], [0, 0, 1]] with rational entries.
- `meta_not_in_mechanical_subterms`: subterm hashes of `root` equal across two values of `meta`.
- `prop_region_roundtrip` (10⁴ `synthetic` Regions): parse, canonicalize, parse again; equal terms.

### End-to-end script and logging

Exercised by `scripts/e2e/skel_author_compile.sh` (N14a-27): `frob design show <file> --json` on the worked
design and on three planted body defects (misspelt field, duplicate name, non-positive size) asserts the exit
code and the refusal code, and logs the refusal pointer and first repair per the shared NDJSON form.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Hand values: d_min of the worked torso 0.10 m; of the thigh capsule 0.05 m; capsule bounding length
  0.21 + 2 × 0.025 = 0.26 m.
- Negative control 1 (planted): a reader that silently accepts an unknown body field. The misspelt-field
  fixture then compiles a body with no Region override and the test fails. This is the defect "tolerate
  unknown fields" that the format registry names.
- Negative control 2 (planted): a capsule read as [length, radius]. The lever-path fixture (`…/capsule/1`
  with bounds 0.15 to 0.30 m) then lands on the radius and the bounds test of BD-055 fails.
- Negative control 3 (planted): `collision` omitted from the canonical form. Two files that differ only in
  writing `"collision":"region"` get two `DesignId`s and the canonical-form test fails.
- Determinism lanes: identical bytes twice, in debug and release, on Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- A union of overlapping primitives has no closed-form moments; this bead only parses it. Whether its mass is
  exact or carries an error bound is decided in N14a-14.
- Mesh Regions must be closed and consistently oriented to be a mass support; the check is the geometry
  kernel's, reached through the airlock, and a mesh that fails it is refused there.
- The material identifier is resolved at compile time; a design names a card and does not embed its values, so
  a corrected card revision reaches every design that names it and invalidates exactly their bodies.
- Imported names are untrusted text and are echoed only in fields carrying the untrusted mark.
