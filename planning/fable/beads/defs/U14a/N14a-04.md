## Background

A design (`frob.design/1`) is a canonical JSON term; BD-054 owns the term and its canonical form. This bead
writes the schema of the Joint block and of the two actuation fields that hang on a joint.

A joint is not one of a list of hand-coded kinds. By construction C-01 the configuration of a robot is a
product of Lie groups, and a joint contributes the map θ ↦ ∏ₖ exp(θₖ·Sₖ) for screws Sₖ in 𝔰𝔢(3): a screw
basis. When the span of the screws is closed under the Lie bracket the joint is a connected subgroup of SE(3),
and such subgroups fall into twelve conjugacy classes (Hervé). The format names the eight a designer writes:
`fixed`, `R` (revolute), `P` (prismatic), `H` (helical), `C` (cylindrical), `E` (planar), `S` (spherical) and
`free`. A universal joint, and any other joint whose screws do not span a subgroup, is written as a product of
these. One kernel generator parameterized by a screw basis then covers every joint, where an `enum JointKind`
needs one hand-written path per case.

Two rules of the block come from ADR-SKEL-03 (no physics field has a silent default). Joint limits must be
written: an interval, or the word `unbounded`. A default range yields a plausible wrong robot (a knee that
bends both ways). An actuator's values may be overridden on the joint, and an override is recorded as a design
decision with provenance `designed`, so that an instance that differs from its card says so.

## Scope

In: the schema, typing, canonical defaults and refusals of the Joint fields `class`, `screws`, `axis`, `pitch`,
`origin`, `limits`, `home`, `damping`, `friction`, `backlash`, and of `actuator` and `transmission`.

Out: the Hervé classification algorithm and generated joint maps (BD-046), which this bead calls; the
kinematic functor that consumes the block (BD-061); mobility of loops (BD-101); card contents and checks
(BD-069); transmission laws (BD-074); joint rows in the solve (BD-084); channel binding (N14a-09).

## Technical approach

Layout (DESIGN): `crates/frob-design/src/blocks/{joint.rs, actuation.rs}`.

Field reference:

| Field | Required | Meaning and rule |
|---|---|---|
| `class` | Yes | `fixed`, `R`, `P`, `H`, `C`, `E` (planar), `S` or `free`. A universal joint and any other non-subgroup joint are written as a product of these |
| `screws`, or `axis` with `pitch` | Yes, except for `fixed` and `free` | The screw basis: for each screw an axis, a point on it and a pitch. `axis` is shorthand for one screw through the joint origin. The stated class is checked against the basis by exact rational linear algebra (theorem card T-11) |
| `origin` | No | `parent_from_joint` at the reference configuration; identity if absent, written out in the canonical form |
| `limits` | Yes, except for `fixed` | An interval per coordinate, a swing-and-twist cone for `S`, or the word `unbounded`. An empty or inverted interval refuses (`FROB-REF-107`) |
| `home`, `damping`, `friction`, `backlash` | No | Reference configuration, and joint-side overrides of the actuator card's values |
| `actuator` | No (a joint without one is passive) | A card identifier, or a card with overrides. An override is recorded with provenance `designed` and changes the instance's card hash |
| `transmission` | No | `gear`, `belt`, `screw`, `linkage`, `cable`, or `coupling` over several joints with a matrix: signed ratio, efficiency pair or loss law, backlash, compliance |

Coordinates per class and what `limits` must be:

| Class | Degrees of freedom | Screws | `limits` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fixed` | 0 | none | none: a `limits` member on a `fixed` joint refuses (`FROB-REF-103`) |
| `R` | 1 | one zero-pitch screw | one angle interval, or `unbounded` |
| `P` | 1 | one pure translation | one length interval, or `unbounded` |
| `H` | 1 | one screw with finite non-zero pitch | one angle interval, or `unbounded` |
| `C` | 2 | a translation and a rotation about one line | a length interval and an angle interval |
| `E` | 3 | two translations in a plane and the rotation normal to it | two length intervals and an angle interval |
| `S` | 3 | three rotations through one point | `{"swing": angle, "twist": [lo, hi]}`, or `unbounded` |
| `free` | 6 | all of 𝔰𝔢(3) | `unbounded` |

Screw spelling (DESIGN, confined to the listed contents). `{"axis": [i, j, k], "point": [x, y, z], "pitch": q}`
for a finite-pitch screw; `{"translation": [i, j, k]}` for a pure translation. Axis components are exact
rationals and give a direction, not necessarily of unit length: with twists written angular part first,
ξ = (ω, v), the screw is S = (â, p × â + h·â) with â the unit direction and h the advance per radian, and a
pure translation is S = (0, â). `pitch` is written as the advance per full turn, a length (`"5 mm"`), so
h = pitch/(2π). Exact tests (rank, bracket closure) are invariant under scaling an axis and use the rational
direction as written; unit vectors are formed once, at lowering.

Typing steps:

1. `class` is one of the eight tokens.
2. Build the screw list: from `screws`, or from the shorthand `axis` (one screw through the joint origin;
   zero pitch for `R`, `pitch` required for `H`, a translation for `P`). Writing both `screws` and `axis`
   refuses with `FROB-REF-103`. A zero axis refuses with `FROB-REF-101`.
3. Count check at preflight stage 1: the number of screws equals the class's degrees of freedom (`free` and
   `fixed` write none). The full check, at stage 3: the class reported by the classifier of BD-046 for the
   basis equals the stated class; a disagreement refuses with `FROB-REF-101` (joint class and screw basis
   disagree), with the computed class in `observed`.
4. `origin`: a frame (`xyz`, `rot`); absent, the identity is written out in the canonical form.
5. `limits`: required. Each interval `[lo, hi]` has the coordinate's dimension (an angle for a rotation, a
   length for a translation; otherwise `FROB-REF-102`) and lo < hi in exact comparison; lo ≥ hi refuses with
   `FROB-REF-107`. A missing `limits` member refuses with `FROB-REF-107` and the message that an interval or
   `unbounded` must be written. For `S`, the swing angle lies in (0, 1/2 turn]. Staging: the preflight table
   gives the empty-range refusal to stage 3 (Mechanism), so the lo ≥ hi and `home` comparisons are functions
   of this block that stage 3 calls; a missing member is found when the document is typed, in stage 1, and is
   reported with the same code (DESIGN: a document without limits has no canonical form).
6. `home`: one value per coordinate, inside its interval; outside refuses with `FROB-REF-107`.
7. `damping`, `friction`, `backlash`: quantities with the dimension the coordinate implies; each recorded
   with its provenance and marked as a joint-side override of the card.
8. `actuator`: an identifier, or `{"card": id, "override": {field: value, …}}`. The instance card hash is the
   hash of the card's content hash and the canonical override object, so two joints with one card and
   different overrides bind different instances, and an override shows in every diff.
9. `transmission`: exactly one kind key with its parameter object, for example `{"belt": {"ratio": "1.55"}}`.
   The ratio is signed. A `coupling` names the joints it couples and its matrix; it is written once, on the
   first of them, and referenced by the others.

Refusals issued here (exit code 3, ranked repairs as typed edits):

| Code | Meaning | Trigger here | Ranked repairs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FROB-REF-101` | Joint class and screw basis disagree | Screw count differs from the class; the classifier reports another class; a zero axis | 1. set `class` to the computed class; 2. the screw edit that yields the stated class |
| `FROB-REF-107` | Empty joint range | lo ≥ hi; `limits` missing; `home` outside the interval | 1. `set-limits` with the two bounds swapped when inverted; 2. `set-limits unbounded`; 3. `class: fixed` for a zero-width interval |
| `FROB-REF-102` | Unit or dimension error | An angle interval on a translation or a length on a rotation | `set-limits` with a unit of the right dimension |
| `FROB-REF-103` | Unknown or duplicate field | `axis` and `screws` together; two transmission kinds | Remove one |

Determinism class D0: all checks are exact rational arithmetic.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three joints of the worked leg parse: `R` about (1,0,0) with limits [−1/8, 1/8] turn; `R` about
      (0,1,0) with [−1/4, 5/12] turn; `R` about (0,1,0) with [−4/9, −1/12] turn and a belt of ratio 1.55.
- [ ] `limits: ["45 deg", "-45 deg"]` refuses with `FROB-REF-107` at stage 3 and offers the swapped interval.
- [ ] A joint with no `limits` refuses with `FROB-REF-107`; `limits: "unbounded"` parses.
- [ ] `class: "R"` with two screws refuses with `FROB-REF-101` at stage 1; `class: "R"` with one pure
      translation refuses with `FROB-REF-101` at stage 3 with observed class `P`.
- [ ] A `P` joint with limits `["0 deg", "30 deg"]` refuses with `FROB-REF-102` (the unit half of the
      first rejection fixture of the joint family).
- [ ] Two joints with card `qdd-m`, one with an override of the gear ratio, have different instance card
      hashes, and the override carries provenance `designed`.
- [ ] A canonical joint always contains `origin`; a design that writes the identity origin and one that omits
      it have one `DesignId`.
- [ ] A universal joint written as two `R` joints about crossing axes with a `massless` intermediate body
      parses; a single joint whose two screws are not closed under the bracket is refused with the product
      form as repair.

- `screw_count_vs_class`: for each of the eight classes, the right count passes and count ± 1 refuses.

- `spherical_three_concurrent_rotations`: axes x, y, z through one point classify as `S`; moving one axis

- `helical_pitch`: `pitch: "5 mm"` gives h = 0.005/(2π) m per radian at lowering; the term keeps `"0.005 m"`.
- `home_outside_limits_refuses`.
- `override_changes_instance_hash`.
- `prop_origin_default_written` (10³ `synthetic` joints): omitted and identity origins canonicalize alike.

Exercised by `scripts/e2e/skel_author_compile.sh` (N14a-27) with three planted joint defects (inverted

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-278 (first rejection fixture of the joint family): a prismatic travel in millimetres read as radians is
  refused at stage 1 with `FROB-REF-102`. Here: a length where an angle is required, and the converse.
- Hand values: −45° = −1/8 turn; 150° = 5/12 turn; −160° = −4/9 turn; −30° = −1/12 turn.
- Known answers of the classifier that this block must pass through unchanged: a planar four-bar's joints are
  four `R`; three concurrent rotations are `S`.
- Negative control 1 (planted): a reader that defaults missing limits to [−π, π]. The missing-limits test
  fails; this is the silent default ADR-SKEL-03 forbids.
- Negative control 2 (planted): a class check that only counts screws. The translation-as-`R` fixture passes
  the mutant and the stage-3 test fails.
- Determinism lanes: identical canonical bytes and refusal pointers twice, debug and release, Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- Mirroring. Under a reflection a joint axis is an axial vector and maps to det(P)·P·a. A mirrored limb that
  writes +P·a instead must negate the coordinate, its limits and its encoder and gear signs. The `mirror`
  constructor does this (BD-056); this block stores what the author wrote for the unmirrored limb only.
- The referee tier's foundation has only fixed, revolute, prismatic and helical joints (FACT: `fs-mbd`
  `JointKind`); `C`, `E` and `S` are lowered to products of one-axis joints there (BD-061).
- A revolute coordinate is stored in state as a unit complex number and an integer turn count; limits wider
  than one turn make the limit law depend on the turn count (N14a-09 records which joints need it).
- Lattice mobility (using subgroup structure for over-constrained loops) is Frontier and lives in the
  mobility analysis; nothing here depends on it.
- Unknown card identifiers and cards that fail their own checks refuse with the card catalog's codes.
