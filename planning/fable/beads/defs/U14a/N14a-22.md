## Background

A robot's limbs meet its body at mechanical ports. For a tree limb attached at such a port, with its joint
torques known and its end effectors free, everything the limb does to its parent is carried by two
quantities: its articulated inertia at the port, a symmetric 6×6 matrix with 21 independent entries, and a
bias wrench of 6 entries. Together they are the limb port packet: 27 scalars, 108 bytes in single precision.
The packet is computed by the backward pass of the articulated-body recursion over the limb. Tier Frontier.

What it is for: the tree-operator route for mechanisms too large for a dense mass matrix, where limbs are
reduced to packets and only the base is solved densely, and the per-limb caches of the analyses. Stated
ceiling, before any measurement: it enables that route above the dense range and has no benefit inside it.
What it is not: a way to decouple contact.

The semidefinite caveat. Eliminating a joint with axis s from an articulated inertia I gives

    I − I·s·(sᵀ·I·s)⁻¹·sᵀ·I,

and this matrix sends s to zero: the limb offers no resistance along its own free joint. With I the identity
and s the first basis vector the result is diag(0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1). The packet is positive semidefinite, never
guaranteed definite, and the type says so. Three consequences bind every consumer:

1. the packet cannot be inverted at the port. The parent adds its own inertia before any solve, and a chain
   of massless parents is singular, which is why preflight stage 2 refuses it;
2. the packet does not describe a limb whose end effector is in contact. Contact rows couple every limb
   through the base, and the coupled solve of the dynamics subsystem is the only correct path for them;
3. the packet is a value for one configuration in one world at one instant. What a module substitution
   reuses is the packet's layout and the sibling limbs' kernels, not numbers.

This bead fixes the type, its byte layout, a host-side reference computation and the proof obligation
PO-SKEL-04. The run-time kernels that produce packets inside the step belong to the tree-operator pilot.
The whole item sits behind a named feature (`frontier-limb-port`), off by default, and has a kill gate
(KILL-SKEL-PORT, decided in N14a-24).

## Technical approach

Layout (DESIGN): `crates/frob-model/src/port/{sym_psd6.rs, packet.rs, recursion.rs}`, compiled only with
`--features frontier-limb-port`.

```rust
/// What a free tree limb presents to its parent at a mechanical port, at one instant.
/// Positive semidefinite, never guaranteed definite: it cannot be inverted at the port.
pub struct LimbPortPacket { inertia: SymPsd6, bias: WrenchIn<PortFrame> }
pub struct SymPsd6([f32; 21]);        // upper triangle, row-major: (0,0) (0,1) … (0,5) (1,1) … (5,5)
```

Conventions. Twists are ξ = (ω, v), angular part first; wrenches are (τ, f); the pairing ⟨w, ξ⟩ is power. A
link's spatial inertia about a frame origin, for mass m, centre of mass c and rotational inertia Ī about the
origin, is I = [[Ī, m·ĉ], [m·ĉᵀ, m·1]] with ĉ the cross-product matrix of c. Moving an inertia from a child
frame to its parent's is I ↦ Xᵀ·I·X with X the twist map from parent to child coordinates.

The reference recursion over one limb, leaves to port, at one configuration, with joint torques τⱼ, velocity
bias terms cⱼ and pⱼ given (zero at rest), as numbered steps:

1. Initialize each link: I_j^A = I_j, p_j^A = p_j.
2. For each link j from the leaves toward the port, with joint screw basis S_j (one column per degree of
   freedom): D_j = S_jᵀ·I_j^A·S_j; u_j = τ_j − S_jᵀ·p_j^A. D_j must be invertible; a zero D_j is the massless
   chain that stage 2 refuses, and here it is an error that names the joint.
3. Reduce: I_j^a = I_j^A − I_j^A·S_j·D_j⁻¹·S_jᵀ·I_j^A, and p_j^a = p_j^A + I_j^a·c_j + I_j^A·S_j·D_j⁻¹·u_j.
4. Accumulate into the parent link: I_parent^A += Xᵀ·I_j^a·X and p_parent^A += Xᵀ·p_j^a. For the limb's
   first link the "parent" is the port: the packet is (Xᵀ·I_1^a·X, Xᵀ·p_1^a) in the port frame.
5. Meaning: if the port frame has spatial acceleration a, the wrench the parent must supply to the limb is
   w = I_port·a + bias. That is the limb's reaction on its parent, with the sign reversed.

API restrictions that carry the three consequences (DESIGN):

- `SymPsd6` has no `inverse`, no `solve` and no `cholesky`. It offers `add(&SymPsd6)`, `apply(&Twist)`,
  `quad(&Twist) -> Energy rate`, and `plus_definite(SymPd6) -> SymPd6`, where `SymPd6` is the type of a
  parent's own inertia; only a `SymPd6` can be factored.
- `LimbPortPacket` is constructed only by the recursion and carries the identifiers of the model, the
  configuration digest and the world address it is valid for. It has no constructor from a module contract:
  a contract guarantees the kind (`sym-psd-6`), never numbers.
- A constructor that would take a limb with an active contact refuses: the function's input type is a limb
  whose end effectors are marked free.

Proof obligation PO-SKEL-04: a limb port packet is symmetric positive semidefinite and reproduces the limb's
reaction on its parent. Verification: comparison with the full articulated recursion on random limbs; the
diag(0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1) case as a fixture.

Determinism. The exact-rational recursion is class D0 and is the oracle. The double-precision one is
bit-identical run to run on one platform. Evidence class of a packet: `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] PO-SKEL-04 on 10⁴ random tree limbs (1 to 8 joints, random screw bases, masses and configurations):
      the packet is symmetric; its smallest eigenvalue is ≥ −ε with ε = 10⁻¹² times its largest (TARGET); and
      for six unit port accelerations the wrench I_port·a + bias equals the wrench obtained from the full
      articulated recursion of the whole limb-plus-port system within 10⁻⁹ relative (TARGET).
- [ ] The fixture with I the identity and s the first basis vector gives exactly diag(0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1).
- [ ] The one-link fixture (a point mass m at distance L along x from a revolute joint about z) gives the
      exact packet whose only non-zero entries are (ω_y, ω_y) = m·L², (ω_y, v_z) = −m·L, (v_x, v_x) = m and
      (v_z, v_z) = m: rank 2, with the joint screw in its null space.
- [ ] The packet's root-joint screw is a null vector: I_port·s = 0 within round-off, for every random limb.
- [ ] The serialized packet is exactly 108 bytes in single precision, 21 inertia entries then 6 bias entries.
- [ ] `SymPsd6` has no inversion or factorization method (a compile-fail test attempts one).
- [ ] A limb whose first joint moves no mass makes the recursion return the massless-chain error naming the
      joint; nothing is divided by zero.
- [ ] The default build (feature off) contains none of this code.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/skel_limb_port.sh` passes and its `summary` line is the PO-SKEL-04 receipt: limbs tried,
      the three measured extremes against their thresholds, the controls caught, the commit and the seed.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-model --features frontier-limb-port port`.

- `identity_fixture_diag_0_1_1_1_1_1` (exact).
- `one_link_point_mass_exact`: m = 2 kg, L = 1/2 m; expected entries 1/2, −1, 2, 2 in the positions above and
  zero elsewhere, compared as rationals.
- `root_screw_in_null_space` (property, 10⁴ cases).
- `psd_and_symmetric` (property, 10⁴ cases; eigenvalues by an exact LDLᵀ with non-negative pivots on the
  rational result).
- `reproduces_reaction` (property, 10⁴ cases): compare with the full recursion.
- `layout_is_27_scalars_108_bytes`.
- `compile_fail_no_inverse` (doc-test that must not build).
- `massless_first_link_errors`.
- `two_link_pilot_values`: the configuration and numbers recorded by the paper pilot (N14a-23) reproduce.

### End-to-end script and logging

No verb exposes a packet, so the script drives the build and the test binaries rather than `frob` verbs; the
tree-operator pilot's script (BD-385) exercises packets inside a step. `scripts/e2e/skel_limb_port.sh` (this
bead; sources the helper of STD-E2E) produces the PO-SKEL-04 record that the kill decision (N14a-24) reads.
Logs go to `target/e2e-logs/skel_limb_port/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside them. Steps, each one
NDJSON line carrying the helper's fields (`ts`, `script`, `bead` "N14a-22", `step`, `event`, the exact command
line, exit code, wall time) plus `status`, the commit and the seed, and on failure the first differing value
(the entry index and both values) and the failing limb's seed:

1. `default_build`: builds `frob` without features and asserts that no symbol of `port::` (`LimbPortPacket`,
   `SymPsd6`) is in the binary and that `frontier-limb-port` is absent from the resolved feature set.
2. `fixtures`: the identity fixture and the one-link fixture, exact; logs each matrix's digest and `exact: true`.
3. `pilot_values`: the two-link values handed over by N14a-23 (packet, rank 3, bias); logs the difference
   (expected exactly zero).
4. `po_skel_04`: the property battery on 10⁴ random limbs; logs `limbs`, `max_symmetry_defect`,
   `min_eig_ratio` against −10⁻¹², `max_reaction_rel_error` against 10⁻⁹, `max_null_residual`, and the
   failing limb's seed when any threshold is crossed.
5. `controls`: planted mutants 1 and 3 below (built under a test-only feature) run against the same battery,
   and mutant 2 (an `inverse` on `SymPsd6`) is the compile-fail doc-test, which must fail to build; each line
   carries `caught: true|false`, and the step fails if any is not caught.
6. `summary`: `gates: ["KILL-SKEL-PORT"]`, `po_skel_04: holds|fails`, and the digest of the log, which is
   the receipt the decision cites.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-264: after eliminating a joint with axis s the packet sends s to zero; the identity fixture gives
  diag(0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1); 27 scalars, 108 bytes; equal to the limb's reaction from the full recursion. Exact
  for the fixture; round-off tolerance on random limbs to set. Control: a packet typed or treated as
  positive definite, or inverted at the port.
- Counterexample CX-129 (a limb's port inertia is positive definite, so it can be inverted at the port and
  can stand for a limb in contact): the identity fixture; a chain of massless parents is singular; contact
  rows couple every limb through the base.
- Negative control 1 (planted): the reduction step without the subtraction (I^a = I^A). The null-space test
  fails and the reaction disagrees.
- Negative control 2 (planted): a `SymPsd6` given an `inverse` that regularizes with a small diagonal. The
  compile-fail test starts compiling, and the identity fixture's inverse is a fabricated number.
- Negative control 3 (planted): the bias accumulated without Xᵀ. The reaction test fails on the first limb
  with an offset joint.

## Considerations

- ✖ KILL-SKEL-PORT. Trigger: PO-SKEL-04 fails, or the tree-operator route is not funded. Fallback: no limb
  port packets; all dynamics go through the whole-model path. Decided at M5 (N14a-24).
- The packet is tempting as a contract quantity. It must not become one: a module's contract may say that
  its port inertia is of kind `sym-psd-6`, and nothing numeric, because the numbers depend on configuration,
  world and instant.
- Single precision loses the exact zero eigenvalue; consumers must never test rank numerically and must
  treat the root screw's direction as known to be free.
- The type sketch fixes intent, not signatures; `WrenchIn<PortFrame>` is the frame-typed wrench of the Lie
  library.
