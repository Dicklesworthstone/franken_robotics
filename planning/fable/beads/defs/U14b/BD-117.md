## Background

Reflex is the subsystem that owns every closed loop that needs no learning. Its reason to exist is time: training
a policy for a candidate design costs minutes to about an hour, while a baseline controller synthesized from the
model in seconds lets a new design be simulated at once. The baseline is for feedback, not for peak performance,
and nothing here is called deployable: a controller that works in the model is a `Tested` object in the model.

Decision ADR-REFLEX-01: every design gets an inspectable, hand-specified baseline before any learned component, so
that a failure can be assigned to the body, the baseline or a later residual; an end-to-end policy cannot say
which. `control synth` detects the morphology class from the model and assembles the controller of that class.
This bead delivers two classes, which the first wheeled archetypes need at M1:

- **Wheeled** (the `diff-drive` archetype): wheel speeds from the commanded body twist through the kinematic
  connection, traction-limited acceleration, path tracking through flat outputs; wheel loops saturate at the
  card's current limit. Tier Solid.
- **Omnidirectional** (the `mecanum` archetype): body twist to wheel speeds through the connection, after the
  growth-vector rank check of analysis A-07. Tier Solid.

The kinematic model (construction C-07: rolling constraints define a distribution, and maneuvering is a Lie
bracket) is the planning model and the known-answer oracle. It is not what the simulator integrates: on the
referee a wheel is a body, a revolute joint and a collider, and slip and traction limits come from contact. The
controller must therefore work when the plant slips a little, and must report when it does not.

## Scope

In: the wheeled and omnidirectional controller classes in `frob-control`; gain derivation from the model; the
saturation report; the crate-level measurement, on the real referee step, of what the M1 rows of GATE-REFLEX-01
for `diff-drive` and `mecanum` require.

Out: `control.synth` and its three-outcome report (BD-118); the estimator (BD-114); the estimated and privileged
state sources and their paired report (N22a-03); the rows of GATE-REFLEX-01 for `diff-drive` and `mecanum`
measured through the binary and recorded (N22c-01, which depends on BD-118 and so on this bead, and which writes
and runs this bead's script below); steered, tracked and balancing wheeled classes (N14b-07, M3); slip-aware
odometry and receding-horizon docking (the next rung of the ladder, not built here); batched execution on
`batch-cpu`, the M2 row of the same gate (N22ap2-01).

## Technical approach

**Interfaces.** The traits `Controller`, `ActionFilter` and `StateSource` are declared beside the step plan in
`frob-dyn`; this bead implements `Controller` for two structs in `crates/frob-control/src/baseline/{wheeled.rs,
omni.rs}`. Input per control tick: the state estimate (base pose T ∈ SE(2) lifted from SE(3), body twist, wheel
rates), a reference (a body twist, or a path with timing), and the model. Output: per-wheel current or torque
commands to the driver, with the layer that produced each command recorded (INV-REFLEX-01).

**Algorithm, per tick.**

1. *Error on the group.* e = log(T_d⁻¹·T) in se(2), with the desired twist expressed in the same tangent frame
   as the measured twist. Near a heading error of π the controller re-charts by a declared rule; a derivative
   requested at the cut receives a typed refusal, never a silent sign flip.
2. *Reference twist.* For a path, the flat output of a differential drive is the position (x, y) of the axle
   midpoint: v_d = √(ẋ² + ẏ²) and ω_d = (ẋ·ÿ − ẏ·ẍ)/(ẋ² + ẏ²). The tracking law (DESIGN) is
   v = v_d·cos e_θ − k_x·e_x and ω = ω_d − v_d·(k_y·e_y + k_θ·sin e_θ), with e = (e_x, e_y, e_θ) in the body
   frame. For an omnidirectional base the commanded twist is ξ = ξ_d − K·e with a diagonal K.
3. *Traction-limited acceleration.* The commanded acceleration and braking are clipped to η·μ·g with η = 0.8
   (DESIGN margin), μ read from the interface card of the wheel contact and g the magnitude of the model's
   gravity vector (never a literal; standard gravity 9.80665 m/s² by the program's conventions); the yaw
   acceleration is clipped by the same force budget through the half-track.
4. *Connection.* Differential drive with wheel radius r and half-track b: ω_R = (v + b·ω)/r and
   ω_L = (v − b·ω)/r. Omnidirectional with wheel i at (xᵢ, yᵢ) and roller tangent tᵢ:
   ωᵢ = (1/r)·(v_x + tᵢ·v_y + (tᵢ·xᵢ − yᵢ)·ω_z). Before synthesis the growth vector of A-07 is read: an
   omnidirectional class requires (3); a map of rank below 3 returns `not-synthesizable` with the missing
   direction.
5. *Wheel loops.* One speed loop per driven wheel: a proportional-integral law on wheel-rate error producing a
   current target, clamped to the card's current limit with anti-windup (the integrator is frozen while
   clamped). The winding-voltage feedforward uses the measured current, R·i, not the target current: with the
   target-current form a sampled loop on a 75 mm wheel with reflected inertia 0.00107890625 kg·m² and a 10 ms
   hold has one-tick spectral radius 1.4820 and is unstable, while the measured-current form gives 0.9207.
6. *Gains from the model, not from a table.* The reflected inertia per wheel is J = N²·J_rotor + J_wheel +
   m_share·r², with N the gear ratio and m_share the share of the base mass the wheel accelerates. The loop
   bandwidth is set as a fraction of the control rate, and the gains follow from J and the card's torque
   constant; A-22 must report a spectral radius below one for the result or synthesis returns
   `not-synthesizable` with that reason. Outer-loop gains k_x, k_y, k_θ come from the linearization's natural
   frequencies.
7. *Actuator bounds are not negotiable.* The driver model enforces the envelope in the plant. A run that
   succeeded only while the controller requested more than the continuous envelope for longer than the card's
   duty rating is marked `actuator-infeasible` and counts as a failure of the engineering constraint.

**Layering (DESIGN).** Analyses A-07 and A-22 live in `frob-analyze`, which ranks after `frob-control` in layer
4 and reads the controller record (N28a-09 depends on N22a-07), so `frob-control` takes no edge to it (rule L-1;
the forbidden list of N22a-01). The growth vector of step 4 is computed by the design-check pass of the
`control.synth` verb (BD-118, in `frob-verbs`) and handed to this bead's synthesis as a typed input; the A-22
radius of step 6 is evaluated by that verb on the record this synthesis produces, and a radius of one or more
turns the outcome into `not-synthesizable` with the A-22 finding as its witness. The tests of this bead that combine the two
(`feedforward_law_is_measured_current`, `rank_drop_not_synthesizable` and the A-22 criterion) live in
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/reflex_wheeled.rs`, since `frob-conformance` may depend on both crates.

**Identity.** `ControllerId` is the content hash of the class, the derived gains, the planning configuration, the
compute-latency model (the delay from decision to command in the clock contract) and the symmetry group actually
used (INV-REFLEX-02).

**Report.** The `ok` outcome carries the saturation report (per wheel: fraction of ticks at the current limit,
peak current, the limit), the fraction of ticks spent in each layer, and the standing `screening`. Evaluation runs
with `--state estimated` and `--state privileged`, and the difference is reported.

**Determinism and evidence.** Deterministic given the model, the reference and the estimator output (class D0 on
the host arithmetic it uses); outputs are class `Tested`, tier `referee`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The seed's condition: the `diff-drive` and `mecanum` archetypes traverse 5 m of flat ground on the referee,
      and the wheel loops saturate at the card's current limit (measured peak current equals the limit within the
      driver's resolution and never exceeds it), shown by the crate-level referee tests below.
- [ ] What the M1 rows of GATE-REFLEX-01 require (TARGET), at crate level on the real referee step: each of the
      two stands for 10 s and traverses 5 m under its baseline controller, reading state through BD-114's
      estimator behind the `StateSource` trait, and again on privileged state; distance, time and drift are
      logged with the difference between the two. The rows are measured through `frob control synth` and
      recorded by N22c-01 (one writer per row); this bead records no gate value.
- [ ] Circle tracking in the ideal model, then torque-limited acceleration and braking, pass (the evidence the
      wheeled class requires before the next rung).
- [ ] Omnidirectional class, the evidence its row requires before the next rung: lateral tracking (a pure
      sideways reference of 0.2 m/s over 2 m in the ideal model ends within 0.05 m of the target and with heading
      error below 0.05 rad; DESIGN thresholds, recorded with the measured values) and a planted roller
      misalignment (all rollers parallel) found as a rank drop: `not-synthesizable` with the missing direction
      named.
- [ ] A-22 reports a spectral radius below one for the synthesized wheel loop; the target-current feedforward
      mutant is caught.
- [ ] Every command carries its layer record; `ControllerId` changes when a gain, the latency model or the class
      changes, and only then.
- [ ] The two classes are exported through the synthesis entry point of `frob-control` that `control.synth`
      (BD-118) dispatches to; reachability through `frob control synth <design> --json` is asserted by N22c-01's
      run of the script below; `crates/frob-control/CONTRACT.md` covers the class.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `diff_connection_values`: r = 0.075 m, b = 0.20 m, v = 0.5 m/s on a circle of radius 1 m (ω = 0.5 rad/s) →
  ω_R = 8.000 rad/s, ω_L = 5.333 rad/s.
- `omni_connection_values`: r = 0.05 m, wheels in the order (+0.25, +0.20), (+0.25, −0.20), (−0.25, +0.20),
  (−0.25, −0.20) m (x forward, y left), tᵢ = −sign(xᵢ·yᵢ); ω_z = 1 rad/s →
  (−9, 9, −9, 9) rad/s; v_y = 0.1 m/s → (−2, 2, 2, −2) rad/s.
- `flat_output_reference`: a circle path x = R·cos(s), y = R·sin(s) gives constant v_d and ω_d = v_d/R.
- `group_error_recharts_at_pi`: e_θ = π − 10⁻⁹ and π + 10⁻⁹ give continuous commands; a derivative request at the
  cut is refused.
- `current_clamp_and_antiwindup`: a step in commanded rate beyond what the card's current allows → current target
  equals the limit; after the step is removed the loop recovers without overshoot from windup.
- `feedforward_law_is_measured_current` (KA-610 ≡ KA-686): the wheel-loop fixture of `fixture:cart-sill-dock`
  (`docs/derivations/fixture-cart-sill-dock/`): a 75 mm wheel of spin inertia 8.7890625·10⁻⁴ kg·m² plus
  0.0002 kg·m² added rotor inertia (0.00107890625 kg·m² in all), shaft damping 0.002 N·m·s/rad, slipping against a
  constant torque; motor k_t = 0.2 N·m/A, k_e = 0.2 V·s/rad, R = 1 Ω, L = 10 mH, gear ratio 1, current limit
  ±6 A; outer loop i_ref = 2 A·s/rad × rate error, sampled and held for 10 ms; inner driver
  v = R·i + k_e·ω + 4 Ω·(i_ref − i), clipped to ±24 V. The exact one-tick map (the hold integrated in closed form)
  has spectral radius 0.9207 (±10⁻⁴); the mutant with R·i_ref in place of R·i gives 1.4820; the
  instantaneous-current approximation gives 2.6917 for both (2.7075 without the shaft damping) and is not used.
- `traction_clip`: μ = 0.5 on a model with standard gravity → commanded acceleration ≤ 0.8·0.5·9.80665 =
  3.92266 m/s²; the same model with gravity scaled to 1.62 m/s² → 0.648 m/s² (the clip reads the model, not a
  constant).
- `referee_stand_traverse` (in `crates/frob-conformance/tests/reflex_wheeled.rs`, on the real referee step with
  the real `diff-drive` and `mecanum` templates of BD-109; no mocks): stand 10 s without a fall, then at least
  5 m of net displacement on flat ground within twice the nominal time, once on BD-114's estimate and once on
  privileged state; the mecanum case is reported `blocked: contact law` when the roller law is not on the
  referee, never passed.
- `wheel_current_saturates_at_limit`: a commanded launch from rest at the traction clip → the peak wheel current
  equals the card limit within the driver's resolution during the launch and never exceeds it; the saturation
  report lists the fraction of ticks at the limit for each wheel; the no-clamp mutant (negative control a) is
  marked `actuator-infeasible`.
- `rank_drop_not_synthesizable`: parallel rollers → outcome `not-synthesizable`, direction (−1, 1, 0) named.
- `omni_lateral_tracking_ideal`: the `mecanum` wheel map above integrated in the ideal kinematic model under a
  reference v_y = 0.2 m/s for 10 s → final position within 0.05 m of (0, 2) m and heading within 0.05 rad.
- `diff_circle_tracking_ideal`: radius 1 m at v_d = 0.5 m/s for one lap in the ideal model, starting 0.1 m off
  the path → cross-track error below 0.01 m after half a lap (DESIGN threshold).
- `controller_id_covers_inputs`: perturb each of class, a gain, the latency model → the hash changes.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reflex_wheeled_baseline.sh`. The script reaches the controller only through `frob control synth`,
whose bead BD-118 depends on this one, and through the state sources of N22a-03, so it is written and run by
N22c-01 (the writer of the `diff-drive` and `mecanum` rows, which depends on both); before that, the same
measurements run at crate level in `referee_stand_traverse` and `wheel_current_saturates_at_limit`. Its content,
fixed here: for `diff-drive` and `mecanum`, `frob design new <ns>-<archetype> --template <archetype> --key <k>`,
`frob control synth <ns>-<archetype> --tier referee --seed 1 --key <k> --json` (the synthesis's standing smoke
reports estimated and privileged state and their difference), `frob sim run <ns>-<archetype> --tier referee
--task stand-10s --seed 1 --key <k> --json`, `frob sim run <ns>-<archetype> --tier referee --task
traverse-5m-flat --state estimated --seed 1 --key <k> --json`, then the same with `--state privileged` under a
distinct key, then `frob telemetry summary` of each run; every key is distinct. Asserts: distance at least 5 m,
no fall or stall event, peak wheel current at most the card limit and equal to it during the commanded launch,
saturation report present, estimated-against-privileged difference reported. Negative-control step
(`e2e_expect_caught`): `frob design new mecanum-par --file fixtures/reflex/mecanum-parallel-rollers.fbot --key <k>`
(the four wheels written out without `mirror`, all roller angles equal; the fixture is delivered by this bead)
then `frob control synth mecanum-par --key <k> --json` must return the outcome `not-synthesizable` naming the
missing direction (−1, 1, 0). Declared plant `no-clamp` (`E2E_PLANT_FAILURE`): the script asserts against a
current limit 10% above the card's, so the peak-equals-limit assertion must fail. NDJSON per step:
command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` (claim `known-answer`, class `Tested`, tier `referee`,
determinism, `ModelId`, `ControllerId`, seed), measured distance (m), time (s), peak current (A) with the limit,
digests of the two runs, first differing value on failure with the run identifiers. Summary line names
GATE-REFLEX-01.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Growth vectors from C-07: differential drive (2, 3); ideal omnidirectional base (3).
- KA-268: parallel-roller mecanum has rank 2 and growth vector (2, 3); the controller must refuse to synthesize.
- A-22 pilot values: spectral radii 1.4820 and 0.9207; the instantaneous-current model gives 2.6917 for both and
  misses the stable case, so the hold is modelled exactly.
- Negative controls: (a) a wheel loop with no clamp: the plant's envelope marks the run `actuator-infeasible`;
  (b) the target-current feedforward; (c) the twist map with the yaw column's sign flipped on one side: circle
  tracking fails.
- Metamorphic: mirroring the reference path left to right mirrors the wheel-rate traces (swap of left and right);
  scaling the path's time by 2 halves the rates below the traction clip.
- Determinism: two runs on one machine have equal digests; debug and release agree in the class the referee
  claims (same platform).

## Considerations

- The mecanum row at M1 depends on the anisotropic roller law existing on the referee. GATE-REFLEX-01 restricts
  M1 to archetypes whose contact law exists at M1 and whose contact count fits the referee's 21-contact cap. If
  the law is not there, the mecanum row is recorded `blocked: contact law`, the kinematic and synthesis tests of
  this bead still pass, and the row closes when the law lands.
- A baseline score is a screening signal until rank agreement with trained specialists has been measured for the
  class and task family (GATE-REFLEX-04). This bead makes no ranking claim.
- KILL-BASELINE (decided at M3 on the design corpus): if fewer than 80% of corpus designs that pass `design check`
  traverse 5 m, synthesis is limited to the classes that pass and the others report `not-synthesizable`.
- Slip: on a low-friction floor the wheel loops track rate while the base does not move. The saturation report
  and the estimator's slip down-weighting make this visible; the controller does not hide it by raising gains.
