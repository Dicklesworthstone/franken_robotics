## Background

Phylum's claim (tier Frontier) is that populations of different body plans can run in one batch as table-driven
cohorts: each morphology is a model table inside padded capacities (level P-B). The plan does not assume this is
the right engine. Decision ADR-PHYLUM-05: a per-family-template arm competes in a head-to-head experiment, and the
plan commits in advance to adopt it if it wins. Reason: templates are the incumbent regime plus per-design rows;
if they cover the mutations agents make and run faster, the table-driven cohort is the wrong engine and should
become an admission and preview path.

The head-to-head has four arms. A: Phylum mixed cohorts. **B (control): per-family templates: at most nine padded
templates, one per family of the eighteen archetypes that share a maximal tree (the grouping is frozen with the
arm), each a fixed maximal tree with lockable joints and per-design parameter tables (level P-A only).** C: an
incumbent's mixed-topology batch. D: the leading incumbent run design by design. This bead builds arm B. A control
arm that is built carelessly makes arm A look good for free, so arm B is built to win: it uses the plain,
unmasked single-structure path (level P-A: values on one tree, one structure table, one parameter table per model
slot, no masks, no padding), which is the cheapest path the step has.

The pre-declared outcome that this arm can trigger (✖ KILL-PHYLUM): if B expresses at least 95% of the mutation
corpus and wins the measure "edit to first accepted result" at K ≤ 128 episodes per design, the templates become
the engine; cohorts are then formed by exact tree with per-design parameter tables, and the table-driven path
remains for admission and preview on `batch-cpu`.

## Scope

In: the frozen grouping of the eighteen archetypes into at most nine families; one maximal-tree template per
family; the lock mechanism; the mapping from a design to (template, lock mask, parameter row) or `inexpressible`
with a reason; the expressibility measurement over the mutation corpus; each template running as a homogeneous
P-A environment on `batch-cpu`.

Out: the experiment itself, its statistics and arms A, C and D (BD-186); P-A slots and parameter rows (BD-169,
a dependency); P-B model tables (BD-170); size buckets (BD-171); device execution of the arm (it rides on the
backends once the P-A path runs there).

## Technical approach

**The grouping (DESIGN proposal; frozen by a recorded hash before the first timed run, as the experiment
requires).** A family is a set of archetypes that are all instances of one maximal tree.

| # | Family | Members | Maximal tree; what a smaller member locks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quadruped | `quad-mammal`, `quad-sprawl`, `wheel-leg` | Four legs of three revolutes plus a wheel joint at each foot (16 actuated). The first two lock the wheel joints |
| 2 | Radial multi-leg | `hexapod-radial`, `octopod` | Eight legs of three revolutes; leg mounts are placement parameters. The hexapod locks and disables two legs |
| 3 | Biped | `biped-point`, `humanoid` | The humanoid tree (29 actuated). The point-foot biped locks arms, waist and ankles |
| 4 | Two-wheel base | `diff-drive`, `balancer-2w`, `mobile-manipulator` | Two driven wheels, a caster and a seven-joint arm. The first two lock the arm; the balancer disables the caster |
| 5 | Steered | `ackermann`, `swerve-4` | Four steer-and-drive modules. The car locks rear steering and couples the front pair |
| 6 | Omnidirectional | `mecanum`, `omni-3` | Four roller wheels with roller angle and placement as parameters. `omni-3` disables one wheel |
| 7 | Tracked | `tracked-flipper` | Two tracks with front and rear flippers |
| 8 | Passive multi-wheel | `rocker-bogie`, `tri-wheel-stair` | Per side: an arm joint carrying one wheel and a sub-arm with two wheels. The tri-wheel cluster locks the sub-arm |
| 9 | Chain | `snake-crawler` | A serial chain at its maximum link count; shorter chains lock distal joints |

**Locking (DESIGN).** A lock is exact elimination, never a stiff limit row. For a design that does not use joint
j of its template:

1. the coordinate q_j is held at a per-design constant; its motion-subspace column is zeroed and its row in every
   factor is an identity row, the same exact rule the padded path uses for padded degrees of freedom;
2. the child link's inertia is folded into the parent's on the host when the parameter row is written:
   𝖯_parent ← 𝖯_parent + G·𝖯_child·Gᵀ, with 𝖯 the 4×4 pseudo-inertia and G the fixed transform at the locked
   value; an absent link has zero inertia and folds to nothing;
3. collision samples of an absent link are disabled by a per-design sample range of zero length; samples of a
   locked but present link are re-parented with the composed transform;
4. an absent actuator's channel is unbound; its card parameters are not read.

**Expressibility.** `fn express(design) -> Result<(TemplateId, LockMask, ParamRow), Inexpressible>`. A design is
expressible exactly when it equals some template instance up to the P-A parameter set (lengths and placements,
inertias, gear ratios, card parameters, radii, friction and roller angles) and a lock mask. Reasons for
`Inexpressible` are an enumeration that the report tallies: `extra-joint`, `joint-class-changed`,
`closure-added`, `contact-law-class-changed`, `limb-count-above-template`, `sensor-layout-outside-template`,
`no-family`. For each edit of the mutation corpus the arm records whether the edited design is expressible and,
if not, the reason. The share expressed is the number the kill rule reads against 95%.

**Running.** Each template compiles once to a single-structure step plan (`single_structure = true`); every
design of the family is one parameter row in a model slot (BD-169). "Runs as a homogeneous environment" means:
one step plan, no masks in the level loops, every world of the batch on the same tree.

**Correctness of an instance.** An instance is not required to be bit-identical to the natively compiled design
(the locked joints change the order of floating-point operations). It is required to be the same rigid system:
forward kinematics and mass properties of the retained links agree to 10⁻¹² relative in `f64`, and a 1 s
drop-and-stand run agrees with the native model within the tier's fidelity envelope.

**Layout and records.** `crates/frob-phylum/src/templates/{families.toml, express.rs, lock.rs}`;
`registries/phylum/template-families.toml` holds the frozen grouping with its hash; the expressibility table is
written as `frob.phylum.express/1` and referenced by the head-to-head's record.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The seed's condition: each template family runs as a homogeneous environment on `batch-cpu`, and the share
      of the mutation corpus the arm can express is recorded, with the tally of reasons for the rest.
- [ ] The grouping has at most nine families, covers all eighteen archetypes, and its hash is recorded before any
      timed run of the head-to-head.
- [ ] Every member archetype maps to its family's template with a lock mask and a parameter row, and its instance
      agrees with the native model: kinematics and mass properties to 10⁻¹² relative.
- [ ] A locked joint is eliminated exactly: zero motion, no limit-row activity, no change in the retained links'
      results when the locked child's (zero) inertia is perturbed.
- [ ] The planted inexpressible edits below are each reported with the right reason; none is silently
      approximated by a nearby instance.
- [ ] Families whose contact laws do not exist at M2 are instantiated and counted for expressibility, and their
      run is recorded `not run: contact law` until the law lands.
- [ ] The arm's measures (3) of the experiment (padding overhead of each template against its members, template
      count, share expressed) are in the record that GATE-PHYLUM-03 publishes.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `families_cover_eighteen_at_most_nine`.
- `express_quad_mammal`: maps to family 1 with the four wheel joints locked.
- `express_hexapod`: family 2 with two legs locked and their samples disabled.
- `lock_is_exact`: a template instance with joint j locked against the same tree with joint j removed: retained
  link poses and the composite inertia agree to 10⁻¹² relative over 10³ random configurations.
- `fold_on_lock_hand_case`: parent 2 kg with rotational inertia 0.02·I kg·m² at its origin, locked child 1 kg with
  0.01·I at (0.3, 0, 0) m → mass 3 kg, centre of mass (0.1, 0, 0) m, inertia diag(0.03, 0.12, 0.12) kg·m² about
  the parent origin.
- `inexpressible_reasons`: add a tail joint to `quad-mammal` → `extra-joint`; change a knee from revolute to
  prismatic → `joint-class-changed`; add a parallel knee linkage → `closure-added`; change a foot from
  `foot-patch` to `wheel-rolling` on `quad-sprawl` → `contact-law-class-changed`; a nine-legged radial design →
  `limb-count-above-template`.
- `param_row_roundtrip`: `express` followed by instantiation reproduces the design's lengths, masses and ratios.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_template_arm.sh`: for each family, builds the template, instantiates each member, runs
`frob sim run --tier batch-cpu` with 64 worlds for 1,000 steps as a homogeneous batch, and compares kinematics
and mass properties with the native model; then runs the expressibility pass over the mutation corpus. NDJSON per
step: command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` (tier `batch-cpu`, determinism class, `ModelId` of
template and of native design, seed), the family, the lock mask's population count, the maximum relative
difference against 10⁻¹², the share expressed with its numerator and denominator, the reason tally, and on
failure the first differing link and value. Summary: families run, families `not run` with reason, share
expressed, gates touched (GATE-PHYLUM-03).

### Known answers and negative controls

- The fold hand case above (mass 3 kg; centre of mass 0.1 m; diag(0.03, 0.12, 0.12) kg·m²); the mutant that adds
  the two inertias without the offset term gives 0.03·I and must fail.
- Negative controls: (a) a lock implemented as a stiff limit row (it shows limit-row activity and a perturbed
  result); (b) an `express` that accepts an edit with an extra joint by dropping it; (c) a template whose padded
  member reads the absent actuator's card.
- Metamorphic: permuting the member designs across model slots changes no per-world result; an expressible edit
  followed by its inverse returns the original parameter row.
- Determinism: twice on one machine and across worker counts within the class `batch-cpu` claims.

## Considerations

- The plan gives the cap (nine), the rule (families that share a maximal tree) and the freeze, not the grouping.
  The table above is a proposal and is the first thing the experiment's owner reviews. A different grouping
  changes the share expressed, which is why it is frozen by hash before data.
- The car's steering linkage is a closure and the rocker-bogie's differential is a closure; a tree template
  represents them only through coupling parameters. Edits that change the closure are `closure-added` or
  inexpressible, and that limitation is part of what the arm measures.
- Padding inside a template is real cost for its small members (a point-foot biped inside the humanoid tree).
  The record reports L_max/E[L] per family so the reader sees it.
- If arm B wins, this bead's templates are the engine: write them as product code, not as a test fixture.
- Compute: the pilot fits the default envelope; the full head-to-head needs the owner's approval (OPEN-PHYLUM-3).
