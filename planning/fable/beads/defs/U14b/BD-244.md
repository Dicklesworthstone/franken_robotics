## Background

Preflight is the verb `design.check`: one verb, eight stages in a fixed order, each able to refuse the next
(decision ADR-SKEL-08). The reason for one verb is that checking before simulating is the cheapest way to remove a
bad design, and a second pre-simulation gate with a second name would be two sources of truth. Stages 1 to 6 need
only the design and land at M1 (BD-110). Stages 7 and 8 also need a task and a scene; they run when those are
supplied and land at M3, when scenes, clearance complexes and the alias search exist:

- **Stage 7, Place** (tier Frontier): reach against the task's heights; sensor coverage of footholds and route;
  clearance of the swept body through the scene's clearance complex. It refuses with FROB-REF-119 when there is
  no admissible route (naming the bottleneck and, if the retrofit catalog has one, the retrofit that removes it)
  or when a required height is out of reach. Budget: 2 s per scene with a compiled clearance complex (TARGET,
  95th percentile).
- **Stage 8, Information** (tier Frontier): a search for two admitted worlds with the same observation transcript
  and disjoint safe actions. It refuses with FROB-REF-120 on an observation alias under every admitted probe,
  returning the two worlds, the transcript and the cheapest separating sensor, rate or probe. Budget: 10 s by
  default; the budget is the caller's.

The two mechanisms are their own beads (N14b-12 and N14b-13), each behind a paper-scale pilot. This bead is the
integration: it makes them stages of `design.check`, builds the planted cases that the gate needs, measures the
gate and supplies the measurement that decides the kill gate KILL-SKEL-ALIAS.

## Scope

In: registration of stages 7 and 8 in the stage runner under the contract of N14b-10 (order, budgets,
`undecided`, `--stages`, `--all`, binding); landing the two late rows that N14bx-01's second table assigns to
these stages at M3 (A-18 clearance and route feasibility, N19b-34, in stage 7 after A-05's reach check; A-17
structural observability rank, BD-120, first in stage 8, seeding the alias search), so that they turn from
`not-run: not-landed` to run when bound; A-17 along a route in stage 8: the call of N20a-34's
`observability::route_finding` and its weak-stretch finding with typed repairs in the design-check envelope (the
plan's design-check warning on a route stretch whose smallest Gramian eigenvalue falls below a threshold); the
request surface `--task T --scene S`; the corpus of 40 planted
cases; the GATE-SKEL-04 measurement for stages 7 and 8; the hand-built-policy check that KILL-SKEL-ALIAS reads;
the end-to-end script, and the two `design check` steps of N19b-34's script that wait for stage 7.

Out: the place analysis (N14b-12) and the alias wrapper (N14b-13); the clearance complex (BD-233); the alias
witness search (BD-240); coverage and curvature analyses (BD-243); the route profile, λ_warn and the library
entry `observability::route_finding` with its repairs (N20a-34); stages 1 to 6 (BD-110); the kill-gate decision
record (N14b-16).

## Technical approach

**Request and order.** `frob design check <design> [--task T] [--scene S] [--stages a-b] [--all]
[--strict-data] [--time-budget <duration>] --json` (`--time-budget` is the verb's option for stage 8's budget,
default 10 s, input `time_budget` of the verb registry). Binding is per analysis, by the staging contract
(N14b-10) and its second table (N14bx-01): stage 7's reach, passability and coverage need a task and a scene,
A-18 needs only a scene with a compiled clearance complex (with no task it checks the routes the scene pack
declares); stage 8's A-17 runs on any design with a sensor, and the alias search needs a task. An unbound
analysis is `not-run: unbound` naming the flag that binds it, a stage with nothing bound is `not-run: unbound`,
and the envelope says so; nothing is silently skipped. Stage 7 runs only if stages 1 to 6 did not refuse (unless
`--all`), and stage 8 only if stage 7 did not refuse (unless `--all`).

**Stage record (per stage).** `{stage, status, evidence, model_id, scene_id?, task_id?, wall_ms, budget_ms,
findings[], refusal?}` with `status` one of `passed`, `refused`, `undecided`, `not-run` (the wire forms fixed by
the staging contract N14b-10; `not-run` carries its reason, here `unbound`). A stage that exhausts its
budget returns `undecided` with what it had established; `undecided` is never a pass and never a refusal. Every
refusal carries ranked repairs as typed edits, which become the envelope's `next_actions`.

**A-17 along a route (stage 8).** When `--task` and `--scene` are bound and the task names a route of the scene,
the A-17 step of stage 8, after the structural rank and before the alias search, calls N20a-34's library entry
`observability::route_finding(design, task, scene, route, budget)` (the structural-rank form at every station,
the linearized form at a coarse spacing, the same code path as `sense.observability`). Each weak stretch it
returns becomes a finding of kind `A-17.weak-stretch`, policy `Reported` (exit 2; never a refusal: FROB-REF-120
stays the alias search's), carrying the stretch's first and last station and its length in metres, the weak
direction by name (for a corridor, translation along the corridor axis), λ_min and λ_warn with units, and
`below_by = 1 − λ_min/λ_warn`, with N20a-34's ranked repairs as typed edits (`move-sensor`; a scene retrofit
that adds a fiducial or door-frame feature within range; a card swap that widens a sensor's range or field of
view), which enter `next_actions`. DESIGN: a finding kind has one severity in N14bx-01's typed rows (High, as
for `A-17.rank-deficient`), so how far λ_min falls below λ_warn travels as `below_by`, which orders the repairs;
the kind is a row of A-17's `kinds` in N14bx-01's `CHECK_ANALYSES` with its policy row in
`registries/preflight.toml` (reported, High). The call is charged to stage 8's budget: on exhaustion the stage is
`undecided` with the stations examined. With no route bound, A-17 runs its structural rank alone and its record
lists the route form as unbound, naming `--task`, so nothing is skipped silently.

**Exit and evidence.** Exit 0 if every stage run passed; 2 if findings or an `undecided` stage exist; 3 on a
refusal. Evidence is the claim product of the inputs: the weakest class among the design's analyses, the scene's
clearance certificates (`Certified` relative to the compiled scene) and the alias witness (class by its source:
`Certified` for a structural or enumerated witness, `Tested` for a searched one).

**The 40 planted cases (DESIGN: the gate fixes the count and the pass rule; this bead fixes the composition and
commits its hash before any stage is tuned).** Stored in `tests/fixtures/preflight/place-info/` with a label
file. Twenty for stage 7 and twenty for stage 8.

| Stage | Cases | Planted truth |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | 6 doorway cases: a box-shaped base against door widths on both sides of its width | 3 pass with a witness path; 3 refuse (119) with the door named as bottleneck |
| 7 | 4 right-angle corridor cases around the closed form L = 2√2·a − 2b | 2 pass, 2 refuse; lengths chosen outside the refinement band |
| 7 | 4 reach cases: a shelf or tray height against the arm's or leg's workspace | 2 pass, 2 refuse (119, height out of reach) |
| 7 | 4 coverage cases: footholds or route outside every sensor's field of view | 2 pass, 2 findings |
| 7 | 2 retrofit cases: a 20 mm threshold on the only route for a small-wheeled base | Refuse (119) naming the threshold; the retrofit is named when the catalog exists |
| 8 | 4 beacon cases: two beacons with mirrored robot positions; the same with a third beacon | 2 refuse (120), 2 pass |
| 8 | 4 left-or-right payload cases | 2 refuse, 2 pass (a label supplied before the start) |
| 8 | 4 junction cases: a target below the scan plane at the end of one of two mirrored branches | 2 refuse, 2 pass (a camera that sees a marker) |
| 8 | 4 passive-alias cases where a permitted probe separates the worlds | Advisory `aliased-passive` naming the probe; no refusal |
| 8 | 4 controls with distinct transcripts, including repeated but distinguishable aisles | Pass |

"Decided correctly" means the stage's status, code and named feature equal the label within the stage's budget.
An `undecided` result is not correct and not wrong. "Decided wrongly" is a refusal on a pass-labelled case or a
pass on a refuse-labelled case.

**The hand-built-policy check.** For every stage-8 witness returned on the corpus, a hand-written policy
(per fixture: go left, go right, probe then choose) is run in both worlds of the witness on the referee. If any
policy satisfies the task in both worlds, the witness is false and the first clause of KILL-SKEL-ALIAS fires.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SKEL-04 for stages 7 and 8 (TARGET): of the 40 planted cases at least 36 are decided correctly within
      budget and none is decided wrongly, measured by `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance
      --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-SKEL-04` with the three registered negative
      controls below each caught in the same run; the counts and per-case times enter `registries/gates.toml`
      only through `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SKEL-04 --receipt <hash>
      --part M3` (N38b-19), from a session that did not implement stage 7 or stage 8 (the M1 part is BD-110's).
- [ ] KILL-SKEL-ALIAS has its measurement on file: the number of stage-8 planted cases decided within budget
      (trigger: fewer than half) and the outcome of the hand-built-policy check on every witness (trigger: any
      witness defeated).
- [ ] Without `--task` and `--scene` the two stages report `not-run: unbound` (stage 8's A-17 still runs on a
      design with a sensor); with `--scene` alone stage 7 runs A-18 and lists the task-bound analyses as
      `not-run: unbound` naming `--task`; with both, both stages run in order after stage 6; `--stages 7-8` runs
      only them on a design whose stages 1 to 6 are memoized as passed.
- [ ] N19b-34's `scripts/e2e/a18_clearance.sh` runs its two `frob design check carrier-a --scene home-fixture
      --json` steps (before and after the repair edit) instead of `e2e_skip "needs BD-244"`, and passes: refused
      with 119 naming the hall-to-bedroom bottleneck before, stage 7 passed after.
- [ ] On N20a-34's corridor route (the end wall out of range at the start), `frob design check` with the task and
      scene bound reports in stage 8 one `A-17.weak-stretch` finding naming translation along the corridor axis,
      with its length and a typed repair, exit 2; with the end wall within range from the start it reports none.
      Stage 11 of `scripts/e2e/sense_observability.sh` (N20a-47's script, run unchanged) passes.
- [ ] Stages 7 and 8 list A-14 (N33b-17's coverage, the result `analyze.coverage` returns) among their analyses
      with its timing inside the stage's budget, in every stage record of the corpus run.
- [ ] A stage over budget returns `undecided` with its partial findings and never a pass or a refusal.
- [ ] Every refusal carries at least one ranked repair as a typed edit and a `next_actions` entry.
- [ ] The label file's hash is committed before either mechanism is tuned on the corpus, and a relabel changes
      the corpus identity.
- [ ] Both stages are reachable through `frob --json` and MCP; cancellation during stage 8 ends the search and
      returns `undecided`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `stages_7_8_unbound_reported`: no task or scene → both `not-run: unbound` (A-17 excepted on a design with a
  sensor), exit unchanged from stages 1 to 6.
- `scene_only_runs_a18`: `--scene` only → stage 7 runs A-18; A-05, A-07 passability and A-14 are `not-run:
  unbound` naming `--task`.
- `late_rows_landed`: at M3 the rows A-17 and A-18 report `run` when bound and never `not-landed`.
- `a14_listed_with_timing`: every stage-7 and stage-8 record of the corpus lists A-14 with its wall time.
- `route_weak_stretch_surfaced`: N20a-34's corridor route with the end wall out of range for the first stretch,
  `--stages 8` with task and scene → stage 8 `passed` with one finding `A-17.weak-stretch` (weak direction
  translation along the corridor axis, `below_by` = 1 since λ_min is zero to tolerance there, `repairs[0].edit`
  in the typed-edit vocabulary), exit 2; the end wall within range from the start → no such finding, exit 0; a
  planted stage 8 that drops `route_finding`'s findings fails the first assertion.
- `stage_order_and_stop`: a stage-7 refusal stops before stage 8; `--all` runs both and returns both findings.
- `undecided_on_budget`: stage 8 with a 1 ms budget on a 10⁴-world family → `undecided`, exit 2, partial count of
  world pairs examined present.
- `refusal_has_typed_repairs`: every 119 and 120 in the corpus has `repairs[0].edit` in the typed-edit vocabulary.
- `evidence_is_weakest_input`: a searched alias witness makes the envelope's class `Tested`.
- `corpus_label_hash_pinned`.
- `hand_policy_check`: on the referee library, each hand-built policy of every `aliased-all-probes` witness of
  the stage-8 cases in both worlds; a planted witness whose worlds a probe-then-choose policy separates is
  reported defeated (the first clause of KILL-SKEL-ALIAS trips); on the corpus the count of defeated witnesses is
  reported as the measurement and is not asserted.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_preflight_place_info.sh` (this bead owns it; header `bead=BD-244 milestone=M3
labels=skeleton,preflight,verb:design.check gates=GATE-SKEL-04,KILL-SKEL-ALIAS seed=1 needs=cpu
plants=bounding-disc,local-rank-proof,pass-on-budget`; it sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh`). N14b-12 and N14b-13
supply rows for their own fixtures, which run in steps 3 and 4. Run verbs carry a key derived from (script, run
namespace, case, step); `design.check` and `task.check` are read verbs and carry none.

1. Corpus identity: the BLAKE3 of the label file equals the committed hash; a relabelled copy gives a different
   corpus identity.
2. Per case, its scene: `frob --json scene gen --family <F> --seed <s> [--param …] --key ppi-<case>-scene` or
   `frob --json scene compile <source> --name <case> --key ppi-<case>-scene`, then `frob --json scene fit` on the
   pack for the clearance complex as N14b-12's rows do, and `frob --json task check <task>` (its `alias_family`
   block, N14b-17, logged for the stage-8 cases).
3. Per stage-7 case: `frob --json design check <design> --task T --scene S --stages 7`; status, code and named
   bottleneck or height against the label; A-14 listed with its timing.
4. Per stage-8 case: `frob --json design check <design> --task T --scene S --stages 8`; status, code, label and
   witness pair against the label; A-17 listed first and A-14 with its timing.
4a. The route leg of A-17: `frob --json design check <design> --task <corridor-route task> --scene <corridor scene>
   --stages 8` on N20a-34's corridor route, with the end wall out of range at the start and then within range:
   one `A-17.weak-stretch` finding with its length, `below_by` and first typed repair, exit 2, then none, exit 0.
   Stage 11 of N20a-47's `scripts/e2e/sense_observability.sh` is the same check from that script's side.
5. The binding matrix on one clean archetype: no `--task` and no `--scene` (stages 7 and 8 `not-run: unbound`,
   A-17 run); `--scene` only (A-18 run, the rest `not-run: unbound` naming `--task`); both; `--stages 7-8` with
   stages 1 to 6 served as memoized; `--all` on a case that refuses at stage 7.
6. Budget: `--time-budget 1ms` on the 10⁴-world family: stage 8 `undecided`, exit 2, the count of pairs examined
   present; a cancellation sent during stage 8 returns `undecided`.
7. The hand-built-policy check: for each `aliased-all-probes` witness of step 4, each hand-built policy of the
   case (registered under `fixtures/controllers/` as `preflight-<case>-<policy>`) in both worlds: `frob --json sim
   run <design> --scene S --task T --family-world <i> --controller fixture:preflight-<case>-<policy> --tier
   referee --seed 1 --key ppi-<case>-<policy>-w<i>` (one key per world and policy; `--family-world <i>` is
   sim.run's input owned by N14b-17, reached through N14b-13: world i of the task's declared alias family, in
   index order, applied in place of the randomization draw); a policy that satisfies the task in both worlds is
   logged as the witness defeated.
8. N19b-34's two `frob --json design check carrier-a --scene home-fixture` steps, before and after its repair
   edit, in place of its `e2e_skip "needs BD-244"`.
9. The gate run of the acceptance criteria (g1_known_answers with `gauntlet-mutations`), its receipt hash
   logged; the record itself is made through `record_gate` by a session that did not implement stage 7 or 8.

NDJSON per step, to `target/e2e-logs/skel_preflight_place_info/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it
(STD-E2E): `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time against the
stage budget, the A-14 timing, the envelope's `evidence` block, `ModelId`, `SceneId`, task identity and its
family digest, stage statuses, refusal code and the named bottleneck or witness pair, the label,
`correct|undecided|wrong`, and on a wrong decision the witness or path artifact needed to reproduce. Summary:
correct, undecided and wrong counts for each stage against 36 of 40 and zero wrong; the stage-8 count decided
within budget against KILL-SKEL-ALIAS's 10 of 20; witnesses defeated; controls caught; gates touched
(GATE-SKEL-04, KILL-SKEL-ALIAS; the decision itself is N14b-16's).

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-291: at least 36 of 40 decided correctly within budget and none decided wrongly; the planted cases are the
  controls.
- KA-274, CX-132: beacons at (−1, 0) and (1, 0), robots at (0, 1) and (0, −1): both read √2 from each, the local
  information matrix is the identity at both points, and the task needs opposite actions: an alias. A third
  beacon at (0, 2) reads 1 and 3 and separates them whenever the range error is below 1.
- KA-275: with two equally likely aliased worlds and noisy sensors no policy succeeds with probability above
  (1 + TV)/2.
- Negative controls registered for the gate: (a) a stage 7 that tests a bounding disc for a rectangular base
  (it passes a doorway narrower than the body or refuses a corridor the body passes); (b) a stage 8 that accepts a
  full-rank local information matrix as proof of distinguishability (it passes the beacon case; CX-132's plant
  `N14b-13:cx-132`); (c) a stage that reports `pass` when its budget ran out. All three are built under
  `--features gauntlet-mutations` and caught in the gate run.
- Determinism: stage results are identical twice on one machine and across worker counts; the alias search's
  enumeration order is fixed by world index.

## Considerations

- KILL-SKEL-ALIAS (decided at M3): if it fires, stage 8 becomes an advisory report built on sensor coverage and
  refuses nothing. The stage keeps its place in the order, and its findings keep their schema, so no caller
  changes.
- Stage 7 inherits the clearance complex's kill gate: if clearance complexes fall back to route-graph
  traversability for a nominal footprint, stage 7 uses door and aisle passability on route graphs and labels its
  verdicts heuristic.
- The retrofit catalog lands at M4. Until then a stage-7 refusal names the bottleneck and reports
  `retrofit: catalog-absent`.
- OPEN-SKEL-3 (how a task with continuous world parameters declares the finite family stage 8 searches) must be
  decided before stage 8 is funded; its default is the task's own randomization grid, recorded in the witness.
- Stages 7 and 8 carry no total time budget: they depend on the size of the scene and of the world family.
