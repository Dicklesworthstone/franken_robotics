## Background

The archetype catalog (`assets/archetypes/`, listed by `design.templates`) is complete when all eighteen
parameterized designs exist. Four landed at M1 (`quad-mammal`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`). The other
fourteen are written in three group beads (legged: N14b-02; wheeled: N14b-03; tracked, hybrid, manipulator and
chain: N14b-04). This bead is where the catalog is closed as a whole and its gates are measured: every archetype
compiles and passes preflight stages 1 to 6 with no refusal, has its symmetry groups on file and confirmed by
the compiler, and has a mass within 15% of its reference robot where one exists.

Three gates close in parts and complete here. They state no milestone of their own because each speaks of all
eighteen archetypes: rows for the first four are recorded at M1, and the whole at M3 when all eighteen exist.

- GATE-SKEL-03 (Known answer): each of the eighteen archetypes compiles, passes stages 1 to 6 of `design check`
  with no refusal, has the symmetry groups on file, and stands or rolls under its baseline controller at the
  milestone the standing gate gives for it. This bead closes the first three clauses; the fourth is BD-247,
  which depends on this bead and enters the gate's M3 part once the standing and rolling rows exist.
- GATE-SKEL-07 (Known answer): shell moments equal their closed forms exactly, and the mass of each archetype
  lies within 15% of the mass on file for its reference robot where one exists. This bead closes the mass
  clause and enters the gate's M3 part; the shell clause belongs to the geometry and inertia functor (BD-062,
  first measured at M1; the gate's tier run repeats it).
- GATE-MARROW-03 (Known answer): Γ_mech and Γ_obs for the eighteen archetypes and for 24 planted asymmetries (a
  moved sensor, a one-sided payload, a changed sole material, a reversed gear) equal the groups on file, with
  the correct breaker named in every case and zero disagreements. This bead supplies the archetype side: the
  eighteen groups on file, the 24 planted designs and a catalog test with zero disagreements. The gate's M3
  part is entered by N13-22, which depends on this bead and adds the sensor-fitted Γ_obs rows and the agreement
  of `analyze.symmetry` with `sense.symmetry`; one bead enters a part, so that a later, wider measurement
  cannot contradict a pass already on record.

Γ_mech is the mechanical symmetry group of the body as built; Γ_obs is its subgroup that also preserves the
sensors, the payload and the task. Only Γ_obs may be used for weight sharing, deduplication and gait orbits. A
design may state the groups it expects, and the statement is an assertion: the compiler computes both groups and
refuses on a mismatch (FROB-REF-109), naming the element that breaks the excluded generator.

## Scope

In: the catalog-wide known answer for the groups (the table below) and the check that the eighteen per-archetype
records agree with it; the 24 planted-asymmetry designs; the mass rows for every archetype with a reference; the
measurement of the three gates over all eighteen (the M3 run of `scripts/e2e/skel_archetypes.sh` with the step
this bead adds for the 24 variants) and the entry of GATE-SKEL-07's M3 part; `design.templates` reporting all
eighteen as available.

Out, with the bead that has it: the per-archetype golden, where "on file" lives: the pinned default instance
`zoo/<name>.fbot`, the expectation file `zoo/<name>.expect.toml` (schema `frob.zoo.expect/1`) with its `[symmetry]`
block, preflight rows and controls, the variant directory, and the generic test that recomputes one archetype's
expectations (NEG-30 for the format and test, the eighteen archetype beads for the files). This bead keeps no
second per-archetype record: it reads the groups on file from those expectation files and stores its planted
asymmetries as zoo variants. The fourteen templates themselves (N14b-02, -03, -04); the baseline controllers,
the standing rows and the entry of GATE-SKEL-03's M3 part (BD-247); the symmetry oracle files, the script
`analyze_symmetry.sh` and the entry of GATE-MARROW-03's M3 part (N13-22; the M1 part was N13-21's); the script
`skel_archetypes.sh` itself and the M1 parts (N14b-06); the symmetry computation (A-03); the design corpus built
from these archetypes (BD-248).

## Technical approach

**Groups on file** (the catalog test's table; the compiler must compute each). DESIGN, so that one record holds
each archetype's group: the group "on file" for an archetype is the field `gamma_mech` of the `[symmetry]` block
of its zoo expectation file (NEG-30), and the default Γ_obs is `gamma_obs_default` there. The table below is the
catalog-wide known answer KA-270; the catalog test asserts three-way equality for every archetype: the
compiler's Γ_mech, the expectation file's, and this table's row.

| Γ_mech as built | Archetypes |
|---|---|
| C₂ × C₂ | `quad-mammal`, `quad-sprawl`, `mecanum`, `tracked-flipper`, `wheel-leg` |
| D₆ (C₆ with handed legs) | `hexapod-radial` |
| D₈ radial; C₂ in a spider layout | `octopod` |
| C₂ | `biped-point`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `ackermann`, `rocker-bogie`, `balancer-2w` |
| D₃ | `omni-3` |
| D₄ on a square base | `swerve-4` |
| C₂, with C₃ or D₃ in each cluster | `tri-wheel-stair` |
| Trivial with the arm off-centre | `mobile-manipulator` |
| C₂ under head-to-tail reversal | `snake-crawler` |

**The 24 planted asymmetries (DESIGN composition; the gate fixes the count and the four kinds).** Four kinds on
six archetypes: `quad-mammal`, `hexapod-radial`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`, `swerve-4`. DESIGN, so
that the gate has one set of 24 and not two: this is the distribution that the symmetry gate's oracle directory
(`tests/fixtures/oracle/symmetry/`, N13-21) froze at M1, when the cases of the four archetypes that existed
then were measured, and that N13-22 measures at M3 through `scripts/e2e/analyze_symmetry.sh`; a set with
`tracked-flipper` in place of `diff-drive` would leave the M1 cases of `diff-drive` outside the gate. The six
cover the group families C₂ × C₂ (legged, and wheeled with handed wheels), D₆, C₂ (legged and wheeled) and D₄.
Each is a one-edit variant of the archetype's default instance with a label naming the group it must reduce to
and the breaker. It is stored in the zoo's variant directory of its archetype,
`zoo/<name>.variants/asym-<kind>.fbot` (`<kind>` is `sensor`, `payload`, `sole` or `gear`) with its own
expectation file (`frob.zoo.expect/1`: the `[symmetry]` block states the groups after the edit and the breaker),
so that a planted asymmetry and a zoo control are one kind of file; an earlier draft stored the variants beside
the template in `assets/archetypes/` with a separate label format. The edit of each kind is the one the oracle's
case file applies, with the same numbers, so that the variant file and the oracle case describe one design.

| Kind | Edit, applied to a fresh copy of the default instance | What must happen |
|---|---|---|
| A moved sensor (`asym-sensor`) | `move-sensor`: one mount of a symmetric sensor pair shifted by 5 mm | Γ_mech unchanged; Γ_obs loses every element that exchanged the pair; that sensor's mount is the breaker |
| A one-sided payload (`asym-payload`) | `add-payload` of 2 kg on the left side | Γ_mech unchanged; Γ_obs loses the elements that move the payload; the payload is the breaker |
| A changed sole material (`asym-sole`) | `set-contact-law` on one foot keeping the law and changing its interface card (the tyre card of one wheel on the wheeled archetypes) to another card that is legal there: its first material equals the end effector's, or stage 1 refuses with FROB-REF-121 before any group is computed | Γ_mech loses the elements that move that foot or wheel, although the encoder permutation of each lost element still exists; the first mismatch is that interface card |
| A reversed gear (`asym-gear`) | `regear` with the opposite sign on one joint | Γ_mech loses the elements that move that joint; the first mismatch is the signed transmission ratio |

Both groups are recomputed after every one of these edits. DESIGN (the reading the zoo expectation file of
`quad-mammal` and the symmetry beads N13-16 and N13-21 fix): a payload reduces Γ_obs only. The algorithm filters
payloads at the step that reduces Γ_mech to Γ_obs ("maps every payload to a payload"), a payload is never a
colour of the mechanical tree, and the law "removing every sensor, payload and task term gives Γ_obs = Γ_mech"
holds only under that reading; the design-language sentence that a payload "enters both symmetry groups" means
that both are recomputed. An earlier form of this table had the payload reduce both groups, which that law
forbids and which the symmetry oracle would have reported as a disagreement.

**Mass rows.** For each preset that cites a reference robot the compiled mass is compared with the reference's
mass on file: pass if within 15%. The mass on file is `mass_on_file.value_kg` in
`assets/robots/<name>/manifest.toml` (schema `frob.robot.asset/1`): the exact decimal sum of the body masses at
the commit pinned in `tools/oracle/models.toml`, written with its source and read date by the validation of the
reference-robot bead. `scripts/e2e/skel_archetypes.sh` reads it there (the reference-robot beads of the M3 rows,
ROBOT-cassie and ROBOT-iiwa7, are dependencies of this bead; those of the M1 rows are dependencies of N14b-06)
and fails if the window in its table, or the mass `design templates` reports, disagrees. Archetypes with
references: `quad-mammal` (Go1, Go2, A1, ANYmal B and C, Spot), `humanoid` (G1 at 33.34014202 kg over the
38-link source; H1), `biped-point` (Cassie), `diff-drive` (Stretch 3 base), `mecanum` (TIAGo omni base),
`mobile-manipulator` (presets `@iiwa7-stretch3` and `@iiwa7-tiago`, each against the sum of the masses on file
of its arm and its base). The other twelve record `no reference on file`, which is not a pass and not a failure
of the gate (the gate says "where one exists").

**Measurement and record.** One command per archetype and preset: `frob design check <id> --stages 1-6 --json`
and `frob analyze symmetry <id> --json`, run by `scripts/e2e/skel_archetypes.sh` at M3 over all eighteen. A
gate part is measured by the gate's tier run, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- <gate>`, which drives the same table and variants and catches
the registered negative controls in the same run, and it is entered by the gate recorder (`cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate <gate> --receipt <hash> --part M3`, N38b-19) from a session
that did not implement the archetypes; the row carries the commit, the command, the receipt hash and the
measured counts. Who enters what:

- GATE-SKEL-07, M3 part: this bead. `pass` only if every preset with a reference is inside its window and the
  shell clause holds in the same run; measured: rows inside their window, rows with no reference (twelve).
- GATE-SKEL-03, M3 part: BD-247. This bead's run measures the compile, stage and group clauses for all
  eighteen; at this point the standing and rolling rows of the fourteen are logged `not run` (no baseline
  controller yet), never passed, so the part cannot be entered here.
- GATE-MARROW-03, M3 part: N13-22. This bead's catalog test and script step show eighteen of eighteen groups
  and 24 of 24 planted variants with zero disagreements, which is the archetype side N13-22 builds on.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The seed's condition: each of the fourteen compiles and passes stages 1 to 6 with no refusal; the M3 part
      of GATE-SKEL-07 is recorded `pass` through the recorder for all eighteen; for GATE-MARROW-03 the catalog
      test and the script step report eighteen of eighteen groups and 24 of 24 planted variants with zero
      disagreements and every breaker correct (the measurement on which N13-22 enters the gate's M3 part).
- [ ] The M3 run of `scripts/e2e/skel_archetypes.sh` over eighteen entries passes its compile, stage, group and
      mass assertions; the standing and rolling rows of the fourteen are logged `not run` with the reason and
      are not counted as passed.
- [ ] `frob design templates --json` reports eighteen available archetypes, and every `DesignId` is identical
      on Linux and macOS.
- [ ] The compiler's Γ_mech equals the table above for all eighteen and equals `gamma_mech` in each archetype's
      zoo expectation file; each template's assertion is accepted.
- [ ] The 24 planted asymmetries exist as zoo variants (four per archetype on the six archetypes named above)
      and each passes the zoo's generic test for a variant as well as the catalog test; the 24 (archetype, kind)
      pairs equal the list frozen in the symmetry oracle directory at M1.
- [ ] All 24 planted asymmetries reduce the group as labelled and name the labelled breaker: zero disagreements.
      In the six payload variants and the six sensor variants Γ_mech equals the archetype's group as built.
- [ ] Every archetype with a reference has a mass row within 15%, each against the `mass_on_file` of its
      reference asset; the twelve without are recorded as such.
- [ ] The task-binding mutation test `reads_superset_mutation` (N24ax-03: for every leaf of a design, a change
      that alters a bound name or an exported value implies the leaf is in the binding's tracked read set)
      passes with zero violations over every (design, task) pair that binds among all eighteen archetypes: it
      iterates the catalog, so the fourteen enter it when they become available here.
- [ ] Zero refusals on the eighteen archetypes at default parameters (the clause of the preflight gate that
      speaks of the archetypes).
- [ ] The negative controls below are caught, and the gate rows cite them.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `catalog_has_eighteen_available`.
- `groups_on_file_all_eighteen`: one assertion per row of the table; `zoo_groups_equal_catalog_table`: for each
  archetype the expectation file's `gamma_mech` equals its row, and a planted disagreement (one file edited to
  `C2`) is reported with the archetype named.
- `planted_asymmetries_24`: for each, the computed groups and breaker equal the label.
- `planted_set_is_the_frozen_list`: the 24 variant files are exactly four kinds (`sensor`, `payload`, `sole`,
  `gear`) on `quad-mammal`, `hexapod-radial`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`, `swerve-4`, equal to the list
  in the symmetry oracle directory; for each case whose oracle case file is on file (the sixteen of the four M1
  archetypes) the variant's groups after the edit and its breaker equal the case file's. A variant added for a
  seventh archetype, or one removed, fails with the pair named.
- `payload_reduces_obs_only`: in each of the six `asym-payload` variants Γ_mech equals the archetype's group as
  built and Γ_obs is the subgroup that fixes the payload (for `quad-mammal`: Γ_mech = C₂ × C₂, Γ_obs = C₂
  generated by the transverse mirror). Planted defect: a colouring that includes payloads in the mechanical
  tree reduces Γ_mech and is caught.
- `changed_sole_keeps_permutation_breaks_group`: the encoder permutation of the mirror still exists and the
  group element does not.
- `mass_rows`: each preset within 15% of its reference; `humanoid@g1` between 28.339 and 38.341 kg; each
  `mobile-manipulator` preset against the sum of its arm's and its base's masses on file.
- `no_refusal_at_defaults`: stages 1 to 6 on all eighteen.
- `templates_use_no_physics_default`: removing any fill, material, contact law, limit or current convention from
  any template makes its compile refuse.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_archetypes.sh` (written at M1 by N14b-06 over the archetypes then available), run at M3 over
all eighteen. This bead adds to it the step over the 24 planted asymmetries (`frob --json design new
<ns>-asym-<name>-<kind> --file zoo/<name>.variants/asym-<kind>.fbot --key <ns>-asym-<name>-<kind>-1`, a
`mutate` verb with one key per variant and pass, then `frob --json analyze symmetry <ns>-asym-<name>-<kind>`,
a read verb, asserting the reduced groups and the breaker against the variant's expectation file) and the M3
summary fields below. The log is the script's
(`target/e2e-logs/skel_archetypes/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript, through the shared helper of
STD-E2E); every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (the script's bead, N14b-06, with `subject: "BD-246"` on the
lines of the step added here), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact
command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
identities, seed). Per archetype, preset and variant it carries `ModelId`, the computed and on-file groups with
the breaker, stage statuses and per-stage times against their budgets, the mass in kg against its window with
the reference's mass on file and its source, digests where two runs are compared, and on failure the first
differing value and the paths of the saved envelope and expectation file. Summary line: archetypes passed,
asymmetries agreed out of 24, mass rows passed and `no reference` count, standing rows `not run`, assertions
passed and failed, controls caught, gates touched (GATE-SKEL-03, GATE-SKEL-07, GATE-MARROW-03).

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-270: the table of Γ_mech above. KA-259: the groups on file for the eighteen and the 24 planted asymmetries,
  with the correct breaker in every case; the 24 are the controls, each of which must change the group.
- CX-118: a quadruped with one forward lidar has Γ_mech of order 4 and Γ_obs of order 2; a policy tied under the
  larger group must walk backwards as the mirror image of walking forwards while seeing only forwards.
- CX-121: changing one foot's sole material leaves the encoder permutation intact and breaks the transition
  symmetry.
- CX-133, KA-276: `quad-mammal` with a forward lidar and `assert_obs: C2xC2` is refused (FROB-REF-109) with the
  lidar mount as breaker.
- Negative controls for the mass clause: a template whose torso fill is changed from a shell to solid (the mass
  leaves its window); for the group clause: a symmetry computation that reads the declaration instead of
  computing (it accepts a false assertion).

## Considerations

- GATE-SKEL-03 stays open at M3 until BD-247 enters its part with the standing and rolling rows, and
  GATE-MARROW-03 until N13-22 enters its part; this bead's close reason says which clauses it measured and
  cites its receipt. If N13-22's wider measurement disagrees with a variant's label, the label is not edited to
  match the candidate: the difference is a finding against the archetype or the variant, decided by the
  Skeleton lead.
- The row of `snake-crawler` in the table above is the catalog's cell. N14bp1-01 carries an open question on
  it (a left-right symmetric chain also has the sagittal mirror, and the reversal depends on where actuator
  mass is carried, so the computed group is expected to be C₂ × C₂ or C₂ by the sagittal mirror); the three-way
  equality of that row is `blocked` with the question named until the Skeleton lead amends the cell or the
  term, and it is never recorded as passed on an edited expectation.
- The six archetypes of the planted set were fixed at M1; changing the set after the first measurement would
  move the gate's cases, so a change is an amendment recorded in the oracle directory and in the zoo together.
- Masses on file come from public models whose figures the plan marks as reported; each row carries its source
  and read date, and a figure that changes on re-reading changes the row by a dated amendment.
- A preset approximates a reference robot's mass and proportions only. No claim is made that an archetype is
  that robot; reference robots enter as themselves by import.
- The morphology generator (M3) and the design corpus (about 200 designs) are built on these templates, so a
  late change to a template after their hashes are frozen changes their identities.
