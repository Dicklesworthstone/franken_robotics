## Background

Beside the eighteen archetypes the catalog names variants that sit behind feature flags and off the critical
path. One of them is tier Solid and is needed at M1: the **open modular class**, any composition of modules.
Random designs from this class drive the property tests of the compiler's gate (functor laws and
incremental-equals-full on 10⁴ random designs and edits). The same population is what the other Skeleton gates
quantify over: canonicalization and the two views on 10⁴ random designs; the invalidation invariant on 10⁴ random
edits; zero refusals on 10³ random valid designs; the fold of fixed joints on 10³ random trees; and the Phylum
pilot's 1,000 generated designs.

A gate that says "on 10⁴ random designs" is only as strong as the generator. If the generator emits only
quadrupeds with mirrored legs, the gate tests almost nothing. This bead therefore makes the generator a
specified, versioned, seeded object with a coverage report, instead of an ad hoc helper inside one test.

It is not the morphology generator of the Phylum subsystem (a probabilistic attribute grammar with a
`GeneratorId`, exact log-probabilities and train and test splits, which lands at M3 and defines the distribution
that a family policy is trained on). This one makes no claim about a distribution of interest; it exists to hit
every constructor, component, field and edit kind.

## Scope

In: the definition of the open modular class; a design generator in three modes; an edit generator over the
typed-edit vocabulary; a shrinker; a coverage report; a development binary. Out: the Phylum generator (BD-287);
the property tests themselves (in the beads that own each gate); the seeded-defect corpus (N14b-18).

## Technical approach

**The class.** A design is in the open modular class when it is a root body with any number of modules mounted
on its mechanical ports through `use(module, port, bindings)`, wrapped in any nesting of `attach`, `serial`,
`mirror`, `radial`, `scale` and `replace`, with or without `closure`. No archetype template is involved.

**Location and form (DESIGN).** `crates/frob-design/src/testing/{gen_design.rs, gen_edit.rs, shrink.rs,
coverage.rs}` behind the cargo feature `testing`, so that every crate's tests can use it as a dev-dependency
without a cycle. A binary `frob-gen-designs` in `frob-conformance` writes files. It is a test utility and is not
in the verb registry.

```rust
pub enum Mode { Raw, WellFormed, ValidByConstruction }
pub struct GenConfig { mode: Mode, max_limbs: u8, max_depth: u8, closures: bool,
                       fixed_joint_rate: Ratio, sensors: bool, payloads: bool, version: u32 }
pub fn sample_design(cfg: &GenConfig, seed: u64, index: u64) -> (Design, Derivation);
pub fn sample_edit(cfg: &GenConfig, design: &Design, seed: u64, index: u64) -> TypedEdit;
pub fn shrink(design: &Design, fails: impl Fn(&Design) -> bool) -> Design;
```

**Modes.**

1. `Raw`: arbitrary well-typed JSON terms of the schema, including unusual key orders, quantities written in
   several units, angles in degrees, turns and radians, wide integers and hashes. For canonicalization and view
   round-trip tests. May fail validation.
2. `WellFormed`: terms that elaborate and validate (ports match, units agree, fills and contact laws present,
   limits non-empty), with no promise about sizing. For functor laws, incremental compilation and invalidation.
3. `ValidByConstruction`: designs that must pass preflight stages 1 to 6. Validity comes from construction rules
   that do not call the checker: a tree only (no closures); shells thinner than a quarter of the smallest
   dimension; joint ranges inside the collision-free range of the module's own contract; actuators chosen so
   that the generator's own closed-form demand (holding torque m·g·ℓ per supporting limb, peak current, steady
   winding temperature) is at most one third of the card's limit; a bus whose ceiling is three times the summed
   demand; budgets set 50% above the generated totals; symmetry assertions omitted. A refusal of such a design is
   a finding against either the checker or these rules and is triaged by hand; it is never fixed by weakening
   the gate.

**Sampling.** Every draw is a pure function of `(version, seed, index, derivation path)` through the
counter-based streams; lengths and angles are drawn on rational grids (millimetres; exact fractions of a turn).
No wall clock, thread count or hash-map order enters. The same `(version, seed, index)` gives the same
`DesignId` on Linux and macOS.

**What is drawn.** Root body (box, cylinder or capsule; shell or declared); 1 to `max_limbs` limbs; for each: a
module from the catalog (three-revolute leg, wheel, wheel-leg, track unit, n-revolute arm, passive caster), its
parameter bindings inside the module's bounds, its arrangement (`mirror`, nested mirrors, `radial(n)`, plain
`attach`); joint classes over `fixed`, `R`, `P`, `H`, `C`, `E`, `S`; fixed joints at `fixed_joint_rate`;
transmissions; sensors with frames, rational rates and clocks; payloads on or off the symmetry planes; a power
bus; budgets; levers and locks.

**Edits.** `sample_edit` draws from the full vocabulary with preconditions that hold on the given design:
`set-param`, `regear`, `swap-actuator`, `add-limb`, `remove-limb`, `move-mount`, `reshape`, `mirror`, `radial`,
`closure-add`, `closure-remove`, `set-fill`, `set-material`, `set-limits`, `set-contact-law`, `swap-module`,
`add-sensor`, `move-sensor`, `remove-sensor`, `retime`, `add-payload`, `move-payload`, `set-bus`,
`swap-battery`, `set-budget`, `declare-lever`, `set-lever`, `lock`, `unlock`, `assert-symmetry`, `rename`,
`migrate`. Edit sequences are sampled with the inverse of an earlier edit at a fixed rate, so round trips occur.

**Coverage report.** For a batch: counts per constructor, component, joint class, fill, contact law, module and
edit kind; the validity rate per mode; the histogram of `n_v`. A batch in which any constructor, joint class,
fill or edit kind occurs in fewer than 1% of samples fails the generator's own test.

**Shrinker.** Given a failing predicate, repeatedly remove a limb, replace a constructor by its first child,
round parameters to defaults and drop sensors and payloads while the predicate still fails; the result is what a
failing property test reports.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `sample_design` and `sample_edit` are pure functions of `(version, seed, index)`: 10⁴ samples give
      identical `DesignId` lists on Linux and macOS, in debug and release.
- [ ] Coverage: over 10⁴ `WellFormed` samples every constructor, joint class, fill, contact law and edit kind
      occurs in at least 1% of samples, and the report is written as `frob.gen.coverage/1`.
- [ ] `ValidByConstruction`: 10³ samples elaborate and validate; when preflight stages 1 to 6 exist, the count
      refused is reported against the gate's requirement of zero, with each refusal triaged.
- [ ] `WellFormed` samples elaborate and validate in at least 99.9% of cases; `Raw` samples parse or refuse with
      a typed refusal and never panic.
- [ ] The shrinker returns a design with at most two limbs on the planted failures below.
- [ ] The generator's version is part of every test's recorded seed line, so a failure reproduces from the log.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `gen_is_pure`: the same `(version, seed, index)` twice → byte-identical canonical JSON.
- `gen_cross_platform_pin`: the first 16 `DesignId` values for seed 0 are pinned in the test.
- `coverage_floor`: the 1% floor per category over 10⁴ samples.
- `valid_by_construction_margins`: for each sample the generator's closed-form demand is at most one third of
  each chosen card's limit.
- `edit_preconditions_hold`: 10⁴ sampled edits all apply without a precondition refusal.
- `edit_inverse_roundtrip`: an edit followed by its sampled inverse returns the original `DesignId`.
- `shrink_planted_failure`: a predicate "has a prismatic joint with a shell body" shrinks to one limb.
- `fixed_joint_rate_respected`: at rate 1/4 the share of fixed joints is within 2 percentage points over 10⁴.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_generator.sh`: runs `frob-gen-designs --mode well-formed --seed 0 --count 1000 --out DIR` and
`--mode valid --count 1000`, registers a sample of the files with `frob design new`, runs `frob design show
--json`, and compares the `DesignId` list with the pinned digest. NDJSON per step: command, exit code, wall time,
generator version, seed, count, list digest, coverage counts with the 1% floor, validity rates, and on failure
the first index whose identity differs with the file path. Summary: samples, identities matched, categories
under the floor.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The identity rule adopted from the Phylum generator's invariant: the same `(generator, seed, index)` gives the
  same `DesignId` on Linux and macOS. Negative control: a generator that reads the wall clock or a hash-map
  order.
- Negative control for coverage: a configuration with `closures: false` and `max_limbs: 1` must fail the
  coverage floor for `closure` and `radial`.
- Metamorphic: raising `max_limbs` never removes a design that the smaller configuration could emit at the same
  derivation path.

## Considerations

- The validity margins (a factor of three) are DESIGN choices that keep generated designs far from the checker's
  thresholds, so that a refusal is unambiguous. They also mean the "valid" population never probes the boundary;
  the seeded-defect corpus probes it from the other side.
- A generator bug can hide a compiler bug (both wrong in the same way). The coverage report and the pinned
  identities make the generator itself reviewable, and the eighteen archetypes are always added to every
  property test's population.
- When the Phylum generator lands at M3 its samples are added to these populations; this generator is kept,
  because its purpose is coverage, not a distribution.
- Cost: sampling is microseconds per design; 10⁴ compiles dominate, within the default compute envelope.
