## Background

The archetype catalog (`assets/archetypes/`, listed by `design.templates`) holds eighteen parameterized designs.
Each exists to exercise named mechanisms and has a known answer that fails if the mechanism is wrong. The first
four land at M1; this bead adds four legged ones at M3. They are built from modules (`.fmodule`, referenced by
content hash through the `use` constructor) and from the `mirror` and `radial` constructors, so that their
symmetry is a property of the term and the compiler can check the group each one asserts.

Why these four. `quad-sprawl` separates static stability from actuator feasibility: a wide stance is very stable
and very expensive to hold. `hexapod-radial` is the first design whose group is not a product of mirrors, and its
handed variant is the smallest case with complex-type irreducible blocks. `octopod` stresses contact capacity and
size buckets. `biped-point` has no static stability at all and is the hardest case for the baseline controller.

## Scope

In: the four templates (design term, manifest, levers, presets, symmetry assertion), their known-answer fixtures
and negative controls, and their rows in the catalog's on-file group table.

Out: the catalog mechanism and `design.templates` (BD-109); the gate measurement over all eighteen (BD-246);
baseline controllers (N14b-08 for six or more legs, N14b-09 for the biped); the symmetry computation (A-03) and
the gait atlas.

## Technical approach

Common rules (inherited from the catalog): every fill, material, contact law, joint limit and current convention
is written; proprioception only by default; a power bus and budgets; levers with bounds and manifold kinds; a
`symmetry` block asserting the on-file group.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | `quad-sprawl` | Torso; `mirror(xz, mirror(yz, leg))`; three-revolute legs splayed outward, wide stance | 12; 18 | C₂ × C₂ | None on file |
| 3 | `hexapod-radial` | Hub; `radial(6, z, leg)`; leg = `use(std/leg-3r…)` | 18; 24 | D₆; C₆ with handed legs | None with a permissive model (OPEN-ARENA-2) |

**`quad-sprawl`: stable and still thermally infeasible.** The default parameters pass stages 1 to 6. The preset
`quad-sprawl@hot` is the fixture: a longer horizontal lever at the knee and a smaller actuator card. Its static
margin (A-08) is large and positive, and stage 6 must refuse it with FROB-REF-132 from the actuator envelope
(A-11). The mechanism under test: holding torque at a joint whose horizontal moment arm is ℓ is τ = (m·g/4)·ℓ
per leg for body mass m shared by four feet; winding current I = τ/(N·k_t) for gear ratio N and torque constant
k_t; copper loss P = R(T)·I² with R(T) = R₀·(1 + α·(T − T₀)); with thermal resistance R_th to ambient the steady
state is T* = T_amb + R_th·R₀·I²·(1 + α·(T_amb − T₀))/(1 − α·R_th·R₀·I²), which exists only if
α·R_th·R₀·I² < 1. The preset's values are chosen from its card so that either no equilibrium exists or T* is
above the card's limit; the test reads them from the card, not from this bead. Second fixture: stiction at
near-stall joints: a commanded motion whose torque is below the joint's breakaway torque produces no motion and
a `stick` state in the joint row, never a creeping joint.

**`hexapod-radial`: orbit counts and block types.** Ten leg types on six slots under the dihedral group of order
12 give (10⁶ + 2·10 + 2·10² + 10³ + 3·10⁴ + 3·10³)/12 = 86,185 distinct robots (the six-fold rotations fix 10
colourings each, the three-fold ones 10², the half turn 10³, the three vertex reflections 10⁴ each, the three
edge reflections 10³ each). With only the sagittal mirror left, because a sensor broke the rest,
(10⁶ + 10³)/2 = 500,500. If the leg module is chiral, no reflection maps the robot to itself and Γ_mech is C₆,
whose two-dimensional real blocks are of complex type: an equivariant map takes two real parameters per pair of
copies of such a block, not one. The tripod gait is the pair (H, K) of subgroups in which alternate legs move
together; static walking keeps three feet down.

**`octopod`: capacity and redundancy.** Eight feet. With one contact sample per foot the referee's solve holds 8
contacts, inside its capacity of 21 (FACT: `fs-contact` sets `MAX_CONTACTS = 21`); with three samples per foot it
needs 24 and must return the typed over-capacity refusal, never a truncated solve. `n_v` = 30 places it in the
36 bucket of the default set {12, 18, 24, 36, 48, 64}. Redundancy: with any one leg disabled (its joints locked,
its foot lifted) the support polygon of the remaining seven feet still contains the centre of mass, and A-08
reports a positive margin for all eight single-leg failures in the radial layout.

**`biped-point`: no static stability.** Two point feet have a support segment, not a polygon. A-08 must report
"no static stability" as a finding with its margin, and must not refuse: stage 6 refuses with FROB-REF-124 only
when a task requires a static margin, and none does here. The archetype also exercises impact events and
underactuation. Its preset `biped-point@cassie` approximates Cassie's mass for the GATE-SKEL-07 row; the template
is a tree and does not reproduce Cassie's closed-loop leg, which belongs to import parity of the reference model.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the four compiles and passes stages 1 to 6 of `design check` with no refusal at default parameters.
- [ ] The compiler's Γ_mech equals the group on file for each (C₂ × C₂; D₆ and C₆ for the handed variant; D₈ and
      C₂ for the two octopod layouts; C₂), and each template's symmetry assertion is accepted.
- [ ] `quad-sprawl@hot` has a positive A-08 margin and is refused at stage 6 with FROB-REF-132; the default is
      not.
- [ ] The hexapod orbit counts 86,185 and 500,500 are returned exactly by `algebra.orbits` on the archetype's
      slot structure.
- [ ] The octopod with one sample per foot stands on the referee's contact solve; with three samples per foot the
      referee returns its over-capacity refusal by name.
- [ ] `biped-point` passes stage 6 with a reported finding "no static stability" and no refusal.
- [ ] `biped-point@cassie` is within 15% of Cassie's mass on file (row of GATE-SKEL-07).
- [ ] Each negative control below is caught.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `quad_sprawl_default_passes`, `quad_sprawl_hot_refused_132_with_positive_static_margin`.
- `thermal_equilibrium_formula`: synthetic card R₀ = 0.2 Ω, α = 0.0039 K⁻¹, R_th = 2 K/W, T₀ = T_amb = 25 °C,
  I = 10 A → α·R_th·R₀·I² = 0.156, T* = 25 + 40/(1 − 0.156) = 72.4 °C; I = 26 A → 1.055 ≥ 1, no equilibrium.
- `near_stall_stiction_sticks`: torque at 0.9 of breakaway → zero velocity for 1 s, state `stick`.
- `hexapod_group_d6`, `hexapod_handed_group_c6`, `hexapod_orbits_86185`, `hexapod_mirror_only_500500`.
- `hexapod_tripod_is_h_k_pair`: the alternate-leg subgroup pair is found in the subgroup lattice.
- `octopod_radial_d8`, `octopod_spider_c2`, `octopod_n_v_30_bucket_36`.
- `octopod_capacity`: 8 contacts accepted; 24 refused by name.
- `octopod_one_leg_out`: eight cases, each with a positive static margin.
- `biped_a08_reports_not_refuses`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Covered by `scripts/e2e/skel_archetypes.sh` (N14b-06), which runs `frob design new --template`, `frob design
check --json` and `frob analyze symmetry --json` for every available archetype and asserts the group on file, the
stage statuses and, for the named presets, the expected refusal. This bead adds its fixtures and expected values
to that script's table; log lines carry the archetype, preset, `ModelId`, the computed and on-file groups, stage
statuses, refusal codes and measured margins and temperatures with their limits.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-270: Γ_mech as built for these four archetypes. Controls: the planted asymmetries of the symmetry gate (a
  moved sensor, a one-sided payload, a changed sole material, a reversed gear), each of which must change the
  group and name its breaker.
- The orbit rows of the Phylum chapter: 86,185 under the dihedral group of order 12; control: counting under the
  rotation subgroup only (which gives (10⁶ + 2·10 + 2·10² + 10³)/6 = 166,870). 500,500 under the sagittal mirror
  only; control: quotienting under the mechanical group when a front-only sensor is mounted.
- Capacity: 8 contacts fit, 24 refuse; control: a build that drops contacts to fit the capacity.
- `quad-sprawl@hot`; control: a support-polygon-plus-total-capacity screen, which accepts it.
- Determinism: each `DesignId` is pinned and equal on Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- The archetype table says `quad-sprawl` "is stable and still fails A-11 thermally", while the gate says every
  archetype passes stages 1 to 6. Resolution adopted here: the default passes, and a named preset is the failing
  fixture. If the Skeleton lead wants the default itself to fail, GATE-SKEL-03 needs an exemption row.
- Hexapod reference model: none with a permissive licence is on file (OPEN-ARENA-2); its baseline scorecard is
  the archetype's own template.
- KILL-MARROW-BLOCKS (decided at M4) concerns exact arithmetic for equivariant blocks at n ≤ 12; the hexapod and
  octopod are inside that range.
- The octopod's capacity fixture depends on an upstream limit (work item FD-FSIM-13: a sustained solve above 21
  contacts). When that lands the three-sample case becomes a pass, and the fixture's expectation is updated by a
  dated amendment, not silently.
