## Background

The archetype catalog holds eighteen parameterized designs, each built to exercise named mechanisms and each with
a known answer that fails if the mechanism is wrong. This bead adds the last five. They are the ones that depend
on contact laws beyond a foot patch and a rolling wheel (`belt`, `skid`, `pad`, two laws on one end effector),
on a kinematic chain long enough to test `serial(k, link)` at scale, or on reference models that enter by import.

The tracked and hybrid ones compile and pass preflight at M3; they stand and roll under their baseline
controllers at M4, when their contact laws close on every tier. That split is part of the gates and is not a
slip: a track is a set of road wheels plus a belt modelled as a contact surface with prescribed tangential
velocity and an anisotropic friction ellipse, with distributed patches and yaw scrub, and that law is Frontier.

## Scope

In: the five templates with manifests, levers, presets and symmetry assertions; their known-answer fixtures and
negative controls; the `mobile-manipulator` presets against its three reference models. For `snake-crawler`
this bead holds the known answers, the levers' use, the preflight rows (stage 4 by curvature) and the controls;
the template term and manifest themselves are N14bp1-01, a dependency, because the curvature analysis (BD-243)
takes that term as the fixture of its own sign test while this bead's stage-4 row needs the analysis. The order
is therefore: the term (N14bp1-01), then the analysis (BD-243), then this bead.

Out: the catalog mechanism (BD-109); gate measurement over all eighteen (BD-246); controllers (N14b-07 tracked,
N14b-08 chain, N14b-09 hybrid and arm on a base); the contact laws, curvature, workspace and singularity
analyses (dependencies); the written form of two contact laws on one end effector, its typing and its
bindings (N14bp2-02, a dependency: the `wheel-leg` template is data that uses it, and needs no special case in
the compiler); the reference models' assets and their masses on file (ROBOT-iiwa7, ROBOT-stretch3,
ROBOT-tiago).

## Technical approach

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | `tracked-flipper` | Chassis; `mirror(xz, track)`; `mirror(xz, mirror(yz, flipper))`; law `belt` | 6; 12 | C₂ × C₂ | None |
| 13 | `wheel-leg` | The `quad-mammal` term with a driven wheel in place of each foot; laws `wheel-rolling` and `pad` on the same end effector | 16; 22 | C₂ × C₂ | None |
| 14 | `tri-wheel-stair` | Chassis; `mirror(xz, cluster)`; cluster = carrier joint with `radial(3, axle, wheel)` | 4; 6 base + 2 carriers + 6 wheels | C₂; C₃ or D₃ in each cluster | None |
| 16 | `mobile-manipulator` | Two driven wheels and a caster; a seven-revolute arm mounted off the centre line | 9; 17 | Trivial with the arm off-centre | KUKA iiwa 7 (in `fs-mbd::robot_models`); Stretch 3, TIAGo (Apache-2.0) |
| 17 | `snake-crawler` | Head body; `serial(8, link)`; the head and each link carry a passive wheel pair across the axis | 8; 3 planar base + 8 joints | C₂ under head-to-tail reversal | None |

**`tracked-flipper`.** (a) Skid steering: under exact lateral no-slip at contact stations a ahead of and behind
the centre, v_y + a·ω = 0 and v_y − a·ω = 0, so ω = 0. A tracked base turns only by slipping, so a turn in place
must cost scrub work on a frictional floor; a model in which it costs none is wrong. (b) Stair nosing with
flippers: the flipper angle that brings the track onto a nosing is a lever of the template. (c) Thermal duty on a
long climb: the envelope analysis must find the climb length at which the drive's winding reaches its limit.

**`wheel-leg`.** Two contact laws on one end-effector class: the wheel rolls (`wheel-rolling`) or the leg steps
with the wheel braked (`pad`). The end-effector schema of M1 holds one law per end effector and admits `pad` on a
`foot` only, so the pair is written with the two optional members that N14bp2-02 (a dependency) adds to the
`contact` object of a rolling wheel, and this template only uses them:
`{"law": "wheel-rolling", "interface": card, "braked": {"law": "pad", "interface": card, "patch_radius": length,
"torsion_radius": length}, "switch": {"signal": "brake", "joint": <the wheel's own axle joint>}}`. `braked` is the
law in force while that joint's brake is engaged, the primary law otherwise. Stage 1 accepts the pair with its
rule and refuses, with FROB-REF-121, a pair without a rule, a patch radius above the wheel's contact radius
(half the tread width of a cylinder wheel) and a torsion radius above the patch radius. Each end effector then
has two contact bindings and one switch in the model, and the template's manifest lists both laws in
`contact_laws`. Wheels and legs enter one mobility analysis: with the wheels free the base gains the rolling
distribution; with them braked the robot is a quadruped.

**`tri-wheel-stair`.** A three-fold group inside a mirror group: each cluster has C₃ (or D₃ if the wheels are
unhanded) acting on its three wheels, and the two clusters are exchanged by the sagittal mirror. The compiler
must report the nested structure, not the product of the outer mirror alone. Cluster geometry against riser and
tread: the spacing between adjacent wheel centres is √3·ρ for wheels on a circle of radius ρ, and the template
exposes ρ as a lever to be compared with the step diagonal √(h² + t²) for riser h and tread t (DESIGN relation;
for h = 0.175 m and t = 0.260 m the diagonal is 0.313 m and ρ = 0.181 m). FACT: `fs-mbd::tri_wheel_cluster`
has the cluster geometry; it serves as the oracle for the stepping kinematics only after its admission test.

**`mobile-manipulator`.** Workspace and singular loci (A-05, A-06) of the seven-joint arm; tray and shelf
heights as task heights for preflight stage 7; a payload that breaks symmetry: with the arm on the centre line
and no payload the base's mirror survives, and a payload held off-centre must be named as the breaker; import
parity of the arm against the iiwa 7 builder. Presets `@iiwa7-stretch3` and `@iiwa7-tiago` give the mass rows of
GATE-SKEL-07. The reference of a preset is a sum of two masses on file: the arm's, `mass_on_file.value_kg` of
`assets/robots/iiwa7/manifest.toml` (ROBOT-iiwa7, a dependency: the arm is a sibling-catalogue builder and no
oracle fixture holds it), and the base's share, the base-only record that the Stretch 3 and TIAGo assets write
for `base.fbot` (the import with everything above the base replaced by one body of equal mass). The whole-robot
mass of Stretch 3 or TIAGo is not the reference: it would count a second arm.

**`snake-crawler`.** Locomotion as holonomy (construction C-08). With shape variables s (joint angles) and body
velocity ξ, the passive wheels impose Pfaffian constraints W_g·ξ + W_s·ṡ = 0; when rank W_g equals the
dimension of the group (three, for SE(2)), ξ = −𝔸(s)·ṡ with 𝔸 = W_g⁻¹·W_s restricted accordingly. The
curvature is (D𝔸)ᵢⱼ = ∂ᵢ𝔸ⱼ − ∂ⱼ𝔸ᵢ − [𝔸ᵢ, 𝔸ⱼ], and a small loop in shape space moves the body by about
exp(−∬ D𝔸). The archetype is the fixture for the sign of the bracket term: flipping it must fail the holonomy
test by an error of order one. Preflight stage 4 runs curvature, not the growth vector, on this base, because it
is a kinematic shape-changer. `serial(8, link)` must compile one link product and reuse it seven times.

DESIGN (one statement, the same in the zoo bead ZOO-snake-crawler, which pins the default instance): the
chain is a head body (the term's root) followed by `serial(8, link)`, where one `link` is an actuated revolute
joint about the vertical axis, a body and a passive wheel pair. That gives nine bodies, eight actuated joints
(the catalog's count), nine wheel pairs (the head carries one too) and nine Pfaffian rows. The `link` subterm
is compiled once and served seven memo hits for its eight repetitions (n uses of a repeated subterm are one
miss and n − 1 hits, as four mirrored legs are one expansion and three hits); the head is a different term
and is compiled on its own. Reason: `serial(k, link)` repeats `link` k times, eight joints need nine bodies,
and counting the head as a ninth use of the link product would contradict the constructor's definition.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the five compiles and passes stages 1 to 6 of `design check` with no refusal at default parameters.
- [ ] Γ_mech computed equals the group on file: C₂ × C₂; C₂ × C₂; C₂ with C₃ or D₃ per cluster; trivial with the
      arm off-centre; C₂ under head-to-tail reversal. For `snake-crawler` the group on file is the catalog's
      cell unless the open question of N14bp1-01 has amended it (its hand derivation finds the sagittal mirror
      as well); until that question is decided this row is `blocked` with the question named, never passed.
- [ ] `tracked-flipper`: the no-slip fixture returns yaw rate zero exactly, and a turn in place under the belt
      law has strictly positive scrub work.
- [ ] `tracked-flipper`: the flipper angle is a declared lever whose bounds contain the nosing angle φ with
      sin φ = (h − r_t)/ℓ_f (24.62° for rise h = 0.175 m, flipper length ℓ_f = 0.30 m and track-wheel radius
      r_t = 0.05 m), and the envelope analysis on the template returns a finite climb length at which the
      drive's winding reaches its limit (the pinned values are ZOO-tracked-flipper's rows).
- [ ] `wheel-leg`: both laws bind to the same end effector with a switching rule (two contact bindings and one
      switch per wheel in `design show --section channels`); a pair without its rule and a torsion radius
      above the wheel's contact radius each refuse with FROB-REF-121; stage 4 returns one report per mode
      (brakes released: wheeled, with the growth vector of the rolling distribution on pose and leg shape;
      brakes engaged: `not-applicable`, a quadruped) and passes.
- [ ] `tri-wheel-stair`: the nested group is reported; the cluster-spacing relation holds in the fixture.
- [ ] `mobile-manipulator`: each preset's mass is within 15% of the sum of the arm's mass on file and its
      base's base-only mass on file; an off-centre payload is named as the breaker of Γ_obs (Γ_mech is
      unchanged by a payload).
- [ ] `snake-crawler`: the holonomy of a small shape loop matches direct integration of the constraints to the
      stated order, and the sign-flipped mutant fails; one link product is compiled for eight links.
- [ ] Each negative control below is caught.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `skid_no_slip_forces_zero_yaw`: a = 0.25 m; the two equations give ω = 0 and v_y = 0.
- `turn_in_place_scrub_work_positive`: a quarter-turn pivot of the template on an interface with μ_lat = 0.8
  dissipates M·π/2, where M is BD-245's belt sum for the template's station count m per belt (μ_lat·W·L/4 for
  even m, μ_lat·W·L·(1 − 1/m²)/4 for odd m; W the template's weight and L its belt ground length, both read
  from the template), within BD-245's 2% (TARGET); a model that turns at zero scrub work fails it (KA-282).
- `flipper_angle_is_a_lever`: the lever exists with manifold S¹ or a bounded interval, and asin((0.175 −
  0.05)/0.30) = 24.62° lies inside its bounds; `tracked_climb_length_finite`: the envelope analysis returns a
  finite climb length to the winding limit on the slope and speed of the zoo's long-climb row.
- `wheel_leg_two_laws_bind`: each of the four wheels has bindings `wheel-rolling` (primary) and `pad` (braked)
  and one switch on its own axle; `wheel_leg_incompatible_laws_refused_121`: a torsion radius above the
  contact radius; `wheel_leg_missing_rule_refused_121`.
- `mobile_manipulator_mass_reference_is_arm_plus_base`: the window of `@iiwa7-stretch3` is ±15% around the
  arm's mass on file plus the base-only mass on file of Stretch 3; using the whole-robot mass moves the
  window and the test fails (planted).
- `tri_wheel_group_nested`: the outer generator swaps clusters, the inner generator cycles wheels.
- `tri_wheel_spacing`: ρ = 0.181 m → spacing 0.3135 m against the diagonal 0.3134 m.
- `mobile_manipulator_payload_breaker`: a 2 kg payload at y = +0.1 m is named.
- `snake_curvature_sign`: a square loop of side ε = 0.1 rad in two adjacent joints: predicted displacement
  exp(−∬ D𝔸) against direct integration of the constraints. The displacement is second order in ε and the
  leading correction on SE(2) is third order (the next term of the path-ordered exponential), so the relative
  error is first order: it must fall by a factor of at least 1.8 at each halving of ε (0.1 → 0.05 → 0.025 rad),
  and the value at ε = 0.025 rad is recorded as the tolerance filed with KA-254 (whose appendix tolerance is
  "to set"). The mutant with the bracket sign reversed has a relative error that does not fall with ε and stays
  of order one (above 0.5).
- `serial_compiles_once` is N14bp1-01's test (one link product and seven hits for eight uses, the head
  separate); this bead adds `snake_stage3_passes_despite_nine_wheel_rows`: the nine wheel rows are contact
  constraints, not closures, so stage 3's mobility test does not refuse the chain (FROB-REF-110 must not fire).
- `groups_on_file`: five assertions accepted.

### End-to-end script and logging

Covered by `scripts/e2e/skel_archetypes.sh` (N14b-06; the script is data-driven and runs every entry of its
table that is available). This bead adds the five entries to `scripts/e2e/data/archetypes.toml` with
`available_from = "M3"`: the default instances take `DesignId`, `n_v` and the groups from the zoo files; the two
`mobile-manipulator` presets carry their own pinned `DesignId` and their mass windows; `standing_row` is due at
M4 for `tracked-flipper` and `wheel-leg`. Per entry the script runs `frob design new --template`, `frob design
check --json`, `frob analyze symmetry`, and, when a task and scene are supplied, stage 7 for the manipulator's
tray and shelf heights. Its log lines (the STD-E2E fields of the script: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block) carry the archetype, preset,
`ModelId`, computed and on-file groups, stage statuses with their times, masses in kg against their windows with
the two masses on file each window was built from, and the first differing value on failure. The tracked and
hybrid standing rows are logged `not run: contact law` until M4, and the summary counts them as not run.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-418: under exact lateral no-slip at stations ahead of and behind the centre the yaw rate is zero, so a
  tracked base turns only by slipping. KA-282 (the first rejection fixture of the track family): turning in
  place at zero scrub work on a frictional floor is caught by the belt-law known answer, the scrub moment of
  BD-245 that `turn_in_place_scrub_work_positive` reads.
- KA-254: the curvature law and the direction of the small-loop displacement; control: the bracket sign
  reversed.
- KA-270: Γ_mech for these five; controls: the planted asymmetries of the symmetry gate, including the off-centre
  payload.
- KA-283 (first rejection fixture of the foot family) applied to `wheel-leg`: a torsion radius above the end
  effector's own contact radius is refused at stage 1 (FROB-REF-121).
- Determinism: pinned `DesignId` per template, equal on Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- Until the tracked and hybrid archetypes reach their standing rows at M4 (GATE-REFLEX-01 lists them there;
  the drive-tied Frontier belt is N18b-06), they are fixtures for preflight and analysis only and no standing or
  rolling claim is made on them. What they use at M3 is BD-245's: the belt's road-wheel form (the Frontier belt's
  named fallback), `pad`, `skid` and the wheel laws on every tier, which is enough for the scrub and no-slip known
  answers above; `pad` itself does not wait for M4.
- A capacity-biased comparison is refused: a tracked design against a point-foot design under an artificially
  low contact cap returns `capacity-biased` and no winner. The tracked template's contact count is recorded in
  its manifest so that the comparison rule can see it.
- The connection of the snake belongs to one contact stratum: if a wheel slips or lifts, the curvature fixture's
  prediction is withdrawn for that motion.
- The tri-wheel relation is a design heuristic labelled DESIGN; the stepping oracle is the sibling geometry
  module after admission, and the row is recorded `blocked: admission` until then.
