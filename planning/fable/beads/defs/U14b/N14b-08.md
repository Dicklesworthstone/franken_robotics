## Background

`frob control synth` assembles a baseline controller from the model by morphology class. For robots with six or
more legs the class is **multi-legged**: a phase-oscillator network on the gait atlas's phase lags, a
support-aware wave or tripod schedule, and the same stance program as the four-legged class. It is tier Solid.
The evidence the class requires before its next rung: static support margin at rest, and fall and energy rates
under disturbance. The next rung, not built here: a symmetry-reduced gait family, and adaptation with one leg
disabled.

Gaits are subgroups (construction C-10): a gait pattern is a pair (H, K) of subgroups of the leg-permutation
group extended by time shifts, and its phase lags follow from the pair. The enumeration is finite group theory
and lives in `frob-algebra`; `frob-control` reads phase tables from there. Reason for the design: a hand-kept
table of gaits per leg count does not survive a new body plan, while the group gives every pattern the body
admits.

The catalog also contains a body with no legs and no driven wheels: `snake-crawler`, a planar serial chain with
passive wheels. The control chapter's class table has no row for it. This bead adds one (DESIGN, tier Frontier
because it rests on the connection-curvature analysis): a travelling-wave shape gait. It shares the oscillator
network with the multi-legged class, which is why both are here.

## Scope

In: the oscillator network; the support-aware schedule; the multi-legged controller for `hexapod-radial` and
`octopod`; the travelling-wave controller for `snake-crawler`; their evidence fixtures and standing rows.

Out: class detection and the gate over all eighteen (BD-247); the stance program, swing law and leg IK (the
legged class, reused); the gait atlas as an analysis with stability margins (M4); orbit computation.

## Technical approach

**Oscillator network (DESIGN).** One phase φᵢ per leg (or per chain joint):

    φ̇ᵢ = ω + Σⱼ Kᵢⱼ·sin(φⱼ − φᵢ − Δᵢⱼ)

with ω the stride frequency, Kᵢⱼ ≥ 0 a coupling over the leg-adjacency graph and Δᵢⱼ the phase lags read from
the atlas for the chosen (H, K) pair. The fixed point φⱼ − φᵢ = Δᵢⱼ is the gait. Phases are integer accumulators
of a rational period in the clock contract; no raw angle is integrated in floating point across episodes.

**Schedule.** Leg i is in stance while (φᵢ mod 2π) < 2π·β for duty factor β. Tripod: two groups of alternate
legs with lag π and β ≥ ½. Wave: successive lags of 2π/n with β = (n − 1)/n (5/6 for six legs, 7/8 for eight).
The stride frequency follows the target Froude number v²/(g·h) for hip height h.

**Support awareness.** A leg may lift only if, with it lifted, the centre-of-mass projection stays inside the
support polygon of the remaining stance feet by the margin the static-stability analysis reports; otherwise its
oscillator is held at the stance boundary and an event is recorded. This makes static walking (three feet down
on six legs) a property of the controller and not of luck.

**Stance and swing.** Stance forces come from the single-step force-distribution program over the friction cones
of the stance feet, solved by the shared cone solver with a fixed iteration count; each world ends `optimal`,
`infeasible` (with a dual certificate) or `unconverged` (the caller takes its fallback, the last feasible
command). Swing feet follow a Cartesian PD law through the derived leg inverse-kinematics kernel to a foothold
chosen by the capture-point heuristic. Joint PD at the driver. Gains from the linearization and the reflected
inertia.

**The chain.** Joint targets θᵢ(t) = A·sin(φᵢ) + c, with the φᵢ of the same network at successive lags δ; A, δ
and the turning offset c are chosen from the connection's curvature integrated over the candidate shape loop
(the displacement per cycle is, to leading order, minus the integral of the curvature over the enclosed
surface), subject to joint limits and to the passive wheels' no-side-slip regime. If the curvature analysis is
unavailable the controller uses a registered default (A, δ) and labels its result `default-gait`.

**Identity and records.** `ControllerId` covers the class, the (H, K) pair, β, ω, the coupling, the stance
program's iteration count, the latency model and the group actually used. Every command carries its layer; every
held lift and every fallback is a telemetry event with its reason (INV-REFLEX-01).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `hexapod-radial` and `octopod` stand for 10 s and traverse 5 m on `batch-cpu` with `--state estimated`
      (rows of GATE-REFLEX-01 at M3); on `referee` where the contact count fits its capacity of 21.
- [ ] `snake-crawler` traverses 5 m by shape changes alone, with no wheel side slip above the recorded bound.
- [ ] The network converges to the tripod and wave lags from random initial phases within a stated number of
      strides.
- [ ] Under the support-aware rule the static margin never goes negative on flat ground at the walking speed;
      with the rule disabled (negative control) it does.
- [ ] A lateral push within the recorded disturbance bound is recovered; the fall rate and energy rate under
      disturbance are recorded (the evidence the class requires).
- [ ] The chain's displacement per cycle agrees with the curvature prediction to the order the analysis states,
      and its sign reverses when the wave direction reverses.
- [ ] A planted infeasible stance program yields a certificate that checks exactly and a fallback event.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `oscillator_fixed_point_tripod`: six oscillators, lags π between alternate groups → converge within 10⁻⁶ rad.
- `oscillator_wave_lags`: n = 6 → successive lags 60°; n = 8 → 45°; duty factors 5/6 and 7/8.
- `phase_is_integer_accumulator`: 10⁹ ticks produce no drift against the rational period.
- `support_rule_holds_leg`: a lift that would leave the margin negative is held and an event is emitted.
- `stance_program_three_states`: an over-constrained load → `infeasible` with y such that Aᵀy ∈ K* and bᵀy < 0,
  checked in rationals.
- `chain_gait_sign`: reversing the wave reverses the net displacement.
- `chain_default_gait_labelled`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Rows in `scripts/e2e/reflex_baselines_all.sh` (owned by BD-247) plus `scripts/e2e/reflex_multileg_evidence.sh`:
`frob control synth`, `frob sim run --tier batch-cpu --task stand-10s`, `--task traverse-5m`,
`--task push-recovery`, `frob telemetry summary`. NDJSON per step: command, exit code, wall time, `evidence`,
`ModelId`, `ControllerId`, the gait pair and duty factor, minimum static margin (m), distance (m), fall count,
energy per metre (J/m), held-lift events, and on failure the first failing tick. Summary names GATE-REFLEX-01.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Tripod gait as an (H, K) pair for the hexapod; static walking with three feet down.
- The friction certificate of the stance program: weight 50 N pushed with 30 N at μ = 0.5 is infeasible with
  y = (0.5, −1), yᵀb = −5 N.
- KA-254: the curvature law used for the chain; control: the bracket sign reversed (the predicted direction is
  wrong by an error of order one).
- Negative controls: (a) the support rule disabled; (b) lags taken from the wrong subgroup pair (a pace on a
  hexapod), which fails static support; (c) an oscillator integrated in `f32` radians across 10⁶ steps (drift).
- Metamorphic: rotating the radial hexapod's heading command by 60° permutes the leg traces by one slot.

## Considerations

- A symmetric controller may be tied only under the intersection of the design's group with the stabilizers of
  payload, gravity, task, observations and the current contact mode. This bead ties nothing; the lags come from
  the atlas and each leg has its own phase.
- With eight sampled feet the referee's contact capacity is exceeded; those runs use `batch-cpu` and the referee
  row is recorded `over capacity`, never truncated.
- The chain's controller is Frontier and inherits the kill condition of the curvature analysis: if that analysis
  is cut, the chain keeps the registered default gait and its result is labelled as such.
- Scores are `screening` until rank agreement is measured for the class and task family.
