## Background

`frob control synth` assembles a baseline controller from the model by morphology class. For robots with six or
more legs the class is **multi-legged**: a phase-oscillator network on the gait atlas's phase lags, a
support-aware wave or tripod schedule, and the same stance program as the four-legged class. It is tier Solid.
The evidence the class requires before its next rung: static support margin at rest, and fall and energy rates
under disturbance. The next rung, not built here: a symmetry-reduced gait family, and adaptation with one leg
disabled.

Gaits are subgroups (construction C-10): a gait pattern is a pair (H, K) of subgroups of the leg-permutation
group extended by time shifts, and its phase lags follow from the pair. The enumeration is finite group theory
and lives in `frob-algebra`; `frob-control` reads phase tables from there. Reason for the design: a hand-kept
table of gaits per leg count does not survive a new body plan, while the group gives every pattern the body
admits. The gait atlas as a verb (`gait.atlas`, BD-332) lands at M4, after this bead; until then the legged
class uses the interim posture of the four-legged gait clock (N22a-10): phase-table files in
`registries/gaits/`, each row verified at load against the subgroup-pair enumeration of `frob-algebra`
(K normal in H, H/K cyclic, the generator's shift equal to 1/|H/K|). This bead writes the tables for six and
eight legs in that format; when the atlas lands it generates the same rows and the files become its golden.

The catalog also contains a body with no legs and no driven wheels: `snake-crawler`, a planar serial chain with
passive wheels. The control chapter's class table has no row for it. This bead adds one (DESIGN, tier Frontier
because it rests on the connection-curvature analysis): a travelling-wave shape gait. It shares the oscillator
network with the multi-legged class, which is why both are here.

## Scope

## Technical approach

**Oscillator network (DESIGN).** One phase φᵢ per leg (or per chain joint):

    φ̇ᵢ = ω + Σⱼ Kᵢⱼ·sin(φⱼ − φᵢ − Δᵢⱼ)

with ω the stride frequency, Kᵢⱼ ≥ 0 the coupling and Δᵢⱼ = ψⱼ − ψᵢ the phase lags of the chosen (H, K) row,
ψ being the row's target phases. The fixed point φⱼ − φᵢ = Δᵢⱼ is the gait. Because the lags are differences of
target phases, the change of variables θᵢ = φᵢ − ψᵢ turns the network into identical Kuramoto oscillators, and
on the complete graph (Kᵢⱼ = K for all i ≠ j) their synchronized state, which is the gait, attracts almost every
initial condition. Coupling only over the leg-adjacency ring does not: a ring of n oscillators also has stable
"twisted" states with a whole number q of extra phase turns around the ring, stable for |q| < n/4, so for six
or eight legs the one-twist state is a second attractor that a random start can fall into. The coupling is
therefore all-to-all (DESIGN), with K set so that the linearized error about the gait decays by a factor e³ per
stride (K·n·T = 3 for stride period T); the ring coupling is kept as a negative control.

Arithmetic (DESIGN, the clock contract of N22a-10): each phase is a fixed-point fraction of a turn (u32), the
stride is a whole number N of control ticks, and the free-running increment is exactly 2³²/N per tick (N chosen
so that it divides evenly, as in N22a-10). The coupling term is computed with the deterministic `sin` of the
math library (D2) and rounded to a whole increment; at the gait it is exactly zero, so a locked network is the
integer clock and never drifts. No raw angle is integrated in floating point across episodes.

| | `wave` | Lk at (k − 1)/8, Rk at (k − 1)/8 + 1/2 | 7/8 | identity | the mirror, shift 1/2 (legs swing back to front on each side) |

Gait choice follows N22a-10's rule extended by leg count: `stand` at zero speed; `wave` when the Froude number
is below 0.2 (DESIGN) or when the support check rejects a faster gait; `tetrapod` (six legs) below 0.5; `tripod`
or `alternating` otherwise. A planted row (tripod with leg 3 at 0) is refused at load with the leg named.

**Schedule.** Leg i is in stance while (φᵢ mod 2π) < 2π·β for duty factor β. Tripod: two groups of alternate
legs with lag π and β ≥ ½. Wave: successive lags of 2π/n with β = (n − 1)/n (5/6 for six legs, 7/8 for eight).
The stride frequency follows the target Froude number v²/(g·h) for hip height h, by the similarity rule of
N22a-10 (stride period T = τ*·√(h/g), rounded to whole control ticks).

**Support awareness.** A leg may lift only if, with it lifted, the centre-of-mass projection stays inside the
support polygon of the remaining stance feet by the margin the static-stability analysis reports; otherwise its
oscillator is held at the stance boundary and an event is recorded. This makes static walking (three feet down
on six legs) a property of the controller and not of luck.

**Stance and swing.** Stance forces come from the single-step force-distribution program over the friction cones
of the stance feet, solved by the shared cone solver with a fixed iteration count; each world ends `optimal`,
`infeasible` (with a dual certificate) or `unconverged` (the caller takes its fallback, the last feasible
command). Swing feet follow a Cartesian PD law through the derived leg inverse-kinematics kernel to a foothold
chosen by the capture-point heuristic. Joint PD at the driver. Gains from the linearization and the reflected
inertia.

**The chain.** Joint targets θᵢ(t) = A·sin(φᵢ) + c, with the φᵢ of the same network at successive lags δ; A, δ
and the turning offset c are chosen from the connection's curvature integrated over the candidate shape loop
(the displacement per cycle is, to leading order, minus the integral of the curvature over the enclosed
surface), subject to joint limits and to the passive wheels' no-side-slip regime. If the curvature analysis is
unavailable the controller uses a registered default (A, δ) and labels its result `default-gait`.

**Lockstep form (the `batch-cpu` rows need it).** The multi-legged controller runs on the batched tiers as
the legged lockstep form of N22a-13 with a leg capacity of eight: the stance program, swing law, fallback under
masks and batched filter are that bead's stages, padded to eight legs with masked rows. This bead adds two
stages before them, both per world and free of control flow: (1) the oscillator update (the u32 phases, the
coupling sum over the world's own legs with −0.0 as the neutral element for padded legs, the rounded
increment); (2) the support rule as a mask (the margin with the leg lifted is computed for every leg; a lift
whose margin would be negative is replaced by a hold through a select, and the held-lift counter is an exact
integer per world). The chain uses stage (1) and one joint-target kernel. In `f64` each stage reproduces its
per-world host form bit for bit; a world's phases, holds and digests do not depend on the other worlds of its
launch, and a launch may mix six-leg, eight-leg, four-leg and wheeled worlds under the class mask of N22ap2-01.

**Identity and records.** `ControllerId` covers the class, the (H, K) pair, β, ω, the coupling, the stance
program's iteration count, the latency model and the group actually used. Every command carries its layer; every
held lift and every fallback is a telemetry event with its reason (INV-REFLEX-01).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `hexapod-radial` and `octopod` stand for 10 s and traverse 5 m on `batch-cpu` with `--state estimated`
      (rows of GATE-REFLEX-01 at M3); on `referee` where the contact count fits its capacity of 21.
- [ ] `snake-crawler` traverses 5 m by shape changes alone, with every wheel's mean lateral slip speed below 5%
      of the body's mean forward speed (DESIGN bound, recorded with the measured values).
- [ ] Every row of the three phase tables passes N22a-10's loader check; a planted row is refused with the leg
      named.
- [ ] From 1,000 seeded random initial phase vectors, the all-to-all network locks to the tripod and to the wave
      lags (every lag within 0.01 rad) within 10 strides in every draw; the ring-coupled control locks to a
      twisted state in at least one draw.
- [ ] Under the support-aware rule the static margin never goes negative on flat ground at the walking speed;
      with the rule disabled (negative control) it does.
- [ ] A lateral push (a body velocity change of 0.3 m/s, applied over 0.1 s at 20 seeded gait phases; DESIGN) is
      recovered in every case on `hexapod-radial` (no fall, heading error back within 0.1 rad within 3 s); the
      fall rate and energy rate under disturbance are recorded for both multi-legged archetypes (the evidence the
      class requires).
- [ ] The chain's displacement per cycle agrees with the curvature prediction at first order: for wave amplitudes
      A = 0.1, 0.05 and 0.025 rad the relative error falls by at least 1.8 per halving of A (the criterion of
      N14b-04's sign test), and its sign reverses when the wave direction reverses.
- [ ] A planted infeasible stance program yields a certificate that checks exactly and a fallback event.
- [ ] Lockstep: on `batch-cpu` in `f64` the oscillator and support stages reproduce the host form bit for bit
      over 1,000 control ticks of 64 worlds; one world address gives identical digests alone, in a batch of 64
      and in a launch mixing `hexapod-radial`, `octopod`, `quad-mammal` and `diff-drive` worlds.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `oscillator_fixed_point_tripod`: six oscillators, lags π between alternate groups → converge within 10⁻⁶ rad.
- `oscillator_wave_lags`: n = 6 → successive lags 60°; n = 8 → 45°; duty factors 5/6 and 7/8.
- `oscillator_all_to_all_basin`: 1,000 seeded draws, six and eight oscillators, all lock within 10 strides.
- `oscillator_ring_twisted_state_control`: ring coupling, n = 6, started at the one-twist state (phases 2πk/6
  added to the target) plus 0.05 rad noise → stays twisted (the control that justifies all-to-all coupling).
- `phase_tables_checked`: the seven rows of the three files load; for each, applying the generator shifts every
  phase by its declared amount and every element of K leaves the phases unchanged; a tripod with leg 3 at 0 is
  refused naming leg 3.
- `gait_choice_by_froude`: Fr 0.1 → `wave`; 0.3 → `tetrapod` (six legs); 0.8 → `tripod`; a support-check
  rejection of `tripod` falls back to `wave` and is recorded.
- `phase_is_integer_accumulator`: 10⁹ ticks produce no drift against the rational period; at lock the coupling
  increment is exactly zero.
- `support_rule_holds_leg`: a lift that would leave the margin negative is held and an event is emitted.
- `stance_program_three_states`: an over-constrained load → `infeasible` with y such that Aᵀy ∈ K* and bᵀy < 0,
  checked in rationals.
- `chain_gait_sign`: reversing the wave reverses the net displacement.
- `chain_default_gait_labelled`.
- `oscillator_lockstep_equals_host_f64`; `support_hold_is_a_select_per_world` (one world of a launch holds a
  lift; its neighbours' phases are bit-identical to a launch without it); `multileg_world_independent_of_batch`
  (alone, 64, permuted slots, mixed with four-leg and wheeled worlds).

### Known answers and negative controls

- Tripod gait as an (H, K) pair for the hexapod; static walking with three feet down.
- The friction certificate of the stance program: weight 50 N pushed with 30 N at μ = 0.5 is infeasible with
  y = (0.5, −1), yᵀb = −5 N.
- KA-254: the curvature law used for the chain; control: the bracket sign reversed (the predicted direction is
  wrong by an error of order one).
- Negative controls: (a) the support rule disabled; (b) lags taken from the wrong subgroup pair (a pace on a
  hexapod), which fails static support; (c) an oscillator integrated in `f32` radians across 10⁶ steps (drift);
  (d) ring-only coupling, which admits the one-twist state; (e) a support rule evaluated for world 0 and applied
  to the whole launch (the per-world hold test fails).
- Metamorphic: rotating the radial hexapod's heading command by 60° permutes the leg traces by one slot.

## Considerations

- A symmetric controller may be tied only under the intersection of the design's group with the stabilizers of
  payload, gravity, task, observations and the current contact mode. This bead ties nothing; the lags come from
  the atlas and each leg has its own phase.
- With eight sampled feet the referee's contact capacity is exceeded; those runs use `batch-cpu` and the referee
  row is recorded `over capacity`, never truncated.
- The chain's controller is Frontier and inherits the kill condition of the curvature analysis: if that analysis
  is cut, the chain keeps the registered default gait and its result is labelled as such.
- Scores are `screening` until rank agreement is measured for the class and task family.
- When the gait atlas (BD-332, M4) lands, it must regenerate the seven rows of this bead's tables; a difference
  is a defect in one of the two and is resolved by a dated amendment, never by editing the table silently.
