## Background

The composed quasi-static screen decides, from per-module capability tables, whether an assembly of modules can
hold a load in a stance. It is cheap (TARGET under a millisecond per assembly), so the co-design cascade wants
to use it at its first two stages to discard candidates before any simulation. Whether it may do that is an
empirical question: a screen that discards the eventual winner does harm that nothing downstream will notice.

The gate that answers it is a Design comparison (GATE-SKEL-10, first milestone M4): on 200 assemblies across the
wheeled, tracked, legged and hybrid families, on carrying, ramp and threshold tasks inside the validity regime,
discarding the bottom 90% by the composed screen keeps at least 9 of the true top 10 found by batched
evaluation; Spearman ρ is at least 0.75 on energy per metre and on payload capacity; and every audited `outer`
exclusion fails its quasi-static requirement on the referee.

Its kill gate (KILL-SKEL-CELL): top-10 recall below 90% or ρ below 0.75: no automatic discard from the screen;
one unsound `outer` exclusion: the exclusion rule is suspended under the prune-soundness audit. Fallback: tables
remain as a sizing report for agents, and `design.check` runs its whole-robot analyses as before.

The plan's rules for such an experiment apply: the decision rule is frozen and its reachable outcomes enumerated
before sampling; the ceiling is stated first (the screen removes whole-robot work only for the candidates it
excludes or admits); the screen's margins are a screening signal that may discard only in strata where rank
agreement has been measured, and this experiment is that measurement.

## Scope

In: the pre-registered protocol; the 200 assemblies; the screen's scores; the batched evaluation that defines
the truth; the three statistics; the audit of exclusions on the referee; the gate record; the wiring of the
screen into cascade stages 1 and 2 under the measured mode. Out: the screen and tables; the cascade itself; the
kill decision record (N14b-31).

## Technical approach

**Protocol (registered, with its hash, before any batched run).**

1. *Assemblies.* 200, stratified 50 per family: wheeled, tracked, legged, hybrid. Each is a composition of
   catalog modules (from the archetypes' modules and the open modular class), with parameters drawn inside
   bounds from recorded seeds. Designs that fail preflight stages 1 to 5 are replaced before the freeze; none
   is replaced after it.
2. *Tasks.* Three: carrying (a payload over flat ground), ramp (a slope with the same payload), threshold (a lip
   crossing). Each instance is checked to lie inside the tables' validity regime (Froude number at most 0.5 and
   the recorded bound on the neglected inertial wrench); an instance outside it is excluded from the statistics
   and counted.
3. *Screen scores.* For each assembly and task: the verdict (`outer` exclusion, `inner` witness, `unresolved`)
   for the task's stance sets, the payload margin, and the estimated energy per metre.
4. *Truth.* Batched evaluation on the fast tier under each assembly's baseline controller, on paired world
   addresses: measured payload capacity (the largest payload at which the task's requirement holds, by
   bisection over a fixed grid) and measured energy per metre. The top 10 are the ten best by the task's
   scorecard.
5. *Statistics, per task.* (a) Recall: rank the assemblies by the screen's margin, keep the top 20 (discard the
   bottom 90%), and count how many of the true top 10 are kept; the gate needs at least 9. (b) Spearman's ρ
   between screen and truth on energy per metre and on payload capacity, with ρ = 1 − 6·Σd²/(n·(n² − 1)) on
   ranks with ties averaged; the gate needs at least 0.75 for each. (c) A bootstrap interval for each, reported
   beside the point value.
6. *Audit of exclusions.* Every `outer` exclusion (or, if there are more than 60, a stratified sample of 60
   fixed by seed before the run) is run on the referee as a quasi-static test of its own requirement and
   stance: the body is placed in the stance under the load and must fail to hold it. One exclusion that holds
   on the referee is an unsound exclusion.
7. *Reachable outcomes, enumerated before sampling.* Recall takes values 0 to 10 per task; the rule "at least 9
   on each of three tasks" is reachable. ρ thresholds are on point estimates, with intervals reported. The
   audit's rule is "zero unsound".

**Decision rule.** GATE-SKEL-10 passes when all three clauses hold on all three tasks. Otherwise the kill gate's
clauses apply separately: a recall or ρ failure removes automatic discard; an unsound exclusion suspends the
exclusion rule.

**Wiring into the cascade.** After the measurement, cascade stage 1 (deduplicate and check) calls the screen
through `design.check` with bound stances, and stage 2 (reuse and synthesize) reuses capability tables as memo
hits. The screen's mode per stratum is written to the rank-agreement ledger: `selective` or `discard-only` where
the measured agreement allows, `uninformative` elsewhere, in which case the stage skips the screen for that
stratum. Excluded and discarded assemblies enter the cascade's blinded audit lane at the manifest's rate.

**Records.** The experiment's tally (per assembly and task: the verdict, the margins, the measured capacity and
energy with their run identities; per audited exclusion: the referee's outcome) is stored as an artifact. The
gate's tier run, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features
gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-SKEL-10` (the Gauntlet's one run form; there is no `frob conformance` command),
recomputes the three statistics per task from that tally, runs controls (a) to (c) and writes the receipt. This
bead is the gate's one recorder: the row of `registries/gates.toml` (the three statistics per task, the audit's
counts, the commit, the command and the receipt hash) is entered from that receipt with `cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SKEL-10 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19), by a session that
implemented neither the tables nor the screen; no script writes it. Also recorded: the protocol and the
assembly list's digest; rows in the rank-agreement ledger keyed by family, task and rung pair.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The protocol's hash, the assembly list's digest and the audit sample's seed are on file before the first
      batched run.
- [ ] GATE-SKEL-10 is recorded with measured values: recall out of 10 per task (threshold 9), ρ for energy per
      metre and for payload capacity per task (threshold 0.75), and the audit's count of unsound exclusions
      (threshold zero), whatever the outcome.
- [ ] The count of task instances outside the validity regime is reported, and none of them enters a statistic.
- [ ] The negative controls below are caught.
- [ ] The cascade uses the screen only in strata whose ledger row permits it, and a stratum with no row is
      `uninformative`.
- [ ] The compute used is reported beside the result, with the owner's approval recorded if it exceeded the
      default envelope.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `spearman_known`: ranks (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) against (2, 1, 4, 3, 5) → Σd² = 4, ρ = 1 − 24/120 = 0.8.
- `recall_rule`: with 200 items and a screen order that places true ranks 1 to 9 and 11 in its top 20, recall is
  9 and the clause passes; with ranks 1 to 8 it fails.
- `outside_validity_excluded`.
- `audit_sample_fixed_by_seed`.
- `ledger_row_written_per_stratum`; `unmeasured_stratum_is_uninformative`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_screen_experiment.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/skel_screen_experiment/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it, `bead: "N14b-30"`): `frob
module characterize` for every module in the assembly list (one `--key` per module), `frob design check
--stages 6` with bound stances for each assembly and task, `frob eval run` on the fast batched tier with paired
world addresses for the truth (the payload bisection is one `eval run` per grid point, with a `--key` derived
from assembly, task and point, so a restarted run receives the stored receipts), `frob sim run --tier referee`
for the audit (one `--key` per audited exclusion), and the gate's tier run `e2e_tier g1_known_answers
GATE-SKEL-10 --mutations`, whose receipt carries the statistics and the controls' outcomes (its hash is logged
for the recorder; the script records no value). NDJSON per
assembly and task: command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` (claim `design-comparison` for the
gate lines, tier, determinism class, identities, seeds), verdict and margins, measured capacity and energy with
units; per task: recall against 9, the two ρ values against 0.75 with intervals; per audited exclusion: the
referee's outcome. Summary: the gate's verdict, the kill gate's clause states, compute used, and
`gates: ["GATE-SKEL-10", "KILL-SKEL-CELL"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Spearman's ρ on the five-item case above is 0.8.
- Negative controls registered for the gate: (a) a screen whose margins are shuffled across assemblies (recall
  and ρ must fail); (b) a planted unsound exclusion: an assembly whose table has one outer row tightened by 20%,
  excluded by the screen and able to hold its load on the referee (the audit must find it); (c) a "truth"
  computed from the screen's own numbers (the pin check on the evaluation's identity must reject it).
- A planted winner: one assembly known from an earlier referee result to belong in the top 10, of a family with
  few examples; it must survive the discard, or the stratum is labelled `screen-biased`.

## Considerations

- A point estimate of ρ on 200 items near 0.75 has a 95% interval of about ±0.065 (Fisher z with the Spearman
  variance factor 1.06: 0.68 to 0.81 at ρ = 0.75), wider when instances outside the validity regime shrink n;
  the gate is written on the point value, and the record prints the interval so that a marginal pass is seen
  as marginal.
- "Truth" here is batched evaluation under baseline controllers, itself a screening-level evaluator until its
  own rank agreement is measured. The gate compares the screen with the next rung up, which is what a discard
  rule needs; it does not claim agreement with trained specialists.
- Tracked and hybrid assemblies need their contact laws on the batch tiers, which close at M4; if they are not
  available those two families are recorded `not run`, and the gate is then partial.
- If the gate fails, nothing built on the tables is lost: they remain a sizing report, and stage 6 keeps its
  whole-robot analyses.
