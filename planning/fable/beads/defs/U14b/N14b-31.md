## Background

A kill gate names its trigger and its fallback in advance and is never quietly re-scoped; its decision is
recorded as an architecture decision record; and when it fires, the fallback is the plan.

KILL-SKEL-CELL governs the composed quasi-static screen: the mechanism that decides from per-module capability
tables whether an assembly can hold a load in a stance, and that the co-design cascade would use to discard and
exclude candidates before simulation. Two different harms are possible, and the gate has a clause for each.

- **Clause A (usefulness).** Trigger: top-10 recall below 90% or Spearman ρ below 0.75 at GATE-SKEL-10.
  Consequence: no automatic discard from the screen.
- **Clause B (soundness).** Trigger: one unsound `outer` exclusion. Consequence: the exclusion rule is suspended
  under the prune-soundness audit.
- **Fallback (either clause):** tables remain as a sizing report for agents; `design.check` runs its whole-robot
  analyses as before.
- **Decided:** the gate has no milestone of its own and is decided at the milestone of the gate beside it, M4.

## Scope

In: reading the experiment's record, applying the pre-registered rule, writing the decision record, and making
or scheduling the downstream changes. Out: the experiment (N14b-30); the screen and the tables.

## Technical approach

**Inputs.** From the GATE-SKEL-10 record, per task (carrying, ramp, threshold): recall out of 10; ρ on energy
per metre; ρ on payload capacity; and overall, the count of audited `outer` exclusions that held their load on
the referee. Also the registered row of the kill gate, to confirm trigger and fallback predate the measurement.

**Rule.**

| Observation | Clause | Outcome (N40-03) and what is in force |
|---|---|---|
| Recall at least 9 of 10 and both ρ at least 0.75 on every task; zero unsound exclusions | none | `not-fired`: the screen may discard in the strata the rank-agreement ledger permits, and its `outer` exclusions stand |
| Recall below 9 or any ρ below 0.75 on any task; zero unsound exclusions | A | `fired`, clause A's fallback for discard: margins are reported and discard nothing. Sound `outer` exclusions with certificates may still refuse at stage 6, because they are bounds, not rankings |
| At least one unsound exclusion | B | `fired`, clause B's fallback: the exclusion rule is suspended; an `outer` exclusion no longer refuses and no longer prunes; every would-be exclusion is run on the next rung. The case becomes a permanent negative control |
| Both | A and B | `fired`, both fallbacks: tables are a sizing report only |
| The tracked and hybrid families `not run` at M4 (N14b-30's partial gate), the measured families passing | none measured | `deferred` to the exit at which those families' batch contact laws close, with that `reason`; the unmeasured strata have no ledger row and are `uninformative`, so the screen discards nothing in them meanwhile |

**Record.** The decision ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SKEL-CELL-M4.md` (the one directory for decision records), with
N40-03's required fields: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered (both clauses) and the
amendments applied (none expected); each measured value of GATE-SKEL-10 with its receipt hash beside the
trigger's number (recall per task against 9, each ρ against 0.75, unsound exclusions against zero); the outcome;
the fallback now in force per clause and the name the capability carries from now on ("capability tables, sizing
report" under both clauses); the beads re-read, each "stands on the fallback", "superseded by this ADR" or
"withdrawn before this decision"; the downstream changes below with each item's state; the recording session,
which did not implement the screen. The register's kill row gains `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session,
receipts }`, the receipts being those of GATE-SKEL-10's record; the ADR has one section per clause.

**Downstream, by clause.**
- *A fires.* The cascade's stages 1 and 2 stop using the screen's margins to discard; the rank-agreement ledger
  rows for the screen are set to `uninformative`; campaign reports describe survivors without reference to the
  screen. `module.characterize` and the tables stay; `design.check` prints the screen's result as a sizing note.
- *B fires.* The mapping from an `outer` exclusion to a stage-6 refusal is removed (by setting the screen's
  `exclusion_refuses` to `false`, the data switch built and tested by N14b-29; clause A acts through the ledger
  rows the screen already reads, so neither clause changes code); the screen returns its
  exclusion as a finding labelled `suspended: prune-soundness`; excluded assemblies are evaluated on the next
  rung; the offending table construction is examined, and the rule is restored only by a dated amendment after a
  clean re-run of the audit.
- *Neither fires.* Nothing changes; the record exists with the measured values.
- *Deferred.* Nothing that discards is switched on in the unmeasured strata; the measured strata follow the
  table's rule for their own values; the ADR names `deferred_to`, and the decision is taken again there.
- *What never changes.* The whole-robot static analysis in stage 6; the shared-bus schedule; anything dynamic.
  Beads that depend on the discard path close with the reason `fallback` or `superseded`, citing the record.

      first batched run.
- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SKEL-CELL-M4.md` exists with N40-03's required fields, states the decision for each
      clause, cites the measured values with their receipts, and is written by a session that did not implement
      the screen; the kill row's decision entry loads under N40-03's checks (outcome one of `not-fired`, `fired`,
      `deferred`; `not-fired` with at least one receipt; `adr` the path above).
- [ ] If clause A fired: no campaign discards on the screen's margins (a planted cascade run shows the screen
      skipped), and the ledger rows read `uninformative`.
- [ ] If clause B fired: no `design.check` run refuses on an `outer` exclusion; the unsound case is in the
      negative-control registry with the assembly that exposed it.
- [ ] The M4 exit review can cite this record for KILL-SKEL-CELL.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `kill_rule_truth_table`: (recall 9, 10, 10; ρ all 0.80; unsound 0) → `not-fired`; (recall 8 on one task) →
  `fired`, A; (ρ 0.74 on one metric of one task) → `fired`, A; (unsound 1) → `fired`, B; (recall 8 and unsound
  1) → `fired`, A and B; (tracked and hybrid `not run`, the rest passing) → `deferred` with its reason.
- `fallback_a_no_discard`: with clause A's configuration the cascade's stage 1 passes every candidate that the
  screen would have ranked in the bottom 90%.
- `fallback_b_no_refusal`: with clause B's configuration the shifted-stance fixture yields a finding labelled
  `suspended: prune-soundness` and no refusal.
- `tables_still_served`: `module.characterize` behaves identically under every configuration.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_kill_cell_decision.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/skel_kill_cell_decision/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it, `bead: "N14b-31"`): reads
GATE-SKEL-10's recorded values and their receipts (and the summary line of the experiment's latest log, which
must agree), applies the rule, prints the outcome per clause, validates the decision entry with `cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example validate_register -- --file registries/gates.toml`, and in each fallback
configuration, in a scratch copy of the registries (`e2e_registries_scratch`, so that run is never cited), runs
`frob design check shifted --stages 6 --json` on N14b-29's shifted-stance fixture and a two-candidate cascade
stage to show the changed behaviour. NDJSON: the inputs with their source paths and digests, the rule's result,
the gate row's hash and date, and for the re-runs the command, exit code and stage status. Summary: the outcome
and the gate.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The rule's truth table is exact.
- Negative controls: a decision computed with the gate's row missing or dated after the measurement must be
  rejected; a recall computed against the screen's own top 10 instead of the batched truth must be rejected by
  the identity check on the truth's evaluation.

## Considerations

- Clause A and clause B are independent on purpose. A screen can rank poorly and still exclude soundly (its
  certificates are bounds); it can rank well and contain one wrong exclusion. Treating them as one switch would
  either throw away sound exclusions or keep unsound ones.
- Clause B fires on the first unsound exclusion, as the registered trigger says ("one unsound `outer`
  exclusion"), and as the prune-soundness audit does inside a campaign (one witness suspends the rule, N28b-30).
  There is no grace cycle before it fires: a repair (the table construction fixed, the audit re-run clean)
  comes after the suspension and restores the rule only through the dated amendment of the downstream list.
  An earlier draft allowed one repair cycle before the clause fired, which re-scoped the gate.
- The fallback costs little: tables as a sizing report are still what an agent reads to choose an actuator, and
  the whole-robot analyses were never removed.
- Priority is one step above the milestone's default because cascade behaviour depends on this decision.
