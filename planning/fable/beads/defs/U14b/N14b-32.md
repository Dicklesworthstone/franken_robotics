## Background

Capability tables and the composed quasi-static screen are reached by an agent through two verbs:
`module.characterize`, which builds a module's table, and `design.check`, whose stage 6 composes tables for an
assembly and returns an exclusion with a certificate, an admission with a witness, or nothing new. Their first
gate is a Known-answer claim due at M3 (GATE-SKEL-09): the two stance cases and the trot counterexample exactly;
inner ⊆ outer on 10⁴ random directions per archetype module; an edit inside one module re-characterizes one
table and no other.

This bead is the script that runs those three clauses through the real binary and records the gate's row. Unit
tests in the crates cover the mathematics; the script covers what only the outside can show: that the verb
returns a job handle or a result correctly, that the memo graph serves the untouched tables, that the
certificate leaves the process and is accepted by the independent auditor, and that the envelope carries the
verdict's type and the load-sharing model.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/skel_capability_screen.sh`, its fixture designs, a small conformance tool for the
inner-in-outer check, and the recorded row of GATE-SKEL-09. Out: the tables and the screen; the 200-assembly
experiment.

## Technical approach

**Fixtures.** `tests/fixtures/capability/four-leg/`: a module `leg-1r` (one revolute joint, a horizontal link of
0.25 m, an actuator card whose torque limit is 10 N·m at the speed bound, a point foot) and three designs that
mount four of them on a body with feet at (±0.2, ±0.2) m: `centred` (16 kg, centre of mass at the origin),
`shifted` (centre of mass 0.05 m forward) and `centred` again with a task whose stance is a diagonal pair
(`trot`). The fixtures are labelled `synthetic`.

**Steps.**

1. `frob module characterize leg-1r --json`: assert a table identifier, 26 directions, support 40 N downward
   (outer at least 40, inner at most 40) and 0 N upward, and the validity record.
2. `frob design check centred --stages 6 --json`: assert an `inner` witness with four forces of 39.24 N, the
   load-sharing model in the envelope, exit 0 or 2.
3. `frob design check shifted --stages 6 --json`: assert an `outer` exclusion, exit 3, a certificate identifier;
   export the certificate with `frob prove bundle` and check it with the offline auditor `frob-verify`: accepted.
   Corrupt one multiplier in a copy of the bundle: rejected.
4. `frob design check centred --task trot-stance --stages 6 --json`: assert an `outer` exclusion for the trot
   stance, and that step 2's result for the four-foot stance is still served unchanged.
5. *Inner in outer.* For each module of each archetype available: `frob module characterize`, then the tool
   `capability-check` (in `frob-conformance`) draws 10⁴ random directions per pose cell from a recorded seed and
   asserts that the inner support never exceeds the outer support.
6. *Edit locality.* On a four-leg archetype: characterize all leg modules (four memo misses or one, by content);
   `frob design edit … set-param` inside one leg; run `frob design check --stages 6`; assert from the envelope's
   memo record that exactly one table was re-characterized and the others were hits with unchanged digests.
   Repeat with `regear` and `swap-actuator`.
7. *Outside validity.* A requirement at a Froude number of 0.8: assert `unresolved` with `outside-validity`,
   never a verdict.
8. *Timing.* Record the wall time of one characterization and the median and 95th percentile of the screen over
   1,000 repetitions on the fixture.
9. *Cancellation.* Start a characterization with a wait of zero, cancel the job, and assert that no partial
   table is served afterwards.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SKEL-09 (TARGET) is recorded from the script's log with measured values: the three stance results
      exact; inner ⊆ outer with zero violations over 10⁴ directions per archetype module; one table
      re-characterized per in-module edit and no other.
- [ ] The certificate of the shifted case is accepted by the offline auditor on its own, and the corrupted copy
      is rejected.
- [ ] The trot result and the four-foot result coexist for the same assembly, each bound to its stance.
- [ ] The script exits non-zero on any failed assertion, is idempotent and seed-explicit, and its controls are
      caught on every run.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

For `capability-check`: `inner_point_outside_outer_detected` (a synthetic table with one inner vertex moved 1%
outward is reported); `seed_reproducible`. For the script's helpers: `memo_record_parser` (hits and misses are
counted from the envelope, not inferred from timing).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_capability_screen.sh`, sourcing the shared helper of STD-E2E and writing
`target/e2e-logs/skel_capability_screen/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Every line has
`ts`, `script`, `bead: "N14b-32"`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` (claim `known-answer`, class, tier `static`, determinism, `ModelId`, table identifiers, seed for the
direction draws), the verdict and its type, the load-sharing model, forces in newtons against their expected
values, the certificate identifier and the auditor's result, memo hits and misses, directions tested and
violations, and timings against their targets. On failure: the first differing value, the cell and direction or
the foot, and the paths of the table, the envelope and the bundle. The final `summary` line gives assertions
passed and failed, controls caught and `gates: ["GATE-SKEL-09"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Four identical one-joint legs, each a horizontal link of 0.25 m with a torque limit of 10 N·m, can each press
  with at most 40 N. Under a 16 kg body (156.96 N) on a square stance with feet at (±0.2, ±0.2) m: centred,
  39.24 N per foot, admitted; centre of mass 0.05 m forward, the front pair must carry 0.625 × 156.96 = 98.1 N
  > 2 × 40 N, excluded for every force distribution, the certificate being the vertical force balance plus the
  pitch moment balance; in a trot two diagonal feet carry 78.48 N each against 40 N.
- Controls run every time: (a) a build flag that makes the screen ignore the stance set must admit the trot case
  (the script must then fail); (b) a table with one outer row tightened by 5% must violate inner ⊆ outer; (c) a
  memo key without the module hash must show zero re-characterizations after an edit (the locality assertion
  must fail).
- Determinism lanes: table digests and verdicts equal twice on one machine, in debug and release; cross-platform
  digests compared and the result recorded with the determinism class the table claims.

## Considerations

- The gate row is recorded by a session that did not implement the tables or the screen.
- The fixtures are synthetic by design: the known answers need round numbers. The inner-in-outer and locality
  clauses run on real archetype modules.
- If the screen is later restricted by its kill gate, steps 3 and 4 change their expected exit from a refusal to
  a finding; the script reads the decision record's configuration and asserts accordingly.
