## Background

Imported robots are full of fixed joints: a head welded to a torso, a logo plate, a sensor bracket, a rubber
hand. A simulator does not want a degree of freedom, a constraint row or a link slot for each. The usual answer
is to "merge" such links into their parents, and the usual ways of doing it are wrong in one of two ways: the
child's inertia is added without the offset term, or the child is dropped.

This bead gives the rule (tier Solid) for lumping a link behind a fixed joint into its parent, in import and in
the model's lowering. The principle: **a folded model is the same rigid body.** A model that omits mass is a
different robot and is named as one; that second half is the reduced-model bead's.

Why the pseudo-inertia. A rigid body's ten inertial parameters are equivalent to the 4×4 matrix
𝖯 = [[Σ, h], [hᵀ, m]], with Σ = ∫ x·xᵀ dm the second moment, h = ∫ x dm = m·c the first moment and m the mass;
the rotational inertia about the origin is Ī = tr(Σ)·I − Σ, and Σ = ½·tr(Ī)·I − Ī recovers it. Under a rigid
transform G = [[R, t], [0, 1]] the pseudo-inertia maps as G·𝖯·Gᵀ. So one sum carries mass, first moment and
second moment about the parent's origin, and it is exact in rationals. Rejected alternative: transforming mass,
centre of mass and inertia tensor separately with the parallel-axis theorem, which is correct and is where the
offset term gets lost in practice.

## Scope

In: the fold as a function in `frob-model`; its use in the lowerings and, on request, in import; re-parenting of
named attachments; the loss-report entries; the gate's battery. Out: reduced models and the mass-dropped refusal
(N14b-34); the importers; the mass roll-up analysis that prints folded groups at preflight stage 2.

## Technical approach

**The fold.** For a child with pseudo-inertia 𝖯_c attached by the fixed transform G = `parent_from_child`, the
parent's pseudo-inertia becomes

    𝖯_p ← 𝖯_p + G·𝖯_c·Gᵀ.

**Algorithm (`crates/frob-model/src/fold.rs`).**

1. Walk the tree from the leaves to the root. For each joint of class `fixed`, the child body is folded into its
   parent; a chain of fixed joints folds transitively into the first ancestor reached through a non-fixed joint
   (or the root). The set of bodies folded into one retained body is a **fold group**.
2. Inertia: apply the sum above with the composed transform from the retained body's frame to the folded
   body's frame. Entries are exact rationals whenever the transforms are (translations in exact decimals,
   rotations by exact fractions of a turn whose sines and cosines are rational); otherwise the computation is in
   double precision and the result is checked against the unfolded sums to 10⁻¹² relative.
3. Joints below a folded body: each non-fixed joint whose parent was folded is re-parented to the retained body,
   with its `parent_from_joint` composed with the fold transform.
4. Named attachments of a folded body (sensor frames, collision shapes, contact sites, port frames, visual
   meshes) are re-parented with the composed transform and **keep their identifiers**. A consumer that asks for
   `head.camera` gets the same world pose as before the fold.
5. Output: the folded tree and a `FoldReport { groups: [ { retained, folded: [ids], transforms } ] }`. The
   loss report gains one entry of class `folded` per folded link, with its mass.
6. The design term is not changed: constructor terms stay unexpanded and fixed joints stay in the canonical
   form. The fold is a compile product of the lowering, memoized like any other, with the fixed joints' fields
   in its influence record. On import, `--fold-fixed` asks the importer to write the folded tree as the design
   and to list each folded link in the report.

**Hand case.** A parent of 2 kg with rotational inertia 0.02·I kg·m² about its origin carries a welded child of
1 kg with inertia 0.01·I about its own centre, placed at (0.3, 0, 0) m. In pseudo-inertias: parent Σ = 0.01·I,
h = 0, m = 2; child in its own frame Σ = 0.005·I, h = 0, m = 1; after G (a translation by t = (0.3, 0, 0)) the
child contributes Σ = 0.005·I + diag(0.09, 0, 0), h = (0.3, 0, 0), m = 1. The fold has mass 3 kg, centre of
mass at (0.1, 0, 0) m, inertia diag(0.03, 0.12, 0.12) kg·m² about the parent origin and diag(0.03, 0.09, 0.09)
about the combined centre. Adding the two inertias without the offset term gives 0.03·I about the origin, wrong
by 0.09 kg·m² on two axes. Dropping the child gives 2 kg.

**The gate's battery (GATE-SKEL-11, the fold clauses).**

1. *Sums.* On 10³ random trees with fixed joints (the generator with a fixed-joint rate, recorded seed): for
   every fold group, mass, first moment and pseudo-inertia of the folded body equal the sums over the unfolded
   links, exactly in rationals and to 10⁻¹² relative in double precision.
2. *Dynamics.* For each tree, forward dynamics of the folded model and of the unfolded model with the fixed
   joints held as constraints, both on the referee, at random states and torques: joint accelerations agree to
   10⁻⁹.
3. *Attachments.* Every named attachment resolves to the same world pose in the folded and unfolded models at
   100 random configurations.
4. *Hand case.* Exact; and the mutant without the offset term fails it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SKEL-11, fold clauses (TARGET): the four parts of the battery pass on 10³ random trees with the
      measured maxima recorded (rational: exact equality; double: at most 10⁻¹² relative; dynamics: at most
      10⁻⁹; attachments: identical poses).
- [ ] The hand case is exact: mass 3 kg, centre of mass (0.1, 0, 0) m, diag(0.03, 0.12, 0.12) kg·m² about the
      parent origin, diag(0.03, 0.09, 0.09) about the combined centre.
- [ ] The no-offset mutant and the drop-the-child mutant each fail the battery.
- [ ] Folding never changes a `DesignId`; it changes the lowered model only, and the fold's product is
      invalidated exactly by edits to a fixed joint's fields or to a folded body's mass fields.
- [ ] Every folded link appears in the loss report on import with `--fold-fixed`, and every attachment keeps its
      identifier.
- [ ] Preflight stage 2's mass roll-up equals the unfolded total to the same tolerances.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `fold_hand_case_exact`: the numbers above, in rationals.
- `fold_pseudo_inertia_values`: Σ = diag(0.105, 0.015, 0.015), h = (0.3, 0, 0), m = 3; Ī = tr(Σ)·I − Σ =
  diag(0.03, 0.12, 0.12).
- `fold_chain_transitive`: three bodies welded in a row fold into one group with composed transforms; the result
  equals folding in either order.
- `fold_with_rotation`: a child rotated 90° about z with inertia diag(1, 2, 3) contributes diag(2, 1, 3) before
  the offset term.
- `fold_reparents_joint`: a revolute joint below a folded body has `parent_from_joint` equal to the composition.
- `fold_keeps_attachment_identifiers`: `head.camera` resolves to the same pose.
- `fold_zero_mass_child`: a massless bracket folds to no change in 𝖯_p and still re-parents its attachments.
- `fold_is_memoized`: editing a non-fixed joint's limits leaves the fold product untouched.
- `no_offset_mutant_fails`; `drop_child_mutant_fails`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_fold.sh`: generates 10³ trees with the development generator (`frob-gen-designs --mode
well-formed --fixed-joint-rate 1/3 --seed S --count 1000`), and for each runs a conformance tool `fold-battery`
that compiles both lowerings, compares the group sums, calls the referee for forward dynamics on both, and
compares attachment poses at 100 configurations; then imports the 38-link humanoid source with `frob design
import --fold-fixed --json` and reads the report. NDJSON per tree: seed and index, fold groups, the maximum
relative differences in mass, first moment and pseudo-inertia against 10⁻¹², the maximum acceleration difference
against 10⁻⁹, attachment mismatches, `evidence` (tier `referee` for the dynamics lines), and on failure the
group, the entry and both values with the tree's file. Summary: trees passed, mutants caught,
`gates: ["GATE-SKEL-11"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The hand case of the chapter; the two mutants are its controls (no offset term: 0.03·I, wrong by 0.09 kg·m² on
  two axes; child dropped: 2 kg).
- Check on the transform law: under a reflection the same G·𝖯·Gᵀ holds with an improper G, which is what the
  `mirror` constructor uses; a fold across a mirrored limb must give the mirror image of the fold of the
  original.
- Metamorphic: folding commutes with a change of base frame; folding a model twice changes nothing; the total
  pseudo-inertia about the root is invariant under folding.
- Determinism lanes: the folded model's digest is identical twice on one machine, across worker counts, in debug
  and release, and on Linux and macOS (class D0 for the rational path).

## Considerations

- The referee lowers fixed joints as constraints only for the comparison; in production the referee receives the
  folded model. If the sibling's articulated model has no constraint for a welded joint, the comparison uses a
  revolute joint locked by a stiff, exact holonomic row, and the row's residual is reported with the result.
- Double-precision paths exist only where transforms are irrational; the report states which fold groups were
  computed exactly.
- A fold changes link numbering in the lowered model. Results are keyed by identifiers, never by index, so
  telemetry and attachments are unaffected; a consumer that kept an index across a fold is in error.
- The template arm of the mixed-batch experiment uses this function to fold locked children; a change here
  changes that arm's instances.
