## Background

Folding a welded link into its parent keeps the robot the same rigid body. Leaving the link out does not: **a
model that omits mass is a different robot and is named as one.** This bead makes that sentence enforceable.

The case that motivates it is in the suite. FACT (`frankensim/crates/fs-mbd/src/robot_models.rs:398–403` at
`483d09c682f2`, read 2026-11-21): the G1 catalog builder states that fixed rubber hands, head, pelvis contour,
logo, waist supports and sensor attachments are omitted and that "their nonzero masses are not lumped into
retained links". Its 30 retained links sum to 31.96314202 kg (summed from the source table). FACT (the pinned
upstream description of the same robot; reported, to be re-read from the primary source before a gate cites
it): four welded links, the head, the logo and the two hands, add 1.377 kg, for 33.34014202 kg over 38 links.

The difference decides verdicts. At 9.81 m/s² the reduced model weighs 313.5584 N and the source assembly
327.0668 N. A static support rated at 320 N passes on the reduction and fails on the robot. A result computed
on the reduced model and reported as a result about the G1 would be wrong, with nothing in the numbers to show
it. Hence three rules (DESIGN): a reduction is declared in the design; an undeclared one is refused at import;
and every result on a reduced design cites the reduction. The builder is used as the profile `g1-reduced-30`,
and the `humanoid` archetype's import parity is judged against the 38-link source.

## Technical approach

**The block.** A design that leaves out bodies of its source carries

```json
"reduced": { "source": "b3:…", "profile": "g1-reduced-30",
             "omitted": [ {"id": "head_link", "mass": "… kg"}, … ],
             "omitted_mass": "1.377 kg" }
```

Rules: `omitted_mass` equals the exact sum of the listed masses; `source` is the hash of the full source
document; a design with a `reduced` block has a different `DesignId` from the full import, as any field change
gives. The block is an optional field of the schema (a minor version adds optional fields); a design without it
claims to be complete with respect to its source. DESIGN (one record, stated with N33c-41): the airlock's M0
annotation of the catalog G1 model uses the same field names (`profile`, `omitted` with `id` and `mass`,
`omitted_mass`) with the masses `unknown` and no `source`, the builder's revision being carried there as
`builder`; it is this record at an earlier stage, not a second schema, and the asset of ROBOT-fsim-g1 carries
the full block.

**Mass accounting at import.**
1. The importer sums the masses of all bodies in the source (Σ_source) and of all bodies it writes to the
   design, after any fold (Σ_design).
2. With no `--reduced` option: if any source body of nonzero mass has neither been written nor folded, the
   import is refused: mass dropped at import without a declared reduction. The refusal's payload names each
   dropped body with its mass and the total, and its repairs are, in order: fold the body (`--fold-fixed`, when
   it hangs on a fixed joint); import it; or declare the reduction (`--reduced <profile>`).
3. With `--reduced <profile>` (the one option of VERBS-design's row; `<profile>` is a profile name resolved in
   `assets/profiles/` or a path to a profile file of the same schema, which is how an explicit list of omitted
   bodies is given, DESIGN): the bodies named by the profile are left out, the block is written, and the
   identity Σ_source = Σ_design + `omitted_mass` is checked exactly. A profile that names a body the source does
   not have is refused with `FROB-REF-103` (a target that does not resolve; pointer `reduced.omitted[<i>]`), and
   one that misses a body that was dropped is refused with this bead's refusal naming that body (DESIGN: the
   first is a misspelt reference, the second is mass dropped without a declaration).
4. The loss report gains one entry of class `omitted` per omitted body, with its mass.
5. A body of zero mass may be dropped without a declaration; it is listed in the report as `dropped`, and its
   named attachments must still be re-parented or reported.

**Profiles.** `assets/profiles/g1-reduced-30.toml`: the source's hash, the 30 retained link names, the omitted
links with their masses (the four welded links of 1.377 kg in total and the massless omissions the builder
lists), and the statement of provenance above. The profile's retained masses must sum to 31.96314202 kg.

**Admission of the builder.** FrankenSim's catalog builder is reached through the airlock. Its admission test
(the mass roll-up through the airlock equals 31.96314202 kg, and the reduction lists its omissions) is the
probe N33c-41 runs at M0 and records for GATE-WS-09; this bead does not run a second probe. It checks that
the profile file agrees with that record: the 30 retained names and their sum, and the omission list as read at
the pin. Interim posture: the builder is used as the reduced profile, never as the import-parity reference.

**Every result cites the reduction.** The compiled model carries the block. The envelope of every verb whose
result depends on mass (`design.check`, analyses, `sim.run`, comparisons, reports) includes
`evidence.reduced = { profile, omitted_mass }` and a warning line; a comparison between designs with different
reductions of the same source is labelled `different-robot` and refuses to produce a preference unless the
caller asks for it explicitly. `design.show` prints the block first.

**The 320 N support check (DESIGN form of the chapter's fixture).** `fixtures/skel/support-320N.toml` holds
the rating (320 N) and g (9.81 m/s²). The check reads the design's total mass m from `frob design show <d>
--mass --json` (whose envelope carries `evidence.reduced` on a reduced design) and passes when m·g ≤ 320 N. It
is a weight comparison, not a `design.check` stage, because an imported robot stops at stage 1 with
`FROB-REF-121` until its end effectors are declared, and the fixture must discriminate on mass alone. The same
function is called by the unit test `support_320N` and by N14b-24's tier test.

**Import parity.** Parity is judged against the full source: a round trip compares total mass, first moment and
pseudo-inertia of every folded group, and the map of named attachments. A reduced design is never an
import-parity reference.

**The refusal's code.** The chapter states the refusal and assigns it no number. It is the ledger row
`import-mass-dropped` of the second allocation round (N35x-02: family `1xx`, the proposal 127 on file, no
interim), registered beside this chapter's codes by N14-REF. N35x-02's allocation run numbers it at M0, before
this M1 bead (a dependency through N32-REF), so it is raised under the assigned number from the start, through
one named constant (`IMPORT_MASS_DROPPED`) that the registry generates from that row; no slug or other stand-in
is ever raised (a slug is not an admitted interim, N35-33 rule 7). It joins `design.import`'s refusal list in
VERBS-design under that number. The implementation never takes "the next free code" of the block or the
proposal 127 for itself (a bead never invents a number); the number is read from the constant. Exit code 3
(refused input), `retry: after-change`; payload each dropped body with its mass and the total; repairs in the
order of step 2.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SKEL-11, reduced-model clauses (TARGET), the parts this bead can show: the `g1-reduced-30` profile file
      lists its omissions and its 30 retained masses sum exactly to 31.96314202 kg; the 320 N requirement passes
      on a design built from the profile's mass table and fails at 33.34014202 kg (`support_320N`). The same
      clauses on the imported 38-link source are measured by N14b-24's special fixtures, because that source is
      MJCF and the MJCF importer (BD-066) depends on this bead. N14b-24 is the gate's one recorder (from its
      tier run's receipt); this bead writes no gate row.
- [ ] Importing a URDF source with a massive link removed and no declaration is refused through BD-065's
      importer, and the payload names the link, its mass and the total; the accounting function is the one
      BD-066 calls for MJCF, so that the 38-link case (four links, 1.377 kg) is refused by the same code in
      N14b-24.
- [ ] Importing with `--reduced <profile>` succeeds on the synthetic source; Σ_source = Σ_design + omitted_mass
      holds exactly; the loss report lists each omitted body.
- [ ] Every envelope computed on the reduced design carries the reduction; a report that omits it fails its
      schema check.
- [ ] A comparison of the reduced and full designs is labelled `different-robot`.
- [ ] The builder's admission row exists (N33c-41's record: 31.96314202 kg through the airlock, omissions
      listed) and the profile file agrees with it.
- [ ] The `humanoid` archetype's mass row and import parity cite the 38-link source, not the profile.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `weights`: 31.96314202 × 9.81 = 313.5584 N; 33.34014202 × 9.81 = 327.0668 N; 33.34014202 − 31.96314202 =
  1.377 kg.
- `undeclared_drop_refused`: a three-link source of 2 kg, 1 kg and 0.5 kg imported with the 0.5 kg link excluded
  → refusal naming it and 0.5 kg (fixture labelled `synthetic`).
- `zero_mass_drop_allowed_and_reported`.
- `declared_reduction_accounts_exactly`: Σ_source = Σ_design + omitted_mass in rationals.
- `profile_mismatch_refused`: a profile naming a link absent from the source → `FROB-REF-103` with pointer
  `reduced.omitted[0]`; a profile missing a dropped link → the mass-dropped refusal naming that link.
- `refusal_raised_through_constant`: the undeclared drop's refusal carries the code of `IMPORT_MASS_DROPPED`,
  which equals the `assigned` number of the ledger row `import-mass-dropped` in
  `registries/refusal_allocations.toml` and the registry row whose `name` is that slug; never a slug and never a
  literal 127.
- `reduced_block_changes_design_id`.
- `envelope_carries_reduction`: `design.check` on a reduced design has `evidence.reduced`.
- `comparison_labels_different_robot`.
- `support_320N`: a static support requirement of 320 N: margin positive on the reduction (313.5584 N), negative
  on the full import (327.0668 N).

### End-to-end script and logging

Rows in `scripts/e2e/skel_import_export.sh` (N14b-24, which runs once the MJCF importer exists): the
undeclared-drop refusal, the declared import, the mass sums, and the 320 N support check on both designs. Until
then this bead's own script `scripts/e2e/skel_reduced.sh` runs the same steps on the synthetic three-link URDF
source (`frob design import fixtures/skel/three-link.urdf --format urdf --name red-a --key red-imp-1 --json`
without, and `… --name red-b --reduced fixtures/skel/three-link-reduced.toml --key red-imp-2 --json` with, the
declaration, distinct keys; `frob design show red-b --mass --json`, asserting `evidence.reduced`; the support
check with a rating scaled to that source: 30 N, against 3.5 kg × 9.81 m/s² = 34.335 N on the full source,
which fails, and 3.0 kg × 9.81 = 29.43 N on the reduction without the 0.5 kg link, which passes) and checks the
profile file's sum. Log lines (STD-E2E
NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/skel_reduced/<utc>.ndjson`, `bead: "N14b-34"`) carry Σ_source,
Σ_design and omitted mass in kilograms, the weights in newtons against 320 N, the refusal's code and payload, the
`evidence.reduced` block, and on failure the first body whose mass is unaccounted for. Declared plant
(`E2E_PLANT_FAILURE`): `silent-drop` (the importer option that drops a link without accounting), which the
undeclared-drop step must catch.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The chapter's known answer: 313.5584 N and 327.0668 N against a support rated at 320 N.
- The profile's sum: 31.96314202 kg over 30 links; the source: 33.34014202 kg over 38 links.
- Negative controls: (a) an importer option that drops links silently (the accounting check must refuse); (b) an
  envelope builder that strips `evidence.reduced` (the schema check must fail); (c) the reduced profile used as
  the import-parity reference (the parity tool must reject a reference that carries a `reduced` block).
- Metamorphic: declaring a reduction and then folding gives the same omitted mass as folding and then declaring;
  importing the full source and deleting the four links by typed edits yields a design that must also carry the
  block, or the edit is refused.

## Considerations

- The upstream figures (1.377 kg; 33.34014202 kg; 38 links) are reported and must be re-read from the primary
  source before a gate cites them; the profile's own sum, 31.96314202 kg, was computed from the builder's table
  and is the firmer number.
- The rule applies to import. A user who builds a simplified robot by hand is not "reducing" anything; a design
  with no source has no `reduced` block.
- Reductions are sometimes right: a 30-link model is a reasonable training model. The rule does not forbid it;
  it forbids calling its results results about the 38-link robot.
- The same pattern applies to any catalog builder in a sibling that omits mass; each gets a profile and an
  admission row, not a silent use.
