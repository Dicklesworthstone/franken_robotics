## Background

`snake-crawler` is archetype 17 of the catalog of eighteen (`assets/archetypes/`, listed by `design.templates`):
a planar serial chain of links joined by actuated revolute joints, each body carrying a pair of passive wheels
across its axis. It has no driven wheel; it moves by changing shape (locomotion as holonomy, construction C-08:
the wheel constraints define a connection 𝔸 that maps shape velocity to body velocity, and a small loop in
shape space moves the body by about exp(−∬ D𝔸), with D𝔸 the curvature). Its two roles in the catalog are to be
the fixture for the sign of the bracket term of the curvature (analysis A-09) and to exercise the
`serial(k, link)` constructor at scale.

This bead exists to break a cycle in the order of work. The curvature analysis (BD-243, analysis A-09) has as its
done-condition the sign test on the `snake-crawler` archetype, and its end-to-end script instantiates it with
`frob design new --template snake-crawler`. The M3 archetype bead N14b-04, which writes the template's known
answers, presets and preflight rows, needs A-09 for the snake's stage-4 row (preflight stage 4 runs curvature,
not the growth vector, on a kinematic shape-changer). Neither can finish first while the template term lives
inside N14b-04. The shared piece is the design term itself, which needs neither analysis: it is authored and
compiled here, both beads depend on this one, and N14b-04 keeps everything about the snake that needs A-09.

## Scope

In: the template files `assets/archetypes/snake-crawler/design.fbot` and `manifest.toml` (catalog format of
BD-109) with the term below, its parameters, bounds, levers, power bus, sensors and budgets; its `symmetry`
assertion; the compile of the term through the memoized pipeline; the checks that need only the compiler (body,
joint and wheel counts, `n_v`, Γ_mech from the symmetry functor compared with a hand derivation, the memo counts
of `serial`); its own end-to-end script and its entry in the table of the archetype end-to-end script; flipping
`available = true` for this archetype in `design.templates`.

Out, with the bead that has it: the connection, its curvature and the holonomy fixture (BD-243, which depends on
this bead); the snake's known answers, its preflight stage-4 and stage-6 rows and its negative controls
(N14b-04, which depends on this bead and on BD-243); the zoo instance and expectation file (ZOO-snake-crawler);
the chain-crawler controller (N14b-08); the catalog mechanism and `design.templates` (BD-109).

## Technical approach

**The term (DESIGN; the one statement shared word for word with N14b-04 and ZOO-snake-crawler).** A head body
(the term's root) followed by `serial(8, link)`, where one `link` is an actuated revolute joint about the
vertical axis, a body and a passive wheel pair. That gives nine bodies, eight actuated joints (the catalog's
count), nine wheel pairs (the head carries one too) and nine Pfaffian rows. Reason: `serial(k, link)` repeats
`link` k times, eight joints need nine bodies, and counting the head as a ninth use of the link product would
contradict the constructor's definition.

The file is written in `frob.design/1` using only fields of the schema's field reference (an unlisted field is
refused with FROB-REF-103), with these parts (DESIGN defaults; every number illustrative):

| Part | Written as | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Head (root) | `body` with `region` capsule, `fill` shell, `material`, `ports` `tail`, `wl`, `wr` | the link body's capsule exactly: r 0.035 m, length 0.12 m; shell 2 mm; `al-6061-t6`; `wl`, `wr` at mid-length, (0, ±0.05 m, 0) from the body's centre |
| Chain | `serial` of 8 `link`, attached at `head.tail` | 8 |
| `link` joint | `joint` class `R`, `axis` (0, 0, 1), `limits`, `actuator` (a generic small servo card) | limits −60° to 60° |
| `link` body | `body` with `region` capsule, `fill` shell, `material`, ports `tail`, `wl`, `wr` | capsule r 0.035 m, length 0.12 m; shell 2 mm; `al-6061-t6`; joint at the front end, `tail` at the rear end, `wl`, `wr` at mid-length |
| Wheel pair (head and every link) | two `wheel` end effectors attached at `wl` and `wr` through passive revolute joints about the lateral axis (`limits: unbounded`, no actuator), each with `region` cylinder, `fill` `solid`, `material`, `contact` law `wheel-rolling` with its interface card | radius 0.03 m, width 0.01 m |
| Power and sensing | `power_bus` with battery, nominal voltage, current limit, `regen`, `sink`; encoders on `joints:*`; an IMU on the head; each with parent, rational rate and clock | — |
| Budgets and levers | `mass_max`, `power_peak_max`, `envelope_max`; levers `link_length` (0.08 to 0.20 m, ℝ₊) and `wheel_offset` (the lateral port offset, 0.03 to 0.08 m, ℝ₊) | — |
| Symmetry | `assert_mech` naming the head-to-tail reversal group | — |

N14b-04 may move a default by a dated amendment of this file, never by a second statement elsewhere.

**The group of this term (hand derivation, written down before the first exact computation).** The catalog's
cell for this archetype is "C₂ under head-to-tail reversal": the map that reverses the link order (body k to
body 8 − k, joint i to joint 9 − i) and negates the joint angles, realized by the half-turn about the vertical
axis through the middle body. Three facts decide whether the compiler computes exactly that group.

1. The reversal needs a palindromic chain: body k and body 8 − k equal in Region, fill, material and
   pseudo-inertia with their ports at mirrored positions; joint i and joint 9 − i equal in class, limits, card
   and transmission. The defaults above are chosen for that: the head is the link's capsule (an earlier form of
   this table gave the head a radius of 0.04 m against the links' 0.035 m, which alone excludes the reversal),
   the wheel ports sit at mid-length and the joint limits are symmetric about zero. What the Regions cannot
   settle is the mass an actuator card adds to the roll-up: if it is carried by the joint's child body, the
   head has no actuator and the last link has one, and the reversal is not a symmetry of the model.
2. The reversal moves the root (the head goes to the last link). It is found only because the symmetry
   computation re-roots a free-base tree at its centre, here the middle body (N13-15's rule for root-moving
   automorphisms); the reported permutation and signs are in this term's own numbering.
3. A chain whose bodies are each left-right symmetric also has the sagittal mirror: it fixes every body,
   exchanges the two wheels of each pair and negates every joint angle (as it negates the waist-yaw angle of
   `humanoid`, a joint that mirror also maps to itself, in a group the catalog does list). With the reversal
   that gives C₂ × C₂
   (the sagittal mirror, the transverse mirror and the half-turn), not C₂. The head IMU, on the centre line,
   is fixed by the sagittal mirror, so Γ_obs is then C₂ generated by that mirror where the catalog's reading
   expects the trivial group (C₂ without the IMU).

By this derivation the term's Γ_mech is C₂ × C₂ if fact 1 holds in the compiled model and C₂ generated by the
sagittal mirror if it does not; in neither case is it the cell's group. DESIGN, fail-closed: this bead does not
shape the term until the cell comes out (wheel pairs staggered along each body would remove both mirrors and
keep the half-turn, at the price of an archetype shaped by its own test) and it does not edit the group on
file. It ships the natural term with the catalog's assertion, computes the group exactly, reports each of the
three elements as present or absent with the first mismatch of an absent one, and hands the difference to the
open question in Considerations. The question is settled before N14b-04 and BD-246 measure the group rows.

**Manifest.** `number = 17`, `layout = "planar serial chain with passive wheels"`, `actuated = 8`, `n_v = 11`
(the count N14b-04 and the zoo bead use: the planar model's 3 base coordinates on the floor plane, the other
three of the floating base held by the wheel contacts, plus 8 joints, with the wheel spins eliminated by the
rolling constraint; 14 with the full floating base, as ZOO-snake-crawler states; a lowering that keeps the 18
passive wheel spins as joint coordinates records its own count beside these, so that the size-bucket and
capacity code read the right number), `gamma_mech = "C2 under
head-to-tail reversal"` with its generator (reverse the link order, negate the joint angles; this is the
catalog's cell, and it changes only by the decision of the open question below), `morphology_class =
"kinematic shape-changer"`, `contact_laws = ["wheel-rolling"]`, `tests = ["C-08 holonomy sign (A-09)",
"serial(k, link) at scale"]`, `reference_robots = []`, `first_milestone = "M3"`.

**What this bead checks, with the compiler only.**

1. The term parses in both views and compiles (preflight stage 1 is the compile): nine bodies, eight actuated
   revolute joints, nine wheel pairs, every wheel bound to `wheel-rolling` with its interface card.
2. The memo graph of the compile (BD-063) holds one `link` product with one miss and seven hits for its eight
   uses, and the head as a separate product.
3. The symmetry functor (N14a-11) computes Γ_mech exactly and the result is compared with the hand derivation
   above, element by element: the sagittal mirror (expected present), the reversal by the half-turn and the
   transverse mirror (present exactly when the compiled pseudo-inertias of body k and body 8 − k are equal for
   every k). The shipped assertion is the catalog's; whether stage 5 accepts it (`frob design check
   --stages 1-5`, FROB-REF-109 with the first mismatch otherwise) is recorded and is not a closing condition of
   this bead. A planted assertion that is false under every reading, `assert_mech: D4`, refuses with
   FROB-REF-109 naming the missing quarter-turn.
4. Mass roll-up (A-02): total mass inside `mass_max`; every body's mass from region, fill and material.
5. `design.templates` lists `snake-crawler` as available, with its parameters and `first_milestone = "M3"`.

Determinism: D0. The `DesignId` of the default instance is pinned and equal on Linux and macOS.

      body's `fill` refuses with FROB-REF-105.

- [ ] BD-243 can instantiate the template with `--template snake-crawler` (its end-to-end script's first step

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `snake_term_counts`: bodies 9, actuated revolutes 8, wheel pairs 9, Pfaffian rows 9.
- `snake_n_v`: 11 planar; 14 with the full floating base.
- `serial_compiles_once`: memo statistics of a cold compile: `link` 1 miss, 7 hits; head 1 miss.
- `serial_link_edit_recompiles_link_once`: `set-param link_length 0.15 m` recomputes the `link` product once and
  the eight instance frames, and nothing of the head's inertial product.
- `snake_sagittal_mirror_present`: the computed group contains the element that fixes every body, exchanges
  `wl` and `wr` wheels and has sign −1 on each of the eight joint coordinates.
- `snake_reversal_iff_palindromic`: the half-turn element (body k to body 8 − k, joint i to joint 9 − i, sign
  −1) is in the group exactly when the compiled pseudo-inertias of body k and body 8 − k are equal for every k;
  both facts are read from the model, and a group that lists the reversal over unequal inertias fails.
- `snake_head_radius_breaks_reversal` (planted): the head's capsule radius set to 0.04 m removes the half-turn
  and the transverse mirror, keeps the sagittal mirror, and records the head's Region as the first mismatch.
- `snake_false_assertion_109`: `assert_mech: D4` → FROB-REF-109 naming the quarter-turn.
- `snake_group_report_complete`: the report names the three elements of the hand derivation, each present or
  absent, and the group's order equals the number present plus one.
- `snake_no_physics_default`: removing a wheel's `contact` → 121; removing a body's `fill` → 105; an inverted
  `link` joint range (60° to −60°) → 107, named once for the `link` term, not eight times.
- `snake_views_roundtrip`: JSON → S-expression → JSON is the identity on the canonical form; one `DesignId`.
- `snake_pinned_id`: the default instance's `DesignId` equals the pinned value.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_snake_term.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/skel_snake_term/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; idempotent; no seed).
DESIGN: a script of its own, because the memo counts and the false-assertion step are not steps of the
data-driven catalog script (`scripts/e2e/skel_archetypes.sh`, N14b-06, to which later beads add table rows and
no code). Steps: `frob design templates --name snake-crawler --json`; `frob design new s --template
snake-crawler --key snk-1 --json`; `frob design show s --json`; `frob design show s --section products --json`
(the memo counts); `frob design show s --section symmetry --json` (the group and its elements); `frob design
view s --as sexpr --json` and back; `frob design check s --stages 1-1 --json`; `frob design check s
--stages 1-5 --json` (the result of the assertion check, recorded as a `metric`, not asserted). Assertions: the
counts above, the memo counts, the pinned `DesignId`, the sagittal mirror present, the reversal present exactly
when the model is palindromic. Negative-control step (`e2e_expect_caught`): `frob design new s-bad --file
fixtures/skel/snake-false-assert.fbot --key snk-bad --json` (the `D4` assertion; the term compiles), then
`frob design check s-bad --stages 1-5 --json` must refuse at stage 5 with FROB-REF-109, exit 3. Every line has
`ts`, `script`, `bead: "N14bp1-01"`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the
exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block; `DesignId`, `ModelId`, the counts
against their expected values, memo misses and hits, the computed group with each element and, for an absent
one, its first mismatch; on failure the first differing value with the paths of the compile record and the
design file. The `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed, controls caught and `gates: []` (the gates
are N14b-04's and BD-246's).

The bead also adds the `snake-crawler` entry to the catalog script's table
(`scripts/e2e/data/archetypes.toml`, `available_from = "M3"`). The entry pins `DesignId` and `n_v` itself until
the zoo file of ZOO-snake-crawler exists, after which it reads them from the zoo (one source per value, the
rule of N14b-06).

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-270 (row `snake-crawler`): on file, Γ_mech as built is C₂ under head-to-tail reversal; this bead measures
  the term against that row and against the hand derivation (C₂ × C₂, or C₂ by the sagittal mirror) and

- Metamorphic: `set-param link_length` to the same value changes neither `DesignId` nor `ModelId`; renaming the
  head rebuilds nothing.
- Determinism: twice on one machine; Linux and macOS; debug and release.

## Considerations

- The holonomy known answer, the stage-4 row and the bracket-sign control are not here, because each needs the
  curvature analysis; a curvature result on this term before BD-243 lands is not claimed.
- Any change of the term after BD-243 has frozen its fixture hash changes that bead's fixture identity; the
  change is a dated amendment that names both beads.
- The over-constraint of nine wheel rows on a three-dimensional group (only slip-free gaits of the serpenoid
  family are consistent with all wheels) is a property of the connection, not of the compile; stage 3's
  mobility test must not refuse the chain for it (the wheel rows are contact constraints, not closures), and
  N14b-04 checks that stage 3 passes.
- OPEN (owner: Skeleton lead; decide before N14b-04 and BD-246 measure the group rows of this archetype): the
  catalog's cell "C₂ under head-to-tail reversal" against the hand derivation above, which finds the sagittal
  mirror in every left-right symmetric chain and makes the reversal depend on where actuator mass is carried.
  Options: (1) amend the cell to the computed group (C₂ × C₂, generators the sagittal mirror and the
  head-to-tail reversal, with Γ_obs = C₂ under the head IMU), keeping the natural term; the known-answer row
  KA-270, the zoo expectation of this archetype and the tables of BD-246, N13-22 and N14b-04 change with it;
  (2) keep the cell and stagger each wheel pair along its body, so that only the half-turn survives. Fail-closed
  default while open: the snake's group rows are `blocked` with this question named, never passed; the template
  term, its counts and its memo behaviour, which are what BD-243 needs, do not wait for the answer.
- Risk: beyond the open question, none but authoring; the bead is small by design (one to two days), because
  its purpose is order.
