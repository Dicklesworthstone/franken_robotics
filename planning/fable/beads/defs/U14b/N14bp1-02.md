## Background

The archetype catalog lists six variants outside its eighteen archetypes. They sit behind cargo feature flags,
are off the critical path, and each belongs to the chapter that owns its physics. One of them, the
**aerial-terrestrial hybrid** (tier Moonshot), is a legged or wheeled chassis with rotor ports: components that
produce thrust and reaction torque, added to the design as a component kind with a shaft port (torque and
angular velocity, power positive into the module) and a mechanical port (wrench and twist at a named frame).
Its physics is the actuator chapter's rotor port element (thrust, reaction torque, the air port and the hover
known answer), which exists only if the kill-gate decision KILL-SINEW-ROTOR admits it. That decision either
admits the rotor port together with this variant or withdraws both.

This bead was split from the variant bead N14b-05 so that the dependency rule holds: a bead that exists on only
one branch of a kill-gate decision depends on the decision bead, and the four other variants must not wait for
it. The decision's outcome is one of the four names of the kill-gate record (N40-03): `fired` (the rotor port and
this variant are withdrawn; N15b-23 closes this bead as `superseded`, citing the ADR), `not-fired` (the admitting
branch: this bead is released through N15b-36), `not-reached` (the pilot was never run; this bead is never
started and closes with the ADR as reason) or `deferred` (only through an ADR that names the later exit; the bead
waits for it). On every branch but `not-fired`, no template, flag or code path is written.

## Scope

In, only on the admitting branch: the template `assets/archetypes/variants/aerial-hybrid/` (design term and
manifest in the variant format of N14b-05), the cargo feature `variant-aerial` in `frob-design`, the rotor
component kind's design-side fields and port signature, the listing behaviour, and the tests below.

Out: the rotor port element and its laws (N15b-36); the decision (N15b-23); the variant mechanism, manifest
fields, computed `status`, listing rules and the other four variants (N14b-05); any flight controller or flight
claim (none is made); aerodynamics beyond what the rotor card states.

## Technical approach

1. **Branch check first.** The bead reads `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SINEW-ROTOR-M6.md` and the latest entry of the
   `decision` array of the KILL-SINEW-ROTOR row in `registries/gates.toml` (the latest entry is in force). Only
   `not-fired` continues. `fired` or `not-reached`: stop; the bead is closed with the ADR as its typed reason
   (N15b-23 does this). `deferred`: stop until the exit the ADR names.
2. **Component kind (DESIGN).** `rotor` as an actuation component: a card reference (the rotor card of N15b-36),
   a mount frame on a body port, a spin axis, a handedness (`cw` or `ccw`), and its two ports: `shaft` (τ, ω) to a
   motor and `mech` (thrust and reaction torque as a wrench at the mount frame). It is listed in the schema's
   field reference behind the feature; with the feature off the field is unknown and refused with FROB-REF-103.
   Fill, material and contact law follow the ordinary body rules (no physics default).
3. **Template.** The `quad-mammal` term with four rotors mounted on the torso through
   `mirror(xz, mirror(yz, rotor))`, handedness alternating so that the reaction torques cancel at hover; a
   wheeled variant of the same layout on the `diff-drive` chassis is a preset. Γ_mech as built is C₂ × C₂ only
   if the mirror flips handedness (a mirror maps a `cw` rotor to `ccw`), exactly as the mecanum template's mirror
   flips the roller angle; the template's `symmetry` assertion states it and the compiler must agree.
4. **Manifest.** The variant fields of N14b-05: `flag = "variant-aerial"`, `tier = "moonshot"`, `physics_owner =
   "actuator chapter (rotor port)"`, `physics_bead = "N15b-36"`, and the computed `status` (`template-only`,
   `compiles` or `simulates`).
5. **Listing.** As every variant: omitted from `frob design templates --json` unless the feature is compiled in
   and `--include-variants` is passed; never returned in `next_actions` (Moonshot).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On `fired` or `not-reached`: the bead is closed citing `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SINEW-ROTOR-M6.md`, and
      `variant-aerial` does not exist in the workspace (a build with `--features variant-aerial` fails as an
      unknown feature).
- [ ] On `not-fired`: the template parses, canonicalizes and compiles with the feature on; its computed `status`
      equals what the preflight stages report.
- [ ] A default build lists exactly eighteen archetypes and refuses the `rotor` field with FROB-REF-103.
- [ ] With the feature on and `--include-variants` the variant is listed with tier `moonshot`, owner and status.
- [ ] The computed Γ_mech is C₂ × C₂ with alternating handedness, and a template whose mirror does not flip
      handedness reduces the group (the reaction torques no longer cancel) and is named as the breaker.
- [ ] No verb returns the variant in `next_actions`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `aerial_absent_by_default`: default build; the field `rotor` → FROB-REF-103; eighteen templates listed.
- `aerial_listed_with_flag_and_option`: feature on and `--include-variants` → listed with tier and status.
- `aerial_mirror_flips_handedness`: the nested mirrors yield two `cw` and two `ccw` rotors on the diagonals.
- `aerial_hover_torque_cancels`: at equal thrust per rotor, the summed reaction torque about the vertical axis is
  zero exactly in rationals; the non-flipping mutant gives four times one rotor's reaction torque.
- `aerial_status_computed`: a hand-written `status: simulates` in the manifest is rejected.
- `aerial_never_suggested`: a refusal whose repairs would include this variant omits it.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_variants.sh` (N14b-05) gains the aerial rows on this branch, as a stage that runs only in a
build with `variant-aerial` (the script is N14b-05's, which this bead depends on): build `frob` with
`--features variant-aerial`, `frob design templates --include-variants --json`, `frob design new a --template
aerial-hybrid --key aer-1 --json`, `frob design check a --json` and `frob analyze symmetry a --json`. Asserts the
listing rule, the computed status and the group. Negative-control step (`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-103 3`):
the default build must refuse `frob design new rotor-probe --file fixtures/skel/aerial-hybrid.fbot --key
aer-neg-1 --json`. The log is the script's (`target/e2e-logs/skel_variants/<utc>.ndjson`, STD-E2E helper): each
line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead: "N14bp1-02"`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time, the
envelope's `evidence` block, the feature set, the variants listed, stage statuses, the computed status and group,
the summed hover reaction torque (0 N·m, exact), and on failure the first mismatch with the template path and the
build features. Summary: assertions passed and failed, variant rows checked; `gates: []`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Hover reaction-torque balance: zero with alternating handedness; four times one rotor's torque with the
  non-flipping mirror (the planted defect).
- Control: a build with `variant-aerial` on by default (the eighteen-count test fails).

## Considerations

- Moonshot, priority backlog; nothing on the critical path depends on it.
- KILL-SINEW-ROTOR has a second reading once the element is built (N15b-23): if the hover known answer or the
  port balance fails on the referee in production code, a second `decision` entry `fired` is appended with its
  own ADR, and the template and the feature are removed with a note, not left as dead data, as for every
  variant whose physics is withdrawn.
- No flight or hover claim is made from the template; the hover known answer above is a static torque balance
  of the design term, not a simulation result. The no-authorization flags apply to any export.
