## Background

`frob-io` imports URDF and MJCF at M1. OpenUSD import is the plan's Frontier importer: only the format's core
specification is settled, and the schemas that describe robots (rigid bodies, joints, drives, articulations) are
still vendor extensions (FACT, source register). An importer written against a moving schema can map today's
files and silently lose tomorrow's fields. The plan's row: ceiling of the benefit, stated before any
measurement: access to assets published only in that format. Pilot: import one published robot asset by
hand-mapping its schema (N14b-23). Falsifier: a mechanical field with no mapping. Kill rule: not built until a
consumer exists.

This bead was split from the deferred-importer bead N14b-22, which now holds SDF only: SDF is Solid and gated by
a consumer alone, while OpenUSD is gated by its pilot as well, and one bead holding both made SDF wait for the
OpenUSD pilot. Nothing of the plan's OpenUSD row is lost: the preconditions, the mapping scope, the reader route
and the rules shared with every importer are here.

## Scope

In: the two preconditions and their check; the choice and admission of the reading route; the robot mapping
(articulation, rigid bodies, joints, drives, collision shapes); the loss report, coverage diff, limits and fuzz
target for this format; `design.import --format usd`.

Out: the pilot (N14b-23, a dependency); scene content inside an OpenUSD file, which goes through the scene
subsystem's import (BD-398 and its beads) with its own loss report; any writer for the format; SDF (N14b-22);
the loss-report schema and comparator (N14b-20); mass-dropping refusal and reduced models (N14b-34).

## Technical approach

**Preconditions (checked first, recorded in the bead).** (1) The pilot's record exists and its falsifier did not
hold: every mechanical field of the piloted asset had a design field to land in. If the pilot's second outcome
was reported (values that depend on composition features a flat mapping cannot resolve), the importer's scope is
"flattened assets only" and the verb refuses an unflattened asset with FROB-REF-122 under `--strict` and lists
the composition arcs as `unsupported` otherwise. (2) A consumer is recorded: a named reference robot, user asset
or benchmark that exists in OpenUSD and in no supported format, with its licence, also committed as
`docs/import/usd-consumer.toml` with the fields of N14b-22's SDF consumer record. Until both hold the bead is not
started. `usd` is a value of `design.import`'s `format` from M1 (VERBS-design; BD-065's format dispatch): until
this importer is linked, `frob design import <file> --format usd` answers one envelope `FROB-REF-724` (`reason:
feature-not-built`, `mechanism: import.usd`, exit 5, `retry: never`), before any byte of the document is read and
with no design created, the message naming this bead's two conditions (N14b-23's pilot record and the consumer
record), exactly as `sdf` does before N14b-22 lands; never 802. The agent guide's entry for `design.import` names
the same conditions. When this bead lands, the dispatch sends the value to this importer with no change to the
input schema, and `design.import`'s block names this bead under "Extended by" for the OpenUSD importer behind
`--format usd`. The pilot covers one
vendor extension at one version (recorded in its result): prims of any other extension or version are
`unsupported` in the loss report (FROB-REF-122 under `--strict`) until a pilot record covers them (DESIGN, so
that the importer never claims a schema nobody mapped).

**Reader route (decided at the time, recorded as an ADR).** A reader for the text and binary layers is not
written in the core. Two routes: (a) a Franken sibling that reads the format, consumed through the airlock with
an admission row at its pin (admission is never inherited); (b) a conversion done outside by the user to a
supported format, with the tool and its version recorded in the source block of the loss report, as for macro
expansion (`expanded_by`). No third-party parser enters the core under either route.

**Mapping.** Rigid bodies: mass, centre of mass, inertia with principal axes (rotated into the body frame once, at
this boundary) to `mass_properties` with `fill: declared` and provenance `datasheet`. Joints: revolute,
prismatic, fixed and spherical to classes `R`, `P`, `fixed`, `S`, with axes, both local frames composed into
`parent_from_joint`, and limits. Drives: as actuator-limit overrides (maximum force to the card's peak, recorded
`default-filled` when absent and filled from a card marked `assumed`). Collision shapes to collision proxies, by
content hash for meshes. The articulation root becomes the design root, with its fixed or floating base. Units
and up-axis metadata are applied once at this boundary (metres-per-unit and the up axis converted to the
project's z-up). Vendor schema extensions, contact and material parameters are `reported` (class
`no-exact-equivalent` for contact, labelled parity-only); mimic or tendon couplings map to a `coupling`
transmission where the pilot's table says so, otherwise they are `unsupported`.

**Shared importer rules.** The loss report `frob.lossreport/1` with `coverage.unaccounted` zero (N14b-20's
helper); `--strict` refuses with FROB-REF-122; an inertia that fails consistency is listed with a proposed repair
and not changed; a body of nonzero mass with no mapping and no declared reduction is refused (N14b-34); imported
strings only in `x-frob-untrusted` fields; limits (a new profile row in the import-limit registry of N37-05,
refused with FROB-REF-832, exit 3) and a fuzz target registered with BD-307; file references that resolve outside
the import root refused with FROB-REF-734 (exit 5) as for URDF.

**The oracle.** The consumer exists only in OpenUSD, so the pinned incumbent of the URDF and MJCF captures cannot
read it unless it does at its pin. Where the same robot exists in URDF or MJCF the comparison is with that import
through N14b-20's comparator; otherwise the capture is taken through the reading route's own tool, pinned, and
added to the capture scripts of BD-067 under their no-regeneration rule, then compared with `fk-parity` (N14b-24).
OPEN (owner: Skeleton lead; fail-closed default): with neither, the parity row is logged `not run`, never passed,
and the importer is not declared done.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Before any work: the pilot record shows the falsifier did not hold, and a consumer with its licence is
      recorded here and in `docs/import/usd-consumer.toml`; once the importer is linked, `--format usd` is
      dispatched to it and no longer answers `FROB-REF-724`, and the generated `design.import` input schema is
      byte-identical to the one before.
- [ ] The reader route's ADR exists in `docs/adr/` (the program's one directory for decision records); under
      route (a) an admission row at the pin exists; under route (b) the
      conversion tool and version appear in every loss report.
- [ ] On the consumer's asset: forward-kinematics parity within 10⁻⁹ m and 10⁻⁹ rad over 10⁴ configurations
      against the oracle (or against the URDF or MJCF import of the same robot through N14b-20's comparator when
      one exists), mass properties equal, and the field-coverage diff empty.
- [ ] Every vendor extension prim or attribute of the asset is `mapped`, `reported` or `refused`; nothing is
      silently dropped.
- [ ] The importer has a fuzz target and limits, and an entry in the format documentation.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In `crates/frob-io/tests/usd.rs` (`cargo test -p frob-io usd`), on the consumer's asset and on synthetic
fixtures labelled `synthetic`:
- `usd_preconditions_recorded`: the pilot's row in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` has a result
  with verdict `falsifier-rejected`, and `docs/import/usd-consumer.toml` parses with a commit of 40 hex digits,
  an admitted SPDX identifier and a matching licence-text hash; a row with verdict `falsifier-holds` fails.
- `usd_uncovered_extension_unsupported`: a prim of a vendor extension (or version) that the pilot's result does
  not name → `unsupported` with its prim path; under `--strict` FROB-REF-122.
- `usd_up_axis_converted_once`: a y-up asset with metres-per-unit 0.01 maps a link at (0, 100, 0) units to
  (0, 0, 1) m in the project frame; converting twice is caught by the parity test.
- `usd_inertia_principal_axes`: principal moments (1, 2, 3) kg·m² with a 90° principal rotation about z (the
  quaternion (√½, 0, 0, √½) written in decimals) map to the body-frame inertia diag(2, 1, 3) within 10⁻¹² kg·m²
  per entry; the mutant that ignores the principal axes gives diag(1, 2, 3) and fails.
- `usd_joint_types`: four types to their classes; an unsupported joint type is `unsupported` with its prim path.
- `usd_drive_as_limit_override`: a drive with maximum force 40 N·m sets the actuator peak; a drive without one is
  `default-filled` from an `assumed` card and listed.
- `usd_composition_arc_reported`: a variant set on a mechanical attribute is `unsupported` (non-strict) or 122
  (strict) when the scope is "flattened assets only".
- `usd_untrusted_text_marked`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/skel_import_usd.sh` (written with the importer; sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/skel_import_usd/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript): `frob design import <asset> --format usd
--name usd-robot --key usd-1 --json`, `frob artifact read <loss-report digest> --json`, `frob design check
usd-robot --stages 1-2 --json`, `cargo run -p frob-conformance --bin fk-parity -- <DesignId> <capture> <identifier
map>` against the consumer's oracle capture (or the comparator against the URDF/MJCF import of the same robot),
and `frob design import <asset> --format usd --strict --name usd-strict --key usd-2 --json` (a distinct key).
Each line: `ts`, `script`, `bead: "N14bp1-03"`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time, the
envelope's `evidence` block, the source hash, the reader route and its pin or conversion tool, coverage numbers,
class counts, maximum position and rotation differences with their thresholds (10⁻⁹ m, 10⁻⁹ rad), and on failure
the worst configuration and link with the capture path. `summary`: assertions passed and failed, `gates: []`.
Declared plant (`E2E_PLANT_FAILURE`): `up-axis-twice`, which the parity step must catch.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-290's rule applied to this format: parity within 10⁻⁹ m and 10⁻⁹ rad over 10⁴ configurations and a loss
  report that lists every dropped field found by the coverage diff.
- Negative controls: an importer that applies the up-axis conversion twice (parity fails); one that drops a
  vendor extension attribute without an entry (the coverage diff fails); one that reads a variant's default
  without reporting the arc (the composition test fails).
- Metamorphic: reordering sibling prims changes neither the design meaning nor the entry set.

## Considerations

- Priority is backlog; no milestone depends on this bead.
- The pilot describes one vendor extension at one version. A consumer asset using a different extension, or a
  later version, needs its own pilot record before this bead may claim it.
- OpenUSD assets often bundle scene content. A robot import takes the articulation and reports the rest.
- Licence: assets available only in this format are often under restrictive terms; the consumer record includes
  the licence, and nothing restrictive is vendored.
