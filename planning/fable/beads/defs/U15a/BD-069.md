## Background

A card is immutable, content-addressed data about a real or generic part. ADR-SINEW-01 makes the card the unit of
actuator knowledge: it is immutable and content-addressed, every field carries a provenance and an uncertainty, and
no actuator constant lives in code. Reason: three later mechanisms all need to know where a number came from.
Randomization draws each parameter from the uncertainty stated on its card and there are no free-floating ranges;
`--strict-data` refuses a verdict that rests on an assumed field; calibration against measurement writes a new card
revision in which identified fields become `measured`. Rejected: constants in code or in an untyped parameter file
(no provenance, so none of the three can work); a single provenance for the whole card (a datasheet rarely gives
friction or thermal capacity, so the honest card is mixed).

The card format is `FMT-FCARD`: extension `.fcard`, schema `frob.card/1`, owner crate `frob-actuate`, canonical (the
BLAKE3 hash of its canonical bytes is the artifact's identity). Its kinds are a closed list in
`registries/formats.toml`: actuator, transmission, battery, interface, material, soil, optical, sensor, payload, and
`absorber` (the safety shield's absorber card, whose fields and validation N22bx-01 adds at M4). This bead
implements the kind-agnostic container and the three Sinew kinds; other subsystems add their kinds to the same
reader, because a second parser for a registered extension fails `check-contracts`.

The starter catalog contains generic classes only (small, medium and large quasi-direct-drive; hobby servo;
strain-wave; hub motor), every field marked `generic`. Real parts enter only with a cited datasheet (OPEN-SINEW-1).

## Scope

In: the schema and its Rust types; provenance and uncertainty per field; canonical bytes and the card identity
(INV-SINEW-06); overrides; the field groups of the three Sinew kinds; the three admission checks; the generic
catalog under `assets/cards/`; the verbs `actuator.list`, `actuator.show`, `actuator.check`.

Out: conversion between current conventions (N15a-02, used here); the checks when a card is bound to a joint and
`--strict-data` at design level (N15a-03); the thermal closed forms used by check 3 (N15a-14) and the envelope
closed forms (N15a-16), which this bead calls (both are pure libraries over plain parameter structs, finished
before this bead; the adapters that fill those structs from a card, `ThermalView::from_card` and
`ElectricalView::from_card`, are written here); randomization draws (seed BD-200); card revisions from calibration
(seed BD-391); the format-registry row and round trip (seed BD-141).

## Technical approach

1. **File shape (DESIGN; canonical JSON, quantities as strings with units, exactly as `.fbot` writes them).**
   ```json
   { "schema": "frob.card/1", "kind": "actuator", "name": "qdd-m", "class": "generic", "revision": 1,
     "fields": {
       "current_convention": {"value": "q-axis", "unc": {"kind": "exact"}, "prov": {"class": "generic"}},
       "kt": {"value": "0.105 N·m/A", "unc": {"kind": "rel", "half_width": "0.10"},
              "prov": {"class": "generic", "note": "class value, not a part"}},
       "r_phase": {"value": "0.20 Ω", "at": {"t_ref": "25 degC"}, "unc": {"kind": "rel", "half_width": "0.05"},
                   "prov": {"class": "datasheet", "cite": {"title": "…", "rev": "…", "locator": "table 3",
                            "source": "b3:… or URL"}}} },
     "licence": "…" }
   ```
2. **Provenance, per field.** `datasheet` (requires `cite` with title, revision or date, locator and a source hash
   or URL); `measured` (requires the dataset hash and calibration cell; written only by the calibration subsystem);
   `generic` (a class value, not a part); `assumed` (a guess, with a mandatory `note`). A field with no `prov` is
   refused.
3. **Uncertainty, per field.** `exact` (enumerations, integers, a ratio fixed by tooth counts), `abs` or `rel`
   half-width, `interval [lo, hi]`, or `normal` with σ. A field with no `unc` is refused. A physical quantity with
   `exact` is accepted but cannot be randomized (the randomization bead refuses a field with no interval).
4. **Identity (INV-SINEW-06).** `CardId` = BLAKE3 over the canonical bytes (sorted keys, normalized quantity
   strings, no insignificant whitespace). The hash covers every field, its provenance and its uncertainty. A card is
   referenced as `name.fcard@b3:<hash>`. An override (a field value overridden where a design binds the card to a
   joint, N15a-03, or by a parameter row such as an experiment axis) never mutates a card: it produces a new card
   value with provenance `assumed` unless one is given, and a new hash, which changes the `ModelId` of every model
   bound to it. The card verbs take no override option; `actuator.check` on `design:joint` (N15a-03) checks the
   card value after the design's overrides.
5. **Actuator card fields.** Base list: torque constant `kt`, back-EMF constant `ke`, winding resistance and
   inductance, rotor inertia `j_rotor`, gear ratio and efficiency map, Coulomb, viscous and Stribeck friction, peak
   and continuous torque, speed limit, bus voltage and current limits, thermal resistances and capacitances, mass,
   envelope geometry, encoder resolution, driver control rate and latency. Fields by group:

   | Group | Fields | Why the field exists |
   |---|---|---|
   | Convention | `current_convention`: `dc`, `phase-peak`, `phase-rms` or `q-axis`; `pole_pairs`; `modulation`: `dc`, `svm-linear` or `six-step` | ADR-SINEW-05. Required; a card without it refuses (FROB-REF-134) |
   | Electrical | `r_phase` at `t_ref`; `alpha_r` (temperature coefficient of resistance); `l_d`, `l_q`; `flux_linkage`; `alpha_flux` (temperature coefficient of magnet flux); `iron_loss` coefficients | Temperature-dependent copper loss; the voltage limit and field weakening |
   | Friction | `coulomb`, `breakaway`, `stribeck_velocity`, `stribeck_shape`: `lorentzian` or `gaussian`; `viscous`; `load_coefficient` per quadrant; `side`: motor or output; `stiction_eps` | The stiction row and its bound |
   | Transmission | signed `gear_ratio`; `efficiency_forward`, `efficiency_back` or a loss law; `backlash`; `tooth_stiffness`; `restitution` | Backlash rows; four-quadrant loss |
   | Thermal | `c_winding`, `c_housing`, `g_winding_housing`, `g_housing_ambient`; `t_max_winding`, `t_max_magnet`; derating bands | The two-node network and the runaway condition |
   | Driver | `control_rate`, `latency`, `regen_policy`: `to-battery`, `shunt` or `clip`; current-loop bandwidth | Braking modes; the clock contract |
   | Sensing | `encoder_bits`, `encoder_side`: motor or output | With backlash the two sides read different angles |
   | Cost | `unit_cost` with currency | The design cost budget |
   Fields that other beads read, admitted by this schema under the same provenance and uncertainty
   rules (an unknown field is a form error, so each name below is known to the schema from M1; the group is
   optional unless stated, and the bead named gives its meaning):

   | Field or group | Card kind | Read by | Rule |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `regen_policy` | actuator | N15b-04 | Required for an actuator on a bus that can receive regeneration; a missing value refuses at binding |
   | `iron_loss` coefficients | actuator | BD-071, BD-072, N15b-27 | Optional; absent means zero loss with the flag `iron loss not modelled` |
   | `tooth_dissipation_time` | actuator, transmission | N15a-09, BD-155 | Required with `backlash` > 0 on a convex tier, together with `tooth_stiffness` |
   | `series_stiffness`, `series_damping`, `deflection_limit` | transmission | N15b-18 | Frontier; the option is admitted only for `measured` or cited `datasheet` stiffness |
   | `rotor` group (thrust coefficient, disc area, radius, reaction-torque coefficient) | actuator | N15b-36 | Moonshot; read only with the rotor-port option |
   | `ageing` group (coefficients of the fade and growth law, with their validity domain) | battery | N15b-17, N15b-14 | Frontier; without it the states f_Q, f_R are scenario constants |
   | authored direction of each port pair | actuator, transmission | N15b-12 | Required; the sign is explicit in the canonical form |
   | `stiction_eps`, `stribeck_shape` defaults | actuator | N15a-08, N15b-26 | Defaults ε = 10⁻³ rad/s and `lorentzian`, changed only by the decision OPEN-SINEW-2 |
   | `brake` group: `holding_torque` with its `side` (motor or output); `engagement_delay` (command to full hold); `engage_speed` (the rate below which the brake may engage); `dynamic_rated` (boolean: may engage at any speed); `unpowered`: `engaged` (spring-applied) or `released` | actuator | N14bp2-02, BD-365, N22b-11, N22b-24, N22bx-02 | Optional; absent means the actuator has no brake. Present, every member is written; `holding_torque` > 0 and `engagement_delay`, `engage_speed` ≥ 0 join the sign rules of check 2. DESIGN, reason: the plan's "brakes where the card has them", the brake summand of the cutoff delay, the holding torque a slope needs and the idle laws' "zero command with the brakes as carded" each read one member, and no other card defines them |
6. **Battery and transmission cards.** Battery: open-circuit voltage against state of charge (a monotone table),
   capacity `q`, `r0` with its temperature dependence, `r1`, `c1`, `r2`, `c2`, charge-current limit,
   end-of-discharge voltage, cold-temperature resistance, optional ageing coefficients, mass, cost. Transmission
   (standalone gear, belt, screw, linkage, cable, coupling): the Transmission group above, plus `coupling_matrix`
   for coupled drives and `lead_angle`, `friction_angle` for screws, each port labelled with its authored direction.
   The models that read them belong to seeds BD-075 and BD-074; the schema and validation are here.
7. **`actuator.check`: three checks, at admission and again at binding.**
   1. *Constants agree.* Convert `kt` and `ke` to the power-invariant modulus k (N15a-02). Pass iff the two
      intervals overlap: |k_from_kt − k_from_ke| ≤ u(kt) + u(ke) with the half-widths propagated. Otherwise
      FROB-REF-130, returning the quadrant and the power created: for k_from_kt > k_from_ke the motoring quadrant
      creates (k_from_kt − k_from_ke)·i·ω watts, evaluated at continuous current and half the no-load speed.
   2. *Every loss term is nonnegative in all four quadrants* (quadrant = signs of shaft torque and speed). Checked
      exactly on the card's decimal strings read as rationals: `r_phase` > 0 and 1 + `alpha_r`·(T − `t_ref`) > 0
      over [ambient floor, `t_max_winding`]; `viscous`, `coulomb`, `breakaway`, each `load_coefficient`, iron-loss
      coefficients and tooth damping ≥ 0; 0 < `efficiency_forward` ≤ 1; `efficiency_back` ≤ 1 (a value ≤ 0 is legal
      and means self-locking); `restitution` in [0, 1]; conductances and capacitances > 0. A loss law of kind
      `single-multiplier` (one η for all quadrants, τ_joint = η·N·τ_motor both ways) is read as the pair
      (η_f, η_b) = (η, 1/η) and so fails η_b ≤ 1 for any η < 1. Otherwise FROB-REF-130 with the quadrant and the
      power created, reported both as a fraction of the transmitted power (η_b − 1 when back-driven) and in watts
      at the evaluation point: `actuator.check --at τ,ω` (joint side) when given, else the card's continuous
      torque at half its no-load speed. The transmission rejection fixture (η = 0.8, N = 10, `--at 10,1`)
      therefore reports back-driven, 0.25 of transmitted power, 2.5 W (KA-279, KA-320).
   3. *Stated ratings are reachable inside the card's own limits.* Peak torque needs a current ≤ the current limit
      and a stall voltage R·I ≤ the modulation limit (N15a-16 at `t_ref`); continuous torque needs a stable thermal
      equilibrium at or below `t_max_winding` under the temperature-dependent law (N15a-14). A rating is a torque,
      so the continuous check holds torque, not current: the cold-equivalent current is I₀ = τ_cont/(η_f·N·k₀) for a
      rating stated at the output (τ_cont/k₀ for one stated at the motor shaft), with k₀ the power-invariant modulus
      at `t_ref`, and when the card gives `alpha_flux` ≠ 0 the loss curve is the torque-hold curve
      P(T) = I₀²·R₀·[1 + α·(T − T₀)]/[1 + α_B·(T − T₀)]² (N15a-14 `Hold::Torque`), whose equilibrium can vanish at a
      tangency below the constant-current threshold; with `alpha_flux` = 0 it reduces to the constant-current law.
      DESIGN, reason: the plan's continuous rating under torque control is the torque-hold value (15.9 A
      cold-equivalent on the worked card, against 18.6 A at constant current), and checking a torque rating at
      constant current would admit a rating that runs away. Failure refuses with FROB-REF-131 (peak outside the
      card's own envelope) or FROB-REF-132 (continuous rating with no admissible equilibrium, or one above
      `t_max_winding`), with the field named in `refusal.pointer` and the largest admissible value in
      `refusal.required`.
8. **Refusals raised here.** FROB-REF-130: a card or transmission that is not passive (loss negative in some
   quadrant, efficiency above one, or constants inconsistent beyond the stated uncertainty); returned with the
   quadrant and the power created; repairs ranked as N15-REF's row: replace a single efficiency by the pair
   `efficiency_forward`, `efficiency_back` (each at most one) or by a loss law; reconcile `kt` and `ke` (a mismatch
   by √(3/2), √2 or √3 names the missing current convention); correct the sign of the loss term named.
   FROB-REF-134: a card without a current convention, or with conventions that disagree; returned with the fields
   in conflict; repairs: state `current_convention` (the three candidate values of k are printed); convert the
   quoted constant with the factor for the stated convention.
   A field without provenance or uncertainty, an unknown field or a unit error is a form error (FROB-REF-103,
   FROB-REF-102). All are exit code 3 with `retry: after-change`.
9. **Verbs (kind `read`, M1).** `actuator.list [--kind K] [--class C]`: name, kind, class, `CardId`, share of
   fields per provenance class; paginated. `actuator.show <card>`: every field with provenance and uncertainty, the
   derived power-invariant modulus, the modulation limit rule, flags. `actuator.check <card> [--strict-data]
   [--at τ,ω]`: the three checks (`--at` sets the evaluation point of the power-created figure); exit 0 clean,
   2 with findings, 3 refused. Under `--strict-data` a field that one of the three checks reads with provenance
   `assumed` refuses with FROB-REF-113 (the card, the field and its provenance, N14-REF's row) instead of
   passing with the field listed. Envelope `evidence.class` is `Estimated` unless every
   field the check reads has provenance `measured` (card values are `Estimated` unless calibrated; a `datasheet`
   field is still `Estimated`), in which case `Tested`; `evidence.weakest` names the field that set the class;
   `determinism` is D0 for check 2 (exact rationals) and D2 for checks 1 and 3.
10. **Catalog.** `assets/cards/actuator/{qdd-s,qdd-m,qdd-l,hobby-servo,strain-wave,hub-motor}.fcard`, plus one
    generic battery and one generic gear, belt and screw card. Every field `generic`, with a `note` deriving the
    value; internal consistency is required (k from `kt` equals k from `ke`; ratings inside limits). The
    `hub-motor` card carries a generic `brake` group (`unpowered: engaged`), so that a wheeled design built from
    the catalog can hold on a slope; the other actuator cards carry none (DESIGN).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A card with one field lacking `prov` is refused, and so is one lacking `unc` (the seed's first condition).
- [ ] Every card in the catalog passes `actuator.check` (the seed's second condition and a row of GATE-SINEW-08).
- [ ] Two byte-different files with the same canonical content have the same `CardId`; changing only a provenance
      class or only an uncertainty changes it (INV-SINEW-06); an override yields a new hash and never edits a file.
- [ ] A card with no `current_convention` refuses with FROB-REF-134; a card whose `kt` and `ke` disagree beyond
      their stated uncertainty refuses with FROB-REF-130 and prints the watts created.
- [ ] `efficiency_forward` = 1.2 refuses with FROB-REF-130; `efficiency_back` = −0.1 is admitted as self-locking.
- [ ] Check 3 holds torque: on the worked thermal card with `alpha_flux` = −0.0011 K⁻¹ a continuous rating of
      16 A cold-equivalent refuses with FROB-REF-132 and 15.5 A passes; with `alpha_flux` = 0, 18 A passes and 20 A
      refuses; `evidence.class` is `Estimated` for every catalog card.
- [ ] The three verbs are reachable through `frob --json`, with schemas in the verb registry and the refusal list.
- [ ] `frob-actuate/CONTRACT.md` documents the format; the reader is the only parser of `.fcard` in the workspace.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `card_roundtrip_canonical`: parse, canonicalize, re-emit; bytes are a fixed point; key order and whitespace of the
  input do not change the hash.
- `hash_covers_provenance_and_uncertainty` (INV-SINEW-06, property test over 10³ generated cards): mutating any one
  of value, unit, `unc`, `prov.class`, `prov.cite` changes the hash; mutating nothing does not.
- `missing_provenance_refused`, `missing_uncertainty_refused`, `datasheet_without_cite_refused`,
  `assumed_without_note_refused`: FROB-REF-103 with the field path in `pointer`.
- `kt_ke_consistency`: `q-axis` card with kt = 0.100 N·m/A ± 2% and ke = 0.100 V·s/rad ± 2% passes; ke = 0.110
  refuses with FROB-REF-130 and reports (0.110 − 0.100)·i·ω.
- `loss_sign_exact`: `viscous` = "-0.001 N·m·s/rad" refuses; "0" passes; the comparison is done on rationals, so
  "1e-30" passes and "-1e-30" refuses.
- `single_multiplier_refused`: a gear card with `loss_law: {kind: single-multiplier, eta: 0.8}`, N = 10, checked
  `--at 10,1`, refuses with FROB-REF-130, quadrant back-driven, fraction 0.25, 2.5 W; the same card written as the
  pair (0.8, 1.25) gives the same refusal; the pair (0.8, 0.8) passes.
- `ratings_reachable`: a card stating a continuous torque whose current is 20 A on the worked thermal card (R₀ =
  0.20 Ω, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹, G = 0.80 W/K, limit 155 °C, ambient 25 °C, `alpha_flux` = 0) refuses with
  FROB-REF-132, since the equilibrium is 190 °C; at 18 A it passes (the largest admissible current is 18.55 A,
  reported in `refusal.required` for the 20 A card).
- `ratings_reachable_torque_hold`: the same card with `alpha_flux` = −0.0011 K⁻¹. A continuous rating whose
  cold-equivalent current is 18 A refuses with FROB-REF-132 and "no stable equilibrium" (above the 16.51 A
  tangency); 16 A refuses with an equilibrium above 155 °C (the limit is met at 15.90 A); 15.5 A passes;
  `refusal.required` reports 15.90 A cold-equivalent converted to torque.
- `peak_rating_reachable`: a card whose peak torque needs I > `i_max`, or whose stall voltage R·I exceeds V_lim,
  refuses with FROB-REF-131 naming the field; the catalog cards pass.
- `override_new_hash`: overriding `kt` returns a new `CardId` and leaves the catalog file's bytes unchanged.
- `strict_data_assumed_field`: `qdd-m` with `r_phase` relabelled `assumed` (with a note) passes `actuator.check`
  with the field listed, and refuses with FROB-REF-113, pointer `fields.r_phase`, under `--strict-data`; the
  catalog card unchanged passes under `--strict-data` (its fields are `generic`).
- `brake_group_rules`: `qdd-m` without `brake` loads and `actuator.show` reports `brake: none`; the same card with
  `brake` {`holding_torque` "2.0 N·m", `side` output, `engagement_delay` "0.005 s", `engage_speed` "0.2 rad/s",
  `dynamic_rated` false, `unpowered` engaged} loads with a different `CardId`; `holding_torque` "-1 N·m" refuses
  with FROB-REF-130, pointer `fields.brake.holding_torque`; a `brake` group without `engagement_delay` refuses with
  FROB-REF-103; the catalog's `hub-motor` card has the group and passes.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_cards.sh` (bead N15a-25) drives `frob actuator list|show|check --json` over the catalog and the
rejection fixtures. Per the shared logging contract it writes newline-delimited JSON and a transcript under
`target/e2e-logs/sinew_cards/`, one line per command with the exit code, the envelope's evidence block, the
`CardId`, and for each refusal the code, pointer, observed and required values.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-294 (every card in the catalog passes `actuator.check`; the single-multiplier gear fixture must refuse) and
KA-279 (rejection fixture, transmission: reverse drive that produces free electrical energy, refused with
FROB-REF-130). Negative controls: a card with provenance stripped; `kt` in `phase-rms` relabelled `q-axis` (a factor
√3 = 1.732, caught by check 1); a catalog card with one loss sign flipped; a second `.fcard` parser planted in
another crate (caught by `check-contracts`); check 3 mutated to hold current instead of torque (it admits the
18 A torque-hold card, and `ratings_reachable_torque_hold` must fail); a `datasheet`-only card reported `Tested`
(the evidence-class assertion must fail). Determinism: check 2 is D0 and is run twice and on both platforms with
identical output bytes.

## Considerations

- **Refusal code for missing provenance.** The plan states that a card without provenance is refused and names no
  code for it. FROB-REF-103 (form error) is used as the fail-closed default; codes 137 to 139 are unallocated in
  Sinew's range and one may be assigned by the owner without changing behaviour. The same applies to check 3, for
  which the plan names no code and this bead uses 131 and 132.
- **OPEN-SINEW-1** (which real actuator parts ship, under what data licence; owner: project lead; blocks nothing
  before M4). Default: generic classes only. Until decided, every result that depends on a card stays `Estimated`,
  and realism and Reality claims are refused.
- **Back-EMF convention.** The plan requires a current convention and does not name a separate one for `ke`. DESIGN
  here: `ke` is stated in the voltage quantity dual to the current convention (per-phase peak, per-phase RMS or
  q-axis volts per rad/s); a line-to-line datasheet figure is converted by the author (÷ √3) and the conversion is
  recorded in the provenance note.
- RISK-32: provenance on every field and the share of `generic` and `assumed` fields printed by `actuator.list` are
  the early signals of common-mode card error; they are not a defence against it.
- Privacy and licence: a card carries a licence field; a cited datasheet is referenced, never embedded.
