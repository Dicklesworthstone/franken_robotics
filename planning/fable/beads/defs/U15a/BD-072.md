## Background

Copper resistance rises with temperature, R(T) = R₀·[1 + α·(T − T₀)], and magnet flux falls,
k(T) = k₀·[1 + α_B·(T − T₀)] with α_B negative. Together they turn a winding into a system with positive thermal
feedback: a current that looks sustainable under a constant-resistance model can have an equilibrium far above the
winding limit, or none at all. On the worked card (R₀ = 0.20 Ω at 25 °C, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹, G = 0.80 W/K,
C = 120 J/K, ambient 25 °C, limit 155 °C; illustrative, none measured) a design sized to hold 20 A passes the
constant-resistance check (125 °C) and is refused by the corrected one (190 °C). ADR-SINEW-04: winding resistance
and magnet flux depend on temperature from L2 upward, and the static thermal check uses the temperature-dependent
law at every level.

Three mechanisms follow from the analysis, and this bead delivers the first two (the third is N15a-15):
1. **The static check uses the corrected law.** Analysis A-11 and stage 6 of the design preflight compute the
   equilibrium, or its absence, for each required operating point and for the root-mean-square current of the
   nominal gait, and refuse with FROB-REF-132.
2. **The continuous rating and the runaway threshold are close together under torque control** (15.9 A and 16.5 A
   cold-equivalent on the card, four percent apart), so `actuator.thermal` reports the margin to the tangency, not
   only the equilibrium temperature.
3. **Episodes are too short to see any of this**, so thermal state starts from a declared duty history.

FACT: FrankenSim's winding model derives resistance from material and geometry and infers no temperature law
(`frankensim/crates/fs-em/src/winding.rs`), so the temperature dependence is new in `frob-actuate` on every tier.
FACT: FrankenSim's controller derates torque on authored bands (`frankensim/crates/fs-control/src/thermal.rs`);
Sinew follows that practice.

## Scope

In: R(T) and k(T) inside the L2 dynamic model on the referee, supplied to the one accessor through which
the L2 component of BD-071 reads resistance and flux; torque derating bands and the latched trip, driving
the torque-scale input and latch bit that BD-071 exposes; the verb `actuator.thermal` with its schema;
refusal FROB-REF-132 with what it returns; the measurement of GATE-SINEW-05 (stepped rows on the L2 model,
static rows from the verb). Out, with the bead that has each: the closed forms and solves (N15a-14, called
here); the L2 current dynamics, the thermal network stepping, the iron-loss term and its flag, and the
constant-resistance rows of GATE-SINEW-01 (seed BD-071, which this bead extends with the two temperature
laws); the thermal node element and the second-law test (N15a-06); duty-history initialization (N15a-15);
the A-11 analysis and its place in `design.check` (seeds BD-103, BD-110); the slow thermal clock of the
batched tiers (N15a-07); the decision on the iron-loss default (N15b-27).

## Technical approach

1. **Temperature laws in the L2 model.** At each referee step the winding element (N15a-05) uses
   R_w = R₀·[1 + α·(T_w − T₀)] at the midpoint winding temperature of the coupled step, and the gyrator modulus
   uses k = k₀·[1 + α_B·(T_m − T₀)], T_m being the magnet temperature (the housing node unless the card provides a
   rotor node). Copper heat i²·R_w(T_w) goes to the winding node (N15a-06). `alpha_r`, `alpha_flux`, `t_ref` are
   card fields; a card without `alpha_r` refuses L2 and above (form error), since a silent zero would reproduce the
   constant-resistance error.
2. **Iron loss (OPEN-SINEW-3).** On when the card gives `iron_loss` coefficients; otherwise zero, with the flag
   `iron loss not modelled` on every result that read the card. The term and the flag are implemented in the
   L2 component (BD-071) and the default is decided by N15b-27; this bead's verb reports the flag and its
   static rows include the term when it is on.
3. **Derating and trip.** The card's derating bands are an authored, ordered list of (winding temperature, torque
   scale) pairs ending at `t_max_winding` with scale 0. The commanded torque is multiplied by the scale of the
   band the winding temperature is in (stepwise, as authored; no interpolation is invented): a pair (Tᵢ, sᵢ)
   applies for Tᵢ ≤ T_w < Tᵢ₊₁, the scale below the first pair is 1, temperatures must increase strictly and
   scales must not increase (a card that violates either is refused as a form error, FROB-REF-103, when an L2
   component or the verb is built from it). Reaching
   `t_max_winding` or `t_max_magnet` trips the actuator: torque is zero and the trip latches until reset (an
   episode reset or an explicit reset command). Entering a band and tripping are events on telemetry level T1; a
   trip sets the failure class `thermal-limit`, and `thermal-runaway` when the static analysis says the commanded
   load had no equilibrium.
4. **Verb `actuator.thermal` (kind `read`, M1).**
   `frob actuator thermal <card|design:joint> (--current I | --torque τ [--hold torque]) [--rms-of <run|gait>]
   [--speed ω] [--ambient T] [--start cold|soaked:T] [--require] --json`.
   `--speed ω` is the motor shaft speed at which the iron-loss term of item 2 is evaluated (P_fe =
   k_h·abs(ω_e) + k_e·ω_e² with ω_e = `pole_pairs`·ω, BD-071's law) and added to the loss curve. Its default
   is 0, a holding duty, where P_fe is zero; with `--rms-of` the run's or gait's own speeds are used and
   `--speed` is not accepted. Without it the static rows could not include a loss that depends on speed.
   Result (`frob.actuator.thermal.out/1`, input `frob.actuator.thermal.in/1`, named as the verb registry
   VERBS-actuator names every verb's schemas): `stable`; `t_equilibrium` or its absence; `time_constant` or `growth_rate`;
   `time_to_limit`; `i_crit`; `i_continuous`; `tangency {current, temperature}` and `margin_to_tangency` under
   torque hold; `largest_sustainable_load`; the network used (one node, or winding and housing in series with the
   condition s < G_wh·G_ha/(G_wh + G_ha)); `constant_resistance_comparison` (labelled as the wrong model, printed
   so the difference is visible); flags. Evidence: claim kind Known answer is never asserted by the verb itself;
   class `Estimated` unless every card field read has provenance `measured` (card values are `Estimated` unless
   calibrated), then `Tested`; `evidence.weakest` names the field that set the class; tier `static`; determinism
   D2; the `ModelId` or `CardId` it was bound to.
5. **Refusal FROB-REF-132.** "No stable thermal equilibrium at a required operating point, or an equilibrium above
   a limit." Raised when the point is required (`--require`, and always from stage 6 and A-11). Returned with it:
   the equilibrium, or the margin to the tangency; and the largest sustainable load. Ranked repairs as typed
   design edits, in N15-REF's order: `swap-actuator` to a card with a higher continuous rating; `regear` (the
   ratio that brings the RMS current under the continuous rating); raise the sink conductance; reduce payload or
   duty to the largest sustainable load. Exit code 3, `retry: after-change`. Without `--require` the same facts are a finding and the exit code is 2.
6. **Gate measurement.** GATE-SINEW-05 (claim Known answer, earliest M1): equilibrium, time constant, critical
   current and time to limit within 0.5% of the closed forms; the two-node condition; the tangency under torque
   holding within 1%; the worked card's 20 A point admitted by the constant-resistance check and refused by the
   corrected one. The dynamic rows are measured by stepping the L2 model on the referee (constant current held by
   an ideal current source) and comparing with N15a-14; the static rows are read from the verb.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Stepped L2 model on the referee, worked card, 20 A from cold: rise at equilibrium 164.7 K, time constant
      247 s (from the 63.2% point), 155 °C crossed at 385 s; each within 0.5%.
- [ ] At 35 A: growth rate 0.00136 s⁻¹ fitted on the stepped trace, limit at 61 s, within 0.5%.
- [ ] `actuator.thermal` returns I_crit = 31.9 A and I_continuous = 18.6 A within 0.5%, and under `--hold torque`
      with α_B = −0.0011 K⁻¹ the limit current 15.9 A and the tangency 16.5 A within 1%.
- [ ] The two-node condition is reported and matches the matrix test on the card's two-node variant.
- [ ] The seed's condition: the 20 A point is admitted by the constant-resistance check and refused by the
      corrected one with FROB-REF-132, returning 190 °C, the largest sustainable load 18.6 A and ranked repairs.
- [ ] A trip latches: after the winding crosses the limit, torque stays zero when the temperature falls, until
      reset; band entries and the trip are T1 events with the joint named.
- [ ] A card with no `alpha_r` refuses at L2; a card with no iron-loss coefficients sets `iron loss not modelled`.
- [ ] The verb is reachable through `frob --json` with schema and refusal list in the registry; cancellation of a
      long `--rms-of` evaluation is tested; the unit tests write the measured values of every row above to their
      output. The gate itself has one recorder: N15a-26 records GATE-SINEW-05 with `cargo run -p
      frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SINEW-05 --receipt <hash>` from the Known-answer
      tier receipt its script produces (static and stepped rows in one run), once N15a-24's referee script has
      passed the stepped rows from the user's side; both scripts depend on this bead.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `l2_resistance_tracks_temperature`: at T_w = 155 °C the winding element uses R = 0.20·(1 + 0.00393·130) =
  0.3022 Ω.
- `flux_falls_with_temperature`: k at 125 °C is k₀·(1 − 0.0011·100) = 0.89·k₀; holding torque then needs
  1/0.89 = 1.124 times the cold current.
- `stepped_equilibrium_matches_closed_form`, `stepped_time_to_limit`, `stepped_runaway_growth`: the dynamic rows.
- `static_check_flips`: the constant-resistance check admits 20 A (125 °C < 155 °C); the corrected check refuses
  (190 °C) with FROB-REF-132 and the stated payload.
- `derating_band_scale`: bands (120 °C, 1.0), (140 °C, 0.6), (155 °C, 0.0): a command of 10 N·m at 100 °C and
  at 130 °C is 10 N·m; at 145 °C it is 6 N·m; at 156 °C it is 0 and the trip flag is set. Bands with a repeated
  temperature or a rising scale are refused (FROB-REF-103) when the L2 component is built.
- `trip_latches_until_reset`, `band_events_emitted`, `missing_alpha_r_refused`, `iron_loss_flag`.
- `rms_duty_equilibrium`: a duty alternating 25 A and 5 A in equal time has I_rms = 18.03 A; the equilibrium uses
  the mean loss and lies below the limit, while the 25 A phase alone would not (18.6 A is the rating).
- `static_iron_loss_at_speed`: the worked card with 7 pole pairs, k_h = 0.002 W/(rad/s) and
  k_e = 1.406 × 10⁻⁶ W/(rad/s)², at 17 A and 25 °C ambient: `--speed 300rad/s` (ω_e = 2,100 rad/s) adds
  P_fe = 4.2 + 6.2 = 10.4 W and the equilibrium is 25 + 68.2/(0.80 − 0.2272) = 144.1 °C; `--speed 0rad/s`
  gives 25 + 57.8/0.5728 = 125.9 °C; `--speed` together with `--rms-of` fails the input schema (FROB-REF-802).

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/sinew_thermal_d4_oracle.rs` (in the conformance crate, so that `frob-actuate` takes
no dependency on it; oracle values are read from the compendium library N12-44, `frob_conformance::compendium::d4`,
and never regenerated here):
- `d4_runaway_oracle`: on the row's own fixture (R₀ = 0.1 Ω, α = 0.0039 K⁻¹, G = 0.5 W/K) the critical current of
  the static library and of the stepped L2 model agrees with the oracle's I_crit ≈ 35.8 A to 10⁻⁹ relative (static)
  and 0.5% (stepped: a current 1% below settles, 1% above grows); below it the settled temperature equals the
  oracle's T_amb + P_amb/(G − s). Negative control: the row's control, the constant-resistance model, never runs
  away and disagrees.
- `d4_entropy_oracle`: σ computed by the L2 model on 10⁴ random states equals the entropy-production row's value
  to 10⁻¹² relative and is ≥ 0; the row's control, a regenerative mode with a negated copper loss, gives σ < 0 in
  both.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_static_sizing.sh` (N15a-26) drives `frob actuator thermal` for every row of the worked-card
table and for the refusal; `scripts/e2e/sinew_referee_dynamics.sh` (N15a-24) runs the stepped thermal rows. Both
log, per assertion, the measured value with unit, the closed-form value, the relative error and the threshold
(0.005 or 0.01), the evidence block, and for the refusal the code, the returned equilibrium, margin and largest
sustainable load.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-304 to KA-311 (the closed forms and the worked card), KA-312 (12.8 K in 20 s), KA-313 (static check of a design
sized to hold 20 A: admitted by the constant-resistance check, refused by the corrected one with FROB-REF-132).
CX-136, CX-137. The registered negative control of GATE-SINEW-05 is the constant-resistance model itself: run as a
mutant, it must fail the equilibrium, time-constant, rating and verdict rows. Further mutants: the slope estimate
in place of the tangency (reports 25.5 A); derating that un-latches when the temperature falls; flux held constant
under torque hold (the limit current reads 18.6 A instead of 15.9 A). Live-dependency proof: changing `alpha_r` on
the card changes the equilibrium. Determinism: referee rows twice with identical digests; static rows identical in
debug and release.

## Considerations

- The planted knee-thermal failure used by the explanation verbs (BD-112) is built on this model: a knee card
  whose RMS current exceeds its continuous rating trips mid-episode, and the first cause is the first derating
  band entered.
- GATE-AUTOPSY-06 (the thermal counterexample of the counterfactual debugger, M3) reuses the same closed forms to
  0.1 s and 0.1 K; the numbers here must not be loosened without re-reading that gate.
- OPEN-SINEW-3 is decided at this gate by the Sinew lead; the default (on when coefficients exist, flagged
  otherwise) holds until then.
- Lumped nodes hide hot spots; card thermal values are `Estimated` unless calibrated, and the verb says so.
