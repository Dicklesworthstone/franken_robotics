## Background

Because an actuator is a port component (N15a-05, N15a-06), its energy balance can be checked every step: electrical
energy in equals mechanical work out plus copper loss i²·R plus friction loss plus gear loss plus the change in
stored magnetic and thermal energy. The difference is the balance residual. It is recorded, and it is what lets the
system-wide energy ledger report a measured residual instead of an estimate. The claim "the port balance closes" is
made only once GATE-SINEW-02 has recorded that residual: 10⁻⁹ relative on the referee (earliest M1, this bead) and
10⁻⁴ relative per episode on single-precision tiers (earliest M2, measured by the batched ledger, seed BD-208).

Why it matters beyond bookkeeping. A wrong gear ratio, a reversed sign or a loss that is applied to the torque and
never booked shows up as energy appearing from, or vanishing to, nowhere, even when the task return looks
excellent. A policy can exploit a model that hands it free energy; the residual is the instrument that sees it.

Three tolerances on energy residuals exist in the system and have different scopes; the looser ones do not relax
this one: 10⁻³ for the whole ledger on single-precision tiers, 10⁻⁴ per episode for the port balance on those
tiers, and 10⁻⁹ on the referee for both.

build its parts. The gate value has one recorder, N15a-24, which runs the legged fixture under its baseline

## Technical approach

1. **The identity, per step and per element.** decrease of chemical energy = mechanical work out + increase of
   magnetic, rotor-kinetic, elastic, polarization and thermal stored energy + heat to ambient + residual. For one
   element with port work W_k into it at each port k (electrical, shaft, thermal) over a step:
   residual = Σ_k W_k − ΔH − ΔU_assigned_out, where ΔH is the change of the element's own stored energy and the
   element's dissipation is not a separate outflow: it is the heat port work it sends to its thermal node.
2. **Port work on the referee.** W_k = h·ūᵀ·ȳ evaluated with the midpoint efforts and flows of the discrete-gradient
   step (N15a-05). With that quadrature the identity holds to round-off for quadratic energies; the residual on the
   referee therefore measures round-off, solver tolerance of the coupled step and any modelling error, and nothing
   is tuned to make it small.
3. **Relative measure.** ρ_step = |residual| / max(Σ_k |W_k| + |ΔH| + |heat to ambient|, E_floor), with
   E_floor = 10⁻¹² J so that an idle element does not divide by zero. The gate statistic on the referee is the
   maximum of ρ_step over all elements and steps of the gate fixtures; it must be ≤ 10⁻⁹ (TARGET). The per-episode
   statistic used on single-precision tiers is |Σ residual| / Σ |W_k| over the episode.
4. **Four rules that keep the account from lying.**
   - *Heat that is stored is stored energy.* Copper, iron, friction and gear losses flow into thermal nodes. They
     are listed for attribution and are not added again as losses: the energy that leaves the system is mechanical
     work at the joint and heat to ambient. Counting stored heat twice produces a false defect.
   - *The identity above, per step and per element*, not only per episode.
   - *Braking modes are separate lines:* motoring; regeneration to the battery; dissipation in a shunt; clipped
     braking. A step is classified from the sign of shaft power, the sign of electrical port power and the driver's
     `regen_policy` (`to-battery`, `shunt`, `clip`); a design that depends on regeneration the bus cannot accept
     shows it on these lines.
   - *The first offending event is named.* When ρ_step exceeds the tolerance, the record holds the earliest step
     and the element whose local balance failed, not only the episode total.
5. **Records (DESIGN sketch).** The record of one element and step is the `PortAccount` of N15b-05 (schema
   `frob.portaccount/1`), and that bead owns its fields and rules. The types below are its projection onto
   the telemetry stream, which this bead writes; where a field name differs, the schema of N15b-05 governs.
   ```rust
   pub struct BalanceLine { pub step: u64, pub element: ElementId, pub mode: BrakingMode,
                            pub work_elec: f64, pub work_shaft: f64, pub heat_to_node: f64,
                            pub d_magnetic: f64, pub d_kinetic: f64, pub d_elastic: f64, pub d_thermal: f64,
                            pub heat_to_ambient: f64, pub residual: f64 }           // joules, f64 on the referee
   pub struct FirstOffence { pub step: u64, pub element: ElementId, pub rho: f64, pub tolerance: f64 }
   pub enum BrakingMode { Motoring, RegenToBattery, Shunt, ClippedBraking }
   ```
   Lines go to the Energetics stream ("every term of the energy ledger, per port"); channels are declared with
   kind `interval mean` for powers and `cumulative counter` for energies, so downsampling cannot lose energy.
   Episode totals are in telemetry level T0; per-step lines in T2 for traced and failing worlds.
6. **INV-SINEW-02: the port energy balance of every element closes each step to a recorded residual.** The
   invariant is the existence and recording of the residual for every element and step, with the first offence
   named when the tolerance is exceeded. An element with no balance line is a test failure.
7. **The planted unaccounted loss.** A test-only mutation multiplies the delivered shaft torque of one element by
   0.98 without booking the missing 2% anywhere. The balance must report a positive residual of 2% of shaft work at
   that element, name it as the first offence at the first loaded step, and fail the gate. A second mutation books
   winding heat both as stored and as loss and must show a deficit equal to that heat.

      itself; the first offence names the mutated element and the first loaded step (the seed's condition).
- [ ] The double-count mutation is caught with a deficit equal to the winding heat.
- [ ] Every element of a bound model has a balance line at every step; a missing line fails the test.
- [ ] The four braking modes each appear on their own line on a fixture that visits all four, and their energies
      sum to the electrical port work.
- [ ] A fresh-context session that did not implement the work reviews the measured values before close.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `motor_axis_balance_closes`: R_w = 1 Ω, L = 10 mH, k = 0.1, J_r = 10⁻⁴ kg·m², b = 10⁻⁵, one thermal node; drive
  v = 12 V for 1 s against τ_load = 0.05 N·m at h = 0.5 ms: maximum ρ_step ≤ 10⁻⁹; Σ work_elec = Σ work_shaft out +
  Δ stored + heat to ambient to 10⁻⁹ relative.
- `four_quadrant_balance`: a sinusoidal voltage with a load that drives the shaft during half of each period; all
  four braking modes occur under `regen_policy` = `to-battery`, `shunt` and `clip` in three sub-runs; each mode's
  line carries the energy the mode moved.
- `unaccounted_loss_caught`, `double_count_caught`: the two mutations of step 7.
- `first_offence_is_earliest`: two elements mutated, one from step 100 and one from step 40: the record names the
  element and step 40.
- `idle_element_no_division`: an unpowered element reports ρ_step = 0.
- `balance_line_completeness`: property test over random small models; one line per element per step.
- `d4_energy_balance_oracle`, in `crates/frob-conformance/tests/sinew_balance_d4_oracle.rs` (in the conformance
  crate, so that `frob-actuate` takes no dependency on it; oracle values are read from the compendium library
  N12-44, `frob_conformance::compendium::d4`, and never regenerated here): the energy-balance row's motor–thermal
  fixture, stepped once by this bead's account in exact rationals, closes with zero residual, line for line equal
  to the oracle (supplied = change in stored + dissipated + exported, heat held in the node counted as stored).
  Negative control, the row's own: a ledger that books the heat entering the node as both stored and lost shows a
  deficit equal to that heat in both the oracle and the account (the same control as `double_count_caught`).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_referee_dynamics.sh` (N15a-24) runs `frob sim run --tier referee` on the gate fixtures with
telemetry level T2, then `frob telemetry energy <run> --json`, and asserts the maximum ρ_step against 10⁻⁹. It
repeats with the mutation feature enabled and asserts a non-zero exit and the first-offence record. Logged per
step: command line, exit code, wall time, evidence block, `max_rho_step` with threshold `1e-9`, the first-offence
record, per-mode energies in joules, digests of the two runs, and on failure the first differing balance line.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-295 (the balance closes each step to a recorded residual: 10⁻⁹ relative on the referee, 10⁻⁴ per episode on
single-precision tiers; control: stored heat also counted as a loss produces a false defect). KA-184 (ledger of a
motor axis in the three electrical modes). CX-142. Negative controls registered for GATE-SINEW-02: the 2%
unaccounted loss; the double count; a gear with one efficiency multiplier in all quadrants (creates 2.5 W in the
back-driven fixture: the 10 N·m, 1 rad/s point with η = 0.8 and N = 10, an energy excess of 2.5 W on that
element's line, caught). Metamorphic relations: reversing time-symmetric lossless fixtures (R_w = b = 0)
returns the stored energy to its start within 10⁻⁹; on the linear fixture (level L1, constant resistance, no
Coulomb or stiction term, thermal node at fixed ambient) scaling voltage and load by c scales every line by c²
(the relation does not hold at L2, where R depends on the heat, and is not asserted there).
Determinism: the referee run twice on one machine gives identical balance digests; debug and release agree.

## Considerations

- The 10⁻⁹ target is a TARGET until measured; if the coupled referee step's solver tolerance dominates the
  residual, the remedy is a tighter tolerance on the gate fixtures, recorded with the measurement, never a wider
  gate.
- The referee's contact solve also exchanges energy; this bead's residual is per actuator element and is separate
  from the mechanical side of the ledger, whose residual line is "integrator and solver".
- Memory: T2 balance lines are sampled by address and kept for failing worlds; T0 totals are always kept.
- Interaction: the Autopsy and Nemesis artifact guards read the residual to reject failures and witnesses that
  rest on simulator-created energy.
