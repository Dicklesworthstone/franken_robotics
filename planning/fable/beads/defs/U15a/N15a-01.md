## Background

Sinew is the subsystem for actuators, transmissions, batteries, thermal limits and power. Its one crate is
`frob-actuate` (core workspace, layer 2, tier Solid, scope cap 12 thousand lines of Rust including tests; a scope
conversation is triggered at 115% of the cap). This bead creates the crate so that every other Sinew bead has a place
to land, and it fixes three things that all of them share: how a port is typed and signed, what a component is, and
the rule that no actuator constant lives in code.

Why actuators are first-class at all. Sim-to-real studies locate the gap in actuator dynamics: identifying armature,
joint friction and delay gave 46% more travel distance and 75% less rotational deviation in one 2026 study; static
friction is commonly missing from randomization; joint friction and actuator delay were called particularly critical
for a point-foot biped (FACT, the plan's source register; to be re-read from the primary source before a gate cites
it). A simulator with exact rigid-body dynamics and an ideal torque source is precise about the wrong thing.

What Sinew claims. Electrical and thermal actuator models already exist in current simulators on the CPU and on the
GPU (FACT, source register). `frob-actuate` claims parity for them, not novelty. What it adds is four things, and
each is a bead family: a card with provenance on every field (BD-069); a port energy balance with a recorded residual
(BD-076); friction and backlash as rows of the solve instead of forces computed before it (N15a-08, N15a-09); and
static functions that refuse a design before any simulation is spent on it (N15a-14, N15a-16, BD-072, BD-073).

What `frob-actuate` owns: the actuator, battery and transmission cards (`.fcard`); the model ladder L0 to L4; the
joint row descriptors that carry stiction and backlash into the solve; the power bus and the battery; the
port-Hamiltonian form of every element and the energy accounting at its ports; and the static functions (torque,
speed and voltage envelope; thermal steady state; bus feasibility) that analysis A-11 (actuator envelope) and stage 6
(statics and sizing) of the design preflight call. Its verbs are `actuator.list`, `actuator.show`, `actuator.check`,
`actuator.envelope`, `actuator.thermal` and `actuator.bus`, all of kind `read`, first milestone M1.

Foundations (FACT, sibling repository `frankensim`, to be re-checked by the admission test INT-FSIM-09 in BD-068):
`fs-em` (about 255k lines), `fs-thermal`, `fs-tribo` (Stribeck, LuGre, rolling loss; a set-valued friction step),
`fs-control` (motor current and speed loops; thermal derating bands; voltage-hexagon limiting), `fs-phs` (the
battery and the interconnection `interconnect_mapped`) and a lumped `dc_motor` system example. DESIGN: on the referee
tier `frob-actuate` reuses these laws through the airlock crate `frob-fsim`; for the batched tiers it re-derives the
L1 and L2 arithmetic as kernels. Rejected: calling FrankenSim crates directly from `frob-actuate` (the airlock is the
only place volatile sibling APIs may be named, so an upstream change is admitted by re-running a test, not by
editing every consumer).

## Technical approach

1. **Layering.** `frob-actuate` is the first crate of layer 2. It may depend on layer 0 (`frob-core`, `frob-fsim`)
   and layer 1 (`frob-lie`, `frob-algebra`, `frob-kir`). It MUST NOT depend on `frob-design` or `frob-model` (later
   rows of the same layer): binding a card to a joint therefore lives in `frob-model` (N15a-03). The manifest
   declares its row of the crate map, `[package.metadata.frob] layer = 2`, `rank = 1`; `xtask check-layers`
   (N34p4-01) enforces the order (an edge to `frob-model`, rank 3, is the finding `L-1.rank`).
2. **Module layout (DESIGN).**
   ```text
   crates/frob-actuate/
     CONTRACT.md
     src/lib.rs
     src/port.rs            ShaftPort, ElecPort, ThermalPort, SignalPort; Component
     src/card/              schema, provenance, canonical bytes, checks, catalog loader      (BD-069)
     src/convention.rs      current conventions, power-invariant dq                          (N15a-02)
     src/ladder.rs          ActuatorLevel, options, level-by-tier capability table           (N15a-04)
     src/phs/               winding, gyrator, rotor, gear, driver, thermal node, structure   (N15a-05, N15a-06)
     src/balance.rs         per-element port balance and residual                            (BD-076)
     src/rows/              stiction, backlash, bound, rotor elimination                      (N15a-08, N15a-09, N15b-08, N15a-17)
     src/thermal/           statics, duty history, derating                                   (N15a-14, N15a-15, BD-072)
     src/envelope/          dq limits, static envelope                                        (N15a-16, BD-073)
     src/transmission/      loss laws, coupling, screw                                        (seed BD-074)
     src/bus/               bus node, battery                                                 (seed BD-075)
     src/reference/         host reference arithmetic for the batched tiers                   (N15a-07)
     tests/
   assets/cards/{actuator,battery,transmission}/*.fcard                                       (BD-069)
   ```
3. **Ports (DESIGN sketch; fixes intent, not signatures).** One sign convention for every port: power is positive
   into the component. Effort and flow per kind: shaft (torque, angular velocity), electrical (voltage, current),
   thermal (temperature in kelvin, heat rate), signal (typed message, carries no power).
   ```rust
   pub struct ShaftPort   { pub torque: Qty<Nm>,  pub omega: Qty<RadPerS> }
   pub struct ElecPort    { pub v: Qty<Volt>,     pub i: Qty<Ampere> }
   pub struct ThermalPort { pub t: Qty<Kelvin>,   pub q_dot: Qty<Watt> }
   /// Power into the component at this port, in watts. Positive means the component receives energy.
   pub trait PowerPort { fn power_in(&self) -> Qty<Watt>; }
   ```
4. **Component.** Each ladder level and each element is a `Component` with shaft, electrical and thermal ports.
   ```rust
   pub trait Component {
       type State;
       fn ports(&self) -> PortSet;
       fn stored_energy(&self, x: &Self::State) -> Qty<Joule>;                 // H(x), never negative for these elements
       fn step(&self, x: &mut Self::State, u: &PortInputs, h: RationalSeconds) -> PortBalance;
   }
   /// What one step did, per element: the quantities the energy ledger adds up.
   pub struct PortBalance { pub work_in: [Qty<Joule>; 3],   // electrical, shaft, thermal port
                            pub d_stored: Qty<Joule>, pub dissipated: Qty<Joule>,
                            pub heat_to_ambient: Qty<Joule>, pub residual: Qty<Joule> }
   ```
   One identity, computed by one helper `PortBalance::close` that every element and BD-076 use:
   residual = Σ work_in − d_stored − dissipated − heat_to_ambient. `dissipated` is the element's own loss (R·i²,
   friction, gear, iron), which it delivers as heat to its assigned thermal node; that node books the same joules
   as positive `work_in[thermal]` and as `d_stored` or `heat_to_ambient`. A dissipating element never also books its
   loss as negative thermal port work (that would count stored heat twice), so at composite level the `dissipated`
   of the elements and the thermal `work_in` of the nodes cancel and only shaft work and heat to ambient leave.
   Quantities use the `qty` re-export of `frob-core`; a raw `f64` never crosses a public function of this crate.
5. **CONTRACT.md, ten sections:** purpose; inputs (cards, port inputs, step); outputs; invariants (INV-SINEW-01 to
   -06, quoted in full); refusals (FROB-REF-130 to -136 with meaning and repairs); determinism class (exact rational
   arithmetic for the structural passivity checks, class D0; `f64` closed forms with FrankenSim's deterministic
   elementary functions for the static functions, class D2 on one platform; batched kernels inherit the class of
   their backend); evidence class of outputs (`Estimated` unless every consumed card field has provenance
   `measured`, since card values are `Estimated` unless calibrated, and a `datasheet` field is still `Estimated`;
   then `Tested`; never higher); validity domain (lumped thermal nodes, linear magnetics, linear
   copper coefficient, constant ambient); oracles (the closed forms of the known-answer registry N15-KA; FrankenSim
   components through the airlock); known gaps (hysteresis, saturation of iron, cogging, ageing unless the option is
   on). Beside it, `contract.toml` in N34-08's record format: `[crate]` with `layer = 2`, `rank = 1`,
   `plane = "compute"`, `determinism = "D0"` (the strongest class the crate claims, its exact passivity checks),
   `evidence = "Tested"`; `[budgets]`; `[safe_mode]` (`on_budget` and `on_calibration`, each `reference-path` or
   `refuse:<registered code>`); `[features]` matching the manifest (none at the scaffold). DESIGN: its `refusals` and `gates`
   arrays name only codes and gates already in `registries/refusals.toml` and `registries/gates.toml`, because
   `check-contracts` reports any other as `contract.unknown-reference`; the Sinew bead that first makes the crate
   raise a code or cite a gate adds it there. `tests/conformance.rs` exists from the scaffold on and runs the port
   tests below; later beads add their laws to it.
6. **No actuator constant in code (ADR-SINEW-01).** A test, `no_constants_in_code`, walks `src/` and fails on any
   numeric literal with a physical unit outside `tests/`, `reference/` test vectors and the mathematical constants
   0, 1, 2, ½ and the square roots of 2 and 3. Every number a model uses is read from a card field with provenance.
7. **Decision table in CONTRACT.md**, under the *Purpose* section (not an eleventh section). ADR-SINEW-01 (the card is the unit of actuator knowledge) → BD-069.
   ADR-SINEW-02 (stiction and backlash are rows of the solve) and ADR-SINEW-03 (row bounds frozen within a solve) →
   N15a-08, N15a-09. ADR-SINEW-04 (temperature-dependent resistance and flux from L2 upward; the static thermal check
   uses the temperature-dependent law at every level) → N15a-14, BD-072. ADR-SINEW-05 (power-invariant dq
   coordinates; a card states its current convention) → N15a-02. ADR-SINEW-06 (a transmission loses energy in every
   quadrant) → N15a-17. ADR-SINEW-07 (implicit rational updates for stiff states and slow rational clocks on batched
   tiers) → N15a-07. ADR-SINEW-08 (thermal state starts from a duty history) → N15a-15.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `port_power_sign`: an electrical port with v = 10 V and i = 2 A into the component reports +20 W; with i = −2 A
  it reports −20 W. The same for shaft (3 N·m, 4 rad/s → 12 W) and thermal (q̇ = 5 W → 5 W).
- `port_balance_identity_shape`: a `PortBalance` built from work_in = (20, −12, 0) J, d_stored = 3 J, dissipated =
  5 J, heat_to_ambient = 0 J has residual 0 J under residual = Σ work_in − d_stored − dissipated −
  heat_to_ambient; a thermal node with work_in = (0, 0, 5) J, d_stored = 3.5 J, heat_to_ambient = 1.5 J also closes
  to 0 J; the composite of the two closes to 0 J with 8 J net port work in (20 J electrical, −12 J shaft),
  3 + 3.5 J stored and 1.5 J to ambient; the
  helper that computes it is the one BD-076 uses.
- `port_balance_double_count_detected`: the winding above also booking its 5 J as work_in[thermal] = −5 J gives a
  residual of −5 J (the false defect of the double count).
- `no_constants_in_code`: as above, with the planted literal as the failing case.
- `layer_violation_fixture` (compile-fail): a module that names `frob_model` does not build.

### End-to-end script and logging
None of its own: a scaffold has no verb. `scripts/e2e/sinew_cards.sh` (N15a-25) is the first script that exercises
the crate through `frob`; it records this bead's key on the steps that load the crate's contract.

### Known answers and negative controls
No known answer is claimed here. Negative controls: the planted layer violation; the planted constant; a contract
file with nine sections, which `check-contracts` must reject.

## Considerations

- The cap of 12 thousand lines is tight for cards, twelve elements, rows, static functions, the bus and the host
  reference arithmetic. The first relief valve is moving test vectors to `frob-conformance`; the second is the scope
  conversation at 13.8 thousand lines. Splitting the crate is not planned.
- RISK-32 (common-mode model error: all tiers share cards and so agree with each other while disagreeing with
  reality) is a property of this crate's design. The scaffold's only contribution is the provenance rule; the
  defences are randomization over stated uncertainty, the card-conditional label of the sensitivity analysis and
  measurement in the calibration subsystem.
- OPEN-SINEW-4 (whether the second polarization pair of the referee battery is composed from existing FrankenSim
  elements or needs upstream work item FD-FSIM-21) is owned by the other Sinew unit; this scaffold reserves
  `src/bus/` for either outcome.
