## Background

A three-phase machine can be described with currents measured three ways: peak phase amperes, RMS phase amperes, or
amperes on the q axis of a rotating frame. A torque constant quoted per peak phase ampere, per RMS phase ampere or
per q-axis ampere differs by factors of √(3/2), √2 and √3, so a card that does not say which one it means carries a
silent torque error of 22% to 73%. ADR-SINEW-05 therefore decides two things: three-phase machines are written in
power-invariant dq coordinates, and a card must state its current convention.

Reasons for the power-invariant choice. (1) FrankenSim's field-oriented controller uses power-invariant
normalization (FACT: `frankensim/crates/fs-control/src/synchronous.rs`), so the referee and our model agree without
a hidden scale. (2) In these coordinates electrical power is v_d·i_d + v_q·i_q with no 3/2 factor, so the torque
constant equals the back-EMF constant; that equality is a passivity check on the card (check 1 of `actuator.check`,
BD-069). Rejected: amplitude-invariant dq (keeps phase amplitudes and puts a 3/2 into every power and torque
expression, which is exactly the factor that goes missing); leaving the convention implicit per vendor.

This bead is the small, pure module that every other Sinew bead calls before it touches a three-phase number.

## Scope

In: the `CurrentConvention` and `Modulation` enumerations; conversion of current, voltage, torque constant, back-EMF
constant and flux linkage between each stated convention and the power-invariant dq frame; the voltage-limit radius
per modulation; detection of conventions that disagree; FROB-REF-134 for a missing or inconsistent convention.

Out: the card schema that stores the convention (BD-069); the voltage and current limit equations and the envelope
(N15a-16, BD-073); the exact voltage hexagon on the referee (BD-073).

## Technical approach

1. **Definitions.** Phase currents i_a, i_b, i_c with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0. The power-invariant transform is the
   orthonormal Clarke–Park map: (i_α, i_β) = √(2/3)·(i_a − ½·i_b − ½·i_c, (√3/2)·(i_b − i_c)), then a rotation by
   the electrical angle θ_e into (i_d, i_q). It preserves power: v_a·i_a + v_b·i_b + v_c·i_c = v_d·i_d + v_q·i_q.
2. **Magnitudes.** For a balanced sinusoid of peak phase amplitude Î and RMS value I_rms = Î/√2, the dq magnitude
   is |i_dq| = √(3/2)·Î = √3·I_rms. The same factors apply to voltages and flux linkages.
3. **The modulus k.** Let k be the torque per q-axis ampere and the back-EMF per rad/s (mechanical) in
   power-invariant coordinates: τ = k·i_q and e_q = k·ω, with k = p·λ for p pole pairs and power-invariant flux
   linkage λ (non-salient case). Conversions of a stated torque constant:

   | `current_convention` | Stated constant | k | Error if misread as `q-axis` |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `dc` | k_t per ampere (brushed) | k = k_t | none |
   | `q-axis` | k_t per q-axis ampere | k = k_t | none |
   | `phase-peak` | k_t per peak phase ampere | k = k_t/√(3/2) | torque over-stated by 22.5% |
   | `phase-rms` | k_t per RMS phase ampere | k = k_t/√3 | torque over-stated by 73.2% |

   Peak against RMS differs by √2 (41.4%). The back-EMF constant is stated in the dual voltage quantity (BD-069):
   per-phase peak volts per rad/s gives k = √(3/2)·k_e; per-phase RMS gives k = √3·k_e; q-axis or DC gives k = k_e.
   A consistent card therefore satisfies k_t = (3/2)·k_e in `phase-peak` and k_t = 3·k_e in `phase-rms`.
4. **Current and voltage limits.** A current limit stated as Î becomes I_max = √(3/2)·Î in dq; as I_rms it becomes
   √3·I_rms. Inductances and resistance per phase are unchanged by the orthonormal transform (L_d, L_q, R are the
   per-phase values for a balanced star-equivalent machine).
5. **Voltage-limit radius by modulation.** `dc`: V_lim = V_bus. `svm-linear`: the inverter's voltage hexagon has
   vertices at √(2/3)·V_bus in power-invariant coordinates; the inscribed circle has radius (√3/2)·√(2/3)·V_bus =
   V_bus/√2, which is the limit the batched tiers use; it is conservative by at most 1 − √3/2 = 13.4% of voltage at
   a vertex. `six-step`: the plan admits the value in the schema and states no radius. Fail-closed DESIGN: the
   referee evaluates the exact hexagon for it; a batched-tier or closed-form envelope for a `six-step` card is
   refused with FROB-REF-136 (a row or option the requested tier does not carry) until a derivation of its
   fundamental-amplitude limit is on file in `docs/derivations/`.
6. **API (DESIGN sketch).**
   ```rust
   pub enum CurrentConvention { Dc, PhasePeak, PhaseRms, QAxis }
   pub enum Modulation { Dc, SvmLinear, SixStep }
   pub struct PiConstants { pub k: Qty<NmPerA>, pub i_max: Qty<Ampere>, pub lambda: Qty<VoltSecond>, pub p: u16 }
   pub fn to_power_invariant(card: &ActuatorCardView) -> Result<PiConstants, Refusal>;   // FROB-REF-134
   pub fn v_lim(modulation: Modulation, v_bus: Qty<Volt>) -> Result<Qty<Volt>, Refusal>; // FROB-REF-136 for SixStep
   ```
   Factors are exact symbolic constants (√2, √3, √(3/2)) evaluated once in `f64`; uncertainty half-widths scale by
   the same factor.
7. **Disagreeing conventions (FROB-REF-134).** Refuse when: `current_convention` is absent; `dc` is stated with
   `pole_pairs` > 0 and `modulation` ≠ `dc`; a three-phase convention is stated with `modulation: dc`; or the card
   gives both `flux_linkage` and `kt` and p·λ differs from k by more than their combined uncertainty in every
   reading of the convention except one other than the stated one (the card is then self-consistent under a
   different convention than it declares). The refusal returns the fields in conflict and the value of k under each
   of the four readings, so the author can see which reading the numbers support. Exit code 3, `retry: after-change`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four conversions reproduce the table above to 1 part in 10¹² in `f64`.
- [ ] Power invariance holds: for 10⁴ random balanced and unbalanced three-phase samples, v·i summed over phases
      equals v_d·i_d + v_q·i_q to 10⁻¹² relative.
- [ ] `v_lim(SvmLinear, 48 V)` returns 33.94 V; `v_lim(Dc, 48 V)` returns 48 V; `SixStep` refuses with FROB-REF-136.
- [ ] A card with no convention, and each of the three inconsistent combinations, refuses with FROB-REF-134 and
      names the fields in conflict.
- [ ] A card whose `kt` was authored per RMS phase ampere and labelled `q-axis` is caught: either by FROB-REF-134
      when `flux_linkage` is present, or by check 1 of `actuator.check` when `ke` is present.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `kt_conversion_table`: k_t = 0.150 N·m/A stated as `phase-peak` gives k = 0.122474 N·m/A; as `phase-rms` gives
  0.086603; as `q-axis` or `dc` gives 0.150.
- `ke_dual_conversion`: per-phase peak k_e = 0.0816497 V·s/rad gives k = 0.100; with k_t = 0.122474 per peak phase
  ampere the two agree (k_t = (3/2)·k_e).
- `current_limit_conversion`: Î = 32.66 A gives I_max = 40.0 A; I_rms = 23.09 A gives 40.0 A.
- `power_invariance_property`: the property of the second acceptance criterion, with seeds recorded.
- `vlim_inscribed_circle`: for 10⁴ random angles the circle of radius V_bus/√2 lies inside the hexagon with
  vertices at √(2/3)·V_bus, and touches it at the six edge midpoints; the largest gap is 13.4% of the vertex radius.
- `convention_conflicts`: the four refusal cases of step 7, each with its returned candidate values of k.

### End-to-end script and logging
Exercised through `scripts/e2e/sinew_cards.sh` (N15a-25): `frob actuator show` on three copies of one generic card
authored in the three conventions must print the same power-invariant modulus; `frob actuator check` on the
mislabelled copy must exit 3. The log records the stated constant, the convention, the computed k with units and the
relative difference against the reference copy with its threshold (10⁻¹²).

### Known answers and negative controls
The conversion factors √(3/2) = 1.224745, √2 = 1.414214 and √3 = 1.732051 are the known answers. KA-314 (voltage
limit of a three-phase bridge in power-invariant coordinates: V_bus/√2 = 33.9 V at 48 V, within 0.5%) is carried
here and re-measured by BD-073 for GATE-SINEW-06. Negative controls: the amplitude-invariant transform substituted
for the orthonormal one (power invariance fails by the factor 3/2); a mislabelled convention; `v_lim` returning the
vertex radius (the inscribed-circle property test fails). Metamorphic relation: scaling every phase quantity by c
scales k-derived torque by c and power by c². The module is pure and is run in debug and release with identical
results.

## Considerations

- The dual convention for `ke` and the handling of `six-step` are decisions of this bead family where the plan is
  silent; both are fail-closed and are listed in the unit's coverage notes for the owner.
- Delta-wound machines and line-to-line figures are converted to the star-equivalent per-phase values by the card
  author; the module does not guess a winding connection.
- Salient machines (L_d ≠ L_q) add the reluctance torque p·(L_d − L_q)·i_d·i_q; k = p·λ then describes only the
  magnet torque and check 1 of `actuator.check` compares magnet terms only.
