## Background

`actuator.check` runs its three checks when a card is admitted and again when it is bound to a joint (BD-069 owns
the checks). The second run exists because a card that is consistent alone can be wrong in place: the joint supplies
the bus voltage, the ambient and sink it rejects heat to, the transmission in front of it and the side on which
friction and the encoder sit. Binding is also where the card's identity enters the model identity, so that changing
one field of one card changes every result computed with it.

`frob-actuate` is the first crate of layer 2 and cannot depend on the model compiler. Binding therefore lives in
`frob-model` (the compile of a design into a `RobotModel`), which calls `frob-actuate`. The design preflight
(`frob design check`) has eight stages; stage 1 (Form) is the compile, and its refusal list includes "a card that
fails its own checks (130, 134)". Stage 6 (Statics and sizing) refuses "a card field `assumed` under
`--strict-data` (113)".

## Scope

In: resolving a design's card references to `CardId`s; the bound-card record `BoundActuator`; re-running the
three checks with the joint's context; raising FROB-REF-130 and FROB-REF-134 at stage 1; the bind point at
which further bind-time checks register (the signed motion witness of N15b-12 is the first); the provenance
summary a model carries per bound card; overrides at bind time; the influence record that says which card
fields a compiled product read. This bead is the only place a card name becomes a `CardId` and the only
holder of a bound card's identity. Out, with the bead that has each: the flat channel tables of the model's
binding section (actuator channel index, joint coordinate, signed transmission binding, channel sign, bus
and sink membership, coupling maps, sensor channels, joint row descriptors, the turn-state table) and the
section digest (N14a-09, the binding functor, which depends on this bead: it takes the `BoundActuator` of
each actuated joint as input, writes `BoundActuator.card` into its channel and resolves no name and forms
no card hash itself; the `BindContext` here is therefore read from the expanded design tree directly, the
bus by the expanded `members`, and not from the functor's tables); the checks themselves (BD-069); the
ladder level and its place in `ModelId` (N15a-04); the signed motion witness of coupled transmissions
(N15b-12, which depends on this bead and runs at the bind point defined here); the per-field bands and
claim products, the read sets of the static functions, the provenance share of a design, and the
`--strict-data` refusal FROB-REF-113 where a verdict is formed (N15b-06, which reads the provenance summary
and the influence record built here); the static sizing refusals 131, 132, 133 at stage 6 (BD-072, BD-073
and seed BD-075 through A-11, seed BD-103).

## Technical approach

1. **Resolution.** A design names a card per actuated joint (`"actuator": "qdd-m"`), a transmission card where one
   is separate, and one battery card on the bus. The compiler resolves each name through the catalog and the
   workspace to a `CardId` (`name.fcard@b3:<hash>`). An unresolved name is a form error (FROB-REF-103), with the
   nearest card names as repairs; this is the one refusal for a card that does not resolve, and the binding
   functor (N14a-09) raises none of its own. The envelope
   field `bound` lists every name with the immutable identifier it was resolved to. An override yields a new card
   value and therefore a new `CardId` (the hash of that value's canonical bytes, as the card catalog defines
   identity); that `CardId` is the identity of the bound card everywhere, including the `card` field of the
   functor's actuator channel. No separate instance hash exists.
2. **The bound-card record (DESIGN sketch).**
   ```rust
   pub struct BoundActuator {
       pub joint: JointId, pub card: CardId, pub transmission: Option<CardId>,
       pub overrides: Vec<FieldOverride>,          // each yields a derived CardId; never edits a card
       pub context: BindContext,                   // bus nominal voltage range, ambient, sink conductance, joint limits
       pub provenance: ProvenanceSummary,          // per field read: class and uncertainty kind
       pub flags: Vec<BindFlag>,                   // e.g. L1ScreenReactiveDrop (N15a-04), IronLossNotModelled
   }
   ```
3. **Checks at bind.** The three checks of BD-069 are run again with `BindContext` substituted for the card's own
   reference conditions: check 1 unchanged; check 2 extended with the bound transmission's loss terms and its
   efficiency pair; check 3 evaluated at the joint's bus voltage range (lowest value) and the design's ambient and
   sink. A failure of check 1 or 2 is FROB-REF-130 and a convention failure is FROB-REF-134, both raised at stage 1
   with the joint path in `refusal.pointer` and ranked repairs as typed design edits (`swap-actuator`, `regear`,
   `set-param`). A failure of check 3 at bind is not raised at stage 1: it is the business of stage 6, where the
   required operating points of the design are known (A-11).
4. **`--strict-data` (FROB-REF-113): the rule, stated here for the data this bead supplies; the refusal
   itself is raised by N15b-06 where the verdict is formed.** Under `--strict-data`, a verdict that rests on a card field of class
   `assumed` is refused: "an actuator card with a required field of class `assumed` when the caller asked for
   `--strict-data`". The refusal names the joint, the card and the field, and its repairs are: cite a datasheet for
   the field, bind a card whose field is `datasheet` or `measured`, or drop the flag. The rule is evaluated per
   analysis from the influence record, so an `assumed` field that the requested verdict never read does not refuse.
   Fields of class `generic` do not refuse under this flag; they keep the result at evidence class `Estimated`.
5. **Identity.** The set of bound `CardId`s (after overrides) is part of the model's content, so it is part of
   `ModelId`. An override on one joint changes one `CardId`, the `ModelId`, and (through the influence records of
   the compile memo) exactly the products that read that card.
6. **Card-conditional results.** Every tier reads the same card, so agreement between tiers says nothing about the
   card. The model therefore exposes, per bound card, the share of fields by provenance class; reports print that
   share, and the sensitivity analysis (A-16) labels a verdict that changes sign inside a field's stated band as
   *card-conditional*, naming the field. This bead supplies the per-field uncertainty and provenance that label
   needs; the label itself is produced by the analysis crate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob_model::bind_actuators` on a design that binds a card with `kt` and `ke` inconsistent beyond
      uncertainty returns FROB-REF-130 tagged for stage 1, pointer at the joint, and at least one typed-edit repair.
      (The stage runner that surfaces it as `frob design check` stopping at stage 1 is N14a-08 and seed BD-110,
      both of which depend on this bead; the CLI path is asserted end to end by N15a-25.)
- [ ] A design binding a card without `current_convention` returns FROB-REF-134 tagged for stage 1.
- [ ] A passive card bound behind a transmission card with `efficiency_forward` > 1 refuses at bind with
      FROB-REF-130 (the pair is not passive although the actuator card alone is).
- [ ] The influence record of each compiled product lists exactly the card fields it read, with their classes:
      for a design whose knee card has `c_winding` of class `assumed`, the thermal product's record contains
      that field and the record of a product that never reads it does not. (The refusal that follows under
      `--strict-data`, FROB-REF-113 for the first and none for the second, is asserted in N15b-06.)
- [ ] Overriding one field on one joint changes `ModelId`; overriding it back restores the original `ModelId`.
- [ ] Every archetype shipped at M1 binds only catalog cards and passes the checks at bind (a row of
      GATE-SINEW-08: every card in the catalog passes `actuator.check`, admitted and bound).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `bind_resolves_names`: `"qdd-m"` resolves to the catalog `CardId`; a misspelt `"qdd-mm"` refuses with
  FROB-REF-103 and suggests the nearest name.
- `bind_reruns_checks_with_context`: a card whose peak torque is reachable at its own 48 V reference and not at a
  24 V bus is admitted as a card, binds without a stage-1 refusal, and carries the flag `peak-unreachable-at-bus`
  for stage 6 to act on.
- `bound_pair_passivity`: the transmission case of the third acceptance criterion.
- `influence_record_by_product`: two compiled products, one reading the `assumed` field `c_winding` and one not;
  the first record lists the field with class `assumed`, the second does not list it (the refusal FROB-REF-113 that
  N15b-06 derives from these records is tested there).
- `override_changes_model_id` and `override_is_reversible`: as in the acceptance criteria; the catalog file's bytes
  are unchanged throughout.
- `provenance_summary_counts`: a card with 3 `datasheet`, 5 `generic` and 2 `assumed` fields reports those counts.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_cards.sh` (N15a-25, which depends on the stage runner) includes the bind cases: `frob design
check <fixture> --stages 1-1 --json` for the inconsistent-card, missing-convention and non-passive-transmission
fixtures, and `frob design check <fixture> --strict-data --json` for the assumed-field fixture. Each line logs the
stage reached, the refusal code, the pointer, the `ModelId` before and after an override, the influence record's
field list, and the evidence block.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-279 (reverse drive that produces free electrical energy is refused by the card check with FROB-REF-130) is
exercised here at bind. Negative controls: a binder that skips the second run of the checks (the bound-pair fixture
then compiles, and the test fails); an influence record that lists every field of the card instead of the fields
read (the second product's record then names `c_winding`, and `influence_record_by_product` fails); an override
that edits the card in place (the file-bytes assertion fails). Live-dependency proof: perturbing one card field changes `ModelId` and at least one
downstream product. Determinism: binding is exact (class D0) and its output is byte-identical across runs and
platforms.

## Considerations

- The split "checks 1 and 2 at stage 1, check 3 at stage 6" follows the preflight's refusal table, which lists only
  130 and 134 at stage 1. If the owner wants an unreachable rating refused at bind, it moves here unchanged.
- The signed motion witness for a reversed transformer runs at this same bind point; its definition and gate
  (GATE-SINEW-10) are owned by the other Sinew unit, and this bead only reserves the call site.
- Performance: binding is part of stage 1, whose budget is 100 ms at the 95th percentile on the archetypes
  (TARGET); the checks are closed forms and exact comparisons and must stay well inside it.
