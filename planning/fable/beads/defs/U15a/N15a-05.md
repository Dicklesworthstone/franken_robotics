## Background

Every Sinew element is written in port-Hamiltonian form (construction C-38):

  ẋ = (J(x) − R(x))·∇H(x) + G(x)·u,   y = G(x)ᵀ·∇H(x),

where x is the element's state, H its stored energy, J a skew-symmetric interconnection matrix, R a positive
semidefinite dissipation matrix, u the port inputs and y the conjugate outputs. Then Ḣ = uᵀ·y − ∇Hᵀ·R·∇H: stored
energy changes by the power at the ports minus what the element dissipates, and dissipation is never negative.
Elements are joined by power-conserving junctions (shaft: speeds identified, torques balance; electrical: voltages
identified, currents balance; thermal: temperatures identified, heat rates balance), so the property survives
composition. This is the form FrankenSim's `fs-phs` implements (FACT: `frankensim/crates/fs-phs`; admitted by the
test INT-FSIM-09 in BD-068, which requires power across an interconnection to sum to zero to 10⁻¹² relative).

Why this form and not a torque limit with an efficiency number. Written this way, passivity and the energy balance
are statements about structure (skew J, semidefinite R) that can be checked exactly, instead of bookkeeping that can
drift. The energy ledger then closes per mechanism, per episode and at the earliest offending event, so a wrong
gear ratio or a sign error shows as energy appearing from nowhere even when the final task return looks excellent.

## Scope

In: the element types and their (state, H, J, R, G, ports) for the electromechanical elements (winding,
gyrator, rotor, ideal gear, driver); their stepping on the referee through the airlock; composition of a
motor axis from elements; the exact per-element check for these five kinds (J skew, R positive
semidefinite, on card rationals) with its `StructureRecord`, in `passivity/structure.rs`; the series-spring
element's structure (its use is N15b-18); the admission predicate `design-has-port-actuators` and the
`actuation` member of `sim.run`'s `needs_groups` (item 8). Out, with the bead that has each: the structural descriptors of
every other element kind, the composite check through junction incidence, the certificates, the place of
the check in `actuator.check` and the theorem-card proposal for PO-SINEW-01 (N15b-09, which extends the
module started here); the thermal node and the second law (N15a-06); stiction and backlash (N15a-08,
N15a-09); the loss element in series with a gear (N15a-17 and seed BD-074); the battery (N15b-03), the bus
(BD-075) and the coupling transmission (BD-074 for square matrices, N15b-10 for the map C(q)); the ladder
levels that compose these elements (seeds BD-070, BD-071, which depend on this bead); the per-step balance
record and residual (N15b-05, BD-076); batched-tier arithmetic (N15a-07).

## Technical approach

1. **The element table (the chapter's full list, with the bead that implements each row).**

   | Element | State | Stored energy H | Structure and dissipation | Ports | Bead |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | Winding | Flux linkage φ = L·i (per axis in dq) | φ²/(2L) | R = R_w(T) | Electrical (v, i) | here |
   | Electromechanical conversion | — | — | A gyrator of modulus k: τ = k·i and e = k·ω. Lossless | Couples winding and rotor | here |
   | Rotor | Angular momentum p = J_r·ω | p²/(2J_r) | Viscous b | Shaft | here |
   | Gear | — | — | A transformer of modulus N, with a loss element in series | Two shafts | here; loss in N15a-17 |
   | Coupling transmission | — | — | A transformer with a matrix modulus T: q̇ = T·θ̇, τ_actuator = Tᵀ·τ_joint | Several shafts | BD-074 (square T); N15b-10 (the map C(q)) |
   | Stiction | — | — | A monotone set-valued resistive relation between torque and speed | Shaft | N15a-08 |
   | Backlash | Relative angle δ | Zero inside the gap; ½·k_tooth·e² beyond it on compliant tiers, e being the excess of \|δ\| over β/2 | Tooth damping; an impact law on the referee | Two shafts | N15a-09 |

   | Battery | Charge q; polarization charges q₁, q₂ | ∫OCV dq + q₁²/(2C₁) + q₂²/(2C₂) | R₀, R₁, R₂ | Electrical | N15b-03 |
   | Bus | Bus capacitor charge q_b | q_b²/(2C_b) | Harness resistance | A node: equal voltages, currents sum to zero | BD-075 |
   | Driver | Controller states, which are signals | — | Modulation as a lossless transformer of duty; switching loss as a resistance | Two electrical, one signal | here |
2. **Worked instance: the brushed or q-axis motor (known answer KA-293).** x = (φ, p), H = φ²/(2L) + p²/(2J_r),
   ∇H = (i, ω), J = [[0, −k], [k, 0]], R = diag(R_w, b), u = (v, −τ_load). The two rows read
   L·di/dt = v − R_w·i − k·ω and J_r·dω/dt = k·i − b·ω − τ_load, and Ḣ = v·i − τ_load·ω − R_w·i² − b·ω².
3. **The dq machine (DESIGN, derived from the voltage and torque equations of the field-weakening bead).** States
   φ_d = L_d·i_d, φ_q = L_q·i_q, p_m = J_r·ω; H = φ_d²/(2L_d) + φ_q²/(2L_q) + p_m²/(2J_r); ∇H = (i_d, i_q, ω). With
   p pole pairs and magnet flux linkage λ,
   J(x) = [[0, 0, p·φ_q], [0, 0, −p·(φ_d + λ)], [−p·φ_q, p·(φ_d + λ), 0]], R = diag(R_w, R_w, b), u = (v_d, v_q,
   −τ_load). J is skew for every state, and the third row reproduces τ = p·[λ·i_q + (L_d − L_q)·i_d·i_q].
4. **Gear and driver.** Ideal gear of modulus N between shafts 1 (motor) and 2 (joint): ω₁ = N·ω₂ and, as port
   quantities with power positive into the element, τ₂ = −N·τ₁ (the joint receives N·τ₁), so
   τ₁·ω₁ + τ₂·ω₂ = 0 (PO-SINEW-03 with r = 1/N, tested in N15a-18). Driver: duty d in [−1, 1] maps bus to phase side as v_phase = d·v_bus, i_bus = d·i_phase (lossless);
   switching loss is a series resistance r_sw ≥ 0 from the card; controller states are signals and store no energy.
5. **Stepping on the referee.** Elements step with FrankenSim's discrete-gradient integrators, which keep the power
   identity in the discrete step (FACT: stated in `fs-phs/src/circuit/battery.rs` for the battery). For the
   quadratic energies above the discrete gradient is the midpoint gradient, so one step solves
   (x⁺ − x)/h = (J(x̄) − R)·∇H(x̄) + G·ū with x̄ = (x + x⁺)/2 and then
   H(x⁺) − H(x) = h·(ūᵀ·ȳ − ∇H(x̄)ᵀ·R·∇H(x̄)) holds to round-off. The call goes through the airlock
   (`frob-fsim`, feature group `actuation`, admitted by BD-068 and N33a-19) to `fs_phs::interconnection::interconnect_mapped`; `frob-actuate` never names
   an `fs-*` crate directly, and it reaches only the canonical mapped interconnection, never the legacy
   `transformer` (whose sign is opposite to the canonical map).
6. **Structural check (PO-SINEW-01: the composite of passive elements through power-conserving junctions is
   passive).** `structure::check(&element)` verifies, exactly on the card's values read as rationals: J + Jᵀ = 0
   entry by entry (state-dependent entries are compared as symbolic monomials); R = Rᵀ and R ⪰ 0 by an exact
   rational LDLᵀ with nonnegative pivots. It returns a
   `StructureRecord` (element, hashes of J and R patterns, pivots) stored with the model. A failure is FROB-REF-130.
   This bead writes the check for its five element kinds. The same module is extended by N15b-09 to every
   other kind (thermal node, battery, bus, stiction, backlash, coupling, spring), to composites (every
   junction's incidence rows sum the port powers to zero), and to the certificates the offline auditor reads.
   PO-SINEW-01 is a candidate theorem card for the proof subsystem; until a checker accepts it, the claim is of
   class `Tested`, and the record says so.
7. **Evidence and determinism.** The structural check is exact (class D0). Stepped states are `f64` on the referee
   and inherit the referee's class (same-platform determinism). Every stepped output carries the evidence rule of
   the crate contract: `Estimated` unless every card field read is `measured` (card values are `Estimated` unless
   calibrated). Each element fills its `PortBalance` with the one helper of N15a-01 (`PortBalance::close`): its own
   loss goes to `dissipated` and is never also booked as negative thermal port work.
8. **The airlock requirement of a run that builds these elements.** The elements reach `fs-phs` through the
   airlock's `actuation` group, which is off in a default build (N33a-19). Under N33c-32's rule (a predicate is
   registered by the bead that needs it) this bead registers the predicate `design-has-port-actuators` in
   `frob_verbs::admission::predicates`: true when the resolved request's design, or a design of `--designs`, has
   an actuator whose resolved level builds this bead's elements on the referee (L1 and above: the ladder levels
   of BD-070 and BD-071 compose them), false when every actuator is at L0 or the design has none. In the same
   change `sim.run`'s row in `registries/verbs.toml` gains `{ groups = ["actuation"], when =
   "design-has-port-actuators" }` in its `needs_groups`, as VERBS-sim's row states, so the lint
   `needs-groups-known` holds at every commit. A binary without the group answers such a run with
   `FROB-REF-790` (`foundation-group-absent`, exit 5) naming `actuation`, before any world is stepped.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] KA-293 holds as a polynomial identity: for symbolic (i, ω, v, τ_load) the element's rows equal
      L·di/dt = v − R_w·i − k·ω and J_r·dω/dt = k·i − b·ω − τ_load, and Ḣ equals v·i − τ_load·ω − R_w·i² − b·ω².
- [ ] `structure::check` passes for every element built from every catalog card and fails (FROB-REF-130) for a
      winding with negative resistance and for a J with one sign flipped.
- [ ] Over 10⁴ random steps of the motor axis the discrete power identity holds to 10⁻¹² relative.
- [ ] The dq element with L_d = L_q reduces to the q-axis instance when i_d = 0 and k = p·λ.
- [ ] No `fs-*` crate is named outside `frob-fsim` (checked by `xtask check-deps`); the legacy `transformer` is
      unreachable from this crate.
- [ ] Each element exposes `stored_energy` and a per-step `PortBalance` consumable by BD-076.
- [ ] The predicate `design-has-port-actuators` is registered and `sim.run`'s `needs_groups` carries its
      `actuation` member; a referee run of an actuated design at L1 in a build without `actuation` is
      `FROB-REF-790` naming the group, and a design without such actuators does not require it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `motor_axis_rows_ka293`: with L = 10 mH, R_w = 1 Ω, k = 0.1, J_r = 10⁻⁴ kg·m², b = 10⁻⁵, at (i, ω, v, τ_load) =
  (2 A, 50 rad/s, 12 V, 0.05 N·m): L·di/dt = 12 − 2 − 5 = 5 V; J_r·dω/dt = 0.2 − 0.0005 − 0.05 = 0.1495 N·m;
  Ḣ = 24 − 2.5 − 4 − 0.025 = 17.475 W, equal to i·(L·di/dt) + ω·(J_r·dω/dt) = 10 + 7.475.
- `locked_rotor_current_step`: R_w = 1 Ω, L = 10 mH, rotor locked, step v = 1 V: current follows
  (V/R_w)·(1 − e^(−t/τ)) with τ = 10 ms; at t = 10 ms it is 0.6321 A within 10⁻³ at h = 0.1 ms (the C-38 fixture).
- `structure_exact_rational`: J skew and R ⪰ 0 for all catalog cards; the two planted failures refuse.
- `dq_reduces_to_q_axis`, `dq_torque_row`: at i_d = −10 A, i_q = 20 A, p = 7, λ = 0.01, L_d = 80 µH, L_q = 120 µH
  the torque row gives 7·[0.2 + (−40·10⁻⁶)·(−200)] = 7·0.208 = 1.456 N·m.
- `discrete_power_identity`: the property of the third acceptance criterion, seeds recorded.
- `gear_power_conservation`: N = 9, τ₁ = 0.5 N·m, ω₂ = 3 rad/s: ω₁ = 27 rad/s, τ₂ = −4.5 N·m as a port quantity,
  and the port powers sum to zero exactly in rationals.
- `port_actuators_require_actuation` (`cargo test -p frob-verbs`, `synthetic` resolved requests, as N33c-32's
  `argument_dependent_requirement`): a `sim.run` request on `--tier referee` whose design has one actuated joint
  at L1 makes the predicate true and, in a build without `actuation`, is refused with `FROB-REF-790`, exit 5,
  naming `actuation`, zero worlds stepped; the same design at `--actuator-level L0`, and a design with no
  actuators, make it false and are admitted; with `--features frob-fsim/actuation` the first request is
  admitted; on the real registry `sim.run`'s row passes `needs-groups-known`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_referee_dynamics.sh` (N15a-24) runs a single-joint fixture on the referee and reads the stored
energy and port powers per element from telemetry. Logged: per element the maximum relative deviation of the
discrete power identity with its threshold (10⁻¹²), the structure record's hash, the `ModelId` and seed.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-293 (exact). KA-183 (locked-rotor current step, τ = 10 ms). KA-294 and INV-SINEW-01 (every element is passive:
its dissipation is nonnegative in all four quadrants of every port) are measured at scale by N15a-23. Negative
controls: a gyrator with τ = k·i and e = 0.9·k·ω (the element is then not lossless and creates 10% of the converted
power; the structure check and the identity test must both catch it); explicit Euler substituted for the
discrete-gradient step (the identity fails at 10⁻¹² and passes only at O(h)); a reversed transformer reached through
the legacy call (caught by the dependency check). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine with identical digests;
debug and release; the structural check byte-identical on both platforms.

## Considerations

- Interconnections with algebraic constraints are deferred in the foundation (FACT, construction C-38's stated
  limit); elements here are joined only through junctions that resolve causality, and an algebraic loop refuses at
  design stage 5 (FROB-REF-108).
- Linear magnetics is the validity domain: saturation and cogging are known gaps recorded in `CONTRACT.md`.
- OPEN-SINEW-3 (iron loss on by default at L2: on when the card gives coefficients, otherwise zero with the flag
  `iron loss not modelled` on every result) adds one dissipative term to R when on; the structure check covers it.
- If `fs-phs` fails its admission at the pinned commit, the fallback is an in-house `f64` discrete-gradient step for
  these quadratic elements (a few dozen lines), with the upstream work item left open.
