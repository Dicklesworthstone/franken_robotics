## Background

An actuator turns electrical energy into mechanical work and heat. If the heat is simply subtracted and forgotten,
the model has an energy sink with no accounting, and the thermal limits that decide a design have nothing to act
on. Sinew therefore gives every actuator thermal nodes that receive every loss as heat. With the thermal nodes
included, the composite of elements is lossless up to its ports: the dissipation terms R of the electromechanical
elements (N15a-05) reappear as heat flowing into thermal storage, and the only energy that leaves the actuator is
mechanical work at the shaft and heat to ambient. That is the first law for the actuator. The second law is a
per-step test: with temperatures in kelvin, the entropy production is never negative. Temperature, not entropy, is
the stored variable of a node; entropy is a derived, reported quantity.

DESIGN choice and its limit, stated plainly: the second-law check has content only as a check on the sign of each
assigned loss. It catches a negative loss from a sign error in a braking mode, and it cannot fail for a correct
model. It is a sign control, not validation of the thermal model (counterexample CX-091: "a passing second-law check
shows the thermal model is right" is false). Rejected: entropy as the state variable (it would make the heat
equation nonlinear in the stored variable for no gain at these temperatures); a second, separate entropy model
(the entropy balance is derived from dU = T·dS of the same node, so it cannot disagree with the energy balance).

## Scope

In: the thermal node element; one-node and two-node (winding, housing) networks as element compositions;
routing of each element's dissipation into a node; the composite's first-law identity; the entropy
production formula and the per-step test; the fault raised when it fails. Out, with the bead that has each:
the L2 component that attaches these nodes to a winding and steps them with it (seed BD-071, which depends
on this bead); the temperature dependence of resistance and flux and the runaway analysis (N15a-14, BD-072);
derating and trips (BD-072); the per-element balance record and residual and the ledger lines (N15b-05,
BD-076 and the Chronicle energy ledger, seed BD-092); the exact structural descriptor of the node's
conductance matrix (N15b-09); the slow thermal clock on batched tiers (N15a-07).

## Technical approach

1. **Node.** State: internal energy U = C·(T − T_ref), with heat capacity C from the card (`c_winding`,
   `c_housing`). Stored energy: U. Port: thermal (T, Q̇). Dissipation: none inside the node; conductances connect it
   to neighbours and to ambient. Energy balance of one node with assigned loss P_loss and conductance G to ambient:
   C·dT/dt = P_loss − G·(T − T_amb).
2. **Two-node network (winding w, housing h).** C_w·dT_w/dt = P_w − G_wh·(T_w − T_h);
   C_h·dT_h/dt = P_h + G_wh·(T_w − T_h) − G_ha·(T_h − T_amb). Copper and iron loss are assigned to the winding node;
   friction, gear and switching loss to the housing node unless the card assigns them elsewhere. The assignment is
   data on the bound card, so the ledger can attribute heat by mechanism.
3. **Loss routing.** Each electromechanical element reports its dissipation ∇Hᵀ·R·∇H per step (N15a-05); the
   composite routes it as Q̇ into the assigned node. Nothing is dissipated "to nowhere": a loss term with no node is
   a construction error caught at bind (FROB-REF-130, reported as an unaccounted loss).
4. **First-law identity of the composite, per step.** Σ port work in (electrical, shaft) = Δ(magnetic + rotor
   kinetic + elastic) + ΔU of all nodes + heat to ambient + residual. Heat that is stored is stored energy: copper,
   iron, friction and gear losses are listed for attribution and are not added again as losses (counterexamples
   CX-090 and CX-142: a ledger that books heat entering the node as both stored and lost shows a deficit equal to
   that heat).
5. **Stepping on the referee.** The node equations are linear in T for given losses; they are stepped with the
   implicit midpoint rule in `f64`, which for a linear system is the discrete-gradient step and gives
   ΔU = h·(P̄_loss − G·(T̄ − T_amb)) exactly in exact arithmetic, T̄ being the midpoint temperature. The copper loss
   uses the resistance at T̄ (the coupling with the winding element is solved together with it at L2).
6. **Entropy production.** For one node at T_w (kelvin) with total assigned loss P_loss and conductance G to
   ambient at T_amb: the node gains entropy at P_loss/T_w − G·(T_w − T_amb)/T_w, the ambient gains
   G·(T_w − T_amb)/T_amb, and the total is

     σ = P_loss/T_w + G·(T_w − T_amb)²/(T_w·T_amb)  ≥ 0  whenever P_loss ≥ 0.

   For the two-node network (DESIGN, same derivation applied per conductance):
   σ = P_w/T_w + P_h/T_h + G_wh·(T_w − T_h)²/(T_w·T_h) + G_ha·(T_h − T_amb)²/(T_h·T_amb).
7. **The per-step test (INV-SINEW-03: entropy production of every thermal node is nonnegative each step).** After
   each step: (a) every individually assigned loss term (copper, iron, viscous, stiction, gear, switching, each
   braking mode) is ≥ −ε_P; (b) σ ≥ −ε_σ, with ε_P and ε_σ equal to 10⁻¹² times the sum of the absolute values of
   the terms (round-off only). A violation raises the fault `second-law-violation`, naming the node and the first
   negative term; the episode's terminal outcome kind is `numerical` (verdict unknown, never counted as a physical
   failure and never dropped from a denominator), and the event is written to the Energetics telemetry stream.
8. **Units.** Card temperatures are authored in degrees Celsius and converted to kelvin once at bind; every
   formula above uses kelvin. A node at or below 0 K is a refusal at bind (unit error, FROB-REF-102).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For the motor axis with one thermal node, Σ port work − Δ stored (magnetic, kinetic, thermal) − heat to
      ambient is zero to 10⁻⁹ relative per step on the referee over a 10 s run (feeds GATE-SINEW-02 in BD-076).
- [ ] Over 10⁶ random operating points in all four shaft quadrants, with temperatures drawn from [250 K, 450 K],
      every assigned loss and σ are nonnegative (the entropy half of GATE-SINEW-08; measured at scale in N15a-23).
- [ ] The sign control fires: a regenerative mode with a negated copper loss gives σ < 0, the fault names the
      winding node and the copper term, and the outcome kind is `numerical`.
- [ ] The double-count control fires: a planted ledger that books winding heat as both stored and lost shows a
      deficit equal to that heat and is flagged.
- [ ] A loss term with no node assignment is refused at bind.
- [ ] Constant-resistance steady state: with P = 80 W and G = 0.80 W/K the rise is 100 K (ΔT = P/G), within 1%.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `node_steady_state_ka333`: P = 80 W, G = 0.80 W/K, C = 120 J/K, constant resistance: T − T_amb → 100.0 K; time
  constant C/G = 150 s (at t = 150 s the rise is 63.21 K within 0.5%).
- `entropy_formula_values`: P_loss = 80 W, T_w = 398.15 K, T_amb = 298.15 K, G = 0.80 W/K:
  σ = 80/398.15 + 0.80·100²/(398.15·298.15) = 0.20093 + 0.06739 = 0.26832 W/K. At equilibrium the node's own
  entropy rate is zero and σ equals the ambient's gain, 80/298.15 = 0.26832 W/K.
- `entropy_nonnegative_property`: the 10⁶-point property at reduced count (10⁴) in the default test run, full
  count under the gate profile.
- `negated_copper_loss_caught` and `double_count_caught`: the two controls above.
- `two_node_reduction`: with G_wh → large the two-node network reproduces the one-node response with
  C = C_w + C_h and G = G_ha within 10⁻⁶.
- `kelvin_conversion_once`: a card at 25 degC binds to 298.15 K; a node at −300 degC refuses.
- `runaway_fixture_c38`: R₀ = 0.1 Ω, α = 0.0039 K⁻¹, G = 0.5 W/K: stepping at 30 A settles and at 40 A grows; the
  switch lies at √(0.5/0.00039) = 35.8 A (the closed forms themselves are N15a-14's).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_referee_dynamics.sh` (N15a-24) records per step the minimum of σ over nodes and the minimum
assigned loss, and asserts both ≥ 0 within tolerance over a four-quadrant excitation; it then re-runs with the
planted negated loss and asserts that the fault is raised at the first braking step. Logged: node, T in kelvin,
P_loss by mechanism, σ with unit W/K and threshold, the fault event with its step index, outcome kind, seed.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-186 (σ ≥ 0 whenever P_loss ≥ 0; control: a regenerative mode with a negated copper loss gives σ < 0 and is
rejected). KA-184 (ledger of a motor axis in the three modes, motoring, regenerative braking, dissipative braking:
supplied = stored change + dissipated + exported; control: the double count). KA-185 (critical current 35.8 A for
the C-38 fixture). KA-294 with INV-SINEW-03 (10⁶ points). KA-333 (rise P/G within 1%). CX-090, CX-091, CX-142.
Metamorphic relation: scaling all powers, conductances and heat capacities by c scales σ by c and leaves the
temperature trajectories unchanged (scaling powers and conductances alone leaves only the equilibria unchanged,
since the time constants C/G then change).
Determinism: the referee lane twice on one machine with identical digests; debug and release.

## Considerations

- Lumped thermal parameters are card values, `Estimated` unless calibrated; a single node hides gradients inside
  the winding, which is a stated validity limit, not something this test can detect.
- The fixture of construction C-38 (I_crit = 35.8 A) and the worked card of the thermal chapter (I_crit = 31.9 A)
  are two fixtures for one formula; the registry keeps both under separate identities and neither replaces the
  other.
- On batched tiers thermal nodes advance on a slower rational clock (N15a-07); the same sign test runs at each
  thermal tick on the accumulated heat.
