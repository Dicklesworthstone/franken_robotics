## Background

On the referee, actuator elements step with discrete-gradient integrators in `f64` (N15a-05). The batched tiers
(`batch-cpu` and the GPU tiers under the lockstep profile) cannot use that code: they run fixed-shape kernels in
`f32`, with no data-dependent control flow across worlds and no transcendental built-ins, at a physics step that is
longer than the electrical time constant of a winding. ADR-SINEW-07 decides how the stiff and slow states are
advanced there: stiff electrical and thermal states take implicit rational updates, and slow states run on their
own rational clocks. Reason: the electrical time constant L/R is below a physics tick, an explicit update is
unstable there, and batched kernels use no transcendental built-ins. Example: L = 100 µH and R = 0.20 Ω give
L/R = 0.5 ms; at a 400 Hz physics step h = 2.5 ms, explicit Euler multiplies a current error by |1 − h·R/L| = 4
per step, while the implicit update multiplies it by 1/(1 + h·R/L) = 1/6.

The plan states the arithmetic in one paragraph; this bead turns it into a host reference implementation with a
stated residual for each choice, so that the kernel bead (seed BD-151: `K-act-l1`, `K-act-l2`, `K-passive`,
`K-act-bus`) lowers a tested program instead of re-deriving it, and so that every kernel has the CPU reference
path the definition of "implemented" requires. Rejected: sub-stepping the explicit update (a data-dependent
sub-step count breaks fixed shapes); an exponential integrator (needs `exp`); stepping thermal nodes every physics
step (their time constants are minutes; the increment per step is below single-precision resolution of the
temperature, which is exactly why the heat is accumulated and applied on a slow clock).

## Technical approach

1. **Current update (L2), per axis.** i₊ = (i + (h/L)·(v − k·ω)) / (1 + h·R(T_tick)/L), where v is the applied
   voltage after the voltage limit, k the power-invariant modulus, ω the rotor speed at the start of the step, and
   R(T_tick) = R₀·[1 + α·(T_tick − T₀)] the resistance at the temperature of the last thermal tick. Only +, −, ×
   and ÷ occur. In dq the same update is applied to both axes with the cross terms ω_e·L_q·i_q and
   ω_e·(L_d·i_d + λ) evaluated at the start of the step; flux uses k(T_tick) = k₀·[1 + α_B·(T_tick − T₀)].
   **Iron loss (L2), BD-071's law under the OPEN-SINEW-3 default.** When the card gives `iron_loss` coefficients,
   P_fe = k_h·abs(ω_e) + k_e·ω_e² with ω_e = p·ω at the start of the step (k_h in W/(rad/s), k_e in W/(rad/s)²).
   Its mechanical drag is never formed as the quotient P_fe/ω (singular at rest, and its first term chatters about
   zero speed): the eddy share N²·k_e·p² is added to the implicit viscous damping on the joint's diagonal, and the
   hysteresis share is exported as the constant `iron_coulomb` = abs(N)·k_h·p, which the stiction row adds to its
   bound (K-joint-rows; N15a-08, seed BD-155). Both shares are per-card constants of the program's parameter
   slot. The heat booked into the winding node's accumulator on the `iron` line is the dissipation the two shares
   produce in the step (the eddy damping's, and the `iron` share of the stiction row's, which the row reports), as
   on the referee, so the node and the line agree to round-off; at steady speed it is h·P_fe. A card without
   coefficients gives zero for all three and carries BD-071's flag `iron loss not modelled` (the condition
   `iron-loss-not-modelled`) on every result, as on the referee.
2. **Its residual.** Multiplying the update by i₊ gives the discrete energy statement
   ½·L·(i₊² − i²) = h·i₊·(v − k·ω) − h·R·i₊² − ½·L·(i₊ − i)². With port work booked as h·v·i₊, conversion work as
   h·k·ω·i₊ and copper heat as h·R·i₊², the balance residual of the winding is exactly ½·L·(i₊ − i)² ≥ 0 per step:
   energy the implicit update removes numerically. It is booked on the residual line, never as copper loss.
3. **L1 arithmetic.** With s the sign of the current reference, the available current is
   i_avail = min(I_max, max(0, (V_lim − s·k_e·N·ω)/R)) and the target is i_tgt = s·min(abs(i_ref), i_avail),
   written with `min`, `max` and `select`. This is the referee's L1 law (BD-070): more current is available
   when braking than when motoring at the same speed, so the speed enters with the sign of the reference
   and not as a magnitude. Then: output torque η·N·k_t·i before the friction row; reflected inertia N²·J_rotor added to the
   joint's diagonal; first-order delay as the implicit rational update y₊ = (y + (h/τ_d)·u)/(1 + h/τ_d).
4. **Slow thermal clock.** Every period is an exact rational number of seconds; the thermal period H_s is an
   integer multiple n_s of the physics step, with an integer phase accumulator, and the thermal update fires when
   the integer step counter is congruent to its phase modulo n_s. No floating-point time is compared. The period is
   part of the model's clock contract and of `ModelId`.
5. **Heat accumulation.** Each physics step adds its heat (copper h·R·i₊², plus the losses assigned to the node)
   to a per-node accumulator held as a compensated sum (a value and a running compensation term, two `f32`), so
   that n_s small increments are not lost to round-off. At a thermal tick the accumulated heat Q is applied and the
   accumulator is reset to zero.
6. **Thermal update at a tick (one node).** C·(T₊ − T) = Q − H_s·G·(T₊ − T_amb), so
   T₊ = (C·T + Q + H_s·G·T_amb)/(C + H_s·G). Two nodes: the 2×2 implicit linear system of the winding–housing
   network solved in closed form (one division by its determinant, which is positive for positive conductances
   and capacities). Residual: none in the energy statement (the update is the energy balance over the period with
   the end-of-period temperature in the outflow); the accuracy cost is first order in H_s·G/C.
7. **Lagged resistance.** The fast update sees T_tick, not the current temperature. Heat is booked with the same
   R(T_tick), so the lag leaves no balance residual; it leaves a model difference from the referee bounded per
   tick by the temperature change over one period times s = i²·R₀·α.
8. **Program form (DESIGN).** `reference::l1::step<F: Real>(state, inputs, params) -> (state, PortBalance)` and
   `reference::l2::step<F: Real>`; no branches on data, no loops with data-dependent bounds, no `exp`, `ln`, `sin`,
   `cos` or `pow`; `sqrt` only where the voltage-limit projection needs a norm. A test parses the functions' call
   graph against the allowed set.
9. **Comparison battery (10⁴ cases; DESIGN, fixed here before any measurement; the plan fixes the size and the
   tolerance, not the cases).** This is the single definition: BD-151 re-measures it on kernels and N15b-35 runs
   it from the user's side as the fixture `sinew-act-fidelity`. Cases are drawn by address: a card from the
   catalog with each field drawn inside its stated uncertainty band; a level (L1 or L2); operating conditions
   (bus voltage or bus state, joint speed, command or load torque, ambient, initial winding temperature).
   Compared quantities, by class:
   (a) L1: current, output torque and electrical power once the delay state has settled;
   (b) L2: settled currents, output torque, copper loss and bus voltage per segment, each segment holding its
       inputs for at least 20 electrical time constants (after five the remaining transient is e⁻⁵, about
       0.7%, which is above the tolerance; after twenty it is below 10⁻⁸); on cards with `iron_loss`
       coefficients, the iron loss P_fe per segment and both drag shares (the eddy damping N²·k_e·p² and
       `iron_coulomb`), with the condition `iron-loss-not-modelled` compared for equality on cards without;
       winding and housing temperatures at the end of the case; electrical energy drawn and heat produced over
       the case (copper and iron lines apart);
   (c) band-limited transients whose fastest input time scale is at least 20 steps, as RMS error over the
       episode relative to the peak. These run on the same cases and are written to the fidelity ledger with an
       envelope declared before measurement; they are reported with the gate and are not judged at 10⁻⁴,
       because a first-order implicit update at 20 steps per time scale carries an error of the order of the
       step divided by that time scale, which is percent-level by construction.
   The relative error of a quantity x is abs(x_batched − x_referee)/max(abs(x_referee), floor), with the floor
   per quantity declared in the fixture so that values near zero do not dominate. The referee side is refined
   by step halving until its own change is below 10⁻⁶. Operating points at which the referee's exact hexagon
   admits more voltage than the inscribed circle are expected conservative differences (at most 13.4% of
   voltage): they are counted, reported in their own ledger row with the sign checked (the batched tier never
   delivers more), and not counted against the tolerance. Transients faster than the step are outside the gate
   and are recorded in the fidelity ledger with their own envelope. Pass: relative difference ≤ 10⁻⁴ on every
   quantity of classes (a) and (b) of every case (GATE-SINEW-03's threshold, measured here on the host
   reference in `f64` and `f32` and re-measured on kernels by BD-151), with class (c) inside its envelope.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The current update is stable for h·R/L up to 10³ on a step input and converges to (v − k·ω)/R.
- [ ] The L2 reference carries BD-071's iron-loss law: P_fe, the eddy damping N²·k_e·p² and `iron_coulomb` =
      abs(N)·k_h·p equal the referee component's values (to 10⁻¹² relative in `f64`, 10⁻⁶ in `f32`), no quotient
      P_fe/ω is formed and the values are finite at ω = 0, the `iron` heat into the winding node equals the
      `iron` line, and a card without coefficients yields zero with `iron-loss-not-modelled`; the battery's class
      (b) includes these quantities on cards with coefficients.
- [ ] The winding's per-step balance residual equals ½·L·(i₊ − i)² to round-off, on 10⁴ random steps.
- [ ] Accumulating 10⁴ increments of 10⁻⁴ J into a node at 10³ J in `f32` loses less than 10⁻⁶ relative with the
      compensated sum, and measurably more with a plain sum (the control).
- [ ] The thermal tick fires exactly ⌊(n − φ)/n_s⌋ + 1 times up to step counter n, for 10³ random (n, φ, n_s).
- [ ] The reference programs contain no call outside the allowed set.
- [ ] On the 10⁴-case battery the host reference agrees with the referee components within 10⁻⁴ relative on every
      compared quantity, in `f64` and in `f32`.
- [ ] Per-episode port-balance statistic |Σ residual| / Σ |port work| ≤ 10⁻⁴ in `f32` on the gate fixtures of
      BD-076 once the ½·L·(i₊ − i)² term is booked on the residual line and reported (the single-precision row of
      GATE-SINEW-02, measured on devices by BD-208).

- [ ] The passivity battery `sinew-passivity` of N15a-23, replayed through the host reference at its smoke
      profile (10⁴ addresses, every shaft quadrant, all three `regen_policy` values) in `f64` and `f32`: zero
      negative loss terms, zero negative entropy productions, and the numerical dissipation ½·L·(i₊ − i)²
      reported per address as its own nonnegative term; the minimum of every term is logged beside N15a-23's
      referee minimum for the same addresses.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `implicit_current_step_values`: L = 100 µH, R = 0.20 Ω, k = 0, h = 2.5 ms, i = 0, v = 1 V: h/L = 25 A/V and
  h·R/L = 5, so i₊ = 25/(1 + 5) = 4.1667 A; the fixed point is v/R = 5 A; after 5 steps the error is
  5·(1/6)⁵ = 6.4·10⁻⁴ A.
- `explicit_euler_unstable_control`: the same case with the explicit update diverges (error ×4 per step).
- `winding_residual_identity`: the identity of step 2 on random inputs.
- `thermal_tick_update`: C = 120 J/K, G = 0.80 W/K, T = T_amb = 25 °C, Q = 80 J over H_s = 1 s:
  T₊ − T = 80/(120 + 0.8) = 0.6623 K.
- `compensated_sum_vs_plain`, `rational_clock_firing_count`, `allowed_call_set`: as in the acceptance criteria.
- `lagged_resistance_no_residual`: with R frozen over a period the node's energy statement closes to round-off.
- `iron_loss_host_reference`: BD-071's card of `iron_loss_law_and_split` (p = 7, k_h = 0.002 W/(rad/s),
  k_e = 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ W/(rad/s)², N = 1) at ω = 100 rad/s: P_fe = 1.4 + 0.49 = 1.89 W, eddy damping
  4.9 × 10⁻⁵ N·m·s/rad, `iron_coulomb` = 0.014 N·m, equal to the referee's in `f64` to 10⁻¹² relative and in
  `f32` to 10⁻⁶; over 400 steps at constant ω = 100 rad/s and h = 2.5 ms the `iron` heat booked is
  400·h·P_fe = 1.89 J; at ω = 0 all values are finite; with N = 9 the eddy damping is 81 times larger
  (3.969 × 10⁻³ N·m·s/rad) and `iron_coulomb` 0.126 N·m; a card without coefficients gives zero and the
  condition `iron-loss-not-modelled`.
- `passivity_battery_host_reference`: the replay of the acceptance criterion; the control is the negated
  copper loss in the regenerative braking mode (N15a-23's planted failure), which the replay must catch on the
  host reference as it does on the referee.

### End-to-end script and logging
The host reference has no verb of its own. Its battery is run from the user's side by
`scripts/e2e/sinew_kernels_fidelity.sh` (N15b-35, M2, which depends on this bead and on the kernels of seed
BD-151): `frob task fixture sinew-act-fidelity` with `--tier batch-cpu --precision f32 --key <k>` against the
referee run under its own key
(the M1 script `sinew_referee_dynamics.sh` of N15a-24 carries only the single-precision port-balance row, run by
seed BD-208 at M2, not this comparison). Logged, per STD-E2E: per compared
quantity the largest relative difference with threshold `1e-4`, the class (c) RMS errors against their declared
envelope, the count and sign of hexagon-versus-circle conservative points, the per-episode balance statistic with
threshold `1e-4`, the thermal period and step as rationals, digests per tier, and on failure the first differing
case address. Until N15b-35 lands, `cargo test -p frob-actuate --test reference_battery -- --nocapture` prints
the same quantities as `metric` lines from the host reference.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-296 (batched L1 and L2 kernels against the referee components on a 10⁴-case battery, 10⁻⁴ relative). KA-295
(single-precision row). KA-331 (electrical time constant L/R) and KA-333 (thermal rise P/G) are reproduced by the
reference at 1%. Negative controls: the explicit update; a plain `f32` sum; thermal state stepped with the current
(not the tick) temperature while heat is booked at the tick temperature (the node's statement no longer closes);
the residual ½·L·(i₊ − i)² silently added to copper loss (the port balance looks perfect and the copper heat is
wrong; caught by comparing copper heat with the referee); iron loss dropped from the host reference (caught by
class (b) on every card with coefficients, while cards without still pass). Determinism lanes: `batch-cpu` twice and across worker
counts with identical digests (class D2); `f32` against `f64` within the stated tolerance; debug and release.

## Considerations

- **What the 10⁻⁴ applies to.** The implicit update is strongly damped and does not track sub-step electrical
  transients of the referee, by design. The battery therefore compares settled values and integrals at matched
  refinement. OPEN (owner: Sinew lead; both drafting readings of this gate asked for confirmation): whether
  band-limited transients, class (c), are ever brought inside the 10⁻⁴ judgment. Default until decided: they
  stay outside it, and their ledger envelope is enforced as a failure of the gate run when exceeded. If a consumer needs the transient (a current-loop stability analysis does), it uses the exact
  sampled-loop map of the analysis crate, not this kernel. This reading of the gate is recorded for the owner.
- KILL-SINEW-ROWS and the kernel-toolchain kill gate do not affect this arithmetic; if the derivation toolchain is
  withdrawn, the reference program is hand-translated to the dialect and this bead's tests become its differential
  check.
- Performance envelope: the L2 update is a few dozen flops per actuator per step; the accumulator adds two floats
  per node.
