## Background

Stiction and backlash enter the solve as rows (N15a-08, N15a-09; solved on the referee by seed BD-084 and on the
batched tiers by seed BD-155). Six hand-checkable cases say whether the rows behave as friction and lost motion
really do. Their gate is GATE-SINEW-04 (claim: Known answer): breakaway within one step of τ_s/r; amplitude loss
per cycle within 2% of 4·τ_c/k and rest inside the band; drift at most ε·t; zero output motion inside the gap; loop
width within 1% of β; closure speed and energy loss within 1%. Referee exact; batched tiers within their stated
regularization. Earliest milestone: M1 on the referee, M2 on the batched tiers.

This bead owns the fixtures, the end-to-end script and the gate record for the referee row. The same script is
extended with a batched section and re-run with `--tier` set to a batched tier by N15b-37 at M2 (BD-155
checks the six cases at kernel level with a reference Newton driver, before the batched step exists). A gate that nothing can fail validates nothing, so each
case has a planted defect it must catch.

## Scope

In: six single-joint rigs as `.fbot` fixtures with their cards, each in two parameter sets and registered so
that `frob task fixture` can run them; the open-loop excitation for each; the measurement from telemetry;
the thresholds per tier; the referee section of `scripts/e2e/sinew_joint_rows.sh`; the negative controls
and their registration; the referee row of the gate receipt. This is the only bead that measures the
referee row of GATE-SINEW-04 from the user's side. Out: the row mechanisms (BD-084, from the descriptors of
N15a-08 and N15a-09); rotor elimination and its gate (N15b-08); the fidelity-ledger quantities for creep
and step dependence (N15a-11); the batched section of the same script, the batched-tier row of the gate
and GATE-SINEW-09 (N15b-37); the kernel-level check of the six cases (BD-155); the values of the contact
chapter's two joint-row known answers KA-402 and KA-403, which are other fixtures with other numbers (a ramp
with τ_s = 2 N·m and 4 N·m/s; a 0.5° gap behind a ratio of 10) and 1% thresholds, owned by N18a-32 as
`joint_row_known_answers()`; their batched measurement for GATE-TRAC-04 (`scripts/e2e/traction_cone_rows.sh`,
N18a-36, M2; and rows 14 and 15 of the Traction battery, N18b-32). In, for GATE-TRAC-04's referee share: the
referee runs of KA-402 and KA-403 are BD-084's rows (`breakaway_on_ramp`, `gap_crossing_time`), and this
script drives them through `frob` as two extra steps asserted against `joint_row_known_answers()` at 1%. The
dead-zone control is one compiled mutant, `mutant-backlash-deadzone`, built with the referee rows (BD-084):
this bead registers it against GATE-SINEW-04 and runs it on cases 5 and 6 and on the KA-403 step, and N18b-32
runs the same build on its row 15.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures (synthetic; every number chosen for this battery, none measured).** Under
   `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sinew/joint_rows/`, each a one-link rig with a revolute joint, gravity off,
   and a card in `assets/cards/fixtures/` whose every field is `assumed`.

   | # | Case | Rig and excitation | Expected | Threshold |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | Torque ramp r·t on a joint at rest with breakaway τ_s | J = 0.02 kg·m², τ_s = 0.30 N·m, τ_c = 0.20 N·m, r = 1.0 N·m/s | Motion begins at t = τ_s/r = 0.300 s | Within one step h |
   | 2 | A mass on a spring of stiffness k with Coulomb friction τ_c, released from rest | J = 0.02 kg·m², k = 20 N·m/rad, τ_c = 0.20 N·m, τ_s = 0.30 N·m, v_s = 10⁻³ rad/s, released at 0.25 rad | Amplitude falls by 4·τ_c/k = 0.040 rad per cycle (turning points 0.25, 0.23, 0.21, …, 0.03, 0.01 rad in magnitude); motion ends inside the band of half-width τ_s/k = 0.015 rad, at 0.01 rad | Loss per cycle within 2%; rest point inside the band |
   | 3 | A held load τ below the bound on a convex tier | τ_b = 0.30 N·m, τ = 0.15 N·m, ε = 10⁻³ rad/s, 10 s | Drift at most ε·t = 0.010 rad (predicted 0.005 rad); zero on the referee | Bound; referee exact |
   | 4 | A motor reversal smaller than the gap | L3 rig, β = 0.020 rad, rotor moved ±0.008 rad (joint-equivalent) about the gap centre, output free and at rest | Zero output motion | Exact on the referee; below the compliant penetration bound on batched tiers |
   | 5 | A slow sinusoid through a gap β | L3 rig, β = 0.020 rad, rotor angle θ/N = 0.10·sin(2π·0.1·t) rad, output held by friction | Input–output loop of width β = 0.020 rad; output amplitude 0.10 − β/2 = 0.090 rad | Width within 1% |
   | 6 | A rotor of reflected inertia J₁ = N²·J_r closing the gap at relative speed Δω onto a link of inertia J₂ at rest, no restitution | N = 9, J_r = 10⁻⁴ kg·m², J₂ = 0.02 kg·m², Δω = 2 rad/s | Common speed J₁·Δω/(J₁ + J₂) = 0.577 rad/s; energy lost ½·J₁·J₂/(J₁ + J₂)·Δω² = 0.0115 J | Both within 1% |

   **Second parameter set, registered under fixture names** (`synthetic`, each with a manifest
   `assets/fixtures/<name>/fixture.toml` in the fixture registry of N24b-03; step h = 2 ms, so that "within one
   step" is a statement about a fixed step): `ramp-breakaway` (r = 1 N·m/s, τ_s = 0.5 N·m; onset 0.5 s);
   `spring-coulomb` (k = 10 N·m/rad, τ_c = 0.1 N·m, τ_s = 0.12 N·m, released from 0.2 rad; 0.04 rad lost per
   cycle; rest inside ±0.012 rad); `held-load` (0.3 N·m against τ_b = 0.5 N·m for 20 s); `reversal-in-gap`
   (β = 0.5°, motor reversal of 0.3° on the output side); `gap-hysteresis` (reflected amplitude 2°,
   β = 0.5°); `gap-closure` (the numbers of case 6). Each case is asserted on both sets; two sets per case
   make a fixture tuned to one number visible. Each of the six manifests, and those of `trac-ramp-breakaway` and
   `trac-gap-crossing` (step 4), declares its run axes before its first run (N24bx-01's `axes` section), so that
   N15b-37 and N18a-36 can run the same fixtures on the batched tiers without a new fixture version:
   `[axes.seed] values = ["9"]`; `[axes.profile] values = ["referee", "lockstep", "strict"] default =
   "referee"`; `[axes.precision] values = ["f64", "f32"] default = "f64"`. An undeclared value is refused by
   `task.fixture` with FROB-REF-530 (`pointer = axes.<axis>`); DESIGN: `referee` is listed and is the default so
   that the referee runs of this bead select a profile the referee registers (`lockstep` and `strict` are the
   batched profiles those beads pass explicitly).
   **Excitations.** Each case's drive is an open-loop schedule (`frob.openloop/1`, N15ap3-01) registered here
   under `fixtures/controllers/open-loop/`: `jr-ramp` (torque ramp from 0 at r, `hold-last`), `jr-release` (zero
   torque; the rig starts at the release angle), `jr-held-load` (torque held at τ), `jr-reversal` (position on the
   motor-side encoder, joint-equivalent ±0.008 rad about the gap centre, a triangle of period 2 s, with kp and kd
   written in the schedule stiff enough that the rotor tracks within 10⁻⁴ rad), `jr-sine` (motor-side position 0.10·sin(2π·0.1·t) rad, joint-equivalent) and
   `jr-closure` (zero torque; the rig starts with the rotor at Δω relative to the link). The registered second
   set carries its schedules in the fixture manifest (the controller a fixture declares).
2. **Measurements.** Case 1: the first step at which |q̇| exceeds 10⁻⁶ rad/s on the referee (a located slip event)
   or ε on convex tiers. Case 2: successive turning points from zero crossings of q̇; the loss per full cycle; the
   final rest angle. Case 3: q(10 s) − q(0). Case 4: max |q − q(0)|. Case 5: the horizontal width of the closed
   curve of q against θ/N at q = 0 over the last full period. Case 6: link speed immediately after the closure
   event (referee) or after the compliant contact settles (convex tiers), and the drop in kinetic energy across it.
3. **"Referee exact; batched tiers within their stated regularization."** On the referee: case 1 within one step,
   cases 3 and 4 exactly zero, cases 2, 5 and 6 at the percentages above. On batched tiers the stated
   regularization is ε for stiction (case 3 bounded by ε·t; case 1 measured at the ε threshold) and the tooth
   compliance for backlash (case 4 bounded by the static deflection τ/k_tooth of the applied torque; cases 5 and 6
   at their percentages with the compliance and dissipation time printed beside the result).
4. **Run from the user's side.** `frob doctor --json` first, for pins and the hardware fingerprint. Then, per
   rig, `frob sim run <rig> --tier <tier> --controller open-loop:<schedule> --seconds <T> --telemetry T2 --seed 1
   --key jr-<case>-<tier> --json` (`--controller` on the referee at M1 is N16ap4-01's input, its `open-loop:`
   form N15ap3-01's), then `frob telemetry query <run> --channels joint.q,joint.qd,rotor.theta --json` and `frob
   telemetry query <run> --channels energy.kinetic --json`, one query per stream (kinematics, energetics), since a
   query whose channels belong to two streams is 802 (N24a-36); the registered second set runs through `frob
   task fixture <name> --tier referee --profile referee --seed 9 --key jr-<name>-referee --json` (the fixture
   verb of N24b-03 with N24bx-01's axes). Every run-kind call carries its own key; repeated runs that are
   compared carry distinct keys. No internal API is called by the script. Event counts are asserted with the values: the
   held load shows zero slip events and a displacement below 10⁻¹² rad after 20 s on the referee; the
   reversal inside the gap shows zero closure events and exactly zero output displacement; the closure case
   shows exactly one closure event. Determinism: the closure case is run twice (keys `jr-closure-a`,
   `jr-closure-b`), and once more on the chaos build (cargo feature `chaos`) with a forced retry of the step that
   contains the closure (`--chaos-plan fixtures/chaos/jr-closure-retry1.toml`, BD-083's chaos-build input, schema
   `frob.chaos.retry/1`, whose one entry `{ world = 0, base_interval = <the interval of the closure>,
   fail_attempts_below = 1 }` fails that interval's checks on attempt 0; key `jr-closure-retry`); the three
   digests are equal (a retried step equals a clean one).
   **Planted controls from the user's side.** On a build with the cargo feature `gauntlet-mutations`, `frob task
   fixture <name> --tier referee --profile referee --seed 9 --control <mutant> --key jr-<name>-<mutant> --json`
   (N24b-03's `control` input; `sim.run`'s `--mutant` is N21b-42's, M2): `mutant-backlash-deadzone` on
   `gap-hysteresis` and `gap-closure`, and `mutant-stiction-prev-velocity` on `held-load`, each of which must
   fail the assertion of its case.
   **GATE-TRAC-04, referee share (two extra steps).** `frob task fixture trac-ramp-breakaway --tier referee
   --profile referee --seed 9 --key jr-trac-ramp --json` (KA-402: breakaway at τ_s/k = 0.5 s for τ_s = 2 N·m,
   k = 4 N·m/s) and `frob task fixture trac-gap-crossing --tier referee --profile referee --seed 9 --key
   jr-trac-gap --json` (KA-403: crossing time 17.45 ms, closure speed
   0.0833 rad/s, energy lost 1.04 mJ), each asserted at 1% against the values of N18a-32's
   `joint_row_known_answers()` (read through the conformance crate, never restated in the script); then the
   KA-403 step again under `mutant-backlash-deadzone` (`frob task fixture trac-gap-crossing --tier referee
   --profile referee --seed 9 --control mutant-backlash-deadzone --key jr-trac-gap-deadzone --json`, on the
   `gauntlet-mutations` build), which must fail it with no closure event. The two
   fixture names are DESIGN; they are registered by this bead in the fixture registry of N24b-03 with BD-084's
   rigs.
5. **Gate receipt.** The gate's rows in the Gauntlet's Known-answer target through the STD-E2E helper, with its
   registered controls: `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SINEW-04 --mutations` (KA-297 to KA-302, G1 in N15-KA).
   The script asserts from the receipt (`e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) that its `gates` entry for
   GATE-SINEW-04 passes on the referee with the six measured values, the step h, ε, k_tooth, the commit, the seed
   and the hardware fingerprint, and that each control of its `controls` is caught, and lists the receipt hash in
   the summary. It never
   runs `record_gate` and never writes `registries/gates.toml` (STD-E2E's `self-recording` rule). This bead is
   the one recorder of the referee part of GATE-SINEW-04 (its batched part is N15b-37's): a fresh-context

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The measurement helpers are unit-tested on synthetic traces (labelled `synthetic`):
- `turning_points_from_trace`: a generated decaying trace with turning points 0.25, −0.23, 0.21 returns a loss per
  cycle of 0.040 rad.
- `loop_width_from_trace`: a generated hysteresis curve of width 0.020 rad returns 0.020 within 10⁻⁶.
- `breakaway_step_detection`: a trace that leaves rest at step 601 with h = 0.5 ms returns 0.3005 s.
- `kinetic_drop_across_event`: speeds (2, 0) → (0.5765, 0.5765) with J₁ = 0.0081 and J₂ = 0.02 return 0.01153 J.

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_sinew_04_referee.rs`: `receipt_schema`; `gate_has_negative_controls`;
`expected_values` (0.5 s; 0.04 rad; 0.012 rad; 0.577 rad/s from 0.0081·2/0.0281; 0.0115 J from
½·(0.0081·0.02/0.0281)·4, evaluated in exact rationals).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_joint_rows.sh [--tiers T,…]` (default `referee` at M1) sources the shared helper of the end-to-end harness and writes
newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_joint_rows/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable
transcript beside it. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` (N15a-12, with BD-084 or BD-155 on tier-specific
steps), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code,
wall time, and the envelope's evidence block (claim Known answer, class, tier, determinism, `ModelId`, seed). Each
`metric` line gives the measured value with its unit and threshold: `breakaway_time_s`, `amplitude_loss_per_cycle`,
`rest_angle`, `hold_drift`, `output_motion_inside_gap`, `loop_width`, `closure_speed`, `closure_energy_loss`,
and the event counts (stick, slip, closure). State
digests are logged for the two repeat runs. On failure the line carries the first differing value, the step index
and the paths of the run and the fixture. The final `summary` line counts assertions passed and failed, lists
`GATE-SINEW-04` and `GATE-TRAC-04` (referee share) and carries the receipt hash of step 5. No private-scene data
is involved.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-297 (case 1), KA-298 (case 2), KA-299 (case 3), KA-300 (case 4), KA-301 (case 5), KA-302 (case 6); KA-181 and
KA-182 of construction C-37 are the same ramp and loop under another identity. CX-172 (backlash as a dead
zone on the torque command). Two of the planted failures are compiled mutants run through the conformance
harness: `mutant-backlash-deadzone` must fail case 6 (no impact) and case 5 (no loop of width β);
`mutant-stiction-prev-velocity` must fail case 3 by chattering (nonzero slip events). Planted failures, each
registered with the case that must catch it:
- friction as a force from the previous velocity (case 3: unbounded drift or chatter; case 1: late or early
  breakaway);
- a breakaway level equal to the Coulomb level (case 1 starts at 0.200 s instead of 0.300 s);
- backlash as a dead zone on the torque command (case 6: no impact, the link speed and energy loss are wrong;
  case 5: no loop) — the control the seed bead for the referee rows names;
- a gap of β/2 instead of β (case 5 width 0.010 rad);
- a rigid mesh with no gap (case 4: the output moves);
- restitution 1 in place of 0 (case 6: no energy lost).
Metamorphic relations: doubling r halves the breakaway time; doubling k halves the loss per cycle and the band;
mirroring the excitation mirrors every trace. Determinism lanes: each case twice on one machine with identical
digests; debug and release builds agree on the referee; across tiers the comparison is by threshold (class D3).

## Considerations

- OPEN-SINEW-2 (default Stribeck shape and ε on batched tiers) is decided at this gate: the case-3 drift and the
  case-1 timing at the default ε = 10⁻³ rad/s, together with the iteration counts of N15b-24, are its evidence.
- Case 2 uses a small Stribeck velocity so that the sliding level is τ_c for almost the whole stroke; with a large
  v_s the loss per cycle exceeds 4·τ_c/k and the case would test the fixture, not the row.
- The cart docking fixture of the arena subsystem (BD-123) also depends on these rows; a failure here shows
  there as creep at the sill.
- One script serves all tiers, which keeps the fixtures identical across tiers.
- Case 5 needs the output held by friction; the rig's output stiction bound is set above the tooth force at the
  slow drive rate, and the script asserts that precondition before measuring.
