## Background

Copper resistance rises with temperature, R(T) = R₀·[1 + α·(T − T₀)], so copper loss at a given current rises as
the winding heats, which heats it further. This positive feedback lengthens the thermal time constant, lowers the
continuous rating, and above a finite current removes the thermal equilibrium altogether (thermal runaway). A
sizing check that holds resistance constant misses all three. ADR-SINEW-04 therefore decides that winding
resistance and magnet flux depend on temperature from level L2 upward, and that the static thermal check uses the
temperature-dependent law at every level.

This bead is the pure library of closed forms and small solves behind that decision. It is called by the verb
`actuator.thermal` and the dynamic model (BD-072), by check 3 of `actuator.check` (BD-069), by analysis A-11 and
stage 6 of the design preflight (seed BD-103), and by the duty-history initialization (N15a-15). It needs no
simulation, so a design can be refused before any simulation is spent on it.

Counterexamples this library exists to prevent. CX-136: "copper resistance can be held constant in the thermal
check" (the worked card at 20 A: 125 °C by the constant model, 190 °C in truth). CX-137: "under torque holding the
runaway threshold can be estimated from the slope of the loss curve at T₀" (25.5 A by the slope, 16.5 A in truth).

## Scope

In: the one-node laws at constant current; the general stability criterion for any loss curve; the two-node
(winding and housing in series) condition and equilibrium; the torque-hold loss curve with its tangency; time to a
limit; the largest sustainable current; the margin to the tangency; the constant-resistance variants kept as a
labelled comparison. Out: the verb and refusal FROB-REF-132 (BD-072); stepping temperatures in time (N15a-06,
seed BD-071); duty histories (N15a-15); derating bands (BD-072).

## Technical approach

Symbols: I current; R₀ resistance at reference temperature T₀; α temperature coefficient of resistance; C heat
capacity; G conductance to ambient; T_amb ambient; T_max limit; θ = T − T_amb.

1. **One node, constant current.** C·dT/dt = I²·R₀·[1 + α·(T − T₀)] − G·(T − T_amb). Write s = I²·R₀·α (extra
   watts of loss per kelvin) and P_amb = I²·R₀·[1 + α·(T_amb − T₀)] (the loss at ambient temperature).
   - If s < G: a stable equilibrium at T = T_amb + P_amb/(G − s), approached with time constant C/(G − s). Both are
     larger than the constant-resistance values P_amb/G and C/G by the factor 1/(1 − s/G).
   - If s ≥ G: no equilibrium. The temperature grows without bound at rate (s − G)/C until a limit or a protection
     acts. The current at which this begins is I_crit = √(G/(R₀·α)).
2. **Transient and time to limit (derived from the linear equation).** With θ₀ the initial rise:
   s < G: θ(t) = θ_eq + (θ₀ − θ_eq)·e^(−t/τ), τ = C/(G − s); the limit θ_max is reached at
   t = τ·ln((θ_eq − θ₀)/(θ_eq − θ_max)) if θ_eq > θ_max, otherwise never.
   s > G: with a = (s − G)/C and θ_∞ = P_amb/(s − G), θ(t) = (θ₀ + θ_∞)·e^(a·t) − θ_∞; the limit is reached at
   t = (1/a)·ln((θ_max + θ_∞)/(θ₀ + θ_∞)). s = G: θ(t) = θ₀ + P_amb·t/C.
3. **Largest continuous current for a limit.** I²·R₀·[1 + α·(T_max − T₀)] = G·(T_max − T_amb), so
   I_cont = √(G·(T_max − T_amb)/(R₀·[1 + α·(T_max − T₀)])); the constant-resistance value √(G·θ_max/R₀) is too
   high by the factor √(1 + α·(T_max − T₀)).
4. **General criterion.** For any loss curve P(T), an operating point has a stable thermal equilibrium if and only
   if P(T) meets the removal line G·(T − T_amb) at a temperature where dP/dT < G. The library exposes this as a
   bracketed root search for the lowest intersection followed by the slope test; iron loss and other
   temperature-independent terms simply add to P.
5. **Network.** For nodes with the loss in the winding node, the condition is that the conductance matrix with s
   subtracted from the winding's diagonal entry stays positive definite. For a winding and a housing in series the
   matrix is [[G_wh − s, −G_wh], [−G_wh, G_wh + G_ha]], and the condition reads s < G_wh·G_ha/(G_wh + G_ha): the
   housing replaces G by the series conductance and lowers every threshold. The equilibrium rises solve that 2×2
   system with right-hand side (P_amb, P_housing).
6. **A controller that holds torque makes it worse.** Magnet flux falls with temperature,
   k(T) = k₀·[1 + α_B·(T − T₀)] with α_B negative, so holding torque needs more current as the motor heats, and the
   loss becomes P(T) = I₀²·R₀·[1 + α·(T − T₀)]/[1 + α_B·(T − T₀)]², where I₀ is the current that gives the torque
   when cold (the cold-equivalent current). This curve is convex, so the equilibrium vanishes at a tangency that
   lies well below the estimate from its slope at T₀, I = √(G/(R₀·(α − 2·α_B))). The linear formula is not
   conservative here, and the library solves the tangency: find (T*, I₀*) with P(T*) = G·(T* − T_amb) and
   dP/dT(T*) = G. When T₀ = T_amb, with u = T − T₀ and b = −α_B, the tangency rise is the positive root of
   2·α·b·u² + 3·b·u − 1 = 0, u* = [−3·b + √(9·b² + 8·α·b)]/(4·α·b), and
   I₀*² = G·u*·(1 − b·u*)²/(R₀·(1 + α·u*)) (derived here; the general case T₀ ≠ T_amb is solved by a bracketed
   Newton iteration on the two equations). The current at which the limit is reached under torque hold is
   I₀ = √(G·θ_max·[1 + α_B·(T_max − T₀)]²/(R₀·[1 + α·(T_max − T₀)])). The continuous rating under torque hold is
   the smaller of the two, and the result reports the margin to the tangency, (I₀* − I₀)/I₀*, not only the
   equilibrium temperature.
7. **API (DESIGN sketch).**
   ```rust
   pub enum Hold { Current(Qty<Ampere>), Torque { i_cold: Qty<Ampere> } }
   pub struct ThermalStatic { pub stable: bool, pub t_eq: Option<Qty<Kelvin>>, pub tau: Option<Qty<Second>>,
       pub growth_rate: Option<f64>, pub time_to_limit: Option<Qty<Second>>, pub i_crit: Qty<Ampere>,
       pub i_cont: Qty<Ampere>, pub tangency: Option<Tangency>, pub margin_to_tangency: Option<f64>,
       pub largest_sustainable: Qty<Ampere>, pub constant_r: ConstantRComparison }
   pub fn analyze(card: &ThermalView, net: Network, hold: Hold, t_amb: Qty<Kelvin>, t_start: Qty<Kelvin>)
       -> ThermalStatic;
   ```
   Arithmetic is `f64` with deterministic `sqrt`, `exp` and `ln` (class D2 per platform); inputs carry the card's
   uncertainty, and `analyze_band` evaluates the corners of the stated band so that a verdict that changes sign
   inside the band can be labelled card-conditional by the caller. `ThermalView` and `Network` are plain parameter
   structs defined in this module (R₀, T₀, α, α_B, C, G or the two-node C_w, C_h, G_wh, G_ha, T_max, iron-loss
   terms, each a quantity with its half-width): the library knows nothing of the card format, because check 3 of
   `actuator.check` (BD-069) calls it and BD-069 therefore comes after it; BD-069 owns `ThermalView::from_card`.

## Acceptance criteria

All on the worked card (illustrative; every number derived for the plan, none measured): R₀ = 0.20 Ω at
T₀ = 25 °C, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹, G = 0.80 W/K, C = 120 J/K, ambient 25 °C, winding limit 155 °C. Tolerance 0.5% unless
stated (GATE-SINEW-05's closed-form rows).

- [ ] At 20 A: 80 W when cold; s = 0.314 W/K; rise 80/(0.80 − 0.314) = 164.7 K, so 190 °C; loss at equilibrium
      132 W; time constant 247 s; time from cold to 155 °C is 385 s. Constant-resistance comparison: rise 100 K
      (125 °C), 150 s, limit never reached.
- [ ] Largest continuous current: 18.6 A (constant resistance: 22.8 A, 19% higher).
- [ ] I_crit = √(0.80/(0.20·0.00393)) = 31.9 A; the constant-resistance model has no such current.
- [ ] At 35 A: no equilibrium; growth rate 0.00136 s⁻¹; limit reached at 61 s (constant resistance: an equilibrium
      306 K above ambient, limit at 83 s).
- [ ] Torque hold with α_B = −0.0011 K⁻¹: limit reached at 15.9 A cold-equivalent (30% below 22.8 A); tangency at
      16.5 A within 1%, at a rise of 199 K; the slope estimate returns 25.5 A and is reported as non-conservative.
- [ ] Two-node condition: stable if and only if s < G_wh·G_ha/(G_wh + G_ha), exact against the matrix test.
- [ ] 20 s from cold at 20 A gives a rise of 164.7·(1 − e^(−20/247)) = 12.8 K.

transcendental rows only where the deterministic functions are unavailable (reported, not hidden).

## Considerations

- Validity: one or two lumped nodes; constant current, or constant torque in the last case; a copper coefficient
  linear in temperature; fixed ambient. Card values are `Estimated` unless calibrated.
- The continuous rating and the runaway threshold are close together under torque control (15.9 A and 16.5 A on the
  card). A design that sits just inside its continuous rating has almost no thermal margin against a slightly
  heavier payload, which is why the margin to the tangency is a first-class output.
- OPEN-SINEW-3 (iron loss on by default at L2: on when the card gives coefficients, otherwise zero with the flag
  `iron loss not modelled`): iron loss enters step 4 as an added term and raises every temperature.
