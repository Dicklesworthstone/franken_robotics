## Background

The transmission chapter of Sinew lists six kinds of transmission behaviour. The mechanism bead for transmissions
is seed BD-074 (gear, belt, screw, linkage, cable and coupling matrices with loss laws), owned by the other Sinew
drafting unit; the four-quadrant loss inside the stiction bound is N15a-17. This bead is the test battery that
holds every transmission statement of the chapter to a hand-checkable answer, so that none of them can be lost
between the mechanism beads:

- **Four-quadrant loss** (Solid). With ω_motor = N·ω_joint: when the motor drives, τ_joint = η_f·N·τ_motor; when
  the load drives, τ_motor = η_b·τ_joint/N. Both lose energy.
- **One multiplier in all quadrants creates energy** (the counterexample; refused with FROB-REF-130).
- **Self-locking** (Solid). A transmission with η_b ≤ 0 cannot be back-driven. For a screw with lead angle a and
  friction angle φ: η_f = tan a/tan(a + φ), η_b = tan(a − φ)/tan a; it locks exactly when φ ≥ a.
- **Coupling transmissions** (Solid). Differential ankles, wrists and waists drive several joints from several
  motors through a constant matrix: q̇ = T·θ̇ and τ_actuator = Tᵀ·τ_joint, which conserves power. The rotor
  inertias reflect into joint space as T⁻ᵀ·diag(J_r)·T⁻¹, which is not diagonal in general.
- **Linkage transmissions** (Solid). A ratio that depends on position is a closure in the design term, and its
  Jacobian is the transformer modulus at each configuration.
- **Belts, cables and series elasticity** (Frontier): an added coordinate per compliant element; N15b-18.

The counterexample the differential protects against (CX-140): "reflected rotor inertia is diagonal in joint
space". For a symmetric differential with unequal rotors it is not, and a controller model or a mass matrix that
assumes it is will be wrong in the coupling terms.

## Scope

In: fixtures, exact expected values and tests for the five Solid behaviours (four-quadrant gear, screw,
square coupling differential, linkage modulus through a closure, ideal gear law); the proof obligation
PO-SINEW-03 as an exact identity; registration of the counterexamples as negative controls. This is a test
bead and follows the mechanisms it exercises. Out, with the bead that has each: implementing the
transmissions (BD-074, N15a-17); the closure constructor the linkage fixture uses (BD-056); non-square
couplings, the torque zonotope and the direction witness (N15b-10, N15b-11, N15b-12; GATE-SINEW-10 is
measured by N15b-13); compliant transmissions (N15b-18); the 10⁶-point passivity battery (N15a-23); the
user-side script for GATE-SINEW-08, which repeats the gear and screw answers through the CLI (N15b-32).

## Technical approach

1. **Fixture set** under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sinew/transmissions/` (synthetic, labelled):

   | Fixture | Inputs | Expected, exact |
   |---|---|---|
   | `gear_back_driven` | (η_f, η_b) = (0.8, 0.8), N = 10, joint driven with 10 N·m at 1 rad/s | τ_motor = 0.8 N·m at 10 rad/s; 8 W out; 2 W lost |
   | `gear_single_multiplier` | η = 0.8 in all quadrants, same drive | Refused with FROB-REF-130; would give 1.25 N·m, 12.5 W, 2.5 W created |
   | `screw_lock_boundary` | a = 5°, φ ∈ {4°, 5°, 6°} | η_b = tan(a − φ)/tan a: 0.1995, 0, −0.1995; locking for φ ≥ a |
   | `differential_symmetric` | q₁ = (θ₁ + θ₂)/2, q₂ = (θ₁ − θ₂)/2; rotor inertias J₁, J₂ | Reflected inertia [[J₁ + J₂, J₁ − J₂], [J₁ − J₂, J₁ + J₂]]; equal rotors decouple, unequal ones do not |
   | `linkage_parallelogram` | A parallelogram four-bar as a closure | Modulus exactly 1 at every configuration |
   | `linkage_variable_ratio` | A crank driving a rocker through a closure, links (crank, coupler, rocker, ground) = (1, 4, 2, 4) units | Modulus from the closure Jacobian equals the derivative of the output angle with respect to the input angle at 100 configurations; power in equals power out at each |
2. **Differential, worked.** T = [[½, ½], [½, −½]], so T⁻¹ = [[1, 1], [1, −1]] and
   T⁻ᵀ·diag(J₁, J₂)·T⁻¹ = [[J₁ + J₂, J₁ − J₂], [J₁ − J₂, J₁ + J₂]]. With J₁ = J₂ = 10⁻⁴ kg·m² the matrix is
   diag(2·10⁻⁴, 2·10⁻⁴); with J₁ = 3·10⁻⁴ and J₂ = 10⁻⁴ it is [[4, 2], [2, 4]]·10⁻⁴ kg·m². Power:
   τ_actuatorᵀ·θ̇ = τ_jointᵀ·T·θ̇ = τ_jointᵀ·q̇ for every τ_joint and θ̇.
3. **Linkage modulus.** For a closure with constraint φ(θ_in, θ_out) = 0, the modulus is
   dθ_out/dθ_in = −(∂φ/∂θ_in)/(∂φ/∂θ_out), which is what the closure's Jacobian gives; the test compares it with a
   central finite difference of the solved position map (step 10⁻⁶ rad, agreement 10⁻⁸) and checks
   τ_in·θ̇_in + τ_out·θ̇_out = 0 as port quantities at each configuration. Near a singular configuration of the
   linkage (∂φ/∂θ_out → 0) the modulus is unbounded; the fixture's configurations stay away from it and one extra
   case asserts that the design preflight reports the singularity instead of returning a number.
4. **PO-SINEW-03: the ideal gear law conserves power: f₂ = r·f₁ and e₁ = −r·e₂ imply e₁·f₁ + e₂·f₂ = 0.** A
   polynomial identity (e₁·f₁ + e₂·f₂ = −r·e₂·f₁ + e₂·r·f₁ = 0), a candidate theorem card for the proof subsystem.
   Verification here: the identity is submitted to the exact polynomial-identity checker of the certificate crate
   and its instance certificate is stored with the fixture; a property test evaluates it on 10⁴ random rational
   triples (r, e₂, f₁) with exact zero. The same identity with the wrong sign (e₁ = +r·e₂) is the negative
   control: it gives 2·r·e₂·f₁ ≠ 0 and the checker must reject it.
5. **Registration.** Each fixture is a record of the negative-control registry where it is a control
   (`gear_single_multiplier` for GATE-SINEW-08; the diagonal-inertia mutant for the differential), and all join
   the `fixtures` battery that runs on every change touching actuators.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All six fixtures produce their expected values: exact in rational arithmetic where stated, and within 10⁻¹²
      relative in `f64` otherwise.
- [ ] `gear_single_multiplier` refuses with FROB-REF-130, naming the back-driven quadrant and 2.5 W created
      (a clause of GATE-SINEW-08).
- [ ] The differential's reflected inertia has the off-diagonal entries J₁ − J₂; the mutant that reports a diagonal
      matrix fails the unequal-rotor case.
- [ ] The linkage modulus equals the finite-difference derivative at 100 configurations, and port powers sum to
      zero at each.
- [ ] PO-SINEW-03 has an accepted instance certificate, and the wrong-sign variant is rejected.
- [ ] The counterexamples CX-135 and CX-140 are present in the negative-control registry with their gates.

### Unit tests
- `gear_back_driven_exact`, `gear_single_multiplier_refused`.
- `screw_lock_boundary_values`: tan(1°)/tan(5°) = 0.19951; zero at φ = a; −0.19951 at φ = 6°.
- `differential_reflected_inertia_equal`, `differential_reflected_inertia_unequal`: the two matrices of step 2,
  exact.
- `differential_power_conservation`: 10⁴ random (τ_joint, θ̇) in rationals; the two powers are equal exactly.
- `linkage_parallelogram_modulus_one`, `linkage_variable_ratio_vs_finite_difference`,
  `linkage_singularity_reported`.
- `po_sinew_03_identity`, `po_sinew_03_wrong_sign_rejected`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_cards.sh` (N15a-25) includes the transmission cards: `frob actuator check` on the
single-multiplier card (exit 3) and on the screw cards (the self-locking one is admitted and flagged
`self-locking`), and `frob actuator show` on the differential card printing its reflected inertia. Logged: the
fixture name, expected and measured values with units, the refusal payload, the certificate hash of PO-SINEW-03.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-320 (back-driven gear), KA-321 (screw), KA-322 (symmetric differential; control: reflected rotor inertia taken
as diagonal in joint space), KA-323 (ideal gear law, exact polynomial identity), KA-279 (the transmission
rejection fixture). CX-135, CX-140. Additional mutants: T used where T⁻¹ belongs in the reflection (gives
[[J₁ + J₂, J₁ − J₂], …]/4, caught by the equal-rotor value 2·10⁻⁴); the linkage modulus taken as the constant
link-length ratio (wrong away from one configuration). Metamorphic relations: swapping the two motors of the
differential swaps J₁ and J₂ and flips the sign of the off-diagonal entries; scaling T by c scales reflected
inertia by 1/c². Determinism: all checks are exact or pure `f64` and are identical across platforms.

## Considerations

- A coupling that varies with configuration has an inertial bias (velocity-product terms from the rotor kinetic
  energy); that rule, the non-square case and the direction witness for a reversed gear belong to the coupled
  transmission subsection owned by the other unit. A lossless transformer conserves power under either sign of its
  modulus, so nothing in this battery can detect a reversed gear; the direction witness exists for that.
- The linkage fixture depends on the design term's closure constructor; if closures on lockstep tiers are
  compliant rows, the modulus test runs on the referee or the strict profile, where closures are bilateral.
