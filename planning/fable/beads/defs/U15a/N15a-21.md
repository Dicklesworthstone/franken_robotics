## Background

Level L4 of the actuator model ladder is hydraulic actuation. It is a Moonshot: a synthesis that lives behind a
feature flag and never sits on the critical path. Electric actuators (levels L1 to L3) cover every archetype the
plan ships; hydraulics would open one more actuator class (heavy legged machines, some construction and
agricultural robots) and nothing else.

Stated before any work, as the rule for upper tiers requires:
- **Ceiling of the benefit:** opens one actuator class; no benefit to electric designs.
- **Pilot and falsifier:** a single cylinder through an orifice: steady speed against the closed form. Falsifier:
  the port balance does not close. The pilot is bead N15b-21 and this epic depends on it.
- **Kill condition and fallback:** not built until a consumer exists. Fallback: electric levels L1 to L3 only.
- **Queue position:** at most four Moonshot items are active at once (an item is active from the funding of its
  pilot until its expiry gate). Hydraulic actuation is seventh in the queue of admitted Moonshot items, with
  milestone "M6 at the earliest"; items whose consumers land earlier, or whose pilots are cheaper, enter first.

Why keep it in the plan at all: the port-Hamiltonian form (N15a-05) was chosen so that a new energy domain is a
new set of elements with the same accounting, not a new simulator. L4 is the test of that claim, and its pilot is
cheap.

## Scope

In (a short plan; tasks are created only when a consumer exists and the pilot has passed): hydraulic elements in
port-Hamiltonian form; the cylinder as a joint actuator; card fields; the known answer and the port balance.
Out: pumps with their own prime movers and accumulators beyond a lumped supply; valve dynamics beyond a
first-order spool; fluid temperature effects on viscosity (a later refinement); anything on the GPU tiers before
the referee form has passed its gate.

## Technical approach

1. **Elements (DESIGN sketch, same structure as every Sinew element).**
   - *Fluid volume (capacitance):* state is the stored fluid volume excess V; pressure p = β·V/V₀ with bulk
     modulus β and chamber volume V₀; stored energy V₀·p²/(2·β). Port: hydraulic (pressure p, flow Q).
   - *Orifice or valve (resistance):* a monotone relation between pressure drop and flow,
     Q = C_d·A_o·√(2·|Δp|/ρ)·sign(Δp), with discharge coefficient C_d, opening area A_o (the control input) and
     fluid density ρ. It dissipates Δp·Q ≥ 0 in every quadrant, as heat into a thermal node.
   - *Piston (transformer):* modulus A_p (piston area) between the hydraulic port and a mechanical port:
     F = A_p·p and Q = A_p·v. Lossless; seal friction is a stiction row of the usual kind (N15a-08).
   - *Supply:* a pressure source with a stated flow limit, drawing electrical or chemical power through the same
     ledger lines as a battery.
2. **Composite.** A cylinder is two chambers, two orifices (meter-in, meter-out) and a piston; a rotary hydraulic
   joint adds a linkage transmission. The composite is passive by structure, and its port balance is the one of
   BD-076 with a hydraulic port added.
3. **Stiffness.** Fluid compliance is stiff (β/V₀ is large); on the referee the chamber pressures are stepped
   implicitly with the mechanical state; on the convex tiers they follow ADR-SINEW-07's rule for stiff states
   (implicit rational updates), which the square-root orifice law does not satisfy as written. The batched form is
   therefore explicitly not planned until the referee form exists and a consumer asks for throughput.
4. **Cards.** Piston areas, stroke, chamber dead volumes, bulk modulus, orifice coefficient and area range, seal
   friction, supply pressure and flow limit, fluid density; each with provenance and uncertainty.
5. **Identity and capability.** `ActuatorLevel::L4Hydraulic` (N15a-04) exists as a variant from M1 so that old
   identities stay readable; requesting it before this epic lands refuses with FROB-REF-136 (an option the
   requested tier does not carry; the returned list of tiers that carry it is empty).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot N15b-21 has run, its outcome and the ceiling are on record, and a consumer is named, before any
      task of this epic opens; otherwise the epic stays closed with the fallback "electric levels L1 to L3 only".
- [ ] Known answer: a single cylinder through an orifice reaches the steady speed of the closed form
      v = (C_d·A_o/A_p)·√(2·(p_s − F/A_p)/ρ) within the tolerance the pilot sets, on the referee.
- [ ] The port balance closes for the hydraulic composite: supply power p_s·Q = load power F·v + orifice loss
      Δp·Q + change in stored fluid energy, to the referee tolerance (10⁻⁹ relative).
- [ ] The orifice loss is nonnegative in all four quadrants of (Δp, Q) and the second-law test passes.
- [ ] A Moonshot slot was free when the pilot was funded, and the slot's accounting is recorded.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `cylinder_steady_speed`: p_s = 10 MPa, A_p = 5·10⁻⁴ m², F = 2,000 N, ρ = 850 kg/m³, C_d = 0.62,
  A_o = 10⁻⁶ m²: Δp = 6 MPa, Q = 7.367·10⁻⁵ m³/s, v = 0.1473 m/s.
- `cylinder_power_balance`: 736.7 W supplied = 294.7 W to the load + 442.0 W in the orifice.
- `orifice_passive_all_quadrants`, `piston_transformer_power_conservation`, `chamber_energy_quadratic`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_hydraulic.sh` (written with the epic): one cylinder rig through `frob sim run --tier referee`,
asserting the steady speed and the closed balance from `frob telemetry energy`. Logged: p_s, F, Δp, Q, v with
units, the closed-form speed, relative error and threshold, the balance residual with threshold `1e-9`, digests.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-336 (a single hydraulic cylinder through an orifice: steady speed equals its closed form, and the port balance
closes; the plan names the closed form without writing it, and the expression above is the one the pilot's
derivation file records). Negative controls: an orifice law with flow proportional to Δp at these pressures (the
steady speed is wrong by a large factor); a piston that converts pressure to force with one area and flow to
speed with another (the transformer creates or destroys power; the balance test must fail).

## Considerations

- Nothing downstream depends on this epic. If it is never funded, the only visible effect is the refusal on
  `L4Hydraulic`.
- Licence and data: hydraulic component data sheets are usually proprietary; the catalog rule applies (generic
  classes only until a cited card exists).
- What would make us revisit sooner: an owner decision to add a hydraulic archetype, which would also move the
  item up the Moonshot queue.
