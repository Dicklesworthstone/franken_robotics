## Background

Sinew's central structural claim is that every element is passive and that the thermal accounting respects the
second law. Two invariants state it: INV-SINEW-01, every element is passive: its dissipation is nonnegative in all
four quadrants of every port; and INV-SINEW-03, entropy production of every thermal node is nonnegative each step.
A model that violates either can hand a controller energy that no source supplied, and an optimizing agent will
find it.

The gate that measures the claim is GATE-SINEW-08 (claim: Known answer; earliest M1): passivity and second law:
over 10⁶ random operating points in all four quadrants every loss and every entropy production is nonnegative; the
single-multiplier gear fixture refuses; every card in the catalog passes `actuator.check`. The mechanism beads
each test their own element; this bead is the battery that runs all of them together at the gate's scale, on every
card of the catalog and on cards drawn from the catalog's stated uncertainty, and records the gate.

Honest limit, stated with the gate: the second-law half is a sign check on each assigned loss. It cannot fail for a
correct model; its one catch is a loss with the wrong sign, typically in a braking mode. That is why the battery
samples all four quadrants and every braking mode, and why its negative controls are sign errors.

## Scope

In: the conformance battery `sinew-passivity`: the sampling plan; the list of loss terms and entropy terms
checked; the three clauses of the gate as assertions of the battery; the negative controls; the smoke
profile; the measured minima handed to the receipt. This is the only bead that defines the 10⁶-point
battery. Out, with the bead that has each: the elements themselves (N15a-05, N15a-06, N15a-17, seed BD-074,
the battery N15b-03, the bus BD-075 and the regeneration paths N15b-04, all of which this battery
exercises); the exact structural check of every element kind and of composites (N15b-09); the transmission
known answers (N15a-18); the run from the user's side, the catalog sweep through the CLI, the certificate
check with the offline auditor, and the writing of the receipt and gate row of GATE-SINEW-08 (N15b-32,
which depends on this bead and registers the battery as the fixture `sinew-passivity-battery`); the
kernels' passivity on devices at M2 (seed BD-151's `K-passive`, checked against this battery's reference
values).

## Technical approach

1. **Cards.** Every card of the generic catalog (six actuator classes, the generic battery and transmissions), and
   for each, 10³ cards drawn by address from the stated uncertainty of every field (so the claim holds over the
   band a randomized run will sample, not only at the nominal values).
2. **Operating points: 10⁶, by address, stratified.** For each point: a card; a quadrant of the shaft (signs of
   torque and speed: forward motoring, forward braking, reverse motoring, reverse braking), 2.5·10⁵ points each;
   current within ± the current limit; speed within ± the speed limit; bus voltage within the card's range;
   winding and housing temperatures in [250 K, 450 K]; ambient in [250 K, 330 K]; a driver `regen_policy` among
   `to-battery`, `shunt`, `clip`; for transmissions the transmitted torque and the direction of power flow. The
   seed and the address scheme are recorded, so any failing point is reproducible alone.
3. **Loss terms checked at each point (each must be ≥ −ε with ε = 10⁻¹² times the term's scale).** Copper
   i²·R(T); iron loss; rotor viscous b·ω²; stiction row dissipation (friction torque times sliding speed); gear
   loss in the applicable quadrant, c_load·|τ_transmitted|·|ω| with c_load = (1 − η_f)/η_f or 1 − η_b; driver
   switching loss; tooth and spring damping; battery internal and polarization resistances; shunt dissipation;
   the numerical dissipation ½·L·(i₊ − i)² of the implicit current update where the host reference arithmetic is
   under test.
4. **Entropy terms.** Per thermal node, σ = P_loss/T_w + G·(T_w − T_amb)²/(T_w·T_amb) for one node and the
   two-node form σ = P_w/T_w + P_h/T_h + G_wh·(T_w − T_h)²/(T_w·T_h) + G_ha·(T_h − T_amb)²/(T_h·T_amb), with
   temperatures in kelvin; each must be ≥ −ε.
5. **Structure, exactly.** For each card, the structural check of N15a-05 (J skew, R positive semidefinite, by
   exact rational arithmetic) is run once; it is the proof-flavoured half of the claim (PO-SINEW-01: the composite
   of passive elements through power-conserving junctions is passive) and is of class `Tested` until a theorem
   card for it is accepted.
6. **The three clauses.**
   (a) all 10⁶ points: zero negative losses and zero negative entropy productions;
   (b) the single-multiplier gear fixture (η = 0.8 in all quadrants, N = 10, load driving the joint with 10 N·m
   at 1 rad/s) refuses with FROB-REF-130, reporting the back-driven quadrant and 2.5 W created;
   (c) every card in the catalog passes `actuator.check`, both as admitted and as bound in the M1 archetypes.
7. **Paths under test ("all tiers").** At M1: the referee element code in `f64` and the host reference arithmetic
   in `f64` and `f32` (N15a-07's formulas as far as they exist). At M2 the same addresses are replayed through the
   device kernels by the kernel bead; this battery's recorded values are their reference.
8. **Harness.** A conformance battery `sinew-passivity` in `frob-conformance`, run by the Gauntlet harness at the
   gate profile (10⁶ points) and at a smoke profile (10⁴ points) on every change touching `frob-actuate`. It is
   registered as the fixture `sinew-passivity-battery`, so that `frob task fixture` runs it from the user's
   side. Its results (the count of points, the minimum of every term, the seed) go into the receipt
   `target/gates/GATE-SINEW-08.json` and the row of `registries/gates.toml`, which the end-to-end bead
   N15b-32 writes with the commit and the hardware fingerprint, recorded by a fresh-context session.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Clause (a): 10⁶ points, minimum of every loss term and of σ reported, all ≥ −ε; zero violations.
- [ ] Clause (b): the single-multiplier fixture refuses with FROB-REF-130 and the stated payload.
- [ ] Clause (c): `actuator.check` passes on every catalog card, admitted and bound.
- [ ] Every negative control below is caught in the same run, each by the term it corrupts.
- [ ] A failing point is reproducible from its address alone (demonstrated on one planted failure).
- [ ] The battery reports measured minima, not the targets, in the form the receipt of N15b-32 reads.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `passivity_smoke_1e4`: the battery at 10⁴ points in the default test run; the 10⁶ profile is the gate run.
- `quadrant_stratification`: exactly one quarter of the points in each shaft quadrant, and all three regen
  policies and both power-flow directions present.
- `structure_all_catalog_cards`: the exact check on every card.
- `address_reproducibility`: recomputing point number 123,456 alone gives the same terms bit for bit.
- `epsilon_is_roundoff_only`: with all inputs exactly representable the terms are exactly ≥ 0 (ε unused).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_cards.sh` (N15a-25) carries clauses (b) and (c) through the real binary
(`frob actuator check` on the fixture and on every catalog card). The 10⁶-point clause is run by the conformance
harness; its structured log follows the same contract: one `metric` line per loss term with `min_value`, unit,
threshold `0`, the number of points and the count of violations; the seed and address scheme; on a violation the
first offending address with every input of that point and the term's value; a `summary` line naming
`GATE-SINEW-08` with assertions passed and failed.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-294 (passivity and second law over 10⁶ random operating points in all four quadrants: every loss nonnegative;
the entropy production nonnegative each step; every card in the catalog passes `actuator.check`; control: the
single-multiplier gear fixture, which must refuse). KA-186 (σ ≥ 0 whenever P_loss ≥ 0; control: a regenerative
mode with a negated copper loss gives σ < 0). Planted failures the battery must catch, each registered against
GATE-SINEW-08: copper loss negated in the regenerative braking mode; the gear loss computed with one multiplier in
all quadrants (negative in the two back-driven quadrants); a gyrator with unequal torque and back-EMF moduli
(creates power in one direction); viscous damping with a sign taken from the commanded instead of the actual
speed; entropy computed with temperatures in degrees Celsius (σ changes sign near 0 °C). Metamorphic relations:
reversing the signs of torque and speed together leaves every loss unchanged; scaling all powers and conductances
by c scales σ by c. Determinism: the battery's summary digest is identical run to run and across worker counts on
one platform, and the `f64` referee values are identical in debug and release.

## Considerations

- The battery proves nothing about the size of any loss, only its sign. Sizes are held by the known answers of the
  thermal, envelope and transmission beads.
- Sampling from the uncertainty band can produce an inadmissible card (for example an efficiency drawn above one
  if a card states a band that crosses it). Such a draw must be refused by the card check, and the battery counts
  it as a correct refusal, never as a pass.
- Runtime: 10⁶ points of closed-form arithmetic is seconds on the host; the cost of the gate is the catalog bind
  checks, not the sampling.
