## Background

A user-visible capability of Sinew is "simulate a robot with real actuators": choose a ladder level, run on the
referee, and read torque, speed, temperature and energy that obey the motor's physics. Two gates measure it on a
running system at M1.

- **GATE-SINEW-01** (claim: Known answer): known answers within 1%: stall torque, no-load speed, electrical and
  mechanical time constants of a DC motor step, thermal steady-state rise ΔT = P·R_th. The level-L1 rows belong
  to the seed bead for levels L0 and L1 and the remaining rows to the seed bead for level L2 (both written by the
  other Sinew drafting unit); the model ladder itself is this unit's subsection, so the script that measures the
  gate on the running binary, and the gate record, are here.
- **GATE-SINEW-02** (claim: Known answer): port energy balance closes to 10⁻⁹ relative on the referee (M1) and
  10⁻⁴ relative per episode on single-precision tiers (M2). The mechanism is BD-076; this script is its
  end-to-end measurement.

The same script hosts the dynamic rows of three neighbouring beads so that one referee session covers them: the
stepped thermal closed forms (BD-072), the speed sweep through field weakening (BD-073), the duty-history case
(N15a-15) and the back-driven gear (N15a-17).

## Scope

In: the fixtures and cards, in two parameter sets, registered so that `frob task fixture` can run them; the
script; the measurements; thresholds; the negative-control re-runs; the gate receipts and records for
GATE-SINEW-01 and the referee row of GATE-SINEW-02. This is the only bead that measures GATE-SINEW-01 from
the user's side; the level beads BD-070 and BD-071 hold the same rows in their unit tests. Out: the
mechanisms; the single-precision row of GATE-SINEW-02 (seed BD-208, which reuses this script with `--tier`);
the static verbs (N15a-26); the power path and GATE-SINEW-07, whose script only observes the port account
(N15b-30).

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures** (synthetic; one-joint rigs under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sinew/dynamics/`, cards with
   every field `assumed`, gravity off):

   | Row | Level | Fixture | Known answer | Threshold |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Stall torque, current-limited | L1 | N = 10, η_f = 0.8, k_t = 0.1 N·m/A, I_max = 20 A, V_lim = 24 V (DC), R = 1 Ω, joint locked | η·N·k_t·min(I_max, V_lim/R) = 0.8·10·0.1·20 = 16.0 N·m | 1% |
   | Stall torque, voltage-limited | L1 | same with R = 2 Ω | 0.8·10·0.1·12 = 9.6 N·m | 1% |
   | No-load speed | L1 | k_e = 0.1 V·s/rad, N = 10, no friction | V_lim/(k_e·N) = 24 rad/s at the joint | 1% |
   | Mechanical time constant | L1 | J_rotor = 10⁻³ kg·m², R = 1 Ω, k_t = k_e = 0.1, no friction, η = 1, voltage step | J·R/(k_t·k_e) = 0.100 s (speed reaches 63.2% of no-load) | 1% |
   | Electrical time constant | L2 | rotor locked, R = 1 Ω, L = 10 mH, voltage step | L/R = 10 ms (current reaches 63.2% of V/R) | 1% |
   | Thermal steady-state rise | L2 | constant loss P = 80 W, G = 0.80 W/K (R_th = 1.25 K/W), `alpha_r` = 0 stated on the card | ΔT = P·R_th = 100 K | 1% |

   **Second parameter set, registered under fixture names** (`synthetic`, each with a manifest
   `assets/fixtures/<name>/fixture.toml` in the fixture registry of N24b-03): `dc-ref-a`
   (one revolute joint, card k_t = k_e = 0.05, R = 0.5 Ω, L = 1 mH, J_rotor = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m², N = 10,
   η = 0.9, I_max = 40 A, V_lim = 24 V, `current_convention: dc`, latency 0, no friction; expected stall
   18.0 N·m, no-load 48 rad/s, electrical 2 ms with the rotor locked, mechanical 20 ms on the bare rotor at
   L1 from a 2 V step, which draws 4 A and stays below the limit); `dc-ref-a-12v` (the same with
   V_lim = 12 V: stall 0.9·10·0.05·24 = 10.8 N·m, voltage-limited); `locked-rotor` (R = 1 Ω, L = 10 mH: the
   current rises as (V/R)·(1 − e^(−t/τ)) with τ = 10 ms); `thermal-const-r` (single thermal node, α = 0,
   P = 80 W, G = 0.80 W/K: rise 100 K). Every row of the gate is asserted on both sets. The electrical row
   of `dc-ref-a` is measured with the rotor locked, for the reason given under Considerations.
2. **Port balance fixtures** (BD-076): the motor axis at L1 and L2 under a four-quadrant excitation for 10 s
   (open-loop schedule `srd-four-quadrant`: a 0.5 Hz torque sine on a rig whose spring load drives the shaft
   during half of each period), and the legged archetype `quad-mammal` with actuators bound at L2 under its
   baseline controller (BD-116, synthesized by `control.synth`, BD-118), standing and stepping for 5 s. The
   legged fixture is this bead's and not BD-076's, because the baseline controller comes after BD-076. Statistic:
   the maximum over elements and steps of ρ_step = |residual| / max(Σ|port work| + |Δ stored| + |heat to
   ambient|, 10⁻¹² J), required ≤ 10⁻⁹.
3. **Neighbouring dynamic rows** (assertions owned by the named beads, run here): thermal equilibrium, time
   constant and time to limit on the worked card at 20 A and 35 A (0.5%); the speed sweep with a saturating
   torque request against the five field-weakening values (0.5%); the undersized-knee fixture from `cold` and from
   the default `steady(task)`; the back-driven gear's 2 W loss line; three distinct `ModelId`s for L0, L1, L2;
   and the `run` stage of N15a-04's `scripts/e2e/sinew_l4_refusal.sh` on the stall rig (`--actuator-level L4`
   refused with FROB-REF-724, exit 5, reason `not-linked`), logged under N15a-04's key.
4. **Commands.** `frob doctor --json` first, to record toolchain, pins and the hardware fingerprint. For each
   rig fixture: `frob sim run <rig> --tier referee --actuator-level <L> --controller open-loop:<schedule>
   --seconds <T> --telemetry T2 --seed 1 --key srd-<row>-<L> --json`, the schedules (`frob.openloop/1`,
   N15ap3-01) registered by this bead under `fixtures/controllers/open-loop/`: `srd-stall` and `srd-no-load`
   (full torque command held), `srd-voltage-step` (the step of the time-constant rows), `srd-thermal-hold` (the
   constant loss of the thermal row) and `srd-four-quadrant`. The legged fixture: `frob control synth
   quad-mammal --key srd-synth --json`, keeping its `ControllerId`, then `frob sim run quad-mammal --tier referee
   --actuator-level L2 --controller <ControllerId> --seconds 5 --telemetry T2 --seed 1 --key srd-legged --json`
   (the controller is named because a run with neither `--task` nor `--controller` binds none, N16ap4-01). The
   registered second set runs through `frob task fixture <name> --tier referee --profile referee
   --actuator-level <L> --measure <stall-torque | no-load-speed | mechanical-time-constant |
   electrical-time-constant | thermal-rise> --seed 7 --key srd-<name>-<measure> --json` (N24b-03's verb with
   N24bx-01's axes and selectors). Each of the four manifests declares, before its first run, `[axes.seed]
   values = ["7"]`, `[axes.actuator_level] values = ["L1", "L2"] default = "L1"` (`locked-rotor` and
   `thermal-const-r`: `["L2"]`) and `[axes.profile] values = ["referee"]`, and gives each row its `measure`
   slug; an undeclared value is FROB-REF-530. Every run-kind call carries its own key, and the repeat runs that
   are compared carry distinct keys. Then `frob telemetry query <run> --channels <list> --json` and `frob
   telemetry energy <run> --json`. On one L1 and one L2 run of the stall rig the script asserts that the
   per-actuator channels the level beads declare are present in `telemetry.query`'s answer: at L1 current,
   voltage, torque and the flags `current-limited` and `voltage-limited` (BD-070); at L2 also i_d, i_q,
   terminal voltage, the node temperatures, the trip latch and the iron-loss power (BD-071). It sends one query
   per stream, since a query whose channels belong to two streams is 802 (N24a-36). The script
   also accepts `--tier batch-cpu --precision f32` and then applies the single-precision threshold of
   GATE-SINEW-02 (10⁻⁴ per episode); that section is written here but exercised by seed BD-208 at M2, and at M1
   it reports `not run`, never `pass`. The batched L1 and L2 comparison of GATE-SINEW-03 is not in this script
   (N15b-35's `sinew_kernels_fidelity.sh`).
5. **Measurement helpers.** Time constants from the 63.2% crossing with linear interpolation between samples and,
   as a cross-check, from a log-linear fit of the residual to the settled value; stall torque from the joint
   reaction with the joint locked; no-load speed from the settled joint speed.
6. **Negative-control re-runs.** The script re-runs the registered fixtures of the second set under the
   registered mutants with `frob task fixture <name> … --control <mutant> --key srd-<name>-<mutant> --json` on a
   build with the cargo feature `gauntlet-mutations` (N24b-03's input; `sim.run`'s conformance input `--mutant`
   is N21b-42's, M2), and requires a non-zero exit from the assertion that guards each: ideal torque source in
   place of L1 on `dc-ref-a` (stall torque unlimited, no-load speed unbounded); winding dynamics dropped at L2 on
   `locked-rotor` (no electrical time constant); the negated copper loss (N15a-06) on `thermal-const-r`;
   `mutant-l1-no-backemf` on `dc-ref-a`, which must fail the no-load-speed row. The controls whose fixtures are
   `sim.run` rigs are run by the tier runs of the gates they guard, with `--mutations`, and must be `caught`
   there: the 2% unaccounted shaft loss and the double-counted heat (BD-076) in step 7's GATE-SINEW-02 receipt,
   which this script asserts; `mutant-l2-const-r` (inert for the thermal-rise row, whose fixture has α = 0, and
   failing the stepped 20 A row of BD-072 instead) and an evaluator defaulting to `cold` (N15a-15) in the
   GATE-SINEW-05 receipt of N15a-26's script.
7. **Gate receipts.** The gates' rows in the Gauntlet's Known-answer target through the STD-E2E helper, each with
   its registered controls: `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SINEW-01 --mutations` (KA-329 to KA-333, G1 in
   N15-KA) and `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SINEW-02 --mutations` (KA-295, G1; the referee part). The script
   asserts from each receipt (`e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) that the gate's line is `pass` with measured
   values, never targets, and that each listed control is `caught`, and lists both receipt hashes in the
   summary. It never runs `record_gate` and never writes `registries/gates.toml` (STD-E2E's `self-recording`
   rule). This bead is the one recorder of GATE-SINEW-01 and of the referee part of GATE-SINEW-02: a
   fresh-context session that did not implement the levels runs `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
   record_gate -- --gate GATE-SINEW-01 --receipt <hash>` and `… --gate GATE-SINEW-02 --part M1 --receipt
   <hash>`. The dynamic rows of GATE-SINEW-05 and -06 asserted in step 3 are the user-side check of BD-072's and
   BD-073's running models; those gates' tier receipts are produced by N15a-26's script, which runs after this
   one and is their one recorder (a recording cites one receipt per tier target, N38b-19).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All six rows of GATE-SINEW-01 pass at 1% on the referee, on both parameter sets, each measured on the
      level named; the receipt and the record list the measured values.
- [ ] The gate is registered in the conformance registry with its negative controls, and the envelope of every
      step carries class Estimated (synthetic card) with the weakest input named, tier `referee`, and
      `reality: no-claim`.
- [ ] The maximum ρ_step on the three port-balance fixtures is ≤ 10⁻⁹ and is recorded (GATE-SINEW-02, referee row).
- [ ] Every negative-control re-run fails the assertion that guards it, in the same script run, and the
      GATE-SINEW-02 receipt lists the 2% unaccounted loss and the double count as `caught`.
- [ ] The per-actuator channels of L1 and L2 (BD-070, BD-071) are present in `telemetry.query`'s answer for an
      L1 and an L2 run.
- [ ] The neighbouring dynamic rows pass at their own thresholds, with each assertion logged under its owning
      bead's key.
- [ ] The script is plain shell over the real `frob` binary, idempotent, seed-explicit, exits non-zero on any
      failed assertion, and with `--tier` set to a tier that does not exist yet reports `not run`, never `pass`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Helpers only, on synthetic traces (labelled `synthetic`):
- `tau_from_632_crossing`: an exponential with τ = 0.100 s sampled at 1 ms returns 0.1000 s within 10⁻⁴.
- `tau_from_loglinear_fit`: the same trace with 0.1% noise returns τ within 0.5%.
- `settled_value_detection`: a trace settling to 24.0 returns 24.0 within 10⁻⁶ after the settling window.
- `max_rho_step`: three balance lines with residuals 10⁻¹², 10⁻¹⁰, 0 over 1 J of exchanged work return 10⁻¹⁰.

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_sinew_01.rs`: `receipt_schema`; `gate_has_negative_controls`;
`fixture_expected_values` (the closed forms of the second set evaluated in exact rationals equal 18, 48,
1/500, 1/50 and 100).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_referee_dynamics.sh [--tier T] [--precision P]`. It sources the shared end-to-end helper and
writes newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_referee_dynamics/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript
beside it. Each line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`,
`end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier,
determinism, `ModelId` with the level, seed). `metric` lines: `stall_torque_nm`, `no_load_speed_rad_s`,
`tau_mech_s`, `tau_elec_s`, `thermal_rise_k`, `max_rho_step`, per-mode energies in joules, the thermal and
field-weakening rows, each with unit, expected value and threshold. Digests of state are logged for the repeat
run. On failure: the first differing value, the step index, the element, and the paths of the run and fixture.
The `summary` line counts assertions, lists `GATE-SINEW-01` and `GATE-SINEW-02` and carries the two receipt
hashes of step 7.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-329 (stall torque), KA-330 (no-load speed), KA-331 (electrical time constant L/R), KA-332 (mechanical time
constant J·R/(k_t·k_e)), KA-333 (thermal rise P/G), all within 1%; KA-183 (locked-rotor current step, τ = 10 ms);
KA-295 (port balance). Negative controls as listed in step 6. Metamorphic relations: doubling V_lim doubles the
no-load speed and leaves both time constants unchanged; doubling R doubles the mechanical time constant and
halves the electrical one. Determinism lanes: every referee fixture is run twice on one machine with identical
state digests; debug and release builds agree; across tiers (M2) the comparison is by threshold.

## Considerations

- The formulas L/R and J·R/(k_t·k_e) are exact only when measured separately: the electrical row locks the rotor,
  and the mechanical row runs at L1, which has no winding dynamics. Measuring both on one coupled L2 step would
  give the roots of the coupled second-order system, which differ from the two formulas by more than 1% unless
  L/R is far below J·R/k²; the fixtures are built to avoid testing the fixture instead of the model.
- The thermal-rise row sets `alpha_r` to zero explicitly: it tests the node, not the temperature law; the
  temperature-dependent rows are BD-072's and show a rise of 164.7 K for the same 80 W cold loss.
- If the referee refuses a fixture for a capacity reason, the script reports the refusal code and marks the row
  `blocked`, never passed.
- The mechanical time constant is measured on the bare rotor; with a load inertia J_load it becomes
  (J + J_load/N²)·R/(k_t·k_e), and each fixture states which it uses.
- A first-order delay in the driver would bias the time-constant rows; the fixtures set latency to zero and
  the delay has its own unit test in BD-070.
- The script needs only the CPU and belongs to the `smoke` battery.
