## Background

Cards are the unit of actuator knowledge in the system: immutable, content-addressed, with a provenance and an
uncertainty on every field. An agent meets them through three read verbs of the `actuator` cluster, first
milestone M1: `actuator.list`, `actuator.show` and `actuator.check` ("cards and their provenance; admission
checks"). This bead is the end-to-end script for that capability, driven from the user's side through the real
`frob` binary, with structured logging. It also carries the two clauses of GATE-SINEW-08 that are verdicts on
cards: the single-multiplier gear fixture refuses, and every card in the catalog passes `actuator.check`.

A refusal is a result, not a crash: every verb writes exactly one JSON envelope with `ok`, `exit`, `evidence`,
`bound`, and on refusal a `refusal` object with `code`, `message`, `pointer`, `observed`, `required`, `retry` and
ranked `repairs`. Exit codes: 0 completed, 2 completed with findings or flags, 3 refused input or question. The
script asserts on those fields, never on prose.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures (rejection cards), its assertions and its log, for the card verbs and for card
binding. Out, with the bead that has each, all of which come before this script: the verbs and checks themselves
(BD-069, N15a-02, N15a-03, N15a-04, N15a-17); the transmission cards and their reflected inertia (seed BD-074);
the verb `design.check` and its stage runner (seed BD-110), through which step 8 reaches stage 1; the
`--strict-data` refusal FROB-REF-113 (N15b-06); the 10⁶-point passivity battery (N15a-23); the receipt of
GATE-SINEW-08, which the passivity script writes after repeating the catalog sweep and the single-multiplier
refusal (N15b-32); the seven refusal codes with their payloads and repairs (N15b-38); the signed motion witness
run at binding (N15b-12, measured by N15b-13); MCP exposure of the same verbs (the surface beads), although the
script has an optional section that calls the generated MCP tools when the server binary is present and asserts
the same envelopes.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures** under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sinew/cards/` (synthetic, labelled; each a small `.fcard`
   derived from a named base card by the mutation its row states: the actuator rows from the catalog's `qdd-m`,
   except `brake_negative_holding`, from the catalog's `hub-motor` (the card with a `brake` group, BD-069);
   the screw and differential rows from the catalog's generic screw and gear cards; `rating_unreachable` from the
   worked thermal card, GB-11's values with `alpha_flux` = 0, so that BD-069's check 3 reduces to the
   constant-current law. `gear_single_multiplier` is not copied: it is N15a-17's card
   `fixtures/sinew/transmissions/single_multiplier.fcard`, used in place):

   | Fixture | Mutation | Expected |
   |---|---|---|
   | `no_provenance` | one field without `prov` | exit 3, FROB-REF-103 (form error), pointer at the field |
   | `no_uncertainty` | one field without `unc` | exit 3, FROB-REF-103 (form error), pointer at the field |
   | `brake_negative_holding` | `brake.holding_torque` = −1 N·m | exit 3, FROB-REF-130, pointer `fields.brake.holding_torque` |
   | `no_convention` | `current_convention` removed | exit 3, FROB-REF-134, fields in conflict listed |
   | `convention_mislabelled` | `kt` authored per RMS phase ampere, labelled `q-axis` | exit 3, FROB-REF-130 (constants inconsistent by a factor √3) |
   | `negative_viscous` | `viscous` = −0.001 | exit 3, FROB-REF-130, quadrant and watts created |
   | `efficiency_above_one` | `efficiency_forward` = 1.2 | exit 3, FROB-REF-130 |
   | `gear_single_multiplier` | η = 0.8 in all quadrants (back efficiency 1.25) | exit 3, FROB-REF-130, back-driven quadrant, 2.5 W created at 10 N·m and 1 rad/s |
   | `self_locking_screw` | lead angle 5°, friction angle 6° | exit 0 or 2, admitted, flag `self-locking` |
   | `differential_unequal` | coupling T = [[½, ½], [½, −½]]; the fixture design `differential_unequal.fbot` binds it to two actuators with rotor inertias 3·10⁻⁴ and 10⁻⁴ kg·m² | admitted by `check` on the card and at `differential_unequal:ankle`; at the binding, `actuator.check`'s `reflected_inertia` (VERBS-actuator, BD-074) is `{joints, matrix}` with matrix [[4, 2], [2, 4]]·10⁻⁴ kg·m², off-diagonal not zero (CX-140), the value BD-074's `differential_reflected_inertia` and N15a-18's `differential_reflected_inertia_unequal` assert; the bare card's answer has no `reflected_inertia` field |
   | `rating_unreachable` | continuous rating set to 20 A cold-equivalent on the worked thermal card | exit 3, FROB-REF-132, `refusal.pointer` at the rating, largest admissible 18.6 A in `refusal.required` |
   | `assumed_field` | `c_winding` of class `assumed` | admitted; refused with FROB-REF-113 only under `--strict-data` by an analysis that reads it |
2. **Steps.**
   1. `frob actuator list --json`: the six generic actuator classes, the generic battery and transmissions are
      present; every row has a `CardId`; the provenance shares sum to 1; pagination fields are present.
   2. `frob actuator show <card> --json` for each catalog card: every field has `prov.class` and `unc.kind`; the
      derived power-invariant modulus is printed; `hub-motor` prints its `brake` group (`unpowered: engaged`) and
      `qdd-m` prints `brake: none`; re-running gives identical bytes.
   3. Identity: copy a catalog card, reorder keys and whitespace, `show` both: same `CardId`. Change one
      provenance class: different `CardId`.
   4. Conventions: three copies of one card authored in `q-axis`, `phase-peak`, `phase-rms` with consistently
      converted constants: `show` prints the same modulus to 10⁻¹² relative.
   5. `frob actuator check <card> --json` for each catalog card: exit 0 (clause (c) of GATE-SINEW-08).
   6. Each fixture of the table: the expected exit code, refusal code, pointer and payload. The
      single-multiplier card is checked as `frob actuator check <card> --at 10,1 --json`, the evaluation point of
      the power-created figure; the two admitted transmission cards are also passed to `actuator show`.
   7. Capability table: `frob actuator show qdd-m --capabilities --json` equals the checked-in table. A
      backlash row the tier does not carry: `frob sim run backlash_no_tooth --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep
      --actuator-level L3 --seconds 1 --seed 3 --key cards-136 --json` (a fixture design binding a card with
      `backlash` > 0 and no `tooth_stiffness`, N15a-09) refuses with FROB-REF-136 and lists the tiers that carry
      the row. At M1 `sim.run` carries only the referee and answers every other tier with FROB-REF-724, exit 5,
      before a step plan is built (BD-024), so until `batch-cpu` is carried (M2) this command is an `e2e_skip`
      with that reason, logged as a skip and never as a pass, as N15b-38 does; the refusal is asserted meanwhile
      by N15a-04's unit test `capability_refusal`, and the step runs unchanged from M2.
   8. Binding: `frob design check <fixture design> --stages 1-1 --json` for a design binding the mislabelled card
      (stage 1, FROB-REF-130) and one binding `no_convention` (FROB-REF-134);
      `frob design check <design> --strict-data --json` for the `assumed_field` design (FROB-REF-113).
   9. Override: a copy of a fixture design whose binding overrides one field on one joint (N15a-03's bind-time
      override; the card verbs take none), each passed to `frob design check <design> --stages 1-1 --json`: the
      `ModelId` in the evidence block differs between the two, and the catalog file's digest is unchanged.
3. **Idempotence and seeds.** The script creates its working copies in a temporary workspace, uses no network,
   has no random input (seed logged as `none`), and can be re-run with the same result.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All nine steps pass on a clean checkout, except the assertion of step 7 marked `e2e_skip` above, which is
      logged as a skip with its reason until `batch-cpu` is carried; every catalog card passes `actuator.check`;
      the single-multiplier fixture refuses with FROB-REF-130 and the stated payload.
- [ ] Each rejection fixture produces exactly its expected exit code and refusal code; none produces exit 1.
- [ ] The log contains one line per command with the fields below, and a final summary naming `GATE-SINEW-08`.
- [ ] The script exits non-zero if any assertion fails, and a deliberately corrupted expectation (a dry-run flag
      that flips one expected code) makes it fail, proving the assertions are live.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
In `crates/frob-conformance/tests/sinew_card_fixtures.rs`:
- `fixture_table_matches_directory`: every fixture is used and every expectation has a fixture.
- `one_mutation_per_fixture`: each rejection card differs from its named base card (`qdd-m`, the generic
  transmission card, or the worked thermal card) by exactly the fields its row names (a structural diff of the
  parsed cards; `hub-motor` for `brake_negative_holding`), so no fixture is refused for an unrelated reason; `gear_single_multiplier` resolves to N15a-17's
  file and is not duplicated in this directory.
- `fixture_cards_parse`: every fixture card parses as a card (the refusals are checks, not parse failures),
  except `no_provenance` and `no_uncertainty`, whose form errors are asserted with their pointers.
Plus a shell-lint of the script.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_cards.sh`. It sources the shared helper of the end-to-end harness and writes newline-delimited
JSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_cards/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line
carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` (the bead whose behaviour the step asserts: BD-069, N15a-02, N15a-03, N15a-04,
N15a-17, N15a-18 or BD-074), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command
line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier `static`, determinism,
identities, seed). `refusal` lines add the code, pointer, observed and required values and the first repair.
`metric` lines: the modulus difference across conventions with threshold `1e-12`; the provenance shares; the
watts created by the single-multiplier fixture (2.5 W); the off-diagonal reflected inertia of the differential
(2·10⁻⁴ kg·m²) at its binding. `skip` lines carry the reason (step 7 at M1), and the summary counts skips apart
from passes. Digests are logged where bytes are compared (`CardId`s,
file digests, repeated `show` output). On failure the line holds the first differing value and the paths of the
card and the envelope. The final `summary` line counts assertions passed and failed and lists `GATE-SINEW-08`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-279 (the transmission rejection fixture: reverse drive that produces free electrical energy, refused with
FROB-REF-130) and KA-294 (every card in the catalog passes `actuator.check`; the single-multiplier fixture must
refuse). The rejection fixtures are themselves the planted failures; a check that lets its fixture through is
broken. Determinism: `list` and `show` output is byte-identical across two runs and across the two platforms; the
checks are exact.

## Considerations

- The fixtures for a field without provenance or uncertainty assert FROB-REF-103, the code the registries give a
  card form error of that kind (N14-REF; the `actuator.check` row of VERBS-actuator), with its pointer and exit
  code; the plan names no dedicated code, and if the owner allocates one only the fixture table changes.
- No private data is involved; cards in the catalog are generic and carry their licence field.
