## Background

Sinew's static functions exist so that a design is refused before any simulation is spent on it. Two read verbs
expose them, first milestone M1: `actuator.thermal` (the thermal steady state and runaway) and `actuator.envelope`
(the torque–speed envelope). An agent sizing a knee actuator asks them two questions: will this current hold for a
shift, and is this torque available at this speed? This bead is the end-to-end script that asks those questions of
the real binary on the two worked cards of the plan and checks every published number, and the one recorder of two
gates, from the tier receipts its script produces:

- **GATE-SINEW-05** (Known answer, M1): equilibrium, time constant, critical current and time to limit within 0.5%
  of the closed forms; the two-node condition; the tangency under torque holding within 1%; the worked card's 20 A
  point admitted by the constant-resistance check and refused by the corrected one.
- **GATE-SINEW-06** (Known answer, M1): the five closed forms within 0.5%; over 10⁴ random operating points the
  inscribed-circle envelope never exceeds the hexagon envelope.

The dynamic rows of the same gates (the stepped L2 model reproducing the closed forms) are measured from the user's
side by the referee script of N15a-24, which runs before this one; a gate is recorded as passed only when both
scripts pass.

## Scope

In: the two worked cards as fixtures; the script; assertions on every tabulated value of `actuator.thermal` and
`actuator.envelope`; refusals 131 and 132 raised by the two verbs; the tier receipts and the records of
GATE-SINEW-05 and GATE-SINEW-06. Out, with the bead that has each: the verbs and libraries (BD-072, BD-073,
N15a-14, N15a-16); `actuator.bus` and the power path (N15b-30); the A-11 analysis and stage 6 of `design.check`,
whose own script plants one defect per check and runs the repair loop (N15b-34), although one step here runs
`design.check` on a planted undersized design to see the same refusals arrive through it (which is why the
typed-edit engine BD-058, A-11 and stage 6 of seed BD-103 and the stage runner of seed BD-110 come before this
bead); the seven refusal codes with payload assertions and repairs that work (N15b-38); the two cards as worked
cards of the card registry, `registries/cards/GB-11.toml` and `registries/cards/GB-12.toml`, each with its
equation, goldens to 10⁻⁹, controls and two-lane test against the library (GB-11 for the thermal card, GB-12 for
the envelope card), and the script `scripts/e2e/worked_cards.sh` that recomputes all worked cards (NEG-02). The
fixtures here are those two cards in the actuator-card format the verbs read; this bead is the gates' one
recorder.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures** (synthetic; illustrative numbers derived for the plan, none measured; all fields `assumed`):
   - `thermal_worked.fcard`: R₀ = 0.20 Ω at 25 °C, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹, G = 0.80 W/K, C = 120 J/K, winding limit
     155 °C; a second variant with `alpha_flux` = −0.0011 K⁻¹; a two-node variant with G_wh and G_ha whose series
     conductance is 0.80 W/K.
   - `envelope_worked.fcard`: λ = 0.010 V·s, L_d = L_q = 100 µH, I_max = 40 A, R = 0, `modulation: svm-linear`,
     evaluated at V_bus = 48 V.
2. **Thermal steps** (`frob actuator thermal thermal_worked … --ambient 25degC --json`), tolerance 0.5% unless
   stated:

   | Request | Expected in the result |
   |---|---|
   | `--current 20A --start cold` | stable; rise 164.7 K (190 °C); time constant 247 s; loss at equilibrium 132 W; time to 155 °C: 385 s; constant-resistance comparison 125 °C, 150 s, never |
   | `--current 35A --start cold` | not stable; growth rate 0.00136 s⁻¹; time to limit 61 s; constant-resistance comparison: equilibrium 306 K above ambient, 83 s |
   | any | `i_crit` 31.9 A; `i_continuous` 18.6 A (constant-resistance comparison 22.8 A) |
   | `--torque … --hold torque` on the flux variant | limit current 15.9 A cold-equivalent; tangency 16.5 A (1%); the slope estimate 25.5 A reported as non-conservative; margin to tangency about 4% |
   | two-node variant at 20 A | the condition s < G_wh·G_ha/(G_wh + G_ha) reported true; with the series conductance lowered below s = 0.314 W/K, reported false and not stable |
   | `--current 20A --require` | exit 3, FROB-REF-132; payload: equilibrium 190 °C, largest sustainable load 18.6 A, ranked repairs |
   | `--current 20A --start cold` (the first request's answer) | its `constant_resistance_comparison` admits the point (125 °C, inside 155 °C) and is labelled the wrong model, while the corrected law puts it at 190 °C: KA-313's verdict flip read from one answer (the verb has no option that switches the checked model) |
   Exit codes follow the verb's rule (BD-072): the first two requests are not required points, so their
   facts (an equilibrium above the limit; no equilibrium) are findings and exit with code 2; only `--require`
   turns them into FROB-REF-132 with exit code 3. The worked card GB-11 states the same rule.
3. **Envelope steps** (`frob actuator envelope envelope_worked --v-bus 48V --json`), tolerance 0.5%:
   V_lim 33.9 V; base speed 3,151 rad/s electrical; no-load speed 3,394 rad/s; at 1.5 times base speed
   i_d = −32.2 A and i_q = 23.7 A, 59% of peak torque; top speed 5,657 rad/s. Then
   `--point <full torque>,<1.5 × base speed> --require`: exit 3, FROB-REF-131, with the point, the boundary and an
   admitting edit. Then the property run: `--shape circle` against `--shape hexagon` at 10⁴ seeded random
   (speed, angle, torque) points; the circle's admitted torque never exceeds the hexagon's.
4. **Through the preflight.** The script derives the undersized design itself in its temporary workspace:
   `frob design edit <reference quadruped> swap-actuator --joints '*.knee' --to <next smaller generic class> --key
   ss-knee-undersized --json`
   (the same mutation as N15b-34's `quad-knee-undersized`, made here so this script needs no other script's
   fixture). Then `frob design check <that design> --json`: stops at stage 6 with FROB-REF-131 or
   FROB-REF-132 for the knee, with typed-edit repairs (`swap-actuator`, `regear`) as `next_actions`.
5. **Gate receipts.** The gates' rows in the Gauntlet's tier targets through the STD-E2E helper, each with its
   registered controls: `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SINEW-05 --mutations` (KA-304 to KA-313, G1 in N15-KA:
   the static rows on the host and the stepped rows on the referee, so the one receipt carries both),
   `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SINEW-06 --mutations` (KA-314 to KA-318) and `e2e_tier g0_laws
   GATE-SINEW-06 --mutations` (KA-319, the circle-inside-hexagon inequality, G0). The script asserts from each
   receipt (`e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) that the gate's line is `pass` with measured values, commit,
   seed and fingerprint, and that each listed control is `caught` (for GATE-SINEW-05 among them the
   constant-resistance model, `mutant-l2-const-r` on the stepped 20 A row and the evaluator defaulting to
   `cold`), and lists the three receipt hashes in the summary. It never runs `record_gate` and never writes
   `registries/gates.toml` (STD-E2E's `self-recording` rule). This bead is the one recorder of both gates (BD-072,
   BD-073): once this script and N15a-24's have passed at one commit, a fresh-context session that did not
   implement the work runs `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SINEW-05 --receipt
   <hash>` and `… --gate GATE-SINEW-06 --receipt <g0 hash> --receipt <g1 hash>` (one receipt per tier target,
   N38b-19).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every value of the two tables is returned within its tolerance; the JSON carries units on every quantity.
- [ ] The seed bead's condition for thermal: the 20 A point is admitted by the constant-resistance comparison and
      refused by the corrected law with FROB-REF-132.
- [ ] The envelope refusal carries FROB-REF-131 with the point, the boundary and an edit.
- [ ] Circle against hexagon: 0 violations in 10⁴ points; the seed and the count are logged.
- [ ] The preflight step returns the same refusal codes with repairs as typed edits.
- [ ] Results carry evidence class `Estimated` (the fixture cards are `assumed`), tier `static`, and the `CardId`.
- [ ] The script exits non-zero on any failed assertion and is idempotent.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own beyond shell-lint, the fixture–table agreement test (every row of the tables has an assertion
in the script and vice versa), and the fixture–card agreement test: every field of `thermal_worked.fcard`
equals the substituted value of the worked card GB-11 (R₀ = 0.20 Ω, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹, G = 0.80 W/K,
C = 120 J/K, 25 °C, limit 155 °C, flux coefficient −0.0011 K⁻¹ in the flux variant) and every field of
`envelope_worked.fcard` that of GB-12 (λ = 0.010 V·s, L = 100 µH, I_max = 40 A, V_bus = 48 V). The test
compares against the constants written here and, once `registries/cards/GB-11.toml` and `GB-12.toml` are
registered, against their substituted tables as read from the registry, so the two hand-written forms of one
card cannot drift apart.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_static_sizing.sh`. It sources the shared end-to-end helper and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/sinew_static_sizing/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Each line: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (BD-072, BD-073, N15a-14 or N15a-16 according to the assertion), `step`, `event`, the exact
command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, `CardId`,
seed). `metric` lines give each measured value with its unit, the closed-form value, the relative error and the
threshold (`0.005`, or `0.01` for the tangency); `refusal` lines give code, pointer, observed, required and the
first repair; the property run logs the point count, violations and the largest observed conservatism (at most
13.4%). On failure: the first differing value and the paths of the card and the envelope. The `summary` line
counts assertions and lists `GATE-SINEW-05` and `GATE-SINEW-06`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-304 to KA-313 (thermal; KA-313 is the verdict flip) and KA-314 to KA-319 (envelope), with the values in the
tables above. CX-136, CX-137, CX-139. Negative controls, run as the registered controls of the gates' tier runs (`--mutations`) and
asserted `caught` from the receipts of step 5: the
constant-resistance law as the checked model (the 20 A refusal disappears: the assertion must fail); the slope
estimate in place of the tangency (16.5 A becomes 25.5 A); the envelope without the voltage ellipse (the 1.5 times
base-speed point is admitted); the circumscribed circle (the property finds violations). Determinism: the verbs'
output is byte-identical across two runs and in debug and release; the property run is seed-explicit.

## Considerations

- The worked cards are illustrative and are labelled so in the fixtures; the script tests the arithmetic and the
  refusals, not any real part.
- The constant-resistance comparison is printed only so that the difference can be shown and tested; it is
  labelled the wrong model and can never be cited for a verdict.
