## Background

Thermal time constants of an actuator are minutes and episodes are seconds. In 20 s from cold at 20 A the winding
of the worked card (R₀ = 0.20 Ω at 25 °C, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹, G = 0.80 W/K, C = 120 J/K) rises by
164.7·(1 − e^(−20/247)) = 12.8 K against an equilibrium rise of 164.7 K. A policy trained and evaluated on episodes
that start at ambient never meets derating. Decision ADR-SINEW-08 therefore says that thermal state starts from a
declared duty history, not from ambient: `cold`, `soaked(T)`, or `steady(task)`, the equilibrium for the task's
mean loss. Evaluation defaults to `steady(task)`; training draws from the three.

The declaration type `ThermalStart`, its resolution into initial node temperatures and its refusals are bead
N15a-15. What that bead leaves open is where the declaration lives and who applies it. A declaration that lives
only in a command-line flag is not part of the task, so two evaluations that differ only in thermal start would
compare as the same task. This bead puts the declaration in the task file `.ftask` (schema `frob.task/1`, nine
sections, canonical JSON hashed into `TaskId`; schema bead N24a-02), and makes the referee evaluation path apply
it at the first tick of every episode. Tier: Solid. Subsystems: Arena carries the field, Sinew gives it meaning.

Rejected: a flag of `sim.run` only (not in the task identity; the comparison machinery could not tell two
starts apart); a field of the design file (the start is a property of the duty, which is the task's, not of the
body); a field of the scenario (a scenario is drawn per world, and the evaluation default must not depend on a
draw).

## Scope

In: the field `thermal_start` in the `reset` section of the task file, with its evaluation value and its training
mixture; its canonical form and its place in `TaskId`; static admission of the field; the S-expression view of
it; applying the resolved start at tick 0 of every episode on the referee through `eval.run` and `sim.run`;
the override flag and the derived task identity it produces; recording the resolved start in the episode.
Out, with the bead that has each: the `ThermalStart` type, the computation of `steady(task)`, the default per
use and the refusal when no admissible equilibrium exists (N15a-15); the L2 component that accepts initial node
temperatures (BD-071) and the temperature laws (BD-072); the per-world write at environment reset on the batched
tiers and the mixture drawn there (N15a-30); the other fields of the `reset` section (the settled-pose cache
specification, owned by the task-file beads).

## Technical approach

1. **The field (DESIGN names).** In the `reset` section:
   ```json
   "thermal_start": {
     "eval": {"start": "steady"},
     "train": {"stream": "thermal-start",
               "mixture": [{"start": "cold", "weight": "1/3"},
                           {"start": "soaked", "t_range": ["ambient", "steady"], "weight": "1/3"},
                           {"start": "steady", "weight": "1/3"}]}
   }
   ```
   `start` is one of `cold`, `soaked`, `steady`. `soaked` takes either `t` (an exact quantity with a temperature
   unit, for example `"80 degC"`) or `t_range` (two bounds, each an exact temperature or one of the symbols
   `ambient` and `steady`, drawn uniformly). `steady` means `steady(task)` with this task as the duty. Weights
   are exact rationals. `stream` names a random stream declared in the `randomization` section.
2. **Canonical form and identity.** The task file's rule is that every field the compiler reads is written out.
   A file without `thermal_start` is canonicalized with the defaults of N15a-15 written explicitly (evaluation
   `steady`; training equal thirds with the `soaked` temperature uniform between ambient and the `steady`
   temperature). `TaskId` is the hash of the canonical bytes, so a file that omits the field and a file that
   states the defaults have the same `TaskId`, and any other value gives a different one.
3. **Static admission** (run by `task.new` and `task.check` with the other checks of the file; each failure is a
   refusal with exit code 3 and a pointer at the field):
   - `start` outside the three values, or an unknown key inside `thermal_start`: the schema's unknown-field
     refusal, FROB-REF-891.
   - `t` or a bound of `t_range` without a temperature unit: the dimension refusal of the task file,
     FROB-REF-535.
   - Weights that are not exact rationals in (0, 1] or do not sum to exactly 1: a form refusal with the sum
     printed; repair: correct the weights.
   - `stream` not declared in `randomization`: a form refusal naming the stream.
   A `soaked` temperature above a limit of the bound model cannot be seen statically, because the task does not
   know the body; it is refused when the task meets a design (step 5).
4. **S-expression view.** `(thermal-start (eval steady) (train :stream thermal-start (cold 1/3) (soaked (ambient
   steady) 1/3) (steady 1/3)))`, lossless against the JSON form like the rest of the file.
5. **Application on the referee.** When `eval.run`, or `sim.run` with a task, binds a task to a design:
   1. The declaration for the use (evaluation, or training with the world's address) is selected.
   2. `resolve(start, model, task, duty source)` of N15a-15 returns a `ThermalInit { t_winding, t_housing,
      derate_band }` per actuator, or refuses with FROB-REF-132 (the equilibrium or the margin to the tangency,
      and the largest sustainable load, returned with it). The refusal is the run's result; no start is
      substituted.
   3. Each L2 component is constructed with those temperatures (the initial-temperature input of BD-071), and
      the derating state at tick 0 follows from them, so an episode may begin inside a derating band.
   4. At level L1 there is no thermal state. The declaration is still recorded, and the result carries the flag
      `thermal-start: no thermal state at L1`, so a reader cannot take an L1 run for a thermal verdict.
6. **Override.** `--thermal-start cold | soaked:<T> | steady` on `sim.run` and `eval.run` replaces the selected
   declaration for that run. An override never edits a task: it yields a derived task whose canonical bytes
   carry the overriding value and whose `TaskId` is new, and the envelope's `bound` field lists both identities.
7. **Record.** The resolved `ThermalInit` per actuator and the duty source's identity are stored with the
   episode's resolved randomization values, so that replay reproduces the temperatures bit for bit and a failure
   can be attributed to its start.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Code in `crates/frob-spec` (the field, canonical form, admission, both views) and `crates/frob-arena` (the
      application at tick 0 and the override); the schema `frob.task/1` lists the field.
- [ ] A task without the field and the same task with the defaults written out have equal `TaskId`s; changing the
      evaluation start to `cold` gives a different `TaskId`.
- [ ] Each of the four admission failures refuses with its code and a pointer at the offending key.
- [ ] `frob eval run` of the planted undersized-knee design (knee at I_rms = 18 A on the worked card, 3% inside its
      18.6 A rating) under the default start begins at 143.8 °C, inside the 0.6 derating band, with a derating
      event at the first step; with `--thermal-start cold` the 20 s episode has no thermal event.
- [ ] A design whose knee runs at I_rms = 20 A is refused with FROB-REF-132 under the default start, returning
      190 °C and 18.6 A, and no episode is run.
- [ ] An overridden run reports a derived `TaskId` different from the task's, with both in `bound`.
- [ ] Replay of a recorded episode reproduces the initial node temperatures bit for bit.
- [ ] An L1 run records the declaration and carries the L1 flag.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-spec/tests/thermal_start_field.rs`:
- `default_written_out`: the canonical bytes of a file without the field contain the defaults.
- `task_id_tracks_start`: equal for omitted and explicit defaults; different for `cold`.
- `weights_must_sum_to_one`: (1/3, 1/3, 1/4) refuses and prints 11/12.
- `soaked_requires_temperature_unit`: `"80 m"` refuses with FROB-REF-535; `"80 degC"` is 353.15 K.
- `unknown_start_refused`: `"warm"` refuses with FROB-REF-891.
- `sexpr_roundtrip`: JSON → S-expression → JSON is byte-identical.

`crates/frob-arena/tests/thermal_start_apply.rs` (fixtures `synthetic`):
- `eval_applies_start_at_tick0`: the worked card at I_rms = 18 A starts at a rise of 64.8/(0.80 − 0.2547) =
  118.8 K above 25 °C.
- `no_silent_cold`: the 20 A design returns the refusal and zero episodes.
- `override_derives_task`, `l1_flag_present`, `replay_reproduces_start`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_referee_dynamics.sh` (bead N15a-24) runs the undersized-knee fixture twice through
`frob eval run <task> --design <design> --tier referee --level L2 --seed 1 --json`, once as written and once with
`--thermal-start cold`, and the 20 A design once. It asserts the first-step derating event, its absence from
cold, the refusal, and the two `TaskId`s. Logged per step, in the shared NDJSON form: the declared and resolved
start per actuator in kelvin, the duty source identity, `TaskId` and derived `TaskId`, the first derating event
time, the refusal payload, digests and seed.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-312 (20 s from cold at 20 A: rise 12.8 K; control: an evaluation that starts every episode at ambient and so
never meets derating); KA-304 (the equilibrium `steady` uses); CX-138 (episodes that start at ambient do not test
the thermal limits of a design). Negative controls: `mutant-thermal-start-ignored` (the field is parsed and
never applied: the undersized knee completes without an event and the test fails); a canonical form that drops
default values (two files with one meaning get two `TaskId`s; caught by `task_id_tracks_start`); a silent
fallback to `cold` on refusal. Determinism: the resolved start is a pure function of `ModelId`, `TaskId`, the
duty source and the world address, compared exactly across runs and platforms.

## Considerations

- The reference tasks of M1 (stand and recover, threshold delivery, timed docking) gain the field with their
  defaults; their recorded `TaskId`s change once, in the change that lands this bead, and that is written in the
  review log.
- `steady` uses the task as its own duty. A task that is one short burst after rest should declare `cold` or
  `soaked`; the task author says which, and the default errs toward the case that decides the design.
- The explanation verbs' planted knee-thermal episode declares `soaked(T)` in its task, because the default start
  of that design is refused; the refusal is the static form of the same finding.
- Open question, fail-closed default: whether a mission query may quantify over thermal start. Default: no; a
  mission reads the task's evaluation start. Owner: Sinew lead with the mission lead.
