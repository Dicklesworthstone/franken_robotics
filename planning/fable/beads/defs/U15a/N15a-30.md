## Background

Decision ADR-SINEW-08: actuator thermal state starts from a declared duty history, not from ambient, because
thermal time constants are minutes and episodes are seconds. The declaration has three values: `cold` (every node
at ambient), `soaked(T)` (every node at T) and `steady(task)` (the equilibrium for the task's mean loss, from the
temperature-dependent closed form). Evaluation defaults to `steady(task)`; training draws from the three, so that
a policy meets derating during training and not for the first time on hardware.

Three beads precede this one. N15a-15 defines the declaration, resolves it into initial winding and housing
temperatures per actuator, and refuses with FROB-REF-132 when `steady(task)` has no admissible equilibrium.
N15a-29 puts the declaration in the task file, where it is part of the task identity, and applies it on the
referee. BD-192 is `VecEnv`, the vectorized environment through which training and evaluation step batches of
worlds, with an explicit `reset(mask, seeds)`. What is still missing is the batched path: thousands of worlds are
reset per second, each needs its own start, and the same world address must receive the same start whatever
batch it sits in. Tier: Solid. Crate `frob-learn`; the thermal state it writes lives in the per-world node state
of the batched actuator kernel `K-act-l2` (BD-151).

Rejected: drawing the start from a host random generator at reset (the result would depend on reset order and
batch composition); warming worlds up by simulation (minutes of simulated time per reset for a state a closed
form gives exactly); one start per batch (a batch would then be all cold or all hot, and the mixture's weights
would hold only across batches).

## Scope

In: the per-design table of resolved starts built when an environment is constructed; the draw of a world's
start from the task's training mixture by world address; the write of initial node temperatures, derating band
and thermal-clock phase at `reset`, in both reset modes; the evaluation path, where every world takes the task's
evaluation start; the entry of the resolved start in the reset view and the episode record; the refusal at
construction when a design has no admissible `steady(task)`. Out, with the bead that has each: the declaration
and its resolution (N15a-15); the task-file field (N15a-29); `VecEnv` itself, its reset modes and seed plans
(BD-192, with world identity in N23a-04); the kernel state that holds node temperatures and the slow thermal
clock (BD-151, with the reference arithmetic of N15a-07); the settled-pose reset cache (BD-195); randomization
of card fields (BD-200).

## Technical approach

1. **Table at construction.** For each model slot of the environment (one per design in the batch) and the bound
   task, compute once on the host, through `resolve` of N15a-15: the `cold` temperatures (the scene's ambient);
   the `steady(task)` temperatures per actuator, T_w and T_h, with the duty source's identity; the admissible
   range of `soaked` temperatures [ambient, min over actuators of the lowest `t_max`]. The table is keyed by
   (`ModelId`, `TaskId`, duty source identity) and memoized.
2. **Refusal at construction.** If `steady(task)` has no admissible equilibrium for a design (s ≥ G, or the
   equilibrium is above a limit) and the selected use needs it (evaluation always; training when the mixture
   gives `steady` a positive weight or uses the symbol `steady` as a bound of `t_range`), construction refuses
   with FROB-REF-132, naming the model slot and returning the equilibrium or the margin to the tangency and the
   largest sustainable load. Repairs: remove the design from the batch; declare a mixture without `steady` for
   training; `regear` or `swap-actuator` on the binding joint. Nothing substitutes `cold`.
3. **Draw by address (training).** For a world being reset, the start is a pure function of the world's address
   (the identity `VecEnv` gives a world across resets and devices) and the stream named by the task: one uniform
   variate selects the mixture entry by cumulative exact weights; for `soaked` with `t_range` a second variate
   places T uniformly between the resolved bounds. The variates come from the counter-based stream at
   (stream name, world address, episode counter), so the same address and episode give the same start in any
   batch, slot, device or worker count.
4. **Write at reset.** For every masked world and every actuator slot: T_w and T_h are written to the node state;
   the compensated heat accumulators are zeroed; the thermal clock's phase accumulator is set to its start value;
   the derating band and the trip latch are set from the temperatures (a world may start inside a band; it never
   starts tripped, because a `soaked` temperature at or above a limit was refused in step 1). No kernel changes:
   these are ordinary state writes of the reset path, in the same pass that restores the settled pose.
5. **Masked autoreset mode.** When a world ends and is reset inside a step, the same write happens after the final
   observation has been copied out, with the episode counter already advanced, so the new episode's draw differs
   from the old one's.
6. **Evaluation.** With the environment constructed for evaluation every world takes the task's evaluation start
   (default `steady(task)`); no draw is made, and the stream is not advanced. Which use an environment has: its
   construction request, N16b-25's `EnvRequest`, carries the field `purpose` (default `evaluation`), and the
   environment's use follows from it (DESIGN: `use: Train` for `training`, `use: Evaluate` for every other
   purpose). `train.start` constructs with `purpose = training`; the `eval.*` and `sense.*` verbs with
   `evaluation`; `sim.run` with `--task` sets the field from the program-wide input `--purpose` (VERBS-sim; this
   bead owns its values `training` and `evaluation`, default `evaluation`; the other values and their admission
   rule are N19b-03's at M3), so that a task's training starts can be inspected without training. The actuator
   level is `sim.run`'s `--actuator-level` (N15a-04's type).
   **The override.** `--thermal-start cold | soaked:<T> | steady` on `sim.run` and `eval.run` is N15a-29's (M1):
   it replaces the task's selected declaration for that run and yields a derived `TaskId`. On the batched path
   this bead applies it the same way: with `--purpose evaluation` every world takes the overriding start; with
   `--purpose training` the override replaces the mixture, so every world takes that one start, no draw is
   made and the stream is not advanced (the derived task has no mixture). A `soaked` temperature outside the
   table's admissible range refuses at construction as in step 2.
7. **Level L1.** L1 has no thermal state: nothing is written, and the reset view carries the flag
   `thermal-start: no thermal state at L1`, as on the referee.
8. **Record.** The reset view and the episode's resolved-randomization record carry, per world, the selected
   start (`cold`, `soaked` with T, `steady`) and the duty source identity; per-actuator temperatures are
   recoverable from the table. Snapshots already contain node temperatures, so restore needs nothing new.
9. **Interaction with the reset cache.** The settled-pose cache (BD-195, a parallel M2 bead) holds mechanical
   state only. Its key does not include the thermal start, and the thermal write follows the pose restore, so
   a cached pose can never carry temperatures from the episode that produced it. Whichever of the two beads
   lands second places its write accordingly and enables the test `thermal_survives_pose_restore`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Code in `crates/frob-learn/src/env/thermal_start.rs`; the reset path calls it in both reset modes.
- [ ] Over 10³ world addresses with the default mixture, the counts of `cold`, `soaked` and `steady` lie inside
      the 99% binomial interval about 1/3 each (between 295 and 372), and `soaked` temperatures lie between
      ambient and the `steady` temperature.
- [ ] The same world address and episode counter receive the same start alone, inside batches of 1,024 and
      4,096, in another slot and on another device (compared exactly).
- [ ] An evaluation environment starts every world at `steady(task)`: the worked card at I_rms = 18 A starts its
      winding 118.8 K above a 25 °C ambient, within 0.5%, in `f64` and within the single-precision tolerance in
      `f32`.
- [ ] A batch that contains one design with I_rms = 20 A on the worked card refuses at construction with
      FROB-REF-132, naming that model slot.
- [ ] A world that starts inside a derating band records a derating event at its first step.
- [ ] After a masked autoreset the next episode's start differs from the previous one's with the probability the
      mixture implies, and equals the start an explicit reset would have produced.
- [ ] Reset throughput with the thermal write is within 1% of reset throughput without it on 4,096 worlds
      (TARGET; measured, with the disclosure block of the performance harness).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-learn/tests/thermal_start_reset.rs` (fixtures `synthetic`):
- `mixture_weights_by_address`: the counts of the second acceptance criterion; a mixture (1/2, 0, 1/2) written
  as two entries never yields `soaked`.
- `same_address_same_start`: across batch sizes, slots and worker counts.
- `eval_uses_task_start`: no draw, stream counter unchanged.
- `steady_refused_at_construction`: the 20 A design; with a training mixture of `cold` only, construction
  succeeds.
- `starts_in_band_emits_event`: bands (120 °C, 1.0), (140 °C, 0.6), (155 °C, 0): a start at 143.8 °C begins in the
  0.6 band and logs the event at step 1.
- `autoreset_advances_episode_counter`, `accumulators_zeroed`, `l1_flag_present`.
- `override_replaces_mixture`: a training environment built with the override `cold` starts all 10³ addresses
  at ambient and leaves the stream counter unchanged; `soaked` at a temperature above the lowest `t_max`
  refuses at construction with FROB-REF-132.
- `snapshot_restore_keeps_temperatures`: restore of a world reset to `soaked(80 °C)` returns 353.15 K.
- `thermal_survives_pose_restore` (enabled once BD-195's cache is present): a world reset from a cached pose
  recorded in a hot episode, with the start `cold`, reads ambient at every node after the reset.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_thermal_start_batched.sh`: `frob sim run <undersized-knee design> --task <task> --tier
batch-cpu --actuator-level L2 --worlds 1024 --seconds 20 --purpose training --seed 5 --key thermal-train --json`
and the same with `--purpose evaluation --key thermal-eval`, and with `--purpose training --thermal-start cold
--key thermal-train-cold` (every world `cold`, no draw); then `frob telemetry query <run> --channels
actuator.t_winding,event.derating --json` for each run; the 64 chosen addresses are re-run alone with `frob sim replay --run
<run> --world <w> --alone --key thermal-alone-<w> --json`, which must report `Bitwise`. Asserts the mixture counts, the
first-step derating events of worlds that start in a band, the absence of thermal events in `cold` worlds over
20 s, and equality of starts for 64 chosen addresses re-run alone. Sources the shared helper and logs NDJSON
lines with: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, counts per start with their binomial bounds, the
`steady` temperature against 143.8 °C with threshold 0.5%, per-address digests, seed, and a summary.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-304 (the equilibrium used by `steady`), KA-312 (12.8 K rise in 20 s from cold, the reason for the mechanism),
CX-138. Negative controls: `mutant-reset-thermal-cold` (reset writes ambient for every world: no world of the
undersized-knee batch meets derating, and the first-step assertion fails); a draw keyed by slot index in place
of world address (the address-equality test fails under a slot permutation); accumulators not zeroed (the first
thermal tick after reset applies heat from the previous episode; caught by `accumulators_zeroed`). Determinism
lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; `batch-cpu` against each device tier within the class the
kernel manifest states.

## Considerations

- A family batch mixes designs; one thermally infeasible design refuses the whole construction. That is the
  fail-closed reading. A softer rule (mark that design's worlds invalid and continue) is an open question for the
  Cortex lead and would need a line in the scorecard; default: refuse.
- The mixture changes what a policy sees at tick 0. Observations that include winding temperature must be
  declared in the task's terms, or the policy cannot tell a hot start from a cold one; that is an
  observability question for the task author, and the mechanism here does not hide it.
- Cost: two state writes per thermal node per reset; no measurable share of a reset that also restores a pose.
