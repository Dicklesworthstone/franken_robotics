## Background

Level L1 (BD-070) treats the winding as a resistor and the motor as cold. Level L2 adds what decides real
designs: winding current dynamics, copper resistance and magnet flux that change with winding temperature, a
two-node thermal network with torque derating, the voltage limit in the dq plane with field weakening, and a
supply whose voltage comes from the two-RC battery with its state of charge (N15b-03, BD-075). Tier: Solid
[S]. Crate `frob-actuate`; this bead is the referee form (double precision, on FrankenSim's port-Hamiltonian
machinery through the airlock crate `frob-fsim`).

Decisions that govern it. ADR-SINEW-04: winding resistance and magnet flux depend on temperature from L2
upward, because copper loss at a given current rises as the winding heats, which heats it further; a
constant-resistance model admits designs that run away. ADR-SINEW-05: three-phase machines are written in
power-invariant dq coordinates and a card must state its current convention; FrankenSim's field-oriented
controller uses that normalization (FACT: `frankensim/crates/fs-control/src/synchronous.rs`), in those
coordinates the torque constant equals the back-EMF constant, and a constant quoted per peak phase ampere,
per RMS phase ampere or per q-axis ampere differs by √(3/2), √2 and √3, so a missing convention is a silent
torque error of 22% to 73%. ADR-SINEW-08: thermal state starts from a declared duty history, not from
ambient, because thermal time constants are minutes and episodes are seconds. FACT: FrankenSim's winding
model derives resistance from material and geometry and infers no temperature law
(`frankensim/crates/fs-em/src/winding.rs`), so the temperature dependence is new work here.

## Technical approach

**Port-Hamiltonian form.** Every element is ẋ = (J(x) − R(x))·∇H(x) + G(x)·u with output y = G(x)ᵀ·∇H(x),
J skew-symmetric, R positive semidefinite, so Ḣ = uᵀy − ∇Hᵀ·R·∇H: stored energy changes by port power minus
dissipation. Worked instance, the brushed or q-axis motor: state x = (φ, p) with flux linkage φ = L·i and
rotor angular momentum p = J_r·ω; H = φ²/(2L) + p²/(2J_r); ∇H = (i, ω); J = [[0, −k], [k, 0]] (a gyrator of
modulus k, τ = k·i and e = k·ω, lossless); R = diag(R_w, b); u = (v, −τ_load). The rows read

  L·di/dt = v − R_w·i − k·ω,    J_r·dω/dt = k·i − b·ω − τ_load,    Ḣ = v·i − τ_load·ω − R_w·i² − b·ω².

**dq machine** (p pole pairs, electrical speed ω_e = p·ω, flux linkage λ):
  L_d·di_d/dt = v_d − R·i_d + ω_e·L_q·i_q,   L_q·di_q/dt = v_q − R·i_q − ω_e·(L_d·i_d + λ),
  τ = p·[λ·i_q + (L_d − L_q)·i_d·i_q],  subject to v_d² + v_q² ≤ V_lim² and i_d² + i_q² ≤ I_max².
On the referee the voltage limit is the exact inverter hexagon, as FrankenSim's controller enforces it
(FACT: `fs-control/src/synchronous.rs`); V_lim follows the bus voltage each step. Enforcing the two limits
each step, the reference currents and field weakening are BD-073; this bead supplies the unconstrained
current dynamics they act on.

**Temperature laws (implemented by BD-072, read here through one accessor).** R(T_w) = R₀·[1 + α·(T_w − T₀)]
from card fields `r_phase` at `t_ref` and `alpha_r`; λ(T_m) = λ₀·[1 + α_B·(T_m − T₀)] from `flux_linkage`
and `alpha_flux` (α_B negative), with T_m the magnet temperature: the housing node unless the card provides
a rotor node, and the single node of a one-node network. The stepped model calls
`winding_params(t_w, t_m) -> (R, λ)` and nothing else; with the constant default (α = α_B = 0) this bead's
gate rows pass, and BD-072 replaces the default with the two laws without touching the current dynamics.

**Thermal network.** Two nodes, winding (w) and housing (h), with capacities `c_winding`, `c_housing` and
conductances `g_winding_housing`, `g_housing_ambient`:
  C_w·dT_w/dt = P_cu + P_fe − G_wh·(T_w − T_h),
  C_h·dT_h/dt = G_wh·(T_w − T_h) + P_fric + P_gear − G_ha·(T_h − T_sink).
P_cu = R(T_w)·(i_d² + i_q²); friction and gear losses go to the housing node (DESIGN allocation); T_sink is
ambient or the shared sink node of the bus. The stored variable of a node is temperature; its internal
energy is U = C·(T − T_ref). Every loss reappears as heat into a node, so the composite is lossless up to
its ports: the only energy that leaves is mechanical work at the shaft and heat to ambient. The second law
is a per-step test with temperatures in kelvin: Σ P_loss,i/T_i + Σ G_ij·(T_i − T_j)²/(T_i·T_j) ≥ 0
(INV-SINEW-03).

**Derating and trip (BD-072).** Torque derating on the approach to a limit uses the card's authored bands,
as FrankenSim's controller does (FACT: `frankensim/crates/fs-control/src/thermal.rs`), applied stepwise as
authored with no interpolation; reaching `t_max_winding` or `t_max_magnet` trips the actuator, and a trip
latches until reset and is an event of class `thermal-limit`. That logic, its events and its tests are
BD-072's. This bead exposes what it needs: the node temperatures each step and a torque-scale input in
[0, 1] with a latch bit in the component state (in the restore set and in snapshots).

**Iron loss.** OPEN-SINEW-3 default: on when the card gives `iron_loss` coefficients; otherwise zero, with
the flag `iron loss not modelled` on every result.

**Initial thermal state.** The component is constructed with initial node temperatures, the resolved
`ThermalInit { t_winding, t_housing, derate_band }` of N15a-15; with none given, both nodes start at
ambient. The declaration (`ThermalStart`: `cold`, `soaked(T)`, `steady(task)`), the computation of the
`steady(task)` equilibrium, its refusal when no admissible equilibrium exists (FROB-REF-132; never a silent
`cold`), the evaluation default and the training mixture are N15a-15's, which depends on BD-072 and so
cannot be a dependency of this bead.

**Integration.** The component steps with FrankenSim's discrete-gradient integrators, which keep the power
identity in the discrete step (FACT: stated in `frankensim/crates/fs-phs/src/circuit/battery.rs` for the
battery); the electrical time constant L/R is below a physics tick, and the implicit update is stable there.
An in-house `f64` reference of the same equations (implicit midpoint, which is the discrete gradient of a
quadratic energy) is kept as the CPU oracle for BD-151.

**State and identity.** State: i_d, i_q, T_w, T_h, the trip latch, loop integrators; all in the restore set
of the transactional step and in every snapshot. `ModelId` includes the level and whether iron loss is on.

- [ ] Resistance and flux are read through the one accessor; the torque-scale input and the latch bit exist

- [ ] Reachable through `frob sim run --tier referee --actuator-level L2 … --json`; thermal and electrical
  channels present in telemetry; cancellation tested.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-actuate/tests/l2_referee.rs` (fixtures `synthetic`):
- `locked_rotor_current_rise`: R_w = 1 Ω, L = 10 mH, step V: i(t) = (V/R_w)·(1 − e^(−t/τ)) with τ = 10 ms;
  63.2% at 10 ms within 1%.
- `electrical_time_constant_dc_ref_a`: L = 1 mH, R = 0.5 Ω, rotor locked, gives 2 ms.
- `thermal_rise_constant_r`: α = 0, P = 80 W, G = 0.80 W/K (single node): rise 100 K within 1%.
- `cold_start_20s_constant_r`: the worked card (R₀ = 0.20 Ω at 25 °C, G = 0.80 W/K, C = 120 J/K) at 20 A
  from cold with the constant default rises 100·(1 − e^(−20/150)) = 12.5 K after 20 s. The
  temperature-dependent rows of the same run (12.8 K after 20 s, KA-312; 155 °C at 385 s) are BD-072's
  stepped rows and N15a-15's `cold_rise_in_20s_ka312`.
- `ph_identity_exact`: with rational L, J_r, k, R_w, b the product ∇Hᵀ·(J − R)·∇H + uᵀy equals the stated
  Ḣ as a polynomial identity.
- `entropy_production_nonnegative`: 10⁴ random two-node states and losses.
- `winding_params_single_call_site`: the stepped model reads R and λ only through the accessor.
- `torque_scale_and_latch_restored`: a retried step restores the scale input and the latch bit.
- `thermal_init_temperatures`: initial temperatures of 80 °C start both nodes at 353.15 K.
- Tests that moved with their mechanisms and are not repeated here: `flux_falls_with_temperature`,
  `derating_band_scale` and `trip_latches_until_reset` (BD-072); the hexagon and circle clamps and the
  field-weakening references (BD-073).
- `iron_loss_flag`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_referee_dynamics.sh` (N15a-24) runs the L2 fixtures with `frob task fixture` and `frob sim
run --tier referee --actuator-level L2 --seed 7 --json`, asserting each row against 1% and logging measured
values, thresholds, evidence block and the gate name per line.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-293 (exact), KA-331 (L/R), KA-333 (P/G). KA-312 (12.8 K in 20 s; tolerance to be set, proposed 1%) and
KA-306 belong to the temperature laws (BD-072, N15a-15). Negative controls here: a mutant that drops the
winding dynamics (no electrical time constant) fails `locked_rotor_current_rise`; a regenerative mode
with a negated copper loss gives negative entropy production and is rejected. `mutant-l2-const-r` (α
ignored) is BD-072's control and must fail its stepped 20 A row; `mutant-thermal-init-cold` (CX-138) is
N15a-15's control and must be caught where evaluation expects `steady(task)`. Metamorphic: scaling C by 2
doubles the thermal time constant and leaves the equilibrium unchanged. Determinism: twice on one machine;
debug and release.

## Considerations

- Lumped thermal parameters are card values, Estimated unless calibrated; the second-law check has content
  only as a sign check on each assigned loss and cannot fail for a correct model.
- The allocation of friction and gear heat to the housing node is DESIGN and is revisited if calibration
  data shows winding heating from the gearbox.
- L1's screening flag sends flagged cards here for envelope claims.
- Memory: four more state scalars per actuator than L1.
