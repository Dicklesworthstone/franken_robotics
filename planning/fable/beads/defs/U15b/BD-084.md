## Background

Friction and lost motion inside a joint decide whether a robot can hold a load at rest, creep, or hunt in a
position loop. Two decisions fix how they are modelled. ADR-SINEW-02: **stiction and backlash are rows of
the solve, not forces computed before it.** A friction force computed from the previous velocity chatters
about zero and cannot hold a load at rest; a row with a bounded multiplier can. A dead zone computed outside
the solve cannot produce the impact when the gap closes. ADR-SINEW-03: **row bounds are frozen within a
solve**; the breakaway bound and its load-dependent part are evaluated from the state at the start of the
step, because a bound that falls with speed makes the friction potential non-convex and the fast tiers rely
on a strictly convex problem. Construction C-37 places joint rows in the same solve as contact, so
drivetrain stiction and lost motion interact with ground contact in one solve. Tier: Solid [S].

Ownership: Sinew (`frob-actuate`) emits row descriptors from the card; the dynamics subsystem (`frob-dyn`)
assembles them with the contact rows; the cones belong to the algebra and contact crates. This bead is the
**referee** form: exact set-valued rows with located stick, slip and closure events, on FrankenSim's
machinery through the airlock. The batched form is BD-155.

## Technical approach

**Row descriptors (DESIGN).**
```rust
pub enum JointRow {
    Stiction(StictionRowDesc),   // defined by N15a-08: joint, side, tau_c, tau_s, v_s, shape, c_load[4], eps, viscous
    Backlash(BacklashRowDesc),   // defined by N15a-09: joint, rotor, ratio, beta, k_tooth, tau_d, restitution
    Limit { joint: JointId, lower: Angle, upper: Angle },
}
```
The two descriptor structs and the functions that build them from a card are N15a-08 and N15a-09; this
bead defines only the wrapping enum and the limit variant. Descriptors are collected per joint by the
binding functor of the model compiler from the card and joint-side overrides; their memo key is the joint
hash with the card version.

**Stiction bound.** With Coulomb level τ_c, breakaway level τ_s, Stribeck velocity v_s, shape σ(x) =
1/(1 + x²) (`lorentzian`) or exp(−x²) (`gaussian`), load coefficient c_load of the current quadrant, and the
superscript ⁻ marking values at the start of the step:

  τ_b = τ_c + (τ_s − τ_c)·σ(v⁻/v_s) + c_load·abs(τ_transmitted⁻) + τ_iron.

τ_iron is the constant hysteresis share of iron loss that a level-L2 actuator with iron-loss coefficients
exports as `iron_coulomb` (BD-071), zero otherwise; it is read from the bound actuator, not from the card,
so the row needs no knowledge of the iron-loss law. The row's dissipation is reported split between the
`friction` (τ_c and the Stribeck excess), `gear` (c_load·abs(τ_transmitted⁻), the transmission loss that
N15a-17 books as gear loss) and `iron` (τ_iron) lines of the port account in proportion to the bound's
shares. Viscous friction is
not in the row; it is implicit damping on the diagonal of the mass matrix (the eddy share of iron loss is
added there too).

**Stiction row on the referee.** The friction torque lies in [−τ_b, τ_b] and opposes motion: a
one-dimensional cone. It is solved set-valued, with regularization ε = 0, by FrankenSim's implicit friction
step (FACT: `frankensim/crates/fs-tribo/src/stribeck_friction.rs` has `solve_implicit_friction` and
`SetValuedFrictionStep`). Stick and slip transitions are events located by the transactional step.

**Backlash rows.** Backlash needs the rotor as its own coordinate (card level L3, the Solid part). With
rotor angle θ, joint angle q and ratio N the relative angle is δ = θ/N − q, and the two rows are the
unilateral gaps

  β/2 − δ ≥ 0   and   β/2 + δ ≥ 0,

each with a nonnegative multiplier, exactly as a joint limit on δ. On the referee they are hard, with the
card's restitution, zero by default. Inside the gap the rotor and the link are uncoupled: the motor
accelerates freely across the gap and arrives with a relative speed, and an encoder on the motor side no
longer reports the joint. Gap closure is an impact event; simultaneous events are solved together per
island by the coupled impulse solve. Requesting backlash rows for a card with no rotor state is refused with
FROB-REF-241 (repair: raise the card level); a row option the tier does not carry is FROB-REF-136.

**Hard stops** are the unilateral limit rows on the joint coordinate.

**Per transaction (inside the referee step).**
1. At the start of the interval evaluate every τ_b from committed state and freeze it (INV-SINEW-05).
2. Register guards: sign change of joint velocity under a stuck row or torque reaching τ_b (stick↔slip);
   abs(δ) reaching β/2 (closure); joint coordinate reaching a limit.
3. Advance with the active set fixed; at the earliest guard time split the interval on the dyadic grid.
4. At an event evaluate every event row on the common pre-event state and solve the island's impulses once.
5. Commit, recording stick, slip, closure and stop events for telemetry.

**Rotor coordinates on the referee.** The referee carries each rotor with full coupling, including the
gyroscopic torque J_r·N·q̇·Ω of a spinning rotor on a link turning at Ω. With J_r = 10⁻⁴ kg·m², N = 9, q̇ =
20 rad/s and Ω = 5 rad/s that is 0.09 N·m (illustrative), under one percent of such an actuator's joint
torques. HYPOTHESIS: it stays negligible for the cards on file; the test is this referee against the reduced
model of N15b-08, recorded in the fidelity ledger by the experiment bead N15a-11.

**Encoders.** `encoder_side` selects θ/N or q; with backlash the two differ by up to β/2 (the semantics are
N15a-09's; the referee exposes both angles).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-dyn/tests/joint_rows_referee.rs` (fixtures `synthetic`, h = 2 ms):
- `breakaway_on_ramp`: torque ramp r = 1 N·m/s, τ_s = 0.5 N·m: motion begins at 0.5 s within one step
  (KA-297); and the referee row of KA-402 at the register's numbers, ramp k = 4 N·m/s against τ_s = 2 N·m:
  breakaway at τ_s/k = 0.5 s within one step and within 1%, asserted against N18a-32's
  `joint_row_known_answers()` (the expected values are read from that function, not restated).
- `spring_coulomb_decay`: inertia on a spring k = 10 N·m/rad with τ_c = 0.1 N·m, τ_s = 0.12 N·m, released
  from 0.2 rad: amplitude falls by 4·τ_c/k = 0.04 rad per cycle within 2%; the motion ends inside
  abs(q) ≤ τ_s/k = 0.012 rad.
- `held_load_no_drift`: 0.3 N·m against τ_b = 0.5 N·m: displacement stays below 10⁻¹² rad over 20 s.
- `reversal_inside_gap`: β = 0.5°, motor reversal of 0.3° (output side): output displacement exactly zero.
- `hysteresis_loop_width`: slow sinusoid of reflected amplitude 2° through β = 0.5°: the input-output loop
  has width 0.5° within 1%.
- `gap_closure_impact`: N = 9, J_r = 10⁻⁴ kg·m² (J₁ = N²·J_r = 0.0081), J₂ = 0.02 kg·m², Δω = 2 rad/s, no
  restitution: common speed J₁·Δω/(J₁ + J₂) = 0.577 rad/s; energy lost ½·J₁·J₂/(J₁ + J₂)·Δω² = 0.0115 J,
  both within 1%.
- `gap_crossing_time`: J = 10⁻⁴ kg·m² at 5 rad/s behind a ratio of 10 crosses a 0.5° gap in β·N/ω₀ =
  17.5 ms and leaves an output of 0.05 kg·m² at J·N·ω₀/(J·N² + I_out) = 0.083 rad/s. This is the referee row
  of KA-403 (GATE-TRAC-04): crossing time 17.45 ms, closure speed 0.0833 rad/s and energy lost at closure
  ½·J·ω₀² − ½·(J·N² + I_out)·ω₁² = 1.25 − 0.208 = 1.04 mJ, each within 1% of N18a-32's
  `joint_row_known_answers()`; the dead-zone mutant produces no closure event and fails it.
- `bounds_frozen`, `encoder_side_differs_by_gap`, `no_rotor_state_refuses_241`, `hard_stop_holds`.
- `iron_share_in_bound`: a descriptor with τ_c = τ_s = 0.1 N·m and `iron_coulomb` = 0.014 N·m holds 0.113
  N·m at rest and breaks away under 0.115 N·m; while sliding, 0.014/0.114 of the row's dissipation is
  reported on the `iron` line and the rest on `friction`, summing to the row total to round-off; with
  c_load = 0.25 and a transmitted torque of 0.4 N·m added (τ_b = 0.214 N·m), the shares while sliding are
  0.1/0.214 `friction`, 0.1/0.214 `gear` and 0.014/0.214 `iron`.
- Property `row_potential_convex`: for random frozen bounds the set-valued map is monotone.

digest.

## Considerations

- OPEN-SINEW-2 (default Stribeck shape and ε on batched tiers) does not affect the referee, which uses ε = 0
  and either shape.
- An accumulation of impacts inside one base interval beyond the event cap is a numerical refusal of the
  transactional step, not a physical failure.
- Wear grows β over life; β is a per-world parameter so that the lifecycle work can vary it.
- The referee's 21-contact capacity is unaffected: joint rows are not contact rows.
