## Background

The studio's rule is to check before simulating: static analyses answer in milliseconds what a rollout
would answer in seconds, and they refuse a design with ranked repairs before any simulation is spent on it.
Stage 6 of the preflight (`design.check`, "statics and sizing") runs A-08, A-10 and A-11 (and, from BD-108,
A-20 to A-23); A-10 and A-11 are this bead. Crate `frob-analyze`, tier Solid [S].

**A-10, co-design closure:** does the mass spiral close, and what is the least mass and power? Heavier
actuators need more torque, which needs heavier actuators. Construction C-12 treats a design with such
feedback as a least fixed point of a monotone map, found by Kleene iteration. The worked card: actuator
mass m_a = τ_req/ρ_τ with torque density ρ_τ; required torque τ_req = c·m·g·L; total mass m = m_f + n·m_a.
So m = m_f/(1 − κ) with loop gain κ = n·c·g·L/ρ_τ, and the design closes only if κ < 1. With n = 12,
c = 0.25, L = 0.30 m, ρ_τ = 15 N·m/kg: κ = 0.59 and m = 2.43·m_f, so a 6 kg structure becomes a 14.6 kg
robot; with L = 0.50 m, κ = 0.98 and m = 53·m_f; κ reaches 1 at L = 0.51 m. Leg length is not free: every
kilogram of structure costs 1/(1 − κ) kilograms of robot.

**A-11, actuator envelope:** do the torque-speed points of standing, crouching, stepping up and the nominal
gait fit inside each card, with thermal steady state? It uses Sinew's static functions (envelope, thermal
equilibrium, bus feasibility) and must use the temperature-dependent law (decision ADR-SINEW-04): on the
worked card a design sized to hold 20 A passes a constant-resistance check (rise 100 K, 125 °C) and is
refused by the corrected one (rise 164.7 K, 190 °C against a 155 °C limit).

## Technical approach

**A-10.**
1. Build the budget graph from the model's budget functor: per actuated joint, the required peak and
   continuous torque as a monotone function of the masses it carries (A-08's load sharing at the declared
   poses gives the coefficients; in the simplest form τ_req,j = c_j·m·g·L_j); per card, mass as a function
   of required torque (continuous: m_a = τ/ρ_τ; with a catalog, the lightest card whose ratings admit the
   requirement, a step function); battery mass as a function of required energy and peak power; structure
   mass fixed.
2. State x = (actuator masses, battery mass) in a product of chains ordered by ≤. The map f(x) = sizes
   required when the robot weighs m_f + Σx. f is monotone.
3. Kleene iteration from the bottom element: x₀ = ⊥, x_{k+1} = f(x_k). On a finite catalog chain it
   terminates; on continuous sizes it stops when ‖Δ_k‖ falls below the tolerance.
4. Convergence estimate: λ̂_k = ‖Δ_k‖/‖Δ_{k−1}‖; remaining error at most λ̂/(1 − λ̂)·‖Δ_k‖. If λ̂ ≥ 1 for
   three consecutive iterations, or an iterate needs a card above the top of its catalog chain, the spiral
   does not close: refuse with FROB-REF-116 (co-design does not close: loop gain at or above one), returning
   the loop gain, the iterate at exit and repairs. If the iteration contracts (λ̂ < 1) but its least fixed
   point exceeds a declared mass or power budget, the spiral closes above the budget: FROB-REF-115 (a design
   budget exceeded), returning the binding budget, the least fixed point against the limit with units and
   the largest contributors. DESIGN (the registry row of `analyze.closure` lists both codes): since the
   iterates increase, an iterate above the budget already proves the overrun; iteration still continues to
   the fixed point (at most 200 iterations) so the payload states the full overrun, and a design whose
   iteration both contracts and passes the budget is 115, never 116.
5. Output: `closure: closed | infeasible`, least total mass, least peak and continuous power, the per-joint
   sizes at the fixed point, loop gain κ (estimated), iterations, and the binding budget (the declared mass or
   power budget with the least margin at the fixed point, and that margin). Ranked repairs when infeasible,
   over budget or close to κ = 1: shorten the lever (a `set-param` edit on the link length), raise torque
   density (`swap-actuator`), `regear`, reduce payload; for 115 also `set-budget` when the limit itself is
   wrong.
6. Typing: the result is `exact` for the stated monotone model and names it; on continuous sizes with a
   catalog surrogate it is Estimated. Determinism D0 on rational inputs.

**A-11.**
1. Operating points per actuated joint (DESIGN: the plan names the four situations and not how they are
   computed): `standing`, `crouching` and `stepping-up` are quasi-static inverse-statics torques at the
   archetype's declared poses with zero speed, using A-08's contact forces under the declared load-sharing
   model; `nominal-gait` is a set of (torque, speed) samples and an RMS current from the archetype's gait
   template (stride, duty factor, speed). Each point records the pose or phase it came from.
2. Envelope: each point must lie inside the card's torque-speed boundary at the stated winding temperature
   and bus voltage (BD-073; L1 law i ≤ min(I_max, (V_lim − k_e·N·ω)/R), and the dq voltage ellipse where
   the card is flagged for L2). Outside: FROB-REF-131 with the point, the boundary at that speed, and the
   gear-ratio or card edit that admits it.
3. Thermal: for each required operating point held, and for the RMS current of the nominal gait, compute
   the equilibrium of the temperature-dependent law or its absence (BD-072): with s = I²·R₀·α and G the
   conductance from winding to ambient (G itself for a one-node card; the series value
   G_wh·G_ha/(G_wh + G_ha) for the two-node winding-and-housing network, KA-311), a stable equilibrium
   T_amb + P_amb/(G − s) exists exactly when s < G; under torque holding the tangency is solved. No equilibrium, or one above `t_max_winding` or `t_max_magnet`:
   FROB-REF-132 with the equilibrium or the margin to the tangency and the largest sustainable load.
   Iron loss (OPEN-SINEW-3 default, decided by N15b-27): when the card gives `iron_loss` coefficients, the
   loss curve is P(T) = I²·R(T) + P_fe with BD-071's law P_fe = k_h·abs(ω_e) + k_e·ω_e², evaluated at the
   point's own speed, ω_e = `pole_pairs`·N·ω_joint (zero for the three quasi-static points; for the nominal
   gait, DESIGN, the mean of P_fe over the gait samples, since the thermal time constants are far longer
   than a stride), and the equilibrium and the largest sustainable current come from N15a-14's general
   criterion, in which P_fe adds as a temperature-independent term. A card without coefficients is evaluated
   with P_fe = 0 and every A-11 result built on it carries the condition `iron-loss-not-modelled` (wording
   `iron loss not modelled`), which propagates by union into the stage-6 report.
4. Bus: the peak demand of the nominal gait's time-resolved profile against the ceiling E²/(4·R) at
   end-of-discharge voltage and cold-temperature resistance (BD-075): FROB-REF-133.
5. Strict data: any `assumed` card field in the read set under `--strict-data`: FROB-REF-113 (N15b-06).
6. Findings carry a code such as `A-11.knee.continuous`, a severity, a message ("Knee continuous torque
   demand exceeds the card by 31% in crouch"), and ranked repairs with predicted effects, for example
   `swap-actuator` to the next card (`predicted: mass +0.9 kg, margin +46%`) and `regear` to ratio 1.9.

**Memo nodes.** Each analysis is a memo node keyed by the model identity restricted to the fields it reads,
its own version and its parameters; an edit outside those fields recomputes nothing.

**Envelope output.** Evidence class the weakest card field read; tier `static`; determinism `exact`;
`reality: no-claim`. Time budget (TARGET, 95th percentile): A-08, A-10 and A-11 share 600 ms of stage 6,
whose whole budget is 755 ms unbound and 875 ms with A-20 and A-24 bound (N14bx-01); stages 1 to 6 complete
within 2 s at the 95th percentile on the archetypes.

## Considerations

- The budget functor applies in the monotone fragment only. Where a change of gearing trades a larger
  static moment for a blocked swing or a new contact mode, the alternatives stay in separate cells or the
  full mechanics are dispatched; A-10 must not force the plant into a monotone model.
- Coupled transmissions: the feasible joint-torque set is the image Cᵀ·B of the motor-torque box, not a box
  of per-joint limits (N15b-10); A-11 reads the generators for joints behind a coupling.
- Kill gate KILL-SYNTH-CODESIGN (M4) restricts the later synthesis lane to this closure analysis; nothing
  here changes.
- A verdict that changes sign inside a card's band is labelled card-conditional at M3 (N15b-07).
