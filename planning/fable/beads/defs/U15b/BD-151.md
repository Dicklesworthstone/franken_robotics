## Background

The batched tiers run every world through the same kernels with no data-dependent control flow across
worlds, in double or single precision, on the CPU and on two GPU backends from one dialect source. Stage 6
of the batched step computes dynamics terms, including actuator torques and passive forces; this bead
supplies its actuator kernels. The referee components (BD-070, BD-071, N15b-03, BD-075) are the trusted
form; here the same physics is re-derived as kernels, and the gate is a **Fidelity** claim: batched L1 and
L2 kernels agree with the referee components within 10⁻⁴ relative on a 10⁴-case battery (GATE-SINEW-03, M2).

Decision ADR-SINEW-07 shapes the arithmetic: stiff electrical and thermal states take implicit rational
updates on the batched tiers, and slow states run on their own rational clocks. Reasons: the electrical time
constant L/R is below a physics tick, so an explicit update is unstable there; and batched kernels use no
transcendental built-ins (the numerics rules ban built-in transcendental functions, fused multiply-add,
fast-math and floating-point atomics; `sqrt` is correctly rounded and allowed). Each of these choices
leaves a residual in the port energy balance, which the single-precision row of GATE-SINEW-02 bounds
(measured by the batched energy ledger, BD-208). Tier: Solid [S]. Crates: `frob-kernels` (kernels),
`frob-actuate` (the laws and CPU references).

## Scope

In: four kernels with manifests, `batch-cpu` skeletons and dialect sources; the CPU references of `K-passive`
and `K-act-bus` (those of `K-act-l1` and `K-act-l2` are the host reference programs of N15a-07, lowered
unchanged); the thermal clock as kernel state; the battery update inside `K-act-l2`'s state; the within-world
bus reduction with brownout and exhaustion flags; the GATE-SINEW-03 battery re-measured on the kernels. This
seed is the capstone for the batched actuator kernels and depends on the bead that builds their arithmetic.
Out, with the bead that has each: the L1 and L2 update formulas, the rational clock, the compensated
accumulator, their residuals, and the definition of the 10⁴-case battery (N15a-07); the run of that battery
from the user's side on every tier with the gate receipt (N15b-35); the joint rows (BD-155); the step that
launches the kernels (BD-159); device backends; the energy-ledger kernel (BD-208); randomization of card
parameters into the parameter row (BD-200).

## Technical approach

**Data.** Per world and actuator slot: state (current or i_d, i_q; delay state; trip latch), parameters from
the model slot's actuator-card slots and the per-world parameter row. Per world and thermal node: T_w, T_h
and compensated heat accumulators. Per world and bus: battery state z, V₁, V₂, bus voltage, flags.

**`K-act-l1`.**
1. V_lim = V_bus for `dc`; V_bus·0.70710678… (the literal 1/√2) for `svm-linear`.
2. s = sign of the current reference; i_avail = min(I_max, max(0, (V_lim − s·k_e·N·ω)/R)), with minimum and
   maximum by explicit selects.
3. i_tgt = s·min(abs(i_ref), i_avail).
4. Delay, implicit and rational: i⁺ = (i + (h/τ_d)·i_tgt)/(1 + h/τ_d).
5. Generalized force N·k_t·i⁺ into the joint's entry; electrical power (R·i⁺ + k_e·N·ω)·i⁺ to the bus sum;
   copper loss to the heat accumulator. Reflected inertia is already in the mass matrix from the lowering.

**`K-act-l2`.**
1. Brushed or q-axis winding: i⁺ = (i + (h/L)·(v − k·ω))/(1 + h·R(T)/L), with R(T) and k(T) taken at the
   temperature of the last thermal tick.
2. dq winding: the implicit update is a 2 × 2 linear solve in closed form,
   (1 + h·R/L_d)·i_d⁺ − h·ω_e·(L_q/L_d)·i_q⁺ = i_d + (h/L_d)·v_d,
   h·ω_e·(L_d/L_q)·i_d⁺ + (1 + h·R/L_q)·i_q⁺ = i_q + (h/L_q)·(v_q − ω_e·λ),
   whose determinant (1 + h·R/L_d)·(1 + h·R/L_q) + (h·ω_e)² is positive.
3. Voltage limit: the commanded (v_d, v_q) is scaled onto the circle inscribed in the inverter's hexagon,
   radius V_bus/√2, which is conservative by at most 1 − √3/2 = 13.4% of voltage at a hexagon vertex (the
   referee enforces the exact hexagon). Current limit: i_d² + i_q² ≤ I_max². Field-weakening reference from
   the closed form of the envelope law (BD-073).
4. Torque p·[λ(T)·i_q + (L_d − L_q)·i_d·i_q]; derating by the card's bands with selects; the trip latch.
5. Thermal nodes advance on a slower rational clock (DESIGN default: every 50 physics steps, 0.1 s at
   h = 2 ms; thermal time constants are minutes). Between ticks each node receives heat in a compensated
   sum. At a tick the two-node implicit update is the closed-form solve of
   (C_w/h_th + G_wh)·T_w⁺ − G_wh·T_h⁺ = (C_w/h_th)·T_w + Q_w/h_th,
   −G_wh·T_w⁺ + (C_h/h_th + G_wh + G_ha)·T_h⁺ = (C_h/h_th)·T_h + Q_h/h_th + G_ha·T_sink.
6. Battery (per bus): Vₖ⁺ = [Vₖ·(1 − h/(2·τₖ)) + h·I/Cₖ]/(1 + h/(2·τₖ)); z⁺ = z − h·I/Q. If z⁺ would pass a
   bound the world's bus is flagged `battery-exhausted` and opened by a select; nothing is clamped.

**`K-passive`.** Passive joint forces: declared joint springs −k_s·(θ − θ₀), using the turn count for laws
typed `lifted` (a linear torsion spring) and the unit complex number for `periodic` ones; declared joint
damping b enters the diagonal of A as h·b (implicit); gravity-compensation springs and payload springs use
the same law.

**`K-act-bus`.** The within-world reduction for a shared bus:
1. Sum member electrical powers and currents per bus in a fixed-shape pairwise tree anchored at member
   index 0, padding with −0.0 (no floating-point atomics; the result does not depend on co-tenant worlds).
2. Shared current limit: factor min(1, current_max/I) written back to the members' current limits.
3. Source E = OCV(z) − V₁ − V₂ (OCV by a piecewise-linear table lookup), R = R₀ + R_harness.
4. Discriminant D = E² − 4·R·P. If D ≥ 0, V = (E + √D)/2. If D < 0 there is no operating point: the world
   gets the `brownout` event flag and its members' drivers are disabled by select under the undervoltage
   policy. No kernel clips the demand.
5. Regeneration sums are routed by policy (N15b-04).
The bus voltage read by `K-act-l1` and `K-act-l2` in a step is the committed voltage from the start of the
step (a one-step lag, the same rule as other state-dependent coefficients held constant inside a solve).

**Manifests.** Class floating; determinism D2, D1 where the device family passed the IEEE probe gate; read
and write footprints; dimensions checked; CPU references `k_act_l1_ref`, `k_act_l2_ref`, `k_passive_ref`,
`k_act_bus_ref` in `frob-actuate`.

**The GATE-SINEW-03 battery (DESIGN: the plan fixes size and tolerance, not the cases).** The battery is
defined once, in N15a-07, and this bead runs it unchanged on the kernels. 10⁴ cases drawn
by address: a card from the catalog with fields drawn inside their bands, a level, and an operating point
(speed, command, bus state, winding temperature). Compared against the referee component:
(a) L1 current, torque and electrical power once the delay state has settled;
(b) L2 settled currents, torque, copper loss and bus voltage with inputs held for at least twenty electrical
    time constants; node temperatures at the end; energy drawn and heat produced over the case;
(c) band-limited transients whose fastest input time scale is at least 20 steps, as RMS error over
    the episode relative to the peak, written to the fidelity ledger with their own declared envelope and
    not judged at 10⁻⁴.
(An earlier draft of this bead held inputs for five time constants and counted class (c) inside the gate.
Five constants leave about 0.7% of the transient, and a first-order implicit update cannot hold 10⁻⁴ on a
transient, so the definition of N15a-07 governs; whether class (c) ever enters the 10⁻⁴ judgment is an OPEN
item for the Sinew lead, default no.) Transients faster than the step are outside the gate and are recorded
in the fidelity ledger with their own envelope. Operating points where the referee's exact hexagon admits more voltage than the inscribed circle
are expected conservative differences (at most 13.4% of voltage); they are counted, reported in their own
ledger row with the sign checked (the batched tier never delivers more), and not counted against the 10⁻⁴
tolerance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Four kernels with manifests in `crates/frob-kernels`; the dialect lint and the dimension checker pass.
- [ ] GATE-SINEW-03 (TARGET; seed done-condition): agreement with the referee components within 10⁻⁴
  relative on the 10⁴-case battery, in `f64` and `f32` on `batch-cpu`; receipt with the worst case.
- [ ] `spark.check` passes for each kernel on each admitted device family.
- [ ] A brownout world is flagged in 100 of 100 above-ceiling trials and no world completes a step at the
  requested power; exhaustion is flagged and never clamped (the two clip mutants are caught).
- [ ] The inscribed-circle limit never exceeds the hexagon limit on 10⁴ random operating points (the
  circle-against-hexagon row of GATE-SINEW-06, measured where the circle is used).
- [ ] A world's outputs are bit-identical under any co-tenancy, slot permutation and bucket capacity.
- [ ] Cancellation mid-chunk leaves no leaked device buffers.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-kernels/tests/k_act.rs`:
- `l1_matches_reference`: 10⁴ random states, kernel against `k_act_l1_ref`, bitwise in `f64`.
- `l1_stall_and_no_load`: the `dc-ref-a` card gives 18.0 N·m at stall and zero torque at 48 rad/s.
- `l2_current_update_formula`: L = 1 mH, R = 0.5 Ω, h = 2 ms, v − k·ω = 1 V, i = 0: i⁺ = (0 + 2)/(1 + 1) =
  1.0 A (steady value 2.0 A).
- `l2_dq_determinant_positive` (10⁶ random inputs).
- `thermal_tick_two_node`: steady input reproduces the closed-form steady temperatures within 10⁻⁵.
- `thermal_heat_accumulator_compensated`: 10⁶ additions of 10⁻³ J in `f32` sum to 1,000 J within 10⁻⁴.
- `battery_update_matches_reference`, `exhaustion_flag_not_clamp`.
- `bus_voltage_closed_form`: E = 48 V, R = 0.08 Ω, P = 1,800 W gives 44.785 V.
- `bus_brownout_flag`: P = 8,000 W sets the flag and no voltage is reported as valid.
- `bus_sum_independent_of_padding`: capacities 4, 8 and 16 give identical bits.
- `passive_spring_lifted`: 0.5 N·m/rad at θ = 2π exerts −π N·m and stores π² J; at θ = 0, zero.
- `circle_inside_hexagon` (10⁴ points).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_kernels_fidelity.sh` (bead N15b-35) runs the battery with `frob task fixture
sinew-act-fidelity --tier batch-cpu --precision f64|f32 --seed 13 --json`, `frob spark check --kernel
K-act-l1` (and the other three), and `frob fidelity ledger`, asserting the 10⁻⁴ threshold and logging the
worst case with its card, operating point, both values and the gate name.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-296 (the battery itself), KA-319 (circle inside hexagon, conservative by at most 13.4%), KA-324 and
KA-325 on the batched tier. Negative controls: `mutant-bus-clip-demand`, `mutant-battery-clip-soc`; an
explicit current update (`mutant-l2-explicit-euler`) must blow up on the stiff fixture (h·R/L = 10) and be
caught. Metamorphic: batch-composition invariance. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker
counts; debug and release; across backends under each class.

## Considerations

- The one-step lag of bus voltage and the temperature frozen between thermal ticks are sources of the
  balance residual; if GATE-SINEW-02's single-precision row fails, the first repair is a shorter thermal
  period.
- The allocation rule under a shared current limit follows BD-075 and is DESIGN.
- Kill gate KILL-KIR (derivation toolchain) only changes how the arithmetic is written: hand-written and
  bound by differential tests.
- Memory: about a dozen scalars per actuator and world; negligible beside the dense Jacobian.
