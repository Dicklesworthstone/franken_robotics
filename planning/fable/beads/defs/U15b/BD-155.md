## Background

The batched tiers (`batch-cpu` and the GPU tiers under the `lockstep` profile) advance thousands of worlds
through the same ten stages with no data-dependent control flow across worlds. Stage 7 builds constraint
rows and stage 8 solves one strictly convex problem per world by Newton's method with a dense Cholesky
factor. Joint stiction, backlash and hard stops enter that same solve as rows (decision ADR-SINEW-02;
construction C-37), which is what lets drivetrain friction and lost motion interact with ground contact.
The referee solves these rows exactly with located events (BD-084); the batched tiers cannot branch per
world, so each row becomes a convex potential with a stated regularization, and the cost of that
regularization is measured against the referee, never hidden. Decision ADR-SINEW-03 keeps row bounds frozen
within a solve so the potential stays convex (theorem card T-08: the fast-tier objective is a sum of
positive-semidefinite terms). Tier: Solid [S]. Crates: `frob-kernels` (the kernel), `frob-actuate`
(descriptors and the bound law).

A kill gate stands over this mechanism. KILL-SINEW-ROWS: if, with stiction rows enabled, the fast-tier solve
needs more than 1.5 times the Newton iterations of the smooth-friction model on the contact battery, or
throughput falls by more than 20%, after one repair cycle, then throughput tiers apply regularized friction
as a force, the rows stay on the referee and the `strict` profile of `batch-cpu`, results from the smooth
model carry a flag, and the fidelity ledger measures the gap.

## Technical approach

**Stiction row.** For joint velocity v at the end of the step, step h and frozen bound τ_b, the potential is

  ℓ(v) = h·τ_b·ψ_ε(v),   ψ_ε(v) = v²/(2ε) for abs(v) ≤ ε,   abs(v) − ε/2 otherwise.

Gradient: h·τ_b·v/ε inside the kink, h·τ_b·sign(v) outside. Hessian term: h·τ_b/ε inside, zero outside.
The row's impulse −∂ℓ/∂v lies in [−h·τ_b, h·τ_b]: it balances any smaller applied impulse with the joint
almost at rest and saturates when the joint slides. The bound, evaluated from the state at the start of the
step (superscript ⁻):

  τ_b = τ_c + (τ_s − τ_c)·σ(v⁻/v_s) + c_load·abs(τ_transmitted⁻) + τ_iron,

with Coulomb level τ_c, breakaway level τ_s, Stribeck velocity v_s, the shape σ(x) = 1/(1 + x²) (the default
on batched tiers because it needs no exponential), the load coefficient of the current quadrant (the
transmission's efficiency pair, BD-074), and τ_iron the hysteresis share of iron loss that `K-act-l2` writes
to the slot's `iron_coulomb` (BD-151; zero at L1 or without coefficients), the same term as the referee row
of BD-084. The row's dissipation is split between the ledger's `friction` (τ_c and the Stribeck excess),
`gear` (the c_load share, N15a-17) and `iron` lines in proportion to the bound's shares, as on the referee.
OPEN-SINEW-2 default: rational shape and ε = 10⁻³ rad/s, both
card-overridable. The Gaussian shape follows the tier capability table of N15a-04: it is card-selectable on
`batch-cpu` (both profiles) through the kernel library's deterministic exponential (range reduction and a
fixed polynomial in basic arithmetic, no built-in transcendental), and a GPU tier whose kernel class does not
admit that exponential refuses it with FROB-REF-136, returning the tiers whose cell is "yes". A request never
falls back to the rational shape in silence. Viscous friction is implicit damping on the diagonal of A.

**What the convex form costs, stated and measured.** A joint holding a torque τ below its bound creeps at
ε·τ/τ_b, so over a time t it drifts by at most ε·t. Because the bound is frozen within a step, a stick-slip
oscillation can depend on h. Both appear in the fidelity ledger against the referee.

**Backlash rows.** δ = θ/N − q, rows β/2 − δ ≥ 0 and β/2 + δ ≥ 0. On the convex tiers they are compliant
with the card's tooth stiffness k and a dissipation time τ_d, in the same convex compliant form as the
contact law's normal row so that there is one regularization (DESIGN): with e⁻ the excess of abs(δ) over
β/2 at the start of the step and ė(v) its rate,

  ℓ(v) = [h·k / (2·(h + τ_d))]·⟨e⁻ + (h + τ_d)·ė(v)⟩₊²,

whose derivative h·k·⟨e⁻ + (h + τ_d)·ė⟩₊ is the implicit spring-damper impulse and whose Hessian is
h·k·(h + τ_d) when active, zero otherwise. Inside the gap the row is inactive and rotor and link are
uncoupled. Backlash rows requested for a card without rotor state refuse with FROB-REF-241.

**Hard stops** are the unilateral limit rows of stage 7 on the joint coordinate, same compliant form.

**Kernel, per world and row (no cross-world control flow).**
1. Bound pass at the start of the step: read committed v⁻ and transmitted torque, compute and store τ_b
   (rational arithmetic and one division; no transcendental).
2. For the Newton iterate: evaluate each row's argument, gradient and curvature with selects (a mask is a
   select, never an arithmetic blend).
3. Write Jacobian entries: e_j for an output-side stiction row; for rotor rows, the per-rotor scalars d_i,
   c_i and the diagonal contribution of N15b-08, so that the dense system keeps size n.
4. Accumulate row dissipation for the energy ledger.
Padding rows are identity rows that trail the active ones and hold the neutral element.

**Manifest.** Class: floating. Inputs and outputs with physical dimensions (checked by the kernel type
checker). Determinism claimed: D2 (bitwise per backend), D1 where the device family passed the IEEE probe
gate. Read and write footprints per buffer; CPU reference symbol `k_joint_rows_ref`. Arithmetic derived by
the derivation toolchain where it can express it, hand-written and bound by differential tests otherwise.

**Capacity.** Row capacity R counts one limit row and one stiction row per actuated joint; each joint at
card level L3 adds two backlash rows. A world over capacity is `Degraded` with a reason, never truncated.
β, τ_c, τ_s, v_s and k are per-world parameters in the parameter row.

**Kill-gate instrumentation.** A comparator model `smooth-friction` applies friction as a regularized force
τ_f = −τ_b·v/√(v² + ε²) before the solve. Counters record Newton iterations per world-step and whole-step
throughput for both models on the contact battery.

**Selecting the model from the surface (DESIGN; needed so that the kill gate is measured from the user's side).**
The joint-friction model is a field of the step plan, `joint_friction = rows | smooth` (default `rows`), part of
`StepPlanId`, set on `sim.run` by the option `--joint-friction rows|smooth`. `smooth` drops the stiction rows
from `K-joint-rows` (backlash and limit rows stay) and applies τ_f before the solve; it is carried by the
lockstep tiers only, and requesting it on `referee` or under `strict` refuses with `FROB-REF-136`, returning the
tiers that carry it. Every result computed with `smooth` carries the condition `smooth-joint-friction` in its
claim product, whether it was requested or (after a KILL-SINEW-ROWS fallback, N15b-24) the default. The
receipt of a lockstep run reports, per world-step, the Newton iteration at which the convergence certificate
latched (a histogram) and the run's world-steps per second, so that both arms are read from receipts. The
option is in the `sim.run` row of VERBS-sim with this bead as its owner (M2, identity `StepPlanId`); the
row's rule is the one above.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `K-joint-rows` exists with manifest, Rust skeleton for `batch-cpu`, dialect source and CPU reference;
  the dialect lint finds no banned built-in.
- [ ] GATE-SINEW-04, batched rows (TARGET; seed done-condition), within the stated regularization: breakaway
  within one step of τ_s/r; amplitude loss per cycle within 2% of 4·τ_c/k and rest inside the band; drift at
  most ε·t; zero output motion inside the gap; loop width within 1% of β plus the tooth deflection τ/k;
  closure speed and energy loss within 1%. Verified here at kernel level on `batch-cpu` in `f64` and `f32`
  with a reference Newton driver; tier-level receipts come from N15b-37.
- [ ] Joint rows of GATE-TRAC-04 (seed done-condition): stiction breakaway and backlash values within 1%.
- [ ] GATE-SINEW-09: twelve backlash joints leave the dense factor at size 18.
- [ ] The Hessian contribution of every row is nonnegative on 10⁶ random inputs; bounds are unchanged across
  Newton iterations of a step (INV-SINEW-05).
- [ ] The iteration and throughput counters and the smooth comparator exist, so that KILL-SINEW-ROWS can be
  decided (seed done-condition; the decision record is N15b-24): the step-plan field `joint_friction` exists
  with both values, enters `StepPlanId`, selects the comparator in the kernel's reference Newton driver, sets
  `smooth-joint-friction`, and is refused with `FROB-REF-136` by step-plan admission under `strict` or on the
  referee. The command-line runs of both arms need the batched step (BD-159), which depends on this bead, so
  they are N15b-24's (its script `kill_sinew_rows.sh`), not this bead's.
- [ ] The kernel's manifest carries what the CPU-versus-device check reads (class, footprints, the CPU
  reference `k_joint_rows_ref`, the input domain); the check `frob spark check --kernel K-joint-rows
  --backend <b>` on each admitted device family is run by N15b-37, which depends on this bead.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-kernels/tests/k_joint_rows.rs`:
- `huber_values`: ε = 10⁻³: ψ(5 × 10⁻⁴) = 1.25 × 10⁻⁴; ψ(2 × 10⁻³) = 1.5 × 10⁻³; gradient continuous at ε.
- `impulse_bounded`: abs(∂ℓ/∂v) ≤ h·τ_b for 10⁶ random v.
- `creep_rate`: τ = 0.3, τ_b = 0.5, ε = 10⁻³: steady creep 6 × 10⁻⁴ rad/s; drift over 20 s is 0.012 rad,
  below ε·t = 0.02 rad.
- `breakaway_on_ramp`: r = 1 N·m/s, τ_s = 0.5 N·m: speed exceeds ε within one step of t = 0.5 s.
- `spring_coulomb_decay`: 4·τ_c/k = 0.04 rad per cycle within 2% (k = 10, τ_c = 0.1).
- `backlash_inside_gap_uncoupled`: reversal of 0.3° in a 0.5° gap gives zero output motion.
- `loop_width`: 0.5° within 1% after removing the tooth deflection.
- `closure_impact`: N = 9, J_r = 10⁻⁴, J₂ = 0.02, Δω = 2: 0.577 rad/s and 0.0115 J within 1% with
  τ_d chosen for no rebound.
- `trac04_backlash_values`: 17.5 ms crossing and 0.083 rad/s output for the 0.5° gap fixture.
- `bound_uses_rational_shape`: σ(1) = 0.5, σ(2) = 0.2.
- `gaussian_on_batch_cpu`: a Gaussian card gives σ(1) = e⁻¹ = 0.367879 within the library exponential's stated
  error, and with τ_c = 0.20 N·m, τ_s = 0.30 N·m, v_s = 0.05 rad/s, c_load = 0 the bound at v⁻ = 0.05 rad/s
  is 0.2 + 0.1·e⁻¹ = 0.23679 N·m within 10⁻⁶ relative in `f64` (the fixture of N15a-08).
- `gaussian_refused_136_on_device_without_exp`: on a device kernel class that does not admit the exponential the
  same card refuses with FROB-REF-136, and the returned tiers equal the capability table's "yes" cells.
- `no_rotor_refused_241`, `padding_rows_neutral`, `f32_matches_f64_within_1e-4`.
- `bound_includes_iron_coulomb`: with τ_c = 0.20 N·m, τ_s = 0.30 N·m, v⁻ = 0, c_load = 0 and `iron_coulomb` =
  0.014 N·m in the actuator slot, τ_b = 0.314 N·m (0.300 with the slot at zero); over a sliding step the row's
  dissipation is booked 0.300/0.314 to the `friction` line and 0.014/0.314 to the `iron` line, and the two
  lines sum to the row's dissipation to 10⁻¹² relative in `f64`.
- `smooth_comparator_selected`: `joint_friction = smooth` removes the stiction rows (row count falls by one
  per actuated joint, backlash and limit rows unchanged), changes `StepPlanId`, applies τ_f = −0.2121 N·m at
  v = 10⁻³ rad/s for τ_b = 0.30 N·m and ε = 10⁻³ rad/s, and sets `smooth-joint-friction`; step-plan admission
  of `smooth` with profile `strict` or tier `referee` refuses with `FROB-REF-136` listing the lockstep tiers.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_joint_rows.sh` (N15b-37) runs the six fixtures on `batch-cpu` and each device tier with
`frob task fixture <name> --tier <tier> --profile lockstep --seed 9 --key <k> --json`, compares with the
referee through `frob fidelity ledger`, and logs measured values, the regularization bound used, thresholds,
iteration counts and the gates touched. `scripts/e2e/kill_sinew_rows.sh` (N15b-24) drives the two arms through
`--joint-friction rows|smooth`.

### Known answers and negative controls

`mutant-backlash-deadzone` fails `closure_impact`; `mutant-bound-updated-in-solve` (τ_b recomputed per

## Considerations

- Single precision: ε = 10⁻³ rad/s gives a Hessian term h·τ_b/ε of about 1 kg·m² at h = 2 ms and τ_b =
  0.5 N·m, larger than a rotor's inertia; diagonal scaling of the solve handles the spread, and
  regularization is stated in scaled variables.
- If the kill gate fires, this kernel remains for `strict`, and the comparator becomes the throughput
  default with its flag.
- Stick-slip dependence on h is a known limitation recorded in the contract's known-gaps section.
