## Background

Every tier of the simulator reads the same actuator card. If the card is wrong, the referee, the CPU batch
tier and both GPU tiers are wrong together, and their agreement says nothing about it (counterexample
CX-143). The program's risk register lists this as RISK-32, common-mode model error, rated high, owned by
the Sinew lead, with a leading indicator: the share of `assumed` and `generic` card fields in benchmark
designs. Three defences are in place and none is a proof:

1. randomization draws each field from its stated uncertainty, so a verdict is a statement over the card's
   band;
2. a verdict that changes sign inside that band is labelled *card-conditional* by the sensitivity analysis
   (A-16) and names the field that decides it;
3. under `--strict-data` a verdict resting on an `assumed` field is refused.

The only evidence about the card itself is measurement, which is the business of the calibration subsystem.
This bead builds the Sinew-side machinery for defences 1 and 3 and the indicator of RISK-32 at M1; defence 2
is N15b-07 at M3, when A-16 exists. Tier: Solid [S]. Crate `frob-actuate`. It rests on decision
ADR-SINEW-01: the card is the unit of actuator knowledge, immutable and content-addressed, with a provenance
(`datasheet` with a citation, `measured`, `generic`, `assumed`; an override in a design is `designed`) and
an uncertainty on every field.

## Scope

In: the band accessor that randomization and sensitivity read; one claim product per card field; the
read-set tracking of static functions; the provenance share of a design, computed over the bound cards and
influence records that N15a-03 builds; the strict-data rule FROB-REF-113 as one function over a verdict's
read set, evaluated per verdict so that an `assumed` field the verdict never read does not refuse, which the
verdict-forming callers invoke (below); the rule that tier agreement never raises the class of a
card-derived result. This bead owns the per-verdict rule for card-derived verdicts; the card's own checks in
`actuator.check --strict-data` apply the same rule (BD-069). Out, with the bead that has each: the
bound-card record, its provenance summary and the influence record of compiled products (N15a-03); the
stage-6 call (A-11 in BD-103, also reached as `analyze.envelope --strict-data`) and its run through `frob
design check` (BD-110; N15b-34's script); drawing randomized values by address (BD-200); the sensitivity
analysis and the label (N15b-07, BD-243); the `reality:` line on reports (BD-139); the card schema and
`actuator.check` (BD-069).

## Technical approach

**Bands (DESIGN).**
```rust
pub struct FieldBand { path: FieldPath, nominal: Qty, uncertainty: Uncertainty, provenance: Provenance }
pub enum Uncertainty { Exact, AbsHalfWidth(Qty), RelHalfWidth(f64), Interval(Qty, Qty), Normal { sigma: Qty } }
impl ActuatorCard { pub fn band(&self, path: &FieldPath) -> FieldBand; pub fn bands(&self) -> Vec<FieldBand>; }
```
The variants are one-to-one with the five `unc` kinds of the card schema (BD-069: `exact`, `abs`, `rel`,
`interval`, `normal`); a `rel` half-width is a fraction of the nominal, not a standard deviation, and `normal`
carries σ in the field's unit. `band(..).interval()` returns [lo, hi] for the three bounded kinds and none for
`normal` and `exact`, so a consumer that needs a bounded band asks for it explicitly.
A field with no interval or spread cannot be randomized: the accessor returns it with `Uncertainty::Exact`
only when the card says so explicitly, and a randomization request on a field without a stated uncertainty
is refused by its consumer (the rule that a card field with no interval refuses randomization).

**One claim product per field.** A card creates a leaf claim product per field from that field's
provenance: `measured` and `datasheet` give class Tested; `generic` and `assumed` give Estimated (DESIGN
mapping; the claim-product type takes the minimum over inputs and has no setter). A single assumed number is
then visible as the weakest input of everything built on it.

**Read sets.** Each static function of the crate (envelope, thermal steady state, bus feasibility) and each
component constructor returns, with its value, the set of field paths it read. The claim product of the
result is the combination over that set, and its `weakest` entry names the card hash and field path.

**Provenance share.** `provenance_share(model) -> Share { assumed, generic, datasheet, measured, designed,
total }` counts the card fields in the union of read sets of a design's bound actuators, batteries and
transmissions. Per card it is the share of its fields in each provenance class that `actuator.list` prints
(VERBS-actuator; `actuator.show` prints each field's provenance); per design it is the figure the Sinew
decisions read (N15b-25). It is not the `cards:` guess-share line that BD-139 prints under the reality line of
every claim-bearing result, `design.check` included (every field of every bound card of nine kinds, read or
not), which is RISK-32's report line; the two counts keep their two names and are never merged.

**Strict data.** With `--strict-data`, any verdict whose read set contains a field of provenance `assumed`
is refused with FROB-REF-113 (an actuator card with a required field of class `assumed` when the caller
asked for strict data), pointer at the field, repair "replace with a cited datasheet or measured value".
`generic` fields are reported in the share and are not refused (DESIGN reading of the refusal's wording).
The check is `strict_data::check(read_set, strict) -> Result<(), Refusal>` and runs where a verdict is formed
under a caller's `--strict-data`: stage 6 of the preflight and `analyze.envelope` (A-11, BD-103) and
`actuator.check` (BD-069), the rows of the verb registry that take `--strict-data`. The static verbs
`actuator.envelope`, `actuator.thermal` and `actuator.bus` take no `--strict-data` (VERBS-actuator); their
results are Estimated with the `assumed` field named as the weakest input, by the read sets above.

**Tier agreement is not card evidence.** A Fidelity claim (a faster tier tracks a slower one) combines with
card-derived products by the minimum rule, so no number of agreeing tiers lifts a result above the class of
its weakest card field. This is enforced by the type (no constructor takes a class) and pinned by a test.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `crates/frob-actuate/src/card/{band,readset,share}.rs` exist; every static function returns its read
  set.
- [ ] For a design whose knee card has `alpha_r` marked `assumed`, the strict-data check over the read set of
  the knee's thermal steady state refuses with FROB-REF-113 naming `…knee.card.alpha_r` and the repair; with
  `strict` off it passes and the result's claim product is Estimated with that field as weakest input
  (`strict_data_refuses_assumed`). The same pair through `frob design check <design> --strict-data --json`
  (exit 3, then 0 or 2) is asserted by N15b-34's script, which depends on this bead through BD-103 and BD-110.
- [ ] `frob actuator show <card> --json` prints the provenance of every field, and `frob actuator list --json`
  the card's share in each provenance class, both computed by this bead's accessor and share functions.
- [ ] For each of the starter catalog's generic classes (small, medium and large quasi-direct-drive; hobby
  servo; strain-wave; hub motor) the share reports 100% `generic`.
- [ ] The negative control `mutant-agreement-raises-class` is caught: a referee-and-batch agreement cannot
  produce a card-derived result above Estimated when a field is `assumed`.
- [ ] A field with no stated uncertainty is refused for randomization.
- [ ] With `--strict-data`, a design whose knee card has `g_housing_ambient` of class `assumed` is refused
  with FROB-REF-113 by the thermal steady-state check, which reads that field, and is not refused by the
  bus-feasibility check, which never reads it (unit test `strict_data_by_influence`: two analyses, one
  reading the assumed field and one not; only the first refuses). `c_winding` is read only by the
  time-to-limit query of `actuator.thermal`, never by the steady state, and the test asserts that too.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-actuate/tests/card_bands.rs`:
- `band_returns_interval`: `r_phase = 0.20 Ω ± 10%` gives the interval [0.18, 0.22] Ω.
- `readset_thermal`: the steady-state function reads exactly `r_phase`, `t_ref`, `alpha_r`,
  `g_winding_housing`, `g_housing_ambient`, `t_max_winding`, `t_max_magnet` (and `alpha_flux`,
  `flux_linkage` under torque holding; the `iron_loss` coefficients when present); the time-to-limit function
  adds `c_winding` and `c_housing`.
- `weakest_input_named`: one `assumed` field among six makes the product Estimated and names it.
- `strict_data_refuses_assumed`, `strict_data_admits_generic_with_share`.
- `share_counts`: a quadruped with twelve identical generic cards counts each distinct field once per
  card version, with totals matching a hand count.
- `no_uncertainty_no_randomization`.
- `agreement_does_not_raise_class`: combining a Fidelity product of class Tested with an Estimated card
  product yields Estimated.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_stage6_sizing.sh` (bead N15b-34) includes the strict-data pair of calls above and asserts
exit codes 3 and 0 or 2, the refusal pointer and the weakest-input line; NDJSON lines carry the command,
exit code, the evidence block with its `weakest` field, and the share.

### Known answers and negative controls
CX-143 is the counterexample. The worked claim-product case is the known answer for combination: a kernel
identity of class Certified, a card field marked `assumed` (Estimated) and a study that is Tested combine to
Estimated with the card field as weakest input. Negative controls: the class-raising mutant; a static
function that under-reports its read set (`mutant-readset-drops-field`) is caught by mutating the dropped
field and observing a changed value with an unchanged read set. Determinism: D0 for shares and read sets.

## Considerations

- OPEN-SINEW-1 (which real parts ship, under what licence) keeps the catalog at generic classes, so at M1
  every benchmark result is Estimated and realism claims are refused; that is intended.
- Read sets must be supersets of what is actually read; the mutation test above is the guard, in the style
  of the compiler's influence records.
- Card prices are a separate open question; a cost verdict under strict data is refused by the design
  chapter's rule, not here.
