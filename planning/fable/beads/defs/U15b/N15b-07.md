## Background

Agreement between simulation tiers cannot show that an actuator card is right, because every tier reads the
same card (counterexample CX-143; risk RISK-32, common-mode model error). The second of the three defences
against that risk is a label: a verdict that changes sign somewhere inside the card's stated uncertainty
band is *card-conditional*, and the result names the field that decides it. An agent that sees the label
knows the cheapest way to settle the verdict is to measure that one field. The label is produced by the
sensitivity analysis A-16 ("which parameters matter most for a chosen metric", automatic differentiation
for static metrics, Morris screening for simulated ones), which arrives with seed bead BD-243 at M3; the
band machinery it reads is N15b-06. Tier: Solid [S].

Related labels elsewhere in the system follow the same pattern and must not be confused with this one:
`model-conditional` (a ranking that changes under a model-form variant) and `tier-sensitive`. All are
condition flags of the claim product, combined by union.

## Scope

In: the band sweep of card-derived verdicts; the sign-change test; the deciding-field rule; the condition
flag `card-conditional` on the claim product; its appearance in `design.check`, the three static actuator
verbs and comparison reports; the negative control that tier agreement does not remove the label. Out: A-16
itself (BD-243); randomized draws by address (BD-200); measurement and card updates from logs (the
calibration subsystem).

## Technical approach

**For static verdicts** (stage 6: envelope, thermal steady state, bus ceiling; class D0 where rational).
1. Take the verdict function v(card fields) → margin (positive passes, negative refuses) and its read set
   (N15b-06).
2. For each field in the read set with a stated uncertainty, evaluate the margin at the band's two ends with
   the others nominal (one-at-a-time, 2·k evaluations), and, because margins of these laws are monotone in
   each field over a band, record whether the sign differs from the nominal sign.
3. Evaluate the two corner points "all fields at their worst end" and "all at their best end" (worst and
   best taken from step 2's slopes).
4. Label: if the nominal, worst and best margins have the same sign, no label. If a single field's two ends
   straddle zero, label `card-conditional` naming that field. If only the joint corner straddles zero, label
   `card-conditional` naming the field with the largest margin change, and list the others.
5. Attach the sensitivity ∂margin/∂field (from A-16's automatic differentiation) so a consumer can rank
   measurements.

**For simulated verdicts** (a study on a batch tier). Worlds already draw card fields from their bands by
address. A-16's Morris screening over those draws gives an elementary effect per field on the verdict
metric; the verdict is `card-conditional` when the study's decision differs between the lower and upper
halves of some field's draws at the study's own evidence threshold. The label names that field. Budget and
study design are A-16's.

**Claim product.** `conditions` gains `card-conditional{field, card}`; class, tier and determinism are
unchanged (the label is a condition, not a downgrade). Combination takes the union, so every result built
on a labelled verdict carries it. Reports print it on the claim line.

**Worked case (illustrative card of the thermal chapter).** R₀ = 0.20 Ω ± 10% at 25 °C, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹,
G = 0.80 W/K, limit 155 °C, ambient 25 °C. Largest continuous current: √(G·ΔT/(R₀·(1 + α·ΔT))) with ΔT =
130 K. Nominal 18.6 A; at R₀ = 0.22 Ω it is 17.7 A; at 0.18 Ω it is 19.6 A. A design that must hold 18.0 A
passes at nominal and fails at the upper end, so its thermal verdict is `card-conditional` on `r_phase`. A
design holding 17.0 A passes everywhere and carries no label.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The 18.0 A design reports `card-conditional` naming `r_phase`; the 17.0 A and 20.0 A designs report no
  label (pass everywhere, fail everywhere).
- [ ] The label appears in the envelope's evidence block of `design.check`, `actuator.thermal`,
  `actuator.envelope`, `actuator.bus`, and propagates into a comparison built on the verdict.
- [ ] Running the same design on the referee and on a batch tier and finding agreement leaves the label in
  place (CX-143's control).
- [ ] A field with no stated uncertainty contributes no sweep and is listed as "band unknown".
- [ ] The simulated-verdict path labels a planted case: two designs whose ranking flips with gear
  efficiency drawn from its band.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-analyze/tests/card_conditional.rs`:
- `thermal_margin_18a_is_conditional`: field `r_phase`; margins +0.6 A, −0.3 A, +1.6 A at nominal, upper
  and lower ends.
- `thermal_margin_17a_unlabelled`, `thermal_margin_20a_unlabelled`.
- `joint_corner_only`: two fields each ±4% whose single sweeps keep the sign and whose joint worst corner
  flips it; the label names the larger contributor and lists both.
- `label_propagates_by_union`: a comparison of a labelled and an unlabelled verdict is labelled.
- `agreement_keeps_label`: adding a Fidelity product leaves `conditions` unchanged.
- `bus_ceiling_conditional_on_harness`: E = 48 V, R = 0.08 Ω ± 15%, demand 6,600 W: ceiling 7,200 W nominal,
  6,261 W at the upper end, so the verdict is conditional on `harness_resistance`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_card_conditional.sh`: `frob design check hold-18a --json`, `frob design check hold-17a
--json`, then `frob sim run` of the labelled design on two tiers followed by `frob explain design`, asserting
that the label and deciding field survive in each envelope. The script sources the shared helper of STD-E2E
and logs to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_card_conditional/<utc>.ndjson`: command, exit code, wall time, the
evidence block with its `conditions`, the three margins with units, and a summary line.

### Known answers and negative controls
The continuous-current figures 18.6 A (nominal; KA-307) and its band ends 17.7 A and 19.6 A computed from the
same formula. CX-143. Negative controls: `mutant-agreement-raises-class` (N15b-06) and
`mutant-label-dropped-on-combine` (the union replaced by the first operand), caught by
`label_propagates_by_union`. Determinism: static path D0; the simulated path under the determinism class of
its study.

## Considerations

- Monotonicity of a margin in each field over a band is assumed for the one-at-a-time sweep; A-16's
  derivative sign at both ends is checked, and a field whose derivative changes sign inside the band is
  swept at five points instead.
- The label is advice about what to measure; it is never a substitute for the calibration lane.
- If A-16 is cut or delayed, the static path of this bead still works from the closed forms and the label is
  limited to stage-6 verdicts.
