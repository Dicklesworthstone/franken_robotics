## Background

Backlash needs the rotor as its own coordinate: inside the gap the rotor and the link move independently.
Twelve backlash joints would raise the velocity count of a quadruped from 18 to 30, and the fast tiers
factor a dense matrix per world per Newton iteration, so a naive treatment would cost about (30/18)³ ≈ 4.6
times the factorization work. It does not have to. Under the usual flexible-joint simplification, in which
a rotor's kinetic energy is that of its own spin, the rotor block of the system matrix is diagonal and each
backlash or motor-side stiction row couples one rotor to one joint. The rotor block is then eliminated in
closed form: its Schur complement changes only diagonal entries of the link block, at a cost linear in the
number of rotors. The dense factorization keeps the size it has with no backlash joints. Tier: Solid [S].
Crate `frob-actuate` for the algebra; the kernels of BD-155 call it. The rotor coordinate and the two
backlash row descriptors it eliminates are defined by N15a-09; the coupling map of a rotor that drives
several joints is the `CouplingMap` of N15b-10.

Rejected: enlarging the factorization (the cost above, and every buffer capacity of the shape class would
change); treating backlash outside the solve (no impact when the gap closes, decision ADR-SINEW-02); a
sparse factorization for 30 unknowns (the bookkeeping costs more than it saves at this size).

The statement to verify is proof obligation PO-SINEW-02: **the rotor-eliminated solve equals the full
solve**, by exact rational comparison on small cases and round-off comparison on 10⁴ random cases. The gate
is GATE-SINEW-09 (M2): PO-SINEW-02 holds, and with twelve backlash joints the dense factorization has the
size it has with none.

## Technical approach

**Setting.** A convex-tier step minimizes over the next velocity v = (q̇, θ̇), with q̇ ∈ ℝⁿ the joint and base
rates and θ̇ ∈ ℝᵐ the rotor rates:

  Φ(v) = ½·(v − v*)ᵀ·A·(v − v*) + Σ_rows ℓ_r(a_rᵀ·v),

A positive definite, each ℓ_r convex. A Newton iteration solves H·Δv = r with H = A + Σ a_r·g_r·a_rᵀ, g_r ≥ 0
the row curvature. Flexible-joint simplification: A = blockdiag(A_ll, D) with D = diag(J_r,i + h·b_i)
(rotor inertia plus implicit rotor damping).

**Rows that touch rotor i** (driving joint j = j(i) through ratio N_i):
- backlash, argument θ̇_i/N_i − q̇_j, total curvature g_i of its two rows: a = (−e_j, e_i/N_i);
- motor-side stiction, argument θ̇_i, curvature s_i: a = (0, e_i).

**Blocks.** H_rr = diag(d_i) with d_i = J_r,i + h·b_i + g_i/N_i² + s_i. H_rl has one entry per rotor,
(H_rl)_{i,j} = −g_i/N_i. H_ll = A_ll + (contact, limit and output-side rows) + Σ_i g_i·e_j·e_jᵀ.

**Elimination (diagonal case), cost O(m).**
1. For each rotor i: c_i = (g_i/N_i)/d_i.
2. Reduced matrix: S = H_ll − Σ_i (g_i/N_i)·c_i·e_j·e_jᵀ. Only the diagonal entry (j, j) changes; the net
   contribution of rotor i to that entry is g_i·(J_r,i + h·b_i + s_i)/d_i, which is nonnegative, so S stays
   positive definite.
3. Reduced right-hand side: r̃_l = r_l + Σ_i c_i·r_r,i·e_j.
4. Factor S (dense, size n) and solve S·Δq̇ = r̃_l.
5. Back-substitute: Δθ̇_i = (r_r,i + (g_i/N_i)·Δq̇_j)/d_i.
The line search, the row potentials and the convergence test are evaluated on the full v, so rotor
velocities remain variables and the iterates are those of the full solve; only the linear solve is
reduced. Limit check: as g_i → ∞ (gap closed and stiff) the diagonal contribution tends to N_i²·(J_r,i +
h·b_i + s_i), the familiar reflected inertia (the reflected inertia N²·J_r of level L1 when there is no
damping and no motor-side row); with no active row (inside the gap) it is zero and the link does not feel
the rotor. The division is always defined: d_i ≥ J_r,i > 0, and a rotor with zero inertia is refused by the
card check as a massless chain.

**A rotor behind a coupling transmission.** The row argument is θ̇_k − C_k·q̇ with C_k the rotor's row of the
coupling map (motor rates θ̇ = C·q̇), so the row vector is a = (−C_kᵀ, e_k), H_rl row k is −g_k·C_k, the rotor
pivot is d_k = J_r,k + h·b_k + g_k + s_k (unit coefficient on θ̇_k, so g_k enters undivided, unlike the
g_i/N_i² of the diagonal case), and the complement subtracts (g_k²/d_k)·C_kᵀ·C_k: a block over the
joints the rotor drives, with off-diagonal entries. In the rigid limit it is the block C_kᵀ·J_r·C_k, which is
added once, in the lowering (N15b-11), and never again. This case costs O(m·w²) for couplings of width w.

**What the simplification neglects.** The gyroscopic torque J_r·N·q̇·Ω of a spinning rotor carried by a link
turning at Ω: with J_r = 10⁻⁴ kg·m², N = 9, q̇ = 20 rad/s, Ω = 5 rad/s it is 0.09 N·m (illustrative), under
one percent of the joint torques of such an actuator. HYPOTHESIS: negligible for the cards on file. Test:
the referee with full coupling (BD-084) against the reduced model on the standard battery, recorded as a
row of the fidelity ledger with its envelope.

**Regularization.** Rotor rows have tiny diagonal entries (J_r of order 10⁻⁴ kg·m²) next to base rows of
order 10 kg; any regularization is stated in the scaled variables of the solver's diagonal scaling, never as
a uniform shift, and elimination happens before scaling so that d_i is never perturbed by a shift sized for
a base row.

**Reference implementations.** `eliminate_rotors_f64` and `eliminate_rotors_rational` in
`crates/frob-actuate/src/rows/eliminate.rs`, the second over exact rationals for the proof-obligation test.
DESIGN sketch of the two entry points: `rows::eliminate::fold(link_diag, link_rhs, rotors: &[RotorTerms])
-> Folded` (steps 1 to 3) and `rows::eliminate::recover(x_link, folded) -> rotor_velocities` (step 5), both
straight-line over rotors, with sums into one diagonal entry taken in rotor-index order.

**Shape-class consequence.** The velocity capacity `n_v` of a shape class counts links only; rotor state
lives in the per-actuator slots. The second clause of GATE-SINEW-09 is therefore checked structurally: the
Cholesky kernel is invoked with the same dimension for the quadruped with twelve backlash joints as with
none, and the assertion reads the kernel's recorded dimension, not a comment.

**PO-SINEW-02, the two verifications.** (1) Exact: every model with up to 3 links and 3 rotors over a
fixed grid of rational inertias, row curvatures and right-hand sides is enumerated, and 10³ random rational
systems with n ≤ 6 and m ≤ 6 are added; the reduced solution and the back-substituted rotor velocities
equal the full solution with zero difference. (2) Round-off: 10⁴ draws of tree size 6 to 30, rotor count up
to the joint count, random active sets; the relative difference of the full and eliminated linear solves
after diagonal scaling is at most 10⁻¹² in `f64` (the proposal for KA-303, whose registry tolerance is "to
set"), and the Newton iterates of whole steps agree to 10⁻¹⁰.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] PO-SINEW-02, exact: on every enumerated small case (up to 3 links and 3 rotors) and on 10³ random
  rational systems with n ≤ 6 and m ≤ 6 the eliminated solution (Δq̇, Δθ̇) equals the solution of the full
  (n + m) system exactly.
- [ ] PO-SINEW-02, round-off: on 10⁴ random double-precision systems the relative difference of the linear
  solves is at most 10⁻¹² (tolerance to be set at the gate; this value is the proposal), whole-step Newton
  iterates agree to 10⁻¹⁰, and the tolerance and the worst case found are recorded with the gate.
- [ ] The sanity limits hold: a rigid mesh (g = 10¹²) reproduces the reflected inertia N²·J_r to 10⁻⁹
  relative, and an open gap contributes zero.
- [ ] The worst case of each verification, the case count and the tolerance used are written as `metric`
  lines of the unit tests; GATE-SINEW-09's receipt and its record are N15b-37's, the gate's one recorder,
  which depends on this bead.
- [ ] This bead's share of GATE-SINEW-09 (TARGET): with twelve backlash joints on the reference quadruped the
  reduced system of the host reference has dimension 18, the size it has with none
  (`factor_size_quadruped`). The shape class and the recorded Cholesky dimension on the batched tiers are
  asserted by N15b-37.
- [ ] The coupled case reproduces the block C_kᵀ·J_r·C_k in the rigid limit to 10⁻¹².
- [ ] The neglected gyroscopic term is stated in `CONTRACT.md` as a known gap with its formula and the
  illustrative 0.09 N·m, and the fidelity-ledger row "rotor gyroscopic coupling neglected" is named there
  as the measurement that settles the hypothesis; producing that row is N15a-11, which depends on this
  bead.
- [ ] S is positive definite whenever H is (property test).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-actuate/tests/rotor_elimination.rs`:
- `one_rotor_hand_case`: n = 1, m = 1, A_ll = 0.02, J_r = 10⁻⁴, N = 9, g = 50, s = 0, h·b = 0: d = 10⁻⁴ +
  50/81 = 0.617384; diagonal contribution g·J_r/d = 0.0080987; S = 0.0280987. Solving with r = (1, 0)
  reproduces the full 2 × 2 solve exactly in rationals.
- `schur_one_rotor_exact`: J_link = 1/50, J_r = 1/10000, N = 9, one active row with g = 1000, right-hand
  side (1, 0): the off-diagonal entry is −1000/9 and d = 1/10000 + 1000/81; the eliminated and the full
  2 × 2 solutions are equal as rationals.
- `rigid_limit_is_reflected_inertia`: g = 10¹², contribution → N²·J_r = 0.0081 within 10⁻⁹.
- `open_gap_no_coupling`: with no active row the link system is untouched and Δθ̇ = r_r/J_r.
- `motor_side_stiction_only`: a motor-side row changes only d_i; the link block is unchanged.
- `small_case_enumeration_rational`, `exact_rational_equivalence` (10³ cases), `roundoff_equivalence`
  (10⁴ cases).
- `diagonal_only_changes`: off-diagonal entries of S equal those of H_ll bit for bit in the diagonal case.
- `coupled_rotor_block`: the one-rotor coupling C = [10, 10] with J_r = 0.01 gives [[1, 1], [1, 1]] in the
  rigid limit.
- `factor_size_quadruped`: twelve backlash joints, n = 18.
- `spd_preserved` (property).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_joint_rows.sh` (bead N15b-37) runs the twelve-backlash quadruped on `batch-cpu` with
`frob task fixture quad-12-backlash --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep --seed 9 --key <k> --json`, reads the
shape class and the solve-stage record from the receipt, asserts factor size 18, and compares the episode
against the referee through `frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --json`.
It also runs `frob task fixture rotor-elimination-equivalence --tier host --key <k> --json`, whose
whole-step row steps the quadruped
with the eliminated and with the full (n + m = 30) solve of this bead's host reference and compares the
iterates within the declared tolerance. Lines log the factor size per run, the equivalence residuals with
thresholds, Newton iteration counts, wall time per step for both forms, and the ledger row. Until that script
lands, `cargo test -p frob-actuate --test rotor_elimination -- --nocapture` prints the same residuals and
factor sizes as `metric` lines.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-303 (the rotor-eliminated solve equals the full solve; 18 velocities in the factor, not 30). Negative
controls: `mutant-eliminate-drop-rhs` (step 3 omitted; iterates differ from the first step) must fail the
exact equivalence; `mutant-eliminate-drop-schur` (the term (g_i/N_i)·c_i dropped, so the link feels a
rigid row with no rotor behind it) must fail it too; `mutant-rotor-diagonal` on the coupled case must fail
`coupled_rotor_block` (it reports diag(1, 1) where [[1, 1], [1, 1]] is true). Metamorphic: permuting rotor
order leaves S bit-identical (sums into one diagonal entry are taken in rotor-index order). Determinism:
D0 for the rational path; D2 for the `f64` path; lanes twice on one machine, across worker counts, debug
and release.

## Considerations

- If the gyroscopic hypothesis is refuted for some card class, that class runs with explicit rotor
  coordinates in the dense block on the fast tiers (larger factor, flagged), and the referee is unaffected.
- A motor-side stiction row with zero curvature outside its kink leaves d_i = J_r,i + g_i/N_i², still
  positive.
- KILL-SINEW-ROWS (N15b-24), if it fires, removes stiction rows from throughput tiers; backlash rows and
  this elimination remain wherever backlash rows remain, and on the `strict` profile in any case.
- The tree-operator route for larger mechanisms keeps the same folding: the folded terms land on existing
  diagonal entries and add no fill.
