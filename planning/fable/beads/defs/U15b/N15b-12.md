## Background

A lossless transformer conserves power under either sign of its modulus. A gear with ratio +N and a gear
with ratio −N both satisfy the power identity, so no energy balance, ledger or passivity check can detect a
reversed gear. A robot with one reversed gear still closes its energy account to round-off while the joint
turns the wrong way. The defect has a concrete source in the foundation the referee is built on. FACT
(`frankensim/crates/fs-phs/src/interconnection.rs:91` at commit `483d09c682f2`, read 2026-11-21): the
suite's legacy `transformer` carries the comment "The legacy transformer has the opposite sign to the
canonical map", the canonical map being `interconnect_mapped`. FACT (reported, not re-run): with two unit
scalar stores, efforts (2, 3) and coefficient 4, one entry point gives rates (12, −8) and the other
(−12, 8); both satisfy 2·12 + 3·(−8) = 0, and a coefficient of −4 in one convention reproduces the other.

The rule that closes the hole is a test of motion, not of power. Tier: Solid [S]. Owner `frob-actuate`;
the airlock crate `frob-fsim` enforces the entry-point rule.

## Scope

In: the authored direction on every transformer, gear and coupling; the signed motion witness and where it
runs; the `--bind <design>:<joint>` form of `actuator.check` and the witness fields of its output; the
refusal; the airlock rule; the catalog sweep and the negated-ratio control. Out: the wiring-level
sign check of ports, which catches reversed port declarations but not a reversed modulus (N15b-01); the
admission probe that records the opposite signs of the sibling's two entry points at the pin (N33c-43, row
11 of the admission table, GATE-WS-09; a dependency, whose probe this bead's airlock lint relies on).

## Technical approach

**Authored direction (DESIGN).** Every transformer, gear and coupling joins two labelled ports, a driving
port and a driven port, with an authored direction: positive rotation of the driving port corresponds to a
stated sign of rotation at the driven port. In cards and designs this is the sign of the signed
`gear_ratio`, the signed entries of a coupling matrix, or, for a screw, the handedness of the lead. The
canonical form writes the sign explicitly; there is no default.

**The witness.** For each driving-port and driven-port pair with a nonzero coupling:
1. Build the lowered system exactly as the tier will run it (through the model lowering and, on the referee,
   through `frob-fsim`), with the driven port free and everything at rest.
2. Apply a unit positive effort on the driving port for one step (or solve for the instantaneous
   accelerations).
3. Read the rate at the driven port. Its sign must equal the authored sign. A zero rate (within a stated
   floor) where the coupling entry is nonzero is also a failure.
4. For a coupling matrix the witness runs per nonzero entry C_kj: unit effort on motor k, joint j free, the
   other joints held.
The witness exercises the implemented path end to end, which is the point: it compares what the code does
with what the author wrote.

**Where it runs.** `actuator.check` runs the witness when a card is bound to a joint (it needs the joint
and the lowering), and stage 1 of the preflight therefore refuses a design with a reversed transmission
before anything else is computed. It runs once per lowering path. At M1 that is the referee lowering. The
witness takes the lowered system as its argument, so the packed lowering of the batched tiers (BD-148, M2,
which does not exist when this bead is done) is witnessed by the same function when that bead registers its
path, with no change here. The bind point is the one N15a-03 defines: that bead builds the bound-card record
and the list of bind-time checks, and this bead registers the witness in that list; the three checks of the
card itself stay BD-069's.

**The `--bind` form (DESIGN; an input this bead adds to `frob.actuator.check.in/1`).** `frob actuator check
<card> --bind <design>:<joint> --json` answers "would this card pass at that joint" without an edit. The card
is bound in memory to the named joint in place of the card of its kind that the design binds there (an
actuator card replaces the actuator and keeps the joint's transmission; a transmission card replaces the
transmission behind the joint's actuator; these are the two kinds a joint binds). N15a-03's bind-time checks
run with that joint's context and the witness runs on the result. Nothing is written: the design keeps its
`DesignId`. The registered form `frob actuator check <design>:<joint>` runs the same checks on the card the
design itself binds. The output `frob.actuator.check.out/1` gains `direction_witness` (`pass`, `fail`, or
`not-bound` for a bare card with no joint) and `witness_entries`, one per driving and driven pair, with the
authored and the observed sign. Reason: the catalog sweep of GATE-SINEW-10 and an engineer choosing an actuator both need the
bind-time verdict for a card the design does not yet name, and one fixture design per card would test nothing
more.

**Refusal.** A reversed witness is refused under the port-sign refusal of the module chapter, FROB-REF-108
(power sign mismatch), with pointer at the transmission, `observed` the measured sign, `required` the
authored sign, and the repair "negate the ratio in the card or correct the lowering". The preflight's stage
table lists this code at stage 5 (Structure: port power balance and sign conventions). The witness raises it
in the compile, which is stage 1, as the binding functor's bus-membership check (N14a-09) already does; the
stage table (N14b-10) lists 108 in row 1 for these two binding raises, so that the defect gate counts this
refusal as made by its owning stage, and stage 5 keeps 108 for the port power balance it checks.

**Airlock rule.** `frob-fsim` reaches only `interconnect_mapped`, through the one adapter N33c-43 wrote,
`frob_fsim::actuation::coupling::transformer(driving, driven, direction: Direction, ratio: PositiveRatio)`,
which BD-068's `PortHamiltonian` calls for every junction: the lowering splits the authored signed ratio into
`Direction` and `PositiveRatio`, and no signed coefficient crosses the airlock. The lint is the source and
symbol check fixed at M0 by N33c-43, re-run here with the witness: no path or `use` item outside the module
`frob_fsim::conformance` names `fs_phs::interconnection::transformer` (or a re-export of it at the pin), and a
default-feature release build of `frob-fsim` contains no symbol of it. It matches that path, not the bare word
`transformer`, so the adapter `coupling::transformer` is not a hit. The admission probe that runs the witness
through both entry points and records that they disagree in sign is N33c-43's, at the pin.

**Catalog sweep.** Every actuator and transmission card in the catalog, bound to a test joint through the
`--bind` form, passes the witness; each of them with its ratio negated in the implementation path (authored
sign kept) fails it. A battery card joins no two ports with a ratio and is not part of the sweep.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The witness is implemented in `crates/frob-actuate/src/check/direction_witness.rs` and called from
  `actuator.check` at binding.
- [ ] GATE-SINEW-10, witness rows (TARGET): the signed motion witness passes on every card of the catalog
  and fails on each card with its ratio negated (every actuator and transmission card; the count is logged
  beside the catalog's count of those two kinds).
- [ ] `frob actuator check <card> --bind <design>:<joint> --json` reports `direction_witness` and
  `witness_entries` and leaves the design's `DesignId` unchanged; a bare card reports `not-bound`.
- [ ] A design with one reversed gear is refused with FROB-REF-108 by the bind-time checks of the compile
  (stage 1, through N15a-03's list), while the reversed transmission's power identity still holds to 10⁻¹²
  (`power_balance_blind_to_sign`, demonstrating that a balance cannot see the defect). The same refusal
  through `frob design check --stages 1` is asserted from the user's side by N15b-13, which depends on this
  bead.
- [ ] `frob-fsim` contains no reference to the legacy transformer entry point outside `frob_fsim::conformance`
  and no symbol of it in a default-feature release build; the lint passes, fails when a `use` of
  `fs_phs::interconnection::transformer` is planted in an `actuation` module, and does not fire on the adapter
  `coupling::transformer`.
- [ ] The two-store fixture through the adapter with ratio 4 reproduces the reported rates: one direction gives
  (12, −8) and the other (−12, 8), exact negatives, in the assignment N33c-43 recorded at the pin.
- [ ] Coupling matrices are witnessed per nonzero entry.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-actuate/tests/direction_witness.rs` and `crates/frob-fsim/tests/transformer_entry.rs`:
- `gear_positive_ratio_passes`: N = +9: unit effort on the motor gives a positive joint rate.
- `gear_negative_ratio_authored_passes`: N = −9 authored and implemented: negative joint rate, passes.
- `gear_ratio_negated_in_lowering_fails`: authored +9, lowered as −9: refused with FROB-REF-108.
- `power_balance_blind_to_sign`: in the same reversed system the power at the driving port plus the power
  at the driven port is zero to 10⁻¹² over the witness step (the per-element account of N15b-05, which
  would close the same way, is not needed to show it).
- `two_store_canonical_rates`: efforts (2, 3), `PositiveRatio` 4 with the direction N33c-43 recorded for
  (12, −8) gives (12, −8); the power sum 2·12 + 3·(−8) is 0.
- `two_store_negated_coefficient`: the same ratio with the other `Direction` gives (−12, 8) (the typed
  counterpart of a coefficient of −4).
- `coupling_per_entry`: C = [[1, 1], [1, −1]]: four witnesses with signs (+, +, +, −).
- `zero_rate_is_failure`: a decoupled lowering (entry dropped) fails.
- `catalog_sweep`: every actuator and transmission card of the catalog passes; every negated variant fails.
- `bind_form_leaves_design_unchanged`: `--bind` on a design whose joint names another card returns the
  witness entries for the offered card, and the design's `DesignId` and file bytes are unchanged; a bare card
  reports `not-bound`.
- `no_legacy_symbol`: the source scan of `frob-fsim` for the path `fs_phs::interconnection::transformer` (or a
  re-export at the pin) outside `frob_fsim::conformance`, and the symbol check of a default-feature release
  build; a planted `use` is caught and the adapter's own name is not matched.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_coupled_transmissions.sh` (bead N15b-13): `frob actuator check <card> --bind
<design>:<joint> --json` over the catalog's actuator and transmission cards (exit 0, `direction_witness:
pass`) and `frob design check quad-reversed-knee --stages 1 --json` (exit 3, FROB-REF-108,
stage 1). The demonstration that the energy balance cannot see the defect stays a unit test,
because no shipped profile may bypass the refusal. Lines log the authored and observed signs, the refusal
object and the gate name.

### Known answers and negative controls
The reported two-store rates (12, −8) and (−12, 8). Negative control: `mutant-ratio-negated` (provisional
counterexample CX-SINEW-c in N15-CX); a witness that tests power instead of motion
(`mutant-witness-uses-power`) passes the reversed system and is caught by
`gear_ratio_negated_in_lowering_fails`. Metamorphic: negating both the authored sign and the implementation
leaves the verdict unchanged. Determinism: the witness verdict is a sign, class D0.

## Considerations

- The reported sibling rates were read, not executed; the unit test above executes them at the pin and a
  difference is recorded as a foundation fact changed, never patched over.
- A linkage whose ratio changes sign across its range (a toggle) is witnessed at its reference
  configuration and at both ends of its declared range.
- If the sibling removes the legacy entry point, the lint becomes vacuous and stays in place.
