## Background

A lossless transformer conserves power under either sign of its modulus. A gear with ratio +N and a gear
with ratio −N both satisfy the power identity, so no energy balance, ledger or passivity check can detect a
reversed gear. A robot with one reversed gear still closes its energy account to round-off while the joint
turns the wrong way. The defect has a concrete source in the foundation the referee is built on. FACT
(`frankensim/crates/fs-phs/src/interconnection.rs:91` at commit `483d09c682f2`, read 2026-11-21): the
suite's legacy `transformer` carries the comment "The legacy transformer has the opposite sign to the
canonical map", the canonical map being `interconnect_mapped`. FACT (reported, not re-run): with two unit
scalar stores, efforts (2, 3) and coefficient 4, one entry point gives rates (12, −8) and the other
(−12, 8); both satisfy 2·12 + 3·(−8) = 0, and a coefficient of −4 in one convention reproduces the other.

The rule that closes the hole is a test of motion, not of power. Tier: Solid [S]. Owner `frob-actuate`;
the airlock crate `frob-fsim` enforces the entry-point rule.

## Scope

In: the authored direction on every transformer, gear and coupling; the signed motion witness and where it
runs; the refusal; the airlock rule; the catalog sweep and the negated-ratio control. Out: the wiring-level
sign check of ports, which catches reversed port declarations but not a reversed modulus (N15b-01); the
admission record of the sibling's two entry points (the suite-consumption chapter's gate, listed under
needs).

## Technical approach

**Authored direction (DESIGN).** Every transformer, gear and coupling joins two labelled ports, a driving
port and a driven port, with an authored direction: positive rotation of the driving port corresponds to a
stated sign of rotation at the driven port. In cards and designs this is the sign of the signed
`gear_ratio`, the signed entries of a coupling matrix, or, for a screw, the handedness of the lead. The
canonical form writes the sign explicitly; there is no default.

**The witness.** For each driving-port and driven-port pair with a nonzero coupling:
1. Build the lowered system exactly as the tier will run it (through the model lowering and, on the referee,
   through `frob-fsim`), with the driven port free and everything at rest.
2. Apply a unit positive effort on the driving port for one step (or solve for the instantaneous
   accelerations).
3. Read the rate at the driven port. Its sign must equal the authored sign. A zero rate (within a stated
   floor) where the coupling entry is nonzero is also a failure.
4. For a coupling matrix the witness runs per nonzero entry C_kj: unit effort on motor k, joint j free, the
   other joints held.
The witness exercises the implemented path end to end, which is the point: it compares what the code does
with what the author wrote.

**Where it runs.** `actuator.check` runs the witness when a card is bound to a joint (it needs the joint
and the lowering), and stage 1 of the preflight therefore refuses a design with a reversed transmission
before anything else is computed. It runs once per lowering path: referee and packed.

**Refusal.** A reversed witness is refused under the port-sign refusal of the module chapter, FROB-REF-108
(power sign mismatch), with pointer at the transmission, `observed` the measured sign, `required` the
authored sign, and the repair "negate the ratio in the card or correct the lowering".

**Airlock rule.** `frob-fsim` reaches only `interconnect_mapped` and passes the authored sign explicitly; no
adapter calls the legacy `transformer`. A lint in the airlock crate (a source check in its test suite)
fails if the legacy symbol is referenced. The admission test for the pin runs the signed motion witness
through both entry points and records that they disagree in sign, which is the documented hazard.

**Catalog sweep.** Every card in the catalog, bound to a test joint, passes the witness; each card with its
ratio negated in the implementation path (authored sign kept) fails it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The witness is implemented in `crates/frob-actuate/src/check/direction_witness.rs` and called from
  `actuator.check` at binding.
- [ ] GATE-SINEW-10, witness rows (TARGET): the signed motion witness passes on every card of the catalog
  and fails on each card with its ratio negated.
- [ ] A design with one reversed gear refuses with FROB-REF-108 at stage 1, while its energy balance still
  closes to 10⁻⁹ (demonstrating that the balance cannot see the defect).
- [ ] `frob-fsim` contains no reference to the legacy transformer entry point; the lint test passes and
  fails when a reference is planted.
- [ ] The two-store fixture reproduces the reported rates (12, −8) through the canonical map with
  coefficient 4, and (−12, 8) with coefficient −4.
- [ ] Coupling matrices are witnessed per nonzero entry.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-actuate/tests/direction_witness.rs` and `crates/frob-fsim/tests/transformer_entry.rs`:
- `gear_positive_ratio_passes`: N = +9: unit effort on the motor gives a positive joint rate.
- `gear_negative_ratio_authored_passes`: N = −9 authored and implemented: negative joint rate, passes.
- `gear_ratio_negated_in_lowering_fails`: authored +9, lowered as −9: refused with FROB-REF-108.
- `power_balance_blind_to_sign`: the same reversed system closes its port account to 10⁻¹².
- `two_store_canonical_rates`: efforts (2, 3), coefficient 4 gives (12, −8); the power sum 2·12 + 3·(−8)
  is 0.
- `two_store_negated_coefficient`: coefficient −4 gives (−12, 8).
- `coupling_per_entry`: C = [[1, 1], [1, −1]]: four witnesses with signs (+, +, +, −).
- `zero_rate_is_failure`: a decoupled lowering (entry dropped) fails.
- `catalog_sweep`: every catalog card passes; every negated variant fails.
- `no_legacy_symbol`: source scan of `frob-fsim`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_coupled_transmissions.sh` (bead N15b-13): `frob actuator check <card> --bind
<design>:<joint> --json` over the catalog (exit 0) and `frob design check quad-reversed-knee --json` (exit 3,
FROB-REF-108, stage 1). The demonstration that the energy balance cannot see the defect stays a unit test,
because no shipped profile may bypass the refusal. Lines log the authored and observed signs, the refusal
object and the gate name.

### Known answers and negative controls
The reported two-store rates (12, −8) and (−12, 8). Negative control: `mutant-ratio-negated` (provisional
counterexample CX-SINEW-c in N15-CX); a witness that tests power instead of motion
(`mutant-witness-uses-power`) passes the reversed system and is caught by
`gear_ratio_negated_in_lowering_fails`. Metamorphic: negating both the authored sign and the implementation
leaves the verdict unchanged. Determinism: the witness verdict is a sign, class D0.

## Considerations

- The reported sibling rates were read, not executed; the unit test above executes them at the pin and a
  difference is recorded as a foundation fact changed, never patched over.
- A linkage whose ratio changes sign across its range (a toggle) is witnessed at its reference
  configuration and at both ends of its declared range.
- If the sibling removes the legacy entry point, the lint becomes vacuous and stays in place.
