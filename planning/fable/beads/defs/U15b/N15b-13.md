## Background

GATE-SINEW-10 is the known-answer gate of the coupled-transmission rules (milestone M1). Its pass criterion,
in full: the one-rotor table is exact: the envelope has one generator (1, 1) N·m, the command (1, −1) N·m is
refused as outside the image, the reflected inertia is [[1, 1], [1, 1]] and the two energies are 0 J and
2 J; the diagonal mutant reports 1 J twice and fails. For 10³ random couplings with up to four motors and
six joints the power τ_jointᵀ·q̇ equals τ_motorᵀ·θ̇ to 10⁻¹², and the mass matrix with pulled-back rotors
equals the mass matrix of the model with explicit rotor coordinates to 10⁻¹⁰. The signed motion witness
passes on every card of the catalog and fails on each card with its ratio negated.

Three mechanism beads implement the rules (N15b-10 the torque set, N15b-11 the rotor inertia and bias,
N15b-12 the witness). This bead is the measurement: one script that drives the real `frob` binary on real
fixtures, records the measured values against the thresholds, and produces the gate receipt. A gate is
recorded as passed only with measured values, the commit, the hardware fingerprint and the seed.

The fixture, from the plan: one base-mounted rotor with θ = 10·(q₁ + q₂), torque limit 0.1 N·m and inertia
0.01 kg·m², driving two revolute joints.

## Scope

In: the fixtures `one-rotor-coupled`, `random-couplings` (10³ generated designs, seed-explicit),
`quad-reversed-knee` and `witness-rig`; the script; the mutant runs; the gate receipt. Out: the mechanisms themselves.

## Technical approach

**Fixtures** (all `synthetic`, under `assets/fixtures/sinew/coupled/`):
- `one-rotor-coupled.fbot`: two links, two revolute joints `j1` and `j2`, one actuator on the base with a
  `coupling` transmission of matrix C = [10, 10]; card with `rotor_inertia = 0.01 kg·m²`, peak torque
  0.1 N·m, an explicit current convention and provenance on every field. The card and the transmission are
  lossless (Coulomb, breakaway and viscous friction 0; both efficiencies 1), and the fixture's run condition
  is a free swing: gravity off, the driver disabled (zero current), initial joint rates (1, 1) rad/s. With
  nothing dissipating, the referee run of step 3 must conserve the links' and the rotor's energy.
- `random-couplings`: a generator keyed by seed produces 10³ couplings with m ≤ 4 motors and n ≤ 6 joints,
  integer entries in [−12, 12] with at least one nonzero per motor, rotor inertias in [10⁻⁵, 10⁻²] kg·m²,
  and for each a second design with explicit rotor bodies constrained by θ = C·q.
- `quad-reversed-knee.fbot`: the reference quadruped with one knee gear lowered with a negated ratio
  through a test card whose authored sign is positive.
- `witness-rig.fbot`: one revolute joint `j1` on a fixed base carrying a 0.05 kg·m² free load, to which each
  catalog card is bound for the witness sweep, and one prismatic joint `p1` carrying a 1 kg free load, for
  the screw card, whose driven port is linear. The rig itself binds the catalog's `qdd-m` actuator at both
  joints, with the generic gear at `j1` and the generic screw at `p1`; the sweep's `--bind` replaces the one
  of the card's own kind and keeps the other.
- The task fixtures that steps 3 and 4 run, `one-rotor-coupled` and `random-couplings`, are registered with
  manifests of N24b-03's format `frob.fixture/1` at `assets/fixtures/<name>/fixture.toml`: the first names
  the design above and declares the rows `inertia` and `energy` with their exact known answers; the second
  names the generator, declares `--seed` as an axis (N24bx-01) and the rows `power-identity` (10⁻¹²) and
  `mass-matrix` (10⁻¹⁰).

**Script `scripts/e2e/sinew_coupled_transmissions.sh`** (plain shell, idempotent, seed-explicit; sources
the shared helper of STD-E2E):
1. `frob actuator envelope one-rotor-coupled:j1 --json` (a coupled group is addressed by any of its joints,
   N15b-10): assert `joint_torque_set.joints == [j1, j2]` and `joint_torque_set.generators == [[1, 1]]` N·m
   (assert lines tagged with N15-KA's row `sinew-late-01`).
2. `frob actuator envelope one-rotor-coupled:j1 --torque "1,-1" --require --json`: assert exit 3,
   FROB-REF-131, a separator present (the coupled-group `--torque` input of N15b-10); the same call without
   `--require`: exit 2, the finding with the same separator (the verb's rule, BD-073). Tagged `sinew-late-01`.
3. `frob task fixture one-rotor-coupled --report inertia,energy --key ct-inertia --json`: assert reflected
   inertia [[1, 1], [1, 1]] kg·m², energy 0 J at rates (1, −1) rad/s and 2 J at (1, 1) rad/s, exactly (assert
   lines tagged with N15-KA's row `sinew-late-02`). Then the referee free swing of N15b-11: `frob sim run
   --fixture one-rotor-coupled --tier referee --seconds 2 --seed 2 --key ct-swing --json` (the fixture form,
   so that its declared run condition applies: joint rates (1, 1) rad/s, gravity off, driver disabled), and
   `frob telemetry energy <run> --json`: total energy (link plus pulled-back rotor) conserved to 10⁻⁹
   relative.
4. `frob task fixture random-couplings --seed 20261122 --key ct-random --json`: assert max abs(τ_jointᵀ·q̇ − τ_motorᵀ·θ̇)
   relative ≤ 10⁻¹² and max mass-matrix difference ≤ 10⁻¹⁰ over the 10³ cases; log the worst case.
5. For each card in `frob actuator list --kind actuator --json` and `frob actuator list --kind transmission
   --json` (the two kinds a joint binds; a battery card has no port pair): `frob actuator check <card> --bind
   witness-rig:j1 --json` (the `--bind <design>:<joint>` form of N15b-12; a screw card is bound at
   `witness-rig:p1`) exits 0 with `direction_witness: pass`. The number of cards checked is logged beside the
   catalog's count of the two kinds.
6. `frob design check quad-reversed-knee --stages 1 --json` (the stage runner and verb of N28ap1-01): exit 3,
   FROB-REF-108, stage 1.
7. Mutants, through the conformance harness: `mutant-rotor-diagonal` must make step 3 report 1 J twice and
   fail; `mutant-torque-box` must make step 2 admit the command and fail; `mutant-ratio-negated` applied to
   every catalog card must make step 5 fail for every card.
8. `gate-receipt`: `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SINEW-10 --mutations` (the gate's two rows on the tier
   N15-KA gives them, `sinew-late-01` and `sinew-late-02`, G1 on the host, with their registered controls, the
   box mutant and the diagonal mutant); assert from the receipt (`e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) that its
   values equal those of steps 1 to 3 and that each listed control is `caught`, and list the receipt hash in
   the summary. The free swing's drift, the random battery's residuals and the witness counts of steps 3 to 5
   are `metric` lines tagged GATE-SINEW-10, each with its threshold. The script never runs `record_gate` and
   never writes `registries/gates.toml` (STD-E2E's `self-recording` rule); GATE-SINEW-10 is recorded from the
   receipt by a session that did not implement the work: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate
   -- --gate GATE-SINEW-10 --receipt <hash>`.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_coupled_transmissions/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript
beside it. Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N15b-13"), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`,
`metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block
(claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds, and on
failure the first differing value and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives
assertions passed and failed and the gate touched. No private-scene data appears (the fixtures are
synthetic).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits zero on a clean tree and non-zero when any assertion fails.
- [ ] The tier receipt records `sinew-late-01` (one generator (1, 1) N·m; refusal of (1, −1) N·m) and
  `sinew-late-02` (inertia [[1, 1], [1, 1]]; energies 0 J and 2 J) with their controls caught, and names
  commit, fingerprint and seed; the log's gate-tagged `metric` lines record the free swing's energy drift
  ≤ 10⁻⁹ relative, power identity residual ≤ 10⁻¹², mass-matrix difference ≤ 10⁻¹⁰, and a witness pass count
  equal to the number of actuator and transmission cards in the catalog, with the fail count equal to it
  under negation.
- [ ] The three mutants are each caught, and the log shows the first failing assertion for each.
- [ ] Running the script twice produces identical measured values (the exact rows bit for bit).
- [ ] The gate is registered in the conformance registry with its negative controls; a registration without
  them fails the registry check. Its value is recorded from the receipt with `record_gate`, by this bead
  alone.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The mechanisms' unit tests live with them. This bead adds `crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_sinew_10.rs`:
- `fixture_generator_is_deterministic`: the same seed yields byte-identical random-coupling designs.
- `receipt_schema`: the receipt validates and contains measured values, not targets.
- `gate_has_negative_controls`: the registry lists the three mutants for this gate.

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified above; this bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
N15-KA's rows on fixture `one-rotor-coupled`: `sinew-late-01`, deliverable torques (t, t) with abs(t) ≤ 1 N·m
(control: per-joint limits of 1 N·m, which admit (1, −1) N·m); `sinew-late-02`, reflected inertia [[1, 1],
[1, 1]] kg·m² against the shortcut's diag(1, 1), rotor energy 0 J at (1, −1) rad/s and 2 J at (1, 1) rad/s
against the shortcut's 1 J and 1 J. Negative controls: the three mutants (provisional counterexamples CX-SINEW-a,
-b, -c). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; both host platforms for the exact rows.

## Considerations

- Steps 1 to 3 are exact in rational arithmetic; tolerances apply only to the random battery.
- The random battery's integer entries keep the explicit-rotor comparison well conditioned; a later battery
  with configuration-dependent couplings belongs to the varying-coupling tests of N15b-11.
- The script needs only the CPU; it runs in the `smoke` battery.
