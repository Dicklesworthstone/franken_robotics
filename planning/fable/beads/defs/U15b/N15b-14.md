## Background

A battery ages: its capacity fades and its internal resistance grows. Ageing answers one class of question,
design margins at end of life: whether the design that passes the stage-6 bus check when new still passes
at eighty percent capacity and higher resistance. It has no effect on a new robot's verdicts, which is the
ceiling of its benefit, stated before any measurement. The plan models it with two slow states, capacity
fade f_Q and resistance growth f_R, with

  Q = Q₀·(1 − f_Q),    R₀ = R₀₀·(1 + f_R),

that advance **between episodes, never within a step**. Battery ageing between episodes is an option beside
the actuator model ladder and is part of the model identity. Tier: Frontier [F].

The item has a kill gate. KILL-SINEW-AGE (decided at M6, before the lifecycle work package): if the ageing
law fails its pilot, or no card with ageing coefficients better than `assumed` exists when the lifecycle
work is funded, the fallback is that f_Q and f_R are user-set constants per scenario, with no evolution
law. The plan also says that with no card data the states are user-set constants. That constant form
therefore exists on both branches of the decision, and this bead builds it. The evolution law is N15b-17
and exists only if the gate does not fire.

## Scope

In: the two states as fields of the battery's scenario parameters; their effect on Q and R₀ on every tier;
their place in `ModelId`; the aged stage-6 bus check; aged model variants as per-world parameters; refusals
for out-of-range values. Out: the law that evolves them (N15b-17); its pilot (N15b-15); the decision record
(N15b-16); lifecycle compression and the other slow states of the fabrication subsystem (backlash wear,
fatigue, insulation), which belong to its own epic.

## Technical approach

**State (DESIGN).**
```rust
pub struct BatteryAge { f_q: Ratio /* in [0, 1) */, f_r: Ratio /* ≥ 0 */, provenance: Provenance }
```
Set per scenario (`--battery-age f_q=0.20,f_r=0.50`), per task randomization range, or per world in the
parameter row. Default (0, 0), written out in the canonical form. Provenance of a user-set value is
`assumed` unless it comes from a card or a measured record, so results built on it are Estimated and are
refused under `--strict-data`.

**Effect.**
1. Capacity: Q = Q₀·(1 − f_Q). Discharge time at constant current becomes Q₀·(1 − f_Q)/I.
2. Series resistance: R₀ = R₀₀·(1 + f_R), applied after the temperature law.
3. Everything downstream follows without special cases: the source voltage and the bus ceiling E²/(4·R),
   brownout, exhaustion, the stage-6 check. A 20% rise in the series resistance R lowers the ceiling by
   17%, so an ageing state can move a passing design to a refusal.
4. The states are constant within an episode on every tier. No kernel writes them.

**Identity.** `ModelId` includes the ageing option (off; constants; evolved) and, for constants set per
scenario, the values; per-world values travel in the parameter row like other card-derived variants (the
padded model tables of BD-170), so aged and new robots can share one batch.

**The option on the verbs.** `--battery-age f_q=<ratio>,f_r=<ratio>` (either member may be omitted and
defaults to 0) on `design.check`, `actuator.bus` and `sim.run`, implemented here. Those verbs' rows list it
with this bead as owner from M6: `battery_age` of `design.check` (VERBS-design, 111 for a value outside its
bounds and 133 for an aged bus that cannot supply the demand), `--battery-age` and 111 on `actuator.bus`
(VERBS-actuator), and `--battery-age f_q=<ratio>,f_r=<ratio>` on `sim.run`, part of `ModelId` (VERBS-sim).

**Aged check in the preflight.** `frob design check … --battery-age f_q=…,f_r=…` and `frob actuator bus …
--battery-age …` evaluate the bus feasibility at the aged values, at end-of-discharge voltage and
cold-temperature resistance as usual. The report states the age it assumed.

**Refusals.** f_Q outside [0, 1) or f_R negative is a unit-or-range refusal of the design language
(FROB-REF-111 for a value outside its bounds); an aged capacity that makes the mission's energy budget
infeasible surfaces through the existing codes (FROB-REF-133, FROB-REF-135), never a new one.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The two states exist on the referee and the batched tiers and change Q and R₀ exactly as above.
- [ ] A design that passes the stage-6 bus check new and fails it at f_Q = 0.20, f_R = 0.50 is reported
  with both verdicts and the age that separates them.
- [ ] Two models that differ only in battery age have different identities; per-world ages in one batch
  give per-world results identical to the same worlds run alone.
- [ ] Within an episode the states are bit-constant (a test reads them at every step).
- [ ] With the option off, digests equal those of a build without the feature.
- [ ] Results that rest on a user-set age are class Estimated and are refused under `--strict-data`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-actuate/tests/battery_age.rs`:
- `capacity_fade`: Q₀ = 36,000 C, f_Q = 0.20, I = 40 A: discharge time 720 s (900 s new).
- `resistance_growth_ceiling`: E = 48 V, R₀₀ = 0.05 Ω, harness 0.03 Ω, f_R = 0.50: R = 0.105 Ω, ceiling
  48²/(4·0.105) = 5,486 W (7,200 W new).
- `twenty_percent_r_seventeen_percent_ceiling`: scaling total R by 1.2 gives 6,000 W, a fall of 16.7%.
- `aged_design_flips`: a 6,500 W peak passes new and refuses with FROB-REF-133 when aged as above.
- `constant_within_episode`, `identity_includes_age`, `option_off_is_inert`, `out_of_range_refused`.
- `per_world_age_batch_invariance`.
- `user_set_age_is_assumed`: with a user-set f_R = 0.50 the bus-feasibility result is class Estimated with
  the age's `f_r` as weakest input (provenance `assumed`), and N15b-06's strict-data check over its read set
  refuses with FROB-REF-113 pointing at that field; an age taken from a measured record is not refused.

### End-to-end script and logging
Fixture `discharge-40a` (`synthetic`; a design file committed by this bead at
`assets/fixtures/sinew/power/discharge-40a.fbot`, run as a positional design; N15b-16 runs it too, and N15b-17
commits its own `discharge-40a-ageing`): the battery and harness of N15b-30's `bus-48v` (OCV flat at 50 V,
R₀₀ = 0.05 Ω, pairs (0.02 Ω, 500 F) and (0.03 Ω, 4,000 F), harness 0.03 Ω) with capacity Q₀ = 36,000 C
(10 A·h), state of charge starting at 1 with lower bound 0, and one constant-current load member drawing
40 A from t = 0; no actuator and no controller. It exhausts at Q₀/I = 900 s new and at Q₀·(1 − f_Q)/I = 720 s
at f_Q = 0.20 (KA-328).

`scripts/e2e/sinew_battery_age.sh`: `frob actuator bus bus-48v --demand 6500W --source-voltage 48V --json`
(exit 0, ceiling 7,200 W; E is pinned at 48 V, as N15b-30 pins it, because the ceilings asserted here are
those of E = 48 V and R = 0.08 Ω), the same with `--battery-age f_q=0.20,f_r=0.50` (exit 3, FROB-REF-133,
ceiling 5,486 W at R = 0.105 Ω); `frob design check bus-48v --battery-age f_q=0.20,f_r=0.50 --json` (the
stage-6 refusal with the age it assumed); `frob sim run discharge-40a --tier referee --seed 4 --key age-new-1
--json` (exhaustion at 900 s) and `frob sim run discharge-40a --tier referee --battery-age f_q=0.20 --seed 4
--key age-old-1 --json` (exhaustion at 720 s; a distinct key, so the second run is not served the first's
receipt). The script sources the shared
helper of STD-E2E and logs to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_battery_age/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: each line
carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (class
Estimated for the user-set age), the ceilings and exhaustion times with units and thresholds (720 s and 900 s
within one referee step; ceilings within 1 W), the two `ModelId`s (which must differ), `refusal` lines with code
and first repair, and on failure the first differing value and the run identifiers. `summary`: assertions passed
and failed, `gates: []`.

### Known answers and negative controls
The ceiling arithmetic of the bus (KA-324) at aged values; discharge time Q/I (KA-328) with the faded
capacity. Negative controls: `mutant-age-ignored` (the states parsed and not applied) must fail
`aged_design_flips`; `mutant-age-written-in-step` (a kernel that updates f_Q within an episode) must fail
`constant_within_episode`. Determinism: as the battery element.

## Considerations

- Open question of the fabrication chapter on where wear, insulation and cell-fade coefficients come from
  (owner Sinew lead): generic cards marked `assumed`, which strict data refuses; it blocks the lifecycle
  gate, not this bead.
- The fall-back form is deliberately small: two numbers per scenario. It must stay useful if the evolution
  law is never funded.
- The `--battery-age` rows of `design.check` (VERBS-design), `actuator.bus` (VERBS-actuator) and `sim.run`
  (VERBS-sim) are in place; the evolved form `--battery-age evolved` on `sim.run` is N15b-17's, not this
  bead's.
- Calendar and cycle ageing interact with temperature; the constant form says nothing about how an age was
  reached.
