## Background

An actuator card is immutable, content-addressed data in which every field carries a provenance
(`datasheet` with a citation, `measured`, `generic`, `assumed`) and an uncertainty. The starter catalog
contains generic classes only (small, medium and large quasi-direct-drive; hobby servo; strain-wave; hub
motor), marked `generic`. Real parts enter only with a cited datasheet. Which real parts ship, and under
what data licence, is an open question for the project owner:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Identifier | OPEN-SINEW-1 |
| Question | Which real actuator parts ship in the catalog, and under what data licence |
| Default until decided (fail closed) | Generic classes only |
| Owner | Project lead |
| Blocks | Nothing before M4 |

The program's consolidated list groups it with the same question for sensor cards and for film and soil
tables: the default is generic classes and ranges labelled `assumed`; the consequence of the default is
that every result that depends on them stays Estimated and that realism and Reality claims are refused;
the decision is due at M4, and the question is asked of the owner at M3, one milestone before, together with
the sensor-card question (OPEN-SENS-4, N20a-32) and the film and soil question (OPEN-TRAC-3, N18b-36). The M4
exit review waits on this decision, which is why its priority is one step above its milestone's. The question
matters for three downstream items: the common-mode risk RISK-32 (tiers agree with each other and disagree with
reality because they share cards), whose leading indicator is the share of `assumed` and `generic` fields in
benchmark designs; the kill gate KILL-SINEW-AGE (decided at M6), one of whose two triggers is that no card with
ageing coefficients better than `assumed` exists when the lifecycle work is funded (the other is the ageing law
failing its pilot); and the calibration subsystem, which revises cards from measurements.

## Scope

In: preparing the decision (candidate parts, sources, licences, what each would unlock); recording the
owner's ruling; if parts are admitted, the admission procedure for each card. Out: writing the cards'
schema and checks (BD-069); calibration from logs (the calibration subsystem); licence joins and private
marks (the safety chapter's beads).

## Technical approach

**What the owner is asked.**
1. Whether any real part enters the shipped catalog at all, or the catalog stays generic and real parts are
   supplied by users in their own namespaces.
2. If parts ship: which families first (the motors of the reference robots are the natural candidates), and
   from which sources (manufacturer data sheets, published identification studies, the program's own bench
   measurements).
3. Under what licence the transcribed data may be redistributed, per source; whether fields may be
   paraphrased from a data sheet; whether a citation without redistribution (a card that names its source
   and ships no numbers) is acceptable.
4. Whether cards for parts carry trademarks or model names, or neutral identifiers with the citation.

**Decision packet (prepared for the owner).**
- A table of candidate parts: source, licence terms as read, fields available with provenance `datasheet`,
  fields that would remain `assumed` (typically thermal conductances, friction, ageing).
- For each candidate, the change in the provenance share of the benchmark designs (N15b-06 computes it).
- The consequences of each option for claims: with generic classes only, every benchmark result is
  Estimated and realism and Reality claims are refused; with cited parts, card-derived results become
  Tested for the cited fields only.
- Risks: licence violation; a data sheet's optimistic ratings entering as `datasheet` (the card checks
  still apply: torque and back-EMF consistency, passivity in four quadrants, ratings reachable inside the
  card's own limits).

**If parts are admitted, per card.**
1. Transcribe with a citation per field (document, revision, page or table).
2. State the current convention (`dc`, `phase-peak`, `phase-rms` or `q-axis`), `pole_pairs` and
   `modulation`; a card without it refuses (FROB-REF-134).
3. Run `actuator.check`: the three checks at admission.
4. Record the licence in the card's metadata so that exports can join licences.
5. Mark every field with no source `assumed`; under `--strict-data` verdicts resting on those are refused.

**Fail-closed default.** Generic classes only. No real part name appears in the shipped catalog; a card
with a part name and no citation is refused at admission.

**How the outcome is recorded.** A ruling is written as `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-SINEW-1.md` (question, ruling, date,
scope, licence per admitted source) and the row OPEN-SINEW-1 of `registries/open.toml` gets `status =
"decided"` with that ADR (N41-03's vocabulary; the identifier is retired, never reused). If no ruling exists at
the M4 exit, the exit reviewer takes the default with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example open_questions
-- default OPEN-SINEW-1 --exit M4` (the example target of N41-03's register commands, BD-025's form; there is
no `frob-conformance` binary), which writes the default ADR (the capability removed: realism and Reality
claims resting on cards; the label
results carry: `Estimated`) and sets `status = "defaulted"`; a later ruling supersedes it from its date.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision packet exists with the candidate table, licence readings and consequences.
- [ ] The owner's ruling is recorded as `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-SINEW-1.md` with its date and scope, and
  `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example open_questions -- due --exit M4` reads OPEN-SINEW-1 as `answered`
  (status `decided` with that ADR); or,
  with no ruling, it reads `defaulted` with the default ADR. It never reads `silent` at the M4 exit.
- [ ] If parts are admitted: each shipped card passes `actuator.check`, carries a citation on every
  `datasheet` field and a licence record; the provenance share of the benchmark designs is recomputed and
  reported.
- [ ] If the ruling is "generic only": the catalog check that refuses uncited part names is in place, and
  reports keep the line stating that results rest on generic cards.
- [ ] KILL-SINEW-AGE's card-inventory clause is re-read against the ruling.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-actuate/tests/catalog_policy.rs`:
- `uncited_part_name_refused`: a card with a manufacturer part name and provenance `generic` refuses.
- `datasheet_field_requires_citation`: provenance `datasheet` without a citation refuses.
- `licence_recorded`: an admitted card exposes its licence to the export join.
- `generic_catalog_share`: with the starter catalog the benchmark designs report 100% `generic`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_catalog_policy.sh` runs `frob actuator list --json`, then `frob actuator show <card> --json`
and `frob actuator check <card> --json` on every card (and `frob actuator check <card> --strict-data --json` on
each card with an `assumed` field, asserting the refusal), asserts that each `datasheet` field has a citation and
each card a licence record, that the refusing fixtures refuse with their codes (uncited part name and
zero-length citation: FROB-REF-103, the form error of incomplete provenance in `actuator.check`'s row; missing
current convention: FROB-REF-134; the `assumed` field under `--strict-data`: FROB-REF-113), and that `cargo run
-p frob-conformance --example open_questions -- due --exit M4` reads OPEN-SINEW-1 as `answered` or
`defaulted`. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/sinew_catalog_policy/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead` (`N15b-25`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence`
block; per card a `metric` line with its content hash, provenance counts per class (`datasheet`, `measured`,
`generic`, `assumed`), licence and the three check verdicts; per refusing fixture a `refusal` line with the code;
the provenance share of the benchmark designs against the value N15b-06 reports; on failure the first card and
field that differ, with the card path; a final `summary` line with assertions passed and failed.

### Known answers and negative controls
The starter catalog's six generic classes are the known state before the decision. Negative control: a
planted card with a fabricated citation string of zero length must be refused, showing the citation check
is not a presence test on the field name alone.

## Considerations

- The decision can also be "users supply their own": then the catalog policy tests still apply to
  user-namespace cards, and private marks keep proprietary data out of exports.
- A real part's data sheet does not make the simulation real: Reality claims still need measurements with
  their own provenance.
- Prices on cards are a separate open question of the design chapter.
