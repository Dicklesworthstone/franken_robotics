## Background

On the batched tiers the stiction row is a convex potential ℓ(v) = h·τ_b·ψ_ε(v), with ψ_ε(v) = v²/(2ε) for
abs(v) ≤ ε and abs(v) − ε/2 otherwise, and a bound τ_b = τ_c + (τ_s − τ_c)·σ(v⁻/v_s) + c_load·abs(τ_transmitted⁻)
+ τ_iron (the hysteresis share of an L2 actuator's iron loss, zero otherwise; BD-084, BD-155) evaluated at the
start of the step. Two defaults in it are an open question:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Identifier | OPEN-SINEW-2 |
| Question | The default Stribeck shape σ and the default stiction velocity ε on batched tiers |
| Default until decided (fail closed) | The rational shape σ(x) = 1/(1 + x²) and ε = 10⁻³ rad/s, both card-overridable |
| Owner | Sinew lead |
| Decide at | GATE-SINEW-04 (its batched rows, M2) |

Why these two. The shape: σ(x) = exp(−x²) is the other candidate; the rational shape needs no exponential,
and batched kernels use no transcendental built-ins. The velocity ε trades two things. A joint holding a
torque τ below its bound creeps at ε·τ/τ_b, so a smaller ε means less drift (at most ε·t over a time t).
The row's Hessian term inside the kink is h·τ_b/ε, so a smaller ε means a stiffer, worse-conditioned
Newton problem, more iterations, and more exposure to single-precision round-off. The kill gate
KILL-SINEW-ROWS (N15b-24) depends on the same trade, which is why the two are decided from the same
measurements.

## Scope

In: the experiment that measures the trade, on the six registered fixtures of N15a-12 (`ramp-breakaway`,
`spring-coulomb`, `held-load`, `reversal-in-gap`, `gap-hysteresis`, `gap-closure`) and a standing quadruped
(the fixture `quadruped-slope-hold`, owned here: the 12-joint reference quadruped standing on a 10° slope
for 20 s under a constant posture feedforward whose joint torques are below each joint's stiction bound, so
that the rows alone hold it; `synthetic`); the six grid fixtures `<name>/stiction-grid`, registered here;
the choice of defaults; recording it; updating the card schema's defaults (BD-069), the descriptor's defaults
(N15a-08) and the kernel's constants (BD-155). Out: the kernel (BD-155); the kill decision (N15b-24), which
is taken from the same runs; the hold-drift ledger quantity (N15a-11).

## Technical approach

**Experiment (registered before running).**
1. Grid: shape in {rational, Gaussian (through a deterministic exponential on `batch-cpu` only)} ×
   ε in {10⁻⁴, 3 × 10⁻⁴, 10⁻³, 3 × 10⁻³, 10⁻²} rad/s, in `f64` and `f32`.
2. Fixtures: the six stiction and backlash cases of GATE-SINEW-04 and a quadruped standing on a slope for
   20 s with joints holding load (drift of the body is the practical measure). The grid is not a value of
   N15a-12's fixtures (their first runs are at M1, and a value added afterwards would change their `FixtureId`):
   each is registered here as a fixture of its own, `<name>/stiction-grid` (`ramp-breakaway/stiction-grid` and
   the other five; N24bx-01's naming for follow-ups), with the same rig and constants. Each of the seven
   manifests declares `axes.variant` = the ten grid points as constant overrides of the card's ε and shape
   fields (`rational-1e-4`, `rational-3e-4`, `rational-1e-3`, `rational-3e-3`, `rational-1e-2` and the five
   `gauss-…` points), `axes.precision = [f64, f32]`, `axes.profile = [lockstep]` and `axes.seed = [17]`, all
   before the first run.
3. Measures per grid point: (a) the six gate rows against the referee; (b) held-load drift against the
   bound ε·t; (c) Newton iterations to the solver's certificate, mean and 95th percentile; (d) the worst
   relative difference between `f32` and `f64`; (e) the difference of breakaway time and of decay per
   cycle between the two shapes.
4. Decision rule: choose the largest ε for which every row of GATE-SINEW-04 passes on every batched tier
   in `f32` and the standing drift stays below 1 mm of body displacement in 20 s (DESIGN threshold), then
   the shape: rational unless the Gaussian shape changes a gate row by more than its tolerance, in which
   case the finding is recorded and the rational shape stays the batched default with the difference in
   the fidelity ledger (the Gaussian shape is not available on device tiers without a deterministic
   exponential).
5. Record the choice as an architecture decision record in `docs/adr/` (`docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-SINEW-2-M2.md`,
   DESIGN name) and set the row OPEN-SINEW-2 of `registries/open.toml` (N41-OPEN1's) to `status = "decided"`
   with that ADR (N41-03's status vocabulary, in which the exit check reads `decided` as answered).
6. If the chosen ε or shape differs from the default, the kernel constants change, so the stiction strata of
   the fidelity ledger are new strata with no history: their envelopes are frozen afresh (`frob eval run
   --battery standard --class <c> --tier <slow tier> --freeze-envelopes --key sd-freeze-<c> --json`,
   N16bp1-01's freeze action, a run that takes its idempotency key)
   and the one re-baseline record of GATE-SINEW-04, cause `m2-baseline` (N16bp1-01's closed list: the one
   re-baseline a gate is permitted when first measured at M2), is written with `cargo run -p frob-chronicle
   --example record_rebaseline -- --gate GATE-SINEW-04 --cause m2-baseline --doc
   docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-SINEW-2-M2.md` (the decision record, whose front matter lists each changed threshold as
   quantity, old and new); a second record for that pair is refused; no frozen envelope is edited.

**Fail-closed default until decided.** Rational shape, ε = 10⁻³ rad/s; a card may override both, and an
override changes the instance's card hash.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The grid results are stored with their receipts; the table of drift, iterations and precision gap per
  grid point is in the decision record.
- [ ] The chosen defaults pass every batched row of GATE-SINEW-04 in `f32` and `f64`.
- [ ] The card schema's defaults and the kernel's constants equal the decision; a test pins them.
- [ ] The open-question register marks OPEN-SINEW-2 `decided`, citing the record.
- [ ] If the chosen ε differs from 10⁻³ rad/s, the fidelity ledger's frozen envelopes for the stiction cases
  are re-baselined once, and that re-baseline is written down.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-kernels/tests/stiction_defaults.rs`:
- `default_constants_match_record`: the kernel's ε and shape equal the recorded decision.
- `creep_bound_holds_on_grid`: for each ε the measured creep is at most ε·τ/τ_b·(1 + 10⁻³).
- `hessian_scale`: h·τ_b/ε at h = 2 ms, τ_b = 0.5 N·m: 10, 1 and 0.1 kg·m² at ε = 10⁻⁴, 10⁻³, 10⁻².
- `card_override_changes_hash`.
- `grid_fixtures_declared` (`crates/frob-conformance`): the seven grid manifests declare the ten variants, both
  precisions, `lockstep` and seed 17; each `<name>/stiction-grid` has the rig and constants of N15a-12's `<name>`
  and its own `FixtureId`; the variant `rational-1e-3` resolves to the default constants exactly.
- `rebaseline_once_for_gate`: with a changed default, one re-baseline record for (GATE-SINEW-04, `m2-baseline`)
  is written and a second is refused naming the first (N16bp1-01's rule), and no frozen envelope is edited.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_stiction_defaults.sh` sweeps the grid over the seven fixtures with `frob task fixture
<fixture>/stiction-grid --tier <batch-cpu|cuda|metal> --profile lockstep --variant <shape>-<ε> --precision
f64|f32 --seed 17 --key sd-<fixture>-<tier>-<variant>-<precision> --json` (`quadruped-slope-hold` under its own
name; a distinct key per grid cell, since the cells are compared), collects drift, iteration counts and gate
rows, and prints the decision table. The Gaussian arm runs on `batch-cpu`; on each device tier present the same
request is asserted to refuse with FROB-REF-136 listing `batch-cpu`, and the refusal is logged as a grid cell,
never skipped silently. An undeclared grid value (`--variant rational-5e-4`) is asserted to refuse with
FROB-REF-530. It
sources the shared helper of STD-E2E and writes `target/e2e-logs/sinew_stiction_defaults/<utc>.ndjson`:
one line per grid point with fixture, tier, shape, ε, precision, the measured drift against ε·t and the
1 mm body threshold, mean and 95th-percentile iterations, the `f32`–`f64` gap, the six gate rows with
their thresholds, and the evidence block; a summary line names the chosen defaults and GATE-SINEW-04.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-299 (creep at ε·τ/τ_b; drift at most ε·t): at τ = 0.3, τ_b = 0.5 the creep is 6 × 10⁻⁵, 6 × 10⁻⁴ and
6 × 10⁻³ rad/s for ε = 10⁻⁴, 10⁻³, 10⁻². Shape values: rational σ(1) = 0.5, Gaussian σ(1) = 0.368.
Negative control: a sweep run that silently ignores the variant's ε override yields identical drift at every
grid point and is rejected by a monotonicity check on drift against ε.

## Considerations

- A smaller ε helps holding loads and hurts conditioning; the answer may differ between `f32` and `f64`,
  in which case the default is the `f32` value and `strict` in `f64` may use a smaller one.
- The decision is revisited if the kill gate fires (the rows then leave the throughput tiers and only
  `strict` uses the default).
