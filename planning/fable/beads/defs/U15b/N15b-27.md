## Background

At actuator level L2 the motor's losses heat a two-node thermal network, and the thermal steady state
decides whether a duty is sustainable. Copper loss is always modelled. Iron loss (hysteresis and eddy
currents in the stator steel, growing with electrical speed) is a second loss with its own card fields
(`iron_loss` coefficients in the electrical group of the card) and is often absent from data sheets.
Whether it is on by default is an open question:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Identifier | OPEN-SINEW-3 |
| Question | Whether iron loss is on by default at L2 |
| Default until decided (fail closed) | On when the card gives coefficients; otherwise zero, with the flag `iron loss not modelled` on every result |
| Owner | Sinew lead |
| Decide at | GATE-SINEW-05 (the thermal known-answer gate, M1) |

Why it matters. Leaving iron loss out makes high-speed duties look cooler than they are, which is the
unsafe direction for a thermal check; leaving it silently at zero would hide that. The default therefore
keeps the omission visible on every result. The alternative defaults are: refuse L2 thermal verdicts for a
card without coefficients (strict, and it would refuse most generic cards), or fill a generic coefficient
marked `assumed` (keeps verdicts flowing and puts an invented number into them).

## Scope

In: the measurement that informs the choice, with its three card variants and three one-joint designs; the
decision; the flag's wording and where it appears; the card-schema default. Out, each a dependency because
the measurement runs on it: the L2 model with the iron-loss law and its drag split (BD-071); the thermal
static functions, their gate and the verb's `--speed` input (BD-072); the envelope verb, which carries the
flag (BD-073); the stiction row that takes the hysteresis share (BD-084); the port account's `iron` line
(N15b-05) and the ledger verb that prints it (BD-092); stage 6 of `design.check` (BD-110, with the analysis
A-11 of BD-103, which adds the iron loss at each operating point's speed).

## Technical approach

**The model under discussion (DESIGN form, as implemented in BD-071).** Iron loss power
P_fe = k_h·abs(ω_e) + k_e·ω_e², with ω_e the electrical speed and the two coefficients from the card,
entering the winding node's heat balance and the port account's `iron` attribution line; mechanically it
is a drag on the rotor whose power is P_fe, so that the energy account closes: the eddy share as viscous
damping N²·k_e·p² on the joint's diagonal, the hysteresis share as the constant abs(N)·k_h·p added to the
stiction row's bound (never the quotient P_fe/ω, which is singular at rest and chatters).

**The static speed.** Iron loss depends on speed, so a static thermal function needs one. `actuator.thermal
--speed ω` (BD-072) gives the motor shaft speed at which P_fe is added to the loss curve, with
ω_e = `pole_pairs`·ω; its default is 0, the holding duty, where P_fe is zero. Stage 6 uses each operating
point's own speed (A-11, BD-103).

**Experiment.**
1. On the thermal worked card (R₀ = 0.20 Ω at 25 °C, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹, G = 0.80 W/K, C = 120 J/K, limit
   155 °C), add iron loss at three sizes relative to copper loss at the continuous rating (0%, 10%, 30%)
   and recompute, for a low-speed holding duty and a high-speed duty: equilibrium temperature, largest
   continuous current, and the verdict of the static check. The three sizes are the three `synthetic` cards
   of the end-to-end script below (no coefficients; 10.4 W and 31.2 W at a motor speed of 300 rad/s), and
   the two duties are `--speed 0rad/s` and `--speed 300rad/s` at 17 A.
2. Count how many catalog cards carry coefficients, and with what provenance.
3. Decision rule (DESIGN): keep the stated default if (a) with coefficients present every row of
   GATE-SINEW-05 still passes, and (b) the flag reaches every consumer (envelope, reports, the
   provenance share). Move to "refuse without coefficients under `--strict-data`" in addition if the 30%
   case flips a verdict on a benchmark design, since a missing loss can then change a decision.
4. Record the choice and close the open question.

**The flag.** A condition `iron-loss-not-modelled` in the claim product, with the human wording
`iron loss not modelled`, attached to every result computed at L2 or by a static thermal function from a
card without coefficients. It propagates by union to anything built on such a result.

**Worked illustration.** At 18.6 A the card's copper loss at the limit is 18.6²·0.20·(1 + 0.00393·130) =
104.5 W, which the conductance removes at a 130 K rise (0.80·130 = 104 W). An added 10% of iron loss
(10.4 W) lowers the copper budget to 93.6 W and the continuous current to 17.6 A; 30% lowers it to
15.5 A. A design holding 17.0 A at speed passes with no iron loss and at 10%, and fails at 30%.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three-size experiment is recorded with its numbers for both duties.
- [ ] The decision is an architecture decision record; the register marks OPEN-SINEW-3 `decided` with it
  (N41-03's status vocabulary).
- [ ] A card without coefficients yields the flag on `sim.run`, `actuator.thermal`, `actuator.envelope` and
  `design.check` results, and on a comparison built on them.
- [ ] A card with coefficients yields no flag and a nonzero `iron` line in the port account, with the
  balance closing to the referee tolerance.
- [ ] GATE-SINEW-05's rows are unaffected with iron loss at zero (they are stated for copper loss).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-actuate/tests/iron_loss.rs`:
- `flag_when_absent`, `no_flag_when_present`, `flag_propagates_by_union`.
- `continuous_current_with_iron_loss`: 18.6 A, 17.6 A and 15.5 A at 0%, 10% and 30%.
- `iron_loss_closes_account`: with the drag split into its viscous and row shares the per-step residual
  stays within 10⁻⁹ relative, including steps that start at rest.
- `iron_loss_nonnegative`: P_fe ≥ 0 for both signs of speed (INV-SINEW-01).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_iron_loss.sh` (plain shell, seed-explicit, idempotent; sources the shared helper of
STD-E2E). Fixtures (`synthetic`, under `assets/fixtures/sinew/iron/`): three variants of the worked card
with 7 pole pairs, so that a motor speed of 300 rad/s is ω_e = 2,100 rad/s: `worked-card` (no `iron_loss`
coefficients); `worked-card-fe10` (k_h = 0.002 W/(rad/s), k_e = 1.406 × 10⁻⁶ W/(rad/s)²: P_fe = 4.2 + 6.2 =
10.4 W); `worked-card-fe30` (three times each: 12.6 + 18.6 = 31.2 W). Three one-joint designs
`hold-17a-at-speed`, `hold-17a-at-speed-fe10` and `hold-17a-at-speed-fe30`, each binding one of the cards,
with the required point 17 A at a motor speed of 300 rad/s and ambient 25 °C. Steps:
1. `frob actuator thermal <card> --current 17A --speed 300rad/s --ambient 25degC --json` on the three cards:
   equilibrium 125.9 °C, 144.1 °C and 180.4 °C (25 + (57.8 + P_fe)/(0.80 − 0.2272), copper loss 57.8 W cold),
   each within 0.5%; `i_continuous` 18.6 A, 17.6 A and 15.5 A; the condition `iron-loss-not-modelled` on the
   first only. The same three with `--speed 0rad/s` (the holding duty): 125.9 °C on all three, and the
   condition still on the first.
2. `frob actuator envelope worked-card --json` carries the condition; on `worked-card-fe10` it does not.
3. `frob design check <design> --stages 6 --json` on the three designs: exit 0 or 2 on the first two, the
   first carrying the condition; exit 3 and FROB-REF-132 on the third, with equilibrium 180.4 °C and largest
   sustainable current 15.5 A. This is the verdict flip the decision rule asks about.
4. `frob sim run --tier referee hold-17a-at-speed-fe10 --actuator-level L2 --seconds 2 --seed 4 --key
   fe-run-fe10 --json`, then `frob telemetry energy <run> --json`: the `iron` line is positive, every step's
   residual is within 10⁻⁹ relative and the condition is absent. The same on `hold-17a-at-speed` (`--key
   fe-run-none`, a distinct key since the two runs are compared): the `iron` line is zero and the condition
   is present.
5. `e2e_expect_caught mutant-iron-loss-silent -- frob actuator thermal worked-card --current 17A --speed
   300rad/s --ambient 25degC --json` (the first command of step 1, in a build with the mutant): the flag is
   then missing and the assertion fails.
Log: NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_iron_loss/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Every
line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N15b-27"), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time and the
envelope's `evidence` block with its conditions; `metric` lines carry the measured temperatures and currents
with units and thresholds; the refusal object is logged whole; a failure names the first differing value
and the artifact paths; the `summary` counts assertions passed and failed and names GATE-SINEW-05.

### Known answers and negative controls
The continuous-current figure 18.6 A of the worked card (KA-307) is the baseline. Negative control: a build
that sets iron loss to zero without attaching the flag (`mutant-iron-loss-silent`) must fail
`flag_when_absent`.

## Considerations

- Iron-loss coefficients are speed- and flux-dependent in reality; a two-coefficient law is a card-level
  approximation and its fields are Estimated unless calibrated.
- Field weakening lowers flux and so iron loss; the simple law ignores that and errs warm, which is the safe
  direction.
- The batched kernel carries the same two-coefficient law (no transcendental needed).
