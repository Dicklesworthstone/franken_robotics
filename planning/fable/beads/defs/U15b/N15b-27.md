## Background

At actuator level L2 the motor's losses heat a two-node thermal network, and the thermal steady state
decides whether a duty is sustainable. Copper loss is always modelled. Iron loss (hysteresis and eddy
currents in the stator steel, growing with electrical speed) is a second loss with its own card fields
(`iron_loss` coefficients in the electrical group of the card) and is often absent from data sheets.
Whether it is on by default is an open question:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Identifier | OPEN-SINEW-3 |
| Question | Whether iron loss is on by default at L2 |
| Default until decided (fail closed) | On when the card gives coefficients; otherwise zero, with the flag `iron loss not modelled` on every result |
| Owner | Sinew lead |
| Decide at | GATE-SINEW-05 (the thermal known-answer gate, M1) |

Why it matters. Leaving iron loss out makes high-speed duties look cooler than they are, which is the
unsafe direction for a thermal check; leaving it silently at zero would hide that. The default therefore
keeps the omission visible on every result. The alternative defaults are: refuse L2 thermal verdicts for a
card without coefficients (strict, and it would refuse most generic cards), or fill a generic coefficient
marked `assumed` (keeps verdicts flowing and puts an invented number into them).

## Scope

In: the measurement that informs the choice; the decision; the flag's wording and where it appears; the
card-schema default. Out: the L2 model (BD-071); the thermal static functions and their gate (BD-072).

## Technical approach

**The model under discussion (DESIGN form, as implemented in BD-071).** Iron loss power
P_fe = k_h·abs(ω_e) + k_e·ω_e², with ω_e the electrical speed and the two coefficients from the card,
entering the winding node's heat balance and the port account's `iron` attribution line; mechanically it
is a drag torque P_fe/ω on the rotor so that the energy account closes.

**Experiment.**
1. On the thermal worked card (R₀ = 0.20 Ω at 25 °C, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹, G = 0.80 W/K, C = 120 J/K, limit
   155 °C), add iron loss at three sizes relative to copper loss at the continuous rating (0%, 10%, 30%)
   and recompute, for a low-speed holding duty and a high-speed duty: equilibrium temperature, largest
   continuous current, and the verdict of the static check.
2. Count how many catalog cards carry coefficients, and with what provenance.
3. Decision rule (DESIGN): keep the stated default if (a) with coefficients present every row of
   GATE-SINEW-05 still passes, and (b) the flag reaches every consumer (envelope, reports, the
   provenance share). Move to "refuse without coefficients under `--strict-data`" in addition if the 30%
   case flips a verdict on a benchmark design, since a missing loss can then change a decision.
4. Record the choice and close the open question.

**The flag.** A condition `iron-loss-not-modelled` in the claim product, with the human wording
`iron loss not modelled`, attached to every result computed at L2 or by a static thermal function from a
card without coefficients. It propagates by union to anything built on such a result.

**Worked illustration.** At 18.6 A the card's copper loss at the limit is 18.6²·0.20·(1 + 0.00393·130) =
104.5 W, which the conductance removes at a 130 K rise (0.80·130 = 104 W). An added 10% of iron loss
(10.4 W) lowers the copper budget to 93.6 W and the continuous current to 17.6 A; 30% lowers it to
15.5 A. A design holding 17.0 A at speed passes with no iron loss and at 10%, and fails at 30%.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three-size experiment is recorded with its numbers for both duties.
- [ ] The decision is an architecture decision record; the register marks OPEN-SINEW-3 closed.
- [ ] A card without coefficients yields the flag on `sim.run`, `actuator.thermal`, `actuator.envelope` and
  `design.check` results, and on a comparison built on them.
- [ ] A card with coefficients yields no flag and a nonzero `iron` line in the port account, with the
  balance closing to the referee tolerance.
- [ ] GATE-SINEW-05's rows are unaffected with iron loss at zero (they are stated for copper loss).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-actuate/tests/iron_loss.rs`:
- `flag_when_absent`, `no_flag_when_present`, `flag_propagates_by_union`.
- `continuous_current_with_iron_loss`: 18.6 A, 17.6 A and 15.5 A at 0%, 10% and 30%.
- `iron_loss_closes_account`: with drag torque P_fe/ω the per-step residual stays within 10⁻⁹.
- `iron_loss_nonnegative`: P_fe ≥ 0 for both signs of speed (INV-SINEW-01).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_iron_loss.sh`: `frob actuator thermal worked-card --current 17A --speed … --json` with
and without coefficients, and `frob design check hold-17a-at-speed --json`; asserts the flag, the verdict
flip at 30%, and the account line. It sources the shared helper of STD-E2E and logs measured temperatures
and currents with thresholds, the evidence block with its conditions, and a summary naming GATE-SINEW-05.

### Known answers and negative controls
The continuous-current figure 18.6 A of the worked card (KA-307) is the baseline. Negative control: a build
that sets iron loss to zero without attaching the flag (`mutant-iron-loss-silent`) must fail
`flag_when_absent`.

## Considerations

- Iron-loss coefficients are speed- and flux-dependent in reality; a two-coefficient law is a card-level
  approximation and its fields are Estimated unless calibrated.
- Field weakening lowers flux and so iron loss; the simple law ignores that and errs warm, which is the safe
  direction.
- The batched kernel carries the same two-coefficient law (no transcendental needed).
