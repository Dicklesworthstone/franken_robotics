## Background

The power path of a robot is everything between the battery's chemistry and the joint's shaft: the two-RC
battery (N15b-03), the shared bus with its power ceiling and brownout event (BD-075), regeneration with its
three policies (N15b-04) and the per-element energy account (N15b-05). A user reaches it through the verb
`actuator.bus` (the shared bus ceiling), through `design.check` (stage 6 refuses an undersized bus),
through `sim.run` (brownout and exhaustion are events of a run), and through `telemetry.energy` (the
ledger with its braking-mode lines). This bead is the end-to-end test of that capability and the
measurement of its gate.

**GATE-SINEW-07** (M1, Known answer): battery and bus: the step response's drop and both time constants
within 1%; the ceiling E²/(4·R) and the voltage at a given power within 0.1%; a demand above the ceiling
produces a brownout event in 100 of 100 trials and never a completed step; exhaustion refuses.

The physics being checked: a source E with series resistance R delivers power P only if P ≤ E²/(4·R), at
V = [E + √(E² − 4·R·P)]/2; above the ceiling there is no operating point. An exhausted battery supplies
nothing, and no code path clips the state of charge.

## Scope

In: fixtures; the script; the mutant runs; the receipt for GATE-SINEW-07; the referee row of the port
balance as an observed (not owned) check. Out: the mechanisms; the referee row of GATE-SINEW-02 as a gate
(seed BD-076 owns it; this script only confirms the account is reachable from the user's side).

## Technical approach

**Fixtures** (`synthetic`, `assets/fixtures/sinew/power/`):
- `bus-48v`: battery OCV flat at 50 V in the tested range, R₀ = 0.05 Ω, pairs (0.02 Ω, 500 F) and
  (0.03 Ω, 4,000 F), harness 0.03 Ω, one programmable constant-power load. Settled at 40 A the source is
  E = 48 V and R = 0.08 Ω.
- `battery-step`: the same battery under a 40 A current step.
- `empty-battery`: capacity 36,000 C, lower state-of-charge bound 0, at a constant 40 A (exhaustion at
  900 s), then a 100 W request.
- `brownout-trials`: 100 seeded draws of E in [20, 60] V, R in [0.02, 0.2] Ω and a demand of 1.01 to 2
  times the ceiling; bus capacitor 2 mF charged to E at t = 0; `brownout = {threshold: 0.4·E, action:
  cutoff, recover_above: 0.9·E}`. Above the ceiling the capacitor current (E − V)/R − P/V is negative at
  every V, and its smallest magnitude, at V = √(P·R), is at least 0.0049·E/R, so every draw reaches the
  threshold within 240·C_b·R ≤ 0.1 s.
- `quad-bus-undersized`: the reference quadruped with a harness resistance that puts its nominal-gait peak
  above the ceiling.
- The three regeneration designs of N15b-04, read by name and never redefined: `flywheel-brake-to-battery`,
  `flywheel-brake-shunt` and `flywheel-brake-clip`, which differ only in the card's `regen_policy` (a 0.02
  kg·m² inertia at 100 rad/s braked at 2 N·m with a copper loss of 20 W, a bus at 48 V; the bus receives 180 W
  at the start). Charge limit 2 A (96 W) for `shunt` and `clip`, and 5 A (240 W) for `to-battery`, a limit
  that accepts the whole surplus, so that no part of it falls back to `clip` (N15b-04's rule 3) and the
  `to-battery` and `clip` designs move differently.

**Script `scripts/e2e/sinew_power_path.sh`** (sources the shared helper of STD-E2E; seed-explicit):
1. `frob task fixture battery-step --tier referee --key pp-step --json`: assert the immediate drop
   40·0.05 = 2.0 V and fitted time constants 10 s and 120 s, each within 1%.
2. `frob actuator bus bus-48v --demand 1800W --source-voltage 48V --json`: assert ceiling 7,200 W, bus
   voltage 44.8 V (44.785) and current 40.2 A (40.19), each within 0.1%.
3. `frob actuator bus bus-48v --demand 8000W --source-voltage 48V --json`: exit 3, FROB-REF-133, payload
   ceiling 7,200 W and demand 8,000 W, a repair present.
4. `frob design check quad-bus-undersized --stages 6 --json` (stage 6 of the assembled preflight, BD-110):
   exit 3, FROB-REF-133; apply `refusal.repairs[0]` with `frob design edit quad-bus-undersized <edit>
   --expected-parent <checked DesignId> --key e2e-power-bus --json` (the registered form of `design.edit`,
   BD-058: without `--key` it is refused with FROB-REF-805, exit 3) and re-check the returned design: exit 0
   or 2.
5. `frob task fixture brownout-trials --tier referee --key pp-brownout --json` (the fixture's 100 seeded
   draws, one world each; `sim.run` has no `--seeds`, and the fixture form keeps the draws and their known
   answers in one registered manifest, as N15b-35 runs it on `batch-cpu`): assert that each of the 100 worlds
   records a `brownout` event, its first one located before 0.1 s (later ones may follow, since `cutoff`
   re-enables the drivers after recovery), and zero intervals in any world whose delivered power reaches the
   request.
6. `frob sim run --fixture empty-battery --tier referee --seed 3 --key pp-empty --json`: assert the
   `battery-exhausted` event located on the dyadic grid at t* = 900 s, N15b-03's exact crossing
   (z₀ − z_min)·Q/I (its `t_refined` equal to 900 s, never the end of a later step); then `frob actuator bus
   empty-battery --demand 100W --soc 0 --json` (the state of charge at its lower bound): exit 3, FROB-REF-135
   with the energy short.
7. `frob sim run --tier referee flywheel-brake-<p> --seed 5 --key pp-regen-<p> --json` for `<p>` in
   `to-battery`, `shunt` and `clip` (three designs, since `--regen` is not a `sim.run` input and `--param` is
   not available before M4), then `frob telemetry energy <run> --json` for each: assert the ledger lines of
   each policy (`to-battery`: `regen_to_battery` only, `shunt` and `clipped_braking` zero; `shunt`: `shunt`
   nonzero, 84 W at the start; `clip`: `clipped_braking` nonzero), the motion (ω(0.2 s) = 80 rad/s within
   0.1% under `to-battery` and `shunt`, which both absorb the full 2 N·m, and 89.24 rad/s under `clip`, whose
   braking torque starts at 1.011 N·m), and a per-step balance residual within 10⁻⁹ relative.
8. Mutants: `mutant-bus-clip-demand` must make step 5 fail; `mutant-battery-clip-soc` must make step 6
   fail; `mutant-ledger-double-heat` must make step 7's residual check fail.
9. `gate-receipts`: the gate's rows in the Gauntlet's tier targets through the STD-E2E helper, each with its
   registered controls, on the tiers N15-KA gives them: `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SINEW-07 --mutations`
   (KA-324, KA-327, KA-328) and `e2e_tier g4_chaos GATE-SINEW-07 --mutations` (KA-325 and KA-326, the
   brownout and exhaustion rows); assert from each receipt (`e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) that its
   values equal those of steps 1 to 6 within the row tolerances and that each listed control is `caught`, and
   list both receipt hashes in the summary. The script never runs `record_gate` and never writes
   `registries/gates.toml` (STD-E2E's `self-recording` rule); GATE-SINEW-07 is recorded from the receipts by a
   session that did not implement the work: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate
   GATE-SINEW-07 --receipt <hash>`.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_power_path/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` ("N15b-30"), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block,
measured values with units and thresholds (volts, amperes, watts, seconds, joules), event counts, the
refusal objects, digests where two runs are compared, the first differing value and artifact paths on
failure, and a `summary` line naming GATE-SINEW-07.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits zero; every row of GATE-SINEW-07 is recorded with its measured value: drop and two
  time constants within 1%; ceiling and voltage within 0.1%; 100 of 100 brownouts and zero completed steps
  at the requested power; exhaustion refused.
- [ ] The stage-6 refusal's first repair passes when applied.
- [ ] The three regeneration policies produce three distinct ledger lines; `clip` alone changes the motion
  (89.24 rad/s against 80 rad/s at t = 0.2 s), since `to-battery` and `shunt` both absorb the full braking
  torque.
- [ ] The three mutants are caught.
- [ ] The tier receipts name commit, fingerprint and seeds, and their values equal the script's.

## Considerations

- The `--source-voltage` option (an input of `actuator.bus`, BD-075) pins E for the closed-form rows; without
  it the verb evaluates E from the battery state at the declared condition (end of discharge, cold), which is
  what stage 6 uses.
- Step 4 is why this script waits for the assembled preflight (BD-110) and the edit verb (BD-058): the
  known answer KA-325 has a stage-6 clause, and it is measured here from the user's side.
- The brownout trials take a few seconds each on the referee; the script runs them as one job and waits.
- The switch-over to the sibling's battery element (N15b-28) re-runs this script unchanged.
