## Background

The power path of a robot is everything between the battery's chemistry and the joint's shaft: the two-RC
battery (N15b-03), the shared bus with its power ceiling and brownout event (BD-075), regeneration with its
three policies (N15b-04) and the per-element energy account (N15b-05). A user reaches it through the verb
`actuator.bus` (the shared bus ceiling), through `design.check` (stage 6 refuses an undersized bus),
through `sim.run` (brownout and exhaustion are events of a run), and through `telemetry.energy` (the
ledger with its braking-mode lines). This bead is the end-to-end test of that capability and the
measurement of its gate.

**GATE-SINEW-07** (M1, Known answer): battery and bus: the step response's drop and both time constants
within 1%; the ceiling E²/(4·R) and the voltage at a given power within 0.1%; a demand above the ceiling
produces a brownout event in 100 of 100 trials and never a completed step; exhaustion refuses.

The physics being checked: a source E with series resistance R delivers power P only if P ≤ E²/(4·R), at
V = [E + √(E² − 4·R·P)]/2; above the ceiling there is no operating point. An exhausted battery supplies
nothing, and no code path clips the state of charge.

## Scope

In: fixtures; the script; the mutant runs; the receipt for GATE-SINEW-07; the referee row of the port
balance as an observed (not owned) check. Out: the mechanisms; the referee row of GATE-SINEW-02 as a gate
(seed BD-076 owns it; this script only confirms the account is reachable from the user's side).

## Technical approach

**Fixtures** (`synthetic`, `assets/fixtures/sinew/power/`):
- `bus-48v`: battery OCV flat at 50 V in the tested range, R₀ = 0.05 Ω, pairs (0.02 Ω, 500 F) and
  (0.03 Ω, 4,000 F), harness 0.03 Ω, one programmable constant-power load. Settled at 40 A the source is
  E = 48 V and R = 0.08 Ω.
- `battery-step`: the same battery under a 40 A current step.
- `empty-battery`: capacity 36,000 C at a constant 40 A (exhaustion at 900 s), then a 100 W request.
- `brownout-trials`: 100 seeded draws of E in [20, 60] V, R in [0.02, 0.2] Ω and a demand of 1.01 to 2
  times the ceiling.
- `quad-bus-undersized`: the reference quadruped with a harness resistance that puts its nominal-gait peak
  above the ceiling.
- `flywheel-brake`: a 0.02 kg·m² inertia at 100 rad/s braked at 2 N·m, bus charge limit 2 A at 48 V.

**Script `scripts/e2e/sinew_power_path.sh`** (sources the shared helper of STD-E2E; seed-explicit):
1. `frob task fixture battery-step --tier referee --json`: assert the immediate drop 40·0.05 = 2.0 V and
   fitted time constants 10 s and 120 s, each within 1%.
2. `frob actuator bus bus-48v --demand 1800W --source-voltage 48V --json`: assert ceiling 7,200 W, bus
   voltage 44.8 V (44.785) and current 40.2 A (40.19), each within 0.1%.
3. `frob actuator bus bus-48v --demand 8000W --source-voltage 48V --json`: exit 3, FROB-REF-133, payload
   ceiling 7,200 W and demand 8,000 W, a repair present.
4. `frob design check quad-bus-undersized --stages 6 --json`: exit 3, FROB-REF-133; apply the first repair
   with `frob design edit …` and re-check: exit 0 or 2.
5. `frob sim run --tier referee brownout-trials --seeds 1..100 --json`: assert 100 worlds with exactly one
   `brownout` event each and zero intervals whose delivered power reaches the request.
6. `frob sim run --tier referee empty-battery --seed 3 --json`: assert the `battery-exhausted` event in the
   interval containing 900 s; then `frob actuator bus empty-battery --demand 100W --json`: exit 3,
   FROB-REF-135 with energy short.
7. `frob sim run --tier referee flywheel-brake --regen to-battery|shunt|clip --seed 5 --json`, then
   `frob telemetry energy <run> --json` for each: assert the expected ledger line is nonzero
   (`regen_to_battery`, `shunt`, `clipped_braking`), three distinct speeds at t = 0.2 s, and a per-step
   balance residual within 10⁻⁹ relative.
8. Mutants: `mutant-bus-clip-demand` must make step 5 fail; `mutant-battery-clip-soc` must make step 6
   fail; `mutant-ledger-double-heat` must make step 7's residual check fail.
9. Write `target/gates/GATE-SINEW-07.json`.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_power_path/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` ("N15b-30"), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block,
measured values with units and thresholds (volts, amperes, watts, seconds, joules), event counts, the
refusal objects, digests where two runs are compared, the first differing value and artifact paths on
failure, and a `summary` line naming GATE-SINEW-07.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits zero; every row of GATE-SINEW-07 is recorded with its measured value: drop and two
  time constants within 1%; ceiling and voltage within 0.1%; 100 of 100 brownouts and zero completed steps
  at the requested power; exhaustion refused.
- [ ] The stage-6 refusal's first repair passes when applied.
- [ ] The three regeneration policies produce three distinct ledger lines and three distinct motions.
- [ ] The three mutants are caught.
- [ ] The receipt names commit, fingerprint and seeds.

## Considerations

- The `--source-voltage` option pins E for the closed-form rows; without it the verb evaluates E from the
  battery state at the declared condition (end of discharge, cold), which is what stage 6 uses.
- The brownout trials take a few seconds each on the referee; the script runs them as one job and waits.
- The switch-over to the sibling's battery element (N15b-28) re-runs this script unchanged.
