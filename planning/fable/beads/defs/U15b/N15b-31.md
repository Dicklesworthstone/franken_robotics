## Background

Stiction, backlash and hard stops are rows of the solve, not forces computed before it: a friction force
computed from the previous velocity chatters about zero and cannot hold a load at rest, and a dead zone
computed outside the solve cannot produce the impact when a gear gap closes. On the referee tier the rows
are exact, with located stick, slip and closure events (BD-084). This bead is the user-side test of that
capability and the measurement of the gate's referee rows; the batched tiers are measured by N15b-37 with
the same fixtures and script structure.

**GATE-SINEW-04** (Known answer; M1 on the referee, M2 on the batched tiers): the six cases: breakaway
within one step of τ_s/r; amplitude loss per cycle within 2% of 4·τ_c/k; drift at most ε·t; zero output
motion inside the gap; loop width within 1% of β; closure speed and energy loss within 1%. Referee exact;
batched tiers within their stated regularization.

The six cases, hand-checkable:

| Case | Expected |
|---|---|
| A torque ramp r·t on a joint at rest with breakaway τ_s | Motion begins at t = τ_s/r, within one step |
| A mass on a spring k with Coulomb friction τ_c, released from rest | Amplitude falls by 4·τ_c/k per cycle; the motion ends inside the band of half-width τ_s/k |
| A held load τ below the bound | No drift on the referee (the convex tiers drift by at most ε·t) |
| A motor reversal smaller than the gap | Zero output motion |
| A slow sinusoid through a gap β | An input-output loop of width β |
| A rotor of reflected inertia J₁ = N²·J_r closing the gap at relative speed Δω onto a link J₂ at rest, no restitution | Common speed J₁·Δω/(J₁ + J₂); energy lost ½·J₁·J₂/(J₁ + J₂)·Δω². With N = 9, J_r = 10⁻⁴ kg·m², J₂ = 0.02 kg·m², Δω = 2 rad/s: 0.577 rad/s and 0.0115 J |

## Scope

In: six registered fixtures; the script's referee section; the dead-zone control; the referee part of the
gate receipt. Out: the mechanism (BD-084); the batched tiers and GATE-SINEW-09 (N15b-37).

## Technical approach

**Fixtures** (`synthetic`, `assets/fixtures/sinew/rows/`, step h = 2 ms):
- `ramp-breakaway`: r = 1 N·m/s, τ_s = 0.5 N·m; expected onset 0.5 s.
- `spring-coulomb`: k = 10 N·m/rad, τ_c = 0.1 N·m, τ_s = 0.12 N·m, released from 0.2 rad; 0.04 rad lost per
  cycle; rest inside ±0.012 rad.
- `held-load`: 0.3 N·m against τ_b = 0.5 N·m for 20 s.
- `reversal-in-gap`: β = 0.5°, motor reversal of 0.3° (output side).
- `gap-hysteresis`: reflected amplitude 2°, β = 0.5°.
- `gap-closure`: the numbers of the table.

**Script `scripts/e2e/sinew_joint_rows.sh`** (plain shell; sources the shared helper of STD-E2E;
seed-explicit; takes `--tiers`, default `referee` at M1):
1. `frob doctor --json` for pins and fingerprint.
2. `frob task fixture ramp-breakaway --tier referee --seed 9 --json`: assert onset time within one step
   (2 ms) of 0.5 s.
3. `spring-coulomb`: assert the amplitude loss per cycle is 0.04 rad within 2% over the first three cycles,
   and that the final rest position lies inside ±0.012 rad.
4. `held-load`: assert displacement below 10⁻¹² rad after 20 s and zero slip events.
5. `reversal-in-gap`: assert output displacement exactly zero and zero closure events.
6. `gap-hysteresis`: assert the loop width is 0.5° within 1%.
7. `gap-closure`: assert common speed 0.577 rad/s and energy loss 0.0115 J within 1%, and exactly one
   closure event.
8. Dead-zone control through the conformance harness: `mutant-backlash-deadzone` must fail step 7 (no
   impact) and step 6 (no loop of width β).
9. Prev-velocity control: `mutant-stiction-prev-velocity` must fail step 4 by chattering (nonzero slip
   events).
10. Determinism: step 7 twice, and once with a forced retry of the step that contains the closure; the
    three digests are equal.
11. Write the referee rows of `target/gates/GATE-SINEW-04.json`.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_joint_rows/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` ("N15b-31"), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), exact
command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block, measured values with units and thresholds,
event counts (stick, slip, closure), digests, the first differing value and artifact paths on failure, and
a `summary` line naming GATE-SINEW-04 (referee rows).

## Considerations

- The cart docking fixture of the arena subsystem also depends on these rows; a failure here will show
  there as creep at the sill.
- "Within one step" for breakaway is a statement about the step, so the fixture fixes h = 2 ms.
- The same script, extended, measures the batched tiers; keeping one script keeps the fixtures identical
  across tiers.
