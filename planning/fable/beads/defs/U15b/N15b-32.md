## Background

An actuator model that can create energy will be found by an optimizer. The chapter's defence is passivity:
every element is written in port-Hamiltonian form with a skew-symmetric structure and a nonnegative
dissipation, so stored energy changes only by port power minus loss, and the second law holds as a per-step
sign test. The classic leak is a transmission with one efficiency multiplier in all quadrants: with η = 0.8
and N = 10, a load driving the joint with 10 N·m at 1 rad/s puts in 10 W, the single-multiplier law gives
1.25 N·m at 10 rad/s at the motor, 12.5 W, and 2.5 W came from nowhere; the four-quadrant law gives 0.8 N·m
and 8 W with 2 W lost.

**GATE-SINEW-08** (M1, Known answer): passivity and second law: over 10⁶ random operating points in all
four quadrants every loss and every entropy production is nonnegative; the single-multiplier gear fixture
refuses; every card in the catalog passes `actuator.check`.

The mechanisms are the transmissions with their loss laws (BD-074), regeneration paths (N15b-04) and the
exact structural check (N15b-09). This bead measures the gate from the user's side.

## Scope

In: the run of the passivity battery from the user's side (the fixture `sinew-passivity-battery`, which is
the registered form of the conformance battery defined by N15a-23); the catalog sweep through
`actuator.check`; the refusing fixtures; the certificate check with the offline auditor; the mutant runs;
the receipt and the gate row of GATE-SINEW-08. This is the only bead that records GATE-SINEW-08. Out, with
the bead that has each: the mechanisms (BD-074, N15a-17, N15b-04, N15b-09); the battery's sampling plan,

unit tests (N15a-18); the card verbs' own script, which also checks the catalog and the rejection fixtures
(N15a-25).

## Technical approach

**Definitions used by the battery.** An operating point is (element, card drawn inside its uncertainty
bands, torque sign and magnitude, speed sign and magnitude, winding and ambient temperatures in kelvin,
electrical mode). Loss terms checked: copper, iron, friction, gear, shunt, harness, battery series and
polarization resistances. Entropy production of a thermal node with loss P_loss, node temperature T_w,
ambient T_amb and conductance G: P_loss/T_w + G·(T_w − T_amb)²/(T_w·T_amb). Points are drawn by address
(a counter-based stream keyed by the point index), so the battery is reproducible on any machine.

**Fixtures** (`synthetic`, `assets/fixtures/sinew/passivity/`): `sinew-passivity-battery` (10⁶ points over
every element kind and every catalog card, a quarter in each quadrant); `gear-single-eta` (η = 0.8 in all
quadrants, N = 10: not a second card but N15a-17's rejection card
`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sinew/transmissions/single_multiplier.fcard`, read in place under this
name, as N15a-25 reads it); `eta-above-one` (efficiency 1.05); `kt-ke-mismatch` (torque and back-EMF
constants that differ by √(3/2), the factor of a missing current convention, beyond the stated uncertainty);
`regen-negated-loss` (a braking mode with a negated copper loss); `negative-conductance` (a winding-to-housing
conductance of −0.5 W/K); `screw-5-3` (lead angle 5°, friction angle 3°) and `screw-3-5` (self-locking);
`screw-hold` (a vertical prismatic joint carrying a 2 kg load under gravity with the driver disabled, run
once behind each of the two screw cards, reporting the load's displacement over 1 s).
`kt-ke-mismatch`, `negative-conductance`, `regen-negated-loss` and `eta-above-one` are the four planted
defects of PO-SINEW-01 (N15b-09), whose end-to-end run is this script.

**Script `scripts/e2e/sinew_passivity.sh`** (sources the shared helper of STD-E2E; seed-explicit):
1. `frob actuator list --json`; for each card `frob actuator check <card> --json`: assert exit 0, the three
   checks reported (constants consistent in power-invariant coordinates within stated uncertainty; every
   loss nonnegative in four quadrants; stated peak and continuous torques reachable inside the card's own
   limits), and the structural certificates present.
2. `frob task fixture sinew-passivity-battery --seed 11 --key pa-battery --json`: assert 10⁶ points
   evaluated, the count of negative losses is 0, the count of negative entropy productions is 0; log the
   minimum loss and the minimum entropy production found and the quadrant counts (250,000 each).
3. `frob actuator check gear-single-eta --at 10,1 --json` (`--at` sets the evaluation point of the
   power-created figure, joint side; a gear card has no rating from which a default point could be taken):
   exit 3, FROB-REF-130, payload quadrant `back-driven` and 2.5 W created at the witness point (10 N·m,
   1 rad/s).
4. `frob actuator check <defect> --json` for each of the four planted defects: exit 3, FROB-REF-130, with
   the element and the matrix entry named (and, for a loss, the quadrant and the power created at the
   witness point).
5. `frob task fixture regen-negated-loss --key pa-regen-neg --json`: the run-time second-law check rejects
   it as well (negative entropy production reported), so the defect is caught on both paths.
6. `frob actuator check screw-5-3 --json`: efficiencies 0.6225 and 0.3991 reported; `screw-3-5`: reported
   self-locking (back efficiency negative). Then the hold on a running system, through the stiction row of
   the referee solve (BD-084): `frob task fixture screw-hold --tier referee --seed 12 --key pa-screw-hold
   --json` reports the load's displacement over 1 s as 0, exactly, behind `screw-3-5` (the row is set-valued
   on the referee and its bound exceeds the load) and as nonzero behind `screw-5-3`, where the same load
   back-drives.
7. Take the certificate bundle (`.frobcert`) stored with one catalog card's version and check it with the
   independent `frob-verify` binary: accepted; a copy with one coefficient changed: rejected.
8. Mutants through the conformance harness: `mutant-gear-single-eta` must make step 2 report negative
   losses in the back-driven quadrant; `mutant-psd-skip-zero-row` must make the structural check accept an
   indefinite matrix and be caught by its unit test, which the script invokes.
9. `gate-receipt`: `e2e_tier g0_laws GATE-SINEW-08 --mutations` (KA-294's tier in N15-KA: the battery
   `sinew-passivity` of N15a-23 at the gate profile, with its registered controls); assert from the receipt
   (`e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) that its point count, quadrant counts and minima equal step 2's and
   that each listed control is `caught`, and list the receipt hash in the summary. The script never runs
   `record_gate` and never writes `registries/gates.toml` (STD-E2E's `self-recording` rule); GATE-SINEW-08 is
   recorded from the receipt by a session that did not implement the work: `cargo run -p frob-conformance
   --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SINEW-08 --receipt <hash>`.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_passivity/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` ("N15b-32"), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, `evidence` block, card
hashes, counts and minima with their zero thresholds, refusal payloads, certificate hashes, first differing
value and artifact paths on failure, and a `summary` naming GATE-SINEW-08.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 10⁶ points, zero negative losses, zero negative entropy productions, 250,000 points per quadrant.
- [ ] Every catalog card passes `actuator.check`; the count equals the catalog size.
- [ ] The single-multiplier gear refuses with the quadrant and 2.5 W; the four planted defects of N15b-09
  refuse with FROB-REF-130 at `actuator.check`, and the negated braking loss is also rejected at run time.
- [ ] The self-locking screw holds on the running referee system: `screw-hold` reports a displacement of
  exactly 0 behind `screw-3-5` and a nonzero one behind `screw-5-3`.
- [ ] The certificate bundle is accepted by the offline auditor and its mutation is rejected.
- [ ] Both mutants are caught.
- [ ] The receipt carries the counts, minima, commit, fingerprint and seed.

## Considerations

- The second-law check cannot fail for a correct model; its content is the sign of each assigned loss, so
  the mutants are what give the gate teeth.
- 10⁶ closed-form evaluations run in well under a minute on one core; the script belongs to the `fixtures`
  battery, not `smoke`.
- The uncertainty-band draws mean that a card passes only if it is passive across its whole stated band,
  not just at nominal.
