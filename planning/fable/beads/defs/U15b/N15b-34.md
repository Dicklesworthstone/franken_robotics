## Background

The studio checks a design before simulating it. `frob design check <design>` runs eight preflight stages
in order and stops at the first refusal; every refusal carries a stable code and ranked repairs expressed
as typed edits, which become the envelope's next actions. Stage 6, "statics and sizing", is where the
actuator subsystem refuses a design before any simulation is spent on it: support margins (A-08), co-design
closure (A-10: does the mass spiral close?), the torque, speed and voltage envelope at the standing,
crouching, stepping-up and nominal-gait points with thermal steady state under temperature-dependent
resistance (A-11), bus feasibility, and budgets. Its refusals: a static margin not met (124); the mass
spiral does not close (116); an operating point outside the envelope (131); no thermal equilibrium or one
above the limit (132); the bus cannot supply the demand (133); budget exceeded (115); a card field
`assumed` under `--strict-data` (113). Stage 6 had a time target of 600 ms at the 95th percentile; since A-20
to A-24 joined it at M1 its budget is 755 ms unbound and 875 ms bound (A-20 and A-24 bound), of which A-08, A-10 and A-11 keep the
600 ms (the dated amendment of N14bx-01); stages 1 to 6 together complete within 2 s at the 95th percentile
on the archetypes.

This bead is the end-to-end test of that capability for the two analyses the actuator chapter's seed owns
(A-10 and A-11, BD-103), as a user or an agent would drive it: check, read the refusal, apply the first
repair, check again.

## Scope

In: fixtures with one planted defect each; the script; the repair loop; timing capture; mutants; the rows of
GATE-ATEL-02 that stage 6 contributes. Out, with the bead that has each: the analyses (BD-103); the other
stages; the 100-defect battery of the preflight gate (the design chapter's); the static verbs called
directly on the worked cards, with every tabulated closed-form value (N15a-26); the seven refusal codes one
by one (N15b-38).

## Technical approach

**Fixtures** (`synthetic`, `assets/fixtures/sinew/stage6/`), each a small variation of the reference
quadruped unless noted:
- `quad-ok`: passes stages 1 to 6.
- `quad-knee-undersized`: knee card replaced by the next smaller generic class (the planted undersized
  actuator): expect an A-11 finding naming the knees, FROB-REF-131 or -132 according to which limit binds.
- `spiral-open`: the worked mass-spiral model with n = 12 actuators, c = 0.25, torque density 15 N·m/kg,
  leg length 0.55 m: loop gain κ = 12·0.25·9.81·0.55/15 = 1.08, does not close: FROB-REF-116.
- `spiral-closed`: the same at 0.30 m: κ = 0.59; least mass 2.43 times the structure mass (6 kg → 14.6 kg).
- `hold-20a`: one joint sized to hold 20 A on the thermal worked card (R₀ = 0.20 Ω at 25 °C,
  α = 0.00393 K⁻¹, G = 0.80 W/K, limit 155 °C): temperature-dependent equilibrium 190 °C: FROB-REF-132.
- `hold-18a`: the same at 18 A: equilibrium 143.8 °C: passes.
- `stall-at-speed`: asks stall torque at a speed above the card's no-load speed: FROB-REF-131.
- `quad-bus-undersized`: nominal-gait peak above E²/(4·R): FROB-REF-133. Defined by the power-path test
  (N15b-30) and read here by name, never redefined (the fixture registry is keyed by name).
- `quad-assumed-alpha`: `quad-ok` with one card's `alpha_r` marked `assumed`.

**Script `scripts/e2e/sinew_stage6_sizing.sh`** (sources the shared helper of STD-E2E):
1. `frob design check quad-ok --stages 1-6 --json`: exit 0 or 2, no refusal; record per-stage timings.
2. `frob design check quad-knee-undersized --json`: exit 3; the refusal's pointer names a knee joint; apply
   `refusal.repairs[0]` with `frob design edit quad-knee-undersized <edit> --expected-parent <checked DesignId>
   --key e2e-s6-knee --json` (the registered form of `design.edit`, BD-058: without `--key` it is refused with
   FROB-REF-805, exit 3); re-check the returned design: no stage-6 refusal.
3. `frob design check spiral-open --json`: exit 3, FROB-REF-116, payload loop gain within 1% of 1.08.
4. `frob design check spiral-closed --json`: no refusal; `result.closure = "closed"`; least mass
   6/(1 − 0.5886) = 14.5843 kg within 10⁻⁶ relative of the value computed from the fixture's parameters.
5. `frob design check hold-20a --json`: exit 3, FROB-REF-132, payload equilibrium 190 °C (within 0.5%) and
   the largest sustainable current 18.6 A (within 0.5%). `hold-18a`: passes.
6. `frob design check stall-at-speed --json`: exit 3, FROB-REF-131 with the point and the boundary.
7. `frob design check quad-bus-undersized --json`: exit 3, FROB-REF-133 with ceiling and demand; apply the
   first repair in the same form as step 2 (`--key e2e-s6-bus`); re-check passes.
8. `frob design check quad-assumed-alpha --json`: passes with evidence class Estimated and the `alpha_r`
   field named as weakest input; with `--strict-data`: exit 3, FROB-REF-113 naming the field.
9. `frob analyze closure spiral-closed --json` and `frob analyze envelope quad-knee-undersized --json` (the
   per-analysis verbs `analyze.closure` and `analyze.envelope` of VERBS-analyze): same findings as inside the
   check; the envelope verb exits 2 with the A-11 finding where the check exits 3 (a verb never refuses on a
   finding; N28a-05), and `frob analyze closure spiral-open --json` likewise exits 2.
10. Timing: repeat step 1 twenty times with an empty memo graph; assert on the reference host the 95th
    percentile of the A-08, A-10 and A-11 share of stage 6 at most 600 ms, of stage 6 at most 755 ms (no
    budget or task bound; 875 ms when A-20 and A-24 are bound, N14bx-01) and of stages 1 to 6 at most 2 s; a
    miss of a stage budget is logged as a finding even if the total passes.
11. Mutants, each in its own control build of `frob` (debug profile, with only that mutant's feature enabled:
    `mutant-thermal-const-r` of `frob-actuate`, planted by N15a-14; `mutant-closure-fixed-iterations` of
    `frob-analyze`, planted by BD-103; one control per build, never a product build, BD-025), run under
    `e2e_expect_caught`: `mutant-thermal-const-r` must make step 5 admit
    `hold-20a`; `mutant-closure-fixed-iterations` must make step 3 report closed. Each is caught.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_stage6_sizing/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` ("N15b-34"), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block
(with `weakest`), findings and refusal objects, the repair applied and the design identifiers before and
after, measured margins and temperatures with units and thresholds, per-stage timings with their budgets,
first differing value and artifact paths on failure, and a `summary`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The planted undersized actuator is found and its first repair passes when applied.
- [ ] The open spiral is reported infeasible with its loop gain; the closed one reports 14.58 kg.
- [ ] The 20 A thermal design is refused with 132 and the 18 A design passes.
- [ ] The bus refusal's first repair passes when applied.
- [ ] Strict data refuses the `assumed` field by name; without the flag the result is Estimated.
- [ ] Stage-6 timing and the stages 1 to 6 total are recorded against 600 ms (the A-08, A-10, A-11 share),
      755 ms (the stage, unbound) and 2 s.
- [ ] Both mutants are caught.

## Considerations

- Which of 131 or 132 fires first for the undersized knee depends on the card class; the script accepts
  either and asserts the pointer and the repair.
- Timing assertions are tied to the reference host named in the fingerprint; on another host the script
  logs the figures and skips the assertion with a reason.
- The repair loop is the capability agents use; a refusal whose first repair does not pass is a defect in
  the analysis, not in the script.
