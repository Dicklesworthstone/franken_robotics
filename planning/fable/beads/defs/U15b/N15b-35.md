## Background

The actuator physics exists twice: as referee components in double precision on the trusted tier (levels
L1 and L2, the two-RC battery, the shared bus), and as batched kernels (`K-act-l1`, `K-act-l2`,
`K-passive`, `K-act-bus`, BD-151) that run thousands of worlds on the CPU and on two GPU backends. The
kernels differ from the referee by design: stiff electrical states take implicit rational updates
(the current update is i⁺ = (i + (h/L)·(v − k·ω))/(1 + h·R(T)/L)), thermal nodes advance on a slower
rational clock and receive heat accumulated in a compensated sum, the resistance seen by the fast update is
the temperature at the last thermal tick, and the voltage limit is the circle inscribed in the inverter's
hexagon. The claim that ties the two together is a **Fidelity** claim, which says a faster tier agrees with
a slower, more trusted tier, and says nothing about whether the trusted tier is right.

**GATE-SINEW-03** (M2, Fidelity): batched L1 and L2 kernels agree with the referee components within 10⁻⁴
relative on a 10⁴-case battery.

This bead measures it from the user's side and adds the device lanes: the CPU-versus-device check per
kernel and batch-composition invariance.

## Scope

In: the run of the GATE-SINEW-03 battery from the user's side (the fixture `sinew-act-fidelity`, which is
the registered form of the battery defined by N15a-07); the script over `batch-cpu` (`f64`, `f32`) and each
admitted device tier; the per-kernel device check; composition invariance; brownout and exhaustion flags on
batched tiers; the receipt of GATE-SINEW-03. This is the only bead that writes that receipt. Out, with the
bead that has each: the kernels (BD-151); the battery's definition and its measurement on the host
reference (N15a-07); the single-precision energy-balance row of the port gate (the batched energy ledger
bead BD-208 owns it).

## Technical approach

**The battery (defined once, in N15a-07; restated here for the script).** 10⁴ cases drawn by address. A case
is: a card from the
catalog with each field drawn inside its uncertainty band; a level (L1 or L2); an operating point (joint
speed, command, bus state, winding temperature). Compared quantities and their class:
(a) L1: current, torque and electrical power once the delay state has settled;
(b) L2: settled currents, torque, copper loss and bus voltage with inputs held for at least twenty
    electrical time constants; on cards with iron-loss coefficients, the iron loss and its two drag shares
    (the eddy damping and `iron_coulomb`, BD-071 and BD-151); node temperatures at the end; energy drawn
    and heat produced over the case;
(c) band-limited transients whose fastest input time scale is at least 20 steps: RMS error over the episode
    relative to the peak, in the fidelity ledger with their own envelope and not judged at 10⁻⁴.
The relative error of a quantity x is abs(x_batched − x_referee)/max(abs(x_referee), floor), with the floor
per quantity declared in the fixture (so that values near zero do not dominate).

**Fixtures** (`synthetic`, `assets/fixtures/sinew/fidelity/`): `sinew-act-fidelity` (the battery, seed
13); `stiff-winding` (h·R/L = 10, which an explicit update cannot survive). Read by name from the
power-path test N15b-30, never redefined: `bus-48v`, `brownout-trials` and `empty-battery`. Every axis value
the script passes is declared in the manifest of the fixture it is passed to (else `FROB-REF-530`), and the
axes are fixed before the first recorded run (an added value changes the `FixtureId`):
`sinew-act-fidelity` declares `axes.precision = [f64, f32]`, `axes.seed = [13]`, `axes.profile = [lockstep]`
and `axes.variant = [alone-64, cotenants-1024, cotenants-4096-permuted]` (the 64 chosen case addresses run
alone; inside a batch of 1,024 with other co-tenants; inside 4,096 with permuted slots and a different bucket
capacity); `stiff-winding` declares `axes.precision = [f64, f32]`.

**Script `scripts/e2e/sinew_kernels_fidelity.sh`** (sources the shared helper of STD-E2E; seed-explicit; every
run-kind call carries `--key`, distinct for every run that is compared with another; a device tier that has not
passed its admission gates is skipped with a logged reason):
1. `frob spark check --kernel K-act-l1 --backend <gpu-cuda|gpu-metal> --reference batch-cpu --seed 13 --key
   sk-<kernel>-<backend> --json`, and the same for `K-act-l2`, `K-passive`, `K-act-bus`, on each device: assert
   the CPU-versus-device verdict inside the determinism class the manifest claims.
2. `frob task fixture sinew-act-fidelity --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep --precision f64 --seed 13 --key
   fx-f64 --json`: assert the worst relative error over the 10⁴ cases is at most 10⁻⁴; log the worst case (card
   hash, level, operating point, both values) and the error distribution as integer log-bin histograms.
3. The same with `--precision f32 --key fx-f32`, and on each device tier (`--tier cuda`, `--tier metal`, with
   `--precision f32` and its own key).
4. `frob fidelity ledger --class <c> --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --battery sinew-act-fidelity --json` for
   each model class `<c>` the fixture's ledger rows declare, and `--tier-pair gpu-cuda,referee` and
   `gpu-metal,referee` for each admitted device: assert a ledger row per quantity and tier pair, inside its
   envelope; class (c) and transients faster than the step are present as their own rows with their own
   envelopes and are not counted against the 10⁻⁴ tolerance (a class (c) row outside its envelope fails the
   run).
5. `frob task fixture stiff-winding --tier batch-cpu --precision f32 --key fx-stiff --json`: the implicit update
   stays bounded and settles to v/R; with `--control mutant-l2-explicit-euler --key fx-stiff-m` (a build with
   the feature `gauntlet-mutations`) the planted explicit update diverges and is caught.
6. `frob task fixture brownout-trials --tier batch-cpu --key fx-brown --json` (the fixture's 100 seeded draws):
   100 worlds flagged `brownout`, none with a completed step at the requested power; `frob task fixture
   empty-battery --tier batch-cpu --key fx-empty --json`: flagged exhausted, state of charge never clamped. The
   same with `--control mutant-bus-clip-demand` and `--control mutant-battery-clip-soc` (distinct keys): both
   mutants are caught.
7. Composition invariance: `frob task fixture sinew-act-fidelity --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep
   --precision f32 --seed 13 --variant <v> --key fx-<v> --json` for the three variants (and on each device
   tier): the 64 chosen worlds' per-world digests are equal across variants.
8. Determinism lanes: step 2 twice under distinct keys (`fx-f64`, `fx-f64-b`) with equal digests; `frob spark
   check --kernel <K> --backend batch-cpu --workers 1|8|<all> --seed 13 --key sk-<K>-w<n> --json` for the four
   kernels with equal digests across worker counts (class D2); steps 2 and 3 in debug and release builds.
9. `gate-receipt`: `e2e_tier g2_parity GATE-SINEW-03 --mutations` (the gate's Fidelity class, N40-02, and
   KA-296's tier G2 in N15-KA: the battery's 10⁴ cases against the referee components, with its registered
   controls); assert from the receipt (`e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) that its worst error per tier and
   precision equals that of steps 2 and 3 for each tier it ran and that each listed control is `caught`, and
   list the receipt hash in the summary. The script never runs `record_gate` and never writes
   `registries/gates.toml` (STD-E2E's
   `self-recording` rule); GATE-SINEW-03 is recorded from the receipt by a session that did not implement the
   work: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SINEW-03 --receipt <hash>`.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_kernels_fidelity/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript:
`ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N15b-35"), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence`
block (claim Fidelity, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), tier and precision, the worst relative
error with the 10⁻⁴ threshold, the worst case's identifiers, histogram bins, digests, event flags and
counts, first differing value and artifact paths on failure, and a `summary` naming GATE-SINEW-03.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Worst relative error at most 10⁻⁴ over the 10⁴ cases on `batch-cpu` in `f64` and `f32` and on each
  admitted device tier, recorded with the worst case.
- [ ] The device check passes for the four kernels on each admitted device family.
- [ ] Brownout and exhaustion are flags, never clips, on the batched tiers; the two clip mutants are caught.
- [ ] Per-world digests are invariant to co-tenants, slot permutation and bucket capacity.
- [ ] The explicit-update mutant is caught on the stiff fixture.
- [ ] The receipt carries measured values, commit, fingerprint and seed; the report wording is the Fidelity
  form: "tracks the referee within 10⁻⁴ on the actuator battery".

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-296 (batched L1 and L2 kernels against the referee components on a 10⁴-case battery: agreement within
10⁻⁴ relative; tier G2, lockstep). KA-319 on the batched tier (the inscribed-circle envelope never exceeds
the hexagon envelope; conservative by at most 1 − √3/2 = 13.4% of voltage at a vertex): cases where the
referee's hexagon admits more voltage than the circle are classed as expected conservative differences and
reported in their own ledger row, with the sign checked. Negative controls: the three mutants above.
Metamorphic: batch-composition invariance. Determinism lanes as in steps 7 and 8.

## Considerations

- Agreement between tiers cannot show that a card is right: every tier reads the same card. The receipt
  says Fidelity, never accuracy.
- The plan fixes the battery's size and tolerance and not its cases; the case definition above is the
  kernels' design choice, and transients faster than the step are reported, not hidden.
- Near a hexagon vertex the circle limit reduces available voltage by up to 13.4%; those cases would fail a
  naive 10⁻⁴ comparison and are separated by rule, not dropped: their count is in the receipt.
