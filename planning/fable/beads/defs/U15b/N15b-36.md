## Background

Beside the actuator model ladder sit two options that are also part of the model identity. One of them is a
**rotor port for flight**: a lumped element with thrust c_T·ρ·A·(Ω·r)² and a reaction torque about its
axis. It is Moonshot [M], off the critical path, and used only by the aerial-terrestrial hybrid variant of
the archetype catalog. This bead builds the element. It exists only on the "not fired" branch of the kill
decision KILL-SINEW-ROTOR (N15b-23) and after the pilot (N15b-22: hover by hand; falsifier: the energy
ledger does not close for a hovering body). If the gate fires, this bead is closed as withdrawn.

The design follows the chapter's rule that every element is a port component whose energy account closes:
the rotor converts shaft work into work on the body and into energy carried away by the air, and that
second part must be a named port, or the ledger shows a deficit equal to the induced power. Crate
`frob-actuate`.

## Scope

In: the rotor card fields and their admission checks through `actuator.show` and `actuator.check`; the
element with its three ports; thrust and reaction torque; axial-flow induced power; the port account lines;
the referee form; the hover known answers on a referee test bench assembled from the element's API (a
one-degree-of-freedom body and four elements, no design term); the option flag in `ModelId`; the batched
kernel only after the referee passes. Out: the design-side `rotor` component kind and the aerial-hybrid
template (N14bp1-02, which depends on this bead); the hover of a design through `frob sim run` (N15bp2-01,
after both); the hybrid archetype's controller; ground effect, blade flapping, forward-flight inflow,
rotor-rotor interaction; any real-flight claim.

## Technical approach

**Card fields (DESIGN names).** `rotor: { radius, thrust_coefficient c_T, figure_of_merit FM (0 < FM ≤ 1),
rotor_inertia, spin_direction (+1 or −1), max_speed }`, each with provenance and uncertainty; air density
is a scene parameter with a default of 1.225 kg/m³ written out. Admission (`actuator.check`, whose row
already lists the refusals used here; no new option): FM outside (0, 1] makes the profile loss negative, so
the card is not passive and is refused with `FROB-REF-130` (returned: the quadrant and the power created,
P_i·(1 − 1/FM) of negative loss at the card's hover point for FM = 1.2); a field without provenance or
uncertainty is `FROB-REF-103`; `--strict-data` with an `assumed` field is `FROB-REF-113`. `actuator.show`
prints the block with every field's provenance.

**Element.** Disc area A = π·r². State: rotor speed Ω (the driving motor's rotor inertia plus
`rotor_inertia`).
1. Thrust along the rotor axis: T = c_T·ρ·A·(Ω·r)².
2. Induced velocity with axial speed v_c of the hub along the thrust axis (climb positive):
   v_i = −v_c/2 + √(v_c²/4 + T/(2·ρ·A)) (the positive root of v_i·(v_i + v_c) = T/(2·ρ·A)); in hover
   v_i = √(T/(2·ρ·A)).
3. Ideal shaft power P_ideal = T·(v_c + v_i); shaft power P_shaft = T·v_c + T·v_i/FM (profile loss carried
   on the induced part).
4. Reaction torque about the axis on the body: Q = −spin_direction·P_shaft/Ω for Ω above a floor
   Ω_floor = 10⁻³·`max_speed` (DESIGN); below the floor Q is zero and thrust is zero.
5. Ports: shaft (Ω, −Q from the motor's side); mechanical (thrust and reaction torque applied to the body
   at the rotor's mount frame); air (energy exported: T·v_i/FM per unit time).
6. Domain: the model refuses descent faster than half the hover induced velocity (no momentum-theory
   solution there) with FROB-REF-131 (operating point outside the envelope) and marks the world's outcome
   accordingly.

**Port account.** Per step: shaft work in = thrust work on the body (T·v_c·h) + energy exported to the air
+ change of rotor kinetic energy + residual. The exported energy is a line of its own in the account
(`exported_to_air`), listed with heat to ambient as energy that leaves the system. Every term uses the
step-average quantities that advance the state, as the pilot fixed (N15b-22). Tolerances: per step 10⁻⁹
relative on the referee (GATE-SINEW-02); accumulated over a run at most 10⁻⁶ of the shaft energy (KA-334,
as the pilot registered it).

**Referee test bench (DESIGN).** `crates/frob-actuate/tests/support/hover_bench.rs`: a point body of mass m
on one vertical degree of freedom, four rotor elements on the L1 motor component, the pilot's two loops
(altitude k_p = 32 N/m, k_d = 16 N·s/m; rotor speed k_Ω = 200 s⁻¹), stepped by the referee integrator at
h = 2 ms, with a 0.2 m altitude step at t = 2 s. The same bench realizes KA-334's row in
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml` for the G1 known-answer target.

**Hover known answers.** Thrust equals weight; ideal induced power T^(3/2)/√(2·ρ·A). Worked numbers
(illustrative): m = 2.0 kg, four rotors, r = 0.10 m, c_T = 0.012, ρ = 1.225 kg/m³: T = 4.905 N per rotor,
Ω = 1,031 rad/s, v_i = 7.98 m/s, P_i = 39.2 W per rotor and 156.6 W in total.

**Tiers.** Referee first. A batched kernel (`K-act-rotor`, DESIGN name) follows only after the referee
known answers pass; the law is a polynomial and one square root, so it meets the kernel numerics rules.

**Telemetry.** The element's account lines appear in the energy ledger (`telemetry.energy`, through the
per-element account of N15b-05) under the element's name, with `exported_to_air` as its own line; its
channels (thrust, Ω, shaft power, exported power, reaction torque) are written to the `energetics` stream.

**Identity, evidence, authorization.** The option is part of `ModelId`. Every figure is class Estimated
(card values uncalibrated). Outputs carry the three no-authorization flags, all false; nothing here is a
flight-performance claim.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Built only with the pilot record and a "not fired" decision on file; otherwise closed as withdrawn.
- [ ] Hover known answer on the referee bench: a 2.0 kg body with four rotors holds altitude with total
  thrust equal to its weight within 10⁻⁶ relative in the last 2 s of a 10 s run, and the induced and shaft
  power equal T^(3/2)/√(2·ρ·A) and that over FM within 10⁻⁵ relative (KA-334 as registered by N15b-22).
- [ ] The port balance closes for the hovering body, including a commanded 0.2 m climb: per step within 10⁻⁹
  relative and accumulated within 10⁻⁶ of the shaft energy; the ledger without the air line shows a deficit
  equal to the exported energy and is flagged.
- [ ] `frob actuator check` admits the fixture rotor card and refuses the card with FM = 1.2 with
  `FROB-REF-130`, exit 3; `frob actuator show` prints the rotor block with provenance.
- [ ] A counter-rotating pair produces zero net reaction torque; a same-direction pair yaws the body.
- [ ] If the hover answer or the balance fails in production code, KILL-SINEW-ROTOR fires and the element is
  withdrawn (the gate's second reading).
- [ ] With the option off, digests equal those of a build without the feature.
- [ ] If the batched kernel is built: it agrees with the referee element within 10⁻⁴ relative on a battery
  of 10⁴ random operating points (the GATE-SINEW-03 threshold for batched actuator kernels).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-actuate/tests/rotor_port.rs`:
- `hover_thrust_speed`: Ω = 1,031 rad/s gives 4.905 N within 0.1%.
- `induced_velocity_hover`: 7.98 m/s; `induced_power_hover`: 39.2 W per rotor.
- `axial_climb_relation`: at v_c = 2 m/s, v_i·(v_i + v_c) = T/(2·ρ·A) to round-off.
- `shaft_power_with_fm`: FM = 0.7 gives 39.2/0.7 = 55.9 W per rotor in hover.
- `reaction_torque_sign`: spin +1 gives negative Q on the body about the axis; a counter-rotating pair
  sums to zero.
- `same_direction_pair_yaws`: two rotors with spin +1 at hover speed apply −2 × 0.0543 N·m about the
  vertical, and a free body's yaw rate grows with that sign.
- `account_closes_with_air_line`, `account_deficit_without_air_line`.
- `descent_domain_refused`: v_c = −4.5 m/s (below −v_h/2 = −3.99 m/s) → `FROB-REF-131` naming the point and
  the boundary; v_c = −3.5 m/s is admitted.
- `option_off_is_inert`.
- `fm_above_one_not_passive`: a card with FM = 1.2 → `FROB-REF-130` with the negative loss; FM = 1.0 admitted.
- `hover_bench_settles`: the bench (seed 8) gives |thrust/weight − 1| below 10⁻⁶ and power errors below 10⁻⁵
  in the last 2 s, and the accumulated residual below 10⁻⁶ of the shaft energy over the run with the climb.
- `kernel_matches_referee` (only when `K-act-rotor` is built): 10⁴ random (Ω, v_c) points inside the domain,
  thrust, torque and exported power within 10⁻⁴ relative of the referee element.

### End-to-end script and logging
Fixture cards (`synthetic`, `assets/fixtures/sinew/rotor/`): `rotor-r10` (r = 0.10 m, c_T = 0.012, FM =
0.70, J_rotor = 2 × 10⁻⁵ kg·m², max_speed 2,000 rad/s) and `rotor-fm-1p2` (the same with FM = 1.2).
`scripts/e2e/sinew_rotor_port.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N15b-36 milestone=M6 labels=sinew,moonshot
gates=KILL-SINEW-ROTOR seed=8 needs=cpu plants=no-air-port,hover-only-inflow,fm-above-one`). Steps:
`card_show` (`frob actuator show rotor-r10 --json`, asserting the six fields with provenance);
`card_check` (`frob actuator check rotor-r10 --json`, exit 0); `card_refused`
(`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-130 3 -- frob actuator check rotor-fm-1p2 --json`); `hover_ka`
(`FROB_TEST_SEED=8 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- ka_334`, the referee bench:
thrust against weight in the last 2 s at 10⁻⁶ relative, shaft power against 55.9 W per rotor and induced
power against 39.2 W at 10⁻⁵ relative, the accumulated residual against 10⁻⁶ of the shaft energy, zero net
reaction torque, the `exported_to_air` line present); `mutants` (the same target with `--features
gauntlet-mutations`, each mutant required to fail). The hover of a design through `frob sim run` and
`frob telemetry energy` is N15bp2-01's, because the design-side component kind is N14bp1-02's and that bead
waits on this one. It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes
`target/e2e-logs/sinew_rotor_port/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; `metric` lines give each
measured value with its closed-form value, unit and threshold, the residuals, the card digests and the
envelope's evidence block with the three no-authorization flags; negative controls are `assert` lines
with `kind: "negative-control"`; on failure, the first step over tolerance with its four ledger terms and
the bench seed; a `summary` names KILL-SINEW-ROTOR.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-334 (tolerances as N15b-22 registered them: 10⁻⁶ relative on thrust, 10⁻⁵ on power, 10⁻⁶ of the shaft
energy on the accumulated residual). Negative controls, registered in
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` with numbers from the allocation bead N38a-40 and compiled
under `--features gauntlet-mutations`: `mutant-rotor-no-air-port` (deficit equal to the exported energy),
`mutant-rotor-hover-only-inflow` (v_i from the hover formula during a climb: residual about −3.92 J, the
climb work, against a tolerance of about 2 × 10⁻³ J), and the card `rotor-fm-1p2` (refused with 130).
Metamorphic: scaling ρ by s and c_T by 1/s leaves thrust unchanged. Determinism: as the referee.

## Considerations

- The item holds one of at most four active Moonshot slots; if another epic takes the slot, the consumer
  clause of the kill gate fires.
- A thrust-producing actuator near people is a safety topic outside this simulator's authority; the
  no-authorization flags state that in every export.
- The reaction torque floor at low Ω is a numerical guard and is listed among the known gaps.
