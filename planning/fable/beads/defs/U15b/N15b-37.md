## Background

On the batched tiers (`batch-cpu` and the GPU tiers under the `lockstep` profile) joint stiction, backlash
and hard stops are convex rows of one Newton solve per world (BD-155), and rotor coordinates are eliminated
in closed form so that the dense factorization keeps its size (N15b-08). The price of the convex form is
stated and measured: a joint holding a torque τ below its bound τ_b creeps at ε·τ/τ_b, so over a time t it
drifts by at most ε·t; backlash rows are compliant with the card's tooth stiffness; breakaway begins when
the speed leaves the kink of width ε. This bead measures, from the user's side, that the batched tiers
reproduce the six known answers within that stated regularization, and that rotor elimination holds.

**GATE-SINEW-04**, batched rows (M2): the six cases (breakaway within one step of τ_s/r; amplitude loss per
cycle within 2% of 4·τ_c/k; drift at most ε·t; zero output motion inside the gap; loop width within 1% of
β; closure speed and energy loss within 1%), batched tiers within their stated regularization.
**GATE-SINEW-09** (M2): PO-SINEW-02 holds (the rotor-eliminated solve equals the full solve), and with
twelve backlash joints the dense factorization has the size it has with none. The contact chapter's joint-row
known answers of **GATE-TRAC-04** (KA-402 breakaway, KA-403 gap crossing) on the batched tiers are measured by
the Traction script of N18a-36 on N15a-12's fixtures `trac-ramp-breakaway` and `trac-gap-crossing`, not here;
this script neither redefines those fixtures nor writes that receipt.

## Scope

In: the batched section of `scripts/e2e/sinew_joint_rows.sh` (the script and its referee section are
N15a-12); the twelve-backlash quadruped fixture; the rotor-elimination equivalence fixture; the assertion
that the fidelity-ledger rows defined by N15a-11 exist for these fixtures and lie inside their envelopes;
the receipts of the batched row of GATE-SINEW-04 and of GATE-SINEW-09.
Out, with the bead that has each: the kernels (BD-155) and the elimination algebra (N15b-08); the
definition of the ledger quantities and the decision on the gyroscopic hypothesis (N15a-11); the kill
decision (N15b-24), which reads the counters this script also logs; the choice of ε (N15b-26); the batched
joint rows of GATE-TRAC-04 (KA-402, KA-403) and their receipt (N18a-36, on N15a-12's fixtures).

## Technical approach

**Fixtures.** The six registered by N15a-12 (`ramp-breakaway`, `spring-coulomb`, `held-load`,
`reversal-in-gap`, `gap-hysteresis`, `gap-closure`), read by name and never redefined; the axis values passed
below (`axes.profile` `lockstep` and `strict`, `axes.precision` `f64` and `f32`, `axes.seed` 9) are declared
in their manifests by N15a-12 (`axes.seed = ["9"]`, `axes.profile = ["referee", "lockstep", "strict"]`,
`axes.precision = ["f64", "f32"]`); any other value is `FROB-REF-530`. Plus (`synthetic`, registered here):
- `quad-12-backlash`: the reference quadruped (18 velocities) with all twelve joints at card level L3 with
  backlash β = 0.5° (`axes.seed = [9]`, `axes.profile = [lockstep]`, `axes.variant = [alone, cotenants]`, the
  second placing the same world in a batch with other designs and another slot order).
- `rotor-elimination-equivalence`: 10³ rational systems with n ≤ 6 link velocities and m ≤ 6 rotors, and
  10⁴ double-precision systems, generated by seed; plus a whole-step row, the `quad-12-backlash` world stepped
  200 times on the host reference of N15b-08 with the eliminated solve and with the full (n + m = 30) solve
  (`axes.seed = [9]`).

**Script section** (same file, `--tiers batch-cpu,cuda,metal`; a tier whose admission gates have not
passed is skipped with a logged reason; every run-kind call carries `--key`, distinct per tier, precision,
fixture and repetition, since runs are compared):
1. For each tier and for `--precision f64` and `f32` where the tier offers both: run the six fixtures with
   `frob task fixture <name> --tier <tier> --profile lockstep --precision <p> --seed 9 --key
   jr-<name>-<tier>-<p> --json`.
2. Read from each envelope the regularization the tier reports for the row (`stiction_eps`, the creep
   bound ε·t, `tooth_stiffness`, the dissipation time) and assert:
   - breakaway: the speed exceeds ε within one step of τ_s/r = 0.5 s;
   - decay: 0.04 rad per cycle within 2%; rest inside ±(τ_s/k + ε·t_settle);
   - held load: drift at most ε·t (0.02 rad for ε = 10⁻³ rad/s over 20 s), and equal to ε·(τ/τ_b)·t =
     0.012 rad within 5%;
   - reversal inside the gap: zero output motion beyond stiction creep;
   - loop width: 0.5° within 1% after removing the reported tooth deflection;
   - closure: 0.577 rad/s and 0.0115 J within 1%.
3. Mutants (a build with the feature `gauntlet-mutations`): `--control mutant-backlash-deadzone` on `gap-closure` and
   `--control mutant-bound-updated-in-solve` on `spring-coulomb`, on `batch-cpu` with their own keys: each
   fails its assertion of step 2.
4. `frob fidelity ledger --class <c> --tier-pair <tier>,referee --quantity
   joint-hold-drift,stick-slip-step-sensitivity --json` for each model class of the fixtures and each fast tier
   run: assert one row per case and tier pair, each disagreement inside its frozen envelope; print the
   step-dependence row of the stick-slip case.
5. `frob task fixture quad-12-backlash --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep --seed 9 --key jr-quad12 --json`:
   read the shape class and the solve-stage record from the receipt; assert dense factor size 18 (not 30).
6. `frob task fixture rotor-elimination-equivalence --tier host --seed 9 --key jr-elim --json`: assert exact
   equality on the rational cases and relative difference at most 10⁻¹² on the 10⁴ double-precision cases;
   on the whole-step row, Newton iterates of the two forms agree to 10⁻¹⁰ at every step, with the factor size
   (18 and 30) and the wall time per step of both forms logged; with `--control mutant-eliminate-drop-rhs --key
   jr-elim-m` the equivalence fails.
7. Compare the `quad-12-backlash` episode with the referee's full-coupling run and assert, with `frob fidelity
   ledger --class quadruped --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --quantity rotor-gyro-neglect --json`, that the ledger
   row `rotor-gyro-neglect` (the quantity N15a-11 defines; "rotor gyroscopic coupling neglected") exists for it
   with its measured disagreement.
8. `frob spark check --kernel K-joint-rows --backend <gpu-cuda|gpu-metal> --reference batch-cpu --seed 9 --key
   sk-joint-rows-<backend> --json` on each device tier.
9. Determinism lanes: step 5 twice under distinct keys (`jr-quad12`, `jr-quad12-b`); `frob spark check --kernel
   K-joint-rows --backend batch-cpu --workers 1|8 --seed 9 --key sk-joint-rows-w<n> --json` (equal digests across
   worker counts); step 5 with `--variant cotenants --key jr-quad12-c` (the same world inside a batch of other
   designs and slot order); the world's digests are equal within the tier's class.
10. Log Newton iteration counts per fixture (inputs to N15b-24).
11. `gate-receipts`: the gates' rows in the Gauntlet's Known-answer target through the STD-E2E helper, each
    with its registered controls: `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SINEW-04 --mutations` (the batched rows,
    KA-297 to KA-302, G1 in N15-KA) and `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SINEW-09 --mutations` (KA-303, host);
    assert from each receipt (`e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) that its values equal those of steps 2,
    5 and 6 and that each listed control is `caught`, and list both receipt hashes in the summary. The script
    never runs `record_gate` and never writes `registries/gates.toml` (STD-E2E's `self-recording` rule); the
    batched part of GATE-SINEW-04 (its referee part is N15a-12's) and GATE-SINEW-09 are recorded from the
    receipts by a session that did not implement the work: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
    record_gate -- --gate GATE-SINEW-04 --part M2 --receipt <hash>` and `… --gate GATE-SINEW-09 --receipt
    <hash>`.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sinew_joint_rows/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` ("N15b-37"), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, `evidence` block, tier
and precision, measured values with units, the threshold and the regularization bound applied, event and
iteration counts, factor size, digests, first differing value and artifact paths on failure, and a
`summary` naming the two gates, GATE-SINEW-04 and GATE-SINEW-09.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Batched rows of GATE-SINEW-04 pass on `batch-cpu` in `f64` and `f32` and on each admitted device
  tier, with the regularization bound that was applied recorded beside each measured value.
- [ ] GATE-SINEW-09: factor size 18 with twelve backlash joints; equivalence exact on rationals and within
  10⁻¹² in double precision.
- [ ] The six fixtures are N15a-12's, read by name; no fixture of GATE-TRAC-04 is redefined here and no
  GATE-TRAC-04 receipt is written (N18a-36 measures its batched joint rows).
- [ ] Fidelity-ledger rows exist for every case and tier pair, and for the gyroscopic hypothesis.
- [ ] A world's digest is unchanged by co-tenants and worker count.
- [ ] Mutants caught: `mutant-eliminate-drop-rhs` (step 6), `mutant-backlash-deadzone` (closure),
  `mutant-bound-updated-in-solve` (decay or line search).

## Considerations

- If KILL-SINEW-ROWS fires, the stiction rows on throughput tiers are recorded as "fell back" with the
  fidelity-ledger gap, and this script runs the stiction cases under the `strict` profile only; the
  backlash and elimination rows are unaffected.
- The regularization bounds come from the tier, so a new default ε needs no script change.
- Single precision: the closure case compares energies of order 10⁻² J; the 1% tolerance leaves room for
  `f32` round-off, and the script logs the `f32`-to-`f64` gap.
