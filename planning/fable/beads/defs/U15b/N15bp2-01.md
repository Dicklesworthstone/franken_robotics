## Background

The rotor port for flight is a Moonshot [M] actuator option (epic BD-419): a lumped element with thrust
T = c_T·ρ·A·(Ω·r)² (A = π·r² the disc area, ρ the air density, Ω the rotor speed, r the radius, c_T the
thrust coefficient), a reaction torque about its axis, and an air port that carries the induced and profile
power away. The element is built in `frob-actuate` by N15b-36, which proves its known answers on a referee
test bench assembled in code. The design-side component kind `rotor` and the aerial-hybrid variant template
are built by N14bp1-02, which depends on N15b-36. A design that mounts rotors can therefore be simulated
through the `frob` binary only after both exist, and no bead before this one can show that the element is
reachable through `frob sim run --json` and that its account lines reach `frob telemetry energy`, which the
epic requires when the kill gate KILL-SINEW-ROTOR (N15b-23) admits the port.

This bead exists only on the admitting branch: it depends on N15b-36 and N14bp1-02, which depend on the
decision; if the gate fires, it closes as `superseded` with the decision record cited. It was split from
N15b-36 in polishing round 2, because N15b-36's script ran a design with rotors, which needs the component
kind of a bead that waits on N15b-36.

## Scope

In: the fixture design `assets/fixtures/sinew/rotor/hover-1dof.fbot`; the conformance fixture controller
`fixtures/controllers/rotor-altitude-pd.toml`; the script `scripts/e2e/sinew_rotor_hover_sim.sh` and its
assertions; one unit test of the fixture. Out: the element, its card checks and its bench known answers
(N15b-36); the component kind, template and listing (N14bp1-02); the decision (N15b-23); any flight
controller for a product design (none is built; the fixture controller is never a product controller).

## Technical approach

**Fixture design (`synthetic`).** One rigid body of mass m = 2.0 kg on a vertical prismatic joint to the
world (one degree of freedom, as in the pilot N15b-22), four `rotor` components with the card `rotor-r10` of
N15b-36 (r = 0.10 m, c_T = 0.012, FM = 0.70, J_rotor = 2 × 10⁻⁵ kg·m²), handedness alternating (two `cw`,
two `ccw`), each driven by an L1 motor card through its shaft port; air density 1.225 kg/m³ written out in
the scene; g = 9.81 m/s². The file is parsed with the cargo feature `variant-aerial` on (N14bp1-02).

**Fixture controller (DESIGN, the pilot's loops).** Altitude: T_cmd = m·g + k_p·(z_ref − z) − k_d·ż with
k_p = 32 N/m and k_d = 16 N·s/m (ω_n = 4 rad/s, ζ = 1); per rotor Ω_cmd = √(T_cmd/(4·c_T·ρ·A·r²)); rotor
speed loop gain k_Ω = 200 s⁻¹ on the motor's velocity command. Reference: z_ref = 0 until t = 2 s, then
0.2 m. The controller is a conformance fixture (`--controller fixture:rotor-altitude-pd`), the form the
archetype catalog script uses for `fixture:zero-gain`: a registered file under `fixtures/controllers/`,
present in every build (VERBS-sim's `--controller` row), and never a product controller.

**Expected values (hand, from the pilot).** Per rotor at hover: T = 4.905 N, Ω = 1,030.6 rad/s,
v_i = 7.983 m/s, induced power 39.156 W, shaft power 55.937 W, reaction torque 0.05428 N·m; four rotors
223.75 W of shaft power; net reaction torque zero with alternating handedness; climb work m·g·Δh = 3.924 J.

**Procedure (the script).**
1. Build `frob` with `--features variant-aerial`.
2. `frob design new hov --file assets/fixtures/sinew/rotor/hover-1dof.fbot --key rph-1 --json`.
3. `frob sim run hov --tier referee --seconds 10 --seed 8 --controller fixture:rotor-altitude-pd --key rph-2
   --json`.
4. `frob telemetry energy <run> --json`: the rotor elements' lines, `exported_to_air` present, and the
   accumulated residual.
5. `frob telemetry query <run> --stream energetics --from 8s --to 10s --json` (thrust, Ω, shaft and
   exported power, reaction torque per rotor) and `--stream kinematics` over the same window (height and
   vertical speed of the body).
6. A second run with a distinct key (`rph-3`), same seed: digests equal (determinism of the referee).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The fixture design parses and compiles with `variant-aerial` on; a default build refuses it with
  `FROB-REF-103` naming `rotor`.
- [ ] In the last 2 s of the run, total thrust over weight differs from 1 by at most 10⁻⁶; per-rotor shaft
  power is within 10⁻⁵ relative of 55.937 W and induced power within 10⁻⁵ relative of 39.156 W (KA-334 as
  registered by N15b-22); the body's height is within 10⁻⁶ m of 0.2 m.
- [ ] The energy ledger of the run lists `exported_to_air` per rotor and closes: accumulated residual at
  most 10⁻⁶ of the shaft energy over the whole run including the climb.
- [ ] The net reaction torque about the vertical is zero within 10⁻⁹ N·m.
- [ ] Two runs with distinct keys and the same seed give byte-equal state digests.
- [ ] The envelope carries class Estimated and the three no-authorization flags, all false.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/fixtures_sinew_rotor.rs`:
- `hover_1dof_parses_with_feature`: with `variant-aerial`, the fixture canonicalizes, has one prismatic joint
  and four rotors with alternating handedness, total mass 2.0 kg.
- `hover_1dof_refused_without_feature`: without the feature, `FROB-REF-103` naming `rotor`.
- `rotor_altitude_pd_constants`: the controller file's k_p, k_d and k_Ω equal 32, 16 and 200, and its hover
  command gives Ω_cmd = 1,030.6 rad/s within 10⁻⁴ relative.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sinew_rotor_hover_sim.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N15bp2-01 milestone=M6
labels=sinew,moonshot,e2e gates=KILL-SINEW-ROTOR seed=8 needs=cpu plants=no-air-port,hover-only-inflow`)
runs the six steps above. It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes
`target/e2e-logs/sinew_rotor_hover_sim/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time, and the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). `metric` lines give thrust over weight,
per-rotor shaft and induced power, height and net reaction torque, each with its hand value, unit and
threshold; the ledger's lines and residual in joules and as a fraction of shaft energy; the two runs' state
digests. On failure: the first window sample over tolerance, the run identifier, the world address and the
fixture paths. A final `summary` counts assertions and names KILL-SINEW-ROTOR.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-334 through the design path. Negative controls (`e2e_expect_caught`, with the mutants of N15b-36 compiled
under `--features gauntlet-mutations`): `mutant-rotor-no-air-port` must open the ledger by the exported
energy; `mutant-rotor-hover-only-inflow` must leave a residual of about −3.92 J, the climb work. Determinism
lane: twice on one machine (step 6).

## Considerations

- A hover of a legged or wheeled aerial-hybrid template is not attempted: its controller is out of scope of
  the epic, and no flight claim is made.
- The fixture controller is a test artefact; the honest-status table never lists it as a capability.
- If KILL-SINEW-ROTOR's second reading fires later (the known answer fails in production code), this
  script's failure is one of its inputs.
