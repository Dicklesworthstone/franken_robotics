## Background

A kill gate is a decision named in advance, with its trigger, its milestone, the measurement that decides
it, its fallback, and what changes downstream. Each is recorded as an architecture decision record when
decided, by a bead of the unit that owns the subsection (the rule of the fallback ledger N40-23).

The plan's Frontier table names no kill gate for series elasticity, belt and cable compliance (N15b-18), only
the condition that the item is not built until a card with a measured stiffness exists. The gate register
carries one all the same: **KILL-SINEW-SEA**, entered by N40-23 through an `introduce` amendment, so that the
series-elastic pilot (N15b-19) has a stop rule to name and the pilot register (N38a-09) can accept its record.
As registered:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Trigger | The series-elastic pilot's falsifier holds (the stepped two-mass resonance differs by more than 1% from √(k·(J₁ + J₂)/(J₁·J₂)) at the referee step) **and** no remedy inside the referee's design passes |
| Decided at | M6, mode `pilot-before-funding`: due when WP-33 asks to fund N15b-18, and at the latest at the M6 exit |
| Measurement (`reads`) | The pilot record of N15b-19 in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`: per case and step the stepped and hand frequencies, the remedies rerun, the verdict and `limits` |
| Fallback | Rigid transmissions with backlash rows; results flagged compliance not modelled |
| Downstream if it fires | N15b-18 is not built and is closed with reason `fallback`; L3 stays at its Solid part (backlash, BD-084 and BD-155); the status table and reports name the fallback |

Without this bead the row has no decider: at the M6 exit the cross-check N40-30 would find no owner declaration
for KILL-SINEW-SEA (`owner-declaration-missing`), and the capability rider of N00-02 (a Frontier capability needs
a pilot result and a kill-gate decision) could never be met for the compliant level. Tier of the mechanism:
Frontier [F].

## Scope

In: reading the pilot record; evaluating the two clauses of the trigger exactly as registered; the decision
record `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SINEW-SEA-M6.md`; the gate row's `decision` table and the owner declaration; updating
N15b-18 and the status table. Out: running the pilot (N15b-19); building the element (N15b-18); entering the row
and its amendment (N40-23); N15b-18's second precondition, a card with a `measured` or cited `datasheet`
stiffness, which is N15b-18's own acceptance and not part of this trigger.

## Technical approach

**Procedure.**
1. Read the pilot's record (N15b-19): for each registered case (A to E) and step (2 ms, 1/480 s, h/2, 2·h) the
   hand ω, the stepped ω_h and the ratio; the remedies rerun (sub-stepping by 2 and by 4, the exact two-mass
   update, the validity bound ω·h ≤ 0.35 with a refusal above it) with their ratios; the verdict
   (`falsifier-rejected` or `falsifier-holds`), `limits` (the remedy and the stiffness bound per tier), the
   reviewer and the receipt. A missing result is not a pass: step 3 decides it.
2. Evaluate the clauses separately, each from the record alone. Clause 1, the falsifier holds: some case has
   abs(ω_h/ω − 1) > 1% at the referee step under the implicit midpoint rule. Clause 2, no remedy passes: no
   remedy in the record brings every case that failed clause 1 inside 1% in the rerun (the validity bound
   counts as passing only for the cases it admits; those it refuses are named in `limits` as refused on that
   tier).
3. Name the outcome in the four names of the kill-gate record (N40-03): `fired` if both clauses hold;
   `not-fired` if the pilot has a result and either clause fails, the remedy that passed (if any) being the one
   N15b-18 adopts; `not-reached` if WP-33 never asked to fund N15b-18 and the pilot has no result by the M6
   exit (an unfunded mechanism is recorded as unfunded, never as passed and never as killed); `deferred` only
   through an ADR that names the later exit and its reason.
4. Write `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SINEW-SEA-M6.md` (the program's one directory for decision records) with the fields
   N40-03 requires: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered and the `introduce` amendment that
   entered it; each input with its receipt hash beside the clause it decides (the pilot record's identifier and
   verdict, the worst ratio at the referee step against 1%, the remedy's worst ratio against 1%); the outcome
   and which clause decided; the fallback in force and the name the capability carries from now on ("series
   elasticity: rigid transmissions with backlash rows; compliance not modelled"); the beads re-read (N15b-18:
   released with the named remedy, or stands on the fallback); what changes downstream; the recording session,
   which neither ran the pilot nor will implement N15b-18.
5. Registry: write the `decision` table (`outcome`, `adr`, `date`, `session`, and `receipts`, the hashes of the
   receipts the inputs cite, at least one for `not-fired`; N40-03's form) of the KILL-SINEW-SEA row in
   `registries/gates.toml`, and the gate's owner declaration that N40-30 compares with the ledger (`[[gate]]`
   with `role = "owner"`, the ledger's current `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied, not
   restated, and `decision` with each measured value citing its receipt) in `frob-actuate`'s `contract.toml`.
6. If `fired`: close N15b-18 with reason `fallback`, citing the ADR; set the status table row to "series
   elasticity: fallback (KILL-SINEW-SEA)". If `not-reached`: N15b-18 is not started and closes with the ADR as
   its reason, and the status table records the mechanism as unfunded, not as killed (N40-03: the capability
   does not exist and is not described); compliant drives are modelled as before, rigid with backlash rows. If
   `not-fired`: N15b-18 is released, with the remedy the ADR names as its referee form; it is still built only
   when its measured-stiffness card exists.
7. A later reading never edits the first: if N15b-18, once built, fails its two-mass known answer (KA-335) on
   the referee with the adopted remedy, the gate is read again and a second `decision` entry with its own ADR is
   appended (N40-03's array); the latest entry is in force.

**Fail-closed default until decided.** N15b-18 is not funded: the pilot register funds it only through N15b-19's
record (`gated_beads = ["N15b-18"]`), and no code for the compliant element exists. A compliant drive is modelled
as a rigid transmission with backlash rows.

**Who decides.** The Sinew lead prepares the record; a fresh-context session that did not run the pilot reviews
and records it, as exit reviews are.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_gates.rs`:
- `kill_sinew_sea_record_complete`: trigger, inputs, outcome, date and reviewer present; the ADR's path is
  `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SINEW-SEA-M6.md`.
- `kill_sinew_sea_rule`: the pure rule on synthetic records: every case inside 1% at 2 ms → `not-fired`, no
  remedy; case D at 0.9725 at 2 ms with sub-stepping by 2 at 0.9929 → `not-fired`, remedy "sub-stepping by 2"
  (clause 2 fails); case C at 0.9037 at 2 ms with the only remedy rerun, sub-stepping by 2, at 0.9725 → `fired`
  (both clauses hold); no pilot result at the M6 exit → `not-reached`; every returned outcome is one of N40-03's
  four.
- `kill_sinew_sea_clauses_are_a_conjunction`: a record whose falsifier holds and whose exact-update remedy brings
  every case inside 1% is `not-fired`; the same record with the remedy's ratios removed is `fired`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_sinew_sea.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N15bp4-01 milestone=M6 labels=sinew,kill-gate
gates=KILL-SINEW-SEA seed=0 needs=cpu plants=not-fired-with-falsified-pilot`) re-derives the outcome from the
stored pilot record (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`, the identifier the row's `reads` names),
compares it with the ADR and the gate row, and runs `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger`
(asserting that no failure names KILL-SINEW-SEA) and `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_gates --
kill_sinew_sea`. It exits non-zero on disagreement. It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes
`target/e2e-logs/kill_sinew_sea/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; it logs the pilot record's digest, per case the
worst ratio at the referee step and under each remedy against the 1% threshold, the value of each clause, the
derived and recorded outcomes, and a `summary` with assertions passed and failed naming KILL-SINEW-SEA. It writes
nothing to the registry; the recording session does.

### Known answers and negative controls
The trigger's clauses are the known answers of the rule: falsifier holds and no remedy passes → `fired`;
falsifier holds and a remedy passes → `not-fired` with that remedy; falsifier does not hold → `not-fired`; pilot
never run by the M6 exit → `not-reached`. The ratios in the rule test are N15b-19's midpoint map
(2/h)·atan(ω·h/2)/ω for its cases C and D (589.0 and 294.5 rad/s) at h = 2 ms and 1 ms. Negative control (plant
`not-fired-with-falsified-pilot`): a record that says `not-fired` while the stored pilot shows both clauses
holding must make the script fail, and `kill_ledger` must report `outcome-contradicts-measurement`.

## Considerations

- The plan's own condition, a card with a measured stiffness, can keep N15b-18 unbuilt after a `not-fired`
  decision; the record must keep the two apart, so that a later card reopens the item with the pilot's result
  still valid.
- The validity-bound remedy passes by refusing stiff cards on a tier; the ADR names the stiffness bound per tier
  so that the refusal (FROB-REF-136 on a batched tier, N15b-18) is not mistaken for coverage.
- A fired gate is not a failure of the program: rigid transmissions with backlash rows answer every question
  about stiff quasi-direct drives, where the benefit of compliance may be nil.
