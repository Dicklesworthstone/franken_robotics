## Background

Before a simulator's contact behaviour can be trusted, its contact-free dynamics must reproduce answers that are
known in closed form. GATE-KIN-01 (claim: Known answer; earliest milestone M1; TARGET thresholds) is that
statement for Kinesis: pendulum period within 10⁻⁶ relative at h = 1 ms; symmetric-top precession within 10⁻⁴;
free-flight momentum conserved to 10⁻¹² relative over 10⁶ steps on the variational lane; and the ballistic
invariants of the construction "dynamics is the Euler–Poincaré equation on a tree" (C-04). This bead builds the
battery, puts every truth on file with its derivation, and records the gate.

The underlying mathematics, so that the bead stands alone. With body velocity ξ of the base and joint rates ṡ,
the body momentum is μ = 𝕀(s)·(ξ + 𝔸(s)·ṡ), where 𝕀(s) is the locked (composite rigid-body) inertia and 𝔸 the
mechanical connection; the base obeys μ̇ = ad*_ξ μ plus applied wrenches, with ad*_ξ = ad_ξᵀ (no minus sign). In
generalized coordinates M(q)·v̇ + h(q, v) = B(q)·u + J(q)ᵀ·f + f_ext. Centroidal momentum is the momentum map,
so in ballistic flight the centre of mass follows a parabola and angular momentum about it is constant. Those
are exact tests of the whole recursion, independent of any oracle.

Tier: Solid. Sites: the referee (FrankenSim's articulated dynamics through the airlock) and, for the free-flight
row, the free-body variational step used as an oracle (BD-087).

## Scope

In: the fixtures, their derivation files, the test binary in `frob-conformance`, registration of each row's
tolerance and negative control, and the gate record. Out: the free-body variational step itself (BD-087); the
articulated variational integrator's long-horizon rows (BD-384); contact rows (the contact battery, BD-085);
parity against an external engine (BD-089).

## Technical approach

Each row is a fixture under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/kin/`, a derivation file
`docs/derivations/<fixture>.md` (model, derivation, numeric value with its digits, error budget: truncation
order, round-off bound, tier envelope), and a test in `crates/frob-conformance/tests/kin_known_answers.rs`. The
suite's rule is that a printed value is carried as printed; where the plan names a closed form without writing
it, the derivation file must state it before the row can support the gate. The forms below are the standard ones
and are what the files state.

1. **Pendulum period (KA-356).** A rigid link on one revolute joint with a horizontal axis: mass m, distance ℓ
   from pivot to centre of mass, inertia I_p about the pivot (I_p = I_com + m·ℓ²). Released at rest at
   amplitude θ₀. ω₀ = √(m·g·ℓ/I_p); k = sin(θ₀/2); the period is T = (4/ω₀)·K(k), with K the complete elliptic
   integral of the first kind, computed as K(k) = π/(2·AGM(1, √(1 − k²))), AGM being the arithmetic–geometric
   mean. Fixture (DESIGN): a solid sphere of mass 1 kg and radius 0.05 m at ℓ = 1 m, so I_p = 1.001 kg·m² and
   ω₀ = √(9.81/1.001) rad/s; θ₀ = 60°, k = ½, K(½) = 1.6857503548…; T ≈ 2.1540 s (the derivation file carries
   15 digits). Measurement: simulate 20 periods at h = 1 ms on the referee; locate downward zero crossings of
   the angle by the transaction's event location (or by cubic interpolation where no event machinery is
   active); the period is the mean spacing. Pass: |T_sim − T|/T ≤ 10⁻⁶.
2. **Symmetric-top precession (KA-357).** A heavy symmetric top with a fixed point: mass m, distance ℓ from the
   pivot to the centre of mass, axial inertia I₃, transverse inertia I₁ about the pivot, total spin ω₃ about the
   symmetry axis, nutation angle θ from the vertical. Steady precession at rate Ω satisfies
   I₁·Ω²·cos θ − I₃·ω₃·Ω + m·g·ℓ = 0; at θ = 90° this is exactly Ω = m·g·ℓ/(I₃·ω₃). Fixture (DESIGN): m = 1 kg,
   ℓ = 0.1 m, I₃ = 2.0 × 10⁻³ kg·m², I₁ = 1.1 × 10⁻² kg·m², ω₃ = 100 rad/s, θ = 90°, started on the steady
   solution: Ω = 0.981/0.2 = 4.905 rad/s, precession period 1.2810 s. The pivot is a spherical joint, lowered
   on the referee to three one-axis joints. Measurement: azimuth of the symmetry axis over 5 precession periods,
   least-squares slope. Pass: |Ω_sim − Ω|/Ω ≤ 10⁻⁴.
3. **Free-flight momentum on the variational lane (KA-355, KA-173).** A free asymmetric rigid body (principal
   inertias 1, 2 and 3 kg·m², mass 1 kg, DESIGN) with a general initial twist and no potential, advanced by
   the free-body variational step (half kick, implicit Legendre solve, drift with the Cayley retraction and
   transport of momentum by the wrench pull-back, half kick). Pass: spatial momentum Ad_g⁻ᵀ·μ conserved to
   10⁻¹² relative over 10⁶ steps; energy error bounded and divided by a factor between 3.6 and 4.4 when h is
   halved (order 2); the map run backwards returns the initial state to round-off.
4. **Ballistic invariants (KA-358, KA-047).** The reference quadruped thrown into free flight with scripted
   joint motion and no contact. Asserted: (a) the centre of mass follows the discrete parabola of the
   integrator (constant second difference −g·h² along z, zero along x and y); (b) linear momentum changes by
   exactly m·g·t along −z; (c) angular momentum about the centre of mass is constant; (d) the diagnostic
   "change of angular momentum about one fixed inertial origin minus the external angular impulse about that
   origin" is zero. The suite prints the tolerance of this row as `to set`; the derivation file sets it from
   the integrator's order (DESIGN proposal: 10⁻¹² relative for (a) and (b), which are exact in exact
   arithmetic; the O(h²) bound of the referee's stepper for (c) and (d), stated with its constant).
5. **Structure identities (KA-048).** On 10⁴ random states of each first archetype: kinetic energy equals
   ½·vᵀ·M·v (computed link by link against the mass-matrix form); Ṁ − 2C is skew-symmetric (tested as
   xᵀ(Ṁ − 2C)x = 0 for random x, with Ṁ by exact differentiation of the kernel program or by a central
   difference with a stated bound). Tier G0 on the host.
6. **Planar 2R chain (KA-049, KA-050).** Link lengths 0.4 m and 0.3 m, joint angles 0 and π/2, axes along +z:
   tool point (0.4, 0.3) m; translational Jacobian J = [[−0.3, −0.3], [0.4, 0.0]] m/rad; a tool force (10, 0) N
   gives both joint torques −3 N·m; joint rates (0.2, −0.1) rad/s give tool velocity (−0.03, 0.08) m/s; both
   power computations give −0.3 W. For a space screw column (ωᵢ, vᵢ) the point-velocity column at p is
   vᵢ + ωᵢ × p. Exact in rational arithmetic on the host; reproduced on the referee.
7. **Registration.** Every row gets its record in the known-answer registry (number, case, truth, tolerance,
   tier and site, control) and each control a record in the negative-control registry attached to GATE-KIN-01.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: GATE-KIN-01 is recorded with measured values: pendulum relative error (≤ 10⁻⁶ at
      h = 1 ms), precession relative error (≤ 10⁻⁴), free-flight momentum drift (≤ 10⁻¹² relative over 10⁶
      steps), and the ballistic invariants inside their filed tolerances.
- [ ] Every row has a derivation file with value, digits and error budget; no row is left `to set` or without a
      control (the suite's rule: such a row cannot support a gate).
- [ ] Each negative control below fails its row when planted.
- [ ] The battery runs under `cargo test -p frob-conformance kin_known_answers` and is wired into the
      `fixtures` battery of the conformance crate.
- [ ] A live-dependency proof per row: changing m, ℓ or g in the fixture changes the measured value as the
      closed form predicts (period scales as √(I_p/(m·g·ℓ)); Ω scales as 1/ω₃).
- [ ] The oracle files were never regenerated from the candidate: truths come from derivations and from the
      free-body oracle only.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `ka::pendulum_period_60deg`: T from the AGM formula against simulation; threshold 10⁻⁶ relative. Also a
  small-angle cross-check at θ₀ = 1° against 2π/ω₀·(1 + θ₀²/16).
- `ka::top_precession_horizontal`: Ω = 4.905 rad/s; threshold 10⁻⁴ relative.
- `ka::free_flight_momentum_1e6_steps`: drift of the six momentum components; threshold 10⁻¹² relative.
- `ka::free_body_order_two`: energy-error ratios at h, h/2, h/4 lie in [3.6, 4.4].
- `ka::free_body_reversal`: 10³ steps forward and back return the state to within 10⁻¹² relative.
- `ka::ballistic_com_parabola`, `ka::ballistic_angular_momentum`, `ka::ballistic_origin_diagnostic`.
- `ka::kinetic_energy_identity`, `ka::mdot_minus_2c_skew` (10⁴ states each).
- `ka::planar_2r_exact`: the rational values above, exact.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_known_answers.sh`: runs each fixture through the binary, for example
`frob sim run crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/kin/pendulum-60.fbot --tier referee --profile referee --step 1ms
--seconds 45 --seed 1 --json`, then `frob telemetry query <run> --fields q,qdot,momentum,com --json`, computes
the measured quantity with the shared helper, and asserts it against the truth read from the derivation file's
machine-readable header. Log: one `metric` line per row with `measured`, `truth`, `unit`, `rel_error`,
`threshold`, the `evidence` block (claim "Known answer", tier `referee`, identities, seed) and the fixture
digest; one `assert` line per control with `control_caught`; a `summary` with `gates: ["GATE-KIN-01"]`.
Logs go to `target/e2e-logs/kin_known_answers/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it.

| KA-357 top | Ω = m·g·ℓ/(I₃·ω₃) at θ = 90° | 10⁻⁴ | The coadjoint taken with the wrong sign (ξ ↦ −ad_ξᵀ in the Euler–Poincaré equation), which reverses gyroscopic terms |
| KA-355 / KA-173 free body | Order 2; reversal to round-off; momentum map conserved | 10⁻¹² relative over 10⁶ steps; Legendre solve to 10⁻¹³ relative | An additive quaternion update; and the Legendre solve with the differential at +h·ξ, which keeps momentum exact and destroys the energy bound (so it is caught by energy and reversal, never by momentum) |
| KA-358 / KA-047 ballistic | Parabola; constant angular momentum about the centre of mass; zero origin diagnostic | Filed with the derivation | A wrench moved between frames with the twist map (the moment arm is lost) |
| KA-048 identities | T = ½·vᵀMv; Ṁ − 2C skew | Round-off bound filed | A Coriolis term dropped |
| KA-049 planar 2R | The values in item 6 | Exact | The lower three rows of the space Jacobian taken as the point Jacobian |

Metamorphic relations: halving the time step moves each result by the integrator's order; re-expressing every
quantity in another body frame leaves powers and energies unchanged; a horizontal translation and yaw of the
world frame changes nothing. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; one platform for the
referee.

## Considerations

- The plan names the pendulum and top closed forms without writing them; the forms above are standard and must
  be confirmed in the derivation files, which are the authority once on file.
- Whether 10⁻⁶ on the period is reachable at h = 1 ms depends on the referee stepper's order: a second-order
  scheme has a relative period error of about (ω₀·h)²/24 ≈ 4 × 10⁻⁷ at ω₀ ≈ 3.13 rad/s, inside the threshold
  with little room. If the measured error exceeds it, the finding is recorded and the gate is not relaxed
  without a dated amendment to the register.
- The top spins at ω₃·h = 0.1 per step, inside the Cayley bound of 0.2; the referee may use the exponential.
- The free-flight row runs on the free-body oracle at M1; the same rows on an articulated tree belong to the
  Frontier variational lane and stay open until that lane's gate.
