## Background

The default integrator (semi-implicit Euler with a group update) is right for throughput and makes no claim
about energy over long horizons. Some questions need more: cost of transport over minutes of simulated time,
passive-dynamics walkers, long ballistic or coasting phases. For those the plan offers a structure-preserving
lane (Frontier tier): a linear-time variational integrator for articulated bodies in reduced coordinates, on the
`referee` and on `batch-cpu` under the `strict` profile.

What a variational integrator is, in one paragraph. Instead of discretizing the equations of motion, discretize
the action: choose a discrete Lagrangian L_d(q_k, q_k₊₁) approximating the integral of the Lagrangian over one
step, and take the stationarity conditions of the sum (the discrete Euler–Lagrange equations) as the update.
Symmetries of L_d give exactly conserved discrete momenta (discrete Noether), and the map is time-reversible
when L_d is symmetric. For one rigid body the result is the four-step free-body map that already serves as an
oracle (BD-087): half kick; implicit solve of the discrete Legendre relation (Dcay_{−h·ξ})⁻ᵀ·𝕄·ξ = μ⁺; drift
g ← g·cay(h·ξ) with the momentum carried by the wrench pull-back (Ad_c)ᵀ; half kick.

What is and is not claimed (DESIGN, and the wording every output must keep). Claimed for the free body: second
order in h, time-reversible to round-off, exact conservation of the discrete momentum map in the absence of
potential and input. Not claimed: any symplectic or energy-drift property for an articulated tree with a
configuration-dependent mass matrix (an explicit kick–drift splitting is not the variational step there), or for
any trajectory with dissipative contact impulses, where the property has no meaning. The articulated lane stays
Frontier until the long-horizon rows of GATE-KIN-01 pass on a tree. A conservation claim always names the
quantity, the symmetry, the boundary conditions, the integrator and the tolerance.

Ceiling, stated before any test: none on throughput; the lane exists for trust in cost of transport over
minutes of simulated time. Kill gate KILL-VARIATIONAL (decided at M5): the implicit solve costs more than twice
a semi-implicit step on `batch-cpu`, or the articulated form fails its momentum row after one repair cycle;
fallback: the free-body step is kept as a known-answer oracle and every rollout uses the default integrator.
This bead is not ready until its pilot (N16a-21) has a recorded result whose falsifier does not hold.

## Scope

In: the articulated discrete Lagrangian and its discrete Euler–Lagrange update in reduced coordinates; the
linear-time residual recursion and the root solve; its use as the smooth-advance of the transactional step on
`strict` and on the referee; the momentum and energy rows on a tree; the cost measurement against the factor-two
limit. Out: the free-body step (BD-087); the kill decision record (N16a-22); any use on the lockstep or GPU
tiers (not offered: the solve is implicit); the nonholonomic rolling rows of the same construction (wheels).

## Technical approach

Symbols: q joint configuration (base pose in SE(3), joints as group coordinates); gᵢ(q) the pose of link i; 𝕄ᵢ
its spatial inertia; Sᵢ its screw basis; V the potential (gravity, springs); τ applied generalized forces; h the
step; cay the Cayley map; (Dcay_x)⁻¹ the inverse left-trivialized differential.

1. **Discrete Lagrangian (trapezoidal, per link).** For link i define its discrete body velocity over a step,
   ξᵢ,ₖ = (1/h)·cay⁻¹(gᵢ(q_k)⁻¹·gᵢ(q_k₊₁)). Then
   L_d(q_k, q_k₊₁) = (h/2)·Σᵢ ξᵢ,ₖᵀ·𝕄ᵢ·ξᵢ,ₖ − (h/2)·(V(q_k) + V(q_k₊₁)).
   For one free body this is exactly the oracle's L_d. It depends on poses only through gᵢ,ₖ⁻¹·gᵢ,ₖ₊₁, so it is
   invariant under a common rigid motion of the whole tree: the total spatial momentum of a free-floating tree
   is conserved exactly. It is symmetric under exchange of endpoints with h → −h, so the map is reversible and
   of second order.
2. **Update in position–momentum form.** State (q_k, p_k), with p the discrete generalized momentum.
   - Solve for q_k₊₁: p_k + D₁L_d(q_k, q_k₊₁) + (h/2)·τ_k = 0 (n equations, implicit).
   - Then p_k₊₁ = D₂L_d(q_k, q_k₊₁) + (h/2)·τ_k₊₁.
3. **Linear-time residual.** One evaluation of the residual of the implicit equation costs O(n):
   - forward pass (root to leaves): gᵢ(q_k₊₁) from the trial q_k₊₁; ξᵢ,ₖ; the link's discrete momentum
     μᵢ = (Dcay_{−h·ξᵢ,ₖ})⁻ᵀ·𝕄ᵢ·ξᵢ,ₖ (the same 6 × 6 relation as the free body);
   - backward pass (leaves to root): accumulate each link's momentum into its parent with the wrench pull-back
     of the relative transform at step k, then project on the joint's screw basis: rᵢ = Sᵢᵀ·(accumulated
     momentum at i) − (p_k)ᵢ − (h/2)·(τ_k − ∂V/∂q)ᵢ.
4. **Root solve.** Newton-type iteration on q_k₊₁ with the step applied by the retraction. Initial guess: the
   default integrator's prediction. Jacobian: (a) on the referee, the exact Jacobian by forward-mode
   differentiation of the recursion (dense, n ≤ 60); (b) the quasi-Newton variant that uses the mass matrix
   M(q_k) (or its articulated-body recursion, which keeps each iteration O(n)) as the approximate Jacobian.
   Stop at relative residual 10⁻¹³ (TARGET three to five iterations); a failure to converge in the iteration
   cap is a failed check of the transaction (which retries with a smaller sub-interval), never a silently
   accepted step.
5. **Inside the transactional step.** The integrator replaces the smooth advance of a sub-interval (step T4.5)
   when the plan's `integrator` is `variational`; events, island impulses and checks are unchanged. An impulse
   at an event is applied to the discrete momentum p. The restore set gains p_k (registered as a `Restorable`).
   `integrator` is a field of `StepPlanId`.
6. **Referee lane.** On the `referee` profile the same code runs over the referee's model views through the
   airlock; where FrankenSim's own discrete Euler–Poincaré step is admitted at the M5 pin it is used for the
   per-body relation and cross-checked against this crate's implementation (two implementations of one
   specification, as for the contact law).
7. **Selection and labels.** `sim.run --profile strict --integrator variational` (and `--profile referee`); the
   option `--integrator default|variational` (default `default`) is this bead's addition to the `sim.run` row of
   VERBS-sim, listed there with this bead as owner, and is refused as not in the build (FROB-REF-724, exit 5) while the feature
   `frontier-variational` is off or KILL-VARIATIONAL has fired. A request on a GPU tier is refused with the profile
   refusal FROB-REF-222 (`strict` and `referee` are not available on `gpu-cuda` or `gpu-metal`; repair: run on
   `batch-cpu`), before any buffer is allocated; with `--profile lockstep` it is refused with FROB-REF-802, exit 3
   (DESIGN: the plan gives no code; the value `variational` is outside the option's domain unless the profile is
   transactional, because the implicit solve needs the transaction's check-and-retry). Results print the integrator, the
   Newton iteration statistics, and the exact wording of what is claimed; with any dissipative contact impulse
   in the run the energy line is printed as "not applicable: dissipative impulses".
8. **Cost measurement.** On `batch-cpu` under `strict`, for the reference quadruped and humanoid in
   contact-free motion (flight and passive swing): time per simulated second of the variational integrator at
   step h_v against the default integrator at the step h_s that matches its energy error over 60 s (matched
   accuracy), and the per-step ratio at equal h. Both are paired lower and upper bounds. The limit is two.
9. **Feature and crate.** `frob-dyn/src/integrate/variational.rs`, behind `frontier-variational` (off by
   default). The derivative of the Cayley map and its transpose are derived kernels with their polynomial
   identity certificates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition (a): the momentum and energy known answers pass: on a free-floating tree with no
      potential and no input, total spatial momentum conserved to 10⁻¹² relative over 10⁶ steps; order 2 by
      step halving (energy error falling by a factor between 3.6 and 4.4 per halving); reversal to round-off;
      with one link the integrator reproduces the free-body oracle bit for bit.
- [ ] Seed done-condition (b): cost at matched accuracy is recorded against the factor-two limit, with the
      per-step ratio at equal h, for both reference robots.
- [ ] Seed done-condition (c): the inputs KILL-VARIATIONAL is decided on are on record as hash-named receipts
      (the performance harness's for the cost ratios; the Gauntlet's for the momentum row, whose gate part is
      recorded from it with `record_gate` by a session that did not implement the integrator; this bead writes
      no gate row): the paired per-step cost ratio of the implicit solve against the default integrator's advance
      inside the same `strict` transaction (clause A reads the lower bound of this interval against 2), the
      matched-accuracy ratio beside it, and the articulated momentum row with the number of repair cycles used
      (clause B: still failing after one repair cycle). The decision and its ADR are N16a-22, which depends on
      this bead; if it fires, this bead's lane is disabled as N16a-22's killed branch states and the bead keeps
      its measurements as the record.
- [ ] With gravity, vertical angular momentum and horizontal linear momentum of a free-floating tree are
      constant to round-off.
- [ ] The additive-quaternion mutant and the wrong-sign-differential mutant fail their rows.
- [ ] A non-converged implicit solve never commits a step.
- [ ] The integrator is refused on `lockstep` and on GPU tiers; `StepPlanId` differs from the default
      integrator's.
- [ ] Outputs carry the claim wording above and never the word "symplectic" for a tree.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `var::one_link_equals_free_body`: bit equality with BD-087's map on 10⁴ random states.
- `var::tree_momentum_1e6`: the reference quadruped in free flight with zero joint torque; six momentum
  components; threshold 10⁻¹² relative.
- `var::tree_order_two`: energy-error ratios at h = 2, 1, 0.5, 0.25 ms in [3.6, 4.4].
- `var::tree_reversal`: 10³ steps forward, then the reversed map, returns (q, p) to 10⁻¹² relative.
- `var::gravity_symmetries`: vertical angular and horizontal linear momentum under gravity.
- `var::double_pendulum_energy_bounded`: a planar double pendulum over 600 s at h = 2 ms: energy error stays
  inside a band with no secular trend (slope test), while the default integrator's drifts. This tests a
  HYPOTHESIS about the implicit tree map and feeds the long-horizon rows; it is never printed as a claim, since
  the lane claims no energy-drift property on a tree until those rows pass.
- `var::residual_linear_time`: operation count of one residual evaluation grows linearly in a serial chain of
  8, 16, 32 and 64 links.
- `var::newton_converges`: iterations ≤ 5 at 10⁻¹³ on 10⁴ states of each reference robot at h = 2 ms.
- `var::nonconvergence_fails_check`: an iteration cap of 1 makes the transaction retry, not commit.
- `var::restore_set_has_momentum`: a forced retry with p_k omitted from the restore set changes the digest.
- `var::dcay_identity`: the stated (Dcay_x)⁻¹ times a finite-difference differential is the identity; the
  rotation block inverts to 2/(4 + ‖ω‖²)·(2·I − ω̂).

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-355: free-body variational step: order 2 by step halving (ratio between 3.6 and 4.4); reversal to
  round-off; momentum map conserved without potential; free-flight momentum to 10⁻¹² relative over 10⁶ steps;
  Legendre solve to 10⁻¹³ relative. Control: an additive quaternion update.
- KA-173 (construction row): evaluating the Legendre relation with the differential at +h·ξ leaves momentum
  exact, because the drift conserves it for any ξ, and destroys the energy bound: detection is by energy and
  reversibility, never by momentum. This is why the momentum row alone cannot pass the gate.
- KA-054: free flight under the structure-preserving step: momentum conserved, energy error bounded, measured
  order two.
- Metamorphic: halving the time step moves results by the integrator's order (two); a common rigid motion of
  the initial state moves the trajectory rigidly and changes no invariant.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; `strict` across worker counts; the referee lane
  on one platform.

## Considerations

- The implicit solve is the cost and the risk: near a kinematic singularity or with a very stiff spring the
  Newton iteration can need a smaller step; the transaction's retry ladder handles that as a numerical matter.
- Contact ends energy conservation by design; the lane is for flight, swing and passive phases, and for
  evaluating the default integrator against it.
- If the kill gate fires, nothing on the critical path is lost: every rollout already uses the default
  integrator, and the free-body step stays as a known-answer oracle.
- Compute is small (CPU only); the 10⁶-step rows run in the gate lane.
