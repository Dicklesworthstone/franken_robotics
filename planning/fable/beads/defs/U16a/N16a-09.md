## Background

The transactional step has three numerical limits that are DESIGN defaults, not measurements:

- L_max = 16: the deepest dyadic level at which a base interval may be cut. At a 2 ms base interval it resolves
  31 ns, so an approach at 3 m/s is located to within 0.1 µm.
- E_max = 64: the most events allowed inside one base interval; the guard against accumulation points of
  impacts.
- N_retry = 3: the number of retries after the declared plan (four numerical plans in all).

Exceeding L_max or E_max ends the transaction with `FROB-REF-220`; exhausting the retries ends it with
`FROB-REF-221`; in both cases the world is `Faulted` with cause `numerical-fault`. The three constants are open
question OPEN-KIN-5 (owner: Kinesis lead). The rule for open questions applies: the default removes a capability
(here: it refuses) and never adds a claim; the question is decided by evidence where evidence exists; at its
deadline an undecided question takes its default, recorded as an ADR.

Why it needs a decision bead: the constants trade refusals against cost. Too small, and the referee refuses
scenes it should complete, which lowers the referee's coverage (the figure of GATE-FID-03) and can leave an M1
fixture without a completed run. Too large, and a transaction near a genuine accumulation point burns time
before refusing. The milestone's stop decision names this explicitly: re-plan the referee if its refusal rate on
the M1 fixtures leaves the door-sill fixture without a completed run.

There is a second reason the owner must look at L_max: the gate row "impact time within 10⁻⁹ s" is finer than
the 31 ns cell that L_max = 16 gives at 2 ms. The step reports a refined event time inside the final cell
(BD-082), which satisfies the row; the alternative is L_max = 21 (cell 0.95 ns at 2 ms), which costs five more
bisection levels per event.

## Scope

In: the measurement protocol, the decision rule, the ADR, and the change of defaults (made once) if the evidence
asks for one. Out: implementing the limits (N16a-06), event location (BD-082) and the ladder (BD-083); the
referee's capacity refusals (`FROB-REF-721`), which no constant here affects; the same question on the `strict`
profile's own kernels at M2, which re-reads this record against the standard battery once that battery is
frozen. This is the one decision bead of OPEN-KIN-5 (a second draft, N16c-10, is superseded by this one).

## Technical approach

1. **Protocol, registered before the first run** (a record in the pilot and experiment registry of the
   conformance crate, with the constants, the fixture list and the decision rule below).
2. **Fixtures (the M1 set; the standard battery does not exist until M2):** the dynamics known-answer battery
   (pendulum, top, ballistic flight); the transactional rows (ballistic drop, tied sliders); the contact
   known-answer battery on the referee; the thin-barrier fixture (10⁶ randomized crossings); the sill fixture
   `fixture:wheel-over-lip` (BD-086: a torque-driven wheel climbing a sharp sill: flat, edge, top), which needs
   witness migration in the transaction (BD-093), and the door sill of `fixture:cart-sill-dock` (BD-123); stand and drop tests
   of the first archetypes (`quad-mammal`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`) under their baseline
   controllers, and the reference quadruped walking; the ballistic drop with restitution 0.5 and 0.9; the
   bouncing-ball accumulation fixture as the positive control for `FROB-REF-220`.
3. **Measured, per fixture and in total:** transactions run; share committed at attempt 0, 1, 2 and 3; share
   ended by `FROB-REF-220` split by cause (level, count); share ended by `FROB-REF-221` split by the failing
   check; the count ended by `FROB-REF-224`; the distribution of the deepest level used and of events per base
   interval, with the deepest level and the largest event count per fixture; wall-clock per transaction at the
   50th and 95th percentiles and per committed interval; the worlds lost (`Faulted`) per fixture.
4. **Sweep.** L_max ∈ {12, 16, 20, 21}; E_max ∈ {16, 64, 256}; N_retry ∈ {1, 3, 5}. One factor at a time
   around the default; results are paired by world address (the same episodes under each setting).
   Arithmetic check of resolution, exact in rationals: ℓ₀/2ᴸ for ℓ₀ = 2 ms gives 488.28125 ns, 30.517578125 ns,
   1.9073486328125 ns and 0.95367431640625 ns at L = 12, 16, 20, 21; the location error at 3 m/s is 1.5 µm,
   0.09 µm, 0.006 µm and 0.003 µm. Time stays rational, so the grid is exact at every L; the sub-interval
   length handed to the `f64` solve is ℓ₀ rounded once to `f64` and then divided by 2ᴸ, a power-of-two scaling
   that is exact in `f64`, so no level adds rounding of its own.
   **Surface (DESIGN; the VERBS-sim row this bead owns).** A setting enters through the `sim.run` input `--txn
   l-max=<L>,e-max=<E>,retries=<N>`; an omitted member keeps its default, and a value outside the domain of
   N16a-06 and BD-083 is `FROB-REF-802`. The input is admitted only under the transactional profiles (`referee`,
   `strict`); under `lockstep` it is `FROB-REF-802` with `pointer` `/txn`, because a lockstep step has no
   transaction to bound. The three values enter `StepPlanId`, and `FROB-REF-220` and `FROB-REF-221` are raised as
   the limits bind.
5. **Decision rule (DESIGN, written before data):**
   - keep the defaults if, at the defaults, every M1 fixture has at least one completed run per scenario, no
     non-accumulation fixture loses more than 1 world in 10³ to `FROB-REF-220` or `FROB-REF-221`, and the
     accumulation fixture is refused;
   - raise a limit only if the sweep shows that the raise removes at least half of that cause's refusals on a
     non-accumulation fixture, the wall-clock per committed interval grows by at most 20%, and the
     95th-percentile cost per transaction grows by less than a factor of two;
   - lower a limit only if the sweep shows no fixture uses the headroom (the deepest level used is at least four
     below L_max; events per base interval never exceed a quarter of E_max) and the lower value changes no
     refusal count and no committed digest;
   - an accumulation point (a ball with restitution bouncing to rest) must still be refused with `FROB-REF-220`
     at every admitted E_max: no setting may turn it into a silent approximation, and no tolerance is loosened
     to hide a missing sill run;
   - if no setting in the sweep gives the door sill a completed run, the stop decision of M1 fires: the referee
     is re-planned, and this bead records that outcome instead of a set of constants.
6. **Record.** An ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KIN-OPEN-5.md` (proposed identifier ADR-KIN-12) with the measured table, the chosen constants, the reason,
   and the consequence for identities: the three constants are part of the transactional numerics in
   `StepPlanId`, so a change of default changes the default identity of every transactional result and is
   announced as such. The change is made once, before the M1 gate rows are recorded. The open-question
   registry row is closed with a pointer to the ADR.
7. **Fail-closed default until the ADR exists:** 16, 64 and 3, with refusal on exhaustion.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `open5::sweep_harness_pairs_by_address`: the same world addresses are used under every setting.
- `open5::counts_add_up`: committed + refused-220 + refused-221 + refused-224 + refused-721 equals transactions
  run, per fixture (224 and 721 are terminal refusals that no constant here moves, but they end transactions).
- `open5::positive_control_refused`: the accumulation fixture ends in `FROB-REF-220` under all nine settings.
- `open5::lowering_changes_no_digest`: for a fixture whose deepest level is 9, L_max = 12 and 16 give equal
  digests.
- `open5::dyadic_resolution_table`: 2 ms/2¹² = 488.28125 ns, /2¹⁶ = 30.517578125 ns, /2²⁰ = 1.9073486328125 ns,
  /2²¹ = 0.95367431640625 ns, exact in rationals.
- `open5::emax_guard`: a `synthetic` interval with 65 events is refused at E_max = 64 and committed at 256 only
  if every event is bracketed.
- `open5::constants_in_profile_identity`: two profiles differing in N_retry have different identities.
- `open5::txn_input_admission`: `--txn l-max=20` under `--profile referee` is accepted with E_max and N_retry
  at their defaults and a `StepPlanId` different from the default's; it validates under `--profile strict`; under
  `--profile lockstep` it is `FROB-REF-802` with `pointer` `/txn`.
- `open5::accumulation_always_refused`: negative control, a mutant that drops events beyond E_max instead of
  refusing must fail.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_open5_refusal_rate.sh`: runs `frob sim run --fixture <fixture> --tier referee --profile referee
--txn l-max=<L>,e-max=<E>,retries=<N> --seed <s> --key open-kin-5-<fixture>-<L>-<E>-<N>-<s> --json` (the
`--txn` input above; the key because a run verb without an idempotency key is refused with `FROB-REF-805`, exit 3) over
the fixture list and the sweep. A fixture is named by its registered name (VERBS-sim's one spelling; the positional
`fixture:<name>` is retired): `wheel-over-lip` and `thin-barrier` (BD-086), `cart-sill-dock` (BD-123),
`kin/tied-sliders` (BD-083), `kin/ballistic-drop` (N16c-02) and `kin/accumulation` (N16a-10, the bouncing ball of
the positive control); the restitution-0.9 drop is this bead's, written under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/kin/`
and registered as `kin/ballistic-drop-e09`. An archetype run takes the archetype design as the positional with
N16ap4-01's `--task` and `--controller` (`--task stand-10s`, baseline controller, for the stand rows) in place of
`--fixture`. The script aggregates with `frob telemetry failures --json`, and writes one `metric` line per (fixture, setting) with
`transactions`, `committed_by_attempt`, `refused_220`, `refused_221`, `refused_224`, `refused_721`, `p50_ms`, `p95_ms`, the constants and the
`step_plan_id`, the deepest level used and the largest event count; `refusal` lines carry the full refusal
block; `assert` lines record the sill completion and the accumulation refusal. The summary line lists the
settings that satisfy the decision rule and the adopted constants. Exit non-zero if the positive control was not
refused or a count does not add up. Logs under `target/e2e-logs/kin_open5_refusal_rate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`
with a transcript beside it.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Positive control: restitution 0.5 from a 1 mm drop accumulates impacts; refusal is the correct outcome. The
  bouncing ball with restitution r has flight times shrinking by r each bounce, so infinitely many impacts fall
  before t*·(1 + r)/(1 − r), where t* is the time of the first impact. For the fixture (r = 0.5, a 1 mm drop)
  t* = √(2·0.001/9.81) = 14.278 ms and the accumulation time is 3·t* = 42.835 ms, inside the base interval
  [42, 44) ms, which must be refused. At L_max = 16 the level clause fires first (the gap between impacts falls
  below one 30.5 ns cell after about 14 impacts in that interval, BD-082); at L_max = 21 about 19 impacts are
  located first (impacts 6 to 24, the gap t*·0.5ᵏ dropping below the 0.95 ns cell at k = 24, by BD-082's
  count), so at E_max = 16 the count clause fires instead. Either way the outcome is `FROB-REF-220`, and
  the measurement table records which clause fired. (For a 1 m drop the same formula gives 3 × 0.4515 s =
  1.3546 s.)
- Negative control: a build whose event counter is not reset per base interval refuses the pronk fixture after
  16 strides; the harness must show that fixture as refused and flag the regression against the default table.
- KA-343 (resolution at L_max = 16 and 2 ms: 31 ns, 0.1 µm at 3 m/s) is recomputed for each swept L_max:
  488 ns at 12, 1.9 ns at 20, 0.95 ns at 21.

## Considerations

- What changes downstream with each outcome: a larger L_max lowers the referee's real-time factor, which lowers
  the share of device work a background referee can shadow; a smaller N_retry raises the `Faulted` share, which
  lowers the referee's own coverage per robot class.
- The decision is re-read at M2 on the frozen standard battery (the question's stated evidence is "the refusal
  rate on the standard battery"; at M1 only fixtures exist). The M2 re-read may amend the ADR once.
- Excluded worlds are always counted in summaries; a lower refusal rate obtained by scoring faulted worlds as
  failures is forbidden.
- Raising N_retry lengthens the worst case of a transaction linearly; raising L_max lengthens bisection by one
  step per level; raising E_max lengthens the event list. None changes a committed digest of an interval that
  committed before, which the sweep verifies.
- These constants do nothing for the referee's capacity limits (21 contacts, fixed witnesses); those refusals
  are reported separately and have their own postures.
- Owner: Kinesis lead. Deadline: M1 exit. Blocks: the referee rows of GATE-KIN-04 being cited with final
  constants; the M1 stop decision. Because it blocks them it is one step more urgent than its milestone.
