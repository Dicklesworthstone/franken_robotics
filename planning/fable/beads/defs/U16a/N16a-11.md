## Background

Every rollout needs a time integrator. The default one in FrankenRobotics is deliberately plain (Solid tier):
semi-implicit Euler. The solve stage returns the end-of-step velocity v₊; the integrate stage sets the
configuration by a group update q₊ = q·τ(h·v₊), where τ is a retraction from the Lie algebra to the group. Joint
damping and reflected rotor inertia are implicit in the matrix A of the solve. Default physics step h = 2 ms
with control every tenth step (open question OPEN-KIN-1, decided by measurement at M2).

Why this design, and what was rejected:

- **Group update instead of an additive update.** Adding a quaternion increment and renormalizing needs an
  order-h² projection every step; a group update is group-valued in exact arithmetic, so the only thing left to
  remove is round-off: one normalization per step by a correctly rounded square root. Runge–Kutta on Euler
  angles is rejected for its singularities.
- **The Cayley retraction on the throughput tiers.** cay(x) = (I − x/2)⁻¹·(I + x/2) is rational: it uses only
  +, −, ×, ÷ and √, which IEEE 754 requires to be correctly rounded, so it is a candidate for bit-identical
  execution on CPU, CUDA and Metal and it can be certified as a polynomial identity. Its cost is accuracy: it
  rotates by 2·atan(‖ω‖·h/2) instead of ‖ω‖·h (relative error about (‖ω‖·h)²/12), so its use is bounded by
  ‖ω‖·h ≤ 0.2 per substep and refused beyond (`FROB-REF-212`; repairs: substep; use the exponential).
- **Implicit damping.** Joint damping treated explicitly limits the stable step; put into A it costs nothing
  (A stays symmetric positive definite) and keeps the convex objective of the solve intact.
- **Not symplectic, and not claimed to be.** No energy-drift or symplectic property is claimed for this
  integrator. The structure-preserving lane is a separate, Frontier mechanism.

A known hazard that the integrator's contract must state (the held-command known answer): a loop that is stable
for the exact held-input plant can be unstable under this integrator at one step per control tick. For an ideal
slider with a held acceleration command, the exact one-tick map is x′ = x + w + u/2, w′ = w + u, while one
semi-implicit step (velocity, then position) gives x′ = x + w + u, w′ = w + u; with the gain u = −(3/2)·(x + w)
the spectral radii are 1/2 and (1 + √3)/2 ≈ 1.366. This is why the default decimation is ten and why
step-refinement studies change only h.

## Scope

In: the definition of A and of the free velocity; the order of operations in stages 8 and 9 as far as the
integrator fixes it; the group update per coordinate class; the Cayley bound and its refusal; decimation and
control-interval handling on the lockstep tiers; a host `f64` reference of one integrator step; and, for a
linear plant, the update over one control interval at decimation d returned as an explicit matrix
(`integrate::linear_tick_map(plant, d)`, exact in rationals), which is the map the sampled-loop analysis reads
when it evaluates a loop with the tier's own integrator (N16c-42). Out: the kernels
`K-cay-step`, `K-normalize`, `K-anchor` (BD-157); the Newton solve (BD-156); the step driver (BD-159); the
variational lane (BD-087, BD-384); the transactional profiles' advance (BD-082).

## Technical approach

Symbols: q configuration; v generalized velocity (base body twist, then joint rates); M(q) mass matrix; h_b(q, v)
bias forces; τ_a applied generalized forces (actuators, passive forces, external forces); D ⪰ 0 the diagonal
joint-damping matrix; J_r the diagonal of reflected rotor inertias N²·J_rotor; J the constraint rows; ℓ_c the
convex potential of row block c; h the step.

1. **Assembly (stage 6 → 7).** A = M(q) + J_r + h·D. A is symmetric positive definite because M ≻ 0, J_r ⪰ 0,
   D ⪰ 0 (this is half of proof obligation PO-KIN-01: A ≻ 0 for every admitted parameter row).
2. **Free velocity.** v* solves A·v* = (M + J_r)·v + h·(τ_a − h_b). (Derivation: the implicit-damping update
   (M + J_r)·(v₊ − v)/h = τ_a − h_b − D·v₊ + constraint impulses/h; without constraints v₊ = v*.) One dense
   Cholesky solve with the factor of A, or folded into the first Newton iteration.
3. **Solve (stage 8).** v₊ = argmin over v of ½·(v − v*)ᵀ·A·(v − v*) + Σ_c ℓ_c(J_c·v), by the lockstep Newton
   solve; minus the gradient of ℓ_c is the constraint impulse. The integrator requires only that stage 8
   returns v₊ and its gradient-norm certificate.
4. **Integrate (stage 9).** q₊ = q·τ(h·v₊), coordinate class by coordinate class:
   - floating base in SE(3): g₊ = g·cay(h·ξ₊), with cay(ω, v) having rotation cay(ω̂) = I + 4/(4 + ‖ω‖²)·(ω̂ +
     ω̂²/2) and translation (I − ω̂/2)⁻¹·v; then the anchored-position update (integer cell index plus an offset
     in [−4, 4) m, shifted exactly when it leaves the cell);
   - revolute joints: the unit complex number z₊ = z·((1 − a²/4) + i·a)/(1 + a²/4) with a = ω·h, plus the
     integer turn counter (incremented when the argument crosses the cut);
   - prismatic joints: d₊ = d + h·ḋ₊;
   - multi-column joints: the product of one-parameter updates in the screw basis order.
   Then one normalization per step (`K-normalize`) and the energy-ledger accumulation (`K-ledger-step`).
5. **Cayley bound.** Before stage 9, the per-world maximum of ‖ω‖·h over rotating coordinates is compared with
   0.2. On the lockstep tiers a world over the bound is refused with `FROB-REF-212` under the caller's
   overflow-style policy (the world is marked and frozen, never silently integrated); on the transactional
   profiles it is a domain check that triggers a smaller sub-interval. The bound is a DESIGN constant printed in
   the contract.
6. **Decimation.** World-steps per control interval are fixed at admission by the clock contract and are part
   of `StepPlanId`. Default: d = 10 at h = 2 ms (50 Hz control). No schedule is required to be an integer ratio
   of the physics tick: a control period that is not a multiple of h is served by its phase accumulator under
   the boundary ordering rule (`hold`: the tick is realized at the first boundary at or after its nominal
   instant, lateness less than h, recorded on the packet). Commands are held over the open interval after their
   actuation instant.
7. **Host reference.** `frob_dyn::integrate::reference_step_f64(model, state, tau, h) -> state` implements
   items 1 to 4 in plain `f64` with dense linear algebra, for use by tests and by the parity harness's
   forward-dynamics row. It is the CPU reference path of the capability.
8. **Reported with every result.** Integrator id (`semi-implicit-euler`), retraction (`cayley` or
   `exponential`), h, decimation, the maximum ‖ω‖·h seen, and the group-membership residual.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With no constraints and no damping, one step reproduces v₊ = v + h·M⁻¹·(τ_a − h_b) and q₊ = q·cay(h·v₊)
      against the host reference to round-off, in `f64` and `f32`.
- [ ] A = M + J_r + h·D is symmetric positive definite on 10⁴ sampled states per reference robot and for every
      admitted parameter row (Cholesky succeeds; smallest pivot recorded).
- [ ] Damped joint, closed form: a single joint with inertia I and damping d decays as v_{k+1} = v_k·I/(I + h·d)
      per step, exactly.
- [ ] A world with ‖ω‖·h > 0.2 is refused with `FROB-REF-212` and never integrated; a world at 0.19 passes.
- [ ] Group membership: the residual stays within four units in the last place over 10⁷ steps with the per-step
      normalization on.
- [ ] Measured order by step halving on the pendulum: the position error at a fixed time falls by a factor
      between 1.8 and 2.2 per halving (first order in position for semi-implicit Euler), recorded.
- [ ] The held-command slider at decimation one reproduces the one-tick map x′ = x + w + u, w′ = w + u exactly,
      and at decimation d the map x′ = x + w + (d + 1)·u/(2d), w′ = w + u.
- [ ] A 30 Hz controller on 400 Hz physics fires at boundaries 0, 14, 27 and 40 with lateness 0, 2, 1 and 0
      quanta, and never every thirteenth step.
- [ ] `linear_tick_map` returns the slider's one-tick matrix at every decimation of the test below, bit-equal
      in rationals to the map obtained by stepping the integrator d times, so that an analysis and the tier
      cannot disagree about the integrator.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `integrate::free_step_matches_reference` (10⁴ states, both precisions inside their class).
- `integrate::implicit_damping_scalar`: I = 0.02 kg·m², d = 0.5 N·m·s/rad, h = 2 ms: factor
  0.02/(0.02 + 0.001) = 0.952381 per step; explicit damping would give 0.95.
- `integrate::reflected_inertia_in_a`: N = 9, J_rotor = 10⁻⁴ kg·m² adds 0.0081 kg·m² to the joint's diagonal.
- `integrate::cayley_angle`: ω = 20 rad/s, h = 2 ms: rotation 2·atan(0.02) against 0.04 rad, relative error
  1.33 × 10⁻⁴.
- `integrate::cayley_bound`: ω·h = 0.21 refused, 0.19 admitted.
- `integrate::se3_translation`: ω = (0, 0, 2), v = (1, 0, 0), h = 1: a quarter turn about z and translation
  (1/2, 1/2, 0); the mutant that pairs cay(ω̂) with v unchanged returns (1, 0, 0) and fails.
- `integrate::turn_counter`: a revolute joint spun through 3.5 turns reports turn count 3 and the right
  residual angle.
- `integrate::slider_one_tick_maps`: d = 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 16, 32 against (d + 1)/(2d); spectral radii
  1.366, 0.784, 1/2, 1/4, 0.395, 0.451, 0.476 for the gain u = −(3/2)·(x + w).
- `integrate::phase_accumulator_schedule`: the 400 Hz and 30 Hz schedule above; 600 decisions in 20 s, not 616.
- `integrate::group_membership_long`: 10⁷ steps of a spinning body.

logged as metrics.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The Cayley map on SE(3): ω = (0, 0, 2), v = (1, 0, 0) → quarter turn, translation (1/2, 1/2, 0). Control: the
  map that leaves v unchanged.
- KA-052 / KA-053 (construction rows): the update lies exactly on the group; residual within four units in the
  last place over 10⁷ steps. Control: an additive quaternion increment followed by renormalization.
- The held-command table: exact map radius 1/2 (64 of 64 cases pass), one semi-implicit step radius
  (1 + √3)/2 ≈ 1.366 (1 of 64, the origin), two steps (5 + √57)/16 ≈ 0.784 (15 of 64). The gate for that table
  is owned by the held-command bead; this bead supplies the integrator it tests.
- Planted defect: position updated before velocity (explicit Euler); the slider map becomes x′ = x + w,
  w′ = w + u and the damped-joint factor becomes 0.95; both tests fail.
- Metamorphic: halving the time step moves results by the integrator's order.

## Considerations

- OPEN-KIN-1 (default step and Newton iteration budget; default 2 ms and 4 iterations with warm start; owner
  Kinesis lead) is decided by the fidelity–throughput frontier at M2 (bead N16a-16). If the default moves,
  every performance row is restated with its new decimation before any gate cites it.
- `K-cay-step` at M0 covered a single body; the articulated version and `K-normalize` are BD-157. This bead
  fixes the semantics those kernels implement.
- The integrator's energy behaviour is measured, not asserted: the created-energy audit reads the ledger
  residual accumulated in stage 9.
- The exponential retraction (with branch-free small-angle polynomials) is offered on the referee only; it is a
  different `StepPlanId`.
