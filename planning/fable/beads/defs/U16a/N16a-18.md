## Background

At M2 the batched step exists on `batch-cpu`: ten stages in a fixed order, a lockstep Newton solve under masks,
fixed-shape buffers whose overflow is flagged, and padding rules that make a world's bits independent of its
batch. Unit tests cover each piece in its crate. This bead is the proof from the outside: a shell script that
drives the real `frob` binary and records, in structured logs, that a quadruped drops, stands and walks, that an
overflow is flagged and never silent, that one world's digests are the same alone and in any batch, and that the
kernels agree with the pinned oracle. It covers the `batch-cpu` rows of three gates:

- GATE-KIN-03 (Parity): `batch-cpu` in `f64` against `fs-mbd`: kinematics, inverse dynamics, forward dynamics
  and mass matrix within 10⁻¹⁰ relative on 10⁴ states per reference robot.
- GATE-KIN-05 (Known answer, determinism): 1,000 episodes with identical digests solo and in 100 random
  co-tenancies, capacities and slot assignments, per back end; the path-table Jacobian gives the same digests as
  the dense one.
- GATE-KIN-07 (Known answer): `Degraded` world-steps at most 10⁻⁴ of world-steps per class on the standard
  battery; every forced overflow flagged; capacities printed in every disclosure block.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures, assertions, logs and the tier receipts of the gate rows it covers on `batch-cpu`. Out: device rows
(the device beads re-run the same script with `--tier gpu-cuda` and `--tier gpu-metal`); the battery-wide
`Degraded` rate (recorded with the frozen standard battery); parameter variants (N16a-19).

## Technical approach

Script: `scripts/e2e/kin_lockstep_batch.sh`. Plain shell; sources the shared helper of STD-E2E; idempotent;
seeds explicit; exits non-zero on failure after writing the summary. A `--smoke` mode shrinks counts for the
`smoke` battery; the default mode is the gate run.

Steps (each is one `step` value in the log). Every `frob sim run` and `frob sim replay` below also passes `--key
kin-batch-<step>-<n>`, `<n>` counting the calls of the step: both are job-submitting run verbs, and a call
without an idempotency key is refused with `FROB-REF-805` (KNOWN_DRIFTS 5). A control build (steps 8 and 10) is
the `frob` binary built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,<crate>/nc-<nnnn>`, the feature of the control's
record (BD-025's form; one control per build, never a product build), and the step runs against it with
`e2e_expect_caught <NC-nnnn>`.

1. `admission`: `frob sim run quad-mammal --tier batch-cpu --json` echoes `profile: "lockstep"`, `law: "CL-1"`,
   `precision: "f32"`; `--tier gpu-cuda --profile referee` exits 3 with `FROB-REF-222`.
2. `estimate`: `frob sim estimate quad-mammal --worlds 8192 --tier batch-cpu --json` reports C = 32, R = 160,
   31 kB per world from the byte model (about 35 kB with state), the `step_plan_id`, and never runs physics
   (wall time under one second; no run id created).
3. `drop`: `frob sim run quad-mammal --scene plane --worlds 64 --seconds 2 --tier batch-cpu --seed 7
   --init drop=0.1m --json` → no `Faulted` world; final base height within 1 cm of the standing height; the
   receipt reports `energy_ledger: present` (K-ledger-step bound by BD-208) and the ledger closes within the
   single-precision residual bound.
4. `stand`: 10 s under the baseline controller → base height drift under 1 cm, zero `Degraded`.
5. `walk`: 20 s at the baseline's commanded speed → at least 5 m of forward displacement in every world;
   results labelled `provisional`; `integrator: "semi-implicit-euler"`, `retraction: "cayley"`, `h: 1/500`,
   `decimation: 10` and `max_omega_h` (below 0.2) read from the envelope and logged (N16a-11); the Newton
   iteration count launched (the class constant, 4) and the gradient-norm certificate at exit (median and 99th
   percentile) logged as `metric` lines (BD-159).
6. `determinism`: step 5 repeated, and repeated with `--workers 1`, `8` and `24` → identical per-world digests.
7. `identities`: the same fixture at `--worlds 1`, `256` and `4096` → equal `step_plan_id`; with
   `--newton-iterations 8` → a different `step_plan_id` and equal `model_id`.
8. `batch-independence`: world address 1337 run solo and inside `--worlds 4096` at `--capacity contacts=32`,
   `64` and `128`, in 100 random slot assignments and co-tenancies drawn from seed 11 (1,000 episodes in the
   gate run, 20 in smoke) → identical digest chains. Control build of KA-344's registered control (a reduction
   tree shaped by capacity) → at least one chain differs and the step records `control_caught: true`.
9. `path-table`: the same seeded quadruped and humanoid batches with `--jacobian dense` and `--jacobian
   path-table` → identical digest chains world by world; both `step_plan_id` values, bytes per world for both
   from `frob sim estimate … --json`, and the count of worlds that raised `dense-row-overflow` (must be 0 for
   the comparison to count, N16a-13) are logged; on mismatch the first differing step, stage and world and the
   replay command.
10. `overflow`: the forced-overflow fixture (40 simultaneous contact candidates at C = 32) under
    `--on-overflow refuse` → exit 5, `FROB-REF-722`, repairs listing "raise capacity (new StepPlanId)", "reset",
    "continue-flagged"; under `reset` → the world resets and the episode is recorded as capacity-ended; under
    `continue-flagged` → the run completes with exit 2, `degraded.contact_overflow = 1` and the flag on every
    result of that world. Control build of KA-349's registered control (a silent truncation of the contact set,
    BD-159's mutant) → the flag is absent and the assertion fails as required.
11. `cayley-bound`: a spinning-body fixture with `--step 20ms` → `FROB-REF-212` with the repairs "substep; use
    the exponential".
12. `spin-translate` (the command-line run of the group-update and re-anchoring kernels of BD-157, whose own
    script runs only the kernel harness because nothing it depends on runs `sim.run`): the `synthetic` fixture
    `tests/fixtures/kinesis/free_sphere_spin.fbot`, one free rigid body (a 1 kg sphere of radius 0.1 m, so its
    inertia is isotropic) in an empty scene with gravity off and declared bounds of ±16 km, started at
    (10,000, 0, 0) m (cell 1,250 at offset 0 on x) with body twist ω = (6, 0, 8) rad/s and v = (0.3, 0, 0.4)
    m/s. Because v is parallel to ω and the inertia is isotropic the bias force is zero and the exact motion is
    a screw along (0.6, 0, 0.8); ‖ω‖·h = 0.02 at h = 2 ms. `frob sim run <fixture> --tier batch-cpu --worlds 1
    --seconds 2000 --seed 7 --json` (10⁶ steps, 1,000 m of travel) at `--workers 1` and `--workers 8` →
    identical per-step digest chains; `frob telemetry query <run> --stream kinematics --channels
    membership_residual_ulp,anchor_offset,reanchor_count --json` → the maximum of |‖q‖² − 1| over all 10⁶ steps
    at most 4 units in the last place in `f32`, every offset inside [−4, 4) m, and re-anchor counts 75 on x, 0
    on y and 100 on z (the body ends near (10,600, 0, 800) m, cells 1,325 and 100 at offsets near 0, so the
    counts do not depend on the offset's accumulated rounding). Logged as `metric` lines: the residual with its
    threshold, the re-anchor count per axis against its expected value, the final cell indices and offsets,
    and both digest chains' final digests; on a mismatch the first differing step and coordinate with both
    bit patterns. The run is one world and takes seconds, so smoke mode runs it unchanged.
13. `parity`: N16a-17's four rows for each reference robot (kinematics, inverse dynamics, mass matrix, forward
    dynamics) in the Gauntlet's tier target, `e2e_tier g2_parity GATE-KIN-03 --mutations` → maximum relative
    error at most 10⁻¹⁰ in `f64` and the planted candidates caught; the receipt hash is kept for step 19.
14. `fence`: `frob telemetry query <run> --stream runtime --channels batch_epoch --json` while a long run is in progress (bounded
    wait, then job handle) → every row read belongs to a completed batch.
15. `cancel`: a 60 s run cancelled after 2 s with `frob job cancel` → the receipt reports the last committed
    batch per world; re-running to the same point gives the same digests.
16. `disclosure`: the envelope's disclosure block contains contact capacity, row capacity, mean active contacts
    per world, `Degraded` and `Faulted` counts, profile, precision profile, step and decimation.
17. `replay`: `frob sim replay --run <run of step 5> --world 1337 --json` (on the recorded backend, no
    `--tier`) reproduces the digest chain of world 1337 from step 5.
18. `held-command`: the held-command slider fixture (N16c-41) on `batch-cpu` at decimation 1 and 4 → 64-case
    pass counts 1 and 64 logged as `metric` lines with the one-tick radius beside each; the full GATE-KIN-13
    record on three backends is N16c-45's.
19. `gate-receipts`: runs the remaining `batch-cpu` tier rows through the STD-E2E helper with their controls,
    `e2e_tier g5_determinism GATE-KIN-05 --mutations` (KA-344) and `e2e_tier g4_chaos GATE-KIN-07 --mutations`
    (KA-349), and lists their receipt hashes with step 13's in the summary. The script never runs `record_gate`
    and never writes `registries/gates.toml` or the bead log (the STD-E2E lint `self-recording`). This bead is
    the one recorder of GATE-KIN-03 (from step 13's receipt; BD-159 and N16a-17 record nothing) and of the
    `batch-cpu` part of GATE-KIN-05 (the device rows are BD-181's): a session that did not implement the work
    runs `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate <id> --part M2 --receipt <hash>` for each.
    GATE-KIN-07 is recorded whole by N16b-03 (the battery-wide `Degraded` rate and the forced-overflow sweep);
    the `g4_chaos` receipt here is a check of KA-349 from the user's side and is not recorded.

Logging. Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/kin_lockstep_batch/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript
beside it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N16a-18"), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`,
`refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class,
tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds, digests where state is
compared, and on failure the first differing value (step, stage, buffer, world) with the artifact paths and the
replay command. The final `summary` line: assertions passed and failed, controls caught, and
`gates: ["GATE-KIN-03", "GATE-KIN-05", "GATE-KIN-07"]` with the rows touched.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a clean build; each of the two control builds makes its step fail.
- [ ] `metric` lines exist for: forward displacement (m, ≥ 5), base height drift (m, ≤ 0.01), digest-chain
      mismatches (count, = 0) for determinism, batch independence and path table, parity errors per robot and
      row (≤ 10⁻¹⁰), degraded counts by reason, and for the spin-translate run the membership residual (ulp,
      ≤ 4) and the re-anchor counts per axis (75, 0, 100) with digest chains equal at 1 and 8 workers.
- [ ] Every refusal step logs code, exit code and first repair: `FROB-REF-222` (3), `FROB-REF-722` (5),
      `FROB-REF-212` (3).
- [ ] The log validates against the STD-E2E schema; no private-scene data appears (fixtures are synthetic).
- [ ] The smoke mode completes in under five minutes on eight cores (TARGET).
- [ ] Re-running gives identical digests and metrics.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Shell-level tests of the script's helpers under the STD-E2E harness: `extracts_digest_chain`,
`compares_chains_reports_first_diff`, `parses_disclosure_block`, `fails_when_control_not_caught`.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script (Technical approach). Lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1, 8 and 24; debug and
release; and, in the device beads, `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal` each against its determinism class (bit equality
where the cross-backend class has been attained, the decision-level equivalence test otherwise).

### Known answers and negative controls

| Row | Truth | Tolerance | Control that must fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| KA-344 | Identical digests at capacities 32, 64 and 128 and in any co-tenancy; path table equals dense | exact | A reduction tree shaped by capacity |
| KA-349 | Every forced overflow flagged `Degraded` or refused (`FROB-REF-722`) | exact for the flag | A silent truncation of the contact set |
| KA-360 | `batch-cpu` `f64` against `fs-mbd` on 10⁴ states per reference robot | 10⁻¹⁰ relative | The planted candidates of the parity harness |
| KA-348 | Bytes per world from the byte model (Q: 31 kB at C 32, R 160) | to the kilobyte | The Jacobian term computed with links instead of coordinates |
| KA-053, KA-790 | Group membership within four units in the last place over 10⁶ steps; centred offsets re-anchor exactly (75, 0 and 100 crossings on the spin-translate run) | 4 ulp; exact counts | The kernel mutants of BD-157 (no normalization; uncentred anchor), run at kernel level there |

Metamorphic relations exercised: permuting world indices permutes the results and changes nothing else; adding
inactive worlds or unused capacity changes no active world's digest; splitting a rollout at a snapshot and
resuming equals running it whole (step 15).

## Considerations

- The gate run of step 8 (1,000 episodes × 101 runs) is hours of `batch-cpu` time; it runs in the gate lane and
  its count is recorded, while smoke keeps the mechanism under test on every change.
- If no device family holds batch-composition invariance, M2 can still exit on `batch-cpu`: this script is
  then the record for the Known answer and Parity gates, and device gates are recorded `not run` with the
  reason.
- The `sim.run` inputs the script uses are VERBS-sim rows: `--on-overflow` and `--init` (N16b-25),
  `--capacity` (N16a-14), `--jacobian` (N16a-13), `--step` and `--newton-iterations` (N16a-03, N16a-04);
  `--workers` is the operator parameter of `sim.run` that BD-160 registers (a dependency). The telemetry
  channel names of steps 12 and 14 are DESIGN here and are settled with the channel declarations of their
  producers.
