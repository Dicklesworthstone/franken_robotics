## Background

This is the seed bead that turns the trust ladder from a design into measured numbers at the M2 exit. Its
done-condition is: GATE-FID-01, with the one permitted re-baseline written down; GATE-KIN-07; GATE-ARENA-04 on the
batch tiers; KILL-CONTACT decided.

The fidelity ledger (stores and comparator in N16b-02) records how far a faster configuration lands from a slower,
more trusted one on the standard battery (N16b-01). An **envelope** is the frozen tolerance within which a faster
tier must agree with a slower one for a result to be promoted. Promotion (BD-263) asks one question per audited
episode: did the two tiers disagree beyond the frozen envelope? That question is only honest if the envelope was
fixed before the outcomes were seen. An envelope tuned after outcomes voids every error bound of promotion; this
is the primary risk of the whole ladder, and the countermeasure is implemented here: the envelope is hashed into
the ledger before any promotion samples, and a promotion in a stratum with no frozen envelope is refused
(`FROB-REF-821`).

The bead also serves BET-03: convex compliant contact (law CL-1), audited by a referee, is good enough for
locomotion decisions. BET-03 is falsified if on the standard battery the fast tier disagrees with the referee
beyond the fidelity thresholds on slip distance, stance impulse or cost of transport and no parameter mapping
fixes it. KILL-CONTACT is the stop rule of that bet and is decided on the measurements of this bead.

Rejected alternatives: per-run tolerances chosen by the caller (they are tuned after outcomes); a single global
tolerance (stance impulse and base displacement do not share a scale); silently widening a threshold when a gate
fails (the plan permits exactly one re-baseline, with a written rationale, then freezes).

## Scope

In: the M2 measurement of `batch-cpu` against `referee`; the gate thresholds; the single re-baseline; the envelope
record and its freeze; the verb `fidelity.ledger`; the batch-tier rows of GATE-ARENA-04; recording the
measurements KILL-CONTACT is decided on; confirming that GATE-KIN-07 is on record (measured by N16b-03).
Out: the battery definition (N16b-01); ledger tables and comparator (N16b-02); the referee's own coverage report
(BD-213); promotion (BD-263); the created-energy audit rules (N16b-14), except that their bound is re-baselined
in the same event.

## Technical approach

**1. Measure.** Run every address of the frozen battery of each reference class on `batch-cpu` under the
`lockstep` profile and on `referee` (same address, scene draw, disturbances, randomization and policy). Where the
referee refuses by name (contact cap `FROB-REF-721`, fixed-witness gate, missing joint class, kinematic loop), the
`strict` profile of `batch-cpu` adjudicates and the pair records `adjudicator = batch-cpu:strict`. Write pair rows
and stratum summaries (N16b-02).

**2. Compare with the gate (GATE-FID-01, TARGET).** Per reference class, median and 95th-percentile disagreement:

| Quantity | Median at most | 95th percentile at most |
|---|---|---|
| base displacement over 2 s | 2% | 5% |
| energy per metre | 3% | 8% |
| stance impulse | 5% | 12% |

**3. Re-baseline, once.** If a threshold is missed after parameter mapping (fitting CL-1's stiffness, dissipation
time and friction to the referee's law on the contact battery of BD-168) and one repair cycle, the thresholds may
be re-baselined once. The re-baseline is a document `docs/adr/ADR-FID-01-rebaseline.md` stating, per quantity: the
measured distribution, the old and new threshold, the physical reason, and what was tried first. The created-energy
bound of the energy audit (1% of the absolute work exchanged in any 0.5 s window on a fast tier; 10⁻⁶ relative on
the referee) is re-baselined in the same document and at no other time. After the document is committed, the
thresholds are frozen; a second re-baseline is not a code path.

**4. Freeze the envelopes.** For each (stratum, quantity) write an envelope record, schema
`frob.fidelity.envelope/1`:

```
envelope { stratum_key, quantity, bound, unit, floor, basis: "p95", battery_id, kernel_versions,
           frozen_at, rationale: Option<doc hash>, session }
EnvelopeId = BLAKE3(canonical bytes)
```

DESIGN, envelope values: for the three gated quantities the bound is the frozen 95th-percentile threshold (5%, 8%,
12%, or their re-baselined values). For the other quantities the plan gives no number; the bound is the measured
95th percentile of that quantity's disagreement on the M2 battery, rounded up to two significant digits and
written in the freeze record. Task decisions have no tolerance: any differing decision is an exceedance. With the
envelope at the battery's 95th percentile, the battery's own exceedance rate is about 5%, which is the rate p₀
that the promotion table treats as faithful.

Freezing is one ledger transaction per stratum. A frozen envelope is immutable. A new kernel version, law,
profile, step plan or battery version is a new stratum with no envelope, and promotion there is refused with
`FROB-REF-821` (repair: freeze the envelope first, by running the battery for that stratum).

**5. The verb `fidelity.ledger` (read, first milestone M2).** Input schema `frob.fidelity.ledger.in/1`: `class`,
`quantity` (optional, default all), `tier_pair`, optional `scene_class`, `profile_pair`, `law_pair`,
`kernel_versions`, a cursor. Output schema `frob.fidelity.ledger.out/1`: rows of
`{ stratum_key, n, unpaired_by_cause, median, p95, p99, max, bias, rho, decisions { agree, discordant_fast_only,
discordant_slow_only }, envelope { id, bound, unit, frozen_at } | null, adjudicators }`, paginated against a
committed frontier. An unfrozen stratum prints `envelope: null` and the next action "freeze". CLI:
`frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --quantity energy-per-metre --json`. Exit 0;
exit 2 when a returned stratum misses its gate threshold. Evidence: claim Fidelity, class Tested, determinism D3.

**6. Fixture 1 on the batch tiers (GATE-ARENA-04, batch rows).** With the envelopes frozen, run the analytic layer
of `fixture:cart-sill-dock` on `batch-cpu` (lockstep and strict) and on each admitted device tier: every analytic
row must agree with the referee's value inside the frozen envelope for its quantity; the creep rigs (commanded
speed 0.02 m/s) must flip outcome between the friction grid points that bracket the analytic threshold, on a grid
of spacing 0.01 that excludes points within 0.01 of the threshold on the fast tiers (TARGET; 0.005 on the
referee); a run exactly at the threshold carries the `boundary` flag; both negative controls are caught: the
wheel-loop row under explicit Euler that updates the current before the wheel speed at a 0.5 ms step (it reports
a spectral radius of 1.497 where 0.9207 is true), and a creep rig with the rear lip removed.

**7. KILL-CONTACT.** Trigger: GATE-FID-01 fails on slip or creep after parameter mapping and one repair cycle.
Fallback: CL-2 (Hunt–Crossley normal law with regularized friction in convex form) becomes the default at a
published lower throughput; if that also fails, locomotion claims are restricted to the referee tier and the fast
tiers are labelled exploratory. Decided at M2 as an ADR. This bead produces the slip-distance and creep rows the
decision reads and does not close until the ADR is on record.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Pair rows exist for every battery address of every reference class on (`batch-cpu`, `referee`), with the
      adjudicator recorded per pair.
- [ ] GATE-FID-01 is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with the measured median and 95th percentile per
      quantity and class against 2%/5%, 3%/8% and 5%/12% (TARGET), by a session that did not implement the work.
- [ ] At most one re-baseline document exists; if it exists it lists every changed threshold with its rationale,
      and the gate row cites its hash.
- [ ] Every stratum of the M2 battery has a frozen envelope whose hash is in the ledger; `fidelity.ledger`
      prints the bound and the freeze date.
- [ ] The promotion admission check (a library function `require_frozen_envelope` at M2, reached through
      `eval.promote` from M3) returns `FROB-REF-821` on an unfrozen stratum, with the repair "freeze the
      envelope first".
- [ ] Negative control caught: an envelope changed after outcomes exist is detected by hash mismatch and the
      dependent promotion is refused.
- [ ] GATE-KIN-07 is on record (N16b-03): `Degraded` world-steps at most 10⁻⁴ of world-steps per class on the
      battery, every forced overflow flagged, capacities printed in every disclosure block.
- [ ] GATE-ARENA-04, batch-tier rows: every analytic row inside the envelope, creep rigs flip at the stated grid
      points, both negative controls caught.
- [ ] KILL-CONTACT is decided and recorded as an ADR on the slip and creep rows measured here.
- [ ] `fidelity.ledger` is reachable through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`, with schema, exit codes and a tested
      cancellation of the measurement job (request, drain, finalize; no partial stratum published).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-dyn/tests/envelope.rs`:
- `envelope_hash_covers_floor_and_bound`: changing the bound from 0.05 to 0.051, or the floor from 1 cm to 2 cm,
  changes `EnvelopeId`.
- `freeze_is_immutable`: a second freeze of a frozen (stratum, quantity) is refused; the row is unchanged.
- `new_kernel_version_has_no_envelope`: the same class under a new kernel version returns `envelope: null`.
- `refusal_821_on_unfrozen`: `require_frozen_envelope` on a stratum with no envelope returns `FROB-REF-821`
  with the repair "freeze the envelope first" and exit code 3; on a frozen stratum it returns the `EnvelopeId`.
- `exceedance_bit`: with bound 5% on displacement, d = 4.9% gives 0 and d = 5.1% gives 1; d = 1% with a differing
  success bit gives 1.
- `rebaseline_once`: a second re-baseline request fails with a message naming the first document.
- `gate_fid01_evaluator`: synthetic strata (labelled `synthetic`) with medians 1.9%, 2.1% produce pass and fail.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/fidelity_ledger.sh` (N16b-30): runs the battery pairs on two tiers for the reference quadruped,
freezes, reads `fidelity.ledger`, attempts a promotion on an unfrozen stratum once the verb exists (M3; before
that the admission check is covered by the unit tests), and tampers with an envelope row in a scratch store.
Logged per step: command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block, the `BatteryId`, `EnvelopeId`s, per-quantity
median and 95th percentile with thresholds, and on failure the first address whose pair differs.

### Known answers and negative controls

- GATE-FID-01 thresholds as above; the gate records measured values, not the targets.
- CX-149: a state error inside the envelope with a different decision is an exceedance.
- Negative control (a-a, scripted adversary): "envelope chosen after the outcomes" (the control of KA-350): the
  script writes pairs, then edits the envelope; the hash check must catch it.
- Negative controls of GATE-ARENA-04: the explicit-Euler wheel-loop mutation (1.497 against 0.9207) and the creep
  rig without its rear lip.
- Live-dependency proof: perturbing CL-1's stiffness by a factor of two must change the stance-impulse
  distribution.
- Determinism lanes: the measurement twice on one machine and across worker counts gives identical pair rows on
  each tier (class D2 within a tier); the cross-tier comparison is D3.

## Considerations

- The rule for the envelopes of ungated quantities is a DESIGN choice of this bead and must be restated in the
  freeze record so that a reader of the ledger sees how each bound arose.
- FD-FSIM-02 (a persistent contact time stepper upstream) will change the referee; its posture is to re-baseline
  the fidelity rows against the new stepper as a new stratum, not to edit frozen envelopes.
- RISK-31 and BET-34: where the referee completes fewer than 90% of a class's scenarios, the strict profile is
  the named adjudicator for the remainder and the ledger rows say so.
- OPEN-KIN-6 (created-energy bound; owner Kinesis lead) is settled in the single re-baseline.
- Cost: one pass over three classes is about 570 scenarios × 20 s on each tier; the referee side is the cost
  (0.15 to 0.8 core-hours at 0.2 to 1.0 times real time), so the measurement can be repeated on every kernel
  change.
- Revisit if the 95th-percentile basis yields an exceedance rate on fresh addresses far from 5%: the basis is
  then wrong for that quantity and the stratum is re-measured as a new version, never edited.
