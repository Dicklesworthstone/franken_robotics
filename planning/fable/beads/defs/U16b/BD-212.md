## Background

This is the seed bead that turns the trust ladder from a design into measured numbers at the M2 exit. Its
done-condition is: GATE-FID-01, with the one permitted re-baseline written down; GATE-KIN-07; GATE-ARENA-04 on the
batch tiers; KILL-CONTACT decided.

FrankenRobotics runs one model on three tiers: the slow, most trusted `referee`; `batch-cpu`, the CPU reference of
the batched step (profiles `lockstep`, the fixed-step batched step, and `strict`, a transactional event-located step
in `f64`); and the GPU tiers. The fidelity ledger (strata, quantities, pair rows, summaries: N16b-02) records how
far a faster configuration lands from a slower, more trusted one on the standard battery (N38a-20: about 200
scenarios per robot class, content-addressed by its `BatteryId`). An **envelope** is the frozen tolerance within
which a faster tier must agree with a slower one for a result to be promoted; its record, freeze routine, tamper
check, the refusal `FROB-REF-821` and the read verb `fidelity.ledger` are built by N16bp1-01. Promotion (BD-263)
asks one question per audited episode: did the two tiers disagree beyond the frozen envelope? That question is only
honest if the envelope was fixed before the outcomes were seen. This bead produces the measured numbers those
envelopes are frozen from, at M2, once.

The bead also serves BET-03: convex compliant contact (law CL-1), audited by a referee, is good enough for
locomotion decisions. BET-03 is falsified if on the standard battery the fast tier disagrees with the referee
beyond the fidelity thresholds on slip distance, stance impulse or cost of transport and no parameter mapping
fixes it. KILL-CONTACT is the stop rule of that bet; it is decided by the contact chapter's decision bead N18b-33
on the measurements of this bead.

Rejected alternatives: silently widening a threshold when a gate fails (the plan permits exactly one re-baseline,
with a written rationale, then freezes); measuring on a hand-picked subset (the battery is frozen and addressed so
that every tier is measured on the same scenarios); re-using the M1 referee-only numbers (they say nothing about
the batched step).

## Scope

In: the M2 measurement of `batch-cpu` against `referee` on every address of the frozen battery of each reference
class; the gate evaluation and the GATE-FID-01 record; the single re-baseline document; freezing the M2 envelopes
with N16bp1-01's routine; re-evaluating the batch-tier rows of fixture 1 (GATE-ARENA-04) against the frozen
envelopes and recording that half of the gate (its one recorder: N24b-09, which cannot see a frozen envelope,
supplies the rows and a receipt against its provisional bands); recording the slip-distance and creep rows
KILL-CONTACT is decided on; writing and freezing the `coefficient-lag` rows of each reference class; confirming that GATE-KIN-07 is on record (measured by N16b-03).
Out: the battery definition (N38a-20); ledger tables and comparator (N16b-02); the pairing hook of the battery run
(N16bp2-01); the lag accumulator (N18a-29); the envelope record, freeze routine,
tamper check, `FROB-REF-821` and the verb `fidelity.ledger` (N16bp1-01); the referee's own coverage report
(BD-213); promotion (BD-263); the created-energy audit rules (N16b-14), except that their bound is re-baselined in
the same document; the decision on that bound (N16c-11, OPEN-KIN-6) and the decision on the default step and
iteration budget (N16a-16, OPEN-KIN-1); the lowering of fixture 1's rigs into batched worlds and its per-tier row
work (N24b-09); the KILL-CONTACT decision record (N18b-33, which depends on this bead).

This seed bead is the capstone of its components: it depends on N38a-20 (battery), N16b-02 (ledger tables),
N16bp1-01 (envelope mechanism and verb), N16b-03 (the GATE-KIN-07 record, which in turn waits on the capacity
decision N16a-15) and N24b-09 (the batch-tier rows of fixture 1); and its one re-baseline is made after the two
decisions N16a-16 and N16c-11 are on file, whose records the re-baseline document cites. No component and neither
decision depends on this bead.

## Technical approach

**1. Measure.** Run every address of the frozen battery of each reference class on `batch-cpu` under the
`lockstep` profile and on `referee` (same address, and therefore the same scene draw, disturbances, randomization
and policy; the pairs are formed by the pairing hook of the battery run, N16bp2-01). Where the referee refuses by name (contact cap
`FROB-REF-721`, FrankenSim's fixed-witness gate, a missing joint class, a kinematic loop), the `strict` profile of
`batch-cpu` adjudicates and the pair records `adjudicator = batch-cpu:strict`. A slow-tier world that ends
`numerical-fault` yields no pair and is counted as unpaired by cause. Write pair rows and stratum summaries
(N16b-02).

**2. Compare with the gate (GATE-FID-01, TARGET).** Per reference class, median and 95th-percentile disagreement:

| Quantity | Median at most | 95th percentile at most |
|---|---|---|
| base displacement over 2 s | 2% | 5% |
| energy per metre | 3% | 8% |
| stance impulse | 5% | 12% |

Disagreement d is N16b-02's: abs(fast − slow) / max(abs(slow), floor), with the floors of its quantity registry
(1 cm for displacement, 1 J/m for energy per metre, 1 N·s for stance impulse). Percentiles come from the stratum's
integer histogram, so the gate's numbers are reproducible bit for bit.

The gate is run as the Gauntlet's parity entry, `FROB_TEST_SEED=17 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g2_parity
-- GATE-FID-01` (with `--features gauntlet-mutations` when its controls run): the entry submits the battery jobs of
step 1, evaluates the table above, performs steps 3 and 4 through the library routines, and writes a hash-named
receipt with the measured values per class and quantity and one line per negative control of the test plan. A
session that did not implement the work records the gate from that receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance
--example record_gate -- --gate GATE-FID-01 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19, the one writer of `registries/gates.toml`;
no script writes a gate row).

**3. Re-baseline, once.** If a threshold is missed after parameter mapping (fitting CL-1's stiffness, dissipation
time and friction to the referee's law on the contact battery of BD-168) and one repair cycle, the thresholds may
be re-baselined once. The re-baseline is a document `docs/adr/ADR-FID-01-rebaseline.md` stating, per quantity: the
measured distribution, the old and new threshold, the physical reason, and what was tried first. The created-energy
bound of the energy audit (1% of the absolute work exchanged in any 0.5 s window on a fast tier; 10⁻⁶ relative on
the referee) is re-baselined in the same document and at no other time, citing the decision record N16c-11; the
document also cites N16a-16's record of the default step and Newton budget the measurement ran under. The
document's hash is written with N16bp1-01's recorder, `cargo run -p frob-chronicle --example record_rebaseline --
--gate GATE-FID-01 --cause m2-baseline --doc docs/adr/ADR-FID-01-rebaseline.md`; after that, the
thresholds are frozen and a second re-baseline under that cause is refused by the store. (The only other causes
the store accepts are the two upstream changes of the referee's contact stepper, FD-FSIM-02 and FD-FSIM-12, once
each, and they freeze new strata without touching these.)

**4. Freeze the M2 envelopes.** With the gate evaluated (and the re-baseline, if any, recorded), freeze every
stratum of the M2 battery with N16bp1-01's routine, one ledger transaction per stratum, by the bound rule that
bead writes into each record: the three gated quantities at their frozen 95th-percentile thresholds (5%, 8%, 12%,
or the re-baselined values); every other quantity (creep distance among them) at its measured 95th percentile rounded up to two significant
digits; task decisions with no tolerance. The intrinsic `coefficient-lag` rows are written and frozen in the same
pass: during the `lockstep` run of each reference class the lag accumulator of N18a-29 is drained per world
address by the pairing hook, its serialized histograms are merged per (coefficient law, contact mode) into
intrinsic strata (N16b-02), and each is frozen at its measured 95th percentile (`basis = MeasuredP95`). This is
the plan's rule that the one-step lag of a state-dependent friction coefficient is a measured quantity in the
fidelity ledger (ADR-TRAC-06): N18a-29 serializes the rows, and this bead is where they enter the ledger. A class
whose battery exercises no state-dependent coefficient law records `coefficient_laws: 0` and no row. With the envelope at the battery's 95th percentile the battery's own
exceedance rate is about 5%, which is the rate p₀ that the promotion table treats as faithful. A new kernel
version, law, profile, step plan or battery version is afterwards a new stratum with no envelope, and promotion
there is refused with `FROB-REF-821` until it is measured and frozen in turn.

**5. Fixture 1 on the batch tiers (GATE-ARENA-04, batch rows).** N24b-09 lowers `fixture:cart-sill-dock` into
batched worlds and records its rows against provisional bands. Here, with the envelopes frozen, its analytic rows
on `batch-cpu` (lockstep and strict) and on each admitted device tier are re-evaluated against the frozen envelope
of their quantity: every analytic row must agree with the referee's value inside it; the creep rigs (commanded
speed 0.02 m/s) must flip outcome between the friction grid points that bracket the analytic threshold, on a grid
of spacing 0.01 that excludes points within 0.01 of the threshold on the fast tiers (TARGET; 0.005 on the
referee); a run exactly at the threshold carries the `boundary` flag; both negative controls are caught: the
wheel-loop row under explicit Euler that updates the current before the wheel speed at a 0.5 ms step (it reports a
spectral radius of 1.497 where 0.9207 is true), and a creep rig with the rear lip removed. A row inside N24b-09's
provisional band but outside the frozen envelope fails the gate honestly; the envelope is not widened. The
re-evaluation writes a receipt per tier, and the batch-tier part is recorded from it by a session that did not
implement the work, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ARENA-04 --receipt
<hash> --part M2` (the referee part is M1's).

**6. KILL-CONTACT measurements.** Trigger: GATE-FID-01 fails on slip or creep after parameter mapping and one
repair cycle. Fallback: CL-2 (Hunt–Crossley normal law with regularized friction in convex form) becomes the
default at a published lower throughput; if that also fails, locomotion claims are restricted to the referee tier
and the fast tiers are labelled exploratory. Decided at M2 as an ADR by N18b-33. This bead writes the slip-distance and
creep-distance rows per class and the creep-rig rows of step 5 into a decision input record
(`registries/decisions/KILL-CONTACT.input.toml`: per class, median and 95th percentile of slip-distance and of
creep-distance disagreement, the creep brackets per tier, whether parameter mapping and the repair cycle were applied, and the
ledger frontier) that N18b-33 reads. The seed's done-condition "KILL-CONTACT decided" is met when N18b-33 records
its ADR on this input; the decision is never made here, so the capstone does not wait on its own successor.

**7. Cancellation.** The measurement is a daemon job: request, drain, finalize; a cancelled job publishes no
partial stratum summary and freezes nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Pair rows exist for every battery address of every reference class on (`batch-cpu`, `referee`), with the
      adjudicator recorded per pair and numerical outcomes counted as unpaired by cause.
- [ ] GATE-FID-01 is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with the measured median and 95th percentile per
      quantity and class against 2%/5%, 3%/8% and 5%/12% (TARGET), from the receipt of the `g2_parity` entry with
      `record_gate`, by a session that did not implement the work; the receipt shows both controls caught.
- [ ] At most one re-baseline document exists; if it exists it lists every changed threshold with its rationale,
      cites the decision records of N16a-16 and N16c-11, and the gate row cites its hash.
- [ ] Every stratum of the M2 battery has a frozen envelope whose hash is in the ledger;
      `frob fidelity ledger --class <c> --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --json` prints the bound, its basis and the
      freeze date for each.
- [ ] `frob fidelity ledger --class <c> --tier-pair batch-cpu,batch-cpu --quantity coefficient-lag --json` prints,
      for each reference class, one frozen row per (coefficient law, contact mode) the battery exercised, with n,
      median, 95th percentile and its `EnvelopeId`, or `coefficient_laws: 0` for a class that exercised none.
- [ ] Negative control caught: an envelope changed after outcomes exist is detected by hash mismatch and the
      dependent promotion admission returns `FROB-REF-821`.
- [ ] GATE-KIN-07 is on record (N16b-03): `Degraded` world-steps at most 10⁻⁴ of world-steps per class on the
      battery, every forced overflow flagged, capacities printed in every disclosure block.
- [ ] GATE-ARENA-04, batch-tier rows: every analytic row inside its frozen envelope, creep rigs flip at the stated
      grid points, both negative controls caught; the measured values are recorded per row and tier as the M2 part
      of the gate.
- [ ] The KILL-CONTACT input record exists with the slip and creep rows; N18b-33 can be started on it.
- [ ] A cancelled measurement job leaves no partial stratum and no envelope.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-dyn/tests/fidelity_gate.rs` (the envelope mechanism's own tests are N16bp1-01's):
- `gate_fid01_evaluator`: synthetic strata (labelled `synthetic`) with displacement medians 1.9% and 2.1% (95th
  percentiles 4.0%) produce pass and fail; a stratum at exactly 2.0% passes (thresholds are "at most").
- `gate_uses_rebaselined_thresholds`: with a recorded re-baseline raising the energy-per-metre 95th percentile from
  8% to 9%, a stratum at 8.5% passes and the verdict names the document hash.
- `freeze_after_gate`: the measurement driver freezes no stratum before the gate evaluation and the re-baseline
  record (if any) are written (ledger sequence numbers ordered).
- `unpaired_by_cause`: a referee refusal `FROB-REF-721` with a strict-profile adjudication yields a pair with
  `adjudicator = batch-cpu:strict`; a strict-profile `numerical-fault` yields no pair and one unpaired count.
- `kill_contact_input_record`: the input record lists per class the slip-distance and creep-distance median and
  95th percentile and the six creep brackets per tier; a missing class fails the writer.
- `coefficient_lag_rows_frozen`: a `synthetic` battery run with two coefficient laws in two contact modes yields
  four intrinsic strata, each frozen with `basis = MeasuredP95`; a run with no state-dependent law yields none and
  the class reports `coefficient_laws: 0`.
- `cancel_publishes_nothing`: a job cancelled after half the addresses leaves no summary row and no envelope.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/fidelity_ledger.sh` (N16b-30): runs the battery pairs on two tiers for the reference quadruped,
freezes, reads `fidelity.ledger`, attempts a promotion on an unfrozen stratum once the verb exists (M3; before
that the admission check is covered by N16bp1-01's unit tests), and tampers with an envelope row in a scratch
store. Logged per step: command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block, the `BatteryId`, `EnvelopeId`s,
per-quantity median and 95th percentile with thresholds and units, the adjudicator counts, and on failure the first
address whose pair differs between two runs, with the paths of both pair rows.

### Known answers and negative controls

- GATE-FID-01 thresholds as above; the gate records measured values, not the targets.
- CX-149: a state error inside the envelope with a different decision is an exceedance.
- Negative control (scripted adversary): "envelope chosen after the outcomes" (the control of KA-350): the script
  writes pairs, then edits the envelope; the hash check must catch it.
- Negative controls of GATE-ARENA-04: the explicit-Euler wheel-loop mutation (1.497 against 0.9207) and the creep
  rig without its rear lip.
- Live-dependency proof: perturbing CL-1's stiffness by a factor of two must change the stance-impulse
  distribution; a gate that the perturbation cannot move validates nothing.
- Determinism lanes: the measurement twice on one machine and across worker counts gives identical pair rows on
  each tier (class D2 within a tier); the cross-tier comparison is D3.

## Considerations

- The rule for the envelopes of ungated quantities is a DESIGN choice restated in each freeze record (N16bp1-01),
  so a reader of the ledger sees how each bound arose.
- FD-FSIM-02 (a persistent contact time stepper upstream) will change the referee; its posture is to measure the
  new stepper as a new stratum, not to edit frozen envelopes.
- RISK-31 and BET-34: where the referee completes fewer than 90% of a class's scenarios, the strict profile is
  the named adjudicator for the remainder and the ledger rows say so.
- OPEN-KIN-6 (created-energy bound; owner Kinesis lead) is settled in the single re-baseline (decision N16c-11).
- Cost: one pass over three reference classes is about 600 scenarios (200, 208 and 198 per class) × 20 s, so
  12,000 world-seconds on each tier; the referee side is the cost: 3.3 to 16.7 core-hours at 1.0 to 0.2 times real
  time per core, which is 0.14 to 0.69 hours of wall-clock on 24 cores. The measurement can therefore be repeated
  on every kernel change.
- Revisit if the 95th-percentile basis yields an exceedance rate on fresh addresses far from 5%: the basis is
  then wrong for that quantity and the stratum is re-measured as a new version, never edited.
