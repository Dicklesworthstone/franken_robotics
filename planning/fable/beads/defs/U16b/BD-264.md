## Background

Promotion (BD-263) says whether a fast-tier result is faithful. It does not say what the number would have been on
the higher tier. The tier-correction ledger supplies that number from the pairs promotion already pays for, with
an estimator that stays unbiased when the fast tier is wrong. ADR-KIN-04: promotion returns an estimate of the
higher-tier value with a correction and an interval, as well as a label, because a pass or fail label discards the
measured tier bias.

The construction (C-25, "fidelity tiers are coupled estimators"): the same world address run on two tiers gives a
coupled pair with correlation ρ. Three estimators use the coupling: a control variate, a multilevel telescoping
sum, and a doubly robust correction in which the system itself draws which addresses to audit. Because the audit
probability is set by the system and logged, not estimated, the expectation of the corrected term equals the
expected higher-tier outcome whatever the cheap prediction is: a poor prediction widens the interval and cannot
bias it. Tier: Solid as mathematics; Frontier for its use across contact-rich tiers (pilot: N16b-07).

Honest limits, stated before any measurement. The saving in higher-tier episodes is at most 1/(1 − ρ²): 1.96 at
ρ = 0.7, 5.3 at 0.9, 10 at 0.95, 25 at 0.98. Paired worlds decouple under contact, so the plan expects about two.
The estimator is adopted because it is nearly free, not because it is a large factor. Rejected alternatives: a
unit-coefficient correction with no audit design (it does not have the quoted variance and is biased when targets
are chosen after seeing fast-tier results); a learned correction of the fast tier (it hides the artifacts the
ladder exists to expose); clipping inverse weights (it introduces bias; a floor on the audit probability bounds
the weights instead).

Seed done-condition: GATE-CHRON-09; GATE-FID-08; KILL-DR decided.

## Technical approach

**Ledger row (`tier_correction`, schema `frob.tier-correction.row/1`).** Key: model class, scene class, task,
quantity, tier pair, kernel versions, effort profile, battery version. Measures: number of paired addresses; mean
and variance on each tier; mean and variance of the difference; per-episode correlation ρ_ep (separately for
continuous metrics and binary outcomes); regression coefficient β and intercept c; the audit probabilities used;
the effective sample size; the share of the stratum's scenarios the referee can complete; estimator version;
ledger frontier. The first rows are imported from the pilot records of N16b-07.

**The estimator.** N addresses ran on the fast tier; Y⁰ᵢ is the fast outcome at address i (for a comparison, the
paired difference B − A at that address).
1. Before any higher-tier outcome of the round exists, fix a prediction mᵢ = ĝ(Y⁰ᵢ). ĝ is the affine map
   c + β·Y⁰ᵢ the ledger holds for that quantity and stratum (from earlier rounds only), and the identity
   (β = 1, c = 0) in a new stratum. ĝ is frozen and hashed first.
2. Fix an audit probability pᵢ ≥ p_min = 1/20 for each address and log it. DESIGN default: constant
   p = max(1/20, min(1, B/N)) for a higher-tier budget B; if N·p would exceed B in expectation, only the first
   B/p addresses of the schedule take part. A declared stratified design may raise pᵢ for chosen addresses (for
   example fast-tier failures), provided every pᵢ is fixed and logged before the draw.
3. Draw Aᵢ ~ Bernoulli(pᵢ) from the counter-based stream keyed by `(WorldAddr, promotion_id)` (stream name
   `referee-audit`). Persist the assignment before dispatch; it survives retries.
4. Where Aᵢ = 1, run the same address on the higher tier (same scene draw, disturbances, randomization and
   policy) to get Y¹ᵢ.
5. Form Zᵢ = mᵢ + Aᵢ·(Y¹ᵢ − mᵢ)/pᵢ and report θ̂ = (1/N)·Σᵢ Zᵢ with a confidence sequence valid at any stopping
   time. The schedule is walked in its fixed hashed order and the sequence is updated at every address (Zᵢ = mᵢ
   when Aᵢ = 0), so stopping early against a declared indifference margin is valid. Zᵢ is bounded once the metric
   has a span: |Zᵢ − mᵢ| ≤ |Y¹ᵢ − mᵢ|/p_min, so the weights 1/pᵢ are bounded by 20 with no clipping and no
   clipping bias.

**Properties to preserve.** E[Aᵢ/pᵢ] = 1 given everything fixed before the draw, so E[Zᵢ | past] is the expected
higher-tier outcome for any frozen ĝ (PO-KIN-05, PO-CHRON-02). With mᵢ = 0 the estimate is the plain
inverse-probability mean. With every pᵢ = 1 it is the plain paired higher-tier mean, exactly: this is the
regression test. With no audited address it is refused. Variance: about
(1/N)·[Var(Y¹) + E((1 − p)/p·(Y¹ − m)²)]; for a constant audit fraction n/N, N much larger than n and the best
linear prediction this is (1 − ρ²)·Var(Y¹)/n.

**Control-variate form.** θ̂_cv = Ȳ¹(n) − β·(Ȳ⁰(n) − Ȳ⁰(N)); at the optimal β its variance is
Var(Y¹)/n·(1 − ρ²·(1 − n/N)). Example: n = 64, N = 6,400, ρ = 0.9 gives a ratio of 0.198, a factor of 5.0. It is
reported beside the doubly robust estimate when the audit fraction is constant; the two agree in expectation.
FACT: FrankenSim's `fs-uq` implements linear control variates and multilevel allocation for a per-call closure; a
batched entry point is upstream work item FD-FSIM-17 (P2). Interim posture: the coupled estimators are ours and
`fs-uq` supplies allocation and control-variate arithmetic only; switch-over rule: re-run GATE-CHRON-09 with the
sibling driver and keep ours if the intervals differ.

**Limits and fallbacks.** Budget: 256 higher-tier episodes per promotion (DESIGN); on exhaustion the result stays
`provisional`. The estimator is switched off for a stratum, and promotion falls back to the plain paired
e-process on higher-tier episodes alone, when the effective sample size (Σ wᵢ)²/Σ wᵢ² (wᵢ = Aᵢ/pᵢ over audited
addresses) falls under 30% of the audited count, or when ρ_ep is under 0.7 on at least 200 paired addresses.
The receipt names the trigger. Loss constants in the contract (DESIGN): wrong establishment 100, a higher-tier
episode 1, a wrong refusal 10.

**Refusal.** `FROB-REF-811`: a tier-corrected estimate requested across identities (pairs from two model, kernel
or battery identities), with no audited address, or below the effective-sample floor. Repair: higher-tier
episodes alone. Exit 3.

**Receipt fields added to `frob.promotion/1`.** `estimate { method: "dr", theta, interval, level, rho,
replaced_count, ess_share, p_min, g_hash, n_audited, N }`, where `replaced_count` is the number of higher-tier
episodes a higher-tier-only mean would have needed for the same variance, V̂ar(Y¹)/V̂ar(θ̂), printed beside
`n_audited`. A promotion in a stratum the referee cannot complete returns `provisional: referee-unavailable`.

**Invariants.** INV-CHRON-13: the audit set of a round is fixed before any higher-tier outcome of the round
exists, and every audit probability is at least the floor.

**Layering.** Estimator in `frob-opt::promote::tier_correction`; rows in `frob-chronicle`; class of outputs:
Tested; determinism: a deterministic function of addresses and outcomes.

      the paired mean at pᵢ = 1 (exact, bitwise on the same summation order); the planted between-tier reversal

      established verdict reported at the 50th, 95th and 99th percentiles against 16 / 64 / 256 (TARGET).
- [ ] The measurements KILL-DR is decided on are recorded: ρ_ep per stratum on at least 200 paired addresses, and
      the coverage figure of GATE-CHRON-09 (the decision itself is N16b-10).

- [ ] The switch-off rule fires on a synthetic stratum with ρ = 0.5 and on one with effective sample size 25% of
      the audited count, and the receipt names the trigger.
- [ ] `FROB-REF-811` in its three cases.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: audit probabilities logged after the draw; audit bits chosen after seeing
      fast-tier failures.
- [ ] `eval.promote --method dr` works through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`; cancellation tested.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/tier_correction.rs`:
- `p_one_equals_paired_mean`: N = 200, all pᵢ = 1: θ̂ equals mean(Y¹) exactly.
- `m_zero_is_ipw`: mᵢ = 0: θ̂ = (1/N)·Σ Aᵢ·Y¹ᵢ/pᵢ.
- `constant_offset_recovered`: Y¹ = Y⁰ + 0.2 with identity ĝ: θ̂ − mean(Y⁰) → 0.2 (within the sequence).
- `unbiased_by_enumeration`: Y ∈ {0, 1} with mean θ, constant m, p = 0.1: E[Z] = m + p·(θ − m)/p = θ for
  m ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1}, by exact enumeration of the four outcomes.
- `added_variance`: perfect prediction adds 0; m wrong by a constant ½ with p = 0.1 adds 9 × ¼ = 2.25.
- `weights_bounded_by_20`: every 1/pᵢ ≤ 20; a requested p of 0.01 is raised to 0.05 and logged.
- `ess_trigger`, `rho_trigger`, `refusal_811_cases`.
- `null_optional_stopping`: 10⁴ seeded null comparisons from the synthetic generator of N16b-06, stopping at the
  first crossing: false-establishment frequency at most α within the exact binomial acceptance bound.
- `wrong_prediction_same_mean`: ĝ replaced by a constant, by −Y⁰ and by noise: the mean of θ̂ over 10⁴ rounds is
  unchanged within 3σ; the interval widens.
- `cv_variance_ratio`: n = 64, N = 6,400, ρ = 0.9 gives 0.198 ± sampling error.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/promotion.sh` (N16b-31) runs `frob eval promote r:… --method dr --json` on plant B and on an honest
comparison, and `--method paired` beside it. Logged: g_hash, p_min, N, n_audited, θ̂ with interval and units, ρ,
`replaced_count`, effective-sample share with the 30% threshold, the trigger if any, and on failure the first
address whose audit bit differs from the persisted assignment.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-351: equals the paired mean at pᵢ = 1; the inverse-probability mean at mᵢ = 0; recovers a constant tier
  offset; type-I error at most α; a planted reversal is not established. Control: audit probabilities logged
  after the draw.
- KA-133 (unbiasedness by enumeration), KA-134 (added variance 2.25), KA-135 (stopping error at or below α over
  10⁴ null studies), KA-719 (every address audited equals the plain mean; a prediction off by a constant is still
  unbiased), KA-132 (control-variate variance).
- CX-062 and CX-324: a cheap prediction that is only roughly right costs variance (2.25, worse than auditing
  everything); audit bits chosen after the outcomes bias the estimate.
- Metamorphic MR-33: the tier-corrected estimate has the same mean for every fast-tier prediction; with every
  address audited it is the plain mean.
- Determinism lanes: identical receipts twice on one machine, across worker counts, debug and release.

## Considerations

- KILL-DR (decision bead N16b-10). The register resolves the two statements of this kill gate: the trigger is
  ρ_ep below 0.7 on 200 paired addresses in every stratum tested, or GATE-CHRON-09 coverage failing after one
  repair cycle; the fallback is that promotion reports the label only, from higher-tier episodes alone, while
  the ledger keeps recording ρ_ep. The effective-sample-size clause is a recorded diagnostic, not a trigger.
- The plan prints two different sets of loss constants for the promotion decision; they belong to OPEN-ATEL-1
  and do not change the estimator.
- Inverse weights can consume the budget: with p = 1/20 one audited address stands for twenty; the budget rule
  above bounds the expected audits, and the receipt prints both N and n_audited.
- The background referee (BD-214) draws its samples with recorded inclusion probabilities, so they can serve as
  the audited half of this estimator and take confirmation off the wall-clock critical path without reducing
  its cost.
- Revisit if GATE-CHRON-09 coverage on the battery falls short: one repair cycle (wider sequence, or ĝ refit on
  more history), then the kill gate.
