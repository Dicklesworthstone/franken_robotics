## Background

A fidelity envelope bounds how far a number moves between tiers; a design studio also needs to know whether the
order of designs moves. This bead measures rank agreement between physics tiers and gives the measurement
consequences, per stratum of design space, because a cheap tier is biased toward designs that are easy to control
and the bias is not uniform.

The same record type serves the controller rungs of the fidelity ladder (baseline controller, family policy,
fine-tuned policy, specialist), which the studio allocates; Kinesis owns the tier-pair rows. HYPOTHESIS: tier
pairs agree far better than controller rungs, since they share the controller. Tier: Frontier, behind the feature
`frontier-rank-agreement`; pilot N16b-11. Ceiling: a rung that only discards saves at most the fraction it
discards.

Rejected alternatives: one global correlation number per tier pair (it hides the non-uniform bias); selecting
finalists on the fast tier on faith (successive halving on faith); a plain Kendall τ over all designs (the
decision is made near the top, so the top band and top-k recall are what the thresholds read).

Seed done-condition: GATE-FID-04; KILL-RANK decided for tier pairs.

## Scope

In: the design-set draw, paired scoring on two tiers, the statistics, the conformal margin, the per-stratum
permitted use, the ledger rows, the verb, GATE-FID-04, and the measurements of the tier-pair reading of
KILL-RANK.
Out: the rung-pair experiment and its modes (BD-310, BD-328); the decision record (N16b-12); the pilot (N16b-11).

## Technical approach

**Procedure (numbered).**
1. Design set: at least 40 designs in one robot class, drawn by a keyed counter-based stream before any score
   exists (the pre-registration of BD-145 fixes the set, the strata and the thresholds 0.7 and 0.5). The list hash
   is written to the ledger first.
2. One fixed controller for the whole set.
3. Each design is scored on the fast tier and on the higher tier at the same addresses. For the gate: 256 paired
   episodes per design, from which the per-episode correlation ρ is reported. Score = mean task metric over the
   addresses. The adjudicator per address is recorded.
4. Per stratum of design space compute and store the record (schema `frob.rank-agreement/1`, table
   `rank_agreement`):

```
key        robot class · task family · pair (lo, hi) [a tier pair here] · stratum
           policy and kernel versions · effort profile · battery version
measures   n_designs
           Kendall τ = (concordant − discordant pairs)/C(n, 2), with a bootstrap interval
           top-weighted τ: τ on the top band (designs in the top m of either tier, m = max(10, 3k))
           top-k recall of lo against hi, k = 10
           Spearman ρ over the stratum
           mean signed residual per region (the bias of the fast tier)
           conformal margin q̂ at level α · exchangeability martingale value
           per-episode cross-tier correlation ρ_ep on the paired addresses
           mode: selective | discard-only | uninformative     (the permitted use)
           mode_reason: measured | unmeasured | winner-lost | calibration-invalid
provenance design-list hash · pre-registration · claim_id · recording session
```

The record type and its `mode` values are the ones the rung-pair measurement writes into the same `rank_agreement`
table (BD-328, SPEC-C-26): one table, one vocabulary, so a consumer reads a tier-pair row and a rung-pair row the
same way. §16.9's three uses ("select finalists", "discard only", "not used for ranking") are these three modes.

5. Bootstrap: 2,000 seeded resamples of designs, percentile intervals at 95%. All thresholds are applied to the
   **lower** interval bound.
6. Conformal margin: on the n designs that ran both tiers, q̂ is the ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉-th smallest residual
   |y_hi − ĝ(y_lo)|, with ĝ the frozen affine map of the stratum. With n = 39 and α = 0.05 it is the 38th
   smallest; fewer than 19 designs cannot support α = 0.05, because ⌈(n + 1)·0.95⌉ then exceeds n, and such a
   stratum is `unmeasured`.

**Consequences (TARGET thresholds on the lower interval bound).**

| Mode | Top-weighted τ | Top-10 recall | Further conditions | What the fast tier may do in that stratum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `selective` | ≥ 0.7 | ≥ 0.8 | calibration valid (at least 19 designs at α = 0.05, exchangeability martingale below 20); the planted winner kept | Select finalists. Every design within 2·q̂ of the k-th best fast score still goes up. |
| `discard-only` | 0.5 to 0.7 (or ≥ 0.7 with recall < 0.8 or calibration invalid) | any | — | Discard only. It may drop the clearly dominated and may not choose among the rest. |
| `uninformative` | < 0.5, or unmeasured, or the planted winner lost in this stratum | any | — | Not used for ranking. Every candidate that survives static analysis is scored on the higher tier, and the campaign estimate says so before it starts. |

A stratum with τ ≥ 0.7 and recall below 0.8 is `discard-only` (`mode_reason = measured`); one with τ ≥ 0.7 whose
calibration is invalid is `discard-only` (`calibration-invalid`). An unmeasured stratum is `uninformative`
(`unmeasured`; fail closed), and so is a stratum whose audit shows the planted winner discarded, whatever its τ
(`winner-lost`). Consumers are refused by the codes shared with the rung ladder: selecting or discarding on a tier
pair without a record that meets its threshold, `FROB-REF-537`; asking the fast tier to select in a stratum whose
mode is not `selective`, `FROB-REF-545`; pooling rank evidence or calibration across two model identities,
`FROB-REF-548` (repair: restart the rows in the new identity). The measurement itself refuses a design set that
holds a design not drawn by step 1's keyed stream (calibration from survivors: the margin of step 6 would be
computed on designs chosen after scores), `FROB-REF-546`, raised by N28b-14's calibration input check, which the
margin of step 6 goes through. All exit 3.

**Planted-population fixtures (DESIGN, the certifier check's inputs).** The planted populations of the certifier
check are registered as named fixtures of the row's `fixture` input, one TOML file each under
`crates/frob-opt/fixtures/rank_agreement/<name>.toml` (`name`, `synthetic = true`, `class`, the strata, per
design and stratum the scores `y_lo` and `y_hi`, the planted top-weighted τ, and the declared `mode` and
`mode_reason`): `tier-pair-planted-0.8` (`selective`, recall at least 0.8, calibration valid, winner kept),
`tier-pair-planted-0.6` (`discard-only`), `tier-pair-planted-0.3` (`uninformative`), each with `mode_reason =
measured`, and `tier-pair-winner-lost` (top-weighted τ above 0.7 with the planted winner scored poorly on the
fast tier: `uninformative`, `winner-lost`). The scores of each are chosen so that the lower bootstrap bound of
step 5 (2,000 resamples, keyed by the fixture name) lies inside the band of its declared mode; the registration
test below recomputes every fixture and fails if a computed mode differs from the declared one. The form that
reads them, `frob eval rank-agreement --fixture <name> --class <c> --key <k> --json` (runs nothing, tier
`static`, every output labelled `synthetic`), is N23b-35's fixture form, which loads this directory and adds its
own `forty-design-winner-third` to it.

**Verb.** DESIGN default for the registered name (the registry admits one name per mechanism): the measurement
is reached as `eval.rank-agreement` with a tier pair, kind `run`, first milestone M3 for tier pairs:
`frob eval rank-agreement --designs <set> --pair tier:batch-cpu,referee --class quadruped --key ra-1 --json` (a
`run` verb: without `--key` it is refused with `FROB-REF-805`). The dynamics
chapter's verb table and one registry row call the same measurement `fidelity.rank`; N16b-27 carries that naming
question to the owner and both spellings are covered by this one implementation. Input schema
`frob.eval.rank-agreement.in/1` (`designs`, `pair`, `class`, `task`, `episodes_per_design`, `controller`,
`idempotency_key`, budget); output schema `frob.eval.rank-agreement.out/1`: the records with `mode` and
`mode_reason` per stratum, the per-design scores, and `next_actions`. Exit 0; exit 2 when any stratum is
`uninformative`; exit 8 while running. The row's `first_milestone` stays M4 (the rung pairs of BD-328); from M3
`frob capabilities --json` lists the verb as implemented with `form: "tier-pair"`, and a rung pair offered before
BD-328 lands answers `FROB-REF-724` naming the form (VERBS-eval).

**GATE-FID-04.** Rank agreement between `batch-cpu` and the referee on the 40-design set per reference class, with
the permitted use per stratum written to the ledger; per-episode ρ on 256 paired episodes per design reported.

**Compute.** 40 designs × 256 episodes × 20 s = 204,800 higher-tier world-seconds per class: 2.4 to 11.9 hours on
24 cores at 0.2 to 1.0 times real time per core. It needs the envelope requested in the pre-registration.

**Layering.** Statistics in `frob-opt::rank` (layer 4); paired scoring through the battery runner and the
cross-tier replay; rows in `frob-chronicle`. Outputs: claim Fidelity, class Tested, determinism D3 across tiers.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FID-04 recorded per reference class: τ, top-weighted τ, top-10 recall with intervals, ρ on 256 paired
      episodes per design, and `mode` (the permitted use) per stratum in the ledger.
- [ ] The design list and thresholds are in the ledger before the first score (ordering asserted).
- [ ] The mode table is applied exactly, on lower bounds, including the τ ≥ 0.7 with recall < 0.8 case, the
      invalid-calibration case, the lost-winner case and the unmeasured case; a tier-pair row and a rung-pair row
      of BD-328 are read by one reader without a mapping.
- [ ] A consumer that orders a population on an `uninformative` stratum is refused (`FROB-REF-537`); one that asks
      a `discard-only` stratum to select is refused (`FROB-REF-545`); pooling across two model identities is
      refused (`FROB-REF-548`); a design set holding a design not drawn by the keyed stream is refused
      (`FROB-REF-546`).
- [ ] The four planted-population fixtures are registered under `crates/frob-opt/fixtures/rank_agreement/`, each
      recomputing to its declared mode.
- [ ] KILL-RANK, tier-pair reading: the share of strata with top-weighted τ below 0.5 is recorded for the
      reference classes, ready for the decision bead N16b-12.
- [ ] Planted populations with known rank correlation on each side of 0.7 and 0.5 receive the prescribed
      `permitted_use`, and the interval covers the planted value.
- [ ] A planted winner that the fast tier represents poorly stops the fast tier from discarding in that stratum.
- [ ] The verb is reachable through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`; cancellation publishes no partial record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/rank_agreement.rs`:
- `kendall_exact`: orders (1, 2, 3, 4) and (1, 3, 2, 4) give 0.667; ties handled by the τ-b convention and
  documented.
- `top_band_m`: k = 10, n = 40 gives m = 30; k = 4 gives m = 12 capped by n.
- `conformal_index`: n = 39, α = 0.05 selects the 38th smallest residual; n = 18 returns `unmeasured`.
- `two_q_hat_rule`: fast scores (10, 9.6, 9.5, 9.0, 8.0), k = 2, q̂ = 0.2: designs within 0.4 of 9.6 advance, so
  the first three advance and 9.0 does not.
- `mode_table`: lower bounds (0.72, 0.85) with valid calibration → `selective`; (0.72, 0.75) → `discard-only`;
  (0.72, 0.85) with martingale 25 → `discard-only` (`calibration-invalid`); (0.55, any) → `discard-only`;
  (0.45, any) → `uninformative`; no record → `uninformative` (`unmeasured`); (0.80, 0.90) with the planted winner
  discarded → `uninformative` (`winner-lost`).
- `bootstrap_seeded_and_covering`: planted τ = 0.6 at n = 40 is inside the interval in at least 93% of 1,000
  synthetic populations.
- `draw_before_scores`, `refusal_537_on_unmeasured`, `refusal_545_select_on_discard_only`,
  `refusal_548_pooling_two_identities`, `refusal_546_design_not_drawn` (a set with one design added after the
  draw is refused before any score).
- `planted_fixtures_registered`: each of the four fixture files parses, is labelled `synthetic`, and recomputed
  through the procedure gives its declared `mode` and `mode_reason` (0.8 → `selective`, 0.6 → `discard-only`,
  0.3 → `uninformative`, the lost winner → `uninformative`/`winner-lost`), with the planted τ inside the
  bootstrap interval; a fixture file whose declared mode is edited fails the test.
- `shared_vocabulary`: a row written by this bead deserializes with BD-328's reader and its `mode` round-trips.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/rank_and_modelform.sh` (N16b-32): scores a 40-design set of the corpus on `batch-cpu` and on
`batch-cpu:strict` standing in for the referee at a reduced episode count, runs the verb, and asserts the record
and the mode; then repeats with a planted population. Logged: design-list hash, n, τ, top-weighted τ,
recall and their intervals against 0.7, 0.5 and 0.8, the martingale value against 20, ρ, `mode` and `mode_reason`
per stratum, and on failure the first design whose two ranks differ most.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The Kendall and conformal arithmetic above (exact).
- Certifier check for this ledger: planted populations with known top-weighted rank correlation on each side of
  each threshold in force (the four registered fixtures above); the measured τ covers the planted value in its
  bootstrap interval and the mode assigned is the one the thresholds prescribe.
- Negative control (procedure): computing q̂ from the promoted designs only under-covers on a fixture where the
  residual correlates with the fast score; the test detects the under-coverage.
- Negative control (planted-design): the lost planted winner.
- CX-318 (rank agreement near the top can be assumed): published neighbours report rank correlations of 0.40
  overall and 0.50, 0.60, 0.83; the default for an unmeasured stratum is therefore "not used".
- Determinism lanes: identical records twice on one machine and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- Sample size per stratum: the conformal margin needs at least 19 designs in a stratum, so a class measured with
  the minimum of 40 designs supports at most two strata; more strata need more designs (max(40, 19·S) for S
  strata). This follows from the arithmetic above and should be reflected in the pre-registration.
- KILL-RANK. The register resolves the two statements: the kill gate proper concerns agreement between evaluation
  rungs and is owned by the studio (decided at M4). The tier-pair statement (top-weighted τ below 0.5 in more
  than half the strata for the reference classes; fallback: tier pairs promote individual results only, and no
  population is ordered across tiers) is the per-stratum rule of GATE-FID-04 and holds without a kill gate. The
  reading is recorded by N16b-12.
- Naming: the plan gives this verb two names; the default here follows the registry's one-name rule and the seed
  bead's title.
- RISK-27: if tier pairs disagree, cheap tiers cannot eliminate designs and campaign cost rises toward
  higher-tier-only; the campaign estimate must say so before it starts.
