## Background

A fidelity envelope and a promotion say that one number survives a change of tier. Designs are compared, so what
matters is whether the **ranking** survives. A ranking can be an artifact of the model form: of the compliance of
the contact law, of the shape of the friction cone, of a penalty stiffness, of the time step, or of solver
residual. The model-form rank check re-evaluates a ranking of finalists under variants that should not matter to
a physically meaningful preference, and types the result. Tier: Solid. It serves North-Star scenario NS-09 (a
design whose rank among finalists changes under another admissible contact law is labelled model-conditional and
may not spend prototype budget), BET-03 (falsified if the ranking of finalists changes under the model-form
check) and RISK-05 (the convex contact model's creep misleads wheel and stair results).

The same check is stage 5 of the co-design cascade (rank robustness of finalists, analysis A-19, BD-346) and the
last of the exploitation detectors' companions in training (BD-267). One implementation serves all three; the
registered verb is `fidelity.modelform`.

Rejected alternatives: flagging single policies only (a flag on one policy is not enough when designs are
compared); a single robustness score (the consumer needs to know which variant flipped the ranking, because that
names the experiment that would settle it); skipping the check to save compute (the same campaign with the stage
skipped reports the exploit as the winner).

Seed done-condition: GATE-FID-05; GATE-CORTEX-15.

additional order-of-the-top-two condition (BD-346); the detectors (BD-267).

## Technical approach

**Input.** A ranking from a final round: an ordered list of designs (or policies) with their scores, the base
step plan, the addresses they were scored on, and k (DESIGN default k = 8). The check runs on the top k and the
runners-up; DESIGN: the runners-up are ranks k + 1 to 2k, so 16 designs at the default, or all the remaining
designs where fewer than 2k were ranked; a ranking of fewer than k + 1 designs is refused as too short to test
(DESIGN: `FROB-REF-802`, the input fails its schema constraint `k < len(ranking)`; exit 3; repairs: lower `--k`,
or rank more designs).
Where the entries are trained policies (one specialist per design), each finalist is re-scored with its own
policy as it stands; nothing is retrained for a variant (the learning side, N23b-08, states why and tests it).

**Procedure (numbered).**
1. Build the variants of the base configuration (N16b-13): law, cone, stiffness ×½ and ×2, step h/2, tolerance.
   Each changes one thing. Unadmitted arms are listed `not-run` with their reason.
2. Budget: at most 10% of the campaign's compute (TARGET). Given the campaign's recorded compute, the number of
   admitted arms a and the 2k designs, choose the episodes per design per arm as
   E = floor(0.10 · campaign cost / (a · 2k · cost per episode)), capped at the final round's episode count and
   not below 16 (DESIGN floor); if 16 does not fit, arms are dropped in a fixed order (tolerance, stiffness ×2,
   stiffness ×½, step, law, and the cone arm last) and reported `not-run: budget`. The episode set is a prefix
   of the final round's address schedule, so the variants are paired with the base by address.
3. Re-score the 2k designs under each admitted variant on those addresses; rank them. Variant runs are placed on
   the tier of the ranking's base step plan (`batch-cpu` or a device tier; a `strict` arm on `batch-cpu`). A
   variant world that overflows its contact or row capacity follows the caller's overflow policy (input
   `on_overflow`): under `refuse` the check stops with `FROB-REF-722` (capacity overflow, refused; exit 5) when
   the world ran on `batch-cpu`, and with `FROB-REF-610` (exit 6, raised by N21b-23's device path and passed
   through unchanged) when it ran on `gpu-cuda` or `gpu-metal`; the two codes are not merged (KNOWN_DRIFTS 6), and
   both carry the repairs "raise the capacity, which is a new `StepPlanId`" and "run with `continue-flagged`".
   Under `continue-flagged` the episode is scored and carries `Degraded`, and the variant's overlap is reported
   with the count of degraded episodes beside it.
4. Top-k overlap per variant: |top-k under the base ∩ top-k under the variant|.
5. Energy audit: read the created-energy flags of every episode of the base ranking and of each variant
   (N16b-14). The audit is clean when no finalist's episodes carry `energy-created`.
6. Result (the one rule; every caller, the cascade and the learning side included, gets this verdict):
   - `model-robust { variants, top_k_overlap, energy_audit }` when, under every admitted variant, the top-k
     overlap is at least k − 1 and the order inside the top k survives within noise, and the energy audit is
     clean;
   - `model-conditional { flipped_by, rankings: {base, per variant} }` otherwise, where `flipped_by` lists each
     variant whose overlap fell below k − 1 or under which a settled pair reversed, and `energy-audit` if a
     finalist was flagged. A ranking that contains an `energy-created` result is `model-conditional` whatever
     the overlaps.
   Order within noise (DESIGN). The definition of the check gives the overlap and energy conditions; the
   learning chapter's statement of the same check adds that the top-k set "and its order within noise" must
   survive, without saying how noise is judged. One reading is fixed here so that the verb has one verdict: a
   pair of top-k designs counts as flipped only if its paired comparison was settled in the base configuration
   and is settled in the opposite direction under the variant (anytime-valid paired comparison on the common
   addresses of step 2, level 0.05); a pair that is unsettled in either configuration is noise and never flips
   a ranking. So two finalists that differ by less than noise yield `model-robust` with those two unordered.
   On results the two outcomes are the labels `model-robust` and `model-conditional`. A check with a `not-run`
   arm reports the arm; it may still return `model-robust` over the arms that ran, and says which did not.
7. A preference between two designs is stated only if it holds under both qualified contact laws.

**Consequences of `model-conditional`.**
- The ranking is reported as fidelity-dependent, with both orders.
- The result carries the condition `model-conditional` and cannot be promoted to `established`.
- It cannot authorize a build packet or spend prototype budget; the consuming verbs refuse (`FROB-REF-830`;
  citing it as established is `FROB-REF-820`; presenting it in a report, export or claim as a design comparison
  without the variant that flips it is `FROB-REF-557`; the precedence among the three is N16b-04's). These codes
  are raised by the consumers (N23b-08, `eval.promote`, the report verbs and the build and spend verbs) and stay
  on their rows only: this verb sets the condition and raises none of them, so its registry row lists the codes
  its handler can return, `FROB-REF-610` and `FROB-REF-722` (N28-REF; VERBS-fidelity's round-3 correction).
- The cheapest experiment that would settle it is named in `next_actions` (for a law flip: the same finalists on
  the referee; for a step flip: a step-refinement study; for a stiffness flip: a contact-parameter
  identification; for an energy flag: the offending contact mode and world).

**Cost meter.** TARGET 5 to 10% of a campaign; the gates' limit is 10%. The check meters its own device-seconds
and the round's campaign total and records the ratio in `cost.share`; above 10% the round is labelled
`modelform-over-budget` and the cost clause of either gate fails.

**Demotion rule.** If over 20 campaigns the overlap is at least 7 of 8 in 95% of them with no energy flag, the
check becomes a periodic audit (run on a declared fraction of campaigns, the fraction recorded in the policy).
The ledger holds one row per campaign: `top8_overlap_min` (the minimum over variants) and `energy_flag`. The
rule reads the last 20 rows: at least 19 of 20 with `top8_overlap_min ≥ 7` and no energy flag in any of the 20
switches the state from `every-campaign` to `periodic`. DESIGN default for "periodic", which the plan does not
fix (an open point for the owner of the fidelity ledger): one campaign in five, chosen by a keyed stream before
the campaign starts; the check returns to every campaign at the first `model-conditional` audit. The state is
in the ledger and on every campaign report, and the cascade's finalist stage reads it.

**Verb.** `fidelity.modelform` (kind `run`, first milestone M3, capability `fidelity:run`; it takes an
idempotency key and a budget, waits a bounded time and otherwise returns a job handle with exit 8).
`frob fidelity modelform --ranking <result> --k 8 --budget-share 0.10 --key mf-1 --json`, or `--study <id>` to
check the ranking of that study's final round (the registry example). Inputs (VERBS-fidelity's row): `--ranking`
or `--study`; `--k` (default 8); `--budget-share` (default 0.10); `--on-overflow refuse|continue-flagged`
(default `refuse`, the default for evidence-bearing evaluation of N21b-23; `reset` is 802, because a reset
episode has no score to rank). Input schema
`frob.fidelity.modelform.in/1`; output schema `frob.fidelity.modelform.out/1` (the registry's name), whose
`result` is the model-form record `frob.modelform/1`:
`{ ranking, k, variants: [{ family, arm, base_step_plan, step_plan, model_id, param_digest_base, param_digest,
overlay, admitted, reason, top_k_overlap, order }], energy_audit: { clean, flagged: [...] }, verdict:
"model-robust" | "model-conditional", flipped_by, cost: { share, episodes_per_arm }, next_actions }`; each variant
entry carries every field of N16b-13's `ModelFormVariant` record, so that a reader can audit that each variant
changed one thing (INV-KIN-07's digests) from the verb's own output. Exit 0 for `model-robust`, 2 for `model-conditional` or any `not-run` arm, 8 while running.

**Layering.** Orchestration in `frob-opt::modelform` (layer 4); variants from `frob-dyn`; flags from the energy
ledger. Output claim: Design comparison, class Tested; the verdict is a deterministic function of the scores.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FID-05: the planted pyramid-cone winner and the planted penetration-recovery winner are returned as
      `model-conditional` (flipped by `cone` and by `energy-audit` respectively); a genuine winner is
      `model-robust`; the measured cost is at most 10% of the campaign's compute. It runs as the Gauntlet entry
      `FROB_TEST_SEED=17 cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test g1_known_answers --
      GATE-FID-05` (KA-353, the two planted winners as its controls, both `caught` in the receipt), and a session
      that did not implement the check records it from the receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
      record_gate -- --gate GATE-FID-05 --receipt <hash>`.
- [ ] GATE-CORTEX-15 (the seed's done-condition): the planted box-cone and penetration-energy exploits flip a
      planted ranking and are typed `model-conditional`; a genuine winner stays `model-robust`; the cost is at
      most 10% of the campaign; a request to promote the conditional result fires `FROB-REF-830`. The gate is
      measured through this verb on rankings of trained policies and recorded by N23b-08, which also builds the
      830 check; this bead closes on GATE-FID-05 and its own tests, and the value of GATE-CORTEX-15 is on record
      when N23b-08 closes.
- [ ] A settled pair inside the top k that reverses under a variant gives `model-conditional` naming that
      variant even when the overlap is k; an unsettled pair that swaps does not.
- [ ] A check whose metered cost exceeds 10% of its campaign is labelled `modelform-over-budget`.
- [ ] Top-k overlap of exactly k − 1 under one variant returns `model-robust`; k − 2 returns
      `model-conditional` naming that variant.
- [ ] A `model-conditional` ranking prints both orders and names a settling experiment in `next_actions`.
- [ ] Unadmitted and over-budget arms are reported `not-run` with reasons; none is silently skipped.
- [ ] The demotion counter is recorded per campaign and the rule is evaluated from the ledger.
- [ ] Negative control caught: the same campaign with the check skipped reports the planted exploit as the
      winner; with the check it does not.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp` (task cluster `fidelity` and `inherit`); cancellation
      tested; `CONTRACT.md` updated.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/modelform_check.rs`:
- `overlap_threshold`: k = 8, overlaps (8, 7, 8, 7, 8) → `model-robust`; (8, 6, 8, 8, 8) → `model-conditional
  { flipped_by: [variant 2] }`.
- `energy_flag_blocks_robust`: all overlaps 8 with one finalist flagged → `model-conditional
  { flipped_by: [energy-audit] }`.
- `budget_episode_count`: campaign cost 10,000 units, a = 6, 2k = 16, unit cost per episode 1: E =
  floor(1,000/96) = 10, below the floor of 16, so arms are dropped until 16 fits (a = 3: floor(1,000/48) = 20).
- `prefix_addresses_paired`: variant episodes are a prefix of the base schedule.
- `both_orders_printed`, `settling_experiment_named`.
- `demotion_rule`: 20 campaigns with 19 at overlap ≥ 7 of 8 and no energy flag → periodic audit; 18 → not; 19 of
  20 with one energy flag → `every-campaign`; one conditional audit while `periodic` switches the state back.
- `order_within_noise`: an unsettled pair that swaps is not a flip; a settled pair that reverses is, and the
  variant is listed in `flipped_by`; a ranking whose top two differ by less than noise is `model-robust` with
  those two unordered.
- `cost_ratio`: a check of 9 device-seconds in a campaign of 100 passes; 11 is labelled `modelform-over-budget`.
- `both_laws_preference`: a preference that holds under one qualified contact law only is reported with both
  orders.
- `not_run_arm_reported`.
- `ranking_too_short`: k = 8 on a ranking of 8 designs → `FROB-REF-802`, exit 3; on 9 designs it runs.
- `overflow_policy`: a variant world on `batch-cpu` forced over its contact capacity under `refuse` →
  `FROB-REF-722`, exit 5; the same on a device tier (on a machine with the device; logged `not run` otherwise) →
  `FROB-REF-610`, exit 6, passed through with code, exit and repairs unchanged; no `--on-overflow` → `refuse`;
  `--on-overflow reset` → `FROB-REF-802`; under `continue-flagged` the overlap is reported with one degraded
  episode counted.
- `refusal_list_matches_row`: the generated refusal list of `fidelity.modelform` is exactly {610, 722}.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/rank_and_modelform.sh` (N16b-32): builds the three planted rankings from N16b-15 (pyramid-cone
winner, penetration-recovery winner, genuine winner), runs `frob fidelity modelform` on each, then attempts
`frob eval promote` on a conditional result. Logged: per variant the family, arm, overlap against k − 1; the
energy audit; the verdict; the cost share against 0.10; the refusal code; on failure the first design whose rank
moved across the top-k boundary.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-353: the planted pyramid-cone winner (a pyramid grants √2·μ along its diagonals, 41% more than the cone) and
  the planted penetration-recovery winner return `model-conditional`; a genuine winner returns `model-robust`;
  cost at most 10%. The two planted winners are the controls.
- KA-591, KA-692: the box-cone exploit is typed `model-conditional`; a genuine winner stays `model-robust`; an
  honest policy on the circular cone is not flagged; with the stage skipped the exploit is reported as the
  winner.
- CX-148, CX-258: a pyramid of the same μ is not equivalent to the cone for ranking designs.
- Metamorphic MR-39: a ranking survives the variants with overlap at least k − 1, or is typed
  `model-conditional`.
- Determinism lanes: identical verdicts twice on one machine and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- The cascade's stage adds a stricter condition (the order of the top two must survive, and no finalist's
  advantage may coincide with cone excess); that belongs to BD-346 and is layered on this result type.
- A cone flip can only be observed if the pyramid arm is admitted; without it the cone family is `not-run` and
  the planted cone exploit must then be caught by the cone-excess monitor of the energy ledger instead.
- RISK-05 and pre-mortem story 5: a high share of `model-conditional` finalists is a warning signal about the
  contact law, tracked per campaign.
- The 10% budget is a TARGET; the measured share is printed in every result so that a drift is visible.
