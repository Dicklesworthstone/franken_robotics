## Background

Every step closes a discrete work–energy balance: the change in mechanical energy, minus actuator work, minus work
by prescribed surfaces and external forces, plus modelled dissipation (contact damping, sliding friction, joint
damping, stiction), leaves a residual. The energy ledger already reports that residual on its own line and never
folds it into a physical term. The audit adds a sign and a consequence: a **positive** residual is energy the
integrator or the solver created. A fast contact model can hand a policy energy that no actuator supplied, and a
learning or search process will find it.

Hand-checkable counterexample, and the first planted exploit (CX-147): a position-level penetration correction
that removes a depth d in one step gives the body a velocity d/h from nothing. With m = 1 kg, d = 1 mm and
h = 2 ms that is 0.5 m/s and ½·1·0.5² = 0.125 J per event. The compliant law on the same penetration at
k = 10⁵ N/m stores ½·k·d² = 0.05 J and returns less. A hopping policy that finds the first behaviour gains height
from the solver. The second planted exploit is the pyramid friction cone.

Tier: Solid. Division of work, in three parts. The energy ledger's kernel `K-ledger-step`, with the signed
residual accumulator, the five per-mode accumulators of the positive part and the window buffer, is the
ledger-kernel bead's (BD-208). The rule that turns the window data into the flag (the 0.5 s window, the
denominator, the threshold per tier, the zero-denominator rule and the flag record with its worst window) is
N29b-02's; the three monitors on the batched tiers (`energy-created`, `contact-active`, `cone-excess`), the
verb `fidelity.energy` with its schema, and the energy half of GATE-CHRON-08 are N29a-22's. This bead depends on
all three and is the dynamics side: what the step must hand the kernel, how compliance storage is treated, which
contact mode a step's positive residual is tagged with, and what a flag does to a result. This section of the
design defines no label or threshold of its own beyond the one flag.

## Technical approach

**Residual per world and step (stage 9 of the lockstep step; the commit of a transactional interval).**

r = ΔE_kinetic + ΔE_potential − (W_actuators + W_external − D)

where ΔE_potential includes gravity and every modelled elastic store, W_external includes work by prescribed
surfaces and external forces, and D ≥ 0 is the modelled dissipation of the step: contact normal damping, sliding
friction, joint damping, stiction and backlash rows. r > 0 means the step gained energy nothing supplied.

**Storage is not creation.** Energy stored in contact compliance is stored energy: for CL-1 a penetration d
against stiffness k holds ½·k·d² and can return at most that. The audit counts it in ΔE_potential, as the ledger
counts heat stored in a modelled thermal mass. Consequence for the kernel: the contact elastic energy of every
active contact is evaluated at the start and end of the step from the same state the solve used.

**Accumulation.** `K-ledger-step` (BD-208) accumulates per world, in double-float (an unevaluated sum of two
floats, so that `f32` tiers do not lose the residual in rounding):
- `resid_sum`: Σ r, the signed residual accumulator;
- `created_by_mode[m]`: Σ max(0, r), the positive part, kept separately from the signed sum and split by the
  contact mode in which it arose: five accumulators per world, one per mode;
- `created_sum`: the sum of the five, derived when read and not stored a second time.

Contact-mode tag of a step. The five modes are the kernel's (BD-208), and the verb `fidelity.energy` prints
its totals under the same five names (N29a-22): `none`, `sticking`, `sliding`, `impact`, `limit`. The rule that
gives a step exactly one of them is this bead's (DESIGN; the plan says only "tagged by the contact mode in which
it arose"), computed from the active-set bits of stage 8, first match wins:
1. `impact`: the set of active contacts changed in this step (a contact became active, a contact became
   inactive, or both). Reason for putting it first: created energy in such a step comes most often from the
   make or break event itself, as in the penetration-correction case.
2. `limit`: no contact change, and a joint-limit or hard-stop row is active.
3. `sliding`: at least one active contact is sliding.
4. `sticking`: every active contact is sticking.
5. `none`: no active contact and no limit row (flight).
Whether an `impact` step was a touchdown, a release or both is not kept in an accumulator; it is on the Contact
stream's make and break events for traced and failing worlds. The tag is a small integer per world per step;
reductions over worlds use fixed-order trees, never floating-point atomics.

**The flag (one rule, owned by the energy ledger: N29b-02 for the rule, N29a-22 for its evaluation on the
batched tiers; restated here, not redefined).** A result carries `energy-created` when created energy
exceeds 1% of the absolute work exchanged in any 0.5 s window on a fast tier, or 10⁻⁶ relative on the referee
(TARGET; re-baselined once at M2 together with GATE-FID-01, in BD-212's re-baseline document). The window is
evaluated at control-interval granularity: at h = 2 ms and ten world-steps per control interval a 0.5 s window is
25 control intervals; the step exports per-interval sums of created energy and of absolute work
(|W_actuators| + |W_external| + D) for the ledger's sliding window.

**Consequences.** A flagged episode keeps its result and gains the flag. A result flagged `energy-created` cannot
be `established`. A ranking that contains one is `model-conditional`. Both follow from the label algebra
(N16b-04): the condition is joined by union and blocks the established status.

**What `fidelity.energy` must be able to return from this data.** Created-energy totals per world and contact
mode for a run, with the worlds that crossed the bound. The verb and its output schema `frob.fidelity.energy.out/1` are
N29a-22's and this bead defines no output shape of its own; the requirement on the step is that its data
suffice for that schema: per contact mode (the five names above) the created-energy total with the absolute
work exchanged, and for each world over the bound its worst window (ticks, created energy C, work exchanged W,
ratio) and its flag.

**Relation to algorithmic exchange.** The energy an impulse-level contact step exchanges with the velocity
predictor is booked on its own ledger line and is not dissipation; it is first order in h and vanishes under
refinement. The audit's residual is computed with that line included, so a resting block under an impulse step
does not appear to create or destroy energy.

**Invariant.** INV-KIN-06: energy created by the simulator is recorded per step, and a result over the bound is
labelled.

**Determinism and evidence.** The accumulators are part of the per-world state covered by digests; class D2
within a tier. They are part of the transaction's restore set on the transactional profiles (a retried interval
must not double-count).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The per-world accumulators `resid_sum` and the five of `created_by_mode` (BD-208's kernel state) exist
      on `referee`, `batch-cpu` (both profiles) and each device tier, in double-float, and are inside the
      restore set; `created_sum` read back equals the sum of the five.
- [ ] Every step gets exactly one of the five modes by the precedence rule; the names equal those the kernel
      and `fidelity.energy` use (`none`, `sticking`, `sliding`, `impact`, `limit`).
- [ ] Hand case: the position-level correction at m = 1 kg, d = 1 mm, h = 2 ms records 0.125 J of created energy
      per event (within 1%), tagged `impact` when the correction acts in the step in which the contact became
      active and `sticking` when it acts in a later step of the same contact.
- [ ] CL-1 on the same penetration at k = 10⁵ N/m records 0.05 J as stored energy and zero created energy
      beyond the tier's residual bound.
- [ ] GATE-FID-06, unit part: both planted exploits of the gate (N16b-15's M1 `penetration-recovery`, by
      `energy-created`, and M2 `pyramid4`, by `cone-excess`) are flagged within one episode; an honest policy
      under CL-1 is unflagged. (N16b-15's third plant, M3 `explicit-penalty`, belongs to GATE-CHRON-08.) (The standard-battery clause and the gate row are recorded by N16b-33.)
- [ ] A flagged result's claim product carries the condition `energy-created` with status `provisional`, cannot
      be made `established`, and a ranking product containing it is `model-conditional` (N16b-04's algebra,
      asserted here on the products this bead's runs produce). The verb-level consequences, `eval.promote`'s exit
      2 with `blocked_by: energy-created` and `fidelity.modelform`'s verdict, are asserted by their M3 beads
      (BD-263, BD-266) and by `scripts/e2e/promotion.sh` (N16b-31), which depend on this bead.
- [ ] Negative control caught: a build that folds the residual into contact dissipation records zero created
      energy on the planted exploit, and the gate's test fails on it.
- [ ] Forced retry on a transactional profile leaves the accumulators equal to a clean run's (digest equality).
- [ ] `frob-dyn/CONTRACT.md`: the residual definition, the storage rule and INV-KIN-06 under invariants.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-dyn/tests/energy_audit.rs`:
- `position_correction_creates_0p125_J`: single 1 kg body, penetration 1 mm removed in one 2 ms step:
  created = 0.125 J ± 1%.
- `compliant_storage_is_not_creation`: CL-1 spring k = 10⁵ N/m compressed 1 mm and released without damping:
  stored 0.05 J, returned ≤ 0.05 J, created ≤ residual bound.
- `sign_convention`: a damped drop has r ≤ bound at every step; a planted +1 mJ injection per step yields
  created_sum = n × 1 mJ.
- `mode_tags`: a scripted contact history (flight, touchdown, stick, slip, release, flight) produces the tags
  `none`, `impact`, `sticking`, `sliding`, `impact`, `none`, and the positive part lands in the right bins; a
  step with a touchdown and an active limit row is `impact` (precedence); a step with a limit row and a sliding
  contact and no contact change is `limit`.
- `double_float_keeps_small_residual`: in `f32`, 10⁶ steps each adding 10⁻⁹ J to a sum near 1 J: the
  accumulator reports 10⁻³ J ± 1%, where a plain `f32` sum reports 0.
- `window_sums_at_control_granularity`: 25 intervals form the 0.5 s window at the defaults.
- `retry_does_not_double_count`.
- `inv_kin_06_property`: random worlds with random injections; every world over the bound is flagged and none
  under it is.
- `flag_reaches_claim_product`: the claim product of a run with one flagged world carries `energy-created`, status
  `provisional`, and combining it into a ranking product gives `model-conditional`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/created_energy_gate.sh` (N16b-33; the name `scripts/e2e/energy_audit.sh` belongs to the script of
N29a-22 for GATE-CHRON-08) runs the two planted exploits and the honest policy through `frob sim run` and reads
`frob fidelity energy <run> --json`. Logged: created energy per world and mode in
joules, the worst window with its ratio against 1%, the flag, and on failure the first step whose residual sign
differs from the expectation.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-354: the position-level correction creates 0.125 J per event at 1 kg, 1 mm, 2 ms and raises
  `energy-created` within one episode; CL-1 at k = 10⁵ N/m stores 0.05 J, returns less and creates none; an
  honest policy under CL-1 is unflagged. Control: the residual folded into contact dissipation.
- KA-709: a stiff penalty bounce at a step near the stability limit raises `energy-created`; the convex law at
  the default step does not. Control: the audit switched off, in which case the bouncing policy wins the
  comparison.
- CX-147: penetration cannot be removed by a position-level correction at no cost in energy.
- Determinism lanes: accumulators equal twice on one machine, across worker counts, and solo against any
  co-tenancy.

## Considerations

- OPEN-KIN-6 (the created-energy bound per law and step; owner Kinesis lead) prints its default as "1% of
  actuator work per episode", while the rule in force is 1% of the absolute work exchanged in any 0.5 s window.
  The windowed rule is the one the energy ledger owns and the gate uses; the open question is closed by the
  single re-baseline at M2.
- An episode-level denominator would hide a short burst of created energy inside a long episode; the window is
  what makes a single exploit event visible.
- Cost: six accumulators per world (the signed residual and the five per-mode sums of its positive part) and
  one tag per step; negligible beside the solve. Telemetry level T0 carries the totals.
- The contact passivity and cone-excess monitors (the other two monitors of the audit, N29a-22) catch the
  pyramid exploit from the contact side; this bead's residual catches it only when the extra traction turns into
  net energy.
- The mode enumeration was first drafted here with seven values (`flight`, `touchdown`, `stick`, `slip`,
  `release`, `joint-limit`, `mixed`); the kernel and the verb have five, and one kernel cannot have two layouts.
  The five are kept, `touchdown`, `release` and `mixed` fold into `impact` by the precedence rule, and the finer
  distinction stays readable on the Contact stream.
