## Background

Two shortcuts in contact simulation hand a policy something physics would not, and both are tempting because
they make a solver simpler or faster:

1. **Position-level penetration recovery.** Removing a penetration depth d in one step gives the body a velocity
   d/h from nothing: 0.5 m/s and 0.125 J per event at m = 1 kg, d = 1 mm, h = 2 ms. A hopping policy that finds
   this gains height from the solver (CX-147).
2. **The pyramid friction cone.** A four-sided pyramid of the same μ admits (μ·γₙ, μ·γₙ) in the tangent plane, of
   norm √2·μ·γₙ: 41% of traction that does not exist along the diagonals (CX-148, CX-026). A design or gait that
   pushes diagonally wins on the pyramid and not on the cone.
3. **The explicit penalty spring at its stability limit.** A contact spring k·d without damping, its force taken
   from the penetration at the start of the step and integrated semi-implicitly, is stable only while h·√(k/m) < 2.
   At the limit a body bouncing on it gains height from the integrator ("trampolining"): 1 kg on k = 10⁶ N/m at
   h = 2 ms, dropped from 0.2 m, rebounds to about 0.26, 0.57 and 3.75 m. No production law is a penalty law, so
   without a planted one the known fixture of the created-energy audit (KA-709) has nothing to run under.

The trust ladder's gates require these to be planted and caught: GATE-FID-06 (energy audit: both planted exploits
flagged within one episode, an honest policy under CL-1 unflagged), GATE-FID-05 and GATE-CORTEX-15 (model-form
check: the planted pyramid-cone and penetration-recovery winners are returned as `model-conditional`, a genuine
winner as `model-robust`), and GATE-CHRON-08 (the created-energy audit flags every planted trampolining and
cone-excess exploit and no honest fixture). A gate that nothing can fail validates nothing, so the plants are
built once, as registered controls, and reused.

## Scope

In: the three solver mutations as labelled step-plan options, requested through the plant kind `mutation` of
N29a-25's plant file; the `pyramid4` cone kind as a runtime-selectable,
labelled, non-production cone kind in `frob-contact` (the model-form check's cone arm, N16b-13, and conformance
are its only requesters); a hopper and a diagonal-push task that exploit them; an honest policy; three planted
rankings for the model-form check; registry records.
Out: the mechanisms that must catch them (N16b-14, BD-266, the energy ledger's monitors in BD-208); the
tier-sensitive policy and the tier-reversal pair, which build on these mutations (N16b-06).

## Technical approach

**Mutation M1, `penetration-recovery` (kind `mutation`).** A step-plan option, compiled only under the
`conformance` feature, that replaces the compliant normal response of law CL-1 by a position-level projection:
after the velocity solve, each penetrating contact's depth d is removed by displacing the body along the contact
normal within the same step. The option changes the `StepPlanId` and prints its label in every result. Expected
ledger signature: created energy of ½·m_eff·(d/h)² per event; 0.125 J at the hand case.

**Mutation M2, `pyramid4` (kind `mutation`).** The friction constraint |γₜ₁| ≤ μ·γₙ and |γₜ₂| ≤ μ·γₙ in place of
the second-order cone ‖γₜ‖ ≤ μ·γₙ, as a labelled cone kind. Unlike M1 it has a second, legitimate use: it is the
cone arm of the model-form check (the elliptic cone against a four-sided pyramid of the same μ), so the cone kind
itself is compiled in every build, with kernels on `batch-cpu` and each device tier, and is selectable only
through the model-form variant builder (N16b-13) or by a conformance request. What is conformance-only is its use
as the *base* law of a run or campaign (the planted ranking R_cone). Expected signature: along the tangent diagonal the
transmitted friction reaches √2·μ·N; the cone-excess monitor reads a utilization ‖f_t‖/(μ·f_n) of √2 ≈ 1.414
(flag threshold: utilization above 1.05 for more than 1% of stance time).

**Mutation M3, `explicit-penalty` (kind `mutation`).** A step-plan option, compiled only under the `conformance`
feature like M1, that replaces the compliant normal response of law CL-1 by a penalty force f_n = k·d with no
damping term, where d is the penetration at the **start** of the step, integrated semi-implicitly (velocity
first, then position); friction rows are those of CL-1. The stiffness is the option's own parameter (default
k = 10⁶ N/m), and the option changes the `StepPlanId` and prints its label in every result. Its plants are
measured on lockstep step plans (`batch-cpu` and the device tiers); under a transactional profile (`batch-cpu:
strict`, the referee) the step's energy-ledger residual check (BD-161 T5; check 5 of the referee) sees the
creation and retries or faults, which is the transactional step working, not a plant that lost its potency.
Expected signature: at h·√(k/m) = 2, a step that starts with penetration d = v·h at approach speed v ends at
speed −v + (h²·k/m)·v = 3·v outward (gravity's g·h neglected), so energy is created at each impact; ledger
residual r > 0 on the contact steps and net positive contact work over the episode (`energy-created` and
`contact-active`, both asserted by N29a-22).

**Fixture F3, the trampoline drop (validity case).** A 1 kg point body dropped from 0.2 m onto flat ground under
M3 at k = 10⁶ N/m, h = 2 ms. Known values (host simulation of the point-mass model, the same model N29b-02 steps
on the host): first three apexes 0.258, 0.566 and 3.754 m. Honest counterpart: the same drop under CL-1 at the
default step, whose apexes do not exceed the drop height. N29a-22's planted trampolining fixture is this drop.

**Fixture F1, the hopper.** A single 1 kg body with one actuated prismatic leg on flat ground, h = 2 ms. Policy
P_exploit: an open-loop stroke that lands with a small penetration at every hop. Under M1 the apex height grows
hop over hop; under CL-1 it decays or holds. Policy P_honest: the same stroke under CL-1 at k = 10⁵ N/m with its
dissipation time. Known values: per-event created energy 0.125 J under M1 when d = 1 mm; stored energy 0.05 J
under CL-1 at the same d.

**Fixture F2, the diagonal push.** A block of weight N on a flat floor with μ = 0.5, pushed along the tangent
diagonal by a ramped force. Under the cone it slides at μ·N; under M2 it holds until √2·μ·N. With N = 100 N:
50 N against 70.7 N.

**Planted rankings for the model-form check (kind `planted-design`).** Three small campaigns of 16 designs each,
built on the reference quadruped with a locomotion task, scored on the stated base step plan:
- R_cone: base step plan carries M2; the planted winner is a design whose gait pushes along the foot's tangent
  diagonal (splayed stance), ranked first under the pyramid and outside the top 8 under the cone.
- R_energy: base step plan carries M1; the planted winner is a design with heavy feet and a stomping gait that
  harvests the correction, ranked first under M1; its episodes carry `energy-created`.
- R_genuine: base step plan is the production law; the winner's advantage is a longer leg at equal mass, which
  survives every variant (top-8 overlap at least 7 under each).
Each ranking is built by a seeded script that also checks validity: the planted winner must be first under its
base and must lose at least two ranks of top-8 overlap (R_cone) or carry the flag on at least 90% of its
episodes (R_energy); R_genuine must keep overlap ≥ 7 under all admitted variants on the build machine. A build
that fails its validity check fails loudly; a plant that is not a plant is worse than none.

**Registry.** `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` records: M1 (seed CX-147; gates GATE-FID-05,
GATE-FID-06, GATE-CHRON-08, GATE-CORTEX-15), M2 (seed CX-148; gates GATE-FID-05, GATE-FID-06, GATE-TRAC-04,
GATE-CORTEX-15, GATE-CHRON-08), M3 (seed KA-709; target the trampolining fixture F3, defect "penalty spring
integrated at h·√(k/m) = 2", expect `energy-created` and `contact-active`; gate GATE-CHRON-08), the mutation
"residual folded into contact dissipation" (gate GATE-FID-06), the mutation "audit switched off" (gate
GATE-CHRON-08), the three rankings (gates GATE-FID-05, GATE-CORTEX-15). Each with `target`, `defect`, `expect`
and `last_seen`.

**Requesting a mutation (DESIGN; the plant file of N29a-25).** A run asks for M1, M3 or M2 as its base law
through the one conformance-build input that carries plants, `--conformance-plant <file>` (N29a-25's option on
`sim.run`, `sim.replay` and the conformance harness; VERBS-sim's conformance-build-only table). This bead
registers the plant kind `mutation` in N29a-25's section registry `registries/plant-kinds.toml` (owning bead
N16b-15; crates `frob-contact` and `frob-kernels`; admission function `plants::exploits::admit_mutation`; hook:
the contact normal response for M1 and M3, the cone kind for M2), with the section form
`"mutation": [{ "name": "penetration-recovery" } | { "name": "explicit-penalty", "k_n_per_m": 1000000 } |
{ "name": "pyramid4-base" }]`. One mutation per file (a plant tests one defect; two entries are rejected at
admission, naming both). The file's canonical bytes enter the `StepPlanId` by N29a-25's rule, which gives the
identity change and the label required above. The registered files are
`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/exploits/plant/{penetration-recovery,explicit-penalty,pyramid4-base}.json`;
the scripts that run the plants (N16b-31, N16b-32, N16b-33, N29a-22) pass one of them, for example `frob sim run
<F1 hopper> --conformance-plant crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/exploits/plant/penetration-recovery.json --tier
batch-cpu --seed 17 --key <k> --json`. A release build has no such input in its schema and answers it with
`FROB-REF-802`.

**Guard.** M1 and M3, and M2 as a base law, are refused by every verb unless the binary was built with `conformance`
and the request carries the conformance scope. M2 as a model-form arm is admitted only when requested by the
variant builder of N16b-13 inside `fidelity.modelform`; any other request for `pyramid4` is refused with the
label of the mutation in the payload. A result computed under either is labelled and can never be cited as a
result of the production law.

**Location.** `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/exploits/`, `crates/frob-conformance/src/plants/exploits.rs`;
the option hooks live behind `#[cfg(feature = "conformance")]` in `frob-kernels` and `frob-contact`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] M1 on the hand case creates 0.125 J per event (within 1%); the production law creates none beyond the
      residual bound.
- [ ] M2 on F2 transmits 70.7 N ± 0.5% along the diagonal at μ = 0.5, N = 100 N; the cone transmits 50 N ± 0.5%.
- [ ] F1: apex height grows under M1 with P_exploit over 20 hops and does not under CL-1.
- [ ] The three rankings build from their seeds and pass their validity checks, with the measured overlaps and
      flag shares recorded.
- [ ] M3 on the hand case: one contact step at h·√(k/m) = 2 starting from d = v·h ends at 3·v outward (gravity
      neglected, within 10⁻¹² relative in `f64`); F3 under M3 reaches apexes 0.258, 0.566 and 3.754 m (within 1 mm) and its
      ledger residual is positive on the contact steps; F3 under CL-1 at the default step never exceeds 0.2 m.
- [ ] Eight registry records exist with their gates.
- [ ] A default build refuses M1 and M3 and refuses M2 as a base law; it admits M2 only as the model-form cone arm
      requested by the variant builder; a conformance build labels every result computed under either.
- [ ] The honest policy P_honest is unflagged by the energy audit on 64 addresses.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/plants_exploits.rs`:
- `m1_hand_case`: 1 kg, 1 mm, 2 ms → Δv = 0.5 m/s, ΔE = 0.125 J.
- `m2_diagonal`: friction limit ratio pyramid/cone = 1.4142 ± 0.005.
- `hopper_gains_height_under_m1`: apex(20) > apex(1) under M1; apex(20) ≤ apex(1) under CL-1.
- `m3_one_step_gain`: v = 1 m/s, d = v·h = 2 mm, k = 10⁶ N/m, m = 1 kg, h = 2 ms, no gravity → outgoing speed
  3 m/s, energy 0.5 J in, 4.5 J out.
- `m3_drop_apexes`: F3 gives apexes (0.258, 0.566, 3.754) m within 1 mm; the mutant that takes the force from
  the end-of-step penetration (implicit in position) does not gain height and must miss.
- `m3_force_from_start_of_step`: the force at a step equals k times the penetration before the position update,
  bitwise.
- `rankings_are_deterministic`: two builds, identical bytes.
- `r_cone_validity`, `r_energy_validity`, `r_genuine_validity` as stated above.
- `options_need_conformance`: refusal of M1, of M3 and of M2 as a base law in a default build.
- `pyramid_arm_only_via_variant_builder`: in a default build `pyramid4` requested through the variant builder is
  admitted and labelled; the same cone kind requested directly on a run, through `sim.run`'s `--law` input (the
  cone kind is part of the law and of the `StepPlanId`, N16b-13; `sim.run` has no cone option of its own), is
  refused with the mutation's label in the payload.
- `labels_on_mutated_results`: the evidence block names the mutation.
- `mutation_plant_kind`: on a conformance build a plant file with the section `mutation: [{ name:
  "penetration-recovery" }]` is admitted, the run's `StepPlanId` differs from the unplanted run's and the receipt
  lists the plant `applied`; a file with two mutation entries, or an unknown name, is rejected at admission naming
  them; the kind is listed in `registries/plant-kinds.toml` with this bead as owner; a default build answers
  `--conformance-plant` with `FROB-REF-802`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Used by `scripts/e2e/created_energy_gate.sh` (N16b-33; the name `scripts/e2e/energy_audit.sh` belongs to the
script of N29a-22 for GATE-CHRON-08, which runs M1, M2 and M3 as its planted fixtures) and
`scripts/e2e/rank_and_modelform.sh` (N16b-32). They log the plant identifiers, seeds and validity measurements
(created energy per event in joules, diagonal force in newtons, F3's rebound apexes in metres, overlaps), so that
a failed gate can be separated from a plant that lost its potency.

## Considerations

- The planted rankings depend on kernels; after a kernel change the validity checks re-run, and a plant that
  fails is rebuilt under a new seed with a new registry revision.
- The contact chapter (BD-154) keeps the pyramid only as a test mutant and states that it exists "only as a
  labelled mutant and model-form arm, never as a production law"; no other bead builds the runtime cone kind, so
  this bead adds it in `frob-contact`, under that crate's rules, with the selection guard above.
- The mutations must never leak into a default build: the guard is tested, and the layer checks forbid a
  default feature from enabling `conformance`.
- No private scene data; the fixtures may appear in logs and reports.
