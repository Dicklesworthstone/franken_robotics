## Background

Two shortcuts in contact simulation hand a policy something physics would not, and both are tempting because
they make a solver simpler or faster:

1. **Position-level penetration recovery.** Removing a penetration depth d in one step gives the body a velocity
   d/h from nothing: 0.5 m/s and 0.125 J per event at m = 1 kg, d = 1 mm, h = 2 ms. A hopping policy that finds
   this gains height from the solver (CX-147).
2. **The pyramid friction cone.** A four-sided pyramid of the same μ admits (μ·γₙ, μ·γₙ) in the tangent plane, of
   norm √2·μ·γₙ: 41% of traction that does not exist along the diagonals (CX-148, CX-026). A design or gait that
   pushes diagonally wins on the pyramid and not on the cone.

The trust ladder's gates require these to be planted and caught: GATE-FID-06 (energy audit: both planted exploits
flagged within one episode, an honest policy under CL-1 unflagged), GATE-FID-05 and GATE-CORTEX-15 (model-form
check: the planted pyramid-cone and penetration-recovery winners are returned as `model-conditional`, a genuine
winner as `model-robust`), and GATE-CHRON-08 (the created-energy audit flags every planted trampolining and
cone-excess exploit and no honest fixture). A gate that nothing can fail validates nothing, so the plants are
built once, as registered controls, and reused.

## Scope

In: the two solver mutations as labelled step-plan options available only to conformance; a hopper and a
diagonal-push task that exploit them; an honest policy; three planted rankings for the model-form check; registry
records.
Out: the mechanisms that must catch them (N16b-14, BD-266, the energy ledger's monitors in BD-208); the
tier-sensitive policy and the tier-reversal pair, which build on these mutations (N16b-06).

## Technical approach

**Mutation M1, `penetration-recovery` (kind `mutation`).** A step-plan option, compiled only under the
`conformance` feature, that replaces the compliant normal response of law CL-1 by a position-level projection:
after the velocity solve, each penetrating contact's depth d is removed by displacing the body along the contact
normal within the same step. The option changes the `StepPlanId` and prints its label in every result. Expected
ledger signature: created energy of ½·m_eff·(d/h)² per event; 0.125 J at the hand case.

**Mutation M2, `pyramid4` (kind `mutation`).** The friction constraint |γₜ₁| ≤ μ·γₙ and |γₜ₂| ≤ μ·γₙ in place of
the second-order cone ‖γₜ‖ ≤ μ·γₙ, as a labelled cone kind. Expected signature: along the tangent diagonal the
transmitted friction reaches √2·μ·N; the cone-excess monitor reads a utilization ‖f_t‖/(μ·f_n) of √2 ≈ 1.414
(flag threshold: utilization above 1.05 for more than 1% of stance time).

**Fixture F1, the hopper.** A single 1 kg body with one actuated prismatic leg on flat ground, h = 2 ms. Policy
P_exploit: an open-loop stroke that lands with a small penetration at every hop. Under M1 the apex height grows
hop over hop; under CL-1 it decays or holds. Policy P_honest: the same stroke under CL-1 at k = 10⁵ N/m with its
dissipation time. Known values: per-event created energy 0.125 J under M1 when d = 1 mm; stored energy 0.05 J
under CL-1 at the same d.

**Fixture F2, the diagonal push.** A block of weight N on a flat floor with μ = 0.5, pushed along the tangent
diagonal by a ramped force. Under the cone it slides at μ·N; under M2 it holds until √2·μ·N. With N = 100 N:
50 N against 70.7 N.

**Planted rankings for the model-form check (kind `planted-design`).** Three small campaigns of 16 designs each,
built on the reference quadruped with a locomotion task, scored on the stated base step plan:
- R_cone: base step plan carries M2; the planted winner is a design whose gait pushes along the foot's tangent
  diagonal (splayed stance), ranked first under the pyramid and outside the top 8 under the cone.
- R_energy: base step plan carries M1; the planted winner is a design with heavy feet and a stomping gait that
  harvests the correction, ranked first under M1; its episodes carry `energy-created`.
- R_genuine: base step plan is the production law; the winner's advantage is a longer leg at equal mass, which
  survives every variant (top-8 overlap at least 7 under each).
Each ranking is built by a seeded script that also checks validity: the planted winner must be first under its
base and must lose at least two ranks of top-8 overlap (R_cone) or carry the flag on at least 90% of its
episodes (R_energy); R_genuine must keep overlap ≥ 7 under all admitted variants on the build machine. A build
that fails its validity check fails loudly; a plant that is not a plant is worse than none.

**Registry.** `frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` records: M1 (seed CX-147; gates GATE-FID-05,
GATE-FID-06, GATE-CHRON-08, GATE-CORTEX-15), M2 (seed CX-148; gates GATE-FID-05, GATE-FID-06, GATE-TRAC-04,
GATE-CORTEX-15, GATE-CHRON-08), the mutation "residual folded into contact dissipation" (gate GATE-FID-06), the
mutation "audit switched off" (gate GATE-CHRON-08), the three rankings (gates GATE-FID-05, GATE-CORTEX-15). Each
with `target`, `defect`, `expect` and `last_seen`.

**Guard.** The two options are refused by every verb unless the binary was built with `conformance` and the
request carries the conformance scope; a result computed under either is labelled and can never be cited as a
result of the production law.

**Location.** `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/exploits/`, `crates/frob-conformance/src/plants/exploits.rs`;
the option hooks live behind `#[cfg(feature = "conformance")]` in `frob-kernels` and `frob-contact`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] M1 on the hand case creates 0.125 J per event (within 1%); the production law creates none beyond the
      residual bound.
- [ ] M2 on F2 transmits 70.7 N ± 0.5% along the diagonal at μ = 0.5, N = 100 N; the cone transmits 50 N ± 0.5%.
- [ ] F1: apex height grows under M1 with P_exploit over 20 hops and does not under CL-1.
- [ ] The three rankings build from their seeds and pass their validity checks, with the measured overlaps and
      flag shares recorded.
- [ ] Seven registry records exist with their gates.
- [ ] A default build refuses both options; a conformance build labels every result computed under them.
- [ ] The honest policy P_honest is unflagged by the energy audit on 64 addresses.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/plants_exploits.rs`:
- `m1_hand_case`: 1 kg, 1 mm, 2 ms → Δv = 0.5 m/s, ΔE = 0.125 J.
- `m2_diagonal`: friction limit ratio pyramid/cone = 1.4142 ± 0.005.
- `hopper_gains_height_under_m1`: apex(20) > apex(1) under M1; apex(20) ≤ apex(1) under CL-1.
- `rankings_are_deterministic`: two builds, identical bytes.
- `r_cone_validity`, `r_energy_validity`, `r_genuine_validity` as stated above.
- `options_need_conformance`: refusal in a default build.
- `labels_on_mutated_results`: the evidence block names the mutation.

### End-to-end script and logging

Used by `scripts/e2e/energy_audit.sh` (N16b-33) and `scripts/e2e/rank_and_modelform.sh` (N16b-32). They log the
plant identifiers, seeds and validity measurements (created energy per event in joules, diagonal force in
newtons, overlaps), so that a failed gate can be separated from a plant that lost its potency.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-354 (0.125 J per event; 0.05 J stored under CL-1), KA-055 and KA-401 (the pyramid gives √2·μ·N where the
  maximum friction is μ·N in every direction), KA-710 (cone-excess utilization √2), KA-353 (the two planted
  winners).
- CX-147, CX-148, CX-026, CX-258.
- These fixtures are themselves the negative controls of four gates; their own controls are the validity checks.
- Determinism: fixtures build identically twice and across platforms (exact seeds; the build records the tier
  and kernel versions it was validated on).

## Considerations

- The planted rankings depend on kernels; after a kernel change the validity checks re-run, and a plant that
  fails is rebuilt under a new seed with a new registry revision.
- R_cone needs the pyramid arm to be a selectable cone kind. If the contact chapter ships it only as a test
  build, this bead adds the runtime selection under the conformance feature in `frob-contact`, under that crate's
  rules.
- The mutations must never leak into a default build: the guard is tested, and the layer checks forbid a
  default feature from enabling `conformance`.
- No private scene data; the fixtures may appear in logs and reports.
