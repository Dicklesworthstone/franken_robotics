## Background

The two step disciplines of the system treat events differently, and the limits of the claim must be stated
exactly. The lockstep tiers (`batch-cpu`, `gpu-*` under the `lockstep` profile) are time-stepping schemes with a
velocity-scaled contact margin; **they do not locate events**. A contact whose approach is faster than the margin
allows, or a thin barrier, can be detected late or missed. The referee and the `strict` profile do locate events
(one transaction per accepted interval, with conservative advancement and bisection on a dyadic grid).

Because the fast tiers can miss a contact, the fidelity ledger carries a quantity for it: "the count of contacts
the certified detector found and the fast tier missed". This bead produces that count. FACT: FrankenSim provides
event location (`fs-time::rk45_until_event`) and certified continuous collision detection
(`fs-contact::certified_ccd`); its own contact step states that independent endpoint gap checks are not
continuous collision detection, so tunnelling detection is ours to do, by auditing the fast tier's path
(interim posture until upstream work item FD-FSIM-03 lands; the audit stays in any case).

Rejected alternatives: certified CCD on every pair of every step of every world (it would cost more than the
step; the audit is sampled and reports its sampling probability); trusting the margin (the ledger exists so that
this trust is a measured number).

## Scope

In: window sampling by address, the audit of one window, the count and its classification, ledger rows, the
thin-barrier known answer.
Out: the contact margin itself and the lockstep narrow phase (the batched-contact beads); the tunnelling audit
primitive and `contact.audit` verb (BD-168); thin-barrier fixtures under the transactional step (BD-086); event
location in the transaction (BD-082).

## Technical approach

1. **Window sampling.** A window is one control interval of one world. Window (world w, tick t) is audited iff
   the counter-based draw keyed by `(WorldAddr, t, missed-contact-audit)` is below p_w·2³² (p_w declared; DESIGN
   default 10⁻³). The sample is a function of addresses and touches no physics stream.
2. **Inputs of one audit.** The fast tier's committed states at each world-step boundary of the window (from the
   run's telemetry or by deterministic replay of the world, BD-204), and its contact list per step (pair
   identities and gaps).
3. **Swept-path query.** For every candidate body pair of the broad phase over the window, and for each
   world-step [tᵢ, tᵢ₊₁], run the certified swept query on the motion that interpolates the fast tier's two
   committed poses on the group (the same retraction the step used), through the airlock's
   `certified_ccd`. It returns "no contact on the path, certified", or an interval containing the first time of
   contact, with a bound on its error.
4. **Event location on the referee path (optional mode).** Re-advance the window from its start state on the
   referee with event location (`rk45_until_event`), giving the times at which a guard changes sign (touchdown,
   lift-off); used to report how late the fast tier was.
5. **Classification.** A certified contact on the path is
   - `missed` if the pair never appears in the fast tier's contact list during the window and the fast tier's
     end pose has the pair separated (tunnelled) or interpenetrating beyond the margin with no contact row;
   - `late` if the pair appears only after the certified time of contact by more than one world-step, with the
     penetration depth at first detection recorded;
   - `seen` otherwise.
6. **Output.** Per window `{ addr, tick, pairs_checked, seen, late, missed, worst_delay_s, worst_depth_m }`.
   Per stratum the fidelity ledger gains the quantity "missed contacts": counts weighted by 1/p_w, the rate per
   world-second, and the list of the worst windows. A stratum with any `missed` contact prints it in
   `fidelity.ledger` and makes the corresponding battery pair an exceedance.
7. **Refusals passed through.** A swept query that the certified detector cannot decide within its budget is
   counted `undecided`, never `seen`.

**Layering.** The audit of one window is a pure function in `frob-dyn::audit::missed_contact` over
`frob-contact`'s primitives and the airlock; which windows run is decided by the battery runner or the
background referee's scheduler. Determinism: the sampled set is exact; the counts are deterministic for a given
recorded path. Evidence: Fidelity claim input, class Tested; a certified "no contact" is the detector's own
evidence class as FrankenSim's contract states it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the thin-barrier fixture with the endpoint-only mutation planted in the fast tier, the audit reports
      exactly one `missed` contact at the certified time; with the production margin it reports `seen`.
- [ ] On the standard battery (`batch-cpu` lockstep) the quantity "missed contacts" is written per stratum with
      its sampling probability; the count is identical under two batch compositions.
- [ ] `late` contacts carry the delay in seconds and the depth in metres at first detection.
- [ ] Undecided queries are reported as `undecided` with their count.
- [ ] The audit adds no draw to any physics stream (world digests unchanged with the audit on).
- [ ] `frob-dyn/CONTRACT.md` states, under validity domain, that lockstep tiers do not locate events and names
      this audit as the measurement of what they miss.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-dyn/tests/missed_contact_audit.rs`:
- `thin_barrier_tunnel` (synthetic fixture): a sphere of radius 1 cm approaches a plate 2 mm thick at 30 m/s
  with a 2 cm gap, h = 2 ms. The sphere travels 6 cm in one step, so both endpoint gaps are positive. Certified
  time of contact: 0.02/30 = 0.667 ms into the step. With the endpoint-only mutation: `missed = 1`. With the
  velocity-scaled margin (30 m/s × 2 ms = 6 cm ≥ 2 cm): `seen = 1`.
- `late_detection_reported`: a margin forced to half the approach per step yields `late` with a delay of one
  step and a recorded depth.
- `sample_is_address_function`: the audited window set is equal under slot permutation.
- `undecided_not_seen`: a query with its budget forced to zero is `undecided`.
- `weights_one_over_p`: with p_w = 0.1 and 5 missed in the sample the stratum estimate is 50.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/fidelity_ledger.sh` (N16b-30) reads the quantity through `frob fidelity ledger --quantity
missed-contacts --json` after the battery run, and a conformance step runs the thin-barrier fixture with the
mutation. Logged: p_w, windows audited, pairs checked, seen, late, missed, undecided, the worst delay in seconds,
and on failure the first window's pair and certified interval.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-338's control, "an endpoint-only gap check", is this audit's planted defect: it must be counted as a missed
  contact.
- The thin-barrier and witness-migration fixtures are the controls of BET-34 (fixtures that a fixed-step tier
  gets wrong); the audit must flag the thin-barrier one.
- Negative control (mutation): an audit that checks only step end points (no swept query) reports zero on the
  tunnel fixture; the test fails on it.
- Determinism lanes: counts identical twice on one machine and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- The audit follows the fast tier's own path. It certifies whether that path crossed geometry; it does not say
  what a correct trajectory would have been. The referee re-run of step 4 is the tool for that.
- Cost: one certified swept query per candidate pair per world-step of an audited window; at p_w = 10⁻³ it is a
  small fraction of a CPU core for a device run. The probability is printed with every count.
- When FD-FSIM-03 gives FrankenSim a contact step with its own continuous detection, the referee's path needs no
  audit, and this audit continues to serve the lockstep tiers.
- RISK-31: where the referee cannot complete a scenario, this audit still runs, because it needs only geometry
  queries and the fast tier's path.
