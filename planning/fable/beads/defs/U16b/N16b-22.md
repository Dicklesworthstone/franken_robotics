## Background

Robots contain kinematic loops: a parallel knee, a steering linkage, a parallelogram that keeps a foot level. A
design declares one with the constructor `closure(port_a, port_b)`. The rule for how a loop is simulated depends
on the tier: on the lockstep tiers a closure is a **compliant equality row** in the convex solve, with its
compliance stated; under a transactional profile closures are bilateral rows that hold exactly to tolerance;
exact closures on the referee wait for FrankenSim's constraint lane (upstream work item FD-FSIM-04).

The compliant row on the lockstep tiers is labelled Frontier. A Frontier mechanism runs a paper-scale pilot with
a written falsifier before its work is funded. The chapter text names no pilot for the compliant row itself (it
names one for the closed-form elimination of planar four-bar loops, which is a different mechanism), so this
bead defines one, as a DESIGN proposal to be ratified by the Kinesis lead before the mechanism bead (N16b-23)
starts. The question it answers: does a soft closure, at a stiffness the solver can carry in single precision at
the default step, hold a loop closed tightly enough that joint torques and foot trajectories agree with the
exact loop?

## Scope

In: a planar four-bar on `batch-cpu` solved two ways, the sweep over stiffness and step, the comparison, the
verdict.
Out: the production row kernel and its integration (N16b-23); closed-form loop elimination and its gate
(the bead for single-loop planar closures in the same chapter, owned by another unit).

## Technical approach

1. **Mechanism.** The parallelogram four-bar with crank a = 1, coupler b = 2, output c = 1, ground d = 2 (units
   of 0.1 m), for which the exact solution on the open branch is φ = θ and the transmission ratio is n = 1 for
   every θ; and the crank-rocker (a, b, c, d) = (1, 3, 2.5, 3), for which φ(90°) = 98.857° and n = 0.3722 on the
   open branch. Link masses 0.2 kg each; gravity on; the crank driven by a PD position servo through a 2 s sweep
   that stays away from the toggle (the sine of the transmission angle above 0.17).
2. **Reference arm.** The `strict` profile in `f64` with the closure as bilateral rows A·v = 0 in the solve and
   the loop residual checked at every commit.
3. **Compliant arm.** The `lockstep` profile in `f32` and `f64` with a spanning tree and one closure row per
   constrained direction (two for a planar pin). DESIGN form of the row: with closure error φ_c(q) and Jacobian
   J_c, an implicit spring-damper with stiffness k_c and dissipation time τ_c contributes the convex term
   ℓ_c(v₊) = h·[½·(h·k_c + k_c·τ_c)·‖J_c·v₊‖² + k_c·φ_cᵀ·(J_c·v₊)] to the step's objective, so the row is a
   quadratic in the unknown velocity and keeps the objective strictly convex.
4. **Sweep.** k_c ∈ {10⁴, 10⁵, 10⁶, 10⁷} N/m with τ_c = 2·h; h ∈ {2 ms, 1 ms}; Newton iterations at the default
   budget (4, with warm start).
5. **Measures.** Maximum and RMS loop residual ‖φ_c‖ over the sweep; crank torque error against the reference
   (RMS, relative); output-angle error against the closed form; solver convergence (iterations used, line-search
   failures); in `f32`, whether diagonal scaling keeps the factorization stable (the smallest pivot after
   scaling).
6. **Falsifier (DESIGN, proposed here).** No stiffness in the sweep achieves, at h = 2 ms in `f32` within the
   default iteration budget, both a maximum loop residual below 10⁻⁴ m (0.1 mm on a 0.1 m link) and an RMS
   crank-torque error below 2% of the reference. If the falsifier holds, loops on lockstep tiers are admitted
   only through closed-form elimination where it applies, and other loops are refused on lockstep tiers and
   routed to `strict`.
7. **Ceiling.** None on throughput: the row adds one or two constraint rows per loop. The benefit is admission of
   looped mechanisms on fast tiers.
8. **Output.** `docs/delivery/pilots/closure-row.md` with the sweep table and the verdict; the chosen default
   (k_c, τ_c) if supported.
9. **Pilot register** (GATE-GAUNT-08, N38a-09). After the Kinesis lead's ratification of the falsifier and before
   the first sweep cell runs, the record `PILOT-KIN-04` is written to
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (schema `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`; identifier proposed, the
   register assigns the next free number of the set if it is taken) with status `registered`: `mechanism` =
   "compliant closure row on the lockstep tiers", `tier` = "F", `bead` = "N16b-22", `work_package` = "WP-15"
   (the mechanism bead N16b-23 is what its result funds), `protocol` = the hash of steps 1 to 8 with the
   ratification reference, `falsifier` = the sentence of step 6 with its two numbers (10⁻⁴ m, 2%), `ceiling` =
   "none on speed: the benefit is admission of looped mechanisms on fast tiers", `fallback` = { ships = "loops on
   lockstep tiers admitted only through closed-form elimination where it applies; other loops refused on lockstep
   tiers and routed to `strict`", kill_gate = "to-register" (proposed wording: "KILL-CLOSUREROW: no stiffness of
   the sweep meets both bounds at h = 2 ms in `f32` within the default budget") }, `cost` (a few CPU core-hours),
   `tool` = { site = "batch-cpu" }. The result is appended as a `[[pilot.result]]` whose `protocol_blake3` equals
   the registered hash; verdict `falsifier-holds` or `falsifier-rejected`.

Binary: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --bin pilot-closure-row`. The row is implemented inside the pilot binary
against the `batch-cpu` harness (a test-only constraint hook), not in the production kernels.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/pilot_closure_row.rs`:
- `parallelogram_closed_form`: φ(θ) = θ at 37 angles.
- `crank_rocker_closed_form`: φ(90°) = 98.857°, n = 0.3722 (open branch); φ = −135.727°, n = −0.1722 (crossed
  branch) is not what the reference arm returns for a mechanism assembled open.
- `row_is_convex`: the row's Hessian contribution h·(h·k_c + k_c·τ_c)·J_cᵀJ_c is positive semidefinite on random
  configurations.
- `residual_falls_with_stiffness`: the maximum loop residual decreases monotonically from k_c = 10⁴ to 10⁶ in
  `f64`.
- `row_gradient_is_spring_damper`: the gradient of ℓ_c at v₊ equals −h times the implicit spring-damper force
  −k_c·(φ_c + h·J_c·v₊) − k_c·τ_c·J_c·v₊ projected by J_cᵀ, to 10⁻¹² relative in `f64` on random states.
- `falsifier_evaluator`: a planted table in which the best `f32`, h = 2 ms cell has residual 0.8 × 10⁻⁴ m and
  torque error 1.5% gives `falsifier-rejected` with that cell as the default; one with 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ m, or with
  2.1%, in every cell gives `falsifier-holds`; a cell that meets both only at h = 1 ms does not count.
- `sweep_table_shape`: 4 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32 cells, each carrying the five measures; a missing cell fails the
  table's validation.
- `open_chain_mutation_fails`: with the closure row dropped the maximum residual exceeds 10⁻² m (link scale)
  and the comparison returns `fail`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_closure_row.sh` (idempotent; refuses to overwrite a recorded verdict): checks that the
pilot record is registered with the ratification cited, runs the pilot binary over the full sweep (32 cells,
minutes on one machine, so the per-change lane runs the two mechanisms at h = 2 ms in `f32` and the nightly
lane the full table), and validates the pilot record. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/pilot_closure_row/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript; every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`
(`N16b-22`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code,
wall time and the evidence block (claim Fidelity, class Tested, tier `batch-cpu`, profile, precision,
determinism class). `metric` lines, one per sweep cell: mechanism, k_c in N/m, τ_c in s, h in s, precision,
maximum and RMS loop residual in metres against 10⁻⁴, RMS crank-torque error against 2%, output-angle error in
rad, Newton iterations used, line-search failures, smallest pivot after scaling. `assert` lines: the reference
arm's closed forms (φ = θ; 98.857° and 0.3722), the open-chain control failing, the falsifier verdict. On
failure: the cell, its seed and the first tick whose residual crossed the bound. Final `summary`: cells run,
assertions passed and failed, the verdict and the chosen (k_c, τ_c). The pilot binary also writes its per-cell
NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/pilot-closure-row/`. No `frob` verb is added.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The four-bar closed forms above (parallelogram identity; crank-rocker 98.857° and 0.3722).
- Negative control (mutation): the closure row dropped (an open chain); the residual grows to the link scale
  within the sweep and the comparison must fail.
- Negative control (instrument): the crossed-branch root reported as the open one must be caught by the
  reference comparison.
- Determinism: the table is identical twice on one machine in each precision.

## Considerations

- The falsifier's numbers (10⁻⁴ m, 2%) are this bead's proposal; the plan states none for the compliant row.
  They are recorded as DESIGN and must be ratified, not silently adopted.
- A very stiff row conditions the Hessian badly in single precision; the pilot's pivot measure is there to show
  where the diagonal scaling of the solve stops helping.
- A loop driven through its toggle is outside the sweep on purpose: there the transmission ratio is unbounded and
  no soft row will track it.
