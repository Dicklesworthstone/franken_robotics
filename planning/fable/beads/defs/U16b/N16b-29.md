## Background

A proof obligation is a mathematical statement the design relies on, with a named route by which it is
discharged: a proof, a certificate, a derivation kept on file, or a test at a stated size. The dynamics chapter
has five. They matter because other mechanisms are built on them: batch-composition invariance rests on two
facts about padded arithmetic; the transactional step's retries rest on the refinement law of random inputs; the
lockstep solve's uniqueness rests on strict convexity; the tier correction's unbiasedness rests on one
expectation identity.

This bead puts each obligation on record: a derivation file under `docs/derivations/kinesis/`, the test that
checks it at its stated size, and, where the route is a certificate, the link to the theorem card. Like the
invariant register (N16b-28) it is the release's view; the mechanisms carry their own tests.

## Scope

In: five derivation files, five tests, the certificate link for PO-KIN-01, registry rows.
Out: the mechanisms (the batched step, the Cholesky kernel, the reduction kernels, the retry ladder and the
estimator), and the instance certificates of individual kernels (the certificate beads).

## Technical approach

| ID | Obligation | Class and route |
|---|---|---|
| PO-KIN-01 | The lockstep objective stays strictly convex for every admitted parameter row and lagged friction coefficient: A ≻ 0 and each ℓ_c convex (theorem card T-08: the fast-tier contact objective has a positive-semidefinite Hessian). | Certified per law; tested per parameter row |
| PO-KIN-02 | The leading block of a padded Cholesky factor equals the unpadded factor. | Proved (elementary); tested bit for bit |
| PO-KIN-03 | A power-of-two-aligned pairwise sum with trailing −0.0 entries equals the sum over the real prefix, for every capacity (with +0 entries it fails when the prefix sums to −0.0). | Proved by induction on the tree; tested |
| PO-KIN-04 | The dyadic refinement law reproduces the law of Brownian increments: child variances ℓ/2, zero covariance. | Derivation on file; statistical test at 10⁶ draws |
| PO-KIN-05 | The expectation of Zᵢ given the past equals the expected higher-tier outcome for any frozen ĝ when pᵢ is known. | Derivation on file; null simulation |

**Derivations to write (content, not pointers).**

*PO-KIN-01.* The lockstep step minimizes Φ(v) = ½·(v − v_f)ᵀ·A·(v − v_f) + Σ_c ℓ_c(J_c·v) with A the mass
matrix plus implicit joint damping and reflected inertia. A ≻ 0 because the mass matrix of a mechanism with
physically consistent inertias is positive definite and the added terms are positive semidefinite. Each ℓ_c is a
convex potential of the law (certified per law). A sum of a strictly convex function and convex functions is
strictly convex, so the minimizer is unique. Friction coefficients that depend on state (film thickness, sliding
speed, temperature) are evaluated from committed state and held constant inside a solve, so the lag does not
break convexity. Hessian: H = A + JᵀGJ with G ⪰ 0.

*PO-KIN-02.* Let H be n × n symmetric positive definite and H' = diag(H, I_p) its padding with identity rows and
zero right-hand side. The Cholesky recurrence computes column j of L from columns 1 … j − 1 and row j of the
matrix only; for j ≤ n those are entries of H alone, so the leading n × n block of L' is L, in the same
operation order and therefore bit for bit. The padded columns give L'ⱼⱼ = 1 and zero elsewhere. Padded links are
identity transforms with zero inertia appended after the real ones.

*PO-KIN-03.* Reductions over a padded axis use pairwise trees aligned to powers of two with the real entries in
a prefix. Induction on the tree height: a subtree whose leaves are all −0.0 sums to −0.0 (since
(−0.0) + (−0.0) = −0.0), and x + (−0.0) = x for every float x including −0.0 and +0.0, so a node with one real
child and one all-padding child returns the real child's value unchanged. Hence the tree over capacity P reduces
to the tree over the smallest power of two that holds the real entries, whatever P is. With +0.0 padding the
claim fails when the prefix sums to −0.0, because (−0.0) + (+0.0) = +0.0 changes the bits. Why grouping must not
depend on capacity: in `f32` with a = 10⁸, b = −10⁸, c = 1, (a + b) + c = 1 and a + (b + c) = 0, because the
spacing near 10⁸ is 8.

*PO-KIN-04.* A node with increment S over length ℓ, S ~ N(0, ℓ), gives its left child L = S/2 + √(ℓ/4)·Z with Z
an independent standard normal, and its right child R = S − L = S/2 − √(ℓ/4)·Z. Then Var(L) = ℓ/4 + ℓ/4 = ℓ/2,
Var(R) = ℓ/2, Cov(L, R) = Var(S/2) − ℓ/4 = ℓ/4 − ℓ/4 = 0, and L + R = S: the law of Brownian increments on the
two halves, conditioned on their sum. The root is S = √ℓ₀·Z₀. Every node is a pure function of
`(WorldAddr, base_interval, stream_id, level, index)`, so no noise state is stored and a retry can refine the
path but never redraw it.

*PO-KIN-05.* With mᵢ = ĝ(Y⁰ᵢ) and pᵢ fixed before the draw and Aᵢ ~ Bernoulli(pᵢ) independent of Y¹ᵢ given the
past: E[Zᵢ | past] = mᵢ + E[Aᵢ | past]·(E[Y¹ᵢ | past] − mᵢ)/pᵢ = mᵢ + (E[Y¹ᵢ | past] − mᵢ) = E[Y¹ᵢ | past]. The
identity does not involve ĝ. Var(Zᵢ) = Var(Y¹ᵢ) + E[(1 − pᵢ)/pᵢ·(Y¹ᵢ − mᵢ)²].

**Tests** (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/po_kin/`).
- `po_kin_01`: for every admitted parameter row of the reference robots and 10³ random states each, the smallest
  eigenvalue of A (by an exact-rational LDLᵀ on a rounded copy, or a certified lower bound) is positive, and
  each row's potential passes a midpoint-convexity check on 10⁴ random pairs; the certificate for CL-1 is linked.
- `po_kin_02`: random SPD matrices of size 18 and 35 padded to 32, 64 and 128: the leading block of the padded
  factor equals the unpadded factor bit for bit, in `f32` and `f64`, on `batch-cpu` and each device back end.
- `po_kin_03`: random prefixes of length 1 to 100 padded to every power of two up to 1,024 with −0.0: sums
  bit-equal; the +0.0 mutation fails on the prefix (−0.0).
- `po_kin_04`: 10⁶ parent draws: sample variances of the two children within 3σ of ℓ/2, sample covariance within
  3σ of 0, L + R = S exactly; and bit-equality of repeated reads of a node.
- `po_kin_05`: a host-level null simulation on synthetic paired outcomes (labelled `synthetic`): for ĝ in
  {identity, constant, negation, noise}, the mean of Z over 10⁵ rounds equals the mean of Y¹ within 3σ; exact
  enumeration for Y ∈ {0, 1}, p = 0.1.

**Registry.** The ledger is the program's one obligations file,
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/obligations.toml`, whose schema, loader and row checks are N28cp2-01's (a
dependency): this bead appends the five PO-KIN rows in that shared row schema and makes no second file. One
`[[obligation]]` row per obligation: `id`, `statement` (the table above), `mechanism` (PO-KIN-01: N18a-16, N12-51,
BD-159; PO-KIN-02: BD-156, N16a-12; PO-KIN-03: BD-152, N35-13; PO-KIN-04: N16c-01, BD-083; PO-KIN-05: BD-264),
`derivation` (the path under `docs/derivations/kinesis/`, for example `docs/derivations/kinesis/po-kin-04.md`),
`discharge` (the test name and its acceptance bound with its numbers, as listed under Tests), `due` = "M2",
`status`, `record` = "none", `claim_ids` (the claim identifiers of the recorded test runs), `gate` (empty; the
gate rows that cite these obligations are their mechanisms'), `recorded_by`. Each row is `discharged` at this
bead's close with the claim identifiers of its test's run. PO-KIN-05 is discharged here by the route the
plan gives it (derivation on file and the null simulation); the same identity on the real estimator is
PO-CHRON-02, which BD-264 discharges at M3 (GATE-FID-08), and PO-KIN-05's `discharge` field names that test
(`wrong_prediction_same_mean`) beside its own. The harness report is `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
registry_report -- obligations --chapter 16 --json` (N28cp2-01's report).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The five tests above, plus:
- `po_kin_03::f32_grouping_counterexample`: (10⁸ + (−10⁸)) + 1 = 1 and 10⁸ + ((−10⁸) + 1) = 0 in `f32`.
- `po_kin_03::negative_zero_prefix`: prefix (−0.0) padded with −0.0 to 8 sums to −0.0 (sign bit set); padded
  with +0.0 it sums to +0.0.
- `po_kin_04::child_moments_exact_formula`: Var(L) = ℓ/2 and Cov(L, R) = 0 from the derivation's algebra,
  checked symbolically on rationals with Z's variance as 1.
- `po_kin_05::enumeration`: Y ∈ {0, 1} with mean θ, constant m, p = 0.1: E[Z] = θ for m ∈ {0, ¼, ½, 1}.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kinesis_surface.sh` (N16b-35) runs the suite through the conformance harness
(`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test po_kin`, structured report enabled). Logged per obligation: identifier,
route, sample size, the measured statistic with its bound (for example the child variance against ℓ/2 ± 3σ),
result, and the derivation file's hash.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-345 (padded Cholesky: leading block equal bit for bit), KA-346 (pairwise sum with trailing −0.0; the
  capacity-dependent grouping counterexample), KA-340 (refinement: half-interval variance ℓ/2, halves
  uncorrelated, sum equal to the parent; inside 3σ at 10⁶ draws), KA-351 and KA-133 (tier-correction identities
  and unbiasedness by enumeration).
- CX-144 (redraw), CX-146 (capacity-shaped reduction).
- Determinism lanes: bit-level tests identical across debug and release and across back ends.

## Considerations

- The counterexample catalogue's entry for the reduction rule prints the canonical zero as +0 in one place; the
  obligation itself, the determinism chapter and the known-answer row all say −0.0, and the +0.0 case is the
  stated failure. The derivation file records −0.0.
- A higher-order stochastic integrator would need a joint law for its iterated integrals and is not offered;
  PO-KIN-04 covers the first-order driver only.
- PO-KIN-01 is certified per law: adding a contact law adds a certificate obligation before the law is admitted
  on lockstep tiers.
