## Background

The fidelity ledger is the user-visible record of how far a fast tier lands from a trusted one. An agent meets it
through one read verb, `fidelity.ledger`, and through the refusal it gets when it asks to promote a result in a
stratum whose envelope was never frozen. This bead is the end-to-end script for that capability: it drives the
real `frob` binary on the real battery of one reference class, from an empty store to frozen envelopes, and it
checks the two negative controls that make the capability trustworthy (a tampered battery, and an envelope edited
after outcomes). It is the reduced-scale rehearsal of GATE-FID-01; the gate's full-scale record is BD-212's.

## Scope

In: one script, its fixtures, assertions and log schema.
Out: the mechanisms (N16b-01, N16b-02, BD-212, N16b-21); full-scale gate runs.

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/fidelity_ledger.sh`, plain shell, sources the shared helper of STD-E2E, exits non-zero
on the first failed assertion, idempotent (it works in a fresh scratch store under `target/e2e/fidelity_ledger/`
and deletes nothing outside it), seed-explicit (`--seed 17` everywhere a seed is accepted).

**Steps.**
1. `battery`: list the standard battery for the quadruped class and print its identity. Assert the scenario
   count is between 180 and 220 and that the identity equals the frozen one.
2. `tamper-battery`: copy the battery file into the scratch store, delete one scenario, attempt to load it under
   the frozen version. Assert a refusal naming both hashes.
3. `measure`: run the battery (a 32-address subset in the per-change lane; all addresses nightly) on
   `batch-cpu` lockstep and on the referee with the same addresses; where the referee refuses, the strict
   profile adjudicates. Assert pair rows exist for every address with a non-numerical outcome on both tiers, and
   that numerical outcomes are counted by cause.
4. `read-unfrozen`: `frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --json`. Assert
   `envelope: null` and the next action "freeze" on every stratum.
5. `promote-unfrozen` (enabled when `eval.promote` lands at M3; until then skipped with a logged reason, the
   admission check being covered by BD-212's unit tests): promote a result of the stratum. Assert
   `FROB-REF-821`, exit 3, repair "freeze the envelope first".
6. `freeze`: freeze the envelopes. Assert each stratum now prints a bound, a unit and a freeze date, and that a
   second freeze is refused.
7. `gate-read`: read median and 95th percentile for base displacement over 2 s, energy per metre and stance
   impulse. Record them against 2% and 5%, 3% and 8%, 5% and 12%. In the reduced lane the values are logged as
   metrics; the assertion is only that they are present with units and that the evaluator's verdict field
   exists. In the nightly lane they are asserted against the thresholds in force (the re-baselined ones if the
   re-baseline document exists).
8. `tamper-envelope`: in a copy of the store, edit one envelope bound after the pairs exist. Assert the hash
   mismatch is detected and promotion on that stratum is refused.
9. `missed-contacts`: read the quantity `missed-contacts`; in a conformance build, run the thin-barrier fixture
   with the endpoint-only mutation and assert one missed contact.
10. `determinism`: repeat steps 3 and 6 with a different worker count. Assert identical pair-row digests per
    tier and identical envelope identities.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/fidelity_ledger/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a human-readable transcript
beside it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (the bead under test at that step), `step`, `event` (`start`,
`cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and their
thresholds, digests where state is compared, and on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths
needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives counts of assertions passed and failed and the gates touched
(GATE-FID-01). Logs contain no private-scene data.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, is executable, passes on a machine with no GPU, and fails (non-zero exit) when any
      assertion fails.
- [ ] All ten steps log `start`, at least one `cmd` and `assert`, and `end`.
- [ ] Both tamper steps are caught; removing either check from the product makes the script fail.
- [ ] The log validates against the STD-E2E line schema; the summary names GATE-FID-01.
- [ ] Two consecutive runs produce identical digests for every compared artifact.
- [ ] The reduced lane completes in under ten minutes on the development machine (TARGET).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own; the script is the test. A shell-lint check and a dry-run mode (`E2E_DRY_RUN=1`, which prints
the commands without running them and validates the log schema) run in the per-change lane.

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified above. Commands used: `frob task list --battery standard --class quadruped --json`,
`frob eval run --battery standard --class quadruped --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep --seed 17 --json`,
the same with `--tier referee`, `frob fidelity ledger … --json`, `frob eval promote r:… --json` (from M3), and
the store-inspection helper of STD-E2E for the tamper steps.

### Known answers and negative controls

- GATE-FID-01's thresholds (2%/5%, 3%/8%, 5%/12%) as logged metrics or assertions by lane.
- Negative controls exercised: battery with a scenario removed; envelope chosen after the outcomes (the control
  of KA-350); endpoint-only gap check (the control of KA-338) on the thin-barrier fixture.
- CX-149 is exercised by a pair fixture whose state error is inside the envelope and whose success bit differs:
  the script asserts it counts as an exceedance.
- Determinism lanes: twice; across worker counts; debug and release builds of `frob` in the nightly lane.

## Considerations

- The reduced lane uses a 32-address subset chosen by address hash, so it is the same subset every time.
- Referee time dominates; the nightly lane runs the full battery and is where the gate assertions bind.
- At M2 the promotion verb is registered and answers "not implemented in this build"; step 5 therefore runs
  from M3, and the M2 evidence for the 821 path is the unit test of the admission check.
