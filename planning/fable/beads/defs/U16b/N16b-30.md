## Background

The fidelity ledger is the user-visible record of how far a fast tier lands from a trusted one. An agent meets it
through one read verb, `fidelity.ledger`, and through the refusal it gets when it asks to promote a result in a
stratum whose envelope was never frozen. This bead is the end-to-end script for that capability: it drives the
real `frob` binary on the real battery of one reference class, from an empty store to frozen envelopes, and it
checks the two negative controls that make the capability trustworthy (a tampered battery, and an envelope edited
after outcomes). It is the reduced-scale rehearsal of GATE-FID-01; the gate's full-scale record is BD-212's.

## Scope

In: one script, its fixtures, assertions and log schema.
Out: the mechanisms (the frozen battery N38a-20, pair rows N16b-02, the envelope store, freeze action, tamper
check and the verb `fidelity.ledger` N16bp1-01, the M2 measurement BD-212, missed contacts N16b-21); full-scale
gate runs.

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/fidelity_ledger.sh`, plain shell, sources the shared helper of STD-E2E, exits non-zero
on the first failed assertion, idempotent (it works in a fresh scratch store under `target/e2e/fidelity_ledger/`
and deletes nothing outside it), seed-explicit (`eval.run`'s battery form takes no seed: the battery's items
carry their own `(run_seed, world, episode)` addresses, so two runs of one battery share their addresses by
construction, and the `BatteryId` echoed in `bound` is logged on every run; `FROB_TEST_SEED=17` on the Gauntlet
row of step 9).

**Steps.**
1. `battery`: start the fast half of the measurement (the first command of step 3) and read the battery from what
   `eval.run` itself reports: the `BatteryId` echoed in the envelope's `bound` block and the `battery.items` list
   of the run record (no verb lists a battery at M2; `eval.items` is an M3 verb). Assert that the identity equals
   the frozen one and, in the nightly lane on `standard`, that the scenario count is between 180 and 220 (in the
   per-change lane, that it equals the item count of the `smoke` manifest).
2. `tamper-battery`: copy the battery file into the scratch store, delete one scenario, attempt to load it under
   the frozen version. Assert a refusal naming both hashes.
3. `measure`: run the battery (the `smoke` battery of the quadruped, N24b-01, in the per-change lane; `standard`
   nightly; `eval.run` has no option that selects part of a battery, and a part could never be frozen) on
   `batch-cpu` under its default `lockstep` profile and on the referee, on the battery's own addresses (the same
   `BatteryId` in both runs' `bound`, asserted); where the referee refuses by name, the pairing hook (N16bp2-01) routes the address to the `strict`
   profile. Assert pair rows exist for every address with a non-numerical outcome on both tiers, and
   that numerical outcomes are counted by cause.
4. `read-unfrozen`: `frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --json`. Assert
   `envelope: null` and the next action "freeze" on every stratum. Then the case table (N16bp1-01 step 10):
   `frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --cases --json` returns zero rows on the fresh store; after the
   STD-E2E store-inspection helper writes one synthetic case through `record_case` (labelled `synthetic`), the
   same read returns that one row with `kind: "case"` and schema `frob.fidelity.case/1`, the strata read above
   is unchanged (no case enters a stratum), and `--cases --quantity energy-per-metre` is `FROB-REF-802` (the
   input `cases` of `fidelity.ledger`'s row, VERBS-fidelity).
5. `promote-unfrozen` (a stage that runs once `frob capabilities --json` lists `eval.promote` as implemented, at
   M3; until then it is logged `skipped` with that reason, the admission check being covered at M2 by
   N16bp1-01's unit test `refusal_821_on_unfrozen`): promote a result of the stratum. Assert `FROB-REF-821`, exit
   3, repair "freeze the envelope first". Its passing is not this bead's criterion (KNOWN_DRIFTS 7): the same
   refusal through the verb is accepted at M3 by N16b-31 (`scripts/e2e/promotion.sh`, step 5), which depends on
   BD-263.
6. `freeze` (the freeze action of N16bp1-01, an option of the battery run). (a) Without the scope
   `fidelity:admin`, `--freeze-envelopes` is refused with `FROB-REF-720`, exit 5, and the run itself proceeds.
   (b) Incomplete strata: in a second scratch store the option is given on the fast-tier run, before the referee
   has run any address, and reports `not-frozen: incomplete` for every stratum (a freeze is tied to a battery
   that is complete on both tiers). (c) The freeze proper: the referee run of step 3 is repeated under a new key
   with the option (pair writes are idempotent on stratum and address, so the repeat adds no row), on `smoke` in
   the per-change lane and on `standard` nightly. Assert each frozen stratum
   now prints a bound, a unit, a freeze date and its `EnvelopeId`; that repeating the freeze with identical
   content returns the same `EnvelopeId`s (idempotent); and that a freeze of the same stratum with one bound
   altered is refused with the existing `EnvelopeId` in the payload.
7. `gate-read`: read median and 95th percentile for base displacement over 2 s, energy per metre and stance
   impulse. Record them against 2% and 5%, 3% and 8%, 5% and 12%. In the reduced lane the values are logged as
   metrics; the assertion is only that they are present with units and that the evaluator's verdict field
   exists. In the nightly lane they are asserted against the thresholds in force (the re-baselined ones if the
   re-baseline document exists). The same step reads the intrinsic rows (`--tier-pair batch-cpu,batch-cpu
   --quantity coefficient-lag`) and logs n, median and 95th percentile per coefficient law and contact mode, or
   `coefficient_laws: 0`.
8. `tamper-envelope`: in a copy of the store, edit one envelope bound after the pairs exist. Assert that
   `fidelity.ledger` prints the stratum as not frozen with detail `envelope-hash-mismatch` and both hashes, and
   that the library check `require_frozen_envelope`, called through the STD-E2E store-inspection helper, refuses
   with `FROB-REF-821` and the same detail. The same refusal through `eval.promote` is a stage that runs from M3
   (as step 5) and is accepted by N16b-31 (its step 9a).
9. `missed-contacts`: `frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --quantity
   missed-contacts --json`, asserting the quantity's counts are present with their windows; then KA-338's row with
   its control, `FROB_TEST_SEED=17 cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test
   g1_known_answers -- KA-338` (the thin-barrier fixture with the endpoint-only gap check planted in the fast
   tier, N16b-21's control), asserting from its receipt one missed contact classified `never` and the control
   `caught`.
10. `determinism`: repeat steps 3 and 6 with a different worker count. Assert identical pair-row digests per
    tier and identical envelope identities.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/fidelity_ledger/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a human-readable transcript
beside it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (the bead under test at that step), `step`, `event` (`start`,
`cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and their
thresholds, digests where state is compared, and on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths
needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives counts of assertions passed and failed and the gates touched
(GATE-FID-01). Logs contain no private-scene data.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, is executable, passes on a machine with no GPU, and fails (non-zero exit) when any
      assertion fails.
- [ ] All ten steps log `start`, at least one `cmd` and `assert`, and `end` (step 5, a stage that cannot run
      before `eval.promote` exists at M3, logs `skipped` with its reason instead); the `bead`
      field names N16bp1-01 on steps 4 to 6 and 8, BD-212 on steps 3 and 7, N38a-20 on steps 1 and 2, N16b-21 on
      step 9.
- [ ] Step 6 shows the scope refusal, the incomplete-stratum report, the idempotent re-freeze and the refused
      altered freeze.
- [ ] Both tamper steps are caught; removing either check from the product makes the script fail.
- [ ] The log validates against the STD-E2E line schema; the summary names GATE-FID-01.
- [ ] Two consecutive runs produce identical digests for every compared artifact.
- [ ] The reduced lane completes in under ten minutes on the development machine (TARGET).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own; the script is the test. A shell-lint check and a dry-run mode (`E2E_DRY_RUN=1`, which prints
the commands without running them and validates the log schema) run in the per-change lane.

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified above. Commands used, in the registry form of `eval.run` (a design, a battery, a tier, the optional
class): `frob eval run quad-a --battery <smoke|standard> --class quadruped --tier batch-cpu --key fl-3a --json`
(its `BatteryId` read from `bound` and logged), the same with `--tier referee --key fl-3b`, the same referee call with `--freeze-envelopes --key
fl-6` (and, in the second scratch store, the fast-tier call with `--freeze-envelopes --key fl-6b` before any
referee run), `frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee [--quantity <q>] --json`,
`frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --tier-pair batch-cpu,batch-cpu --quantity coefficient-lag --json`,
`frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --cases --json`, `frob eval promote r:… --key fl-5 --json` (from M3),
the Gauntlet row of step 9, and the store-inspection helper of STD-E2E for the tamper steps and the synthetic
case. The repeated runs of the determinism step carry keys of their own (`fl-10a`, `fl-10b`) and must show
`idempotent_replay: false`, so that no digest is compared with a stored copy of itself.

### Known answers and negative controls

- GATE-FID-01's thresholds (2%/5%, 3%/8%, 5%/12%) as logged metrics or assertions by lane.
- Negative controls exercised: battery with a scenario removed; envelope chosen after the outcomes (the control
  of KA-350); endpoint-only gap check (the control of KA-338) on the thin-barrier fixture.
- CX-149 is exercised by a pair fixture whose state error is inside the envelope and whose success bit differs:
  the script asserts it counts as an exceedance.
- Determinism lanes: twice; across worker counts; debug and release builds of `frob` in the nightly lane.

## Considerations

- The reduced lane uses the `smoke` battery, a frozen manifest of its own, so it is the same set every time and
  its strata can be frozen; `standard` is measured in the nightly lane.
- Referee time dominates; the nightly lane runs the full battery and is where the gate assertions bind.
- At M2 the promotion verb is registered and answers "not implemented in this build"; step 5 therefore runs
  from M3, and the M2 evidence for the 821 path is the unit test of the admission check.
