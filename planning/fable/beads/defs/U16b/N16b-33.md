## Background

A fast contact model can hand a policy energy that no actuator supplied. The created-energy audit makes that
measurable, and the verb `fidelity.energy` shows an agent the created-energy totals per world and contact mode
for a run, with the worlds that crossed the bound. GATE-FID-06 (Known answer, M2) requires: both planted
exploits flagged within one episode; an honest policy under CL-1 unflagged; created energy on the standard
battery below the frozen bound.

This bead is the end-to-end script of the capability and the bead that records the gate, because the gate's
last clause needs the frozen battery and the bound re-baselined with the fidelity gate, which the unit-level
bead (N16b-14) does not have.

## Scope

In: the script `scripts/e2e/created_energy_gate.sh`; the policy-level exploits and the honest policy through
the verb; the consequence step (promotion blocked, ranking `model-conditional`); the gate's measurement on the
standard battery; the record of GATE-FID-06.
Out, with the bead that has it: the step side of the audit and the contact-mode tag (N16b-14); the ledger
kernel (BD-208); the three monitors, the verb `fidelity.energy` and its schema, and the script
`scripts/e2e/energy_audit.sh`, which runs the monitors' own planted and honest fixtures on `batch-cpu` and on
each device and records the energy half of GATE-CHRON-08 (N29a-22, a dependency of this bead: every step here
reads that verb); the plants (N16b-15). The two scripts measure two gates on one mechanism and share no file:
the name `energy_audit.sh` and the log directory `target/e2e-logs/energy_audit/` are N29a-22's (DESIGN: both
units had drafted a script of that name; the unit that owns the audit keeps it, and this one is named after
what it records).

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/created_energy_gate.sh`; STD-E2E helper; scratch store under
`target/e2e/created_energy_gate/`;
non-zero exit on failure; idempotent; seed 17 (`--seed 17` on `sim.run`; `eval.run`'s battery form takes no
seed, its items carrying their own addresses, and the `BatteryId` from `bound` is logged, VERBS-eval). The
planted steps need a conformance build of `frob`; the
script checks for it and reports the planted steps `not run` (a failure of the gate lane, a skip of the
per-change lane) when absent.

**Steps.**
1. `hand-case`: the single-body fixture with the position-level correction (mutation M1), one touchdown with
   1 mm penetration at h = 2 ms. `frob fidelity energy <run> --json`. Assert created energy 0.125 J ± 1% for
   the event, tagged with its contact mode (one of the verb's five: `none`, `sticking`, `sliding`, `impact`,
   `limit`; here `impact`, the step in which the contact became active, by the rule of N16b-14), and the flag
   `energy-created`.
2. `compliant-case`: the same body under CL-1 at k = 10⁵ N/m. Assert stored energy 0.05 J at 1 mm, created
   energy below the tier's residual bound, no flag.
3. `hopper-exploit`: the hopper with P_exploit under M1 for one episode. Assert the flag within the episode,
   apex height rising, the worst window's ratio above 1%.
4. `pyramid-exploit`: the diagonal-push task under the pyramid cone (mutation M2) for one episode. Assert the
   audit's flags for the episode: `cone-excess` with utilization near √2, and `energy-created` if the extra
   traction turned into net energy; at least one audit flag must be raised within the episode.
5. `honest`: P_honest under CL-1 on 64 addresses. Assert no flag on any world.
6. `consequence`: at M2, assert that the flagged run's claim product (the evidence block `fidelity.energy`
   passes through) has status `provisional` and carries the condition `energy-created`, and run the label rule's
   own tests on it (`cargo test -p frob-core --test trust_label -- conditions_union_and_block
   cite_refuses_provisional`, N16b-04: the condition joins by union and blocks `established`; citing it as
   established is `FROB-REF-820`). The verb halves need M3 verbs and run once `frob capabilities --json` lists
   them as present, being logged `skipped` with the reason until then: `frob eval promote <flagged result> --key
   eg-6 --json` exits 2 with `blocked_by: energy-created` and status `provisional` (BD-263), and a ranking that
   contains the result, checked with `frob fidelity modelform --ranking <r> --k 8 --key eg-6b --json` (BD-266),
   is `model-conditional` with the condition `energy-created`.
7. `folded-residual-control`: a conformance build in which the residual is folded into contact dissipation.
   Assert that step 1 then reports zero created energy, which the script treats as the control being caught
   (the gate's test fails on that build).
8. `battery`: run the standard battery of each reference class on `batch-cpu` lockstep (subset per change; all
   addresses nightly) and read created energy per world. Assert that created energy is below the frozen bound
   in every 0.5 s window of every world (1% of the absolute work exchanged on the fast tier; 10⁻⁶ relative on
   the referee; or the re-baselined values), and log the worst ratio per class.
9. `determinism`: repeat step 3 with another worker count and in another co-tenancy. Assert identical
   created-energy totals and flags.
10. `gate-receipt`: in the gate lane, run the gate's Gauntlet entry, `FROB_TEST_SEED=17 cargo test -p
    frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-FID-06` (module
    `crates/frob-conformance/src/gates/fid_06.rs`, created by this bead: it drives the fixtures of steps 1 to 5 and
    the battery of step 8 through the real binary, runs the folded-residual control of step 7, and writes a
    hash-named receipt under `target/gauntlet/receipts/` with the measured values and one line per registered
    control), and log the receipt hash. The script writes no gate row (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9): a session that did not
    implement the audit records the gate from the receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
    record_gate -- --gate GATE-FID-06 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19).

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/created_energy_gate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` plus a transcript. Every line:
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, measured
values with units and thresholds (created energy in joules against 0.125 J; stored energy against 0.05 J; the
window ratio against 0.01; utilization against 1.05), digests, and on failure the first world, step and contact
mode whose residual differs from expectation. The `summary` line names GATE-FID-06. No private-scene data.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FID-06 is recorded from the receipt of its Gauntlet entry (step 10) with `record_gate`, with measured
      values: both planted exploits flagged within one episode; the honest policy under CL-1 unflagged on 64
      addresses; the worst created-energy ratio on the standard battery per reference class below the frozen
      bound. Recorded by a session that did not implement the audit; no script writes the row.
- [ ] The folded-residual control is registered for this gate and was caught in the same gate run (the receipt
      lists it `caught`; the recorder refuses a receipt that does not).
- [ ] The script passes on a machine with no GPU and fails when the audit is switched off.
- [ ] `fidelity.energy` output carries units, the contact-mode split and the list of worlds over the bound.
- [ ] The log validates against the STD-E2E schema and names the gate.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own. Shell lint and dry-run per change.

### End-to-end script and logging

As above. Commands: `frob sim run … --seed 17 --key eg-<step> --json` (for the plants, with `--conformance-plant
<file>` naming N16b-15's mutation file: `penetration-recovery.json` for steps 1 and 3, `pyramid4-base.json` for
step 4),
`frob fidelity energy <run> --json`, `frob telemetry energy <run> --json` for the ledger lines, the
`cargo test` call of step 6, and `frob eval run <reference design of c> --battery standard --class <c> --tier
batch-cpu --key eg-8-<c> --json` for step 8 (`lockstep` is the tier's default profile, so no
`--profile` is passed and the script needs no option of N16bp2-01); the Gauntlet entry and the recorder call of
step 10; from M3 also the two verb calls of step 6 (`frob eval promote`, `frob fidelity modelform`).
Every `run` verb carries its own idempotency key, so a re-run of the script resumes instead of duplicating work.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-354: 0.125 J per event at 1 kg, 1 mm, 2 ms; CL-1 at k = 10⁵ N/m stores 0.05 J, returns less and creates
  none; an honest policy under CL-1 is unflagged. Control: the residual folded into contact dissipation.
- KA-710 (step 4): the diagonal push on the pyramid reaches utilization √2; a circular-cone run must not be
  flagged. KA-709 (the stiff bounce raises `energy-created`) is asserted by N29a-22's script on N16b-15's
  mutation M3 and is not repeated here.
- CX-147, CX-148.
- Live-dependency proof: halving the planted penetration depth must quarter the created energy per event
  (0.125 J → 0.031 J).
- Determinism lanes: twice; across worker counts; solo against co-tenants.

## Considerations

- The gate text says "both planted exploits flagged". The pyramid exploit is a traction exploit; whether it
  shows as created energy depends on the task. The script accepts any flag of the created-energy audit's three
  monitors for it and logs which fired; the model-form check catches the same plant through the cone arm.
- The bound in force is whatever the single re-baseline at M2 froze; the script reads it from the ledger and
  never hard-codes 1%.
