## Background

A fast contact model can hand a policy energy that no actuator supplied. The created-energy audit makes that
measurable, and the verb `fidelity.energy` shows an agent the created-energy totals per world and contact mode
for a run, with the worlds that crossed the bound. GATE-FID-06 (Known answer, M2) requires: both planted
exploits flagged within one episode; an honest policy under CL-1 unflagged; created energy on the standard
battery below the frozen bound.

This bead is the end-to-end script of the capability and the bead that records the gate, because the gate's
last clause needs the frozen battery and the bound re-baselined with the fidelity gate, which the unit-level
bead (N16b-14) does not have.

## Scope

In: the script; the gate's measurement on the standard battery; the gate record.
Out: the audit (N16b-14, BD-208); the plants (N16b-15).

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/energy_audit.sh`; STD-E2E helper; scratch store under `target/e2e/energy_audit/`;
non-zero exit on failure; idempotent; `--seed 17`. The planted steps need a conformance build of `frob`; the
script checks for it and reports the planted steps `not run` (a failure of the gate lane, a skip of the
per-change lane) when absent.

**Steps.**
1. `hand-case`: the single-body fixture with the position-level correction (mutation M1), one touchdown with
   1 mm penetration at h = 2 ms. `frob fidelity energy <run> --json`. Assert created energy 0.125 J ± 1% for
   the event, tagged with its contact mode, and the flag `energy-created`.
2. `compliant-case`: the same body under CL-1 at k = 10⁵ N/m. Assert stored energy 0.05 J at 1 mm, created
   energy below the tier's residual bound, no flag.
3. `hopper-exploit`: the hopper with P_exploit under M1 for one episode. Assert the flag within the episode,
   apex height rising, the worst window's ratio above 1%.
4. `pyramid-exploit`: the diagonal-push task under the pyramid cone (mutation M2) for one episode. Assert the
   audit's flags for the episode: `cone-excess` with utilization near √2, and `energy-created` if the extra
   traction turned into net energy; at least one audit flag must be raised within the episode.
5. `honest`: P_honest under CL-1 on 64 addresses. Assert no flag on any world.
6. `consequence`: attempt to promote the flagged result. Assert it is blocked by `energy-created`; build a
   ranking containing it and assert the ranking is `model-conditional`.
7. `folded-residual-control`: a conformance build in which the residual is folded into contact dissipation.
   Assert that step 1 then reports zero created energy, which the script treats as the control being caught
   (the gate's test fails on that build).
8. `battery`: run the standard battery of each reference class on `batch-cpu` lockstep (subset per change; all
   addresses nightly) and read created energy per world. Assert that created energy is below the frozen bound
   in every 0.5 s window of every world (1% of the absolute work exchanged on the fast tier; 10⁻⁶ relative on
   the referee; or the re-baselined values), and log the worst ratio per class.
9. `determinism`: repeat step 3 with another worker count and in another co-tenancy. Assert identical
   created-energy totals and flags.
10. `record`: in the gate lane, write the GATE-FID-06 row with the measured values of steps 1 to 5 and 8.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/energy_audit/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` plus a transcript. Every line:
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, measured
values with units and thresholds (created energy in joules against 0.125 J; stored energy against 0.05 J; the
window ratio against 0.01; utilization against 1.05), digests, and on failure the first world, step and contact
mode whose residual differs from expectation. The `summary` line names GATE-FID-06. No private-scene data.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FID-06 is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with measured values: both planted exploits flagged
      within one episode; the honest policy under CL-1 unflagged on 64 addresses; the worst created-energy
      ratio on the standard battery per reference class below the frozen bound. Recorded by a session that did
      not implement the audit.
- [ ] The folded-residual control is registered for this gate and was caught in the same gate run.
- [ ] The script passes on a machine with no GPU and fails when the audit is switched off.
- [ ] `fidelity.energy` output carries units, the contact-mode split and the list of worlds over the bound.
- [ ] The log validates against the STD-E2E schema and names the gate.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own. Shell lint and dry-run per change.

### End-to-end script and logging

As above. Commands: `frob sim run` (with the conformance options for the plants), `frob fidelity energy <run>
--json`, `frob telemetry energy <run> --json` for the ledger lines, `frob eval promote`, `frob fidelity
modelform`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-354: 0.125 J per event at 1 kg, 1 mm, 2 ms; CL-1 at k = 10⁵ N/m stores 0.05 J, returns less and creates
  none; an honest policy under CL-1 is unflagged. Control: the residual folded into contact dissipation.
- KA-709 and KA-710: the stiff bounce raises `energy-created`; the diagonal push on the pyramid reaches
  utilization √2; a circular-cone run must not be flagged.
- CX-147, CX-148.
- Live-dependency proof: halving the planted penetration depth must quarter the created energy per event
  (0.125 J → 0.031 J).
- Determinism lanes: twice; across worker counts; solo against co-tenants.

## Considerations

- The gate text says "both planted exploits flagged". The pyramid exploit is a traction exploit; whether it
  shows as created energy depends on the task. The script accepts any flag of the created-energy audit's three
  monitors for it and logs which fired; the model-form check catches the same plant through the cone arm.
- The bound in force is whatever the single re-baseline at M2 froze; the script reads it from the ledger and
  never hard-codes 1%.
