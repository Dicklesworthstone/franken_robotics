## Background

The background referee is visible to an agent in three places: the admin verb `fidelity.shadow` that starts,
stops and configures it for a job; the `referee_coverage` block that every training and evaluation receipt
carries (with a share of zero when no background referee ran); and the read verb `fidelity.coverage`, which
returns both coverage figures for a run or a class: the share of device work that was shadowed, and the
referee's own coverage, that is, how much of the class's battery the referee can complete at all. This bead is
the end-to-end script of that capability. Its M2 section rehearses the clauses of GATE-FID-07 recorded at the
M2 exit; its M3 section (enabled when the later beads have landed) rehearses the detection clauses, the
kill-gate default and the fallback.

## Scope

In: one script with an M2 section and an M3 section, assertions, logging.
Out: the mechanisms (BD-214, N16b-17, N16b-18, BD-213, N16b-20) and the gate records (BD-214 at M2, BD-268 at
M3).

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/background_referee.sh`; STD-E2E helper; scratch store under
`target/e2e/background_referee/`; non-zero exit on failure; idempotent; seed 17 (`--seed 17` on `sim.run`;
`eval.run`'s battery form takes no seed, because the battery's items carry their own addresses, and the
`BatteryId` echoed in `bound` is logged instead). Fast tier: the device tier if present, otherwise `batch-cpu`
lockstep (the no-GPU variant), with the tier logged.

**M2 section.**
1. `no-sampler`: run the quadruped velocity task for about 30 s of wall time with no background referee, and one
   evaluation of the `smoke` battery on the fast tier. Assert both receipts carry `referee_coverage` with
   `share: 0`, zero counts and no adjudicator.
2. `start`: start the same run; `frob fidelity shadow start --job <id> --p 0.002 --cpu-cores 4 --tier referee
   --json`. Assert the admin scope is required (a call without `fidelity:admin` is refused with `FROB-REF-720`,
   exit 5) and the configuration echoes; on a build without the feature `frontier-shadow` (checked once in the
   nightly lane with the default-feature binary) the verb answers `FROB-REF-724`, exit 5.
3. `status`: after 30 s, `frob fidelity shadow status --job <id> --json`. Assert shadowed world-seconds above
   zero, the measured referee real-time factor printed, the predicted share c·ρ_rt/(T·h) printed beside the
   measured share, `dropped_snapshots` present.
4. `stop-and-receipt`: stop the shadow, then the run. Assert the receipt's `referee_coverage` has
   `gpu_world_seconds`, `shadowed_world_seconds`, `share` equal to their ratio, `one_step_defects`,
   `outcome_pairs`, `adjudicator`.
5. `coverage-verb`: `frob fidelity coverage --run <id> --json` and `--class quadruped`. Assert both figures are
   present and distinct: the run's shadow share; the class's referee completion share with its breakdown by
   refusal cause (contact cap, fixed-witness gate, missing joint class, kinematic loop), the strict share and
   the promotion wall-clock percentiles; the adjudicator named when completion is below 90%.
6. `composition`: repeat step 2 with a different world count (and, in the no-GPU variant, another operator-only
   `--workers` count of `sim.run`, which changes the tiling of worlds over workers; `eval.run` has no such input),
   each run under its own key. Assert the sampled
   (address, tick) list restricted to common addresses is identical. (No verb option chooses a slot order; the
   100-permutation slot test is BD-214's unit test `sample_is_address_function`.)
7. `no-physics-touch`: assert per-world state digests of the run with and without the sampler are equal.
8. `throughput`: three on/off pairs back to back (ten in the nightly lane). Log the ratio and its lower bound
   against 0.95; assert in the nightly lane.
9. `planted-kernel-defect` (conformance build): run with the planted compaction-stage defect. Assert that
   `frob fidelity shadow status --job <id> --json` reports stage 5 as `suspect` within the first minute and no
   other stage; log the time to the first defect in seconds and the defects by stage (N16b-17).
10. `stalled-worker`: pause the CPU workers. Assert the device job's step time is unchanged within noise and
    `dropped_snapshots` grows.

**M3 section** (skipped with a logged reason until BD-268's dependencies exist).
11. `planted-policy`: run the planted tier-sensitive policy as an evaluation job with the outcome lane on.
    Assert the alarm fires and log the measured and predicted detection times against the factor of two.
12. `honest`: the honest control raises no alarm in the same time.
13. `ledger-pairs`: assert the outcome pairs appear in the fidelity ledger and, where the probability floor
    holds, in the tier-correction ledger.
14. `kill-default`: force `shadow.enabled = false`. Assert `fidelity.shadow start` answers with the fallback's
    description and starts nothing.
15. `fallback-audit` (only if N16b-20 was built, that is KILL-SHADOW fired on this back end; if N16b-20 was
    closed as `branch not taken`, the step is skipped with that reason): with the default off, run a short job
    with the planted defect, wait for the offline audit job (`fidelity.shadow audit --run <id>`, enqueued by the
    daemon). Assert no CPU worker ran during the job, the run receipt prints share zero with the pending-audit
    note, the snapshot stream is pinned until the audit has read it, and the linked audit receipt names the
    planted stage with `outcome_pairs: 0`.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/background_referee/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` plus a transcript. Every
line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block,
measured values with units and thresholds (share against 0.001; throughput ratio bound against 0.95; detection
time against twice the prediction), digests (sampled-set digest, world digests), and on failure the first
differing value with artifact paths. The `summary` line names GATE-FID-07. No private-scene data: snapshots are
never written to the log, only their digests and counts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The M2 section passes on a machine with no GPU (fast tier `batch-cpu`) and on each device machine.
- [ ] Step 1 proves that a run with no sampler prints a share of zero; step 4 proves the block's arithmetic.
- [ ] Steps 6 and 7 fail if sampling is made slot-dependent or touches a physics stream (checked once against
      the registered mutations).
- [ ] The M3 section exists as script stages (steps 11 to 15) that skip with a logged reason naming the missing
      bead keys. Their passing is not this bead's criterion (no bead waits on what waits on it): steps 11 to 13
      are accepted by BD-268, step 14 by N16b-19 and step 15 by N16b-20, each of which depends on what its steps
      need.
- [ ] The log validates against the STD-E2E schema and names the gate.
- [ ] Cancelling the script mid-run leaves no shadow worker, snapshot queue or device buffer behind (checked by
      `frob doctor`).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own. Shell lint and dry-run per change.

### End-to-end script and logging

As above. Commands: the shadowed device job is `frob sim run quad-a --task flat-velocity --tier <fast tier>
--worlds <n> --seconds <s> --seed 17 --key br-<step> --json` (with `--workers <w>` in step 6 of the no-GPU
variant), the evaluation receipt of step 1 is `frob eval run quad-a --battery smoke --class quadruped --tier
<fast tier> --key br-1b --json`, then `frob fidelity shadow start|status|stop|configure --job <id>
--json` (and `audit --run <id>` on the fallback
branch), `frob fidelity coverage --run <id> --json` and `--class <c> --json`, `frob run show <id> --json` for the
receipt, `frob doctor --json`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-361 (coverage arithmetic: the predicted share), KA-363 (planted kernel defect localized; planted policy
  detected within twice the predicted time; coverage at least 0.1%; loss at most 5%).
- Negative controls exercised: the planted single-kernel defect; the planted tier-sensitive policy;
  arrival-order sampling and a sampler on a physics stream (as registered mutations).
- Determinism lanes: the sampled set twice and across compositions; world digests with and without the sampler.

## Considerations

- At four cores and reduced durations the measured share is small and is logged, not asserted, in the
  per-change lane; the gate's 0.1% is asserted in the gate lane on the full core count.
- The script is the same on CUDA and Metal; only the snapshot path differs underneath (explicit readback against
  a blit read after command-buffer completion).
