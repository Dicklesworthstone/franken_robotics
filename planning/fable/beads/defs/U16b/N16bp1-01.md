## Background

FrankenRobotics runs one robot model on three tiers: the slow, most trusted `referee`; `batch-cpu`, the CPU
reference of the batched step; and the GPU tiers `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal`. The **fidelity ledger** (strata,
quantities, pair rows and stratum summaries: N16b-02) records, per stratum, the distribution of disagreement d
between a faster configuration and a slower one on the standard battery (N38a-20). A **stratum** is the key
(model class, scene class, quantity, tier pair, profile pair, contact-law pair, kernel versions, step-plan pair,
battery version).

An **envelope** is the frozen tolerance on d inside which a faster tier counts as agreeing with a slower one for one
(stratum, quantity). Promotion (BD-263) asks one question per audited episode: did the two tiers disagree beyond the
frozen envelope? The error bounds of promotion hold at any stopping time only if the envelope was hashed into the
ledger before any promotion sampled an outcome. An envelope tuned after outcomes voids every bound; this is the
primary risk of the trust ladder (the DC-01 decision core's stated risk), and its countermeasure is this bead: the
envelope is a content-hashed, immutable ledger record, and a promotion in a stratum without one is refused
(`FROB-REF-821`, "Envelope not frozen"; repair: freeze the envelope first).

This bead was split from the seed bead BD-212 so that the mechanism, which can be built and tested on synthetic
strata, does not wait for the M2 measurement campaign, and so that beads needing only the envelope mechanism or the
read verb can start early. BD-212 keeps the measurement on the frozen battery, GATE-FID-01 with its single
re-baseline, and the act of freezing the M2 envelopes with this bead's machinery.

Rejected alternatives: per-run tolerances chosen by the caller (they are tuned after outcomes); one global
tolerance (stance impulse and base displacement do not share a scale); an editable envelope with an audit trail
(an edit after outcomes is exactly the failure; immutability plus a new stratum is simpler and fails closed);
freezing as a side effect of reading (a `read` verb changes nothing).

## Scope

In: the envelope record and its identity; the freeze routine and its immutability; the bound rule written into
each record; tamper detection by hash; the admission function `require_frozen_envelope` with `FROB-REF-821`; the
exceedance-bit function every consumer uses; the single re-baseline record (a second is refused) and its example
target `record_rebaseline`; the table of tier-sensitive cases written from outside the battery, its write function
and its read input; the freeze action reached from the surface; the read verb `fidelity.ledger` with its schemas,
pagination, exit codes and reachability through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`.
Out: running the battery and the M2 measurement, GATE-FID-01, the re-baseline document's content and the freeze of
the M2 envelopes (BD-212); strata, quantities, floors, pair rows and summaries (N16b-02); the battery and its
identity (N38a-20); promotion and its table (BD-263, N16b-05); the registry row of `fidelity.ledger` (VERBS-fidelity,
which this bead implements).

## Technical approach

**1. Envelope record (schema `frob.fidelity.envelope/1`, table `fidelity_envelope` in `frob-chronicle`).**

```
DESIGN
struct Envelope {
    stratum_key: StratumKey,        // N16b-02, includes battery version and kernel versions
    quantity: QuantityId,           // registry of N16b-02
    bound: f64,                     // on d, in the quantity's disagreement unit (relative or absolute)
    unit: Unit,                     // "relative", "m", "rad", "N·s", "count", "bit"
    floor: f64,                     // the quantity's floor from N16b-02's registry, in its physical unit
    basis: Basis,                   // GateThreshold { gate: "GATE-FID-01" } | MeasuredP95 | DecisionExact
    battery_id: BatteryId,          // BLAKE3 of the battery manifest (N38a-20)
    kernel_versions: KernelVersions,
    pair_set_hash: Blake3,          // hash of the (address, pair-row digest) list the bound was computed from
    frozen_at: UtcTimestamp,
    rationale: Option<Blake3>,      // hash of the re-baseline document, if any
    session: SessionId,
}
EnvelopeId = BLAKE3(canonical bytes of every field above)
```

Canonical bytes are the canonical JSON of the record (keys sorted, numbers in shortest round-trip form); the floor
is inside the hash, so a changed floor is a different envelope.

**2. Bound rule (DESIGN; written into every record's `basis` so a reader of the ledger sees how each bound arose).**
- The three gated quantities take the GATE-FID-01 95th-percentile thresholds in force: base displacement over 2 s
  5%, energy per metre 8%, stance impulse 12% (relative), or their re-baselined values (BD-212).
- Every other continuous quantity: the measured 95th percentile of d on the stratum's pair rows, rounded up to two
  significant digits (for example 0.0731 → 0.074; 3.04 → 3.1). The plan gives no number for these.
- Task decisions (success, terminal class, verdict of each comparison): no tolerance; any differing decision is an
  exceedance (`basis = DecisionExact`, `bound = 0`).
- Intrinsic rows (N16b-02; at M2 the one quantity `coefficient-lag`, per coefficient law and contact mode): the
  measured 95th percentile of Δ rounded up to two significant digits (`basis = MeasuredP95`). No pair exists, so
  `pair_set_hash` is the hash of the (address, histogram digest) list, and `exceeds` is never evaluated on such
  an envelope. It records the lag the battery showed at the freeze, so that a later change of law or step is a
  new stratum and a regression is visible beside the frozen number.
With the bound at the battery's 95th percentile, the battery's own exceedance rate is about 5%, which is the rate
p₀ = 0.05 that the promotion table (N16b-05) treats as faithful.

**3. Freeze routine** `frob_chronicle::fidelity::freeze(stratum, frontier, rule) -> Result<Vec<EnvelopeId>>`:
1. Read the stratum's pair rows at a pinned committed frontier; refuse if any battery address of the stratum lacks
   a pair row or an `unpaired` cause (incomplete measurement; the completeness query is N16bp2-01's
   `stratum_complete`). For an intrinsic stratum, refuse if any address of the fast run lacks its accumulator
   histogram. For a stratum whose quantity is measured on a committed measurement record instead of a whole
   battery run (N16bx-04's `port-wrench-projection`, whose record lists its addresses, links and directions and
   is committed to the ledger before the stratum's first pair row), the address set is that record's list, read
   by the record's hash, and the same completeness rule applies to it; a record committed after a pair row of its
   stratum is refused (it could have been chosen after outcomes).
2. Compute each quantity's bound by the rule above from the integer histogram of N16b-02 (so the 95th percentile
   is reproducible bit for bit).
3. In one ledger transaction per stratum write one `Envelope` per quantity, or nothing.
4. A frozen (stratum, quantity) is immutable: a second freeze with identical content returns the existing
   `EnvelopeId` (idempotent); with different content it is refused with the existing `EnvelopeId` in the payload.
A new kernel version, contact law, profile, step plan or battery version is a new stratum with no envelope.

**4. Tamper detection.** `verify(envelope_id)` recomputes the hash from the stored fields. DESIGN: a record whose
recomputed hash differs from its `EnvelopeId`, or whose `pair_set_hash` no longer matches the stored pair rows, is
treated as not frozen: `require_frozen_envelope` returns `FROB-REF-821` with detail `envelope-hash-mismatch` and both
hashes, and the dependent promotion is refused.

**5. Admission** `require_frozen_envelope(stratum, quantity) -> Result<EnvelopeId, Refusal>`: returns the verified
`EnvelopeId`, or `FROB-REF-821` ("Envelope not frozen": promotion requested for a quantity whose envelope is not
hashed in the ledger; ranked repairs: (1) freeze the envelope first by measuring the battery for that stratum and
running the freeze action; (2) promote under a stratum that has one), exit code 3. At M2 this is a library function
with its own tests; from M3 `eval.promote` (BD-263) calls it first.

**6. Exceedance bit** `exceeds(envelope, pair) -> bool`, used by promotion, the background referee and every
other consumer so that one definition exists: 1 if d > bound for the quantity (DESIGN: d equal to the bound is
inside), or if any task decision of the pair differs; else 0. A state error inside the envelope with a different
decision is an exceedance (CX-149).

**7. Single re-baseline record, per cause.** `record_rebaseline(gate, cause, doc_hash, changed: [(quantity, old,
new)])` writes one row per (gate, cause); a second call for the same pair is refused with a message naming the
first document's hash. `cause` is a closed list (DESIGN): `m2-baseline`, the one re-baseline a gate is permitted
when it is first measured at M2 (GATE-FID-01 by the plan, BD-212); and `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` and
`upstream:FD-FSIM-12`, the two upstream
changes that replace the referee's contact stepper, whose switch-over rule in the plan is "re-baseline the
fidelity ledger once". Any other cause is refused. There is no code path that edits a frozen envelope: a
re-baselined threshold reaches the ledger only through strata frozen after the record exists (BD-212 orders it
so), and an upstream re-baseline freezes the new strata that the new referee kernels create, while the old strata
and their envelopes stay as they were. A script or a gate run reaches the function through a Cargo example target
of this crate, on the pattern of the gate recorder (neither a verb nor a binary): `cargo run -p frob-chronicle
--example record_rebaseline -- --gate <gate> --cause <cause> --doc <path>`. It reads the document at `<path>`,
computes its BLAKE3 hash, takes the changed thresholds `(quantity, old, new)` from the document's front matter,
calls `record_rebaseline`, and prints one JSON object (`{ gate, cause, doc_hash, changed, recorded }`); a refused
second record or an unknown cause exits non-zero with the refusal and writes nothing. Users: BD-212
(`--gate GATE-FID-01 --cause m2-baseline`), NFp2-01 (`--cause upstream:FD-FSIM-02`), NFp3-11 (`--gate GATE-FID-01
--cause upstream:FD-FSIM-12`).

**8. Freeze action on the surface (DESIGN).** The plan names the repair "freeze the envelope first" but registers
no verb for it, and `fidelity.ledger` is a `read` verb that changes nothing. Freezing is therefore an option of the
battery run: `frob eval run <design> --battery standard --class <c> --tier <slow tier> --freeze-envelopes --key
<k> --json`. When the
run has completed every address of a stratum on both tiers, the job calls the freeze routine for each such stratum
and lists the new `EnvelopeId`s in its result; a stratum that is still incomplete is reported `not-frozen:
incomplete`. The option requires the capability scope `fidelity:admin` (capability tokens: N32b-01); without it the
run proceeds and the option is refused with `FROB-REF-720` (scope missing), exit 5. This ties every freeze to a full
battery measurement, never to a hand-picked subset; the one other caller of the routine outside the Gauntlet
harness, N16bx-04's example target, freezes strata whose address set is a measurement record committed before
any of their pairs (item 3, step 1), which is not hand-picked either. The option is in `eval.run`'s row of the `eval` registry
(VERBS-eval lists it with this bead as its owner). The handler it extends is N24c-04's, a dependency, and
completeness is read from the pair rows that the pairing hook N16bp2-01 writes. The same routine is callable from
the Gauntlet harness, which is how BD-212 freezes inside its gate run.

**9. Verb `fidelity.ledger`** (kind `read`, first milestone M2, capability `fidelity:read`, MCP tool
`fidelity_ledger`, owner `frob-dyn` with the store in `frob-chronicle`).
- Input `frob.fidelity.ledger.in/1`: `class` (required), `tier_pair` (required, ordered fast,slow), `quantity`
  (optional, default all), `scene_class`, `profile_pair`, `law_pair`, `kernel_versions`, `battery` (`--battery`,
  a battery name or a `BatteryId`; optional, default the frozen battery of the class), `cases` (flag: list the
  tier-sensitive cases of step 10 in place of strata), and the pagination properties the generator adds
  (`cursor`, `limit`, `follow`). Intrinsic rows are asked for with a tier pair that
  names one tier twice (`--tier-pair batch-cpu,batch-cpu --quantity coefficient-lag`); such a row has
  `kind: "intrinsic"`, its sub-key `{ coefficient_law, contact_mode }` and `null` in every pair-only field, and a
  pair row has `kind: "pair"`.
- Output `frob.fidelity.ledger.out/1`: rows of `{ stratum_key, kind, sub_key, n, unpaired_by_cause, median, p95,
  p99, max, bias, rho { continuous, binary }, decisions { agree, discordant_fast_only, discordant_slow_only },
  envelope { id, bound, unit, basis, frozen_at, rationale } | null, adjudicators { referee, batch-cpu:strict }, gate
  { id, threshold_median, threshold_p95, verdict } | null }` (`kind` is `pair` or `intrinsic`; `sub_key` is `{
  coefficient_law, contact_mode }` on an intrinsic row and `null` otherwise), paginated against a committed
  frontier (`next_cursor`, frontier pinned for the whole walk). With `--cases` (step 10) the rows are the
  tier-sensitive cases instead.
- An unfrozen stratum prints `envelope: null` and `next_actions: ["freeze"]` with the freeze command line.
- Exit codes: 0; 2 when a returned stratum misses its gate threshold (a finding, not an error); 3 on a schema
  failure (`FROB-REF-802`); the surface-wide pagination refusals 806 and 807 apply.
- Evidence: pass-through of the stored rows (claim Fidelity, class Tested, determinism D3 across tiers); the read
  itself is exact.
- CLI example: `frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --quantity energy-per-metre
  --json`.

**10. Tier-sensitive cases from outside the battery (DESIGN; plan §26.6).** A failure that the Nemesis artifact
guard (BD-272, M3) labels `artifact-candidate` is "written to the fidelity ledger as a tier-sensitive case,
because a failure of the simulator is also a finding". Such a world is not a battery address, so a case enters no
stratum, no distribution, no envelope and no promotion; it has its own table `fidelity_case` in `frob-chronicle`
(schema `frob.fidelity.case/1`): `{ case_id, source ("nemesis.guard"), class, run, world (WorldAddr and world
identity), model_id, candidate (the failure's identity), quantity ("task-decision"), pair_or_variant ({ tier_pair:
[fast, slow] } or { variant: "half-step" | "iterations-x2" | "law-2" | "tol-div-10" }), step_plan_ids: [original,
replay], outcomes: [original, replay], recorded_at }`, with `case_id` = BLAKE3 of the canonical bytes of the other
fields except `recorded_at`. Write: `frob_chronicle::fidelity::record_case(case) -> CaseId` through the evidence
sink, idempotent on `case_id` (a second guard run over the same failure adds nothing). Read: `fidelity.ledger
--class <c> --cases` lists the cases of a class, newest first, paginated against the same pinned frontier; with
`--cases`, `--tier-pair` is optional and filters on the case's tier pair, and `--quantity`, `--profile-pair`,
`--law-pair` and `--battery` are `FROB-REF-802`. A case row has `kind: "case"`, the record above, and `null` in
every stratum field. Until BD-272 lands the table is empty and the read returns no rows (exit 0). The input
`cases` is on the verb's row (VERBS-fidelity: `--cases`, a flag).

**Layering.** Records and the freeze transaction in `frob-chronicle`; the pure exceedance and bound functions in
`frob-dyn::fidelity::envelope`; the verb handler in `frob-dyn` through `frob-verbs`. No scheduler or estimator here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob.fidelity.envelope/1` records are written by one transaction per stratum, are immutable, and carry their
      basis; `EnvelopeId` covers every field including floor, basis, pair-set hash and rationale.
- [ ] `require_frozen_envelope` returns `FROB-REF-821`, exit 3, repair "freeze the envelope first" on an unfrozen
      stratum and on a tampered record (detail `envelope-hash-mismatch` with both hashes); it returns the
      `EnvelopeId` on a frozen, verified stratum.
- [ ] A new kernel version, law, profile, step plan or battery version yields `envelope: null`.
- [ ] A second re-baseline record for one (gate, cause) is refused, naming the first document; a cause outside the
      closed list is refused; `m2-baseline`, `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` and `upstream:FD-FSIM-12` for GATE-FID-01 are
      each accepted once, independently of one another;
      `cargo run -p frob-chronicle --example record_rebaseline -- --gate GATE-FID-01 --cause m2-baseline --doc
      <path>` writes the record and a second identical call exits non-zero.
- [ ] `record_case` writes a `frob.fidelity.case/1` row idempotently; `fidelity.ledger --class <c> --cases` lists
      it with `kind: "case"`; no case appears in any stratum, summary or envelope.
- [ ] An intrinsic `coefficient-lag` stratum freezes with `basis = MeasuredP95`, is listed by `fidelity.ledger`
      under a tier pair naming one tier twice, and is never passed to `exceeds`.
- [ ] The freeze refuses an incomplete stratum and is idempotent on identical content.
- [ ] `fidelity.ledger` is reachable through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp` (in process and through `frobd --mcp`)
      with the registry's schemas; its `--help --json` matches the golden file of VERBS-fidelity; an unfrozen
      stratum prints `next_actions: ["freeze"]`; pagination returns the same rows as a single page at a pinned
      frontier.
- [ ] `--freeze-envelopes` without the `fidelity:admin` scope is refused with `FROB-REF-720`, exit 5.
- [ ] `frob-chronicle/CONTRACT.md` and `frob-dyn/CONTRACT.md` describe the record, the freeze, the refusal and the
      verb.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-chronicle/tests/fidelity_envelope.rs` and `crates/frob-dyn/tests/envelope.rs` (real store in a
temporary directory; strata labelled `synthetic`):
- `envelope_hash_covers_floor_and_bound`: changing the bound from 0.05 to 0.051, or the floor from 1 cm to 2 cm,
  changes `EnvelopeId`.
- `freeze_is_immutable`: a second freeze of a frozen (stratum, quantity) with a different bound is refused and the
  row is unchanged; with identical content it returns the same id.
- `freeze_refuses_incomplete_stratum`: 199 of 200 addresses paired → refused, nothing written.
- `freeze_record_address_set`: a `synthetic` stratum whose address set is a committed measurement record of 3
  addresses freezes when all 3 are paired or carry an unpaired cause, is refused with 2 of 3, and a record
  committed after the stratum's first pair row is refused.
- `new_kernel_version_has_no_envelope`: the same class under a new kernel version returns `envelope: null`.
- `refusal_821_on_unfrozen`: `require_frozen_envelope` on a stratum with no envelope returns `FROB-REF-821` with
  the repair "freeze the envelope first" and exit code 3; on a frozen stratum it returns the `EnvelopeId`.
- `tamper_detected`: editing the stored bound from 0.05 to 0.06 after freezing makes `require_frozen_envelope`
  return `FROB-REF-821` with detail `envelope-hash-mismatch`.
- `exceedance_bit`: with bound 5% on displacement, d = 4.9% gives 0, d = 5.0% gives 0, d = 5.1% gives 1; d = 1%
  with a differing success bit gives 1 (CX-149).
- `bound_rule_p95_rounding`: measured p95 0.0731 → 0.074; 3.04 → 3.1; gated quantities take 5%, 8%, 12%.
- `rebaseline_once_per_cause`: a second `m2-baseline` record for GATE-FID-01 fails with a message naming the first
  document; `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` for the same gate is accepted once and refused the second time;
  `upstream:FD-FSIM-12` is accepted once after both of those records exist, and refused the second time, and the
  same holds in the other order (each cause independent of the others); the cause `tuning` is refused.
- `record_rebaseline_example`: the example target on a scratch document whose front matter changes energy per
  metre from 0.08 to 0.09 prints `recorded: true` with the document's BLAKE3 hash; the same call again exits
  non-zero naming that hash and leaves the store unchanged.
- `case_table_separate`: a `synthetic` case (`variant: "half-step"`, outcomes `[violated, success]`) written twice
  leaves one row; `fidelity.ledger --cases` lists it with `kind: "case"`; the stratum summaries and envelopes of
  the class are byte-identical before and after; `--cases --quantity energy-per-metre` is `FROB-REF-802`.
- `intrinsic_freeze_and_read`: a `synthetic` `coefficient-lag` stratum with a 95th percentile of 0.0412 freezes at
  bound 0.042 with `basis = MeasuredP95`; `fidelity.ledger` with `--tier-pair batch-cpu,batch-cpu --quantity
  coefficient-lag` lists it with its sub-key; a freeze with one address's histogram missing is refused.
- `ledger_verb_pagination`: 120 strata read in pages of 25 equal one read of 120, at a pinned frontier.
- `ledger_verb_unfrozen_next_action`: an unfrozen stratum prints `envelope: null` and `next_actions: ["freeze"]`.
- `ledger_verb_exit_2_on_missed_gate`: a synthetic stratum with median 2.1% on displacement returns exit 2.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/fidelity_ledger.sh` (N16b-30) drives this bead's surface: `read-unfrozen`, `freeze`, the second-freeze
refusal, `tamper-envelope` and the 821 path. Logged per STD-E2E: the exact command, exit code, wall time, the
evidence block, `BatteryId`, every `EnvelopeId` with its bound, unit and basis, the refusal code and detail, and on
failure the first stratum whose envelope differs between two runs.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-350's negative control: an envelope chosen after the outcomes; the tamper test must catch it.
- CX-149: a state error inside the envelope with a different decision is an exceedance.
- Negative control (mutation): `EnvelopeId` computed without the floor; `envelope_hash_covers_floor_and_bound` fails.
- Negative control (mutation): the freeze writes quantities in separate transactions; a fault injected between two
  writes leaves a partly frozen stratum and the atomicity test fails.
- Determinism lanes: identical `EnvelopeId`s for the same pair rows twice on one machine, across worker counts,
  debug and release, Linux and macOS (the record is class D0).

## Considerations

- Who may freeze: freezing is an admin act because a freeze is the moment the risk of tuning closes. Open question
  (owner: Kinesis lead): whether an agent's token may ever carry `fidelity:admin`. Fail-closed default: no; agents
  receive `FROB-REF-821` and the repair names the admin action.
- The bound rule for ungated quantities is a DESIGN choice; if the exceedance rate on fresh addresses drifts far
  from 5%, the stratum is re-measured as a new version, never edited.
- FD-FSIM-02 (a persistent contact time stepper upstream) changes the referee; the new referee is a new stratum
  with no envelope, not an edit of frozen ones. Its re-measurement and freeze are recorded once under the cause
  `upstream:FD-FSIM-02` (step 7).
- Storage: one envelope is under 1 kB; a few thousand per release are negligible.
- Privacy: envelopes hold statistics and hashes only, never scene contents.
