## Background

A kill gate is a decision named in advance, with its trigger, its milestone, the measurement that decides it, its
fallback, and what changes downstream. Each is recorded as an architecture decision record when decided, by a
bead of the unit that owns the mechanism (the rule of the fallback ledger N40-23, followed by N40-24).

The variants table lists a dual-arm base, two arms closing a loop through a grasped payload, at tier Frontier;
the plan names no kill gate for it. The gate register carries one all the same: **KILL-RUNTIMECLOSURE**, entered
by N40-24 through an `introduce` amendment, so that the pilot `PILOT-KIN-05` (N16bx-01) has a stop rule to name
and the pilot register (N38a-09) can accept its record. As registered:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Clause (a) or (c) of the runtime-closure pilot's falsifier holds: under `strict`, a capture velocity or energy loss of fixture A off by more than 10⁻⁹ relative, or an energy-ledger residual across an attachment above the transaction's tolerance; or a world's digest changed by a co-tenant's attachment |
| Decided at | M6, mode `pilot-before-funding`: due when WP-33 asks to fund N16bx-02, and at the latest at the M6 exit |
| Measurement (`reads`) | The record `PILOT-KIN-05` in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`: fixture A's capture velocities, energy losses and closure forces under `strict`, the energy-ledger residual per attachment, the solo and mixed-cohort digests, the lockstep sweep with the verdict of clause (b), the decision (PASS, PARTIAL or FAIL) and the verdict |
| Fallback | No run-time loops: a dual-arm variant carries its payload welded to both tools at compile time, with no grasp transition |
| Downstream if it fires | N16bx-02 is not built and is closed with reason `fallback`; the dual-arm variant offers the compile-time weld only; the status table names the fallback |

Clause (b), the lockstep criterion, is measured in the same run and is not part of the trigger: a PARTIAL
decision (only (b) holds) is `not-fired` and admits N16bx-02 under `strict` only. Without this bead the row has no
decider and the M6 exit finds no owner declaration (`owner-declaration-missing`, N40-30). Tier: Frontier [F].

## Scope

In: reading the pilot record; evaluating clauses (a) and (c) exactly as registered, and reporting clause (b)
beside them; the decision record `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-RUNTIMECLOSURE-M6.md`; the row's `decision` entry and the owner
declaration; N16bx-02's release form and the status table. Out: running the pilot (N16bx-01); building the
closure (N16bx-02); entering the row (N40-24).

## Technical approach

**Procedure.**
1. Read `PILOT-KIN-05` (N16bx-01): fixture A under `strict` (post-capture velocities against 0.15 m/s and 1/15
   m/s, energy losses against 0.015 J and 1/24 J, closure forces), the energy-ledger residual across each
   attachment beside the transaction's tolerance, the solo and mixed-cohort digests, the lockstep table with
   clause (b)'s verdict, the decision, the verdict, the reviewer, the receipt and the `protocol_blake3`, which
   must equal the registered hash. A missing result is not a pass: step 3 decides it.
2. Evaluate each clause from the record alone. Clause (a): some capture velocity or energy loss differs from its
   truth by more than 10⁻⁹ relative, or some ledger residual exceeds the tolerance. Clause (c): some world's
   digest differs between its solo run and the mixed cohort. Clause (b) is recorded beside them and decides only
   the profiles of a release. Check the record's decision and verdict against this evaluation (FAIL and
   `falsifier-holds` exactly when (a) or (c) holds); a disagreement stops the bead and is reported to the
   pilot's owner, since the verdict sets funding.
3. Name the outcome in N40-03's four: `fired` if (a) or (c) holds; `not-fired` if the pilot has a result and
   neither holds, with the release form PASS (both profiles) or PARTIAL (`strict` only); `not-reached` if WP-33
   never asked to fund N16bx-02 and the pilot has no result by the M6 exit; `deferred` only through an ADR
   naming the later exit and its reason.
4. Write `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-RUNTIMECLOSURE-M6.md` with N40-03's fields: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger
   as registered and the `introduce` amendment; each input with its receipt hash beside the clause it decides
   (the worst relative error of fixture A against 10⁻⁹, the worst ledger residual against the tolerance, the
   count of digests that changed); clause (b)'s verdict and the release form; the outcome and which clause
   decided; the fallback in force and the capability's name from now on ("dual-arm base: compile-time weld
   only"); the beads re-read, each with N40-03's status (N16bx-02: "stands on the fallback" and closed on `fired`
   or `not-reached`; released in the form the ADR names on `not-fired`); the recording session, which neither ran
   the pilot nor implements N16bx-02.
5. Registry: the `decision` entry of the row in `registries/gates.toml` (N40-03's form; for this
   `pilot-before-funding` row `receipts` holds the `receipt` hash of the `[[pilot.result]]` read in step 1,
   committed at `receipts/pilots/<hash>.json` by N38a-09's rule, and is empty only on `not-reached`) and the owner
   declaration
   (`[[gate]]`, `role = "owner"`, the ledger's current fields copied, `decision` with each measured value citing
   its receipt) in `frob-dyn`'s `contract.toml`.
6. If `fired` or `not-reached`: close N16bx-02 with reason `fallback`, citing the ADR; the status table row reads
   "dual-arm run-time closure: fallback (KILL-RUNTIMECLOSURE)". If `not-fired`: N16bx-02 is released with the
   form the ADR names (PASS: `strict` and `lockstep`; PARTIAL: `strict` only, a lockstep request refused with
   `FROB-REF-724` and the repair `--profile strict`) and the supported default (k_c, τ_c) if the record gives one.

**Fail-closed default until decided.** No run-time attachment exists in any build; dual-arm carrying uses the
compile-time weld.

**Who decides.** The Dynamics lead prepares the record; a fresh-context session that did not run the pilot
reviews and records it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_gates.rs`:
- `kill_runtimeclosure_record_complete`: trigger, inputs, outcome, date and session present; the ADR's path is
  `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-RUNTIMECLOSURE-M6.md`.
- `kill_runtimeclosure_rule`: on synthetic records: every fixture-A value within 10⁻⁹ relative, residuals inside
  tolerance, digests unchanged, (b) not holding → `not-fired`, PASS; the same with (b) holding → `not-fired`,
  PARTIAL; a capture velocity of 1/15 m/s·(1 + 2·10⁻⁹) → `fired` by (a); one mixed-cohort digest differing from
  its solo run → `fired` by (c); no result at the M6 exit and no funding request → `not-reached`.
- `kill_runtimeclosure_b_alone_never_fires`: (b) holding at every stiffness of the sweep with (a) and (c) not
  holding → `not-fired`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_runtimeclosure.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N16bp4-02 milestone=M6 labels=kinesis,kill-gate
gates=KILL-RUNTIMECLOSURE seed=0 needs=cpu plants=not-fired-with-cotenant-digest-change`) re-derives the outcome
and the release form from the stored record, compares them with the ADR and the gate row, and runs `cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (asserting no failure names KILL-RUNTIMECLOSURE) and `cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test kill_gates -- kill_runtimeclosure`. It exits non-zero on disagreement. It sources
`scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes `target/e2e-logs/kill_runtimeclosure/<utc>.ndjson`; every line carries
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; it logs the record's
digest, per clause the measured value against its threshold, the derived and recorded outcomes and release form,
and a `summary` naming KILL-RUNTIMECLOSURE. It writes nothing to the registry.

### Known answers and negative controls
Fixture A's truths are the record's references: 0.15 m/s and 1/15 m/s, 0.015 J and 1/24 J, closure forces
(10, 10) N and (9, 8) N. Negative control (plant `not-fired-with-cotenant-digest-change`): an entry `not-fired`
while the stored record shows a co-tenant's digest change makes the script fail, and `kill_ledger` reports
`outcome-contradicts-measurement`.

## Considerations

- The fallback is the FAIL form of the pilot's decision table. A failure of clause (a) is a failure under
  `strict` itself, so no profile keeps the run-time closure when the gate fires.
- Single-arm carrying adds no loop and is untouched by any outcome.
