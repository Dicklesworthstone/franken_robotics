## Background

The fidelity ledger is a table, keyed by (model class, scene class, quantity, tier pair, profile pair,
contact-law pair, kernel versions), of the distribution of disagreement between a faster configuration and a
slower one on the standard battery. Its quantities are listed by the dynamics chapter: base displacement,
yaw, stance impulse, slip distance, peak joint torque, energy per metre, time of first failure, missed
contacts, the energy-ledger residual and task decisions. For each (stratum, quantity) an **envelope**, the
tolerance within which the faster tier is accepted, is frozen and hashed into the ledger before any promotion
samples; a promotion in a stratum with no frozen envelope is refused (FROB-REF-821, "envelope not frozen";
repair: freeze the envelope first). An envelope tuned after outcomes voids every error bound of promotion.

The manufacturing chapter promotes load cases like any other result. Its envelope load cases are extracted
from a load campaign on a fast tier, and "envelope cases are re-extracted on the referee before they enter a
packet": the bead that does this (N31b-35) replays the world on the referee around the instant of the case and
compares the two tiers' values "with the ledger's frozen envelope ε for port-wrench projection on this
stratum": `established` when |p_ref − p_fast| ≤ ε·|p_ref|, `tier-sensitive` otherwise. The quantity
`port-wrench projection` is not among the ledger's quantities, nothing measures its disagreement on a
battery, and nothing freezes an envelope for it. As drafted, every re-extraction would be refused with
FROB-REF-821, or, worse, someone would pick ε while looking at re-extracted cases.

This bead adds the quantity, measures it, and freezes its envelopes before the first re-extraction can run.
Tier Solid (it is the trust ladder applied to one more quantity). Rejected alternatives: reusing the envelope
of `peak joint torque` (a joint torque is one coordinate of what a link's ports carry and its disagreement is
dominated by actuator limits, not by contact peaks); a single global ε (the disagreement of wrench peaks
depends strongly on contact law pair and on scene class: stairs against flat floor).

## Scope

In: the definition of the quantity and of one audited pair; the battery addresses it is measured on; the
measurement run; the stratum summaries; the envelope records and their freeze; `fidelity.ledger` output for
the quantity; the ordering guard that makes a re-extraction impossible before the freeze.

Out: load campaigns, load coordinates, scales and direction sets (N31a-06, N31a-08, N31a-10); the
re-extraction itself and its labels (N31b-35); GATE-FOUNDRY-14 (N31b-37); the promotion e-processes and the
tier-correction ledger (BD-263, BD-264), which are not used here: a load case is compared once against the
envelope, it is not sampled sequentially.

## Technical approach

**1. Definition (taken from the manufacturing chapter's load space, so that the ledger and the consumer use
one formula).** For a link class with load coordinates ℓ ∈ ℝⁿ (the port wrenches as resultants at each port
frame and the body field; n = 18 for a two-port link, 24 for three ports), the campaign's frozen scale vector
s (the campaign maximum of |ℓⱼ| at iteration 0 of the structure loop) and a direction d of the campaign's
direction set, the projection of a sample is p = d·(ℓ ⊘ s), dimensionless. The audited quantity for one
(world address, link, direction) is the **relative disagreement of the envelope value**:

  δ = |p_ref − p_fast| / |p_ref|,

where p_fast is the fast tier's maximum of p over the episode, attained at tick t*, and p_ref is the referee's
own maximum of p inside the window [t* − 0.5 s, t* + 0.5 s] of a comparison replay from the nearest fast-tier
snapshot (the same window and the same formula the consumer uses). One pair is one δ. Pairs with
|p_ref| < p_floor = 0.05 are outside the quantity's domain (DESIGN: a projection below 5% of the campaign scale
is not an envelope candidate, and δ is ill-conditioned there); they are counted under the cause `below-floor`
and take no part in the percentiles.

**2. Stratum key.** The ledger's key with `quantity = "port-wrench-projection"`: (model class, scene class,
tier pair, profile pair, contact-law pair, kernel versions), plus two sub-keys carried in the envelope record
because disagreement differs sharply between them: `port_kind` ∈ {`contact-end` (a link with an end effector),
`interior`} and `direction_set` (`axes` or `axes+pairs`).

**3. Addresses.** The measurement uses the frozen standard battery of each reference class, restricted to
the battery's locomotion and manipulation episodes that the load campaign's task mix covers (the list is
written into the measurement record before the run). For each address, each link class and each direction of
the `axes` set, one pair. Pairs whose replay cannot be made (a referee refusal: contact capacity, a kinematic
loop) are counted in `unpaired_by_cause` and never dropped silently.

**4. Measurement run.** Fast tier: the campaign's tier and profile with T2 on every world. Referee: the
comparison replay of item 1. Output per stratum: n, the unpaired counts by cause, median, 95th and 99th
percentile and maximum of δ, the bias (median of signed (p_fast − p_ref)/|p_ref|: a compliant contact model is
expected to under-read peaks, and the sign says so), and the rank correlation of p_fast and p_ref.

**5. Freeze.** Through the freeze routine of the envelope store (N16bp1-01, `frob_chronicle::fidelity::freeze`),
with the measurement record's address list as the stratum's address set, so that its completeness check (every
listed address has a pair or an unpaired cause) applies here too (the address set of a stratum measured on a
committed measurement record, N16bp1-01 step 1). The surface is not `eval.run --freeze-envelopes`: that option
freezes the strata of a whole battery run from the pairing hook's rows, and this measurement is a committed
slice made of `sim.run` runs and referee replays of windows, which `eval.run` cannot select. It is this bead's
example target in `frob-foundry` (the crate that holds the projection; DESIGN):
`cargo run -p frob-foundry --example port_wrench_ledger -- --record <measurement record> [--freeze]`. It reads
the runs and replays the record names, computes each pair's series with N31a-10's projection and δ with the
ledger function of item 1, writes the pair rows and the unpaired causes to the ledger (idempotent on stratum,
address, link and direction), and with `--freeze` calls `frob_chronicle::fidelity::freeze` for each complete
stratum, printing the `EnvelopeId`s or `not-frozen: incomplete`; it writes no gate row. For each (stratum, port kind, direction set) one envelope record of the ledger's
schema `frob.fidelity.envelope/1`: `quantity = "port-wrench-projection"`, `bound` = the measured 95th percentile of
δ rounded up to two significant digits, `unit = "relative"`, `floor = 0.05`, `basis = MeasuredP95` (N16bp1-01's
rule for a continuous quantity with no gate threshold), the battery
identifier, kernel versions, `frozen_at`, the session. `EnvelopeId` = BLAKE3 of the canonical bytes. One
ledger transaction per stratum; immutable afterwards. A new kernel version, contact law, profile, step plan
or battery version is a new stratum with no envelope. The plan gives no numeric threshold for this quantity,
so there is no gate value to re-baseline against: the envelope is what was measured, and the record says so.

**6. Ordering guard.** The re-extraction reads the envelope by `EnvelopeId` and writes that identifier and
the ledger hash into every record. The ledger refuses to freeze an envelope for a stratum in which a
re-extraction record already exists (there can be none, because re-extraction refuses without an envelope;
the test plants one to prove the refusal). The freeze transaction's sequence number is lower than that of any
re-extraction record that cites it; the offline check of a build packet verifies this from the ledger.

**7. Surface.** `frob fidelity ledger --class <c> --tier-pair <fast>,referee --quantity port-wrench-projection
--json` (class and tier pair are required inputs of the verb, N16bp1-01) returns the stratum summaries with
their envelopes or `envelope: null`; the human form prints the bias sign.

**8. Use by the consumer (stated so that the two beads agree).** `established` iff
|p_ref − p_fast| ≤ ε·|p_ref| with ε the stratum's bound; otherwise `tier-sensitive`, the referee's value
replaces the fast one, and both are reported. A case with |p_ref| < p_floor is outside the envelope's stated
domain: it stays `provisional` with the reason `below-envelope-floor` and the referee's value is still the one
reported. Such a case is admitted to a build packet with its label printed and the referee's value (N31b-36's
packet-side admission, DESIGN there), because its value is the referee's and the envelope makes no statement
below its floor: the provisional reason is not a packet refusal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `port-wrench-projection` is a registered ledger quantity with the definition of item 1 in
      `frob-dyn/CONTRACT.md`. One definition in the workspace (contract lint): δ, the ±0.5 s window maximum
      and the floor are one exported function of the ledger in `frob-dyn`, taking the scalar projection series;
      the projection p = d·(ℓ ⊘ s) is N31a-10's function in `frob-foundry` (which sits above `frob-dyn` and
      hands the series down), called by the measurement run, never re-implemented. The consumer N31b-35, which
      depends on this bead, calls both; that call is its criterion.
- [ ] The measurement record (addresses, link classes, direction set) is committed before the measurement
      run; the run reports per stratum n, unpaired by cause, median, p95, p99, maximum, bias and rank
      correlation.
- [ ] An envelope is frozen for every stratum the first load campaign will use (the reference quadruped and
      the mobile manipulator, flat and stair scene classes, the campaign's tier pair), split by port kind;
      each has an `EnvelopeId`; `fidelity.ledger` prints them.
- [ ] Before the freeze, the admission call a re-extraction makes, `require_frozen_envelope` of N16bp1-01 on
      that stratum, returns FROB-REF-821 (repair: freeze the envelope first) and `fidelity.ledger` prints
      `envelope: null` with `next_actions: ["freeze"]`; after it, the same call returns the envelope with its
      `EnvelopeId` and the ledger hash. The re-extraction's own refusal and its record citing the `EnvelopeId`
      are N31b-35's criteria.
- [ ] A frozen stratum is immutable: repeating the freeze with identical content returns the same
      `EnvelopeId` (idempotent, N16bp1-01), and a freeze with a different bound is refused with the existing
      `EnvelopeId` in the payload; a freeze attempted after a planted re-extraction record exists is refused
      with the record named.
- [ ] Synthetic check of the definition: p_fast = 0.80 and p_ref = 1.00 give δ = 0.20, `established` with
      ε = 0.25 and `tier-sensitive` with ε = 0.15; p_fast = 0.03 and p_ref = 0.02 is below the floor, is counted
      under `below-floor` in a measurement and is `provisional: below-envelope-floor` in a re-extraction.
- [ ] A new contact-law pair is a stratum with `envelope: null`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-dyn/tests/fidelity_port_wrench.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-dyn fidelity_port_wrench` (and
`cargo test -p frob-foundry fidelity_port_wrench_series` for the one test placed there).
- `delta_definition`: the synthetic values above, exact; `below_floor_excluded`.
- `projection_matches_foundry` (in `crates/frob-foundry/tests/fidelity_port_wrench_series.rs`, because the
  projection is `frob-foundry`'s): the series the measurement run hands to the ledger equals N31a-10's
  projection on 10³ random (ℓ, s, d) triples, bit for bit (the shared function, not a copy).
- `window_maximum_is_referees_own`: a referee trace whose maximum falls 0.2 s after t* is found; one 0.7 s
  after is outside the window and the in-window maximum is used.
- `envelope_rounding_up`: a measured p95 of 0.1834 freezes as 0.19.
- `freeze_immutable` (identical content returns the same id; a different bound is refused);
  `freeze_after_reextraction_refused` (a planted re-extraction record in the ledger);
  `require_frozen_envelope_before_freeze_821` (the port-wrench stratum before and after its freeze).
- `freeze_incomplete_measurement_refused`: one address of the measurement record with neither a pair nor an
  unpaired cause blocks the freeze of its stratum.
- `unpaired_counted_by_cause`.
- `ledger_example_writes_pairs_and_freezes` (in `crates/frob-foundry/tests/fidelity_port_wrench_series.rs`): the
  example target on a scratch store and a two-address synthetic record writes one pair row per (address, link,
  direction), is idempotent on a second run, and with `--freeze` prints one `EnvelopeId` per complete stratum
  and `not-frozen: incomplete` for a stratum with an address missing.
- `stratum_key_distinguishes_port_kind`.
- `bias_sign`: a fixture in which the fast tier under-reads every peak by 10% reports bias −0.10.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/fidelity_port_wrench.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/fidelity_port_wrench/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript): `frob --json fidelity ledger --class
<c> --tier-pair <fast>,referee --quantity port-wrench-projection` before the measurement (`envelope: null`);
the measurement run through `frob --json sim run … --key pw-run` on the battery slice with T2 everywhere and
`frob --json sim replay --tier referee --comparison … --key pw-<addr>` on the windows (seed and addresses from the
measurement record; `--comparison` because a fast-tier snapshot restored on the referee is under another
numerical profile, and without it the replay is a continuation and `FROB-REF-894`, VERBS-sim); `cargo run -p frob-foundry --example port_wrench_ledger -- --record <record>` (pair rows,
asserting every listed address has a pair or an unpaired cause), then the same with `--freeze`; the ledger
query again; the same freeze repeated, returning the same `EnvelopeId`; in a scratch copy of the store, one
pair row altered through the STD-E2E store-inspection helper so that the recomputed bound differs, and the
repeated freeze refused with the existing `EnvelopeId` in the payload; and, in a second scratch store, the
freeze with one address of the record missing, reported `not-frozen: incomplete`. The before-the-freeze query asserts `envelope: null` and
`next_actions: ["freeze"]`; the one after asserts the bound and the `EnvelopeId`. A re-extraction before and
after the freeze (`frob foundry loads --for packet`) is the script step of N31b-35, which depends on this bead.
Lines carry `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim,
class `Tested`, tier pair, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` lines per stratum (n, median, p95, bias)
and the frozen bound; `refusal` lines with FROB-REF-821 and the repair; the `EnvelopeId` and ledger hash; on
failure the first pair whose δ differs and its replay address. `summary`: assertions passed and failed;
`gates: []` (GATE-FOUNDRY-14 is measured by its own script).

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the synthetic δ values; the rounding row. No physical known answer exists for the envelope
itself: it is a measured distribution, and the record is its evidence. Negative controls: an envelope written
after a re-extraction (the ordering guard); δ normalized by p_fast instead of p_ref (a fast tier that
under-reads looks better than it is: 0.25 instead of 0.20 on the first synthetic row); the referee value read
at t* instead of at the referee's own maximum (a 5 ms shift of an impact peak reads as a large disagreement:
the fixture with a narrow peak shows it); a pair dropped when the referee refuses. Determinism: the
measurement's addresses and windows are fixed by record; summaries are integer-histogram based and identical
across shard splits.

## Considerations

- Peak contact wrenches are what compliant fast-tier contact gets most wrong. The measured p95 may be large
  (tens of percent on contact-end links). That is information: it says how many envelope cases will be
  relabelled `tier-sensitive`, and it is why the referee's value is the one that enters a packet.
- The envelope is per campaign tier pair. A campaign moved to another fast tier needs its own measurement
  before its cases can be promoted.
- The 0.5 s window is shared with the consumer by definition; changing it in one place changes the quantity
  and requires a new stratum.
- A planted fast-tier spike (the gate's negative control, bead N18bx-03) must be relabelled by the
  re-extraction; it must never be inside the battery this envelope is measured on.
