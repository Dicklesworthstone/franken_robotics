## Background

Under a transactional step profile (the referee, and `strict` on `batch-cpu`) a physics interval can be split at located events and can be retried under a finer numerical plan. A random input that acts **over** an interval (a Brownian disturbance force on the base, process noise inside an actuator) must then mean the same thing however the interval is cut. Two kinds of random input exist:

- a draw **scheduled at a sample instant** (a sensor's noise at its sample time) keeps its original stamp; splitting the interval does not touch it;
- a **driver that acts over the interval** needs a rule that says what its increment over a sub-interval is. That rule is the refinement law.

The law chosen is the standard construction of Brownian motion on a dyadic tree (a Brownian bridge by midpoint refinement), with every node a pure function of its address. Because nothing is stored, a retry, a restart from a checkpoint and a migration to another device all read the same path; a retry can refine the path and can never redraw it. This is invariant INV-KIN-03 ("a random input is a function of its address and interval; it is never redrawn") and proof obligation PO-KIN-04 ("the dyadic refinement law reproduces the law of Brownian increments: child variances ℓ/2, zero covariance").

Rejected alternatives. (1) Redraw on a rejected or split step: biased; with a fair sign rejected when negative and redrawn once, P(positive) = ½ + ½·½ = ¾ (CX-144). (2) A sequential generator whose state is checkpointed: makes a draw depend on how many draws came before, so one extra sub-interval shifts every later draw. (3) Scaling one coarse draw by √(sub-length/length) for each piece: gives perfectly correlated pieces, which is not Brownian. (4) A higher-order stochastic integrator: it would need the joint law of iterated integrals; not offered.

Tier Solid [S]. Crate `frob-dyn`, consumed by the transaction (BD-081, BD-082) and by the retry ladder (BD-083).

## Scope

In: the dyadic tree and its addressing; the increment query for any dyadic sub-interval; the driver registry with the three law kinds; admission refusal `FROB-REF-223`; the statistical and exactness tests; a golden vector that pins the addressing. Out: the stream primitive and the normal construction (the Numerics chapter's addressed streams); the retry loop (BD-083); the task-level coupling test that checks rate invariance of `process` exposures (Arena); device kernels for lockstep tiers, where the step is never split.

## Technical approach

**Time.** Time is rational. The physics grid and the clock boundaries cut time into base intervals [t0, t1) of length ℓ₀ ≤ h, fixed by the schedule and never by the state. Inside a base interval a split may fall only at t0 + ℓ₀·k/2ᴸ for L ≤ L_max = 16.

**Address (DESIGN).**

```rust
/// Noise that acts over an interval is defined on the dyadic tree of the base interval.
pub struct NoiseAddress { world: WorldAddr, base_interval: u64, stream: StreamId, level: u8, index: u32 }
```

`base_interval` is the ordinal of the base interval in the run's schedule. `stream` names the driver (and the component, for a vector driver) in the stream registry. Each address yields one standard normal Z(address).

**The law.** Let a node be a dyadic cell (L, i) = [t0 + ℓ₀·i/2ᴸ, t0 + ℓ₀·(i + 1)/2ᴸ) of length ℓ = ℓ₀/2ᴸ, with increment S(L, i).

1. Root: S(0, 0) = √ℓ₀ · Z(level 0, index 0).
2. Split of node (L, i) with increment S over length ℓ: draw Z = Z(level L + 1, index i); left child S(L + 1, 2i) = S/2 + √(ℓ/4)·Z; right child S(L + 1, 2i + 1) = S − S(L + 1, 2i).
3. Each child then has variance ℓ/4 + ℓ/4 = ℓ/2; the covariance of the two is ℓ/4 − ℓ/4 = 0; their sum is the parent by construction. By induction every cell at level L has variance ℓ₀/2ᴸ and disjoint cells are uncorrelated (jointly Gaussian, hence independent): the law of Brownian increments.

The key fields are fixed by the design; which address owns the split variate of a node is fixed here (the level of the children, the index of the parent; 2ᴸ splits lead from level L to L + 1, so the index is below 2¹⁶) and pinned by a golden vector so that it never drifts.

**Query.** `increment(driver, world, base_interval, cell)`:

1. For a single cell (L, i): descend from the root along the bits of i, L normal draws, no stored state.
2. For a sub-interval [a, b) whose end points are dyadic at different levels: decompose into the maximal dyadic cells it contains (canonical, at most 2·L_max cells) and sum their increments in ascending time order.
3. Physical increment = intensity σ (driver units per √s) × S. A vector driver uses one stream per component.

Cost: at most 16 normal draws per cell; a memo of the current descent path inside one transaction attempt is allowed and is never part of the restore set, because it is a cache of a pure function.

**Driver registry (DESIGN).** Every stochastic driver of a world declares one of:

| Law | Meaning | Under a transactional profile |
|---|---|---|
| `SampleStamped` | The draw belongs to a sample instant and keeps its stamp | Admitted; unaffected by splits |
| `BrownianDyadic { intensity }` | Increment over any sub-interval from the tree above | Admitted |
| none registered | — | Refused at admission |

**Refusal.** `FROB-REF-223`, "No refinement law": raised at admission of a run under `strict` or `referee` when a stochastic driver has no registered law. Envelope fields: `pointer` names the driver and its port, `required` lists the two admissible laws. Ranked repairs: (1) register the law for the driver; (2) move the draw to a sample instant. Retry class `after-change`. Exit code 3.

**Determinism and evidence.** The integer stream is class D0; a normal variate built from deterministic elementary functions is D2 per backend and D1 across backends only where the IEEE probe gate has passed. On the referee the law runs in `f64` on the host. The addresses of the drivers a world read are part of its replay record; no noise state is stored anywhere.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Module `crates/frob-dyn/src/noise/refine.rs` implements the tree, the query and the registry; `CONTRACT.md` states the law, the addressing and the golden vector.
- [ ] PO-KIN-04 at 10⁶ independent parents (ℓ = 2 ms): the sample variance of each half-increment is within 3σ of ℓ/2, that is within a relative 3·√(2/10⁶) = 4.24 × 10⁻³; the sample correlation of the halves is within 3·10⁻³ of zero; left + right equals the parent to one unit in the last place.
- [ ] Additivity at every level: for 10⁴ random cells (L ≤ 16), the sum of the 2ᵐ descendants at depth m equals the cell's increment to round-off.
- [ ] Purity: the increment of a cell is identical when queried first, last, after any other query, in another process and after a checkpoint restore (bit equality).
- [ ] The redraw mutant (a build flag that draws a fresh variate when a step is rejected) fails the fair-sign fixture: observed P(positive) within 3σ of ¾ and outside 3σ of ½ at 10⁶ trials (σ = 5 × 10⁻⁴).
- [ ] A driver with no law under `--profile strict` or `--profile referee` returns `FROB-REF-223` with both repairs; the same driver under `lockstep` is admitted.
- [ ] Adding a new driver stream changes no increment of any existing driver.
- [ ] The golden vector (root and the first two levels for one fixed address) is committed and compared in CI.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-dyn noise::`, real streams, no mocks.

- `children_variance_and_covariance`: 10⁶ parents; asserts the three statistics above with the stated bounds; seed and address range printed.
- `sum_of_children_is_parent`: exact-to-round-off identity on 10⁵ nodes at random levels.
- `level_variance_scales`: variance of cells at level L is ℓ₀/2ᴸ within 3σ for L = 0, 1, 4, 8, 16.
- `disjoint_cells_uncorrelated`: correlation of two non-overlapping cells at different levels within 3·10⁻³.
- `pure_function_of_address`: permuted query order, fresh process, restore from checkpoint: equal bits.
- `non_aligned_interval_decomposition`: the sub-interval [3/8, 11/16) of a base interval has the canonical cover [3/8, 1/2) ∪ [1/2, 5/8) ∪ [5/8, 11/16), that is cells (level 3, index 3), (3, 4) and (4, 10); its increment equals the sum over the level-4 cells 6 to 10.
- `refusal_223_at_admission`: unregistered driver under each transactional profile.
- `stream_insertion_invariance`: registering an extra stream leaves earlier increments unchanged.
- `redraw_mutant_biases_sign` (negative control, feature `mutants`).

### End-to-end script and logging

Covered by `scripts/e2e/kin_retry_refinement.sh` (bead N16c-02): a referee run with a Brownian base disturbance is executed with no forced retry and with a forced retry at chosen intervals; the script asserts equal digests and equal per-interval noise increments read from telemetry, and exercises `FROB-REF-223` through `frob sim run --profile referee --json`. Logging per the STD-E2E helper: command, exit code, evidence block, the statistics with their 3σ bounds, digests, and the first differing interval on failure.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-340: half-interval variance ℓ/2 for a parent of length ℓ; halves uncorrelated; sum equals the parent; tolerance 3σ at 10⁶ draws. Negative control: redraw on rejection, P(positive) = ¾.
- CX-144: the fair-sign fixture, registered in the negative-control registry.
- Metamorphic: split, rejoin, reject and migrate on one noise path all read the same increments (the check of INV-KIN-03); halving the physics step changes no coarse increment.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; 1 and 24 workers.

## Considerations

- The normal construction is the Numerics chapter's; if it changes, every noise path changes and the golden vector must be regenerated under a new stream version, never silently.
- Only Gaussian additive drivers have a law at M1. A Poisson or jump driver would need its own conditional law (binomial thinning); it is refused by `FROB-REF-223` until one is registered.
- L_max = 16 bounds the descent; OPEN-KIN-5 owns the constant (fail-closed: refusal `FROB-REF-220` at the event-location side).
- Interaction: the task audit's rate-invariance check (halving the physics step changes no field value, and a process increment over a coarse interval equals the sum of its fine increments) relies on this law for `process` exposures.
- Privacy: addresses contain no scene data; logs record stream names, not scene content.
