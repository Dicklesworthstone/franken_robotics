## Background

The Kinesis known-answer table has ten rows, each paired with a mutation that must fail. Bead N16c-03 carries the five rows of the transactional step and the free-body integrator (M1). This bead carries the other five: padding invariance and parameter variants on the lockstep tiers (M2), the audit for energy created by the simulator (M2), and the promotion table and the tier correction of the trust ladder (M3). The mechanisms are built by their own beads (the batched step and its variants, the batch-composition invariance test, the energy ledger and audit, the promotion rule, the tier-correction ledger). What this bead adds is the five mutants as registered, buildable defects and one battery that runs each known answer against the correct build and against its mutant, so that the chapter's table is executable in one place.

Terms used below. A *digest* is the BLAKE3 hash of a world's committed snapshot. *Co-tenancy* is the set of other worlds in the same batch. *Capacity* is the fixed size of a per-world buffer (contacts C, rows R, degrees of freedom). The *parameter row* is the optional per-world row of design and randomization values a world reads beside its model slot. The *envelope* of a quantity is the frozen bound on tier disagreement stored (and hashed) in the fidelity ledger before any promotion samples are drawn. An *exceedance* is an audited episode pair whose fast-tier and higher-tier values differ by more than the envelope.

The milestone is M3 because the last two rows need the promotion pipeline; rows 5, 6 and 9 need only M2 mechanisms, and the script's `--rows m2` selector runs them alone (on an M2 checkout as well). The M2 gates those rows serve are recorded by their own beads with their own controls (BD-181, BD-169, BD-208, N16b-33); this battery adds the executable table, not the M2 record.

## Scope

In: registry rows, five control builds (three reused from the mechanism beads, two written here; see Registry and battery), battery rows and the script's `m2` and `m3` selectors. Out: the mechanisms; the gates' own measurement beads (this battery supplies their negative controls, it does not replace the 1,000-episode and 256-variant runs).

## Technical approach

**The five rows.**

| # | KA | Case | Truth | Tolerance | Mutant (control) | Mutant's observable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | KA-344 | One world stepped at contact capacities 32, 64 and 128, solo and in random co-tenancies and slot assignments | Its digests are equal at every capacity and in any co-tenancy | Exact (digest) | `capacity-shaped-tree` (N35-15): a per-world sum grouped by the buffer's capacity instead of a pairwise tree anchored at index 0 over power-of-two blocks | A digest changes with capacity. Smallest case in `f32`: a = 10⁸, b = −10⁸, c = 1: (a + b) + c = 1 and a + (b + c) = 0, because the spacing near 10⁸ is 8 |
| 6 | KA-347 | A batch of P parameter variants of one tree (P = 256 at the gate; P = 8 in this battery) | Equal to P homogeneous runs bit for bit on one back end | Exact (bits) | `mutant-late-param-read` (BD-169): the parameter row is read again after its first use in the step, after a kernel has overwritten a shared scratch column | One variant's digest differs from its homogeneous run |
| 7 | KA-350 | Promotion table at p₀ = 0.05, p₁ = 0.30, α⁺ = 0.01, α⁻ = 0.05, from ln E⁺ = (n − k)·ln((1 − p₀)/(1 − p₁)) + k·ln(p₀/p₁), n audited pairs with k exceedances | A clean pair multiplies the evidence by 0.95/0.70 = 1.357; an exceedance divides it by 6; promotion (E⁺ ≥ 100) takes 16 clean pairs, or 22 pairs with one exceedance; two exceedances in the first three pairs reject (E⁻ ≥ 20) | Exact (integer table, regenerated and compared in CI) | `envelope-after-outcomes`: the envelope is chosen (or re-hashed) after the audited outcomes exist | A planted tier-sensitive result promotes; the ledger shows an envelope hash dated after the first audited pair |
| 8 | KA-351 | Doubly robust tier correction Zᵢ = mᵢ + Aᵢ·(Y¹ᵢ − mᵢ)/pᵢ, with mᵢ the frozen prediction, Aᵢ ~ Bernoulli(pᵢ) the audit draw and Y¹ᵢ the higher-tier outcome | With pᵢ = 1 the mean of Zᵢ equals the plain paired mean exactly; with mᵢ = 0 it is the inverse-probability mean; with a constant tier offset δ it recovers δ | Exact for the identities | `probabilities-after-draw`: pᵢ is logged (and may be set) after Aᵢ is drawn | On a null fixture with pᵢ chosen as a function of Aᵢ the estimate is biased by a known amount (below) |
| 9 | KA-354 | A position-level penetration correction that removes depth d in one step: m = 1 kg, d = 1 mm, h = 2 ms | The body gains d/h = 0.5 m/s and ½·m·(d/h)² = 0.125 J per event from nothing; the flag `energy-created` is raised within one episode. The compliant law CL-1 on the same penetration at k = 10⁵ N/m stores ½·k·d² = 0.05 J, returns less, and creates none | Exact for the flag | `fold-residual-into-dissipation` (BD-208's planted fold): the positive ledger residual is added to the contact-dissipation line | The planted exploit runs unflagged and the ledger closes |

**Numbers the fixtures need.** Row 7: ln(0.95/0.70) = 0.30538, ln(0.05/0.30) = −1.79176, ln 100 = 4.60517, so 4.60517/0.30538 = 15.08 → 16, and (4.60517 + 1.79176)/0.30538 = 20.95 → 21 clean plus one exceedance = 22; reject side: 2·1.79176 − 0.30538 = 3.278 ≥ ln 20 = 2.996. Row 8 mutant: with outcomes Y¹ ≡ 1, m ≡ 0 and the cheat "p = 1 when A = 1, p = ½ when A = 0" after a fair draw, the mean of Z is ½ instead of 1; the correct protocol with p = ½ fixed before the draw gives mean 1. Row 9: the energy flag's rule is created energy above 1% of the absolute work exchanged in any 0.5 s window on a fast tier; a hopping fixture of ten events per second creates 1.25 J/s against a few joules of exchanged work.

**Registry and battery.** As in N16c-03: rows in `known_answers.toml` and `controls.toml` with derivation files under `docs/derivations/kinesis/`; test-target rows `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g5_determinism -- kinesis::ka344 kinesis::ka347` (rows 5, 6) and `--test g1_known_answers -- kinesis::ka350 kinesis::ka351 kinesis::ka354` (rows 7, 8, 9); the controls run under `--features gauntlet-mutations`, where the control runner (N38b-04) rebuilds the tier target once per control with that control's `nc-<nnnn>` feature and expects the row to fail with the registered observable. Rows 5 and 6 run on `batch-cpu` and, where the device is present, on `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal` (absent devices are recorded `not run`, never passed). Mutants live in the crates that own the mechanisms: `frob-kernels` (5, 6, 9), `frob-opt` or `frob-dyn` (7), `frob-chronicle` (8), each a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch at its target site, built only as `--features gauntlet-mutations,<crate>/nc-<nnnn>`, one control per build, and recorded as a `[[control]]` record of BD-025's schema (`kind = "mutation"`, `feature`, `target`, `defect`, `expect`), as in N16c-03. Three of the five already exist as negative controls of the mechanism beads and are registered and run here unchanged, never rebuilt under a second name: row 5 is `capacity-shaped-tree` of the batch-composition mutants (N35-15, run by BD-181); row 6 is `mutant-late-param-read` of the parameter-variant step (BD-169); row 9 is the planted fold of the positive residual into a dissipation line (BD-208). This bead writes the two that no mechanism bead owns: `envelope-after-outcomes` (row 7) and `probabilities-after-draw` (row 8).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`ka344_padding_capacities`, `ka347_parameter_variants`, `ka350_promotion_table`, `ka351_tier_correction_identities`, `ka354_energy_created`; `mutant_<id>_is_caught` × 5; `f32_grouping_counterexample` (the 10⁸ case evaluates to 1 and 0 in `f32`); `negative_zero_padding` (a pairwise sum over a real prefix with trailing −0.0 equals the prefix sum at capacities 4, 8, 64; with +0.0 padding and a prefix summing to −0.0 the bits change).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kinesis_known_answers.sh --rows m2|m3` (the script N16c-03 owns; this bead adds its `m2` and `m3` stages) drives the fixtures through the verbs (row 5: `frob spark check --invariance --backend <b> --workload Q --axes capacity,cotenancy --cotenancies 16 --key ka344-<b> --json`, BD-181's form, since capacities come from the shape class and `sim.run` has no capacity option; row 6: `frob sim run --designs <8-variant designs file> --tier batch-cpu --key ka347-batch --json` (a positional design beside `--designs` is 802) against eight homogeneous runs `frob sim run --designs <the same file> --select <i> --tier batch-cpu --key ka347-h<i> --json`; rows 7 and 8: `frob eval promote <result> --method paired --key ka350 --json` and `--method dr --key ka351`; row 9: `frob sim run … --key ka354 --json` in the conformance build and scope, then `frob fidelity energy <run> --json`), then the conformance rows above with and without `--features gauntlet-mutations`, and logs NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/kinesis_known_answers/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: per row the command, exit code, evidence block, measured value, threshold, digests compared, and for each mutant `expected: "fail"` with the observed defect; on failure the first differing value and artifact paths; a `summary` line naming the gates touched. Non-zero exit if a correct row fails or a mutant passes.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-344, KA-347, KA-350, KA-351, KA-354; CX-146 (grouping by capacity), CX-147 (penetration removed at no energy cost). Metamorphic relations: permuting slots permutes results; doubling the capacity changes no digest; scaling d by 2 quadruples the created energy at fixed h. Determinism lanes for rows 5 and 6: twice on one machine; 1 and 24 workers; debug and release; per back end against class D2.

## Considerations

- The canonical padding value for sums is −0.0, not +0.0: (−0.0) + (+0.0) = +0.0 changes the bits of a sum that is −0.0. One appendix row of the plan says "+0"; the chapter text and the Numerics chapter say −0.0, and this bead follows them.
- Row 9's energy figure is hand arithmetic; the flag's window and denominator are owned by the energy ledger, and this battery checks the flag, not the bound.
- If KILL-DR fires (the tier correction falls back to the plain paired e-process), row 8 keeps its identities as tests of the estimator code that remains and its mutant stays registered.
- Device rows need the CUDA host and the Mac; a missing device is `not run`.
