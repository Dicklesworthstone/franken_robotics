## Background

The lockstep tiers (`batch-cpu` and the GPU tiers) run a fixed physics step h and a fixed number of Newton iterations per step, under masks, so that the launch graph stays static and the iteration count is part of the identity (`StepPlanId`) every result carries. Two numbers therefore sit under every throughput figure and every fidelity figure of the program: h and the iteration budget. The design default is h = 2 ms with control every tenth step (50 Hz) and 4 Newton iterations with warm start. It is an open question (OPEN-KIN-1), owned by the Kinesis lead, to be decided by the **fidelity–throughput frontier measured at M2**.

An open question in this program is a decision written so that not taking it is safe: it has an owner, a deadline expressed as the gate or milestone it blocks, a fail-closed default, and the evidence that would decide it. At its deadline an undecided question takes its default, and the default is recorded as an architecture decision record. This bead is that decision, with the experiment that produces its evidence.

Why it blocks. The performance targets are stated at h = 2 ms and decimation 10; if the default moves, every such row must be restated before any gate cites it (OPEN-PERF-4). The fidelity envelopes of the ledger are frozen per step plan, so the default must be fixed before the one permitted re-baseline of GATE-FID-01. It is therefore one step more urgent than its milestone.

Fail-closed default until decided: **h = 2 ms, decimation 10, 4 Newton iterations with warm start**; no figure is quoted at another default.

## Scope

In: the frontier experiment, the decision rule, the ADR, and the restatement trigger. Out: the per-task choice of step (a task may always declare its own h); the capacity decision (N16c-08); the created-energy bound (N16c-11).

## Technical approach

**Experiment (DESIGN).** On workloads Q (reference quadruped, 18 degrees of freedom, C = 32, R = 160) and H (reference humanoid, 35 degrees of freedom, C = 64, R = 320), on the standard battery:

1. Grid: h ∈ {1, 2, 2.5, 4} ms (each a rational number of seconds that divides the 20 ms control period: decimations 20, 10, 8, 5) × Newton iterations ∈ {2, 3, 4, 6, 8} × warm start {on, off}. Physical parameters and every clock are held fixed in seconds; only numerical parameters vary (ADR-KIN-05).
2. Fidelity per cell, `batch-cpu` in `f32` against the referee at the same addresses: median and 95th percentile of the disagreement in base displacement over 2 s, energy per metre and stance impulse (the GATE-FID-01 quantities, thresholds 2%/5%, 3%/8%, 5%/12%); the rate of discordant task decisions; the `Degraded` rate; the `energy-created` flag rate; the solver's gradient-norm certificate at exit (median and 99th percentile).
3. Throughput per cell on each device: world-steps/s and env-steps/s (one env-step is one control interval), each as the 95% lower bound of a paired ratio against the default cell, at least ten pairs, with the disclosure block.
4. The held-command check: for each decimation in the grid, the tier's one-tick spectral radius for the reference controllers (analysis A-22 evaluated with the tier's integrator). A decimation below 3 is known to be able to destabilize a held command that the exact hold handles (radius 1.366 at decimation 1, 0.784 at 2 on the slider fixture), so a cell is marked `hold-unsafe` if any reference loop's tier radius exceeds one.

**Decision rule, fixed before the data (DESIGN).** A cell is *admissible* if every fidelity quantity is inside its GATE-FID-01 threshold, discordant decisions pass the decision-level equivalence test against the cell with (h/2, twice the iterations), the `Degraded` rate is at most 10⁻⁴, no reference loop is `hold-unsafe`, and the created-energy flag rate is not above the default cell's. Among admissible cells choose the one with the highest env-steps/s lower bound on H; the default is replaced only if the gain's lower bound is at least 1.15 over the default cell on H and not below 1.0 on Q. Otherwise the default stands.

**Outputs.** A record `frob.frontier/1` (grid, per-cell metrics with units, the admissible set, the choice and the rule's hash), an ADR (proposed identifier ADR-KIN-08, "default step and iteration budget") with the measured table, and, if the default moved, the list of performance rows restated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rule's text and hash are committed before the first cell runs.
- [ ] All 4 × 5 × 2 cells × 2 workloads are measured or marked `not run` with a reason; no cell is silently dropped.
- [ ] The frontier record lists per cell the three fidelity quantities (median, 95th), the discordant-decision rate, `Degraded` and `energy-created` rates, and the paired throughput lower bound.
- [ ] The decision is recorded as an ADR naming the chosen (h, decimation, iterations, warm start) and whether the default stood.
- [ ] If the default moved: every row of the performance targets is restated at the new decimation and no gate cites a stale row (checked by the status generator); the fidelity ledger's envelopes are re-keyed to the new step plan before the GATE-FID-01 re-baseline.
- [ ] Negative control: a planted cell whose step also halves the control period is rejected by the study as "refinement study that changes a clock".

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `frontier_rule_is_pure`: the admissible set and the choice are a function of the record only; shuffling cell order changes nothing.
- `frontier_rule_keeps_default_on_tie`: a synthetic record with a 1.10 gain keeps the default.
- `grid_cells_hold_clocks_fixed`: every generated cell has the control period at 20 ms in rational seconds.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_step_frontier.sh` runs `frob sim run --tier batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal --step <h> --newton-iterations <n> --warm-start on|off --battery standard --json` per cell and `frob eval promote`-style paired comparisons against the referee, then applies the rule. NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_step_frontier/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: per cell a `cmd` line and `metric` lines (value, unit, threshold, evidence block, `StepPlanId`, fingerprint digest, disclosure block), `assert` lines for admissibility, a final `summary` with the chosen cell, the rule hash and counts.

### Known answers and negative controls

The clock-changing cell above; a planted cell with 1 iteration must be inadmissible (fidelity outside threshold); the held-command arithmetic (decimations 1 and 2 `hold-unsafe` on the slider fixture; 5, 8, 10, 20 safe, with radii between 0.25 and 0.5). Determinism: each cell is run twice and its digests compared against class D2.

## Considerations

- Budget: 80 cells × 2 workloads on two device families exceeds the default machine envelope if run at full battery size; the bead names a reduced battery (200 addresses per cell) and needs the owner's approval for more.
- A coarser step raises throughput per world-step and lowers it per env-step only if the decimation drops; the rule is stated in env-steps for that reason.
- Risk: fidelity thresholds are themselves re-baselined once at M2; the order is fixed: decide the default here first, then re-baseline.
- Owner: Kinesis lead. Deadline: before the GATE-FID-01 re-baseline at M2.
