## Background

"Sleeping" is the common simulator optimization of not stepping bodies that are at rest; "islands" is the partition of bodies into groups with no constraint between them so that each group is solved alone. Under the transactional profiles (the referee and `strict`) islands are always formed, because coupled impulses are solved once per island. On the lockstep tiers the step is ten fixed stages over fixed-capacity buffers with no data-dependent control flow across worlds, and a world is normally one robot, which is one island because every foot couples through the floating body. The open question (OPEN-KIN-2, owner: Kinesis lead, decided by the M2 profile) is whether sleeping and islands are worth their complexity for single-robot worlds on those tiers. **Default: no.**

The question matters most for interior scenes: doors, drawers, gates, lifts and carts are articulated models in the scene, and most of them are idle most of the time. Whether idle fixtures sleep is this question.

Why the default is no. (1) Fixed shapes: a sleeping body still occupies its buffer slots, so on a device it saves only masked arithmetic unless worlds are regrouped by work, which is the separate mechanism of row-class solve queues. (2) Determinism: a wake-up rule must be a function of the world's own state and must never miss a contact, or a world's digest starts to depend on a threshold. (3) Fidelity: a sleeping threshold is a numerical parameter that can hide creep and stored contact energy that the energy ledger must see.

This question blocks nothing; it is a low-urgency decision record with a measurement.

Fail-closed default until decided: no sleeping and no island splitting on lockstep tiers; every body is stepped every step.

## Scope

In: the measurement on the M2 profile, the ceiling computed before any implementation, the decision and its ADR. Out: implementing sleeping (a new bead if the decision is yes); islands under transactional profiles (already the rule); row-class queues (their own beads).

## Technical approach

**Measurement (no new mechanism).**

1. On the M2 profile of the masked lockstep path, for workloads Q and H on flat ground and for one interior fixture set (a `synthetic` room with four idle articulated fixtures built from model primitives: a hinged door, a drawer, a gate and a cart; the scene library's fixtures, N19a-07, replace it when they land at M3), record per stage the share of step time, and per world-step the count of bodies whose twist norm and whose contact-impulse change are below a candidate rest threshold for at least 0.5 s (a counter, read through `telemetry.histogram`).
2. Compute the idle share f_idle of step time attributable to those bodies' links, rows and solve columns, using the stage shares and the per-stage size exponents (p = 1 for kinematics, p = 2 for assembly, p = 3 for the dense factor): f_idle = Σ_stages share_stage · (1 − (1 − s)^p_stage), with s the idle fraction of the world's coordinates, that is, the work a stage would no longer do if the idle bodies were removed from it.
3. Ceiling, stated before any speed test: speedup ≤ 1/(1 − f_idle + o), with o the relative overhead of rest tests and wake checks (HYPOTHESIS o = 0.02).

**Decision rule, fixed before the data.** Sleeping is proposed only if the ceiling is at least 1.15 on the interior fixture set and the count of idle bodies is at least a quarter of all bodies there; for bare single-robot worlds the answer is recorded as no unless the ceiling is at least 1.15 on Q or H. If proposed, the proposal must state the wake rule as a guarded predicate on the world's own state with a swept-bound argument that no contact is missed, and must keep digests independent of co-tenants (INV-KIN-04).

**Output.** An ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KIN-OPEN-2.md` (proposed identifier ADR-KIN-09, "sleeping on lockstep tiers"; the one directory for decision records, named as the other Kinesis open-question decisions are: ADR-KIN-OPEN-1 and -3 to -6) with f_idle per workload, the ceiling and the decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rest counters and stage shares are recorded for Q, H and the interior fixture set, with the disclosure block.
- [ ] The ceiling is computed and filed before any sleeping code exists.
- [ ] The ADR records yes or no against the rule; with no, the default stands and the scene chapter's fixtures are stepped every step.
- [ ] With yes, a follow-up bead exists with a pilot, a determinism test (digests equal with sleeping on and off on 1,000 episodes) and a kill gate (gain below 1.15, or one missed wake).
- [ ] Negative control for the measurement: a planted fixture set with no idle bodies reports f_idle = 0 within counter resolution.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `idle_share_formula`: f_idle from synthetic stage shares and sizes equals the hand value: stage shares one half cubic, three tenths quadratic, one fifth linear, and one of four equal bodies idle (s = ¼) give f_idle = ½·(1 − 27/64) + (3/10)·(1 − 9/16) + (1/5)·(1/4) = 37/128 + 21/160 + 1/20 = 301/640 = 0.4703125, compared exactly in rationals; with s = 0 the value is 0.
- `ceiling_before_speed`: the decision record is rejected if it has no ceiling field.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_sleep_profile.sh` runs, for each of the three fixture sets of the measurement (Q on a plane, H on a plane, the `synthetic` interior room) and for the planted no-idle set, `frob sim run <design> --scene <scene> --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep --worlds 4096 --seconds 20 --seed 7 --telemetry T1 --key ksp-<set>-batch-cpu --json` and the same with `--tier gpu-cuda --key ksp-<set>-gpu-cuda` (`gpu-metal`, key `ksp-<set>-gpu-metal`, where the device is present; a tier without its device is logged `not-run`), reads the rest counter with `frob telemetry histogram <run> --channel <rest-counter channel> --json` (N29a-15; the run positional) and the stage shares from the M2 profile, and computes f_idle and the ceiling; the no-idle set must give f_idle = 0 within counter resolution. NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_sleep_profile/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript, through the STD-E2E helper (every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, wall time): commands, exit codes, evidence blocks, per-stage shares (unit: fraction of step), idle-body counts, f_idle, the ceiling and the decision; a `summary` line.

### Known answers and negative controls

The no-idle fixture; a hand case for the ceiling: f_idle = 0.3 and o = 0.02 give 1/(0.72) = 1.39; f_idle = 0.1 gives 1.09, below the bar.

## Considerations

- Interaction with row-class solve queues: both remove masked work from the same stages, so their gains do not multiply; if queues are kept, sleeping must be measured on top of them.
- Interaction with the energy ledger: a body put to sleep with stored contact energy must have that energy accounted, or the audit for created energy will flag the wake-up.
- Owner: Kinesis lead. Deadline: none that blocks; decided with the M2 profile.
