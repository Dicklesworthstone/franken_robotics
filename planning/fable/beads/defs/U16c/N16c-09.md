## Background

A step profile is a promise about what a step did. The `strict` profile exists only on `batch-cpu`: it runs the transactional step (one transaction per accepted interval, events located, a commit fence, retries that restore all state) in `f64`, one world at a time per worker, on the program's own kernels. Its uses are adjudication where the referee cannot complete (the referee's solve admits at most 21 contacts and keeps fixed witnesses), the "step" and "law" arms of the model-form rank check, and debugging.

Contact laws: CL-1 is a linear compliant normal law with a dissipation time and regularized Coulomb friction with a stated stiction speed; it is the default on lockstep tiers. CL-H is the hard-contact law (Signorini complementarity on the normal, Coulomb friction with maximum dissipation, non-associated); it is the referee's written specification and, as a Frontier option, a batch law. Today `strict` runs **CL-1 with located events**, and CL-H "once GATE-TRAC-09 passes".

The open question (OPEN-KIN-4, owner Kinesis lead): which law `strict` runs **by default** once CL-H is admitted. Default: CL-1 with located events, with CL-H as a named variant. It blocks nothing and is decided together with GATE-TRAC-09 at M5.

Why it is a real choice. If `strict` adjudicates with CL-1, an `established` label from `batch-cpu:strict` shares the compliant law's artifacts (bounded creep of a stuck contact, energy stored in compliance, a separation velocity μ·‖u_T‖ acquired by a sliding contact) with the fast tier it adjudicates, and agreement between them says less. If it adjudicates with CL-H, it matches the referee's specification but depends on a solver with no convergence guarantee at a fixed pass count, and its refusal rate (`Degraded: unconverged`, `FROB-REF-221`) lowers the coverage the profile exists to provide.

Fail-closed default until decided: CL-1 with located events; every `established` result names its adjudicator and its law (`batch-cpu:strict/CL-1`).

## Scope

In: the comparison that informs the decision, the rule, the ADR, and the change of default if chosen. Out: implementing CL-H in batch (its own bead); the model-form check's law arm (already compares the laws where admitted).

## Technical approach

**Evidence to collect at M5, on the standard battery scenarios where `strict` is the adjudicator (the scenarios the referee refuses):**

1. Completion: the share of those scenarios `strict` completes under CL-1 and under CL-H, with refusals by cause (`FROB-REF-220`, `-221`, `-224`, unconverged).
2. Agreement with the referee on the scenarios both can run: for base displacement over 2 s, energy per metre and stance impulse, the median and 95th-percentile disagreement of `strict/CL-1` and of `strict/CL-H` against the referee; discordant task decisions.
3. Promotion outcomes: for 200 fast-tier results promoted with each adjudicator law, the count whose label differs (`established` under one, `tier-sensitive` under the other).
4. Cost: wall-clock of a promotion at the 50th and 95th percentiles under each law.

**Decision rule, fixed before the data.** CL-H becomes the default law of `strict` if (a) GATE-TRAC-09 has passed and KILL-HARDCONTACT has not fired; (b) its completion share is not more than five percentage points below CL-1's in any reference class; (c) its disagreement with the referee is no larger than CL-1's on every quantity (95th percentile); and (d) at least 1% of promotions change label between the laws (otherwise the choice does not matter and the cheaper law stays). Otherwise CL-1 stays the default and CL-H stays a named variant (`--law cl-h`).

**Output.** An ADR (proposed identifier ADR-KIN-11) with the four tables. If the default changes: the `strict` profile's identity changes (the law is part of the `StepPlanId`), every ledger stratum keyed by the profile pair is re-opened, and results adjudicated before the change keep their recorded law.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four measurements are on file for every reference class in which `strict` adjudicates.
- [ ] The rule's text is committed before the measurements.
- [ ] The ADR records the choice; `frob sim run --profile strict` without `--law` runs the chosen default and prints the law in the evidence block.
- [ ] Every `established` label printed after the decision names adjudicator and law.
- [ ] If the default changed, no ledger envelope frozen under the old default is applied to a result adjudicated under the new one (a planted reuse is refused as an unfrozen envelope, `FROB-REF-821`).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `strict_default_law_is_recorded`: the evidence block of a `strict` result carries the law identifier.
- `rule_is_pure`: the decision is a function of the four tables.
- `ledger_stratum_keyed_by_law`: two strata differing only in the adjudicator's law are distinct keys.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_strict_law.sh` runs `frob sim run --tier batch-cpu --profile strict --law cl-1|cl-h --battery standard --json` on the adjudication scenarios and `frob eval promote --adjudicator strict --law …` on 200 results, and applies the rule. NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_strict_law/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: commands, exit codes, evidence blocks with the law, completion shares, disagreement quantiles with units, label changes, promotion wall-clock; refusal lines with codes; a `summary` line with the decision.

### Known answers and negative controls

The sliding-block separation artifact as a discriminating fixture: a block sliding at speed u_T under the associated (compliant) law acquires a separation velocity μ·‖u_T‖, and under CL-H it does not; the script asserts the sign of the difference. Negative control: the planted reuse of an envelope across laws.

## Considerations

- If KILL-HARDCONTACT fires, the question closes with the default (CL-1) and CL-H remains the referee's specification and a model-form arm under `strict` through per-island ADMM or Gauss–Seidel with a residual flag.
- A preference between two designs is stated only if it holds under both qualified contact laws; this decision does not weaken that rule.
- Owner: Kinesis lead. Deadline: none that blocks; decided with GATE-TRAC-09.
