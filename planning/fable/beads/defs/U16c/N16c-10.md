## Background

The transactional step of the referee and of the `strict` profile locates events on a dyadic grid inside each base interval and retries a failed interval under a ladder of numerical plans. Three constants bound that work:

- **L_max = 16**: a split may fall only at t0 + ℓ₀·k/2ᴸ with L ≤ L_max. At a base interval of 2 ms this resolves 2 ms/2¹⁶ = 31 ns, so an approach at 3 m/s is located to within 0.1 µm.
- **E_max = 64**: at most 64 events inside one base interval. An event that cannot be bracketed within L_max, or a 65th event, ends the transaction with `FROB-REF-220` instead of an approximation: the guard against accumulation points of impacts.
- **N_retry = 3**: the retry ladder has attempts 0 to 3; exhaustion marks the world `Faulted` with `numerical-fault` (`FROB-REF-221`).

All three are DESIGN defaults and together are OPEN-KIN-5 (owner Kinesis lead), decided by **the refusal rate on the M1 fixtures and battery**. The fail-closed default is refusal: when a bound is hit the system refuses by name, it never approximates.

Why it is urgent. The M1 stop decision reads this measurement: the referee is re-planned if its refusal rate on the M1 fixtures leaves the sill fixture (a torque-driven wheel climbing a sharp sill, flat to edge to top) without a completed run. The referee's coverage figure at M2 also depends on it. So the constants are decided at M1, one step more urgent than the milestone's default.

## Scope

In: measuring refusal causes and rates as functions of the three constants on the M1 fixtures; the rule; the ADR; changing the constants once. Out: the mechanisms (event location BD-082; the ladder BD-083); the referee's capacity refusals (`FROB-REF-721`), which no constant here affects.

## Technical approach

**Measurement.** Fixtures: the M1 contact battery on the referee, the thin-barrier and sill fixtures, the tied sliders, the ballistic drop with restitution 0.5 and 0.9, the reference quadruped standing, dropping and walking under the baseline controller.

1. At the defaults (16, 64, 3) record per fixture: transactions attempted; committed by attempt (the attempt histogram); refusals by code (220 split by cause: bracket depth or event count; 221; 224); the deepest level used; the largest event count in one base interval.
2. Sensitivity sweep, each constant alone with the others at default: L_max ∈ {12, 16, 20}; E_max ∈ {16, 64, 256}; N_retry ∈ {1, 3, 5}. Record the same quantities and the wall-clock per committed interval.
3. Arithmetic check of resolution: ℓ₀/2ᴸ for ℓ₀ = 2 ms gives 488 ns, 31 ns, 1.9 ns at L = 12, 16, 20; location error at 3 m/s is 1.5 µm, 0.09 µm, 0.006 µm. In `f64` with rational time the grid is exact at every L up to 52.

**Decision rule, fixed before the data.**

- A constant keeps its default unless the sweep shows that a larger value removes at least half of that cause's refusals on the M1 fixtures at a wall-clock cost of at most 20% per committed interval, in which case the larger value is adopted.
- A smaller value is adopted only if it changes no refusal count and no digest of a committed interval.
- If, at the best setting, the sill fixture has no completed run, the measurement is handed to the M1 exit review as the trigger of the referee re-plan; this bead does not hide that by loosening a tolerance.
- An accumulation point (a ball with restitution bouncing to rest) must still be refused with `FROB-REF-220` at every admitted E_max: no setting may turn it into a silent approximation.

**Output.** An ADR (proposed identifier ADR-KIN-12) with the tables and the adopted constants. The constants are part of the step-profile identity, so a change gives the profile a new identity and is made once, before the M1 gate rows are recorded.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The attempt histogram and the refusals by code and cause are on file for every M1 fixture at the defaults and for the nine sweep cells.
- [ ] The sill fixture's completion is stated explicitly (completed, or not completed with the refusing code and cause).
- [ ] The ADR records (L_max, E_max, N_retry) and whether each default stood.
- [ ] The accumulation fixture is refused with `FROB-REF-220` at every admitted setting.
- [ ] After a change, the forced-retry digest test and the impact-time row of GATE-KIN-04 are re-run and pass under the new identity.
- [ ] The refusal rate per robot class is passed to the referee-coverage report as its "transaction" cause.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `dyadic_resolution_table`: 2 ms/2¹² = 488.28125 ns, /2¹⁶ = 30.517578125 ns, /2²⁰ = 1.9073486328125 ns, exact in rationals.
- `emax_guard`: a synthetic interval with 65 events is refused at E_max = 64 and committed at 256 only if every event is bracketed.
- `constants_in_profile_identity`: two profiles differing in N_retry have different identities.
- `accumulation_always_refused` (negative control: a mutant that drops events beyond E_max instead of refusing must fail).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_transaction_constants.sh` runs `frob sim run --tier referee --profile referee --fixture <f> --lmax <L> --emax <E> --retries <N> --json` over the fixtures and the sweep, reads refusal counts and attempt histograms from the envelope and `frob telemetry summary`, and applies the rule. NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_transaction_constants/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: per cell the command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, attempts, refusals by code and cause, deepest level, largest event count; `refusal` lines carry the full refusal block; `assert` lines for the sill completion and the accumulation refusal; a `summary` line with the adopted constants.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-343 (2 ms/2¹⁶ = 31 ns; 3 m/s located to within 0.1 µm). The bouncing ball with restitution r has impact intervals shrinking by r each bounce, so infinitely many impacts fall before t* · (1 + r)/(1 − r); with r = 0.5 and the first impact at 0.4515 s the accumulation time is 1.3546 s, and the base interval containing it must be refused. Negative control: the event-dropping mutant.

## Considerations

- Raising N_retry lengthens the worst case of a transaction linearly; raising L_max lengthens bisection by one step per level; raising E_max lengthens the event list. None changes a committed digest of an interval that committed before, which the sweep verifies.
- These constants do nothing for the referee's capacity limits (21 contacts, fixed witnesses); those refusals are reported separately and have their own postures.
- Owner: Kinesis lead. Deadline: M1 exit.
