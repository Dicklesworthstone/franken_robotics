## Background

GATE-KIN-08 is the gate of row-class solve queues (a regrouping of lockstep worlds by the work their constraint solve needs, so that execution groups hold worlds with similar active row counts that are still iterating). It makes two claims at once: Known answer (determinism), that queues change no digest, and Performance, that they pay. Threshold (TARGET), earliest M2 on CUDA and on Metal when its dispatch cost is measured:

1. digests identical to the masked path on 1,000 episodes per backend under 100 random co-tenancies and queue capacities;
2. overflow to the general lane at most 1% of world-steps on the standard battery;
3. a mixed cohort with queues reaches at least 0.70 of its weighted homogeneous throughput;
4. the 95% lower bound of the paired whole-step ratio against the masked path is at least 1.15 at the batch size named in the receipt.

This bead is the end-to-end script that measures all four from the user's side and produces the receipt the gate row cites. It also runs **arm E** of the mixed-batch head-to-head experiment: arm A (mixed cohorts with model tables, size buckets and slot dispatch) with queues on, reported on that experiment's measure (2). The pre-declared outcomes of the head-to-head are unchanged by arm E.

A performance ratio is admitted only as the one-sided 95% lower confidence bound of a paired ratio: at least ten pairs; the two arms of a pair run back to back in randomized order on the same host state, workload and addresses; with ℓᵢ = ln(aᵢ/bᵢ), mean ℓ̄ and standard deviation s over n pairs, the bound is exp(ℓ̄ − t·s/√n), t the 95th percentile of Student's distribution with n − 1 degrees of freedom (1.833 for n = 10); compilation, admission, synchronization, transfer, fallback and verification are in the denominator; on the Mac a 20-minute sustained load precedes measurement, on the CUDA host the host load is recorded. Every figure carries the disclosure block.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures, the four clauses, the three known answers and controls of the mechanism, arm E, the receipt. Out: the kill decision (N16c-17), which reads this bead's output.

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/kin_rowqueue_gate.sh --backend batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal [--worlds N] [--seed S]`; sources the STD-E2E helper; idempotent; seed-explicit (default 16190).

**Fixtures.** The standard battery for workloads Q and H; `fixture:kin/trot-queue` (a quadruped with four samples per foot trotting on flat ground: 8 active contacts in diagonal support, 16 in double support, C = 24, class bounds 6, 12, 24; `synthetic` gait under the baseline controller); a mixed cohort of three body plans in one bucket (the cohort of clause 3 of BD-183, variants on devices, a dependency).

**Steps.**

Every run below carries its own idempotency key, distinct per clause, backend, workload, pair, arm and seed (`rq-gate-<clause>-<b>-<…>-$SEED`), so that no call returns another's stored receipt.

1. `frob doctor --json` and `frob spark devices --json` (a read: the build identity and the fingerprint digest of each device; a fresh fingerprint is `spark.fingerprint`'s run, not needed here).
2. Clause 1, digests, through BD-181's invariance harness with the `solve-queues` axis that N16c-15 adds: `frob spark check --invariance --backend <b> --workload <Q|H> --axes cotenancy,solve-queues --episodes 1000 --cotenancies 100 --key rq-gate-inv-<b>-<w>-$SEED --json`. The harness runs each of the 1,000 episodes (addresses fixed by seed) alone on the masked path and inside 100 random co-tenancies with queues on at capacities drawn by an address-keyed stream from {0, 32, …, N}, and compares every episode's digest chain with its alone run. Assert zero mismatches on every axis. (`sim.run` has no co-tenant input, and `sim.replay --co-tenants` reproduces one world without queues, so neither can carry this clause.)
3. Known answer 2, the trot fixture: `frob sim run --fixture kin/trot-queue --tier <b> --profile lockstep --solve-queues auto --telemetry T1 --key rq-gate-trot-<b>-$SEED --json`; read the per-step class with `frob telemetry query <run> --stream solver --channels queue_class --json` (N16c-14's channel); assert it alternates class 1 (8 ≤ 12) and class 2 (16 ≤ 24) with the support phases.
4. Clause 2, overflow: `frob eval run <Q|H design> --battery standard --tier <b> --profile lockstep --solve-queues auto --key rq-gate-ovf-<b>-<w>-$SEED --json` (a battery is `eval.run`'s input, which forwards the option, N16c-15; the battery form takes no seed, its addresses being the items' own); general-lane world-steps ÷ world-steps ≤ 0.01 from the receipt's counters.
5. Clause 3, mixed cohort and arm E: `frob phylum sweep --designs <plan-1>,<plan-2>,<plan-3> --task <task> --worlds-per-design 256 --tier <b> --solve-queues auto --key rq-gate-mix-<b>-$SEED --json` (the three body plans of BD-183's cohort, named explicitly: a `@cohort:` reference is not a registered form of `--designs`) and the homogeneous runs of each plan, `--designs <plan-i> --worlds-per-design 768 … --key rq-gate-hom-<b>-<i>-$SEED`; rate ÷ weighted homogeneous rate ≥ 0.70. Submit the same configuration to the head-to-head harness as arm E and record its measure (2).
6. Clause 4, paired ratio: at the batch size N named on the command line (default 8,192; also 65,536 where memory allows), at least ten pairs of whole-step timings, `frob sim run <design> --tier <b> --profile lockstep --worlds <N> --seconds 2 --solve-queues auto --key rq-gate-pair-<b>-<n>-q` and `--solve-queues off --key rq-gate-pair-<b>-<n>-m`, randomized order, warm, through the performance harness (BD-216) after the thermal protocol; compute the lower bound; assert ≥ 1.15. Record f, ℓ and h for the same runs and the kill-gate inequality h < f·(1 − ℓ).
7. Gauntlet rows and controls: `FROB_TEST_SEED=$SEED cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g5_determinism -- GATE-KIN-08` (clause 1 and the two known answers) and `FROB_TEST_SEED=$SEED cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-KIN-08` (the performance clauses, read from this script's measurements), then the same with `--features gauntlet-mutations`: the control runner rebuilds the tier target once per registered control, each a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` mutant in `frob-kernels` or `frob-dyn` (`reset-time-sort`: a truncation flag or zero saving, per-class world-steps showing the trot fixture parked in class 2; `class-sized-reduction`: a digest mismatch), and the script fails if either is not `caught`.
8. Emit the receipt path per backend. The gate's value is recorded from the receipt by a session that did not implement the mechanism, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-KIN-08 --receipt <hash> --part M2` (convention 9: this bead is the gate's measuring bead; the script writes nothing to the register).

**Logging.** NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_rowqueue_gate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N16c-16"), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed, labels), measured values with units and thresholds (digest mismatches: count, threshold 0; general-lane share, threshold 0.01; mixed-cohort ratio, threshold 0.70; paired lower bound with n, ℓ̄, s, threshold 1.15; f, ℓ, h), the disclosure block for every timing, digests where state is compared, and on failure the first differing world, step and stage and the run and snapshot paths. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed, `gates: ["GATE-KIN-08"]`, the backend, the batch size.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on `batch-cpu` for clauses 1 to 3 (clause 4 is a device clause) and on CUDA for all four; on Metal it runs only after the fingerprint has a measured per-dispatch encode cost, otherwise it logs `not-run` for Metal.
- [ ] Zero digest mismatches in 1,000 episodes × 100 co-tenancies and queue capacities per backend.
- [ ] General-lane share ≤ 1% of world-steps on the standard battery (measured value logged).
- [ ] Mixed cohort with queues ≥ 0.70 of its weighted homogeneous throughput; arm E's measure (2) is filed with the head-to-head record.
- [ ] Paired lower bound ≥ 1.15 at the named batch size, with at least ten pairs and the full disclosure block; or the measured bound is logged as a fail and handed to the kill decision.
- [ ] The trot fixture follows the 8 and 16 alternation; both mutants are caught.
- [ ] The script leaves one hash-named receipt per backend, recorded by a non-implementing session with `record_gate`; a backend's qualification flag (read by the `experimental-execution` label) flips only on that backend's recorded part.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script helpers: `paired_lower_bound` reproduces a hand case (ten ratios all equal to 1.20 give a bound of 1.20; ratios alternating 1.10 and 1.30 give ℓ̄ = ln√1.43, s = 0.0880, bound = exp(0.1788 − 1.833·0.0880/√10) = 1.136); `general_lane_share` from counters; `digest_chain_compare` reports the first differing step.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script, as specified. Run time TARGET: under 30 minutes per backend excluding the thermal soak.

### Known answers and negative controls

(1) A world's digests with queues on equal its digests on the masked path, bit for bit. (2) The trot fixture: per-step assignment follows the 8 and 16 alternation, and the reset-time sort is caught by a truncation flag or by zero saving. (3) A planted kernel that sizes a reduction tree by the class bound changes a digest and is caught. Metamorphic: permuting worlds permutes results; changing queue capacities changes no digest. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; 1 and 24 workers on `batch-cpu`; debug and release; each device against class D2.

## Considerations

- Clause 4 is where the mechanism most likely fails: at 8,192 worlds the predicted bound is 1.09 to 1.39 depending on launch cost and live fraction. A fail is an expected, recordable outcome and triggers KILL-ROWQUEUE.
- Budget: the paired runs and the head-to-head arm are inside the default envelope at 8,192 worlds; the 65,536-world row needs memory headroom and is optional.
- Queues and design-family lanes are measured each alone and both together when both exist; the simpler configuration with the larger measured gain is kept.
- `--solve-queues` is N16c-15's option of `sim.run` and `phylum.sweep`, forwarded by `eval.run` and exercised by the `solve-queues` axis of the invariance harness (both added by N16c-15 and listed in the `eval.run` row of VERBS-eval and the `spark.check` row of VERBS-spark). Every other spelling the script uses is in its verb's row: `--fixture` and `--telemetry T1` on `sim.run`, `--battery` and `--profile` on `eval.run`, `--designs` and `--worlds-per-design` on `phylum.sweep`, `--invariance --axes --episodes --cotenancies` on `spark.check`.
