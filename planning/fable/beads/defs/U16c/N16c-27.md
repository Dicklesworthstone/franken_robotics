## Background

GATE-KIN-10 (claims: Known answer and Design comparison; milestone M6) is the gate of step adjoints through the converged contact solve: design gradients obtained by one reverse sweep (two triangular solves with the step's own Cholesky factor per world-step, plus derived reverse kernels), exposed as `phylum.gradient --method adjoint`. Threshold (TARGET):

1. the scalar and block fixtures are exact, with their labels;
2. on a 100-case suite of short horizons, 80 with no change of active set and 20 controls with a planted change: cosine against central differences at least 0.95 in at least 72 of the 80, and all 20 controls labeled `one-sided` or `fixed-mode`, or refused;
3. the reverse sweep takes at most twice the forward sweep's wall time on the reference quadruped at 2,048 worlds.

This bead is the end-to-end script that measures the three clauses from the user's side and produces the receipt. It also records the number the kill gate reads: the median cosine on the design corpus at K = 8.

Labels: `smooth` (every contact and joint row strictly inside one region of its law with margin above a guard), `fixed-mode` (derivative with active sets frozen), `one-sided` (a row sits on a boundary; both one-sided values on request).

## Scope

In: the script; the two exact fixtures; construction of the 100-case suite; the timing clause; the corpus cosine; the receipt. Out: the kill decision (N16c-28).

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/kin_stepadjoint_gate.sh --backend batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal [--seed S]`; sources the STD-E2E helper; idempotent; default seed 16210.

**Exact fixtures (`synthetic`).**

- `fixture:adjoint/scalar-ift`: one degree of freedom, Φ(v; z) = ½·m·(v − z)² + ½·g_c·v² with m = 2. Three cases: contact closed with g_c = 6 (expected v⁺ = m·z/(m + g_c) = z/4, dv⁺/dz = ¼, label `smooth`); contact open, g_c = 0 (dv⁺/dz = 1, `smooth`); state exactly on the boundary (label `one-sided`; `--both-sides` returns 1 and ¼).
- `fixture:adjoint/block-push`: a 1 kg block on a floor with μ = 0.5, pushed with force u for one step of 0.01 s; breakaway μ·m·g = 4.905 N. Ideal Coulomb: u = 4 N gives v⁺ = 0, dv⁺/du = 0, label `fixed-mode` (stick), `uninformative: true`; u = 6 N gives v⁺ = 0.01 × (6 − 4.905) = 0.01095 m/s, dv⁺/du = 0.01 s/kg, label `smooth`. Regularized law with stiction speed 1 mm/s: stick derivative 0.01/(1 + 49.05) ≈ 2.0 × 10⁻⁴ s/kg, fifty times smaller than in slip, `uninformative: true`.

**The 100-case suite.** Built once by script, frozen and hashed before any adjoint is run on it. Each case: a robot (reference quadruped, wheeled base, or a single leg on a rail), a design with 6 to 30 parameters, a smooth metric (tracking error plus energy), a horizon of 2 to 8 control intervals, 64 seeds. *Regular cases (80)*: selected so that the forward runs show no change of active set inside the horizon in any seed (standing, steady stance phases, rolling contact). *Controls (20)*: the same construction with a planted change of active set inside the horizon: five touchdowns, five lift-offs, five stick-to-slip transitions, five joint-limit engagements. For every case the reference gradient is the paired central-difference stencil at the same θ with relocation of nothing (lockstep tiers do not locate events), δ = 1% of each parameter's range.

**Steps.**

1. Build identity and fingerprint.
2. Clause 1: run both fixtures through `frob phylum gradient --method adjoint --json`; assert values to 10⁻¹² in `f64` on `batch-cpu` (and to the `f32` envelope 10⁻⁵ relative on devices) and the labels listed.
3. Clause 2: for each of the 100 cases run `--method adjoint` and `--method fd`; compute the cosine between the two gradient vectors. Assert: at least 72 of the 80 regular cases have cosine ≥ 0.95; each of the 20 controls is labeled `one-sided` or `fixed-mode`, or refused (`FROB-REF-540`, or the derivative refusal `FROB-REF-263` raised near grazing or across a change of active set). A control returned as `smooth` fails the gate regardless of its cosine.
4. Clause 3: reference quadruped, 2,048 worlds, K = 16, decimation 10: wall time of the forward sweep (with the store on) and of the reverse sweep, at least ten paired repetitions, warm; assert the 95% upper bound of the ratio reverse ÷ forward ≤ 2.0. Record the store size and compare with `frob sim estimate --gradient adjoint`.
5. Corpus cosine for the kill gate: on a 40-design sample of the design corpus at K = 8, the cosine against central differences per design; report the median.
6. Mutants: `stale-factor` (adjoint solve with the previous iterate's factor) must drop below 72 of 80; `drop-labels` (every step reported `smooth`) must fail on the controls.
7. Record through `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- GATE-KIN-10` and emit the receipt path.

**Logging.** NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_stepadjoint_gate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N16c-27"), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claims Known answer and Design comparison; class `Estimated`; tier; determinism; identities; seed), measured values with units and thresholds (fixture derivatives with expected values; per-case cosine against 0.95; counts 72/80 and 20/20; the timing ratio against 2.0 with its disclosure block; the corpus median against 0.5), the suite hash, and on failure the first failing case with its label histogram and the artifact paths. `summary`: counts and `gates: ["GATE-KIN-10"]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The suite of 100 cases is frozen and hashed before the adjoint is run on it, with the planted change of each control documented.
- [ ] Both exact fixtures pass with their labels on `batch-cpu` in `f64`.
- [ ] At least 72 of the 80 regular cases reach cosine 0.95; all 20 controls are labeled `one-sided` or `fixed-mode`, or refused.
- [ ] Reverse sweep at most twice the forward sweep's wall time on the reference quadruped at 2,048 worlds, with the disclosure block.
- [ ] The corpus median cosine at K = 8 is recorded.
- [ ] Both mutants fail the suite.
- [ ] The receipt is cited in `registries/gates.toml` for GATE-KIN-10 by a session that did not implement the mechanism.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script helpers: `cosine` on known vectors; `count_rule` (71 of 80 fails, 72 passes; 19 of 20 labelled plus one `smooth` fails); `ratio_upper_bound` on a hand case (ten ratios of 1.5 give 1.5).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script, as specified. Run time TARGET: under one hour on one CUDA device; the suite's stencil references dominate.

### Known answers and negative controls

The scalar fixture (¼; 1; one-sided 1 and ¼), the block fixture (0; 0.01 s/kg; 2.0 × 10⁻⁴ s/kg), the store arithmetic (160 × 27 kB = 4.32 MB per world; 8.85 GB at 2,048 worlds). The 20 controls are the gate's own negative controls; the two mutants are the script's. Determinism lanes: the adjoint gradient of each case is computed twice and must be bit-identical on one backend; 1 and 24 workers on `batch-cpu`.

## Considerations

- Compute: the stencil references for 100 cases at 64 seeds and up to 61 worlds per seed are a few million world-steps; inside the default envelope.
- The threshold "72 of 80" mirrors the pilot rule of the hybrid-orbit construction (any control not refused or labelled is a failure; fewer than 72 of 80 regular cases at cosine 0.95 is a failure).
- A pass here licenses the adjoint only on smooth objectives over short horizons where most steps are `smooth`; the gate says nothing about objectives decided by a change of contact sequence.
