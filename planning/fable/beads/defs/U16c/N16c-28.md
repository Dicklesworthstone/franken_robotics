## Background

✖ KILL-STEPADJOINT is the pre-declared condition under which step adjoints through the converged contact solve are abandoned. The mechanism (Moonshot, milestone M6) computes design gradients by a reverse sweep that uses the implicit function theorem at each converged lockstep solve (two triangular solves with the factor the step already holds) and derived reverse kernels for the explicit stages; it is exposed as `phylum.gradient --method adjoint`. It is the specified mechanism behind the M6 item "gradients through contact" and the revisit clause of the non-goal "no gradients through contact in the rigid core before M6" (revisited only if a rigid legged co-design result shows contact gradients beating population methods).

**Trigger** (any one):

1. the pilot's falsifier fires: on the incumbent engine, the median cosine against central differences is below 0.5, or adjoint ascent needs more than a quarter of the derivative-free arm's wall-clock to reach the same objective value;
2. GATE-KIN-10 fails after one repair cycle;
3. the median cosine on the design corpus is below 0.5 at a horizon of K = 8 control intervals.

**Fallback.** The paired finite-difference stencil where its own gate holds, otherwise rank-based search (an evolution strategy, racing); the contact response batches (reuse of the converged factor for many right-hand sides at one state, with per-column bounds and a mode guard) keep supplying adjoint columns at a single state.

**Milestone.** M6, with GATE-KIN-10. A Moonshot is priority 4 whatever its milestone; the decision is one step more urgent because three mechanism beads exist only on its "keep" branch.

Two decision points: point A after the pilot (N16c-23), before any derivative kernel is written, is the bead N16cp2-01 (split from this one in polishing round 2, because the beads this one waits on exist only when point A passes); point B after the gate run (N16c-27) is this bead. This bead exists only on point A's `pass` branch: on `fired` it is closed `superseded` by N16cp2-01's ADR.

## Scope

In: point B: re-reading trigger 1 from point A's record, evaluating triggers 2 and 3 on recorded results; the decision entry and the ADR; closing or keeping the dependent beads; the state of `--method adjoint`; checking the owner declaration unchanged. Out: the measurements (N16c-23, N16c-27); point A and the owner declaration (N16cp2-01); the decision-record schema (N40-03).

## Technical approach

**Inputs.** The pilot record of N16c-23 in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (proposed identifier `PILOT-KIN-14`, or the number the register assigned; median cosine; time-to-objective ratio) with point A's release in its `consequence` (N16cp2-01); the GATE-KIN-10 receipt (fixtures; counts out of 80 and 20; timing ratio; corpus median cosine at K = 8); the repair-cycle log if the gate failed once.

**Procedure.**

1. Trigger 1 (pilot, decided at point A by N16cp2-01): re-read and cited, never re-evaluated on new numbers; this bead runs only after point A passed.
2. Point B: a failed GATE-KIN-10 starts one repair cycle (one bounded attempt to fix the cause, then a full re-run on the same frozen suite); a second failure fires trigger 2. Independently, a corpus median cosine below 0.5 at K = 8 fires trigger 3 even if the gate passed on its suite, because the suite's regular cases are selected to have no change of active set and the corpus is not.
3. Append the decision entry to the KILL-STEPADJOINT row of `registries/gates.toml` (N40-03's table: `exit = "M6"`, `outcome` `fired` or `not-fired`, `adr`, `date`, `session`, `receipts` listing the GATE-KIN-10 receipt hashes, at least one, as `not-fired` requires; point A wrote no entry on its release branch, so this is the row's only entry) and write the ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-STEPADJOINT-M6.md` (the program's one directory and naming for kill-gate decisions) with N40-03's required fields: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered and the amendments applied; the numbers beside their thresholds with receipt hashes; the trigger that fired or that none did; the outcome; the fallback in force and the name the capability carries; the beads re-read (N16c-24 to N16c-26, each "stands on the fallback" or "superseded by this ADR"); what changes downstream; the recording session; and the state of the non-goal: on `fired` NONGOAL-15 stays in force beyond M6 with this record as its reason; on `not-fired` it is retired for smooth, short-horizon design objectives only. The owner declaration written by N16cp2-01 is checked unchanged (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger`).

**What changes downstream.**

- On `fired`: N16c-24, N16c-25, N16c-26 close with `fallback` or `superseded`; `--method adjoint` returns to the capability refusal it had before the mechanism (`FROB-REF-724`, exit 5, BD-353), with this ADR as its reason; `opt.start`, the refinement lane and sensitivity analysis keep the stencil where the stencil's own gate holds, and rank-based search otherwise; reverse kernels of explicit stages that already exist stay available to single-state response batches; the "gradients through contact" epic records the outcome.
- On `not-fired`: the option stays, guarded batch by batch by the agreement check; a first-order policy learner remains out of scope and would need its own pilot.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists at the M6 exit, cites the pilot record and the gate receipt by hash, and states for each of the three triggers whether it fired and on what number.
- [ ] If `fired`: `phylum.gradient` serves only `paired-fd`; a request with `--method adjoint` is refused with `FROB-REF-724`, exit 5, zero world-steps, the reason citing the ADR; `frob schema phylum.gradient` marks the method not implemented; consumers run on the fallback.
- [ ] If `not-fired`: GATE-KIN-10's row is `pass`; the corpus median cosine at K = 8 is at least 0.5 and recorded.
- [ ] The decision was recorded by a session that did not implement the mechanism; the KILL-STEPADJOINT row in `registries/gates.toml` carries the entry with the ADR's path and at least one receipt, and the register's load checks accept it; the owner declaration is unchanged.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/kin_stepadjoint_decision.sh` exits 0.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_stepadjoint_point_b.rs`, on scratch copies of the registry files:

- `kill_rule_is_pure`: gate fail then pass on re-run and corpus median 0.62 → `not-fired`; gate pass and corpus median 0.44 → `fired`; gate fail twice → `fired`; corpus median exactly 0.5 → `not-fired`; a scratch record without point A's release → refused as out of order (point A's own cases are N16cp2-01's unit tests).
- `fallback_state`: with the kill recorded, `--method adjoint` is refused with `FROB-REF-724` and exit 5 and the schema marks it not implemented.
- `entry_form`: the entry carries `outcome`, `adr`, `date`, `session` and `receipts`; `keep` or `kill` as an outcome, or a `not-fired` entry without a receipt, fails the register's load check.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_stepadjoint_decision.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N16c-28 milestone=M6 labels=kinesis,kill-gate gates=KILL-STEPADJOINT,GATE-KIN-10 seed=0 needs=cpu plants=suite-pass-corpus-0.3`) (owned by this bead) reads the records read-only, runs `FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_stepadjoint_point_b` and `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger`, evaluates the rule and verifies the resulting surface: `frob schema phylum.gradient --json`; one gradient request per method on the reference quadruped, `frob phylum gradient --design quad-a --metric tracking-error --task stairs-0.4 --method paired-fd --key ksd-fd-1 --json` and the same with `--method adjoint --horizon 8 --key ksd-adj-1` (on `fired`: `e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-724 5 --` with the reason naming the ADR; on `not-fired`: a gradient with its label and cosine). NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_stepadjoint_decision/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript, through the STD-E2E helper (every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time): input hashes, an `assert` line per trigger with measured value and threshold (0.5; ¼; gate status; 0.5), the verification commands with exit codes, on failure the first contradicting field with the record path, and a `summary` line with `gates: ["KILL-STEPADJOINT", "GATE-KIN-10"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the rule cases above. Negative control: a planted record in which the gate passes on its suite and the corpus median is 0.3 must produce `fired` (a suite chosen to be smooth does not outvote the corpus).

## Considerations

- The ceiling was stated before any test: about 30 times fewer world-steps per gradient at 30 parameters, on smooth short-horizon objectives only; several incumbents already differentiate their step, so nothing in the ten-fold thesis counts on this.
- Sunk-cost guard: thresholds are evaluated on records by a fresh session; no threshold is renegotiated after the numbers exist.
