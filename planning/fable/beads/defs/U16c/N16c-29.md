## Background

A kinematic loop (a closure) is a place where the mechanism's graph is not a tree: a parallel knee linkage, a steering linkage, a parallelogram that keeps a foot level. The dynamics kernels run on a spanning tree, so a loop must be imposed somehow. On the lockstep tiers a closure is a **compliant equality row** in the convex solve, with its compliance stated; under the transactional profiles closures are bilateral rows; exact closures on the referee wait on an upstream constraint lane in FrankenSim's repository. Compliance means closure error and one more constraint block per loop.

For the loops robots most often contain, neither is needed. A planar four-bar has one degree of freedom and a closed-form position solution, so the loop can be removed from the constraint system when the model is compiled: the dependent joints become known functions of one input angle, and the step runs in reduced coordinates. That is the Frontier mechanism "closed-form elimination of single-loop planar closures".

**Ceiling.** One constraint block fewer per such loop, and no closure error. No throughput claim is made, and a spatial or multi-loop mechanism gains nothing.

Every Frontier item needs a paper-scale pilot before it is funded. The pilot named for this one is: the parallel-knee archetype on `batch-cpu` both ways (compliant row; eliminated), comparing knee torque and foot trajectory over a 2 s stride against the bilateral-row reference. This bead runs it as a small standalone prototype, and adds a paper census that answers in advance the second kill trigger (whether the recognizer would match at least half of the closures in the archetype library).

## Scope

In: a host prototype of one leg with a parallel knee, stepped three ways; the comparison; the census; falsifiers; the record. Out: the production recognizer (N16c-30), kernels (N16c-31) and reduced embedding in the step (N16c-32).

## Technical approach

**The four-bar closed form (used by the prototype).** Ground link d from O₁ to O₂, input crank a at angle θ, coupler b, output link c at angle φ, both angles measured from the ground link. With (k_x, k_y) = (d − a·cos θ, −a·sin θ) and R = ‖k‖,

  φ = atan2(k_y, k_x) ± arccos((b² − c² − R²)/(2·c·R)),

the two signs being the two assembly branches (+ is the open branch for the parallelogram). Coupler angle ψ = atan2(c·sin φ − a·sin θ, d + c·cos φ − a·cos θ). Transmission ratios: n = dφ/dθ = a·sin(ψ − θ)/(c·sin(ψ − φ)), dψ/dθ = a·sin(φ − θ)/(b·sin(ψ − φ)).

**Prototype (host, `f64` and `f32`).** A planar leg: hip joint, thigh, a knee driven through a parallel four-bar (crank at the hip side, coupler rod, output on the shank), shank and point foot; link lengths and inertias of the reference quadruped's leg, linkage (a, b, c, d) chosen as a near-parallelogram (labelled `synthetic`). A prescribed 2 s stride (hip and crank trajectories from the baseline gait) with the foot in swing and in stance against a flat floor.

1. *Reference:* bilateral rows A·v = 0 on the cut joint, solved with the projector P = M⁻¹ − M⁻¹Aᵀ(A·M⁻¹·Aᵀ)⁻¹A·M⁻¹ at a step of 0.25 ms in `f64`, with drift checked.
2. *Compliant row:* the lockstep form: the cut joint as a compliant equality row with the default compliance, h = 2 ms, `f32`.
3. *Eliminated:* reduced coordinates q = (hip, θ) with dependent joints from the closed form; mass matrix ΓᵀMΓ and bias Γᵀ(M·Γ̇·θ̇ + h_bias) with Γ = ∂q/∂θ; h = 2 ms, `f32`; and the same in `f64` at 0.25 ms.

**Measurements.** Knee (crank) torque over the stride: RMS and maximum difference from the reference. Foot trajectory: maximum position difference. Loop residual (distance between the two images of the cut point) over 10⁶ steps for forms 2 and 3. Constraint rows per step for forms 2 and 3.

**Census (paper).** List every closure in the eighteen archetypes and in the edit catalogue (`closure-add` examples), and apply the recognition rule by hand: exactly one independent loop of four revolute joints with parallel axes; mobility one for the loop; no other closure sharing a link; transmission angle at least about 10° over the declared range.

**Falsifiers (registered before the run; DESIGN, chosen to match the gate and the kill gate).** (a) The eliminated form in `f64` does not agree with the bilateral-row reference to 10⁻⁹ relative in joint acceleration over the stride. (b) In `f32` at h = 2 ms the eliminated form is not closer to the reference than the compliant row in both knee torque (RMS) and foot trajectory (maximum). (c) The census matches fewer than half of the closures. Any one fires KILL-LOOPELIM before the mechanism is built.

**Record.** `frob.pilot/1` entry `kin-loopelim`: linkage, trajectories, the three forms' errors, residuals, the census table, the verdict.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot is registered with its three falsifiers before the prototype runs.
- [ ] The prototype reproduces the four-bar known answers before it is used: parallelogram a = c = 1, b = d = 2 gives φ = θ and n = 1 on the open branch; at θ = 90° the crossed branch gives φ = −143.13° with the coupler end at (1.2, −0.6); crank-rocker (1, 3, 2.5, 3) at θ = 90° gives φ = 98.857°, n = 0.3722 (open) and φ = −135.727°, n = −0.1722 (crossed).
- [ ] Knee torque and foot trajectory errors of forms 2 and 3 against form 1 are tabulated over the 2 s stride, with units.
- [ ] The loop residual of the eliminated form stays below 10⁻⁶ m for 10⁶ steps in `f32`; the compliant row's residual is reported beside it.
- [ ] The census lists each archetype closure with match or the reason for no match, and the matched share.
- [ ] The verdict is recorded; a negative result is recorded in full and handed to the kill decision.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Host tests in `tools/pilots/kin_loopelim/`:

- `fourbar_known_answers` (the values above, to 10⁻¹⁰ rad).
- `ratio_formula_matches_finite_difference`: n and dψ/dθ against central differences at 100 random admissible configurations.
- `reduced_equals_bilateral_single_step`: one step of forms 1 and 3 from the same state agree to 10⁻¹² in acceleration.
- `crossed_branch_mutant_detected` (negative control): the prototype with the wrong sign folds the knee the other way and fails the foot-trajectory comparison grossly (centimetres, not micrometres).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_loopelim_pilot.sh` runs the prototype and the census check. NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_loopelim_pilot/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: commands, exit codes, wall time, input hashes, `metric` lines (torque RMS and maximum error in N·m; foot error in m; loop residual in m; rows per step), three `assert` lines for the falsifiers with measured values and thresholds, the census share against 0.5, and a `summary` line with the verdict and `gates: ["KILL-LOOPELIM"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

The four-bar values; derived check values for the crank-rocker at θ = 90° (computed at drafting time, to be re-derived exactly): coupler angle ψ = 29.345° and dψ/dθ = −0.05479 on the open branch, ψ = −66.215° and dψ/dθ = 0.25479 on the crossed branch; sine of the transmission angle 0.9367 on both. Negative control: the crossed-branch mutant.

## Considerations

- The prototype is throwaway code under `tools/pilots/`; it is not a second implementation to maintain.
- A near-toggle knee (transmission angle under about 10° somewhere in its range) is refused by the mechanism and stays a row; if the reference robot's own knee is of that kind the mechanism helps less than the census suggests, which the census records.
- An idea-pass record already places "symbolic elimination of kinematic loop closures" as a Frontier option for templates with closed-form closures; this pilot is its funding condition.
