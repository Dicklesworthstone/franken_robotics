## Background

When the compiler marks a kinematic loop as an eliminable planar four-bar (N16c-30), the dependent joints of the spanning tree become closed-form functions of one input angle θ, and the dynamics need those functions and their first and second derivatives at every step on every tier. This bead emits them as kernels from the kernel IR (`frob-kir`), the single source from which Rust (`batch-cpu`) and the shared device dialect (CUDA, Metal) are generated, so that the three tiers evaluate the same straight-line program.

**The closed form.** Ground link d, input crank a at angle θ, coupler b, output link c at angle φ, both angles measured from the ground link. The loop equation |O₂ + c·(cos φ, sin φ) − a·(cos θ, sin θ)| = b has the two solutions

  φ = atan2(k_y, k_x) ± arccos((b² − c² − R²)/(2·c·R)),  (k_x, k_y) = (d − a·cos θ, −a·sin θ),  R = ‖k‖.

The two signs are the two assembly branches; the sign is data, fixed per loop by the compiler from the design's reference pose. The kernels needed: φ(θ), the transmission ratio n(θ) = dφ/dθ and n′(θ), and the same three for the coupler angle ψ(θ). With them the dependent joints follow θ: q̇_dep = n(θ)·θ̇ and q̈_dep = n(θ)·θ̈ + n′(θ)·θ̇², and the transformer modulus of a linkage transmission is n(θ) exactly.

Why emitted from the IR and not hand-written: one source for three tiers; dimension typing (angles in, pure ratios out); a certificate that the emitted derivative kernels equal the symbolic derivatives of the position kernel; and the bans of the kernel dialect (no built-in sine, cosine, arctangent or arccosine; only deterministic elementary functions written in the dialect) apply uniformly.

Funded only if the pilot (N16c-29) does not falsify the mechanism.

## Scope

In: the IR programs; emitted kernels on three tiers; closed-form derivative expressions and their certificates; known-answer and precision tests; a branch-safe formulation. Out: recognition and branch choice (N16c-30); use in the step (N16c-32).

## Technical approach

**Kernels (DESIGN names).** Per eliminated loop, batched over worlds; inputs θ and the loop's constants (a, b, c, d, branch sign s = ±1) from the model slot or the parameter row (so link lengths can vary per world as parameter variants).

| Kernel | Output | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| `K-fourbar-pos` | φ, ψ | φ = atan2(k_y, k_x) + s·arccos((b² − c² − R²)/(2·c·R)); ψ = atan2(c·sin φ − a·sin θ, d + c·cos φ − a·cos θ) |
| `K-fourbar-rate` | n = dφ/dθ, m = dψ/dθ | n = a·sin(ψ − θ)/(c·sin(ψ − φ)); m = a·sin(φ − θ)/(b·sin(ψ − φ)) |
| `K-fourbar-accel` | n′ = dn/dθ, m′ = dm/dθ | Differentiate the rate formulas: with D = sin(ψ − φ), n′ = [a·cos(ψ − θ)·(m − 1)·D − a·sin(ψ − θ)·cos(ψ − φ)·(m − n)]/(c·D²), m′ = [a·cos(φ − θ)·(n − 1)·D − a·sin(φ − θ)·cos(ψ − φ)·(m − n)]/(b·D²) |

The rate formulas follow from differentiating the loop closure a·e(θ) + b·e(ψ) = d·x̂ + c·e(φ), with e(α) = (cos α, sin α): a·e′(θ) + b·e′(ψ)·m = c·e′(φ)·n, solved for n and m. D = sin(ψ − φ) is the sine of the transmission angle; the compiler guarantees |D| ≥ 0.17 over the declared range, so the divisions are safe.

**Formulation for precision.** In single precision the arccosine loses accuracy near ±1; the compiler's toggle guard keeps |cos μ| ≤ 0.98544 for the transmission angle, but the arccosine's own argument (b² − c² − R²)/(2·c·R) can still approach ±1 for some linkages. The IR therefore computes φ from a two-argument arctangent of the triangle's sine and cosine (cos β = (b² − c² − R²)/(2·c·R), sin β = √(1 − cos² β) computed as √((1 − cos β)·(1 + cos β)), φ = atan2(k_y, k_x) + s·atan2(sin β, cos β)), which is the same function and is better conditioned. Square root is correctly rounded and allowed; `det_atan2`, `det_sin` and `det_cos` are the dialect's deterministic functions.

**Certificates and law tests.** (1) Loop-closure identity: |O₂ + c·e(φ) − a·e(θ)|² − b² = 0, as a polynomial identity in (cos, sin) of the angles, certified. (2) The rate and acceleration kernels equal the symbolic derivatives of the position kernel (instance certificate through the symbolic sibling; or exact-rational randomized identity tests recorded as `Tested`). (3) Finite-difference agreement on the host in `f64`.

**Determinism.** Class D2 per backend; D1 across backends only where the IEEE probe gate has passed for the device family (the kernels use deterministic elementary functions, so they are candidates for D1).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three kernels are emitted for `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal from one IR source, with manifests (inputs: angle, four lengths, branch; outputs: angles and dimensionless ratios) that pass the dimension checker and the dialect lint.
- [ ] Known answers in `f64` to 10⁻¹⁰ rad: parallelogram a = c = 1, b = d = 2, open branch: φ = θ and n = 1 for every θ in (10°, 170°); crossed branch at θ = 90°: φ = −143.13010°, coupler end at (1.2, −0.6); crank-rocker (1, 3, 2.5, 3) at θ = 90°: φ = 98.85736°, n = 0.37222 (open); φ = −135.72726°, n = −0.17222 (crossed).
- [ ] Derived check values (computed at drafting time; the test re-derives them symbolically): crank-rocker at 90°: ψ = 29.34467°, m = −0.05479, n′ = 0.16134, m′ = 0.22947 (open); ψ = −66.21457°, m = 0.25479, n′ = 0.31866, m′ = 0.25053 (crossed); parallelogram crossed at 90°: n = −0.6, m = 0.4, n′ = m′ = 0.48; parallelogram open: m = 0, n′ = m′ = 0.
- [ ] Loop residual |O₂ + c·e(φ) − a·e(θ)| − b: below 10⁻¹² (relative to b) in `f64` and below 10⁻⁶ m in `f32` for link lengths up to 1 m, over 10⁶ random admissible inputs.
- [ ] Rate and acceleration kernels agree with central differences of the position kernel to 10⁻⁷ relative in `f64`.
- [ ] Certificates (or `Tested` records with reasons) exist for the three identities and are accepted by the offline auditor.
- [ ] CPU-versus-device check passes inside the class for each kernel; a planted one-unit-in-the-last-place change in one prelude is reported.
- [ ] Negative control: the `wrong-branch` mutant (sign s ignored) fails the crossed-branch known answers.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `fourbar_pos_known_answers`, `fourbar_rate_known_answers`, `fourbar_accel_known_answers` (values above).
- `parallelogram_identity`: φ = θ, n = 1 on the open branch at 1,000 angles.
- `loop_residual_f64`, `loop_residual_f32` (property, 10⁶ inputs).
- `rate_matches_finite_difference`, `accel_matches_finite_difference` (property).
- `atan2_formulation_equals_acos_formulation` in `f64` to round-off and is no worse in `f32` near |cos β| = 0.999.
- `branch_sign_is_data`: one compiled kernel serves both branches with s = ±1.
- Mutant `wrong_branch_caught`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_loopelim_gate.sh` (N16c-33) runs `frob spark check --kernel K-fourbar-pos|rate|accel --json` on each backend and the known-answer rows. Log lines: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block (determinism class, backend, fingerprint digest), each known answer with expected and measured value and the 10⁻¹⁰ rad threshold, loop residuals with thresholds, kernel digests per backend, and the first failing input on a mismatch.

### Known answers and negative controls

The four known answers of the mechanism (three computed here; the change point is refused upstream by the compiler). The wrong-branch mutant. Metamorphic: scaling all four lengths by a common factor changes no angle and no ratio; mirroring the linkage (θ → −θ with the branch flipped) maps φ → −φ. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; each device against D2.

## Considerations

- If the derivation toolchain cannot certify the trigonometric identities algebraically, they are certified in the (cos, sin) polynomial form with the unit-circle relations as side conditions, or recorded `Tested`; the word certificate is not used for what was only tested.
- Link lengths per world (parameter variants) are admitted only inside the compiler's toggle guard; a parameter row that would move a loop into its toggle is rejected at admission of the variant, not discovered in a kernel.
- The kernels are tiny (tens of operations); they add nothing measurable to the step and remove one constraint block.
