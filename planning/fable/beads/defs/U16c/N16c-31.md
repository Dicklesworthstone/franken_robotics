## Background

When the compiler marks a kinematic loop as an eliminable planar four-bar (N16c-30), the dependent joints of the spanning tree become closed-form functions of one input angle θ, and the dynamics need those functions and their first and second derivatives at every step on every tier. This bead emits them as kernels from the kernel IR (`frob-kir`), the single source from which Rust (`batch-cpu`) and the shared device dialect (CUDA, Metal) are generated, so that the three tiers evaluate the same straight-line program.

**The closed form.** Ground link d, input crank a at angle θ, coupler b, output link c at angle φ, both angles measured from the ground link. The loop equation |O₂ + c·(cos φ, sin φ) − a·(cos θ, sin θ)| = b has the two solutions

  φ = atan2(k_y, k_x) ± arccos((b² − c² − R²)/(2·c·R)),  (k_x, k_y) = (d − a·cos θ, −a·sin θ),  R = ‖k‖.

The two signs are the two assembly branches; the sign is data, fixed per loop by the compiler from the design's reference pose. The kernels needed: φ(θ), the transmission ratio n(θ) = dφ/dθ and n′(θ), and the same three for the coupler angle ψ(θ). With them the dependent joints follow θ: q̇_dep = n(θ)·θ̇ and q̈_dep = n(θ)·θ̈ + n′(θ)·θ̇², and the transformer modulus of a linkage transmission is n(θ) exactly.

Why emitted from the IR and not hand-written: one source for three tiers; dimension typing (lengths and dimensionless unit-complex components in, dimensionless components and pure ratios out); a certificate that the emitted derivative kernels equal the symbolic derivatives of the position kernel; and the bans of the kernel dialect (no built-in sine, cosine, arctangent or arccosine) are met by construction, because the formulation below needs none of them.

Started only when the point-A decision of KILL-LOOPELIM (N16cp3-02) has released the mechanism after the pilot (N16c-29).

## Scope

In: the IR programs, in a trigonometry-free unit-complex form; emitted kernels on three tiers; closed-form derivative expressions and their certificates; the turn counters of the two dependent joints; known-answer and precision tests; a branch-safe formulation; this bead's script. Out: recognition and branch choice (N16c-30); use in the step (N16c-32).

## Technical approach

**No trigonometry (DESIGN; this is what makes the kernels expressible at all).** The batched state stores a revolute coordinate as a unit complex number z = (cos θ, sin θ) with an integer turn counter, and no batched kernel calls a trigonometric function; the kernel IR has no sine, cosine or arctangent operator, and the registry of deterministic elementary functions (N21a-11) holds no `det_sin`, `det_cos` or `det_atan2` (a call to an unregistered `det_*` function is a build error). The closed form above is therefore evaluated on unit complex numbers, where it is algebraic: the kernels read e(θ) = (cos θ, sin θ) from the crank's coordinate and write e(φ) and e(ψ), using only +, −, ×, ÷, √ and one select-free sign factor s. The angles φ and ψ in radians appear only on the host, where tests and telemetry take a two-argument arctangent in `f64`.

**Kernels (DESIGN names).** Per eliminated loop, batched over worlds; inputs e(θ) and the loop's constants (a, b, c, d, branch sign s = ±1) from the model slot or the parameter row (so link lengths can vary per world as parameter variants).

| Kernel | Output | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| `K-fourbar-pos` | e(φ), e(ψ) | k = (d − a·cos θ, −a·sin θ); R = √(k_x² + k_y²); s_p = (b + c + R)/2; N = (s_p − c)·(s_p − R); D_p = s_p·(s_p − b); cos β = (N − D_p)/(N + D_p); sin β = 2·√(N·D_p)/(N + D_p); cos φ = (k_x·cos β − s·k_y·sin β)/R; sin φ = (k_y·cos β + s·k_x·sin β)/R; cos ψ = (d + c·cos φ − a·cos θ)/b; sin ψ = (c·sin φ − a·sin θ)/b |
| `K-fourbar-rate` | n = dφ/dθ, m = dψ/dθ | n = a·sin(ψ − θ)/(c·sin(ψ − φ)); m = a·sin(φ − θ)/(b·sin(ψ − φ)) |
| `K-fourbar-accel` | n′ = dn/dθ, m′ = dm/dθ | Differentiate the rate formulas: with D = sin(ψ − φ), n′ = [a·cos(ψ − θ)·(m − 1)·D − a·sin(ψ − θ)·cos(ψ − φ)·(m − n)]/(c·D²), m′ = [a·cos(φ − θ)·(n − 1)·D − a·sin(φ − θ)·cos(ψ − φ)·(m − n)]/(b·D²) |

In the rate and acceleration rows a sine or cosine of a difference of two angles is a product of components, never a function call: sin(α − γ) = cos γ·sin α − sin γ·cos α and cos(α − γ) = cos γ·cos α + sin γ·sin α.

Where the position row comes from: β is the arccosine term of the closed form, so that φ = atan2(k_y, k_x) + s·β and e(φ) is e(atan2(k_y, k_x)) = k/R rotated by s·β. The angle g = π − β is the interior angle, at O₂, of the triangle with sides c and R and opposite side b, and the half-angle formula gives tan²(g/2) = N/D_p; cos β = −cos g and sin β = sin g then follow from the tangent half-angle identities. The rate formulas follow from differentiating the loop closure a·e(θ) + b·e(ψ) = d·x̂ + c·e(φ), with e(α) = (cos α, sin α): a·e′(θ) + b·e′(ψ)·m = c·e′(φ)·n, solved for n and m. D = sin(ψ − φ) is the sine of the transmission angle; the compiler guarantees |D| ≥ 0.17 over the declared range, so the divisions are safe.

**Why the half-angle form, and not cos β = (b² − c² − R²)/(2·c·R) directly (DESIGN, for single precision).** The compiler's toggle guard bounds the transmission angle μ (sin μ ≥ 0.17), not β: by the law of sines in that triangle, sin β = b·sin μ/R, so a short coupler makes sin β small while the loop is far from its toggle, and an error ε in a directly formed cos β becomes ε/sin β in φ. Forming sin β as √((1 − cos β)·(1 + cos β)) does not help, because the cancellation is already in 1 ∓ cos β. N and D_p are products of differences of side lengths and N + D_p has no cancellation. Measured at drafting time (IEEE single precision, 10⁶ random admissible linkages with lengths 0.02 to 1 m, both branches; to be re-measured on the emitted kernels): the direct forms, whether finished with an arccosine or with √((1 − cos β)·(1 + cos β)), reach a loop residual of 4.2 × 10⁻⁶ m and exceed 10⁻⁶ m on about 0.05% of inputs; the form of the table stays below 4 × 10⁻⁷ m on all of them and reproduces every known answer and derived check value below. Pinned case: (a, b, c, d) = (0.6, 0.05, 0.8, 1.0) m at θ = 55° has sin μ = 0.931 and sin β = 0.057; the direct form's residual there is about 1.7 × 10⁻⁶ m, the table's about 3 × 10⁻⁸ m. e(φ) and e(ψ) leave the kernel with a norm within a few units in the last place of one; the step's own normalization of unit-complex coordinates (`K-normalize`) applies to them as to any revolute coordinate.

**Turn counters of the dependent joints (DESIGN).** A revolute coordinate is the pair (unit complex, signed 32-bit turn counter), and limit rows read the turn-resolved angle. `K-fourbar-pos` therefore also updates the counters of the two dependent tree joints (the output joint, whose coordinate is e(φ), and the crank–coupler joint, whose coordinate is e(ψ − θ) = e(ψ)·conj(e(θ))), by the crossing rule of the group update: the cut is the negative real axis; with (c, s) the stored coordinate before and (c′, s′) the recomputed one, the counter increments if c′ < 0, s ≥ 0 and s′ < 0, and decrements if c′ < 0, s < 0 and s′ ≥ 0, by selects on integer masks (class D0). The rule reads only the signs before and after, so it is valid while a coordinate's argument moves by less than a quarter turn per step: from the upper half plane into the third quadrant the way round that does not cross the cut is at least π/2 long. The crank moves by at most 0.2 rad per step (the bound of the Cayley update), so a dependent joint moves by at most 0.2·|n| or 0.2·|m − 1| rad; the compiler admits a loop to elimination only when both ratios are bounded by 7 over the declared range (the ratio bound of N16c-30), which keeps the step below 1.4 rad, under π/2 = 1.571. At environment creation the counters are set from the compiler's reference pose.

**Certificates and law tests.** (1) Loop-closure identity: |O₂ + c·e(φ) − a·e(θ)|² − b² = 0, as a polynomial identity in (cos, sin) of the angles, certified. (2) The rate and acceleration kernels equal the symbolic derivatives of the position kernel (instance certificate through the symbolic sibling; or exact-rational randomized identity tests recorded as `Tested`). (3) Finite-difference agreement on the host in `f64`.

**Determinism.** Class D2 per backend; D1 across backends only where the IEEE probe gate has passed for the device family (the kernels use only +, −, ×, ÷ and √, each correctly rounded, so they are candidates for D1). The turn-counter update is exact-integer arithmetic on sign bits, class D0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three kernels are emitted for `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal from one IR source, with manifests (inputs: the crank's unit complex, four lengths, branch; outputs: two unit complex numbers, two turn counters and dimensionless ratios) that pass the dimension checker and the dialect lint; the emitted text contains no trigonometric call and inlines no `det_*` function.
- [ ] Known answers in `f64` to 10⁻¹⁰ rad (the angle taken on the host as the two-argument arctangent of the kernel's unit complex, plus 2π times its turn counter): parallelogram a = c = 1, b = d = 2, open branch: φ = θ and n = 1 for every θ in (10°, 170°); crossed branch at θ = 90°: φ = −143.13010°, coupler end at (1.2, −0.6); crank-rocker (1, 3, 2.5, 3) at θ = 90°: φ = 98.85736°, n = 0.37222 (open); φ = −135.72726°, n = −0.17222 (crossed).
- [ ] Derived check values (computed at drafting time; the test re-derives them symbolically): crank-rocker at 90°: ψ = 29.34467°, m = −0.05479, n′ = 0.16134, m′ = 0.22947 (open); ψ = −66.21457°, m = 0.25479, n′ = 0.31866, m′ = 0.25053 (crossed); parallelogram crossed at 90°: n = −0.6, m = 0.4, n′ = m′ = 0.48; parallelogram open: m = 0, n′ = m′ = 0.
- [ ] Loop residual |O₂ + c·e(φ) − a·e(θ)| − b: below 10⁻¹² (relative to b) in `f64` and below 10⁻⁶ m in `f32` for link lengths up to 1 m, over 10⁶ random admissible inputs.
- [ ] Rate and acceleration kernels agree with central differences of the position kernel to 10⁻⁷ relative in `f64`.
- [ ] Certificates (or `Tested` records with reasons) exist for the three identities and are accepted by the offline auditor.
- [ ] CPU-versus-device check passes inside the class for each kernel; a planted one-unit-in-the-last-place change in one prelude is reported.
- [ ] Negative control: the `wrong-branch` mutant (sign s ignored) fails the crossed-branch known answers.
- [ ] Negative control for the single-precision residual: the `direct-cosine` mutant (cos β formed as (b² − c² − R²)/(2·c·R) and sin β as √((1 − cos β)·(1 + cos β))) exceeds 10⁻⁶ m on the pinned short-coupler case and fails `loop_residual_f32`.
- [ ] Turn counters: on the open crank-rocker (1, 3, 2.5, 3), whose crank turns fully inside the toggle guard (cos μ = (5.25 + 6·cos θ)/15 lies between −0.05 and 0.75), with the crank driven through three full turns in each direction at 0.2 rad per step: the output joint stays between 87.1° and 138.6° and its counter never changes; the crank–coupler joint's turn-resolved angle ψ − θ equals the host's continuous value at every step and has lost three turns after three forward turns of the crank.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/fourbar_kernels.sh` exits 0.

- `parallelogram_identity`: φ = θ, n = 1 on the open branch at 1,000 angles.

- `unit_complex_form_equals_angle_form`: on 10⁴ random admissible inputs in `f64`, the angle of the kernel's e(φ) equals atan2(k_y, k_x) + s·arccos((b² − c² − R²)/(2·c·R)) computed on the host, to round-off; in `f32` on the pinned case (0.6, 0.05, 0.8, 1.0) m at θ = 55° the loop residual is below 10⁻⁶ m.
- Mutant `direct_cosine_caught`: the same case with cos β formed directly exceeds 10⁻⁶ m.

- `turn_counter_follows_crossings`: the crank-rocker case of the acceptance list (output counter constant; crank–coupler counter −3 after three forward crank turns, +3 after three backward); `turn_counter_initial_from_reference_pose`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/fourbar_kernels.sh` (owned by this bead, convention 11; the gate script `scripts/e2e/kin_loopelim_gate.sh` of N16c-33 runs it as a stage and repeats the kernel checks in its `kernels` step). Steps: `unit` (`FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-kir -p frob-kernels fourbar` for the tests above, asserting the count of tests run is the number listed, so that a filter matching nothing fails); `emit` (the three kernels emitted for the three tiers, the dialect lint and the dimension checker run on the manifests, and the emitted text scanned for a trigonometric call or a `det_` call); `spark` (`frob spark check --kernel K-fourbar-pos --backend <backend> --reference batch-cpu --seed 0 --key fk-pos-<backend> --json`, the same for `K-fourbar-rate` and `K-fourbar-accel`, on each available device backend, an absent device logged `not run`); `controls` (`FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-KIN-11`, under which the control runner builds `wrong-branch` and `direct-cosine` and must report each `caught`). It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes `target/e2e-logs/fourbar_kernels/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; the evidence block (determinism class, backend, fingerprint digest); each known answer with expected and measured value and the 10⁻¹⁰ rad threshold; loop residuals against 10⁻¹² relative and 10⁻⁶ m; kernel digests per backend; on a mismatch the first failing input; and a `summary` with assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-KIN-11"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

The four known answers of the mechanism (three computed here; the change point is refused upstream by the compiler): rows `loop-par-open`, `loop-par-crossed`, `loop-cr-open` and `loop-cr-crossed` of the Kinesis known-answer registry (N16-KA enters the rows and takes their numbers from the allocation bead; this bead supplies the tests they name). The wrong-branch mutant and the `direct-cosine` mutant, registered as controls in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` (BD-025's `[[control]]` schema), each a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch in `frob-kernels` built one per build by the control runner with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-kernels/nc-<nnnn>` (N38b-04); the umbrella feature alone plants nothing. Metamorphic: scaling all four lengths by a common factor changes no angle and no ratio; mirroring the linkage (θ → −θ with the branch flipped) maps φ → −φ. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; each device against D2.

## Considerations

- The three identities are algebraic in the unit-complex components (polynomial up to the one square root in sin β, with the unit-circle relations cos² + sin² = 1 as side conditions), which is the form the derivation toolchain certifies; where it cannot, the identity is recorded `Tested`, and the word certificate is not used for what was only tested.
- The closed form is written with an arctangent and an arccosine wherever it is stated as mathematics; no kernel evaluates it that way. If a later consumer needs φ in radians on a device, that is a new deterministic function added to the registry by its own recipe (N21a-11), not a change to these kernels.
- Link lengths per world (parameter variants) are admitted only inside the compiler's toggle guard; a parameter row that would move a loop into its toggle is rejected at admission of the variant, not discovered in a kernel.
- The kernels are tiny (tens of operations); they add nothing measurable to the step and remove one constraint block.
