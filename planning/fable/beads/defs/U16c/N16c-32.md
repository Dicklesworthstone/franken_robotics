## Background

A kinematic loop that the compiler recognizes as a planar four-bar (N16c-30) has one degree of freedom, and closed-form kernels give its dependent joint angles and their derivatives as functions of the input angle θ (N16c-31). This bead uses them in the step: the loop's joints leave the constraint system and the dynamics run in **reduced coordinates**, in which the three tree joints of the loop are replaced by the single coordinate θ.

What changes for that loop on every tier: no closure row, no compliance and no drift remain, and the transformer modulus of the linkage transmission is n(θ) exactly. On the lockstep tiers this replaces a compliant equality row (whose compliance is a stated modelling error); under the transactional profiles it replaces bilateral rows; on the referee it removes the wait for an upstream exact-closure lane for these loops.

**The mathematics.** Let q be the spanning-tree coordinates and q_r the reduced coordinates (q with the loop's two dependent joints removed). The embedding is q = g(q_r) with Γ = ∂q/∂q_r: the identity on unaffected coordinates, and in the column of θ the entries 1 (θ itself), n(θ) (the output joint) and the coupler's tree-joint ratio. Then q̇ = Γ·q̇_r and q̈ = Γ·q̈_r + Γ̇·q̇_r, with Γ̇'s nonzero entries n′(θ)·θ̇ and the coupler's counterpart: q̇_dep = n(θ)·θ̇ and q̈_dep = n(θ)·θ̈ + n′(θ)·θ̇². Substituting into the tree equations M·q̈ + h = τ + (closure forces) and projecting with Γᵀ (closure forces do no work on admissible motions) gives

  (ΓᵀMΓ)·q̈_r + Γᵀ·(M·Γ̇·q̇_r + h) = Γᵀ·τ,

the reduced mass matrix ΓᵀMΓ and bias Γᵀ(M·Γ̇·θ̇ + h).

Rejected alternatives: keeping the compliant row and tightening its compliance (stiffer rows worsen conditioning in single precision and still drift); Baumgarte-style stabilization of a bilateral row (adds a numerical parameter that behaves like a spring).

Tier Frontier [F]; started only after the point-A decision of KILL-LOOPELIM (N16cp3-02) has released the mechanism after the pilot (N16c-29), through N16c-30 and N16c-31, which depend on it.

## Scope

In: the reduction in the lockstep step (stages 6 to 9) and in the transactional step; constraint rows and actuator torques mapped through Γ; integration of θ with exact reconstruction of dependent joints; shape-class accounting; the CPU reference; the option that keeps a loop as a row for comparison; this bead's script. Out: recognition (N16c-30) and kernels (N16c-31); the closure rows themselves, compliant on the lockstep tiers and bilateral under a transactional profile, which are bead N16b-23 and are what the comparison option and the fallback of KILL-LOOPELIM run; the gate script (N16c-33).

## Technical approach

**Coordinate convention for the coupler.** The spanning tree cuts the loop at the coupler–output joint. Its tree joints are: the crank joint (angle θ, kept), the crank–coupler joint (relative angle ψ − θ, dependent, ratio m − 1 with m = dψ/dθ), and the output joint on ground (angle φ, dependent, ratio n). The compiler's record fixes offsets so that these equal the tree's joint coordinates at the reference pose.

**Lockstep step, per world and per eliminated loop.**

1. Stage 1 (kinematics): before the level sweep, `K-fourbar-pos` writes the dependent joint coordinates from θ (each as the stored form of a revolute coordinate, a unit complex number with its turn counter, computed from the crank's unit complex without trigonometry, N16c-31), and `K-fourbar-rate` writes their velocities n·θ̇ and (m − 1)·θ̇. The level kernels then run unchanged on the full tree.
2. Stage 6 (dynamics terms): mass matrix M and bias h on the tree as usual. A new kernel `K-loop-reduce` forms M_r = ΓᵀMΓ and h_r = Γᵀ(M·Γ̇·q̇_r + h) using n, m, n′, m′. Because Γ differs from the identity in one column per loop, the reduction is a rank-structured update: fold the two dependent rows and columns into θ's row and column, then delete them. Reflected rotor inertia and implicit joint damping in the matrix A are reduced the same way.
3. Stage 7 (rows): every constraint row J (contacts on links of the loop, limits and stiction on dependent joints) becomes J_r = J·Γ. A limit on the output joint is therefore a row on θ scaled by n(θ). No closure row is emitted for the loop.
4. Stage 8 (solve): unchanged, in reduced coordinates (dimension n_v − 2 per eliminated loop).
5. Stage 9 (integrate): θ₊ = θ + h·θ̇₊ (the joint's own retraction); dependent joints are **recomputed** from θ₊ by `K-fourbar-pos`, never integrated. The loop residual is therefore the kernel's round-off, with no drift.
6. Actuators: joint torques map by Γᵀ: τ_θ,total = τ_crank + n·τ_output + (m − 1)·τ_coupler. An actuator defined through the linkage (a motor on the crank driving the knee) sees the transformer modulus n(θ) exactly. Reflected rotor inertia follows ΓᵀAΓ: a rotor of inertia J_rotor on a dependent joint enters θ's diagonal entry multiplied by that joint's ratio squared (n²·J_rotor for a rotor on the output joint, (m − 1)²·J_rotor on the crank–coupler joint), and a rotor on the crank itself enters unchanged.

**Reference-pose consistency check (admission).** When an environment is created, each eliminated loop's position kernel is evaluated at the reference pose's θ₀ on the tier that will run it, and its dependent joint coordinates are compared with the reference pose recorded by the compiler (tolerance 10⁻⁹ rad in `f64`, 10⁻⁴ rad in `f32`). A mismatch means the kernel and the model disagree about the assembly branch (or about the linkage); the run is refused before any step, with the loop, both values and the ranked repairs (1) recompile the model, (2) if it persists, keep the loop as a row (`--closures rows`). It is trigger (b) of N16-REF's row `loop-elimination-inadmissible` (block `1xx`, design and model, exit 3, retry `after-change`), whose number N35-33's first-round run assigns at M0, raised behind one named constant with `stage: admission`, `pointer` the closure and `observed` the two values; a generic error never stands in for it. This is the check that refuses a crossed-branch root for a knee assembled open.

**Energy ledger.** Work at the eliminated joints is accounted through Γ (power τᵀ·q̇ = (Γᵀτ)ᵀ·q̇_r), so the ledger closes without a closure-force term; the compliant row's stored energy line disappears for that loop.

**Transactional step (`strict`, referee wrapper).** The same reduction in `f64`; the bilateral rows A for that loop are not created. Other closures keep their rows.

**Shape class.** The degree-of-freedom count of the model drops by 2 per eliminated loop, the link count is unchanged, and the row capacity loses the loop's closure rows. Whether a model is reduced is part of its `ModelId` (the compiler's record), so reduced and row forms never share digests.

**Run option (DESIGN; added to `sim.run` by this bead, whose VERBS-sim row names this bead as owner).** `--closures auto|rows`, default `auto`. It passes the compiler's closure policy (N16c-30): `auto` eliminates the loops the recognizer admits; `rows` compiles the same design with every loop kept as a row (a different `ModelId` when the design has an eliminable loop), for the gate's comparison and for the fallback. The value is echoed in the receipt beside the `ModelId`; there is no value that forces elimination. Any other value is `FROB-REF-802` (arguments fail the schema), exit 3.

**Per-world variants.** Link lengths of an eliminated loop may vary per world through the parameter row; the kernels read them per world. A variant that would violate the toggle guard is refused when the variant is admitted, before any world exists: the compiler's toggle test and ratio bound (N16c-30) are re-run on the variant's lengths over the declared range, and a variant that fails either is refused; message "variant moves an eliminated loop inside its toggle guard", `pointer` the variant and the closure, `observed` the smallest sin μ over the range, `required` 0.17, ranked repairs (1) change the variant's link lengths or narrow the joint range, (2) run the family with `--closures rows`; block `1xx` (design and model), exit 3, retry `after-change`. It is trigger (c) of the same row `loop-elimination-inadmissible`, raised behind the same named constant with `stage: variant`.

**Determinism.** Class D2 per backend; the reduction kernel has no cross-world data flow.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `K-loop-reduce` exists on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal; the step of a model with an eliminated loop emits no closure row for it and runs at dimension n_v − 2 per loop.
- [ ] Single-step parity in `f64`: reduced-coordinate forward dynamics equals the bilateral-row solution (projector P = M⁻¹ − M⁻¹Aᵀ(A·M⁻¹·Aᵀ)⁻¹A·M⁻¹) to 10⁻¹² relative in q̈ on 10⁴ random states of the parallel-knee leg and of the two known-answer linkages with unit link inertias.
- [ ] Over 2 s on the referee wrapper, reduced-coordinate forward dynamics stays within 10⁻⁹ relative of the bilateral-row solution (the gate's parity clause, measured in N16c-33).
- [ ] On the lockstep tiers in single precision the loop residual stays below 10⁻⁶ m for 10⁶ steps.
- [ ] Parallelogram check: with a = c and b = d on the open branch n = 1 and m = 0, so Γ's column is (1, −1, 1) on (crank, coupler-relative, output) and the coupler translates without rotating; its angular velocity is zero to round-off at every step.
- [ ] Actuator torque through the linkage: at the crank-rocker's θ = 90° (open branch) an output torque of 1 N·m appears as 0.3722 N·m at θ; the port energy balance closes to the tier's residual bound.
- [ ] Energy: a frictionless eliminated linkage swinging under gravity conserves the ledger's mechanical energy to the integrator's bound with no closure term; the same linkage as a compliant row shows its stored-energy line.
- [ ] Parameter variants: 256 worlds with different link lengths in one batch equal 256 homogeneous runs bit for bit on `batch-cpu`; a variant whose lengths bring sin μ below 0.17 inside the declared range is refused at admission with zero worlds created, the observed minimum and both repairs.
- [ ] `frob sim run <design> --closures rows --key <k> --json` runs the parallel-knee leg with its closure as a row and a `ModelId` different from the default run's; the receipt echoes the option.
- [ ] The reference-pose consistency check refuses, before any step and with no world-step counter moved, a model whose kernel returns the other branch's root (the `wrong-branch` mutant on the crossed parallelogram: −143.13° expected, 90° returned), with the code of `loop-elimination-inadmissible`, `stage: admission`, both values, the two repairs in order and exit 3.
- [ ] Negative controls: omitting the Γ̇ term (bias without M·Γ̇·q̇_r) fails single-step parity at nonzero θ̇; integrating the dependent joints instead of recomputing them makes the loop residual drift above 10⁻⁶ m; the control runner reports both `caught`.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/reduced_loop_dynamics.sh` exits 0.

## Considerations

- Near the guard (sine of the transmission angle near 0.17) n and n′ are large and the reduced mass matrix is ill-scaled; diagonal scaling of the solve absorbs most of it, and the conditioning certificate reports the rest. A loop that degrades conditioning beyond the solver's certificate is reported and can be kept as a row.
- Joint limits on dependent joints become state-dependent rows on θ; the limit row's coefficient changes sign with n, which the row kernel must handle.
- KILL-LOOPELIM fallback: `--closures rows` behaviour becomes the only behaviour.
