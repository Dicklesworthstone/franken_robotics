## Background

GATE-KIN-11 (claims: Known answer and Parity; first milestone M2) is the gate of closed-form elimination of single-loop planar closures: a kinematic loop recognized as a planar four-bar is removed from the constraint system at compile time, its dependent joints follow closed-form kernels of one input angle, and the step runs in reduced coordinates. Threshold (TARGET):

1. the four known answers to 10⁻¹⁰ rad in double precision;
2. reduced-coordinate forward dynamics within 10⁻⁹ relative of the bilateral-row solution on `referee` over 2 s;
3. on the lockstep tiers in single precision the loop residual stays below 10⁻⁶ m for 10⁶ steps;
4. the crossed-branch mutant and a loop driven through its toggle are both refused.

The four known answers:

- Parallelogram, a = c = 1 and b = d = 2: on the open branch φ = θ and n = 1 for every θ.
- The same linkage at θ = 90° on the crossed branch: the coupler end is at (1.2, −0.6) and φ = −143.13°. A solver that returns this root for a knee assembled open has folded the leg the other way.
- A crank-rocker with (a, b, c, d) = (1, 3, 2.5, 3) at θ = 90°: φ = 98.857° and n = 0.3722 on the open branch; φ = −135.727° and n = −0.1722 on the crossed one.
- The parallelogram at θ = 0 is a change point: all four links are collinear and elimination is refused.

This bead is the end-to-end script that measures the four clauses from the user's side and produces the receipt. It also reports the recognizer's matched share over the archetype library, which the kill gate reads.

## Scope

In: the script, the task variants and the leg's stride task, four clauses, the two refusals, the matched share, the receipt. Out: the linkage fixture designs and the recognizer's own script (N16c-30); the kill decision (N16cp3-02 at the pilot, N16c-34 after this gate).

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/kin_loopelim_gate.sh [--backend batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal] [--seed S]`; sources the STD-E2E helper; idempotent; default seed 16220.

**Fixtures (designs in the design language, `synthetic` unless noted; the six `design:fourbar/*` designs are made by N16c-30, whose own script checks them, and are reused here unchanged; the task variants `hold-<deg>` and the toggle task are this bead's).**

| Fixture | Content |
|---|---|
| `design:fourbar/parallelogram-open` | a = c = 1, b = d = 2 in units of 0.1 m (so the crossed coupler end (1.2, −0.6) is (0.12, −0.06) m; angles and ratios do not depend on the unit), reference pose θ₀ = 90°, φ₀ = 90°, range 20° to 160° |
| `design:fourbar/parallelogram-crossed` | The same lengths, reference pose θ₀ = 90°, φ₀ = −143.13° |
| `design:fourbar/crank-rocker-open`, `…-crossed` | (1, 3, 2.5, 3), reference poses at θ₀ = 90° on each branch |
| `design:fourbar/parallelogram-at-change-point` | Reference pose θ₀ = 0 |
| `design:fourbar/through-toggle` | The parallelogram with declared range −30° to 160° (includes θ = 0) and a task that drives θ through 0 |
| `design:leg/parallel-knee` | The parallel-knee leg of the reference quadruped archetype |
| task variants `hold-<deg>` | For each admissible linkage: the crank's initial angle θ = `<deg>` (30, 60, 90, 120 and 150 for the open parallelogram; 90 for the other three), zero velocity, gravity off, no controller |

**Steps.**

1. Build identity and fingerprint; then the mechanism beads' own scripts run as stages (`scripts/e2e/closure_recognizer.sh` of N16c-30, `scripts/e2e/fourbar_kernels.sh` of N16c-31 and `scripts/e2e/reduced_loop_dynamics.sh` of N16c-32), each `summary` line copied into this log.
2. Clause 1: `frob design check <design> --json` on the four admissible linkages (each must report `eliminated` with its branch); then `frob sim run <design> --task hold-<deg> --tier batch-cpu --profile strict --precision f64 --steps 1 --telemetry T2 --key lk-ka-<design>-<deg> --json` (the task variant `hold-<deg>` of each fixture design sets the crank's initial angle θ with zero velocity and gravity off; `--steps` is N16b-25's option and the levels are uppercase, lowercase being 802), reading the dependent joints' coordinates from the first committed state with `frob telemetry query <run> --stream kinematics --json` (joint state is on the `kinematics` stream, BD-090; there is no `state` stream). A revolute coordinate is stored as a unit complex number with a turn counter and the kernels use no trigonometry (N16c-31), so the script forms φ on the host as the two-argument arctangent of the output joint's unit complex plus 2π times its counter, and the coupler end point from φ. Assert to 10⁻¹⁰ rad: φ = θ at θ ∈ {30°, 60°, 90°, 120°, 150°} (parallelogram, open); φ = −143.13010° and coupler end (1.2, −0.6) (crossed, 90°); φ = 98.85736° and φ = −135.72726° (crank-rocker). The ratios n = 1, 0.37222 and −0.17222 are kernel outputs and not state; they are asserted by the registry rows of step 8, which compare the kernels' outputs directly, and the script logs their measured values from that run's receipt. Fourth answer: `frob design check` on the change-point design returns the branch refusal (exit 3) with repairs "move the reference pose off the change point" then "declare the branch".
3. Clause 2: `design:leg/parallel-knee` under a 2 s stride (prescribed hip and crank torques from the baseline gait, foot in swing and stance) on `--tier referee` twice, each run under its own `--key` (two runs that are compared never share a key, or the second returns the first one's stored receipt): with the loop eliminated, and with `--closures rows` (bilateral rows). Compare joint accelerations and positions at every committed interval: maximum relative difference ≤ 10⁻⁹. The energy-ledger residual of the eliminated run (`frob telemetry energy <run> --json`) is logged against the tier's bound, with no closure term in the ledger.
4. Clause 3: the same leg on each lockstep backend in `f32` for 10⁶ steps (2,000 simulated seconds at 2 ms, or 64 worlds for 15,625 steps each); the loop residual (distance between the two images of the cut point, recomputed in `f64` on the host from the committed joint angles) stays below 10⁻⁶ m at every sampled step; report the maximum. For contrast, the compliant-row form's residual is logged, with no threshold.
5. Clause 4, refusals: (a) the crossed-branch mutant: a build whose kernel ignores the recorded branch (the registered control `wrong-branch`, built under `--features gauntlet-mutations` by the control runner in step 8) must be refused: on `parallelogram-crossed` the kernel returns 90° where the model's reference pose records −143.13°, and the run is refused before any step by the reference-pose consistency check at environment creation (the same check refuses the crossed root for `design:leg/parallel-knee` assembled open); (b) `through-toggle`: `frob design check` reports the closure as `row: near-toggle` with the θ at which sin μ falls below 0.17, and elimination is refused (the plan's refusal: the loop stays a row with that reason in the report; it is not an error, exit 0 with the finding). The task that drives θ through 0 then runs with the loop as a row: its `ModelId` equals that of `--closures rows`, the receipt shows the closure row, the degree-of-freedom count is not reduced, and no `K-fourbar-*` kernel is launched for that loop (launch counter zero). No option forces elimination past the guard.
6. Recognizer share: `frob design check <archetype> --json` over every archetype that contains a closure; matched share = eliminated ÷ closures; logged against one half.
7. Kernels (step `kernels`): `frob spark check --kernel <K> --backend <backend> --key lk-<K>-<backend> --json` for `K-fourbar-pos`, `K-fourbar-rate`, `K-fourbar-accel` and `K-loop-reduce`: the class attained, the kernel digest per backend and, on a mismatch, the first failing input.
8. Gauntlet rows and receipt: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-KIN-11` (the rows `loop-par-open`, `loop-par-crossed`, `loop-cr-open`, `loop-cr-crossed` of the known-answer registry, N16-KA) and `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g2_parity -- GATE-KIN-11` (parity), then both again with `--features gauntlet-mutations`, under which the control runner builds the mutants `wrong-branch`, `omit-gamma-dot` and `integrate-dependents` and requires each to be caught. The script emits the receipt path; the gate value is recorded from it by the reviewing session with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-KIN-11 --receipt <hash>`.

**Logging.** NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_loopelim_gate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N16c-33"), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim, class, tier, profile, precision, determinism, `ModelId` of each form, seed), measured values with units and thresholds (angles in rad against 10⁻¹⁰; parity residual against 10⁻⁹; loop residual in m against 10⁻⁶; matched share against 0.5), refusal blocks in full, and on failure the first failing angle or step and the run and snapshot paths. `summary`: counts and `gates: ["GATE-KIN-11"]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four known answers pass to 10⁻¹⁰ rad in double precision, the fourth as a refusal.
- [ ] Reduced-coordinate forward dynamics is within 10⁻⁹ relative of the bilateral-row solution on `referee` over 2 s.
- [ ] Loop residual below 10⁻⁶ m for 10⁶ steps in single precision on `batch-cpu` and on each available device (an absent device is `not run`).
- [ ] The crossed-branch mutant is caught (refused before any step at environment creation) and the through-toggle loop is refused elimination: reported `row: near-toggle` and run as a row with no four-bar kernel launched.
- [ ] The recognizer's matched share over the archetype library is reported.
- [ ] The receipt is cited in `registries/gates.toml` for GATE-KIN-11 by a session that did not implement the mechanism.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script helpers: `angle_compare` handles wrap-around at ±180°; `loop_residual_from_angles` reproduces zero for exact closed-form angles and 0.01 m for a planted 0.01 m offset; `relative_difference` on known vectors.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script, as specified. Run time TARGET: under 15 minutes on the CPU tier; the 10⁶-step residual run dominates.

### Known answers and negative controls

The four known answers with the values above, as the registry rows `loop-par-open`, `loop-par-crossed`, `loop-cr-open` and `loop-cr-crossed` (entered and numbered by N16-KA; the change-point refusal belongs to the two parallelogram rows); derived check values for the crank-rocker (coupler angle 29.345° open and −66.215° crossed; sine of the transmission angle 0.9367). Negative controls (rows of `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml`, run under `--features gauntlet-mutations`): the crossed-branch mutant `wrong-branch`; the loop driven through its toggle; for the parity clause, the mutant `omit-gamma-dot` that omits the Γ̇ term from the bias, which must exceed 10⁻⁹ within the first stance phase; and for the residual clause, `integrate-dependents`, whose loop residual must drift above 10⁻⁶ m. Metamorphic: scaling all four link lengths leaves angles and ratios unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; devices against D2.

## Considerations

- The parity clause compares two different models (reduced and rows), so their digests are not expected to be equal; the comparison is a tolerance on accelerations and positions, which is why it is a Parity claim.
- At M2 only the first archetypes exist; the matched share is restated when the remaining archetypes land, and the kill gate's second trigger is re-read then.
- If the referee's bilateral rows for this loop are not available at M2 (they are the transactional step's rows A), the parity clause runs against `strict` in `f64` and the receipt names the adjudicator.
