## Background

GATE-KIN-12 (claims: Known answer and Performance; first milestone M2) is the gate of ancestor-sparse assembly and the solve-stage ledger: a solve path that performs the constraint solve's row assembly, factorization, triangular solves and row evaluations on each link's ancestor coordinates only, selected by `sim.run --solve-path {dense, ancestor}`. Threshold (TARGET):

1. the ledger reproduces 375, 969, 1,742 and 7,140 and the row counts exactly, and reports a ratio of 1 for a chain;
2. the three-coordinate fill case takes the dense path and is flagged;
3. the two paths pass the decision-level equivalence test on the standard battery;
4. on the M2 device profile the whole-step gain is at least 1.15 for the 35-coordinate humanoid at 8,192 worlds.

Where the numbers come from: the reference quadruped (18 coordinates: a six-coordinate base and four three-coordinate legs) has a leaves-first factor of 35 + 4·(21 + 28 + 36) = 375 multiply-adds against (18³ − 18)/6 = 969 dense; the reference humanoid (35 coordinates: base, two six-coordinate legs, a three-coordinate waist, two seven-coordinate arms) has 35 + 2·251 + 85 + 2·560 = 1,742 against 7,140; a foot row costs 45 against 171 and 78 against 630.

This bead is the end-to-end script that measures the four clauses from the user's side and produces the receipt. It also records the three numbers the kill gate reads: the device gain on the humanoid, the device gain on the quadruped, and the share of battery world-steps that take the dense path.

## Scope

In: the script, fixtures, the four clauses, the kill-gate inputs, the receipt. Out: the mechanism beads' own scripts (N16c-37, N16c-38), which this script runs as stages; the kill decision (N16cp3-03 at the pilot, N16c-40 after this gate).

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/kin_ancestor_gate.sh --backend batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal [--worlds N] [--seed S]`; sources the STD-E2E helper; idempotent; default seed 16230.

**Fixtures.** The reference quadruped and humanoid; `design:chain/arm-12` (a 12-joint serial arm on a fixed base: a single chain); `fixture:solve/three-coordinate-fill` (a one-coordinate base with two one-coordinate limbs, A = [[4, 1, 1], [1, 2, 0], [1, 0, 2]], one contact row J = (0, 1, 1) with curvature 1 between the limbs, right-hand side (0, 1, 0); `synthetic`), in two forms: the solve-level form, which hands exactly these matrices to the solve kernels of a tier and is a row of the conformance crate, and the design `design:fill/three-coordinate` that realizes the same topology for the user-side run (a 2 kg base sliding on a rail, carrying two 1 kg sliders on the same axis, each with 1 kg of reflected rotor inertia, and one contact pair between the two sliders: the mass-like matrix is the arrow pattern [[4, 1, 1], [1, 2, 0], [1, 0, 2]] and the contact row is supported on both limbs, here as (0, 1, −1); the user-side run asserts only the routing and the flag, the exact solution being the solve-level form's); the standard battery.

**Steps.**

1. Build identity and fingerprint; then the mechanism beads' own scripts run as stages (`scripts/e2e/ancestor_solve.sh` of N16c-37 and `scripts/e2e/sim_solve_path.sh` of N16c-38), each `summary` line copied into this log.
2. Clause 1, ledger: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features frob-dyn/solve-ledger --test kin_solve_ledger -- --nocapture` (the ledger's test target, N16c-36; `--test pilots` named no target) on Q, H and the chain, then `frob design check <design> --json` for the count summary. Assert exactly: factor 375 and 969 (quadruped), 1,742 and 7,140 (humanoid); foot-row contribution 45 and 171, 78 and 630; solves 216 and 324, 647 and 1,225 (multiply-adds plus one diagonal operation per coordinate, the counting convention of N16c-37; the log line also carries the raw multiply-adds 198 and 306, 612 and 1,190); row evaluation 9 and 18, 12 and 35; chain: every ratio exactly 1 (286 and 286, 144 and 144 at n = 12) and the "nothing to exploit" note.
3. Clause 2, fill, in two parts. (a) The exact solve, on the solve-level form: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-KIN-12` runs the fill case (N16c-37's solve-level fixture, which the known-answer registry carries as the control case of its ancestor rows) by handing A, J, the curvature and the right-hand side to the ancestor path's kernels on `batch-cpu` in `f64`; it asserts that the world is routed to the dense kernels and that the solution equals (−1/14, 11/28, −3/28) to 10⁻¹². The same command with `--features gauntlet-mutations` builds the `skip-fill` mutant, whose solution is (−1/10, 11/30, 1/30); the control runner must report it caught, and the script fails if it is not. (b) The flag, from the user's side: `frob sim run design:fill/three-coordinate --solve-path ancestor --tier batch-cpu --precision f64 --steps 1 --telemetry T2 --key fill-anc --json`; assert that the step is flagged `solve-path: dense-fallback` in telemetry and that the receipt's dense-fallback count is 1 of 1 world-steps (`--steps` is N16b-25's option; telemetry levels are uppercase, lowercase being 802).
4. Clause 3, equivalence: `frob spark check --decisions --pair solve-path:dense,ancestor --backend <backend> --workload <Q|H> --battery standard --key eq-<backend>-<workload> --json` (the invocation of N16c-38 over the decision-level test of BD-177) on a frozen ensemble of at least 4,096 addresses per workload with frozen baseline controllers. Assert: one-sided 95% upper bound on the rate of discordant task decisions ≤ 1%; net one-sided discordance bound ≤ 0.5%; continuous outcomes (return, energy per metre, time to failure) equivalent by two one-sided tests inside the fidelity-ledger margins; top of the design ranking agrees. Discordant worlds are listed with their margins.
5. Clause 4, device gain: humanoid H, 8,192 worlds, on the M2 device profile: at least ten pairs of (ancestor, dense) whole-step timings, back to back in randomized order, warm, after the thermal protocol on the Mac and with host load recorded on the CUDA host; gain = dense time ÷ ancestor time; lower bound exp(ℓ̄ − t·s/√n) with ℓᵢ the log ratios and t the 95th percentile of Student's distribution with n − 1 degrees of freedom (1.833 at n = 10). Assert lower bound ≥ 1.15. Repeat for the quadruped Q (kill-gate input, threshold 1.10, no assertion in the gate).
6. Kill-gate input: over the standard battery under `--solve-path ancestor` (`frob eval run <Q|H design> --battery standard --tier <backend> --solve-path ancestor --key fb-<class>-<backend> --json`), dense-fallback world-steps ÷ world-steps, per robot class (threshold 20%).
7. Determinism of each path: two runs per path, under four distinct keys, give identical digests per path and different digests across paths; a run on one path that follows an identical run on the other reports no memo hit; a mixed study (`frob study plan` on a manifest whose two arms state `solve_path` `dense` and `ancestor`) is refused with both step-plan identities and exit 3; the receipt of every run shows `solve_path`, the dense-fallback count and its share.
8. Kernels (step `kernels`): `frob spark check --kernel <K> --backend <backend> --key ak-<K>-<backend> --json` for `K-hess-anc`, `K-chol-anc`, `K-solve-anc` and `K-row-eval-anc`: the class attained, the kernel digest per backend and, where a D2 comparison fails, the first differing world and stage.
9. Gauntlet rows and receipt: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-KIN-12` (the rows `ancestor-quadruped`, `ancestor-humanoid` and `ancestor-chain` of the known-answer registry, N16-KA, and the fill row of step 3) and the same with `--features gauntlet-mutations` for the controls. The script emits the receipt path; the gate value is recorded from it by the reviewing session with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-KIN-12 --receipt <hash>`.

**Logging.** NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_ancestor_gate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N16c-39"), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, `StepPlanId` of each path, seed), measured values with units and thresholds (counts with expected integers; the fill solution; discordance bounds against 1% and 0.5%; the paired lower bound with n, mean and standard deviation against 1.15 and 1.10; the dense-fallback share against 20%), the disclosure block on every timing, digests where compared, refusal blocks, and on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths. `summary`: counts and `gates: ["GATE-KIN-12"]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ledger reproduces 375, 969, 1,742 and 7,140 and the row counts exactly, and a ratio of 1 for the chain.
- [ ] The three-coordinate fill case takes the dense path, is flagged, and solves to (−1/14, 11/28, −3/28); the `skip-fill` mutant is caught.
- [ ] The two paths pass decision-level equivalence on the standard battery on each available backend.
- [ ] Whole-step gain lower bound ≥ 1.15 for the 35-coordinate humanoid at 8,192 worlds on the M2 device profile, with at least ten pairs and the full disclosure block; or the measured bound is logged as a fail and handed to the kill decision at point B (N16c-40).
- [ ] The quadruped's gain and the dense-fallback share per class are recorded.
- [ ] Each path is bit-reproducible and a mixed study is refused.
- [ ] The receipt is cited in `registries/gates.toml` for GATE-KIN-12 by a session that did not implement the mechanism.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script helpers: `paired_lower_bound` (ten ratios of 1.20 give 1.20; alternating 1.10 and 1.30 give 1.136); `exact_binomial_upper_bound` (0 discordant of 4,096 gives about 7.3 × 10⁻⁴; 30 of 4,096 gives about 1%); `rational_compare` for the fill solution.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script, as specified. Run time TARGET: under 40 minutes per device excluding the thermal soak.

### Known answers and negative controls

The four factor counts, the row counts and the chain; the fill case with its exact solutions; the incumbent ceiling arithmetic for context (assembly 38.31 ns and solve 123.55 ns of 262.33 ns is 61.7%; both vanishing gives 2.61; a three-fold reduction gives 1.70). Negative controls: `skip-fill`; a planted bias of one decision in fifty between the paths must fail the equivalence test (the test's own control). Determinism lanes per path: twice on one machine; 1 and 24 workers on `batch-cpu`; debug and release; each device against D2.

## Considerations

- Clause 4 is the uncertain one: fewer operations are not a faster step, and on a device the sparse path trades arithmetic for gathers and irregular loops. A fail is a recordable outcome and feeds KILL-ANCESTOR.
- The M2 device profile is the profile frozen for the M2 performance rows (capacities, step, iterations, decimation); the batch size 8,192 is the gate's, and the receipt names it.
- If only one device family is available, the gate is recorded for that family and `not run` for the other.
