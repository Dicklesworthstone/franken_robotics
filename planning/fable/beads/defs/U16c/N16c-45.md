## Background

GATE-KIN-13 (claim: Known answer; milestone M2) is the gate of the held-command fixture: an ideal slider with commanded acceleration and a control period of 10 ms, whose controller reads the position error x and the scaled velocity w = h·v at each tick and holds u = −(3/2)·(x + w) (u = h²·a) until the next; 64 initial pairs (x, w) in {−3, …, 4}² mm; a case passes when max(abs(x), abs(w)) ≤ 1/32 mm at tick 16. The controller, its rate, the observation and the actuation are the same in every row; only the numerical step between ticks differs.

| Integration between ticks | One-tick map | Spectral radius | Cases passed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact for the held input | x′ = x + w + u/2, w′ = w + u | 1/2 | 64 of 64 |
| One semi-implicit step: velocity, then position | x′ = x + w + u, w′ = w + u | (1 + √3)/2, about 1.366 | 1 of 64, the origin |
| Two such steps of h/2 | x′ = x + w + 3u/4, w′ = w + u | (5 + √57)/16, about 0.784 | 15 of 64 |
| d such steps of h/d, for d = 3, 4, 8, 16, 32 | x′ = x + w + (d + 1)·u/(2d), w′ = w + u | 1/2, 1/4, 0.395, 0.451, 0.476 | 64 of 64 |

Threshold of the gate:

1. the four rows reproduce exactly in rational arithmetic on the host;
2. in single precision on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal the pass or fail decision of each of the 64 cases equals the rational one for the exact map and for d = 1, 2 and 4;
3. analysis A-22 reports a tier radius of 1.366 at d = 1 and withholds the stability label;
4. the mutant study that halves the control period with the step is refused.

A plan-time pilot already found agreement on all 192 decisions of the first three rows between a rational referee and a native single-precision kernel on the Metal device and on each of two CUDA cards, with device read-backs matching independently reconstructed single-precision terminal states byte for byte. That is a recorded FACT about a pilot, "to be re-run before a gate cites it"; this bead re-runs it as part of the gate.

## Scope

In: the script; the four clauses; the pilot re-run; the receipt. Out: the fixture, the analysis and the study refusal themselves (N16c-41, N16c-42, N16c-43); the cross-decimation verdict rule (N16c-44, M3, not part of this gate).

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/kin_held_command_gate.sh [--backends batch-cpu,gpu-cuda:0,gpu-cuda:1,gpu-metal] [--seed S]`; sources the STD-E2E helper; idempotent; default seed 16240 (the fixture has no random input; the seed fixes world addresses).

**Steps.**

1. `frob doctor --json`, `frob spark fingerprint --json`: build identity and fingerprint digest per device.
2. Clause 1, host: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- held_command::rational`. Assert exactly: one-tick matrices [[1/4, 1/4], [−3/2, −1/2]], [[−1/2, −1/2], [−3/2, −1/2]], [[−1/8, −1/8], [−3/2, −1/2]] and, for d sub-steps, [[c, c], [−3/2, −1/2]] with c = 1 − 3·(d + 1)/(4d); radii 1/2, (1 + √3)/2, (5 + √57)/16 and 1/2, 1/4, √(5/32), √(13/64), √(29/128) for d = 3, 4, 8, 16, 32 (as algebraic numbers, with enclosures 1.36602 to 1.36603 and 0.78436 to 0.78437); counts 64, 1, 15 and 64 for every d ≥ 3; the 15 passing cases at d = 2 are (−2, 3), (−2, 4), (−1, 1), (−1, 2), (−1, 3), (−1, 4), (0, −2), (0, −1), (0, 0), (0, 1), (0, 2), (1, −3), (1, −2), (1, −1), (2, −3).
3. Clause 2, tiers: for each backend, `frob sim run --fixture held-command-slider --tier <backend> --precision f32 --integration exact-hold --json` and `--substeps 1`, `2`, `4` (64 worlds each, control period 10 ms in all). From committed state at tick 16 compute each case's decision with the guarded predicate. Assert: the 4 × 64 = 256 decisions per backend equal the rational decisions. On mismatch log the case, the terminal state in both arithmetics and the margin (the closest decision is case (±1, 0) at d = 2: 0.032624 mm against 0.03125 mm, a 4.4% margin).
4. Pilot re-run: for the first three rows on Metal and on each CUDA card, compare the device read-back of terminal states with a single-precision reconstruction computed independently on the host (the same operation order, strict IEEE single precision, no fused multiply-add): byte equality of all 3 × 64 terminal state pairs per device, and equality of the 192 decisions with the rational referee. Record the outcome as the re-run of the plan-time pilot.
5. Clause 3: `frob analyze sampled-loop --fixture held-command-slider --tier batch-cpu --decimation 1 --json` and `frob design check --json`. Assert `rho_exact` = 1/2, `rho_tier` = (1 + √3)/2 with decimal 1.366, label `stable-exact-only` (the stability label withheld), and that the finding names the smallest stable decimation (2).
6. Clause 4: `frob study plan --kind step-refinement` with a mutant arm whose control period is halved with the step (10 ms control at a 10 ms step against 5 ms control at a 5 ms step). Assert refusal "refinement study that changes a clock" at admission, before any step, naming the control clock and both periods, exit 3. Then the valid study (steps 10 ms, 5 ms, 2.5 ms at a fixed 10 ms control period) is admitted and reproduces 1, 15 and 64 passes.
7. Mutants: against `position-then-velocity` the d = 2 count is 7 and the script must fail clause 2; against `hold-resampled` the d = 2 count is 64 and the script must fail; against `ignore-tier-radius` clause 3 must fail; against `decimation-held` clause 4's valid-study construction must be refused by its own admission check.
8. Record through `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- GATE-KIN-13` and emit the receipt path.

**Logging.** NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kin_held_command_gate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N16c-45"), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim Known answer; class; tier; determinism; `StepPlanId`; fingerprint digest; seed), measured values with expected exact values (radii, counts), per backend the number of matching decisions out of 256 and the first mismatching case with both terminal states, the byte-equality result of the pilot re-run per device, the A-22 finding's radii and label, the refusal block of the mutant study, and on failure the artifact paths. `summary`: assertions passed and failed, backends run and `not run`, `gates: ["GATE-KIN-13"]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four rows reproduce exactly in rational arithmetic on the host (matrices, radii, counts, the d = 2 passing set).
- [ ] In single precision on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal, each of the 64 cases' decisions equals the rational one for the exact map and for d = 1, 2 and 4; a device that is not present is recorded `not run` and the gate is not recorded as passed for it.
- [ ] The pilot re-run is on file: 192 decisions agree on Metal and on each CUDA card, and terminal states match the independent single-precision reconstruction byte for byte; or the difference is recorded and the gate stays open for that device.
- [ ] A-22 reports a tier radius of 1.366 at d = 1 and withholds the stability label.
- [ ] The mutant study that halves the control period with the step is refused; the valid study gives 1, 15, 64.
- [ ] All four mutants are caught.
- [ ] The receipt is cited in `registries/gates.toml` for GATE-KIN-13 by a session that did not implement the fixture, the analysis or the study check.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script helpers: `decision_compare` reports the first mismatching case; `byte_compare_states` detects a one-bit difference; `parse_a22_finding` extracts radii and label; `refusal_matches` checks message, observed clocks and exit code.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script, as specified. Run time TARGET: under 2 minutes per backend (64 worlds for 16 ticks, four integrations).

### Known answers and negative controls

The table (the gate's four rows) and the pinned details: case (1, 0) at d = 1 ends at (18817/256, 2037/16) = (73.50390625, 127.3125) mm; the worst terminal value under the exact hold is 1.489 × 10⁻⁴ mm; the origin stays exactly at zero in every row. Negative controls: the four mutants. Metamorphic: negating all initial conditions leaves every decision unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; 1 and 24 workers on `batch-cpu`; debug and release; each device twice, bit-identical terminal states (class D2), and across the three backends bit-identical where the device family has attained bit equality with the CPU reference (the pilot found byte equality on these kernels).

## Considerations

- What the gate does not show: an ideal slider has no actuator limit, no contact and no sensor path; a robot's loop needs its own check.
- Byte equality across backends depends on strict single-precision evaluation with no contraction of multiply-adds; on Metal the safe arithmetic mode and the start-up canaries of the device chapter must be on, or the pilot re-run will differ and say so.
- The gate was defined after the gate register's main tables were compiled; its row lives in the register's annex and in the registry row written by the Kinesis gate-ledger bead.
