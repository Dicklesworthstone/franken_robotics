## Background

Step adjoints through the converged contact solve are a Moonshot [M] mechanism of milestone M6: design gradients
from one reverse sweep per batch (two triangular solves with the factor each lockstep step already holds, plus
derived reverse kernels for the explicit stages), exposed as `phylum.gradient --method adjoint`. A Moonshot is
funded only after its paper-scale pilot has run and been recorded (GATE-GAUNT-08). Its kill gate, ✖
KILL-STEPADJOINT, has three triggers: (1) the pilot's falsifier fires (on the incumbent engine the median cosine
against central differences is below 0.5, or adjoint ascent needs more than a quarter of the derivative-free arm's
wall-clock to reach the same objective value); (2) GATE-KIN-10 fails after one repair cycle; (3) the median cosine
on the design corpus is below 0.5 at a horizon of K = 8 control intervals. Fallback: the paired finite-difference
stencil where its own gate holds, otherwise rank-based search; the contact response batches keep supplying adjoint
columns at a single state.

Trigger 1 is read twice in the plan's order of work: once after the pilot, before any derivative kernel is written
(point A), and with the other two after the gate run (point B). This bead is point A. It was split from N16c-28 in
polishing round 2 (DESIGN): N16c-28 needs the gate receipt and therefore waits on the kernels, the sweep, the verb
and the gate script (N16c-24 to N16c-27); those four exist only on the "pilot passed" branch, and a bead that
exists on one branch of a decision depends on that decision. With one bead for both points, a falsified pilot
would leave nobody able to record the kill: N16c-28 could never start, because its prerequisites would never be
written. Priority 3, one step above the Moonshot default of 4, because four mechanism beads wait on it.

## Scope

In: reading the pilot record; evaluating trigger 1 by its registered rule; on `fired`, the decision entry, the ADR,
the closing of the branch beads and the freed Moonshot slot; on `pass`, the release record that lets N16c-24 start;
the gate's owner declaration; the checks below. Out: running the pilot (N16c-23); the kernels, sweep, verb and gate script (N16c-24 to N16c-27);
triggers 2 and 3 and the final decision of the gate (N16c-28); the decision-record schema and the ADR's required
fields (N40-03); the Moonshot queue (NAB-21) and the owner's funding order (OPEN-PROG-5, N41-09).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs (each cited by digest).** The pilot record of N16c-23 in
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (schema `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`, proposed identifier `PILOT-KIN-14`,
or the number the register assigned; the draft entry name `kin-stepadjoint` is only its protocol file's name): its
frozen protocol digest, the
registration commit, the median cosine over the 20 replicate batches, the time-to-objective ratio of adjoint ascent
to the derivative-free arm, the verdict (`falsifier-holds`, `falsifier-rejected`, `inconclusive`, or `void`), the
negative-control side run (the non-smooth objective must have fired falsifier (a)); the KILL-STEPADJOINT row of
`registries/gates.toml`; the Moonshot queue's count of active items (NAB-21).

**2. The rule (fail-closed, a pure function of the records).**
1. The pilot record is missing, `void`, `inconclusive`, registered after its first batch, its result's
   `protocol_blake3` differs from the registered hash, or its negative-control side run did not fire falsifier (a) (a pilot that cannot fail is not a pilot): **not a pass**. Nothing is
   released; the outcome is `pending` with the reason, and the pilot is repaired and re-run under a new
   registration.
2. Median cosine below 0.5, or time ratio above 0.25: **fired** on trigger 1.
3. Otherwise (median cosine at least 0.5 and ratio at most 0.25): **pass**.

**The owner declaration.** On either branch this bead writes the gate's owner declaration (`[[gate]]` with
`role = "owner"`, the ledger row's `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied, not restated) where
N16c-05 places Kinesis declarations, because the point-B bead exists only if this one does not fire; N16c-28 checks
it unchanged, and `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (N40-30) compares it with the ledger.

**3. On `fired`.**
1. Append the decision entry to the KILL-STEPADJOINT row (N40-03's table: `exit = "M6"`, `outcome = "fired"`,
   `adr`, `date`, `session`), entered by a session that did not run the pilot.
2. Write `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-STEPADJOINT-M6.md` with N40-03's required fields: the kill gate; the milestone; the
   trigger as registered and the amendments applied; both measured values with the pilot receipt's hash beside 0.5
   and 0.25; the outcome; the fallback in force ("design gradients by the paired stencil where its gate holds,
   otherwise rank-based search") and the name the capability carries from now on; the beads re-read (N16c-24 to
   N16c-28, each "stands on the fallback" or "superseded by this ADR"); what changes downstream; the recording
   session.
3. Close N16c-24, N16c-25, N16c-26 and N16c-27 with reason `fallback` citing the ADR, and N16c-28 with reason
   `superseded` by this decision; the epic BD-422 records the outcome.
4. `--method adjoint` keeps BD-353's capability refusal `FROB-REF-724` (exit 5, zero world-steps), whose reason now
   names the ADR; `frob schema phylum.gradient` marks the method not implemented.
5. The non-goal NONGOAL-15 (no gradients through contact in the rigid core) stays in force beyond M6 with the ADR
   as its reason; the Moonshot slot held since the pilot was funded is freed in the queue (NAB-21).

**4. On `pass`.** Write the release into the pilot record's `consequence` ("mechanism released: N16c-24") and a
line in the review log, both citing the pilot receipt; no decision entry is written to the gate row, which is
decided at point B by N16c-28 (DESIGN: a `not-fired` entry here would show the gate as decided before GATE-KIN-10
exists). The item keeps its Moonshot slot.

Determinism class D0 (records and a pure rule). Nothing is written to the run ledger beyond the records above.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rule is implemented as a pure function over the pilot record and reproduces the unit cases below.
- [ ] On `fired`: the decision entry, the ADR with every required field and both values cited by receipt hash, the
      five beads closed with their reasons, the refusal `FROB-REF-724` naming the ADR, and the freed slot are on
      record.
- [ ] On `pass`: the pilot record's consequence names N16c-24 as released; the gate row carries no decision entry.
- [ ] A missing, void, inconclusive or late-registered pilot, or one whose negative-control side run did not fire,
      releases nothing.
- [ ] The session that records the outcome is not the session that ran the pilot.
- [ ] On either branch the owner declaration exists once and `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger`
      passes for KILL-STEPADJOINT.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/kin_stepadjoint_point_a.sh` exits 0.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_stepadjoint_point_a.rs`, on scratch copies of the registry files:
- `cosine_below_half_fires`: median cosine 0.49, ratio 0.10 → `fired`.
- `slow_ascent_fires`: median cosine 0.80, ratio 0.26 → `fired`.
- `both_clear_passes`: median cosine 0.51, ratio 0.24 → `pass`.
- `unfit_pilot_releases_nothing`: a record marked `void`; a record registered after its first batch; a record
  whose non-smooth side run kept a cosine of 0.7 → `pending` in each case, never `pass`.
- `pass_writes_no_gate_entry`: after `pass`, the KILL-STEPADJOINT row has no `decision` entry and the pilot record's
  consequence names N16c-24.
- `hash_mismatch_releases_nothing`: a result whose `protocol_blake3` differs from the registered hash → `pending`.
- `declaration_copies_ledger`: the declaration's trigger and fallback text is byte-identical to the ledger row; one
  changed byte is refused.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_stepadjoint_point_a.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N16cp2-01 milestone=M6 labels=kinesis,kill-gate
gates=KILL-STEPADJOINT seed=0 needs=cpu plants=release-against-falsified`). Steps: `inputs` (read the pilot record
and the gate row read-only, logging their digests); `rule` (`FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
kill_stepadjoint_point_a`, then recompute the branch from the records and assert it equals the recorded outcome);
`declaration` (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger`); `surface` on `fired` (`frob schema phylum.gradient --json` marks `adjoint` not implemented, and
`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-724 5 -- frob phylum gradient --design quad-a --metric tracking-error --task
stairs-0.4 --method adjoint --key ksa-pa-1 --json`, the reason naming the ADR) or on `pass` (the pilot record's
consequence names N16c-24); `plant` (a scratch pilot record with median cosine 0.45 whose consequence says
"released" must make the step fail). The script sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes
`target/e2e-logs/kin_stepadjoint_point_a/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; an `assert` line per input value with its
threshold (0.5; 0.25), the derived and recorded outcome, the refusal with its code and exit, the records' digests,
on failure the first contradicting field and the record path, and a `summary` naming KILL-STEPADJOINT. It writes
nothing to the registry; the recording session does.

### Known answers and negative controls

The four rule cases above are the known answers. Negative control (plant `release-against-falsified`): a record that
releases the mechanism against a falsified pilot must fail the script and the unit test. Determinism: the rule's
output is identical twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- The pilot ran on a foreign engine; a pass releases the work but proves nothing about our kernels, which is why
  triggers 2 and 3 are read again at point B on our own gate.
- Sunk-cost guard: the thresholds are those registered with the pilot before its first batch; they are not
  renegotiated after the numbers exist, and the recording session did not run the pilot.
- If the owner withdraws the item's Moonshot slot before point A, the outcome is `pending` with that reason and
  nothing is released; the queue's order is the owner's (OPEN-PROG-5).
