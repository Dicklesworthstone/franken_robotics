## Background

Ancestor-sparse assembly and solve is a Frontier [F] mechanism of milestone M2: a contact or limit row is nonzero
only on its link's ancestor coordinates, so row assembly, a leaves-first factorization of H = A + JᵀGJ, the two
triangular solves and the row evaluations shrink (for the reference humanoid the factor drops from 7,140 to 1,742
multiply-adds). The architecture decision ADR-KIN-07 assumed, as a HYPOTHESIS, that at 18 to about 60 coordinates
the dense factor is under a tenth of the step, so sparse bookkeeping would not pay. Its kill gate, ✖ KILL-ANCESTOR,
has three triggers in the ledger: (1) the pilot's falsifier holds (on the solve-stage ledger the four operations
together are under one fifth of the measured `batch-cpu` step for both reference robots); (2) the device gain is
below 1.15 on the humanoid and below 1.10 on the quadruped; (3) more than 20% of battery world-steps take the dense
path. Fallback: the dense path for 18 to about 60 coordinates; the tree route only where KILL-TREEOP leaves it.

Trigger 1 is read after the ledger pilot (N16c-36), before the sparse kernels are written (point A); triggers 2
and 3 after the gate run (N16c-39, point B, N16c-40). This bead is point A. It was split from N16c-40 in polishing
round 3 (DESIGN, the split round 2 made for KILL-STEPADJOINT, N16cp2-01, and KILL-INTERACT, N16cp2-91): N16c-40
depends on the gate run, which exists only when the mechanism was released, so a falsified pilot would have left
nobody able to record the kill. Priority 0, the priority of the decision it was split from, because the mechanism
beads wait on it.

## Scope

In: reading the pilot record; the rule; on `fired`, the decision entry, the ADR, the closing of the branch beads
and the hypothesis status; on `pass`, the release record that lets N16c-37 start; the gate's owner declaration;
this bead's tests and script. Out: the ledger and its run (N16c-36); the ancestor table (N16c-35, which stays on
either branch); the sparse kernels, the verb option and the gate script (N16c-37 to N16c-39); the decision on the
gate's receipt (N16c-40); the decision-record schema (N40-03).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs (each cited by digest).** The pilot record of N16c-36 in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`
(schema `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`, proposed identifier `PILOT-KIN-11`, or the number the register assigned): the
registered protocol hash and the result appended under it, with the ledger record `frob.solveledger/1` it cites
(per robot and Newton iteration: the four operations' counts both ways and measured shares of the `batch-cpu`
step; the combined share for Q and for H; the factor's own share; the two-limb share); the KILL-ANCESTOR row of the
fallback ledger in `registries/gates.toml` (N40-29: trigger, fallback, `decided = "M2"`, mode
`pilot-before-funding`, `reads`).

**2. The rule (fail-closed, a pure function of the records).**
1. The record is missing, `void` or `inconclusive`, was registered after the first ledger run, its result's
   `protocol_blake3` differs from the registered hash, the ledger's known answers (factor 969 and 7,140 dense, 375
   and 1,742 sparse; ratio 1 on the chain) are not reproduced in the cited ledger record, or its recorded verdict
   differs from the verdict steps 2 and 3 derive from its values: **not a pass**; `pending` with the reason,
   nothing released.
2. The combined share is below 0.20 for Q **and** for H: **fired** on trigger 1 for the 18-to-35 range.
3. Otherwise: **pass**. A combined share of exactly 0.20 for either robot does not fire ("under one fifth").

**3. The owner declaration.** On either branch this bead writes the gate's owner declaration (`[[gate]]` with
`role = "owner"`, the ledger row's `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied, not restated) where
N16c-05 places Kinesis declarations, because the point-B bead exists only if this one does not fire; `cargo test
-p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (N40-30) compares it with the ledger.

**4. On `fired`.**
1. Append the decision entry to the KILL-ANCESTOR row (N40-03's table: `exit = "M2"`, `outcome = "fired"`, `adr`,
   `date`, `session`), entered by a session that did not run the pilot.
2. Write `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-ANCESTOR-M2.md` with N40-03's required fields: the kill gate; the milestone; the
   trigger as registered and the amendments applied; the combined shares for Q and H with the pilot record's hash
   beside 0.20; the outcome; the fallback in force ("the dense path for 18 to about 60 coordinates") and the name
   the capability carries from now on; the beads re-read (N16c-37 to N16c-40, each "stands on the fallback" or
   "superseded by this ADR"); what changes downstream; the recording session. The draft identifier ADR-KIN-18 is
   withdrawn.
3. Close N16c-37, N16c-38 and N16c-39 with reason `fallback` citing the ADR, and N16c-40 with reason `superseded`
   by this decision.
4. Downstream: runs keep the dense path. The value `ancestor` of `--solve-path` is never built (its owner N16c-38
   closes), and under VERBS-sim's rule for a value behind a kill gate ("724 when its feature is not built or
   killed") it answers `FROB-REF-724`, exit 5, the reason naming the ADR; VERBS-sim's row names N16a-24 (the `tree`
   value, M6) as the option's owner after a point-A kill. N16-REF's post-kill row `ancestor-path-after-kill` (held
   in the `8xx` block, raised as `FROB-REF-802` with `detail` the slug and the coordinate range in `observed`) is
   the point-B kill's, where the path was built and stays admitted above 60 coordinates. The ancestor table (N16c-35) and the ledger stay, because
   the tree-operator pilot at M5 and the profile's solve-stage breakdown read them.
5. The hypothesis register: H-04b closed as "not a lever at 18 to 35 coordinates" with the measured combined
   shares; ADR-KIN-07's hypothesis "the dense factor is under a tenth of our step at 18 to 35 degrees of freedom"
   marked confirmed or refuted with the ledger's measured factor share (N16c-36 prints it); nothing in the ten-fold
   thesis changes, since the mechanism was never counted in it.

**5. On `pass`.** Write the release into the pilot record's `consequence` ("mechanism released: N16c-37") and a
line in the review log, both citing the pilot record's hash; no decision entry is written to the gate row, which
is decided at point B by N16c-40. The hypothesis status is recorded at point B.

Determinism class D0 (records and a pure rule).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rule is a pure function over the pilot record, the ledger record it cites and the ledger row, and
      reproduces the unit cases below.
- [ ] On `fired`: the entry, `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-ANCESTOR-M2.md` with every field N40-03 requires and the shares
      cited by the record's hash, the four beads closed with their reasons, and the hypothesis entries updated are
      on record; the register's load checks accept the entry; `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example pilots --
      fundable --bead N16c-37 --json` answers `Blocked` with reason `falsifier-holds`.
- [ ] On `pass`: the pilot record's consequence names N16c-37 as released; the gate row carries no decision entry;
      the same command answers `Fundable`.
- [ ] On either branch the owner declaration exists once and `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger`
      passes for KILL-ANCESTOR.
- [ ] An unfit pilot record (missing, void, inconclusive, late, hash-mismatched, or with a known answer not
      reproduced) releases nothing.
- [ ] The session that records the outcome is not the session that ran the pilot.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/kin_ancestor_point_a.sh` exits 0.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_ancestor_point_a.rs`, on scratch copies of the registry files (`synthetic`
records in the register's schema):
- `both_under_fifth_fires`: combined shares (0.18, 0.19) → `fired`.
- `one_robot_clears_passes`: (0.18, 0.24) → `pass` (the rule needs both robots under one fifth).
- `exactly_fifth_passes`: (0.20, 0.12) → `pass`.
- `known_answer_missing_refused`: a ledger record whose dense factor count at n = 18 is 968 → `pending`.
- `unfit_pilot_releases_nothing`: a `void` record; a result with another protocol hash; a `falsifier-rejected`
  verdict over shares (0.18, 0.19) → `pending`, never `pass`.
- `pass_writes_no_gate_entry`: after `pass` the row has no `decision` entry and the consequence names N16c-37.
- `fired_entry_form`: `exit = "M2"`, `outcome = "fired"`, ADR path `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-ANCESTOR-M2.md`, a date and a
  session; `kill` as the outcome fails the load check.
- `declaration_copies_ledger`: trigger and fallback text byte-identical to the ledger row; one changed byte refused.
- `self_recording_refused`: a recording session equal to the pilot's session is refused.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_ancestor_point_a.sh` (owned by this bead; header `# e2e: bead=N16cp3-03 milestone=M2
labels=kinesis,kill-gate gates=KILL-ANCESTOR seed=0 needs=cpu plants=release-against-falsified`; idempotent).
Steps: `inputs` (the pilot record, the ledger record and the gate row read-only, with digests); `rule`
(`FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ancestor_point_a`, then recompute the branch from the
records and assert it equals the recorded outcome); `declaration` (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
kill_ledger`); `surface` (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example pilots -- fundable --bead N16c-37 --json`:
`Blocked` with `falsifier-holds` on `fired`, `Fundable` on `pass`; on `fired` also the ADR's path exists and the
hypothesis entries name it); `plant` (a scratch record with shares (0.18, 0.19) whose consequence says "released"
must make the step fail). It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes
`target/e2e-logs/kin_ancestor_point_a/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code and wall time; the
record's identifier and hash; per robot the combined share against 0.20 and the factor's share against 0.10 on
`assert` and `metric` lines; the known answers re-read from the ledger record; the derived and recorded outcome; on
failure the first contradicting field and the record path; a `summary` with assertions passed and failed, naming
KILL-ANCESTOR and GATE-KIN-12. It writes nothing to the registry.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the rule cases above; 0.20 exactly does not fire; the ledger's counts (969/375, 7,140/1,742, ratio 1
on the chain) are re-read, not recomputed. Negative controls: the planted release against a falsified record (plant
`release-against-falsified`); a rule that fires when either robot is under one fifth returns `fired` on (0.18, 0.24)
and must fail `one_robot_clears_passes`; an amendment of the threshold dated after the pilot's result is refused
(N40-03's `amended-after-measurement`). Determinism: a pure function of the records.

## Considerations

- The ledger is a CPU measurement; a pass releases a device experiment and proves nothing about the device gain,
  which point B reads on the gate.
- A kill here does not close the idea above 60 coordinates; that question has its own pilot and kill gate
  (KILL-TREEOP, M5), which reads the ancestor table and the ledger this bead leaves in place.
- Sunk-cost guard: the threshold is the one registered before the first ledger run; the recording session did not
  run the pilot.
