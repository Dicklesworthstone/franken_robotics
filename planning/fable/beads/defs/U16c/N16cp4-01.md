## Background

✖ KILL-LOOPELIM is the kill gate of closed-form elimination of single-loop planar closures (Frontier, M2): a
kinematic loop recognized as a planar four-bar is removed from the constraint system at compile time and the step
runs in reduced coordinates. Its current trigger text (N40-29: the defining row's two clauses and the amendment
that adds the pilot's falsifier) has three clauses: the parity part of GATE-KIN-11 fails on any archetype loop after
one repair cycle; or the recognizer matches fewer than half of the closures in the archetype library; or the
pilot's falsifier holds. Fallback: compliant equality rows on the lockstep tiers and bilateral rows under the
transactional profiles.

The second clause depends on which archetypes exist. At M2 only the first archetypes of the catalog are files, so
the point-B decision (N16c-34) counts the closures of the archetypes present then and fixes the rule: matched share
= eliminated closures ÷ all closures of the archetypes present at the time of reading; below one half fires. The
remaining fourteen archetypes land at M3 (BD-246). The kill row therefore carries `reread = ["M3"]` (N40-29), and
the M3 exit (BD-319) is due an entry of this row with the ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-LOOPELIM-M3.md`; a missing entry
blocks that exit like any decision. This bead takes the reading on all eighteen archetypes and records that entry,
as N17bp1-01 does for KILL-LIMBKERNEL's coverage clause and N19c-19 for KILL-POOL. Only the second clause is read
again: the parity clause and the pilot's falsifier were decided at M2 and are cited, never re-evaluated.

## Scope

In: the preconditions read from the row's M2 entry; the census of every closure in the eighteen archetypes through
`design.check`; the share and the rule; the M3 decision entry and its ADR; the comparison with the pilot's paper
census; on `fired`, the fallback's state checked on the surface; this bead's tests and script. Out: the recognizer
(N16c-30); the M2 decisions at the pilot (N16cp3-02) and on the gate's receipt (N16c-34); authoring the archetypes
(BD-246); the decision-record schema and the due list (N40-03); the M3 exit review (BD-319).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs (each cited by digest).** The KILL-LOOPELIM row of `registries/gates.toml` with its M2 entry and its
ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-LOOPELIM-M2.md`; the archetype catalog (`assets/archetypes/`, the eighteen listed by
`frob design templates --json`) at the commit read, each with its pinned default instance `zoo/<name>.fbot`; the
pilot's census file `docs/pilots/kin-loopelim-census.toml` (N16c-29) for the comparison of step 4.

**2. Preconditions (fail-closed, before anything is measured).**
1. The row has no M2 entry, or its M2 entry was edited after it was written: **pending**, nothing written; the M2
   exit's decision is missing and is not taken here.
2. The entry in force is `fired` (point A, N16cp3-02, or point B, N16c-34): nothing is measured. The M3 entry is
   `fired` again, with the same `fallback_form`, and its ADR states that the re-read clause was not evaluated
   because the fallback has been in force since the M2 ADR it cites; a re-read never reverses a fired gate.
3. `design templates` lists fewer than eighteen archetypes, or an archetype's pinned instance is missing: no
   reading is taken; the entry is `deferred` with `deferred_to = "M4"` and the reason naming the missing
   archetypes, by an ADR at the same path, so the M3 exit reports the row deferred, not missing.
4. The entry in force is `not-reached` (the mechanism was never funded): the M3 entry is `not-reached` with the
   same reason, and nothing is measured. It is `deferred` (the M2 decision moved to M3 by its ADR): this bead waits
   until that deferred decision is recorded, applies item 2 or item 5 to it, and its own entry is then the row's
   second M3 entry, with `reading = "reread"` and the ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-LOOPELIM-M3-reread.md` (N40-03's form
   for a second entry at one exit).
5. Otherwise (`not-fired` in force at M2): steps 3 to 5 run.

**3. The census (DESIGN: the denominator is fixed before any count).** The list of the eighteen archetypes, the
commit and the recognizer version are written to the script's log before the first check runs. For each archetype,
`frob design check zoo/<name>.fbot --json` under the default closure policy `auto`; each closure's finding is
`eliminated` or `row` with its reason (N16c-30's finding). An archetype refused before its closures are reported
is counted fail-closed: its closures, counted from the design file's `closure` constructors, enter the denominator
as not eliminated, and the refusal code is listed. Matched share = eliminated ÷ all closures of the eighteen. An
archetype with no closure adds nothing to either count. If the eighteen have no closure at all the share is
undefined and the clause cannot fire (it reads "fewer than half of the closures"); the entry is `not-fired` with
the count of zero stated.

**4. Comparison with the pilot's census (logged, not a rule input).** Each closure's verdict beside the verdict the
paper census N16c-29 wrote for it. Every difference is listed with archetype, closure, census verdict and recognizer verdict, so that the ADR
can say whether the pilot's paper reading held.

**5. The rule and the record.** Share below one half → `fired`; one half or more → `not-fired` (exactly one half
does not fire, as at M2). The decision entry is appended to the row's `decision` array, the M2 entry untouched:
`{ exit = "M3", outcome, adr = "docs/adr/ADR-KILL-LOOPELIM-M3.md", date, session, receipts }`, with `receipts`
the hash of the receipt this bead's script leaves (the census result: per archetype its closures and verdicts, the
counts, the share, the inputs' digests), committed under `receipts/gates/` as N40-03's load check requires of a
`not-fired` entry; on `fired` the entry also carries `fallback_form`, a phrase of the row's `fallback`. The ADR
has N40-03's required fields: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered and the amendment applied;
the share with its numerator and denominator beside one half and the receipt hash; the M2 entry cited for the two
clauses not re-read; the outcome; the fallback in force and the name the capability carries; the beads re-read
(N16c-30, N16c-31, N16c-32, each "stands" or "stands on the fallback"); what changes downstream; the step-4
differences; the recording session, which did not implement the mechanism.

**6. On `fired` at M3.** The downstream changes are those of N16c-34's `fired` branch, applied from this entry:
the compiler's closure policy (N16c-30) is fixed to `rows`, so it stops marking closures eliminable and
`design.check` reports every closure as `row`; models compile with all loops as rows (their `ModelId`s are those
of `--closures rows`); `--closures rows` stays accepted; the four-bar kernels may remain as a known-answer oracle;
the counterexample rows on the wrong root and the toggle remain. On `not-fired` nothing changes.

Determinism: the census is a function of the archetype files and the recognizer version (compile products are
class D0); the rule is a pure function of the counts and the M2 entry.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At the M3 exit the KILL-LOOPELIM row carries an entry with `exit = "M3"`, its ADR at
      `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-LOOPELIM-M3.md` (`-M3-reread.md` in the deferred case of precondition 4) with N40-03's
      required fields, and the register's load checks accept it;
      the M2 entry is byte-identical before and after.
- [ ] The share is computed over all eighteen archetypes, with every closure's verdict and reason listed, the
      archetypes refused before their closures counted as not eliminated with their codes, and the denominator
      written before the first check.
- [ ] With `not-fired` in force at M2, the outcome is `fired` exactly when the share is below one half, and a
      `not-fired` entry cites at least one receipt that resolves under `receipts/gates/`.
- [ ] With `fired` in force at M2, nothing is measured and the M3 entry restates `fired` citing the M2 ADR.
- [ ] Fewer than eighteen archetypes on file gives a `deferred` entry naming M4, never a share over a partial
      catalog.
- [ ] Every difference between the recognizer's verdicts and the pilot's paper census is listed in the ADR.
- [ ] On `fired`: `frob design check design:leg/parallel-knee --json` reports its closure as `row`, and
      `frob sim run design:fourbar/parallelogram-open --closures auto --key kin-loopelim-m3-auto --json` and the same with
      `--closures rows --key kin-loopelim-m3-rows` (the `sim.run` option of N16c-32; each run under its own key)
      return the same `ModelId`.
- [ ] The decision is recorded by a session that did not implement the mechanism.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/kin_loopelim_m3_reread.sh` exits 0.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_loopelim_m3.rs`, on scratch copies of the registry files (`synthetic` counts):
- `share_rule`: 9 of 16 eliminated → `not-fired`; 8 of 16 → `not-fired`; 7 of 16 → `fired`.
- `refused_archetype_counts_against`: 9 eliminated of 16 in seventeen archetypes plus one archetype refused before
  its closures with 2 closures in its file → 9 of 18 = one half → `not-fired`; with 3 closures in its file → 9 of
  19 → `fired`.
- `no_closures_does_not_fire`: 0 of 0 → `not-fired`, the zero count stated.
- `fired_at_m2_is_restated`: an M2 entry `fired` → an M3 entry `fired` with the same `fallback_form`, no census run;
  an M2 entry `not-reached` → an M3 entry `not-reached`.
- `deferred_waits_then_second_reading`: an M2 entry `deferred` to M3 with no M3 decision yet → `pending`; once the
  deferred decision is recorded `not-fired`, the census runs and the entry carries `reading = "reread"` and the
  `-M3-reread.md` ADR path.
- `partial_catalog_defers`: seventeen archetypes → `deferred`, `deferred_to = "M4"`, the missing name in the reason.
- `m2_entry_untouched`: the M2 entry's bytes equal before and after; an edited M2 entry → `pending`.
- `entry_form`: the M3 entry carries `exit`, `outcome`, `adr`, `date`, `session`, `receipts`; `keep` or `kill` as
  an outcome, a `not-fired` entry without a receipt, or an ADR path other than
  `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-LOOPELIM-M3.md` (or the `-M3-reread.md` path with its `reading`) fails the register's load.
- `self_recording_refused`: a session that implemented N16c-30, N16c-31 or N16c-32 is refused as recorder.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kin_loopelim_m3_reread.sh` (owned by this bead; header `# e2e: bead=N16cp4-01 milestone=M3
labels=kinesis,kill-gate gates=KILL-LOOPELIM seed=0 needs=cpu plants=share-below-half`; idempotent). Steps:
`inputs` (the row, the M2 ADR, the census file and `frob design templates --json` read-only, with digests; the
archetype list and its count written before any check); `census` (`frob design check zoo/<name>.fbot --json` for
each of the eighteen, every closure's verdict and reason logged); `compare` (each verdict beside the paper
census's); `rule` (`FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_loopelim_m3`, then the outcome
recomputed from the logged counts and compared with the recorded entry); `ledger` (`cargo test -p frob-conformance
--test kill_ledger`, the owner declaration of KILL-LOOPELIM unchanged); `surface` (on `fired`, `frob design check`
on the parallel-knee archetype, and the two `frob sim run ... --closures auto|rows` calls of the acceptance
criterion on `design:fourbar/parallelogram-open` (N16c-30's fixture), each under its own `--key`, comparing the receipts' `ModelId`); `plant` (a scratch catalog in which
one archetype's eliminable four-bar is made spatial so that the share falls below one half must give `fired`). It
sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes `target/e2e-logs/kin_loopelim_m3_reread/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall
time; per archetype its closures with verdict and reason; the counts and the share against one half; the
census differences; the derived and recorded outcome; on failure the first contradicting field with the file path;
the receipt's hash; and a `summary` naming KILL-LOOPELIM. It writes nothing to the registry.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the rule cases above; one half exactly does not fire. Negative controls: the planted spatial loop
(`share-below-half`) must fire; a rule that drops refused archetypes from the denominator passes the 9-of-19 case
and must fail `refused_archetype_counts_against`; a run that reads the share before writing the archetype list is
refused by the script. Determinism: two runs on one commit give identical verdict lists and the same receipt hash.

## Considerations

- The denominator rule was fixed at M2 by N16c-34 and is applied unchanged; it cannot be chosen after the counts
  exist. The step-4 comparison with the paper census informs the ADR and never moves the threshold.
- A kill at M3 costs no guarantee: every closure becomes a row, which is the behaviour before the mechanism, and the
  fidelity ledger already measures the compliant rows' error. Models compiled with eliminated loops get new
  `ModelId`s under `rows`, which the ADR names under what changes downstream.
- Sunk-cost guard: the recording session did not build the recognizer, the kernels or the reduced dynamics.
