## Background

A campaign should not simulate the same closed-loop system twice. Three mechanisms remove duplicates, each exact
and each with a stated limit; none is a source of large factors except on fixtures built to have them. This seed
bead composes them into one pipeline and one verb:

1. **The similarity canonical form** (Solid): a design divided by its own scales of mass, length and time, so
   that descriptions differing only in units coincide and exact similarity images are recognised (N17a-22).
2. **Orbit representatives under the observation-preserving subgroup** (Solid): one canonical representative per
   orbit of Γ_obs on the slots of a combinatorial sweep, with the count known in advance from the cycle index
   (N17a-23) and results transported across the orbit with the recorded group element (N17a-24).
3. **The mission-observable digest** (Frontier): two designs that lower to the same model tables under the same
   task, scenes, policy, kernels, tier and profile are the same experiment (N17a-26).

The honest sizes: orbits save a factor between two and twelve on symmetric sweeps, shrinking as sensors break
symmetry (86,185 orbits of 10⁶ labelings under the dihedral group of order 12, a reduction of 11.6; 500,500 under
a mirror alone, a reduction of 2.0); the digest's factor is a property of the fixture (24,546 classes from 735,471
ballast placements, a reduction of 30.0) and is 1 in general. Algebra is spent at campaign time, where it deletes
simulations exactly and before any run, not in the per-step loop.

ADR-PHYLUM-04 governs: deduplication uses only the observation-preserving symmetry subgroup, because the
mechanical group ignores sensors, payload and task; a quadruped with a front-only lidar has a four-element
mechanical group and a two-element closed-loop group, and merging under the larger one merges designs the mission
can tell apart. ADR-PHYLUM-06 governs the digest: exact sharing only.

The seed's done-condition: GATE-PHYLUM-01 and KILL-QUOTIENT decided. GATE-PHYLUM-01 (Known answer, M3, TARGET):
the orbit and ballast rows exact; zero false quotients in 10⁴ sampled class pairs by replay; the lidar and
equal-Σx controls caught. KILL-QUOTIENT (decided at M3): one false quotient survives a repair cycle, or digest
classes save less than 5% of dispatches on the campaign corpus; fallback: orbit representatives only, with the
digest kept as a cache key with no claim. This bead carries the exact rows and the controls; the replay audit and
the savings measurement are bead N17a-28, and the decision is bead N17a-29, both of which test this bead and
therefore depend on it. The bet served is campaign quotienting (BET-31): exact deduplication removes a measurable
share of campaign work without removing a winner.

## Scope

In: the quotient pipeline over a design set or a sweep; the map from every design to its representative, group
element and class; result expansion after a sweep; the saved-dispatch report by mechanism; the verb
`phylum.quotient`; the sweep and cascade hooks; the fallback configuration. Out: the three mechanisms themselves
(N17a-22, -23, -24, -26); the invalidation contract and `phylum.reuse` (BD-286); the gate's replay audit and
savings measurement (N17a-28); the kill decision (N17a-29); quotients and cuts inside mission family search
(BD-357).

## Technical approach

Types (DESIGN):

```rust
pub struct QuotientPlan {
    dispatch: Vec<DesignId>,                       // the experiments that actually run
    map: BTreeMap<DesignId, Assignment>,           // every input design, including those that run
    report: SavedReport,
}
pub enum Assignment {
    Runs,
    OrbitMember { representative: DesignId, element: GroupElem },      // results by transport
    DigestMember { representative: DesignId, digest: MissionDigest },  // results shared unchanged
    SimilarityImage { representative: DesignId, alpha: (Ratio, Ratio, Ratio) },  // only for equal experiment keys
}
pub struct SavedReport { designs: u64, dispatched: u64, by_similarity: u64, by_orbit: u64, by_digest: u64,
                         orbit_count_expected: Option<u64> /* cycle index */, singleton_share: Ratio }
```

Pipeline `quotient(designs or sweep, task, scenes, group?) -> QuotientPlan`:

1. **Similarity.** Compute each design's similarity key; designs with equal experiment-level keys are assigned
   to the least `DesignId` among them, with the factors recorded. (Almost always a no-op inside one campaign; it
   removes unit and description duplicates.)
2. **Orbits.** For a sweep (k types over n slots of a chassis), take Γ_obs for the chassis and task; refuse with
   `FROB-REF-161` if a requested group is not observation-preserving; enumerate canonical representatives; check
   that their number equals the cycle-index count (PO-PHYLUM-01). For a listed design set, compute each design's
   orbit key and assign members to the representative with the group element that maps them.
3. **Digest classes.** Among the remaining representatives, compute mission-observable digests for the stated
   task, scenes, policy, kernels, tier and profile; assign each class to its least `DesignId`. Skipped when the
   flag `mission-digest` is off or KILL-QUOTIENT has fired.
4. **Plan.** `dispatch` is the set of designs assigned `Runs`. The report counts what each mechanism removed, and
   never multiplies the factors.
5. **Result expansion.** After the dispatched designs have results, each non-dispatched design receives its own:
   orbit members by transport (distributional results always; per-address results only at mapped addresses;
   address-paired comparisons re-run), digest members by sharing unchanged, similarity images by rescaling. Every
   expanded result is keyed by its own `DesignId`, labelled with how it was obtained and from which
   representative, and is otherwise indistinguishable in schema from a run result.

Verb `phylum.quotient` (kind `read`, cluster `families`, first milestone M3; it never runs physics):

- `frob phylum quotient --sweep <chassis> --slots <slot set> --types <t₁,…,t_k> [--task <T>] [--group <obs|file>]
  --json` returns the group order, cycle types, raw count kⁿ, orbit count, reduction, and the representatives
  (paginated; a long answer is paginated and says so, never cut silently).
- `frob phylum quotient --designs <set> --task <T> --scenes <S> [--policy <P> --tier <t> --profile <p>] --json`
  returns similarity classes, orbit classes, mission-digest classes for the set, with the fields that split any
  two requested classes (`--explain <a>,<b>`), and the `SavedReport`.
- Output schema `frob.phylum.quotient.out/1` (DESIGN). Envelope evidence: class `Tested`; tier `static`;
  determinism `exact`; identities of the design set, the group and the task. Refusals: `FROB-REF-161` (group not
  observation-preserving; repairs: use Γ_obs; declare the invariance and let the metamorphic test check it; exit
  3) and, on the memoized path, `FROB-REF-162`.

Hooks: `phylum.sweep` gains the option to dispatch a `QuotientPlan` (DESIGN name `--quotient`), and stage 1 of the
co-design cascade (deduplicate and check) calls the same pipeline; that stage's built-in control is two designs
that must merge and two that must split, which this bead provides as a fixture: the mirror pair (must merge under
Γ_obs) and the equal-Σx pair or the lidar pair (must split).

A **false quotient** is two designs placed in one class whose outcomes differ beyond their determinism class.
When one is found (by the replay audit, a shadow recompute or a user report) the offending mechanism stops
serving that kind of class at once; one repair cycle is allowed; a false quotient that survives it fires the kill
gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Orbit rows exact: 86,185 representatives for ten types on six slots under the dihedral group of order 12,
      equal to the cycle-index count; 500,500 under the sagittal mirror alone (KA-365, KA-366).
- [ ] Ballast row exact: 24,546 mission-digest classes from 735,471 placements (KA-367).
- [ ] Lidar control caught: with a front-only sensor mounted, quotienting under the mechanical group refuses
      `FROB-REF-161`; quotienting under Γ_obs returns the mirror-only count.
- [ ] Equal-Σx control caught: the two placements with Σx = 5 and Σx² = 17 against 13 are in different classes.
- [ ] The merge-and-split fixture of the cascade's first stage passes: the mirror pair merges, the lidar pair and
      the equal-Σx pair split.
- [ ] A sweep run through a `QuotientPlan` returns, for every input design, a scorecard keyed by its own
      `DesignId`, with provenance for every expanded result; the dispatched count equals the plan's.
- [ ] The saved-dispatch report gives counts per mechanism and never a product of factors.
- [ ] With `mission-digest` off (the fallback configuration), orbit representatives still work and no digest
      class is reported.
- [ ] The verb is reachable through `frob phylum quotient --json` with exactly one envelope per call, paginated
      output for large sweeps, `CONTRACT.md` entries, and cancellation tested on a 10⁶-labeling sweep.
- [ ] GATE-PHYLUM-01's replay clause (zero false quotients in 10⁴ sampled class pairs) is measured and recorded by
      N17a-28, and KILL-QUOTIENT is decided by N17a-29, before this capability is reported as implemented.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `pipeline_order_and_counts`: a set of 40 designs containing 5 unit-changed duplicates, 10 mirror images and 8
  digest duplicates dispatches 17 and reports 5, 10 and 8 by mechanism.
- `assignment_map_total`: every input design appears exactly once in the map; representatives are `Runs`.
- `expansion_provenance`: each expanded scorecard names its representative and mechanism; `DesignId` keys are the
  members' own.
- `group_refusal_propagates`: an explicit mechanical group with a breaker returns `FROB-REF-161` from the verb.
- `fallback_mode`: flag off → no `DigestMember` assignments; counts by orbit unchanged.
- `merge_and_split_fixture`: mirror pair merged; lidar pair and equal-Σx pair split.
- `pagination_stable`: paging through 86,185 representatives yields each once, in lexicographic order, at a
  committed frontier.
- `no_factor_product`: the report type has no field holding a product of reductions.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_quotient.sh` (owned by bead N17a-27) drives the verb on the hexapod sweep, the ballast set,
the lidar and mirror fixtures and a small sweep run through a `QuotientPlan`. This bead contributes the steps
`quotient.plan`, `quotient.expand` and `quotient.merge_split`; each logs input designs, dispatched designs and
savings by mechanism as `metric` events, the known-answer assertions with their KA ids, refusals with exit codes,
and the envelope's evidence block.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-365: 86,185 canonical representatives, reduction 11.6, enumerated count equal to the cycle-index count;
control: counting under the rotation subgroup only (166,870). KA-366: 500,500, reduction 2.0; control: quotienting
under the mechanical group when a front-only sensor is mounted, which must refuse. KA-367: 735,471 placements,
24,546 classes, reduction 30.0; control: two placements with equal Σx and different Σx² merged. KA-380 (zero false
quotients in 10⁴ replayed in-class pairs) is measured by N17a-28. Counterexamples CX-153, CX-154, CX-156, CX-157.
Metamorphic relations MR-04 (orbit representatives) and MR-05 (equal projections replay bit-identically).
Determinism lanes: the plan is byte-identical twice, across worker counts, in debug and release, on Linux and
macOS (D0); expanded results inherit the class of their source.

## Considerations

- Kill gate, named in advance: KILL-QUOTIENT, decided at M3. Triggers: one false quotient survives a repair
  cycle, or digest classes save less than 5% of dispatches on the campaign corpus. Fallback: orbit
  representatives only; the digest is kept as a cache key with no claim. The bet's own falsifiers add: a planted
  winner losing rank 1, or fewer than six of the top eight agreeing with the unquotiented arm.
- The invalidation contract for reuse after an edit, the third mechanism of the same section, is its own seed
  bead and depends on this one; this bead must expose the digest and the orbit map it needs.
- The morphology generator's splits depend on the orbit keys computed here; family search in mission queries
  reuses the quotient for its cuts.
- Savings are reported in the exact-reuse column of the reuse ledger and are never added to evidence carried
  forward or to training avoided.
- Risk: a sensor or task term added later shrinks Γ_obs and invalidates earlier orbit classes; classes are
  therefore keyed by the group identity and recomputed when it changes.
