## Background

Level P-C of batch variation is the design family: as model variants (different trees inside the padded
capacities of one shape class), plus grouping by shared structure and current contact mode. A **family lane**
groups worlds that share more than a shape class: the same tree, the same sensor graph and, for a stretch of time,
the same active contact mode. Inside a lane three things become shared constants instead of per-world data:
structure tables and motion subspaces; the constraint-row layout, so the contact-compaction, row-construction and
solve stages need no compaction and no masks; and the sparsity of the factor. Continuous parameters stay in
per-slot columns. The general masked lane, which pads and masks every world to the class capacities, is the
baseline and the fallback; a lane is an optimization of it and never a different simulator.

The governing decision is ADR-PHYLUM-06: exact sharing only. A quantity is shared between designs when all of its
inputs and rounding rules agree; a nearby trajectory is never reused, because a smooth distance in design space
gives no right to reuse contact history. Reuse with an error certificate over contact is a research problem of
the mission-query subsystem and is out of scope here. The rejected alternative is state-dependent sharing between
similar designs on the throughput tiers.

The ceiling is stated before any speed test. If a fraction f of matched wall time is improved by a factor r and
the method adds a relative overhead h (guard tests, regrouping, extra launches), then under a serial cost model
speedup ≤ 1/(1 − f + f/r + h), where "matched" means the same completed work on the strongest baseline's critical
path, a baseline that also caches graphs, factors and compiled kernels. Three consequences: improving 85% of the
work without limit cannot reach ten (the bound is 6.7); with f = 0.97, r = 40 and h = 0.02 the bound is about
13.5; and a pruning ratio is never multiplied by a speedup that was measured on work the pruning already removed.
For the lane itself, FACT: constraint assembly is about 15% of an incumbent's humanoid step (38 of 262 ns per
world-step in a published trace). HYPOTHESIS: f is 0.15 to 0.35 on our step, r at most 4 and h about 0.02, so the
ceiling is 1.10 to 1.32. The lane is a modest optimization, funded only if its pilot (N17a-11) does not predict a
bound below 1.15, and removed by KILL-FAMILYLANE if the measured gain is below 1.15.

This seed bead's done-condition: measured f, r and h are recorded on the strong baseline before any ratio is
quoted; KILL-FAMILYLANE is decided (by N17a-14 on N17a-13's measurements; this bead delivers what they read). It
serves the bet on mixed-topology batches and the family batches that
mission queries over design families run on the GPU, which is why it follows the ceiling-before-speed check
(BD-354) and the family batch itself (BD-355).

## Scope

In: lane formation from a cohort (grouping keys, lane tables, fixed capacities); the shared constants and the
static row layout handed to the kernels; the lane execution option and its labels; the bit-equality requirement
against the general masked lane; the instrumentation that yields f, r and h; the registration of the speed
comparison with its ceiling. Out: segmentation at guards and regrouping at control-interval boundaries
(N17a-12); the measurement campaign and its end-to-end script (N17a-13); the kill decision (N17a-14); row-class
solve queues (owned by the dynamics chapter); the pilot (N17a-11).

## Technical approach

Lane key (DESIGN): `(StructureHash, SensorLayoutId, ModeSignature)`. `StructureHash` is the hash of the tree in
canonical link order (as in shape-class fitting). `ModeSignature` is the set of active constraint rows of a
world after contact compaction, in two parts. The contact part is the one published contact-set signature of the
step (`ContactSetSig.set`, bead N16ax-01): active contacts identified by their order-independent 32-bit
`ContactKey` (kind, robot-side sample or primitive, counterpart, manifold point), never by candidate index,
which changes when the narrow phase reorders its list. Because a lane's correctness depends on equality (a world
on a layout built for another mode is wrong), equal `set` values are confirmed with the exact form
`active_keys(world)`, the sorted key column compared element-wise, before a world joins or stays in a lane. The
non-contact part, which this bead adds to the contact signature, is the sorted list of active limit rows, loop
and rolling closure rows, and active stiction or backlash rows. Stick and slip inside the friction cone are
resolved by the convex solve and are not part of the lane key (`ContactSetSig.mode` is not read), because they
change no row layout.

Lane tables (DESIGN):

```rust
pub struct Lane { key: LaneKey, capacity: u32, members: IndexList /* world indices, ascending */,
                  rows: StaticRowLayout /* active rows in ascending candidate order */,
                  factor_pattern: PatternId, shared: SharedConstants }
pub struct SharedConstants { structure: TableRef, motion_subspaces: TableRef, sub_assemblies: Vec<TableRef>,
                             actuator_cards: Vec<TableRef>, collision_geometry: Vec<TableRef> }
pub struct LanePlan { lanes: Vec<Lane> /* at most the generation's lane count */, general: IndexList }
```

1. **Formation.** At environment creation and at each regrouping boundary, group the cohort's real worlds by lane
   key with a deterministic count, exclusive scan and write in ascending world order. Keep the most populated
   groups, up to the generation's fixed lane count (DESIGN default 8 per cohort) and each lane's fixed capacity
   (a multiple of the run length); every other world, and every overflow, goes to the general masked lane. Fixed
   lane count and capacities mean shapes and the launch graph never change.
2. **Shared constants.** Constants shared by a lane's members are stored once and referenced by index: structure
   tables and motion subspaces, tables of unchanged rigid sub-assemblies, actuator cards, collision geometry.
   Sharing is by content hash of design constants only. Continuous parameters occupy per-slot columns. Level P-A
   sharing of structure tables is kept even if lanes are killed.
3. **Static row layout.** For a lane, the row list is the signature's rows in the same ascending order that
   compaction would produce. The contact and solve stages are launched over the lane's index list with that
   layout in the step-plan header; they read and write each world's rows in place. No state moves, and no new
   arithmetic is introduced: the lane variants are the same derived kernels without the compaction pass and
   without mask selects.
4. **Bit equality (requirement).** A world's digests with lanes on equal its digests on the general masked lane,
   bit for bit (INV-KIN-04, INV-PHYLUM-02). The argument: the masked path's inactive rows are identity rows with
   zero right-hand side that follow every active row; the leading block of a padded factor equals the unpadded
   factor (PO-KIN-02); per-world reductions are anchored pairwise trees whose shape does not depend on a bound
   (PO-KIN-03); the lane keeps the operation order on the active rows. Where a shared factor pattern would skip a
   structural zero whose omission could change a signed zero, the term is kept. The digest test decides; a
   mismatch is a defect of the lane, never a new tolerance.
5. **Execution option.** Lanes are an execution option of the step plan: the input `--family-lanes` of `sim.run`
   (its row in VERBS-sim) and of `phylum.sweep` (VERBS-phylum), behind the crate flag `family-lanes`. Its
   identity is `exec_options_digest`, recorded in the receipt outside `StepPlanId` (N16a-03 item 3), not
   `StepPlanId`: lanes change no digest (step 4), so a run with lanes and the same run without them share their
   `StepPlanId` and differ in `exec_options_digest`. The input is refused with `FROB-REF-724` (exit 5) while the
   flag `family-lanes` is off in the build or while KILL-FAMILYLANE's decision in force is `fired`. Until the
   digest comparison has passed on a back end, a run with lanes carries the label `experimental-execution` and is
   refused as promotion evidence. The placement receipt records whether lanes ran, how many, their occupancy and
   the share of world-steps in the general lane.
6. **Instrumentation.** Per-stage timers and counters produce, on the strong baseline (general masked lane, warm
   caches, captured launch graphs): f, the share of whole-step matched wall time in the improved stages; r, the
   factor by which those stages speed up inside lanes; h, the added relative overhead of guard tests, regrouping
   and extra launches. The three are recorded in a receipt before any ratio is computed or printed.
7. **Registered comparison.** The speed comparison "lanes against the general masked lane on the mixed workload"
   is registered with the ceiling-before-speed check of N36-05. This bead builds the ceiling record
   `frob.perf.ceiling/1`: experiment `family-lanes-vs-general-masked-lane`, goal 1.15, baseline the warm profile
   of the general masked lane on the mixed workload with its hardware fingerprint, the current profile's f, r and
   h, and the ceiling 1/(1 − f + f/r + h) from N36-05's `ceiling(f, r, h)`. The record is filed in the ledger
   before the comparison's first run, and the comparison calls `admit_experiment(record)`, the only raiser of
   `FROB-REF-866`: a ceiling below the goal, or a lane ratio requested with no filed record, is refused there with
   the ranked repairs of its N35-REF row. This bead neither compares the ceiling with the goal itself nor writes a
   list of repairs.

Determinism class: D2 per back end, unchanged from the masked lane. Evidence: results are `provisional` fast-tier
results exactly as without lanes; a lane changes no claim.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Lane formation is a pure function of the cohort and the signatures: identical across runs, worker counts
      and platforms; lane count and capacities never exceed the generation's constants.
- [ ] On the mixed workload, every world's digest chain with lanes on equals its chain on the general masked lane,
      on `batch-cpu` and on each admitted device family (zero mismatches over 1,000 episodes).
- [ ] No kernel compile and no launch-graph capture occurs when lanes re-form (counters are zero).
- [ ] f, r and h are recorded on the strong baseline in a receipt whose timestamp precedes every receipt that
      quotes a lane ratio; a ratio printed without that receipt is refused.
- [ ] The comparison files its ceiling record `frob.perf.ceiling/1` before its first run (ledger order) and is
      admitted by N36-05's `admit_experiment`; with a profile whose ceiling is below 1.15 that call refuses
      `FROB-REF-866` and nothing runs.
- [ ] With lanes off, behaviour and digests are those of the general masked lane (the fallback is reachable by a
      flag at any time).
- [ ] Runs with lanes carry `experimental-execution` until the digest comparison is recorded for the back end.
- [ ] The fallback KILL-FAMILYLANE names (the general masked lane only, with shared structure tables kept) is
      the build's behaviour with the flag off, and the capability's status row names the gate as open; the
      decision is N17a-14's, on N17a-13's measurements, both after this bead, and if it fires the capability is
      described by that fallback's name.
- [ ] Cancellation during a lane step drains and finalizes with no leaked device resource.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `lane_formation_pure` (property): random cohorts and signatures; shuffled input gives identical lane plans.
- `lane_capacity_overflow`: 100 worlds of one key into a lane of capacity 64 → 64 members, 36 in the general lane.
- `static_layout_order`: the lane's row list equals the compaction output order for the same signature.
- `lane_key_candidate_order_free`: reversing a world's narrow-phase candidate list (the conformance hook of the
  contact signature) leaves its lane key and lane unchanged; flipping one contact from stick to slip leaves them
  unchanged; adding one active contact or one active limit row moves the world out of the lane.
- `lane_key_exact_confirm` (`synthetic`): two worlds whose `ContactSetSig.set` values are forced equal while their
  sorted key columns differ are not placed in one lane.
- `lanes_equal_masked_small`: a 4-design cohort on `batch-cpu`, 200 steps: digests identical with lanes on and off;
  the two runs' `StepPlanId` values are equal and their `exec_options_digest` values differ.
- `family_lanes_refused`: with the flag `family-lanes` off, and with a `synthetic` KILL-FAMILYLANE decision record
  whose outcome is `fired` placed in force, `sim.run --family-lanes` and `phylum.sweep --family-lanes` each answer
  `FROB-REF-724`, exit 5.
- `signed_zero_skip_guard`: a factor pattern whose skipped term would turn −0.0 into +0.0 keeps the term; the
  digest equals the dense path.
- `shared_constants_by_hash`: two designs with equal sub-assembly hashes reference one table; a one-bit parameter
  change gives two.
- `fr_h_receipt_order`: a ratio request before the f, r, h receipt and the ceiling record exist is refused by
  `admit_experiment` with `FROB-REF-866` (pointer `ceiling`).
- `ceiling_refusal_866`: a record with goal 1.15 and a profile giving ceiling 1.10 is filed and refused by
  `admit_experiment` with `FROB-REF-866` (pointer `goal`) and the repairs read from the N35-REF row; with ceiling
  1.20 it is admitted; a build that compares ceiling and goal in this crate is caught because no ceiling record
  is filed.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_family_lanes.sh` (owned by bead N17a-13) runs the mixed workload with lanes off and on
through the real `frob` binary and asserts digest equality and the recorded f, r, h. This bead contributes the
steps `lanes.form`, `lanes.digest_equal` and `lanes.ceiling`: lane count, occupancy and general-lane share as
`metric` events, digests as `assert` events with the first differing world, interval and stage on failure, the
ceiling against its goal, and the envelope's evidence block with the `experimental-execution` label.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-373: speedup ≤ 1/(1 − f + f/r + h): 6.7 for f = 0.85 improved without limit; about 13.5 for f = 0.97, r = 40,
h = 0.02; 1.10 to 1.32 for the family lane at f = 0.15 to 0.35, r = 4, h = 0.02. Counterexample CX-163 (a pruning
ratio multiplied by a speedup measured on work the pruning already removed). Negative controls: (1) a lane kernel
that sizes a reduction tree by the lane's row count in place of anchoring it: a digest changes and is caught;
(2) a lane whose layout orders rows by lane-local index: caught by the layout-order test and by digests; (3) a
lane sharing a state-dependent quantity between two designs: caught by digests; (4) a ratio quoted on a cold
baseline or one without captured graphs: refused by the disclosure block. Metamorphic relations: MR-01 and MR-02
with lanes on; lane membership permuted leaves results unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across
worker counts; debug and release; per back end against D2.

## Considerations

- Kill gate, named in advance: KILL-FAMILYLANE fires if the measured whole-step gain is below 1.15 on the mixed
  workload, or if h ≥ f·(1 − 1/r) (the bound is then at most 1). Decided at M4. Fallback: the general masked lane
  only, with P-A sharing of structure tables kept.
- The substrate depends on an earlier decision. KILL-PHYLUM is decided at M2 (bead N17b-10, a dependency): if it
  did not fire, lanes are formed inside mixed cohorts as described here; if it fired, per-family templates are
  the engine, lanes lose their model-variant substrate and reduce to lanes inside one template, and this bead's
  kill gate is read on that basis. The lane mechanism is built against whichever engine that record names.
- Lanes and row-class solve queues both remove masked work from the same stages, so their gains do not multiply.
  The profile measures each alone and both together, and the simpler configuration that reaches the larger
  measured gain is kept.
- Lanes help only while worlds stay in one mode. A trotting quadruped changes its active contact set twice per
  stride; lanes whose membership churns every interval pay h and gain little. The eligibility share from the
  pilot bounds what is achievable.
- Memory: lane index lists and static layouts are small (bytes per world); shared constants reduce table bytes.
- On Metal every dispatch of a chunk is encoded serially into one command buffer and the cost of an added dispatch
  is unmeasured; admission of lanes is per back end.
- What would make us revisit: a tree-sparse factor or queues that already remove most masked work shrink f for
  lanes toward zero; then the kill gate fires by its second trigger.
