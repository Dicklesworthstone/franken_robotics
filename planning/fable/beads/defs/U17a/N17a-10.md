## Background

The one property that mixed-topology batching can claim over the published incumbent mixed batch is that a world
inside the batch is the same world outside it: INV-PHYLUM-02, a world of a mixed cohort has the same digests as
the same world in a homogeneous batch of its own model. It rests on INV-KIN-04 (a world's digests depend on its
address and identities only: not on its slot, its co-tenants, the capacity of its buffers or the world count;
determinism class D2) and on exact padding (PO-KIN-02: the leading block of a padded Cholesky factor equals the
unpadded factor; PO-KIN-03: a power-of-two-aligned pairwise sum with trailing −0.0 padding equals the sum over the
real prefix for every capacity). FACT: one incumbent that documents bitwise replay of a batch holds a world equal
to its isolated run only to 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻⁴ in its own tests; the property is therefore worth having only if it is
exact. Replay of one world on `batch-cpu`, forks, slot dispatch, paired statistics and the memo graph all rest on
it, and BET-18 is falsified if any of 512 designs of a mixed cohort fails to reproduce the digests of its
single-design run.

`frob-phylum` is a crate of its own because this statement is tested at its boundary. This bead is that test and
the measurement of its gate. GATE-PHYLUM-02 (claim: Known answer, determinism; milestone M2; TARGET): each of 512
designs in a mixed cohort has the same digests as in a homogeneous batch, on `batch-cpu` and per GPU back end, at
three bucket sizes and under slot dispatch with 100 random co-tenancies. The batch-independence fixture is shared
with the gates of the dynamics, device, telemetry, fork and daemon chapters: the fixture is one, it is run once
per milestone, and each gate is compared with the threshold in its own row. This bead records the Phylum row.

## Scope

In: the crate-boundary test in `crates/frob-phylum/tests/`; the gate run at full size; the end-to-end script; the
registered negative controls; the gate receipt. Out: the kernel-level composition-invariance test and its gates
(BD-181); the tables and masks themselves (BD-170); device back ends (BD-183); the digest kernels and replay
(BD-203); first-divergence search (BD-206), which this bead only calls on failure.

## Technical approach

Design set. 512 designs spanning at least three shape classes: the first archetypes (dog-like quadruped, humanoid,
differential drive, mecanum base) and designs produced from them by the frozen corpus of structural edits (limb
added or removed, mounts moved, joint classes changed, wheels with rim samples beside point feet), plus parameter
variants. The set is frozen by content hash before the run; a relabel or regeneration changes its identity.
Scripted members are labelled `synthetic`.

Configurations, for every design d with its W = 16 world addresses and a horizon of 1,000 world-steps (step 2 ms,
decimation 10, 4 warm-started Newton iterations, contact law and profile as the step plan states):

1. **Homogeneous reference.** d alone in a batch of its own model, in its smallest fitting class.
2. **Mixed cohort.** All 512 designs under the bucket plan of the planner.
3. **Three capacities.** The same worlds with every cohort's capacities raised to the next two admitted sizes
   (degrees of freedom, and contact capacity at 32, 64 and 128), so that the padding differs and the worlds do not.
4. **Co-tenancy.** 100 seeded random co-tenancies under slot-level admission: random other jobs, random slot
   permutations, random arrival orders, random reserve occupancy.
5. **Back ends.** `batch-cpu` in single precision under the lockstep profile, `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal`. Each back
   end is compared with its own homogeneous reference (class D2). Equality across back ends is asserted only where
   the device family has passed its bit-equality gate (class D1); otherwise the cross-back-end comparison is
   reported as a tolerance comparison and is not part of this gate.

Comparison. For every world, the digest chain (the in-kernel digest at each commit fence, and the BLAKE3 digest of
the committed snapshot at the end of every control interval) of configurations 2, 3 and 4 equals that of
configuration 1, bit for bit. The measured value of the gate is the number of worlds with any mismatch, and its
threshold is zero. Results are joined by `(WorldAddr, ModelId)`; slot and cohort never appear (INV-PHYLUM-01).

On a mismatch the run does not stop at "failed": it calls the first-divergence search over the digest chain to
name the first control interval, then the per-stage digests of that interval to name the stage, and writes the
two snapshots, so that the world can be replayed alone with `frob sim replay --world <addr> --tier batch-cpu`.

Crate-boundary test (runs in the crate's own test target on `batch-cpu`, small enough for every change): 32
designs × 16 worlds, 200 steps, two capacities, 10 co-tenancies, the same comparison and the same controls.

Recording. The gate is registered in `registries/gates.toml` with its failing controls before the passing run is
written. The run produces a hash-named receipt naming the commit, the hardware fingerprint, the seed, the design
set identity, the measured mismatch count per configuration and back end, and the outcome of every control. The
recording session is not the session that implemented the mechanisms.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-PHYLUM-02 measured: zero of 512 × 16 worlds differ from their homogeneous digests in the mixed cohort,
      at three capacities, and in each of 100 co-tenancies, on `batch-cpu` and on each available GPU back end; the
      receipt carries the counts, not the target.
- [ ] A back end that is not available is recorded `not run` with the reason, never as passed (the no-GPU variant
      of the milestone records the device rows that way).
- [ ] Every registered negative control below is caught: the run with the planted defect reports at least one
      mismatch and localizes it to the planted stage.
- [ ] INV-PHYLUM-01 holds in the same run: envelopes compare equal after sorting by address under slot and
      co-tenant permutation (KA-376).
- [ ] The crate-boundary test passes under `cargo test -p frob-phylum --test batch_independence` in debug and
      release, twice, and across worker counts 1, 8 and 24 on `batch-cpu`.
- [ ] On a forced mismatch the script emits the first differing world address, control interval and stage, and the
      two snapshot paths; replaying that world alone reproduces the homogeneous digest.
- [ ] The gate row in `registries/gates.toml` cites the receipt; the honest-status row for mixed cohorts reads
      "partial" until this and the performance gates of the head-to-head are recorded.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `batch_independence_small` (the crate-boundary test above): asserts zero mismatches over 32 × 16 worlds.
- `capacity_invariance_reduction`: a per-world sum over a padded axis at capacities 32, 64 and 128 with −0.0
  padding returns identical bits; with +0.0 padding and a real prefix summing to −0.0 the bits differ (the
  control).
- `padded_factor_leading_block`: the Cholesky factor of a matrix padded with identity rows has a leading block
  bit-equal to the unpadded factor (PO-KIN-02).
- `cotenancy_seeds_reproducible`: the 100 co-tenancy descriptors are a pure function of the run seed.
- `join_by_address_only`: the comparison refuses to join on slot index (a type-level check: the join key type has
  no slot field).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_cohort_invariance.sh`, plain shell driving the real `frob` binary (and `frobd` for the
co-tenancy step): `frob phylum plan --designs @fixture:gate-phylum-02 --worlds-per-design 16 --json`; `frob sim
run --design <d> --worlds 16 --tier <t> --steps 1000 --seed 4242 --json` per design for the references; `frob
phylum sweep --designs @fixture:gate-phylum-02 --worlds-per-design 16 --tier <t> --seed 4242 --json` for the mixed
cohort, repeated with `--capacity-step 1` and `2` (a DESIGN test option that raises every cohort's capacities by
one or two admitted sizes); the same sweep submitted to `frobd` beside seeded co-tenant jobs 100 times; `frob
telemetry divergence` and `frob sim replay --world <addr> --tier batch-cpu` on failure. The script is idempotent
and seed-explicit and exits non-zero on any mismatch or any control that passes.

It sources the `STD-E2E` helper and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_cohort_invariance/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line,
exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim Known answer, class `Tested`, tier, determinism D2,
`ModelId` and `StepPlanId`, seed), the digests compared, the mismatch count against its threshold of zero, and on
failure the first differing value: world address, control interval, stage, both digests and the snapshot paths.
The final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed, mismatches per configuration and back end, controls
caught, and the gate touched (GATE-PHYLUM-02). Designs of the fixture are public archetypes and scripted edits, so
the log contains no private-scene data.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-375: identical digests per world for each of 512 designs, mixed against homogeneous, at three bucket sizes and
under 100 random co-tenancies, on `batch-cpu` and per GPU back end (exact; tier G5). KA-376: results invariant
under slot and co-tenant permutation. Negative controls (each registered before the passing run, each must be
caught): (1) a reduction tree shaped by capacity: in single precision a = 10⁸, b = −10⁸, c = 1 give
(a + b) + c = 1 and a + (b + c) = 0, because the spacing near 10⁸ is 8; (2) +0.0 padding of a summed quantity;
(3) a batch-wide observation normalizer; (4) a variant row changed mid-step; (5) a parameter row read after its
first use in the step; (6) a padded contact left active; (7) a bucket overflow that truncates silently instead of
flagging `Degraded`. Metamorphic relations: MR-01 (batch-composition invariance over batch, slot, co-tenancy,
bucket capacity and shard count) and MR-02 (a world of a mixed cohort equals the same world in a homogeneous
batch). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; per back end against
class D2; across back ends against D1 only where licensed.

## Considerations

- Compute: 8,192 worlds × 1,000 steps is 8.2 M world-steps per configuration; with 512 references, three
  capacities and 100 co-tenancies the run is roughly 10⁹ world-steps per back end. On `batch-cpu` this exceeds one
  hour on eight cores, so the full run needs the named-gate compute class (both machines, all cores, two hours per
  run) once the owner ratifies the envelope; under the default envelope the co-tenancy step uses a seeded
  subsample of 64 designs and the receipt says so.
- Capacity overflow is not a mismatch to hide: a world marked `Degraded` in one configuration and not in another
  is reported as a capacity finding with its reason (the label is part of the result).
- If no device family holds batch-composition invariance, the milestone falls back to its no-GPU variant and
  mixed cohorts are claimed on `batch-cpu` only.
- KILL-PHYLUM does not depend on this gate's performance, but a mismatch that survives one repair cycle falsifies
  BET-18 outright and makes the per-family-template fallback the engine.
- A world's `StepPlanId` differs between capacities by construction (the shape class is part of it); only state
  digests are compared.
