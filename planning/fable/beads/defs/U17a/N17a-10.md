## Background

The one property that mixed-topology batching can claim over the published incumbent mixed batch is that a world
inside the batch is the same world outside it: INV-PHYLUM-02, a world of a mixed cohort has the same digests as
the same world in a homogeneous batch of its own model. It rests on INV-KIN-04 (a world's digests depend on its
address and identities only: not on its slot, its co-tenants, the capacity of its buffers or the world count;
determinism class D2) and on exact padding (PO-KIN-02: the leading block of a padded Cholesky factor equals the
unpadded factor; PO-KIN-03: a power-of-two-aligned pairwise sum with trailing −0.0 padding equals the sum over the
real prefix for every capacity). FACT (about incumbents; to be re-read): the one incumbent solver that publishes a
mixed batch is documented as not deterministic, and an incumbent that documents bitwise replay of a batch holds a
world equal to its isolated run only to 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻⁴ in its own tests; the property is therefore worth claiming
only if it is exact, and only after its gate. Replay of one world on `batch-cpu`, forks, slot dispatch, paired
statistics and the memo graph all rest on it, and BET-18 is falsified if any of 512 designs of a mixed cohort
fails to reproduce the digests of its single-design run.

`frob-phylum` is a crate of its own because this statement is tested at its boundary. This bead is that test and
the measurement of its gate. GATE-PHYLUM-02 (claim: Known answer, determinism; milestone M2; TARGET): each of 512
designs in a mixed cohort has the same digests as in a homogeneous batch, on `batch-cpu` and per GPU back end, at
three bucket sizes and under slot dispatch with 100 random co-tenancies. It is the gate of INV-PHYLUM-02 and of
INV-PHYLUM-01 (every result of a cohort is keyed by world address and `ModelId`; slot, cohort and arrival order
appear in no result). The CPU rows are first produced by BD-170 on a reduced run and the device mechanisms by
BD-183; nothing before this bead runs the whole gate as stated: all 512 designs, every back end, three
capacities, 100 co-tenancies. The batch-independence fixture is shared with the gates of the dynamics, device,
telemetry, fork and daemon chapters: the fixture is one, it is run once per milestone, and each gate is compared
with the threshold in its own row. This bead records the Phylum row.

Terms. A *digest chain* is the sequence of per-step state digests of one world; device digests are a tripwire and
the identity of record is a BLAKE3 hash over committed snapshots on the host. *Co-tenancy* means other jobs'
worlds share the persistent environment. Determinism class D2 means the same bits run to run and across batch
size, slot, co-tenant worlds, bucket capacity and shard count on one device family.

This bead is the single carrier of the measurement. Bead N17b-06 specified the same run and is superseded by this
one; its protocol details, tests, controls and considerations are folded in here. Where the two specifications
differed (horizon, the reading of "three bucket sizes", script and test-target names), one value is fixed below
and the other is named beside it.

## Scope

In: the crate-boundary test in `crates/frob-phylum/tests/`; the gate run at full size on `batch-cpu`, `gpu-cuda`
and `gpu-metal`; the comparison by world address; first-divergence localization on failure; the end-to-end
script; the registered negative controls; the gate record and its receipt. Out: the kernel-level
composition-invariance test and its gates (BD-181); the tables and masks themselves and the reduced CPU rows
(BD-170, N17b-02); cohort formation (N17a-03) and slot-level admission (N17a-09, N17b-05), which are exercised
here, not built; device back ends (BD-183); the digest kernels and replay (BD-203); the first-divergence search
(BD-206), which this bead only calls on failure; fixing a divergence (it goes to the bead of the kernel named by
the localization); bit equality between a device and the CPU (a separate determinism class and gate); throughput
(BD-186); the daemon's own re-run of this fixture for its dispatch gate (BD-295, one milestone later).

## Technical approach

Design set. The 512 designs of the frozen head-to-head workload (bead N17b-03; identity `WorkloadId`, alias
`@workload:h2h-512`): the first archetypes (dog-like quadruped, humanoid, differential drive, mecanum base) and
designs produced from them by the frozen corpus of structural edits (limb added or removed, mounts moved, joint
classes changed, wheels with rim samples beside point feet), plus parameter variants. Before the run the script
checks that the set spans at least three shape classes. The set is frozen by content hash; a relabel or
regeneration changes its identity. Scripted members are labelled `synthetic`. The fixture name
`@fixture:gate-phylum-02` is an alias of the workload and resolves to the same identity.

Configurations, per back end, for every design d with its W = 16 world addresses and a horizon of 1,000
world-steps (step 2 ms, decimation 10, 4 warm-started Newton iterations, flat-ground task, contact law and
profile as the step plan states; a 500-step horizon with snapshots at steps 100, 250 and 500 was the
alternative: the longer horizon is fixed, and its snapshot comparison at every control interval contains those
three):

1. **Homogeneous reference.** d alone in a batch of its own model, in its smallest fitting class. On devices the
   homogeneous runs are batched by design: one environment per design, shape class equal to the design's own
   unpadded size rounded to its native bucket. Recorded per world: the digest chain and the host BLAKE3 of the
   committed snapshot at the end of every control interval.
2. **Mixed cohort.** All 512 designs (the same addresses) under the bucket plan of the planner, each design in
   its native bucket.
3. **Three capacities.** The same worlds again with each design in the next larger bucket, and in the largest
   bucket that admits it, so that the padding differs and the worlds do not. A design already in the largest
   bucket (or with only one larger bucket) is instead run at raised buffer capacities of its bucket's contact and
   row axes, contact capacity at 32, 64 and 128, until three sizes exist for it. This is the reading shared with
   BD-170's CPU rows.
4. **Slot dispatch, 100 co-tenancies.** The mixed population 100 times under slot-level admission, each time
   with: a random slot permutation; a random set of co-tenant jobs (other designs' episodes, different tasks)
   filling between 10% and 90% of the slots; random arrival orders; random admission ticks for a tenth of the
   designs; random reserve occupancy; one and two shards on the two-card machine. All randomness of the harness
   is a pure function of one run seed and is logged.
5. **Back ends.** `batch-cpu` in single precision under the lockstep profile, `gpu-cuda` (each card) and
   `gpu-metal`. Each back end is compared with its own homogeneous reference (class D2). Equality across back
   ends is asserted only where the device family has passed its bit-equality gate (class D1); otherwise the
   cross-back-end comparison is reported as a tolerance comparison and is not part of this gate.

Comparison. For every world address and `ModelId`, the digest chain (the in-kernel digest at each commit fence,
and the BLAKE3 digest of the committed snapshot at the end of every control interval) of configurations 2, 3 and
4 equals that of configuration 1, bit for bit. The measured value of the gate is the number of worlds with any
mismatch, and its threshold is zero. Results are joined by `(WorldAddr, ModelId)`; slot and cohort never appear,
and the result sets of two co-tenancy runs, sorted by world address, are byte-identical (INV-PHYLUM-01).

On a mismatch the run does not stop at "failed": it calls the first-divergence search over the two digest chains
to name the first control interval, then the per-stage digests of that interval to name the stage, kernel and
quantity, files them in the divergence ledger, and writes the two snapshots, so that the world can be replayed
alone with `frob sim replay --world <addr> --tier batch-cpu`. The gate is recorded FAIL with that localization.

Sizes. 512 designs × 16 worlds = 8,192 worlds; 1,000 steps; (1 + 3 + 100) passes: about 852 M world-steps per
back end (426 M at the 500-step alternative). At 2 M world-steps/s (a TARGET, not a measurement) that is about
seven minutes of device time per GPU back end.

Crate-boundary test (runs in the crate's own test target on `batch-cpu`, small enough for every change): 32
designs × 16 worlds, 200 steps, two capacities, 10 co-tenancies, the same comparison and the same controls.

What passing licenses. With this gate current on a back end, a world of a sweep may be replayed alone on
`batch-cpu` or on the same device family and compared by digest; paired comparisons on common addresses inside a
mixed cohort are `paired-exact`; forks from snapshots of a mixed cohort are deterministic.

Recording. The gate is registered in `registries/gates.toml` with its failing controls before the passing run is
written; a gate record without its caught controls is rejected by the conformance loader. One row per back end:
worlds compared, worlds differing (0 required), designs covered (512 required), capacities, co-tenancies (100
required), the hardware fingerprint, commit, seed, `WorkloadId`, and the list of negative controls caught in the
same run. The run produces a hash-named receipt carrying the same fields with the measured mismatch count per
configuration and back end and the outcome of every control. The recording session is not the session that
implemented the mechanisms.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-PHYLUM-02 measured and recorded per back end: zero of 512 × 16 worlds differ from their homogeneous
      digests in the mixed cohort, at three capacities, and in each of 100 co-tenancies, on `batch-cpu`,
      `gpu-cuda` (each card) and `gpu-metal`; the receipt carries the counts (512 designs, three capacities, 100
      co-tenancies, 0 differing worlds), not the target.
- [ ] A back end that cannot be run (device absent, or not ratified for device claims) is recorded `not run` with
      the reason, never as passed (the no-GPU variant of the milestone records the device rows that way).
- [ ] Every registered negative control below is caught in the same run that records the gate: the run with the
      planted defect reports at least one mismatch in the pass named for it and localizes it to the planted stage.
- [ ] INV-PHYLUM-01 holds in the same run: result sets are byte-identical after sorting by address across
      co-tenancy runs and under slot and co-tenant permutation (KA-376).
- [ ] The crate-boundary test passes under `cargo test -p frob-phylum --test batch_independence` in debug and
      release, twice, and across worker counts 1, 8 and 24 on `batch-cpu`.
- [ ] On a forced mismatch the script emits the first differing world address, control interval and stage, and the
      two snapshot paths; a divergence-ledger entry names step, stage, kernel and quantity; replaying that world
      alone reproduces the homogeneous digest.
- [ ] The receipt names commit, hardware fingerprint, seed and `WorkloadId`; the gate row in
      `registries/gates.toml` cites the receipt; the honest-status row for mixed cohorts reads "partial" until
      this and the performance gates of the head-to-head are recorded.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In `crates/frob-phylum/tests/batch_independence.rs` (`cargo test -p frob-phylum --test batch_independence`); no
separate `gate_phylum_02` test target exists, and the tests N17b-06 listed under that name live here.

- `batch_independence_small` (the crate-boundary test above): asserts zero mismatches over 32 × 16 worlds.
- `small_mixed_equals_solo`: quadruped, hexapod and humanoid, 8 worlds each, 200 steps on `batch-cpu`: zero
  differing worlds (the smoke form of the gate, also reachable through the script's `--smoke` mode).
- `compare_by_address`: two result sets in different slot orders compare equal; one flipped bit in one world's
  step-17 digest is reported with that address and step.
- `join_by_address_only`: the comparison refuses to join on slot index (a type-level check: the join key type has
  no slot field).
- `capacity_plan_three_sizes`: a design of 20 degrees of freedom is assigned buckets 24, 36 and 64; one of 60 is
  assigned bucket 64 at three contact and row capacities.
- `capacity_invariance_reduction`: a per-world sum over a padded axis at capacities 32, 64 and 128 with −0.0
  padding returns identical bits; with +0.0 padding and a real prefix summing to −0.0 the bits differ (the
  control).
- `padded_factor_leading_block`: the Cholesky factor of a matrix padded with identity rows has a leading block
  bit-equal to the unpadded factor (PO-KIN-02).
- `cotenancy_seeds_reproducible` / `cotenancy_schedule_from_seed`: the 100 co-tenancy descriptors (slot
  permutations, co-tenant sets, admission ticks) are a pure function of the run seed; seed 5 yields the same 100
  on Linux and macOS.
- `gate_requires_controls`: a gate record without its caught controls is rejected by the conformance loader.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_cohort_invariance.sh` (the script N17b-06 called `scripts/e2e/gate_phylum_02.sh` is this
one; one file exists, under this name), plain shell driving the real `frob` binary (and `frobd` for the co-tenancy
step). For each back end: `frob phylum plan --designs @workload:h2h-512 --worlds-per-design 16 --json`; `frob sim
run --design <d> --worlds 16 --tier <t> --steps 1000 --seed 4242 --json` per design for the references; `frob
phylum sweep --designs @workload:h2h-512 --worlds-per-design 16 --task flat-stand --tier <t> --seed 4242 --json`
for the mixed cohort, repeated with `--capacity-step 1` and `--capacity-step max` (a DESIGN test option that
places every design in the next larger bucket, or in the largest bucket that admits it, raising contact and row
capacities where no larger bucket exists); the same sweep submitted to `frobd` beside seeded co-tenant jobs 100
times; `frob telemetry divergence` and `frob sim replay --world <addr> --tier batch-cpu` on any differing pair.
A `--smoke` mode runs `small_mixed_equals_solo` through the CLI and claims no gate. The script is idempotent and
seed-explicit and exits non-zero on any mismatch or any control that passes.

It sources the `STD-E2E` helper and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_cohort_invariance/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line,
exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim Known answer, class `Tested`, tier, determinism D2,
`ModelId` and `StepPlanId`, seed), the digests compared, and on failure the first differing value: world address,
control interval, stage, both digests and the snapshot paths. `metric` events: `worlds_compared`,
`worlds_differing` (threshold 0), `designs_covered` (512), `cotenancies` (100). `assert` events: one per back end
and per pass. The final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed, mismatches per configuration and back
end, controls caught, and `gates: ["GATE-PHYLUM-02"]`. Designs of the fixture are public archetypes and scripted
edits, so the log contains no private-scene data.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-375: identical digests per world for each of 512 designs, mixed against homogeneous, at three bucket sizes and
under 100 random co-tenancies, on `batch-cpu` and per GPU back end (exact, by digest; tier G5). KA-376: slots and
co-tenants permuted; envelopes compare equal after sorting by world address.

Negative controls (each registered before the passing run, each must be caught, each in the pass named):

1. A reduction tree shaped by capacity, planted in one kernel (CX-146): in single precision a = 10⁸, b = −10⁸,
   c = 1 give (a + b) + c = 1 and a + (b + c) = 0, because the spacing near 10⁸ is 8. The capacity pass must
   differ.
2. +0.0 padding of a summed quantity in place of −0.0: a world whose sum is −0.0 changes bits with capacity. The
   capacity pass must differ.
3. A batch-wide observation normalizer: two worlds observing 1 and 3 and normalized by their batch mean see −1
   and +1; the first alone sees 0. The co-tenancy pass must differ.
4. A variant row changed mid-step.
5. A parameter row read after its first use in the step.
6. A padded contact left active.
7. A bucket overflow that truncates silently instead of flagging `Degraded`.
8. Results placed by arrival order: the slot-permutation comparison must differ.

Metamorphic relations: MR-01 (batch-composition invariance over batch, slot, co-tenancy, bucket capacity and
shard count) and MR-02 (a world of a mixed cohort equals the same world in a homogeneous batch). Determinism
lanes: the whole run twice on one machine; across worker counts on `batch-cpu`; debug and release; per back end
against class D2; across back ends against D1 only where licensed.

## Considerations

- Compute: with 512 references, three capacities and 100 co-tenancies the run is roughly 10⁹ world-steps per back
  end. On `batch-cpu` this exceeds one hour on eight cores, so the full run needs the named-gate compute class
  (both machines, all cores, two hours per run) once the owner ratifies the envelope. Under the default envelope
  the `batch-cpu` co-tenancy pass is reduced, and the receipt says how: first, all 512 designs at 4 worlds per
  design and 200 steps (about 43 M world-steps), which keeps every design in the comparison; if that still does
  not fit, a seeded subsample of 64 designs at full size.
- One differing world fails the gate. A failure that survives a repair cycle falsifies a clause of the
  mixed-batch bet (BET-18: "any of the 512 designs of a mixed cohort fails to reproduce the digests of its
  single-design run") and removes replay outside the batch from the claims; it is reported to the milestone exit
  review and to the KILL-PHYLUM decision (N17b-10), whose record then says that replay outside the batch is not
  a kept use. It is not itself a trigger of KILL-PHYLUM, which is decided on the head-to-head's performance
  outcomes: a digest failure does not by itself make the per-family-template fallback the engine.
- A failure only under co-tenancy with shared environments points at slot dispatch; its fallback is one
  environment per job and its kill gate belongs to the daemon.
- Capacity overflow is not a mismatch to hide: a world marked `Degraded` in one configuration and not in another
  is reported as a capacity finding with its reason (the label is part of the result).
- Device claims depend on the owner's ratification of the GPU back ends; until then device rows are labelled
  exploratory. If no device family holds batch-composition invariance, the milestone falls back to its no-GPU
  variant and mixed cohorts are claimed on `batch-cpu` only.
- The gate proves equality on this workload and these seeds. It is a tested property (evidence class `Tested`),
  not a proof; the kernels' footprint manifests and the padding obligations are what make it hold by
  construction.
- A world's `StepPlanId` differs between capacities by construction (the shape class is part of it); only state
  digests are compared.
- GATE-PHYLUM-02 requires slot dispatch at M2 while the daemon's dispatcher is an M3 work item: configuration 4
  uses the environment-level slot placement available at M2 (N17a-09, N17b-05), and the daemon re-runs this
  fixture as the digest part of its own gate one milestone later.
