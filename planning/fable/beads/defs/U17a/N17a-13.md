## Background

Design-family lanes (BD-356, N17a-12) are an optimization whose ceiling was stated before it was built:
speedup ≤ 1/(1 − f + f/r + h), expected to lie between 1.10 and 1.32. The kill gate KILL-FAMILYLANE fires if the
measured whole-step gain is below 1.15 on the mixed workload, or if the overhead satisfies h ≥ f·(1 − 1/r). The
seed bead's done-condition adds an ordering rule: measured f, r and h are recorded on the strong baseline before
any ratio is quoted. This bead is the measurement and the end-to-end script of the capability as a user sees it:
the same population run with lanes off and on, the same digests, and a paired ratio with its lower bound.

The rules of measurement come from the performance program and are restated here because the result decides a
kill gate. "Matched" means the same completed work on the strongest baseline's critical path, and that baseline
also caches graphs, factors and compiled kernels. Every ratio is a paired lower bound, not a mean. Cold runs are
kept apart. Any Performance figure carries the full disclosure block; a figure without it is refused as a
Performance claim. A speed comparison is registered with its ceiling first and refused if the ceiling is below
its goal.

## Scope

In: the measurement protocol, the script `scripts/e2e/phylum_family_lanes.sh`, the receipts for f, r, h and for
the paired ratio, the comparison with row-class solve queues, and the comparison of the measured gain with the
pilot's predicted bound. Out: the mechanism (BD-356, N17a-12); the decision (N17a-14).

## Technical approach

Workload: the mixed workload of the head-to-head (512 topologies with realistic contact tails: wheels with rim
samples and tracks beside point feet), at 16 worlds per design in the planner's buckets, plus a homogeneous
stratum (one tree, 8,192 worlds) where lanes should do best. Machines: one RTX 4090; the M5 Ultra where the back
end has admitted lanes. Settings fixed and disclosed: step 2 ms, decimation 10, 4 warm-started Newton iterations,
contact law and capacities of the step plan, policy excluded.

Protocol:

1. **Register** the comparison "lanes against the general masked lane, whole step, mixed workload, goal 1.15"
   with the ceiling-before-speed check. It computes the ceiling from the current profile; below 1.15 the
   comparison refuses (`FROB-REF-866`) and the script records that outcome as the result.
2. **Baseline profile (strong baseline).** Run the general masked lane warm: kernels cached, launch graphs
   captured, factors warm-started. From per-stage timers record f, the share of whole-step matched wall time in
   compaction, row construction and masked solve work. Write receipt R1.
3. **Lane profile.** Run with lanes on. Record r, the ratio of the same stages' time per world-step for worlds in
   mode lanes, and h, the added time of guard tests, regrouping and extra launches as a share of baseline step
   time; record the eligibility share e and overflow rate. Write receipt R2. R1 and R2 exist before step 5.
4. **Digest equality.** For every world, the digest chain with lanes on equals the baseline's. Any mismatch stops
   the measurement: a speed figure for a run that changed results is not recorded.
5. **Paired whole-step ratio.** A fixed-n study: at least ten timed repetitions per cell after warm-up, baseline
   and lanes interleaved, cold runs kept apart, a thermal soak on the Mac. Compute the 95% lower bound of the
   paired ratio of whole-step time (baseline over lanes) per stratum and for the mixed workload. Write receipt R3
   with the disclosure block: hardware and driver; back end, compiler and kernel versions; world count; time step,
   substeps and decimation; solver iterations and tolerance; contact capacity and mean active contacts; sensors;
   policy excluded; scene; determinism class; warm-up and duration; launch graphs captured; median and
   99th-percentile step time; peak memory; row capacity; `Degraded` and `Faulted` counts; profile and precision
   profile; fingerprint digest; placement receipt; cold or warm; buckets, variants per bucket and padding
   fraction; thermal state; host load; number of pairs, lower bound and the comparator's configuration.
6. **Consistency.** Compare the measured ratio with the bound computed from the measured f, r, h (the measured
   ratio must not exceed the bound beyond its interval; if it does, the serial model does not describe the run and
   the critical path is measured directly) and with the pilot's predicted bound.
7. **Queues.** Where row-class solve queues are admitted on the back end, repeat step 5 for queues alone and for
   lanes with queues. Their gains do not multiply; report each alone and both together, and name the simpler
   configuration that reaches the larger measured gain.
8. **Verdict inputs.** Emit the two trigger evaluations for KILL-FAMILYLANE: lower bound of the mixed-workload
   ratio against 1.15, and h against f·(1 − 1/r).

Script: plain shell, idempotent, seed-explicit; commands `frob spark fingerprint --json`; `frob phylum sweep
--designs @fixture:mixed-512 --worlds-per-design 16 --tier gpu-cuda --seed 9001 --steps 1000 --json` with and
without `--family-lanes` (and `--solve-queues`); `frob sim run --design quad-ref --worlds 8192 ...` for the
homogeneous stratum; `frob spark place --json` for the receipts. Exits non-zero on a digest mismatch, a missing
receipt order (R3 without R1 and R2), a missing disclosure field or fewer than ten repetitions in a cell. A kill
trigger that fires is not a script failure: the script's job is to measure and record.

Logging: sources the `STD-E2E` helper; newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_family_lanes/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code,
wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim Performance, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed),
measured values with units and thresholds (f, r, h, e, the lower bound against 1.15, h against f·(1 − 1/r)),
digests compared, and on failure the first differing world address, control interval and stage with snapshot
paths. The `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed, the three receipt ids and the kill gate touched.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Receipts R1 (f on the strong baseline) and R2 (r, h, e) exist and precede R3; the script refuses to compute a
      ratio otherwise.
- [ ] Zero digest mismatches between lanes on and off across the measured runs, per back end.
- [ ] The 95% lower bound of the paired whole-step ratio is recorded per stratum and for the mixed workload with
      at least ten interleaved repetitions per cell and the complete disclosure block.
- [ ] The measured ratio, the bound from measured f, r, h and the pilot's predicted bound are recorded side by
      side; a measured ratio above the bound is flagged for a direct critical-path measurement.
- [ ] Queues alone, lanes alone and both together are reported where queues are admitted; a product of the two
      gains is never printed.
- [ ] Both kill-trigger evaluations are emitted with their inputs for bead N17a-14.
- [ ] A registered comparison whose ceiling is below 1.15 ends with the `FROB-REF-866` refusal recorded as the
      outcome and no timed run.
- [ ] The log validates against the `STD-E2E` schema and ends with a `summary` line.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `paired_lower_bound_synthetic` (`synthetic` timings): pairs with a true ratio of 1.20 and 2% noise give a lower
  bound in [1.17, 1.20]; a true ratio of 1.10 gives a lower bound below 1.15.
- `receipt_order_enforced`: R3 requested with R1 missing is refused.
- `trigger_evaluation`: lower bound 1.149 → first trigger true; 1.150 → false; h = 0.15, f = 0.2, r = 4 → second
  trigger true (0.15 ≥ 0.15); h = 0.14 → false.
- `bound_consistency_flag`: measured 1.40 against a bound of 1.30 ± 0.03 raises the critical-path flag.
- `no_product_of_gains`: the report type has no field for a product of queue and lane ratios.

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified above: `scripts/e2e/phylum_family_lanes.sh`, logs under `target/e2e-logs/phylum_family_lanes/`.
Beads BD-356 and N17a-12 contribute named steps to it (`lanes.form`, `lanes.digest_equal`, `lanes.ceiling`,
`lanes.trot`, `lanes.grazing`, `lanes.random_schedules`).

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-373 for the bound's arithmetic (1.10 to 1.32 at f = 0.15 to 0.35, r = 4, h = 0.02). Counterexample CX-163.
Negative controls, each of which must make the script fail or refuse: (1) a ratio computed against a cold
baseline or one without captured launch graphs (disclosure block); (2) a ratio computed on the lane-eligible
worlds only and presented as whole-step (the matched-work check: both arms must complete the same world-steps);
(3) a lane build that changes one digest (the equality step); (4) the multiplication error: reporting queue gain
times lane gain; (5) a timer that includes policy inference. Determinism lanes: the timed workloads record
digests, identical across repetitions per back end (D2); a repetition whose digests differ is discarded and
reported.

## Considerations

- Compute class: the repetitions on 8,192 worlds fit the default envelope on a device; the Mac's thermal soak adds
  wall time. Nothing above the default envelope runs without approval.
- The expected outcome is close to the threshold. A lower bound between 1.10 and 1.15 fires the kill gate; the
  mechanism is then removed, not tuned indefinitely (one repair cycle is the program's norm before a kill).
- If lanes are admitted on CUDA and not on Metal, the Metal rows are recorded `not run` with the reason.
- The ratio is a statement about this engine against itself. It is never quoted as a gain over an incumbent.
