## Background

A kernel specialized to one design (constants folded, loops unrolled, padding removed) may replace the
table-driven kernel for designs that survive several rounds of a race and will run for a long time. It is a
Frontier mechanism and a gated optimization: it is never the default and never part of the edit loop.

**Ceiling, stated first.** FACT (a published incumbent step trace for a humanoid at 8,192 worlds; to be re-read at
its primary source): everything a design-specialized recursion could touch (kinematics, centre-of-mass terms,
composite inertia, bias forces, forward acceleration) sums to 53 of 262 ns, 20% of the step; the solve is 124 ns
and constraint construction 38 ns. An infinitely fast specialized recursion gives 262/209 = 1.25 and one twice as
fast gives 1.11; on a manipulation scene the share is 16% and the ceiling 1.19. Generated code does not reliably
beat a good generic library on CPUs either (FACT). The expected gain is therefore 1.05 to 1.15 and the cap is near
1.2, which is also the kill threshold: the honest expectation is that this mechanism is not built.

**Gate to fund.** Specialization is built only if our own M2 profile shows a specializable share s of at least one
third of the step, so that the ceiling 1/(1 − s) is at least 1.5. **Pilot before funding**, two engineer-days:
three arms on one 12-joint quadruped at 8,192 worlds, measured on the whole step: (a) the padded table-driven
kernel, (b) the unpadded table-driven kernel, (c) a fully unrolled, constant-folded kernel written by script.

**Kill gate KILL-SPECIALIZE** (the device chapter's statement governs; this chapter is its consumer): in the pilot
(c) over (b) is below 1.2 on the whole step, or (c) takes more than 60 s to compile; or the M2 profile shows a
specializable share under one third. The profile clause can fire before any pilot is funded. Fallback: the
table-driven kernel only; derivation stays for correctness, certificates and the derivative kernels of CPU
solvers, where rigid-body derivatives dominate.

## Scope

In: the registered pilot record; reading s from the M2 profile; the three-arm harness (arms (a), (b) and (c)) and
its measurement; the digest comparison of arm (c) with arm (b); compile-time measurement; the verdict inputs for
KILL-SPECIALIZE. The harness is built once, in this bead, at M2.

Out: the fourth arm (d), the limb-archetype kernel, with its own falsifier (N17b-24, which depends on this bead
and adds its arm to this harness in the same measurement run, so that the four arms are timed interleaved and
no arm is built twice); the decision itself (the device chapter's decision bead N21c-18 for the first reading
and BD-371 for the final one) and the production specialized kernels (BD-371); the promotion mechanism
(N17a-16).

Milestone. The pilot runs at M2: it reads the M2 profile, its profile clause can close the question before any
kernel is written, and the limb-archetype level, whose gate and kill gate are at M2, is by definition a fourth
arm of this pilot at no added calendar time. The decision that funds the work package is taken at M4.

## Technical approach

1. **Register** in `frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` before measuring: mechanism (specialization of
   survivors, Frontier); protocol (this section, frozen by hash); falsifiers (s < 1/3; (c)/(b) < 1.2 on the whole
   step; compile of (c) above 60 s); computed ceiling (1.25 at s = 0.20; 1/(1 − s) in general); fallback and kill
   gate; cost (two engineer-days, under one device-hour); tool and pin (this engine at the pilot's commit).
2. **Read s.** From the per-stage timers of the cost-model calibration (N17a-06) on the reference quadruped and on
   the mixed workload: s = time in the tree recursions of stage 1 (link poses and twists) and stage 6 (mass
   matrix, bias forces, and the centre-of-mass and composite-inertia terms inside them) over whole-step time.
   Record s with its interval and 1/(1 − s). If the upper end of the interval is below 1/3, the profile clause has
   fired: record it, skip to step 7 and hand the result to the decision bead.
3. **Build the three arms** for one 12-joint quadruped (18 degrees of freedom, 13 links):
   (a) the table-driven kernels in a class padded above the robot (for example the 24 bucket), masks on;
   (b) the same kernels in a class of exactly the robot's size, the single-structure path with no padding;
   (c) kernels emitted by a script from the same derivation with this design's constants folded and the level
   loops unrolled. Unrolling keeps the operation order, and only exact constant operations are folded, so (c) is
   required to reproduce (b)'s digests bit for bit.
4. **Measure** at 8,192 worlds, warm, launch graphs captured, policy excluded, as a fixed-n study: at least ten
   timed repetitions per arm after warm-up, arms interleaved, cold runs apart, the 95% lower bound of each paired
   whole-step ratio (b)/(c) and (a)/(b), each with the full disclosure block. Measure the compile time of (c) on a
   CPU worker (cold, then cached).
5. **Compare digests** of (c) and (b) over 1,000 steps on the pinned corpus of worlds. A mismatch is recorded as a
   finding against the claim that specialization can be bit-identical, and makes arm (c) inadmissible as
   interchangeable.
6. **Consistency check**: the measured (b)/(c) must not exceed the ceiling 1/(1 − s) beyond its interval; if it
   does, s was mis-measured and step 2 is repeated.
7. **Record** the result in the pilot registry and the review log: s, the ratios with lower bounds, compile times,
   the digest result, and the evaluation of each kill clause. Hand the receipt ids to the decision bead.

Arithmetic references: 53/262 = 0.2023; 262/209 = 1.2536; a recursion twice as fast gives 262/(262 − 26.5) =
1.1125; a 16% share gives 1/0.84 = 1.19; s = 1/3 gives 1.5.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot record predates the first measurement and names the three falsifiers, the ceiling, the fallback,
      the kill gate, the cost and the pin.
- [ ] s is recorded with an interval on the reference quadruped and on the mixed workload, with 1/(1 − s).
- [ ] If s ≥ 1/3: the three arms exist, the paired lower bounds of (b)/(c) and (a)/(b) on the whole step are
      recorded with disclosure blocks, and the compile time of (c) is recorded cold and cached.
- [ ] The digest comparison of (c) against (b) is recorded: equal, or the first differing world, step and stage.
- [ ] Each clause of KILL-SPECIALIZE is evaluated to a number and a boolean and handed to the decision bead.
- [ ] The result, positive or negative, is in the review log; on a fired clause the status table names the
      fallback (the table-driven kernel only) and N17a-16 is marked not funded.
- [ ] The harness is reusable: adding a fourth arm needs a kernel set and a label, no harness change.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `ceiling_values`: s = 53/262 → 1.2536; half-time recursion → 1.1125; s = 0.16 → 1.190; s = 1/3 → 1.5.
- `share_from_stage_timers` (`synthetic` timers): stage times summing to 262 with 53 in the recursions give
  s = 0.2023 and the profile clause fires.
- `kill_clause_evaluation`: ratio lower bound 1.199 → fired; 1.200 → not; compile 60.1 s → fired; 60.0 → not;
  s interval [0.30, 0.36] → not fired by the profile clause (upper end at least 1/3).
- `ratio_cannot_exceed_ceiling`: measured 1.4 with s = 0.2 raises the consistency flag.
- `arm_registry_extensible`: a fourth arm registers without modifying the three.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/pilots/phylum_specialize_three_arm.sh` drives the real binary: `frob spark fingerprint --json`; `frob sim
run --design quad-12 --worlds 8192 --tier gpu-cuda --steps 1000 --seed 31 --kernels <arm> --json` for arms a, b, c
interleaved ten times (`--kernels` is a DESIGN pilot option selecting a registered kernel set); `frob spark
precompile --kernels c --json` for the compile time; `frob spark check --kernels c --against b --json` for the
digest comparison. It uses the `STD-E2E` helper and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_specialize_three_arm/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: per line `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim
Performance, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), the measured value with unit and threshold (s against
1/3, the ratio against 1.2, compile seconds against 60), digests compared, and the disclosure block per cell. The
`summary` line states each kill clause's outcome and the gate touched. A fired clause is a recorded result, not a
script failure; a missing disclosure field or fewer than ten repetitions is a failure.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-374: 1.25 for an infinitely fast specialized recursion, 1.11 for one twice as fast, 1.19 on a manipulation
scene with a 16% share (exact arithmetic on a FACT to be re-read at its source). Counterexample CX-164 (a kernel
specialized to one design gives a large step speedup: false). Negative controls: (1) a ceiling computed as 1 + s
(1.20) in place of 1/(1 − s); (2) a ratio measured on the recursion stages only and reported as whole-step; (3) an
arm (c) that reassociates a sum: its digests differ from (b) and it is rejected as interchangeable; (4) a cold
compile time reported as cached. Determinism lanes: arms (b) and (c) produce identical digests twice on one
machine and across worker counts on `batch-cpu`; per back end against class D2.

## Considerations

- The same pilot is described by the device chapter, which governs the kill gate. If that chapter's drafting unit
  also emits a pilot bead for the three arms, the two are one piece of work and should be merged, keeping this
  bead's Phylum-side reading of s and the device chapter's decision.
- The three arms are also the reference arms of the limb-archetype kernel pilot (a fourth arm: the quadruped as a
  base and four instances of a serial three-revolute limb archetype). If the profile clause stops this pilot at
  step 2, the harness and arms (a) to (c) are still built, because that pilot needs them.
- Padding removal alone is arm (b) against (a) and is available without any specialized kernel through size
  buckets; the pilot separates it from constant folding so that folding is not credited with padding's gain.
- Compute: under one device-hour; within the default envelope.
- What would make us revisit: a profile after an optimization pass in which the solve shrinks and the recursions'
  share rises above one third.
