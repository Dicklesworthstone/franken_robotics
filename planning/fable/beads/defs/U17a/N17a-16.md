## Background

Specialization of survivors is a gated optimization of Phylum: a kernel specialized to one design (constants
folded, loops unrolled, padding removed) may replace the table-driven kernel for designs that survive several
rounds of a race and will run for a long time. It is not a verb; it is an option of `phylum.sweep`, and it stays
off until its gate to fund passes (our own profile shows a specializable share of the step of at least one third,
and the three-arm pilot shows a whole-step gain of at least 1.2 with a compile of at most 60 s). The device
chapter owns the specialized kernels themselves and the kill gate KILL-SPECIALIZE (seed bead BD-371). This bead is
the Phylum side: deciding which survivor is worth a compile, running the compile off the critical path, swapping a
running design onto its specialized kernel without changing any result, and enforcing the invariant that makes
the swap safe.

INV-PHYLUM-05: a specialized kernel reproduces table-driven digests or is a new kernel version. The first case
makes results interchangeable; the second owes the fidelity battery like any new kernel version. The design rule
that makes the first case reachable is that unrolling keeps the operation order and only exact constant
operations are folded; reassociation changes bits and is not an exact rewrite.

Why only survivors: with a ceiling near 1.2 and a compile of tens of seconds, specializing every design of a
generation would spend more time compiling than it saves. The promotion inequality below is that statement made
exact. The alternative rejected is specialization inside the edit loop: it would put a compile back on the path
from an edit to the first step, which is the latency this subsystem exists to remove.

## Scope

In: the promotion decision and its surface, in `frob-phylum`: which designs are eligible, the option on
`phylum.sweep` and its refusal, the promotion inequality with its inputs read from records, the request for a
switch made to the kernel layer, the decision to roll a promoted design back after a later mismatch, the
identities and labels of results after a swap, the promotion report in the envelope, and the invariant
INV-PHYLUM-05 measured through the sweep.

Out, with the bead that has each: the switch itself, in `frob-kernels`: the background compile with its 60 s cap,
the check of the bit-identity fence before first use, the target bucket, the versioned state map, the two-phase
move at an acknowledged chunk boundary, the digest verification, the abort paths, the inverse move and the
receipt (N21c-20, a dependency: this bead requests a switch and reads its receipt; it compiles nothing, builds no
state map and copies no state); the fence and its refusal `FROB-REF-613` (N21c-19); generating, compiling and
caching the specialized kernels and the gate GATE-SPARK-14 (BD-371); the race (BD-341); the gate to fund, which
is the specializable-share measurement (N21c-16), and the three-arm pilot (N21c-17, which superseded the Phylum
unit's draft N17a-15), read by the first decision on KILL-SPECIALIZE (N21c-18); limb-archetype kernels (a
separate mechanism with zero compile per design).

The two chapters that describe this mechanism use two names for one thing: the "table-driven kernel" of this
bead is the "generic kernel" of N21c-20.

## Technical approach

1. **Eligibility.** A design is a survivor when the race or cascade marks it as advancing to a rung that will run
   it for a long time (finalist training or a long confirmation). The option (DESIGN name `--specialize-survivors`
   on `phylum.sweep`; crate flag `specialize-survivors`) must be on, and KILL-SPECIALIZE must be on record as not
   fired; otherwise the option is refused as not implemented in this build (`FROB-REF-724`).
2. **Promotion inequality (implemented and tested here and nowhere else; BD-371 repeats the rule for its reader
   and records its two measured inputs, g and C_compile, with the gate).** Promote when T_remaining·(1 − 1/g) > C_compile, where
   T_remaining is the estimated remaining device time of that design's job on its current kernel (the point value
   of the cost estimate of the scheduled work), g is the whole-step gain on record for its shape class, and
   C_compile is the measured compile cost for the class. DESIGN (settled with N21c-20, which had stated the rule a
   second time): g is the paired lower bound of the recorded gate measurement, never the point estimate and never
   a guess, so that a promotion is not bought on the optimistic end of an interval. Worked: g = 1.2 and
   T_remaining = 600 s save 100 s, more than a 60 s compile: promote. T_remaining = 400 s saves 66.7 s: promote.
   T_remaining = 300 s saves 50 s: do not. The thresholds: at g = 1.2 and a 60 s compile more than 360 s must
   remain; at g = 1.11, more than 605 s; at g = 1.25, more than 300 s, and with a 20 s compile more than 100 s
   (T_remaining = 120 s saves 24 s: promote).
3. **Compile.** Requested from the kernel layer when the inequality holds. The compile runs on CPU workers in the
   background, never blocking the cohort, under the 60 s cap per design; a compile that reaches the cap is
   cancelled and the design stays on the table-driven kernel. All of that is N21c-20's; this bead records the cap
   event it reports and counts the design as `capped`. The specialized kernel is a new `KernelId` and another
   entry of the binary kernel cache.
4. **Equivalence before first use.** Only a specialized kernel that is bit-identical to the table-driven kernel is
   swapped in. The check is the bit-identity fence of the device chapter (N21c-19), consulted by the switch
   (N21c-20) before it prepares anything: with a current identity row the kernel is digest-equivalent with a
   receipt and results are interchangeable; without one the kernel is refused as a specialized kernel
   (`FROB-REF-613`, raised by the fence). Such a kernel could enter only as a new kernel version through the
   fidelity battery, which neither bead does.
5. **Swap.** Performed by N21c-20 at a control-interval boundary that the host acknowledges (a chunk boundary),
   through its versioned state map, which moves every state buffer, warm-start buffers and packet queues
   included, from the cohort's padded slot layout to the specialized environment's unpadded layout. What this bead
   relies on and asserts through the sweep: world addresses do not change, so random streams, resets and task
   assignment are unchanged.
6. **After the swap.** Results stay keyed by world address and `ModelId`. They carry the new `StepPlanId` and the
   equivalence receipt. The digest chain continues unbroken: the first control interval after the swap has the
   digest the table-driven kernel would have produced.
7. **Rollback.** Any mismatch detected after a swap by the background referee, the divergence ledger or a later
   digest check is a decision of this bead: the design goes back to the table-driven kernel at the next boundary
   and the kernel entry is quarantined. The move back is N21c-20's inverse move through the inverse state map; the
   quarantine is recorded with the mismatch that caused it.

Determinism: class D2 per back end, unchanged by the swap. Evidence: a promoted run's claim product is that of the
table-driven run; the swap adds no claim and removes none.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The promotion decision follows the inequality: the worked cases give promote (600 s at g = 1.2), promote
      (400 s), do not (300 s), do not (604 s at g = 1.11), promote (301 s at g = 1.25), promote (120 s at g = 1.25
      with a 20 s compile).
- [ ] g is the paired lower bound on record and C_compile the recorded measurement for the shape class; with no
      record the option refuses.
- [ ] A design whose compile N21c-20 cancels at the cap keeps running on the table-driven kernel and is reported
      `capped`; a design whose kernel the fence refuses (`FROB-REF-613`) is never swapped in and is reported with
      that code. (No leaked worker or cache entry after a cancelled compile is N21c-20's acceptance.)
- [ ] Across a swap, every world's digest chain equals the chain of an unswapped run, on `batch-cpu` and per
      admitted device family (INV-PHYLUM-05), for swaps at 100 random boundaries.
- [ ] A rollback decided here after a planted later mismatch returns the design to the table-driven kernel at
      the next boundary with all state buffers bit-identical to an unswapped run (the round trip of the state map
      itself is N21c-20's acceptance).
- [ ] With the option off or the kill gate fired, `phylum.sweep` behaves exactly as without this bead and the
      option is refused with `FROB-REF-724`.
- [ ] The envelope reports, per promoted design: the inequality's inputs, compile seconds, the equivalence
      receipt, the boundary of the swap and the `KernelId`s before and after.
- [ ] Cancellation of the sweep during a compile or a swap drains and finalizes with no leaked device resource.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `promotion_inequality_cases`: (600 s, 1.2, 60 s) → promote; (400, 1.2, 60) → promote (66.7 against 60);
  (300, 1.2, 60) → no; (604, 1.11, 60) → no; (606, 1.11, 60) → promote; (301, 1.25, 60) → promote;
  (120, 1.25, 20) → promote (24 against 20).
- `gain_is_lower_bound`: a record with point estimate 1.25 and lower bound 1.15 is evaluated at 1.15.
- `capped_compile_is_reported`: when the switch reports a cancelled compile, the design stays on the table-driven
  kernel and one cap event is in the report (the cancellation at 60 s itself is tested in N21c-20, `abort_paths`).
- `rollback_on_later_mismatch`: a planted divergence after the swap sends the design back at the next boundary
  and quarantines the kernel (the map round trip is tested in N21c-20, `state_map_roundtrip`).
- `swap_preserves_digest_small`: a 12-joint quadruped on `batch-cpu`, swap at interval 7 of 20 using a
  specialized kernel set that is the unpadded table-driven path under another `KernelId`: digest chains equal.
- `mismatch_blocks_swap`: a planted specialized kernel with a reassociated sum has no identity row at the fence
  and is not swapped; the report carries `FROB-REF-613`.
- `option_gated`: with the kill gate recorded as fired the option refuses with `FROB-REF-724`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_survivor_promotion.sh` runs `frob phylum sweep --designs @fixture:race-survivors
--worlds-per-design 64 --tier gpu-cuda --specialize-survivors --seed 77 --key promo-on-<run-id> --json` and the
same without the option under a second key (`--key promo-off-<run-id>`), then compares per-world digests and
envelopes (after sorting by address) and asserts the promotion report. `phylum.sweep` is a `run` verb: a call
without a key is `FROB-REF-805`, and the two runs must not share one, because a repeated key returns the first
run's stored receipt and the comparison would then compare a run with itself; both envelopes must show
`idempotent_replay: false` and distinct run identifiers. It
sources the `STD-E2E` helper and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_survivor_promotion/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: per line `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block, the
inequality's inputs with units, compile seconds against the 60 s cap, the `KernelId`s, digests compared, and on
failure the first differing world address, interval and stage with snapshot paths. The `summary` line gives
assertions passed and failed and designs promoted, declined and capped.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-374 bounds what g can be (1.25 at a 20% share; 1.11 for a recursion twice as fast). Negative controls: (1) a
specialized kernel that reassociates one sum (caught by the equivalence check and by the digest chain); (2) a swap
performed mid-interval (caught by a digest break at the swap); (3) a state map that omits warm-start buffers
(caught by the digest of the first interval after the swap); (4) promotion decided with a guessed g (refused: no
record). Counterexample CX-164. Metamorphic relation: the boundary at which the swap happens changes no digest.
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; per back end against D2.

## Considerations

- Kill gate, named in advance: KILL-SPECIALIZE fires on a measured whole-step gain below 1.2, a compile above 60 s
  per design, or a specializable share under one third. Fallback: the table-driven kernel only. Derived derivative
  kernels for CPU solvers are a separate matter and stay. Given an expected gain of 1.05 to 1.15, this bead is
  more likely than not never to be built.
- Two statements of the bit-identity rule exist: the device chapter requires a specialized kernel to be
  bit-identical outright, while Phylum's invariant also admits a non-identical kernel as a new kernel version that
  owes the fidelity battery. They are compatible as implemented here: only digest-equivalent kernels are swapped
  in by this mechanism.
- Memo keys contain kernel versions, so episodes run after the swap are stored under the specialized `KernelId`;
  serving them for requests keyed by the table-driven kernel is not claimed here even with an equivalence receipt.
- The compile runs on CPU workers that the daemon also uses for referee sampling and checks; the cap bounds the
  interference.
