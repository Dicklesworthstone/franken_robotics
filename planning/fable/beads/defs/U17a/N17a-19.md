## Background

A family policy is trained on designs drawn from a generator and judged on designs it has not seen. "Not seen"
is harder than it sounds. Splitting designs into training and held-out sets by seed alone does not keep the test
set unseen: a mirrored copy of a training design is the same closed-loop system, and split by seed alone it can
appear in the test set. A score on it measures memory, not transfer.

A `DesignSplit` therefore separates training designs from held-out designs by seed and by structural stratum, and
groups designs in one orbit of the observation-preserving group on the same side, so a mirrored copy of a training
design cannot appear in the test set. A family-policy score is admitted only for designs absent from the policy's
training manifest. One clause of the generator's gate GATE-PHYLUM-07 is exactly this: no orbit straddles a
`DesignSplit`.

The group matters (ADR-PHYLUM-04). The observation-preserving subgroup Γ_obs is the subgroup of the mechanical
symmetry group whose elements also preserve the sensor suite, every payload, the direction of gravity and every
observation, action, reward and requirement term of the task. Grouping under the larger mechanical group would
remove from the test set designs that the closed loop can tell apart (with a forward lidar, the fore-aft mirror
image of a design is a different experiment); grouping under no group leaks mirror copies. The alternative
rejected is deduplication by `DesignId` only, which treats a design and its mirror image as unrelated.

## Scope

In: the `DesignSplit` object and its identity; orbit keys for generated designs; side assignment; structural
strata; the manifest of each side; the admission predicate for scores; the straddle check; the `--split <spec>`
option of `phylum.generate`, through which the gate script reaches all of these. Out: computing Γ_obs
(Sensorium and Marrow: BD-241, BD-102); orbit canonicalization itself (N17a-23, which this bead calls); the
generator (BD-287); training and scoring family policies (BD-320, BD-321).

## Technical approach

Types (DESIGN: intent, not signatures):

```rust
pub struct DesignSplit {
    pub generator: GeneratorId,
    pub split_seed: u64,
    pub group: GroupId,                     // Γ_obs of the family's chassis frame under the stated task
    pub strata: Vec<Stratum>,               // structural strata, each with its held-out fraction
    pub train_seeds: Vec<SeedRange>, pub held_out_seeds: Vec<SeedRange>,
    pub manifest_train: Blake3, pub manifest_held_out: Blake3,   // hashes of the sorted orbit keys per side
}
pub enum Stratum { ParametricInBox, SingleLimbEdit, OutOfClass }   // the three held-out sets fixed before training
pub enum Side { Train, HeldOut, Excluded(Reason) }
pub struct OrbitKey(Blake3);                // identity of the canonical representative of a design's orbit
```

1. **Orbit key.** A generated design is a chassis with slots (hips, wheel mounts, arm mounts) carrying limb types
   and per-limb parameters. Treat each slot's content (limb type and its parameter tuple, as exact rationals) as
   the slot's label. Γ_obs acts on labelings by permuting slots, with mirror-image limb types exchanged under
   improper elements. The orbit key is the hash of the lexicographically least labeling in the orbit, together
   with the group-invariant remainder of the design (chassis parameters, payload, sensors). Two designs have the
   same key exactly when one is the image of the other under an element of Γ_obs. Designs that lower to the same
   `ModelId` share a key by construction.
2. **Stratum.** Assigned from structure alone: the topology signature (limb count and limb types) equal to a
   template's and parameters inside the declared box → `ParametricInBox`; one limb added, removed or replaced
   relative to a template → `SingleLimbEdit`; a topology signature outside the family → `OutOfClass` (designs the
   policy must decline, never score).
3. **Side.** For a design d with key κ and stratum t: side = `HeldOut` if the keyed hash
   BLAKE3(split_seed ‖ t ‖ κ), read as a fraction in [0, 1), is below the stratum's held-out fraction, else
   `Train`. Because the side is a function of κ, every member of an orbit lands on one side whatever seed
   produced it. Separation by seed is kept as a second, independent barrier: training designs are drawn from
   `train_seeds` and held-out candidates from `held_out_seeds`; a candidate whose key-side disagrees with the
   seed range it came from is `Excluded` and counted, never moved.
4. **Manifests.** Each side's manifest is the sorted list of orbit keys actually used, hashed. The split's
   identity is the hash of its fields and both manifests; a family policy records it.
5. **Admission predicate.** `admit_score(policy, design)`: admitted only if the design's key is absent from the
   policy's training manifest and present in, or assignable to, the held-out side of the policy's own split, and
   its stratum is not `OutOfClass`. Otherwise it returns a typed finding (training-set member; out of class) that
   the caller turns into its refusal. A score admitted this way is still not a ladder rung unless the design is
   inside the generator's support (N17a-20).
6. **Straddle check.** `verify(split, train_designs, held_out_designs)` recomputes all keys and requires the two
   key sets to be disjoint; it returns the first straddling orbit with both members and the group element that
   maps one to the other.
7. **Verb option.** `frob phylum generate --generator <g> --seed <s> --count <n> --split <spec> --json`, where
   `<spec>` names a split specification (the fields of `DesignSplit` before its manifests: generator, split seed,
   group, strata with fractions, seed ranges; a file or `@fixture:<name>`). Each returned sample carries its orbit
   key, stratum and side; the envelope carries the split identity, both manifest hashes, counts per side and
   stratum, the excluded candidates with their reasons, and the result of `verify` over the samples returned
   (`straddling: []`, or the first straddling orbit with both members and the group element). The option is added
   to the verb of BD-287 as `--locate` and `--report` are (N17a-20, N17a-18), and is registered in the option
   table of the `phylum` cluster.

Determinism class D0 throughout: keys are exact rational labelings and integer permutations; nothing is floating
point.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the mirror fixture (a quadruped whose left-front thigh is 0.24 m and the other three 0.21 m, against the
      same design with the right-front thigh long), with a forward lidar so that Γ_obs is the left-right mirror
      alone, both designs have one orbit key and one side, for every split seed tested (10⁴ seeds).
- [ ] On the same chassis the fore-aft image (left-rear thigh long) has a different key; it may fall on either
      side, and does across seeds (the split does not over-merge under the mechanical group).
- [ ] For the gate fixture (10⁴ generated designs per family), no orbit has members on both sides: the straddle
      check returns none (KA-385, GATE-PHYLUM-07).
- [ ] A split by seed alone (the planted control) is caught: the straddle check returns the mirror pair with the
      group element.
- [ ] `admit_score` rejects a design whose key is in the training manifest even when its `DesignId` is new, and
      rejects every `OutOfClass` design.
- [ ] The split identity changes when the generator, the split seed, a fraction, the group or either manifest
      changes; two machines compute the same identity.
- [ ] Held-out fractions are met within sampling error per stratum, and excluded candidates are counted in the
      envelope.
- [ ] `frob phylum generate … --split <spec> --json` emits exactly one envelope carrying per-sample sides and keys,
      the split identity and the straddle result; built with the mutation `N17a-19:cx-166` (sides assigned by
      seed parity), `straddling` is non-empty and names the mirror pair.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `orbit_key_mirror_equal`: the mirror pair above under the left-right mirror: equal keys.
- `orbit_key_mechanical_not_used`: with the forward lidar, the fore-aft pair has unequal keys; without any sensor
  (Γ_obs = C₂ × C₂) all four images share one key.
- `side_is_function_of_key` (property): random designs and random group elements: side(g·d) = side(d).
- `strata_assignment`: template with parameters in the box → `ParametricInBox`; one limb removed →
  `SingleLimbEdit`; a hexapod under a quadruped family → `OutOfClass`.
- `seed_range_disagreement_excluded`: a held-out-seed candidate whose key side is `Train` is `Excluded`, not moved.
- `straddle_detected`: a `synthetic` split built by seed parity reports the mirror pair.
- `admit_score_rules`: training-manifest member → rejected; held-out member → admitted; out of class → rejected.
- `split_identity_sensitivity`: each field change changes the identity.
- `same_model_same_key`: two `DesignId`s with one `ModelId` (a rename) share a key.

### End-to-end script and logging

Covered by `scripts/e2e/phylum_generate.sh`, owned by N17a-21 (which depends on this bead; the split has no
surface but the `--split` option of `phylum.generate`, so this bead writes no script): its step 6 runs `frob
phylum generate --generator @fixture:gen-quad --seed <s> --count <n> --split @fixture:split-mirror --json` for 10⁴
designs per side, runs the straddle check over both sides, asserts zero straddling orbits and that the mirror pair
shares a side, and logs the number of orbits per side, excluded candidates and straddling orbits (threshold zero)
as `metric` and `assert` events, the two `DesignId`s and the group element on failure, and the envelope's evidence
block (class `Tested`, determinism `exact`, the split identity). The admission predicate has no verb of its own
(its callers turn its finding into their refusal) and is covered by `admit_score_rules`.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-385: no orbit straddles a `DesignSplit`. Counterexample CX-166: splitting by seed alone lets a mirrored copy of
a training design into the test set; the source text gives no numeric case, so the mirror fixture above, split by
seed parity, is the case this bead supplies (N17-CX's `case_supplied_by`). Its control is registered in N17-CX and
planted by this bead: `N17a-19:cx-166` (kind `mutation`, a `cfg(feature = "cx-166")` branch of `frob-phylum`'s side
assignment, built only with `gauntlet-mutations`: sides assigned by seed parity, with the mirror pair generated
across the boundary). The control runner (N38b-04) applies it to KA-385's row, `FROB_TEST_SEED=20261122 cargo
test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations,frob-phylum/cx-166 --test g5_determinism -- KA-385`, which
must fail with the straddle check naming the mirror pair and the group element, and passes without the feature.
Counterexample CX-153 guards the other direction: quotienting under the
mechanical group when a front-only sensor is mounted merges designs the closed loop can tell apart. Negative
controls: (1) a split by seed parity (the mutation `N17a-19:cx-166`); (2) a split keyed on `DesignId`; (3) a split grouped under Γ_mech with a
forward lidar (over-merging: detected because the fore-aft pair is forced onto one side for every seed).
Metamorphic relation: applying any element of Γ_obs to every design leaves both manifests unchanged. Determinism
lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS (D0).

## Considerations

- Γ_obs depends on the task: a task whose goal orientation breaks a mirror shrinks the group and therefore the
  orbits. A split is valid for the task it was built under; reusing it under another task requires recomputing
  keys, and the split identity includes the group for that reason.
- If a symmetry was established by tolerance (an imported design), the group is classed `Tested`, and the split
  inherits that class; orbit grouping under a tolerance-based symmetry is reported as such.
- Near-duplicates that are not exact orbit members (a thigh 0.2401 m against 0.24 m) are different designs here.
  Exact sharing only: similarity in design space is not grounds for grouping, and held-out evaluation over a
  parametric neighbourhood is exactly what the `ParametricInBox` stratum is for.
- If campaign quotienting falls back at its kill gate, orbit representatives remain (they are the Solid part), so
  this bead is unaffected.
