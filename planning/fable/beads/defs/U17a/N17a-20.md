## Background

A family policy is trained on a generator's distribution. Applied to a design the generator could never have
produced, its score says nothing the training evidence supports: FACT, zero-shot transfer is 70 to 85% of
specialist return inside a morphology class in a parametric neighbourhood of the trained templates, 10 to 40%
across topology-diverse designs at affordable pretraining, and outright failure outside the training
distribution. The generator therefore has to answer a membership question about any design, not only emit
designs.

**Support.** `phylum.generate --locate` returns the derivation of a given design under a generator, or reports
that it lies outside the support. A family policy applied to a design outside the support of its training
generator is labelled out-of-distribution and its score is not a rung of the evaluation ladder (`FROB-REF-541`).

The alternative rejected is a distance threshold in some feature space ("close enough to the training set").
Exact sharing only is the subsystem's rule, and a learned or hand-tuned distance would be a second, unstated
distribution. Membership is decided by the grammar itself: either a derivation exists or it does not, and when it
does not, the first production that fails is named so the caller can see how far outside the design is.

## Scope

In: the inverse of sampling (parsing a design against a generator specification); the derivation and exact
probability of a located design; the outside-support report with the failing production; the out-of-distribution
label; the support predicate offered to the family-policy path; the refusal `FROB-REF-541`; the `--locate` option.
Out: sampling (BD-287); validity and rejection (N17a-18); splits and the training-manifest check (N17a-19); the
policy's own membership refusal by parameter box and topology signature, which the learning subsystem owns.

## Technical approach

1. **Locate.** Input: a design (term or `DesignId`) and a generator. The design is canonicalized by the design
   language. The grammar is matched top-down against the canonical term:
   a. archetype: the term's root structure matches an archetype of the specification;
   b. body plan: limb count and arrangement, read off the `mirror` and `radial` constructors and the slot list;
   c. limb type per slot: the slot's module reference is one of the weighted limb types admitted at that slot;
   d. parameters: each parameter value equals, as an exact rational, a point of that parameter's grid inside its
      declared bounds, with non-zero weight, under the inherited attributes in force at that node;
   e. cards: each actuator card is one the catalogue query admits with non-zero weight, at the pinned catalogue
      digest.
   Success yields the derivation (the same `Step` list sampling would have produced) and the exact probability as
   a rational. If several derivations yield the term, all are returned (up to a cap, DESIGN default 64) with the
   summed probability.
2. **Outside support.** Failure yields `OutsideSupport { first_failure }` where `first_failure` names the
   production (derivation path), the field of the design at fault, the observed value and the admitted set or
   grid (for a parameter: the nearest grid points below and above). Matching continues past the first failure to
   count how many productions fail in total, so a design one grid step off is distinguishable from a foreign
   topology.
3. **Consistency with sampling.** For every sampled design, `locate` returns the derivation it was sampled with
   and the same probability. This round trip is the test that the two directions implement one distribution.
4. **Support predicate.** `in_support(generator, design) -> bool` is `locate(...).is_ok()` and is what the
   family-policy path calls before scoring. Result on failure: the score, if computed at all for diagnostics, is
   labelled `out-of-distribution`, carries no rank-agreement licence, and is excluded from every ladder rung.
5. **Refusal `FROB-REF-541`**, "Outside generator support": raised when a family-policy score is requested for a
   design outside its training generator's support. Envelope: `pointer` (the design and the first failing
   production), `observed`, `required` (the admitted set), `affected_claims`, `retry: after-change`. Ranked
   repairs: (1) fine-tune the family policy on the design; (2) train a specialist; (3) extend the generator (a new
   `GeneratorId`, which requires retraining to claim coverage). Exit code 3 (refused question).
6. **Verb surface.** `frob phylum generate --generator <g> --locate <design> --json` (kind `read`). Envelope
   evidence: class `Tested`, tier `static`, determinism `exact`, identities `GeneratorId` and `DesignId`.

Determinism class D0: matching compares canonical terms and exact rationals; no tolerance is applied anywhere. A
design whose parameter is given to finite decimal precision but does not land on a grid point is outside the
support, by design.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Round trip: for 10⁴ sampled designs per fixture generator, `locate` returns the sampled derivation and the
      identical exact probability.
- [ ] A design with one parameter one grid step outside its bounds is reported outside the support with that
      parameter's path, the observed value and the nearest admitted points, and exactly one failing production.
- [ ] A design of another family (a hexapod under a quadruped generator) is reported outside the support at the
      body-plan production, with several failing productions.
- [ ] A design that uses an actuator card absent from the pinned catalogue digest is outside the support.
- [ ] A specification with two derivations for one term returns both and the summed probability.
- [ ] `in_support` is false exactly when `locate` fails; a family-policy score requested for such a design is
      refused with `FROB-REF-541`, the three repairs in order, exit 3; a diagnostic score, if produced, carries the
      label `out-of-distribution` and is absent from ladder inputs.
- [ ] Results are identical on Linux and macOS, debug and release.
- [ ] The option is reachable through `frob phylum generate --locate … --json` with exactly one envelope.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `locate_round_trip` (property): sample then locate; derivation and probability equal.
- `locate_off_grid`: bounds [0.18, 0.26] m on a grid of step 0.01 m: 0.215 m is outside (between 0.21 and 0.22);
  0.27 m is outside (above the bound); 0.22 m is inside.
- `locate_foreign_topology`: failing production is the body plan; failure count greater than one.
- `locate_unknown_card`: a card with zero weight or absent from the catalogue digest → outside.
- `locate_ambiguous_sum`: two derivations with probabilities 1/8 and 1/24 → reported sum 1/6.
- `locate_respects_attributes`: a limb length admissible only under a larger body scale is outside when the body
  scale chosen in the term is the smaller one.
- `refusal_541_shape`: code, pointer, observed, required, the three repairs, exit code 3.
- `ood_label_excluded_from_ladder`: a diagnostic record with the label is filtered by the rung-input builder.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_generate.sh` (bead N17a-21). This bead contributes the steps `locate.round_trip`,
`locate.outside` and `locate.refuse541`: each runs `frob phylum generate … --locate <design> --json` (and, for the
refusal, the family-policy scoring verb on an out-of-support design once that verb exists; until then the step is
logged as `skipped` with its milestone), logs the derivation length, the exact probability and the failing
production as `metric` and `assert` events, the `refusal` object with its exit code, and the envelope's evidence
block.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the exact probabilities of the fixture designs (for example a design chosen with weights 1 of 4,
1 of 3 and one grid point of 5 has probability 1/60) and the sum 1/8 + 1/24 = 1/6. Negative controls: (1) a
locator that compares parameters in floating point with a tolerance (admits 0.2150000001 m as 0.215 m and, worse,
an off-grid value as its neighbour: caught by the off-grid test); (2) a locator that ignores the catalogue digest;
(3) a scoring path that omits the support test (caught by the refusal step). Metamorphic relation: locating a
design and locating its re-serialization in the other lossless view of the design language give the same result.
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS (D0).

## Considerations

- Two different refusals guard a family policy. This one (`FROB-REF-541`) is about the generator's support. The
  learning subsystem has its own refusal for a design outside the policy's declared class membership (a rule on
  the parameter box and the topology signature). A design can pass one and fail the other; both are checked and
  each names its own repair.
- Mutation children (typed edits applied to a generated design) often leave the support. That is expected: such a
  child is scored only after fine-tuning or by a specialist, or the generator is extended.
- Grid membership is strict. An agent that nudges a parameter by hand will usually leave the support; the
  outside-support report gives the nearest admitted points so the agent can snap deliberately if it wants a
  covered design.
- Inside the support is necessary, not sufficient, for a score to rank designs: the rung also needs its measured
  rank agreement with specialists for that class and task family.
