## Background

`phylum.generate` is the read verb through which an agent or a training job obtains designs from a stated
distribution: it samples designs from a generator with derivations and log-probabilities; `--locate` tests
support; `--report` gives rejection rates and the bucket histogram. Its gate is GATE-PHYLUM-07 (claim: Known
answer; milestone M3; TARGET): the generator is reproducible across platforms; probabilities are exact on
enumerable sub-grammars; rejection rates are reported; no orbit straddles a `DesignSplit`.

This bead is the end-to-end test of that capability from the user's side and the measurement of the gate. The
cross-platform clause cannot be tested inside one process: it needs the same commands run on the Linux host and
on the Mac, and a comparison of what each produced against a golden list that neither run regenerated. The gate
matters beyond Phylum: a family policy records the `GeneratorId` and `DesignSplit` it was trained on, and every
later claim about held-out designs is only as good as the reproducibility of those two objects.

## Scope

In: the script `scripts/e2e/phylum_generate.sh`, its fixture generators and golden lists, the two-host procedure,
the gate receipt. Out: the mechanisms (BD-287, N17a-18, N17a-19, N17a-20), which contribute named steps.

## Technical approach

Fixtures (committed under `fixtures/phylum/generate/`, all `synthetic` specifications built from public
archetypes):

- `gen-quad-small`: an enumerable sub-grammar of the quadruped family: 2 archetypes × 3 body plans × parameter
  grids of 4 and 5 points (120 derivations).
- `gen-quad`, `gen-biped`, `gen-wheeled`, `gen-multileg`, `gen-tracked`, `gen-wheelleg`: one generator per family
  class.
- `gen-reject-30`: enumerable, 3 of 10 designs invalid under one refusal code; `gen-reject-60`: 60% invalid.
- `gen-ambiguous`: two derivations of one term (probabilities 1/8 and 1/24).
- `split-mirror`: a split over `gen-quad` with a forward lidar in the family frame (Γ_obs is the left-right
  mirror); the mirror pair fixture (left-front thigh 0.24 m against right-front thigh 0.24 m).
- Golden lists: for each generator, the `DesignId`s and exact probabilities of indices 0 to 999 at seed 20261122,
  recorded once on one host by an independent reference implementation of the addressed sampler (a short script
  in the conformance fixtures, not the product code) and committed; never regenerated from the candidate.

Script, plain shell, idempotent and seed-explicit:

1. `frob capabilities --json`, `frob schema phylum.generate --json`: verb present, kind `read`, cluster `families`.
2. **Reproducible.** `frob phylum generate --generator @fixture:gen-quad --seed 20261122 --count 1000 --json`,
   twice; then in two halves (`--start 0 --count 500`, `--start 500 --count 500`); then under the debug build.
   Assert the digest of the `DesignId` list is identical in all cases and equals the golden list. Repeat for each
   family generator.
3. **Across platforms.** The script writes `target/e2e-logs/phylum_generate/<host>.ids` and, when given
   `--compare <other-host-file>`, asserts byte equality with the list produced on the other platform. The gate
   run executes the script on Linux and on macOS and exchanges the files.
4. **Exact probabilities.** On `gen-quad-small` request all 120 designs (the full index range of the enumerable
   fixture is produced by `--enumerate`, a DESIGN option valid only for specifications marked enumerable) and
   assert that the probabilities, as exact rationals, sum to 1/1; assert `gen-ambiguous` reports 1/6 for the
   doubly derived term; assert the importance weight between two fixture generators equals the pinned rational.
5. **Rejection rates reported.** `--report` on `gen-reject-30` gives 30% under the expected refusal code and
   acceptance mass 7/10 exactly; on `gen-reject-60` the flag `badly-specified` is set; on each family generator a
   report exists with a rate per code and a bucket histogram, and its figures equal `frob phylum plan` on the
   accepted set.
6. **No orbit straddles a split.** Generate 10⁴ designs per side under `split-mirror`; run the straddle check;
   assert zero straddling orbits; assert the mirror pair shares a side.
7. **Support.** `--locate` on 1,000 sampled designs returns their derivations and probabilities; on the off-grid
   and foreign-topology fixtures returns outside-support with the failing production. Where the family-policy
   scoring verb exists, request a score for an out-of-support design and assert `FROB-REF-541`, exit 3, three
   repairs in order; otherwise log the step as `skipped` with its milestone.
8. **Negative controls** (test hooks): rerun step 2 with the wall-clock hook and with the hash-map-order hook
   enabled; the script must observe a mismatch with the golden list in each case and report the control as caught.

Logging: sources the shared helper of `STD-E2E`; newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_generate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line,
exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim Known answer, class, tier `static`, determinism
`exact`, `GeneratorId`, the split identity, seed), measured values with their pinned values or thresholds (list
digests, probability sums as rationals, rejection rates against 50%, straddling orbits against zero), and on
failure the first differing index with both `DesignId`s and derivations. The final `summary` line gives assertions
passed, failed and skipped, the controls caught and the gate touched (GATE-PHYLUM-07).

Gate record: the gate is registered in `registries/gates.toml` with its failing controls before the passing run.
The receipt names the commit, both hardware fingerprints, the seed, the generator identities, and per clause the
measured value: number of differing `DesignId`s across platforms (threshold 0), deviation of probability sums from
one (threshold exactly 0), generators with a rejection report (all), straddling orbits (0).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a correct build on Linux and on macOS, and the two hosts' id lists are byte-identical
      for every family generator (KA-385).
- [ ] Probability sums on enumerable sub-grammars are exactly one; the ambiguous term reports 1/6.
- [ ] Every registered generator has a rejection report; the 30% and 60% fixtures report as specified.
- [ ] Zero orbits straddle the split over 10⁴ designs per side; the mirror pair shares a side.
- [ ] Round-trip location holds for 1,000 designs; the outside-support fixtures are reported with their failing
      productions; the `FROB-REF-541` step passes or is logged as skipped with its milestone.
- [ ] Both negative controls are caught.
- [ ] GATE-PHYLUM-07 is recorded with measured values in a receipt that a session other than the implementing one
      entered; the status row for the generator stays "partial" until the remaining conditions of "implemented"
      hold (contract, reachability, cancellation).
- [ ] The log validates against the `STD-E2E` line schema and ends with a `summary` line.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Shell helper tests: `assert_rational_eq` (compares "1/6" and "4/24" as equal only after exact reduction),
`assert_list_digest`. The independent reference sampler in the conformance fixtures has its own test: it
reproduces three hand-derived samples of `gen-quad-small` (addresses and choices worked by hand in the derivation
file) before its golden lists are trusted.

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified above: `scripts/e2e/phylum_generate.sh`; commands `frob capabilities`, `frob schema`, `frob phylum
generate` (with `--report`, `--locate`, `--split`, `--enumerate`), `frob phylum plan`; logs under
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_generate/`.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-385: the same `(GeneratorId, seed, index)` gives the same `DesignId` on Linux and macOS; log-probabilities are
exact and sum to one on enumerable sub-grammars; no orbit straddles a `DesignSplit`. Negative control of the row:
a generator that reads the wall clock or a hash-map order. Further controls exercised: a split by seed alone
(CX-166: a mirrored copy of a training design in the test set); a locator with a floating-point tolerance; a
report that divides by accepted in place of drawn. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts
(the generator is embarrassingly parallel over indices and must not depend on the count); debug and release;
across platforms (class D0).

## Considerations

- The golden lists must come from an implementation that is not the candidate; otherwise the cross-platform test
  degenerates into "the code agrees with itself". The reference sampler is deliberately small (the addressed
  Philox draw, exact rejection, and the grammar walk for the enumerable fixture) and is reviewed like a fixture.
- The catalogue digest and the design-language schema version are inside `GeneratorId`; when either changes, the
  golden lists are extended with new identities, never rewritten in place.
- The script runs in the fixtures battery; its 10⁴-design split step is the slowest (dominated by orbit keys) and
  must stay under a minute on the reference host.
- Fixtures use public archetypes only; the log holds identifiers and counts, no private design data.
