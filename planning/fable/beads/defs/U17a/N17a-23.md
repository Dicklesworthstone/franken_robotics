## Background

A campaign should not simulate the same closed-loop system twice. The first of the three mechanisms of campaign
quotienting is exact and Solid: **orbit representatives under the observation-preserving subgroup**. Before a
combinatorial sweep of k module types over n slots (legs to hips, wheels to corners, actuators to joints), Phylum
takes the group Γ_obs (the subgroup of the mechanical symmetry group that also preserves every sensor pose and
field of view, the payload, the task's goal orientation and the terrain's symmetry), computes its action on the
slots, and enumerates one canonical representative per orbit: the lexicographically least labeling in the orbit.
The count is known in advance from the cycle index, which is the known answer: by the orbit-counting lemma the
number of distinct designs with k types is |Γ|⁻¹·Σ_{g ∈ Γ} k^c(g), where c(g) is the number of cycles of g on the
slots.

Worked, exact: ten leg types on the six slots of a radial hexapod under the dihedral group of order 12 give
(10⁶ + 2·10 + 2·10² + 10³ + 3·10⁴ + 3·10³)/12 = 86,185 orbits, a reduction of 11.6. If sensors or the task leave
only the sagittal mirror, the count is (10⁶ + 10³)/2 = 500,500, a reduction of 2.0. The honest range is two to
twelve, and it shrinks as sensors break symmetry; the saving is bounded by |Γ_obs|, is a factor of two for most
sensed robots, and is stated before any sweep is priced. Algebra is spent at campaign time, where it deletes
simulations, and not in the per-step loop.

ADR-PHYLUM-04 fixes the group: deduplication uses only Γ_obs. The mechanical group ignores sensors, payload and
task. A quadruped with a front-only lidar has a four-element mechanical group and a two-element closed-loop
group; merging under the larger one merges designs the mission can tell apart. A request to quotient under a
group that is not observation-preserving is refused (`FROB-REF-161`). The rejected alternative, quotienting under
the mechanical group, is the counterexample on file.

## Scope

In: the slot action of Γ_obs for a sweep; the action on type labels including mirror partners; canonical
representatives; orbit sizes; the group element mapping each member to its representative; the cycle-index count
and the check that the enumerated count equals it; the refusal; the optional wreath-product acting group. Out:
computing Γ_mech and Γ_obs (BD-102, BD-241); the group primitives, Burnside counts and wreath products of the
algebra crate (BD-049); transporting results across an orbit (N17a-24); the verb (BD-285).

## Technical approach

Inputs: a chassis design with a slot set X of n slots; a type alphabet T of k module types; the group Γ_obs of
the chassis under the stated task, as delivered by the symmetry analysis: its elements with exact isometries,
body permutations and the breaker list (for each excluded mechanical element, the first field that excludes it).

1. **Slot action.** Each g ∈ Γ_obs permutes the mount ports that carry the slots; that gives a permutation π_g of
   X. The cycle type of every π_g is computed once.
2. **Type action.** A proper element maps a module type to itself. An improper element (a reflection) maps a
   type to its mirror partner τ_g(t): the type itself if the module is mirror-symmetric, otherwise a registered
   mirror-image module. Elements for which some type of T has no mirror partner are dropped from the acting group
   for this sweep (they do not map the alphabet to itself) and listed with that reason. The remainder is a group.
3. **Action on labelings.** For ℓ: X → T, (g·ℓ)(x) = τ_g(ℓ(π_g⁻¹(x))).
4. **Count.** Burnside: N = |Γ|⁻¹·Σ_g Fix(g), where Fix(g) is the product over the cycles of π_g of the number of
   types admissible on that cycle: k when τ_g is the identity (so Fix(g) = k^c(g)), and for a mirror with chiral
   types, k on even cycles and the number of self-mirror types on odd cycles. N is an exact integer; a
   non-integer quotient is an integrity fault.
5. **Canonical representatives.** Slots are ordered by the canonical tree order of the design language and types
   by canonical type index. The representative of an orbit is its lexicographically least labeling. Enumeration
   is orderly generation: labelings are built slot by slot, and a prefix is abandoned as soon as some g maps it to
   a lexicographically smaller prefix; a complete labeling is kept exactly when ℓ = min over g of g·ℓ. For sweeps
   up to about 10⁸ raw labelings the plain form (test every labeling against |Γ| images) is sufficient: the
   hexapod sweep is 10⁶ × 12 images.
6. **Orbit data.** For each representative: its orbit size |Γ|/|Stab(ℓ)|. For any member, `to_representative(ℓ)`
   returns the representative and a group element g with g·ℓ = representative; this element is what result
   transport uses.
7. **Check (PO-PHYLUM-01).** The number of canonical representatives enumerated equals the cycle-index count.
   Class D0, exact; checked for every sweep before anything is returned. A mismatch is an integrity fault with
   both numbers, never a returned sweep. The count's evidence class is `Tested`, or `Certified` when the
   certificate of the counting identity for this group instance is emitted and checked.
8. **Refusal `FROB-REF-161`**, "Group not observation-preserving": raised when a quotient (or a weight tie) is
   requested under a group that moves a sensor, the payload or the task, that is, any requested element that is
   not in the computed Γ_obs. Envelope: `pointer` (the first excluded element and its breaker field, for example
   the lidar mount), `observed` (the requested group), `required` (Γ_obs with its order), `retry: after-change`.
   Ranked repairs: (1) use Γ_obs; (2) declare the invariance and let the metamorphic test check it. Exit code 3.
9. **Wreath products (Frontier option, off by default).** When each module has its own automorphism group K that
   preserves its attachment-port invariants and the hub group H preserves the decorated ports, the acting group
   is K ≀ H = K^|X| ⋊ H of order |K|^|X|·|H|. Both conditions are checked before it is used; if either fails the
   plain slot action is used and the reason reported.
10. **Output record** per sweep: raw count kⁿ, group order, cycle types, orbit count, reduction factor, the
    representatives (paged), dropped elements with reasons.

Data are integers and exact field elements only; nothing is floating point. Determinism class D0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Hexapod, ten types, dihedral group of order 12: 86,185 representatives, equal to the cycle-index count;
      reduction 11.6 (KA-365). With three types: 92; with two: 13.
- [ ] Same sweep with only the sagittal mirror: 500,500 (KA-366).
- [ ] The negative control "counting under the rotation subgroup only" yields 166,870 and is detected as a wrong
      group for the full-symmetry fixture.
- [ ] Quadruped with four corner slots: (k⁴ + 3·k²)/4 under C₂ × C₂ (27 at k = 3) and (k⁴ + k²)/2 under the
      left-right mirror alone (45 at k = 3).
- [ ] The lidar counterexample: with a front-only sensor mounted, a request to quotient the hexapod sweep under
      the mechanical group refuses `FROB-REF-161`, naming the sensor mount as breaker, with both repairs in order.
- [ ] PO-PHYLUM-01 holds on 10³ random sweeps (random small groups from the registered chassis, n ≤ 8, k ≤ 6),
      each also verified by brute-force canonicalization.
- [ ] Every member maps to its representative by the returned group element: g·ℓ equals the representative, for
      all members of 10³ random orbits.
- [ ] Representatives are the lexicographically least members; the enumeration is identical on Linux and macOS,
      debug and release.
- [ ] Wreath fixture: two slots swapped by the hub, each holding one of k types in one of two orientations that
      the module's half-turn identifies: group order 8, count (k² + k)/2, 6 at k = 3 against 36 raw assignments;
      with a module whose automorphism does not preserve its port, the wreath option is declined with the reason.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `hexapod_dihedral_counts`: k = 2, 3, 10 → 13, 92, 86,185; equality with brute force for k = 2, 3.
- `hexapod_mirror_only`: k = 10 → 500,500; k = 2 → 36.
- `rotation_subgroup_control`: k = 10 → 166,870 (must differ from 86,185).
- `quadruped_counts`: k = 3 → 27 and 45; k = 10 → 2,575 and 5,050.
- `burnside_equals_enumeration` (property, PO-PHYLUM-01): random sweeps; counts agree.
- `to_representative_maps` (property): returned element maps member to representative.
- `least_labeling`: for the orbit of (2, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1) on the hexagon the representative is (1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2).
- `chiral_types_under_mirror`: two mutually mirror-image leg types and one symmetric type under a mirror with
  three two-cycles: Fix(mirror) = 3³; with one odd cycle only the symmetric type is admissible on it.
- `refusal_161_shape`: code, pointer with breaker, observed, required, repairs, exit code 3.
- `wreath_c2_c2`: order 8; counts 1, 3, 6 for k = 1, 2, 3.
- `noninteger_count_is_fault`: a planted wrong cycle type makes the Burnside sum non-divisible and raises the
  integrity fault.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-365 (86,185; reduction 11.6; enumerated equals cycle-index count) with its control, counting under the
rotation subgroup only; KA-366 (500,500; reduction 2.0) with its control, quotienting under the mechanical group
when a front-only sensor is mounted, which must refuse. Counterexample CX-153. Theorem card T-24: the number of
canonical representatives generated for a campaign equals |Γ|⁻¹·Σ_g k^c(g). Further planted defects: a wrong
cycle index (caught by the count check); a canonical test that compares against generators only and not all
elements; a type action that ignores chirality. Metamorphic relations: relabeling the types by a permutation
permutes representatives and preserves the count; conjugating the group by a slot renumbering preserves the
count. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS (D0).

## Considerations

- INV-PHYLUM-03 (quotients use Γ_obs only, and equal mission digests mean equal lowered tables) has its first
  half enforced here; the lidar counterexample is its check.
- Γ_obs depends on the task and on every mounted sensor; a sweep's orbits are valid only for that task and sensor
  suite. A sweep over sensor placements changes the group from member to member and is not a slot sweep in this
  sense; it is refused with the breaker named.
- If a symmetry was established by tolerance (an imported chassis), the group and therefore the quotient are
  classed `Tested` with the tolerance recorded; no certificate is emitted.
- Size limit: enumeration cost is kⁿ·|Γ| in the plain form. Beyond the configured budget the verb returns the
  count (exact, from the cycle index) and a paged representative generator rather than a full list.
- KILL-QUOTIENT's fallback is exactly this bead: orbit representatives only. It is the Solid part and survives.
- Order of work with theorem card T-24 (bead CARD-T-24). The card closes at M4, because the checker of its
  `orbit-count` certificate (N12-18) does; this bead is M3 and does not wait for it. At M3 the count check of
  PO-PHYLUM-01 runs here against the construction's law tests (SPEC-C-22) and the count is classed `Tested`; when
  the card and its checker exist, the certificate a sweep emits for its own count is checked in this crate's
  tests and the class becomes `Certified`. The link to the card is `related`, not a dependency.
- The same counter sizes contact-mode enumerations: four feet, each open, sticking or slipping, under C₂ × C₂
  give (3⁴ + 3·3²)/4 = 27 strata.
