## Background

The second mechanism of campaign quotienting is the **mission-observable digest** (Frontier): two designs that
lower to the same model tables, under the same task, scenes, policy, kernels, tier and profile, are the same
experiment, and are evaluated once. The digest is the hash of exactly what the step plan and the task read. Two
rules make it sound and useful. It is structural, not proved: "equal digests imply identical traces" is a
statement about the simulator that no proof checker decides; it holds by construction, because the kernels read
nothing but the tables, and it is checked by replay. And lowering is exact: welded sub-assemblies are rolled up
in exact rational arithmetic from the design's rational parameters and rounded once, so designs that are equal in
exact arithmetic lower to equal bits.

Being Frontier, the mechanism has a pilot before it is funded. The construction (C-22, the mission-observable
projection quotient, specified by N10b-12) states it: for the projection quotient, **the ballast fixture end to
end with replay. Falsifier: one merged pair whose replays differ in any bit. Ceiling: fixture-dependent, and 1
in general. Fallback: orbit deduplication only.** Before campaign quotienting by digest is funded, a paper-scale
pilot must show, end to end on a fixture built to have many merges, that no merged pair replays differently in
any bit. One such pair falsifies the mechanism. This bead is that pilot, and it is the only one: its record in
the pilot register is `PILOT-C-22`.

The fixture, exact: eight identical ballast slugs in 24 collinear slots give C(24, 8) = 735,471 placements, which
enter rigid-body dynamics only through the count, Σx and Σx² (total mass, first moment and second moment along
the slot line). There are 24,546 distinct (Σx, Σx²) pairs, so 24,546 classes, a reduction of 30.0. The negative
control: two placements with equal Σx and different Σx² must fall in different classes. The factor is a property
of this fixture and says nothing about the factor on a real brief: adding a placement-dependent hot spot, a
harness length or a collision shape to what the mission reads enlarges the digest and splits classes, down to
singletons. What the pilot can show is that the mechanism is exact; what it can refute is the claim that equal
tables give equal traces in this engine.

Terms. *Ballast fixture*: the eight slugs in 8 of 24 collinear slots on a rigid carrier. *Merged pair*: two
different placements with equal (Σx, Σx²), hence equal digest. *Replay*: running the same episode (same task,
scene, policy, tier, world address) for each design and comparing the chain of state digests. *World address*:
(run seed, world, episode); random streams are keyed by it.

## Scope

In: the registered pilot record `PILOT-C-22` with its frozen protocol; the fixture as a design family; a
prototype digest (hash of the lowered tables); exhaustive class counting; replay of sampled merged pairs with
bitwise comparison, in two stages (many pairs on one tier; fewer pairs on every tier, solo and in a mixed
batch); the controls; the arms; the falsifier evaluation; the ceiling; the verdict. Out: the production digest
with its declared read set, memoization and class reports (N17a-26); the verb and `phylum.quotient` as product
features (BD-285); the specification of the projection quotient (N10b-12); the gate GATE-PHYLUM-01 with its
audit of 10⁴ pairs on real campaign sets at M3 (N17a-28); the kill decision (N17a-29); orbit deduplication,
which is Solid and needs no pilot.

## Technical approach

1. **Register** `PILOT-C-22` in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (the register of N38a-09) before
   any run: mechanism (mission-observable digest, Frontier); protocol (this section, frozen by hash); falsifier
   (one merged pair whose replays differ in any bit); ceiling (30.0 on this fixture; 1 in general); fallback
   (orbit deduplication only); kill gate (KILL-QUOTIENT); cost (CPU core-hours: stage A is CPU only and under one
   hour; stage B is 1,080 design-episodes per tier: 270 pairs, two designs each, solo and in a mixed batch);
   tools and pins (this engine's tiers and lowering at the pilot's commit).
   The result is added afterwards. Evidence class `Tested`, determinism class as measured per tier.
2. **Fixture.** A design family `fixture:ballast-24-8`: a rigid carrier link with 24 collinear slots at positions
   x = 1, 2, …, 24 slot pitches along one body axis, eight identical slugs welded into eight of them (fixed
   joints, folded into the carrier). All parameters are rationals. No collision shape, sensor or task term
   depends on which slots are filled. Constants (DESIGN, one fixture file shared with the construction's
   host-level row in N10b-12, whose values these are): slug mass 3/10 kg, slot pitch 1/40 m. Reason for the
   pitch: the rail of 24 slots is then 0.6 m and fits along a wheeled reference base, and 1/40 is not a binary
   fraction, so that control (b) below is not vacuous. The carrier is used on two hosts:
   - *Host A, pendulum.* The carrier is the swinging link of a one-joint pendulum that also strikes a floor, so
     that the trajectory depends on mass, centre of mass and inertia and includes contact.
   - *Host B, wheeled base.* A wheeled reference archetype carries the rail along its longitudinal axis and
     drives a fixed 10 s velocity profile with one turn and one braking event on flat ground, so that mass,
     first moment and second moment all matter.
3. **Count on the host.** Enumerate all 735,471 placements; compute (Σx, Σx²) as integers; assert 24,546 distinct
   pairs (the exact truth, independent of any engine code).
4. **Lower and hash.** Lower every placement through the real compile and lowering path (fixed joints folded by
   the exact rational sum 𝖯_parent + G·𝖯_child·Gᵀ, rounded once to single precision) and hash the lowered tables
   of the carrier. Assert exactly 24,546 distinct hashes, and that hash classes coincide with (Σx, Σx²) classes.
5. **Replay, stage A (many pairs, one tier; host A).** Draw 1,000 pairs of distinct placements inside one class,
   by a keyed hash of the class id (so the draw is reproducible), with at least 100 pairs from the largest
   classes. Run both members for 2,000 world-steps on `batch-cpu` under the lockstep profile at the same world
   addresses, and compare the digest chain (the in-kernel digest at every commit fence and the snapshot digest
   every control interval) bit for bit.
6. **Replay, stage B (every tier, solo and mixed; host B).** By a keyed draw before any run: 200 merged pairs
   (equal (Σx, Σx²), different placements), spread over small and large classes; 50 control pairs with equal Σx
   and different Σx² (the {1, 4} against {2, 3} pattern embedded in otherwise equal placements); 20 identical
   pairs (the same placement twice: an A/A arm). Each design of each pair runs the episode on each tier
   available at the time (`batch-cpu` in `f64` and `f32`; the referee; a GPU tier if present), solo and inside
   a mixed batch with random co-tenants. Digest chains are recorded per step.
7. **Arms.** The mechanism (evaluate one member per class and reuse); the conventional alternative (memoize on
   full model identity only, which evaluates every placement); the fallback (orbit deduplication only).
8. **Measures.** For merged pairs: the first step at which digests differ, if any. For control pairs: whether the
   digests differ (they must). For A/A pairs: zero differences. Dispatches saved on the pair set.
9. **Controls.** (a) Equal Σx, different Σx²: slots {1, 4} and {2, 3} beside six common slugs give Σx = 5 twice
   and Σx² = 17 against 13; the two placements must have different hashes and different replays. (b) A lowering
   that sums the slugs' inertia contributions in single precision in placement order: the number of distinct
   hashes must exceed 24,546 (equal exact sums round differently), demonstrating why the roll-up is exact. (c) A
   placement-dependent quantity added to what the mission reads (a per-slug collision sample): classes must split
   toward singletons (735,471 distinct hashes). (d) Potency: a planted digest that omits the second moment must
   merge the 50 control pairs, and their replays must then differ: the pilot must report those as false
   quotients, while the A/A arm must report none. (e) A reduction tree shaped by batch capacity makes a
   design's digests depend on its co-tenants; the solo-against-mixed comparison of stage B must catch it.
10. **Falsifier and verdict.** The falsifier holds if any replayed merged pair, in either stage, differs in any
    bit, or if step 4's classes do not coincide with the exact classes. The verdict is recorded as `funded`
    (zero differing merged pairs: the falsifier does not hold) or `fallback` (one or more: the falsifier holds),
    with the first differing step and state component. On `fallback` the digest is not funded as a deduplication
    mechanism: orbit deduplication ships alone and N17a-29 records KILL-QUOTIENT on the pilot. Otherwise the
    result (pairs replayed, zero differences) is attached to N17a-26.
11. **Ceiling (stated before the run).** Fixture-dependent, and 1 in general. On this fixture the class count
    gives a factor of 30.0 on dispatches (735,471/24,546); on a campaign corpus the factor is whatever the corpus
    has, and the kill gate requires at least 5% of dispatches saved. Exact reuse after edits that change
    dynamics is at most about a tenth of compute (FACT, source register, to be re-read).
12. **Fallback and kill gate.** Orbit deduplication only. Kill gate KILL-QUOTIENT (decided at M3 by N17a-29): one
    false quotient survives a repair cycle, or digest classes save less than 5% of dispatches on the campaign
    corpus; then the digest is kept as a cache key with no claim.
13. **The six parts of a method experiment.** Ceiling (step 11); competing arms (step 7); potency control
    (control (d)); paired comparison (pairs by design pair, tier and world address; the reported quantity is a
    count of differing pairs, exact); pre-declared fallback (step 12); reachability (a single differing merged
    pair falsifies; both verdicts are reachable).
14. **Record** the result in the pilot registry and the review log, with the seed, the draw keys, the pair lists,
    the fixture hash and the commit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot record `PILOT-C-22` predates the runs and names the falsifier, ceiling, fallback, kill gate, cost
      and pin; its protocol, falsifier, ceiling and fallback are committed before its result.
- [ ] Host enumeration gives 735,471 placements and 24,546 classes (KA-367, the same row as KA-127).
- [ ] The real lowering path gives exactly 24,546 distinct table hashes, coinciding with the exact classes.
- [ ] Stage A: 1,000 in-class pairs replayed for 2,000 steps each: the number of pairs with any differing digest
      is recorded (the pilot passes only at zero).
- [ ] Stage B: 200 merged pairs, 50 control pairs and 20 A/A pairs ran on every available tier, solo and in a
      mixed batch; the pair list and its draw key are in the record.
- [ ] Merged pairs with any differing digest: 0 (TARGET). Any other value records the verdict `fallback`, with
      the first differing step and state component.
- [ ] All 50 control pairs differ in digest; all 20 A/A pairs are identical.
- [ ] Control (a) splits; control (b) yields more than 24,546 hashes; control (c) yields 735,471; the planted
      digest without the second moment (control (d)) is reported as producing false quotients.
- [ ] The verdict (`funded` or `fallback`) is in the review log; on `fallback` the fallback is named in the
      status table and N17a-26 is marked not funded.
- [ ] Campaign quotienting by digest does not start before the record has a result (GATE-GAUNT-08).
- [ ] Re-running the script reproduces the same pair samples and the same outcome byte for byte.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Tests of the pilot's own harness (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/pilot_c22.rs`; run with the script):

- `enumerate_small`: three slugs in eight slots: 56 placements, 54 classes; the first collision is {1, 5, 6} and
  {2, 3, 7} with Σx = 12 and Σx² = 62. Four slugs in twelve slots: 495 placements, 395 classes.
- `equal_sum_control_values`: {1, 4} and {2, 3}: Σx = 5 and 5; Σx² = 17 and 13.
- `pair_sampler_reproducible`: the keyed draw returns the same 1,000 pairs twice (stage A).
- `pair_draw_is_keyed`: the same key gives the same 270 pairs (stage B); merged pairs have equal (Σx, Σx²) and
  different slot sets; control pairs have equal Σx and unequal Σx².
- `float_rollup_differs`: on a `synthetic` set of 100 placements with equal exact sums, a single-precision
  placement-order sum yields more than one distinct bit pattern.
- `first_divergence_search`: on two synthetic digest chains (labelled `synthetic`) that differ from step 37 on,
  the search returns 37.
- `falsifier_evaluator`: zero differing merged pairs → `funded`; one → `fallback`.
- `lowered_tables_bit_equal`: for each merged pair the lowered pseudo-inertia is bit-equal before any run (a
  pair that fails here is a lowering defect, reported separately from a replay difference).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_mission_digest_ballast.sh` (the one script of `PILOT-C-22`, under `scripts/e2e/` so that the
harness's runner and linter cover it; idempotent; draw keys printed;
it refuses to overwrite a record that has a result) drives the real binary: `frob design check
@fixture:ballast-24-8#<placement> --stages 1-2 --json` on a sample for validity; the lowering-and-hash pass
through a pilot subcommand of the conformance harness; for stage A `frob sim run
@fixture:ballast-24-8#<placement> --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep --worlds 4 --steps 2000 --seed 5 --key
c22-<run>-a-<pair>-<member> --json` (the design is the positional) for each member of each sampled pair; for
stage B, for each design, `frob sim run <design> --task <fixed task> --tier <tier> --seed 5 --world-addr <w> --key
c22-<run>-b-<pair>-<member>-<tier>-solo --json`, and in the mixed batch `frob sim run --designs <member and
co-tenants> --worlds-per-design 1 --task <fixed task> --tier <tier> --seed 5 --key
c22-<run>-b-<pair>-<member>-<tier>-mixed --json` (results joined by world address and `ModelId`), then `frob sim
replay --run <r> --world <w> --key c22-<run>-replay-<pair>-<member>-<tier> --json` on each design and the
harness's own first-divergence search over the two digest chains (`frob telemetry divergence <r₁> <r₂> --world
<w> --json` is used in addition where it exists, and on any mismatch); every run carries its own key, so no
member's envelope is a stored replay of its partner's; it validates the record. It uses the `STD-E2E` helper and
writes
newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/phylum_mission_digest_ballast/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a
transcript: per line `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N17a-25`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`,
`refusal`, `end`), the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim Known answer,
class Tested, tier, determinism class, `ModelId`, design and episode identities, seed), the counts against
their pinned values (735,471; 24,546), one `metric` line per (pair, tier, co-tenancy) with both final digests
and the first divergent step or `none`, `assert` lines for the three pair kinds and the potency control, and on
a difference the first differing merged pair with the step, the control interval and stage, the state
component and the two values, with snapshot paths. The `summary` line gives pairs replayed per stage and per
pair kind, pairs differing (threshold zero), the outcome of each control, the verdict, and the gates touched
(KILL-QUOTIENT, GATE-GAUNT-08).

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-367 (identical with the construction's fixture row KA-127, one fixture identity): C(24, 8) = 735,471
placements; 24,546 distinct (Σx, Σx²) pairs; reduction 30.0; merged members replay bit-identically; slots
{1, 4} and {2, 3} have Σx = 5 and Σx² = 17 and 13 and must not merge. Counterexamples CX-154 (placements with
the same first moment are the same experiment: false), CX-157 (equal mission digests prove identical traces:
false; the rule is structural and checked by replay, class `Tested`, D2) and CX-059. Metamorphic relation
MR-05: designs with equal mission-observable projections replay bit-identically; a digest that omits the second
moment is its negative control. Negative controls: the digest without the second moment; a roll-up that sums
rounded terms in placement order (it makes merged pairs lower to different bits); a reduction tree shaped by
batch capacity (it makes a design's digests depend on its co-tenants). Determinism lanes: the whole pilot twice
on one machine and across worker counts on `batch-cpu`; each design twice on one machine; solo against mixed
batch; debug and release on the CPU tiers; across tiers each against its own determinism class (bit equality
is required within a tier, never across tiers).

## Considerations

- The pilot needs the batched step and digests on `batch-cpu`, hence M2; stage A is cheap enough for the default
  compute envelope. If only one tier exists when the pilot runs, stage B runs there and the record lists the
  tiers still owed.
- A pass does not establish a saving on real campaigns: independent material or compliance changes make every
  class a singleton. The pilot does not show the quotient is useful, only that it is safe on a fixture rich in
  merges. Usefulness is the second trigger of the kill gate (classes saving less than 5% of dispatches on the
  campaign corpus) and is measured later.
- A difference traced to batch composition or reduction order is a defect of the determinism lane, not of the
  quotient; the pilot separates the two by running solo first.
- If the exact roll-up were not implemented in the lowering path, step 4 would report more than 24,546 hashes;
  that is a defect of lowering to be fixed there, and it is why control (b) is part of the pilot.
- The fixture is shared with the construction's host-level row; its constants live in one fixture file.
