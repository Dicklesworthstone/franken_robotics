## Background

`phylum.quotient` is the read verb that tells an agent, before it pays for a campaign, how many of its designs
are distinct experiments: orbit representatives under Γ_obs with the orbit count, and mission-digest classes for
a design set, with the fields that split them. Its gate is GATE-PHYLUM-01 (claim: Known answer; milestone M3;
TARGET): the orbit and ballast rows exact; zero false quotients in 10⁴ sampled class pairs by replay; the lidar
and equal-Σx controls caught.

This bead is the end-to-end test of the capability from the user's side: one script that drives the real `frob`
binary through every known answer, every refusal and one real use (a sweep that dispatches representatives only
and expands results to all members). It measures the known-answer and control clauses of the gate. The replay
clause, which needs 10⁴ pairs of episodes, is measured by bead N17a-28 with the same harness conventions; the two
receipts together are the gate's record.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures, its assertions and log; the known-answer and control clauses of the gate. Out: the
mechanisms (BD-285, N17a-22, -23, -24, -26), which contribute named steps; the replay audit and savings
measurement (N17a-28); the kill decision (N17a-29).

## Technical approach

Fixtures (committed under `fixtures/phylum/quotient/`, built from public archetypes; scripted members labelled
`synthetic`):

- `hexapod-10`: the radial hexapod chassis with six leg slots and ten leg module types, no sensor (Γ_obs is the
  dihedral group of order 12); `hexapod-10-sagittal`: the same with a sensor and task that leave only the sagittal
  mirror; `hexapod-10-lidar`: the same with a front-only lidar, used to request the mechanical group.
- `quad-3`: a quadruped chassis with four corner slots and three types, with and without a forward lidar.
- `ballast-24-8`: eight identical slugs in 24 collinear slots (735,471 placements); `ballast-eq-sum`: the two
  placements {1, 4} and {2, 3} beside six common slugs.
- `mirror-pair`, `scaled-pair` (unit-changed and constant-density pairs), `bus-pair` (shared against separate
  current-limited buses), `wreath-2`: two slots, k types, two orientations identified by a half-turn.
- `sweep-small`: the quadruped sweep with three types (81 labelings) under a mirror-invariant flat task.

Script `scripts/e2e/phylum_quotient.sh`, plain shell, idempotent, seed-explicit:

1. `frob capabilities --json`, `frob schema phylum.quotient --json`: verb present, kind `read`, cluster
   `families`, refusal list contains `FROB-REF-161`.
2. **Orbit rows.** `frob phylum quotient --sweep @fixture:hexapod-10 --json`: raw count 10⁶, group order 12,
   orbit count 86,185, reduction 11.6, enumerated count equal to the cycle-index count. With three types 92, with
   two 13. `hexapod-10-sagittal`: 500,500, reduction 2.0. `quad-3`: 27 without the lidar, 45 with it.
3. **Lidar control.** `frob phylum quotient --sweep @fixture:hexapod-10-lidar --group mechanical --json`: exit 3,
   `FROB-REF-161`, the breaker naming the lidar mount, repairs in order (use Γ_obs; declare the invariance and
   let the metamorphic test check it). The same fixture with `--group obs` returns the mirror-only count.
4. **Rotation-subgroup control.** With the test hook that restricts the group to rotations the count becomes
   166,870 and the script must flag the mismatch with the pinned 86,185.
5. **Ballast row.** `frob phylum quotient --designs @fixture:ballast-24-8 --task ballast-swing --json`: 735,471
   designs, 24,546 classes, reduction 30.0 (paged output; the script walks every page and counts).
6. **Equal-Σx control.** `--designs @fixture:ballast-eq-sum --explain a,b`: two classes; the split report names
   inertia entries of the carrier and maps them to the slot placements.
7. **Similarity.** `scaled-pair`: the unit-changed pair is one class; the constant-density pair is two, with the
   actuator group τ_max/(m₀·g·ℓ₀) named as differing.
8. **Bus control.** `bus-pair`: two classes; the split report names the bus.
9. **Wreath.** `wreath-2` with the wreath option: group order 8, count (k² + k)/2, 6 at k = 3 against 36 raw.
10. **Quotiented sweep.** `frob phylum sweep --designs @fixture:sweep-small --task flat-mirror-invariant
    --worlds-per-design 16 --tier batch-cpu --seed 606 --quotient --json` against the same sweep without the
    option: the first dispatches 45 designs for 81 inputs (the left-right mirror is the group once the task's
    forward direction is fixed); every input has a scorecard keyed by its own `DesignId`; for the 36 expanded
    members the transported scorecard equals, after applying the group element, the scorecard the unquotiented
    sweep computed for the representative; provenance fields are present.
11. **Never runs physics** (steps 2 to 9): `cost.gpu_s == 0` and no arena lease.
12. **Determinism.** Steps 2 to 9 twice, comparing envelope digests byte for byte; once in the debug build.

Logging: sources the shared helper of `STD-E2E`; newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_quotient/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line,
exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim Known answer, class `Tested`, tier, determinism,
identities of the design set, group and task, seed), measured values with their pinned values (counts,
reductions, classes, dispatched designs), envelope digests where two runs are compared, and on failure the first
differing value with the fixture path and saved envelopes. The final `summary` line gives assertions passed and
failed, known answers and refusal codes touched, controls caught, and the gate touched (GATE-PHYLUM-01).

Gate record: the gate is registered in `registries/gates.toml` with its failing controls (rotation subgroup;
mechanical group with a front-only sensor; equal Σx merged) before the passing run. The receipt of this script
carries the measured counts, the control outcomes, the commit, the hardware fingerprint and the seed.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Shell helper tests: `assert_count_paged` (walks pages at a committed frontier and detects a repeated or missing
representative), `assert_repairs_order`, `assert_scorecard_transport` (applies a signed permutation to a
scorecard and compares). Fixture tests in the crate: each fixture parses; `hexapod-10` has six slots and the
stated group order; `ballast-eq-sum` has Σx = 5 for both and Σx² = 17 and 13.

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified above: `scripts/e2e/phylum_quotient.sh`; commands `frob capabilities`, `frob schema`, `frob phylum
quotient`, `frob phylum sweep`, `frob spark place`; logs under `target/e2e-logs/phylum_quotient/`.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-365 (86,185; control: rotation subgroup only), KA-366 (500,500; control: mechanical group with a front-only
sensor must refuse), KA-367 (735,471 placements in 24,546 classes; control: equal Σx and different Σx² merged),
KA-377 in the quotiented sweep (distributional results reused across the mirror). Counterexamples exercised:
CX-153, CX-154, CX-156. Metamorphic relations: MR-04 on the sweep; MR-05 on the ballast pair. Each control is a
test hook that plants one defect and must turn the script red: a canonical test against generators only; a digest
that omits the second moment; a transport that forgets the lateral sign; a group filter that ignores sensors.
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS (class D0 for steps 2 to 9; the sweep
step per back end against D2).

## Considerations

- The dispatched count of 45 in step 10 assumes the fixture task fixes a forward direction, leaving the left-right
  mirror; if the fixture task is changed the pinned count changes with the group, which is the point of pinning
  the group order in the same envelope.
- Enumerating 735,471 ballast designs through the verb is the script's slowest step; it must stay within the
  fixtures battery's budget (a minute on the reference host), which bounds how the digest pass may be implemented
  (hashing lowered tables, with compile products memoized across placements).
- If KILL-QUOTIENT fires, steps 5, 6 and 8 are re-pinned to the fallback: the digest verb output is no longer a
  deduplication report, and the script asserts that no class-level claim is printed.
