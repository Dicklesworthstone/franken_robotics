## Background

Campaign quotienting has a Solid part and a Frontier part. Orbit representatives under the observation-preserving
subgroup are exact counting and enumeration and are not at risk. The mission-observable digest (two designs that
lower to the same model tables under the same task, scenes, policy, kernels, tier and profile are one experiment)
rests on a structural rule checked by replay, and its saving is a property of the campaign in hand. Its kill gate
is KILL-QUOTIENT.

**Trigger.** One false quotient survives a repair cycle, or digest classes save less than 5% of dispatches on the
campaign corpus. **Decided** at M3. **Fallback.** Orbit representatives only; the digest is kept as a cache key
with no claim. A false quotient is two designs placed in one class whose outcomes differ beyond their determinism
class.

The bet behind the gate (BET-31, campaign quotienting) has further falsifiers, recorded with the decision: the
planted winner loses rank 1, or fewer than six of the top eight agree with the unquotiented arm. Its fallback is
deduplication by canonical form and content hash only.

The decision is its own bead so that it is made by a rule on recorded numbers, by a session that did not build
the digest, and so that beads on the "kept" branch can depend on it.

## Scope

In: the decision function, its inputs, the record (an architecture decision record and the kill gate's row in
`registries/gates.toml`), and the downstream changes on each branch. Out: the measurements (N17a-25, N17a-28) and
the mechanisms (BD-285, N17a-26).

## Technical approach

Inputs, by receipt id: the pilot record of the digest on the ballast fixture (N17a-25: pairs replayed, pairs
differing, the falsifier verdict); from the audit (N17a-28): part A (differing pairs among 10⁴ in-class pairs,
per back end, and the orbit-pair mismatches), part B (share of dispatches saved by digest classes on the campaign
corpus), part C (planted-winner rank in both arms, top-eight overlap, false equivalences, the coupled-bus
outcome); and the repair-cycle log if a false quotient was found.

Decision procedure:

1. **Pilot branch.** If the pilot's falsifier held (a merged ballast pair whose replays differ in any bit, or
   classes that do not coincide with the exact classes), the digest was never funded: N17a-26 and the digest part
   of BD-285 are closed as not funded and the kill is recorded on the pilot alone.
2. **Trigger 1, false quotient.** If part A found one or more differing pairs: the digest stopped serving that
   class kind at once; one repair cycle was allowed; part A was repeated in full. `fired_false = (differing pairs
   after the repair cycle) > 0`. A false quotient found in orbit pairs is treated the same way against the orbit
   transport rule, and if it survives a repair cycle it is escalated as a defect of the Solid part (it does not
   fire this gate; it blocks the gate GATE-PHYLUM-01).
3. **Trigger 2, saving.** `fired_saving = (pooled share of dispatches saved by digest classes) < 5%`.
4. **Bet falsifiers.** `bet_failed = planted winner rank ≠ 1 in the quotiented arm, or top-eight overlap < 6, or
   any false equivalence in part C, or the coupled-bus pair merged`. A bet failure is recorded with the decision
   and triggers the same fallback, because a quotient that removes a winner is worse than no quotient.
5. **Outcome.** `fired = pilot falsifier ∨ fired_false ∨ fired_saving ∨ bet_failed`.
6. **Record.** The decision record lists every input with its receipt id, each clause as a number and a boolean,
   the branch taken and the session that decided; the row of KILL-QUOTIENT in `registries/gates.toml` is set to
   decided with the measured values.

Downstream on **fired**: the crate flag `mission-digest` stays off; `phylum.quotient` reports orbit
representatives and similarity classes only and prints no digest classes; `phylum.sweep` and the cascade's first
stage dispatch orbit representatives only; the digest remains a memo key under the ordinary store policy
(bit-identical hits or not served, one hit in 64 recomputed in shadow) and no envelope makes a class-level claim;
the status table describes the capability as "orbit representatives". The invalidation contract for reuse after
edits is unaffected (it records dependency digests of fields read, which is the cache-key use). Family search in
mission queries keeps its orbit quotient and loses digest-based merging.

Downstream on **not fired**: digest classes are reported as deduplication with their measured saving; the audit
of 10⁴ pairs becomes a standing part of the conformance battery at each later milestone; the measured saving, not
a promised factor, enters campaign estimates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision record exists and cites every input receipt; each clause is evaluated to a number and a boolean.
- [ ] The row of KILL-QUOTIENT in `registries/gates.toml` reads decided, with measured values, commit, hardware
      fingerprint and seed.
- [ ] The decision was recorded by a session that did not implement BD-285 or N17a-26.
- [ ] On the fired branch: `frob phylum quotient --designs … --json` prints no digest classes and no class-level
      claim; a quotiented sweep dispatches orbit representatives only; the status table names the fallback.
- [ ] On the not-fired branch: the saving printed by `phylum.quotient` on the campaign corpus equals the audit's
      measured value, and the audit is registered in the battery.
- [ ] Replaying the decision function on the recorded inputs reproduces the recorded outcome.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `decide_false_quotient`: 0 differing pairs → not fired by trigger 1; 1 differing pair after the repair cycle →
  fired; 1 before and 0 after → not fired, with the repair recorded.
- `decide_saving`: 4.9% → fired; 5.0% → not fired by trigger 2.
- `decide_bet`: planted winner at rank 2 → fired; top-eight overlap 5 → fired; overlap 6 with rank 1 and no false
  equivalence → not fired by the bet clause.
- `decide_pilot_branch`: pilot falsifier held → fired, digest marked not funded.
- `orbit_mismatch_escalates`: an orbit-pair mismatch does not fire this gate and blocks GATE-PHYLUM-01 instead.
- `decision_replay`: recorded inputs reproduce the recorded booleans.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_kill_quotient.sh` reads the receipts from the ledger (`frob artifact read <id> --json`), runs
the decision function in replay mode through `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_gates -- KILL-QUOTIENT`,
compares its output with the decision record and the gate row, and then exercises the branch in force: on fired,
`frob phylum quotient --designs @fixture:ballast-24-8 --json` must print no digest classes; on not fired it must
print 24,546. It sources the `STD-E2E` helper and logs newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_kill_quotient/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: per line `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block, each input with its
value, threshold and receipt id, and the branch taken; the `summary` line names the kill gate and its outcome.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the thresholds themselves (zero false quotients; 5% of dispatches; rank 1; six of eight). Negative
controls, each rejected by the replay check: (1) a decision that counts orbit savings toward the 5% (the trigger
is on digest classes); (2) a decision taken on the ballast fixture's reduction of 30.0 in place of the campaign
corpus; (3) a decision record whose inputs do not resolve to receipts; (4) a decision that ignores a false
quotient found and "fixed" without repeating the audit. Determinism lane: the decision function is exact
arithmetic on recorded values (class D0).

## Considerations

- Firing is a likely and acceptable outcome: the digest's factor is 1 in general, and real briefs rarely contain
  designs that differ only in unread fields. The fallback loses little and keeps every exact guarantee.
- A false quotient is evidence that some kernel reads something outside the declared tables. Even on the fired
  branch that defect must be found and fixed, because the same undeclared read undermines the memo key.
- The M3 exit review requires every gate of M3 to have a recorded value and a caught negative control; this
  decision and GATE-PHYLUM-01's record are among them.
- Reopening needs a new decision record on new measurements (for example a campaign class with many label-only
  variants), never a relabelling.
