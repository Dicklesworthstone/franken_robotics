## Background

Campaign quotienting (crate `frob-phylum`, seed bead BD-285) removes duplicate work from a campaign in two exact
ways: one canonical representative per orbit of the observation-preserving symmetry group Γ_obs (Solid), and the
mission-observable digest (Frontier), a hash of exactly what the step plan and the task read, so that two designs
with equal digests under the same task, scenes, policy, kernels, tier and profile are one experiment. Two numbers
decide whether the digest is kept as a deduplication claim, and both are stated "on the campaign corpus":

- the kill gate KILL-QUOTIENT (decided at M3) fires if one false quotient survives a repair cycle, **or if digest
  classes save less than 5% of dispatches on the campaign corpus**; fallback: orbit representatives only, with
  the digest kept as a cache key and no claim;
- the bet on campaign quotienting (BET-31) is falsified by one false equivalence, a planted winner that loses
  rank 1, fewer than six of the top eight agreeing with the unquotiented arm, or the same 5% clause.

The plan uses the term and never defines the corpus. That is a defect with a known failure mode: a ratio measured
on an undefined set can be moved by choosing the set. The digest's factor is exactly 30.0 on the ballast fixture
(eight identical slugs in 24 collinear slots: 735,471 placements, 24,546 classes), and that factor is a property
of the fixture: the rule for this mechanism is that the factor is reported, not promised, and a fixture built
to have a large factor shows that the mechanism is exact and says nothing about the factor on a real brief,
where it may be 1. A corpus assembled after looking at class counts could put the measured saving anywhere
between those two. This bead therefore
defines the corpus, builds its first version, and freezes it by content hash before any saving is measured.

Terms. A *campaign* is one search over designs for one mission: an agent or an optimizer proposes sets of
designs and dispatches each set for evaluation. A *campaign design set* is the set of distinct designs of one
such dispatch together with the context the digest depends on. A *dispatch* here is one design evaluated; the
saving of a mechanism on a set is the share of its designs that need not be dispatched. The *design corpus* is
the separate, frozen list of about 200 single designs with planted defects, winners and single causes (BD-248,
registered by N24b-02); it supplies parents and planted winners to this corpus and is not itself a set of
campaign dispatches.

Rejected alternatives. (1) Using the design corpus as it stands: it holds single designs chosen to carry labels,
not the sets a campaign dispatches, and has no slot sweeps or edit lineages. (2) Waiting for recorded campaigns
of real agents: none exist before the kill gate is due at M3. (3) Letting the audit bead choose its sets: the
bead that measures would then also choose what it is measured on.

## Scope

In: the definition of a campaign design set and of the campaign corpus; the file format and identities; the four
source classes and the composition rule of version 1; the authoring of version 1; the freeze record and its
enforcement; the loader that resolves `@corpus:<set>` to a design list with its context; the per-set facts
computed at freeze. Out: the corpus registry, its loader and its freeze mechanism for single designs (N24b-02),
which this bead extends by one file kind; authoring the design corpus and its planted winners (BD-248); the
generator (BD-287) and the frozen corpus of structural edits (N17b-03), which are read; the quotient mechanisms
(BD-285, N17a-23, N17a-26); the measurements on this corpus (N17a-28) and the decision (N17a-29).

## Technical approach

**Campaign design set (DESIGN).** A record with: `name`; `designs`, a set of distinct `DesignId`s (two entries
with one `DesignId` are one entry: content-hash duplicates are removed before any quotient is counted, because
deduplication by canonical form and content hash is the fallback that exists without this mechanism); `context`
= (`TaskId`, scene family identifier, policy identity or `baseline`, kernel set version, tier, step profile);
`source` (one of the classes below, with what is needed to rebuild every member); `planted_winner` (absent, or
the blinded identity of one member); `private` (bool). `CampaignSetId` = BLAKE3 of the canonical bytes of
{designs sorted by `DesignId`, context identities, source record}.

**Source classes (DESIGN).** Each is a way campaigns produce design sets.

| Class | What it is | How a member is rebuilt |
|---|---|---|
| `slot-sweep` | k module types over the n slots of a symmetric body, full Cartesian product | Archetype, slot list, type list, labeling |
| `lever-grid` | One design with several continuous levers on a rational grid, levers the mission reads | Parent `DesignId` and the typed parameter edits |
| `generated` | A population from a registered generator at a stated seed and index range | (`GeneratorId`, seed, index) |
| `lineage` | The designs visited by an edit sequence from a design-corpus parent, edits drawn from the frozen corpus of structural edits | Parent and the ordered typed edits |

A later version may add the class `recorded` (design sets taken from the ledger of real campaigns, once the
co-design cascade runs them); a new version is a new corpus identity.

**Composition rule of version 1 (DESIGN; checked by the loader).**

1. At least 12 sets; at least two of each source class; between 2,000 and 20,000 designs in total, so that
   counting dispatches is host work and needs no device.
2. Two `slot-sweep` sets are fixed: three leg types on the six slots of the radial hexapod (3⁶ = 729 designs)
   under a task and sensor suite that leave the dihedral group of order 12, and the same sweep with a front-only
   sensor mounted, which leaves only the sagittal mirror.
3. At least four sets contain a planted winner taken from the design corpus's planted-winner entries of the
   set's class (a design whose top rank is on record from a full evaluation), under a blinded identity whose
   mapping is sealed in the manifest hash. One set contains the coupled-bus pair (two modules with identical
   local summaries on one current-limited bus), which must never be merged.
4. No set comes from a fixture family built to have a known large digest factor. The ballast family and any set
   tagged `fixture-family` are refused for this corpus kind; they remain known answers and fillers of the replay
   audit, never part of the saving.
5. Selection is blind to the mechanism under test: no candidate set is passed through `phylum.quotient` before
   the freeze. The authoring script is linted for calls to the quotient functions, and the freeze record states
   the rule.
6. The share of designs per source class is written in the manifest and printed beside every figure measured on
   the corpus.

**Files and identity.** `assets/corpus/<version>/campaigns.toml` beside the design corpus's files, read by a
loader extension `crates/frob-arena/src/corpus/campaigns.rs` (DESIGN). `CampaignCorpusId` = BLAKE3 of {version,
`CampaignSetId`s sorted}. A ledger record `campaign-corpus-frozen {CampaignCorpusId, date, actor}` is written
once per version. The loader refuses a file whose sets do not hash to the frozen identity, rebuilds every member
from its source record and refuses a member whose rebuilt `DesignId` differs. `@corpus:<set>` resolves a set
name or `CampaignSetId` to its design list and context; a private set is logged by identifier only.

**Facts computed at freeze, without the mechanism under test.** Per set: the number of designs; the validity of
every member under `design check`; for `slot-sweep` sets the orbit count from the cycle index
|Γ|⁻¹·Σ_{g ∈ Γ} k^c(g) (c(g) = number of cycles of g on the slots), computed with the group code of
`frob-algebra`. For the two fixed sets: (3⁶ + 2·3 + 2·3² + 3³ + 3·3⁴ + 3·3³)/12 = 1,104/12 = 92 representatives
under the dihedral group, and (3⁶ + 3³)/2 = 378 under the mirror alone. These are the expected dispatch counts
after orbit representatives; the digest's further saving is what the audit measures and is not predicted here.

**Order rule.** A receipt that reports a saving or a rank on this corpus cites a `CampaignCorpusId` whose freeze
record precedes the registration of that measurement's protocol. The kill decision reads only such receipts. A
second version made after a measurement needs a recorded reason, and the decision of record stays with the
version frozen before the first measurement.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `assets/corpus/v1/campaigns.toml` exists, loads, satisfies the six composition rules, and its manifest
      prints the share of designs per source class.
- [ ] Every member of every set rebuilds to its recorded `DesignId` from its source record, on Linux and macOS.
- [ ] `CampaignSetId` and `CampaignCorpusId` are identical across runs and platforms and independent of the order
      in which designs and sets are listed.
- [ ] The freeze record exists; a file edited after the freeze (one design swapped) is refused with the two
      identities named.
- [ ] A set tagged `fixture-family`, or the ballast family under any name (detected by its source record), is
      refused for this corpus kind.
- [ ] The two fixed sweeps hold 729 designs each, and their cycle-index counts are 92 and 378.
- [ ] At least four sets carry a planted winner with a sealed blinded identity; the coupled-bus pair is present in
      one set.
- [ ] The authoring script contains no call to the quotient mechanism (lint), and the freeze record says so.
- [ ] `@corpus:<set>` resolves from the command line for every set; a private set appears in logs by identifier
      only.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-arena corpus::campaigns`:

- `set_id_order_independent`: permuting the design list and the set list changes no identity.
- `duplicate_design_collapses`: a set listing one `DesignId` twice has one member and the same identity as the
  set listing it once.
- `rebuild_each_source_class`: one member of each class is rebuilt from its source record and hashes to its
  `DesignId`; a tampered edit list is refused.
- `composition_rules`: fixtures violating each of rules 1 to 4 are refused with the rule named (11 sets; one
  class with a single set; no planted winner; a ballast set).
- `frozen_identity_enforced`: a swapped member after the freeze is refused.
- `slot_sweep_counts`: cycle-index counts 92 (dihedral, order 12) and 378 (mirror) for k = 3, n = 6; and, as a
  cross-check of the formula, 86,185 and 500,500 for k = 10.
- `order_rule`: a receipt whose protocol registration precedes the freeze record of the corpus it cites is
  rejected by the gate loader.
- `authoring_lint`: a planted call to the quotient pipeline in the authoring script fails the lint.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_campaign_corpus.sh`, plain shell driving the real `frob` binary, idempotent, no seed beyond
those recorded in the sets. For every set it resolves `@corpus:<set>` (`frob design show` on a keyed sample of
32 members and `frob design check` on the same sample); for the two fixed sweeps it runs `frob algebra orbits`
with the stored group and compares the count with 92 and 378; it attempts to load the planted bad corpora of the
unit tests and expects the refusals. It sources the `STD-E2E` helper and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_campaign_corpus/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block (class
`Tested`, tier `static`, determinism `exact`, identities); `metric` events give sets, designs per source class
and the two orbit counts against their expected values; `assert` events carry the identities compared and, on
failure, the first differing member with its source record; the `summary` lists assertions passed and failed.
It claims no gate. Private sets are logged by identifier only.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 729 designs, 92 and 378 orbit representatives for the two fixed sweeps (exact); the composition
counts of the manifest (exact). Negative controls, each of which must be caught: (1) the ballast family added as
a set; (2) a member swapped after the freeze; (3) a planted winner without its sealed mapping; (4) an authoring
script that consults the quotient; (5) a receipt registered before the freeze. Determinism lanes: identities
twice on one machine, debug and release, Linux and macOS (class D0).

## Considerations

- The expected result on an honest corpus may be a digest saving below 5%, in which case KILL-QUOTIENT fires and
  orbit representatives remain. That outcome is acceptable and the corpus is not adjusted to avoid it.
- OPEN (owner: Phylum lead): what real campaigns dispatch is not known before agents run them. Fail-closed
  default: version 1 as composed here; without a frozen corpus the saving is recorded `not run`, the second
  trigger of the kill gate is undecided, and no deduplication claim is made for the digest. Recorded campaign
  sets are added in a later version when the cascade produces them.
- The `lever-grid` sets are expected to contain no digest duplicates at all, and are included for that reason:
  a corpus of sweeps and lineages alone would overstate how often real work repeats.
- The planted winners come from the design corpus, whose winners have their rank on record only from a full
  evaluation; where the design corpus has fewer than four usable winners at M3, the sets carry those that
  exist, rule 3 is recorded as unmet, and the rank clause of the bet is measured on the sets that have one.
- Size: at most 20,000 designs; building and checking the corpus is host work of minutes.
- Privacy and licence: sets built from private designs are flagged and never written to logs beyond their
  identifiers; the public sets use the shipped archetypes only.
