## Background

Phylum (crate `frob-phylum`) bets that many different robot trees can step in one batch as table data inside padded
size buckets (BET-18). The plan does not assume the bet holds. ADR-PHYLUM-05: a per-family-template arm competes in
a head-to-head, and the plan commits in advance to adopt it if it wins, because templates are the incumbent regime
plus per-design rows, and if they cover the mutations agents make and run faster, the table-driven cohort is the
wrong engine and should become a preview path. This experiment decides whether the table-driven cohort is the
engine, and its outcome rules are written before any data exists.

What mixing can gain was computed before any speed test. Against a comparator that runs D designs one after another
with W worlds each, the per-step ratio is D·(c_L + W·τ)/(c_L + Ω·D·W·τ), with τ the unpadded cost of a world-step,
c_L the launch or switching overhead and Ω the padding overhead; at D = 512 and Ω = 1.3 with illustrative values it
is about 40 at W = 16, 11 at W = 64, 3.5 at W = 256, 1.4 at W = 1,024 and 1.1 at W = 2,048. The benefit is occupancy
at few worlds per design. That comparator is the weakest fair one; a comparator that batches parameter variants of
one tree, compiles in parallel or keeps one template per family closes most of the gap, and the gap against it is
what this experiment measures.

GATE-PHYLUM-03 (Performance; TARGET; milestone M2): the head-to-head run and published with all four arms. For the
cohort to remain the engine: at least 0.70 of the harmonic mean of the homogeneous rates at padding waste of at most
25% on cohorts of at least 256 designs; a lower bound above 1 on the ratio against arm B for edit to first accepted
result at K ≤ 128; and at most eight buckets.

## Scope

In: registration, protocol, execution on both machines, statistics, the mechanical evaluation of the pre-declared
outcomes, the published report, the gate record.
Out: the arms' implementations (cohort: BD-170, BD-183, N17b-04, N17b-05; templates: BD-185; incumbents: N17b-09);
the workload and corpus (N17b-03); the architecture decision record and the downstream switch (N17b-10).

## Technical approach

**Arms.**

| Arm | What runs | Machines |
|---|---|---|
| A | Phylum mixed cohorts: model tables, size buckets, slot dispatch | Both |
| B (control) | Per-family templates: at most nine padded templates, one per family of the eighteen archetypes that share a maximal tree (the grouping is frozen with the arm), each a fixed maximal tree with lockable joints and per-design parameter tables (parameter variants only) | Both |
| C | An incumbent's mixed-topology batch, out of process, at its pinned version | Linux machine |
| D | The leading incumbent run design by design with warm caches, parallel compilation and per-world parameter batching wherever the tree is shared | Linux machine |

Variants reported beside arm A and not changing the outcome rules: arm A with limb-archetype kernels (measures 1 and
2) and arm E, arm A with row-class solve queues on (measure 2), each only if its own gate has passed.

**Workload.** The 512 topologies with contact tails and the sealed half of the mutation corpus (N17b-03);
K = 1, 8, 32 and 128 episodes per design.

**Measures.**

1. *Edit to first accepted result.* Wall time from the release of a block of unseen structural edits to a
   completed, accepted cohort: every child admitted, its K episodes complete and committed with scorecards and
   digests, no `Faulted` world. Blocks: ten blocks of 100 sealed edits; each block is run at each K by each arm,
   with every cache restored to the frozen post-warm-up snapshot before each cell. Computed on the subset of edits
   every compared arm can express, and reported also on each arm's own subset.
2. *Throughput.* World-steps per second relative to a homogeneous batch of each bucket's largest design. Gate
   statistic: ρ_mix / H, where ρ_mix is the mixed population's rate and H = (Σ_b w_b/ρ_b)⁻¹ is the harmonic mean
   over buckets b of the homogeneous rate ρ_b of that bucket's largest design at the same world count, weighted by
   the share w_b of worlds in the bucket. Measured at population sizes of 256 and 512 designs. Steps of worlds
   marked `Degraded` or `Faulted` are not in any numerator.
3. *Padding overhead, bucket count, and the share of the mutation corpus each arm can express.* Padding waste is
   Ω₁ − 1 with Ω₁ = L_max/E[L] inside a bucket (L in degrees of freedom); the bucket count is the number the planner
   needs to hold waste at or below 25%.
4. *Digest invariance.* The record of GATE-PHYLUM-02 (N17b-06).
5. *Run-to-run digests of the mixed batch for arm C* under each of its solver's deterministic modes that accepts a
   heterogeneous batch; if none does, that is recorded as the finding.

**Statistics (a fixed-n study).** The sample size is fixed in advance: at least ten timed repetitions per cell
after warm-up; cold runs kept apart; the arms of a pair run back to back in randomized order and interleaved in
time; a 20-minute sustained load precedes measurement on the Apple machine and host load is recorded on the Linux
one. Every ratio is the one-sided 95% lower confidence bound of a paired ratio: with ℓᵢ = ln(aᵢ/bᵢ), mean ℓ̄ and
standard deviation s over n pairs, the bound is exp(ℓ̄ − t·s/√n), t = 1.833 for n = 10. The verdict is computed
once, at n; outcomes may be looked at before n and nothing is decided on them. A threshold applies to the bound,
never to the mean. Every number carries the disclosure block (hardware and driver, versions, world count, step and
decimation, iterations, capacities, cold or warm with cache state, buckets, variants per bucket and padding
fraction, thermal state, host load, determinism class, pairs, comparator revision).

**Registration, before any timed run (the six parts of a method experiment).**

1. *Ceiling.* From the measured switching cost and world-step cost of the pilot (N17b-01) the mixing-ratio bound is
   computed per K; if the ceiling for a ratio is below the value the gate needs, that cell is refused as a speed
   test and recorded so.
2. *Competing arms.* The mechanism (A), the strongest conventional alternative at equal resources (D), the
   mechanism's own fallback (B), and the incumbent that hosts the same method (C).
3. *Potency control.* A planted slowdown of 1.5 in one arm must be detected; an A/A cell must give a bound at or
   below 1.02.
4. *Paired lower bound.* As above; ten pairs of constant ratio r must return r.
5. *Pre-declared fallback.* The outcome rules below, with KILL-PHYLUM.
6. *Reachability pre-analysis.* The list of verdicts the rules can return at n = 10 and the power at declared
   alternatives (true ratios of 0.5, 0.7, 0.86 for throughput; 0.8, 1.0, 1.25 for the ratio against arm B), hashed
   into the study record. A verdict that cannot be reached at n is not promised.

The study is registered as a fixed-n study in the ledger with the workload and corpus identities, the freeze
manifests of arms C and D, the compute-envelope decision and the pre-analysis hash.

**Pre-declared outcome rules (evaluated mechanically on the published numbers).**

| Rule | Condition | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| R1 gate, throughput | Lower bound of ρ_mix/H at least 0.70 at 256 and at 512 designs, with padding waste at most 25% | Clause met |
| R2 gate, edit to result | Lower bound of T_B/T_A above 1 at every K ∈ {1, 8, 32, 128} | Clause met |
| R3 gate, buckets | At most eight buckets | Clause met |
| K1 kill | Lower bound of ρ_mix/H below 0.5 on 512 designs, after at most one optimization pass | KILL-PHYLUM fires |
| K2 kill | More than eight buckets needed | KILL-PHYLUM fires |
| K3 kill | Arm B expresses at least 95% of the mutation corpus and wins measure 1: lower bound of T_A/T_B above 1 at every K ≤ 128 | KILL-PHYLUM fires: templates become the engine; the table-driven cohort becomes an admission and preview path |
| W1 withdrawal | Arm C ties or beats A on measures 1 and 2: on neither is the lower bound of A's advantage over C above 1 | The status table says so; the two performance differentiators (throughput; time from an edit to the first GPU step) are withdrawn; the cohort verbs are kept for replay of one world outside its batch, deterministic forks and a Metal tier |

The gate is PASS when R1, R2 and R3 hold and all four arms are published. If no kill fires and a gate clause fails
(for example a throughput bound between 0.5 and 0.70, or a split across K against arm B), the gate is recorded FAIL
with the clause, the cohort is labelled below tolerance, and the case goes to the decision bead and the milestone
exit review; nothing is promoted by default. KILL-BUCKET, the device-side statement of the same decision (more than
eight buckets to hold cubic-stage overhead at or below 2, or the three-body-plan mixed batch below 0.5 after one
optimization pass), defers to KILL-PHYLUM: its verdict is printed beside K1 and K2 and the record of KILL-PHYLUM is
the decision of record.

**The fail-closed variant.** If the compute envelope is not approved (N17b-07), only the pilot-scale protocol runs:
64 topologies × 64 worlds × 1,000 steps on one device within one hour, with the same arms, statistics and rules.
The report's first line then says that the outcome was decided on the pilot alone, and the two-machine rows are
`not run`. If arms C or D were not frozen (N17b-08), their columns print `not run` with the reason and the
publication clause of the gate is not met.

**Publication.** A report under `docs/` with one table per measure, all four arm columns, the per-stratum breakdown,
the disclosure blocks, the outcome rules with their inputs and verdicts, and the row for the project's status
table; the same content as a ledger record; one row in `registries/gates.toml` citing the receipt (commit,
hardware fingerprints, seed, identities).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The study is registered before the first timed run with all six parts; the registration hash precedes every result file.
- [ ] Seed done-condition, part 1: GATE-PHYLUM-03 is published with all four arms (or with `not run` columns and their reasons, in which case the gate is recorded as not passed on the publication clause), with measured values against 0.70, 1 and eight buckets.
- [ ] Seed done-condition, part 2: the KILL-PHYLUM and KILL-BUCKET verdicts are computed by the pre-declared rules K1 to K3 and printed with the clause that fired or the statement that none did; the record is handed to N17b-10.
- [ ] Rule W1 is evaluated and its consequence, if any, is written into the status-table row.
- [ ] Every ratio is a paired 95% lower bound from at least ten pairs; every number has a complete disclosure block; cold runs appear only in their own table.
- [ ] The potency control is detected and the A/A cell is at or below 1.02.
- [ ] Measure 5 is on file for arm C.
- [ ] The variants (limb-archetype kernels, row-class queues) are reported or marked `not run`; they change no rule.
- [ ] Under the default envelope the pilot-scale variant is run and labelled.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-phylum --test head_to_head`)

- `harmonic_reference`: buckets with world shares (0.5, 0.3, 0.2) and homogeneous rates (4.0, 2.0, 1.0) M world-steps/s give H = 1/(0.125 + 0.15 + 0.2) = 2.105 M; a mixed rate of 1.6 M gives 0.76.
- `paired_bound`: ten pairs of constant ratio 0.8 → 0.8; ten ratios alternating 0.7 and 0.9 → a bound below 0.8 (0.735 to three figures).
- `rules_truth_table`: synthetic inputs at the boundaries: throughput bound 0.70 → R1 met, 0.699 → not met, 0.499 → K1; buckets 8 → R3 met, 9 → K2; share 0.95 with B faster at all K → K3; share 0.949 → no K3; B faster at three of four K → neither R2 nor K3 (recorded as split).
- `withdrawal_rule`: A's advantage over C bounded above 1 on measure 2 only → W1 does not apply; on neither → W1 applies.
- `reachability_enumeration`: the rule set returns exactly the enumerated verdicts on a grid of inputs, and each is reached.
- `mixing_ratio_ceiling`: 40.6, 11.3, 3.46, 1.44, 1.11 at W = 16, 64, 256, 1,024, 2,048.
- `not_run_columns`: a report with arm C `not run` renders and marks the publication clause unmet.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_head_to_head.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_head_to_head/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. It verifies the registration, restores cache
snapshots, and for each cell and repetition runs arm A through
`frob phylum sweep --designs <block> --worlds-per-design <K> --task <task> --tier <tier> --seed <s> --json`, arm B
through the template environments of BD-185, and arms C and D through the runners of N17b-09, in randomized,
interleaved order. Each timed repetition is a `cmd` event with the exact command, exit code, wall time and (for arms
A and B) the envelope's `evidence` block; `metric` events carry each measure with its unit, the paired bound and its
threshold; `assert` events evaluate R1 to R3, K1 to K3 and W1; on failure the log gives the first clause that
failed and the cells behind it. The `summary` lists assertions and
`gates: ["GATE-PHYLUM-03", "KILL-PHYLUM", "KILL-BUCKET"]`. A `--pilot` mode runs the 64 × 64 × 1,000 protocol; a
`--smoke` mode runs eight designs on `batch-cpu` with two repetitions and claims nothing.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-372 (the mixing ratios) and KA-371 (the worked card: 14.8 ms against 5.0 ms at 8,192 worlds; crossover at 360 worlds) check the ceiling arithmetic. KA-375 is measure 4.
- Negative controls: the planted 1.5 slowdown (must be detected); the A/A cell; a ratio submitted without a paired bound must be refused as a performance ratio; a figure without a disclosure block must be refused; a speed claim made only against the sequential comparator must not be printed as the headline (CX-162).
- Determinism lanes: workload, block order and seeds are identical across repetitions (asserted by hash); arm A's digests under each repetition equal the GATE-PHYLUM-02 record.

## Considerations

- All three differentiators can be lost here; that is the purpose. RISK-29 and the pre-mortem entry "mixed batches lost to per-family templates" name the early signals: the M1 pilot of padding overhead and bucket count, and the share of the corpus arm B expresses.
- The readings fixed in this bead where the plan's wording leaves room: "wins at K ≤ 128" means at every tested K; the homogeneous reference is each bucket's largest design, so the throughput ratio isolates mixing overhead and padding is bounded separately by the waste clause; "cohorts of at least 256 designs" means populations of 256 and 512 designs. They are frozen in the registration and may be amended by the Phylum lead before it, never after.
- The plan states a pass at 0.70 and a kill below 0.5 and is silent in between; this bead routes that case to a recorded FAIL and a decision, not to a default.
- Compute: the full experiment needs the envelope class for a named gate measurement, repeated per arm (open question OPEN-PHYLUM-3).
- The Apple machine's throughput at these shapes is unmeasured; its rows are measurements of arms A and B only.
