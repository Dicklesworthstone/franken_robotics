## Background

A design campaign should not simulate the same closed-loop system twice, and it must never serve a result that an
edit has made stale. Phylum (crate `frob-phylum`) owns three exact mechanisms against duplicate work: orbit
representatives under the observation-preserving symmetry group, the mission-observable digest (both in seed bead
BD-285), and the **invalidation contract**, this bead (tier Solid). The contract: every memoized result records the
digest of the fields it read; after an edit a result is reused exactly when that digest is unchanged, and otherwise
recomputed.

The contract is hygiene and an evidence rule, not a speed lever. FACT (about the phase shares of published loops;
HYPOTHESIS for this system until its phase ledger exists): rollouts and training are at least nine tenths of
per-design compute and depend on every mass and geometry parameter, so exact reuse after a dynamics-affecting edit
is at most about a tenth of compute. What the contract buys is that a reused verdict is as good as a recomputed one
and that an agent is told which evidence an edit destroyed. The governing decision is ADR-PHYLUM-06: exact sharing
only; a quantity is shared between designs when all of its inputs and rounding rules agree, and a nearby trajectory
is never reused, because a smooth distance in design space gives no right to reuse contact history.

Terms. Γ_obs is the subgroup of a robot's mechanical symmetry group that also preserves every sensor pose and field
of view, the payload, the task's goal orientation and the terrain's symmetry. A *distributional* result is a
statistic over a world distribution (a scorecard, a verdict); a *per-address* result belongs to one world address.
*Influence records* are the compiler's per-product sets of source paths read, at field granularity.

## Scope

This seed bead is the capstone of the invalidation contract: it depends on the component beads that build its
parts and is finished when they are and the gate is recorded.

In: the reuse decision for an edit and across designs; what survives and what does not; the use of orbit
transport inside that decision (which grade a result reused across an orbit receives, and when it is refused);
the positive control and the three negative controls; the 10⁴ random-edit equivalence campaign; GATE-PHYLUM-05.
Out, in component beads this bead depends on: dependency digests, shadow recompute and quarantine (N17b-13);
the transport mechanism itself: classifying a stored result as distributional, per-address or paired by
address, moving it by a group element, mapping addresses, and the rule that voids address pairing (N17a-24; the
enum variants `OrbitDistributional` and `OrbitAddressMapped` below name its two outcomes and are not a second
implementation); the mission-observable digest with its memo key (N17a-26) and orbit enumeration (N17a-23),
both reached through BD-285.
Out, elsewhere: the verb's surface and end-to-end script (N17b-14, which depends on this bead); verdict
revalidation with assumption polarity (the revalidation engine, BD-292), of which this contract is the identity
and dependency grades.

## Technical approach

**Types (DESIGN).**

```rust
pub enum Licence {
    Identity,                         // the result's full key is unchanged
    Dependency,                       // every changed field lies outside the result's read set
    OrbitDistributional { g: GroupElem },   // d′ = g·d, g ∈ Γ_obs, result is distributional
    OrbitAddressMapped { g: GroupElem },    // d′ = g·d, per-address result, address mapped through g
}
pub enum ReuseVerdict {
    Survives { licence: Licence },
    Invalidated { forced_by: FieldPath, also: Vec<FieldPath> },
    Refused { code: RefusalCode },    // FROB-REF-162: no dependency digest, or the node kind is quarantined
}
```

**Decision procedure for an edit d → d′ and a cached result ρ of d (numbered).**

1. If ρ has no dependency digest, or its node kind is quarantined: `Refused` with `FROB-REF-162`; ρ is not served.
2. Recompute the digest over d′ restricted to ρ's read set, with ρ's non-design identities unchanged. If it equals
   ρ's digest: `Survives`, with licence `Identity` when the full key is unchanged and `Dependency` otherwise.
3. Otherwise, if d′ = g·d for an element g of Γ_obs (a mirror swap, a rotation of a radial body): a distributional
   result over a world distribution that g preserves `Survives` with `OrbitDistributional`; a per-address result
   `Survives` with `OrbitAddressMapped` only where the scene generator maps the address through the group action,
   using the rule that the design g·d in world w behaves as d does in world g⁻¹·w; a comparison paired by address
   against a third design is invalidated and re-run.
4. Otherwise `Invalidated`, with `forced_by` the first field of the read set, in canonical path order, whose value
   differs, and `also` the others.

**What survives (must be reproduced by tests).** Replacing a leaf module keeps its siblings' local analyses,
derivations and kernels: their read sets lie inside the sibling subterms. An edit to a field the mission does not
read (a label, a visual mesh) keeps everything.

**What does not.** An edit that touches a shared battery bus, a shared thermal sink, a kinematic loop across
branches, or any mass or geometry of a robot in contact invalidates every episode-level result of that design,
because the floating body couples all of it: every episode reads the whole lowered table. A substitution is
leaf-local only when the replacement has the same port signature, the replaced module is a leaf with no closure to
another branch, it reaches shared resources only through its declared electrical and thermal ports with their
assumptions still met, and each reused product read only fields outside the replaced subterm; anything else is
global and invalidates everything downstream of the model identity.

**Keys for coupled resources.** A local summary without time correlation is not a sound key. A result that depends
on a shared bus is keyed on the time-resolved demand profiles of every load on that bus, never on per-module peak
summaries.

**Controls.**

- *Positive control.* A mirror swap under an element of Γ_obs must reuse every distributional result, and every
  per-address result after the address mapping.
- *Negative control 1.* A shared-bus edit must invalidate.
- *Negative control 2.* A reversed port sign must be refused (at composition, with the design language's refusal
  for a reversed sign or an algebraic loop); it must never reach the reuse decision as a valid design.
- *Negative control 3, the coupled bus.* Two fingers with identical local summaries on a bus limited to one unit
  of current have no feasible one-tick schedule (both would draw in the same tick: 2 > 1) and two feasible two-tick
  schedules (A then B; B then A). A contract that adds peak demands prunes a feasible design.

**The equivalence campaign.** 10⁴ random edit sequences, seeds 0 to 9,999, each of 4 to 12 typed edits drawn from
renames, parameter and material edits, sensor moves, leaf module swaps, mount moves, bus and battery edits, payload
edits, mirror swaps under Γ_obs and edit-then-inverse pairs, on a small modular quadruped family. Tracked results:
three static analyses, the step-plan identity, a 32-step episode digest per design on tier `batch-cpu`, and a
four-world scorecard. After every edit the tracked results are obtained twice, through the contract and by full
recomputation with memoization off, and compared byte for byte. The campaign also reports the share reused per
edit class, so that a pass is not vacuous: label edits must reuse everything and mass edits must reuse no rollout.

**Evidence.** A reuse verdict is exact (determinism class D0 for digests; episodes compared inside class D2 on one
back end). In reports the reused compute is the "exact reuse" column of the reuse ledger, never added to evidence
carried forward or to training avoided by warm starts.

**Layout.** `crates/frob-phylum/src/reuse/{contract.rs, orbit.rs, campaign.rs}`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: GATE-PHYLUM-05 is recorded with measured values: the positive control reuses (every distributional result; every per-address result after mapping); each of the three negative controls invalidates or refuses as stated; and 10⁴ random edit sequences give results byte-equal to full recomputation, with 0 mismatches.
- [ ] INV-PHYLUM-04 holds: no result is served whose dependency digest changed; the campaign's instrumentation counts zero such servings.
- [ ] A leaf module swap reuses the siblings' local analyses and kernels and invalidates every rollout and every whole-robot certificate, with `forced_by` naming a field of the swapped subterm.
- [ ] A rename and a visual-mesh edit reuse everything; a mass edit on a robot in contact reuses no rollout.
- [ ] An edit followed by its inverse restores every result with zero evaluations (metamorphic relation MR-10).
- [ ] An unmapped per-address result offered across an orbit is not served; a comparison paired by address against a third design is re-run.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` documents the procedure, the licences, the controls and the statement that the contract is not a speed lever.
- [ ] The gate receipt names commit, seed range and fixture identities; the script writes no gate record, and GATE-PHYLUM-05 is recorded from the receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-PHYLUM-05 --receipt <hash>` in a session that did not implement the contract (N38b-19).

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-phylum reuse::contract`)

- `rename_keeps_everything`: every tracked result `Survives { Identity or Dependency }`; zero recomputations.
- `leaf_swap_keeps_siblings`: swapping one leg module keeps the other three legs' workspace analyses and inverse-kinematics kernels; mass roll-up, symmetry groups, budgets and every episode are invalidated.
- `mass_edit_invalidates_rollouts`: thigh shell 2 mm → 2.5 mm: every episode `Invalidated`, `forced_by` a path under the edited body.
- `shared_bus_edit_invalidates` (negative control 1): 6-cell → 8-cell pack: every envelope, thermal and episode result invalidated.
- `reversed_port_sign_refused` (negative control 2): composition refuses; the reuse function is never called.
- `coupled_bus_not_pruned` (negative control 3): enumeration gives 0 one-tick and 2 two-tick schedules; a key from summed peaks is rejected by the key-derivation test.
- `mirror_swap_reuses` (positive control): d′ = g·d with g the sagittal mirror in Γ_obs: scorecards and verdicts reused; per-address results equal those of d at g⁻¹·w after transport; with a front-only sensor mounted and g a rotation outside Γ_obs, nothing is reused by orbit.
- `unmapped_address_not_served`: with a scene generator that cannot map addresses, per-address results are recomputed.
- `edit_inverse_zero_evaluations` (MR-10): edit then inverse; evaluation counter stays at zero.
- `forced_by_is_first_differing_path`: two fields changed; `forced_by` is the first in canonical order and `also` holds the second.
- `undigested_result_refused_162` (step 1): a cached scorecard stored without a dependency digest → `Refused { FROB-REF-162 }` and the result is not served even when the edit is a rename; the same scorecard with a digest but its node kind quarantined by N17b-13 → `Refused { FROB-REF-162 }`; after the quarantine is lifted → `Survives`.

### End-to-end script and logging

The capability's script is `scripts/e2e/phylum_reuse.sh`, owned by N17b-14 (the verb `phylum.reuse`, which
depends on this bead; this bead's script therefore does not call that verb). This bead adds
`scripts/e2e/gate_phylum_05.sh` (sources the helper of `STD-E2E`; log
`target/e2e-logs/gate_phylum_05/<utc>.ndjson`): it runs the four controls through `cargo test -p frob-phylum --test
reuse_controls` and the campaign through `FROB_REUSE_SEEDS=0..10000 cargo test -p frob-phylum --release --test
reuse_campaign` (the seed range is read from the environment, because the test harness refuses an unknown
`--seeds` argument), logging per control a `cmd` event (command, exit code, wall time, `evidence` block), per 1,000 sequences a `metric`
event (sequences done, mismatches with threshold 0, share reused per edit class), and `assert` events for each
clause of the gate. The campaign writes every verdict (seed, edit index, result, survives / invalidated with
`forced_by` / refused) to `target/reuse_campaign/verdicts.ndjson`, the record N17b-14's script replays through the
verb. So that the user's path is exercised and not only the library, the script then replays sequences 0 to 99
edit by edit through the real binary, with registered inputs only: `frob design edit <d> <edit…> --key
g05-<run>-<seq>-<i>-edit --json`; the static analyses with the memo graph serving, `frob design show <d′> --products
--json`, against the same call with `design.show`'s `--no-memo`; the 32-step episodes and the four-world scorecard
with the memo graph serving, `frob phylum sweep --designs <d′> --worlds-per-design 4 --task flat-stand --tier
batch-cpu --seed <s> --key g05-<run>-<seq>-<i>-sweep --json`, against the episode digests recomputed by `frob sim
run --tier batch-cpu --designs <d′> --worlds-per-design 4 --task flat-stand --steps 32 --seed <s> --key
g05-<run>-<seq>-<i>-solo --json` (the sweep's task, since the world addresses and the environment depend on it; the
scorecard's recomputation is the campaign's, above). `design.edit` is a `mutate` verb and the other
two are run verbs: every call carries its own key (`<run>` names this invocation; a call without one is
`FROB-REF-805`), and every envelope must show `idempotent_replay: false`, since a replayed sweep would report a
stored result as recomputed. It asserts that the tracked results are byte-equal between the two paths after every
edit and that the products and results the envelopes mark as reused (`memo.served` true in the cost block's
`memo: { hit, served, shadowed, key }`, N28b-38) are exactly those the campaign's record lists as surviving. On a mismatch it logs
the seed, the edit index, the result kind, both digests and the first differing byte, with the path of the saved
sequence for replay. The `summary` names `gates: ["GATE-PHYLUM-05"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-377 (mirror swap under Γ_obs: every distributional result reused; per-address results reused after mapping), KA-378 (coupled bus: zero one-tick and two two-tick schedules; the design is feasible), KA-379 (10⁴ random edit sequences equal full recomputation; the positive control reuses; a shared-bus edit invalidates; a reversed port sign is refused).
- Planted failures the gate must catch: a shared-bus edit reused; an unmapped per-address result reused; pruning by summed peak demand; a memo key without delivery time (the control of MR-10).
- Counterexamples: CX-155 (peak-demand summaries), CX-156 (orbit members have equal distributions, not equal outcomes at equal addresses), CX-159 (reuse is not a speed lever).
- Determinism lanes: the campaign twice on one machine and across worker counts (identical digests); debug and release; Linux and macOS for the analyses (class D0).

## Considerations

- The contract is only as complete as the influence records. A product that reads a field without recording it is the failure the shadow recompute and the live-dependency tests of N17b-13 exist to find.
- Orbit transport depends on Γ_obs being right. Γ_obs is recomputed after every edit that can break or restore a symmetry; a stale group would license a false reuse, which is why the positive control includes a case that must not reuse.
- KILL-QUOTIENT (the digest quotient saving less than 5% of dispatches, or a false quotient surviving a repair cycle) does not remove this contract: its fallback keeps orbit representatives and keeps the digest as a cache key with no claim.
- The broader kill gate on reuse by implication belongs to the revalidation engine; this contract is the part that stays in every outcome (identity and dependency grades).
- Privacy: dependency digests are hashes; they never carry scene content into logs.
