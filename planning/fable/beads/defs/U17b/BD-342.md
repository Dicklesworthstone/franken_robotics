## Background

A population dispatch returns a map, not a winner. Each `phylum.sweep` updates a quality-diversity map (a
MAP-Elites grid or a centroidal tessellation over behaviour descriptors such as mass, speed, energy per metre and
footprint), and the envelope carries the cells that changed, their elites with provisional scores, the count per
cell and the coverage of descriptor space. An agent's turn then yields many different good designs instead of one
optimum, which is how fewer serial rounds are bought. Mutations are typed edits, including adding and removing a
limb, and one generation is one mixed batch under the class's family policy. This is Frontier work in crate
`frob-phylum`, surfaced through `phylum.map` and through `opt.archive` of the optimization cluster.

Three statements from the chapter govern the bead.

- *Merging.* A cell keeps the maximum under a total order (score, then `DesignId`), so merging maps is commutative,
  associative and idempotent, and maps from two cards or two agents combine in any order.
- *Bias.* A population scored under one shared policy drifts toward designs that policy steers easily. The envelope
  therefore reports descriptor coverage beside quality, and elites stay `provisional` until the rank-agreement
  record permits selection in their stratum.
- *Ceiling.* FACT (published figures, to be re-read): population co-design reports end-to-end compute gains of
  about three to four, and batched black-box search cuts rounds almost linearly in batch size up to about 128.
  HYPOTHESIS: campaigns on the benchmark need at most half the serial rounds of one-design-per-turn search. For
  parametric families this is not engine-specific; an incumbent batches the same sweep.

GATE-PHYLUM-08 (Design comparison; TARGET; milestone M4): on the benchmark, campaigns reach the acceptance target
in at most half the serial rounds of one-design-per-turn search at equal compute, with compute reported beside
rounds.

The parts are separate beads: the map type and its laws (N17b-17), the per-dispatch update (N17b-29), storage and
the two verbs (N17b-18), and the paper pilot that must not meet its falsifier (N17b-16). This seed bead assembles
them into the campaign capability and closes the gate.

## Scope

In: the campaign loop that uses maps per dispatch (a round driver over the existing verbs); the two-agent archive
test; the gate experiment, its registration and record.
Out: the optimizer and racing proper (BD-341) and the six-stage cascade (BD-352), which consume the archive;
rank-agreement measurement (BD-328); promotion of elites.

## Technical approach

**The campaign loop (orchestration only; every number comes from the verbs it calls).**

1. Read the campaign map (`opt.archive`).
2. Propose a generation: parents drawn uniformly over filled cells; children by typed edits (parameter moves on
   declared levers, module swaps, a limb added or removed), each child passing preflight.
3. One `phylum.sweep` of the generation under one shared controller, with `campaign` set: the envelope's
   `map_delta` says which cells changed.
4. Candidates for confirmation are taken only from strata whose selection standing permits it; where the standing
   is `discard-only`, `not-ranked` or `unmeasured`, every candidate that would be accepted is first re-scored on
   the higher rung, and the cost is charged to the campaign.
5. Stop when the benchmark's acceptance rule accepts a design for the brief, or the budget ends.

A *serial round* is one pass through steps 1 to 4: one turn of an agent or of the scripted driver.

**The two-agent archive test.** Two agents in two namespaces run disjoint generations of one campaign on the two
cards; their archives are merged in both orders; the merged artifacts must be byte-identical and equal to the
campaign map rebuilt from the dispatch records.

**The gate experiment (a registered method experiment; fixed-n study).**

- *Arms.* M: the loop above with a generation of B designs per round (B fixed in the registration; default 64 at 16
  worlds each). S: one design per turn, evaluated on B × 16 worlds, with the same proposal rule, the same shared
  controller and the same confirmation rule. Compute per round is equal by construction; total compute per brief is
  capped equally and reported beside rounds for both arms, confirmation and higher-rung re-scoring included.
- *Briefs.* At least ten briefs of the design benchmark, fixed in the registration; arms are paired by brief and
  seed.
- *Outcome.* Rounds to the first accepted design, restricted at the round cap, a number of rounds per brief fixed
  in the registration before the first run and equal for both arms (a campaign that does not reach acceptance
  contributes the cap and is counted as censored).
- *Statistic and threshold.* The paired ratio rounds(M)/rounds(S), with its one-sided 95% upper confidence bound
  from the log-ratios: exp(ℓ̄ + t·s/√n), where ℓᵢ = ln(rounds_M,i / rounds_S,i) for brief-and-seed pair i, ℓ̄ and s
  are the mean and sample standard deviation of the n values ℓᵢ, and t is the 0.95 quantile of Student's t with
  n − 1 degrees of freedom (1.833 at n = 10). PASS requires the bound at or below 0.5, with the compute of both
  arms printed in the same table.
- *The six parts written before the first run.* Ceiling: the ratio cannot be below 1/B, and the published
  end-to-end figure of three to four bounds what compute parity can show. Competing arms: M, S, and the fallback
  (the archive as a reporting surface). Potency control: a planted brief whose accepted design is one edit from
  the root must be solved by both arms in round 1; an A/A pair (S against S) must give a bound that includes 1.
  Paired bound: as above; constant ratios return themselves. Pre-declared fallback: below. Reachability
  pre-analysis: with n briefs and the round cap, the verdicts reachable (pass, fail, undecided because censoring
  exceeds a registered share) are enumerated with the power at true ratios of 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75.
- *Agent-driven variant.* Where the benchmark's agent harness exists, the same two arms are run with an agent in
  place of the scripted proposal rule; it is reported beside the scripted result and does not replace it.

**Pre-declared fallback.** The chapter names no kill gate for this mechanism. If the pilot's falsifier held, or the
gate's bound is above 0.5, the claim about serial rounds is withdrawn: the maps, the delta and both verbs remain as
reporting and archive surfaces, the status table says the lever was not shown, and no performance or
design-comparison claim cites maps per dispatch.

**Recording.** The registration, the per-brief table (rounds, compute, censoring, accepted design identities), the
statistic and the verdict in the ledger; one row in `registries/gates.toml` citing the receipt (commit, hardware
fingerprint, seeds, benchmark identity). The script writes the hash-named receipt and no gate record; this bead
records GATE-PHYLUM-08 from it with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-PHYLUM-08
--receipt <hash>`, in a session that did not implement the loop (N38b-19).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition, part 1: GATE-PHYLUM-08 is recorded with measured values: the upper 95% bound of rounds(M)/rounds(S) over at least ten paired briefs against the threshold 0.5, with compute per arm beside rounds.
- [ ] Seed done-condition, part 2: merging two agents' archives in both orders gives identical bytes, and the result equals the campaign map rebuilt from dispatch records.
- [ ] Every round's envelope carries the map delta with coverage beside quality; no elite is used for selection in a stratum whose standing does not permit it without higher-rung re-scoring.
- [ ] The experiment was registered with its six parts before the first run, and the pilot's verdict (N17b-16) is cited.
- [ ] The potency control is solved in round 1 by both arms and the A/A pair's bound includes 1.
- [ ] Accepted designs are accepted by the benchmark's rule, not by provisional map scores.
- [ ] If the gate fails, the fallback text is in the status table and the verbs' contracts carry no claim about rounds.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-phylum` lists the map capability with its evidence class (provisional elites; a design-comparison gate).

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-phylum --test maps_campaign`)

- `generation_is_one_sweep`: a generation of 64 children results in exactly one `phylum.sweep` job and one map delta.
- `selection_respects_standing`: an elite in an `unmeasured` stratum is re-scored on the higher rung before confirmation; one in a `select` stratum is not.
- `round_counter`: rounds increase by one per pass; a confirmation that fails does not end the campaign.
- `upper_bound_formula`: ten pairs of constant ratio 0.4 → bound 0.4; ratios alternating 0.3 and 0.5 → a bound above their geometric mean 0.387 and below 0.5 (0.453 to three figures).
- `censoring_accounting`: campaigns hitting the cap contribute the cap and raise the censored share.
- `two_agent_merge_bytes`: two archives built from disjoint dispatch sets merge to identical bytes in both orders.
- `equal_compute_per_round`: arm S's round costs B × 16 worlds, arm M's B designs × 16 worlds; the ledger's compute columns agree within 1%.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/gate_phylum_08.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes
`target/e2e-logs/gate_phylum_08/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. For each brief and seed it runs both arms through
the real verbs (`frob opt archive --campaign <id> --json`, a single-kind read verb with no action word;
`frob design edit`, `frob phylum sweep --campaign …`, the benchmark's acceptance verb; for the two-agent merge
`frob phylum map --merge <A> <B> --json` and `frob phylum map --merge <B> <A> --json` in two stores, then
`frob phylum map --campaign <id> --json` for the map rebuilt from the dispatch records). `design.edit` is a `mutate`
verb and `phylum.sweep` a run verb: every edit and every generation's sweep carries its own key,
`--key g08-<run>-<brief>-<seed>-<arm>-<round>-<i>` (`<run>` names this invocation; a call without one is
`FROB-REF-805`), never shared between arms, rounds or briefs, and every envelope must show `idempotent_replay:
false`, since a replayed sweep would return a stored map delta and make a round cost nothing. It logs per round a
`cmd` event for every command (exact line, exit code, wall time, `evidence` block) and a `metric` event (round index, cells changed, coverage, compute spent). At the end it logs per brief the
rounds and compute of both arms, the paired ratio and bound against 0.5, the censored share, and `assert` events
for the gate, the controls and the two-agent merge (with artifact digests). On failure it names the briefs whose
ratio exceeded the threshold and the rounds at which each arm stalled. `summary`: `gates: ["GATE-PHYLUM-08"]`. A
`--smoke` mode runs one synthetic brief for three rounds on `batch-cpu` and claims nothing.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-386 (merge commutative, associative, idempotent) through two agents' archives.
- Negative controls: the A/A pair; the planted one-edit brief; a planted driver that counts a generation of 64 as 64 rounds must make the ratio exceed 1 and be flagged by the round-counter test; a planted acceptance on a provisional score must be rejected by the benchmark's rule; a table that reports rounds without compute must be refused by the report builder.
- Determinism lanes: with seeds fixed, the scripted driver's sequence of designs and every map identity are identical run to run on `batch-cpu` (class D2); device runs are compared by decisions, not bits, across families.

## Considerations

- The comparison is not engine-specific for parametric families: an incumbent batches the same sweep. The gate is a statement about the studio's loop, and the status table must say so.
- Rounds are cheap to halve at the cost of sample efficiency; that is why compute is printed beside rounds and capped equally.
- Shared-policy bias can make the map reward what the policy steers well. If the rank-agreement record shows low agreement in most strata, almost every candidate is re-scored on the higher rung and the compute column, not the rounds column, shows the cost.
- The upstream archive crate keeps the first arrival on equal fitness; any path that lets its `add` decide an elite reintroduces order dependence, which the two-agent test would catch.
- Compute: design campaigns need the envelope class for a design campaign (48 device-hours as proposed); under the default envelope the experiment runs with fewer briefs and wider intervals and says so.
