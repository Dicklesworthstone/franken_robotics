## Background

For a design with P continuous parameters θ and a smooth metric J, Phylum estimates ∂J/∂θ in one dispatch. Worlds
are laid out as a stencil: for each seed, one world at θ and two at θ ⊕ δ·eᵢ and θ ⊖ δ·eᵢ for each parameter, moved
along the parameter's own retraction; every world of a seed shares every random draw; the estimate is the mean over
seeds of central differences (J₊ − J₋)/(2δ) with a confidence interval. A stencil of 20 parameters and 64 seeds is
41 × 64 = 2,624 worlds. This is Frontier work in crate `frob-phylum`, exposed as the `run` verb `phylum.gradient`
(first milestone M4), which returns the paired finite-difference gradient with intervals, per-pair divergence
steps and the `truncated-horizon` label where it applies.

It serves the studio's optimization loop: agents are the discrete optimizer and the studio supplies fast continuous
optimization underneath; a neighbourhood of designs evaluated in one mixed batch cuts sequential agent rounds. The
ceiling was stated first: at most the ratio of evaluations, about 2P + 1 worlds per gradient against a few hundred
evaluations of a derivative-free generation, and only on the smooth, short-horizon part of the objective, because
paired worlds decouple under contact. Deriving gradients through the contact solve itself is deferred to a much
later milestone; the paired stencil is the method available now and stays the reference for that later method.

GATE-PHYLUM-06 (known answer and method; milestone M4): paired gradient: the ballistic row within 1%; binary
metrics refused; on the pilot task a variance ratio of at least 2 at the admitted horizon and sign agreement of at
least 70% over 20 replicates. The register's reading of the row: the variance-ratio and sign-agreement comparison
governs; the ballistic row and the binary-metric refusal are known-answer preconditions.

✖ KILL-FDGRAD: the pilot's falsifier fires, or no end point is confirmed by a specialist; fallback, rank-based
search (CMA-ES, racing) with the stencil kept as a sensitivity report for analysis A-16. Its first reading (the
pilot) is bead N17b-20; this bead takes the final reading.

## Technical approach

**Input (`frob.phylum.gradient.in/1`).** `design`; `levers` (default: every unlocked continuous lever); `metric`;
`task`; `horizon` in control intervals (default: the smaller of the task's horizon and the admitted horizon, 50
unless a longer one has been admitted for the class); `seeds` S (default 64); `delta` per lever (default 3% of the
bounds range); `controller` (one policy for the whole stencil); `tier`; `measure_variance_ratio` (adds the
independent arm); `stencil_only`; `method` (`paired-fd`; `adjoint` is refused with the capability refusal); and the
`run` verb's idempotency key, budget and bounded wait.

**Pipeline (numbered).** 1. Resolve identities. 2. Admission tests on metric, horizon and determinism
(`FROB-REF-540` with the sub-reason, before any world runs). 3. Build the stencil (parameter variants of one tree,
shared addresses per seed). 4. Run it as one cohort. 5. Per pair, find the divergence step and truncate. 6. Refuse
on early divergence, or estimate with intervals. 7. Write the envelope and, if measured, the variance-ratio row.

**Output (`frob.phylum.gradient.out/1`).**

```json
{ "stencil": "st:…", "worlds": 1600, "horizon": 50, "mode": "optimizer-input",
  "gradient": [ {"lever": "thigh", "estimate": {"value": -3.1, "unit": "J/m per m"},
                 "ci95": [-4.4, -1.8], "pairs": 64, "truncated": 9,
                 "divergence_steps": {"min": 31, "q1": 50, "median": 50, "q3": 50, "max": 50},
                 "labels": ["truncated-horizon"], "variance_ratio": 3.4} ],
  "at_bound": ["knee_ratio"],
  "pairs_detail": {"cursor": "…"},
  "warnings": ["shared-policy gradient: check the end point with a specialist or a fine-tuned policy"] }
```

`evidence = { class: "Estimated", tier, determinism, standing: "provisional", seed }`. Per-pair divergence steps
are available in full through pagination. `next_actions` suggests the confirmation of an end point; under the
fallback mode (`sensitivity-report`) it suggests nothing that steps along the gradient.

**Refusals and exits.** `FROB-REF-540` (exit 3) for a non-smooth metric, a horizon above the admitted cap, pairs
diverged early, or an uncontrolled determinism class; the surface's budget and capability refusals; exit 8 when
accepted and still running; exit 2 when completed with `truncated-horizon` or `at-bound` flags; exit 0 otherwise.

**The gate measurement.** On the pilot task (the reference quadruped, 12 design parameters, a fixed policy):

1. Preconditions: the ballistic row within 1% (∂R/∂v = 2·v·sin 2α/g for R = v²·sin 2α/g) and the refusal of a
   binary metric, both through the verb.
2. Variance ratio at the admitted horizon, measured by the verb with its independent arm: the median over the 12
   parameters of Var[independent differences]/Var[paired differences] must be at least 2.
3. Sign agreement over 20 replicate calls with disjoint seed sets: for parameters whose pooled interval excludes
   zero, the share of replicates with the pooled sign, averaged over those parameters, must be at least 70%.

**End-point confirmation and the final reading of KILL-FDGRAD.** Three ascents of 20 steps each start from three
designs of the pilot class (normalized steps of the default δ, projected into bounds, one stencil per step). For
each, a specialist or a fine-tuned policy is obtained at the start design and at the end design, and the two are
compared on the metric by the anytime-valid paired comparison on common world addresses at level 0.05. An end point
is *confirmed* when the comparison settles in favour of the end design. The kill gate fires if no end point of the
three is confirmed. The record of N17b-20 is completed with this reading; if it fires, the mode is switched to
`sensitivity-report`, the gate is recorded as not passed on its method clause, and the downstream re-scoping of
N17b-20 applies.

**Layout.** Executor in `crates/frob-phylum/src/gradient/verb.rs`, registered through `frob-verbs`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-phylum gradient::verb`)

- `default_levers_and_horizon`: a design with 12 continuous and one `finite` lever → 12 parameters; horizon defaults to 50 with no admission row.
- `envelope_fields`: every parameter entry has an estimate with a unit, an interval, pair counts and divergence quartiles; `evidence.class` is `Estimated`.
- `refusals_before_running`: binary metric, horizon 80 without admission, uncontrolled class → `FROB-REF-540` with `metric`, `horizon`, `determinism`; the job ledger shows zero world-steps.
- `adjoint_refused`: `--method adjoint` → capability refusal, exit 5.
- `fallback_mode_output`: under `sensitivity-report` the envelope carries the label and no stepping suggestion.
- `sign_agreement_computation`: 20 synthetic replicate envelopes with a known pattern give the expected percentage.
- `confirmation_rule`: comparison records (settled for end, tied, settled for start) → 1 of 3 confirmed → the gate does not fire; (tied, tied, settled for start) → fires.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_gradient.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_gradient/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Steps:
(1) `frob phylum gradient --design @fixture:ballistic --levers launch_speed --metric range --seeds 16 --tier
batch-cpu --json`, asserting the estimate within 1% of 2.0387 s for v = 10 m/s, α = 45°;
(2) the same with `--metric landed-beyond-10m`, asserting exit 3, `FROB-REF-540`, `pointer: metric`;
(3) the grazing fixture, asserting the `truncated-horizon` label and divergence steps of 160 ± 1;
(4) on the pilot task, `frob phylum gradient --design quad-ref --task quad-flat-velocity --metric tracking-error
--seeds 64 --measure-variance-ratio --tier <tier> --json` for 20 disjoint seed sets, computing the variance ratio
and the sign agreement;
(5) the three ascents and their confirmations through `frob design edit`, `frob phylum gradient` and
`frob eval compare`.
Every command is a `cmd` event with the exact line, exit code, wall time and the `evidence` block; `metric` events
carry each measured value with its unit and threshold (1%, 2, 70%); `assert` events carry each clause of the gate
and of the kill rule; on failure the log gives the first parameter or replicate that failed, with the stencil
identity and the artifact paths to replay it. `summary`: `gates: ["GATE-PHYLUM-06", "KILL-FDGRAD"]`. A `--smoke`
mode runs steps 1 to 3 only.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-381 (ballistic body: ∂R/∂v = 2·v·sin 2α/g, recovered to O(δ²), within 1%); KA-382 (bouncing body near grazing: pairs flagged at their divergence step and truncated); KA-383 (2,624 worlds for 20 parameters and 64 seeds); KA-384 (shared draws within a stencil seed).
- Negative controls the gate must catch: a binary success metric admitted; divergence ignored and the full horizon averaged; a planted unshared stream (the variance ratio must collapse toward 1 and fail clause 2).
- Method controls: an A/A ascent (zero step length) must not be confirmed; a planted metric with a known linear dependence on one lever must be recovered with the right sign in all 20 replicates.
- Counterexample: CX-165.
- Determinism lanes: the envelope is byte-identical on a repeated call with the same key and seeds on `batch-cpu` (class D2), across worker counts, in debug and release; across device families gradients agree within their intervals.

## Considerations

- Bias: under a shared policy the gradient points toward designs that policy handles; that is why a step is only kept after a check on a higher rung, and why the kill gate has its second clause.
- RISK-47: search or gradients can exploit a contact-order or model-form artifact; a derivative is used only inside its validity label, and a ranking that flips under another admissible contact law is a signal to distrust the step.
- Compute: one gradient of 12 parameters at 64 seeds is 1,600 worlds for 50 control intervals; the gate's 20 replicates with the independent arm and the three ascents with specialists need the envelope class for a design campaign, and the specialists dominate the cost.
- The end-point clause needs specialists, which are themselves Frontier work with their own kill gates. If no specialist or fine-tuned policy is available at the milestone, the clause is undecidable, the mechanism is recorded as not kept, and the fallback mode holds until it can be decided.
- If the kill gate fires, the verb remains and is described by the name of its fallback: a sensitivity report.
