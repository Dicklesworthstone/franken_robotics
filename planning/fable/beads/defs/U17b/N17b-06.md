## Background

Of the three things that could distinguish Phylum's mixed batches, one is a property and not a number: any one
world of a mixed cohort can be replayed outside its cohort, bit for bit, and forks of it are deterministic. FACT
(about incumbents; to be re-read): the one incumbent solver that publishes a mixed batch is documented as not
deterministic, and an incumbent that documents bitwise replay of a batch holds a world equal to its isolated run
only to 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻⁴ in its own tests. The property is therefore worth claiming only if it is exact, and only after
its gate.

GATE-PHYLUM-02 (known answer, determinism; milestone M2): each of 512 designs in a mixed cohort has the same
digests as in a homogeneous batch, on `batch-cpu` and per GPU back end, at three bucket sizes and under slot
dispatch with 100 random co-tenancies. It is the gate of invariant INV-PHYLUM-02 ("a world of a mixed cohort has the
same digests as the same world in a homogeneous batch of its own model") and of INV-PHYLUM-01 ("every result of a
cohort is keyed by world address and `ModelId`; slot, cohort and arrival order appear in no result").

The CPU rows of the gate are produced by BD-170 and the device mechanisms by BD-183. Nothing so far runs the whole
gate as stated: all 512 designs, every back end, three capacities, 100 co-tenancies. This bead is that run and its
record. It shares one fixture with the batch-composition invariance gates of the kernel and numerics chapters: the
fixture is one, it is run once per milestone, and each gate is compared with the threshold in its own row.

Terms. A *digest chain* is the sequence of per-step state digests of one world; device digests are a tripwire and
the identity of record is a BLAKE3 hash over committed snapshots on the host. *Co-tenancy* means other jobs' worlds
share the persistent environment. Determinism class D2 means the same bits run to run and across batch size, slot,
co-tenant worlds, bucket capacity and shard count on one device family.

## Scope

In: the full gate run on `batch-cpu`, `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal`; the comparison by world address; first-divergence
localization on failure; the negative controls; the gate record.
Out: fixing a divergence (it goes to the bead of the kernel named by the localization); bit equality between a
device and the CPU (a separate class and gate); throughput (BD-186).

## Technical approach

**Protocol (numbered), per back end.**

1. *Homogeneous reference.* For each of the 512 workload designs, run a homogeneous batch of its own model: 16
   worlds, 500 physics steps, flat-ground task, seed fixed. Record each world's digest chain and the host BLAKE3
   of the committed snapshots at steps 100, 250 and 500. On devices the homogeneous runs are batched by design
   (one environment per design, shape class equal to the design's own unpadded size rounded to its native bucket).
2. *Mixed, three bucket sizes.* Run the same worlds (same addresses) in mixed cohorts three times: each design in
   its native bucket; in the next larger bucket; and in the largest bucket that admits it. A design already in the
   largest bucket is run at three buffer capacities of that bucket's contact and row axes instead.
3. *Slot dispatch, 100 co-tenancies.* Run the mixed population 100 times with: a random slot permutation; a random
   set of co-tenant jobs (other designs' episodes, different tasks) filling between 10% and 90% of the slots; random
   admission ticks for a tenth of the designs; one and two shards on the two-card machine. All randomness of the
   harness comes from one seed and is logged.
4. *Compare.* For every world address and `ModelId`, the digest chain and the three snapshot hashes of steps 2 and
   3 must equal those of step 1. The count of differing worlds must be 0.
5. *Result keying.* The result sets of two co-tenancy runs, sorted by world address, must be byte-identical
   (INV-PHYLUM-01).
6. *On any difference.* Run the first-divergence search over the two digest chains, then the per-stage digests of
   the divergent step, and file the step, stage, kernel and quantity in the divergence ledger. The gate is recorded
   FAIL with that localization.

**Sizes.** 512 designs × 16 worlds = 8,192 worlds; 500 steps; (1 + 3 + 100) passes: about 426 M world-steps per
back end. At 2 M world-steps/s (a TARGET, not a measurement) that is under four minutes of device time per GPU back
end; on `batch-cpu` the co-tenancy pass is reduced to 4 worlds per design and 200 steps (about 43 M world-steps),
which fits the default compute envelope.

**What passing licenses.** With this gate current, a world of a sweep may be replayed alone on `batch-cpu` or on
the same device family and compared by digest; paired comparisons on common addresses inside a mixed cohort are
`paired-exact`; forks from snapshots of a mixed cohort are deterministic.

**Gate record.** One row per back end in `registries/gates.toml`: worlds compared, worlds differing (0 required),
designs covered (512 required), capacities, co-tenancies (100 required), the fingerprint of the hardware, commit,
seed, `WorkloadId`, and the list of negative controls caught in the same run.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-PHYLUM-02 is recorded PASS for `batch-cpu`, `gpu-cuda` (each card) and `gpu-metal` with measured counts: 512 designs, three capacities, 100 co-tenancies, 0 differing worlds.
- [ ] Result sets are byte-identical after sorting by address across co-tenancy runs.
- [ ] All four negative controls below are caught in the same run that records the gate.
- [ ] A back end that cannot be run (device absent, or not ratified for device claims) is recorded `not run` with the reason and the gate is not called PASS for it.
- [ ] A failure produces a divergence-ledger entry naming step, stage, kernel and quantity, and the replay artifacts for the world.
- [ ] The receipt names commit, hardware fingerprint, seed and `WorkloadId`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-phylum --test gate_phylum_02`)

- `compare_by_address`: two result sets in different slot orders compare equal; one flipped bit in one world's step-17 digest is reported with that address and step.
- `capacity_plan_three_sizes`: a design of 20 degrees of freedom is assigned buckets 24, 36 and 64; one of 60 is assigned bucket 64 at three row capacities.
- `cotenancy_schedule_from_seed`: seed 5 yields the same 100 slot permutations and co-tenant sets on Linux and macOS.
- `gate_requires_controls`: a gate record without its four caught controls is rejected by the conformance loader.
- `small_mixed_equals_solo`: quadruped, hexapod and humanoid, 8 worlds each, 200 steps on `batch-cpu`: zero differing worlds (a smoke form of the gate).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/gate_phylum_02.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E`; log
`target/e2e-logs/gate_phylum_02/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. For each back end it runs
`frob phylum sweep --designs @workload:h2h-512 --worlds-per-design 16 --task flat-stand --tier <tier> --seed 23 --json`
in the configurations of steps 1 to 3 (homogeneous runs through `frob sim run --designs <d>`), then
`frob telemetry divergence` on any differing pair. Events: `cmd` for every command with exit code, wall time and the
envelope's `evidence` block (class, tier, determinism D2, identities, seed); `metric` events `worlds_compared`,
`worlds_differing` (threshold 0), `designs_covered` (512), `cotenancies` (100); `assert` events per back end and per
pass with the digests compared and, on failure, the first differing world address, step, stage and value and the
snapshot paths; a `summary` with assertions and `gates: ["GATE-PHYLUM-02"]`. A `--smoke` mode runs
`small_mixed_equals_solo` through the CLI and claims no gate.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-375: identical digests per world for each of 512 designs, mixed against homogeneous, at three bucket sizes and under 100 random co-tenancies, on `batch-cpu` and per GPU back end (exact, by digest; tier G5).
- KA-376: slots and co-tenants permuted; envelopes compare equal after sorting by world address.
- Negative control 1 (CX-146): a reduction tree shaped by capacity (planted in one kernel). In `f32` with a = 10⁸, b = −10⁸, c = 1, (a + b) + c = 1 and a + (b + c) = 0; the capacity pass must differ.
- Negative control 2: a batch-wide normalizer. Two worlds observing 1 and 3 and normalized by their batch mean see −1 and +1; the first alone sees 0. The co-tenancy pass must differ.
- Negative control 3: padding with +0.0 in place of −0.0; a world whose sum is −0.0 changes bits with capacity.
- Negative control 4: results placed by arrival order; the slot-permutation comparison must differ.
- Metamorphic relations MR-01 (batch-composition invariance) and MR-02 (mixed cohort equals homogeneous batch).
- Determinism lanes: the whole run twice on one machine; across worker counts on `batch-cpu`; debug and release; per back end against class D2.

## Considerations

- One differing world fails the gate. A failure that survives a repair cycle falsifies a clause of the mixed-batch bet (BET-18: "any of the 512 designs of a mixed cohort fails to reproduce the digests of its single-design run") and removes replay outside the batch from the claims; it is reported to the milestone exit review and to the KILL-PHYLUM decision (N17b-10).
- A failure only under co-tenancy with shared environments points at slot dispatch; its fallback is one environment per job and its kill gate belongs to the daemon.
- Device claims depend on the owner's ratification of the GPU back ends; until then device rows are labelled exploratory.
- The gate proves equality on this workload and these seeds. It is a tested property (evidence class Tested), not a proof; the kernels' footprint manifests and the padding obligations are what make it hold by construction.
