## Background

The Phylum chapter states seven invariants and three proof obligations. The project's testing rule is that every
invariant and proof obligation has a property test (or a certificate, or a proof) in the bead whose mechanism it
constrains, and that one which no mechanism bead of a unit carries gets its own test bead. Six of the ten are
carried by mechanism beads of this drafting unit. Four constrain mechanisms whose seed beads are written elsewhere
(campaign quotienting, the morphology generator, the bucket planner). This bead gives those four their named tests
in `frob-phylum`, and it keeps the index of all ten, so that the chapter's table can be checked mechanically: each
identifier maps to a test that exists and runs.

**The ten statements and where each is tested.**

| ID | Statement | How it is checked | Test lives in |
|---|---|---|---|
| INV-PHYLUM-01 | Every result of a cohort is keyed by world address and `ModelId`. Slot, cohort and arrival order appear in no result. | Permute slots and co-tenants; envelopes compare equal after sorting by address | N17b-04, N17b-06 |
| INV-PHYLUM-02 | A world of a mixed cohort has the same digests as the same world in a homogeneous batch of its own model. | GATE-PHYLUM-02 | BD-170, N17b-06 |
| INV-PHYLUM-03 | Quotients use Γ_obs only, and equal mission digests mean equal lowered tables. | The lidar counterexample; replay inside sampled classes | this bead |
| INV-PHYLUM-04 | A memoized result is served only when its dependency digest is unchanged. | Positive and negative controls of the invalidation contract; shadow recompute | BD-286, N17b-13 |
| INV-PHYLUM-05 | A specialized kernel reproduces table-driven digests or is a new kernel version. | Digest comparison on the pinned corpus | N17b-26, N17b-27 (limb-archetype level); the per-design level in the survivor-specialization work |
| INV-PHYLUM-06 | A generated design is a pure function of `(GeneratorId, seed, index)` and its log-probability is exact. | Regeneration on both platforms; probabilities sum to one on enumerable sub-grammars | this bead |
| INV-PHYLUM-07 | The worlds of one stencil seed share every random draw. | Stream addresses compared; a planted unshared stream is caught | N17b-21 |
| PO-PHYLUM-01 | The number of canonical representatives enumerated equals the cycle-index count. | Class D0, exact; checked for every sweep | this bead |
| PO-PHYLUM-02 | 1/r ≤ Ω_p ≤ r⁻ᵖ for p ≥ 1. | Proved (two lines); property-tested on random populations | this bead |
| PO-PHYLUM-03 | Map merge is commutative, associative and idempotent. | Proved from the total order; property-tested | N17b-17 |

Terms. Γ_mech is a robot's mechanical symmetry group; Γ_obs is its subgroup that also preserves every sensor pose
and field of view, the payload, the task's goal orientation and the terrain's symmetry. A canonical representative
of an orbit of slot labelings is its lexicographically least member. The cycle-index (Burnside) count of labelings
of slots X with k types under a group Γ is |Γ|⁻¹·Σ_{g ∈ Γ} k^c(g), with c(g) the number of cycles of g on X.
Ω_p = L_maxᵖ/E[Lᵖ] is the padding overhead for work growing as the p-th power of size L, and r = E[L]/L_max.

## Scope

In: named property and known-answer tests for INV-PHYLUM-03, INV-PHYLUM-06, PO-PHYLUM-01 and PO-PHYLUM-02 against
the real mechanisms; the index test over all ten identifiers; the two-line proof of PO-PHYLUM-02 written into the
crate's contract.
Out: the mechanisms themselves (BD-285 quotienting, BD-287 generator, BD-171 planner) and the gates they close.

## Technical approach

**PO-PHYLUM-02, proof to be recorded.** Lower bound: for 0 ≤ L ≤ L_max and p ≥ 1, Lᵖ ≤ L_maxᵖ⁻¹·L, so
E[Lᵖ] ≤ L_maxᵖ⁻¹·E[L] and Ω_p = L_maxᵖ/E[Lᵖ] ≥ L_max/E[L] = 1/r. Upper bound: by Jensen's inequality for the
convex map x ↦ xᵖ, E[Lᵖ] ≥ E[L]ᵖ, so Ω_p ≤ (L_max/E[L])ᵖ = r⁻ᵖ; for p ≤ 3 this is at most r⁻³. The largest robot,
not the mean, sets the cost.

**Tests against the real code.**

1. *PO-PHYLUM-01.* Call the quotient mechanism's enumerator on slot actions and compare the number of
   representatives it emits with the cycle-index count computed independently in this test from the group's
   permutation list, in exact integers. Fixtures: ten types on six slots under the dihedral group of order 12
   (cycle counts: identity 6; two rotations by one step 1; two by two steps 2; one half turn 3; three reflections
   through opposite slots 4; three through opposite edges 3), expected 86,185; the same under the sagittal mirror
   alone, expected 500,500; three types under the order-12 group, expected 92, confirmed by brute force over 3⁶
   labelings; a wreath-product case with two swappable slots and a module half-turn, expected (k² + k)/2 = 6 for
   k = 3 against 36 raw assignments. Property: for random permutation groups on up to eight slots and up to four
   types, the enumerator's count equals the cycle-index count and every emitted labeling is the least of its orbit.
2. *INV-PHYLUM-03.* (a) A request to quotient a hexapod carrying a front-only sensor under its mechanical group is
   refused with `FROB-REF-161`, naming the breaking element; under Γ_obs it returns 500,500. (b) For 1,000 sampled
   pairs of designs with equal mission digests, the lowered tables are byte-equal; for the two ballast placements
   with slots {1, 4} and {2, 3} (equal Σx = 5, Σx² = 17 against 13) the digests differ.
3. *INV-PHYLUM-06.* (a) For three generators and 1,000 indices each, `(GeneratorId, seed, index)` regenerates the
   same `DesignId` twice in one process, in a second process, and on the other platform (identities compared from
   a committed fixture file). (b) On an enumerable sub-grammar (limb count in {2, 4, 6} with probabilities 1/4, 1/2,
   1/4; limb type leg or wheel with 2/3, 1/3; one parameter on a four-point grid, uniform: 24 designs) the
   generator's probabilities are exact rationals that sum to exactly 1, and the reported log-probability of each
   design equals the logarithm of its rational.
4. *PO-PHYLUM-02.* Property test over random populations (2 to 50 distinct sizes between 2 and 80, random weights,
   p in {1, 1.5, 2, 3}) using the planner's own overhead function: 1/r ≤ Ω_p ≤ r⁻ᵖ, evaluated in exact rationals
   for integer p and with a relative slack of 10⁻¹² otherwise; plus the three table populations.

**The index.** A table in the test crate maps each of the ten identifiers to a fully qualified test name; the index
test fails if a name does not resolve to an existing test, so a renamed or deleted test cannot silently drop an
invariant.

**Layout.** `crates/frob-phylum/tests/invariants.rs` and `crates/frob-phylum/tests/invariant_index.rs`; fixtures
under `crates/frob-phylum/tests/fixtures/`, marked `synthetic` where they are.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four test groups above exist, call the real enumerator, digest, generator and planner (no mocks, no reimplementation under test), and pass.
- [ ] Counts 86,185, 500,500, 92 and 6 are reproduced exactly; the ballast pair splits; the mechanical-group request is refused with `FROB-REF-161`.
- [ ] Generator identities match the committed fixture on Linux and macOS; the 24 probabilities sum to exactly 1.
- [ ] The padding bound holds on 10⁴ random populations and on the three table populations (Ω values 1.32, 1.58, 1.76; 1.78, 2.96, 4.50; 3.27, 9.59, 23.3).
- [ ] The index test resolves all ten identifiers to existing tests across the crate and its sibling test targets.
- [ ] The proof of PO-PHYLUM-02 is in `CONTRACT.md` under invariants.
- [ ] Each planted defect below is caught.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_invariants.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E`; log
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_invariants/<utc>.ndjson`. It runs the same four checks from the user's side:
`frob phylum quotient --slots 6 --types 10 --group @fixture:hexapod-plain --json` (expect 86,185),
the same on `@fixture:hexapod-front-lidar` with `--group mech` (expect exit 3, `FROB-REF-161`) and with Γ_obs
(expect 500,500); `frob phylum generate --generator @fixture:subgrammar-24 --seed 1 --count 24 --report --json`
(expect probabilities summing to 1 and stable identities on a second run); `frob phylum plan` on the three table
populations (expect the nine Ω values). Each call is a `cmd` event with exit code, wall time and the `evidence`
block; each expectation an `assert` event with observed and expected values; the `summary` lists assertions and
`gates: []`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-365 (86,185 representatives; the number enumerated equals the cycle-index count), KA-366 (500,500), KA-367 (24,546 classes of 735,471 placements; equal Σx with different Σx² must not merge), KA-368 and KA-369 (padding table and bound), KA-380 (10⁴ sampled pairs of designs inside one mission-digest class replay to identical digests: zero false quotients; the full replay is run by the quotienting bead, and this suite checks table equality on 1,000 pairs), KA-385 (generator reproducible; probabilities exact).
- Planted defects: counting under the rotation subgroup only (gives (10⁶ + 20 + 200 + 10³)/6 = 166,870, not 86,185) must fail the count test; quotienting under the mechanical group with a front-only sensor must be refused, not answered; a digest that omits the second moment must merge the ballast pair and fail; a generator reading the wall clock or a hash-map order must change an identity; a padding function that averages sizes before raising to the power (computing E[L]ᵖ in place of E[Lᵖ]) returns r⁻ᵖ and must fail the table test for p > 1.
- Counterexamples: CX-153, CX-154, CX-160, CX-166.
- Determinism lanes: all four groups are exact (class D0) and identical on both platforms, in debug and release.

## Considerations

- The tests of this bead overlap the acceptance of the mechanisms' own beads on purpose: the index is the single place where the chapter's ten statements are seen together.
- If campaign quotienting falls back at its kill gate (orbit representatives only; the digest kept as a cache key with no claim), `inv03_equal_digest_equal_tables` stays as a test of the cache key and loses its link to any claim.
- The enumerator must scale: 10⁶ labelings under a group of order 12 is enumerated in well under a second on one core; the property test bounds its sizes so that the suite stays inside the smoke battery.
