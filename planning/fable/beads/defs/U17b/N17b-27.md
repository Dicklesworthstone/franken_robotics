## Background

Limb-archetype kernels (tier Frontier; beads N17b-25 and N17b-26) run kinematics and the dynamics terms per limb
instance with code specialized to the limb's joint-class sequence and depth, compiled once per kernel version,
bit-identical to the table-driven kernel. Their claim is reach with zero compile per design, at a modest gain. The
gate has five clauses and a kill gate behind it.

GATE-PHYLUM-09 (Performance; known answer, determinism; TARGET; earliest M2): every registered archetype reproduces
table-driven digests bit for bit on the pinned corpus; at most 16 archetypes cover at least 90% of the limbs of the
design corpus; zero kernel compiles while 1,000 corpus edits are admitted; the 95% lower bound of the paired
whole-step ratio against the table-driven kernel is at least 1.15 at 8,192 worlds; both hand controls reproduce.

✖ KILL-LIMBKERNEL: the pilot's falsifier holds; or coverage is below 80% with 16 archetypes; or the gate's ratio
fails after one repair cycle. Fallback: the table-driven kernel for every design, per-design specialization for
survivors under its own gate, and the limb port interface kept for the tree-operator route and the per-limb caches.
The first reading (pilot and coverage) is bead N17b-28; this bead takes the final reading (the ratio clause).

The two hand controls come from the chapter's counterexample: *a port packet is not the limb when its foot is
down.* A point mass m on a massless leg stands on a floor under gravity g. Treated as a free tree limb, its packet
reports the body's inertia and a bias equal to the weight m·g, and the base equation closed from the packet gives a
downward acceleration g. With the contact row the normal force is m·g and the acceleration is zero. Any path that
closes the base equation from port packets without the contact rows is wrong by exactly g. The second control is
the packet with value diag(0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1): it is accepted as a packet and refused as an invertible matrix.

In the head-to-head experiment this level is reported as a variant of the cohort arm on two measures (edit to first
accepted result; throughput).

## Scope

In: the five clauses' measurements; the hand controls as tests; the gate record; the final reading of the kill
gate and its record; the numbers handed to the head-to-head as the arm-A variant.
Out: the mechanism (N17b-25, N17b-26); the first reading (N17b-28); the head-to-head itself (BD-186).

## Technical approach

**Clause 1: digests.** On the pinned corpus (the standard battery's episodes for every robot class that has a
`Split` plan, plus the 512-design workload at 16 worlds), run with `--limb-kernels` off and on, on `batch-cpu` and
on each device back end. For every world, the digest chain must be equal. Each registered archetype must be
exercised by at least 1,000 instances; an archetype not exercised is recorded as untested and the clause is not
met for it.

**Clause 2: coverage.** With the registry of at most 16 entries, limb coverage (matched limbs over all limbs) on
the design corpus must be at least 90%. Joint coverage and design coverage are reported beside it. Until the
design corpus exists the figure is computed on the 512-design workload and on the generated designs of the paper
pilot and recorded as `interim`; an interim figure cannot make the clause pass.

**Clause 3: zero compiles.** With the limb path on, admit the first 1,000 sealed edits of the mutation corpus into
running cohorts (the latency campaign's procedure). The binary kernel cache's compile counter before and after
must be equal. The count of edits whose child took the `Split` path and the `Whole` path is reported.

**Clause 4: whole-step ratio.** On one RTX 4090 at 8,192 worlds, the reference quadruped workload (12 joints, 18
degrees of freedom, the reference step) is run with the table-driven kernel and with the archetype path, ten or
more paired repetitions interleaved in randomized order after warm-up, completion-timed over stages 1 to 9. With
ℓᵢ = ln(t_table,i/t_limb,i), the one-sided 95% lower bound exp(ℓ̄ − t·s/√n) (t = 1.833 for n = 10) must be at least
1.15. The same measurement is reported for the reference humanoid, for the 512-design workload and on Metal; those
rows are reported and not gated. Every number carries the disclosure block.

**Clause 5: hand controls.**

- *Standing point mass.* A synthetic one-degree-of-freedom fixture: vertical coordinate z of a body of mass
  m = 2 kg under g = 9.81 m/s², with a limb packet whose vertical inertia entry is m and whose bias is the weight
  m·g = 19.62 N, and one normal contact row with zero gap and zero approach velocity. Expected: through the engine's
  assembly and coupled solve, acceleration 0 and normal force 19.62 N; through a deliberately wrong path that
  closes the base equation from the packet alone (kept in the test crate only), acceleration −9.81 m/s². "Both
  reproduce" means both numbers are obtained: the right answer from the engine, and the wrong one from the
  shortcut, off by exactly g. A simulation-level form repeats it with a body on a leg link of mass 10⁻⁶·m standing
  on a floor: with `--limb-kernels` on, the body's acceleration after one step is below 10⁻⁵·g.
- *Singular packet.* The value diag(0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1), obtained by eliminating a joint with axis s (the first basis
  vector) from the identity inertia by I − I·s·(sᵀI·s)⁻¹·sᵀI. It must be accepted by the packet type (symmetric
  positive semidefinite), and any attempt to invert it at the port must be refused: the type offers no inverse, and
  the checked factorization used by the coupled solve reports a zero pivot.

**Final reading of KILL-LIMBKERNEL.** If clause 4's bound is below 1.15, one repair cycle is allowed: one
optimization pass under the performance program's rules (its ceiling computed first; identical digests; one lever),
then one re-measurement. If the bound is still below 1.15, the kill gate fires: the archetype kernels are removed
from the kernel set (no result changes, because they are bit-identical), `--limb-kernels` is refused, and the
record of N17b-28 is completed. If clause 1 fails for an archetype after one repair, that archetype is removed from
the registry and coverage is recomputed; if coverage then falls below 80%, the kill gate fires on coverage.

**Record.** One row in `registries/gates.toml` with the five measured clauses (worlds compared and differing;
coverage figures and their population; compile counter delta; ratio bound with pairs; the four control numbers),
the commit, the hardware fingerprint, seeds and corpus identities; the kill row completed with the final reading.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Clause 1: zero differing worlds on the pinned corpus on every back end, with every registered archetype exercised by at least 1,000 instances.
- [ ] Clause 2: limb coverage reported with joint and design coverage; marked `interim` unless measured on the design corpus; at least 90% on the design corpus for a PASS.
- [ ] Clause 3: compile counter delta of 0 over 1,000 admitted corpus edits.
- [ ] Clause 4: the paired 95% lower bound at 8,192 worlds, against 1.15, with a complete disclosure block.
- [ ] Clause 5: acceleration 0 and normal force 19.62 N from the engine; −9.81 m/s² from the shortcut; the singular packet accepted as a packet and refused for inversion.
- [ ] GATE-PHYLUM-09 is recorded with all five measured values; PASS only if all five are met (an interim coverage figure yields `partial`, not PASS).
- [ ] KILL-LIMBKERNEL's final reading is recorded: fired or not, with the clause and the repair cycle's before and after.
- [ ] The whole-step and edit-to-result numbers of the limb path are handed to the head-to-head as the variant of the cohort arm, or marked `not run`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-kernels --test gate_phylum_09`)

- `standing_point_mass_coupled`: m = 2, g = 9.81 → a = 0 (|a| < 10⁻¹² in `f64`), λ = 19.62 N.
- `standing_point_mass_shortcut_is_wrong_by_g`: the packet-only closure returns a = −9.81 exactly.
- `singular_packet_accepted`: diag(0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1) constructs as a limb port packet; its smallest eigenvalue is 0.
- `singular_packet_not_invertible`: the checked factorization returns the zero-pivot refusal; no `inverse` exists on the type (a compile-fail test).
- `elimination_formula`: I − I·s·(sᵀI·s)⁻¹·sᵀI with I the identity and s = e₁ equals diag(0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1) exactly.
- `gate_rule`: bounds 1.15 → met; 1.149 → not met; an untested archetype → clause 1 not met; coverage 0.90 interim → `partial`.
- `repair_cycle_rule`: 1.12 then 1.16 → gate met, kill does not fire; 1.12 then 1.14 → kill fires; a second repair is not admitted.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/gate_phylum_09.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes
`target/e2e-logs/gate_phylum_09/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Steps: (1) digest runs with
`frob phylum sweep --designs @workload:h2h-512 --worlds-per-design 16 --task flat-stand --tier <tier> --seed 31
[--limb-kernels] --json` and the battery's episodes; (2) `frob phylum plan … --json` for coverage; (3) the
admission of 1,000 sealed edits with `frob spark cache --json` read before and after; (4) the paired timing of
`frob sim run --designs quad-ref --worlds 8192 --tier gpu-cuda [--limb-kernels] --json` through the performance
harness; (5) the hand-control tests. Events: `cmd` per command with the exact line, exit code, wall time and the
`evidence` block; `metric` events for worlds differing (0), instances per archetype (≥ 1,000), coverage (0.90),
compile delta (0), ratio bound (1.15), control values (0; 19.62 N; −9.81 m/s²); `assert` events per clause; on a
digest failure the first differing world, step, stage and value with snapshot paths. `summary`:
`gates: ["GATE-PHYLUM-09", "KILL-LIMBKERNEL"]`. A `--smoke` mode runs clause 5 and a 32-world digest comparison on
`batch-cpu`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: the standing point mass (0 and m·g with rows; −g without); the singular packet (KA-264 and KA-051 of other chapters give the same matrix); 262/209 = 1.25 as the ceiling no measured ratio may exceed on a step whose touched stages hold a fifth of the time (KA-374).
- Negative controls: a planted base assembly that skips the constraint rows when every limb is an archetype instance must give −g on the simulation-level standing fixture and fail clause 5; a planted archetype kernel with a reassociated sum must fail clause 1; a planted per-design compile on admission must make the compile delta nonzero and fail clause 3; an A/A timing (table-driven against itself) must give a bound at or below 1.02; a planted 1.2 slowdown must be detected.
- Counterexamples: the two limb-packet cases of the registry N17-CX; CX-129 and CX-024 (the same packet in the design-language and dynamics chapters); CX-164.
- Determinism lanes: clause 1 twice on one machine, across worker counts, debug and release, per back end against class D2.

## Considerations

- The gate's ratio (1.15) is at the top of the hypothesis range (1.05 to 1.15), and the ceiling for a step whose touched stages hold 20% is 1.25 with an infinitely fast recursion. A failure of clause 4 is a likely outcome; the kill gate then removes the kernels at no cost to any recorded result.
- The plan puts the gate at M2 and defines its coverage clause on a corpus that arrives at M3. The honest record at M2 is `partial` with an interim figure; the gate is completed when the corpus exists, and nothing may cite it as PASS before.
- "At 8,192 worlds" names no workload; this bead gates on the reference quadruped, which is the pilot's robot and the favourable case, and reports the humanoid and the mixed workload beside it so the favourable choice is visible.
- If the kill gate on mixed batches has fired, this gate is recorded `not attempted` with that reason.
- Device rows depend on the owner's ratification of device back ends; until then they are labelled exploratory.
