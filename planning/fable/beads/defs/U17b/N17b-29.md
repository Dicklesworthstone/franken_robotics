## Background

Phylum's population verb `phylum.sweep` returns one envelope with a provisional scorecard per design. From
milestone M4 it also returns a map: each sweep updates a quality-diversity map over behaviour descriptors such as
mass, speed, energy per metre and footprint, and the envelope carries the cells that changed, their elites with
`provisional` scores, the count per cell and the coverage of descriptor space. An agent's turn then yields many
different good designs instead of one optimum, which is how fewer serial rounds are bought (a HYPOTHESIS tested by
the gate in BD-342). The verb's first version (N17b-04) reserved a `map_delta` field and left it null. This bead
fills it.

The map type, its total order and its order-independent merge are bead N17b-17. What is added here is the path from
a sweep's scorecards to map entries, and the statement of what changed.

The chapter's warning that this bead must make visible: a population scored under one shared policy drifts toward
designs that policy steers easily. The envelope therefore reports descriptor coverage beside quality, and elites
stay `provisional` until the rank-agreement record permits selection in their stratum.

## Scope

In: two new inputs of `phylum.sweep`; descriptor extraction and quantization; entry and dispatch-map construction;
recording the dispatch map; the delta against the campaign map; the envelope block and its warning.
Out: the map type (N17b-17); reading and merging maps and the campaign head's storage rules (N17b-18); the gate on
rounds (BD-342); proposing the next population from a map (the optimizer).

## Technical approach

**New inputs.** `campaign` (optional): the campaign whose map this dispatch joins; without it the sweep still
builds and stores its own dispatch map. `map_spec` (optional): a `MapSpecId`; the default is the specification
registered for the task (descriptor definitions with units, quantization steps, bounds, bins or centroids, score
metric, strata).

**From scorecards to entries (numbered).**

1. For every design with status `scored`, read each descriptor from its declared source:
   - *mass* [kg]: the model's mass roll-up (an exact static quantity, not a rollout statistic);
   - *footprint* [m²]: the area of the planform bounding polygon of the nominal stance, from geometry (static);
   - *speed* [m/s]: the scorecard's mean forward speed over the design's worlds;
   - *energy per metre* [J/m]: the scorecard's energy over distance travelled.
   Other descriptors follow the same pattern: a registered metric with a unit and a denominator.
2. Rollout descriptors are means over the design's valid worlds, summed in a fixed order (world address order)
   so that the value is the same bits inside one determinism class whatever the cohort was. Worlds marked
   `Degraded` or `Faulted` are excluded as the scorecard excludes them. A design with fewer valid worlds than the
   specification's minimum (default: half of the worlds requested) yields no entry and is listed under
   `map_delta.skipped` with the reason.
3. Quantize descriptors and the score to integers in units of the specification's steps (round half to even).
4. Form the entry `(DesignId, EvalId, descriptor, score, standing: provisional)`, where `EvalId` is the hash of the
   dispatch's identity: task, world addresses, controller or policy, tier, step-plan identity and kernel versions.
   `unresolved` designs produce no entry.
5. Build the dispatch map from the entries, store it as a content-addressed artifact and record
   `dispatch → MapId`.
6. If `campaign` is given: read the campaign map before, merge the dispatch map into it (the store advances the
   head), and compute the delta between before and after. Without a campaign the delta is against the empty map.

**Envelope block (`execution.map_delta`).**

```json
{ "spec": "mapspec:…", "dispatch_map": "map:…", "campaign_map": {"before": "map:…", "after": "map:…"},
  "cells_changed": 37,
  "changed": [ {"index": [3, 7, 2, 1], "count": 9,
                "old_elite": {"design": "d:…", "score": "0.58"},
                "new_elite": {"design": "d:…", "score": "0.61", "standing": "provisional"},
                "stratum": "s:12", "selection": "unmeasured"} ],
  "coverage": {"before": 0.0750, "after": 0.0824, "clamped": 3},
  "quality": {"before": "…", "after": "…"},
  "skipped": [ {"design": "d:…", "reason": "valid worlds 5 of 16, below the minimum 8"} ],
  "warnings": ["shared-policy scores: coverage is reported beside quality; elites are provisional"] }
```

A cell is *changed* when its elite changed or its count grew. The list is paginated with the rest of a long result.

**What does not change.** The `results` part of the sweep's envelope is untouched by this bead: it still holds no
slot, cohort or arrival information, and adding a campaign does not alter any design's scorecard. The map is a
function of `results` and of the campaign's earlier dispatches; it is never an input to the physics.

**Determinism and evidence.** Static descriptors are exact. Rollout descriptors are class D2 per back end; after
quantization the cell is stable inside a class. The block inherits the envelope's evidence (class Tested, the
sweep's tier, standing provisional).

**Layout.** `crates/frob-phylum/src/map/from_sweep.rs`; the default map specification of each task family lives
with the task's registered metrics.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `phylum.sweep` accepts `campaign` and `map_spec`, and its envelope carries the block above; without either input it carries the dispatch map and a delta against the empty map.
- [ ] Every scored design contributes exactly one entry or one `skipped` line; unresolved designs contribute neither.
- [ ] The delta equals `delta(before, after)` computed independently from the stored maps.
- [ ] Two sweeps of one campaign submitted in either order leave the same campaign map (identical `MapId`).
- [ ] Permuting slots and co-tenants changes neither the entries nor the dispatch `MapId`.
- [ ] Coverage appears beside quality in every block; every new elite is `provisional`; the shared-policy warning is present when one controller scored the population.
- [ ] The `results` part of the envelope is byte-identical with and without `campaign`.
- [ ] On tier `batch-cpu` the dispatch `MapId` is identical run to run and across worker counts.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-phylum map::from_sweep`)

- `descriptor_sources`: a fixture scorecard with mass 14.2 kg, speed 0.613 m/s, energy per metre 41.27 J/m and footprint 0.0841 m² quantizes (steps 1 g, 1 mm/s, 0.01 J/m, 1 cm²) to 14,200, 613, 4,127 and 841.
- `fixed_order_mean`: the same per-world values presented in two slot orders give the same descriptor bits.
- `skipped_below_minimum`: 5 valid worlds of 16 → no entry, one `skipped` line; 8 of 16 → an entry.
- `unresolved_no_entry`: a design refused for size produces no entry and no `skipped` line (it is already in `results` as unresolved).
- `delta_matches_stored_maps`: the block's `changed` list equals the independent delta of the two stored maps.
- `order_of_dispatches_irrelevant`: dispatches A then B and B then A leave one campaign `MapId`.
- `results_unchanged_by_campaign`: `results` bytes equal with and without `campaign`.
- `eval_id_changes_with_identity`: the same designs under another task or another controller get other `EvalId`s and are separate members of a cell.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/phylum_sweep_map_delta.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E`; log
`target/e2e-logs/phylum_sweep_map_delta/<utc>.ndjson`. It runs
`frob phylum sweep --designs @fixture:gen-a --task flat-stand --worlds-per-design 8 --tier batch-cpu --seed 1
--campaign qd-e2e --json`, then the same for `@fixture:gen-b`, then repeats both in the other order under a second
campaign name. It asserts: `cells_changed` equals the length of `changed`; coverage after is at least coverage
before; the two campaigns end with the same `MapId`; `results` equals that of a sweep without `--campaign`. `cmd`
events carry exact commands, exit codes, wall time and the `evidence` block; `metric` events carry cells changed,
coverage and skipped counts; on failure the first differing cell and both elites are logged with the map artifact
paths. The `summary` lists assertions and `gates: []`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known values: the quantization fixture above; an empty campaign plus one dispatch gives `cells_changed` equal to the dispatch map's filled cells and coverage before of 0.
- KA-386 underlies the order-independence assertion.
- Negative controls: a planted mean summed in slot order must change a descriptor under slot permutation and fail the invariance test; a planted update that keeps the first arrival on equal score must make the two campaign orders disagree; a block without coverage must be rejected by the schema.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1, 8, 24; debug and release; per back end against class D2.

## Considerations

- Descriptor definitions decide what "different" means; a poor choice fills the map with near-duplicates. The specification is content-addressed so that a change is a new map, and the default per task family is reviewed with the task.
- A design scored with few worlds has a noisy descriptor and a noisy score; it can enter a cell it does not belong to and can displace a better elite. The count per cell and the provisional standing are the visible guards; confirmation is the gate bead's job.
- Under a family policy, designs outside the policy's training generator support are out of distribution and their scores are not a rung of the evaluation ladder; such designs are listed under `skipped` with that reason once the support test exists.
