## Background

Two fixtures decide whether the transactional step detects contact honestly. Both exist because a plausible shortcut
is wrong, and each ships with the mutation that must fail.

**Thin barrier (the shortcut: "if the gap is positive at both ends of a step, no contact occurred in it").** A
sample sphere of radius 2 mm moves at 5 m/s with a step h = 2 ms, so it travels 10 mm in a step. A plate 2 mm thick
lies 1 mm ahead. Clearing the plate takes 1 + 4 + 2 = 7 mm (the gap, the sphere's diameter, the plate), so at the
end of the step the sphere is 3 mm beyond the far face. Both endpoints are clear and the step encloses a collision.
Under a transactional profile the broad phase runs on bounds swept over the sub-interval and events are located, so
the hit must be found every time.

**Sharp sill (the shortcut: "friction memory can stay on a contact when its feature changes").** A torque-driven
wheel climbs a sharp sill: its contact moves from the flat, to the edge, to the top. A contact carries tangential
memory (stick anchor, accumulated tangential displacement, warm-start impulse). If that memory is kept on a feature
that changed, a couple appears that the contact forces do not explain. The rule (INV-TRAC-06) is that tangential
memory is transported or reset at every witness change and each reset is an event.

GATE-TRAC-06 (claim: Known answer; TARGET): zero missed hits in 10⁶ randomized thin-barrier crossings under
transactional profiles, and the endpoint-only mutation caught; on the sill, work and impulse within 1% of their
refined values at h/4 and no couple above 10⁻⁹ N·m in `f64`; the capacity-bias comparison labelled. This bead owns
the thin-barrier referee rows at M1 (its seed done-condition) and the sill fixture with its oracles. The sill's
referee rows need witness migration wired into the transaction (BD-093, a later bead that uses this fixture), so
they are measured and recorded by N18b-38, which depends on both and runs the sill at the tolerances it decides.
The `strict` rows close at M2 (BD-161), the batched rows and the capacity-bias label at M2 (BD-168).

## Technical approach

Fixtures live in `crates/frob-conformance/src/fixtures/{thin_barrier,wheel_over_lip}.rs` and are reachable by
name: identities `fixture:thin-barrier` and `fixture:wheel-over-lip`, run from the command line as `frob sim run
--fixture thin-barrier` and `--fixture wheel-over-lip` (the `--fixture` input of `sim.run`; the positional
`fixture:<name>` form is ruled out by VERBS-sim). The sill keeps the name and the geometry of the airlock's
INT-FSIM-07 probe (BD-035), so the refusal half at M0, the completion under migration (BD-093) and the gate rows
(N18b-38) all run one fixture.

**Thin barrier.**
1. Canonical case: sphere radius r = 2 mm, speed v = 5 m/s along +x, h = 2 ms, a plate of thickness w = 2 mm whose
   near face is d = 1 mm from the sphere surface. Endpoint gaps: +1 mm before, +3 mm after.
2. Randomized family (seeded, 10⁶ valid crossings): r ∈ [1, 10] mm; v ∈ [0.5, 10] m/s; w ∈ [0.5, 5] mm;
   incidence angle θ up to 60° from the plate normal; h = 2 ms; d uniform in (0, v·h·cos θ − 2r − w), the travel
   along the plate normal being v·h·cos θ, so that the endpoint pose is clear on the far side; plate as a thin box
   primitive whose lateral extent exceeds the lateral travel v·h·sin θ plus 2r, so the path cannot pass its edge.
   A draw for which v·h·cos θ − 2r − w ≤ 0 (no clear far endpoint exists) is discarded and counted; draws continue
   until 10⁶ valid crossings have run. Draws are addressed by `(seed, index)` so any crossing can be replayed
   alone.
3. Oracle: every crossing is a hit by construction. The step must commit with a touchdown event located inside
   the sub-interval, or (if the law is inelastic) leave the sphere on the near face. A committed interval whose end
   pose is beyond the plate with no event is a missed hit.
4. Event time check on the canonical case: touchdown at t* = d/v = 0.2 ms after the interval start, located to the
   dyadic grid (2⁻¹⁶ of the base interval, about 31 ns at 2 ms).
5. Mutant `endpoint-only`: the narrow phase is asked only at the two endpoints and the swept broad phase is
   bypassed. It must report at least one miss on the canonical case and the test must fail.
6. **The `sim.run` input `--count <n>` (defined here; VERBS-sim's row names this bead as its owner, M1).** Domain: a
   positive integer, the number of **valid** draws of the fixture's randomized family to run; draws are taken at
   indices 0, 1, 2, … of the address `(seed, index)` under `--seed` until n valid draws have run, and a discarded
   draw (here: v·h·cos θ − 2r − w ≤ 0) is counted and never run. Without `--count` the fixture runs its canonical
   case only. The value is written into the run manifest and so enters `RunId`. Fixture-scoped: `--count` without
   `--fixture`, with a fixture that declares no randomized family (`wheel-over-lip`), or with 0 or a non-integer
   is `FROB-REF-802`, exit 3, `pointer` `/count`. Output block `draws` of `frob.sim.run.out/1`: `valid`,
   `discarded`, `missed_hits`, `refused_220` (crossings ended by `FROB-REF-220`), and for the first missed or
   refused draw its `(seed, index)` and parameters (r, v, w, θ, d), so it can be replayed alone.

**Wheel over a sharp sill.**
1. Geometry (DESIGN defaults): wheel radius R = 0.10 m, mass 5 kg, sill height s = 0.03 m with a sharp 90° lip,
   μ = 0.8, constant axle torque τ = 5 N·m, base h = 2 ms. Quasi-static check that the fixture climbs: the wheel
   pivots about the lip when τ > m·g·√(2·R·s − s²) = 5 × 9.81 × 0.0714 = 3.50 N·m.
2. Expected feature sequence of the wheel's witness on the environment: `flat` → `edge` → `top`. The lip is
   first touched while the flat contact is still active (centre √(2·R·s − s²) = 0.071414 m before the lip), so
   the edge contact appears as `new-contact` with zero memory and exactly one `witness-reset` event, the flat
   contact then separates as the wheel pivots, and the edge-to-top change is `adjacent-feature` with memory
   transported (the normal is vertical on both sides). Expected at the default tolerances: one reset, one
   transport (the geometry is BD-093's).
3. Refinement: run at h, h/2 and h/4. Quantities compared: total work done by the axle torque up to the instant
   the wheel centre passes the lip; total contact impulse (normal and tangential, world frame) over the same span.
   Each at h must lie within 1% of its value at h/4.
4. Unexplained couple: at every committed sub-interval, the wheel's angular momentum change about its centre minus
   the impulse of the applied torque minus Σ rᵢ × pᵢ over contact impulses pᵢ at lever arms rᵢ. Its magnitude divided
   by the sub-interval length must stay below 10⁻⁹ N·m in `f64`.
5. Mutant `keep-memory` (the switch is N18a-14's): tangential memory is copied unchanged across a feature
   change. On the referee it must produce a couple above the bound or break the 1% convergence, and the test
   must fail.
6. The tolerances of migration (OPEN-TRAC-5: the continuity tolerance δ_w, default the sample radius; the largest
   normal rotation transported, default 30°) are read from the plan's constants, and outside them the
   fail-closed rule holds (reset with a `witness-reset` event). The sweep that decides OPEN-TRAC-5 on this
   fixture is N18b-38, which depends on this bead and reuses `fixture:wheel-over-lip` with the tolerances passed
   as test-only plan parameters; the fixture therefore accepts them as inputs.
7. What this bead runs on the sill. Items 2 to 4 are oracles over a run's event record and committed intervals:
   a feature-sequence extractor that reports each change class and counts resets against `witness-reset` events,
   a refinement comparator for work and impulse across h, h/2, h/4, and the unexplained-couple computation. This
   bead proves them on hand-built synthetic traces (a correct trace passes; a trace with a silent reset, a
   wrong class, a 2% work difference or a 10⁻⁶ N·m residual couple fails). The referee run of the fixture at the
   decided tolerances, with the `keep-memory` mutant, is N18b-38's, because the transaction cannot complete the
   sill before BD-093 wires migration into it.

Outputs: claim Known answer, class `Tested`, tier `referee`, determinism D2 per platform. Every run stores the
event list (kind, time as a rational, feature ids) so the sequence can be diffed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 10⁶ seeded thin-barrier crossings on the referee: zero missed hits; the count of discarded draws is reported (the seed done-condition).
- [ ] The `endpoint-only` mutant is caught (it misses the canonical case).
- [ ] The canonical touchdown is located at 0.2 ms within one dyadic cell.
- [ ] Sill fixture `wheel-over-lip` defined in `frob-conformance` with BD-035's geometry (R 0.10 m, 5 kg, s 0.03 m, μ 0.8, τ 5 N·m, h 2 ms) and reachable by name; the expected record is feature sequence `flat, edge, top`, `new-contact` with one `witness-reset` at the lip, `adjacent-feature` transported at the top.
- [ ] The sill oracles (feature sequence and change classes, reset-to-event audit, work and impulse refinement at 1%, unexplained couple at 10⁻⁹ N·m) pass a correct synthetic trace and fail each planted bad trace.
- [ ] The sill fixture takes δ_w and the rotation limit as test-only parameters (for the OPEN-TRAC-5 sweep of N18b-38), with the defaults (sample radius, 30°) when none are passed.
- [ ] For the 10⁶ crossings, the number of discarded draws and the number of crossings ended by `FROB-REF-220` are both reported; the latter is zero.
- [ ] `frob sim run --tier referee --fixture thin-barrier --count 10000 --seed 7 --key tb-count-7 --json` reports the `draws` block (`valid = 10000`, `discarded`, `missed_hits = 0`, `refused_220 = 0`); `--count` without `--fixture`, with `--fixture wheel-over-lip`, or with `--count 0` is `FROB-REF-802`, exit 3, `pointer` `/count`.
- [ ] The thin-barrier rows of GATE-TRAC-06 on the referee are recorded by this bead (the one recorder of those rows) with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-06 --receipt <hash>` from the receipt of the long-lane run `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-TRAC-06/referee/thin-barrier`, with measured values, commit, fingerprint and seed; the sill rows are N18b-38's.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `thin_barrier_canonical_hit`: asserts one touchdown event with t* − t0 = 0.2 ms ± 31 ns and no pose beyond the plate.
- `thin_barrier_random_1e6` (long lane; a 10⁴ subset runs in the default lane): asserts `missed == 0`.
- `thin_barrier_endpoint_only_mutant_misses`: asserts `missed ≥ 1` under the mutant feature flag.
- `sill_oracle_feature_sequence`: on a synthetic record, asserts the ordered feature ids and change classes (`new-contact` at the lip, `adjacent-feature` at the top); a record with the classes swapped, or with a reset and no `witness-reset` event, fails.
- `sill_oracle_refinement`: synthetic work and impulse series at h, h/2, h/4 with differences of 0.5% pass and of 2% fail.
- `sill_oracle_couple`: a synthetic interval whose angular momentum change matches torque and contact moments passes; one with a residual moment of 10⁻⁶ N·m fails.
- `sill_tolerances_are_parameters`: the fixture accepts δ_w and the rotation limit and records them in the run's plan; the expected record it states for δ_w = 0 (every move is a jump) has two `witness-reset` events (both transitions reset), and for the defaults one (the lip's `new-contact`); N18b-38 runs both on the referee.
- The referee tests `sill_feature_sequence`, `sill_refinement_work_impulse`, `sill_no_unexplained_couple` and `sill_keep_memory_mutant_fails` are defined in this fixture's module and run by N18b-38 once BD-093 has landed.
- `thin_barrier_oblique_geometry`: for θ = 60°, v = 5 m/s, r = 1 mm, w = 2 mm the admissible d range is (0, 1) mm (v·h·cos θ = 5 mm, minus 2r = 2 mm and w = 2 mm); a draw with θ = 60°, r = 2 mm, w = 2 mm (5 − 4 − 2 < 0) is discarded and counted.
- `count_option_contract`: `--count 100` with seed 7 runs exactly 100 valid draws and reports `valid + discarded` equal to the last index drawn plus one; two runs with the same seed and count give the same draw list digest, and `--count 100` and `--count 101` give different `RunId`s; the draw at `(7, i)` run alone reproduces the parameters and event record it had inside the batch; `--count` without a fixture, on `wheel-over-lip`, and `--count 0` each return `FROB-REF-802` with `pointer` `/count`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/traction_referee.sh` (bead N18a-35, which owns the script and depends on this bead), steps
`thin_barrier` and `sill`: `frob sim run --tier referee --fixture thin-barrier --seed <s> --count 10000 --key
traction-referee-thin_barrier-<s> --json` (`--count 1000000` in the `--long` lane), and, once BD-093 has wired
migration into the transaction (a dependency of N18a-35's sill step, not of this bead), `frob sim run --tier
referee --fixture wheel-over-lip --step <h> --key traction-referee-sill-<h>-<s> --json` at h = 2, 1 and 0.5 ms as
a regression at the registry tolerances (the sill rows themselves are N18b-38's). Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, the `draws` block, the metrics
`missed_hits` (threshold 0), `discarded_draws`, `touchdown_error_s`, `work_rel_diff`, `impulse_rel_diff`,
`max_couple_nm` with thresholds, the event-sequence digest, and on failure the seed and index of the first missed
crossing or the first offending sub-interval with the `.frun` path.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-408 (thin barrier: 10 mm of travel, 7 mm to clear, 3 mm beyond; zero missed hits in 10⁶) and KA-409 (sill:
sequence flat, edge, top; 1% at h/4; 10⁻⁹ N·m). Counterexamples CX-169 and CX-183 are the two mutants. The
admission test INT-FSIM-04 (a thin step between two clear endpoint poses detected on 1,000 of 1,000 seeded sweeps by
the sibling's spacetime broad phase) is the sibling-side counterpart. Metamorphic: translating or rotating the
fixture, and halving every length with the speed, leave `missed_hits` at zero. Determinism: the same seed gives the
same event digest twice on one machine and in debug and release.

## Considerations

- A dyadic split limit (L_max = 16) or more than 64 events in one base interval ends a transaction with `FROB-REF-220`; such a crossing counts as refused, not as detected, and the count must be zero for the fixture's parameter ranges.
- If the sibling's fixed-witness gate refuses on the sill even with migration between calls, the refusal is surfaced and the row is carried by the `strict` profile; the referee row is then recorded (by N18b-38) with that reason.
- Seed order: BD-093 comes after this bead and depends on it for the fixture, so this bead cannot wait for the migration wiring; that is why the sill's referee rows sit with N18b-38.
- Runtime: 10⁶ crossings of a single sphere are cheap, but the long lane is kept out of the default `cargo test`.
- The lockstep tiers do not locate events; their protection is the speculative margin (N18a-12) and what they still miss is measured by the tunnelling audit, not by this bead.
