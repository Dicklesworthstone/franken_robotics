## Background

Two fixtures decide whether the transactional step detects contact honestly. Both exist because a plausible shortcut
is wrong, and each ships with the mutation that must fail.

**Thin barrier (the shortcut: "if the gap is positive at both ends of a step, no contact occurred in it").** A
sample sphere of radius 2 mm moves at 5 m/s with a step h = 2 ms, so it travels 10 mm in a step. A plate 2 mm thick
lies 1 mm ahead. Clearing the plate takes 1 + 4 + 2 = 7 mm (the gap, the sphere's diameter, the plate), so at the
end of the step the sphere is 3 mm beyond the far face. Both endpoints are clear and the step encloses a collision.
Under a transactional profile the broad phase runs on bounds swept over the sub-interval and events are located, so
the hit must be found every time.

**Sharp sill (the shortcut: "friction memory can stay on a contact when its feature changes").** A torque-driven
wheel climbs a sharp sill: its contact moves from the flat, to the edge, to the top. A contact carries tangential
memory (stick anchor, accumulated tangential displacement, warm-start impulse). If that memory is kept on a feature
that changed, a couple appears that the contact forces do not explain. The rule (INV-TRAC-06) is that tangential
memory is transported or reset at every witness change and each reset is an event.

GATE-TRAC-06 (claim: Known answer; TARGET): zero missed hits in 10⁶ randomized thin-barrier crossings under
transactional profiles, and the endpoint-only mutation caught; on the sill, work and impulse within 1% of their
refined values at h/4 and no couple above 10⁻⁹ N·m in `f64`; the capacity-bias comparison labelled. This bead owns
the referee rows at M1. The `strict` rows close at M2 (BD-161), the batched rows and the capacity-bias label at M2
(BD-168).

## Scope

In: the two fixtures, their randomized generators, their oracles and mutants, on the referee tier under the

## Technical approach

Fixtures live in `crates/frob-conformance/src/fixtures/{thin_barrier,sill}.rs` and are reachable by name
(`fixture:thin-barrier`, `fixture:wheel-sill`).

**Thin barrier.**
1. Canonical case: sphere radius r = 2 mm, speed v = 5 m/s along +x, h = 2 ms, a plate of thickness w = 2 mm whose
   near face is d = 1 mm from the sphere surface. Endpoint gaps: +1 mm before, +3 mm after.
2. Randomized family (seeded, 10⁶ crossings): r ∈ [1, 10] mm; v ∈ [0.5, 10] m/s; w ∈ [0.5, 5] mm; d uniform in
   (0, v·h − 2r − w) so that the endpoint pose is clear on the far side; incidence angle up to 60° from the plate
   normal; plate as a thin box primitive. Cases where the far endpoint is not clear are discarded by construction
   and counted. Draws are addressed by `(seed, index)` so any crossing can be replayed alone.
3. Oracle: every crossing is a hit by construction. The step must commit with a touchdown event located inside
   the sub-interval, or (if the law is inelastic) leave the sphere on the near face. A committed interval whose end
   pose is beyond the plate with no event is a missed hit.
4. Event time check on the canonical case: touchdown at t* = d/v = 0.2 ms after the interval start, located to the
   dyadic grid (2⁻¹⁶ of the base interval, about 31 ns at 2 ms).
5. Mutant `endpoint-only`: the narrow phase is asked only at the two endpoints and the swept broad phase is
   bypassed. It must report at least one miss on the canonical case and the test must fail.

**Wheel over a sharp sill.**
1. Geometry (DESIGN defaults): wheel radius R = 0.10 m, mass 5 kg, sill height s = 0.03 m with a sharp 90° lip,
   μ = 0.8, constant axle torque τ = 5 N·m, base h = 2 ms. Quasi-static check that the fixture climbs: the wheel
   pivots about the lip when τ > m·g·√(2·R·s − s²) = 5 × 9.81 × 0.0714 = 3.50 N·m.
2. Expected feature sequence of the wheel's witness on the environment: `flat` → `edge` → `top`, with change
   classes `adjacent-feature` at both transitions and no `witness-reset` while the world contact point moves
   continuously.
3. Refinement: run at h, h/2 and h/4. Quantities compared: total work done by the axle torque up to the instant
   the wheel centre passes the lip; total contact impulse (normal and tangential, world frame) over the same span.
   Each at h must lie within 1% of its value at h/4.
4. Unexplained couple: at every committed sub-interval, the wheel's angular momentum change about its centre minus
   the impulse of the applied torque minus Σ rᵢ × pᵢ over contact impulses pᵢ at lever arms rᵢ. Its magnitude divided
   by the sub-interval length must stay below 10⁻⁹ N·m in `f64`.
5. Mutant `keep-memory`: tangential memory is copied unchanged across a feature change. It must produce a couple
   above the bound or break the 1% convergence, and the test must fail.
6. The tolerances of migration (OPEN-TRAC-5: the continuity tolerance δ_w, default the sample radius; the largest
   normal rotation transported, default 30°) are swept on this fixture: δ_w ∈ {½, 1, 2} × sample radius and
   rotation limits {15°, 30°, 45°}; the bead records which settings pass and proposes the values to freeze. The
   fail-closed default until then is reset with an event.

Outputs: claim Known answer, class `Tested`, tier `referee`, determinism D2 per platform. Every run stores the
event list (kind, time as a rational, feature ids) so the sequence can be diffed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 10⁶ seeded thin-barrier crossings on the referee: zero missed hits; the count of discarded draws is reported (the seed done-condition).
- [ ] The `endpoint-only` mutant is caught (it misses the canonical case).
- [ ] The canonical touchdown is located at 0.2 ms within one dyadic cell.
- [ ] Sill: feature sequence `flat, edge, top`; work and impulse at h within 1% of h/4; no couple above 10⁻⁹ N·m.
- [ ] The `keep-memory` mutant is caught.
- [ ] Every tangential-memory reset on the sill appears as a `witness-reset` event in the record; none is silent.
- [ ] The OPEN-TRAC-5 sweep is recorded with a proposed δ_w and rotation limit.
- [ ] GATE-TRAC-06 referee rows are recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with measured values, commit, fingerprint and seed.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `thin_barrier_canonical_hit`: asserts one touchdown event with t* − t0 = 0.2 ms ± 31 ns and no pose beyond the plate.
- `thin_barrier_random_1e6` (long lane; a 10⁴ subset runs in the default lane): asserts `missed == 0`.
- `thin_barrier_endpoint_only_mutant_misses`: asserts `missed ≥ 1` under the mutant feature flag.
- `sill_feature_sequence`: asserts the ordered feature ids and change classes.
- `sill_refinement_work_impulse`: asserts relative differences ≤ 0.01 between h and h/4.
- `sill_no_unexplained_couple`: asserts max couple ≤ 10⁻⁹ N·m.
- `sill_keep_memory_mutant_fails`: asserts the couple bound or the convergence bound is violated under the mutant.
- `sill_tolerance_sweep`: runs the nine settings and writes the table.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/traction_referee.sh` (bead N18a-35), steps `thin_barrier` and `sill`: `frob sim run --tier referee
--fixture thin-barrier --seed <s> --count 1000000 --json` and `frob sim run --tier referee --fixture wheel-sill
--json` at three step sizes. Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, the metrics
`missed_hits` (threshold 0), `discarded_draws`, `touchdown_error_s`, `work_rel_diff`, `impulse_rel_diff`,
`max_couple_nm` with thresholds, the event-sequence digest, and on failure the seed and index of the first missed
crossing or the first offending sub-interval with the `.frun` path.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-408 (thin barrier: 10 mm of travel, 7 mm to clear, 3 mm beyond; zero missed hits in 10⁶) and KA-409 (sill:
sequence flat, edge, top; 1% at h/4; 10⁻⁹ N·m). Counterexamples CX-169 and CX-183 are the two mutants. The
admission test INT-FSIM-04 (a thin step between two clear endpoint poses detected on 1,000 of 1,000 seeded sweeps by
the sibling's spacetime broad phase) is the sibling-side counterpart. Metamorphic: translating or rotating the
fixture, and halving every length with the speed, leave `missed_hits` at zero. Determinism: the same seed gives the
same event digest twice on one machine and in debug and release.

## Considerations

- A dyadic split limit (L_max = 16) or more than 64 events in one base interval ends a transaction with `FROB-REF-220`; such a crossing counts as refused, not as detected, and the count must be zero for the fixture's parameter ranges.
- If the sibling's fixed-witness gate refuses on the sill even with migration between calls, the refusal is surfaced and the row is carried by the `strict` profile; the referee row is then recorded with that reason.
- Runtime: 10⁶ crossings of a single sphere are cheap, but the long lane is kept out of the default `cargo test`.
- The lockstep tiers do not locate events; their protection is the speculative margin (N18a-12) and what they still miss is measured by the tunnelling audit, not by this bead.
