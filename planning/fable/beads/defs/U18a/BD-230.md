## Background

A constant friction coefficient per floor texel misses the effect that decides slips on a lubricated floor:
friction depends on how long and how hard the foot has pressed. This law captures that at the contact, with no
fluid solver. (A fluid lattice in a training batch is refuted by arithmetic: a spill 0.1 to 1 mm thick needs cells
of 0.25 mm, about 39 GB per square metre of floor.) Worked values, hand-checkable from the integrated law below,
for a thick film thinning to 10 µm: water (1 mPa·s) under a patch of radius 2 cm carrying 100 N takes 0.038 s;
cooking oil (60 mPa·s) under the same patch takes 2.3 s; water under a patch of radius 6 cm carrying 400 N takes
0.76 s. Against a stance of about 0.3 s, a small foot on water reaches boundary friction almost at once, while oil
and large flat feet ride a film for the whole stance. A map of constant coefficients cannot represent that
difference (counterexample CX-177).

Ownership is split: Habitat (`frob-scene`) owns the film patch (a region with a fluid class, an initial thickness
h₀ and a wetness latent) and the thickness state; Traction (`frob-contact`) owns the law and the cone row that
consumes the coefficient. In this Solid tier the patch is static and inexhaustible: the robot neither removes nor
carries film. Films the robot spreads, slosh and coupled fluid islands are later Frontier and Moonshot work that
write the film field this law reads.

Decision ADR-TRAC-06: a friction coefficient that depends on state (film thickness, sliding speed, temperature) is
evaluated from committed state and held constant inside a solve, so the step stays strictly convex; the lag is one
step and is a measured quantity in the fidelity ledger. The law is semi-empirical; its outputs are classed
`Estimated` and the Reality claim is `no-claim` until a spill-pad dataset is imported.

## Technical approach

**Inputs, from the interface card for the floor's condition:** viscosity η (Pa·s); initial film thickness h₀ (m,
from the scene's film field); combined roughness σ (m); boundary-lubricated coefficient μ_b; dry coefficient μ_d
that applies where there is no film; the calibrated ranges of Π and h/σ. From the contact: normal load N (N), patch
radius a (m, from the sample geometry), tangential speed v (m/s).

**State.** One film thickness h per contact sample, held in contact memory and in the restore set of the
transactional step; set to h₀ at first touch on a patch and reset when the sample leaves the patch (the lifecycle
rules are N19b-05's contract and are implemented here). Stored as s = h⁻² (m⁻²), because the update is linear in s;
N19b-05's host reference `thin(h, F, η, R, Δt)` takes and returns h = 1/√s.

**Telemetry (DESIGN: the thickness is the state that explains a wet-floor slip, and GATE-HAB-06's script, N19b-06,
reads it back from a run).** This bead declares the channel `film_thickness` in the `contact` stream (N24a-36's
nine streams): level T2 (traces at control rate for the run's address-keyed sample of worlds and every failing
world), kind State (downsampled by a sample or a minimum-maximum envelope), unit m, one column per contact sample,
value h = 1/√s at the end of each committed control interval, and exactly 0 where the sample holds no film (off
every patch, or on a patch with h₀ = 0). It is read with `frob telemetry query <run> --stream contact --channels
film_thickness --json`; a run without T2 recording for the world has no samples of it, marked as a gap.

**Squeeze (Stefan's law).** dh/dt = −2·N·h³/(3π·η·a⁴), which integrates to 1/h² − 1/h₀² = 4·N·t/(3π·η·a⁴). The
update over a sub-interval Δt with the load of that sub-interval is

  s ← s + 4·N·Δt/(3π·η·a⁴),

exact for a load constant over the step, unconditionally stable, and needing one division and one square root. From
a thick film the time to reach thickness h is t = 3π·η·a⁴/(4·N·h²); doubling a multiplies it by 16. This is the
update of N19b-05's `thin` (there with F for N and R for a) and is written on the host once, in that function: the
CPU reference of `K-film` calls it for the state half, and the kernel is its port, equal to it bit for bit in `f64`
with a load constant over the step. The symbolic check of the identity d(h⁻²)/dt = 4·N/(3π·η·a⁴) is N19b-05's
PO-HAB-04 certificate (FrankenSymPy), which this bead depends on; PO-TRAC-02 states the same identity in the
contact chapter's notation and its derivation file (`docs/derivations/po-trac-02.md`) is written by N18b-31, which
depends on this bead and cites that certificate, so this bead does not wait for it.

**Coefficient.** With Λ = h/σ and the blend ψ(Λ) = clamp((3 − Λ)/2, 0, 1) (ψ = 1 for h ≤ σ, ψ = 0 for h ≥ 3σ,
monotone between):

  μ_eff = ψ·μ_b + (1 − ψ)·μ_v,  μ_v = η·v·π·a²/(h·N).

The second term is Couette shear, F = η·v·A/h with A = π·a². μ_v is capped at μ_b (a cap that also guards the
division when N is near zero; a contact where the cap binds is flagged `viscous-capped`). DESIGN (the conflict
settled, stated identically in N19b-05): the scene chapter writes the viscous term capped,
μ_v = min(μ_b, η·|v_t|·π·R²/(h·F)), and the contact chapter writes it uncapped; the capped form governs in both
beads, because without it the coefficient is unbounded as the load goes to zero and the scene chapter's known answer
for the limits (KA-439) is stated with the cap. The contact chapter's "pure Couette shear for h ≥ 3σ" is asserted
where the cap does not bind. With h₀ = 0 the law
returns μ_d exactly, by a select taken before any arithmetic. A squeezed film recovers to μ_b, not to μ_d: a wiped
wet floor is still not a dry one (counterexample CX-178).

**Regime indicator.** The Hersey number Π = η·v/(p·a) with p = N/(π·a²) is reported per contact. Outside the card's
calibrated range of Π or h/σ the result is flagged `extrapolated`; `frob contact probe --film` called with
`--strict-data` (the `strict_data` input of `contact.probe`, VERBS-contact) refuses instead with `FROB-REF-242`
"Film law out of domain" (exit code 3; repairs: use a calibrated card; accept `extrapolated`). A run has no such
option: inside `frob sim run` the contact is flagged `extrapolated` and never refused.

**In the solve.** Order within a step: (1) stage 7, `K-film` reads s, the lagged N and v from committed state,
writes μ_eff and the flags, and `K-cone-setup` consumes μ_eff; (2) the solve runs with μ_eff constant; (3) at
integration the kernel advances s with the solved N. Under a transactional profile the same two halves run per
sub-interval. `K-film` is class `float`, determinism D2, branch-free (selects for the dry case, the two limits and
the cap), with padding lanes holding neutral values. CPU reference in `f64` and `f32`.

**Row queues (KILL-ROWQUEUE).** The film rows are a row class of the solve. KILL-ROWQUEUE is decided at M2 by
N16c-17, a dependency. On its keep branch (the gate has not fired when `K-film` lands), `K-film` gains the
index-list entry point of N16c-13, `(index_list, len, row_bound)`, beside its masked form, with a test that the two
give bit-equal outputs on the same rows; N16c-14 then builds step plans with film rows queued instead of masked.
If the gate fired, `K-film` keeps the masked form only and nothing else changes. Until this bead lands, N16c-14
builds plans with film rows masked.

**Oracles.** The closed form and the time to thickness are checked against the compendium's D.3 oracles of N12-43
(`d3_squeeze_film_closed_form`, `d3_squeeze_film_time_to_thickness`), which implementations never regenerate.

**Referee cross-check.** FACT: `fs-tribo` has `resistive_film` and `stribeck_friction` modules; on the referee the
law's coefficient is compared with them on the gate's states and the difference recorded in the fidelity ledger.

**Verb.** `contact.probe` evaluates the film at a stated contact state and returns μ_eff, the regime (boundary,
mixed, full film, dry), Π, h/σ and validity flags, with evidence class `Estimated`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-TRAC-05: the three Stefan times within 2% of 0.038 s, 2.3 s and 0.76 s; Couette force within 10⁻⁶ relative in `f64`; the dry limit bit-identical to the dry card; μ_b for h ≤ σ, pure Couette shear for h ≥ 3σ (where the cap does not bind) and continuity between, on 10⁴ random states. This bead's tests carry the values; the gate is recorded once, by N18a-37 from its script's receipt (one recorder per gate).
- [ ] GATE-HAB-06: through a compiled scene with a film patch, the three squeeze times within 2%; doubling the pad radius multiplies the time by 16; dry and thick-film limits recover the card coefficients. The one record of this gate is N19b-06's.
- [ ] INV-TRAC-07 holds as a property test; the integrated Stefan law (PO-TRAC-02) is checked numerically here against N19b-05's `thin` and the D.3 oracles of N12-43, and symbolically by N19b-05's PO-HAB-04 certificate (the PO-TRAC-02 derivation file is N18b-31's, which depends on this bead).
- [ ] On KILL-ROWQUEUE's keep branch (N16c-17), the index-list entry point of `K-film` is bit-equal to the masked path; on its fired branch the masked form alone is shipped and the record says so.
- [ ] The coefficient digest is equal before and after the solve on every step of a test run (the coefficient is constant inside a solve).
- [ ] The film state is in the restore set: a forced retry reproduces the first attempt's digest.
- [ ] `film_thickness` is a declared T2 channel of the `contact` stream: on a run recorded at T2 over a film patch, `frob telemetry query <run> --stream contact --channels film_thickness --json` returns per contact sample the thickness in m, equal to 1/√s of the stored state, and 0 for a sample off the patch.
- [ ] `frob contact probe --film --strict-data` outside the card's range returns `FROB-REF-242`; without `--strict-data`, and inside a `frob sim run`, the result carries the `extrapolated` flag.
- [ ] `K-film` has a manifest, CPU reference, differential device check and a registry row; `frob contact probe --film --json` carries the evidence block with class `Estimated`.
- [ ] The measurements for KILL-FILM are produced by N18a-33 and the decision is recorded by N18a-34 (the seed's "KILL-FILM decided").

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `film_stefan_times`: on `K-film`, h₀ = 1 mm to 10 µm at the three cases, integrating with Δt = 2 ms and with one closed-form step; asserts 0.0377 s, 2.262 s, 0.7634 s within 2%, and equality with N19b-05's `thin` bit for bit in `f64` on the CPU reference (the host-side times themselves are N19b-05's test `squeeze_times`).
- `film_blend_values`: ψ(1) = 1, ψ(2) = ½, ψ(3) = 0, clamped outside.
- `film_update_exact_constant_load`: one step of Δt equals n steps of Δt/n to round-off.
- `film_r4_scaling`: a = 2 cm against 4 cm at equal load gives a ratio of 16 within 10⁻¹².
- `film_couette_sled`: η = 0.06 Pa·s, v = 0.5 m/s, a = 0.05 m, h = 0.3 mm, σ = 50 µm (h/σ = 6, full film), N = 50 N, μ_b = 0.1 (the cap does not bind: μ_v = 0.7854/50 = 0.0157 < 0.1); asserts F = μ_eff·N = η·v·π·a²/h = 0.7854 N within 10⁻⁶ relative and no `viscous-capped` flag.
- `film_dry_limit_bits`: h₀ = 0 returns the bit pattern of μ_d for 10⁴ random cards.
- `film_limits_and_continuity`: 10⁴ random states; μ_eff = μ_b for h ≤ σ; equals μ_v for h ≥ 3σ wherever μ_v ≤ μ_b (and μ_b with `viscous-capped` otherwise); |μ_eff(h + δ) − μ_eff(h)| → 0 as δ → 0 across σ and 3σ.
- `film_recovers_to_boundary_not_dry`: a long squeeze ends at μ_b ≠ μ_d.
- `film_cap_flag`: a state with η·v·π·a²/(h·N) > μ_b returns μ_b at h ≥ 3σ with `viscous-capped`.
- `film_strict_data_refuses`: Π outside the card range: the probe with `strict_data` refuses with `FROB-REF-242`; the same state without it, and in a stepped run, is flagged `extrapolated`.
- `film_state_reset_on_leaving_patch`: h returns to h₀ at the next touch.
- `film_thickness_channel`: a T2-recorded referee run of a 2 cm pad under 100 N on a water patch (h₀ = 1 mm) and a second pad 1 m away off the patch: the channel's samples for the first equal 1/√s of the contact memory bit for bit in `f64` and decrease monotonically while the load holds; the second reads exactly 0 at every sample; the channel's kind is State and its unit m in the stream's declaration.
- `film_matches_d3_oracles`: the closed form and the time to 10 µm for the three cases equal N12-43's
  `d3_squeeze_film_closed_form` and `d3_squeeze_film_time_to_thickness` within 10⁻¹² relative.
- `film_index_list_equals_masked` (keep branch of KILL-ROWQUEUE only): 10⁴ random row sets through
  `(index_list, len, row_bound)` and through the mask give bit-equal μ_eff, flags and s.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/traction_film.sh` (bead N18a-37), steps `probe_limits`, `squeeze_times`, `sled`, `scene_patch`. Logged
per STD-E2E: `frob contact probe` (a `read` verb) and `frob sim run … --key <k>` (a `run` verb, one key per run)
command lines, exit codes, wall time, evidence block (class
`Estimated`), measured times, forces and coefficients with units, truths and tolerances, the Hersey number, flags,
coefficient digests before and after the solve, and on failure the first failing state.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-404 (Stefan times), KA-405 (Couette sled), KA-406 (dry card bit for bit), KA-407 (limits and continuity), and
Habitat's KA-437 to KA-439 (exact update, squeeze times, limits). Negative controls: a constant coefficient per
texel (fails the squeeze-time rows); a law that returns μ_d after a squeeze (fails KA-407); a law evaluated from
the candidate state inside the solve (the before/after digest differs). Metamorphic MR-26: zero thickness equals
the dry card bit for bit, the law is continuous in thickness, doubling pad radius multiplies squeeze time by 16.
Determinism: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; across backends per class.

## Considerations

- KILL-FILM (N18a-34): if on the wet-floor battery the law changes fewer than 10% of episode outcomes against a constant patch at μ_b, or adds more than 5% to step time, the lockstep tiers fall back to static condition cards and the law stays on transactional profiles for audits.
- Validity: the law assumes a smooth, rigid, flat pad; treads and rough floors drain faster, so drainage time is an upper bound and predicted slip is pessimistic for treaded soles (HYPOTHESIS, tested only by imported data).
- OPEN-TRAC-3 and OPEN-HAB-8: film cards use generic ranges labelled `assumed`, which keeps every result `Estimated`; this blocks realism claims, not validity. Owner: project lead.
- The cap on μ_v and the specific blend come from the scene-side statement of the law; the "pure Couette shear" limit is therefore tested where the cap does not bind, and the cap's binding is flagged.
- The register lists the scene-side squeeze-time row at 1% in one place and 2% in the gate; the gate's 2% governs.
