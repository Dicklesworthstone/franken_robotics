## Background

At a converged step of the convex contact solve, the Newton matrix H = A + JᵀGJ has already been factored (A is
the positive-definite generalized inertia with implicit damping, J the constraint Jacobian, G the positive
semidefinite curvature of the contact potentials). The same factor can solve many right-hand sides cheaply:
counterfactual impulses ("what if this foot were pushed 4 N sideways"), adjoint columns for sensitivities, and the
whole paired-difference stencil of a design gradient at one state. This is a **diagnostic query, not a stepping
path**. Tier: Frontier, in `frob-contact`.

What the mechanism adds is not speed over a competent baseline. Stated ceiling: against a baseline that also
factors once, the flop ratio is 1. Against a full re-solve per query it is about 14 for 64 columns at 96 rows, and
only for queries of that shape. What it adds is **a bound attached to every column and a guard on the contact
mode**: a linear response on a frozen active set is silently wrong the moment a column would make a sticking
contact slide or an open contact close. Four things make it trustworthy: the identity a batch is bound to (the
anchor), the tests a column must pass, a certified lower eigenvalue bound, and a gate.

Kill gate, named in advance: KILL-RESPONSE (decision bead N18a-26). Trigger: the mode guards reroute more than half
the columns on battery states (measured first by the pilot N18a-25), or a bound violation or false mode acceptance
survives one repair cycle. Fallback: full solves per query.

## Scope

In: the anchor digest; the batch solve; the four per-column acceptance tests; the rounding margin ρ; the certified
λ_lo by exact LDLᵀ; reroutes to the full solve with counting; the verb and its schema; GATE-TRAC-11.

Out: the full solve itself (the lockstep stage-8 solve); the design-gradient stencil that consumes columns (BD-353);
step adjoints over a horizon (the step-adjoint mechanism, which keeps using these single-state columns as its
fallback); the pilot (N18a-25); the kill decision (N18a-26).

## Technical approach

**Anchor (DESIGN).** A batch is keyed by an anchor digest over everything its factor depends on, at one converged
step of one world: the configuration, the mass matrix, the constraint Jacobian, the regularization, the cone
parameters, and the active set with a stick or slip label per row. A batch is served only against the anchor it
was factored for; any other request refactors, and a request that insists on a stale anchor is refused by name
("response batch requested against a stale anchor"; the code number is to be allocated in the refusal registry).

**Batch solve.** For each right-hand side b (a column), x̂ = H⁻¹·b by two triangular solves with the anchor's
Cholesky factor, in the precision of the step that produced the anchor.

**Per-column acceptance.** A column is accepted only when all four hold, each evaluated in that same precision:
1. The residual r̂ = b − H·x̂ is formed explicitly in the working precision, not estimated from the solve, together
   with a bound ρ on the rounding error of forming it: ρ = γ·‖ |H|·|x̂| + |b| ‖₂ with γ = (n + 1)·u/(1 − (n + 1)·u),
   n the row length and u the unit roundoff (2⁻²⁴ in single precision). A residual formed in a wider format uses
   that format's u.
2. Every sticking row remains strictly inside its cone, and the slack is reported.
3. No open row closes and no active normal force changes sign.
4. The bound ‖x̂ − x*‖₂ ≤ (‖r̂‖₂ + ρ)/λ_lo is attached to the column.

Without ρ the expression is not a bound: in single precision H = 3 and b = 1 give x̂ = 0.33333334, a computed
residual of exactly 0 and a true error of 9.9 × 10⁻⁹; with ρ = 2.4 × 10⁻⁷ the bound is 7.9 × 10⁻⁸. A column that
fails test 2 or test 3 is **rerouted** to the full solve and counted.

**The lower bound λ_lo.** Because H = A + JᵀGJ with G positive semidefinite, a lower bound on the smallest
eigenvalue of A is one for H. λ_lo is a shift s for which A − s·I is shown positive definite by the `rational-ldlt`
proof kind (an exact LDLᵀ over the rationals with positive pivots, re-checkable by the offline auditor), computed
once per anchor on the host. A depends on the configuration and not on the active set, so one certificate serves
every column. Where a tighter value is wanted the same proof kind is applied to H − s·I. A batch whose λ_lo has no
certificate reports its bounds as class `Estimated`.

**Verb.** `contact.response`, kind `run`, first milestone M4, cluster `contact`. Input (`frob.contact.response.in/1`):
a run and step (or an anchor digest), a column specification (explicit vectors, named counterfactual loads, or a
stencil), precision. Output (`frob.contact.response.out/1`): the anchor digest; per column the solution, the
attached bound, the cone slack, status `accepted` or `rerouted`, and the class; the rerouted share; λ_lo with its
certificate reference; flop counts. It takes an idempotency key and a budget and the bounded synchronous wait.
Evidence: claim Known answer for the gate rows; bounds are `Certified` when λ_lo carries its certificate, otherwise
`Estimated`; determinism D2.

**Cost model (hand-checkable).** At n = 96 unknowns and 64 columns a full solve per column costs
n³/3 + 2n² = 294,912 + 18,432 = 313,344 flops, 20,054,016 in all; one factor and 64 solves cost
294,912 + 64 × 18,432 = 1,474,560. The ratio is 13.6 and reaches 10 from 23 columns.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-TRAC-11: the three hand fixtures reproduce and the λ = 3 mutant is caught.
- [ ] On 10⁴ battery contact states with 64 random columns each, solved in `f32` and compared with a full `f64` re-solve of every column: zero accepted columns whose true error exceeds the attached bound, and zero accepted columns whose re-solve has a different active set or stick label.
- [ ] The rerouted share is reported per robot class and recorded (the seed done-condition); the measurement feeds KILL-RESPONSE (N18a-26).
- [ ] A batch requested against a changed anchor is refused; a changed configuration, Jacobian, regularization, cone parameter or stick label each changes the digest.
- [ ] λ_lo certificates are emitted as `rational-ldlt` entries in a `.frobcert` bundle and accepted by `frob-verify`; an uncertified batch is labelled `Estimated`.
- [ ] `frob contact response --json` returns the envelope with evidence block; cancellation mid-batch returns no partial column as accepted.
- [ ] The ceiling statement (ratio 1 against a factor-once baseline) is printed with any speed figure the verb reports.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `response_bound_at_equality`: H = [[2, 1], [1, 2]] (eigenvalues 1 and 3, λ_lo = 1), b = (3, 3), x* = (1, 1), x̂ = (1.01, 0.99): residual (−0.01, 0.01) of norm 0.01414 and true error of norm 0.01414; the bound holds with equality (up to ρ).
- `response_lambda3_mutant_caught`: the same case with λ = 3 claims 0.00471 and is violated; the test must flag it.
- `response_mode_guard_block`: a block resting with μ·N = 5 N, anchor in stick: a tangential load of 4 N is accepted (friction answers 4 N, no motion); a load of 6 N is rerouted (the frozen sticking set reports no motion; the true response slides under a net 1 N).
- `response_rho_scalar_case`: single precision, H = 3, b = 1: computed residual 0, true error 9.9 × 10⁻⁹, ρ = 2.4 × 10⁻⁷, bound 7.9 × 10⁻⁸ ≥ true error; without ρ the claimed bound 0 is violated.
- `response_flop_count`: the counter reports 313,344 per full solve, 1,474,560 for the batch, ratio 13.6, crossover at 23 columns.
- `response_anchor_digest_sensitivity`: six single-field edits, six different digests; an unchanged state, an equal digest.
- `response_open_row_closing_reroutes`, `response_normal_sign_change_reroutes`.
- `response_lambda_lo_certificate_checks`: the auditor accepts the certificate and rejects one with a pivot edited to be negative.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/traction_response.sh` (bead N18a-39): runs a short `frob sim run` to a converged step, then `frob
contact response` with the three fixtures and a 64-column random batch, then `frob prove verify` on the bundle.
Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, wall time, evidence block, anchor digest, per-column bound and true error
(from the `f64` re-solve), accepted and rerouted counts with the 50% threshold, λ_lo and its certificate digest,
flop counts, and on failure the first violating column with its right-hand side stored as an artifact.

### Known answers and negative controls
The three hand fixtures above and the scalar rounding case. Negative controls: the λ = 3 mutant; a guard that
ignores the cone slack (the 6 N load is accepted and the gate must catch the false mode acceptance); a bound
without ρ; a batch served against a stale anchor. Metamorphic: scaling H and b by a power of two leaves the
accepted set and the relative bounds unchanged; permuting columns permutes results. Determinism: twice on one
machine; across worker counts; debug and release.

## Considerations

- KILL-RESPONSE fires on a reroute share above one half or on a surviving bound or mode failure; after it fires the verb answers by full solves and keeps its interface.
- λ_lo from A is loose when contact curvature dominates; the tighter certificate on H − s·I costs one exact factorization per anchor on the host and is optional.
- Exact rational LDLᵀ at n = 96 is affordable once per anchor but not per column; it must stay off any stepping path.
- RISK-47: a derivative or response used outside its validity label; the mode guard and the per-column status are the mitigation.
- The stale-anchor refusal has no number in the registry yet; until one is allocated the verb must not ship.
