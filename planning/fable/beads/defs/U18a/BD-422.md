## Background

Derivatives of a rollout with respect to design parameters would let a design search take gradient steps instead
of sampling populations. Through contact they are dangerous: a contact sequence changes discontinuously, a stuck
contact has a zero derivative that says nothing about what happens past breakaway, and a derivative taken on faith
steers a search into a model artifact (RISK-47). The program therefore keeps a non-goal until this milestone
(NONGOAL-15: no gradients through contact in the rigid core before M6; revisit if a rigid legged co-design result
shows contact gradients beating population methods) and admits the mechanism only as a Moonshot, off the critical
path, funded after its pilot. Two earlier pieces already exist and stay the references: labelled derivatives
through isolated events for periodic gaits (BD-334) and paired finite-difference design gradients over short
horizons (BD-353). Single-state adjoint columns are available from the response batch (BD-367).

FACT: several incumbents already differentiate their step, so this is not a differentiator. The value here is the
discipline: every derivative carries a label that says where it is valid, and a query at a grazing event is
labelled or refused, never answered with a bare matrix.

## Scope

In (as children of this epic): the short plan; the pilot; the mechanism (reverse sweep through the converged
contact solve on lockstep tiers, verb `phylum.gradient --method adjoint`); the labels and guards; the gate and the
kill decision. Out: gait-orbit derivatives (BD-334); the finite-difference stencil (BD-353); a first-order policy
learner over short horizons (a separate experiment with its own pilot, not part of this epic).

## Technical approach

**Mechanism in brief.** On lockstep tiers the solve stage returns the velocity v⁺ minimizing a strictly convex
objective Φ(v; z), where z collects the state at the start of the step, the action and the design parameters. At
convergence r(v, z) = ∇ᵥΦ = 0 and H = A + JᵀGJ is already factored. The implicit function theorem gives
dv⁺/dz = −H⁻¹·∂r/∂z. In reverse mode, for the cotangent v̄ of a scalar loss: solve H·λ = v̄ (two triangular solves
with the factor the step holds), then z̄ ← z̄ − (∂r/∂z)ᵀ·λ. Nothing is differentiated through Newton iterations or
the line search. The transposed Jacobians of the kinematics, dynamics, row and integration stages are straight-line
reverse programs derived at build time, each with an instance certificate. Horizon at most 32 control intervals;
step buffers are stored for the whole horizon (4.32 MB per quadruped world at 16 intervals of 10 world-steps;
8.85 GB for 2,048 worlds) and the job is refused when the store does not fit.

**Labels (the done-condition).** `smooth`: every contact and joint row is strictly inside one region of its law
with normalized margin above a guard. `fixed-mode`: taken with active sets frozen. `one-sided`: a row sits on a
boundary; both one-sided values are returned on request. A step whose solve did not converge has no derivative; its
world is dropped from the batch gradient and counted. The label of a horizon gradient is the weakest on its path,
reported with the count of steps under each label. Near a grazing contact, an ambiguous event order, a change of
active set or frictional degeneracy a derivative request is refused with the offending state and directions
(`FROB-REF-263`).

**Guards.** Agreement: in every batch 8 random unit directions are also evaluated by paired central differences on
64 seeds; cosine below 0.65 halves the horizon and repeats; below 0.5 at 8 intervals the verb returns the
finite-difference result labelled `adjoint-rejected`. Smooth metrics only (a binary outcome is refused).
Determinism class D2 or better. A gradient with respect to an inertia is a tangent vector of the inertia cone and
a step along it uses the retraction; under a symmetry of the closed loop the gradient is averaged over the
observation-preserving subgroup.

**Pilot before funding.** Paper-scale and outside the workspace, on an incumbent engine that already ships
derivatives: one quadruped, 30 design parameters, a smooth tracking-and-energy objective over 16 control intervals,
256 worlds, 20 replicate batches; arms at equal wall-clock are adjoint ascent, the stencil and one derivative-free
generation. Falsifier: median cosine against central differences below 0.5, or adjoint ascent needs more than a
quarter of the derivative-free arm's wall-clock to reach the same objective value. Ceiling: one gradient costs
about two sweeps against 2P + 1 = 61 for the stencil at P = 30, at most about 30 times fewer world-steps per
gradient, on smooth objectives where most steps are `smooth`, and nothing where the objective is decided by a
binary outcome or a change of contact sequence.

**Kill gate.** KILL-STEPADJOINT: the pilot's falsifier fires, or the gate fails after one repair cycle, or the
median cosine on the design corpus is below 0.5 at 8 intervals. Fallback: the paired stencil where its own gate
holds, otherwise rank-based search; response batches keep supplying adjoint columns at a single state.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Carried by the children. The two hand fixtures every child must reproduce: (1) scalar implicit function,
Φ(v; z) = ½·m·(v − z)² + ½·g_c·v² gives v⁺ = m·z/(m + g_c) and dv⁺/dz = m/(m + g_c); with m = 2 and g_c = 6 the
derivative is 1/4, with the contact open it is 1, and at the boundary the label must be `one-sided` with values 1
and 1/4. (2) A zero gradient is not a stationary point: a 1 kg block on a floor with μ = 0.5 pushed with force u
for one 0.01 s step breaks away at 4.905 N; at u = 4 N, v⁺ = 0 and dv⁺/du = 0; at u = 6 N, v⁺ = 0.01095 m/s and
dv⁺/du = 0.01 s/kg; under a regularized law with stiction speed 1 mm/s the stick derivative is about
2.0 × 10⁻⁴ s/kg, set by a numerical parameter, so a `fixed-mode` gradient in stick is reported as uninformative.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gradients_through_contact.sh` (written by the mechanism child): `frob phylum gradient --method adjoint`
on the two fixtures and on a 100-case suite. Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, evidence block, the label
and per-label step counts of every gradient, cosine against central differences with its 0.95 and 0.5 thresholds,
refusal events with `FROB-REF-263`, and the sweep's wall time against twice the forward sweep.

### Known answers and negative controls
Suite of 100 short horizons: 80 with no change of active set (cosine at least 0.95 in at least 72) and 20 controls
with a planted change (all labelled `one-sided` or `fixed-mode`, or refused). Negative controls: a matrix returned
at a grazing state; a gradient reported without a label; divergence of a paired stencil ignored.
