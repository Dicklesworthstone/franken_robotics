## Background

Reference robots arrive as MJCF files whose contact behaviour is described in MuJoCo's own soft-contact
parameters. To compare this engine with the pinned MuJoCo oracle on the same model (a Parity claim), those
parameters must be turned into something our laws can run. CL-M is that mapping: a law in the registry, Solid,
admitted **for parity runs only**. The mapping is lossy, and the rule is that **the loss report says so**: every
MuJoCo contact field is listed as mapped exactly, mapped approximately with its residual, or dropped.

Why a separate law identity instead of silently writing CL-1 parameters: a result produced from mapped parameters
must be recognisable as such. RISK-13 (reference robot imports hide lossy contact mappings; leading indicator:
fields missing from a loss report) is mitigated by the loss report and by the `parity-only` label, which the claim
product refuses to cite for any claim other than Parity. For design work the imported model is given interface
cards with provenance instead.

## Scope

In: the field-by-field mapping; the loss report entries; the `LawId::ClM` descriptor and its label; unit tests of
the mapping; two parity fixtures against oracle captures. Out: the MJCF reader and the model-level loss report
(BD-066); oracle capture scripts (BD-067); the compliant potential itself (CL-M evaluates through CL-1's
construction once M2 lands, and through the referee's compliant feet at M1).

## Technical approach

MuJoCo facts below are FACT to recheck against the documentation of the pinned oracle version before a gate cites
them; the mapping is DESIGN.

| MuJoCo field | Meaning there | Mapping | Loss entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| `solref` = (timeconst, dampratio), positive form | Reference acceleration a_ref = −b·v − k·r with b = 2/(d_max·timeconst), k = d/(d_max²·timeconst²·dampratio²), in acceleration space | Physical stiffness k_phys = m_ref·k·d_max and dissipation time τ_d = b/k, with m_ref a recorded nominal effective contact mass | approximate: MuJoCo's stiffness scales with effective mass, ours is N/m; residual reported at the nominal load |
| `solref` negative form (−stiffness, −damping) | Direct stiffness and damping | k_phys and τ_d = damping/stiffness, scaled by d_max | approximate |
| `solimp` = (d₀, d_width, width, midpoint, power) | Position-dependent impedance d(r) ∈ (0, 1) | d_max only | dropped: width, midpoint, power, d₀ |
| `friction[0]` | Sliding coefficient | μ | exact |
| `friction[1]` | Torsional coefficient, a length | r_eff = friction[1]/μ, so the limiting spin moment μ·N·r_eff equals friction[1]·N | exact for the limit; the coupling with sliding follows our one cone |
| `friction[2]` | Rolling coefficient, a length | A rolling-resistance row when rolling rows exist; otherwise none | dropped until rolling rows land |
| `condim` 1, 3, 4, 6 | Contact dimensionality | 1: μ = 0; 3: spin row masked; 4: four-dimensional cone; 6: four-dimensional cone plus rolling rows | exact for 1, 3, 4; approximate for 6 |
| Cone type (pyramidal or elliptic) | Friction cone shape | Always the elliptic cone | approximate when the source is pyramidal, with the residual reported at the tangent diagonal: the source's pyramid is spanned by the edges n ± μ·tᵢ, so its section is the square inscribed in the cone's circle and it grants μ/√2 along a diagonal (about 29% less than the cone), where the axis-aligned box \|f₁\|, \|f₂\| ≤ μ·N of the same μ grants √2·μ (41% more); no pyramid of either kind is a production law here |
| `impratio` | Ratio of frictional to normal impedance | none (a numerical parameter of the source solver) | dropped |
| `margin`, `gap` | Activation distance; inactive band | Base speculative margin m₀ = margin − gap | approximate: `gap` semantics lost |
| `solmix`, `priority`, per-geom friction | Pair combination rules | Resolved per geom pair into one interface entry (by the source's rule: priority, then maximum friction, then `solmix` weights) | exact when resolved; the rule used is recorded |

**Loss report entry (per geom pair).** `{ pair, field, status: exact | approximate | dropped, source_value,
mapped_value, residual, note }`, appended to the import's loss report; the model's field-coverage diff must show
every contact field in exactly one status.

**Label.** Results under CL-M carry `law: CL-M` (the law table's spelling, N18b-28) and `parity-only`; the
evidence block names the oracle pin. A CL-M result cited for Fidelity, Known answer or any design claim is refused
by the claim product.

**Parity fixtures (DESIGN).** The two parity cases are registered fixtures of N24b-03's registry
(`assets/fixtures/<name>/fixture.toml`, schema `frob.fixture/1`, kind `synthetic`), each naming in `designs` the
`DesignId` of the imported MJCF model and in `rows` the oracle capture it is compared with (BD-067's pin):
`clm-box-slide` (a box sliding on a plane, μ = 0.8) and `clm-sphere-rest` (a sphere resting on a plane). At M1 they
run on the referee as `frob sim run --tier referee --fixture <name> --law CL-M --seed <s> --key <k> --json` (the
`--fixture` input of N24b-03 and the `--law` input of N18b-28, both M1; a positional imported design arrives only
with N16b-25 at M2).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every MuJoCo contact field in the table appears in the loss report of an imported model with one of the three statuses; a model with a pyramidal cone reports the cone as `approximate`.
- [ ] Mapping tests reproduce the formulas: timeconst 0.02 s, dampratio 1, d_max 0.9 give b = 111.1 s⁻¹ and k = 2,778 s⁻² (with d = d_max), hence τ_d = 0.04 s.
- [ ] friction = (0.8, 0.02, 0.001) with condim 4 gives μ = 0.8 and r_eff = 25 mm, and the rolling coefficient is listed as dropped until rolling rows exist.
- [ ] Parity fixtures against oracle captures: a box sliding on a plane decelerates at μ·g within 1%; a resting sphere's static penetration is within 5% of the oracle's (TARGET set here; the loss report's residual explains the rest).
- [ ] A CL-M result cited as anything but Parity is refused.
- [ ] The `parity-only` label appears in the envelope of every CL-M run.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `clm_solref_positive_mapping`: the numbers above (b = 2/(0.9·0.02) = 111.1; k = 0.9/(0.81·0.0004) = 2,778; τ_d = b/k = 0.04 s).
- `clm_torsional_to_r_eff`: 0.02/0.8 = 25 mm.
- `clm_condim_cases`: 1, 3, 4, 6.
- `clm_pyramid_reported_approximate`.
- `clm_loss_report_complete`: no MuJoCo contact field without a status (a planted unmapped field fails the test).
- `clm_parity_only_label`, `clm_claim_refused_outside_parity`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/traction_clm_parity.sh`: `frob design import <mjcf> --format mjcf --name <fixture> --key
clm-<fixture>-<source hash> --json` (loss report; the verb line of BD-066), then `frob sim run --tier referee
--fixture <clm-box-slide|clm-sphere-rest> --law CL-M --seed <s> --key clm-run-<fixture>-<s> --json` on the two
parity fixtures, compared with the pinned oracle captures, and the lowercase `--law cl-m` asserted as
`FROB-REF-802`. Logged per STD-E2E: commands,
exit codes, evidence block (law, oracle pin, `parity-only`), the loss-report counts by status, deceleration and
penetration against the oracle with tolerances, and on failure the first differing field or sample.

### Known answers and negative controls
Sliding deceleration μ·g = 7.848 m/s² at μ = 0.8. Negative controls: a mapping that writes a pyramid in place of
the elliptic cone, in either form (the source's edge pyramid gives μ·N/√2 along a tangent diagonal, the
axis-aligned box √2·μ·N; both are caught by the cone's diagonal row, which must give μ·N within 0.5%); a loss
report with the `solimp` shape fields omitted (caught by the completeness test).

## Considerations

- The nominal effective mass m_ref makes the stiffness mapping model-dependent; it is recorded per pair and is the main source of residual.
- Oracle pins are never regenerated from the candidate; if the MuJoCo version changes, the captures and the FACTs above are re-read.
- CL-M is not a route to realism: imported parameters carry no provenance, so design results use interface cards.
