## Background

Each M6 epic needs its own short plan, a paper-scale pilot and a kill rule before work starts. The epic "CL-H on
GPU tiers" (BD-424) would run the hard-contact law's fixed-point iteration on devices: P outer passes, each the
existing convex solve chain with a per-contact shift, all worlds running every pass under a mask. The `batch-cpu`
law (BD-386) settles whether eight passes suffice numerically in `f64` and `f32` on the CPU. Three things remain
unknown for devices and are what this pilot measures: whether P repeated chains fit one static launch graph on
both device families; what a pass costs on a device relative to one CL-1 solve; and whether single-precision
residuals on devices agree with `batch-cpu`.

The plan names no falsifier for this epic's pilot; the thresholds below are proposed here from the gate's own
numbers and are fixed before the run (DESIGN).

## Scope

In: a prototype that reuses the existing stage-8 kernels unchanged and adds two small kernels (shift update,
natural-map residual); the three measurements on one CUDA device and on Metal; the record. Out: the production
port and admission (children of BD-424).

## Technical approach

1. **Register** hypothesis, falsifier, the state sample (the same 10⁴ battery contact states as N18a-22), seeds,
   device fingerprints and commit.
2. **Prototype.** The step plan is captured with the solve chain repeated P = 8 times; between repeats the shift
   kernel writes s_c = μ·‖u_T,c‖ and the residual kernel writes the natural-map residual and updates the `done`
   mask by select. No host round trip between passes.
3. **Measure.** (a) Shape: one captured graph serves every step for 10⁴ steps with no recapture; on Metal, the
   chain fits the per-function argument limit and one synchronous batch per chunk. (b) Cost: step time at P = 8
   against P = 0 (plain CL-1), paired, as a ratio per device. (c) Agreement: the share of states with residual at
   most 10⁻⁴ after eight passes on each device, against `batch-cpu` in `f32` on the same states, and the first
   divergent state if any.
4. **Falsifier (proposed).** Any of: a recapture or a per-world relaunch is needed; the step-time ratio exceeds
   P + 1 = 9 (a pass costs more than one CL-1 solve plus overhead); fewer than 95% of states reach 10⁻⁴ within
   eight passes on a device while `batch-cpu` does.
5. **Record** the result in the review log; the epic's short plan cites it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Registered before the run, with both device fingerprints.
- [ ] The three measurements are recorded per device with intervals; the divergence ledger entry is attached for any disagreement with `batch-cpu`.
- [ ] The verdict is written to the review log and BD-424 proceeds or is closed accordingly.
- [ ] No leaked device resources after cancellation mid-chain (request, drain, finalize).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `clh_gpu_pilot_mask_is_select`: a converged world's state is bit-identical after extra passes.
- `clh_gpu_pilot_kernels_vs_cpu`: shift and residual kernels against CPU references on 10⁴ states.
- `clh_gpu_pilot_graph_static`: the captured graph's digest is constant over the run.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/pilot_clh_gpu.sh`: runs the prototype through `frob sim run --tier gpu-cuda|gpu-metal --law cl-h`
behind a pilot flag. Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, evidence block with tier, fingerprint and seed,
`recaptures` (threshold 0), `step_time_ratio` (threshold 9), `share_within_8_passes` (threshold 0.95), the first
divergent state, and a summary with the falsifier's verdict.

### Known answers and negative controls
The sliding block: no separation velocity on the device within single-precision tolerance. Negative control: P = 0
must reproduce plain CL-1 digests exactly (the prototype adds nothing when the passes are off).

## Considerations

- Single-precision conditioning near rigid contact can stall the iteration on devices where `f64` converges; cards over the scaled-conditioning bound are excluded from the sample and reported.
- If KILL-HARDCONTACT fired at M5, this pilot is not run.
- The cap of four active Moonshot items does not bind this Frontier pilot, but the funding order of M6 epics is an owner decision.
