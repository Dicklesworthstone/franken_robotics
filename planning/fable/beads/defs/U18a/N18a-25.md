## Background

A response batch reuses the factor of a converged contact step to answer many right-hand sides on a **fixed active
set**: counterfactual impulses, adjoint columns, a finite-difference stencil at one state. Its guard sends any
column that would cross a stick, slip or open boundary back to the full solve, because a linear response on a
frozen set is wrong across such a boundary. The mechanism only pays if most columns stay inside their mode. If the
guard reroutes most of them, the batch is a slower way of doing full solves. That share is not known in advance,
so it is the pilot: **the share of columns the guard reroutes on battery states**, measured before the mechanism
(BD-367) is funded. The kill gate it feeds: KILL-RESPONSE, triggered when guards reroute more than half the
columns; fallback, full solves per query.

Ceiling, stated first: against a baseline that also factors once, none in flops; against a full re-solve per query
the flop ratio is about 14 for 64 columns at 96 rows (13.6 exactly: 20,054,016 against 1,474,560), reaching 10
from 23 columns. With a rerouted share ρ_r, the ratio against full re-solves falls to about
1/((1 − ρ_r)/13.6 + ρ_r): 1.9 at ρ_r = 0.5, which is why one half is the kill threshold.

## Scope

In: a host prototype of the guard alone (no certificates, no verb); column families representative of the three
consumers; the rerouted share per robot class and contact mode; the registered falsifier; the record, which is
the first reading of the usefulness trigger of KILL-RESPONSE.

Out: the mechanism, funded only after this pilot: anchors (N18b-39), per-column acceptance and bounds
(N18b-40), λ_lo certificates (N18b-41), factor reuse and the verb (BD-367); the gate-size sweep that measures
the share again (N18b-42); the decision (N18a-26). Also out: the construction's own pilot of factor reuse
(N10a-14, which depends on this bead): on the contact known answers and one 21-contact stack, with twelve
base-wrench columns per state, it checks that the per-column bound (‖b − A·x̂‖ + ρ)/λ dominates the true error
and measures the solve share of a counterfactual burst and the reuse timing. It runs the guard prototype built
here, unchanged, and writes no second guard; the rerouted share that the kill gate reads is this pilot's.

## Technical approach

1. **Register** hypothesis, falsifier, column families, the state sample, seeds and commit before running, as a
   row of `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (schema `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`, N38a-09), with the four
   parts its registration check requires: the falsifier (step 6), the ceiling of the Background (none in flops
   against a factor-once baseline; 13.6 against full re-solves at 64 columns), `fallback.ships` "full solves per
   query" and `fallback.kill_gate` "KILL-RESPONSE".
2. **States.** 10⁴ converged contact states from the standard battery on `batch-cpu`, addressed and stratified by
   robot class and by mode mix (all stick; mixed stick and slip; near lift-off).
3. **Columns, 64 per state, in three families.** (a) Counterfactual loads: tangential and normal impulses at each
   contact with magnitudes drawn log-uniformly from 1% to 100% of the contact's current normal impulse.
   (b) Adjoint columns: unit cotangents on base velocity components. (c) Stencil columns: the right-hand-side
   changes produced by design-parameter perturbations of the size the paired-difference gradient uses.
4. **Guard prototype.** Solve each column with the anchor's factor in `f64`; flag it rerouted if any sticking row
   leaves its cone (slack ≤ 0), any open row closes, or any active normal force changes sign. Verify each flag
   against a full nonlinear re-solve: record false accepts (the re-solve has a different active set or stick
   label though the guard accepted) and false rejects.
5. **Measure.** Rerouted share overall, per class, per mode mix and per column family, with intervals; false
   accepts (must be zero for the guard to be sound) and false rejects (lost benefit).
6. **Falsifier.** The guard reroutes more than half the columns on battery states (overall, or for every column
   family a consumer would use).
7. **Record** the result in the pilot register row and the review log; revise the mechanism's scope (for
   example, offer only the families whose share is low).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Registered before the run; column families and thresholds unchanged afterwards.
- [ ] A table of rerouted shares by class, mode mix and family, with intervals; false-accept and false-reject counts.
- [ ] The implied flop ratio against full re-solves at the measured share is computed and recorded beside the ceiling.
- [ ] The verdict is written to the review log and handed to BD-367 and N18a-26.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `guard_block_4n_accept_6n_reroute`: a block with μ·N = 5 N anchored in stick: a 4 N tangential load is accepted, a 6 N load is rerouted.
- `guard_open_row_closing`: a push that brings a 0.1 mm open gap to contact is rerouted.
- `guard_normal_sign_change`: a pull exceeding the contact's normal force is rerouted.
- `guard_vs_full_resolve`: on 10³ `synthetic` single-contact states the guard has zero false accepts.
- `ratio_formula`: at ρ_r = 0.5 the implied ratio is 1.86; at 0 it is 13.6.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/pilot_response_reroute.sh` (helper of STD-E2E): checks the registration row exists and precedes the
run; snapshots states through `frob sim run <battery design> --scene <battery scene> --tier batch-cpu --seed <s>
--key prr-<stratum>-<batch> --json` (one key per stratum and batch, so no run returns another's stored receipt)
and runs the pilot binary on the snapshots. Logged to `target/e2e-logs/pilot_response_reroute/<utc>.ndjson`,
every line `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block
with battery identity and seed; `metric` lines with `rerouted_share` (threshold 0.5) overall and per stratum
and column family, `false_accepts` (threshold 0), `false_rejects`, `implied_flop_ratio`; on failure the first
false accept with its state address and column; and a summary with the falsifier's verdict.

### Known answers and negative controls
The block fixture (4 N accepted, 6 N rerouted). Negative control: a guard that ignores cone slack accepts the 6 N
load and must be counted as a false accept by the re-solve comparison; a sample of free-flight states (no
contacts) must show a rerouted share of zero.

## Considerations

- Column magnitude drives the share: small perturbations (stencils, adjoints) rarely cross a mode boundary, large counterfactuals often do. The table by family is the useful output, not one number.
- States near lift-off or at the friction limit reroute almost always; their share of battery states is itself a recorded property of the workload.
- A second trigger of the kill gate (a bound violation or a false mode acceptance surviving one repair cycle) is measured later by GATE-TRAC-11, not here; this pilot's false-accept count is its early warning.
