## Background

The response batch (verb `contact.response`, BD-367) is a Frontier diagnostic: it reuses one factor for many
right-hand sides on a fixed active set, attaches an error bound to each column, and reroutes columns that would
cross a contact-mode boundary. It is kept only if it is both useful (most columns stay inside their mode) and
sound (no accepted column violates its bound or hides a mode change). KILL-RESPONSE is the rule that decides, and
the seed bead for the mechanism is done only when this decision is on record.

## Scope

In: applying both triggers to the recorded measurements; checking that at most one repair cycle was taken for
the soundness trigger; the decision; the ADR; downstream changes.

Out: the pilot and the first reading of the usefulness trigger (N18a-25); the mechanism (BD-367 and its
components N18b-39, N18b-40, N18b-41); the gate-size sweep, the repair-cycle re-run and the counts both
triggers are read from (N18b-42, a dependency); entering the ADR at the milestone exit (BD-378).

## Technical approach

**Triggers (TARGET).**
1. *Usefulness.* The mode guards reroute more than half the columns on battery states.
2. *Soundness.* A bound violation or a false mode acceptance that survives one repair cycle (GATE-TRAC-11 requires
   zero accepted columns whose true error exceeds the attached bound and zero accepted columns whose `f64`
   re-solve has a different active set or stick label, on 10⁴ battery contact states with 64 random columns each).

**Milestone.** M4.

**Measurements that decide it.** The rerouted share per robot class from the pilot (N18a-25) and again from the
gate-size sweep of N18b-42; the gate's counts of bound violations and false mode acceptances, before and after at most one
repair cycle.

**Decision rule.**
1. Rerouted share at most one half for the column families the consumers use, and GATE-TRAC-11 passes (possibly
   after one repair cycle): the gate does **not fire**; `contact.response` is admitted.
2. Rerouted share above one half: the gate **fires** on usefulness. If the share is low for one column family
   (for example small stencil perturbations) and high for another, the decision may admit the verb for the low
   family only and records the restriction.
3. A bound violation or false mode acceptance remains after one repair cycle: the gate **fires** on soundness,
   whatever the share.

**Fallback when it fires.** Full solves per query.

**What changes downstream when it fires.**
- `contact.response` keeps its interface and answers every column by a full solve; columns carry status `full-solve`, no fixed-set bound is attached, and the flop counter reports the true cost.
- The paired-difference design gradient (BD-353) and sensitivity analyses that consumed batch columns budget for one full solve per column.
- The step-adjoint epic (BD-422) loses its single-state fallback "response batches keep supplying adjoint columns"; its plan then names the paired stencil as the only fallback.
- No result produced earlier under an accepted batch is invalidated by a usefulness kill; a soundness kill marks earlier accepted columns from the affected version as `unverified` until re-solved.

**Record.** An ADR with both triggers, the measured shares and counts (not the targets), commit, fingerprint,
seeds, any repair taken, the decision, any family restriction and the fallback in force; the gate's row in
`registries/gates.toml` is set accordingly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists and cites the pilot's and the gate's recorded numbers.
- [ ] At most one repair cycle is recorded for the soundness trigger.
- [ ] If fired: `frob contact response --json` returns columns with status `full-solve` and the verb's cost model reports the full cost; a test shows it.
- [ ] If not fired: the verb is listed as admitted with the rerouted share per robot class in its documentation.
- [ ] The decision is taken by a session that did not implement BD-367.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `kill_response_rule_table`: (share, soundness) pairs map to `not-fired`, `fired-usefulness`, `fired-soundness`; a low share with one surviving false acceptance is `fired-soundness`.
- `kill_response_fallback_status`: with the gate marked fired, every column's status is `full-solve`.
- `kill_response_family_restriction`: a restriction to the stencil family refuses a counterfactual-load batch by name.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_response_record.sh`: reads the pilot log and the GATE-TRAC-11 record, recomputes the decision,
compares with the registry, and (when fired) runs the fallback check. Logged per STD-E2E: `rerouted_share`
against 0.5 per class, `bound_violations` and `false_mode_acceptances` against 0, the decision and the ADR path.

### Known answers and negative controls
Rule fixtures: a share of 0.35 with zero violations gives `not-fired`; 0.62 gives `fired-usefulness`; 0.20 with
one false acceptance after repair gives `fired-soundness`. Negative control: a record with two repair cycles is
rejected.

## Considerations

- Priority is one step above its milestone because gradient and sensitivity consumers size their budgets on it.
- The implied benefit at a share of one half is under a factor of two against full re-solves; keeping the mechanism below that would be complexity without a return.
- A soundness kill is the more serious outcome: it means a column was reported with a bound it did not satisfy, and the ADR must say how that was detected.
