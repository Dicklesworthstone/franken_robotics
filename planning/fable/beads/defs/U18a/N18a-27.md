## Background

The four-dimensional friction cone couples sliding and spinning through one length, the **effective patch radius**
r_eff: the cone is √(λ_t1² + λ_t2² + (λ_s/r_eff)²) ≤ μ·λₙ, so the largest spin moment a contact can transmit is
μ·N·r_eff. r_eff is a property of the pressure distribution over the contact patch, not of the body's size alone:

- uniform pressure on a disk of radius a: torsional capacity (2/3)·μ·N·a, so r_eff = 2a/3;
- Hertzian pressure (a sphere pressed on a plane): (3π/16)·μ·N·a, so r_eff = 3π·a/16 ≈ 0.59·a.

A wrong r_eff shifts how much sliding friction a pivoting foot keeps, so its source must be explicit and recorded.
OPEN-TRAC-2 asks which source governs: the sample geometry, a pressure assumption per material, or a card field.
Default in force until decided: **2a/3 from sample geometry, overridden by a card**; decided with GATE-TRAC-04.
A contact with r_eff = 0 masks its spin row and is the three-dimensional cone. r_eff is classed `Estimated` unless
calibrated.

## Scope

In: the two derivations on file (PO-TRAC-04) with a symbolic check; the resolution order that produces r_eff per
contact (end-effector declaration, card, sample geometry); its provenance in the model and in results; the
three-way measurement and the ADR that settle OPEN-TRAC-2, written when GATE-TRAC-04 is recorded; exposure
through `contact.probe`. The measurement feeds r_eff values straight into the cone's parameter row, so this
bead does not wait for the card kind.

Out: the cone itself (BD-154); the interface-card kind with its `r_eff` and pressure-model fields, the mask of
the spin row in the parameter rows, and the spin rows of GATE-TRAC-04 whose record cites this bead's ADR
(BD-167, which depends on this bead); the error of the ellipsoidal approximation against patch quadrature
(N18a-28).

## Technical approach

**Derivations (PO-TRAC-04).** For an axisymmetric pressure p(r) on a disk of radius a with ∫ p dA = N, the
limiting torsional moment under pure spin is M = ∫₀ᵃ μ·p(r)·r·2π·r dr.
- Uniform, p = N/(π·a²): M = 2π·μ·p·a³/3 = (2/3)·μ·N·a.
- Hertzian, p(r) = p₀·√(1 − r²/a²) with N = (2/3)·π·a²·p₀: M = 2π·μ·p₀·a³·∫₀¹ x²·√(1 − x²) dx = 2π·μ·p₀·a³·(π/16)
  = (3π/16)·μ·N·a.
Both are written in `docs/derivations/torsional_capacity.md` and checked by a FrankenSymPy script that evaluates
the two integrals symbolically (class `Certified` for the identities).

**Resolution order per contact (DESIGN, the default of OPEN-TRAC-2).** This bead owns the order; BD-167 supplies
the card fields it reads and masks the spin row in the parameter rows.
1. If the end effector's `foot-patch` law declares an explicit torsion radius, use it (provenance `design`); a
   value above the foot's own contact radius is refused at design time (`FROB-REF-121`).
2. Otherwise, if the interface card for the material pair and condition carries `r_eff`, use it; else if its
   `pressure` model is not `Unspecified`, apply it to the sample's patch radius a (`Uniform`: 2a/3; `Hertzian`:
   3π·a/16); provenance `card` in both cases, and the card's `r_eff` takes precedence over its pressure model
   when both are present. The field names are BD-167's (`r_eff: Option<Length>`, `pressure: PressureModel`);
   the enum `PressureModel { Uniform, Hertzian, Unspecified }` is defined here beside the two derivations that
   give its values meaning, and BD-167's card field has this type. The resolution function takes these values
   as plain inputs (`CardRadiusSource { r_eff, pressure }`), so it is built and tested before the card kind.
3. Otherwise r_eff = (2/3)·a with a the sample's patch radius from the sample geometry (provenance `geometry`).
4. A point sample (a = 0) has r_eff = 0 and a masked spin row.
r_eff < 0 reaching the resolution is refused with `FROB-REF-240` (the same refusal BD-167 raises when a card with
r_eff < 0 is loaded). The resolved value and its provenance are stored per sample in the compiled model, so they
are part of the model identity, and printed by `contact.probe`.

**Deciding OPEN-TRAC-2.** With GATE-TRAC-04's fixtures in hand (BD-154), run the spin-stop and coupled-sliding
rows three ways: r_eff from geometry with uniform pressure; from a per-material pressure assumption (compliant
soles uniform, hard spherical feet Hertzian); from a card field fitted to the referee's moment. The reference is
the referee's own result (BD-080, through `contact.probe` of N18b-29): the same foot or wheel is represented by a
dense test layout of point samples, at most 21 (the referee's capacity), each an exact-Coulomb point contact with
the referee's normal impulses, so the limiting spin moment is the referee's load-weighted sum
μ·Σᵢ λₙ,ᵢ·‖xᵢ‖ and the stop time is the referee's run. Record, per reference foot and wheel, the error of the
limiting spin moment and of the stop time against that reference, and the layout used. The patch-quadrature
reference of N18a-28 is not used here: it depends on this bead (through BD-167 and N24b-12) and measures the
cone's error over the whole limit surface afterwards, never the choice of r_eff source. Recommendation rule (proposed, fixed before measuring): keep the default unless the
per-material assumption reduces the spin-moment error by more than half on at least two reference contacts, in
which case the per-material profile becomes the default source and the card override stays.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] PO-TRAC-04: both derivations are on file and the symbolic check reproduces 2/3 and 3π/16.
- [ ] For a = 30 mm: r_eff = 20 mm (uniform) and 17.7 mm (Hertzian); with μ = 0.6 and N = 100 N the pure-spin limit is 1.2 N·m (uniform).
- [ ] The resolution order is implemented and tested, with provenance recorded per sample and printed by `contact.probe`.
- [ ] The three-way measurement against the referee's dense-layout moment and stop time is recorded per reference foot and wheel, and OPEN-TRAC-2 is resolved as an ADR that BD-167 cites when GATE-TRAC-04 is recorded.
- [ ] The referee reference reproduces KA-397 for a uniform disk (21 samples on rings at equal-area radii): stop time within 2% of 0.153 s.
- [ ] Numerical quadrature of both pressure profiles (10⁶ points) reproduces the closed forms to 10⁻⁶ relative.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `r_eff_uniform_is_two_thirds_a`, `r_eff_hertz_is_3pi_over_16_a`: closed forms against numerical quadrature.
- `r_eff_resolution_order` (on `CardRadiusSource` inputs, no card kind needed): an explicit torsion radius on the end effector wins; then the card's `r_eff` wins, also over a card `pressure` model; a `Hertzian` pressure model gives 17.7 mm and `Uniform` 20 mm at a = 30 mm, each over geometry; geometry default otherwise, also with `pressure: Unspecified`; point sample gives zero and a masked row.
- `r_eff_negative_refuses_240`: a negative r_eff from an end-effector declaration or a `CardRadiusSource` input refuses with `FROB-REF-240` (the card-load refusal is BD-167's `card_invalid_cone`).
- `r_eff_in_model_identity`: changing the resolved r_eff of a sample (through the end-effector torsion radius, or a `CardRadiusSource` input of the compiled model) changes `ModelId`; the same check through a loaded card's `r_eff` field is BD-167's, which depends on this bead.
- `spin_limit_moment`: a disk a = 30 mm, μ = 0.6, N = 100 N transmits 1.2 N·m ± 0.5% before spinning.
- `symbolic_integrals_check`: the FrankenSymPy script's output equals the stored constants.
- `referee_dense_layout_moment`: for a uniform disk a = 30 mm, 21 samples of equal area, μ = 0.6, N = 100 N, the load-weighted sum μ·Σᵢ λₙ,ᵢ·‖xᵢ‖ is within 2% of 1.2 N·m; a layout of 22 samples is refused by the referee's capacity check (`FROB-REF-721`) and is never silently truncated.
- `open_trac2_rule_fixed`: the recommendation rule, applied to planted error tables (per-material halves the error on two contacts; on one contact only), returns `switch` and `keep` respectively.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/traction_cone_rows.sh` (bead N18a-36), step `r_eff`: `frob contact probe` on a foot with and without

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-398 (r_eff = 2a/3 and 3π·a/16) and KA-397 (spin stop at t = 3·a·ω₀/(4·μ·g) = 0.153 s for a = 0.05 m,
ω₀ = 20 rad/s, μ = 0.5 under uniform pressure). Negative control: r_eff = a for a uniform disk makes the disk stop
in 0.102 s instead of 0.153 s and must fail the row.

## Considerations

- Real soles are neither uniform nor Hertzian; the card field exists so a calibrated value can replace both, and without calibration every result that depends on r_eff stays `Estimated`.
- r_eff also converts MuJoCo's torsional friction coefficient on import (a length divided by μ); that mapping is the parity-only law's.
- Tiny positive r_eff values scale the spin row badly; the solve's power-of-two diagonal scaling handles the conditioning, and a value below a card-declared floor is treated as a point contact with a recorded flag.
