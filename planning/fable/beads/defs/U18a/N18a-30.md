## Background

Dry friction often falls from a static level to a lower kinetic level as sliding speed rises (the Stribeck
effect). It produces stick-slip: a foot or a wheel that chatters instead of sliding smoothly. In this system
velocity-dependent dry friction **uses the lagged-coefficient mechanism** (the coefficient is evaluated from
committed state and held constant inside a solve) and **stays Frontier until it has cards with provenance**: a
Stribeck curve with invented parameters would add a behaviour nobody can defend.

Two things are unknown and make this a pilot. A coefficient frozen within a step can make a stick-slip oscillation
depend on the step size h, which would make results a discretization artifact. And it is not known whether the
effect changes any outcome the studio decides (slip on a low-friction crossing, a wheel's traction limit). The
plan names no pilot for this item, so this bead registers one (DESIGN: thresholds proposed here and fixed before
the run). FACT: `fs-tribo` has a `stribeck_friction` module with an implicit set-valued friction step
(`solve_implicit_friction`, `SetValuedFrictionStep`), which is the referee-side oracle.

## Scope

In: a prototype coefficient law on `batch-cpu` through the lagged mechanism; a stick-slip fixture; the comparison
with the referee oracle; the step-size study; an outcome study on two battery scenarios; the record. Out: the
admitted law and its cards (N18a-31).

## Technical approach

1. **Register** hypothesis, falsifiers, fixtures, seeds and commit in the pilot register
   (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`, schema `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`) before the first run, with the
   four parts N38a-09 checks at registration: the falsifiers (step 6), the ceiling `none on speed` with its reason
   (a fidelity feature: stick-slip where the cards show it, at the cost of one coefficient evaluation per contact),
   and `fallback.ships` and `fallback.kill_gate` (step 7). The
   prototype law is linked only under the test-only cargo feature `pilot-stribeck` (DESIGN), which reads the three
   Stribeck fields of a pilot interface card and marks results `pilot-law`; the release binary refuses such a card
   on any tier with `FROB-REF-246` (exit 3), as N18a-31 does until it is admitted.
2. **Coefficient law (DESIGN).** μ(v) = μ_k + (μ_s − μ_k)·σ(v/v_str), with static coefficient μ_s, kinetic
   coefficient μ_k, Stribeck speed v_str and shape σ(x) = 1/(1 + x²) (the rational shape, which needs no
   exponential) or exp(−x²) as a card would declare; v is the committed sliding speed of the contact.
3. **Stick-slip fixture.** A 1 kg block on a belt moving at 0.05 m/s, held by a spring of 500 N/m, with
   μ_s = 0.6, μ_k = 0.4, v_str = 0.01 m/s. Truth for the idealized two-level law: the block sticks until the
   spring force reaches μ_s·m·g = 5.886 N (extension 11.77 mm), then slips; the oscillation is compared with the
   referee's set-valued step in amplitude and period. The fixture lives under
   `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/traction/stick-slip-belt/` (design, scene and the pilot card), written by this
   bead and reused by N18a-31, and runs as `frob sim run --fixture traction/stick-slip-belt` (BD-080's form for
   conformance fixtures).
4. **Step-size study.** The same fixture at h = 2 ms, 1 ms and 0.5 ms, set with `sim.run`'s `--step <h>` (N16a-03,
   VERBS-sim; the physical parameters held) under `lockstep`, and under `strict` with located stick and slip
   events.
5. **Outcome study.** The low-friction crossing and the wheel traction-limit scenarios of the battery, with the
   Stribeck law against a constant coefficient at μ_k and at μ_s, 200 addresses each.
6. **Falsifiers (proposed).** Any of: at h = 2 ms the stick-slip amplitude or period differs from the referee by
   more than 10%; halving h changes the amplitude by more than 5% (a step-size artifact); fewer than 5% of
   episode outcomes change against both constant-coefficient arms (the effect decides nothing).
7. **Record** the result, including a negative one, in the pilot register (verdict per falsifier, the tables of
   step 3 to 5; `fallback.kill_gate` `KILL-STRIBECK`, the kill row N40-24 enters in the gate register by an
   `introduce` amendment dated before this protocol is frozen, since the plan names no kill gate for Stribeck
   friction: trigger "a falsifier of the Stribeck pilot holds" (the three of step 6), decided M6, mode
   `pilot-before-funding`, `reads` this pilot's record; where that wording and this protocol's numbers differ, the
   protocol's numbers govern; `fallback.ships` "constant dry coefficients, or the lagged law on transactional
   profiles only if only the step-size falsifier fired", the row's fallback, as N18a-31's admission rule reads it)
   and in the review log; revise tier and scope.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `stribeck_curve_limits`: μ(0) = μ_s; μ(v) → μ_k as v grows; monotone decreasing; both shapes.
- `stribeck_breakaway_extension`: 11.77 mm.
- `stribeck_uses_committed_speed`: the coefficient digest is equal before and after the solve.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/pilot_stribeck.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N18a-30 milestone=M6 labels=traction,frontier,pilot seed=0
needs=cpu plants=candidate-speed,equal-levels`). Steps: `registered` (the pilot row precedes the first run);
`release_refuses` (`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-246 3 -- frob sim run --fixture traction/stick-slip-belt --tier
batch-cpu --profile lockstep --seconds 10 --key pstr-rel --json` on the release binary); then on the
`pilot-stribeck` build: `fixture` (`frob sim run --fixture traction/stick-slip-belt --step <h> --tier batch-cpu
--profile <lockstep|strict> --seconds 10 --seed 0 --key pstr-<h>-<profile> --json` at h = 2, 1 and 0.5 ms, and
the referee's set-valued step with `--tier referee --profile referee --key pstr-<h>-ref`); `outcomes` (each of the two battery scenarios with the Stribeck card and with
constant cards at μ_k and μ_s, `--worlds 200 --seed 0`, keys `pstr-<scenario>-<arm>`, the seeds and hence the
world addresses common to the three arms); `plants`. The script sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes
`target/e2e-logs/pilot_stribeck/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time, the evidence block (class `Estimated`, card
provenance `assumed`); `metric` lines for amplitude and period against the referee with the 10% threshold, the
h-halving change against 5%, the breakaway extension against 11.77 mm, the outcome-change shares with intervals
against 5% and the battery identity, the coefficient digests before and after each solve; on a missed threshold
the fixture, step size, profile and the `.frun` path; and a `summary` with each falsifier's verdict and the pilot
identifier.

### Known answers and negative controls
Breakaway at spring force μ_s·m·g = 5.886 N (extension 11.77 mm). Negative controls: μ_s = μ_k (plant
`equal-levels`) must produce no stick-slip (steady sliding at extension μ_k·m·g/k = 7.85 mm), and the harness must
report zero oscillation amplitude; a law evaluated from the candidate speed inside the solve (plant
`candidate-speed`) must fail the before-and-after coefficient digest check.
