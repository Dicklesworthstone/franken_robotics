## Background

Velocity-dependent dry friction (the Stribeck effect: friction falling from a static to a kinetic level with
sliding speed) is admitted in this system only as a **lagged coefficient**: evaluated from committed state, held
constant inside the solve, so the step stays strictly convex (decision ADR-TRAC-06). It **stays Frontier until it
has cards with provenance**, and it is funded only if its pilot (N18a-30) shows that the lagged form reproduces
stick-slip without a step-size artifact and that the effect changes outcomes. Friction parameters come from
interface cards for a pair of surface materials and their condition; a Stribeck curve is three more fields on
such a card.

## Scope

In: the card fields and their validation; the coefficient law as a kernel through the lagged mechanism; its
anisotropic and spin handling; admission per tier from the pilot's result; validity flags. Out: the pilot
(N18a-30); joint-level stiction with a Stribeck shape (the joint-row beads, which use the same curve on a joint
torque); the film law (BD-230), which takes precedence on a patch that carries a film.

## Technical approach

1. **Card fields.** `mu_static`, `mu_kinetic`, `stribeck_speed` (m/s), `stribeck_shape` (`rational` or
   `gaussian`), each with per-field provenance and the calibrated speed range. Validation: μ_s ≥ μ_k ≥ 0,
   v_str > 0; a violation is `FROB-REF-240` (invalid cone parameters). A card whose required field is `assumed`
   under `--strict-data` is refused by the card rule.
2. **Law.** μ(v) = μ_k + (μ_s − μ_k)·σ(v/v_str), σ(x) = 1/(1 + x²) for `rational` (the default on batched tiers:
   no exponential) or exp(−x²) for `gaussian` (through the deterministic elementary functions). v is the
   committed generalized sliding speed √(v_t1² + v_t2² + (r_eff·ω)²), so sliding and spinning share the curve as
   they share the cone. For an anisotropic cone the curve scales μ₁, μ₂ and μ_s by the same factor.
3. **Mechanism.** The law is a coefficient kernel registered with the lagged mechanism (N18a-29): it reads the
   committed epoch only, writes the coefficient buffer before the cone setup, and is covered by the before/after
   digest check and the `coefficient-lag` ledger row.
4. **Precedence.** On a contact whose patch carries a film (h₀ > 0) the film law supplies the coefficient and the
   Stribeck law is not evaluated; with no film the Stribeck law replaces the constant dry coefficient.
5. **Admission.** By the pilot's result: on every tier if neither the accuracy nor the step-size falsifier fired;
   on transactional profiles only if the step-size falsifier fired; otherwise not offered. A request elsewhere is
   `FROB-REF-246`. Outside the card's calibrated speed range the result is flagged `extrapolated`.
6. **Evidence.** Outputs are `Estimated` unless the card's fields are `measured` with a source; Reality stays
   `no-claim`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot's verdict is cited; the admitted tiers follow it.
- [ ] Card validation refuses μ_s < μ_k and v_str ≤ 0 with `FROB-REF-240`.
- [ ] The coefficient digest is equal before and after the solve on every step of a stick-slip run (the law is lagged).
- [ ] The stick-slip fixture reproduces the referee's amplitude and period within 10% on the admitted tiers.
- [ ] With `mu_static = mu_kinetic` the results are bit-identical to the constant-coefficient law.
- [ ] `contact.probe` prints μ(v), the regime (static-dominated, transition, kinetic) and the `extrapolated` flag.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `stribeck_card_validation`.
- `stribeck_law_values`: μ_s = 0.6, μ_k = 0.4, v_str = 0.01 m/s, rational shape: μ(0) = 0.6, μ(0.01) = 0.5, μ(0.03) = 0.42.
- `stribeck_equal_levels_is_constant_bits`.
- `stribeck_uses_generalized_slip_speed`: pure spin at r_eff·ω = 0.01 m/s gives the same μ as sliding at 0.01 m/s.
- `stribeck_film_takes_precedence`.
- `stribeck_not_admitted_refuses`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/traction_cone_rows.sh --law-option stribeck` (script of N18a-36, extra step `stribeck`): probe values
and the stick-slip fixture. Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, evidence block (class `Estimated`, card
provenance), μ(v) at the probe speeds, amplitude and period against the referee with the 10% threshold,
coefficient digests, and the first failing sample.

### Known answers and negative controls
Curve values above; breakaway at spring force μ_s·m·g in the stick-slip fixture (5.886 N for 1 kg at μ_s = 0.6).
Negative controls: a law evaluated from the candidate velocity inside the solve (digest check fails); a curve with
μ_k > μ_s accepted silently (validation test fails).

## Considerations

- Without admitted measured tables (OPEN-TRAC-3; owner: project lead) the law runs only on generic ranges labelled `assumed` and every dependent result stays `Estimated`; realism claims are refused.
- Stick-slip can depend on h because the bound is frozen within a step; the fidelity ledger's step arm and the lag row make that visible.
- A falling friction curve is a classic source of exploitable behaviour in learned policies; the energy audit and the model-form check apply unchanged.
