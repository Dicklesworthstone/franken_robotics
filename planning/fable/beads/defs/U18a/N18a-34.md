## Background

The film-friction law (BD-230) is Solid, but it has a kill gate because a semi-empirical law that changes nothing
is cost without benefit. The register's KILL-FILM governs this law only. (A different stop rule, KILL-ADVECT,
governs the later Frontier mechanism in which the robot spreads and carries film; the two once shared a name and
are two gates.) The law never waits for its own kill decision: BD-230 is done when the law and its tests exist,
and this bead, which depends on the battery that runs it (N18a-33), records the decision that keeps the law on
the lockstep tiers or confines it to transactional profiles.

## Scope

In: applying the trigger to the wet-floor battery's record; the decision; the ADR; downstream changes. Out: the
battery and measurement (N18a-33); the law (BD-230).

## Technical approach

**Trigger (TARGET).** On the wet-floor battery (an oil patch and a water patch, one wheeled and one legged robot,
200 randomized episodes each) the law changes **fewer than 10% of episode outcomes** against a
constant-coefficient patch at μ_b, **or adds more than 5% to step time**.

**Milestone.** M3.

**Measurement that decides it.** The receipt of N18a-33 (`receipts/gates/<hash>.json`): the pooled share of
changed outcomes with its interval, the per-cell shares, and the step-time overhead per backend with the no-patch
control.

**Decision rule.**
1. Pooled share at least 10% and overhead at most 5% on every lockstep backend: the gate does **not fire**; the
   law stays on every tier.
2. Pooled share below 10%, or overhead above 5% on a backend: the gate **fires**, for all lockstep tiers in both
   cases (KILL-FILM is decided whole, N40-24: static cards on one backend and the film law on another would make
   a scene's friction model depend on where it ran).

**Fallback when it fires.** Static condition cards with one coefficient per texel on the lockstep tiers; the film
law is kept on the transactional profiles (`referee`, `strict`) for audits.

**What changes downstream when it fires.**
- On lockstep tiers a film patch is read as its condition card's constant coefficient (the boundary-lubricated value for a wetted region); `K-film` leaves the lockstep step plan, which is a new step-plan identity.
- The per-contact film thickness state remains in contact memory for transactional profiles only.
- A fast-tier result on a film scene carries the label `film: static-card`, and promotion of such a result compares it with a `strict` run in which the law is active; the fidelity ledger gains a row for that difference.
- The Frontier epic for robot-advected films (BD-413) names "static film-friction patches under the contact law" as its own fallback and is unaffected in definition; its value case weakens and its plan must say so.
- BET-36 (living worlds change decisions) loses its Solid film half on the fast tiers; the bet's record is updated.

**Record.** The ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FILM-M3.md` in N40-03's decision format, with every field it requires: the
trigger as registered and any dated amendments; each measured share and overhead (not the targets) with the
receipt hash of N18a-33, the battery identity, commit, fingerprint and seeds, set beside the trigger's number; the
outcome (`not-fired` or `fired`); the fallback in force and the name the capability carries ("static condition
cards on the lockstep tiers; the film law on transactional profiles"); the beads re-read (BD-230, BD-413, BET-36),
each "stands on the fallback" or "superseded by this ADR"; the downstream changes above; the session that recorded
it. The KILL-FILM row of `registries/gates.toml` gets `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }` with
that receipt hash; a kill row carries no measured value. The row has no `scope_kind`, so the decision is this one
entry for all lockstep tiers, and the ADR names the backend whose overhead fired it when rule 2's second case
applies.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FILM-M3.md` exists with every field N40-03 requires and cites the battery by identity and its receipt by hash.
- [ ] If fired: a lockstep run on a film scene uses the constant card coefficient (coefficient digest constant over an episode), carries `film: static-card`, and `strict` still runs the law; a test shows all three.
- [ ] If not fired: the law's admission on lockstep tiers is recorded with the measured shares.
- [ ] The decision is taken by a session that did not implement BD-230 or run N18a-33.
- [ ] The KILL-FILM row of `registries/gates.toml` carries its `decision` entry (outcome, ADR path, date, session, receipt hashes) and passes the register load; the measured values are in the cited receipt.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `kill_film_rule_table`: (share, overhead) = (0.14, 0.03) gives `not-fired`; (0.07, 0.03) gives `fired`; (0.20, 0.08) gives `fired`; an overhead of 0.08 on one backend and 0.02 on the others gives one `fired` entry with no `scope`.
- `kill_film_fallback_static_card`: with the gate marked fired, the lockstep coefficient on a film patch equals the card constant at every step, and the `strict` profile's coefficient varies with dwell.
- `kill_film_label_present`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_film_record.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): reads the battery record and the gate registry,
recomputes the decision, and (when fired) runs the fallback check on one film scene under `lockstep` and `strict`:
`frob --json scene compile <film scene> --key kf-cmp`, `e2e_scene_ready`, then `frob --json sim run <robot> --scene
<id> --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep --key kf-run-lockstep` and the same with `--profile strict --key
kf-run-strict` (two runs compared, two keys). Logged per STD-E2E:
`outcomes_changed_share` against 0.10, `step_time_overhead` against 0.05, the decision, the ADR path, and the
fallback assertions.

### Known answers and negative controls
Rule fixtures above. Negative control: a record whose battery identity differs from the frozen one is rejected
(a shortened battery cannot decide the gate).

## Considerations

- Priority is one step above its milestone because the lockstep step plan (and so every M3 training baseline on wet scenes) depends on it.
- A share just under 10% with an interval that crosses it is still a fire by the rule; the ADR shows the interval, and re-opening needs a new battery with more episodes, registered in advance.
- The trigger as stated has no repair cycle; an optimization pass on `K-film` before the decision is recorded would need an owner decision and would be recorded as such.
