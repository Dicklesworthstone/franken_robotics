## Background

The second user-visible Traction capability is the batched contact path: the four-dimensional friction cone, the
joint-level rows, the default convex law and the collision kernels, on `batch-cpu` and on devices. GATE-TRAC-04
(claim: Known answer; TARGET) is measured here: spin stop, ring co-stop, stiction breakaway and backlash values
within 1%; coupled and diagonal forces within 0.5% of μ·N/√2 and μ·N; the pyramid and independent-row mutations
fail. The script drives the real `frob` binary on real fixtures and is the source of the gate record. It also
carries the device half of the primitive-pair parity (the batched kernels against the CPU primitives of
GATE-TRAC-02) and the mechanism checks that have no gate of their own (speculative margin, lagged coefficients,
patch quadrature, effective radius).

## Scope

In: one script with the steps below, structured logging, negative controls, tier and law parameters; the
batched joint rows of GATE-TRAC-04 (KA-402, KA-403 on N15a-12's fixtures) and the GATE-TRAC-04 receipt, which
no other script writes. Out: the mechanisms (their beads); the referee share of GATE-TRAC-04 (N15a-12); the
Sinew joint-row cases and GATE-SINEW-04/09 on the batched tiers (N15b-37); the tunnelling and capacity audits and the full batched battery (BD-168 with N18b-20
and N18b-23; their script is N18b-24); the read verbs' own script (N18b-30).

## Technical approach

**Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/traction_cone_rows.sh [--tier batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal] [--profile
lockstep|strict] [--law CL-1|CL-2] [--law-option stribeck]` (law values spelled as N18b-28's table spells them;
a lowercase `cl-1` passed through to `sim.run` would be `FROB-REF-802`). Plain shell, non-zero exit on failure, idempotent,
seed-explicit. A device tier that is not present is reported as `skipped: device-unavailable` with exit code 6
from the probing command, never as a pass.

**Steps.** A step that runs a fixture without a named command runs `frob sim run <fixture> --tier <tier>
--profile <profile> --law <law> --key tc-<step>-<tier>-<profile> --json`; every run-kind call carries its own
`--key`, distinct per step, tier, profile and repetition, since runs are compared. The fixture verb spells the
device tiers `cuda` and `metal`.
1. `collision_kernels`: `frob spark check --kernel <K> --backend <tier> --reference batch-cpu --seed <s> --key
   tc-sc-<K>-<tier> --json`, one call per kernel K in `K-geom-pose`, `K-bp-sort`, `K-bp-pairs`, `K-np-prim`, and
   on `batch-cpu` again with `--workers 1` and `--workers 8` (distinct keys); assert the 10⁶-pair parity (gap and
   normal within 10⁻⁹ m in `f64`; within the declared bound in `f32`) and equal pair-list digests at the two
   worker counts.
2. `speculative_margin`: `frob sim run fixtures/thin-barrier-sphere.fbot --scene fixtures/thin-barrier.fscene
   --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep --seed <s> --key tc-margin-<d>-<v>-<s> --json` (N18a-12's fixture pair;
   one distinct key per case) with the plate (2 mm) as a thin primitive, its near face d = 1 mm and d = 3 mm
   ahead of a 2 mm sphere, at 1, 5 and 20 m/s with h = 2 ms; assert `margin ≥ v·h + ε_g`, the contact row active
   at the start of the crossing step, and no tunnelling (the step-level verdict that N18a-12 leaves to this
   script, run through `K-np-thin` of BD-166). Tunnelling control: the build with `--features gauntlet-mutations`
   and both `margin-base-only` and BD-166's `endpoint-only-thin`, under which the d = 3 mm case at 5 m/s must tunnel (both
   endpoints clear, 10 mm of travel against 9 mm to clear). `margin-base-only` alone cannot make it tunnel,
   because the sweep of `K-np-thin` finds the crossing whatever the margin; that mutant alone is caught at
   N18a-12's level (`margin_base_only_mutant_inactive`). d = 1 mm cannot catch either mutant and is not used as a
   control.
3. `cl1_law`: `frob contact probe` at three hand states (stick, open, slide: the slide case returns a normal
   impulse of 1.6 and a tangential impulse of 0.8 for unit regularization and μ = 0.5); the incline run; assert the
   threshold within 1% of μ and creep at most the stiction speed.
4. `cone_probe`: `frob contact probe --cone` at v = r_eff·ω with μ = 0.6, N = 100 N, r_eff = 20 mm; assert
   tangential force 42.43 N and spin moment 0.849 N·m within 0.5%.
5. `spin_stop`: disk a = 0.05 m, ω₀ = 20 rad/s, μ = 0.5; assert stop at 0.153 s within 1%.
6. `ring_costop`: thin ring, v₀ = a·ω₀ = 1 m/s, μ = 0.5; assert v/(a·ω) constant within 1% and stop at 0.288 s
   within 1%.
7. `diagonal`: a force ramp along the tangent-plane diagonal; assert breakaway at μ·N within 0.5%.
8. `r_eff`: probe a foot with and without a card override; assert provenance and values (2a/3 by default); then
   the spin-stop fixture under N18a-27's three r_eff sources (geometry, per-material pressure, fitted card
   value), logging per source the spin-moment and stop-time errors against the referee's dense-layout reference
   with its sample count, and the recommendation of N18a-27's fixed rule.
9. `patch_quadrature`: the limit-surface sweep and the sole-strip capacities (0.525, 0.690, 0.619 of N).
10. `lagged_coefficients`: a battery sample with digest checking (its own `--key`); assert zero digest
    mismatches; then `frob fidelity ledger --class <c> --tier-pair batch-cpu,batch-cpu --quantity coefficient-lag
    --json` per robot class (a pair naming one tier twice reads the intrinsic rows, VERBS-fidelity; they exist
    once BD-212 has frozen them), asserting one row per (coefficient law, contact mode) with n, median, 95th and
    99th percentile and maximum, and no pair-only field.
11. `stiction_ramp` and `backlash_closure` (the batched joint rows of GATE-TRAC-04; its referee share is
    N15a-12's): `frob task fixture trac-ramp-breakaway --tier <tier> --profile <profile> --seed 9 --key
    tc-ramp-<tier>-<profile> --json` and `frob task fixture trac-gap-crossing --tier <tier> --profile <profile>
    --seed 9 --key tc-gap-<tier>-<profile> --json`, the fixtures N15a-12 registers with BD-084's rigs (read by
    name, never redefined; their manifests declare these axis values); assert at 1% against N18a-32's
    `joint_row_known_answers()`, read through the conformance crate and never restated (KA-402: breakaway at
    τ_s/k = 0.5 s for τ_s = 2 N·m and k = 4 N·m/s; KA-403: crossing time 17.45 ms, closure speed 0.0833 rad/s,
    energy lost 1.04 mJ), with the tier's regularization (ε for stiction, the tooth compliance for backlash)
    logged beside each value.
12. `mutants`: the registered controls `pyramid-cone`, `independent-spin-row`, `dead-zone-backlash` (the build of
    BD-084's `mutant-backlash-deadzone`), `margin-base-only` (built together with BD-166's `endpoint-only-thin`,
    step 2) and `in-solve-coefficient`, each one compiled mutant
    in a build with `--features gauntlet-mutations` (selected with `--control <name>` where its step runs through `task.fixture`, as the
    KA-403 step of step 11 does with key `tc-gap-m`); the corresponding steps must fail; each caught control is
    logged.

**Logging.** The script sources the STD-E2E helper and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/traction_cone_rows/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block
(claim, class, tier, profile, law, determinism class, kernel and plan identities, hardware fingerprint, seed),
measured values with units and thresholds, kernel and state digests, and on failure the first differing value
with the fixture and episode paths. The final `summary` line lists assertions passed and failed, controls caught,
and gates touched (GATE-TRAC-04; the kernel parity rows tied to GATE-TRAC-02).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script passes on `batch-cpu` under `lockstep` and under `strict`, and on each present device tier, at M2.
- [ ] Every value of GATE-TRAC-04 is logged as a measured number with its tolerance. GATE-TRAC-04 is recorded by this bead, its one recorder, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-04 --receipt <hash>` from the receipt the script writes (the script never writes the record itself); the record cites the log by path and digest.
- [ ] The joint rows of GATE-TRAC-04 are measured on N15a-12's `trac-ramp-breakaway` and `trac-gap-crossing`
      (no private copy of either fixture), against `joint_row_known_answers()` at 1%, on `batch-cpu` under both
      profiles and on each present device tier; this script writes the GATE-TRAC-04 receipt.
- [ ] All five mutants are caught; a mutant flag that does not change behaviour fails the script.
- [ ] Across tiers, results agree inside the determinism class each backend claims, and a disagreement writes a divergence-ledger entry naming the first differing kernel output.
- [ ] Two runs on one machine give identical digests; two worker counts give identical digests.
- [ ] A missing device is reported as skipped with its reason and exit code 6 from the probe, and the summary counts it separately from passes.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `e2e_log_schema_traction_cone_rows`: the produced log validates against the STD-E2E line schema.
- `e2e_mutant_flags_exist`: each of the five flags is recognised by the test build and rejected by a release build.
- `e2e_skip_is_not_pass`: a simulated absent device yields `skipped` in the summary, not `passed`.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. Self-test: `--self-test` runs steps 3, 4 and 12 on `batch-cpu` and validates the log.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-397, KA-399, KA-400, KA-401, KA-402, KA-403, KA-424, KA-425, KA-413; the CL-1 region values. Negative controls:
the five mutants (counterexamples CX-148, CX-171, CX-172, CX-169 and the in-solve coefficient update).
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; `batch-cpu` against CUDA and
Metal, each against its class.

## Considerations

- The joint-row steps depend on the batched joint-row kernels; if their kill gate removes them from the batched solve, those steps run on `strict` and the referee only and the summary says so.
- Device runs need the device arena and leases to be released on cancellation; the script cancels one run on purpose and asserts no leaked buffers.
- Optional parameters (`--law CL-2`, `--law-option stribeck`) reuse the same steps for Frontier laws once admitted.
