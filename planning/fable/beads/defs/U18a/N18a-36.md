## Background

The second user-visible Traction capability is the batched contact path: the four-dimensional friction cone, the
joint-level rows, the default convex law and the collision kernels, on `batch-cpu` and on devices. GATE-TRAC-04
(claim: Known answer; TARGET) is measured here: spin stop, ring co-stop, stiction breakaway and backlash values
within 1%; coupled and diagonal forces within 0.5% of μ·N/√2 and μ·N; the pyramid and independent-row mutations
fail. The script drives the real `frob` binary on real fixtures and is the source of the gate record. It also
carries the device half of the primitive-pair parity (the batched kernels against the CPU primitives of
GATE-TRAC-02) and the mechanism checks that have no gate of their own (speculative margin, lagged coefficients,
patch quadrature, effective radius).

## Scope

In: one script with the steps below, structured logging, negative controls, tier and law parameters. Out: the
mechanisms (their beads); the tunnelling and capacity audits and the full batched battery (BD-168 with N18b-20
and N18b-23; their script is N18b-24); the read verbs' own script (N18b-30).

## Technical approach

**Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/traction_cone_rows.sh [--tier batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal] [--profile
lockstep|strict] [--law cl-1|cl-2] [--law-option stribeck]`. Plain shell, non-zero exit on failure, idempotent,
seed-explicit. A device tier that is not present is reported as `skipped: device-unavailable` with exit code 6
from the probing command, never as a pass.

**Steps.**
1. `collision_kernels`: `frob spark check --kernels K-geom-pose,K-bp-sort,K-bp-pairs,K-np-prim --json`; assert
   the 10⁶-pair parity (gap and normal within 10⁻⁹ m in `f64`; within the declared bound in `f32`) and equal
   pair-list digests at two worker counts.
2. `speculative_margin`: the thin-barrier fixture with the plate as a thin primitive at 1, 5 and 20 m/s under
   `lockstep`; assert `margin ≥ v·h + ε_g` and no tunnelling.
3. `cl1_law`: `frob contact probe` at three hand states (stick, open, slide: the slide case returns a normal
   impulse of 1.6 and a tangential impulse of 0.8 for unit regularization and μ = 0.5); the incline run; assert the
   threshold within 1% of μ and creep at most the stiction speed.
4. `cone_probe`: `frob contact probe --cone` at v = r_eff·ω with μ = 0.6, N = 100 N, r_eff = 20 mm; assert
   tangential force 42.43 N and spin moment 0.849 N·m within 0.5%.
5. `spin_stop`: disk a = 0.05 m, ω₀ = 20 rad/s, μ = 0.5; assert stop at 0.153 s within 1%.
6. `ring_costop`: thin ring, v₀ = a·ω₀ = 1 m/s, μ = 0.5; assert v/(a·ω) constant within 1% and stop at 0.288 s
   within 1%.
7. `diagonal`: a force ramp along the tangent-plane diagonal; assert breakaway at μ·N within 0.5%.
8. `r_eff`: probe a foot with and without a card override; assert provenance and values (2a/3 by default).
9. `patch_quadrature`: the limit-surface sweep and the sole-strip capacities (0.525, 0.690, 0.619 of N).
10. `lagged_coefficients`: a battery sample with digest checking; assert zero digest mismatches.
11. `stiction_ramp` and `backlash_closure`: breakaway at τ_s/k within 1%; crossing 17.5 ms and closure speed
    0.083 rad/s within 1%.
12. `mutants`: with the test-only flags `pyramid-cone`, `independent-spin-row`, `dead-zone-backlash`,
    `margin-base-only`, `in-solve-coefficient`, the corresponding steps must fail; each caught control is logged.

**Logging.** The script sources the STD-E2E helper and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/traction_cone_rows/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block
(claim, class, tier, profile, law, determinism class, kernel and plan identities, hardware fingerprint, seed),
measured values with units and thresholds, kernel and state digests, and on failure the first differing value
with the fixture and episode paths. The final `summary` line lists assertions passed and failed, controls caught,
and gates touched (GATE-TRAC-04; the kernel parity rows tied to GATE-TRAC-02).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script passes on `batch-cpu` under `lockstep` and under `strict`, and on each present device tier, at M2.
- [ ] Every value of GATE-TRAC-04 is logged as a measured number with its tolerance, and the gate record cites the log by path and digest.
- [ ] All five mutants are caught; a mutant flag that does not change behaviour fails the script.
- [ ] Across tiers, results agree inside the determinism class each backend claims, and a disagreement writes a divergence-ledger entry naming the first differing kernel output.
- [ ] Two runs on one machine give identical digests; two worker counts give identical digests.
- [ ] A missing device is reported as skipped with its reason and exit code 6 from the probe, and the summary counts it separately from passes.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `e2e_log_schema_traction_cone_rows`: the produced log validates against the STD-E2E line schema.
- `e2e_mutant_flags_exist`: each of the five flags is recognised by the test build and rejected by a release build.
- `e2e_skip_is_not_pass`: a simulated absent device yields `skipped` in the summary, not `passed`.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. Self-test: `--self-test` runs steps 3, 4 and 12 on `batch-cpu` and validates the log.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-397, KA-399, KA-400, KA-401, KA-402, KA-403, KA-424, KA-425, KA-413; the CL-1 region values. Negative controls:
the five mutants (counterexamples CX-148, CX-171, CX-172, CX-169 and the in-solve coefficient update).
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; `batch-cpu` against CUDA and
Metal, each against its class.

## Considerations

- The joint-row steps depend on the batched joint-row kernels; if their kill gate removes them from the batched solve, those steps run on `strict` and the referee only and the summary says so.
- Device runs need the device arena and leases to be released on cancellation; the script cancels one run on purpose and asserts no leaked buffers.
- Optional parameters (`--law cl-2`, `--law-option stribeck`) reuse the same steps for Frontier laws once admitted.
