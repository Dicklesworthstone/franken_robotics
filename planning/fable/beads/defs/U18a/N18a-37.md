## Background

The film-friction law is user-visible in two ways: an agent can ask what the law says at a contact state
(`contact.probe` returns μ_eff, the regime, the Hersey number and validity flags), and a scene with a film patch
changes how a robot slips. This script exercises both through the real `frob` binary on real fixtures and is the
source of the record for GATE-TRAC-05 (the law's own gate: Stefan times within 2%; Couette force within 10⁻⁶
relative in `f64`; the dry limit bit-identical; the limits at h ≤ σ and h ≥ 3σ and continuity between them on 10⁴
random states). It also touches GATE-HAB-06 (the law as scenes consume it: the three squeeze times within 2%; the
fourth-power scaling with pad radius; dry and thick-film limits recover the card coefficients) with its
`scene_patch` smoke step, but the measurement and the one record of that gate are N19b-06's
(`scripts/e2e/film_patch_gate.sh`); this script writes no GATE-HAB-06 row. Outputs of the law are classed
`Estimated`; the script checks that the class is present on every envelope.

## Scope

In: one script, its steps, logging, negative controls. Out: the law and its unit tests (BD-230); the battery's
definition (N18a-33); the kill decision (N18a-34).

## Technical approach

**Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/traction_film.sh [--tier batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal] [--profile lockstep|strict]
[--with-battery]`. Plain shell, non-zero exit on failure, idempotent, seed-explicit; generated scenes only.

**Steps.**
1. `probe_limits`: `frob contact probe --film` over 10⁴ seeded random states; assert μ_eff = μ_b for h ≤ σ, the
   Couette value for h ≥ 3σ where the cap does not bind, continuity across σ and 3σ, and the `viscous-capped` flag
   where it binds.
2. `dry_limit`: h₀ = 0 for 10⁴ random cards; assert the returned coefficient's bit pattern equals the dry card's.
3. `squeeze_times`: single-pad fixtures pressed at constant load from h₀ = 1 mm; assert time to 10 µm of 0.038 s
   (water 1 mPa·s, radius 2 cm, 100 N), 2.3 s (oil 60 mPa·s, same pad) and 0.76 s (water, radius 6 cm, 400 N),
   each within 2%; and that doubling the radius at equal load multiplies the time by 16.
4. `sled`: a sled on a full film (η = 0.06 Pa·s, v = 0.5 m/s, pad radius 0.05 m, h = 0.3 mm, σ = 50 µm, load
   50 N, μ_b = 0.1, the parameters of BD-230's `film_couette_sled`, so μ_v = 0.0157 and the cap does not bind);
   assert a drag force of 0.7854 N within 10⁻⁶ relative in `f64` and no `viscous-capped` flag.
5. `scene_patch`: `frob scene gen … --key tf-gen-patch` a flat floor with one water patch and one oil patch,
   `e2e_scene_ready <id>`, then `frob sim run <legged robot> --scene <id> --telemetry T2 --key tf-run-patch`
   across both; assert the per-contact film state appears in telemetry (`frob telemetry query <run> --stream
   contact --channels film_thickness --json`, BD-230's T2 channel), resets to 0 when a sample leaves the patch, and
   the coefficient digest is equal before and after each solve.
6. `strict_data`: `frob contact probe --film … --strict-data --json` at a state outside the card's calibrated
   Hersey range (the `strict_data` input of `contact.probe`, VERBS-contact); assert refusal `FROB-REF-242`, exit
   code 3; the same probe without the flag, assert the `extrapolated` flag.
7. `retry_restores_film`: on the `chaos` build (test feature `chaos`), the patch scene of step 5 run under
   `--profile strict` with `--chaos-plan fixtures/chaos/film-retry1.toml --key tf-run-retry` (BD-083's
   chaos-build input, schema `frob.chaos.retry/1`; the plan holds one entry `{ world = 0, base_interval = <the
   first interval in which a pad rides a film>, fail_attempts_below = 1 }`, one forced retry) against the
   undisturbed run of the same build, scene and seed (key `tf-run-clean`); assert equal digests (the film state is
   in the restore set) and that the forced run's receipt records the one retry.
8. `wet_floor_battery` (with `--with-battery`): the two-arm battery of N18a-33 (`frob eval run`, one key per arm and
   tier, arm B with N18a-33's `--film-law off`); log the changed-outcome share and the step-time overhead against 10% and 5%.
9. `negative_controls`: test-only mutants `film-constant-coefficient`, `film-returns-dry-after-squeeze`,
   `film-in-solve-update`; steps 3, 1 and 5 respectively must fail.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/traction_film/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` through the STD-E2E helper, with a
transcript. Each line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command line, exit code, wall time, the
`evidence` block (class `Estimated`, tier, profile, determinism, identities, seed), measured times, forces and
coefficients with units, truths and tolerances, Hersey numbers and flags, coefficient digests, and on failure the
first failing state with the fixture path. The `summary` line lists assertions, controls caught and gates touched.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script passes on `batch-cpu` under both profiles and on each present device tier at M3.
- [ ] GATE-TRAC-05 is recorded by this bead, its one recorder, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
      record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-05 --receipt <hash>` from the summary receipt the script writes (the script
      never writes the record itself); the record cites the log by path and digest, with measured values. The
      script declares GATE-HAB-06 touched (`e2e_gate`) and writes no row for it (that record is N19b-06's).
- [ ] The three mutants are caught.
- [ ] Every envelope produced in the script carries evidence class `Estimated` and a Reality field of `no-claim`.
- [ ] Two runs give identical digests; a cancelled run leaves no partial store.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `e2e_log_schema_traction_film`: the log validates against the STD-E2E line schema.
- `e2e_film_states_seeded`: the 10⁴ probe states are reproduced exactly from the printed seed.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. Self-test: `--self-test` runs steps 2, 3 and 6 on `batch-cpu`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-404 (Stefan times), KA-405 (Couette sled), KA-406 (dry card bit for bit), KA-407 (limits and continuity),
KA-438 and KA-439 (scene-side rows). Negative controls: the three mutants (counterexamples CX-177 and CX-178 and
the lagged-coefficient rule). Metamorphic MR-26. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts;
across backends per class.

## Considerations

- The battery step is long and is run for the kill-gate record and nightly, not on every change.
- If KILL-FILM fires, steps 5 and 8 run on the transactional profiles only and the lockstep runs assert the static-card fallback instead.
