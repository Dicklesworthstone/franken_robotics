## Background

`contact.response` is the user-visible form of the response batch: at one converged contact step it answers many
right-hand sides with the step's factor, attaches an error bound to each column, and reroutes any column that
would cross a stick, slip or open boundary to a full solve. GATE-TRAC-11 (claim: Known answer; TARGET; M4): the
three hand fixtures reproduce and the λ = 3 mutation is caught; on 10⁴ battery contact states with 64 random
columns each, solved in `f32` and compared with a full `f64` re-solve of every column, zero accepted columns whose
true error exceeds the attached bound and zero accepted columns whose re-solve has a different active set or stick
label; the rerouted share is reported per robot class; a batch requested against a changed anchor is refused. This
script drives the real `frob` binary and the standalone auditor and is the source of that record.

## Scope

In: one script, its steps, logging and negative controls. Out: the mechanism (BD-367); the pilot (N18a-25); the
kill decision (N18a-26).

## Technical approach

**Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/traction_response.sh [--long]`. Plain shell, non-zero exit on failure, idempotent,
seed-explicit.

**Steps.**
1. `anchor`: `frob sim run --tier batch-cpu` a standing quadruped to a converged step; record the anchor digest
   printed by `frob contact response --describe`.
2. `fixture_bound`: the 2 × 2 fixture H = [[2, 1], [1, 2]], b = (3, 3), x̂ = (1.01, 0.99); assert residual norm
   0.01414, true error norm 0.01414, λ_lo = 1 and bound ≥ true error.
3. `fixture_mode_guard`: a block with μ·N = 5 N anchored in stick; assert a 4 N tangential column is `accepted`
   (no motion, friction 4 N) and a 6 N column is `rerouted`, and that the rerouted column's full solve slides under
   a net 1 N.
4. `fixture_flops`: n = 96, 64 columns; assert the reported counts 313,344 per full solve, 1,474,560 for the
   batch, ratio 13.6, and that the envelope prints the ceiling statement (ratio 1 against a factor-once baseline).
5. `fixture_rounding`: single precision H = 3, b = 1; assert computed residual 0, ρ = 2.4 × 10⁻⁷, bound
   7.9 × 10⁻⁸ ≥ the true error 9.9 × 10⁻⁹.
6. `battery_columns`: 10³ battery contact states by default, 10⁴ with `--long`, 64 random columns each in `f32`,
   with a full `f64` re-solve of every column; assert zero bound violations and zero false mode acceptances among
   accepted columns; log the rerouted share per robot class.
7. `stale_anchor`: advance the world one step and request the batch against the old anchor; assert the named
   refusal with exit code 3.
8. `lambda_lo_certificate`: `frob prove bundle` and the standalone `frob-verify`; assert `accept`, and `reject`
   for a certificate with one pivot edited; assert that a batch without a certificate labels its bounds
   `Estimated`.
9. `cancel`: cancel a `--long` batch mid-run; assert no column is reported as accepted without its bound and no
   job is orphaned (recoverable by its idempotency key).
10. `negative_controls`: test-only mutants `lambda-3`, `guard-ignores-slack`, `bound-without-rho`; steps 2, 3 and
    5 respectively must fail.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/traction_response/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` through the STD-E2E helper,
with a transcript. Each line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command line, exit code, wall time,
the `evidence` block (claim, class `Certified` or `Estimated`, tier, determinism, identities, seed), the anchor
digest, per-column bound and true error, accepted and rerouted counts, the rerouted share against the 50%
threshold of the kill gate, λ_lo and its certificate digest, flop counts, and on failure the first violating
column with its right-hand side saved as an artifact. The `summary` line lists assertions, controls caught and
GATE-TRAC-11.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script passes at M4 on `batch-cpu`; the GATE-TRAC-11 record cites the `--long` log by path and digest.
- [ ] All three mutants are caught.
- [ ] The rerouted share per robot class appears in the log and in the gate record.
- [ ] The stale-anchor refusal and the certificate rejection are both demonstrated.
- [ ] Two runs give identical anchor digests and identical accepted sets.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `e2e_log_schema_traction_response`: the log validates against the STD-E2E line schema.
- `e2e_columns_seeded`: the random columns are reproduced from the printed seed.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. Self-test: `--self-test` runs steps 2 to 5 without a simulation.

### Known answers and negative controls
The three hand fixtures of the response batch (bound at equality; mode guard at 4 N and 6 N; the flop count) and
the scalar rounding case. Negative controls: the three mutants. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across
worker counts.

## Considerations

- If KILL-RESPONSE fires, steps 3 and 6 assert the fallback instead: every column has status `full-solve` and the cost model reports the full cost.
- The verb must not ship while the stale-anchor refusal has no registry number; step 7 checks the code against the registry.
