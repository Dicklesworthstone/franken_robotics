## Background

CL-H is selectable by a user as `--law CL-H` (the law table's spelling, N18b-28; lowercase is `FROB-REF-802`) once
its gate has passed. GATE-TRAC-09 (claim: Fidelity; TARGET; M5 on `batch-cpu`, devices at M6): residual at most
10⁻⁴ within eight passes on at least 95% of battery contact states and 10⁻⁶ under `strict`; zero creep on the
incline below threshold; no separation velocity on a sliding block; agreement with the referee on the Solid rows
of the Traction battery. This script drives the real `frob` binary on BD-386's registered fixtures and writes the
receipt that record is taken from. With a tier parameter it also serves the device rows at M6.

## Scope

In: one script, its steps, logging, negative controls, a tier parameter. Out: the law (BD-386); the device port
(children of BD-424); the kill decision (N18a-23).

## Technical approach

**Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/traction_clh.sh [--tier batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal] [--profile lockstep|strict]
[--long]`. Plain shell, non-zero exit on failure, idempotent, seed-explicit. Every run of the run verb carries a
key built from the step, the profile and the seed, for example
`frob sim run --fixture sliding-block --tier batch-cpu --profile strict --law CL-H --seconds 2 --seed 1 --key tclh-slide-strict-1 --json`;
the determinism lane's second run uses a distinct key (`…-rerun`), so it is computed again and not answered from
the first run's stored receipt.

**Steps.**
1. `admission`: before the gate record exists, `frob sim run --fixture sliding-block --law CL-H --key
   tclh-adm-<profile>-<seed> --json` must refuse with `FROB-REF-246`, exit code 3 (run against a scratch registry
   in which the cell is unset); with the record present it must be accepted. `--profile strict` on a device tier must refuse with `FROB-REF-222`; the lowercase `--law cl-h` must
   refuse with `FROB-REF-802`.
2. `sliding_block`: `--fixture sliding-block` (1 kg block sliding at 1 m/s, μ = 0.5); assert normal separation
   velocity at most the residual tolerance (the associated model would give 0.5 m/s).
3. `incline_creep`: `--fixture incline-creep` (tan α = 0.45 with μ = 0.5 for 2 s); assert displacement at most
   10⁻⁹ m under `strict` and at most the residual tolerance times the duration under `lockstep`.
4. `battery_residual`: 10³ battery contact states by default, 10⁴ with `--long`; assert at least 95% reach a
   natural-map residual of 10⁻⁴ within eight passes; under `strict`, every committed step at most 10⁻⁶; log the
   pass histogram and the count of worlds flagged `Degraded: unconverged`.
5. `referee_agreement`: the Solid rows of the Traction battery under CL-H against the referee; each inside its
   row tolerance.
6. `unconverged_flag`: `FROB_TEST_CLH_PASSES=1 frob sim run --fixture multi-contact-slide --law CL-H --key
   tclh-p1-<profile>-<seed> --json` on the `gauntlet-mutations` build (BD-386's test-only pass override, not a
   `sim.run` option); assert the `Degraded: unconverged` flag and its count in the receipt.
7. `cost`: paired step time with and without the law; log the ratio for the disclosure block.
8. `negative_controls`: test-only mutants `associated-model` (the shift term dropped) and `accept-unconverged`
   (the flag suppressed), in a build with `--features gauntlet-mutations`; steps 2 and 6 respectively must fail.
9. `receipt`: on the `--long` run, the script writes its summary receipt and logs its hash; it never writes the
   gate record. The `batch-cpu` rows of GATE-TRAC-09 are recorded by this bead, their one recorder (the device
   rows are N18ax-03's), with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-09
   --receipt <summary receipt hash>`; the record cites the log by path and digest. This record is what the
   decision N18a-23 reads.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/traction_clh/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` through the STD-E2E helper, with a
transcript. Each line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command line, exit code, wall time, the
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, profile, law, pass count, plan identity, determinism, hardware fingerprint,
seed), residual percentiles against 10⁻⁴ and 10⁻⁶, the share within eight passes against 95%, creep and
separation velocity with their bounds, the `Degraded` count, per-row referee differences, the cost ratio, and on
failure the first state over the bound saved as an artifact. The `summary` line lists assertions, controls caught
and GATE-TRAC-09.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script passes on `batch-cpu` under both profiles at M5; the GATE-TRAC-09 record cites the `--long` log by path and digest.
- [ ] Close (N41-17 rules 1 and 4: a gate in a done-condition is its recorded value with its caught negative controls, and acceptance is the gate's row): a GATE-TRAC-09 value outside its bound is repaired once (a fix and a full re-run); a value still outside is recorded as failed, this bead closes on that record with both mutants caught, and KILL-HARDCONTACT (N18a-23, which depends on this bead) fires on it, so the decision never waits on a pass it exists to judge.
- [ ] Both mutants are caught.
- [ ] The admission refusals (246 and 222) are demonstrated.
- [ ] The pass histogram, the `Degraded` count and the cost ratio are in the log.
- [ ] Two runs give identical digests; cancellation between passes leaves committed state untouched.
