## Background

CL-H is selectable by a user as `--law cl-h` once its gate has passed. GATE-TRAC-09 (claim: Fidelity; TARGET; M5
on `batch-cpu`, devices at M6): residual at most 10⁻⁴ within eight passes on at least 95% of battery contact
states and 10⁻⁶ under `strict`; zero creep on the incline below threshold; no separation velocity on a sliding
block; agreement with the referee on the Solid rows of the Traction battery. This script drives the real `frob`
binary on real fixtures and is the source of that record. With a tier parameter it also serves the device rows at
M6.

## Scope

In: one script, its steps, logging, negative controls, a tier parameter. Out: the law (BD-386); the device port
(children of BD-424); the kill decision (N18a-23).

## Technical approach

**Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/traction_clh.sh [--tier batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal] [--profile lockstep|strict]
[--long]`. Plain shell, non-zero exit on failure, idempotent, seed-explicit.

**Steps.**
1. `admission`: before the gate record exists, `frob sim run --law cl-h` must refuse with `FROB-REF-246`, exit
   code 3 (run against a scratch registry in which the cell is unset); with the record present it must be
   accepted. `--profile strict` on a device tier must refuse with `FROB-REF-222`.
2. `sliding_block`: 1 kg block sliding at 1 m/s, μ = 0.5; assert normal separation velocity at most the residual
   tolerance (the associated model would give 0.5 m/s).
3. `incline_creep`: tan α = 0.45 with μ = 0.5 for 2 s; assert displacement at most 10⁻⁹ m under `strict` and at
   most the residual tolerance times the duration under `lockstep`.
4. `battery_residual`: 10³ battery contact states by default, 10⁴ with `--long`; assert at least 95% reach a
   natural-map residual of 10⁻⁴ within eight passes; under `strict`, every committed step at most 10⁻⁶; log the
   pass histogram and the count of worlds flagged `Degraded: unconverged`.
5. `referee_agreement`: the Solid rows of the Traction battery under CL-H against the referee; each inside its
   row tolerance.
6. `unconverged_flag`: a run with the pass count forced to 1 on a multi-contact sliding state; assert the
   `Degraded: unconverged` flag and its count in the receipt.
7. `cost`: paired step time with and without the law; log the ratio for the disclosure block.
8. `negative_controls`: test-only mutants `associated-model` (the shift term dropped) and `accept-unconverged`
   (the flag suppressed); steps 2 and 6 respectively must fail.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/traction_clh/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` through the STD-E2E helper, with a
transcript. Each line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command line, exit code, wall time, the
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, profile, law, pass count, plan identity, determinism, hardware fingerprint,
seed), residual percentiles against 10⁻⁴ and 10⁻⁶, the share within eight passes against 95%, creep and
separation velocity with their bounds, the `Degraded` count, per-row referee differences, the cost ratio, and on
failure the first state over the bound saved as an artifact. The `summary` line lists assertions, controls caught
and GATE-TRAC-09.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script passes on `batch-cpu` under both profiles at M5; the GATE-TRAC-09 record cites the `--long` log by path and digest.
- [ ] Both mutants are caught.
- [ ] The admission refusals (246 and 222) are demonstrated.
- [ ] The pass histogram, the `Degraded` count and the cost ratio are in the log.
- [ ] Two runs give identical digests; cancellation between passes leaves committed state untouched.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `e2e_log_schema_traction_clh`: the log validates against the STD-E2E line schema.
- `e2e_scratch_registry_isolated`: the admission step's scratch registry does not alter the repository's registry.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. Self-test: `--self-test` runs steps 1, 2 and 6 on `batch-cpu`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-421 (no separation velocity on a sliding block; zero creep below threshold; residual and pass targets;
agreement with the referee). Negative controls: the two mutants (counterexample CX-181 and the silent-acceptance
rule). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; on devices, across batch size, slot and
co-tenants.

## Considerations

- If KILL-HARDCONTACT fires, step 1 asserts that `--law cl-h` under `lockstep` refuses permanently and the remaining steps run under `strict` only.
- The device runs at M6 reuse this script unchanged except for the tier parameter and add the divergence-ledger check against `batch-cpu`.
