## Background

A kill gate is a decision named in advance, with its trigger, its milestone, the measurement that decides it, its
fallback, and what changes downstream. Each is recorded as an architecture decision record when decided, by a
bead of the unit that owns the mechanism (the rule of the fallback ledger N40-23, followed by N40-24).

The plan's Frontier table gives Stribeck dry friction as a lagged coefficient a pilot and a fallback and names no
kill gate. The gate register carries one all the same: **KILL-STRIBECK**, entered by N40-24 through an
`introduce` amendment, so that the pilot (N18a-30) has a stop rule to name and the pilot register (N38a-09) can
accept its record. As registered:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Trigger | A falsifier of the Stribeck pilot holds: (F1) at h = 2 ms the stick-slip amplitude or period differs from the referee by more than 10%; (F2) halving h changes the amplitude by more than 5%; (F3) fewer than 5% of episode outcomes change against both constant-coefficient arms |
| Decided at | M6, mode `pilot-before-funding`: due when WP-74 asks to fund N18a-31, and at the latest at the M6 exit |
| Measurement (`reads`) | The pilot record of N18a-30 in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`: amplitude and period at h = 2, 1 and 0.5 ms on `lockstep` and `strict` with the referee values, the outcome-change shares with intervals for both scenarios, the verdict per falsifier |
| Fallback | Constant dry coefficients; the lagged law on transactional profiles only, if only the step-size falsifier fired |
| Downstream if it fires | Constant form: N18a-31 is closed with reason `fallback` and a Stribeck request is refused (`FROB-REF-246`). Transactional form: N18a-31 is released for transactional profiles only. The status table names the form |

The fallback names two forms. A `fired` entry for the transactional form carries `fallback_form = "the lagged law
on transactional profiles only"`, text that occurs verbatim in the row's fallback; a `fired` entry without
`fallback_form` is read as constant dry coefficients, the form that withdraws the law (N40-03). Without this bead
the row has no decider and the M6 exit finds no owner declaration (`owner-declaration-missing`, N40-30). The
trigger is read from the pilot alone, so N18a-31 waits for this decision (N40-30, step 2, rule 7). Tier:
Frontier [F].

## Scope

In: reading the pilot record; evaluating the three falsifiers exactly as registered; choosing the form; the
decision record `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-STRIBECK-M6.md`; the row's `decision` entry and the owner declaration; N18a-31's
release form and the status table. Out: the pilot (N18a-30); the law (N18a-31); entering the row (N40-24).

## Technical approach

**Procedure.**
1. Read N18a-30's record: the tables of its steps 3 to 5, the verdict per falsifier, the reviewer, the receipt and
   the `protocol_blake3`, which must equal the registered hash. A missing result is not a pass: step 3 decides it.
2. Evaluate each falsifier from the record alone: F1, the amplitude or period at h = 2 ms off the referee by more
   than 10% (on either profile); F2, the amplitude at h = 1 ms differing from h = 2 ms, or at 0.5 ms from 1 ms, by
   more than 5%; F3, the outcome-change share below 5% against the μ_k arm and against the μ_s arm, each share as the
   protocol computes it over its two scenarios. Check the recorded per-falsifier verdicts against the evaluation; a disagreement stops the bead and
   is reported to the pilot's owner.
3. Name the outcome in N40-03's four: `fired` if any falsifier holds, with `fallback_form = "the lagged law on
   transactional profiles only"` exactly when F2 alone holds and no `fallback_form` otherwise; `not-fired` if the
   pilot has a result and none holds; `not-reached` if WP-74 never asked to fund N18a-31 and the pilot has no
   result by the M6 exit; `deferred` only through an ADR naming the later exit and its reason.
4. Write `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-STRIBECK-M6.md` with N40-03's fields: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as
   registered and the `introduce` amendment; each input with its receipt hash beside the falsifier it decides;
   the outcome, the falsifiers that held and the form; the fallback in force and the capability's name from now
   on ("Stribeck friction: constant dry coefficients" or "Stribeck friction: transactional profiles only"); the
   beads re-read (N18a-31); the recording session, which neither ran the pilot nor implements N18a-31.
5. Registry: the `decision` entry of the row in `registries/gates.toml` (N40-03's form, with `fallback_form` as in
   step 3) and the owner declaration (`[[gate]]`, `role = "owner"`, the ledger's current fields copied,
   `decision` with each measured value citing its receipt) in `frob-contact`'s `contract.toml`.
6. Downstream: on the constant form or `not-reached`, close N18a-31 with reason `fallback` citing the ADR; on the
   transactional form, N18a-31 is released with admission on transactional profiles only and `FROB-REF-246`
   elsewhere; on `not-fired`, N18a-31 is released on every tier. The status table agrees.

**Fail-closed default until decided.** No Stribeck law exists in any build; dry friction uses the constant
coefficients of the card.

**Who decides.** The Traction lead prepares the record; a fresh-context session that did not run the pilot
reviews and records it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-STRIBECK-M6.md` exists with N40-03's required fields, cites the pilot record by digest,
  gives each falsifier's measured value beside its threshold and names the form.
- [ ] The row carries the `decision` entry, with `fallback_form` present exactly when F2 alone held, and `cargo
  test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` passes for this gate.
- [ ] The outcome and the form are reproducible from the pilot record alone.
- [ ] N18a-31 is closed `fallback` or released with the admission the ADR names; the status table agrees.
- [ ] The record is in place before N18a-31 starts and before the M6 exit review (BD-427).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_gates.rs`:
- `kill_stribeck_record_complete`: trigger, inputs, outcome, date and session present; the ADR's path is
  `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-STRIBECK-M6.md`.
- `kill_stribeck_rule`: on synthetic records: amplitude 4% off at 2 ms, halving changes it by 3%, outcome shares
  8% and 11% → `not-fired`; amplitude 12% off → `fired`, no `fallback_form`; only halving changing the amplitude
  by 7% → `fired` with `fallback_form = "the lagged law on transactional profiles only"`; F1 and F2 both holding
  → `fired`, no `fallback_form`; shares 3% and 4% against both arms → `fired` by F3, no `fallback_form`; a share
  of 3% against one arm and 6% against the other → F3 does not hold; no result and no funding request at the M6
  exit → `not-reached`.
- `kill_stribeck_form_verbatim`: an entry with `fallback_form = "transactional profiles only"` fails N40-03's
  load (`fallback_form` not verbatim).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_stribeck.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N18ap4-02 milestone=M6 labels=traction,kill-gate
gates=KILL-STRIBECK seed=0 needs=cpu plants=transactional-form-with-accuracy-falsifier`) re-derives the outcome and
the form from the stored record, compares them with the ADR and the gate row, and runs `cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (asserting no failure names KILL-STRIBECK) and `cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test kill_gates -- kill_stribeck`. It exits non-zero on disagreement. It sources
`scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes `target/e2e-logs/kill_stribeck/<utc>.ndjson`; every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; it logs the record's digest,
per falsifier the measured values against their thresholds, the derived and recorded outcomes and forms, and a
`summary` naming KILL-STRIBECK. It writes nothing to the registry.

### Known answers and negative controls
The fixture's breakaway is μ_s·m·g = 5.886 N at an extension of 11.77 mm (N18a-30). Negative control (plant
`transactional-form-with-accuracy-falsifier`): an entry carrying the transactional form while the stored record
shows F1 holding makes the script fail, since that form is allowed only when F2 alone held.

## Considerations

- F3 alone means the law is accurate and decides nothing; the gate fires and the law is not built, because a law
  that changes no outcome is cost without benefit.
- N18a-31's admission rule reads this entry's form rather than the pilot's verdicts, so that the two cannot
  disagree.
