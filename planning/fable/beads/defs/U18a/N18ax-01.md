## Background

The mission subsystem (Augur) has a verb, `mission.distinguish`, that designs the cheapest experiment able to
separate physical mechanisms that imply different design decisions. Its funded extension, the experiment
grammar, treats a probe as "a small robot with a task": the probe is compiled through the same power, frame,
observation and contact interfaces as a production body, and every proposal returns an *executable simulated
experiment* beside a parts list and a measurement contract. The reason given for reusing the body interfaces
is normative: the reading a probe predicts must come from the same contact laws, clocks and sensor models as
the mission it informs.

The plan's own probe fixture cannot be simulated with the contact laws that exist. A gripper holds its
closure displacement on a compliant pad, and the normal load then *relaxes in time*:

  R(r, t) = q + (1 − q)·exp(−t / (τ·f(r))),   r = a/a₀ (pad contact radius over the reference radius),

with long-time fraction q ∈ [0.18, 0.22] and time constant τ ∈ [0.9, 1.1] s. Four laws differ only in f:
V, f = 1 (intrinsic relaxation); P, f = r² (relaxation time grows with the square of contact size); C,
f = min(r, 1)² (capped drainage length); and a withheld law D that follows P early and reaches V's value at
t = τ. The contact chapter (Traction) has four laws: CL-1 (linear compliant normal with one dissipation
time: a spring and a damper in parallel, which does not relax at held displacement), CL-2 (Hunt–Crossley),
CL-M (a parity mapping) and CL-H (hard contact). A compliant floor covering is a stiffness in series. None
carries a load that decays at fixed indentation, and none has a parameter that depends on contact size. At
plan time the fixture was evaluated from the formula (the laws are artificial and the loads stipulated); the
product must run it.

This bead adds the missing law as a card-selected variant of CL-1, called **CL-1R** here (DESIGN name): the
standard linear solid in the normal direction, with a relaxation-rate schedule that may depend on the
contact-size ratio. Tier: Frontier, and conditional: it is needed only by the experiment grammar, which
exists only if the kill gate KILL-AUGUR-PROBE did not fire (if it fires, the fallback is the hand-declared
expert grid and the direct measurement, evaluated from supplied reading sets, and this law is parked).

Terms. *Indentation* δ ≥ 0 is the normal approach of the two surfaces under a compliant law. An *interface
card* gives contact parameters for a pair of surface materials and their condition. A *convex incremental
potential* is the per-step energy whose derivative is the contact force; the contact construction of the
project requires each compliant law to be one, so that the step is a convex program. *Constitutive
dissipation* is energy a law with a dissipation function turns into heat, as opposed to energy the solver's
algorithm exchanges.

Rejected alternatives: a Prony series with many arms fitted per law (more expressive at one radius, which is
exactly what the fixture shows cannot select the hardware; and it cannot express law D); a separate
"experiment simulator" for probes (forbidden by the grammar's rule above); a compliant passive joint inside
the fixture model (it would carry the relaxation, and lose the dependence on contact size, which is the
distinguishing variable).

## Scope

In: the law, its card block, its per-site state, its update and incremental potential; contact-size ratio
from the end effector's declared patch radius; admission (`strict` and `referee` rows); energy-ledger lines;
`contact.laws` and `contact.probe` entries; the four synthetic law cards V, P, C, D; the eight pad-hold
fixtures the script runs (`fixtures/contact/pad-hold-<law>-<pad>.fbot`, law ∈ {v, p, c, d}, pad ∈ {compact,
broad}: a one-body gripper that closes to a displacement and holds); known answers.

Out: the experiment term and probe compiler that use it (N25c-17); the selection algorithm (BD-291); tangential
relaxation and rate-dependent friction (not required by any fixture); lockstep and GPU admission (not
requested; the law is refused there); calibration of a real pad (the reality chapter).

## Technical approach

Layout (DESIGN): `crates/frob-contact/src/laws/cl1r.rs`; cards under `registries/cards/interface/synthetic/`.

**1. The element.** Normal force at a site with indentation δ and one internal variable z (same unit as δ):

  Fₙ = k∞·δ + k₁·(δ − z),      dz/dt = ρ(s, r)·(δ − z)/τ,      z = 0 when contact is made.

k∞ + k₁ = k₀ is the instantaneous stiffness and q = k∞/k₀. ρ is a dimensionless rate schedule, s = (t −
t_made)/τ is the normalized age of the contact, and r is the contact-size ratio. For a displacement held from
the instant of contact, Fₙ(t)/Fₙ(0) = q + (1 − q)·exp(−∫₀ˢ ρ ds′), which is R(r, t) with:

| Law | ρ(s, r) |
|---|---|
| V | 1 |
| P | 1/r² |
| C | 1/min(r, 1)² |
| D | for r ≥ 1: k for s < 1/4 and 1 + (1 − k)/3 for s ≥ 1/4, with k = 1/r²; for r < 1: 1/r² |

**2. The card block** (in an interface card of the pair and condition; `frob.card.interface/1`, minor
addition): `relaxation: { q, tau, a0, rate: { kind: "intrinsic" | "area" | "capped-area" | "schedule",
breaks: [s…], values: [expr in r…] } }`, each field with provenance (`measured`, `datasheet`, `assumed`; the
four fixture cards are `synthetic`). Validity: 0 < q ≤ 1, τ > 0, a₀ > 0, every rate value > 0 on the card's
declared range of r. A card with `relaxation` selects CL-1R; without it CL-1 is unchanged, bit for bit.

**3. Contact-size ratio.** r = a/a₀ with a the *declared patch radius* of the end-effector primitive (a flat
pad of radius a). It is a constant of the compiled model, not a function of the instantaneous patch (DESIGN;
reason: the fixture's laws are parameterized by pad radius, and a state-dependent r would make the rate
depend on δ and lose convexity). An end effector with no declared patch radius is refused at compile with
FROB-REF-121 (contact law missing or incompatible with its end effector).

**4. Update over a step of length h (exponential, implicit in δ).** With E = exp(−(1/τ)·∫ρ dt) over the step,
the integral taken exactly across schedule breaks (piecewise constant, so a sum of at most a few terms):

  z⁺ = δ⁺ + (z − δ⁺)·E,      Fₙ⁺ = k∞·δ⁺ + k₁·E·(δ⁺ − z).

The incremental potential W(δ⁺) = ½·k∞·δ⁺² + ½·k₁·E·(δ⁺ − z)² is convex quadratic with stiffness k∞ + k₁·E,
so the law enters the cone program of the step like CL-1 with a state-dependent offset. For a held
displacement the update is exact at any h (δ − z decays by E per step). The dissipation time and the
regularized friction of CL-1 are kept unchanged on top.

**5. State.** One `f64` z and the made-instant per contact site, in contact feature memory: part of every
snapshot and of the restore set of the transactional step; reset when contact is broken (a re-made contact
starts relaxed-free, z = 0; stated in the card's contract as a limit of the model).

**6. Energy.** Store: ½·k∞·δ² + ½·k₁·(δ − z)². Dissipation per step: the decrease of the arm's energy not
returned as work, k₁·(δ − z)·dz integrated over the step, which is ≥ 0 by construction; booked on the line
*constitutive dissipation: normal damping*. Nothing is booked as algorithmic exchange.

**7. Admission.** Rows in the law-admission table: `referee` admitted where the foundation's contact step
can carry the state-dependent offset, otherwise routed to `strict`; `strict` admitted; lockstep and GPU
tiers not admitted (FROB-REF-246, law not admitted on this tier or profile; repair: `--profile strict`). A
card that fails validity is not admitted on any tier (FROB-REF-246 with reason `card-invalid` and the field).

**8. Surface.** `frob contact laws --json` lists `CL-1R` (the law table's spelling, N18b-28; VERBS-sim) with
its parameter classes (q, τ, a₀ and the rate are physical; nothing numerical is added). `frob contact probe --law
CL-1R --card <c> --radius <a> --hold <δ> --at <t> --json` returns Fₙ(t)/Fₙ(0), the regime (which schedule
segment) and validity. The held-displacement inputs `radius` (the declared patch radius a, m), `hold` (the held
indentation δ, m) and `at` (time since contact was made, s; a list is accepted for a history) are this bead's,
added to the verb as a branch of the law probe; the row of `contact.probe` (VERBS-contact) lists them as its
held-displacement form, with the law spelled `CL-1R` as the law table spells it. A lowercase `--law cl-1r` is
`FROB-REF-802`, as on `sim.run`. `hold` without `at`, or the reverse, is `FROB-REF-802`; `--law CL-1R` on a
card without a `relaxation` block is FROB-REF-246 with reason `card-invalid`.

Determinism: D0 on `strict` for one platform and binary; the exponential uses the project's deterministic
elementary functions. Evidence of runs: class `Tested`; cards V, P, C, D carry `synthetic` and no reality.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A displacement held on the referee or `strict` reproduces R(r, t) for laws V, P, C and D within 10⁻⁹
      relative at h = 1/480 s and at h = 1/60 s (the update is exact for a held displacement).
- [ ] The fixture's values at q = 0.20, τ = 1 s, T = 1 s, μ = 0.5: compact pad (r = 1, 120 N) supports
      29.66 N under V and under P; broad pad (r = 2, 100 N) supports 24.72 N under V and 41.15 N under P.
- [ ] At r = 1 the four laws give identical histories for every q, τ and t tested (bit-identical forces).
- [ ] Law D equals P for s ≤ 1/4 and equals V's value at s = 1, for r = 2 and r = 3.
- [ ] The probe reading sets at r = 2, t = 125 ms with read error ±0.02 over the nuisance box are
      [0.8737, 0.9362] under V and C and [0.9520, 0.9982] under P.
- [ ] Without a `relaxation` block the step is bit-identical to CL-1 on the CL-1 battery.
- [ ] Energy: over a load, hold, unload cycle the ledger closes to the referee's residual bound, dissipation
      is non-negative in every step, and no created-energy flag is raised.
- [ ] A lockstep request is refused with FROB-REF-246; an end effector without a patch radius with
      FROB-REF-121; a card with q = 0 or a negative rate is not admitted.
- [ ] `contact.laws` and `contact.probe` reach the law through `frob --json` and MCP; `CONTRACT.md` of the
      crate lists the law with its validity domain and known gaps.

`cargo test -p frob-contact --test cl1r`:

- `held_step_matches_closed_form`: for each law and r ∈ {1/4, 1/2, 1, 3/2, 2, 3}, t ∈ {1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2,
  1, 2} s (the fixture's probe grid), q ∈ {0.18, 0.20, 0.22}, τ ∈ {0.9, 1.0, 1.1} s.
- `values_at_mission_dwell`: 0.494304 (V, any r; and P at r = 1), 0.823041 (P, r = 2) at q = 0.20, τ = 1 s,
  t = 1 s; tangential capacities 29.66, 24.72 and 41.15 N.
- `laws_coincide_at_unit_radius`.
- `law_d_alias_then_departure`: r = 2, t = 0.125 s equals P exactly; t = 1 s equals V.
- `schedule_break_inside_step_is_exact`: a step straddling s = 1/4 equals two sub-steps split at the break.
- `incremental_potential_convex`: stiffness k∞ + k₁·E > 0 for 10⁴ random admissible cards and steps.
- `dissipation_nonnegative` (property test over random displacement histories); `ledger_closes_on_cycle`.
- `state_in_snapshot`: restore mid-hold and continue: forces bit-identical to the uninterrupted run.
- `no_block_is_cl1_bitwise`; `lockstep_refused_246`; `no_patch_radius_refused_121`; `invalid_card_not_admitted`.
- `probe_hold_branch`: `--law CL-1R --card <P> --radius <2·a₀> --hold <δ> --at 1` returns 0.823041 at q = 0.20,
  τ = 1 s; `--hold` without `--at` is `FROB-REF-802`; `--law cl-1r` (lowercase) is `FROB-REF-802`; a card without
  `relaxation` is FROB-REF-246 `card-invalid`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/contact_cl1r_pad.sh --seed <s>`: (1) `frob contact laws --json`, assert `CL-1R`; (2) for each of
the four cards and both pads, `frob sim run fixtures/contact/pad-hold-<law>-<pad>.fbot --tier referee
--seconds 2 --seed <s> --key cl1r-<law>-<pad> --json` (the fixture of Scope; one key per run, so no run is
answered with another's stored receipt), and once more on `strict` (key `cl1r-<law>-<pad>-strict`) for the
determinism lane, then `frob telemetry query` for the
normal load at t = 0.125 s and 1 s; (3) assert the ratios and the three capacities; (4) `frob contact probe`
for the reading-set corners; (5) the three refusal controls (the lockstep request `frob sim run … --tier
batch-cpu --profile lockstep --key cl1r-lockstep` → FROB-REF-246, the fixture without a patch radius → FROB-REF-121, the card
with q = 0 → FROB-REF-246 `card-invalid`); (6) `frob telemetry energy` and assert closure
and no flag. NDJSON per STD-E2E to `target/e2e-logs/contact_cl1r_pad/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a
transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence
block, law, r, q, τ, measured ratio against expected with its tolerance, the ledger residual against its
bound, refusal payloads, and on failure the first differing value with the run identifier. The `summary`
line gives assertions passed and failed; no gate is recorded by this script.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: the closed form above; the fixture table (29.66 against 24.72 N under V; 41.15 against
  29.66 N under P); the reading sets, whose nearest ends are 0.0158 apart.
- Negative controls: a Kelvin–Voigt pad (CL-1 alone) shows no relaxation at held displacement and must fail
  the first criterion; explicit Euler on z at h = 1/60 s must fail the 10⁻⁹ tolerance; r taken from the
  instantaneous patch area must fail the unit-radius coincidence during loading; relaxation booked as
  algorithmic exchange must fail the ledger-line check.
- Metamorphic relations: scaling k∞ and k₁ by one factor leaves R unchanged; under P, doubling the radius
  quadruples the time to reach a given ratio.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; `strict` against `referee` within the fidelity
  envelope.

## Considerations

- The four laws are artificial. The card kind is general enough for a measured single-arm relaxation, and
  any use beyond the fixture needs a measured card and a validity range; a real poroelastic or viscoelastic
  pad has a spectrum of times that one arm does not capture, and the contract says so.
- A probe that is perfect on V, P and C is wrong on D in every case, by construction. This bead makes D
  simulable so that the grammar's held-out test can be run, not so that D is admitted to a mechanism set.
- If KILL-AUGUR-PROBE fires this bead is not funded; nothing else depends on the law.
- Reset on contact loss discards the pad's memory; a pad re-loaded within its relaxation time would in
  reality start partly relaxed. Out of scope until a fixture needs it.
