## Background

GATE-TRAC-09 is the fidelity gate of the hard-contact law CL-H (Signorini complementarity on the normal,
Coulomb friction with maximum dissipation, non-associated) as a batch law: "CL-H on `batch-cpu`: residual at
most 10⁻⁴ within eight passes on at least 95% of battery contact states and 10⁻⁶ under `strict`; zero creep on
the incline below threshold; no separation velocity on a sliding block; agreement with the referee on the
Solid rows of the contact known answers." The gate register gives it two milestones: "M5; GPU at M6". The
milestone table lists GATE-TRAC-09 under M5 and again under M6.

The M5 rows have their beads: the law on `batch-cpu` (BD-386) and the script that measures and records it
(N18a-40, `scripts/e2e/traction_clh.sh`, which takes a tier parameter "so that it also serves the device rows").
For M6, the epic "CL-H on GPU tiers" (BD-424) names the device rows, and the device pilot (N18a-24) measures
launch-shape stability, pass cost and residual agreement, but carries no gate. No task bead runs the gate on
devices and writes its record, so the M6 exit review (BD-427) would find GATE-TRAC-09's M6 part named by an
epic and measured by nobody. This bead is that measurement and record. Tier Frontier.

It exists only on the branch of KILL-HARDCONTACT (decision N18a-23) that keeps CL-H as a batch law, and only
if the device port (N18ax-02) was built. On the other branch it closes with the kill decision cited and the
gate's M6 parts left `not-run` (N40-01's status, printed "not run") with the detail `epic not funded`.

## Scope

In: the device rows of the gate on one CUDA device and on Metal: what is measured, on which states, against
which thresholds; the record in the gate register; the per-backend admission record that lifts refusal
FROB-REF-246; the negative controls on devices; the cost disclosure. Out: the port (N18ax-02); the script's
steps (N18a-40, reused unchanged through its tier parameter); the `batch-cpu` rows (M5); the `strict` row at
10⁻⁶, which is CPU only because `strict` is refused on GPU tiers (FROB-REF-222).

## Technical approach

1. **Rows (TARGET thresholds; each measured per backend, `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal`, under `lockstep` in
   `f32`).**

   | Row | Measured | Threshold |
   |---|---|---|
   | D1 residual | Share of the battery's contact states whose natural-map residual is at most 10⁻⁴ after eight passes; 10⁴ states (the same sample as the CPU pilot and the device pilot) | at least 95% |
   | D2 incline | Creep distance of a block on an incline below the friction threshold over 10 s | zero within the device's single-precision tolerance (the bound is the tier's position resolution over the run, written in the record before the run) |
   | D3 sliding block | Normal separation velocity of a block sliding at constant speed | zero within the same kind of bound; the associated model's artifact would be μ·‖u_T‖ |
   | D4 referee agreement | The Solid rows of the contact known answers under CL-H on the device against the referee | each inside its row tolerance |
   | D5 agreement with `batch-cpu` | Stage digests of sampled worlds replayed on `batch-cpu` in `f32` | inside the determinism class the backend claims; every disagreement a divergence-ledger entry with its cause |
   | D6 unconverged | Count of worlds flagged `Degraded: unconverged` on the battery runs | reported, never hidden; consistent with D1 |
   | D7 cost | World-steps per second with CL-1 and with CL-H at P = 8, paired, with the disclosure block | reported; the ratio is compared with the pilot's limit of 9 as information, not as a pass condition of this gate |

   The "10⁻⁶ under `strict`" clause of the gate has no device row.
2. **Procedure.** `scripts/e2e/traction_clh.sh --tier gpu-cuda --long` and `--tier gpu-metal --long`, on the
   two reference machines, by a session that did not write the port. Thresholds, state sample, seeds, device
   fingerprints and commit are written to the gate register's pending row before the first run. Admission for
   the measurement uses the conformance-only switch of the port (the law is not yet admitted to users).
3. **Record.** `registries/gates.toml`, gate `GATE-TRAC-09`, parts `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal` beside the M5
   part `batch-cpu`: per row the measured value, threshold, unit, pass or fail; the log path and digest; the
   commit; the hardware fingerprint; the seed; the battery identity; the step-plan identity (which carries
   P = 8 and the bound). Each part is written by the gate recorder (N38b-19, KNOWN_DRIFTS 1):
   `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-09 --receipt <hash> --part M6`, the
   receipt being the `--long` run's envelope for that backend, whose tier gives the part's `scope` (`gpu-cuda`
   or `gpu-metal`); never by hand. The row resolves through `evidence.trace`.
4. **Admission.** No admission record is written: N18b-28's loader derives the cell (law `CL-H`, tier, profile
   `lockstep`) from the M6 part's row-by-tier results recorded in step 3 (`admitted` for a backend whose rows all
   pass, `failed` for one with a failing row, `pending` for one with no results), and the admission check stops
   refusing `--law CL-H` on an admitted tier with FROB-REF-246 (the law table's spelling, N18b-28; a lowercase
   `cl-h` stays `FROB-REF-802`). Admission is per backend: one may pass and the other not.
5. **One repair cycle.** If a row fails, one repair cycle is allowed (a defect in the port is fixed and every
   row is re-run on that backend); a second failure records the M6 part as failed for that backend, the law
   stays refused there, and the epic's fallback applies (CL-H on `batch-cpu` only). The M5 result is not
   affected by an M6 failure.
6. **Negative controls on devices** (the gate must be able to fail): the shift kernel disabled by the
   conformance switch (D3 must fail with a separation velocity near μ·‖u_T‖); P reduced to 1 (D1 must fall
   below 95% or D6 must rise, as the CPU pilot's pass-count table predicts); a friction coefficient perturbed
   by 10% in the device's card slab only (D4 or D5 must fail).
7. **KILL-HARDCONTACT at M6.** The row carries `scope_kind = "backend"` and `reread = ["M6"]` (N40-24); N18a-23
   wrote its M5 entry `backend:batch-cpu`, and this bead writes the two device entries of its `decision` array,
   `{ outcome, adr, date, session, receipts, exit = "M6", scope = "backend:gpu-cuda" }` and the same with
   `scope = "backend:gpu-metal"`, with one ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-HARDCONTACT-M6.md` in N40-03's form holding one
   section per backend (the device pilot N18a-24's record and this gate's receipts cited by hash). Outcome per
   backend: `not-fired` when its rows pass, possibly after the one repair cycle of step 5; `fired` when a row
   still fails after it (fallback for that backend: CL-H on `batch-cpu` and the referee only, the device keeps
   CL-1 and refuses `--law CL-H`); `not-reached` on the unfunded branch (the port was not built because the
   device pilot's falsifier held, the owner did not fund the epic, or the M5 entry fired), the M6 parts then
   `not-run` as above. The recording session is not one that implemented the port.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pending row with thresholds, sample, seeds, fingerprints and commit exists before the first run.
- [ ] Rows D1 to D7 are recorded for `gpu-cuda` and for `gpu-metal` with measured values, or the bead closes
      on the unfunded branch with the kill decision cited.
- [ ] Each of the three negative controls is caught on each backend, and the record lists them with
      `caught: true`.
- [ ] On a pass, `frob sim run --fixture sliding-block --law CL-H --tier <backend> --key <k> --json` is admitted on that backend and still refused
      with FROB-REF-246 on a backend that has no passing record; `--profile strict` on a device is still
      FROB-REF-222.
- [ ] The KILL-HARDCONTACT row's `decision` array holds, beside N18a-23's `backend:batch-cpu` entry, the entries
      `backend:gpu-cuda` and `backend:gpu-metal` at `exit = "M6"`, each citing `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-HARDCONTACT-M6.md`
      (every N40-03 field present), and the register loads with the row no longer due at M6.
- [ ] The cost ratio is published with the disclosure block for each backend.
- [ ] The measurement was made by a session that did not implement the port, and the M6 exit review can cite
      the register rows.
- [ ] Results on devices stay `provisional` until promoted; the record says so.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_trac_09_device.rs`:
- `register_row_schema`: the device parts parse, and a row without a fingerprint or a seed is rejected.
- `pending_before_measured`: a measured row whose pending row is missing or later-dated is rejected.
- `admission_reads_record`: with a synthetic passing record for `gpu-cuda` only, the law registry admits
  CUDA and refuses Metal (FROB-REF-246).
- `failed_part_keeps_refusal`: a failing record leaves the law refused on that backend.
- `thresholds_match_gate_text`: 95%, 10⁻⁴, eight passes, as constants compared with the register's gate text.
- `kill_hardcontact_device_entries`: a pass on CUDA and a failure after one repair on Metal give the entries
  `backend:gpu-cuda` `not-fired` and `backend:gpu-metal` `fired`, and the derived cells admit CUDA and refuse Metal
  with FROB-REF-246 naming the fallback; the unfunded branch gives two `not-reached` entries; an entry with no
  `scope`, or `scope = "family:gpu-cuda"`, fails the register load with `decision-scope-invalid`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/traction_clh.sh --tier gpu-cuda --long` and `--tier gpu-metal --long` (bead N18a-40's script;
this bead runs it and owns the device parts of the record). The log, NDJSON at
`target/e2e-logs/traction_clh/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript, has on every line `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time and the evidence block (claim Fidelity, class `Tested`,
tier, profile, law, pass count, plan identity, determinism, hardware fingerprint, seed); `metric` lines for
D1 to D7 with units and thresholds; `assert` lines for the three negative controls; state digests where
device and CPU are compared; on failure the first state, world or row that differs and the artifact paths to
reproduce. The `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-TRAC-09"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: zero creep on the incline below the friction threshold; no separation velocity on a sliding
block (the associated model gives μ·‖u_T‖: 0.5 m/s at μ = 0.5 and 1 m/s); the Solid rows of the contact known
answers against the referee (sliding threshold within 1% of μ; a sphere rolling down an incline at
(5/7)·g·sin α; spin stop at 3·a·ω₀/(4·μ·g)). Negative controls: the three of item 6. Determinism lanes: twice
on one device; device against `batch-cpu` in `f32` inside the class; solo against co-tenants.

## Considerations

- The device rows can fail while the CPU rows pass (single precision, a fixed pass count, a static graph).
  That outcome is useful: it tells the model-form check that populations must be run on `batch-cpu` under
  CL-H, at the published cost.
- Metal and CUDA are admitted separately; a study that mixes backends under CL-H must not pool them unless
  both have passed.
- The gate's residual threshold is the training bound. Nothing here licenses an evaluation claim at 10⁻⁶ on
  a device.
- If the M5 part failed and KILL-HARDCONTACT fired, there is nothing to measure; the register's M6 parts stay
  `not-run` with the detail `epic not funded` and this bead closes on that record.
