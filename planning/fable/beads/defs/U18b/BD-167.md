## Background

Contact parameters are data with provenance, not constants in code. They come from an **interface card** for the
pair of surface materials and their condition (dry, wet, greasy, dusty), following the rule that friction belongs
to an interface system and not to an unordered pair of materials (FACT about the consumed simulation suite's
charter, to be re-read). Stiffness, dissipation time and friction coefficients are physical parameters; the
stiction speed of a regularized friction law is a numerical parameter, reported, and never changed by a step
variant (ADR-KIN-05). A missing pair is a refusal, never a default coefficient: a silent default would make every
comparison between a rubber sole and a steel wheel a comparison of the default.

This bead puts those cards under the batched contact solve at M2 and adds the two row kinds that wheels and feet
need beyond the basic cone:

- **Rolling resistance** for feet and wheels is an extra row of the same solve. It is not part of the friction
  cone.
- **Torsional friction** is the fourth dimension of the contact cone (normal, two tangential, spin), not a
  separate row (ADR-TRAC-03; the cone itself is BD-154). What this bead owns is where the effective patch radius
  r_eff comes from and when the spin row is masked, plus the simulated known answers that depend on it.

Why one cone and not independent rows (CX-171): with sliding speed v and spin ω the tangential force on the cone
is μ·N·v/√(v² + (r_eff·ω)²), which is μ·N/√2 at v = r_eff·ω. Independent rows grant μ·N of sliding friction and
μ·N·r_eff of torsional friction at once, 41% more in magnitude than the surface can supply, and a foot that
pivots while it slides would hold where it should slip.

It also completes the verbs `contact.laws` and `contact.probe` (M1 forms in N18b-28 and N18b-29) with the cards
in use and with card-driven evaluation. Tier Solid. Serves bet BET-03 (convex compliant contact audited by a
referee is good enough for locomotion decisions) by making its parameters inspectable.

## Technical approach

**Interface card (DESIGN).** A `.fcard` of kind `interface`, immutable and content-addressed, each field with a
provenance (`measured`, `datasheet` with a citation, `assumed`) and an uncertainty:

```rust
// DESIGN sketch
pub struct InterfaceCard {
    pub system: InterfaceKey,          // (mover material, substrate material, condition)
    pub normal: NormalParams,          // stiffness k [N/m], dissipation time τ_d [s]; restitution e for CL-H
    pub mu: Ratio,                     // isotropic coefficient, ≥ 0
    pub aniso: Option<Aniso>,          // (μ₁, μ₂, μ_s) for elliptic cones; each > 0
    pub r_eff: Option<Length>,         // override of the effective patch radius, ≥ 0
    pub pressure: PressureModel,       // Uniform | Hertzian | Unspecified
    pub rolling_arm: Option<Length>,   // d = c_rr·R, the rolling-resistance arm [m]
    pub film: Option<FilmParams>,      // η, σ, μ_b and calibrated ranges (consumed by the film law)
    pub numerical: Numerical,          // stiction speed v_s [m/s]; class "numerical", reported
}
pub enum Condition { Dry, Wet, Greasy, Dusty }
```

`InterfaceKey` is ordered (mover side, substrate side). A card may declare `symmetric: true`; otherwise the
reversed pair is a different system. Validation at load: μ < 0, r_eff < 0 or a non-positive anisotropy is
`FROB-REF-240` ("Invalid cone"; repair: fix the card).

**Lookup and the missing-pair refusal.** At model-and-scene binding every (collision sample material, substrate
region material, region condition) triple that can meet resolves to one card. No fallback card exists. A triple
with no card refuses the binding and names the triple. FACT: the consumed material database refuses a missing
pair at its own boundary (admission test INT-FSIM-09, BD-068); this lookup preserves that behaviour for local
cards. An end effector whose design names no contact law or an incompatible one is the design-level refusal
`FROB-REF-121`. The refusal for a pair missing from the card table has no code in the refusal registry at the
time of writing; this bead appends one to the `2xx` block of `registries/refusals.toml` (codes are never reused)
with repairs "add a card for the pair and condition" and "choose a material that has one".

**Parameter rows.** `K-cone-setup` reads, per contact row group, a parameter record gathered from the card:
(μ or μ₁, μ₂, μ_s; r_eff; k; τ_d; v_s; rolling arm). Per-world randomization from card uncertainty enters through
the world's parameter row. Invariant INV-TRAC-02: friction parameters come from interface cards, and a
state-dependent coefficient is constant inside a solve (ADR-TRAC-06). Check: the card identity is in the model's
provenance, and a digest of the coefficient slab taken before and after stage 8 is identical.

**Where r_eff comes from (precedence, DESIGN; OPEN-TRAC-2; the order is implemented and tested by N18a-27, and
this bead supplies the card fields and the mask).** (1) An explicit torsion radius on the end
effector's `foot-patch` law; a value above the foot's own contact radius is refused at design time
(`FROB-REF-121`). (2) The card's `r_eff`, or its pressure model applied to the sample's contact radius a.
(3) Default: 2a/3 from sample geometry. For uniform pressure on a disk of radius a the torsional capacity is
(2/3)·μ·N·a, so r_eff = 2a/3; for a Hertzian pressure it is 3π·a/16 ≈ 0.589·a (PO-TRAC-04). r_eff = 0 masks the
spin row and the contact uses the three-dimensional cone. The source used is recorded per contact class and shown
by `contact.laws`.

**Rolling-resistance row (DESIGN).** For a contact whose card carries a rolling arm d, add a row on the relative
angular velocity about the two tangent axes, ω_T, with moment impulse λ_r bounded by ‖λ_r‖ ≤ d·N₀·h, where N₀ is
the committed normal force of the contact (lagged one step and constant inside the solve, ADR-TRAC-06, so the
objective stays strictly convex). On lockstep tiers it is a convex potential with the same regularization as the
friction law, with a stated regularization rate ω_s reported as a numerical parameter; under a transactional
profile it is an exact row. The row dissipates d·N per radian rolled, that is c_rr·N per metre for d = c_rr·R.

**Verbs.** `contact.laws` gains the cards in use per contact class, each parameter with its class (physical or
numerical), provenance and the r_eff source. `contact.probe` evaluates a law, cone or rolling row at a stated
contact state using a named card and returns impulse, regime (open, stick, slip, spin-coupled), μ_eff and
validity flags. Schemas `frob.contact.laws.out/1` and `frob.contact.probe.out/1`; outputs are `Estimated` when
any consumed card field is `assumed`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Interface cards load with per-field provenance; an invalid cone refuses with `FROB-REF-240`; a missing
      (pair, condition) refuses the binding and names the triple (seed done-condition).
- [ ] Incline known answer on `strict` and `batch-cpu`: measured sliding threshold within 1% of μ; creep below
      it at most the stated stiction speed.
- [ ] Rolling known answers: a sphere rolls down an incline at (5/7)·g·sin α without slip; a disk rolls with
      v = ω·r within 0.1% (time-averaged over whole sample periods); the rolling-resistance coast-down matches
      c_rr·g/(1 + I/(m·R²)) within 1%.
- [ ] Mecanum base commanded sideways moves sideways with yaw below 1° (seed done-condition).
- [ ] Spin stop at t = 3·a·ω₀/(4·μ·g) and thin-ring co-stop at t = √(v₀² + a²·ω₀²)/(μ·g), each within 1%
      (rows of GATE-TRAC-04, TARGET); the independent-row mutation fails the ring row.
- [ ] INV-TRAC-02 digest check passes on 10⁴ steps; a planted in-solve coefficient update is caught.
- [ ] OPEN-TRAC-2 decided with this gate: the ADR written by N18a-27, with the measured effect of the three r_eff
      sources, is cited in the GATE-TRAC-04 record of the spin
      rows.
- [ ] `frob contact laws --json` and `frob contact probe --json` return the card-backed fields; `CONTRACT.md` of
      `frob-contact` documents the card kind and the rows.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/{interface_card.rs, rolling_row.rs, reff.rs}`.

- `card_round_trip`, `card_invalid_cone` (μ = −0.1; r_eff = −1 mm; μ₂ = 0 → `FROB-REF-240`).
- `missing_pair_refused`: rubber-on-steel present, rubber-on-ice absent; binding refuses and names the triple.
- `ordered_key`: (rubber, steel) present, (steel, rubber) absent unless `symmetric`.
- `reff_precedence`: design torsion radius 10 mm beats card 15 mm beats geometry 2a/3 = 20 mm for a = 30 mm;
  Hertzian model gives 17.7 mm.
- `spin_stop`: a = 0.05 m, ω₀ = 20 rad/s, μ = 0.5, uniform pressure → 0.153 s ± 1%.
- `ring_costop`: r_eff = a, I = m·a², v₀ = a·ω₀ = 1 m/s, μ = 0.5 → v/(a·ω) constant within 1% and both stop at
  0.288 s ± 1%.
- `coupled_force`: at v = r_eff·ω the tangential force is μ·N/√2 within 0.5% (μ = 0.6, N = 100 N, a = 30 mm:
  42.4 N and 0.85 N·m).
- `rolling_coastdown`: solid disc, c_rr = 0.02, v₀ = 1 m/s on a level floor: deceleration
  (2/3)·0.02·9.81 = 0.1308 m/s², stop after 7.65 s and 3.82 m, each within 1%.
- `sphere_incline`: α = 10°: acceleration (5/7)·9.81·sin 10° = 1.217 m/s² with μ ≥ (2/7)·tan α.
- `coefficients_constant_in_solve` (INV-TRAC-02).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/contact_cards_rows.sh` (helper of STD-E2E; the verb-level script is N18b-30): runs `frob sim run`
on the incline, rolling-disc, spin-stop and mecanum fixtures on `--profile strict` and `--tier batch-cpu`,
asserts each known answer, then repeats the incline with the card removed and expects the missing-pair refusal.
NDJSON lines carry command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, each measured value with unit and
threshold, digests, and on failure the first differing value and artifact paths. `summary` names GATE-TRAC-04.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-387 (incline), KA-388 (sphere, 5/7), KA-389 (v = ω·r), KA-394 (mecanum yaw below 1°), KA-397 (0.153 s),
KA-398 (2a/3 and 3π·a/16), KA-399 (μ·N/√2), KA-400 (0.288 s). CX-171 as the independent-row mutation. Negative
controls: a default card substituted for a missing pair; a coefficient rewritten inside the solve. Metamorphic:
swapping the order of two contacts leaves results unchanged; scaling mass and stiffness together leaves the
incline threshold unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine, worker counts, debug and release, each
back end against its class.

## Considerations

- OPEN-TRAC-2 (source of r_eff) is decided with GATE-TRAC-04; the default holds until then. Owner: Traction lead.
- The ellipsoidal cone approximates the true limit surface of a patch; its error against patch quadrature on the
  referee is a fidelity-ledger quantity, not a gate here. r_eff is `Estimated` unless calibrated.
- The lag of state-dependent coefficients (one step) is a measured quantity in the fidelity ledger.
- Stribeck (velocity-dependent dry friction) uses the same lagged-coefficient mechanism and stays Frontier until
  it has cards with provenance; it is not built here.
- OPEN-TRAC-3: which measured tables may enter the card catalogue. Until decided, generic ranges labelled
  `assumed` keep every dependent result `Estimated` (N18b-36).
- Risk RISK-05 (creep of the convex model misleads wheel and stair results): creep below the incline threshold is
  measured here and reported, never hidden by tuning v_s per fixture.
