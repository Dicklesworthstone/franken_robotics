## Background

The contact subsystem (crate `frob-contact`, layer 3) is trusted on a tier only when its known answers pass on
that tier. The Traction known-answer battery has 29 rows (fixtures in N18b-32). The referee rows are measured at
M1 and the `strict` rows at M2 by other beads; this bead measures the rows that belong to the **batched tiers**:
`batch-cpu` under the `lockstep` profile in `f64` and `f32`, and the GPU tiers within the stated creep of the
fast law. It is the last bead of the M2 contact work: it assembles the gate records and wires the two audits
that make the fast tiers' known weaknesses into reported numbers.

Two weaknesses are structural and are measured, not argued away:

- **Tunnelling.** Lockstep tiers do not locate events. They use a contact margin that grows with approach speed
  (at least v·h + ε_g, with ε_g the gap error bound) so that fast feet do not pass through thin geometry between
  steps. What is still missed is counted by the tunnelling audit (`contact.audit`, N18b-23) against a certified
  detector on sampled windows. Counterexample CX-169: a sample sphere of radius 2 mm at 5 m/s with h = 2 ms
  travels 10 mm; a 2 mm plate lies 1 mm ahead; clearing it takes 1 + 4 + 2 = 7 mm, so both endpoints are clear
  and the step encloses a collision.
- **Capacity.** Every buffer has a capacity fixed at environment creation. A world that overflows is flagged
  `Degraded` or refused; no contact is ever removed to fit (ADR-TRAC-08, INV-TRAC-04). A comparison whose arms
  overflow unequally is labelled `capacity-biased` (N18b-22), because removing or losing over-cap contacts
  biases exactly the robots a design studio exists to compare: tracks overflow where point feet finish.

Tier Solid. Serves bet BET-03 and risk RISK-40 (contact capacity biases a comparison against the morphology with
more contacts).

## Scope

In: running the battery on the batched tiers and recording the M2 parts of GATE-TRAC-01 and GATE-TRAC-06 (this
bead is the one recorder of both M2 parts: its batched rows, and the `strict` rows that BD-161 measures, cited by
their receipts); recording GATE-TRAC-03 from the audit; the capacity-bias fixture; the row-by-tier result table stored beside the
gates. Out: the mechanisms themselves (the speculative margin N18a-12, static capacity N18b-20, patch reduction
N18b-21, bias rule N18b-22, the audit N18b-23, fixtures N18b-32); referee rows (BD-085, BD-086); `strict` rows
(BD-161); the end-to-end script (N18b-24).

## Technical approach

**Which rows run where (DESIGN, recorded with the gate).** A row runs on a batched tier when it tests a Solid
mechanism the lockstep step implements. The table is data in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml`
(each row's `sites`: `strict`, `lockstep-f64`, `lockstep-f32`, `cuda`, `metal`, `referee`), produced by N18b-32:

| Rows | Batched-tier expectation |
|---|---|
| Incline, sphere on incline, rolling disc, box stack, dropped body, capsule against a spherical field, mecanum, ring on a cone | Pass at the row tolerance; rows for the fast law pass on GPU tiers within the stated creep (stiction speed × duration) |
| Spin stop, slide-and-spin, ring co-stop, diagonal traction, stiction breakaway, backlash | Pass (these are also rows of GATE-TRAC-04) |
| Stefan squeeze, full-film sled, no-film law | Not at M2; they land with the film law at M3 |
| Thin barrier | On lockstep: the hand-checkable crossing is detected under the speculative margin; residual misses on randomized crossings are counted by the audit, not required to be zero. Zero missed hits is a transactional-profile row |
| Wheel over a sharp sill | On `batch-cpu` lockstep: feature sequence flat, edge, top; work and impulse reported under step halving. The 1% and 10⁻⁹ N·m bounds are transactional-profile rows |
| Clearance with errors, enclosures | Host rows; not batched |
| Sole straddling a strip, load-weighted slip metric | Pass on lockstep; the slip-metric row is a metamorphic relation on every tier |
| Painlevé rod | Referee only |
| Soil, track thrust, tyre force, skid-steer, rut isolation | M4 rows |
| Capacity-bias fixture | Lockstep: returns `capacity-biased` and no winner |

**Running the battery.** `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --
GATE-TRAC-01/<tier>/<row>` (N18b-32's test form; with `--features gauntlet-mutations` when a row's control runs)
drives the real `frob` verbs as an agent would (`sim.run --tier batch-cpu --profile lockstep`, then `--tier
gpu-cuda`, `--tier gpu-metal`, each call with its own `--key`), each row with an explicit seed, and writes a
hash-named receipt carrying the commit, the hardware fingerprint, the seed, the `StepPlanId`, the frozen
`BatteryId` and the measured value against the threshold. A GPU row that cannot run (no device, or a device family
without a controlled determinism class) is recorded `not run` with the reason; it is never recorded as passed.
The part is entered with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-01 --receipt
<hash>` (and likewise for GATE-TRAC-03 and GATE-TRAC-06).

**Row-by-tier results, machine-readable (DESIGN; read by N18b-28 and by the recorder of GATE-TRAC-09 at M5 and
M6).** A gate part has one status for all its tiers, so the law-admission table of N18b-28 derives admission per
(tier, profile) from the results stored with the part. The receipt that the M2 part of GATE-TRAC-01 cites carries
`row_results`: one entry `{ ka, site, outcome, receipt }` per battery row and per site column of N18b-32
(`strict`, `lockstep-f64`, `lockstep-f32`, `cuda`, `metal`) on which the row ran, `outcome` being `pass` or
`fail` and `receipt` the hash of that row run's own receipt (the `strict` entries are BD-161's measurements, cited
by their receipts). A site that did not run has no entry, never `pass`, and is listed in `not_run = [{ ka, site,
reason }]`. An entry with a site outside the five, an outcome other than `pass` or `fail`, or a receipt hash that
does not resolve fails the load. GATE-TRAC-09's recorder reuses this shape.

**GATE-TRAC-03 (fidelity).** On the M2 battery the fast tiers miss fewer than 1 contact per 10⁶ world-steps that
the certified detector finds. The count, the audited world-steps, the inclusion probability and an exact binomial
upper bound come from `contact.audit`. With zero observed misses in n audited world-steps the 95% upper bound is
about 3/n, so the record states n and the bound beside the point estimate (DESIGN: at least 3·10⁶ audited
world-steps per scene class so that the bound itself is below 10⁻⁶).

**GATE-TRAC-06, batched rows.** The capacity-bias comparison: a tracked design against a point-foot design under
an artificially low contact cap must return `capacity-biased`, print the overflow share of each arm and name no
winner; citing it as a verdict refuses with `FROB-REF-822`. The thin-barrier and sill fixtures run on lockstep
as in the table above and their numbers are stored beside the transactional rows for comparison.

**Capacity audit.** For every reference robot and battery scenario class, `contact.capacity` (N18b-20) reports
static contact maxima against the lockstep capacities and the referee's cap of 21; this bead checks those
figures against the run: the largest active contact and row counts seen on the battery never exceed the static
maxima, and `Degraded` world-steps are reported per class (their bound, at most 10⁻⁴ of world-steps per class, is
GATE-KIN-07 and is measured by the batched-step bead). It also reports, per class, the share of active contact rows
that only the margin's speed term created (N18a-12's per-step counter), so that capacities can be set with the
cost of the margin in view.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Batched rows of GATE-TRAC-01 recorded with measured values on `batch-cpu` (`f64` and `f32`) and on each
      available GPU tier; the row-by-tier table is stored with the gate (TARGET thresholds: incline threshold
      within 1% of μ; v = ω·r within 0.1%; box stack drift below 1 mm over 10 s; mecanum yaw below 1°; total
      mechanical energy never increases under the inelastic drop; capsule gap error at most the brick's
      interpolation bound).
- [ ] GATE-TRAC-03 recorded: missed contacts per 10⁶ world-steps below 1 on the M2 battery, with the audited
      count, the inclusion probability and the upper bound; the planted defect produces counted misses (the
      base-only margin of N18a-12 together with BD-166's endpoint-only thin test: either alone is caught by the
      other protection and produces no miss on thin geometry).
- [ ] The M2 part of GATE-TRAC-06 recorded once, by this bead, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
      record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-06 --receipt <hash> --part M2`:
      BD-161's `strict` rows cited by their receipts (zero missed hits in 10⁶ thin-barrier crossings with the
      endpoint-only mutant caught; sill work and impulse within 1% at h/4, no couple above 10⁻⁹ N·m), the lockstep
      thin-barrier and sill numbers stored beside them, and the capacity-bias fixture, which returns
      `capacity-biased` and no winner (seed done-condition) with the contacts-removed mutation caught.
- [ ] Forced overflow at every capacity is flagged or refused; no contact is dropped silently (INV-TRAC-04,
      INV-KIN-05).
- [ ] Observed contact maxima on the battery are at or below the static maxima from `contact.capacity`.
- [ ] Every receipt names commit, fingerprint, seed, `StepPlanId` and `BatteryId`; rows not run are listed with
      reasons.
- [ ] The M2 part's receipt carries `row_results` as specified: every battery row marked for `strict`,
      `lockstep-f64` and `lockstep-f32` has an entry with a resolving receipt hash; on a machine without a GPU the
      `cuda` and `metal` sites have no entry and appear in `not_run`; after recording, `frob contact laws --json`
      (N18b-28) shows CL-1 `admitted` on `batch-cpu` under `lockstep` and `pending` on an absent GPU tier.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/g1_known_answers.rs` rows and `crates/frob-contact/tests/batched_battery.rs`.

- `incline_threshold_lockstep`: μ = 0.5; sweep tan α; stays below 0.495 and slides above 0.505; creep below
  threshold at most v_s.
- `box_stack_ten`: drift below 1 mm over 10 s on `batch-cpu` `f64` and `f32`.
- `drop_energy_monotone`: total mechanical energy is non-increasing at every step under the inelastic law.
- `capsule_vs_sphere_field`: gap error at most the stated interpolation bound of the brick.
- `diagonal_traction`: maximum friction μ·N in every tangent direction within 0.5%; the pyramid mutation gives
  √2·μ·N and fails.
- `thin_barrier_margin`: the 2 mm sphere at 5 m/s, h = 2 ms: with the plate 1 mm ahead and with it 3 mm ahead
  the crossing is detected on the production build; at 3 mm it is missed only under `margin-base-only` and
  `endpoint-only-thin` together (each alone still detects it: the sweep or the 11 mm margin holds the pair).
- `row_results_shape`: a recorded part with two rows on three sites and an absent `cuda` site loads; a planted
  `pass` for a site that did not run, an unknown site and a dangling receipt hash each fail the load.
- `capacity_bias_fixture`: tracked against point-foot at a cap of 8 contacts → label `capacity-biased`, per-arm
  overflow printed, no winner.
- `forced_overflow_flagged`: capacities 8, 16, 32 each overflowed by one contact → `Degraded` with a reason, or
  `FROB-REF-722` under the refuse policy.

### End-to-end script and logging

The capability script is `scripts/e2e/contact_capacity_audit.sh` (bead N18b-24). This bead adds
`scripts/e2e/contact_battery_batched.sh` (helper of STD-E2E): for each batched row, `frob sim run --tier
batch-cpu --profile lockstep --precision f64|f32 --fixture traction/<fixture> --seed <s> --key
bb-<fixture>-<precision>-1 --json` on the row's fixture of N18b-32 (and `--tier gpu-cuda` or `gpu-metal` with
`--profile lockstep` where a device is present, logged with `e2e_skip` and its reason where it is not), the
assertion of its known answer, and a second run under a distinct key (`…-2`) to compare digests (a repeated key
would return the first run's stored receipt). NDJSON lines carry `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, measured value, unit
and threshold, digests, and on failure the first differing value and artifact paths; `summary` lists
GATE-TRAC-01, -03 and -06 with pass and fail counts. No private-scene data is logged.

## Considerations

- The reading of "batched rows" of GATE-TRAC-06 above (capacity-bias label on lockstep; thin barrier and sill
  reported, with their zero-miss and 1% bounds remaining transactional rows) is a DESIGN interpretation: the
  gate's zero-miss clause is stated for transactional profiles only.
- The standard battery is frozen by N38a-20, a dependency of this bead; GATE-TRAC-03 and the batched rows are
  recorded against that frozen `BatteryId`, which every receipt names, and a re-baseline of the battery (N38a-20's
  change event) requires them to be re-run.
- KILL-CONTACT (N18b-33) is decided on the fidelity gate GATE-FID-01, not here; this bead's creep and slip
  figures are inputs to it.
- GPU rows depend on device back ends. Under the no-GPU variant of M2 they are `not run` and the gate closes for
  `batch-cpu` only, with that scope stated.
