## Background

Soft ground decides whether a wheeled or tracked design works outdoors and on loose indoor substrates: it sinks
(and may belly out), and its traction depends on slip. The soft-soil model is Frontier: Bekker–Wong
pressure–sinkage and Janosi–Hanamoto shear, with persistent deformation (ruts and footprints) as a Moonshot in
per-world mutable state.

- Pressure and sinkage: p = (k_c/b + k_φ)·zⁿ for a contact of width b; a plate under pressure p sinks
  z = (p/(k_c/b + k_φ))^(1/n).
- Shear: τ = (c + σ·tan φ)·(1 − exp(−j/K)) for shear displacement j.
- Track thrust at slip i, with j = i·x along a track of length L and ground area A under weight W:
  F = (A·c + W·tan φ)·(1 − (K/(i·L))·(1 − exp(−i·L/K))).

The pieces are built by other beads: the law functions and soil cards (N18b-12), the soil rows in the step with
their simulated fixtures (N18b-13) and the kill-gate decision (N18b-18). The rut field (N18b-17) is a Moonshot
at priority 4, so this bead does not wait on it: the gate's rut row is recorded against the named fallback
and lifted once by N18b-17, which records the M4 part again (below). This seed bead
is where they become a capability: the read verb `contact.soil` ("sinkage and thrust of a wheel or track on a
soil card, with validity"), the soil rows admitted on the tiers, the gate GATE-TRAC-08 recorded with measured
values, and KILL-SOIL decided. If the kill gate fired, this bead closes on the fallback (a rigid floor with
rolling resistance and a drag card labelled `assumed`; soft-ground results labelled exploratory) and is
described by that name.

## Scope

In: the verb and its command-line spellings; admission of the soil laws per tier in the law-admission table;
the device rows that follow the all-tier pattern; the gate record assembled from its rows; closing on capability
or fallback; the script `scripts/e2e/soil_verb.sh`. Out: the mechanisms listed above; the rut field and the
measured rut row (N18b-17); the capability script in kept and fallback modes (N18b-19, which runs this bead's
script as stages); soil at batch scale and on devices beyond the gate's rows (the M6 epic); granular media
under legs (N18b-15).

## Technical approach

**Verb (DESIGN).** `contact.soil`, kind `read`, first milestone M4, cluster `contact`, capability
`contact:read` (as its row in the verb registry, VERBS-contact), MCP tool `contact_soil`, idempotent, static cost
(no physics). It answers before any simulation: how far will this wheel or track sink on this soil, how much can
it pull at a given slip, and is the query inside the card's validity.

- Input `frob.contact.soil.in/1`: `soil` (a soil card by name or hash); `load` (N, the vertical load on the
  running gear); one of `plate { width, pressure }`, `wheel { part | radius, width; slip }` (a wheel part by
  name, or its radius and width), `track { length, width, count, slip }`, or
  `design { design, end_effector, load_case }` (the end effector's patch geometry and the static load share are
  read from the compiled model); optional `slip_sweep` (at most 1,024 points, paginated); optional
  `clearance` (a belly clearance to compare the sinkage with). A wheel needs no design: the plan's verb answers
  for "a wheel or track", so a bare wheel is a first-class input form.
- Output `frob.contact.soil.out/1`: sinkage (m), ground pressure (Pa), thrust (N) and its limit (N), the thrust
  curve for a sweep, drawbar pull = thrust − resistance where a rolling-resistance arm is declared, the
  comparison "sinkage exceeds clearance" as a finding, the regime report (fractions of the card's pressure,
  slip and sinkage ranges), the quadrature used (closed form for a uniform track; station quadrature for a
  wheel or a design), the card identity with provenance per field, and `next_actions`.
- Refusals: `FROB-REF-243` when pressure, slip or sinkage lies outside the card's validity range (repairs:
  another card; the rigid-floor model); the generic refusals for unknown cards and misspelt fields. No value is
  clipped to the range.
- Evidence: claim kind none (a studio answer); class `Estimated` (semi-empirical law; weakest input the card
  when a field is `assumed`); tier label Frontier; determinism D2 on the CPU reference.
- For a wheel the verb uses the rim-sample station quadrature of the step (one code path); it does not carry a
  separate wheel closed form.

**Command-line spellings (DESIGN; this bead fixes them, as VERBS-contact leaves flag spellings to the
implementing bead).** Quantities carry their units, as everywhere on the command line.

| Flag | Field | Form |
|---|---|---|
| `--soil <card>` | `soil` | A card name or hash; required |
| `--plate <width>@<pressure>` | `plate` | `0.1m@20kPa`; the plate form takes no `--load` and no slip |
| `--wheel <part>` or `--wheel <radius>x<width>` | `wheel` | `w-75`, or `0.15x0.1` in metres |
| `--track <length>x<width>x<count>` | `track` | `0.5x0.1x2` (metres, metres, an integer) |
| `--design <design> --end-effector <id> [--load-case <name>]` | `design` | Load case default `static` (the compiled model's static load share) |
| `--load <force>` | `load` | Required with `--wheel` and `--track`; with `--design` it overrides the load share |
| `--slip <i>` | `slip` | A fraction in [0, 1]; without `--slip` or `--slip-sweep` the answer is sinkage, pressure and the thrust limit, with no thrust at a slip |
| `--slip-sweep <from>:<to>:<points>` | `slip_sweep` | At most 1,024 points, paginated by the envelope's cursor |
| `--clearance <length>` | `clearance` | The belly clearance compared with the sinkage |

Exactly one of `--plate`, `--wheel`, `--track` and `--design` is given; two, or none, is a field error
(`FROB-REF-802` with the pointer to the second form), never a default. Examples: `frob contact soil --soil
soil-a --plate 0.1m@20kPa --json`, `frob contact soil --soil soil/generic-loam --track 0.5x0.1x2 --load 500N
--slip 0.2 --json`, and the registry's example `frob contact soil --wheel w-75 --soil sand-dry --load 40N
--json`; all parse to the one input schema.

**Tiers.** Soil rows are admitted per (tier, profile) in the law-admission table when their rows of the gate
pass there: `strict` and `batch-cpu` lockstep from N18b-13; GPU tiers through kernels for the soil normal law
and the lagged shear limit with a retained differential check against the CPU reference, their rows recorded
`not run` with a reason where no device family holds a controlled class.

**GATE-TRAC-08 (known answer, TARGET).** Soil, track and tyre: law evaluations match the closed forms within
10⁻⁶ in `f64`; simulated plate sinkage and drawbar pull within 2% of them; rut leak zero over 10³ resets. The
record is assembled from: tyre law rows (N18b-04 or its fallback note); belt and track law rows (N18b-06);
soil law rows (N18b-12); simulated sinkage and drawbar rows (N18b-13); the rut-leak row. Each row carries its
measured value, threshold, tier, seed, commit and fingerprint. The rows run in one Gauntlet run,
`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations --
GATE-TRAC-08`, so the rows' negative controls (the n = 0.7 card of KA-837, the linear tyre law of N18b-04, the
single-patch belt of N18b-06) are caught in the same run. This bead is the one recorder of GATE-TRAC-08 at M4: a
session that implemented none of the beads whose rows the record holds (this bead, N18b-04, N18b-06, N18b-12,
N18b-13) records it when this bead closes, with one receipt per tier target the part's entries run in, all from
one commit (N38b-19's receipts of a part): `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate
GATE-TRAC-08 --receipt <g1_known_answers hash> [--receipt <g4_chaos hash>] --part M4`, the `g1_known_answers`
receipt from the run above (the law and simulated rows, KA-415 to KA-417 among them) and a `g4_chaos` receipt
only if the rut entry runs at M4 when this bead records; the script writes no gate record. The rut-leak row:
persistent ruts are a Moonshot (N18b-17, priority 4, which comes after this bead through N18b-19) and this bead
does not wait on them; the row is recorded against the fallback named in advance, "off; terrain resets each
episode", with that reason and no measured value claimed. That row is lifted once by N18b-17, which records the
M4 part again under N38b-19's rule "Recording a part again": receipts of both tiers from runs at its own commit,
recorded by a session that implemented neither bead; the new recording replaces this part's fields, dated by
`recorded.date`, and this bead's receipts stay committed under `receipts/gates/` (the only later recording of the
part); a fallback row is never shown as a measured pass.

**KILL-SOIL.** Evaluated by N18b-18 on those rows and on the soil-card sourcing checklist, and recorded there
as `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOIL-M4.md`. This bead consumes the decision: kept → the capability is as specified;
fired → the verb reports the fallback in force, soft regions run on the rigid fallback and carry
`exploratory`, and this bead's close reason names the fallback.

**Oracle.** The verb's values are compared with the compendium's D.3 oracles (N12-43,
`crates/frob-conformance/src/compendium/d3.rs`: the pressure–sinkage and shear rows), which an implementation
never regenerates; a disagreement is a defect of the verb or of N18b-12's evaluators, never a reason to edit
the oracle.

      design's end effector; it is registered in `registries/verbs.toml` and served by CLI and MCP from that
      entry.

      p = 20 kPa → 19.6 mm; two tracks 0.5 m × 0.1 m under 500 N, c = 1 kPa, φ = 30°, K = 25 mm → limit 389 N
      and 293 N at 20% slip.

      0.55 m, g = 9.81 m/s²): 45 kg on 8 cm tracks (p = 441.45 N / 0.088 m² = 5.0165 kPa) sinks 3.52 cm
      (0.03523 m); 75 kg on 8 cm sinks 9.79 cm (0.09787 m) and the verb reports "sinkage exceeds clearance" for
      an 8 cm belly clearance; 75 kg on 12 cm sinks 4.96 cm (0.04964 m); each to 10⁻⁵ m.
- [ ] The verb's sinkage and shear values equal the D.3 oracles of N12-43 on the known-answer inputs and on
      sampled admitted inputs.

      `FROB-REF-802`; `scripts/e2e/soil_verb.sh` passes on a clean checkout with `batch-cpu` only.
- [ ] The wheel input form answers with station quadrature (a wheel of radius 0.15 m and width 0.1 m under 200 N
      on the first card returns a sinkage, a thrust curve and a validity report) and the registry's example
      parses and answers.
- [ ] A query outside the card's ranges returns `FROB-REF-243` with exit code 3 and both repairs.
- [ ] GATE-TRAC-08's M4 part recorded once, by this bead, with `--part M4` from one receipt per tier the part's
      entries run in at one commit (the one `g1_known_answers` run above, controls caught in it, and a
      `g4_chaos` receipt only if the rut entry runs at M4) by a session that implemented none of the row beads,
      with measured values for every row (the seed done-condition), or rows recorded against named fallbacks with
      reasons; the rut row reads the fallback "off" with its reason (N18b-17 comes after this bead and lifts it by
      recording the part again).
- [ ] KILL-SOIL decided and recorded as `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOIL-M4.md` by N18b-18 (the seed done-condition);
      if fired, the bead closes on the fallback by name.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/soil_verb.rs`.

- `verb_plate`, `verb_wheel`, `verb_track`, `verb_design`: the four input forms; values as above; the wheel
  form and a design whose end effector is that same wheel return identical sinkage and thrust bits.
- `verb_cli_forms`: every row of the spelling table, the registry's example and the track example parse to the
  documented input schema; a misspelt flag (`--sol`) is refused with `FROB-REF-802`, never defaulted; `--plate`
  with `--track`, and a call with no input form, are refused with `FROB-REF-802`; `--wheel w-75` without
  `--slip` returns sinkage, pressure and the thrust limit and no thrust at a slip.
- `verb_matches_d3_oracle`: plate sinkage and shear stress from the verb equal the D.3 oracle functions of
  N12-43 (`crates/frob-conformance/src/compendium/d3.rs`, the row pressure–sinkage and shear) on the
  known-answer inputs and on 10³ seeded admitted inputs, within 10⁻¹² relative in `f64`; track thrust, which D.3
  does not carry, is checked against N18b-12's closed form and its derivation file.
- `verb_sweep_monotone`: thrust increases with slip over the admitted range; pagination fields present.
- `verb_clearance_finding`: 9.79 cm against 8 cm → finding, exit code 2; 3.52 cm → no finding, exit 0.
- `verb_refuses_out_of_range`: `FROB-REF-243`; no clipping.
- `verb_wheel_uses_quadrature`: a wheel query's output names the station quadrature and matches the step's
  per-sample evaluation on the same state.
- `evidence_estimated_frontier`: class and label; weakest-input pointer to the card.
- `gate_record_complete`: the assembled GATE-TRAC-08 record has every row with a measured value or a named
  fallback; with no measured rut row present, the rut row reads "off; terrain resets each episode" with its
  reason and is not counted as a measured pass.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/soil_verb.sh` (this bead's; it sources the STD-E2E helper; `SEED` explicit, default 1; the
capability script `scripts/e2e/contact_soil.sh` of N18b-19 comes after this bead and runs this script as its
`verb` and `refusal` stages, so the commands live here once). Fixture cards `soil-a` and `soil-b` are the
illustrative cards of N18b-12.

1. `verb`: `frob contact soil --soil soil-a --plate 0.1m@20kPa --json` → 19.62 mm within 10⁻⁶ relative of the
   closed form; `--soil soil-a --track 0.5x0.1x2 --load 500N --slip 0.2` → 293.29 N, limit 388.68 N;
   `--slip-sweep 0:1:21` → thrust increasing with slip, pagination fields present; `--wheel 0.15x0.1 --load
   200N --slip 0.2` → a sinkage, a thrust and a validity report, the output naming the station quadrature;
   `--design <wheel fixture design> --end-effector <wheel>` with the same load → the same bits as the wheel
   form; `--soil soil-b --track 0.55x0.08x2 --load 441.45N --clearance 0.08m` → 3.52 cm, exit 0;
   `--load 735.75N` on the same tracks → 9.79 cm, exit 2 with the finding "sinkage exceeds clearance";
   `--track 0.55x0.12x2 --load 735.75N` → 4.96 cm; the registry's example `--wheel w-75 --soil sand-dry --load
   40N` parses (exit 0, or exit 3 with a named refusal if that card is absent from the fixture set, never exit
   1). Assert class `Estimated`, tier label Frontier, and the card named as weakest input.
2. `refusal`: a plate pressure above the card's range → exit 3, `FROB-REF-243`, repairs "another card" and "the
   rigid-floor model" in rank order, no clipped value; `--sol soil-a` and `--plate` with `--track` → exit 3,
   `FROB-REF-802`.
3. `gate`: the GATE-TRAC-08 record read back: every row has a measured value with its threshold and receipt,
   or a named fallback with a reason; the KILL-SOIL decision file `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOIL-M4.md` exists and the
   verb's evidence names the mode in force.
4. `mcp`: `contact_soil` through `frob-mcp` with the plate input returns the CLI envelope's bytes; stage 1
   repeated returns identical bytes.

Logging: NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/soil_verb/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`BD-366`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact
command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier label, determinism,
card identity and provenance per field); `metric` lines with each value, unit, closed form and tolerance
(sinkage in m, pressure in Pa, thrust in N), the regime report; `refusal` lines with code, pointer, observed,
required and repairs; on failure the first differing value and artifact paths; a `summary` line with assertion
counts naming GATE-TRAC-08 and KILL-SOIL. A self-test changes the expected 19.62 mm to 21.0 mm through the
helper's expected-value override and stage `verb` must fail.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-415, KA-416; KA-837 (the late plate-sinkage row with n = 0.5: 0.03523, 0.09787, 0.04964 m) and its n = 0.7
control (0.091646, 0.190127, 0.117076 m, that is 9.16, 19.01 and 11.71 cm; the printed 9.17 came from p rounded

a validity check that clips; a verb answer with no provenance; a gate record that marks an unmeasured row as
passed, or a fallback rut row as measured. Determinism lanes: the verb is
bit-reproducible on the CPU reference across debug and release; simulated rows per their own beads.

## Considerations

- OPEN-TRAC-3 (which measured soil tables are admitted) keeps every result `Estimated` on `assumed` cards and
  blocks claims of realism, not validity; it also feeds KILL-SOIL's second trigger.
- The illustrative cards used in known answers are arithmetic fixtures, not soils.
- A later planning model for one soft-soil regime and the M6 epic "soft soil at scale" both depend on this
  bead; if it closes on the fallback they are re-read against it.
- North-Star scenario "tracks on rubble" needs the soft-soil leg with the sinkage model active and labelled
  Frontier in the evidence; it is the scenario bead NS-08's (which depends on this bead), and N18b-09 runs it.
- Priority: this bead is at priority 2 and the rut field at 4 (the Moonshot rule); a prerequisite must be at
  least as urgent as what waits on it, which is why the rut row is lifted by N18b-17 recording the part again
  and is not a dependency.
