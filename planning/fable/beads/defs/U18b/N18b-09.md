## Background

North-Star scenario NS-08, "tracks on rubble": a tracked robot climbs stairs and crosses rubble; on soft soil
the sinkage model is active and labelled Frontier in the evidence. The scenario is the user-visible proof that
wheeled, tracked and hybrid bodies are simulated by contact physics (slip, scrub, traction limits, sinkage) and
that the system says honestly which parts of that physics are Solid and which are Frontier.

This bead is the end-to-end script for the capability "simulate and inspect wheeled, tracked and hybrid robots".
It drives the real `frob` binary on real fixtures the way an agent would, takes each law through its known
answers and refusals, ends with the scenario, and leaves a structured log. One script for the capability, not
one per law. The scenario itself (stairs and rubble on rigid ground, and the soft-soil leg) has one
implementation, the scenario script of bead NS-08; this script's last stage runs it and adds no scenario step
of its own.

## Scope

In: one shell script with stages (`wheel`, `tyre`, `belt`, `hybrid`, `ns08`), its fixtures and its log. The
first four stages are the law-level checks of wheels, tyres, belts and hybrids; the stage `ns08` calls the
scenario script and reads its result.

Out, and which bead has it:

- The scenario's inputs, demo file, steps and assertions on the stair scene, the rubble scene and the soft-soil
  plot, in the reduced CPU lane and on devices: NS-08 (`demos/ns-08/`, `scripts/e2e/ns_08.sh`), on which this
  bead depends. The two stages this script once had for the scenario (`ns08-rigid`, `ns08-soil`) are that
  script now; their assertions are listed under stage 5 and are all carried by NS-08.
- The mechanisms: N18b-01 to N18b-06, BD-365, BD-366; the all-tier law and hybrid checks are the stages of
  BD-365's script `scripts/e2e/traction_tiers.sh`, which this script runs.
- The soil verb's own script: N18b-19.

## Technical approach

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/wheels_tracks_ns08.sh`, plain shell, sourcing the shared helper of the end-to-end
harness; stages selectable with `--stage`; idempotent (explicit idempotency keys per step); `SEED` explicit
(default 1337); non-zero exit on failure after the summary line. A stage whose law fell back at its pilot runs
against the fallback and asserts the fallback's name in the evidence.

**Stages and assertions.**

1. `wheel`: `frob design templates --json` → `diff-drive`, `mecanum`; `frob sim run --fixture
   traction/<fixture of KA-389> --tier batch-cpu --seconds 5 --seed $SEED --key wt-disc --json` (N18b-32's
   battery row 3, the fixture named by the row's `fixture` field) → v = ω·r within 0.1% (time-averaged); `frob
   sim run --fixture traction/<fixture of KA-394> --tier batch-cpu --seed $SEED --key wt-mecanum --json` (row 7,
   the base commanded sideways by the fixture's own task) → yaw below 1°.
2. `tyre`: runs `scripts/e2e/tyre_laws.sh` (owned by the tyre-law bead N18b-04, so that the law was verified
   when it landed) and reads its `assert` and `summary` lines; no second copy of its commands. What it asserts:
   `frob contact laws --design crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/traction/tyre_corner/vehicle.fbot --json` → the
   tyre law and card with provenance; the cornering run of `traction/tyre_corner` on `--tier batch-cpu --profile
   strict` → lateral force at the measured slip angle within 2% of the law; an out-of-range slip query → exit 3,
   `FROB-REF-243` (on the tyre pilot's fallback branch that script runs its host step only and logs the rest as
   `e2e_skip` naming the verdict, which this stage reports under the fallback's name).
3. `belt`: runs `scripts/e2e/belt_tracks.sh` (owned by the belt bead N18b-06) the same way. What it asserts:
   `frob contact capacity bt-tracked --json` (a design made from the `tracked-flipper` template) → the belt
   class against the referee cap (does not fit) and the lockstep class (fits); turn in place on `strict` →
   scrub work per turn within 2% of 2π·μ_lat·W·L/4 on the compiled W, L and μ_lat; drawbar stall at μ_long·W;
   `frob sim run bt-tracked … --tier referee --seed 5 --key bt-referee` on the same base → exit 5,
   `FROB-REF-721`.
4. `hybrid`: runs `scripts/e2e/traction_tiers.sh --stage hybrid` (owned by BD-365) the same way and reads its
   log. What it asserts: the `wheel-leg` archetype with legs locked → rolling digests equal the plain wheel's;
   with the axle braked → holds an incline below the friction threshold, the law in force per contact is `pad`
   and a brake release produces one `law-switch` event per contact; `tri-wheel-stair` climbs the step its
   geometry admits (0.175 m riser, 0.260 m tread) and not the 0.30 m riser.
5. `ns08`: run `scripts/e2e/ns_08.sh --lane cpu` (bead NS-08) and, on each device tier present,
   `scripts/e2e/ns_08.sh --lane device`; read the `assert` and `summary` lines of its log and assert that the
   scenario is demonstrated, or is printed under a fallback's name. This stage runs no simulation of its own.
   What the scenario script asserts, so that this script's reader knows what the stage stands for: on rigid
   ground the `tracked-flipper` archetype under its baseline controller climbs the stair scene and crosses the
   rubble scene, with task success, a non-zero scrub energy on the turn segments, the belt law named with tier
   label Frontier in the `evidence` block, the `Degraded` share printed, and promotion or labelling against
   `strict` for a sample of episodes; on a soft-soil patch bound to a soil card the sinkage model is active
   (non-zero sinkage reported in telemetry), the evidence labels it Frontier and class `Estimated` for an
   `assumed` card, and thrust falls with slip as the shear law predicts (drawbar pull at 20% slip within 2% of
   the closed form on `strict`).
6. Determinism: stage 1 repeated with new keys (`wt-disc-repeat`, `wt-mecanum-repeat`) → identical digests; a
   second worker count (`--workers 8`, keys `wt-disc-w8`, `wt-mecanum-w8`) → identical digests. (The repeat of
   the scenario's stair run is a step of the scenario script.)
7. MCP parity for one run verb and one read verb used above.

**Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/wheels_tracks_ns08/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a
human-readable transcript beside it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N18b-09`), `step`, `event` (`start`,
`cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds
(speed ratio, yaw in degrees, forces in N, scrub work in J, sinkage in mm, success, `Degraded` share), digests
where state is compared, and on failure the first differing value and artifact paths (run identity, world
address, replay command). A final `summary` line gives assertion counts and the gates touched (GATE-TRAC-08).
Fixtures are public synthetic scenes; no private-scene data is logged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, sources the shared helper and passes on a clean checkout with `batch-cpu` only; device
      steps are skipped with a logged reason when no device is present.
- [ ] The stage `ns08` passes: the scenario script of NS-08 reports NS-08 demonstrated (stairs and rubble on
      rigid ground, and the soft-soil leg with the sinkage model active and labelled Frontier in the evidence),
      and this script contains no second copy of the scenario's commands.
- [ ] Each stage logs at least one `assert` with measured value and threshold; refusals `FROB-REF-243` and
      `FROB-REF-721` are observed with their exit codes (3 and 5).
- [ ] A stage whose law fell back reports the fallback's name; the script does not fail for a fallback, it
      fails if the evidence hides it.
- [ ] Repeat runs reproduce digests; exit code 1 never appears.
- [ ] A self-test shows the script fails when an expected value is altered.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own. Harness self-test: altering the expected scrub work by 10% (through the expected-value
override of the STD-E2E helper, passed to `belt_tracks.sh`) makes stage `belt` fail, and a called script that
exits 0 with a failed `assert` line in its log also fails the stage (this script trusts the log, not only the
exit code).

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified: `scripts/e2e/wheels_tracks_ns08.sh` driving `frob design templates`, `frob sim run` and
`frob-mcp` directly; `tyre_laws.sh` (N18b-04), `belt_tracks.sh` (N18b-06) and `traction_tiers.sh --stage hybrid`
(BD-365) for the law-level stages; and, for its last stage, the scenario script `scripts/e2e/ns_08.sh` of NS-08.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-389 (v = ω·r), KA-394 (mecanum yaw), KA-417 (tyre force), KA-418 (no-slip yaw zero), KA-282 (zero scrub work
caught). KA-416 (track thrust at 20% slip: 293 N for the illustrative card) is the known answer of the
scenario's soil leg and is asserted by NS-08's script. Planted failures the script must catch (run by CI with
mutant builds): a no-slip tracked base that turns for free; a belt row without the sprocket column. The third
plant, a soil run whose evidence omits the Frontier label, is a control of the scenario script and must fail
this script's `ns08` stage through it. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine;
two worker counts; debug and release.

## Considerations

- The stair and rubble scenes depend on scene generators and on the tracked baseline controller; a failure
  there is reported by the scenario script with its first cause, and this script repeats that cause in its own
  log, so that a controller defect is not booked as a contact defect.
- Run time target: under twenty minutes on `batch-cpu` (TARGET), the `ns08` stage included; the scenario's CPU
  lane uses the `smoke` episode count (four worlds per scene) and is not a statistical claim.
- The scenario demonstrates capability and labelling. It makes no claim about a physical tracked robot.
