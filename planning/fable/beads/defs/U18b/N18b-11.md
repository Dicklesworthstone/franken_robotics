## Background

Soft soil under wheels and tracks is a Frontier mechanism: Bekker–Wong pressure–sinkage and Janosi–Hanamoto
shear. Every Frontier item needs a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier before its work is funded: small enough
to run on a CPU in a day, exact or hand-checkable where it can be, registered before it runs, with an outcome
not known in advance.

The governing specification gives soft soil a kill gate (KILL-SOIL: the soil-track-tyre gate fails after one
repair cycle, or no soil card can be sourced with provenance; fallback: a rigid floor with rolling resistance
and a drag card labelled `assumed`, with soft-ground results labelled exploratory) but names no separate pilot
for it. DESIGN of this bead: the pilot is the paper-scale part of that kill gate, run first. It has two halves
that can each fail: the arithmetic (do independent evaluations of the closed forms agree, including a
mistranscription control) and the smallest simulation (does a plate pressed into a power-law ground settle at
the closed-form sinkage in a convex compliant solve, for exponents below and above one). The card-sourcing
question is the owner decision N18b-36 and is not repeated here.

The laws:

- Pressure and sinkage: p = (k_c/b + k_φ)·zⁿ for a contact of width b, so a plate under pressure p sinks
  z = (p/(k_c/b + k_φ))^(1/n).
- Shear: τ = (c + σ·tan φ)·(1 − exp(−j/K)) for shear displacement j under normal stress σ.
- Track thrust: for a track of length L and ground area A under weight W at slip i, with j = i·x along the
  track, F = (A·c + W·tan φ)·(1 − (K/(i·L))·(1 − exp(−i·L/K))).

## Scope

In: an independent host reference for the three closed forms in the conformance crate; the known-answer values
with their mistranscription controls; a one-plate sinkage simulation on `batch-cpu` with a throw-away normal
law; the pilot record and the funding decision for N18b-12 and N18b-13. Out: production evaluators and cards
(N18b-12); shear in the step and drawbar pull (N18b-13); ruts; the sourcing decision (N18b-36).

## Technical approach

**Part 1: closed forms on the host (`f64`, and exact rationals where the exponent permits).** The direct
formulas for sinkage and shear are the compendium's D.3 oracles (N12-43, `crates/frob-conformance/src/compendium/
d3.rs`), used as they are and never regenerated; the reference `crates/frob-conformance/src/oracles/soil.rs`
adds the track-thrust closed form and the independent numerical evaluations, with units carried as typed
quantities so that kPa/mⁿ⁻¹ and kPa/mⁿ cannot be confused. Each known answer is computed twice, by the direct
formula and by numerical inversion or quadrature (sinkage by solving p = k·zⁿ with bisection; thrust by
integrating the D.3 shear law over 10⁴ stations), and the two must agree to 10⁻⁹ relative.

**Part 2: one plate in a convex solve (`batch-cpu`, `strict` where affordable).** A rigid plate of width b and
area A_p under a dead load is released onto ground with the power-law normal response N(z) = A_p·k·zⁿ. The
pilot's throw-away normal law is the potential ψ(z) = A_p·k·z^(n+1)/(n + 1) evaluated at the end-of-step
penetration; it is convex in the normal velocity for n > 0 (a convex nondecreasing function of an affine map).
For n < 1 the tangent stiffness n·k·A_p·zⁿ⁻¹ is unbounded as z → 0, so the law is linearized below a small
z_ε; the pilot measures how z_ε affects the settled sinkage and the conditioning figure of the solve. Cases:
n = 1.1 with the first card (target 19.6 mm) and n = 0.5 with the second (target 3.52 cm), each with
dissipation so that the plate settles.

**Decision rule, fixed before the run.** The falsifier holds if (a) the two host evaluations of any known
answer disagree beyond 10⁻⁹, or the mistranscription control is not distinguished at a tolerance of 10⁻⁵ m; or
(b) the settled sinkage of either plate case differs from the closed form by more than 2% on `strict`, or the
n = 0.5 case cannot be solved in `f64` within the scaled-conditioning admission bound for any z_ε that keeps
the 2% target. Both verdicts are reachable: (b) is a real unknown for n < 1.

**Fixtures (DESIGN).** The throw-away normal law is linked only under the test-only cargo feature `pilot-soil`.
For each z_ε tried, two conformance fixtures (the plate design with the pilot card carrying that z_ε, the scene
and the settle task) are committed under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/traction/`:
`pilot_soil_n11_z<k>` (first card, 19.6 mm target) and `pilot_soil_n05_z<k>` (second card, 3.52 cm target),
k indexing the z_ε values in the protocol.

**Record.** One record of N38a-09's pilot register (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`, schema
`frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`), entered with status `registered` before the first run: an identifier of the set `TRAC`
(`PILOT-TRAC-<nn>`, the next free number the register assigns), `mechanism` = "soft soil: Bekker–Wong
pressure–sinkage and Janosi–Hanamoto shear as laws and soil rows in the step", `tier = "F"`, `owner =
"§18.17"`, `bead = "N18b-11"`, `work_package = "WP-30"`, `gated_beads = ["N18b-12", "N18b-13"]`, `protocol` =
`docs/pilots/trac-soft-soil.md` (the table, the plate cases, the z_ε values and the decision rule above) with its

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/pilot_soil.rs` (host).

- `sinkage_n11`: (20/1,510)^(1/1.1) = 0.01962 m.
- `thrust_limit`: 0.1 m²·1,000 Pa + 500 N·tan 30° = 388.68 N.
- `thrust_20`: 388.68·(1 − 0.25·(1 − e⁻⁴)) = 293.29 N.
- `sinkage_n05`: 0.035234 m, 0.09787 m, 0.04964 m for the three load cases.
- `control_n07`: 0.091646 m, 0.190127 m, 0.117076 m (to 10⁻⁵ m; the 0.091660 m that a p rounded to 5.017 kPa gives is outside it).
- `direct_is_d3`: the direct sinkage and shear values are the D.3 oracle functions' outputs, bit for bit.
- `quadrature_agrees`: thrust by 10⁴-station integration within 10⁻⁹ relative of the closed form at slips 0.05,
  0.2, 0.8.
- `units_typed`: mixing k_c and k_φ units fails to compile.
- `potential_convex`: ψ″ ≥ 0 for n = 0.5, 1.0, 1.1 on z > 0, and the linearized branch is C¹ at z_ε.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-415 (plate sinkage 19.6 mm), KA-416 (389 N and 293 N), and KA-837, the late plate-sinkage row with n = 0.5

## Considerations

- The statement of this pilot is this bead's DESIGN reading of the pilot-before-funding rule for a mechanism
  whose only printed control is its kill gate; it is recorded as such in the pilot register.
- The illustrative constants are not sourced soil data and must not be presented as a soil; sourced tables are
  the subject of the owner decision.
- For n < 1 the unbounded stiffness at first touch interacts with the single-precision admission bound on scaled
  κ·u; a card that needs `f64` is refused on `f32` tiers by the stiffness admission, which is acceptable for a
  Frontier law and is reported.
