## Background

Every Frontier mechanism has a kill gate named in advance: a trigger with a number, the milestone at which it
is decided, the measurement that decides it, and the fallback that replaces the capability if it fires. A kill
gate that fires is not a failure of the project; it replaces a capability by a cheaper one, and everything that
depended on the capability is re-read against the fallback. "Decided" means that the trigger was evaluated on
measured values and that the outcome, either way, is recorded as an architecture decision record.

**KILL-SOIL.** Trigger: gate GATE-TRAC-08 fails after one repair cycle, **or** no soil card can be sourced with
provenance. Fallback: a rigid floor with rolling resistance and a drag card labelled `assumed`; soft-ground
results labelled exploratory. Decided at M4.

GATE-TRAC-08 (known answer, TARGET thresholds): soil, track and tyre law evaluations match the closed forms
within 10⁻⁶ in `f64`; simulated plate sinkage and drawbar pull within 2% of them; rut leak zero over 10³
resets.

Why the second trigger exists: a soil model is semi-empirical. With no card that has provenance, every
soft-ground result rests on generic ranges labelled `assumed`; the laws would still pass their arithmetic, but
the system could say nothing about any real ground, and a cheaper model labelled the same way serves equally.

## Scope

In: evaluating both triggers on measured receipts; reading the one permitted repair cycle; the decision record
and the kill row's decision entry; applying the fallback if the gate fires; listing what changes downstream.
Out: producing the measurements and running the repair cycle (N18b-12, N18b-13, the measuring beads); recording
GATE-TRAC-08 (BD-366, which comes after this decision); the rut row (N18b-17, which exists only on the kept
branch of this decision); the owner's sourcing decision (N18b-36); the fallback's rolling-resistance row, which
already exists (BD-167).

## Technical approach

**Inputs.**

1. The soil rows of GATE-TRAC-08 as measured, read from their Gauntlet receipts (not from
   `registries/gates.toml`: the gate is recorded later, by BD-366, which consumes this decision): the
   law-evaluation receipt of N18b-12's run `g1_known_answers -- GATE-TRAC-08/soil-laws` and the simulated
   sinkage and drawbar receipt of N18b-13's run `g1_known_answers -- GATE-TRAC-08/soil-sim` on `strict` and
   `batch-cpu`, each with its measured values, thresholds and caught controls; the receipt files the outcome rests
   on are committed under `receipts/gates/` with the decision entry. DESIGN reading of "GATE-TRAC-08 fails" for
   this kill gate: the soil rows decide it. The tyre and belt rows of the same gate have their own
   named fallbacks (rigid wheels; road wheels with anisotropic friction) and the rut row has its own ("off");
   all are recorded with the gate by BD-366, not here. The rut row in particular cannot be an input: the rut
   pilot and mechanism (N18b-16, N18b-17) exist only on the kept branch of this decision and depend on it.
2. The sourcing checklist of the owner decision OPEN-TRAC-3 (N18b-36): whether at least one soil card was
   sourced with provenance (primary source read, values transcribed on file, licence line present).

**Procedure.**

1. Read the receipts. If every soil row passes at its threshold, trigger 1 is not met.
2. If a soil row fails, one repair cycle is allowed, in N40-03's form (§5): the measuring bead (N18b-12 or
   N18b-13) opens it with a `note` line of its log, `cause = "repair-cycle: <failed receipt hash>: <hypothesis>"`
   (for example the linearization depth for exponents below one, the station count, the shear-displacement
   advection), makes one bounded change to the mechanism and closes it with one re-measurement with the same
   threshold on the same fixtures and seeds; this bead reads the opening line, the diff and the re-measurement's
   receipt. A value still outside its bound meets trigger 1. Thresholds, fixtures and controls are not edited
   during the cycle. A second cycle is not a repair: it is a `deferred` decision of this bead, with `deferred_to`
   (a later exit) and a `reason`, and needs the ADR.
3. Read the sourcing checklist. If no source passed, trigger 2 is met regardless of the gate rows.
4. The outcome is one of N40-03's closed set: `not-fired` (neither trigger met), `fired` (either met), `deferred`
   (step 2), or `not-reached` (the soil pilot N18b-11 recorded `falsifier-holds` on its simulation half, so the
   soil rows of the step were never funded; the pilot record is cited and no measurement is claimed).
5. Write the kill row's decision entry in `registries/gates.toml`, `decision = { outcome, adr =
   "docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOIL-M4.md", date, session, receipts = [<hashes>] }`, at least one receipt for
   `not-fired`; the row gains no measured value (the values are in the receipts and the ADR).
6. Record the decision as `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOIL-M4.md` with the fields N40-03 requires: the kill gate; the
   milestone (M4); the trigger as registered and any amendments applied; each measured value with its receipt
   hash beside the trigger's number (the soil rows of GATE-TRAC-08, and the sourcing checklist's outcome per
   source); the outcome; the fallback now in force and the name the capability carries from now on; the beads
   re-read, each "stands on the fallback" or "superseded by this ADR"; what changes downstream (the list
   below); and the recording session, which must not be a session that implemented the soil mechanism. It also
   states the alternative considered (continue with `assumed` cards only).

**If the gate does not fire.** Soil stays Frontier on the tiers where its rows passed; `contact.soil` and the
soil rows are admitted in the law-admission table; results on `assumed` cards stay `Estimated`.

**If the gate fires: the fallback.** Soft regions are modelled as a rigid floor with (a) the rolling-resistance
row (arm d = c_rr·R from a card) and (b) a **drag card** labelled `assumed`: a per-region resistive force
opposing travel, F_drag = c_d·N with c_d a card field of provenance `assumed`, applied through the same
rolling-resistance and friction rows and never as hidden damping. Every result on such a region carries the
label `exploratory` in its conditions and cannot be established or cited as a soft-ground verdict.

**What changes downstream if it fires.**

- `contact.soil` (BD-366): the verb stays as a host evaluator of the closed forms where the trigger was
  sourcing, or is reported `not admitted` where the trigger was the gate; in both cases its output states the
  fallback in force.
- Soil rows in the step (N18b-13) are switched off in the law-admission table (`status: fallback`); requesting
  them refuses with the law-not-admitted code and names the fallback.
- Belt tracks (N18b-06): their thrust-against-slip behaviour on soil is unavailable; on rigid ground they are
  unaffected. The belt pilot's own fallback ("the rigid fallback of KILL-SOIL; road wheels with anisotropic
  friction") refers to this decision.
- Scenario "tracks on rubble": its rigid-ground legs stand; its soft-soil leg is demonstrated on the fallback
  and labelled exploratory.
- Ruts (N18b-17) and the M6 epic "soft soil at scale" are closed on "off" and "not funded" respectively.
- The later lifted planning model for one soft-soil regime, which reads soil cards, loses its plant model and
  falls under its own kill gate.
- Granular media under legs (N18b-15) names "soft-soil law with a card labelled `assumed`" as its fallback;
  with soil killed, that fallback degrades to this bead's rigid fallback.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both triggers are evaluated on measured receipts; the ADR cites the soil rows' receipts and the sourcing
      checklist by identity.
- [ ] At most one repair cycle, opened by the measuring bead's `repair-cycle` note line and closed by one
      re-measurement on unchanged fixtures, seeds, thresholds and controls; a request for a second is recorded
      `deferred` with `deferred_to` and `reason`.
- [ ] The outcome, in N40-03's closed set (`not-fired`, `fired`, `deferred`, `not-reached`), is recorded as
      `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOIL-M4.md` with every field N40-03 requires and as the kill row's entry `decision = {
      outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }` (at least one receipt resolving under `receipts/gates/` for
      `not-fired`; no measured value on the row), by a session that did not implement the soil mechanism, and
      entered at the M4 exit review.
- [ ] If fired: the law-admission table shows the soil rows as `fallback`; a soft region runs on the rigid
      fallback with the `exploratory` label; citing such a result as a soft-ground verdict is refused; the
      downstream beads listed above are re-read and their status updated.
- [ ] If not fired: the soil rows stay admitted in the law-admission table on the tiers where their receipts
      passed, and BD-366, the recorder of GATE-TRAC-08, reads this decision; this bead records no gate.
- [ ] A test shows the fallback path works before it is needed (a region with the drag card runs and is
      labelled), so that a fired gate does not start new development.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/kill_soil.rs`.

- `trigger_logic`: table cases over (gate rows pass or fail, repaired or not, card sourced or not) return the
  stated outcome: (pass, –, sourced) → `not-fired`; (fail, repaired-pass, sourced) → `not-fired`; (fail,
  repaired-fail, sourced) → `fired`; (pass, –, not sourced) → `fired`; (fail, second cycle requested, sourced) →
  `deferred` with `deferred_to` and `reason`; (soil pilot `falsifier-holds` on simulation) → `not-reached`.
- `decision_entry_form`: the entry this bead writes loads under N40-03's checks; a `not-fired` entry with no
  receipt, an outcome outside the closed set, and a measured value on the kill row are each refused.
- `fallback_region_runs`: a region with rolling resistance c_rr = 0.05 and a drag card c_d = 0.1 (`assumed`):
  a towed 100 N load needs (0.05 + 0.1)·100 N = 15 N to move at constant speed, within 1%.
- `exploratory_label_propagates`: the claim product of a run over a fallback region has `exploratory`; a
  promotion request on it refuses.
- `law_table_fallback_status`: with the kill fixture set, the soil rows report `fallback` and name it.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kill_soil.sh` (helper of STD-E2E; header `# e2e: bead=N18b-18 milestone=M4
labels=traction,kill-gate seed=1 needs=cpu`): `unit` (`FROB_TEST_SEED=1 cargo test -p frob-contact --test
kill_soil`); `inputs` (reads the soil rows' receipts of N18b-12 and N18b-13 by hash, the repair-cycle note lines
of their logs if any, and the sourcing checklist), printing the outcome the trigger logic returns; `fallback`
(exercises the fallback region through `frob sim run --fixture traction/kill_soil_tow --tier batch-cpu --seed 1
--key ks-tow --json`, the fixture committed by this bead: a 100 N towed load on a region with c_rr = 0.05 and a
drag card c_d = 0.1 labelled `assumed`, followed by `frob telemetry query <run> --stream contact --json` for the
tow force), asserting the `exploratory` label and 15 N within 1%; `decision` (when the ADR exists, checks that
`docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOIL-M4.md` carries every N40-03 field and that the kill row's decision entry names an
outcome of the closed set and receipts that equal the inputs' hashes and resolve under `receipts/gates/`); the
script writes neither the entry nor the ADR. NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/kill_soil/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript; lines carry `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time,
the soil row values against thresholds with their receipt hashes, the trigger evaluation, the `evidence` block
with its conditions, and the ADR identity when present; on failure the first differing value. `summary` states
the outcome (`not-fired`, `fired`, `deferred` or `not-reached`) and the gates touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-415 and KA-416 (the rows whose 2% simulated tolerance decides trigger 1); KA-419 (rut leak) is recorded with
the gate by BD-366 and N18b-17 and is not an input here. Derived here: the tow force of the fallback,
(c_rr + c_d)·N. Negative controls: a decision recorded with a threshold edited during the repair cycle (the
receipt's threshold hash differs from the registry's and the test fails); a fired gate with the soil rows still
`admitted`; an ADR missing one of N40-03's fields, or recorded by the implementing session.

## Considerations

- The decision is made by a session that evaluates recorded values; it is reviewed at the milestone exit by a
  fresh-context session that did not implement the work.
- Trigger 2 depends on an owner decision; if that decision is late, the fail-closed reading is that no card is
  sourced, and the gate fires on trigger 2 until one is.
- The fallback's drag coefficient is `assumed` by definition; no realism is claimed for it, which is the point
  of the `exploratory` label.
