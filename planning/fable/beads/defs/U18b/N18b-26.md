## Background

Single precision is the only arithmetic on Metal and the only fast one on an RTX 4090 (double precision runs at
about 1/64 of the single-precision rate, FACT to recheck). The contact solve factors one small dense matrix
H = A + JᵀGJ per world, where G grows with contact stiffness. The accuracy of that solve is governed by the
condition number κ of the scaled matrix times the unit round-off u = 6.0·10⁻⁸.

FACT (computed estimates on one humanoid, to be re-run before a gate cites them): at default compliant settings
the scaled κ is 2·10³ to 9·10³ (κ·u about 10⁻⁴ to 5·10⁻⁴); with near-rigid contact it is 2·10⁵ to 5·10⁵
(κ·u = 0.012 to 0.03). A card stiff enough pushes κ·u toward one, where a single-precision Newton step has no
correct digits.

DESIGN: stiffness is a physical parameter that comes from an interface card, so the question "can this card run
in single precision" is decided at admission, before any world is stepped, and the answer is a named refusal with
repairs instead of a degraded run discovered later. A card whose stiffness puts the estimated scaled κ·u above
the admission bound on an `f32` tier is refused there (`FROB-REF-244`) and runs on `batch-cpu` in `f64`. The
bound is a TARGET with default 0.05 and is open question OPEN-TRAC-4, decided by GATE-TRAC-10.

Rejected alternatives: silently adding regularization to stiff cards (changes the physics the card states, and a
uniform shift ε·I is large for a rotor-inertia row and negligible for a base row); letting the run proceed and
relying on the run-time `Degraded: ill-conditioned` flag alone (wastes a campaign and biases comparisons toward
soft cards); half-precision or mixed-precision factors (refused outright, N18b-27).

## Scope

In: the admission estimate of scaled κ·u for a (model, interface card set, step plan) triple; the refusal
`FROB-REF-244` with its repairs; the automatic `f64` route on `batch-cpu`; the third clause of GATE-TRAC-10
("cards over the bound refused"); recording the decision of OPEN-TRAC-4. Out: the per-step κ̂ and the run-time
flag (BD-156 and the numerics unit); the solve itself.

## Technical approach

**The estimate (DESIGN).** At step-plan admission on a tier whose precision is `f32`:

1. Draw N_adm admission states by address from the model's stance set (default 64): nominal stance poses of the
   archetype with every stance contact active, plus the declared worst case (all end-effector samples in
   contact), because κ grows with the number and stiffness of active rows.
2. For each state assemble, on the host in `f64`, A (mass matrix and implicit terms at the step plan's step h), the
   contact Jacobian J and the Hessian blocks G of the admitted law at zero sliding velocity with the card's
   stiffness, dissipation time and friction parameters; form H = A + JᵀGJ.
3. Apply the power-of-two scaling rule of N18b-25 to obtain Ĥ.
4. Compute κ₂(Ĥ) = λ_max/λ_min by a symmetric eigen-solve in `f64`.
5. κ_adm = the maximum over the admission states; the admission figure is κ_adm·u with u = 2⁻²⁴ ≈ 6.0·10⁻⁸.
6. Compare with the bound b_adm (default 0.05, a named constant in `registries/` that GATE-TRAC-10 fixes).

**Outcomes.** κ_adm·u ≤ b_adm: admitted; the figure, the bound and the sample identity are written into the
admission record and reported by `sim.estimate` and `contact.laws`. κ_adm·u > b_adm on an `f32` tier: refusal
`FROB-REF-244`, "Stiffness beyond single precision", exit code 3, with `observed` = κ_adm·u, `required` = b_adm,
`pointer` = the card and field (the stiffness of the interface card that dominates), `retry` = `after-change`,
and ranked repairs: (1) run on `batch-cpu` in `f64` (the envelope gives the exact command); (2) a softer
qualified card. Under the placement planner's default policy a job that names no tier is routed to `batch-cpu`
in `f64` with a warning and the route is printed; a job that names an `f32` tier is refused.

**What the estimate is and is not.** It is an estimate from sampled states, classed `Estimated`; it bounds
nothing. The run-time conditioning figure κ̂ (a lower bound read off the factor) and its limit remain in force
during the run. Admission states are a function of the model and the address stream only, so the decision is
deterministic and replays.

**Deciding OPEN-TRAC-4.** GATE-TRAC-10 measures, on 10⁴ sampled contact states per reference robot at default and
near-rigid cards, the relative error of the `f32` Newton step against `f64`. The decision record (an ADR in
`docs/adr/`, the one directory of decision records) states
the bound at which the 5% near-rigid error target is still met with margin, the false-refusal rate on the
default cards (must be zero) and the missed-refusal rate (cards admitted whose measured step error exceeds 5%).
Fail-closed default until then: 0.05.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob_contact::admission::stiffness_kappa_u(model, cards, plan) -> AdmissionFigure` exists and is called by
      step-plan admission on every `f32` tier.
- [ ] A card over the bound on an `f32` tier returns `FROB-REF-244` with observed and required values and both
      repairs, and the envelope's `next_actions` carries the `f64` repair command: the same request with
      `--tier batch-cpu --precision f64` and a fresh `--key`. `--precision` takes N35-04's values
      `{default|stiff|f32|f64}`, resolved per tier, with `f64` admitted on the referee and `batch-cpu` only
      (`FROB-REF-604` elsewhere); the option is N35-04's, which depends on this bead through BD-156, so this bead
      prints the command and N18b-30, once the batched step exists, asserts at the command line that it runs.
- [ ] Step-plan admission (the profile-by-tier admission of N16a-02, keyed by the `StepPlanId` of N16a-03) calls
      the estimate on every `f32` tier and writes the figure into the admission record, from which
      `sim.estimate` prints it; `contact.laws` with a `design` and an `f32` `tier` evaluates the same function on
      the host for that tier's default plan (an `f64` eigen-solve, no device and no stepping needed) and prints
      the figure, the bound and the sample identity in its per-design block.
- [ ] Default cards of every reference robot are admitted (zero false refusals); a planted near-rigid card with
      stiffness raised until κ_adm·u = 0.06 is refused; one at 0.04 is admitted.
- [ ] The third clause of GATE-TRAC-10 is recorded with measured values (TARGET).
- [ ] The decision of OPEN-TRAC-4 is recorded as an ADR with the measured error curve; the constant in the
      registry matches it.
- [ ] The admission figure appears in `frob contact laws --design <d> --tier <f32 tier> --json` (this bead's
      script) and in `frob sim estimate --json` (N18b-30's stage, with the batched tiers).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/admission_kappa.rs`.

- `rank_one_closed_form`: a rank-one contact operator W with shift ρ·I, ρ = 10⁻²·tr(W)/48: the eigenvalues are
  tr(W) + ρ and ρ, so κ = 1 + 48/0.01 = 4,801 exactly; the function returns 4,801 within 10⁻⁹ relative and
  κ·u = 2.86·10⁻⁴ for `f32`.
- `monotone_in_stiffness`: doubling the card stiffness never lowers κ_adm on the stance fixtures.
- `threshold_strict`: figures 0.0500 (admitted) and 0.0501 (refused).
- `deterministic`: the same model, cards and plan give the same figure and the same admission states twice and
  across worker counts.
- `repairs_present`: the refusal carries both repairs in the stated order and exit code 3.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/stiffness_admission.sh` (helper of STD-E2E), owned by this bead. The batched step (BD-159) reaches
this bead through the solve (BD-156), so `frob sim run` on an `f32` tier cannot run when this bead lands; the
script therefore drives what exists then, and the command-line run is a stage of N18b-30, which depends on this
bead and on the batched step and runs this script first. Steps: (1) `FROB_TEST_SEED=<s> cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test g3_metamorphic -- GATE-TRAC-10/admission` (with `--features gauntlet-mutations` for the
mutant pass; tier G3, the tier of KA-422; the Gauntlet's tiers are
test targets, never an agent verb: ADR-GAUNT-01), which calls step-plan admission on host fixtures with no
stepping: the default card set of every reference robot admitted (zero false refusals), the planted near-rigid
card at κ_adm·u = 0.06 refused with `FROB-REF-244` (observed 0.06, required 0.05, both repairs in rank order,
the `f64` repair command in `next_actions`), the card at 0.04 admitted, the unscaled-estimator mutant
distinguished; (2) the same target at two worker counts and twice (identical figures and admission-state
digests); (3) `frob contact laws --design <fixture design with the near-rigid card> --tier gpu-cuda --profile
lockstep --json` (N18b-28's verb; the figure is computed on the host, so no device is needed) prints the figure
0.06, the bound 0.05 and the sample identity, and the same with the default card set prints a figure under the
bound and the same digest twice. NDJSON lines carry `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command,
exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, the refusal block, κ_adm·u against b_adm, the admission-state digest
and the receipt hash; on failure the first differing value and artifact paths. `summary` names GATE-TRAC-10.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-422 (cards over the bound refuse with FROB-REF-244). KA-423's κ = 4,801 as the closed-form check of the
estimator. Negative control: an estimator that skips the scaling of step 3 reports the raw κ (2 to 3·10⁶
near-rigid, κ·u = 0.12 to 0.18) and refuses cards the scaled figure admits; the test plants it and requires the
near-rigid humanoid card set to distinguish the two (scaled 0.01 to 0.03, admitted; raw 0.12 to 0.18, refused).
The default card set cannot: its raw κ of 3·10⁴ to 10⁵ gives κ·u of 0.0018 to 0.006, under the bound either way.

## Considerations

- The estimate depends on the admission sample. A design whose worst conditioning occurs outside the stance set
  (a fall with many body contacts) is caught at run time by κ̂, not here; the envelope says which check fired.
- Mixed batches: the figure is computed per model slot; a batch is admitted only if every slot is.
- OPEN-TRAC-4 owner: Traction lead. What would reopen it: a device family whose measured step error at the bound
  exceeds the target, or the `stiff` profile (one refinement step) moving the usable bound upward.
