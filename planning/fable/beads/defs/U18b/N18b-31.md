## Background

The contact subsystem states seven invariants (properties that must hold in every run) and four proof
obligations (mathematical claims that the implementation relies on). An invariant with no test is a comment,
and a proof obligation with no derivation on file is a belief. Each of them is implemented or relied on by a
mechanism bead, and those beads carry their own tests. This bead exists so that the whole set is checked in
one place, with one test target per identifier, and so that none is lost if a mechanism bead's scope shifts:
it is the audit of the chapter's laws, in the lowest verification tier (laws and self-tests) and in the
metamorphic tier.

It also writes the two derivations that no mechanism bead owns as code: the integrated squeeze-film law and
the torsional capacities of a patch.

## Scope

In: one named test per invariant and per proof obligation; the derivation files; the mapping from identifier to
owning mechanism bead and to its registered negative control. Out: the mechanisms; the known-answer battery
(N18b-32); gates (each identifier's test is cited by the gate of its mechanism).

## Technical approach

**Invariants.**

| ID | Statement | How it is checked | Owning mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| INV-TRAC-01 | Every law on a lockstep tier is a convex potential, or is the hard-contact law and reports its residual | Hessian positive semidefinite by construction (theorem card T-08), property-tested over each law's parameter range; residual present in every receipt of the hard-contact law | Law-admission table (N18b-28); law implementations |
| INV-TRAC-02 | Friction parameters come from interface cards; a state-dependent coefficient is constant inside a solve | Card provenance in the model; a digest of the coefficient slab taken before and after stage 8 is identical | Interface cards (BD-167); film law (BD-230); tyre and soil limits |
| INV-TRAC-03 | An outer enclosure contains its body and an inner enclosure is contained in it under every admitted error. `clear` uses only disjoint outers; `penetrates` uses only intersecting inners | Randomized bodies with known truth; the 6 mm case (6 mm nominal gap with 4 mm outward error per side: outers overlap by 2 mm, answer `unknown`) | Mesh path and enclosures (BD-232) |
| INV-TRAC-04 | No contact is removed to fit a capacity; a comparison with unequal overflow is labeled | Forced overflow at every capacity; the capacity-bias fixture | Capacity (N18b-20, N18b-22) |
| INV-TRAC-05 | Impulses and forces are typed apart; no tolerance applies to both | The kernel dimension checker: a kernel that compares or adds a quantity in N·s with one in N fails to build | Kernel IR; every contact kernel |
| INV-TRAC-06 | Tangential memory is transported or reset at every witness change and each reset is an event | The sill fixture (a torque-driven wheel climbing a sharp sill: feature sequence flat, edge, top); a mutation that keeps memory on a changed feature | Witness migration (BD-093, BD-086) |
| INV-TRAC-07 | The film law returns the dry card exactly when no film is present, μ_b for h ≤ σ and pure Couette shear for h ≥ 3σ, and is continuous in h between | Bit comparison at h₀ = 0; limits and continuity on 10⁴ random states | Film law (BD-230) |

**Proof obligations.**

| ID | Obligation | Class and route |
|---|---|---|
| PO-TRAC-01 | The four-dimensional cone is a linear image of the second-order cone; projection and Moreau identities hold | Certified by polynomial identity; property tests in the laws tier |
| PO-TRAC-02 | The integrated Stefan law follows from dh/dt = −2·N·h³/(3π·η·a⁴) | Derivation on file; symbolic check by the symbolic sibling |
| PO-TRAC-03 | Cholesky of S·H·S equals S times Cholesky of H bit for bit for power-of-two S, absent overflow and underflow | Proved (scale invariance of rounding under the radix); tested on 10⁵ systems (carried by N18b-25) |
| PO-TRAC-04 | The torsional capacities (2/3)·μ·N·a and (3π/16)·μ·N·a follow from the uniform and Hertzian pressure integrals | Derivation on file |

**Derivations written by this bead** (`docs/derivations/`).

- *PO-TRAC-01* (`po-trac-01.md`). The cone 𝒦 = {(λₙ, λ_t1, λ_t2, λ_s) : √(λ_t1² + λ_t2² + (λ_s/r_eff)²) ≤ μ·λₙ}
  is T·𝒬 with 𝒬 = {(x₀, x̄) : ‖x̄‖ ≤ x₀} the second-order cone in ℝ⁴ and T = diag(1/μ, 1, 1, r_eff): setting
  λₙ = x₀/μ, λ_t = (x̄₁, x̄₂), λ_s = r_eff·x̄₃ gives ‖(λ_t1, λ_t2, λ_s/r_eff)‖ = ‖x̄‖ ≤ x₀ = μ·λₙ. The dual cone
  is T⁻ᵀ·𝒬 = {μ·‖(v_t1, v_t2, r_eff·ω_n)‖ ≤ v_n}. The projection identities (P(x) ∈ 𝒦, P(x) − x ∈ 𝒦*,
  ⟨P(x), P(x) − x⟩ = 0, in the metric induced by T) reduce to the second-order cone's, which are per-case
  polynomial identities and inequalities; the instance certificate is of kind `poly-identity`. With r_eff = 0
  the spin row is masked and the cone is the three-dimensional one.
- *PO-TRAC-02* (`po-trac-02.md`). Separate variables: dh/h³ = −c·dt with c = 2·N/(3π·η·a⁴). Integrating from
  h₀ at t = 0: −1/(2h²) + 1/(2h₀²) = −c·t, hence 1/h² − 1/h₀² = 2·c·t = 4·N·t/(3π·η·a⁴). From a thick film
  (1/h₀² → 0) the time to thickness h is t = 3π·η·a⁴/(4·N·h²). The symbolic check differentiates the integrated
  form and recovers the rate law.
- *PO-TRAC-04* (`po-trac-04.md`). Uniform pressure p = N/(π·a²) on a disc of radius a: torsional capacity
  ∫₀ᵃ μ·p·r·2π·r dr = (2/3)·π·μ·p·a³ = (2/3)·μ·N·a, so r_eff = 2a/3. Hertzian pressure p(r) = p₀·√(1 − r²/a²)
  with p₀ = 3·N/(2π·a²): ∫₀ᵃ μ·p(r)·2π·r² dr = 2π·μ·p₀·a³·∫₀^{π/2} sin²θ·cos²θ dθ = 2π·μ·p₀·a³·π/16 =
  (3π/16)·μ·N·a, so r_eff = 3π·a/16 ≈ 0.589·a.

**Test targets.** `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0 -- TRAC/<id>` for laws and proof obligations;
`--test g3` for the metamorphic forms (INV-TRAC-02 digest equality, INV-TRAC-04 under slot permutation). Each
identifier maps to its negative control in `controls.toml`; the control must fail the identifier's test.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Eleven named test targets exist, one per identifier, each passing on the mechanisms that exist at M3 and
      reporting `not built` with the owning bead for any that does not.
- [ ] Three derivation files exist (PO-TRAC-01, -02, -04) and PO-TRAC-03's is cited from its own bead; each is
      referenced by the registry row of the known answer it supports (the squeeze times; r_eff = 2a/3 and
      3π·a/16; the cone identities).
- [ ] PO-TRAC-01 has an instance certificate of kind `poly-identity` that the offline auditor replays.
- [ ] PO-TRAC-02's symbolic check passes (differentiating the integrated law returns the rate law).
- [ ] Each invariant's negative control fails its test: a non-convex planted law (01); a coefficient updated
      inside the solve (02); the nominal gap taken as clearance (03); contacts removed to fit capacity (04); a
      kernel mixing N·s and N (05); tangential memory kept on a changed feature (06); a film blend that rounds
      at h₀ = 0 (07).
- [ ] The identifier-to-bead mapping is stored as data and checked: no identifier of the chapter lacks a test
      and a control.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/traction_laws.rs`.

- `inv_trac_01_convexity`: 10⁴ random states per convex law; smallest eigenvalue of each Hessian block at
  least −10⁻¹² relative. `inv_trac_01_residual`: schema check of a hard-contact receipt.
- `inv_trac_02_digest`: coefficient slab digest before and after stage 8 over 10⁴ steps with film, tyre and
  soil limits active where built.
- `inv_trac_03_enclosures`: 10⁴ generated pairs with known truth, no false `clear`, no false `penetrates`;
  6 mm nominal with 4 mm per side → `unknown`; 10 mm → `clear` with 2 mm to spare.
- `inv_trac_04_overflow`: forced overflow at capacities 8, 16, 32, 64: flagged or refused, sample-list digest
  equals the detected set.
- `inv_trac_05_dimensions`: a compile-fail (kernel build-fail) fixture that adds an impulse to a force.
- `inv_trac_06_memory`: sill fixture feature sequence and reset events; the keep-memory mutant produces a
  couple above 10⁻⁹ N·m.
- `inv_trac_07_film_limits`: bit equality at h₀ = 0; μ_b for h ≤ σ; Couette for h ≥ 3σ; continuity on 10⁴
  states (difference across a step of 10⁻⁶·σ bounded by a Lipschitz constant times the step).
- `po_trac_01_identities`: projection and Moreau identities on 10⁵ random points, isotropic and anisotropic,
  and the r_eff = 0 case.
- `po_trac_02_stefan`: numerical integration of the rate law against the closed form within 10⁻⁹; the three
  times 0.0377 s, 2.26 s, 0.763 s.
- `po_trac_04_integrals`: quadrature of both pressure integrals against (2/3)·μ·N·a and (3π/16)·μ·N·a within
  10⁻¹⁰ relative; μ = 0.6, N = 100 N, a = 30 mm → 1.2 N·m and 1.060 N·m.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/traction_laws.sh` (helper of STD-E2E): runs the `g0` and `g3` targets for the eleven identifiers
with the controls off and then on, and `frob prove verify` on the PO-TRAC-01 certificate. NDJSON lines carry
the identifier, the command, exit code, wall time, pass or `not built`, the control's outcome, and the
certificate hash; a `summary` line counts identifiers passed, pending and failed.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-398 (r_eff = 2a/3 and 3π·a/16), KA-404 (Stefan times), KA-406 and KA-407 (film limits), KA-410 (clearance
with errors), KA-420 (capacity bias), KA-422 (scale invariance), KA-424 (the four-dimensional cone as a linear
image of the second-order cone; r_eff = 0 masks the spin row). Controls as listed under acceptance.

## Considerations

- Several identifiers belong to mechanisms built by other units; where a mechanism bead already has the test,
  this bead's target calls the same test function so that there is one test and two entry points, not two
  tests that can drift.
- Milestone M3 is the latest milestone among the mechanisms (film law, enclosures); tests for earlier
  mechanisms run from the milestone at which those land.
- A proof obligation's class is recorded honestly: PO-TRAC-03 is Proved on paper and Tested per back end;
  PO-TRAC-01 is Certified by instance; PO-TRAC-02 and -04 are derivations on file with a symbolic or numeric
  check, and are never called proved.
