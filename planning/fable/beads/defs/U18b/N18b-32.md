## Background

A known answer is a case whose truth is known without running the candidate: a closed form, a conservation law,
a hand-checkable count. The contact subsystem has a battery of 29 such cases. Gate GATE-TRAC-01 is the statement
that every row that tests a Solid mechanism passes on `strict` and on `batch-cpu`, and on `referee` for the rows
the consumed referee solve can express (that list is recorded with the gate); rows for the fast law pass on GPU
tiers within the stated creep.

The battery is run by three other beads on three sets of tiers (referee at M1, `strict` at M2, batched tiers at
M2). To keep them from drifting into three different batteries, the rows are defined once, here, as fixtures in
`frob-conformance`: scene, bodies, cards, drive, duration, the measured quantity, the truth with its formula,
the tolerance, the negative control that must fail, and the tiers on which the row is meaningful. Rules that
bind the battery: one fixture identity per physical case; a known answer is derived on file (one derivation file
per row under `docs/derivations/`) and never regenerated from the candidate; a row is registered with its
failing control before its passing test is written, because a gate that nothing can fail validates nothing.

Several rows were printed with the tolerance "to set". This bead fixes them (each recorded in the registry
with its reason) so that no row can pass by having no bound. Where the bead that carries a row has already set
its tolerance (BD-085 for KA-388 and KA-395, N18a-28 for KA-413), the fixture takes that value, so that the
registry N18-KA, the carrier and this battery state one number; the others are set here as DESIGN.

## Scope

In: the 29 fixtures with tolerances, controls and the row-by-tier applicability table; the test targets that the
tier beads invoke. Out: running the rows on each tier and recording the gate (BD-085, BD-161, BD-168); the
mechanisms the rows test; the full fixtures of the Arena unit.

## Technical approach

**Registry rows.** `frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml`, one entry per row: `ka`, `fixture`, `truth`
(formula and substituted value), `tolerance`, `tiers`, `control`, `derivation`, `milestone`. Test target:
`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- GATE-TRAC-01/<tier>/<row>`.

**The rows.** Tiers: R referee, S `strict`, L lockstep. "Late" marks rows whose mechanism lands after M2; they
are registered now and report `not built` until then.

**Fixture discipline.** Every fixture is a public synthetic scene with an explicit seed; randomized rows (the
thin-barrier crossings) draw by address; each fixture declares the mutation flag that plants its negative
control, and the control must fail the row on every tier where the row runs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 29 registry entries exist, each with truth, tolerance, tiers, control and a derivation file; no entry has
      an empty tolerance.
- [ ] Every row's test target exists and runs on the host at M1 (reporting `not built` for late rows), and every
      control is registered before its row's passing test.
- [ ] On the referee at M1 the expressible list is recorded beside GATE-TRAC-01 with the reason for each row
      left out.
- [ ] Each negative control fails its row on at least one tier at M1 where the mechanism exists (rows 1, 3, 5,
      7, 19, 20); the rest as their mechanisms land.
- [ ] The values in the table reproduce from their formulas in the host derivation tests (exact rationals
      where rational).
- [ ] The battery identity (a hash of the registry entries and fixture files) is printed in every receipt that
      cites GATE-TRAC-01.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/traction_battery_host.rs` (derivations, host only).

- `spin_stop_value`: 3·0.05·20/(4·0.5·9.81) = 0.1529 s; the control with r_eff = a stops at
  I·ω₀/(μ·N·a) = a·ω₀/(2·μ·g) = 0.1019 s.
- `ring_costop_value`: √2/(0.5·9.81) = 0.2883 s; independent rows: v stops at v₀/(μ·g) = 0.2039 s.
- `backlash_values`: β·N/ω₀ = (0.5°·π/180)·10/5 = 0.01745 s; J·N·ω₀/(J·N² + I_out) = 0.005/0.06 = 0.0833 rad/s.
- `strip_capacity`: z = 0 → 0.525; z = 12 mm, w = 40 mm → 0.25 + 0.55·0.8 = 0.690; w = 70 mm → 0.25 +
  0.55·0.6714 = 0.619.
- `thin_barrier_arithmetic`: 5 m/s × 2 ms = 10 mm; 1 + 4 + 2 = 7 mm; 3 mm beyond the far face.
- `sinkage_value`, `thrust_values`: 19.6 mm; 388.7 N and 293.3 N.
- `registry_complete`: 29 entries, none with an empty tolerance or control except rows whose printed control is
  "none stated", for which this bead's control is recorded.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/traction_battery_host.sh` (helper of STD-E2E): runs the host derivation target and
`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- GATE-TRAC-01/host`, then lists the registry through the
conformance crate's printer; logs per row the truth, the recomputed value, the tolerance and the control's
status; `summary` names GATE-TRAC-01 and the battery identity.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-387 to KA-420 as tabulated (KA-390, KA-398, KA-407, KA-411, KA-412 belong to their mechanism beads and are
not battery rows). Counterexamples seeding the controls: CX-169, CX-171, CX-172, CX-173, CX-179, CX-180,
CX-183, CX-148. Determinism: the host target is exact or `f64` and bit-reproducible across debug and release.

## Considerations

- The tolerances marked DESIGN or TARGET replace "to set" and are owned by this registry until the conformance lead
  revises them; a later tightening is a recorded registry change, never an edit to a passing test.
- Two rows share a formula with other chapters under different illustrative cards (a backlash gap closing at
  0.577 rad/s with 0.0115 J elsewhere, against 17.5 ms and 0.083 rad/s here). Both are internally consistent;
  each keeps its own fixture identity.
- The track rejection fixture of the design-language unit ("turning in place at zero scrub work") points to a
  belt-law known answer that this battery does not list under that name; the nearest row is 27 (skid-steer under
  exact no-slip: yaw rate zero), and the scrub-work answer itself is carried by the belt bead N18b-06.
