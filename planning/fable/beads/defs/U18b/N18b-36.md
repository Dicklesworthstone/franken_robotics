## Background

The film-friction law (wet and greasy floors) and the soft-soil laws (pressure–sinkage and shear) are
semi-empirical: their parameters are measured properties of real fluids, floors and soils. The viscosity of a
spilled liquid, the combined roughness of a floor, the boundary-lubricated coefficient, and the soil constants
k_c, k_φ, n, c, φ and K come from tables. Every card field carries a provenance (`measured`, `datasheet` with a
citation, `assumed`) and an uncertainty, and the class of a result is the class of its weakest input.

Open question OPEN-TRAC-3: **provenance for film and soil cards: which measured tables are admitted.** Owner:
the project lead. It is grouped with two sibling questions (which real actuator parts and which sensor cards
enter the catalogues, and under what data licences) and falls due at M4.

Fail-closed default, in force until this is decided: generic ranges labelled `assumed`, which keep every result
that depends on them `Estimated`; claims of realism for wet floors and soft ground, and any Reality claim, are
refused. The question blocks claims of realism, not validity: the laws run, pass their known answers and
compare designs on `assumed` cards from M3 and M4 onward. It also bears on a kill gate: KILL-SOIL fires if
GATE-TRAC-08 fails after one repair cycle **or no soil card can be sourced with provenance**, so the answer to
this question is one of the two inputs of that decision.

Standing rule that constrains the answer: the values a gate pins are derived on file from a cited table read at
its primary source; no value is taken from a script that is not in the repository. A number recalled from
background knowledge is not a sourced value until its primary source has been read and recorded.

## Scope

In: the decision, recorded as the ADR `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-TRAC-3.md`, with the admitted sources (possibly none), their licences, the fields
they supply and the provenance class each field receives; the sourcing check that feeds KILL-SOIL. Out: writing
the laws; building the cards' schema; actuator and sensor catalogues.

## Technical approach

**What must be decided.**

1. *Soil tables.* Whether at least one published pressure–sinkage and shear parameter table (k_c, k_φ, n, c, φ,
   K for named soils, with moisture state) is admitted; from which primary source; under what licence or
   fair-use basis the numeric values may be stored in a card in this repository; and what uncertainty is
   attached where the source gives none.
2. *Film tables.* The same for viscosities of the liquids of the hazard lists (water, cooking oil, detergent
   solution) with temperature, and for floor roughness and boundary-lubricated coefficients by floor finish.
3. *Provenance class.* For each admitted field: `measured` (the project has a measurement), `datasheet` (a cited
   published value) or `assumed`. A cited textbook table is `datasheet` with its citation; it is not `measured`.
4. *Scope of realism claims.* Whether an admitted `datasheet` card lifts the refusal of realism claims for wet
   floors and soft ground, or whether such claims wait for the calibration lane (measured data from a bench or a
   real place). DESIGN recommendation to the owner: `datasheet` cards make results citable as "on the cited
   card", still `Estimated`, and Reality claims remain refused until calibrated data exists.
5. *Licences.* Every admitted table has a licence line in the licence manifest; a table whose licence does not
   permit redistribution may be referenced by citation only, with values entered by the user, and never ships.

**The sourcing check (feeds KILL-SOIL).** A source counts as "a soil card sourced with provenance" only if: the
primary text was read (not a summary); the card's values are transcribed into `docs/derivations/soil-cards.md`
with page or table reference; the derived known-answer values a gate pins are recomputed from that file; and
the licence line exists. The check is a checklist with a recorded yes or no per source, and its outcome (at
least one source passes, or none does) is written into the KILL-SOIL decision record
(`docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOIL-M4.md`, N18b-18) as the measured input of its second trigger.

**Decision record (ADR).** Options considered (admit named tables as `datasheet`; admit none and stay
`assumed`; admit only user-supplied measured cards), the choice, the reason, the effect on evidence classes,
and what would reopen it. Until the ADR exists, the default holds everywhere and is enforced mechanically:
generic cards carry `assumed`, and a realism or Reality claim over them is refused by the claim product.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-TRAC-3.md` records the owner's decision on soil tables, film tables, provenance
      classes, the scope of realism claims and licences, with the alternatives and reasons, and the open-question
      registry of N41-OPEN2 points to it.
- [ ] The sourcing checklist is filled for every candidate source, with a yes or no outcome and the reason; its
      result is cited by the KILL-SOIL decision.
- [ ] Until the ADR exists, a test shows the fail-closed default in force: every film and soil card in the
      shipped catalogue has provenance `assumed`; results on them are `Estimated`; a realism claim over them is
      refused.
- [ ] After the ADR: admitted cards exist with citation, uncertainty and licence line, or the catalogue is
      unchanged and the ADR says so.
- [ ] No gate-pinned value comes from a script outside the repository or from an unread source.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/card_provenance.rs`.

- `generic_cards_are_assumed`: every film and soil card under `assets/` has provenance `assumed` unless a
  citation and a licence line exist.
- `assumed_keeps_estimated`: the claim product of a soil-thrust result on an `assumed` card has class
  `Estimated`, and its weakest-input pointer names the card field.
- `realism_refused`: a request that cites a wet-floor or soft-ground result as realistic on an `assumed` card is
  refused with `FROB-REF-819` (exit 3) from the evidence boundary (N37-26's precedence), `observed` `Estimated`
  and `weakest` naming the card and its field; no contact-card code exists and 312 is not widened.
- `datasheet_requires_citation`: a card field with provenance `datasheet` and no citation fails to load.
- `licence_line_required`: an admitted table without a licence manifest entry fails the catalogue check.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/card_provenance.sh` (helper of STD-E2E): `frob contact laws --json` (every film and soil card in the
shipped catalogue listed with provenance `assumed`, or with citation and licence line where the ADR admitted
it); `frob contact probe --card <generic interface card> ... --json` and `frob contact probe --film --card
<generic film card> ... --json` (N18b-29's `--film` selector, the film branch of BD-230), asserting class
`Estimated` and the weakest-input pointer naming the `assumed` field in the `evidence` block; then a realism
claim over such a result, which needs a stored result: `frob sim run quad-mammal --scene <a scene whose floor
binds the generic interface card> --tier batch-cpu --key cp-assumed --json` followed by `frob report run <that
run> --claims-realism --json` → `FROB-REF-819`, exit 3 (a result cited above its claim product, raised at the
evidence boundary by N37-26's precedence; `observed` `Estimated`), asserted with `e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-819 3`
and with the card and its field in `weakest` (312 stays the code for `assumed` scene dimensions and is not
widened; there is no contact-card code). The verb `contact.soil` (BD-366) comes after
this bead through the kill decision N18b-18, so the same assertion on a soil card at the command line is made
by the `verb` stage of BD-366's `soil_verb.sh` (which N18b-19 also runs) and is not repeated here; the soil card's provenance is checked here through the
catalogue listing and the unit tests. NDJSON lines carry `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command,
exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, the card identity and provenance per field, and the refusal block;
on failure the first differing field and artifact paths. `summary` records whether `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-TRAC-3.md`
exists and which default is in force.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-027 and CX-014 (a result is reported at the class of its weakest input: a friction coefficient from an
interface-card field marked `assumed` makes the result `Estimated`). Negative control: a planted card with
provenance `datasheet` and an empty citation; a planted claim-product combination that takes the strongest
input.

## Considerations

- This is an owner decision: an agent prepares the options and the sourcing checklist and does not choose.
- The illustrative soil constants used in known answers (k_c = 1 kPa/mⁿ⁻¹, k_φ = 1,500 kPa/mⁿ, n = 1.1; c =
  1 kPa, φ = 30°, K = 25 mm; and the second card with n = 0.5) are illustrative substitutions, not sourced soil
  data. They test arithmetic and must not be presented as a soil.
- If no source passes the check, KILL-SOIL's second trigger is met and soft-ground results are labelled
  exploratory on a rigid floor with rolling resistance and a drag card labelled `assumed`.
- What would reopen the decision: bench or field data entering through the calibration lane.
