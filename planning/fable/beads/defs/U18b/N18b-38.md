## Background

A contact carries a witness pair (one point on each body, in body coordinates), a contact frame, and tangential
memory: the stick anchor of a regularized friction law, accumulated tangential displacement, and the warm-start
impulse. The consumed referee step assumes witnesses and frame are fixed; a wheel rolling over a lip, a sliding
sole and a rocking foot violate that. At every redetection the contact subsystem therefore re-localizes the
witnesses by closest features, classifies the change as `same-feature`, `adjacent-feature` (face to edge to
face around a lip) or `new-contact`, and then:

- keeps the tangential memory on the same feature;
- for an adjacent feature whose world contact point moved continuously (inside a tolerance δ_w),
  **transports** it: rotates it by the minimal rotation that takes the old normal to the new one and projects
  it onto the new tangent plane;
- for a new contact, or when the contact point jumped, **resets** the tangential memory to zero and emits a
  `witness-reset` event.

Tangential memory is never kept silently on a changed feature (invariant INV-TRAC-06).

Two numbers in that rule are open (OPEN-TRAC-5): **δ_w**, the largest movement of the world contact point that
still counts as continuous, and **the largest normal rotation that is transported** instead of reset. Default:
the sample radius and 30°. Owner: Traction lead. Decided on the sill fixture at M1. Fail-closed default:
beyond the tolerances, reset with an event. A reset is always safe (it discards memory and is announced); the
tolerances only decide how often memory is preserved, which affects creep, spurious couples and convergence
under step refinement.

Why it matters: too tight, and a wheel rolling over a rounded sill resets its friction memory at every step,
which shows up as stick-slip chatter and as work that does not converge under step halving; too loose, and
memory is carried across a real discontinuity, producing a couple that the contact forces do not explain.

## Technical approach

**Fixtures.** (1) The sill, `fixture:wheel-over-lip` of BD-086 (R = 0.10 m, 5 kg, sill 0.03 m with a sharp
lip, μ = 0.8, τ = 5 N·m, h₀ = 2 ms): a torque-driven wheel climbing a sharp sill, flat to edge to top. Truth:
feature sequence flat, edge (`new-contact`, one `witness-reset`, since the lip is touched while the flat contact
is still active), top (`adjacent-feature`, memory transported); work and impulse converge under step halving
(within 1% of their refined values at h/4); no couple above 10⁻⁹ N·m in `f64` that the contact forces do not
explain. (2) A rounded sill, `fixture:rounded-sill` (`synthetic`, defined by this bead in
`crates/frob-conformance/src/fixtures/rounded_sill.rs`): the same wheel and drive, with the lip rounded to a
radius equal to twice the sample radius, where the normal turns through 90° continuously: memory should be
transported throughout and no reset should occur at fine steps. (3) A jump, `fixture:tread-jump` (`synthetic`,
defined by this bead in `fixtures/tread_jump.rs`): a foot sample that leaves one tread and lands on the next
within one step, the two treads placed so that the world contact point moves by five sample radii, above every
δ_w of the grid: memory must reset.

The **sample radius** is the radius of the contact sample spheres in the fixture's compiled model (a wheel
carries rim samples, a foot its sole samples; the compile report lists them). It is the length the default δ_w
refers to; the script logs its value in metres for each fixture, so the grid below is in logged lengths.

**Sweep (referee at M1).** δ_w ∈ {¼, ½, 1, 2, 4} × sample radius; rotation limit ∈ {10°, 20°, 30°, 45°, 60°,
90°}; step h ∈ {h₀, h₀/2, h₀/4}. For each combination record: the feature sequence; the count of
`witness-reset` events; work and impulse and their differences from the h/4 values; the largest unexplained
couple (the moment about the wheel centre not accounted for by the sum of contact forces times their arms);
the convergence order under halving; on fixture 3, whether a reset occurred.

**Decision rule (DESIGN, fixed before the sweep).** Choose the loosest pair (δ_w, rotation limit) for which, on
all three fixtures: the sill's bounds hold (1% at h/4; couple at most 10⁻⁹ N·m); the jump fixture resets; and
the rounded sill has no reset at h₀/4. Among pairs that qualify, prefer the one with the fewest resets at h₀
on the sill. If no pair qualifies, the fail-closed pair is used (the tightest, which resets most) and the
finding is recorded with the fixture that failed. If the default (sample radius, 30°) qualifies it is kept,
since a default that passes is not changed for a marginal gain.

**How a run receives the tolerances (DESIGN).** `frob sim run --fixture <name> --migration
delta-w=<metres>,max-rotation-deg=<degrees>` (flag spelling DESIGN). The option is accepted only together with
`--fixture` and a transactional profile, and is a usage error otherwise (`FROB-REF-802`, exit 3, as VERBS-sim's
row for the option states), so no user design is ever run
at tolerances other than the registry's; the pair is written into the run's plan and printed in the envelope.
Without the option a fixture runs at the registry constants (before this bead's decision: the defaults, the
sample radius and 30°). The step is passed as `--step <h>`. Reason: the sweep has to go through the real binary,
and the mechanism (N18a-14) and the fixture (BD-086) already take the two values as parameters.

**Per-step rotation bound.** For a wheel of radius R rolling at speed v over a sharp edge with step h, the
normal turns by about v·h/R per step while on the edge: at v = 1 m/s, h = 2 ms, R = 50 mm that is 0.04 rad
(2.3°) per step, so a 30° limit is never the binding constraint in rolling contact at these step sizes; it
binds when a sample swings from a face to an adjacent face in one step, which is the case the limit exists
for. The sweep confirms this arithmetic.

**Record.** An ADR with the sweep table, the chosen pair and the reason; the constants enter
`registries/contact.toml` as named numerical parameters (reported with every result that used them, never
changed by a step variant or a retry), and are listed with class Numerical in the parameter classification
registry of `frob-dyn` (`crates/frob-dyn/src/params.rs`, N16a-04), where a parameter without a class does not
compile.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The sweep table exists for the three fixtures on the referee, with resets, work and impulse differences,
      the unexplained couple and the convergence order per combination.
- [ ] The decision rule was registered before the sweep; the ADR states the chosen pair and cites the table.
- [ ] With the chosen pair: the sill's feature sequence is flat, edge, top, with the change classes above and
      every reset recorded as a `witness-reset` event (none silent); work and impulse within 1% of refined values
      at h/4; no couple above 10⁻⁹ N·m in `f64` (the sill rows of GATE-TRAC-06 on the referee), checked by
      BD-086's oracles and recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with measured values, commit, fingerprint, the
      chosen pair and the seed, by a session that did not implement the bead.
- [ ] The keep-memory mutation (memory kept on a changed feature) produces an unexplained couple above the
      bound and is caught.
- [ ] Beyond the chosen tolerances the mechanism resets and emits `witness-reset`; no path keeps memory
      silently.
- [ ] The constants are in the registry and appear in the envelope of results from transactional profiles.
- [ ] `fixture:rounded-sill` and `fixture:tread-jump` exist in `frob-conformance`, are labelled `synthetic` and
      are reachable by name; each logs its sample radius in metres.
- [ ] `--migration` is accepted only with `--fixture` under a transactional profile (`FROB-REF-802`, exit 3,
      otherwise), and the
      pair it sets is printed in the envelope of that run.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/witness_tolerances.rs`.

- `rotation_per_step`: v = 1 m/s, h = 2 ms, R = 50 mm → 0.04 rad per step on the edge.
- `transport_is_minimal_rotation`: the transported tangential vector equals the old one rotated by the minimal
  rotation taking n_old to n_new and projected on the new tangent plane; its norm does not increase.
- `beyond_limit_resets`: a normal rotation of 31° with a 30° limit resets and emits one event; 29° transports.
- `jump_resets`: a contact point that moves more than δ_w resets; on `fixture:tread-jump` every pair of the
  grid resets (the point moves five sample radii, the largest δ_w is four).
- `migration_option_fixture_only`: the option without `--fixture` is `FROB-REF-802` (exit 3); with it, the pair
  appears in the run's plan and in the envelope.
- `rounded_sill_no_reset`: at h₀/4 the count of resets is zero with the chosen pair.
- `sill_convergence`: work and impulse at h₀ are within 1% of their values at h₀/4 (the refined values), and
  the differences from the h₀/4 values decrease from h₀ to h₀/2; the convergence order is reported.
- `keep_memory_mutant`: the unexplained couple exceeds 10⁻⁹ N·m.
- The referee tests that BD-086 defines in the fixture's module (`sill_feature_sequence`,
  `sill_refinement_work_impulse`, `sill_no_unexplained_couple`, `sill_keep_memory_mutant_fails`) run here at the
  chosen pair; `sill_tolerances_are_parameters` on the referee: δ_w = 0 gives two `witness-reset` events (both
  transitions reset), the defaults give one (the lip's `new-contact`).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/open_trac_5.sh` (helper of STD-E2E), plain shell, idempotent, seed-explicit, log under
`target/e2e-logs/open_trac_5/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Steps:

1. `sweep`: `frob sim run --tier referee --profile referee --fixture <f> --migration
   delta-w=<m>,max-rotation-deg=<deg> --step <h> --seed <s> --key open-trac-5-<f>-<i>-<s> --json` for the three
   fixtures over the grid (i is the combination's index). Every call carries its own `--key`: `sim.run` is a
   job-submitting run verb, and a call without an idempotency key is refused with `FROB-REF-805`, exit 3.
2. `select`: applies the registered decision rule to the sweep table and logs the pair it selects.
3. `option_is_fixture_only`: `frob sim run g1 --tier referee --migration delta-w=0.005,max-rotation-deg=30
   --key open-trac-5-nofix --json` (no `--fixture`) must exit 3 with `FROB-REF-802` naming `/migration`, and no
   job is submitted.
4. `sill_rows`: `frob sim run --tier referee --profile referee --fixture wheel-over-lip --step <h> --key
   open-trac-5-sill-<h>-<s> --json` at h₀, h₀/2 and h₀/4 with the chosen pair now in the registry (no
   `--migration`), then the Gauntlet's cargo form of the same rows, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<s> cargo test -p
   frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-TRAC-06/referee` (there is no `frob conformance` command),
   which writes the receipt, and its control builds `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-contact/nc-<nnnn>` for
   `keep-memory` and for `endpoint-only` (the numbers are read from the control registry by their catalogue
   entries, never typed), each of which must fail and is logged as a caught control; a control build that
   passes fails the script. The target's filter also re-runs the thin-barrier rows that BD-086 recorded, so
   this receipt carries the whole referee line of the gate with both of its controls caught in the same run.

NDJSON lines carry `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the
`evidence` block, per-combination reset counts, work and impulse differences against the 1% threshold, the
unexplained couple against 10⁻⁹ N·m, digests, the pair the rule selects, for step 4 the feature sequence with
change classes, the reset count against the `witness-reset` event count, the gate rows' measured values with
thresholds and the receipt hash, and on failure the first differing value with the `.frun` path, the fixture and
the seed. `summary` states the decision, the sill rows' verdict, the controls caught and GATE-TRAC-06.

The sill rows enter `registries/gates.toml` from that receipt through the gate recorder (`cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-06 --receipt <hash>`, N38b-19), run by a session
other than the implementer's; the recorder refuses a pass whose listed controls were not caught in the same
run. `scripts/e2e/traction_referee.sh` (N18a-35) re-runs the sill at the registry constants as its `sill` step.

## Considerations

- The chosen constants are numerical parameters: they are reported and frozen per release, and a change is a
  recorded event that re-runs the sill rows.
- If the sibling's fixed-witness gate still refuses on the sill with migration between calls (the transaction
  ends with `FROB-REF-721` after bisection to the finest dyadic cell, BD-093), the refusal is surfaced, the
  sweep on that fixture has no completed runs, the defaults (sample radius, 30°) stay in the registry, and the sill's
  referee rows are recorded as not passed with that reason; the `strict` profile carries them at M2 (BD-161).
  No tolerance is ever chosen from runs that did not complete.
- On the sharp sill itself the two limits are not approached (the lip is a `new-contact` whatever the pair, and
  the edge-to-top change has a continuous point and no rotation), so the pair is discriminated by the rounded
  sill (too tight a pair resets on a continuous turn) and by the jump (too loose a pair transports across a
  discontinuity). A sweep in which every pair qualifies keeps the default and says so.
- The `strict` profile at M2 re-runs the same fixtures; if its best pair differs from the referee's, the
  stricter pair is used on both and the difference is recorded.
- An upstream work item asks the sibling for a moving-surface owner in its sustained step (witness migration
  across an edge with tangential memory transported or reset); its switch-over rule is to re-baseline the
  fidelity ledger once, and these tolerances would then be re-decided against the sibling's rule.
