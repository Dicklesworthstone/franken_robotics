## Background

A response batch reuses the Cholesky factor of a converged contact step to solve many right-hand sides b
(counterfactual impulses, adjoint columns, stencil columns) on a **fixed active set**. Each solution x̂ is the
change in generalized velocity that the load b would cause *if no contact changed mode*. Two things can make a
column wrong, and neither announces itself:

1. *Arithmetic.* The factor and the solves are in the working precision of the step (single precision on
   devices). The error is bounded by the residual divided by the smallest eigenvalue, but only if the residual
   is the true residual, and a residual computed in floating point is not: in single precision H = 3 and b = 1
   give x̂ = 0.33333334, a computed residual of exactly 0 and a true error of 9.9·10⁻⁹. A bound formed from the
   computed residual alone would claim zero error.
2. *Physics.* The linear response is valid only while every row stays in the mode it had at the anchor. A
   block resting on a floor with μ·N = 5 N, anchored in stick, answers a tangential load of 4 N with 4 N of
   friction and no motion: correct. For a load of 6 N the frozen sticking set still reports no motion, while
   the true response slides under a net force of 1 N.

This bead builds the acceptance rule that closes both holes: a column is accepted only when four tests hold,
each evaluated in the precision of the step that produced the anchor; a column that fails a mode test is
rerouted to the full solve and counted. What the mechanism claims is exactly this per-column bound and guard;
it claims no speed against a baseline that also factors once. Tier Frontier.

## Scope

In: the four tests; the rounding margin ρ; the slack and mode predicates per row kind; the attached bound and
its evidence class; reroute accounting. Out: the anchor (N18b-39); λ_lo (N18b-41); factor reuse, the verb and
the full-solve path (BD-367); the gate run (N18b-42).

## Technical approach

Notation: n is the row length of H (the number of unknowns), u the unit round-off of the working precision
(2⁻²⁴ in single precision, 2⁻⁵³ in double), γ = (n + 1)·u/(1 − (n + 1)·u). All quantities are in the scaled
variables of the stage-8 solve (DESIGN reading: the scaled system is the one in which a norm over mixed
metre and radian unknowns is dimensionless), and H denotes the scaled Hessian of the anchor.

**Test 1: explicit residual with its rounding bound.** Form r̂ = b − H·x̂ explicitly in the working precision
(a matrix–vector product and a subtraction; never an estimate carried out of the triangular solves). Together
with it form ρ, a bound on the rounding error of forming it:

  ρ = γ·‖ |H|·|x̂| + |b| ‖₂,

where |H| and |x̂| are entrywise absolute values. A residual formed in a wider format (for example the
double-float accumulation of the `stiff` precision profile) uses that format's u. The test passes when r̂ and
ρ are finite.

**Test 2: every sticking row remains strictly inside its cone.** From x̂ compute the change of each row
group's constraint-space velocity Δu_c = J_c·x̂ and of its impulse Δλ_c = −G_c·Δu_c (the frozen Hessian block
of the anchor). For each row labelled `stick`: slack_c = μ·(λₙ + Δλₙ) − ‖(λ_t1 + Δλ_t1, λ_t2 + Δλ_t2,
(λ_s + Δλ_s)/r_eff)‖ (the anisotropic form for elliptic cones). The test passes when slack_c > 0 for every
sticking row; the smallest slack is reported with the column. DESIGN completion for rows labelled `slip` (the
mechanism's guard covers "a stick, slip or open boundary"): the row stays slipping when the component of its
new sliding velocity along the anchored sliding direction stays above the law's stiction speed; otherwise the
column fails this test.

**Test 3: no open row closes and no active normal force changes sign.** For each `open` row: the predicted
end-of-step gap g_c + h·(uₙ,c + Δuₙ,c) stays positive. For each active unilateral row (contact normals, limits,
backlash): λₙ + Δλₙ > 0. Any violation fails the test.

**Test 4: the bound is attached.** ‖x̂ − x*‖₂ ≤ (‖r̂‖₂ + ρ)/λ_lo, with λ_lo a lower bound on the smallest
eigenvalue of H. Without ρ the expression is not a bound. The bound is stored with the column together with
‖r̂‖₂, ρ, λ_lo and the class of λ_lo: `Certified` when λ_lo comes from an exact certificate, `Estimated`
otherwise, and the bound inherits that class.

**Outcome per column.** `accepted` (all four hold): x̂ is returned with its bound, slack and class.
`rerouted` (test 2 or 3 failed): the column is sent to the full solve of the batched step with b applied as
an external impulse, the full solve's answer is returned with its own solve certificate and the label
`rerouted: <first failed predicate and row>`, and the reroute is counted. `failed` (test 1 or 4 not finite):
treated as a reroute and counted separately (DESIGN). The batch result reports the rerouted share overall and
per robot class.

**Worked values (hand-checkable; they are tests).**

- *Residual margin.* Single precision, H = 3, b = 1, n = 1: x̂ = fl(1/3) = 0.33333334; H·x̂ rounds to 1, so
  r̂ = 0; true error 9.93·10⁻⁹. γ = 2u/(1 − 2u) = 1.19·10⁻⁷; |H|·|x̂| + |b| = 2.0; ρ = 2.4·10⁻⁷; with λ_lo = 3
  the bound is 7.9·10⁻⁸, which covers the true error.
- *The bound at equality.* H = [[2, 1], [1, 2]] has eigenvalues 1 and 3, so λ_lo = 1. For b = (3, 3) the
  solution is (1, 1). The perturbed x̂ = (1.01, 0.99) has residual (−0.01, 0.01) of norm 0.01414, and its true
  error also has norm 0.01414: the error lies along the eigenvector of the smallest eigenvalue. A bound formed
  with λ = 3 would claim 0.00471 and is violated; that mutation must be caught.
- *The mode guard.* The block with μ·N = 5 N anchored in stick: a load of 4 N is accepted with slack 1 N
  (times h as an impulse); a load of 6 N has slack −1 N and is rerouted; the full solve returns sliding under a
  net 1 N.
- *The flop count.* At n = 96 unknowns and 64 columns, a full solve per column costs n³/3 + 2n² = 294,912 +
  18,432 = 313,344 flops, 20,054,016 in all. One factor and 64 solves cost 294,912 + 64 × 18,432 = 1,474,560.
  The ratio is 13.6 and reaches 10 from 23 columns. Against a baseline that also factors once it is 1.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four tests are implemented as stated and evaluated in the precision of the anchoring step; ρ uses
      γ = (n + 1)·u/(1 − (n + 1)·u) with the u of the format that formed the residual.
- [ ] The three fixtures reproduce (residual margin; bound at equality; mode guard) and the λ = 3 mutation is
      caught (first clause of GATE-TRAC-11).
- [ ] An accepted column carries its bound, the residual norm, ρ, λ_lo with its class, and the smallest
      sticking slack; a rerouted column carries the failed predicate and row, and the full solve's certificate.
- [ ] On 10⁴ battery contact states with 64 random columns each, solved in `f32` and compared with a full
      `f64` re-solve of every column: zero accepted columns whose true error exceeds the attached bound, and
      zero accepted columns whose re-solve has a different active set or stick label (measured by N18b-42).
- [ ] The rerouted share is reported per robot class.
- [ ] A bound formed without ρ, a bound with a non-lower-bound λ, and a guard that checks only normal sign are
      each caught by a registered negative control.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/response_acceptance.rs`.

- `residual_margin_f32`: the H = 3, b = 1 case: computed residual exactly 0; true error 9.93·10⁻⁹ (from exact
  rational arithmetic on the `f32` value); ρ = 2.38·10⁻⁷; bound 7.95·10⁻⁸ ≥ true error.
- `bound_at_equality`: the 2 × 2 case in `f64`: residual norm 0.014142, true error 0.014142, bound with
  λ_lo = 1 holds with equality up to ρ; with λ = 3 the claimed 0.004714 is below the true error and the test
  that checks "bound ≥ true error" fails, as required.
- `mode_guard_block`: 4 N accepted with slack 1 N·h; 6 N rerouted; the full solve's tangential acceleration is
  1 N divided by the block's mass.
- `open_row_closes`: a column that drives an open sample into the floor is rerouted.
- `normal_sign_change`: a column that lifts a supporting foot (λₙ + Δλₙ ≤ 0) is rerouted.
- `slip_reversal`: a column that reverses a sliding row's direction is rerouted.
- `gamma_values`: n = 1, u = 2⁻²⁴ → γ = 1.19·10⁻⁷; n = 96 → γ = 5.78·10⁻⁶.
- `flop_count`: 313,344; 20,054,016; 1,474,560; ratio 13.6; break-even for a ratio of 10 at 23 columns.
- `bound_property`: 10⁵ random symmetric positive definite systems in `f32` against exact rational solutions:
  the attached bound is never below the true error.

### End-to-end script and logging

Covered by `scripts/e2e/contact_response.sh` (bead N18b-42), stage `columns`: `frob contact response` on the
block fixture with loads of 4 N and 6 N, asserting `accepted` with slack and `rerouted` with the predicate;
NDJSON lines carry command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, per-column outcome, bound, residual
norm, ρ, λ_lo and its class, slack, and the reroute count.

### Known answers and negative controls

The four worked values above; the late known-answer row "residual bound in single precision" (H = 3, b = 1:
the bound without ρ claims zero error). Negative controls: the bound without ρ; the λ = 3 mutation; a guard
with test 2 removed (accepts the 6 N column). Metamorphic: scaling b by a positive factor scales x̂ and the
bound and leaves acceptance unchanged only while slacks stay positive (a factor of 1.5 on the 4 N column flips
it to rerouted). Determinism: outcomes identical across worker counts and across two runs.

## Considerations

- KILL-RESPONSE: guards reroute more than half the columns on battery states (first trigger, decided with the
  pilot), or a bound violation or a false mode acceptance that survives one repair cycle (second trigger,
  decided with GATE-TRAC-11). Fallback: full solves per query; this code path is then removed.
- The reading that the bound is stated in scaled variables is a DESIGN interpretation: a Euclidean norm over
  unknowns in metres per second and radians per second has no unit, and the scaled system is where one norm
  means one thing.
- The guards are sufficient conditions on the linearized response; they can reroute a column whose true
  response would have stayed in mode (a cost, counted) and must never accept one that leaves it (a defect,
  gated at zero).
