## Background

Gate GATE-TRAC-12 is a known-answer gate on the construction of swept bounds: a bound swept over a sub-interval
must enclose the path of the geometry, and for a rotating link the path is an arc, not the chord between its end
positions. An undersized bound discards a pair in the broad phase, and a pair discarded there never reaches the
narrow phase: a missed contact with no later chance of detection.

The gate has two milestones. Its M1 rows are measured in two beads, the containment row in N18b-43, which builds
the bounds, and the arc fixture on the referee in N18b-45, which records the M1 part. This bead measures and
records the M2 part: the same
fixtures on the **transactional batch profile** (the `strict`
profile of `batch-cpu`: the transactional, event-located step in `f64` on our own kernels), and the clause that
ties the construction to the running system: in the tunnelling audit (`contact.audit`) no missed contact is
attributed to a broad-phase rejection.

Why the second clause exists: the fixture rows show that the bound is right on designed cases; the audit clause
shows that on real battery scenarios the broad phase is never the reason a contact was missed. A miss may still
occur on a lockstep tier for other reasons (the speculative margin), and those are counted by another gate; a
miss caused by a rejected pair would mean the enclosure is wrong somewhere the fixtures did not look.

Full statement of the gate (TARGET): the arc fixture is detected on 1,000 of 1,000 seeded variants (orbital
radius 0.2 to 2 m, angle up to π, sample radius 1 to 10 cm, an obstacle on the arc and outside the padded
chord); the chord-only mutant fails the gate. On 10⁴ random two- and three-joint sweeps the path-length bound
contains 1,000 sampled path points per sweep with no exception. In the tunnelling audit no missed contact is
attributed to a broad-phase rejection. M1 on the referee, M2 on the transactional batch profile.

## Scope

In: the `strict` rows of the gate; the audit-attribution clause on the M2 battery as the gate entry
`GATE-TRAC-12/audit`; the record of the gate's M2 part ("on the transactional batch profile"). Out: the
construction (N18b-43); the referee rows and the M1 part's record (N18b-45, BD-147's register); the audit mechanism (N18b-23); the lockstep miss rate
(GATE-TRAC-03, BD-168).

## Technical approach

**Fixture rows on `strict`.** The fixtures of N18b-45 are tier-independent descriptions (scene, arm, obstacle,
sweep); the conformance target `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --
GATE-TRAC-12/strict` runs them through `sim.run --tier batch-cpu --profile strict`:

1. *Arc fixture*, 1,000 variants drawn by address with the same seeds as the referee row, so the two tiers see
   the same cases: the collision is detected (a contact event at the obstacle inside the sub-interval that
   encloses the crossing) in 1,000 of 1,000.
2. *Chord-only mutant*: the broad phase with zero dilation must fail to detect at least one variant (no contact
   event, or `FROB-REF-221` from check 6's audit pass, which publishes nothing); the row fails under the mutant,
   which is the registered negative control: N18-CX's late entry `trac-arc`, plant `N18b-43:trac-arc` (the build
   `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-contact/chord-only-swept-bound` of N18b-43, the feature as its control
   record writes it), which this bead runs on the batch profile, as that registry row states.
3. *Random sweeps*: 10⁴ two- and three-joint sweeps, 1,000 sampled path points each, all inside the
   path-length bound. This row is a host property of the bound and is re-run here against the batched kernel's
   box (the kernel's output, not the host function) so that a transcription error in the kernel is caught.

**Audit-attribution clause.** The gate entry `GATE-TRAC-12/audit` of `g1_known_answers` (DESIGN; a gate value is
recorded only from a Gauntlet tier receipt, N38b-19, never from verb envelopes) runs `contact.audit` with
`--detector strict` over the `standard` battery of N38a-20 (the M2 battery) on each lockstep tier and on the
`strict` profile itself, and on the free-plate fixture below, and collects misses by cause; its command-line form
per audited tier is `frob contact audit --battery standard --tier <t> --profile <p> --detector strict --key <k>
--json`. Pass condition: the count with cause
`broad-phase-rejection` is zero on every tier and scene class. The audit decides the cause by checking, for each
certified contact interval, whether the pair was a candidate of the audited tier's broad phase in that step;
this needs the audited run to record its candidate set for sampled windows (a telemetry channel enabled by the
audit request, off otherwise).

Note on tiers: the lockstep tiers do not use swept bounds over sub-intervals; their broad phase uses the
margin-inflated boxes at step endpoints. The clause therefore tests two things: on `strict`, the swept-bound
construction; on lockstep tiers, that the margin-inflated broad-phase box is never tighter than the narrow
phase's own margin (a pair inside the narrow-phase margin must always be a broad-phase candidate). That box
belongs to the dynamic pairs of `K-bp-sort` and `K-bp-pairs` (BD-158), which feed `K-np-prim`; a sample against
a scene thin primitive is found through the scene index by `K-np-thin` (BD-166) and never passes the dynamic
broad phase, so the lockstep half of the clause is exercised by dynamic pairs.

**Planted control for the clause (DESIGN).** The test-only build feature `bp-box-base-only` of `frob-contact`
(built by this bead behind `gauntlet-mutations`, registered in the control registry as this clause's control
with `feature = "bp-box-base-only"`, so its build is `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-contact/bp-box-base-only`,
BD-025; a
build, not a run option, since `sim.run` has none, as N18b-23 runs its controls) inflates the boxes of
`K-bp-sort` by m₀ + ε_g only, while every activation test keeps the full margin m₀ + ‖v_c‖·h + ε_g (N18a-12). The
fixture, registered by this bead as `assets/fixtures/free-plate/fixture.toml` (DESIGN name; `sim.run --fixture
free-plate`), is the thin-barrier geometry with the plate as a free body, so that the pair is dynamic: a sphere of
radius 2 mm at 5 m/s, h = 2 ms, toward a 2 mm plate whose near face is 3 mm ahead (it clears the plate within
the step, both endpoints clear). Under the flag the pair is never a candidate and the sphere passes: the audit
reports the crossing as a miss attributed to `broad-phase-rejection`; on the production build the box includes
the speed term (11 mm + ε_g > 3 mm), the pair is a candidate, the contact activates and there is no miss. A box
shrunk by only 1 mm below the narrow-phase margin would not serve: with the default m₀ = 1 mm it still holds
‖v_c‖·h + ε_g, at least the distance either body travels in the step, so the pair is a candidate in time and the
control could never fail.

**Recording.** The M2 part's record lists, per tier, the detected count, the mutant's undetected count, containment
exceptions, the audited world-steps and the misses by cause, with commit, fingerprint, seed and fixture
identity. It is recorded with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-12
--receipt <hash> --part M2` from one run of `FROB_TEST_SEED=<s> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-TRAC-12/strict GATE-TRAC-12/audit` in which both controls
(`trac-arc` and `bp-box-base-only`) were caught, by a session other than the implementer's; the recorder refuses
a pass whose control was not caught in the same run. The M1 part is N18b-45's record and is not touched here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Arc fixture on `strict`: 1,000 of 1,000 detected; the chord-only mutant fails (TARGET).
- [ ] Random sweeps: zero containment exceptions against the batched kernel's boxes.
- [ ] Audit over the M2 battery: zero misses attributed to `broad-phase-rejection` on every audited tier, with
      the audited world-step count printed.
- [ ] Planted control for the clause: under `bp-box-base-only` the free-plate fixture produces at least one miss
      attributed to `broad-phase-rejection`, and the production build none, so the clause is shown able to
      fail.
- [ ] The referee and `strict` rows used identical seeds and fixture identities (compared by digest).
- [ ] The M2 part of GATE-TRAC-12 recorded PASS with measured values in `registries/gates.toml`, through
      `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-12 --receipt <hash> --part M2`,
      from one `g1_known_answers` run of the entries `GATE-TRAC-12/strict` and `GATE-TRAC-12/audit` (the latter
      auditing the `standard` battery of N38a-20 on every audited tier, and the free-plate fixture) with both
      controls caught in that run.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/swept_bounds_strict.rs`.

- `arc_detected_strict`: the hand-checkable case (1 m arm, −60° to +60°, tip sphere 5 cm, obstacle at (1, 0) m)
  produces a contact event on `strict`.
- `kernel_box_contains_host_bound`: for 10⁴ random sweeps the box written by the broad-phase kernel contains the
  host capsule's box (outward rounding preserved in `f64`).
- `candidate_implies_margin`: for 10⁵ random sample poses, any pair whose endpoint gap is below the
  narrow-phase margin is present in the broad-phase candidate list.
- `candidate_set_telemetry`: the audit-enabled channel records the candidate set for sampled windows only and
  leaves state digests unchanged.
- `bp_box_base_only_control`: on the free-plate fixture the flag removes the pair from the candidate list at the
  step start while the full-margin activation test on the same gap (3 mm < 11 mm + ε_g) would activate it; the
  production box lists it.

### Known answers and negative controls

The arc counterexample values (padded chord reaches 0.55 m; sagitta bound 1.05 m; path-length dilation 0.589 m),
N18-KA's late row `trac-ka-arc`. Negative controls: the chord-only mutant (plant `N18b-43:trac-arc` of N18-CX's
late entry `trac-arc`, feature `chord-only-swept-bound`); the broad-phase box without its speed term (`bp-box-base-only`, built here). Metamorphic: the detection count is
invariant under a rigid motion of each variant. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1, 8, 24
(the broad-phase pair order is identical across worker counts); debug and release.

## Considerations

- If the audit finds a broad-phase rejection on a battery scenario, the finding is a defect in the enclosure or
  in the lever-arm compile products, not a tolerance to be relaxed; the gate stays open until the cause is
  removed.
- The clause's power depends on audited world-steps; the record states the count so that a zero is not read as
  more than it is.
- Under the no-GPU variant of M2 the lockstep audit rows cover `batch-cpu` only.
