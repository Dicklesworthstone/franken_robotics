## Background

FrankenRobotics refuses to run a contact solve in single precision (`f32`) when the problem is too stiff for it.
N18b-26 estimates, at step-plan admission, the scaled condition number of the contact Newton system times the
unit round-off of `f32` (κ_adm·u, with u = 2⁻²⁴ ≈ 6.0·10⁻⁸) and refuses with `FROB-REF-244` above a bound b_adm.
The plan leaves that bound open: OPEN-TRAC-4, "the admission bound on scaled κ·u for `f32` tiers", fail-closed
default 0.05, owner the Traction lead, decided by GATE-TRAC-10 (plan §18.18, §18.17). GATE-TRAC-10 requires the
`f32` Newton step to stay within 0.5% of the `f64` step at default cards and within 5% at near-rigid cards, and
cards over the bound to be refused.

The decision needs the measured error of the `f32` Newton step as a function of κ_adm·u. That step is the solve
of BD-156, and BD-156 depends on N18b-26 (its gate run includes N18b-26's admission fixture). N18b-26 therefore
cannot measure the curve it would decide from; it ships the fail-closed default as one registry constant. This bead
comes after both: it measures the curve, applies a rule fixed before the measurement, writes the decision record,
and sets the constant.

## Scope

In: the stiffness sweep and its error curve; the decision rule; the decision record `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-TRAC-4.md`;
the registry constant b_adm set to the decided value; the fixtures of N18b-26 and N18b-30 that name values
either side of the bound restated around it when it moves; the open-question registry's row marked decided.
Out: the estimate and the refusal (N18b-26); the solve and the recording of GATE-TRAC-10 (BD-156, the gate's one
recorder); the run-time conditioning figure κ̂ (BD-156).

## Technical approach

**Sweep (DESIGN).** For each reference robot of GATE-TRAC-10 and its default interface-card set, scale the card
stiffness by 2^k for k = 0, 1, … until the admission figure κ_adm·u of N18b-26 reaches 0.2, giving a grid of
figures from the default-card value upward (the near-rigid fixture of N18b-26 at 0.06 and the card at 0.04 are on
it). At each grid point draw 10⁴ contact states by address in the way GATE-TRAC-10 draws them, and solve one Newton
step of BD-156 twice on `batch-cpu`, in `f32` and in `f64`, from the same state. Record per state the relative
error ‖δ₃₂ − δ₆₄‖/‖δ₆₄‖ and per grid point the maximum and the median over the states, beside κ_adm·u for that
card set. The sweep is a test target of this bead,
`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-contact --release --test admission_bound_sweep`, which writes the table
to `target/open-trac-4/sweep.json` with the seed, the build identity, the rule hash and the hash of BD-156's
receipt of GATE-TRAC-10 that it was run beside.

**Decision rule (DESIGN, fixed before measuring; its text and constants are hashed into the sweep file).**
The bound is the largest grid value g such that, for every reference robot, the maximum relative step error over
every card set whose figure is at most g is at most 2.5% (half the 5% near-rigid target: the margin), and every
default card set is admitted (false-refusal rate zero). The missed-refusal rate is the share of swept card sets
admitted under g whose maximum step error exceeds 5%; it must be zero. If no grid value at or above the largest
default-card figure meets the rule, the bound stays at 0.05 only where 0.05 meets it and is otherwise the largest
value that does, and the record states that the default cards are not admitted with margin (a GATE-TRAC-10 finding
for BD-156's record, never hidden by the bound).

**Record.** `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-TRAC-4.md`: the error curve per robot (the table above), the decided bound, the
false-refusal and missed-refusal rates, the alternatives (a lower bound refusing more cards; the `stiff` precision
profile with one refinement step, which could move the usable bound upward), the receipt hashes, and what would
reopen it (a device family whose measured step error at the bound exceeds the target, or the `stiff` profile).
The constant b_adm in `registries/` takes the decided value in the same change; N41-OPEN2's row for OPEN-TRAC-4
is marked decided with the record's path.

**When the bound moves.** The fixtures that name values either side of it (N18b-26's admission fixture at 0.04 and
0.06 with `threshold_strict` at 0.0500 and 0.0501; N18b-30's near-rigid card) are restated as b_adm − 0.01,
b_adm + 0.01 and b_adm, b_adm + 0.0001 in the same change, and the keys of their beads are named in the commit
message; when it does not move, nothing else changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The sweep table exists for every reference robot of GATE-TRAC-10 with at least eight grid points from the
      default-card figure to 0.2, each with 10⁴ states, the maximum and median relative errors, and the hashes of
      the rule and of BD-156's GATE-TRAC-10 receipt.
- [ ] The decision rule's text and constants were committed before the sweep ran: the sweep file carries the rule
      hash, and a rule changed after the sweep makes `adr_rule_hash_matches` fail.
- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-TRAC-4.md` states the bound, the curve, the false-refusal rate (zero) and the missed-refusal
      rate (zero), the alternatives and what would reopen it.
- [ ] b_adm in the registry equals the ADR's bound (`adr_matches_registry`), and the estimate of N18b-26 reads it:
      its `threshold_strict` and admission fixture pass around the decided value.
- [ ] N41-OPEN2's row for OPEN-TRAC-4 is marked decided and names the record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/admission_bound_sweep.rs` and `crates/frob-contact/tests/open_trac_4.rs`.

- `decision_rule_synthetic`: synthetic curves with known crossings (error 2.5% reached at 0.08 on one robot and
  0.11 on another) → bound 0.08; a curve whose default cards already exceed 2.5% → the record's finding is set.
- `false_refusal_zero`: with the decided bound, every default card set of every reference robot is admitted.
- `missed_refusal_counted`: a synthetic card set admitted under the bound with a 6% step error is counted as a
  missed refusal and fails the rule.
- `adr_rule_hash_matches`: the ADR, the sweep file and the committed rule carry the same hash.
- `adr_matches_registry`: the bound in the ADR's front block equals b_adm in `registries/`.
- `sweep_deterministic`: the same seed gives the same table twice and at two worker counts.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/open_trac_4.sh` (helper of STD-E2E): runs the sweep target, then the decision tests above, then
`frob contact laws --design <fixture design with the near-rigid card> --tier gpu-cuda --profile lockstep --json`
(N18b-28's verb; the figure is computed on the host) and asserts that the printed bound equals the ADR's and that
the card's figure is compared with it; the GATE-TRAC-10 receipt is read, never written (BD-156 records the gate).
NDJSON lines carry `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time, per grid point the
robot, κ_adm·u, the maximum and median errors against 2.5% and 5%, the decided bound, the two rates, the rule and
receipt hashes, and on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths; a final `summary` line.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-422 (cards over the bound refuse with `FROB-REF-244`; the 0.5% and 5% step-error clauses); KA-423's κ = 4,801
(κ·u = 2.86·10⁻⁴ in `f32`) as the closed-form check of the figure on the sweep's first point. Negative controls: the
rule applied without its margin (bound at a 5% error) gives a different bound and fails
`decision_rule_synthetic`; an ADR bound that differs from the registry fails `adr_matches_registry`; a rule edited
after the sweep fails `adr_rule_hash_matches`.

## Considerations

- OPEN-TRAC-4 owner: the Traction lead, who signs the record.
- The bound is a property of `f32` arithmetic on this solve, so `batch-cpu` in `f32` is the reference; a device
  family is admitted to the bound by its own measurement, and one that exceeds the target reopens the decision.
- Raising the bound admits stiffer cards on `f32` tiers and lowers the share routed to `f64`; lowering it refuses
  more; either way the change is one constant, one record and the restated fixtures.
