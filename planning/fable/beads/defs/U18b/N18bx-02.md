## Background

The mission chapter has a grammar of experiments beside its grammar of bodies: a probe is "a small robot with a
task", compiled through the same power, frame, observation and contact interfaces as a production body, and
each proposal returns an executable simulated experiment, a bill of fixture parts and a measurement contract.
Its reference fixture is a gripper that holds its closure displacement on a compliant pad. The pad's normal
load relaxes with time, and the supported tangential load at the handover decides which pad to buy:

  R(r, t) = q + (1 − q)·exp(−t/(τ·f(r))),  r = a/a₀,  q ∈ [0.18, 0.22],  τ ∈ [0.9, 1.1] s,

with a compact pad A of contact radius a₀ and initial load 120 N, a broad pad B of radius 2·a₀ and initial load
100 N, Coulomb coefficient 0.5 and handover at T = 1 s. Four synthetic laws differ only in how relaxation
depends on contact size:

| Law | f(r) | Preferred pad at T (values at q = 0.20, τ = 1 s) |
|---|---|---|
| V | 1 (intrinsic relaxation) | Compact: 29.66 N against 24.72 N |
| P | r² (time grows with the square of contact size) | Broad: 41.15 N against 29.66 N |
| C | min(r, 1)² (capped drainage length) | Compact, as V for r ≥ 1 |
| D (withheld) | for r ≥ 1 the exponent is −[k·min(s, 1/4) + (1 + (1 − k)/3)·max(s − 1/4, 0)], s = t/τ, k = 1/r²; for r < 1 as P | Compact: follows P until s = 1/4, then reaches the V value at s = 1 |

At r = 1 all four histories coincide for every q, τ and t, so a recording at the compact radius cannot choose
the pad; a probe at r = 2 and 125 ms separates V and C from P and is fooled by D. The laws are artificial and
the loads stipulated; contact-size dependence of relaxation is physically motivated.

The probe compiler (N25c-17) must run such an experiment as a simulation. The interface card of the contact
chapter (BD-167) has a stiffness, a dissipation time and friction data; nothing in it makes a held load decay
over a second, and nothing depends on contact size. This bead adds that: an optional card group and the
contact-memory state that realizes it. Tier Frontier: it exists for the experiment grammar and is funded only
on the branch of KILL-AUGUR-PROBE that keeps the grammar (decision bead N25c-16); the plan-time pad pilot
(re-run by BD-043) is its paper-scale pilot. Rejected alternatives: scripting R(r, t) as a prescribed force in
the fixture (then the probe is not compiled through the contact interface, and a preload change or a re-grip
cannot be simulated); a full viscoelastic layer model (the non-goal on soft bodies; nothing in the plan needs
it).

## Scope

## Technical approach

**1. Model (DESIGN).** The pad's normal direction is a standard linear solid whose relaxation rate may depend
on contact size and on contact age. With normal approach δ(t) ≥ 0, instantaneous stiffness k₀ (the card's
normal stiffness), k∞ = q·k₀ and k₁ = (1 − q)·k₀:

  N = max(0, k∞·δ + N₁),  dN₁/dt = k₁·δ̇ − (λ(s, r)/τ)·N₁,

with N₁ the force in the relaxing branch, s = t_c/τ the contact age in units of τ (t_c is the time since the
contact was made), r = a/a_ref the ratio of the contact's patch radius to the card's reference radius, and
λ ≥ 0 a dimensionless rate that is piecewise constant in s. For a displacement applied at t_c = 0 and held,
N(t)/N(0⁺) = q + (1 − q)·exp(−∫₀^s λ dσ), which is R(r, t) with exponent E(s, r) = ∫₀^s λ dσ.

**2. The card group** (each field with `prov` and `unc`, as the container requires):

```json
"relaxation": {
  "q":     { "value": "0.20", "unc": {"kind": "interval", "lo": "0.18", "hi": "0.22"}, "prov": {…} },
  "tau":   { "value": "1.0 s", "unc": {"kind": "interval", "lo": "0.9 s", "hi": "1.1 s"}, "prov": {…} },
  "a_ref": { "value": "0.010 m", "unc": {"kind": "exact"}, "prov": {…} },
  "rate":  { "value": [ { "until_s": null, "law": "r^-2" } ], "unc": {"kind": "exact"}, "prov": {…} }
}
```

`rate` is an ordered list of segments in contact age; `until_s` is the segment's end in units of τ (`null` for
the last). `law` is one of a closed set of size expressions: `1`; `r^-2`; `min(r,1)^-2`; `affine-in-k` with
coefficients (c₀, c₁), meaning c₀ + c₁·k with k = 1/r²; or `table` (a monotone table of λ against r). An optional
second list `rate_below_ref` replaces `rate` for r < 1. The four fixture cards:

| Card | `rate` | `rate_below_ref` |
|---|---|---|
| V | [{`1`}] | absent |
| P | [{`r^-2`}] | absent |
| C | [{`min(r,1)^-2`}] | absent |
| D (`synthetic`, withheld) | [{until 1/4: `affine-in-k` (0, 1)}, {then: `affine-in-k` (4/3, −1/3)}], that is k and then 1 + (1 − k)/3 | [{`r^-2`}] |

**3. Update (exact for δ̇ constant over a sub-interval h and λ constant within a segment).**
N₁⁺ = e^{−λ·h/τ}·N₁ + k₁·δ̇·(τ/λ)·(1 − e^{−λ·h/τ}) for λ > 0, and N₁⁺ = N₁ + k₁·δ̇·h for λ = 0. If a segment
boundary s_b falls inside the sub-interval, the update is applied to the part before it and then to the part
after it. t_c advances by h; both N₁ and t_c live in contact memory and in the restore set of a transaction,
exactly as the film thickness does; they are discarded when the contact breaks (a re-grip starts at age zero).
The coefficient offered to the solve follows the rule for state-dependent contact data: computed from committed
state and held inside a solve.

**4. Patch radius.** a is the contact's effective patch radius: the card's `r_eff` if present, otherwise the
end effector's declared pad radius. A contact with neither cannot use the group, and the card is refused at
binding as incomplete (form error FROB-REF-103, pointer `relaxation.a_ref` or `r_eff`; repair: declare the pad
radius).

**5. Energy.** Stored: ½·k∞·δ² + N₁²/(2·k₁). Dissipated in the branch: (λ/τ)·N₁²/k₁ ≥ 0 per unit time, booked to
the energy ledger under the line `pad-relaxation`. With q ∈ (0, 1], τ > 0 and λ ≥ 0 the element is passive; a
card with q ≤ 0, q > 1 or a negative rate is refused as not passive (FROB-REF-130), returning the power created
at a unit state.

**6. Availability and refusals.** `--tier batch-cpu --profile strict` in `f64` only. A model bound to a card
with the group, requested on a lockstep profile, a device tier or the sibling-backed referee, is refused with
FROB-REF-246 (law not admitted on that tier or profile), repair: `--tier batch-cpu --profile strict`. No gate
for other tiers is planned; adding one is a dated amendment.

**7. Surfaces.** `frob contact laws --json` prints the group. `frob contact probe --card <id> --radius <a>
--hold <t> [--preload <N₀>] --json` returns R(r, t), N(t) and the supported tangential load μ·N(t), with the
interval over the card's q and τ uncertainty and the class `Estimated` (or lower when a field is `assumed`).

**8. Evidence.** Outputs are `Estimated`: the law is a declared model of a material, its fixture cards are
`synthetic`, and a probe verdict built on it is a conditional witness whose condition (the admitted mechanism
set, that is, the set of cards) travels in the certificate.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/relaxation.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-contact relaxation`; real `strict` step, no
mocks.
- `step_hold_matches_closed_form`: R = q + (1 − q)·e^{−t/τ} at 10 times; error below 10⁻¹² (the update is exact).
- `size_laws`: V, P, C at r ∈ {1/4, 1/2, 1, 3/2, 2, 3}: exponents t/τ, t/(τ·r²), t/(τ·min(r, 1)²).
- `law_d_piecewise`: r = 2 (k = 1/4): exponent 0.0625 at s = 1/4 and exactly 1 at s = 1; a sub-interval that
  straddles s = 1/4 is split.
- `pad_values`: 29.66, 24.72, 41.15 N (0.5 × 120 × 0.49430; 0.5 × 100 × 0.49430; 0.5 × 100 × 0.82304).
- `coincide_at_reference_radius`.
- `ramp_then_hold`: δ ramps over 50 ms then holds; N₁ from the exact update equals the analytic convolution.
- `unilateral_clamp`: retracting faster than the branch relaxes gives N = 0, never tension.
- `passivity_property`: random admissible cards and displacement histories; stored plus dissipated energy
  equals work in, to the ledger tolerance; dissipation never negative.
- `non_passive_cards_refused_130`; `tier_refusals_246`; `missing_radius_103`.
- `memory_in_restore_set`: a failed attempt of a transaction restores N₁ and t_c.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/traction_pad_relaxation.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/traction_pad_relaxation/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript): `frob --json contact laws` on the
four fixture cards; `frob --json contact probe` for the pad values and the probe readings; `frob --json sim run
--tier batch-cpu --profile strict` on the gripper-hold fixture for each law and pad (seed 0), then `frob --json
telemetry query` for the normal load at T and the ledger lines; the refused tiers; the invalid cards. Lines
carry `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim, class
`Estimated`, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` lines with loads in newtons and normalized
readings against the values above and their tolerances; `refusal` lines with code and first repair; on failure
the first differing value and the card and snapshot paths. `summary`: assertions passed and failed;
`gates: []` (GATE-AUGUR-11 is measured by the probe side).

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers (the pad-probe rows of the mission chapter's table): 29.66 N compact; 24.72 N and 41.15 N broad;
the reading intervals [0.8737, 0.9362] and [0.9520, 0.9982]; coincidence at r = 1. Negative controls: a size
law applied to τ as τ·r instead of τ·r² (P broad reads 0.5 × 100 × (0.2 + 0.8·e^{−1/2}) = 34.26 N, caught);
contact age not reset on re-grip; an explicit Euler update of N₁ at h = 0.5 s (R(1 s) = 0.2 + 0.8 × 0.25 =
0.40 instead of 0.4943, caught by the closed-form row); relaxation applied to k∞ as well (the load decays to
zero instead of q). Metamorphic: scaling a and a_ref together changes nothing; scaling the preload scales
every load and no normalized reading. Determinism: twice on one machine and debug against release, identical
digests under `strict`; across the two host platforms within the tier's class (the update calls `exp`).

## Considerations

- The model is the simplest that reproduces the fixture and lets a preload or a dwell change be simulated. It
  is not a claim about any real elastomer; a card for a real pad needs measured relaxation at two radii at
  least, and that is the bench lane's business.
- The withheld law D ships as a card so that the conditional-witness lesson is testable (a probe perfect in
  class reverses on it); it must never be in a default mechanism set.
- `exp` keeps the law off the exact kernel classes; that is one reason it is confined to the `strict` CPU
  profile.
- If the experiment grammar is not funded, the expert grid and the direct measurement remain and need no
  simulated pad; nothing else depends on this group.
