## Background

A design can be kinematically valid and still hit itself. A leg whose abduction range lets one thigh sweep through
the other, a wheel that intersects the chassis at its mount, a knee that reaches the ground before the foot: each
wastes every simulation and training run launched on that design, and each is decidable from geometry and joint
ranges alone, in milliseconds, before anything is simulated. Analysis A-13 answers the question "do limbs sweep
through each other or the ground over their ranges?". It is one of the static analyses of the studio (subsystem
Atelier, crate `frob-analyze`, tier Solid), it runs inside `frob design check` at preflight stage 3 ("Mechanism":
Hervé class of each screw basis; loop assembly; mobility; singularity at the reference pose; self-collision and
ground clearance over the declared ranges), and it serves the bet that static analysis prunes bad designs before
simulation without throwing away a winner (its main value is feedback in seconds).

The mathematics named for it is swept charts. FACT: FrankenSim's `fs-motion` crate provides swept charts and sits
behind the airlock's `referee` feature group. A second fact shapes the method: a link that rotates does not move
along the chord between its end positions, so a sweep bound built from end poses alone misses mid-range
interference (a 1 m arm turning from −60° to +60° has its tip pass through (1, 0) m while the chord between its end
positions lies on x = 0.5 m; the bound must add the sagitta R·(1 − cos(Δθ/2))).

Alternatives rejected: sampling joint space on a grid (misses thin interference regions between samples and gives
no bound); testing only the reference pose (finds the planted wheel, misses every range problem).

## Scope

In: the pair set and its classification, the sweep over joint-range boxes with one-sided enclosures, ground
clearance, witnesses, the refusal with repairs as typed edits, the per-body swept charts as a reusable artifact,
memo keys and reuse rules, the verb, the budget behaviour. Out: collision primitives and exact pair tests
(`frob-contact`, BD-079); fitting primitives to link Regions (the model compiler, BD-062); the preflight driver and
stage timing (BD-110); the memo graph (BD-111); clearance of the whole body through a scene, which is analysis
A-18 on clearance complexes.

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** The compiled `RobotModel`: kinematic tree, joint types and declared ranges, collision primitives
   per link (spheres, capsules, boxes, cylinders, rounded boxes) with their recorded fitting error ε_fit, the
   reference pose, the declared ground-contact links (feet, wheels) and the nominal stance height.
2. **Pair set.** All unordered pairs of links, with adjacent pairs (parent and child) handled by trimming each
   primitive near the shared joint by its own radius, so that the unavoidable overlap at the joint is not
   reported. Pairs equivalent under the design's mechanical symmetry group are analysed once and the result
   copied with the group element recorded.
3. **Relative motion.** The relative pose of two links depends only on the joints on the tree path between them.
   For a pair with k such joints the search space is the box of their k declared ranges.
4. **Three-valued sweep (branch and bound).** For a sub-box of joint intervals:
   a. Outer test: bound each primitive's swept set over the sub-box. For a sample of radius r with end positions
      A and B, the path lies inside the segment AB dilated by ½·√(L² − ‖B − A‖²) plus r, where L is an upper
      bound on path length: the sum over the moving joints of interval width times the largest lever arm of the
      sample about that joint (for one revolute joint the tighter sagitta bound applies). Inflate by ε_fit. If
      the two outer sets are disjoint, the sub-box is `clear` with a lower bound on clearance.
   b. Inner test: at the sub-box centre, call the exact primitive-pair distance. If it is below −ε_fit·2 (the
      primitives interpenetrate even after allowing for fitting error on both), the configuration is a witness of
      interference.
   c. Otherwise bisect the interval with the largest lever-arm-weighted width and recurse, down to a resolution
      floor (DESIGN default: 0.5° for revolute, 1 mm for prismatic) or until the stage budget is spent.
   d. Result per pair: `always-clear { min_clearance ≥ c }`, `interferes { witness: joint values, depth }`, or
      `unresolved { clearance in [lo, hi] }` when the remaining sub-boxes are within the fitting error.
   Comparisons that decide `clear` use outward rounding.
5. **Reference pose.** The exact pair test at the reference pose runs first; interpenetration there is reported
   immediately (the planted-wheel case).
6. **Ground clearance.** With the base at its nominal stance height over a flat ground and joints over their
   declared ranges: (a) at the reference pose no primitive of a non-contact link may be below the ground plane;
   (b) over the ranges, the analysis reports the minimum over configurations of (lowest point of non-contact
   links − lowest point of contact links); a negative value means a knee, belly or housing reaches the ground
   before any foot or wheel, reported with the witness configuration. The same sweep bound is used with the
   ground plane as the second body.
7. **Refusal.** `FROB-REF-123` "Self-collision or interpenetration at the reference pose or over declared ranges".
   Exit 3 from `design check`. Ranked repairs as typed edits, which become the envelope's `next_actions`: narrow
   the limiting joint range to exclude the witness (`set-limits`, with the computed limit), move the mount,
   change the link's Region dimensions. A pair that is `unresolved` is a finding, not a refusal.
8. **Outputs.** Per pair: classification, clearance bound, witness; the list of `always-clear` pairs with their
   margins, which seeds the precomputed self-collision filter list of the contact broad phase (excluded pairs
   stay inspectable); ground-clearance profile; and **swept charts per body**: for each link, the volume it
   sweeps over its joint ranges expressed in each neighbour's frame, stored as an artifact by digest. The
   structure loop later subtracts these swept volumes from a link's design region.
9. **Memo and reuse.** The analysis is a memo node keyed by the model identity restricted to the fields it reads
   (tree, ranges, collision primitives, reference pose), its version and parameters. Results are stored per limb
   (pairs inside one limb) and per limb pair. After a leaf-local module substitution, self-collision inside
   sibling limbs is reused and pairs between the new limb and the others are recomputed; a `set-limits` edit
   invalidates the pairs whose path contains the joint.
10. **Verb and envelope.** `analyze.<name>` for A-13 (read, M1; the registry owns the name), and inside
    `design.check`. Evidence: class `Tested`, tier `static`, the model identity, determinism reproducible on the
    CPU (interval-style arithmetic with outward rounding; no device). A patch may require it:
    `"constraints": { "require": ["A-13"] }`.
11. **Budget.** Stage 3 has a 95th-percentile budget of 400 ms (TARGET) shared with mobility and the
    reference-pose singularity check. On exhaustion A-13 returns `undecided` with the pairs established so far;
    `undecided` is never a pass and never a refusal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A planted limb interference is found: on the quadruped template with the hip adduction range widened, A-13
      returns `interferes` for the two thighs with a witness configuration, and `design check` refuses with
      `FROB-REF-123` and a `set-limits` repair (the seed's done-condition).
- [ ] The rejection fixture "drive wheel that intersects the body at its nominal mount pose" is refused at
      stage 3 with `FROB-REF-123`.
- [ ] No false clear: on 10³ random two- and three-joint mechanisms, every pair reported `always-clear` has
      positive clearance at 10⁴ sampled configurations.
- [ ] The mid-range interference fixture (clear at both range ends, colliding in between) is found; the
      chord-only mutant misses it.
- [ ] Ground clearance at the reference pose and over ranges is reported with witnesses.
- [ ] Swept charts per body are emitted and reproduce: a point sampled on the link at a random admissible
      configuration lies inside its chart (10⁵ samples).
- [ ] Reuse rules hold: after a leg-module substitution only pairs involving the new leg are recomputed.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob --json design check` and its own `analyze` verb; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-analyze`
      documents it; stage-3 timing is recorded per archetype toward GATE-ATEL-02.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `thigh_pair_closed_form` (`synthetic`, derived here and to be re-derived before it is pinned): two thigh
  capsules of radius r = 0.04 m and length ℓ = 0.25 m hinged at hips w = 0.20 m apart, each adducting toward the
  other by α_L and α_R. The distal ends meet when w − ℓ·(sin α_L + sin α_R) ≤ 2r, that is
  sin α_L + sin α_R ≥ 0.48. With both ranges limited to 13° (sum of sines 0.450) the pair is `always-clear` with
  clearance 0.20 − 0.25·0.450 − 0.08 = 7.5 mm; with ranges to 20° (sum 0.684) it `interferes`, and the repair
  limit for equal ranges is asin(0.24) = 13.9°.
- `planted_wheel_at_mount` (KA-281): refusal 123 at the reference pose.
- `mid_range_only_collision`: a 1 m arm sweeping −60° to +60° about the origin with a tip sphere of 0.05 m and a
  second link's sphere at (1, 0) m: both ends clear, interference found near 0°.
- `chord_only_mutant` (negative control): the sweep bound without the sagitta (chord padded by radius reaches
  x = 0.55 m) reports clear and fails the previous test.
- `adjacent_pair_trim`: a parent and child sharing a joint are not reported at the joint.
- `fit_error_respected`: a pair whose nominal clearance is below 2·ε_fit is `unresolved`, not `always-clear`.
- `ground_reference_pose`: a housing placed 5 mm below the ground plane at the reference pose is refused.
- `knee_before_foot`: a leg whose knee can drop below the foot within its range is reported with the witness.
- `symmetry_copy`: mirrored pairs under the design's symmetry group are computed once.
- `budget_returns_undecided`: with a 1 ms budget on the humanoid template the result is `undecided` with partial
  pairs.
- Property test `clear_is_sound`: sampled configurations never contradict an `always-clear`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/analyze_self_collision.sh`: `frob --json design check assets/archetypes/quad-mammal.fbot` (exit 0 or
2); `frob --json design edit ... set-limits` to widen the adduction range; `frob --json design check` again
(exit 3, code 123); apply the first repair from `next_actions`; `frob --json design check` (exit 0 or 2); then the
planted-wheel fixture. Logs each command with exit code and wall time, the `evidence` block (class, tier static,
model identity), the witness joint values, clearance bounds in metres, stage-3 time against 400 ms, the repair
applied, and a `summary` naming GATE-ATEL-02.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-281 (planted wheel refused at stage 3, `FROB-REF-123`; the fixture is the planted failure, and a check that
lets it through is broken). The thigh closed form (13.9°, 7.5 mm). The sagitta case of the arc sweep. Negative
control: the chord-only mutant. Metamorphic relations: narrowing a joint range never turns `always-clear` into
`interferes`; scaling the whole design uniformly leaves classifications unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on
one machine; debug and release; across platforms (CPU only).

## Considerations

- Primitives are fitted approximations of link Regions; a verdict is relative to them and their recorded fitting
  error. Thin housings poorly covered by primitives are a known gap, stated in `CONTRACT.md`.
- The humanoid template (29 actuated coordinates, self-collision over large ranges) is the stress case for the
  400 ms budget; pair pruning by bounding spheres and symmetry is what keeps it inside.
- A static clear is not a dynamic guarantee: compliance and joint-limit overshoot can produce contact the
  analysis excludes; the failure taxonomy has a `self-collision` class for that.
- Interim posture if `fs-motion` is not admitted through the airlock in time: the sweep bounds above are
  implemented natively from the stated formulas (they need only primitive geometry), and `fs-motion` is used as a
  parity oracle on small fixtures once admitted.
- This seed bead was routed to the scene chapter by keyword ("clearance"); its content belongs to the studio's
  analyses and its parent is the static-analysis work package.
