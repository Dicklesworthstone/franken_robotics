## Background

A scene can look plausible and be unable to support a robot: a floor that exists only for the renderer, a mirrored
room, a door 3 cm narrower than its label, a glass wall with no optical card. Every result computed in such a scene
is wrong in a way no later check will find (risk RISK-41: a scene looks plausible and cannot support a robot;
mitigation: scene acceptance, feasibility tags, clearance complexes). The rule is that a compiled scene is not
usable until its acceptance report is complete. Acceptance is a set of mechanical checks with known outcomes; none
is a matter of taste. The report is part of the pack and its hash is in the manifest.

This bead is the verb `scene.accept`, the report schema, the runner that executes the checks (compiler stage 10),
the rule that makes an unaccepted pack unusable, and the gate GATE-HAB-04. The individual checks are built by their
own beads and assembled here.

## Scope

In: the verb, the runner, the report schema with its identity and measurement parts, the completeness rule and its
enforcement at load, waivers, the gate measurement on 100 scenes per archetype. Out: the checks themselves
(registration N19a-21, enclosures N19a-22, deviation N19a-23, ray fixtures N19a-24, gauge and dynamic fixtures
N19a-25, leak fixture N19a-09); optics and friction separation as a gate (BD-229, GATE-HAB-05); the feasibility tag
(BD-234); the end-to-end script (N19a-43).

## Technical approach

1. **Verb.** `frob scene accept <scene> --key <k> [--only <checks>] [--tiers referee,batch-cpu,cuda,metal]
   [--waive region=<id> --reason <text>] [--sync-wait-ms N]`, and the preview form `frob scene accept --source
   <path> --preview [--ray <ox,oy,oz>/<dx,dy,dz>]… --key <k>` (item 6). Kind `run`, so every call carries an
   idempotency key and two calls a test compares carry distinct keys; capability `scene:run`; first milestone M3;
   result schema `frob.scene.accept.out/1` (the registry's name; an earlier draft wrote `frob.scene.accept/1`). It
   may run long (dynamic fixtures on large scenes), so it accepts the bounded synchronous wait and otherwise
   returns a job handle (exit 8). Exit 0 when complete with nothing listed, 2 when complete with findings or
   waivers, 3 on a refusal. `--only` takes names from one closed list (DESIGN; one name per section or fixture):
   `registration` (the four-point registration of N19a-21), `enclosure-selftest`, `deviation`, `maps`,
   `feasibility`, `board`, `gauges`, `normals`, `support`, `silhouette` (the ray and visual registration fixture
   of N19a-24, a different check from `registration`), `ripple`, `continuity`, `leak`; an unknown name fails the
   input schema (`FROB-REF-802`, exit 3). Waivers are given here and only here: the deviation refusal
   `FROB-REF-317` is raised by this verb (the registry lists 312 and 317 on `scene.accept`), never by
   `scene.compile`, whose stage 10 lists an over-threshold region as a finding (exit 2) and leaves the waiver to
   acceptance.
2. **What the report contains.**

| Section | Source | Verdict it carries |
|---|---|---|
| Compile receipts, stages 1 to 10 | The compiler | Counts, findings, refusals met and repaired |
| Registration | Four-point registration | Class established, residuals, u_reg; or `not-applicable` for generated scenes |
| Enclosure self-test | The 6 mm doorway case run with the pack's own bounds | `unknown`, as it must be |
| Collision-to-visual deviation | Per region, bounded and sampled | Listed regions, waivers |
| Maps | Every region has an interface card and an optical card; latents and their conditionals | Structural completeness |
| Feasibility | The tag for the nominal footprint, with the cut attached when `impossible` | `witnessed`, `impossible`, `unresolved` |
| Fixtures | The eight fixtures below | Pass or fail with measured values |

3. **The eight fixtures.**

| Fixture | What it checks | Passing outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Metric calibration board | A board of known dimensions placed in the scene is measured by ray kernels | Measured lengths equal the known lengths within the brick bound |
| Door-clearance gauges | For each door of clear width w and field bound b, a go gauge of width w − 2b and a no-go gauge of width w + 2b are swept through | The go gauge passes and the no-go gauge is blocked |
| Normal-direction probe | ∇φ at probes on each tagged surface | Normals point out of solids; floor normals are +z within tolerance |
| Support test | A unit block is released above each support surface on the referee | It comes to rest on the surface; a floor that exists only in the renderer fails here |
| Ray and visual registration | Identifier buffers drawn from the field and from the GLB at fixed viewpoints | Silhouette overlap at or above the pack threshold |
| Quantization ripple | A 10 kg block at rest on a brick floor with no overlay | Normal-force ripple at most 1% of weight (TARGET) |
| Tile continuity | Rays and swept wheels crossing brick and tile boundaries | No gap or step at a boundary |
| Mutable-state leak | Wet a tile in world i, reset, and read world j and the next episode of world i | No trace outside the declared persistence |

4. **Two parts (DESIGN).** The identity part holds verdicts and bounds computed on the CPU reference path in
   reproducible arithmetic; its digest is the manifest's `acceptance` field and therefore part of the `SceneId`.
   The measurement part holds per-tier, per-device measured values (ripple, overlaps, timings), stored beside the
   pack and keyed by `SceneId` and hardware fingerprint. A report is **complete** when every section is present,
   every fixture has a passing verdict or a recorded waiver where a waiver is allowed (only deviation may be
   waived), and the measurement part exists for each tier on which the pack is about to be used.
   **Compile and accept.** Compiler stage 10 runs the identity checks and writes the identity part, so its digest
   is fixed when the pack is frozen. `scene.accept` on a frozen pack recomputes the identity part on the CPU
   reference path and compares its digest with the manifest's `acceptance` field (a difference is the integrity
   fault `scene-pack-identity-mismatch`, 9xx, exit 9, N35x-02: the pack is corrupt, never re-labelled), then
   measures and stores the measurement part for the requested tiers. A pack compiled before stage 10 existed has
   no identity part; its report is incomplete and the repair is to recompile (a pack is immutable, so acceptance
   never writes an identity into it). **Visual sections.** The sections that read the GLB (collision-to-visual
   deviation with its waivers, and the silhouette overlap of the ray and visual registration fixture) sit outside
   the identity, as the GLB does (ADR-HAB-04): they are stored with the measurement part, keyed by `SceneId` and
   the GLB digest. Swapping the GLB leaves the `SceneId` unchanged and makes the report incomplete until the pack
   is accepted again against the new GLB (N19a-23's GLB-swap test is the check).
5. **Unusable until complete.** Verbs that run a simulation, a training job or a study on a pack (`sim.run`,
   `eval.run`, `train.start`, `opt.start`, `study.submit`) check the report first, through the run-admission
   precondition table of N32ax-01. This bead declares its row there: `[[admission_check]] id =
   "habitat.scene-accepted"`, owner `frob-scene`, verbs the five above, formats the scene pack, refusals
   `["FROB-REF-318"]`, no override, `basis = ["scene_acceptance"]` (the standing index the check reads, in
   N32ax-01's sense: the stored acceptance parts keyed by `SceneId`, hardware fingerprint and GLB digest;
   `scene.accept` increments its version in `admission_basis` in the transaction that stores a part, so a report
   completed between stage A and stage E reruns stage A), first milestone M3; it runs in stage A, before any
   budget is charged. The check reads the report only through the pack reader's acceptance-report accessor
   (`ScenePack::acceptance_report`, the accessor `scene.accept` also uses), never from a side file, so what the
   reader refuses comes first and 318 is raised only on a pack the reader opens: a stored pack that cannot be
   decoded as written is `FROB-REF-303`, a recomputed identity that differs is `scene-pack-identity-mismatch`
   (exit 9), and a scan pack imported without its scan profile (`provenance.scan_profile = absent`, NEG-23, whose
   reader refuses that accessor with the geometry accessors) is `scan-profile-absent`, whose repair (re-import
   with the scan profile) is the one that works, where "run `scene.accept`" would meet the same refusal; for the
   same reason `scene.accept` on such a pack answers `scan-profile-absent`. A pack with an incomplete or failed
   report is refused with `FROB-REF-318` "The scene pack has not
   passed acceptance" (the row entered by the decision bead N19c-42, constant `PACK_NOT_ACCEPTED`; exit 3; fields
   `scene_id`, `missing_sections[]`, `failed_fixtures[]`; ranked repairs: 1. run `scene.accept` on the pack and
   read the sections it names; 2. recompile with the failing input corrected, the report naming the stage and the
   fixture; 3. for a diagnostic run, use a pack whose report records the waiver or the failed fixture explicitly,
   whose results are labelled diagnostic). A missing measurement part for the tier in use counts as a missing
   section. The check cannot be skipped by a flag.
   The feasibility section is present when stage 9 has written the tag (BD-234, a later seed that this runner
   reads through the manifest); until then the section reads `not-run` and the report is incomplete.
   **Scripts written before the refusal existed.** The e2e scripts of M0 to M2 (and the M3 scripts that landed
   before this bead) run `sim.run` and the other run verbs on compiled packs that nobody accepted, because the
   verb did not exist; switching the refusal on would turn every one of them into a 318 failure. In the same
   change this bead adds to the STD-E2E helper the function `e2e_scene_ready <scene_id> [--tiers …]`, which
   calls `frob --json scene accept <id> --key e2e-ready-<run_id>-<scene_id>` when `frob capabilities` lists
   `scene.accept` (one key per script run and pack, so a repeated call in one run is the same idempotent request;
   it logs the report digest and `complete`) and is a logged no-op before; it converts every existing script that
   binds a compiled pack in a run verb (found by a search of `scripts/e2e/` for the five run verbs with `--scene`
   or a pack argument) to call it before the run, and runs those scripts after the switch. So that scripts
   written later keep the rule, the same change adds the STD-E2E linter rule `scene-ready-before-run`: a script
   whose log has a `cmd` line running one of the five run verbs on a compiled pack with no earlier
   `e2e_scene_ready` line for that pack fails the lint. The refusal itself stays unskippable; a script gets past
   it only by accepting the pack.
6. **Runner.** Executes checks in dependency order, each cancellable; services it needs from higher layers (ray
   kernels, the referee, batched tiers) are supplied by the verb executor through traits `frob-scene` defines.
   `--only` runs a subset and yields a partial report that is marked incomplete.
   **Preview on uncompiled geometry.** `--source <path> --preview` loads a scene source without compiling it and
   runs the checks that need no pack: the four-point registration (a reflected or non-rigid source is refused
   with `FROB-REF-302`, exit 3, exactly as compile stage 2 refuses it) and, for each `--ray`, the first hit
   through the instanced host ray path `frob_sense::host_rays::cast_instanced` of N19c-39 (hit parameter in world
   metres under scaled instances). A preview report is never complete, is never stored beside a pack and binds
   no run; it is how a check on a source that does not compile (the reflected room) or on bare geometry (the
   two-plane occlusion fixture of N19c-40) is run through the verb.
7. **Evidence.** The envelope's `evidence` names the claim kind Known answer for the fixtures, class `Tested`
   (class `Certified` only for verdicts that carry an offline-checkable certificate), the tiers used, the
   determinism class and the scene identity. `reality` is `no-claim`.
8. **Gate GATE-HAB-04** (Known answer, M3; TARGET thresholds): every fixture passes on 100 scenes per archetype;
   the reflected room is rejected and the three-point control accepts it; the 6 mm case returns `unknown`. The
   gate receipt records, per archetype, the count of scenes, the count passing each fixture, the worst measured
   value per fixture, the commit, the hardware fingerprint and the seeds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob --json scene accept` returns a report with all seven sections and eight fixture verdicts on the
      archetype fixture scenes; the report validates against its schema.
- [ ] The gate machinery is in place and passes on the scenes available to this bead: the runner writes the
      GATE-HAB-04 receipt (per archetype: scene count, passes per fixture, worst measured value per fixture against
      its threshold, commit, hardware fingerprint, seeds); on ten generated warehouse and ten generated kitchen
      scenes (BD-225) every fixture passes; the reflected room is rejected (`FROB-REF-302`) with the three-point
      control accepting it; the 6 mm doorway returns `unknown`. The gate PASS over 100 scenes per archetype on all
      seven archetypes, with the feasibility section filled, is recorded by N19a-43, which depends on this bead
      and on the later generator and tag seeds, and closes this seed's done-condition.
- [ ] Each planted defect is caught by its fixture: mirrored room, narrowed door, renderer-only floor, inverted
      solid, shifted GLB, planted seam, shared mutable pool, untruncated quantization.
- [ ] A pack with an incomplete report is refused by `sim.run` with a pointer to `scene.accept`; after acceptance
      it runs.
- [ ] Switching the refusal on breaks no earlier script: `e2e_scene_ready` is in the STD-E2E helper, every
      existing script under `scripts/e2e/` that binds a compiled pack in a run verb calls it before the run
      (a search lists none that does not), and those scripts, rerun after the switch, report no `FROB-REF-318`.
- [ ] The identity part is byte-identical on Linux and macOS for the same scene; the measurement part differs
      only in measured values and timings.
- [ ] Cancellation mid-run leaves no partial report marked complete and no device resources.
- [ ] The accept steps of the fixture scripts of N19a-09, N19a-21, N19a-22, N19a-23, N19a-24 and N19a-25, logged
      as skips until this verb exists, run and pass from `scripts/e2e/scene_accept.sh`.
- [ ] The run-admission row `habitat.scene-accepted` is in `registries/verbs.toml` with the fields above and
      passes N32ax-01's table check; the STD-E2E linter rule `scene-ready-before-run` fails a planted script that
      runs a pack without `e2e_scene_ready`.
- [ ] The preview refuses the reflected-room source with `FROB-REF-302` and reports the two-plane first hit at
      2.000 m; a preview report is never `complete` and admits no run.
- [ ] Acceptance of a frozen pack whose identity part was altered on disk ends in `scene-pack-identity-mismatch`
      (exit 9); swapping the GLB leaves the `SceneId` unchanged and the report incomplete until re-accepted.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` documents the verb's inputs, outputs, refusals, determinism class and evidence class.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `report_sections_complete`: schema test over a generated report.
- `partial_report_is_incomplete`: `--only board` yields `complete: false`.
- `incomplete_pack_refused_by_run`: `sim.run` on an unaccepted pack exits 3 with `FROB-REF-318` and
  `missing_sections` listing what is absent; a pack whose only gap is the measurement part of the tier in use is
  refused the same way; after `scene.accept` completes the report the same run is admitted.
- `no_skip_flag`: enumerates the flags of the five run verbs in the verb registry and runs each with an
  unaccepted pack; every combination is refused with 318 (negative control: a planted `--skip-acceptance` flag
  that admits the pack fails this test).
- `only_deviation_is_waivable`: a waiver on the support test is rejected.
- `identity_part_excludes_measurements`: two measurement parts, one identity digest.
- `planted_defect_matrix`: a table of eight planted defects against the fixture expected to fail; each fails
  exactly its fixture (and no other fixture masks it).
- `always_pass_runner_mutant` (negative control): a runner that returns pass for every fixture fails the planted
  defect matrix on all eight rows.
- `generated_scene_registration_not_applicable`.
- `scene_ready_helper`: before `scene.accept` is registered the helper logs a no-op and exits 0; after, it accepts
  the pack (its `cmd` line carries `--key`) and the following run is admitted; a planted script that runs a
  compiled pack without calling it is listed by the search and fails the `scene-ready-before-run` lint.
- `only_names_closed`: every `--only` name maps to exactly one section or fixture; `registration` and
  `silhouette` select different checks; an unknown name exits 3 with `FROB-REF-802`.
- `accept_recomputes_identity`: a pack whose stored identity part was altered fails with the identity-mismatch
  fault, exit 9; an untouched pack reproduces its digest.
- `glb_sections_outside_identity`: swapping the GLB changes neither `SceneId` nor the identity digest and marks
  the deviation and silhouette sections missing until re-acceptance.
- `preview_reflected_and_two_planes`: the preview of the reflected-room source refuses with 302; the preview of
  `fixtures/scenes/two-planes.src` returns the foreground plane at 2.000 m; the preview report has
  `complete: false` and a run naming it is refused.
- `admission_row_declared`: the `habitat.scene-accepted` row loads, names the five run verbs and 318, has no
  override and has `basis = ["scene_acceptance"]`.
- `reader_refusal_precedes_318`: `sim.run` on an unaccepted pack whose stored blob was altered is refused with
  `scene-pack-identity-mismatch` (exit 9) and not with 318; a report stored between stage A and stage E (a
  `scene.accept` completing in the window) makes the admission rerun stage A and admit (the `admission_basis`
  version of `scene_acceptance` changed).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/scene_accept.sh` (sources the `STD-E2E` helper): generates ten warehouse and ten kitchen scenes with
`frob --json scene gen … --seed <s> --key acc-gen-<family>-<s>` (seeds fixed in the script), runs `frob --json
scene accept <id> --key acc-<id>` on each (exit 0, seven sections, eight fixture verdicts, `complete: true`);
`--only board --key acc-only-<id>` on one (`complete: false`); the reflected room (N19a-21's fixture
`fixtures/imports/asym-room-mirrored.glb` with its four-landmark sidecar), which `frob --json scene compile
fixtures/imports/asym-room-mirrored.glb --key acc-cmp-refl` refuses at stage 2 (exit 3, `FROB-REF-302`, no pack),
and the same source through `frob --json scene accept --source fixtures/imports/asym-room-mirrored.glb --preview
--key acc-prev-refl` (exit 3, `FROB-REF-302`); the two-plane preview (`--source fixtures/scenes/two-planes.src --preview
--ray <o>/<d> --key acc-prev-2p` with the fixture's ray from `fixtures/rays/scaled_instances.json`: first hit
the foreground plane at 2.000 m, not the background at 3 m; `complete: false`); the 6 mm doorway (`unknown`); `frob
--json sim run <probe> --scene <unaccepted pack> --tier referee --key acc-run-before` (exit 3, `FROB-REF-318`,
`missing_sections` listed, the repair naming `scene.accept`), then the same run after acceptance with key
`acc-run-after` (exit 0); an accept with `--sync-wait-ms 0` then `frob --json job cancel` (no report marked
complete); then the fixture scripts whose accept steps waited for this verb: `scene_state_leak.sh` (N19a-09),
`scene_registration.sh` (N19a-21), `scene_enclosures.sh` (N19a-22), `scene_visual_deviation.sh` (N19a-23),
`scene_accept_ray_fixtures.sh` (N19a-24) and `scene_accept_gauge_fixtures.sh` (N19a-25), asserting each exits 0
with no `e2e_skip "needs BD-228"` line; then the search for run verbs on
compiled packs in `scripts/e2e/` (every hit calls `e2e_scene_ready` first) and a rerun of the scripts it lists,
none refused with `FROB-REF-318`. Every line carries the charter fields
(`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence`
block); per scene each fixture's measured value against its threshold; the refusal blocks; the identity-part
digest; on failure the first failing fixture, the scene's seed and the pack path. Final `summary` with counts per
fixture, naming GATE-HAB-04 (machinery). The gate run over 100 scenes per archetype is N19a-43's
(`scripts/e2e/scene_accept_gate.sh`).

## Considerations

- Cost: 700 scenes with dynamic fixtures is the gate's main expense; the support lattice is thinned on large
  scenes with the count recorded.
- Real-place imports will carry waivers; every report that cites such a scene shows them.
- Tolerances still to set (normal direction, continuity step, silhouette threshold, the deviation refusal
  threshold) are compile settings recorded in the identity once frozen.
- If a fixture is found to be wrong, the fix is a new fixture version in the compile settings, which changes the
  identity of scenes accepted under it; old reports are not edited.
