## Background

A surface region has two unrelated jobs: it grips or slips under a wheel, and it reflects, transmits or absorbs
light for a sensor. The decision (ADR-HAB-03) is that contact properties and optical properties are two maps,
joined only by declared shared latent variables. Reason: a glossy tile can produce a bad depth sample and low
traction for unrelated reasons, and a model that ties them together fabricates a correlation (CX-199). A policy
trained on such a fabricated correlation learns "shiny means slippery" and fails on a dry polished floor.

Where physics does couple them, the coupling is explicit. A region's `wetness` in [0, 1] selects the interface
condition (dry or wet) through one recorded conditional and raises the normal-incidence reflectance `r0` and lowers
`roughness` through another. Randomization draws the latent once, so a wet floor is both slippery and glossy in the
same episode, and each conditional has its own provenance.

This bead completes the region table with its optical card reference and latents, emits it at compile stage 7
(emit the region table and maps: interface, optical, semantic, and for ingested scenes the unknown mask), places
the fixture and movable tables in the pack with their regions, and passes the gate that proves the separation.
There is one region table. N19a-04 writes its rows at M2 with the semantic labels and the interface side
(`material` and `condition`, which BD-167's binding and the M2 ripple row read before M3); this bead extends the
same rows with the optical reference, the latents and their conditionals, and from M3 owns the table's emission.
It creates no second table.

## Technical approach

1. **Region table (DESIGN; the rows of N19a-04 extended, not a new type).**

```rust
pub struct Region {
    id: RegionId, semantic: SemanticLabels,
    interface: InterfaceRef,     // material + condition: dry | wet | greasy | dusty  -> selects contact cards
    optical: CardDigest,         // an optical card, kind `optical`
    latents: Vec<LatentBinding>, // named scalars, each with its conditionals
}
pub struct LatentBinding { name: LatentName, range: (f64, f64), draw: Distribution,
                           to_interface: Option<Conditional>, to_optical: Option<Conditional>,
                           provenance: (Provenance, Provenance) }   // one per conditional
```

   The interface side names a material and a condition; Traction resolves the pair of materials in contact and
   the condition to a contact card. The optical side names an optical card. Neither side can read the other:
   there is no accessor from a contact kernel to an optical field or from a ray kernel to an interface field.
2. **Latents.** A latent is a named scalar per region and episode (`wetness`, `fog`, `dust`). The pack carries the
   value the generator drew (BD-225 emits it as a latent value plus the references of its two conditionals, one
   addressed draw per region); a task's randomization plan may redraw it per episode by address. Either way there
   is one value per region per episode, and both conditionals read that one value.
   - `to_interface`: a recorded rule from the latent to the interface condition, for example
     `wetness ≥ 0.5 ⇒ condition = wet, else dry` (threshold and direction recorded).
   - `to_optical`: a recorded rule from the latent to optical fields, for example `r0 ← r0_dry + (r0_wet −
     r0_dry)·wetness` (raises `r0`) and `roughness ← roughness_dry·(1 − k·wetness)` (lowers `roughness`); the
     direction of each effect is part of the record.
   Each conditional carries its own provenance (`assumed` until measured). A region with no latent has two fully
   independent properties. Declaring a coupling by any other means (computing friction from `r0`, choosing an
   optical class from the material name) is not possible through the schema.
3. **Compile stage 7.** Emits the region table, the interface map, the optical map, the semantic map and, for
   ingested scenes, the unknown mask; refuses a region with no optical card (`FROB-REF-316`); verifies that every
   surface of every fixture and movable is a region with both references; writes the stage receipt (regions,
   cards used, latents, conditionals, counts of `assumed` fields).
4. **Fixtures and movables in the pack.** The fixture table (model digest, parameter table with provenance,
   attachment, behaviour, regions) and the movable table (body, pose, behaviour, regions) are immutable and
   shared across worlds; their joint states and poses are per-world mutable state.
5. **The three checks (enforcing ADR-HAB-03).**
   a. Changing the optical card of a region leaves every physics digest unchanged in a run whose controller does
      not consume exteroception.
   b. Changing the interface card of a region leaves every ray-kernel output unchanged.
   c. Changing a shared latent changes both in the recorded direction.
6. **The fixture.** One floor tile in four states: matte dry, glossy dry, matte wet, glossy wet. This bead commits
   it, because its own unit tests run on it and N19a-44's gate script (which depends on this bead) reuses it:
   `fixtures/scenes/tile4/` (`synthetic`), a 4 m × 4 m floor with one 1 m × 1 m tile region at the centre, optical
   cards `matte` (class `diffuse`) and `glossy` (class `specular-floor`, lower `roughness`, higher `r0`), interface
   conditions `dry` and `wet` on one floor material, as five sources `md.src`, `gd.src`, `mw.src`, `gw.src` and
   `latent.src` (the tile's cards from a `wetness` latent with both conditionals, drawn at w = 0 and w = 1). A
   reference robot (or a sliding block and a fixed depth sensor) runs an open-loop script over the tile in each state.
   Compared: physics digests per step; ray-kernel outputs (ranges, masks). Expected pattern: matte dry against
   glossy dry: physics identical, rays differ; matte dry against matte wet with the optical card held (interface
   condition changed directly): rays identical, physics differs; latent `wetness` 0 against 1: friction falls and
   `r0` rises, both in the recorded direction.
7. **Gate GATE-HAB-05** (Known answer, M3): the four-state tile behaves as above on every tier (referee,
   `batch-cpu`, CUDA, Metal), bit for bit within the determinism class of each tier.
8. **Counterexample CX-199 (its plant and its case).** The plan states CX-199 in words only, with the control "a
   build that derives friction from `r0`". This bead builds that plant, `BD-229:cx-199`: the Cargo feature
   `cx-199-friction-from-r0` of `frob-scene`, under the umbrella `gauntlet-mutations` and never in a default or
   release build, under which the interface resolution of a region scales the friction of its contact card down
   as the optical card's `r0` rises, in the real component (no mock). It must fail check (a) in this bead's
   `friction_from_r0_mutant` and in N19a-44's gate run. This bead then records the case in CX-199's entry of
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/counterexamples.toml` (N19-CX): `case_supplied_by = "BD-229:
   fixtures/scenes/tile4/"`, `numeric = true` and `plant = "BD-229:cx-199"`, with `plan_case` left `"words"`,
   and re-runs N38b-05's seeding tool, so that CX-199's control turns from `obligation` to `runnable` under its
   existing NC number.
9. **A surface without a region.** Stage 7's check that every fixture and movable surface carries both
   references has one code: a surface that belongs to no region is `FROB-REF-316` with `observed: no-region` and
   `pointer` at the fixture or movable and its surface (DESIGN: a surface with no region has no optical card; a
   fixture table that names a region which does not exist stays N19a-04's `FROB-REF-303`).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `no_cross_accessor`: a compile-fail test: the contact-side view type has no optical field and the reverse.
- `latent_drawn_once`: for 10³ episodes the wetness seen by the interface conditional equals the one seen by the
  optical conditional.
- `conditional_directions`: wetness 0 → 1 raises `r0` and lowers `roughness`; the interface condition flips from
  dry to wet at the threshold.
- `kitchen_wetness_0_7_selects_wet`: on a BD-225 kitchen pack whose zone carries `wetness` 0.7 (the generator's
  latent value and its two conditional references), the interface conditional selects the wet condition
  (threshold 0.5) and the optical conditional raises `r0` above its dry value; one resolved value is read by
  both. (Moved here from BD-226, which could not depend on this later seed.)
- `one_region_table`: the rows N19a-04 writes at M2 and the rows this bead emits are one table with one hash; the
  semantic and interface fields of an M2 pack are byte-equal after this bead's extension.
- `independent_regions_stay_independent`: with no latent, 10³ random optical cards leave friction untouched.
- `tile_optical_change_physics_equal`: digests of a 500-step slide equal for matte dry and glossy dry.
- `tile_interface_change_rays_equal`: lidar and depth outputs equal for dry and wet interface with the optical
  card held.
- `tile_latent_changes_both`: friction coefficient offered to the cone falls, and the specular return weight
  rises.
- `friction_from_r0_mutant` (negative control, KA-436, CX-199; built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,
  cx-199-friction-from-r0`): fails `tile_optical_change_physics_equal` on `md.src` against `gd.src`.
- `fixture_surface_without_region_refused`: a gallery fixture with one surface outside every region is refused
  with `FROB-REF-316`, `observed: no-region`, pointer at the fixture and surface.

### End-to-end script and logging

The gate script is N19a-44's (`scripts/e2e/scene_four_state_tile.sh`): compiles the four tile variants, runs the
scripted slide and scan on each tier, compares digests with `frob --json telemetry divergence`. This bead adds
`scripts/e2e/scene_maps.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/scene_maps/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript) for what it owns on the referee and `batch-cpu`: `frob --json scene gen --family kitchen-commercial/galley
--seed <s> --key mp-gen-<s>` and `frob --json scene show <id>` (per region the two references, the latents
and both conditionals with provenance; the zone with `wetness` 0.7 has condition `wet` and raised `r0`); `frob
--json scene compile fixtures/scenes/fixture-gallery.src --key mp-cmp-gallery` (every fixture and movable surface
a region with both cards; the fixture and movable tables carry the model digests of stage 8, N19a-18, a
dependency) and the same with one fixture surface stripped of its region (`--key mp-cmp-strip`, exit
3, `FROB-REF-316`, `observed: no-region`); the five tile sources compiled (`--key mp-cmp-tile-<variant>`, exit 0 or

## Considerations

- "Unchanged" for check (a) holds only when the controller does not consume exteroception; with a closed loop the
  optical change legitimately alters physics through the controller. The fixture uses an open-loop script.
- Dropout through one pane or one glare is common to all reads of an episode; the latent mechanism is also what
  lets a sensor model fail consistently across repeated reads rather than independently per sample.
- The film-friction law reads the film thickness state and the card's roughness, not the optical card; a wet
  floor's gloss comes only through the `wetness` latent.
- Hazard rows that use latents: wet floor (film patch with wetness latent), glass-fronted case (glass with a fog
  latent), dust (optical volume and interface condition `dusty`).
