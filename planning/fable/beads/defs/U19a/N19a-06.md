## Background

Sensors fail on glass, mirrors, wet floors and steam, and those failures decide home, office and kitchen missions.
For a ray kernel to reproduce them, every surface region must say how it responds to light, and that statement must
be separate from how the region grips a wheel (the decision that contact and optical properties are two maps,
joined only by declared latents). This bead defines the optical card, the optical volume and the per-zone lighting
level, and the rule that a region with no optical card is a compile refusal.

Reason for the refusal (DESIGN): a default that nobody chose is how a glass wall becomes a painted one. A generator
with no better information writes `diffuse` with provenance `assumed`, and the pack counts it; it never omits the
card.

Alternative rejected: one "material" record with gloss and friction together. It fabricates a correlation: a glossy
tile can give a bad depth sample and low traction for unrelated reasons.

## Scope

In: the card schema and its checks, optical volumes with their fixture attachment and schedule, lighting levels per
zone, the loader, the compile refusal. Out: the region table with its two card references and the shared latents
(BD-229); the ray rules that consume the card (Sensorium: BD-187 for the kernels, BD-237 for failure modes); the
interface card (Traction).

## Technical approach

1. **Card.** An optical card is a `.fcard` (schema `frob.card/1`) of kind `optical`, with per-field provenance
   (`measured`, `datasheet`, `assumed`), in the same manner as an interface card.

| Field | Meaning | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `class` | One of `diffuse`, `glass`, `mirror`, `specular-floor`, `retroreflector`, `absorber`, `emitter` | Selects the ray rule |
| `r0` | Reflectance at normal incidence, used in the polynomial Fresnel approximation R(θ) = r₀ + (1 − r₀)·(1 − cos θ)⁵, θ the incidence angle | Reflection weight for glass, mirrors and wet or polished floors |
| `transmittance` | Fraction passed through a thin pane | Through-glass returns |
| `albedo_ir`, `albedo_vis` | Diffuse albedo in the sensor bands | Return strength, speckle visibility |
| `roughness` | Width of the specular lobe | Whether a time-of-flight beam returns from a glossy surface off normal |
| `retro_gain` | Retroreflective gain | Saturation and bloom (Frontier) |
| `emission` | Emitted irradiance in the sensor band (lamps, sunlit patches) | Ambient washout of projected patterns |

   Admission checks (DESIGN, added by this bead): `r0`, `transmittance`, albedos in [0, 1]; `r0 + transmittance ≤ 1` for `glass`; `roughness ≥
   0`; `retro_gain ≥ 1` for `retroreflector`; fields irrelevant to the class may be absent, never zero-by-default.
   A failed check is a card refusal with the field named.
2. **What each class means to a ray** (stated so a card author can choose; the kernels are Sensorium's): `diffuse`
   returns at the hit range; `absorber` returns nothing; `glass` returns directly only near normal incidence and
   otherwise passes the ray through or mirrors it, whichever is stronger; `mirror` follows the mirror direction for
   one bounce and reports a phantom behind the surface; `specular-floor` behaves as `mirror` with weight R(θ) and
   as `diffuse` otherwise, with R rising with the wetness latent; `retroreflector` returns with gain; `emitter`
   has no range and raises ambient level. One bounce at most, so the launch shape of a ray kernel is fixed.
3. **Optical volume.** A box or ellipsoid in the scene frame with an extinction coefficient σ in m⁻¹ and a
   backscatter albedo. A ray crossing length ℓ keeps exp(−σ·ℓ) of its power one way and exp(−2σ·ℓ) for an active
   sensor. Steam near a dishwasher, a kettle or a fryer, and dust in a warehouse, are volumes **attached to a
   fixture** (they move with it) with an on and off schedule: a list of rational-time intervals in episode time,
   or `always`. The schedule is part of the pack; whether a volume is on at an instant is a pure function of
   episode time.
4. **Lighting level.** Each zone (a set of regions) carries an ambient irradiance level in the sensor bands, with
   provenance. It is the "dim hallway" hazard and the "motion-activated light" retrofit: camera exposure
   lengthens as it falls. A zone without a level takes the building default, which is itself a declared field.
5. **Loader.** `OpticalCard::load` verifies the schema, the checks and the provenance of every field; cards are
   content-addressed and referenced by digest from the region table.
6. **Refusal.** At compile stage 7 (emit the region table and maps), a region with no optical card reference is
   refused: `FROB-REF-316` "A surface region has no optical card"; repair: assign one, with provenance `assumed`
   if necessary; exit code 3. The refusal's `pointer` names the region and its semantic label.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The seven classes load; an eighth, unknown class is a load error.
- [ ] Each admission check rejects its planted violation and names the field.
- [ ] A pack with one region lacking an optical card is refused with `FROB-REF-316` naming the region.
- [ ] A generator-written `diffuse` card with provenance `assumed` is accepted and increments the pack's assumed
      count.
- [ ] An optical volume follows its fixture's pose and is on exactly in its scheduled intervals.
- [ ] `frob --json scene show <id>` lists optical cards per region, volumes with schedules and zone lighting.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `seven_classes_roundtrip`: one card per class; write, read, byte-equal.
- `glass_energy_bound`: `r0 = 0.7`, `transmittance = 0.5` is refused (sum 1.2 > 1).
- `missing_card_is_316`: synthetic two-region pack, one region without a card; expect code 316, exit 3, pointer to
  the region.
- `no_silent_diffuse` (negative control): a test build that fills a missing card with `diffuse` must fail the
  previous test.
- `volume_schedule_exact`: schedule [2 s, 5 s) in rational time; on at t = 2, off at t = 5.
- `volume_attached_pose`: moving the fixture by a transform moves the volume by the same transform.
- `fresnel_polynomial_values`: R(0) = r₀; R(90°) = 1; for r₀ = 0.04 at 60°, R = 0.04 + 0.96·0.5⁵ = 0.07.
- `extinction_worked_case`: σ = 2 m⁻¹ and a 5% threshold give a cutoff length ln(20)/4 = 0.749 m (0.75 m to two
  digits) for an active sensor.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/scene_optical_cards.sh`: compiles `fixtures/scenes/glass-partition.src` (synthetic) with and without
an optical card on the pane; asserts exit 0 and exit 3 with code 316; runs `frob --json scene show`. Logs commands,
exit codes, the refusal block, the card digests and a `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the Fresnel values and the 0.75 m steam cutoff above. Negative control: the silent-default build.
Metamorphic relation: changing only an optical card changes no physics digest (measured by the four-state tile,
GATE-HAB-05, in BD-229). Determinism: card digests identical on Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- Every optical value is `assumed` until a card records a datasheet or a measurement; no optical value is ever
  estimated from an image.
- `retro_gain` saturation and bloom are Frontier and gated; the field is carried now so cards do not change.
- The exponential in attenuation is evaluated by the deterministic exponential of the kernel dialect, not here.
- Lighting level interacts with camera exposure (Sensorium); a zone level is data, the exposure model is theirs.
