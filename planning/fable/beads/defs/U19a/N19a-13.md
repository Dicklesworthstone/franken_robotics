## Background

A signed distance needs an inside and an outside. A mesh with a hole, a flipped face or a self-intersection has
neither, and a field computed from it has patches where "inside" leaks out: a robot can then stand inside a wall or
fall through a floor. Stage 4 of the compiler checks that each solid part is watertight and consistently oriented,
then computes its signed distance with FrankenSim's distance-field machinery, reached through the airlock.

Decision (DESIGN): a scene that fails the watertight check is **refused, not patched**. An import may request the
repair pipeline explicitly; every closed hole is then recorded with its receipt and marked `derived: hole-fill`,
and its neighbourhood becomes an unknown region. Reason: an automatic repair invents geometry (closing a scan can
create a wall where a door exists), and invented geometry must be visible and must never support a claim.
Alternative rejected: silent hole filling.

The airlock is the one crate, `frob-fsim`, allowed to name types from FrankenSim's volatile crates; everything
else uses FrankenRobotics-native traits it exposes, so that an upstream API change costs one crate.

## Scope

In: the watertight and orientation checks, the signed-distance computation behind an airlock trait, the explicit
repair path and its records, the refusal, the stage receipt. Out: brick emission and the per-brick bound (BD-163,
N19a-14); overlay emission (N19a-17); the unknown mask's consumers (N19c-03) and ingestion of real places (N19c-04,
capstone BD-398); the toolchain admission itself (BD-031) and the admission of the representation crates through
the airlock's geometry group (N33a-30).

## Technical approach

1. **Facts about the sibling (FACT, `frankensim` at commit `483d09c682f2`).** `fs-rep-sdf` provides `TiledSdf`
   (dense Morton-tiled grids with a constructed error enclosure and sphere-traced raycasts), `VdbGrid` (a sparse
   tile tree, root map to 32³ internal nodes to 8³ bitmasked leaves, with deterministic iteration order),
   `NarrowBand` and `AdaptiveSdf`. `fs-rep-mesh` provides generalized winding numbers (exact, and an octree
   approximation whose error is measured against exact), a BVH mesh chart with watertight ray tests and a repair
   pipeline with receipts. `fs-rep-frep` provides constructive solids as a DAG with value and gradient, a Lipschitz
   bound and an interval range derived from the same graph. `fs-topo` provides manifold and self-intersection
   certificates. The three representation crates depend on `fs-qty` only through `fs-evidence` (their manifests),
   so the scene compiler carries the toolchain risk tested at INT-TOOLCHAIN-01.
2. **Airlock surface (DESIGN; trait names fix intent, not signatures).** In `frob-fsim`, feature group `geometry`:
   `GeometryKernel::check_closed(mesh) → ClosedReport`, `::orientation(mesh) → OrientationReport`,
   `::self_intersections(mesh) → Vec<Witness>`, `::signed_distance_chart(solid) → Chart` where `Chart` exposes
   `value(p)`, `gradient(p)`, `enclosure()` (the constructed error bound of the source chart) and, for
   constructive solids, `interval_range(box)`; `MeshExchange::repair(mesh, policy) → (mesh, Vec<RepairReceipt>)`.
3. **Checks, per part tagged `support`, `keep-out` or `transparent`.**
   a. Closed manifold: every edge has exactly two incident faces (certificate from `fs-topo`).
   b. Consistent orientation: adjacent faces traverse their shared edge in opposite directions; the generalized
      winding number is 1 inside and 0 outside at sampled probe points (exact winding on small parts, the octree
      approximation with its measured error otherwise).
   c. No self-intersection (certificate from `fs-topo`).
   Constructive solids from generators are closed by construction; the check is their interval range being
   well-formed.
4. **Signed distance.** For a constructive solid, the chart is the `fs-rep-frep` DAG (value, gradient, unit
   Lipschitz bound, interval range). For a mesh, the chart is the BVH mesh chart with sign from winding numbers.
   The chart and its enclosure are handed to stage 5.
5. **Refusal.** `FROB-REF-301` "Source geometry is not watertight or not consistently oriented". Ranked repairs:
   request explicit repair with receipts; supply a closed model. Exit 3. The refusal names the part and gives a
   witness (an open edge, a flipped face pair or an intersecting triangle pair).
6. **Explicit repair.** With `--repair fill` on an import (the option of `scene.compile` as BD-163 spells it; the
   default policy `none` never repairs), `MeshExchange::repair` runs; each closed hole
   yields a receipt (hole boundary, area filled, method) stored in the acceptance report; the filled faces carry
   `derived: hole-fill`; and the neighbourhood of each fill, a ball of radius equal to the hole's largest extent
   plus the truncation distance (DESIGN), is written to the unknown mask. Unknown is never changed to free or
   occupied without a recorded observation or a recorded, named assumption (INV-HAB-04).
7. **Receipt.** Per part: closed, oriented, self-intersection results; chart kind; source enclosure; repair
   receipts; unknown volume added.
8. **Determinism.** The stage is a CPU job with deterministic iteration order; its outputs are D2 per platform and
   are tested for equality across Linux and macOS because `SceneId` must match (INV-HAB-10).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A cube with one face removed, a cube with one face flipped and two interpenetrating cubes declared as one
      solid are each refused with `FROB-REF-301` and a witness.
- [ ] With explicit repair the open cube compiles; the report holds one repair receipt; the fill is marked
      `derived: hole-fill`; the unknown mask is non-empty around it.
- [ ] Without the repair flag no hole is ever closed (a counter of repair calls is zero).
- [ ] The signed distance of analytic fixtures (sphere, box, torus as constructive solids) matches closed form
      within the chart's enclosure at 10⁴ sample points.
- [ ] `frob-scene` names no FrankenSim type (the layering check passes).
- [ ] The admission test for the representation crates passes on the chosen toolchain, or the stage runs through
      the airlock's `remote` worker mode with identical results.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `open_cube_301`, `flipped_face_301`, `self_intersection_301`: each returns code 301 with a witness of the
  expected kind.
- `repair_requires_flag`: the open cube without `--repair` is refused; with it, compiles.
- `repair_marks_unknown`: after repair, points within the fill neighbourhood read `unknown` in the mask.
- `silent_repair_mutant` (negative control): a build that repairs without the flag fails `repair_requires_flag`.
- `sdf_sphere_closed_form`: radius 0.5 m at the origin; value at (1, 0, 0) is 0.5 m, at the origin −0.5 m.
- `sdf_box_corner`: the unit box [−0.5, 0.5]³; value at (1, 1, 0.25), nearest to the vertical edge at x = y = 0.5,
  is √(0.5² + 0.5²) = 0.7071 m.
- `winding_inside_outside`: winding number 1 at the centroid, 0 at a far point, on a closed mesh.
- `chart_enclosure_holds`: |chart − closed form| ≤ enclosure at 10⁴ points.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/scene_compile_watertight.sh` (sources the `STD-E2E` helper): `frob --json scene compile` on the three
defective fixtures (exit 3, code 301, witness kind asserted), on the open cube with `--repair fill` (exit 0 or 2),
and `frob --json scene show <id> --receipts` to print repair receipts and the unknown volume; `frob --json scene
compile fixtures/scenes/sphere-r05.src` (`synthetic`: a constructive sphere of radius 0.5 m at the origin, the
case of `sdf_sphere_closed_form`) and `frob --json scene show <id> --probe 0,0,0` on that pack (−0.5 m within
the bound). Every compile call carries its own
idempotency key (`--key wt-<fixture>`, and a different one for the open cube with and without `--repair fill`).
The verb `scene.compile` is BD-163's, which
depends on this bead: until BD-163 is closed the steps are logged with `e2e_skip "needs BD-163"` (never counted as
a pass), and N19a-42 requires this script to run them and pass. Every line carries the charter fields (`ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block);
`refusal` blocks with witnesses; repair receipts; unknown volume in m³; the repair-call counter; on failure the
first differing value and the fixture path. Final `summary` with counts.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the sphere and box distances above. Negative controls: the silent-repair mutant; a sign-flipped
chart must fail `sdf_sphere_closed_form` at the origin. Parity (tier G2): the airlock chart against FrankenSim's
own value on the same fixtures. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- Toolchain risk: the representation crates sit behind the nightly coupling tested at INT-TOOLCHAIN-01; if the
  in-process route fails, the worker fallback (the airlock's `remote` mode) must give the same chart values, at a
  compile-time cost that the receipt reports.
- Interim posture if the admission test for these crates is not yet on record: generated scenes compile from
  `frob-scene`'s own constructive-solid evaluation of the overlay primitive set, and mesh imports are refused as
  "capability not admitted" (exit 5). Switch-over rule: enable the airlock path when the admission test passes.
- The octree winding approximation has a measured error; near thin walls the exact form is used.
