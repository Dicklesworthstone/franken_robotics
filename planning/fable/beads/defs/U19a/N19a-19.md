## Background

A spawn region says where a robot may start. A scene whose spawn poses put the robot inside a shelf, across a door
swing, or half over a stair edge produces episodes that fail at step zero for reasons that have nothing to do with
the design under test, and those failures pollute every success rate computed on the scene. Stage 9 of the compiler
checks spawn poses for interference and tip-over when the scene is compiled, for the family's nominal footprint,
so that a bad spawn is a compile finding and not ten thousand mysterious falls. The same stage then computes the
clearance complex for the nominal footprint and writes the feasibility tag; that second half is the feasibility-tag
bead (BD-234).

The nominal footprint is a declared property of a scene family: a base shape and height that stands for "a robot
this family is meant for" (for example a rectangle 0.50 m × 0.70 m, as in the manifest example
`"footprint": "nominal-0.50x0.70"`).

## Scope

In: the two geometric checks on spawn poses, the valid-pose mask per spawn region, findings and the stage receipt.
Out: the clearance complex and feasibility tag (BD-234, built on BD-233); robot-specific placement checks at task
binding (preflight stage "place", BD-244) and robot static stability (BD-100): this stage knows only the nominal
footprint, not a robot.

## Technical approach

1. **Pose lattice.** For each spawn region, enumerate poses on a deterministic lattice: positions every 10 cm over
   the region (DESIGN default), headings in the region's allowed interval at 16 steps per turn. The lattice, not
   random sampling, makes the mask a pure function of the scene.
2. **Interference check.** Place the nominal footprint prism (the base shape extruded from the step-over height to
   the nominal height) at the pose. The pose is free of interference when the scene distance from every point of
   the prism's surface sampling to static geometry, overlays, fixture swept volumes (a door's leaf sweep, a
   drawer's travel) and settled movables exceeds the margin m_s + b, where m_s is the family's spawn margin
   (default 5 cm) and b the field bound of the bricks it touches. Using the bound makes the statement one-sided:
   a pose accepted here is clear under the field's stated error. The stage reads fixture swept volumes and settled
   movables through the pack's fixture and movable tables, which are empty until the fixture library (N19a-07)
   and stage 8 (N19a-18) land at M3 and fill them; at M2 the door-sweep case is tested with a synthetic swept
   volume entered through the same table accessor (DESIGN: the stage takes the sweeps as data, so nothing in it
   changes when the fixture library lands).
3. **Tip-over check.** A pose is stable for the nominal footprint when, under the whole base shape: (a) every
   sample point lies over a region tagged `support` (not a keep-out, not `unknown`, not a movable's top); (b) the
   support height varies by at most the family's step tolerance across the footprint (no stair edge, kerb or
   dock lip under the base; default 10 mm); (c) the support slope, from the field gradient, is at most the
   family's spawn slope limit (default 5%). These three conditions keep the nominal body's centre of mass over a
   level support polygon; a robot-specific stability margin is computed later, at binding.
4. **Unknown space.** Spawn and goal placement are not allowed in or adjacent to unknown space. "Adjacent" is
   the rule of the unknown-space treatment table, which N19c-03 owns (M5, built on this stage): a pose is invalid,
   with reason `unknown`, when the nominal footprint at that pose, inflated by one coarse cell S (the pack's coarse
   spacing, 0.16 m by default), touches unknown space (a region labelled `unknown`, or a coarse cell flagged in
   the unknown mask, such as the neighbourhood of a stage-4 hole fill). The footprint is inflated, not the
   reference point's distance compared with a footprint radius: that reading has no adjacency margin and is not
   used. N19a-04 has already refused, at validation, a spawn or goal region whose dilation by ρ + S touches an
   `unknown` region; this check also sees the cells that compile stages flag after validation, and records the
   reason per cell.
5. **Mask.** Each spawn region gets a bit mask over its pose lattice and a valid fraction. The task layer draws
   spawn poses only from valid lattice cells (the draw is by address, so it is reproducible).
6. **Findings.** A spawn region with valid fraction zero refuses the compile: "a spawn region has no admissible
   pose for the nominal footprint", exit 3, naming the region, the lattice size and the counts per failing
   reason; ranked repairs: move or enlarge the spawn region; remove or move the obstruction named most often;
   declare the family's nominal footprint with provenance if it is wrong. §19.16 gives this situation no code: it
   is the refusal `spawn-region-no-admissible-pose` (class `scene`, family `3xx`, exit 3, retry `after-change`; block
   fields `region`, `lattice_cells`, `counts_by_reason`, `dominant_obstruction`), row 8 of the late-codes decision
   N19c-42, which files it in the allocation ledger and enters it under the number the ledger assigns. This stage
   names it only through the constant `frob_scene::refusal::late::SPAWN_NO_ADMISSIBLE_POSE` and never by a number of
   its own (an earlier text proposed `FROB-REF-326`, a number three other beads had printed for a different
   refusal). A fraction below a family threshold (default 0.5) is a finding (exit 2); each invalid cell records
   its first failing reason (`interference: <object>`, `off-support`, `step-under-base`, `slope`, `unknown`).
7. **Goals.** The same two checks run on goal regions, with the difference that a goal with no admissible pose is
   not an error: the stage records `goal_admissible: false` for the region in its receipt and hands it to the
   second half of stage 9, where it makes the scene `impossible` for the nominal footprint (BD-234, which depends
   on this bead). Such a scene is kept, tagged and counted, never resampled. Unknown space is blocked for
   witnesses and free for cuts (N19c-03), so a goal supports an impossibility only through reasons that hold
   whatever the unknown space holds: when every cell is invalid and some cell fails for `unknown` alone, the
   receipt records `goal_admissible: unknown`, which BD-234 tags `unresolved`, never `impossible`.
8. **Receipt.** Per region: lattice size, valid fraction, counts per failing reason, the margin and bound used.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On a room fixture with a shelf, every spawn lattice cell overlapping the shelf (inflated by the margin and
      bound) is invalid with reason `interference`.
- [ ] Cells inside a door's leaf sweep are invalid.
- [ ] Cells where the base overhangs a stair edge by any amount are invalid with reason `step-under-base`.
- [ ] A spawn region wholly inside an obstacle, or wholly on a `keep-out` region, makes the compile fail with
      exit 3 and the refusal `spawn-region-no-admissible-pose` (row 8 of N19c-42), naming the region, the lattice
      size, the counts per failing reason and the dominant one; with the region moved clear (the first repair) the
      same source compiles.
- [ ] A goal region wholly inside an obstacle compiles (exit 0 or 2) and the stage-9 receipt records
      `goal_admissible: false` for it (the resulting tag `impossible` is asserted by BD-234); a goal region whose
      every cell is invalid, some for `unknown` alone, records `goal_admissible: unknown`.
- [ ] A pose whose footprint, inflated by one coarse cell, touches unknown space is invalid with reason
      `unknown`, including where the reference point lies farther than the footprint radius from it.
- [ ] The mask is byte-identical across platforms and recompiles.
- [ ] A task's spawn draws fall only in valid cells over 10⁵ draws.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `shelf_interference_mask`: 4 m × 4 m room, shelf 1 m × 0.4 m at the centre, nominal footprint 0.50 × 0.70 m,
  margin 5 cm, bound 1 mm: a pose centred on the shelf is invalid; a pose whose footprint edge is 6 cm from the
  shelf is valid; one at 4 cm is invalid.
- `door_sweep_invalid`: poses inside the quarter-disc of a 0.80 m door leaf (a synthetic swept-volume row in the
  fixture table, labelled `synthetic`: the door fixture itself is N19a-07's, M3) are invalid with reason
  `interference: <door>`; with the row removed the same poses are valid.
- `stair_edge_invalid`: a base overhanging a 150 mm riser by 1 cm is invalid.
- `slope_limit`: on a 10% ramp with a 5% limit every pose is invalid with reason `slope`; on a 3% floor valid.
- `spawn_fully_blocked_is_error`; `spawn_on_keepout_all_invalid` (a spawn region drawn wholly over a region
  labelled `keep-out`: every cell `off-support`, compile refused); `goal_fully_blocked_not_error` (receipt
  carries `goal_admissible: false`, exit 0 or 2).
- `unknown_adjacent_invalid`: nominal footprint 0.50 × 0.70 m (radius ρ = 0.43 m), S = 0.16 m, the long axis
  pointing at a flagged coarse cell: with the footprint edge 0.15 m from the cell the pose is invalid with reason
  `unknown` (the inflated footprint touches it); at 0.20 m it is valid.
- `radius_reading_mutant` (negative control): a build that invalidates a pose only when its reference point lies
  within ρ of unknown space accepts the 0.15 m pose (reference point 0.15 + 0.35 = 0.50 m > 0.43 m) and fails
  `unknown_adjacent_invalid`.
- `goal_unknown_only_is_unknown`: a goal region whose cells are invalid, half for `interference` and half for
  `unknown` alone, records `goal_admissible: unknown`; the same region with the unknown cells made known and clear
  records `goal_admissible: true`.
- `no_bound_mutant` (negative control): a build that omits the field bound accepts a pose at margin + ½ bound
  and fails a boundary test built with a 20 mm bound.
- `draws_respect_mask`: 10⁵ addressed draws, all in valid cells.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/scene_compile_spawn.sh` (sources the `STD-E2E` helper): `frob --json scene compile
fixtures/scenes/spawn-room.src --key sps-1` (`synthetic`), `frob --json scene show <id> --receipts` for the stage-9
receipt; then the blocked-spawn fixture `fixtures/scenes/spawn-blocked.src` (`--key sps-2`; exit 3 with the refusal
`spawn-region-no-admissible-pose` and the region named, through `e2e_expect_refusal` with the code read from the
registry by slug) and the blocked-goal fixture (`--key sps-3`; exit 0 or 2 with `goal_admissible: false` in the
receipt); then the first fixture again under a new key (`--key sps-4`), comparing the mask digest. Every
line carries the charter fields (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall
time, the envelope's `evidence` block); `metric` lines give per-region lattice size and valid fraction against
the expected value, margin and bound in metres; `refusal` lines give code, region and first repair; on failure the
first differing cell index and reason and the fixture path. Final `summary` with counts.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the 6 cm valid and 4 cm invalid cases against a 5 cm margin plus 1 mm bound. Negative control: the
no-bound mutant. Metamorphic relation: shrinking the nominal footprint never turns a valid cell invalid.
Determinism lanes: mask identical across platforms; debug and release.

## Considerations

- The nominal footprint is a property of the family and counts as an `assumed` dimension unless given provenance.
- A scene acceptable for the nominal footprint may still be unsuitable for a particular robot; the robot-specific
  checks at binding are the authority for that robot, and a refusal there carries the bottleneck.
- The lattice step trades compile time against mask resolution; it is a compile setting and part of the identity.
- Movables settle (stage 8) before this stage runs, so the mask reflects where clutter actually rests.
