## Background

An imported scene is placed in the scene frame by a transform. The class of that transform (proper rigid motion
with a scale, or something else) must be established, not assumed, because an import pipeline can mirror a room or
shear it without anyone noticing: a mirrored home has every door hinged on the other side and every left turn a
right turn, and it still looks like a home.

The tempting check is three labelled, non-collinear landmarks and a known length. It fixes a proper rigid
registration and nothing more: a reflection through the plane of the three landmarks passes the same three checks
with zero residual (CX-184). Smallest case, checkable by hand: landmarks A = (0, 0, 0), B = (1, 0, 0),
C = (0, 1, 0). The map (x, y, z) ↦ (x, y, −z) fixes all three, so a mirrored room registers perfectly. A fourth
landmark D = (0, 0, 1), for example a ceiling marker, maps to (0, 0, −1) and shows a residual of 2 m. A gravity
witness does not replace the fourth point: if the three landmarks lie in a vertical plane, the reflection through
that plane preserves gravity (CX-185).

Rule (DESIGN): an import whose format or pipeline admits a parity change or a general affine map requires four
non-coplanar correspondences. Alternative rejected: three landmarks plus a gravity direction, for the reason above.

## Scope

In: the registration solve from four or more correspondences, the checks on its linear part, the tetrahedron
volume rule, the scale-witness match, the glTF camera convention check, the stored registration residual and
uncertainty, the refusal, the three-point control as a negative control. Out: the import pipeline and scale labels
(BD-398); the acceptance verb and report (BD-228); the transform graph inside a source file (N19a-11).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** n ≥ 4 labelled correspondences (sᵢ in source coordinates, tᵢ in scene coordinates), each with a
   stated position uncertainty; a scale witness (an object of known size or a measured baseline) with its
   uncertainty; the registration tolerance τ_reg from the import profile.
2. **Why four suffice (PO-HAB-07).** An affine map that fixes four non-coplanar points is the identity; hence two
   affine maps that agree on four non-coplanar points are equal, so four correspondences determine the
   registration. Proof for the card: if f(x) = L·x + d fixes p₀..p₃, then L·(pᵢ − p₀) = pᵢ − p₀ for i = 1, 2, 3;
   the three differences are a basis because the points are non-coplanar; so L = I and d = 0. Filed as a
   linear-algebra card with a symbolic check.
3. **Solve.** With exactly four: L = T·S⁻¹, where S = [s₁ − s₀, s₂ − s₀, s₃ − s₀] and T = [t₁ − t₀, t₂ − t₀,
   t₃ − t₀] as 3×3 matrices, and d = t₀ − L·s₀. With more than four: the least-squares affine map, with per-
   landmark residuals reported.
4. **Non-degeneracy.** The tetrahedron of the four landmarks (the best-conditioned four when n > 4) must have
   volume V = |det S|/6 of at least 1% (TARGET) of D³, D being the largest separation between landmarks, so that
   the fourth point is not nearly coplanar.
5. **Class checks on L.** Compute G = LᵀL and c² = trace(G)/3. Require (a) ‖G − c²·I‖_F ≤ τ_reg·c² (L is a
   similarity: no shear, no non-uniform scale); (b) det L > 0 (no reflection); (c) c matches the scale witness
   within the combined uncertainty. Residuals after the fit must be within the landmarks' uncertainties.
6. **Outputs stored in the pack.** The registration transform; the residual per landmark and their root mean
   square (the pack's "registration residual"); the registration uncertainty u_reg (a bound on position error of
   any scene point from landmark uncertainty and lever arm), which later enters every clearance cell's error; the
   tetrahedron volume ratio; the class verdict.
7. **glTF camera.** FACT (Khronos glTF 2.0 specification, to be re-read before a gate cites it): a glTF camera
   looks along its local −Z axis with +Y up. The conversion from that frame to the sensor optical frame is a
   fixed proper rotation, checked with an off-axis point in front of the lens: a point at camera coordinates
   (+0.2, +0.1, −1.0) (right, up, one metre ahead) must have positive depth in the optical frame and project to
   the right of and above the image centre. A sign error on any axis puts it behind the camera or in the wrong
   quadrant.
8. **Refusal.** Failure of any of the above is `FROB-REF-302` "The transform class of an import is not
   established: a reflection or affine map is not excluded, or winding disagrees with the determinant". Ranked
   repairs: supply a fourth non-coplanar landmark; declare the source handedness; fix winding. Exit 3. The
   refusal reports which check failed with observed and required values (for example `det L = −1.0`,
   `volume ratio 0.003 < 0.01`).
9. **Three-point control.** A separate routine implementing the naive check (rigid fit to three landmarks and a
   length) is kept in the test tree only, to demonstrate that it accepts the reflected room.
10. **Where it runs.** The registration is a compiler check (plan §19.5.1: "the compiler checks that its linear
    part L …"). Compile stage 2 (N19a-11) refuses an import whose transform class is not established; from this
    bead on, stage 2 establishes it by calling this solve when the source carries correspondences, read (DESIGN)
    from a landmark sidecar beside the source, `<source>.landmarks.json`: a list of `{label, source_xyz,
    scene_xyz, sigma_m}` and a scale witness `{label, length_m, sigma_m}`. The verdict and the stored values of
    step 6 enter the identity part of the acceptance report as its registration entry, which `scene.accept`
    (BD-228) reads and prints; a generated scene's entry is `registration: not-applicable`.
11. **Planted controls of N19-CX.** The counterexample registry names this bead the carrier of two `mutation`
    plants, which it builds in the real registration path of `frob-scene` (not in the test tree, where the
    three-point routine of item 9 lives) as Cargo features under the umbrella `gauntlet-mutations`, never in a
    default or release build: `N19a-21:cx-184`, feature `cx-184-three-landmarks` (the solve fits three landmarks
    and a length and checks neither the parity nor det L), under which stage 2 accepts the mirrored room; and
    `N19a-21:cx-185`, feature `cx-185-gravity-fourth` (a gravity witness accepted as the fourth correspondence),
    under which the vertical-plane case of CX-185 is accepted. With either plant on, the check it breaks fails
    (KA-429's row of GATE-HAB-04 for CX-184; `vertical_plane_gravity_trap` for CX-185); with both off they pass.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The reflected asymmetric room is rejected with `FROB-REF-302`, reporting det L = −1 and a 2 m residual at
      the off-plane marker under the proper-rigid hypothesis; the three-point control accepts the same input with
      zero residual.
- [ ] A registration with a nearly coplanar fourth landmark (volume ratio under 1%) is refused.
- [ ] A sheared import (L with ‖G − c²·I‖_F above tolerance) is refused; a pure similarity with the right scale is
      accepted and its c reported.
- [ ] A scale that disagrees with the witness beyond uncertainty is refused.
- [ ] The glTF camera off-axis point lands in the expected quadrant with positive depth; each single-axis sign
      mutant fails.
- [ ] The pack stores residuals, u_reg, the volume ratio and the verdict.
- [ ] `scene.compile` of a GLB import with a four-landmark sidecar establishes the class through this solve in
      stage 2; without a sidecar and without declared handedness the import is still refused as N19a-11 states.
- [ ] PO-HAB-07 card is on file with its symbolic check.
- [ ] This bead's own `scripts/e2e/scene_registration.sh` runs the live import cases of PO-HAB-07 on its own
      fixtures (N12x-03's card script runs no compile, convention 7): step `reg-mirror-3pt` (the mirrored room
      with three landmarks) and step `reg-mirror` (with four) both exit 3 with `FROB-REF-302`, the second
      reporting det L = −1 and the 2 m residual of KA-429 at the off-plane marker.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `reflected_room_rejected` (KA-429): sources A, B, C, D as above; targets A, B, C and (0, 0, −1). L = diag(1, 1,
  −1), LᵀL = I, det L = −1; refused. Residual of D under the best proper rigid fit to A, B, C: 2 m.
- `three_point_control_accepts` (negative control for the gate): the naive routine returns residual 0 on A, B, C.
- `vertical_plane_gravity_trap` (CX-185): landmarks (0,0,0), (1,0,0), (0,0,1) and gravity −z; the reflection
  y ↦ −y fixes all three and gravity; the four-point rule with a fourth landmark at (0, 1, 0) rejects it.
- `volume_ratio_values`: for A, B, C, D: V = 1/6, D_max = √2, ratio (1/6)/2.828 = 0.0589, accepted; with
  D = (0, 0, 0.05): V = 0.00833, ratio 0.00295, refused.
- `shear_refused`: L = [[1, 0.1, 0], [0, 1, 0], [0, 0, 1]].
- `nonuniform_scale_refused`: L = diag(1, 1, 1.05) at τ_reg = 0.01.
- `similarity_accepted`: L = 2·R for a random rotation R; c = 2 reported; witness 2.0 ± 0.01 matches; witness
  1.9 ± 0.01 refuses.
- `affine_fixing_four_is_identity` (PO-HAB-07, property test over random non-coplanar quadruples in exact
  rationals).
- `gltf_camera_quadrant` and its three sign mutants.
- `cx_184_plant_caught` (negative control, CX-184; built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,cx-184-three-landmarks`):
  `reflected_room_rejected` fails (the mirrored room is accepted); `cx_185_plant_caught` (CX-185; built with
  `--features gauntlet-mutations,cx-185-gravity-fourth`): `vertical_plane_gravity_trap` fails. Neither feature
  is in the default or release feature set.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/scene_registration.sh` (sources the `STD-E2E` helper). The sidecar is found by name beside its
source, so each case is its own fixture file with its own `<source>.landmarks.json`, and each compile carries its
own key (`scene.compile` is a `run` verb): `frob --json scene compile fixtures/imports/asym-room.glb --key
reg-asym` with its four-landmark sidecar (exit 0 or 2; `frob --json scene show <id> --receipts` gives det L > 0,
c, the volume ratio and per-landmark residuals), `asym-room-mirrored.glb --key reg-mirror` with the same landmarks
(exit 3, `FROB-REF-302`, observed `det L = −1.0` and the 2 m residual at the off-plane marker under the
proper-rigid hypothesis, KA-429), `asym-room-3pt.glb --key reg-3pt` and `asym-room-mirrored-3pt.glb
--key reg-mirror-3pt` with three landmarks (exit 3, `FROB-REF-302`, a fourth landmark required: never accepted,
even for the mirrored room that the three-point control accepts), `asym-room-coplanar.glb --key reg-coplanar` with the
nearly coplanar fourth landmark (exit 3, volume ratio 0.00295 against 0.01), `asym-room-scale19.glb --key
reg-scale` with a scale witness of 1.9 ± 0.01 m for a 2.0 m baseline (exit 3), and `asym-room-sheared.glb --key
reg-shear` (exit 3). The import here is a synthetic GLB through `scene.compile`; real-place ingestion
(`scene.import`, BD-398) is M5 and is not used. Then `frob --json scene accept <id> --only registration --key
reg-accept` prints the entry: the
verb is BD-228's, which depends on this bead, so until BD-228 is closed that step is logged with `e2e_skip "needs
BD-228"` (never counted as a pass), and BD-228's acceptance requires this script to run it and pass. Every line
carries the charter fields (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, the
envelope's `evidence` block); `metric` lines give det L, c, the volume ratio and residuals in metres against their
thresholds; `refusal` lines carry code, the failed check, observed and required; on failure the first differing
value and the fixture and sidecar paths. Final `summary` names GATE-HAB-04. Fixtures are synthetic; no private
scene is used.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-429 (reflected room; residual 2 m; control: the three-point check accepts). CX-184, CX-185. Determinism: the
solve on rational inputs is exact (class D0); floating inputs give identical bits across platforms.

## Considerations

- Landmark labels are human or pipeline input; a mislabelled pair shows up as a large residual, not as a
  reflection, and is reported per landmark.
- u_reg is tens of millimetres in a scan; below that floor the correct output of a clearance query is a
  measurement request, not a verdict.
- Generated scenes need no registration; the stage records `registration: not-applicable` for them.
- The kill gate for ingestion (KILL-INGEST: the real-place gate cannot pass with a scale label of `witnessed` or
  better after one repair cycle; fallback: author the place in the interior grammar from a tape-measure survey)
  is decided elsewhere; this check is one of the things that gate exercises.
