## Background

A scene has two geometries: the collision field that physics and sensors read, and a visual mesh (GLB) that the
viewer draws. When they disagree, a human or an agent looking at a rendering sees one world while the robot lives
in another: a counter that looks solid and is absent from physics, a doorway drawn wider than it is. The decision
(ADR-HAB-04) is that the visual mesh is read by the viewer only; physics, sensors and stills read the collision
field; and the deviation between the two is measured per region and reported. Reason: one authority for geometry
inside the model identity, and a presentation asset that can be replaced without changing a digest. Alternative
rejected: sensing or rendering stills from the visual mesh, which would let presentation data into results.

Invariant INV-HAB-02: physics, sensors and stills read only the collision field, the overlays and the maps; no code
path carries visual-mesh data into a result.

## Scope

In: the deviation measurement with its two evidence types, the listing and refusal thresholds, waivers, the
`optical-only` requirement, the deviation map for the viewer, and the structural test of INV-HAB-02. Out: drawing
stills from the collision field (BD-191) and the viewer's heat-map overlay (BD-300); the silhouette comparison
fixture, which catches collision geometry with no visual counterpart (N19a-24); tagging (N19a-12).

## Technical approach

1. **What is measured.** For each region tagged collidable (`support`, `keep-out`, `transparent`), sample the
   visual mesh surface belonging to that region and evaluate the collision field there. For a sample x on the
   visual surface, the deviation is |φ̂(x)|: how far the drawn surface is from the surface physics has.
2. **Two evidence types, kept apart.**
   - **Bounded** deviation: where the visual surface is sampled at a known spacing h (a surface-covering sample
     set), the unit Lipschitz constant of a distance field gives sup |φ̂| ≤ (largest sampled value) + h over the
     sampled patch. Recorded as `bounded`, with h.
   - **Sampled** deviation: elsewhere (patches the sampler could not cover at the stated spacing: slivers,
     degenerate triangles), the largest sampled value with no bound. Recorded as `sampled`.
   The report never merges the two into one number.
3. **Listing threshold.** A region whose deviation exceeds half a fine voxel plus the brick bound (10 mm + b at the
   default 2 cm spacing) is listed in the acceptance report with its region identifier, semantic label, the
   deviation, its evidence type and the location of the worst sample.
4. **Refusal threshold.** Above the pack's threshold (a compile setting recorded in the identity; DESIGN default to
   be set when the fixtures are frozen, proposed one fine voxel plus the brick bound) a listed region makes the
   pack unacceptable without a waiver. `scene.compile` measures and lists the region as a finding (exit 2) and
   still freezes the pack; the refusal is raised by `scene.accept` (BD-228; the registry lists 317 on that verb):
   `FROB-REF-317` "Collision-to-visual deviation exceeds the pack threshold with no waiver". Ranked repairs:
   correct the collision model; tag the part `optical-only`; waive with a reason. Exit 3. A pack so refused has
   an incomplete report, so a run on it meets `FROB-REF-318`.
5. **Waiver.** A waiver is given at acceptance (`frob scene accept <id> --waive region=<id> --reason <text> --key
   <k>`); it names the region, a reason in free text (stored as untrusted text) and the session that granted it;
   it is part of the acceptance report and therefore visible to every consumer. A waived region stays listed.
   Because the GLB is outside the identity (ADR-HAB-04), the deviation section and its waivers are keyed by
   `SceneId` and the GLB digest: a new GLB needs a new acceptance and new waivers.
6. **Optical-only.** Parts of the visual mesh with no collision counterpart must carry the tag `optical-only`. The
   check: every visual triangle either belongs to a collidable region (and is measured) or to a part tagged
   `optical-only`, `movable`, `jointed` or `deformable-by-proxy` (measured against that object's own collision
   primitives at its authored pose); a triangle that belongs to nothing is a refusal under the same code.
7. **Deviation map.** Per region and per visual vertex the deviation is stored as a blob beside the pack (not in
   the identity) so that the viewer can overlay it as a heat map.
8. **Stills.** Stills for agents are drawn from the collision field by ray kernels, so an agent looks at what
   physics has. The viewer draws the GLB.
9. **Structural test of INV-HAB-02.** Compile one scene with two different GLBs (the correct one and a grossly
   different one: `synthetic`); every physics digest, every ray-kernel output and every still must be identical;
   only the viewer's draw and the deviation report differ. The crate-wide structural tests of INV-HAB-01 and -02
   (no pack mutators, identity on every byte, the GLB decoder reachable only from the viewer by the `xtask
   check-deps` rule, test `glb_reachable_only_from_viewer`) are N19cp1-01's and are not repeated here; this bead
   owns the consumer-level digest test above.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On a fixture whose visual counter is displaced 30 mm from its collision box, compile lists the region with a
      `bounded` deviation between 30 mm and 30 mm + h (exit 2), and `scene.accept` refuses the pack with
      `FROB-REF-317` unless the region is waived.
- [ ] On a matching fixture, every region's bounded deviation is at most the listing threshold and nothing is
      listed.
- [ ] A decorative part with no collision is accepted when tagged `optical-only` and refused when untagged.
- [ ] A waiver is recorded with its reason and the region remains listed.
- [ ] Swapping the GLB changes no physics digest, no sensor output and no still (INV-HAB-02).
- [ ] Bounded and sampled deviations are reported in separate fields with their evidence type.
- [ ] The deviation map loads in the viewer overlay (checked by the viewer's own bead; here: the blob exists and
      has one value per visual vertex).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `displaced_counter_listed`: 30 mm offset, h = 5 mm; reported bounded deviation in [30, 35] mm; code 317.
- `matching_scene_clean`: maximum bounded deviation at most 10 mm + b.
- `lipschitz_bound_holds`: on random smooth surfaces (`synthetic`), the true maximum deviation (dense sampling at
  h/10) never exceeds the reported bound.
- `sliver_reported_as_sampled`: a degenerate triangle patch yields a `sampled` entry, not a bound.
- `untagged_decoration_refused`; `optical_only_accepted`.
- `waiver_recorded_and_listed`.
- `glb_swap_changes_nothing`: digests of a 100-step referee run, a lidar scan and a still are equal for the two
  GLBs.
- `visual_into_physics_mutant` (negative control): a test build whose ray kernel reads the GLB makes
  `glb_swap_changes_nothing` fail.
- `compile_lists_accept_refuses`: compile of the displaced counter exits 2 with the region listed and a frozen
  pack; the acceptance runner on it returns 317; with a waiver it returns exit 2 and the region still listed.
- `glb_swap_needs_new_acceptance`: after a GLB swap the deviation section of the old GLB does not satisfy
  completeness for the new one. (The dependency check that no crate but the viewer reaches the GLB decoder is
  N19cp1-01's `glb_reachable_only_from_viewer`.)

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/scene_visual_deviation.sh` (sources the `STD-E2E` helper). Compile steps, one key per call
(`scene.compile` is a `run` verb): `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/counter-offset.src --key vd-cmp-counter`
(`synthetic`, h = 5 mm) expecting exit 2 with the region listed and a bounded deviation in [0.030, 0.035] m;
`decoration-untagged.src --key vd-cmp-untagged` (exit 2, the untagged triangles listed) and
`decoration-optical-only.src --key vd-cmp-optical` (exit 0); the matching fixture `--key vd-cmp-match` (exit 0,
nothing listed). Accept steps: `frob --json scene accept <counter> --key vd-acc-counter` (exit 3, `FROB-REF-317`);
the same with `--waive region=counter --reason "test fixture" --key vd-acc-counter-waived` (exit 2, the region
listed and the waiver recorded); `<untagged> --key vd-acc-untagged` (exit 3, 317); `<optical-only> --key
vd-acc-optical` (exit 0). GLB swap (INV-HAB-02): the matching fixture compiled against `room.glb` (`--key
vd-cmp-room`) and against `room-gross.glb` (`--key vd-cmp-gross`) gives one `scene_id`; each pairing is accepted
before it is run (`e2e_scene_ready`; the gross pairing with its listed regions waived, reason "INV-HAB-02 swap
test", key `vd-acc-gross`); on each, `frob --json sim run <probe> --scene <id> --tier referee --seconds 1 --key
vd-run-<room|gross>` and `frob --json telemetry stills <run>` give equal state and still digests, and a lidar scan
of the same pose gives equal ray outputs; `frob --json scene show <id> --receipts` shows the deviation report
differing. `scene.accept` is BD-228's, which depends on this bead: until BD-228 is closed every accept step is
logged with `e2e_skip "needs BD-228"` (never counted as a pass, and `e2e_scene_ready` is then its logged no-op),
and BD-228's acceptance requires this script to run them and pass. Every line carries the charter fields (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block); `metric` lines give per-region
deviations in metres with evidence type (`bounded` or `sampled`), h and threshold; the refusal block; the waiver
record; the digests compared; on failure the first differing digest or region and the fixture paths. Final
`summary` names GATE-HAB-04. Reasons are logged as untrusted text.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: the 30 mm offset recovered within h. Negative controls: the visual-into-physics mutant; an untagged
decoration. Metamorphic relation: replacing the GLB by any other mesh leaves every result digest unchanged.
Determinism: deviation values identical across platforms (CPU reference path).

## Considerations

- The measurement is one-directional (visual to collision). Collision geometry with no visual counterpart (an
  invisible wall) is caught by the silhouette comparison fixture, and both must pass.
- Real-place imports have the largest deviations (repaired holes, simplified proxies); the waiver path exists for
  them and every waiver is visible in reports that cite the scene.
- A watertight proxy may serve a navigation experiment while gripper contact with the same object stays
  unresolved; the pack records which, and the deviation listing is where a reviewer sees it.
- Private scenes: the deviation map and stills inherit the private mark; logs carry digests, not geometry.
