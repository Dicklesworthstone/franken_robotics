## Background

Once scenes carry feasibility tags, two ways remain to mislead with them. One is to drop the awkward scenes: a
success rate computed only over scenes where success was possible looks better and means something else. The other
is to mix populations: a battery in which 5% of scenes were deliberately made impossible (to test that a robot
recognizes and reports an impossible mission) has a different ceiling from one with none. The rules of this bead
make both visible and mechanical.

- Every generated scene carries a tag for the family's nominal footprint: `witnessed`, `impossible` with the cut
  attached, or `unresolved`.
- A family may declare a quota of intentionally impossible scenes, tagged `impossible: intended`; scenes that are
  impossible without having been intended are tagged `impossible: incidental`.
- No scene is resampled because of its tag.
- Split manifests state the count in each class, scorecards state which classes entered a metric, and a success
  rate is always reported with its numerator and denominator.

Invariant INV-HAB-05: no scene is dropped or resampled because of its feasibility tag; the counts in a manifest
equal a recount.

## Scope

In: the quota mechanism in generators, the two impossible classes, the structural and audited guarantee of no
resampling, the count record a batch of scenes carries, the recount check, and the reporting rule. Out: computing
the tag (BD-234); split manifests and group rules (BD-235, N19c-11) and the recount of counts inside a split's
manifest at audit, which calls the check defined here (N19c-13); scorecard accounting in Arena (BD-254); report
rendering (BD-302). This bead supplies the counts and the check those consumers call.

## Technical approach

1. **Quota.** A family parameter `impossible_quota` q in [0, 1) (default 0). For integer seed s, the scene is
   *intended impossible* exactly when ⌊(s + 1)·q⌋ > ⌊s·q⌋, with q held as a rational p/r and the floors evaluated
   in exact integer arithmetic (never in floating point). This is a pure function of the seed and the quota, so
   the scene is still a deterministic function of (family, parameters, seed), and any N consecutive seeds contain
   between q·N − 1 and q·N + 1 intended scenes. Reason for stratifying instead of drawing independently (DESIGN
   arithmetic): the gate asks for the intended share to be within one percentage point of quota over 1,000 seeds;
   independent draws at q = 5% have a standard deviation of √(0.05·0.95/1000) = 0.69 percentage points, so the
   gate would fail by chance about one run in eight (a Binomial(1000, 0.05) count lies outside 40 to 60 with
   probability 0.127).
2. **Planting.** For an intended-impossible seed the generator applies one planted obstruction chosen by address
   from the family's list (for example a door narrower than the nominal base, a blocked aisle, a riser above the
   nominal step height). The planted feature is recorded in the resolved-value table.
3. **Classes.** After the tag is computed: `impossible` and intended ⇒ `impossible: intended`; `impossible` and
   not intended ⇒ `impossible: incidental`; intended and not `impossible` ⇒ the tag stands as computed and a
   generator finding `planted-obstruction-ineffective` is written (the plant failed to block; the scene is kept
   and counted under its true tag, and the finding counts against the family).
4. **No resampling, structurally.** The generator API takes (family, parameters, seed) and returns one scene; it
   has no retry loop and cannot observe the tag, which is computed after the scene's geometry is frozen. A batch
   request for seeds s₀ … s₀ + N − 1 returns exactly N packs.
5. **No resampling, audited.** A batch manifest lists every seed requested and the `SceneId` produced. The audit
   (`SceneBatch::audit`) checks: the seed list is the requested range with no gap and no duplicate; each
   `SceneId` regenerates from its seed (compared with a second generation of the range or of a sample of it,
   step 8); the class counts equal a recount from the packs' own tags.
6. **Count record.** `{ "witnessed": n₁, "impossible_intended": n₂, "impossible_incidental": n₃, "unresolved":
   n₄, "total": N }` with n₁ + n₂ + n₃ + n₄ = N, carried by batch manifests and by split manifests.
7. **Reporting rule.** A consumer that computes a success rate over scenes must state which classes entered it and
   must report numerator and denominator. `frob-scene` exposes `FeasibilityCounts` and a helper that formats
   "k of n (classes: witnessed, unresolved)"; a metric constructed without a class selection does not type-check
   (DESIGN: the selection is a required argument).
8. **Where a caller reaches it (DESIGN; three additions to two verbs, each added by this bead, with the input
   schemas changed additively and the help-schema snapshots under `fixtures/golden/verbs/scene/` updated in the
   same change; the registry bead VERBS-scene is asked to list them on its rows).**
   - The family parameter `impossible_quota` of the seven interior families, set with `--param
     impossible_quota=<q>` (a decimal or a fraction `p/r` in [0, 1); default 0; outside the range it is refused as
     any out-of-range family parameter is, naming the field). The generators of the seven archetypes (BD-225,
     BD-226) take the quota and the planting list from this bead.
   - The batch form of `scene.gen`: `--seeds a..b` (both ends inclusive, a ≤ b) in place of `--seed s`; giving
     both, or neither, is `FROB-REF-802`, exit 3. One call with one `--key` generates the scenes of the range as
     one job (progress `scenes` of N, the denominator the verb family already declares for a batch; the budget
     and admission rules of a `run` verb apply to the whole batch) and returns a batch identifier
     `batch:<digest>` and the batch manifest: family, parameters, the requested range, per seed the `SceneId`,
     and the count record. A repeated call with the same key returns the stored batch. A single-seed call is
     unchanged.
   - The read verb `scene.list` gains `--batch <id> --counts`, which returns the stored count record, the
     recount from the packs' own tags and the verdict of the stored part of the audit: the seed list is the
     requested range with no gap and no duplicate, every listed `SceneId` is in the store and verifies against
     its own content, and the counts equal the recount. A failed audit is a finding (exit 2) naming the missing
     or duplicated seeds, the pack that is absent or does not verify, or the count that differs.
   - Regeneration is not done by a `read` verb, which may only return stored or cheaply computed facts: a
     caller checks that each `SceneId` regenerates from its seed by generating the range, or a sub-range of it,
     again under a second key and comparing the two batch manifests seed by seed. `SceneBatch::audit` in the
     library takes the second manifest as an optional argument and reports the first seed whose `SceneId`
     differs; the unit tests and the gate run (N19a-45, on 50 seeds) use it that way.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Over 1,000 consecutive seeds with q = 0.05 exactly 50 scenes are intended impossible (49 to 51 for an
      arbitrary start), and each is tagged `impossible: intended` or carries the finding.
- [ ] A scene made impossible by accident (a planted clutter collision in a non-intended seed) is tagged
      `impossible: incidental`.
- [ ] The batch audit passes on a clean batch and fails on a batch from which one scene was removed, one in which
      a seed was replaced by another, and one whose count record was edited.
- [ ] `frob --json scene gen --family warehouse --seeds 0..9 --key k` returns one batch identifier and a batch
      manifest of ten `SceneId`s with its count record; `--seed` together with `--seeds` is `FROB-REF-802`,
      exit 3; `frob --json scene list --batch <id> --counts` returns the record, the recount and the stored
      audit's verdict without compiling anything; both help-schema snapshots are updated.
- [ ] The generator has no code path that reads a feasibility tag (dependency check) and no retry on tag.
- [ ] A success rate constructed without class selection does not compile; the formatted output always shows
      numerator and denominator.
- [ ] Counts in the batch manifest equal the recount on 1,000 warehouse seeds at q = 0.05 here; the same over
      1,000 seeds of each of the seven archetypes is GATE-HAB-02's row, recorded by N19a-45, and the recount
      inside split manifests is GATE-HAB-14's, recorded by N19c-15 through N19c-13, which calls this check.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `quota_stratified_exact`: q = 0.05, seeds 0..999: 50 intended; seeds 137..1136: between 49 and 51.
- `quota_zero_none`; `quota_pure_function_of_seed`: the same seed in two different batches has the same class.
- `iid_would_fail` (documented arithmetic test): standard deviation √(0.05·0.95/1000) = 0.689 points; under
  independent draws the count is Binomial(1000, 0.05) and P(count outside 40 to 60) = 0.127 exactly (the normal
  approximation without continuity correction gives 0.147), which is why stratification is used.
- `bernoulli_quota_mutant` (negative control): `is_intended(seed, q)` replaced by an independent addressed draw.
  The stratified rule gives 49 to 51 intended seeds in each of the twenty ranges starting at 0, 1,000, …, 19,000;
  the mutant fails the assertion (each range passes with probability 0.172, all twenty with less than 10⁻¹⁵).
- `batch_form_inputs`: `--seeds 5..3`, `--seed` with `--seeds`, and neither, are each `FROB-REF-802`; a repeated
  key returns the stored batch identifier; two keys over one range give equal `SceneId` lists.
- `stored_audit_is_cheap`: `scene.list --batch --counts` compiles nothing (the compile counter of the store is
  unchanged across the call).
- `intended_and_incidental_labels`.
- `ineffective_plant_is_finding`: a plant that leaves a second route open yields the true tag and the finding.
- `audit_detects_dropped_scene`, `audit_detects_swapped_seed`, `audit_detects_edited_counts` (negative controls).
- `resampling_generator_mutant` (negative control): a test generator that retries until `witnessed` produces a
  batch whose seed list has gaps; the audit fails and names the missing seeds.
- `rate_requires_class_selection`: compile-fail test.
- `rate_shows_numerator_denominator`: formatted string contains "of".

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-457 (counts of `witnessed`, `impossible` and `unresolved` scenes equal a recount; no scene resampled). Known
answer: 50 of 1,000 at q = 5%. Negative controls: the three tampered batches and the resampling mutant.
Determinism: classes are exact functions of the seed (class D0).

## Considerations

- "Intended" is a statement about the generator's plan, "impossible" a statement about the scene; they are kept
  apart so that a failed plant cannot inflate the intended count.
- Scenes used by the blinded qualification lane are generated from sealed seeds that discovery cannot enumerate;
  their counts are reported through the lane's handle, never their seeds.
- Risk RISK-15 (agents optimize the scorecard by moving failures out of the denominator): this bead's
  numerator-and-denominator rule and class selection are part of the mitigation.
- A quota above zero lowers the achievable success rate by design; reports must say so, which the class selection
  makes automatic.
