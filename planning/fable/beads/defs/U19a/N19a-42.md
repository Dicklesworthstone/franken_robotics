## Background

`scene.compile` turns source geometry into a scene pack through eleven fixed stages, each of which writes a
receipt: decode under limits; build the metric transform graph; tag; check watertightness and orientation and
compute signed distance; emit bricks, directory and pool with a per-brick error bound; emit analytic overlays and
their index; emit the region table and maps; compile fixtures and movables and settle them; check spawn poses and
tag feasibility; run the acceptance fixtures; freeze. A user sees none of that directly; what a user sees is a
command that either returns a pack with receipts or refuses with a code and a repair. This bead is the end-to-end
script that drives the real `frob` binary through the compile path on real fixtures, checks every receipt and
every compile refusal, and measures the field rows of the gate GATE-HAB-01.

GATE-HAB-01 (Known answer, M2; TARGET): compiled field against source geometry, error at most half a fine voxel at
10⁶ surface samples and never above the recorded brick bound; analytic shapes compiled to bricks exact to the
interpolation bound, and analytic overlays exact to round-off; quantization ripple on a resting 10 kg block at
most 1% of weight on every tier.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures, assertions, structured log, negative controls and the gate receipt for the rows it
measures. Out: the mechanisms (their beads); the brick-pool gate (BD-164); terrain generation (N19a-30); the
acceptance gate (N19a-43). The ripple row of GATE-HAB-01 needs a batched step and device tiers; the script
measures it on the tiers available at M2 and records the others as `not run`, never as passed.

## Technical approach

1. **Script.** `scripts/e2e/scene_compile.sh`: plain shell, sources the STD-E2E helper, seed-explicit, idempotent
   (a second run verifies sealed packs instead of rewriting them), exits non-zero on failure after writing the
   summary.
2. **Fixtures** (all `synthetic`, under `fixtures/scenes/`): `analytic-set.src` (sphere, box, wedge, torus as
   constructive solids), `threshold-room.src` (a room with a 20 mm threshold, a grate and a stair flight),
   `closed-mesh.glb` (a watertight mesh room), and the defect fixtures `open-cube.glb`, `mirrored-room.glb`,
   `depth-bomb.glb`, `nine-overlays.src`.
3. **Steps and assertions.**

| Step | Command | Assertion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/analytic-set.src --sync-wait-ms 0` | Exit 8 with a job handle whose progress total is 11 |
| 2 | `frob --json job wait <id>` | Exit 0 or 2; result lists eleven receipts, stages 1 to 11 in order |
| 3 | `frob --json scene show <id>` | Manifest validates; `field.bound_m` present; overlay, region and tag counts match the fixture |
| 4 | `frob --json scene accept <id> --only field-bound` | 10⁶ surface samples; maximum error at most 0.010 m (half a fine voxel at 2 cm) and at most the brick bound; zero violations |
| 5 | Probes on overlays via `scene show --probe` | Tabulated analytic distances reproduced to 10⁻¹² m |
| 6 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/threshold-room.src` | A 20 mm threshold reads as a 20 mm step; the grate is one periodic overlay; slot histogram maximum at most 8 |
| 7 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/open-cube.glb` | Exit 3, `FROB-REF-301`, witness is an open edge; repairs listed |
| 8 | The same with `--repair fill` | Exit 0 or 2; one repair receipt; unknown volume non-zero |
| 9 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/mirrored-room.glb` | Exit 3, `FROB-REF-302` |
| 10 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/depth-bomb.glb` | Exit 3, `FROB-REF-303`, observed depth 33 against 32 |
| 11 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/nine-overlays.src` | Exit 3, `FROB-REF-306`, the cell and nine overlays named |
| 12 | Recompile step 1's source | Same `scene_id`; no rewrite |
| 13 | `frob --json scene compile ... ` then `frob --json job cancel <id>` mid-run | No sealed directory for the cancelled job |
| 14 | Ripple: `frob --json scene accept <id> --only ripple --tiers referee,batch-cpu` | Ripple at most 1% of weight on each tier run; other tiers recorded `not run` |

4. **Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/scene_compile/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a
   human-readable transcript beside it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N19a-42"), `step`, `event` (`start`,
   `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
   `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and their
   thresholds (maximum field error in metres against 0.010 and against `bound_m`; ripple as a fraction against
   0.01; probe differences), `scene_id` digests where state is compared, and on failure the first differing value
   and the artifact paths needed to reproduce (the fixture, the pack directory, the receipt). `refusal` events
   carry code, message, pointer, observed, required and the ranked repairs. A final `summary` line gives counts of
   assertions passed and failed and the gate touched (GATE-HAB-01). Fixtures are synthetic; no private-scene data
   is logged.
5. **Gate receipt.** For the rows measured, the script writes the measured values (not the targets), the commit,
   the hardware fingerprint and the seed into the gate register entry for GATE-HAB-01, marking unmeasured tiers.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All fourteen steps pass on the reference host; the log validates against the STD-E2E schema.
- [ ] Each of the four refusals is produced with its code, pointer and ranked repairs, and exit code 3.
- [ ] The field-bound and overlay rows of GATE-HAB-01 are recorded with measured values; the ripple row is
      recorded for each tier actually run.
- [ ] Each negative control below makes the script fail and name the assertion.
- [ ] The script runs twice in a row with identical assertion outcomes and identical `scene_id`s.
- [ ] Cancellation leaves no sealed pack and no device resources.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The script's helpers are tested in `crates/frob-scene/tests/`: `receipt_order_checker` (rejects a receipt list
with stage 5 before stage 4), `refusal_block_parser` (extracts code, pointer, observed, required), `probe_table_
loader`.

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified above. Runtime target on the reference host: under five minutes (TARGET), dominated by the 10⁶-sample
field check.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-426 (field against source: error at most the recorded brick bound and at most half a fine voxel; overlays
exact to round-off; control: a brick with its bound halved fails), KA-427 (ripple at most 1% of weight), KA-428
(brick byte layout: 1,024 bytes of samples, 128 bytes of selectors). CX-188. Negative controls, each run by
pointing the script at a planted build or fixture:
- one brick's recorded bound halved: step 4 fails and names the brick;
- the threshold forced through bricks: step 6 fails (a ramp, not a step);
- the watertight check disabled: step 7 returns exit 0 and the assertion fails;
- overlay capacity silently truncated to eight: step 11 returns exit 0 and the assertion fails;
- a compile setting left out of the hash: step 12's companion check (changing the setting must change the
  identity) fails.
Determinism lanes: step 12 twice on one machine; debug and release binaries; worker counts 1 and 16; identities
compared with those pinned on the other platform.

## Considerations

- At M2 only terrain and simple rooms exist; interiors with fixtures, movables and feasibility tags exercise
  stages 7 to 10 fully at M3, where N19a-43 and N19a-45 take over. Until then stages 7 to 10 write receipts that
  say `not-applicable` or `skipped` with the reason, and the script checks for that wording rather than for
  absence.
- The 10⁶-sample check is the dominant cost; a `--quick` mode with 10⁴ samples is offered for the `smoke` battery
  and labels its summary accordingly; it does not write a gate receipt.
- The kill gate of the brick pool (measured deduplication below 1.5; fallback: dense per-variant fields behind the
  same directory interface) does not change this script: the field-against-source checks hold on both branches.
