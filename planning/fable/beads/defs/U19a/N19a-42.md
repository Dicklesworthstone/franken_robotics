## Background

`scene.compile` turns source geometry into a scene pack through eleven fixed stages, each of which writes a
receipt: decode under limits; build the metric transform graph; tag; check watertightness and orientation and
compute signed distance; emit bricks, directory and pool with a per-brick error bound; emit analytic overlays and
their index; emit the region table and maps; compile fixtures and movables and settle them; check spawn poses and
tag feasibility; run the acceptance fixtures; freeze. A user sees none of that directly; what a user sees is a
command that either returns a pack with receipts or refuses with a code and a repair. This bead is the end-to-end
script that drives the real `frob` binary through the compile path on real fixtures, checks every receipt and
every compile refusal, and measures the field rows of the gate GATE-HAB-01.

GATE-HAB-01 (Known answer, M2; TARGET): compiled field against source geometry, error at most half a fine voxel at
10⁶ surface samples and never above the recorded brick bound; analytic shapes compiled to bricks exact to the
interpolation bound, and analytic overlays exact to round-off; quantization ripple on a resting 10 kg block at
most 1% of weight on every tier.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures, assertions, structured log, negative controls and the gate receipt for the rows it
measures; the compile-side legs that earlier beads could not run (the per-stage scripts of N19a-10 to N19a-17,
which log `e2e_skip "needs BD-163"` until the driver lands, and the long-verb behaviour of `scene.compile` that
N19a-02 hands over: exit 8, `job.wait`, the same `SceneId` as a waited run, and an idempotency key reused with
different content). Out: the mechanisms (their beads); the brick-pool gate (BD-164); terrain generation
(N19a-30); the acceptance gate (N19a-43). The verb `scene.accept` is M3 (BD-228), so nothing here calls it: the
field rows are measured through the G1 binding of KA-426 that N19a-14 provides, and the ripple row of
GATE-HAB-01 through `sim.run` and `telemetry.query` on the tiers available at M2 (the referee and `batch-cpu` in
`f64` and `f32`); device tiers absent from the host are recorded `not run`, never as passed, and the acceptance
fixture N19a-25 repeats the ripple row on every tier at M3.

## Technical approach

1. **Script.** `scripts/e2e/scene_compile.sh`: plain shell, sources the STD-E2E helper, seed-explicit, idempotent
   (a second run verifies sealed packs instead of rewriting them), exits non-zero on failure after writing the
   summary.
2. **Fixtures** (all `synthetic`, under `fixtures/scenes/`): `analytic-set.src` (sphere, box, wedge, torus as
   constructive solids), `threshold-room.src` (a room with a 20 mm threshold, a grate and a stair flight),
   `closed-mesh.glb` (a watertight mesh room), `ripple-block.src` (a brick-only floor with no overlay, one floor
   region whose `material` and `condition` select an interface card of stiffness 10⁵ N/m, and a spawn region at its
   centre), and the defect fixtures `open-cube.glb`, `mirrored-room.glb`, `depth-bomb.glb`, `nine-overlays.src`.
   The block of the ripple row is not part of the scene: movable objects arrive at M3, and `sim.run` runs a design.
   It is the one-body design fixture `fixtures/designs/block-10kg` (`synthetic`: a rigid 10 kg box with a
   0.3 m × 0.3 m base and eight contact samples on that base), spawned at rest on the floor.
3. **Steps and assertions.**

| Step | Command | Assertion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/analytic-set.src --sync-wait-ms 0 --key k1` (every compile of this table carries `--key`; steps 6 to 14 use `k6` to `k14`, one key per call) | Exit 8 with a job handle whose progress unit is `stages` and total 11 |
| 2 | `frob --json job wait <id>` | Exit 0 or 2; result lists eleven receipts, stages 1 to 11 in order |
| 3 | `frob --json scene show <id> --receipts` | Manifest validates; `field.bound_m` present; overlay, region and tag counts match the fixture; stages 7 to 10 say `not-applicable` or `skipped` with the reason where the fixture has nothing for them |
| 4 | Field rows: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --release --test g1_known_answers -- ka:426` (the Gauntlet's run form; the entry is N19a-14's) on the packs of steps 2 and 6 and on `closed-mesh.glb` | 10⁶ surface samples; maximum error at most 0.010 m (half a fine voxel at 2 cm) and at most the brick bound; zero violations; class `Certified` for the generated pack, `Tested` for the mesh |
| 5 | Probes on overlays via `frob --json scene show <id> --probe x,y,z` | Tabulated analytic distances reproduced to 10⁻¹² m |
| 6 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/threshold-room.src` | A 20 mm threshold reads as a 20 mm step; the grate is one periodic overlay; slot histogram maximum at most 8 |
| 7 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/open-cube.glb` | Exit 3, `FROB-REF-301`, witness is an open edge; repairs listed |
| 8 | The same with `--repair fill` | Exit 0 or 2; one repair receipt; unknown volume non-zero |
| 9 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/mirrored-room.glb` | Exit 3, `FROB-REF-302` |
| 10 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/depth-bomb.glb` | Exit 3, `FROB-REF-303`, observed depth 33 against 32 |
| 11 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/nine-overlays.src` | Exit 3, `FROB-REF-306`, the cell and nine overlays named |
| 12 | Recompile step 1's source with no `--sync-wait-ms` (the CLI waits) under a new key, `--key k12` (the key of step 1 would return that job's stored receipt without compiling) | Same `scene_id` as the job of steps 1 and 2; the sealed directory is verified, not rewritten |
| 13 | `frob --json scene compile ... --sync-wait-ms 0` then `frob --json job cancel <id>` mid-run | No sealed directory for the cancelled job; `frob --json job status <id>` reads cancelled |
| 14 | Ripple: `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/ripple-block.src --key k14`; `frob --json sim run fixtures/designs/block-10kg --scene <scene_id> --worlds 1 --seconds 4 --tier referee --key rip-ref`, then `--tier batch-cpu --precision f64 --key rip-f64` and `--tier batch-cpu --precision f32 --key rip-f32`; for each run `frob --json telemetry query <run> --stream contact --channels normal_force --from 2s --to 4s` (the block has settled by 2 s) | Ripple = max over the queried steps of the absolute difference between total normal force and W = 98.1 N, divided by W: at most 0.01 on each tier run; the model binds to the pack through the floor region's material and condition with no further input; device tiers absent from the host recorded `not run` |
| 15 | `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/threshold-room.src --key k1` (the key of step 1 with different content) | Exit 7 with `FROB-REF-803` (key reused with different resolved content); the conflict names the existing job; no second job and no second charge (`frob --json job find --verb scene.compile --key k1` returns one job) |
| 16 | Run `scripts/e2e/scene_compile_decode.sh`, `scene_compile_transforms.sh`, `scene_compile_tagging.sh`, `scene_compile_watertight.sh`, `scene_field_bound.sh`, `scene_region_labels.sh`, `scene_overlay_primitives.sh`, `scene_overlay_index.sh` | Each exits 0 and none logs an `e2e_skip "needs BD-163"` line |
| 17 | Run `scripts/e2e/habitat_known_answers.sh --stage compile` (the registry bead N19-KA's script; its default stage is the registry stage only, and the compile stage, the box-room compile `frob --json scene compile … --key hka-1` checked against KA-426 and KA-428, is the hook this bead runs) | Exits 0; the compile stage ran and passed (no skip line for it) |

4. **Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/scene_compile/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a
   human-readable transcript beside it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N19a-42"), `step`, `event` (`start`,
   `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
   `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and their
   thresholds (maximum field error in metres against 0.010 and against `bound_m`; ripple as a fraction against
   0.01; probe differences), `scene_id` digests where state is compared, and on failure the first differing value
   and the artifact paths needed to reproduce (the fixture, the pack directory, the receipt). `refusal` events
   carry code, message, pointer, observed, required and the ranked repairs. A final `summary` line gives counts of
   assertions passed and failed and the gate touched (GATE-HAB-01). Fixtures are synthetic; no private-scene data
   is logged.
5. **Gate receipt.** The gate is run in the Gauntlet's form through the helper, `e2e_tier g1_known_answers
   GATE-HAB-01` (that is, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --release --test
   g1_known_answers -- GATE-HAB-01`), which executes the gate's entries that exist at M2 and writes one
   hash-named receipt holding the measured values (not the targets), the commit, the hardware fingerprint, the
   seed and the unmeasured tiers marked `not run`: the field entries of KA-426 (bound by N19a-14) and the ripple
   entries `ka:427@referee` and `ka:427@lockstep`, which this bead binds (the block design on the ripple pack, run
   in process: the same fixture and the same measure as step 14). The script asserts that the receipt's ripple
   values equal those of step 14 on the same tier and precision, digit for digit, and logs the receipt hash; it
   never records the value (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9; the linter's `self-recording` rule). A session that did not
   implement the compiler records the receipt as the M2 part with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
   record_gate -- --gate GATE-HAB-01 --receipt <hash> --part M2`. The record is partial by design: the clause
   "on every tier" is completed at M3 by N19a-25 on the device tiers.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All seventeen steps pass on the reference host; the log validates against the STD-E2E schema.
- [ ] Each of the four refusals is produced with its code, pointer and ranked repairs, and exit code 3.
- [ ] The field-bound and overlay rows of GATE-HAB-01 are recorded with measured values; the ripple row is
      recorded for each tier actually run, with the contact-sample count and card stiffness.
- [ ] The long-verb behaviour handed over by N19a-02 holds on a real compile: exit 8 with `stages` of 11,
      `job.wait` to completion, the same `SceneId` as a waited run, and exit 7 on a reused key with different
      content.
- [ ] The eight per-stage scripts of N19a-10 to N19a-17 run their compile steps and pass, with no skip left.
- [ ] Each negative control below makes the script fail and name the assertion.
- [ ] The script runs twice in a row with identical assertion outcomes and identical `scene_id`s.
- [ ] Cancellation leaves no sealed pack and no device resources.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The script's helpers are tested in `crates/frob-scene/tests/`: `receipt_order_checker` (rejects a receipt list
with stage 5 before stage 4, and one with ten receipts), `refusal_block_parser` (extracts code, pointer, observed,
required), `probe_table_loader`, `ripple_from_column` (a recorded normal-force column whose worst step is 98.1 ±
0.98 N gives 0.0100 and passes; one at ± 1.0 N gives 0.0102 and fails), `skip_line_detector` (a sub-script log
holding one `e2e_skip` line is reported, never counted as passed).

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified above. Runtime target on the reference host: under five minutes (TARGET), dominated by the 10⁶-sample
field check.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-426 (field against source: error at most the recorded brick bound and at most half a fine voxel; overlays
exact to round-off; control: a brick with its bound halved fails), KA-427 (ripple at most 1% of weight), KA-428
(brick byte layout: 1,024 bytes of samples, 128 bytes of selectors). CX-188. Negative controls, each run by
pointing the script at a planted build or fixture:
- one brick's recorded bound halved: step 4 fails and names the brick;
- the threshold forced through bricks: step 6 fails (a ramp, not a step);
- the watertight check disabled: step 7 returns exit 0 and the assertion fails;
- overlay capacity silently truncated to eight: step 11 returns exit 0 and the assertion fails;
- a compile setting left out of the hash: step 12's companion check (changing the setting must change the
  identity) fails;
- the ripple fixture compiled with the field quantized over ±10 m (CX-188): step 14 reads a ripple far above 1%
  and fails;
- an idempotency store that admits a reused key with new content: step 15 returns a second job and fails.
Determinism lanes: step 12 twice on one machine; debug and release binaries; worker counts 1 and 16; identities
compared with those pinned on the other platform.

## Considerations

- At M2 only terrain and simple rooms exist; interiors with fixtures, movables and feasibility tags exercise
  stages 7 to 10 fully at M3, where N19a-43 and N19a-45 take over. Until then stages 7 to 10 write receipts that
  say `not-applicable` or `skipped` with the reason, and the script checks for that wording rather than for
  absence.
- The 10⁶-sample check is the dominant cost; a `--quick` mode with 10⁴ samples is offered for the `smoke` battery
  and labels its summary accordingly; it does not write a gate receipt.
- The kill gate of the brick pool (measured deduplication below 1.5; fallback: dense per-variant fields behind the
  same directory interface) does not change this script: the field-against-source checks hold on both branches.
