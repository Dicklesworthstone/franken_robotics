## Background

`scene.accept` is the verb that decides whether a compiled scene may be used at all. Its gate, GATE-HAB-04 (Known
answer, M3; TARGET thresholds), reads: every acceptance fixture passes on 100 scenes per archetype; the reflected
room is rejected and the three-point control accepts it; the 6 mm case returns `unknown`. The gate has two halves
that pull in opposite directions, and both must be measured on the running system: acceptance must pass on honest
scenes at scale (seven archetypes, 700 scenes), and it must fail on each kind of dishonest scene. A gate that
nothing can fail validates nothing, so the planted defects are part of the script, not an appendix to it.

## Scope

In: the end-to-end script, its fixtures and planted defects, assertions, structured log and the gate receipt. Out:
the verb and report (BD-228); each check's implementation (N19a-09, N19a-21 to N19a-25); feasibility and identity
across platforms (N19a-45).

## Technical approach

1. **Script.** `scripts/e2e/scene_accept_gate.sh`: plain shell over the real `frob` binary; sources the STD-E2E
   helper; seeds are explicit (seeds 0 to 99 per archetype); idempotent; exits non-zero on failure after the
   summary. A job-array mode runs scenes in parallel through the daemon with the same log format.
2. **Part A, honest scenes.** For each generator family in {`home`, `apartment`, `office`, `warehouse`, `plant`,
   `retail`, `kitchen-commercial/galley`} and each seed 0..99:
   a. `frob --json scene gen --family <family> --seed <s> --key acc-gen-<family>-<s> --sync-wait-ms 30000` (a
      `run` verb, one key per scene; 30,000 ms is the largest admitted wait, `FROB-REF-802` above it), then `frob
      --json job wait <id>` when it returns exit 8;
   b. `frob --json scene accept <id> --tiers referee,batch-cpu --key acc-<family>-<s>` (device tiers added where
      present);
   c. assert `complete: true`, every one of the eight fixtures passing, no unwaived listing, and the feasibility
      section filled with a tag (BD-234; the section reads `not-run` before that seed lands, which is why the
      PASS is recorded here and not by BD-228).
   Fixtures checked per scene: metric calibration board (lengths within the brick bound); door-clearance gauges
   (go gauge of width w − 2b passes, no-go gauge of width w + 2b blocked, at every door); normal-direction probe
   (normals out of solids, floor normals +z within tolerance); support test (a unit block comes to rest on each
   support surface, on the referee); ray and visual registration (silhouette overlap at or above the pack
   threshold); quantization ripple (10 kg block on a brick floor, ripple at most 1% of weight); tile continuity
   (no gap or step at brick and tile boundaries); mutable-state leak (wet a tile in world i, reset, read world j
   and the next episode of world i: no trace outside the declared persistence).
3. **Part B, the three named known answers.**
   a. Reflected asymmetric room: `frob --json scene compile fixtures/imports/asym-room-mirrored.glb --key
      acc-cmp-refl` with its four-landmark sidecar (the registration runs in compile stage 2, N19a-21; real-place
      `scene.import` is M5) → exit 3, `FROB-REF-302`, det L = −1, off-plane marker residual 2 m, and no pack; the
      same source through the verb's preview, `frob --json scene accept --source
      fixtures/imports/asym-room-mirrored.glb --preview --key acc-prev-refl` → the same refusal and values.
   b. Three-point control: `cargo test -p frob-scene --test registration three_point_control_accepts` on the same
      landmarks → the naive routine accepts with zero residual (the control lives in the test tree and is never
      reachable from `scene.compile`).
   c. Six-millimetre doorway: `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/doorway-6mm.src --key acc-cmp-6mm`, then
      `frob --json scene accept <id> --only enclosure-selftest --key acc-6mm` → verdict `unknown`, outer overlap
      2 mm, inner separation 14 mm.
   d. Two-plane first hit (handed here by N19c-40, which runs the same fixture at kernel level through N19c-39's
      test at M2): `frob --json scene accept --source fixtures/scenes/two-planes.src --preview --ray <o>/<d> --key
      acc-prev-2p`, with the fixture's ray from `fixtures/rays/scaled_instances.json` → first hit the foreground
      plane at 2.000 m (not the background at 3 m), `complete: false`.
   e. `scripts/e2e/habitat_known_answers.sh --stage accept`, the accept stage of N19-KA's script (KA-429, KA-430;
      the script's default stage is the registry stage only, and this stage is the hook this bead runs), logged
      there as a skip until the verb exists. Its reflected-room case uses the preview form `frob --json scene
      accept --source fixtures/imports/asym-room-mirrored.glb --preview --key hka-2` (exit 3, `FROB-REF-302`,
      det L = −1, residual 2 m), since the room does not compile and there is no pack to accept; its 6 mm case
      compiles `fixtures/scenes/doorway-6mm.src` (`--key hka-3c`) and runs `scene accept <id> --only
      enclosure-selftest --key hka-3` (`unknown`, outer overlap 2 mm, inner separation 14 mm). The stage must pass
      with no `e2e_skip` line.
4. **Part C, planted defects** (each must make acceptance fail on exactly its fixture):

| Planted defect | Fixture that must fail |
|---|---|
| A door narrowed by three bounds | Door-clearance gauges (go gauge blocked) |
| A floor present only in the GLB | Support test (block falls through) |
| One solid with inside and outside exchanged | Normal-direction probe |
| GLB shifted by 10 cm | Ray and visual registration |
| A brick offset by 5 mm at a boundary | Tile continuity |
| Field quantized over ±10 m | Quantization ripple |
| Mutable pool indexed without the world address | Mutable-state leak |
| Scene compiled with a 1% unit error | Metric calibration board |

5. **Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/scene_accept_gate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a
   transcript beside it. Per line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N19a-43"), `step`, `event`, the exact command line,
   exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim Known answer, class, tier, determinism,
   identities, seed), each fixture's measured value with unit and threshold, report digests, and on failure the
   first failing fixture with its worst sample and the paths of the pack and report. A `metric` event per
   archetype gives counts passing each fixture out of 100. The final `summary` gives assertions passed and failed
   and names GATE-HAB-04.
6. **Gate receipt.** Per archetype: scenes run, scenes passing each fixture, worst measured value per fixture;
   the three known answers' observed values; the eight planted-defect outcomes; commit, hardware fingerprint,
   seeds. A PASS only if Part A is 700 of 700, Part B is three of three (with the preview, two-plane and
   known-answer checks d and e passing) and Part C is eight of eight. The script writes the receipt and the log;
   it never writes the gate's status (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9). A session that did not implement acceptance records the
   value with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-HAB-04 --receipt <hash>`; this
   bead is the gate's one recorder, and the fixture beads (N19a-09, N19a-21 to N19a-25) record nothing of it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Part A: 100 of 100 scenes per archetype have a complete report with all eight fixtures passing.
- [ ] Part B: the reflected room is rejected with `FROB-REF-302` at compile and through the preview; the
      three-point control accepts it; the 6 mm case returns `unknown`; the two-plane preview returns the
      foreground at 2.000 m; the accept stage of N19-KA's `habitat_known_answers.sh` (KA-429, KA-430) runs and
      passes.
- [ ] Part C: each planted defect fails its fixture and only acceptance of that scene.
- [ ] GATE-HAB-04 is recorded with measured values, commit, hardware fingerprint and seeds; this record closes
      the done-condition of the seed BD-228, which proves the machinery on twenty scenes and leaves the PASS here.
- [ ] The log validates against the STD-E2E schema and contains no private-scene data.
- [ ] A rerun reproduces every verdict; report identity parts are byte-identical between runs.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Helpers in `crates/frob-conformance/tests/`: `gate_tally` (700/700 required; 699 is a fail), `defect_matrix_
checker` (a defect that fails two fixtures, or none, is flagged), `three_point_control_is_isolated` (the control
routine is not linked into `frob-scene`).

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified above. Budget: the support test on the referee dominates; TARGET under four hours for 700 scenes on
the reference host with the daemon's job array, recorded in the receipt.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-429 (reflected room; control: the three-point check accepts the reflection with zero residual), KA-430 (6 mm
doorway `unknown`; control: a nominal-surface test says "clear"), KA-431 (gauges; control: a door narrowed by 3
bounds blocks the go gauge), KA-432, KA-433, KA-434 (control: a floor that exists only in the renderer fails),
KA-435, KA-458 (control: a pool indexed without the world address fails), KA-427. Counterexamples CX-184 to
CX-188. Negative control on the script itself: an acceptance runner that reports pass unconditionally passes
Part A and fails all eight rows of Part C. Determinism lanes: report identity parts twice on one machine and
across platforms; measurement parts per tier against its class.

## Considerations

- Some archetype scenes legitimately carry findings (a brick touching more than four regions); findings do not
  fail the gate, unwaived refusals do.
- Tolerances still to set (normal direction, continuity step, silhouette threshold) must be frozen before the gate
  run; the receipt records the values used.
- If the device tiers are not all present on the host, the gate receipt lists the tiers measured; the gate's
  wording does not require every tier, but the ripple row of the field gate does.
- Interim use before all seven archetypes exist: the script accepts an archetype list and writes a partial
  receipt marked `partial`, which is not a PASS.
