## Background

`scene.gen` produces interiors from (family, parameters, seed) for seven archetypes: home, apartment, office,
warehouse, manufacturing plant, retail store and commercial kitchen. Each archetype is a typed attribute grammar
(Building → Floors → Rooms → Zones → Fixtures → Clutter) with dimensional constraints (door widths, aisle widths,
counter heights, stair geometry) and a seed; geometry is emitted as constructive solids and compiled; dimensions
are drawn from the archetype's hazard registry. A generator earns trust only at scale: one good-looking kitchen
proves nothing about the thousandth.

The gate GATE-HAB-02 (Known answer, M3; TARGET) states what scale must show: for each archetype, 1,000 seeds yield
valid scenes: all constraints satisfied; every scene tagged `witnessed`, `impossible` or `unresolved` for the
nominal footprint; at most 5% `unresolved`; the share of `impossible: intended` within one percentage point of the
family's quota; and no scene resampled. The same seed gives the same `SceneId` on Linux and macOS. The pre-mortem
watches this gate's shares of `unresolved` and unintended `impossible` scenes as the early signal of "a scene looks
plausible and cannot support a robot".

## Scope

In: the end-to-end script over 7,000 scenes, constraint checking, tag and class counting, the quota check, the
no-resample audit, hazard listing, cross-platform identity, negative controls, the structured log and the gate
receipt. Out: the grammar and proxies (BD-225, BD-226), the hazard registry and verb (BD-227), the tag (BD-234),
accounting (N19a-26), acceptance fixtures at scale (N19a-43).

## Technical approach

1. **Script.** `scripts/e2e/scene_interiors_feasibility.sh`: plain shell over the real `frob` binary and daemon;
   sources the STD-E2E helper (`scripts/e2e/lib.sh`); seeds explicit (0..999 per archetype); idempotent; exits
   non-zero on failure after the summary. Header: `bead=N19a-45 milestone=M3
   labels=habitat,scene,gate,verb:scene.gen gates=GATE-HAB-02 seed=1 timeout_s=21600 needs=cpu,frobd,second-host
   plants=resampling-generator,narrow-doors,always-witnessed,bernoulli-quota,altered-identity`. The gate lane is
   7,000 compiles and its timeout is above the harness's one-hour limit, so it runs only with
   `--approved-envelope <id>` (the owner's written approval of the six-hour budget); without it the script
   refuses the gate lane and offers the quick lane. On the gate lane every `scene gen` call (steps a and e) cites
   `--envelope-scope gate:GATE-HAB-02`, so that each batch job is admitted and metered under the approved row of
   that scope (N36-20); a batch above the class in force for it is refused with `FROB-REF-761` before it starts,
   and the script logs the refusal, stops and writes no gate receipt.
2. **Steps per archetype A** (generator families `home`, `apartment`, `office`, `warehouse`, `plant`, `retail`,
   `kitchen-commercial/galley`; hazard registries by their registry names, `manufacturing-plant`, `retail-store`
   and `commercial-kitchen` for the last three, the pairing table of BD-227; with the family's
   `impossible_quota` q_A set explicitly, default 0.05 for the gate run):
   a. `frob --json scene gen --family A --seeds 0..999 --param impossible_quota=<q_A> --key sif-<A>
      --sync-wait-ms 0`, on the gate lane with `--envelope-scope gate:GATE-HAB-02` (step 1) (the batch form of
      N19a-26; one key per archetype) → exit 8, job handle; `frob --json job wait <id>`.
   b. `frob --json scene list --batch <id> --counts` → the count record {witnessed, impossible_intended,
      impossible_incidental, unresolved, total}, the recount and the stored audit's verdict.
   c. For each scene: `frob --json scene show <scene>` → constraint report, feasibility tag with bottleneck,
      hazards drawn, `dimensions_assumed`.
   d. `frob --json scene hazards <registry name of A>` → the registry with the provenance of every dimension.
   e. The batch audit: the stored part from step b (no gap or duplicate in the seed list; every pack present and
      verifying; the counts equal a recount from the packs' own tags), and regeneration on a sample of 50: `frob
      --json scene gen --family A --seeds 0..49 --param impossible_quota=<q_A> --key sif-<A>-again` (cited as
      in step a), whose 50
      `SceneId`s must equal those of the first batch (a second key, because the first would return the stored
      batch).
3. **Assertions.**

| Clause of the gate | Measurement | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| All constraints satisfied | Constraint violations reported by the grammar's checker, recounted independently from the compiled field (door clear widths by gauge, aisle widths by probe, stair rise and run from overlays) | Zero in 1,000 scenes |
| Every scene tagged | Scenes with a tag in {witnessed, impossible, unresolved} | 1,000 of 1,000 |
| Unresolved share | unresolved / total | At most 5% |
| Intended share | impossible_intended / total against q_A | Within one percentage point |
| Quota rule (beside the gate's clause; it is what catches a wrong sampling rule) | Seeds the generator planted an obstruction in: those tagged `impossible: intended` plus those carrying the `planted-obstruction-ineffective` finding | Exactly ⌊1000·q_A⌋ − ⌊0·q_A⌋ = 50 at q_A = 0.05, the count the stratified rule of N19a-26 gives for seeds 0..999 |
| No scene resampled | Batch audit | Passes; 1,000 seeds, 1,000 packs |
| Identity across platforms | `scene_id` of each seed against `fixtures/identity/interiors_ids.json` pinned on the other platform | Equal for all 7,000 |

   Additional assertions: every scene's `dimensions_assumed` is positive and equals a recount from its
   resolved-value table; every `impossible` scene has a named bottleneck; every drawn hazard dimension lies in its
   registry range; intended-impossible scenes are tagged `impossible: intended` or carry the
   `planted-obstruction-ineffective` finding.
4. **Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/scene_interiors_feasibility/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`
   with a transcript beside it. Per scene one `metric` line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N19a-45"), `step`,
   archetype, seed, `scene_id`, tag and class, bottleneck label, `dimensions_assumed`, constraint-violation count,
   wall time. Per command a `cmd` line with the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's
   `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). Per clause an `assert` line with the
   measured value, unit and threshold. On failure: the first offending seed, the differing value (for identity:
   the first differing component digest) and the artifact paths. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed
   and the gate touched (GATE-HAB-02).
5. **Gate receipt.** Per archetype: counts per class, the unresolved share, the intended share against quota,
   constraint violations, audit result, identity matches; commit, hardware fingerprints of both platforms,
   compiler version. PASS only if every clause holds for all seven archetypes. The script writes the receipt and
   the log; it never writes the gate's status. A session that did not implement the generators records the
   value with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-HAB-02 --receipt <hash>`, and
   the recorder refuses a receipt whose log says `scale: "small"`, lacks a caught control, or covers fewer than
   seven archetypes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For each of the seven archetypes, 1,000 seeds: zero constraint violations; 1,000 tagged scenes; at most 50
      `unresolved`; intended-impossible count within 10 of q_A × 1,000; exactly 50 seeds with a planted
      obstruction at q_A = 0.05 (the quota rule); audit passes, and the 50 regenerated `SceneId`s equal the
      batch's.
- [ ] All 7,000 `SceneId`s equal those pinned on the other platform.
- [ ] Counts in the batch manifest equal the recount (invariant: no scene is dropped or resampled because of its
      tag; the counts in a manifest equal a recount).
- [ ] Each negative control below makes the script fail on the intended clause.
- [ ] GATE-HAB-02 recorded with measured values, commit, hardware fingerprints and seeds; this record closes the
      done-condition of the seed BD-234 (which asserts the tag clauses on 100 seeds) and the 1,000-seed
      draw-and-represent clauses that the seven hazard beads N19a-31 to N19a-37 leave to this run: every drawn
      dimension in its registry range and represented by its stated mechanism, every Frontier-only row
      `not-represented`.
- [ ] The log validates against the STD-E2E schema; no private-scene data appears in it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Helpers in `crates/frob-conformance/tests/`: `share_within_point` (50 of 1,000 against 5% passes; 60 passes; 61
fails), `quota_rule_exact_count` (seeds 0..999 at q = 1/20 give 50; seeds 0..199 give 10; seeds 0..49 give 2),
`audit_gap_detector`, `independent_constraint_recount` (door width measured by gauge disagrees with a planted
wrong label), `bernoulli_plant_is_detectable` (with the plant's fixed stream key, at least one of the two
200-seed counts differs from 10), `receipt_covers_seven` (a receipt built from six archetypes is marked
`partial` and the recorder's check refuses it).

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified above. Budget: 7,000 compiles with clearance complexes; TARGET under six hours on the reference host
with the daemon's job array, recorded in the receipt; because that is above one hour the gate lane needs the
approved envelope named in step 1. A `--quick` mode (50 seeds, two archetypes; also what `E2E_SMALL=1` selects)
fits the one-hour envelope, serves the `standard` battery, logs `scale: "small"` with `gates_claimable: false`
and writes no gate receipt.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-464 (identity across platforms and the tag conditions, 1,000 seeds per archetype), KA-457 (feasibility counts
equal a recount; no scene resampled), KA-451 (doorway fits with total margin under about 149 mm are `unresolved`
before local refinement, which is why refinement is needed to stay under 5%). Negative controls, each a planted
build or fixture that the script must catch:
- a generator that resamples until `witnessed`: the audit finds missing seeds;
- a generator with a constraint bug (doors 2 cm narrower than their label): the independent recount finds
  violations although the grammar's own checker reports none;
- a tagger that always answers `witnessed`: scenes with a planted narrow door are not `impossible`, and the
  intended share is zero against a 5% quota;
- independent Bernoulli quota draws instead of the stratified rule: the quota-rule clause fails. (The gate's own
  one-point band would catch this build only about one run in eight, since a Binomial(1000, 0.05) count lies
  outside 40 to 60 with probability 0.127; the exact count 50 is hit by independent draws with probability
  0.058 per archetype and by all seven with probability about 2 × 10⁻⁹, which is why the script asserts it.)
- a pinned identity file with one entry altered: the identity clause fails and names the seed.
Each is run as `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE=<plant>` on the quick lane and must exit 1 with a failed assertion that names
the plant (`run-all.sh --verify-plants`). For `bernoulli-quota` the quick lane's 50 seeds are too few to tell
the rules apart, so that plant runs seeds 0..199 of its two archetypes (the stratified rule gives exactly 10
each); the planted draws are addressed and therefore repeatable, and the plant's stream key is fixed when the
plant is written so that at least one of the two counts differs from 10 (true of a random key with probability
0.98), which the helper test `bernoulli_plant_is_detectable` asserts.
Determinism lanes: the batch twice on one machine; worker counts 1 and 16; debug and release on a 50-seed subset;
Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- Within the milestone the warehouse and the commercial kitchen come first, because the milestone's scenarios need
  them; the script accepts an archetype list and writes a receipt marked `partial` until all seven exist.
- The unresolved share depends on the clearance mechanism's local refinement; if the clearance kill gate fires
  (one false witness or cut surviving a repair cycle, or more than 30% of doorway queries unresolved after
  refinement), tags come from the heuristic fallback and are reduced to `unresolved` where the heuristic and a
  fine brute-force grid disagree, and this gate must be re-read against that branch.
- The open question on dimension norms blocks this gate only for claims of realism, not for validity; the script
  asserts that the assumed count is positive precisely so that no report can cite these scenes as realistic.
- Retail checkout lanes (0.6 to 0.9 m) and apartment doors are where `unresolved` concentrates; the per-archetype
  breakdown in the receipt shows it.
