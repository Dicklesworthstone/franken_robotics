## Background

Habitat (crate `frob-scene`) supplies fixtures as ordinary articulated or prescribed models: a hinged door
with a closer torque law τ = τ₀ + k·θ + c·θ̇ (τ₀ preload, k spring rate, c damping, θ the opening angle), a
sliding door on a prismatic joint, a two-way swing door, drawers and cabinet doors, swing gates and
turnstiles, a lift with prescribed car motion and sliding doors, conveyors and escalators as moving
surfaces. A robot can be caught by any of them: between a closing leaf and its jamb, between lift doors,
under a returning swing door, in the nip where a belt runs under a guard.

Three consumers need to know **where** on a fixture that can happen and **how hard** the fixture closes:

- the failure taxonomy has a class `pinch` among the classes for the world and for perception, "reported
  only when the feature that can produce it is on in the scene or the model". Its monitor fires when "a
  link of the robot is inside a declared pinch zone while the fixture's closing force on it exceeds the
  hazard row's limit, or one link bears opposing normal forces from two surfaces of the same fixture";
- the runtime shield's reduced-order templates for a base enforce "keep-out zones, speed limits,
  door-pinch zones" as barrier rows;
- reachable-set shield tables use "a door pinch zone" as one of their worked boundaries.

The fixture library (bead N19a-07) carries the closer law and no zone, so the monitor's enabling condition
("a fixture with a declared pinch zone") can never be true. This bead adds the declaration.

DESIGN decision: a pinch zone is **derived from the fixture's own geometry and joint**, with the parameters
that cannot be derived declared in the fixture's parameter table with provenance. Rejected alternatives:
hand-drawn boxes in each scene (they drift from the door they describe when a generator changes a width,
and a retrofit edit to the door would not move them); detecting pinches purely from opposing contact
forces (the second clause of the monitor does that, but it sees a pinch only after it happens and gives a
shield nothing to keep out of); treating the whole swept volume as the zone (a robot passing through an
open doorway would be "in a pinch zone" for the whole crossing, and the class would mean nothing). Tier
Solid.

## Scope

In: the zone types per fixture kind and their geometry as a function of joint state; the closing-force
function; the fixture-table fields and their provenance; emission into the scene pack and the per-world
evaluation; the query consumers call; the telemetry channel; the `pinch_zones` block of `scene.show`.

Out: the `pinch` failure monitor and its thresholds (N24a-38); barrier rows and templates of the shield
(N22b-21, BD-338) and reachable-set tables (BD-440); the fixture models and closer laws themselves
(N19a-07); declared behaviours (N19a-08); per-world joint state pools (N19a-09); hazard registries and the
limits their rows state (the hazard registry beads; a row's limit is read, not defined here); injury or
force-limit claims about people (no such claim is made anywhere in this bead).

## Technical approach

**1. Zone types (DESIGN; `crates/frob-scene/src/fixture/pinch.rs`).**

| Fixture | Zone | Geometry at joint state q | Closing direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hinged door, cabinet door, swing gate | `latch-edge` | The wedge between the leaf's free edge and the strike jamb: a prism of the leaf height whose cross-section is the triangle (hinge-side excluded) between leaf face and jamb plane within `reach` of the free edge | Toward θ = 0 |
| The same | `hinge-edge` | The gap between the leaf's hinge-side edge and the hinge jamb, of width w_h(θ) = 2·e·sin(θ/2) for hinge offset e, over the leaf height | Toward θ = 0 |
| Swing (saloon) door | `latch-edge` on both sides | As above, for θ of either sign | Toward θ = 0 |
| Sliding door, lift door | `leading-edge` | The slab between the leading edge and the strike (or the opposing leaf), of thickness equal to the remaining travel x_open − x, over the door height | Toward closed |
| Drawer | `front-gap` | The slab between drawer front and carcass, thickness = extension | Toward closed |
| Lift car | `sill-shear` | The slab between car sill and landing sill while the car moves with doors not fully closed, thickness = level offset magnitude | Car motion |
| Conveyor, escalator | `nip` | A cylinder segment along the line where the belt passes under a guard or meets a roller, radius `reach` | Belt direction |

A zone exists only while the fixture can close on it: `latch-edge` for 0 < θ ≤ θ_stop; `leading-edge` for
0 < x_open − x; a `nip` whenever the belt speed is non-zero. Each zone record: fixture identifier, type,
the frame it is attached to (leaf or static part), its parametric shape, `reach` (default 0.10 m from the
closing edge, `assumed`), and a minimum aperture `a_min` below which the zone is considered closed (default
0.005 m, `assumed`).

**2. Closing force.** The force the fixture can exert on an object at a point p inside the zone, with no
contact model involved:

- hinged or swing leaf: F_close(θ, θ̇, p) = max(τ_closer(θ, θ̇), 0) / r(p), where r(p) is the distance from
  the hinge axis to p (lever arm). The static value uses θ̇ = 0. At the free edge of a leaf of width W,
  F = τ/W;
- sliding door or drawer with a return spring or drive: the declared `closing_force` (N), or for a
  prescribed automatic door the declared `drive_force_limit` (N); a prescribed fixture with no declared
  limit has an unbounded closing force, reported as `unbounded`;
- lift sill shear and conveyor nip: `unbounded` (prescribed motion follows its trajectory regardless of
  contact), unless the fixture table declares a `stall_force`.

The kinetic contribution is reported separately as the leaf's kinetic energy ½·I·θ̇² (or ½·m·ẋ²) at the
instant, so that an energy-budget consumer can read it; this bead makes no statement about peak force
during an impact.

**3. Fixture-table fields added (each with provenance; `assumed` in generated scenes).** `pinch.reach`,
`pinch.a_min`, `hinge_offset` (e), `closing_force` or `drive_force_limit`, `stall_force`, and
`pinch.exempt` (a list of zone types a fixture is declared not to have, for example a door with a finger
guard on the hinge side; an exemption carries provenance like any other field). A closing fixture whose
table lacks the fields takes the defaults and the pack counts them among `dimensions_assumed`.

**4. Emission and evaluation.** At compile, the fixture stage writes a `pinch_zones` table into the pack
(immutable, shared by every world). At run time the zone geometry is evaluated from the per-world fixture
joint state, so nothing new is stored per world. Query (DESIGN):

```rust
pub struct PinchHit { fixture: FixtureId, zone: ZoneType, aperture: Length, closing_force: ForceBound,
                      leaf_kinetic_energy: Energy, depth: Length }
pub enum ForceBound { Finite(Force), Unbounded }
pub fn pinch_query(pack: &ScenePack, state: &FixtureStates, body: &Capsule) -> SmallVec<[PinchHit; 2]>;
```

`depth` is how far the body's bounding capsule reaches into the zone. The batched form is a kernel stage
over (world, link capsule, zone) pairs after broad-phase culling by the fixture's bounding box; it is
enabled only for scenes whose pack has a non-empty zone table (feature `scene-pinch`), so scenes without
closing fixtures pay nothing.

**5. Telemetry.** Channel `scene.pinch` of kind `event`, with fields (fixture, zone type, link, aperture,
closing force or `unbounded`, leaf kinetic energy), emitted on entry and exit and at each requirement tick
while inside; channel `scene.pinch.depth_max` of kind `interval-extremum`. These are what the failure
monitor and a task requirement can read.

**6. Surface.** `frob scene show <id> --json` gains `pinch_zones`: one row per zone with type, fixture,
parameters, provenance and the static closing force at the free edge. Class `Tested`; determinism: the
table is D0, the batched query D2 per backend.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every closing fixture kind of the table yields its zones; a cart, a castor chair and a static part yield none.
- [ ] Hand case: a hinged door of leaf width 0.80 m with τ₀ = 20 N·m and k = 0 reports a static closing force of 25 N at the free edge and 50 N at 0.40 m from the hinge.
- [ ] With k = 10 N·m/rad at θ = 1 rad and τ₀ = 20 N·m the closing torque is 30 N·m and the edge force 37.5 N.
- [ ] `hinge-edge` aperture follows w_h(θ) = 2·e·sin(θ/2): for e = 0.02 m at θ = 90 degrees, 28.3 mm.
- [ ] A capsule placed in the doorway of a door held fully open is not in any zone beyond `reach` of the closing edge; the same capsule 0.05 m from the latch edge of a door at θ = 20 degrees is in `latch-edge`.
- [ ] A lift's `sill-shear` zone exists only while the car moves with doors not fully closed, with closing force `unbounded`.
- [ ] A closed fixture (aperture below `a_min`) has no active zone.
- [ ] The zone table is part of the pack's actors aspect: a typed edit that changes a closer torque changes the reported closing force and no geometry digest.
- [ ] `scene.show` lists the zones; the `scene.pinch` channel appears in a run on the self-closing-door fixture and is absent from a run on a scene with no closing fixture; scenes without zones show no change in step cost (measured, within noise).

- `door_edge_force_lever_arm`: the two hand cases above.
- `hinge_gap_formula`: e = 0.02 m at θ = 30, 90, 180 degrees → 10.35 mm, 28.28 mm, 40.00 mm.
- `zone_absent_when_closed`, `zone_absent_beyond_stop`.
- `sliding_leading_edge_thickness`: travel 0.90 m, x = 0.60 m → slab thickness 0.30 m.
- `swing_door_both_signs`.
- `prescribed_is_unbounded`, `declared_stall_force_is_finite`.
- `exempt_zone_not_emitted`: a hinge-side exemption removes `hinge-edge` only, and the exemption's provenance is printed.
- `capsule_in_open_doorway_not_pinched` (the false-positive control), `capsule_at_latch_edge_pinched`.
- `leaf_kinetic_energy`: leaf inertia 8.53 kg·m² (a 40 kg leaf 0.80 m wide about its hinge, m·W²/3) at 0.5 rad/s → 1.07 J.
- `batched_matches_reference`: 10⁴ random (state, capsule) pairs, kernel against the CPU reference, within the tier's class.

   carries its own key, `pz-run-<step>`; `--controller open-loop:<schedule>` is N15ap3-01's form, which resolves
   the schedule `stop-in-doorway` to its `frob.openloop/1` file, and `--telemetry` and `--seed` are `sim.run`'s
   rows in VERBS-sim); `frob telemetry query <run>
   --channels scene.pinch,scene.pinch.depth_max --json` (N24a-36): assert an entry event with closing force 40 N

3. The same with the door held open by a declared stop: assert no event.
4. A typed edit of the closer torque (τ₀ from 32 to 16 N·m), recompiled (`--key pz-cmp-door16`): the reported edge force halves to 20 N

5. A scene with no closing fixture: assert the channel is absent and the pack's zone table is empty; the same

Every line carries the charter fields (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall
time, the envelope's `evidence` block); the `SceneId`; zone rows; each `scene.pinch` event with its fields;
`metric` lines for closing forces in newtons and apertures in metres against their expected values and the step
time with its noise band; on failure the first differing value and the pack and run paths. Final `summary` with
counts.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Static lever-arm values: 20 N·m over 0.80 m is 25 N; 30 N·m over 0.80 m is 37.5 N; 32 N·m over 0.80 m is 40 N, which is the failure monitor's positive fixture ("a self-closing door with a 40 N closing force and a robot stopped in the doorway"; its negative fixture is "a door held open").
- Negative controls: a zone defined as the full swept volume (the open-doorway test fails); closing force computed with the leaf width for every point (the mid-leaf case returns 25 N instead of 50 N); a zone table keyed without the fixture's joint state (the closed-door test fails).
- Metamorphic: mirroring the scene swaps the door's hand and maps each zone to its mirror image with equal forces; scaling τ₀, k and c by a factor scales every finite closing force by it.
- Determinism: zone table D0 across platforms; query lanes twice, across worker counts, debug and release, per backend.

## Considerations

- The closing force is what the fixture's passive or prescribed elements can apply; contact forces during an impact are larger for a short time and are the business of the contact solver and the energy budget.
- `reach` and `a_min` are conventions with provenance `assumed`; a published building or machinery norm would replace them through the provenance-promotion path of the hazard dimensions.
- A prescribed fixture is an external energy source; `unbounded` is the honest value and makes any limit comparison fail, which is the conservative result.
- Cost: zones are few (two per door); the batched stage is a capsule-against-prism test per nearby link.
