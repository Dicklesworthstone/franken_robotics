## Background

The interior generators of Habitat (crate `frob-scene`) expand a typed attribute grammar, Building → Floors
→ Rooms → Zones → Fixtures → Clutter. Productions carry rational weights and attributes carry
distributions; every draw comes from a counter-based stream addressed by (grammar, seed, derivation path,
attribute name). Many draws are discrete: whether a zone has a wet patch, whether a cart stands in an
aisle, which of three door types a room gets, how many rack bays an aisle has.

The adversarial-world subsystem (Nemesis) searches for worlds in which a design fails and must say how
unusual each one is. Its world-density bead (N26a-03) attaches to each quantity of a world its nominal
density, "a card parameter with a density on its uncertainty support; **a discrete scene feature with a
probability mass from the generator**", and reports the ratio between an edited world and a typical draw.
Its sampling bead (N26a-24) builds a defensive mixture proposal over continuous germs and discrete
features with exact importance weights, which needs the nominal mass p of every discrete alternative,
because the weight bound w ≤ 1/ε holds only if the proposal q satisfies q ≥ ε·p pointwise. The grammar
beads own the draws and state no export of their probabilities, so both consumers currently mark discrete
features `undefined_for` and carry a partial density.

The export is possible, and exact, because of a decision the generators already obey. ADR-HAB-06:
infeasible scenes are kept, tagged and counted; a generator never resamples to hide a scene (INV-HAB-05:
the count of derivations equals the count of seeds). With no rejection step, the law of a derivation is
exactly the product of its draws. DESIGN decision: record the mass of every discrete draw **at its site**
in the pack, and compute family-level marginals exactly from the grammar by a generating-function
recursion. Rejected alternatives: estimating masses by sampling (a mass of 10⁻⁴ needs millions of scenes
and still has an interval, while the weights must be exact); exporting only family marginals (a discrete
edit acts at one place, and the mass that matters is the conditional one at that place); letting Nemesis
read the grammar's weights itself (a second interpretation of the grammar, in another crate). Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: feature keys; the site record with its alternatives and masses, written for drawn and for absent
alternatives; the mass of a typed scene edit relative to a base pack; exact marginals per family by
recursion; the consistency test against frequencies; terrain families' discrete parameters; the `draws`
form of `scene.show` and the `--feature-law` form of `scene.hazards`.

Out: densities of continuous quantities (card uncertainty, the density bead N26a-03 itself); the world
model, causal paths and legality of adversarial edits (N26a-02, BD-270); proposals, germs and weights
(N26a-24); the hazards-drawn list and incidence rows, which assert presence and not probability (N19a-38);
the grammar and its productions (BD-225, BD-226).

## Technical approach

**1. Feature keys.** Every discrete draw of a generator has a stable key
`<family>/<symbol>/<name>`, for example `kitchen-commercial/zone.galley/film`, `warehouse/aisle/cart`,
`office/room/door-type`. Keys are declared in the grammar beside the draw (a production group or an
attribute with a discrete distribution) and are part of the generator's version. A hazard registry row
that a generator realizes by a discrete draw names the key.

**2. Site records (DESIGN; an addition to the pack's provenance block, schema `frob.scene.draws/1`).**

```rust
pub struct DiscreteSite { pub site: SitePath,            // derivation path of the draw, e.g. /floor0/kitchen/zone[2]/film
    pub key: FeatureKey, pub alternatives: Vec<(AltName, Ratio)>,   // masses sum to exactly 1
    pub drawn: AltName, pub anchor: Option<TargetRef>,   // region, fixture or pose the alternative would occupy
    pub conditional_on: Option<SitePath> }               // the structural draw that made this site exist
```

One record is written for **every** discrete draw the derivation evaluated, including those whose outcome
was "absent": a dry zone records `film: absent` with its mass and the region where a patch would have been.
That is what lets "add a film patch in zone 2" be recognized as the other alternative of an existing site.
Masses are the exact rationals of the grammar (weight over the sum of weights of the group; the
probabilities of a discrete attribute distribution). Records are sorted by site path; the table is covered
by the pack's provenance digest, not by `SceneId` (it describes how the scene was drawn, not what it is).

**3. Mass of an edit.**

```rust
pub enum EditMass { Ratio { site: SitePath, from: Ratio, to: Ratio },       // p(to) / p(from), exact
                    OutOfSupport { reason: SupportReason },                 // the generator never draws this
                    NotDiscrete }                                           // a parameter change: a density, not a mass
pub fn edit_mass(base: &ScenePack, edit: &SceneEdit) -> EditMass;
```

A typed scene edit that adds, removes or swaps a feature is matched to a site by its target and kind: the
site whose `anchor` is the edit's target and whose alternatives include the edit's result. Then the ratio
is mass(result) / mass(drawn). An edit with no matching site (a cart in an aisle class whose grammar has no
cart draw; a film patch on a region that no production wets) is `OutOfSupport`: its nominal mass is zero,
which the caller reports as outside the nominal law and not as a small number. When the added alternative
brings further draws (a cart has a mass and a position), those are returned as the dependent sites and
continuous attributes the caller must also account for; a dependent discrete site's mass is conditional on
its parent, so the joint mass of a subtree is the product along `conditional_on` (chain rule).

**4. Exact marginals of a family.** For reports and for stratification, per feature key and family
parameters: P(at least one instance), the expected count, and the distribution of the count up to a cap.
Computed on the grammar, not by sampling. For a symbol A and feature key k define the probability
generating function of the number of instances of k in the subtree of A:

    G_A(s) = Σ_p w_p · Π_c G_c(s)        (the product runs over the children c of production p)

Children are independent given the production; a child repeated a random number N of times contributes
Σ_n P(N = n)·G_c(s)ⁿ.

with G = s for a site alternative that is an instance and G = 1 otherwise, w_p the normalized production
weights. The grammar's hierarchy has fixed depth and bounded repetition counts, so G_start is a polynomial
with rational coefficients computed bottom-up. P(at least one) = 1 − G_start(0); the expected count is
G′_start(1). The recursion requires draws to be independent given the derivation path, which the addressed
streams guarantee; an attribute declared `derived` (computed from others, not drawn) contributes no factor.
A grammar construct for which independence does not hold (a count tied to a drawn dimension) is declared
`coupled` and its marginal is computed by exact enumeration over the coupling variable or reported
`not-computed` with the reason; it is never approximated silently.

**5. Terrain families.** Their discrete parameters (which of the ten families, the tile type on a
curriculum grid, the presence of a gap or a step) get keys and site records the same way; a family with no
discrete draw has an empty table.

**6. Surface (DESIGN: two options this bead adds to two read verbs, each changing its verb's input schema
additively and its help-schema snapshot in the same change; VERBS-scene lists both, `--draws` on `scene.show`
and `--feature-law [--param <name>=<value>]…` on `scene.hazards`, each naming this bead).** `frob scene show <id> --draws --json` lists the site records of a pack (schema `frob.scene.draws/1`).
`frob scene hazards <archetype> --feature-law [--param <name>=<value>]… --json` prints the exact marginals per
key (schema `frob.scene.feature-law/1`) for the generator family that pairs with the archetype's registry name
(BD-227's pairing table: `commercial-kitchen` is `kitchen-commercial/galley`), at the family's default
parameters or at those given with `--param`, the spelling `scene.gen` uses for the same parameters. Both are
reads: they compile nothing and take no key. A terrain family has no hazard registry, so its marginals are
reached through the library (`frob_scene::feature_law::marginals(family, params)`) and its site records through
`scene.show --draws`; no verb form is added for it (its tables are small or empty). Class `Tested`; all masses
exact (D0).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every discrete draw of the seven interior archetypes and the terrain families has a feature key; a discrete draw without a key fails the generator's completeness test.
- [ ] Every generated pack carries one site record per evaluated discrete draw, including absent alternatives with their anchors; masses in each record sum to exactly 1.
- [ ] Hand grammar A (three zones, film present with probability 1/4 each, independently): P(at least one) = 37/64, expected count 3/4; `edit_mass` for adding film to a dry zone is 1/3 and for removing it 3.
- [ ] Hand grammar B (zone count 2, 3 or 4 with masses 1/4, 1/2, 1/4; film 1/4 per zone): P(none) = ¼·(3/4)² + ½·(3/4)³ + ¼·(3/4)⁴ = 441/1,024 and P(at least one) = 583/1,024.
- [ ] For a site with masses 1/100 (drawn) and 99/100, `edit_mass` returns the exact ratio 99 for the swap to
      the common alternative and 1/99 for the reverse; log₁₀(1/99) = −1.996 is the value the density bead
      N26a-03 (which depends on this bead) reports from it.
- [ ] Over 10⁴ seeds per shipped family (derivations evaluated by the grammar engine, not compiled), the observed frequency of each key's "at least one" event lies inside the exact binomial interval of the computed marginal at level 1 − 0.001/K, where K is the number of keys tested in the run (Bonferroni: the chance of any false alarm in the whole run is at most 0.1%, where a per-key 99.9% interval over 300 keys would fail an honest generator in 1 − 0.999³⁰⁰ ≈ 26% of runs); a mismatch names the key, the count and the interval.
- [ ] An edit with no site returns `OutOfSupport`; a parameter edit returns `NotDiscrete`.
- [ ] The site table changes when a production weight changes and the generator version with it; `SceneId` of an unchanged scene does not.
- [ ] `scene.show --draws` and `scene.hazards --feature-law` are reachable through `frob --json`, appear in the two verbs' help-schema snapshots, and compile nothing; `--feature-law` with `--param` gives the marginals at those family parameters, and with a generator family in place of the registry name it is refused with the pairing hint of BD-227.

`crates/frob-scene/tests/feature_law.rs`; the real grammar engine; hand grammars labelled `synthetic`.
- `site_masses_sum_to_one`: property test over every pack of 1,000 seeds.
- `absent_alternative_recorded_with_anchor`.
- `pgf_three_zones`: 37/64 and 3/4, exact rationals.
- `pgf_random_count`: 583/1,024.
- `pgf_nested`: a room present with probability 1/2 holding the three-zone kitchen: P(at least one) = 37/128.
- `edit_mass_toggle`: 1/3 and 3; composing add then remove gives 1.
- `edit_mass_out_of_support`, `edit_mass_parameter_is_not_discrete`.
- `dependent_sites_chain_rule`: adding a cart (mass 1/5) whose type is one of two (masses 2/3, 1/3) gives joint mass 2/15 for the first type against 4/5 for absent.
- `coupled_construct_enumerated`: a count tied to a drawn width is enumerated exactly; a planted independence assumption on it gives a different, wrong value that the frequency test rejects.
- `frequency_matches_marginal`: the 10⁴-seed check on two archetypes.

### Known answers and negative controls
- 1 − (3/4)³ = 37/64; (1/4)/(3/4) = 1/3; P(none) for grammar B = ¼·(3/4)²·(1 + 3/4)² = (9/16)·(49/64) = 441/1,024; log₁₀(0.01/0.99) = −1.996.
- Negative controls: a generator that resamples a zone until it has no film (the frequency test fails, and so does the derivation count of INV-HAB-05); a site table that omits absent alternatives (the add-film edit returns `OutOfSupport` wrongly); marginals computed with unnormalized weights (sums differ from 1).
- Metamorphic: adding an unrelated production elsewhere changes no mass of an existing site; scaling all weights of a group by a constant changes nothing.
- Determinism: tables and marginals exact, identical twice, on Linux and macOS, in debug and release.

## Considerations

- The masses describe the generator, not buildings: every weight is `assumed` until a cited source replaces it, and the table carries the provenance of each weight so that a rarity statement can say "rare under an assumed law".
- A scene from an explicit set, a compiled source or an ingested place has no generator law: its table is absent and consumers keep their `undefined_for` marking for it.
- Polynomial sizes are small (counts bounded by the grammar's repetition caps); the cap on the count distribution is the grammar's own maximum.
- If a generator ever gains a rejection step, this export becomes wrong; the no-resample audit is the guard and the two are tested together.
