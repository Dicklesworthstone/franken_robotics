## Background

A scene can contain assets made by other people: scanned objects, kitchen fixtures, whole interiors from an external
generator, or a research dataset a user supplies. What may be shipped depends on each asset's licence, and one
research-only object inside a pack makes the whole pack unshippable. RISK-11 states the exposure ("asset licences restrict
what can ship"; likelihood medium; signal: a pack whose licence join is not redistributable; owner Habitat lead) and its
mitigation: procedural generators first, licence manifests enforced at pack time, and GATE-SAFE-05.

The policy (DESIGN, with the licences as FACT from a research pass dated 2026-11-21, to be re-checked at admission):

- **Only permissively licensed assets enter the default pipeline**: the fully procedural generators of `frob-scene`;
  Infinigen Indoors (BSD-3) as an offline external generator; Google Scanned Objects (CC-BY 4.0) for household clutter;
  RoboCasa (code MIT, assets CC BY 4.0) for kitchen fixtures and layouts.
- **Research-only collections are supported as user-supplied imports and are never redistributed**: HM3D (academic,
  non-commercial), HSSD (CC BY-NC 4.0), PartNet-Mobility (non-commercial research terms), BEHAVIOR-1K (its own agreement;
  not used, import only).
- **Not used by default or reference only**: Objaverse-XL (licence per object); ProcTHOR (code Apache-2.0, dataset licence
  not verified: generator reference only); RoboBenchMart, MarketGen and the Isaac Sim 6.2.0 Warehouse Creator (licences
  not read: prior art only). An asset whose licence was not read is marked so and stays outside the default pipeline.
- **The starter pack ships only synthetic and redistributable fixtures.**

The reason for enforcing this in the pack format and not in a style guide: a licence that travels with the bytes cannot be
forgotten when a scene variant, a replay or a report is derived from the pack. Rejected alternative: a project-level list
of "allowed datasets" checked by review, which says nothing about a pack once it has been copied.

This bead is Habitat's half. Section 37 of the plan owns the general rule: the licence classes and the join over any
derivation graph (N37-17), the one export door with the export refusal `FROB-REF-733` and the notices (N37-18), the
private mark with `FROB-REF-731` (BD-305) and `scene.purge`. Asset-populated interiors themselves arrive at M5 (BD-399);
this bead makes the manifest and the refusal exist first, at M3, when `scene.import` first admits assets.

**Which code refuses what (DESIGN; the plan defines `FROB-REF-310` in the scene chapter as "a pack or report would export
a research-only or private asset" and `FROB-REF-733` and `FROB-REF-731` in the safety chapter, and does not divide
them).** `FROB-REF-310` is raised by one function, the pack export check of this bead, and only when the artifact being
written for export is itself a scene pack (a compiled pack, a scene variant, or a kit or starter pack that contains
scene packs): the offending entry is in the pack's own manifest. Every other export (a report, a run bundle, a still, a
replay, a policy, a certificate bundle, a build packet, a design) is decided at the export door of N37-18 from the join
over the artifact's lineage: `FROB-REF-733` for the licence set, `FROB-REF-731` for the private mark. Reasons: the plan
says a pack is refused "at pack time (`FROB-REF-310`, and §37)", so the pack is this code's subject and §37 has the rest;
a report is assembled from the artifact graph and holds no pack manifest, so only the lineage join can find the asset,
and its refusal carries the path to the root asset that the repair needs; and one request must get exactly one code. The
words "or report" in the registry text of code 310 are kept as the plan wrote them and are not a second raising site.

## Scope

In: the per-pack licence manifest (the blob behind `provenance.licences` in `.fscene`); the schema and loader of the
asset-source register `assets/licences/sources.toml` and the policy that decides which rows may be default-pipeline
rows; the admission re-check; the default-pipeline filter; the pack export check `ScenePack::export_check`, the only
raiser of `FROB-REF-310`, with its one call site for scene packs; propagation of entries to derived scene variants; the
licence-manifest check of the starter pack (join class and source keys).

Out, with the bead that has it:
- The one export door every exporting verb passes, the decision by licence class for every export kind other than a
  scene pack, `FROB-REF-733` with its lineage path, the assembly and embedding of the notices block (the `NOTICES` file
  of an exported pack, the Notices section of a report), third-party notices for binaries, the laundering battery and
  the measurement of GATE-SAFE-05: N37-18. That bead depends on this one: its door calls `export_check` for a scene pack
  and returns the refusal unchanged.
- The lineage join for non-scene artifacts (N37-17); the private mark's read and export rule `FROB-REF-731`, the private
  root, the path jail and `scene.purge` (BD-305).
- The values of each register row with its admission reading, import profile and tests: one bead per source, NEG-17 (the
  project's own generators, with the purity check of a generated pack and of the starter pack and the regeneration of
  the starter pack from its seeds) to NEG-27. This bead owns the row schema and the loader they are checked against.
- Importing one asset through the compiler, which writes its entry through this manifest (verb `scene.import`, asset
  lane, N19a-46); the per-asset catalogue and the population of interiors (BD-399); the provenance block that holds the
  manifest's digest (N19a-05); importer limits and fuzzing (BD-307).

## Technical approach

**1. Licence classes** (the five of the safety chapter, ordered from least to most restrictive for the join):
`redistributable` < `attribution` < `research-only` < `proprietary` < `unknown`. `unknown` is treated as the most
restrictive. The class of a set is the maximum of its members.

**2. The licence manifest (DESIGN).** Canonical JSON, schema `frob.scene.licences/1`, hashed into `SceneId` through the
manifest. One entry per asset contained in the pack:

```rust
pub struct LicenceEntry {
    asset: AssetId,                 // content hash of the asset as imported
    source: SourceKey,              // key into the asset-source register, or "user-supplied"
    licence: String,                // e.g. "BSD-3-Clause", "CC-BY-4.0", "CC-BY-NC-4.0"
    class: LicenceClass,
    redistribution: Redistribution, // Allowed | AllowedWithNotice | Forbidden | NotRead
    notice: Option<String>,         // attribution text carried into exported reports
    read_on: Date, admission: Option<AdmissionId>,
}
pub struct LicenceManifest { entries: Vec<LicenceEntry>, class: LicenceClass /* the join, recomputed on read */ }
```

Procedural content has one entry with source `frob-scene` and class `redistributable`. The entry travels with every pack
that contains the asset: a scene variant derived from a base (a clutter variant, a retrofit variant, a mirrored layout)
copies the base's entries and adds its own.

**3. The asset-source register.** `assets/licences/sources.toml` (DESIGN location; the repository keeps licence manifests
under `assets/`), one row per source. Fields of a row (the row beads NEG-17 to NEG-27 fill them in):

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `key` | string | The source key a manifest entry names (`frob-scene`, `infinigen-indoors`, `google-scanned-objects`, `robocasa`, `procthor`, `hssd`, `hm3d`, `partnet-mobility`, `behavior-1k`, `objaverse-xl`, `robobenchmart`, `marketgen`, `isaac-sim-warehouse-creator-6.2.0`) |
| `licence` | string | The licence identifier as read (`BSD-3-Clause`, `CC-BY-4.0`, `CC-BY-NC-4.0`, …), or `per-object`, or `not read` |
| `class` | licence class | One of the five classes of step 1, or `per-object` for a source whose class is resolved for each object at import and is never the class of a pack |
| `default_pipeline` | bool | True only for the project's own generators and the three permissive third-party sources (Infinigen Indoors, Google Scanned Objects, RoboCasa): four rows. For a third-party row it becomes true only after the admission reading of step 4 |
| `redistribute` | `always` \| `with-notice` \| `with-attribution` \| `never` \| `per-object` | What the licence allows; `never` for the research-only collections and for every row with `read = false` |
| `read` | bool | False for a source whose licence text was not read |
| `kind` | string | What the source is (`procedural-generator`, `external-offline-generator`, `object-collection`, `scene-collection`, `prior-art`, …) |
| `use` | string | The use the policy gives it (the "Use here" column of the source table) |
| `citation`, `read_on` | string, date | The source-register citation and the date the licence text was read |

A row may carry further fields that only its source needs (a separate code licence, a per-user acceptance flag, an
import profile name); the loader admits a field only if the row bead that owns the row declares it, and refuses an
unknown key or an unknown field. The register's `redistribute` value sets the `redistribution` of the entries written
for that source (DESIGN): `always` → `Allowed`; `with-notice` and `with-attribution` → `AllowedWithNotice`; `never` →
`Forbidden`; any row with `read = false` → `NotRead`; `per-object` → whichever of these the object's own stated licence
gives.

**4. Admission re-check.** Before a source's first use at a milestone, its licence text is re-read from the primary source
and the row's date and digest of the text are updated in the dependency-admission record. A row with `read = false` or a
date older than the admission of the consuming milestone cannot be used by a generator in the default pipeline. A
user-supplied import that names the row is still possible: its entry takes the more restrictive of the row's class and
the class the caller declares (so `unknown` for a row whose licence was not read, whatever is declared) and the row's
`redistribution`. DESIGN: naming a registered row means accepting the project's reading of that source, and a flag must
not be able to lift a restriction the register states; the per-source beads rely on this (an import that names
`procthor` or `hssd` cannot be exported, NEG-21 and NEG-22). A caller who holds a file from no registered source, or who
has read a licence the project has not, imports with source `user-supplied` and a declared class (`unknown` when
nothing is declared), and owns that declaration: reports say "licence as declared".

**5. Default-pipeline filter.** Generators and `scene.import` in default mode draw only from rows with
`default_pipeline = true`. A user-supplied import requires an explicit flag naming the source, and its entries are written
with `redistribution = Forbidden` when the source is research-only. A draw or import from a row with
`default_pipeline = false` (research-only, per-object, not read, or prior art) without `--user-supplied` is refused with
the worded refusal `asset-source-not-default-pipeline`: block `pointer` (`/source`), `observed` (the source key, its
class and `read`), `required` (a row with `default_pipeline = true`, or `--user-supplied`); ranked repairs: 1. re-run
with `--user-supplied` (with `--licence-ack` for a `research-only` or `proprietary` row), accepting that the pack can never be exported;
2. choose a default-pipeline source. Retry `after-change`; family `3xx` (a scene refusal), exit 3; nothing is written.
The number is the ledger's (`registries/refusal_allocations.toml`, N35x-02; requested there, no proposal, a bead never
picks one); the refusal is raised behind the one named constant `ASSET_SOURCE_NOT_DEFAULT` in
`crates/frob-scene/src/refusal/late.rs`, whose code until a number is assigned is the admitted interim `FROB-REF-802`
(the nearest existing meaning: an input that fails its schema, exit 3, nothing charged) with
`detail: "asset-source-not-default-pipeline"`, as BD-227 does for `hazard-registry-invalid`. Raisers: `scene.import`
(the forms of N19a-46 and BD-398) and the generators' asset draw in default mode (from M5 the populated-interior draw of
`scene.gen --populated`, BD-399). Every test and script asserts on the constant's code, so the assignment changes one
constant and no behaviour.

**6. The pack-time export check.** `ScenePack::export_check(&self, target: ExportTarget) -> Result<Notices, Refusal>`:
1. Recompute the join of the entries; a stored `class` that differs from the recomputation is refused as a corrupt manifest.
2. If the pack is marked `private`, or any entry is `research-only`, `proprietary`, `unknown` or `NotRead`, refuse with
   `FROB-REF-310` ("a pack or report would export a research-only or private asset"), naming each offending asset, its
   source and class. An `attribution` entry with no notice text is treated as `unknown` here, because the notice cannot
   be honoured (the same rule the export door applies to every other export kind, N37-18). Ranked repairs: remove the
   asset; keep the pack local. Exit code 3.
3. Otherwise return the notice items of the pack, one per `attribution` entry (source, licence identifier, notice text),
   sorted by source then licence. The check returns them and embeds nothing: the notices block and the `NOTICES` file of
   an exported pack are assembled from these items by the export door (N37-18).

Who calls it (DESIGN, with the reason in the Background): it is called exactly when the artifact being written outside
the local store is a scene pack, a scene variant, or a kit or starter pack that contains scene packs (once per scene pack
contained). The call site is the handler of `artifact.export` (BD-097) for an artifact of kind scene pack; when the
export door `frob_verbs::export::admit` of N37-18 lands, the door makes this call for export kind `pack` in place of its
own privacy and licence steps and returns the refusal unchanged, and the handler's direct call is removed. A report, a
run bundle, a still, a replay or any other artifact derived from the pack never reaches this function: it is refused at
the door with `FROB-REF-733` (licence set) or `FROB-REF-731` (private mark), each with exit code 5. A private scene pack
is refused here whatever scopes and flags the caller holds: the pack is the scan, and a private scan stays on the machine
that holds it; the scope `private:export` with its flag (BD-305) releases artifacts derived from it, never the pack.

**7. Starter pack.** A check, run in CI and by `check-contracts`, that every scene and fixture under the starter pack has
a licence manifest whose join is `redistributable` and whose entries all have source `frob-scene` (procedural content)
or belong to files authored in the repository and tagged `synthetic`. DESIGN: the plan's rule is "the starter pack ships
only synthetic and redistributable fixtures"; "redistributable" is read as the licence class of that name, and within it
as the project's own generators, so that the starter pack carries no notice obligation and every scene in it can be
regenerated bit for bit from its recorded seeds and checked instead of trusted (the purity check and the regeneration
are NEG-17's, which runs after this check). An `attribution` asset from a default-pipeline source (a scanned object, a
kitchen fixture) is therefore refused by this check even though the same asset may be exported in an ordinary pack with
its notice. Admitting a third-party fixture into the starter pack would be a change of this rule in both beads.

**8. Classes.** D0 (set arithmetic on declared data). The licence class is a fact about declared inputs, not a legal
opinion; reports say "licence as declared".

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every compiled pack carries a licence manifest; a pack with no manifest is refused by the reader of BD-162.
- [ ] The register loader accepts the row schema of step 3 and refuses an unknown key and an undeclared field; the register holds one row per source of the policy above (the values of each row are accepted by its row bead, NEG-17 to NEG-27), and the project's generators and the three permissive third-party sources are the only rows that can have `default_pipeline = true` (four rows).
- [ ] A pack containing one research-only asset is refused at export with `FROB-REF-310`, exit 3, names the asset, and exports once the asset is removed.
- [ ] A private pack is refused at export with `FROB-REF-310` regardless of its licences and of the caller's scopes and flags.
- [ ] `FROB-REF-310` has exactly one raising site, `ScenePack::export_check`, and that function is called only for a scene pack, a scene variant, or a kit or starter pack containing scene packs (a test over the code registry's raising-site list; a planted second raiser in a report handler fails it).
- [ ] `unknown` and not-read sources are treated as most restrictive; a default-mode generator cannot draw from them; an `attribution` entry with no notice text is refused as `unknown`.
- [ ] A default-mode draw or import from a row with `default_pipeline = false` and no `--user-supplied` is refused with `ASSET_SOURCE_NOT_DEFAULT` (interim `FROB-REF-802`, `detail: "asset-source-not-default-pipeline"`, exit 3), naming the source key, class and `read`, with the two ranked repairs, and writes nothing.
- [ ] A derived variant carries every entry of its base (property test over random derivation chains).
- [ ] The starter-pack check passes; it fails when a planted research-only fixture is added, and it fails when a planted `attribution` entry from a default-pipeline source is added.
- [ ] The export check of a pack with `attribution` entries returns one notice item per entry, in the stated order; the embedding of those items in the exported pack is accepted in N37-18.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-scene/tests/licence.rs`, `cargo test -p frob-scene licence`; real manifests; stand-in assets labelled `synthetic`.
- `join_is_max`: {redistributable, attribution} → attribution; adding research-only → research-only; adding unknown → unknown.
- `export_refused_310_names_asset`: three assets, one `CC-BY-NC-4.0` research-only: refusal lists exactly that asset.
- `private_refused_310`: also with a token holding `private:export` and the per-call flag.
- `notices_returned`: two CC-BY assets yield two notice items from the check, sorted by source then licence; `attribution_without_notice_is_unknown_310`.
- `code_310_single_raising_site`: the raising-site list of `FROB-REF-310` in the refusal registry is `ScenePack::export_check` alone.
- `register_row_schema`: a row with every field of step 3 loads; a row with an undeclared field, an unknown `redistribute` value or `default_pipeline = true` on a research-only row is refused; `redistribute_maps_to_entry_redistribution` (the five values of the mapping).
- `default_pipeline_filter`: a generator configured with an asset keyed to a research-only row of a test register (`synthetic`; the real rows are entered by NEG-17 to NEG-27, which depend on this bead) is refused in default mode with the code of `ASSET_SOURCE_NOT_DEFAULT` (interim `FROB-REF-802`, `detail = "asset-source-not-default-pipeline"`, `observed` naming the row), and nothing is written; with the user-supplied flag the entry is written `Forbidden`.
- `not_read_is_outside_default`: a row with `read = false` cannot be drawn.
- `variant_inherits_entries`: 1,000 random chains of derived variants; the entry set of each descendant is a superset of its ancestors'.
- `stored_class_must_match_recompute`: a manifest whose stored class is weaker than its entries is refused.
- `starter_pack_only_redistributable`: a starter pack of generated scenes and `synthetic` files passes; with one research-only stand-in it fails; with one `attribution` stand-in from a default-pipeline source it fails.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/scene_licences.sh` (STD-E2E helper; header `bead=N19b-02 milestone=M3 labels=habitat,safety,scene seed=1 needs=cpu plants=stored-class-trusted,unknown-permissive`; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/scene_licences/`). Every `run` verb call carries its own `--key`. Steps: `frob --json scene compile fixtures/hab/licences/room_three_assets.src --key sl-compile-1` (a `synthetic` room source with two procedural props and one stand-in asset declared research-only in its source file: the compiler writes the three entries through this bead's manifest; no import verb is needed, so the script does not wait on `scene.import`, N19a-46, which depends on this bead); `frob --json scene show <id>` (asserts the manifest lists three entries and class `research-only`); `frob --json artifact export <id> --out exports/ --key sl-export-1` on the scene pack (`artifact.export` is a `run` verb of BD-097; asserts exit 3, refusal code `FROB-REF-310`, the asset named, the repairs "remove the asset" and "keep the pack local" in that order); `frob --json scene compile fixtures/hab/licences/room_two_assets.src --key sl-compile-2` (the same room without the stand-in, with one `attribution` stand-in in its place) and `frob --json artifact export <id> --out exports/ --key sl-export-2` (exit 0, one notice item in the envelope's result); the same export for a pack marked `private` in the scratch store (exit 3, `FROB-REF-310`, whatever scopes the session holds); the two planted builds through `e2e_expect_caught` (`stored-class-trusted`: a manifest whose stored class was edited to `redistributable` exports; `unknown-permissive`: a pack with an `unknown` entry exports). The script exports the scene pack only; exports of a run, a still or a report over the same scene, which must answer `FROB-REF-733`, are the licence battery of `scripts/e2e/safe_private_licence.sh` (N37-21). Each line logs `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block, the refusal code with its pointer and ranked repairs, digests of the manifests, and on failure the first differing entry and the pack path; the `summary` gives assertion counts, refusals expected and controls caught, and claims no gate. No asset content is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-832: over random derivation graphs the licence set equals the join of the inputs and a research-only asset cannot enter an exported report or pack (exact; the graph-wide form is GATE-SAFE-05, measured by N37-18 and recorded by BD-305; this bead supplies the scene-pack row, the one row of that battery whose expected code is `FROB-REF-310`).
- KA-827: a private, research-only scene, a replay of it and a still of the replay are private and research-only at every step (exact; BD-305).
- Negative controls: a build whose export check reads the stored class without recomputing must fail `stored_class_must_match_recompute`; a build that treats `unknown` as permissive must fail `join_is_max`.
- Determinism: results are set arithmetic; twice on one machine and across platforms give identical refusals.

## Considerations

- OPEN-SAFE-2 (whether policy weights trained in a private or research-only scene inherit its mark and licence set; default: they inherit both; owner: project owner) is decided outside this bead; the manifest type must be reusable for policy artifacts.
- The licences in the register are FACT from a dated reading and are re-checked at admission; a changed upstream licence is a register change with a new date, never an edit in place of history.
- Legal judgement is out of scope: the tool enforces declared classes and refuses on doubt.
- The research-only import path must not write asset bytes into any shared cache; storage under the private root is BD-305's.
- What would make us revisit: a need to ship a populated demo scene, which would require an `attribution`-only populated archetype built from the permissive sources.
