## Background

A place changes because a robot is in it: a wheel drags oil out of a patch, a carried pot sloshes, a castor pushes a rug
into a fold, a cord wraps an axle. A scene whose only state is rigid geometry and a friction map cannot produce these
failures. The strong response, coupled fluid, cloth and cable solvers in every training world, is refuted by arithmetic
(N19b-04 carries the calculations). ADR-HAB-07 therefore admits living matter in three tiers and keeps only the Solid tier
on the critical path. Reason: a contact-level film law and rigid proxies stand in for coupled matter at negligible cost;
whether they reproduce the failures of coupled models is decided by a pilot (N19b-16), not assumed. It serves GOAL-33
(model worlds the robot changes: a film-friction law and rigid proxies in the core; advected films, slosh, cables and cloth
behind flags) and BET-36 (living worlds change decisions).

| Tier | Mechanism | State it adds | Evidence class of its outputs | First milestone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solid [S] | Film-friction patches: a contact-level law in which friction on a wetted region depends on dwell time, load and pad size | One thickness value per contact sample | Estimated | M3 |
| Solid [S] | Rigid proxies: a rug as a thin free slab, a cord as a fixed ridge or a free rod, clutter and toys as rigid bodies | Ordinary rigid bodies | Tested | M3 |
| Frontier [F] | Robot-advected films: a 2.5-dimensional film field the robot spreads and carries | Sparse film tiles per world, one reservoir per contact body | Estimated | M6 |
| Frontier [F] | Payload slosh by an equivalent mechanical model | One to three sprung masses per horizontal axis | Estimated; Known answer for modal frequency | M6 |
| Frontier [F] | Articulated proxies: a cord as a chain of rigid links, a rug as hinged plates | Articulated bodies | Tested | M6 |
| Moonshot [M] | Coupled islands: free-surface lattice fluid, implicit cloth, Cosserat rod, on the CPU referee for finalists only | An island beside one world | Estimated | After M6, behind a flag |

Nothing here is claimed as new. FACT (public documentation read on 2026-11-21, to be re-read from the primary source before
a gate cites it): incumbent engines ship cables, cloth and particle solvers coupled to rigid robots, and one documents that
it does not guarantee determinism for scenes with non-rigid bodies. The claim made is narrower: the Solid and Frontier
mechanisms stay inside the project's determinism classes because they are rigid bodies, joints and integer-indexed fields.

This bead is the common frame for all six rows: how a scene pack declares living matter, how a tier is admitted or refused
for a run, and how a fallback is substituted and recorded when a Frontier mechanism is absent or was killed. The mechanisms
themselves are other beads.

## Scope

In: the `living` array of the `.fscene` manifest (schema, typed value, validation); the tier and feature-flag admission
table; the fallback substitution rule and its record in the episode manifest; the evidence class attached to each
mechanism's outputs; the rule that an island never enters a training batch.

Out: the film patch and its thickness state (N19b-05; the law's cone row is BD-230); rigid-proxy bodies (BD-231); advected
films (N19b-08, N19b-09); slosh (N19b-13); chain cords and hinged rugs (N19b-17); islands (N19b-21); the pool mechanics of
per-world state and the leak fixture (N19a-09).

## Technical approach

**1. Declarations (DESIGN).** Each element of `living`:

```rust
pub enum LivingKind { FilmPatch, RigidProxy(RigidProxyKind), AdvectedFilm, SloshPayload, ChainCord, HingedRug,
                      Island(IslandKind) }
pub enum RigidProxyKind { RugSlab, CordRidge, CordRod, Clutter }
pub enum IslandKind { FluidBox, Cloth, Rod }
pub struct LivingDecl {
    kind: LivingKind, tier: Tier,                 // S, F or M; fixed by kind, checked on read
    target: Target,                               // region id, movable id, payload id or a box
    params: Params,                               // per kind; every value with provenance
    cards: Vec<CardRef>,                          // fluid, interface or payload cards
    solid_alternative: Option<Box<LivingDecl>>,   // required for every F or M declaration
}
```

Example from the plan's manifest: `{ "kind": "film-patch", "tier": "S", "region": 31, "thickness_m": 0.0004, "fluid": "water" }`.
A Frontier or Moonshot declaration must carry its Solid alternative: a chain cord carries a fixed ridge or a free rod, a
hinged rug carries a free slab, an advected film carries a static film patch, a slosh payload carries rigid ballast with
the same total mass, an island carries the proxy it is an oracle for. This mirrors the hazard registries, whose rows
marked Frontier are represented by the Frontier mechanism and by the Solid alternative beside it until the mechanism lands.

**2. Feature flags.** Every Frontier and Moonshot mechanism in a mixed crate is gated by a named feature that no default
build enables: `frontier-film-advect`, `frontier-slosh`, `frontier-proxies`, `moonshot-islands` in `frob-scene`.

**3. Admission, as numbered steps, when a pack is bound to a run** (inputs: the declarations, the binary's features, the
recorded kill-gate decisions in `registries/gates.toml`, the run's tier and purpose):
1. A Solid declaration is always admitted.
2. A Frontier declaration is admitted when its feature is built and its kill gate (KILL-ADVECT, KILL-SLOSH, KILL-PROXY)
   has no `killed: fallback adopted` record. Otherwise its `solid_alternative` is substituted. One fallback keeps the
   mechanism for some purposes: when KILL-PROXY's decision in force is `fired` with the form "articulated proxies in
   evaluation batches only" (N19b-19), a chain cord or hinged rug is admitted for runs of purpose `evaluation`,
   `oracle-check` or `counterexample-verification` and substituted, with reason `purpose-not-eligible`, in `training`
   and `throughput` runs. The form is read from the decision entry (a field `fallback_form`, requested of N40-03); a
   `fired` decision without it is read as the form "not at all" (fail-closed: substituted for every purpose).
3. A Moonshot island is admitted only on the CPU referee, only for a run whose purpose is `oracle-check` or
   `counterexample-verification`, only with `moonshot-islands` built, only while KILL-LIVING has no `killed: fallback
   adopted` record (after that record, only the oracle fixtures that already exist in the siblings run, under the same
   purposes; any other island declaration substitutes its alternative with reason `killed: KILL-LIVING`), and never in a
   training batch. A training or throughput run that names an island is refused, whatever the build; other runs
   substitute the alternative.
4. Every substitution is written to the episode manifest as `living_substitutions: [{declared, used, reason}]`, with reason
   `feature-not-built`, `killed: <gate>`, `tier-not-eligible` or `purpose-not-eligible`. A caller may pass `--require-living-tier F` (or `M`) to
   turn a substitution into a refusal instead.
5. A declaration whose parameters are outside its model's validity is refused by the owning mechanism (for films,
   `FROB-REF-307`), not substituted.
6. **Refusal codes (the ruling of N32-REF, the owner of the `7xx` block).** The refusals of steps 3 and 4 split by
   cause, with disjoint reason sets, both exit 5 and both with the block fields `mechanism` (the `LivingKind`),
   `declared_tier`, `reason` and `run_purpose`:
   - `FROB-REF-724` ("the capability is not in this build or was killed at its kill gate", retry `never`), reason
     `feature-not-built` or `killed: <gate>`: a substitution that `--require-living-tier` forbids, for a mechanism
     whose feature is off in this build or whose kill gate has a `killed: fallback adopted` record. Ranked repairs:
     (1) drop `--require-living-tier` and accept the recorded Solid alternative; (2) build with the named feature
     (not offered when the reason is `killed`).
   - `FROB-REF-726` (DESIGN, new in N32-REF: "a mechanism that is in this build is not admitted for the run's
     declared purpose or tier", retry `after-change`), reason `island-in-training`, `tier-not-eligible` or
     `purpose-not-eligible` (the third proposed here and requested of N32-REF's row): a training or throughput run
     that names an island; a forced tier on a tier the mechanism's admission rule excludes; a forced tier in a run
     whose purpose a kill decision's fallback form excludes (step 2; the block adds `decision`, the ADR path). Ranked
     repairs: (1) accept the recorded Solid alternative (drop `--require-living-tier`); (2) run the island under
     purpose `oracle-check` or `counterexample-verification` on the CPU referee, or, for `purpose-not-eligible`, run
     under purpose `evaluation`.
   The purpose check runs first, so a training run that names an island answers 726 `island-in-training` on every
   build, whether or not `moonshot-islands` is built: the repair is to change the purpose, never to build a feature
   (DESIGN reading of the ruling, whose reason sets put `island-in-training` only in 726; confirmation requested of
   N32-REF). An earlier draft used 724 for all four reasons; N32-REF ruled that 724 would tell an agent the island
   is not implemented, which is false when it is built.
7. **Run inputs (DESIGN; the VERBS-sim rows this bead owns from M3).** `--purpose
   training|throughput|evaluation|oracle-check|counterexample-verification` on `sim.run` and `sim.estimate`, default
   `evaluation`, recorded in the manifest: one input for two requests. N15a-30 owns `training` and `evaluation` from M2
   (it replaces that bead's `--mode train|eval`; a `training` run draws the thermal start); this bead adds `throughput`,
   `oracle-check` and `counterexample-verification` and the admission of steps 2 and 3, which reads the value. An
   unknown value is 802. `--require-living-tier F|M` on `sim.run`: the living-world tier the run must use; every
   substitution step 4 would make becomes the refusal of step 6 instead (724 or 726, by reason). `sim.estimate` applies
   the same admission to the request it prices: it reports the `living_substitutions` the run would record, and a
   request the admission refuses gets the run's code (an admission refusal is not a capacity finding, so it is not
   folded into `fits: false`). Requested of VERBS-sim: `--require-living-tier` on `sim.estimate` and its 724 and 726
   attributed to this bead at M3; of N32-REF: `sim.estimate` among 726's raising sites.

**4. Evidence classes.** The envelope of any result computed with a living mechanism takes, through the claim product, the
class of the table above: `Estimated` for film friction, advected films, slosh and islands; `Tested` for rigid and
articulated proxies. Because the claim product takes the weakest input, one film patch makes a whole episode's friction-
dependent metrics `Estimated`. Reality is `no-claim` everywhere until measured data is imported. When KILL-LIVING's
fallback is in force, reports that involve deformable matter say that it is represented by proxy.

**5. State.** Film thickness per contact sample, film tiles, sloshing payload states and proxy poses are per-world mutable
state from fixed-capacity pools, addressed by world, and part of every snapshot. INV-HAB-03: such state never affects
another world, and never a later episode unless the task declares persistence for that world address.

**6. Determinism.** Declarations are data (D0). Admission is a pure function of declared inputs and recorded decisions.

**7. Layout.** `crates/frob-scene/src/living/{decl.rs, admit.rs}`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The `living` array round-trips through the `.fscene` reader and writer; an unknown kind or a tier that disagrees with its kind is refused.
- [ ] A Frontier or Moonshot declaration without a Solid alternative is refused at compile.
- [ ] The admission table behaves as specified for every (kind, feature built or not, gate killed or not, run purpose) combination, and every substitution appears in the episode manifest.
- [ ] An island named by a training run is refused with `FROB-REF-726`, exit 5, reason `island-in-training`, retry `after-change`, on every build (with and without `moonshot-islands`); `--require-living-tier F` turns a substitution into a refusal whose `reason` names the substitution it replaced: `FROB-REF-724` for `feature-not-built` or `killed: <gate>`, `FROB-REF-726` for `tier-not-eligible`.
- [ ] With a `killed: fallback adopted` record for KILL-LIVING, an island declaration outside the existing sibling oracle fixtures substitutes its alternative with reason `killed: KILL-LIVING`.
- [ ] `--purpose` accepts its five values on `sim.run` and `sim.estimate`, defaults to `evaluation`, is recorded in the manifest and refuses any other value with 802; `sim.estimate` reports the substitutions and refusals the run would get.
- [ ] With a planted KILL-PROXY decision `fired` in the form "evaluation batches only", a chain cord is admitted in an `evaluation` run and substituted with reason `purpose-not-eligible` in a `training` run; with `--require-living-tier F` the training run is refused with `FROB-REF-726`, reason `purpose-not-eligible`; a planted `fired` decision without a form substitutes for every purpose.
- [ ] The evidence class of an episode with a film patch is `Estimated` and with only rigid proxies is `Tested`.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` lists the six mechanisms with tier, flag, evidence class and fallback.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-scene/tests/living_decl.rs`, `cargo test -p frob-scene living_decl`.
- `decl_roundtrip`: one declaration of each of the ten kinds and sub-kinds; canonical bytes stable.
- `tier_fixed_by_kind`: `chain-cord` declared `S` is refused.
- `frontier_requires_alternative`.
- `admission_matrix`: enumerates 6 mechanisms × {feature on, off} × {gate open, killed} × the five purposes; expected outcome table checked exactly (120 rows).
- `purpose_domain_and_default`: no `--purpose` gives `evaluation` in the manifest; each of the five values is accepted; `--purpose train` returns 802.
- `purpose_not_eligible_after_proxy_cost_kill`: a planted KILL-PROXY decision `fired` with `fallback_form` "evaluation batches only": a chain cord under `evaluation` is admitted, under `training` substituted with `reason = purpose-not-eligible`, under `training` with `--require-living-tier F` refused with `FROB-REF-726`, `reason = purpose-not-eligible`, `run_purpose = training`; the same decision without `fallback_form` substitutes under `evaluation` too.
- `estimate_matches_run_admission`: for every row of `admission_matrix`, `sim.estimate` reports the same substitutions and the same refusal code and reason as `sim.run`.
- `substitution_recorded`: a hinged rug with `frontier-proxies` off yields `used = rug-slab`, `reason = feature-not-built`.
- `island_never_in_training`: an island in a 4,096-world training request, with and without `moonshot-islands` built, returns `FROB-REF-726`, exit 5, `reason = island-in-training`, `run_purpose = training`, retry `after-change`; never 724 (a mutant that answers 724 on the build without the feature fails).
- `island_after_kill_living`: with a planted `killed: fallback adopted` record for KILL-LIVING, a fluid-box island under `oracle-check` yields `used = film-patch`, `reason = killed: KILL-LIVING`.
- `require_tier_refuses_724`: a hinged rug with `frontier-proxies` off and `--require-living-tier F` returns `FROB-REF-724` with `reason = feature-not-built`; without the flag it substitutes.
- `require_tier_not_eligible_726`: with `frontier-proxies` built, a hinged rug forced with `--require-living-tier F` on a tier its admission rule excludes returns `FROB-REF-726` with `reason = tier-not-eligible`; the 724 and 726 reason sets are disjoint.
- `evidence_class_weakest`: film patch plus rigid proxies → `Estimated`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/living_tiers.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/living_tiers/`). On a synthetic kitchen fixture with one film patch, one rug declared as a hinged rug with a slab alternative and one cord declared as a chain with a ridge alternative: `frob scene compile <fixture> --key lt-cmp --json`, `frob scene show <id> --json` (three declarations with tiers); `e2e_scene_ready <id>`; `frob sim run <synthetic base design> --scene <id> --tier referee --seed 7 --key lt-run-default --json` on a default build (asserts two `living_substitutions`, evidence class `Estimated`); the same with `--require-living-tier F --key lt-run-require` (asserts `FROB-REF-724`, exit 5, `reason = feature-not-built`, repairs present); and `frob sim run <synthetic base design> --scene <island scene> --purpose training --worlds 4096 --seed 7 --key lt-run-train --json` on this bead's synthetic scene declaring one fluid-box island with a film-patch alternative (asserts `FROB-REF-726`, exit 5, `reason = island-in-training`); then `frob sim estimate <synthetic base design> --scene <island scene> --worlds 4096 --seconds 10 --purpose training --json` (asserts the same code and reason, allocating nothing) and `frob sim estimate <synthetic base design> --scene <id> --worlds 64 --seconds 10 --json` on the kitchen fixture (asserts the two substitutions the run recorded and `run_purpose = evaluation`, the default). Lines log the command, exit code, wall time, evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), the substitutions as structured values, and on failure the first differing declaration.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-440 and CX-190, the reason a rug's Solid form is a free slab and not a fixed step: pushing an undriven wheel of radius r under load W over an edge of height h takes W·tan α with tan α = √(h·(2r − h))/(r − h); r = 25 mm and h = 10 mm give 4/3 of the load, 67 N for a 50 N castor; a 2 kg rug resists with about 0.5 × 19.6 N ≈ 10 N and slides first. The test of the body is BD-231's; the arithmetic is asserted here so the declaration rule cannot drift.
- Negative controls: a build that substitutes silently must fail `substitution_recorded`; a build that admits an island into a training batch must fail `island_never_in_training`.
- Metamorphic: reordering the `living` array changes neither admission results nor `SceneId` (the canonical form sorts by target then kind).
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine and across platforms, identical admission records.

## Considerations

- The cap of four active Moonshot items program-wide (OPEN-PROG-5, OPEN-AUGUR-10: which M6 epics are funded and in which order) is an owner decision; this bead only guarantees that an unfunded mechanism degrades to its Solid alternative with a record.
- Risk: results compared across runs with different substitutions. The episode manifest records them, and paired studies must compare on equal substitution sets.
- Sensorium reads the wetness latent of film patches for specular floors; the latent is declared once per region so that a wet floor is both slippery and glossy in the same episode.
- What would make us revisit: a Frontier mechanism promoted to Solid after its gate, which is a change of the table by ADR.
