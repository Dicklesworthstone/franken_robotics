## Background

ADR-HAB-07 says living matter (films, sloshing payloads, cords, rugs) enters the simulator in three tiers and only the
Solid tier is on the critical path. The reason is a refutation, by arithmetic from stated assumptions, of the strong form:
a resolved fluid lattice, cloth solver or rod solver in every training world. The project's hygiene rule is that a killed
idea stays on the page with the calculation that killed it, and that a counterexample to a tempting shortcut enters the
counterexample catalogue and the negative-control registry (CX-192: "living matter means a resolved fluid, cloth or cable
solver in every training world"). This bead puts that calculation on record in executable form, so that when an assumption
changes (a machine with more memory, a sibling that gains a GPU path) the decision is visibly re-opened instead of silently
inherited. Tier Solid; it is a test and record bead with no product mechanism.

Terms. D3Q19: a lattice-Boltzmann scheme with 19 populations per cell. Lattice Mach number Ma: flow speed over lattice sound
speed. Relaxation time τ: the single-relaxation parameter that sets viscosity; the scheme loses stability as τ → ½. Control
tick: one policy step, 50 Hz here.

## Scope

## Technical approach

All figures are DESIGN arithmetic from stated assumptions; none is a measurement. Each becomes one test that asserts the
printed value to the printed precision and names its inputs.

1. **Memory of a room of fluid.** A D3Q19 lattice in single precision with two population buffers needs
   19 × 4 × 2 = 152 bytes per cell. A 10 m × 10 m × 3 m room at 1 cm is 3.0 × 10⁸ cells, 45.6 GB for one world. At 5 mm it
   is 2.4 × 10⁹ cells, 365 GB, more than the working-set limit of the large-memory machine (about 222.7 GiB, FACT measured
   on that machine, to be re-read). At 8,192 worlds the 1 cm case is about 374 TB. One cubic metre at 5 mm is 8 × 10⁶ cells
   and 1.22 GB.
2. **A spill is thinner than any affordable lattice.** A film 0.1 to 1 mm thick needs cells of 0.25 mm or less to be
   resolved through its depth. One square metre of floor with a 4 mm slab of such cells is 4,000 × 4,000 × 16 =
   2.56 × 10⁸ cells, 39 GB per square metre.
3. **Lattice Mach number at control rate.** The lattice sound speed is Δx/(√3·Δt), so a flow of speed u has
   Ma = √3·u·Δt/Δx. Stepping the fluid once per 50 Hz control tick (Δt = 20 ms) with u = 1 m/s gives Ma = 0.35 at 10 cm
   cells, 3.5 at 1 cm and 6.9 at 5 mm. FACT: the sibling lattice crate declares `MACH_LIMIT = 0.3` for its low-Mach regime
   (`frankensim/crates/fs-lbm/src/lib.rs`). Holding Ma at 0.1 with 5 mm cells needs Δt ≤ 0.29 ms, which is 70 fluid steps
   per control tick.
4. **Relaxation time.** τ = 3·ν·Δt/Δx² + ½, with ν the kinematic viscosity. For water (ν = 10⁻⁶ m²/s) at 10 cm cells and
   20 ms steps, τ − ½ = 6 × 10⁻⁶; at 5 mm cells and the 0.29 ms step it is 3.5 × 10⁻⁵. A single-relaxation scheme is not
   stable that close to ½.
5. **Throughput.** If coupled matter is active in 5% of worlds and the batch must keep at least a tenth of its rigid-only
   throughput, a coupled world may cost at most c rigid worlds with 0.95 + 0.05·c ≤ 10, so c ≤ 181. FACT: the sibling cloth
   solver reports drapes that take tens of CPU seconds for a few thousand vertices and states it has no GPU path by design
   (`franken_textiles/README.md`); the sibling rod solver states it is fixture-scale statics and that dynamics on SE(3) are
   successor scope (`frankensim/crates/fs-solid/src/rod.rs`); FrankenSim lists GPU execution as not implemented
   (`frankensim/README.md`). A batched rigid step costs microseconds per world, so the bound is missed by orders of magnitude.
6. **Island cost** (reused by the kill rule for islands). One cubic metre at 5 mm holding Ma ≤ 0.1 at 1 m/s needs about
   3,460 fluid steps of 8 × 10⁶ cells per simulated second: an offline computation.

**Conclusion recorded in the ADR.** No fluid lattice, cloth solver or rod solver runs inside a training batch. What the
failures need is a friction that depends on contact history, and objects that can be pushed, lifted and wrapped; both come
from mechanisms the engine already has. Alternatives rejected: (a) resolved matter in every world (items 1 to 5); (b) a
lattice fluid advanced once per control tick (items 3 and 4); (c) no living matter at all, which cannot produce
history-dependent slip, slosh resonance or entanglement.

**What re-opens the decision** (written into the ADR): a working-set limit above the 5 mm room figure; a sibling fluid,
cloth or rod solver with a batched GPU path whose cost per world is under 181 rigid worlds at 5% activity; a
multi-relaxation scheme admitted through the airlock that is stable at the τ of item 4.

**Implementation.** The tests live with the counterexample catalogue in `frob-conformance` (`tests/cx/cx_192.rs`), compute
in `f64` with the assumptions as named constants, and print a table that the ADR includes by generation, so the ADR's
numbers cannot drift from the tests. Sibling facts are held as pinned constants with source path and commit; their
re-reading is part of the admission record of the fluid bridge.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cx/cx_192.rs`, a module of the known-answer target, run as `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed>
cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- CX-192` (the Gauntlet's form, ADR-GAUNT-01; the negative
controls with `--features gauntlet-mutations`).
- `lbm_bytes_per_cell`: 19 × 4 × 2 = 152.
- `room_memory`: 10 × 10 × 3 m at 0.01 m → 3.0e8 cells → 45.6 GB (decimal); at 0.005 m → 2.4e9 cells → 364.8 GB, asserted greater than 222.7 GiB; × 8,192 worlds at 1 cm → 373.6 TB; 1 m³ at 5 mm → 8.0e6 cells → 1.216 GB.
- `spill_slab`: 1 m² × 4 mm at 0.25 mm → 2.56e8 cells → 38.9 GB.
- `mach_at_control_rate`: √3 × 1 × 0.02 / {0.1, 0.01, 0.005} = {0.346, 3.46, 6.93}; each compared with the pinned limit 0.3.
- `substeps_for_mach_0_1`: Δt = 0.1 × 0.005/√3 = 0.2887 ms; ceil(20 ms / Δt) = 70.
- `relaxation_margin`: 3 × 1e-6 × 0.02 / 0.1² = 6.0e-6; 3 × 1e-6 × 0.29e-3 / 0.005² = 3.48e-5.
- `throughput_bound`: largest c with 0.95 + 0.05·c ≤ 10 is 181.
- `island_steps_per_second`: 1/0.2887 ms = 3,464.
- `reopen_on_changed_assumption`: with the working-set constant overridden, the test returns the re-open signal.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/living_refutation.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/living_refutation/`). It runs the Gauntlet's counterexample row CX-192 (`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- CX-192`, asserting every figure of the six calculations, then the same with `--features gauntlet-mutations`, each negative control asserted caught through `e2e_expect_caught`; there is no `frob conformance` command) and then, from the user's side, `frob sim estimate` (a read verb, no key) on a request that names a fluid island inside a 4,096-world training batch (CX-192's plant `N19b-04:cx-192`, the fixture of the acceptance), `frob sim estimate <synthetic base design> --scene <island scene> --worlds 4096 --seconds 10 --purpose training --json` (expects `FROB-REF-726`, exit 5, `reason = island-in-training`, the refusal of N19b-03 under the ruling of N32-REF: the island is refused for its purpose, not for being absent from the build) and on a request for one island beside one referee world with purpose `oracle-check` on the default build, `frob sim estimate <synthetic base design> --scene <island scene> --worlds 1 --seconds 10 --tier referee --purpose oracle-check --json` (the island scene is N19b-03's synthetic scene declaring one fluid-box island with a film-patch alternative; `--purpose` is N19b-03's input of `sim.estimate`; expects an estimate with a `living_substitutions` entry, reason `feature-not-built`; the island's own cost estimate belongs to N19b-21 and is not asserted here). Each line logs the command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, each computed figure as a `metric` with its unit and the asserted value, and on failure the first figure that differs. The summary names no gate.

### Known answers and negative controls
- CX-192 with every number above. Related killed-idea records: "lattice fluid at control rate" (items 3 and 4) and "full-room fluid in every world" (items 1, 2, 5).
- Negative control: a variant of the estimate that drops the factor 2 for the second population buffer (76 bytes per cell) must be caught by `lbm_bytes_per_cell`; a variant that uses Δx/Δt as the sound speed (omitting √3) must be caught by `mach_at_control_rate`.
- Determinism: exact arithmetic on constants; identical on every platform.

## Considerations

- These are not performance measurements and must never be cited as such; a report may cite them only as the reason for the tiering.
- The FACTs about siblings are dated. If a sibling changes, the fix is to update the pinned constants with the new commit and let the re-opening test decide, not to edit the conclusion.
- The 222.7 GiB working-set figure and the 181-world bound are also inputs of KILL-LIVING ("an island costs more than 181 rigid worlds at 5% activity with no route to less").
