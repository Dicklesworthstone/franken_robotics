## Background

On a lubricated floor, friction depends on how long and how hard a foot or wheel has pressed: the film under it has to be
squeezed out before the surfaces touch. A constant coefficient per floor patch cannot express that (counterexample CX-189),
and no fluid grid is needed to express it. The Solid tier of living worlds does it with one number per contact sample, the
film thickness h under that sample. Ownership is split by design: **Habitat owns the patch and the thickness state** (what
is on the floor, how thick it starts, how it thins); **Traction owns the cone row that consumes the resulting coefficient**
(BD-230, kernel `K-film`, gate GATE-TRAC-05, kill gate KILL-FILM). This bead is Habitat's side. Tier Solid, crate
`frob-scene`, layer 2; outputs are `Estimated` because the law is semi-empirical.

Notation. The plan's scene chapter writes F for the normal load on a pad of radius R and v_t for tangential speed; its
contact chapter writes N, a and v for the same quantities. η is dynamic viscosity (Pa·s), σ the combined roughness on the
interface card (m), μ_b the boundary-lubricated coefficient of the card for the floor's condition, μ_d the dry coefficient.

## Scope

In: the film-patch declaration compiled into a per-region patch table; the fluid class data; the wetness latent and its
two recorded conditionals; the lifecycle rules of the per-contact thickness, as the contract the contact crate implements;
the exact thickness update as the one host reference function, `thin`, with its symbolic certificate (PO-HAB-04) and the
three hand-checkable squeeze times on the host.

Out, with the bead that has each: the friction coefficient of the film (the blend, the Couette term and its cap), the
storage of the thickness in contact memory and in the restore set, the kernel `K-film` on every tier with its CPU
reference, the regime indicator (Hersey number), the validity flags, `FROB-REF-242` and the gates GATE-TRAC-05 and
GATE-HAB-06 (BD-230, which depends on this bead: it reads this patch table, implements these lifecycle rules, and its
kernel's update is a port of `thin`, compared with it bit for bit in `f64`); the scene-level script of GATE-HAB-06
(N19b-06); films the robot moves (N19b-08, N19b-09).

## Technical approach

**1. The patch.** A film patch is a surface region with a fluid class (density ρ, dynamic viscosity η), an initial
thickness h₀ and a wetness latent. In the Solid tier the patch is **static and inexhaustible**: the robot neither removes
nor carries film; every new contact starts from h₀.

Compile output (DESIGN): a patch table indexed by region identifier, `FilmPatch { patch_id, fluid: FluidRef, h0: Length,
condition: Condition }`, hashed into the pack, and exposed to devices as a buffer described by the manifest. The fluid
class is a row of a material card (kind `material`, class `fluid`) with ρ and η, each with provenance; ν = η/ρ. Generators
draw h₀ and η from the hazard registries (all `assumed`): water films 0.1 to 2 mm; oil or grease 0.05 to 1 mm at 30 to
100 mPa·s; plant oil or coolant 0.05 to 1 mm at 20 to 100 mPa·s; retail spills 0.1 to 2 mm.

**2. The wetness latent.** A region's `wetness` ∈ [0, 1] is one named scalar. Two separately recorded conditionals read it:
(a) contact: wetness selects the interface condition (dry or the patch's wet or greasy condition) and scales the initial
thickness (DESIGN default h₀ = wetness × declared thickness); (b) optics: wetness raises the reflectance at normal incidence
and lowers roughness on the optical card. Randomization draws the latent once per region per episode, so a wet floor is
both slippery and glossy in the same episode, and each conditional has its own provenance. Contact and optical properties
are otherwise independent maps (ADR-HAB-03).

**3. Thickness state and lifecycle.** Each contact sample on a patch carries one state variable, the film thickness h
(the contact crate stores it as s = h⁻², because the update is linear in s; that storage is BD-230's, and `thin` below
takes and returns h):
1. At first touch of a sample on a patch, h ← h₀ of that patch.
2. While the sample stays in contact on the patch, h thins by step 4 below.
3. When the sample leaves the patch (lifts off, or slides onto a region with no patch), the state is reset; a later touch
   starts again from h₀.
4. The state is per contact, per world: it is part of contact memory, of the transactional step's restore set and of every
   snapshot, and it never affects another world or a later episode (INV-HAB-03).
5. With h₀ = 0 there is no film and the consumer must return the dry card exactly.

**4. Drainage.** For a flat circular pad of radius R pressed with normal force F on a Newtonian film, the squeeze-film
relation is dh/dt = −2·F·h³ / (3π·η·R⁴) (FACT: Stefan's squeeze-film formula from lubrication theory, to be re-read from a
primary source before a gate cites it). Equivalently d(h⁻²)/dt = 4·F / (3π·η·R⁴), so h⁻² grows linearly at a rate set by
the load. The update

  h⁻²(t + Δt) = h⁻²(t) + 4·F·Δt / (3π·η·R⁴)

is exact for a load constant over the step (PO-HAB-04), unconditionally stable, and needs one division and one square root,
which the kernel dialect allows. Host reference: `frob_scene::living::film::thin(h, F, η, R, Δt) -> h` computing
h' = 1/√(h⁻² + 4·F·Δt/(3π·η·R⁴)); for F ≤ 0 it returns h unchanged. R is the pad radius of the contact sample.

**5. What the consumer does with the thickness.** The friction coefficient is the contact crate's law and is specified,
implemented and tested in BD-230 only: with film ratio Λ = h/σ it blends the boundary coefficient μ_b and a viscous
(Couette) term by w(Λ) = clamp((3 − Λ)/2, 0, 1) (BD-230 writes the same function as ψ), caps the viscous term at μ_b, holds
the coefficient constant inside a solve, and returns the dry card exactly for h₀ = 0. This bead supplies what that law
reads from the scene: the patch, its fluid's η, h₀ and the condition. DESIGN (the conflict settled, stated identically in
BD-230): the scene chapter writes the viscous term capped, μ_v = min(μ_b, η·|v_t|·π·R²/(h·F)), and the contact chapter
writes it uncapped; the capped form governs in both beads, because without it the coefficient is unbounded as the load
goes to zero and the scene chapter's known answer for the limits (KA-439) is stated with the cap. The contact chapter's
"pure Couette shear for h ≥ 3σ" is then asserted where the cap does not bind, and a contact where it binds is flagged
`viscous-capped`.

**6. Hand-checkable consequences.** The time for a thick film to thin to h₁ is t = 3π·η·R⁴ / (4·F·h₁²). To 10 µm:

| Pad and fluid (viscosities assumed) | R | F | Time to 10 µm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small foot on water, η = 1.0 mPa·s | 20 mm | 100 N | 0.038 s |
| Small foot on cooking oil, η = 60 mPa·s | 20 mm | 100 N | 2.3 s |
| Large flat foot on water | 60 mm | 400 N | 0.76 s |

Against a stance of about 0.3 s (assumed), the small foot on water reaches boundary contact within the stance; the same
foot on oil and the large foot on water do not.

**7. Validity and evidence.** The law assumes a smooth, rigid, flat pad. Treads and rough floors drain faster, so the
drainage time is an upper bound and predicted slip is pessimistic for treaded soles (HYPOTHESIS; the test is a spill-pad
traction dataset imported through the Reality lane, N19b-22). Until one exists every output of the law is `Estimated` and
the Reality cell is `no-claim`. σ is `assumed` on every card until measured (OPEN-HAB-8).

**8. Determinism.** The update uses +, ×, ÷ and one square root, each correctly rounded, in a fixed order; the patch table
is integer-indexed. The mechanism therefore stays inside classes D1 and D2 as the device family earns them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A scene with a film patch compiles to a patch table whose hash is in the manifest; a patch with no fluid class, a negative thickness or a wetness outside [0, 1] is refused.
- [ ] `thin` reproduces the three times above within 0.1% on the host, and one step of length t equals 1,000 steps of t/1,000 to round-off (exactness for constant load).
- [ ] PO-HAB-04 has an instance certificate: the symbolic identity d(h⁻²)/dt = 4·F/(3π·η·R⁴) from the differential law, checked by FrankenSymPy (class Certified).
- [ ] The lifecycle rules hold on the host reference: first touch sets h₀, leaving the patch resets, h₀ = 0 means no film.
- [ ] Drawing the wetness latent once changes both the contact condition and the optical response in the recorded direction, and changing only the optical card changes no patch-table byte.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` states the validity domain (smooth rigid flat pad, Newtonian film, constant load over a step), the `Estimated` class and the open hypothesis.
- [ ] The film-patch leg of N19a-09's `scripts/e2e/scene_state_leak.sh`, logged there as `e2e_skip "needs N19b-05"` because N19a-09 comes first, runs and passes (no film state carried across a reset), with no skip line left.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-scene/tests/film_patch.rs`, `cargo test -p frob-scene film_patch`.
- `squeeze_times`: (η, R, F) = (1.0e-3, 0.020, 100), (60e-3, 0.020, 100), (1.0e-3, 0.060, 400), from h₀ = 1 mm to 10 µm: 0.0377 s, 2.262 s, 0.763 s (each within 0.1% of 3π·η·R⁴/(4·F·h₁²) and within 2% of the rounded table values).
- `r4_scaling`: doubling R multiplies the time by 16 exactly to round-off; halving F doubles it; doubling η doubles it.
- `step_exactness`: one step against 1,000 sub-steps under constant load: relative difference below 10⁻¹².
- `no_load_no_thinning`: F = 0 returns h; F < 0 returns h.
- `lifecycle`: touch, hold 0.1 s, leave, touch: the second contact starts from h₀.
- The blend values and the limits of the coefficient (w(1) = 1, w(2) = ½, w(3) = 0; μ = μ_b at Λ ≤ 1 and μ = μ_v at
  Λ ≥ 3; continuity on 10⁴ random states) are tests of the law and are BD-230's (`film_blend_values`,
  `film_limits_and_continuity`, `film_cap_flag`); no coefficient is computed in this crate.
- `latent_drives_both`: wetness 0 → dry condition and base optical card; wetness 1 → wet condition, thickness h₀, raised r0, lowered roughness.
- `patch_table_hash_in_manifest`.

### End-to-end script and logging
The scene-level script is `scripts/e2e/film_patch_gate.sh` of N19b-06. This bead adds `scripts/e2e/film_patch_compile.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/film_patch_compile/`): `frob scene compile <synthetic tile room with one water patch> --key fp-cmp --json`, `frob scene show <id> --json` (asserts one `film-patch` declaration, tier `S`, thickness and fluid), and a compile of a planted patch with wetness 1.3 (`--key fp-cmp-wet13`; asserts exit 3); then reruns N19a-09's `scripts/e2e/scene_state_leak.sh` and asserts its film-patch leg passes with no `e2e_skip "needs N19b-05"` line. Lines log command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, the patch parameters as metrics with units, digests of the patch table, and the first differing field on failure.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-437 (update exact for a constant load; symbolic, G0). KA-438 (0.038 s, 2.3 s, 0.76 s; tolerance 2%, the bound of the contact chapter, which owns the law; an older registry row says 1% and is superseded). KA-439 (limits recover μ_b and μ_v) is asserted by BD-230, which has the coefficient.
- CX-189: the three times against a 0.3 s stance show one patch behaving three ways; a constant-coefficient patch is the shortcut.
- Negative controls: an update written with R² in place of R⁴ must fail `r4_scaling` (the time would scale by 4); an explicit Euler update dh = −2·F·h³·Δt/(3π·η·R⁴) must fail `step_exactness`; a build that keeps h across lift-off must fail `lifecycle`.
- Metamorphic (MR-26): a film law at zero thickness equals the dry card bit for bit and is continuous in thickness; doubling pad radius multiplies squeeze time by 16.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine, debug and release, across platforms: identical bits for `thin`.

## Considerations

- **The cap on the viscous term is settled** (step 5): the capped form governs, BD-230 implements and tests it, and this bead computes no coefficient.
- **OPEN-HAB-8** (sources for film roughness, carried thickness and transfer fraction; default `assumed`, outputs `Estimated`, Reality `no-claim`; owner Habitat lead; blocks any Reality claim about slip on films). Carried by N19b-22.
- The fluid class as a row of a material card is a DESIGN choice (the card kinds are a closed list without a fluid kind); if the card owner prefers the interface card to carry η, only the lookup changes.
- KILL-FILM (trigger: on the wet-floor battery the law changes fewer than 10% of episode outcomes against a constant-coefficient patch at μ_b, or adds more than 5% to step time; fallback: static condition cards on the lockstep tiers, the law kept on transactional profiles for audits) is measured on BD-230's law by the wet-floor battery (N18a-33) and decided by its decision bead (N18a-34). If it fires, this bead's patch table remains and supplies the static condition.
- Memory: one thickness value per contact sample; negligible.
