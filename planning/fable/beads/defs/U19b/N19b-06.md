## Background

The film-friction law has two gates because it has two owners. GATE-TRAC-05 (owned by the contact chapter, measured by
BD-230) tests the law itself at a contact: Stefan times within 2%, Couette force within 10⁻⁶ relative in double precision,
the dry limit bit-identical, the limits at h ≤ σ and h ≥ 3σ and continuity between them on 10⁴ random states. GATE-HAB-06
tests the same physics **as scenes consume it**: through a compiled scene pack with a film patch, a region lookup from the
bricks, the per-contact thickness state, and a running simulation on every tier. A law that is correct in isolation can
still be wrong in a scene: the wrong region's patch can be read, the thickness can survive a lift-off, a tier can skip the
update. This bead is the measurement of GATE-HAB-06.

The gate, verbatim in substance (claim: Known answer; threshold is a TARGET; earliest milestone M3): the three squeeze
times within 2%; the R⁴ scaling; dry and thick-film limits recover the card coefficients. It serves BET-36 (living worlds
change decisions), whose Solid half rests on this gate and GATE-TRAC-05.

## Scope

In: the scene fixture, the pad fixture, the end-to-end script, the registered negative controls, the gate record in
`registries/gates.toml` with measured values per tier.

Out: the law and its kernel (BD-230); the patch table and thickness reference (N19b-05); the wet-floor battery
(N18a-33) and the KILL-FILM decision it feeds (N18a-34); task families that use film patches (BD-253).

## Technical approach

**1. Scene fixture (`synthetic`).** A 2 m × 2 m flat tile floor compiled to a pack with four regions: region A dry (no
patch); region B a water patch, h₀ = 1 mm, η = 1.0 mPa·s; region C a cooking-oil patch, h₀ = 1 mm, η = 60 mPa·s; region D a
water patch with h₀ = 0. One interface card for tile with σ = 5 µm, μ_b and μ_d distinct (for example 0.25 and 0.80, all
`assumed` and stated in the fixture) so that the limits are distinguishable.

**2. Pad fixture.** A rigid flat circular pad on a vertical prismatic guide, one contact sample whose patch radius is the
pad radius R, pressed by a constant commanded normal force F with gravity compensated, so the load is constant from first
touch as the law assumes. A second horizontal actuator can drag the pad at a set tangential speed for the limit tests.
Three pads: (R, F) = (20 mm, 100 N), (60 mm, 400 N) and (40 mm, 100 N) for the scaling check. The drag speed for the
limit tests is v_t = 0.10 m/s (DESIGN, stated in the fixture). The fixture is registered as `fixture:film-pad` in the
fixture registry of N24b-03: every constant above (regions, card, pads, speed, seed 11) and every expected value of
step 3 is recorded and hashed before the first run (INV-ARENA-04), so no tolerance or constant can move after an
outcome is seen.

**3. Measurements, as numbered steps, on each tier** (`referee`, `strict`, `batch-cpu`, and each device tier present):
1. **Squeeze times.** Lower each pad onto its patch; from first touch, record the time at which the per-contact thickness
   crosses 10 µm, interpolating between steps. Expected, from t = 3π·η·R⁴/(4·F·h₁²): 0.038 s (20 mm, 100 N, water),
   2.3 s (20 mm, 100 N, oil), 0.76 s (60 mm, 400 N, water). Pass: each within 2%.
2. **R⁴ scaling.** The 40 mm pad at 100 N on water must take 16 times the 20 mm pad's time, within 2%.
3. **Dry limit.** On region D (h₀ = 0) and on region A, drag the pad slowly under load; the measured friction ratio at
   incipient sliding equals μ_d of the card, bit for bit against the same run with no patch table at all.
4. **Squeezed limit.** On region B after the thickness has fallen below σ (Λ ≤ 1), the ratio equals μ_b.
5. **Thick-film limit.** On region C immediately after touch (Λ ≥ 3), the ratio equals the viscous value
   η·v_t·π·R²/(h·F) at the commanded speed, within the tier's stated tolerance; it is below μ_b for the fixture's speed
   (20 mm pad, 100 N, η = 60 mPa·s, h = h₀ = 1 mm, v_t = 0.10 m/s: 7.54 × 10⁻⁵, against μ_b = 0.25, so the cap does not
   bind and no `viscous-capped` flag is raised).
6. **Lifecycle through the scene.** Lift the pad off region B and set it down again: the second squeeze time equals the first.
7. **Region lookup.** Set the pad down straddling nothing: one metre away on region A the thickness channel reports no film.

**4. Recording.** The gate's value comes from a receipt of the known-answer tier, never from a file the script
writes: GATE-HAB-06's rows KA-438 and KA-439 are G1 rows, so this bead adds the gate's entries to the target
`g1_known_answers` (module `crates/frob-conformance/tests/hab/film_gate.rs`, filtered by `GATE-HAB-06`), which runs
measurements 1 to 7 through the fixture on each present tier, and carries CX-189 and the three planted defects below
as its controls under `gauntlet-mutations`. The script runs that tier as its last step, `e2e_tier g1_known_answers
GATE-HAB-06 --mutations`, which logs the receipt's hash. The gate row in `registries/gates.toml` is entered from
that receipt by a session that did not implement the work, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
record_gate -- --gate GATE-HAB-06 --receipt <hash>` (there is no `frob conformance` command or verb); the row
cites the commit, the command, the output and the receipt hash, and lists the claim identifiers. A tier that is not present on the machine is recorded `not run`,
which is not a pass. The gate passes when every present tier passes and at least `referee` and `batch-cpu` are present.

**5. Evidence.** Claim kind Known answer; class `Tested`; the outputs of the law itself remain `Estimated`; Reality `no-claim`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three squeeze times are within 2% of 0.038 s, 2.3 s and 0.76 s on every present tier.
- [ ] Doubling the pad radius multiplies the squeeze time by 16 within 2%.
- [ ] No film and h₀ = 0 return the dry coefficient bit for bit; Λ ≤ 1 returns μ_b; Λ ≥ 3 returns the viscous value.
- [ ] Each registered negative control is caught in the same run.
- [ ] GATE-HAB-06 has a row in `registries/gates.toml` with measured values, commit, hardware fingerprint and seed, entered by `record_gate` from the receipt of `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-HAB-06 --mutations`, whose controls list CX-189 and the three planted defects as `caught`.
- [ ] The script is idempotent, seed-explicit and exits non-zero on any failed assertion.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The unit tests of the mechanism are in N19b-05 and BD-230. This bead adds fixture self-tests in `crates/frob-conformance/tests/hab_film_fixture.rs`:
- `fixture_regions`: the compiled fixture has four regions and three patches with the stated parameters.
- `pad_load_is_constant`: over the first 0.5 s after touch the normal force on the pad stays within 0.5% of the command.
- `expected_times_from_formula`: the fixture's expected values are computed from 3π·η·R⁴/(4·F·h₁²), not typed in: 0.03770 s, 2.2619 s, 0.7634 s.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/film_patch_gate.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/film_patch_gate/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript). Commands, per tier T (`referee`, `batch-cpu`, each device tier present, and for the `strict` row `--tier batch-cpu --profile strict`, since `strict` is a step profile of `sim.run` and not a `--tier` value): `frob scene compile fixtures/hab/film_pad_room --key fp-compile --json` (asserts the `SceneId` the fixture records); `frob sim run --fixture film-pad --tier T --seed 11 --telemetry T2 --key fp-<T>-a --json` (`--fixture` is the one spelling of a registered fixture, which supplies the pad rig, the scene and the task, so neither a positional design nor `--scene` is passed; the determinism lane repeats it with `--key fp-<T>-b`, a distinct key, so the second call runs and does not return the first one's stored receipt); `frob telemetry query <run> --stream contact --channels film_thickness --json` (the per-contact thickness: BD-230's T2 channel `film_thickness` of the `contact` stream, in m and 0 off the patch, which is why the run records `--telemetry T2`); then the three limits at the fixture's states through `contact.probe`'s film branch (BD-230), with the fixture's card file and its constants: dry, `frob contact probe --film --card <fixture card> --h0 0 --radius 20mm --normal 100N --slip 0.10m/s --json` (asserts μ_d bit for bit); squeezed, `frob contact probe --film --card <fixture card> --eta 1mPa.s --h0 1mm --dwell 0.2s --radius 20mm --normal 100N --slip 0.10m/s --json` (the water film thinned to 4.34 µm, Λ = 0.87 ≤ 1; asserts μ_b and the regime `boundary`); thick film, `frob contact probe --film --card <fixture card> --eta 60mPa.s --h0 1mm --dwell 0s --radius 20mm --normal 100N --slip 0.10m/s --json` (Λ = 200 ≥ 3; asserts 7.54 × 10⁻⁵ and no `viscous-capped` flag); and GB-21's squeeze form `frob contact probe --film --eta 1mPa.s --radius 20mm --normal 100N --h0 1mm --until-h 10um --json` beside step 1 (asserts 0.0377 s); then the three planted defects below, each built with `--features gauntlet-mutations` plus the control's own feature and run through `e2e_expect_caught` (the run must fail the named step; a plant that passes is a failure of the control); last, the gate's tier run `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-HAB-06 --mutations` (asserts exit 0, the receipt's GATE-HAB-06 line `pass`, and CX-189 and the three planted defects in its `controls` with outcome `caught`; the `cmd` line carries the receipt hash the recording session cites). Assertions: the seven measurements above. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), each measured value with its unit and threshold (`squeeze_time_s`, `ratio_16`, `mu_measured`), state digests where runs are compared, and on failure the first differing value and the run and scene identities needed to reproduce. The final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-HAB-06"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-438: 0.038 s, 2.3 s, 0.76 s, tolerance 2% (the contact chapter's bound; both gates use 2%). KA-439: the limits. KA-437: exactness of the update, which makes the times independent of step size (run at the default step and at half the step; times agree within 0.1%).
- CX-189 as a control: a constant-coefficient patch at μ_b (the shortcut) must fail step 1, because its thickness never evolves.
- Planted defects that must be caught: the update with R² instead of R⁴ (fails steps 1 and 2); a region lookup shifted by one region identifier (fails step 7); a thickness that survives lift-off (fails step 6).
- Metamorphic (MR-26): zero thickness equals the dry card bit for bit; continuity in thickness.
- Determinism lanes: the script runs twice on one machine and compares digests (class D2 on each tier); across worker counts on `batch-cpu`; debug and release on the host tiers; across backends against each backend's class (D1 where earned, otherwise the D3 envelope on the measured times).

## Considerations

- The pad fixture exists to hold the load constant; a foot of a walking robot does not, and its squeeze time is not a known answer. Robot-level effects are for the wet-floor battery and KILL-FILM.
- If KILL-FILM fires (the law changes fewer than 10% of outcomes or costs more than 5% of step time), the fallback keeps the law on the transactional profiles only; this gate is then measured and recorded for those profiles, and the lockstep measurements are not taken (the recorder never writes `killed`: that status is derived by N40-03's propagation from KILL-FILM's decision in force with outcome `fired`).
- The viscosities and roughness in the fixture are `assumed`; the gate tests arithmetic through the scene path, not realism.
- Cost: seconds of simulation per tier; no budget concern.
