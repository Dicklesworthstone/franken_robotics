## Background

This is the update rule of the advected film field of N19b-08: how film is dragged by a sliding contact, how it spreads
under gravity, and how a wheel or foot picks it up and lays it down again. The design requirement that shapes everything
is conservation: film volume, cells plus reservoirs, must be conserved (INV-HAB-07), because a film that appears or
vanishes at a contact boundary would fabricate or erase the very slip events the mechanism exists to produce. The plan
gives the smallest counterexample to the obvious but wrong formulation (CX-191), and that counterexample is the negative
control of the mass gate. Tier Frontier, feature `frontier-film-advect`; owner `frob-scene`; funded only after the pilot.

Terms. Finite volume: each cell's thickness changes only by fluxes through its four faces, and a flux leaving one cell
enters its neighbour. Upwind: transport takes the thickness of the cell the flow comes from. Lubrication limit: a thin
viscous film spreads under gravity as a nonlinear diffusion with mobility proportional to h³.

## Scope

In: the transport and spreading fluxes; operator order and positivity; pickup and redeposition as paired transfers; tile
allocation requests raised by the update; the per-step volume audit; the CPU reference and the kernel-dialect source; the
telescoping proof on file (PO-HAB-05); the fixture `spill-track` registered in N24b-03's fixture registry, which this
bead's smoke script and N19b-10's gate script run.

Out: storage, quota, admission and `FROB-REF-307` (N19b-08); the gate measurements (N19b-10); the friction law (BD-230).

## Technical approach

**1. The update.** For each cell, h ← h − (Δt/A)·Σ_faces F_f, with A = Δx² the cell area, and face flux

  F_f = [ 1_f·h_up·(v̄·n)_f − (g/(3ν))·h_f³·(∇h·n)_f ]·L_f,

where L_f = Δx is the face length and n the face normal. The first term is transport by the sliding contact: 1_f marks
faces of cells under a contact patch, h_up is the upwind thickness, v̄ the mean tangential velocity of the patch. The
second is gravity spreading in the lubrication limit (g gravity, ν kinematic viscosity), with (∇h·n)_f = (h_R − h_L)/Δx
and the face mobility taken as the **arithmetic mean of the cubes**, h_f³ = ½·(h_L³ + h_R³), so that a wet cell beside a
dry one has a nonzero flux and the front advances. A harmonic or geometric mean gives zero at a wet-dry face and the spill
never spreads. Under the step bound of N19b-08 the gravity term keeps h ≥ 0.

**2. Why the flux form (the counterexample).** Writing the transport as 1·∇·(h·v̄), with the contact indicator outside the
divergence, creates or destroys film at the patch boundary. Three cells with h = (0, 1, 0), the indicator on the middle
cell only and v̄ = +1: the masked update removes Δt/Δx from the middle cell and adds it nowhere. The face-flux form moves
the same amount into the right-hand cell.

**3. One film sub-step per world, as numbered steps** (DESIGN: sequential operator splitting in the fixed order transport,
spread, pick up, redeposit. Each of steps 2, 4, 5 and 6 computes all of its fluxes from the state left by the step before
it, never from the state at the start of the sub-step: transport at the Courant bound may empty a cell, and a spreading
flux computed from the pre-transport thickness could then drive it negative. Within one step each face flux is evaluated
once in a canonical orientation, left to right and bottom to top, so both cells of a face use identical bits):
1. **Mark.** From committed contact state, mark the cells whose centre lies under each contact patch and record the patch's
   v̄ and body. If two patches claim a face, the lower body index supplies v̄.
2. **Transport.** For each face with 1_f = 1 (either adjacent cell marked): q_f = h_up·(v̄·n)·Δt/Δx in thickness units,
   converted to integer quanta toward zero. Admission requires the Courant sum (|v̄_x| + |v̄_y|)·Δt/Δx ≤ 1 (DESIGN; a
   violating step is sub-cycled), so a cell never sends out more than it holds.
3. **Allocate.** Faces that send film into an unallocated tile raise allocation requests; N19b-08 serves them by count,
   scan and write inside the world's quota. Then apply the transport transfers as paired integer subtract and add.
4. **Spread.** For every face in the domain: q_f = (g·Δt/(3ν·Δx²))·½·(h_L³ + h_R³)·(h_L − h_R), positive from L to R,
   in quanta toward zero; allocate as in step 3; apply. With Δt ≤ Δx²/(4·D_max), each face moves at most a quarter of the
   difference, so four faces cannot empty a cell.
5. **Pick up.** For each marked cell under body b: ΔV = min(β_p·V_cell, V_cap(b) − V_res(b)); cell −= ΔV; V_res(b) += ΔV.
6. **Redeposit.** For each body touching the floor with V_res > 0: ΔV = β_d·V_res, split over its marked cells in equal
   integer shares with the remainder to the lowest-index cell; V_res −= ΔV; cells += shares.
7. **Audit.** Sum cells and reservoirs of the world as an exact integer; it must equal the sum at reset. A mismatch marks
   the world `Faulted: film-mass`. The total per world is written to telemetry as the channel `volume_total`
   (DESIGN) in the `contact` stream, where BD-230's `film_thickness` is (N24a-36's nine streams have no `film`
   stream): level T2, kind State, unit m³, one value per world per physics step, so that a run recorded with
   `--telemetry T2 --trace-rate 1/1` can be audited after the fact on every world.
Each transfer is one paired statement, so what leaves one store enters the other. Domain-box edges are no-flux walls.

**4. Transfer coefficients (DESIGN).** The interface card of a contact body gives a transfer fraction per revolution f and
a pickup fraction (both `assumed` until sourced). They are converted on the CPU at admission to rates per rolled distance,
λ = −ln(1 − f)/C with C the circumference, so that β_d = min(1, λ·Δs) for a rolled distance Δs in the sub-step and the
carried film falls by (1 − f) per revolution in the limit of small steps. No transcendental function is evaluated in the
kernel. For a foot, the same fraction applies per footfall. The pilot's worked point is then a property of the kernel: a
wheel of radius 0.1 m carrying 20 µm with f = ½ lays down about 10, 5 and 2.5 µm on its first three revolutions.

**5. Sloped floors (DESIGN option).** Where the declaration supplies a floor-height map b(x, y), the spreading gradient is
taken of (h + b), which makes film drift toward a drain on a dished floor. With b = 0 the plan's flat-floor formula and
all known answers are unchanged. The option is off unless the map is supplied.

**6. Proof obligation PO-HAB-05.** The face-flux update conserves total volume exactly in exact arithmetic: summing the
cell updates, every interior face flux appears twice with opposite sign and cancels (a telescoping sum), wall fluxes are
zero, and each pickup or redeposit is a paired transfer. The identity is written out in `docs/proofs/po-hab-05.md`; the
per-run audit of step 7 checks it on every world and every sub-step.

**7. Kernel and reference.** The update is specified once as a straight-line `frob-kir` program with bounded loops over
a tile's cells and emitted to Rust (the `batch-cpu` reference, the CPU path every claim needs) and to the kernel dialect
for CUDA and Metal. It uses +, −, ×, comparisons and integer conversion only. Proposed kernel name `K-film-advect`.
Determinism: state is integer; the floating-point flux is computed in a fixed order with no fused multiply-add, so the
kernel is D2 on every backend and D1 across backends wherever the device family holds bitwise algebraic kernels.

**8. Cost.** TARGET: at most 15% added step cost with a film active in every world (also a KILL-ADVECT trigger).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three-cell case moves Δt/Δx from the middle cell to the right-hand cell and conserves volume; the masked non-conservative mutant loses Δt/Δx and is caught.
- [ ] Total volume, cells plus reservoirs, is constant on every world at every sub-step (exact with integer quanta; in any case within 10⁻⁶ relative over 10⁵ steps with pickup and redeposition).
- [ ] No cell goes negative under the admitted step and Courant bounds, on 10⁶ random sub-steps.
- [ ] A wet cell beside a dry cell produces a nonzero flux; the harmonic-mean mutant stalls and is caught.
- [ ] The CPU reference and each device path produce the class stated in their manifests; a retained differential check against the CPU path exists for the device kernels.
- [ ] `docs/proofs/po-hab-05.md` carries the telescoping identity.
- [ ] Cancellation mid-run leaves no leaked tile slots (request, drain, finalize).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-scene/tests/film_update.rs`, `cargo test -p frob-scene --features frontier-film-advect --test film_update`; real kernel on `batch-cpu`.
- `three_cell_transport`: h = (0, 1, 0), indicator on cell 1, v̄ = +1, Δt/Δx = 0.1: result (0, 0.9, 0.1); sum 1.
- `telescoping_conservation`: property test, random wet patterns, random patch motions, pickup and redeposit, 10⁵ sub-steps: integer sum unchanged.
- `positivity`: 10⁶ random sub-steps at the bounds; minimum thickness ≥ 0.
- `split_order_positivity`: a cell emptied by transport at Courant number 1 (h = (0, 1, 0), v̄·Δt/Δx = 1) beside a dry cell, spreading at the stability bound in the same sub-step: thickness stays ≥ 0; the mutant that computes spreading from the pre-transport state drives the middle cell negative (or saturates the `u32` underflow check) and is caught.
- `front_advances`: one wet cell among dry cells loses film to all four neighbours in one sub-step.
- `paired_reservoir_transfer`: pickup limited by capacity; redeposit shares sum exactly to ΔV.
- `tracking_worked_point`: wheel r = 0.1 m, carried 20 µm, f = ½, rolled over dry floor: mean deposit per revolution 10, 5, 2.5 µm within 2% (lattice and step discretization).
- `spreading_planar_exponent_quick`: a strip release for 2 × 10⁴ sub-steps; fitted front exponent within 5% of 1/5 (the 2% bar on 1/8 is the gate's, N19b-10).
- `canonical_face_orientation`: the flux a cell computes for its right face equals, bit for bit, the flux its neighbour computes for its left face.
- `batch_independence`: a world's field digest is equal alone and inside a 4,096-world batch.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-441 and CX-191: three-cell transport; the face-flux form conserves, the masked form 1·∇·(h·v̄) removes Δt/Δx from the middle cell and adds it nowhere (registered negative control; it must fail the mass assertion).
- KA-442: total volume constant with pickup and redeposition (INV-HAB-07).
- KA-443: viscous spreading, radius ∝ t^(1/8) axisymmetric and front ∝ t^(1/5) planar (FACT: similarity solutions for viscous gravity currents, to be re-read from the primary source before a gate cites them); control: removing the h³ factor changes the exponent (linear diffusion gives ½).
- Further planted defects: harmonic-mean mobility (front stalls); a face flux evaluated in opposite orientations by the two cells (conservation breaks in the last bit under floating storage).
- Metamorphic: mirroring the floor mirrors the field; translating the scenario by whole cells translates the field; doubling viscosity doubles spreading time.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; batch composition; debug and release; across backends against each manifest's class.

## Considerations

- KILL-ADVECT fires if the mass gate fails after one repair cycle, the cost exceeds 15% of the step, or rankings agree with static patches at rank correlation 0.95 or above; fallback: static film-friction patches. Build the audit and the cost meter first.
- Where a field write does not go through this update (for example a scripted spill event), it must be a paired transfer from a declared source reservoir, or conservation and the fork-reuse scheme both break.
- The squeeze under a pad is sub-grid and does not move cell volume; if a later fidelity study shows pads plough film sideways, that is a new flux term with its own counterexample, not a change to this one.
- Performance: only allocated tiles are visited; marking is proportional to contact samples.
- All outputs are `Estimated`; Reality `no-claim` (OPEN-HAB-8).
