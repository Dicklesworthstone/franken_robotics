## Background

GATE-HAB-07 is the acceptance gate of the Frontier mechanism "robot-advected films" (N19b-08, N19b-09). Its claim is Known
answer, its earliest milestone M6, and its threshold (a TARGET until measured) reads: relative volume residual at most
10⁻⁶ over 10⁵ steps with pickup and redeposition; spreading exponent within 2% of 1/8; the non-conservative control fails;
no cross-world leak in 10⁶ world-episodes. It tests the Frontier half of BET-36 (living worlds change decisions) at the
level of correctness; whether the mechanism is worth keeping is KILL-ADVECT's question (N19b-11), which reads this gate's
mass result as its first trigger. The project's rule is that a gate that nothing can fail validates nothing, so this gate
names its own failing control: the transport term written with the contact indicator outside the divergence.

## Scope

In: the four measurements, their fixtures, the mutant builds, the end-to-end script, the gate record with measured values
per tier.

Out: the mechanism (N19b-08, N19b-09); the ranking and cost measurements that decide KILL-ADVECT (N19b-11); the admission
gate of the sibling fluid bridge INT-FSIM-17 (BD-413).

## Technical approach

**1. Mass residual.** Fixture `fixture:spill-track` (N19b-09's, `synthetic`): a 4 m × 2 m flat floor, a 1 m × 1 m water
spill of 0.5 mm, a four-wheeled base scripted to shuttle across the spill and onto the dry floor, with the card's pickup
and transfer fractions set to ½ (assumed). The two fixtures of steps 2 and 4 are this bead's, registered like it in the
fixture registry of N24b-03 (`assets/fixtures/<name>/fixture.toml`), each supplying its design, scene and task. Run 10⁵ physics steps (200 s at 2 ms). At every step record total film volume per
world, cells plus reservoirs. Measure r = max over time of |V(t) − V(0)| / V(0), per world. Pass: r ≤ 10⁻⁶ on every world
of a 64-world batch on every present tier. (With integer quanta the expected value is exactly zero.)

**2. Spreading exponent.** Fixture `fixture:film-release`: a 2 m × 2 m flat floor with no robot (its design is a static
probe, one fixed body clear of the release, because a run binds a design); a volume of 1 litre of
the 65 mm²/s oil released as a disc of radius 0.15 m at the centre (initial thickness about 14 mm is above the 10 mm limit,
so the admission sub-cycles; the sub-cycle count is recorded). The similarity solution for a constant-volume viscous
gravity current gives radius ∝ t^(1/8) in the axisymmetric case (FACT: to be re-read from the primary source before the
gate cites it). Measure the root-mean-square radius R_rms(t) = √(Σ h·r² / Σ h) of the field, which is proportional to the
front radius for a self-similar profile and needs no wet-dry threshold. Fit log R_rms against log t by least squares over
a window fixed before the run (DESIGN: from 4 s to 256 s, a factor 64 in time, over which the radius grows by 64^(1/8) =
1.68). Pass: fitted exponent within 2% of 1/8, that is in [0.1225, 0.1275]. The planar case (a strip release, front
∝ t^(1/5)) is run and its exponent recorded beside it; it is not part of the gate's threshold.

**3. The non-conservative control.** A mutant build in which transport is 1·∇·(h·v̄) with the indicator outside the
divergence. Run measurement 1 with it. It must fail (the three-cell case loses Δt/Δx per step at the patch boundary). The
gate records the mutant's residual beside the candidate's. A second mutant removes the h³ factor from the mobility; it
must fail measurement 2 (linear diffusion spreads as t^(1/2)).

**4. Cross-world leak.** Fixture `fixture:film-leak`: a batch of 4,096 worlds in which odd-indexed worlds carry a spill
and a scripted wheel pass and even-indexed worlds are dry, run for 245 short episodes (4,096 × 245 = 1,003,520
world-episodes). After every reset and at every episode end assert: every dry world's film tiles and reservoirs are empty;
every wet world's post-reset field digest equals the digest of its declared initial condition; no tile slot of one
world's quota was written by another. Pass: zero violations. Then change each world's slot and co-tenants: 64 world
addresses drawn with the script's seed (32 odd, 32 even) are re-run alone with `--world-addr <w>` (`sim.run`, N16a-12),
which puts the world in the first slot of a batch of one; the per-address digests must equal the batch's.

**5. Recording.** Per tier (`batch-cpu` always; each device tier present; the referee where the mechanism is admitted
there), the script writes the measured residual, exponent, mutant results and leak count. The value of GATE-HAB-07 is
recorded with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-HAB-07 --receipt <summary receipt
hash>` by a session that did not implement the mechanism; the row carries commit, command, output, receipt hash,
hardware fingerprint and seed. A tier that is absent is `not run`. If the gate fails, one repair cycle is
allowed; a second failure triggers KILL-ADVECT.

**6. Evidence.** Claim kind Known answer; class `Tested`. Simulation outputs of the mechanism remain `Estimated`; Reality
`no-claim`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Relative volume residual at most 10⁻⁶ over 10⁵ steps with pickup and redeposition, on every world and present tier.
- [ ] Fitted axisymmetric spreading exponent within 2% of 1/8 over the pre-registered window.
- [ ] The masked-transport mutant fails the mass measurement; the no-h³ mutant fails the exponent measurement; both failures are recorded.
- [ ] Zero cross-world or cross-episode leaks in at least 10⁶ world-episodes, and per-address digests unchanged when 64 sampled worlds are re-run alone (`--world-addr`).
- [ ] GATE-HAB-07 has a recorded row with measured values, not targets.
- [ ] Close (N41-17 rules 1 and 4: a gate in a done-condition is its recorded value with its caught negative controls, and acceptance is the gate's row): a mass residual or exponent outside its bound is repaired once (a fix and a full re-run); a value still outside is recorded as failed, this bead closes on that record, and KILL-ADVECT (N19b-11, which depends on this bead) decides on it, so the decision never waits on a pass it exists to judge.
- [ ] The script is idempotent and seed-explicit, and exits non-zero on failure.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Fixture self-tests in `crates/frob-conformance/tests/hab_film_advect_fixtures.rs`:
- `release_volume`: the initial disc holds 1.000 litre within one quantum per cell.
- `fit_recovers_planted_exponent`: the fitting routine applied to synthetic R = c·t^(1/8) with 1% multiplicative noise returns 0.125 ± 0.001; applied to t^(1/2) it returns 0.5 (so the fit can tell the mutant).
- `leak_checker_catches_planted_leak`: a field with one stray quantum in a dry world is reported.
- `window_is_fixed_before_run`: the fit window is read from the registered fixture file, not from the data.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/film_advect_gate.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/film_advect_gate/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript). Commands per tier T (every run with its own key, so no run returns another's stored receipt): `frob sim run --fixture spill-track --tier T --worlds 64 --seconds 200 --seed 21 --telemetry T2 --trace-rate 1/1 --key fag-mass-T --json` (10⁵ steps at 2 ms, every world traced); `frob telemetry query <run> --stream contact --channels volume_total --json` (the per-step `volume_total` of N19b-09's audit); `frob sim run --fixture film-release --tier T --seconds 256 --seed 22 --telemetry T2 --trace-rate 1/1 --key fag-release-T --json` (the fixture's design is the static probe of step 2); `frob telemetry query <run> --stream contact --channels rms_radius --json` (√(Σ h·r²/Σ h) about the release centre, a T2 channel of kind State, unit m, that this bead declares in the `contact` stream beside N19b-09's `volume_total`, DESIGN; N24a-36's nine streams have no `film` stream); `frob sim run --fixture film-leak --tier T --worlds 4096 --episodes 245 --seed 23 --key fag-leak-T --json` (no length input, so each episode runs to the fixture task's horizon, `sim.run`'s `--task` rule), then for each of the 64 sampled addresses w the same with `--world-addr <w>` and `--key fag-leak-w<w>-T`, comparing its digest with the batch's for that address; then the first two again on a `frob` built with `gauntlet-mutations`, with `--mutant film-masked-transport` (key `fag-mass-mut-T`) and `--mutant film-no-cube-mobility` (key `fag-release-mut-T`) respectively, the two mutants of step 3, registered by this bead as `Control` rows of kind `mutation` in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` with their variant kernels of `K-film-advect` under `crates/frob-conformance/kernels/mut/` and loaded by N21b-42's one loader of `--mutant`; and finally `record_gate` as in step 5. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), and metrics with units and thresholds: `volume_residual_rel` (≤ 1e-6), `spreading_exponent` ([0.1225, 0.1275]), `planar_exponent` (reported), `leak_violations` (0), `mutant_residual_rel`, `mutant_exponent`, `subcycles`. State digests are logged where worlds are compared; each mutant gives one `assert` line with `caught`; on failure the first differing world, step and tile, with the run identity. The `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-HAB-07"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-442: volume constant with pickup and redeposition; relative residual at most 10⁻⁶ over 10⁵ steps; no cross-world leak in 10⁶ world-episodes; control: the non-conservative form fails the mass gate.
- KA-443: radius ∝ t^(1/8) axisymmetric, front ∝ t^(1/5) planar; exponent within 2% of 1/8; control: removing the h³ factor changes the exponent.
- KA-441 and CX-191: the three-cell case behind the first control.
- KA-458 and INV-HAB-03: mutable-state isolation; control: a pool indexed without the world address.
- Determinism lanes: the script runs measurement 1 twice on one machine and compares field digests; across worker counts on `batch-cpu`; across slots and co-tenants (measurement 4's re-runs alone); debug and release on host tiers; across backends against the kernel's class.

## Considerations

- The exponent 1/8 is very flat: a factor 64 in time moves the radius by only 68%. The fit window, the lattice (at least 15 cells of radius at the start of the window) and the use of the second moment are chosen for that; if the fit proves too noisy at 2 cm, the fixture refines the lattice, never the tolerance.
- Run time: 10⁵ steps of 64 worlds and 128,000 steps of one world are seconds to minutes on `batch-cpu`; the leak test is the longest and is sized by episode length, not by step count.
- The viscosity and transfer fractions are `assumed`; the gate tests the numerics, not the physics of a real spill.
- A pass here does not fund anything by itself: KILL-ADVECT may still fire on cost or on rank correlation.
