## Background

A kill gate names its trigger and its fallback in advance and is never quietly re-scoped; its decision is recorded as an
ADR at the milestone named in the gate register; a kill gate that fires is not a failure of the program, because the
fallback is the plan. KILL-ADVECT governs the Frontier mechanism "robot-advected films" (N19b-08, N19b-09).

**Trigger (any one):** the advected film fails its mass gate after one repair cycle; or it costs more than 15% of the step;
or design rankings on the spill battery agree with those from static patches at a rank correlation of 0.95 or above.
**Fallback:** static film-friction patches under the film law of the contact chapter; advection is not built (or, if
already built, is withdrawn behind its flag and never admitted). **Decided:** M6, with GATE-HAB-07.

Why these three. The first is correctness: a film solver that loses mass fabricates slip. The second is the price: the
mechanism has no throughput benefit, so its cost must stay small. The third is the benefit: the only reason to carry a
film field is that it changes which design wins; if rankings with and without it agree at 0.95, it changes nothing a
designer would act on. This decision settles the advection part of BET-36 (falsifier H-16: films advected by the robot
change design rankings).

## Scope

In: the three measurements, the decision rule frozen before data, the ADR, the gate-register row, and the downstream
changes on each branch.

Out: the mass gate measurement itself (N19b-10, read here); building the mechanism (N19b-08, N19b-09); the paper pilot
(N19b-07), which decides funding before any of this.

## Technical approach

**1. Freeze the rule (before any data).** Register in the delivery log: the three triggers with their numbers; the rank
statistic (DESIGN: Kendall's τ-b over the design set, chosen because the project's other rank-agreement thresholds are
stated in τ); the design set; the task family and seeds; the cost workloads and devices; the number of repair cycles (one).
List the reachable verdicts: `fired: mass`, `fired: cost`, `fired: no ranking change`, `not fired`. A threshold or the
statistic changes only by a dated amendment made before the measurement it governs.

**2. Measurement A, mass.** Read GATE-HAB-07 from `registries/gates.toml`. If it failed, one repair cycle is allowed
(a fix to N19b-09 and a full re-run of N19b-10). A second failure fires the gate.

**3. Measurement B, cost.** With the performance harness (paired runs, the disclosure block, the paired lower bound):
workloads Q (the reference quadruped batch) and one wheeled-base batch, on `batch-cpu` and on each device tier present;
arms "static patch in every world" and "advected film active in every world" on identical seeds and world counts. Report
the added step cost in percent with its paired 95% interval. The gate fires when the cost exceeds 15% of the step:
operationally, when the interval's lower end is above 15% on any workload-device pair a training batch would use; an
interval that straddles 15% is extended until it does not, up to a registered budget, and is otherwise recorded
`unresolved` and escalated to the owner (fail-closed: not admitted to training batches).

**4. Measurement C, ranking.** On the spill battery (the multi-pass low-friction crossing family, with parameters drawn
from the sub-box the pilot found to matter), evaluate every design of the frozen design set twice on common world
addresses: once with static patches, once with advection. Rank the designs by the family's scorecard metric in each arm.
Compute τ-b between the two rankings, with a bootstrap interval over world addresses. The gate fires when the rank
correlation is 0.95 or above. Also report the designs whose rank changes by more than two places and the failure classes
(`slip` on a tracked film, for example) that appear only in the advection arm: these are the benefit, stated in the
units the plan's ceiling uses (failure classes a static patch cannot produce).

**5. Decide and record.**
- `not fired`: the mechanism stays Frontier behind its flag; N19b-03's admission continues to admit it; BD-413's
  done-condition is met on this point.
- `fired`: write the fallback adoption. `registries/gates.toml` gets `killed: fallback adopted` for KILL-ADVECT. BD-413
  closes with reason `fallback` and the fallback's name ("static film-friction patches"). N19b-03's admission then
  substitutes the static patch for every `advected-film` declaration and records it. The mechanism's code, if it exists,
  stays behind its feature and is described by the name of the fallback in every status table. Dependents are re-read:
  far-field field reuse across forks loses its principal field kind (its own kill gate asks whether any admitted
  living-world fixture spends at least 0.8 of fork compute on fields); the viewer's film overlay shows static patches only;
  hazard-registry rows keep their Solid representation.
- Either way: an ADR `docs/delivery/adr/KILL-ADVECT.md` with the measured values, the frozen rule, the verdict, the
  commit, hardware fingerprints and seeds, written by a session that did not implement the mechanism.

**6. Evidence.** Measurement B is a Performance claim with its disclosure block; C is a Design comparison of methods on a
declared study; A is a Known answer. They are reported separately and never merged into one number.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_advect_rule.rs`:
- `rule_enumeration`: for every combination of (mass pass or fail after repair, cost interval below, straddling or above 15%, τ below or at least 0.95) the rule returns exactly one of the enumerated verdicts or `unresolved`.
- `tau_b_known_values`: identical rankings give 1; a single adjacent swap among 40 designs gives 1 − 2/780 = 0.99744; a reversed ranking gives −1.
- `threshold_inclusive`: τ = 0.95 fires ("0.95 or above").
- `cost_rule`: an interval [15.2%, 17%] fires; [12%, 14.9%] does not; [14%, 16%] is `unresolved` until extended.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_advect_decide.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/kill_advect_decide/`). Commands: `frob eval run --battery spill-multipass --designs <frozen set> --arm static --seed 31 --json` and the same with `--arm advect`; `frob eval rank-agreement --runs <a> <b> --json` (or the comparison verb the ranking set uses); the performance harness on workloads Q and wheeled; `frob evidence trace <claim>` for the GATE-HAB-07 row. Assertions: registration precedes data; all three measurements present; the recorded verdict equals the rule applied to the recorded values. Lines log the command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, metrics with units and thresholds (`added_step_cost_pct` with interval against 15, `tau_b` with interval against 0.95, `volume_residual_rel` against 1e-6), identities of the design set and battery, and on failure the first value that contradicts the verdict. Summary: `gates: ["KILL-ADVECT"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Planted triggers the decision script must catch: a synthetic result set in which both arms produce identical rankings (must return `fired: no ranking change`); a cost table with 20% added cost (must return `fired: cost`); a gate row marked failed twice (must return `fired: mass`).
- A decision script that returns `not fired` on any of the three is defective (a gate that nothing can fail validates nothing).
- Determinism: rankings are computed on common world addresses under class D2; the decision is a pure function of recorded values.

## Considerations

- The 0.95 threshold is on rank correlation, not on outcome agreement; with a small design set τ is coarse (one adjacent swap among 40 is 0.997), so the design set must be large enough that 0.95 is reachable from both sides. Enumerate that before sampling.
- The plan does not name the rank statistic or the design set; both are DESIGN choices here and must be confirmed by the owner of the rank-agreement experiment before the rule is frozen.
- The spill battery must contain multi-pass routes; on single-pass routes advection cannot matter and the gate would fire for the wrong reason.
- Compute: measurement C is a design-set campaign at evaluation scale; it needs the envelope class the M6 epics are funded under (owner decision on which epics are funded).
- All film parameters are `assumed` (OPEN-HAB-8); a ranking change found here is `Estimated` and carries Reality `no-claim`.
