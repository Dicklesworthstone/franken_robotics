## Background

GATE-HAB-08 (claim Known answer; earliest milestone M6; threshold a TARGET) covers the two Frontier mechanisms that are
ordinary rigid-body constructions: "Slosh and proxies: first-mode frequency within 3% at three fill depths; catenary sag
within 2% at 20 links; the free-slab rug slides in the castor case." This bead measures the first clause, the slosh rows,
for the sprung-mass payload module of N19b-13; the proxy rows are measured by N19b-18. The epic BD-414 closes on these rows.

What the gate protects: the sprung-mass model is only useful if its resonance sits where the real liquid's does, because
the failure it exists to expose is resonance between a gait and the payload. A model whose frequency ignores the fill
depth would put the resonance in the wrong place for every partly filled container.

## Scope

In: the fixture, the frequency estimator, the three fill depths, the registered negative control, the end-to-end script
and the gate record for the slosh rows.

Out: the proxy rows of the same gate (N19b-18); rankings on the carried-container battery (N19b-15).

## Technical approach

**1. Fixture (`synthetic`).** `fixture:tray-shake`: a rectangular container with inner length L = 0.30 m along the motion,
mounted on a frictionless horizontal slide that is held fixed after an initial horizontal velocity impulse to the
container, with liquid modelled by one retained mode along the motion and damping ratio ζ = 0.01 (assumed), at three fill
depths chosen before the run: h = 0.05 m, 0.10 m and 0.15 m (h/L = 1/6, 1/3, 1/2). The fill is a task parameter (N19b-13,
step 4), so the fixture is registered in N24b-03's fixture registry as three fixtures, `fixture:tray-shake-h05`, `-h10`
and `-h15`, one per depth, each supplying the tray rig, its task and its constants, hashed before the first run.

**2. Truth.** ω₁² = (π·g / L)·tanh(π·h / L) with g = 9.81 m/s²:

| h | ω₁ | f₁ |
|---|---|---|
| 0.05 m | 7.026 rad/s | 1.118 Hz |
| 0.10 m | 8.956 rad/s | 1.425 Hz |
| 0.15 m | 9.707 rad/s | 1.545 Hz |

The damped frequency differs from ω₁ by the factor √(1 − ζ²), 0.005% at ζ = 0.01, far inside the tolerance.

**3. Measurement, as numbered steps, per tier** (`referee`, `strict`, `batch-cpu`, each device tier present):
1. Run the fixture for 20 s at the tier's default step and record the modal displacement x₁(t) from telemetry.
2. Estimate the frequency from the mean period between successive upward zero crossings, each located by linear
   interpolation between samples, over all complete cycles after the first.
3. Compare with the table. Pass: relative error at most 3% at each of the three depths.
4. Repeat at half the step; the estimates must agree within 0.5% (the result is not an artefact of the step).
5. Metamorphic check: the three frequencies are strictly increasing in fill depth.

**4. Negative control.** A mutant build whose admission uses the deep-water formula (tanh dropped), giving 10.136 rad/s at
every depth: relative errors 44%, 13% and 4.4% against the table, so it must fail at all three depths. The gate records
the mutant's failure in the same run.

**5. Recording.** The gate's value comes from a receipt of the known-answer tier (KA-446 is a G1 row), never from a
file the script writes. This bead registers the slosh rows as entries of `g1_known_answers` against GATE-HAB-08
(module `crates/frob-conformance/tests/hab/slosh_gate.rs`: steps 1 to 5 per depth and present tier) with the
deep-water mutant as their control under `gauntlet-mutations`, and the script ends with `e2e_tier g1_known_answers
GATE-HAB-08 --mutations`. That run selects every entry registered against the gate, so once N19b-18 has registered
the proxy rows they are proved again in it. GATE-HAB-08 has one part (M6) and two measuring beads in the register
(N19c-29: this bead and N19b-18); each records that part from the receipt of its own run of the whole gate as it
then stands, the later recording being the one in force (as the waves of GATE-CHRON-08 are, N29b-06 item 10), with
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-HAB-08 --receipt <hash>` by a session that did
not implement the module; `measured` names the row groups the receipt covers, with the frequencies per depth and
tier, commit, command, output, receipt hash, hardware fingerprint and seed. Absent tiers are `not run`. The gate as a whole passes when both these rows and the proxy rows have
passed; if these rows fail, one repair cycle is allowed before KILL-SLOSH fires.

**6. Evidence.** Claim Known answer, class `Tested`. Outputs of the model in tasks stay `Estimated`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] First-mode frequency within 3% of 7.026, 8.956 and 9.707 rad/s at fill depths 0.05, 0.10 and 0.15 m, on every present tier.
- [ ] Halving the step changes each estimate by less than 0.5%.
- [ ] The deep-water mutant fails at all three depths and its failure is recorded.
- [ ] The slosh rows of GATE-HAB-08 are recorded with measured values, by `record_gate` from the receipt of `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-HAB-08 --mutations`, whose controls list the deep-water mutant as `caught`.
- [ ] Close (N41-17 rules 1 and 4: a gate in a done-condition is its recorded value with its caught negative control, and acceptance is the gate's row): a depth outside 3% is repaired once (a fix to N19b-13 and a full re-run); a value still outside is recorded as failed, this bead closes on that record, and KILL-SLOSH (N19b-15, which depends on this bead) fires on it, so the decision never waits on a pass it exists to judge.
- [ ] The script is idempotent, seed-explicit and exits non-zero on failure.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/hab_slosh_fixture.rs`:
- `truth_from_formula`: the three table values are computed, not typed in.
- `estimator_on_synthetic`: the zero-crossing estimator applied to a sampled damped sinusoid at 1.425 Hz, 500 Hz sampling, ζ = 0.01, returns 1.425 Hz within 0.01%; applied at 100 Hz sampling, within 0.05%.
- `fixture_is_single_mode`: the fixture's module has exactly one sprung mass along the motion.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/slosh_gate.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/slosh_gate/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript). Per tier T (`referee`, `batch-cpu`, each device tier present, and for the `strict` row `batch-cpu` with `--profile strict`, key suffix `strict`, since `strict` is a step profile of `sim.run` and not a `--tier` value) and depth h (`h05`, `h10`, `h15`): `frob sim run --fixture tray-shake-<h> --tier T --seconds 20 --seed 41 --telemetry T2 --key sg-<h>-T --json` (`--fixture` is the one spelling of a registered fixture, which supplies the rig and the task, so neither a positional design nor `--task` is passed; T2 so the modal channel is recorded); `frob telemetry query <run> --stream kinematics --channels slosh_disp --json` (the mode-1 displacement column N19b-13 declares in the `kinematics` stream); the half-step run with `--step <half the tier's default>` and `--key sg-<h>-T-half` (`sim.run`'s `--step <duration>` option, N16a-03); then the same on the mutant build (`--features gauntlet-mutations`, `--key sg-<h>-T-mut`); every run has its own key, so none returns another's stored receipt; then the gate's tier run `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-HAB-08 --mutations` (asserts the receipt's GATE-HAB-08 line `pass` and the deep-water mutant `caught`; the `cmd` line carries the receipt hash). The script stops there and never records (STD-E2E's `self-recording` lint); the part is recorded from that hash by a separate session, as in step 5. Each line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), and metrics with units and thresholds: `omega1_rad_s`, `rel_error` (≤ 0.03), `half_step_change` (≤ 0.005), `mutant_rel_error`. The mutant gives one `assert` per depth with `caught`. On failure: the first depth and tier that differ and the run identity. Summary: assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-HAB-08"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-446: ω₁ = 8.96 rad/s (1.43 Hz) for L = 0.30 m and h = 0.10 m, within 3% at three fill depths (G1, all tiers); control: halving the fill must lower the frequency (8.956 → 7.026 rad/s).
- Negative control registered: the deep-water mutant.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine with equal digests; across worker counts on `batch-cpu`; debug and release; across backends against each tier's class (the 3% bound is the D3 envelope where bit equality is not held).

## Considerations

- The three depths are a DESIGN choice (the plan says "three fill depths" without naming them); they span the tanh range from 0.48 to 0.92 so that a fill-insensitive model cannot pass.
- The gate checks the model against linear theory, not against a real liquid; Reality stays `no-claim`.
- A zero-crossing estimator is used instead of a spectral peak to avoid resolution limits on a 20 s record.
