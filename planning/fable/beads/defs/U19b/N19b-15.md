## Background

KILL-SLOSH governs the Frontier mechanism "payload slosh by an equivalent mechanical model" (BD-414, N19b-13). A kill gate
names its trigger and fallback in advance, is decided at the milestone in the gate register and recorded as an ADR, and
firing is not a failure: the fallback is the plan.

**Trigger (either):** the frequency gate (the slosh rows of GATE-HAB-08) fails after one repair cycle; or the sprung
masses change no ranking of gaits or designs on the carried-container battery. **Fallback:** rigid ballast, with a spill
metric computed from payload acceleration. **Decided:** M6.

Why the second trigger. The mechanism has no throughput benefit; its only value is that it changes a decision: which gait
to use with a tray, which design carries a pot. If the ranking of gaits and the ranking of designs are the same with sprung
masses as with rigid ballast, the extra degrees of freedom buy nothing and ballast plus a spill metric says as much.

## Scope

In: the frozen decision rule; the carried-container battery and its two arms; the fallback's definition (rigid ballast
and the spill metric), which must exist whether or not the gate fires because it is the Solid alternative; the ADR; the
gate-register row; downstream changes on each branch.

Out: the frequency measurement (N19b-14, read here); the module (N19b-13); the pilot (N19b-12). If that pilot's
falsifier held, the mechanism was never funded (N19b-13 and N19b-14 closed `withdrawn` citing the pilot and its `[[pilot.result]]`, N41-17 rule 7) and
this bead defines the fallback and records the outcome `not-reached` (step 5).

## Technical approach

**1. The fallback, defined first (DESIGN).** Rigid ballast: the liquid is a rigid body of mass m_l at the liquid's centre,
fixed in the container. Spill metric from payload acceleration: with a_h the horizontal acceleration of the container
along one of its horizontal axes of inner length L, a_v its vertical acceleration, g gravity, H the container height and
h the fill depth, the quasi-static free surface tilts by tan θ = |a_h| / (g + a_v) and rises at the wall by
(L/2)·tan θ. The spill margin is s = (H − h) − (L/2)·|a_h| / (g + a_v), evaluated per axis at every control tick; the
episode's metric is its minimum, and the liquid is counted spilled when s < 0. The plan states only that the metric is
computed from payload acceleration; this formula is the DESIGN reading and is labelled `Estimated` (it ignores sloshing
dynamics by construction). The same metric is reported in the sprung arm so the two arms share an outcome.

**2. Freeze the rule before data.** Register: both triggers; the battery (tasks, container range, designs, gaits, seeds,
world addresses); the ranking metric (the task family's scorecard success with the spill margin as a requirement term);
the evidence level at which a pairwise reversal counts (an anytime-valid paired comparison at the level the comparison
verb uses); the damping ratios to sweep (the damping is `assumed`, so the rule is evaluated at a low and a high value);
one repair cycle for the frequency gate. Reachable verdicts, in N40-03's outcomes: `fired` with the trigger that fired
(`frequency` or `no ranking change`, named in the ADR), `not-fired`, `not-reached` (step 5), and `deferred` only by an
ADR with `deferred_to` and `reason`.

**3. Measurement A, frequency.** Read the slosh rows of GATE-HAB-08 from `registries/gates.toml`. On a failure one repair
cycle (a fix to N19b-13 and a re-run of N19b-14) is allowed; a second failure fires the gate.

**4. Measurement B, rankings.** The carried-container battery: the open-container payload-transport tasks on the
warehouse and kitchen proxies, containers drawn from the sub-box the pilot found inside the step-frequency band, one
legged and one wheeled reference robot plus the design set registered in step 2, and for the legged robot a set of gaits
at several step frequencies. Two arms on common world addresses: (i) sprung masses, (ii) rigid ballast, registered with
the rule in step 2 as two frozen battery variants that differ only in the payload declaration (`carried-container-sprung`
and `carried-container-ballast`). For each arm rank
the gaits (per robot) and rank the designs by the metric. The sprung masses "change a ranking" when at least one pair of
gaits or one pair of designs is ordered differently in the two arms and the reversal is supported at the registered
evidence level in a paired comparison. If no such pair exists at either damping value, the gate fires.
Report alongside: the count of `slosh-tip` failures in the sprung arm, the gaits whose step frequency lies within 10% of
the container's first mode, and the added degrees of freedom and step cost.

**5. Decide and record.**
- `not-reached` (the pilot N19b-12's falsifier held, read from its pilot register record): no measurement is taken; the
  fallback of step 1 is in force; the ADR lists N19b-13 and N19b-14 as "withdrawn before this decision"; BD-414 closes
  `fallback` citing it.
- `not-fired`: the module stays Frontier behind `frontier-slosh`; N19b-03 keeps admitting it.
- `fired`: the KILL-SLOSH row of `registries/gates.toml` gets its `decision` with outcome `fired`, and N40-03's
  propagation (step 7: it kills only a read gate that failed) sets GATE-HAB-08 to `killed` with detail
  `KILL-SLOSH: rigid ballast with a spill metric` when the gate failed (trigger `frequency`); on `no ranking change`
  a GATE-HAB-08 that passed keeps `pass`. The register keeps one status per gate, so a killed GATE-HAB-08 covers
  the proxy rows too, whose own reading is N19b-19's; BD-414 closes with reason `fallback`
  and the name "rigid ballast with a spill metric"; N19b-03's admission substitutes ballast for every sloshing-payload
  declaration and records it; the kitchen hazard row "hot liquid in a pot or tray" is represented by ballast and the
  spill metric; the failure class `slosh-tip` is no longer produced and reports say the payload is rigid.
- In every case the ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SLOSH-M6.md` in N40-03's decision format (the one directory for decision
  records): the trigger as registered and any dated amendments; the measured values with their receipts, commit, hardware
  fingerprints and seeds, beside the trigger (or, for `not-reached`, the pilot record's hash); the frozen rule and the
  verdict; the outcome (`not-fired`, `fired`, `not-reached` or `deferred`); the fallback in force and the name the
  capability carries; the beads re-read, each "stands on the fallback", "superseded by this ADR" or "withdrawn before
  this decision"; and the session that recorded it, which did not implement the module. The KILL-SLOSH row gets
  `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }`, `receipts` listing the receipt hashes of the measurements the
  outcome rests on (at least one for `not-fired`; for `not-reached`, the pilot result's receipt).

**6. Claims.** Measurement B is a Design comparison of methods on a declared study; A is a Known answer. Both arms'
outputs are `Estimated`; Reality `no-claim`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-scene/tests/spill_metric.rs` and `crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_slosh_rule.rs`:
- `spill_margin_values`: L = 0.30 m, H = 0.12 m, h = 0.10 m, a_v = 0: a_h = 1.0 m/s² → rise 15.3 mm, s = +4.7 mm (no spill); a_h = 1.5 m/s² → rise 22.9 mm, s = −2.9 mm (spill); the threshold acceleration is g·2·(H − h)/L = 1.308 m/s².
- `spill_margin_vertical`: a_v = −g/2 (descending step) halves the threshold to 0.654 m/s².
- `ballast_mass_and_position`: the ballast body has mass ρ·L_x·L_y·h at height h/2 above the container floor.
- `rule_enumeration`: every combination of (frequency pass or fail after repair) × (supported reversal exists or not, at each damping value) maps to one enumerated verdict.
- `reversal_needs_evidence`: two rankings that differ only by a pair whose paired comparison is inconclusive count as no change.
- `pilot_falsified_is_not_reached`: a pilot record whose falsifier holds gives outcome `not-reached` without reading either measurement.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_slosh_decide.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/kill_slosh_decide/`). Commands: for each design of the registered set, `frob eval run <design> --battery carried-container-sprung --key ksd-<design>-sprung --json` and `frob eval run <design> --battery carried-container-ballast --key ksd-<design>-ballast --json` (distinct keys per arm; the battery's seed is part of its frozen identity); `frob eval scorecard <run> --json` on each run; `frob eval compare <a> <b> --task <task> --battery <arm battery> --key ksd-cmp-<pair>-<arm> --json` (the paired comparison verb, once per arm with its own key, so neither arm returns the other's stored receipt) on each candidate reversal; `frob evidence trace <clm:…> --json` on the claim identifiers of the GATE-HAB-08 slosh rows. Assertions: registration precedes data; both arms ran on identical world addresses; the recorded verdict equals the rule applied to the recorded values. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N19b-15"), `step`, `event`; lines log the exact command line, exit code, wall time, evidence block, the two rankings as ordered identity lists, each candidate reversal with its evidence value and level, `slosh_tip_count`, `added_dof`, `added_step_cost_pct`, one `assert` per planted case with `caught`, and on failure the first value that contradicts the verdict. Summary: assertions passed and failed, the outcome and the ADR path, `gates: ["KILL-SLOSH", "GATE-HAB-08"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- The spill threshold 1.308 m/s² above is a hand-checkable known answer of the fallback metric.
- Planted cases the decision script must catch: identical rankings in both arms (must return `fired: no ranking change`); a gate row failed twice (must return `fired: frequency`); a ranking with one supported reversal (must return `not-fired`).
- Determinism: both arms run under class D2 on common addresses; the decision is a pure function of recorded values.

## Considerations

- The damping ratio decides how sharp the resonance is and is `assumed`; a verdict that flips between the low and high damping values is recorded as `not-fired` with the dependence stated, and OPEN-HAB-8-style sourcing is requested for the damping.
- The set of gaits must include step frequencies on both sides of the container's first mode, or the battery cannot show a resonance and the gate would fire for the wrong reason; enumerate this before sampling.
- A wheeled carrier excites slosh by braking and turning, not by stepping; include a braking-profile comparison (two deceleration profiles as the "gaits" of the wheeled robot).
- Compute is an evaluation-scale campaign and falls under the envelope the M6 epics are funded with.
