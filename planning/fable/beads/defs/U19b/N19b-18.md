## Background

GATE-HAB-08 (claim Known answer; earliest milestone M6; threshold a TARGET): "Slosh and proxies: first-mode frequency
within 3% at three fill depths; catenary sag within 2% at 20 links; the free-slab rug slides in the castor case." The
slosh rows are measured by N19b-14. This bead measures the two proxy rows and the cost target of the articulated proxies
("a world with one cord costs at most twice a rigid-only world of the same robot"), which KILL-PROXY reads. The epic
BD-415 closes on these rows.

What each row protects. The catenary is the simplest shape a chain of links must get right: if 20 links do not hang
like a cord, they will not drape over a threshold or round an axle like one. The castor case is the hand calculation
that justifies representing a rug as a free body at all: a fixed step cannot reproduce a rug that is pushed instead of
climbed.

## Scope

In: the two fixtures, the measurements, the registered negative controls, the cost measurement, the end-to-end script and
the gate record for the proxy rows.

Out: the slosh rows (N19b-14); the pilot (N19b-16); the kill decision (N19b-19); the rigid proxies themselves,
the castor fixture's scene and task files with its fixed-step variant, and the first measurement of the castor
case at M3 as the seed condition and known-answer record of KA-440 (BD-231, a dependency; that bead writes no
gate row, and this bead defines no second castor scene: `fixture:castor-rug` is BD-231's fixture registered as
a fixture task); the chain cord itself (N19b-17).

## Technical approach

**1. Hanging chain (`fixture:hanging-chain`, `synthetic`).** A chain cord of length 1.2 m, N = 20 links, bending stiffness
zero and light joint damping, both ends anchored by spherical joints to supports 1.0 m apart at equal height, released
and settled on the referee until kinetic energy is below the settle threshold. It is written as a design file,
`fixtures/hab/hanging-chain-20.fbot` (N19b-17's chain module with both ends anchored to the world), with the control
variant `hanging-chain-2.fbot`. Truth: the catenary with parameter a
solving 1.2 = 2a·sinh(0.5/a), a = 0.4695 m, sag a·(cosh(0.5/a) − 1) = 0.292 m. Measure the vertical distance from the
support line to the lowest joint. Pass: within 2% of 0.292 m, that is in [0.2865, 0.2982] m. (The exact polygon of 20
pinned links sags 0.2927 m, 0.13% above the catenary, so a correct implementation has margin.) Also record the horizontal
reaction at an anchor against the catenary's value w·a (w the weight per unit length).

**2. Castor at a rug edge (`fixture:castor-rug`, `synthetic`).** A rug as a thin free rigid slab (the Solid proxy of
BD-231): mass 2 kg, edge height h = 10 mm, rug-to-floor friction 0.5 (assumed), on a flat floor. An undriven castor of
radius r = 25 mm carrying W = 50 N is pushed horizontally against the edge by a force ramped slowly, at 2 N/s as in
BD-231, from 0 to 80 N. The fixture is BD-231's (scene `fixtures/scenes/proxies/castor-rug.src`, task
`fixtures/castor-rug.ftask`), registered as the fixture task `fixture:castor-rug`.
Truth, by statics: to climb the edge the castor needs W·tan α with tan α = √(h·(2r − h))/(r − h) = 4/3, that is 66.7 N;
the slab resists sliding with about 0.5 × 19.6 N ≈ 9.8 N. The rug slides long before the castor climbs. Measure the push
force at which the slab first moves more than 1 mm (the onset) and the rise of the castor's axle. Pass, by the one rule
of the castor case that BD-231 states (DESIGN; the plan fixes only the verdict): the slab slides, with its onset within
10% of 9.8 N, and when the slab has moved 50 mm the castor's axle has risen less than 1 mm, so the castor has not begun
to climb. (An earlier draft of this row asked only that the axle had not risen by the full edge height h; the two beads
measure one known answer and use one threshold, the stricter.)

**3. Negative controls (registered; each must be caught in the same run).**
- The chain built with N = 2 links: sag 0.332 m, 13% off, must fail the 2% bound. A single 1.2 m link cannot be anchored
  at both supports at all and must be refused by the fixture ("a chain with no bending limit and one link fails").
- The rug replaced by a fixed step of the same height: the "rug" never slides, and the castor blocks until about 66.7 N
  and then climbs; the row's assertion "the slab slides" must fail ("a fixed step is climbed or blocks").

**4. Cost.** With the performance harness (paired runs, disclosure block): the reference wheeled base in a rigid-only
home hallway against the same scene with one 20-link cord on the floor, on `batch-cpu` and each device tier present, at
equal world counts and seeds. Report the step-cost ratio with its paired 95% interval. TARGET: at most 2. The figure is
recorded for KILL-PROXY; it is not part of GATE-HAB-08's threshold.

**5. Tiers.** Both rows are referee known answers; they are also run on `strict` and `batch-cpu` and reported, since
articulated proxies are meant to run in batches. A tier that cannot complete the chain fixture because of contact or
closure limits is recorded with its refusal, not skipped silently.

**6. Recording.** The proxy rows of GATE-HAB-08 are recorded with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate --
--gate GATE-HAB-08 --receipt <summary receipt hash>` by a session that did not implement the proxies, with measured sag,
slide onset, control results, commit, command, output, receipt hash, hardware fingerprint and seed. Claim Known answer, class `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Sag of the 20-link chain within 2% of 0.292 m on the referee; anchor reaction reported.
- [ ] The free slab slides in the castor case and the castor does not climb first.
- [ ] The two-link chain fails the sag bound; the fixed-step control fails the slide assertion; both failures are recorded.
- [ ] The cord cost ratio is measured with its interval and recorded against the 2× target.
- [ ] The proxy rows of GATE-HAB-08 are recorded with measured values; the script is idempotent, seed-explicit and exits non-zero on failure.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/hab_proxy_fixtures.rs`:
- `catenary_truth`: solve 1.2 = 2a·sinh(0.5/a) by bisection → a = 0.46954, sag 0.29234.
- `castor_truth`: tan α = √(0.010 × 0.040)/0.015 = 4/3; 50 N × 4/3 = 66.67 N; slab resistance 0.5 × 2 × 9.81 = 9.81 N.
- `fixture_chain_is_20_links_zero_bending`.
- `slide_detector`: a synthetic displacement trace crossing 1 mm at a known time is detected at that sample.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/proxy_gate.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/proxy_gate/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript). Commands (each run with its own key, so none returns another's stored receipt): `frob sim run fixtures/hab/hanging-chain-20.fbot --tier referee --seconds 20 --seed 61 --key pg-chain-20 --json`; `frob telemetry query <run> --stream proxy --json` (the joint heights of N19b-17's `proxy` stream); `frob sim run fixtures/castor-probe.fbot --scene <BD-231's castor-rug pack> --task fixtures/castor-rug.ftask --tier referee --seed 62 --key pg-castor --json`; the two control variants, `fixtures/hab/hanging-chain-2.fbot` with `--key pg-chain-2` and BD-231's fixed-step pack `castor-step.src` with `--key pg-castor-step`; the same fixtures with `--tier batch-cpu --profile strict` (`strict` is a step profile of `sim.run`, not a `--tier` value) and with `--tier batch-cpu`, keys suffixed `strict` and `batch-cpu`; the performance harness pair for the cost; finally `record_gate` as in step 6. Lines carry `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), and metrics with units and thresholds: `sag_m` ([0.2865, 0.2982]), `anchor_reaction_n`, `slide_onset_n` (9.8 ± 10%), `castor_rise_m` (< 0.001 at 50 mm of slide), `control_sag_m`, `control_slid` (false), `cord_cost_ratio` with interval (target ≤ 2). Each control gives one `assert` line with `caught`. On failure: the first differing value and the run identity. Summary: assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-HAB-08"]`.

## Considerations

- The slide-onset tolerance, the 1 mm onset travel, the 50 mm slide distance and the 1 mm bound on axle rise are DESIGN choices, the same in BD-231; the plan fixes only the verdict. The record of this row cites BD-231's M3 record of KA-440 and states whether the M6 values agree with it.
- Friction 0.5 between rug and floor is `assumed`; with a much higher value (above 66.7/19.6 ≈ 3.4) the verdict would reverse, which is physically implausible and is stated so the fixture's parameters are not mistaken for a general law.
- The chain anchored at both ends is a closure through the world; if a tier refuses it, hang the chain from two posts by contact instead and record the change.
- The cost ratio depends on contact count; report the number of active contacts beside it.
