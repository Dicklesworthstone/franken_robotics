## Background

KILL-PROXY governs the Frontier articulated proxies: the chain cord and the hinged-plate rug (BD-415, N19b-17). A kill gate
names its trigger and fallback in advance, is decided at the milestone in the gate register (M6) and recorded as an ADR;
firing is not a failure, because the fallback is the plan.

**Trigger (either):** the proxy pilot's falsifier fires, that is, the Solid proxies (the fixed ridge or free rod, and the
free slab) already reproduce the failures of the articulated ones (the chain and the hinged plates); or the cost exceeds
its target (a world with one cord costs at most twice a rigid-only world of the same robot) with no route to less.
**Fallback:** Solid proxies in training batches; articulated proxies in evaluation batches only, or not at all.

Why these triggers. The articulated proxies have no throughput benefit; their value is the failure classes they expose
that the Solid proxies miss (a cord wrapped round an axle, a rug folded at its edge). If the Solid proxies already
reproduce those failures, the articulated ones add cost and nothing else. If they do add failures but cost too much,
they can still earn their keep in evaluation, where worlds are few.

## Scope

In: the frozen rule; reading the pilot's share T and the measured cost ratio; the choice among the fallback's two forms;
the ADR; the gate-register row; downstream changes on each branch.

Out: running the pilot (N19b-16); measuring the gate rows and cost (N19b-18); the island decision (N19b-20), which reads
the same pilot against the opposite question. When the pilot alone fires the gate (T ≥ 0.90 for both mechanisms), the
proxies are not built: N19b-17 and N19b-18 close `withdrawn` citing the pilot N19b-16 and its articulated-proxies
`[[pilot.result]]` (N41-17 rule 7; never counted as work done), so this decision does not wait for a build it makes
unnecessary. When the envelope row R23 is not approved, the pilot does not run (N19b-16 closes `withdrawn`, `not run: no
envelope, R23`), N19b-17 and N19b-18 close `withdrawn` citing R23, and this bead records `not-reached` (N44-01).

## Technical approach

**1. Freeze the rule before data** (registered with the pilot, so before either measurement): 
- Reading 1 (pilot): T, the share of the articulated proxies' failures in the pilot (cord and rug scenes, both robots)
  that the Solid proxies reproduce in the same failure class within one second. The falsifier fires when T ≥ 0.90
  (DESIGN: the plan states the falsifier in words and gives no number; 0.90 mirrors the threshold the plan sets for the
  island tier and is fixed before data). The share is read per scene as well: if it fires for cords and not for rugs,
  the decision is per mechanism.
- Reading 2 (cost): the cord cost ratio from N19b-18 with its paired interval. "Above the target with no route to less"
  is decided after one optimization pass on the profile: the ratio's interval lies wholly above 2 after that pass, and
  the profile names no remaining lever (fewer links, contact culling, evaluation-only use).
- Reachable verdicts, per mechanism: `not fired`; `fired: solid suffices` (not built); `fired: cost` (evaluation batches
  only). Enumerate which are reachable at the pilot's failure counts before reading T. They map onto N40-03's four
  outcomes: `not fired` is `not-fired`; both `fired:` verdicts are `fired`, with the fallback form ("not at all" or
  "evaluation batches only") in the ADR; `not-reached` when R23 is not approved or the pilot did not run; `deferred`
  only by an ADR with `deferred_to` and `reason` (for example a second optimization pass). KILL-PROXY has no
  `scope_kind` in the register (N40-03's table has no per-mechanism kind; one is requested), so the row carries one
  decision: `fired` when either mechanism's verdict is a `fired:` verdict, `not-fired` when neither is, with the
  per-mechanism verdicts and forms in the ADR.

**2. Decide, as numbered steps.**
1. Read T per scene from N19b-16's articulated-proxies record in the pilot register (tier `F`, kill gate KILL-PROXY;
   the tier-`M` record sharing the same protocol and receipt is KILL-LIVING's). If T ≥ 0.90 for a mechanism: `fired: solid suffices` for it. Its
   fallback form is "not at all": the mechanism is not built or is withdrawn; the Solid proxy is the product.
2. Otherwise read the cost ratio. If within 2 (interval's upper end at most 2, or its point value at most 2 with the
   interval recorded): `not fired`; the mechanism stays Frontier and may be admitted to any batch.
3. Otherwise run one optimization pass and re-measure. If the re-measured interval lies wholly above 2 and the profile
   names no remaining lever: `fired: cost`; if the interval still straddles 2, or a lever remains, `not fired` with the
   interval and the remaining lever recorded in the ADR (the reading of step 1 of the rule is not relaxed). Its
   fallback form is "evaluation batches only": the admission rule of N19b-03 refuses the mechanism in training batches
   and substitutes the Solid alternative there, with the substitution recorded.
4. Record.

**3. Downstream on each branch.**
- `not fired`: nothing changes; BD-415's done-condition is met on this point.
- `fired: solid suffices`: the KILL-PROXY row of `registries/gates.toml` gets its `decision` with outcome `fired`, and
  N40-03's propagation sets the rows that read it (the proxy rows of GATE-HAB-08) to `killed` with detail
  `KILL-PROXY: Solid proxies`; BD-415 closes with reason `fallback` ("Solid proxies"); N19b-17 and N19b-18, if not yet
  built, have closed `withdrawn` citing the pilot's `[[pilot.result]]` and are listed in the ADR as "withdrawn before
  this decision" (if built, they are re-read as "superseded by this ADR"); hazard rows that name hinged plates or a chain cord
  are represented by their Solid forms; reports about cords and rugs say "rigid proxy".
- `fired: cost`: outcome `fired` with the fallback's name "Solid proxies in training batches; articulated proxies in
  evaluation batches only"; the proxy rows stay recorded for evaluation batches; the decision entry names the form
  (`fallback_form`, requested of N40-03), which N19b-03's admission reads (its step 2): a chain cord or hinged rug is
  admitted under `--purpose evaluation`, `oracle-check` or `counterexample-verification` and substituted, with reason
  `purpose-not-eligible`, under `training` or `throughput`. A training estimate or run that requires the articulated
  tier (`--purpose training --require-living-tier F`) is refused with `FROB-REF-726` (proposed in N32-REF), reason
  `purpose-not-eligible` (requested of N32-REF's row and of VERBS-sim's `sim.estimate` line), exit 5, retry
  `after-change`, block `mechanism`, `reason`, `declared_tier`, `run_purpose` and `decision` (this ADR's path); ranked
  repairs: (1) drop `--require-living-tier` and accept the Solid alternative; (2) run under `--purpose evaluation`.
- `not-reached` (R23 not approved, or the pilot did not run): no reading is taken; the fallback is in force from the
  start; N19b-17 and N19b-18 are listed in the ADR as "withdrawn before this decision"; BD-415 closes `fallback`
  ("Solid proxies") citing the ADR.
- In every case the ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-PROXY-M6.md` in N40-03's decision format (the one directory for decision
  records): the trigger as registered and any dated amendments; T per scene and its interval, the cost ratio and
  interval, each with its receipt, commit, fingerprints and seeds, beside the trigger's numbers; the frozen rule and the
  verdict per mechanism; the outcome; the fallback form in force and the name the capability carries; the beads re-read,
  each "stands on the fallback", "superseded by this ADR" or "withdrawn before this decision"; and the session that
  recorded it, which did not implement the proxies. The KILL-PROXY row gets
  `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }`, `receipts` listing the pilot result's receipt and the cost
  record's (at least one for `not-fired`).

**4. Claims.** T is a Fidelity figure between model forms on an incumbent instrument; the cost ratio is a Performance
figure with its disclosure block. They are reported separately.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rule (both readings, the 0.90 number, the per-mechanism split, the optimization-pass allowance) is registered before the pilot's data exists (commit order).
- [ ] T per scene and the cost ratio are cited from their records with intervals.
- [ ] The verdict per mechanism is one of the enumerated verdicts and the row's outcome one of N40-03's four, mapped as in step 1; `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-PROXY-M6.md` exists with every field N40-03 requires; the KILL-PROXY row of `registries/gates.toml` carries `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }`, and on `fired: solid suffices` the proxy rows of GATE-HAB-08 read `killed` with their detail.
- [ ] On `fired: cost`, a training batch that declares a chain cord runs with the Solid alternative and records the substitution (reason `purpose-not-eligible`), while an evaluation batch runs the chain; the same training batch with `--require-living-tier F` is refused with `FROB-REF-726`, reason `purpose-not-eligible`.
- [ ] If R23 is not approved, the outcome `not-reached` is recorded with the ADR and BD-415 closes `fallback`; this bead never waits for a pilot that cannot run.
- [ ] The decision script catches the planted cases below.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_proxy_rule.rs`:
- `rule_enumeration`: (T below or at least 0.90) × (cost within 2, above 2 before the pass and within after, above 2 after the pass) per mechanism maps to exactly one verdict.
- `threshold_inclusive`: T = 0.90 fires.
- `per_mechanism_split`: T_cord = 0.95, T_rug = 0.60 yields `fired: solid suffices` for the cord and proceeds to the cost reading for the rug.
- `uninformative_pilot`: a scene with fewer than the registered minimum of failures yields no reading and the mechanism stays unfunded until the pilot is extended (fail-closed).
- `outcome_mapping`: (`fired: solid suffices` for the cord, `not fired` for the rug) gives row outcome `fired` with both verdicts in the ADR; (`not fired`, `not fired`) gives `not-fired`; an R23 record of status `declined` gives `not-reached` without reading T.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_proxy_decide.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/kill_proxy_decide/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript). It reads the pilot record and the cost record (`frob evidence trace <clm:…> --json` on their claim identifiers), applies the rule, and compares with the recorded verdict and the gate row's `decision`; on a `fired: cost` record it also runs `frob sim estimate <wheeled design> --scene <cord scene> --worlds 1024 --seconds 10 --purpose training --json` (asserts the substitution with reason `purpose-not-eligible`), the same with `--require-living-tier F` (asserts `FROB-REF-726`, exit 5, `reason = purpose-not-eligible`; `--require-living-tier` on `sim.estimate` is requested of VERBS-sim, and until the row lists it this step is logged with `e2e_skip` naming the option), and the same with `--purpose evaluation --require-living-tier F` (asserts the chain admitted, no substitution). Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N19b-19"), `step`, `event`; lines log the exact command line, exit code, wall time, evidence block, `share_T` per scene with interval against 0.90, `cord_cost_ratio` with interval against 2, the verdict per mechanism, one `assert` per planted case with `caught`, and on failure the first value that contradicts the verdict. Summary: assertions passed and failed, the outcome and the ADR path, `gates: ["KILL-PROXY", "GATE-HAB-08"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Planted cases the decision script must catch: a pilot record with T = 1.0 (must return `fired: solid suffices`); a cost record of 3.1 after the pass (must return `fired: cost`); T = 0.4 with cost 1.6 (must return `not fired`).
- A decision script that returns `not fired` on the first two is defective.
- Determinism: the decision is a pure function of recorded values.

## Considerations

- The 0.90 reading for T is a DESIGN number; the owner may amend it by a dated note before the pilot runs, never after.
- T compares Solid proxies with articulated proxies inside the incumbent engine; if the incumbent's articulated proxies are themselves poor stand-ins (low S_F in the pilot), a high T means little. Read T together with S_F and say so in the ADR.
- Contact count drives the cord's cost; "no route to less" must consider fewer links (8 is the floor) before the gate fires on cost.
- The decision is independent of KILL-LIVING, though both read one pilot.
