## Background

KILL-LIVING decides whether the Moonshot tier of living worlds, coupled islands (a free-surface lattice fluid in a small
box, an implicit cloth, a Cosserat rod, each beside one world on the CPU referee, for finalists only), is ever built. It
is the kill gate behind ADR-HAB-07 and the decisive gate of BET-36 ("a contact-level film-friction law and rigid proxies
stand in for coupled matter; whether they reproduce the failures of coupled models is the pilot").

**Trigger (either):** the proxies reproduce 90% or more of the full-model failures (the number GATE-HAB-09 reports), or
an island costs more than 181 rigid worlds at 5% activity with no route to less. **Fallback, named now:** the Solid tier
and the Frontier proxies are the product; coupled islands are not built beyond the oracle fixtures that already exist in
the sibling libraries; reports say that deformable matter is represented by proxy. **Decided:** M6, before the island
work package.

Why the two triggers. If the proxies already fail the way resolved matter fails, an island adds an expensive oracle for
answers the proxies give. And an island that costs more than the throughput bound allows cannot even be used sparingly:
the bound comes from requiring a batch with coupled matter active in 5% of worlds to keep a tenth of its rigid-only
throughput, 0.95 + 0.05·c ≤ 10, so c ≤ 181 rigid worlds per coupled world.

This is a Moonshot decision: nothing on the critical path waits for it, and the program holds at most four active Moonshot
items, chosen by the owner.

## Scope

In: the frozen rule; reading the pilot's pooled share; the island cost reading; the ADR; the gate-register row; what
changes downstream on each branch.

Out: the pilot itself (N19b-16); the island contract and any island code (N19b-21, BD-416); the kill gate of the
articulated proxies (N19b-19).

## Technical approach

**1. Freeze the rule before data** (registered with the pilot):
- Reading 1: S_F, the pooled share of full-model failures on the incumbent instrument that the proxies (a 20-link chain,
  a hinged thin plate, a friction patch, and the patch with the dwell-time law) reproduce in the same failure class
  within one second. Fires at S_F ≥ 0.90. The share is also read per scene (cord, rug, oil), because the three island
  kinds (rod, cloth, fluid) are separate work: a per-kind verdict is recorded where the pooled reading does not fire.
- Reading 2: island cost c in rigid worlds, at 5% activity. From the refutation arithmetic: one cubic metre of fluid at
  5 mm holding lattice Mach number at most 0.1 at 1 m/s needs about 3,460 fluid steps of 8 × 10⁶ cells per simulated
  second, which is an offline computation; the sibling cloth solver reports tens of CPU seconds per drape for a few
  thousand vertices. The reading is taken from measured sibling costs at admission where available, otherwise from this
  arithmetic, labelled as an estimate. "No route to less" means no smaller box, coarser lattice or shorter window that
  still serves the island's two purposes (an oracle for the proxies on finalists; verification of a counterexample
  attributed to living matter).
- Reachable verdicts, per island kind: `fired: proxies suffice`; `fired: cost`; `not fired`.

**2. Decide, as numbered steps.**
1. Read pooled S_F and its interval from the GATE-HAB-09 row. If S_F ≥ 0.90: `fired: proxies suffice` for all kinds.
2. Otherwise, per kind, read the per-scene share; a kind whose scene share is at least 0.90 is `fired: proxies suffice`.
3. For each remaining kind, read c. Note the bound applies to activity inside batches; an island used offline beside one
   referee world for a finalist is outside a batch, so the cost reading fires only if the island cannot complete its two
   purposes within the evaluation budget registered for finalists (DESIGN reading of "no route to less"); record the
   arithmetic either way.
4. Record.

**3. Downstream.**
- `fired` (any kind): `registries/gates.toml` gets `killed: fallback adopted` for KILL-LIVING (with the kinds named);
  BD-416 closes with reason `fallback` for those kinds ("Solid tier and Frontier proxies; islands not built"); N19b-21
  closes `superseded` for them; N19b-03's admission refuses island declarations of those kinds everywhere and
  substitutes the proxy; every report that involves deformable matter carries the sentence "deformable matter is
  represented by proxy"; the upstream work item for dynamic rods and a batched cloth stays at priority P3 with no
  consumer in this program.
- `not fired` (a kind): that island kind may be proposed for funding under the Moonshot cap; N19b-21 becomes eligible.
- In every case an ADR `docs/delivery/adr/KILL-LIVING.md` with S_F pooled and per scene, intervals, the cost reading and
  its provenance, the frozen rule, the verdicts, commit and instrument pin, written by a session that did not run the pilot.

**4. Claims.** S_F is a Fidelity figure between model forms on an incumbent instrument, class `Tested`. The cost reading is
DESIGN arithmetic unless measured.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rule is registered before the pilot's data exists (commit order), with the per-kind split and the reading of "no route to less".
- [ ] S_F pooled and per scene are cited from the GATE-HAB-09 record with intervals and failure counts.
- [ ] The verdict per island kind is one of the enumerated verdicts; the ADR exists; `registries/gates.toml` carries the KILL-LIVING row.
- [ ] On `fired`, island declarations of the killed kinds are refused or substituted by admission and reports carry the proxy sentence.
- [ ] The decision script catches the planted cases below.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_living_rule.rs`:
- `rule_enumeration`: pooled share below or at least 0.90, per-scene shares, cost within or beyond budget: exactly one verdict per kind.
- `threshold_inclusive`: 0.90 fires ("90% or more").
- `bound_181`: the largest c with 0.95 + 0.05·c ≤ 10 is 181; c = 182 violates the bound.
- `island_arithmetic`: 1/(0.1 × 0.005/√3) = 3,464 steps per second of 8.0 × 10⁶ cells.
- `uninformative_pilot`: fewer full-model failures than the registered minimum gives no reading; islands stay unfunded (fail-closed).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_living_decide.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/kill_living_decide/`). It reads the GATE-HAB-09 record (`frob evidence trace`), applies the rule, compares with the recorded verdicts, and, on a `fired` record, runs `frob sim estimate` on a scene that declares an island of a killed kind and asserts the not-admitted refusal or the recorded substitution, and `frob report run` on a proxy episode asserting the proxy sentence. Lines log the command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, `share_frontier_pooled` and per scene with intervals against 0.90, `island_cost_rigid_worlds` against 181, verdicts per kind, and on failure the first contradicting value. Summary: `gates: ["KILL-LIVING"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- CX-192 supplies the arithmetic behind reading 2 (152 bytes per lattice cell; 45.6 GB for a room at 1 cm; lattice Mach 3.5 at 1 cm and a 50 Hz step against a limit of 0.3; c ≤ 181).
- Planted cases: a pilot record with S_F = 0.93 (must return `fired: proxies suffice` for all kinds); S_F = 0.70 with oil-scene share 0.95 (must fire for the fluid island only); S_F = 0.50 with all per-scene shares below 0.90 and cost within budget (must return `not fired`).
- Determinism: a pure function of recorded values.

## Considerations

- A share of 90% measured with a few dozen failures has a wide interval; the ADR states it, and the owner may fund a larger pilot before accepting a `not fired` that would open Moonshot spending.
- The pilot's full models are an incumbent's, not truth; `not fired` licenses a proposal, not a claim that islands are accurate.
- If the sibling bridge's admission test fails, the fluid film stays the Solid film law and coupled islands are dropped regardless of this gate.
- The proxy sentence in reports is part of the fallback and must be tested, because it is the only place a reader learns that cords and rugs were rigid.
