## Background

Every number that makes a film slippery in this simulator is `assumed`: the combined roughness σ on interface cards (which
sets where the film law hands over to boundary friction), and, for films the robot moves, the carried thickness and the
transfer fraction per revolution. The plan records this as an open question.

**OPEN-HAB-8.** Sources for film roughness, carried thickness and transfer fraction. **Fail-closed default (in force until
decided):** the values are `assumed`, every output of the film mechanisms is `Estimated`, and the Reality cell is
`no-claim`. **Owner:** Habitat lead. **Blocks:** any Reality claim about slip on films. The contact chapter has the sibling
question OPEN-TRAC-3 (provenance for film and soil cards: which measured tables are admitted; default generic ranges
labelled `assumed`; owner project lead; blocks claims of realism for wet floors, not validity).

The film law also carries a stated HYPOTHESIS with a named test. The law assumes a smooth, rigid, flat pad; treads and
rough floors drain faster, so the drainage time is an upper bound and the predicted slip is pessimistic for treaded soles.
The test is a spill-pad traction dataset imported through the Reality lane; until one exists the law's outputs stay
`Estimated`.

The project's rule for anything that needs a bench measurement: it is written as an import lane with a recorded `no-claim`
until the data exists, and predictions are frozen before comparison. This bead is that lane for films, plus the owner's
decision on sources. It blocks no mechanism; it is what must happen before anyone may say a simulated slip is realistic.

## Scope

In: the decision record for OPEN-HAB-8 (and the film half of OPEN-TRAC-3); the dataset schema for spill-pad traction
measurements; the frozen-prediction procedure for the film law; the hypothesis test; a `synthetic` rehearsal; the rule that
turns `assumed` card fields into `measured` ones; the refusal of realism claims while the default holds.

Out: collecting data (no bench exists; sourcing is the owner's); the general Reality lane machinery (dataset admission,
sealed splits, bands: the Ground beads); the film mechanisms themselves.

## Technical approach

**1. The decision to obtain.** Ask the owner, with the options written out: (a) admit a cited published source for σ on
common floor and sole material pairs and for film transfer on rolling contacts, recorded in the source register with its
licence; (b) commission or import a spill-pad measurement (below); (c) leave the default in force. Record the answer as an
ADR. Until an answer other than (c) is recorded, nothing else in this bead changes any card.

**2. Dataset schema (DESIGN), an import under the ground-data manifest.** One row per trial: pad identifier and geometry
(radius R, tread pattern and effective land area, material); floor material and its measured roughness; fluid (density,
viscosity at the measured temperature); initial film thickness h₀ and how it was set; normal load F and its time profile;
dwell time before the tangential pull; tangential speed; measured friction ratio μ(t); instrument uncertainty for each
quantity; trial conditions. For the advected-film parameters: carried film thickness on a wheel leaving a wetted strip
(by mass or by optical thickness) and the deposited thickness per revolution over at least four revolutions.

**3. Frozen predictions.** Before measured μ(t) is opened: for each trial, compute the law's prediction from the trial's
inputs with the card as it stands: thinning by h⁻²(t + Δt) = h⁻²(t) + 4·F·Δt/(3π·η·R⁴); film ratio Λ = h/σ;
μ = w(Λ)·μ_b + (1 − w(Λ))·μ_v with w(Λ) = clamp((3 − Λ)/2, 0, 1), where μ_b is the card's boundary coefficient for the
condition and μ_v = min(μ_b, η·|v_t|·π·R²/(h·F)) is the viscous (Couette) term at the trial's tangential speed v_t, in the
capped form that governs (the cap is settled in N19b-05 and BD-230; a contact where it binds is flagged
`viscous-capped`, and its prediction is reported with that flag). Store predictions and the card identity under a digest;
the Reality lane refuses a comparison whose prediction digest postdates the data's unsealing.

**4. The hypothesis test.** H: for treaded soles the measured time to boundary friction (first time μ(t) reaches 0.9·μ_b)
is at most the predicted time (the law is pessimistic about slip duration). Decision rule fixed before unsealing: over the
treaded trials, the share with measured time ≤ predicted time, with an exact binomial interval; H is supported when the
interval's lower end exceeds one half, refuted when its upper end is below one half, otherwise undecided. For smooth flat
pads the law should be neither pessimistic nor optimistic: report the ratio of measured to predicted time and its spread.

**5. What changes when data is admitted.** σ on the affected interface cards gets provenance `measured: <dataset>` with its
uncertainty; a new card revision is written (cards are immutable and content-addressed); results recomputed with the new
revision may carry a reality band for film slip for the calibrated family; the pilot of the advected film (N19b-07) is
re-run on the sourced parameter box. Everything else stays `assumed`.

**6. Refusal while the default holds.** A report or claim that states a simulated slip on a film is representative of a
real floor is refused through the reality line that every report carries: with any `assumed` film field in the claim's
lineage, the line reads `reality: no-claim` and a request to print otherwise is refused. Under `--strict-data`, a verdict
that depends on an `assumed` film field is refused outright.

**7. Rehearsal (`synthetic`).** A generated dataset with planted σ and a planted tread speed-up factor, produced by the
film law itself plus noise, used to test the lane end to end: the frozen-prediction order is enforced, the planted factor
is recovered, and H is reported correctly for a planted pessimistic and a planted optimistic law.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The owner's answer to OPEN-HAB-8 (and the film half of OPEN-TRAC-3) is recorded as an ADR, or the default is recorded as still in force with the date asked.
- [ ] The dataset schema is registered and a `synthetic` rehearsal dataset passes admission.
- [ ] Predictions are frozen before unsealing in the rehearsal, and a comparison with a late prediction digest is refused.
- [ ] The hypothesis rule returns `supported`, `refuted` and `undecided` on three planted rehearsal datasets.
- [ ] With every film field `assumed`, a report asserting realism of slip on a film is refused and prints `reality: no-claim`.
- [ ] No card field changes provenance without an admitted dataset or a cited source in the source register.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-ground/tests/film_lane.rs` (the lane) and `crates/frob-scene/tests/film_provenance.rs`:
- `prediction_for_trial`: R = 20 mm, F = 100 N, η = 1.0 mPa·s, h₀ = 1 mm, σ = 5 µm: time to Λ = 1 (h = 5 µm) is 3π·η·R⁴/(4·F·h²) = 0.151 s; time to 10 µm is 0.038 s.
- `freeze_before_unseal`: a prediction digest created after unsealing is refused.
- `hypothesis_rule`: 40 treaded trials with 30 early → supported (lower bound above ½); 10 early → refuted; 20 early → undecided.
- `provenance_only_with_source`: attempting to set σ to `measured` without a dataset identity is refused.
- `strict_data_refuses_assumed_film`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/film_reality_lane.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/film_reality_lane/`), in the registered forms of the Reality lane's verbs (the film lane adds no flag of its own): `frob ground ingest <synthetic spill-pad dataset> --design <pad rig> --task-family <pad pull> --key frl-ingest --json`; `frob ground predict --dataset <id> --part holdout --cell <the pad rig's cell> --model <pad rig> --scene <spill-pad scene> --task <pad pull> --metric time_to_boundary_s:0.90 --decision <the rule of step 4, typed> --study fixed-n:40 --key frl-pred --json` (freezes the predictions; the simulated side is the film law on the trial's inputs with the card as it stands); `frob ground score <prediction> --key frl-score --json` (opens the sealed part once: hypothesis verdict and time ratios); a second `frob ground predict` on the same sealed part after the score, with its own key `frl-pred-late` (asserts FROB-REF-883: a late prediction cannot be compared); `frob report run <film episode> --json` with assumed cards (asserts `reality: no-claim` on the slip statement); the same call with `--reality calibrated` (asserts the schema refusal FROB-REF-802: no parameter sets a status); and a copy of that report edited to read `reality: calibrated […]`, fed back through `frob artifact read --json` (asserts the reality guard's FROB-REF-889: no band record for the cell). Lines log the command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, `share_early`, its interval, `time_ratio_median`, the verdict, digests of predictions and data with their order, and on failure the first trial that differs. The rehearsal data is `synthetic` and labelled so in every line.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-438 supplies the law's times (0.038 s, 2.3 s, 0.76 s) that the prediction code must reproduce on smooth-pad trials.
- Negative controls: a rehearsal dataset generated with the planted law but scored against a card with σ doubled must be reported as a mismatch (the lane can detect a wrong card); a report that prints a realism sentence with `assumed` film fields must be caught by the reality line's negative battery.
- Determinism: predictions are exact functions of trial inputs; identical across platforms.

## Considerations

- No dataset exists and none may ever be collected; the correct steady state is then `no-claim`, permanently and visibly. That is an acceptable outcome of this bead.
- A published roughness table would move σ only; carried thickness and transfer fraction would stay `assumed`, and the advected-film mechanism would remain `Estimated` with Reality `no-claim`.
- Data licences: a measured table admitted to the card catalogue needs a licence that permits redistribution with the cards, or it stays a local import.
- The same lane shape serves the damping ratio of sloshing payloads and the flexural rigidity of cords, which are also `assumed`.
