## Background

The default lattice of a clearance complex (cells of 5 cm, 32 headings) has an error term of 74.6 mm for a footprint of
reference radius 0.40 m. A body centred in a doorway has clearance equal to half the total margin, and a witness needs a
positive lower bound, so a doorway fit with total margin under 2e ≈ 149 mm comes back `unresolved`. The hazard lists put
home doors at 0.70 to 0.90 m and plausible bases at 0.45 to 0.70 m; if both are drawn uniformly, about 22% of pairs have a
margin under 149 mm, and they are the near fits, the only cases in which the question is hard (counterexample CX-194: "the
default clearance lattice alone answers doorway fits"). The plan's conclusion: the default lattice alone is not the
mechanism; **local refinement is part of it**. Cells whose interval [c_lo, c_hi] straddles the query margin and that lie
on a candidate bottleneck are subdivided in x, y and ψ. At Δ = 12.5 mm and 128 headings e is 18.7 mm; at Δ = 5 mm and 512
headings it is 6.0 mm (DESIGN arithmetic). Below that the floor is 2·(ε_field + u_reg): about 2 mm in a generated scene,
and tens of millimetres in a scan, where the correct output is a measurement request.

The kill gate reads this bead's result directly: KILL-FCLEAR fires if more than 30% of doorway queries on the archetype
battery are still `unresolved` after local refinement. Tier Frontier; crate `frob-scene`.

## Scope

In: selection of cells to refine; subdivision and recomputation of their values; the iteration and its stop rules; the
floor; budgets and cancellation; recording patches in the `.fclear`; the hand-off to a probe request when the floor is the
obstacle.

Out: cell values at one level (N19b-26); the sweep (N19b-28); the probe-request verb (BD-291); the verb surface (N19b-32).

## Technical approach

**1. What a refinement step does.** Subdividing a cell by a factor of two in x, y and ψ replaces it with eight children,
each with Δ/2 and δ/2, so the geometric part of e, (Δ/2)·√2 + ρ·(δ/2), halves. Children are computed exactly as base
cells are (N19b-26), with the obstacle raster re-evaluated at a pitch that keeps the raster term below the children's
geometric term (DESIGN: pitch at most a tenth of the child Δ, never coarser than the scene's field bound allows). The
children are stored as a patch (N19b-27). Mixed levels are adjacent across shared face area.

| Level | Δ | Headings | Geometric e at ρ = 0.40 m |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 50 mm | 32 | 74.6 mm |
| 1 | 25 mm | 64 | 37.3 mm |
| 2 | 12.5 mm | 128 | 18.7 mm |
| 3 | 6.25 mm | 256 | 9.3 mm |
| 4 | 3.125 mm | 512 | 4.7 mm |

(The plan's 6.0 mm figure is for Δ = 5 mm with 512 headings, a non-dyadic combination; the dyadic ladder above brackets it.)

**2. The loop, as numbered steps, for a query (margin w, start, goals):**
1. Run the sweep (N19b-28). If the verdict is `Witnessed` or `Impossible`, stop.
2. Otherwise collect the **candidate bottleneck cells**: cells that straddle the margin (c_lo ≤ w < c_hi) and lie on the
   frontier returned by the sweep, that is, on the outer-complex connection between the start's inner component and the
   goals. Add their straddling face neighbours once (a one-cell halo), so that a refined doorway is not boxed in by
   coarse cells.
3. Stop with `Unresolved: floor` if every candidate's geometric e is already below the floor term ε_field + u_reg:
   further subdivision cannot separate c_lo from c_hi. In an ingested scene emit the data for a probe request (the
   three-dimensional box round the bottleneck, the quantity needed, for example "the clear width of door D3", and the
   accuracy that would settle it, namely the current band); nothing is assumed in its place.
4. Stop with `Unresolved: budget` if the job's budget (maximum level, maximum added cells, wall time) is exhausted or the
   job is cancelled; return the refined complex so far, w_in, w_out and the frontier.
5. Subdivide every candidate by one level in all three axes, compute children, append the patch, and go to step 1.
The loop terminates: each pass strictly lowers the candidates' e or stops.

**3. Soundness under refinement.** Each child's values are valid enclosures for its own box, so INV-HAB-06 holds cell by
cell at every level; refinement can only move cells from "straddling" to inner or to excluded, so w_in never decreases and
w_out never increases (the band only narrows). A refined complex therefore never reverses an earlier `Witnessed` or
`Impossible`.

**4. Defaults (DESIGN).** Maximum level 4; maximum added cells 4 million per query (16 MiB); the nominal-footprint
complex at scene compile refines only for the doorways on the family's routes. All three are request parameters.

**5. Reporting.** The answer carries the refinement record: levels used, cells added, the final band [w_in, w_out), the
floor value and which stop rule ended the loop. `unresolved` answers always say why: `band` (budget), `floor: field`,
`floor: registration`, or `unknown-region`.

**6. Determinism.** Candidate selection, subdivision order and patch order are functions of cell indices; the refined
`.fclear` is byte-identical for equal requests on every platform (D0).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On a doorway fixture with total margin 60 mm (body 0.60 m, door 0.66 m, ρ = 0.40 m) the base lattice returns `Unresolved` and refinement to level 2 or 3 returns `Witnessed`.
- [ ] On the corridor fixture a 1.30 m body becomes `Witnessed` and a 1.40 m body `Impossible` after refinement, with the limit 1.346 m inside the reported band at every pass.
- [ ] w_in is non-decreasing and w_out non-increasing over passes on 1,000 random queries; no pass reverses a decided verdict.
- [ ] A fit whose margin is below 2·(ε_field + u_reg) stops with `Unresolved: floor` and, in an ingested fixture, with the probe-request data.
- [ ] Budget exhaustion and cancellation return a consistent partial complex and release all memory; the same request re-run to completion gives the same final bytes as an uninterrupted run.
- [ ] Refinement touches only candidate cells and their halo: the count of added cells on the doorway fixture is under 1% of the base lattice.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-scene/tests/clearance_refine.rs`, `cargo test -p frob-scene --features frontier-fclear clearance_refine`.
- `level_table`: geometric e per level equals the table (74.6, 37.3, 18.7, 9.3, 4.7 mm at ρ = 0.40 m).
- `doorway_60mm`: base `Unresolved` (needs 2e < 60 mm, so e < 30 mm: level 2); after refinement `Witnessed` with w_in > 0.
- `corridor_bracket`: at each pass L_in ≤ 1.3456 ≤ L_out for the family of lengths.
- `band_monotone`: property test over random scenes and queries.
- `floor_stop`: a generated fixture with ε_field = 1 mm and margin 1.5 mm stops at the floor; the answer names `floor: field`.
- `registration_floor`: an ingested fixture with u_reg = 15 mm and margin 20 mm stops with `floor: registration` and probe data naming the door and ±band.
- `budget_and_cancel`: cancel after the first pass; partial complex valid; no leaked allocations; a resumed run matches.
- `halo_once`: neighbours are added once per pass, not recursively.
- `deterministic_patches`: equal requests give equal patch bytes.

### End-to-end script and logging
Through `scene.fit` in `scripts/e2e/scene_fit_gate.sh` (N19b-35), which exercises refinement on the corridor pairs and the doorway battery. This bead adds to it a cancellation case: `frob scene fit ... --sync-wait-ms 0` (job handle, exit 8), `frob job cancel <id>`, `frob job status <id>` (drained, finalized), then a fresh run to completion. Lines log the command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, per pass `level`, `cells_added`, `w_in_mm`, `w_out_mm`, the stop rule, and on failure the first cell whose values differ from an uninterrupted run.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-449 (18.7 mm at 12.5 mm and 128 headings; 6.0 mm at 5 mm and 512 headings), KA-451 (unresolved under 149 mm at the default lattice), CX-194 (about 22% of uniform door and base pairs are near fits).
- Negative controls: a refinement that subdivides only in x and y (the heading term stays at 39.3 mm and the doorway fixture never resolves: the test must catch it); a refinement that recomputes children with the parent's raster (the raster term dominates and the band stalls above the expected value); a loop without the floor stop (it must be caught by `floor_stop` exhausting its budget).
- Metamorphic: refining a decided region changes no verdict; refining twice in one request equals refining in two requests.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; across platforms.

## Considerations

- KILL-FCLEAR's second trigger is measured on this loop's output: more than 30% of doorway queries on the archetype battery still `unresolved` after refinement. The battery must report the stop rule of each unresolved query, because "unresolved at the floor of a scan" is the method working and "unresolved at budget in a generated scene" is the method failing.
- Cost grows with the area of straddling cells; a long narrow corridor at near-fit width refines along its whole length. The budget makes that visible; the estimate is reported before a job is admitted.
- The floor in a generated scene is about 2 mm; thresholds of that order belong to the transition predicates, not to the planar complex.
- Honest limit: refinement narrows the band; it never turns a geometric answer into a statement about steering or traction.
