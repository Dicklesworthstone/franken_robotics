## Background

GATE-HAB-10 is the acceptance gate of clearance complexes (claim Known answer; earliest milestone M3; threshold a TARGET
until measured): the corridor closed form is bracketed by witness and cut within the refined band on 100 (a, b) pairs;
zero false witnesses and zero false cuts on the seven archetype fixtures against a brute-force grid at four times the
resolution; the checker accepts every emitted certificate and rejects six mutated ones. One false witness or false cut
that survives a repair cycle fires KILL-FCLEAR. This bead is the measurement, and it is also the end-to-end test of the
user-visible capability `scene.fit`: it drives the real `frob` binary on compiled scenes, from the user's side.

Why these three clauses. The corridor has an exact answer, so it tests the bounds against truth, in both directions and at
the hardest place (near the limit). The archetype fixtures test real scene geometry, where no closed form exists, against
an independent exhaustive search. The certificate clause tests that what the verb says can be re-checked by a program
that did not produce it, and that the checker can say no.

## Scope

In: the corridor battery; the seven archetype fixtures with their brute-force comparators; the certificate accept and
reject checks; the doorway `unresolved` statistics that KILL-FCLEAR reads; the registered negative controls; the script;
the gate record.

Out: the mechanism and verb (N19b-26 to N19b-32); the pairwise predicate's gate (N19b-33, GATE-TRAC-07); the kill decision
(N19b-36).

## Technical approach

**1. Corridor battery (100 pairs).** For each of 100 seeded pairs (a, b), corridor width a from 0.80 to 1.50 m and body
width b from 0.40·a to 0.85·a, generate and compile an L-shaped corridor scene of width a with a right-angle turn. Truth:
a rectangle of width b and length L can be carried round the corner exactly when L ≤ 2√2·a − 2b (derivation: with the
outer long side touching both outer walls at inclination θ, the inner corner clears the body when
L ≤ (a·sin θ + a·cos θ − b)/(sin θ·cos θ); the right-hand side is smallest at θ = 45°); for a = 0.90 m and b = 0.60 m the
limit is 1.346 m. For each pair, by bisection on L with `scene.fit` at margin zero and refinement on:
L_in = the largest length returned `witnessed`, L_out = the smallest returned `impossible`. Pass, per pair:
L_in ≤ L* ≤ L_out (no witness above the limit, no cut below it) and L_out − L_in at most the stated refined band
(twice the final e plus the floor 2·(ε_field + u_reg), reported by the verb). The pairs are kept at b ≤ 0.85·a because a
rectangle whose two sides are both at most a can also pass by pure translation, without the rotating manoeuvre the closed
form describes; above b ≈ 0.914·a the true limit is L ≤ a. Ten extra pairs with b from 0.92·a to 0.98·a are run against
that truth, max(a, 2√2·a − 2b), and reported beside the gate (DESIGN addition; not part of the 100).

**2. Archetype fixtures (seven).** One compiled structural-proxy scene per archetype (home, apartment, office, warehouse,
manufacturing plant, retail store, commercial kitchen), each with at least three route queries built from its hazard
list: a doorway or lane near the footprint's width (for example home door 0.70 to 0.90 m, retail checkout lane 0.6 to
0.9 m), a right-angle turn (apartment corridor 0.90 to 1.10 m, kitchen galley 0.9 to 1.2 m), and an open route. For each
query and for footprints straddling the passable limit:
- **Brute-force comparator** (independent code in `frob-conformance`): a grid at four times the resolution in x, y and ψ
  (cells of Δ/4 and four times the headings) over the same slab, with collision decided by exact tests of the footprint
  polygon against the scene's obstacle set evaluated from the pack with its bound, in two variants: a conservative-free
  variant (a grid cell is free only if the footprint inflated by the grid cell's own reach is collision-free) and a
  conservative-blocked variant (blocked only if the deflated footprint collides).
- **False witness:** any configuration sampled on the witness path (each path cell sampled at its 27 lattice points)
  collides in the exact test, or the conservative-blocked variant shows start and goal disconnected.
- **False cut:** the conservative-free variant finds a connected free path from start to goal.
Pass: zero false witnesses and zero false cuts over all queries.

**3. Certificates.** Every `witnessed` and `impossible` answer in clauses 1 and 2 is bundled and re-checked by
`frob-verify` on a host without the product binaries: all accepted. The six registered mutations (a non-adjacent witness
step; a witness cell with c_lo not above the margin; a witness that misses the start or a goal; a cut with an unlabelled
cell whose c_hi exceeds the margin; a cut with adjacent cells of different labels; a cut whose start and goal share a
label) are applied to specimens from the battery: all rejected, each at its expected rule.

**4. Doorway statistics (read by KILL-FCLEAR).** On the archetype battery (100 generated scenes per archetype, every door
and lane on a declared route, the family's nominal footprint and the reference robots' footprints), record the share of
doorway queries still `unresolved` after local refinement, split by stop rule (band at budget, field floor,
registration floor, unknown region). Reported, not thresholded here.

**5. Negative controls (registered; each must fail the gate when planted).** The inscribed-disc and circumscribed-disc
tests in place of the complex (wrong on the corridor in both directions); the cell bound without its heading term
(a false witness near the corridor limit); a witness search with diagonal adjacency (a false witness through a corner
contact on an archetype fixture); a checker that skips the adjacency rule (accepts mutation 1).

**6. Recording.** GATE-HAB-10's row in `registries/gates.toml` is entered by a session that did not implement the
mechanism: per-pair L_in, L*, L_out and band; counts of queries, false witnesses, false cuts; certificates accepted and
mutants rejected; commit, command, output, receipt hash, hardware fingerprint, seeds. Claim Known answer, class `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On 100 (a, b) pairs: L_in ≤ 2√2·a − 2b ≤ L_out and L_out − L_in within the refined band, for every pair.
- [ ] On the seven archetype fixtures: zero false witnesses and zero false cuts against the brute-force grid at four times the resolution.
- [ ] `frob-verify` accepts every emitted certificate and rejects the six mutated ones.
- [ ] Every registered negative control fails the gate when planted, in the same run.
- [ ] The doorway `unresolved` share after refinement is recorded per archetype with stop rules.
- [ ] GATE-HAB-10 has a recorded row with measured values; the script is idempotent, seed-explicit and exits non-zero on failure.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/hab_fclear_battery.rs`:
- `corridor_truth`: (0.90, 0.60) → 1.3456; the ten wide pairs use max(a, 2√2·a − 2b).
- `corridor_scene_generator`: the compiled L-corridor has the requested width within the brick bound (door-gauge style check: a go gauge of width a − 2·bound passes, a no-go gauge of a + 2·bound is blocked).
- `brute_force_self_test`: on an analytic doorway the conservative-free variant finds a path exactly when the door is wider than the body by more than its grid reach, and the conservative-blocked variant blocks exactly when narrower.
- `mutation_factory`: each of the six mutations changes exactly the intended property of a specimen.
- `bisection_monotone`: `witnessed` answers are monotone in L on a pair (no `witnessed` above an `impossible`).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/scene_fit_gate.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/scene_fit_gate/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript). From the user's side: `frob scene gen --family fixture/l-corridor --param width=<a> --seed <s> --json`; `frob scene fit <id> --footprint rect:<b>x<L> --from west --to north --json` inside the bisection; `frob scene gen` and `frob scene fit` on the seven archetype fixtures; `frob prove bundle` and `frob-verify` on each decided answer; the mutated bundles; the same `scene_fit` calls through `frob-mcp` on a sample, comparing envelope digests; the verb-surface cases contributed by N19b-32 (refusals, memo hit, job handle, cancellation). Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), and metrics with units and thresholds: per pair `L_in_m`, `L_star_m`, `L_out_m`, `band_m`; `false_witnesses` (0), `false_cuts` (0), `certs_accepted`, `mutants_rejected` (6 of 6), `unresolved_share` per archetype. Digests are logged where CLI and MCP answers or repeated runs are compared. On failure: the first offending pair or query, the configuration (x, y, ψ) that collides or the brute-force path found, and the scene and complex identities. Final `summary`: counts and `gates: ["GATE-HAB-10"]`.

## Considerations

- The brute-force comparator is itself a discretization; the two conservative variants make its verdicts one-sided, so a disagreement is always a defect of the complex or of the comparator, never grid noise. Disagreements are triaged with the exact collision test at the reported configuration.
- Run time: 100 pairs × about 12 bisection steps with refinement, plus seven fixtures at four times the resolution (64 times the cells of the base lattice per query region); the brute force is restricted to the neighbourhood of each route to stay within minutes on the host.
- The translation regime for wide bodies is a correction to the closed form's stated domain; it is reported so that no one later "fixes" a correct witness for a wide, short body.
- A failure allows one repair cycle; a false witness or cut that survives it fires KILL-FCLEAR.
