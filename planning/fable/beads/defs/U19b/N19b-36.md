## Background

KILL-FCLEAR governs the Frontier mechanism "clearance complexes" (construction C-33; BD-233 and its children). A kill gate
names its trigger and its fallback in advance and is never quietly re-scoped; its decision is recorded as an ADR at the
milestone named in the gate register (M3); a kill gate that fires is not a failure, because the fallback is the plan.

**Trigger (either):** one false witness or one false cut that survives a repair cycle; or more than 30% of doorway queries
on the archetype battery still `unresolved` after local refinement. **Fallback:** the route-graph traversability check for
a nominal footprint, labelled heuristic, with the feasibility tag reduced to `unresolved` wherever the heuristic and a
fine brute-force grid disagree. **Decided:** M3.

Why these triggers. The mechanism's whole value is a two-sided, certified answer. A false witness sends a design into
rollouts it cannot pass; a false cut removes a design that fits, and it does so with a certificate, which is worse than
no answer. And a mechanism that returns `unresolved` for nearly a third of the near fits decides too little to be worth
its machinery; the near fits are the only cases in which the question is hard.

Many beads stand behind this decision: feasibility tags for generated scenes, the place stage of preflight, the
solvability oracle of the adversarial search, mission queries' route conditions and the retrofit search all consume
clearance answers. The decision is therefore one step more urgent than its milestone.

## Scope

In: the frozen rule; reading the gate battery's false-answer counts and the doorway statistics; the fallback's definition
(it must exist on either branch, because it is also the heuristic comparison); the ADR; the gate-register row; downstream
changes on each branch.

Out: the measurements (N19b-35); the mechanism; the consumers' own handling of `unresolved`.

## Technical approach

**1. The fallback, defined first.** The route-graph traversability check for a nominal footprint: the scene's route graph
has edges through doorways, aisles and transitions, each annotated at compile with its clear width and height from the
scene geometry (measured by the compiler with the field bound); a nominal footprint, reduced to a width and a turning
diameter, passes an edge when its width plus twice the bound is below the edge's clear width, and passes a turn when its
turning diameter is below the junction's inscribed width. The answer is `passable` or `blocked` per route and is always
labelled `heuristic`: it is a scalar test, known to be wrong in both directions for non-disc bodies (a 0.60 m × 1.30 m
body in a 0.90 m right-angle corridor is one counterexample), and it carries no certificate. The feasibility tag under the
fallback: wherever the heuristic and a fine brute-force grid (the gate's comparator) disagree for the nominal footprint,
the tag is reduced to `unresolved`; elsewhere it is the heuristic's answer, labelled. The check lives in `frob-scene`
and is reached through `scene.fit` with the optional input field `fallback` (`none` by default, or `heuristic`; DESIGN,
added by this bead to `frob.scene.fit.in/1` as an additive field, with the help-schema snapshot of VERBS-scene
regenerated); on the `fired` branch the default becomes `heuristic` and the complex path is off.

**2. Freeze the rule before data.** Register: the two triggers with their numbers; what counts as a repair cycle (one fix
to the mechanism followed by a full re-run of the gate battery); the doorway population (the archetype battery: 100
generated scenes per archetype, every door and lane on a declared route, the nominal footprint and the reference robots'
footprints); the reading of "unresolved after local refinement" (refinement at its default budget; all stop rules count,
and the split by stop rule is reported, with the generated-scene share read against 30% because generated scenes have no
registration floor). Reachable verdicts: `fired: false answer`, `fired: unresolved share`, `not fired`.

**3. Decide, as numbered steps.**
1. Read the GATE-HAB-10 record. If it shows zero false witnesses and zero false cuts, trigger one does not fire.
2. If it shows any, allow one repair cycle and re-read. If a false witness or cut is still present (the same or a new
   one), the gate fires: `fired: false answer`.
3. Read the doorway statistics: U = the share of doorway queries still `unresolved` after refinement over the registered
   population. If U > 0.30, the gate fires: `fired: unresolved share`. Report U per archetype and per stop rule.
4. Otherwise `not fired`.

**4. Downstream.**
- `not fired`: clearance complexes are the product at their Frontier tier; BD-233's done-condition is met on this point.
- `fired`: the fallback is in force; BD-233 closes with reason `fallback` ("route-graph
  traversability check for a nominal footprint, labelled heuristic"). Then, bead by bead: `scene.fit` answers from the
  fallback, labelled heuristic, with no certificates and no standing cuts (so refusal 309 is never raised); feasibility
  tags follow the rule of section 1; the place stage of preflight falls back to A-07 passability on route
  graphs; the solvability oracle's route condition becomes a heuristic necessary condition and says so; the retrofit
  search loses impossibility cuts as evidence and localizes from counterexample worlds and alias witnesses only; the
  `complex-connectivity` proof kind has no producer and is disabled; reports print "route feasibility: heuristic".
  Dependents that need a certified cut are re-read and closed `superseded` or re-scoped.
- Either way an ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FCLEAR-M3.md` with the required fields of the kill-gate record of N40-03: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as
  registered and any amendments; each measured value (the false-answer counts before and after the repair cycle, U and
  its split) with its receipt hash beside the trigger's number; the outcome; the fallback in force and the name the
  capability carries from now on; the beads re-read, each "stands on the fallback" or "superseded by this ADR"; what
  changes downstream; plus the frozen rule, commit, fingerprints and seeds. It is written by a session that did not
  implement the mechanism.
- Either way the KILL-FCLEAR row that the fallback ledger N40-24 entered in `registries/gates.toml` gets its decision
  entry in the form of N40-03, `decision = { outcome, adr = "docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FCLEAR-M3.md", date, session, receipts }`,
  through the recorder: `fired: false answer` and `fired: unresolved share` are outcome `fired` (the reason is kept in
  the ADR), `not fired` is `not-fired` with at least one receipt (the GATE-HAB-10 record and the doorway statistics).
  The owner declaration (`[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`, the ledger's `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and
  `reads` copied, not restated, and once decided its `decision` with the measured values and their receipts) is written
  in `frob-scene`'s `contract.toml`, which the cross-check N40-30 compares with the ledger row.

**5. Claims.** The counts are Known-answer results of the gate; U is a measured share on a named population. Neither is
a statement about real buildings.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The fallback check exists, is reachable through `frob --json`, is labelled `heuristic` in every answer, and reproduces the documented wrong answer on the corridor counterexample (so that its label is earned).
- [ ] The rule, the repair-cycle definition and the doorway population are registered before the gate battery's data exists (commit order).
- [ ] The false-answer counts and U (overall, per archetype, per stop rule) are cited from the gate record.
- [ ] The verdict is one of the enumerated verdicts, recorded as an N40-03 outcome in the KILL-FCLEAR row's `decision` entry; `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FCLEAR-M3.md` exists; `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (N40-30) passes for KILL-FCLEAR (owner declaration present, trigger and fallback equal to the ledger's, outcome consistent with the measured values).
- [ ] On `fired`, `scene.fit` answers carry the heuristic label and no certificate, and the feasibility tag is `unresolved` on a planted scene where heuristic and brute force disagree.
- [ ] The decision script catches the planted cases below.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-scene/tests/route_heuristic.rs` and `crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_fclear_rule.rs`:
- `heuristic_width_rule`: footprint width 0.60 m, bound 2 mm, door 0.66 m → passable; door 0.60 m → blocked.
- `heuristic_is_wrong_on_corridor`: 0.60 m × 1.30 m in a 0.90 m right-angle corridor: a circumscribed-diameter rule blocks a body that passes; an inscribed-width rule passes a 1.40 m body that jams.
- `tag_reduced_on_disagreement`.
- `fallback_input_field`: `scene.fit` with `fallback: heuristic` answers `passable` or `blocked` labelled `heuristic`,
  with no certificate and no standing cut recorded; omitting the field keeps the complex path; an unknown value is
  refused by the input schema.
- `rule_enumeration`: (false answers before repair: none or some) × (after repair: none or some) × (U ≤ 0.30 or > 0.30) maps to one verdict.
- `threshold_strict`: U = 0.30 does not fire ("more than 30%"); U = 0.31 fires.
- `any_false_answer_after_repair_fires`: one false cut after repair fires even if the original false witness is fixed.
- `outcome_names`: both `fired` verdicts map to N40-03's `fired` with the reason kept and `not fired` to `not-fired` with a non-empty `receipts`; the ADR path is `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FCLEAR-M3.md`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_fclear_decide.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/kill_fclear_decide/`): reads the GATE-HAB-10 record (`frob evidence trace <clm:…> --json` on the claim identifier of that record; `evidence.trace` takes a claim identifier in its `clm:<64 hex>` form, never a gate name), recomputes the verdict from recorded values and compares it with the ADR and with the row's `decision` entry; runs `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (asserting no finding for KILL-FCLEAR); runs `frob scene fit --scene <corridor fixture> --footprint rect:0.60x1.30 --from west --to north --fallback heuristic --key kf-heur-1 --json` (asserts the label, the documented wrong answer of the scalar rule and the absence of a certificate); on a `fired` record, runs `frob scene show <planted scene> --json` and asserts the reduced tag; then the three planted decision records below, each through `e2e_expect_caught`. Lines log the command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, `false_witnesses`, `false_cuts`, `repair_cycles`, `unresolved_share` overall and per archetype against 0.30, the verdict, and the first contradicting value on failure. Summary: `gates: ["KILL-FCLEAR"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- CX-193 (the scalar disc test is wrong both ways) is the reason the fallback is labelled heuristic; CX-194 gives the arithmetic behind the second trigger: at the default lattice about 22% of uniform door and base pairs are near fits, 1.4% at the first useful refinement and 0.14% at the next, so a share above 30% after refinement means refinement is not working.
- Planted cases the decision script must catch: a gate record with one false cut after a repair cycle (`fired: false answer`); a statistics record with U = 0.42 (`fired: unresolved share`); a clean record with U = 0.05 (`not fired`).
- Determinism: a pure function of recorded values.

## Considerations

- The second trigger must not punish honesty in scans: an `unresolved` at the registration floor of an ingested scene is the correct output and comes with a measurement request. The registered population is generated scenes; scan results are reported separately.
- A single false answer is a soundness defect; "survives a repair cycle" exists so that a fixed bug does not kill the mechanism, not so that a second bug is forgiven.
- If the gate fires, a certified family answer about places is no longer available to mission queries; that narrows what the mission layer can certify and must be stated in its reports.
- The fallback is what an incumbent planning library's nominal check would also give; no advantage is claimed for it.
