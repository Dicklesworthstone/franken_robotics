## Background

The epic "RGB and splat scenes" (BD-420) builds nothing until three records exist: a short plan with its kill gate
and fallback (N19b-23), a paper-scale pilot that has run (N19b-24), and the owner's decision to fund it. For the
Frontier sensor models the plan's funding rule is strict: a model is funded only when a registered task needs it,
it has no speed benefit to claim, and it falls back to the mask-only model (geometry-correct depth, timing, labels
and optical classes from the collision field) if it adds more than 25% to the ray stage it joins. Splat scenes (a
captured place as a set of oriented, coloured Gaussian primitives, rendered only for RGB observations) are Moonshot
until the plan says otherwise, so they also compete for one of at most four active Moonshot slots.

This bead takes that decision. It was split from N19b-23 in polishing round 2: the decision reads the pilot's
result, and the pilot reads the plan's frozen protocol, so one bead could not hold both the plan and the decision.
Priority 2, one step above the M6 default, because the RGB mechanism (N20a-28) and any work-package bead wait on it.

## Scope

In: reading the pilot's records and the plan; the recommendation section of the plan; the owner's decision; the ADR;
closing or releasing the dependents. Out: the plan and the kill gate (N19b-23); the census and fixtures (N19b-24);
any implementation (N20a-28 and the work-package beads written after a decision to fund).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs (each cited by digest).** The two pilot records in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (RGB,
tier F; splat, tier M) with their appended results: the agreed "needs pixels" list, both reviewers' columns and
their disagreements, the five fixture values, the paper cost and its bound on primitives per ray; the short plan
`docs/delivery/plans/rgb-splat-scenes.md`; the kill-gate row in `registries/gates.toml`; the Moonshot queue and the
count of active Moonshot items at M6 (NAB-21).

**2. The rule (fail-closed).**
1. The pilot's falsifier holds (the agreed list is empty, verdict `falsifier-holds`): **drop**. The epic closes with
   reason `fallback` (the mask-only model); N20a-28 closes `superseded` citing the ADR; no work-package bead is
   written. The owner cannot fund against a falsified pilot (the pilot register's funding rule refuses it).
2. The list is non-empty: the bead writes the recommendation (fund, defer or drop) with the list of tasks that need
   pixels, the decision each changes, the bound on primitives per ray and the open risks; the owner chooses.
   - **Fund RGB**: N20a-28 is released; the work-package beads named in the plan's section 11 are written. The
     splat part is funded only if a Moonshot slot is free under the cap; otherwise it is recorded as deferred.
   - **Defer**: the ADR names the exit at which the decision is taken again; N20a-28 stays blocked; nothing is
     described as available.
   - **Drop**: as in rule 1.
3. A pilot that has not run, or whose record is `void`, is not a pass: the decision is `defer`, never `fund`.

**3. Record.** The decision is also the decision of N19b-23's kill gate, whose mode `pilot-before-funding` takes it
when funding is requested, so it has the kill-gate form of N40-03: the ADR `docs/adr/ADR-<gate name>-M6.md` (the name
the register assigns; `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-RGBSPLAT-M6.md` under the working name; the program's one directory for
decision records) with the required fields of N40-03's record plus the inputs with digests, the rule's evaluation, the
recommendation, the owner's choice and date, the dependents released or closed, and the session that recorded it; and
the gate row's decision entry, entered through the recorder, `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }`:
**drop** is `not-reached` (the epic is not funded; the reason, `falsifier-holds` or the owner's choice, is kept in the
ADR); **defer** is `deferred`, adding `deferred_to` (the re-decision exit) and `reason`; **fund** is `not-fired`, the
trigger evaluated against the pilot's records and the cost bound they state, cited by their receipts. The plan's
"Recommendation" section is filled with the same text and a link to the ADR.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists with every input cited by digest and states which branch of the rule applied.
- [ ] The kill-gate row carries the decision entry in the form of N40-03 matching the branch: `not-reached` for drop, `deferred` with `deferred_to` and `reason` for defer, `not-fired` with at least one receipt for fund.
- [ ] A falsified pilot yields `drop`; a missing or void pilot yields `defer`; neither yields `fund`.
- [ ] Exactly one of: N20a-28 released, N20a-28 blocked with the re-decision exit named, or N20a-28 and the epic
      closed with the ADR as reason.
- [ ] No work-package bead for the epic exists unless the outcome is `fund`.
- [ ] The splat part is funded only with a free Moonshot slot, and the queue's count is cited.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/rgb_splat_decision.rs` (the rule as a pure function over the records):
- `falsified_pilot_drops`: empty agreed list → `drop`, whatever the owner's input.
- `missing_pilot_defers`: no result, or a `void` record → `defer`.
- `nonempty_list_needs_owner`: list ["fiducial-dock"] with owner input `fund` → `fund`; with no owner input →
  `pending`, never `fund`.
- `splat_needs_slot`: four active Moonshot items → splat part `deferred` even under `fund`.
- `outcome_names`: drop → `not-reached`, defer → `deferred` with a `deferred_to` later than M6 and a non-empty `reason`, fund → `not-fired` with a non-empty `receipts`; `pending` writes no decision entry.

### End-to-end script and logging
Step `decision` added to `scripts/e2e/rgb_splat_preparation.sh` (bead field `N19bp2-01`): reads the ADR and the pilot
records, recomputes the rule's branch, asserts it equals the recorded one and that the kill-gate row's decision entry carries the matching N40-03 outcome and the ADR path, asserts that the plan's recommendation
section links the ADR, and asserts that `frob capabilities --json` lists no RGB or splat capability unless the outcome
is `fund`. Plant `fund-against-falsified`: a synthetic ADR that funds with an empty list must fail the step. Every
line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time, the inputs'
digests and the derived and recorded branch; the `summary` counts assertions.

### Known answers and negative controls
The four unit cases are the known answers of the rule. Negative control: the planted ADR above.

## Considerations

- A decision by default is not allowed: an unanswered owner question leaves the decision `pending`, and the
  fail-closed product is the mask-only model.
- Funding RGB does not fund splat scenes; the two parts have different tiers and different slots.
- Captured appearance is private; the ADR cites tasks and digests, never images.
