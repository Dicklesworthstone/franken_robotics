## Background

Real-place ingestion is the Frontier capability that turns a capture of an actual home or workplace into a private
scene pack, so that a candidate robot can be evaluated in one particular place. It has no effect on decision time; it
is the precondition for any Reality claim about that place, and it serves the North-Star scenario "your own kitchen".

The rule of the project for Frontier work is that a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier runs before the work package is
funded. For ingestion the risk is not algorithmic; it is that the upstream capture pipeline does not export what the
importer must have. Two pipelines exist in the owner's projects (FACT). First, a route from walkthrough video to an
agent-built Blender twin with stable feature identifiers and a GLB export, with an accepted plan for a **twin
package** that links visual, geometric and traversability representations (the reconstruction skills and
`franken_surveillance_system/BLENDER_TWIN_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md`). Second, `franken_drone_geometry_reconstruction` (at
`db45703afc5e`), whose executable chain today runs from media custody through clocks, calibration, scale witnesses,
keyframes, correspondences and relative pose to an audited relative bundle problem; its own status file says it does
not yet optimize cameras, emit metres or publish geometry. `scene.import` therefore consumes the twin package first.

What the importer needs from a package, and why:

1. **Four non-coplanar landmarks** with known scene coordinates. Three landmarks and a known length accept a mirrored
   room with zero residual (A = (0,0,0), B = (1,0,0), C = (0,1,0) are all fixed by (x, y, z) ↦ (x, y, −z); a fourth
   point D = (0,0,1) shows a residual of 2 m). The tetrahedron of the four must have a volume of at least 1% of the
   cube of their largest separation.
2. **A scale witness**: an object of known size or a measured baseline in the capture, with its uncertainty, so that
   the scale label can be `witnessed` or better. Without one, metric tasks are refused or verdicts stay unresolved.
3. **Coverage or confidence per region**, so that unobserved space can be marked `unknown` instead of free or solid.

## Scope

In: the inspection of one existing twin package against a written checklist; the pilot record; the decision it
triggers (build the importer, or open the upstream work item first). Out: any importer code (N19c-04, BD-398); the
drone route's own admission (INT-FDGR-01, another unit); the kill-gate decision after the gate is attempted (N19c-08).

## Technical approach

1. **Choose the package.** One twin package produced by the walkthrough route, of a place for which the owner permits
   use. It stays in the private root; nothing from it enters the repository or a log except the checklist result.
2. **Checklist (each item is present, partial or absent, with the path inside the package where it was found).**
   - Geometry: a GLB or mesh with stable feature identifiers; units and axis convention stated; triangle counts.
   - Landmarks: at least four labelled points with coordinates in a stated frame; compute the tetrahedron volume
     V = |det[B − A, C − A, D − A]|/6 and the largest pairwise separation L; pass if V ≥ 0.01·L³.
   - Scale witness: the object or baseline, its true size, its measured size in the capture, and the stated
     uncertainty; which of the four scale labels (`relative-only`, `estimated`, `witnessed`, `surveyed`) it supports.
   - Coverage or confidence: a per-region or per-surface field; whether unseen undersides and occluded volumes are
     distinguishable from observed free space.
   - Articulation: hinge axes and limits for doors and drawers, if any (optional for the pilot; recorded).
   - Privacy metadata: whether the package marks itself private (recorded; the pack is private regardless).
3. **Falsifier.** If any of the three required items (four non-coplanar landmarks, a scale witness, coverage or
   confidence per region) is absent, the first deliverable of the work package is the upstream work item FD-FSS-01
   (franken_surveillance_system: add them to the twin package), not an importer. The interim posture while that item
   is open is a tape-measure survey authored in the interior grammar with every dimension recorded as `measured`
   (N19c-06). If all three are present, the importer work proceeds on the package as the import contract (OPEN-HAB-5,
   N19c-09).
4. **Record.** A pilot record in the conformance registry (`frob.pilot/1`, DESIGN name): the package's digest (not its
   content), the checklist with verdicts, the tetrahedron ratio V/L³, the scale label supportable, the falsifier
   verdict, the date and the reviewer. Class: an inspection, not a measurement of the product.
5. **Cost.** Half an engineer-day (DESIGN estimate): no code beyond a script that reads the package's metadata.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] One twin package is inspected and the checklist is on record with a verdict per item and the paths inspected.
- [ ] The tetrahedron ratio is computed for the package's landmarks, or "fewer than four landmarks" is recorded.
- [ ] The falsifier verdict is recorded as `importer-first` or `upstream-first`; on `upstream-first`, FD-FSS-01 is opened in this graph and mirrored in the sibling's tracker, and the surveyed-proxy bead (N19c-06) is the interim path.
- [ ] No private content appears in the record, the repository or any log (digests and verdicts only).
- [ ] The record is cited by the mechanism beads (N19c-02 to N19c-05) as their pilot.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`tools/pilots/ingest_inspect/tests.rs` (a small host tool; no product code):
- `tetra_ratio_unit`: A = (0,0,0), B = (1,0,0), C = (0,1,0), D = (0,0,1): V = 1/6, L = √2, V/L³ = 0.0589 ≥ 0.01, pass.
- `tetra_ratio_nearly_coplanar`: D = (0, 0, 0.01): V = 0.001667, L = √2, ratio 0.00059 < 0.01, fail.
- `three_landmarks_is_absent`: a package fixture with three landmarks yields `absent` for the landmark item.
- `scale_label_from_witness`: a witness of true size 0.800 m measured 0.796 m with stated ±0.004 m supports `witnessed`; no witness supports at most `estimated`.
- `checklist_requires_paths`: a verdict of `present` without a path is rejected.
All fixtures are `synthetic` package skeletons, labelled so.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/pilot_ingest_inspect.sh` (STD-E2E helper): runs the inspection tool on the synthetic skeletons (one
complete, one missing landmarks, one missing coverage) and asserts the three falsifier verdicts; the private package
is inspected by hand with the same tool and only its record is stored. NDJSON lines: command, exit code, wall time,
the checklist verdicts, `tetra_ratio` with its threshold 0.01, the falsifier verdict; logs under
`target/e2e-logs/pilot_ingest_inspect/`; no package content is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-429 and CX-184 (the mirror through the plane of three landmarks; residual 2 m at the fourth) are the reason for item 1; CX-185 (a gravity witness does not replace the fourth point when the three lie in a vertical plane).
- Negative control: the skeleton with three landmarks must be reported `upstream-first`; a tool that accepted three would fail.

## Considerations

- **KILL-INGEST** is the kill gate this pilot serves. Trigger: the real-place gate cannot be passed with a scale label of `witnessed` or better after one repair cycle. Fallback: the place is authored in the interior grammar from a tape-measure survey, with every surveyed dimension recorded as `measured`, and the pack says it is a proxy.
- FD-FSS-01 is conditional: it is opened only if this pilot finds the items missing. FD-FDGR-01 (publish geometry from the drone route: optimized cameras, metres with a scale witness, coverage and uncertainty per region) is independent of this pilot and gates the second source.
- The pilot inspects one package; a second package with a different capture style may differ. The record says so.
- Privacy: governed by the private-places rule; the inspector works inside the private root.
