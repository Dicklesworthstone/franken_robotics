## Background

Real-place ingestion is the Frontier capability that turns a capture of an actual home or workplace into a private
scene pack, so that a candidate robot can be evaluated in one particular place. It has no effect on decision time; it
is the precondition for any Reality claim about that place, and it serves the North-Star scenario "your own kitchen".

The rule of the project for Frontier work is that a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier runs before the work package is
funded. For ingestion the risk is not algorithmic; it is that the upstream capture pipeline does not export what the
importer must have. Two pipelines exist in the owner's projects (FACT). First, a route from walkthrough video to an
agent-built Blender twin with stable feature identifiers and a GLB export, with an accepted plan for a **twin
package** that links visual, geometric and traversability representations (the reconstruction skills and
`franken_surveillance_system/BLENDER_TWIN_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md`). Second, `franken_drone_geometry_reconstruction` (at
`db45703afc5e`), whose executable chain today runs from media custody through clocks, calibration, scale witnesses,
keyframes, correspondences and relative pose to an audited relative bundle problem; its own status file says it does
not yet optimize cameras, emit metres or publish geometry. `scene.import` therefore consumes the twin package first.

What the importer needs from a package, and why:

1. **Four non-coplanar landmarks** with known scene coordinates. Three landmarks and a known length accept a mirrored
   room with zero residual (A = (0,0,0), B = (1,0,0), C = (0,1,0) are all fixed by (x, y, z) ↦ (x, y, −z); a fourth
   point D = (0,0,1) shows a residual of 2 m). The tetrahedron of the four must have a volume of at least 1% of the
   cube of their largest separation.
2. **A scale witness**: an object of known size or a measured baseline in the capture, with its uncertainty, so that
   the scale label can be `witnessed` or better. Without one, metric tasks are refused or verdicts stay unresolved.
3. **Coverage or confidence per region**, so that unobserved space can be marked `unknown` instead of free or solid.

This bead is the one inspection of a twin package in the program. The drone lane's pilot (N33c-25, M3) was drafted
with the same inspection as its Part B; it now keeps only the schema half of INT-FDGR-01, and its fourth checklist
item (the stated handedness and up axis of the export) is folded in here. DESIGN, milestone: the inspection runs at
M3, as that draft had it, so that the answer (and FD-FSS-01, if needed) is known two milestones before the importer's
work package is funded at M5; it costs half a day and needs nothing built at M3.

## Scope

In: the inspection of one existing twin package against a written checklist; the pilot record; the decision it
triggers (build the importer, or open the upstream work item first); a re-inspection appended as a new result when a
package from a landed FD-FSS-01 exists. Out: any importer code (N19c-04, BD-398); the drone route's schema fixtures and
its own pilot record (N33c-25, the M3 half of INT-FDGR-01); the kill-gate decision after the gate is attempted (N19c-08).

## Technical approach

1. **Choose the package.** One twin package produced by the walkthrough route, of a place for which the owner permits
   use. It stays in the private root; nothing from it enters the repository or a log except the checklist result.
2. **Checklist, frozen and committed before the package is opened (each item is `present`, `partial`, `ambiguous`
   (the files alone do not settle it) or `absent`, with the path and field inside the package where it was found;
   answered from the package's files alone).**
   - Geometry: a GLB or mesh with stable feature identifiers; units and axis convention stated; triangle counts.
   - Landmarks: at least four labelled points with coordinates in a stated frame; compute the tetrahedron volume
     V = |det[B − A, C − A, D − A]|/6 and the largest pairwise separation L; pass if V ≥ 0.01·L³.
   - Scale witness: the object or baseline, its true size, its measured size in the capture, and the stated
     uncertainty; which of the four scale labels (`relative-only`, `estimated`, `witnessed`, `surveyed`) it supports.
   - Coverage or confidence: a per-region or per-surface field; whether unseen undersides and occluded volumes are
     distinguishable from observed free space.
   - Handedness and up axis of the export, stated (a glTF camera looks along local −Z with +Y up; a parity change
     must be excludable from what the package states, or the import needs the fourth landmark to exclude it).
   - Articulation: hinge axes and limits for doors and drawers, if any (optional for the pilot; recorded).
   - Privacy metadata: whether the package marks itself private (recorded; the pack is private regardless).
3. **Falsifier.** If any of the three required items (four non-coplanar landmarks, a scale witness, coverage or
   confidence per region) is absent, the first deliverable of the work package is the upstream work item FD-FSS-01
   (franken_surveillance_system: add them to the twin package), not an importer. The interim posture while that item
   is open is a tape-measure survey authored in the interior grammar with every dimension recorded as `measured`
   (N19c-06). If all three are present, the importer work proceeds on the package as the import contract (OPEN-HAB-5,
   N19c-09).
4. **Record.** A `[[pilot]]` record of the pilot register `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (schema
   `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`, N38a-09; the earlier name `frob.pilot/1` is retired), written before the inspection with:
   `mechanism` (real-place ingestion, twin-package lane), `tier = "F"`, `owner` (§19.13), `bead`, `work_package`
   (WP-32), `gated_beads = ["N19c-02", "N19c-03", "N19c-04", "N19c-05", "N19c-07", "BD-398"]` (the mechanism beads
   of this lane: scale labels, the unknown mask, the twin adapter, probe requests, the GATE-HAB-03 run and the
   `place` form of `scene.import`; WP-32 holds three upper-tier mechanisms, so the per-mechanism funding rule of
   N38a-09 needs the list, and each of these beads reads its readiness with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
   pilots -- fundable --bead <key> --json`; the surveyed proxy N19c-06, the decisions N19c-08 to N19c-10 and the
   dressing lane are not listed), `protocol` (the frozen checklist's path, BLAKE3 and date), `falsifier` (step 3), `ceiling` (no effect on
   decision time; the precondition of a Reality claim about one place), `fallback` (`ships` the surveyed proxy,
   `kill_gate` KILL-INGEST), `cost`, `tool` (the inspection tool below and its pin, site `host`). The appended
   result holds: the package's digest and version (not its content), the checklist with verdicts and paths, the
   tetrahedron ratio V/L³, the scale label supportable, the recommendation `importer-first` or `upstream-first`, the
   date and the reviewer. Its `verdict`, in the register's vocabulary: `falsifier-rejected` when the three required
   items are present (the importer is funded on this lane); `falsifier-holds` when any is absent (the importer is
   not funded on this lane until a package from a landed FD-FSS-01 is inspected again and that appended result
   rejects the falsifier). Class: an inspection, not a measurement of the product.
5. **Cost.** Half an engineer-day (DESIGN estimate): no code beyond a small host tool that reads the package's
   metadata, `tools/pilots/ingest_inspect/` (run with `cargo run --manifest-path
   tools/pilots/ingest_inspect/Cargo.toml -- <package> --checklist <frozen checklist> --json`; a repository tool,
   not a verb), which prints the checklist verdicts and the tetrahedron ratio as one JSON object.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The checklist is committed before the package is opened (its commit is an ancestor of the result's), and the pilot record loads under `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1` with every required field.
- [ ] One twin package is inspected and the checklist is on record with a verdict per item (including handedness and up axis) and the paths inspected.
- [ ] The tetrahedron ratio is computed for the package's landmarks, or "fewer than four landmarks" is recorded.
- [ ] The recommendation is recorded as `importer-first` (verdict `falsifier-rejected`) or `upstream-first` (verdict `falsifier-holds`); on `upstream-first`, FD-FSS-01 is opened in this graph and mirrored in the sibling's tracker, and the surveyed-proxy bead (N19c-06) is the interim path.
- [ ] No private content appears in the record, the repository or any log (digests and verdicts only).
- [ ] The record is cited by the mechanism beads (N19c-02 to N19c-05) as their pilot, and its `gated_beads` lists the six beads of step 4: `fundable --bead N19c-04 --json` answers `Blocked` with reason `registered` while the record has no result, and after the result the answer its verdict implies (`Fundable`, or `Blocked{falsifier-holds}` printing the surveyed-proxy fallback and KILL-INGEST), independently of the drone lane (N33c-25) and the dressing lane (N19a-40).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`tools/pilots/ingest_inspect/tests.rs` (a small host tool; no product code):
- `tetra_ratio_unit`: A = (0,0,0), B = (1,0,0), C = (0,1,0), D = (0,0,1): V = 1/6, L = √2, V/L³ = 0.0589 ≥ 0.01, pass.
- `tetra_ratio_nearly_coplanar`: D = (0, 0, 0.01): V = 0.001667, L = √2, ratio 0.00059 < 0.01, fail.
- `three_landmarks_is_absent`: a package fixture with three landmarks yields `absent` for the landmark item.
- `scale_label_from_witness`: a witness of true size 0.800 m measured 0.796 m with stated ±0.004 m supports `witnessed`; no witness supports at most `estimated`.
- `checklist_requires_paths`: a verdict of `present` without a path is rejected.
- `handedness_unstated_is_ambiguous`: a skeleton whose export states no axis convention yields `ambiguous` for that item, never `present`.
- `verdict_follows_items`: three required items present → `falsifier-rejected`; any of them `absent` → `falsifier-holds`; `partial` or `ambiguous` on a required item → `falsifier-holds` (fail-closed).
All fixtures are `synthetic` package skeletons, labelled so.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/pilot_ingest_inspect.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; header `plants=three-landmarks`): runs the
inspection tool on the synthetic skeletons (one complete, one missing landmarks, one missing coverage, one with an
unstated axis convention) and asserts the falsifier verdicts; `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
pilot_register` over the real register file with the new record; `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example pilots --
complete --json` (the record names its mechanism) and `-- fundable --bead N19c-04 --json` and `-- fundable --package
WP-32 --json` (the per-mechanism lines, the answer logged with its reason); `git merge-base --is-ancestor` for
checklist before result. The private package is inspected by hand with the same tool and only its record is stored. Every
NDJSON line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; `assert`
lines carry the checklist verdicts, `metric` lines `tetra_ratio` with its threshold 0.01, and the verdict and
recommendation; on failure the skeleton and the first item whose verdict differs. A final `summary` line counts
assertions. Logs under `target/e2e-logs/pilot_ingest_inspect/`; no package content is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-429 and CX-184 (the mirror through the plane of three landmarks; residual 2 m at the fourth) are the reason for item 1; CX-185 (a gravity witness does not replace the fourth point when the three lie in a vertical plane).
- Negative control: the skeleton with three landmarks must be reported `upstream-first`; a tool that accepted three would fail.

## Considerations

- **KILL-INGEST** is the kill gate this pilot serves. Trigger: the real-place gate cannot be passed with a scale label of `witnessed` or better after one repair cycle. Fallback: the place is authored in the interior grammar from a tape-measure survey, with every surveyed dimension recorded as `measured`, and the pack says it is a proxy.
- FD-FSS-01 is conditional: it is opened only if this pilot finds the items missing. FD-FDGR-01 (publish geometry from the drone route: optimized cameras, metres with a scale witness, coverage and uncertainty per region) is independent of this pilot and gates the second source.
- The pilot inspects one package; a second package with a different capture style may differ. The record says so.
- Funding granularity (settled by N38a-09's per-mechanism rule): WP-32 holds three independent Frontier mechanisms
  (this lane, the drone scan lane of N33c-25, and asset-populated interiors of N19a-40), so the package can be
  `Partial`. A `falsifier-holds` here blocks the six beads in `gated_beads` (`fundable_bead`), not the surveyed proxy
  (N19c-06, the interim posture this verdict calls for, a fallback that no record gates) or the dressing work. The
  shared ingestion beads are gated by this lane's record because the plan makes the twin package the first import
  contract; `fundable_bead` answers a bead with the mechanism of every record that lists it, so the drone lane's
  record should list its own adapter work and not these six (open; fail-closed default: a bead listed by both
  records stays blocked until both verdicts reject their falsifiers).
- Privacy: governed by the private-places rule; the inspector works inside the private root.
