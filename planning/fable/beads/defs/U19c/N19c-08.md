## Background

Every Frontier capability in this project names, before it is built, the measurement that would stop it and what the
project does instead. For real-place ingestion (a capture of an actual home or workplace becomes a private scene pack)
that rule is the kill gate **KILL-INGEST**.

- **Trigger.** GATE-HAB-03 cannot be passed with a scale label of `witnessed` or better after one repair cycle.
  (GATE-HAB-03: one real place ingested end to end, privately, with its scale label and unknown regions respected, and
  at least one probe request emitted. The scale label is one of `relative-only`, `estimated`, `witnessed`, `surveyed`;
  `witnessed` means scale fixed by an object of known size or a measured baseline in the capture.)
- **Fallback, named now.** The place is authored in the interior grammar from a tape-measure survey, with every
  surveyed dimension recorded as `measured`, and the pack says it is a proxy.
- **Decided at.** M5, with GATE-HAB-03.

Why the trigger is about scale: a reconstruction without an authoritative scale cannot carry metric tasks, and a
clearance verdict on it is `unresolved` inside the scale interval; an importer that only ever yields `estimated` packs
delivers viewing, not evaluation. Why one repair cycle: the likely repairs are known (add a witness object to the
capture; add the fourth landmark; fix the package export through the upstream item FD-FSS-01), and a second cycle
without a pass means the upstream pipeline is not ready, which is not this project's work to finish.

## Scope

In: reading the gate's record, running at most one repair cycle, recording the decision as an architecture decision
record, and applying its consequences to the graph and the documents. This bead is the only place where KILL-INGEST
is decided and the only writer of its record. Out: the mechanisms and the gate script (N19c-02 to N19c-07); the
capstone of real-place ingestion BD-398, whose done-condition names this kill gate: it assembles the lane the gate
measures and makes the import envelope and loss report carry the values read here (scale label achieved, landmark
residual, unknown fraction, probe-request count), and it decides nothing; the surveyed-proxy mechanism (N19c-06),
which exists on both branches.

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** The pilot record (N19c-01: did the package export landmarks, a witness and coverage?); the gate row of
   GATE-HAB-03 from the private lane (N19c-07) with its measured scale label, landmark residual, unknown fraction and
   probe-request count; the state of the upstream items FD-FSS-01 and FD-FDGR-01.
2. **Decision rule (mechanical).**
   - If the gate row is `PASS` with label `witnessed` or `surveyed`: **not fired**. Ingestion stays in the product
     as Frontier.
   - If the gate failed or passed only at `estimated` or `relative-only`: run **one repair cycle**: record the
     cause (missing witness, missing landmark, registration residual above tolerance, coverage absent, a defect in
     the importer), apply the matching repair (recapture with a witness; add a landmark; fix the importer; open or
     advance FD-FSS-01), and re-run the gate script once.
   - If after that cycle the row is still not `PASS` at `witnessed` or better: **fired**.
   - If no capture exists at M5: the gate row is `no-claim`; the kill gate is recorded **deferred**, by this ADR
     naming the later exit at which it is decided again (the first milestone exit after a capture exists, M6 by
     default), and the fallback posture holds meanwhile. A `no-claim` is never read as a pass.
   - If the importer was never funded (the pilots N19c-01 and N33c-25 both recorded `falsifier-holds` and no
     package from FD-FSS-01 rejected the falsifier by M5): **not reached**; the fallback posture holds. In this
     case the gated beads this bead depends on (BD-398 and N19c-07, listed in the twin-lane record's `gated_beads`)
     never become ready; they are closed as not funded, with the `fundable --bead` answer (`Blocked`, reason
     `falsifier-holds`) as the recorded reason, and that closure releases this bead, so the decision is recorded
     whichever way the pilots and the gate fall (KNOWN_DRIFTS 10).
   These are the four outcomes of the kill-gate register (N40-03): `not-fired`, `fired`, `not-reached`, `deferred`
   (`not-decidable` is not an outcome name).
3. **Record.** An ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-INGEST-M5.md` (every decision record of the program lives in `docs/adr/`)
   with the required fields of the kill-gate register (N40-03): the kill gate, the milestone, the trigger as
   registered and any amendments, the measured values that decided it (scale label, landmark residual, unknown
   fraction, probe-request count) each with its receipt hash beside the trigger's threshold, the outcome, the
   fallback in force and the name the capability carries from now on, the beads re-read (each "stands on the
   fallback" or "superseded by this ADR"), what changes downstream, the repair cycle taken, the commit and date, and
   the recording session (a fresh-context session that did not implement the importer). The kill row's decision
   entry in `registries/gates.toml` follows N40-03: `decision = [{ exit = "M5", outcome, adr, date, session,
   receipts }]`, with `receipts` the hashes of the GATE-HAB-03 receipts the outcome rests on (at least one for
   `not-fired`), no `scope` (KILL-INGEST has no `scope_kind`, and a `scope` on such a row fails the load as
   `decision-scope-invalid`), and a `deferred` entry adding `deferred_to` (the later exit) and `reason`; the
   re-decision after a deferral appends a second entry and never edits the first. **Helper.** `tools/gates/src/kill_ingest.rs`: pure functions on the
   parsed GATE-HAB-03 rows and pilot results (the repair-cycle state, the outcome), with a small binary
   `tools/gates/src/bin/decide_kill_ingest.rs` that reads `registries/gates.toml` and
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` and prints the rows read, the cycle state and the outcome as one
   JSON object. It is a repository tool run with `cargo run`, not a verb: the gate register is a file, and there is
   no `frob gates` command (the pattern of KILL-POOL's helper, N19c-18).
4. **Consequences if fired.**
   - Real places enter only as surveyed proxies (N19c-06); `scene.import --kind place` remains for viewing-grade
     packs and returns its packs labelled at most `estimated`, with every metric task refused or unresolved.
   - GATE-HAB-03 is recorded `no-claim`; reports on such places print "surveyed proxy" and `reality: no-claim`.
   - The capability is described by the name of its fallback ("surveyed proxies"), not as real-place ingestion.
   - Beads that exist only on the ingestion branch (drone-route subprocess integration, capture-specific acceptance
     work) are closed as not planned; the scale-label, unknown-mask and probe-request beads remain, since proxies and
     any later capture use them.
   - The upstream items stay open in their own trackers; re-opening this decision requires a capture that passes.
5. **Consequences if not fired.** The package and version that passed become the import contract (closing
   OPEN-HAB-5), and the drone route is added when FD-FDGR-01 lands and INT-FDGR-01 passes on it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision is recorded in `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-INGEST-M5.md` with N40-03's required fields and one of the register's four outcomes, the measured scale label and the gate rows it read (by receipt hash).
- [ ] If a repair cycle ran, its cause, its repair and the second gate run are recorded; there is no third run before the decision.
- [ ] On `fired`: the graph shows the ingestion-only beads closed as not planned, the README status table names the fallback, and GATE-HAB-03 reads `no-claim`; `scene.import --kind place` packs are labelled at most `estimated`.
- [ ] On `not-fired`: OPEN-HAB-5 is closed with the package and version that passed.
- [ ] On `deferred` or `not-reached`: the fallback posture is stated as in force; a deferral names its later exit, and a follow-up bead exists to re-decide when a capture exists.
- [ ] The review is by a session that did not implement the importer.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`tools/gates/tests/kill_ingest.rs` (the small decision helper that reads gate rows):
- `pass_witnessed_not_fired`, `pass_surveyed_not_fired`.
- `pass_estimated_requires_repair_cycle`: a first row at `estimated` returns "repair cycle required", not a verdict.
- `second_failure_fires`: two rows, neither at `witnessed` or better → `fired`.
- `no_claim_is_deferred`: a `no-claim` row yields `deferred` with a later exit named, never `not-fired`.
- `pilots_held_not_reached`: both ingestion pilots at `falsifier-holds` and no gate row → `not-reached`.
- `outcome_vocabulary_closed`: the helper emits only `not-fired`, `fired`, `not-reached`, `deferred`.
- `third_run_rejected`: a third gate row before a decision is an error.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/decide_kill_ingest.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; header `gates=KILL-INGEST
plants=edited-scale-label`): reads the gate rows and the pilot results with the helper (`cargo run --manifest-path
tools/gates/Cargo.toml --bin decide_kill_ingest -- --gates registries/gates.toml --pilots
crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml --gate GATE-HAB-03`; there is no `frob gates` command), prints the
outcome and the consequences checklist, and verifies that the ADR, the kill row's decision entry and the build agree
(on `fired`, a `scene import --kind place` of the synthetic twin package with `--key dki-imp-1` returns a pack
labelled at most `estimated`). Every NDJSON line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit
code, wall time, the rows read (receipt hashes, digests and labels only), the repair-cycle state, the outcome, the
ADR path and digest; on a disagreement the two values that differ and where each was read; a final `summary` line
naming KILL-INGEST. Logs under `target/e2e-logs/decide_kill_ingest/`. No private data.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the helper cases above on authored gate rows and pilot records.
- Negative control: a gate row whose scale label was edited from `estimated` to `witnessed` without a matching private log digest must be rejected (the row's digest chain is checked).

## Considerations

- The decision can be made early: if the pilot (N19c-01) finds the package cannot export a scale witness and the upstream item will not land by M5, the fallback posture is simply in force and this bead records `not-reached`, `deferred` or `fired` at M5.
- The risk on the "not fired" side is a single lucky capture; the record states that one place passed, not that the route is robust.
- Privacy: the ADR cites digests and labels, never the place.
- Kill gates are binding: a capability that fell back is described by its fallback everywhere, including the honest-status table.
