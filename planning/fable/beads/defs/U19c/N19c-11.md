## Background

A train and test split of scenes is only as good as its notion of "the same place". Scene identifiers do not capture
it: a layout and its mirror image have different `SceneId`s and the same aisles; a policy trained on one and scored on
the other has seen the test (counterexample CX-196). Splits are therefore made over **groups**. A group contains a
base scene and everything derived from it: mirrored layouts, clutter variants, retrofit variants, variants that share
its brick pool, and every scan of the same building. A group never straddles a split (INV-HAB-09).

The split itself (rules, parts, the straddle refusal `FROB-REF-311`, the verb) is BD-235. This bead answers the prior
question: given a scene pack, which group is it in, and why. Every pack already carries a `group` field in its
provenance block (for example `bldg-00417`); what is missing is the rule that fills it and the check that the rule
was not bypassed. Tier Solid, M3.

Design choice and alternatives. Groups are derived from **provenance** (how the scene was made), because that is
exact and cheap, and are then **cross-checked geometrically**, because provenance can be wrong or absent (a mirrored
copy compiled as if it were new). Rejected: geometry alone (two different homes generated by one grammar can look
alike, and a threshold would merge them wrongly or miss them); provenance alone (the planted mirrored layout of the
gate could be relabelled and pass).

## Scope

In: the group key for generated scenes; inheritance along typed edits; pool families; building identity for scans and
proxies; the mirror-invariant layout fingerprint and the tolerant deep check; the group index with a reason for every
merge; the role declaration that generators implement (`structural`, `mirror`, `clutter` or `condition` per parameter
and per seed stream) and the role tables of the shipped generators (the terrain families and the seven interior
archetypes), entered here as data against those generators' parameter lists, since no generator bead states them.
Out: the split and its verb (BD-235); typed scene edits and the lineage record they write (N19b-44) and the catalog
built on them (N19b-38); the generators themselves (BD-165, BD-225, BD-226, N19a-28).

## Technical approach

**1. Group key of a generated base scene (DESIGN).**
`GroupId = "g:" ‖ BLAKE3("frob.group/1" ‖ family ‖ archetype variant ‖ canonical structural parameters ‖ structural
seed)`. A generator declares, per parameter and per seed stream, one of four roles: `structural` (walls, openings,
stairs, fixed fixtures, hazard dimensions drawn into geometry), `mirror` (a handedness or flip flag), `clutter`
(movable placement, toys, pallets) and `condition` (wet or dry, lighting, card draws). Only `structural` enters the
key. Hence a layout and its mirror, and all clutter and condition variants of one layout, share a group by construction.

**2. Derived scenes inherit.** A pack produced from another pack by a typed scene edit (a retrofit kit, a moved wall,
a changed fixture, re-settled clutter) records `provenance.derived_from = <parent SceneId>` and takes the parent's
group. Inheritance is transitive; the root of the chain is the group's **base scene**.

**3. Pool families.** A variant compiled into the brick pool of a base (it names the base so that untouched bricks
are shared) is in the base's group. This is the plan's "variants that share its brick pool": sharing by declared
lineage. Unrelated scenes can also share bricks in the content store (identical rack bays in two warehouses); that
alone does not merge groups, but the overlap is reported (item 6).

**4. Buildings.** A scene ingested from a capture, and a surveyed proxy, take `GroupId = "bldg:" ‖ building identity`,
where the identity is declared at import and is required for real places. Every scan, rescan, proxy and edited variant
of one building therefore shares a group, which is what building-level holdouts hold out.

**5. The layout fingerprint (DESIGN hardening).** For each pack compute, once, at freeze:
- `fp_exact`: for each of the eight symmetries g of the square in the horizontal plane (four rotations about z, each
  with and without a mirror; z is never flipped), transform every band brick by g: its brick coordinates are mapped by
  g, and its content hash is recomputed on its samples permuted by g (exact, because an on-lattice symmetry permutes
  lattice samples); translate the transformed coordinates so that the smallest occupied brick corner is the origin;
  hash the sorted list of (coordinates, transformed content hash) pairs. `fp_exact` is the minimum of the eight
  hashes. A layout and any on-lattice mirror or quarter-turn of it have equal `fp_exact`; two layouts built from the
  same bricks in different places do not, because each brick's position is in the hash (a multiset of per-brick
  hashes with the extents only would ignore where each brick is, so moving an interior wall within the same
  extents, or permuting rooms, would give equal fingerprints and merge distinct layouts). Generators place modules on the coarse lattice, so generated mirrors are on-lattice.
- `fp_coarse`: the coarse occupancy grid (sign of the coarse sample, spacing S), stored compactly for the deep check.
Two packs with equal `fp_exact` are the same layout: their groups are merged with reason `fingerprint`. The **deep
check** (`frob scene split --action check --split <S> --deep`) compares packs of one family with equal sorted extents pairwise on `fp_coarse`
under the eight symmetries and flags a pair whose best Jaccard overlap of occupied cells is at least 0.98 (TARGET,
DESIGN) as `possible-ungrouped-variant`. HYPOTHESIS: distinct structural seeds of one archetype fall well below that
threshold; the test is the distribution measured on the archetype battery (recorded with the hygiene gate).

**6. The group index.** A union-find over group identifiers; every union stores its reason (`lineage`, `mirror-role`,
`pool-family`, `building`, `fingerprint`, `declared`) and the two packs that caused it, so an audit can explain any
membership. `group_of(pack)` returns the class representative (the lexicographically smallest member identifier, for
stability). Brick overlap between packs of different groups is reported as a number; at or above 50% of either pack's
band bricks (DESIGN) the check emits the same `possible-ungrouped-variant` finding. Findings exit 2 and never merge
silently: a person or agent declares the merge or records why the scenes are distinct.

**7. Where it runs.** At freeze (the manifest's `group` is written and is part of `SceneId`); at `scene.split add`
and `check` (the index is rebuilt and compared with the stored groups); at import of a real place.

**8. Layout.** `crates/frob-scene/src/split/{group.rs, fingerprint.rs, index.rs}`. Fingerprints are integer data (D0).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A generated layout, its mirror, three clutter variants and two condition variants have one `GroupId`; two different structural seeds have different ones.
- [ ] Every parameter and seed stream of every shipped generator family has a row in its role table; a parameter with no row fails the table's completeness test instead of defaulting silently to `structural`.
- [ ] A retrofit variant and a moved-wall variant carry `derived_from` and the base's group; a chain of three edits resolves to the root's group.
- [ ] Two imports of one building, and its surveyed proxy, share a `bldg:` group; imports of two buildings do not.
- [ ] A mirrored copy of a layout compiled from source with a fresh, unrelated `group` label is merged with the original by `fp_exact`, with reason `fingerprint` recorded.
- [ ] The deep check flags an off-lattice mirror (shifted by 0.05 m) as `possible-ungrouped-variant`; it does not flag any pair among 200 distinct seeds of each available archetype (the measured Jaccard distribution is recorded).
- [ ] Every union in the index has a reason and two witnesses; `group_of` is stable under insertion order.
- [ ] The `group` field is part of `SceneId`, and a pack without one is refused at freeze.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-scene/tests/groups.rs`; packs compiled from `synthetic` fixtures and from the terrain and interior generators as they exist.
- `structural_roles_only_in_key`: changing the clutter seed, the wetness draw or the mirror flag leaves `GroupId` unchanged; changing a door width changes it.
- `mirror_shares_group`, `quarter_turn_shares_fingerprint`.
- `lineage_is_transitive`: base → retrofit → moved wall: all three in the base's group.
- `independent_mirror_caught_by_fp_exact`: source mirrored in x and compiled with `group = "other"`: index merges with reason `fingerprint`.
- `fp_exact_symmetry_property`: for random on-lattice box layouts, all eight transforms have equal `fp_exact`; a layout with one interior wall moved by one coarse cell within the same extents does not, and neither does a layout whose two rooms of equal size are swapped with one door left in place (the same bricks in different positions).
- `deep_check_off_lattice_mirror`: Jaccard ≥ 0.98 for the shifted mirror; < 0.90 for 50 random distinct layouts (the numbers are recorded, the second is a measurement, not a pinned truth).
- `brick_overlap_warning_not_merge`: two warehouses sharing 60% of bricks through identical bays: finding emitted, groups unchanged.
- `union_reasons_recorded`, `representative_stable`.
- `building_group_for_scans_and_proxy`.

### End-to-end script and logging
Covered by the hygiene gate script (N19c-15), which plants a mirrored layout and a retrofit variant. This bead adds
`scripts/e2e/scene_groups.sh` (STD-E2E helper): `frob --json scene gen --family warehouse --seed <s> --key
gr-gen-base` (a base; `warehouse` is an interior family of `scene.gen`), the same seed with the generator's
`mirror`-role parameter, named in its role table (item 1), flipped (`--param <mirror parameter>=<flipped> --key
gr-gen-mirror`) and a different seed (`--key gr-gen-other`); `frob --json scene
compile fixtures/hab/groups/mirrored_base.src --key gr-cmp-mirror` (an independently mirrored source with an
unrelated `group` label); `frob --json scene show <id>` on all four, asserting the groups and fingerprints this bead
writes at freeze (base and mirror share a group by key; the other seed does not; the mirrored
source's `fp_exact` equals the base's). Then the split steps: `frob --json scene split --name groups-e2e
--group-by building --rule 'hash-bucket:0..10000=train' --key gr-sp-create` (the default action `create`; every
group to `train`), `frob --json scene split --action add --split groups-e2e <four scene ids> --expected-parent <the
split_id create returned> --key gr-sp-add` (a `mutate` action) and `frob --json scene split --action check --split
groups-e2e --deep` (read-only, no key), asserting the `fingerprint` merge of the mirrored source and its reason.
`scene.split` is BD-235's verb, and BD-235 depends on this bead: until BD-235 is closed the three split steps are
logged with `e2e_skip "needs BD-235"` (never counted as a pass); BD-235's acceptance requires them to run and pass,
and BD-235 exposes this bead's deep check as the `--deep` option of `--action check`. NDJSON lines: command, exit code,
wall time, evidence block, group identifiers, fingerprints (digests), Jaccard values with the 0.98 threshold; logs
under `target/e2e-logs/scene_groups/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-456 and CX-196: the mirror pair must land in one group. MR-31: a mirror image, a clutter variant, a retrofit variant and another scan of the same building belong to the split group of the base scene.
- Negative controls: the registered plant of CX-196, `N19c-11:cx-196` (N19-CX: a Cargo feature under
  `gauntlet-mutations` that makes `group_of` return the pack's own `SceneId`, so a split keys on identifiers), must
  fail `mirror_shares_group` and `independent_mirror_caught_by_fp_exact` here and BD-235's
  `mirror_pair_straddle_refused`; a key that includes the mirror flag must fail `mirror_shares_group`; a fingerprint without symmetry canonicalization must fail `independent_mirror_caught_by_fp_exact`; a fingerprint that drops brick positions (a multiset of brick hashes) must fail `fp_exact_symmetry_property`; an index that merges on brick overlap alone must fail `brick_overlap_warning_not_merge`.
- Determinism lanes: identifiers and fingerprints identical twice, in debug and release, on Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- The fingerprint, its eight symmetries and the two thresholds (0.98, 50%) are DESIGN additions to the plan's rule; they are labelled as such in the contract and the thresholds are to be frozen from the measured distributions before the gate run.
- Role declarations are a new obligation on every generator; a shipped generator without them fails the role-table completeness test (acceptance above). Treating an undeclared parameter as `structural` would over-split (safe for hygiene, wasteful for breadth), which is why a missing row is a test failure and never a silent default.
- Over-merging costs breadth (fewer independent groups), under-merging costs validity; findings are biased toward asking.
- Fingerprints of private packs are private.
