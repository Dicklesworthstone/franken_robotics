## Background

Two gates say that train and test hygiene works on a running system.

- **GATE-HAB-14** (Known answer, M3): a mirrored layout and a retrofit variant planted across a split are refused;
  feasibility counts in manifests equal recounts.
- **GATE-ARENA-02** (Known answer, M3): the scene-split check rejects an evaluation that reuses a training scene.

Terms. A **scene split** assigns scenes to training, development and held-out parts. Splits are made over **groups**:
a base scene with its mirrored layouts, clutter variants, retrofit variants, pool-sharing variants and every scan of
the same building; a group never straddles a split (INV-HAB-09, refusal `FROB-REF-311`, repair "move the whole
group"). Every generated scene has a **feasibility tag** (`witnessed`, `impossible: intended`, `impossible:
incidental`, `unresolved`); no scene is dropped or resampled because of its tag and the counts in a manifest equal a
recount (INV-HAB-05). At scoring, an evaluation on a scene (or a scene of a group) in the policy's training manifest
is refused (`FROB-REF-502`). The tempting shortcut both gates guard against is splitting by `SceneId` alone: a layout
and its mirror have different identifiers and the same aisles.

This bead is the end-to-end script that measures GATE-HAB-14 from the user's side and records it, and that
carries the story one step further, to a policy trained on the split and refused at scoring on what it trained
on. That last step exercises the mechanism of GATE-ARENA-02; the gate itself is measured and recorded by the
bead that owns the scoring check (N24a-31), on its own planted cases.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures, assertions, negative controls and structured log; the record of GATE-HAB-14 (the
planted straddles refused with `FROB-REF-311`; manifest counts equal to the recount); the reuse assertions of
step 8 as a chained check on this script's own split and trained policy.

Out, and where it is: the measurement and the record of GATE-ARENA-02 (100 of 100 planted reuses across the M3
archetypes refused and none of 100 clean evaluations refused, by `scripts/e2e/gate_arena_02_scene_split.sh`),
together with the scoring check itself, the training scene manifest and `FROB-REF-502`: N24a-31, on which this
bead depends. This script writes no row for that gate; its step 10 reads the row and fails if it is absent or
not `pass`. The other mechanisms (BD-235, N19c-11, N19c-13); sealed families (N19c-14, tested by their own
script).

## Technical approach

**1. Script.** `scripts/e2e/scene_split_hygiene.sh`, plain shell driving the real `frob` binary (and `frobd` for the
training step), idempotent, seed-explicit (`--seed 0x5eed0E14`), non-zero exit on any failed assertion.

**2. Fixtures (all generated; nothing private).**
- 40 warehouse scenes and 40 commercial-kitchen scenes from the M3 structural proxies, structural seeds 1 to 40 each:
  `frob --json scene gen --family warehouse --seed <s> --key hy-gen-w<s>` and `frob --json scene gen --family
  kitchen-commercial/galley --seed <s> --key hy-gen-k<s>` (a `run` verb: one key per scene).
- For base scene W7 (warehouse, seed 7): its **mirror** (seed 7 with the family's `mirror`-role parameter flipped,
  `--param mirror=true --key hy-gen-w7m`; no interior family declares that parameter yet, and its addition is
  requested of BD-225, whose role is entered in N19c-11's table), and an **independently mirrored copy** compiled from
  mirrored source with an unrelated group label (`frob --json scene compile fixtures/hab/groups/w7_mirrored.src --key
  hy-cmp-w7x`; to exercise the fingerprint).
- For base scene K3 (kitchen, seed 3): a **retrofit variant**, produced by a typed scene edit that adds a wedge
  overlay (a threshold ramp of rise 20 mm and run 240 mm) with `derived_from = K3` (`frob --json scene compile --base
  <K3> --edits fixtures/hab/edits/threshold_ramp.json --key hy-der-k3`, N19b-44's derived form); and a **clutter
  variant** (seed 3 with one `clutter`-role parameter of N19c-11's table changed, `--param <clutter parameter>=<v>
--key hy-gen-k3c`).
- A small policy trained for a fixed short budget on the split's training part (the reference wheeled robot on a
  navigation task), to obtain a real training manifest.

**3. Steps and assertions.**
`scene.split` is a `mutate` verb (BD-235): every writing call below carries its own `--key` and, after `create`,
`--expected-parent <the split's current identity>`.
1. `frob --json scene split create --name hygiene --group-by building --rule 'hash-bucket:0..7000=train'
   --rule 'hash-bucket:7000..8500=development' --rule 'hash-bucket:8500..10000=held-out' --key hy-sp-create`;
   `scene split add --split hygiene <80 ids> --expected-parent <S0> --key hy-sp-add` the 80
   base scenes. Assert exit 0, 80 members, and that the per-part counts by feasibility class sum to 80.
2. Read W7's part P. `scene split add --split hygiene <mirror of W7> --to <a part other than P> --expected-parent
   <S1> --key hy-sp-mirror`: assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-311`, the
   block names W7's group and part P, and repair 1 is "move the whole group". Repeat with the independently mirrored
   copy: assert the same refusal, with merge reason `fingerprint` in the block.
3. Same for the retrofit variant and the clutter variant of K3: assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-311`.
4. Apply repair 1 (`scene split move-group --split hygiene <group> --to <part> --expected-parent <S1> --key
   hy-sp-move`): assert exit 0 and that every member of the group moved.
5. `frob --json scene split check --split hygiene --deep`: assert exit 0; record the largest Jaccard overlap among
   distinct seeds (a measurement reported against the 0.98 threshold).
6. **Recount.** Independently recount feasibility classes by reading each member pack's tag through `scene show`
   and compare with the manifest: assert equality per part and class. Then corrupt a copy of the manifest by one
   count and assert that `scene split check` on the copy refuses it with exit 9 and the integrity row
   `scene-split-count-mismatch` of N19c-42 (row 7, family `9xx`; the proposal `FROB-REF-326` is withdrawn, so the
   script matches the slug and the code of `SPLIT_COUNT_MISMATCH`, never a typed number), the block naming the part,
   the class, the stored and the recounted value.
7. **Training.** `frob --json train start <reference design> --task fixtures/tasks/hygiene-nav.ftask --scenes
   <the training part's scene ids> --algo ppo --steps 200000 --seed 0x5eed0E14 --key hy-train` (the task's `world`
   section names split `hygiene`, part `train`), then `frob --json train export <training run> --at latest --out
   <scratch>/policies/`. Assert the policy manifest carries the split digest and that the training scene manifest
   lists only scenes of the training part, and only those actually used.
8. **Reuse rejected (the mechanism of GATE-ARENA-02, exercised here and recorded by N24a-31).** Each case is
   N24a-31's form, an exported policy as the controller of `eval.run` on a fixture battery (an N24b-01 manifest the
   script writes under its scratch battery root, one per case, holding the named scenes): `frob --json eval run
   <reference design> --controller <exported policy> --battery hy-train-scene --tier referee --key hy-ev-train`
   on a training scene: assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-502` with `by: scene`, and zero worlds run. Repeat with batteries
   holding the mirror of a training scene and a retrofit variant of a training scene (`--key hy-ev-mirror`, `--key
   hy-ev-retrofit`): assert `FROB-REF-502` with `by: group`. Then the policy form on the held-out part, `frob --json
   eval run <reference design> --policy <exported policy> --task fixtures/tasks/hygiene-nav.ftask --split held-out
   --seeds 1 --key hy-ev-held` (the held-out part of the split the task names): assert exit 0 or 2 and a scorecard
   whose evidence records the leak check with its counts.
9. **Audit.** `frob --json scene split audit --split hygiene --runs <the training run>`: assert zero violations.
10. Write the record of GATE-HAB-14. Read the row of GATE-ARENA-02 written by N24a-31's script and assert that it
    is present and `pass`; log the three reuse refusals of step 8 beside it as `assert` lines, not as a gate
    measurement.

**4. Logging.** The script sources the STD-E2E helper and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/scene_split_hygiene/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds (members, counts per class, Jaccard maximum, worlds run
before refusal), digests of split manifests before and after each mutation, and on failure the first differing value
with the scene and group identifiers needed to reproduce. The `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and
names GATE-HAB-14 as measured and GATE-ARENA-02 as read.

**5. Gate record.** One row in `registries/gates.toml`, for GATE-HAB-14, with the measured values (refusals
observed: 4 of 4 planted straddles; recount differences: 0), the commit, the hardware fingerprint and the seed,
entered with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-HAB-14 --receipt <hash>` (there is
no `frob conformance` command). Its known answers KA-456 and KA-457 are G1 rows and also run in the form of
ADR-GAUNT-01, `FROB_TEST_SEED=0x5eed0E14 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-HAB-14`; the
mutant builds of the negative controls below are built with `--features gauntlet-mutations` and are rows of
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml`, each run by the script through `e2e_expect_caught`.
The reuse figures of step 8 (reuse refusals: 3 of 3; worlds run before refusal: 0) are logged as assertions and
do not enter a gate row: one gate has one measurement, and the row of GATE-ARENA-02 is written by N24a-31 with
its own measured counts. Reviewed by a fresh-context session that did not implement the mechanisms.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-HAB-14 recorded PASS: the mirrored layout (both forms) and the retrofit variant planted across the split are refused with `FROB-REF-311`; manifest counts equal the recount in every part and class.
- [ ] On this script's split and trained policy, evaluation on a training scene, on its mirror and on its retrofit variant is rejected with `FROB-REF-502` before any world runs, and evaluation on the held-out part is admitted; the row of GATE-ARENA-02 recorded by N24a-31 is present and `pass` when this script ends, and this script has written no row for that gate.
- [ ] Each negative control below makes the script fail at the named step.
- [ ] The script is idempotent: a second run with the same seed produces the same split digests and the same assertion results.
- [ ] The script passes on Linux and macOS; split identities are equal across the two.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Helpers of the script (`scripts/e2e/tests/scene_split_hygiene.bats`): `recount_matches_manifest_parser` (the
recount helper parses `scene show` output correctly on a fixture of 5 scenes with known tags); `picks_other_part`
(the helper that chooses "a part other than P" never returns P); `gate_row_has_measured_values`.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script (section above).

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-456: a layout and its mirror image, and a retrofit variant, planted across a train and test split are refused (exact). KA-457: feasibility counts equal a recount (exact).
- Negative controls, each run by the script against a mutant build or a planted fixture and required to fail:
  1. A split keyed by `SceneId` alone (CX-196, the mutation plant `N19c-11:cx-196` of N19-CX, under which `group_of` returns the `SceneId`): step 2 must then accept the mirror, and the script must report the gate as failed.
  2. A group key that ignores `derived_from`: step 3 accepts the retrofit variant.
  3. A split builder that drops `impossible: incidental` scenes: step 1's sum is below 80.
  4. A scoring hook that checks identifiers only: step 8's mirror evaluation is admitted.
  5. A scoring hook that runs after the first chunk: worlds run before refusal is above zero.
  Controls 4 and 5 are registered against GATE-ARENA-02 by N24a-31; this script repeats them at step 8 so that
  the chained check cannot pass on a mutant scoring hook.
- Metamorphic (MR-31): a mirror image, a clutter variant, a retrofit variant and another scan of the same building belong to the split group of the base scene.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- The retrofit catalog lands at M4; at M3 the "retrofit variant" is a typed scene edit with recorded lineage (N19b-44, the same mechanism the catalog will use). When the catalog exists, the step is re-run with a catalog kit and must give the same refusal.
- The training step makes the script slower than a pure Habitat test (minutes on one card); a prebuilt policy fixture with an authored manifest covers the fast lane, and the trained policy covers the real one.
- If fewer than seven archetypes have landed, the script runs on those that have and the gate row says which; the gate needs at least the warehouse and the kitchen, which the M3 scenarios use first.
- Hash-bucket rules make part sizes approximate (about 70, 15 and 15 percent); the assertions use the realized counts.
