## Background

Habitat stores a scene's distance field in band bricks (8³ samples of 16 bits near surfaces) and shares identical
bricks between scene variants through a content-addressed **brick pool**; each variant is a dense directory of pool
slots. The bet behind the pool is a HYPOTHESIS: the unique-brick count of a warehouse or retail scene is a small
fraction of its band bricks, and a layout variant adds a few percent (illustration, not a result: a 2.5 GB warehouse
base whose variants each change 5% of bricks costs 153 MB per variant, so about 88 variants fit beside the base in
16 GB where three dense copies would). Deduplicating bricks across repeated modules and variants could change the
number of scenes per device by more than the narrow band does.

The stop rule was named before any measurement. **KILL-POOL.** Trigger: measured deduplication on the archetype
battery is below 1.5. Fallback: variants fall back to dense per-variant fields behind the same directory interface,
and the dense scene counts apply as written (for example 160 to 210 homes at 2 cm on one RTX 4090 and about 2,700 on
the large-memory machine). Decided at M2.

**Deduplication** here is band bricks counted with multiplicity over unique bricks, measured by the pool gate's script
on compiled scenes. Both branches already exist in the code: the pool has a shared mode and private modes behind one
directory trait, so the decision changes a default and the planning figures, not an interface.

## Scope

In: the decision at M2 from the measured ratios; its record; its consequences for defaults, documents and the graph.
Out: measuring (N19c-17); the mechanism (BD-164); the re-read on the seven interior archetypes at M3 (N19c-19).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** The gate row of GATE-HAB-13 from N19c-17: per scene of the M2 battery, occupancy and deduplication
   (full record and distance-only), bytes dense, band and pooled, and marginal bytes per variant.
2. **The statistic.** Three numbers are computed and all are recorded: (a) *within-scene* deduplication of each
   scene; (b) *battery* deduplication, Σ band bricks over the number of unique bricks of the whole battery admitted
   into one pool image; (c) *variant* deduplication on the variant set, that is Σ band bricks of a base and its
   variants over the unique bricks of that set. The trigger's "measured deduplication on the archetype battery" is
   read as (b) restricted to the scenes that stand for archetypes (the rack-aisle hall, the box home and their
   variants, and at M3 the seven proxies); terrain families and the mesh import are reported beside it and do not
   enter the trigger. This reading is a DESIGN choice recorded with the decision.
3. **Decision rule.** If the statistic is at least 1.5: **not fired**; `shared` stays the default pool mode. If it
   is below 1.5: **fired**; the default becomes private per-variant storage. If the full-record ratio is below 1.5
   and the distance-only ratio is at least 1.5, the cause is region labels in the brick record: one repair cycle is
   allowed (address the distance block and the label block separately, or fix region assignment for repeated modules)
   followed by one re-measurement, and then the rule is applied.
4. **Honest scope of an M2 decision.** The seven interior archetypes land at M3, so the M2 battery uses stand-ins. The
   record says so, and the decision is re-read at M3 on the real proxies (N19c-19); an M2 "not fired" can still fire
   at M3, and that is recorded as the decision of record.
5. **Record.** ADR `docs/adr/KILL-POOL.md` and the kill-gate row in `registries/gates.toml`: the three statistics, the
   threshold 1.5, the verdict, the battery's identity (scene identifiers), commit, fingerprint, date and reviewer.
6. **Consequences if fired.** Default `PoolMode` becomes `PrivateBand` (or `PrivateDense` where a job asks for it);
   the world bank plans with the dense table; the retrofit search and Nemesis, which make many variants of one base,
   lose their memory advantage and their batch sizes are re-planned from the dense counts; documents describe the
   capability as "per-variant fields", not "shared brick pool"; the pool code stays (it is the same interface) and is
   not deleted, so a later scene class can re-open the question by measurement.
7. **Consequences if not fired.** The measured marginal bytes per variant replace the illustration in the documents
   and in the world-bank planning figures.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision is recorded with the three statistics, the reading of "archetype battery" used, and the verdict.
- [ ] The default pool mode in the build equals the verdict's branch, and a test asserts it.
- [ ] On `fired`: the README status table and the pool's contract name the fallback; the world-bank planning table is the dense one.
- [ ] On `not fired`: the documents quote measured marginal bytes per variant, not the 5% illustration.
- [ ] The record states that the M2 battery uses stand-ins and names the M3 re-read bead.
- [ ] At most one repair cycle precedes the decision; the review is by a session that did not implement the pool.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`tools/gates/tests/kill_pool.rs` (the helper that reads the gate row):
- `battery_dedup_formula`: scenes with (band, unique-in-union) = (1,000; 400), (2,000; 500) and union unique 700 → battery deduplication 3,000 / 700 = 4.29.
- `fires_below_threshold`: 1.49 → fired; 1.50 → not fired.
- `label_cause_allows_one_repair`: full-record 1.2, distance-only 2.1 → "repair cycle allowed"; a second such row → the rule is applied to the full-record value.
- `terrain_and_mesh_excluded_from_trigger`.
- `default_mode_matches_verdict`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/decide_kill_pool.sh` (STD-E2E helper): reads the gate row (`frob --json gates show GATE-HAB-13`),
computes the statistics, prints the verdict and checks that the ADR, the registry row and the build's default mode
agree. NDJSON lines: command, exit code, the three statistics with the threshold 1.5, the verdict, a summary; logs
under `target/e2e-logs/decide_kill_pool/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the helper cases above on authored rows.
- Negative control: a gate row whose unique-brick count was computed per scene and summed (double counting shared bricks) gives battery deduplication 3,000 / 900 = 3.33 instead of 4.29 on the first test's data; the helper must use the union.

## Considerations

- A kill decision that blocks planning figures for other work is one step more urgent than its milestone.
- Scans share least; if real places dominate later use, the measured ratio on scans decides their storage mode separately.
- OPEN-HAB-2 (a one-sample apron on bricks) interacts: an apron makes each brick depend on its neighbours and lowers deduplication; if the apron is adopted, this decision is re-measured.
- The threshold 1.5 is the plan's; it is not tuned after seeing data.
