## Background

GATE-HAB-13 asks that "measured occupancy and deduplication ratios are recorded for every archetype", and KILL-POOL
fires if "measured deduplication on the archetype battery is below 1.5". Both are placed at M2, but the seven interior
archetypes (home, apartment, office, warehouse, manufacturing plant, retail store, commercial kitchen) are generated
by the interior grammar whose structural proxies land at M3. The M2 gate run and decision therefore use stand-ins
(N19c-17, N19c-18). This bead closes the gap: it re-runs the gate's measurement on the real proxies when they exist,
records the per-archetype rows the gate text asks for, tests the hypotheses about variant cost, and re-reads the kill
gate on the battery it was written for.

What is being tested (each a HYPOTHESIS until this bead measures it):

- The unique-brick count of a warehouse or retail scene is a small fraction of its band bricks (repeated rack bays
  and shelving units are placed on the coarse lattice so that their bricks are identical and stored once).
- A layout variant adds a few percent of bricks; the illustration assumed 5% (125 MB on a 2.5 GB base) plus a 28 MB
  directory.
- The band keeps a fraction min(1, (A/V)·t·s) of the volume with t between 8 and 20 voxels: 45 to 84 MB for a 2 cm
  home with labels, 1.6 to 3.9 GB for a warehouse.
- A cluttered home, whose surface-to-volume ratio is highest, gains least.

## Scope

In: the M3 run of the pool gate's script on the seven proxies and their variants; the per-archetype table; the
variant-cost measurement; the comparison with the byte model; the re-read of KILL-POOL. Out: the mechanisms; the M2
decision; world-bank resident counts (N19c-24), which use this bead's bytes.

## Technical approach

1. **Battery.** For each of the seven archetypes, 20 scenes from structural seeds 1 to 20 at the default spacings
   (fine 2 cm, coarse 16 cm, T = 8 cm); for the home and the warehouse also 5 scenes at 1 cm. For three base scenes
   per archetype, a variant set: two clutter-only variants, one moved-partition variant, one added-overlay variant
   (a threshold ramp), one fixture-swap variant.
2. **Run.** `scripts/e2e/brick_pool_gate.sh --battery archetypes --seed 0x5eed0D13` (the script of N19c-17 with a
   battery argument). The bit-equality assertions run unchanged on every scene and tier present.
3. **Per-archetype table (the gate's record).** For each archetype: coarse cells, band bricks, unique bricks,
   occupancy (median and range over the 20 seeds), within-scene deduplication, deduplication of the 20-scene set in
   one pool, bytes dense, band with labels and pooled, and the measured band width t implied by occupancy
   (t = occupancy·V/(A·s), with A from the compiled surface area).
4. **Variant cost.** For each variant kind: new bricks as a share of the base's band bricks, and marginal bytes
   (new bricks × 1,160 + directory bytes, or zero directory bytes when the directory is shared). Reported against
   the 5% illustration; clutter-only and overlay variants are expected at exactly zero new bricks.
5. **Model check.** Compare measured band bytes with the byte model's interval (N19c-16) per archetype; a measured
   value outside the interval is reported with the implied t, and the planning figures are corrected from
   measurement (the model's interval is a hypothesis; the measurement wins).
6. **KILL-POOL re-read.** Compute the battery deduplication over the seven archetypes' scenes and variant sets with
   the helper of N19c-18 and apply the threshold 1.5. The verdict is appended to the kill-gate row as the decision
   of record; if it differs from the M2 verdict the ADR is amended and the default pool mode changes with it.
7. **Record.** The gate row of GATE-HAB-13 gains the seven archetype rows (measured values, commit, fingerprint,
   seed); the planning tables in the pool's and the bank's contracts are regenerated from them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Zero mismatches between pooled and dense fields at 10⁶ points per scene on all 140 default-spacing scenes and the 10 fine-spacing scenes, on every tier present.
- [ ] The per-archetype table is recorded with occupancy, deduplication and bytes for all seven archetypes.
- [ ] Variant cost is recorded per variant kind; clutter-only and overlay variants add zero bricks (asserted, not only reported).
- [ ] The measured band bytes are compared with the byte model and the planning figures are updated from measurement.
- [ ] KILL-POOL is re-read on the archetype battery and the verdict of record is written, with the default pool mode consistent with it.
- [ ] If an archetype's proxy has not landed, its row is `not run` with the reason and the gate row says the record is incomplete.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `implied_band_width`: occupancy 0.40, V = 300 m³, A = 1,000 m², s = 0.02 m → t = 0.40 × 300 / (1,000 × 0.02) = 6.0 voxels (below the model's 8: reported as outside the interval).
- `variant_marginal_bytes`: base 2,155,172 band bricks (2.5 GB at 1,160 bytes), 5% new → 107,759 bricks = 125 MB, plus a 28 MB directory = 153 MB.
- `set_dedup_uses_union` (the helper of N19c-18 applied to a 20-scene set).
- `table_row_complete`: a row missing any of the listed fields is rejected by the record's schema.

### End-to-end script and logging
The script is N19c-17's with `--battery archetypes`; logs under `target/e2e-logs/brick_pool_gate/` with `battery:
archetypes` in every line, the per-archetype metrics (`occupancy`, `dedup_scene`, `dedup_set`, `bytes_*`,
`implied_t`), the variant metrics (`new_brick_share`, `marginal_bytes`) and the KILL-POOL statistic against 1.5. The
summary names GATE-HAB-13 and KILL-POOL.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-463 (bit equality). KA-459 (the dense and band figures from a recount of compiled bricks). KA-462 (directory bytes).
- Expected orderings, stated before the run so that they can fail: warehouse and retail deduplication above home deduplication; clutter-only variants at zero new bricks; a 1 cm scene near eight times the dense bytes of its 2 cm form.
- Negative controls: the mutants of N19c-17; and a grammar build that places rack bays off the coarse lattice (shifted by 5 cm), which must show the warehouse's deduplication collapsing toward 1 (this is the control that the measurement is sensitive to what it claims to measure).
- Determinism: the same seeds give the same brick counts on Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- OPEN-HAB-3 (default spacings against 1 cm interiors) is decided with this bead's measured memory and the fidelity ledger (N19c-32).
- OPEN-TRAC-1 (dense bricks or a sparse brick tree for interiors at 1 cm; default dense bricks with a coarse outer level; owner Traction lead; decided by measured memory at M3) reads the same table.
- 140 scenes at 2 cm are a few gigabytes pooled and far more in private-dense form; the dense comparison images are built and released one scene at a time.
- A result near the threshold (between 1.3 and 1.7) should be reported with its spread over seeds, since the kill rule has no margin of its own.
