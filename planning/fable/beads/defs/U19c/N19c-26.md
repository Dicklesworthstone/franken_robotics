## Background

The world bank keeps many compiled scenes resident on a device; on the large-memory machine it holds about five times
what both training cards hold together. Its use for **training** rests on a hypothesis: that a policy trained on more
distinct places does better on places it has not seen. The plan fixed the stop rule in advance.

- **KILL-BANK-BREADTH.** Trigger: held-out success does not rise with scene breadth beyond its confidence sequence
  (measured by training the reference quadruped task at a fixed step budget on 16, 160 and 1,600 distinct homes and
  scoring on a held-out group). Fallback: the bank is used for evaluation, qualification, replay, clearance and
  retrofit search and for large scenes, and training keeps per-card resident sets. Decided at M3.
- **OPEN-HAB-7.** Whether scene breadth in training improves held-out success. Default until decided (fail-closed):
  the bank serves evaluation. Owner: Habitat lead. Decided by the same experiment.
- The two-machine bet (BET-35) lists "a larger world bank does not reduce the held-out gap" among its falsifiers, so
  this decision is also evidence for or against that bet.

"Per-card resident sets" means each training card holds the scenes of its own job (about 160 to 210 homes at 2 cm by
dense arithmetic), with no attempt to route training through the large bank. Nothing about the bank's storage,
admission or scene-change mechanism is removed on either branch; what changes is which jobs are planned to use
breadth and what the documents claim.

## Scope

In: applying the pre-registered rule to the experiment's record; recording the decision; closing OPEN-HAB-7; the
consequences for defaults, planning and documents. Out: running the experiment (N19c-25); the bank (BD-236).

## Technical approach

1. **Input.** The study record of N19c-25: the pre-registration hash; per building, the held-out success of arms
   B16, B160 and B1600; the confidence sequences of the three paired contrasts at overall level 0.05; per-seed
   contrasts; the placebo contrast (two B16 runs); the generalization gaps; the configuration and budget.
2. **Decision rule (fixed in the pre-registration).**
   - **Not fired** (breadth helps): the confidence sequence of success(B1600) − success(B16) lies wholly above zero
     at the end of the registered evaluation, and the placebo contrast contains zero.
   - **Fired** (no established rise): that sequence contains zero or lies below it.
   - If the placebo contrast excludes zero, the experiment is **unsound** (training noise exceeds what the design
     can resolve): no verdict; the default of OPEN-HAB-7 stays in force and the record says what a sound rerun needs.
   - The B160 contrasts are reported and do not decide: they show whether any gain saturates early.
3. **Effect size is recorded beside the verdict.** A rise that is established and small (for example under two
   percentage points of success, a DESIGN marker, not a threshold of the rule) is flagged "established, small", so
   that the planning consequence in item 5 can be weighed by the owner.
4. **Record.** ADR `docs/delivery/adr/ADR-KILL-BANK-BREADTH-M3.md`, the kill-gate row in `registries/gates.toml`, and the entry
   closing OPEN-HAB-7: verdict, the three intervals, effect size, the budget and configuration at which it holds,
   the commit and fingerprint, the reviewer (a fresh-context session that did not run the experiment). The record
   states the scope honestly: one robot, one task family, one archetype, one budget.
5. **Consequences if fired.** Training jobs are planned on per-card resident sets; the fleet plan does not route
   training scenes through the large bank; the bank's stated purpose in the documents is evaluation, qualification,
   replay, clearance and retrofit search, and large scenes; the default scene-set size for training is what one card
   holds; BET-35's world-bank clause is recorded as falsified at this scope. The scene-change-at-reset mechanism
   stays (evaluation sweeps and qualification use it).
6. **Consequences if not fired.** Training task defaults may name scene sets larger than one card's resident set;
   the planner admits training scene sets by breadth, using rotation between chunks or the large-memory machine as
   the measured configuration allows; the measured gain per tenfold breadth is written into the planning notes, and
   the breadth at which it saturates (from the B160 contrasts) becomes the default.
7. **Re-opening.** The decision can be re-opened only by a new pre-registered experiment (another task family, a
   longer budget, another archetype), never by re-analysing this one.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision is recorded as `fired`, `not-fired` or `unsound`, with the three confidence sequences, the placebo contrast and the effect size.
- [ ] The rule applied is byte-identical to the one in the pre-registration (the record cites its hash).
- [ ] OPEN-HAB-7 is closed with the verdict, or stated as still open with its default in force when the experiment was unsound.
- [ ] On `fired`: training defaults use per-card resident sets, and the README status table and the bank's contract state the bank's purpose as evaluation, qualification, replay, clearance and retrofit search, and large scenes.
- [ ] On `not-fired`: the measured gain and the saturation breadth are in the planning notes, and training scene-set defaults reflect them.
- [ ] The scope statement (robot, task family, archetype, budget) is part of the record; the review is by a session that did not run the experiment.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`tools/gates/tests/kill_bank_breadth.rs` (the decision helper on authored study records):
- `rise_established_not_fired`: interval [0.021, 0.094] for B1600 − B16, placebo [−0.012, 0.015] → `not-fired`.
- `zero_included_fires`: [−0.010, 0.048] → `fired`.
- `negative_fires`: [−0.060, −0.005] → `fired`.
- `placebo_excludes_zero_unsound`: placebo [0.011, 0.040] → `unsound`, whatever the main contrast.
- `rule_hash_must_match`: a record whose rule text differs from the registered one is rejected.
- `small_effect_flag`: an interval wholly within (0, 0.02) is `not-fired` with the flag "established, small".

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/decide_kill_bank_breadth.sh` (STD-E2E helper): reads the study record (`frob --json study show <id>`),
applies the helper, prints the verdict and the consequence checklist, and checks that the ADR, the registry row, the
open-question entry and the training defaults in the build agree. NDJSON lines: command, exit code, the intervals,
the verdict, each consistency check; logs under `target/e2e-logs/decide_kill_bank_breadth/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the six helper cases.
- Negative controls: a helper that decides on the point estimate must fail `zero_included_fires`; one that ignores the placebo must fail `placebo_excludes_zero_unsound`; an ADR that says `not-fired` while the training default still uses per-card sets must fail the consistency check.

## Considerations

- A kill decision that fixes planning defaults for training is one step more urgent than its milestone.
- A null result is informative only at the tested budget; the record must not be quoted as "breadth does not help".
- The cost of being wrong differs by direction: wrongly keeping breadth costs memory and scene compilation; wrongly dropping it costs generalization. The owner may weigh an "established, small" result either way; the record keeps the rule's verdict separate from that judgment.
- Family policies and inheritance (later milestones) may change the answer, which is why re-opening is allowed by a new registered experiment.
