## Background

A **known answer** is a case whose truth is fixed in advance by a derivation or a construction; a mechanism "recovers
KA-n within tolerance", and the expected value is never regenerated from the code under test. Wherever one is stated
it carries a **negative control**: a planted defect the test must catch.

The Habitat chapter (scenes, living worlds, clearance, retrofits) closes with a table of eighteen such cases, each
with its truth and, for fourteen of them, a control. Their tests live in the mechanism beads that own each case, and
the chapter's known-answer registry lists them by number. What no mechanism bead provides is the view from the user's
side: one command that runs the whole table through the real binary, shows each truth recovered and each control
caught, and fails loudly when a row has silently gone missing or a control has stopped biting. This bead is that
runner and its report. It also registers, as extension rows, the known answers of the two subsections added after
the suite was numbered.

## Scope

In: the battery definition (`habitat`), the runner, the per-row report, the extension rows, the check that every row
of the chapter's table is present with its control. Out: the tests themselves (owned by the beads named per row);
the registry of numbered known answers (N19-KA); the counterexample registry (N19-CX).

## Technical approach

1. **Battery.** `crates/frob-conformance/batteries/habitat.toml` (DESIGN path): one entry per row with `row`, `ka`,
   `truth`, `tolerance`, `test` (symbol or verb fixture), `control` (mutant or planted fixture), `milestone`, `bead`.
   The runner (`frob --json conformance run --battery habitat`) executes each due row twice: on the real build,
   expecting the truth; with the control enabled, expecting the test to fail. A row not yet due is reported
   `pending` with its milestone; a due row with no test is a failure of this battery.

5. **Report.** One line per row: identifier, truth, measured value with unit, tolerance, pass or fail, control name,

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The battery lists all eighteen rows of the chapter's table and the eight extension rows, each with truth, tolerance, test, control, bead and milestone.
- [ ] Every row whose milestone has been reached runs and passes, and its control is caught; a due row without a test, or with a missed control, fails the battery.
- [ ] Rows 14, 15 and 18 have the designed controls, and each fails its test when enabled.
- [ ] `frob --json conformance run --battery habitat` is reachable from the command line and returns the per-row report in its envelope.
- [ ] The battery's row list is checked against the chapter's registry (N19-KA): a registry row with section §19.18 that is missing from the battery fails a test.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/habitat_battery.rs`:
- `battery_has_eighteen_plus_eight_rows`.
- `arithmetic_truths` (rows whose truth is closed-form, pinned here independently of the mechanisms): squeeze times 0.0377 s, 2.26 s, 0.763 s; castor and wheel ratios 4/3, 0.75, 1/12; h_max = 2.48 mm; ω₁ = 8.955 rad/s; corridor limit 1.3456 m; error term 35.36 + 39.27 = 74.63 mm; the HKA-D and HKA-E numbers.
- `designed_controls_bite`: the three designed controls change the pinned values (35.4 mm; √(20·120)/30 = 1.63; half the bytes).
- `missed_control_fails_battery`: a row whose control is a no-op makes the runner exit non-zero.
- `pending_before_milestone_is_not_failure`, `due_row_without_test_is_failure`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/habitat_known_answers.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): runs `frob --json conformance run --battery
habitat --seed 0x5eed0018` on the host tier and on each device tier present; writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/habitat_known_answers/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Per row one `metric` event (identifier, KA
number, measured value with unit, truth, tolerance, tier, evidence block) and one `assert` event for the control
(`caught: true|false`); on failure the first differing value and the fixture path. The `summary` line gives rows
passed, failed, pending, controls caught and missed, and the gates the rows feed (GATE-HAB-01, -04 to -08, -10, -11,
-13 to -16).

### Known answers and negative controls
This bead is the battery of both. Its own control: `missed_control_fails_battery`. Metamorphic relations run beside
the table: MR-29 (inner complex ⊆ free space ⊆ outer complex; a finer grid only narrows the band), MR-30 (pooled
equals dense; one `SceneId` per seed on both machines), MR-31 (mirror, clutter, retrofit and rescan share the base's
split group). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS; each device tier against
its class.

## Considerations

- Three discrepancies between the numbered suite and the chapter are recorded by the registry bead and matter here: the squeeze-film tolerance (the suite prints 1%, the chapter and the gate 2%; 2% is used); the wet-floor tile count (352 against 361 whole tiles); the resident-count column (computed from 189 against 203 GB).
- Rows 8 to 12 are Frontier mechanisms due at M6 and may never come due if their kill gates fire; a row whose mechanism fell back is marked `withdrawn: <kill gate>`, not deleted.
- The runner must not become the place where expected values are edited; truths live in the registry and in this bead's arithmetic pins.
