## Background

**GATE-HAB-16** (Known answer, M2). **The two sphere points return clearances of 0.10 m and 0.20 m after compilation,
and the pre-compilation query returns the interval of rule 1 and never the local value. The two-plane fixture returns
the foreground at 2 m through the compiled pack, through the viewer's pick and through the adapter; the mutant that
sorts local parameters returns the background and fails. On 10³ random instances with anisotropic scales in
[0.25, 4], depth against an analytic plane is within 1 mm plus the brick bound.**

What the gate protects. Geometry reached through an instance transform with a scale yields local quantities that are
not world quantities. The rules: (1) a local signed distance d bounds the world distance to [σ_min(A)·d, σ_max(A)·d]
for the transform's linear part A, and a local distance is never returned in metres; (2) normals transform by A⁻ᵀ and
are renormalized; (3) a ray's hit parameter from a primitive that renormalizes the local direction converts by
s_W = s_L/‖A⁻¹·d‖, before hits are sorted, range-gated or used as optical depth. The compiler bakes scales, so a
compiled pack is in world metres.

The fixtures. **Sphere:** a unit sphere scaled by (2, 0.5, 1); at (0, 0.6, 0) the local distance is 0.20 and the
clearance 0.10 m; at (2.2, 0, 0) the local distance is 0.10 and the clearance 0.20 m; both inside [0.5·d, 2·d].
**Two planes:** an opaque foreground plane at 2 m in an instance of scale 1/2 (local parameter 4) and a background
plane at 3 m in an instance of scale 3 (local parameter 1); sorting local parameters returns the occluded plane.

This bead is the gate's script and record. The gate is defined with the subsection and listed among those added by
the final audit; its register row is created from that definition.

## Scope

In: the script, fixtures, assertions on four paths (pre-compilation query, compiled pack, viewer pick, sibling
adapter), the random-instance depth test, logging, the gate record. Out: the mechanisms (N19a-11, N19c-38, N19c-39,
BD-163, the ray kernels).

## Technical approach

**1. Script.** `scripts/e2e/gate_hab_16_scaled_instances.sh --seed 0x5eed0A16`, plain shell over the real `frob`
binary, idempotent, non-zero exit on a failed assertion.

**2. Steps and assertions.**
1. **Pre-compilation interval.** `frob --json scene show --source fixtures/scenes/aniso-sphere.src --probe 0,0.6,0`
   and `--probe 2.2,0,0`. Assert each result is an interval with `resolved: false` or, if resolved, a method other
   than "local"; assert the intervals are [0.10, 0.40] m and [0.05, 0.20] m to 10⁻⁹; assert that the values 0.20
   (first point) and 0.10 (second point) are not returned as a point distance anywhere in the envelope.
2. **Compiled sphere.** `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/aniso-sphere.src`; `frob --json scene show <id>
   --probe …` at the two points. Assert 0.10 m and 0.20 m within the pack's recorded brick bound, and that the bound
   is at most half a fine voxel.
3. **A probe that must not fit.** Assert that a 0.15 m spherical probe at (0, 0.6, 0) is reported as intersecting
   by the enclosure test on the compiled pack (the local value would have admitted it).
4. **Two planes, compiled pack.** `frob --json scene compile fixtures/scenes/two-planes.src`;
   `frob --json telemetry stills --depth --camera <fixture pose> --pixel center` (a depth sample along the fixture
   ray, drawn from the collision field by the ray kernels) on `batch-cpu` and each device tier present. Assert a
   range of 2.000 m within 1 mm plus the brick bound and the foreground's region identifier; with a range gate of
   2.5 m assert the same hit.
5. **Two planes, pre-compilation host path.** `frob --json scene accept --preview-rays
   fixtures/scenes/two-planes.src`: assert the first hit at 2 m, foreground.
6. **Two planes, viewer pick.** The viewer's headless pick test on the same scene and ray: assert the foreground
   and 2 m (to the viewer's float precision, 10⁻⁴ m).
7. **Two planes, adapter.** The adapter over the sibling ray primitive: assert converted parameters 2 m and 3 m and
   the foreground first. Before the renderer route is admitted this runs against the in-crate reproduction of the
   sibling's convention and the row is recorded `adapter: convention-reproduction`; once admitted it runs through the
   real primitive and the row is recorded `adapter: sibling`.
8. **The mutant.** Run steps 4 to 7 with the registered mutant that sorts hits by local parameter: assert it returns
   the background plane (3 m) and that the script's assertion fails on it (the control must bite on every path that
   has a sorting step).
9. **Random instances.** `frob --json conformance run --case scaled-instance-plane-depth --instances 1000`: for each
   of 10³ instances with per-axis scales drawn by address from [0.25, 4] (log-uniform), a random rotation and a
   random analytic plane placed 1 to 6 m along a random ray, compile and cast; assert abs(depth − closed form) ≤
   1 mm + the brick bound for every instance; log the worst case.
10. Write the gate record.

**3. Logging.** STD-E2E helper; NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/gate_hab_16_scaled_instances/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript. Each line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the
envelope's evidence block (claim Known answer, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with
units and thresholds (`clearance_m` against 0.10 and 0.20; `interval_lo_m`, `interval_hi_m`; `range_m` against
2.000; `first_hit` against `foreground`; `depth_error_m` against 0.001 + bound), digests of the compiled packs, and
on failure the first differing value with the fixture and the instance's transform. The `summary` line gives
assertions passed and failed and names GATE-HAB-16.

**4. Gate record.** A row for GATE-HAB-16 in `registries/gates.toml`: the two clearances and their bounds, the two
intervals, the first hit and range on each of the four paths (with the adapter's mode), the mutant's result, the
worst depth error over the 10³ instances with its bound, the tiers run, commit, hardware fingerprint and seed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After compilation the two sphere points return 0.10 m and 0.20 m (within the recorded brick bound); before compilation the query returns [0.10, 0.40] m and [0.05, 0.20] m and never the local value.
- [ ] The two-plane fixture returns the foreground at 2 m through the compiled pack (on every tier present), through the viewer's pick and through the adapter.
- [ ] The mutant that sorts local parameters returns the background and fails the script.
- [ ] On 10³ random instances with anisotropic scales in [0.25, 4], depth against an analytic plane is within 1 mm plus the brick bound for every instance.
- [ ] The gate row carries measured values, the adapter's mode, commit, fingerprint and seed; if the adapter clause ran only against the convention reproduction, the row says so and the clause is re-run when the renderer route is admitted.
- [ ] The script passes on Linux and macOS with identical host results.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Helpers (`scripts/e2e/tests/gate_hab_16.bats` and the conformance case's tests): `no_local_value_scanner` (an
envelope containing the point value 0.20 for the first probe is flagged; an interval whose upper end is 0.20 for the
second probe is not, because it is an interval end); `log_uniform_scales_in_range` (10³ addressed draws all within
[0.25, 4]); `closed_form_plane_depth` (ray origin o, unit direction d, plane n·x = c: depth (c − n·o)/(n·d)); the
fixture files' own invariants (the planes are at 2 m and 3 m along the fixture ray; the instance scales are 1/2 and 3).

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script (section above).

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the sphere clearances 0.10 m and 0.20 m with their intervals; the foreground at 2 m; local parameters 4 and 1; the plane-depth closed form on 10³ instances.
- Negative controls: the sort-by-local-parameter mutant (required by the gate); a query path that returns the local distance (step 1 and step 3 must fail it); a compiler build that does not bake a non-uniform scale (step 2 returns 0.20 and 0.10); a ray path that omits the conversion in the adapter (step 7 returns 4 and 1).
- Metamorphic: applying one more rigid motion to the whole fixture changes no asserted value.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS; each device tier against its class for step 4.

## Considerations

- The gate is due at M2, but the renderer route that carries the real sibling primitive is admitted at M3; the adapter clause is therefore recorded in two modes, and the M2 record is complete for the other clauses.
- The viewer's pick is JavaScript and is driven headless; if the viewer bundle is not built on a machine, that row is `not run` with the reason, and the gate is not complete there.
- "1 mm plus the brick bound" makes the random-instance tolerance depend on each pack's recorded bound; the script logs both terms so that a loose bound cannot hide an error.
- Shapes for the random-instance test are analytic planes because their depth has a closed form under any instance transform; curved shapes are covered by the sphere fixture.
