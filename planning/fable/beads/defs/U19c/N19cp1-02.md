## Background

Far-field field reuse is a Moonshot mechanism: a **fork** (a re-run of a recorded world from a snapshot with one
change) shares the parent's field tiles (a liquid film on the floor, and on the evaluation tier thermal,
optical-volume and fluid-island fields) and recomputes only the tiles whose inputs differ. With a the share of a
fork's compute spent on field tile updates, d the dirty fraction of those updates and b the bookkeeping share, a fork
costs (1 − a) + a·d + b of a full recomputation; with every far tile clean the factor is 1/((1 − a) + b): 1.23 at
a = 0.2, 4.8 at a = 0.8 and 16.7 at a = 0.95, for b = 0.01.

The project's rule is that a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier runs before a Moonshot mechanism is funded. The stop
rule was named before the work. **KILL-FARFIELD.** Trigger, any one of: (1) one digest mismatch that survives a repair
cycle; (2) the pilot's falsifier (d above 4.21% on the two-room case, b above 5%, or one digest mismatch); (3) no
admitted living-world fixture reaches a ≥ 0.8. **Fallback:** the full-replay forks, which are exact and need no
dependency tracking.

Triggers (2) and (3) can be read before any mechanism is built: (2) from the pilot's record (N19c-33), (3) from the
phase ledgers of the living-world fixtures that exist, measured without reuse. Trigger (1) and the gate's cost clause
can only be read after the mechanism and its gate (N19c-34 to N19c-36). The decision is therefore taken in two
stages, and they sit at two different points of the work graph: this bead is **stage A**, which the mechanism bead
N19c-34 depends on, so that no mechanism work starts before the verdict exists; stage B, after the gate, is N19c-37.
Stage A is a bead of its own because inside N19c-37 it would depend on the gate that comes after the mechanism, and
its acceptance ("dated before any mechanism bead started") could not be met.

## Scope

In: reading the pilot's record and applying trigger (2); measuring or reading the largest a that admitted
living-world fixtures reach without reuse and applying trigger (3); the stage A verdict and its record in the
kill-gate record and the ADR; the consequences of a stage A firing; the decision helper's stage A part, which
N19c-37 extends.

Out, with the bead that has it: the pilot program and its measurements (N19c-33); the mechanism (N19c-34, N19c-35);
the gate's measurement (N19c-36); stage B, the repair cycle on a gate mismatch and the final verdict (N19c-37); the
kill record's trigger, fallback and decision point (the fallback ledger, N40-29); the advected film and its own kill
gate (N19b-08 to N19b-11).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.**
   - The pilot record (N19c-33's row in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`, schema
     `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`; the draft name `frob.pilot/1` is retired): d pooled over 100 forks on the two-room case, b, the digest
     mismatch count, the cone check, commit and machine. The open-plan variant's d is read and reported; it does not
     enter the falsifier.
   - The decision of KILL-ADVECT (N19b-11): whether the advected film is an admitted field.
   - The phase ledger (the M2 step profile of N21a-27) of each admitted living-world fixture that exists, run without
     reuse at its registered workload: a = time in field tile updates over the step time. Candidates: the advected
     spill fixtures of the film gate (if KILL-ADVECT did not fire) and any evaluation-tier field island that is
     admitted. A fixture that is not admitted is listed and does not count.
2. **Trigger (2), as numbered steps.** (1) Read d, b and the mismatch count. (2) If d > 0.0421, or b > 0.05, or
   mismatches ≥ 1: **fired at stage A**, trigger 2, naming the quantity. (3) Otherwise the pilot clears trigger 2,
   with each value recorded beside its threshold. Boundaries: d = 0.0421 and b = 0.05 clear (the falsifier says
   "above").
3. **Trigger (3).** If no admitted fixture's measured a is at least 0.8 (including the case that there is no admitted
   living-world field at all, for example because KILL-ADVECT fired and no evaluation-tier field is admitted):
   **fired at stage A**, trigger 3, with the largest a observed and its fixture. a = 0.80 clears. The largest a and
   its fixture are recorded either way and handed to stage B.
4. **Record.** `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FARFIELD-M6.md` (the program's one directory for decision records; KNOWN_DRIFTS 4),
   with N40-03's required fields, and the first entry of the `decision` array of the KILL-FARFIELD row that the
   fallback ledger created in `registries/gates.toml` (N40-29): `exit = "M6"`, the outcome in N40-03's names
   (`fired` for `fired: trigger 2` or `fired: trigger 3`; `not-fired` for "cleared at stage A", its reads limited to
   triggers 2 and 3), `adr`, `date`, `session` and `receipts` (the pilot result's receipt and those of the
   phase-ledger runs; N40-03 asks at least one for `not-fired`), with no `reading`; and in the ADR d, b,
   mismatches, the largest a with its fixture, each beside its threshold with its receipt, the pilot record's
   identity, commit, and the reviewer (a fresh-context session that did not implement the pilot). The owner
   declaration (`[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`, the ledger's `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads`
   copied, not restated, and `decision = [{ exit = "M6", outcome, adr, measured = [{ name, value, unit, receipt }]
   }]` carrying d, b, the mismatch count and the largest a) goes in `frob-scene`'s `contract.toml`, which the
   cross-check N40-30 compares with the ledger. Stage B (N19c-37) appends a second entry with `reading = "final"`
   and its own ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FARFIELD-M6-final.md` (N40-03 step 6), to the row and to this declaration;
   this entry is never edited. This bead writes no trigger, fallback or decision-point field of the ledger row:
   those are the ledger's.
5. **Consequences if fired at stage A.** N19c-34, N19c-35 and N19c-36 are closed as not planned with this record
   cited; N19c-37 records the final verdict `fired` at stage A without a stage B; no reuse code path is built; the
   fork receipt schema keeps the `field_reuse` block with `enabled: false, reason: "withdrawn"`; documents describe
   forks as full-replay forks, by the name of the fallback.
6. **Consequences if cleared.** N19c-34 may start. The record says what remains open for stage B: trigger 1 and the
   gate's cost clause, both read from GATE-HAB-15.
7. **Helper.** `tools/gates/src/kill_farfield.rs`, function `stage_a(pilot, fixtures) -> StageA`, pure, on parsed
   records; N19c-37 adds `stage_b`, which refuses to run without a stage A record.
8. **Re-opening.** A stage A firing can be re-opened only by a new pilot on a field kind or fixture not covered (for
   example an evaluation-tier island with a different stencil), never by re-running the same pilot until it passes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The stage A record exists (`docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FARFIELD-M6.md` and the first `decision` entry) with d, b, the mismatch count and the largest a with its fixture, each beside its threshold, and the verdict `fired` (with the trigger and the quantity named) or `cleared at stage A` (outcome `not-fired` on triggers 2 and 3); `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` passes for KILL-FARFIELD.
- [ ] The record is dated before any of N19c-34, N19c-35 or N19c-36 started (N19c-34 depends on this bead), and cites the pilot record by identity.
- [ ] On `fired`: the three mechanism and gate beads are closed as not planned citing this record; the build contains no active reuse path; the fork receipt carries `enabled: false, reason: "withdrawn"`.
- [ ] The helper's stage A cases below pass, and its verdict on the real pilot record equals the recorded verdict.
- [ ] The review is by a session that did not implement the pilot.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`tools/gates/tests/kill_farfield.rs` (stage A part; authored records, labelled `synthetic`):
- `pilot_d_above_fires`: d = 0.045 → fired, trigger 2, quantity d; d = 0.042 → not fired on d; d = 0.0421 → not fired (boundary).
- `pilot_b_above_fires`: b = 0.051 → fired; b = 0.050 → not fired.
- `pilot_one_mismatch_fires`: one mismatch with d and b in range → fired, trigger 2.
- `no_fixture_reaches_a`: largest a = 0.79 → fired, trigger 3; 0.80 → cleared; an empty fixture list → fired, trigger 3, with the reason "no admitted living-world field".
- `unadmitted_fixture_does_not_count`: a fixture with a = 0.93 that is not admitted leaves trigger 3 fired.
- `open_plan_not_in_falsifier`: an open-plan d of 0.09 with a two-room d of 0.02 → cleared, and the open-plan value is in the record.
- `threshold_from_algebra`: the d threshold equals (0.1 − 0.05 − 0.01)/0.95 = 0.0421 to four places (the dirty fraction at which a = 0.95 and b = 0.01 give a factor of 10).
- `record_shape`: the stage A record has every field of step 4 and no trigger, fallback or decision-point field; its outcome is `fired` or `not-fired` (never `cleared` as an outcome name), it has no `reading` and at least one receipt, and its ADR path is `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FARFIELD-M6.md`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/decide_kill_farfield.sh --stage A` (STD-E2E helper; N19c-37 adds `--stage B` to the same script):
reads the pilot record from `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` read-only (the register has no `frob`
verb; KNOWN_DRIFTS 1) and runs `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test pilot_register -- habitat_farfield`; measures
the phase ledger of each admitted living-world fixture without reuse by a profiled run, `frob --json sim run
<fixture design> --scene <fixture> --worlds 1 --seconds <registered workload> --tier <tier> --seed <s>
--profile-kernels --key kfa-a-<fixture>-<r>` for r = 1 … 3 (the three profile runs of the spread), then `frob --json
run show <run> --phase-ledger` (VERBS-run lists the option on `run.show`; it prints N21a-27's step profile, and
N21a-27, a dependency, builds both the option and `--profile-kernels`). a is the profile's `field_tile_share`, a
per-kernel figure: on a run recorded without `--profile-kernels` it is `unavailable` with exit 2, so the script
asserts exit 0 and a numeric `field_tile_share` on each of the three runs; applies the helper,
prints the verdict and the consequence checklist, and checks that the ADR's stage A section, the kill-gate record's
stage A outcome and the state of N19c-34 to N19c-36 agree with the verdict, and runs `cargo test -p frob-conformance
--test kill_ledger` (asserting that no failure names KILL-FARFIELD). A planted pilot record with d = 0.05 is run
through the same path and must give `fired: trigger 2`. NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/decide_kill_farfield/<utc>.ndjson`
with a transcript; every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time; then d, b
and mismatches against 4.21%, 5% and 0, each fixture's a against 0.8 with its admission status, the pilot record's
identity and commit, the verdict, and each consistency check with the two values compared and where each was read; on
a disagreement the file and field; a final `summary` line with assertions passed and failed, the stage and
`gates: ["KILL-FARFIELD"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the helper cases above; the threshold 4.21% from the algebra; the ceiling values 1.23, 4.8 and 16.7.
- Negative controls: a helper that reads the open-plan d into the falsifier must fail `open_plan_not_in_falsifier`; a helper that counts unadmitted fixtures must fail `unadmitted_fixture_does_not_count`; a helper that uses "at least" for the d and b thresholds must fail the boundary cases; the planted pilot record with d = 0.05 must give a firing in the script.
- Determinism: the helper is pure; the same records give the same verdict on every platform.

## Considerations

- A kill decision that blocks mechanism work is one step more urgent than its Moonshot priority.
- The a measured here is without reuse and on the fixtures that exist now; stage B re-reads a on the admitted fixtures of the gate. A stage A clearance on a single fixture near 0.8 is recorded with its spread over three profile runs, so that stage B is not surprised.
- If the advected film was killed (KILL-ADVECT) and no evaluation-tier field is admitted, trigger 3 fires here by default; that is a formality worth recording once, with the reason.
- Exactness is never traded: one pilot mismatch fires the gate whatever d and b are.
- The pilot's a is near 1 by construction (no rigid-body solve) and is not the a of trigger 3; the record keeps the two apart.
