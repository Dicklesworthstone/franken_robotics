## Background

Designs related by a symmetry form an **orbit**, and Phylum (the subsystem for families of bodies) avoids
simulating every member. The rule that governs what may be shared is exact: members of an orbit have equal
outcome *distributions*, not equal outcomes at equal addresses; **the design g·d in world w behaves as d
does in world g⁻¹·w**. So a distributional result (a scorecard over a world distribution the group
preserves) is shared across an orbit, while "a result tied to an address is shared only after the address
is mapped through the group action, where the scene generator can do that; a comparison paired by address
against a third design is re-run". Counterexample CX-156 is the shortcut this forbids (serving the
representative's per-address results unchanged to every orbit member), and known answer KA-377 is the
positive control: under a mirror swap by an element of Γ_obs (the observation-preserving symmetry subgroup)
every distributional result is reused and per-address results are reused after the address mapping.

A **world address** is `WorldAddr = (run_seed, world, episode)`, and every random stream is keyed by it.
The transport bead (N17a-24) and the invalidation contract (BD-286) call `map_address(g, w)` "supplied by
the scene generator" and fall back to recomputation when it returns nothing. Nothing supplies it: the
equivariance test of the stored Γ_obs tuple (BD-241) builds image worlds privately and returns no address.
With streams keyed by address, the map from an address to a scene is not invertible, so "the address whose
scene is the image" exists only if addresses are laid out so that it does. This bead lays them out.

DESIGN decision: **orbit blocks**. In a family that opts in, consecutive world indices form blocks of |G|
worlds (G the family's effective law group, bead N19cx-01) that share one base draw and differ only by the
group element applied to it. Rejected alternatives: searching seeds for a matching scene (no bound, and a
near match is not an image); extending `WorldAddr` with a group tag (the address is a workspace-wide
convention: results, telemetry and pairing are keyed by the three fields, and a fourth would touch every
consumer); building the image as an unaddressed scratch world, as the equivariance test does (a result at
no address cannot be served, paired or replayed). Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: the opt-in `orbit_blocks` of a family descriptor; block and slot arithmetic; keying of Habitat's streams
by the block address; `orbit_slot` and `map_address` with their refusal reasons; the image certificate and
its verifier; the address form of `scene.show`; the export other subsystems need to couple their own
streams to the block.

Out: the law group and its routes (N19cx-01); classifying results, transporting values and voiding paired
comparisons (N17a-24); the invalidation contract and GATE-PHYLUM-05 (BD-286); the equivariance test and
noise-stream permutation of sensors (BD-241); coupling of the randomization, disturbance and sensor-noise
streams to the block (their owners: Arena, Cortex and Sensorium; this bead only exports the slot); the
replay check that decides whether a transported per-address result is bit-identical (N17a-24).

## Technical approach

**1. Opt-in.** `orbit_blocks = true` in `frob.scene.family/1`, admitted only when every element of the
effective law group has the route `orbit-tag`. It is part of `FamilyId`. Let k = |G| (1, 2, 4 or 8) and
e₀ … e_{k−1} the elements of G in the canonical order of the group's multiplication table (identity first).

**2. Block arithmetic.** For world index n: block b = n div k, slot j = n mod k. The **block address** of
`w = (s, n, ep)` is `B(w) = (s, k·b, ep)`.

**3. Keying.** Every Habitat stream of the world (structure, dimensions, clutter, conditions, hazards,
fixture parameters, schedules, actor routes, spawn and goal regions) is keyed by `B(w)` instead of `w`, so
all k worlds of a block draw the same base scene S_b. One further draw, t_b, uniform on G, comes from the
stream `scene.law.orbit` keyed by `B(w)`. The element applied to the world in slot j is
h(w) = e_j · t_b, applied as the generator's last stage (the orbit tag of N19cx-01):
scene(w) = h(w) · S_b.
Consequences: within a block every element of G appears exactly once (right multiplication by t_b permutes
G), so a batch made of whole blocks is exactly balanced; and for each single world h(w) is uniform on G, so
the law of every world is the family's law, unchanged by the layout.

**4. The map.**

```rust
pub struct OrbitSlot { pub block: WorldAddr, pub slot: u8, pub element: LawElem }      // DESIGN
pub fn orbit_slot(f: &SceneFamily, w: WorldAddr) -> Option<OrbitSlot>;
pub fn map_address(f: &SceneFamily, gamma: LawElem, w: WorldAddr) -> Result<MappedAddress, NoImage>;
pub struct MappedAddress { pub addr: WorldAddr, pub certificate: ImageCertificate }
pub enum NoImage { NotBlocked, NotInLawGroup(LawBreaker), SceneSet, Ingested, Edited { edit: usize } }
```

`map_address(γ, (s, k·b + j, ep)) = (s, k·b + j′, ep)` where e_{j′} = γ·e_j (one table lookup). Then
scene(w′) = e_{j′}·t_b·S_b = γ·e_j·t_b·S_b = γ·scene(w): world w′ is γ applied to world w. The inverse
direction used by the orbit rule ("d′ = g·d in w behaves as d in g⁻¹·w") is `map_address(γ⁻¹, w)`. The map
is a group action on addresses: `map(γ₁, map(γ₂, w)) = map(γ₁·γ₂, w)` and `map(e, w) = w`.
`NoImage` reasons: the family has no orbit blocks; γ is not in the effective law group (the breaker of
N19cx-01 is attached, for example a world-fixed lighting direction or a fixture with no opposite hand); the
task's `world` names an explicit scene set or an ingested real place (no image exists); the world's scene
is a variant produced by typed scene edits after generation and the named edit has no image rule.

**5. Certificate and verifier.** `ImageCertificate { family: FamilyId, gamma, from: WorldAddr, to:
WorldAddr, source_digest_from, source_digest_to, image_digest }`, where `image_digest` is the BLAKE3 hash of
γ applied to the canonical source description of the `from` world. `verify_image` regenerates both sources
and checks `image_digest == source_digest_to`: an exact comparison of rationals on the millimetre grid under
a signed coordinate permutation. It then samples the two compiled packs at 10⁴ counter-addressed points p:
the distance at γ·p in the image against the distance at p in the original must agree within the sum of
the two packs' per-brick error bounds (PO-HAB-01), the region labels must be equal, and normals must
correspond under the linear part of γ. Whether the two fields agree **bit for bit** is measured and
recorded per family in the certificate (`field_bitwise: bool`); it is never assumed.

**6. What the certificate does not cover.** It certifies Habitat's draws only. For d′ = g·d in w′ to
behave exactly as d in w, the streams other subsystems key by address (initial state, randomization,
disturbances, sensor noise) must correspond too. `orbit_slot` is the export they need: a stream that opts
into orbit coupling keys its draws by `block` and permutes its identifiers by `element`. The certificate
lists `covered_streams` (Habitat's) so that a consumer can see what is and is not coupled; the replay check
of the transport bead decides whether a result is served as bit-identical.

**7. Interactions.** Mutable scene state (door angles, film thickness) starts from the pack at reset, so it
needs no mapping. The world bank holds each of a block's k packs as its own variant, sharing bricks where
the image maps bricks to bricks. All k packs of a block share one split group, so a block never straddles a
train and test split. A fork's child has its own address; its block is that of its parent.

**8. Surface.** No new verb. `frob scene show --family <name> --address <seed>:<world>:<episode> --json`
returns the orbit slot; with `--image <element>` it returns the mapped address and the certificate, or the
`NoImage` reason in the envelope's `detail` with exit code 0 (an absent image is an answer, not an error).
`scene.gen` gains the world form `frob scene gen --family <name> --address <seed>:<world>:<episode> --key <k>`
(the pack of that world, generated from its block address and slot; exclusive with `--seed`, `FROB-REF-802`
otherwise). VERBS-scene lists both, with this bead under "Extended by" on `scene.show` and the world form on
`scene.gen`, and the help-schema snapshot changes additively.
Schemas `frob.scene.orbit-slot/1` and `frob.scene.image-certificate/1`. Class `Tested`; determinism D0 for
the map and the source comparison.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For a family with |G| = 2 and one with |G| = 8: over 256 blocks, the k scenes of each block are pairwise images of each other under the stated elements (exact source comparison), and each block contains every element once.
- [ ] `map_address` is a group action: identity, composition and inverse hold on 10⁴ random (γ₁, γ₂, w).
- [ ] The marginal tag of a fixed slot over 4,096 blocks is uniform within 4 standard deviations.
- [ ] `verify_image` passes for every generator of `synthetic/blocked-d4` and of every shipped blocked family on 64 blocks; the field check holds within the PO-HAB-01 bounds; `field_bitwise` is recorded.
- [ ] `map_address` returns `NotBlocked`, `NotInLawGroup`, `SceneSet`, `Ingested` and `Edited` in the five planted situations, and a caller given `NoImage` is not handed any address.
- [ ] The planted defect "image address computed with e_j·γ instead of γ·e_j" is caught on the eight-element group (it passes on abelian groups, so the test uses a quarter turn and a mirror).
- [ ] `orbit_slot` is exported in `CONTRACT.md` with the coupling rule; the address form of `scene.show` is reachable through `frob --json`.
- [ ] Turning `orbit_blocks` on changes `FamilyId`, and results recorded under the old identity are not served for the new one.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-scene/tests/orbit_blocks.rs`; real generators; `synthetic` families where planted.
- `block_slot_arithmetic`: n = 13, k = 4 → block 3, slot 1, block address world 12.
- `block_shares_base_draw`: the canonical sources of a block's worlds, with their tags undone, are identical.
- `tags_form_the_group`: {h(w)} over a block equals G for 1,000 random t_b.
- `map_is_left_action`: the composition law above; `map(γ, w)` stays inside the block.
- `map_mirror_then_quarter_turn_order_matters`: with r the quarter turn (x, y) ↦ (−y, x) and m the mirror (x, y) ↦ (x, −y), r·m is the mirror through the diagonal (x, y) ↦ (y, x) and m·r is (x, y) ↦ (−y, −x); the two mapped addresses differ.
- `certificate_roundtrip`, `certificate_rejects_wrong_gamma` (a certificate for m offered for r fails).
- `no_image_reasons`: the five cases.
- `door_hand_in_image`: a left-hinged door at (2.40, 0.80) m in w is right-hinged at (2.40, −0.80) m in the mirror image.
- `episode_preserved`: the mapped address has the same `run_seed` and `episode`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-377 (per-address half): the image address exists, the certificate verifies, and the positive control of the invalidation contract can reuse per-address results after mapping.
- CX-156: the unmapped address. The test serves d's result at w to d′ at w and must observe a different world (source digests differ) unless the scene is itself symmetric.
- Metamorphic relation MR-04 (orbit representatives): evaluating a member and transporting equals evaluating the representative; this bead supplies the address half of its runner.
- Negative controls: right action in place of left action; a generator that keys clutter by `w` instead of `B(w)` (`block_shares_base_draw` fails); a verifier that compares `SceneId`s instead of images (it rejects every true image, since image packs have different identities).
- Determinism: map and certificate D0: twice, across worker counts, debug and release, Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- Interiors of most tasks will not opt in at first, so per-address sharing stays rare and distributional sharing carries the saving; this is the honest size of the mechanism (a factor of at most |Γ_obs|, two for most sensed robots).
- Blocks reduce the number of independent base scenes in a batch of N worlds to N/k. A study that needs N independent scenes must say so; the family's descriptor, hence the `TaskId` that names it, records the choice, and a paired comparison is unaffected because both designs see the same addresses.
- Exact balance inside blocks is a variance reduction for symmetric quantities and must not be read as independence: clustered statistics treat a block as one cluster.
- If a later schema admits n-fold analytic families, block size follows |G| with no change to the map.
