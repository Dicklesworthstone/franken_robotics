## Background

**What it is.** An analysis that says which directions of state error a sensor suite cannot see over a window of motion. For an estimator error state δx (in the invariant error coordinates the estimator uses), a window of N steps, state-transition matrices Φ_k from the window start, measurement Jacobians H_k and noise covariances R_k,

  W = Σ over k = 0, …, N − 1 of Φ_kᵀ·H_kᵀ·R_k⁻¹·H_k·Φ_k.

Its eigenvalues are information per unit error along its eigenvectors. Two forms exist. The *linearized* form (this bead) uses the Jacobians of the sensor models and of the estimator's error dynamics. The *empirical* form (BD-239) perturbs the initial state and replays the window as a batch; it needs no Jacobian. Owner: `frob-sense` for H, R and the empirical driver; the estimator supplies Φ. Verb: `sense.observability`. Atelier lists it as analysis A-17 ("observability and aliasing: can the state the task needs be reconstructed from the sensor suite at the declared rates and delays? are there two admitted worlds with the same observation history that need different actions?"), beside sensor coverage. This is construction C-20 ("observability is a Gramian modulo a gauge"); it replaces sensor placement by coverage geometry alone and calibration judged by residual size. It serves GOAL-31. Tier: Solid.

**Gauge.** With an IMU, encoders, contact switches and gravity, global position and yaw cannot be observed. The null space has dimension four, and the analysis must find exactly that, analytically and by finite perturbation (PO-SENS-09). A larger null space is a finding; a smaller one is a defect in a sensor model.

**Its limit.** The Gramian is local. Two aisles that are exact copies of each other give a full-rank Gramian in each, yet nothing distinguishes one aisle from the other (CX-205). Infinitesimal ambiguity is the Gramian's subject; finite ambiguity is the alias witness's. Therefore the analysis never reports full rank alone: it also runs a global ambiguity check and reports the alternatives that would change a decision.

**Label.** Gramian conditioning is a screening signal: it may discard under the ladder's rules and may not establish a comparison between designs.

## Scope

In: sensor Jacobians H_k and covariances R_k for the M1 sensors (IMU, encoders, contact switches, range beams of fixtures); assembly of W on the referee in double precision; scaling; the gauge and the restricted spectrum; named weak directions; the structural-rank form for `design check`; the global-ambiguity flag on declared world pairs; the reduced verb on the CPU; the registration of analysis A-17 in `frob-analyze` (step 9: the `Analysis` of N28a-04 with its finding kind, from which N28a-05 generates `analyze.observability`); the linearized rows of GATE-SENS-08.

Out: the empirical form and the agreement between forms (BD-239); the route profile and the full verb at M3 (N20a-34); placement scoring (N20a-36); the alias witness machinery proper (BD-134 and the alias beads) and FROB-REF-120, which only the alias search raises; joining stage 8 of `design check` (BD-244, M3) and the `check` policy row (N14bx-01).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs.** A design with its sensor suite and cards; a nominal motion over a window (states x_k, k = 0…N − 1, on the referee); the estimator's error-coordinate manifest (dimension n, a name and a unit per coordinate, left or right convention); Φ_k from the estimator; a declared scaling D = diag(d_i) of the error coordinates (default from the manifest: 1 m, 1 rad, 1 m/s, and each bias's card scale); the declared gauge.

**2. Jacobians.** H_k = ∂h/∂δx at x_k, with h the noiseless sensor function (the same code as the sensor model, evaluated on dual numbers), perturbing the state on the group in the estimator's convention. R_k from the cards (noise densities discretized at the sensor's period; range noise at the nominal range). A sample masked at x_k contributes no row. Sensors at different rates contribute rows only at their own sample instants.

**3. Assembly and scaling.** W as above in double precision; W̃ = D·W·D so that eigenvalues are comparable across coordinates of different units ("whitened units").

**4. Gauge and spectrum** (numbered steps).
1. Build the gauge basis G (n × g): for the proprioceptive suite, three translations of the base and yaw about the gravity direction, expressed in the error coordinates at the window start; orthonormalize in the scaled coordinates.
2. Check that the gauge is in the null space: ‖W̃·G‖ ≤ tol·‖W̃‖ (tol = 10⁻⁹ in double precision). A gauge direction that carries information means the null space is smaller than the gauge: a defect in a sensor model (for example, gravity applied twice makes yaw appear observable). Report it as such.
3. Restrict: W_⊥ = Pᵀ·W̃·P with P = I − G·Gᵀ; symmetric eigen-decomposition.
4. Report: the null dimension of W̃; the number of null directions outside the gauge (each is a finding, with its eigenvector); λ_min, the smallest eigenvalue outside the gauge (the index); its eigenvector in named coordinates, phrased from its dominant components (for example "translation along the corridor axis").

**5. Structural rank for `design check`.** The rank of the stacked observability matrix [H_0; H_1·Φ_1; …] with exact rational arithmetic at rational sample states of the nominal motion; returned with the gauge dimension subtracted. It runs without a simulation and is the part of A-17 marked for `check`.

**6. Global ambiguity flag.** For each declared candidate pair (from the scene's declared repetitions and symmetries: a translated aisle, a mirrored layout; or a fixture's explicit pair), evaluate the noiseless sensor function at corresponding states along the window in both worlds. If the readings agree within the cards' noise at every sample, report `ambiguous: locally full rank, globally aliased` with the pair, even when λ_min is large. Finding such pairs without a declaration is the alias witness's job.

**7. Hand-checkable cases.**
- Corridor: a robot at (x, y) between walls y = ±a measures a − y with variance s². H = (0, −1); standing still for N samples, W = (N/s²)·diag(0, 1); the null vector is (1, 0): position along the corridor is unobservable. A second beam at 45° reads (a − y)·√2 and adds nothing along x. A beam that reaches an end wall at x = b reads b − x and makes W full rank. With N = 100 and s = 0.01 m: W = diag(0, 10⁶) m⁻²; with the 45° beam, diag(0, 3 × 10⁶); with the end-wall beam, diag(10⁶, 10⁶).
- Global ambiguity: range anchors at (−1, 0) and (1, 0) both read √2 from (0, 1) and from (0, −1); with unit variances the information matrix is the identity at both points, yet a controller that must pick the side fails. A third anchor at (0, 2) reads 1 or 3, and closed reading intervals of half-width ε separate exactly when ε < 1.

**8. Verb (reduced form at M1).** `frob sense observability <design> --task <task> --window <N> --key <k> --json` on the referee: returns the spectrum, the gauge check, the named weakest direction, the structural rank and the ambiguity flag, with the label `screening`. The verb is of kind `run` with capability `sense:run` (its row in VERBS-sense), so every call carries an idempotency key, and two calls a test compares carry distinct keys. Spellings follow the row: `--form linearized|empirical|both`, whose only value at M1 is `linearized` (the default; `empirical` and `both` answer `FROB-REF-724`, exit 5, until BD-239); `--tier` (`referee` at M1); the scaling and gauge overrides. `frob capabilities --json` lists the verb with `form: "linearized-cpu"`. Refusals: none of its own; the row lists 440 and 441, raised by the admission of the world the window runs in, and the codes appended for a `run` verb (805 for a missing key). Schema `frob.sense.observability.out/1`; evidence block: tier `referee` or `static`, determinism `exact` for the structural rank and the double-precision class for the spectrum.

**9. Registration as analysis A-17.** The same code is registered in `frob-analyze` as the `Analysis` A-17 under the contract of N28a-04: declared reads (the sensor suite, its cards and mounts, the estimator's error-coordinate manifest, the task's nominal motion and the declared gauge), so the memo key follows the contract; output the structural rank, the restricted spectrum and the named weak directions, with the conditioning labelled `screening` and the structural rank `exact`; one finding kind, `A-17.rank-deficient` (High), naming each null direction outside the gauge with its eigenvector, which is the row N14bx-01 gives `check` (reported, never refusing). N28a-05 generates the verb `analyze.observability` (VERBS-analyze: `analyze:read`, `frob.analyze.observability.out/1`, refusals 120 and 551) from this registration; it calls this computation and adds no second implementation. As every analysis verb, it reports a finding with exit 2 and never refuses on one.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Linearized rows of GATE-SENS-08 (seed done-condition): the corridor case returns W = (N/s²)·diag(0, 1) with null vector (1, 0) named as translation along the corridor axis, unchanged by the 45° beam and made full rank by the end-wall beam; the proprioceptive suite on the reference legged robot returns a null space of dimension exactly four equal to the span of base translations and yaw.
- [ ] Two identical aisles are reported `ambiguous` despite full local rank (seed done-condition); the two-anchor fixture likewise, and the third anchor removes the flag when the reading half-width is below 1.
- [ ] A planted sensor-model defect (gravity applied twice in the IMU model) makes a gauge direction carry information and is reported as a model defect, not as improved observability.
- [ ] A planted extra unobservable direction (encoders removed from one leg) is reported as a finding with its named eigenvector.
- [ ] The structural rank agrees with the numerical rank on the fixtures and runs without a simulation within the `design check` budget.
- [ ] Results carry the label `screening`; the verb is reachable through `frob --json` with schema and evidence block; cancellation is tested; a call without `--key` is `FROB-REF-805`, exit 3, and `--form empirical` is `FROB-REF-724`, exit 5, in a build without BD-239.
- [ ] A-17 is registered under N28a-04's contract (declared reads; a field outside them read is a contract-test failure): `frob analyze observability` on the corridor fixture (no gauge declared) returns `A-17.rank-deficient` naming translation along the corridor axis, exit 2, schema-valid against `frob.analyze.observability.out/1`; with the end-wall beam it returns no finding and exit 0; a repeated call is a memo hit.
- [ ] PO-SENS-09's analytic rank computation is on file and the finite-perturbation check (on the referee, central differences in double precision) agrees with it.

Negative control: the oracle's "full local rank reported as distinguishability" row must disagree with this bead's output on the two-anchor fixture.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_observability.sh` (N20a-47) runs the corridor, anchor, aisle and gauge fixtures through `frob sense observability … --json` on the referee, asserts the spectra, null dimensions, names and flags, and logs eigenvalues with units and thresholds, the gauge residual, and the gate `GATE-SENS-08` with the note "linearized rows".

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-487 (corridor Gramian: null vector (1, 0); full rank with an end wall), KA-488 (gauge of dimension four; a smaller null space is a defect in a sensor model). CX-205 (a full-rank Gramian does not mean the robot knows where it is). Negative controls: gravity applied twice; the mutant that reports rank without the ambiguity check (must fail the aisle fixture). Metamorphic: translating or yawing the whole world leaves W unchanged (the gauge); doubling every variance halves W. Determinism: double precision on the referee, identical twice; structural rank exact.

## Considerations

- Honest limits (from the construction): full local rank does not imply distinguishability; an estimator that reports small uncertainty while drifting is a failure the Gramian does not see, and filter consistency is tested separately.
- With biases in the state the invariant error loses its state-independence; the Gramian remains a local index and nothing here claims an estimator bound.
- The Fisher information on parameters is the same object with parameters in place of states; the calibration chapter reuses the assembly.
- Uses to come: a warning in the design check where a route has a stretch whose smallest eigenvalue falls below a threshold; an input to the assumption monitors of runtime shields; a placement score (Frontier). Each is another bead.
