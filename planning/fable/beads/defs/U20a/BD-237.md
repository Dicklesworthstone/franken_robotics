## Background

Depth cameras, spinning and solid-state lidar patterns, height scans around the base, and semantic and instance labels are all ray queries: sphere tracing against the scene distance field, exact tests against overlays, and analytic ray tests against the moving primitives (the ray-kernel seed bead, BD-187). What a ray *reports* at its first hit depends on what it hit. A glass door, a mirror, a wet floor, a black absorber, a kettle's steam and a sunlit tile are where real depth sensors fail, and they are common in the homes, kitchens and warehouses the scene families describe. A simulator whose every surface is diffuse trains policies that have never seen a phantom or a hole.

This bead is the capstone of the optical failure modes: it owns the dispatch by optical class, the simple classes, the cost rule, and the gate. The specular classes, volumes, pattern-projecting cameras and the correlated-failure latent are its child beads. It serves GOAL-19 (sensors with the ways real ones fail). Tier: Solid. Decisions in force: ADR-SENS-06 (sensors read the collision field, the overlays and the optical map, never the visual mesh); ADR-SENS-07 (a missing measurement is a masked value with a reason); ADR-SENS-08 (a failure with a physical cause is a function of the ray-and-scene interaction and of a latent drawn per surface region and episode, not an independent coin per sample).

DESIGN: one bounce at most, so the launch shape is fixed and the cost of a ray on a specular class is at most doubled. The reflectance formula uses only multiplication, so no elementary function enters the kernel.

## Scope

In: the class dispatch; the rules for `diffuse`, `absorber`, `retroreflector` (gain only), `emitter` and unknown regions; return strength and the `weak` mask; the cost accounting; the GATE-SENS-07 battery and its record.

Out (child beads): `glass`, `mirror`, `specular-floor` (N20a-21); optical volumes (N20a-22); pattern-projecting depth cameras (N20a-23); the per-region latent (N20a-24); range, grazing and blind-zone masks (N20a-20). Out (elsewhere): the ray kernels themselves (BD-187); retroreflector saturation and bloom, and volume backscatter, which are Frontier (N20a-30); sensor cards and their gate (BD-238).

## Technical approach

**1. The table that governs every ray.**

| Optical class | Ray rule |
|---|---|
| `diffuse` | A return at the hit range, with strength from albedo, incidence and range; masked `weak` below the detection threshold |
| `absorber` | No return; masked `absorbed` |
| `glass` | Reflectance by the polynomial R(θ) = r₀ + (1 − r₀)·(1 − cos θ)⁵. A direct return only within the card's acceptance angle of normal incidence; otherwise the ray continues through the pane to whatever lies behind, or follows the mirror direction, whichever return is stronger. A sensor with several echoes reports both |
| `mirror` | The ray follows the mirror direction for one bounce. The reported point lies on the original ray at the unfolded path length, which is the mirror image of the true surface: a phantom behind the mirror |
| `specular-floor` | As `mirror` with weight R(θ), as `diffuse` otherwise; R rises with the wetness latent. Phantoms appear below the floor at grazing angles |
| `retroreflector` | A return with gain; saturation and bloom are Frontier |
| `emitter` | No range; raises the ambient level seen by pattern-projecting depth cameras |
| Unknown region | `invalid: unknown-region` |

**2. Dispatch** (kernel `K-ray-optical`, after the first-hit kernel returns distance d, unit normal n, region identifier and whether the hit is an overlay or a moving primitive). The region's card class selects the branch by a table lookup, not by data-dependent launches: every ray executes the same sequence (first hit; an optional second segment whose length is zero for non-specular classes; the report stage), so the launch shape is fixed. Moving primitives (robot links, movable objects) carry an optical card too; a link without one takes the design's default card, which must be declared.

**3. Return strength** (DESIGN; multiplications and one division). With ρ the albedo in the sensor's band (`albedo_ir` for active infrared sensors, `albedo_vis` for visible), θ the incidence angle (cos θ = |u·n| for ray direction u) and d_ref the card's reference range,

  S = ρ · cos θ · (d_ref/d)²,

normalized so that S = 1 for a white surface at normal incidence at the reference range. The sensor card's `detection_threshold` S_min decides: S < S_min gives mask `weak`.

**4. The simple classes.** `absorber`: mask `absorbed`, regardless of range. `retroreflector`: S = `retro_gain` · (d_ref/d)², with no cos θ factor (the return goes back along the ray); values above the sensor's saturation level are reported at the hit range with the flag `saturated` (bloom into neighbouring samples is Frontier). `emitter`: active ranging gets no return (mask `no-return`); the region contributes its `emission` to the ambient irradiance field that pattern-projecting cameras read. Unknown region: mask `invalid: unknown-region`.

**5. Order of the report stage** (one order for every ray sensor, so reasons are reproducible): no hit → `no-return`; unknown region; class rule (may redirect or mask); optical volumes along every segment (attenuation); strength against threshold (`weak` or `attenuated`, by which factor took it below threshold); range limits and blind zone; grazing; noise; quantization.

**6. Cost.** A specular class adds at most one extra segment per ray; the receipt reports the fraction of rays that took a second segment and the ray-stage time with and without optical rules.

**7. GATE-SENS-07** (known answer, M3): the mirror, glass, steam and pattern-dropout cases; every masked sample carries a reason; repeated reads through one pane fail together.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SENS-07 recorded with measured values (seed done-condition): the mirror phantom at (5, 2, 0) for a target at (1, 2, 0) and a mirror plane x = 3; the glass pane's direct return near normal incidence and the surface behind otherwise; the steam slab losing every return beyond 0.75 m at σ = 2 m⁻¹ and a 5% threshold; each pattern-dropout cause with its own reason; a reason on every masked sample of every fixture; repeated reads through one pane failing together.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: `glass` treated as `diffuse`; one-way attenuation for an active sensor; independent coins through one pane; a kernel that writes a range for an `absorber` hit.
- [ ] Diffuse strength: a white wall at normal incidence at d_ref reads S = 1; at twice the range, 1/4; at 60° incidence, 1/2; with S_min = 0.05 a surface of albedo 0.1 at 60° at 1.5·d_ref (S = 0.0222) is masked `weak`.
- [ ] An `absorber` panel yields `absorbed` on every ray that hits it and a valid return beside it; a region marked unknown yields `invalid: unknown-region`.
- [ ] Changing a region's optical card leaves every physics digest unchanged in a run whose controller does not consume exteroception (the scene chapter's separation of optics from contact).
- [ ] The launch shape is identical for scenes with and without specular regions; the measured added ray-stage cost is recorded.
- [ ] Outputs agree across backends inside their determinism class, with reason codes byte-identical on the fixtures.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/optical_classes.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense optical_classes`.
- `diffuse_strength_values`: 1, 1/4, 1/2 and 0.0222 for the four cases above.
- `weak_mask_below_threshold`.
- `absorber_masked_absorbed`, `unknown_region_masked`.
- `retroreflector_gain_and_flag`: gain 50 at 2·d_ref gives S = 12.5 and `saturated` when the sensor's saturation level is 10.
- `emitter_no_range`.
- `dispatch_fixed_shape`: the kernel's launch signature is equal for an all-diffuse scene and a scene with mirrors.
- `reason_on_every_masked_sample` (property over the optical fixtures): mask code non-zero ⇔ value hidden.
- `report_stage_order`: a ray that is both beyond range and `weak` reports the reason the order assigns (`weak`).
- `moving_primitive_card`: a robot link with an `absorber` card masks the rays that hit it.
- `optics_do_not_touch_physics`: digests equal after an optical card edit.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh` (N20a-46) runs the gate battery through `frob sim run` on the mirror, glass, steam, pattern and absorber fixtures, reads samples and masks through `frob telemetry query` and `frob telemetry inspect-pixel`, asserts the values and reasons, and logs measured values with units, the reason histogram per fixture and the gate `GATE-SENS-07`.

CX-203, CX-204. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across batch sizes; across backends inside the class; reason codes exact.

## Considerations

- OPEN-SENS-5 (owner: Sensorium lead; decided when a measured card for one depth camera and one lidar exists): whether glass reports the stronger return or draws between returns. Fail-closed default: the stronger return, deterministic.
- OPEN-SENS-4: every optical parameter is `assumed` until a card records a datasheet or a measurement; a Reality claim that rests on such a field is refused (`FROB-REF-447`).
- A region without an optical card is a scene compile refusal, not a silent `diffuse`; this bead never invents a class.
- The one-bounce limit under-models mirror corridors and glass behind glass; the second specular hit is reported `no-return`, and the contract lists multi-bounce as a known gap.
- Privacy: masks and phantoms of a private scene are private; logs carry counts and fixture values only.
