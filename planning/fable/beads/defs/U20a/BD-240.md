## Background

Alias witnesses come from four sources.

| Source | Method | Tier | Class of the result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structural | A hidden parameter has no information path to any delivered packet before t*: no ray can intersect geometry that depends on it, and no contact with it can occur before a computed earliest time | Solid | `Certified` relative to the model, for every policy |
| Symmetry and repetition | Candidate world pairs from the scene's own symmetries and repeated modules under which the sensor suite is invariant and the goal is not | Solid to propose; checked | As checked |
| Enumerated | On a finite abstraction (a lattice of actions and readings in exact arithmetic), exhaust every causal policy tree | Solid for small models | `Certified` for the abstraction |
| Searched | The adversarial search looks over world pairs for minimal transcript distance subject to disjoint success sets | Frontier | `Tested`, and labeled experiment-specific unless one of the rows above also applies |

The first three exist as separate beads (N20a-38, N20a-39, BD-134). This seed bead adds the fourth, the search over a mission's admitted world family, and measures the capability's gate over all four. The search matters where an alias has no tidy explanation: two generated homes that happen to look the same from the hallway, a fixture state that no symmetry relates. It is Frontier: it runs only after its pilot passed (N20a-42) and KILL-ALIAS did not fire (N20a-43); its fallback is the three Solid sources plus pairs declared by a mission author. It serves GOAL-31.

Ceiling of the benefit: the witness saves the training compute that would have been spent on missions unsolvable under their interface; the share is measured on the M3 mission corpus by the pilot. A witness ends a futile policy search; it does not speed a search that can succeed.

## Scope

In: the transcript-distance objective; candidate generation over world families; the disjointness test; acceptance through the checker and the refuter; labeling; the GATE-SENS-09 battery over all lanes; recording the KILL-ALIAS decision.

Out: the Solid lanes; the witness type and checker; the verb surface and refusal 445 (N20a-41); the pilot and the decision.

## Technical approach

**1. Objective.** For worlds w₁, w₂ of the mission's family, a behaviour set B (the nominal action sequence and the task's finite probe family) and commitment time t*:

  d(w₁, w₂) = max over b in B of [ number of delivered packets up to t* whose bytes differ, then, as a tie-break for guidance, the largest payload difference normalized by the card's noise ].

A pair is a candidate when the packet count is zero for every b in B. The normalized difference gives the search a slope toward zero; it never certifies anything.

**2. Search** (numbered steps).
1. Seed pairs: the outputs of the symmetry lane's pre-check that were dropped by a small margin; random pairs from the family; pairs differing in one hidden parameter.
2. Propose edits within the legality set (the same legal-edit vocabulary the adversarial search uses: fixture positions and states, region cards, module choices of the interior grammar, latencies within their bounds), scored by d and constrained to keep the success sets different.
3. Evaluate d by batched replay of both worlds under B (world pairs × behaviours as one batch on a throughput tier), then confirm zero-distance pairs on the referee.
4. Test disjointness of S₁ and S₂ at t*: by enumeration over the task's action lattice where it is finite, by interval enclosures of the success predicate otherwise; a pair whose disjointness cannot be established is reported `unresolved`, never as a witness.
5. Budgeted: the search returns its best pairs and what it covered when the budget ends.

**3. Acceptance.** A zero-distance, disjoint pair goes to the independent checker (replay of both worlds on the referee under each behaviour of B, byte comparison of every delivered packet up to t*). Then the refuter: an attempt to exhibit a policy that satisfies the mission in both worlds (the probe family extended beyond B, and seeded random policies). Only a pair that passes the checker and survives the refuter is accepted.

**4. Labeling.** Source `Searched`; class `Tested`; scope "the probe set B", stated in the witness and in every message ("no policy among the tested probes separates these worlds; this is not a bound over all policies"). If the structural lane certifies the same pair, or it is a symmetry pair, or the mission's abstraction is exhausted, the witness is upgraded to that lane's class and scope.

**5. GATE-SENS-09** (known answer; M1 for the docking rows, M3 complete): the T-junction and the late-packet docking witnesses are found and pass the checker; on 64 planted layouts every planted alias is found and no false witness is reported.

The battery: (a) the simulated T-junction with a planar lidar on a compiled scene (found by the structural and symmetry lanes; checked; bound 1/2; with a 29 m budget, none found); (b) the late-packet docking witness (enumerated lane; BD-134), and the on-time control; (c) the 64-layout suite of the pilot, re-run through the production verb with all lanes: 32 planted found, none on the 32 clean, zero false witnesses under the refuter.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SENS-09 recorded with measured values (seed done-condition): T-junction witness found and checked; late-packet docking witness found and checked; 32 of 32 planted aliases found; 0 witnesses on the 32 clean layouts; 0 false witnesses.
- [ ] KILL-ALIAS is decided and its ADR is cited (seed done-condition).
- [ ] Every searched witness carries class `Tested` and its probe scope; none is reported as a bound over all policies unless upgraded by another lane, and an upgrade names the lane.
- [ ] The refuter runs on every accepted searched witness; a planted probe-incomplete case (two worlds identical under B and separated by a longer probe within the task's family) is not accepted.
- [ ] A pair with zero transcript distance and overlapping success sets is not reported; a pair whose disjointness cannot be established is reported `unresolved`.
- [ ] On the fallback branch the same battery is recorded for the Solid lanes alone, with the count of planted aliases they reach, and the capability is described as "alias witnesses: structural, enumerated and declared".
- [ ] Zero-distance decisions made on a throughput tier are confirmed on the referee before acceptance; a tier disagreement is reported, not resolved silently.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/alias_search.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense --features frontier-alias-search alias_search`.
- `distance_zero_iff_packets_equal`.
- `search_finds_planted_fixture_state`: a hidden fixture state behind an opaque door is found within the default budget on three seeds.
- `overlapping_success_sets_not_reported`.
- `unresolved_when_disjointness_unknown`.
- `probe_incomplete_rejected_by_refuter`.
- `label_tested_and_scope`; `upgrade_by_structural_lane`.
- `referee_confirmation_required`: a pair equal on a single-precision tier and different on the referee is dropped with a tier-disagreement note.
- `budget_stop_reports_coverage`.
- `legal_edits_only`: no proposal leaves the legality set.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_alias.sh` (N20a-48) runs the gate battery through `frob sense alias` (all lanes), `frob-verify` on exported certificates, and `frob gauntlet run --gate GATE-SENS-09`; it logs per fixture the lane, class, scope, bound and checker result, the three suite totals against 32, 0 and 0, the refuter's attempts, and the gates `GATE-SENS-09` and `KILL-ALIAS`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-489 (T-junction; with a 29 m budget the witness is rejected), KA-492 (planted suite), KA-502 (late-packet docking). CX-206, CX-208. Negative controls: the 29 m budget; the on-time docking pair; the probe-incomplete pair; a search objective that ignores arrival instants (it would call the late and on-time docking worlds equal: caught by the checker's byte comparison of instants). Determinism: seeded search; the same seeds give the same pairs on one tier; accepted witnesses replay bit for bit on the referee.

## Considerations

- KILL-ALIAS (trigger: one false witness from the searched lane; fallback: structural, enumerated and declared witnesses only; M3) stays armed after the decision; a user's override run that succeeds in both worlds of a searched witness is a false witness and fires it.
- The legality set bounds what worlds the search may propose; a pair outside the mission's admitted family is not a witness about the mission.
- The search's cost is batched replays; it should stay well below the training compute it could save, and the response reports its own cost beside the measured ceiling.
- Privacy: witnesses contain world definitions and inherit their privacy class.
