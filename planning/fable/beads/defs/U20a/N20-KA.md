## Background

A known answer is an analytic or hand-checkable truth with a value, a tolerance, an execution tier and, where one exists, a negative control: a mutation or planted failure that must make its test fail (the rule: a gate that nothing can fail validates nothing). The known-answer suite is tier G1 of the verification program (G0 for laws and exact identities, G3 for metamorphic relations, G4 for chaos, G5 for determinism). A capability counts as implemented only when its known-answer tests pass against a pinned oracle that was never regenerated from the candidate.

This bead is the registry of the sixty known answers of Sensorium (the subsystem that owns sensors, clocks, packet queues, images and the sensing analyses; crate `frob-sense`, layer 3). It exists so that no enumerable truth of the chapter is lost: every mechanism bead of Sensorium cites rows from here and also states inline the values it needs. Numbers are append-only: a row is never renumbered and a number is never reused (INV-GAUNT-07).

Labels. Fixture constants are DESIGN choices. A truth derived from them by hand is DESIGN arithmetic with a derivation file under `docs/derivations/`. A truth that quotes a planning pilot or a sibling's source is FACT, to be re-run from its primary source before a gate cites it (KA-504). A threshold inside a row is the TARGET of the gate named beside it. Nothing here has been measured on FrankenRobotics code.

## Scope

In: the sixty registry records below; the completeness meta-test; tracking of the 18 tolerances marked `to set` and the 35 rows with no stated control (plan-time counts for §20), which are obligations, not tests.
Out: the tests themselves (owner column); the counterexample registry `N20-CX` and the refusal registry `N20-REF` (both written by the unit that owns §20.13 to §20.25).

## Technical approach

**Record form** (one TOML table per row in `frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml`): `id`, `where_stated`, `case`, `truth`, `tolerance`, `tier` (G0 to G5), `site`, `negative_control`, `shared_fixture` (the "≡" mark), `test` (path and test name), `owner_bead`, and `status` per site (`not run`, `pass`, `fail`). Sites: `host` (exact or double-precision arithmetic in a test), `referee`, `strict` (`batch-cpu` under the strict profile), `lockstep` (throughput tiers), `cuda`, `metal`, `all tiers`.

**Rules that bind the rows.** (1) A row runs first on the weakest site that can decide it, then on every tier its gate names. (2) A G3 row is a relation with an exact expected equality; G4 and G5 rows have truths of the form "zero violations" or "equal digests". (3) A row printed with `to set` or `none stated` cannot support a gate until the missing part is registered (GATE-GAUNT-02); the owner bead proposes the tolerance or the control and the registry records who set it and when. (4) Values are carried as printed and are not improved in transit. (5) Tolerance `exact` applies to integers, rationals, Booleans, verdicts, labels and digests.

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/registry_ka_sens.rs`, `cargo test -p frob-conformance registry_ka_sens`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/registry_ka_sens.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/registry_ka_sens/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it): runs `frob gauntlet list --chapter 20 --json` and asserts 60 rows; `frob gauntlet status --chapter 20 --json` and logs per-row status as `metric` lines (`ka`, `site`, `status`); asserts no row cited by a recorded gate is `to set`. Each line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block; the summary line gives assertion counts and `gates: []`.

### Known answers and negative controls
The registry is itself the list.
