## Background

An inertial measurement unit reports specific force and angular rate in its own frame. The ideal reading is kinematics; the instrument adds scale error, axis misalignment, a bias that wanders, white noise, a finite range and a sample rate, and its packets arrive late. An estimator's drift, and whether a policy survives transfer, are set by those additions.

The kinematics (carried by the referee-tier proprioception bead BD-099, restated here because every check depends on it): for a sensor rigidly mounted at offset r from a body point O with angular velocity ω and angular acceleration α, the point acceleration is

  a_P = a_O + α × r + ω × (ω × r),

and the reading is f = R_WSᵀ·(a_P − g), with R_WS the sensor's orientation in the world and g the gravitational acceleration vector. FACT (sibling code): `fs-mbd` exposes `prepare_point_motion`, `point_motion` and `specific_acceleration_world` for rigid and articulated bodies; Sensorium uses them on the referee and derives the batched kernel from the same expression. First checks: at rest the reading is (0, 0, +g) with zero rate; in free fall without rotation it is zero; in free fall spinning at 5 rad/s with a 0.10 m perpendicular offset it is 5² × 0.10 = 2.5 m/s² toward the axis; gravity enters exactly once for prescribed and for floating bases.

From the failure-mode table: "IMU: bias random walk, scale, misalignment; model: correlated noise process in the checkpoint; first check: Allan-deviation slope of the simulated stream"; "IMU: saturation on impact; model: clipping at the card range, flag `saturated`; first check: a drop test that exceeds range"; "IMU: mount offset; model: the lever-arm terms; first check: the spinning free-fall case". Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: card fields; the ordered measurement chain; discretization of noise densities; the bias process with checkpointed state; saturation and its flag; quantization; the Allan-deviation check and the drop test.

Out: the lever-arm kinematics and the at-rest, free-fall and turntable cases (BD-099 on the referee, BD-187 on batched tiers); the impact jump term (sampled-readings bead); the estimator.

## Technical approach

**1. Card fields** (kind `sensor.imu`, per-field provenance), for accelerometer and gyroscope separately: `range` (± value), `noise_density` N (m/s²/√Hz or rad/s/√Hz), `bias_walk` K (m/s³/√Hz or rad/s²/√Hz), `bias_initial_sigma`, `scale_error` (three values), `misalignment` (three small angles per triad), `lsb` (quantization step), `sample_rate` (a rational in the clock lattice), exposure (zero for an instantaneous device, or an integration window), and the link reference for latency.

**2. The chain, in this order** (per sample of period Δt).
1. Ideal x: specific force f and rate ω in the sensor frame from the kinematics above, at the sample instant (or averaged over the exposure window where the card declares one).
2. Misalignment and scale: y = (I + diag(s))·M·x, with M the small-angle misalignment matrix (identity plus the skew pattern of the card's angles).
3. Bias: y ← y + b_k, with b_0 drawn by key with standard deviation `bias_initial_sigma` once per episode and b_{k+1} = b_k + K·√Δt·n_k (the correlated process of N20a-04; state in the checkpoint).
4. White noise: y ← y + (N/√Δt)·n′_k (the discretization of a continuous white density N at sample period Δt), keyed by (sensor, sample index, component).
5. Saturation: each component clipped to ±`range`; if any clips, the packet carries the flag `saturated`. The value stays a number: a clipped reading is what the instrument reports.
6. Quantization: round to the nearest multiple of `lsb`.
7. Stamp and enqueue with the device timestamp and the link's latency.

The order is part of the model: saturating before adding bias, or quantizing before clipping, gives different numbers, and the card states the order (DESIGN: the order above).

**3. Constants.** √Δt and 1/√Δt are computed once at admission from the rational period with the deterministic square root; the per-sample work is multiply and add.

**4. Allan-deviation check.** For a stream at rest: white noise alone gives σ_A(τ) = N/√τ (slope −1/2 on log-log axes); the bias walk alone gives σ_A(τ) = K·√(τ/3) (slope +1/2); together the curve has its minimum near τ = √3·N/K. The test computes the overlapping Allan deviation of a simulated stream and fits both branches.

**5. Drop test.** A body falls onto a stiff floor so that the true deceleration exceeds `range`; the reading must equal ±`range` during the overload with `saturated` set, and the flag must clear afterwards.

**6. Kernel.** `K-sense-imu`: float class, CPU reference; bias state slab in the checkpoint.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Allan check (GATE-SENS-14 row; tolerance proposed, since the registry has none: fitted slopes within ±0.05 of −1/2 and +1/2, and fitted N and K within 10% of the card): on a 2-hour simulated stream at 200 Hz with N = 2 × 10⁻³ m/s²/√Hz and K = 4 × 10⁻⁵ m/s³/√Hz.
- [ ] Drop test (exact): with `range` = 16 g and a true peak above it, every overloaded sample reads exactly 16 g × 9.81 m/s² in magnitude after quantization and carries `saturated`; no sample outside the overload carries it.
- [ ] Scale and misalignment: with a scale error of 1% on z and no other error, the at-rest reading is 1.01·g on z; with a 10 mrad misalignment about y, the at-rest x reading is g·0.010 in magnitude (first order).
- [ ] The chain order is fixed: the two swapped-order mutants (bias after saturation; quantize before clip) produce different outputs on a fixture designed to separate them and are caught.
- [ ] The bias state survives checkpoint and restore byte for byte; removing it from the checkpoint is caught.
- [ ] Noise and bias are keyed: adding a second IMU leaves the first one's stream unchanged.
- [ ] Every card field reports provenance.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/imu_chain.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense imu_chain`.
- `allan_white_slope`: K = 0; slope −0.5 ± 0.05; σ_A(1 s) = N within 10%.
- `allan_walk_slope`: N = 0; slope +0.5 ± 0.05; σ_A(τ) = K·√(τ/3) within 10% at τ = 100 s.
- `allan_minimum_location`: minimum within a factor 1.5 of √3·N/K = 86.6 s for the card above.
- `saturation_exact_and_flag`, `flag_clears_after_overload`.
- `scale_error_one_percent`, `misalignment_ten_mrad`.
- `order_bias_before_saturation`: true 15.9 g with bias +0.2 g and range 16 g reads 16 g and is flagged; the swapped mutant reads 16.1 g unflagged.
- `quantization_lsb`: outputs are integer multiples of `lsb`.
- `noise_discretization`: sample standard deviation of 10⁶ samples equals N/√Δt within 1%.
- `bias_checkpoint_roundtrip`, `noise_keyed_independent_of_other_sensors`.

### End-to-end script and logging
Exercised by `scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh` (N20a-46, the sensor-card half): `frob sim run` on the at-rest long-stream fixture and the drop fixture; `frob telemetry query` exports the stream; the script computes the Allan curve with the conformance tool (`frob gauntlet run --ka KA-512`), asserts slopes and fitted densities against the proposed tolerances, asserts the clipped value and flag counts, and logs the fitted N, K, slopes and thresholds.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-512 (Allan-deviation slope matches the declared process; tolerance to set, proposed above; no control stated: proposed control, a bias drawn fresh each sample instead of accumulated, which shows no +1/2 branch), KA-513 (clips at the card range with `saturated`; proposed control: a model that passes the true value through). KA-466 to KA-469 (the kinematic cases; control: gravity applied twice) are measured by BD-099 and BD-187 and are re-run here through the full chain with every error term set to zero, which must leave them unchanged. Determinism: float class of the physics; integer flags exact.

## Considerations

- Temperature dependence of bias, vibration rectification and g-sensitivity of gyroscopes are not modeled; they are named as known gaps in the contract.
- The discretization N/√Δt assumes the device's own anti-alias filter passes the full sample band; a card may instead give an RMS noise at its output rate, and the card schema accepts either with a declared convention.
- A 2-hour stream at 200 Hz is 1.44 million samples of one world: seconds of compute. The Allan test belongs to the standard battery's sensor group, not to every test run.
- Values are `assumed` until a datasheet or measurement is recorded; a Reality claim on an assumed field is refused (`FROB-REF-447`).
