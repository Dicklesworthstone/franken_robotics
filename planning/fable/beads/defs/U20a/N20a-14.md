## Background

An inertial measurement unit reports specific force and angular rate in its own frame. The ideal reading is kinematics; the instrument adds scale error, axis misalignment, a bias that wanders, white noise, a finite range and a sample rate, and its packets arrive late. An estimator's drift, and whether a policy survives transfer, are set by those additions.

The kinematics (carried by the referee-tier proprioception bead BD-099, restated here because every check depends on it): for a sensor rigidly mounted at offset r from a body point O with angular velocity ω and angular acceleration α, the point acceleration is

  a_P = a_O + α × r + ω × (ω × r),

and the reading is f = R_WSᵀ·(a_P − g), with R_WS the sensor's orientation in the world and g the gravitational acceleration vector. FACT (sibling code): `fs-mbd` exposes `prepare_point_motion`, `point_motion` and `specific_acceleration_world` for rigid and articulated bodies; Sensorium uses them on the referee and derives the batched kernel from the same expression. First checks: at rest the reading is (0, 0, +g) with zero rate; in free fall without rotation it is zero; in free fall spinning at 5 rad/s with a 0.10 m perpendicular offset it is 5² × 0.10 = 2.5 m/s² toward the axis; gravity enters exactly once for prescribed and for floating bases.

From the failure-mode table: "IMU: bias random walk, scale, misalignment; model: correlated noise process in the checkpoint; first check: Allan-deviation slope of the simulated stream"; "IMU: saturation on impact; model: clipping at the card range, flag `saturated`; first check: a drop test that exceeds range"; "IMU: mount offset; model: the lever-arm terms; first check: the spinning free-fall case". Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the IMU card fields beyond the first form of BD-099 (`bias_initial_sigma`, the exposure window, `lsb`, the small-angle parameterization of misalignment); the admission-time constants of the discretized noise; the initial bias draw; the first checks of the IMU rows of the failure-mode table (the Allan-deviation check, the drop test, the scale and misalignment checks, the swapped-order mutants) on the referee and, through the kernel of N20b-44, on batched tiers.

## Technical approach

**1. Card fields** (kind `sensor.imu`, per-field provenance), for accelerometer and gyroscope separately: `range` (± value), `noise_density` N (m/s²/√Hz or rad/s/√Hz), `bias_walk` K (m/s³/√Hz or rad/s²/√Hz), `bias_initial_sigma`, `scale_error` (three values), `misalignment` (three small angles per triad), `lsb` (quantization step), `sample_rate` (a rational in the clock lattice), exposure (zero for an instantaneous device, or an integration window), and the link reference for latency.

**2. The chain, in this order** (per sample of period Δt).
1. Ideal x: specific force f and rate ω in the sensor frame from the kinematics above, at the sample instant (or averaged over the exposure window where the card declares one).
2. Misalignment and scale: y = M·(I + diag(s))·x, with s the per-axis scale errors and M the small-angle misalignment matrix (identity plus the skew pattern of the card's angles). Scale is applied first and misalignment second; this is the order fixed by BD-099 and implemented by `K-sense-imu` (N20b-44).
3. Bias: y ← y + b_k, with b_0 drawn by key with standard deviation `bias_initial_sigma` once per episode and b_{k+1} = b_k + K·√Δt·n_k (the correlated process of N20a-04; state in the checkpoint).
4. White noise: y ← y + (N/√Δt)·n′_k (the discretization of a continuous white density N at sample period Δt), keyed by (sensor, sample index, component).
5. Saturation: each component clipped to ±`range`; if any clips, the packet carries the flag `saturated`. The value stays a number: a clipped reading is what the instrument reports.
6. Quantization: round to the nearest multiple of `lsb`.
7. Stamp and enqueue with the device timestamp and the link's latency.

The order is part of the model: saturating before adding bias, or quantizing before clipping, gives different numbers, and the card states the order (DESIGN: the order above).

**3. Constants.** √Δt and 1/√Δt are computed once at admission from the rational period with the deterministic square root; the per-sample work is multiply and add.

**4. Allan-deviation check.** For a stream at rest: white noise alone gives σ_A(τ) = N/√τ (slope −1/2 on log-log axes); the bias walk alone gives σ_A(τ) = K·√(τ/3) (slope +1/2); together σ²_A(τ) = N²/τ + K²·τ/3, whose minimum is at τ = √3·N/K. The test computes the overlapping Allan variance from the cumulative sum θ of the stream, σ²_A(τ) = Σ_k (θ_{k+2m} − 2θ_{k+m} + θ_k)² / (2τ²·(L − 2m)) with τ = m·Δt, on a grid of τ from 0.01 s to T/5. Two statistical facts shape the check (DESIGN). The branch slopes are measured on separated streams (white only; walk only), where each branch spans four decades with many independent clusters. The combined stream is judged by fitting the two-term model (weighted least squares on σ²_A with relative weights, linear in N² and K²/3) to the variance averaged over 64 worlds at distinct world addresses: a single 2-hour stream has too few independent clusters above the 87 s minimum, and in a simulation of this fixture the +1/2 slope fitted over [500, 1,440] s on one world ranged from 0.35 to 0.92 and K read at 1,000 s from 0.74 to 1.85 of the card, while the 64-world fit gave N within 0.2% and K within 4%. The 64 worlds are one batch, which is how every tier runs anyway.

**5. Drop test.** A body falls onto a stiff floor so that the true deceleration exceeds `range`; the reading must equal ±`range` during the overload with `saturated` set, and the flag must clear afterwards.

**6. Kernel.** `K-sense-imu` (float class, CPU reference; bias state slab in the checkpoint) is N20b-44's. This bead adds to it the initial bias draw, exposure averaging where the card declares a window, and the quantization rounding, and runs the first checks through it on every batched tier.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Allan check (GATE-SENS-14 row; tolerances set by this bead at three or more times the estimator's own sampling spread on this fixture, and carried by the KA-512 row of N20-KA marked "(set: N20a-14)"): with N = 2 × 10⁻³ m/s²/√Hz and K = 4 × 10⁻⁵ m/s³/√Hz at 200 Hz, (a) on one 2-hour white-only stream the slope over τ ∈ [0.01, 100] s is −1/2 ± 0.02 and σ_A(1 s) = N within 2%; (b) on one 2-hour walk-only stream the slope over the same range is +1/2 ± 0.05 and σ_A(1 s) = K/√3 within 5%; (c) on 64 worlds of a 2-hour combined stream, the two-term fit gives N within 2% and K within 10% of the card, and the fitted minimum √3·N/K within 15% of 86.6 s.
- [ ] Drop test (exact): with `range` = 16 g and `lsb` = `range`/2¹⁵ (a power-of-two ratio, so the clipped value quantizes to itself in binary floating point) and a true peak above the range, every overloaded sample reads exactly 16 × 9.81 = 156.96 m/s² in magnitude after quantization and carries `saturated`; no sample outside the overload carries it.
- [ ] Scale and misalignment: with a scale error of 1% on z and no other error, the at-rest reading is 1.01·g on z; with a 10 mrad misalignment about y, the at-rest x reading is g·0.010 in magnitude (first order).
- [ ] The chain order is fixed: the two swapped-order mutants (bias after saturation; quantize before clip) produce different outputs on a fixture designed to separate them and are caught.
- [ ] The bias state survives checkpoint and restore byte for byte; removing it from the checkpoint is caught.
- [ ] Noise and bias are keyed: adding a second IMU leaves the first one's stream unchanged.
- [ ] Every card field reports provenance.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/imu_chain.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense imu_chain`.
- `allan_white_slope`: K = 0, one 2-hour stream; slope over τ ∈ [0.01, 100] s is −0.5 ± 0.02; σ_A(1 s) = N within 2% (simulated spread of this fixture: ±0.01 and ±0.6%).
- `allan_walk_slope`: N = 0, one 2-hour stream; slope over τ ∈ [0.01, 100] s is +0.5 ± 0.05; σ_A(1 s) = K·√(1/3) within 5% (simulated spread: 0.48 to 0.51 and ±2%). Not read at τ = 100 s on one stream, where one world's value ranged from 0.82 to 1.15 of the model.
- `allan_two_term_fit_64_worlds`: the combined card on 64 worlds; fitted N within 2%, K within 10%.
- `allan_minimum_location`: the fitted minimum √3·N/K within 15% of 86.6 s for the card above.
- `fresh_bias_mutant_no_walk_branch` (negative control of KA-512): a bias drawn afresh each sample instead of accumulated gives a walk-only slope near −1/2 and fails `allan_walk_slope`.
- `saturation_exact_and_flag`, `flag_clears_after_overload`.
- `scale_error_one_percent`, `misalignment_ten_mrad`.
- `order_bias_before_saturation`: true 15.9 g with bias +0.2 g and range 16 g reads 16 g and is flagged; the swapped mutant reads 16.1 g unflagged.
- `quantization_lsb`: outputs are integer multiples of `lsb`.
- `noise_discretization`: sample standard deviation of 10⁶ samples equals N/√Δt within 1%.
- `bias_checkpoint_roundtrip`, `noise_keyed_independent_of_other_sensors`.
- `initial_bias_draw`: over 10⁴ episodes the sample standard deviation of b₀ equals `bias_initial_sigma` within 3% (the estimator's relative spread is about 1/√(2·10⁴) = 0.7%); one world address gives one b₀ on every re-run, and b₀ is drawn once per episode, not per sample.
- `exposure_window_average`: with a window equal to the period of a sinusoidal rate the reading is zero within rounding; a constant rate reads unchanged; with no window declared the reading is the instantaneous value.
- `card_fields_provenance`: every field of section 1 on a loaded card reports its provenance; a card with a field lacking it is refused at load.

### End-to-end script and logging
Exercised by `scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh` (N20a-46, the sensor-card half, stage 3): `frob sim run` on the at-rest long-stream fixture (64 worlds in one batch) and the drop fixture, each call with its own `--key` (a run verb); `frob telemetry query` exports the streams; the Allan curve is computed by the known-answer row in its cargo form, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- KA-512` (the Gauntlet has no `frob` command or verb), and KA-513 likewise with `-- KA-513`; the script asserts slopes and fitted densities against the registered tolerances, asserts the clipped value and flag counts, and logs the fitted N, K, slopes and thresholds. The negative controls (`fresh_bias_mutant_no_walk_branch`, the pass-through clip) run with `--features gauntlet-mutations`.

## Considerations

- Temperature dependence of bias, vibration rectification and g-sensitivity of gyroscopes are not modeled; they are named as known gaps in the contract.
- The discretization N/√Δt assumes the device's own anti-alias filter passes the full sample band; a card may instead give an RMS noise at its output rate, and the card schema accepts either with a declared convention.
- A 2-hour stream at 200 Hz is 1.44 million samples of one world, and the 64-world combined fixture is 92 million samples of the chain alone (no physics beyond a body at rest): seconds on a batched tier, about a minute on the referee. The Allan test belongs to the standard battery's sensor group, not to every test run; the two single-stream unit tests run in `cargo test`.
- Values are `assumed` until a datasheet or measurement is recorded; a Reality claim on an assumed field is refused (`FROB-REF-447`).
