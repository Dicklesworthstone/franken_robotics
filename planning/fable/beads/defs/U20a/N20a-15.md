## Background

A controller and an estimator do not see a motor's current, a winding's temperature or a battery's voltage; they see what a readout circuit reports: filtered, scaled, offset, clipped and quantized. A foot does not report contact; a switch with a threshold and hysteresis does. From the proprioception list: "motor current and estimated torque; winding and housing temperature; battery voltage and current, read through a calibrated readout with clipping and lag. First checks: the step response of a resistor-inductor circuit and a thermal cool-down" and "foot contact switches with threshold and hysteresis". From the failure-mode table: "Current, temperature, voltage: clipping, lag; model: first-order readout with limits; first check: circuit step response" and "Contact switch: chatter, hysteresis; model: threshold pair; first check: a slow load ramp".

These are small models. They matter because thermal protection, current limiting and contact-aided estimation are all driven by them, and a design that passes on true quantities can fail on reported ones. Tier: Solid. Values are `assumed` until a card records a datasheet or a measurement.

## Scope

In: one readout model shared by the scalar channels, with its checkpointed state; the estimated-torque channel; the contact switch with its state; card fields; kernels; the two first checks.

Out: the electrical and thermal dynamics being observed (the actuator chapter); force-torque sensors (N20a-16); the ordered overload chain for saturating force readouts (the force-history bead of the late subsections).

## Technical approach

**1. The readout** (card kind `sensor.readout`; fields with provenance: `tau` lag time constant, `gain_error`, `offset`, `lo`, `hi` limits, `lsb`, clock and link). For a true quantity x sampled every Δt with filter state y:

1. Lag, exact for an input held over the sample: y_{k+1} = y_k + (1 − exp(−Δt/τ))·(x_k − y_k). The factor is an admission-time constant.
2. Calibration error: z = (1 + `gain_error`)·y + `offset`.
3. Keyed white noise if the card declares a noise level.
4. Clip to [`lo`, `hi`]; set the packet flag `saturated` if clipped.
5. Quantize to `lsb`.

The state y is per world and per channel and is part of every checkpoint (it is a filter state, like a bias walk).

**2. Channels.** Motor current (true winding current through a readout); estimated torque (the driver's estimate k_t·î from the reported current and the card's torque constant, never the true shaft torque); winding temperature and housing temperature (the thermal network's node temperatures; a winding sensor that is physically on the housing declares the housing node as its source); battery voltage and battery current.

**3. Known responses used by the first checks.**
- Resistor-inductor circuit, step of voltage V at t = 0, resistance R, inductance L, electrical time constant τ_e = L/R: true current i(t) = (V/R)·(1 − exp(−t/τ_e)). Through a readout of lag τ_r ≠ τ_e the reported current is (V/R)·[1 − (τ_e·exp(−t/τ_e) − τ_r·exp(−t/τ_r))/(τ_e − τ_r)].
- Thermal cool-down of one node from T₀ toward ambient T_a with thermal time constant τ_th: T(t) = T_a + (T₀ − T_a)·exp(−t/τ_th); the readout adds its own lag by the same two-lag formula.

**4. Contact switch** (card kind `sensor.contact-switch`: `f_on`, `f_off` with `f_on` > `f_off`, optional debounce in samples, clock, link). State s ∈ {0, 1} in the checkpoint. At each sample with normal force F: if s = 0 and F ≥ `f_on`, s ← 1; if s = 1 and F ≤ `f_off`, s ← 0; otherwise unchanged. With debounce d the change takes effect only after d consecutive samples agree. Chatter is what appears when the force ripples across a single threshold; the pair removes it for ripple smaller than `f_on` − `f_off`. On impulse-level steps the force over a sample is the impulse divided by the sample period (the same averaging as the force sensor).

**5. Kernels.** `K-sense-readout` and `K-sense-switch`, float and exact-integer respectively, with CPU references.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Circuit step (GATE-SENS-14 row; tolerance proposed: 1% of V/R at every sample): V = 12 V, R = 0.5 Ω, L = 1 mH (τ_e = 2 ms), τ_r = 1 ms, sampled at 10 kHz: the reported current follows the two-lag formula; at t = 2 ms the true current is 24·(1 − e⁻¹) = 15.17 A and the reported current is 24·[1 − (2·e⁻¹ − 1·e⁻²)/1] = 24·(1 − 0.6004) = 9.59 A.
- [ ] Thermal cool-down (tolerance proposed: 0.5 K): T₀ = 90 °C, T_a = 25 °C, τ_th = 120 s: at t = 120 s the node is at 25 + 65·e⁻¹ = 48.9 °C and the readout (τ_r = 5 s) reports the two-lag value.
- [ ] Clipping: a true current of 80 A through a readout with `hi` = 60 A reports 60 A with `saturated`; the flag clears when the value returns inside the limits.
- [ ] Slow load ramp (GATE-SENS-14 row; exact in sample index): with `f_on` = 10 N and `f_off` = 6 N, a ramp from 0 to 20 N and back at 1 N per sample switches on at the first sample with F ≥ 10 N and off at the first sample with F ≤ 6 N; a ±1 N ripple around 8 N causes no transition; a single-threshold mutant at 8 N chatters and is caught.
- [ ] Readout and switch states survive checkpoint and restore byte for byte.
- [ ] Estimated torque equals k_t times the reported current, not the true torque: with a 10% gain error it is off by 10%.
- [ ] Every card field reports provenance.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/readout.rs`, `tests/contact_switch.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense readout contact_switch`.
- `rl_step_two_lag`: 9.59 A at 2 ms within 1%; whole curve within 1% of V/R.
- `lag_factor_exact`: for Δt = 0.1 ms and τ = 1 ms the factor is 1 − exp(−0.1) to the last bit of the deterministic exponential.
- `thermal_cooldown_two_lag`.
- `gain_offset`: gain error +2%, offset 0.1 A on a steady 10 A reads 10.3 A.
- `clip_and_flag`, `flag_clears`.
- `quantize_lsb`.
- `estimated_torque_uses_reported_current`.
- `switch_ramp_thresholds`: on at F = 10, off at F = 6.
- `switch_ripple_no_chatter`; `single_threshold_mutant_chatters` (negative control).
- `switch_debounce`: with d = 3 the transition is delayed by 3 samples.
- `checkpoint_roundtrip_readout_and_switch`.

### End-to-end script and logging
Exercised by `scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh` (N20a-46, the sensor-card half): `frob sim run` on the circuit-step, cool-down and load-ramp fixtures; `frob telemetry query` reads the reported channels; the script asserts the values above and logs measured values, units (A, °C, N, sample index) and thresholds.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-514 (circuit step response and thermal cool-down through a first-order readout with limits; tolerance to set, proposed above; no control stated: proposed control, a readout with zero lag, which reads 15.17 A at 2 ms and fails), KA-515 (switches at the threshold pair, with hysteresis; proposed control: the single-threshold mutant). Determinism: readout in the physics' float class; switch state exact.

## Considerations

- A thermal protection trip driven by the reported temperature can lag the true winding temperature by the readout's time constant; the design check's actuator-envelope analysis uses true values and should be compared with a run using reported ones.
- The two-lag formula is singular at τ_e = τ_r; the test fixtures avoid equality, and the implementation never evaluates the closed form (it is only the oracle).
- Interaction with the contact-aided estimator: a switch that reports contact late or early changes when a contact point is added to the filter state; the estimator's own tests cover that with this model as the source.
