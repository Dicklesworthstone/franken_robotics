## Background

Wheel odometry is dead reckoning from wheel encoders. From the proprioception list: "wheel odometry: encoder displacement, which equals travel only while the wheel rolls. First checks: a circular arc on a dry floor; a deliberate slip on a film patch, where the odometry error must equal the slip distance in the contact telemetry." From the failure-mode table: "Wheel odometry: slip; model: none added: the encoder is honest and the wheel is not; first check: slip on a film patch."

The design decision is in that sentence. No slip noise is added to odometry. The encoder reports wheel rotation correctly (with the encoder's own quantization and faults), the contact model decides whether the wheel rolls or slips, and the odometry error is whatever the mechanics produce. Alternative rejected: an odometry noise model proportional to distance, which is what estimators usually assume and is wrong in the way that matters (slip is concentrated on wet patches, thresholds and hard accelerations, not spread evenly). The wheeled estimator uses wheel odometry with the IMU on the invariant filter; whether it detects slip is the estimator's job, and it can only be tested against an odometry source that slips for physical reasons. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the odometry channel computed from wheel encoder counts through the base's kinematic connection; exact integration on SE(2); the two first checks; the identity "odometry error equals slip distance".

Out: the encoder model (N20a-13); the unwrapping validity predicate and the `ambiguous` flag (N20b-40, a dependency: this bead calls them on every increment); the slip-distance metric of the contact telemetry (N24b-11); contact and film mechanics; the estimator and slip detection; visual or lidar odometry.

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs.** Per wheel, the encoder's count increments Δc over one odometry period Δt (the odometry clock is a declared clock; its samples use delivered encoder packets, so encoder latency and loss propagate). Δc is taken between the last two *delivered* counts, never against a count that was lost: the motion of a period whose packet was lost arrives with the next delivered packet, and no travel is lost. The increment is unwrapped modulo the counter by the validity predicate of N20b-40 with the gap that actually occurred; an increment that fails it carries `ambiguous` and the pose is not advanced on it as if it were known. Wheel angle increment Δφ = Δc·2π/cpr (through the gear ratio if the encoder is on the motor side); rolling travel estimate Δs = r·Δφ with r the wheel radius on the card.

**2. Body twist from wheel travel.** By the base type's kinematic connection: differential drive with track width W: Δd = (Δs_R + Δs_L)/2, Δψ = (Δs_R − Δs_L)/W; steered and omnidirectional bases use their own connection matrices (least squares over the wheels when over-determined, with the residual reported as a consistency channel: a non-zero residual is direct evidence of slip).

**3. Pose update on the group**, exact for a constant twist over the period (never the Euler step): with heading ψ, for Δψ ≠ 0: Δx_body = (Δd/Δψ)·sin Δψ, Δy_body = (Δd/Δψ)·(1 − cos Δψ); for Δψ = 0: Δx_body = Δd, Δy_body = 0; then rotate by ψ and add; ψ ← ψ + Δψ. The series form is used for |Δψ| below 10⁻⁶.

**4. Outputs.** A packet per odometry period with (Δd, Δψ) and the integrated pose, the connection residual, flags inherited from the encoder packets (`held`, `late`, `ambiguous`), and the usual instants.

**5. The slip identity.** For a wheel, travel of the contact point relative to the floor is rolling travel minus slip: r·Δφ = Δs_true + Δs_slip along the rolling direction, where Δs_slip is the signed longitudinal slip at the contact (its absolute time integral is the slip distance the contact telemetry accumulates). Odometry therefore over-reports the wheel's path by the signed slip, and on a straight run in which both wheels slip equally the odometry distance error equals the slip distance in the contact telemetry.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Circular arc on a dry floor (GATE-SENS-14 row; tolerance set by this bead and carried by KA-517 as "(set: N20a-17)": 1 mm and 1 mrad after a quarter circle at the fixture's resolution): a differential-drive base with r = 0.10 m and W = 0.50 m driven at v = 1 m/s and yaw rate 1 rad/s. Over π/2 s that is a quarter circle ending at (1, 1) m with heading π/2; the outer wheel's encoder travel is 1.25·π/2 = 1.9635 m and the inner's 0.75·π/2 = 1.1781 m. Simulation time is rational and π/2 s is not, so the simulated fixture ends at its last odometry instant t_e at or before π/2 s. DESIGN: a 10 ms odometry period on 400 Hz physics, t_e = 1.57 s, 0.80 ms short. Odometry and truth are each compared with the closed form at t_e: (sin t_e, 1 − cos t_e) m with heading t_e, and wheel travels 1.25·t_e and 0.75·t_e m. At 1.57 s that is (0.99999968, 0.99920367) m and 1.57 rad, which is 0.80 mm and 0.80 mrad from the quarter-circle values, too close to the tolerance to compare against those.
- [ ] Slip on a film patch (GATE-SENS-14 row; tolerance set by this bead and carried by KA-518 as "(set: N20a-17)": the larger of 1 mm and 0.5% of the slip): on a straight run across a patch on which the wheels slip, odometry distance minus true distance equals the slip distance reported by the contact telemetry.
- [ ] The Euler-step mutant (Δx = Δd·cos ψ, Δy = Δd·sin ψ with the old heading) fails the arc check at the registered tolerance when the odometry period is 50 ms (t_e = 1.55 s, 31 periods: the mutant ends 35 mm from the closed form, the exact update within rounding).
- [ ] No noise source other than the encoder's own enters odometry (a test asserts the odometry stream is a pure function of delivered encoder packets and card constants).
- [ ] The connection residual is zero on the dry arc (to rounding) and non-zero on a fixture in which only one wheel slips.
- [ ] Encoder packet loss propagates: with one encoder packet lost, the odometry packet for that period carries `held` and the pose is not advanced with fabricated motion; the next odometry packet takes Δc against the last delivered count and advances the pose by the whole travel of both periods (the arc fixture with one packet dropped still ends at the closed form at t_e within tolerance).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/odometry.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense odometry`.
- `arc_quarter_circle`: synthetic exact wheel travels give (1, 1, π/2) to 10⁻¹² in double precision (synthetic periods need not lie on the clock lattice).
- `arc_closed_form_at_rational_end`: on the 10 ms and 50 ms grids the arc's last instants 1.57 s and 1.55 s give odometry equal to (sin t_e, 1 − cos t_e, t_e) to 10⁻¹², and the pose at 1.57 s differs from (1, 1, π/2) by 0.80 mm and 0.80 mrad (asserted, so that the reference stays the closed form at t_e).
- `straight_line`: equal travels of 2 m give (2, 0, 0).
- `spin_in_place`: travels +0.3927 m and −0.3927 m with W = 0.5 give Δψ = π/2 (0.7854/0.5 = 1.5708) and no translation.
- `exact_update_vs_euler`: at a 50 ms period on the arc, the exact update's error is below 10⁻⁹ m and the Euler mutant's exceeds 1 mm.
- `small_angle_series`: Δψ = 10⁻⁹ matches the series within 10⁻¹⁵.
- `slip_identity_synthetic`: wheel rotation equivalent to 1.20 m with a planted slip of 0.20 m gives odometry 1.20 m against truth 1.00 m.
- `residual_detects_single_wheel_slip` (a four-wheel base with one wheel's travel perturbed).
- `pure_function_of_encoder_packets`.
- `lost_packet_holds`.
- `lost_packet_catches_up`: on the synthetic arc at a 50 ms period with packet 10 lost, packet 10's odometry is `held` and the end pose equals the no-loss end pose to 10⁻¹² (the exact update over the doubled period is exact for a constant twist); a mutant that drops the lost period's increment (it differences packet 11 against a reconstructed count for packet 10) loses one period's travel, 0.05 m of arc and 0.05 rad of heading, and fails.

### End-to-end script and logging
Exercised by `scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh` (N20a-46, the sensor-card half): `frob sim run` of the arc and of the film-patch fixture, each call with its own `--key` (a run verb); `frob telemetry query` reads odometry, truth and the contact slip-distance channel; the script asserts the arc end pose against the closed form at the run's last odometry instant t_e (not against (1, 1, π/2), which no rational end time reaches) and the identity (odometry error − slip distance) within tolerance, and logs t_e, the pose differences and all three distances in metres with the threshold.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-517 (encoder displacement equals travel while the wheel rolls; tolerance and control set by this bead and carried by the registry row: 1 mm and 1 mrad, and the Euler-step mutant), KA-518 (odometry error equals the slip distance in the contact telemetry; tolerance and control set by this bead: the larger of 1 mm and 0.5% of the slip, and an odometry source that reads true base velocity, which shows zero error on the patch and fails). Metamorphic: reversing the run reverses the odometry path on a dry floor; adding a film patch changes odometry error and leaves the encoder-to-odometry map unchanged. Determinism: float class of the physics.

## Considerations

- The film-patch check needs the film-friction law, which lands at M3; until then the slip check runs on a low-friction plane with a hard acceleration (the wheels spin up), which exercises the same identity.
- Wheel radius on the card is a calibration constant; a 1% radius error gives a 1% odometry scale error. That is a card field with provenance, not noise, and calibration against reality owns it.
- Lateral (scrub) slip on skid-steer and tracked bases appears in the connection residual, not in the longitudinal identity; the tracked-base estimator consumes the residual.
