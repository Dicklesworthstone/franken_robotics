## Background

Wheel odometry is dead reckoning from wheel encoders. From the proprioception list: "wheel odometry: encoder displacement, which equals travel only while the wheel rolls. First checks: a circular arc on a dry floor; a deliberate slip on a film patch, where the odometry error must equal the slip distance in the contact telemetry." From the failure-mode table: "Wheel odometry: slip; model: none added: the encoder is honest and the wheel is not; first check: slip on a film patch."

The design decision is in that sentence. No slip noise is added to odometry. The encoder reports wheel rotation correctly (with the encoder's own quantization and faults), the contact model decides whether the wheel rolls or slips, and the odometry error is whatever the mechanics produce. Alternative rejected: an odometry noise model proportional to distance, which is what estimators usually assume and is wrong in the way that matters (slip is concentrated on wet patches, thresholds and hard accelerations, not spread evenly). The wheeled estimator uses wheel odometry with the IMU on the invariant filter; whether it detects slip is the estimator's job, and it can only be tested against an odometry source that slips for physical reasons. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the odometry channel computed from wheel encoder counts through the base's kinematic connection; exact integration on SE(2); the two first checks; the identity "odometry error equals slip distance".

Out: the encoder model (N20a-13); contact and film mechanics; the estimator and slip detection; visual or lidar odometry.

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs.** Per wheel, the encoder's count increments Δc over one odometry period Δt (the odometry clock is a declared clock; its samples use delivered encoder packets, so encoder latency and loss propagate). Wheel angle increment Δφ = Δc·2π/cpr (through the gear ratio if the encoder is on the motor side); rolling travel estimate Δs = r·Δφ with r the wheel radius on the card.

**2. Body twist from wheel travel.** By the base type's kinematic connection: differential drive with track width W: Δd = (Δs_R + Δs_L)/2, Δψ = (Δs_R − Δs_L)/W; steered and omnidirectional bases use their own connection matrices (least squares over the wheels when over-determined, with the residual reported as a consistency channel: a non-zero residual is direct evidence of slip).

**3. Pose update on the group**, exact for a constant twist over the period (never the Euler step): with heading ψ, for Δψ ≠ 0: Δx_body = (Δd/Δψ)·sin Δψ, Δy_body = (Δd/Δψ)·(1 − cos Δψ); for Δψ = 0: Δx_body = Δd, Δy_body = 0; then rotate by ψ and add; ψ ← ψ + Δψ. The series form is used for |Δψ| below 10⁻⁶.

**4. Outputs.** A packet per odometry period with (Δd, Δψ) and the integrated pose, the connection residual, flags inherited from the encoder packets (`held`, `late`, `ambiguous`), and the usual instants.

**5. The slip identity.** For a wheel, travel of the contact point relative to the floor is rolling travel minus slip: r·Δφ = Δs_true + Δs_slip along the rolling direction, where Δs_slip is the signed longitudinal slip at the contact (its absolute time integral is the slip distance the contact telemetry accumulates). Odometry therefore over-reports the wheel's path by the signed slip, and on a straight run in which both wheels slip equally the odometry distance error equals the slip distance in the contact telemetry.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Circular arc on a dry floor (GATE-SENS-14 row; tolerance proposed: 1 mm and 1 mrad after a quarter circle at the fixture's resolution): a differential-drive base with r = 0.10 m and W = 0.50 m driven at v = 1 m/s and yaw rate 1 rad/s for π/2 s ends at (1, 1) m with heading π/2 in odometry and in truth; the outer wheel's encoder travel is 1.25·π/2 = 1.9635 m and the inner's 0.75·π/2 = 1.1781 m.
- [ ] Slip on a film patch (GATE-SENS-14 row; tolerance proposed: the larger of 1 mm and 0.5% of the slip): on a straight run across a patch on which the wheels slip, odometry distance minus true distance equals the slip distance reported by the contact telemetry.
- [ ] The Euler-step mutant (Δx = Δd·cos ψ, Δy = Δd·sin ψ with the old heading) fails the arc check at the proposed tolerance when the odometry period is 50 ms.
- [ ] No noise source other than the encoder's own enters odometry (a test asserts the odometry stream is a pure function of delivered encoder packets and card constants).
- [ ] The connection residual is zero on the dry arc (to rounding) and non-zero on a fixture in which only one wheel slips.
- [ ] Encoder packet loss propagates: with one encoder packet lost, the odometry packet for that period carries `held` and the pose is not advanced with fabricated motion.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/odometry.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense odometry`.
- `arc_quarter_circle`: synthetic exact wheel travels give (1, 1, π/2) to 10⁻¹² in double precision.
- `straight_line`: equal travels of 2 m give (2, 0, 0).
- `spin_in_place`: travels +0.3927 m and −0.3927 m with W = 0.5 give Δψ = π/2 (0.7854/0.5 = 1.5708) and no translation.
- `exact_update_vs_euler`: at a 50 ms period on the arc, the exact update's error is below 10⁻⁹ m and the Euler mutant's exceeds 1 mm.
- `small_angle_series`: Δψ = 10⁻⁹ matches the series within 10⁻¹⁵.
- `slip_identity_synthetic`: wheel rotation equivalent to 1.20 m with a planted slip of 0.20 m gives odometry 1.20 m against truth 1.00 m.
- `residual_detects_single_wheel_slip` (a four-wheel base with one wheel's travel perturbed).
- `pure_function_of_encoder_packets`.
- `lost_packet_holds`.

### End-to-end script and logging
Exercised by `scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh` (N20a-46, the sensor-card half): `frob sim run` of the arc and of the film-patch fixture; `frob telemetry query` reads odometry, truth and the contact slip-distance channel; the script asserts the arc end pose and the identity (odometry error − slip distance) within tolerance and logs all three distances in metres with the threshold.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-517 (encoder displacement equals travel while the wheel rolls; tolerance to set, proposed above; proposed control: the Euler-step mutant), KA-518 (odometry error equals the slip distance in the contact telemetry; proposed control: an odometry source that reads true base velocity, which shows zero error on the patch and fails). Metamorphic: reversing the run reverses the odometry path on a dry floor; adding a film patch changes odometry error and leaves the encoder-to-odometry map unchanged. Determinism: float class of the physics.

## Considerations

- The film-patch check needs the film-friction law, which lands at M3; until then the slip check runs on a low-friction plane with a hard acceleration (the wheels spin up), which exercises the same identity.
- Wheel radius on the card is a calibration constant; a 1% radius error gives a 1% odometry scale error. That is a card field with provenance, not noise, and calibration against reality owns it.
- Lateral (scrub) slip on skid-steer and tracked bases appears in the connection residual, not in the longitudinal identity; the tracked-base estimator consumes the residual.
