## Background

A tactile array is a grid of small force sensors (taxels) on a pad: a fingertip, a palm, a foot sole. Each taxel has a finite footprint and reports the normal load on it and, in some devices, the shear, each up to a saturation level. From the proprioception list: "Tactile arrays [F]: finite taxel footprints with normal and shear saturation. First check: the resultant load against the sum over taxels. No task before M6 needs them."

Tier: Frontier. It is built only if its pilot's verdict is `fund` (N20a-18), which in turn requires a registered task that needs it. It claims no speed benefit. Its fallback is the mask-only model (a contact mask per taxel), adopted if the model adds more than 25% to the cost of the stage it joins. It is a child of the tactile sensing and compliant pads epic.

Alternatives rejected: a point-sampled pressure at each taxel centre (loses load between centres, so the sum over taxels does not equal the resultant); a learned tactile image (nothing to check it against).

## Scope

In: the card; the map from a pad's contact set to taxel readings; saturation with flags; the conservation check; the kernel; the cost measurement that decides the fallback.

Out: pad compliance and contact mechanics (the epic and the contact chapter); slip detection from tactile data (a consumer).

## Technical approach

**1. Card** (kind `sensor.tactile`, per-field provenance): pad frame and surface (a planar or developable patch attached to a link), grid (rows, columns, pitch), taxel footprint (rectangle; fill factor), `normal_sat` (N per taxel), `shear_sat` (N per taxel, or absent), `noise_sigma`, `lsb`, exposure, clock, link.

**2. From contact set to taxels** (per sample; numbered steps).
1. Take the pad's contact samples accumulated over the exposure (points on the pad surface with normal force f_n and tangential force vector f_t, exposure-averaged exactly as a force-torque reading is, each impulse once).
2. Express each point in pad surface coordinates (u, v).
3. Distribute each sample's load onto taxels by footprint overlap of the sample's area element (a contact sample carries the area it represents; a point sample with no area goes wholly to the taxel that contains it, and to the nearest taxel if it falls in a gap, with the gap load counted in a `gap_load` channel).
4. Per taxel: n_ij = Σ f_n shares; s_ij = Σ f_t shares (two components in pad axes).
5. Saturate: n_ij ← min(n_ij, `normal_sat`); shear magnitude clipped to `shear_sat`; a saturated taxel sets the per-sample flag bit `saturated` in the taxel flag plane.
6. Noise, quantization, stamp, enqueue.

**3. Conservation.** Before saturation, Σ n_ij + `gap_load` equals the exposure-averaged normal force on the pad, and Σ s_ij the tangential force; the centre of pressure Σ n_ij·(u_ij, v_ij)/Σ n_ij equals the pad's centre of pressure up to half a pitch. After saturation the sum is lower, and the difference is reported in a `clipped_load` channel so that the check stays exact.

**4. Kernel and cost.** `K-sense-tactile` (float; CPU reference). The added cost is measured as a fraction of the contact-and-sensing stage on the registered task; above 25% the mask-only fallback is adopted and recorded.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot record with verdict `fund` exists and predates the first commit of this mechanism; otherwise this bead is closed as `not funded` or `fallback` with the reason.
- [ ] Conservation (first check; tolerance proposed: 10⁻⁶ relative in double precision, 10⁻⁴ in single): Σ taxels + `gap_load` + `clipped_load` equals the pad's exposure-averaged normal force on the indentation fixture and on a rolling-contact fixture.
- [ ] Saturation: taxels inside the analytic saturation radius of the Hertz fixture (2.969 mm for P = 10 N, a = 4 mm, 200 kPa equivalent per-taxel limit) carry the saturation flag and no taxel outside it does, to within one pitch.
- [ ] The point-sampling mutant fails conservation.
- [ ] The measured cost fraction is recorded; if above 25%, the fallback is in force and the capability is described as "contact mask per taxel".
- [ ] Every card field reports provenance; readings carry masks for taxels outside the pad's valid area.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/tactile.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense --features frontier-tactile tactile`.
- `sum_equals_resultant_uniform`: a uniform 100 kPa over a 10 mm × 10 mm patch on a 1 mm grid gives 10 N in total and 0.1 N per fully covered taxel.
- `sum_equals_resultant_hertz`: 10 N within tolerance at three sphere positions.
- `centre_of_pressure_half_pitch`.
- `saturation_flags_inside_radius`, `clipped_load_accounts_for_difference`.
- `shear_sum_equals_tangential`.
- `gap_load_counted`: with fill factor 0.8, load on gaps is in `gap_load` and conservation holds.
- `point_sampling_mutant_fails` (negative control).
- `impulse_counted_once`: a 0.02 N·s tap inside a 10 ms exposure gives a summed reading of 2 N in one window.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_tactile.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/sense_tactile/`): `frob sim run` on the indentation fixture; `frob telemetry query` for the taxel channel, the force-torque channel of the same pad and the `clipped_load` and `gap_load` channels; asserts conservation and the saturation region; logs sums in newtons with thresholds, the cost fraction against 25%, and the evidence block. Not run until the mechanism is funded.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-523 (resultant equals the sum over taxels). Negative controls: point sampling; saturation applied before exposure averaging on an impulsive tap (the ordered-chain question: the card states the order, and the swapped order must change the reading on the tap fixture). Determinism: float class of the physics.

## Considerations

- Most contact tiers resolve a pad's contact as a handful of points; a pressure map built from them is only as fine as the contact sampling. The contract states the effective resolution (the larger of the taxel pitch and the contact sample spacing).
- Shear before gross slip depends on pad compliance the rigid tiers do not model; shear readings are labeled with the contact law that produced them.
- Values are `assumed` until a datasheet or measurement is recorded.
