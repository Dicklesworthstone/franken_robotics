## Background

A tactile array is a grid of small force sensors (taxels) on a pad: a fingertip, a palm, a foot sole. Each taxel has a finite footprint and reports the normal load on it and, in some devices, the shear, each up to a saturation level. From the proprioception list: "Tactile arrays [F]: finite taxel footprints with normal and shear saturation. First check: the resultant load against the sum over taxels. No task before M6 needs them."

Tier: Frontier. It is built only if its pilot's verdict is `fund` (N20a-18), which in turn requires a registered task that needs it. It lives behind the feature `frontier-tactile` of `frob-sense` (off by default). It claims no speed benefit. Its fallback is the mask-only model (a contact mask per taxel), adopted if the model adds more than 25% to the cost of the stage it joins; its kill gate is KILL-TAXEL (N38a-37 enters the row; N38ap3-01 decides it at M6). It is a child of the tactile sensing and compliant pads epic.

Like every sensor of the system it obeys the clock contract (a rational period, an exposure window, a delivered packet with capture and arrival instants) and reads only what the contact step produced; it never reaches into state the instrument could not see.

Alternatives rejected: a point-sampled pressure at each taxel centre (loses load between centres, so the sum over taxels does not equal the resultant); a taxel image synthesized from a single patch by an assumed pressure profile (it would report a distribution the dynamics never computed); a learned tactile image (nothing to check it against).

## Scope

In: the tactile-array sensor, specified once, here: the card; the map from a pad's contact set to taxel readings; saturation with flags; noise, quantization and packets under the clock contract; the conservation check and its tolerance (KA-523); the kernel; the cost measurement that decides the fallback; the sensor's unit tests and its indentation script.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- the pad that feeds the sensor at its finest: a fingertip pad as a grid of micro-patch contacts in `frob-contact` (2 × 2 and 4 × 4 four-dimensional contact samples, each with the area it represents), the pad compliance card, the capacity refusal, the grasp-slip comparison against one patch per pad, the step cost of the pads and the grasp script that reads this sensor through observations: N38a-31 (Moonshot; it depends on this bead). This sensor does not need micro-patches to exist: it reads whatever contact set a pad has, down to one point sample per pad, and states the effective resolution;
- pad compliance and contact mechanics in general (the tactile epic BD-418, this bead's parent, whose pad pilot is N18b-35, which superseded the draft N38a-30, and the contact chapter); slip detection from tactile data (a consumer).

## Technical approach

**1. Card** (kind `sensor.tactile`, per-field provenance): pad frame and surface (a planar or developable patch attached to a link), grid (rows T_u, columns T_v, pitch), taxel footprint (rectangle; fill factor; each taxel integrates load over its own rectangle), `normal_sat` (N per taxel; F_n,max), `shear_sat` (N per taxel, or absent; F_t,max), `noise_sigma`, `lsb` (the resolution quantum), exposure, clock (period, exposure, latency), link.

**2. From contact set to taxels** (per sample; numbered steps).
1. Take the pad's contact samples accumulated over the exposure (points on the pad surface with normal force f_n and tangential force vector f_t in the pad frame, exposure-averaged exactly as a force-torque reading is: each sample's contact impulse is accumulated over the window, each impact impulse is added once, and the sum is divided by the exposure duration to give a mean force per sample).
2. Express each point in pad surface coordinates (u, v).
3. Distribute each sample's load onto taxels by footprint overlap of the sample's area element: taxel j receives Σ_c w_jc·f_c with w_jc the overlap area of taxel j and the area element of sample c divided by that element's area, so Σ_j w_jc = 1 for a sample fully covered by taxels. A contact sample carries the area it represents (a micro-patch cell of N38a-31 carries its cell); a point sample with no area goes wholly to the taxel that contains it, and to the nearest taxel if it falls in a gap, with the gap load counted in a `gap_load` channel.
4. Per taxel: n_ij = Σ f_n shares; s_ij = Σ f_t shares (two components in pad axes). The unsaturated reading is r_ij = (n, s_u, s_v)_ij.
5. Noise from the taxel's own address-keyed stream, added to (n, s_u, s_v).
6. Saturate: n_ij is clamped to [0, `normal_sat`]; the shear vector is scaled to magnitude at most `shear_sat`; a saturated taxel sets the per-sample flag bit `saturated` in the taxel flag plane of the packet. Noise comes before the clamp (DESIGN, the same order as the IMU and scalar-readout chains, N20a-14 and N20a-15): a clipping converter never reports more than its limit, so a saturated taxel reads exactly `normal_sat`, and the opposite order would report values above the limit on about half the samples.
7. Quantization to `lsb` (`lsb` = 0 on a card means no quantization, which the conformance fixtures use); the packet is stamped and queued with its capture and arrival instants.

**3. Conservation.** Before saturation, Σ n_ij + `gap_load` equals the exposure-averaged normal force on the pad, and Σ s_ij the tangential force; the centre of pressure Σ n_ij·(u_ij, v_ij)/Σ n_ij equals the pad's centre of pressure up to half a pitch. After saturation the sum is lower, and the difference is reported in a `clipped_load` channel so that the check stays exact. Noise and quantization are by design not conserved, so the conservation check runs on fixture cards with `noise_sigma` = 0 and `lsb` = 0.

Tolerance of KA-523 (DESIGN; the known-answer suite left it "to set" and now carries this bead's value marked "(set: N20a-19)"; two drafts had set it differently: 10⁻⁶ relative in double precision in an earlier draft of this bead, 10⁻¹² in the pad bead N38a-31). One value per precision: 10⁻¹² relative in double precision and 10⁻⁴ relative in single precision. Reason: the map is a partition of every sample's load by weights that sum to one, so the equality is exact in exact arithmetic and only the round-off of the additions remains; 10⁻⁶ in double precision would pass a build that loses a boundary share of one part in a million.

**4. Kernel and cost.** `K-sense-tactile` (float; CPU reference). The added cost is measured as a fraction of the contact-and-sensing stage on the registered task and carried in the receipt with paired bounds (DESIGN: the fraction is the median over five paired repeats after warm-up, each the stage time on the same frames with the sensor on divided by the time with it off, minus one; its lower and upper bounds are the smallest and largest of the five). This bead measures and records; it does not decide. The fallback is adopted under the tactile array's kill gate KILL-TAXEL (the row N38a-37 enters at M4, a dependency; decided at M6 by N38ap3-01, which compares the lower bound with 0.25 and records straddling bounds without firing). A lower bound above 0.25 opens this bead's one repair cycle in N40-03's form (a `repair-cycle` note line naming the failed receipt and the hypothesis, one bounded change to the mechanism, one re-measurement on the same task and threshold, the first receipt kept); a value still above it is recorded and the bead closes `recorded` (N41-17), which makes the decision bead ready.

**5. Evidence, labels and surface.** A reading carries the tier and determinism class of the contact step that fed the sensor; known answers are class `Tested`; card provenance (`generic`, `assumed`) is carried into every reading's claim product. No new verb: the sensor's clock appears in `sense.schedule`, its cost and footprint in `sense.profile`, and it is read through observations like any other sensor.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot record of N20a-18 with verdict `falsifier-rejected` (`fund`) exists and its ledger sequence predates the first commit of this mechanism; if the verdict is `falsifier-holds`, or the record is `withdrawn` because no registered task is due (N20a-18, step 5), this bead is not started and closes `withdrawn` with a pointer to BD-418 (N41-17; "not funded" is not a close reason).
- [ ] Conservation (first check; KA-523): Σ taxels + `gap_load` + `clipped_load` equals the pad's exposure-averaged normal force within 10⁻¹² relative in double precision and 10⁻⁴ in single, on the indentation fixture and on a rolling-contact fixture, for any taxel grid that covers the pad; the tangential resultant likewise.
- [ ] Saturation: taxels inside the analytic saturation radius of the Hertz fixture (2.969 mm for P = 10 N, a = 4 mm, 200 kPa equivalent per-taxel limit) carry the saturation flag and no taxel outside it does, to within one pitch; with saturation the resultant is below the pad force and the reading says so through `clipped_load`.
- [ ] The point-sampling mutant and the total-in-every-taxel mutant each fail conservation.
- [ ] Packets obey the clock contract: checkpoint and restore at any instant reproduce every later delivered reading byte for byte.
- [ ] The measured cost fraction is recorded in the receipt with its paired lower and upper bounds (section 4); above 25% after the one repair cycle the bead closes `recorded` on that receipt and KILL-TAXEL's decision (N38ap3-01) puts the fallback in force, the capability then described as "contact mask per taxel".
- [ ] Every card field reports provenance; readings carry masks for taxels outside the pad's valid area.
- [ ] The feature `frontier-tactile` is off by default; the contract of `frob-sense` states the mechanism, its effective resolution and its gaps.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/tactile.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense --features frontier-tactile --test tactile`.
- `sum_equals_resultant_uniform`: a uniform 100 kPa over a 10 mm × 10 mm patch on a 1 mm grid gives 10 N in total and 0.1 N per fully covered taxel.
- `sum_equals_resultant_hertz`: 10 N within tolerance at three sphere positions.
- `ka_523_resultant_equals_sum`: contact samples with areas (four cells of a 2 × 2 pad) carrying normal forces (1, 2, 3, 4) N, read by a 4 × 4 taxel grid: Σ taxels = 10 N; the tangential resultant likewise.
- `overlap_weights_sum_to_one`: for 2 × 2 cells and 3 × 3 taxels each cell's weights sum to 1.
- `centre_of_pressure_half_pitch`.
- `saturation_flags_inside_radius`, `clipped_load_accounts_for_difference`.
- `normal_saturation`: 6 N on a taxel with `normal_sat` = 5 N reads 5 N and sets the flag.
- `noise_before_clamp`: with `noise_sigma` = 0.5 N, 8 N on a 5 N taxel reads exactly 5 N, flagged, in every one of 10⁴ samples (the noise would have to fall below −6σ to bring it under the limit); the swapped-order mutant (clamp, then noise) reads above 5 N in about half of them and fails.
- `shear_saturation_scales_vector`: shear (3, 4) N with `shear_sat` = 2.5 N reads (1.5, 2.0) N.
- `shear_sum_equals_tangential`.
- `gap_load_counted`: with fill factor 0.8, load on gaps is in `gap_load` and conservation holds.
- `point_sampling_mutant_fails` (negative control).
- `nc_total_in_every_taxel`: a build that writes the pad total into each taxel of a 4 × 4 grid fails the resultant test by the factor 16.
- `impulse_counted_once`: a 0.02 N·s tap inside a 10 ms exposure gives a summed reading of 2 N in one window; a 0.2 N·s impact gives 20 N mean force, once.
- `checkpoint_restores_tactile_packets`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_tactile.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/sense_tactile/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript), on a build with `frontier-tactile`: `frob sim run fixtures/sense/tactile-indent.fbot --tier referee --seconds 1 --seed 19 --key st-indent-<run-id> --json` (the Hertz indentation fixture of N20a-18 as a design: a sphere pressed on a pad carrying a fixture tactile card with `noise_sigma` = 0 and `lsb` = 0), the saturating variant with `--key st-indent-sat-<run-id>`, and the rolling-contact fixture `fixtures/sense/tactile-roll.fbot` with `--key st-roll-<run-id>` (each run its own key, suffixed with the script's run identifier, so none returns another's or an earlier script run's stored receipt); `frob telemetry query <run> --stream sensing --json` for the taxel channel, the force-torque channel of the same pad and the `clipped_load` and `gap_load` channels; asserts conservation and the saturation region; then KA-523's G1 row in its cargo form, `FROB_TEST_SEED=19 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- KA-523` (the Gauntlet has no `frob` verb, ADR-GAUNT-01), reading its receipt and asserting the row's tolerance (10⁻¹² relative in double precision, 10⁻⁴ in single) on every site it runs; then the point-sampling and total-in-every-taxel mutants under `--features gauntlet-mutations` with keys `st-mut-<name>-<run-id>`, each an `assert` with `caught`. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N20a-19"), `step`, `event`; it logs the exact command lines, exit codes and wall time, sums in newtons with thresholds, the cost fraction against 25%, and the evidence block; a `summary` with assertions passed and failed. Not run until the mechanism is funded. The grasp fixture on micro-patch pads, which reads this sensor at three pad resolutions, is the pad bead's script `scripts/e2e/tactile_pad_grasp.sh` (N38a-31).

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-523 (resultant equals the sum over taxels; tolerance set here, in section 3; its registered control is the total-in-every-taxel mutation). Negative controls: point sampling; the total in every taxel; a sensor that reads contact forces at the end of the step in place of over the exposure (the impulse test fails); saturation applied before exposure averaging on an impulsive tap (the ordered-chain question: the card states the order, and the swapped order must change the reading on the tap fixture). Metamorphic relation: duplicating a taxel grid at double resolution preserves the resultant. Determinism: float class of the physics; twice on one machine and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- Most contact tiers resolve a pad's contact as a handful of points; a pressure map built from them is only as fine as the contact sampling. The contract states the effective resolution (the larger of the taxel pitch and the contact sample spacing).
- Shear before gross slip depends on pad compliance the rigid tiers do not model; shear readings are labeled with the contact law that produced them.
- Values are `assumed` until a datasheet or measurement is recorded. Without imported tactile measurements nothing here is a reality claim; every reading carries its card's provenance.
- Two tiers, two features, two pilots: this sensor is Frontier (`frontier-tactile`, pilot N20a-18, fallback the contact mask per taxel); the micro-patch pads are Moonshot (`moonshot-tactile` in `frob-contact`, pilot N18b-35, fallback one patch per pad). The sensor works with either pad.
