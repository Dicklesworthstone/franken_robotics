## Background

A ray kernel returns the geometric distance to the first hit. An instrument returns something less: a value with range-dependent noise, quantized to its own step, and nothing at all when the surface is too near, too far or seen at too shallow an angle. From the exteroception text: "Noise models cover range-dependent noise, grazing-angle dropout and range limits." From the failure-mode table: "Depth, any: near-plane blind zone, range limit, grazing dropout; model: range and incidence masks; first check: plane swept through the near limit" and "Lidar: range quantization, blind zone; model: card fields; first check: analytic sphere."

Because the ray kernels are ordinary kernels, sensor outputs have the same determinism class as the physics; the masks are exact-integer data. Tier: Solid. Values are `assumed` until a card records a datasheet or a measurement.

## Scope

In: the card fields for range behaviour (this bead owns their names: `range_min`, `range_max`, `range_kind`, `range_lsb`, the noise coefficients, `grazing_law` with both variants `cut { theta_max }` and `ramp { theta_0, theta_1 }`, `detection_threshold`); the per-ray stages in full, added to the first form in BD-187's sensor kernels: the stage order, quantization of the reported value after the kind conversion, the guarded comparison at the range limits and at the `cut` grazing threshold; the card check that rejects a `ramp` card until its stage exists; the two first checks (KA-519, KA-520) and their GATE-SENS-14 rows.

Out: first-hit kernels, lidar patterns, height scans and labels, and the first form of the range stages inside `K-sense-depth`, `K-sense-lidar` and `K-sense-hscan` (BD-187, a dependency: gate masks, the `cut` grazing threshold, σ(t) noise, the choice between ray distance and optical depth; its GATE-SENS-01 and -02 rows); optical classes and volumes (BD-237 and its children); pattern-projector validity (N20a-23); per-beam and per-row instants (BD-188); the region latent and the kernel stage of the `ramp` grazing law, which is the latent's first consumer (N20a-24, M3, which depends on this bead: the latent does not exist at M2, so a `ramp` stage here could only be a per-sample coin, which ADR-SENS-08 forbids).

## Technical approach

**1. Card fields** (on `sensor.depth` and `sensor.lidar` cards, per-field provenance): `range_min` (the near limit; below it is the blind zone), `range_max`, `range_kind` (`ray`: distance along the ray; `z`: optical depth, the component along the optical axis), `range_lsb` (quantization step; absent means no quantization), `noise` coefficients (σ₀ in m, σ₁ dimensionless, σ₂ in m⁻¹), `grazing_law` (`cut { theta_max }` or `ramp { theta_0, theta_1 }` with θ₀ < θ₁, angles from the surface normal), `detection_threshold` (the minimum normalized return strength; below it a `diffuse` return is masked `weak` by BD-187's rule, and the attenuation rule of N20a-22 compares against the same field). Angles are converted to cosines on the host when the card is loaded (c_max = cos θ_max, c₀ = cos θ₀, c₁ = cos θ₁), so the kernel never evaluates an inverse or direct trigonometric function.

**2. Stages per ray** (after the optical-class rule has produced a distance d, a unit normal n and a strength; numbered in execution order).
1. Range limits: d < `range_min` gives mask `near-blind`; d > `range_max` gives `beyond-range`. Both are strict predicates and are evaluated in the `Guarded` class of the kernel dialect (DESIGN, the plan's guarded evaluation): compute p̂ = d − `range_min` (and `range_max` − d) in kernel precision together with the bound E = c·u·S, where u is the unit round-off, S the sum of the absolute values of the terms and c the constant `frob-kir` derives and stores in the kernel manifest; if |p̂| > E the sign of p̂ decides; otherwise the predicate is re-evaluated exactly on the stored inputs in the exact-integer class (every finite binary32 or binary64 value is an integer times a power of two). Backends with bit-equal inputs therefore take the same branch; under class D3, where d itself differs across tiers within a tolerance, a sample within that tolerance of a limit may legitimately differ and is counted by the decision-level equivalence test.
2. Grazing: with cos θ = |u·n| for unit ray direction u and unit normal n. `cut`: mask `grazing` when |u·n| < c_max, a guarded predicate of products (no inverse cosine in the kernel). `ramp`: the failure probability is p(cos θ) = clamp((c₀ − cos θ)/(c₀ − c₁), 0, 1), which rises linearly in cos θ from 0 at θ₀ to 1 at θ₁; the ray is masked `grazing` when the region's per-episode latent for the cause `grazing-dropout` is below p, so repeated reads at the same angle through the same region fail together (ADR-SENS-08). The formula is fixed here; the stage that evaluates it is N20a-24's. At this bead's milestone the card check rejects a card whose `grazing_law` is `ramp` with the reason "the ramp law needs region latents", never degrading it to `cut` or to a per-sample coin.
3. Noise: d ← d + (σ₀ + σ₁·d + σ₂·d²)·n_k, with n_k a standard normal variate (mean 0, variance 1) drawn from the counter-based stream of N20a-04 keyed by (world address, sensor, sample index, ray index), never by draw order. A triangulating device has σ₂ dominant (error grows with the square of range); a time-of-flight lidar has σ₀ dominant.
4. Kind: for `range_kind = z` the reported value is d·(u·a) with a the optical axis; otherwise d.
5. Quantization: round to the nearest multiple of `range_lsb` (ties away from zero, so that every backend rounds the same way).
6. Re-check limits after noise is not done: limits apply to the geometry, so that mask boundaries are exact and reproducible.

**3. First checks.**
- Plane through the near limit (KA-519): `range_min` = 0.30 m; a plane perpendicular to the optical axis is placed at 0.25, 0.29, 0.30, 0.31 and 0.35 m. Central-ray samples are masked `near-blind` at 0.25 and 0.29 m and valid at 0.30, 0.31 and 0.35 m (the limit is d < `range_min`). The plane is an analytic primitive, whose ray test along the optical axis returns the stored value of its offset exactly, so at 0.30 m the central-ray distance equals the stored `range_min` bit for bit: that row tests strictness at equality, and the guarded predicate takes its exact fallback there.
- Analytic sphere (KA-520): a sphere of radius R = 1 m centred D = 5 m in front of a lidar; a beam at angle α from the centre line returns d(α) = D·cos α − √(R² − D²·sin² α); with `range_lsb` = 0.01 m the on-axis beam reads 4.00 m and the beam at α = 0.1 rad reads 4.11 m (unquantized 4.10851 m); a beam with D·sin α > R misses. With `range_min` = 4.05 m (an artificial blind zone for the test) the on-axis beam is masked and the 0.1 rad beam is valid.

**4. Kernel.** The stages run inside the sensor kernels of BD-187 (`K-sense-depth`, `K-sense-lidar`, `K-sense-hscan`: float for noise and scaling, exact-integer for mask codes, each with its CPU reference; one launch for all rays of a sensor). This bead adds the stage order, quantization, the guarded limit and grazing comparisons and the `ramp` card check to them and introduces no separate range kernel; each guarded predicate is registered in the kernel manifest with its derived constant c.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Near-limit sweep (GATE-SENS-14 row, exact in masks): the five plane positions give masks `near-blind`, `near-blind`, valid, valid, valid on the central ray, on the referee and every batched tier; the 0.30 m row is decided by the exact fallback and the log records that it was.
- [ ] Analytic sphere (GATE-SENS-14 row; tolerance proposed: half a quantization step plus the ray kernel's own bound): 4.00 m on axis and 4.11 m at 0.1 rad; a missing beam is `no-return`; the artificial blind zone masks exactly the beams with d below it.
- [ ] `beyond-range`: a wall at 12 m with `range_max` = 10 m is masked on every ray.
- [ ] `cut` grazing: with θ_max = 80°, a plane seen at 79° incidence is valid and at 81° is masked `grazing`.
- [ ] A card whose `grazing_law` is `ramp` is rejected by the card check with its reason, and no kernel built by this bead draws a per-sample dropout coin (lint on the kernel sources).
- [ ] Noise scaling: with quantization off, the sample standard deviation of 10⁵ returns from a plane at 2 m equals σ₀ + 2σ₁ + 4σ₂ within 1%; noise is keyed (unchanged by adding another sensor, and by changing the batch slot of the world).
- [ ] `range_kind`: a ray 30° off axis hitting a perpendicular plane at z = 2 m reports 2.000 m as `z` and 2.309 m as `ray`; the mutant that swaps them is caught (the control of the depth known answer).
- [ ] Every masked sample has its reason; every card field reports provenance.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-519 (samples inside the blind zone are masked by the range mask; no control stated: proposed control, a model that reports the true distance inside the blind zone; a second control, the non-strict comparison d ≤ `range_min`, masks the 0.30 m row and must be caught), KA-520 (ranges equal the analytic sphere distances quantized by the card fields; tolerance to set, proposed above; proposed control: quantization by truncation, which reads 4.10 m off axis). KA-465's control (ray distance swapped for optical depth) is exercised by `range_kind_z_vs_ray`. Metamorphic: scaling the scene and every range field by a common factor scales every reported range by that factor and leaves masks unchanged. Determinism: masks exact; ranges in the physics' class.

## Considerations

- A comparison of a float distance with a limit is a strict predicate; near the limit two backends whose distances differ (class D3) may disagree by one sample. Fixtures on distance-field geometry place surfaces away from a limit by more than the ray bound; the at-limit row of the near sweep uses an analytic plane, whose distance equals the stored limit, so strictness is tested at equality on every backend. Elsewhere the disagreement is a class D3 difference recorded as such.
- Real blind zones depend on reflectivity and on multi-path; the model is a hard limit from the card, stated as a known gap.
- The `ramp` law with a per-region latent is what makes grazing dropout persistent; an independent coin per sample would let repetition average it away (the reason for ADR-SENS-08). Between M2 and M3 a sensor whose datasheet describes a soft grazing roll-off is modelled with `cut` at the angle of 50% dropout, and its card says so in the provenance note.
