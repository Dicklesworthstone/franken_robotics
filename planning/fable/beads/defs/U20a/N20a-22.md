## Background

Steam near a dishwasher, a kettle or a fryer, and dust in a warehouse, attenuate light. In the scene they are optical volumes: a box or an ellipsoid with an extinction coefficient σ in m⁻¹ and a backscatter albedo, attached to a fixture with an on and off schedule. From the exteroception text: a ray that crosses a volume of extinction coefficient σ over a length ℓ keeps the fraction exp(−σ·ℓ) of its power one way and exp(−2σ·ℓ) for an active sensor, computed with the deterministic exponential of the kernel dialect. Below the detection threshold the return is masked `attenuated`. Worked case: with σ = 2 m⁻¹ (assumed) and a 5% threshold, an active sensor loses every return behind more than ln(20)/4 = 0.75 m of steam.

The shortcut this prevents (CX-204): attenuating an active sensor once. A lidar or a projected-pattern camera sends light out and receives it back through the same medium, so the loss is over the round trip; the one-way law keeps returns the two-way law loses. Failure-mode row: "Lidar: attenuation in steam and dust; model: optical volumes; first check: slab threshold". Tier: Solid. Backscatter from the volume itself (a false near return) is Frontier and is not built here.

## Scope

In: the path length of a ray segment inside each volume; the attenuation factor; its composition with return strength; the reason attribution; schedules; the slab known answer.

Out: the scene's volume data (the scene chapter, N19a-06); backscatter (N20a-30); class rules (BD-237, N20a-21, a dependency because this bead charges the second segment that N20a-21 produces).

## Technical approach

**1. Path length.** For each ray segment (the first segment, and the second segment of a specular class) and each active volume, the length ℓ_i of the segment inside the volume is computed analytically: slab test for an oriented box (entry and exit parameters clamped to the segment), quadratic for an ellipsoid (transform to the unit sphere, solve, clamp). The number of volumes tested per ray is bounded by the scene's per-zone volume capacity, fixed at environment creation.

**2. Optical depth and factor.** τ = Σ σ_i·ℓ_i over the volumes and over every segment of the ray's path. An active sensor (lidar, time-of-flight, projected pattern) multiplies its return strength by A = exp(−2τ); a passive reading (ambient light reaching a camera) uses exp(−τ). The exponential is the dialect's deterministic function, so the factor is in the same determinism class as the rest of the ray stage.

**3. Decision.** With S the strength the class rule produced and S_min the detection threshold: if S·A ≥ S_min the return is valid (its strength recorded as S·A); if S ≥ S_min and S·A < S_min the mask is `attenuated`; if S < S_min already, the mask is `weak` (the reason names the cause that removed the return).

**4. Schedule.** A volume is active at the ray's own instant (the beam or row instant), read from the fixture's schedule state; the schedule is scene state and is deterministic.

**5. The slab.** A slab of thickness L in front of a white wall at normal incidence at the reference range (S = 1), σ = 2 m⁻¹, S_min = 0.05: the return survives while exp(−4L) ≥ 0.05, that is L ≤ ln(20)/4 = 0.7489 m. At L = 0.70 m the factor is exp(−2.8) = 0.0608 (valid); at L = 0.80 m it is exp(−3.2) = 0.0408 (masked `attenuated`). Under the one-way mutant the wall would stay visible up to ln(20)/2 = 1.498 m.

**6. Kernel.** `K-ray-volume`, float class with CPU reference; mask codes exact.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Slab (GATE-SENS-07 row; tolerance: the transition lies between L = 0.74 m and L = 0.76 m): at σ = 2 m⁻¹ and a 5% threshold an active sensor's return from a unit-strength wall is valid at L = 0.70 m (strength 0.0608) and masked `attenuated` at L = 0.80 m.
- [ ] The one-way mutant (exp(−τ) for an active sensor) keeps the return at L = 0.80 m and at L = 1.00 m and is caught. It is planted on the real `K-ray-volume` stage as the `frob-sense` mutation feature `attenuation-one-way`, the name of CX-204's control in N20-CX (BD-025: implies `conformance`, never a default, never in the `gauntlet-mutations` umbrella; one control per build), and `one_way_mutant_caught` runs as `cargo test -p frob-sense --features attenuation-one-way --test volume`.
- [ ] Path lengths: a ray crossing a 1 m cube along a body diagonal has ℓ = √3 m; through a unit sphere along a chord at half-radius offset, ℓ = √3 m; a ray that ends inside a volume is charged only up to its hit point.
- [ ] Two volumes compose additively in optical depth; a volume switched off by its schedule contributes nothing at instants after the switch.
- [ ] The second segment of a mirror or glass path is charged too: a mirror seen through 0.4 m of steam with the target 0.4 m beyond the mirror, both inside the volume, has τ = σ·0.8.
- [ ] Reason attribution: a dark surface already below threshold is `weak`, not `attenuated`.
- [ ] Results agree across backends inside the class; masks byte-identical on the fixtures.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh` (N20a-46) runs the steam-slab fixture at L = 0.70, 0.75 and 0.80 m with a lidar through `frob sim run`, reads returns and masks through `frob telemetry query`, asserts the outcomes (valid at 0.70 m with strength 0.0608; `attenuated` at 0.75 m, strength 0.0498, and at 0.80 m, strength 0.0408), and logs the measured strength against the 5% threshold and the slab thickness.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-484 (power fraction exp(−2σ·ℓ); every return lost behind more than 0.75 m at σ = 2 m⁻¹ and a 5% threshold; tolerance set by this bead and carried by N20-KA, as above; control: one-way attenuation for an active sensor). CX-204. Metamorphic: splitting one slab into two adjacent slabs of the same σ changes nothing; doubling σ and halving L changes nothing. Determinism: float class of the physics.

## Considerations

- σ is `assumed` until a card records a measurement; real steam is non-uniform and time-varying, and the box or ellipsoid with one σ is a declared idealization.
- Extinction depends on wavelength; the scene's volume carries one σ per sensor band where the card provides it, and the kernel reads the band of the sensor.
- Backscatter (the volume returning light before the surface does, producing a false near point) is Frontier; without it the model errs toward "lost return" and never toward "false obstacle", which the contract states.
