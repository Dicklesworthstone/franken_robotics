## Background

**ADR-SENS-08: a failure with a physical cause is a function of the ray-and-scene interaction and of a latent drawn per surface region and episode, not an independent coin per sample.** Reason: independent coins let repetition average away an error that in a real sensor persists.

**Why causes are not coins.** Two payload classes need different grips, and a sensor reads the class correctly three times in four. With independent errors, a majority of three reads is right 27/32 of the time, of five 459/512, of seven 3,807/4,096 (93%). If the error is common to all reads, as it is when the same pane of glass or the same glare is in every frame, the answer stays right three times in four however many reads are taken. A model that draws dropout independently per sample overstates what repetition buys. Hence the rule: the dropout of a ray through a given region is determined by geometry, the optical card and one latent per region and episode, and repeated reads through the same pane fail together.

This matters beyond perception quality. Sensor-budget decisions ("take seven reads instead of buying a better sensor") reverse depending on whether errors are shared; the analysis that prices redundancy reads the declared error dependence of the cards, not the per-reading accuracy. The latent is how that dependence is declared in the model. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the latent type, its keying and registration; the rule by which a probabilistic failure law consumes it; its presence in the checkpoint; the kernel stage of the `ramp` grazing law, the latent's first consumer, and the card check that admits `ramp` cards; the repeated-reads known answer and its negative control.

Out: the deterministic range stages, the `cut` grazing law, the stage order and the names and formula of the `grazing_law` card field (N20a-20, a dependency, which rejects `ramp` cards until this bead lands); glass draws if adopted (N20a-21, OPEN-SENS-5); the scene's own latents such as wetness (the scene chapter draws them; this bead reads them); the decision-theoretic use of repeated reads under a deadline (N20a-40).

## Technical approach

**1. The latent.** For each (surface region, cause) pair that a sensor card or optical card declares as probabilistic, one uniform value u ∈ [0, 1) per episode, drawn as the exact conversion of a keyed integer:

  u = uniform( philox(world_address, hash(region identifier, cause name), episode index) ).

It is independent of sensors, samples, rays and time within the episode. Cause names are registered (`grazing-dropout`, `glass-return`, `glare`, and any a card adds). Values are stored in the per-world latent slab and are part of every checkpoint, as the clock contract's checkpoint rule requires for these latents.

**2. Consumption rule.** A failure law gives a probability p(geometry, card) for the ray. The ray fails if and only if u_region,cause < p. Consequences: (a) at fixed geometry every read through that region in that episode has the same outcome; (b) as geometry changes (a different incidence angle) the outcome changes monotonically with p, without flicker; (c) across episodes the failure frequency at fixed geometry is p; (d) two sensors of the same modality looking through the same region share the fate, and sensors of a different modality use a different cause name and are independent unless the card declares a shared cause.

**2a. The `ramp` grazing stage.** Inserted at stage 2 of N20a-20's per-ray order (after the range limits, before noise) in `K-sense-depth`, `K-sense-lidar` and `K-sense-hscan`, written as one dialect function of (cos θ, law constants, latent) that every grazing check calls, including the pattern camera's (N20a-23) on the larger of its camera-ray and projector-ray incidences. With cos θ = |u·n| and the host-precomputed constants c₀ = cos θ₀ and c₁ = cos θ₁ (θ₀ < θ₁):

  p(cos θ) = clamp((c₀ − cos θ)/(c₀ − c₁), 0, 1),

which rises linearly in cos θ from 0 at θ₀ to 1 at θ₁. The ray is masked `grazing` when u_region,`grazing-dropout` < p (a guarded predicate). Once this stage exists the card check admits `grazing_law = ramp { theta_0, theta_1 }`, requiring θ₀ < θ₁ ≤ 90°.

**3. Declared dependence.** A sensor card states, per failure cause, its dependence class: `per-region-episode` (the default for anything with a physical cause), `per-sample` (electronic noise only; never for dropout), or `shared-with: <cause>`. The alias and redundancy analyses read this field.

**4. The fixture.** A classification through a pane: in each episode the pane's latent decides, with probability 1/4, that the reading through it is wrong for the whole episode. A consumer takes n reads and votes.

Expected results. With the latent: accuracy 3/4 for n = 1, 3, 5, 7 (within the binomial interval over episodes). With the independent-coin mutant: 3/4, 27/32, 459/512, 3,807/4,096. The exact fractions follow from Σ over k > n/2 of C(n, k)·(3/4)ᵏ·(1/4)ⁿ⁻ᵏ: for n = 3, 27/64 + 27/64 = 27/32; for n = 5, (270 + 405 + 243)/1,024 = 459/512; for n = 7, 3,807/4,096.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Repeated reads through one pane fail together (GATE-SENS-07 clause): in 4,096 episodes, every episode's n = 7 reads through the pane are all right or all wrong; the fraction of right episodes lies in the 99.9% binomial interval around 3/4.
- [ ] The independent-coin mutant is caught: its majority-of-seven accuracy lies near 3,807/4,096 = 0.929, outside the interval around 3/4. It is planted on the real latent consumption rule as the `frob-sense` mutation feature `dropout-independent-coins`, the name of CX-203's control in N20-CX (BD-025: implies `conformance`, never a default, never in the `gauntlet-mutations` umbrella; one control per build), and `independent_coin_mutant_caught` runs as `cargo test -p frob-sense --features dropout-independent-coins --test latent`.
- [ ] The exhaustive enumeration of the independent case reproduces 27/32, 459/512 and 3,807/4,096 exactly in rational arithmetic (the oracle the mutant is compared with).
- [ ] A latent's value is unchanged by adding sensors, by the number of rays cast, by batch size, slot and backend; it changes with the episode index and with the region.
- [ ] Monotonicity: sweeping incidence across the `ramp` grazing law in one episode gives exactly one transition from valid to masked.
- [ ] `ramp` frequency: with θ₀ = 60° and θ₁ = 85°, at the incidence where p = 0.25 the fraction of 4,096 episodes in which the ray is masked lies in [0.228, 0.272] (99.9% binomial interval), and every read inside one episode agrees; a `ramp` card is admitted and a card with θ₀ ≥ θ₁ is refused.
- [ ] Latents survive checkpoint and restore byte for byte; every probabilistic dropout law in the sensor kernels consumes a latent and none draws per sample (a lint on the kernel sources).
- [ ] Each sensor card's failure causes carry a dependence class, and `per-sample` on a dropout cause is refused by the card check.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/latent.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense --test latent`.
- `majority_oracle_exact`: 27/32, 459/512, 3,807/4,096 by enumeration in rationals.
- `reads_fail_together`: 4,096 episodes × 7 reads; zero mixed episodes.
- `episode_accuracy_three_quarters`: within [0.727, 0.773] (99.9% interval for 4,096 trials at 3/4).
- `independent_coin_mutant_caught`: mutant accuracy above 0.9.
- `latent_key_independence`: add a sensor, double the rays, change the slot: same u.
- `latent_changes_with_episode_and_region`.
- `ramp_single_transition`.
- `ramp_formula_values`: θ₀ = 60°, θ₁ = 85°: p = 0 at 60° and below, p = 1 at 85° and beyond, p = 0.5 at cos θ = (c₀ + c₁)/2 = 0.29358.
- `ramp_frequency_quarter`: the acceptance interval above; `ramp_per_sample_coin_mutant_caught`: a build that draws a fresh coin per sample shows mixed episodes and fails.
- `ramp_card_admission`: `ramp { 60°, 85° }` admitted; `ramp { 85°, 60° }` refused.
- `two_sensors_same_modality_share_fate`; `different_modality_independent` (correlation of fates over 4,096 episodes within 0.05 of zero).
- `checkpoint_roundtrip_latents`.
- `dependence_class_required`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-486 (27/32, 459/512, 3,807/4,096 with independent errors; 3/4 with a common fault; control: independent coins through one pane; shares its fixture with the construction-level row KA-113). CX-203. Metamorphic: permuting the order of reads within an episode changes nothing; renaming a region changes its latent (a region's name is its identity). Determinism: latents are exact-integer draws, class D0.

## Considerations

- A per-episode latent makes failures persistent within an episode and variable across episodes, which is the regime a robust policy must handle; a task may also pin a latent to a value to study a specific fault.
- Partial dependence (errors that are correlated but not identical) is expressed by combining a shared cause with a per-region cause; anything finer needs a measured joint law, which the mission chapter's channel-law mechanism owns.
- The same construction is used by fault injection (a region-level glare fault) and by the adversarial search, which may set latents within their legal range.
