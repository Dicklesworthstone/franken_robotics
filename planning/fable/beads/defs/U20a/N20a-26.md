## Background

With midpoint sampling a depth image of a moving scene has crisp edges in a slightly wrong place. A real depth camera integrates over its exposure; a pixel that saw the foreground for part of the exposure and the background for the rest returns a depth between them, a "flying pixel", or nothing. From the camera tier table: "[F] Exposure integration with two or four sub-instants per row, with a pixel masked `mixed` when its sub-samples disagree by more than a threshold (flying pixels at depth edges)." Failure-mode rows served: "Depth, projected pattern: … flying pixels" and "Camera: rolling-shutter skew, blur, long exposure in dim light; first check: moving bar".

Tier: Frontier. Benefit ceiling: none on speed; it changes what a perception policy must tolerate. It is built only if its pilot's verdict is `build` (N20a-25) and KILL-EXPOSURE has not fired (N20a-27). Kill gate: more than 25% added to the ray stage, or no change in any mask statistic beyond noise; fallback: midpoint sampling (the Solid model of BD-188).

## Scope

In: sub-instant schedule per row; agreement test and the `mixed` mask; candidate selection that keeps cost bounded and the launch shape fixed; the measured cost; the moving-bar check through the real kernels.

Out: the pilot; the decision; radiometric blur of RGB images (N20a-28).

## Technical approach

**1. Sub-instants.** For a row r with exposure [t_s + r·t_row, t_s + r·t_row + t_exp) and J ∈ {2, 4} from the card: t_{r,j} = t_s + r·t_row + (j + ½)·t_exp/J, j = 0…J − 1. Each sub-instant's rays use the pose from the segmented pose history at that instant; nothing is blended across a boundary at which an impact reset a velocity.

**2. Agreement.** For a pixel with sub-sample depths z_0 … z_{J−1} (each already processed by the optical rules and possibly masked): if all are valid and max − min ≤ δ_mix (card field, metres, or a fraction of depth), the pixel's value is their mean; if valid sub-samples disagree by more than δ_mix, the pixel is masked `mixed`; if some sub-samples are masked and others valid, the pixel takes the mask reason of the first masked sub-sample (a pane that covered the pixel for part of the exposure) and counts as `mixed` in the statistics channel.

**3. Bounded cost** (DESIGN proposed here: the plan fixes the sub-instant counts, the `mixed` rule and the 25% limit, not how the limit is met).
1. Pass 1 is the midpoint image of BD-188 (every pixel, one ray).
2. Candidates: pixels whose midpoint depth differs from a 4-neighbour's by more than δ_mix, dilated by the largest image-space sweep the motion can produce during the exposure, B_max = f·‖v_rel‖·t_exp/z (computed per frame from the camera's twist and the scene's moving primitives, capped by a card maximum).
3. The candidate list has a fixed capacity C per image (a card or profile field; default 6% of pixels for J = 4 and 12% for J = 2, so that the worst case stays under 25% extra rays). Candidates are compacted with the shared count-scan-write kernel; pass 2 casts J rays for each listed pixel (for J = 2 and 4 the midpoint is not one of the sub-instants; the midpoint value is kept only for non-candidates).
4. Overflow: candidates beyond C keep their midpoint value and the image carries the flag `integration-truncated` with the overflow count. Nothing is silently dropped.

Because C is fixed, the ray launch has a fixed shape and its worst-case cost is C·J rays per image, declared in advance.

**4. Measured cost.** The receipt reports ray-stage time with integration on and off for the same frames; the ratio minus one is the "added ray cost" that KILL-EXPOSURE reads (threshold 25%).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot's `build` verdict and the kill decision's `not fired` record predate the first commit; otherwise the bead is closed as `fallback` (midpoint sampling).
- [ ] Moving bar through the real kernels: with f = 600 pixels, z₁ = 1 m, z₂ = 2 m, t_exp = 10 ms, v = 1 m/s and J = 4, each row has 9 ± 2 `mixed` pixels (two edges × 6 × 3/4); at v = 0 it has none; midpoint sampling has none at any speed.
- [ ] Agreement: a pixel whose sub-samples lie within δ_mix reports their mean; beyond δ_mix it is `mixed` and carries no number.
- [ ] Added ray-stage cost at the default capacities is at most 25% on the reference camera and battery (measured, recorded); the worst-case bound C·J is reported in `sense.profile`.
- [ ] Overflow sets `integration-truncated` with the count; a test with a tiny capacity shows the flag and no change in non-candidate pixels.
- [ ] Across an impact, sub-instant poses follow the segmented history (the linear-blend mutant produces `mixed` pixels on a scene that did not move after the impact and is caught).
- [ ] Reasons and flags are byte-identical across backends on the fixture; values inside the class.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/exposure_integration.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense --features frontier-exposure exposure_integration`.
- `sub_instants_J2_J4`: for t_exp = 8 ms, J = 4 gives offsets 1, 3, 5, 7 ms; J = 2 gives 2 and 6 ms.
- `agree_within_delta_mean`: depths (1.000, 1.002, 1.001, 1.003) with δ_mix = 0.01 give 1.0015.
- `disagree_masks_mixed`: depths (1.0, 1.0, 2.0, 2.0) give `mixed`.
- `partial_mask_inherits_reason`: (valid, valid, `pattern-through-glass`, `pattern-through-glass`) gives that reason and increments the mixed statistic.
- `moving_bar_mixed_count`: 9 ± 2 per row.
- `static_scene_identical_to_midpoint`: byte-equal images at v = 0.
- `candidate_dilation_covers_band`: every `mixed` pixel of an exhaustive all-pixel integration is in the candidate list.
- `capacity_overflow_flag`.
- `cost_bound_reported`: C·J rays.
- `impact_segmented_no_false_mixed`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_camera_timing.sh` (N20a-45) has an integration stage, run only when the feature is funded: the moving-bar scene through `frob sim run` with integration on and off; it asserts the `mixed` counts, reads the two ray-stage times from the receipt and logs the added-cost ratio against 0.25, the candidate fraction and any truncation; summary gate `KILL-EXPOSURE`.

### Known answers and negative controls
The pilot's closed forms (band width B = f·v·t_exp/z₁; `mixed` count 2·B·3/4 per row). Negative controls: sub-instants all placed at the midpoint (no `mixed` pixels at any speed: must fail the moving-bar test); candidate selection without dilation (misses `mixed` pixels that the exhaustive reference finds: must fail `candidate_dilation_covers_band`). Determinism: masks exact; values in the physics' class; batch-independent.

## Considerations

- The trigger stays armed after the decision: if the measured added cost on a device exceeds 25% at the capacities needed to avoid truncation on the standard battery, the gate fires then and midpoint sampling is the product.
- `mixed` pixels reduce valid coverage; a policy trained with them sees holes at moving edges, which is the realism the row buys.
- The candidate capacity is a declared approximation; `integration-truncated` in more than a small share of frames on a task is a finding that the task needs a larger capacity or J = 2.
