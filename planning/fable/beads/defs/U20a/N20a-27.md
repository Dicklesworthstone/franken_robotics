## Background

KILL-EXPOSURE governs the Frontier mechanism "exposure integration" (N20a-26): two or four sub-instants per camera row, with a pixel masked `mixed` when its sub-samples disagree. A kill gate names its trigger and its fallback in advance and is never quietly re-scoped; its decision is recorded as an ADR at the milestone the gate register names.

**Trigger (either):** exposure integration costs more than 25% of the ray stage; or it changes no mask statistic beyond noise. **Fallback:** midpoint sampling (each row sampled once, at the midpoint of its exposure; the Solid camera model). **Decided:** M2.

Why these triggers. The mechanism claims no speed benefit; its only value is that images contain the holes real images contain at moving depth edges. If a policy's inputs do not change measurably (no mask statistic moves beyond the sensor's own noise over the speeds the hazard lists imply), the mechanism buys nothing; if it adds more than a quarter to the most expensive sensing stage, it costs more than a guard without a measured benefit should.

The same cost rule is the fallback rule for the other Frontier sensor models that are on no task's path before M5 (tactile arrays, event cameras, radiometric RGB, retroreflector bloom, backscatter from optical volumes): each falls back to its mask-only model if it adds more than 25% to the stage it joins. This decision is one step more urgent than its milestone because the camera-timing seed bead's exit records it.

## Scope

In: the frozen rule; reading the pilot record (mask statistics against noise; the ray-count cost estimate); the ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-EXPOSURE-M2.md`; the decision entry on the kill row; the owner declaration N40-30 compares with the ledger; consequences on each branch; the re-arming rule for the measured device cost.

Out: the pilot (N20a-25); the mechanism (N20a-26); the KILL-EXPOSURE row itself in `registries/gates.toml` with its trigger, fallback, decided, mode and reads (the fallback ledger N40-24, a dependency; N20b-21 writes nothing in a kill record: this bead records the decision and its ADR against that row, and BD-188 cites the outcome in its gate record).

## Technical approach

**1. The fallback, defined first.** Midpoint sampling: row r of a frame sampled at t_s is cast from the sensor pose at t_s + r·t_row + t_exp/2. It represents motion during exposure as a displacement, produces no `mixed` pixels, costs one retraction per row and no additional rays, and is what GATE-SENS-06 measures. It ships on either branch.

**2. Freeze the rule before data.** Registered before the pilot runs, inside the pilot's frozen protocol (N20a-25 carries it, because this bead starts only after the pilot has run; this bead verifies the digest and applies the rule unchanged): the speed range (from the hazard lists; default 0.05 to 3 m/s) and exposures {2, 8, 16} ms; "changes no mask statistic beyond noise" means the pilot's falsifier holds (differences in depth error below σ_z and in masked-pixel fraction below the static baseline's sampling variation, at every speed and exposure); "costs more than 25% of the ray stage" is read at M2 from the pilot's ray-count estimate for the cheapest configuration that avoids truncation on the fixture (two or four sub-instants, candidate-only casting), and is read again from the measured ray-stage ratio once the mechanism exists. Reachable outcomes (N40-03): `not-fired`, and `fired` with one of two branches named in the ADR, "no mask statistic changed" or "cost above 25%".

**3. Decide** (numbered steps).
1. Read the pilot record (`PILOT-SENSE-<nn>` of N20a-25, by its `[[pilot.result]]`). If its status is `falsifier-holds` (verdict `do not build`): `fired`, branch "no mask statistic changed".
2. Otherwise read the cost estimates. If every configuration that reproduces the pilot's `mixed` statistics without truncation costs more than 25% extra rays: `fired`, branch "cost above 25%".
3. Otherwise `not-fired`, with the admissible configurations listed (for example: four sub-instants with a candidate capacity of 6% of pixels; two sub-instants with 12%).
4. Write `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-EXPOSURE-M2.md` with N40-03's required fields (the trigger as registered and its amendments, each measured value with its receipt hash beside the trigger's number, the outcome and branch, the fallback in force, the beads re-read, what changes downstream, the deciding session, which is not a session that implemented N20a-25 or N20a-26) and the pilot's tables; enter the decision on the kill row as `decision = { outcome = "not-fired" | "fired", adr = "docs/adr/ADR-KILL-EXPOSURE-M2.md", date, session, receipts }` (the receipts of the pilot's result); and write the owner declaration in the block N40-30 compares with the ledger: `[[gate]] id = "KILL-EXPOSURE"`, `role = "owner"`, `trigger` and `fallback` carrying N40-24's text ("Exposure integration costs more than 25% of the ray stage, or changes no mask statistic beyond noise"; "Midpoint sampling"), `decided = "M2"`, `mode = "exit"`, `reads` naming the pilot record, with `decision = { outcome, adr = "ADR-KILL-EXPOSURE-M2", measured = [the depth-error and mask-fraction differences against their noise scales, the cost fractions for two and four sub-instants, each with value, unit and receipt] }`. The status `killed` of the gates the row reads, if any, is set by N40-03's `apply_decisions`, not written here.
5. Re-arming: after N20a-26 lands, its receipt carries the measured added ray-stage cost on the reference camera and battery; if it exceeds 25%, this rule, applied unchanged, gives `fired` (branch "cost above 25%"), recorded as a second decision entry appended to the row (`exit = "M3"`, ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-EXPOSURE-M3.md`) by a session that did not implement N20a-26; the M2 entry and its ADR are kept, never edited (N40-03 step 1). N20a-26, which depends on this bead, carries that check in its acceptance.

**4. Downstream.**
- `not-fired`: N20a-26 proceeds within the listed configurations; BD-188 cites the outcome in its gate record.
- `fired`: N20a-26 closes `withdrawn` citing the pilot's `[[pilot.result]]` on the branch "no mask statistic changed" (it was never funded), or `fallback` on the branch "cost above 25%" (N41-17); the `mixed` reason code stays reserved and unused; `sense.profile` reports "exposure: midpoint" for every camera; the flying-pixel part of the failure-mode table is marked "not modeled (fallback)" on sensor cards; BD-188 cites the outcome. No gate of the Solid tier changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rule applied (ranges, the two readings of the trigger, the verdict set, the re-arming clause) is the one frozen in the pilot's protocol, whose digest and date predate the pilot's first run; a rule that differs from the frozen one is refused by the decision function.
- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-EXPOSURE-M2.md` exists at M2 with N40-03's required fields, the outcome and the pilot's tables; the KILL-EXPOSURE row of `registries/gates.toml` carries one decision entry whose `adr` is that path and whose receipts resolve in the artifact graph; the bead closes on either outcome.
- [ ] The owner declaration matches the ledger under `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (trigger and fallback equal N40-24's text; `decided = "M2"`; the outcome only in `decision`).
- [ ] On `fired`, N20a-26 is closed `withdrawn` (branch "no mask statistic changed") or `fallback` (branch "cost above 25%") and sensor cards and `sense.profile` state the fallback; on `not-fired`, the admissible configurations are recorded and N20a-26 is unblocked.
- [ ] BD-188's exit record cites the decision.
- [ ] The re-arming rule is stated in the ADR as a second appended entry (exit M3, `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-EXPOSURE-M3.md`) that N20a-26's measured ratio can trigger; no entry is ever edited.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_exposure_rule.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_exposure_rule`.
- `verdict_no_statistic`: a synthetic pilot record with status `falsifier-holds` gives `fired`, branch "no mask statistic changed".
- `verdict_cost`: a record whose cheapest non-truncating configuration costs +46.3% gives `fired`, branch "cost above 25%".
- `verdict_not_fired`: a record with a +23.1% configuration gives `not-fired` and lists it.
- `rearm_on_measured_cost`: a measured ratio of 1.31 after a `not-fired` decision gives a second entry `fired` with `exit = "M3"` and ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-EXPOSURE-M3.md`; the M2 entry is byte-identical before and after.
- `decision_entry_form`: the entry has `outcome`, `adr` (the step-4 path), `date`, `session` and at least one receipt for `not-fired`; an `adr` of another path fails as `decision-adr-path`.
- `rule_frozen_before_run`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_exposure_decide.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/kill_exposure_decide/`): the pilot register and the gate register have no `frob` verb (ADR-GAUNT-01), so the script reads N20a-25's `PILOT-SENSE-<nn>` record of `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (through `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example pilots -- fundable --bead N20a-26 --json`) and the KILL-EXPOSURE row of `registries/gates.toml` read-only, runs `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_exposure_rule`, `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (the owner declaration against the ledger) and N40-03's read-only `scripts/e2e/kill_gate_records.sh --live --ids` on the KILL-EXPOSURE identifier (the row is one complete kill row whose trigger and fallback text equal the seeded text), and drives `frob --json evidence trace <claim_id>` on the claim the row's receipt cites (the closure's hashes must recompute); it asserts that the outcome of the decision entry in force equals the one recomputed from the pilot record and, when the mechanism exists, from the measured ratio, and that `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-EXPOSURE-M2.md` exists; it writes no record; logs the cost fractions against 0.25 and the statistic differences against their noise scales; summary names `KILL-EXPOSURE`.

### Known answers and negative controls
The pilot's arithmetic (a 640-pixel row with two edges and an 18-pixel sweep has 74 candidate pixels, 11.6% of the row: +46.3% with four sub-instant rays each, +23.1% with two). Negative control: a pilot record whose tables were edited after execution fails its receipt digest and is refused by the decision function.

## Considerations

- The decision is made on a paper-scale estimate at M2 and confirmed on a device later; both readings are recorded so that a reader can see which one decided.
- A task with faster relative motion than the hazard lists imply can re-open the question with its own range; that is a new, dated amendment, not a quiet change of threshold.
- Whichever way it goes, images of moving scenes remain displaced correctly by the rolling-shutter and midpoint model; what is at stake is only the mixed pixels at edges.
