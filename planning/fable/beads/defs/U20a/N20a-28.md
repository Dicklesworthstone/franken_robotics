## Background

Two Frontier items concern colour images.

- **RGB cameras for policies [F].** Rasterized textured meshes with the radiometric model: shot and read noise, glare, lens distortion. Not needed by any task before M5.
- **Hero renders [F].** FrankenSim's spectral path tracer, offline only (FACT: its `tracer` feature is Frontier-gated and off by default).

Neither is on the critical path. Depth, lidar, height scans and labels serve every task before M5, and they are ray queries against the collision field. RGB is different in kind: it needs the *visual* mesh and its textures, which the rest of Sensorium deliberately does not read (ADR-SENS-06: sensors read the collision field, the overlays and the optical map, never the visual mesh). An RGB policy camera is therefore a declared exception with its own identity and its own check against the collision-to-visual deviation report; a hero render is not a sensor at all, and ADR-SENS-01 forbids using it as one (observations never come from a renderer or a viewer).

Rule for Frontier sensor models off every task's path before M5: funded only when a registered task needs it; one analytic fixture as its pilot; no speed benefit to claim; fallback to the mask-only model if it adds more than 25% to the stage it joins. Ceiling of the benefit: none on speed; it widens the set of policies that can be trained (those that need appearance) and is bounded by the share of registered tasks that cannot be done from depth and labels, which is zero before M5.

## Scope

In: the radiometric sensor model applied to a rasterized image; lens distortion; the exception's guard rails (identity, deviation bound, privacy); the route for hero renders and the rule that keeps them out of observations; the analytic checks.

Out: the rasterizer and textured or splat scene assets (the RGB epic BD-420, this bead's parent, and the scene chapter); the epic's plan with its kill gate and the owner's funding decision (N19b-23) and its pilot (N19b-24), both dependencies of this bead; stills drawn from the collision field (N20a-33).

## Technical approach

**1. Radiometric chain** (per pixel and colour channel; DESIGN sketch, parameters on a card of kind `sensor.rgb` with per-field provenance and all values `assumed` until recorded).
1. Scene irradiance E on the pixel from the rasterized, lit image (linear units), integrated over the exposure (midpoint or integrated, as for depth cameras; automatic exposure from the zone's lighting level).
2. Signal electrons: n_e = η·E·A·t_exp/E_photon-equivalent, summarized on the card as a conversion constant κ so that n_e = κ·E·t_exp.
3. Glare: a veiling term g·Ē added to every pixel, with Ē the mean of pixels above the saturation level before clipping (a declared, crude lens-flare model), plus bloom to 4-neighbours of saturated pixels [optional].
4. Shot noise: variance n_e (Gaussian approximation with keyed draws; exact Poisson below a card threshold of 20 electrons through a keyed inverse table).
5. Read noise: variance σ_r², added in electrons.
6. Full well: clip at n_full, flag `saturated`.
7. Gain and quantization: DN = round(n/K_g), clipped to the bit depth.

**2. Lens distortion.** Applied in the projection: for normalized coordinates (x, y) with r² = x² + y², x_d = x·(1 + k₁·r² + k₂·r⁴) + 2·p₁·x·y + p₂·(r² + 2x²), y_d likewise with p₁ and p₂ exchanged. The same polynomial is offered to depth cameras' pixel directions.

**3. Guard rails for the exception.** (a) An RGB policy camera is a registered sensor kind whose identity includes the visual assets' digests; it is part of the observation service like any sensor and obeys the clock contract, masks, namespaces and keyed noise. (b) A region whose collision-to-visual deviation exceeds the pack's threshold is rendered with a warning flag on the affected pixels' region channel, and a task may refuse such scenes. (c) Images of a private scene are private.

**4. Hero renders.** An offline export for humans: a recorded snapshot is sent through the airlock to FrankenSim's path tracer when the workspace is built with the tracer feature; the result is a presentation artifact with the three no-authorization flags, never registered as a sensor, never readable by a policy or a requirement. DESIGN: exposed as a quality option of the stills verb available only in such a build; a request without the feature is refused as "capability not built" (exit code 5).

**5. Analytic checks (the fixture named as pilot for the radiometric part).** Photon transfer on a uniform grey card: for mean signal n_e electrons, temporal variance in electrons is n_e + σ_r², so variance in DN against mean in DN is a line of slope 1/K_g. Worked value: n_e = 10,000 and σ_r = 10 give a signal-to-noise ratio 10,000/√10,100 = 99.5.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Work starts only after the RGB epic's pilot verdict and funding decision are on record; until then the bead stays open with that reason.
- [ ] Photon transfer: over 64 exposure levels the fitted slope of variance against mean equals 1/K_g within 2% and the intercept equals σ_r²/K_g² within 10%; the signal-to-noise ratio at 10,000 electrons is 99.5 ± 1.
- [ ] Distortion: with k₁ = −0.1 a point at normalized radius 1 images at radius 0.9; the inverse map recovers it within 10⁻⁶.
- [ ] Saturation and glare: a pixel above full well reads the maximum code with `saturated`; with g = 0 no veiling appears; with g > 0 the dark region's mean rises by g·Ē.
- [ ] An RGB camera cannot be created without visual asset digests in its identity; a hero render cannot be bound to an observation channel (refused with `FROB-REF-442`).
- [ ] Added cost above 25% of the stage it joins triggers the fallback (a mask-and-label image without radiometry), recorded.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/rgb.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense --features frontier-rgb rgb`.
- `photon_transfer_slope`, `snr_at_10k`: 99.5.
- `read_noise_floor`: at zero signal the standard deviation is σ_r/K_g in DN.
- `full_well_clip_flag`.
- `distortion_forward_inverse`.
- `glare_veiling_level`.
- `noise_keyed`: adding a second camera leaves the first image's noise unchanged.
- `hero_render_not_a_sensor`: binding refused with 442.
- `identity_includes_asset_digests`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_rgb.sh` (STD-E2E helper; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/sense_rgb/`), not run until funded: `frob sim run` on the grey-card fixture at 64 exposure levels; `frob telemetry query` exports images; the conformance tool fits the photon-transfer line; the script asserts slope, intercept and signal-to-noise ratio, logs them with thresholds and the stage-cost ratio against 1.25; a second stage requests a hero render without the feature and asserts exit code 5.

### Known answers and negative controls
The photon-transfer relation and the 99.5 ratio. Negative controls: a model that adds shot noise with constant variance (the slope is zero: fails); a camera wired to the viewer's frame buffer (refused with 442; the noninterference test would also fail). Determinism: image bytes in the physics' class; keyed noise independent of batch.

## Considerations

- The sim-to-real gap for RGB is dominated by appearance, lighting and materials, not by sensor noise; the radiometric model is necessary and far from sufficient, and the contract says so.
- Licences of textures and splat assets are tracked by the scene chapter's asset register; a pack without a licence record cannot feed an RGB camera.
- At most four late epics are active at once under the program's rule; this bead claims no slot before a registered task exists.
