## Background

Agents and people need to look at a simulation. Sensorium supplies two things for that, and keeps both from ever becoming inputs to the simulation.

- **Stills for agents.** `frob telemetry stills` draws contact sheets (several views at key frames) with the same ray kernels as the sensors, shaded simply, together with an identifier buffer. `frob telemetry inspect-pixel` maps a pixel to a link, a fixture, a region or a contact. A vision-capable agent can look and then ask (FACT: the loop a sibling project uses for garments). Stills are drawn from the collision field, so they show what physics has, and they are private when the scene is.
- **The viewer.** It draws physical overlays from the same snapshots: contact forces and friction cones, film thickness, sensor frusta and individual rays coloured by mask reason, unknown regions, the collision-to-visual deviation map, and the bottleneck of a clearance cut. The viewer never feeds anything back to physics: the simulator owns state and the viewer reads immutable snapshots.

Why stills come from the collision field and not from the visual mesh: an agent debugging a robot that trips on nothing must see the geometry the contact solver has, not the geometry an artist drew; the difference between the two is itself a reported quantity (the collision-to-visual deviation). The viewer draws the visual asset and can overlay that deviation as a heat map.

The rule above both (ADR-SENS-01, INV-SENS-01): observations come only from the observation service; no observation depends on presentation state (viewer, stills, telemetry level or wall clock). Tier: Solid. Hero renders and RGB policy cameras are Frontier (N20a-28).

## Scope

In: the still renderer and the identifier buffer in `frob-sense::present`; pixel lookup; the overlay data Sensorium owns (sensor frusta, rays by mask reason, unknown regions hit, the seven instants of a sample for a timeline); the structural guarantee that none of it can affect a run; privacy marking.

Out: the verbs, their schemas, key-frame selection and contact-sheet layout policy (`telemetry.stills`, BD-191, and `telemetry.inspect-pixel`, N24a-40, both of which depend on this bead); the viewer page and the overlays owned by other subsystems (contact cones, film, clearance, energy; the viewer beads N32b-10 and N32b-11); the per-row and per-beam poses the overlay honours (BD-188, a dependency); the noninterference battery (BD-215).

## Technical approach

**1. Still renderer** (DESIGN). Input: an immutable snapshot (committed poses of all primitives, scene identity, contact set), a list of virtual cameras (pose, intrinsics, resolution; presets: orbit, top, side, robot-follow, sensor's-eye for a named sensor), and options. For each pixel one ray is cast with the standard first-hit ray kernels against the scene distance field, overlays and moving primitives. Output per view: a shaded colour image and an identifier buffer.
- *Shading:* flat colour by identifier kind, modulated by |n·u| (a headlight Lambert term); unknown regions hatched; optional depth fog. No textures, no lighting model.
- *Identifier buffer:* per pixel a packed 32-bit value: kind ∈ {none, link, fixture, region, contact} in the top bits, index in the rest, plus a parallel range buffer. Contacts are drawn as small discs at contact points so that they are pickable.
- *Contact sheet:* views × key frames tiled into one image with a caption table; the tiling is deterministic.

**2. Pixel lookup.** `inspect_pixel(still, x, y)` returns the identifier (kind, index, the object's name), the range and world point, and for a contact its telemetry address. The telemetry verb adds the quantity under an overlay; this function supplies the geometry.

**3. Sensing overlay data** (a pure function of a snapshot and the recorded packets): for each sensor, its frustum or pattern envelope as line segments; a decimated set of its rays (cap per sensor) with origin, end point and mask reason code, so the viewer can colour by reason; the unknown regions any ray ended in; and for the latest packet of each sensor the seven instants (sample, exposure, timestamp, arrival, decision, command, actuation where a command resulted), for a timeline. Rolling-shutter and per-beam poses are honoured, so a drawn ray is the ray the sensor cast.

**4. No way back.** The functions take `&Snapshot` and write only to their own output buffers. They run on the host from snapshot copies, or as kernels that read snapshot slabs and never the live state slabs. They are not part of the step plan, hold no clock, and draw no keyed random numbers. Attaching them changes neither the observation service identity nor any digest.

**5. Privacy.** Outputs inherit the scene's privacy class; a still of a private scene is a private artifact and is excluded from logs and exports unless the caller holds the scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A still of the reference quadruped on a plane, from the four preset cameras, has an identifier buffer in which a pixel on each named link maps to that link, a pixel on the floor to its region and a pixel on a contact disc to that contact (the seed stills bead's done-condition, "a pixel maps to the right link in fixtures", is met through this buffer).
- [ ] Stills show the collision field: on a fixture whose visual mesh has a decorative part with no collision counterpart, the part is absent from the still; a collision-only blocker is present.
- [ ] Sensor's-eye view of a depth camera reproduces that sensor's valid-pixel mask for the same instant (same rays, same hits).
- [ ] Overlay data: every listed ray's end point equals the sensor's recorded hit for that ray, and its reason code equals the packet's mask code; on a fixture with a pane whose optical class has no ray rule yet and a wall beyond `range_max`, the pane's rays carry `unsupported-optical-class` and the far wall's rays `beyond-range` at M2 (from M3 the same fixture shows the glass rule's outcome for that pane, with no change to this bead); on a rolling-shutter camera yawing at 2 rad/s, the first and last rows' drawn rays start from the two different row poses.
- [ ] Drawing stills, attaching the viewer feed, or changing the telemetry level leaves every physics digest and every delivered observation unchanged over 10⁴ steps (INV-SENS-01 at unit level).
- [ ] A still of a private scene carries the private mark, and the end-to-end log of a run on a private scene contains no image bytes or geometry.
- [ ] Rendering the same snapshot twice gives identical bytes; on two backends, identical identifier buffers on the fixtures.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/present.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-sense present`.
- `id_buffer_links`: 12 named links of the quadruped each hit by a pixel aimed at its centroid.
- `id_buffer_region_and_contact`.
- `collision_not_visual`: decorative part absent, collision-only blocker present.
- `sensor_eye_matches_sensor_mask`.
- `overlay_rays_match_packets`; `overlay_reason_codes` (`unsupported-optical-class` on the pane, `beyond-range` on the far wall); `overlay_rolling_shutter_row_poses` (first-row and last-row ray origins differ by the yaw the camera turned during readout).
- `seven_instants_timeline`: the timeline of the seven-instant fixture's first packet equals its recorded instants.
- `no_state_write`: the functions compile only against `&Snapshot` (a compile-fail test passes a mutable environment).
- `presentation_noninterference_unit`: digests equal with and without stills every step.
- `private_scene_marks_output`.
- `deterministic_bytes`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_present.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; seed-explicit; idempotent; NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/sense_present/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript). The verbs that show these stills to a user (`telemetry.stills`, BD-191; `telemetry.inspect-pixel`, N24a-40) depend on this bead, so the script drives the library through a small example binary of the crate (`cargo run -p frob-sense --example present_still`, DESIGN name; its own options, not options of a `frob` verb), which runs a fixture on the referee to a snapshot and calls the public functions on it:
1. `present_still --fixture quad-plane --seed 1 --views orbit,top,side,follow --json`: a pixel aimed at each of the 12 named links, one on the floor and one on a contact disc map to that link, region and contact.
2. `present_still --fixture decorative-part --views orbit --json`: the decorative part is absent from the identifier buffer and the collision-only blocker present.
3. `present_still --fixture pane-beyond-range --views sensor:depth --overlay sensing --json`: the sensor's-eye valid-pixel mask equals the depth camera's recorded mask; every overlay ray's end point and reason code equal the packet's (`unsupported-optical-class` on the pane, `beyond-range` on the far wall at M2).
4. `present_still --fixture yaw-rolling --overlay sensing --json`: first-row and last-row ray origins differ by the yaw turned during readout.
5. `present_still --fixture quad-plane --ticks 10000 --digests --stills-every-step` and the same without stills: equal physics and delivered-observation digests at every step.
6. `present_still --fixture private-room --views orbit --json`: the output carries the private mark; the script then asserts that its own log holds no image bytes or geometry.
7. Step 1 twice: identical image and identifier-buffer bytes.
Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N20a-33"), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; `metric` lines give identifiers, counts and digests (never image content); a final `summary` line counts assertions and gives `gates: []`. From the user's side the same checks run as a stage of `scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh` (N20a-46, which depends on BD-191 and N24a-40): a still with the sensing overlay on the glass-pane fixture, three `inspect-pixel` queries, and a repeat with stills disabled under a different idempotency key.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-505 (presentation noninterference: every physics digest and every delivered observation unchanged when the viewer, stills or the telemetry level is turned on or off) is measured on every tier by BD-215; this bead's unit test is its first line of defence. Negative controls: a still renderer that advances a noise counter (digests change: caught); an identifier buffer built from the visual mesh (the decorative part appears: caught). Determinism: identifier buffers exact; shaded images in the ray kernels' class.

## Considerations

- Stills are for understanding, not for measurement; anything quantitative comes from telemetry channels with units and evidence classes.
- Ray-cast stills at 640 × 480 for four views cost about as much as one depth camera for four frames; they are drawn on demand from snapshots, never every step.
- The decimation cap on overlay rays is a presentation choice; the full ray set is in telemetry at the level that records payloads.
- If a task ever needs a human-quality image, that is the hero-render route (offline, Frontier), and it is not an observation either.
