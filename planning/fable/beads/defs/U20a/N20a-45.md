## Background

From M2 a camera that feeds a policy has an exposure and a rolling shutter, and each lidar beam fires at its own instant. The user-visible consequence is that images and scans of a moving robot are skewed and smeared the way real ones are. This bead is the end-to-end script that shows it from the user's side and measures GATE-SENS-06: skew within 0.5 pixel of 19.2; seam displacement within 1 cm of 0.5 m; no fabricated blur across an impact; at most 5% added ray-stage cost.

The hand-checkable known answers behind it: a camera with a focal length of 600 pixels and a 16 ms readout that yaws at 2 rad/s images a vertical edge near the optical axis with a skew of 600 × 2 × 0.016 = 19.2 pixels from the first row to the last; a lidar spinning at 10 Hz on a robot yawing at 1 rad/s turns 0.1 rad during a scan, so a wall 5 m away is displaced by 0.5 m across the scan seam.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/sense_camera_timing.sh`, fixtures, assertions, log; an optional stage for exposure integration that runs only if that Frontier mechanism was funded.

Out: unit tests; kernels.

## Technical approach

**1. Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/sense_camera_timing.sh [--tier batch-cpu|cuda|metal|referee] [--seed N]`. Plain shell, shared helper, idempotent, seed-explicit, non-zero exit on failure. `$T` is the tier as `frob` spells it: the script's `cuda` and `metal` are passed as `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal`. Every `frob sim run` carries its own `--key` (`ct-<stage>-<fixture>-<repeat>-<run-id>`; a `run` verb, KNOWN_DRIFTS 5), so that the compared runs of stages 2, 3, 6, 7 and 10 and the five cost repeats are separate runs and not one stored receipt. Card variants are fixture variants, since `sim.run` takes no card option: `yaw-edge-global` (readout `global-instantaneous`), `yaw-edge-reversed` (`row_order` reversed), `yaw-edge-dim` (the mount's `lighting_level` halved), `impact-frame-stop` (the dead-stop variant).

**2. Fixtures** (`synthetic`). `yaw-edge`: a camera (focal length 600 pixels, 480 rows, readout 16 ms, exposure 4 ms) on a body prescribed to yaw at 2 rad/s, facing a wall with one vertical black-to-white edge near the optical axis at 3 m (depth and label channels both carry the edge). `spin-lidar`: a 10 Hz spinning lidar on a body yawing at 1 rad/s in a square room with a wall at 5 m across the seam direction. `impact-frame` (BD-188's CX-202 fixture): the same camera with a 2 ms exposure, translating sideways at +1 m/s in front of a vertical edge 0.6 m away (1 mm of travel moves the edge 1 pixel), with an impact at the boundary t = 9 ms after the frame's sample instant that reverses it to −1 m/s, and a dead-stop variant whose velocity is reset to zero there. `moving-bar`: a bar at z₁ = 1 m moving laterally at 1 m/s in front of a wall at z₂ = 2 m, seen by a depth camera with focal length 600 pixels, exposure 10 ms and four sub-instants per row (N20a-26's worked fixture: band width B = f·v·t_exp/z₁ = 6 pixels per edge, so 2·6·3/4 = 9 `mixed` pixels per row; for the optional stage).

**3. Stages.**

| # | Stage | Commands | Asserts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Skew | `frob sim run yaw-edge --tier $T --telemetry T2 --key …`; `frob telemetry query <run> --stream sensing --channels label --from <frame 0's first row> --to <its last row> --json` | the edge's column at the last row minus at the first row is 19.2 ± 0.5 pixels (edge located per row at sub-pixel precision by the label transition) |
| 2 | Control | the same on `yaw-edge-global` (the camera card set to `global-instantaneous`) | skew below 0.5 pixel; outputs labeled as an idealization in `frob sense profile` |
| 3 | Row order | the same on `yaw-edge-reversed` (`row_order` reversed) | skew −19.2 ± 0.5 pixels |
| 4 | Seam | `frob sim run spin-lidar`; scan 0 through `frob telemetry query` | the wall points adjacent across the seam are 0.50 ± 0.01 m apart; a stationary run shows less than 0.01 m |
| 5 | Beam instants | `frob sense profile` and the packet's per-beam offsets | equal to the pattern definition for every beam (exact) |
| 6 | Impact | `frob sim run impact-frame` and its dead-stop variant; the edge column per row through `frob telemetry query <run> --stream sensing --channels label`, per-row poses from the telemetry debug channel | rebound: the edge's offset is 1.00 px on rows 0 and 479 and 8.98 px on rows 239 and 240 (±0.01 px), no row beyond the true 9 mm excursion; dead stop: rows from 240 on at 9.00 px |
| 7 | Cost | two runs of `yaw-edge` (rolling and global-instantaneous), receipts' phase ledgers | ray-stage time ratio at most 1.05 (median of 5 repeats after warm-up) |
| 8 | Auto exposure | `yaw-edge-dim` (the camera mount's `lighting_level` halved; the per-zone field arrives with N19a-06 at M3; from then the stage also runs with a zone level, which takes precedence) | the next longer exposure entry and `exposure_source` are on the packets; row-instant offset grows by half the exposure difference; the first-to-last-row skew is unchanged |
| 9 | Open-frame checkpoint | `frob sim run yaw-edge --tier $T --telemetry T3 --snapshot-every 1 --key …` (snapshots at every chunk, N29ap1-02); `frob sim replay --run <r> --world 0 --from-snapshot <the snapshot whose tick falls inside frame 1's readout, an open rolling exposure> --capture t2 --key … --json` (BD-203), and frame 1 read from both runs through `frob telemetry query` | byte-equal image and equal delivered-observation digest (BD-098's open-exposure checkpoint, INV-SENS-03) |
| 10 | Exposure integration (only if funded; owned by N20a-26 at M3, which runs this script) | `moving-bar` with integration on and off | 9 ± 2 `mixed` pixels per row with it on, none off; added ray-stage cost at most 25% |

**4. Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sense_camera_timing/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` plus transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (tier, determinism class, device fingerprint, identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds (pixels, metres, ratio), image and scan digests, and on failure the first row or beam at which the measurement left its tolerance with the run identity to reproduce. Summary: assertion counts and `gates: ["GATE-SENS-06", "KILL-EXPOSURE"]` (the second only when stage 10 ran or the kill decision's record is read and echoed).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script passes on every tier that runs cameras at M2 with stages 1 to 9.
- [ ] The script writes no gate record (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9): it logs the receipt hashes of its runs, and once GATE-SENS-06 is recorded by BD-188 (the gate register's measuring bead, with `record_gate` from its own tests' receipt) it reads the record back and asserts that the recorded skew, seam displacement and cost ratio agree with its own measurements within their tolerances.
- [ ] `--self-test` shows the controls are caught: the global-shutter build fails stage 1's expectation; the linear-blend mutant fails stage 6.
- [ ] The log validates against the end-to-end log schema and contains measured values with units for every assertion.
- [ ] Stage 7 uses a declared repeat count and reports the spread; a noisy host is reported as `inconclusive` rather than as a pass.
- [ ] Stage 10 is skipped with an explicit log line when exposure integration was not funded, and the summary then echoes the kill decision's status.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The script's own checks (`scripts/e2e/tests/sense_camera_timing.bats`): editing an expected value makes it fail; `--self-test` passes only if both controls are caught; one summary line.

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified: `scripts/e2e/sense_camera_timing.sh`, log under `target/e2e-logs/sense_camera_timing/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-479 (skew 19.2 pixels; control: a global-shutter build shows none), KA-480 (0.5 m at the seam), KA-481 (no blur beyond the segmented motion; control: a linear pose blend across the reset), KA-470 (beam instants). CX-202. Metamorphic stages (cheap, included): doubling the yaw rate doubles the skew within tolerance; a stationary robot shows neither skew nor seam displacement. Determinism: repeated runs give identical image digests on one tier; across tiers images agree inside the class and the measured skew within tolerance.

## Considerations

- Sub-pixel edge location must use the label or depth transition, not a rendered intensity, so that the measurement does not depend on shading.
- The 5% cost target is measured on the reference camera at its default resolution; the log records resolution and world count so that a later regression is comparable.
- Timing assertions on shared hardware are noisy; the script reports the median and the spread and never asserts a single sample.
