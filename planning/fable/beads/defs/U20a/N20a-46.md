## Background

A sensor model is useful to a designer in proportion to the failures it shares with the instrument, and each failure mode has a first independent check. This bead is the end-to-end script that runs those first checks from the user's side, through the real `frob` binary: the proprioceptive and range checks when the sensor models land at M2, and the optical failure modes, fault injection and the card gate when they land at M3. It measures GATE-SENS-14 (every failure mode marked Solid has its first check passing, and every card field reports its provenance) and GATE-SENS-07 (the mirror, glass, steam and pattern-dropout cases; every masked sample carries a reason; repeated reads through one pane fail together), both M3.

The script is created at M2 with the stages whose mechanisms exist then; each later bead adds its stage as part of its own acceptance. One script, because a user's question is one question: "does this sensor suite fail the way the real one does, and does the system tell me why a sample is missing?"

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh`, its fixtures, stages, assertions and log.

Out: unit tests; the camera-timing checks (N20a-45); the clock-contract checks (N20a-44).

## Technical approach

**1. Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh [--tier referee|batch-cpu|cuda|metal] [--seed N] [--stage <name>]`. Plain shell, shared helper, idempotent, seed-explicit, non-zero exit on failure. The tier is passed to `frob` as it spells it (`cuda` and `metal` as `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal`). Every call of a `run` verb (`frob sim run`, `frob eval run`, `frob sense causal-test`) carries its own `--key` (`of-<stage>-<fixture>-<variant>-<run-id>`; KNOWN_DRIFTS 5), so that the compared runs of stages 2, 14 and 15 are separate runs and not one stored receipt.

**3. Controls.** `--self-test` switches on, one at a time: `glass` treated as `diffuse` (stage 9 must fail); one-way attenuation (stage 10); independent coins through one pane (stage 13); a kernel that writes a range under a mask (stage 12); a card with a field lacking provenance (stage 1); saturation disabled (stage 3). Each must be caught.

**4. Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sense_optical_failures/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` plus a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (the bead that owns the stage), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds (counts, m/s², A, °C, N, N·m, m, fractions), reason histograms as `metric` lines (`sensor`, `reason`, `count`), digests where runs are compared, and on failure the first differing value with the fixture and run identity. A final `summary` line with assertion counts and `gates: ["GATE-SENS-07", "GATE-SENS-14"]`. Fixtures are synthetic and public; images are never written to the log (identifiers and counts only), so the same script is safe on a private scene.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At M2 the script passes stages 1 (partial), 2 to 7 and 15 on the referee and `batch-cpu`; this bead closes on those stages.
- [ ] Stages 8 to 14, 16 and 17 and the complete stage 1 are fixed here (fixtures, expected values, controls) and log `not due` with their owning bead until it lands; passing them on every tier that runs the sensors is the acceptance of the beads named in the "Since" column (N20a-21, N20a-22, N20a-23, N20a-24, N20a-31, BD-237, BD-238), which run this script. The script writes no gate record (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9): BD-237 and BD-238, the gate register's measuring beads, record GATE-SENS-07 and GATE-SENS-14 with `record_gate` from stage 17's receipts.
- [ ] `--self-test` reports every control of a due stage caught (at M2: the card field lacking provenance on the partial stage 1, and saturation disabled); the controls of stages not yet due log `not due` with their owning bead and are caught when that bead runs the script.
- [ ] The log validates against the end-to-end log schema; every assertion line has measured value, expected value, tolerance and unit; the summary names both gates.
- [ ] A stage whose mechanism is not yet built logs "not due" with the owning bead and does not count as a pass.
- [ ] Re-running with the same seed reproduces identical measured values and reason histograms.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The script's own checks (`scripts/e2e/tests/sense_optical_failures.bats`): an edited expectation fails; `--self-test` passes only when every control of a due stage is caught (all six once every stage is due) and fails if a due control is reported `not due`; `--stage` runs exactly one stage; one summary line.

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified: `scripts/e2e/sense_optical_failures.sh`, log under `target/e2e-logs/sense_optical_failures/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-482 (mirror), KA-483 (glass; control: `glass` as `diffuse`), KA-484 (steam; control: one-way attenuation), KA-485 (pattern dropout reasons), KA-486 (repeated reads; control: independent coins), KA-510 to KA-522 (sensor first checks), KA-516 and KA-475 (force-torque). CX-203, CX-204. Determinism: masks and counts exact across tiers; float values inside their class with the stated tolerances.

## Considerations

- Several first checks have tolerances the registry still lists as "to set"; the script takes its tolerances from the registry, so a stage whose tolerance is unset reports `blocked` and the gate cannot be recorded until the owner sets it.
- The 4,096-episode stage and the 2-hour IMU stream are the long ones; both are single small worlds and run in seconds to a minute.
- The script is the natural place to notice a silent fallback: a Frontier model requested by a fixture and not built must show up in stage 1 as `not modeled (fallback)`.
