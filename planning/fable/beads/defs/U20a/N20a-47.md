## Background

`sense.observability` tells a designer which directions of state error a sensor suite cannot see along a motion, names them, and warns where a route is blind. This bead is its end-to-end script: the real `frob` binary on the hand-checkable fixtures, with a structured log. It is created at M1 with the linearized form on the referee (the M1 rows of GATE-SENS-08) and gains its remaining stages at M3, when the empirical form on batched tiers and the full verb land. GATE-SENS-08 (known answer): the corridor case and the four-dimensional gauge, by the linearized and the empirical form, agreeing to within 5% in the non-zero eigenvalues.

Hand-checkable values behind it. Corridor: a robot at (x, y) between walls y = ±a measures a − y with variance s²; standing still for N samples, W = (N/s²)·diag(0, 1); the null vector is (1, 0); a second beam at 45° adds nothing along x; a beam that reaches an end wall makes W full rank. With N = 100 and s = 0.01 m the non-zero entry is 10⁶ m⁻². Gauge: with an IMU, encoders, contact switches and gravity the null space has dimension four (position and yaw). Limit: two aisles that are exact copies give full rank in each and are still ambiguous.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/sense_observability.sh`, fixtures, stages, assertions and log.

Out: unit tests; the alias script (N20a-48), to which the ambiguity stage points.

## Technical approach

**1. Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/sense_observability.sh [--tier referee|batch-cpu|cuda|metal] [--seed N]`. Plain shell, shared helper, idempotent, seed-explicit, non-zero exit on failure.

**2. Fixtures** (`synthetic`): `corridor-side-beam`, `corridor-two-beams` (adds the 45° beam), `corridor-end-wall` (adds a forward beam within range of an end wall), `two-anchors` and `three-anchors` (range anchors at (−1, 0), (1, 0) and (0, 2)), `twin-aisles`, `gauge-quadruped` (the reference legged robot with IMU, encoders and contact switches, standing and trotting in place), `corridor-route` (a route that starts out of range of the end wall and ends within it).

**3. Stages.**

**4. Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sense_observability/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` plus transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed; the label `screening`), measured eigenvalues with units and expected values and tolerances, the pairwise relative differences between forms against 0.05, null dimensions, names of weak directions, flags, worlds used by the empirical form, and on failure the first eigenvalue out of tolerance with the request needed to reproduce. Summary: assertion counts and `gates: ["GATE-SENS-08"]`, with the note "linearized rows" until stage 8 exists.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At M1 the script passes stages 1 to 7 on the referee, and the GATE-SENS-08 record is written with status "M1 rows (linearized)".
- [ ] At M3 it passes all stages on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal for the empirical form, and the gate record cites its log by receipt.
- [ ] `--self-test` shows the controls caught: gravity applied twice (stage 5 reports the defect; without the report the test fails); the closed-loop mutant of the empirical form (stage 8 disagreement above 5%); a build without the ambiguity check (stage 6 fails).
- [ ] The log validates against the end-to-end log schema; every eigenvalue assertion has measured, expected, tolerance and unit.
- [ ] A stage whose mechanism is not built logs "not due" and does not count as a pass.
- [ ] Re-running with the same seed reproduces identical output bytes on one tier.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The script's own checks (`scripts/e2e/tests/sense_observability.bats`): an edited expectation fails; `--self-test` requires all three controls caught; one summary line.

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified: `scripts/e2e/sense_observability.sh`, log under `target/e2e-logs/sense_observability/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-487 (corridor Gramian; the two forms agree within 5%), KA-488 (gauge of dimension four; a smaller null space is a defect in a sensor model). CX-205. Metamorphic stages included: translating or yawing the whole corridor scene leaves the spectrum unchanged; doubling the beam's variance halves the non-zero eigenvalue. Determinism: the linearized form in double precision is identical on repeat; the empirical form is bit-reproducible per backend and within the 5% gate across backends.

## Considerations

- The M1 form of the verb is reduced (fixtures and the referee); the script addresses fixtures by name so that the same stages keep working when the full request schema arrives.
- The 5% agreement is the gate's cross-form tolerance, not a claim about estimator accuracy; the log repeats the `screening` label on every result line for that reason.
- Stage 6 deliberately ends with a pointer to the alias script: a locally full-rank, globally ambiguous route is the hand-over between the two analyses.
