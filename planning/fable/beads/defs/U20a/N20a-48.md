## Background

`sense.alias` answers a question no amount of training answers: can any policy on these sensors satisfy this mission? Its output is a checkable witness or an honest "none found", and a standing witness stops futile training with refusal 445. This bead is the capability's end-to-end script, driving the real `frob` binary and the offline auditor.

It is created at M1 with the docking rows of GATE-SENS-09 (the late-packet witness found and checked on the CPU, which the first mission slice needs) and gains the remaining stages at M3 (the simulated T-junction, the stochastic case, the verb's refusals, and the 64-layout suite). GATE-SENS-09 (known answer): the T-junction and the late-packet docking witnesses are found and pass the checker; on 64 planted layouts every planted alias is found and no false witness is reported.

Hand-checkable values behind it. Docking: three one-second ticks, hidden gain g ∈ {1, 2}, one reading of v₁; with the packet late, a common command vector gives final position g·(2u₀ + u₁), which cannot equal 1 for both gains; with the packet on time at U = ¾ and probe ¼, the commands (¼, ½, −¾) and (¼, 0, −¼) both reach (1, 0). T-junction: branches of 10 m, plate detected only within 0.5 m, battery 12 m; a wrong turn costs 9.5 + 9.5 + 10 = 29 m; success at most 1/2. Repeated reads at accuracy 3/4: 27/32, 459/512, 3,807/4,096 if independent; 3/4 with a common fault; a lead-of-two rule gives 9/10 with 3.2 reads, and 225/256 when forced to guess after six.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/sense_alias.sh`, fixtures, stages, assertions and log.

Out: unit tests; the docking grid on every backend (the docking fixture's own script, BD-190); the pilot's script (N20a-42).

## Technical approach

**1. Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/sense_alias.sh [--tier referee|batch-cpu|cuda|metal] [--seed N] [--stage <name>]`. Plain shell, shared helper, idempotent, seed-explicit, non-zero exit on failure.

Every `frob sense alias` and `frob train start` call carries its own `--key` (both are `run` verbs; KNOWN_DRIFTS 5) and uses only the options of its row: fixture variants are missions named with `--mission` (BD-134's `docking-late` and `docking-on-time`, N20a-37's `t-junction-abstract` and `t-junction-abstract-29m`) or cards bound with `--card`, never link, latency or battery options. Fixtures of this script: `tests/fixtures/alias/docking-late-1q.fmission` (BD-134's docking mission with its link latency set to one quantum), the `aisle-delivery-fiducial` mission and the fiducial scene revision of stage 12.

**3. Controls.** `--self-test` switches on, one at a time: a link mutant with arrival equal to sample (stage 1 must lose its witness and fail); a checker that skips disjointness (stage 6's 29 m variant would be accepted: must be caught); a checker comparing only at the commitment instant (a re-converging pair fixture must be caught); an analysis assuming independence regardless of the card (stage 9); a registry keyed by mission alone (stage 12).

**4. Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/sense_alias/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` plus transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), the witness's identity, source, class, scope and bound (as an exact fraction), transcript digests of both worlds, counts of behaviours replayed, success values as fractions and decimals against thresholds, refusal envelopes in full, and on failure the first differing packet (sensor, sequence, field) with the two world definitions' identities. Summary: assertion counts and `gates: ["GATE-SENS-09", "KILL-ALIAS"]`, with the note "docking rows (M1)" until stage 13 exists. World definitions of private scenes are referenced by identity and never printed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At M1 the script passes stages 1 to 6 on the referee; this bead closes on those stages. The script writes no gate record (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9): the M1 docking rows of GATE-SENS-09 are recorded by BD-134, the gate register's measuring bead for them, with `record_gate`, and the script reads the record back once it exists.
- [ ] Stages 7 to 14 are fixed here (missions, commands, expected values, controls, and the fallback branch below) and log `not due` with their owning bead until it lands. Every one of them reaches users through N20a-41's verb, so passing stages 7 to 12 is N20a-41's acceptance (it runs this script unchanged; the lanes are accepted on their own scripts: N20a-39's `scripts/e2e/sense_alias_symmetry.sh`, N20a-40's `scripts/e2e/sense_alias_stochastic.sh`, and N20a-38's), and passing stages 13 and 14 is BD-240's, which records the M3 part of GATE-SENS-09 from its receipt with `record_gate`. On the fallback branch of KILL-ALIAS, stage 13 reports the Solid lanes' counts and the summary says so.
- [ ] `--self-test` reports every control of a due stage caught (at M1: the link mutant, the checker skipping disjointness, the checker comparing only at commitment); the controls of stages 9 and 12 log `not due` until N20a-40 and N20a-41 land.
- [ ] The offline auditor is run from a clean directory without the workspace's data (the bundle alone) and accepts the certificate.
- [ ] The log validates against the end-to-end log schema; bounds and success values are logged as exact fractions.
- [ ] A stage whose mechanism is not built logs "not due" and does not count as a pass; re-running with the same seed reproduces identical witnesses.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The script's own checks (`scripts/e2e/tests/sense_alias.bats`): an edited expectation fails; a `run` call without `--key`, or with an option outside the `sense.alias` row (`--link`, `--link-latency`, `--battery-m`), fails the script's lint; `--self-test` requires every control of a due stage caught (three at M1, all five from M3) and fails if a due control reports `not due`; `--stage` isolates one stage; one summary line.

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified: `scripts/e2e/sense_alias.sh`, log under `target/e2e-logs/sense_alias/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-489 (T-junction; control: with a 29 m budget the witness is rejected), KA-490 (the theorem's bound), KA-491 (sequential reads under a deadline), KA-492 (planted suite), KA-499, KA-500, KA-502 (docking). CX-206, CX-207, CX-208. Determinism: stages 1 to 6 are exact (class D0) and byte-identical on repeat and on a second machine; searched results are seeded.

## Considerations

- The M1 stages need no ray sensors and no GPU; they are part of the first mission slice's evidence and should run in seconds.
- Stage 11 blocks a training command on purpose; the script must check the refusal's content (code, witness, repairs), not only the exit code, because exit code 3 is shared by every refused input.
- "None found" stages assert the per-lane coverage fields too, so that an empty search cannot pass as an answer.
