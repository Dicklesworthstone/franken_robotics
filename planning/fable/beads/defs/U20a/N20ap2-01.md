## Background

Sensorium lists two Frontier image items beside the Solid stills for agents: RGB cameras for policies (N20a-28,
under the RGB epic BD-420) and **hero renders**: images for people, made by FrankenSim's spectral path tracer,
offline only. FACT: the tracer is the sibling feature `tracer` of `fs-render`, Frontier-gated and off by default
upstream. The airlock gives it an eleventh feature group, `tracer` (ADR-SUITE-03, N33bx-02), and N33bx-07 builds the
`HeroRender` entry (`frob_fsim::tracer`: a frozen snapshot in, an EXR image with a `RenderIdentity` out), its
admission lane and its pilot; the user-side wiring is funded only if that pilot passes.

A hero render is not a sensor. ADR-SENS-01: observations never come from a renderer or a viewer; NONGOAL-01: no
photorealistic renderer is built here. The render is a presentation artifact, private when its scene is private,
carrying the three no-authorization flags. This bead wires it to the user as a quality option of the stills verb
and holds the line at run time. It was split from N20a-28 in polishing round 2: hero renders are funded by the
tracer's pilot, not by the RGB epic's census, and must not be withdrawn when RGB is dropped.

## Scope

In: the option `--quality hero` (with `--spp`) of `telemetry.stills`; the admission predicate `stills-quality-hero`; the call into `HeroRender`; the presentation
artifact and its identity; the refusal on a binary without the group; the runtime refusal of any attempt to use a
hero image as an observation; tests and script. Out: the trait, admission lane and pilot (N33bx-07); the stills
from the collision field and the identifier buffer (N20a-33, BD-191); RGB cameras (N20a-28); the registry row's text
(VERBS-telemetry, whose `telemetry.stills` row carries the option, its group need and the refusal 790).

## Technical approach

**Surface.** `frob telemetry stills <run> --world <address> --at <instant> --quality hero [--spp <n>] --json`.
`--quality` takes `standard` (default: the Solid still from the collision field) or `hero`. `--spp` (samples per
pixel, default 256, at most the tracer's `RenderLimits`) is accepted only with `hero`; otherwise `FROB-REF-802`.
With `hero` the verb needs the group `tracer`: its row carries `needs_groups = [{ groups = ["tracer"], when =
"stills-quality-hero" }]` (N33c-32's argument-dependent form, which the suite-consumption registry N33-REF allows),
and this bead registers the predicate `stills-quality-hero` (true when the resolved request has `quality = "hero"`)
in `frob_verbs::admission::predicates`, so that N33c-32's lint `needs-groups-known` accepts the row. A binary built
without the group therefore refuses a hero request at admission, before any job or budget, with
`FROB-REF-790` (`foundation-group-absent`, exit 5, `required: "tracer"`, repairs: use a build that has the group;
rebuild with `--features frob-fsim/tracer`; run its admission gate).

**Steps (hero).**
1. Resolve the recorded snapshot at the instant from the run (no simulation is run).
2. Build `HeroScene` from the snapshot: bodies with their collision-registered meshes and a `SurfaceLook` from the
   pack's appearance where one is admitted, otherwise a neutral grey; one light set from the zone's lighting level.
   Unknown regions of an ingested place are rendered as a flat marked colour, never as guessed appearance.
3. Call `HeroRender::render` with `HeroOptions { width, height, samples_per_pixel, max_bounces = 8, seed }`, the seed
   derived from the run's seed and the world address, under the caller's `Cx` (cancellation returns a typed
   cancellation and no image).
4. Write the artifact: the EXR and a PNG preview, with a header carrying the `RenderIdentity` (snapshot hash, camera,
   options, seed, sibling revision, stack receipt), the three no-authorization flags (all false) and the privacy
   mark of the scene. Evidence: *pass-through*, as for every still; the artifact enters no result, no requirement
   and no ledger value.

**Never an observation.** A hero image has no conversion to an observation sample (N33bx-07's compile-fail test). At
run time, registering a task, wrapper or consumer whose observation source is a hero artifact is refused by the
observation service with `FROB-REF-442` (clause (a): an observation requested from presentation state); this bead
adds `hero-render` to the presentation sources that clause lists (N20a-01's check).

**Privacy.** A hero image of a private scene is private: it is written only under the session's export root with the
private scope and flag, otherwise `FROB-REF-731`; logs and records carry hashes, never pixels.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On a default binary, `frob telemetry stills <run> --quality hero --json` exits 5 with `FROB-REF-790` naming
      `tracer`, before any job is created.
- [ ] When N33bx-07's verdict funds the tracer: on a binary built with `--features frob-fsim/tracer` (whose
      admission record passes), the same call writes the presentation artifact with its `RenderIdentity` and the
      three no-authorization flags.
- [ ] When N33bx-07's verdict funds the tracer: two calls with equal run, world, instant, options and seed give
      equal image bytes.
- [ ] A task whose observation source is a hero artifact is refused with `FROB-REF-442`.
- [ ] `--spp` without `--quality hero` is refused with `FROB-REF-802`; a private scene without the private scope is
      refused with `FROB-REF-731`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-chronicle/tests/stills_hero.rs` (lane `--features frob-fsim/tracer` where marked):
- `hero_absent_refuses_790` (default build): the admission check names `tracer`; no job record exists afterwards.
- `predicate_stills_quality_hero`: the predicate is registered and holds for a request with `--quality hero`, not for
  `--quality standard` or the default; on the default build a standard still is admitted (no 790), and
  `needs-groups-known` passes on the generated row.
- `hero_artifact_has_identity_and_flags` (tracer lane): a furnace fixture (one convex body of albedo 0.5 in a uniform
  environment, N33bx-07's pilot scene) at 64 × 64 and 16 spp; header fields present; flags false.
- `hero_deterministic` (tracer lane): two renders, equal bytes.
- `hero_not_observation_442`: registering an observation source of kind `hero-render` → 442.
- `spp_without_hero_802`; `private_hero_needs_scope_731`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_hero.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N20ap2-01 milestone=M6 labels=sensorium,frontier,verb seed=3
needs=cpu plants=hero-as-observation`; sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` of STD-E2E; log
`target/e2e-logs/sense_hero/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript). Steps: `record` runs `frob design new qm --template
quad-mammal --key hero-des-1 --json` and `frob sim run qm --tier referee --seconds 0.1 --seed 3 --key hero-run-1
--json`; `default_binary` runs
`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-790 5 -- frob telemetry stills <run> --world 0 --at t=0.1 --quality hero --json` and
asserts `required` is `tracer`; `tracer_binary` (skipped with exit 77 and a logged reason if the tracer build or its
admission record is absent) runs the same call with `FROB_BIN` pointing at the tracer build and asserts the artifact,
its identity and its three flags, then repeats it and compares the image digests; `not_observation` registers the
planted task of plant `hero-as-observation` and expects `FROB-REF-442`. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block; `metric` lines
give image size, spp, wall time and the image BLAKE3 (never pixels); a final `summary` counts assertions.

### Known answers and negative controls
Furnace scene: every body pixel reads the albedo within the tracer pilot's tolerance (N33bx-07's known answer, reused,
not re-derived). Negative controls: the hero-as-observation task (442); the default binary (790).

## Considerations

- The tracer's cost is large and offline; the verb's budget applies, and a render above `RenderLimits` is refused by
  name. Long renders return a job handle (exit 8) like any long tool.
- If N33bx-07's pilot verdict is `dropped` or its admission does not pass, the fallback named there holds (no hero
  renders; stills from the collision field remain) and `--quality hero` stays refused with 790 on every binary.
  This bead still delivers the option, its refusals (790, 802, 731) and the 442 source check; the two tracer-lane
  acceptance items are recorded `not run` citing that verdict, and the bead closes `pass` on its default-binary
  tests (N41-17 rule 2; a `fallback` close would need a kill-gate ADR, and the tracer has no gate row).
- Captured appearance in a hero image is private data and is never logged.
