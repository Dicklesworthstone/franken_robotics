## Background

Sensorium (crate `frob-sense`) treats sensing as part of the world's state. A sample becomes a **packet**
carrying its sensor identifier, sequence number, its instants (exposure, arrival and the rest of the seven
instants of a sample), a validity mask with reasons and a payload. Packets wait in a fixed-capacity ring
per world and sensor until the boundary at which their arrival instant has passed. The **link model**
gives each packet its fate from counter-based streams keyed by world address, sensor, sequence number and
component: lost or not, its latency in clock quanta, duplicated or not. Rings, open exposures, correlated
noise processes and latents are inside every checkpoint, because in-flight packets cannot be re-derived
once the physical history that produced them is gone.

Counterfactual debugging forks a run at a tick f and changes something. For an **information
intervention** (a different latency, noise scale or dropout law; an estimator replaced by privileged
state) the rule is that the child copies the physical state and has its "packet queues and filter state
re-initialized as the intervention states". The restore bead (N27a-05, in the telemetry crate) fixes the
defaults: when latency or jitter changes, "every in-flight packet keeps its sampling instant and value and
receives a delivery instant under the new law; packets whose new delivery instant is at or before f are
delivered at f in sampling order"; when the estimator is replaced by privileged state, the filter state is
discarded. To do that today it would have to edit the ring layout and the checkpoint section of another
crate. DESIGN decision: Sensorium owns two entry points that transform its own checkpoint section, and
callers never touch the layout. Rejected alternatives: letting the fork code rewrite rings (the layout
then has two owners and the invariant "a packet is never delivered before its arrival instant" has no
single guard); re-running the sensors from the fork tick to refill queues (impossible: the exposures of
in-flight packets lie before f); dropping in-flight packets at a fork (the child would see a sensing gap
that the intervention did not ask for, and every effect attributed to the intervention would include it).
Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: `retime_in_flight` for a changed link law; `reinit_state` for sensor-chain state with its closed set
of sources; the trait an estimator implements to be re-initialized through the same call; capacity and
admission checks under the new law; the change record returned to the caller; exactness and the
preserved invariants.

Out: classifying a fork and choosing which entry point applies (N27a-02, N27a-05); child addresses and
stream modes (N27a-04); the label `Diagnostic` and where it travels (N27a-05; this bead only reports
whether a source is physically realizable); the link model for packets sampled from f on, which is the
ordinary model under the child's card (N20a-08); the estimator's own implementation of the trait (the
control crate); scheduled sensor faults (N20a-31).

## Technical approach

Both entry points are pure host functions on the Sensorium checkpoint section (`SenseCheckpoint`: clocks,
open exposures, packet ring images, command ring images, process states, latents, delivered-set digests).
They run on the snapshot image before the child is loaded on any tier, so there is no device kernel and
the result is identical for every backend.

**1. `retime_in_flight`.**

```rust
pub fn retime_in_flight(cp: &SenseCheckpoint, sensor: SensorId, new_link: &LinkCard, f: Quanta,
                        addr: WorldAddr) -> Result<(SenseCheckpoint, RetimeRecord), RetimeRefusal>;
pub struct RetimeRecord { retimed: u32, delivered_at_fork: Vec<PacketId>, unchanged: u32, fields: Vec<FieldPath> }
```

Numbered steps, integer arithmetic only:
1. Admission of the new card against the allocated ring: required capacity = ⌈(L′ + E)/T⌉ + W′, doubled if
   duplication is declared (L′ the new `latency_max`, plus the new `dup_extra_max` if duplication is declared,
   as BD-098 sizes the ring; E the exposure window, T the period, W′ the new reorder window). W′ must be at
   least ⌈(L′_max − L′_min)/T⌉, or ⌈(L′_max + dup_extra_max′ − L′_min)/T⌉ when duplication is declared (the
   admission rule of N20a-08); a card that understates it is refused by that same link-card check before any
   packet is touched. The ring capacity was fixed at launch, so a larger requirement is refused with
   FROB-REF-441 ("history buffers cannot cover the declared latency and exposure window"), `observed` the
   allocated capacity, `required` the new figure. On either refusal the fork does not start. Where the new
   card declares no duplication, dup_extra_max′ is 0 in step 2.
2. For each packet in the ring whose arrival is after f, keep sensor, sequence, copy index, exposure instant,
   mask and payload reference (a packet with arrival at or before f is in the ring only when the section was
   taken before stage 4 of f; it is delivered at f exactly as in the parent and is not retimed). Recompute its latency ℓ′ under the new law from the **same keyed draw** the
   link model uses for that packet, (addr of the parent packet, sensor, sequence, component): `fixed` gives
   L′_min; `uniform` gives L′_min + (u_lat mod (L′_max − L′_min + 1)) with the same bias removal; `table`
   selects by the new cumulative weights. A duplicate (copy index 1) gets ℓ′ plus
   (u_extra mod (dup_extra_max′ + 1)).
3. New arrival a′ = t_exposure + ℓ′. If a′ ≤ f, set arrival := f and mark the packet for delivery at the
   boundary f. The packets so marked are delivered at f in **sampling order**: by (exposure instant, sensor
   identifier, sequence number, copy index). All others are delivered later in the usual total order
   (arrival, sensor identifier, sequence number, copy index). The marked packets come after every packet the
   parent delivers at f, whose deliveries are unchanged: if the section was taken after stage 4 (delivery) of
   the boundary at f and before stage 5 (decision), they are appended to the delivered and not yet consumed
   set that stage 5 reads; if it was taken before stage 4, stage 4 of f delivers the packets of ordinary
   arrival f first, in the usual total order, and the marked ones after them. DESIGN: the order at f stays
   total with no ties (PO-SENS-02), the decision at f sees the marked packets, and the parent's delivered set
   at f is a prefix of the child's, so the child's delivered-set digest chain extends the parent's. A section
   taken after stage 5 of f cannot honour "delivered at f" (the decision at f has already read its inputs), so
   it is refused (`RetimeRefusal::AfterDecision`, with the stage it was taken at) and the fork does not start.
4. Loss is not redrawn: a packet in flight was not lost and stays in flight; a packet already recorded as
   dropped stays dropped. The new loss and duplication probabilities apply to packets sampled from f on.
5. Rebuild the ring image in arrival order and recompute the section's digest.

**2. `reinit_state`.**

```rust
pub enum StateSource { Keep, Cold, Stationary, RescaleToLaw, FromTruth, Value(Vec<u8>) }
pub fn reinit_state(cp: &SenseCheckpoint, target: &StateTarget, src: &StateSource, f: Quanta,
                    child: WorldAddr, truth: Option<&TruthView>) -> Result<(SenseCheckpoint, ReinitRecord), ReinitRefusal>;
pub trait ReinitFromSource { fn reinit(&mut self, src: &StateSource, f: Quanta, child: WorldAddr,
                                        truth: Option<&TruthView>) -> Result<ReinitRecord, ReinitRefusal>; }
```

Targets inside Sensorium: a correlated noise process (bias random walk, coloured noise), a per-region
latent, a filter history of a sensor chain (the force-sensor filter and integrator, the scalar readout
lag), an encoder's unwrapping and missed-count state, an open exposure. The estimator and any controller-
side filter implement `ReinitFromSource` and are re-initialized through the same call by their owner.

| Source | Meaning | Realizable on hardware |
|---|---|---|
| `Keep` | State unchanged | Yes |
| `Cold` | The state the component has at episode reset under its card, drawn from its keyed stream at the child's address and tick f | Yes (a power cycle) |
| `Stationary` | For a noise process: a draw from its stationary law under the child's card, keyed by (child address, sensor, f) | Yes |
| `RescaleToLaw` | For a noise process whose scale changed from σ to σ′: state × σ′/σ, so the process is stationary under the new law without a new draw | Yes |
| `FromTruth` | The state is set from the simulator's true state at f (a bias estimate to the true bias; an estimator to the true pose) | No: reported `realizable: false` |
| `Value` | An explicit state supplied by the intervention, validated against the target's schema and bounds | As the intervention declares |

A source that the target does not admit (`Stationary` on an encoder counter; `FromTruth` on a target with
no physical referent) is refused, with the admitted sources listed. An open exposure admits only `Keep`
and `Cold` (`Cold` abandons the partial integral; the packet of that exposure is then recorded as dropped
with reason `fork-reinit`, never fabricated).

**3. What is preserved (checked by tests).** INV-SENS-02 in the child: no packet is delivered before its
arrival instant, and a decision at t depends only on packets that arrived at or before t. History: the
parent's delivered sets up to f and their digests are untouched; the child's first delivered set differs
only by the packets delivered at f under step 3. Conservation: the multiset of (sensor, sequence, copy) in
flight after `retime_in_flight` equals the one before, minus those delivered at f. Values: no payload byte
and no mask changes.

**4. Records.** Each call returns the field paths it rebuilt or discarded, the counts, and
`realizable`. The caller writes them into the child's manifest under `restore`.

**5. Classes.** Both functions are deterministic functions of (checkpoint, change, f, addresses); integer
fields D0; process states that are rescaled or redrawn are in the generator's class. Code:
`crates/frob-sense/src/reinit/{retime.rs, state.rs}`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Hand case A (period 10 ms, fixed latency 30 ms, fork at f = 100 ms, section taken after stage 4 of f; in flight: samples at 80, 90, 100 ms with arrivals 110, 120, 130 ms; the sample at 70 ms arrived and was delivered at f). `latency := 0`: all three are delivered at f in the order 80, 90, 100, after the 70 ms packet, so the child's delivered set at f is (70, 80, 90, 100) with the parent's (70) as its prefix, and the ring is empty after the boundary.
- [ ] Hand case B (same state, `latency := 15 ms`): arrivals 95 → f, 105, 115 ms; one packet delivered at f.
- [ ] Hand case C (`latency := 50 ms` on a ring sized for 30 ms with E = 0, W = 0: capacity 3, required 5): refused with FROB-REF-441, nothing changed.
- [ ] With a jittered law, each packet's new latency is the keyed draw for its (sensor, sequence): the result is unchanged by adding a sensor, by batch size, by slot and by the order of calls on different sensors.
- [ ] After any admitted `retime_in_flight`, the child run satisfies INV-SENS-02 on 10⁴ ticks, and the conservation identity of step 3 holds.
- [ ] `reinit_state` with `Keep` returns a byte-identical section; `Cold` equals the component's reset state under the child's card; `FromTruth` reports `realizable: false`; an inadmissible (target, source) pair is refused with the admitted sources.
- [ ] The parent's snapshot and delivered-set digests are byte-identical before and after both calls.
- [ ] The restore code of the fork mechanism contains no direct access to ring or process layouts (checked by module privacy: the fields are not public outside `frob-sense`).
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-sense` documents both entry points, the sources and the preserved invariants.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-sense/tests/reinit.rs`; real rings and link model; cards labelled `synthetic`.
- `retime_fixed_to_zero`, `retime_fixed_shorter`, `retime_capacity_refused`: hand cases A, B, C.
- `retime_uniform_uses_keyed_draw`: latency uniform on [10, 40] ms changed to [0, 20] ms; each packet's ℓ′ equals 0 + (u_lat mod 21) quanta for its recorded u_lat.
- `retime_duplicate_in_flight`: a copy-1 packet gets ℓ′ plus its keyed extra, and is never delivered before copy 0 of the same packet (when both are clamped to f they are delivered in copy order).
- `retime_does_not_redraw_loss`: raising `p_loss` to 1 leaves every in-flight packet in flight.
- `delivery_at_fork_in_sampling_order`: two sensors with interleaved samples; order is by (exposure instant, sensor, sequence, copy).
- `fork_deliveries_follow_parent_at_f`: sensor B (not retimed) has a packet of ordinary arrival f; sensor A is retimed to latency 0 with two packets clamped to f; from a section taken before stage 4 and from one taken after it, the child's delivered set at f is B's packet then A's two in sampling order, and its digest chain extends the parent's at f; an A packet whose old arrival is exactly f keeps it and its parent position in both placements; a section taken after stage 5 is refused with `AfterDecision`.
- `conservation_of_in_flight_set`: property test over random rings and laws.
- `inv_sens_02_after_retime`: property test: 10⁴ ticks after random admitted retimings.
- `reinit_sources_per_target`: the admission table; `rescale_to_law` multiplies by exactly σ′/σ; `stationary` has the stated variance over 10⁵ draws.
- `open_exposure_cold_drops_packet_with_reason`.
- `parent_untouched`: digests before and after.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_fork_reinit.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; seed-explicit; idempotent; NDJSON under
`target/e2e-logs/sense_fork_reinit/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript). The fork verb `run.fork` and its restore
handling (N27a-05, N27a-08) depend on this bead, so the script drives the two entry points on a real checkpoint
without them, through a small example binary of the crate (`cargo run -p frob-sense --example reinit_checkpoint`,
DESIGN name) with three modes: `--fixture … --checkpoint-at <t>` runs a fixture on the referee and writes the
Sensorium section at a chosen boundary; `--checkpoint <path> --retime …` or `--reinit …` calls the public entry
point and writes the child section and the record as JSON; `--restore <child> --ticks <n>` restores a section
through BD-098's restore path and runs on. These are the example's own options, not options of a `frob` verb:
1. `frob sim run <walker with a 100 Hz IMU (BD-099) on a fixed 30 ms link> --seed 3 --telemetry T2 --key
   fork-reinit-parent-<run-id> --json` (the parent run, recorded; `sim.run` is a `run` verb and takes a key), then `reinit_checkpoint --fixture <the same walker> --seed 3 --checkpoint-at 0.100s
   --out <path> --json`, which runs the same world on the referee to the boundary at f and writes BD-098's
   Sensorium checkpoint section after stage 4 (delivery) of that boundary and before stage 5 (decision), BD-098's
   "just before a controller tick" instant: the sample at 100 ms has been enqueued and the one at 70 ms delivered,
   so the samples at 80, 90 and 100 ms are in flight as in hand case A (no `frob` verb writes a checkpoint at a
   chosen stage of a boundary: `sim.run` has no such option); assert the section's delivered-set digests up to f
   equal the parent's, read with `frob telemetry query <parent run> --stream sensing --json`.
2. `reinit_checkpoint --checkpoint <path> --retime imu --latency 0s --out <child> --json`: assert the record
   names the IMU ring as rebuilt and three packets delivered at the fork tick in sampling order (80, 90, 100 ms).
3. The same with `--latency 0.050s`: exit code 3, FROB-REF-441 with observed and required capacities (3 and 5),
   and no child file written.
4. `reinit_checkpoint --checkpoint <path> --reinit imu.bias --source from-truth`, then `--source keep`: assert
   `realizable: false` with the field path for the first, and a byte-identical Sensorium section for the second.
5. `reinit_checkpoint --restore <child> --ticks 10000 --out-deliveries <file> --json` restores the child of step 2
   through BD-098's restore path on the referee, runs 10⁴ ticks and writes every delivery with its arrival
   instant: assert no delivery precedes its arrival instant; then re-read the parent with `frob telemetry query`
   and assert its delivered-set digests up to f are unchanged.
Through the user's fork verb the same information intervention (IMU latency 0 s) and the privileged-estimator
case are exercised by `scripts/e2e/run_fork.sh` (N27a-08), which logs this bead's record inside the child's
`restore` block.
Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, the parent and child digests of the
Sensorium section, packet identifiers with old and new arrival instants in quanta, the record's counts and
field paths, refusal envelopes in full, and on failure the first packet that violates an assertion.

### Known answers and negative controls
- The three hand cases, in quanta of 1 ms: arrivals (110, 120, 130) → (100, 100, 100); → (100, 105, 115); refusal at capacity 3 against 5.
- Negative controls: a retime that redraws latency from an unkeyed generator (the batch-independence assertion fails); one that delivers clamped packets in ring order after a reorder (sampling-order test fails); one that drops in-flight packets (conservation fails); a `Cold` open exposure that fabricates a packet from the partial integral.
- Metamorphic: retiming to the same law is the identity; retiming A → B → A restores the original arrivals for packets not delivered at the fork.
- Determinism: both functions twice, in debug and release, on Linux and macOS: identical bytes; a child loaded on each admitted tier starts from the same section digest.

## Considerations

- A changed law can demand more ring capacity than was allocated at launch; refusing is the only sound answer, and the repair is to launch the parent with the larger bound declared.
- The defaults for which source applies to which change belong to the restore bead; this bead offers the closed set and reports realizability, so that a new kind of intervention is a new row there and not new code here.
- `RescaleToLaw` and `Stationary` give different children; both are legitimate readings of "noise scale changed", which is why the source is always recorded.
- Private scenes: the functions read no payload contents.
