## Background
Sensorium (`frob-sense`, layer 3, Solid, scope cap 14 thousand lines) owns what a robot is allowed to know and when. Its rule: a sensor is a map from a **history** of physical states and a calibrated stochastic process to a timestamped observation with a frame, units, a noise specification, a validity mask and a quality flag. It is a Component of the design: it reads ports and fields and emits a typed signal. Observations come only from the deterministic observation service that is part of the model identity (ADR-SENS-01), never from a renderer, a viewer, presentation timestamps or level-of-detail state, because a result must not depend on whether someone was watching.

This bead delivers the first sensors, on the referee tier (the CPU reference that steps FrankenSim's rigid-body and contact code in `f64`): the inertial measurement unit (IMU) with its mount offset, and the joint encoders, each described by a sensor card, plus the first form of the read verb `sense.profile`. They exist at M1 because the invariant filter (BD-114) consumes IMU and encoder samples, the linearized observability Gramian (BD-120) needs their measurement models, and the first mission slice reasons about what the robot can know.

Decisions that bind this bead, with their reasons:
- **ADR-SENS-07: a missing measurement is a masked value with a reason, never a number.** A numeric zero is a measurement only where the schema says so. Rejected: sentinel values (NaN, zero, last value), because a consumer cannot tell a held or absent value from a real one.
- **ADR-SENS-10: noise streams are keyed by logical identity** (world address, sensor, sample index, component), never by draw order. Reason: adding a sensor must not change what the others read. Rejected: one sequential generator per world, which shifts every later draw when a sensor is added.
- **ADR-SENS-05 (part): the states of correlated noise processes (bias random walks) are part of every checkpoint**, because a restore that forgets a bias is a different world.
- Every sensor parameter value is `assumed` until a card records a datasheet or a measurement; a Reality claim that rests on an `assumed` field is refused (FROB-REF-447, carried by N20b-18).

Terms. *Specific force* is what an accelerometer measures: acceleration minus gravitational acceleration, in the sensor frame. *Lever arm* is the offset r from a body reference point O to the sensor. *Sensor card* is a `.fcard` of kind `sensor`: a typed parameter record with per-field provenance (`measured`, `datasheet`, `assumed`). *Referee* is the M1 simulation tier.

## Scope
In: the IMU model on the referee (specific force and angular rate with lever arm; then the error chain of misalignment, scale, bias random walk, white noise, saturation and quantization; sample rate; latency field), which is the authoritative definition of the chain and of its order for the whole chapter; the joint encoder's count model in its M1 form (resolution, quantization, index offset, wraparound, count noise, velocity by the `difference` estimator with its window delay) on a rigid transmission; the sensor card schema for these two kinds; addressed noise on the host under the key scheme of N20a-04; the use of the mask and flag types of N20a-02; `sense.profile` v0 (suite, cards with provenance, clocks) for proprioceptive suites; the referee rows of GATE-SENS-01 for the three IMU cases and the turntable case.
Out: the crate skeleton, the mask-reason and flag registry with the `Sample` type, and the stream-key scheme with the correlated-process state type (N20a-01, N20a-02, N20a-04, dependencies); packet queues, link models and delivery (BD-098); the same sensors with noise on batched tiers (N20b-44, BD-187); the completed encoder model: incremental counters, the missed-count process with its state, backlash seen through the transmission card, the tracking-loop velocity estimator (N20a-13); the IMU rows of the failure-mode table: the initial bias draw, exposure averaging, the Allan-deviation and drop-test first checks (N20a-14); the integrating accelerometer across an impact (N20b-39); encoder unwrapping ambiguity (N20b-40); the load-cell cut (N20b-41); ordered force chains (N20b-28); the complete `sense.profile` (N20b-16); the failure-mode first checks gathered under GATE-SENS-14 (BD-238); contact switches and electrical and thermal readouts (N20a-15), force-torque sensors (N20a-16), wheel odometry (N20a-17), tactile arrays (N20a-19).

## Technical approach
**Module layout (DESIGN).** `crates/frob-sense/src/{card.rs, imu.rs, encoder.rs, noise.rs, mask.rs, profile.rs}`; tests in `crates/frob-sense/tests/`; fixtures in `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sensorium/proprio/`.

**IMU kinematics.** For a sensor rigidly mounted at offset r (expressed in the world frame at the sample instant) from a body point O with linear acceleration a_O, angular velocity ω and angular acceleration α of the body:
- point acceleration a_P = a_O + α × r + ω × (ω × r);
- specific force reading f = R_WSᵀ·(a_P − g), where R_WS is the rotation from the sensor frame S to the world frame W and g is the gravitational acceleration vector in W (for z up, g = (0, 0, −9.81) m/s² unless the scene declares otherwise);
- angular rate reading ω_S = R_WSᵀ·ω.
Gravity enters exactly once, in the term −g, for prescribed bases and for floating bases alike. FACT: `fs-mbd` exposes `prepare_point_motion`, `point_motion` and `specific_acceleration_world` for rigid and articulated bodies; the referee path calls them through the FrankenSim airlock crate (`frob-fsim`) and rotates into S. The batched kernel (N20b-44) is derived from the same expression, so the two cannot disagree on the gravity convention.

**IMU error chain, in this fixed order (DESIGN; the order is part of the card schema version).**
1. Ideal values (f, ω_S) at the sample instant.
2. Misalignment and scale: x₁ = M·(I + diag(s))·x, with M a 3×3 matrix near the identity and s the per-axis scale errors (card fields, separately for accelerometer and gyroscope).
3. Bias: x₂ = x₁ + b_k, with b a random walk b_{k+1} = b_k + σ_b·√(Δt)·w_k, w_k standard normal per axis, Δt the sample period, σ_b the card's bias random-walk density, in (m/s²)/√s for the accelerometer and (rad/s)/√s for the gyroscope. b is per-world state and is written to every checkpoint.
4. White noise: x₃ = x₂ + n·√(f_s)·v_k, with n the card's noise density (m/s²/√Hz or rad/s/√Hz), f_s the sample rate and v_k standard normal.
5. Saturation: each axis is clipped to the card range ±L and the packet flag `saturated` is set if any axis clipped.
6. Quantization, if the card declares a least significant bit: round to nearest multiple, ties to even.
Normal variates are built from addressed uniform draws (below). The discrete conventions in 3 and 4 are DESIGN and are stated in `CONTRACT.md` so that the Allan-deviation first check (BD-238) has a definite target.

**Encoders.** Card fields (kind `sensor.encoder`; the field names are those of the completed model in N20a-13, of which M1 implements a subset, so that the card schema does not change between M1 and M2): counts per revolution `cpr`, `side` (`motor` or `load` side of the transmission), `index_offset` (counts), `kind` (`absolute-single-turn`, reported modulo `cpr`; or `absolute-multi-turn` with `turn_bits`; `incremental` with `counter_bits` arrives with N20a-13), `noise_sigma` (standard deviation of shaft-angle noise before quantization, in radians; 0 for none), `p_miss` (the probability that a count edge is missed; must be 0 at M1, the declared loss process with its state being N20a-13's), `velocity_estimator` (`difference { window: N }` at M1; `period-timing` and `tracking-loop` are admitted values completed in N20a-13), sample rate. Model: with the shaft angle θ in turns at the encoder's side (noise converted from radians to turns), c = floor((θ + noise)·cpr) + index_offset, which for an integer index offset equals floor(θ·cpr + index_offset + noise in counts), and the delivered count is c mod cpr for `absolute-single-turn`. Backlash as seen through the transmission (a load-side motion smaller than the dead zone of the transmission card is invisible to a motor-side encoder; the encoder reads the coordinate on its own side of the gap and adds no extra model) is implemented by N20a-13 once the transmission card exists; at M1 the encoder is tested on a rigid transmission. Velocity by the `difference` estimator over a window of N periods T is v̂_k = (c_k − c_{k−N})/(cpr·N·T) turns per second, and the estimate is stamped with the delay N·T/2 its window implies; the packet carries that delay so a consumer never mistakes it for a current value. First check: a prescribed rotation against a known tick count.

**Addressed noise (host form).** A draw is Philox keyed by (world address, sample index, stream_id, component), built on FrankenSim's `fs-rand` (FACT: counter-based Philox streams) through the airlock. `stream_id` is the hash of the sensor's port name and the purpose, registered in the stream registry; the key scheme and the purpose names are N20a-04's (white measurement noise, including count noise, is purpose `noise`; the bias walk is the correlated process `process`, `bias`), and the labels `white`, `bias` and `count-noise` used in this bead and in the kernels of N20b-44 denote those streams. No generator state exists; a draw depends on nothing else. A uniform `f32` in [0, 1) is (x ≫ 8)·2⁻²⁴, exact.

**Masks and flags.** `mask.rs` holds `MaskReason` (an enum; a masked sample carries exactly one reason) and the packet flags (`saturated`, later `ambiguous`, `overloaded`); the type, the closed registry of reasons and flags and the `Sample` accessor are N20a-02's, and this bead uses them. A masked value's payload bytes are a fixed pattern that the typed accessor refuses to return as a number (INV-SENS-05). At M1 the IMU and encoders mask only for `fault: dead` and `not-yet-sampled` (registry names of N20a-02).

**Sensor card (schema `frob.card.sensor/1`, an instance of the `.fcard` schema of BD-069).** Common fields: `kind` (`imu`, `encoder`, later kinds), `frame`, `units` per channel, `rate` (a rational in hertz), `latency` (rational seconds), `exposure` (rational seconds, 0 for instantaneous), `timestamp_point` (`start`, `middle`, `end`), every field with provenance. A card that fails its own checks (negative rate, range below noise floor, units that do not match the kind) is refused by the card loader with the design-language card refusal already defined for cards.

**`sense.profile` v0 (read; kind `read`; determinism D0).** `frob sense profile <design> [--task T] --json` returns, inside the standard envelope: the sensor suite of the design (id, kind, parent link, mount transform, card identity), each card field with its provenance and a count of `assumed` fields, and each sensor's `ClockSpec` (period and phase as exact rationals, rate error, intra-step policy). Output schema `frob.sense.profile.out/1`; later beads add link models, queue capacities, chains, grades and unambiguous speeds without changing existing fields. Evidence block: class `Tested`, tier `static`, determinism `exact`, model identity, no seed, Reality `no-claim`.

**Evidence and determinism of samples.** Referee samples in `f64` are class D2 on the referee (same bits run to run); draws are D0. Sample values are `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Code in `crates/frob-sense` with `CONTRACT.md` (ten sections: purpose, inputs, outputs, invariants, refusals, determinism class, evidence class of outputs, validity domain, oracles, known gaps); the known gaps name the sensors and tiers not yet covered.
- [ ] The three IMU cases and the turntable case of GATE-SENS-01 pass on the referee (the seed done-condition): at rest the reading is (0, 0, +g) with zero rate; in free fall without rotation the specific force is zero; in free fall spinning at 5 rad/s with a 0.10 m perpendicular offset it is 2.5 m/s² toward the axis; on a turntable the gyroscope reads the table rate. Tolerance with noise off: 10⁻⁹ relative in `f64` (TARGET, to set at the gate).
- [ ] Gravity enters exactly once for a prescribed base and for a floating base: the at-rest case passes on both.
- [ ] Negative control caught: a build with gravity applied twice reads (0, 0, 2g) at rest (or −g in free fall) and fails all three IMU cases.
- [ ] Encoder: a prescribed rotation of K turns yields exactly K·cpr counts with the index offset and wraparound applied (KA-510); the `difference` velocity carries its N·T/2 delay.
- [ ] Adding a second IMU to the design leaves every sample of the first IMU and of every encoder bit-identical (ADR-SENS-10 at unit level).
- [ ] A checkpoint taken mid-run and restored reproduces the bias state and every subsequent sample byte for byte.
- [ ] `frob sense profile quad-mammal --json` returns one envelope that validates against `frob.sense.profile.out/1`, lists provenance per field and the `assumed` count; reachable on the CLI and over MCP as `sense_profile`.
- [ ] Cancellation of a referee run with sensors enabled leaves no partial packet published.
- [ ] The referee rows of GATE-SENS-01 are recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with measured values, commit and seed.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
(`cargo test -p frob-sense`; real components, no mocks; fixtures labelled `synthetic` where built by hand.)
- `imu_at_rest_reads_plus_g` (KA-466): level sensor, body at rest on a prescribed base and on a floating base resting on a plane; expect f = (0, 0, 9.81), ω_S = 0.
- `imu_free_fall_reads_zero` (KA-467): body released with ω = 0; expect f = 0 at every sample.
- `imu_spinning_free_fall_lever_arm` (KA-468): ω = 5 rad/s about z, r = 0.10 m along x in the body; expect |f| = 2.5 m/s² directed toward the axis (−x in the body), since ω × (ω × r) = −ω²·r_⊥ and a_O = g.
- `imu_turntable_reads_table_rate` (KA-469): prescribed rotation Ω about z; gyroscope reads (0, 0, Ω); accelerometer reads gravity plus Ω²·ρ toward the axis for a sensor at radius ρ.
- `imu_error_chain_order`: with a card of scale 1%, bias fixed at 0.1 m/s² and range 20 m/s², an input of 30 m/s² reads 20 m/s² with `saturated` set (scale and bias applied before the clip).
- `encoder_prescribed_rotation_counts` (KA-510): cpr = 4,096, 3.25 turns, index offset 17: multi-turn count 13,312 + 17; single-turn 1,024 + 17.
- `encoder_velocity_window_delay`: N = 4, T = 1 ms: delay field 2 ms.
- `noise_addressed_not_ordered`: property test over random suites: permuting, adding or removing other sensors leaves a sensor's draw sequence unchanged.
- `bias_state_in_checkpoint`: restore reproduces bytes.
- `masked_value_is_not_a_number`: the typed accessor returns the reason, never a float (INV-SENS-05).
- `profile_schema_roundtrip`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_proprio_referee.sh` (sources the shared helper of STD-E2E; the chapter-level script N20b-22 re-runs these rows on every tier): `frob design templates`, `frob sim run` on the four fixtures above with seed 0 and noise off, `frob sense profile quad-mammal --json`; asserts each reading against its truth and the profile schema. Logs to `target/e2e-logs/sense_proprio_referee/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it: each `cmd` with exact command line, exit code and wall time; the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); a `metric` line per reading with value, unit, truth and tolerance; on failure the first differing component and the run's artifact path; a final `summary` with assertions passed and failed and gate GATE-SENS-01 (referee rows).
### Known answers and negative controls
KA-466 (0, 0, +g; zero rate), KA-467 (zero), KA-468 (2.5 m/s² toward the axis), KA-469 (table rate), KA-510 (tick count). Control: gravity applied twice (the one control the chapter states for the three IMU cases). Added control (synthetic): lever-arm term omitted, which passes KA-466 and KA-467 and fails KA-468, showing why the spinning case exists. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS on the referee, against class D2.

## Considerations
- The referee's `f64` path relies on FrankenSim's elementary functions; a cross-ISA bit claim is not made until the upstream deterministic-functions work item lands.
- Encoder backlash depends on the transmission card; until BD-074 lands the encoder is tested on a rigid transmission and the reversal test (KA-511) stays open under BD-238.
- OPEN-SENS-4 (which cards ship with datasheet or measured fields): default all `assumed`, Reality `no-claim`. Nothing here blocks on it.
- The noise conventions (σ·√Δt for the walk, n·√f_s for white noise) must match what the invariant filter's process model assumes; a mismatch shows as a miscalibrated innovation test in BD-114, not here.
- Memory: one bias state of six `f64` per IMU per world; negligible.
