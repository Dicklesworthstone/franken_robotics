## Background
A morphology has two symmetry groups (construction C-09, architecture decision ADR-MARROW-06), and an agent designing a robot needs both printed for it before it ties policy weights, deduplicates candidates or reads a block-diagonal modal analysis.

- **Γ_mech**, the mechanical group: automorphisms of the labelled kinematic tree realized by an isometry of the body at its reference configuration, preserving link Regions, fills and materials (hence masses and pseudo-inertias), joint screw bases under the parity rule, joint limits, transmissions with signed ratios, actuator cards, end-effector contact laws with interface cards, and the topology of the power bus. It ignores what the robot sees and what it is asked to do.
- **Γ_obs ≤ Γ_mech**, the observation-preserving subgroup: the elements that also preserve the sensor suite as a set (mount, field of view, scan pattern, rate, clock and noise card of each sensor), every payload, the direction of gravity at the reference configuration, and every observation, action, reward and requirement term of the task, each of which must declare how it transforms. An element under which some term has no declared transformation is excluded.

The smallest case that fixes the rule (CX-209, KA-680): a quadruped built symmetric fore-aft and left-right has Γ_mech = C₂ × C₂ of order four. Add one forward-facing lidar (or depth camera) on the sagittal plane. The left-right mirror maps the sensor to itself; the fore-aft mirror and the half-turn do not: an obstacle ahead is seen, an obstacle behind is not. So Γ_obs = C₂, and the breaker of the other two elements is the sensor mount. A policy tied under C₂ × C₂ would have to respond to the unseen obstacle as the mirror image of its response to the seen one.

This bead is analysis A-03 of the studio's analysis table and its verb. A-03 answers: what is the symmetry group, how does it act, what is the smallest edit that restores a near-symmetry, and which subgroup also preserves the sensors, the payload and the task. The group computation itself lives in `frob-algebra::symmetry` (determinism class D0; Marrow's work, beads N13-15 for Γ_mech and N13-16 for the representations, Γ_obs and the breakers). It is run once per model by the symmetry functor of the model compiler (`frob-model`, bead N14a-11), which stores both groups, the representations and the breakers with their restoring edits in the compiled model. This bead owns what sits on top, in `frob-analyze` (a higher layer than `frob-model`, so the functor cannot call it): the analysis wrapper that reads that stored section, the near-symmetry report, the repair edits for near-symmetries, the list of forced ties, the verb, and the report that stage 5 of `design check` returns. The comparison of the computed groups with a symmetry the design declares is not here: it is made in `frob-model` (N14a-11), the only code allowed to read the declaration. Tier: Solid. Symmetry is computed, never declared: an author's declaration is an assertion to check. Rejected alternative: hand-written mirror functions with per-robot sign tables, which is what the computation replaces.

Why the report includes near-symmetries: designs are often meant to be symmetric and are not (an imported model with one thigh 1 mm longer, an IMU mounted 12 mm off the sagittal plane). Reporting "trivial group" hides the cheap repair. The descent that verifies a candidate isometry records its first mismatch with its field path and its size, and A-03 turns that into a typed edit.

## Technical approach
**Inputs.** A design identity (resolved to its `ModelId`), optionally a task (for the task terms and payload) and a scene family (for the world-law declaration). Without a task, Γ_obs is computed against the sensor suite, payloads and gravity only, and the output says so (`obs_scope: "sensors-payload-gravity"`).

**Algorithm (the analysis wrapper).** The eight steps of the group computation run in `frob-algebra::symmetry` and are run by the symmetry functor (N14a-11) when the model is compiled; steps 1 to 4 below read their results from the model's symmetry section (the design-level groups, and the task-level group stored beside them under the task's digest when a task is supplied) and compute nothing a second time. The wrapper calls the algebra itself only for the collect-all descent of step 5 and the fixed subspaces of step 6. What each stored item is:
1. Γ_mech: node colours by isometry-invariant keys, canonical tree form, candidate isometries (at most 2·m·(m − 1)·(m − 2) from m non-collinear attachment points in one class on the root body; signed axis permutations of the root's principal frame plus declared `mirror` and `radial` generators when fewer), verification by descent, closure, multiplication table, classes and characters.
2. The induced representations, as integer signed permutations (one `u16` index and one `i8` sign per channel per element) on joint positions, velocities, actuator channels, the base twist, and each observation term by its declared kind (invariant scalar, polar vector, axial vector, joint-indexed, foot-indexed, sensor-indexed, or a scan with a ray permutation). Under an isometry with orthogonal part P, polar vectors map by P and axial vectors by det(P)·P, so a mirrored limb can need a sign change at its encoder.
3. The Γ_obs filter. For each element the first field that excludes it is its **breaker**: a field path (`sensors.lidar_front.mount`, `payloads.tote.pose`, `task.reward.heading`, `gravity`) with the size of the mismatch.
4. For each breaker, the smallest typed edit that would restore the element, as the functor stored it (N14a-11 computes it; the kinds are `move-sensor`, `add-sensor` (add the image sensor with the same card), `remove-sensor`, `move-payload`, or, for a task term with no declared transformation, the declaration that is missing). The wrapper adds the edit's predicted effect and prints it as a command.
5. Near-symmetry report. For every candidate isometry that failed verification, re-run the descent in collect-all mode (DESIGN: the first-mismatch descent is the fast path; collect-all runs only for the report). A candidate with k mismatching fields, each numeric, is a near-symmetry of rank k; report rank-1 and rank-2 candidates (DESIGN default; `--near-rank` changes it) sorted by the largest relative mismatch. Each entry carries: the would-be element (isometry and permutation), each mismatching field path, its two values, the absolute and relative difference, and the typed edit (`set-param`, `move-mount`, `set-material`, `regear`, `move-sensor`) that makes the field equal its image. For an IMU mounted off the plane of symmetry the entry offers two repairs: move the IMU by the stated offset, or declare the lever-arm correction part of the observation map (the observation term then reports the specific force at a point on the plane and transforms as a vector).
6. Fixed subspaces. For each non-identity element of Γ_obs and each representation, list the fixed subspace, so the report can name the observations at which a deterministic tied controller is forced to issue tied actions (the two-latches case: two identical latches of which exactly one must close; at a mirror-fixed observation a tied deterministic policy commands both alike and fails, while a policy that draws one at random is equivariant in law and succeeds). The report states that a failed search over tied deterministic policies excludes only that class.
7. Classify the evidence: route (i) by construction (limbs are instances of one subterm under `mirror` or `radial`) and route (ii) by exact field arithmetic (ℚ for mirrors; the real subfield of the cyclotomic field of order lcm(n, 4) for n-fold rotations, n ≤ 12) are D0 and `Certified` when the certificate is emitted; route (iii) by tolerance, for imported designs, is `Tested`, records the tolerance, and feeds the near-symmetry report.

**Output (`frob.analyze.symmetry.out/1`, inside the standard envelope).** `mech`: order, name where recognized (C2, C2xC2, Dn, Cn), generators, elements; `obs`: the same, with `obs_scope`; `representations` per channel space; `breakers`: per excluded element {element, field path, size, repair edit with predicted effect}; `near_symmetries`; `forced_ties`; `route` per element; `continuous_stabilizer` when a body of revolution with a single axial limb has one (only the constructor-generated finite subgroup is used). `next_actions` carries the repair edits as commands. Evidence: class per step 7, tier `static`, determinism `exact`, model identity, no seed.

**Registry entry.** Not written by hand: N28a-05's generator emits it from this analysis's registration, and `check-generated` holds it equal to the row VERBS-analyze entered. `analyze.symmetry`, cluster `analyze`, kind `read`, tier S/F (the Solid core is this bead's; the Frontier extension `--symmetry-events`, from M6, is N25d-02's), task clusters `analyze-body` and `symmetry`, capability `analyze:read` (the cluster's one read scope; an earlier draft of this bead had `design:read`, which N28a-05 and VERBS-analyze replaced for all twenty-six analysis verbs), first milestone M1, output schema `frob.analyze.symmetry.out/1`, refusals FROB-REF-109 (through `design check`), FROB-REF-214 (a representation with a constituent of quaternionic type, or a symmetry group outside the supported families), FROB-REF-215 (an exact evaluation over its bit budget: the collect-all descent and the fixed subspaces of steps 5 and 6 run in exact field arithmetic), and FROB-REF-551 (on every analysis verb by the cluster rule), and from M6 FROB-REF-263 and FROB-REF-852 (both N25d-02's, raised only with `--symmetry-events`), idempotent, cost "static: 150 ms at the 95th percentile on archetypes (TARGET, the stage-5 budget)". MCP tool `analyze_symmetry`.

**Stage 5 of `design check`.** The design may carry assertions (`assert_mech: C2xC2`, `assert_obs: C2`). The comparison of each with the computed group is made by `frob_model::symmetry::assert` (N14a-11), which the stage runner (BD-110) calls at stage 5; this analysis never sees the assertions. A difference is refused with FROB-REF-109 "declared symmetry differs from computed", exit 3. What this bead supplies to that refusal is the report: the computed groups, the breaker of every excluded element with its restoring edit, and the near-symmetry entries, attached as `next_actions` after the comparison's own ranked repairs (first `assert-symmetry` with the computed group, then the breaker's restoring edit, then removing the assertion; that order is N14a-11's). Known case: a forward lidar on a symmetric quadruped with `assert_obs: C2xC2` is refused with the lidar mount as breaker.

**Fixtures (DESIGN).** The archetype templates (`quad-mammal` and the others) and the stage runner arrive with later seeds (BD-109; BD-110, which depends on this bead), so this bead's tests and script run on committed synthetic designs in `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/atelier/symmetry/`, labelled `synthetic`: `sym-quad` (a quadruped built symmetric fore-aft and left-right, 4 legs × 3 joints), `sym-biped` (2 legs × 6 joints, one mirror), `sym-diff` (a two-wheel differential base) and `sym-mecanum` (four mecanum wheels), shaped like the four M1 archetypes so that the timing and nested-groups rows measure the same sizes. The same rows on the archetypes themselves, and the stage-5 refusal through `frob design check --stages 5`, are BD-110's (it depends on this bead and on the templates).

**Memo node.** Key: the model identity restricted to the fields the analysis reads (tree, Regions, fills, materials, joints, transmissions, actuator cards, contact laws, bus topology, sensors, payloads, task terms), the analysis version and its parameters. An edit outside those fields recomputes nothing. Typed edits declare their symmetry effect (`move-sensor`: Γ_mech preserved, Γ_obs recomputed and reported; `add-payload`: both recomputed), and the analysis is the function that honours the declaration.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `frob analyze symmetry <design> [--task T] --json` returns one envelope with the fields above; generated on the CLI and as MCP tool `analyze_symmetry`; schema under `schemas/`.
- [ ] The seed done-condition: a symmetric quadruped with one forward lidar reports Γ_mech = C₂ × C₂, Γ_obs = C₂ and the lidar mount as breaker for the fore-aft mirror and the half-turn, each with a restoring edit.
- [ ] The seed done-condition: a planted 0.4% asymmetry (one thigh length scaled by 1.004 on one leg of `sym-quad`) is found as a rank-1 near-symmetry with the field path, the relative size 0.4% and the `set-param` edit that restores the element; applying the edit and re-running yields the full group.
- [ ] An IMU moved 12 mm off the sagittal plane is reported as a near-symmetry of Γ_obs with both repairs.
- [ ] Removing every sensor, payload and task term gives Γ_obs = Γ_mech (the nested-groups law).
- [ ] With `assert_obs: C2xC2` on the forward-lidar quadruped, the stage-5 report this bead attaches to the refusal FROB-REF-109 that N14a-11's comparison raises carries the computed group C₂, the lidar mount as breaker and its restoring edit among `next_actions` (asserted by calling the attachment with N14a-11's comparison result; the user-side `frob design check --stages 5` leg with exit 3 is asserted by BD-110).
- [ ] The groups, representations and breakers the verb prints are byte-equal to the model's symmetry section; a build in which the wrapper recomputes a group that differs from the section fails the test.
- [ ] The two-latches fixture lists the mirror-fixed observation under `forced_ties` and the report words the exclusion as "tied deterministic class only".
- [ ] A changed sole material on one foot: the encoder permutation still exists in `representations` and the element is absent from Γ_mech, both recorded (CX-121).
- [ ] The analysis wrapper completes within 150 ms at the 95th percentile on the four archetype-shaped fixtures (TARGET; measured by this bead's script and logged with the fixtures named); the stage-5 time on the archetypes themselves is GATE-ATEL-02's, measured and recorded by BD-110.
- [ ] The A-03 known answers this verb serves (KA-680, the forward-lidar case, CX-209, CX-119, CX-121) pass with measured values in this bead's tests and script; negative control caught: a build that reports Γ_mech as Γ_obs fails the forward-lidar case. This bead writes no gate record: the M1 part of GATE-ATEL-02 is recorded by BD-110 and the M1 part of GATE-MARROW-03 by N13-21, both of which depend on this bead and re-measure these rows on the archetypes.

### Known answers and negative controls

Planted failures: Γ_mech returned as Γ_obs; a parity table that flips all three axes of a mirrored joint (must fail the descent); a near-symmetry report that drops the field path. Determinism: D0; identical envelope bytes for identical requests on Linux and macOS.

## Considerations
- The time budget assumes groups of at most a few dozen elements; a radial design with n above 12 uses the constructor route or the tolerance route (OPEN-MARROW-2, default no exact extension).
- A continuous stabilizer is reported, never enumerated.
- The M1 sensor inputs do not include timing: a spinning lidar on the plane of symmetry is accepted here and correctly excluded at M3 by the timing predicate of N20b-01; the M1 output carries `scan_timing: "not-checked"` so nobody reads it as a licence for tying.
- Hard equivariance is not free (FACT, source register, to be re-read: augmentation makes an ordinary network about as data-efficient as an equivariant one); this verb reports groups and makes no learning claim.
- Retired when a design's Γ_obs is trivial: the report then says tying is off and augmentation alone remains.
