## Background
For every tuple (design, sensor profile, task, scene family) the studio stores the observation-preserving subgroup Γ_obs: the elements of the mechanical symmetry group Γ_mech that also preserve every sensor (mount, card and pattern **including its timing**), every clock, link and latency of the interface, the payload's inertia and any declared asymmetry of the load, the task (goal set, requirement predicates, reward terms) and the law of the scenes. The elements that preserve a structure form a subgroup, so Γ_obs ≤ Γ_mech. Owner: `frob-sense` for the predicates (N20b-01) and this verb; `frob-algebra` for the group operations.

The M1 analysis `analyze.symmetry` (BD-102) prints both groups for a design. This bead is the M3 completion on the sensing side: the group is computed with the timing-aware predicates, stored under its tuple so that consumers are licensed against exactly the interface they will run (N20b-02), exposed by the read verb `sense.symmetry`, and **tested on running worlds**. The test is what turns a computed group into evidence: a group computed from cards and mounts says the closed loop is symmetric; the equivariance test runs the closed loop and checks.

Two hand cases the gate requires on every template: a forward depth camera on a fore-aft and left-right symmetric quadruped gives Γ_obs of order two inside Γ_mech of order four (the left-right reflection survives if rows are read out top to bottom); a spinning lidar on the plane of symmetry breaks the reflection under per-beam instants, at first order in the product of body rate and scan time, and is restored only if the scan is treated as instantaneous, with the report saying which was assumed.

Tier: Solid for the computation, storage and test. Equivariant policies built on Γ_obs are Frontier and belong to the learning chapter. Serves the rule that only the observation-preserving subgroup may be used for weight sharing, augmentation, deduplication of closed-loop results and gait-orbit quotients (INV-SENS-08).

## Scope
In: the stored `ObsGroup` record and its tuple key; the verb `sense.symmetry`; the equivariance test (the world-image construction, the permutation of noise stream identifiers, the comparison); the two hand cases as fixtures on every template; the gate record for GATE-SENS-10.
Out: predicates (N20b-01); the guard and FROB-REF-443 (N20b-02); the end-to-end script (N20b-03); the group algorithms (Marrow); the metamorphic test of the whole simulator under Γ_mech with the world transformed (theorem card T-10, Marrow and Gauntlet).

## Technical approach
**Stored record (schema `frob.sense.obsgroup/1`).** Key: (`DesignId`, digest of the sensor profile: suite, cards, patterns, clocks, links; `TaskId`; scene family identifier). Value: Γ_mech (order, generators), Γ_obs (elements, generators), the induced representations on every channel space as integer signed permutations (`u16` index and `i8` sign per channel per element), the excluded elements each with its breaker (family, field path, size, restoring typed edit), `scan_timing` (`per-beam` or `instantaneous`), the evidence route per element, and the identity of the equivariance-test receipt once one exists. The record is content-addressed and is a memo node: any typed edit that touches a field in the key produces a new key (`add-sensor`, `move-sensor`, `remove-sensor`, `add-payload`, `move-payload`, a task change, a scene-family change).

**Verb `sense.symmetry`** (kind `read`, first milestone M3, tier S, capability `design:read`, MCP tool `sense_symmetry`). `frob sense symmetry <design> --task T [--scene-family F] [--scan-timing per-beam|instantaneous] [--test]`. Returns Γ_obs, the elements of Γ_mech it excludes and the sensor or task that excludes each, the `scan_timing` assumed, and, with `--test` or when a receipt exists, the equivariance-test outcome. Output schema `frob.sense.symmetry.out/1`. Refusals: FROB-REF-443 is raised by consumers, not by this read; an unsupported group family is FROB-REF-214. Evidence: class `Certified` or `Tested` by route for the group; the test outcome is `Tested` with the tier and seed of the runs; determinism `exact` for the group.

**The equivariance test (metamorphic relation MR-25).** For each generator g of Γ_obs:
1. Take a world w: a world address, the design, a scene drawn from the family, the task, a controller or policy, a seed.
2. Build the image world g·w: the scene transformed by g's world isometry, the initial state transformed by the induced representation ρ_X(g) (joint coordinates by the signed permutation, base pose and twist by the isometry with the polar and axial rule), commands transformed by ρ_U(g) if the controller is open-loop, or the same closed-loop controller with its symmetric counterpart where the controller itself is not equivariant (the test of the *interface* uses a prescribed open-loop command sequence so that controller asymmetry cannot mask or fake a result).
3. **Permute the noise stream identifiers by g**: the stream of sensor s′ = g·s in the image world is given the stream identifier of s in w, and per-ray components are permuted by the pattern permutation π. Without this the two worlds draw different noise and nothing can match.
4. Run both worlds for the test horizon (DESIGN default 2 s of simulated time, to cover at least 20 scans of the slowest sensor).
5. Compare the delivered observation streams packet by packet. For each packet of sensor s in w and the corresponding packet of g·s in g·w, require: equal sequence numbers; equal sample, exposure and arrival instants (integers, exact); equal masks under the channel permutation; payloads equal under the signed permutation.
6. Pass for g when every packet corresponds.
For an element h of Γ_mech **outside** Γ_obs the same procedure is run and must **fail**; that is the negative control, and it is run for every excluded element of every template.

**What "equal" means for float payloads (DESIGN, with a stated risk).** The chapter requires exact correspondence up to the signed permutation. Integer fields and masks are compared exactly. Float payloads are compared bit for bit after applying the signed permutation; a sign flip is exact in IEEE arithmetic and reflections here are signed coordinate permutations, so exactness is attainable where the kernels' reduction order is carried along by the permutation. Where a per-world reduction orders its terms by link or ray index, the mirrored world adds the same terms in another order and the last bits can differ. The test therefore records, per channel, the largest difference in units in the last place; the gate requires zero for integer fields and masks and zero for float payloads on the referee and the strict CPU profile; on throughput tiers a non-zero value is a finding that must be traced to a named reduction and either fixed (index-covariant reduction) or recorded as a tolerance decision. A non-member must fail by a margin far outside round-off (a mask or a range differing by more than the sensor noise), so that the control cannot pass by tolerance.

**Fixtures.** `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sensorium/symmetry/`: for each template, the template with (a) a forward depth camera with top-to-bottom rows on the sagittal plane, (b) a spinning lidar on the plane of symmetry, each with a scene family declared invariant under the template's reflections and a goal-free tracking task whose terms declare their transformations.

**Tier and determinism.** The group is D0. The test runs on the referee at M3 for every template and on the batched tiers for the templates that run there; outputs carry the determinism class of the tier they ran on.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `frob sense symmetry … --json` returns one envelope validating against `frob.sense.symmetry.out/1`, with excluded elements and their breakers; reachable over MCP.
- [ ] The record is stored under its four-part key; editing the sensor mount, the payload, the task or the scene family yields a different key and the old record is not returned.
- [ ] GATE-SENS-10 (the seed done-condition): the two cases of the chapter hold on every template: the forward-camera case gives Γ_obs equal to the subgroup of Γ_mech fixing the camera (order two inside order four on `quad-mammal`), and the spinning-lidar case is broken under per-beam instants and preserved only under the recorded instantaneous-scan assumption.
- [ ] The equivariance test passes for every generator of Γ_obs and fails for every element of Γ_mech outside Γ_obs, on every template; counts recorded (members passed, non-members failed, zero exceptions).
- [ ] Float-payload differences: zero units in the last place on the referee and the strict CPU profile; per-channel maxima recorded for throughput tiers.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: (i) the noise-stream permutation omitted (members then fail, showing the test can fail); (ii) the timing clause removed from the predicates (the spinning-lidar reflection is admitted as a member and then fails the test); (iii) a non-member presented as a member fails.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` section for the verb; the gate recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with measured counts, commit, fingerprint and seeds.

## Considerations
- The image-world construction needs every initial-state field to have a declared transformation (bias states, queue contents, turn counters); a field without one makes the test refuse to run rather than pass vacuously.
- Cost: two worlds per generator per template; small. The full sweep over eighteen templates and both fixtures is a few hundred short runs.
- If exact float correspondence proves unattainable on a throughput tier, that is a finding for the determinism program, not a reason to weaken the member/non-member separation.
- A design whose Γ_obs is trivial has nothing to test; the verb says so and consumers fall back to no tying and plain augmentation-free training.
- Every move of a mount by the visibility optimizer (N20b-36) invalidates the record; the optimizer must recompute before reusing any licensed symmetry.
