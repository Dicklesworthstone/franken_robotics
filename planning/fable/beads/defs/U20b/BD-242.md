## Background
Extrinsic calibration of a sensor rig answers: where, relative to the base, is each sensor really mounted, and is the set of pairwise measurements consistent? The studio treats it as weighted least squares on a graph and says so. Nodes are sensor frames and the base; unknowns are corrections δ_v in 𝔰𝔢(3) to each mount transform, with the base anchored at zero to remove the six-dimensional gauge; edges are pairwise relative-pose measurements (camera to IMU, lidar to odometry, hand to eye), each with a residual in 𝔰𝔢(3) and a 6 × 6 weight, the inverse of its covariance. The Gauss–Newton step solves (Bᵀ·W·B)·δ = Bᵀ·W·r with B the block incidence matrix carrying adjoint blocks on the tail side, and the update is applied on the group by a retraction.

The verb `sense.calibrate` exposes this: "extrinsic corrections with covariance, the consistency residual per edge, and a fault attribution where the graph supports one". Its first milestone is M3 in simulation and M5 on imported logs (N20b-08). Three consumers: the simulated robot's self-check after an impact (N20b-06); fault injection, where a planted extrinsic bump must be found; and Ground, which runs the same solver on logs from a real rig.

What the verb must get right beyond the solve is the difference between two faults. A bumped sensor is a change of extrinsics: the solve absorbs it, the estimate at that node moves by the bump and the consistency residual r_⊥ = (I − B·(Bᵀ·W·B)⁻¹·Bᵀ·W)·r stays at noise level. A faulty edge is inconsistent round every cycle through it: r_⊥ is not small. And it must refuse what the graph cannot support: on a tree r_⊥ is identically zero and localization is refused (FROB-REF-446); a single cycle detects but does not identify a bad edge. Tier: Solid. Owner: `frob-sense`.

## Scope
In: the verb (inputs, outputs, schemas, refusals, job behaviour); the simulated measurement front end that produces pairwise edges from a simulated rig; assembling the graph from a design's sensor suite; reporting corrections, covariance, per-edge residuals, the support report and the attribution; the gate GATE-SENS-11 at the level of the verb (the 100-seed bump record and the residual patterns).
Out: solver internals (N20b-04); diagnosis internals (N20b-05); the bump fault and the self-check (N20b-06); the end-to-end script (N20b-07); real front ends and imported logs (N20b-08).

## Technical approach
**Verb** (`registries/verbs.toml`): name `sense.calibrate`; cluster `sense`; kind `run` (takes an idempotency key and a budget; bounded synchronous wait; otherwise a job handle with exit 8); tier S; capability `sim:run`; input schema `frob.sense.calibrate.in/1`; output schema `frob.sense.calibrate.out/1`; refusals FROB-REF-446, FROB-REF-447 (through the Reality guard, only when a Reality claim is requested); first milestone M3 (simulation), extended at M5 (imported logs); MCP tool `sense_calibrate`. Example: `frob sense calibrate rig-a --episode ep:… --anchor base --localize --json`.

**Inputs.** A design (its sensor suite gives the nodes and the believed mounts); a source of edges, one of (a) `--episode <id>`: a simulated episode from which the front end below produces pairwise measurements; (b) `--edges <file>` in schema `frob.sense.calib.edges/1`: explicit edges (tail, head, measured pose, weight, provenance), which is what real logs will supply; `--anchor <node>` (default the base; if the rig has no edge to the base, the caller must name a sensor declared coincident with the base, otherwise FROB-REF-446 "no anchor"); `--reference <calibration id>` for the bump test; `--localize` to request fault attribution; `--retraction exp|cay`.

**The simulated front end (DESIGN; labelled `synthetic`).** The chapter specifies the solver and takes pairwise measurements as given. In simulation at M3 an edge between sensors i and j is produced as T_ij = (T_i^true)⁻¹·T_j^true·exp(ξ), where T^true are the true mounts in the episode (after any bump), and ξ is a noise twist drawn by an addressed stream (world address, edge identifier, component) from N(0, Σ_ij). Σ_ij comes from the two sensor cards (a `pairwise_extrinsic_noise` field per sensor kind pair, with provenance; `assumed` until a card says otherwise) and W_ij = Σ_ij⁻¹. Which pairs produce an edge is declared in the sensor profile (`calibration_pairs`), defaulting to every pair of sensors with overlapping fields of view or a shared motion (IMU with any moving sensor). The front end is deliberately simple and is marked `synthetic` in the envelope: it tests the graph mathematics and the attribution, not a detector for calibration targets. Real front ends (target detection, scan registration, motion-based alignment) arrive with imported logs.

**Algorithm.**
1. Resolve the design and the edge source; build the graph; run the structural check (anchor, connectivity).
2. Solve (N20b-04); obtain δ̂, the updated mounts and Σ_δ.
3. Compute r_⊥ per edge, the global consistency statistic and the support report (N20b-05).
4. If `--reference` is given, run the bump test per node. If `--localize` is given, run the bad-edge and bump attribution; if the support report does not allow the requested localization, refuse with FROB-REF-446 and the repairs.
5. Return the envelope; store the solution as a calibration record (content-addressed: design, edge set digest, anchor, retraction, solution) that later runs can name as `--reference`.

**Output.** `corrections`: per node the twist (angular in rad, linear in m), also as degrees and millimetres, and its 6 × 6 covariance; `mounts`: updated transforms; `edges`: per edge r_⊥ (six components), angular and linear norms with units, the whitened norm, `detectable`, `identifiable`; `consistency`: statistic, degrees of freedom, verdict; `support`: per node `bump_separable`; `attribution`: one of `none`, `bump at <node>` with the change, `bad edge <edge>`, `inconsistent, not localized` with candidates; `front_end: "synthetic" | "explicit"`; `anchor`; `iterations`, `converged`. Evidence block: class `Tested`; tier of the episode (or `static` for explicit edges); determinism class of the source; identities (design, episode or edge digest, calibration record); seed; `reality: "no-claim"`.

**Refusals.**
| Code | Trigger | Ranked repairs | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| FROB-REF-446 | No anchor; a component not connected to the anchor; fault localization on a graph without the needed cycles | Anchor the base (or name a sensor coincident with it); add pairwise measurements (the report lists which edges would make each edge identifiable) | 3 |
| FROB-REF-447 | The caller asked for a Reality claim and an edge weight or a card field it rests on is `assumed` | Record a datasheet or a measurement; drop the claim | 3 |

**Gate fixture for GATE-SENS-11.** `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sensorium/calib/rig4.toml`: a base with four sensors (IMU declared coincident with the base and used as anchor, depth camera, lidar, wheel-odometry frame) and all six pairwise edges among the four; synthetic edge noise of 0.01° and 0.15 mm per axis (fixture parameters, DESIGN, to be confirmed against the covariance the solver reports so that the 0.1° and 1 mm tolerance is at least six standard deviations of the estimate including lever-arm coupling; the values are frozen in the fixture before the 100 seeds are run and are never tuned per seed); a planted lidar bump of 2° about the lidar's vertical axis and 15 mm along its x axis; 100 seeds.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `frob sense calibrate … --json` returns one envelope validating against `frob.sense.calibrate.out/1`; reachable over MCP; job behaviour and cancellation tested (a cancelled run publishes no partial calibration record).
- [ ] GATE-SENS-11 (the seed done-condition): the triangle and four-sensor residual patterns hold (β/3 on every edge of the triangle; β/2 on the bad edge, β/4 on the four adjacent edges, 0 on the opposite edge among four sensors); the planted bump of 2° and 15 mm is recovered within 0.1° and 1 mm and attributed to the lidar node in 100 of 100 seeds, with r_⊥ at noise level.
- [ ] A tree-shaped rig with `--localize` is refused with FROB-REF-446, exit 3, and the repair names the edges to add.
- [ ] A rig with no edge to the base and no declared anchor is refused with FROB-REF-446 "no anchor".
- [ ] Negative controls caught at the level of the verb: the Euclidean projector with anisotropic weights changes the per-edge residuals and fails the pattern test; the tail-adjoint frame mutant fails the bump recovery; a build that attributes by largest raw residual mislabels a bump as a bad edge on at least one seed.
- [ ] The envelope marks the synthetic front end and carries `reality: "no-claim"`; a Reality claim request with `assumed` weights is refused with FROB-REF-447.
- [ ] The gate is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with the measured worst-case recovery error over the 100 seeds (degrees and millimetres), the attribution count, commit and seeds.

## Considerations
- The anchor rule is an interpretation the fixture makes explicit: the chapter anchors the base, and a rig described only by sensor-to-sensor edges has no edge to it. Declaring one sensor coincident with the base is the smallest way to keep the gauge fixed without inventing a measurement.
- The synthetic front end cannot show front-end failure modes (a target seen at a grazing angle, degenerate motion for hand-eye). Those enter with logs; until then results say `synthetic`.
- Attribution assumes one fault. Two faults are reported as `inconsistent, not localized` unless one dominates.
- Cost: negligible (a dense solve of at most a few hundred unknowns); the verb is `run` because it reads an episode and writes a record, not because it is expensive.
- Calibration expiry and drift belong to Ground; a calibration record here has no expiry.
