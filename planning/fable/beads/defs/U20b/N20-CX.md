## Background
A counterexample is the smallest case in which a tempting shortcut gives the wrong answer. The counterexample catalogue seeds the negative-control registry (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml`): each shortcut becomes a planted defect that some gate's test must catch, because a gate that nothing can fail validates nothing. A CX number is never reused and never renumbered; new rows append. Mechanism beads cite CX numbers in their test plans and state the values they need inline; this bead is the chapter's registry, so that the sixteen numbered entries and the shortcuts of the three late subsections exist as data with an owner test each.

## Scope
In: the sixteen catalogue rows of the Sensorium chapter as entries of the counterexample catalogue `crates/frob-conformance/registry/counterexamples.toml`, in the record schema of N38b-05 (`id`, `shortcut`, `case`, `numeric`, `plan_case`, `case_supplied_by`, `rule`, `rule_ids`, `defined_in`, `same_as`, `label`, `control_kind`, `plant`, and `slug` for an appended entry), with the shortcut, the case with its numbers, the rule protected, the plant (the control name of the last column: the build feature or fixture that puts the shortcut into the real component) and the catching test with its bead; the four late shortcuts as appended entries under the slugs L-a to L-d; a registry check and the chapter's control script. Out: the rows of `controls.toml` and their `NC-` identifiers, which only the seeding tool of N38b-05 writes (re-run over these entries, append-only and idempotent); the control runner and the rule that a gate with no control cannot pass (N38b-04); the numbering of L-a to L-d (the decision N20b-45 files the block request with the allocator of N38a-40, `owner = "N20-CX"`); the catching tests themselves (the beads named per row).

**Word-only entries.** CX-202, CX-212 and CX-215 are among the catalogue's seventeen word-only entries (`plan_case = "words"`). CX-212 takes `numeric = true` with `case_supplied_by = "N20b-05: weighted triangle"` (weights (1, 1, 4): (4β/9, 4β/9, −β/9) against the Euclidean (β/3, β/3, −β/3)); CX-215 takes `numeric = true` with `case_supplied_by = "CX-371"` (the numerics chapter's sequential-stream case); CX-202 stays `numeric = false` until the camera-timing bead BD-188 supplies a fixture, and the seeding writes it as an obligation. Identities across catalogues: CX-203 is the case of CX-052 and CX-214 the case of CX-049; both keep their own entries (one control per protected rule) and record the earlier entry in `same_as`.

**Kind, plant, label and gates per entry (DESIGN).** Every entry is `label = "DESIGN"` (the cases are arithmetic or
constructed fixtures; none quotes a measurement). `control_kind`: `planted-world` for CX-205 and CX-206 (a world
pair that separates the shortcut from the rule: the twin aisles; the T-junction pair), `mutation` for the other
eighteen (the shortcut built into the real component under a Cargo feature of `gauntlet-mutations`). `plant` is
`<first catching bead>:<control name of the last column>`, for example `BD-078:sched-every-13th`,
`N20a-37:tjunction-29m-budget`, `N20b-28:chain-order-swapped`: the name under which that bead builds the plant,
which it may refine by updating the entry, keeping the name's stem. `rule_ids` hold the identifiers of the rule
column, its gates among them; CX-207 and CX-210 name no gate, so theirs carry the defining sections (§20.12,
§20.13), which the seeding tool resolves through `registry/rule_gates.toml`, and an entry that resolves to no gate
is written to `registry/seed_findings.toml` with `gate-added` requested.

**Registry check.** `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0_laws -- sensorium_cx_registry`: each of the sixteen CX numbers and four appended slugs is present with a `plant`, a catching test path and an owner bead; after the seeding tool is re-run each has exactly one control in `controls.toml` whose `seed` names it (an appended entry by its number once N20b-45 maps it, by its slug before); every entry has a `control_kind` from the closed list, a `plant` naming its catching bead and the `label`; and, reading the control runner's history `registry/control_history.ndjson` (N38b-04), each control's latest recorded run is reported: `caught`, `missed` (a defect in the catching test, which fails this check), or `never run` with the catching bead and its milestone (reported, not failed, until that milestone).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `counterexamples.toml` holds twenty entries for this chapter (CX-200 to CX-215 and the four appended slugs) with shortcut, case, protected rule, `plant`, catching test and owner; this bead writes no row of `controls.toml`, and after the seeding tool of N38b-05 is re-run each entry has exactly one control, the tool's second run changes no byte, and the join passes.
- [ ] Every entry carries the `control_kind`, `plant` and `label` stated above; CX-207 and CX-210 resolve to a gate through `rule_gates.toml` or are listed in `seed_findings.toml`, never dropped.
- [ ] The registry check passes; it fails if an entry is removed, has no catching test, or its control's latest recorded run is `missed`, and it reports a control never run as pending with its catching bead.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/sensorium_controls.sh` exits 0.
- [ ] The four pending slots are reported to the catalogue owner for numbering (the decision N20b-45 files one block request of four with the allocator of N38a-40 and maps L-a to L-d to it in order); no existing number is reused.
- [ ] The two cross-catalogue identities are recorded: CX-203 ≡ CX-052 and CX-214 ≡ CX-049 (one fixture identity per physical case).
- [ ] The three word-only entries carry `plan_case = "words"`; CX-212 and CX-215 carry `numeric = true` and a non-empty `case_supplied_by`; CX-202 carries `numeric = false` and is reported as an obligation (not runnable, makes no gate eligible) until its fixture exists.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
(`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0_laws -- sensorium_cx`)
- `sixteen_numbered_entries_present` (CX-200 to CX-215).
- `four_pending_slots_present`.
- `each_entry_has_catching_test_and_owner`.
- `control_must_fail_its_test`: a synthetic entry whose history line is `missed` is rejected; one with no history line is listed `never run`.
- `kinds_plants_labels`: CX-205 and CX-206 are `planted-world`, the other eighteen `mutation`; every `plant` names its catching bead; every `label` is DESIGN.
- `cross_catalogue_identities_recorded`.
- Arithmetic spot checks of the numbers quoted (so a transcription error in the registry is caught): 400/13 rounds to 30.77; ⌈8,000/13⌉ = 616; 616/600 − 1 = 2.67%; 27/32, 459/512, 3,807/4,096 by exact enumeration of majorities with p = 3/4; ln(20)/4 = 0.7489 m; 9.5 + 9.5 + 10 = 29; 200 × 0.001/0.020 = 10; 0.4 × 0.5/0.2 = 1.0; 100 − 2 × 9.81 = 80.38 and −2 × (9.81 + 2) = −23.62.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sensorium_controls.sh` (helper of STD-E2E; it calls only cargo targets of its dependencies; NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/sensorium_controls/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript): runs the unit tests and the registry check; runs N38b-05's seeding tool, `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test seed_controls -- --ignored seed`, twice, asserting that the second run leaves the digest of the committed `controls.toml` unchanged; runs the join, `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test seed_join`; then reports, per entry, its control's state (`runnable`, `obligation` for CX-202, `stale` while the catching bead has not built the plant) and its latest recorded outcome from the control runner's history. Running the catching gate's target with and without the control's feature (the form of ADR-GAUNT-01) is the control runner's (N38b-04), invoked by the catching beads' own scripts and by the chapter battery (`scripts/e2e/sensorium_known_answers.sh`, N20b-20), not by this script. Each line carries, beside the helper's standard fields (command line, exit code, wall time), the CX number or slug, the control's `NC-` identifier, the plant, the gate, the state and the latest outcome; the digest of `controls.toml` before and after the second seeding; on failure the first missing entry or unresolved gate. The `summary` counts entries, controls caught, never run and missed out of twenty.
### Known answers and negative controls
The table is the content. Determinism: registry data; the arithmetic spot checks are exact or to four significant figures.

## Considerations
- Milestones differ by row (clock rows at M1; optics at M3; visibility at M3); the registry is written at M1 so that every gate can be registered with its failing control before its passing test, and entries are enabled as their mechanisms land.
- Controls are build features or fixtures in `frob-conformance` only; none may be compiled into a release binary.
- Owed by the catching beads, not by this registry: each cites its CX number (or slot) and states the values inline; the carriers of the four slots are N20b-28 (L-a, plant `N20b-28:chain-order-swapped`), N20b-32 (L-b, `N20b-32:visibility-scene-only`), N20b-40 (L-c, `N20b-40:unwrap-nearest-always`) and N20b-41 (L-d, `N20b-41:loadcell-contact-sum`). Each control is observed caught before its gate records a pass.
- Catching tests of the other Sensorium unit are named by their bead keys (BD-078, BD-188, N20a-06, N20a-07, N20a-16, N20a-22, N20a-24, N20a-37, N20a-39, N20a-40); a catching bead that is later split or merged keeps the CX number in its test plan, and the registry check fails on a catching test path that no longer exists.
