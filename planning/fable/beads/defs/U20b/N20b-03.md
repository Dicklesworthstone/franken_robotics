## Background
GATE-SENS-10 is the acceptance gate of the observation-preserving symmetry subgroup: "Γ_obs: the two cases of the chapter on every template; the equivariance test passes for members and fails for non-members" (TARGET; claim kind Known answer; first milestone M3). The two cases are (a) a quadruped-style design that is symmetric fore-aft and left-right carrying a forward depth camera on its sagittal plane, where Γ_obs has order two inside a mechanical group of order four because the left-right reflection survives when rows are read out top to bottom and the fore-aft reflection and the half-turn do not; (b) a spinning lidar on the plane of symmetry, where a reflection reverses the firing order of the beams and is therefore broken under per-beam instants and restored only when the scan is declared instantaneous.

The gate is measured on a running system through the real `frob` binary, so it has its own end-to-end bead. "Every template" means every archetype template the design language ships at M3 (eighteen): for a template whose mechanical group has no reflection of the right kind, the script records what the cases reduce to (for example, a template with only a sagittal mirror: the forward camera keeps the whole group, and the spinning lidar still breaks the mirror) and still requires the member and non-member tests to behave. The reason for running every template (DESIGN): the predicates touch parity rules, pattern permutations and clock comparisons that differ by body plan; a gate measured on the quadruped alone would not exercise wheeled bases, radial groups or designs with a trivial group.

## Scope
In: the shell script, its fixtures list, its assertions, its structured log, and the gate receipt for GATE-SENS-10. Out: the mechanisms (N20b-01, N20b-02, BD-241).

## Technical approach
**Script.** `scripts/e2e/sense_symmetry.sh [--templates all|<list>] [--tier referee|batch-cpu|cuda|metal] [--seed N]`. Plain shell driving the real `frob` binary; idempotent (its designs live in a scratch namespace it creates and deletes); seed-explicit (default seed 20,130); exits non-zero on any failed assertion.

**Steps, per template T.**
1. `frob design templates --json` and `frob design new sym-T --from T`.
2. Case (a): `frob design edit sym-T add-sensor --card depth-generic --parent <root> --mount <on the sagittal plane, facing +x> --readout rows-top-to-bottom`; `frob analyze symmetry sym-T --json` (records Γ_mech); `frob sense symmetry sym-T --task track-open-loop --scene-family flat-mirror --json`. Assert: Γ_obs equals the subgroup of Γ_mech whose elements fix the camera mount and preserve row instants (the expected element list is computed by an independent table in the fixture file, written by hand for the eighteen templates and committed before the predicates are run); every excluded element has a breaker in family `SensorMount`.
3. `frob sense symmetry sym-T … --test --json`: assert every generator of Γ_obs passes and every element of Γ_mech outside Γ_obs fails; record the first differing packet for each failure.
4. Case (b): replace the camera with `lidar-spin-generic` on the plane of symmetry at 10 Hz; repeat with `--scan-timing per-beam` (assert each reflection of Γ_mech is excluded with family `PatternTiming`, and fails `--test` when presented as a member through the control switch) and with `--scan-timing instantaneous` (assert the reflections that fix the mount are members, `--test` passes, and the envelope records `scan_timing: "instantaneous"`).
5. Refusal path: `frob sense symmetry sym-T … --license-check augmentation --element <an excluded element>`; assert `ok: false`, refusal code FROB-REF-443, exit code 3, breaker named, three ranked repairs.
6. Negative controls, each run with a conformance feature flag of the binary built for tests: `no-stream-permutation` (members must now fail), `no-timing-clause` (the lidar reflection is admitted and must fail `--test`). A control that does not fail the gate fails the script.
7. `frob design delete sym-T`.

**Gate record.** The script emits a receipt (hash-named, under `target/e2e-logs/sense_symmetry/`) that the recording session enters in `registries/gates.toml` for GATE-SENS-10: templates run, members passed, non-members failed, exceptions (must be zero), controls caught, tier, commit, hardware fingerprint, seed. The Gauntlet row `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- gate_sens_10` wraps the same script so the gate cannot be recorded without it.

**Logging (STD-E2E).** Newline-delimited JSON at `target/e2e-logs/sense_symmetry/<utc>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N20b-03`), `step`, `event`. `cmd` lines carry the exact command line, exit code and wall time and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). `assert` lines carry the template, the case, expected and observed group orders and element lists. `metric` lines carry packets compared, digests of both observation streams, and the per-channel maximum difference in units in the last place against its threshold of zero. `refusal` lines carry code, pointer, observed breaker and repairs. On failure: the first differing packet (sensor, sequence number, channel, both values) and the paths of both episode files. The final `summary` gives assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-SENS-10"]`. No scene geometry or private-scene data is written; the fixtures are generated flat scenes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The script exists, is executable, sources the STD-E2E helper, and runs to exit 0 on a correct build for all eighteen templates on the referee.
- [ ] For `quad-mammal`: Γ_mech order 4, Γ_obs order 2 in case (a); in case (b) no reflection under per-beam instants, the mount-fixing reflections under the instantaneous assumption.
- [ ] Members pass and non-members fail the equivariance test on every template; zero exceptions; counts recorded.
- [ ] The FROB-REF-443 path returns exit 3 with the breaker and repairs.
- [ ] Both negative controls are caught (the script exits non-zero when run against either control build, and its self-test mode asserts exactly that).
- [ ] The expected-group table for the eighteen templates is committed before the first run and its digest is in the receipt.
- [ ] GATE-SENS-10 is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with measured values, not targets.
- [ ] Re-running the script with the same seed produces identical `metric` digests (determinism of the log's measured content).

## Test plan
### Unit tests
The script's helpers are tested by `scripts/e2e/tests/sense_symmetry_selftest.sh`: `expected_table_parses` (eighteen rows, each with a group name and element list); `control_build_fails_gate` (runs step 3 against the `no-stream-permutation` build on `quad-mammal` and requires a non-zero exit); `log_lines_validate` (every emitted line validates against the STD-E2E log schema).
### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script: `scripts/e2e/sense_symmetry.sh`, as specified above.
### Known answers and negative controls
KA-493: Γ_obs of order two inside Γ_mech of order four (control: the fore-aft reflection fails the equivariance test). KA-494: the spinning lidar under reflection is broken with per-beam instants. CX-209 and CX-210 are the counterexamples the two cases come from. Planted failures: the two control builds. Determinism lanes: the group results across Linux and macOS (D0, exact); the test streams twice on one machine and, where the tier is batched, across worker counts.

## Considerations
- Templates whose own geometry already breaks symmetry (an off-centre arm on the mobile manipulator, trivial Γ_mech) make both cases vacuous; the script records them as `vacuous: trivial-group` and they count toward "every template" without counting as evidence. At least the templates with a non-trivial group must pass non-vacuously.
- Batched-tier runs wait on the templates' batched baselines; the M3 record is on the referee for all templates and on batched tiers where available, each row labelled with its tier.
- Runtime budget (TARGET): under ten minutes for the full sweep on the reference host.
