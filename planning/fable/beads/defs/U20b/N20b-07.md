## Background
GATE-SENS-11 (Known answer, TARGET thresholds, first milestone M3): "Calibration: the triangle and four-sensor residual patterns; the planted bump recovered within 0.1° and 1 mm and attributed to the right node in 100 of 100 seeds." It accepts the pose-graph calibration of sensor extrinsics: weighted least squares over pairwise relative-pose measurements with corrections in 𝔰𝔢(3), a consistency residual taken with the projector that is orthogonal in the weight metric, and a diagnosis that separates a bumped sensor (absorbed by the solve, residual at noise level) from a faulty edge (inconsistent round every cycle through it).

The gate is measured through the real `frob` binary on a simulated rig, so it has its own end-to-end bead. The script also runs the single-edge row of the late known-answer table (KA-849) from the user's side, because that row is the smallest case that distinguishes the correct tail adjoint from the planted frame mutant. It is a demonstration through the verb, its log lines tagged `ka: KA-849`; the row's part of GATE-GAUNT-11 is recorded in one place only, from the receipt of `adopted_known_answers.sh M3` (N38b-46), which runs N38b-42's conformance test, and not here. N38b-42 depends on this bead and cites these `ka: KA-849` metric lines as the verb's evidence.

Why the patterns are part of the gate and not only the bump (DESIGN): the bump test alone would pass with a diagnosis that calls everything a bump. The scalar patterns (β/3 on every edge of a triangle; β/2, β/4 and 0 on the six edges among four sensors) are what show the residual has the right structure, and the refusal on a tree is what shows the tool does not answer questions the graph cannot support.

## Scope
In: the shell script, its fixtures, assertions, structured log and the gate receipt. Out: the mechanisms (N20b-04, N20b-05, N20b-06, BD-242).

## Technical approach
**Script.** `scripts/e2e/sense_calibrate.sh [--seeds 100] [--seed-base 4100] [--tier referee]`. Plain shell, real binary, idempotent (scratch namespace), seed-explicit, non-zero exit on failure.

**Steps.**
Every `sense.calibrate` and `sim.run` call carries its own `--key` (both are `run` verbs; KNOWN_DRIFTS 5), and every `--edges` call names its design positionally (the bare-frame fixture designs `tri-rig` and `k4-rig`, committed with BD-242's gate fixture, and `single-edge-rig`, committed here with `single_edge.json`; step 4 runs `frob sense calibrate single-edge-rig --edges single_edge.json --key cal-one --json`).
1. *Scalar patterns.* `frob sense calibrate tri-rig --edges fixtures/…/triangle_beta.json --anchor n0 --key cal-tri --json` and the same for `k4-rig` with `k4_beta.json` (`--key cal-k4`). The edge files hold pure x-translation measurements (rotations frozen by very large angular weights, DESIGN: the files state this) with one edge biased by β = 0.012 m. Assert per-edge |r_⊥| along x: 0.004 m on all three edges of the triangle; 0.006 m on the bad edge, 0.003 m on the four adjacent edges and 0 on the opposite edge of the four-node graph (tolerance 10⁻⁹ m). Assert the support report: triangle `detectable` and not `identifiable`; four-node all `identifiable`.
2. *Localization on the patterns.* With `--localize`: the triangle is refused with FROB-REF-446 (exit 3, repair lists edges to add); the four-node graph returns `bad edge` with the biased edge.
3. *Anisotropic control.* The triangle with weights (1, 1, 4): assert r_⊥ = (4β/9, 4β/9, −β/9); run the `euclidean-projector` control build and assert it returns (β/3, β/3, −β/3) and that the script's check rejects it.
4. *Single edge.* `single_edge.json`: predicted pose of sensor 2 in sensor 1 a translation of (1, 0, 0) m; sensor 1 off by 0.01 rad about z; assert the edge residual at the uncorrected mounts has angular part (0, 0, −0.01) rad and linear part (0, −0.01, 0) m; run the `tail-adjoint` control build and assert its linear part is (0, +0.01, 0) m and is rejected.
5. *Bump, 100 seeds.* For seed s in the range: `frob sim run rig4 --task rig-sweep --seed s --fault extrinsic-bump:lidar:rz=2deg,x=15mm@t=3s --key cal-run-s --json`; the reference from the pre-bump part of the same episode, `frob sense calibrate rig4 --episode <ep> --window 0..3s --anchor imu --key cal-ref-s --json` (BD-242's `--window`); then `frob sense calibrate rig4 --episode <ep> --window 3s..6s --anchor imu --reference <the reference's calibration id> --localize --key cal-s --json`. Assert: attribution `bump at lidar`; recovered rotation within 0.1° and translation within 1 mm of the planted bump; consistency verdict `consistent`. Count successes; the gate needs 100 of 100.
6. *No bump.* Ten seeds without the fault: attribution `none`.
7. *Tree.* `frob sense calibrate tree-rig --episode … --localize --key cal-tree --json`: FROB-REF-446, exit 3. Without `--localize` the solve succeeds and every edge is `detectable: false`.
8. *No anchor.* A rig with sensor-to-sensor edges only and no `--anchor`: FROB-REF-446 "no anchor".
8a. *Reality claim.* `frob sense calibrate rig4 --episode <ep> --anchor imu --claim reality --key cal-real --json`: assert `ok: false`, FROB-REF-447, exit 3, the assumed fields listed (the synthetic edge noise and the shipped cards), both repairs; the same call without `--claim reality` succeeds with `reality: "no-claim"`. This is the user-side trigger of N20b-18's guard.
9. *Self-check.* `frob telemetry query <ep> --stream sensing --json`, filtered by the script to events of kind `calib.self_check` and to the lidar packets' flags: the bump episode contains a `bump at lidar` verdict and subsequent lidar packets carry `extrinsics-suspect`.

**Gate record.** A hash-named receipt with: the pattern values measured; the worst recovery error over the seeds in degrees and millimetres against 0.1° and 1 mm; the attribution count out of 100; controls caught (Euclidean projector, tail adjoint, tree localization refused); tier; commit; fingerprint; seed range. Entered in `registries/gates.toml` for GATE-SENS-11 by the recording session with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SENS-11 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19; this bead is the gate's one recorder, BD-242 producing receipts only); the single-edge values are logged as `metric` lines tagged `ka: KA-849` for N38b-42 to cite, not entered as a gate row. The Gauntlet's G1 row for the gate, run as `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-SENS-11` (the G1 target of N38a-02; the Gauntlet has no `frob` verb, ADR-GAUNT-01), wraps the script.

**Logging (STD-E2E).** `target/e2e-logs/sense_calibrate/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Each line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N20b-07`), `step`, `event`. `cmd`: exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed; `front_end: synthetic`). `metric`: per-edge residual norms with units and expected values; recovery errors in degrees and millimetres against thresholds; the consistency statistic with its degrees of freedom and quantile. `refusal`: code, pointer, repairs. `assert`: pass or fail with expected and observed. On failure: the seed, the first seed that failed, the planted and recovered twists, and the episode and calibration-record paths. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-SENS-11"]`; seeds attributed correctly.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The script runs to exit 0 on a correct build; every step's assertions are present in the log.
- [ ] Pattern values match to 10⁻⁹ m; the anisotropic case matches (4β/9, 4β/9, −β/9).
- [ ] 100 of 100 seeds: bump attributed to the lidar node, within 0.1° and 1 mm, residual at noise level.
- [ ] Tree localization and missing anchor are refused with FROB-REF-446 and exit 3; a Reality claim on the synthetic rig is refused with FROB-REF-447 and exit 3, and the same call without the claim succeeds with `reality: "no-claim"`.
- [ ] Each control build (`euclidean-projector`, `tail-adjoint`) makes the script exit non-zero, verified by the self-test.
- [ ] GATE-SENS-11 recorded with measured values; the single-edge demonstration logged with `ka: KA-849` (its GATE-GAUNT-11 row is recorded from N38b-46's receipt of N38b-42's test, not here).
- [ ] Two runs with the same seed base produce identical `metric` values.

Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts on the referee.

## Considerations
- The scalar edge files freeze rotation with large angular weights instead of using a separate scalar code path, so the patterns are measured on the same solver the bump uses.
- If the 100-seed run shows a failure, the seed is kept as a minimized regression fixture; the thresholds and the fixture noise are not adjusted to pass.
- Runtime (TARGET): under five minutes on the reference host; each seed is a few seconds of simulated time on the referee.
