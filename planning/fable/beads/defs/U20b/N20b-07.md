## Background
GATE-SENS-11 (Known answer, TARGET thresholds, first milestone M3): "Calibration: the triangle and four-sensor residual patterns; the planted bump recovered within 0.1° and 1 mm and attributed to the right node in 100 of 100 seeds." It accepts the pose-graph calibration of sensor extrinsics: weighted least squares over pairwise relative-pose measurements with corrections in 𝔰𝔢(3), a consistency residual taken with the projector that is orthogonal in the weight metric, and a diagnosis that separates a bumped sensor (absorbed by the solve, residual at noise level) from a faulty edge (inconsistent round every cycle through it).

The gate is measured through the real `frob` binary on a simulated rig, so it has its own end-to-end bead. The script also carries the single-edge row of the late known-answer table, because that row is the smallest case that distinguishes the correct tail adjoint from the planted frame mutant and it must be shown from the user's side once.

Why the patterns are part of the gate and not only the bump (DESIGN): the bump test alone would pass with a diagnosis that calls everything a bump. The scalar patterns (β/3 on every edge of a triangle; β/2, β/4 and 0 on the six edges among four sensors) are what show the residual has the right structure, and the refusal on a tree is what shows the tool does not answer questions the graph cannot support.

## Scope
In: the shell script, its fixtures, assertions, structured log and the gate receipt. Out: the mechanisms (N20b-04, N20b-05, N20b-06, BD-242).

## Technical approach
**Script.** `scripts/e2e/sense_calibrate.sh [--seeds 100] [--seed-base 4100] [--tier referee]`. Plain shell, real binary, idempotent (scratch namespace), seed-explicit, non-zero exit on failure.

**Steps.**
1. *Scalar patterns.* `frob sense calibrate --edges fixtures/…/triangle_beta.json --anchor n0 --json` and the same for `k4_beta.json`. The edge files hold pure x-translation measurements (rotations frozen by very large angular weights, DESIGN: the files state this) with one edge biased by β = 0.012 m. Assert per-edge |r_⊥| along x: 0.004 m on all three edges of the triangle; 0.006 m on the bad edge, 0.003 m on the four adjacent edges and 0 on the opposite edge of the four-node graph (tolerance 10⁻⁹ m). Assert the support report: triangle `detectable` and not `identifiable`; four-node all `identifiable`.
2. *Localization on the patterns.* With `--localize`: the triangle is refused with FROB-REF-446 (exit 3, repair lists edges to add); the four-node graph returns `bad edge` with the biased edge.
3. *Anisotropic control.* The triangle with weights (1, 1, 4): assert r_⊥ = (4β/9, 4β/9, −β/9); run the `euclidean-projector` control build and assert it returns (β/3, β/3, −β/3) and that the script's check rejects it.
4. *Single edge.* `single_edge.json`: predicted pose of sensor 2 in sensor 1 a translation of (1, 0, 0) m; sensor 1 off by 0.01 rad about z; assert the edge residual at the uncorrected mounts has angular part (0, 0, −0.01) rad and linear part (0, −0.01, 0) m; run the `tail-adjoint` control build and assert its linear part is (0, +0.01, 0) m and is rejected.
5. *Bump, 100 seeds.* For seed s in the range: `frob sim run rig4 --task rig-sweep --seed s --fault extrinsic-bump:lidar:rz=2deg,x=15mm@t=3s --json`; `frob sense calibrate rig4 --episode <ep> --anchor imu --reference <pre-bump calibration> --localize --json`. Assert: attribution `bump at lidar`; recovered rotation within 0.1° and translation within 1 mm of the planted bump; consistency verdict `consistent`. Count successes; the gate needs 100 of 100.
6. *No bump.* Ten seeds without the fault: attribution `none`.
7. *Tree.* `frob sense calibrate tree-rig --episode … --localize`: FROB-REF-446, exit 3. Without `--localize` the solve succeeds and every edge is `detectable: false`.
8. *No anchor.* A rig with sensor-to-sensor edges only and no `--anchor`: FROB-REF-446 "no anchor".
9. *Self-check.* `frob telemetry events <ep> --kind calib.self_check --json`: the bump episode contains a `bump at lidar` verdict and subsequent lidar packets carry `extrinsics-suspect`.

**Gate record.** A hash-named receipt with: the pattern values measured; the worst recovery error over the seeds in degrees and millimetres against 0.1° and 1 mm; the attribution count out of 100; controls caught (Euclidean projector, tail adjoint, tree localization refused); tier; commit; fingerprint; seed range. Entered in `registries/gates.toml` for GATE-SENS-11 (and the single-edge row for GATE-GAUNT-11) by the recording session. The Gauntlet row `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- gate_sens_11` wraps the script.

**Logging (STD-E2E).** `target/e2e-logs/sense_calibrate/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Each line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N20b-07`), `step`, `event`. `cmd`: exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed; `front_end: synthetic`). `metric`: per-edge residual norms with units and expected values; recovery errors in degrees and millimetres against thresholds; the consistency statistic with its degrees of freedom and quantile. `refusal`: code, pointer, repairs. `assert`: pass or fail with expected and observed. On failure: the seed, the first seed that failed, the planted and recovered twists, and the episode and calibration-record paths. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-SENS-11", "GATE-GAUNT-11"]`; seeds attributed correctly.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The script runs to exit 0 on a correct build; every step's assertions are present in the log.
- [ ] Pattern values match to 10⁻⁹ m; the anisotropic case matches (4β/9, 4β/9, −β/9).
- [ ] 100 of 100 seeds: bump attributed to the lidar node, within 0.1° and 1 mm, residual at noise level.
- [ ] Tree localization and missing anchor are refused with FROB-REF-446 and exit 3.
- [ ] Each control build (`euclidean-projector`, `tail-adjoint`) makes the script exit non-zero, verified by the self-test.
- [ ] GATE-SENS-11 recorded with measured values; the single-edge row recorded for GATE-GAUNT-11.
- [ ] Two runs with the same seed base produce identical `metric` values.

KA-496: bump of 2° and 15 mm recovered within 0.1° and 1 mm, attributed, 100 of 100 (control: a tree refuses localization). Late row: single edge, angular (0, 0, −0.01) rad and linear (0, −0.01, 0) m (control: Ad(T̂₁₂) on the tail gives (0, +0.01, 0) m). CX-211, CX-212. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts on the referee.

## Considerations
- The scalar edge files freeze rotation with large angular weights instead of using a separate scalar code path, so the patterns are measured on the same solver the bump uses.
- If the 100-seed run shows a failure, the seed is kept as a minimized regression fixture; the thresholds and the fixture noise are not adjusted to pass.
- Runtime (TARGET): under five minutes on the reference host; each seed is a few seconds of simulated time on the referee.
