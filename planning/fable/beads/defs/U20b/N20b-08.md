## Background
`sense.calibrate` has two first milestones: M3 in simulation and M5 on imported logs. At M3 the pairwise relative-pose measurements between sensors come from a synthetic front end inside the simulator. At M5 the Reality lane (Ground, the subsystem that calibrates the simulator against measurement) admits datasets recorded on a real rig, and "Ground runs the same solver on logs from a real rig". The solver, the consistency residual and the refusals do not change; what changes is where edges come from, what may be claimed about the result, and one alias that only real timing exposes.

That alias: a synchronized clock does not separate a sensor's exposure delay from a translation of its mount along the direction of travel. A range sensor with fixed orientation approaching a wall at coordinate L along a straight line at speed v, with exposure delay δ_t and forward mount offset b, reads L − v·(t + δ_t) − b: only v·δ_t + b appears. At +2 m/s a delay of 20 ms and an offset of 40 mm give the same 101 samples of a one-second pass at 100 Hz. The same alias binds the extrinsic graph when a faulty edge is a time offset on one pair: on data with one axial velocity, a timing fault on an edge and a mount translation on one of its nodes produce the same residuals. A tool that silently assigns the discrepancy to the mount writes a wrong extrinsic into a card.

Reality is an import lane: nothing here requires a robot to exist, the resting state is `no-claim`, and the mechanism is exercised first on the synthetic rehearsal (a dataset generated with planted parameters and labelled `synthetic`). Tier: Solid. Owners: `frob-sense` (solver and verb), `frob-ground` (admission, card updates).

## Scope
In: edge ingestion from an admitted dataset; provenance and Reality labelling of the result; the velocity-diversity check and the identified-combination report for the delay-offset alias; proposed card updates with calibration identity; the refusal path for Reality claims over `assumed` fields; tests on the synthetic rehearsal.
Out: dataset admission and splits (Ground's ingest); clock fitting; front ends that turn raw sensor data into relative poses (they run outside or upstream and deliver edges with covariances; this bead defines the edge record they must produce); calibration expiry and drift retirement (Ground).

## Technical approach
**Edge record from logs (schema `frob.sense.calib.edges/1`, the same schema as explicit edges at M3, with added fields).** Per edge: `tail`, `head` (sensor identifiers of the admitted rig description), `measured` (pose of head in tail), `covariance` (6 × 6, with provenance: `measured` from repeated captures, `datasheet`, or `assumed`), `interval` (the time span the estimate was computed over), `axial_velocities` (the set of body velocities along each sensor's axis during that span, as a summary: minimum, maximum, sign changes), `method` (`target`, `registration`, `motion`, `hand-eye`), `dataset_cell` (the Ground cell it came from).

**Flow.**
1. `frob sense calibrate <rig> --dataset <id> --cell <cell> --anchor base [--localize] --json`. The dataset must be admitted by Ground (a dataset with no declared cell is refused there); a read of a sealed validation or holdout split is refused by Ground's rules before this verb sees data.
2. Build the graph; solve; compute r_⊥, the support report and the diagnosis (unchanged code).
3. **Velocity-diversity check (DESIGN).** For every edge flagged by the bad-edge test, and for every node whose correction has a translation component along its sensor's axis, inspect `axial_velocities` over the contributing spans. If every span has the same axial velocity v (within the dataset's stated resolution), the time-offset and translation explanations are not separable: the verb reports the **identified combination** "delay times speed plus offset = <value> m" for that edge or node, marks the corresponding correction components `unidentified: delay-offset alias`, and proposes no update to them. It lists, in order of the separation they give, the excitations that would separate the two: the same pass reversed without rotating the robot (at −v the two explanations differ by 2·v·δ_t); a second speed; an independent exposure mark on a shared hardware line, which measures the delay directly.
4. **Card updates.** For components that are identified and whose covariance has provenance `measured`, the verb proposes a card update (a typed edit to the sensor's mount with provenance `measured`, the calibration identity, the dataset cell and the covariance). It applies nothing; Ground's identification verb owns the write and the expiry.
5. **Claims.** The envelope's `reality` field is `no-claim` unless Ground supplies a calibrated cell; a request for a Reality claim that rests on a sensor or optical card field of class `assumed`, or on an edge covariance that is `assumed`, is refused with FROB-REF-447 (repairs: record a datasheet or a measurement; drop the claim). Evidence class of the corrections: `Estimated`.

**Synthetic rehearsal fixture.** A generated dataset (label `synthetic`) of the four-sensor rig with planted mount errors, a planted 20 ms time offset on the camera–lidar pair, and passes at +2 m/s only in cell A and at +2 m/s and −2 m/s in cell B.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `sense.calibrate --dataset` runs the same solver and diagnosis as the simulated path (one code path; a test asserts identical outputs when the same edges are supplied both ways).
- [ ] On rehearsal cell A (one axial velocity) the planted time offset is reported as the identified combination with value v·δ_t = 0.040 m, the affected components are marked `unidentified: delay-offset alias`, no card update is proposed for them, and the three separating excitations are listed in order.
- [ ] On cell B (reversed pass added) the 20 ms offset is flagged as a faulty edge and the planted mount errors are recovered within their reported intervals; the predicted separation of the two explanations is 0.080 m.
- [ ] A card update is proposed only for identified components with `measured` covariance, and carries the calibration identity and dataset cell.
- [ ] A Reality claim requested with an `assumed` field is refused with FROB-REF-447, exit 3.
- [ ] A sealed split cannot be read through this verb.
- [ ] Negative control caught: a build that assigns the single-velocity discrepancy to the mount proposes a 40 mm card update on cell A and fails the test.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` updated: validity domain (identified components only), evidence class `Estimated`, the alias.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
(`cargo test -p frob-sense calib::logs`; the dataset is the generated rehearsal, labelled `synthetic`; no mocks)
- `same_edges_same_answer_sim_and_log_paths`.
- `single_velocity_reports_combination`: v = 2 m/s, δ_t = 0.020 s, b = 0: combination 0.040 m; no update.
- `reversed_pass_separates`: separation 2·v·δ_t = 0.080 m to 10⁻⁹.
- `second_speed_separates_by_twenty_mm`: at +1 m/s the explanations differ by 0.020 m.
- `assumed_covariance_blocks_reality_claim_447`.
- `card_update_carries_identity_and_cell`.
- `sealed_split_refused`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_calibrate_logs.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): `frob ground ingest` of the rehearsal dataset, `frob sense calibrate rig4 --dataset … --cell A`, then `--cell B`, then a Reality-claim request. Asserts the combination value, the absence and presence of proposed updates, the separation, and the refusal. Logs to `target/e2e-logs/sense_calibrate_logs/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: commands with exit codes and wall times; evidence blocks (class `Estimated`, `reality`, dataset and cell identities, `synthetic` label); `metric` lines for the combination, the separation and the recovered mounts with their intervals; `refusal` lines; a summary. No raw log samples are written to the e2e log (datasets may be private).
### Known answers and negative controls
The delay-offset alias values (v·δ_t + b = 40 mm on the +2 m/s pass; 80 mm separation on the reversed pass; 20 mm at +1 m/s), which are Ground's known answer applied to the extrinsic graph; KA-495 and KA-496 on logged edges. Control: assigning the single-velocity discrepancy to the mount. Determinism: identical envelopes for identical datasets on Linux and macOS.

## Considerations
- This bead is not ready until Ground's dataset admission exists; if no dataset is ever admitted (a named program risk), it is complete on the synthetic rehearsal and the capability is described as "exercised on synthetic data only".
- OPEN-SENS-4 (which sensor and optical cards ship with datasheet or measured fields; default all `assumed`, Reality `no-claim`) decides whether any Reality claim can pass here.
- Front-end quality dominates real calibration error; the verb reports each edge's method and covariance provenance so a weak front end is visible in the answer.
- Privacy: datasets recorded in real places can be private; the envelope carries identities and summaries, never raw frames.
