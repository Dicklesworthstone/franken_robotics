## Background
The docking fixture (N20b-13) has a hidden gain g ∈ {1, 2}, command bound U, read error ε on the one informative packet, and the exact boundary ε < (U + ½)/4 for a robust causal policy to exist. Two further facts about it are in the chapter, and each protects a rule that applies far beyond docking.

**A hypothesis set widened by a hair.** Admit a third gain, 33/32. At U = ¾ and ε = 1/64 the reading (65/64)·p is possible for g = 1 and for g = 33/32 under every first command p, so both worlds receive the same remaining commands; their final positions are c and 33c/32, and the smallest achievable worst error is 1/65 m, at c = 64/65. The chapter concludes that every cell admitted under the two-gain mission is wrong for the three-gain mission. **The rule this fixture enforces everywhere: a verdict is bound to the declared support of the hidden parameters, the delivery deadline, the error units and the target tolerance, and a change to any of them invalidates it** (CX-214: "a verdict survives a small widening of the hypothesis set" is the tempting shortcut).

**Arithmetic.** The fixture is evaluated in exact integer arithmetic (class D0) and, as a control, in single precision. FACT (an executed boundary-screen experiment on the Metal device of the development machine and on both RTX 4090 cards, listed in the source register and to be re-read, and reproduced at the exact-kernel gate, before any gate cites it): on the dense grid of 65,536 cases, one of the two grids of the kernel chapter's boundary corpus, unguarded single precision gave 452 false admissions and 23,438 false exclusions; a guard band with exact fallback gave no final errors; an exact integer kernel gave none and produced identical bytes on every device. The fixture is therefore also the first test of the exact-integer kernel class and of guarded single precision.

Tier: Solid. Owner: `frob-sense` with `frob-conformance`.

## Scope
In: the three-gain amendment evaluated exactly on the grid; the binding record of a verdict and the test that a change to any bound field invalidates it; the host-side single-precision and guarded evaluations as controls on the dense corpus; the statement of where single precision is and is not a control.
Out: the two-gain fixture (N20b-13); the device kernels and their byte identity (BD-030, BD-190); the revalidation verb; the finite-tolerance docking family over a continuous gain interval (a mission-chapter fixture).

## Technical approach
**Three gains, exactly.** With gains G = {1, 33/32, 2}, a robust policy needs the reading intervals of every pair of gains that require different second commands to be disjoint: |g₁ − g₂|·p > 2ε for each pair, and the command limits must hold for each gain: 1/g − U ≤ p ≤ (1/g + U)/2 and |1/g − 2p| ≤ U. The closest pair (1 and 33/32) gives p/32 > 2ε, that is ε < p/64; with the largest admissible p = (U + ½)/2 the three-gain boundary is ε < (U + ½)/128. On the grid (U = ½ + i/32, ε = j/64) this is 64·j < 32 + i, which holds only for j = 0.

**The worked point.** U = ¾, ε = 1/64: for every admissible p ≤ ¾ < 1, |65p/64 − p| = p/64 ≤ 1/64 and |65p/64 − 33p/32| = p/64 ≤ 1/64, so the reading 65p/64 is possible under g = 1 and under g = 33/32. A policy issues one continuation (u₁, u₂) for that reading; with v₃ = 0 imposed, the final positions are c = 2p + u₁ under g = 1 and 33c/32 under g = 33/32. Minimizing max(|c − 1|, |33c/32 − 1|) gives 1 − c = 33c/32 − 1, c = 64/65, worst error 1/65 m.

**Counts, and a discrepancy with the chapter's wording (recorded, not smoothed over).** Exact enumeration of the 561 cells: under the two-gain mission 344 are admitted; under the three-gain mission exactly 17 are admitted (the column ε = 0, j = 0, for every i, where an exact reading with p > 0 identifies the gain and the limits for g = 33/32 are satisfied by the same p interval); so **327 of the 344 admitted cells reverse, and the 17 cells with ε = 0 stay admitted**. The chapter's sentence "every cell admitted under the two-gain mission is wrong for the three-gain mission", and the gate wording "the third-gain amendment reverses every admitted cell", are exact for every cell with positive read error (all 327) and over-state the ε = 0 column. The test asserts the enumerated numbers (327 reversed, 17 unchanged, 0 newly admitted) and the unit's coverage file reports the wording to the lead so the gate text can say "every admitted cell with ε > 0".

**The binding record (DESIGN).** A docking verdict is stored with the fields it depends on, and its digest covers them:
```rust
// DESIGN. Fixes intent, not signatures.
pub struct VerdictBinding {
    support: Vec<Ratio>,            // declared support of the hidden gain, sorted: {1, 2} or {1, 33/32, 2}
    deadline: PacketDeadline,       // BeforeCommand(1) | AfterCommand(1) | AfterCommand(2), by the index k of u_k
                                    // (u₁ is the second command): on time; after the second; after the third
    error_units: Unit,              // the unit of ε (m/s, the unit of the v₁ reading)
    target_tolerance: Ratio,        // 0 for the exact mission; positive for a finite-tolerance mission
    command_bound: Ratio, read_error: Ratio,
}
```
Rule: a stored verdict may be reused only under an identical binding. Any change invalidates it: the verdict must be recomputed, or transported only by a polarity argument that is itself checked (widening the support can only reverse an admission, never an exclusion; narrowing it the opposite). The fixture provides the four single-field changes as test cases: support {1, 2} → {1, 33/32, 2} (327 admissions reverse); deadline before-second-command → after-third-command (all 344 reverse); error units (ε given in mm/s read as m/s: a cell moves by a factor of 1,000 in ε); target tolerance 0 → 1/65 m at (U = ¾, ε = 1/64) under three gains (the pair {1, 33/32} becomes satisfiable at exactly that tolerance and not below).

**Single precision as a control: where it bites.** On the 561-cell grid every quantity is a dyadic rational with at most seven fractional bits, so an `f32` evaluation of ε < (U + ½)/4 is exact there and cannot serve as a negative control. The control is the dense boundary corpus: 65,536 rows with inputs on a grid of 2⁻²⁸ concentrated near the boundary. On it:
1. the exact integer evaluator (this crate, host) labels every row; its byte vector is the oracle;
2. a bare `f32` evaluation of the residual's sign misclassifies rows (the recorded pilot counts, 452 false admissions and 23,438 false exclusions, are the pinned expectation once reproduced; until then the test asserts only that both counts are positive and records them);
3. a guarded evaluation (decide in `f32` when |r̂| exceeds the proved error bound; defer to exact arithmetic otherwise; the pilot's guard was 2⁻²¹ against a derived bound of 5·2⁻²⁵) has zero final errors, and its fallback fraction is recorded.
The host forms of 2 and 3 are written here so the control exists at M1 without a device; the device forms and the byte-identity claim are the kernel beads'.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Three-gain enumeration on the 561 grid: 17 admitted (all with j = 0), 327 of the 344 two-gain admissions reversed, none newly admitted; the worked point gives c = 64/65 and worst error 1/65 m exactly.
- [ ] The reading 65p/64 is verified possible under g = 1 and g = 33/32 for every admissible p on a rational lattice at U = ¾, ε = 1/64.
- [ ] Each of the four single-field binding changes produces a different binding digest and the stored verdict is refused for reuse; recomputation gives the stated outcome for each.
- [ ] Polarity: widening the support never turns an excluded cell into an admitted one (property test over random extra gains in (1, 2)).
- [ ] On the dense corpus the exact evaluator's labels are the oracle; the bare `f32` evaluation has false admissions and false exclusions (counts recorded; equal to 452 and 23,438 when the corpus is the registered one); the guarded evaluation has zero final errors.
- [ ] On the 561 grid the bare `f32` evaluation agrees with the exact one everywhere, and the test says why (documenting that the grid is not a precision control).
- [ ] Negative controls caught: a reuse check that ignores `support`; a guard band narrower than the derived error bound (must produce at least one final error on the corpus).
- [ ] The discrepancy with the chapter's "every admitted cell" wording is written in the test's doc comment and in the unit's coverage file.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/docking_amend_host.sh` exits 0, its stage 0 (N20b-13's script) included.

## Considerations
- The dense corpus is defined by the kernel-class probe; this bead consumes its registered definition and digest and must not carry a second copy of its constants.
- With a continuum of gains no exact guarantee survives positive read error; the three-gain case is the smallest instance of that fact, and the finite-tolerance family belongs to the mission chapter.
- The binding record is the docking instance of a general rule; other verdict types (requirement verdicts, certificates, alias witnesses) carry their own bindings, and the general mechanism is the assumption lattice of the proof subsystem.
