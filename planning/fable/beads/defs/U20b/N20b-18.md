## Background
Every sensor and optical parameter value in the studio is `assumed` until a card records a datasheet or a measurement. Sensor cards and optical cards are `.fcard` records with per-field provenance: `measured`, `datasheet` or `assumed`. The studio's results carry a claim product with seven kinds of claim; the Reality claim ("the simulation agrees with physical measurement") is `no-claim` everywhere except where imported real-robot measurements with their own provenance, predictions frozen before comparison and a reality band for a calibrated family support it. The weakest input wins by construction: a proved kernel fed an assumed coefficient produces an estimated result.

The refusal that enforces this for sensing is **FROB-REF-447: "A Reality claim rests on a sensor or optical card field of class `assumed`"**, with ranked repairs: record a datasheet or a measurement; drop the claim. Its open question is OPEN-SENS-4 (which sensor and optical cards ship with datasheet or measured fields; default: all `assumed`, Reality `no-claim`; owner: the project lead; it blocks any Reality claim that depends on sensing).

Why a refusal and not a label (DESIGN): a label on a result that says "sensor noise assumed" is read once and then copied without it. A Reality claim is the one statement that says a number describes the physical world; if the dropout rate of a depth camera on glass, or the latency of a link, was a guess, the statement is false however good the physics is. The guard makes the claim impossible to produce, and names the field to measure. Rejected: a global `--strict-data` switch alone (it exists for design checks on actuator cards; here the trigger is the claim, not a flag the caller may forget).

Tier: Solid. Owner: `frob-sense` for the dependency computation; the claim product type and the Reality lane are owned elsewhere.

## Scope
In: computing, for a result, the set of sensor and optical card fields it depends on; the check at the point where a Reality claim is requested or attached; the refusal with the offending fields; the count of `assumed` fields that every sensing result reports; tests.
Out: the cards, their provenance fields and the card-side predicate over a field set (BD-238, a dependency, which states the rule of FROB-REF-447 in the card contract; the optical-card bead of the scene chapter); deciding which cards ship with real data (the owner decision N20a-32); issuing reality bands (Ground). This bead is the one place where the refusal is raised.

## Technical approach
**Dependency set (DESIGN, `crates/frob-sense/src/provenance.rs`).** For a run, an analysis or a calibration, the sensing dependency set is the set of (card identity, field path) pairs whose values were read to produce any delivered observation or sensing analysis that the result used:
1. every field of every sensor card of a sensor whose packets were delivered to a consumer that contributed to the result (noise densities, bias walks, ranges, latency, exposure and readout, clock rate error, link parameters, chain stages, pulse-model fields, `pairwise_extrinsic_noise`, frame-symmetry declarations);
2. every field of every optical card of a region that any cast ray hit or passed (class, `r0`, transmittance, albedos, roughness, retro gain, emission), and the extinction coefficient and backscatter albedo of every optical volume a ray crossed. The ray kernels already record the region of each hit; the set is accumulated as a per-run bitset over regions, a statistic that never feeds state;
3. shared latents with their conditionals (a wetness latent that raises `r0`), each conditional with its own provenance.
The set is recorded in the run's evidence as a digest plus the count of fields by provenance class and a boolean `reality_claimable` (DESIGN: true exactly when no field of the set is `assumed`, so a caller can see before asking whether a Reality claim would be refused), and in full on request (`frob evidence trace`).

**The check.** A function `require_reality_claimable(deps) -> Result<(), Refusal>` is called wherever a Reality claim would be attached: when a verb is asked for a result with `claims` including Reality, and when Ground attaches a band to a result. If any field in the dependency set has provenance `assumed`, it refuses with FROB-REF-447. The refusal envelope carries `pointer` (the first offending card and field), `observed` (the list of assumed fields, capped with a count), `required` ("provenance datasheet or measured for every sensing field the claim rests on"), `affected_claims: ["Reality"]`, `retry: after-change`, and the ranked repairs: (1) record a datasheet or a measurement for the listed fields (the typed card update, with the experiment that would measure each field where the identifiability analysis can name one); (2) drop the claim (the same result is available with `reality: "no-claim"`). Exit code 3.

**What is not refused.** Results without a Reality claim are never refused by this guard; they carry `reality: "no-claim"` and the assumed-field count. A `datasheet` field is admitted with its provenance shown; whether a datasheet value is good enough is Ground's question (bands), not this guard's.

**Interaction with regions never seen.** A Reality claim about a result in which no ray touched a region does not depend on that region's optical card; the dependency set is what was read, not what exists. This keeps one unmeasured wall in an unused room from blocking a claim about a corridor.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Every sensing result's evidence block carries the dependency digest, the counts of fields by provenance and `reality_claimable`, which is false exactly when the guard would refuse.
- [ ] With all cards at their shipped default (all `assumed`), a request for a Reality claim on any sensing result is refused with FROB-REF-447, exit 3, naming the offending fields and both repairs.
- [ ] After every field in the dependency set is marked `datasheet` or `measured` in a test catalog (synthetic), the same request is admitted; reverting one optical field of a region that a ray hit restores the refusal and names that field.
- [ ] Changing the provenance of an optical card of a region no ray touched does not change the outcome.
- [ ] A shared latent's conditional with `assumed` provenance is in the set when the latent influenced a hit region.
- [ ] The region bitset is a statistic: enabling it changes no physics digest and no delivered observation (checked with the presentation-style noninterference comparison).
- [ ] Negative control caught: a guard that inspects only sensor cards admits a claim resting on an assumed optical card and fails the test.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` records the rule, the dependency definition and the refusal.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
(`cargo test -p frob-sense provenance::`)
- `default_cards_refuse_reality_447`.
- `all_measured_admits` (synthetic catalog labelled as such).
- `one_assumed_optical_field_on_hit_region_refuses_and_names_it`.
- `untouched_region_does_not_block`.
- `latent_conditional_in_dependency_set`.
- `link_latency_field_in_dependency_set`: a result that consumed packets from a link with an assumed latency is blocked.
- `no_claim_result_never_refused`.
- `reality_claimable_matches_guard`: over the fixture catalogs, `reality_claimable` is true exactly when `require_reality_claimable` returns `Ok`.
- `dependency_bitset_is_not_an_observable`.
- `control_sensor_only_guard_fails`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sense_reality_guard.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): `frob sim run` on a corridor fixture with a depth camera, once with the shipped default cards and once with the synthetic all-measured catalog, and once more with one optical field of a hit region reverted to `assumed`; for each run `frob evidence trace <the run's claim identifier> --json`: assert the dependency digest, the counts by provenance class and the full dependency set in the closure, `reality_claimable: false` for the default and reverted runs and `true` for the all-measured run, and that an unmeasured wall of an unused room does not appear in the set. The guard has no verb of its own; a Reality claim is requested through its callers, which depend on this bead: `frob sense calibrate … --claim reality` (BD-242, asserted by N20b-07's script) and `frob report run <run> --claim reality` (BD-302, requested). This script runs those two legs when `frob capabilities --json` shows the verb served (assert `ok: false`, FROB-REF-447, exit 3, fields listed for the default and reverted runs; admitted with `reality` still `no-claim` unless a band exists for the all-measured run) and otherwise logs `e2e_skip 'needs BD-242'` or `e2e_skip 'needs BD-302'`. Logs to `target/e2e-logs/sense_reality_guard/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: commands, exit codes, wall times, evidence blocks with the provenance counts, `refusal` lines with code, pointer, observed fields and repairs, and a summary. Card values are not logged, only field paths and provenance classes.
### Known answers and negative controls
No numbered known answer; the rule's fixtures are the three cases above. GATE-SENS-14's second clause ("every card field reports its provenance") is the input this guard needs; the guard's tests fail if any field lacks provenance. Control: the sensor-only guard. Determinism: the dependency set is a function of the run's hits; identical across repeated runs in class D2.

## Considerations
- OPEN-SENS-4 holds the default (all `assumed`, Reality `no-claim`); until the owner decides which cards ship with data, this guard refuses every sensing Reality claim, which is the intended fail-closed state.
- The guard is conservative about provenance, not about accuracy: a `measured` field with a wide interval passes here and widens Ground's band.
- A program risk is that calibration data never arrives; the guard then keeps Reality at `no-claim` and blocks nothing else.
- The per-region bitset costs one bit per region per world per run; negligible.
