## Background
The Sensorium chapter states nine invariants and nine proof obligations. An invariant is a property that must hold in every run; a proof obligation is a statement that must be established by a named kind of evidence (an induction on file, a symbolic identity, a derivation, a theorem card, an exhaustive check). The conversion rule for this plan is that each such identifier is carried, with its statement, in the test plan of the bead whose mechanism it constrains, as a property test, a certificate or a proof; and one that no mechanism bead carries gets its own test bead.

Two units share this chapter, and the identifiers are split between their mechanisms. This bead is the chapter-level index that guarantees none is dropped: a registry file listing all eighteen with their statements, the kind of evidence required, the test or artifact that supplies it and the planted defect that the test must catch; and a completeness check that fails when a listed test is missing, is not run, or cannot fail. It also directly owns the tests of the identifiers that belong to this unit's mechanisms and are not fully owned elsewhere. Reason (DESIGN): a list in prose drifts; a registry that the Gauntlet reads and refuses to pass with a hole does not. The guard against false progress applies: a property with no failing control validates nothing.

## Scope
In: `crates/frob-conformance/registry/sensorium_invariants.toml`; the completeness test; the property tests this unit owns (INV-SENS-01, -06, -07, -08, -09; PO-SENS-05, -06, -08), by reference to the beads that implement them, plus any gap found; a mutant per identifier.
Out: implementing mechanisms; the clock, optics, observability and alias tests of the other Sensorium unit (indexed here, written there).

**Statements of the two short proofs indexed for PO-SENS-07** (carried in full by their theorem cards; stated here so the index stands alone). Deterministic case: a causal policy maps delivered observations, its past actions and its own draws to an action; if the transcripts are identical in two worlds under every permitted behaviour, the action at the commitment time is the same in both, and it lies outside one of two disjoint success sets; private randomization does not help because the same draw gives the same action in both worlds. Stochastic case: with equal prior weight on two worlds and a fixed experiment with observation laws P and Q, any decision rule chooses correctly with probability at most (1 + TV(P, Q))/2.

**Completeness check.** `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0 -- sensorium_index_complete`:
1. every one of the eighteen identifiers has a row;
2. every row's `test` resolves to an existing test target or artifact (a derivation file under `docs/derivations/`, a theorem-card bundle);
3. every row's `control` resolves to a registered negative control in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml`, and the latest recorded run of that control *failed its test* (a control that passes is a defect in the test);
4. rows whose mechanism has not reached its milestone are marked `pending` with the milestone; a `pending` row past its milestone fails the check.
The check runs at every milestone exit; the exit review reads its output.

**Tests this unit must add if the mechanism beads leave a gap.** After the mechanism beads land, the index is diffed against their test lists. For this unit's identifiers the expected tests are: `nonint::presentation_*` (INV-SENS-01), `nonint::sensor_set_*` (INV-SENS-06), `causal::schema::actor_schema_refuses_*` and `actor_run_binds_only_schema_channels` (INV-SENS-07), `symmetry::guard::*` (INV-SENS-08), `nonint::batch_*` (INV-SENS-09), `symmetry::closure_exhaustive_templates` (PO-SENS-05), `fixtures::docking::grid_counts_344_217_9` (PO-SENS-06), `calib::consistency::projector_is_w_orthogonal_random` and `tree_residual_vanishes` (PO-SENS-08). Any missing one is written here.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The registry file lists all eighteen identifiers with statement, evidence kind, test, control, owner and milestone.
- [ ] The completeness check passes at M3 with no `pending` row; at M1 and M2 it passes with only rows whose milestone is later marked `pending`.
- [ ] Every control is recorded as having failed its test on the latest run.
- [ ] Deleting a row, pointing a row at a missing test, or marking a control as passing each makes the completeness check fail (three negative controls of the check itself).
- [ ] The two derivations (PO-SENS-05 closure card, PO-SENS-06 boundary) and the algebraic card for PO-SENS-08 exist under `docs/derivations/`.
- [ ] The index is printed by `cargo test … -- --nocapture sensorium_index_print` as a table for the exit review.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
(`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0 -- sensorium_index`)
- `sensorium_index_complete` (the check above).
- `index_rejects_missing_row`, `index_rejects_missing_test`, `index_rejects_passing_control`.
- `index_pending_rows_respect_milestones`.
- `statements_match_contract_md`: each invariant's statement in the registry equals the one in the owning crate's `CONTRACT.md` (text comparison after normalization), so the two cannot drift.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/sensorium_invariants.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): runs the eighteen tests and their controls through `cargo test` targets and the relevant `frob` verbs where a property is observable from the user's side (`frob sense causal-test`, the noninterference script, `frob sense symmetry --test`, `frob sense calibrate` on the anisotropic fixture). Logs to `target/e2e-logs/sensorium_invariants/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: one `assert` line per identifier with its statement, the test run, pass or fail; one `assert` line per control with "failed as required" or the defect; commands with exit codes and wall times; a summary with 18 identifiers, the number passing, the number pending, and the controls caught.
### Known answers and negative controls
Each row's control, as listed. The index's own controls: a missing row, a missing test, a passing control. Determinism: the registry is data; the check is deterministic.

## Considerations
- The index does not weaken ownership: a failing property is fixed in its mechanism bead. The index only makes absence visible.
- Statements are copied verbatim into the registry; if a later revision of an invariant changes its wording, the `CONTRACT.md` comparison forces both to change together.
- PO-SENS-03 depends on the symbolic sibling's admission; until its certificate is available the row's evidence is a numeric identity test labelled `Tested`, and the row says so instead of claiming `Certified`.
