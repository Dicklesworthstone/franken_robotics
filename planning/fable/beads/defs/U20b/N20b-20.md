## Background
The chapter closes with a table of known answers: 27 cases, each a truth that a correct Sensorium must reproduce and, for most, a negative control (a specific wrong build that must fail). The known-answer suite is tier G1 of the verification program; its registry (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml`, one derivation file per row) is the source of the numbered rows KA-465 to KA-524 for this chapter. Three late subsections add further answers (the force chain's rectangular and half-sine pulses; the box-and-mast visibility case; the rebound window, the two-speed encoder and the load-cell cut) that are stated with their gates and are not yet numbered.

Individual mechanism beads test their own rows. This bead is the chapter-level run: one script that executes every row through the real `frob` binary or the Gauntlet target that wraps it, checks the truth and the control, and writes one log from which an exit review can read the whole chapter's state. It exists for two reasons (DESIGN). The rows are owned by two drafting units and many beads; without one battery nobody can see whether a row fell between them. And a row and its control must be run together: a row reported as passing while its control also passes is a defective test, which only a run that executes both can show.

## Scope
In: the battery manifest, the script, its log and summary; the mapping from the chapter's 27 rows to numbered rows, gates and owning beads; slots for the late answers. Out: the tests themselves (owned by the beads named in the manifest) and the registry bead for the numbered rows.

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/sensorium_known_answers.sh [--milestone M1|M2|M3] [--tier …] [--seed 2000]`. For each manifest entry whose milestone is at or before the requested one: run the entry's `runner` (a `frob` command sequence or `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- <row>`), assert the truth at its tolerance, then run the same entry against its control build and assert that it fails. Entries whose control is "none stated" are run without a control and are counted separately; the summary reports how many rows have no control so the number is visible. Exit non-zero on any truth failure, any control that passes, or any entry with no runner.

**Logging (STD-E2E).** `target/e2e-logs/sensorium_known_answers/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Per entry: a `cmd` line (exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block); a `metric` line with the measured value, unit, truth and tolerance; an `assert` line for the truth and one for the control ("failed as required"); on failure the first differing value and the artifact path. The final `summary`: rows run, rows passed, controls caught, rows without a stated control, rows pending for a later milestone, and the gates touched (GATE-SENS-01 to -18 as applicable).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The manifest contains the 27 chapter cases, the additional numbered rows and the three late slots, each with a runner and an owner; an entry without a runner fails the script.
- [ ] At M3 every row at or before M3 passes at its tolerance and every stated control fails as required.
- [ ] The summary reports the count of rows with no stated control (8 of the 27 chapter cases leave the control blank: the turntable, the lidar pattern, lidar motion distortion, the mirror, the corridor Gramian, the gauge, sequential reads under a deadline, and the spinning lidar under reflection) so that the gap is visible and not silently treated as covered.
- [ ] Running with `--milestone M1` runs only M1 rows (the clock rows on the CPU, the docking rows, the causal rows on the referee, the IMU rows on the referee) and reports the rest as pending.
- [ ] The script's own control: with one truth value in the manifest changed (a self-test copy with 19.2 pixels replaced by 19.9), the script exits non-zero.
- [ ] The log validates against the STD-E2E schema.

All of them: the table is the content. Determinism lanes: rows of class D0 (clock, docking, symmetry groups, pose-graph patterns) are compared exactly and across platforms; float rows at their tolerances per tier.

## Considerations
- The battery duplicates no test logic; it calls runners owned by mechanism beads. If a mechanism bead renames a test, the manifest's runner breaks loudly.
- Rows owned by the other Sensorium unit are listed with "other unit" until its keys are known; the lead resolves owners when both units' beads are loaded.
- The late slots need numbered rows so that the registry and the printed appendix agree; until then they are run from the manifest and reported under their gate.
