## Background
Every sensor and optical parameter value is `assumed` until a card records a datasheet or a measurement. Sensor cards (noise densities, bias walks, ranges, latencies, exposure and readout, failure-mode parameters) and optical cards (class, reflectance at normal incidence, transmittance, albedos, roughness, retroreflective gain, emission, and the extinction of optical volumes) carry per-field provenance: `measured`, `datasheet` or `assumed`. A Reality claim that rests on an `assumed` field is refused (FROB-REF-447; repairs: record a datasheet or a measurement, or drop the claim), and results that depend on assumed fields stay `Estimated`.

**The open question (OPEN-SENS-4).** Which sensor and optical cards ship with datasheet or measured fields. Fail-closed default until decided: all `assumed`, Reality `no-claim`. Owner: the project lead. It blocks any Reality claim that depends on sensing. The program-wide register groups it with the questions for actuator parts and for film and soil tables: which real parts and tables enter the catalogues, and under what data licences; default generic classes and ranges labelled `assumed`; consequence: every result that depends on them stays `Estimated`, and realism and Reality claims are refused; due at M4.

Why this is the owner's decision (DESIGN): it is not an engineering trade-off inside the simulator. It commits the project to transcribing vendor datasheets (a licence and attribution question), to naming real products in a catalogue (a legal and maintenance question), and possibly to sourcing measurements (bench packages that cost money and time). The simulator is built so that the answer "none" costs a capability (Reality claims about sensing) and never the critical path. A named program risk is that calibration data never arrives; the resting state `no-claim` blocks nothing.

## Scope
In: the options and their consequences prepared for the owner; the criteria a card must meet to carry `datasheet` or `measured`; the record of the decision; what changes downstream on each branch. Out: writing the cards (the sensor-card and optical-card beads); the guard (N20b-18); admitting measured data (Ground).

## Technical approach
**Prepare for the owner (a short decision brief, committed under `docs/decisions/`).**
1. *Inventory.* From `sense.profile` over the reference designs and the scene families: every sensor-card and optical-card field, its current provenance (all `assumed` by default), and how many results in the M3 corpus depend on it (the dependency sets of the provenance guard). This ranks fields by how much evidence a real value would unlock.
2. *Candidates.* For each sensor kind used by a registered task (one IMU class, one encoder class, one depth camera, one lidar) and for the optical classes of the interior archetypes (painted wall, glass pane, mirror, polished floor wet and dry, steam): which public datasheets give which fields; which fields no datasheet gives (dropout on glass, multipath, grazing limits) and would need measurement.
3. *Licence review.* For each candidate source: whether transcribed values may be redistributed, the attribution required, and whether the product may be named. A source that fails is recorded as rejected with the reason.
4. *Options.* (a) Ship nothing real: all `assumed`; Reality `no-claim` for sensing (the default). (b) Ship `datasheet` fields for a named short list, with licences cleared; Reality claims still need Ground's bands. (c) As (b), plus commission or import measurements for the fields datasheets omit, through Ground's admission and bench packages.
**Criteria for provenance classes (binding whatever the option).** `datasheet`: the field's value, unit and conditions are transcribed from a cited document with version and page, registered in the source register with "to be re-read from the primary source before a gate cites it"; an interpolated or inferred value is `assumed`, not `datasheet`. `measured`: the value comes from a dataset admitted by Ground, with its cell, interval and calibration identity. A card may mix classes per field.
**Record.** An architecture decision record signed by the owner: the option chosen, the short list, the licence findings, and the date after which the default stands if no decision is made (the M4 exit). Downstream: on (a) nothing changes; on (b) the named cards are updated field by field and the provenance guard admits claims that rest only on those fields plus Ground's bands; on (c) the bench packages are scheduled as optional imports.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The decision brief exists with the inventory, the candidates, the licence review and the three options, and is reviewed by the owner.
- [ ] The decision record exists, or the M4 exit records that the default stands (all `assumed`, Reality `no-claim`), which is a valid outcome.
- [ ] The criteria for `datasheet` and `measured` are written in the card contract; a card field marked `datasheet` without a registered source is rejected by the card loader (a test).
- [ ] If option (b) or (c) is chosen, each updated field cites its source; the provenance guard's dependency report shows the count of `assumed` fields falling accordingly.
- [ ] No Reality claim that depends on sensing is issued before this record and Ground's bands exist; an attempt is refused with FROB-REF-447.
- [ ] Negative control: a card whose `datasheet` field cites a document that does not contain the value (a planted mismatch in a fixture) is caught by the source re-read check.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-sense card::provenance`: `datasheet_field_requires_registered_source`; `measured_field_requires_dataset_identity`; `inferred_value_is_assumed`; `default_catalog_all_assumed` (the shipped catalog has zero non-assumed sensing fields until the record says otherwise).
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/open_sens_4_inventory.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): runs `frob sense profile` over the reference designs and `frob scene show` over the scene families, aggregates fields by provenance, and requests one Reality claim to confirm the refusal. Logs to `target/e2e-logs/open_sens_4_inventory/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: commands, exit codes, `metric` lines for the counts of fields by provenance class per card kind, the `refusal` line for FROB-REF-447, and a summary. Only field paths and classes are logged, never licensed values.
### Known answers and negative controls
No numbered known answer. Controls: a `datasheet` field with no source; a planted source mismatch. Determinism: registry data.

## Considerations
- Fail-closed default: all `assumed`, Reality `no-claim`. The decision can only widen what may be claimed.
- Datasheet values describe a part under the vendor's conditions; they move a field from `assumed` to `datasheet` and do not by themselves support a Reality claim.
- The same decision format serves the actuator and contact-table questions; preparing them together saves the owner a second licence review.
- Privacy and licence: measured datasets may be private; cards derived from them carry the dataset's privacy mark.
