## Background
GATE-SENS-15 (Known answer; M2): "The rectangular rows are exact; the swapped-order mutant is rejected; on the referee an impulse with grade `none` yields `overload: unknown` and an `undetermined` peak-load atom in 100 of 100 cases; on a compliant tier whose step resolves the pulse with at least 10 samples the clipped reading is within 2% of the closed form."

The gate accepts the rule that force, torque, current and pressure readouts that saturate act on a force history through an ordered chain declared on the sensor card (a transfer function, an overload law, exposure integration, quantization), and that a tier whose contacts are impulses says so instead of inventing a peak. The rectangular rows: a 1 N·s pulse lasting 1 ms (1,000 N) or 10 ms (100 N) in a 20 ms exposure, with a clip at 200 N before integration, reads 10 N and 50 N; clipping after averaging reads 50 N for both, which is the mutation that must fail. The half-sine rows: J = 1 N·s with τ_c = 10 ms has a peak of 157 N, no clipping and a reading of 50 N; with τ_c = 1 ms a peak of 1,571 N and a clipped reading of 9.59 N.

The gate is defined in a subsection added after the gate register's tables were compiled; it is binding and is listed in the register's index of late gates with milestone M2. It is measured on a running system through the real `frob` binary, so it has its own end-to-end bead.

## Scope
In: the script, fixtures, assertions, structured log, gate receipt. Out: the chain, the grades, the kernel.

## Technical approach
**Script.** `scripts/e2e/sense_force_chain.sh [--tiers referee,batch-cpu,gpu-cuda,gpu-metal] [--seed 2300] [--cases 100]` (the tier names of `sim.run`'s `--tier` grammar, passed unchanged; `cuda` alone is FROB-REF-802). Plain shell, real binary, idempotent, seed-explicit, non-zero exit on failure. Every `sim.run` call carries its own `--key` (a `run` verb; compared runs are distinct jobs, KNOWN_DRIFTS 5): the key names the fixture, the build, the tier and the seed.

**Fixtures** (`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sensorium/force_chain/`): `prescribed-force-{1ms,10ms}-{clip-int,int-clip}` (four committed designs: a body held by a prescribed external force profile so that the sensor's input is exactly a rectangular pulse of 1 N·s lasting 1 ms or 10 ms, no contact model involved, carrying the card `ft-clip-then-integrate` (chain: clip 200 N, integrate, exposure 20 ms) or `ft-integrate-then-clip`; the variants are separate fixtures because `sim.run` takes a design, not card or pulse flags); `drop-compliant` (a compliant interface tuned to a near half-sine pulse, with four physics steps giving 5, 10, 20 and 40 samples across the pulse); and, committed by N20b-29 in the same directory and reused here, `drop-impulse` (a body dropped on the referee's event step, interface card without a pulse model), `drop-impulse-pulse` (the same with a half-sine pulse model of fixed τ_c = 10 ms and 1 ms) and the task `peak-load-limit.ftask` with one atom "peak sensor load ≤ 500 N". Every `sim.run` call whose packets the script reads carries `--telemetry T2` (sensor packets are recorded at T2; a query of a T1 run returns a `level-not-enabled` gap).

**Steps.**
1. *Rectangular rows.* `frob sim run prescribed-force-1ms-clip-int --seconds 0.04 --tier referee --telemetry T2 --key fc-1ms --json` and the same for `prescribed-force-10ms-clip-int` with `--key fc-10ms`; read the packet with `frob telemetry query <run> --stream sensing --channels ft --json`. Assert readings exactly 10 N and 50 N.
2. *Swapped-order mutant.* The same two runs (keys `fc-1ms-swapped`, `fc-10ms-swapped`) on the control build with the plant `N20b-28:chain-order-swapped` (N20-CX's late slot L-a; the `frob` binary built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-sense/chain-order-swapped`, the plant's own feature never being in the umbrella, BD-025; the build ignores the card's order and integrates first): assert it reads 50 N twice and that the gate check rejects it. Separately, with the legitimate card (`prescribed-force-{1ms,10ms}-int-clip`) on the correct build, assert 50 N twice: the order is the card's, and a different card is a different sensor (this distinguishes "the card says so" from "the build ignores the card").
3. *Profile.* `frob sense profile <design> --task peak-load-limit --json`: assert the chain is listed in declared order; grade `none` for the referee with `drop-impulse`, `modeled` with `drop-impulse-pulse` (provenance shown), `resolved` for compliant tiers; `undecidable_atoms` contains the peak-load atom where the grade is `none`.
4. *Grade none, 100 cases.* For 100 seeds (drop heights and masses drawn by address): `frob sim run drop-impulse --tier referee --seed s --telemetry T2 --key fc-drop-s --json`; assert the packet's `overload` is `unknown` (read with `frob telemetry query <run> --stream sensing --channels ft --json`), the linear reading equals the impulse divided by the exposure length within 10⁻⁹ relative, and `frob task verdict <run> --task peak-load-limit --json` (the run positional, as the `task.verdict` row of VERBS-task has it) returns `undetermined` with reason `force-history-grade: none`. Count 100 of 100. Then assert the peak-load claim on one of those runs, `frob task verdict <run> --task peak-load-limit --assert --json` (N20b-29's flag on the `task.verdict` row): `e2e_expect_refusal <the code N20b-45 applies to sens-peak-load-ungraded> 3` with the three repairs in order. This step is the user-side trigger of that refusal for N20b-45.
5. *Modeled pulse.* `drop-impulse-pulse` with J normalized to 1 N·s: assert peak 157 N and reading 50 N at τ_c = 10 ms; peak 1,571 N and reading 9.59 N at τ_c = 1 ms (tolerance 0.5%).
6. *Compliant tier, 2%.* For each batched tier and each step size of `drop-compliant`: read the unclipped force history (`frob telemetry query <run> --stream contact --json`, level T2), compute J, τ_c and the closed-form clipped reading in the script's helper, compare with the sensor's reading. Assert within 2% for 10, 20 and 40 samples; for 5 samples assert the `under-resolved` flag and log the difference.
7. *Checkpoint.* No user verb writes or restores a checkpoint at a chosen instant, so the restore runs through N20b-28's unit target (`cargo test -p frob-sense chain::chain_state_in_checkpoint`, which restores inside an open exposure of `prescribed-force-1ms-clip-int` and writes the next reading's digest to `target/conformance/chain/chain_state_in_checkpoint.json`); assert that digest equals the digest of the same reading in the uninterrupted `fc-1ms` run of step 1.

8. *Conformance rows* (the Gauntlet has no `frob` verb, ADR-GAUNT-01): `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-SENS-15` (one run covers every site of the gate's entries; an unavailable device site is `Skipped` with its reason); assert on the hash-named receipt under `target/gauntlet/receipts/` that every run entry is `pass` and the `chain-order-swapped` control is `caught`, and log its hash.

**Gate record.** The script writes no record. The recording session enters GATE-SENS-15 in `registries/gates.toml` from the step-8 receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SENS-15 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19; this bead is the gate's one recorder, N20b-28, N20b-29 and N20b-30 producing receipts only), citing this script's log for the user-side values: the two rectangular readings; the mutant's readings and the rejection; the 100-of-100 count; the modeled-pulse values; per tier and sample count the relative difference against 2%; commit, fingerprint, seeds.

**Logging (STD-E2E).** `target/e2e-logs/sense_force_chain/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Lines carry `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N20b-31`), `step`, `event`; `cmd` with exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` with value, unit, truth and threshold (`reading_N`, `peak_N`, `rel_diff` against 0.02, `samples_across_pulse`, `undetermined_count` against 100); `assert`; `refusal` for the peak-load claim; on failure the first failing seed or step size with the force-history artifact path; `summary` with assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-SENS-15"]`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The script runs to exit 0 on a correct build; steps 1 to 5 and 7 on the referee, step 6 on each available batched tier.
- [ ] Rectangular rows exact (10 N, 50 N); the control build is rejected; the legitimately reordered card reads 50 N twice.
- [ ] 100 of 100 grade-`none` cases yield `overload: unknown` and an `undetermined` atom; the asserted claim (`task verdict … --assert`) is refused with the slot's code, exit 3 and the three repairs.
- [ ] Modeled half-sine rows at 157 N, 50 N, 1,571 N, 9.59 N within tolerance.
- [ ] Compliant rows within 2% at 10 or more samples; `under-resolved` at 5.
- [ ] GATE-SENS-15 recorded from the step-8 receipt with measured values; a tier not available is `Skipped` with its reason in that receipt and logged by the script with the reason.
- [ ] The self-test confirms that the script fails against the control build and against a build that reports `overload: no` under grade `none`.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`scripts/e2e/tests/sense_force_chain_selftest.sh`: `closed_form_helper_values` (the helper returns 9.594 N for J = 1, τ_c = 0.001, L = 200, T = 0.020 and 50 N for τ_c = 0.010); `control_builds_fail_script`; `log_lines_validate`; `hundred_cases_are_seed_explicit`.
### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script `scripts/e2e/sense_force_chain.sh`, as above.
### Known answers and negative controls
The rectangular rows (10 N and 50 N; mutant 50 N twice); the half-sine rows (157 N and 50 N; 1,571 N and 9.59 N); the gate's `none` row and 2% row. Planted failures: `chain-order-swapped`; `overload-no-under-none`; a default pulse substituted when the card has none. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts on batched tiers; debug and release on the referee.

## Considerations
- The prescribed-force fixture isolates the chain from contact modelling, which is why the rectangular rows can be exact.
- The compliant row uses J and τ_c measured from the same run so that the 2% tests the chain and the step resolution, not the contact law's fidelity.
- Runtime (TARGET): under five minutes including 100 referee drops.
