## Background
GATE-SENS-16 (Known answer; TARGET; first milestone M3): "The box-and-mast case: hidden floor boundary at 1.000 m and 0.600 m within 1 cm; no hidden region beyond the body's front edge with the payload removed; a planted occlusion by a swinging limb is found and attributed to the link; lead and blind interval identical on every tier inside their determinism class."

The gate accepts the visibility query: along a nominal motion, every ranging sensor's pattern is cast against the scene, the robot's own collision primitives at the pose of that instant and the declared payload; a target is visible when at least the card's minimum number of valid returns land on it; and each target gets a lead (touch instant minus first-visible instant) and a blind interval (touch instant minus last-visible instant), with the occluder that caused each gap. The hand case: a depth camera at the body's front edge at height H looking down and forward, and a carried box whose top front corner is 0.4 m ahead of the camera and 0.3 m above the floor; the ray grazing that corner meets the floor at x = 0.4·H/(H − 0.3): 1.0 m at H = 0.5 m and 0.6 m at H = 0.9 m; with the next foothold 0.2 m ahead of the camera at 1 m/s the blind intervals are 0.8 s and 0.4 s.

The gate is defined in a late subsection and listed in the register's index of late gates (M3). It is measured through the real `frob` binary on every tier.

## Scope
In: the script, fixtures, assertions, structured log, gate receipt. Out: the query and the verb.

## Technical approach
**Script.** `scripts/e2e/sense_visibility.sh [--tiers referee,batch-cpu,gpu-cuda,gpu-metal] [--seed 2400]` (the tier names of `sim.run`'s `--tier` grammar, passed unchanged to `sense.visibility`; `cuda` alone is FROB-REF-802). Plain shell, real binary, idempotent (scratch designs), seed-explicit, non-zero exit on failure.

**Fixtures** (`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sensorium/visibility/`): scene `flat-footholds` (a flat floor with marked foothold targets every 0.1 m along x, and every 1 cm between 0.5 m and 1.1 m ahead of the start, where the two boundaries lie); design `box-bot-h05` (a wheeled body of 20 kg in total with its centre of mass 0.30 m above the floor and a half-track of 0.2 m, with a 100 Hz depth camera at its front edge at H = 0.5 m, looking down and forward with a field of view covering the floor from 0.1 m to 3 m ahead) carrying payload `box` (top front corner 0.4 m ahead of the camera, 0.3 m above the floor); `box-bot-h09` (the same with the camera on a mast at H = 0.9 m, the 1 kg mast head included in the 20 kg, so the centre of mass is at 0.32 m); `quad-swing` (the reference quadruped with a torso camera placed so that a front shank crosses its field of view once per stride); `glass-front` (`box-bot-h05` without the box, facing a foothold target behind a glass pane, for the masked-returns control); `box-bot-near` (as `box-bot-h05` with a near-field camera whose depression angles run from atan(0.5/0.9) to atan(0.5/0.1), so that at H = 0.5 m it sees the floor from 0.1 m to 0.9 m ahead); committed nominal motion records (N20b-33's `--motion` form, each naming its scene): `straight-1mps` (scene `flat-footholds`, straight route, drive at 1 m/s) and `trot-0p8` (scene `flat-footholds`, straight route, trot at a stride of 0.8 s); task `carry-box` declaring a latency chain plus planning horizon of 0.5 s.

**Steps.**
1. *Box, H = 0.5 m.* `frob sense visibility box-bot-h05 --payload box --motion straight-1mps --tier <tier> --key vis-h05-<tier> --json`. From the per-target timelines compute the nearest floor target ever visible at an instant (the hidden boundary): assert 1.000 m within 1 cm. Assert the foothold 0.2 m ahead has a blind interval of 0.8 s (within one camera sample period) and that every gap is attributed to `payload`.
2. *Mast, H = 0.9 m.* Same with `box-bot-h09` (key `vis-h09-<tier>`): assert 0.600 m within 1 cm and 0.4 s.
3. *Payload removed.* `--payload none` on both designs (keys `vis-h05-bare-<tier>`, `vis-h09-bare-<tier>`): assert no target beyond the body's front edge is hidden at any instant and there are no findings.
4. *Swinging limb.* `frob sense visibility quad-swing --payload none --motion trot-0p8 --tier <tier> --key vis-swing-<tier> --json`: assert gaps recur with the stride period (0.8 s within one sample period) and are attributed to the named shank link; assert `per_sensor` reports a non-zero self-occlusion fraction.
5. *Tier identity.* Repeat steps 1, 2 and 4 on each available tier; assert lead and blind interval (integers in quanta) are equal across tiers; where a tier's determinism class permits float differences in ray results, a differing integer must be traced to a named ray and is reported as a failure of "inside their determinism class" unless the class is D3 and the difference is one sample period at a grazing return, which is logged with the ray.
6. *Stage 7.* With the box, floor nearer than 1.0 m is hidden from `box-bot-near`'s camera, whose view ends 0.9 m ahead, so every foothold is `never-seen` and has no lead. `frob design check box-bot-near --task carry-box --scene flat-footholds --stages 7 --json` (the task's latency chain plus horizon is 0.5 s): assert the lead refusal with the code N20b-45 assigned (its slot alias `sens-lead-too-short` resolving to the same row), exit 3, the cause `payload`, and ranked repairs as typed edits (raise or tilt the mount, move the payload, shorten the latency). Apply the first repair, the `move-sensor` raising the camera's mount by 0.4 m, by running the `frob design edit box-bot-near move-sensor …` command exactly as the check's `next_actions` returns it, with `--expected-parent <the design id the check reported> --key vis-fix-<seed> --json` (a `mutate` verb whose target and value are positional; the row has no per-field flag such as `--dz`; H = 0.9 m with the depression angles unchanged, so the view now reaches 0.9 × 0.9/0.5 = 1.62 m ahead; the hidden boundary moves to 0.6 m; footholds are visible from 1.62 m to 0.6 m ahead; lead (1.62 − 0.2)/1 = 1.42 s, which exceeds 0.5 s) and assert the check passes, with the blind interval of 0.4 s reported as a finding or as within the task's bound.
7. *Price cross-check.* `frob analyze stability box-bot-h05 --json` and the same for `box-bot-h09`: assert tip-over angles atan(0.2/0.30) = 33.7° and atan(0.2/0.32) = 32.0° within 0.1° (the mast's price is visible in the other analysis).
8. *Controls.* Control builds, each the `frob` binary built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-sense/<feature>` for one of N20b-32's plant features (a plant's own feature is never in the umbrella, BD-025): `visibility-scene-only` (the plant `N20b-32:visibility-scene-only` of N20-CX's late slot L-b; no self or payload occluders): step 1 must report no hidden region and so fail the gate check; `visibility-no-self` (payload but not the robot's links): step 4 must fail; `visibility-counts-masked`: the glass-fronted target fixture `glass-front` must be reported visible and fail.
9. *Conformance rows* (the Gauntlet has no `frob` verb, ADR-GAUNT-01): `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-SENS-16` (the G1 target of N38a-02; one run covers every execution site, an unavailable site `Skipped` with its reason); assert on the hash-named receipt under `target/gauntlet/receipts/` that every run entry is `pass` and the L-b control `caught`, and log its hash.

**Gate record.** The script writes no record. The recording session enters GATE-SENS-16 in `registries/gates.toml` from the step-9 receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SENS-16 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19; this bead is the gate's one recorder, N20b-32 and N20b-33 producing receipts only), citing this script's log for the user-side values: hidden boundaries (m) against 1.000 and 0.600 with tolerance 0.01; blind intervals (s); payload-removed result; swinging-limb period and attribution; per-tier integer equality; controls caught; commit, fingerprint, seed.

**Logging (STD-E2E).** `target/e2e-logs/sense_visibility/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N20b-34`), `step`, `event`. `cmd`: exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). `metric`: `hidden_boundary_m` with truth and tolerance, `blind_interval_s`, `lead_s`, `gap_period_s`, `self_occlusion_fraction`, tip-over angles. `assert`: expected and observed, including the attributed occluder. `refusal`: the stage-7 line with repairs. Digests of the visibility timelines per tier where compared; on failure the first differing target and instant and the run paths. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-SENS-16"]`; tiers run.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The script runs to exit 0 on a correct build on every available tier.
- [ ] Hidden boundaries 1.000 m and 0.600 m within 1 cm; blind intervals 0.8 s and 0.4 s within one sample period; attribution `payload`.
- [ ] No hidden region beyond the front edge with the payload removed.
- [ ] The swinging limb is found with the stride period and attributed to the named link.
- [ ] Lead and blind interval identical across tiers inside their class.
- [ ] The stage-7 refusal (the code N20b-45 assigned, exit 3) and the repair path work end to end; after the mast raise the lead is 1.42 s against the required 0.5 s.
- [ ] Each control build fails the gate check; verified by the self-test.
- [ ] GATE-SENS-16 recorded from the step-9 receipt with measured values; an unavailable tier is `Skipped` with its reason in that receipt and logged by the script.

`scripts/e2e/tests/sense_visibility_selftest.sh`: `hand_formula_helper` (0.4·0.5/0.2 = 1.0 and 0.4·0.9/0.6 = 0.6); `mast_raise_footprint_helper` (fixed depression angles: the near camera's far edge 0.9 × 0.9/0.5 = 1.62 m; lead (1.62 − 0.2)/1 = 1.42 s); `motion_records_name_scene` (each committed motion record names `flat-footholds`); `fixtures_place_box_corner_correctly` (the design files put the corner at 0.4 m ahead and 0.3 m up); `control_builds_fail`; `log_lines_validate`.
### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script `scripts/e2e/sense_visibility.sh`, as above.
### Known answers and negative controls
The box-and-mast case (1.000 m, 0.600 m; 0.8 s, 0.4 s; 33.7° to 32.0°); payload removed; the planted swinging limb. Controls: scene-only occluders; no self occluders; masked returns counted. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across tiers; across worker counts on batched tiers.

## Considerations
- The 1 cm tolerance requires foothold targets spaced finely near the boundary; the fixture spaces them at 1 cm between 0.5 m and 1.1 m.
- The camera's sample period bounds the resolution of intervals (33 ms at 30 Hz); the fixture uses 100 Hz so that 0.8 s and 0.4 s are resolved to 10 ms.
- Runtime (TARGET): under two minutes per tier; the query is kinematic.
