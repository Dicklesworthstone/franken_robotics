## Background
GATE-SENS-16 (Known answer; TARGET; first milestone M3): "The box-and-mast case: hidden floor boundary at 1.000 m and 0.600 m within 1 cm; no hidden region beyond the body's front edge with the payload removed; a planted occlusion by a swinging limb is found and attributed to the link; lead and blind interval identical on every tier inside their determinism class."

The gate accepts the visibility query: along a nominal motion, every ranging sensor's pattern is cast against the scene, the robot's own collision primitives at the pose of that instant and the declared payload; a target is visible when at least the card's minimum number of valid returns land on it; and each target gets a lead (touch instant minus first-visible instant) and a blind interval (touch instant minus last-visible instant), with the occluder that caused each gap. The hand case: a depth camera at the body's front edge at height H looking down and forward, and a carried box whose top front corner is 0.4 m ahead of the camera and 0.3 m above the floor; the ray grazing that corner meets the floor at x = 0.4·H/(H − 0.3): 1.0 m at H = 0.5 m and 0.6 m at H = 0.9 m; with the next foothold 0.2 m ahead of the camera at 1 m/s the blind intervals are 0.8 s and 0.4 s.

The gate is defined in a late subsection and listed in the register's index of late gates (M3). It is measured through the real `frob` binary on every tier.

## Scope
In: the script, fixtures, assertions, structured log, gate receipt. Out: the query and the verb.

## Technical approach
**Script.** `scripts/e2e/sense_visibility.sh [--tiers referee,batch-cpu,cuda,metal] [--seed 2400]`. Plain shell, real binary, idempotent (scratch designs), seed-explicit, non-zero exit on failure.

**Fixtures** (`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sensorium/visibility/`): scene `flat-footholds` (a flat floor with marked foothold targets every 0.1 m along x); design `box-bot-h05` (a wheeled body with a depth camera at its front edge at H = 0.5 m, looking down and forward with a field of view covering the floor from 0.1 m to 3 m ahead) carrying payload `box` (top front corner 0.4 m ahead of the camera, 0.3 m above the floor); `box-bot-h09` (the same with the camera on a mast at H = 0.9 m, mast head 1 kg, robot 20 kg, half-track 0.2 m); `quad-swing` (the reference quadruped with a torso camera placed so that a front shank crosses its field of view once per stride); motions: straight drive at 1 m/s; trot at a stride of 0.8 s.

**Steps.**
1. *Box, H = 0.5 m.* `frob sense visibility box-bot-h05 --payload box --scene flat-footholds --route straight --speed 1.0 --tier <tier> --json`. From the per-target timelines compute the nearest floor target ever visible at an instant (the hidden boundary): assert 1.000 m within 1 cm. Assert the foothold 0.2 m ahead has a blind interval of 0.8 s (within one camera sample period) and that every gap is attributed to `payload`.
2. *Mast, H = 0.9 m.* Same with `box-bot-h09`: assert 0.600 m within 1 cm and 0.4 s.
3. *Payload removed.* `--payload none` on both designs: assert no target beyond the body's front edge is hidden at any instant and there are no findings.
4. *Swinging limb.* `frob sense visibility quad-swing --payload none --scene flat-footholds --route straight --gait trot --json`: assert gaps recur with the stride period (0.8 s within one sample period) and are attributed to the named shank link; assert `per_sensor` reports a non-zero self-occlusion fraction.
5. *Tier identity.* Repeat steps 1, 2 and 4 on each available tier; assert lead and blind interval (integers in quanta) are equal across tiers; where a tier's determinism class permits float differences in ray results, a differing integer must be traced to a named ray and is reported as a failure of "inside their determinism class" unless the class is D3 and the difference is one sample period at a grazing return, which is logged with the ray.
6. *Stage 7.* Design `box-bot-near`: as `box-bot-h05` but with a near-field camera whose view covers the floor from 0.1 m to 0.9 m ahead only. With the box, floor nearer than 1.0 m is hidden, so every foothold is `never-seen` and has no lead. `frob design check box-bot-near --task carry-box --scene flat-footholds --stages 7 --json` with a declared latency chain plus horizon of 0.5 s: assert the reserved lead refusal, exit 3, the cause `payload`, and ranked repairs as typed edits (raise or tilt the mount, move the payload, shorten the latency). Apply the proposed `move-sensor … --dz +0.4` (H = 0.9 m: the hidden boundary moves to 0.6 m, footholds are visible from 0.9 m to 0.6 m ahead, lead (0.9 − 0.2)/1 = 0.7 s, which exceeds 0.5 s) and assert the check passes with blind interval 0.4 s reported as a finding or as within the task's bound.
7. *Price cross-check.* `frob analyze stability box-bot-h05` and `box-bot-h09`: assert tip-over angles 33.7° and 32.0° within 0.1° (the mast's price is visible in the other analysis).
8. *Controls.* Control builds `visibility-scene-only` (no self or payload occluders): step 1 must report no hidden region and so fail the gate check; `visibility-no-self` (payload but not the robot's links): step 4 must fail; `visibility-counts-masked` : a glass-fronted target fixture must be reported visible and fail.

**Gate record.** Receipt: hidden boundaries (m) against 1.000 and 0.600 with tolerance 0.01; blind intervals (s); payload-removed result; swinging-limb period and attribution; per-tier integer equality; controls caught; commit, fingerprint, seed. Entered in `registries/gates.toml` for GATE-SENS-16; `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- gate_sens_16` wraps the script.

**Logging (STD-E2E).** `target/e2e-logs/sense_visibility/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N20b-34`), `step`, `event`. `cmd`: exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). `metric`: `hidden_boundary_m` with truth and tolerance, `blind_interval_s`, `lead_s`, `gap_period_s`, `self_occlusion_fraction`, tip-over angles. `assert`: expected and observed, including the attributed occluder. `refusal`: the stage-7 line with repairs. Digests of the visibility timelines per tier where compared; on failure the first differing target and instant and the run paths. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-SENS-16"]`; tiers run.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The script runs to exit 0 on a correct build on every available tier.
- [ ] Hidden boundaries 1.000 m and 0.600 m within 1 cm; blind intervals 0.8 s and 0.4 s within one sample period; attribution `payload`.
- [ ] No hidden region beyond the front edge with the payload removed.
- [ ] The swinging limb is found with the stride period and attributed to the named link.
- [ ] Lead and blind interval identical across tiers inside their class.
- [ ] The stage-7 refusal and repair path works end to end.
- [ ] Each control build fails the gate check; verified by the self-test.
- [ ] GATE-SENS-16 recorded with measured values.

### Known answers and negative controls
The box-and-mast case (1.000 m, 0.600 m; 0.8 s, 0.4 s; 33.7° to 32.0°); payload removed; the planted swinging limb. Controls: scene-only occluders; no self occluders; masked returns counted. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across tiers; across worker counts on batched tiers.

## Considerations
- The 1 cm tolerance requires foothold targets spaced finely near the boundary; the fixture spaces them at 1 cm between 0.5 m and 1.1 m.
- The camera's sample period bounds the resolution of intervals (33 ms at 30 Hz); the fixture uses 100 Hz so that 0.8 s and 0.4 s are resolved to 10 ms.
- Runtime (TARGET): under two minutes per tier; the query is kinematic.
