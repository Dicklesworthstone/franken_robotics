## Background
GATE-SENS-18 (Known answer; M2): "The rebound window reads 200 m/s² on the referee to 10⁻⁹ relative, and the adjacent windows read zero; the mutant without the jump term and the closed-closed window mutant both fail. The encoder fixture: +80 and −20 turns per second deliver bit-equal samples, `sense.alias` returns the pair, and the largest unambiguous speed is reported as 50 turns per second at ε = 0; after a planted packet loss the estimate is flagged `ambiguous` in 100 of 100 cases. The load-cell rows read +80.38 N and −23.62 N to 10⁻⁹, and each of the four card mutants (side, sign, origin, tare) changes the reading and is caught."

The gate accepts three additions to the proprioceptive models, each fixing what a sampled reading means where the instantaneous formula gives a wrong or ambiguous number: an integrating accelerometer across an impact (the window's reading is the mean specific force including the velocity jump of the mounted point; a body in ballistic flight rebounding from −1 to +1 m/s inside a 10 ms window reads +200 m/s² upward while every smooth sample is zero); unwrapping a sampled modulo encoder (valid only when Ω·Δt + 2·ε < 1/2 turn; at 100 Hz, +80 and −20 turns per second deliver identical samples); and the load-cell cut (a six-axis force-torque sensor reads the momentum balance of everything distal to its declared cut: a 2 kg foot under a 100 N ground reaction reads +80.38 N untared, and −23.62 N in swing with an upward acceleration of 2 m/s²; the sole's contact sum would read 100 N and 0 N).

The gate is defined in a subsection added by the final audit and is listed in the gate register's final-audit table. It is measured through the real `frob` binary.

## Scope
In: the script, fixtures, assertions, structured log, gate receipt. Out: the three mechanisms.

## Technical approach
**Script.** `scripts/e2e/sense_sampled_readings.sh [--tier referee] [--seed 2800] [--cases 100]`. Plain shell, real binary, idempotent, seed-explicit, non-zero exit on failure. Every `sim.run` call carries `--tier referee`, `--telemetry T2` (the script reads sensor packets, which are recorded at T2; a query of a T1 run returns a `level-not-enabled` gap) and its own `--key`. Mutant steps need control builds: the `frob` binary built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,<crate>/<feature>` for one plant at a time (`frob-sense/imu-no-jump` of N20b-39, the closed-closed plant of N20a-06 by the feature CX-201's control record names, `frob-sense/unwrap-nearest-always` of N20b-40, `frob-sense/loadcell-contact-sum` of N20b-41; a plant's own feature is never in the umbrella, BD-025); the script checks `frob doctor --json` for the feature.

**Fixtures** (`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/sensorium/sampled/`): `bounce` (a non-rotating body in ballistic flight that rebounds from −1 to +1 m/s on the referee's event step, with an integrating IMU of 10 ms exposure; the launch is timed so the impact falls strictly inside one window, and a second variant puts it exactly on a window boundary); `spin-encoder` (one joint with a single-turn encoder sampled at 100 Hz, zero noise, joint speed limit 100 turns per second), committed as the two constant-speed designs `spin-encoder-p80` and `spin-encoder-m20` (+80 and −20 turns per second, equal initial phase) and with the mission `spin-direction` ("report the direction of rotation", whose success sets for the two speeds are disjoint); `spin-loss` (the same at 30 turns per second with a link that drops one named packet, the packet index and initial phase drawn by address from the seed); `foot-stance-<card>` and `foot-swing-<card>` (a 2 kg foot below a force-torque sensor: stationary under a prescribed 100 N upward ground reaction; and contact-free with a prescribed upward acceleration of 2 m/s²), one design per card variant `ft-cut`, `ft-cut-side-flipped`, `ft-cut-sign-flipped`, `ft-cut-origin-moved`, `ft-cut-tared`. Noise-free cards and card variants are properties of the committed fixtures, because `sim.run` takes designs and has no noise, speed or card flags.

**Steps.**
1. *Rebound window.* `frob sim run bounce --tier referee --seed <seed> --telemetry T2 --key sr-bounce-<seed> --json`; `frob telemetry query <run> --stream sensing --channels imu --json`. Assert the window containing the impact reads +200 m/s² on the upward axis to 10⁻⁹ relative and the windows before and after read 0 (absolute tolerance 10⁻⁹ m/s²).
2. *Boundary impact.* The boundary variant: assert the impact is in the window that opens at that instant (that window reads 200, the previous reads 0).
3. *Mutants.* Control build `imu-no-jump`: step 1 reads 0 in the impact window: the gate check must fail. Control build `window-closed-closed`: step 2 reads 200 in both windows: the gate check must fail.
4. *Encoder equality.* `frob sim run spin-encoder-p80 --tier referee --seconds 1 --seed <seed> --telemetry T2 --key sr-p80 --json` and the same for `spin-encoder-m20` with `--key sr-m20` (the compared runs are distinct jobs, KNOWN_DRIFTS 5); compare the encoder channel's digests (`frob telemetry summary <run> --digests --json`) and the 100 samples byte for byte (`frob telemetry query <run> --stream sensing --channels encoder --json`): assert bit-equal.
5. *Unambiguous speed and alias.* `frob sense profile spin-encoder-p80 --json`: assert `largest_unambiguous_speed` = 50 turns per second and `unambiguous: false` (limit 100). `frob sense alias spin-encoder-p80 --mission spin-direction --key al-enc-<seed> --json` (the structural two-speed source of N20b-40 is consulted for each encoder whose limit exceeds v_max): assert a witness whose two worlds are constant speeds differing by 100 turns per second (the pair +80 and −20 for the fixture), class `Certified`, with separating changes listed.
6. *Packet loss.* For 100 seeds (the dropped packet's index and the initial phase drawn by address): `frob sim run spin-loss --tier referee --seconds 1 --seed s --telemetry T2 --key sr-loss-s --json`; assert the velocity estimate spanning the 20 ms gap carries `ambiguous` (30 × 0.02 = 0.6 turn ≥ 1/2) and that a consumer schema without the flag receives a masked value. Count 100 of 100. Control build `unwrap-nearest-always` (the plant `N20b-40:unwrap-nearest-always` of N20-CX's late slot L-c): the flag is absent: the gate check must fail.
7. *Load cell.* `frob sim run foot-stance-ft-cut --tier referee --telemetry T2 --key sr-stance --json` and `foot-swing-ft-cut` with `--key sr-swing`, read with `--stream sensing --channels ft`: assert +80.38 N and −23.62 N to 10⁻⁹ (the exact values 100 − 2 × 9.81 and −2 × (9.81 + 2)).
8. *Card mutants.* Repeat step 7 with each of the four variant-card designs (`foot-{stance,swing}-ft-cut-side-flipped` and so on, each run with its own key `sr-<stance|swing>-<variant>`): assert each changes at least one reading (force or moment) from the reference and that the comparison flags it: side flipped (−80.38 N, +23.62 N); sign flipped; origin moved by 0.05 m (moment differs by 4.019 N·m in stance); tared at stance (0 N, −104.00 N). Control build `loadcell-contact-sum` (the plant `N20b-41:loadcell-contact-sum` of N20-CX's late slot L-d): reads 100 N and 0 N: the gate check must fail.

9. *Conformance rows* (the Gauntlet has no `frob` verb, ADR-GAUNT-01): `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-SENS-18` (the G1 target of N38a-02, running the gate's known answers by the numbers N20b-45 allocated, and its registered controls in the same run; one run covers every execution site); assert on the hash-named receipt under `target/gauntlet/receipts/` that every run entry is `pass` and every control `caught`, and log its hash.

**Gate record.** The script writes no record. The recording session enters GATE-SENS-18 in `registries/gates.toml` from the step-9 receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SENS-18 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19; this bead is the gate's one recorder, N20b-39, N20b-40 and N20b-41 producing receipts only), citing this script's log for the user-side values: the three window readings; the boundary assignment; mutants caught (no jump term; closed-closed); the digest equality; v_max; the witness identity; the flagged count out of 100; the two load-cell readings with their errors; the four card mutants' readings; commit, fingerprint, seeds.

**Logging (STD-E2E).** `target/e2e-logs/sense_sampled_readings/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Lines carry `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N20b-42`), `step`, `event`; `cmd` with exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` with value, unit, truth and tolerance (`window_accel_mps2` against 200 and 0 at 10⁻⁹; `vmax_tps` against 50; `ambiguous_count` against 100; `ft_force_N` against 80.38 and −23.62 at 10⁻⁹; `ft_moment_Nm`); digests of the two encoder channels; `assert` per check including each mutant "caught"; on failure the first differing sample index or seed and the run paths; `summary` with assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-SENS-18"]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Rebound window 200 m/s² to 10⁻⁹ relative; adjacent windows zero; boundary impact assigned to the opening window.
- [ ] Both accelerometer mutants fail the gate check.
- [ ] +80 and −20 turns per second deliver bit-equal samples; the alias verb returns the pair; v_max is 50 turns per second at ε = 0.
- [ ] `ambiguous` flagged in 100 of 100 planted-loss cases; the nearest-always mutant fails.
- [ ] Load-cell rows +80.38 N and −23.62 N to 10⁻⁹; each of the four card mutants changes the reading and is caught; the contact-sum mutant fails.
- [ ] GATE-SENS-18 recorded from the step-9 receipt with measured values.
- [ ] The self-test confirms each control build makes the script exit non-zero.

- Runtime (TARGET): under three minutes including 100 loss cases.
