## Background

Spark derives kernel arithmetic once (symbolic derivation by FrankenSymPy in the annex `derive/frob-derive`,
lowering to the kernel IR `frob-kir`, emission as Rust and as GPU dialect text). That route is Frontier and has a
kill gate decided at milestone M2 by bead BD-221:

**KILL-KIR.** Trigger: at M2 the derivation toolchain cannot express or emit more than 20% of the kernel
arithmetic, or emitted kernels miss the speed bound of bet BET-01 (derived kernels more than 1.3 times slower than
hand-tuned equivalents on the same backend) after one optimization pass. Fallback: arithmetic is hand-written in
Rust and the dialect and bound by differential tests; certificates fall back per the certificate kill gate
KILL-LEMMA. The skeleton pattern is unaffected.

This bead exists only on the branch where the gate fires. It is written in advance because the plan requires
every kill gate's fallback to be named before the measurement, so that the measurement cannot be argued with
afterwards. If KILL-KIR does not fire, this bead closes with reason `withdrawn` citing the decision record
(`withdrawn: not needed: KILL-KIR not fired, docs/adr/ADR-KILL-KIR-M2.md`, N41-17's typed close rules).

What changes downstream if it fires: every kernel body that was generated becomes a pair of hand-written sources
(Rust and dialect); the guarantee "the two were emitted from one program" is replaced by a tested guarantee
"the two agree on a differential corpus"; certificates that were bound to a derived program's content hash are
replaced by the certificate fallback; derivative programs must be written by hand or excused; decision
ADR-SPARK-02 is amended (not deleted): one shared dialect source stays, derived arithmetic is withdrawn. What does
not change: hand-written skeletons, the dialect and its lint, the dimension checker (which still type-checks
hand-written bodies expressed through the IR builder), the kernel classes, the backends, every determinism gate.
Tier: Solid (the fallback is ordinary engineering).

## Scope

In: the conversion procedure from generated to hand-written bodies; the differential-test contract and corpus;
the changes to the library index, the generated-code check and the certificate links; the scope decision (whole
library or only the kernels that failed).
Out: the decision itself and its measurement (BD-221); the certificate fallback's own design; rewriting any
skeleton.

## Technical approach

**Scope rule (DESIGN).** The kill gate has two clauses and they imply different scopes.
- *Share clause fired* (the toolchain expresses or emits at most 20% of kernel arithmetic): all kernel arithmetic
  becomes hand-written; `derive/frob-derive` is kept only for certificates on the CPU side and for derivative
  kernels of CPU solvers, where it still pays.
- *Speed clause fired* (derived kernels miss the 1.3 bound): only the kernels that miss it are hand-written;
  kernels inside the bound stay generated. The library index records which is which.

**Conversion, per kernel body.**
1. Freeze the last generated Rust and dialect text as the starting point (it is correct by test, only slow or
   incomplete), remove the `GENERATED` header, move the files from `generated/` to `handwritten/`, and delete the
   manifest row from the derivation manifest so that the generated-code check no longer owns them.
2. The engineer may then restructure for speed, under the dialect rules and bans (no fused multiply-add, no
   transcendental built-ins, masks as selects), keeping one operation per statement where bit equality between
   Rust and dialect is wanted.
3. Register in the library index: `generated_body = "hand-written"`, `derivative = present | excused(<reason>)`.

**The differential-test contract (what "bound by differential tests" means).** For every hand-written body:
1. *Rust against dialect, per backend*: the CPU-versus-device check on a frozen corpus of at least 10⁶ seeded
   inputs per kernel, including the edge set (zeros of both signs, subnormals, largest finite values, inputs at
   the admission bounds of the stage), compared under the kernel's determinism class: bit-equal for class D1; at
   most 4 ulp per output for class D3, with the disagreement histogram stored.
2. *Against an independent oracle*: FrankenSim's corresponding routine through the airlock where one exists
   (forward kinematics, articulated dynamics), or a double-precision evaluation of the source formula written
   separately from the kernel, within a stated tolerance in ulp of the `f32` result.
3. *Identities*: the algebraic identities the derived kernel would have carried as certificates are kept as
   property tests on the corpus (for example: the Cayley update lands on the group to 4 ulp; the adjoint composes;
   ⟨J·v, w⟩ = ⟨v, Jᵀ·w⟩ for each derivative pair), classed `Tested` instead of `Certified`.
4. *Dimension typing*: the body is also expressed once through the IR builder so that the dimension checker runs
   on it (proof obligation PO-SPARK-01 survives the fallback); a body that cannot be so expressed carries a
   reviewed waiver.
5. *Mutation*: each kernel's differential test must catch a planted one-ulp change in one backend's source and a
   planted sign flip in one term.

**Evidence consequences.** Outputs of hand-written kernels are evidence class `Tested`; any claim that previously
cited a kernel certificate is re-issued or withdrawn; the claim product of every result that passes through such a
kernel reflects the weaker class. The honest-status table and the mechanism register are regenerated.

**Decision record.** The decision bead (BD-221) writes the kill-gate decision record
`docs/adr/ADR-KILL-KIR-M2.md` (every decision record lives in `docs/adr/`, a kill-gate decision under its gate's
name and milestone, with the fields the kill-gate record bead N40-03 requires): which clause fired, the measured
share and ratios with their receipt hashes, the outcome, the fallback in force. This bead completes it on the
fallback branch with what only the conversion knows: the scope chosen, the list of kernels converted with their old
and new `KernelId`s, the certificates re-issued or withdrawn, and the reversal condition (a later toolchain version
that expresses more than 20% and meets the 1.3 bound on the frozen comparison set).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] If KILL-KIR did not fire: this bead closes `withdrawn` (not needed), citing BD-221's `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-KIR-M2.md`. Otherwise all of the following.
- [ ] Every kernel in scope has hand-written Rust and dialect bodies under `handwritten/`, no stale manifest row, and an index entry marked `hand-written`.
- [ ] Each such kernel passes the differential contract parts 1 to 4 on each backend it claims, with corpus size, disagreement histogram and attained class recorded in the divergence ledger.
- [ ] Each kernel's differential test catches its two planted mutants (one-ulp change, sign flip).
- [ ] `xtask check-generated` passes with the reduced manifest; an attempt to keep a `GENERATED` header on a hand-written file fails the check.
- [ ] Certificates formerly bound to converted kernels are re-issued under the certificate fallback or listed as withdrawn; no envelope reports `Certified` for a converted kernel.
- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-KIR-M2.md` exists with the decision bead's fields and this bead's additions (scope, converted kernels with both ids, certificates re-issued or withdrawn, reversal condition); ADR-SPARK-02 carries an amendment note.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-kernels handwritten`)
- `handwritten_matches_frozen_generated`: for each converted kernel, outputs equal the last generated version inside 4 ulp on 10⁵ inputs (a regression guard for the conversion step itself).
- `differential_rust_vs_dialect_corpus`: the corpus digest is checked in; the comparison runs on `batch-cpu` and each device.
- `oracle_agreement`: per kernel against its independent oracle within the stated tolerance.
- `identity_property_tests`: group membership, adjoint composition, adjoint duality, per kernel.
- `dimension_typing_still_runs`: the IR-builder twin of each hand-written body type-checks; a seeded unit error is rejected with `FROB-REF-250`.
- `mutant_one_ulp_is_caught`, `mutant_sign_flip_is_caught` (negative controls, per kernel).
- `stale_generated_header_fails` (negative control for the generated-code check).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/spark_kir_fallback.sh` (owned by this bead; it exists only on the fallback branch and sources the
shared end-to-end helper): for each converted kernel runs `frob spark check --kernel <name> --backend
batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal --key <k> --json` and asserts that the check row it writes is current for the
kernel's new `KernelId` (the hand-written body changes the id, so a row from the derived body must not satisfy it;
`--require-current-check` is a `spark.place` option and is not used here), then the mutant builds, expecting
failure. Logged
per line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time, evidence block (class `Tested`),
corpus size, maximum disagreement in ulp against the threshold, the attained determinism class, digests, and on
failure the first differing input. A final `summary` line lists kernels converted and `gates: ["KILL-KIR"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- The kernels' own known answers still apply unchanged (for example the Cayley fixtures and the adjoint fixture of the pilot).
- Negative controls: the two mutants per kernel; the stale header.
- Determinism lanes: unchanged from the generated kernels: twice on one machine, across worker counts, debug and release, across backends against each one's class.

## Considerations

- Three hand-written languages' worth of arithmetic (Rust, dialect, and derivatives) is exactly what the derivation route was meant to replace; the fallback's cost is ongoing maintenance, and the reversal condition is recorded so the route can be reopened.
- Bit equality between hand-written Rust and dialect is harder to keep than between two emissions of one program; where class D1 was attained with generated bodies and is lost with hand-written ones, the family's class is lowered honestly and downstream licences (pairing, replay) follow the weaker class.
- Per-design specialization depends on exact, mechanical transformations of a program; if arithmetic is hand-written, specialization for survivors loses its basis and its own kill gate is read accordingly.
- Certificate coverage for derived kernels at M5 shrinks to whatever stays generated.
