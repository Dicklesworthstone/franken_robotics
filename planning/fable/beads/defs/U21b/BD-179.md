## Background

Devices run single precision: Metal has no double type, and an RTX 4090 runs double precision at about 1/64 of its
single-precision rate (FACT). BET-08 is the bet that single precision is adequate on the GPU, given four measures:
anchored positions (an integer cell index plus an `f32` offset in [−4, 4) m from the centre of an 8 m cell, so that
resolution is at worst 0.24 µm anywhere in a scene); group-preserving updates with per-step normalization
(construction C-05, "time stepping stays on the group": Cayley steps land on the rotation group; unit quaternions
and unit complex numbers are renormalized each step);
fixed-order pairwise or compensated sums; and a contact solve carried out in power-of-two-scaled variables with a
reported conditioning figure. Accumulators that need more (time offsets, odometry, the energy ledger) use a
double-float pair. Half precision is not used anywhere in the step.

BET-08's falsifier: the precision battery fails at long horizons (constraint violation, group-membership error or
energy error exceed their envelopes), or a strict predicate decided in guarded single precision disagrees with
exact integer evaluation. Its arm is `batch-cpu` in double precision on the same worlds. Its fallback, named in
advance: double-float accumulators on the failing quantities; quantities that still fail are evaluated on
`batch-cpu` and labelled.

Two gates measure the bet. **GATE-SPARK-05** (Known answer, M2, TARGET): precision battery: over 10⁶ steps,
group-membership error within 4 ulp, closure and contact violations inside their envelopes, energy-ledger residual
under 10⁻⁴ relative, at distances up to 1 km from the origin. **GATE-SPARK-18** (Known answer, M2, TARGET):
PO-NUM-02 (the computed Cholesky factor is invariant under power-of-two scaling) holds on each backend; on 10⁴
contact states per reference robot the relative error of the `f32` Newton step against `f64` is at most 10·κ₂·u,
and at most 10⁻³ on near-rigid states under the `stiff` profile; κ̂ never exceeds the κ₂ computed in `f64`.

This bead is the capstone that builds the battery, runs both gates on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal, and records
them. The mechanisms it rests on are their own beads: the anchored-position representation and its exactness proof
(N21b-37), the conditioning certificate and its harness (N21b-38), double-float accumulators (N21b-36), the
integration and anchoring kernels (BD-157), the solve kernels (BD-156) and the energy ledger on batched tiers
(BD-208). Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the precision battery (scenarios, placements, horizons, measures, thresholds, reference arm); the translation
tests out to 1 km; running the conditioning harness on each backend; the negative controls, including the
uncentred shift; the end-to-end script; the two gate receipts.

## Technical approach

**Battery definition (frozen in `frob-conformance/battery/precision/` with a content hash before the first run).**

| Dimension | Values |
|---|---|
| Workloads | Reference quadruped Q (18 degrees of freedom, C = 32, R = 160, h = 2 ms, four warm-started Newton iterations, decimation 10) walking under the baseline controller on flat and rough ground; reference humanoid H (35, C = 64, R = 320) standing and walking; one closed-loop mechanism fixture for closure violation |
| Horizon | 10⁶ world-steps per world (2,000 s of simulated time at h = 2 ms), in episodes with masked resets; violations are tracked as running maxima over the whole horizon |
| Translations | The same worlds with every cell index offset by 0, 13 (104 m), 63 (504 m) and 125 (1,000 m) cells along a diagonal direction; and, separately, a world whose robot walks continuously in one direction so that it crosses cells (at 1 m/s for 2,000 s it crosses 250 cells) |
| Placements | `batch-cpu` in `f32`; each RTX 4090; Metal. Reference arm: `batch-cpu` in `f64` on the same world addresses |
| Worlds | 256 world addresses per workload and translation (enough for maxima over many contact histories; small enough for the `f64` arm) |

**Measures and thresholds.**
1. *Group-membership error.* For every rotational state: | ‖q‖² − 1 | measured in units in the last place at 1
   (one ulp is 2⁻²³ ≈ 1.19·10⁻⁷): the running maximum over all worlds, joints and steps must be at most 4 ulp. For
   unit complex joint coordinates the same test on c² + s².
2. *Closure and contact violations.* The maximum loop-closure residual and the maximum contact violation
   (penetration beyond the law's compliance, complementarity residual) must lie inside the envelopes frozen in the
   fidelity ledger for the workload; the same quantities from the `f64` arm are logged beside them.
3. *Energy-ledger residual.* The energy ledger accounts for every joule (kinetic, potential, stored, actuator work,
   dissipation, contact work); its residual relative to the total energy that flowed must stay under 10⁻⁴ over the
   horizon. The ledger's accumulators are double-float; if a device's canary failed, this criterion is recorded
   `not run` for that device.
4. *Translation invariance.* Because positions are cell plus offset and scene fields are addressed cell-relative,
   a world translated by a whole number of cells must produce **bit-identical** offsets, velocities, contact
   states and digests (of everything except the cell indices) at every step. This is the sharp form of "at
   distances up to 1 km": the run at 125 cells is compared digest by digest with the run at 0.
5. *Cell crossings.* In the walking-through-cells world, every re-anchoring is exact: the represented coordinate
   8·c + o, evaluated exactly on the host from snapshots, changes across a re-anchoring step only by the step's own
   motion; violations and group error show no excursion at crossing steps.
6. *Faults.* No world is marked `Faulted`; `Degraded` worlds are counted by reason and are zero for
   `ill-conditioned` under the default impedance.

**Conditioning on devices (GATE-SPARK-18).** Run N21b-38's two harnesses on each backend: the bitwise metamorphic
test of PO-NUM-02 on 10⁴ matrices, and the Newton-step comparison on 10⁴ contact states per reference robot
sampled from the battery's own runs (with the near-rigid subset under the `stiff` profile). Record the three
clauses with measured maxima.

**Negative controls (each must fail the battery or its test).**
- *The uncentred [0, 8) shift* (seed done-condition): the conformance variant of `K-anchor` with offsets in [0, 8).
  It fails the exhaustive exactness test at o = −2⁻²⁵ (returns 8.0, moved and out of range) and fails the battery's
  cell-crossing measure in the walking world.
- *Absolute `f32` positions without anchoring*: a conformance configuration that stores world positions as plain
  `f32`. At 1,000 m the spacing is 2⁻¹⁴ m (61 µm); contact violation leaves its envelope and translation invariance
  fails.
- *Normalization removed*: `K-normalize` skipped; group-membership error grows past 4 ulp within the horizon.
- *Padding with +0.0 in the energy sums*: changes ledger bits across capacities (caught by the determinism lane).
- *Scaling by 10 instead of a power of two*: breaks PO-NUM-02 (in N21b-38).

**If a measure fails on the real configuration.** BET-08's fallback applies and is recorded, not improvised:
the failing quantity gets a double-float accumulator (for example a positional accumulator or a longer-lived sum);
if it still fails it is evaluated on `batch-cpu` and every result that depends on it is labelled. The gate is then
recorded as failed for the `f32` path with the fallback in force.

**Gate records.** One hash-named receipt per backend for GATE-SPARK-05 (running maxima of each measure against its
threshold, digests of the translation pairs) and for GATE-SPARK-18 (matrices tested and differing; maxima of
ε/(κ₂·u); the near-rigid maximum; count of κ̂ > κ₂), with commit, fingerprint digest and seed; registry rows entered
by a recording session that did not implement the work.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SPARK-05 on each of `batch-cpu` (`f32`), CUDA and Metal: over 10⁶ steps, group-membership error at most 4 ulp; closure and contact violations inside their ledger envelopes; energy-ledger residual under 10⁻⁴ relative; at translations of 0, 104 m, 504 m and 1,000 m.
- [ ] Translation by whole cells is bit-identical: the digests of the 1,000 m run equal those of the origin run at every chunk, on every backend.
- [ ] The walking-through-cells world crosses at least 250 cells with exact re-anchoring and no excursion in any measure at crossing steps.
- [ ] GATE-SPARK-18 on each backend: PO-NUM-02 bit for bit on 10⁴ matrices; ε ≤ 10·κ₂·u on 10⁴ contact states per reference robot; ε ≤ 10⁻³ on near-rigid states under `stiff`; κ̂ ≤ κ₂ for every state.
- [ ] The uncentred [0, 8) shift fails the battery (seed done-condition), and each other negative control fails its measure in the same run.
- [ ] The `f64` reference arm is run on the same world addresses and its violations and energy residual are logged beside the `f32` figures.
- [ ] No `Faulted` world; `Degraded` counts by reason are reported; receipts carry measured values, commit, fingerprint digest and seed.
- [ ] If any measure fails, the recorded outcome names the quantity and the fallback applied (double-float accumulator, or evaluation on `batch-cpu` with a label).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `group_error_ulp_metric`: ‖q‖² = 1 + 4·2⁻²³ is 4 ulp (passes); 1 + 5·2⁻²³ is 5 ulp (fails).
- `energy_residual_metric`: a synthetic ledger (labelled `synthetic`) with 10⁴ J of flow and a 0.5 J imbalance gives 5·10⁻⁵ (passes); 2 J gives 2·10⁻⁴ (fails).
- `battery_definition_hash_frozen`: the battery directory's content hash equals the registered value; a changed scenario changes it.
- `translation_pairs_share_addresses`: the translated worlds use the same world addresses, designs, policies and seeds as the origin worlds.
- `short_battery_cpu`: 10⁴ steps on `batch-cpu` `f32` against `f64` for Q: all measures inside thresholds; translation digests equal.
- `uncentred_fails_short_battery`, `absolute_f32_fails_at_1km`, `no_normalize_fails_group_error` (negative controls at 10⁵ steps).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/spark_precision_battery.sh [--tier batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal] [--steps N] [--seed N]`. Steps:
`anchor_exhaustive` (2²⁵ values through `K-anchor` per backend); `battery` (`frob sim run --tier <t> --battery
precision --steps 1000000 --json` per workload and translation, and the `f64` arm on `batch-cpu`); `translation`
(digest comparison of translated pairs); `crossing` (the walking world, with host-side exact evaluation of
8·c + o from snapshots); `po_num_02` and `newton_vs_f64` (the conditioning harnesses); `controls` (each negative
control, expecting failure); `gate` (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- GATE-SPARK-05 GATE-SPARK-18`).
Logging through the STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/spark_precision_battery/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`: every
`cmd` with exact command line, exit code and wall time; the envelope's evidence block (claim known-answer, class,
tier, determinism, identities with fingerprint digest, seed); `metric` lines for the running maximum of group
error in ulp (threshold 4), the largest closure and contact violations with their envelopes, the energy residual
(threshold 10⁻⁴), translation digest pairs (threshold: equal), cells crossed, `Faulted` and `Degraded` counts, the
conditioning maxima with their thresholds; on failure the first step and world address at which a measure left its
threshold, with the snapshot paths of both arms. The `summary` counts assertions and names both gates.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-541 (precision battery over 10⁶ steps at distances up to 1 km: group-membership error within 4 ulp; closure
  and contact violations inside their envelopes; energy-ledger residual under 10⁻⁴ relative).
- The numerics chapter's known answers: offset −2⁻²⁵ m in a [0, 8) cell (the uncentred shift returns 8.0); factor
  of D·H·D against D times the factor of H (bit-identical); H = diag(1, 10⁻⁸) with residual (0, 10⁻⁷) (the scaled
  test fails).
- Known answers of spacing: 0.24 µm inside a cell; 7.6 µm at 100 m and 61 µm at 1 km for plain `f32`.
- Metamorphic relations: whole-cell translation (bit-identical); mirroring; scaling every length by 4, time by 2
  and mass by 2 reproduces significand bits where the kernels are scale-homogeneous.
- Determinism lanes: each placement twice (identical digests, class D2); `f32` devices against `f32` `batch-cpu`
  in the class the family holds; across capacities for the energy sums.

## Considerations

- Cost: 10⁶ steps × 256 worlds × (2 workloads + fixture) × 4 translations is about 3·10⁹ world-steps per backend,
  roughly half an hour on one RTX 4090 at the interim floor rate, longer on Metal, and several hours for the `f64`
  arm on the Mac's CPU. The `f64` arm exceeds the default compute envelope's hour if run as one job; it is split
  into per-workload jobs, or run under an approved envelope class (OPEN-SPARK-2). Rows not run are recorded
  `not run`.
- The envelopes for closure and contact violation come from the fidelity ledger; until that ledger is frozen the
  corresponding clause can be measured but not recorded as passed.
- OPEN-NUM-3 (cell side, default 8 m) and OPEN-NUM-4 (the ill-conditioning limit and the `stiff` refinement count;
  decided by GATE-SPARK-18) are informed by this bead's measurements.
- A battery that passes at the origin and fails only at 1 km would indicate a kernel that touches absolute
  position; the translation digests localize it immediately, which is why the translated runs compare digests and
  not only violations.
- Thermal throttling on the Mac affects time, not bits; the battery asserts no timing.
- KILL-D1 does not affect this gate: every measure is either an invariant of one placement or a comparison with
  `f64` envelopes, never a bitwise comparison between device and CPU.
