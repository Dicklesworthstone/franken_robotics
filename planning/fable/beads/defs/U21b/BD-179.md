## Background

Devices run single precision: Metal has no double type, and an RTX 4090 runs double precision at about 1/64 of its
single-precision rate (FACT). BET-08 is the bet that single precision is adequate on the GPU, given four measures:
anchored positions (an integer cell index plus an `f32` offset in [−4, 4) m from the centre of an 8 m cell, so that
resolution is at worst 0.24 µm anywhere in a scene); group-preserving updates with per-step normalization
(construction C-05, "time stepping stays on the group": Cayley steps land on the rotation group; unit quaternions
and unit complex numbers are renormalized each step);
fixed-order pairwise or compensated sums; and a contact solve carried out in power-of-two-scaled variables with a
reported conditioning figure. Accumulators that need more (time offsets, odometry, the energy ledger) use a
double-float pair. Half precision is not used anywhere in the step.

BET-08's falsifier: the precision battery fails at long horizons (constraint violation, group-membership error or
energy error exceed their envelopes), or a strict predicate decided in guarded single precision disagrees with
exact integer evaluation. Its arm is `batch-cpu` in double precision on the same worlds. Its fallback, named in
advance: double-float accumulators on the failing quantities; quantities that still fail are evaluated on
`batch-cpu` and labelled.

Two gates measure the bet. **GATE-SPARK-05** (Known answer, M2, TARGET): precision battery: over 10⁶ steps,
group-membership error within 4 ulp, closure and contact violations inside their envelopes, energy-ledger residual
under 10⁻⁴ relative, at distances up to 1 km from the origin. **GATE-SPARK-18** (Known answer, M2, TARGET):
PO-NUM-02 (the computed Cholesky factor is invariant under power-of-two scaling) holds on each backend; on 10⁴
contact states per reference robot the relative error of the `f32` Newton step against `f64` is at most 10·κ₂·u,
and at most 10⁻³ on near-rigid states under the `stiff` profile; κ̂ never exceeds the κ₂ computed in `f64`.

This bead is the capstone that builds the battery, runs both gates on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal, and records
them. The mechanisms it rests on are their own beads: the anchored-position representation and its exactness proof
(N21b-37), the conditioning certificate and its harness (N21b-38), double-float accumulators (N21b-36), the
integration and anchoring kernels (BD-157), the solve kernels (BD-156) and the energy ledger on batched tiers
(BD-208). Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the precision battery (scenarios, placements, horizons, measures, thresholds, reference arm); the translation
tests out to 1 km; running the conditioning harness on each backend; the negative controls, including the
uncentred shift; the end-to-end script; the two gate receipts.

Out: every mechanism listed above; the measurement of the energy-ledger residual clause and the completed
GATE-SPARK-05 record (N21bp2-01, which depends on this bead and on the energy ledger of the batched tiers, BD-208,
a later seed than this one); `sim.run` on the batched tiers with `--task` and `--precision` (N16b-25, N35-04) and
the numeric certificate block the measures are read from (N35-27), all dependencies; the strict predicate half of
BET-08's falsifier (GATE-SPARK-08, BD-030). The fidelity ledger (BD-212) is not read: its rows are cross-tier
disagreements of task-level quantities and hold no closure or contact violation.

## Technical approach

**Battery definition (frozen in `frob-conformance/battery/precision/` with a content hash before the first run).**

| Dimension | Values |
|---|---|
| Workloads | Reference quadruped Q (18 degrees of freedom, C = 32, R = 160, h = 2 ms, four warm-started Newton iterations, decimation 10) walking under the baseline controller on flat and rough ground; reference humanoid H (35, C = 64, R = 320) standing and walking; one closed-loop mechanism fixture for closure violation |
| Horizon | 10⁶ world-steps per world (2,000 s of simulated time at h = 2 ms), in episodes with masked resets; violations are tracked as running maxima over the whole horizon |
| Translations | The same worlds with every cell index offset by (k, k, 0) cells for k = 0, 13, 63 and 125: 0, 104 m, 504 m and 1,000 m along each of x and y, so 0, 147 m, 713 m and 1,414 m from the origin (the `f32` spacing that matters is that of each coordinate); and, separately, a world whose robot walks continuously along x so that it crosses cells (at 1 m/s for 2,000 s it passes at most 250 cell faces) |
| Placements | `batch-cpu` in `f32`; each RTX 4090; Metal. Reference arm: `batch-cpu` in `f64` on the same world addresses |
| Worlds | 256 world addresses per workload and translation (enough for maxima over many contact histories; small enough for the `f64` arm) |

The directory holds `scenarios.toml` (one row per scenario: workload, design, registered task, translation in
cells, world count, horizon in seconds, seed) and, once the reference arm has run, `envelopes.toml` (measure 2).
A translation is a property of the scenario's task, not a verb option: the four tasks of a workload differ only in
their scene fixture, which is the same scene authored with its origin moved by (k, k, 0) cells, and reset poses are
given in the scene's frame, so they move with it. Each scenario is one `sim.run` job; the measures are read from
the numeric certificate block of the job's result (N35-27: running maxima of group-membership error, of closure
and contact violation and of the energy-ledger residual, and the `Degraded` and `Faulted` counts by reason).

**Measures and thresholds.**
1. *Group-membership error.* For every rotational state: | ‖q‖² − 1 | measured in units in the last place at 1
   (one ulp is 2⁻²³ ≈ 1.19·10⁻⁷): the running maximum over all worlds, joints and steps must be at most 4 ulp. For
   unit complex joint coordinates the same test on c² + s².
2. *Closure and contact violations.* The maximum loop-closure residual and the maximum contact violation
   (penetration beyond the law's compliance, complementarity residual) must lie inside their envelopes. DESIGN:
   the plan says "inside their envelopes" and gives no number, and no other record holds one, so the envelope of
   a quantity is fixed from the `f64` reference arm before any `f32` arm runs: E = max(2·M₆₄, F), where M₆₄ is
   the `f64` arm's running maximum of that quantity for the workload on the same addresses and horizon, and F is
   a floor: 2⁻¹⁸ m (3.8 µm, sixteen offset spacings) for the loop-closure residual and for penetration beyond
   compliance, and 2⁻¹⁶ (1.5·10⁻⁵) of the normal impulse for the complementarity residual. Reason: the step's
   violations are set by its fixed iteration count and not by its precision, so adequate single precision means
   staying within a small factor of double precision; the floor keeps a quantity that double precision drives to
   round-off from failing on single-precision round-off alone. M₆₄ and E are written to
   `battery/precision/envelopes.toml` with the battery hash, and registered as thresholds of BET-08's falsifier
   clause, before the first `f32` run; an edit afterwards is a new battery version. The factor 2 and the floors
   are TARGETs. The `f64` values are logged beside every `f32` value.
3. *Energy-ledger residual.* The energy ledger accounts for every joule (kinetic, potential, stored, actuator work,
   dissipation, contact work); its residual relative to the total energy that flowed must stay under 10⁻⁴ over the
   horizon. The ledger's accumulators are double-float; if a device's canary failed, this criterion is recorded
   `not run` for that device. The battery defines this measure and its threshold; it is measured by N21bp2-01,
   because the ledger kernel of the batched tiers (BD-208) is a later seed than this bead. Until then every run of
   the battery records the clause `not run: energy ledger not yet on the batched tiers`.
4. *Translation invariance.* Because positions are cell plus offset and scene fields are addressed cell-relative,
   a world translated by a whole number of cells must produce **bit-identical** offsets, velocities, contact
   states and digests (of everything except the cell indices) at every step. This is the sharp form of "at
   distances up to 1 km": the run at 125 cells is compared digest by digest with the run at 0.
5. *Cell crossings.* In the walking-through-cells world, every re-anchoring is exact. Snapshots and telemetry
   exist per control tick and not per physics step, so the check is an audit that only the conformance build has:
   for this scenario the step copies the base body's cell indices and offsets immediately before `K-anchor` into
   an audit slab, latched at the first step of a control tick at which a cell index changes; the host reads the
   latched copy and the post-anchor values at that tick's fence and compares the represented coordinate 8·c + o
   in exact arithmetic (N21b-37's `coord_exact`). Required: equal at every crossing (`anchor_inexact_shifts = 0`),
   the audit's crossing count equal to the number of changes of the base body's cell index over the horizon (no
   crossing missed), at least 200 crossings (DESIGN: 250 is the most a walk at exactly 1 m/s can make, so a gait a
   little slower than its command must not fail the measure; fewer than 200 means the world did not walk, and the
   measure is recorded `not run` with that reason, never `pass`), and measures 1 and 2 at crossing ticks, recorded
   by the same audit, inside their thresholds.
6. *Faults.* No world is marked `Faulted`; `Degraded` worlds are counted by reason and are zero for
   `ill-conditioned` under the default impedance.

**Conditioning on devices (GATE-SPARK-18).** Run N21b-38's two harnesses on each backend: the bitwise metamorphic
test of PO-NUM-02 on 10⁴ matrices, and the Newton-step comparison on 10⁴ contact states per reference robot
sampled from the battery's own runs (with the near-rigid subset under the `stiff` profile). Record the three
clauses with measured maxima.

**Negative controls (each must fail the battery or its test).**
- *The uncentred [0, 8) shift* (seed done-condition): the conformance variant of `K-anchor` with offsets in [0, 8).
  It fails the exhaustive exactness test at o = −2⁻²⁵ (returns 8.0, moved and out of range) and fails the battery's
  cell-crossing measure in the walking world (the audit counts inexact shifts: for almost every small δ the sum
  8 − δ is rounded to the spacing 2⁻²¹ m just below 8).
- *Absolute `f32` positions without anchoring*: a conformance configuration that stores world positions as plain
  `f32`. At 1,000 m the spacing is 2⁻¹⁴ m (61 µm); contact violation leaves its envelope and translation invariance
  fails.
- *Normalization removed*: `K-normalize` skipped; group-membership error grows past 4 ulp within the horizon.
- *Padding with +0.0 in the energy sums*: changes ledger bits across capacities (caught by the determinism lane).
- *Scaling by 10 instead of a power of two*: breaks PO-NUM-02 (in N21b-38).

**If a measure fails on the real configuration.** BET-08's fallback applies and is recorded, not improvised:
the failing quantity gets a double-float accumulator (for example a positional accumulator or a longer-lived sum);
if it still fails it is evaluated on `batch-cpu` and every result that depends on it is labelled. The gate is then
recorded as failed for the `f32` path with the fallback in force.

**Gate records.** One hash-named receipt per backend for GATE-SPARK-05 (running maxima of each measure against its
threshold, digests of the translation pairs; the energy clause `not run` until N21bp2-01 measures it and records
the gate) and for GATE-SPARK-18 (matrices tested and differing; maxima of
ε/(κ₂·u); the near-rigid maximum; count of κ̂ > κ₂), with commit, fingerprint digest and seed; registry rows entered
by a recording session that did not implement the work (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate --
--gate GATE-SPARK-18 --receipt <hash>`; GATE-SPARK-05 is recorded the same way by N21bp2-01, once its last clause
has a measured value).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SPARK-05, the clauses this bead measures, on each of `batch-cpu` (`f32`), CUDA and Metal: over 10⁶ steps, group-membership error at most 4 ulp; closure and contact violations inside the envelopes fixed from the `f64` arm (`envelopes.toml`, written and hashed before the first `f32` run); at translations of 0, 13, 63 and 125 cells on each horizontal axis (coordinates up to 1,000 m, 1,414 m from the origin). The energy-ledger residual clause (under 10⁻⁴ relative) is defined here, recorded `not run` by this bead, and measured by N21bp2-01, which records the gate.
- [ ] Translation by whole cells is bit-identical: the digests of the 1,000 m run equal those of the origin run at every chunk, on every backend.
- [ ] The walking-through-cells world crosses at least 200 cells, and the audit counts every change of the base body's cell index; the conformance audit reports `anchor_inexact_shifts = 0`, and measures 1 and 2 at crossing ticks stay inside their thresholds.
- [ ] GATE-SPARK-18 on each backend: PO-NUM-02 bit for bit on 10⁴ matrices; ε ≤ 10·κ₂·u on 10⁴ contact states per reference robot; ε ≤ 10⁻³ on near-rigid states under `stiff`; κ̂ ≤ κ₂ for every state.
- [ ] The uncentred [0, 8) shift fails the battery (seed done-condition), and each other negative control fails its measure in the same run.
- [ ] The `f64` reference arm is run on the same world addresses and its violations and energy residual are logged beside the `f32` figures.
- [ ] No `Faulted` world; `Degraded` counts by reason are reported; receipts carry measured values, commit, fingerprint digest and seed.
- [ ] If any measure fails, the recorded outcome names the quantity and the fallback applied (double-float accumulator, or evaluation on `batch-cpu` with a label).
- [ ] `spark_precision_battery.sh --stiff-refine-steps 0`, `1` and `2` each run `newton_vs_f64` with that count (logged with source `variable`), label the run `exploratory`, skip the `gate` step with that reason, and report the near-rigid ε maxima; `--stiff-refine-steps 3` stops before any step.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `group_error_ulp_metric`: ‖q‖² = 1 + 4·2⁻²³ is 4 ulp (passes); 1 + 5·2⁻²³ is 5 ulp (fails).
- `energy_residual_metric`: a synthetic ledger (labelled `synthetic`) with 10⁴ J of flow and a 0.5 J imbalance gives 5·10⁻⁵ (passes); 2 J gives 2·10⁻⁴ (fails).
- `battery_definition_hash_frozen`: the battery directory's content hash equals the registered value; a changed scenario changes it.
- `translation_pairs_share_addresses`: the translated worlds use the same world addresses, designs, policies and seeds as the origin worlds.
- `short_battery_cpu`: 10⁴ steps on `batch-cpu` `f32` against `f64` for Q: all measures inside thresholds; translation digests equal.
- `uncentred_fails_short_battery`, `absolute_f32_fails_at_1km`, `no_normalize_fails_group_error` (negative controls at 10⁵ steps).
- `spacing_matches_compendium_d2`: the `f32` spacing figures this battery relies on (2⁻²² m ≈ 0.24 µm at the edge of a centred 8 m cell, 2⁻¹⁷ m ≈ 7.6 µm at 100 m, 2⁻¹⁴ m ≈ 61 µm at 1 km) and the represented coordinate 8·c + o of the crossing measure equal the values returned by the compendium's D.2 oracle library (N12-42) for the same inputs, exactly; the oracle is an independent computation and is never regenerated by this bead.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/spark_precision_battery.sh [--tier batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal] [--steps N] [--seed N]
[--stiff-refine-steps 0|1|2]`. `--stiff-refine-steps r` is a pass-through for N21b-38's refinement-count hook: the
script exports `FROB_STIFF_REFINE_STEPS=r` to the `newton_vs_f64` step only (no product step plan reads it), every
log line of the run carries `refine_steps: r` with its source (`variable`), the run is labelled `exploratory`, and
the `gate` step is skipped with that reason, so a non-default run never feeds a gate receipt; three runs (0, 1, 2)
give the near-rigid ε table that N35-29's evidence item 3 reads. A value outside {0, 1, 2} stops the script before
any step with the harness's named error. Steps:
`anchor_exhaustive` (2²⁵ values through `K-anchor` per backend); `reference` (the `f64` arm first: per row of
`scenarios.toml`, `frob sim run <design> --task <task> --worlds 256 --seconds 2000 --tier batch-cpu --precision
f64 --seed <seed> --key … --json` under the key rule below; then `envelopes.toml` is written and its hash logged;
the script's `--steps N` sets `--seconds` to N·h); `battery` (the same rows with `--tier <t> --precision f32`);
`translation` (digest comparison of translated pairs); `crossing` (the walking world in the conformance build,
with the anchor audit of measure 5); `po_num_02` and `newton_vs_f64` (the conditioning harnesses); `controls`
(each negative control, expecting failure); `gate` (`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-SPARK-18`, and the same with the filter `GATE-SPARK-05`,
whose entry reports the energy clause `not run` until N21bp2-01; KA-796 runs under `--test g3_metamorphic`).
Every job-submitting call carries its own idempotency key (`--key
prec-<run-id>-<step>-<scenario>-<tier>-<n>-s<seed>`, `<run-id>` the script run's UTC timestamp and `<n>` the run's
index within the step, so that no two calls share a key and a second run of the script is not answered from the
first's stored envelopes; a call without one is `FROB-REF-805`, N32a-09's admission refusal from M1); the two runs of each determinism lane use distinct keys and must show
`idempotent_replay: false`, so that a digest comparison never compares a stored receipt with itself.
Logging through the STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/spark_precision_battery/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`: every
`cmd` with exact command line, exit code and wall time; the envelope's evidence block (claim known-answer, class,
tier, determinism, identities with fingerprint digest, seed); `metric` lines for the running maximum of group
error in ulp (threshold 4), the largest closure and contact violations with their envelopes, the energy residual
(threshold 10⁻⁴), translation digest pairs (threshold: equal), cells crossed, `Faulted` and `Degraded` counts, the
conditioning maxima with their thresholds; on failure the first step and world address at which a measure left its
threshold, with the snapshot paths of both arms. The `summary` counts assertions and names both gates.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-541 (precision battery over 10⁶ steps at distances up to 1 km: group-membership error within 4 ulp; closure
  and contact violations inside their envelopes; energy-ledger residual under 10⁻⁴ relative).
- KA-790 (offset −2⁻²⁵ m in a cell; control: the uncentred [0, 8) shift returns 8.0) and KA-791 (PO-NUM-01,
  exhaustive over 2²⁵ values per backend), in their device forms; KA-796 (factor of D·H·D against D times the
  factor of H, bit-identical on 10⁴ matrices per backend; control: a scaling by 10); KA-798 (H = diag(1, 10⁻⁸)
  with residual (0, 10⁻⁷): the scaled test fails); KA-799 (κ̂ never exceeds the κ₂ computed in `f64`; ε at most
  10·κ₂·u, and at most 10⁻³ on near-rigid states under `stiff`).
- KA-792 (spacing: 0.24 µm inside a cell; 7.6 µm at 100 m and 61 µm at 1 km for plain `f32`); CX-369, CX-370.
- Metamorphic relations: whole-cell translation (bit-identical); mirroring; scaling every length by 4, time by 2
  and mass by 2 reproduces significand bits where the kernels are scale-homogeneous.
- Determinism lanes: each placement twice (identical digests, class D2); `f32` devices against `f32` `batch-cpu`
  in the class the family holds; across capacities for the energy sums.

## Considerations

- Order of work: the energy ledger on batched tiers (BD-208) is a later seed bead, so it is related to this bead
  and not a dependency of it. The battery, the anchoring criteria and the conditioning criteria do not need it.
  This bead closes on the clauses it measures. The energy-ledger residual clause is measured by N21bp2-01, which
  depends on this bead and on BD-208, re-runs the battery's `f64` and `f32` arms with the ledger on and records
  GATE-SPARK-05. No acceptance criterion of this bead waits on a later bead.
- Cost: five scenarios (Q flat, Q rough, H standing, H walking, the closure fixture) × 4 translations, plus the
  crossing world, are 21 jobs of 256 worlds × 10⁶ steps, about 5.4·10⁹ world-steps per backend: about 45 minutes on
  one RTX 4090 at the interim floor of 2.0 M world-steps per second if the jobs fill the device together (a batch
  of 256 worlds alone runs below that rate), longer on Metal, and several hours for the `f64` arm on the Mac's
  CPU. The `f64` arm exceeds the default compute envelope's hour if run as one job; it is split
  into per-workload jobs, or run under an approved envelope class (OPEN-SPARK-2). Rows not run are recorded
  `not run`.
- The envelopes for closure and contact violation come from the battery's own `f64` arm (measure 2), not from
  the fidelity ledger. If the `f64` arm itself shows a violation that grows over the horizon, that is a defect of
  the step and is reported as one; it is never absorbed into the envelope.
- OPEN-NUM-3 (cell side, default 8 m) and OPEN-NUM-4 (the ill-conditioning limit and the `stiff` refinement count;
  decided by GATE-SPARK-18) are informed by this bead's measurements.
- A battery that passes at the origin and fails only at 1 km would indicate a kernel that touches absolute
  position; the translation digests localize it immediately, which is why the translated runs compare digests and
  not only violations.
- Thermal throttling on the Mac affects time, not bits; the battery asserts no timing.
- KILL-D1 does not affect this gate: every measure is either an invariant of one placement or a comparison with
  `f64` envelopes, never a bitwise comparison between device and CPU.
