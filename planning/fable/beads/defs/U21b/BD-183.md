## Background

This is the bead where "many different robots in one launch" is shown on real devices and measured. Three kinds of
variation ride on a world's row of indices: parameter variants (one topology, different masses, lengths, gains,
card and contact parameters: tier Solid, parity with incumbent practice, stated as a requirement: no kernel compile
and no graph capture per design); topology variants in one bucket (different trees inside padded capacities: tier
Frontier); and design-family buckets (shared constants by content hash: Frontier, later). The device mechanisms
are in place from the layout rules (N21b-15), the variant tables and admission (N21b-16) and the two backends
(BD-172, BD-174); the `batch-cpu` rows of the same gates are BD-169 and BD-170. This bead runs them on CUDA and
Metal and records the gate.

**GATE-SPARK-10 (Performance, M2, TARGET).** (1) Per-world parameter variants cost at most 5% against identical
worlds at 8,192 worlds. (2) 256 topology variants of one bucket run on one captured graph with zero compiles after
warm-up. (3) A mixed batch of three body plans reaches at least 0.70 of the harmonic mean of its homogeneous
rates. (4) Admitting a variant into a free slot changes no other world's digest.

**Device rows of two neighbouring gates.** GATE-KIN-06 (Parity, M2): a batch of 256 parameter variants of each
reference robot equals 256 homogeneous runs bit for bit on `batch-cpu` and on each GPU back end. GATE-PHYLUM-02
(Known answer, determinism, M2): each of 512 designs in a mixed cohort has the same digests as in a homogeneous
batch, on `batch-cpu` and per GPU back end, at three bucket sizes and under slot dispatch with 100 random
co-tenancies.

Context for the 0.70 figure. FACT (source register): one incumbent solver publishes a mixed batch of three-,
four- and five-legged bodies at 0.86 of the harmonic mean of its homogeneous rates (86 k samples/s at 2,048
environments); that solver is maximal-coordinate and documented as not deterministic. The tolerance here is 0.70
and the ambition 0.86, on one RTX 4090 at 8,192 worlds. Below 0.5 after one optimization pass KILL-BUCKET fires.
The mixed-batch clause is also the kernel-level precondition of GATE-PHYLUM-03, the head-to-head experiment that
decides whether the table-driven cohort stays the engine.

## Scope

In: the measurement harness for the four clauses on each device family; the device rows of GATE-KIN-06 and
GATE-PHYLUM-02; the end-to-end script; the gate receipts; the figures handed to the KILL-BUCKET decision.

Out: the mechanisms (N21b-15, N21b-16, BD-169, BD-170); the performance harness itself (BD-216: paired lower
bound, unit ordering, disclosure block), which this bead uses; the batch-composition invariance gate GATE-SPARK-15
(BD-181); the head-to-head with its four arms (BD-186); edit-to-first-step latency (BD-184); optimization passes
(BD-217).

## Technical approach

**Workloads.** Reference quadruped Q (18 degrees of freedom, 13 bodies, C = 32, R = 160, h = 2 ms, four
warm-started Newton iterations, decimation 10) and reference humanoid H (35, 30, C = 64, R = 320). The three body
plans of the mixed batch are fixed in the M2 workload registry before any run (DESIGN default: the reference
quadruped at 18 degrees of freedom, the wheel-leg quadruped at 22 and the hexapod at 24, in equal shares in the 24
bucket; OPEN-SPARK-3's decision, N21b-18, freezes the bucket set and capacities first). The 256 topology variants
and the 512 designs come from the Phylum pilot's generated corpus.

**Clause 1: parameter variants cost at most 5%.** Arm A: 8,192 identical worlds of Q. Arm B: 8,192 worlds of Q
with a distinct parameter row per world (masses, lengths, gains, card and contact parameters drawn by address from
card uncertainty). Same addresses, same step count, physics only, decimation 10. Measure world-steps per second in
both arms as a paired ratio B/A over at least ten pairs run back to back in randomized order on the same host
state. The admitted figure is the one-sided 95% lower bound exp(mean − t·s/√n) of the log ratios (t = 1.833 for
n = 10). Pass: lower bound at least 0.95 (ambition 0.98). No kernel compile and no graph capture may occur between
arms beyond the first warm-up (counters asserted).

**Clause 2: 256 topology variants on one captured graph.** Create one bucket with 256 model variants; warm up;
then run 10⁴ control intervals. Assert `graph_captures == 1` (CUDA; on Metal: one step plan, no re-plan),
`compiler_invocations == 0` after warm-up, and no `Faulted` world.

**Clause 3: mixed batch against the harmonic mean.** Homogeneous arms: each of the three body plans alone at 8,192
worlds in the same bucket capacity it would use alone, giving rates R₁, R₂, R₃ (world-steps/s). Mixed arm: 8,192
worlds with shares f₁, f₂, f₃ (equal thirds) in one bucket, rate R_mix. The harmonic mean for the mix is
H = 1 / (f₁/R₁ + f₂/R₂ + f₃/R₃), the rate of running the same worlds homogeneously one plan after another. The
figure is R_mix / H as a paired lower bound over at least ten interleaved repetitions. Pass: at least 0.70. The
padding fraction, bucket capacity and variants per bucket go into the disclosure block.

**Clause 4: admission changes no other digest.** As N21b-16's test, at gate scale: 8,192 worlds over 255 variants;
admit a 256th between chunks at a seeded chunk index; compare every other world's per-chunk digests over the
following 10³ chunks with a run without the admission. Pass: all equal.

**GATE-KIN-06 device rows.** For each reference robot: one batch of 256 parameter variants (one world each, or 32
each to fill runs) against 256 homogeneous runs of the same worlds; per-world digests bit-identical on each GPU
back end. Negative control (the gate's own): a parameter row read after its first use in the step.

**GATE-PHYLUM-02 device rows.** 512 designs in mixed cohorts at three bucket sizes (the registered capacities that
hold them), each world's digests compared with the same world in a homogeneous batch of its own model, per GPU back
end; then the same under 100 random co-tenancies (random assignment of other admitted worlds to the remaining
slots, changed at masked resets by rewriting `WorldRow`s, which is the environment-level form of slot dispatch).
Negative control: a reduction tree shaped by capacity.

**Statistics and disclosure.** Every performance figure carries the full disclosure block (hardware and driver;
backend, compiler and kernel versions; world count; time step, substeps and decimation; solver iterations and
tolerance; contact and row capacity and mean active contacts; sensors; whether policy and learning are included;
scene; determinism class; warm-up and duration; whether launch graphs were captured; median and 99th-percentile
step time; peak memory; the unit of every rate; `Degraded` and `Faulted` counts; profile and precision profile;
fingerprint digest; placement receipt; cold or warm; for a mixed batch the buckets, variants per bucket and padding
fraction; thermal state and soak; host load; number of pairs, the lower bound and the comparator's configuration).
On this Mac a 20-minute sustained load precedes measurement; on `trj` the host load is recorded. A figure without
the block is refused (`FROB-REF-862`); a ratio without a paired lower bound is refused (`FROB-REF-863`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SPARK-10 clause 1: the paired lower bound of the parameter-variant ratio at 8,192 worlds of Q is at least 0.95 on one RTX 4090 (and reported on Metal), with zero compiles and zero captures between arms.
- [ ] Clause 2: 256 topology variants run 10⁴ control intervals with `graph_captures == 1` and `compiler_invocations == 0` after warm-up.
- [ ] Clause 3: the mixed batch of three body plans reaches a paired lower bound of at least 0.70 of the harmonic mean of its homogeneous rates on one RTX 4090 at 8,192 worlds; the measured value is recorded whatever it is and handed to the KILL-BUCKET decision (below 0.5 after one optimization pass is its trigger).
- [ ] Clause 4: admitting a variant changes no other world's digest over 10³ chunks.
- [ ] GATE-KIN-06 device rows: 256 parameter variants equal 256 homogeneous runs bit for bit on CUDA and on Metal, for Q and H; the late-read negative control is caught.
- [ ] GATE-PHYLUM-02 device rows: 512 designs, three bucket sizes, 100 random co-tenancies: every world's digests equal its homogeneous run per GPU back end; the capacity-shaped reduction control is caught.
- [ ] Every figure has its disclosure block and its paired lower bound; receipts name commit, fingerprint digest and seed; the gate rows are entered by a recording session that did not implement the work.
- [ ] If a device family is unavailable or a measurement exceeds the approved envelope, that row is recorded `not run` with the reason, never estimated.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `harmonic_mean_known_answer`: rates 3.0 M, 2.0 M and 1.5 M world-steps/s in equal shares give H = 1/((1/3)(1/3.0 + 1/2.0 + 1/1.5)) = 2.0 M; a mixed rate of 1.4 M is 0.70, exactly at tolerance; 1.0 M is 0.50.
- `paired_bound_known_answer`: ten pairs of constant ratio 0.97 give a bound of 0.97; log ratios with mean ln 0.96 and standard deviation 0.02 give exp(ln 0.96 − 1.833·0.02/√10) = 0.949, which fails 0.95 although the mean passes.
- `parameter_variants_bit_equal_small`: 32 parameter variants against 32 homogeneous runs on `batch-cpu` and on each device, 1,000 steps.
- `mixed_cohort_equals_homogeneous_small`: 16 designs, two bucket sizes.
- `cotenancy_rewrite_is_address_keyed`: rewriting `WorldRow`s at masked resets changes no continuing world's digest.
- `counters_between_arms`: no compile and no capture when switching between arms of clause 1.
- `late_parameter_read_detected` and `capacity_shaped_reduction_detected` (negative controls).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/spark_gate10_variants.sh [--tier gpu-cuda|gpu-metal] [--pairs N] [--seed N]`. Steps: `layout`
(`frob spark place --json`; run table and execution width); `param_cost` (paired `frob sim run --tier <t>
--workload Q --worlds 8192 --variants parameter|none --json`, interleaved); `topology_256`; `mixed_three`
(three homogeneous runs and the mixed run, interleaved, ten repetitions); `admit`; `kin06_device`;
`phylum02_device`; `gate` (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g5 -- GATE-SPARK-10 GATE-KIN-06
GATE-PHYLUM-02` to write the hash-named receipts). Assertions as in the acceptance criteria. Logging through the
STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/spark_gate10_variants/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`: every `cmd` with exact command
line, exit code, wall time; the envelope's evidence block (claim performance or known-answer, class, tier,
determinism, identities, seed); `metric` lines for each rate with unit and decimation, each per-pair ratio, the
lower bound with its threshold (0.95; 0.70), the counters with threshold 0 or 1, padding fraction, thermal state
and host load; digests where state is compared; on failure the first differing world address, step and both digest
values, and the snapshot paths. The `summary` lists assertions and the three gates touched.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-545 (admission; 256 topology variants on one captured graph). KA-347 (a batch of P parameter variants of one
  tree equals P homogeneous runs bit for bit; negative control: the parameter row read after its first use in the
  step). KA-344 (padding and co-tenancy: identical digests; negative control: a reduction tree shaped by
  capacity).
- Metamorphic relations: MR-01 (batch-composition invariance) and MR-02 (P parameter variants in one batch equal P
  homogeneous runs; a world of a mixed cohort equals the same world in a homogeneous batch).
- Negative controls for the performance clauses: an A/A run (both arms identical) must give a lower bound that does
  not clear 1.02; a mean substituted for the lower bound must be caught by `paired_bound_known_answer`.
- Determinism lanes: each device twice; one card against two shards; devices against `batch-cpu` in the class the
  family holds.

## Considerations

- KILL-BUCKET (N21b-19): trigger: more than eight buckets needed to hold Ω₃ ≤ 2 on the reference family corpus, or
  this bead's mixed batch below 0.5 of the harmonic mean after one optimization pass. Fallback: at most nine padded
  per-family templates, each a homogeneous environment, scheduled in turn; parameter variants are unaffected.
  KILL-PHYLUM (the Phylum chapter's decision) governs where the two differ.
- OPEN-SPARK-2 (the compute envelope; owner; default one device, one hour, 16 GiB, 8 cores): clause 3 with ten
  interleaved repetitions of four runs fits the default on one card; the full GATE-PHYLUM-02 matrix on both
  machines may need envelope class E1, and rows that do not fit are recorded `not run`.
- OPEN-SPARK-1: until the annex design is ratified, device results are exploratory and no Performance or Parity
  claim rests on them; the gate values are still recorded.
- Masked lanes that exit early pay less than the padding bound, which is why the mixed batch can beat Ω-based
  pessimism; the measurement, not the bound, decides.
- The ratio in clause 1 is expected near 1 because parameter rows cost bytes, not branches; if it fails, the first
  suspect is an extra indirection per access, visible as bytes touched per world-step in the profile.
- `trj` is often heavily loaded (FACT): pairs are interleaved and the load is recorded; a run with load above the
  harness's limit is discarded by rule, not by judgment.
