## Background

A kill gate is a decision the plan commits to in advance: a trigger with a number, the milestone at which it is
decided, the measurement that decides it, and a fallback named beforehand. KILL-METAL governs the Metal backend as
a rollout tier (tier Frontier). It protects the program from carrying a GPU tier on this Mac that is too slow to
train on or cannot be replayed.

**Trigger.** GATE-SPARK-04 fails after one optimization pass, or the Metal backend cannot hold run-to-run
determinism. GATE-SPARK-04 (Performance, M2, TARGET): Metal throughput at least 25% of one RTX 4090 at matched
settings on this M5 Ultra (BET-09), reported beside an incumbent with a Metal path measured on the same machine.
As with every performance ratio in the project, the threshold applies to the one-sided 95% lower confidence bound
of a paired ratio, never to a mean.

**Fallback.** Metal becomes a development and replay tier (single-device debugging, small batches, the viewer);
CUDA is the training tier; the Mac trains on `batch-cpu` using its 36 CPU cores. The Mac keeps the world bank,
proof and symbolic work, replay and the referee.

**Milestone.** M2; recorded as an architecture decision record with the other M2 kill decisions at the M2 exit
review.

This bead is the decision itself: it gathers the two measurements, applies the rule, records the outcome, and
states what changes downstream on each branch.

## Scope

In: checking that the inputs exist and are valid; applying the trigger; writing the ADR; updating the registers
(gate status, mechanism tier, the honest-status table); listing the downstream consequences.

Out: measuring GATE-SPARK-04 (BD-217, which records it pass or fail with the disclosure block); the determinism
lanes (N21b-39); building the backend (BD-174); the owner's answer on a Spark-owned Metal binding (N21b-09, an
input here).

## Technical approach

**Inputs required before the decision may be recorded.**
1. From BD-217: row 12 of the performance targets (reference quadruped Q, physics only, `gpu-metal`, this M5 Ultra,
   as a ratio to row 1 measured on one RTX 4090, decimation 10) as a paired lower bound from at least ten pairs
   (bound = exp(mean of the log ratios − t·s/√n), t the 95th percentile of Student's distribution with n − 1 degrees
   of freedom, 1.833 for n = 10), with the complete disclosure block, after a 20-minute sustained load on the Mac;
   the same quantity for an incumbent with a Metal path measured on this machine; and a statement of whether the
   one permitted optimization pass has been spent, with its ceiling computed beforehand.
2. From N21b-39: run-to-run digest equality on Metal over the determinism lanes, pass or fail, with the first
   divergence if it failed.
3. From N21b-04: the pilot's prediction and binding term, for comparison with the measurement.
4. From N21b-09: the owner's decision on OPEN-SPARK-5 (default: no Spark-owned binding).

**Decision rule (enumerated before the data are read).**

| GATE-SPARK-04 lower bound | Optimization pass spent | Run-to-run determinism | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≥ 0.25 | either | holds | `holds`: Metal is a rollout tier |
| < 0.25 | no | holds | `pending`: spend the one pass on the term the profile names, re-measure once, return to this table |
| < 0.25 | yes | holds | `fires` on throughput |
| any | either | does not hold | `fires` on determinism (a repair of a specific defect that restores determinism is allowed before the M2 exit; an unexplained difference is not waived) |

A measurement that could not be made (no envelope approved for it, device unavailable) leaves the gate
`not-run`; the kill gate then cannot be recorded as `holds`, and the tier stays labelled Frontier-unproven with
the fallback in force for planning purposes.

**If it fires.** Record `killed` for GATE-SPARK-04 naming KILL-METAL and the fallback now in force. Downstream:
- the role table of the two machines changes its training row for the Mac from "small and medium runs on
  `gpu-metal`" to "`batch-cpu` on 36 cores"; the fleet scheduler's resource model drops Metal as a training
  placement (it stays a replay and evaluation placement for small batches);
- row 12 of the performance targets is reported as measured and withdrawn as a tolerance; the published wording of
  the Metal tier becomes "development and replay tier";
- Metal rows of training gates are recorded `not run: KILL-METAL`; determinism and replay gates on Metal still
  apply, because a replay tier must replay;
- the Metal backend, its commit fence, leases, snapshots and the CPU-versus-device check remain supported;
- FD-FT-01's priority is re-read: if the pilot and the profile both name gateway launch cost as the binding term,
  the upstream work is the route to re-open the tier, and the ADR says what measurement would re-open it.

**If it holds.** Record the measured bound; the Mac's training role stands; BET-09 is recorded as supported at the
measured value; the stretch ambition (0.60) is reported as met or not.

**Record.** `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-METAL.md`: inputs with their receipts, the table row that applied, the outcome, the
consequences taken, and the reviewer. The gate registry row for KILL-METAL is entered by a recording session that
did not implement the backend.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists and cites the receipts of GATE-SPARK-04 (paired lower bound, pairs, disclosure block, incumbent Metal-path figure) and of the determinism lanes.
- [ ] The outcome is one of `holds`, `fires on throughput`, `fires on determinism`, or `not-run` with the reason; `pending` is not a final state at M2 exit.
- [ ] If the outcome is `fires`, every downstream item listed above is updated in the same change set (role table, target row 12, gate statuses, status table), and a search for "rollout tier" finds no remaining claim for Metal.
- [ ] If the outcome is `holds`, the measured bound is in the gate registry with the fingerprint digest and commit.
- [ ] The decision rule table in the ADR is the one above, unchanged; a deviation is itself recorded as a decision with its reason.
- [ ] The M2 exit review (a fresh-context session) signs the record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `kill_metal_rule_enumeration`: a table-driven test of the decision function over all combinations of (bound ≥ 0.25 or not, pass spent or not, determinism holds or not, measurement present or not) returns the outcomes above; every outcome is reachable.
- `paired_lower_bound_known_answer`: ten pairs with constant ratio r give a bound of exactly r; ten log ratios with mean ln 0.30 and standard deviation 0.10 give exp(ln 0.30 − 1.833·0.10/√10) = 0.283, which passes; mean ln 0.26 with the same spread gives 0.245, which fails although the mean passes.
- `registry_refuses_pass_without_controls`: the gate registry refuses to record `pass` for GATE-SPARK-04 when its negative controls were not caught in the same run.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_metal_decision.sh`: reads the stored receipts of BD-217 and N21b-39 (`frob artifact read`),
recomputes the paired lower bound from the per-pair figures, applies the rule, and prints the outcome; it fails if
the recomputed bound differs from the recorded one or if a required input is missing. Logged with the STD-E2E
helper: each input's identifier and digest, the per-pair ratios, the bound and its threshold 0.25, the determinism
verdict, the rule row applied, and the outcome; the `summary` names KILL-METAL and GATE-SPARK-04.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the two paired-bound cases above (0.283 passes; 0.245 fails).
- Negative controls: a mean used in place of the lower bound must change the second case's verdict and be caught;
  a decision recorded with the determinism input missing must be refused.

## Considerations

- The plan charges the fallback honestly elsewhere: M2 also has a no-GPU variant, and "BET-09: Metal is not a
  rollout tier" has a pre-written consequence (CUDA trains; the Mac keeps the world bank, proof, replay and
  `batch-cpu`).
- The fleet ceiling does not depend on this outcome: adding the Mac's GPU to training adds at most its share of
  the pair of cards (0.125 at the 25% threshold, 0.30 at 60%), so a fleet's gain can only come from moving
  non-training work off the training cards.
- RISK-08 and row 14 of the pre-mortem ("the Metal gateway's limits throttled the Mac tier") describe the failure
  this gate exists to catch early; the pilot N21b-04 is its M0 signal.
- OPEN-SPARK-2: if no compute envelope beyond the default is approved, GATE-SPARK-04 may be `not run`; the default
  (one device, one hour) is expected to admit the measurement, since it is one device for minutes.
