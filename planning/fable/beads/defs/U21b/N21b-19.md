## Background

Topology variants in one bucket and design-family buckets are tier Frontier. Their kill gate, stated in advance:

**KILL-BUCKET.** Trigger: on the reference family corpus more than eight buckets are needed to hold Ω₃ ≤ 2, or the
mixed batch of GATE-SPARK-10 runs below 0.5 of the harmonic mean of its homogeneous rates after one optimization
pass. Fallback: at most nine padded per-family templates, each a homogeneous environment, scheduled in turn;
parameter variants are unaffected. Milestone: M2.

Here Ω₃ = L_max³ / E[L³] is the padding overhead of a bucket on cubic-cost stages (L is a robot's degree-of-freedom
count, L_max the bucket's capacity): the factor by which a dense factorization on padded worlds exceeds the unpadded
work. The harmonic mean of the homogeneous rates is the rate of running the same worlds one body plan after
another, each alone.

**Who owns the decision.** The Phylum chapter owns the one decision on the mixed batch: KILL-PHYLUM governs where
the two differ, KILL-BUCKET is its device-side statement and defers to it, and the mixed-batch clause of
GATE-SPARK-10 is the kernel-level precondition of GATE-PHYLUM-03. KILL-PHYLUM's trigger: GATE-PHYLUM-03 fails:
below 0.5 of the harmonic mean of the homogeneous rates on 512 designs, more than eight buckets, or the
per-family-template arm covers at least 95% of the mutation corpus and is faster. Its fallback: per-family
templates become the engine; cohorts are formed by exact tree with per-design parameter tables; the table-driven
path remains for admission and preview on `batch-cpu`. The experiment that decides both, with the per-family
template control arm, is the head-to-head (BD-186), whose done-condition is that KILL-PHYLUM and KILL-BUCKET are
decided by the pre-declared outcomes.

This bead is the device-side decision record: it takes the two Spark measurements and the head-to-head's outcome,
applies the rule, and states what changes in Spark on each branch.

## Scope

In: verifying the inputs; applying the trigger; recording the ADR and the gate status; listing the Spark-side
consequences of each branch; marking the beads that exist only on one branch.

Out: the measurements (BD-171 for the bucket count; BD-183 for the mixed-batch ratio; BD-186 for the head-to-head
and KILL-PHYLUM); the per-family template arm itself (BD-185); any optimization pass (BD-217).

## Technical approach

**Inputs required.**
1. From BD-171 (`phylum.plan` on the reference family corpus): the smallest number of buckets B* for which every
   bucket has Ω₃ ≤ 2, with the corpus identity and the bucket boundaries.
2. From BD-183 (GATE-SPARK-10, clause 3): the paired lower bound of the mixed batch of three body plans over the
   harmonic mean of its homogeneous rates, on one RTX 4090 at 8,192 worlds, with its disclosure block, and whether
   the one permitted optimization pass has been spent (with its ceiling computed beforehand).
3. From BD-186: the published outcome of the head-to-head with all four arms and the KILL-PHYLUM verdict.

**Decision rule (enumerated before the data are read).**

| B* | Mixed-batch lower bound | Optimization pass spent | KILL-PHYLUM | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ 8 | ≥ 0.5 | either | not fired | `holds`: mixed-topology buckets stay on devices |
| > 8 | any | either | any | `fires` on bucket count |
| ≤ 8 | < 0.5 | no | not fired | `pending`: spend the one pass, re-measure once |
| ≤ 8 | < 0.5 | no | fired | `fires by deference`: KILL-PHYLUM governs; the pass is not spent on a mechanism already killed |
| ≤ 8 | < 0.5 | yes | any | `fires` on throughput |
| ≤ 8 | ≥ 0.5 | either | fired | `fires by deference`: KILL-PHYLUM governs |

The rows are exhaustive and disjoint: every combination of the four inputs matches exactly one row. (An earlier
draft left B* ≤ 8, bound < 0.5, pass not spent, KILL-PHYLUM fired without a row.)

Note the two thresholds on the mixed batch: 0.70 is the gate's tolerance (GATE-SPARK-10 passes or fails on it) and
0.5 is the kill trigger. Between 0.5 and 0.70 the gate is recorded as failed, the mechanism is not killed, and the
defect is carried with a disclosure on every claim that rests on the mixed batch.

**If it fires (Spark-side consequences).**
- Device buckets hold one tree each: at most nine padded per-family templates (one per family of the archetypes
  that share a maximal tree; each a fixed maximal tree with lockable joints and per-design parameter tables), each
  a homogeneous environment with its own arena, kernel set and launch graph, scheduled in turn. The control arm of
  the head-to-head (BD-185) is the implementation that becomes the engine.
- Parameter variants are unaffected: they are tier Solid and stay.
- Topology-variant tables and admission on devices (N21b-16) are withdrawn from the device tiers and remain on
  `batch-cpu` for admission and preview; design-family buckets (N21b-17) are not built.
- `phylum.plan` reports templates instead of size buckets for device placement; the planner's size-bucket path
  stays for `batch-cpu`.
- Gates: GATE-SPARK-10 is recorded `killed` for clauses 2 and 3 (clause 1 and the digest clause still apply to
  templates); the status table and the published wording drop mixed-topology batching on devices.
- Edit-to-first-step on devices then needs a template that covers the edit; an edit outside every template takes
  the cool or cold path, and the share of the mutation corpus the templates can express is reported.

**If it holds.** The measured B* and ratio are recorded; N21b-17 is unblocked for M4; the status table states the
mixed-batch figure with its disclosure.

**Record.** `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-BUCKET.md` with inputs, the table row that applied, the outcome and the
consequences; the gate registry row is entered by a recording session that did not implement the mechanisms, at
the M2 exit review with the other kill decisions.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR cites the three inputs with their receipts and states the outcome as one of `holds`, `fires on bucket count`, `fires on throughput`, `fires by deference`; `pending` is not a final state at M2 exit.
- [ ] The outcome agrees with KILL-PHYLUM's recorded verdict (if KILL-PHYLUM fired, this record says `fires by deference` or one of its own triggers; it never says `holds`).
- [ ] If the outcome is a `fires` variant, the consequences listed above are applied in the same change set and the beads that exist only on the `holds` branch (N21b-17; device use of N21b-16) are closed as `killed: KILL-BUCKET` with this ADR as the reason.
- [ ] If the outcome is `holds`, the measured values are in the gate registry with commit, fingerprint digest and seed.
- [ ] The decision table in the ADR is the one above, unchanged.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `kill_bucket_rule_enumeration`: a table-driven test of the decision function over B* ∈ {8, 9}, bound ∈ {0.45, 0.60, 0.75}, pass spent or not, KILL-PHYLUM fired or not (24 combinations); every combination matches exactly one row, every outcome in the table is reachable, and no combination returns `holds` when KILL-PHYLUM fired.
- `omega3_bucket_count_known_answer`: a ratio-1.25 bucket set caps Ω₃ at 1.25³ = 1.95 ≤ 2 and needs eight buckets to span 12 to 66 degrees of freedom; a ratio-1.3 set caps it at 2.197 > 2.
- `harmonic_mean_ratio_known_answer`: rates 3.0 M, 2.0 M and 1.5 M in equal shares give a harmonic mean of 2.0 M; a mixed rate of 0.9 M is 0.45 (fires after the pass), 1.2 M is 0.60 (gate fails, not killed), 1.5 M is 0.75 (passes).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_bucket_decision.sh`: reads the stored receipts of BD-171, BD-183 and BD-186
(`frob artifact read`), recomputes B* by re-running `frob phylum plan` on the recorded corpus identity and the
ratio from the per-pair figures, applies the rule and prints the outcome; fails if a recomputed value differs from
the recorded one or an input is missing. Logged per the STD-E2E helper: input identifiers and digests, B* against
the limit 8, the lower bound against 0.5 and 0.70, KILL-PHYLUM's verdict, the rule row applied and the outcome;
`summary` names KILL-BUCKET.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers as in the unit tests.
- Negative controls: a decision recorded as `holds` while KILL-PHYLUM is `fired` must be refused by the consistency
  check; a mean used in place of the paired lower bound must be caught.

## Considerations

- The plan committed in advance to adopt the per-family-template arm if it wins (it is the incumbent regime plus
  per-design rows); this decision must not be softened after the data are in.
- What is lost if it fires: mixed-topology populations in one launch on devices and the warm admission path for
  arbitrary trees. What remains: replay of one world outside its batch, deterministic forks, parameter variants,
  the Metal tier, and admission and preview of arbitrary trees on `batch-cpu`.
- The compute envelope for the full head-to-head (512 topologies, four arms) is an owner question (OPEN-PHYLUM-3);
  under the default envelope KILL-PHYLUM is decided on the pilot, and this record then cites the pilot-scale
  figures and says so.
- A bucket count above eight on a corpus dominated by one outlier hybrid is a property of the corpus; the
  planner's refusal `FROB-REF-741` (stratify the generator; raise the waste limit knowingly; split the campaign)
  is the per-population answer, and it does not by itself fire the kill gate unless the reference family corpus
  shows it.
