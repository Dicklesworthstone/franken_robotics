## Background

GATE-SPARK-02 (Fidelity, M2, TARGET): every M2 kernel and the full step agree between `batch-cpu` (`f32`) and each
device inside the declared class: bit-equal for class D1; for class D3 at most 4 ulp per kernel output,
statistical equivalence of 1,000-step rollouts, and the decision-level test. It is the gate behind every citation
of a device result (no device result is citable without a current CPU-versus-device check) and behind the only
claim Spark makes about its kernel toolchain: arithmetic derived once, emitted for a CPU reference, CUDA and Metal,
with the measured cross-backend disagreement stored per kernel.

`frob spark check` is the user-visible capability; this bead is its end-to-end script at M2 scale and the place
where the gate is assembled for both device families. The mechanisms: the M2 check and currency (BD-176), sweeps
and localization (N21b-33), the decision-level equivalence test (BD-177), on top of the M0 comparator and ledger
(N21b-32). Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the script, its steps and assertions, planted divergences, the structured log, and the gate receipts per
device family.

Out: mechanisms; the M0 gate GATE-SPARK-00 (BD-020's own script); the KILL-D1 decision (N21b-34), which reads this
script's results.

## Technical approach

**Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/spark_check_gate02.sh [--backend gpu-cuda[:n]|gpu-metal] [--seed N] [--worlds N]`.
Defaults: each device of the host; seed 1; 4,096 worlds per reference workload for step and rollout levels.
Conformance build (for planted divergences and the fingerprint override). Idempotency keys from seed and step.

**Steps and assertions.**

| Step | Commands | Assertions |
|---|---|---|
| `class` | `frob spark probe --suite ieee --device <d> --json` (stored report) | The family's attainable class is read and fixes which form of the gate applies (D1 or D3); if no full probe report exists the script stops: the gate cannot be assembled without it |
| `kernels` | `frob spark check --all --backend <b> --json` | Every kernel in the registry has a result; exact and guarded kernels byte-equal; float kernels byte-equal (D1 family) or within 4 ulp with no NaN or infinity mismatch (D3 family); each result has a ledger row id |
| `step` | `frob spark check --step --backend <b> --workload Q --worlds 4096 --json`; the same for H | Per-stage outputs and end-of-step state agree inside the class |
| `rollout` | `frob spark check --rollout 1000 --backend <b> --workload Q --worlds 4096 --json`; the same for H | D1 family: per-step digests equal for all 1,000 steps. D3 family: continuous outcomes equivalent by two one-sided tests inside the fidelity ledger's margins; the decision-level report is `equivalent` (upper bound on discordant decisions at most 1%; net one-sided bound at most 0.5%; rankings agree at the top) |
| `sweeps` | `frob spark check --all --sweeps all --backend <b> --json` | Every axis (batch size, slot, capacity, co-tenants, shards) passes for every kernel and for the step |
| `currency` | `frob spark place --tier strict:<b> --workload Q --worlds 1024 --require-current-check --json` | Clean plan: every kernel `current` |
| `stale` | The same after the conformance fingerprint override changes the driver string | `FROB-REF-605`; then `frob spark check --all` again restores currency under the new key |
| `plant_kernel` | `spark check --kernel <k>` against the conformance twin with one constant changed by one unit in the last place | Exit 2; non-zero bin 1; worst case names the lane; `--localize` reports the element |
| `plant_step` | `spark check --step` with one stage's device kernel replaced by a reassociated variant | Exit 2; localization names that stage and kernel |
| `plant_decision` | `spark check --rollout 1000` with a planted bias of one decision in fifty | The decision-level test fails (a bias of 2% exceeds the 1% bound at 4,096 worlds) |
| `plant_states_only` | The 1.0000001 m against 0.9999999 m case on a one-world fixture | Reported as discordant and `boundary-sensitive` although the state error is 2·10⁻⁷ m |
| `gate` | `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g5 -- GATE-SPARK-02` over the result file | One hash-named receipt per device family; registry rows entered by a recording session that did not implement the work |

**Pass rule per device family.** In the D1 form: zero differing bytes across `kernels`, `step` and `rollout`. In
the D3 form: every float kernel within 4 ulp, the equivalence tests pass, and the decision-level test passes. In
both forms: exact and guarded kernels byte-equal, sweeps pass, and all planted divergences are caught in the same
run. The receipt states which form applied and why.

**Logging.** The script sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/spark_check_gate02/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`. `cmd` lines: exact command line, exit code, wall time. Result lines carry the `evidence` block
(claim fidelity, class, tier, determinism class attained, identities with fingerprint digest, seed). `metric`
lines: per kernel the class claimed and attained, the largest ulp distance with its threshold (0 or 4) and the
histogram; for rollouts the equivalence statistics with margins, the discordant count, the upper bounds with their
thresholds (1% and 0.5%); sweep results per axis; ledger row ids; currency before and after. On failure: the first
differing value located as step, stage, kernel and quantity with both bit patterns, and the artifact paths needed
to reproduce (captured inputs, snapshots, the two runs' identifiers). The `summary` gives assertion counts and the
gate touched, per device family.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script completes on this Mac (Metal) and on `trj` (each RTX 4090), exits 0 when the gate's form for the family is met, and non-zero otherwise, with a log that validates against the STD-E2E schema.
- [ ] GATE-SPARK-02 is recorded per device family with measured values and the form that applied (D1 or D3).
- [ ] All four planted divergences are caught in the same run that passes the real comparison.
- [ ] Currency behaves as specified across the fingerprint override.
- [ ] The result file is sufficient for the KILL-D1 decision: per family, the list of float kernels with class attained and first divergence.
- [ ] Rows that cannot be run (a family absent; the D3 rollout exceeding the approved envelope) are recorded `not run` with the reason, never estimated.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `e2e_log_schema_gate02`.
- `gate_form_follows_probe`: with a stored probe report of class D1 the script asserts byte equality; with D2/D3 it asserts the tolerance form; with none it refuses to assemble the gate.
- `result_file_feeds_kill_d1`: the decision script of N21b-34 parses it.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. A reduced mode compares two `batch-cpu` instances (`f32` against `f32` through two code
paths, and `f32` against `f64` to exercise the D3 form with a real tolerance) with 10⁴ lanes and 256 worlds; it
runs in continuous integration and records no gate.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-525 (matches the Rust reference inside its determinism class on all backends: bit-equal for D1; at most 4 ulp
  per kernel output for D3).
- The decision-level known answer (1.0000001 m against 0.9999999 m: discordant and `boundary-sensitive`; negative
  control: a check that compares states only) and GATE-SPARK-16's control (a planted bias of one decision in fifty
  must fail).
- Negative controls: the one-ulp kernel twin; the reassociated stage kernel; a comparator that ignores NaN
  mismatches (conformance variant; must fail `kernels` on a fixture lane with a planted NaN).
- Determinism lanes: the script's ledger row ids are identical on repeat; device against `batch-cpu` in the class
  the family holds; `batch-cpu` across worker counts.

## Considerations

- The D3 rollout on 4,096 worlds of H needs a `batch-cpu` reference of 4.1·10⁶ world-steps in `f32`; at the
  planning rate for the Mac's CPU that is under a minute, and on `trj`'s loaded CPU it may be several; both fit the
  default envelope.
- The statistical-equivalence margins come from the fidelity ledger's frozen envelopes; until that ledger is frozen
  the D3 form of the gate cannot be recorded as passed, only measured.
- OPEN-NUM-2 (thresholds 1% and 0.5% and the ensemble size) is frozen once at M2 with a written rationale; the
  script reads the thresholds from the registry.
- A family that fails the gate's D1 form but meets its D3 form has passed the gate in the D3 form and fired
  KILL-D1; both facts are recorded, and neither is softened.
