## Background

A *specialized kernel* is the generic (data-driven) kernel with one design's constants folded in and its loops
unrolled. It is Frontier work with a low ceiling, stated before any test. FACT (an incumbent's own event trace of a
humanoid at 8,192 worlds): of 262 ns per world-step, the stages a design-specialized recursion could touch
(kinematics, centre-of-mass terms, composite inertia, bias forces, forward acceleration) sum to 53 ns, 20% of the
step; the solve is 124 ns and constraint construction 38 ns. An infinitely fast recursion would give 262/209 =
1.25; a recursion twice as fast gives 1.11; a manipulation scene gives a share of 16% and a ceiling of 1.19.
Generated code also does not reliably beat a good generic library. The expected gain is 1.05 to 1.15.

Decision ADR-SPARK-08 therefore says: per-design specialization is built for survivors only, **only if our own
profile justifies it**, and only bit-identical to the generic kernel. "Justifies" has a number: the M2 profile must
show a specializable share s of at least **one third** of the step, the share at which the ceiling 1/(1 − s)
reaches 1.5. The kill gate KILL-SPECIALIZE has three trigger clauses; the third is "the M2 profile shows a
specializable share under one third", and the gate register notes that this clause is read at M2 and can close the
question before any pilot is funded. This bead is that reading. The cap near 1.2 is also the kill threshold of
the pilot, so the honest expectation is that this mechanism is not built; specialization is never the default
and never part of the edit loop.

The cohort chapter states the same gate to fund from its side (a specializable share of at least one third, so
that 1/(1 − s) ≥ 1.5) and drafted the reading a second time as part of its own pilot bead (N17a-15). The device
chapter governs this kill gate, so that bead is superseded: its reading of s from the cohort calibration's stage
timers is kept here as a cross-check, and its three-arm pilot is N21c-17.

## Scope

In: the definition of the specializable set over our own kernels; the measurement of s per reference workload and
backend from the M2 profile, with its interval; the same share read a second way from the per-stage timers of the
cohort cost-model calibration, as a cross-check; the derived ceilings; the verdict of the profile clause; the
record. This is the only bead that reads s for the gate to fund and for KILL-SPECIALIZE. Out: the profile itself
(the record `frob.stepprofile/1` and its profiling mode, N21a-27, run on Q and H for the parity rows by BD-217;
N21a-27 also prints a derived share from its stage table for display, which this bead's record carries beside its
own s, and which never decides); the stage timers (N17a-06); the three-arm pilot, which takes s and its ceiling from this bead's record
(N21c-17); the decision (N21c-18).

## Technical approach

**Definitions (DESIGN, frozen before the profile is read).**
- *Whole step:* one physics world-step of the batched step plan (all stages: kinematics, dynamics terms,
  actuators, collision, constraint construction, solve, integration), excluding policy, observation and learning.
- *Specializable set:* the kernels whose work is a recursion over one design's tree with that design's constants:
  the kinematics family (`K-fk-*`, `K-twist-*`), the dynamics-term family (`K-crba-*`, `K-rnea-*`: composite
  inertia, bias forces, centre-of-mass terms) and the recursion part of forward acceleration where the step plan
  has one. Not in the set: constraint construction (`K-jac-*`, `K-cone-*`), the solve (`K-hess`, `K-scale`,
  `K-chol`, `K-linesearch`, `K-refine`), collision, integration, actuators, sensing. Sparsity in constraint
  assembly and solve is carried as data and measured separately; it is not specialization.
- s = Σ device time of the specializable set ÷ device time of the whole step, per world-step, at 8,192 worlds.
- *Interval of s:* s is computed for each timed repetition of the profile cell (the performance harness takes at
  least ten), and the record carries the mean with its 95% interval over the repetitions. A cell with fewer than
  ten repetitions cannot decide.
- *The stage-timer reading (cross-check, from the cohort chapter's side):* from the per-stage timers of the cohort
  cost-model calibration (N17a-06), s_stage = time in the tree recursions of stage 1 (link poses and twists) and
  of stage 6 (mass matrix, bias forces, and the centre-of-mass and composite-inertia terms inside them) ÷
  whole-step time, on the reference quadruped and on the calibration's mixed workload, each with its interval.

**Steps.**
1. From the M2 profile records `frob.stepprofile/1` (N21a-27: per-kernel device time in profiling mode, median
   over a stated number of steps, normalized to the unprofiled total) take per-kernel device time for workloads Q (reference quadruped: 18 degrees of freedom, 13
   bodies, contact capacity 32, row capacity 160, step 2 ms, 4 warm-started Newton iterations) and H (reference
   humanoid: 35, 30, 64, 320), on `gpu-cuda` (one RTX 4090) and on `gpu-metal`. Per-kernel timing on Metal needs one
   batch per kernel in profiling mode, because the gateway's receipt has a kernel count and total device seconds
   only; on CUDA it needs per-launch timing outside graph replay. The method and its perturbation (profiled step
   time ÷ unprofiled step time) are recorded.
2. Compute s, the ceiling 1/(1 − s) (infinitely fast recursion) and 1/(1 − s/2) (recursion twice as fast), for
   each (workload, backend).
3. Cross-check: compute s_stage for the reference quadruped and compare it with the per-kernel s of workload Q
   on the same backend. The two measure the same share by different attributions and must agree within their
   intervals; a disagreement is recorded as a finding (a mis-tagged kernel or a mis-attributed stage) and both
   readings are repeated before a verdict is written. s_stage on the mixed workload is recorded and does not
   decide.
4. Verdict of the clause, three-valued, on the training tier (`gpu-cuda`); the Metal figures are recorded and do
   not decide:
   - **met** if, for at least one reference workload, the lower end of the interval of s is at least 1/3;
   - **not met** (the profile clause of KILL-SPECIALIZE has fired) if, for every reference workload, the upper
     end of the interval of s is below 1/3;
   - **undecided by the profile** otherwise (no interval lies wholly at or above 1/3, and some interval reaches
     1/3): the clause has not fired, the gate to fund is not met by the profile, and the pilot's own falsifier
     decides.
   DESIGN: the plan says only "shows a specializable share under one third". Two drafts of this reading
   differed: one compared the point value of s with 1/3, the other fired the clause only when the upper end of
   the interval was below 1/3. The rule above reads both ends of the interval symmetrically and names the band
   between them, so a point value above 1/3 whose interval reaches below it is `undecided`, not `met`. Reason: a kill clause
   is a recorded finding about a mechanism and must not fire on a difference smaller than the measurement's own
   spread, and funding must not be justified by one either; in the band the question goes to the pilot, which
   costs two engineer-days. The tool evaluates the two clauses as separate checks, the kill clause (`not met`)
   first and the funding clause (`met`: the lower end of s at least 1/3) after it, so that a build missing the
   funding clause still cannot fund a share whose interval lies below one third.
5. Write the record `frob.profile.specializable/1`: the kernel list of the set, per-kernel times, s per case with
   its interval, s_stage per case with its interval and the result of the cross-check, both ceilings, the
   profile's identity, fingerprint digest, disclosure block, and the verdict. Enter it in the review log and hand
   its identifier to the pilot (N21c-17) and to the decision bead (N21c-18).

The analysis is a small tool in `frob-conformance` (`specializable_share`) that reads a profile receipt and the
kernel registry's family tags. Evidence class `Estimated`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The specializable set is written down, by kernel identifier, before the profile numbers are read (ledger timestamps).
- [ ] s with its interval, 1/(1 − s) and 1/(1 − s/2) are recorded for Q and H on CUDA and on Metal (or `not-run` with a reason for Metal), with the profiling perturbation.
- [ ] s_stage is recorded with an interval on the reference quadruped and on the mixed workload, with 1/(1 − s_stage), and agrees with the per-kernel s of workload Q within the intervals, or the disagreement is recorded as a finding and resolved before the verdict.
- [ ] The tool reproduces the incumbent-trace hand case exactly.
- [ ] The verdict (`met`, `not met` or `undecided by the profile`) is recorded with the measured shares and intervals, not the threshold.
- [ ] The planted input `N21c-16:cx-164` (CX-164, with its `removed_check`) is planted and caught: in the build without the funding clause the request is still `not met`.
- [ ] The record is entered in the review log with its identifier and digest, the form in which the pilot (N21c-17) and the kill-gate decision (N21c-18), both of which depend on this bead, read it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance specializable_share` on `synthetic` profile receipts:
- `share_incumbent_trace`: stages 53 (specializable), 124 (solve), 38 (constraint construction), 47 (other) ns → s = 0.2023, ceiling 1.2536, twice-as-fast 1.1125.
- `share_manipulation`: s = 0.16 → ceiling 1.1905.
- `share_threshold`: s = 1/3 exactly (computed in rational arithmetic from integer nanoseconds, 100 of 300, zero-width interval) is `met`; 99 of 300 with a zero-width interval is `not met`.
- `share_interval_band`: s = 0.32 with interval [0.30, 0.36] is `undecided by the profile` (the clause has not fired: the upper end is at least 1/3); with interval [0.30, 0.33] it is `not met`; s = 0.34 with interval [0.30, 0.38] is `undecided by the profile` (the point value is above 1/3, the lower end is not); s = 0.40 with interval [0.35, 0.45] is `met`.
- `ceiling_at_one_third`: s = 1/3 → 3/2.
- `share_from_stage_timers` (`synthetic` timers): stage times summing to 262 with 53 in the recursions of stages 1 and 6 give s_stage = 0.2023, which agrees with a per-kernel s of 0.2023; with zero-width intervals the verdict, read from the per-kernel s, is `not met` (s_stage cross-checks and never decides alone).
- `stage_and_kernel_readings_disagree_is_flagged`: s = 0.20 against s_stage = 0.35 with narrow intervals raises the cross-check finding and blocks the verdict.
- `too_few_repetitions_cannot_decide`: a cell with 9 repetitions yields no verdict.
- `set_membership_from_family_tags`: a kernel tagged `solve` is never counted, whatever its name.
- `missing_kernel_time_is_error`: a profile lacking a kernel of the step plan is rejected, not treated as zero.
- `cx164_funding_request_below_third_not_funded` (the control `N21c-16:cx-164`, below): the fixture's funding request is `not met`, with the kill clause named and both intervals' upper ends below 1/3, both in the normal build and in the build with the funding clause removed.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/specializable_share.sh`: reads the stored M2 profile records for Q and H on `gpu-cuda` and
`gpu-metal` with `frob artifact read <profile record> --json` (they are produced by the `profile` step of
`scripts/e2e/spark_parity_rows.sh`, BD-217; a missing or stale record for the current fingerprint stops the script
with that repair; `--profile` on `sim.run` is the execution profile, not kernel profiling, so the script does not
re-run the profile itself); reads the cohort calibration's stage timers (N17a-06) the same way; runs `cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example specializable_share -- --profile <record> --stages <record>` for each case; asserts the
set was frozen before the profile's timestamp, every kernel of the step plan has a time, and the verdict follows
the three-valued rule. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/specializable_share/<utc>.ndjson`
with a transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, command lines, exit codes, wall times, the evidence
and disclosure blocks of the records read, per-kernel times with units, s and s_stage with their intervals against
1/3, both ceilings, the perturbation factor against 1.5, the record digests, on failure the first kernel or case
that breaks a rule, and a `summary` line with the verdict.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-549 (53 of 262 ns; 1.25, 1.11, 1.19; one third for 1.5) and KA-374, the cohort chapter's row for the same
arithmetic (1.25 for an infinitely fast specialized recursion, 1.11 for one twice as fast, 1.19 on a manipulation
scene with a 16% share; exact arithmetic on a FACT to be re-read at its source). Arithmetic references: 53/262 =
0.2023; 262/209 = 1.2536; a recursion twice as fast gives 262/(262 − 26.5) = 1.1125; a 16% share gives 1/0.84 =
1.19; s = 1/3 gives 1.5. CX-224 and CX-164 (a kernel specialized to one design gives a large step speedup:
false) are the counterexamples behind the threshold. CX-164 is planted here, as the control N17-CX catalogues for
it and N38b-04's control runner runs: `N21c-16:cx-164`, kind `planted-input`, gate KILL-SPECIALIZE, fixture
`cx-164.toml` (a funding request for specialization with a measured share below one third: a `synthetic` profile
record on `gpu-cuda`, ten repetitions per cell, with s = 0.2023 in [0.19, 0.21] for Q, the incumbent trace's 53 of
262 ns, and 0.16 in [0.15, 0.17] for H), `removed_check` = "the s ≥ 1/3 clause of the funding rule" (a test-only
build of `specializable_share` without the funding clause, so the request reaches the verdict); `expect`: the kill
clause's reading returns `not met`, never `met`, and `cx164_funding_request_below_third_not_funded` passes in both
builds. Negative controls: a ceiling computed as 1 + s (1.20 at
s = 0.20) in place of 1/(1 − s) must fail `share_incumbent_trace`; a planted profile in which the solve's time is attributed to the kinematics family
(family tags, not names, must decide, and the unit test must catch the mis-tag through the registry lint); a
profile taken with profiling perturbation above 1.5 is flagged `perturbed` and cannot decide. Metamorphic: scaling
all kernel times by a constant leaves s unchanged.

## Considerations

- If the share is under one third on every workload, the three-arm pilot need not run for funding purposes; it may
  still run as a measurement because a sibling mechanism adds a fourth arm to it.
- Our step differs from the incumbent's: a heavier solve lowers s further; a lighter one raises it. That is why
  the share is measured on our own profile.
- Profiling mode changes launch structure; s is a ratio and is less sensitive to that than absolute times, but the
  perturbation is recorded.
- What would make us revisit: a profile after an optimization pass in which the solve shrinks and the recursions'
  share rises above one third. The reading is then repeated on that profile and the decision bead re-read.
