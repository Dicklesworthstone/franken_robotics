## Background

Ten mechanisms were proposed for the device tier and rejected, each by a calculation that can be re-run. A rejected
idea returns: someone will propose it again with a benchmark from another context. This bead (with its companion
for the six memory and scheduling ideas) makes each refutation executable, records what is used instead, and adds
a guard that keeps the rejected mechanism out of kernel source. It covers the four that concern precision and
statistics.

**1. Factor the contact Hessian in half precision on tensor cores and refine in single precision.** Refuting
calculation: half precision has unit round-off u = 2⁻¹¹ ≈ 4.9·10⁻⁴, and iterative refinement needs κ·u < 1 (κ the
condition number). On contact states of three robots, after unit-diagonal scaling, κ·u ≥ 1 in 37 to 64% of states
and refinement fails outright in 9 to 18% (FACT, a recorded measurement). A rank-one worst case with the proposed
shift has κ = 1 + 48/0.01 = 4,801, so κ·u ≈ 2.3. Each refinement step costs about 3n² operations against n³/3 for
the factorization it replaces, a ratio of 9k/n for k steps: 2.3 at n = 27 and k = 7, 1.5 at n = 35 and k = 6. The
published four- to five-fold gain is against double precision at n = 22,000, and Apple GPUs have no such units.
*Used instead:* single precision in scaled variables. The factorization is 1,944 operations at n = 18 and 14,292
at n = 35, under a tenth of the step, so there is little to win. Half precision is not used anywhere in the step.

**2. Mergeable quantile sketches whose result depends on insertion order.** A two-centroid sketch that merges its
closest pair, fed {0, 3, 5, 9}: in the order 0, 3, 5, 9 it ends as (8/3 with weight 3; 9 with weight 1); in the
order 0, 9, 5, 3 it ends as (1.5 with weight 2; 7 with weight 2). Same data, different sketch, so the statistic
depends on shard count and arrival. *Used instead:* integer log-bin histograms: counts are integers, merging is
addition, and a quantile is reported as a bin interval. The device chapter describes the bin as the sign, the
exponent and the top five mantissa bits of the `f32` pattern (a bin is at most 3.2% wide, exactly 2⁻⁵ = 3.125% of
its lower edge); that pattern form is the host type `Hist14` of N35-21 and serves values without a declared
quantum. For a declared telemetry channel, which is what `K-x-hist` accumulates on devices, the bin is the same
octave rule applied to the value quantized by the channel's quantum (N29a-13, N29a-14). Both are built on one
bin function and have the same resolution at five sub-bucket bits.

**3. Unordered floating-point scatter, accepted if "99.9% of windows" replay bitwise.** 0.999¹⁰⁰⁰ = 0.37: a
campaign of a thousand windows would replay exactly about one time in three. *Used instead:* stable-key sort and
segmented reduction; floating-point atomics are banned.

**4. Tensor-core products for a 256-wide policy.** The learner and inference together are about 13% of training
wall-clock on one incumbent stack, so removing them entirely gives at most 1/(1 − 0.13) = 1.15; reduced precision
also leaves class D2. *Used instead:* plain `f32` products in the dialect; device matrix paths only inside the
labelled D4 training profile.

## Scope

In: the registry `registries/rejected_mechanisms.toml` with its four rows of this bead; for each of the four: the
decision row, an executable known-answer test of the refuting arithmetic as the device chapter prints it, a small
executable demonstration where one exists, and the guard.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- for half precision: the refusal `FROB-REF-245` at the step plan, the surface and the kernel manifest; the
  contact chapter's calculation as known answers (KA-423: u₁₆, κ = 4,801, κ·u₁₆ = 2.344, the three cost ratios,
  the break-even) with its derivation file; and the one software half-precision type with the 48-row rank-one
  fixture and its statement of what "does not contract" means: N18b-27, a dependency. This bead's row cites
  that derivation and those tests, runs its demonstration on that fixture, and keeps for itself the operation
  counts of KA-552 and the build-time guard on kernel source. The worked card of the same calculation is GB-22.
- for sketches: the histogram types and their laws (N35-21 for the `f32`-pattern form and the bin function;
  N29a-13 for the channel form), on which the property test here runs.
- the entries of the program's killed-idea register `registries/killed_ideas.toml` (KI-01 for half precision
  and the others: NAB-33, NAB-34), a different file from this bead's registry.
- the mechanisms used instead (their own beads); the six other rejected mechanisms (N21c-26); the
  counterexample registry rows (N21-CX cites these tests).

## Technical approach

A test module `frob-conformance::rejected::precision` (tier G1, host only, exact or hand-checkable arithmetic) and
rows in `registries/rejected_mechanisms.toml` (`id`, `idea`, `calculation`, `used_instead`, `guard`, `test`,
`first_milestone`: the milestone from which the guard's named test can run; before it the row reads `pending` in
the generated decision record and its check is not a failure, after it a missing run is).

1. **Half precision.**
   - Row: `calculation` names the derivation file `docs/derivations/half-precision.md` of N18b-27 and the card
     GB-22; `test` names the tests below and N18b-27's `kappa_rank_one`, `kappa_u16` and
     `refine_does_not_contract`; `used_instead` is single precision in power-of-two-scaled variables; `guard`
     is the type-list and lint guard below together with the refusal `FROB-REF-245` of N18b-27.
   - Arithmetic tests of this bead (KA-552): n³/3 = 1,944 at n = 18 and 14,291.67 (rounded 14,292) at n = 35;
     9k/n = 2.333 at (27, 7) and 1.543 at (35, 6). The values u = 2⁻¹¹, κ = 1 + 48/0.01 = 4,801 and
     κ·u = 4,801 ÷ 2,048 = 2.344 are asserted once, by N18b-27 (KA-423), and this bead's test only checks that
     the row's printed figures equal those the cited tests assert.
   - Demonstration: on the emulated half-precision type and the 48-row rank-one fixture of N18b-27 (the whole
     Cholesky factorization computed operation by operation in software binary16; A = w·wᵀ + 0.01·I with
     ‖w‖² = 48; 200 seeded directions; ten refinement steps with `f64` residuals) refinement does not contract,
     in the fixture's sense (DESIGN, the same in N18b-27 and GB-22): the error after ten steps is at least 10⁻²
     of its initial value in at least 90% of seeds and at least its initial value in at least half. This bead
     defines no emulation of its own: a factor computed exactly and then rounded to an 11-bit significand is
     not one (refinement on it contracts on every seed), and a 3 × 3 instance is too small to show the effect
     reliably. What stays here as a `synthetic` hand check is the small matrix: 48·v·vᵀ + 0.01·I with v a unit
     vector in ℝ³ has eigenvalues 48.01 and 0.01, so κ = 4,801, and factored in `f32` it solves to within
     10·κ·2⁻²⁴ relative error.
   - Guard: the kernel IR's type list is `f32`, `f64` (CPU targets only), `f32dd`, `u32`, `bool` and their vectors;
     the parser rejects a half-precision type; the dialect lint rejects half-precision type names of both source
     languages. Violation: `FROB-REF-251` (a banned operation in kernel source) at build time.
2. **Sketches.**
   - Demonstration: a two-centroid closest-pair sketch implemented in the test module reproduces both end states
     above exactly (rational arithmetic).
   - Property test: for random multisets of `f32` and random permutations and shardings, the log-bin histogram
     (sign, exponent, top five mantissa bits: the type `Hist14` of N35-21, not a second implementation) merged
     by addition gives identical bin counts.
   - Guard: statistics that cross worlds are computed only by registered reducers of class `stat`; the dependency
     policy denies sketch crates in the core workspace; a `stat` kernel's output never feeds state (INV-SPARK-04).
3. **Unordered scatter.**
   - Arithmetic test: 0.999¹⁰⁰⁰ = 0.3677.
   - Demonstration in `f32`: (16,777,216 + 1) + 1 = 16,777,216 but 16,777,216 + (1 + 1) = 16,777,218: two arrival
     orders, two sums.
   - Guard: the dialect lint's rule 2 (no floating-point atomics; integer atomics only for maximum, minimum, or and
     wrapping add) and the single segmented-reduction primitive with a fixed tree shape.
4. **Tensor-core policy products.**
   - Arithmetic test: 1/(1 − 0.13) = 1.149.
   - Guard: outside the `fast-training` profile `K-gemm` resolves to the dialect kernel; requesting a device matrix
     path under a profile that claims class D2 is refused (`FROB-REF-247`: the determinism class a verb requires is
     not attainable on the requested placement; raised by the licence check of N35-07, since the classifier gives
     a kernel set with the device matrix path class D4). The request is spelt as the profile's own surface spells
     it: `--determinism` names the determinism profile (`--profile` names a step profile and never this) and the
     matrix path is the knob `matrix_path=device` of the profile's knob table (N35-06; the trainer's side is
     N23a-18). Weights produced under the D4 profile are tagged `trained_under: D4` and no number observed in such
     a run is evidence (`FROB-REF-861`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four rows exist in the rejected-mechanism registry with calculation, replacement, guard and test name; the generated decision record prints them.
- [ ] Every arithmetic value above is asserted by a named test, in exact or rational arithmetic where the value is exact.
- [ ] The half-precision demonstration fails to contract on N18b-27's fixture by its two shares over 200 seeds, and the `f32` solve of the 3 × 3 matrix converges within its bound; no emulation of half precision is defined in this bead.
- [ ] The row for half precision cites N18b-27's derivation file and tests and the card GB-22, and its printed figures (4.9·10⁻⁴, 4,801, 2.3, 1,944, 14,292, 2.3 and 1.5) equal what the cited tests assert.
- [ ] The sketch demonstration reproduces both end states; the histogram property test passes for 10⁴ random cases.
- [ ] The lint rejects a half-precision type and a floating-point atomic in a planted kernel source; `K-gemm` on a device matrix path is refused under a D2 profile (library level from M2; through `frob train start --knob matrix_path=device` from M3, when N35-06 adds the option).
- [ ] Each guard has a planted violation that it catches.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance rejected::precision`:
- `chol_op_counts` (1,944 and 14,292), `refine_cost_ratio` (2.333, 1.543). The unit round-off and κ·u (2.344) are asserted by N18b-27's `u16_is_2_pow_minus_11`, `kappa_rank_one` and `kappa_u16`; `half_row_figures_match_cited_tests` checks the row against them.
- `half_refinement_does_not_contract_rank_one` (N18b-27's fixture and type; the two shares over seeds 0 to 199), `single_precision_solves_rank_one` (the 3 × 3 matrix: eigenvalues 48.01, 0.01, 0.01; relative error within 10·κ·2⁻²⁴).
- `sketch_order_0_3_5_9` → {(8/3, 3), (9, 1)}; `sketch_order_0_9_5_3` → {(3/2, 2), (7, 2)}.
- `histogram_merge_order_independent` (property); `histogram_bin_width_at_most_3_2_percent`: adjacent bin edges differ by a factor of at most 1 + 2⁻⁵.
- `scatter_replay_probability` (0.3677); `f32_sum_order_changes_bits`.
- `policy_amdahl_bound` (1.149).
- Lint fixtures: `lint_rejects_half_type`, `lint_rejects_float_atomic`; `gemm_device_path_refused_under_d2`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/rejected_mechanisms.sh` (shared with N21c-26): runs the conformance target
`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- rejected::`, then the guards that
have a user surface: a build of a planted kernel source through `cargo xtask check-generated` and the dialect
lint, expecting `FROB-REF-251`; and, from M3 only, `frob train start <design> --task <task> --scenes <set>
--determinism device-replay --knob matrix_path=device --key rm-gemm-<run-id> --json` (a fixture design, task and
scene set, so that the request passes the schema and reaches the licence check), expecting `FROB-REF-247` before the job starts and no job
record. The option `--knob` is N35-06's (M3), so at M2 that step logs `e2e_skip 'needs N35-06 (M3)'` and is never
counted as passed, and row 4's CLI guard carries `first_milestone = "M3"`; its library-level form,
`gemm_device_path_refused_under_d2` (a planted kernel set with the device matrix path classified D4 by N35-02 and
refused by N35-07's licence check under a D2 requirement), runs from M2. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/rejected_mechanisms/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, each
command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block, each asserted value against its expected value, the
refusal codes with repairs, skips with their reason, and a `summary` line listing the rows covered and pending.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-552 (Cholesky counts and the refinement ratio); CX-225, CX-228, CX-230, CX-231. This bead defines the four
plants by which N21-CX's entries and compiled-in table name its fixtures (names confirmed as N21-CX writes them):
`N21c-25:half-precision-factor` (CX-225: the half-precision factorization of N18b-27's rank-one fixture refined in
single precision, run by `half_refinement_does_not_contract_rank_one`), `N21c-25:two-centroid-sketch` (CX-228: the
closest-pair sketch fed {0, 3, 5, 9} in two orders, `sketch_order_0_3_5_9` and `sketch_order_0_9_5_3`, against
`histogram_merge_order_independent`), `N21c-25:f32-sum-reordered` (CX-230: 16,777,216, 1, 1 in two arrival orders,
`f32_sum_order_changes_bits`) and `N21c-25:gemm_device_path_refused_under_d2` (CX-231: the planted kernel set with
the device matrix path under a D2 requirement, the test of that name). Negative controls are the
planted violations of each guard, and for the arithmetic a mutant constant (u = 2⁻¹⁰) that must make N18b-27's
`kappa_u16` fail on its exact value and, through it, `half_row_figures_match_cited_tests` here; a mutant
operation count (n³/6) must make `chol_op_counts` fail. Lanes: host only, Linux and macOS, debug and release, identical results.

## Considerations

- The 37 to 64% and 9 to 18% figures are a recorded measurement on three robots; the test suite re-measures κ·u on
  the reference robots' contact states when the conditioning certificate exists and records the share beside the
  recorded one.
- Reopening half precision would need hardware with fast half-precision units on both device families and a
  conditioning distribution with κ·u < 1; neither holds today.
- The 13% figure is one incumbent stack's; our own share is measured by the phase ledger and replaces it in the
  test's comment, not in the decision.
