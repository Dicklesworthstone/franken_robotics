## Background

The Metal backend (`spark/frob-spark-metal`, over FrankenTorch's `ft-kernel-metal` gateway) is a Frontier
mechanism: "Metal backend as a rollout tier [F], KILL-METAL". Register row: **trigger**: GATE-SPARK-04 fails after
one optimization pass, or the Metal backend cannot hold run-to-run determinism. **Fallback**: Metal becomes a
development and replay tier (single-device debugging, small batches, the viewer); CUDA is the training tier; the
Mac trains on `batch-cpu` using its 36 CPU cores. **Milestone**: M2. It is one of the four kill decisions that
define the M2 exit, each recorded as an ADR.

GATE-SPARK-04 (Performance, M2, TARGET): Metal throughput at least 25% of one RTX 4090 at matched settings on the
M5 Ultra, reported beside an incumbent with a Metal path measured on the same machine. That is the threshold of
BET-09 (stretch: 60%); it is row 12 of the throughput targets (workload Q, physics only, `gpu-metal`, ratio to the
CUDA row as measured, as a paired lower bound).

Why the gate may fire (pre-mortem item "the Metal gateway's limits throttled the Mac tier"; RISK-08): the gateway
today is synchronous (dispatch and submit block until the command buffer finishes), takes at most 31 buffer
arguments per kernel, binds buffers at offset zero, has shared storage only, compiles from source only, and
reports only a kernel count and total device seconds (FACT, read in source). Spark works inside those limits with
packed slabs, offset tables, one batch per chunk of control intervals and one blocked host thread per device; the
missing features are an upstream work item in FrankenTorch's repository (FD-FT-01), never a local patch, and a
Metal binding of our own is not built without an owner decision (OPEN-SPARK-5, default: no). Leading indicators:
step-plan launch counts and blocked host time per chunk on Metal. On the arithmetic side the threshold needs only
about 120 GB/s of traffic, a tenth of the chip's bandwidth, so command-buffer overhead and arithmetic rate decide.

## Scope

In: the decision rule; reading the gate measurement and the determinism lanes; the ADR; downstream changes under
each outcome; the conditions under which the decision is revisited. Out: measuring GATE-SPARK-04 and the
optimization pass (BD-217); the determinism tests; the upstream work item.

## Technical approach

**Inputs.** (a) GATE-SPARK-04's record: the paired lower bound of Metal throughput over one RTX 4090 at matched
settings, before and after the one optimization pass, with the disclosure block (including thermal state after the
20-minute soak, launch counts, blocked host time), and the incumbent's Metal figure on the same machine. (b) The
determinism lanes on Metal: the full step twice on one machine and across batch size, slot, co-tenants and bucket
capacity (class D2 for the device family). (c) The state of OPEN-SPARK-2 and OPEN-SPARK-5.

**Rule.**
1. Metal cannot hold run-to-run determinism (any digest difference between two identical runs of the full step on
   the same device, driver and compiler, not explained by a recorded defect that one repair removes): **fired**.
2. GATE-SPARK-04's bound is at least 0.25 (before or after the pass) and rule 1 does not apply: **not fired**.
3. The bound is below 0.25 before the pass: one profile-guided optimization pass (one lever at a time, each landing
   only with identical digests and a paired lower bound of at least 1.03 on the row); re-measure. Still below
   0.25: **fired**.
4. GATE-SPARK-04 was `not run` (no envelope, or the CUDA row it is a ratio to does not exist): **undecided**; Metal
   results stay labelled exploratory, the M2 exit records the reason, and the gate is decided when the measurement
   exists.

Bit equality with `batch-cpu` is *not* part of this gate: a family that is deterministic for itself but not
bit-equal to the CPU falls under KILL-D1 (class D2 for itself, D3 against others).

**Fallback when fired.** Metal becomes a development and replay tier: single-device debugging, small batches, the
viewer. CUDA is the training tier. The Mac trains on `batch-cpu` using its 36 CPU cores. The Mac keeps the world
bank, proof, replay and `batch-cpu` roles.

**Downstream when fired.** The role table's `train` cell for the Mac changes from "small and medium runs on
`gpu-metal`" to `batch-cpu` training only; no Performance or Parity claim cites a Metal figure; throughput row 12
is recorded as failed with its measured ratio and BET-09 as falsified; `strict:gpu-metal` requests remain valid for
debugging and replay and carry the tier label; the placement planner's cost model stops preferring Metal for
throughput work; large-scene evaluation on the Mac runs on `batch-cpu` and the fleet arithmetic is redone with that
rate. The upstream work item continues: when it lands its switch-over rule re-runs the gateway admission tests,
GATE-SPARK-02 and GATE-SPARK-04, and a passing re-measurement reopens this decision with a new ADR.

**Downstream when not fired.** Metal is a rollout tier for small and medium runs; its measured ratio (not the
target) is published with the disclosure block; the Mac's share in the fleet ceiling (0.125 to 0.30 of the pair of
cards) is replaced by the measured share.

**Record.** An ADR with the trigger text, the measured bound before and after the pass (n, ℓ̄, s), the determinism
lane results, launch counts and blocked host time, commit, fingerprint digest, seeds, the rule number, the outcome
and the fallback in force; the KILL-METAL row of the gate register.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists, cites GATE-SPARK-04's record and the determinism lanes by identity, and states the rule number that applied, with measured values.
- [ ] If fired: the role registry row is changed and its test updated; a Performance claim citing a Metal figure is refused; the Mac's `batch-cpu` training path is exercised by a test run; row 12 and BET-09 are recorded with measured values.
- [ ] If not fired: the measured ratio is published with the full disclosure block beside the incumbent's Metal-path figure on the same machine.
- [ ] If undecided: the reason is recorded, Metal results carry `exploratory`, and the M2 exit review lists the open decision.
- [ ] The optimization pass, if used, is one pass and is described; a second pass or a binding of our own is not taken without an owner decision.
- [ ] The decision is taken by a session that did not implement the backend, as part of the M2 exit review.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `kill_metal_rule_table`: (nondeterministic) → fired by rule 1; (bound 0.31) → not fired; (0.18 then 0.27 after the pass) → not fired; (0.18 then 0.22) → fired; (gate not run) → undecided; (bound exactly 0.25) → not fired.
- `kill_metal_ignores_cpu_bit_equality`: a family that is D2 for itself and D3 against `batch-cpu` does not fire this gate.
- `kill_metal_fallback_role_row`: with the gate fired, `preferred_machines("train")` lists the Mac only with its `batch-cpu` condition.
- `kill_metal_blocks_metal_performance_claim`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kill_metal_record.sh`: reads GATE-SPARK-04's receipt and the Metal determinism receipts through
`frob artifact read … --json`, recomputes the decision, compares with the gate registry, and, when fired, runs
`frob train start … --tier batch-cpu --json` on the Mac for a short smoke run to show the fallback path works. Logs
per the end-to-end standard: the bound against 0.25 with n, ℓ̄ and s, launch counts, blocked host time, the
determinism verdict with the first differing world if any, the rule number, the ADR path, and a `summary` line.

### Known answers and negative controls

Negative controls: a decision recorded from a mean and not a paired lower bound (rejected: `FROB-REF-863`); a
figure without a disclosure block (`FROB-REF-862`); a record that repeats the target 0.25 as its measurement
(rejected by the registry check); a ratio taken against a CUDA figure from another commit or settings (rejected:
"matched settings" requires the same workload, step, iterations, capacities and world count in both receipts).

## Considerations

- Urgency is one step above its milestone: the Mac's training role, the fleet arithmetic and the wording of every
  Mac performance statement depend on it.
- The thermal protocol matters on the Mac: a figure without the 20-minute soak overstates sustained throughput.
- OPEN-SPARK-1 (owner ratification of the annex design) is independent: if it stays open, all GPU results are
  exploratory whatever this gate says.
- A fired gate costs the Metal rollout tier and no milestone: M2 has a variant with no device at all.
