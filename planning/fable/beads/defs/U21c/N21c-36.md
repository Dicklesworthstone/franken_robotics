## Background

**The question.** OPEN-SPARK-3: the bucket set and the capacities of each reference workload. Owner: the Spark
lead. Decided by the M2 profile; it blocks GATE-SPARK-10 (the variants gate). **Fail-closed default:** capacities
of {12, 18, 24, 36, 48, 64} degrees of freedom, as the population chapter registers them, and the reference
workloads' capacities: Q (reference quadruped, 18 degrees of freedom, 13 bodies) with contact capacity 32 and row
capacity 160; H (reference humanoid, 35, 30) with 64 and 320. Row capacity is four rows per contact plus one limit
row and one stiction or backlash row per actuated joint, rounded up: 128 + 12 + 12 and 256 + 29 + 29.

**Why it matters.** A *bucket* is one shape class on one device: one arena, one compiled kernel set, one step
plan, one launch graph. Designs of different sizes share a bucket by padding to its capacity, and padding costs
work. If kernels do full work on padding, the cost of padding a batch to its largest member is
Ω_p = L_max^p / E[L^p] for work that grows as the p-th power of size L, with 1/r ≤ Ω_p ≤ r⁻ᵖ where r is the mean
size over the maximum. Worked values: degrees of freedom 18 and 35 mixed evenly give 1.3, 1.6 and 1.8 for p = 1, 2,
3; mixed 90 to 10 they give 1.8, 3.0 and 4.5. With the default capacities the worst member of a bucket sits just
above the previous capacity (25 in the 36 bucket), for Ω₁ ≤ 1.44 and Ω₃ ≤ 3.0. More buckets mean less padding but
one more step plan per step each. If a step plan costs L seconds of launch overhead and a world-step costs t,
splitting N worlds off pays when **N·(1 − 1/Ω)·t > L**. HYPOTHESIS: with about 60 launches of 5 to 10 µs each and
t = 0.5 µs, the break-even at Ω = 1.3 is 2,600 to 5,200 worlds. The launch cost is measured by the fingerprint and
the bucket set is frozen at M2. Masked lanes that exit early pay less than the bound (an incumbent runs a mixed
batch of three body plans at 0.86 of the harmonic mean of its homogeneous rates; FACT).

The kill gate next door (KILL-BUCKET) fires if more than eight buckets are needed to hold Ω₃ ≤ 2 on the reference
family corpus, or the mixed batch runs below 0.5 of the harmonic mean after one optimization pass.

## Scope

In: the measurement of L and t; padding statistics of candidate bucket sets on the reference populations; the
break-even table; the frozen set and capacities as a registry record; the decision note. Out: the bucket planner
and `phylum.plan` (BD-171); the variants gate (BD-183); the head-to-head and its kill gate.

## Technical approach

1. **Measure L.** From the fingerprint's launch block per device: per-launch or graph-replay cost, and the step
   plan's launch count for workloads Q and H on each backend (CUDA with graph capture; Metal with one batch per
   chunk: the per-dispatch encode cost times kernels per step plus the commit-and-wait cost amortized over the
   chunk). L = host and device overhead of one more step plan per step, in seconds.
2. **Measure t.** Per-world step time at 8,192 worlds for Q and H on each backend from the first full-step runs
   (physics only, warm, graphs captured), and its growth exponent p per stage from runs at capacities 18, 36 and 64
   (assembly and the dense solve grow faster than kinematics).
3. **Populations.** The first archetypes and the 1,000 generated designs of the M1 population pilot, plus the
   reference family corpus where it exists. For each candidate set (the default; the default with 32 in place of
   36; a coarser set {16, 32, 64}; a finer set adding 30 and 42) compute per bucket: members, Ω₁, Ω₂, Ω₃ from the
   member sizes, the padded bytes per world, and the number of buckets with at least one member.
4. **Break-even table.** For each adjacent pair of buckets and each backend: N* = L ÷ ((1 − 1/Ω)·t), the world
   count above which keeping the two buckets separate pays. A bucket whose expected world count in the reference
   campaigns is below N* is merged into its neighbour.
5. **Capacities.** Contact and row capacities per bucket from the static contact maxima and the capacity gate:
   raise a capacity if more than one world-step in 10⁴ is `Degraded` for capacity on the standard battery;
   otherwise keep the default.
6. **Freeze.** Write `registries/shape_buckets.toml`: the set, each bucket's capacities (degrees of freedom, links,
   depth, contacts, rows, samples, actuators), the measurements behind it (L, t, p, Ω, N*), fingerprint digests and
   commit. The registry digest enters every shape-class identity. Record the decision note and close OPEN-SPARK-3.
   After the later optimization profile exists, re-read L and t once and record a confirmation or a dated revision;
   a revision re-measures the variants gate.

Evidence class of the inputs: `Estimated` (timings) and exact (padding arithmetic).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] L and t are measured on CUDA and on Metal (or `not run` with a reason for a backend) with the disclosure block; the 5 to 10 µs and 0.5 µs hypotheses are replaced by measurements.
- [ ] The padding table reproduces the hand values: 18 and 35 mixed evenly → 1.32, 1.58, 1.76; mixed 90 to 10 → 1.78, 2.96, 4.50; the worst member of the default set (25 in 36) → Ω₁ = 1.44, Ω₃ = 2.99.
- [ ] The break-even table is recorded for each adjacent pair and backend, and reproduces 2,600 to 5,200 worlds for the hypothesis inputs.
- [ ] The frozen registry exists, is content-addressed, and the variants gate is measured under it.
- [ ] If more than eight buckets are needed to hold Ω₃ ≤ 2 on the reference family corpus, that finding is reported to the bucket kill gate.
- [ ] OPEN-SPARK-3 is closed in the open-question register with the measured values.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance bucket_decision`:
- `omega_even_mix`: sizes {18, 35} with weights ½, ½ → Ω₁ = 35/26.5 = 1.321, Ω₂ = 1,225/774.5 = 1.582, Ω₃ = 42,875/24,353.5 = 1.761.
- `omega_90_10_mix`: weights 0.9, 0.1 → 35/19.7 = 1.777, 1,225/414.1 = 2.958, 42,875/9,536.3 = 4.496.
- `omega_bounds`: 1/r ≤ Ω_p ≤ r⁻ᵖ for random size multisets (property test).
- `worst_member_default_set`: for {12, 18, 24, 36, 48, 64} the worst ratio is 36/25 = 1.44 (the others: 18/13 = 1.385, 24/19 = 1.263, 48/37 = 1.297, 64/49 = 1.306); cubed 2.986.
- `worst_member_alternate_set`: with 32 in place of 36 the worst ratio is 48/33 = 1.455; cubed 3.077.
- `break_even_hypothesis`: L = 60 × 5 µs = 300 µs, Ω = 1.3, t = 0.5 µs → N* = 300 ÷ (0.2308 × 0.5) = 2,600; with 10 µs per launch 5,200.
- `row_capacity_rule`: 4·32 + 12 + 12 = 152 → 160; 4·64 + 29 + 29 = 314 → 320.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/bucket_decision.sh`: `frob spark fingerprint --json` (launch block), `frob sim run --workload Q|H
--worlds 8192 --tier gpu-cuda|gpu-metal --json` at three capacities, `frob phylum plan --designs @population:m1
--buckets <candidate> --json` for each candidate set, then the analysis. Logs per the end-to-end standard: command
lines, exit codes, L, t and p with units, Ω per bucket, N* per pair, the chosen set, registry digest, fingerprint
digests, and a `summary` line.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-547 (padding overhead) and KA-548 (break-even). Negative controls: a padding calculator that averages sizes
before raising to the power p (it returns 1.0 for the even mix at p = 1 and must fail `omega_even_mix`); a
break-even computed with Ω in place of 1 − 1/Ω. Metamorphic: scaling all sizes by a constant leaves Ω unchanged.

## Considerations

- The plan's own texts disagree on one capacity: the chapter and its open-question default say 36 (worst member 25,
  Ω₁ ≤ 1.44, Ω₃ ≤ 3.0); the known-answer and counterexample catalogues quote the set with 32 (worst member 33 in
  the 48 bucket, Ω₁ ≤ 1.45, Ω₃ ≤ 3.1); and one register lists the default as link counts where the chapter says
  degrees of freedom. The default with 36 in degrees of freedom governs until this bead freezes a set; both
  variants are candidates above and both hand values are tested.
- The decision is by measurement, not by the owner; it is urgent because the variants gate, the head-to-head and
  every shape-class identity wait on it.
- On Metal the launch cost is per chunk, not per step; the break-even there may favour more buckets than on CUDA.
  One set is frozen for both unless the measured costs differ by more than a factor of two, in which case the
  record carries a per-backend merge map over one common set.
