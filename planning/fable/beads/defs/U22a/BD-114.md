## Background
Every evaluation in the studio can run on estimated state or on privileged simulator truth, and the difference is reported, because a controller that only works on privileged state is not a result. That needs an estimator for any design, built from its model with no tuning. The plan's choice is a contact-aided invariant extended Kalman filter: the floating-base state (rotation R, velocity v, position p) is an element of the matrix Lie group SE₂(3), extended by k contact points to SE₂₊ₖ(3); IMU dynamics on that group are group-affine, so the invariant error obeys a linear equation whose matrix does not depend on the estimated trajectory. Inputs are the IMU, the joint encoders and the foot contact switches; outputs are base pose and velocity with covariance. Wheeled bases use wheel odometry with the IMU on the same filter structure. Tier: Solid. Crate: `frob-control`.

Why this filter and not a conventional one. A quaternion extended Kalman filter linearized at a moving estimate gains a spurious observable yaw direction and becomes overconfident (FACT, the plan's source register); the invariant filter's unobservable subspace is the same four directions at every estimate. The conventional filter is kept as the comparator (N22a-02), not discarded.

The plan adds correctness conditions, each implemented here:
- **Declared error convention.** The filter manifest states whether its error is left- or right-invariant and carries the covariance transport between the two, Σ_right = Ad_X̂·Σ_left·Ad_X̂ᵀ to first order. Biases, delays and uncertain contact add terms that are not group-affine, so log-linearity is evaluated, not assumed: the filter reports the measured departure from the linear error equation.
- **Gauge freedom.** Under proprioception and gravity, absolute position and yaw are unobservable: a subspace of dimension four. The filter's linearization must have exactly that null space at every estimate (proof obligation PO-REFLEX-01), checked analytically and by finite perturbation of the true initial state along those directions.
- **Contact handling.** A contact point is added to the state at touchdown and marginalized at liftoff. A contact whose slip estimate exceeds its threshold is down-weighted, never trusted and never silently dropped.

Gate (GATE-REFLEX-03, Known answer, M1): filter consistency inside χ² bounds on the simulated IMU; position drift under 2% of distance on a flat trot, scored by the gauge rule; PO-REFLEX-01; the `overconfident` flag fires on a planted bias.

## Technical approach
All formulas follow the published contact-aided invariant filter (DESIGN: its use here; the plan fixes the group, the inputs, the outputs and the conditions above). x^ denotes the skew matrix of a vector; Exp and Log are the group exponential and logarithm from `frob-lie`.

**State.** X = [[R, v, p, d₁ … d_k], [0, I]] ∈ SE₂₊ₖ(3), a (5 + k)×(5 + k) matrix, with dᵢ the world position of contact i; biases θ = (b_g, b_a) ∈ ℝ⁶. Capacity k_max is the number of feet (or zero for a wheeled base). Covariance P has dimension 9 + 3k + 6.

**Error (default: right-invariant).** η = X̂·X⁻¹ = Exp(ξ) with ξ = (ξ_R, ξ_v, ξ_p, ξ_d₁, …); bias error ζ = θ̂ − θ. The left-invariant form X⁻¹·X̂ is available on request. Covariance transport (first order): Σ_right = Ad_X̂·Σ_left·Ad_X̂ᵀ, with Ad_X = [[R, 0, 0, 0], [v^·R, R, 0, 0], [p^·R, 0, R, 0], [d^·R, 0, 0, R]] (one row block per contact).

**Propagation** over one IMU period Δt with gyroscope reading ω̃ and accelerometer (specific force) reading ã, gravity g:
1. R̂⁺ = R̂·Exp((ω̃ − b̂_g)·Δt).
2. a_w = R̂·(ã − b̂_a) + g; v̂⁺ = v̂ + a_w·Δt; p̂⁺ = p̂ + v̂·Δt + ½·a_w·Δt².
3. d̂ᵢ⁺ = d̂ᵢ; θ̂⁺ = θ̂.
4. Error dynamics d/dt (ξ, ζ) = A·(ξ, ζ) + noise, with block rows (R, v, p, dᵢ, b_g, b_a):
   A = [[0, 0, 0, 0, −R̂, 0], [g^, 0, 0, 0, −v̂^·R̂, −R̂], [0, I, 0, 0, −p̂^·R̂, 0], [0, 0, 0, 0, −d̂ᵢ^·R̂, 0], [0 …], [0 …]].
   The upper-left block is constant: that is the log-linear property. The bias columns depend on the estimate: those are the terms that are not group-affine.
5. P⁺ = Φ·P·Φᵀ + Φ·Ĝ·Q·Ĝᵀ·Φᵀ·Δt with Φ = exp(A·Δt) (the constant block is nilpotent, so its exponential is the finite series I + A·Δt + ½·A²·Δt²; the bias columns are integrated to first order) and Ĝ = blockdiag(Ad_X̂, I). Q holds the gyroscope and accelerometer noise densities, the contact-point process noise (expressed in the contact frame) and the bias random walks, all read from the sensor cards with their provenance.

**Leg-kinematics update** for each foot i in contact, with forward kinematics h_p(α̃) (foot position in the body frame from encoder angles α̃) and its Jacobian J_p:
1. The measurement has the right-invariant form Y = X⁻¹·b + V with Y = (h_p(α̃), 0, 1, −1ᵢ) and b = (0, 0, 1, −1ᵢ).
2. Residual z = R̂·h_p(α̃) − (d̂ᵢ − p̂). To first order z = −H·ξ + R̂·J_p·w_α with H = [0, 0, −I, +Iᵢ, 0, 0].
3. N = R̂·J_p·Cov(w_α)·J_pᵀ·R̂ᵀ; S = H·P·Hᵀ + N; K = P·Hᵀ·S⁻¹.
4. (δξ, δζ) = K·z; X̂⁺ = Exp(δξ)·X̂; θ̂⁺ = θ̂ + δζ; P⁺ = (I − K·H)·P·(I − K·H)ᵀ + K·N·Kᵀ.
5. The innovation record (z, S, dimension, status) goes to the scorer.

**Wheel-odometry update** (wheeled bases, k = 0). The body-frame velocity ṽ_b from wheel rates through the kinematic connection of the base is the right-invariant observation Y = (ṽ_b, −1, 0) = X⁻¹·(0, −1, 0) + V: residual z = R̂·ṽ_b − v̂, and to first order z = −H·ξ + R̂·w with H = [0, +I, 0, 0, 0] (the same convention as the leg update), N = R̂·Cov(ṽ_b)·R̂ᵀ. For a nonholonomic base the zero lateral body velocity is one more row with its own noise. Odometry equals travel only while the wheel rolls: a wheel whose slip estimate exceeds its threshold is down-weighted exactly as a foot is.

**Contact handling.**
1. Touchdown (contact switch asserted, after the card's hysteresis): append d̂ᵢ = p̂ + R̂·h_p(α̃). In right-invariant coordinates the new error is ξ_d = ξ_p + R̂·J_p·w_α, so P is augmented by duplicating the position block's rows and columns and adding R̂·J_p·Cov(w_α)·J_pᵀ·R̂ᵀ to the new diagonal block.
2. Liftoff: remove the contact's rows and columns (exact marginalization of a Gaussian).
3. Slip estimate: the estimated world velocity of the contact point, v̂_c = v̂ + R̂·((ω̃ − b̂_g) × h_p + J_p·α̇), which is zero for a holding contact. When ‖v̂_c‖ exceeds the foot's threshold (manifest value; default three standard deviations of its own predicted noise), the contact's process noise for that tick is multiplied by the manifest's down-weight factor (default 100) and its update is applied with N inflated by the same factor. The contact stays in the state; a telemetry event `contact-downweighted` records the foot, the estimate and the threshold. Nothing is dropped without an event.

**Log-linearity, measured.** For a battery run the filter also propagates a set of probe errors ξ₀ (several magnitudes and directions, with and without bias error) through the nonlinear dynamics beside the estimate, and reports the departure ‖Log(X̂_t·X_t⁻¹) − Φ_t·ξ₀‖/‖ξ₀‖ against time, error magnitude and bias error. With zero bias error and no delay the departure is at round-off for any ξ₀; otherwise it is a number in the report. The manifest carries the label `imperfect-invariant` whenever bias states, modelled delays or slip down-weighting are active.

**Gauge (PO-REFLEX-01).** In right-invariant coordinates the unobservable directions are constant: yaw is ξ = (g/‖g‖, 0, 0, 0 …) and the three translations are ξ = (0, 0, e_j, e_j, …, e_j) (the same displacement of p and of every contact), all with zero bias components. Verification: (a) analytic: H·Φ_k·n = 0 for each of the four vectors n and every step k; (b) numeric rank: the stacked observability matrix [H; H·Φ₁; H·Φ₂·Φ₁; …] over a window with at least one contact has a null space of dimension exactly four (singular-value gap: the fourth-smallest below 10⁻¹⁰ of the largest, the fifth-smallest above 10⁻⁶) on 10³ random trajectories with excitation sufficient for the biases; (c) finite perturbation: translating the true initial state by t and rotating it about gravity by ψ leaves every packet unchanged and transforms the estimate by exactly that gauge.

**Manifest** (`frob.control.filter/1`, DESIGN): kind `inekf-contact` or `inekf-wheel`; error convention; group and k_max; gravity; IMU extrinsics (the lever arm of the sensor card); noise parameters with provenance; slip thresholds and down-weight factor; initial covariance; the `imperfect-invariant` label. It is canonical JSON, content-hashed, and recorded with every estimate. Initialization at rest: tilt from the accelerometer, yaw and position at the gauge origin, contacts from kinematics.

**The `synthetic` fixture.** A closed-form flat trot for 20 s at 0.5 m/s (10 m): vertical oscillation of 10 mm at twice the 2 Hz stride frequency, 1° roll and pitch oscillation, diagonal foot pairs alternating with feet stationary in stance; IMU readings from the analytic derivatives through the IMU model of the sensing chapter (specific force f = R_WSᵀ·(a_P − g), with lever arm), at 200 Hz, with noise and bias walks drawn from addressed streams; leg-odometry packets h_p with 5 mm noise. Every value is in the fixture file and labelled `synthetic`.

**Determinism and evidence.** `f64` on the host with the deterministic elementary functions; class D2 run to run. Estimates are `Tested` objects of the model; reality is `no-claim`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] GATE-REFLEX-03 at library level on the synthetic IMU (the seed bead's done-condition): mean normalized innovation inside its two-sided 95% χ² bounds in at least 43 of 50 seeds; aligned position drift under 2% of distance (under 0.20 m of 10 m) in every seed, scored by the gauge rule of N22a-02; PO-REFLEX-01 by all three verifications; measured values recorded against thresholds.
- [ ] The `overconfident` flag fires on a planted bias (second clause): an unmodelled accelerometer bias of 0.2 m/s² with the bias states frozen sets the flag; the unplanted run does not.
- [ ] The comparator's null space has dimension three on the same trajectories (the spurious yaw direction), and the rank test reports it; the invariant filter's is four at every estimate.
- [ ] Linearization check: A and H agree with central differences of the nonlinear error map to 10⁻⁶ relative on 100 random states (a sign error in any block fails).
- [ ] With zero bias error the log-linearity departure is below 10⁻⁹ for probe errors up to 1 rad and 1 m over 1 s; with a gyroscope bias error of 0.01 rad/s it is reported nonzero and the manifest is labelled `imperfect-invariant`.
- [ ] Contact augmentation and marginalization reproduce a batch least-squares reference to 10⁻⁹; a planted slip of 0.10 m on one foot produces a `contact-downweighted` event, keeps the contact in the state, and keeps drift under 2%.
- [ ] The wheeled variant holds drift under 2% of distance over two laps of a 1 m circle at 0.5 m/s on the synthetic wheel fixture.
- [ ] Σ_right = Ad_X̂·Σ_left·Ad_X̂ᵀ holds to 10⁻¹² on 10⁴ random states; the manifest states the convention; `CONTRACT.md` documents the filter in its ten sections.

### End-to-end script and logging
The user-side script is `scripts/e2e/control_estimate.sh` (N22a-05), which drives `frob control estimate` on a recorded referee run and writes the gate row. This bead's runner `scripts/e2e/reflex_inekf.sh` executes the test binary (debug and release, twice) and converts results to the shared newline-delimited JSON format at `target/e2e-logs/reflex_inekf/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: per seed a `metric` line with mean NIS and its bounds, aligned drift in metres and as a fraction against 2%, the null-space dimension with the five smallest singular values, the log-linearity departure; `assert` lines for each criterion with measured value, threshold and unit; the manifest hash and seeds; on failure the first seed and tick at which a bound was left and the fixture path; a `summary` line naming GATE-REFLEX-03.
### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-553 (the unobservable subspace has dimension four; a filter linearized at a moving estimate in the conventional way gains a spurious yaw direction and the rank test must see it), KA-555 (consistency on a flat trot; drift under 2% of distance; a planted bias raises `overconfident`), KA-081 (normalized estimation error squared inside its χ² bounds).
- Negative controls registered against GATE-REFLEX-03: the comparator on the rank test; the planted bias; a filter that trusts a slipping contact (drift above 2% on the slip fixture); a filter that drops a slipping contact without an event (the event assertion fails); a sign flip in the g^ block of A (the finite-difference check fails).
- Metamorphic: the gauge transform; time reversal is not a symmetry and is not tested; relabelling feet permutes contact blocks and leaves the pose estimate bit-identical after sorting contacts by identity.
- Determinism: twice on one machine; debug and release; identical bytes.

## Considerations
- RISK: baseline controllers and estimators can mask design flaws or create false ones; the signal is disagreement between estimated-state and privileged-state runs, which N22a-03 reports for every evaluation.
- The exactness of the null space rests on the constant block of A. Any future change that makes the linearization depend on the estimate in those rows (for example an additive correction on position) must fail the rank test; that is what PO-REFLEX-01 is for.
- Latency and out-of-sequence packets are handled by the state-source bead; this filter processes packets in the order it is given.
- Exteroceptive updates (vision, lidar) are out of scope; they would shrink the gauge and need their own obligation.
- Performance envelope (TARGET): one propagation plus four kinematic updates in under 20 µs on one core in `f64`, so a filter per world is affordable on `batch-cpu`.
