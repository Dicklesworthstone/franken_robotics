## Background
Controller kind `mpc-sample`: at each control tick it samples perturbed command sequences, rolls each out on the design's own compiled model inside the batch, and applies the first command of a weighted average. It needs no training and no gait. It is prior art on incumbent simulators, including real-time control of a real quadruped from about 30 samples on a CPU (FACT, the plan's source register), so it is no differentiator. It is in the plan because two roles need it:

- **Out-of-distribution fallback.** When the baseline is `not-synthesizable` or `synthesized-failed`, and the family policy reports the design out of distribution, the sampling controller gives a first behaviour to look at, with telemetry, in one to three episodes.
- **Demonstration source.** Its rollouts are demonstrations for distillation. The arithmetic limits this too: at the cheapest published setting and the throughput target, 4,000 simulated seconds of demonstrations cost about 67 GPU-minutes, more than one quadruped training run. It pays when pooled (200 designs at 20 s each for a family prior, against 200 specialists at about 7 minutes each, about 23 GPU-hours), or for a class that has a morphology-independent cost and no shaped reward yet.

The decision that bounds it (ADR-REFLEX-03): a screening rung, an out-of-distribution fallback and a demonstration source; never a stand-in for a trained policy as the evaluator of record. The bet it serves (BET-27, Frontier): with class-default costs it controls designs the synthesized baseline cannot, and supplies demonstrations for distillation; falsified if, on designs marked `not-synthesizable`, fewer than half traverse the reference task under it, or a 20 s episode costs more than 1 GPU-minute for a quadruped or 6 for a humanoid at the median, or distillation from its demonstrations does not reduce training steps. Its pilot (N22a-22) must have recorded `fund`.

Seed done-condition: the controlled world's digest does not change with the fork count; FROB-REF-817 fires on a planted acceptance request.

## Technical approach
**Controller.** `SamplingController` implements `Controller` with record kind `mpc-sample`. Its `planning` block holds: N_s, N_it, T_h, h_p, the number of knots and spline family, the temperature λ, the initial noise scale σ₀ per channel as a fraction of joint range, the annealing factor γ, the cost-term hash (class default or task-declared), the planning step plan (reduced Newton iterations and contact samples), the planning-model identity with the source of its parameters, and the latency model. All of it is in the `ControllerId`.

**Per control tick.**
1. Read the state view and delivered observations.
2. Nominal knots: the previous solution shifted by one control period (at the first tick: the present joint positions held).
3. For i = 1 … N_it: draw N_s perturbations from the addressed streams (sample 0 unperturbed); `K-fork` the controlled world into the planning arena; roll the planning worlds H = T_h/h_p steps on the planning step plan, driving them with their splines through the actuator kernel; `K-cost`; `K-weight` (softmax weights, knot update, noise shrink).
4. Evaluate the final spline at the present time; the joint targets are the command, tagged with the controller's nominal layer (`mpc-sample`). If the tick was flagged `plan-failed` (every sample infinite), the previous plan is kept and a fallback event `fallback:plan-failed` is recorded; after a declared number of consecutive failures the freeze fallback runs.
5. Latency: by default the command computed at tick k is applied at tick k + 1 (a modelled delay of one control period); a measured figure for a named computer may replace it; zero delay is allowed only when labelled.

**Costs with no per-design tuning.** One weight vector per morphology class in `registries/mpc_costs/<class>.toml` over the six non-dimensional terms of `K-cost` (twist tracking, attitude, height band, power, friction utilization above 0.8, limit barriers). A task may declare its own cost; its hash then replaces the class default in the record. No cost contains a hand-built swing or stepping reference.

**Planning model and state.** `--planning-model plant` (default: the plant's own parameters, including its randomization draw), `nominal` (card nominal values, drawn independently of the plant's randomization) or `estimated`; `--state privileged` (default) or `estimated`. Results carry `model-privileged` unless the source is not `plant` and the state is estimated. The planning step plan is coarser than the plant's; the controller reports the gap between predicted and realized cost per tick as a diagnostic.

**The verb `control.mpc`** (kind `run`, cluster `control`, first milestone M3; idempotency key and budget; bounded synchronous wait, else a job handle with exit 8).
- Input `frob.control.mpc.in/1`: design, task, optional planner setting (default: the class's registered setting), tier, seeds, `--episodes n` (capped at three per design unless one of the following is present), `--demonstrations <job>` (the rollouts are demonstrations for the named distillation job), `--compare <id>` (a paired screening comparison, up to the class's break-even count), `--freeze` (write a planner record for the delivered-controller lane), `--planner lifted:<id>` (a lifted planning model; later milestone), `--planning-model`, `--state`.
- Before admission the request is priced (N22a-24): model steps per tick and per episode, estimated cost, its percentage of training once for the class; a request beyond its cap is rejected with the arithmetic.
- Output `frob.control.mpc.out/1`: per episode the requirement verdict (as a screening result), the run identity, the labels (`screening: sampling-control`, `model-privileged` or not, planning step, latency), the pricing record with predicted and measured cost, planner diagnostics (fraction of `plan-failed` ticks, predicted-against-realized cost gap, share of each cost term in the selected plans), and, for demonstrations, the demonstration-set identity.
- Refusals: FROB-REF-817 does not arise in this verb (it produces screening results); it arises when its episodes are offered as evidence (N22a-25). FROB-REF-421's repair "admit the sampling controller for screening" points here.
- Evidence block: claim kind none (screening), class `Tested`, tier, determinism class, identities including the `ControllerId` with its planning block, seeds, `reality: no-claim`.

**Fallback wiring.** When `control.synth` reports `not-synthesizable` or `synthesized-failed` with a control cause, its routes include this verb with the class's default setting and the three-episode cap; when the family policy exists and reports the design out of distribution, the route is marked as the recommended first look.

**Demonstrations.** With `--demonstrations`, each control tick's policy-namespace observation, the applied command and the planner's cost are written to a demonstration set bound to the distillation job, with the controller's labels (a policy distilled from `model-privileged` demonstrations inherits the provenance in its manifest). The verb prints the pooled-cost arithmetic so that a single-design request shows that it costs more than training.

**Digest independence.** The controller only reads the controlled world and writes its command; forks have no side effect on their source (the kernel tests of N22a-23). At controller level: with the applied commands recorded and replayed, the controlled world's digest chain is identical whether 0, 30 or 2,048 planning worlds are forked and rolled beside it each tick; and with the planner record fixed, a controlled world's digest is independent of co-tenants, slot and arena capacity.

**Determinism and evidence.** Class D2 per device family: a planner run replays exactly from (world address, tick, iteration, sample). Results are `Tested` screening results.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The seed's first clause: on `batch-cpu`, over 500 ticks of the reference quadruped, the controlled world's digest chain is identical with 0, 30 and 2,048 forks per tick under replayed commands, and identical across co-tenancy, slot and arena capacity with the planner on.
- [ ] The seed's second clause: a planted request to accept a design on this verb's episodes is refused with FROB-REF-817 (through the evidence-boundary check), exit 3, with both ranked repairs.
- [ ] With the class-default cost and no per-design tuning, the reference quadruped and a differential-drive base track a forward velocity command for 20 s on `batch-cpu` in at least one of three episodes each (the gate's success rule applied to two designs; the population measurement is BD-331's).
- [ ] A design the baseline reports `not-synthesizable` for lack of a gait (a three-legged variant) receives a first behaviour: three screening episodes with telemetry and a verdict, labelled, whatever the verdict is.
- [ ] A planner run replays byte for byte: the same world address and seeds give identical knot vectors, costs and commands on a second run and across worker counts.
- [ ] Requests are priced before admission; a fourth episode without an exception is rejected; `--compare` admits up to the class's break-even count; every result carries `screening: sampling-control`, the planning step and the latency label; `model-privileged` follows the two-condition rule.
- [ ] The default latency is one control period (the command of tick k acts at tick k + 1), verified in the control-record chain; `--latency zero` runs and is labelled `zero-delay`.
- [ ] `--demonstrations` writes a demonstration set whose rows equal the control ticks of the episodes, with observations from the policy namespace only.
- [ ] The verb is in the registry with schemas and an example, reachable through `frob --json`, the daemon and the MCP tool; cancellation of a running job drains and leaves no planning-arena memory allocated.
- [ ] The measured cost per 20 s episode on the development machine is recorded for the quadruped setting with its disclosure block (the thresholds are judged at the gate).

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-558 (model steps per tick N_s·H·N_it: 65,536; 204,800; 40,500; the verb's pricing must print these for the three published settings), KA-559 (299 episodes for a 1% bound at 95%: printed when a request asks for evaluation-sized episode counts).
- Counterexamples carried: CX-245, CX-233, CX-009 (the sampling controller as an evaluator).
- Negative controls: a controller that writes warm-start state back to the plant from a planning world (the fork-count test fails); perturbations drawn from an unaddressed generator (the replay test fails); a result missing its screening label (schema validation fails); the planted acceptance request.
- Metamorphic: with σ₀ = 0 the controller applies its nominal plan and its digest equals a run with N_s = 1; increasing N_s never changes sample 0.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; across arena capacities; debug and release.

## Considerations
- Kill gate KILL-MPC (decided at M4 by BD-331): if the gate fails after one repair cycle, the sampling controller is kept as a single-world CPU debugging aid and demonstrations come from the baseline and from specialists. The verb then refuses batched tiers and multi-design requests, and the fallback route in synthesis reports is removed.
- Its plant equals its model. Everything it reports is conditional on that, which is why the label is default-on and why two conditions, not one, clear it.
- Memory scales with N_s per controlled world; the verb's admission refuses a setting that does not fit the device, naming the largest N_s that does.
- The planner's coarse step can exploit model error between the planning and plant step plans; the predicted-against-realized gap is the diagnostic, and a large gap is reported, not hidden in the verdict.
- Published results are sensitive to the spline family and to hand-built references; both are fixed and declared here, so a failure is a finding about the stated configuration.
