## Background
A baseline controller is synthesized from a design's model in seconds, and it is for feedback, not for peak performance. When it fails for a reason that lies in the controller and not in the body, the plan climbs a ladder of control rungs, each tried only when the rung below has failed for a recorded reason that is not a design refusal, and each naming the evidence it needs:

| Rung | Controller | Cost per design (TARGET) | Escalate when |
|---|---|---|---|
| R0 | Synthesized baseline | Under 5 s of CPU | `synthesized-failed` with a control cause in the failure taxonomy |
| R1 | Baseline with a handful of gains tuned by derivative-free search | Under 2 min on a fast tier | Tracking or recovery limits remain after tuning |
| R2 | Baseline plus bounded residual | At most 20% of a specialist's training steps | `residual-saturated`, or the class has no baseline |
| R3 | Whole-body inverse dynamics plus residual | As R2 | The task needs contact sequences the baseline cannot schedule |
| R4 | Specialist policy trained end to end, or a family policy fine-tuned | One training run | Finalists only |

Rungs R0 and R1, the residual wrapper, the whole-body program and the read verb `control.ladder` exist from M3 (BD-116, N22a-19, N22a-16, N22a-14, N22a-18). What M4 owes is the learned rungs as something that actually runs, and the number that decides whether they deserve to exist. The residual command is u = limit(u_base(x̂, t) + Δ(o)) with |Δⱼ| ≤ ρⱼ times the channel's range and a zero-initialized output layer, so training starts from the baseline's behaviour (decision ADR-REFLEX-01: an inspectable baseline first; a residual may be added and never replaces the baseline silently, so that a failure can be assigned to the body, the baseline or the residual).

The hypothesis under test: a policy that only corrects a working baseline reaches a specialist's return in a small fraction of the specialist's training steps (TARGET 20%). The kill gate KILL-RESIDUAL (decided at M4): trigger, the pilot's falsifier holds (the pilot on an incumbent tool found the residual needs more than 50% of the specialist's steps for the same return; that is N22a-27), or at M4 the residual rung needs more than 50% of a specialist's steps on the corpus; fallback, named in advance, the residual rung is removed and the baseline remains a behaviour-cloning warm start. A kill gate that fires is not a failure of the program: the fallback is the plan.

Seed done-condition: the residual rung's steps against a specialist's are recorded on the corpus; KILL-RESIDUAL decided.

## Scope
In: the rung runners for R2 and R3 in `frob-control` (preconditions, budget, construction of the wrapped controller, the rung result record the ladder reads); the pre-registered corpus protocol; the step-ratio statistic; the kill-gate measurement, its gate-ledger row and the draft of the decision record; the switch that applies either outcome to the ladder registry; the script and logs.
Out: the wrapper and its bit-level obligation (N22a-16); the whole-body program (N22a-14); R1's search (N22a-19); the ladder verb (N22a-18); the training loop, the residual head and specialists' recipe (the learning subsystem, BD-198); R4 and family policies; the final decision record, which a session that did not implement this work writes from this measurement (the kill-gate decision bead N22c-15 of the unit that drafts the gates subsection; it depends on this bead, so it is listed as related and not as a dependency); distillation from the baseline (BD-323).

## Technical approach
**Rung runners (DESIGN; module `frob-control::ladder`).**
1. *Preconditions, checked against the ledger through the ladder's rules.* R2 is planned for a design and task only when: R1's result exists and says that tracking or recovery limits remain after tuning; the failure is a control cause, not a design refusal (no checked infeasibility certificate, no `actuator-infeasible` marking, no design-check finding as first cause); the evidence the class row requires is present; the rung is `available` (its pilot recorded `fund`, its kill gate has not fired). R3 is planned when R2's result carries `residual-saturated`, or when the class has no baseline at all.
2. *Base controller.* R2: the R1-tuned baseline (or R0's if R1 changed nothing). R3: the whole-body program controller (unknowns joint accelerations, contact wrenches and joint torques; constraints the equations of motion, the four-dimensional contact cone, the actuator box at the current speed, joint limits; a prioritized list of task residuals with slack), driven by the baseline's gait clock where one exists and by a posture and centroidal reference where the class has none.
3. *Wrapped controller.* `ResidualWrapper` over the base, authority ρ per channel from the controller record (default 0.3), residual policy declared `untrained` and verified to output exactly zero on a probe batch. Its `ControllerId` covers the base record, ρ, the limiter and the policy identity.
4. *Budget.* The operational cap of the rung is 0.2·S_class, where S_class is the class reference: the median attainment step count of specialists of that morphology class and task family in the ledger. With no reference in the ledger the rung refuses to start and names the missing reference (fail closed: a rung without a denominator cannot say it was cheap).
5. *Training.* The wrapped controller is handed to the learning subsystem as an environment stage: the learner's action is the raw residual a ∈ [−1, 1]ⁿ, the applied command is logged beside it, and evaluation looks run at a registered cadence on a development stream disjoint from training worlds. Training stops at attainment of the task's level or at the cap.
6. *Rung result record* (`frob.control.rung/1`, written to the ledger, read by `control.ladder`): rung; design; task; base and wrapped `ControllerId`; policy identity; steps used and the cap; `attained` with its look, or `budget-exhausted`; return and requirement verdict; `residual_use` per channel; the flag `residual-saturated` with channel and pinned fraction when a channel sits at its bound for more than the declared fraction of ticks (default 0.2); fraction of ticks per layer (`baseline+residual`, `shield-filtered`, `fallback:<reason>`, `backup`); the comparison kind; outcome `ok`, `residual-saturated`, `budget-exhausted` or `failed:<taxonomy class>`; standing `screening`. A saturated result says in words that the baseline is not the controller for this result.

**The corpus protocol (committed to the pre-registration record before the first run; a later edit is refused by the record).**
1. *Population.* Corpus designs with frozen labels that pass the design check, belong to a morphology class whose baseline passed validation on the corpus, and carry no planted control-relevant defect. A stratified sample of 30 (DESIGN default: by class in proportion to the corpus, at least 8 in each of the two largest classes, at most 4 from the costliest class). The planted-defect designs are run as a separate control stratum.
2. *Arms, three seeds each, on paired world addresses.* Specialist: the reference recipe from scratch with the class budget B. Residual (R2): the wrapped controller with ρ = 0.3 under the same recipe, network body, observation schema and environment count, also allowed to run to B so that ratios up to one are measured (the operational cap of 0.2 is a rule of the rung, not of the measurement).
3. *Level.* For design d, R*_d = 0.95 times the specialist's final return (mean over seeds).
4. *Attainment.* S is the step count of the first look at which a procedure valid for the registered cadence rejects "at or below R*_d" (level series αₖ = 0.05/[k·(k + 1)]; a first crossing alone is descriptive and is recorded as `crossing`). Runs that do not attain within B are right-censored at B.
5. *Per-design ratio.* r_d = median over seeds of S_res divided by median over seeds of S_spec. A design whose baseline alone already attains R*_d is recorded `baseline-suffices` with r_d = 0 and is excluded from the kill statistic (there is nothing for a residual to learn; including such designs would pull the median toward zero for free). A design whose residual arm is censored in two or more seeds has r_d recorded as "at least 1".
6. *Statistic.* The median of r_d over eligible designs with a 95% bootstrap interval (10⁴ resamples over designs, addressed seed); the per-class medians; the share of designs with r_d ≤ 0.2 (the rung's TARGET).
7. *Kill rule.* KILL-RESIDUAL's M4 clause holds when the median of r_d exceeds 0.5.
8. *Control stratum.* Every planted-defect design must end `residual-saturated`, `actuator-infeasible`, `budget-exhausted` or failed. One that attains cleanly is listed as `defect-masked` and must be explained (a wrong label, or a defect that is not control-relevant under a residual) before the row is read as a pass: a residual that hides an undersized knee is the failure the bounded authority exists to prevent.
9. *R3.* For designs whose R2 run ended `residual-saturated`, R3 is run with the same budget rule and its r_d is recorded. Reported; not part of the kill statistic.
10. *Comparison kind.* `retuned-equal-resources`: each body's residual is trained under the same budget rule. Stated in every table.
11. *Cost (DESIGN estimate).* About 40 GPU-hours on the two RTX 4090, roughly halved where specialists of the rank-agreement calibration set are reused.

**Decision rule, record and consequences.**
- The measurement, the per-design table and the statistic are written to the gate ledger as the M4 clause of KILL-RESIDUAL, with commit, hardware fingerprint and seeds, and a draft decision record (`docs/adr/KILL-RESIDUAL.md`) citing both clauses (the pilot's f and this median).
- *If the gate fires:* `registries/control_ladder.toml` marks R2 and R3 `available: false` with the decision cited, so `control.ladder` recommends R4 after R1 and says which rungs were skipped and why; the rung runners refuse to start; the residual head is withdrawn from the list of trainable heads over a baseline; the baseline remains a warm start through behaviour cloning (supervised imitation of the baseline on states the student visits); the wrapper is kept only as the test article of its zero-residual obligation.
- *If it does not fire:* R2 and R3 are `available: true`; the measured median and interquartile range of r_d replace the TARGET in the ladder's output, with provenance; classes whose own median exceeds 0.5 are listed, and the ladder marks R2 unavailable for those classes only if the owner amends the register to allow a per-class reading (as written, the gate is decided on the corpus as a whole).

**Evidence.** Claim: Design comparison (a population statement about the corpus sample). Class `Tested`. Determinism: training runs are class D2 per device family; pairing is by world address.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] R2 and R3 run from the `frob` binary on a design whose R1 result justifies them, and write a rung result record that `frob control ladder` then reports; they refuse to start for a design refusal, for missing evidence, with no class reference, or when the rung is unavailable, each with the reason.
- [ ] The operational cap is 0.2·S_class and is enforced: a run stops at the cap with `budget-exhausted`.
- [ ] A planted residual that pins one channel sets `residual-saturated`, and the ladder then recommends R3.
- [ ] The protocol record is committed before the first corpus run; a planted edit of the kill threshold after the first result is refused.
- [ ] The residual rung's steps against a specialist's are recorded on the corpus (the seed's done-condition, first half): every sampled design has S_spec, S_res or censoring marks per seed, r_d, and its eligibility status; the tally sums to the sample size.
- [ ] The statistic (median, interval, per-class medians, share at or below 0.2) is computed by the script and recomputed by an independent implementation in the conformance crate, agreeing to 10⁻⁹.
- [ ] The M4 clause of KILL-RESIDUAL is recorded with the measured median against 0.5, and the draft decision record exists with both clauses (second half of the done-condition; the decision itself is recorded by the decision bead from this measurement).
- [ ] Whichever way the decision goes, the registry switch is applied and verified by the script through `frob control ladder`.
- [ ] Experiment controls are recorded: an arm with ρ = 0 never attains on eligible designs; an arm over a mismatched baseline (another class's controller) does not attain faster than the specialist; relabelling the control stratum as healthy changes no run.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`crates/frob-control/tests/ladder_rungs.rs` and `crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_residual.rs`; real controllers and real short training runs on `batch-cpu` with a small registered test recipe (labelled as such); no mocks.
- `r2_requires_r1_control_cause`; `r2_refused_for_design_refusal`; `r2_refused_without_class_reference`; `r2_refused_when_unavailable`.
- `budget_is_fifth_of_class_reference`: S_class = 50,000,000 → cap 10,000,000; a run stops there.
- `untrained_residual_is_exactly_zero_at_start`.
- `saturation_sets_flag_and_escalates_to_r3`; `r3_base_is_whole_body_program`.
- `rung_record_schema_complete`; `ladder_reads_rung_record`.
- `per_design_ratio`: S_res = (12, 15, 20)·10⁶ and S_spec = (60, 70, 80)·10⁶ → r_d = 15/70 = 0.2143.
- `censoring_rules`: two censored residual seeds → "at least 1"; a baseline that attains alone → `baseline-suffices`, excluded.
- `median_and_bootstrap_reproducible`: the same seed gives the same interval; the ratios (0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.6, 0.9) have median 0.3 and do not fire; (0.4, 0.6, 0.7, 0.9, 1.0) have median 0.7 and fire.
- `defect_masked_is_listed`.
- `protocol_record_refuses_late_edit`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/reflex_residual_rung.sh`, in three parts. (a) Smoke: builds a quadruped variant with a planted swing-tracking defect, runs `frob control synth`, the R1 search, then `frob train start --design <d> --task traverse-5m-flat --controller baseline+residual --rho 0.3 --rung R2 --json` with the test recipe, and asserts the rung record and the answer of `frob control ladder <d> --json`. (b) Corpus: registers the protocol (`frob study plan`), runs or resumes both arms over the sample and the control stratum, reads `frob train status` looks, computes the statistic through the conformance tool, writes the gate row and the draft decision record. (c) Switch: applies the registry change for the measured outcome and asserts the ladder's output. Logging through the shared helper to `target/e2e-logs/reflex_residual_rung/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: per run and look `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim Design comparison, class `Tested`, tier, determinism, identities of design, base and wrapped controllers and policy, seeds), training steps, return with its lower bound, `crossing` and `attained`; per design a `metric` line with S_spec, S_res, r_d and status; then `metric` lines for the median against 0.5 and against the 0.2 TARGET with the interval, and the control stratum's outcomes. Final `summary` with `gates: ["KILL-RESIDUAL"]`. Design contents are not logged, only identities and outcomes.
### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-557 holds on every wrapped controller before training starts (a zero residual reproduces the baseline's digests); the script checks it on the smoke design.
- Arithmetic fixed before the runs: the kill threshold 0.5, the TARGET 0.2, the level series, the ceiling (training steps per design fall by at most 1/r, on the training phase only, for classes that have a baseline).
- Negative controls: the ρ = 0 arm; the mismatched baseline; a tally that drops censored designs from the median (rejected by the conformance check); a statistic computed on first crossings instead of attainment (the conformance check recomputes from the look series and disagrees).
- Metamorphic: permuting the order of designs changes no count; re-running with the same seeds reproduces S values exactly in class D2.
- Determinism lanes: the smoke part twice on one machine and across worker counts; a five-design subset of the corpus part twice with identical look series.

## Considerations
- The exclusion of `baseline-suffices` designs and the sample size are defaults of this bead; the plan says only "on the corpus". Both are fixed in the registration, not after the data. If the owner intends the median over all sampled designs, the registration must say so before the first run.
- The pilot and this measurement are two clauses of one kill gate. If the pilot's falsifier held, this bead does not run its corpus part; its rung runners are not built, and only the registry switch is applied.
- Risk (RISK-17): a controller can mask a design flaw. The bounded authority, the saturation flag, the `actuator-infeasible` marking and the control stratum are the countermeasures; none of them proves the absence of masking outside the corpus.
- A residual's training provenance (steps, seeds, determinism class) is carried in the policy's manifest; a wrapped controller's results are `Tested` in the model and say nothing about reality.
- The M4 exit review recomputes the statistic from the logs in a fresh-context session; it does not re-run training unless a digest differs.
