## Background
A designer asks what a longer thigh does to a trot's cost of transport. One way to answer is to change the thigh, re-simulate until the gait settles, and difference: two settled rollouts per parameter, each many strides long, and the answer is the difference of two noisy numbers. The gait crate answers from the orbit itself. A gait is a fixed point x̄* of the reduced stride map P̄_θ, so the implicit-function theorem gives the motion of the fixed point directly. At a fixed point with I − M nonsingular, where M = D P̄(x̄*) is the monodromy,

    d x̄*/dθ = (I − M)⁻¹·∂P̄/∂θ.

For a metric J(x̄*, θ) accumulated over the stride (cost of transport, peak torque margin, friction utilization),

    dJ/dθ = ∂J/∂θ + λᵀ·∂P̄/∂θ   with   (I − M)ᵀ·λ = ∇J.

It costs one more tangent world per design parameter and one small solve (proof obligation PO-GAIT-02: design gradients agree with central differences away from labeled non-smooth points; verification, the 100-case suite of GATE-GAIT-02). It is refused at a fold (FROB-REF-431), where I − M is singular and the fixed point has no derivative. The gradient of a multiplier is taken by differences along the continuation path. Where a gradient is refused, the caller is routed to derivative-free search in the design studio.

The other half of this bead's title is the rule that makes the number usable (decision D-15): every derivative carries its validity label, one of `smooth`, `saltation`, `discrete-step`, `fixed-mode`, `one-sided`, `generalized`, `smoothed`, `finite-difference`, `refused`, and a query near grazing or at an ambiguous event order is refused by name. A gradient an optimizer can trust is one that says when it cannot be trusted. Simultaneous touchdowns tied by a symmetry are not a reason to refuse (decision ADR-REFLEX-05).

Ceiling, stated before any speed claim: one linear solve replaces 2·n finite-difference rollouts for n design parameters, on periodic gaits over regular ground only. Tier Frontier. The gate (GATE-GAIT-02, Known answer, M4): on a 100-case suite, in at least 72 of 80 regular cases the gradient's cosine against central differences is at least 0.95; all 20 grazing and fold controls are labeled or refused; no case is refused for simultaneity alone; the bar fixture returns rest, v/4 and the sign of the coupling term.

Seed done-condition: GATE-GAIT-02.

## Technical approach
**Algorithm.**
1. *Orbit.* Take a stored `GaitOrbit` (by identity) or solve one. Its monodromy M, its label and its selections come with it. If its Jacobian was `refused`, the call returns that refusal.
2. *Fold test.* Compute the smallest singular value s_min of I − M in the dimensionless chart. If s_min ≤ τ_fold (DESIGN default 10⁻⁴, recorded), the gradient is refused with FROB-REF-431: payload s_min, the near-null direction in named coordinates, and one-sided estimates (secant slopes of x̄* and of each metric on either side, taken from a continuation path through the point when one exists). Exception: when the singular direction lies in a non-trivial symmetry block and the requested parameter directions preserve the gait's spatial group K, the solve is done in the trivial block alone and the result notes the singular block; a parameter direction that breaks K is refused there.
3. *Parameter derivatives.* For each requested parameter θᵢ: ∂P̄/∂θᵢ by the two-step central difference in the parameter (four parameter-variant worlds), with its label.
4. *Metric partials.* ∇_x̄ J and ∂J/∂θᵢ by central differences of the stride metrics that the same tangent worlds already report; no extra worlds.
5. *Adjoint solve.* (I − M)ᵀ·λ = ∇J by LU with partial pivoting and one step of iterative refinement, once per metric. Then dJ/dθᵢ = ∂J/∂θᵢ + λᵀ·∂P̄/∂θᵢ. On request, d x̄*/dθᵢ = (I − M)⁻¹·∂P̄/∂θᵢ, one solve per parameter.
6. *Label of a gradient.* The weakest label among those of M, ∂P̄/∂θᵢ and the metric partials. If M is `generalized` (several selection Jacobians at a simultaneous impact), one gradient is computed per selection; selections that agree within 10⁻³ relative are merged, and otherwise the set is returned with `generalized`, the number of selections and which ordering each belongs to.
7. *Non-smooth metrics.* A peak torque margin is a minimum over ticks and joints, a friction utilization a maximum. Where one argument is active by a clear margin the gradient is that argument's. Where two are within 1% of each other the result is `generalized` with one gradient per candidate.
8. *Multiplier gradients.* From a continuation path record through the orbit: multipliers at neighbouring accepted points are matched by block and continuity and differenced along the path; label `finite-difference` with the path step. Without a path, a two-point continuation of ±δ in the parameter is run first.
9. *Routing.* Every refused entry carries `route: derivative-free` with the parameter box and the name of the studio's search verb.

**Cost.** Beyond the orbit: 4 worlds per parameter over one stride, one LU of an n × n matrix shared by all metrics, one back-substitution per metric. TARGET for the reference trot with six parameters and three metrics: under 1 s of CPU after the orbit.

**The verb.**
```toml
[[verb]]
name = "gait.gradient"
cluster = "gait"
kind = "run"
tier = "F"
capabilities = ["design:read", "run:write"]
input_schema = "frob.gait.gradient.in/1"
output_schema = "frob.gait.gradient.out/1"
refusals = ["FROB-REF-431"]
idempotent = true
cost_model = "orbit (if absent) + 4 worlds per parameter + one n×n solve"
first_milestone = "M4"
task_clusters = ["gaits"]
examples = ["frob gait gradient orb:… --params thigh_len,shank_len,body_mass --metrics cot,torque_margin --json"]
```
Input: an orbit identity, or a design, gait and tier from which one is solved; `--params` or `--edit <typed edit>` (the parameters the edit changes); `--metrics`; `--multipliers`; `--state-sensitivity`; `--check fd` (re-solve the orbit at θ ± δ and report the cosine); budget and idempotency key. Output: per metric and parameter the value with units, label, method, step sizes, the selection it belongs to, and the check's cosine when run; the fold margin s_min against τ_fold; refusals with payload and repairs; the route for refused entries; the orbit identity and standing `screening: gait-orbit`. Evidence block: claim none; class `Tested`; tier; determinism D2; identities; `reality: no-claim`. Exit 0; 2 when some entries are refused or `generalized` (completed with findings); 3 when the whole request is refused; 8 when accepted and still running.

**The 100-case suite (frozen in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/gait_gradient_suite.toml` before any regular case is first run; thresholds τ_graze, τ_fold and the tolerances are frozen in the same file).** Each case names its fixture, tier, design point, at least three parameters (so that a cosine is informative), its metrics and its class.

| Class | Count | Content (DESIGN composition) |
|---|---|---|
| Regular | 16 | Rimless wheels with 6, 8, 10 and 16 spokes at four slopes above each wheel's critical slope; parameters (γ, l, α); metric ω*²; closed-form gradient |
| Regular | 8 | Compass gait at eight parameter points; parameters slope, mass ratio, leg length |
| Regular | 16 | Planar biped under a clock-forced baseline on the event-located profile where available, else compliant |
| Regular | 24 | Quadruped trot on the compliant tier; parameters among thigh length, shank length, body mass, hip width, gear ratio, foot friction; metrics cost of transport, torque margin, friction utilization |
| Regular | 8 | Quadruped bound (touchdowns in mirror pairs) |
| Regular | 8 | Hexapod tripod (three feet at once) |
| Grazing control | 10 | Two balls whose apex touches a ceiling; four quadruped strides with swing clearance set to scuff; four biped toe-stubs |
| Fold control | 10 | Four saddle-node maps within τ_fold of the fold; three rimless wheels at the critical slope where the rolling fixed point ceases to clear its apex; three gait orbits at a fold located by continuation |

*Pass rule.* (a) Regular: g is the gradient over the case's parameters, scaled by the parameter scales; g_fd is the central difference obtained by re-solving the orbit at θ ± δ with events re-located; the case passes when g·g_fd/(‖g‖·‖g_fd‖) ≥ 0.95. A regular case that is refused counts as a miss. At least 72 of 80 must pass. (b) Controls: each of the 20 must return `refused` (FROB-REF-431) or a gradient labelled `one-sided` or `generalized`; an ordinary label on a control fails the gate. (c) No regular case with a symmetry-tied simultaneous touchdown (the 40 trot, bound and tripod cases) may be refused with simultaneity as the only reason. (d) The bar fixture returns rest for the coupled solve, v/4 for the ordered solve at β = 1/3, and the sign of the coupling term (1 − β)/m.

**Determinism and evidence.** Class D2 on `batch-cpu` in `f64`. A gradient is `Tested`; its label is part of the result. The gate's claim is Known answer.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Affine fixture P(x) = A·x + B·θ with A = [[0.5, 0.2], [0, 0.3]], B = (1, 1), J = x₁: dJ/dθ = 18/7 = 2.571429 with λ = (2, 4/7), within 10⁻⁹; d x̄*/dθ = (18/7, 10/7).
- [ ] Rimless wheel, eight spokes, l = 0.5 m, γ = 4°: the gradient of ω*² with respect to (γ, l, α) is (29.95984 s⁻²/rad, −4.189992 s⁻²/m, −11.70220 s⁻²/rad) within 10⁻⁵ relative, and the multiplier's gradient with respect to α from path differences is −2·sin(4α) = −2 within 10⁻⁴.
- [ ] Saddle-node map at λ = 10⁻¹⁰: refused with FROB-REF-431, s_min in the payload, one-sided estimates present, the three ranked repairs (use the one-sided estimates returned; move the parameter; derivative-free search), exit 3.
- [ ] A scuffing stride: refused with FROB-REF-431 carrying the grazing payload from the Jacobian.
- [ ] A trot with regular clearance: gradients for six parameters and three metrics are returned, none refused; where selections differ the result is `generalized` with each selection's gradient.
- [ ] A torque-margin metric with two joints within 1% of each other is `generalized` with two gradients.
- [ ] With `--check fd` the reported cosine on the reference trot is at least 0.95.
- [ ] The suite file exists with 100 cases in the stated classes and its thresholds, committed before the first regular run; a planted edit of a threshold after the first run is refused by the registry's pre-registration check.
- [ ] GATE-GAIT-02 at unit level: at least 72 of 80 regular cases pass; 20 of 20 controls are labeled or refused; zero simultaneity-only refusals; the bar clause holds. The measured counts are written with the gate row (the seed's done-condition; the running-system record is N22a-39's).
- [ ] The gate's negative control is shown to fail: a build with τ_graze = τ_fold = 0 returns ordinary gradients on the controls and fails (b).
- [ ] The verb is in the registry with schemas and an example, reachable through `frob --json`, the daemon and the MCP tool `gait_gradient` with identical envelopes; cancellation drains and frees the tangent batch.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-564 (the 100-case suite: at least 72 of 80 regular at cosine 0.95; all 20 controls labeled or refused; none refused for simultaneity alone); KA-563 (the bar: rest, v/4, coupling sign); KA-560 (rimless wheel, with the closed-form gradient derived from it); KA-097 (validation against perturbations with events re-located).
- Counterexamples: CX-238 (simultaneous impacts in a fixed order), CX-043 (a derivative at grazing).
- Negative controls: zero thresholds; an implementation that solves with M restricted to Fix(K) for a symmetry-breaking parameter (the cosine collapses on the asymmetric-leg cases); a gradient that omits ∂J/∂θ (fails cases whose metric depends on the parameter directly, such as mass in cost of transport); a check that compares against an un-re-solved finite difference.
- Metamorphic: scaling a parameter's unit scales its gradient entry inversely; the gradient along a typed edit equals the directional derivative assembled from its parameters; mirroring the design mirrors the gradient.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts and batch compositions; debug and release.

## Considerations
- The composition of the suite is a default; the plan fixes only the counts (80 and 20), the thresholds and the clauses. It is frozen before data, and thresholds are tuned on control cases only: a regular case that a too-cautious threshold refuses is a miss, so over-refusal cannot pass the gate.
- Cases on the rimless wheel and the compass gait are autonomous loops; they run through the event-section path, which is owned by the autonomous-orbit beads. If that path is not yet available the 24 cases are reported `not run` and the gate cannot pass: 72 of 80 leaves room for eight misses, not twenty-four.
- Risk RISK-47 (search or gradients exploit a contact-order or model-form artifact): the label, the selections and the fold margin are the countermeasures here; rank robustness of finalists under other contact laws is checked by the studio, not by this verb.
- If the gait kill gate fires, gradients remain available as diagnostics with standing `diagnostic`; nothing ranks designs by them.
- The referee route for these derivatives waits on upstream work; until then event-located cases run on the workspace's own event-located profile and on host fixtures, and the gate record names the adjudicator.
