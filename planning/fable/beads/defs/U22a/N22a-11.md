## Background
GATE-REFLEX-01 (Known answer) is the standing gate of the baseline controllers: each archetype stands for 10 s and traverses 5 m of flat ground under its baseline controller. It is staged by milestone. At M1: the first archetypes (`quad-mammal`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive` and `mecanum`), on the `referee` tier only, restricted to those whose contact law exists at M1 and whose contact count fits the referee's 21-contact cap. At M2: the same set on `batch-cpu`. At M3: all eighteen archetypes on `batch-cpu`, except tracked and hybrid archetypes, which follow at M4 with their contact laws.

This bead is the script and the measurement for the legged row at M1: the reference quadruped on the referee. A script never writes a gate record; its tier run produces a receipt, and the M1 part of the gate is recorded from a receipt by its one recorder, N22c-01 (the register names "BD-118 with N22c-01" at M1). It is written from the user's side: an agent with only the `frob` binary creates the design, asks for a controller, runs the two behaviours and reads the verdicts. The same script carries the planted failures that prove the row can fail: a body that cannot stand must come back as `not-synthesizable` with a witness, never as a fallen robot recorded as a controller failure, and never as a pass. The wheeled rows are recorded by the wheeled controller's script and the overall M1 record by the synthesis verb's bead; the M2 `batch-cpu` row of this archetype is N22a-13.

## Scope
In: `scripts/e2e/reflex_legged_baseline.sh`, its fixtures (seeds, the two traverse tasks at their commanded speeds, the weak-knee design file), assertions, structured log; the legged case `gate_reflex_01_legged_referee` for (`quad-mammal`, `referee`, M1) under the GATE-REFLEX-01 entry of `g1_known_answers`, with its registered negative controls, and the tier run that writes its receipt.
Out: the record of the M1 part of GATE-REFLEX-01 (N22c-01, the gate's one recorder at M1, enters it from its tier run's receipt, which carries this bead's legged case); the controller and its parts (BD-116 and its dependencies); the synthesis verb and report (BD-118, N22a-12); the other archetypes' cases (`humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`) and the verb-level script over the four first archetypes (N22c-01, which depends on this bead and checks its own `quad-mammal` measurements against this bead's receipt); the M2 rows (N22a-13 for `quad-mammal` on `batch-cpu`, N22c-47 for the other three) and the M3 rows (BD-247).

## Technical approach
**Steps** (plain shell; idempotent by idempotency keys; every seed explicit; sources the shared end-to-end helper). Every run or mutate call carries its own key, `rlb-<step>-<variant>-<seed>`, distinct per step, gait and seed (KNOWN_DRIFTS 5), so that no call returns an earlier call's stored receipt. The design is positional on every verb; the gait is a synthesis choice and the commanded speed a task parameter.
1. `frob design new e2e-quad --template quad-mammal --key rlb-1-design --json`; `frob design check e2e-quad --json` must complete with no refusal.
2. `frob control synth e2e-quad --key rlb-2-auto --json`: assert outcome `ok`; class `legged`; a `ControllerId` (`<c-auto>`); a static-support witness; the chosen gait; synthesis CPU time (logged against the 5 s TARGET); standing `screening`. Then `frob control synth e2e-quad --gait walk --key rlb-2-walk --json` and `--gait trot --key rlb-2-trot` give `<c-walk>` and `<c-trot>`, each outcome `ok`.
3. `frob control show e2e-quad --json`: assert the record lists gains with their derivation inputs, the stance program with its iteration count and fallback, the schedule, `phase_source: clock`, the latency model and `group: none`.
4. Stand: for seeds 1 to 5, `frob sim run e2e-quad --tier referee --task stand-10s --controller <c-auto> --state privileged --seed <s> --key rlb-4-stand-<s> --json`. Assert the requirement verdict passes: 10 s elapsed; base height within ±10% of nominal; tilt below 5°; no contact by any body other than a foot; fraction of ticks in layer `baseline` equal to 1.
5. Traverse: for seeds 1 to 5 and for each of (`<c-walk>` on `traverse-5m-flat-0.3`, `<c-trot>` on `traverse-5m-flat-0.6`; the two tasks are the registered `traverse-5m-flat` with its commanded speed fixed at 0.3 m/s and 0.6 m/s, task files under `fixtures/reflex/` added by this bead): `frob sim run e2e-quad --tier referee --task <task> --controller <c> --state privileged --seed <s> --key rlb-5-<gait>-<s> --json`. Assert that the run header's `ControllerId` is the one named; displacement of at least 5 m along the commanded direction within twice the nominal time; no fall; fallback fraction 0; the friction-utilization and saturation reports are present.
6. Estimated state: repeat one traverse (`<c-trot>`, seed 1) with `--state estimated --key rlb-6-trot-1`; assert it completes and that the report prints the estimated-versus-privileged difference in distance and success.
7. Planted body defect: `frob design new e2e-quad-weak --file fixtures/reflex/e2e-quad-weak.fbot --key rlb-7-design --json` (the quad-mammal design with every knee actuator replaced by the card `fixtures/reflex/knee_weak.fcard`), then `frob control synth e2e-quad-weak --key rlb-7-synth --json`. Assert outcome `not-synthesizable`, a certificate whose exact check is reported as passed, and a binding set naming a knee torque box. Exit code 2 (completed with findings), not 3 and not 0.
8. Planted class defect: a three-legged variant, `frob design new e2e-quad-3leg --template quad-mammal --key rlb-8-design --json` and the typed edit `frob design edit e2e-quad-3leg remove-limb <one hind leg's instance identifier, as frob design show e2e-quad-3leg --json lists it> --key rlb-8-edit --json`: `frob control synth e2e-quad-3leg --key rlb-8-synth --json` returns `not-synthesizable` with the reason N22a-10 states ("no gait in the atlas satisfies static stability with these leg counts").
9. Planted controller defect: a starved stance solver. The iteration count is a field of the controller record, which is the controller's only input (N22a-07), and `control.synth` takes no option that overrides it (its inputs are the design, an optional gait and task class), so this control runs on a mutation build of the binary in which the stance program's iteration count is 1 (built and run by the script through the end-to-end standard's mutation step, like the two mutation builds of the acceptance criteria). On it: `frob control synth e2e-quad --gait trot --key rlb-9-synth --json`, then the trot traverse of seed 1 under the controller it returns (`--key rlb-9-trot-1`). Assert that the `ControllerId` differs from `<c-trot>`, that the run's report shows a nonzero fallback fraction with reasons `fallback:unconverged:stance`, and that the result is not reported as a clean `baseline` traverse.
10. Determinism: the trot traverse of seed 1 is run twice under distinct keys (`rlb-10-a`, `rlb-10-b`); state digests at the end and at ten intermediate checkpoints are equal.
11. Tier run (step `receipt`): `FROB_TEST_SEED=1 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-REFLEX-01`. The entry holds this bead's case `gate_reflex_01_legged_referee` (`quad-mammal` on the referee: stand-10s and the two traverses over seeds 1 to 5, under both state sources) and, under `gauntlet-mutations`, its registered negative controls (the friction pyramid and the footstep rule without the capture-point term, the two mutation builds of the last criterion); it writes one hash-named receipt under `target/gauntlet/receipts/`. The script asserts on the receipt's `gates` line and `controls` (each `caught`), and that each of its measured values (minimum stand margin, minimum distance, worst fallback fraction, synthesis time) agrees with the receipt's for the same seed. The script writes no gate row: N22c-01 records the M1 part from its tier run's receipt, which carries this case, and fails when its own `quad-mammal` measurements disagree with this bead's receipt.

**Contact capacity.** Four point feet give at most four simultaneous contacts, far inside the referee's cap of 21; the script asserts the peak contact count it observed so that a later foot model with several contact points cannot silently exceed the cap.

**Thresholds (TARGET, from the gate).** Stand 10 s; traverse 5 m.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The script exits 0 on a correct build and non-zero when any assertion fails; a second run is idempotent and reports the same measurements and digests.
- [ ] Five of five seeds stand for 10 s and five of five traverse 5 m in each gait on the referee.
- [ ] Each planted failure is reported for what it is: weak knees and three legs as `not-synthesizable` with witness or reason; the starved solver as a run with visible fallback.
- [ ] The estimated-state traverse completes and its difference from the privileged run is printed.
- [ ] The log at `target/e2e-logs/reflex_legged_baseline/<utc>.ndjson` and its transcript carry every required field, and the `summary` line names GATE-REFLEX-01.
- [ ] The tier run of step 11 writes a receipt whose GATE-REFLEX-01 line carries the legged case's measured values (equal to the script's), commit, fingerprint and seeds, with each registered control `caught`; the script and this bead write no row of `registries/gates.toml`.
- [ ] Mutation check: a build whose friction cone is replaced by a pyramid, and a build whose footstep rule lacks the capture-point term, each change at least one logged measurement beyond its tolerance or fail an assertion; both are registered as the legged case's negative controls and caught in the tier run of step 11.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`scripts/e2e/tests/reflex_legged_baseline_helpers.sh` tests the helper functions on stored envelopes produced by the real binary: extraction of the outcome, the certificate verdict, the layer fractions and the requirement verdict; exact decimal comparison against thresholds; comparison of the script's measurements with a receipt's line. The case `gate_reflex_01_legged_referee` is tested in `crates/frob-conformance/tests/` on stored run records: its verdict on values just inside and just outside the stand and traverse thresholds.
### End-to-end script and logging
Script: `scripts/e2e/reflex_legged_baseline.sh`. Log: one JSON object per line with `ts`, `script` = `reflex_legged_baseline`, `bead` = `N22a-11`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim Known answer, class `Tested`, tier `referee`, determinism class, identities: design, model, `ControllerId`, state-source kind, task; seed), measured values with units and thresholds (for example `{"metric":"displacement_m","value":5.31,"threshold":5.0,"gait":"trot","seed":3}`), digests at checkpoints for step 10, and on failure the first differing or failing value with the run identifier and the paths of the run's episode file, the envelope and the fixture patches. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed, `gates: ["GATE-REFLEX-01"]`. No scene or trajectory content is logged.
### Known answers and negative controls
- The known answer is the gate itself: stand 10 s; traverse 5 m; on the referee at M1.
- Negative controls in the script: steps 7, 8 and 9. Mutation builds of the last criterion.
- Metamorphic relation checked in the script: the trot traverse with the design mirrored left to right and the lateral command mirrored gives the mirrored final displacement within the referee's round-off envelope.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine (step 10); the binary built in debug and release (digests equal on the referee, class D2).

## Considerations
- If the quadruped's foot contact law or the referee step is not available at M1 for this archetype, the row is recorded `not run` with the reason, exactly as the gate's own restriction allows; it is not replaced by a different tier.
- The humanoid's M1 row depends on a balance controller that the plan places at a later milestone; this script does not cover it and the coverage file of this unit flags the gap.
- Run time (TARGET): under ten minutes on the development machine. The referee is slow by design; five seeds per behaviour is the stated compromise.
- The M1 exit review re-runs the script in a fresh-context session and compares its receipt with the recorded part.
