## Background
GATE-REFLEX-01 (Known answer) is the standing gate of the baseline controllers: each archetype stands for 10 s and traverses 5 m of flat ground under its baseline controller. It is staged by milestone. At M1: the first archetypes (`quad-mammal`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive` and `mecanum`), on the `referee` tier only, restricted to those whose contact law exists at M1 and whose contact count fits the referee's 21-contact cap. At M2: the same set on `batch-cpu`. At M3: all eighteen archetypes on `batch-cpu`, except tracked and hybrid archetypes, which follow at M4 with their contact laws.

This bead is the script and the record for the legged row at M1: the reference quadruped on the referee. It is written from the user's side: an agent with only the `frob` binary creates the design, asks for a controller, runs the two behaviours and reads the verdicts. The same script carries the planted failures that prove the row can fail: a body that cannot stand must come back as `not-synthesizable` with a witness, never as a fallen robot recorded as a controller failure, and never as a pass. The wheeled rows are recorded by the wheeled controller's script and the overall M1 record by the synthesis verb's bead; the M2 `batch-cpu` row of this archetype is N22a-13.

## Scope
In: `scripts/e2e/reflex_legged_baseline.sh`, its fixtures (seeds, the weak-knee and three-leg design patches), assertions, structured log, and the GATE-REFLEX-01 row for (`quad-mammal`, `referee`, M1).
Out: the controller and its parts (BD-116 and its dependencies); the synthesis verb and report (BD-118, N22a-12); the other archetypes' rows; the M2 and M3 rows.

## Technical approach
**Steps** (plain shell; idempotent by idempotency keys; every seed explicit; sources the shared end-to-end helper).
1. `frob design new --template quad-mammal --name e2e-quad --json`; `frob design check e2e-quad --json` must complete with no refusal.
2. `frob control synth e2e-quad --json`: assert outcome `ok`; class `legged`; a `ControllerId`; a static-support witness; the chosen gait; synthesis CPU time (logged against the 5 s TARGET); standing `screening`.
3. `frob control show e2e-quad --json`: assert the record lists gains with their derivation inputs, the stance program with its iteration count and fallback, the schedule, `phase_source: clock`, the latency model and `group: none`.
4. Stand: for seeds 1 to 5, `frob sim run --tier referee --design e2e-quad --task stand-10s --state privileged --seed <s> --json`. Assert the requirement verdict passes: 10 s elapsed; base height within ±10% of nominal; tilt below 5°; no contact by any body other than a foot; fraction of ticks in layer `baseline` equal to 1.
5. Traverse: for seeds 1 to 5 and for each of (`walk` at 0.3 m/s, `trot` at 0.6 m/s): `frob sim run --tier referee --design e2e-quad --task traverse-5m-flat --gait <g> --speed <v> --state privileged --seed <s> --json`. Assert displacement of at least 5 m along the commanded direction within twice the nominal time; no fall; fallback fraction 0; the friction-utilization and saturation reports are present.
6. Estimated state: repeat one traverse (trot, seed 1) with `--state estimated`; assert it completes and that the report prints the estimated-versus-privileged difference in distance and success.
7. Planted body defect: `frob design edit e2e-quad --replace-actuator knee:* --card fixtures/reflex/knee_weak.fcard --as e2e-quad-weak`, then `frob control synth e2e-quad-weak --json`. Assert outcome `not-synthesizable`, a certificate whose exact check is reported as passed, and a binding set naming a knee torque box. Exit code 2 (completed with findings), not 3 and not 0.
8. Planted class defect: a three-legged variant (one leg removed by a typed edit): `frob control synth` returns `not-synthesizable` with the reason that no gait in the table satisfies static stability with this leg count.
9. Planted controller defect: `frob control synth e2e-quad --override stance.iterations=1 --as e2e-quad-starved`, then a traverse. Assert the run's report shows a nonzero fallback fraction with reasons `fallback:unconverged:stance`, and that the result is not reported as a clean `baseline` traverse.
10. Determinism: the trot traverse of seed 1 is run twice; state digests at the end and at ten intermediate checkpoints are equal.
11. Gate record: write the row GATE-REFLEX-01 / `quad-mammal` / `referee` / M1 with measured values (minimum stand margin, minimum distance, worst fallback fraction, synthesis time), commit, hardware fingerprint and seeds; PASS only if steps 2 to 10 pass.

**Contact capacity.** Four point feet give at most four simultaneous contacts, far inside the referee's cap of 21; the script asserts the peak contact count it observed so that a later foot model with several contact points cannot silently exceed the cap.

**Thresholds (TARGET, from the gate).** Stand 10 s; traverse 5 m.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The script exits 0 on a correct build and non-zero when any assertion fails; a second run is idempotent and reports the same measurements and digests.
- [ ] Five of five seeds stand for 10 s and five of five traverse 5 m in each gait on the referee.
- [ ] Each planted failure is reported for what it is: weak knees and three legs as `not-synthesizable` with witness or reason; the starved solver as a run with visible fallback.
- [ ] The estimated-state traverse completes and its difference from the privileged run is printed.
- [ ] The log at `target/e2e-logs/reflex_legged_baseline/<utc>.ndjson` and its transcript carry every required field, and the `summary` line names GATE-REFLEX-01.
- [ ] `registries/gates.toml` holds the row with measured values, commit, fingerprint and seeds, and cites the log.
- [ ] Mutation check, recorded once: a build whose friction cone is replaced by a pyramid, and a build whose footstep rule lacks the capture-point term, each change at least one logged measurement beyond its tolerance or fail an assertion.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`scripts/e2e/tests/reflex_legged_baseline_helpers.sh` tests the helper functions on stored envelopes produced by the real binary: extraction of the outcome, the certificate verdict, the layer fractions and the requirement verdict; exact decimal comparison against thresholds; composition of the gate row.
### End-to-end script and logging
Script: `scripts/e2e/reflex_legged_baseline.sh`. Log: one JSON object per line with `ts`, `script` = `reflex_legged_baseline`, `bead` = `N22a-11`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim Known answer, class `Tested`, tier `referee`, determinism class, identities: design, model, `ControllerId`, state-source kind, task; seed), measured values with units and thresholds (for example `{"metric":"displacement_m","value":5.31,"threshold":5.0,"gait":"trot","seed":3}`), digests at checkpoints for step 10, and on failure the first differing or failing value with the run identifier and the paths of the run's episode file, the envelope and the fixture patches. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed, `gates: ["GATE-REFLEX-01"]`. No scene or trajectory content is logged.
### Known answers and negative controls
- The known answer is the gate itself: stand 10 s; traverse 5 m; on the referee at M1.
- Negative controls in the script: steps 7, 8 and 9. Mutation builds of the last criterion.
- Metamorphic relation checked in the script: the trot traverse with the design mirrored left to right and the lateral command mirrored gives the mirrored final displacement within the referee's round-off envelope.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine (step 10); the binary built in debug and release (digests equal on the referee, class D2).

## Considerations
- If the quadruped's foot contact law or the referee step is not available at M1 for this archetype, the row is recorded `not run` with the reason, exactly as the gate's own restriction allows; it is not replaced by a different tier.
- The humanoid's M1 row depends on a balance controller that the plan places at a later milestone; this script does not cover it and the coverage file of this unit flags the gap.
- Run time (TARGET): under ten minutes on the development machine. The referee is slow by design; five seeds per behaviour is the stated compromise.
- The M1 exit review re-runs the script in a fresh-context session and compares rows.
