## Background
A baseline controller's score for a design is cheap. Whether it means anything about how the design would do under a trained policy is a separate, measurable question, and the plan refuses to assume the answer. The synthesis report therefore carries a standing: a baseline score is `screening` until GATE-REFLEX-04 has passed for the design's class and task family.

GATE-REFLEX-04 (Design comparison; M3 for legged and wheeled classes, M4 for tracked and hybrid): rank agreement of the baseline rung with trained specialists on a calibration set of 30 to 40 designs per morphology class and task family, thresholds fixed before data. A Kendall τ whose lower 95% bound is at least 0.7 lets the rung select; at least 0.5 lets it discard the bottom half; below 0.5 the rung removes only designs that fail outright and its results are labelled `baseline-only: unranked`.

The thresholds were an open question (candidate pairs for "select" and "discard": (0.7, 0.5) and (0.6, 0.3); no published measurement decides between them). The gate register settles it: one pair of thresholds, 0.7 and 0.5, everywhere a rule depends on τ; below 0.5, or with no record, a rung neither selects nor discards. That is the fail-closed default this bead implements: an unmeasured stratum licenses nothing beyond removing outright failures.

Why this matters (risk RISK-27, high): rank agreement between rungs may be too low for cheap rungs to eliminate designs; then most designs are trained anyway and the compute ratio is near one. What published neighbours report does not support agreement of 0.7 near the top (rank correlations of 0.40 overall and 0.50 to 0.83 on three robots for an easier comparison; sparse Kendall τ of 0.236, 0.740 and 0.159 in architecture search with shared weights; FACT, the plan's source register). The baseline is a scorer like any other and has no exemption.

This bead is the Reflex side of the gate: it does not train specialists or compute τ (that experiment is a seed bead of the learning unit, with its pilot at M3); it makes the baseline's reports and every consumer of a baseline score obey the measurement, and it carries the gate in the sense of deciding what the rung may do.

## Scope
In: the `Standing` type on baseline results; its derivation from a rank-agreement record; the validity key of a record; the permissions each standing grants; the labels; the refusal to act beyond a standing; tests with synthetic records.
Out: the experiment (specialists, τ with its bootstrap bound, top-band definition; BD-328 and its pilot BD-310); pre-registration (BD-145); the evaluation ladder's allocation rules (BD-329); the conformal margins of the calibrated layer.

## Technical approach
**The statistic consumed (stated so the record can be checked, not recomputed here).** For n designs scored by two rungs, Kendall τ = (concordant pairs − discordant pairs)/C(n, 2). The gates use τ on the top band: the designs in the top m of either rung, m = max(10, 3·k) for a campaign that keeps k finalists, and the decision is taken on the lower 95% bootstrap bound. A record carries: class, task family, the rung pair (`baseline`, `specialist`), stratum, n, τ, its lower bound, top-k recall, the versions of the controller-synthesis rules, kernels and battery, the pre-registration identity, and the recording session.

**Standing.**
```rust
pub enum Standing {
    Screening { label: &'static str /* "baseline-only: unranked" */, reason: NoRankReason },
    DiscardBottomHalf { record: RankAgreementId, tau_lower: f64 },
    Select { record: RankAgreementId, tau_lower: f64 },
}
```
Derivation for a result with class c, task family f, stratum s:
1. Look up the record for (c, f, baseline against specialist, s) with matching versions. None → `Screening { reason: no-record }`.
2. Record found but made under different synthesis-rule, kernel or battery versions → `Screening { reason: stale-record }`; the stale record is named.
3. Record valid, calibration set smaller than 30 designs → `Screening { reason: under-sized }`.
4. τ lower bound ≥ 0.7 → `Select`; 0.5 ≤ lower bound < 0.7 → `DiscardBottomHalf`; lower bound < 0.5 → `Screening { reason: low-agreement }`.
5. The thresholds are constants read from the pre-registration record; code that passes other thresholds is refused at the boundary (a threshold edited after the first result is refused by the study record).

**What each standing permits (enforced where a baseline score is consumed).**
- `Screening`: the score may be shown and may be used to remove designs that fail outright (reported `not-synthesizable`, or failing the behaviour under the three-outcome report). It may not rank, select or discard among designs that move. Every table that prints such scores carries the label `baseline-only: unranked` in its header.
- `DiscardBottomHalf`: additionally, a consumer may discard designs in the bottom half of the baseline ranking of a stratum (at most ⌊n/2⌋ of n). It may not pick winners.
- `Select`: the baseline ranking may be used to choose among designs.
A consumer asks `standing.permits(Action)`; an action beyond the standing is refused with the ladder's refusal for a rung acting without a record, naming the standing, the record or its absence, and the measurement that would lift it. The evaluation ladder may be stricter than this ceiling; it may not be looser.

**Reports.** The `ok` variant of the synthesis report and every evaluation run under a baseline controller print the standing with its reason or record. Finalists chosen under any standing other than `Select` are described as "survivors", never as "the top k".

**The gate row.** When the measurement for a class and task family arrives (from the pilot at M3 for legged and wheeled classes, from the experiment at M4 for the rest), this bead's check reads the record, verifies its internal consistency (n between 30 and 40 or more; pre-registration identity matches; versions current), derives the standing, and writes the GATE-REFLEX-04 row per class and task family with τ, its lower bound and the resulting mode. The gate "passes" for a stratum when the standing is `DiscardBottomHalf` or `Select`; otherwise the row records `baseline-only: unranked` and nothing downstream is blocked: the rung simply does not eliminate.

**Determinism and evidence.** D0: the standing is an exact function of the record and the version keys. The standing itself is metadata; the claim it qualifies is a Design comparison.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] With no record, a baseline result has standing `Screening` with label `baseline-only: unranked`; an attempt to discard a moving design on its baseline score is refused; removing a `not-synthesizable` design is permitted.
- [ ] Synthetic records with lower bounds 0.72, 0.55 and 0.45 give `Select`, `DiscardBottomHalf` and `Screening { low-agreement }`; the boundary values 0.70 and 0.50 are inclusive on the permissive side as the gate states ("at least").
- [ ] A record made under a different synthesis-rule version, or with 25 designs, gives `Screening` with the reason named.
- [ ] Under `DiscardBottomHalf` at most ⌊n/2⌋ designs of a stratum can be discarded through the API; a request for one more is refused.
- [ ] Thresholds other than those of the pre-registration record are refused.
- [ ] Every synthesis report and baseline evaluation prints the standing; a campaign table produced under `Screening` carries the label in its header and the word "survivors".
- [ ] The GATE-REFLEX-04 row is written per class and task family as records arrive, with τ, its lower bound, n and the mode; a missing measurement is recorded `not run`, not as a pass.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`crates/frob-control/tests/standing.rs`; records are `synthetic` fixtures written through the real ledger writer.
- `no_record_is_screening`; `screening_permits_only_outright_removal`.
- `thresholds_map_to_modes` (0.72, 0.70, 0.55, 0.50, 0.45).
- `stale_or_undersized_record_is_screening`.
- `discard_cap_is_half`.
- `thresholds_come_from_registration`; `late_threshold_edit_refused`.
- `kendall_tau_reference`: rankings (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) and (1, 3, 2, 4, 5) have 9 concordant and 1 discordant pairs, τ = 0.8 (a check that the record's τ field is interpreted with the stated definition).
- `reports_print_standing`; `survivors_not_top_k`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/reflex_standing.sh`: `frob control synth e2e-quad --json` (standing `screening`); `frob eval run` on five designs under the baseline followed by an attempt to discard on score through the campaign verb (expect the refusal); then, with a synthetic record installed in a scratch ledger at lower bound 0.55, the same discard succeeds for the bottom two of five and fails for a third; with 0.72 a selection succeeds. It logs through the shared helper to `target/e2e-logs/reflex_standing/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: each command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block, the standing and its reason, refusals with code and repairs, and a `summary` line naming GATE-REFLEX-04. Synthetic records are labelled `synthetic` in the log and never written to the real ledger.
### Known answers and negative controls
- The thresholds 0.7 and 0.5 on the lower 95% bound; the τ definition; the reference value 0.8 for the five-design example.
- Negative controls: a consumer that reads the point estimate of τ instead of its lower bound (a record with τ = 0.75 and lower bound 0.62 must give `DiscardBottomHalf`, not `Select`); a consumer that treats a missing record as permissive; a record whose thresholds were edited after data.
- Metamorphic: relabelling designs leaves the standing unchanged; adding designs to a campaign does not change the standing of a stratum (it depends on the calibration record only).
- Determinism: D0.

## Considerations
- Milestones: the gate register places this gate at M3 for legged and wheeled classes, while the seed bead that runs the experiment on our engine sits at M4 and its pilot at M3. This bead works with whichever record exists: at M3 the pilot's record if it meets the registration, otherwise `not run`. The mismatch is listed in this unit's coverage file for the lead.
- The gate text says "discard the bottom half"; the studio's fixed allocation table advances the top third in its discard-only mode. The standing here is a ceiling expressed as data (`may discard at most half`); the ladder's owner must reconcile its table with it. Also flagged in the coverage file.
- An earlier default of the chapter ("until the gate has run the baseline rung discards only") is superseded by the gate register's fail-closed rule (with no record a rung neither selects nor discards); "removes only designs that fail outright" is how the two read together.
- If rank agreement is low for every class, the kill gate for rank agreement fires at program level; this bead's behaviour is then simply `Screening` everywhere.
