## Background
The baseline controller's gains and gait constants come from the model through a few fixed ratios (how many control periods per closed-loop period, the damping ratio, the dimensionless stride period, swing height and step length as fractions of leg length, the gain on the capture-point term). Those ratios are defaults, and a body that fails under the defaults may only need different ones. The control ladder's second rung is exactly that: R1, the baseline with a handful of gains tuned by derivative-free search; cost TARGET under 2 minutes on a fast tier; escalate when tracking or recovery limits remain after tuning.

R1 sits between a five-second synthesis and a residual that costs a fifth of a training run. It is tried only when R0 failed for a recorded control cause, and its job is to answer cheaply whether the failure was the constants or the controller's structure. If tuning fixes it, no learning was needed. If limits remain, the ladder has a recorded reason to escalate.

Two discipline points make it honest. First, a search over gains evaluated on a set of worlds will look better on those worlds than it is; the result reported for R1 is therefore measured on held-out worlds the search never saw. Second, when bodies are compared, controllers retuned with equal resources compare the best systems, while a shared controller isolates the change in the body; an R1 result is of the first kind and says so, with its budget.

Tier: Solid. Crate: `frob-control` (module `tune`).

## Scope
In: the search space (which constants, their boxes); the search procedure with addressed randomness and a fixed shape; the objective from the task's requirement; tuning and held-out world sets; the R1 controller record and report; the escalation signal; tests with a closed-form optimum.
Out: the defaults being tuned (N22a-06, N22a-10); the batched execution of the baseline (N22a-13); the ladder verb that recommends R1 (N22a-18); the studio's general optimizers and racing (BD-341); the residual rung (N22a-16, BD-330).

## Technical approach
**Search space (at most eight parameters, DESIGN; "a handful").** Per class, a declared subset of: κ_c (control periods per closed-loop period, box [5, 20]); ζ (damping ratio, [0.5, 1.5]); the outer-to-inner loop ratio ([2, 6]); τ* (dimensionless stride period, ±30% of the default); swing apex as a fraction of leg length ([0.1, 0.35]); maximum step length as a fraction of leg length ([0.3, 0.8]); the multiplier on the capture-point term ([0.5, 2]); nominal body height as a fraction of leg extension ([0.6, 0.95]). Wheeled classes use their own subset (wheel-loop damping, path-tracking look-ahead, acceleration limit fraction). Everything else in the controller record is unchanged. The boxes are registry data and part of the search record.

**Procedure (fixed shape, deterministic).**
1. Generation 0: N_c candidates (default 256) from a quasi-random set in the box, generated from counter-based streams keyed by (design identity, task, generation, candidate index), so the search replays exactly. Candidate 0 is always the default vector.
2. Evaluation: each candidate runs the task on N_t tuning worlds (default 4; addresses derived from the design and a `tune` salt) in one batch on the fast tier. The batch is candidates × worlds; a candidate's result does not depend on the other candidates in the batch.
3. Score: lexicographic: first the number of tuning worlds whose requirement verdict passes; then the mean of the task's primary margin (tracking error, recovery margin, distance); runs marked `actuator-infeasible` or with more than 5% of ticks in fallback count as failing.
4. Generations 1 to G − 1 (default G = 3): the next candidates are drawn around the best quartile's mean with a per-coordinate spread equal to that quartile's standard deviation times 1.2, clipped to the box (a cross-entropy step), from the same addressed streams.
5. Selection: the best candidate of the last generation by the score.
6. Validation: the selected vector and the default vector are each run on N_v held-out worlds (default 16; addresses from a `validate` salt, disjoint from the tuning set). The R1 result is the held-out result. If the selected vector is not better than the default on the held-out worlds by the paired comparison's lower bound, R1 reports `no-improvement` and returns the default.
7. Budget: default 3 × 256 × 4 episodes plus validation. For 20 s episodes at a 2 ms step that is about 3·10⁷ world-steps, which the fast tier is meant to finish well inside the 2 minute TARGET; a time or step budget given by the caller reduces N_c first.

**Outputs.**
- A controller record whose gains carry `tuned: true`, the selected ratios, and a search record: boxes, N_c, N_t, G, N_v, seeds and salts, the score trace per generation, the default's and the selected vector's tuning and held-out results. The record has a new `ControllerId`.
- An R1 report: `improved` (held-out requirement passes and the paired lower bound of the improvement is positive), `no-improvement`, or `limits-remain` (the best tuned vector still fails the same requirement on held-out worlds). `limits-remain` is the ladder's escalation condition; the report lists which requirement and which layer limits remain (tracking, recovery), from the same first-failed-layer analysis as R0.
- `comparison_kind: retuned-equal-resources` with the budget in world-steps, so that a comparison of two bodies under R1 can check that both were tuned with the same budget.
- Standing: an R1 score is a baseline score; it is `screening` under the same rank-agreement rule as R0.

**Surface.** `frob control synth <design> --rung R1 --task <task> [--budget …] --json`, a `run` request: it takes an idempotency key and a budget, returns a job handle when it exceeds the synchronous wait, and its result is the R1 report with the new controller.

**Determinism and evidence.** Class D2 on one device family: the whole search is a function of the design, the task, the boxes, the counts and the salts. Results are `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Closed-form fixture: on the double-integrator plant ẍ = u with u = −ω²·x − 2·ζ·ω·ẋ, ω = 10 rad/s, initial offset 1 and the integral of squared error as the task margin, a one-parameter search over ζ in [0.1, 3] returns ζ within 0.03 of 0.5; the reported minimum is within 2% of 1/ω = 0.100 (the continuous-time value is (1 + 4·ζ²)/(4·ζ·ω): 0.125 at ζ = 1 and 0.100 at ζ = 0.5).
- [ ] The search replays: two runs with the same inputs give byte-identical search records and the same `ControllerId`; permuting candidate order in the batch does not change any candidate's result.
- [ ] The default vector is candidate 0 of generation 0, so the selected vector is never worse than the default on the tuning worlds.
- [ ] The reported R1 result is the held-out one: on a planted noisy task where the in-sample best is a noise artefact, the held-out comparison reports `no-improvement` and returns the default.
- [ ] On the planted swing-clearance defect (a quadruped whose default swing height is too low for a 30 mm sill task), R1 returns `improved` with a larger swing-height fraction; on the weak-knee design R1 is not run at all (the ladder gives no escalation for a design refusal), and if forced it returns `limits-remain` with the support layer named.
- [ ] `limits-remain` makes `frob control ladder` recommend the next available rung with the reason.
- [ ] The measured cost for the reference quadruped with default counts is recorded against the 2 minute TARGET, with the tier and device named.
- [ ] The search record carries `comparison_kind: retuned-equal-resources` and its budget in world-steps.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`crates/frob-control/tests/tune_r1.rs`; real batched tier for controller fixtures; the closed-form plant is a real (small) simulated system labelled `synthetic`.
- `ise_fixture_finds_zeta_half`; `ise_values_match_closed_form` (0.125 at ζ = 1; 0.100 at ζ = 0.5; 0.260 at ζ = 0.1; each within the discretization tolerance).
- `search_replays_byte_identical`; `candidate_independent_of_batch_order`.
- `default_is_candidate_zero`; `selection_never_worse_in_sample`.
- `held_out_reported_not_in_sample`; `noise_artefact_gives_no_improvement`.
- `swing_clearance_defect_improved`; `limits_remain_names_layer`.
- `boxes_respected` (every evaluated candidate inside its box); `budget_reduces_candidates_first`.
- `record_has_comparison_kind_and_budget`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/control_rung_r1.sh`: creates the sill-defect quadruped; `frob control synth <d> --json` (expect `synthesized-failed`); `frob control ladder <d> --task sill-30mm --json` (expect escalation to R1); `frob control synth <d> --rung R1 --task sill-30mm --seed 5 --json` (expect `improved`, a new `ControllerId`, held-out results); a repeat of the last call (expect `idempotent_replay`); `frob control ladder` again (expect control rung R1, no escalation). It logs through the shared helper to `target/e2e-logs/control_rung_r1/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (class `Tested`, tier, determinism, identities including both `ControllerId`s, seeds and salts), the per-generation best score, the tuning and held-out results with their paired interval, the cost in world-steps and seconds against the TARGET, and a `summary` line.
### Known answers and negative controls
- The closed-form optimum ζ = 0.5 with value 1/ω.
- Negative controls: a search that reports its in-sample best (the noise-artefact fixture exposes it: an independent re-evaluation on fresh worlds disagrees with the report); a search that draws candidates from a global generator (the replay test fails across worker counts); a search that widens its box silently (the box test fails).
- Metamorphic: scaling the design by similarity leaves the selected dimensionless ratios unchanged within tolerance (they are dimensionless by construction); adding a parameter with a degenerate box (one value) changes nothing.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; across batch compositions.

## Considerations
- Eight parameters and three generations are deliberately small: R1 must stay two orders of magnitude cheaper than training, or the ladder's economics are gone. A body that needs a wider search needs a different rung.
- Tuned ratios are a property of (design, task): they are not copied to other designs unless a shared-controller comparison asks for exactly that.
- The studio's optimizer, when it lands, may replace the cross-entropy step; the contract here (addressed randomness, fixed shape, held-out validation, the search record) stays.
- Tuning against one task can degrade another; the report lists the task it tuned for, and the synthesis report's validation behaviours (stand, traverse) are re-run with the tuned vector so that a regression is visible.
