## Background
A gait is a fixed point x̄* of the reduced stride map P̄ of a closed loop. Whether small disturbances grow or decay from stride to stride is read from the monodromy matrix M = D P̄(x̄*): its eigenvalues are the Floquet multipliers, and the orbit is locally stable when all of them lie inside the unit circle. Shooting (N22a-31) finds the orbit cheaply inside the symmetric subspace Fix(K) of the gait's spatial symmetry group K, over 1/m of a stride, where m is the order of the cyclic quotient H/K of the gait's spatio-temporal group H with generator σ. This bead is where the plan's rule on that shortcut is enforced.

**Rule: stability is computed over the full reduced state, never over Fix(K) alone.** The monodromy is M = (ρ(σ)⁻¹·D F̄^(N/m)(x̄*))^m with the Jacobian taken in every direction (proof obligation PO-GAIT-01: the monodromy assembled from the twisted factor equals the direct Jacobian of the full stride; verification, both computed in `f64`, relative difference below 10⁻⁸). M is block-diagonalized by the isotypic projectors of the group, so each multiplier is attributed to a symmetry type, and a multiplier outside the unit circle in a non-trivial block is a symmetry-breaking instability named by its block. The spectrum goes to the sibling simulator's Floquet analysis through the airlock, which takes a supplied monodromy and also reports non-normal transient growth.

Counterexample, the smallest (CX-237; the same shortcut is CX-034). Take ẋ = −x and ẏ = +y with the symmetry y ↦ −y. On Fix(K) = {y = 0} every orbit decays with multiplier e^(−T). The full-space multiplier in the antisymmetric block is e^(+T) > 1. A restricted computation reports a stable gait for a robot that falls over sideways.

What a multiplier is and is not (the limits printed with every result): multipliers describe the chosen feedback law on the chosen tier, and local stability only. A locally stable orbit on flat ground supplies no estimate of recovery on a staircase. A non-normal monodromy can amplify a perturbation before it decays, so transient growth is reported beside the spectral radius.

FACT (read on 2026-11-22 at commit `e49c0f7d76` of the sibling repository): `frankensim/crates/fs-spectral/src/floquet.rs` exports `analyze_monodromy(matrix: &[C64], dimension, period, phase_flow: Option<&[C64]>, controls: FloquetControls, cancelled) -> Result<FloquetSpectrum, FloquetError>`. It interprets an explicitly supplied dimensionless monodromy in the caller's disclosed state chart; it neither constructs the evolution nor establishes that the base waveform is periodic. `phase_flow = None` keeps every multiplier in the stability radius, as required for an externally forced system. `FloquetSpectrum` carries the complete spectrum, `spectral_radius`, `classification` (`Stable`, `Unstable` or `Unresolved`, against a `unit_circle_guard` strictly between zero and one) and a growth rate per second. `floquet/transient.rs` exports `stroboscopic_growth(matrix, dimension, periods: &[u64], controls, cancelled)`, which returns for each requested number of periods the largest singular gain of that power of the map with its initial direction. No sibling composes a return map; that is this crate's work.

## Technical approach
**Assembly.**
1. The twisted factor Φ = ρ(σ)⁻¹·D F̄^(N/m)(x̄*), n × n in the dimensionless reduced chart, from n tangent directions over N/m control periods (4n + 1 worlds with the two-step central difference). ρ(σ) includes the clock shift of N/m ticks.
2. M = Φ^m by repeated multiplication in `f64`.
3. The direct Jacobian M_dir = D F̄^N(x̄*) over the whole stride, computed on request and always inside the gate. PO-GAIT-01: ‖M − M_dir‖_F / ‖M_dir‖_F < 10⁻⁸.
4. The Jacobian's label is carried over. A `refused` Jacobian gives the verdict `incomplete: derivative-refused` with the refusal's payload (FROB-REF-431); a `generalized` one hands its selections to the simultaneous-impact beads.

**Commutation and blocks.**
1. For every generator k of K: c_k = ‖ρ(k)·M − M·ρ(k)‖_F / ‖M‖_F. Above 10⁻⁷ the closed loop does not have the declared symmetry at this orbit: the result carries `symmetry-not-verified`, blocks are not formed, and the spectrum is reported unattributed.
2. For each real irreducible character χ of K with dimension d_χ: P_χ = (d_χ/|K|)·Σ_{k∈K} χ(k)·ρ(k). A complex-conjugate pair of characters (cyclic groups of order three or more without a mirror) is combined into one real block. B_χ is an orthonormal basis of the range of P_χ; the block is M_χ = B_χᵀ·M·B_χ; the leakage ‖B_χᵀ·M·B_χ′‖ for χ ≠ χ′ is reported.
3. Labels: `trivial` for the block of Fix(K); every other block is named by its character and by the generators under which it is odd (for the left-right mirror: `mirror-odd`).
4. Attribution under the temporal part (DESIGN): each multiplier λ of M equals μ^m for an eigenvalue μ of Φ, and the m-th root it comes from says which character j ∈ ℤ_m of H/K the perturbation carries. j = 0 preserves the gait's spatio-temporal symmetry; j ≠ 0 breaks it (for a trot with m = 2, a factor eigenvalue at −1 gives a multiplier at +1 whose bifurcating branch is a limping trot). The index is recorded per multiplier.

**Spectrum and transient growth, through the airlock.**
1. `analyze_monodromy` is called on M with `phase_flow = None` (the loop is clock-forced) and the stride period in seconds, and once per block as a cross-check: the multiset union of block spectra must equal the full spectrum within 10⁻⁸, and the block traces must sum to the trace of M.
2. Controls: `unit_circle_guard` 10⁻³ (DESIGN default, recorded): a multiplier within the guard of the unit circle makes the classification `Unresolved`.
3. `stroboscopic_growth` is called for horizons of 1, 2, 5, 10 and 20 strides. The record keeps each gain, the largest gain with its horizon, and its initial direction mapped back to named coordinates.
4. A refusal from the airlock (resource caps, an unresolved eigenbasis, cancellation) is passed on as `unresolved` with the sibling's reason; no partial spectrum is accepted, and no second eigen-solver exists in this crate (DESIGN, reason: one dense spectral owner whose residual checks are already audited; a private fallback would be a second, unaudited source of multipliers).

**Verdict (for an orbit whose Jacobian is single-valued).**
- `stable`: classification `Stable`, PO-GAIT-01 within tolerance when computed, and the computation is over the full reduced state.
- `unstable`: some multiplier outside the unit circle beyond the guard; the result names the block and the temporal index of the largest one, and says `symmetry-breaking` when that block is not trivial or the index is not zero.
- `unresolved`: within the guard, or the airlock declined.
- `incomplete`: any computation on a subspace. The API offers `restricted_spectrum(Φ_K)` for the by-product of shooting (the factor restricted to Fix(K)); its result type has the verdict `incomplete` built in, lists the blocks not examined, and cannot be converted into a complete result.

**Contribution to the orbit record.** Multipliers (real part, imaginary part, modulus, block label, temporal index); spectral radius; guard; classification; verdict; growth samples; commutation residuals; the PO-GAIT-01 difference when computed; the chart identity and scales; the Jacobian's label.

**Interim posture and switch-over.** The spectral functions live in a sibling repository under its own rules and are consumed only through the airlock crate at a pinned commit. Until the admission record exists, calls return `unresolved: spectral-not-admitted`; M, the blocks and PO-GAIT-01 are still computed and recorded. When the admission passes, the adapter is enabled by the stack lock, and the tests below that need multipliers stop reporting `not run`.

**Determinism and evidence.** M is class D2 on `batch-cpu` in `f64`. Host-side spectral results are reproducible on one platform. Multipliers are class `Estimated` (N22a-36) in the model on the named tier, standing `screening`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] PO-GAIT-01: on the twisted affine fixture M equals A² = [[0.25, 0.16], [0, 0.09]] from both routes exactly to round-off; on a nonlinear twisted fixture and on the reference quadruped's trot the relative difference is below 10⁻⁸.
- [ ] The planted transverse instability (T = 1): the full computation returns multipliers e^(−1) = 0.367879 in the `trivial` block and e^(+1) = 2.718282 in the `mirror-odd` block, verdict `unstable`, `symmetry-breaking`; the restricted computation returns 0.367879 with verdict `incomplete` and the missing block listed. This is the third clause of GATE-GAIT-01 at unit level.
- [ ] A build that assembles M from the restricted factor and reports `stable` on that fixture fails the test (the planted defect is caught).
- [ ] A map that does not commute with the declared K is reported `symmetry-not-verified` with the residual and an unattributed spectrum.
- [ ] Block spectra reunite to the full spectrum within 10⁻⁸ and block traces sum to the trace of M.
- [ ] The non-normal fixture M = [[0.5, 10], [0, 0.5]] reports spectral radius 0.5, classification `Stable`, and growth gains 10.0249, 10.0062, 3.1253 and 0.1953 at horizons 1, 2, 5 and 10, with the largest gain flagged.
- [ ] A multiplier at modulus 0.9995 with the default guard yields `unresolved`.
- [ ] A `refused` Jacobian yields `incomplete: derivative-refused` with the refusal payload; an airlock refusal yields `unresolved` with its reason; no partial spectrum appears in either case.
- [ ] Before the airlock group is admitted the result says `unresolved: spectral-not-admitted` and still contains M, blocks and the PO-GAIT-01 difference.
- [ ] Cancellation inside the airlock call returns `Cancelled`.

## Considerations
- Transient growth depends on the chart. The dimensionless chart of the stride-map bead is the disclosed one, and a gain is reported with its direction in named coordinates so that it can be read physically.
- The guard is a numerical guard, not an enclosure of integration error or of the sensitivity of a non-normal spectrum. A result near the circle is `unresolved` on purpose; finite-disturbance recovery tests decide such cases.
- For gaits with K trivial (trot, pace, walk under a single mirror) there is one spatial block; the temporal index is then the only symmetry attribution, and it matters: a multiplier that leaves through index 1 announces a limp, not a fold.
- Two owners meet here. The admission of the airlock group is N33b-11 (M3, a dependency); the orbit verb's seed bead BD-333 (M4) closes GATE-GAIT-01 with this bead's computations and the group gate INT-FSIM-13 with its own call site. This bead needs the first and feeds the second. Until N33b-11's admission record exists, the interim posture above applies and nothing waits on it.
