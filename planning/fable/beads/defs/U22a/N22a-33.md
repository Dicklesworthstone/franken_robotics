## Background
On the throughput tiers contact is compliant and the stride map is a product of step Jacobians with no event bookkeeping (N22a-30). Where contact is rigid and events are located in time (touchdown and liftoff, stick to slip and back, a joint-limit impact, a protection trip), a small perturbation of the state also moves the instant of the event, and the derivative across the event is not the derivative of the reset. For an isolated event with transverse crossing, a perturbation is carried through by the saltation matrix

    Ξ = D R + (f⁺ − D R·f⁻)·D g / (D g·f⁻)

for a guard g(x) = 0, a reset R and the vector fields f⁻ and f⁺ before and after. With explicit time dependence of guard or reset the general form is Ξ = D_x R + (f⁺ − D_x R·f⁻ − D_t R)·D_x g / (D_t g + D_x g·f⁻), and the event time moves by δt = −(D_x g·δx)/(D_t g + D_x g·f⁻). The denominator is the transversality number: the rate at which the guard is crossed. Each event records its chart, its guard margin, its identity and that denominator.

Why this route exists beside the compliant one (decision ADR-REFLEX-04: the compliant route comes first, event-located orbits with saltation matrices are the referee route): the hand-checkable truths of gait analysis are rigid-impact systems. A rimless wheel's multiplier is exactly cos²(2α) only with an instantaneous plastic impact; on a compliant tier it is a nearby number that depends on stiffness. The gate fixtures therefore need an event-located tangent map, and a compliant-tier orbit that matters can be re-examined on a tier that locates events.

FACT (sibling repository, paths as read): `frankensim/crates/fs-orbit` has shooting, pseudo-arclength continuation and monodromy, with a shooting module that disclaims hybrid saltations; `fs-time/src/hybrid.rs` locates events with a transverse, grazing or unresolved classification, and `fs-time/src/hybrid_sensitivity.rs` has event-time sensitivities and a separate saltation correction for a transverse guard; `fs-mbd` has no derivatives of the articulated dynamics with respect to state or design. No sibling composes these into a return map on an articulated model. Hence the posture: hybrid fixtures and the workspace's own `strict` profile are served here; the route on the referee waits on the upstream work item FD-FSIM-20, and until it lands a request for it is answered by name.

Counterexample that fixes the formula (CX-041): bouncing ball, state (y, v), flow (v, −g), guard y = 0, reset (y, −r·v), impact speed v⁻ < 0. The saltation matrix is [[−r, 0], [−(1 + r)·g/v⁻, −r]] with determinant r². Using the reset Jacobian diag(1, −r) as the event derivative gets the sign of the first entry wrong, omits the coupling in the second row, and for r = 1 has determinant −1 where the elastic bounce preserves area with determinant +1.

## Scope
In: the saltation matrix from an event record, in both forms; the per-event record; the tangent map over an interval as an ordered product of flow tangents and saltation matrices; validation of every such derivative against perturbed trajectories with events re-located on both sides; grazing, ambiguous-order and degeneracy refusals; the `saltation` label with its payload; hybrid fixtures (ball, rimless wheel, compass gait) as registered conformance fixtures with their automata; the named answer for the referee route.
Out: the derived saltation kernels per event class (N22ap2-02, which depends on this bead and feeds the orbit verb's seed bead BD-333) and their proof card T-17 (CARD-T-17, M5): until they exist, reset Jacobians come from central differences of the reset, with the source recorded, as stated below, and this bead's formula evaluation is the reference they are differentially tested against; projection onto an event section and removal of the trivial multiplier of an autonomous loop (the section form N22c-36; assembled for the gate by BD-333); simultaneous events (N22a-34); the symmetry identity across an event, D g(x⁺)·Ξ_e(x⁻) = Ξ_(g·e)(g·x⁻)·D g(x⁻), which the symmetric-controller beads test using this bead's matrices (BD-327); the upstream work itself.

## Technical approach
**Event record (DESIGN sketch).**
```rust
pub struct EventRecord {
    pub id: EventId,                 // e.g. touchdown:foot_fl:ground, keyed by stable geometry and clock identity
    pub time: f64, pub chart: ChartId,
    pub x_minus: Vec<f64>, pub x_plus: Vec<f64>,
    pub dg_x: Vec<f64>, pub dg_t: f64,         // guard gradient (row) and explicit time derivative
    pub transversality: f64,                    // D_t g + D_x g·f⁻
    pub transversality_norm: f64,               // abs(transversality)·t_s/g_s
    pub guard_margin: f64,                      // least normalized distance of every other enabled guard from zero at the event
}
```
**Saltation.** Given the record, the reset's Jacobians D_x R and D_t R, and the vector fields f⁻ = f_mode(x⁻) and f⁺ = f_next(x⁺): Ξ as above. A clock-triggered event (a controller switching at a tick, g = t − t_k) has D_x g = 0 and transversality 1, so Ξ = D_x R and no state-dependent time shift arises. Reset Jacobians come from the derived kernel where one exists and from central differences of the reset otherwise; the source is recorded.

**Tangent map over an interval with events e₁ … e_k.** M = Φ_k·Ξ_k·Φ_{k−1}·…·Ξ₁·Φ₀, where Φᵢ is the tangent of mode i's flow from the post-event state of eᵢ to the pre-event state of eᵢ₊₁ at fixed duration. Φᵢ is taken from the mode's tangent program where one exists; otherwise from two-step central differences of the mode's flow advanced for the nominal duration with its guards reported and not acted on (the continued flow that the formula presupposes), with the same Richardson check as the compliant route.

**Validation, applied to every result before it is labelled `saltation`.** For eight random unit directions δ and for each column where practical: the product M·δ is compared with (P(x + ε·δ) − P(x − ε·δ))/(2ε), where P is the interval map evaluated with events re-located on both sides. Agreement within 10⁻⁶ relative is required, and the perturbed trajectories must have the nominal event sequence. On disagreement the result is downgraded to `finite-difference` with `events matched` as found, and carries `saltation-disagreement` with the directions.

**Refusals.**
- *Grazing.* transversality_norm = abs(D_t g + D_x g·f⁻)·t_s/g_s, with g_s the guard's declared scale and t_s the model's time scale. Below τ_graze (the shared threshold of the derivative beads; DESIGN default 10⁻², fixed by control cases before the gradient gate) no matrix is returned.
- *Ambiguous order.* Two events not tied by a symmetry of the nominal state within the event-time resolution of each other. Events tied by a symmetry are not refused; they go to the simultaneous-impact analysis.
- *Active-set change under the perturbation,* seen in validation as a different event sequence: the affected directions are returned as `one-sided` estimates.
- *Frictional degeneracy* (a contact on the boundary between stick and slip at the event).
Grazing is returned at the gait level as FROB-REF-431 (a gait derivative at grazing or at a fold, §22.14) with the offending state, the perturbation directions and the one-sided estimates (ranked repairs: use the one-sided estimates returned; move the parameter; derivative-free search), wrapping the kernel-level FROB-REF-263 when that fired underneath. Ambiguous order and frictional degeneracy are neither grazing nor a fold, so they are returned as FROB-REF-263 itself (C-16's code for exactly these conditions), with the same payload; `gait.gradient` lists both codes.

**Label.** `saltation`, carrying the event identities, guard margins and transversality numbers of every event in the interval.

**Fixtures registered with the conformance crate (hybrid automata in `f64` on the host, each with modes, guards, resets and an analytic or oracle truth).**
1. *Bouncing ball,* restitution r. Truth: the matrix above; event-time shift δt = −δy/v⁻.
2. *Rimless wheel,* spoke half-angle α, spoke length l, slope γ. State (θ, ω) with θ the stance spoke's angle from the vertical and θ̈ = (g/l)·sin θ; guard θ = γ + α; reset θ ↦ θ − 2α, ω ↦ c·ω with c = cos 2α. Saltation at impact: [[c, 0], [(g/l)·(sin(γ − α) − c·sin(γ + α))/ω⁻, c]], determinant c². Return map on the squared post-impact rate: z ↦ c²·(z + b) with b = (4g/l)·sin α·sin γ; fixed point c²·b/(1 − c²); multiplier c². Over one step the tangent map Ξ·Φ has determinant c² (the flow is area-preserving) and eigenvalues 1 (along the flow) and c².
3. *Compass gait,* DESIGN default parameters (hip mass 10 kg, leg mass 5 kg at mid-leg, leg length 1 m, slope 3°): truth from an independent high-precision computation by the oracle tool, pinned.

**Tiers served.** Host fixtures: always. `batch-cpu` under the `strict` profile: articulated models whose events the transactional step locates, behind a crate feature, first exercised on a planar biped. Referee: the adapter returns `route-not-available: referee`, naming FD-FSIM-20; at the surface (a gait verb asked for `--tier referee`) this is the refusal registered under the slug `route-not-available` (N22-REF's row, slug-keyed until the refusal-allocation round gives it a number, exit 3; payload the route, the upstream item and the repair "use `strict` or a compliant tier"), never an unregistered string; switch-over rule: when the upstream item lands and its admission passes, the adapter is enabled and GATE-GAIT-01 and GATE-GAIT-02 are re-run on the referee route.

**Determinism and evidence.** Host and `strict` results are class D2 in `f64`. A `saltation` derivative that passed validation is class `Estimated` (N22a-36); passing validation is what entitles it to the `saltation` label.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Bouncing ball with r = 0.8 and v⁻ = −2 m/s: Ξ = [[−0.8, 0], [8.829, −0.8]] within 10⁻¹², determinant 0.64; the predicted event-time shift for δy = 10⁻⁶ m is 5 × 10⁻⁷ s and matches re-location within 10⁻⁹ s.
- [ ] The reset-Jacobian mutant (diag(1, −r) used as the event derivative) fails validation on the ball and is reported `saltation-disagreement`.
- [ ] Rimless wheel with eight spokes (α = π/8), l = 0.5 m, γ = 4°: fixed point ω*² = 2.094996 s⁻² and multiplier 0.5 within 10⁻⁶ (KA-560); pre-impact rate 2.046947 rad/s; Ξ's lower-left entry −6.065528 s⁻¹; det(Ξ·Φ) = 0.5 and trace = 1.5 within 10⁻⁸. With γ = −2° (uphill, b < 0) no fixed point is reported.
- [ ] A ball whose apex touches a ceiling returns `refused` with FROB-REF-431 wrapping FROB-REF-263, the state, the directions and the one-sided estimates; no matrix.
- [ ] Two unrelated events planted within the event-time resolution of each other are refused as ambiguous (FROB-REF-263, not 431); two events tied by a mirror symmetry are passed to the simultaneous-impact analysis and not refused.
- [ ] Every `saltation` result in the test suite carries, per event, identity, chart, guard margin and transversality number.
- [ ] A request for the referee route returns `route-not-available: referee` with the upstream item named, and at the surface the registered slug `route-not-available`, exit 3, and the repair "use `strict` or a compliant tier".
- [ ] The compass-gait fixture is registered with its oracle truth pinned, or the row is `pending` with the oracle bead named.

## Considerations
- A small or uncertain crossing speed makes a single-event saltation matrix unreliable well before the threshold; the transversality number is printed with every event so that a consumer can be stricter than the default.
- The continued flow (advancing in a mode past its guard) is an analysis device. It must never be reachable from a simulation run; the entry is exposed to analyses only.
- OPEN-GAIT-1 (when the referee route for orbits is built; owner the Kinesis lead with upstream; blocks nothing on the critical path) has the default compliant tiers only. This bead builds what the gates need on fixtures and keeps the articulated `strict` route behind a feature; nothing on the critical path depends on the referee route.
- A sliding impact and its mirror can satisfy the symmetry identity while a normal-speed perturbation changes tangential speed; that cross term stays in the full matrix, which is why the matrix, not a reduced block, is what this bead returns.
