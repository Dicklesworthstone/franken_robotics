## Background
A design studio changes a body one typed edit at a time. If the parent design has a gait orbit, the child's orbit is usually nearby, and solving it from the parent's is far cheaper than solving it from nothing. Continuation does that: it follows the solution set of the shooting equation r(x̄, θ) = 0 along a path in the design parameters θ, one parameter, or the line between two designs joined by a typed edit. A child design warm-starts from its parent's orbit.

Continuation is not guaranteed to arrive. Turning points and bifurcations stop it (FACT, the plan's source register), and a body can simply stop having that gait. The plan's rule: what happens on the path is a typed outcome, not an error.

| Outcome | Detection | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| `arrived` | Residual below tolerance at the target | The orbit and its spectrum at the target design |
| `fold` | A multiplier of the monodromy M at +1 in the trivial block, so I − M is singular and the branch turns back | The parameter value of the fold; no orbit of this type beyond it on this branch |
| `period-doubling` | A multiplier at −1 | The doubled orbit is offered as a new seed |
| `torus` | A complex pair crossing the unit circle | Reported; quasi-periodic motion is not followed |
| `symmetry-breaking` | A multiplier at +1 in a non-trivial block | The broken generator; the lower-symmetry branch is offered as a seed |
| `mode-change` | The orbit's contact sequence changed | The old label no longer applies; a new shooting problem is posed |
| `diverged`, `budget` | Newton failure after step halving; the budget ended | Nothing partial is published as an orbit |

Why pseudo-arclength and not stepping the parameter: natural-parameter continuation fails at a fold, where the solution curve turns back in θ and no solution exists at the next parameter value; it then reports a convergence failure that cannot be told from a bug. Parametrizing by arclength along the curve passes through the turning point, so the fold is located and named. Why bifurcations are named: each tells the designer something different. A fold says the gait ceases to exist beyond a parameter value. A symmetry-breaking point says the gait survives but starts to limp, and names the generator that broke. A mode-change says the footfall pattern itself changed.

The mechanism is tier Frontier; its pilot (N22a-28) must have recorded `fund`.

## Scope
In: design paths (one parameter; the straight line of a typed edit); the pseudo-arclength predictor and corrector in the symmetric subspace; step control; the test functions and their localization; the seven outcomes with their payloads; seeds offered for new branches; the path record; warm start of a child from a parent.

## Technical approach
**Path.** θ(λ) = θ₀ + λ·(θ₁ − θ₀) for λ in [0, 1], where θ₀ and θ₁ differ in the parameters a typed edit changes, or in one named parameter. An edit that changes topology (adds or removes a limb, swaps a wheel for a foot) has no continuous path: the call returns `not-continuable: discrete-edit` and routes to a fresh shooting problem from the atlas.

**Unknowns and equation.** z = (y, λ) with x̄ = B_K·y in the symmetric subspace Fix(K) of the gait, and r(y, λ) = B_Kᵀ·ρ(σ)⁻¹·F̄_θ(λ)^(N/m)(B_K·y) − y. The Jacobian is [J_y | r_λ], with J_y from n_K tangent worlds and r_λ from one more tangent world pair in the parameter (two step sizes, as for every Jacobian).

**Predictor and corrector (DESIGN defaults in parentheses).**
1. Tangent t = (t_y, t_λ): the unit null vector of [J_y | r_λ] in the scaled metric, oriented so that t·t_prev > 0 (initially t_λ > 0).
2. Predictor: z_p = z_k + Δs·t (initial Δs 0.1 in units where the whole path has length about 1).
3. Corrector: Newton on r(z) = 0 together with tᵀ·(z − z_p) = 0, that is, the bordered system [[J_y, r_λ], [t_yᵀ, t_λ]]·δz = −(r, 0). Converged when ‖r‖ ≤ 10⁻⁹ in the scaled norm, within 8 iterations.
4. Step control: on failure Δs is halved (at most 12 times; then `diverged`); after a correction of at most 3 iterations Δs grows by 1.5, capped at 0.25.
5. Landing: when the predictor passes λ = 1 the last step fixes λ = 1 and corrects in y only.
6. Budget: a CPU budget and cancellation are checked at every accepted point; exceeding the budget gives `budget`.

**At every accepted point.** The full-space monodromy M and its factor Φ (M = Φ^m), the isotypic blocks M_χ, the spectrum through the airlock, and the contact-sequence signature are computed and stored in the path record with λ. Test functions:
- fold: t_λ changes sign (equivalently det(I − M) restricted to the trivial block changes sign);
- period-doubling: det(I + M) changes sign;
- symmetry-breaking: det(I − M_χ) changes sign for a non-trivial block χ, or, for a temporal index j ≠ 0, the factor Φ has an eigenvalue crossing the j-th m-th root of unity (for m = 2: det(I + Φ) changes sign);
- torus: the number of multipliers outside the unit circle changes by two and the pair nearest the circle is complex;
- mode-change: the contact-sequence signature differs from the previous point's.

**Localization.** A sign change between two accepted points is located by bisection in arclength with the secant rule on the test function, to 10⁻⁶ of the path's parameter range or 20 bisections. A mode-change is bracketed to 10⁻⁴ of the range, and both signatures are reported.

**Payloads.**
- `arrived`: the `GaitOrbit` record at the target with `parent_orbit` set.
- `fold`: λ and θ at the fold, the last orbit before it, and the statement that no orbit of this type exists beyond it on this branch.
- `period-doubling`: λ; a seed x̄* ± ε·v for a stride of 2N, with v the eigenvector at −1.
- `torus`: λ and the argument of the crossing pair; nothing is followed.
- `symmetry-breaking`: λ; the block; the broken generator (the elements of K that act as −1 on the block, or the temporal generator σ for a temporal index); a seed x̄* + ε·v_χ with the smaller group it keeps.
- `mode-change`: λ; the old and new contact sequences; a seed and the new sequence for a new shooting problem.
- `diverged`, `budget`: the path so far as provenance. No `GaitOrbit` is emitted for the target.

**Policy at stability-changing points (DESIGN).** By default the run stops at the first outcome that is not `arrived`, because a child reached only through a change of stability or symmetry is not the same gait and should not be scored as if it were. With `through = true` the run records period-doubling, torus and symmetry-breaking points as events and continues along the original branch; `fold` and `mode-change` always end the run for that target.

**Path record** (`frob.gait.path/1`): the endpoints and the parameters varied; per accepted point λ, the orbit identity or residual, spectral radius, the test-function values, the contact signature and the step taken; events with their locations; the outcome; cost in worlds, world-steps and CPU seconds. The stored (λ, multipliers) series is what the gradient of a multiplier is differenced from.

**Determinism and evidence.** Class D2 on `batch-cpu` in `f64`: the same path, seed orbit and options reproduce the same accepted points bit for bit. Results are `Tested`, standing `screening`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Affine family x ↦ A·x + (1 + λ)·d with A = [[0.5, 0.2], [0, 0.3]], d = (1, 1): `arrived` at λ = 1 with x̄* = (36/7, 20/7) within 10⁻⁹.
- [ ] Saddle-node family x ↦ x + λ − x² from λ = 0.5 (fixed point √λ, multiplier 1 − 2√λ) toward λ = −0.25: outcome `fold` at λ = 0 within 10⁻⁶, never `diverged`. The same family from λ = 0.5 toward λ = 1.5: `period-doubling` at λ = 1 within 10⁻⁶.
- [ ] Logistic family x ↦ λ·x·(1 − x) from λ = 2.5 to 3.5: `period-doubling` at λ = 3; the offered seed converges with a doubled stride to the two-cycle (0.513045, 0.799455) at λ = 3.2.
- [ ] Rotation family z ↦ λ·R(1 rad)·z from λ = 0.9 to 1.1: `torus` at λ = 1; nothing is followed.
- [ ] Pitchfork family (x, y) ↦ (0.5·x + 0.5, (1 + λ)·y − y³) with K generated by y ↦ −y, from λ = −0.5 to 0.5: `symmetry-breaking` at λ = 0 in the odd block with the mirror named; the offered seed converges to (1, ±0.5) at λ = 0.25.
- [ ] Piecewise family (x ↦ 0.5·x + λ with signature A below x = 1, x ↦ 0.8·x + λ − 0.3 with signature B above), from λ = 0.2 to 0.8: `mode-change` at λ = 0.5 with both signatures.
- [ ] A natural-parameter mutant (λ stepped directly) reports `diverged` on the saddle-node test and fails it.
- [ ] Reference quadruped trot, thigh length scaled from 1.00 to 1.10: `arrived`; the child's orbit equals fresh shooting on the child within 10⁻⁷; the continuation uses fewer world-steps than fresh shooting from the atlas seed (both counts recorded).
- [ ] A topology edit returns `not-continuable: discrete-edit`.
- [ ] `budget` and `Cancelled` are distinct; neither emits an orbit for the target; a planted failure leaves no `GaitOrbit` for the target in the ledger.
- [ ] Two runs with the same inputs give identical path records.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Closed forms above: the fold at λ = 0; period doubling at λ = 1 (saddle-node family) and λ = 3 (logistic); the two-cycle ((λ + 1) ± √((λ − 3)(λ + 1)))/(2λ) = 0.799455 and 0.513045 at λ = 3.2; the torus at λ = 1; the pitchfork at λ = 0 with branches ±√λ; the mode change at λ = 0.5.
- Negative controls: the natural-parameter mutant; a detector that reads the spectral radius only (it reports the pitchfork as a fold or misses the block); a run that publishes its last accepted orbit as the target's orbit on `diverged`.
- Metamorphic: reversing the path from the arrived child returns to the parent's orbit; splitting a path into two consecutive paths arrives at the same orbit; mirroring design and seed mirrors the path.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; across batch compositions; debug and release.

## Considerations
- Kill gate KILL-GAIT (decided at M4 by the measurement in BD-335): if continuation succeeds on fewer than half of 10% design perturbations, or the median solve exceeds 10 minutes of CPU, or orbit cost of transport does not rank designs as specialists do, orbits serve stability diagnostics and warm starts only. This mechanism stays as the warm-start route in either case.
- Each accepted point costs a full monodromy. For screening many children, `spectrum_every = k` computes the spectrum at every k-th point and at the target; test functions are then evaluated on a coarser grid and an event between spectra is located when found. The default is every point.
- Branch switching is offered as seeds and never taken automatically: which branch is the design's gait is a decision for the caller.
- A fold of a gait in a design parameter is a design finding (a limb length beyond which the body cannot trot at that speed under its baseline) and is reported as one, with the parameter value.
- Loops with a free period (passive walkers, event-reset controllers) have a multiplier fixed at one; every point of their branch would read as a fold here, which is why they are refused upstream and continued by the bordered form.
