## Background
`gait.orbit` (kind run, first milestone M4) solves a periodic orbit of a design's closed loop by shooting and returns its multipliers, its residual and the standing `screening`. It is the first user-visible capability of the gait crate, and its gate is a known-answer gate:

GATE-GAIT-01 (Known answer, M4): rimless-wheel fixed point and multiplier within 10⁻⁶; compass-gait multipliers within 10⁻⁶ of an independent high-precision computation; the planted transverse instability is found and the restricted computation is marked incomplete; PO-GAIT-01 (the monodromy assembled from the twisted factor equals the direct Jacobian of the full stride, relative difference below 10⁻⁸ in `f64`).

Each clause guards a way of being wrong that produces plausible numbers. A saltation matrix without its event-time term gives the rimless wheel a multiplier of cos 2α in place of cos² 2α. A stability computation restricted to the symmetric subspace calls a gait stable that falls over sideways (the planted case: ẋ = −x, ẏ = +y with the symmetry y ↦ −y; multiplier e^(−T) on the subspace, e^(+T) in the antisymmetric block). A twisted assembly with a wrong group action or a missing clock shift gives a monodromy that is not the stride's Jacobian. The compass gait has no closed form, so its check is against an oracle that never saw this code.

The mechanisms are tested in process by their own beads. This bead is the script that shows the binary an agent calls returns those answers, with the record's mandatory fields and labels, and hands them to the gate's tier run, whose receipt is what the gate's row is recorded from.

## Scope
In: `scripts/e2e/gait_orbit.sh`, its fixtures, assertions and logs; the four clauses of GATE-GAIT-01 measured through the `frob` binary and evaluated by the gate's tier run into a receipt, with the registered negative control caught in that run; the recording of the gate's row from that receipt, which BD-333 assigns to this bead (one recorder per gate; the script itself writes no row). The script file is first written by the verb's seed bead BD-333 (a dependency) with its own five steps; this bead extends that file and keeps those steps, including the build without the `spectral` group, which answers exit 5 with `FROB-REF-790` (`foundation-group-absent`).
Out: the verb, its inputs and their spellings, and its registry entry (BD-333, VERBS-gait); the admission of the `spectral` group (N33b-11); the mechanisms (N22a-29 to N22a-36); the section form (N22c-36, a dependency) and the bordered form (N22c-37); the autonomous-loop gate GATE-GAIT-04, which re-runs two of these fixtures through both forms (N22c-38); gradients and continuation (N22a-39, N22a-40).

## Technical approach
**Fixtures (registered with the conformance crate; hybrid automata on the host, and one robot).**
- `fixture:rimless-wheel` with parameters spokes, spoke length l and slope. Truth: with α = π/spokes, c = cos 2α and b = (4g/l)·sin α·sin γ, the squared post-impact rate has fixed point c²·b/(1 − c²) and multiplier c².
- `fixture:compass-gait` at its registered parameters. Truth: the oracle's multipliers, read from the known-answer registry.
- `fixture:transverse-instability` with period T: the time-T map of ẋ = −(x − 1), ẏ = +y with K generated by y ↦ −y.
- `fixture:twisted-affine`: half-stride map x ↦ A·x + d with ρ(σ) = −I, A = [[0.5, 0.2], [0, 0.3]], d = (1, 1).
- `quad-ref`: the `quad-mammal` archetype under its synthesized baseline trot on `batch-cpu` in `f64`.

**Steps, with the command each runs and what it asserts.**
0. *Precondition.* `frob doctor --json` must show the `spectral` airlock group admitted. If not, the script logs `blocked: spectral-not-admitted`, exits non-zero, and the gate stays `not run`.
Every `frob gait orbit` call carries `--key go-e2e-<step>` (a `run` verb: without a key it is refused with `FROB-REF-805`, exit 3); the comparisons of step 12 carry keys of their own.
1. *Rimless wheel, eight spokes.* `frob gait orbit fixture:rimless-wheel --set spokes=8,l=0.5,slope_deg=4 --section post-impact --key go-e2e-1 --json` (the wheel has no clock, so it is solved on its impact section; N22c-36). Assert: fixed point ω*² = 2.094996 s⁻² (ω* = 1.447410 rad/s) within 10⁻⁶ relative; the nontrivial multiplier 0.5 within 10⁻⁶; the multiplier along the flow is listed as removed; derivative label `saltation` with one event carrying its identity, transversality number and `source: derived-kernel` with its `KernelId` (the verb-level check of N22ap2-02's binding, which BD-333 depends on); exit 0.
2. *Rimless wheel, six spokes.* `--set spokes=6,l=0.5,slope_deg=12 --section post-impact`: ω*² = 2.719485 s⁻², multiplier 0.25, same tolerances.
3. *Rimless wheel, uphill.* `--set spokes=8,l=0.5,slope_deg=-2 --section post-impact`: the result is a typed outcome without an orbit (b = −1.048 s⁻² < 0, no fixed point), exit 2, and no `GaitOrbit` record in the envelope.
4. *Compass gait.* `frob gait orbit fixture:compass-gait --section auto --key go-e2e-4 --json`: each multiplier within 10⁻⁶ of the pinned oracle value; the trivial multiplier listed as removed; the section the solver chose (heel strike, the stride's only impact) is logged with the oracle's identity and digits beside the measured ones.
5. *Planted transverse instability, full.* `frob gait orbit fixture:transverse-instability --set T=1 --key go-e2e-5 --json`: fixed point (1, 0); multipliers 0.367879 in block `trivial` and 2.718282 in block `mirror-odd`, within 10⁻⁸ relative; verdict `unstable` with `symmetry-breaking` and the block named.
6. *Planted transverse instability, restricted.* The same with `--spectrum-scope restricted --key go-e2e-6` (the diagnostic input of BD-333's grammar that restricts the spectrum to the symmetric subspace): the single multiplier 0.367879; verdict `incomplete`; the unexamined block listed. Assert that the string `stable` does not appear as a verdict.
7. *PO-GAIT-01 on the fixture.* `frob gait orbit fixture:twisted-affine --check-direct --key go-e2e-7 --json`: fixed point (−0.564103, −0.769231); monodromy [[0.25, 0.16], [0, 0.09]] from both routes; `po_gait_01_rel_diff` below 10⁻⁸.
8. *PO-GAIT-01 on a robot.* `frob gait orbit quad-ref --gait trot --tier batch-cpu --check-direct --seed 1 --key go-e2e-8 --json`: outcome `converged`; residual at most 10⁻⁹; (H, K, m) = (C₂, 1, 2); `po_gait_01_rel_diff` below 10⁻⁸; the stride displacement over the stride period within 20% of the commanded speed; a verdict with its basis; each diagonal touchdown reported as a simultaneous group and the call not refused.
9. *The record.* Every `GaitOrbit` returned in steps 1 to 8 is validated against `frob.gait.orbit/1`: `ControllerId`, tier, residual, a full-space spectrum or an explicit incomplete marker, derivative labels, `standing: "screening: gait-orbit"`, the four limit codes. The evidence block has class `Estimated` (the *screening* label; N22a-36), no claim kind and `reality: no-claim`.
10. *Refusal.* `frob gait orbit quad-ref --controller <event-reset variant> --form stroboscopic --period 0.5 --key go-e2e-10 --json` (the form spelling of N22c-36 and N22c-38, where `--form stroboscopic` takes `--period <T>`; 0.5 s is the reference trot's stride period) is refused under the slug `fixed-period-shooting-on-free-period`, exit 3, with the phase source and the repair; it is not reported as a fold.
11. *Surfaces and cancellation.* Step 8 through the daemon and through the MCP tool `gait_orbit`: identical envelopes apart from transport fields. A running solve is cancelled; it drains and reports `cancelled` with no orbit.
12. *Determinism.* Step 8 twice more, with keys `go-e2e-12a-<run-id>` and `go-e2e-12b-<run-id>` (`<run-id>` the log's UTC timestamp, so that each is a fresh solve and not the stored receipt of step 8 or of an earlier run), the second with a different `--workers` count: identical `orbit_id` and identical multipliers bit for bit; the log asserts that neither envelope is a replayed receipt.
13. *Tier run.* The per-clause records of steps 1 to 8 are handed to the conformance crate's tier run (`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-GAIT-01`, the Gauntlet's cargo form; there is no `frob` command for it), which re-evaluates the four clauses against their thresholds from the logged values, runs the gate's registered negative control (the saltation without its event-time term) in the same run, where it must be `caught`, and writes a hash-named receipt under `target/gauntlet/receipts/`. The script prints the receipt hash and writes no gate row.

**Recording (not part of the script).** The row is entered from step 13's receipt by this bead, the one recorder of GATE-GAIT-01 (BD-333 assigns the gate row here), with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-GAIT-01 --receipt <hash>`, by a session that did not implement the orbit mechanisms, carrying the four clauses with measured values against thresholds, the negative control `caught`, commit, hardware fingerprint, the airlock's pin and the adjudicating tier of each clause.

**Idempotence and seeds.** Every command carries an explicit seed where one applies and an idempotency key derived from the script name and step (step 12 adds the run id); a second run of the script replays the stored results of steps 1 to 11 and repeats the assertions. The script exits non-zero on the first failed assertion after writing its summary.

The first is registered as the gate's negative control.
- [ ] The log and transcript are written as specified and contain identities and scalar results only.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
None of its own beyond a self-test of the script's helpers, `scripts/e2e/selftest/gait_orbit_selftest.sh`: the relative-error assertion fails at 2 × 10⁻⁶ and passes at 5 × 10⁻⁷; the verdict scanner finds a planted `stable` in a restricted result; the schema check rejects a record without `limits`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gait_orbit.sh` as specified. Logging through the shared helper to `target/e2e-logs/gait_orbit/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22a-38`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code and wall time, and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities of design, controller and orbit, seed). `assert` lines carry `ka` (the known-answer identifier), expected and measured values with units, the tolerance, and on failure the first differing value with the artifact paths needed to reproduce (the orbit record and, for robot cases, the run). `metric` lines carry each multiplier with its block and temporal index, the spectral radius, the residual, `po_gait_01_rel_diff`, solve CPU seconds, worlds and world-steps. `refusal` lines carry the slug or code, the payload and the repairs. The final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-GAIT-01"]` with the four clause results, the negative control's state in the tier run and the receipt hash.
### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-560 (rimless wheel: fixed point c²·b/(1 − c²), multiplier c², 10⁻⁶; negative control, an uphill slope has no fixed point); KA-561 (compass gait against an independent high-precision computation, 10⁻⁶); KA-562 (planted transverse instability: restricted e^(−T), full e^(+T); the restricted computation must be marked incomplete); KA-093 (multiplier exactly ½ for eight spokes); KA-768 (the oscillator through the airlock) as the precondition's own check.
- Counterexamples exercised: CX-237 and CX-034 (stability on the symmetric subspace only), CX-041 (the reset Jacobian as the event derivative).
- Negative controls: the four planted defects above.
- Metamorphic relations: scaling l by 4 halves ω* (ω*² scales as 1/l) and leaves the multiplier unchanged; changing g and l by the same factor leaves ω*² unchanged; mirroring `quad-ref` mirrors the orbit and leaves the multipliers unchanged.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; host fixtures across platforms within their class.

## Considerations
- Two of the gate's fixtures are loops without a clock. They are solved on an event section, the autonomous path of N22c-36, which is a dependency for that reason; the gate is never recorded as passed on steps 5 to 8 alone.
- The known-answer table places these rows on the referee tier. The orbit route on the referee waits on upstream work in the sibling simulator; the row records which tier adjudicated each clause (host fixtures; `batch-cpu` for the trot), and the clauses are re-run on the referee route when it exists.
- The compass gait's truth is only as independent as its oracle. The oracle is pinned and never regenerated from the candidate; a change of its digits is a registry change with a reason.
- Option names in the commands above follow the spellings that BD-333 fixes for the inputs VERBS-gait lists (`--section` and `--form` come from N22c-36 and N22c-37; `--check-direct`, `--spectrum-scope`, `--set` and `--key` are in BD-333's grammar and in the row of VERBS-gait); a rename there is a mechanical edit here and does not change what is asserted.
