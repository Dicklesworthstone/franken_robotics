## Background
`gait.orbit` (kind run, first milestone M4) solves a periodic orbit of a design's closed loop by shooting and returns its multipliers, its residual and the standing `screening`. It is the first user-visible capability of the gait crate, and its gate is a known-answer gate:

GATE-GAIT-01 (Known answer, M4): rimless-wheel fixed point and multiplier within 10⁻⁶; compass-gait multipliers within 10⁻⁶ of an independent high-precision computation; the planted transverse instability is found and the restricted computation is marked incomplete; PO-GAIT-01 (the monodromy assembled from the twisted factor equals the direct Jacobian of the full stride, relative difference below 10⁻⁸ in `f64`).

Each clause guards a way of being wrong that produces plausible numbers. A saltation matrix without its event-time term gives the rimless wheel a multiplier of cos 2α in place of cos² 2α. A stability computation restricted to the symmetric subspace calls a gait stable that falls over sideways (the planted case: ẋ = −x, ẏ = +y with the symmetry y ↦ −y; multiplier e^(−T) on the subspace, e^(+T) in the antisymmetric block). A twisted assembly with a wrong group action or a missing clock shift gives a monodromy that is not the stride's Jacobian. The compass gait has no closed form, so its check is against an oracle that never saw this code.

The mechanisms are tested in process by their own beads. This bead is the script that shows the binary an agent calls returns those answers, with the record's mandatory fields and labels, and writes the gate's row.

## Scope
In: `scripts/e2e/gait_orbit.sh`, its fixtures, assertions and logs; the four clauses of GATE-GAIT-01 measured through the `frob` binary and written to the gate ledger; the registered negative control shown to fail.
Out: the verb and its registry entry, and the airlock admission (BD-333); the mechanisms (N22a-29 to N22a-36); the autonomous-loop gate, which re-runs two of these fixtures through its own forms (the autonomous-orbit beads); gradients and continuation (N22a-39, N22a-40).

## Technical approach
**Fixtures (registered with the conformance crate; hybrid automata on the host, and one robot).**
- `fixture:rimless-wheel` with parameters spokes, spoke length l and slope. Truth: with α = π/spokes, c = cos 2α and b = (4g/l)·sin α·sin γ, the squared post-impact rate has fixed point c²·b/(1 − c²) and multiplier c².
- `fixture:compass-gait` at its registered parameters. Truth: the oracle's multipliers, read from the known-answer registry.
- `fixture:transverse-instability` with period T: the time-T map of ẋ = −(x − 1), ẏ = +y with K generated by y ↦ −y.
- `fixture:twisted-affine`: half-stride map x ↦ A·x + d with ρ(σ) = −I, A = [[0.5, 0.2], [0, 0.3]], d = (1, 1).
- `quad-ref`: the `quad-mammal` archetype under its synthesized baseline trot on `batch-cpu` in `f64`.

**Steps, with the command each runs and what it asserts.**
0. *Precondition.* `frob doctor --json` must show the `spectral` airlock group admitted. If not, the script logs `blocked: spectral-not-admitted`, exits non-zero, and the gate stays `not run`.
1. *Rimless wheel, eight spokes.* `frob gait orbit fixture:rimless-wheel --set spokes=8,l=0.5,slope_deg=4 --json`. Assert: fixed point ω*² = 2.094996 s⁻² (ω* = 1.447410 rad/s) within 10⁻⁶ relative; the nontrivial multiplier 0.5 within 10⁻⁶; the multiplier along the flow is listed as removed; derivative label `saltation` with one event carrying its identity and transversality number; exit 0.
2. *Rimless wheel, six spokes.* `--set spokes=6,l=0.5,slope_deg=12`: ω*² = 2.719485 s⁻², multiplier 0.25, same tolerances.
3. *Rimless wheel, uphill.* `--set spokes=8,l=0.5,slope_deg=-2`: the result is a typed outcome without an orbit (b = −1.048 s⁻² < 0, no fixed point), exit 2, and no `GaitOrbit` record in the envelope.
4. *Compass gait.* `frob gait orbit fixture:compass-gait --json`: each multiplier within 10⁻⁶ of the pinned oracle value; the oracle's identity and digits are logged beside the measured ones.
5. *Planted transverse instability, full.* `frob gait orbit fixture:transverse-instability --set T=1 --json`: fixed point (1, 0); multipliers 0.367879 in block `trivial` and 2.718282 in block `mirror-odd`, within 10⁻⁸ relative; verdict `unstable` with `symmetry-breaking` and the block named.
6. *Planted transverse instability, restricted.* The same with the registered option that restricts the spectrum to the symmetric subspace: the single multiplier 0.367879; verdict `incomplete`; the unexamined block listed. Assert that the string `stable` does not appear as a verdict.
7. *PO-GAIT-01 on the fixture.* `frob gait orbit fixture:twisted-affine --check-direct --json`: fixed point (−0.564103, −0.769231); monodromy [[0.25, 0.16], [0, 0.09]] from both routes; `po_gait_01_rel_diff` below 10⁻⁸.
8. *PO-GAIT-01 on a robot.* `frob gait orbit quad-ref --gait trot --tier batch-cpu --check-direct --seed 1 --json`: outcome `converged`; residual at most 10⁻⁹; (H, K, m) = (C₂, 1, 2); `po_gait_01_rel_diff` below 10⁻⁸; the stride displacement over the stride period within 20% of the commanded speed; a verdict with its basis; each diagonal touchdown reported as a simultaneous group and the call not refused.
9. *The record.* Every `GaitOrbit` returned in steps 1 to 8 is validated against `frob.gait.orbit/1`: `ControllerId`, tier, residual, a full-space spectrum or an explicit incomplete marker, derivative labels, `standing: "screening: gait-orbit"`, the four limit codes. The evidence block has class `Tested`, no claim kind and `reality: no-claim`.
10. *Refusal.* `frob gait orbit quad-ref --controller <event-reset variant> --stroboscopic --json` is refused under the slug `fixed-period-shooting-on-free-period`, exit 3, with the phase source and the repair; it is not reported as a fold.
11. *Surfaces and cancellation.* Step 8 through the daemon and through the MCP tool `gait_orbit`: identical envelopes apart from transport fields. A running solve is cancelled; it drains and reports `cancelled` with no orbit.
12. *Determinism.* Step 8 twice and with a different worker count: identical `orbit_id` and identical multipliers bit for bit.
13. *Gate row.* The four clauses with measured values against thresholds are written to the gate ledger for GATE-GAIT-01 with commit, hardware fingerprint, the airlock's pin and the adjudicating tier of each clause.

**Idempotence and seeds.** Every command carries an explicit seed where one applies and an idempotency key derived from the script name and step; a second run replays stored results and repeats the assertions. The script exits non-zero on the first failed assertion after writing its summary.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
None of its own beyond a self-test of the script's helpers, `scripts/e2e/selftest/gait_orbit_selftest.sh`: the relative-error assertion fails at 2 × 10⁻⁶ and passes at 5 × 10⁻⁷; the verdict scanner finds a planted `stable` in a restricted result; the schema check rejects a record without `limits`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gait_orbit.sh` as specified. Logging through the shared helper to `target/e2e-logs/gait_orbit/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22a-38`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code and wall time, and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities of design, controller and orbit, seed). `assert` lines carry `ka` (the known-answer identifier), expected and measured values with units, the tolerance, and on failure the first differing value with the artifact paths needed to reproduce (the orbit record and, for robot cases, the run). `metric` lines carry each multiplier with its block and temporal index, the spectral radius, the residual, `po_gait_01_rel_diff`, solve CPU seconds, worlds and world-steps. `refusal` lines carry the slug or code, the payload and the repairs. The final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-GAIT-01"]` with the four clause results.
### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-560 (rimless wheel: fixed point c²·b/(1 − c²), multiplier c², 10⁻⁶; negative control, an uphill slope has no fixed point); KA-561 (compass gait against an independent high-precision computation, 10⁻⁶); KA-562 (planted transverse instability: restricted e^(−T), full e^(+T); the restricted computation must be marked incomplete); KA-093 (multiplier exactly ½ for eight spokes); KA-768 (the oscillator through the airlock) as the precondition's own check.
- Counterexamples exercised: CX-237 and CX-034 (stability on the symmetric subspace only), CX-041 (the reset Jacobian as the event derivative).
- Negative controls: the four planted defects above.
- Metamorphic relations: scaling l by 4 halves ω* (ω*² scales as 1/l) and leaves the multiplier unchanged; changing g and l by the same factor leaves ω*² unchanged; mirroring `quad-ref` mirrors the orbit and leaves the multipliers unchanged.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; host fixtures across platforms within their class.

## Considerations
- Two of the gate's fixtures are loops without a clock. They are solved on an event section, which is the autonomous path; if that path is not available when this script first runs, steps 1 to 4 report `not run`, and the gate cannot be recorded as passed on steps 5 to 8 alone.
- The known-answer table places these rows on the referee tier. The orbit route on the referee waits on upstream work in the sibling simulator; the row records which tier adjudicated each clause (host fixtures; `batch-cpu` for the trot), and the clauses are re-run on the referee route when it exists.
- The compass gait's truth is only as independent as its oracle. The oracle is pinned and never regenerated from the candidate; a change of its digits is a registry change with a reason.
- Option names in the commands above follow the verb's registry entry, which another unit drafts; a rename there is a mechanical edit here and does not change what is asserted.
