## Background
`gait.gradient` (kind run, first milestone M4) returns the design gradient of an orbit and of metrics measured on it, with a derivative-validity label on every number. The fixed point of the stride map moves with a design parameter as d x̄*/dθ = (I − M)⁻¹·∂P̄/∂θ, and a stride metric J changes as dJ/dθ = ∂J/∂θ + λᵀ·∂P̄/∂θ with (I − M)ᵀ·λ = ∇J, where M is the monodromy. The gradient is refused at a fold (I − M singular) and near grazing (a guard crossed with vanishing speed), with refusal FROB-REF-431 and one-sided estimates; simultaneous touchdowns tied by a symmetry are handled, not refused.

Its gate, GATE-GAIT-02 (Known answer, M4): on a 100-case suite, in at least 72 of 80 regular cases the gradient's cosine against central differences is at least 0.95; all 20 grazing and fold controls are labeled or refused; no case is refused for simultaneity alone; the bar fixture returns rest, v/4 and the sign of the coupling term. The proof obligation behind the first clause is PO-GAIT-02: design gradients agree with central differences away from labeled non-smooth points.

The gate has two ways to be passed dishonestly, and the script is built against both. One is a gradient routine that never refuses: it passes the regular cases and returns confident numbers at a fold. The controls catch it. The other is a routine that refuses whenever anything is irregular: it never returns a wrong number and is useless on a trot. The regular count (a refused regular case is a miss) and the simultaneity clause catch that.

The bar fixture is the hand case for the third clause: a rigid bar of mass m and central radius of gyration k arrives flat at speed v on two supports at ±a, with β = a²/k². Solved as one coupled impact it ends at rest. Solved one support first it leaves the first end moving at 2β·(1 − β)·v/(1 + β)², which is v/4 for β = 1/3. The off-diagonal contact-space term (1 − β)/m decides by its sign whether order matters.

## Scope
In: `scripts/e2e/gait_gradient.sh`, its assertions and logs; the suite run through the `frob` binary; the pass rule evaluated by the conformance tool; the bar clause; the gate's row; the registered negative control shown to fail.
Out: the verb and the suite's definition (BD-334); the orbit verb and its impact report (BD-333); the mechanisms (N22a-30 to N22a-37); the section and bordered forms (N22c-36, N22c-37); the decision on OPEN-GAIT-2, the order-sensitivity norm and tolerance (N22c-54). All of these are dependencies.

## Technical approach
**Inputs.** The frozen suite `crates/frob-conformance/registry/gait_gradient_suite.toml` (80 regular cases, 10 grazing controls, 10 fold controls, with thresholds), and the registered fixture `fixture:bar-two-supports`.

**Steps.**
0. *Preconditions.* The suite file's hash equals the one committed in its pre-registration record; the decision record for the order-sensitivity norm and tolerance (N22c-54) is read and its values logged; if it is absent the script logs `blocked: OPEN-GAIT-2`, exits non-zero and leaves the gate `not run`.
Every `run` call carries `--key` (without one it is refused with `FROB-REF-805`, exit 3); the key is derived from the suite's case identifier, so a resumed run replays a finished case's stored receipt, and the determinism lane of step 10 uses fresh run-scoped keys.
1. *Regular cases (80).* For each case: solve or load the orbit (`frob gait orbit <fixture or design> … --key gg-e2e-orb-<case> --json`, the fixture grammar of BD-333), then `frob gait gradient <orbit> --params <p1,p2,p3…> --metrics <m> --check fd --key gg-e2e-<case> --json` (for a registered fixture, `--wrt <k1,k2,k3>` over the continuous keys of its `--set` grammar in place of `--params`). The check re-solves the orbit at θ ± δ with events re-located and returns the central-difference gradient. Record the label, the gradient, the finite-difference gradient, their cosine, and the exit code. A case passes at cosine ≥ 0.95; a refused case is a miss.
2. *Closed-form subset.* For the sixteen rimless-wheel cases the gradient (`--wrt slope,l,g`, the parameters of BD-334's suite; the spoke count is an integer key and cannot be differentiated) is also compared with the closed form (for eight spokes, l = 0.5 m, γ = 4°, g = 9.81 m/s²: (∂/∂γ, ∂/∂l, ∂/∂g) of ω*² = (29.95984 s⁻²/rad, −4.189992 s⁻²/m, 0.2135572 m⁻¹)), within 10⁻⁵ relative. Reported; a disagreement fails the script even if the cosine passes.
3. *Grazing controls (10).* Each must exit 3 with FROB-REF-431 and a payload holding the offending state, the transversality number against its threshold, the perturbation directions and both one-sided estimates, with the ranked repairs in order (use the one-sided estimates returned; move the parameter; derivative-free search); or exit 2 with every returned entry labelled `one-sided` or `generalized`. An entry with any other of the nine labels (for example `smooth` or `saltation`) fails the clause.
4. *Fold controls (10).* The same rule, with the smallest singular value of I − M against its threshold in the payload.
5. *Simultaneity.* Among the forty regular cases with symmetry-tied touchdowns (trot, bound, tripod), count refusals whose only stated reason is simultaneity. The count must be zero. Where selections differ, the result must be `generalized` with one gradient per selection.
6. *Bar, coupled and ordered.* `frob gait orbit fixture:bar-two-supports --set m=2,v=1,beta=1/3 --impact-report --key gg-e2e-bar-1 --json` (the impact report carries N22a-34's group report with per-ordering impulses; `--impact-report` is a diagnostic input of BD-333's grammar): the coupled outcome is rest with impulses 1 N·s at each support; each ordering gives a first impulse of 1.5 N·s, a second of 0.75 N·s, and the first end leaving at 0.25 m/s; surviving kinetic energy 1/16 of 1 J; coupling term +1/3 kg⁻¹, reported as `positive`; order sensitivity (the largest difference over pairs of selections, the measure N22c-54 decides) √3/4 = 0.433013 normalized and 0.612372 in the mass norm, attained by the two mirror orderings, with each ordering 0.25 normalized from the coupled solve; verdict order-sensitive. Then `beta=2` (key `gg-e2e-bar-2`): rest for every ordering; coupling term −0.5 kg⁻¹, reported as `negative`. Tolerance 10⁻⁹ relative on the event-located profile. On the compliant tier with β = 1/3: two selection classes and the label `generalized`.
7. *Routing.* Every refused entry in steps 3 and 4 carries `route: derivative-free` naming the studio's search verb and the parameter box.
8. *Pass rule.* The per-case records are handed to the conformance crate's tier run (`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-GAIT-02`, the Gauntlet's cargo form; there is no `frob` command for it), which recomputes cosines from the logged vectors, evaluates the four clauses (regular passes ≥ 72 of 80; controls 20 of 20; simultaneity-only refusals 0; bar clause true), runs the gate's registered negative controls in the same run, and writes a hash-named receipt. The script prints the receipt hash.
9. *Gate row.* This bead is the one recorder of GATE-GAIT-02 (BD-334 keeps only unit-level counts): the row is entered from step 8's receipt, never by the script, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-GAIT-02 --receipt <hash>`, by a session that did not implement the gradient, carrying the four measured clauses, the thresholds in force (grazing, fold, order-sensitivity norm and tolerance), the negative controls `caught`, commit, hardware fingerprint, and the adjudicating tier per case class.
10. *Surfaces, cancellation, determinism.* One trot case through the daemon and the MCP tool `gait_gradient` with identical envelopes; a running request cancelled cleanly; five cases repeated twice more under fresh keys `gg-e2e-det-a-<run-id>-<case>` and `gg-e2e-det-b-<run-id>-<case>`, the second with a different `--workers` count (the operator-only input of BD-334), with identical outputs bit for bit and neither envelope a replayed receipt.

**Idempotence and resumption.** Each case has an idempotency key from the suite's case identifier; an interrupted run resumes and reaches the same tallies.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/gait_gradient.sh` exists, sources the shared helper, runs steps 0 to 10 against the real binaries, and exits 0 on a correct build.
- [ ] All 100 cases have a record with exit code, label and, for regular cases, both gradient vectors and their cosine; the tally sums to 100.
- [ ] At least 72 of the 80 regular cases have cosine ≥ 0.95, and the rimless-wheel subset also matches its closed form.
- [ ] All 20 controls are refused with FROB-REF-431 or labelled `one-sided` or `generalized`; none returns another label.
- [ ] No case is refused for simultaneity alone.
- [ ] The bar returns rest when coupled, v/4 when ordered at β = 1/3, rest for every ordering at β = 2, and the sign of the coupling term in both cases.
- [ ] GATE-GAIT-02 is recorded through the gate recorder from the tier run's receipt (step 8), with the four clause results, the thresholds in force and every registered negative control `caught` in that receipt.
- [ ] The gate's registered negative control fails the script: a build with the grazing and fold thresholds set to zero returns `smooth` or `saltation` gradients on the controls (step 3 or 4 fails).
- [ ] Three further planted defects each fail at the expected step: a build that refuses every stride with a simultaneous touchdown (step 5); an impact analysis that forces an order for the coupled case (step 6); a gradient that omits the direct term ∂J/∂θ (step 1, on the mass-parameter cases).

## Test plan
### Unit tests
A self-test of the script's helpers, `scripts/e2e/selftest/gait_gradient_selftest.sh`: the cosine helper returns 0.9986 for (1, 2, 3) against (1.1, 1.9, 3.2) and 0.3223 against (3, −1, 1); the tally helper rejects a run with 99 records; the control checker fails on a planted `smooth` label; the simultaneity checker distinguishes "refused: simultaneity" from "refused: grazing".
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gait_gradient.sh` as specified. Logging through the shared helper to `target/e2e-logs/gait_gradient/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22a-39`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time, and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities of design, controller and orbit, seed). Per case an `assert` line with the case identifier and class, the label, the cosine against 0.95 or the refusal code, the fold margin and transversality numbers against their thresholds, and on a miss the two gradient vectors with the parameter whose entries differ most and the paths of the orbit and path records. `refusal` lines carry code, payload summary and repairs. `metric` lines for the bar carry impulses in N·s, velocities in m/s, the coupling term in kg⁻¹ and the order sensitivity. The final `summary` line gives the four clause counts, assertions passed and failed, and `gates: ["GATE-GAIT-02"]`.
### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-564 (the suite's counts and thresholds); KA-563 (the bar: rest; v/4 and 1/16 at β = 1/3; rest for β ≥ 1; coupling (1 − β)/m; negative control, a solver that forces an order reports rocking for the coupled case); KA-560 (rimless wheel) with its closed-form gradient; KA-095 and KA-097 (grazing refusal; validation with re-located events).
- Counterexamples exercised: CX-238 (simultaneous impacts in a fixed order), CX-043 (a derivative at grazing).
- Negative controls: the four planted defects above.
- Metamorphic relations: permuting the suite's order changes no tally; a case's gradient along a typed edit equals the sum of its parameter entries times the edit's parameter changes; rescaling a parameter's unit rescales its entry and leaves the cosine unchanged when both vectors use the same scales.
- Determinism lanes: five cases twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release.

## Considerations
- A cosine near one does not say the magnitudes agree; the record keeps both vectors, and the closed-form subset checks magnitudes where a truth exists.
- The suite's composition and thresholds are frozen before any regular case runs. If a threshold must change after data, the gate register's rule applies: a dated amendment with its reason, made before the measurement it governs, and the whole suite is re-run.
- Twenty-four regular cases run through the event-section path (N22c-36, a dependency). If one of them cannot run it is a miss, not an exclusion: the regular clause is never rescaled below 72 of 80.
- The kill gate for gaits is decided on continuation and rank agreement, not on this gate. A failure here has no kill gate: it blocks the milestone exit until repaired.
