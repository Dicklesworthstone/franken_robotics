## Background
The failure taxonomy of the record subsystem (BD-094) gives every episode a terminal class and a first cause, and N24a-38 adds ten classes of the world and perception group, each with a monitor that is a pure function of committed telemetry, a planted fixture and a clean control (gate GATE-CHRON-08: each failure class's monitor fires on its planted fixture). One of them, `gait-instability`, is entered by N24a-38 with `status = unavailable`: its monitor needs stride events of a declared periodic gait and the scaled coordinates in which the gait analysis measures a deviation, and both come from the gait crate. N24a-38 names the bead that lands that feature as the one that switches the row to `available` and extends the GATE-CHRON-08 fixture list. The gait crate's stride map (N22a-29) is that feature; the monitor itself belongs to `frob-chronicle`, a different crate and layer, so it is built here, after both.

The class answers a question a designer asks of a failed walking run: did the gait lose stability stride by stride before the robot fell, or did it fall for another reason? The orbit analysis answers the same question for an orbit (a spectral radius above one, N22a-32); this monitor answers it for an episode, from telemetry alone.

Row of N24a-38 (DESIGN default, kept): enabling feature, *the controller declares a periodic gait*; first-anomaly predicate, *the stride-to-stride deviation of the base state at the stride event (norm in the scaled coordinates of the gait analysis) grows by a factor above 1 for 4 consecutive strides and exceeds 3 times its value at the first of them*; channels, kinematics at stride events; fixture, a trot at a commanded speed above the controller's stable range; control, the nominal speed. Tier: Solid for the monitor; the fixture uses the Frontier orbit route only to certify that its planted speed is unstable.

## Scope
In: the stride-event extractor over committed telemetry; the deviation in the base part of N22a-29's reduced chart; the predicate with a noise floor; the monitor as a level-T1 event with its first-anomaly tick; the planted fixture and its control, with the planted speed chosen and pinned by the orbit route; the row's switch to `available` with a version bump; the extension of GATE-CHRON-08's fixture list and the re-run of its fixture rows; first-cause ordering of this class against `fall`; tests and the end-to-end script.
Out: the taxonomy, kinds, first-cause rule and `telemetry.failures` (BD-094); the row's creation, the gating and admission rule and the other classes (N24a-38); the stride map, chart and scales (N22a-29); multipliers (N22a-32); the orbit verb (BD-333); recovery from the instability, which is the controller's (the baseline beads).

## Technical approach
**Layering.** `frob-chronicle` (layer 6) reads `frob-gait` (layer 4) through the crate feature `gait`; nothing in `frob-gait` names `frob-chronicle`. With the feature off the row stays `unavailable` and the admission rule of N24a-38 applies unchanged.

**Stride events.** From the run's controller record (N22a-07): a loop with phase source `clock` has stride events at the control ticks where its gait clock returns to `phase0` (the integer counter modulo N, committed with the controller's layer record); a loop with phase source `event-reset` has them at its declared section event (N22c-36's section, read from the same record); a loop with phase source `none` declares no periodic gait, so the feature predicate is false and the class is absent from the output (N24a-38's no-phantom rule).

**Deviation.** At stride event k the monitor reduces the committed base kinematics with N22a-29's `ReducedChart` (removed coordinates dropped, the same scales: lengths by ℓ₀, speeds by √(g·ℓ₀), rates by √(g/ℓ₀)) to the base part b_k = (p_z, tilt, body twist), and sets d_k = ‖b_k − b_{k−1}‖₂ in those dimensionless coordinates. Only base kinematics are read, so the monitor works on runs whose joint and controller states were not logged.

**Predicate.** A growth run starts at stride j when d_{j+1} > d_j and continues while each next d is larger than the one before. The monitor fires at the first stride i of a growth run with i − j ≥ 4, d_i > 3·d_j and d_i > d_floor, and records the tick of stride i as the first-anomaly tick, with j, d_j, d_i and the growth factors in the event. d_floor is a threshold added to the row here: DESIGN default 10⁻² in the scaled norm. Without it, a stable loop whose deviation has settled to solver noise produces four consecutive increases and a threefold rise at round-off level, and the monitor fires on a healthy gait (the noise control below shows it). The value is fixed on the clean control before the planted fixture is first run.

**Row switch.** In `registries/failure-classes.toml` (BD-094's file) the `gait-instability` row gains `thresholds.growth_strides = 4`, `thresholds.growth_ratio = 3`, `thresholds.d_floor = 0.01` (dimensionless, scaled chart), its `fixture` names the planted and clean fixtures below, `available_from = "M4"` and `status = "available"`, and its version is bumped (a change of predicate or threshold bumps the row's version, N24a-38). The admission check of N24a-38 then admits runs whose controller declares a periodic gait, and their `telemetry.failures` output carries the `gait-instability` key, with count 0 when it did not fire.

**Fixtures (synthetic, paired).** Body and controller: the reference quadruped `quad-ref` under its synthesized baseline trot on `batch-cpu` in `f64`, flat ground. The planted speed is chosen by the orbit route, so that "above the controller's stable range" is a measured fact and not a guess: commanded speeds from the nominal 0.6 m/s upward in steps of 0.1 m/s to the actuator envelope, the trot orbit solved at each by N22a-31 and its spectral radius ρ computed by N22a-32; the first speed with ρ > 1 (and a full-space spectrum, not a restricted one) is the planted fixture, pinned in the fixture file with its ρ and the orbit's identity. If no speed within the envelope has ρ > 1, the planted fixture is the nominal speed with the baseline's stance gain detuned until ρ > 1 (BD-335's planted unstable parent), and the row's fixture text names that variant. The clean control is the nominal speed, ρ < 1. Each run is 20 s from the settled nominal state plus one seeded push of 0.05 in the scaled norm.

**First cause.** A planted run usually ends in `fall`. Both events are recorded; the first-cause table of BD-094 names `gait-instability` as the first cause of that `fall` when its first-anomaly tick precedes the fall's.

**Determinism and evidence.** The monitor is a pure function of committed telemetry and the controller record: class D0 given the run. Counts and events are reported in BD-094's evidence block (class `Tested` for counts produced by the harness, `Estimated` for the first-cause ordering, as that bead fixes).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Synthetic stride sequences from x ↦ λ·x with x₀ = 0.1 (labelled `synthetic`): λ = 1.5 fires at stride 5 (d₅ = 0.253125); λ = 1.3 at stride 6; λ = 1.2 at stride 8 (d₈ = 0.0716636); λ = −1.2 (a flip instability) at stride 8 (d₈ = 0.788300); λ = 0.8 never; λ = 1.2 from x₀ = 10⁻⁹ fires only at stride 99 (d₉₉ = 0.0115025), when the deviation first exceeds d_floor.
- [ ] The noise control: λ = 0.8 with seeded additive noise of amplitude 10⁻⁶ per stride over 10⁴ strides never fires with d_floor = 10⁻², and the mutant without the floor fires on it.
- [ ] The planted speed is pinned with its ρ > 1 from a full-space spectrum and the orbit identity; the clean control's ρ < 1 is recorded beside it.
- [ ] On the planted fixture the monitor fires in each of 8 seeded worlds, with the first-anomaly tick before any `fall`; on the clean control it fires in none of 8 worlds over 20 s; the counts, ticks and d values are recorded.
- [ ] The row reads `available` with the new thresholds and a bumped version; a run of `quad-ref` with its trot is admitted and its `telemetry.failures` output has the `gait-instability` key; a loop with phase source `none` has no such key.
- [ ] On a planted run that falls, the first-cause table names `gait-instability` as the first cause of the `fall`, and both events are listed.
- [ ] GATE-CHRON-08's fixture list contains the `gait-instability` pair; its fixture rows pass in the tier run, whose receipt has every registered control `caught`, and the gate is recorded with the new list from that receipt through the gate recorder; the script itself writes no gate row.
- [ ] With the crate feature `gait` off the row stays `unavailable`, and N24a-38's admission refusal for a periodic-gait run names this bead.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`crates/frob-chronicle/tests/gait_instability.rs` (feature `gait`) on the real monitor and the real `ReducedChart`; the robot cases on committed telemetry of real runs of the batched step.
- `stride_events_from_clock`: a clock with N = 25 and phase0 = 3 gives events at ticks 3, 28, 53, …; `stride_events_from_section` (an event-reset loop's section events); `no_periodic_gait_no_class` (phase source `none`: the key is absent).
- `deviation_in_scaled_base_chart`: a base offset of 0.1·ℓ₀ in height gives d = 0.1; yaw and planar position changes give d = 0 (removed coordinates).
- `fires_lambda_1_5_at_5`, `fires_lambda_1_3_at_6`, `fires_lambda_1_2_at_8`, `fires_flip_at_8`, `stable_never_fires`, `floor_delays_to_99` (the values above).
- `growth_run_resets_on_decrease`: d = (1, 2, 3, 2.5, 4, 5, 6, 7.6) fires at the last entry (run from 2.5: four increases, 7.6 > 3 × 2.5), not earlier.
- `noise_floor_control` and the mutant `no_floor_fires_on_noise`.
- `first_anomaly_tick_recorded`; `first_cause_precedes_fall`.
- `row_switch_bumps_version`; `admission_after_switch`; `feature_off_keeps_unavailable`.
- `monitor_is_pure` (the same telemetry gives the same event bit for bit, twice and in debug and release).
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gait_instability_monitor.sh` (plain shell, idempotent, seed 2203 printed): (1) the planted speed's pin is read and its orbit re-solved by the test binary's orbit step, asserting ρ > 1 at the planted speed and ρ < 1 at the nominal one; (2) `frob sim run quad-ref --scene <fixture scene> --task <planted task: trot at the pinned speed> --worlds 8 --seconds 20 --tier batch-cpu --key gim-planted-<run-id> --json` and the same for the control with `--key gim-control-<run-id>` (distinct keys, so neither replays the other or an earlier run's receipt); (3) `frob telemetry failures <run> --json` on each, asserting the counts (8 of 8 planted, 0 of 8 control), the first-anomaly ticks before any fall, and the first-cause table; (4) a run of a loop whose controller declares phase source `none`, asserting that the key is absent; (5) the tier run `FROB_TEST_SEED=2203 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-CHRON-08`, asserting that the extended fixture list passes, that the gate's registered negative controls are `caught` in the same run, and printing the hash-named receipt it writes under `target/gauntlet/receipts/`; the script writes no gate row. Recording (not part of the script): the extended list is entered from that receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-CHRON-08 --receipt <hash>` by a session that did not implement the monitor, as the extension N24a-38 assigns to the bead that switches the row (one recorder per gate: which bead the register names for a later class's re-run is requested of N29b-06, which holds the gate's M2 row). Logging through the shared helper of STD-E2E to `target/e2e-logs/gait_instability_monitor/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line with `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22ap2-03`), `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines with the exact command line, exit code and wall time and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, `ModelId`, `ControllerId`, seed); `metric` lines with ρ at both speeds, per world the stride at which the monitor fired, d_j, d_i against 3·d_j and d_floor, and the fall tick; `assert` lines for the counts, the absent key and the first cause; on failure the world, the stride sequence of d and the run identifiers needed to reproduce; a final `summary` naming GATE-CHRON-08.
### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the synthetic firing strides and values above (exact in `f64` to 10⁻¹²).
- Negative controls: the monitor without d_floor (fires on the noise control); a monitor that compares d_i with d_{i−1} only (fires on the first two increases; caught by `growth_run_resets_on_decrease` and the noise control); a monitor that reads the full state instead of the base part (fails on a run with joint states not logged, and changes counts with logging level); the class reported with count 0 on a loop with no periodic gait (the no-phantom rule fails).
- Metamorphic: rescaling the design by dynamic similarity leaves the firing stride unchanged; translating or yawing the start pose leaves every d unchanged.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; across worker counts of the runs (the monitor sees identical telemetry).

## Considerations
- The predicate and its thresholds are DESIGN proposals of N24a-38 and of this bead. d_floor is new; N24a-38's table is asked to state it (a request in round 2), so that the two beads describe one predicate.
- From M3, when N24a-38 enters the row as `unavailable`, its admission rule (a feature on with its class unavailable is not admitted) would refuse every run whose controller declares a periodic gait, which is every legged baseline from M1, until this bead lands at M4. That is N24a-38's to settle (a request in round 2: exempt rows of later waves from rule (a) until their delivering bead, or narrow the feature predicate); this bead removes the condition once it lands.
- The monitor measures loss of stride-to-stride stability, not every way a gait can fail: a gait that drifts slowly to a fall without growth over four strides is `fall` with another first cause, or none.
- Using the orbit route to pin the planted speed ties the fixture to the Frontier analysis; if that route is unavailable (KILL-GAIT fired), the orbits still serve stability diagnostics, which is all the fixture needs.
