## Background

Reflex synthesizes controllers from models. For a symmetric body the natural economy is a symmetric
controller: tied gains, a linearization split into blocks. The economy is only correct when the *closed
loop in its present situation* has the symmetry. Two groups are stored for each robot-plus-task pair: Γ_mech
(automorphisms of the design that preserve link lengths, pseudo-inertias, joint screws with their reflection
parity, actuators and gear ratios) and Γ_obs ≤ Γ_mech (those that also preserve every sensor pose and field
of view, the payload, the task's goal, and the symmetry of terrain and guards). A quadruped built symmetric
fore-aft and left-right has Γ_mech = C₂ × C₂; with a front-only lidar its closed loop has Γ_obs = C₂. The
group a calculation may actually use is smaller still:

    Γ_use = Γ_obs ∩ Stab(gravity in the body frame) ∩ Stab(boundary conditions)
                  ∩ Stab(current contact mode) ∩ Stab(commanded twist),

computed when it is used. On a side slope the left-right mirror leaves Stab(gravity) and the controller's
left and right gains untie. With an off-centre payload the mirror is not in Γ_obs at all. A reduction
requested under a larger group is refused (FROB-REF-461, invariant INV-REFLEX-04), and the full model always
stays executable.

Decision ADR-REFLEX-07: a symmetric controller is tied only under the intersection of the design's group
with the stabilizers of payload, gravity, boundary conditions, task, observations and current contact mode,
because a group that the closed loop does not have constrains the controller to something false. The idea
this serves is "symmetry-aware control under broken symmetry": use only the generators that the current
payload, terrain and sensors leave intact, found by intersecting stabilizers, and report each breaker.

This bead is the user-visible capability: the verb `control.symmetry`, which returns Γ_mech, Γ_obs, the
stabilizers in force and Γ_use, with the generators each stabilizer removed; assembled from the usable-group
computation (N22b-37), the reduction with its reduced-against-full check (N22b-38), the parity and
in-law rules (N22b-39) and the event identity (N22b-40); and measured at GATE-REFLEX-07. Tier Solid [S].

## Scope

In: the verb, its schema and envelope; wiring of Γ_use into controller synthesis and the `ControllerId`;
the gate's three rows and their fixtures; the gate record. Out: computing Γ_mech and Γ_obs (the static
symmetry analysis and `sense.symmetry`, prerequisites); equivariant policies and the three-arm experiment
(Cortex); the end-to-end script (N22b-41).

## Technical approach

**`control.symmetry`** (kind `read`, first milestone M4, cluster `control`). Input schema
`frob.control.symmetry.in/1`: a design, a task, and an optional context (gravity direction in the body
frame or a slope; a world patch or scene; a contact mode; a commanded twist; default: level ground,
four-foot or all-wheel stance, zero twist), and optional `--events <class>` to run the event-identity
check. Output schema `frob.control.symmetry.out/1`:

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| `mech` | Γ_mech: order, generators, each element's isometry (exact matrix and determinant), body permutation, signed joint permutation |
| `obs` | Γ_obs with, for each element of Γ_mech outside it, the breaker (the sensor mount, payload, task term or scene property) and the typed edit that would restore it |
| `stabilizers` | For gravity, boundary conditions, contact mode and commanded twist: the subgroup, and the elements of the running intersection it removed, with the reason and as generators |
| `use` | Γ_use for the context, its identity, and the equivariance mode (pathwise or in law) the controller manifest declares |
| `events` (on request) | Per generator and event class: identity residual against tolerance, or a grazing flag |
| `evidence` | Claim product: exact contexts give determinism class `exact` and a certificate reference where emitted; toleranced contexts give `Tested` with the tolerance |

The verb changes nothing. It is cheap (finite groups of at most a few dozen elements) except for the
optional event check, which may simulate a landing and therefore accepts a budget.

**Wiring.** Controller synthesis asks this module for Γ_use per contact mode of the controller's schedule
and may tie gains or block-diagonalize only under the returned group; the group used per mode is part of
the `ControllerId`; `control.show` prints it. A request under a larger group is refused:

| Code | Meaning | Trigger | Ranked repairs |
|---|---|---|---|
| FROB-REF-461 | A symmetric reduction was requested under a group not contained in Γ_use | A reduction names an element outside Γ_use; or the reduced-against-full comparison disagrees on either side of a mode switch (a wrong stabilizer) | Use Γ_use; remove the asymmetry with the typed edit that the static symmetry analysis proposes |

Exit code 3; `pointer` names the element and the stabilizer that removed it.

**The gate.** GATE-REFLEX-07 (known-answer claim, TARGET, M4): *planted asymmetries (side slope, off-centre
payload, front-only sensor, one-sided patch) remove exactly the expected generators; the two-latch fixture
passes in law and fails tied; the event identity holds within tolerance on regular landings and is flagged
on grazing ones.*

Row 1 fixtures, on the reference quadruped with Γ_mech = {e, m_lr, m_fa, r} (left-right mirror, fore-aft
mirror, half-turn about the vertical):

| Planted asymmetry | Expected removal | Expected remaining group (standing, level unless stated) |
|---|---|---|
| Side slope of 10° about the forward axis | Stab(gravity) removes m_lr and r | {e, m_fa} |
| Off-centre payload at +y on the transverse mid-plane | Γ_obs loses m_lr and r (breaker: the payload) | {e, m_fa} |
| Front-only sensor (lidar) | Γ_obs loses m_fa and r (breaker: the mount) | {e, m_lr}, that is C₂ |
| One-sided slippery patch covering y > 0 | Stab(boundary conditions) removes m_lr and r | {e, m_fa} |

"Exactly" means the removed set equals the expected set: no element more, none fewer.

Row 2: the two-latch fixture: the in-law controller succeeds on every run; the tied deterministic controller
fails on every run; the report says the tied deterministic class is excluded and nothing more.

Row 3: the event identity D g(x⁺)·Ξ_e(x⁻) = Ξ_(g·e)(g·x⁻)·D g(x⁻) on a reflected four-foot landing with left
and right sole laws exchanged: within tolerance on regular landings, flagged on grazing ones.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob control symmetry` exists, is generated from the verb registry, validates against its schema and
      is reachable through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`.
- [ ] GATE-REFLEX-07 is recorded with measured values: the four planted asymmetries remove exactly the
      expected generators; the two-latch fixture passes in law and fails tied; the event identity holds
      within the recorded tolerance on regular landings and is flagged on grazing ones.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: tying under Γ_mech on the one-sided patch loses return against the
      terrain-conditioned controller; a stabilizer test that treats angular velocity as polar is caught by
      the yaw-command case; an image event without exchanged sole laws fails the identity.
- [ ] FROB-REF-461 fires on a reduction requested under Γ_mech where Γ_use is smaller, with pointer and both
      repairs; INV-REFLEX-04 holds (no reduction without the usable-group token).
- [ ] Synthesized controllers record the group used per mode in their `ControllerId`; on the side slope the
      left and right gains are untied.
- [ ] The full model stays executable: `--verify-reduction` compares reduced and full commands, including
      across a mode switch.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-control` covers the symmetry surface; outputs carry the claim product; the verb
      has a CPU path only and needs none other.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-control/tests/symmetry_verb.rs`, `crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_reflex_07.rs`.

- `gate_planted_asymmetries`: the four rows of the table, asserting set equality of removed elements.
- `gate_two_latch`: in-law 10⁴ of 10⁴ successes; tied deterministic 0 successes; report wording.
- `gate_event_identity`: regular landing within tolerance; grazing landing flagged.
- `verb_output_blocks`: every block present; breakers carry a typed edit; the forward-command context gives
  Stab(twist) = {e, m_lr}; the trot stance gives Stab(mode) = {e, r}.
- `refusal_461_envelope`: code, pointer (`m_lr` removed by `gravity`), repairs in order, exit code 3.
- `controller_id_group`: the same design synthesized on level ground and on the slope has different
  `ControllerId`s and the group fields differ.
- `default_context`: with no context the verb reports level, standing, zero twist and says so.
- `radial_hexapod` (a second group family): a radially symmetric hexapod with a dihedral Γ_mech of order
  12; a front-only sensor leaves the single mirror through the sensor axis (order 2); a forward command
  along that axis leaves the same mirror.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/control_symmetry.sh` (bead N22b-41) drives the verb over the gate fixtures and writes the
NDJSON log; its `summary` names GATE-REFLEX-07.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-575 (planted asymmetries: exactly the expected generators are removed; a side slope removes the
left-right mirror; a front lidar leaves C₂; control: tying under Γ_mech loses return on the one-sided
patch), KA-574 (two latches: the in-law controller succeeds, the tied deterministic one fails), KA-068,
KA-069, KA-070, KA-071 (the same facts under the construction). CX-244 (tie a controller's weights under
the mechanical symmetry group), CX-031, CX-032, CX-033. Determinism lanes: group computations are class D0
and identical on every host, in debug and release; the landing simulations are class D2 on their tier.

## Considerations

- The hexapod's 500,500-against-86,185 orbit count under a front-only lidar (a campaign-quotienting fact)
  is the same lesson at campaign scale: the acting group is Γ_obs, never Γ_mech alone.
- Hard equivariance of learned policies is a separate bet with its own kill gate; if that gate fires,
  symmetry is used for augmentation and metamorphic testing only. This verb and the baseline controller's
  use of Γ_use are Solid and unaffected.
- The expected rows are derived from the parity rule; the plan states two of them outright (the side slope
  and the front lidar) and names the other two asymmetries without their sets. The derivation is recorded in
  the coverage notes so the gate register can confirm it.
- Tolerance for "gravity fixed" and for the event identity are declared numbers recorded in the receipt.
