## Background

A runtime shield's guarantee on a reduced-order template reaches the robot only through the tracking error
between the template and the full body, and that error is measured: the margin ε̂ is the
⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉-th smallest of n per-episode maximum tracking errors, keyed by design class, controller or
policy identity, task family and tier; a running test on conformal p-values raises a drift alarm when
exchangeability fails. A measured quantity deserves a measurement of its own: does the margin actually cover
at the stated rate on episodes it has not seen, does it refuse to be borrowed, and does the alarm fire when
the world moves? That is the gate GATE-SHIELD-04, and this bead measures it.

The same bead delivers the read-side verb through which an agent sees what a shield did: `shield.report`
returns interventions, alarms, the violation bound and the return kept. Without it a shielded result is a
number with no account of how often the filter or the backup produced the behaviour; the project's rule is
that "it worked" is not a result if the backup controller did the work (ADR-REFLEX-08).

It also enforces the plan's rule for every adaptive element of the shield (the margin calibrator, the drift
alarm, the alarm thresholds): it ships with a replay scenario, evidence records, a calibration report and a
demonstrated fallback, or it does not ship.

GATE-SHIELD-04 (known-answer claim, TARGET, M4): *measured margin: empirical coverage at least 1 − α − 0.01
on 10⁴ held-out episodes of the calibrated family; a planted change of policy trips FROB-REF-457; a planted
drift trips the alarm.*

## Scope

In: `shield.report`; the coverage measurement on 10⁴ held-out episodes; the two planted controls; the
adaptive-element checklist and its enforcement at fit; the gate receipt. Out: the calibration and the alarm
themselves (N22b-29, N22b-30); the bound's estimator (N22b-26); the in-envelope and shifted campaign
(BD-339).

## Technical approach

**`shield.report`** (kind `read`, first milestone M4). Input `frob.shield.report.in/1`: a `ShieldId`, and
optionally a run, a batch of runs or a campaign identity to restrict the account. Output
`frob.shield.report.out/1`:

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| Identity | `ShieldId`, scope key (design class, policy or controller identity, task family, tier), levels α and budget with their source |
| Interventions | Ticks with a projected command: count, fraction of ticks, per row; mean and largest ‖u − u_π‖ in the metric R; flag `shield-dominated` when the fraction exceeds the declared fraction |
| Backup | Ticks under the backup, by reason (`row-infeasible`, `rows-conflict`, optimality check, alarm, energy cut); fraction of episodes that ended in the backup |
| Alarms | Per monitor: count, rate per episode, first alarm tick distribution |
| Violation bound | The three numbers with intervals, the stated bound, n, label source `hidden-state`, the statement that the numbers are for this policy only |
| Shifted worlds | One row per declared shifted family, separate, with the statement that the bound is not claimed there |
| Return kept | Ratio shielded over unshielded with its interval, on common world addresses, when a paired set exists |
| Margin | ε̂, ε̂ on the fast tier, the tier term, n, α, regime identity, drift statistic M_t and threshold, state (`valid`, `margin-recalibrating`, `retired`) |
| Cost | Added step cost with its disclosure block when measured |
| Evidence | Claim product: class `Tested` (or `Estimated` in the recalibrating state), tier, determinism, `reality: no-claim`, the three no-authorization flags |

The verb changes nothing and is cheap: it reads stored records. Long accounts are paginated and say so.

**Coverage measurement (gate row 1).** For the subject shields of the campaign bead (the reference quadruped
with its trained policy, and the wheeled base): draw 10⁴ held-out worlds by address from the calibrated
family, disjoint from the calibration set; run one episode each on the calibration tier (the gate's known
answer names the lockstep batched tier) with the shield on; count the episodes whose score (largest tracking
error) is at most ε̂. Decision rule, enumerated: with α = 10⁻³ the threshold is coverage ≥ 0.989, that is
at most 110 exceedances among 10⁴; counts 0 to 110 pass, 111 and above fail. (Under exact calibration about
10 exceedances are expected; the 0.01 allowance leaves room for calibration-draw variability.)

**Planted change of policy (gate row 2).** Take the fitted shield and request a run with a different policy
identity on the same design and task: `shield.fit --reuse-margin` and the run-time start must both refuse
with FROB-REF-457 (*a shield margin is used outside its scope key*; repair: recalibrate for the new policy,
class, task family or tier). The planted policy is one whose tracking is visibly worse, so that a borrowed
margin would in fact under-cover (measured and recorded: its exceedance rate under the borrowed margin).

**Planted drift (gate row 3).** A stream of episodes that switches at a known index from the calibrated
family to a family with degraded tracking (DESIGN: actuator torque limits reduced by 30%, which raises
tracking error without changing the task). The drift alarm must fire after the switch within the recorded
delay and must not fire before it.

**Shipping rule for adaptive elements.** For each of the three elements a checklist record:

| Element | Replay scenario | Evidence records | Calibration report | Demonstrated fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Margin calibrator | Calibrate, then replay the held-out coverage set | Scores digest, index, key | The margin's report | Refusal to run without a margin (FROB-REF-457) |
| Drift alarm | The planted-drift stream | Logged p-values and M_t | Null false-alarm rate and detection delay | The widened-margin recalibrating state with direct routing to the backup |
| Alarm thresholds (each calibrated monitor threshold) | A planted breach and a nominal stream | Alarm events with values | Threshold derivation with its data | The declared static threshold from the card |

`shield.fit` reads the checklist; an element with a missing entry is not enabled, and the fit fails with a
typed error naming the element and the missing companion.

**Receipt.** Coverage count per subject against 110, ε̂, n and α; the two controls' outcomes with refusal
envelopes and the alarm index; the checklist state; commit, fingerprint, seeds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob shield report` exists, is generated from the verb registry, validates against its schema and is
      reachable through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`; it prints every block of the table above.
- [ ] GATE-SHIELD-04 is recorded with measured values: empirical coverage on 10⁴ held-out episodes at least
      1 − α − 0.01 for each subject (at α = 10⁻³: at most 110 exceedances); the planted change of policy
      trips FROB-REF-457 at fit and at run time; the planted drift trips the alarm after the switch and not
      before.
- [ ] The borrowed-margin control is shown to matter: under the planted policy the borrowed margin's
      exceedance count is recorded and exceeds 110.
- [ ] The shipping rule holds: each of the three adaptive elements has its four companions on file, and
      removing any one makes `shield.fit` fail naming it.
- [ ] Reports never show a violation bound for a shifted family or for a different policy, and always carry
      class `Tested` or weaker, `reality: no-claim` and the flags.
- [ ] If KILL-SHIELD fired for a class, `shield.report` for that class reports alarms and interventions
      only, with no bound and without the word shield.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-shield/tests/report.rs`, `crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_shield_04.rs`.

- `coverage_rule`: α = 10⁻³, n = 10⁴: 110 exceedances pass, 111 fail; α = 10⁻²: threshold coverage 0.98,
  200 pass, 201 fail.
- `coverage_synthetic` (`synthetic`): scores from a fixed distribution, calibration n = 2,000, held-out 10⁴:
  exceedances within the binomial range around 10 and below 110.
- `policy_change_refused`: both refusal points, code, pointer `policy`, repair text.
- `borrowed_margin_undercovers`: the planted policy's scores exceed the borrowed ε̂ in more than 110 of 10⁴.
- `drift_alarm_fires_after_switch`: alarm index greater than the switch index and within the recorded delay.
- `report_blocks_present`: every block; `shield-dominated` set when the intervention fraction is above the
  declared one.
- `no_bound_under_shift`: a report restricted to a shifted campaign shows counts and no bound.
- `checklist_enforced`: delete each of the twelve companions in turn; twelve typed failures.
- `pagination`: an account over 10⁵ events is paginated with a cursor and says so.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/gate_shield_04.sh` (shared helper): fits the shield, runs the 10⁴ held-out episodes with
`frob eval run`, reads coverage through `frob shield report --json`, then performs the policy-change and
drift scenarios. NDJSON lines carry the command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, the exceedance
count against 110, ε̂ with n and α, the refusal envelopes, M_t per batch against its threshold, the alarm
index against the switch index; on failure the first exceeding episodes' world addresses. `summary` lists
`gates: ["GATE-SHIELD-04"]`. The user-side smoke of the verb is in `scripts/e2e/shield_runtime.sh`
(N22b-32).

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-573 (measured-margin shield on 10⁴ held-out episodes: empirical coverage at least 1 − α − 0.01; tier
lockstep; negative controls: a planted change of policy trips FROB-REF-457, a planted drift trips the alarm).
Further planted failures: a calibrator that uses rank ⌈n·(1 − α)⌉ (under-covers); a report that pools
shifted worlds into the in-envelope bound (must be caught by `no_bound_under_shift`). Determinism: counts are
exact given class D2 runs; the gate is run twice on one machine and across worker counts with identical
counts.

## Considerations

- OPEN-SHIELD-2 fixes α; the gate reads it from the single registry entry, and a receipt whose α differs is
  rejected.
- Coverage is marginal over calibration draws. A single unlucky calibration can under-cover while the method
  is sound; the 0.01 allowance is the plan's answer, and a failure triggers recalibration with a fresh draw
  once before it counts.
- The report is the place where an agent learns that a result was produced by the backup; its intervention
  and backup fractions should be read before any return figure.
- Privacy: reports contain world addresses and counts, never scene content.
