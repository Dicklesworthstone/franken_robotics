## Background

Every runtime safety layer needs something to fall back on. When the energy monitor's margin reaches zero,
when a shield's row cannot be met inside the actuator box, when a multi-row solve fails its optimality check,
or when an assumption monitor raises an alarm, the command source switches to a **backup controller**. The
backup must be simpler than what it replaces and must come with a statement of where it works: a declared
domain. Backups live in `frob-control`, each with a declared domain.

| Class | Backup | Property |
|---|---|---|
| Wheeled, tracked | Damping injection on the drive outputs, then brakes where the card has them | With collocated outputs y and K_d ⪰ 0, the continuous-time command u = −K_d·y gives dH/dt ≤ −yᵀ·K_d·y ≤ 0 in the port model. A command sampled and held for a period h keeps the stored energy from increasing at the ticks only under the gain bound 2·M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0 on the reflected inertia M̄. Holding on a slope needs the brake's holding torque, and the domain says so. |
| Arm | Posture hold by energy shaping, with damping | Passive with respect to the shaped energy, for a fixed base |
| Legged | Widen the stance, lower the body, freeze | `Tested` only; no passivity claim once contacts switch |

The sampled-data caveat is not a footnote. In continuous time a damper never adds energy. A damper computed
at a tick and held for a period can: 300 N·s/m held for 10 ms on a free 1 kg slider turns +1 m/s into −2 m/s
and adds 1.5 J. A backup that injects energy is worse than none, so the gain bound is checked in exact
rationals for every fitted backup.

Proof obligation PO-SHIELD-04: under damping injection the port model's stored energy is non-increasing: in
continuous time for every K_d ⪰ 0, and at the ticks of a sampled, held command when 2·M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0 (with
actuation latency, when the exact one-tick map of the sampled-loop analysis A-22 contracts in the energy
norm). Verification: a polynomial identity for the continuous statement (a theorem card); an exact rational
check of the gain bound per fitted backup; the energy ledger's monotonicity on replay.

## Scope

In: the `BackupController` interface; the three backups; their domains as data; the gain-bound check; the
brake stage; the `Checked<Command>` they return. Out: when to invoke a backup (N22b-13, N22b-23, N22b-25);
the complete sampled-damping known-answer table, the rule for the work of a held command and GATE-SHIELD-07
(N22c-44 for the gain bound in `shield.fit`, N22c-45 for the work of a held command); fitting and verbs (BD-338).

## Technical approach

**Interface (DESIGN, `crates/frob-control/src/backup/mod.rs`).**

```rust
pub trait BackupController {
    fn kind(&self) -> BackupKind;                         // DampingInjection, PostureHold, WidenLowerFreeze
    fn domain(&self) -> &BackupDomain;                    // where the property below is claimed
    fn property(&self) -> BackupProperty;                 // PassiveSampled { bound }, PassiveShaped, TestedOnly
    fn command(&mut self, est: &StateEstimate) -> Checked<Command>;   // inside the actuator box by construction
}
pub struct BackupDomain { morphology: ClassSet, slope_max: Qty, needs_brake_on_slope: bool,
                          base: BaseKind /* Fixed | Floating */, notes: Vec<DomainNote> }
```

A backup's output is clipped to the actuator box and is a `Checked<Command>` with source `Backup(kind)`; it
is the only constructor of a checked command besides the filter's optimality check.

**One implementation of each piece (shared with the neighbouring beads).** The three command laws are the
functions of `crates/frob-control/src/backup/laws.rs` delivered at M3 by N22bx-02 for the task audit's idle
arm; this bead wraps them behind `BackupController` and does not reimplement them, so an idle run at M3 and a
shield hand-over at M4 execute the same law. Default parameters are read from that bead's record
`registries/controllers/backup.toml` (damping κ = K_d·h/M̄_min = ½; posture hold K_d,j = ½·m_j/h and
K_p,j = (2π·2 Hz)²·m_j; widening factor 1.2; height fraction 0.7), and the domains below restate them rather
than redefining them. The rational check `sampled_gain_bound(M̄_min, h, K_d) → { no_reversal: bool,
passive: bool, pivots }` is defined here and is the function `shield.fit` calls in N22c-44, which adds the
refusal `sampled-damping-above-passivity-bound`, the `--allow-reversal` admission and the A-22 contraction
certificate for a loop with latency or other storage. Until N22c-44 lands, a damping backup whose command
path has actuation latency or other storage reports the property `TestedOnly`, never `PassiveSampled`.

**1. Damping injection (wheeled, tracked).**

- Outputs y: the drive output rates collocated with the drive efforts (wheel or sprocket speeds).
- Command u = −K_d·y with K_d symmetric positive semidefinite (diagonal by default), saturated at the box.
- Continuous-time property: in the port model with stored energy H, dH/dt = yᵀ·u + (other ports) and
  u = −K_d·y gives dH/dt ≤ −yᵀ·K_d·y ≤ 0 when no other port injects.
- Sampled property: for reflected inertia M̄ at the outputs and a command held for the control period h with
  no other force, one tick maps y to (I − h·M̄⁻¹·K_d)·y and the stored energy changes by
  −(h/2)·yᵀ·(2·K_d − h·K_d·M̄⁻¹·K_d)·y. So energy does not increase at the ticks exactly when
  2·M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0, and the rate does not reverse in any direction exactly when M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0.
- Fitting rule: the gain is fitted inside the no-reversal bound by default, K_d ⪯ M̄_min/h, with M̄_min the
  least reflected inertia over the design's mass ranges, and the bound is checked in exact rationals
  (an LDLᵀ with non-negative pivots of M̄_min − h·K_d on rationalized inputs). With one tick of actuation
  latency the scalar loop v′ = v − κ·v_prev, κ = K_d·h/M̄, is stable only for κ < 1, half the bound without
  latency; with latency or other stored energy in the loop the exact one-tick map of A-22 is used and a
  contraction in the energy norm is required.
- Brakes: where the actuator card has a brake, the backup engages it after the output rate falls below a
  declared threshold (or at once if the card rates the brake for dynamic stops). A brake or a shorted
  winding is a physical damper and carries no sampled bound; the backup's card says which it is. Holding on
  a slope needs the brake's holding torque: the domain records `needs_brake_on_slope`, and a design without
  a brake has slope_max = 0 in its domain.

**2. Posture hold by energy shaping, with damping (arm).** u = g(q) − K_p·(q − q_hold) − K_d·q̇, with g the
gravity torque from the model, q_hold the configuration at hand-over (or a declared safe posture), K_p and
K_d positive definite, saturated at the box. Shaped energy H_s = ½·q̇ᵀ·M(q)·q̇ + ½·(q − q_hold)ᵀ·K_p·(q − q_hold);
dH_s/dt = −q̇ᵀ·K_d·q̇ ≤ 0: passive with respect to H_s, for a fixed base. The sampled gain bound applies to
K_d with the joint-space inertia in place of M̄. Domain: fixed base only; on a moving base the property is
not claimed and the kind reports `TestedOnly`.

**3. Widen the stance, lower the body, freeze (legged).** A scripted sequence on the legged baseline: step
the feet outward to a wider stance where a step is available, lower the body height to a declared fraction,
then hold joint positions with damping. Property: `Tested` only; no passivity claim once contacts switch.
Its domain lists the terrain and speed range on which it was tested.

**Ledger and layer.** While a backup runs, every tick's layer record is `backup` and the energy ledger
reports the port model's stored energy at ticks, so monotonicity can be replayed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three backups implement the interface; each has a non-empty declared domain and a property.
- [ ] Damping injection: for gains inside the sampled bound the ledger's stored energy is non-increasing at
      every tick on the referee; a negative damping gain breaks monotonicity and is caught; a gain above the
      bound adds energy and is caught (300 N·s/m held 10 ms on a free 1 kg slider: +1.5 J).
- [ ] The gain bound is checked in exact rationals at fit, and a fitted default gain satisfies M̄_min − h·K_d ⪰ 0.
- [ ] A wheeled design with no brake has slope_max = 0 in its domain; invoking the backup on a slope outside
      the domain is reported as `outside-backup-domain`.
- [ ] Posture hold: H_s is non-increasing on a fixed-base arm on the referee within the ledger's residual
      bound; on a floating base the property reads `TestedOnly`.
- [ ] The legged backup's result is class `Tested` and its domain names what was tested.
- [ ] Every backup command is inside the actuator box and is a `Checked<Command>` with source `Backup`.
- [ ] The wrapped laws are N22bx-02's functions: for the same state and parameter record, the M3 controller
      `backup:damping` and this bead's `DampingInjection::command` give bit-identical commands (likewise for
      posture hold and widen-lower-freeze); a damping backup with one tick of actuation latency reports
      `TestedOnly` until the A-22 certificate of N22c-44 is present.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-control/tests/backup.rs`.

- `slider_overshoot` (the counterexample): m = 1 kg, v = +1 m/s, K_d = 300 N·s/m, h = 0.01 s: force −300 N
  for 10 ms gives v = −2 m/s; kinetic energy 0.5 J → 2 J; +1.5 J. The formula
  −(h/2)·(2·K_d − h·K_d²/m)·v² = −0.005·(600 − 900) = +1.5 J agrees.
- `slider_at_bounds`: K_d = 200 (κ = 2): v → −1 m/s, energy unchanged; K_d = 100 (κ = 1): stops in one tick,
  −0.5 J; K_d = 50: v → 0.5 m/s, energy 0.125 J.
- `gain_bound_exact`: M̄ = diag(1, 4) kg, h = 1/100 s: K_d = diag(100, 400) passes the no-reversal bound
  exactly (zero pivots allowed); diag(101, 400) fails it and passes the energy bound; diag(201, 400) fails
  both.
- `latency_halves_bound`: κ = 3/2 with one tick of latency: the map (v, v_prev) → (v − κ·v_prev, v) has
  spectral radius √(3/2) > 1; κ = 0.9 is stable.
- `negative_gain_caught`: K_d = −10 increases energy at every tick; the monotonicity test fails as required.
- `posture_hold_energy`: two-link arm, K_p = diag(50, 50), K_d = diag(5, 5): H_s decreases monotonically
  from a release at 0.2 rad offset.
- `brake_domain`: slope 5% with and without a brake card.
- `box_respected` (property): random states, all three backups.
- `same_law_as_m3`: 10³ random states per law; the wrapped backup's command equals the M3 plain controller's
  command bit for bit; a mutant that recomputes the damping gain from a hard-coded κ = 1 is caught.
- `latency_reports_tested_only`: a damping backup declared with one tick of actuation latency and no A-22
  certificate has property `TestedOnly`.

Exercised by `scripts/e2e/shield_runtime.sh` (N22b-32): a wheeled run in which the energy monitor cuts; the
script reads `frob telemetry energy <run> --json` and asserts the stored energy at ticks is non-increasing

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-572 (damping-injection backup: stored energy non-increasing in the ledger at every tick for gains inside
the sampled bound; tolerance: the ledger's residual bound; referee). Negative controls: a negative damping
gain; a gain above the bound (the slider, +1.5 J). The slider rows at κ = 3, 2, 1 and the delayed κ = 3/2
case are the sampled-damping subsection's table, whose gate GATE-SHIELD-07 is measured by N22c-46.
Determinism: the exact gain check is class D0; replays are class D2.

## Considerations

- Passivity establishes neither collision avoidance nor fall avoidance; a backup that only removes energy
  can still leave a robot somewhere it should not be. The domain and the shield's violation statistics carry
  that risk, not this property.
- The damping backup assumes collocated outputs. A transmission with significant compliance between motor
  and wheel breaks collocation; the series spring's stored energy then enters the loop and the A-22 map is
  required.
- "It worked" is not a result if the backup did the work: every backup tick is an event, and the fraction of
  ticks spent in the backup is reported with every result.
- A legged backup with a proper domain is an open research problem; the plan claims only `Tested`.
