## Background

A symmetric robot invites symmetric control: tie the left and right gains, design in half the dimension,
block-diagonalize the linearization. Whether that is correct depends on the closed loop, not on the body.
Two finite groups are computed for every pair of a design and a task and stored with it:

- Γ_mech: automorphisms of the design that preserve link lengths, pseudo-inertias, joint screws with their
  reflection parity, actuators and gear ratios.
- Γ_obs ≤ Γ_mech: the subgroup that also preserves every sensor pose and field of view, the payload, the
  task's goal, and the symmetry of terrain and guards.

A quadruped built symmetric fore-aft and left-right has Γ_mech = C₂ × C₂; with a front-only lidar its closed
loop has Γ_obs = C₂.

Even Γ_obs is too large for a particular calculation at a particular moment. On a side slope gravity is not
mirror-symmetric in the body frame; in a trot only the diagonal pair of feet is down; a forward velocity
command is not fixed by the fore-aft mirror. **The group a calculation may use** is the intersection of
Γ_obs with the stabilizers of what that calculation holds fixed, computed when it is used:

    Γ_use = Γ_obs ∩ Stab(gravity in the body frame) ∩ Stab(boundary conditions)
                  ∩ Stab(current contact mode) ∩ Stab(commanded twist)

On a side slope the left-right mirror leaves Stab(gravity) and the controller's left and right gains untie.
With an off-centre payload the mirror is not in Γ_obs at all. A reduction requested under a larger group is
refused (FROB-REF-461, invariant INV-REFLEX-04), and the full model always stays executable.

Decision ADR-REFLEX-07: a symmetric controller is tied only under the intersection of the design's group
with the stabilizers of payload, gravity, boundary conditions, task, observations and current contact mode.
Reason: a group that the closed loop does not have constrains the controller to something false. Rejected:
tying under Γ_mech (counterexample: a policy tied under C₂ × C₂ with a front-only lidar must walk backwards
as the mirror image of walking forwards while seeing only forwards); a group fixed once per design (it is
wrong the moment the slope, the stance or the command changes).

## Scope

In: the four stabilizer computations; the intersection; the report of which elements each stabilizer
removed; recomputation on a change of context; the containment check and FROB-REF-461; INV-REFLEX-04. Out:
computing Γ_mech and Γ_obs (static symmetry analysis and the sensing subsystem, both prerequisites); what a
controller does with Γ_use (N22b-38); parity and equivariance in law (N22b-39); the event identity
(N22b-40); the verb (BD-327).

## Technical approach

**Inputs.** The stored (Γ_mech, Γ_obs) of the robot-plus-task pair, each element with its isometry P_g (an
exact orthogonal matrix, det ±1), its body permutation and its signed permutations on joints, actuators,
contacts and observation terms; and a *context*: gravity direction in the body frame, the world patch in
play (terrain, material field, guards, boundary conditions), the current contact mode, the commanded twist.

**The four stabilizers (each a subgroup of Γ_obs; `crates/frob-control/src/symmetry/use_group.rs`).**

1. *Gravity.* g ∈ Stab(gravity) iff P_g·ĝ = ĝ for the gravity direction ĝ in the body frame (gravity is a
   polar vector). With ĝ given exactly the test is exact; with a measured attitude it uses a declared
   tolerance and the result is classed `Tested` with the tolerance recorded. DESIGN default: the angle
   between P_g·ĝ and ĝ at most θ_g = 0.1° (1.745 × 10⁻³ rad), overridable per call and recorded in the
   receipt; for m_lr this admits a side tilt of at most 0.05°, so the 10° slope of the tests is far outside
   it. A group admitted inside the tolerance (not exactly) is used on the *symmetrized* context: ĝ is
   replaced by its projection onto the fixed subspace of the admitted group, renormalized, and the
   replacement and its angle are reported, so that the reduced and the full calculation of N22b-38 are made
   on the same, invariant problem; the effect of the replacement on the commands is measured by running the
   full model on the unsymmetrized context and is reported as `Tested`.
2. *Boundary conditions.* g ∈ Stab(boundary conditions) iff the world patch is invariant under P_g about
   the body frame: the scene's declared symmetries are used where present; otherwise the height field,
   material (friction) field and guard set over the patch are transformed and compared exactly (grids) or
   within tolerance.
3. *Contact mode.* The mode is a set of (contact role, state) pairs. g acts by its contact permutation σ_g.
   g ∈ Stab(mode) iff σ_g maps the set to itself.
4. *Commanded twist.* For the commanded body twist (v, ω): g ∈ Stab(twist) iff P_g·v = v (polar) and
   det(P_g)·P_g·ω = ω (axial).

**Two context forms (DESIGN).** The context is an argument with no default, in one of two forms. An
*instant* context (gravity, patch, current contact mode, twist) is the one above, for calculations at one
mode (gain tying, block diagonalization). A *stride* context replaces the current contact mode by a stride's
contact sequence (the mode at each of its N ticks) and the requested pair (H, K, m) with its generator σ;
it is the context of a periodic-orbit reduction (N22a-31), whose spatio-temporal generator maps the stance
set at one instant onto the stance set N/m ticks later, so that Stab(current contact mode) would refuse every
trot. For a stride context stabilizers 1, 2 and 4 are computed as above, and stabilizer 3 is replaced by the
*contact-sequence condition*: every element of K maps every mode of the sequence to itself, and σ maps the
mode at tick t onto the mode at tick t + N/m (indices mod N). The `UseGroup` token records the form it was
built for; a token built for a stride context is accepted only by the periodic-orbit reducer, never by a
reduction at one mode, which needs the instant form.

**Intersection and report.** Γ_use is the set intersection, computed in the fixed order above. For each
stabilizer the report lists the elements of the running intersection that it removed, as elements and as a
generating set of what remains ("the generators each stabilizer removed"), with the reason (the vector
component, the terrain cell, the contact role or the twist component that is not fixed).

**Recomputation.** Γ_use is not stored as a constant of the design. It is recomputed when the context
changes: at every contact-mode switch, at a change of commanded twist, and when the gravity direction or
the world patch leaves the tolerance of the last computation. Results are memoized by context digest.

**Containment check and refusal.** Any calculation that requests a reduction names the group H it wants.
H is first passed through the sensing subsystem's Γ_obs consumer guard (N20b-02): an element of H outside
Γ_obs, or a stale Γ_obs, is refused there with FROB-REF-443 (exit 3), so that the coarser error is named
first and the two codes never both fire for one request. If H ⊆ Γ_obs but H ⊄ Γ_use the request is refused:
FROB-REF-461, *a symmetric reduction was requested under a group not
contained in Γ_use*; exit code 3; `pointer` names the first element of H outside Γ_use and the stabilizer
(for a stride context, possibly the contact-sequence condition) that removed it. Ranked repairs: (1) use Γ_use; (2) remove the asymmetry with the typed edit that the static
symmetry analysis proposes (for example move the sensor to the plane of symmetry, centre the payload).
Invariant INV-REFLEX-04: a symmetric reduction uses only the stabilizer intersection stored for the
robot-plus-task pair; the reducer's API takes a `UseGroup` token that only this module can construct, so a
reduction under any other group does not compile. The full model is always available: every reduced
calculation has an unreduced twin that is executed on request and in tests.

**Identity.** The group actually used is part of the `ControllerId` (the content hash of everything that
affects a controller's output).

**Determinism and class.** Exact inputs give class D0 and a `Certified` group statement when the
certificate is emitted; toleranced inputs give `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The module computes the four stabilizers and Γ_use and reports, per stabilizer, the removed elements
      with reasons.
- [ ] The reference quadruped table below is reproduced exactly.
- [ ] A request to reduce under Γ_mech when Γ_obs = Γ_mech and Γ_use is smaller is refused with
      FROB-REF-461, naming the offending element and stabilizer and both repairs; with a front-only lidar the
      same request is refused by the Γ_obs guard with FROB-REF-443 and never reaches the 461 check.
- [ ] Γ_use is recomputed at a contact-mode switch (four-foot stance to trot stance changes the result).
- [ ] A reduction cannot be requested without a `UseGroup` token (compile-fail test).
- [ ] A stride context replaces Stab(current contact mode) by the contact-sequence condition: the reference
      trot under (H, K, m) = ({e, m_lr}, {e}, 2) with the forward command is admitted although its mirror
      exchanges the diagonal stance pairs; ({e, m_lr}, {e, m_lr}) on the same trot is refused with
      FROB-REF-461 naming the contact-sequence condition; a stride-context token is refused by a reduction at
      one mode.
- [ ] Changing Γ_use changes the `ControllerId`.

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-control/tests/use_group.rs`. Reference: a quadruped symmetric fore-aft and left-right, body
frame x forward, y left, z up; Γ_mech = {e, m_lr, m_fa, r} with P(m_lr) = diag(1, −1, 1) (left-right
mirror), P(m_fa) = diag(−1, 1, 1) (fore-aft mirror), P(r) = diag(−1, −1, 1) (half-turn about z). Feet FL,
FR, RL, RR: m_lr swaps FL↔FR and RL↔RR; m_fa swaps FL↔RL and FR↔RR; r swaps FL↔RR and FR↔RL.

| `side_slope` | ĝ = (0, −sin 10°, −cos 10°), symmetric sensors, standing | Stab(gravity) = {e, m_fa}; removed: m_lr and r (the y-component of gravity) |

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-575 (planted asymmetries: a side slope removes the left-right mirror; a front lidar leaves C₂; exactly
the expected generators are removed), KA-070 (Γ_mech = C₂ × C₂ and Γ_obs = C₂ for the front-only lidar),
KA-071 (a one-sided payload removes the reflection from Γ_obs, and the reduced calculation is replaced by

command; `yaw_command` catches it); a Γ_use cached across a mode switch (`recompute_on_mode_switch` catches
it). Determinism: class D0 on exact contexts.

## Considerations

- The stabilizers are computed for the context a calculation declares. A calculation that holds more fixed
  (for example a specific foothold plan) must declare it, or its reduction is unsound; the API requires the
  context as an argument and has no default.
- Tolerances: a slope of 0.01° should not untie gains in practice. The tolerance for "gravity fixed" is a
  declared number (default 0.1° between P_g·ĝ and ĝ, above); inside it the result is `Tested`, the
  calculation runs on the symmetrized context, and the reduced-against-full comparison (N22b-38) is the
  safety net (test `gravity_tolerance`).
- Related refusal in the sensing subsystem: a symmetry consumer naming an element outside Γ_obs is refused
  there (FROB-REF-443). FROB-REF-461 is the finer check against Γ_use.
- The expected sets in the table are derived here from the parity rule (polar by P, axial by det(P)·P); the
  plan fixes the rule and two of the rows.
