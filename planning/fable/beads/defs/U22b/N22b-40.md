## Background

A symmetry can save work across an impact (computing a landing's sensitivity for one foot and reusing it for
its mirror image) only if the symmetry commutes with the event. Away from events this is the ordinary
equivariance of the dynamics. At an event the object that carries small perturbations across is the
**saltation matrix**: for a guard function γ(x) = 0, a reset map R, and the vector fields f⁻ before and f⁺
after the event,

    Ξ = D R + (f⁺ − D R·f⁻)·D γ / (D γ·f⁻),

evaluated at the pre-event state x⁻. It differs from the reset Jacobian D R by a term that accounts for the
event time moving with the perturbation. The plan's rule:

*Symmetry at an event.* For a group element g and a transverse event e with saltation Ξ_e the identity is

    D g(x⁺)·Ξ_e(x⁻) = Ξ_(g·e)(g·x⁻)·D g(x⁻).

Test: reflect a four-foot landing and exchange the left and right sole laws; a one-sided perturbation
transformed before or after the impact must predict the same change. The identity transports a local
derivative to the corresponding event. It does not license block reduction of a trajectory that the
symmetry does not fix.

Three things can go wrong, and the test exists for them: the image event g·e is taken with the original
contact law instead of the exchanged one (two feet with different soles are not interchangeable); the
representation has a parity error; or the event is near grazing, where D γ·f⁻ is close to zero, the
saltation matrix is ill-defined, and the identity must be flagged, not asserted.

The corresponding theorem card (T-18) states the identity as ρ(h)·Ξ_e = Ξ_{h·e}·ρ(h) for each generator h of
the observation-preserving group and each event class of a model, by a polynomial identity per pair or
exact-rational randomized identity testing. For the linear (signed-permutation) actions used here
D g = ρ(g), which gives that form.

## Scope

In: the identity checker for a model's event classes and group generators; the image-event construction
(permuted contact role, exchanged contact law); the tolerance and the grazing flag; a host known answer; the
four-foot landing test; use by the verb. Out: computing saltation matrices and step Jacobians (the gait
beads); using the identity to amortize computations across a design family (the Augur epic); block
reduction of trajectories (not licensed).

## Technical approach

**Objects.** For a model: its event classes (touchdown or liftoff of each contact role under each contact
law; joint-stop events), each with guard γ_e, reset R_e and the flows on either side; the group in force
with ρ(g) on the state; the contact permutation σ_g and the map on contact laws (the sole material and
interface card travel with the foot under g).

**Image event.** g·e is the event of the contact role σ_g(role of e) **with the contact law of e's role**:
reflecting a landing of the left foot gives a landing of the right foot in a world where the right foot
carries the left foot's sole. In a design whose left and right soles are identical the exchange changes
nothing; in one where they differ, g is not a symmetry of the design unless the laws are exchanged, and the
test makes the exchange explicit.

**Check for one (g, e, x⁻) (`crates/frob-control/src/symmetry/event.rs`).**

1. Transversality: τ = |D γ_e(x⁻)·f⁻(x⁻)| / (‖D γ_e‖·‖f⁻‖). If τ is below the grazing threshold of the
   derivative machinery, return `Flagged { reason: grazing, tau }`. No identity is asserted.
2. Compute Ξ_e(x⁻) and Ξ_(g·e)(ρ(g)·x⁻) (referee: event-located saltation; compliant tiers: the Jacobian of
   the step map across the contact transition, which plays the same role because the map is continuous
   there).
3. Residual: E = ρ(g)·Ξ_e(x⁻) − Ξ_(g·e)(ρ(g)·x⁻)·ρ(g), measured relative to ‖Ξ_e‖ in the mass-weighted
   norm.
4. Pass iff the residual is within the declared tolerance (DESIGN: 10⁻⁸ relative for analytic saltation in
   double precision; the finite-difference route's Richardson error estimate for differenced Jacobians).
5. Report per pair: pass, fail (with the largest entry of E and its coordinates), or flagged.

**Perturbation form of the test (the plan's wording).** Take a one-sided perturbation δ (for example a
small change of the left foot's pre-impact speed). Route A: transform, then cross the event:
Ξ_(g·e)(ρ(g)·x⁻)·ρ(g)·δ. Route B: cross the event, then transform: ρ(g)·Ξ_e(x⁻)·δ. The two predicted
changes must agree within tolerance. A finite-perturbation version runs both routes through the simulator
with a small δ and compares the realized post-event states, which checks the saltation computation itself as
well as the identity.

**What the checker refuses to conclude.** It returns per-event transport results. It offers no "reduce this
trajectory" operation: a trajectory that the symmetry does not fix (a one-sided disturbance, an asymmetric
gait) is not reducible because its events are individually transportable.

**Determinism and class.** Analytic saltation on the referee: class D2, known-answer claim. The exact
polynomial identity per event class, when the theorem card's certificate is emitted, is `Certified` for the
model.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The checker runs over every (generator, event class) pair of a model and reports pass, fail or
      flagged with the residual.
- [ ] Host known answer (two bouncing feet, below): with the sole laws exchanged the identity holds exactly;
      without the exchange it fails with the stated entries.
- [ ] Four-foot landing on the reference quadruped, reflected by the left-right mirror with left and right
      sole laws exchanged: the identity holds within tolerance on regular landings, by both the matrix form
      and the perturbation form.
- [ ] A grazing landing (foot arriving nearly tangent to the ground, transversality below threshold) is
      flagged and not asserted.
- [ ] A planted parity error in the representation (a roll-axis sign not flipped) makes the check fail.
- [ ] The API has no trajectory-level reduction built on the identity.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-control/tests/symmetry_event.rs`.

- `saltation_bouncing_ball` (hand-checkable): state (y, v), guard γ = y, reset (y, v) ↦ (y, −r·v), flow
  f = (v, −g) with g = 9.81 m/s². At restitution r = 0.8 and impact speed v⁻ = −2 m/s: f⁻ = (−2, −9.81),
  f⁺ = (1.6, −9.81), D R = diag(1, −0.8), D γ·f⁻ = −2, f⁺ − D R·f⁻ = (3.6, −17.658), so
  Ξ = [[−0.8, 0], [8.829, −0.8]] with determinant 0.64. The bare reset Jacobian diag(1, −0.8) has the wrong
  sign in its first entry and no coupling term.
- `two_feet_exchange` (hand-checkable): two independent bouncing feet L and R with state
  (y_L, v_L, y_R, v_R); the mirror g swaps the two pairs (ρ(g) is the block swap). Sole laws r_L = 0.8,
  r_R = 0.5. Event e: L lands at v⁻ = −2 m/s. Ξ_e = blockdiag(Ξ(0.8), I₂). The image event g·e is R landing
  with L's sole law (0.8): Ξ_(g·e) = blockdiag(I₂, Ξ(0.8)), and ρ(g)·Ξ_e = Ξ_(g·e)·ρ(g) exactly. Without
  exchanging the laws the image uses r_R = 0.5, whose block is [[−0.5, 0], [7.3575, −0.5]]; the residual's
  entries are 0.3 on the diagonal and 8.829 − 7.3575 = 1.4715 in the coupling term, and the check fails.
- `perturbation_routes_agree`: δ = (0, 0.01, 0, 0) (left speed): route A and route B give the same vector to
  round-off in the exchanged case.
- `grazing_flagged`: an event with D γ·f⁻ = 10⁻⁹ relative returns `Flagged`.
- `parity_error_caught`: on the quadruped landing, a representation with one roll-axis sign wrong fails.
- `four_foot_landing`: the reference quadruped dropped flat from 5 cm onto level ground (regular,
  transverse touchdown of all four feet); for g = left-right mirror and each foot's touchdown class, the
  residual is within tolerance; finite-perturbation version with δ on the left-front foot agrees within the
  differencing error.
- `no_trajectory_reduction`: a trybuild test that no public function takes a trajectory and returns a
  reduced one from this module.

### End-to-end script and logging

Exercised by `scripts/e2e/control_symmetry.sh` (N22b-41): `frob control symmetry --design <quad> --events
landing --json` on the regular four-foot landing and on a grazing landing; the script logs, per generator

### Known answers and negative controls

The bouncing-ball saltation matrix [[−0.8, 0], [8.83 s⁻¹, −0.8]] with determinant r² = 0.64 is the plan's
worked card for saltation (GB-27); the two-feet exchange case is derived here from it. Negative controls:
the image event without exchanged sole laws; the planted parity error; the bare reset Jacobian used in
place of the saltation matrix (fails the finite-perturbation comparison). The gate row this bead serves:
the event identity holds within tolerance on regular landings and is flagged on grazing ones. Determinism:
class D2 on the referee; exact on the host cases.

## Considerations

- Simultaneous touchdowns: a flat four-foot landing is a simultaneous group. On the referee it is one
  coupled impact solve; the identity is then checked for the coupled event and, where the analysis reports
  order sensitivity, per ordering. A landing reported `order-sensitive` is checked per selection and the
  result says so.
- The referee's saltation route depends on upstream hybrid-sensitivity support; until it lands the
  compliant-tier Jacobian route carries the test and the referee row is recorded `not run` with the reason.
- Tolerances for differenced Jacobians come from the Richardson check of the derivative machinery; an
  identity "passing" at a tolerance wider than that estimate is reported as inconclusive.
- This is a test and a transport rule, tier Solid. Amortizing it across a design family is a separate
  Frontier/Moonshot item with its own gate and kill gate.
