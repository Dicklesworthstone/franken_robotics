## Background

The energy-budget safety tier (Frontier; crate `frob-shield`; construction C-31) replaces a hard question
with an easy one. Certifying a contact sequence is hard; bounding the energy that can reach a boundary is
not, because passive contact with any friction coefficient and any restitution can only dissipate or
redistribute mechanical energy. For a declared boundary (a stop, a bumper, a door or drawer at the end of
its travel, a lift platform, a compliant cover that may touch a person) the budget is

    E_deliv = T_ub + E_drive + E_ext + E_stored + E_reserve ≤ W_abs

where the left side bounds kinetic energy, the energy the drives can still inject before the cutoff acts,
work by sources that are not cut, stored energy and a reserve, and **W_abs is the lower envelope of the
work the absorber takes before its limit (bottoming, or a stated force limit)**, read from "an interface
card with provenance". The budget document names the card: `"absorber": { "card": "bumper-50N-40mm",
"w_abs_j": "2.00", "provenance": "assumed" }`.

No card can hold that. The interface card of the contact subsystem (BD-167) is keyed by a material pair
and a condition and carries friction, stiffness, rolling and film data; it has no force–displacement
curve, no stroke and no force limit, and the contact chapter specifies no absorber. The budget-term
builder (N22b-11) integrates "the interface card's force–displacement lower envelope F_lo(x)", and the
owner decision OPEN-SHIELD-1 (N22b-17: where absorber envelopes come from before any bench data exist)
decides the provenance of a card whose schema it explicitly leaves out. This bead defines the card.

DESIGN decision: a new card kind `absorber` in the `.fcard` container (the container gives per-field
provenance, uncertainty, immutability and a content address), not new fields on the interface card.
Reasons: an absorber is a part (a bumper, a crush element, a spring stop, a compliant cover), not a pair of
materials; one part meets many materials; and the interface card feeds the contact solver, which must not
change when a safety card is edited. Rejected alternatives: extending the interface card (above); writing
W_abs directly in the `.fenergy` document as the sketch does (then the number has no source, no conditions
and no path for a measurement to replace it: the document keeps `w_abs_j` only as a copy checked against
the card); a scene fixture parameter (a fixture may *reference* an absorber card, but the envelope with its
provenance belongs in the card system that the calibration lane already knows how to update).

## Scope

In: the card kind, its fields and validation; the exact evaluation of W_abs with outward rounding; the
conditions block and its match against a budget's declared conditions; the generic catalog entries;
`shield.budget`'s read of the card and the consistency check of `w_abs_j`; listing through the card verbs; the
kind `absorber` appended to the `FMT-FCARD` row's closed kind list in `registries/formats.toml`, with its
generator for the format harness of BD-141 (which BD-141 leaves to the bead that defines a later kind).

Out: the budget document and its integer scales (N22b-10); the other five terms and the class rule
(N22b-11); the premises, including "the absorber is on every forward path" (N22b-12); which provenance a
source may claim (N22b-17); the bench measurement and its import as a new card version (the calibration
lane; N30a-27 for the posterior-as-card mechanism); peak-force or injury statements beyond the stated force
limit (not supported by the tier at all).

## Technical approach

**1. The card (DESIGN; `crates/frob-shield/src/absorber_card.rs`; container and loader from BD-069).**

```rust
pub struct AbsorberCard {
    pub part: PartRef,                    // name, maker, part number; free text is data, never a path
    pub kind: AbsorberKind,               // ConstantForce | Spring | Crush | Viscous | Tabulated
    pub envelope: LowerEnvelope,          // F_lo(x): piecewise linear, exact decimal knots (x [m], F [N])
    pub stroke: Field<Length>,            // x_bottom: displacement at bottoming
    pub force_limit: Option<Field<Force>>,// if present, W_abs is taken only up to the first x with F_lo(x) = limit
    pub upper_envelope: Option<UpperEnvelope>, // F_hi(x), needed only for a peak-force statement at the absorber
    pub conditions: Conditions,           // temperature range, approach-speed range, prior cycles ≤ n, orientation
    pub reusable: bool,                   // false for a crush element: one use, then the card no longer applies
    pub rate_dependence: RateRule,        // None | LowerBoundAtZeroSpeed | Table
}
```

Every `Field<T>` carries provenance (`assumed`, `datasheet` with a citation, `measured`, `derived`) and an
uncertainty. Validation at load, each a refusal of the card with the container's form refusal FROB-REF-103
(BD-069's fail-closed form code; DESIGN, no new code), exit 3, `pointer` at the offending field:
knots strictly increasing in x starting at x = 0; F_lo ≥ 0 everywhere; the last knot at x = `stroke`; if
`upper_envelope` is present, F_hi ≥ F_lo at every knot; `force_limit` > 0; a `Viscous` kind must declare
`rate_dependence` and may not claim work at zero approach speed.

In the same change the kind `absorber` is appended to the closed kind list of the `FMT-FCARD` row in
`registries/formats.toml` (the nine kinds of M1 plus this one), and a generator of valid absorber cards (every
`AbsorberKind`, random decimal knots satisfying the rules above) is registered for BD-141's format harness, so
that `fmt_fcard_roundtrip` runs over the kind and `fmt_fcard_every_kind_generated` passes.

**2. W_abs, exact.** x_lim = `stroke`, or the smallest x at which F_lo(x) = `force_limit` if a limit is
stated and reached. W_abs = ∫₀^x_lim F_lo dx, the sum of trapezoids over the knots, computed in exact
rational arithmetic from the decimal strings and then **rounded down** to the budget document's energy step
(the left side of the inequality is rounded up there, so both roundings are against admission). Field
uncertainties enter before integration: each knot's force is reduced by its stated uncertainty and the
stroke by its uncertainty. Closed forms the integral must reproduce: constant force F over stroke s gives
F·s; a linear spring of minimum stiffness k_min gives ½·k_min·x_lim²; a crush element with plateau lower
bound F_p from x₀ to s gives at least F_p·(s − x₀).

**3. Conditions.** A budget states its conditions (temperature, approach-speed bound from T_ub's speed
bound, cycle count of the part, mounting orientation). `shield.budget` checks them against the card: a
condition outside the card's range does not scale the envelope; it makes the absorber term **unavailable**,
and the budget is refused under the code the chapter gives for an energy budget whose inputs do not
support the requested claim, FROB-REF-451 ("an energy budget omits a source in the port graph, or a class
above `Estimated` is requested on an `assumed` absorber envelope"), with `pointer` at the failing condition
and the repair "use a card whose conditions cover the case, or measure". DESIGN: no new public code; an
out-of-conditions envelope is treated as weaker than `assumed`. A non-reusable card with prior cycles ≥ 1
is refused the same way.

**4. Class.** The provenance of W_abs is the weakest provenance among the fields the integral used (knots
up to x_lim, the stroke, the limit). The budget builder maps it to a class by the rule of the decision
bead; until that decision is recorded the default holds: every absorber card in the catalog is `assumed`,
every budget on it is `Estimated`, reality is `no-claim`. A card's provenance changes only with an attached
source record of the matching kind.

**5. The `.fenergy` link.** The document's `absorber` block keeps `card` (name and content hash),
`w_abs_j` and `provenance`. `shield.budget` recomputes W_abs from the card when it writes the copy, and every
read of a document against its card (`shield.check <run> --energy-budget <file.fenergy>`) recomputes it and
refuses a document whose `w_abs_j` exceeds the recomputed value (a stale or optimistic copy) with
FROB-REF-451 (the code of section 3, for the same reason: a W_abs above the card's has no source), exit 3,
`pointer` `absorber.w_abs_j`, observed the copy and required the recomputed value, repair "rebuild the budget
with `shield.budget`"; a lower copy is accepted and the lower value is used. The offline auditor recomputes the
same integral from the card bytes inside the proof bundle: the bundle writer embeds the card (payload member
`absorber_card`) and the auditor's absorber step re-implements this integral on its own arithmetic, rule
`energy.absorber-mismatch` (both in N22b-14, which depends on this bead; the member and the rule are listed in
the checker bead N12-17's payload and vocabulary). This bead's evaluator and the auditor's share no code.

**6. Catalog (all `assumed`, labelled as generic).** `bumper-50N-40mm` (constant force 50 N, stroke 40 mm,
W_abs = 2.00 J), `bumper-150N-30mm` (150 N, 30 mm, 4.50 J; the card of the worked artifact), `spring-stop-2000Npm-40mm`
(k_min = 2,000 N/m, 1.60 J; no `/` in a card name, which is also its file name `<name>.fcard`), `cover-foam-ramp` (tabulated, force limit 40 N). They exist so that fixtures
and examples run; none supports a statement about a physical robot.

**7. Surface.** No new verb: the card verbs of the container list and validate absorber cards (DESIGN):
`actuator.list --kind absorber` lists them (the VERBS-actuator row's `--kind` lists `absorber` beside
`actuator`, `battery` and `transmission` from M4, with this bead in its "extended by"), and `actuator.show <card>` and `actuator.check <card>`
take an absorber card by name like any card, the kind being in its header (no selector is needed). On an
absorber card `actuator.check` runs, as its VERBS-actuator row states, the container's form rules (FROB-REF-102
for a unit error; FROB-REF-103 for a field without provenance or uncertainty or an unknown field), the field
rules of section 1 (FROB-REF-103 at the field), and under `--strict-data` FROB-REF-113 for an `assumed` field
the W_abs integral reads; none of the three electromechanical checks and no direction witness apply, and the
output reports them as not applicable to the kind. `shield.budget --absorber <card>` takes an absorber card
in place of the boundary's own (the VERBS-shield row). `shield.budget` reports the card, x_lim, W_abs with
its rounding, the weakest field and the condition check. Determinism exact (D0); the evaluation is host arithmetic over rationals.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A card of each `AbsorberKind` loads, round-trips byte for byte and has a stable content hash across platforms.
- [ ] `absorber` is in the `FMT-FCARD` row's closed kind list of `registries/formats.toml` with its generator, and BD-141's `fmt_fcard_roundtrip` and `fmt_fcard_every_kind_generated` pass with it.
- [ ] `bumper-50N-40mm` evaluates to exactly 2,000,000 at the energy scale 10⁻⁶ J, and `bumper-150N-30mm` to 4,500,000.
- [ ] A force limit below the envelope's maximum truncates the integral at the first crossing; the reported x_lim is exact.
- [ ] Field uncertainty reduces W_abs (never increases it), and W_abs is rounded down to the energy step.
- [ ] Each invalid card of the planted set (decreasing knots, negative force, last knot short of the stroke, F_hi below F_lo, viscous without a rate rule) is refused at load with FROB-REF-103 and the offending field named; `frob actuator check` on a valid absorber card exits 0 with the electromechanical checks reported not applicable, and under `--strict-data` on an `assumed` catalog card exits 3 with FROB-REF-113.
- [ ] A budget whose conditions fall outside the card's is refused with FROB-REF-451 and the condition as `pointer`; so is a used non-reusable card.
- [ ] A `.fenergy` document whose `w_abs_j` exceeds the card's recomputed value is refused with FROB-REF-451, pointer `absorber.w_abs_j`; one that is lower is accepted and the lower value is used.
- [ ] With every other input `measured` and the absorber `assumed`, the budget's class is `Estimated` with `weakest` pointing at the absorber field.
- [ ] The offline auditor recomputes W_abs from the card bytes in the bundle (its absorber step, N22b-14) and agrees with this bead's value on the four catalog cards and the unit-test cards; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-shield` documents the kind.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-shield/tests/absorber_card.rs`; real container loader; cards labelled `synthetic`.
- `constant_force_exact`: 50 N over 0.040 m → 2.00 J exactly.
- `spring_exact`: k_min = 2,000 N/m, stroke 0.040 m → ½·2000·0.0016 = 1.60 J.
- `ramp_with_force_limit`: F_lo rising linearly from 0 to 50 N over 40 mm with a force limit of 40 N: x_lim = 32 mm and W_abs = ½·40·0.032 = 0.64 J (against 1.00 J without the limit).
- `crush_plateau_lower_bound`: knots (0, 0), (5 mm, 300 N), (30 mm, 300 N): W_abs = 0.75 + 7.50 = 8.25 J.
- `uncertainty_only_lowers`: property test over random envelopes and uncertainties.
- `rounding_is_downward`: W_abs = 1.9999995 J at step 10⁻⁶ J stores 1,999,999.
- `invalid_cards_refused`: the five planted defects.
- `conditions_outside_refuse`, `non_reusable_after_use_refused`.
- `document_copy_cannot_exceed_card`: `w_abs_j` "2.50" on `bumper-50N-40mm` → FROB-REF-451, pointer
  `absorber.w_abs_j`, observed 2,500,000 against 2,000,000; "1.90" is accepted and 1,900,000 is used.
- `weakest_field_sets_provenance`: knots `measured`, stroke `assumed` → `assumed`.
- `actuator_check_on_absorber`: `actuator.check` on each catalog card runs the form and field rules only (the
  three electromechanical checks and the witness absent); a card with an unknown field → FROB-REF-103; with
  `--strict-data` an `assumed` stroke → FROB-REF-113.

### End-to-end script and logging
Steps added to `scripts/e2e/shield_energy_budget.sh` (bead N22b-18), under the step names `absorber_*`:
1. `frob actuator list --kind absorber --json` (the four catalog cards) and `frob actuator show bumper-50N-40mm --json`: assert kind `absorber`, stroke, provenance `assumed`. `frob actuator check bumper-50N-40mm --json`: exit 0, the kind's field rules reported and no electromechanical check; with `--strict-data`: exit 3, FROB-REF-113.
2. `frob shield budget <design> --boundary dock_stop_A --reading-max 0.39 --key absorber-2 --json` on the worked card (printed reading): assert `w_abs` 2.00 J, total 1.49 J, margin 0.51 J, class `Estimated`, weakest input the absorber.
3. The exported document of step 2 with `w_abs_j` edited to "2.50", read through `frob shield check <run> --energy-budget <edited.fenergy> --key absorber-3 --json` (the run is the monitored run of N22b-18's step 9): exit code 3, FROB-REF-451, pointer `absorber.w_abs_j`.
4. The same with the temperature condition outside the card's range (key `absorber-4`): exit code 3, FROB-REF-451, pointer at the condition.
5. `frob-verify` on the bundle written by `frob prove bundle <budget of step 2> --out <tmp>/absorber-5.frobcert --key absorber-5 --json` (step 2 itself writes no bundle): the auditor's recomputed W_abs equals the reported one.
Each step logs the command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block, the card hash, x_lim, W_abs as an
integer at the declared scale, and on refusal the code and pointer.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-568 and KA-153 (the drop-stop card): 8 kg at 0.4 m/s gives 0.64 J; a 20 W drive over a 25 ms cutoff adds 0.50 J; gravity on an 8% slope over the 40 mm stroke adds 8·9.81·0.040·0.08 = 0.251136 J, printed as 0.25 J (the printed reading below; the product reading gives 1.491136 J and 50.4432 ms); a series spring 0.10 J; total 1.49 J against W_abs = 50 N × 40 mm = 2.00 J; longest cutoff delay (2.00 − 0.99)/20 = 50.5 ms, or 45.5 ms with a further 0.10 J of reserve.
- The worked artifact's budget: 150 N over 30 mm is 4,500,000 at scale 10⁻⁶ J.
- Negative controls: an evaluator that rounds W_abs to nearest (the rounding test fails); one that ignores the force limit (the ramp test returns 1.00 J); one that extrapolates the envelope beyond a condition range; one that takes the upper envelope for the lower.
- Metamorphic: splitting a segment by inserting a knot on the line leaves W_abs unchanged; scaling every force by c scales W_abs by c exactly.
- Determinism: D0: identical twice, in debug and release, on Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- The whole tier is no stronger than this card: with generic cards every budget is `Estimated`. The kill gate KILL-ENERGY keeps the claim or reduces the tier to an `Estimated` alarm; the card format is needed on both branches.
- Rate dependence is the main physical hazard: foam and viscous dampers take less work when approached slowly or cold. The card must then state a lower bound valid over its whole speed range, or declare a table; a card silent on rate is treated as valid only inside its stated speed range.
- A crush element is single-use; the cycle count of a part is run state supplied by the caller, and the tier cannot check it.
- A scene fixture or a retrofit kit that adds a bump stop references an absorber card by hash, so the building-side and robot-side budgets read the same envelope.
- Open question OPEN-SHIELD-1 (owner: the Sinew lead with Ground): fail-closed default `assumed` cards and `Estimated` claims, enforced here.
