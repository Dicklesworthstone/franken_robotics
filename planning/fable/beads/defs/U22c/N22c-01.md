## Background

`frob control synth <design>` detects a design's morphology class and assembles a baseline controller from the
model, then reports one of three outcomes: `not-synthesizable` with a reason, `synthesized-failed` with a failure
taxonomy, or `ok`. `frob control show` prints the assembled controller. The capability matters because it lets a
new design be simulated at once instead of after a training run.

Its first gate is GATE-REFLEX-01 (claim: Known answer). At milestone M1 the gate's rows are: each of the first
archetypes (`quad-mammal`, a dog-like quadruped with 12 actuated joints; `humanoid`, 29 joints; `diff-drive`, two
driven wheels and a caster; `mecanum`, four mecanum wheels) stands for 10 s and traverses 5 m of flat ground under
its baseline controller, on the **referee** tier only, restricted to archetypes whose contact law exists at M1 and
whose contact count fits the referee's limit of 21 contacts in one solve (FACT:
`frankensim/crates/fs-contact/src/sustained/solve.rs`, `MAX_CONTACTS = 21`). The same result supplies the
"stands or rolls under its baseline controller" rows of GATE-SKEL-03 for the archetypes that have landed.

A gate passes only with a recorded measurement against its threshold and a negative control that has been shown
to fail. This bead is the script that produces that record from the user's side of the tool.

## Scope

In: one shell script driving the real `frob` binary on the four archetypes; assertions on the three-outcome
report, on the stand and traverse measurements, on the layer records and on `control.show`; the planted-defect
controls; the gate rows written to the gate register. Out: the `batch-cpu` rows of M2 (N22c-47); the remaining
archetypes at M3 (BD-247) and the tracked and hybrid ones at M4 (N22c-48).

## Technical approach

Script: `scripts/e2e/control_synth_first_archetypes.sh`. It sources the shared helper of the end-to-end standard,
is idempotent, takes its seed from `FROB_E2E_SEED` (default 1) and exits non-zero on any failed assertion.

Steps, per archetype A in {`quad-mammal`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`}:

1. `frob design templates --json` and `frob design check A --json`: assert the archetype exists and passes preflight stages 1 to 6 with no refusal (a precondition of GATE-SKEL-03).
2. `frob control synth A --tier referee --state estimated --seed $SEED --json`: assert `outcome` is one of the three values; record `controller_id`, the class and the outcome.
3. If the outcome is `ok`: assert from the result `stand_s ≥ 10`, `traverse_m ≥ 5`, no terminal failure class, layer fraction of `backup` and `fallback:*` reported (not asserted zero), `actuator-infeasible` absent, standing `screening`.
4. Repeat step 2 with `--state privileged`; assert the envelope reports the estimated-versus-privileged difference; if the privileged run is `ok` and the estimated one is not, record the row as failed with cause `estimator-dependent`.
5. `frob control show A --json`: assert the identity equals the one from step 2 and every identity field is present (class, gains with derivations, programs with iteration counts, planning configuration, latency model, group used, phase source).
6. If the outcome is `not-synthesizable`: assert a reason is present; where the reason is a program, pass the certificate to `frob-verify` and assert it accepts. Record the gate row for A as `blocked: route-not-built` or `fail` according to the reason, never as `pass`.
7. If the referee refuses for capacity (an island above 21 contacts, FROB-REF-721): record the row as `blocked: referee-capacity`, with the contact count.

Then the controls (step group `controls`):

8. Planted defect: `quad-mammal` with one hip PD gain sign flipped (a fixture design under `assets/fixtures/reflex/`, labelled `synthetic`). Assert `synthesized-failed`, failure class `fall`, a first failed layer, and a next rung with its required evidence.
9. Planted infeasibility: the single-contact pusher fixture (50 N weight, 30 N required push, μ = 0.5). Assert `not-synthesizable` with certificate y = (1/2, −1) and yᵀb = −5 N; nudge the certificate file to (0.49, −1) and assert `frob-verify` rejects it.
10. Planted refusal: `frob control synth <three-legged fixture> --require --json` with no fallback admitted. Assert `ok: false`, code FROB-REF-421, exit 3, three ranked repairs.
11. Determinism: run step 2 twice for `diff-drive`; assert equal `controller_id` and equal episode digests.

Finally the script writes one row per archetype and per gate to the gate register through `frob gate record`
(or the registry's own writer), citing the commit, the command, the output digest and the receipt.

**Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/control_synth_first_archetypes/<utc>.ndjson` with a
readable transcript beside it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22c-01`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`,
`assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence`
block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). Metrics with units and thresholds: `stand_s` (≥ 10 s),
`traverse_m` (≥ 5 m), `synth_cpu_s` (TARGET < 5 s), `layer_fraction.*`, `state_gap_m` (estimated minus
privileged). On failure: the first differing value and the paths of the run's `.frun` episode and the envelope.
A final `summary` line: assertions passed and failed, and the gates touched (GATE-REFLEX-01, GATE-SKEL-03) with
the status per archetype.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, is executable, and exits 0 on a healthy tree and non-zero when any assertion fails.
- [ ] For each first archetype the gate register holds a row with measured `stand_s` and `traverse_m` (or a `blocked` status with its cause), the commit, the command and a receipt hash.
- [ ] The three controls (steps 8 to 10) behave as stated; a tree where the planted defect reports `ok` fails the script.
- [ ] The log contains one `metric` line per measured value with its unit and threshold, and one `summary` line.
- [ ] No log line contains scene data from a private scene (the fixtures are public assets).
- [ ] Rerunning the script with the same seed yields the same digests (step 11) and the same register rows.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The script's helpers are tested in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `parses_three_outcomes` (a recorded envelope of each
kind is classified correctly), `refuses_unknown_outcome` (a fourth value fails), `gate_row_never_pass_when_blocked`
(a `not-synthesizable` input cannot produce a `pass` row).

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified above; this bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answer values asserted: stand 10 s and traverse 5 m (gate thresholds, TARGET); the friction certificate
y = (1/2, −1), yᵀb = −5 N (KA-556). Negative controls: the PD sign flip (must fail the stand or traverse); the
nudged certificate (must be rejected, CX-235); the three-legged body without a fallback (must be refused with
FROB-REF-421). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine with equal digests; the referee runs in `f64`, so the lane
across worker counts applies to the batched rows of N22c-47, not here.

## Considerations

- The humanoid's baseline route (biped balance) is Frontier and lands at M3. At M1 its row is expected to be
  `blocked: route-not-built` unless the legged route can stand it; the script must record that honestly, and the M1
  exit review reads the row as "part due later", not as a failure of the milestone.
- The mecanum row requires the roller-wheel contact law on the referee at M1; if absent the row is `blocked:
  contact-law`.
- A run that ends with a numerical outcome (the solver refused or faulted) leaves the verdict unknown; it is
  logged as `numerical`, not counted as a physical failure, and not dropped.
