## Background

`frob control synth <design>` detects a design's morphology class and assembles a baseline controller from the
model, then reports one of three outcomes: `not-synthesizable` with a reason, `synthesized-failed` with a failure
taxonomy, or `ok`. Its standing smoke always runs under estimated and under privileged state and reports both and
their difference (the verb has no `state` input). `frob control show` prints the assembled controller. The
capability matters because it lets a new design be simulated at once instead of after a training run.

Its first gate is GATE-REFLEX-01 (claim: Known answer). At milestone M1 the gate's rows are: each of the first
archetypes (`quad-mammal`, a dog-like quadruped with 12 actuated joints; `humanoid`, 29 joints; `diff-drive`, two
driven wheels and a caster; `mecanum`, four mecanum wheels) stands for 10 s and traverses 5 m of flat ground under
its baseline controller, on the **referee** tier only, restricted to archetypes whose contact law exists at M1 and
whose contact count fits the referee's limit of 21 contacts in one solve (FACT:
`frankensim/crates/fs-contact/src/sustained/solve.rs`, `MAX_CONTACTS = 21`). The same runs touch the "stands or
rolls under its baseline controller" rows of GATE-SKEL-03, whose M1 part is recorded by N14b-06 from its own tier
run.

A gate passes only with a recorded measurement against its threshold and a negative control that has been shown
to fail. A script never writes a gate record: it produces a receipt, and the gate's one recorder enters the value
from that receipt. This bead is that recorder for the M1 part of GATE-REFLEX-01 (the register declaration names
"BD-118 with N22c-01" at M1; BD-247 states that the M1 and M2 parts are N22c-01's and N22c-47's), and it owns the
scripts that produce the receipt from the user's side of the tool.

## Scope

In: the script `scripts/e2e/control_synth_first_archetypes.sh` driving the real `frob` binary on the four
archetypes; the script `scripts/e2e/reflex_wheeled_baseline.sh`, whose content BD-117 fixes and which BD-117
cannot run (its only reach to the controller is `control.synth`, whose bead BD-118 depends on BD-117), written
and run here as a stage of the first; assertions on the three-outcome report, on the stand and traverse
measurements, on the layer records and on `control.show`; the planted-defect controls; the conformance case
`gate_reflex_01_m1_rows` for `humanoid`, `diff-drive` and `mecanum` under the GATE-REFLEX-01 entry of
`g1_known_answers`; the record of the M1 part of GATE-REFLEX-01 from the tier run's receipt.

Out: the `quad-mammal` measurement on the referee, with its weak-knee and three-leg patches, which is N22a-11's
script and its legged case under the same entry (requested of N22a-11: its case is registered under the
GATE-REFLEX-01 entry of `g1_known_answers` and its script produces a receipt and writes no row); this script still
runs `control.synth` and `control.show` on `quad-mammal` and fails when its stand and traverse measurements
disagree with N22a-11's receipt under the same thresholds. The M1 part of GATE-SKEL-03 (N14b-06). The exact
offline audit of certificates by `frob-verify` (N22c-02 for the planar `lp-farkas` certificates at M1; the conic
kind arrives with N12-16 at M4). The `batch-cpu` rows of M2 (N22c-47 and, for `quad-mammal`, N22a-13); the
remaining archetypes at M3 (BD-247) and the tracked and hybrid ones at M4 (N22c-48).

## Technical approach

Script: `scripts/e2e/control_synth_first_archetypes.sh`, header `# e2e: bead=N22c-01 milestone=M1
labels=reflex,control,gate gates=GATE-REFLEX-01,GATE-SKEL-03 seed=1 needs=cpu plants=pd-sign-ok,identity-no-gains`.
It sources the shared helper of the end-to-end standard, is idempotent, takes its seed from `FROB_E2E_SEED`
(default 1) and exits non-zero on any failed assertion. Every call of a run or mutate verb carries a distinct key
written with `e2e_key` (`n22c01-<step>-<archetype>-<seed>`), so that no call returns an earlier call's stored
receipt.

Steps, per archetype A in {`quad-mammal`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`} (`<ns>` is `n22c01`):

1. `frob design templates --json`; `frob design new <ns>-A --template A --key <k> --json`; `frob design check
   <ns>-A --json`: assert the archetype exists and passes preflight stages 1 to 6 with no refusal (a precondition
   of GATE-SKEL-03).
2. `frob control synth <ns>-A --tier referee --seed $SEED --key <k> --json`: assert `outcome` is one of the three
   values; record `controller_id`, the class and the outcome.
3. If the outcome is `ok`: assert from the result `stand_s ≥ 10`, `traverse_m ≥ 5`, no terminal failure class,
   layer fraction of `backup` and `fallback:*` reported (not asserted zero), `actuator-infeasible` absent,
   standing `screening`.
4. From the same envelope (no second call): assert that both halves of the smoke are present with their
   state-source headers (`estimated`, `privileged`) and that the paired difference is reported; if the privileged
   half passes and the estimated half does not (N22a-03's flag `privileged-only`), the row fails with cause
   `estimator-dependent`; an estimated half printed `not run` (a missing sensor) fails the row with that cause.
5. `frob control show <ns>-A --json`: assert the identity equals the one from step 2 and every identity field is
   present (class, gains with derivations, programs with iteration counts, planning configuration, latency model,
   group used, phase source).
6. If the outcome is `not-synthesizable`: assert a reason is present; where the reason is a program, assert that the
   envelope's certificate carries its exact re-check as passed (PO-REFLEX-03, in process) and re-check it in the
   script's helper `n22c01_planar_farkas` (exact integer arithmetic on the rationals' numerators and denominators:
   membership a ≥ 0 and a² ≥ μ²·c², and yᵀb < 0). The row is `blocked: route-not-built` or `fail` according to the
   reason, never `pass`.
7. If the smoke reports the referee capacity refusal (FROB-REF-721, exit 5, an island above 21 contacts): the row
   is `blocked: referee-capacity`, with the contact count.

   For A = `quad-mammal` the stand and traverse values are compared with N22a-11's receipt, and a disagreement
   fails the script.

Stage `wheeled` (step group `wheeled`): `scripts/e2e/reflex_wheeled_baseline.sh`, written by this bead with the
content BD-117 fixes and run here for `diff-drive` and `mecanum`: `frob design new <ns>-<archetype> --template
<archetype> --key <k>`; `frob control synth <ns>-<archetype> --tier referee --seed 1 --key <k> --json`; `frob sim
run <ns>-<archetype> --tier referee --task stand-10s --seed 1 --key <k> --json`; `frob sim run <ns>-<archetype>
--tier referee --task traverse-5m-flat --state estimated --seed 1 --key <k> --json`, then the same with `--state
privileged` under a distinct key; `frob telemetry summary` of each run. Asserts: distance at least 5 m, no fall or
stall event, peak wheel current at most the card limit and equal to it during the commanded launch, saturation
report present, estimated-against-privileged difference reported. Its control (`e2e_expect_caught`): `frob design
new mecanum-par --file fixtures/reflex/mecanum-parallel-rollers.fbot --key <k>` (BD-117's fixture) then `frob
control synth mecanum-par --key <k> --json` returns `not-synthesizable` naming the missing direction (−1, 1, 0);
declared plant `no-clamp` asserts against a current limit 10% above the card's, so the peak-equals-limit assertion
must fail. Its header names `bead=N22c-01` and its log carries BD-117's fields (distance in m, time in s, peak
current in A with the limit, the digests of the two runs).

Then the controls (step group `controls`):

8. Planted defect: `quad-mammal` with one hip PD gain sign flipped (a fixture design under `assets/fixtures/reflex/`,
   labelled `synthetic`, created with `frob design new <ns>-pdflip --file … --key <k>`). Assert
   `synthesized-failed`, failure class `fall`, a first failed layer, and a next rung with its required evidence.
   Declared plant `pd-sign-ok`: the script asserts `ok` for it, which must fail.
9. Planted infeasibility: the single-contact pusher fixture (50 N weight, 30 N required push, μ = 0.5). Assert
   `not-synthesizable` with certificate y = (1/2, −1) and yᵀb = −5 N, and that `n22c01_planar_farkas` accepts it;
   then the nudged copy (0.49, −1) must be rejected by the same helper (49/100 ≥ 1/2 fails: 98 < 100).
10. Planted refusal: `frob control synth <three-legged fixture> --require --key <k> --json` with no fallback
    admitted. Assert `ok: false`, code FROB-REF-421, exit 3, three ranked repairs.
11. Determinism: run step 2 twice for `diff-drive` under two distinct keys and the same seed; assert equal
    `controller_id` and equal episode digests.
12. Identity follows the body (INV-REFLEX-02 from the user's side; the controller-identity bead N22a-07 comes
    before `control.synth` and cannot call it): `frob design new <ns>-quad-heavy --template quad-mammal --key <k>
    --json`, then one typed edit that doubles the mass of the front-left thigh with its inertia tensor scaled by 2
    about the same centre (`frob design edit <ns>-quad-heavy set-material leg.fl.thigh.material
    fixtures/reflex/thigh-density-x2.fmat --key <k> --json`: a material card of twice the thigh's density,
    labelled `synthetic`; an edit of one image under the `mirror`, so it splits the constructor and is declared
    `reported`; inertia follows the material, never an edit target, BD-058), then `frob control synth
    <ns>-quad-heavy --seed $SEED --key <k> --json` and `frob control show <ns>-quad-heavy --json`. Assert that the
    `controller_id` differs from step 2's for `quad-mammal`; that `control.show` lists, for every joint whose
    reflected inertia contains that link, a changed gain with its derivation inputs (the new reflected inertia
    M̄ⱼ, ωⱼ unchanged unless a reduction is recorded, and K_p = M̄ⱼ·ωⱼ²); and that a field-by-field diff of the two
    records changes only gains and quantities derived from the edited mass. Synthesizing `<ns>-quad-heavy` twice
    under distinct keys gives one identity. Declared plant `identity-no-gains` (a mutation build whose identity
    omits the gains): the inequality assertion must fail.

Then the tier run (step `receipt`): `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-REFLEX-01 --mutations`, which runs
`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations --
GATE-REFLEX-01`. The entry holds this bead's case `gate_reflex_01_m1_rows` (`humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`:
the synthesis smoke on the referee, stand-10s and traverse-5m-flat, both state sources; the PD-sign-flip mutation
under `gauntlet-mutations`, registered as the gate's negative control) and N22a-11's legged case; the run writes
one hash-named receipt under `target/gauntlet/receipts/`. The script asserts on the receipt's `gates` and
`controls` (the control caught) with `e2e_get --file`, and asserts that each of its own measured values agrees with
the receipt's for the same archetype and seed. The script records nothing.

**Recording (not part of the script).** A session that did not implement BD-116, BD-117 or BD-118 enters the M1
part from that receipt: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-REFLEX-01 --receipt
<hash> --part M1` (the gate recorder of N38b-19). The part's `measured` field lists, per archetype, `stand_s`,
`traverse_m`, the worst fallback fraction and the synthesis time, or the `blocked` cause with its count; its
`detail` names any archetype that is `blocked`.

**Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/control_synth_first_archetypes/<utc>.ndjson` (and
`target/e2e-logs/reflex_wheeled_baseline/<utc>.ndjson` for the stage) with a readable transcript beside each.
Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22c-01`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`,
`end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier,
determinism, identities, seed). Metrics with units and thresholds: `stand_s` (≥ 10 s), `traverse_m` (≥ 5 m),
`synth_cpu_s` (TARGET < 5 s), `layer_fraction.*`, `state_gap_m` (estimated minus privileged). Identity lines:
`controller_id` per archetype, the identity read back by `control.show`, and for step 11 both runs'
`controller_id` and episode digests side by side with an `equal` field; for step 12 both identities and the record
diff (field paths and old and new values with units). The `receipt` step's `cmd` line carries the receipt hash. On
failure: the first differing value and the paths of the run's `.frun` episode and the envelope. A final `summary`
line: assertions passed and failed, controls caught, the receipt hash, and the gates touched (GATE-REFLEX-01,
GATE-SKEL-03) with the status per archetype.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both scripts exist, are executable, and exit 0 on a healthy tree and non-zero when any assertion fails.
- [ ] No call passes `--state` to `control.synth`; every run and mutate call carries a distinct `--key`; the
      privileged half and the difference are read from the one synthesis envelope.
- [ ] The tier run writes one receipt that covers the four first archetypes and catches the PD-sign-flip control;
      the M1 part of GATE-REFLEX-01 is recorded from it with `record_gate --part M1` by a non-implementing session,
      with measured `stand_s` and `traverse_m` per archetype (or a `blocked` status with its cause); the script's
      log contains no `record_gate` call (the linter's `self-recording` check passes).
- [ ] The controls of steps 8 to 10 and of the wheeled stage behave as stated; a tree where the planted defect
      reports `ok` fails the script; the nudged certificate is rejected by the helper.
- [ ] Step 12: the mass-doubled `quad-mammal` gets a different `controller_id`, `control.show` lists the changed
      gains with their derivation inputs, and a tree whose identity omits the gains (planted in a mutation build)
      fails the step.
- [ ] The log contains one `metric` line per measured value with its unit and threshold, and one `summary` line.
- [ ] No log line contains scene data from a private scene (the fixtures are public assets).
- [ ] Rerunning the script with the same seed yields the same digests (step 11) and the same receipt contents.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The script's helpers are tested in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `parses_three_outcomes` (a recorded envelope of each
kind is classified correctly), `refuses_unknown_outcome` (a fourth value fails), `gate_row_never_pass_when_blocked`
(a `not-synthesizable` input cannot produce a `pass` status in the receipt comparison), `planar_farkas_exact`
(y = (1/2, −1), μ = 1/2, b = (50, 30) accepted with yᵀb = −5 exactly; (49/100, −1) rejected for membership;
(1/2, −1) with b = (50, 25) rejected for yᵀb = 0). In `crates/frob-conformance`: `gate_reflex_01_m1_rows` (the
case above; for each archetype with a route the smoke measures stand ≥ 10 s and traverse ≥ 5 m on both state
sources, and an archetype without one is reported `blocked` with its reason, never passed).

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified above; this bead is the script and its wheeled stage.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answer values asserted: stand 10 s and traverse 5 m (gate thresholds, TARGET); the friction certificate
y = (1/2, −1), yᵀb = −5 N (KA-556). Negative controls: the PD sign flip (must fail the stand or traverse, and is
the registered control the tier run catches); the nudged certificate (must be rejected, CX-235); the three-legged
body without a fallback (must be refused with FROB-REF-421); the parallel-roller mecanum and the `no-clamp` plant of
the wheeled stage; the `identity-no-gains` plant. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine with equal digests; the
referee runs in `f64`, so the lane across worker counts applies to the batched rows of N22c-47, not here.

## Considerations

- The humanoid's baseline route (biped balance) is Frontier and lands at M3. At M1 its row is expected to be
  `blocked: route-not-built` unless the legged route can stand it; the script must record that honestly, and the M1
  exit review reads the row as "part due later", not as a failure of the milestone.
- The mecanum row requires the roller-wheel contact law on the referee at M1; if absent the row is `blocked:
  contact-law`.
- A run that ends with a numerical outcome (the solver refused or faulted) leaves the verdict unknown; it is
  logged as `numerical`, not counted as a physical failure, and not dropped.
- The wheeled stage calls `sim.run --task` at M1 on the referee; VERBS-sim registers `--task` with its owner at M2,
  and a request for its M1 owner on the referee tier is open (filed this round from the skeleton slice). Until it
  is registered the stage's `sim run --task` calls are refused as an unknown parameter (`FROB-REF-802`) and logged
as `not run`, never passed; the
  stand and traverse rows are then read from the synthesis smoke, which runs the same tasks.
- The conic certificates of three-dimensional contact are checked in process at M1 and reported `Certified
  (in-process; offline kind pending N12-16)`, as BD-251 does; the planar pusher fixture is polyhedral and is the
  `lp-farkas` kind, audited offline by N22c-02.
