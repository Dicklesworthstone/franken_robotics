## Background

Reflex, the control subsystem, states seven invariants (properties its code maintains at all times, prefix `INV-`)
and nine proof obligations (properties with a named verification, prefix `PO-`). They are the chapter's contract
with every consumer: a telemetry reader may assume that every command names the layer that produced it; an
auditor may assume that every infeasibility report carries a certificate that checks exactly. The project's
testing rule is that every invariant and every proof obligation has a property test, and that each test has a
planted violation it catches.

The mechanisms these properties constrain are built in several beads (the invariant filter in BD-114, the legged
and wheeled baselines in BD-116 and BD-117, the cone solver in BD-115). Each mechanism bead tests its own code.
This bead is the **conformance-side battery**: one registered case per property in `frob-conformance`, run against
the assembled system, with its planted violation, so that a property cannot be dropped because its mechanism was
split or refactored. Part I holds the four properties whose mechanisms exist at M1; part II (N22c-03) holds the
other twelve at M4.

## Scope

In: the statement of each of the four properties in the `invariants` section of the owning crate's `CONTRACT.md`;
one conformance case and one planted violation per property; the auditor re-check for PO-REFLEX-03. Out: the
mechanisms; the remaining properties (N22c-03); the telemetry-level form of INV-REFLEX-01 (layer records and
fallback events read back from a run's telemetry stream), whose mechanism N22a-15 lands at M3 and whose case
part II (N22c-03) carries.

## Technical approach

**INV-REFLEX-01. Every command that reaches a driver records the layer that produced it, and every fallback is a
telemetry event with its reason.** Decision ADR-REFLEX-08 gives the reason: "it worked" is not a result if the
backup controller did the work. The layer values are `baseline`, `baseline+residual`, `shield-filtered`,
`fallback:<reason>` and `backup`.
- Case (M1 form, on what exists at M1: the layer tag of every command and the synthesis report's fallback log of N22a-12): run each M1 baseline (quadruped, differential drive, mecanum) for 20 s on the referee through the smoke path of `control.synth`; in process, assert that the count of command samples handed to the actuator stage equals the count of layer tags, tick for tick; from the report, assert that the layer fractions sum to one over the episode's ticks and that every maximal run of consecutive ticks whose layer starts with `fallback` or equals `backup` has exactly one entry in the fallback log with its tick and a non-empty reason, and that every such tick lies inside a logged run. The same statement read from telemetry (layer records, `enter` and `exit` fallback events) is N22c-03's case at M3.
- DESIGN for enforcement: the driver-facing type is `Command<L: LayerTag>`; the function that hands a command to the actuator stage takes the layer as an argument, so a command without a layer does not compile (a compile-fail test).
- Planted violation: a controller build that swaps in the last feasible command on an infeasible tick without logging the fallback. The case must fail with the tick index.

**INV-REFLEX-02. A `ControllerId` covers everything that affects the controller's output: class, gains, programs,
iteration counts, planning configuration, latency model and the group used.**
- Case (metamorphic): for each archetype, enumerate single-field mutations of the controller description (one gain scaled by 1.01; one program's iteration count plus one; a planning step changed; the latency model changed from one control period to zero; the symmetry group changed: from Γ_use to trivial where a controller uses one, and at M1, where every baseline records `group: none`, from `none` to a planted one-generator group, which must change the identity whether or not the rollout changes). For each: if the digest of a 5 s referee rollout changes, the identity must have changed. Conversely, a change that the description does not contain (reordering fields in the source file, whitespace) must leave the identity equal.
- Planted violation: an identity function that omits the iteration count. The mutation "iteration count plus one" changes the rollout digest with an equal identity; the case must report the field.

**PO-REFLEX-01. The filter's linearization has the four-dimensional null space of position and yaw at every
estimate.** Under proprioception and gravity, absolute position (three directions) and yaw (one) are unobservable.
The contact-aided invariant extended Kalman filter on SE₂₊ₖ(3) must have exactly that null space; a filter
linearized at a moving estimate in the conventional way gains a spurious yaw direction and becomes overconfident.
- Verification (as stated by the obligation): a rank test on 10³ trajectories, and finite perturbation of the true initial state.
- Case A (rank): for 10³ seeded trajectories of a flat trot with simulated IMU, encoders and contact switches, assemble the stacked observability matrix from the filter's own transition and measurement Jacobians at its estimates over a window of 50 steps; assert its numerical null space has dimension four (the fourth-smallest singular value below 10⁻¹⁰ of the largest and the fifth-smallest above 10⁻⁶ of the largest: the criterion of the filter's own test in BD-114, so that the mechanism's test and this case cannot disagree) and that the null vectors span the three translations and yaw (principal angle below 10⁻⁶ rad).
- Case B (perturbation): shift the whole scene (robot and ground together) by 1 m along each translation and rotate it by 0.1 rad about the vertical; assert the delivered measurement packets agree with the unshifted run to a relative tolerance of 10⁻⁹ on the referee and that the filter's innovations agree to the same tolerance. Shifting the robot alone would move it relative to the ground (a vertical shift lifts it off the floor), and bit identity is not expected, because the simulator integrates in absolute coordinates whose rounding changes with the offset (DESIGN; the tolerance is revisited with the filter's owner if the measured spread is larger, never loosened silently).
- Planted violation: the conventional quaternion extended Kalman filter used as the comparator, evaluated by Case A: the null space has dimension three (yaw observable in the linearization). The case must see it (known answer KA-553's negative control).

**PO-REFLEX-03. Every infeasibility certificate checks in exact rational arithmetic: Aᵀy ∈ K* and bᵀy < 0.** For a
program "find x in K with A·x = b", such a y proves that no solution exists, because 0 ≤ ⟨Aᵀy, x⟩ = yᵀb < 0 would
follow. The orthant and the second-order cone are self-dual; the four-dimensional contact cone is a second-order
cone after scaling the tangential and spin components by the friction coefficient and the effective patch radius.
- Verification: the offline auditor (`frob-verify`, crate `frob-cert`) re-checks each emitted certificate.
- Exact membership test for a scaled second-order cone {(a, c): a ≥ μ·‖c‖}: a ≥ 0 and a² ≥ μ²·(c·c), all in rationals, so no square root is taken.
- Case: collect every certificate emitted during the M1 batteries and the fixtures (at least: the friction fixture; 10³ random infeasible planar single-contact programs, normal plus one tangential component, generated by demanding a tangential force above μ times the available normal force; 10³ random infeasible three-dimensional single-step force-distribution programs over the four-dimensional contact cone). A certificate whose cone blocks are all orthants or planar friction cones (polyhedral) is of the proof kind `lp-farkas`, which the offline auditor checks at M1 (BD-132): each is written by the case as an entry of a `.frobcert` bundle under `target/conformance/reflex/po_reflex_03/` and passed to `frob-verify`; assert 100% acceptance. A certificate with a second-order block is of the kind `conic-farkas`, whose offline checker arrives at M4 (N12-16): it is re-checked by the same exact rule in the test binary and reported `Certified (in-process; offline kind pending N12-16)`, counted separately, as BD-251 reports it. In every case assert that the solver's end state was `infeasible` (an `unconverged` end state must carry no certificate).
- Planted violation: each certificate nudged by one unit in the last place of one rational component so that it leaves the dual cone; the auditor must reject every nudged certificate. A second mutant marks an `unconverged` run as `infeasible` with the last iterate as y; the auditor must reject it (CX-235).

**Where the cases live.** `crates/frob-conformance/src/reflex/invariants.rs`, registered in the conformance
registry under tier G0 (laws) for INV-REFLEX-01, INV-REFLEX-02 and PO-REFLEX-03's arithmetic, and G1 (known
answers) for PO-REFLEX-01; each registered with its mutant in the negative-control registry (the mutants are
compiled only under `gauntlet-mutations`). CX-235's plant `N22c-02:unconverged_reported_infeasible` is the second
PO-REFLEX-03 mutant (N22-CX names this bead as its planting bead).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `crates/frob-control/CONTRACT.md` lists INV-REFLEX-01 and INV-REFLEX-02 with their statements in its invariants section.
- [ ] The four cases pass on the M1 archetypes; each planted violation fails its case with a message naming the tick, field, direction or certificate.
- [ ] PO-REFLEX-01: null space of dimension four on 10³ of 10³ trajectories; the comparator shows dimension three.
- [ ] PO-REFLEX-03: `frob-verify` accepts every emitted `lp-farkas` certificate and rejects every nudged one, on a machine with no GPU; every `conic-farkas` certificate passes the in-process exact check and every nudged one fails it, with the count reported separately as `Certified (in-process)`.
- [ ] A command constructed without a layer tag fails to compile (compile-fail test recorded).
- [ ] The cases are part of the `smoke` battery and their results feed the rows of GATE-REFLEX-03 (PO-REFLEX-01) and, at M3, GATE-REFLEX-05 (layer records, certificates).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-conformance reflex::invariants`:
- `inv_reflex_01_layer_per_command`: in-process tag count equals command count; layer fractions sum to one; every run of fallback or backup ticks has one fallback-log entry with a reason; the planted silent fallback fails with its tick.
- `inv_reflex_01_compile_fail`: `trybuild` case constructing a driver command with no layer.
- `inv_reflex_02_identity_tracks_output`: five mutations, each changes identity and digest together.
- `inv_reflex_02_identity_ignores_formatting`: whitespace and field order leave the identity equal.
- `po_reflex_01_rank_four`: 10³ trajectories, fourth-smallest singular value < 10⁻¹⁰·σ_max and fifth-smallest > 10⁻⁶·σ_max, principal angle < 10⁻⁶ rad.
- `po_reflex_01_gauge_perturbation`: packets and innovations equal to 10⁻⁹ relative under the four whole-scene shifts; a planted filter that reads absolute position from a sensor model fails it.
- `po_reflex_03_friction_fixture`: A = I, b = (50, 30) N, μ = 1/2, y = (1/2, −1): a = 1/2 ≥ μ·abs(−1) = 1/2 holds with equality, yᵀb = 25 − 30 = −5 < 0.
- `po_reflex_03_random_programs`: 10³ planar certificates accepted by `frob-verify` and 10³ nudged ones rejected; 10³ three-dimensional certificates accepted by the in-process exact check and their nudged copies rejected.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/reflex_invariants_m1.sh` (header `bead=N22c-02 milestone=M1 labels=reflex,conformance
gates=GATE-REFLEX-03,GATE-REFLEX-05 seed=1 plants=silent-fallback,identity-no-iters,nudged-cert`): for each M1
archetype (`quad-mammal`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`) `frob design new n22c02-<archetype> --template <archetype> --key
<k> --json` and `frob control synth n22c02-<archetype> --tier referee --seed 1 --key <k> --json` (a distinct key per
call), reading the layer fractions and the fallback log from the report; then the cases in the Gauntlet form,
`e2e_tier g0_laws reflex::invariants --mutations` (INV-REFLEX-01, INV-REFLEX-02, PO-REFLEX-03) and `e2e_tier
g1_known_answers reflex::invariants --mutations` (PO-REFLEX-01), so that every planted violation is caught in the
same run as its case; then `frob-verify` on each
`.frobcert` file the PO-REFLEX-03 case wrote, and on each nudged copy (expected rejection). Logged per the shared
standard: commands, exit codes, evidence block, receipt hashes of the tier runs, `layer_fraction_sum` (must equal
1), `fallback_runs` and `fallback_log_entries` (must be equal), `fallback_entries_without_reason` (threshold 0),
`null_space_dim` (must equal 4), `certificates_accepted` and `certificates_rejected_after_nudge` (both must equal
the count emitted), with `lp-farkas` (offline) and `conic-farkas` (in process) counted on separate lines, and a
summary naming GATE-REFLEX-03 and GATE-REFLEX-05. The script records no gate value: GATE-REFLEX-03's record is
N22a-05's and GATE-REFLEX-05's is BD-251's, whose tier runs include these cases.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-553 (null space of dimension four; control: the conventional filter gains a spurious yaw direction). KA-556
(y = (0.5, −1), yᵀb = −5 N; control: a nudged certificate fails). CX-235. Metamorphic relation: translating or
yawing the whole world leaves packets and innovations unchanged to 10⁻⁹ relative. Determinism lanes: identities and digests equal twice on
one machine and between debug and release on the referee.

## Considerations

- The two singular-value thresholds of the rank test (10⁻¹⁰ and 10⁻⁶ of the largest) are DESIGN defaults shared with
  BD-114; if the filter's scaling makes the split narrower on some trajectories, the case reports the gap distribution and the threshold is revisited with the filter's owner,
  never loosened silently.
- Certificates must be rounded to rationals by the emitter in a way that keeps them inside the dual cone (round
  toward the interior); a certificate that only checks in floating point is not a certificate.
- The auditor checker for conic certificates (`conic-farkas`) is owned by the proofs subsystem and arrives at M4
  (N12-16); until it exists the case runs the same exact check in the test binary and reports `Certified
  (in-process; offline kind pending N12-16)`, never as audited offline; the M4 exit re-runs `frob-verify` on the
  stored bundles once the kind is admitted (BD-251's rule).
