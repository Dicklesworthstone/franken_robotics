## Background

Reflex states seven invariants and nine proof obligations. Part I of the conformance battery (N22c-02) covers the
four whose mechanisms exist at M1. This bead covers the other twelve, whose mechanisms land at M3 and M4: the
sampling controller, symmetric controllers, gait orbits, the residual wrapper, the energy budget and the runtime
shields. Each property gets one registered conformance case, run against the assembled system, and one planted
violation the case must catch. The mechanism beads test their own code; this battery makes sure that a property
is not lost when a mechanism is split, replaced by its kill-gate fallback, or refactored.

Terms used below. A **shield** is a runtime filter that replaces a proposed command by the nearest one that
keeps a set of barrier rows satisfied inside the actuator box. A **gait orbit** is a fixed point of the map that
advances the closed loop by one stride; its **monodromy** is that map's Jacobian and its eigenvalues are the
Floquet multipliers. The **claim product** is the typed record every result carries: kind of claim, evidence
class (`Proved`, `Certified`, `Tested`, `Estimated`), tier, determinism class, identities and seed. Determinism
class D2 means the same bits run to run and across worker counts on one device family.

## Scope

In: the statements in the owning crates' `CONTRACT.md`; twelve cases with their planted violations. Out: the
mechanisms; the gate measurements themselves, which belong to the mechanism beads (this battery reuses their
fixtures and feeds their rows).

**Kill-gate interaction.** If a mechanism fell back at its kill gate, its properties are tested on the fallback
or retired with a note: after KILL-MPC the sampling controller is a single-world CPU debugging aid and
INV-REFLEX-03 still holds; after KILL-SHIELD nothing is called a shield, INV-SHIELD-01 and PO-SHIELD-02, -03 are
retired, and PO-SHIELD-01, -04 remain (the energy tier and the backups stay); after KILL-GAIT orbits remain as
diagnostics and INV-GAIT-01 and PO-GAIT-01 still hold.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-control`, `frob-gait` and `frob-shield` list their invariants with these statements.
- [ ] All twelve cases pass, with the counts above (10⁶ projections with zero box violations; a relative difference below 10⁻⁸ on the monodromy; 72 of 80 and 20 of 20 on the gradient suite; 10⁴ episodes of integer-exact agreement).
- [ ] Each planted violation fails its case; the recorded failing run is kept as the control's evidence.
- [ ] Compile-fail tests exist for INV-SHIELD-02 and PO-SHIELD-03.
- [ ] A property whose mechanism fell back at a kill gate is recorded as retired with the kill decision cited, never silently skipped.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-conformance reflex::invariants` with one test per row, named after the identifier in lower
case (`inv_reflex_03_…` through `po_shield_04_…`). Hand values asserted: the projection example u = (1, 1/2) at
λ = 1/2; the slider (−2 m/s, +1.5 J); the trivial case of the twisted monodromy with m = 1 (twisted equals
direct exactly). Compile-fail cases under `tests/compile_fail/`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/reflex_invariants_m4.sh` drives `frob eval qualify`, `frob eval promote`, `frob control symmetry`,
`frob gait orbit`, `frob gait gradient`, `frob shield check` and `frob-verify` on the registered fixtures. Logged
per the shared standard for each property: the command, exit code, evidence block, the measured count or
difference against its threshold (`box_violations` = 0, `monodromy_rel_diff` < 10⁻⁸, `gradient_cos_ok` ≥ 72,
`controls_refused` = 20, `admit_bit_mismatches` = 0, `ledger_increases` = 0), and for each planted violation the
first differing value; the summary names the gates touched.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-557 (zero residual), KA-562 (transverse instability, for INV-GAIT-01's full spectrum), KA-564 (gradient
suite), KA-568 and KA-570 (energy card and randomized sequences), KA-571 (projection), KA-572 (damping backup).
Counterexamples CX-237, CX-239, CX-240, CX-245. Determinism lanes: PO-REFLEX-02 and PO-SHIELD-01 are themselves
determinism tests (across worker counts; across backends for the integer predicate); the rest run twice on one
machine with equal results.

## Considerations

- PO-SHIELD-04's continuous-time part is described as a theorem card, but no numbered card carries it; the proofs
  subsystem must register one (a polynomial identity over the entries of M̄, K_d and p). Until then part (a) is a
  host test in exact rationals and is recorded as `Tested`.
- The 10⁶-instance and 10⁴-episode counts are the obligation's own numbers; a smaller smoke subset (10⁴ and 10²)
  runs on every commit, the full counts before the gate is recorded.
- INV-REFLEX-03 was written before the delivered-controller lane existed; the case encodes the amended rule so
  that the lane cannot be used as a general exemption.
