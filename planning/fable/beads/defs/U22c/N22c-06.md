## Background

Baseline synthesis has four Frontier routes. N22c-05 pilots two of them. This bead pilots the other two:

- **Hybrid.** "Roll where the route graph allows, step where it does not; an explicit drive, stand and step mode
  graph with readiness guards." The reference body is the `wheel-leg` archetype: a quadruped with driven wheels
  as feet (16 actuated coordinates), which has two contact laws on one end-effector class and switches between
  rolling and stepping.
- **Arm on a base.** "Operational-space control with the base as a floating frame." The reference body is the
  `mobile-manipulator` archetype: a seven-joint arm on a wheeled base.

The pilot row is the same for all four routes: each controller on one reference robot of its class on the
referee, on that class's first task; falsifier: the class fails its row of GATE-REFLEX-01 (stand 10 s, traverse
5 m of flat ground) after one repair cycle; ceiling: one more morphology class moves without training, no speed
claim; cost: 2 engineer-days per class. A falsified route is not funded: the class reports `not-synthesizable`
and routes to the sampling controller or to training.

Hybrid archetypes close GATE-REFLEX-01 at M4, with their contact laws; the pilot runs at M3 once those laws
exist on the referee, so that the M4 work is funded or not on evidence.

## Scope

In: a minimal mode-graph controller and a minimal operational-space controller; the registered predictions; the
gate-row measurements and one class-specific measurement each; one repair cycle; the recorded outcomes. Out:
transitions selected by predicted energy, slip and clearance (the hybrid route's next experiment);
payload-dependent whole-body planning and impedance (the arm route's next experiment); integration for every
archetype (BD-247).

## Technical approach

1. **Register first.** One record per class: mechanism, tier [F], frozen protocol, falsifier, ceiling, fallback,
   cost, tool pin (the referee commit), seed.
2. **Hybrid controller, minimal form.** Three modes: `drive` (wheels rolling, legs holding a posture under joint
   PD; body twist to wheel speeds through the kinematic connection), `stand` (wheels braked or held, the legged
   stance program regulating the body) and `step` (one leg in swing with its wheel unloaded, the others in
   `stand`). Edges carry **readiness guards**: `drive → stand` when body speed is below a threshold and all wheels
   are in contact; `stand → step` when the static support margin without the swing leg is positive and the swing
   wheel's load is below a threshold; `step → stand` at touchdown with load above a threshold; `stand → drive`
   when all four are loaded and the route graph marks the segment rollable. A guard that is not satisfied holds
   the mode; a mode held beyond a timeout is a `fallback:mode-timeout` event.
3. **Arm controller, minimal form.** Task-space acceleration command at the tool from a pose error on the group,
   e = log(T_d⁻¹·T); joint torques from the whole-body program with the base as a floating frame (unknowns: joint
   accelerations, wheel contact wrenches, torques; constraints: equations of motion, contact cones, actuator
   boxes, joint limits; objective: tool task first, base tip margin second, posture third, each with slack). The
   base holds position under its wheeled baseline.
4. **Tasks.** The gate row for both: stand (hold) 10 s and traverse 5 m of flat ground (the arm rides stowed).
   Class-specific first task (DESIGN reading of "that class's first task"): hybrid, cross a 20 mm sill by one
   `drive → stand → step → stand → drive` cycle per axle; arm, reach a shelf point 0.9 m above the floor and hold
   within 10 mm for 2 s while the base tip margin stays positive.
5. **Evidence required first** (from the class table): hybrid, deployment under load, and impulse and completion
   time across a threshold; arm, grasp retention (not exercised in the pilot), base tip margin, task-space error.
6. **Measure.** `stand_s`, `traverse_m`; hybrid: mode dwell times, guard-hold events, peak impulse at each
   touchdown, sill completion time; arm: tool error (mm), minimum base tip margin (N·m), solver end-state counts;
   both: layer fractions and contact count against the referee's cap of 21 (the wheel-leg row is marginal on
   stair transitions and fits when rolling).
7. **Repair cycle and record** as in N22c-05: one logged repair; a second failure is the falsifier; the outcome
   goes to the register and the review log.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two pilot records written before their runs.
- [ ] Gate-row measurements recorded for both robots, with layer fractions and contact counts.
- [ ] Hybrid: at least the mode sequence and guard events of one sill crossing are recorded; arm: tool error and minimum tip margin are recorded.
- [ ] Each class has a verdict, `falsified` or `not falsified`, handed to BD-247 (funding) and to N22c-48 (the M4 gate rows for hybrid archetypes).
- [ ] A referee capacity refusal (more than 21 contacts) is recorded as such and is not counted as a controller failure.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `pilot_mode_graph_guards`: from each mode only the stated edges exist; with a guard false the mode holds; a held mode past the timeout emits `fallback:mode-timeout`.
- `pilot_mode_graph_no_step_without_support`: with the support margin negative the `stand → step` edge never fires.
- `pilot_osc_program_end_states`: a reachable tool target gives `optimal`; a target that needs torque beyond the box gives `infeasible` with a certificate that names the binding actuator.
- `pilot_osc_error_on_group`: a rotation error just below π gives a finite command; a derivative at the cut is refused, not sign-flipped.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/pilot_frontier_baselines_2.sh`: registers, then runs `frob sim run --tier referee` for each robot
and task. Logged per the shared standard: commands, exit codes, evidence block, `stand_s` (10), `traverse_m` (5),
`sill_completion_s`, `touchdown_impulse_max_ns`, `tool_error_mm` (10), `tip_margin_min_nm` (> 0),
`contacts_max` (21), `guard_hold_events`, the verdicts, and a summary.

### Known answers and negative controls
Gate thresholds 10 s and 5 m (TARGET). Negative controls: the hybrid controller with its support-margin guard
removed must tip or stall on the sill; the arm controller with the tip-margin task removed must lose margin on
the reach. Both must be visible in the measurements. Determinism: equal referee digests for equal seeds.

## Considerations

- The class-specific tasks are a DESIGN reading; if the task families for moving surfaces, curb climbing and
  reach-and-carry (which land at M4) define a different first task, the pilot's gate-row verdict is unaffected.
- Tracked bases are Solid, not Frontier, and are not part of this pilot; their gate rows also close at M4.
- If the wheel-leg contact count exceeds the referee's cap in the step mode, the `strict` profile of the batched
  CPU tier is the adjudicator, and the record says which tier produced each number.
