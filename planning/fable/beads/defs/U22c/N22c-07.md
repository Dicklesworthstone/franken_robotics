## Background

Reflex gives every design an inspectable, hand-specified baseline controller. The Frontier rung above it adds a
small learned network on top: the command is u = limit(u_base(x̂, t) + Δ(o)), where u_base is the baseline, Δ is
the learned **residual** trained by the learning subsystem, and `limit` is the actuator limiter. The residual has
bounded authority, abs(Δⱼ) ≤ ρⱼ per channel (default ρ = 0.3 of the channel's range), and its output layer starts
at zero so that training starts from the baseline's behaviour. The hoped-for benefit is fewer training steps per
design than a specialist policy trained end to end: the rung's cost target is at most 20% of a specialist's
training steps.

The benefit is a hypothesis. Its pilot, from the chapter's pilot table:

| Pilot | Falsifier | Ceiling of the benefit | Cost (DESIGN estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| One quadruped with a hand-written trot controller on an incumbent simulator: residual against end-to-end at equal steps, three seeds | The residual needs more than 50% of the specialist's steps for the same return | Training steps per design fall by at most the inverse of the residual's step fraction, for classes that have a baseline | About 10 GPU-hours |

The pilot runs on an incumbent simulator, outside the workspace, so that it can run before the project's own
training stack carries the residual wrapper. Its falsifier is one of the two triggers of kill gate
KILL-RESIDUAL (the other is the same 50% threshold measured on the design corpus at M4), whose fallback removes
the residual rung and keeps the baseline as a behaviour-cloning warm start.

## Scope

In: the frozen protocol, a hand-written trot controller on the incumbent, two training arms with three seeds
each, the step-fraction measurement, the recorded outcome. Out: the residual wrapper in `frob-control` and the
ladder verb (BD-330); the corpus measurement at M4; the choice of ρ (N22c-52).

## Technical approach

1. **Register first.** Mechanism "baseline plus bounded residual", tier [F]; protocol as below; falsifier
   (median step fraction above 0.5); ceiling (steps per design fall by at most 1/f, where f is the measured step
   fraction, and only for classes that have a baseline; nothing is gained on wall-clock phases the residual does
   not touch); fallback (KILL-RESIDUAL); cost; incumbent name and pinned version; seeds 1, 2, 3.
2. **Task.** Flat-ground velocity tracking for one quadruped reference robot (commanded planar velocity and yaw
   rate), the incumbent's standard reward for that task, 20 s episodes at 50 Hz control.
3. **Baseline.** A hand-written trot: a clock-driven diagonal-pair gait, a capture-point footstep heuristic,
   stance forces from a single-step force distribution over friction cones, swing by Cartesian PD, joint PD.
   Its unassisted return on the task is recorded (R_base).
4. **Arm A, specialist.** PPO end to end with the incumbent's published hyperparameters. Record return against
   environment steps; let R* be the mean return over the last 5% of training and N_A the first step count at
   which a running mean reaches 0.95·R*.
5. **Arm B, residual.** The same network size and hyperparameters; action = limit(u_base + Δ) with abs(Δⱼ) ≤ 0.3
   of range, output layer initialized to zero. Record N_B, the first step count at which the running mean
   reaches the same 0.95·R* (the specialist's level, not its own).
6. **Equal steps.** Both arms get the same step budget, the specialist's published budget. If arm B never reaches
   0.95·R* inside the budget, N_B is recorded as "greater than budget" and the falsifier holds.
7. **Measure.** The step fraction f = N_B / N_A per seed and its median over three seeds; the fraction of ticks on
   which any residual channel sits at its bound (the `residual-saturated` indicator: when a channel is pinned at
   its bound for more than a declared fraction of ticks the baseline is no longer the controller); final returns
   of both arms; wall-clock per arm for context only.
8. **Falsifier.** Median f above 0.5.
9. **Record.** Result, including a negative one, to the pilot register and the review log; if not falsified, the
   measured f replaces the 20% cost target as the working estimate for the rung.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The record is registered before the first training run.
- [ ] Six learning curves (two arms, three seeds) are stored with their seeds, the incumbent's version and the hardware.
- [ ] f is reported per seed and as a median, with N_A, N_B, R*, R_base.
- [ ] The saturation fraction is reported; a run flagged `residual-saturated` is reported as such, not as a residual success.
- [ ] The verdict is handed to N22c-15 (KILL-RESIDUAL) and to BD-330.
- [ ] The zero-initialized residual reproduces the baseline's return at step zero (within the incumbent's run-to-run spread), which shows the wrapper is wired as stated.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
For the pilot's analysis script (`tools/pilots/residual/`): `step_fraction_from_curves` (synthetic curves with
known crossing steps 2·10⁶ and 5·10⁶ give f = 0.4); `never_reaches_is_falsifier` (a curve that stays below the
level gives "greater than budget"); `saturation_fraction` (a synthetic action log pinned 30% of ticks gives 0.30).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/pilot_residual_vs_end_to_end.sh`: launches both arms on the incumbent with explicit seeds, then the
analysis. Logged per the shared standard: commands, exit codes, the incumbent's version and device, per-seed
`n_a_steps`, `n_b_steps`, `step_fraction` (threshold 0.5), `saturation_fraction`, `r_star`, `r_base`, the median
and the verdict. Training curves themselves are artifacts referenced by path; they are not evidence about
FrankenRobotics, and the log labels them `external-tool`.

### Known answers and negative controls
Negative control for the pilot's own sensitivity: arm B with the baseline replaced by zeros (so the "residual"
is an end-to-end policy with clipped authority) must give f near or above 1 or fail to reach the level; if it
shows f well below 1 the measurement is broken. Zero-residual check: return at initialization equals R_base.
Determinism: the incumbent's own determinism class applies and is recorded; three seeds give the spread.

## Considerations

- A result on one quadruped and one task does not transfer to other classes; the M4 corpus measurement is the
  second trigger for that reason.
- The ceiling is on steps, not on design-cycle time: if training is a small share of the cycle the benefit is
  smaller still. The phase ledger decides that, not this pilot.
- Authority ρ = 0.3 is the default pending its open question; if the pilot shows saturation, a second registered
  run at another ρ is a new pilot, not a retune of this one.
