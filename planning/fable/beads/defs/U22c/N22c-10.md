## Background

Certifying a contact sequence is hard. Bounding the energy that can reach a boundary is not. Passive contact,
with any friction coefficient μ ≥ 0, any restitution between 0 and 1 and any sequence of impacts, can dissipate or
redistribute mechanical energy and cannot create it. So if everything that could deliver energy to a boundary (a
stop, a bumper, a door at the end of its travel) is bounded, and the absorber at that boundary can take that
much, the stop holds for every contact sequence, and no contact model appears in the argument. This is the
**energy-budget safety tier** (bet BET-25). For a declared boundary and time t:

    E_deliv(t) = T_ub(t) + E_drive(t) + E_ext + E_stored(t) + E_reserve  ≤  W_abs

T_ub is an upper bound on the kinetic energy of every body on the load path (measured speeds inflated by their
sensing error, masses at their caps, reflected rotor inertia included); E_drive is what the drives can still
inject before the cutoff acts, at most P_max·Δt_cut; E_ext bounds work by sources that are not cut (gravity over
the stroke); E_stored bounds releasable stored energy (springs); E_reserve is a numerical and sensing reserve;
W_abs is the lower envelope of the work the absorber takes before its limit. The longest admissible cutoff delay
is Δt_cut,max = (W_abs − T_ub − E_ext − E_stored − E_reserve) / P_max.

## Scope

In: the worked card by exact arithmetic; a one-degree-of-freedom cart and absorber on the referee; a sweep of
friction, restitution and contact law; measurement of the energy absorbed at the boundary against the budget;
the verdict. Out: the `.fenergy` format, the port-graph walk, the exact-integer monitor kernel and the verb
(BD-337); bench data for absorbers (N22c-55).

## Technical approach

1. **Register first.** Mechanism "energy-budget tier", tier [F]; protocol; falsifier (any exceedance under the
   premises); ceiling (no speed benefit); fallback (kill gate KILL-ENERGY: the claim is withdrawn and the monitor
   remains as an alarm of class `Estimated`); cost; the referee commit; seeds.
2. **The worked card, exact.** A cart of 8 kg effective mass approaches a stop at 0.4 m/s: T_ub = ½·8·0.4² =
   0.64 J. The drive can inject 20 W during a 25 ms cutoff delay: 0.50 J. The floor slopes by at most 8% over a
   40 mm absorber stroke: 8·9.81·0.040·0.08 = 0.25 J (0.251136 before rounding). A series spring holds 0.10 J.
   Total 1.49 J against an absorber rated at 50 N over 40 mm, W_abs = 2.00 J. Longest cutoff delay (2.00 − 0.64 −
   0.25 − 0.10)/20 = 50.5 ms, or 45.5 ms with a further 0.10 J of reserve. Computed in decimal rationals.
3. **The cart on the referee.** One prismatic degree of freedom along x; a cart body whose effective mass is 8 kg
   including a reflected rotor; a drive that pushes at up to 20 W until a cutoff command takes effect 25 ms after
   detection; a slope of 8%; a series spring preloaded to 0.10 J; an absorber modelled as a spring-damper or a
   constant-force crush element that bottoms at 40 mm and 2.00 J; a guide that puts the absorber on the only
   forward path (premise 4). The cart reaches the absorber through an intermediate contact (a bumper plate that
   can slide on the floor) so that friction and restitution act somewhere on the load path.
4. **Sweep.** 10³ runs for the pilot (the gate later uses 10⁴): μ uniform in [0, 5]; restitution uniform in
   [0, 1]; each contact law available on the referee; approach speed in [0, 0.4] m/s; cutoff delay in [0, 25] ms;
   slope in [0, 8%]; spring preload in [0, 0.10] J.
5. **Measure.** The work done on the absorber, W_meas, from the referee's energy ledger (the absorber's stored
   plus dissipated energy at maximum stroke), and the budget's left side evaluated on the same run's inputs.
   An **exceedance** is W_meas above the budget's left side for that run by more than the ledger's residual bound,
   or absorber stroke beyond 40 mm while the budget admitted the run.
6. **Controls that must show exceedances** (they prove the measurement can see one): a budget that counts only
   the 8 kg payload when the cart's effective mass includes a rotor of inertia 5 × 10⁻⁵ kg·m² behind a 50:1
   reduction on a wheel of radius 0.05 m (effective mass 50²·(5 × 10⁻⁵)/0.05² = 50 kg); a budget that omits the
   spring; a cutoff delay of 60 ms against a budget written for 25 ms.
7. **Falsifier.** Any exceedance in the 10³ runs with the premises satisfied.
8. **Record.** Verdict, the largest ratio W_meas / budget, the count of runs, and the controls' exceedances.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The record exists before the runs.
- [ ] The card's figures reproduce exactly: 0.64, 0.50, 0.25, 0.10, 1.49, 2.00 J; 50.5 ms and 45.5 ms.
- [ ] 10³ swept runs are stored with seeds; the count of exceedances is reported (the pilot is not falsified only at zero).
- [ ] Each of the three controls shows at least one exceedance.
- [ ] The largest ratio of measured to budgeted energy is reported, with the ledger's residual bound.
- [ ] The verdict is handed to BD-337 (funding) and N22c-18 (KILL-ENERGY).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`card_arithmetic_exact` (the eight figures in rationals; (2.00 − 0.99)/20 = 0.0505 s);
`reflected_mass_50kg`; `exceedance_predicate` (W_meas = 1.50 J against 1.49 J with residual bound 0.005 J is an
exceedance; 1.492 J is not); `sweep_ranges` (every sampled parameter inside its interval).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/pilot_energy_budget.sh`: writes the register row, runs `frob sim run --tier referee` over the sweep
with explicit seeds, queries the energy ledger (`frob telemetry energy`), and evaluates the predicate. Logged per
the shared standard: commands, exit codes, evidence block, per-run `w_meas_j`, `budget_j`, `ratio`, `stroke_mm`,
the totals `exceedances` (threshold 0) and `control_exceedances` (each at least 1), `ledger_residual_j`, the
verdict and a summary.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-568 (the card) and KA-569 (50 kg reflected mass) by value. Negative controls: the three controls of step 6
(CX-239 for the payload-only budget). Metamorphic relation: raising μ or lowering restitution never raises
W_meas beyond the budget. Determinism: equal seeds give equal referee digests.

## Considerations

- The argument needs passive contact. A contact law that can inject energy numerically would show as an
  exceedance; that is a defect of the simulator found by this test (the simulator is used here as a negative
  control on the budget, never as its justification).
- The card prints the gravity term as 0.25 J although 8·9.81·0.040·0.08 = 0.251136 J: the printed figure is
  rounded down, which a bound may not be. The card is reproduced "to the printed digits" as illustrative
  arithmetic; the swept runs evaluate the budget with outward rounding (0.2512 J at four digits), under which
  the total is 1.4912 J and the longest cutoff delay 50.44 ms. Both sets of figures are logged.
- The absorber envelope is an `assumed` card value in this pilot, so the claim is at most `Estimated` in the
  model, and `no-claim` about reality.
- The budget answers an energy question. It supports no statement about peak force, pressure, quasi-static
  clamping after the stop, contacts that bypass the absorber, or regulatory conformity.
