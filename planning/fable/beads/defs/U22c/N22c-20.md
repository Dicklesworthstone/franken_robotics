## Background

A kill gate is a decision named in advance with its trigger, measurement and fallback; a kill gate that fires is
not a failure, because the fallback is the plan. Its decision is recorded as an ADR.

KILL-SKILL governs the Moonshot layer of skill artifacts. A skill (`.fskill`) states a behaviour at the task
boundary. Its exact layer (Solid) maps actuator effort to the task-space quantity for a body and a contact mode
and returns a feasible-allocation witness or an exact infeasibility witness. The learned layer adds a frozen,
morphology-conditioned residual in task space above the allocation program, with dimensionless inputs, and the
verb `skill.transport` moves a skill artifact to another body with it. (`mission.transport` is a different verb:
it moves a certified claim through an interface map with a bounded error; neither stands in for the other.)

## Scope

In: applying the trigger, recording the ADR, stating the downstream changes. Out: the pilot (N22c-12); the
measurement of GATE-REFLEX-09 and the epic's plan (seed bead BD-436).

## Technical approach

**Trigger.** GATE-REFLEX-09 fails. The gate (claim: Design comparison; TARGET; Moonshot): learned residual
transport reaches at least 18 of 20 feasible unseen bodies within 3 percentage points of tuned baselines with no
adaptation, with both cost denominators reported (the incremental cost per target, and the amortized cost with
pretraining).

**Milestone.** M8. The pilot's falsifier (fewer than 18 of 20 feasible bodies within 3 points, or one infeasible
body not refused) closes the question earlier: a falsified pilot means the epic is not funded and the decision
is recorded at once.

**Measurement that decides it.** The pilot's per-target table (N22c-12) and, if the epic was funded, the gate's
own run on unseen bodies: the count within 3 points; the refusals of infeasible bodies; c_t (cost per target of
the transport), c_b (cost per target of the fastest baseline reaching the same success), the pretraining cost P
and the break-even count P/(c_b − c_t).

**Decision rule.**
1. Pilot falsified → **fires**; the epic's learned layer is not funded.
2. Pilot not falsified, gate reaches 18 of 20 with both denominators reported → `not fired`.
3. Pilot not falsified, gate below 18 of 20, or a denominator missing → **fires**.
4. An infeasible body that the exact layer failed to refuse is a defect of the Solid layer and is fixed whatever
   the decision.

**Fallback, named in advance.** Keep the exact layer and its witnesses; drop the learned residual.

**What changes downstream when it fires.**
- `skill.transport` is withdrawn from the verb registry (a call returns the capability-not-implemented refusal,
  exit 5, citing the ADR); the `residual` field of every `.fskill` stays `null`.
- `skill.compile`, `skill.check` and `skill.show` are unaffected: per-mode maps, allocation, both kinds of
  witness and the refinement check remain.
- Transfer of a skill to a new body is then done by recompiling the exact layer for that body and, where a
  controller above it is learned, by training on the target.
- The epic records "learned layer not built"; the claim-transport half of the epic has its own kill gate.

**Record.** ADR with the measured table, costs, commit, hardware fingerprint, seeds, decision, fallback and
affected beads; rows of KILL-SKILL and GATE-REFLEX-09 in `registries/gates.toml` updated with the same receipt.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR cites the pilot's numbers and, if run, the gate's.
- [ ] `registries/gates.toml` carries the decision and the gate status with measured values.
- [ ] If fired: a test shows `frob skill transport …` returning exit 5 with the ADR cited, and `frob skill check` still returning witnesses.
- [ ] If not fired: both cost denominators and the break-even count are in the record.
- [ ] The decision is made by a session that did not run the measurement.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `kill_skill_rule_table`: pilot falsified → fired; pilot passed and gate 19 of 20 → not fired; pilot passed and gate 17 of 20 → fired; gate 19 of 20 with one denominator missing → fired.
- `transport_withdrawn_after_kill`: the verb returns exit 5.
- `exact_layer_untouched_after_kill`: the two-link arm fixture still returns support 25/3 N from joint 2.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_skill_record.sh`: reads the pilot's and the gate's logs and the registry, asserts agreement,
applies the rule, exercises the fallback when fired. Logged per the shared standard: `within_3pt_count`
(threshold 18 of 20), `infeasible_refused`, `c_t_gpu_s`, `c_b_gpu_s`, `pretrain_gpu_s`, `break_even_targets`,
the decision, the ADR path.

### Known answers and negative controls
The exact layer's fixture (KA-565). Negative control: a record with targets in place of measured values is
rejected by the registry check.
