## Background

A **lifted linear predictor** is a finite matrix acting on a dictionary of functions of the state: lift the
state x to z = ψ(x), advance z by a linear (here bilinear in the input) map, read the state back. As a global
model for control through contact it is **not built**: a contact-rich hybrid flow has no finite-dimensional
invariant subspace of such functions, and size alone settles it (a quadratic dictionary on 60 states has
1 + 60 + 1,830 = 1,891 functions, so one dense map has 1,891² ≈ 3.58 × 10⁶ coefficients before any mode jump is
represented).

One bounded experiment is kept, as a Moonshot off the critical path. It covers a single regime with no
contact-mode change, a tracked or wheeled base driving on soft soil, where the dynamics are smooth and a rollout
of the compiled model is comparatively expensive (soft soil uses a pressure-sinkage law p = (k_c/b + k_φ)·zⁿ and
a shear law τ = (c + σ·tan φ)·(1 − exp(−j/K))). There a lifted model is tried as the **planning model** of the
sampling-based predictive controller and judged at equal tracking accuracy.

A Moonshot item is not funded until its pilot has run. The pilot named for this item: on `batch-cpu` only, fit
and calibrate on the tracked skid-steer base over one soil card, and report band width against horizon.
**Falsifier**: the simultaneous band at a 0.9 s horizon is wider than the task's tracking tolerance (DESIGN
default: 5% of commanded speed and 0.05 m of path offset). Equal accuracy is then unreachable and the experiment
stops. **Ceiling**, stated first: the lifted model can remove at most the planner's rollouts, N_s·H·N_it model
steps per tick (4 × 10⁴ to 2 × 10⁵ in the published configurations) against one plant step; on a design cycle
the ceiling is the share of wall time that the screening rung spends on soft-soil tasks, which is unmeasured and
expected to be small. Nothing in M0 to M7 depends on this item.

## Scope

In: data generation on `batch-cpu` in `f64`; a ridge fit of the bilinear lifted model; a split-conformal
simultaneous band per horizon on held-out trajectories; the band-width curve; the mode-change count; the
verdict. Out: the verb `control.lift` and the production fit (N22c-23); the planner integration (N22c-24); the
closed-loop comparison (N22c-25).

## Technical approach

1. **Register first.** Mechanism "lifted linear planning model, one soft-soil regime", tier [M]; the protocol;
   the falsifier with its two numbers; the ceiling; the fallback (`mpc-sample` on the compiled model; no lifted
   model for any other regime); cost (DESIGN estimate: 2 engineer-days, CPU only); seeds.
2. **Body and regime.** The tracked skid-steer base (12 velocity coordinates, two belts with patch samples). One
   soil card with its declared ranges of pressure, slip and sinkage. Regime box: forward speed in [0.2, 1.0] m/s,
   yaw rate in [−0.5, 0.5] rad/s, slope in [−0.05, 0.05], and every track sample in contact (DESIGN values for
   the pilot; recorded).
3. **Reduced state and inputs.** x ∈ ℝ⁷: forward speed, yaw rate, the slip of each track, the sinkage of each
   track, pitch. u = (u_L, u_R): the two track torque commands. Sample period: the control period, 20 ms.
4. **Data.** 2,000 trajectories of 2 s on `batch-cpu` in `f64` under piecewise-constant random commands that keep
   the state inside the box; 1,000 for fitting, 1,000 held out for calibration and test (split by world address).
   A trajectory that leaves the box or changes contact mode is counted and excluded from the fit.
5. **Model.** Dictionary ψ(x): every monomial of total degree at most 2 in seven variables, C(9, 2) = 36
   functions. Predictor z⁺ = A·z + u_L·B_L·z + u_R·B_R·z with z = ψ(x); the state is read back from the linear
   monomials and lifted again at every step. Fit: with Φ_k = (z_k, u_L,k·z_k, u_R,k·z_k) ∈ ℝ¹⁰⁸ and target
   ψ(x_{k+1}), W = (Σ ψ(x_{k+1})·Φ_kᵀ)·(Σ Φ_k·Φ_kᵀ + λ·I)⁻¹, W = [A B_L B_R], ridge λ chosen on a validation
   split of the fitting set. 3 × 36 × 36 = 3,888 coefficients.
6. **Band.** For each held-out trajectory and horizon h (1 to 45 steps, 0.02 s to 0.9 s), roll the predictor
   open-loop from the measured state under the recorded inputs; the score of a trajectory is its largest
   normalized error over the horizon (speed error over commanded speed; path offset in metres). The
   simultaneous 95% band at horizon h is the ⌈(n + 1)·0.95⌉-th smallest score over the n calibration
   trajectories, with the simultaneous adjustment over horizons taken from `fs-surrogate` through the airlock.
7. **Measure.** Band width against horizon (two curves: speed fraction, path offset); the share of trajectories
   containing a contact-mode change inside the declared regime; fit and calibration time.
8. **Falsifier.** At h = 0.9 s the band exceeds 5% of commanded speed or 0.05 m of path offset.
9. **Record.** Verdict and curves to the register and review log.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The record exists before data generation.
- [ ] The band-width curves are stored for h = 0.02 s to 0.9 s with the calibration count n.
- [ ] The band at 0.9 s is reported against 5% of commanded speed and 0.05 m.
- [ ] The share of trajectories with a contact-mode change inside the regime is reported against the kill gate's 1%.
- [ ] The verdict is handed to N22c-23 (funding) and N22c-26 (KILL-SOILLIFT).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `dictionary_size`: seven variables, degree at most 2, gives 36 monomials; 60 variables give 1,891.
- `linear_recovery`: data from x⁺ = 0.9·x with the dictionary {1, x} returns the map diag(1, 0.9) and the eigenvalue 0.9 to 10⁻⁹.
- `abs_value_control`: reset map x⁺ = abs(x), dictionary {1, x, x²}, samples {−1, 0, 1}: the fit is x⁺ = x² with zero training residual and predicts 0.25 at x = 0.5 where the truth is 0.5.
- `conformal_index`: n = 1,000 and level 0.95 give index ⌈1001·0.95⌉ = 951.
- `split_by_world_address`: no world address appears in both the fitting and the calibration sets.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/pilot_soil_lift.sh`: `frob sim run --tier batch-cpu` for the data, then the fit and calibration
tool. Logged per the shared standard: commands, exit codes, evidence block, `band_speed_frac@0.9s` (threshold
0.05), `band_offset_m@0.9s` (threshold 0.05), `mode_change_share` (kill threshold 0.01), `n_fit`, `n_cal`,
`ridge_lambda`, the verdict and a summary.

### Known answers and negative controls
Linear recovery (0.9 to 10⁻⁹) and the absolute-value control (0.25 against 0.5). Negative control for the band:
calibrating on the fitting set itself must give a narrower band than the held-out one, and the pilot must
refuse to report it. Determinism: data generation is `batch-cpu` `f64` under a replay profile; the fit repeats
bit for bit on one machine.

## Considerations

- A zero residual on the fitting set is no evidence; only the held-out band is, and only inside one mode. The
  absolute-value control exists to keep that rule in view.
- The band holds only inside the calibration distribution (FACT about `fs-surrogate`: it states that the band is
  its only accuracy statement and holds only inside the calibration distribution).
- If the soil kill gate has fired upstream (no soil law on batched tiers), this pilot cannot run and the item is
  closed as not funded.
