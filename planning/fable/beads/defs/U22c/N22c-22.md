## Background

The lifted linear planning model for one soft-soil regime needs a predictor with an input term:

    z⁺ = A·z + u_L·B_L·z + u_R·B_R·z,   z = ψ(x)

FACT: the simulator sibling's surrogate crate provides exact DMD, EDMD over a polynomial dictionary with ridge
regularization, and split-conformal forecast bands per horizon with an optional simultaneous adjustment
(`frankensim/crates/fs-surrogate/src/koopman.rs`); its EDMD is **autonomous** and has no input term. The
input-affine fit is therefore written first in this project (`frob-control`, bead N22c-23), and adding it to
`fs-surrogate` is an upstream work item of this program: FD-FSIM-39 in the register `registries/upstream.toml`
(row written by NFx-01).

The project's rule for siblings (REV-1): sibling repositories belong to the project owner, and a limitation in a
sibling is work this program does in that sibling's repository, under its own `AGENTS.md`, gates and
conventions, as an upstream work item: a bead here with the label `upstream`, mirrored as a bead in the sibling's
tracker. The change is additive (a new entry beside the autonomous fit; existing behaviour unchanged). Every upstream item carries an **interim posture** and a **switch-over
rule**, so that no milestone waits on a sibling change landing.

## Scope

In: making the upstream change in FrankenSim's repository under its rules, through the mirrored bead; the
interim posture; the switch-over rule; the differential test that licenses the switch. Out: the local fit
(N22c-23), which is the interim posture; the register row and its number (NFx-01).

## Technical approach

**Repository and rules.** `frankensim`, crate `fs-surrogate`, under FrankenSim's own `AGENTS.md`, test tiers and
conventions (including its layer checks and its ban on branches and worktrees). This program makes the change
there, tracked by a mirrored bead in FrankenSim's tracker; it is not made from this repository's working tree.

**The change.**
1. An input-affine EDMD entry beside the autonomous one: given triples (x_k, u_k, x_{k+1}) with u_k ∈ ℝᵐ and a
   polynomial dictionary ψ of N functions, fit W = [A B_1 … B_m] ∈ ℝ^(N × N·(m+1)) by ridge least squares on the
   regressor Φ_k = (ψ(x_k), u_1,k·ψ(x_k), …, u_m,k·ψ(x_k)): W = (Σ ψ(x_{k+1})·Φ_kᵀ)·(Σ Φ_k·Φ_kᵀ + λ·I)⁻¹.
2. A rollout that reads the state back from the linear monomials and re-lifts at each step, under a supplied
   input sequence.
3. The existing split-conformal band machinery extended to forecasts under recorded inputs: the score of a
   held-out trajectory is its largest normalized error over the horizon given its own inputs; the per-horizon
   band and the simultaneous adjustment are unchanged.
4. The crate's own statement about validity is kept: the band is the only accuracy statement and holds only
   inside the calibration distribution; the input distribution is part of that calibration distribution and is
   recorded with the model.

**Priority.** P3 in the upstream register's scale (no milestone waits on it).

**Interim posture here.** The bilinear fit, the re-lifting rollout and the input-conditioned scores live in
`frob-control` (N22c-23); only the band quantile and the simultaneous adjustment are taken from `fs-surrogate`
through the airlock crate. The local code is marked as a candidate for deletion at switch-over.

**Switch-over rule.** When the upstream entry lands and the pin advances through the compatibility train (move
the pin, write a new receipt, re-run the admission and parity gates, relock): run the differential test below;
if it passes, replace the local fit by the upstream call, delete the local copy, and re-run GATE-REFLEX-10 as a
check (its recorded part stays N22c-25's).

**Differential test.** On the pilot's data set and on the two hand cases: the upstream fit and the local fit
agree on every coefficient to 10⁻¹⁰ relative (same ridge λ, `f64`), and on the band at every horizon exactly
(same scores, same quantile index).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
In the sibling, under its conventions: `edmd_input_affine_linear_recovery` (data from x⁺ = 0.9·x + 0.5·u with
the dictionary {1, x} recovers A = [[1, 0], [0, 0.9]] and B = [[0, 0], [0.5, 0]] to 10⁻⁹);
`edmd_input_affine_reduces_to_autonomous` (with u ≡ 0 the fit equals the autonomous EDMD's);
`band_under_recorded_inputs_coverage` (on synthetic linear data with Gaussian noise the 95% band covers between
0.93 and 0.97 of 2,000 held-out trajectories). Here, in `crates/frob-control/tests/lift_differential.rs`, built
while both fits exist (after the pin moves, before the local copy is deleted):
`lift_differential_local_vs_upstream` (on the two hand cases and on the pilot's recorded data set: coefficients to
10⁻¹⁰ relative; bands equal at every horizon, same scores and same quantile index);
`lift_differential_detects_permutation` (the upstream fit fed with its input columns permuted fails the
comparison, naming the first differing coefficient).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/upstream_fs_surrogate_switchover.sh` (run only at switch-over; sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh`
(STD-E2E); NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/upstream_fs_surrogate_switchover/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript). In
`fixtures/verbs/control/`: (1) on the build at the old pin, the design and data of N22c-23's script in this run's
own state root (`frob design new tracked-a --template tracked-flipper --key sw-design --json`, then `frob sim run
tracked-a --scene fixtures/scenes/soil_plot_sand_dry.fscene --task soil-lift-excitation --worlds 2000 --seconds 2
--tier batch-cpu --precision f64 --seed 11 --key sw-data --json`), then `frob control lift tracked-a --soil
sand-dry --regime regime.toml --fit --key sw-local-fit --json` and `--calibrate --runs <that run id> --key
sw-local-cal`, storing both reports; (2) on the build at the new pin, `cargo test -p frob-control --test lift_differential`; (3) after
the local copy is deleted, the same two calls with keys `sw-up-fit` and `sw-up-cal`, then a comparison of the
two reports' coefficients (read with `frob artifact read <digest> --json`) and bands; (4) `scripts/e2e/gate_reflex_10.sh`
(N22c-25, a dependency) for the re-run of GATE-REFLEX-10, whose receipt hash this script logs and does not record. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22c-22`), `step`, `event`, the
command, exit code, wall time and the evidence block; also the sibling's pinned commit before and after and the
receipt of the compatibility train; `metric` lines `coef_rel_diff_max` (threshold 10⁻¹⁰), `band_equal` (true),
the two `LiftId`s, and the re-run values of GATE-REFLEX-10 against its thresholds; on failure the first
differing coefficient or horizon with both values. The `summary` gives assertions passed and failed and
`gates: ["GATE-REFLEX-10"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
Linear recovery with an input (above); the autonomous reduction. Negative control: an upstream build with the
input columns permuted must fail the differential test.
