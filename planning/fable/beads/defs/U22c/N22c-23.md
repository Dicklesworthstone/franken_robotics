## Background

The sampling-based predictive controller rolls out N_s·H·N_it model steps per control tick (4 × 10⁴ to 2 × 10⁵ in
published configurations). For one regime in which the dynamics are smooth and a rollout of the compiled model
is comparatively expensive, a tracked or wheeled base on soft soil with no contact-mode change, a cheap **lifted
linear model** is tried as the planner's model. It is a Moonshot, off the critical path, and nothing in M0 to M7
depends on it.

A lifted linear predictor is a finite matrix acting on a dictionary of functions of the state. As a global
model for control through contact it is not built: a contact-rich hybrid flow has no finite-dimensional
invariant subspace of such functions (a quadratic dictionary on 60 states already has 1,891 functions and
1,891² ≈ 3.58 × 10⁶ coefficients per map, before any mode jump). The smallest counterexample: for the reset map
x⁺ = abs(x) with the dictionary {1, x, x²} and samples x ∈ {−1, 0, 1}, the fit x⁺ = x² has zero training residual
and predicts 0.25 at x = 0.5 where the truth is 0.5. A zero residual on the fitting set is no evidence; only a
held-out band is, and only inside one mode. The design therefore makes **admission by a held-out band inside a
declared regime** the whole point of the verb.

The work is funded only if the pilot (N22c-21) was not falsified.

## Scope

In: the model type and its identity; the ridge fit with an input term; the regime record; calibration of a
simultaneous forecast band; the admission rule and its denial; the verb `control.lift` with its report. Out: use
of the model inside the planner (N22c-24); the gate's closed-loop comparison (N22c-25); the upstream change to
the sibling's surrogate crate (N22c-22).

## Technical approach

**Crate.** `frob-control`, module `lift`. Bands (the quantile and the simultaneous adjustment over horizons) come
through the airlock crate from `fs-surrogate`; the input-affine fit is local, because the sibling's EDMD is
autonomous (FACT: `frankensim/crates/fs-surrogate/src/koopman.rs`).

**Model (DESIGN).**

```rust
/// A lifted planning model for one regime. Identity: content hash of every field.
pub struct LiftedModel {
    id: LiftId,                    // "lifted:<hash>"
    state: [StateChannel; 7],      // forward speed, yaw rate, slip L, slip R, sinkage L, sinkage R, pitch
    inputs: [InputChannel; 2],     // track torque commands u_L, u_R
    dictionary: PolyDictionary,    // every monomial of total degree ≤ 2: C(9, 2) = 36 functions
    a: Mat36, b_l: Mat36, b_r: Mat36,   // 3 × 36 × 36 = 3,888 coefficients
    period: Rational,              // the control period the triples were sampled at
    regime: Regime,
    band: ForecastBand,            // simultaneous 95% band per horizon step, with n and the level
    provenance: FitProvenance,     // ModelId of the compiled model, tier, profile, data hash, ridge λ, seeds
}
pub struct Regime {
    soil_card: CardId,             // with its declared ranges of pressure, slip and sinkage
    speed_box: Interval, yaw_rate_box: Interval, slope_range: Interval,
    all_track_samples_in_contact: bool,   // must be true: one contact mode
}
```

**Predictor.** z⁺ = A·z + u_L·B_L·z + u_R·B_R·z with z = ψ(x). The state is read back from the linear monomials
and lifted again at every step, so the iterate stays on the image of ψ. One planning step is three
matrix–vector products, 3,888 multiply–adds.

**Fit.** Triples (x_k, u_k, x_{k+1}) from `batch-cpu` rollouts in `f64` inside the regime, under commands that
excite both tracks. Regressor Φ_k = (ψ(x_k), u_L,k·ψ(x_k), u_R,k·ψ(x_k)) ∈ ℝ¹⁰⁸; W = [A B_L B_R] =
(Σ ψ(x_{k+1})·Φ_kᵀ)·(Σ Φ_k·Φ_kᵀ + λ·I)⁻¹ with ridge λ chosen on a validation split. Channels are scaled to unit
range before lifting; the scales are part of the model.

**Calibration.** On trajectories held out from the fit (split by world address): for each, roll the predictor
open-loop from the measured state under the recorded inputs over the planning horizon; the score is the largest
normalized error over the horizon. The simultaneous 95% band is the ⌈(n + 1)·0.95⌉-th smallest score, with the
simultaneous adjustment over horizon steps.

**Admission.** A model is admitted only if all hold: (1) the regime record is complete; (2) the share of
calibration trajectories that contain a contact-mode change inside the declared regime is at most 1%; (3) the
band was calibrated on at least the minimum count for its level (for 95%, n ≥ 19, so that the index exists) and
on data disjoint from the fit; (4) the band at the planning horizon is no wider than the task's tracking
tolerance (DESIGN default: 5% of commanded speed and 0.05 m of path offset). A fit that passes its training
residual and fails (2), (3) or (4) is **denied admission** and the report says which clause failed.

**Queries.** A query whose state lies outside the regime (soil card ranges, speed or yaw box, slope range, a
track sample out of contact) is refused (worded refusal "lifted planner outside its calibrated regime", slug
`lifted-planner-out-of-regime`, exit 3, payload: the violated field and the measured value; repairs: serve the
tick with `mpc-sample` on the compiled model; refit for the new regime). The slug is registered in N22-REF and
has a row in the numbering ledger of N35x-02 (family `4xx`, no number yet); the refusal is raised by slug, its
ledger row's interim form, and VERBS-control lists it by slug on `control.lift` and on `control.mpc --planner
lifted:<id>`; it joins their `refusals` by number when the ledger run numbers it. Data generation that drives the soil law outside the
card's validity range surfaces `FROB-REF-243` from the contact layer (BD-366), as the verb's row lists.

**Verb.** `frob control lift <design> --soil <card> --regime <file> (--fit | --calibrate --runs <run id> |
--report) --key <idempotency key> --json`, kind `run` (the registry's example: `frob control lift tracked-a
--soil sand-dry --regime regime.toml --fit --key lift-1 --json`). Output schema `frob.control.lift.out/1`: the
`LiftId`, the regime, n_fit and n_cal, λ, the band per horizon step, the mode-change share, the admission verdict
with the failing clause if any, fit and calibration cost. Evidence: class `Estimated`; labels `screening:
sampling-control` and `surrogate`; the result inherits every rule of the sampling controller, including that it
never stands in for a trained policy as the evaluator of record. VERBS-control's row lists `runs <run id>` as
the input of the `calibrate` mode. The verb's refusals are `FROB-REF-243` and the worded slug above: it fits and
calibrates a planning model and synthesizes no controller, so it never raises `FROB-REF-421`; its tier is `M`, the
Moonshot tier of the row.

**The regime fixture.** This bead delivers `fixtures/verbs/control/regime.toml` (the file the cluster script
of VERBS-control runs the registry example with; the design `tracked-a` and the excitation task are the pilot
N22c-21's fixtures): `soil_card = "sand-dry"` (its pressure, slip and sinkage ranges are read from the card,
not restated), `speed_box = [0.2, 1.0]` m/s, `yaw_rate_box = [-0.5, 0.5]` rad/s, `slope_range = [-0.05, 0.05]`,
`all_track_samples_in_contact = true`, `period = "1/50"` s, `horizon_steps = 45`, `band_level = 0.95`,
`tolerance = { speed_frac = 0.05, path_offset_m = 0.05 }`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Linear recovery: data from x⁺ = 0.9·x with the dictionary {1, x} returns the map diag(1, 0.9) and the eigenvalue 0.9 to 10⁻⁹.
- [ ] The absolute-value control returns 0.25 against 0.5 at x = 0.5 and that fit is denied admission.
- [ ] Empirical coverage of the simultaneous 95% band is at least 0.93 on 1,000 held-out trajectories (TARGET, a clause of GATE-REFLEX-10).
- [ ] Every out-of-regime query is refused with the violated field named.
- [ ] `frob control lift … --json` is reachable with a documented schema; outputs carry `Estimated`, `surrogate` and `screening: sampling-control`.
- [ ] A model's identity changes with any coefficient, regime bound, band value or provenance field.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-control` documents the module (validity domain: one regime, one contact mode); cancellation of a fit is tested.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-control/tests/lift.rs`:
- `lift_linear_recovery_1e9`: as above.
- `lift_abs_value_denied`: zero training residual, prediction 0.25, admission denied with clause "held-out band".
- `lift_dictionary_36`: seven channels give 36 monomials in a fixed, documented order.
- `lift_relift_each_step`: after one step the iterate equals ψ of its own linear part.
- `lift_fit_matches_normal_equations`: on `synthetic` bilinear data with known A, B_L, B_R and no noise, the fit recovers them to 10⁻⁸ with λ = 0.
- `lift_split_disjoint`: no world address in both sets; a planted overlap is refused.
- `lift_admission_clauses`: one fixture per clause; each is denied with its clause named.
- `lift_out_of_regime_refused`: speed above the box, slope outside the range, a track sample out of contact: each refused.
- `lift_band_min_count`: n = 18 at 95% is refused (no index); n = 19 gives the largest score.

### Known answers and negative controls
Linear recovery (0.9 to 10⁻⁹); absolute-value control (0.25 against 0.5; denied). Negative controls: a mutant that
admits on the training residual must admit the absolute-value fit and fail the gate: this is the counterexample
`reflex-lift-resid` of N22-CX (CX-R22-a), whose plant this bead builds as `N22c-23:admit_on_training_residual`
(the admission rule reads the fitting residual in place of the held-out band; compiled only under
`gauntlet-mutations`), and which `cargo test -p frob-control --features gauntlet-mutations --test lift --
lift_abs_value_denied` and GATE-REFLEX-10's tier run (N22c-25, `--mutations`) must catch; a mutant that calibrates
on the fitting set must show a band narrower than the held-out one and be refused by the split check. Determinism:
the fit is `f64` on the host and repeats bit for bit; the `LiftId` is stable across platforms.

## Considerations

- Kill gate KILL-SOILLIFT (the pilot's falsifier; more than 1% of calibration trajectories with a mode change;
  or the gate's accuracy or cost clause failing after one repair cycle) stops the item; the fallback is
  `mpc-sample` on the compiled model, and no lifted model is offered for any other regime.
- The band holds only inside the calibration distribution, which includes the distribution of inputs; a planner
  that drives the inputs outside it voids the band. The planner integration clamps its samples to the calibrated
  input range for that reason.
- The refusal has no numeric code yet; its slug is registered in the chapter's refusal registry (N22-REF) and
  in N35x-02's ledger, whose numbering run assigns its number.
- Feature-flagged (`lifted-planner`), off by default.
- This bead exists only on the keep branch of KILL-MPC (decision bead N22c-16, M4), on which it depends: if the
  sampling controller is reduced to a single-world CPU debugging aid, there is no planner for a lifted model to
  serve and the item is closed as not built.
