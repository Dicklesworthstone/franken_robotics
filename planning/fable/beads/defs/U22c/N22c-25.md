## Background

`frob control lift` fits and calibrates a bilinear lifted model of a tracked or wheeled base on soft soil for one
regime with no contact-mode change, and `frob control mpc --planner lifted:<id>` uses it as the planning model of
the sampling-based predictive controller, falling back to the compiled model on any tick outside the regime.
The item is a Moonshot. Its convergence gate (earliest milestone M6):

| Gate | Claim | Threshold (TARGET) |
|---|---|---|
| GATE-REFLEX-10 | Design comparison | Linear recovery to 10⁻⁹; the absolute-value control returns 0.25 against 0.5 and that fit is denied admission; empirical coverage of the simultaneous 95% band at least 0.93 on 1,000 held-out trajectories; closed-loop tracking error with the lifted planner within 10% of the compiled-model planner's on 100 episodes paired by world address; planner cost per tick at least ten times lower at that accuracy; every out-of-regime query refused. |

A gate passes only with a recorded measurement against its threshold and a registered negative control that has
been shown to fail. This bead is the script that produces the six measurements from the user's side.

## Scope

In: one script; the six clauses; the negative controls; the gate row. Out: the mechanisms (N22c-23, N22c-24);
the kill decision (N22c-26).

## Technical approach

Script: `scripts/e2e/gate_reflex_10.sh`, seed-explicit (`FROB_E2E_SEED`, default 1), idempotent, non-zero exit
on any failed assertion. Tier: `batch-cpu` under the replay profile, `f64`.

1. **Linear recovery.** `frob control lift --fixture linear-0p9 --fit --json`: assert the fitted map equals
   diag(1, 0.9) and the eigenvalue 0.9 within 10⁻⁹.
2. **Absolute-value control.** `frob control lift --fixture abs-value --fit --calibrate --json`: assert training
   residual 0, prediction 0.25 at x = 0.5 (truth 0.5), and `admission: denied`.
3. **Fit and calibrate on the body.** `frob control lift tracked-flipper --soil <card> --regime <file> --fit
   --calibrate --json` on 1,000 fitting and 1,000 calibration trajectories; then evaluate coverage on a further
   1,000 held-out trajectories: the share whose realized error stays inside the simultaneous 95% band over the
   whole planning horizon. Assert coverage ≥ 0.93 and `admission: admitted`.
4. **Paired closed loop.** 100 world addresses. For each: `frob control mpc tracked-flipper --task
   soil-velocity-tracking --planner compiled --episodes 1` and the same with `--planner lifted:<id>`, same
   address and seed. Tracking error per episode: root-mean-square body-twist error over the episode. Assert the
   paired ratio (mean lifted error) / (mean compiled error) ≤ 1.10, with its paired interval logged.
5. **Planner cost.** From the per-tick cost logs of step 4: assert (compiled cost per tick) / (lifted cost per
   tick) ≥ 10 in the common unit, on ticks served by the lifted planner, with the fallback ticks counted and
   reported separately.
6. **Out-of-regime sweep.** 200 queries constructed outside the regime, 50 per field (soil range, speed or yaw
   box, slope range, a track sample lifted off): assert each is refused with the violated field named; and one
   closed-loop episode driven across the box edge: assert every out-of-regime tick shows
   `fallback:lifted-out-of-regime`.
7. **Record.** Write the gate row with the six measured values, the commit, the command lines, the output
   digest and the receipt.

**Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/gate_reflex_10/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every
line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22c-25`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the
envelope's `evidence` block (class `Estimated`, tier, determinism, identities including the `LiftId`, seed).
Metrics with thresholds: `eig_abs_err` (10⁻⁹), `abs_pred_at_0p5` (0.25), `admission_abs` (`denied`),
`band_coverage` (≥ 0.93), `tracking_ratio` (≤ 1.10), `planner_cost_ratio` (≥ 10), `out_of_regime_refused` (200 of
200), `fallback_ticks`, `lifted_ticks`. On failure: the first differing value, the world address, and the paths
of the episode files. A `summary` line: assertions passed and failed; gate GATE-REFLEX-10 with its status.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists and exits 0 only when all six clauses hold.
- [ ] The gate register holds a row for GATE-REFLEX-10 with the six measured values (not the targets), commit and receipt.
- [ ] Each negative control below has a recorded failing run.
- [ ] Episodes of step 4 are paired by world address and seed; an unpaired comparison is refused by the script.
- [ ] Logs contain no private-scene data.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Helpers in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `paired_ratio` (errors (1.0, 2.0) against (1.05, 2.1) give 1.05);
`coverage_count` (935 of 1,000 inside gives 0.935, passing; 925 gives 0.925, failing); `cost_ratio_excludes_fallback_ticks`.

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified above; this bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: linear recovery (eigenvalue 0.9 to 10⁻⁹); the absolute-value fit (0.25 against 0.5). Negative
controls, each run through the same script and required to fail its clause: (a) a build that admits on the
training residual admits the absolute-value fit; (b) a band calibrated on the fitting set fails coverage on the
held-out set or is refused by the split check; (c) a build without the per-tick regime check fails the
out-of-regime sweep; (d) a lifted model fitted on half the input range fails the tracking clause when the
planner's samples are not clamped. Determinism lanes: the paired episodes repeat bit for bit on one machine and
across worker counts on `batch-cpu`.

## Considerations

- The cost clause is read "at that accuracy": if the tracking clause fails, the cost ratio is reported and not
  judged.
- The accuracy and cost clauses each allow one repair cycle before the kill gate fires; the script logs
  `repair_cycle_used`.
- Coverage of 0.93 against a nominal 0.95 allows for the finite calibration set; the calibration count is logged
  so the allowance can be checked against the binomial spread.
