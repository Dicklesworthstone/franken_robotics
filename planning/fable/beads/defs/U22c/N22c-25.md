## Background

`frob control lift` fits and calibrates a bilinear lifted model of a tracked or wheeled base on soft soil for one
regime with no contact-mode change, and `frob control mpc --planner lifted:<id>` uses it as the planning model of
the sampling-based predictive controller, falling back to the compiled model on any tick outside the regime.
The item is a Moonshot. Its convergence gate (earliest milestone M6):

| Gate | Claim | Threshold (TARGET) |
|---|---|---|
| GATE-REFLEX-10 | Design comparison | Linear recovery to 10⁻⁹; the absolute-value control returns 0.25 against 0.5 and that fit is denied admission; empirical coverage of the simultaneous 95% band at least 0.93 on 1,000 held-out trajectories; closed-loop tracking error with the lifted planner within 10% of the compiled-model planner's on 100 episodes paired by world address; planner cost per tick at least ten times lower at that accuracy; every out-of-regime query refused. |

A gate passes only with a recorded measurement against its threshold and a registered negative control that has
been shown to fail. This bead is the script that produces the six measurements from the user's side.

## Scope

In: one script; the six clauses; the negative controls; the method-experiment entry that admits the paired
episodes beyond the screening cap; the conformance case whose tier run produces the gate's receipt; the M6 part
of the gate recorded from it. Out: the mechanisms (N22c-23, N22c-24);
the fixtures they deliver (`tracked-a` and `soil-lift-excitation` from the pilot N22c-21, `regime.toml` from
N22c-23, `soil-velocity-tracking` from N22c-24); the kill decision (N22c-26).

## Technical approach

Script: `scripts/e2e/gate_reflex_10.sh`, seed-explicit (`FROB_E2E_SEED`, default 1), idempotent, non-zero exit
on any failed assertion. Tier: `batch-cpu` under the replay profile, `f64`.

1. **Linear recovery.** The two synthetic hand cases have no design, soil or regime, so they run through the
   crate's tests of N22c-23 on the production fit: `cargo test -p frob-control --test lift --
   lift_linear_recovery_1e9 --exact`, whose output the script parses: assert the fitted map equals
   diag(1, 0.9) and the eigenvalue 0.9 within 10⁻⁹.
2. **Absolute-value control.** `cargo test -p frob-control --test lift -- lift_abs_value_denied --exact`:
   assert training residual 0, prediction 0.25 at x = 0.5 (truth 0.5), and admission denied with clause
   "held-out band".
3. **Fit and calibrate on the body.** In `fixtures/verbs/control/` (N22c-23's `regime.toml`): data from `frob
   sim run tracked-a --scene soil:sand-dry --task soil-lift-excitation --worlds 3000 --seconds 2 --tier
   batch-cpu --precision f64 --seed $FROB_E2E_SEED --key gr10-data-<seed> --json`; `frob control lift tracked-a
   --soil sand-dry --regime regime.toml --fit --key gr10-fit-<seed> --json` on 1,000 fitting trajectories, then
   `--calibrate --runs <run id> --key gr10-cal-<seed>` on 1,000 calibration trajectories (split by world
   address); then evaluate coverage on the further 1,000 held-out trajectories: the share whose realized error
   stays inside the simultaneous 95% band over the whole planning horizon. Assert coverage ≥ 0.93 and
   `admission: admitted`.
4. **Paired closed loop.** 100 world addresses, i = 1 … 100, seed s_i derived from `FROB_E2E_SEED` and i. The
   200 episodes on one design and task exceed the sampling controller's screening allowance of three, so the
   comparison is registered as an experiment in the method-experiment register (N38a-07), `EXP-REFLEX-10` (DESIGN
   name; the register's identifier governs), whose protocol, frozen before the first run, declares 200 episodes
   for (`tracked-a`, `soil-velocity-tracking`) and 1 for the edge-crossing episode of step 6, and every request
   names it with `--protocol <id>` (N22a-24's rule); the episodes stay `screening: sampling-control`. For each i:
   `frob control mpc tracked-a --task soil-velocity-tracking --planner compiled --episodes 1 --seed s_i
   --protocol <id> --key gr10-c-<i> --json` and the same with `--planner lifted:<id> --key gr10-l-<i>` (distinct
   keys, so no episode is served another's receipt), same address and seed. A first request without
   `--protocol` beyond the third episode is refused with `mpc-episode-cap`, which the script asserts once. Tracking error per episode: root-mean-square
   body-twist error over the episode. Assert the paired ratio (mean lifted error) / (mean compiled error)
   ≤ 1.10, with its paired interval logged.
5. **Planner cost.** From the per-tick cost logs of step 4: assert (compiled cost per tick) / (lifted cost per
   tick) ≥ 10 in the common unit, on ticks served by the lifted planner, with the fallback ticks counted and
   reported separately.
6. **Out-of-regime sweep.** 200 queries constructed outside the regime, 50 per field (soil range, speed or yaw
   box, slope range, a track sample lifted off). `control.mpc` has no initial-state input (its `--state` is the
   state source, `privileged` or `estimated`), so each query is a task: the script writes 200 task documents,
   each `soil-velocity-tracking` with its episode starting outside the regime along one field (generated from the
   regime file and logged with their digests), registers each with `frob task new oor-<field>-<j>.ftask --key
   gr10-t-<field>-<j> --json`, and poses it as `frob control mpc tracked-a --task <its TaskId> --planner
   lifted:<id> --episodes 1 --key gr10-q-<field>-<j> --json` (one episode per task, inside the allowance): assert
   the first tick of each is refused by the lifted planner with the violated field named and served by the
   compiled planner; and one closed-loop episode on `soil-velocity-tracking` driven across the box edge (under
   `--protocol <id>`): assert every out-of-regime tick shows `fallback:lifted-out-of-regime`.
7. **Receipt.** `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-REFLEX-10 --mutations`: the tier run of the gate's entry, which
   holds this bead's case `gate_reflex_10` (the six clauses through the real fit, the real calibration and the
   real lifted and compiled planners on `batch-cpu` `f64`, over the same seeded addresses, with controls (a) to
   (d) below compiled under `gauntlet-mutations` and caught in the same run, (a) being N22c-23's plant
   `N22c-23:admit_on_training_residual`). It writes one hash-named receipt; the script asserts on its `gates` and
   `controls` and that its values equal the script's own. The script records nothing.

**Recording (not part of the script).** A session that did not implement N22c-23 or N22c-24 enters the M6 part
from that receipt: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-REFLEX-10 --receipt <hash>
--part M6`, with the six measured values; the commit, the command lines and the output digest are the receipt's.

**Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/gate_reflex_10/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every
line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22c-25`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the
envelope's `evidence` block (class `Estimated`, tier, determinism, identities including the `LiftId`, seed).
Metrics with thresholds: `eig_abs_err` (10⁻⁹), `abs_pred_at_0p5` (0.25), `admission_abs` (`denied`),
`band_coverage` (≥ 0.93), `tracking_ratio` (≤ 1.10), `planner_cost_ratio` (≥ 10), `out_of_regime_refused` (200 of
200), `fallback_ticks`, `lifted_ticks`, `repair_cycle_used`, the calibration count n; each call's idempotency
key and run identifier; the digests of the regime file, the generated task documents and the `LiftId`; the
experiment's identifier and protocol hash; the receipt hash on the `receipt` step's `cmd` line. On failure:
the first differing value, the world address, and the paths of the episode files. A `summary` line: assertions
passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-REFLEX-10"]` with its status.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists and exits 0 only when all six clauses hold.
- [ ] The tier run writes one receipt that catches controls (a) to (d); the M6 part of GATE-REFLEX-10 is recorded from it with `record_gate --part M6` by a non-implementing session, with the six measured values (not the targets); the script's log contains no `record_gate` call.
- [ ] The paired episodes run under the registered experiment's `--protocol`, whose protocol was frozen before the first run; no `--state` value other than `privileged` or `estimated` is passed.
- [ ] Each negative control below has a recorded failing run.
- [ ] Episodes of step 4 are paired by world address and seed; an unpaired comparison is refused by the script.
- [ ] Logs contain no private-scene data.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Helpers in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `paired_ratio` (errors (1.0, 2.0) against (1.05, 2.1) give 1.05);
`coverage_count` (935 of 1,000 inside gives 0.935, passing; 925 gives 0.925, failing); `cost_ratio_excludes_fallback_ticks`.

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified above; this bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: linear recovery (eigenvalue 0.9 to 10⁻⁹); the absolute-value fit (0.25 against 0.5). Negative
controls, each run through the same script and required to fail its clause: (a) a build that admits on the
training residual admits the absolute-value fit; (b) a band calibrated on the fitting set fails coverage on the
held-out set or is refused by the split check; (c) a build without the per-tick regime check fails the
out-of-regime sweep; (d) a lifted model fitted on half the input range fails the tracking clause when the
planner's samples are not clamped. Determinism lanes: the paired episodes repeat bit for bit on one machine and
across worker counts on `batch-cpu`.

## Considerations

- The cost clause is read "at that accuracy": if the tracking clause fails, the cost ratio is reported and not
  judged.
- The accuracy and cost clauses each allow one repair cycle before the kill gate fires; the script logs
  `repair_cycle_used`.
- Coverage of 0.93 against a nominal 0.95 allows for the finite calibration set; the calibration count is logged
  so the allowance can be checked against the binomial spread.
