## Background

A kill gate is a decision named in advance with its trigger, measurement and fallback; a kill gate that fires is
not a failure, because the fallback is the plan. Its decision is recorded as an ADR. A kill gate with no
milestone of its own is decided at the milestone of the gate beside it, here M6.

KILL-SOILLIFT governs the one bounded experiment with lifted linear models: a bilinear predictor on a
36-function polynomial dictionary of a 7-entry reduced state, fitted for a tracked or wheeled base on soft soil
in a single regime with no contact-mode change, and used as the planning model of the sampling-based predictive
controller. As a global model for control through contact the idea is not built at all. The benefit's ceiling
is the ratio of planner rollout cost to lifted cost inside the controller, and on a design cycle only the share
of wall time the screening rung spends on soft-soil tasks, which is expected to be small.

## Scope

In: applying the three trigger clauses at the points where their measurements exist, recording the ADR, stating
the downstream changes. Out: the pilot (N22c-21), the mechanism (N22c-23, N22c-24) and the gate measurement
(N22c-25).

## Technical approach

**Trigger.** Any of:
1. the pilot's falsifier holds: the simultaneous band at a 0.9 s horizon is wider than the task's tracking
   tolerance (DESIGN default: 5% of commanded speed and 0.05 m of path offset);
2. more than 1% of calibration trajectories contain a contact-mode change inside the declared regime;
3. the accuracy clause or the cost clause of GATE-REFLEX-10 fails after one repair cycle (accuracy: closed-loop
   tracking error with the lifted planner within 10% of the compiled-model planner's on 100 episodes paired by
   world address; cost: planner cost per tick at least ten times lower at that accuracy).

**Milestone.** M6. Clauses 1 and 2 are read from the pilot and can close the question before any mechanism work
is funded.

**Measurement that decides it.** From N22c-21: the band at 0.9 s (two figures) and the mode-change share. From
N22c-25, if the work was funded: the paired tracking ratio and the planner cost ratio, before and after one
repair cycle.

**Decision rule.** The outcome is named in the four names of the kill-gate record (N40-03): `fired`,
`not-fired`, `not-reached`, `deferred`.
1. Pilot band above tolerance, or mode-change share above 0.01 → `fired`; N22c-23, N22c-24 and N22c-25 are not
   funded and are closed `not-funded` citing the pilot record, which releases this decision from its dependency
   on N22c-25 so that it is recorded as fired with the pilot's numbers.
2. Pilot clean and the gate's accuracy and cost clauses pass (at first or after one repair) → `not-fired`, citing
   the receipts of the pilot and of GATE-REFLEX-10.
3. Pilot clean and either clause fails after the repair → `fired`.
4. The other clauses of the gate (linear recovery, the absolute-value control, band coverage, refusal of
   out-of-regime queries) are correctness clauses: a failure there is a defect to fix, not a kill trigger, and
   the gate stays `fail` until fixed.
5. The pilot was never run (KILL-SOIL fired upstream and BD-366 closed on its fallback, so there is no soil law
   on the batched tiers), or the sampling controller was reduced to a single-world CPU debugging aid at KILL-MPC
   (N22c-16) so that N22c-23 is closed as not built → `not-reached`, citing that ADR: the mechanism was not
   funded, the capability does not exist and is not described. (An earlier draft said "fired by consequence";
   N40-03 has no such outcome, and `fired` would claim a measurement that was never made.)
6. A second repair cycle, or any move of the decision past M6, is `deferred` and needs an ADR naming the later
   exit and its reason.

**Fallback, named in advance.** `mpc-sample` on the compiled model, as the sampling controller already
specifies; no lifted model is offered for any other regime.

**What changes downstream when it fires (or is `not-reached`).**
- `control.lift` and `control.mpc --planner lifted:<id>` are refused by the dispatch as a capability not in the
  build: `FROB-REF-724`, reason `killed: KILL-SOILLIFT` after `fired` (`not-linked` when `not-reached`), exit 5,
  citing the ADR; the feature flag `lifted-planner` is removed; `frob capabilities --json` lists the fallback
  and not the lifted planner.
- Soft-soil screening runs on the compiled planning model at its usual cost; nothing in M0 to M7 changes.
- The upstream item for an input-affine fit in the sibling's surrogate crate is closed `superseded` here.
- The absolute-value counterexample stays in the catalogue: it guards any later proposal to admit a surrogate on
  its training residual.

**Record.** `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOILLIFT-M6.md` (the program's one directory for decision records) with the
fields N40-03 requires: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered by the fallback ledger N40-29 and
any amendments; each measured value with its receipt hash beside the clause it decides (the pilot record's
identifier, its two band values against 0.05 and 0.05 m and its mode-change share against 0.01; if run, the
tracking ratio against 1.10 and the cost ratio against 10, before and after the repair cycle); commit, hardware
fingerprint and seeds; the outcome and which clause decided; the fallback now in force and the name the
capability carries ("planning model: compiled only"); the beads re-read (N22c-23, N22c-24, N22c-25: released,
or superseded by this ADR; N22c-22: closed `superseded`); what changes downstream; the recording session.
Registry: the `decision` table (`outcome`, `adr`, `date`, `session`, and `receipts`, the hashes of the receipts
the outcome rests on: for `not-fired` and for rule 3, N22c-25's and the `receipt` of the pilot's
`[[pilot.result]]`, committed at `receipts/pilots/<hash>.json` (N38a-09) and resolved there by N40-03; under rule 1
the pilot result's alone; under rule 5 none, since nothing was measured, and the ADR cites the upstream ADR) of the KILL-SOILLIFT row that N40-29 entered in `registries/gates.toml`, and the gate's owner declaration (`[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`, the
ledger's `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied, not restated, and `decision` with each
measured value citing its receipt) in `frob-control`'s `contract.toml`, which the cross-check N40-30 compares
with the ledger; the GATE-REFLEX-10 row's status from N22c-25's receipt. A later reading appends a second
`decision` entry with its own ADR; the first is never edited.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOILLIFT-M6.md` cites the pilot's band and mode-change figures and, if run, the gate's two ratios with the repair cycle; the outcome is one of N40-03's four and the deciding clause is named.
- [ ] `registries/gates.toml` carries the KILL-SOILLIFT `decision` table and the GATE-REFLEX-10 status with measured values, and `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (N40-30) passes for this gate (owner declaration present, trigger and fallback equal to the ledger's, outcome consistent with the measured values).
- [ ] If `fired` or `not-reached`: tests show `frob control lift` returning `FROB-REF-724` (exit 5) with the ADR cited and `--planner lifted:<id>` refused, while `frob control mpc --planner compiled` is unaffected.
- [ ] If not fired: the admitted regime and the measured ratios are in the record, with the statement that no other regime is covered.
- [ ] The decision is made by a session that did not run the measurements.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_gates.rs` (the pure rule and the record) and
`crates/frob-control/tests/lift_kill.rs` (the refusal):
- `kill_soillift_rule_table`: (band 0.07 of speed) → `fired`; (band 0.03, 0.04 m; mode share 0.02) → `fired`; (clean pilot; ratio 1.06, cost 14×) → `not-fired`; (clean; ratio 1.18 after repair) → `fired`; (clean; ratio 1.06, cost 6× after repair) → `fired`; (band 0.07, gate not run) → `fired`, and the record names N22c-23, N22c-24 and N22c-25 as `not-funded`; (correctness clause failing) → gate `fail`, kill not decided; (no pilot result, KILL-SOIL fired) → `not-reached`; (clean pilot, KILL-MPC fired) → `not-reached`; every returned outcome is one of N40-03's four.
- `kill_soillift_record_complete`: the record parses with trigger, inputs and receipts, outcome, date and session; the ADR path is `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SOILLIFT-M6.md`; a `not-fired` record with no receipt is refused.
- `lift_withdrawn_after_kill`: with the outcome `fired`, `control.lift` and `control.mpc --planner lifted:<id>` return `FROB-REF-724` with reason `killed: KILL-SOILLIFT`, exit 5; `--planner compiled` still runs.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_soillift_record.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N22c-26 milestone=M6 labels=reflex,kill-gate,moonshot
gates=KILL-SOILLIFT,GATE-REFLEX-10 seed=0 needs=cpu plants=not-fired-with-falsified-pilot`) re-derives the
outcome from the stored inputs: it reads the pilot's row and result in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`,
the logs of `pilot_soil_lift` and `gate_reflex_10` and the gate rows, asserts that the values agree, applies the
rule, compares the derived outcome with the ADR and the `decision` table, runs `cargo test -p frob-conformance
--test kill_ledger` (asserting that no failure names KILL-SOILLIFT), and, when the outcome is `fired` or
`not-reached`, runs `e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-724 5 -- frob control lift tracked-a --soil sand-dry --regime
regime.toml --fit --key ksl-1 --json` and, on a design it creates (`frob design new ksl-quad --template
quad-mammal --key ksl-design --json`), `frob control mpc ksl-quad --task track-forward-20s --planner compiled
--episodes 1 --seed 1 --key ksl-2 --json` (asserting it runs; N22c-24's task `soil-velocity-tracking` does not
exist when the item was not funded, so the check uses the sampling controller's registered task). It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh`
(STD-E2E) and writes `target/e2e-logs/kill_soillift_record/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code and wall time; `metric` lines
`band_speed_frac@0.9s` (0.05), `band_offset_m@0.9s` (0.05 m), `mode_change_share` (0.01), `tracking_ratio`
(1.10), `planner_cost_ratio` (10), `repair_cycle_used`, each with the digest of the record it came from; the
derived and recorded outcomes; the refusal block; the ADR path; on disagreement the first differing field. The
`summary` gives assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["KILL-SOILLIFT", "GATE-REFLEX-10"]`. It writes nothing
to the registry; the recording session does.

### Known answers and negative controls
Rule fixtures above. Negative controls: a record with targets in place of measured values is rejected by the
registry check; a record that says `not-fired` while the stored pilot band is 0.07 of speed (plant
`not-fired-with-falsified-pilot`) must make the script fail, and `kill_ledger` must report
`outcome-contradicts-measurement`.
