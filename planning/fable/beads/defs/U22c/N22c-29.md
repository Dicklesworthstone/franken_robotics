## Background

The pre-slip aliasing fixture is a cart approaching a keep-out line in two worlds that differ only in friction
and deliver identical observations until braking begins. It tests one rule of the runtime shields: an assumption
whose monitor cannot put the state inside the backup controller's domain in time may not be listed as
monitored; its whole range goes inside the filter's quantifier. It also tests the sampled-data form of a backup
domain, D = {(s, v) : s ≥ v²/(2·μ_min·g) + v·Δt}, whose last term is the inter-sample margin.

The convergence gate, as its defining row states it:

> GATE-SHIELD-06 (Known answer, M4). On the referee and on the `strict` profile, over 1,000 paired episodes with
> v₀ drawn from [1, 3] m/s and friction pairs from [0.3, 0.8]: the two transcripts are bit-equal before the first
> braking tick in every pair. Arm 1 violates in the low-friction world in every episode whose stopping distance
> exceeds its trigger range, with the overshoot within 1 mm of the closed form. Arm 2 has no violation in 10⁴
> episodes. The mutant without the inter-sample term produces at least one violation, none larger than v₀·Δt.
> `shield.fit` refuses Arm 1's monitored-assumption listing and prints both admitting changes.

This bead is the script that measures it through the user's surface, the conformance case whose tier run
produces the gate's receipt, and the gate's one recorder. It also owns the `sim.run` input `--param`, by which a
run sets the free parameters a fixture design or a controller declares (the VERBS-sim row names this bead as its
owner); the episodes of the gate are drawn through it.

## Scope

In: the `sim.run` input `--param` (its definition, implementation and tests); one script; the five clauses on two
tiers; the conformance case `gate_shield_06` under the GATE-SHIELD-06 entry of `g1_known_answers`; the M4 part of
the gate recorded from that case's receipt. Out: the fixture, its free-parameter declarations, its controllers
and listings (N22c-27); the timeliness rule and `shield.check`'s `late-alarm` (N22c-28); the relation of a
fixture run with free values to the fixture's recorded hash, which N24b-03 states (its free-parameter rule, which
VERBS-sim's `--param` row cites).

## Technical approach

**The `sim.run` input `--param <name>=<value>` (repeatable; DESIGN, owner of the VERBS-sim row).** It sets one
free parameter that the run's fixture design or its controller declares, for this run only.
1. Syntax: `<name>=<value>`; `<name>` matches `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`; `<value>` is an exact decimal string with an
   optional unit token (`v0=2.0`, `v0=2.0m/s`); a bare number is read in the coherent SI unit of the parameter's
   declared dimension and echoed with that unit (the rule of `design.edit`'s values). Repeating a name is 802.
2. Resolution, before the request is digested: the name is looked up first among the free parameters declared by
   the design (or the fixture's design), then among those declared by the controller (`--controller` or the
   fixture's). A name declared by neither, or declared but fixed by the fixture (in its `constants`), is
   `FROB-REF-802` with `pointer` at `/param/<name>` and the declared free names listed; a value outside the domain
   a controller declares is `FROB-REF-802` naming the domain; with `--fixture`, a value outside the domain the
   fixture's `free` table declares is `FROB-REF-530` with `pointer` `free.<name>.domain` (N24b-03's rule, step 4);
   a unit of the wrong dimension is `FROB-REF-102`.
3. Identity: a design parameter enters the `ModelId` of the run's world; a controller parameter enters the
   `ControllerId`; both are written into the run manifest, so they enter `RunId`. Parameters left unset take their
   declared defaults, which the manifest also records.
4. With `--fixture`, N24b-03's free-parameter rule: the run's fixture identity and `ConstantsHash` cover the
   declared free parameters' names, domains and defaults, never a run's values, so a run that sets declared free
   parameters inside their domains matches the recorded hash and records the values; a value outside a declared
   domain is 530 (`free.<name>.domain`); a changed domain or default is a changed fixture and is 530.
5. Without a fixture or a controller that declares free parameters, any `--param` is 802.
Unit tests in `crates/frob-verbs/tests/sim_param.rs` (the real input parser and the real fixture registry; the
designs are `synthetic`): `param_sets_declared_free` (`mu=0.3` on the pre-slip cart changes the `ModelId` and the
delivered deceleration in world B); `param_controller_side` (`kd=150` on a controller that declares `kd` changes the
`ControllerId`, not the `ModelId`); `param_undeclared_802` (`zeta=1` lists the declared names); `param_fixed_802`
(`g=9.8` on the pre-slip fixture, whose `constants` fix g); `param_fixture_out_of_domain_530` (`mu=0.9` on the
pre-slip fixture: 530 with `pointer` `free.mu.domain`); `param_controller_out_of_domain_802` (`kd` outside the
domain its controller declares: 802 naming the domain); `param_unit_102` (`v0=2s`); `param_repeat_802`;
`param_free_values_not_530` and `param_changed_domain_530`.

**Script `scripts/e2e/gate_shield_06.sh`** (header `bead=N22c-29 milestone=M4 labels=reflex,shield,gate
gates=GATE-SHIELD-06 seed=1 plants=no-timeliness`); seed from `FROB_E2E_SEED` (default 1); idempotent; non-zero
exit on any failed assertion. It runs every step on `--tier referee` and on `--tier batch-cpu --profile strict`.

Constants: g = 9.81 m/s²; commanded deceleration 5.0 m/s²; Δt = 0.02 s; belief set μ ∈ [0.3, 0.8], μ_min = 0.3.
Draws, from counter-based streams keyed by (seed, episode): v₀ uniform in [1, 3] m/s; μ_hi and μ_lo uniform in
[0.3, 0.8] with μ_lo ≤ μ_hi; a start range that sets the sampling phase.

Every `run` call carries its own `--key` (`g6-<step>-<episode>-<world>-<tier>`), so that the two worlds of a
pair, the two tiers and the three controllers never return one another's stored receipt. The fixture's draws are
passed as `--param <name>=<value>` (`mu`, `v0`, `s0`, the free parameters N22c-27's cart declares); the fixture
declares no controller, so `--controller` names one of its three registered fixture controllers.

0. **Fixture stage.** `scripts/e2e/reflex_preslip_fixture.sh` (N22c-27's script) runs first; its failure fails
   this script.
1. **Pairs and transcripts.** For 1,000 pairs: `frob sim run --fixture pre-slip-aliasing --controller
   arm1-nominal-monitor --param mu=<μ_hi> --param v0=<v₀> --param s0=<s₀> --tier <tier> --key g6-1-<i>-hi-<tier>
   --json` and the same with `mu=<μ_lo>` (key `g6-1-<i>-lo-<tier>`); `frob telemetry divergence <run hi> <run lo>
   --json` (expected exit 0 or 2: the runs part after braking). Assert the first divergence is at or after the
   first braking tick in every pair (`transcript_equal_pairs` = 1,000). A pair that diverges earlier voids the
   fixture on that tier and fails the script.
2. **Arm 1.** For each pair, the low-friction run. Closed form: s_trig = v₀²/(2·5.0) + 0.05; s_b the range at the
   first tick with s ≤ s_trig; a = min(5.0, μ_lo·g); m = s_b − v₀²/(2·a). Assert: whenever v₀²/(2·a) > s_trig the
   episode is a violation (the gate's clause); and in every episode abs(m_sim − m) ≤ 0.001 m. The expected
   violation count is the number of episodes with closed-form m < 0, which includes episodes whose stopping
   distance lies between s_b and s_trig. Log the count of violating episodes and the expected count.
3. **Arm 2.** 10⁴ episodes (the 2,000 runs of the pairs' worlds plus 8,000 further draws) with `--controller
   arm2-belief-shield`. Assert zero episodes cross the line. Log the distribution of the stop margin (the cost).
4. **Mutant.** The same 10⁴ draws with `--controller mutant-continuous-barrier` (a fixture controller of N22c-27,
   present in every build; STD-E2E has no step that builds `frob` with `gauntlet-mutations`, and the planted
   defect itself, `N22c-27:drop_intersample_term` in Arm 2's filter, is caught in the tier run of step 7). Assert
   at least one violation, and that no overshoot exceeds v₀·Δt for its episode.
5. **`shield.fit`.** `frob shield fit assets/fixtures/pre-slip-aliasing/cart.fbot --controller arm1-nominal-monitor
   --tasks <the fixture's task> --listing assets/fixtures/pre-slip-aliasing/listing-arm1.toml --tier <tier> --key
   g6-5-arm1-<tier> --json`: assert `ok: false`, the refusal slug `monitored-assumption-not-timely`, exit 3, and a
   payload with the first distinguishing tick, the state there, and two admitting changes; at v₀ = 2.0 m/s assert
   the changes read `brake by s ≤ 0.7196 m` and `speed limit 1.5696 m/s` (to four decimals, rounded down so that
   the printed limit admits; the root is 1.569687, quoted as 1.57 m/s in the plan). Then `--listing
   …/listing-arm2.toml --key g6-5-arm2-<tier>`: assert it fits. Declared plant `no-timeliness` (injected by the
   script under `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE`, STD-E2E): the Arm 1 call is pointed at N22c-28's `cart-timely.fbot`, so the
   listing is admitted and the refusal assertion must fail; the `shield.fit` build without the timeliness test is
   control (b) of the tier run, compiled under `gauntlet-mutations`.
6. **`shield.check`.** Replay one Arm 1 low-friction run (`frob shield check <run> --shield <id> --key
   g6-6-<tier> --json`, with the shield fitted in step 5 under the Arm 2 listing): assert a `late-alarm` finding
   with its distance to the backup domain.
7. **Receipt.** `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SHIELD-06 --mutations`: the tier run of the gate's entry, which
   holds this bead's case `gate_shield_06` (the five clauses on both tiers over the same seeded draws, through the
   real referee and `strict` steps, the real fixture controllers and the real `shield.fit`; controls (a) to (c)
   below compiled under `gauntlet-mutations` and caught in the same run) and the fixture's known-answer row
   `reflex-preslip-cart`. It writes one hash-named receipt; the script asserts on its `gates` and `controls` and
   that its counts equal the script's own. The script records nothing.

**Recording (not part of the script).** A session that did not implement N22c-27, N22c-28 or BD-338 enters the M4
part from that receipt: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SHIELD-06 --receipt
<hash> --part M4`. The part's `measured` field gives, for each of the two tiers, the counts of the five clauses and
the largest closed-form error; the gate has one part at M4 that covers both tiers.

**Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/gate_shield_06/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every
line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22c-29`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim Known answer, class `Tested`, tier, determinism, identities, seed). Metrics with
thresholds: `transcript_equal_pairs` (1,000 of 1,000), `arm1_expected_violations` (episodes with closed-form
m < 0) and `arm1_violations` (equal), `arm1_overshoot_err_max_m` (≤ 0.001), `arm2_violations` (0 of 10⁴),
`arm2_margin_m` (median and maximum), `mutant_violations` (≥ 1), `mutant_overshoot_over_v0dt_max` (≤ 1),
`fit_refused` (true), `admitting_changes` (2). The `receipt` step's `cmd` line carries the receipt hash. On
failure: the episode index, its draws, the first differing packet and the run's file paths. A `summary` line:
assertions passed and failed; controls caught; the receipt hash; gate GATE-SHIELD-06 with its status per tier.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `sim.run --param` behaves as specified: its unit tests pass, and the generated `sim.run` input schema lists
      it (an additive change).
- [ ] The script exits 0 only when all five clauses hold on both tiers; it calls `sim.run` with `--fixture
      pre-slip-aliasing` (never a positional `fixture:` form) and every run call carries a distinct key.
- [ ] The tier run writes one receipt that catches controls (a) to (c); the M4 part of GATE-SHIELD-06 is recorded
      from it with `record_gate --part M4` by a non-implementing session, with measured values for both tiers; the
      script's log contains no `record_gate` call.
- [ ] The hand case at v₀ = 2.0 m/s, μ = (0.8, 0.3) is among the episodes (episode 0 is pinned to it, with
      `s0=2.01` so that a tick falls at 0.45 m and Arm 1 brakes there; at the fixture's default `s0` of 2.02 m it
      would brake at 0.42 m) and reproduces 0.2296 m, 0.7196 m and 1.5696 m/s.
- [ ] The negative controls below have recorded failing runs.
- [ ] Rerunning with the same seed reproduces every count and digest.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The `--param` tests above. Helpers in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `closed_form_margin` (v₀ = 2.0, μ = 0.3, s_b = 0.45
gives m = −0.229579); `sampling_phase_range` (s_b always in (s_trig − v₀·Δt, s_trig]); `pair_draw_ordering`
(μ_lo ≤ μ_hi always). In `crates/frob-conformance`: `gate_shield_06` (the case above, on a 100-pair subset per
commit and the full counts before recording).

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified above; this bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: 0.400 m and 0.6796 m stopping distances; crossing speed 1.162 m/s; overshoot 0.2296 m; threshold
0.7196 m; speed limit 1.5696 m/s; mutant overshoot 0.0196 m from a tick at 0.70 m. Negative controls run through
the same script and the same tier run: (a) the mutant controller (must violate, by at most one period of travel;
N22-CX `reflex-intersample`); (b) a `shield.fit` build without the timeliness test (must admit Arm 1's listing,
failing clause 5; N22-CX `reflex-untimely`); (c) a tier with a contact law that reads μ while coasting (must void
the fixture at step 1). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts on `strict`; referee against
`strict` agree on every violation verdict and on margins within 1 mm.

## Considerations

- "Paired" means the two worlds of a pair share every draw except friction. The script refuses to compare runs
  whose seeds differ.
- If a draw gives μ_lo·g ≥ 5.0 the delivered deceleration is the commanded 5.0 m/s² in both worlds; with μ in
  [0.3, 0.8] the largest μ·g is 7.848, so such draws occur (μ_lo > 0.5097). They are not all non-violations: the
  trigger's 0.05 m margin is shorter than one period of travel v₀·Δt when v₀ > 2.5 m/s, so the braking tick can
  fall below v₀²/10 and the cart overshoots by up to v₀·Δt − 0.05 m (0.01 m at 3 m/s) even at full friction. The
  script therefore counts expected violations from the closed form m < 0, never from the friction alone, and
  logs the count of full-friction overshoots separately as `arm1_sampling_overshoots`.
- `--param` is a general input, also used by N22c-46 (the free slider's `m`, `v0` and its controller's `kd`, `h`,
  `latency_ticks`); the rule that a fixture's hash covers declared domains and not run values is N24b-03's, and
  fails closed: a changed domain or default is always 530.
- The gate is a known answer in the model; it makes no claim about real floors.
