## Background

The pre-slip aliasing fixture is a cart approaching a keep-out line in two worlds that differ only in friction
and deliver identical observations until braking begins. It tests one rule of the runtime shields: an assumption
whose monitor cannot put the state inside the backup controller's domain in time may not be listed as
monitored; its whole range goes inside the filter's quantifier. It also tests the sampled-data form of a backup
domain, D = {(s, v) : s ≥ v²/(2·μ_min·g) + v·Δt}, whose last term is the inter-sample margin.

The convergence gate, as its defining row states it:

> GATE-SHIELD-06 (Known answer, M4). On the referee and on the `strict` profile, over 1,000 paired episodes with
> v₀ drawn from [1, 3] m/s and friction pairs from [0.3, 0.8]: the two transcripts are bit-equal before the first
> braking tick in every pair. Arm 1 violates in the low-friction world in every episode whose stopping distance
> exceeds its trigger range, with the overshoot within 1 mm of the closed form. Arm 2 has no violation in 10⁴
> episodes. The mutant without the inter-sample term produces at least one violation, none larger than v₀·Δt.
> `shield.fit` refuses Arm 1's monitored-assumption listing and prints both admitting changes.

This bead is the script that measures it through the user's surface and writes the gate row.

## Scope

In: one script; the five clauses on two tiers; the gate row. Out: the fixture (N22c-27) and the rule (N22c-28).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/gate_shield_06.sh`; seed from `FROB_E2E_SEED` (default 1); idempotent; non-zero exit on any
failed assertion. It runs every step on `--tier referee` and on `--tier batch-cpu --profile strict`.

Constants: g = 9.81 m/s²; commanded deceleration 5.0 m/s²; Δt = 0.02 s; belief set μ ∈ [0.3, 0.8], μ_min = 0.3.
Draws, from counter-based streams keyed by (seed, episode): v₀ uniform in [1, 3] m/s; μ_hi and μ_lo uniform in
[0.3, 0.8] with μ_lo ≤ μ_hi; a start range that sets the sampling phase.

1. **Pairs and transcripts.** For 1,000 pairs: `frob sim run fixture:pre-slip-aliasing --controller
   arm1-nominal-monitor --world mu=<μ_hi>` and the same with `mu=<μ_lo>`; `frob telemetry divergence` on the two
   delivered-packet streams. Assert the first divergence is at or after the first braking tick in every pair
   (`transcript_equal_pairs` = 1,000). A pair that diverges earlier voids the fixture on that tier and fails the
   script.
2. **Arm 1.** For each pair, the low-friction run. Closed form: s_trig = v₀²/(2·5.0) + 0.05; s_b the range at the
   first tick with s ≤ s_trig; a = min(5.0, μ_lo·g); m = s_b − v₀²/(2·a). Assert: whenever v₀²/(2·a) > s_trig the
   episode is a violation; and in every episode abs(m_sim − m) ≤ 0.001 m. Log the count of violating episodes.
3. **Arm 2.** 10⁴ episodes (the 2,000 runs of the pairs' worlds plus 8,000 further draws) with `--controller
   arm2-belief-shield`. Assert zero episodes cross the line. Log the distribution of the stop margin (the cost).
4. **Mutant.** The same 10⁴ draws with `--controller mutant-continuous-barrier`. Assert at least one violation,
   and that no overshoot exceeds v₀·Δt for its episode.
5. **`shield.fit`.** `frob shield fit fixture:pre-slip-aliasing --listing arm1 --json`: assert `ok: false`, the
   refusal slug `monitored-assumption-not-timely`, exit 3, and a payload with the first distinguishing tick, the
   state there, and two admitting changes; at v₀ = 2.0 m/s assert the changes read `brake by s ≤ 0.7196 m` and
   `speed limit 1.5697 m/s` (to four decimals). Then `--listing arm2`: assert it fits.
6. **`shield.check`.** Replay one Arm 1 low-friction run: assert a `late-alarm` finding with its distance to the
   backup domain.
7. **Record.** One gate row per tier with the measured counts and the largest closed-form error, the commit, the
   command lines, the output digest and the receipt.

**Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/gate_shield_06/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every
line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22c-29`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim Known answer, class `Tested`, tier, determinism, identities, seed). Metrics with
thresholds: `transcript_equal_pairs` (1,000 of 1,000), `arm1_expected_violations` and `arm1_violations` (equal),
`arm1_overshoot_err_max_m` (≤ 0.001), `arm2_violations` (0 of 10⁴), `arm2_margin_m` (median and maximum),
`mutant_violations` (≥ 1), `mutant_overshoot_over_v0dt_max` (≤ 1), `fit_refused` (true), `admitting_changes` (2).
On failure: the episode index, its draws, the first differing packet and the run's file paths. A `summary`
line: assertions passed and failed; gate GATE-SHIELD-06 with its status per tier.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 only when all five clauses hold on both tiers.
- [ ] The gate register holds rows for GATE-SHIELD-06 on `referee` and on `strict` with measured values, commit and receipt.
- [ ] The hand case at v₀ = 2.0 m/s, μ = (0.8, 0.3) is among the episodes (episode 0 is pinned to it) and reproduces 0.2296 m, 0.7196 m and 1.5697 m/s.
- [ ] The negative controls below have recorded failing runs.
- [ ] Rerunning with the same seed reproduces every count and digest.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Helpers in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `closed_form_margin` (v₀ = 2.0, μ = 0.3, s_b = 0.45 gives m = −0.229579);
`sampling_phase_range` (s_b always in (s_trig − v₀·Δt, s_trig]); `pair_draw_ordering` (μ_lo ≤ μ_hi always).

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified above; this bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: 0.400 m and 0.6796 m stopping distances; crossing speed 1.162 m/s; overshoot 0.2296 m; threshold
0.7196 m; speed limit 1.5697 m/s; mutant overshoot 0.0196 m from a tick at 0.70 m. Negative controls run through
the same script: (a) the mutant controller (must violate, by at most one period of travel); (b) a `shield.fit`
build without the timeliness test (must admit Arm 1's listing, failing clause 5); (c) a tier with a contact law
that reads μ while coasting (must void the fixture at step 1). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across
worker counts on `strict`; referee against `strict` agree on every violation verdict and on margins within 1 mm.

## Considerations

- "Paired" means the two worlds of a pair share every draw except friction. The script refuses to compare runs
  whose seeds differ.
- If a draw gives μ_lo·g ≥ 5.0 the low-friction world also holds and Arm 1 does not violate; with μ in
  [0.3, 0.8] the largest μ·g is 7.848, so such draws occur (μ_lo > 0.5097) and are counted as expected
  non-violations when the stopping distance is within the trigger.
- The gate is a known answer in the model; it makes no claim about real floors.
