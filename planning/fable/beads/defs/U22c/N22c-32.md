## Background

The sampling-based predictive controller (kind `mpc-sample`) samples command sequences at every tick and rolls
them out on a model. As an evaluator of designs it is refused (FROB-REF-817): a few hundred episodes under it
cost more than training once, and its plant equals its model. The refusal guards one substitution: reporting a
sampling-controller score as if a trained policy had earned it.

The **delivered-controller lane** (Frontier) covers the case the refusal was not written for: the mission's
delivered controller is itself a sampling controller. An accepted answer is a design, a controller and an
evidence package; when the controller in the package is `mpc-sample`, episodes under it are the only evidence
about it. The lane admits those episodes as the scoring source of acceptance, promotion and qualification,
**for that planner record only**, when four conditions hold. Otherwise the refusal stands.

Why the first condition is not a formality. A cart at 2 m/s must stop before a wall on a floor whose friction
is drawn from {0.2, 0.8}; stopping distance is v²/(2·μ·g). A planner that reads the plant's draw brakes at
1.019 m on the slippery floor and 0.255 m on the grippy one and never fails. The same planner holding the
nominal μ = 0.5 begins braking 0.408 m from the wall and overruns by 0.612 m (1.0194 − 0.4077) on every slippery
world, half of the draws, unless its estimator detects the floor in time. The privileged run reports zero
failures in 299 episodes for a controller that fails every second episode.

Ceiling, stated first: no speed benefit at any published setting (299 failure-free episodes for a 1% bound at
95%; break-even at 2.0 GPU-seconds per quadruped episode and 12.0 per humanoid episode, against about 20 and 62
at published settings). The benefit is a capability: such a design can be accepted at all, with its price on the
ledger.

## Scope

In: the task or mission declaration; the four conditions as an admission check on episodes; the amended refusal
with the missing condition named; the verdict string; voiding on a change of controller; the phase-ledger
charge; the planted input of the counterexample `reflex-privileged`; this bead's own script. Out: the gate's
measurement on the cart, the corpus and the named computers (N22c-33); the planner record, freezing and the latency model (N22c-31); the qualification lane itself (seed
bead BD-259); the promotion rule (BD-263); the revalidation engine (BD-292).

## Technical approach

**Declaration.** A task (`.ftask`) or mission (`.fmission`) may declare `controller.delivered = mpc-sample`
(DESIGN). The declaration requires a frozen planner record (schema `frob.control.planner/1`), whose digest is
part of the `ControllerId`. Without the declaration, nothing in this bead applies.

**Admission check (owner: `frob-arena`), applied to every scoring episode of an acceptance, promotion or
qualification request whose controller kind is `mpc-sample`.** The episodes are admitted for that record only
when all four hold:

1. **Not model-privileged.** The planning model's parameters do not come from the plant's randomization draw,
   and `state_source = estimated`. An episode flagged `model-privileged` is refused as acceptance evidence.
   Check: the record's `parameter_source ∈ {nominal, estimated}`, `state_source = estimated`, and the episode's
   flag is absent.
2. **A plant the planner does not own.** The plant runs on the tier the battery names and is promoted to the
   referee by the trust ladder's promotion rule, as for any controller (a result on a fast tier is
   `provisional`; it becomes `established` after re-evaluation of a sample of episodes on a higher tier with the
   difference inside the promotion envelope; one that fails is `provisional: tier-sensitive`). The planner keeps
   its own model and its own planning step. Check: the episode's plant tier equals the battery's; the planner's
   `planning_model` and step are those of the record on every tier, including the referee re-evaluation.
3. **Measured latency.** The record's `compute_target` carries a measurement. The planner's output is delayed by
   the measured 99th-percentile planning time, rounded up to whole control periods, and a tick that misses its
   deadline holds the previous plan. A record with a modelled or zero delay is refused for acceptance. Check:
   `compute_target.measured = true`, and the episode's applied delay equals the record's.
4. **The same bar.** The battery, the requirement verdicts and the failure bound are those of any other
   controller, and the cost is charged to the phase ledger in real GPU-seconds and CPU-seconds. Check: the
   request's battery and failure bound are the task's standard ones (no lane-specific relaxation), and a
   ledger entry exists for the episodes.

**One check site.** This check is not a second gate beside the evidence boundary: it is installed as that
boundary's lane hook (N22a-25, a dependency), `lane.admits(task, controller_record, episodes) -> Admit |
Missing(condition)`, in place of the default hook that always answers `no-record`. The boundary consults it
twice, at admission on the controller record the request names (`bind_ahead`, before a commitment is written or
a level spent; there checks 1 and 3 and the declaration are decidable and checks 2 and 4 are deferred) and when
the verdict is written on the recorded episodes (`bind`, all four). The hook reports the first failing item in
this order: `no-record` (no declaration, or no frozen record digest in the `ControllerId`), `model-privileged`,
`plant-owned`, `latency-unmeasured`, `bar-differs`.

**Refusal.** An acceptance request whose scoring episodes are `mpc-sample` without a frozen, non-privileged,
latency-measured planner record is refused under FROB-REF-817 (exit 3), with the missing condition named in the
payload: `no-record`, `model-privileged`, `plant-owned`, `latency-unmeasured` or `bar-differs`. Repairs: train or
fine-tune a policy for the finalist; keep the sampling result as screening; or (for a declared lane) the
specific fix, such as re-freezing with estimated state or measuring latency on a named computer.

**Verdict.** The verdict names what was accepted: `accepted with delivered controller
mpc-sample@<record digest>`. It says nothing about a trained policy for the same design. A later change of
controller (a new record digest, or a different controller kind) voids it through the revalidation engine: the
verdict's dependency on the `ControllerId` is recorded, and `result.revalidate` reports it stale.

**Consumers.** `eval.qualify` consumes the record: a committed request to the blinded lane carries the record
digest; the receipt cites it. The cascade and the ladder treat a delivered-controller verdict as evidence about
(design, record) only, never as a score comparable with trained-policy scores of other designs.

**Ledger.** The phase ledger prints the lane's cost for the request (episodes × measured GPU-seconds per
episode) beside the cost of training once for the same design class, so that the price of the capability is
visible in every report.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With the declaration and a record meeting all four conditions, a qualification request over `mpc-sample` episodes is admitted and returns `accepted with delivered controller mpc-sample@<digest>` when the battery's bar is met.
- [ ] Each of the five failing cases is refused with FROB-REF-817 and its named condition.
- [ ] Without the declaration, every `mpc-sample` acceptance, promotion or qualification request is refused as before.
- [ ] The cart known answer holds: the privileged run is refused as evidence; the nominal-model planner overruns by 0.612 m within 1% on the slippery draws.
- [ ] Changing the planner record or the controller kind marks the verdict stale under revalidation.
- [ ] Every delivered-controller verdict on a fast tier is either promoted on the referee plant or labelled `tier-sensitive`; none remains unlabelled.
- [ ] The phase ledger shows the lane's cost and the cost of training once side by side for each such request.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-arena/tests/delivered_lane.rs` (real components; the cart is a `synthetic` fixture):
- `lane_admits_conforming_record`: all four checks pass; the verdict string carries the digest.
- `lane_refuses_each_missing_condition`: five cases, five payloads.
- `no_declaration_no_lane`: the pre-existing refusal fires.
- `hook_is_the_only_check_site`: the lane's decision is reached only through N22a-25's `bind_ahead` and `bind`
  (the arena has no other function that admits `mpc-sample` episodes for an evidence purpose); `bind_ahead`
  refuses a privileged or latency-unmeasured record before any commitment, and a record that passes
  `bind_ahead` but whose episodes ran on another tier than the battery's is refused by `bind` with `plant-owned`.
- `cart_braking_distances`: 4/(2·0.2·9.81) = 1.0194 m; 4/(2·0.8·9.81) = 0.2548 m; 4/(2·0.5·9.81) = 0.4077 m; overrun 0.6117 m.
- `cart_privileged_never_fails`: 299 privileged episodes, zero failures, and the request over them is refused with `model-privileged`.
- `cart_nominal_fails_on_slippery`: with μ drawn from {0.2, 0.8} the nominal planner overruns on every μ = 0.2 world by 0.612 m within 1%.
- `verdict_voided_by_new_record`: a changed budget gives a new digest and a stale verdict.
- `planner_keeps_its_model_on_referee`: during promotion the planner's model identity and step are unchanged while the plant tier changes.
- `lane_cost_on_ledger`: the ledger entry equals episodes × measured GPU-seconds, and the training-once figure is printed beside it.

### Known answers and negative controls
The cart (1.019 m, 0.255 m, 0.408 m, 0.612 m; 299 episodes). Arithmetic: 600/299 = 2.0 and 3,600/299 = 12.0
GPU-seconds per episode. Negative controls: the privileged run offered as evidence (must be refused; the
counterexample `reflex-privileged` of N22-CX, whose planted input this bead registers as
`N22c-32:privileged_run_offered` in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml`, bound to GATE-REFLEX-11 and
run again by N22c-33's tier run); a lane
build that checks the controller kind's label instead of the identity (episodes relabelled `baseline` must still
be refused); a record with zero delay (must be refused). Determinism: admission decisions are exact and
identical on every platform; episodes replay under the device or reference replay profile.

## Considerations

- The lane must not become a general exemption. The invariant "sampling-controller results never enter
  acceptance, promotion or qualification evidence" is amended to "except under this lane, for that record only",
  and the conformance battery tests the amended form.
- Kill gate KILL-MPCRECORD (more than 10% of delivered-controller verdicts reverse at the referee; or no named
  computer meets the deadline in 99% of ticks for any class on the pilot set) withdraws the lane: the rule
  reverts to screening and fallback only, and a mission that requires a sampling controller returns
  `provisional: controller-not-qualifiable` with its screening episodes.
- "Estimated" parameters must come from an estimator that ran on delivered observations; an estimate seeded from
  the plant's draw is `plant` by another name and is refused.
