## Background

The delivered-controller lane lets a mission whose deliverable controller is a sampling-based predictive
controller (kind `mpc-sample`) be accepted on that controller's own episodes, under a frozen planner record and
four conditions: not model-privileged; a plant the planner does not own (promoted to the referee like any
controller); measured latency; and the same bar, with the cost on the phase ledger. Its gate:

| Gate | Claim | Threshold (TARGET) | First milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| GATE-REFLEX-11 | Known answer; Fidelity | The cart: the privileged run is refused as evidence, and the nominal-model planner overruns by 0.612 m within 1% on the slippery draws. On the M3 corpus, every delivered-controller verdict on the fast tier survives promotion to the referee plant or is labelled `tier-sensitive`; the measured 99th-percentile planning time fits the control period on the named computer in at least 99% of ticks; the phase ledger prints the lane's cost beside the cost of training once | M3 |

This bead is the script that measures the gate from the user's side, and the bead that has the gate entered:
the row is entered through the gate recorder from the tier run's receipt, never written by the script.

## Scope

In: one script with four clause groups; the cart fixture; the method-experiment entry for the cart's 299
privileged episodes; negative controls; the conformance case whose tier run produces the gate's receipt; the M3
part recorded from it. Out: the mechanisms (N22c-31, N22c-32); the kill decision (N22c-34).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/gate_reflex_11.sh`; seed from `FROB_E2E_SEED` (default 1); idempotent; non-zero exit on any
failed assertion. Every run-kind call (`control mpc`, `eval qualify`, `eval promote`) carries `--key
gr11-<clause>-<design>-<step>`; two runs that are compared (the privileged and the nominal planner on the same
world draws) never share a key. Order of execution: clause C's latency measurement runs first, because a record
cannot be frozen without a measurement (N22c-31), then A, B and D.

**A. The cart (Known answer).** The fixture `cart-wall-two-frictions`, delivered and registered by this bead
in N24b-03's fixture registry (`assets/fixtures/cart-wall-two-frictions/fixture.toml`, kind `synthetic`, its
constants hashed before the first run): the design `cart-wall` (`cart-wall.fbot` beside the manifest: a one-axis
cart with a braking drive and a wall ahead, its friction a randomized parameter drawn from {0.2, 0.8}) and the task
`cart-stop` (`cart-stop.ftask`: start at 2 m/s, requirement stop-before-wall), entered in the session with `frob
design new cart-wall --file assets/fixtures/cart-wall-two-frictions/cart-wall.fbot --key gr11-A-new --json` and
`frob task new assets/fixtures/cart-wall-two-frictions/cart-stop.ftask --key gr11-A-task --json`. A cart at 2 m/s
must stop before a wall; friction drawn from {0.2, 0.8}; g = 9.81 m/s²; stopping distance v²/(2·μ·g).
1. Privileged planner (`parameter_source = plant`): 299 episodes, run as `frob control mpc cart-wall --task
   cart-stop --planning-model plant --state privileged --episodes 299 --protocol <id> --seed $SEED --key
   gr11-A-priv --json`. The 299 episodes exceed the screening allowance of three per design, so the cart clause
   is registered as an experiment in the method-experiment register (N38a-07) whose frozen protocol declares 299
   episodes for this fixture, and `--protocol` names it (N22a-24's rule 1); the episodes stay labelled
   `screening: sampling-control` and `model-privileged`. Assert zero failures. Then offer that controller as
   evidence in the two ways the surface allows, and assert exit 3 and FROB-REF-817 for each: (i) `frob control
   mpc cart-wall --task cart-stop --planning-model plant --state privileged --freeze --key gr11-A-priv-freeze
   --json`, refused with the condition `model-privileged` (a privileged configuration cannot be frozen); (ii)
   `frob eval qualify --candidate design=cart-wall,controller=<the privileged ControllerId> --task cart-stop
   --claim 'failure probability of stop-before-wall at most 0.01' --key gr11-A-priv-qual --json`, refused at
   admission with the condition the lane hook reports for a controller with no frozen record (`no-record`),
   with no commitment written and no level spent.
2. Nominal-model planner (μ = 0.5 in the planning model, estimated state, measured latency in the record):
   `frob control mpc cart-wall --task cart-stop --planning-model nominal --state estimated --latency
   measured:<computer> --freeze --episodes 299 --seed $SEED --key gr11-A-nominal --json` (the freeze exception
   admits the count; per-world telemetry is needed here, which the blinded qualification lane does not return), on the
   same 299 world draws; assert braking begins 0.408 m from the wall (4/(2·0.5·9.81) = 0.4077 m) within one
   control period of travel; assert the overrun on every μ = 0.2 world is 0.612 m within 1% (1.0194 − 0.4077 =
   0.6117 m; accept 0.6056 to 0.6178 m), and that every μ = 0.8 world stops short.
3. Assert the privileged braking distances: 1.019 m at μ = 0.2 and 0.255 m at μ = 0.8, within one control
   period of travel.

**B. Promotion on the corpus (Fidelity).** For each corpus design whose task declares `controller.delivered =
mpc-sample` (the gate's corpus subset; at least one design per morphology class that has a sampling
controller): `frob control mpc <design> --task <task> --planning-model nominal --state estimated --latency
measured:<computer> --freeze --key gr11-B-<design>-freeze --json`, then the battery on the fast tier through
`frob eval qualify <design> --task <task> --claim '<the task's standard claim>' --key gr11-B-<design>-qual
--json` (the request carries the record's digest; the verdict is `accepted with delivered controller
mpc-sample@<digest>` or a failure of the same bar as any controller), then `frob eval promote <result> --tier
referee --key gr11-B-<design>-prom --json` to re-evaluate a sample of episodes on the referee plant with the
planner unchanged. Assert every verdict ends `established` or `provisional: tier-sensitive`; count the reversals (a
verdict that changes between tiers). Log `verdicts`, `established`, `tier_sensitive`, `reversal_fraction`.

**C. Planning time (run first).** For each configuration to be frozen: `frob control mpc <design> --task <task>
--measure-latency --computer <name> --key gr11-C-<design>-<computer> --json` over at least 10⁴ ticks. Assert the share of ticks whose planning time fits the control period is at least 0.99 on the
named computer; log median, 99th percentile and the delay in whole periods.

**D. Ledger.** `frob run show <run> --phase-ledger --json` on the runs of each request (`run.show`'s phase
ledger, N21a-27's step profile; `eval.ledger` is the qualification campaign's index and levels spent, read here
only to log the levels): assert it prints the lane's cost (GPU-seconds and CPU-seconds actually charged) and,
beside it, the cost of training once for the design's class. Log both and their ratio, and the break-even reference 600/299 = 2.0 and 3,600/299 = 12.0
GPU-seconds per episode.

**Record.** The gate is measured by its tier run, which the script's last step runs as `e2e_tier
g1_known_answers GATE-REFLEX-11 --mutations` (that is, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance
--test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-REFLEX-11`: this bead's case `gate_reflex_11`, the
cart entries and the registered controls (a) to (d) below, caught in the same run; the promotion, timing and
ledger clauses read this script's logged values), which writes a hash-named receipt. A session that did not
implement the lane enters the M3 part through the gate recorder (N38b-19): `cargo run -p frob-conformance
--example record_gate -- --gate GATE-REFLEX-11 --receipt <hash> --part M3`. The row carries: cart overrun and its error, the two refusal checks, counts of verdicts by label, the
reversal fraction, the deadline-fit share per computer, and the ledger figures; commit, command lines, output
digest, receipt. The script prints the tier-run command and the receipt hash and enters nothing itself.

**Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/gate_reflex_11/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every
line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22c-33`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the
envelope's `evidence` block including the planner record digest and the machine fingerprint. Metrics with
thresholds: `privileged_failures` (0 of 299), `privileged_refused` (true), `nominal_overrun_m` (0.612 ± 1%),
`unlabelled_verdicts` (0), `reversal_fraction` (reported; kill threshold 0.10), `ticks_within_deadline`
(≥ 0.99), `lane_gpu_s`, `train_once_gpu_s`. On failure: the first differing value, the world draw, the episode
file paths. A `summary` line: assertions passed and failed; gate GATE-REFLEX-11 with its status.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 only when clauses A to D hold.
- [ ] The gate register holds a row for GATE-REFLEX-11 with measured values, commit and receipt, entered through the gate recorder from a receipt in which the four registered controls are `caught`, by a session that did not implement the lane.
- [ ] The privileged controller is refused as evidence in both forms of clause A.1 (at `--freeze` with `model-privileged`; at `eval qualify` at admission, with no level spent), and its 299 screening episodes are admitted only under the registered protocol: the same request without `--protocol` is refused with the worded refusal `mpc-episode-cap`.
- [ ] The reversal fraction and the deadline-fit share are in the log in the form the kill decision reads.
- [ ] The negative controls below have recorded failing runs.
- [ ] No log line contains private-scene data; corpus designs are named by identity only.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Helpers in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `overrun_window` (0.6117 m ± 1% is [0.6056, 0.6178]); `reversal_fraction`
(3 reversals in 40 verdicts is 0.075); `deadline_share` (9,950 of 10,000 ticks inside is 0.995, passing; 9,890
is 0.989, failing); `ledger_ratio`.

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified above; this bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: braking distances 1.019 m, 0.255 m and 0.408 m; overrun 0.612 m; 299 episodes; 2.0 and 12.0
GPU-seconds. Negative controls through the same script: (a) a lane build that admits the privileged run must
fail clause A (N22-CX `reflex-privileged`; A.1 (ii) is N22c-32's planted input `N22c-32:privileged_run_offered`
on the cart); (b) a planner record with zero delay must be refused at freeze; (c) a promotion that swaps the
planner's model for the referee's must be caught by the identity check (the planner must keep its own model);
(d) a ledger report that omits the training-once figure must fail clause D. Determinism lanes: the cart and the
corpus episodes replay bit for bit on one machine and across worker counts; planning time is a measurement and
is reported with its spread.

## Considerations

- The gate's row names two claims, Known answer and Fidelity. The register's rule is one claim per gate with the
  governing part named; here the cart is the known-answer precondition and the promotion clause governs
  (Fidelity). The row is recorded with that reading until the register's tables are regenerated.
- If no corpus task declares a delivered sampling controller, clause B has nothing to measure and the gate row is
  `not run`, which is not a pass; the script then fails with that message.
- Planning-time measurements depend on thermal state and co-tenancy; the fingerprint records both.
