## Background

A kill gate is a decision named in advance with its trigger, measurement and fallback; a kill gate that fires is
not a failure, because the fallback is the plan. Its decision is recorded as an ADR. A kill gate with no
milestone of its own is decided at the milestone of the gate beside it: here M3, with GATE-REFLEX-11.

KILL-MPCRECORD governs the delivered-controller lane: the exception by which episodes of a sampling-based
predictive controller count as acceptance evidence when that controller is itself what the mission delivers,
under a frozen planner record and four conditions (not model-privileged; a plant the planner does not own;
measured latency; the same bar with the cost on the ledger). The lane exists for a capability, not for speed.
It is worth keeping only if its verdicts hold up on the referee and if some real computer can run the planner
inside the control period.

## Scope

In: applying the two trigger clauses, recording the ADR, stating the downstream changes. Out: the pilot
(N22c-30), the mechanisms (N22c-31, N22c-32) and the gate measurement (N22c-33).

## Technical approach

**Trigger.** Either:
1. more than 10% of delivered-controller verdicts reverse at the referee; or
2. no named computer meets the deadline in 99% of ticks for any class on the pilot set.

**Milestone.** M3.

**Measurement that decides it.** Clause 1 from N22c-33: over the corpus designs with a declared delivered
sampling controller, the fraction of verdicts that change when a sample of episodes is re-evaluated on the
referee plant with the planner unchanged (a verdict labelled `tier-sensitive` whose two tiers disagree on
acceptance is a reversal). Clause 2 from N22c-30 and N22c-33: per named computer and per class on the pilot
design set (thirty designs: quadruped and humanoid variants and five wheeled), the share of ticks whose planning
time fits the control period; the clause holds when that share is below 0.99 for every computer and every
class.

**Decision rule.**
0. The pilot (N22c-30) was falsified → **fires** at that point with the pilot's numbers (its deadline clause is
   this gate's clause 2): N22c-31, N22c-32 and N22c-33 are not funded and are closed `not-funded` citing the pilot
   record, which releases this decision from its dependency on N22c-33. If the pilot was not run, because the
   sampling-controller pilot N22a-22 was falsified, nothing was measured: the outcome is `not-reached` (N40-03:
   the mechanism was not funded, its pilot was not run), citing N22a-22's pilot record, and the three beads are
   closed `not-funded` the same way.
1. Reversal fraction ≤ 0.10 and at least one (computer, class) pair meets the deadline in at least 99% of ticks
   → `not fired`. The lane is offered only for the classes and computers that meet the deadline; others are
   refused at freeze with `latency-unmeasured` or a deadline finding.
2. Reversal fraction > 0.10 → **fires**.
3. No pair meets the deadline → **fires**.
4. Fewer than ten verdicts in the measurement: the fraction is reported with its interval and the decision is
   `blocked: insufficient verdicts`, which keeps the lane closed (fail-closed) until more exist. In the gate
   register this is the outcome `deferred` (one of the four outcomes a kill row may carry: `not-fired`, `fired`,
   `not-reached`, `deferred`), with the reason "insufficient verdicts" and the later exit named (M4); the gate
   it reads stays `not-run` and the lane stays unavailable until the deferred decision is taken.

**Fallback, named in advance.** The rule of the sampling controller as first written: screening and fallback
only. A mission that requires a sampling controller returns `provisional: controller-not-qualifiable` with its
screening episodes.

**What changes downstream when it fires.**
- The declaration `controller.delivered = mpc-sample` is refused at task and mission admission, citing the ADR;
  `control.mpc --freeze` is withdrawn; FROB-REF-817 reverts to its unconditional form for acceptance, promotion
  and qualification.
- Existing verdicts of the form `accepted with delivered controller mpc-sample@<digest>` are marked stale by the
  revalidation engine and re-reported as `provisional: controller-not-qualifiable`.
- The invariant "sampling-controller results never enter acceptance, promotion or qualification evidence"
  returns to its unamended form, and the conformance case is switched back.
- Screening by the sampling controller, its out-of-distribution fallback role and its use as a demonstration
  source are unaffected (those are governed by KILL-MPC).

**Record.** The decision ADR is `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-MPCRECORD-M3.md` (every kill-gate decision is
`docs/adr/ADR-<gate-name>-<milestone>.md`) with the fields the kill-gate record requires (N40-03): the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered and the amendments applied; each measured value with its receipt hash, set beside the trigger's number (measured values, never the targets); the outcome; the fallback now in force and the name the capability carries from now on; the beads re-read, each either "stands on the fallback" or "superseded by this ADR"; what changes downstream; commit, hardware fingerprint and seeds of the measurement; and the recording session, which must not be a session that implemented the mechanism or ran the measurement; here the reversal fraction with its interval and verdict count, and the per-computer, per-class
deadline shares. The kill row of KILL-MPCRECORD in the gate register (`registries/gates.toml`, `kind = "kill"`)
receives `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }` with `outcome = "not-fired"` (rule 1), `"fired"`
(rules 0, 2 and 3), `"not-reached"` (rule 0's pilot not run) or `"deferred"` (rule 4, adding `deferred_to = "M4"`
and `reason`), and `receipts` the hash of N22c-33's receipt (required for `not-fired`; under rule 0's `fired`
the `receipt` of N22c-30's `[[pilot.result]]`, committed at `receipts/pilots/<hash>.json` (N38a-09) and resolved
there by N40-03; under `not-reached` none); "not fired" and "fires" in this bead are the first two register values.
GATE-REFLEX-11's own status was entered from N22c-33's receipt through the gate recorder; when the outcome is
`fired` the register's `apply_decisions` gives it the status `killed` with the detail "KILL-MPCRECORD: screening
and fallback only". This bead writes no gate status by hand.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-MPCRECORD-M3.md` exists with every required field and cites the measured reversal fraction, the verdict count and the deadline shares per named computer and class, with the receipt hash.
- [ ] The kill row of KILL-MPCRECORD carries `decision` with outcome `not-fired`, `fired`, `not-reached` or `deferred`, the ADR path, the date, the session and the receipt hash, and the register's load checks accept it (a `deferred` outcome names its later exit and reason); GATE-REFLEX-11 shows its measured values and, when the gate fired, the status `killed` through `apply_decisions`.
- [ ] If fired: tests show the declaration refused at admission, `--freeze` withdrawn, a mission that requires a sampling controller returning `provisional: controller-not-qualifiable` with its screening episodes, and old lane verdicts stale.
- [ ] If not fired: the list of (computer, class) pairs for which the lane is offered is in the record.
- [ ] The decision is made by a session that did not run the measurements.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_mpcrecord_decision.rs` (the rule as a pure function of the recorded numbers; fixtures labelled `synthetic`):
- `kill_mpcrecord_rule_table`: (0.05 reversals of 40; one pair at 0.995) → not fired; (0.15 of 40) → fired; (0.05; best pair 0.97) → fired; (2 of 6 verdicts) → blocked (register outcome `deferred` to M4, reason "insufficient verdicts"), lane closed; (pilot falsified, gate not run) → fired with the pilot's numbers, and the record names N22c-31, N22c-32 and N22c-33 as `not-funded`; (pilot not run, N22a-22 falsified) → `not-reached`, with no receipt.
- `fallback_status_string`: a mission requiring the controller returns exactly `provisional: controller-not-qualifiable`.
- `old_verdicts_go_stale`: after the kill, revalidation reports lane verdicts stale.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_mpcrecord_record.sh`: reads the gate's log and receipt, the pilot's record and the gate
register, asserts agreement, applies the rule, checks that the kill row's `decision` and the ADR say the same,
and exercises the fallback when fired (`frob control mpc <design> --task <task> --freeze --key kmrr-freeze
--json` must be refused citing the ADR; a mission that requires a sampling controller must report exactly
`provisional: controller-not-qualifiable` with its screening episodes). The script sources the shared helper of STD-E2E, is idempotent (it reads logs, receipts and the register and writes only its own log), and exits non-zero on the first failed assertion after its summary. Log: `target/e2e-logs/kill_mpcrecord_record/<utc>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step` and `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`); `cmd` lines carry the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block; `metric` lines carry the value, its unit and its threshold; `assert` lines carry observed and expected, and on failure the first differing value with the paths of the measurement log, the receipt, the register row and the ADR. Metrics: `reversal_fraction` (threshold 0.10) with `verdicts`, `ticks_within_deadline.<computer>.<class>` (threshold 0.99); then the decision, the ADR path and the fallback status of one mission. Declared plants (STD-E2E header `plants`, switched by `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE`, checked by `run-all.sh --verify-plants`): `targets-as-measured` (a copy of the row whose measured fields hold the thresholds: the agreement assertion must fail) and `decided-before-measurement` (a decision dated before the receipt: the ordering assertion must fail). Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed, plants caught, `gates: ["KILL-MPCRECORD", "GATE-REFLEX-11"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
Rule fixtures above. Negative control: a record with targets in place of measured values is rejected by the
registry check.

## Considerations

- One step more urgent than its milestone: task admission and the conformance case for the amended invariant
  depend on it.
- If KILL-MPC later reduces the sampling controller to a single-world CPU debugging aid, the lane can only be
  served on that path; the decision is then revisited by a dated amendment, not silently.
- The break-even arithmetic (2.0 and 12.0 GPU-seconds per episode against about 20 and 62 at published settings)
  is not a trigger: the lane was never justified by speed.
