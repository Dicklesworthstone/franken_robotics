## Background

`frob-gait` computes whether a gait exists as a periodic orbit of the closed loop (a design under its
controller), whether it is stable, how it moves under design changes and how stride metrics change with design
parameters. Its first formulation strobes a **clock-forced** loop over one stride of a known period: the
controller's gait clock forces the loop, the stride is N control periods, and the gait is a fixed point of the
stroboscopic map P = F^N with monodromy M = D P.

A passive walker, a controller whose phase is reset by touchdown, and any loop that reads no clock have no such
period. On an orbit of an **autonomous** loop with period T*, a state displaced along the flow returns displaced
by the same amount, so the vector field f(x*) is an eigenvector of the time-T* monodromy with multiplier exactly
1, for every design and every controller. Consequences for the clock-forced equations: I − M is singular; the
fold test of continuation ("a multiplier at +1 in the trivial block") fires on every orbit; the gradient solve
(I − M)ᵀ·λ = ∇J has no solution or infinitely many; and that multiplier says nothing about stability. Without a
remedy, every event-driven controller would be reported as sitting on a fold.

This bead adds the classification that detects the case, the refusal that stops the wrong solve, and the first
remedy: the **section form**. The second remedy, the bordered form, is N22c-37. Frontier. This bead is built
whatever the pilot's (N22c-35) verdict: KILL-FREEPERIOD's fallback, named in advance, is "autonomous loops are
analysed on an event section only", which is exactly this bead without N22c-37, and the classification and the
refusal stay in force when the gate fires. The pilot decides only whether N22c-37 is funded. (An earlier draft
made this bead conditional on the pilot too, which would have left the fallback with nothing to run.)

## Scope

In: the phase source in `ControllerId`; loop classification; the refusal; the return map on a section and its
Jacobian; multipliers, fold test and stability stated on the section; the record's removed coordinates; `gait.orbit`
on autonomous loops with a named event; the synthetic fixture `fixture:planar-fold` (below). Out: the bordered form, the stride-time derivative and the adjoint for
loops with no section (N22c-37); the gate script (N22c-38).

## Technical approach

**Classification (DESIGN).** A `ControllerId` declares its phase source: `clock`, `event-reset` or `none`. A loop
is `clock-forced` only under `clock`, and `autonomous` otherwise. The field is part of the identity's hash.

**Refusal.** `gait.orbit` refuses a stroboscopic solve on an autonomous loop (worded refusal "fixed-period
shooting on a loop with a free period", slug `fixed-period-shooting-on-free-period`, exit 3). Payload: the phase
source, and the transverse events available as sections. Repairs: solve on a named section; or solve the
bordered form. The refusal is distinct from the `fold` outcome and must never be reported as one.

**Section form.** Where the stride has a repeatable transverse event with guard g(x) = 0 (touchdown of a named
foot; a phase variable crossing a threshold; on compliant tiers, a contact force crossing a threshold), the
orbit is a fixed point of the return map P_Σ on the section Σ = {g = 0}, of dimension one less than the state:

1. **Shooting.** Unknown x ∈ Σ, parametrized by an orthonormal basis V of the tangent space of Σ at the current
   iterate (the columns of V span the complement of ∇g). Residual r(x) = P_Σ(x) − x after removal of the
   symmetry coordinates (horizontal translation, and yaw when heading is not tracked) and with the twisted
   condition of a spatio-temporal symmetry where one applies. Newton with a line search, seeded as for the
   clock-forced solve. The return time T(x) is an output: it is the stride time.
2. **Jacobian.** M is the tangent map of the flow from Σ back to Σ at the nominal return time: on compliant tiers
   the product of step Jacobians, with the last step cut at the guard crossing; on the referee the product of
   flow tangent maps and saltation matrices. The section Jacobian is the saltation product projected along the
   flow:

       D P_Σ = (I − f⁺·∇gᵀ/(∇gᵀ·f⁺))·M   restricted to the tangent space of Σ,

   in coordinates Vᵀ·(I − f⁺·∇gᵀ/(∇gᵀ·f⁺))·M·V, an (n − 1) × (n − 1) matrix, where f⁺ is the vector field just
   after the event. The denominator ∇gᵀ·f⁺ is the transversality number; below its threshold the event is
   grazing and the request is refused with FROB-REF-431, returning the one-sided estimates.
3. **Multipliers.** Those of M without the trivial one. Stability, folds and symmetry-breaking are stated on Σ:
   a fold is a multiplier of D P_Σ at +1 in the trivial symmetry block; the block attribution by isotypic
   projectors is unchanged.
4. **Gradient equation on Σ.** For a stride metric J, (I − D P_Σ)ᵀ·λ = ∇_Σ J and dJ/dθ = ∂J/∂θ + λᵀ·∂P_Σ/∂θ, with
   ∂P_Σ/∂θ the projected parameter derivative; nonsingular away from folds. (The stride-time derivative through
   the section is obtained from the return-time sensitivity δT = −(∇gᵀ·δx_T)/(∇gᵀ·f⁺); the general treatment is
   in N22c-37.)

**What stays.** The symmetry reduction, the rule that stability is computed over the full reduced state (never
over the symmetric subspace alone), the nine derivative labels (`smooth`, `saltation`, `discrete-step`,
`fixed-mode`, `one-sided`, `generalized`, `smoothed`, `finite-difference`, `refused`) and the typed outcomes of continuation (`arrived`,
`fold`, `period-doubling`, `torus`, `symmetry-breaking`, `mode-change`, `diverged`, `budget`) apply unchanged.

**Record.** The removed coordinates listed in a `GaitOrbit` record gain the phase; the record also carries
`loop: autonomous`, the section's event identity, the return time T*, the trivial multiplier (listed as removed,
with its eigenvector's alignment to f) and the standing `screening`.

**Verb.** `frob gait orbit <design> --gait <id> --section <event> --key <key> --json` (`--section` is in BD-333's
grammar; a `run` call without `--key` is FROB-REF-805). With `--section auto` the solver picks the transverse
event with the largest transversality number and reports the choice. This bead adds the explicit fixed-period
request that BD-333's grammar leaves to it: `--form stroboscopic` with `--period <T>` (on an autonomous loop it is
refused as above; on a clock-forced loop `--period` must equal the clock's stride period N·Δt_c, otherwise
FROB-REF-802), and `--form section`, equal to giving `--section`; `--form bordered` is N22c-37's. VERBS-gait's row
lists `--form` and `--period` with this bead as owner.

**Fixture.** This bead registers `fixture:planar-fold` (`synthetic`, DESIGN; phase source `none`): ṙ =
r·(θ + r² − r⁴), φ̇ = 1, with the one continuous key `theta` and the section `phi=0`. Its orbits r*² =
(1 ± √(1 + 4θ))/2 (outer stable, inner unstable) merge in a fold at θ = −1/4, where the section multiplier
e^(2π·2r*²·(1 − 2r*²)) reaches 1. It is the fold control of BD-334's gradient suite (solved through this bead's
section form) and the planted fold of N22c-37.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A stroboscopic request on a loop whose phase source is `event-reset` or `none` is refused with the slug above and is not reported as a fold.
- [ ] Planar loop (ṙ = r·(μ − r), φ̇ = 1; section φ = 0): the section multiplier equals e^(−2πμ) to 10⁻⁹ and dr*/dμ = 1 to 10⁻⁹ by the section's implicit-function solve.
- [ ] Rimless wheel: on the post-impact section the multiplier is cos²(2α), 1/2 for eight spokes; the full monodromy's multipliers are 1 and cos²(2α), and the 1 is listed as removed.
- [ ] The compass gait passes through the section path with multipliers within 10⁻⁶ of the independent high-precision values.
- [ ] A grazing section (transversality below threshold) is refused with FROB-REF-431 and one-sided estimates.
- [ ] Every result carries `loop`, the section identity, T*, the removed coordinates including the phase, derivative labels and `screening`.
- [ ] Changing the phase source changes the `ControllerId`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-gait/tests/section_form.rs`:
- `classification_from_phase_source`: `clock` → clock-forced; `event-reset`, `none` → autonomous.
- `stroboscopic_refused_on_autonomous`: slug, exit 3, payload lists the available events; outcome is not `fold`.
- `planar_loop_section_multiplier`: μ ∈ {0.1, 0.5, 1, 2} against e^(−2πμ) to 10⁻⁹ (0.533488, 0.0432139, 0.00186744, 3.4873 × 10⁻⁶).
- `planar_loop_dr_dmu`: 1 to 10⁻⁹.
- `planar_fold_fixture`: at θ = −0.24 the outer orbit has r*² = 0.6 and section multiplier e^(−0.48π) = 0.221360, the inner r*² = 0.4 and e^(0.32π) = 2.732753, both to 10⁻⁹; at θ = −1/4 + 10⁻¹² (ε = 10⁻⁶ from the fold) the outer multiplier is 0.99998743 (1 − 4π·ε to first order) to 10⁻⁸; `--set theta=…` is the only accepted key.
- `projection_annihilates_flow`: (I − f⁺·∇gᵀ/(∇gᵀ·f⁺))·f⁺ = 0 to machine precision; the projected matrix has rank n − 1.
- `rimless_wheel_section`: cos²(2α) for α ∈ {π/8, π/6, π/10} (0.5, 0.25, 0.654508).
- `trivial_multiplier_listed`: the eigenvalue of M nearest 1 has an eigenvector within 10⁻⁶ rad of f and is reported as removed.
- `section_choice_invariance`: two different transverse sections of one orbit give the same nontrivial multipliers to 10⁻⁸.
- `grazing_section_refused`: FROB-REF-431 with one-sided estimates.

### Known answers and negative controls
Planar loop and rimless wheel closed forms (appended known answers `reflex-planar-loop` and `reflex-rimless-autonomous` of N22c-04;
KA-560 for the wheel's fixed point). Negative controls: a build that runs the stroboscopic solve on the planar loop reports a fold on
every orbit (counterexample `reflex-free-period` of N22-CX, CX-R22-e, whose plant this bead builds as
`N22c-36:stroboscopic_on_autonomous_loop`, compiled only under `gauntlet-mutations`: the classification bypassed
so that the stroboscopic solve runs) and must fail the gate; a section Jacobian without the
projection keeps the multiplier at 1 and must fail `trivial_multiplier_listed`; a monodromy computed on the
symmetric subspace only must be marked incomplete (CX-237). Metamorphic relation: the nontrivial multipliers do
not depend on the choice of section. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts for the
tangent-world Jacobian; debug and release.

## Considerations

- OPEN-GAIT-1 (when the referee route for orbits is built; owner: the dynamics lead with upstream; blocks nothing
  on the critical path; default: compliant tiers only). On compliant tiers a touchdown is not an instantaneous
  event, so the section is placed on a smooth guard (a force or phase threshold); on the referee route, which
  waits on the upstream item for hybrid shooting on articulated models, the section is the event itself and the
  saltation matrix applies. Until then the hand fixtures run on the host and on the referee's event-located
  flow for small models only.
- If the orbit kill gate fires, this machinery remains as a stability diagnostic and is not used for ranking.
- Kill gate KILL-FREEPERIOD's fallback is exactly this bead without N22c-37: autonomous loops on an event
  section only.
- The transversality threshold is the one already used for grazing refusals; it is not a new constant.
