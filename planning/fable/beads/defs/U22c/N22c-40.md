## Background

A gait is analysed as a fixed point of a return map; its stability is read from the Floquet multipliers of the
map's Jacobian. A gait that can **alternate between stride modes** (a step up and a step down; a left and a right
asymmetric stride; a slipping and a gripping stride) has one return-map Jacobian per mode, and a multiplier test
on each mode alone does not decide the alternation.

Counterexample, by hand. On a two-dimensional section let A = [[1/2, 1], [0, 1/2]] and B = Aᵀ. Repeating either
stride alone has both multipliers at 1/2. The two-stride product B·A = [[1/4, 1/2], [1/2, 5/4]] has an
eigenvalue 3/4 + 1/√2, about 1.457. The alternating pattern amplifies a disturbance by (1 + √2)/2, about 1.207,
per stride while both isolated tests pass.

The proposed stability object (Frontier) brackets the growth rate over all **admitted** sequences of strides: a
lower bound from products along admitted cycles, an upper bound from one common quadratic norm found by a
small semidefinite program. Its pilot: the counterexample, then a quadruped trot over alternating risers on the
compliant tier, with the bracket compared against 200 sampled admitted sequences of 50 strides. **Falsifier**: a
sampled sequence grows under a `stable-under-switching` verdict, or the bracket is undecided on more than half
of the multi-mode gaits of the battery. **Ceiling**: none on speed; it removes one class of false stability
labels.

## Scope

In: a prototype of the two bounds; the counterexample; per-mode Jacobians of one trot over alternating risers
from the orbit solver; 200 sampled sequences; the comparison; the verdict. Out: the mechanism inside
`frob-gait` with its exact checker and verb surface (N22c-41); the gate (N22c-42).

## Technical approach

1. **Register first**, as one entry of the pilot register `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (schema
   `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`, N38a-09). Mechanism "stability over admitted stride sequences", tier [F]; protocol; the two
   falsifier clauses; ceiling (none on speed); fallback (kill gate KILL-STRIDESEQ: no switching verdict is
   issued; multi-mode gaits are tested by finite-disturbance recovery over sampled admitted sequences, class
   `Tested`, and per-mode multipliers carry the flag `single-mode-only`); cost (DESIGN estimate: 2
   engineer-days, CPU only); seeds.
2. **Prototype of the bracket.**
   - *Admitted language*: a directed graph on modes (which mode may follow which).
   - *Lower bound*: enumerate the admitted cycles up to length L = 4 (with a cap on the count); for each cycle
     form the product of its Jacobians in order and take ρ(product)^(1/length); the largest value is the lower
     bound, and the cycle that attains it is the witness word.
   - *Upper bound*: search for one matrix P ≻ 0 and a rate γ with A_σᵀ·P·A_σ ⪯ γ²·P for every mode σ, by
     bisection on γ with a semidefinite feasibility problem at each step (an existing conic solver is
     acceptable for the pilot). The least feasible γ is the upper bound. A common quadratic norm is sufficient
     and not necessary.
   - *Verdict*: `stable-under-switching` only if the upper bound is below one; `unstable-sequence` with its word
     if the lower bound is above one; `undecided` otherwise, with both numbers.
3. **The counterexample.** Expected: lower bound at length two (1 + √2)/2 = 1.207107; eigenvalue of B·A
   3/4 + 1/√2 = 1.457107; no common contraction exists (the bisection finds no feasible γ < 1.2071); verdict
   `unstable-sequence` with the word (A, B). Also a control pair of commuting contractions (A = diag(1/2, 1/3),
   B = diag(1/3, 1/2)): upper bound 1/2 with P = I; verdict `stable-under-switching`.
4. **The trot over alternating risers.** A 12-joint quadruped under its baseline trot on a floor of risers that
   alternate up and down by a fixed height (DESIGN: ±20 mm, one riser per stride). Two stride modes: `up` and
   `down`; admitted graph: `up → down`, `down → up`, and, for a terrain grammar that allows repeats, `up → up`
   and `down → down`. The two-stride periodic orbit and each mode's return-map Jacobian on a common section
   (after removal of the symmetry coordinates, with the base height measured from the ground under the stride's
   start so that an `up` and a `down` stride map the chart to itself) are computed by the prototype: the orbit
   world of `gait.orbit` is flat ground (N22a-29) and has no terrain input before the mechanism bead N22c-41,
   which follows this pilot, so the prototype shoots on stride evaluations of the closed loop on `batch-cpu`
   over the riser scene (`frob sim run`, the same step) and differentiates them by central differences, and
   `frob gait orbit` on flat ground supplies the seed.
5. **Sampling.** 200 admitted sequences of 50 strides, drawn from the graph with seed 1. For each: apply the
   product of the sampled Jacobians to 20 random unit perturbations, and also run the nonlinear closed loop on
   `batch-cpu` over the same riser sequence from a perturbed state of size 10⁻³ in the section's norm. Growth
   rate per stride: (‖δ₅₀‖/‖δ₀‖)^(1/50).
6. **Measure.** The bracket [lower, upper]; the largest sampled growth rate (linear and nonlinear); whether any
   sampled sequence grows (rate above 1 beyond the transient allowed by the norm's conditioning) under a
   `stable-under-switching` verdict; and, over the multi-mode gaits available in the battery, the share on which
   the verdict is `undecided`.
7. **Falsifier.** A sampled sequence grows under a `stable-under-switching` verdict; or the bracket is undecided
   on more than half of the multi-mode gaits.
8. **Record.** Verdict, bracket, sampled rates, the undecided share, enumeration counts and solver times.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The record exists before the first run.
- [ ] The counterexample returns 1.457107, 1.207107 and the word (A, B); the commuting pair returns `stable-under-switching` with γ = 1/2.
- [ ] The trot's bracket, its 200 sampled rates and the nonlinear check are stored with seeds.
- [ ] No sampled linear rate exceeds the upper bound (a property of the bound; a violation is a defect in the prototype, reported separately from the falsifier).
- [ ] The undecided share over the battery's multi-mode gaits is reported against one half.
- [ ] The verdict is handed to N22c-41 (funding) and N22c-43 (KILL-STRIDESEQ).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
For `tools/pilots/stride_seq/`: `product_ba` (B·A = [[1/4, 1/2], [1/2, 5/4]] exactly); `eigen_ba` (3/4 ± 1/√2 =
1.457107 and 0.042893; determinant 1/16); `per_stride_rate` (√1.457107 = 1.207107 = (1 + √2)/2);
`single_mode_rates` (ρ(A) = ρ(B) = 1/2); `cycle_enumeration_counts` (complete graph on two modes with
self-loops, primitive cycles counted up to rotation: 2 of length 1, 1 of length 2, 2 of length 3, 3 of length
4);
`commuting_pair_common_norm` (γ²·I − AᵀA = diag(0, 5/36) and diag(5/36, 0) at γ = 1/2, both positive
semidefinite); `sampled_rate_below_upper_bound` on random stable pairs.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/pilot_stride_sequences.sh`: `frob gait orbit quad-ref --gait trot --tier batch-cpu --key
pss-seed --json` for the flat-ground seed; the prototype for the two-stride orbit, its per-mode Jacobians and the
bracket; `frob sim run quad-ref --scene terrain:<riser sequence n> --tier batch-cpu --key pss-seq-<n> --json` (a
`terrain:` scene as in VERBS-sim's example `terrain:stairs@0.4`) for each of
the 200 nonlinear sequences (one key per sequence, so no comparison reads a replayed receipt); then `cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test pilot_register` to validate the entry. Logged per the shared
standard: commands, exit codes, evidence block, `lower_bound`, `upper_bound`, `witness_word`,
`sampled_rate_max.linear`, `sampled_rate_max.nonlinear`, `grew_under_stable_verdict` (must be false),
`undecided_share` (threshold 0.5), `cycles_enumerated`, `sdp_cpu_s`, the verdict.

### Known answers and negative controls
The counterexample's three numbers and word. Negative control: a per-mode multiplier test labels the
counterexample stable; the pilot shows the bracket does not. A second control: a pair with a known common
contraction that is rotated by a non-orthogonal change of basis must still be found stable by the search (the
norm is not assumed to be the identity). Determinism: host arithmetic and `batch-cpu` replay; results repeat
exactly on one machine.

## Considerations

- The verdict is local, like every multiplier: it says nothing about the recovery basin, which is tested with
  finite disturbances. The nonlinear check in step 5 uses small perturbations for that reason.
- The upper bound ignores the graph (it bounds arbitrary switching), so it can be undecided on a gait whose
  admitted language is narrow; that is the second falsifier's concern.
- Section dimensions of 40 to about 130 make the semidefinite problem a few thousand unknowns; the pilot records
  solver time so the mechanism can decide whether symmetry blocks are needed.
