## Background

A damping-injection backup controller commands u = −K_d·y on collocated outputs. In continuous time it never
adds energy. Computed at a tick and held for a control period h, it can: a free 1 kg slider at +1 m/s under a
gain of 300 N·s/m held for 10 ms ends at −2 m/s with 1.5 J more kinetic energy. The sampled-data condition is
that stored energy does not increase at the ticks exactly when 2·M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0 (M̄ the reflected inertia at the
outputs), that the rate does not reverse exactly when M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0, and that with one tick of actuation latency
the scalar loop is stable only for κ = K_d·h/M̄ < 1. `shield.fit` computes and enforces the bound and refuses a
gain outside it. The gate:

> GATE-SHIELD-07. Known answer. The four rows are exact. `shield.fit` refuses 300 N·s/m at 10 ms on an inertia of
> 1 kg and admits 100 N·s/m. Over 10⁴ random triples (M̄, K_d, h) with 2·M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0 the ledger's stored energy
> at ticks never increases, and each of 10³ planted violations shows an increase. The continuous-time identity
> remains a theorem card. M4.

The four rows:

| Case | Value | Mutation that must fail |
|---|---|---|
| The slider, κ = 3 | −2 m/s; +1.5 J | Work taken as initial power times duration: −3 J |
| κ = 2 | The speed reverses to −1 m/s; energy unchanged | none stated |
| κ = 1 | Stops in one tick; −0.5 J | none stated |
| κ = 3/2 with one tick of latency | Diverges: the map has spectral radius √(3/2) | A bound of κ < 2 applied to the delayed loop |

## Scope

In: one script; the registration of the free slider as the fixture `free-slider` and its fixture controller
`sampled-damper`; the four rows; the fit refusal and admission (through N22c-44's script as a stage); the random
triples and the planted violations on the ledger; the conformance case `gate_shield_07` whose tier run produces
the gate's receipt; the M4 part of the gate recorded from it. Out: the mechanisms (N22c-44, N22c-45); the slider
design file and N22c-44's own script (N22c-44); the `sim.run` input `--param` (N22c-29).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/gate_shield_07.sh`; seed from `FROB_E2E_SEED` (default 1); idempotent; non-zero exit on any
failed assertion. Tiers: `referee`, and `batch-cpu` under the replay profile for the bulk runs.

**The fixture (registered here, DESIGN).** `assets/fixtures/free-slider/fixture.toml` in N24b-03's schema
`frob.fixture/1`: kind `synthetic`; the design `assets/fixtures/free-slider/slider.fbot` (N22c-44's file: one
prismatic body, a collocated force actuator, no friction, no other force), which declares the free parameters `m`
(declared mass, kg, domain (0, 100], default 1) and `v0` (initial rate, m/s, domain [−10, 10], default 1); no
controller, so a run names one; the fixture controller `fixtures/controllers/sampled-damper.toml` (the damping
injection u = −K_d·y computed at each tick on the committed rate and held for one period), which declares `kd`
(N·s/m, ≥ 0 for the gate rows, negative admitted only under the planted-violation flag of step 7), `h` (s, the
hold period, the task's control period by default) and `latency_ticks` (0 or 1, default 0); rows: the four rows
below with their derivation files under `docs/derivations/free-slider/` and the appended known-answer row
`reflex-sampled-damper` of N22c-04. Its hash is recorded by `task.fixture` before the first run.

Every `run` call carries its own `--key` (`g7-<step>-<i>`), so that no row returns another row's stored receipt.
The free parameters are set with `sim.run --param <name>=<value>` (N22c-29's input); one tick is `--seconds 0.01`
at h = 10 ms.

1. **Row κ = 3.** `frob sim run --fixture free-slider --controller sampled-damper --param m=1 --param v0=1
   --param kd=300 --param h=0.01 --seconds 0.01 --tier referee --key g7-1 --json`, then `frob telemetry energy
   <run> --json`. Assert the speed after one
   tick is −2 m/s and the ledger's stored-energy change is +1.5 J, both within the ledger's residual bound
   (exact in the fixture's rational evaluation). Log the naive estimate F·v·h = −3 J as the mutant's value and
   assert it differs.
2. **Row κ = 2.** `kd=200`: speed −1 m/s; stored-energy change 0.
3. **Row κ = 1.** `kd=100`: speed 0; stored-energy change −0.5 J.
4. **Row κ = 3/2 with latency.** `kd=150 --param latency_ticks=1 --seconds 0.4` (40 ticks): the loop is v′ = v − 1.5·v_prev,
   whose one-tick map [[1, −1.5], [1, 0]] has determinant 1.5 and complex eigenvalues of modulus √(3/2). Assert
   the spectral radius reported for the map is 1.224745 within 10⁻⁹; assert the simulated envelope grows: the
   largest abs(v) over ticks 31 to 40 is at least 100 times the largest over ticks 1 to 10 (the envelope grows
   by 1.5¹⁵ ≈ 438 over 30 ticks); assert that the undelayed bound (κ < 2) would have admitted the gain.
5. **Fit.** Stage `scripts/e2e/shield_sampled_damping.sh` (N22c-44's script, run as is): `frob shield fit
   assets/fixtures/free-slider/slider.fbot … --backup damping --param kd=300 --param h=0.01 --key … --json` is
   refused with `ok: false`, the slug `sampled-damping-above-passivity-bound`, exit 3, payload with M̄ = 1 kg,
   h = 0.01 s, K_d = 300 N·s/m, the bound applied, and the gain limits 100 N·s/m (no reversal, the default) and
   200 N·s/m (passivity, admitted with `--allow-reversal`); with `kd=300 --allow-reversal` the refusal still fires
   and names the passivity bound; with `kd=100` it fits and the shield record contains the rational check's inputs
   and result, which `frob-verify` reproduces from its `rational-ldlt` entry. This script asserts on the stage's
   summary and copies its two gain limits and the verdicts into its own metrics.
6. **10⁴ random triples inside the bound.** For i in 1..10⁴: draw the output dimension n ∈ {1, 2, 3}, a symmetric
   positive definite M̄, a positive semidefinite K_d and h ∈ [1, 20] ms, rejecting draws that violate
   2·M̄ − h·K_d ⪰ 0; build the `synthetic` n-output free system with reflected inertia M̄; run 50 ticks from a
   random rate; read the ledger's stored energy at ticks. Assert no tick-to-tick increase beyond the ledger's
   residual bound in any run (`ledger_increases` = 0).
7. **10³ planted violations.** Draw triples with the smallest eigenvalue of 2·M̄ − h·K_d negative by a margin of
   at least 5% of ‖M̄‖, and start along the corresponding eigenvector. Assert each run shows at least one
   increase (`violations_detected` = 1,000 of 1,000). Include a negative gain among them.
8. **Theorem card.** Assert that the certificate registry lists the continuous-time identity
   (dH/dt = −yᵀ·K_d·y under u = −K_d·y) as theorem card T-39 (N12x-04; the registry's number governs if the
   numbering decision closes differently) and that `frob-verify` accepts its instance; if the card is not yet
   registered, log `theorem_card: missing` and fail this step only (the gate row then records the clause as
   open).
9. **Receipt.** `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-SHIELD-07 --mutations`: the tier run of the gate's entry, which
   holds this bead's case `gate_shield_07` (the four rows, the fit outcomes, the 10⁴ triples and the 10³ planted
   violations over the same seeded draws through the real referee and `batch-cpu` steps, the real ledger and the
   real `shield.fit`, with controls (a) to (c) below compiled under `gauntlet-mutations` and caught in the same
   run). It writes one hash-named receipt; the script asserts on its `gates` and `controls` and that its counts
   equal the script's own. The script records nothing.

**Recording (not part of the script).** A session that did not implement N22c-44 or N22c-45 enters the M4 part
from that receipt: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SHIELD-07 --receipt <hash>
--part M4`, with the four rows' measured values, the fit outcomes, the two counts and the card's status.

**Logging.** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/gate_shield_07/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every
line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N22c-46`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the
envelope's `evidence` block (claim Known answer, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). Metrics with
thresholds: `v_after.k3` (−2 m/s), `dH.k3` (+1.5 J), `v_after.k2` (−1 m/s), `dH.k2` (0), `v_after.k1` (0),
`dH.k1` (−0.5 J), `spectral_radius.delayed` (1.224745 ± 10⁻⁹), `envelope_growth.delayed` (≥ 100),
`fit_refused_300` (true), `fit_admitted_100`
(true), `max_gain_no_reversal` (100 N·s/m), `max_gain_passive` (200 N·s/m), `ledger_increases` (0 of 10⁴ runs), `violations_detected` (1,000 of
1,000), `theorem_card` (`accepted`). On failure: the triple, the tick, the two energies, and the run's file
paths. A `summary` line: assertions passed and failed; gate GATE-SHIELD-07 with its status.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Helpers in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `slider_rows` (v⁺ = (1 − κ)·v and ΔH = ½·((1 − κ)² − 1) for κ ∈ {3, 2, 1}:
+1.5, 0, −0.5 J); `delayed_growth` (the map [[1, −κ], [1, 0]] has determinant κ and, for κ > 1/4, eigenvalue modulus √κ); `triple_generator_respects_bound`
(every accepted draw satisfies the rational check; every planted violation fails it).

### End-to-end script and logging
As specified above; this bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
The four rows; the fit's gain limits M̄/h = 100 N·s/m (no reversal) and 2·M̄/h = 200 N·s/m (passivity); known
answer KA-572. Negative controls
through the same script: (a) `shield.fit` built without the bound admits 300 N·s/m and must fail step 5; (b) the
undelayed bound applied to the delayed loop admits κ = 3/2 and must fail step 4's admission check; (c) a ledger
that books the work of a hold as initial power times duration shows −3 J in step 1 and must fail; (d) the 10³
planted violations are themselves the control for step 6's detector. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine;
across worker counts on `batch-cpu`; referee against `batch-cpu` agree on every increase or non-increase
verdict.

## Considerations

- "Exact" for the four rows means exact in the fixture's rational evaluation and within the ledger's residual
  bound on the simulated tiers; the script logs both.
- The step that integrates a held command matters: the fixtures use the tier's own held-command step, whose
  known answers are owned by the dynamics chapter; a mismatch there shows up first in rows 1 to 3.
- The theorem card for the continuous-time identity is T-39 (N12x-04, a dependency of N22c-44); step 8 makes a
  missing registration visible instead of silently passing.
