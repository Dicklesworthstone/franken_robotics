## Background

A gate is a numeric threshold with a recorded measurement. The project's rules for gates: a gate passes only
with a recorded measurement against its threshold, as a row of `registries/gates.toml` that cites a commit, the
command, its output and a receipt hash; a gate with no record has the status `not run`, which is not a pass;
statuses are `not run`, `pass`, `fail`, `killed: fallback adopted` and `blocked: ⟨open question⟩`. Each gate
names exactly one of the seven claims (Proof, Known answer, Parity, Fidelity, Design comparison, Reality,
Performance). Every convergence gate has a registered negative control that it catches; a gate whose control
has not been shown to fail has not passed. A kill gate names its trigger and its fallback in advance and is
never quietly re-scoped; its decision is an ADR. Where a chapter and the gate register differ, the register
governs the gate's identity, claim, earliest milestone and numbers; the full pass criterion is the row in the
defining subsection, and a clause the register's brief leaves out is not waived. The project's honest-status
table is generated from this registry.

Reflex (estimation, control, gaits, skills, shields) defines 23 convergence gates and 12 kill gates. They are
measured and decided in many beads, several of them written by other people. This bead enters every one of
them in the registry with its full criterion, so that a threshold cannot be lost or softened between the
definition and the measurement, and adds the registry test that keeps the rows honest.

## Scope

In: the 35 registry rows with claim, full threshold, earliest milestone, measuring bead, measuring script, and
negative control; initial status `not run`; a registry test. Out: the measurements and the decisions themselves.

## Technical approach

**Row format (DESIGN).** `[[gate]]`: `id`, `kind` (`convergence` or `kill`), `claim`, `threshold` (full text),
`milestones` (parts), `defined_in`, `measured_by` (bead key and script path), `negative_controls` (identifiers),
`status`, and on record `commit`, `command`, `output_digest`, `receipt`, `measured` (values, never targets).
Kill rows add `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided_at`, `adr`.

**Readings fixed by the register.** One pair of rank-agreement thresholds everywhere a rule depends on τ: 0.7 to
select and 0.5 to discard; below 0.5, or with no record, a rung neither selects nor discards. One pair of
sampling-controller cost thresholds: 1 and 6 GPU-minutes. GATE-SHIELD-01 and KILL-ENERGY are due at M4 (the
energy tier lands at M4). GATE-REFLEX-01's referee rows are due at M1 and its `batch-cpu` rows at M2. The gates
of the six later subsections (GATE-REFLEX-10, -11; GATE-SHIELD-06, -07; GATE-GAIT-04, -05) and their kill gates
are binding, with the row in the defining subsection governing the threshold; a kill gate with no milestone of
its own is decided at the milestone of the gate beside it.

**Registry test.** A build-time check over the Reflex rows: every row has one claim (the one double-claim row
carries the governing part), at least one milestone, a `measured_by` bead and script, and at least one negative
control; a row with a `status` other than `not run` has a commit, a command, an output digest, a receipt and
measured values; a `threshold` edited after a row left `not run` is refused unless a dated amendment precedes
the measurement.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All 35 rows are in `registries/gates.toml` with status `not run` and the text above.
- [ ] The registry test passes, and fails when a row lacks a negative control, when a `pass` row holds targets in place of measured values, and when a threshold is edited after measurement without an amendment.
- [ ] The generated status table prints each Reflex gate as open until a measured value exists.
- [ ] Each row's `measured_by` resolves to an existing bead and, once written, to an existing script.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gates_reflex.rs`: `reflex_gate_count` (23 convergence, 12 kill);
`each_gate_one_claim`; `each_gate_has_negative_control`; `pass_requires_measured_values` (a `pass` row with the
string "at least" in its measured field is rejected); `threshold_edit_needs_amendment`;
`kill_rows_have_trigger_fallback_milestone`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/reflex_gate_register.sh`: `frob gate list --subsystem reflex --json` (or the registry reader) and
the status-table generator. Logged per the shared standard: counts by kind and status, rows failing a check,
and a summary; it asserts that no Reflex gate prints as passed without a receipt.

### Known answers and negative controls
The counts 23 and 12. Negative controls: a planted row with no negative control; a planted `pass` with targets;
a planted threshold edit. Determinism: the registry and the generated table are byte-identical for equal inputs.

## Considerations

- GATE-REFLEX-11 is printed with two claims; the register's rule gives one, and the promotion clause (Fidelity)
  is taken as governing with the cart as its known-answer precondition, pending regeneration of the register's
  tables.
- Several kill gates are also named in the done-conditions of mechanism beads ("… decided"); the decision
  beads listed here are where the ADR is written, and the mechanism beads supply the measurement.
- The open question on the rank-agreement threshold pair is settled by the register (0.7 and 0.5); the row for
  GATE-REFLEX-04 reflects that.
