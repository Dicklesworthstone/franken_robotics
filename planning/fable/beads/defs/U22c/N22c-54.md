## Background

Trot, bound and pronk put two or four feet down at one instant by symmetry, so gait analysis must handle
simultaneous impacts and may not refuse them. On an event-located tier an exactly simultaneous group is one
coupled impact solve; nearby trajectories reach the same feet in some order, so the stride map has one smooth
piece per ordering. The analysis computes the outcome for every ordering of a group of up to four feet (24
orderings) and for the coupled solve, and reports the **order sensitivity**: the largest difference between
outgoing velocities. The gait's verdict is `stable` only if all selections agree within tolerance, or if one
quadratic norm contracts under every selection; otherwise it is `order-sensitive`.

Fixture, by hand: a rigid bar of mass m and central radius of gyration k falls flat at speed v onto supports at
±a, plastic impacts, β = a²/k². The coupled solve stops the bar. One support first: the first end later leaves
at 2β·(1 − β)·v/(1 + β)²; for a uniform bar on supports at ±l/3 (β = 1/3) that is v/4, with 1/16 of the kinetic
energy surviving; for β ≥ 1 every ordering gives rest. The switch is the sign of the off-diagonal contact-space
term J₁·M⁻¹·J₂ᵀ = (1 − β)/m.

Open question OPEN-GAIT-2: **the norm and tolerance for order sensitivity**. Owner: the Reflex lead. It blocks
GATE-GAIT-02 (the 100-case gradient suite, in which no case may be refused for simultaneity alone and the bar
fixture must return rest, v/4 and the sign of the coupling term). Fail-closed default: the mass norm, and 1% of
the pre-impact speed.

A second question of the same family, OPEN-GAIT-1, blocks nothing and is recorded here: **when the referee route
for orbits is built**. Owner: the dynamics lead, with upstream. Default: compliant tiers only.

## Technical approach

**The measure (DESIGN, the default made precise).** For a simultaneous group with pre-impact generalized
velocity v⁻ and outcomes v⁺_s for each selection s (each ordering and the coupled solve), with mass matrix M at
the impact configuration:

    sensitivity = max over selections s, s′ of ‖v⁺_s − v⁺_s′‖_M / ‖v⁻‖_M,     ‖w‖_M = √(wᵀ·M·w).

The mass norm is chosen because it is the kinetic-energy norm, independent of the choice of generalized
coordinates and of units per coordinate. The group is **order-insensitive** when sensitivity ≤ 0.01 (1% of the
pre-impact speed in the mass norm).

**Check on the bar.** β = 1/3. The coupled solve gives v⁺ = 0. Left support first leaves the left end rising at
v/4 and the right end at rest: centre speed v/8 upward and angular velocity −v/(8a), with 1/16 of the kinetic
energy, so against the coupled solve ‖v⁺_L − 0‖_M/‖v⁻‖_M = √(1/16) = 0.25. Right support first is the mirror image
(angular velocity +v/(8a)), so the two orderings differ by an angular velocity of v/(4a) alone, and
‖v⁺_L − v⁺_R‖_M/‖v⁻‖_M = k/(4a) = 1/(4·√β) = √3/4 = 0.4330. The sensitivity, the maximum over pairs, is 0.4330,
far above 0.01: `order-sensitive`. In general, for β < 1 the pair of orderings gives 2·√β·(1 − β)/(1 + β)² and an
ordering against the coupled solve gives √β·(1 − β)/(1 + β)^(3/2) (0.25 at β = 1/3); β ≥ 1: every selection
gives rest; sensitivity 0: insensitive. The sign of (1 − β)/m predicts which. (An earlier draft reported 0.25,
the ordering-against-coupled pair only, which is not the maximum its own definition takes.)

**Decision rule.** The default stands unless the owner records, before GATE-GAIT-02 is measured, a different
norm or tolerance with its reason. Candidates the owner may weigh: a tolerance tied to the tier's own velocity
error bound in place of a fixed 1%; a norm restricted to the coordinates that survive the symmetry reduction.
Whatever is chosen is one number and one norm for every gait, written in the ADR and in the gate's fixture
file.

**OPEN-GAIT-1, recorded.** The compliant-tier orbit route (differentiating the continuous step map) comes
first and is the project's own. The referee route (event-located flow with saltation matrices, hybrid multiple
shooting on articulated models) waits on an upstream work item in the simulator sibling (derivatives of the
articulated dynamics with respect to state and design, and a hybrid multiple-shooting solver; priority P3;
switch-over rule: re-run GATE-GAIT-01 and GATE-GAIT-02 on the referee route). Until then: compliant tiers only;
small hand fixtures run on the host. What reopens it: the upstream item landing, or a gait gate failing for a
reason traced to compliant-contact smoothing.

**Record.** The ADR `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-GAIT-2.md` (every decision record lives in `docs/adr/`):
norm, tolerance, date, reason; the open-questions register marks it closed and keeps OPEN-GAIT-1 open with its
default and owner.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
In `crates/frob-gait/tests/open_gait_2.rs`, on N22a-34's group report (its own tests are in `simultaneous.rs`):
- `mass_norm_is_energy_norm`: ‖w‖_M² = 2·(kinetic energy) for random M and w.
- `sensitivity_bar_beta_third`: the maximum is √3/4 = 0.4330127 to 10⁻¹², attained by the pair (left first, right first); each ordering against the coupled solve gives 0.25 to 10⁻¹².
- `sensitivity_bar_closed_form`: for β ∈ {0.2, 1/3, 0.5, 0.8} the pair of orderings gives 2·√β·(1 − β)/(1 + β)² to 10⁻¹².
- `sensitivity_bar_beta_ge_one`: 0 for β ∈ {1, 2}.
- `sensitivity_coordinate_invariant`: a linear change of generalized coordinates leaves the measure unchanged.
- `tolerance_boundary`: 0.0099 is insensitive; 0.0101 is sensitive.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/open_gait_2_order_sensitivity.sh`: `frob gait orbit fixture:bar-two-supports --set
m=2,v=1,beta=<β> --impact-report --key og2-bar-<β> --json` for β ∈ {1/3, 1, 2} (BD-333's grammar; the impact report
carries N22a-34's group report), asserting 0.433013 normalized and 0.612372 in the mass norm at β = 1/3 and 0 at
β = 1 and 2, and `frob gait orbit quad-ref --gait trot --key og2-trot --json`, asserting the per-group sensitivity
and verdict present; it also checks that the ADR exists, predates the first GATE-GAIT-02 receipt and states the
norm and tolerance the reports use. Log `target/e2e-logs/open_gait_2_order_sensitivity/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript: every line with `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines with the exact command, exit
code, wall time and the evidence block; metrics `order_sensitivity` per group (tolerance 0.01),
`coupling_term_sign`, the verdict label, the ADR path; on failure the first differing value and the record's
path; a `summary` line with assertions passed and failed, naming GATE-GAIT-02.

### Known answers and negative controls
The bar: rest when coupled; first end leaves at v/4 for β = 1/3; rest for β ≥ 1 (known answer KA-563). Negative
control: a solver that forces an order reports rocking for the coupled case (CX-238); a sensitivity computed in
the Euclidean norm of mixed linear and angular coordinates must change under a change of units and fail the
invariance test.

## Considerations

- On compliant tiers nothing is special at a simultaneous touchdown; the corresponding check there splits the
  touchdown times by ±δ and compares one-sided Jacobians, labelling the result `generalized` if they differ. The
  tolerance decided here applies to the event-located analysis.
- The 1% default is a DESIGN number with no published basis; it is recorded as such.
- If orbits are reduced to diagnostics at their kill gate, order sensitivity remains a reported diagnostic.
