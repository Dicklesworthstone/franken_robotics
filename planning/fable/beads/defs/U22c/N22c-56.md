## Background

A runtime shield filters commands so that barrier rows on a reduced-order template stay satisfied. Two numbers
set how strict it is, and both are value judgements about acceptable risk, which is why the **project owner**
decides them.

1. **The calibration level α.** The gap between the template and the full robot is covered by a measured margin:
   n calibration episodes on a fast tier across the calibrated family; the score of an episode is its largest
   tracking error; the margin ε̂ is the ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉-th smallest score. For an episode exchangeable with the
   calibration set, the error is at most ε̂ with probability at least 1 − α. The barriers are tightened by ε̂.
   Gate GATE-SHIELD-04 then asks for empirical coverage of at least 1 − α − 0.01 on 10⁴ held-out episodes.
2. **The per-episode violation budget.** The bound P(violation) ≤ E[N_breach]·δ₁ + P(N_alarm ≥ 1)·δ₂ + δ₃ is
   compared with a stated budget in gate GATE-SHIELD-03 ("in-envelope violations within the stated bound" over
   5,000 in-envelope episodes).

Open question OPEN-SHIELD-2: the values of α and of the budget. Owner: the project owner. It blocks
GATE-SHIELD-03 and GATE-SHIELD-04; the register of open questions asks for the decision by M3 and notes that
nothing beyond the default itself is at risk in the meantime. Fail-closed default: α = 10⁻³ and a budget of
10⁻³ per episode.

## Scope

In: the consequences of each choice laid out for the owner; the decision recorded as an ADR; the two numbers
held in one configuration record that the fitting, the gates and the reports read. Out: the shields and their
gates (seed beads BD-338, BD-339, BD-340); the pilot (N22c-11).

## Technical approach

**What each number costs (arithmetic for the owner; no measurement).**
- *Calibration count.* The conformal index ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉ exists only when it is at most n, that is n ≥
  (1 − α)/α: 999 episodes for α = 10⁻³; 99 for α = 10⁻²; 9,999 for α = 10⁻⁴. With fewer the margin is infinite
  and the shield must refuse to run.
- *Margin size.* A smaller α takes a higher quantile of the tracking error, so ε̂ grows and the barriers tighten:
  less usable space and lower return. The pilot's falsifier (N22c-11) is exactly this: a margin implied by the
  99.9th-percentile tracking error above 0.3 m.
- *What the coverage gate can resolve.* With 10⁴ held-out episodes and α = 10⁻³, the expected number of
  exceedances is 10; the gate's threshold 1 − α − 0.01 = 0.989 allows 110. The slack of 0.01 dominates at this
  α, so the gate tests gross miscalibration, not α itself; the count of exceedances is logged for that reason.
- *What the violation gate can resolve.* With 5,000 in-envelope episodes and a budget of 10⁻³, the expected
  count at the budget is 5. Zero observed violations gives a one-sided 95% upper bound of about 3/5,000 =
  6 × 10⁻⁴, inside the budget; 5 observed violations give an upper bound of about 2.1 × 10⁻³, which cannot
  confirm the budget. A budget of 10⁻⁴ could not be confirmed with 5,000 episodes at all (zero violations bound
  the rate only at 6 × 10⁻⁴).

**Options for the owner.**

| Option | α | Budget per episode | Calibration episodes needed | Remark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Default | 10⁻³ | 10⁻³ | at least 999 | Confirmable at the gate's episode counts only if observed violations are 0 to 1 |
| Looser | 10⁻² | 10⁻² | at least 99 | Cheaper calibration; margins smaller; a weaker statement |
| Stricter | 10⁻⁴ | 10⁻⁴ | at least 9,999 | Not confirmable at 5,000 episodes; the gates' counts would have to grow by dated amendment |

**Decision record.** An ADR with α, the budget, the date and the owner's reason, written before GATE-SHIELD-03
and GATE-SHIELD-04 are measured (thresholds are fixed before data). The values live in one record
(`shield.policy`: `alpha`, `violation_budget`) read by `shield.fit`, `shield.report` and both gate scripts; they
are part of the margin's scope key, so a change of α invalidates stored margins (FROB-REF-457 on reuse).

**Until decided.** The default is in force. If the owner has not confirmed it by the M3 exit review, the review
lists the question under "owner input wanted", and the two gates are measured at the default when their
mechanisms land at M4.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists before either gate is measured and states α and the budget with the owner's reason; or the exit review records that the default was confirmed.
- [ ] One configuration record holds both numbers; a second definition anywhere fails a registry test.
- [ ] `shield.fit` refuses to compute a margin from fewer than ⌈(1 − α)/α⌉ calibration episodes (999 at the default) and says how many are needed.
- [ ] A stored margin calibrated at one α is refused at another (FROB-REF-457).
- [ ] `shield.report` prints α, the budget, the calibration count, the conformal index used and the observed exceedance and violation counts with their upper bounds.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `conformal_index_exists`: α = 10⁻³: n = 998 has no index; n = 999 gives index 999; α = 10⁻²: n = 99 gives 99; α = 10⁻⁴: n = 9,999 gives 9,999.
- `margin_refused_below_minimum_n`: 500 episodes at α = 10⁻³ is refused with the needed count 999.
- `coverage_threshold`: at α = 10⁻³ the gate's coverage threshold is 0.989, that is at most 110 exceedances in 10⁴.
- `zero_violation_upper_bound`: 0 of 5,000 gives a 95% upper bound of 1 − 0.05^(1/5000) = 5.99 × 10⁻⁴.
- `alpha_in_scope_key`: a margin stored under α = 10⁻³ is refused under α = 10⁻² with FROB-REF-457.
- `single_source_of_alpha`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/open_shield_2_policy.sh`: `frob shield fit` with 998 and 999 calibration episodes, `frob shield
report`, and a reuse attempt under a changed α. Logged per the shared standard: `alpha`, `violation_budget`,
`n_calibration`, `conformal_index`, the refusals with their codes, and the ADR's status.

### Known answers and negative controls
Index arithmetic above. Negative control: a build that computes the margin as the empirical (1 − α) quantile
without the (n + 1) correction returns a finite margin at n = 500 and must fail `margin_refused_below_minimum_n`.

## Considerations

- The bound is estimated for one deployed policy and estimated again when the policy changes; it is not claimed
  under a shift of the world distribution. α and the budget do not change that.
- Whatever the choice, shield results are class `Tested`; no value of α converts them into a proof.
- If the owner chooses the stricter option, the gates' episode counts must be amended (dated, before
  measurement) so that the budget is confirmable; otherwise the gate would pass or fail on noise.
- One step more urgent than its milestone: two gates and the calibration effort are sized by it.
