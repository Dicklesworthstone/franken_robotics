## Background
Distillation trains a *student* policy by supervised learning to imitate a *teacher*. It is the cheapest way to obtain a policy when something that already behaves well exists, and the program has four such things. The specification's statement: one mechanism, four teachers; the student is trained by supervised learning on states the student itself visits, relabeled by the teacher (dataset aggregation).

| Teacher | What it gives | Cost and caveat |
|---|---|---|
| The baseline controller (the synthesized, inspectable controller of the control subsystem) | A warm start for any class that has a baseline | Seconds. The student inherits the baseline's limits. |
| The sampling controller (the sampling-based predictive controller, which optimizes a cost by rolling out the simulator itself) | Demonstrations for a class that has a cost and no shaped reward; pooled seeds for a family prior | About 67 GPU-minutes per 4,000 simulated seconds at the cheapest configuration and the throughput target. The teacher is `model-privileged` (it uses the true model and state). |
| Specialists (per-design trained policies) | A family policy distilled from many specialists | The specialists must exist; it pays when they were trained anyway for finalists |
| A privileged teacher (a policy that reads `critic-only` channels) | A proprioceptive student from a teacher that reads privileged channels | The student's schema is run with those channels absent |

Why dataset aggregation and not plain behaviour cloning on teacher rollouts: a student trained only on states the teacher visits has never seen the states its own small errors lead to, and its errors compound; relabeling the *student's own* visited states with the teacher's action corrects exactly the distribution the student will face. Plain behaviour cloning from the baseline is the special case of iteration zero and is the "warm start for free" named among the Frontier algorithms.

Rules (binding): the student is evaluated on its own rollouts with its own observation schema, and teacher states are never used at evaluation. The distillation gap is reported as the student's share of the teacher's return, with the requirement verdicts of both. A student trained on shuffled teacher labels must fail the threshold. A distilled policy's manifest lists its teachers, and it takes the weakest standing among them.

Tier Frontier: not funded before its pilot has run, implemented only when pilot and kill-gate decision are on record. Gate GATE-CORTEX-14; kill gate KILL-DISTILL (students stay below 70% of their teachers' requirement pass rate after one repair cycle → distillation is limited to the privileged-teacher case; warm starts come from behaviour cloning only), decided in bead N23a-21. Ceiling stated before measurement: a warm start from the baseline is nearly free; the sampling teacher pays only when pooled over a family or where no shaped reward exists; nothing here is on the critical path. Rejected alternatives: one bespoke pipeline per teacher (four mechanisms to test instead of one); evaluating the student on teacher-visited states (measures memorization, not behaviour).

## Scope
In: the `Teacher` interface and its four implementations; the dataset-aggregation loop on device; the supervised loss under the registered action law; the mixing schedule; the shuffled-label control; the manifest's teacher list and standing rule; the verb `train.distill`; the measurement of GATE-CORTEX-14 and the inputs of KILL-DISTILL. Out: the teachers themselves (baseline controllers for every archetype; the sampling controller; specialists from the PPO trainer); the family policy's architecture and pretraining (BD-320; this mechanism can feed it); the pre-funding pilot (N23b-21); the gate-record type that requires the shuffled-label control (N23b-15, used here); the kill decision (N23a-21); the per-algorithm decision KILL-ALTALGO, whose row for privileged teacher and student governs the privileged-teacher path (N23b-23, pilots BD-324); the residual head (N23a-20).

## Technical approach
**Interface (DESIGN).**
```rust
pub trait Teacher {
    fn id(&self) -> TeacherId;                       // controller, policy or sampling-controller identity
    fn standing(&self) -> Standing;                  // evidence standing of the teacher's own results
    fn privilege(&self) -> Privilege;                // none | critic-only-channels | model-privileged
    /// Label a batch of student-visited states with the teacher's action (mean command), on the device.
    fn relabel(&mut self, states: &TeacherStateView, cx: &Cx<'_>) -> Result<ActionView<'_>, Refusal>;
}
```
`TeacherStateView` is what each teacher needs: the baseline controller reads its estimator's state; a specialist or privileged teacher reads its own observation schema (privileged channels included) projected from the same worlds; the sampling controller takes world snapshots and plans from them (it forks the worlds, so relabeling with it is by far the most expensive and is budgeted per state).

**The loop (numbered steps).**
1. *Initialize.* Student network from the specification; optional initialization by behaviour cloning on a first batch of teacher rollouts (iteration 0).
2. *Roll out the mixture.* For iteration i, each world acts with the teacher's action with probability βᵢ and the student's otherwise, drawn per world per episode by address (βᵢ = β₀·dᶦ, DESIGN default β₀ = 1, d = 0.5, so the student drives almost all states after a few iterations).
3. *Relabel.* For every visited state, store the student's observation (its own schema) and the teacher's action for that state.
4. *Aggregate.* Append to the dataset on the device (a ring with capacity and reservoir replacement by address; invalid and padded samples have weight zero).
5. *Fit.* Supervised epochs on the aggregated dataset. Loss under the student's registered action law (rule P-3: the likelihood is computed for the random variable the student will sample): for `latent-then-limit`, the negative log-density of the teacher's pre-limit command under the student's Gaussian, or equivalently a mean-squared error on means with a separately fitted log standard deviation (a teacher that only exposes its applied, limited action supplies that value, and the stored label is marked `applied-only`); for `clipped-with-endpoint-mass`, the negative log-likelihood of the teacher's applied (clipped) action under the student's mixed law: the Gaussian density strictly inside the bounds and the endpoint mass (Φ((lo − μ)/σ) at the lower bound, 1 − Φ((hi − μ)/σ) at the upper) where the label sits on a bound. A dataset whose labels were stored under one law is never fitted under the other (FROB-REF-527). Masked sums divided by the valid count, as in every loss of the crate.
6. *Evaluate.* The student alone, on its own rollouts with its own observation schema, on the development stream: return and requirement verdicts. Teacher states are not available to this evaluation by construction (the evaluation environment is built from the student's manifest only). The evaluation builder, here and at every evaluation of a distilled policy, refuses before any episode runs a request whose state source is anything other than the student's own rollouts under its own observation schema (a teacher's visited states, or a teacher's observation schema): the refusal `distilled-student-on-teacher-states` (a request row of N23-REF in the allocation ledger, family 5xx, exit 3, retry `after-change`, `defined_in = "§23.10"`; no number yet, so it is raised behind the constant `frob_learn::refusal::DISTILLED_STUDENT_ON_TEACHER_STATES` and assignment is a one-line change). Envelope: `pointer` = the state source in the request; `observed` = the teacher-state source with the teacher's identity; `required` = the student's own rollouts and manifest schema. One repair: evaluate the student on its own rollouts (drop the teacher-state source); `fewer_repairs_reason`: "a student is never scored on teacher states; there is no admitted alternative".
7. *Stop* at the registered budget or when the student's requirement pass rate has not improved for a registered number of iterations.

**Privileged teacher.** The student's actor schema excludes the teacher's `critic-only` channels; the student is run with those channels absent (the isolated-inference path of the access-class bead), and the actor-schema refusal (FROB-REF-524) guards against a student that reads them. This is the case that survives the kill gate.

**Controls and reporting.**
- *Shuffled-label control*: the same loop with the teacher's labels permuted across states (a fixed permutation by address). It must fail the threshold. A distillation gate record without it is refused (FROB-REF-544).
- *Gap*: student return / teacher return on the same development stream, and the requirement pass rates of both; for the sampling-controller teacher also its cost (GPU-seconds per simulated second and total).
- *Zero-weight and random students* fail the same threshold (the behaviour-not-plumbing rule).

**Manifest.** `trained_on.teachers: [TeacherId…]` with each teacher's privilege; `standing` of the student = the weakest standing among its teachers and its own evaluation; a student of a `model-privileged` teacher carries that mark so that its demonstrations' provenance is never lost.

**Verb `train.distill`** (kind run; first milestone M4; "distill a teacher into a student policy"). Arguments: `teacher` (one or more identities with kinds `baseline`, `sampling`, `specialist`, `privileged`), `student` network, `task`, `design` (or a design set for pooled distillation into one student), mixing schedule, dataset capacity, budget, seed, idempotency key, the determinism profile (`--determinism`, spelled as on every verb that takes one, N35-06: `device-replay`, class D2, the default; `--profile` names a step profile and never a determinism profile; `fast-training` is enabled only on `train.start`); `--control shuffled-labels` (the shuffled-label control run, labelled as such and never exported as a policy); `--relabel-budget <GPU-seconds>` (the sampling teacher's relabelling budget); `--dry-run` (trains nothing and spends no device time: resolves the teachers, applies the admission checks, including the restriction a fired KILL-DISTILL imposes (a teacher kind not admitted is exit 5 naming the decision), binds the channels by role and reports the plan). The `train.distill` row of VERBS-train lists every input above, the four teacher kinds, the refusals 524 and 531 raised here and `distilled-student-on-teacher-states` as a worded refusal until its number is allocated. Admission refusals: unknown teacher; a teacher whose action channels cannot be bound to the design by role (FROB-REF-531); a student schema that includes a privileged channel (FROB-REF-524); a sampling teacher whose relabeling budget exceeds the session's (budget refusal). Output: the job handle; on completion the student's `PolicyId`, the gap report, the control results and the evidence block.

**Behaviour-cloning warm start (`train.start --warm-start bc:<controller>`).** Iteration zero of the loop above on its own: supervised fitting of the student's actor on the teacher controller's own rollouts, with no aggregation and no further iterations; the result initializes an ordinary `train.start` job (PPO), and its manifest records `init: bc:<controller>` with the teacher's identity and standing. It is the cheapest warm start (the baseline supplies demonstrations for free) and is the named fallback of KILL-DISTILL (warm starts from behaviour cloning only) and of KILL-RESIDUAL, so it exists on every branch of those gates. This bead builds it; the row `bc-warmstart` of `registries/algorithms.toml` (N23a-14) moves from `pilot-pending` to `admitted` with the pilot record of N23b-21 when this bead closes. The `train.start` row of VERBS-train lists the input `--warm-start bc:<controller>` with this bead as its extender.

**Gate.** GATE-CORTEX-14 (Known answer and Design comparison; M4; TARGET): the shuffled-label control fails; a student of a baseline or specialist teacher reaches at least 90% of the teacher's requirement pass rate; the figure for a sampling-controller teacher is recorded with its cost. Method: ten paired seeds, a 95% paired lower bound, on at least 10 designs for the baseline teacher and on the designs that have specialists for the specialist teacher.

**Determinism and evidence.** Class D2 per backend under the default profile. Evidence class `Tested`; results never claim more standing than the weakest teacher's.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The pilot's record and its falsifier outcome are on file before this bead starts (Frontier rule).
- [ ] GATE-CORTEX-14 is recorded with measured values: the shuffled-label control fails the threshold; baseline-teacher and specialist-teacher students reach at least 90% of their teachers' requirement pass rate (paired lower bound); the sampling-teacher figure is recorded with its cost (the seed bead's done-condition, first half). The script writes no gate status: it prints the hash of the result receipt, and a session that did not implement the verb enters the gate from it with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-CORTEX-14 --receipt <hash>`.
- [ ] The inputs of KILL-DISTILL are recorded (student-to-teacher pass-rate ratios per teacher kind, and whether a repair cycle was used); the decision is recorded by bead N23a-21, which depends on this bead (the seed bead's done-condition, second half).
- [ ] `frob train start <design> --task <t> --warm-start bc:<baseline> --key <k> --json` starts a PPO job whose initial actor reproduces the baseline's actions on its own rollouts within the supervised loss's tolerance and whose manifest records the warm start; `frob train distill … --dry-run --json` trains nothing and reports the admission outcome.
- [ ] The student is evaluated only on its own rollouts with its own schema: an evaluation request that supplies teacher states is refused with `distilled-student-on-teacher-states` (N23-REF's ledger row, 5xx, exit 3) before any episode runs, asserted by the raising test `evaluation_on_teacher_states_refused`, and the evaluation environment's channel list equals the student manifest's.
- [ ] A privileged-teacher student's actor schema contains no `critic-only` channel, isolated inference is bit-equal to ordinary inference, and its return is reported beside the teacher's.
- [ ] The student's manifest lists its teachers with privileges, and its standing equals the weakest among them.
- [ ] `frob train distill --json` is in the verb registry with schema, examples and refusal list; cancellation drains and leaves no leaked device resources.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: the shuffled-label student passing (the gate fails by its own rule); a student evaluated on teacher-visited states (refused); a student that reads a privileged channel (FROB-REF-524); a zero-weight student presented as distilled (fails the threshold and the head-norm check).

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`dagger_mixture_probability_by_address` (the share of teacher-driven worlds per iteration equals β₀·dᶦ within binomial error, for example β₀ = 1 and d = 0.5 → shares 1, 0.5, 0.25, 0.125 at iterations 0 to 3, and is identical across batch sizes); `relabel_uses_student_visited_states`; `dataset_reservoir_by_address_reproducible`; `supervised_loss_masked_sums`; `shuffled_labels_break_learning` (on a small fixture the shuffled student stays below threshold while the true student passes); `evaluation_env_has_only_student_channels`; `evaluation_on_teacher_states_refused` (teacher states offered to the evaluation builder, once as a teacher's visited-state set and once as a teacher's observation schema, are refused with `distilled-student-on-teacher-states` before any episode: zero episodes run, zero device-seconds; the envelope's `pointer`, `observed`, `required`, `retry = after-change`, exit 3 and the single repair with its `fewer_repairs_reason` are asserted; this is the receipt N23-REF's script reads); `privileged_student_schema_excludes_critic_only`; `teacher_bind_by_role_or_refuse_531`; `manifest_lists_teachers_and_weakest_standing`; `sampling_teacher_budget_refusal`; `bc_warm_start_is_iteration_zero` (one supervised pass on the baseline's rollouts, no aggregation; the manifest records `init: bc:<controller>`; the job that follows is ordinary PPO); `dry_run_spends_nothing` (zero device-seconds; with a fixture registry in which KILL-DISTILL restricts to privileged teachers, `--teacher baseline:…` → exit 5 naming the decision and `--teacher privileged:…` → admitted); `distill_cancellation_no_leak`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/cortex_distill_experiment.sh`, this bead's own script and the measuring run of GATE-CORTEX-14 (N23a-36's `cortex_amortize.sh`, which depends on this bead, exercises the verb at small scale in its step `distill` and does not re-measure the gate). Every `run` call carries its own key, distinct per design, teacher and seed. (1) Baseline teacher, on at least 10 registered designs d of the reference class: `frob control synth <d> --key ds-cs-<d> --json`; for ten paired seeds s, `frob train distill --teacher baseline:<id> --design <d> --student mlp:256,256,128 --task <task> --seed <s> --key ds-b-<d>-<s> --json`, then `frob job wait` and `frob train status <job> --json`. The job's result carries the gap report: student and teacher return and requirement pass rate on the same development stream, the teacher scored by the job itself (in `eval.run` the development stream is a population of the policy form only, whose `--policy` takes a family or a checkpoint; a controller is evaluated there through `--controller` on the single-task form, which is not the same stream). The student is re-scored from the user's side with `frob eval run <d> --policy <student> --task <task> --stream dev --key ds-ev-<d>-<s> --json`. (2) Controls on the same designs and stream: the shuffled-label run with the same seeds (`--control shuffled-labels`, keys `ds-shuf-<d>-<s>`); the zero-weight and random students through N23b-15's control forms (`frob eval run <d> --policy zero:<student> --task <task> --stream dev --key ds-zero-<d> --json` and `--policy random:<student>:<seed>`, key `ds-rand-<d>`); each must fall below the threshold. (3) Specialist teacher, on the designs that have specialists: `--teacher specialist:<PolicyId>` (keys `ds-spec-<d>-<s>`), the teacher also scored with `frob eval run <d> --policy <PolicyId> --task <task> --stream dev --key ds-ev-t-<d> --json`. (4) Where the sampling controller is available, `--teacher sampling:<id> --relabel-budget <GPU-seconds>` (keys `ds-samp-<d>-<s>`), its pass-rate ratio recorded with its cost; where its kill gate has fired, the row is logged `not run` with the decision. (5) A privileged teacher (`--teacher privileged:<PolicyId>`, key `ds-priv-<d>`): the student's actor schema in its manifest has no `critic-only` channel. (6) `frob train distill --teacher baseline:<id> --design <d> --student mlp:256,256,128 --task <task> --dry-run --key ds-dry --json` spends zero device-seconds. (7) The behaviour-cloning warm start `frob train start <d> --task <task> --warm-start bc:<id> --steps <small> --key ds-bc-<d> --json`, its manifest recording `init: bc:<id>`. The script writes no gate status: when every clause is measured it prints the hash of the result receipt and its log's digest, from which the recording session enters GATE-CORTEX-14 (acceptance criteria). NDJSON (shared helper of `STD-E2E`) at `target/e2e-logs/cortex_distill_experiment/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: exact commands, exit codes, wall time, evidence blocks, `metric` lines (student and teacher return and requirement pass rate per design and seed; the paired ratio with its 95% lower bound against the 90% target and the 70% kill threshold; shuffled-control, zero-weight and random pass rates against the threshold; sampling teacher GPU-seconds per simulated second), refusals, and on failure the design, seed, iteration and dataset digest; `summary` naming GATE-CORTEX-14 and the recorded KILL-DISTILL inputs. A reduced mode (`E2E_SMALL=1`) runs one design and two seeds to exercise the mechanics, labels itself so and is never cited for the gate.
### Known answers and negative controls
KA-590 (zero-weight, random and shuffled-label policies fail the return threshold); CX-257. Live-dependency proof: replacing the teacher by a different controller must change the student's behaviour. Metamorphic relation: permuting the order of worlds leaves the aggregated dataset's digest unchanged (addressed reservoir). Determinism lanes: twice; across worker counts; per backend.

## Considerations
- Pilot (must precede funding; run with existing tools outside the workspace as a development-time experiment): students for 10 designs from the sampling-controller teacher and from a hand-written baseline, inside the sampling controller's own pilot; falsifier: students below 70% of the teacher's requirement pass rate; cost about 10 GPU-hours.
- The student inherits the teacher's limits: a baseline that cannot cross a sill produces a student that cannot either; the gap report makes the ceiling visible, and fine-tuning with PPO afterwards is the ordinary next step.
- The sampling controller's kill gate may have fired (it is then a single-world debugging aid and demonstrations come from the baseline and from specialists); the sampling-teacher row is then recorded as `not run` with the reason.
- If KILL-DISTILL fires, only the privileged-teacher path and behaviour cloning remain; the verb stays, restricted, and reports the fallback's name.
- Risk-register link: if baselines fail on new morphologies so that every design needs training after all, behaviour-cloning warm starts from whatever baseline exists are one of the named mitigations.
- The privileged-teacher path answers to two gates. It is the fallback that KILL-DISTILL keeps, and "privileged teacher and student" is also one of the six algorithms piloted against PPO for KILL-ALTALGO (decision N23b-23). DESIGN reading: `train.distill --teacher privileged:<id>` checks that row of the algorithm registry at start and answers the capability refusal (exit code 5) until the row is `admitted`; the baseline, specialist and sampling-controller teachers do not wait for it. This bead is related to N23b-23 and does not depend on it, because it exists on both branches.
