## Background
A policy that performs well in simulation by reading something a real robot could never read is worthless, and the leak is usually invisible in a learning curve. Two rules of the environment contract close the two ways it happens.

**E-8 Access classes.** Every observation channel is one of `actor`, `critic-only` or `evaluation-only`. The actor's schema (the list of `actor` channels with their layout) is frozen when training starts, and the actor is *run with the other channels absent*: they are not zeroed or masked inside a shared tensor, they are not in the tensor the actor network receives. A read outside the schema is refused (FROB-REF-524). This is what makes asymmetric actor-critic training safe: the critic may read privileged `critic-only` channels (true friction, terrain height map, contact forces) to reduce variance, while the actor may not.

**E-9 Causality.** Two histories with identical delivered observations, past actions and policy random draws up to a decision instant yield the same command. The test perturbs, one at a time, things the actor must not be able to see at that instant: future terrain, hidden friction, and measurements not yet delivered. If the command changes, information is leaking, through a schema error, a queue that delivers early, a normalizer fitted on privileged data, or shared randomness.

Together they discharge proof obligation **PO-CORTEX-04**: the actor's output does not depend on any channel outside its schema, nor on anything not yet delivered. Verification is the causal test plus inference with the other channels absent.

Why access classes and not a convention: the suite's history shows that conventions are not kept under pressure; a typed projection that physically omits the channels cannot be bypassed by an indexing mistake. Rejected alternative: a mask multiplied into a shared observation tensor (a masked value still occupies memory the network can be wired to; a bug in the mask is silent).

Tier Solid; crate `frob-learn::space`. The single-world causal test on the referee already exists as the verb `sense.causal-test` (BD-119); the every-tier version is BD-215. This bead supplies the Cortex side: the schema types, the projection, and a harness that runs the perturbation test against any `VecEnv` implementation and any policy.

## Scope
In: `AccessClass` and the classification of each channel from the task program; the tensor `Layout` of a schema and the projection that builds the actor's and the critic's input tensors; the freezing of the actor schema at the start of training with its digest in the checkpoint manifest; FROB-REF-524; isolated inference of a policy with the non-actor channels absent; the causality harness and its three perturbation families; the property test for PO-CORTEX-04. Out: the schema types themselves. `ChannelId`, `ActorSchema`, `CriticSchema` (marked training-only), `freeze_actor_schema`, the four namespaces with their access table, the schema-time refusal `FROB-REF-442` and the absent execution mode of the observation service are defined once, in `frob-sense`, by N20a-03 (a dependency); `frob-learn::space` re-exports the two schema types and defines no second ones. Also out: the extension of access classes to rewards, flags, masks and `info` fields (rule E-10: the contract and `ActorView` are N23b-31, the four planted leaks and GATE-CORTEX-19 are N23b-32); the network that consumes the two inputs (N23a-12); privileged-teacher distillation (BD-323); the verb `sense.causal-test` itself (BD-119, BD-215).

## Technical approach
**Types (DESIGN).**
```rust
pub use frob_sense::{ActorSchema, ChannelId, CriticSchema};      // defined in N20a-03; not redefined here
pub enum AccessClass { Actor, CriticOnly, EvaluationOnly }
pub struct Layout { /* offset, shape and unit of each channel of a schema inside its tensor */ }
pub fn layout_of_actor(schema: &ActorSchema) -> Layout;          // a pure function of the frozen schema
pub fn layout_of_critic(schema: &CriticSchema) -> Layout;        // actor ∪ critic-only
pub struct SchemaView<'a> { pub tensor: TensorView<'a>, pub masks: TensorView<'a>, pub ages: TensorView<'a> }
```
One schema type, one identity (DESIGN). An earlier form of this bead declared its own `ActorSchema` and `CriticSchema` with a `layout`, a `digest` and a `frozen_at` field. They are now the types of `frob-sense`: the layout is computed from the schema, the digest is the schema's own content identity (the one that is part of the policy's identity in every evaluation record), and the run that froze it is recorded beside the digest in the checkpoint manifest, not inside the type. The learner's classes map onto the channel declarations of `frob-sense`: an `actor` channel is declared with actor access in the policy-observation namespace and may enter an `ActorSchema`; a `critic-only` channel may be listed only by a `CriticSchema`, which carries the training-only mark; an `evaluation-only` channel enters neither.
1. *Classification.* The access class of each channel comes from the task program (an observation term without a declared class is refused at task admission by the term contract). `evaluation-only` channels are produced only for scorers and telemetry and never enter either network.
2. *Projection.* `project_actor(obs, &ActorSchema) -> SchemaView` gathers the actor channels into a separate device tensor with its own allocation id. The gather is a kernel stage in the step plan, so the actor input exists on the device without a host copy; the critic input is a second gather. The actor network's forward kernel is given only the actor tensor's buffer.
3. *Freezing.* The `ActorSchema` is built once per training run, by `freeze_actor_schema` of `frob-sense` from the task's `actor` channels; its digest goes into the checkpoint manifest (`observation_schema.actor`) with the identifier of the run that froze it. Any later request to add a channel, or to evaluate the checkpoint with a schema of different digest, is refused.
4. *FROB-REF-524.* Raised when (a) a network specification names an input channel whose class is not `actor` for the actor (the specification's field `actor_channels`: by default every `actor` channel of the task; a caller may name a subset to train on fewer inputs, written as a list of channel ids or as edits of the default, `+id` and `-id`; `train.start` passes the field through as `--actor-channels <list | +id | -id>`, which the `train.start` row of VERBS-train lists with 524 among its refusals, and naming a channel of another class there is this refusal), (b) a checkpoint's actor schema names a channel that the current task classifies as `critic-only` or `evaluation-only`, or (c) a host caller requests an actor view that includes such a channel. Payload: the channel id, its class, the requesting component. Ranked repairs: move the channel to the critic; or declare a privileged teacher and distill (the student's schema is then run with those channels absent). Exit code 3.

   **442 and 524 (DESIGN; the plan gives both codes to an actor schema that touches a privileged channel and does not divide them).** They are divided by raising site, so that one event has one code. `FROB-REF-524` is the learner's: `frob-learn` raises it when one of its components asks to read, as the actor, a channel that the task classifies `critic-only` or `evaluation-only` (a network specification; a checkpoint's schema checked against the current task; a host caller's request for an actor view). That check runs before any schema is frozen, so such a request never reaches the freeze. `FROB-REF-442`, second clause, is the freeze's: `freeze_actor_schema` in `frob-sense` raises it, for whatever caller, when a channel offered for an actor schema is privileged by source (the truth namespace; a belief or measurement channel whose producer had privileged inputs; a task or curriculum parameter; a reward or termination signal) or is not of actor access. A learner's request that passed the class check and is still privileged by source (a task that classifies a truth channel as `actor`) is therefore 442. The same text stands in N20a-03, N23a-03 and the learning registry N23-REF.
5. *Absent-channel inference.* `infer_actor_isolated(policy, actor_view)` runs the actor in an environment built without the non-actor channels at all and compares commands with the ordinary run. The build is the absent execution mode of the observation service (N20a-03: the environment is built from the actor schema alone, buffers outside it are not allocated and no kernel argument refers to them); what this bead adds is the recompilation of the task program with those observation terms removed, the policy network on a `VecEnv`, and the comparison: they must be bit-identical in class D2. This is the second half of PO-CORTEX-04 and is cheap enough to run in every training gate.
6. *Causality harness.* `causality_check(env_factory, policy, seeds, perturbation) -> CausalReport`. For each seed: run world A to decision instant t and record delivered observations, actions and the policy's random draws; build world B identical to A except for one perturbation that cannot have been delivered by t; step both with the same draws; compare the command at t (and at every earlier instant). Perturbation families: (i) *future terrain*: the scene differs only beyond the region any sensor has covered by t; (ii) *hidden friction*: an interface parameter that no `actor` channel measures; (iii) *undelivered measurements*: a packet sampled before t whose arrival instant is after t carries a different value. Each family is applied alone. The report gives, per family, the number of seeds, the number of differing commands and the first differing instant with the channel that carried the difference (found by bisection over channels using the isolated path).
7. *Recurrent policies.* The hidden state is part of the compared history: equal delivered observations and draws imply equal hidden state, which the harness asserts before comparing commands.

**Determinism and evidence.** The comparison is bitwise (class D2 on one backend). The report carries evidence class `Tested`, the tier, the seeds and the task identity. A run that fails the check is labelled `leak-sensitive` by the exploitation detectors (the leak detector of N23b-06 consumes this harness).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Rule E-8 fixture: an actor network specification that names a `critic-only` channel is refused with FROB-REF-524 naming the channel; the same specification with the channel moved to the critic trains.
- [ ] The actor's input tensor has a different allocation id from the critic's and contains exactly the bytes of the actor channels; no kernel bound to the actor receives another buffer (checked from the step plan's argument table).
- [ ] Isolated inference equals ordinary inference bit for bit on 10³ random states for an MLP and a GRU policy.
- [ ] Rule E-9 fixture: on the reference task, each of the three perturbation families changes no command in 100 of 100 seeds for an honest policy.
- [ ] Negative controls caught, each in 100 of 100 seeds: (a) a policy wired to the true friction coefficient (planted through a schema bug that classifies it `actor`); (b) a packet queue that delivers one tick early; (c) an observation normalizer whose statistics were computed with a privileged channel folded in; (d) a terrain height scan whose footprint extends beyond the declared sensor range.
- [ ] PO-CORTEX-04 is recorded as discharged with the two test names and their outputs.
- [ ] `frob-learn` defines no schema type of its own: `ActorSchema` and `CriticSchema` in `frob_learn::space` are re-exports of `frob-sense`'s, and the digest in a checkpoint manifest equals the identity `frob-sense` computes for the same schema.
- [ ] A truth-sourced channel that a task misclassifies as `actor` is refused at the freeze with FROB-REF-442, not 524; a `critic-only` channel requested for the actor is refused with FROB-REF-524 before any freeze is attempted.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`actor_schema_rejects_critic_only_channel` (expects code 524, pointer to the channel); `misclassified_truth_channel_is_442_at_freeze`; `schema_types_are_reexports` (the type identities of `frob_learn::space::ActorSchema` and `frob_sense::ActorSchema` are equal); `actor_tensor_is_separate_allocation`; `isolated_inference_bit_equal_{mlp,gru}`; `schema_digest_in_manifest`; `frozen_schema_refuses_late_channel`; `causality_future_terrain_no_change`; `causality_hidden_friction_no_change`; `causality_undelivered_measurement_no_change`; `planted_leak_{friction,early_delivery,privileged_normalizer,overlong_scan}_caught`; property test `po_cortex_04_random_schemas` (random partitions of channels into the three classes; perturbing any non-actor channel's values leaves the actor's output bits unchanged).
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/cortex_env.sh` (bead N23a-34), steps `access-classes` and `causality` (that script depends on the every-tier causal test BD-215 and on the training verbs N23a-14; this bead's own acceptance needs only the library harness): `frob sense causal-test <reference quadruped> --task <reference task> --tier batch-cpu --twins all --seeds 100 --key e9 --json` (the actor is the design's baseline controller for the task, the verb's default) and a training start with a deliberately mis-specified actor, `frob train start <reference quadruped> --task <reference task> --actor-channels +friction_true --dry-run --key e8 --json`, asserting exit code 3 and refusal code FROB-REF-524. NDJSON lines: `refusal` (code, pointer, repairs), `metric` (seeds, differing commands per family, threshold 0), and on failure the first differing instant, the channel and the two command values.
### Known answers and negative controls
The four planted leaks above are registered as negative controls against GATE-CORTEX-06 (rules E-8 and E-9). Metamorphic relation: permuting the order of non-actor channels in the task file leaves every actor output unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; `batch-cpu` debug and release.

## Considerations
- Rule E-10 (N23b-31) widens the contract to every field the actor can read, including rewards and `info`; this bead's types must leave room for a fourth class (`trainer-only`) without changing the digest of existing schemas.
- The docking fixture of the sensing chapter (a late velocity packet that makes a mission infeasible for any causal policy) is the sharpest known case of perturbation family (iii) and is reused as a fixture where the adapter and the every-tier test need it.
- Cost: the isolated path doubles inference for the checked states only; the harness runs 100 seeds of a short horizon and is sized to run in every gate.
