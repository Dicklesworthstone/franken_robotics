## Background
During a rollout the policy and the simulator exchange tensors thousands of times per second. If any of them is copied to the host, training throughput collapses to the speed of that copy. The design rule is that **rollout storage stays on the device for the whole collection**: observations, sampled and applied actions, log-probabilities, values, rewards, flags and final observations are written by kernels into preallocated device slabs and read by the learning kernels in place. Observations never leave the device during a rollout.

Two further rules of the tensor-view layer are implemented here because they are about what is stored and how it is read.

*Masks apply everywhere.* Batches contain padding: padding worlds (a bucket's capacity exceeds the live count) and padded degrees of freedom (robots of different sizes share one padded layout). Padding must be masked in kernels, rewards and gradient accumulation. A padded element never contributes to a mean. Together with invalid worlds (numerical faults) this is invariant **INV-CORTEX-07**: invalid and padded samples have weight zero in every loss and statistic, and are counted in every failure report. A mean taken over a padded tensor without the mask is wrong by a factor that depends on the batch composition, which is exactly the kind of error that changes when a design is moved between buckets.

*Diagnostics never wait on the step.* Progress displays, telemetry sampling and `train.status` need to read statistics while training runs. They read **double-buffered ring slots**: the device writes slot e while the host reads slot e − 1 under a host-read lease, so a diagnostic read cannot stall or race the step.

Tier Solid; crate `frob-learn::rollout`. Rejected alternatives: host-side rollout buffers with a per-step read-back (the kill-gate fallback keeps one read-back per *rollout*, never per step); zero-filling padding and dividing by the full extent (biased); a lock around statistics (stalls the step).

## Scope
In: `RolloutStorage` layout and allocation in the device arena; the write stages after each step; the valid-sample weight tensor; masked reductions (count, sum, mean, variance triple) as the only reductions the learner may call; the diagnostic ring; storage of hidden state and episode-position metadata needed by sequence policies. Out: advantage computation (N23a-07); minibatch sampling and the update (N23a-10); sequence handling (N23a-11); telemetry reservoirs and histograms on devices (BD-207).

## Technical approach
**Layout (DESIGN).** For a collection of T control intervals and N logical worlds, structure-of-arrays slabs in the arena's rollout region, indexed [t][world][component], allocated once at environment creation (no device allocation afterwards):
- `obs_actor[T+1]`, `obs_critic[T+1]` (the schema projections; index T holds the observation after the last step);
- `final_obs_actor[T]`, `final_obs_critic[T]` (written only for worlds that ended at t; needed for the value bootstrap at a truncation);
- `action_sampled[T]` (the latent command or the applied command, according to the registered action law), `action_applied[T]`, `logp[T]`, `value[T+1]`, `value_final[T]`;
- `reward[T]`, `dt_sim[T]`, flags `terminated[T]`, `truncated[T]`, `invalid[T]`, `needs_reset[T]`;
- `weight[T]` (1 for a valid sample, 0 for an invalid world, a padding world, or a step excluded by the step mask);
- `hidden[T+1]` for recurrent networks, `episode_step[T]` (position of the transition within its episode) and `episode_id[T]`;
- per-channel masks and ages where the schema delivers them.
Sizes are computed from the admitted task and the network specification and reported by the cost model; a collection that does not fit the device budget is refused at admission, not at step T/2.

**Writes.** A stage `K-rollout-write` at the end of the environment step copies (by indexed device copy) the step's views into row t under the device's write epoch. In masked-autoreset mode `final_obs` comes from the step view's `final_obs`; in explicit mode it is the step's `obs` for ended worlds.

**The weight tensor and masked reductions.** `weight[t][w] = live[w] ∧ ¬invalid[t][w] ∧ stepped[t][w]`. Per-component masks for padded degrees of freedom multiply any per-component quantity (action log-probabilities are summed over live components only; entropy likewise). The module exports only masked reductions: `count(w)`, `sum(x, w)`, `mean(x, w) = sum/count` (refused when count = 0), and `stats(x, w) -> (n, μ, S)` with S the sum of squared deviations, all computed with a stable, fixed-order segmented reduction so that the result is class D2 and independent of slot order. There is no unmasked `mean` in the public API; a compile-fail test holds this. The neutral elements used for padding (0 for sums, the declared neutral for extrema) are recorded in the kernel manifest.

**Failure accounting.** `invalid_count`, `padded_count` and per-cause failure counts are reduced with the same machinery and written to the training receipt for every collection, so a sample excluded from learning is never excluded from the report.

**Diagnostic ring.** `StatsRing { slots: [Slab; 2], write_epoch }`: a fixed set of scalars per collection and per update (mean return of completed episodes, episode lengths, valid counts, clip counts, the learner's statistics). The device writes slot `e mod 2`; the host reads slot `(e − 1) mod 2` under a `HostRead` lease of epoch e − 1. A host read of the slot being written is refused by the lease layer.

**Determinism and evidence.** Storage and reductions are class D2 per backend; with the same seeds, two collections from one snapshot produce bit-identical storage (this is the storage half of the frozen-behaviour rule P-2). The receipt records bytes allocated, valid, invalid and padded counts.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A 1,000-step collection on a device backend performs zero host copies of observation or action tensors (the receipt's charged-copy list is empty and the device's transfer counter is unchanged apart from diagnostic ring reads).
- [ ] INV-CORTEX-07: with 25% padding worlds and one planted invalid world, every statistic the learner consumes (advantage mean and variance, value loss, policy loss, entropy, observation statistics) equals the same statistic computed on the dense batch of valid samples only, bit for bit in class D2; the invalid and padded counts appear in the receipt.
- [ ] Padded degrees of freedom: a 12-joint robot stored in an 18-joint padded layout yields the same summed log-probability and entropy as in a 12-joint layout.
- [ ] Moving a design between two bucket capacities changes no statistic (batch-composition invariance of the reductions).
- [ ] The diagnostic ring is readable while a step is in flight; the read returns epoch e − 1 values; an attempted read of slot e is refused.
- [ ] Two collections from one snapshot with the same seeds have identical storage digests.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: an unmasked mean (differs by the padding fraction); a mean over a zero-count mask returning 0 instead of refusing; a padded action component included in the log-probability.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`rollout_no_host_copies_1000_steps`; `masked_mean_equals_dense_mean_bitwise`; `inv_cortex_07_all_statistics` (parameterized over each statistic); `padded_dof_logp_and_entropy_unchanged`; `bucket_capacity_invariance`; `zero_count_mean_refused`; `invalid_and_padded_counts_in_receipt`; `final_obs_row_written_only_for_ended_worlds`; `ring_reads_previous_epoch`; `ring_read_of_write_slot_refused`; `two_collections_one_snapshot_identical`; `no_unmasked_mean_in_api` (trybuild); property test `masked_stats_merge_associative` (splitting the valid set into two parts and merging the (n, μ, S) triples by the exact pairwise formula reproduces the whole).
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/cortex_train.sh` (bead N23a-35), step `rollout-storage`: a short `frob train start --steps 2000 --worlds 256 --json` with a planted invalid world and a padded bucket; asserts zero charged copies, the invalid count 1 in `train.status`, and equality of the reported statistics with a dense reference run. NDJSON `metric` lines: bytes of rollout storage, copies charged (threshold 0), valid, invalid and padded counts, storage digest; on failure the first statistic that differs with both values.
### Known answers and negative controls
The dense-versus-masked equality is exact. The three planted defects are registered against GATE-CORTEX-07 (rule P-9, valid samples only). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; bucket capacities 256 and 1,024; `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal each within D2.

## Considerations
- Memory: T × N × observation width dominates; at 8,192 worlds, 32 steps and a few hundred observation components in single precision this is a few hundred megabytes, well inside one 24 GiB card, but height-scan or image channels change that; the cost model must report it before allocation.
- The read-back-once-per-rollout fallback of the learner-kernel kill gate reads this storage through one host-read lease at the end of a collection; the layout is therefore contiguous per field.
- Privacy: diagnostic slots contain aggregates only; no observation values are written to logs.
