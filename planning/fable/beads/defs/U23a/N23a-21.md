## Background
Distillation trains a student policy to imitate a teacher by supervised learning on states the student itself visits, relabeled by the teacher. The program uses one mechanism with four teachers: the synthesized baseline controller (a warm start for any class that has a baseline), the sampling-based predictive controller (demonstrations where a cost exists but no shaped reward), per-design specialists (to distill a family policy from many of them) and a privileged teacher that reads `critic-only` channels (to obtain a proprioceptive student). It is a Frontier mechanism: useful if students come close to their teachers, a waste if they do not.

**KILL-DISTILL** (decided at M4).
- *Trigger*: students stay below 70% of their teachers' requirement pass rate after one repair cycle.
- *Fallback, named in advance*: distillation is limited to the privileged-teacher case; warm starts come from behaviour cloning only.

The pass threshold of the mechanism's gate is higher than the kill threshold on purpose: the gate (GATE-CORTEX-14) asks that a student of a baseline or specialist teacher reaches at least 90% of the teacher's requirement pass rate; between 70% and 90% the mechanism has failed its gate but is not killed, and its use is advisory (a warm start that is then fine-tuned) until a repair passes the gate. Below 70% after one repair cycle it is killed.

This bead is the decision: it reads the measurements, applies the rule, records the outcome as an architecture decision record and restricts the verb if the gate fires. The same rule also applies before funding: the pilot's falsifier (students below 70% of the teacher's requirement pass rate, on 10 designs, from the sampling-controller teacher and from a hand-written baseline) is the paper-scale version of this trigger; if the pilot's falsifier held, the mechanism bead was never funded and this decision records that outcome instead.

## Scope
In: the inputs, the rule, the record, the restriction of `train.distill` on the fired branch, and what changes downstream. Out: the measurements (BD-323 and the pilot N23b-21); the mechanism's code; the per-algorithm decision on the privileged-teacher path (N23b-23).

## Technical approach
**Inputs (from recorded receipts).** Per teacher kind (baseline, specialist, sampling controller, privileged): the number of designs, the student's and the teacher's requirement pass rates on the same development stream, their ratio with a 95% paired lower bound over ten paired seeds, whether a repair cycle was used, and the shuffled-label control's result (a run whose control did not fail is not evidence and is excluded).

**Rule.** For each of the three non-privileged teacher kinds, `killed(kind) = ratio_lower_bound(kind) < 0.70 after one repair cycle`. The kill gate fires for the mechanism as a whole when it fires for the baseline *and* the specialist teachers (the two the gate requires; DESIGN: the trigger says only "students stay below 70%", read here as the students of every required teacher kind, so that one kind above the threshold keeps the mechanism alive for that kind; the reading is entered as a dated amendment of the trigger before the first GATE-CORTEX-14 receipt, N40-03 step 4, because an amendment dated after the measurement it governs is refused as `amended-after-measurement`; the owner confirms or changes it then, not in the record); the sampling-controller teacher's figure is recorded with its cost and can be killed individually without firing the gate (it also has the sampling controller's own kill gate above it). "One repair cycle" is one bounded attempt recorded as its own receipt (for example a change of mixing schedule, dataset capacity or loss form); a second attempt needs the owner's explicit extension.

**Outcomes** (the register's words, N40-03, in brackets).
- *Not fired, gate passed* (ratios ≥ 0.90) [`not-fired`]: distillation is admitted for the teacher kinds that passed; the algorithm registry row `teacher-student` moves to `admitted` with the receipt identifiers (the row `bc-warmstart` is admitted by BD-323 on every branch, because it is the fallback's warm start, and this bead does not write it).
- *Not fired, gate not passed* (0.70 ≤ ratio < 0.90) [`not-fired`]: the teacher kind is `advisory`: `train.distill` runs, its students are labelled `distilled-advisory`, may be used as warm starts for PPO fine-tuning, and may not be reported as policies in their own right or used to seed a family prior.
- *Undecided* [`deferred`]: the baseline row fires but the specialist row was not measured (no specialists exist yet; see Considerations), so the conjunction cannot be evaluated: the register entry carries `deferred_to = "M5"` (DESIGN: the next exit) and the reason, and the advisory branch's restrictions apply to the baseline teacher meanwhile. If the baseline row does not fire, the gate is `not-fired` on the baseline receipt whatever the specialist row would show.
- *Fired* [`fired`, also when the pilot's falsifier held before funding, on the pilot's numbers]: `train.distill` accepts only `--teacher privileged:…`; other teacher kinds are refused as not admitted (process exit code 5) with a message naming this decision. Warm starts come from behaviour cloning only (iteration zero of the loop: supervised fitting on the teacher's own rollouts, with no aggregation), exposed as `train.start --warm-start bc:<controller>` (built by BD-323 on every branch). The capability is described by the fallback's name wherever it is reported.

**What changes downstream if it fires.** A family policy can no longer be distilled from specialists (the family-policy pretraining route by reinforcement learning on the generated design distribution is unaffected); the sampling controller's demonstrations are not used for students; the residual head and inheritance are unaffected; the M4 exit review lists the kill and the reduced scope of the verb.

**The record.** `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-DISTILL-M4.md` (the kill-gate record form `docs/adr/ADR-<gate-name>-<milestone>.md`, with the fields N40-03 lists) with the trigger text, the table of inputs with receipt identifiers, the rule's evaluation per teacher kind, the outcome and its consequences, and the beads re-read (on a pilot falsifier, BD-323 and the gate-measurement work are listed "withdrawn before this decision"); the kill-gate register row is updated; the `teacher-student` row of `registries/algorithms.toml` is set accordingly. This bead writes the owner declaration N40-30 compares with the ledger, in `crates/frob-learn/contract.toml`: `[[gate]] id = "KILL-DISTILL", role = "owner"` with the ledger row's trigger (amendments replayed), fallback, `decided = "M4"`, `mode = "exit"` and `reads = ["GATE-CORTEX-14"]` (N40-25), and, once decided, `decision = { outcome, adr = "docs/adr/ADR-KILL-DISTILL-M4.md", measured = [{ name, value, unit, receipt }] }` with each teacher kind's ratio lower bound.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The decision record exists and cites, for each teacher kind measured, the receipt with the student-to-teacher pass-rate ratio, its lower bound, the repair-cycle flag and the shuffled-label control's failure.
- [ ] The registry rows and the verb's behaviour equal the outcome: on the fired branch `frob train distill --teacher baseline:… --json` returns exit code 5 naming the decision, and `--teacher privileged:…` still runs.
- [ ] On the advisory branch, students carry the label `distilled-advisory` in their manifest and a request to report one as a standalone policy is refused.
- [ ] The M4 exit review lists KILL-DISTILL with its outcome in N40-03's words; if the pilot's falsifier held before funding, the outcome is `fired` on the pilot's numbers and BD-323 is closed `withdrawn`, citing N23b-21's `[[pilot.result]]` (N41-17, rule 7); a `deferred` entry names `deferred_to` and a reason.
- [ ] The owner declaration in `crates/frob-learn/contract.toml` equals the ledger row and carries the decision with its receipts: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` passes (N40-30).
- [ ] The decision is recorded by a session that did not implement the mechanism.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`kill_distill_rule_truth_table` (ratios 0.95, 0.80, 0.69 for each of baseline and specialist; with and without a repair cycle; the boundary 0.70 does not fire, 0.699 fires; with the specialist row `not run`, baseline 0.69 gives `deferred` and baseline 0.75 gives `not-fired`); `registry_rows_match_adr`; `fired_branch_refuses_non_privileged_teachers_exit_5`; `advisory_label_blocks_standalone_report`; `excluded_run_without_failed_shuffled_control`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_distill_decision.sh`: reads GATE-CORTEX-14's row in `registries/gates.toml` and the receipt it cites, `target/gauntlet/receipts/<blake3>.json` (schema `frob.gauntlet.receipt/1`, N38a-02; `frob evidence trace` takes a claim identifier and has no gate form), evaluates the rule, compares with the committed record and the algorithm registry, then probes the verb (`frob train distill --teacher baseline:<id> --design <d> --task <t> --dry-run --key kd-base --json` and the same with `--teacher privileged:<id> --key kd-priv`, BD-323's dry run, which spends nothing; then `frob train start <d> --task <t> --warm-start bc:<id> --dry-run --key kd-bc --json`, which is admitted on every branch; both verbs are run kind, so each probe carries its own key, without which FROB-REF-805 would mask the exit code under test) and asserts the exit codes implied by the outcome; it ends with `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` for the owner declaration. NDJSON (shared helper of `STD-E2E`) at `target/e2e-logs/kill_distill_decision/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `metric` lines per teacher kind (ratio, lower bound, thresholds 0.90 and 0.70, designs, seeds), `assert` lines for rule = record = registry = verb behaviour, `summary` naming KILL-DISTILL.
### Known answers and negative controls
The rule's truth table. Negative controls: a record that says "not fired" against a receipt whose lower bound is below 0.70 after repair fails the script; a registry row left `admitted` on the fired branch fails the unit test.

## Considerations
- The risk this guards against is modest (distillation is off the critical path), which is why the fallback keeps the two cheapest and most defensible uses.
- A distilled student takes the weakest standing among its teachers; an advisory student additionally cannot raise its standing without its own evaluation.
- If the specialists needed for the specialist-teacher row do not exist when M4 exits (they are produced by finalist training and by the family-policy experiment), that row is recorded as `not run` and the decision is taken on the baseline row alone: `not-fired` if the baseline row does not fire, otherwise `deferred` as stated under Outcomes.
