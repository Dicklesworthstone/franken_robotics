## Background
A family policy is one set of weights per morphology class, conditioned on the design through tokens built from the design's typed description. `policy.apply` is the verb that makes it useful to an agent: given a design, it binds the class's family policy to that design and returns either a ready-to-evaluate policy (no adaptation) or starts a brief fine-tune. Registry row: `policy.apply`, kind run, first milestone M4, "apply a family policy to a design, with or without fine-tuning; refused out of distribution".

The scenario it serves: an agent lengthens the femur by 8% and widens the stance; the family policy of the quadruped class walks the new design with no training; the report gives its return as a share of the specialist reference for the class, the in-distribution check, and the rank-agreement status that says whether this rung may eliminate designs. When the design is shortlisted, the agent moves on to inheritance or a fine-tune.

The essential safety property is the refusal. A family policy applied outside its class or its pretraining distribution "fails outright" in every published study, and it fails silently: the robot is driven badly and a number comes back. The rule is that membership is decided by a stated rule on the parameter box and the topology signature, and outside it the policy is refused (FROB-REF-534) and the fallback (the baseline controller or the sampling controller as a screen; a specialist for a finalist) applies. The control named for the family-policy bet says the same: designs outside the class must be flagged out-of-distribution, not walked badly in silence.

Tier Frontier (it exists only if a family policy exists). Rejected alternatives: a confidence score from the network deciding membership (a learned score has no guarantee outside its training distribution, which is precisely where it is needed); best-effort application with a warning (a warning does not stop a ladder from eliminating designs on a meaningless score).

## Technical approach
**Inputs (schema `frob.policy.apply.request/1`, DESIGN).** `policy` (a family policy identity, or `class:<name>` to select the class's current family policy), `design`, `task`, `fine_tune` (`none` | `steps:<n>` | `fraction:<f of the class's reference specialist steps>` | `to-target`), `seed`, `idempotency_key`, `score_for_ladder` (bool), budget, profile.

**Admission, in order (each failure a refusal with ranked repairs).**
1. *A family policy exists for the class.* If none (the compute envelope was not approved, or the family kill gate fired), refuse as capability not admitted, naming the decision in force and the fallback rungs.
2. *Membership.* `in_support(design, policy.envelope)`: the design's canonical topology signature is one of the generator's; every conditioned parameter lies inside the registered box; actuator card family and payload are in range. Outside → **FROB-REF-534** (a family policy applied outside its class or its pretraining distribution; repairs, ranked: screen with the baseline or the sampling controller; train a specialist; exit code 3). The payload names the first violated bound (parameter, value, bound) or the topology difference. The generator-support refusal of the morphology-family subsystem covers the same condition for batch family scoring; this verb surfaces 534 and includes the generator's identity.
3. *Task.* The task family must be the one the policy was pretrained for (`trained_on.task` family) and pass the term contract; the action law and reset mode must match.
4. *Binding.* Tokens are built from the design's canonical form (one per joint and one per contact site; heading-normalized frames), so two descriptions of one robot give one input; the per-token decoder's outputs are bound to the design's action channels by role through the mapping table (unit, sign, bounds, rate). A binding failure is the mapping-table refusal (FROB-REF-531).
5. *Rung use.* If `score_for_ladder` is set, the rank-agreement guard applies (a record must exist with a mode that permits the use; otherwise FROB-REF-537). Until the record lookup of bead N23a-24 exists no record can exist, so the flag always refuses: the path fails closed from its first commit.

**No-adaptation path.** Returns a `BoundPolicy` identity (the family weights plus the design's token table and gather/scatter maps, stored in the content store with a manifest whose `trained_on.donor` is the family policy) and, if requested, runs a short evaluation on the task's development stream. The report contains: `in_distribution: {inside: true, margins: per-parameter distance to the nearest bound as a fraction of the box}`, `class_reference` (the specialist reference return for the class, with its provenance), `return_share_of_reference`, the requirement pass rate, `rank_agreement: {record, mode}` or `unranked`, and the evidence block. A design near the edge of the box (any margin below a registered fraction, DESIGN default 5%) gets the flag `near-support-edge`.

**Fine-tune path.** Starts a training job (the ordinary PPO job) initialized from the bound policy, with the budget implied by `fine_tune`; `fraction:0.05` is the registered 5% rung. The job's manifest names the family policy as donor; the result is a specialist checkpoint for that design with its own manifest. While running, the verb returns the job handle (process exit code 8).

**Outputs and exit codes.** 0 or 2 (flags such as `near-support-edge`, `unranked`) on completion; 3 for the refusals above; 5 when no family policy is admitted; 8 for a running fine-tune.

**Determinism and evidence.** Binding and the membership check are exact (class D0); evaluation under the deterministic device profile is class D2. Every output carries the claim product; a no-adaptation score is evidence class `Tested` with the tier of the evaluation.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The verb is in the registry with request and response schemas, examples and its refusal list, reachable through `frob --json` and the agent tool surface.
- [ ] Applying the family policy to an in-support design returns a bound policy whose actions on the canonical description and on a re-described design (permuted joints, changed axis conventions) agree to round-off.
- [ ] Each of the twelve registered out-of-class designs is refused with FROB-REF-534 and the correct reason, and nothing is scored (no evaluation job is created).
- [ ] A design exactly on a box bound is admitted with the flag `near-support-edge`; one beyond it is refused.
- [ ] `--fine-tune fraction:0.05` starts a job whose step budget equals 5% of the class's reference specialist steps, with the donor recorded in the resulting manifest.
- [ ] `--score-for-ladder` without a rank-agreement record is refused with FROB-REF-537; without the flag the score is returned labelled `unranked`.
- [ ] With no family policy admitted, the verb returns exit code 5 naming the decision in force and the fallback rungs.
- [ ] Cancellation of a fine-tune drains and finalizes with no leaked device resources.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: a membership check implemented on raw (non-canonical) parameters (fails on a re-described in-support design or admits a re-scaled out-of-box one); an apply path that evaluates before checking membership (an evaluation job appears for an out-of-class design); tokens built in file order (fails at the first exchange of two legs).

### Known answers and negative controls
The registered out-of-class set (twelve designs with known reasons) is the planted negative set. Negative controls as listed, registered against GATE-CORTEX-11 (the decline clause). Metamorphic relation: a similarity scaling of the whole design by a power of two leaves membership and the bound policy's normalized inputs unchanged. Determinism lanes: the binding digest is identical across machines; evaluation twice on one machine is bit-identical.

## Considerations
- The class reference return must itself have provenance (which specialists, on which stream); a share of an undefined reference is refused.
- A design can be inside the parameter box and still far from any trained design in the interior of a sparse box; the support margins are reported so that an agent sees how close to the edge it is, and per-region rank agreement is the proper guard.
- Policy weights trained on private scenes carry their mark into bound policies and fine-tuned children.
- The verb is deliberately not a way to load an arbitrary checkpoint onto an arbitrary design: that is `eval.run` with a specialist's manifest binding, or inheritance through a typed edit.
