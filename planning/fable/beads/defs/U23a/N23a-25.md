## Background
A family policy is one set of weights per morphology class, conditioned on the design through tokens built from the design's typed description. `policy.apply` is the verb that makes it useful to an agent: given a design, it binds the class's family policy to that design and returns either a ready-to-evaluate policy (no adaptation) or starts a brief fine-tune. Registry row (of record in the registry bead VERBS-policy, which this bead implements): `policy.apply`, kind run, first milestone M4, capability `policy:run`, schemas `frob.policy.apply.in/1` and `frob.policy.apply.out/1`, MCP tool `policy_apply`, example `frob policy apply fam:quad-1 --design quad-a --key pa-1 --json`, "apply a family policy to a design, with or without fine-tuning; refused out of distribution".

The scenario it serves: an agent lengthens the femur by 8% and widens the stance; the family policy of the quadruped class walks the new design with no training; the report gives its return as a share of the specialist reference for the class, the in-distribution check, and the rank-agreement status that says whether this rung may eliminate designs. When the design is shortlisted, the agent moves on to inheritance or a fine-tune.

The essential safety property is the refusal. A family policy applied outside its class or its pretraining distribution "fails outright" in every published study, and it fails silently: the robot is driven badly and a number comes back. The rule is that membership is decided by a stated rule on the parameter box and the topology signature, and outside it the policy is refused (FROB-REF-534) and the fallback (the baseline controller or the sampling controller as a screen; a specialist for a finalist) applies. The control named for the family-policy bet says the same: designs outside the class must be flagged out-of-distribution, not walked badly in silence.

Tier Frontier (it exists only if a family policy exists). Rejected alternatives: a confidence score from the network deciding membership (a learned score has no guarantee outside its training distribution, which is precisely where it is needed); best-effort application with a warning (a warning does not stop a ladder from eliminating designs on a meaningless score).

## Scope
In: the verb, its schemas and refusals; the envelope check at the verb (a call of the membership check that BD-320 exposes on the `FamilyPolicy` artifact, whose predicate and registered parameters come from N23a-23; this bead owns the call site, the refusal payload and the `near-support-edge` flag); token construction through the design compiler's canonical form and binding of outputs through the mapping table; the no-adaptation path; the fine-tune path as a managed job; the report fields (in-distribution check, class reference, rank-agreement status); the rung-scoring entry point's guard. ## Technical approach
**Inputs (schema `frob.policy.apply.in/1`; the fields beyond the registry's three are DESIGN).** `policy` (positional: a family policy identity such as `fam:quad-1`, or `class:<name>` to select the class's current family policy), `--design`, `--task`, `--fine-tune` (`none` | `steps:<n>` | `fraction:<f of the class's reference specialist steps>` | `to-target`), `--seed`, `--key` (the idempotency key of every `run` verb), `--score-for-ladder select|discard` (the requested rung use, which divides 537 from 545 below; DESIGN, stated with N23a-24; the row shows the flag without a value, and the value is requested of VERBS-policy), budget, profile.

**Admission, in order (each failure a refusal with ranked repairs).**
1. *A family policy exists for the class.* If none (the compute envelope was not approved, or the family kill gate fired), refuse as capability not admitted (exit code 5), naming the decision in force and the fallback rungs. DESIGN, one event one code: 534 is the membership refusal of a family policy that exists; the absence of any family policy is the not-admitted capability, never 534 (as the learning registry N23-REF also states).
2. *Membership.* `policy.envelope.admits(design)` (BD-320's wrapper of N23a-23's predicate `in_support`, which adds the generator's locate check, so that this verb and every other scoring path decline by one rule): the design's canonical topology signature is one of the generator's; every conditioned parameter lies inside the registered box; actuator card family and payload are in range. Outside → **FROB-REF-534** (a family policy applied outside its class or its pretraining distribution; repairs, ranked: screen with the baseline or the sampling controller; train a specialist; exit code 3). The payload names the first violated bound (parameter, value, bound) or the topology difference. The generator-support refusal of the morphology-family subsystem (`FROB-REF-541`, by the generator's derivation test) covers the same condition for batch family scoring; this verb raises 534, includes the generator's identity, and cites the 541 finding in the payload when the generator reports it (the division of N23-REF: both may apply, the policy raises 534).
3. *Task.* The task family must be the one the policy was pretrained for (`trained_on.task` family) and pass the term contract; the action law and reset mode must match.
4. *Binding.* Tokens are built from the design's canonical form (one per joint and one per contact site; heading-normalized frames), so two descriptions of one robot give one input; the per-token decoder's outputs are bound to the design's action channels by role through the mapping table (unit, sign, bounds, rate). A binding failure is the mapping-table refusal (FROB-REF-531).
5. *Rung use.* If `score_for_ladder` is set, the rank-agreement guard applies (a record must exist with a mode that permits the use). With no record, a request to select or to discard is FROB-REF-537, as is a request to discard through an `uninformative` record; a request to select through a record whose mode is not `selective` is FROB-REF-545 (the division of N28b-23; the `policy.apply` row in VERBS-policy lists both codes). Until the record lookup of bead N23a-24 exists no record can exist, so the flag always refuses: the path fails closed from its first commit.

**No-adaptation path.** Returns a `BoundPolicy` identity (the family weights plus the design's token table and gather/scatter maps, stored in the content store with a manifest whose `trained_on.donor` is the family policy) and, if requested, runs a short evaluation on the task's development stream. The report contains: `in_distribution: {inside: true, margins: per-parameter distance to the nearest bound as a fraction of the box}`, `class_reference` (the specialist reference return for the class, with its provenance), `return_share_of_reference`, the requirement pass rate, `rank_agreement: {record, mode}` or `unranked`, and the evidence block. A design near the edge of the box (any margin below a registered fraction, DESIGN default 5%) gets the flag `near-support-edge`.

**Fine-tune path.** Starts a training job (the ordinary PPO job) initialized from the bound policy, with the budget implied by `fine_tune`; `fraction:0.05` is the registered 5% rung. The job's manifest names the family policy as donor; the result is a specialist checkpoint for that design with its own manifest. While running, the verb returns the job handle (process exit code 8).

**Outputs and exit codes.** 0 or 2 (flags such as `near-support-edge`, `unranked`) on completion; 3 for the refusals above; 5 when no family policy is admitted; 8 for a running fine-tune.

**Determinism and evidence.** Binding and the membership check are exact (class D0); evaluation under the deterministic device profile is class D2. Every output carries the claim product; a no-adaptation score is evidence class `Tested` with the tier of the evaluation.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The verb is in the registry with request and response schemas, examples and its refusal list, reachable through `frob --json` and the agent tool surface.
- [ ] Applying the family policy to an in-support design returns a bound policy whose actions on the canonical description and on a re-described design (permuted joints, changed axis conventions) agree to round-off.
- [ ] Each of the twelve registered out-of-class designs is refused with FROB-REF-534 and the correct reason, and nothing is scored (no evaluation job is created).
- [ ] A design exactly on a box bound is admitted with the flag `near-support-edge`; one beyond it is refused.
- [ ] `--fine-tune fraction:0.05` starts a job whose step budget equals 5% of the class's reference specialist steps, with the donor recorded in the resulting manifest.
- [ ] `--score-for-ladder select` and `--score-for-ladder discard` without a rank-agreement record are each refused with FROB-REF-537; without the flag the score is returned labelled `unranked`. (The 545 answer through a recorded `discard-only` mode needs N23a-24's record lookup, which depends on this bead: it is this bead's unit test against a fixture record and N23a-24's end-to-end probe.)
- [ ] With no family policy admitted, the verb returns exit code 5 naming the decision in force and the fallback rungs.
- [ ] Cancellation of a fine-tune drains and finalizes with no leaked device resources.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: a membership check implemented on raw (non-canonical) parameters (fails on a re-described in-support design or admits a re-scaled out-of-box one); an apply path that evaluates before checking membership (an evaluation job appears for an out-of-class design); tokens built in file order (fails at the first exchange of two legs).

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`apply_in_support_binds_and_reports_margins` (a femur +8% inside a ±30% box: admitted, margin 22 percentage points of the nominal length reported for that parameter); `apply_out_of_class_refused_534_{class,box,topology}` (a biped: class; a limb +50% against a ±30% box: box, naming parameter, value and interval; an extra spine joint: topology signature; each exit 3); `apply_boundary_closed_and_flagged`; `apply_redescribed_design_same_actions`; `apply_tokens_from_canonical_form_not_file_order`; `apply_binding_failure_is_531`; `apply_fine_tune_fraction_budget` (`fraction:0.05` with the class reference at 4.0 × 10⁷ steps → a budget of 2.0 × 10⁶ steps); `apply_score_for_ladder_requires_record_537` (no record → 537; a `discard-only` fixture record and a select request → 545; the same record and a discard request → admitted); `apply_without_family_policy_exit_5`; `apply_idempotent_replay`; `apply_no_evaluation_job_on_refusal`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/policy_apply.sh`, this bead's own script (N23a-36's `cortex_amortize.sh` runs it as its stage `policy-apply`): `frob design edit <quad> set-param femur_length +8% --expected-parent <quad> --key pa-e2e-ed --json`; `frob policy apply class:quadruped --design <edited> --task <velocity> --key pa-e2e-1 --json` (expects exit 0 or 2 and the report fields); the same with `--fine-tune fraction:0.05 --key pa-e2e-2` (expects exit 8, then completion through `frob job wait`); `frob policy apply class:quadruped --design <biped> --task <velocity> --key pa-e2e-out-1 --json` and the same with `--design <quad with a limb 50% longer> --key pa-e2e-out-2` (each expects exit 3, FROB-REF-534 and its reason, and no evaluation job in `frob job list`); `frob policy apply class:quadruped --design <edited> --task <velocity> --score-for-ladder select --key pa-e2e-3 --json` and the same with `--score-for-ladder discard --key pa-e2e-4`, with no record (each expects exit 3 and 537; the 545 probe through a `discard-only` record is N23a-24's, whose record lookup depends on this bead). The script sources the shared helper of `STD-E2E` and logs to `target/e2e-logs/policy_apply/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. NDJSON: exact commands, exit codes, wall time, the evidence block, `metric` lines (return share of the class reference; per-parameter support margins; fine-tune steps against the 5% budget), `refusal` lines (code, pointer, reason, repairs), the bound-policy digest; `summary` with the gates touched.
### Known answers and negative controls
The registered out-of-class set (twelve designs with known reasons) is the planted negative set. Negative controls as listed, registered against GATE-CORTEX-11 (the decline clause). Metamorphic relation: a similarity scaling of the whole design by a power of two leaves membership and the bound policy's normalized inputs unchanged. Determinism lanes: the binding digest is identical across machines; evaluation twice on one machine is bit-identical.

## Considerations
- The class reference return must itself have provenance (which specialists, on which stream); a share of an undefined reference is refused.
- A design can be inside the parameter box and still far from any trained design in the interior of a sparse box; the support margins are reported so that an agent sees how close to the edge it is, and per-region rank agreement is the proper guard.
- Policy weights trained on private scenes carry their mark into bound policies and fine-tuned children.
- The verb is deliberately not a way to load an arbitrary checkpoint onto an arbitrary design: that is `eval.run` with a specialist's manifest binding, or inheritance through a typed edit.
