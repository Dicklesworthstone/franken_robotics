## Background
The family policy (one set of weights per morphology class, conditioned on the design) is the program's main bet for avoiding per-design training, and its most expensive one: it needs an owner-approved pretraining envelope of the order of 100 GPU-hours for one class. Published evidence supports it only narrowly (70 to 85% of specialist return with no adaptation inside a parametric neighbourhood, on flat or mildly varied ground with fixed actuation; 10 to 40% across topology-diverse designs; outright failure outside the training distribution). A kill gate fixes in advance when the bet is abandoned and what replaces it.

**KILL-FAMILY** (decided at M4).
- *Trigger*: at the family-policy gate, return with no adaptation below 70% of specialists on more than half of the held-out set **and** fine-tuning above 20% of specialist steps; **or** the pilot's falsifier holds (median return with no adaptation below 40% of specialists on parametric held-outs, or fine-tuning above 50% of steps).
- *Fallback, named in advance*: no family policy: specialists with inheritance from the nearest parent, and the baseline screens.

The trigger's first clause is a conjunction on purpose: a family policy that walks poorly with no adaptation but fine-tunes in a small fraction of the steps is still worth having as an initializer (it fails its gate's zero-shot clause but is not killed); one that walks well with no adaptation but fine-tunes slowly is still worth having as a screen. Only when both fail is it dropped.

This bead gathers the measurements, applies the rule, records the outcome and, if the gate fires, switches the milestone to its variant with no family policy. It also records the two outcomes that reach the same end without a measurement: the compute envelope was never approved (the fail-closed default: no pretraining), or the pilot's falsifier held before funding.

## Scope
In: the inputs, the rule, the decision record, registry and verb consequences, the milestone-variant declaration, the lost-factor record. Out: the measurements (BD-321, the pilot N23b-19); the envelope decision (N23b-24); inheritance (BD-322), which becomes the main reuse mechanism on the fired branch.

## Technical approach
**Inputs (from recorded receipts).**
1. The envelope decision's state: approved, declined or undecided at M4's opening.
2. The pilot's record: median zero-shot share on the parametric held-outs; fine-tuning fraction; falsifier held or not.
3. From the family-policy experiment: the fraction of held-out designs whose zero-shot share is below 0.70; the median fine-tuning fraction against the early-stopped specialist; the per-terrain breakdown; the decline and re-description results; whether the gate passed.

**Rule (mechanical).**
`fire = pilot_falsifier ∨ (fraction_below_0.70 > 0.5 ∧ median_fine_tune_fraction > 0.20)`.
`no_policy = fire ∨ envelope ∈ {declined, undecided}` (the fallback is in force in either case; the record distinguishes "killed", the register's `fired`, from "untested", the register's `not-reached`: the mechanism was not funded, N40-03).

**Outcomes** (the register's words, N40-03, in brackets).
- *Not fired, gate passed* [`not-fired`]. The family policy is admitted for the class and task family: `policy.apply` serves it; it may act as a rung only in the mode its rank-agreement record permits. The record states the margins and the measured break-even count.
- *Not fired, gate not passed* [`not-fired`, with the role restriction in the record] (one clause of the gate failed but the kill conjunction did not hold). The family policy is kept in a restricted role stated in the record: *initializer only* (when zero-shot is poor and fine-tuning is cheap: `policy.apply` requires `--fine-tune`, and no no-adaptation score is ever offered to the ladder) or *screen only* (when zero-shot is good and fine-tuning is slow: scores are subject to the rank-agreement mode; fine-tuning from it is not recommended and the report says so).
- *Fired, or no policy by default* [`fired` when the kill conjunction holds or the pilot's falsifier held, on the pilot's numbers in the second case; `not-reached` when the envelope was declined or is undecided at M4's opening]. No family policy. `train.family` and `policy.apply` are refused as not admitted (process exit code 5), naming this record and the fallback. The rungs of the ladder are then: the static check, the baseline controller, the sampling controller, gait orbits, and per-design specialists that inherit from the nearest parent. The ladder runs per class in the mode its measured rank agreement permits, which is `uninformative` until a record exists. The mapping table and manifests are unaffected (they are needed for inheritance and for loading policies).

**The milestone variant.** On the fired or default branch, M4 exits in its variant with no family policy: the family-policy gate is recorded as failed or `not run`, the family-policy bet as falsified or untested, the corresponding North-Star scenario as not demonstrated, and the lost-factor record of the ten-fold ledger (N39a-26) states which factor of the ten-fold thesis was lost (the training-avoidance factor shrinks to what inheritance and the baseline screen deliver). Every other artifact of the milestone lands unchanged.

**What changes downstream.** Beads that exist only with a family policy (the rung calibration against the family policy; the family rows of the amortization end-to-end script) close with N41-17's typed reasons: `superseded`, citing this record, on the `fired` branch; `withdrawn: not run: no envelope, R7` where the envelope was not approved (N44-01), or `withdrawn` citing the pilot's `[[pilot.result]]` when its falsifier held (N41-17, rule 7), and the record lists them as "withdrawn before this decision"; the rank-agreement experiment still runs for the baseline-controller rung; the Moonshot tree policy (a variant of the family architecture) loses its control arm and is not funded.

**The record.** `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FAMILY-M4.md` (the kill-gate record form `docs/adr/ADR-<gate-name>-<milestone>.md`, with the fields N40-03 lists): trigger text, the inputs with receipt identifiers, the rule's evaluation, the outcome, the role restriction if any, the milestone-variant declaration and the lost-factor note; kill-gate register updated; `registries/algorithms.toml` and the verb registry's admission flags set accordingly. This bead writes the owner declaration N40-30 compares with the ledger, in `crates/frob-learn/contract.toml`: `[[gate]] id = "KILL-FAMILY", role = "owner"` with the ledger row's trigger (amendments replayed), fallback, `decided = "M4"`, `mode` and `reads` as N40-25's row holds them, and, once decided, `decision = { outcome, adr = "docs/adr/ADR-KILL-FAMILY-M4.md", measured = [{ name, value, unit, receipt }] }` with the fraction below 0.70, the median fine-tuning fraction and, where it decided, the pilot's values.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The decision record exists with the three inputs (or the statement that an input does not exist because the envelope was not approved), the rule's evaluation and the outcome.
- [ ] On the fired or default branch: `frob train family --class quadruped --generator <g> --split <s> --task-family velocity --dry-run --key kf-tf-dry --json` and `frob policy apply class:quadruped --design <reference> --key kf-probe --json` return exit code 5 naming the record and listing the fallback rungs; the M4 milestone record declares the variant with no family policy; the lost factor is recorded in the ten-fold ledger (N39a-26).
- [ ] On a restricted-role branch, the restriction is enforced by the verb (initializer only: a no-adaptation score request is refused; screen only: the report carries the advisory text).
- [ ] The M4 exit review lists KILL-FAMILY with its outcome and distinguishes killed (`fired`) from untested (`not-reached`).
- [ ] Dependent beads that are no longer applicable are closed with the typed reasons above (N41-17), not left open.
- [ ] The owner declaration in `crates/frob-learn/contract.toml` equals the ledger row and carries the decision with its receipts: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` passes (N40-30).
- [ ] The decision is recorded by a session that did not implement the family policy.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`kill_family_rule_truth_table`: (pilot falsifier true → fire); (fraction 0.6, fine-tune 0.25 → fire); (fraction 0.6, fine-tune 0.15 → not fired, initializer only); (fraction 0.4, fine-tune 0.25 → not fired, screen only); (fraction 0.4, fine-tune 0.15 → not fired); boundaries: fraction exactly 0.5 and fine-tune exactly 0.20 do not satisfy the strict inequalities; `no_policy_when_envelope_undecided`; `verbs_refuse_exit_5_on_fired_branch`; `role_restriction_enforced`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_family_decision.sh`: reads GATE-CORTEX-11's row in `registries/gates.toml` and the receipt it cites, `target/gauntlet/receipts/<blake3>.json` (schema `frob.gauntlet.receipt/1`, N38a-02; `frob evidence trace` takes a claim identifier and has no gate form), and the envelope decision (row R7 of `registries/compute_envelope.toml` and `docs/adr/OPEN-CORTEX-1.md`), evaluates the rule, compares with the committed record and registries, probes `frob train family --class quadruped --generator <g> --split <s> --task-family velocity --dry-run --key kf-tf-dry --json` (a run verb: its dry run needs no approval token but carries a key) and `frob policy apply class:quadruped --design <reference> --key kf-probe --json` (the registry's positional form) for the exit codes the outcome implies, and runs `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` for the owner declaration. NDJSON (shared helper of `STD-E2E`) at `target/e2e-logs/kill_family_decision/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `metric` lines (fraction below 0.70 against 0.5; median fine-tune fraction against 0.20; pilot median against 0.40 and fine-tune against 0.50), `assert` lines for rule = record = registry = verb behaviour, `summary` naming KILL-FAMILY and the milestone variant.
### Known answers and negative controls
The rule's truth table. Negative controls: a record that keeps the family policy while the receipts satisfy the kill conjunction fails the script; a milestone record that omits the variant declaration on the fired branch fails the check.

## Considerations
- The risk register rates "family policies do not transfer, lose plasticity along lineages, or their pretraining is never approved" as high; this gate, the inheritance gate, scheduled resets and the milestone variant are its mitigations.
- Break-even context for the owner: at 60 GPU-hours of pretraining and a fine-tuning fraction of 0.1, the policy repays itself only after about 590 quadruped designs or about 70 to 130 humanoid designs; a campaign smaller than that should not regret a fired gate.
- A fired gate for one class says nothing about another class; the record is per class, and a later request for another class goes back through the envelope decision.
