## Background
At M4 the learning subsystem's user-visible promise changes from "train a policy" to "avoid training where you can": apply the class's family policy to a new design with no training; inherit a child's policy from its parent through the edit that joins them; distill a policy from something that already behaves well; add a small residual to a baseline controller. Each mechanism is Frontier, each has a pilot, a gate and a kill gate, and each has its own experiment bead that measures its gate at full scale. What no single mechanism bead proves is that an agent can actually drive the workflow end to end through the command surface, on whichever branch the kill gates left standing, and get honest answers: refusals where the system should refuse, labels where a result is experimental or advisory, donors and teachers named in every manifest.

The scenario is the North-Star scenario for this milestone: an agent lengthens the femur by 8% and widens the stance; the family policy of the quadruped class, conditioned on the design, walks it with no training; the report gives its return as a share of the specialist reference for the class, the in-distribution check, and the rank-agreement status that says whether this rung may eliminate designs; when the design is shortlisted, inheritance from its parent reaches the target return in a small share of the steps of an early-stopped from-scratch control.

This bead is the end-to-end script for that capability. Because every mechanism here can have been killed or left unfunded, the script is **branch-aware**: it reads the kill-gate and envelope decisions and asserts the behaviour each outcome implies (the mechanism works and reports correctly; or it is refused as not admitted, naming the decision and the fallback). A script that only passes on the all-green branch would itself be a gate that cannot fail.

## Scope
In: the script; its branch detection; small-scale runs of each mechanism from the user's side with their refusals, labels and manifests asserted; logging; confirming receipts for the three gates' user-side clauses (the decline clause of the family gate; the verb clauses of inheritance and distillation). Out: the full-scale gate experiments (their own beads' scripts: the family experiment, the inheritance experiment, the calibration); the mechanisms; pretraining and `train.family` (BD-320), exercised here only through the envelope refusal.

## Technical approach
**Branch detection (step `branches`).** `frob evidence trace --kill-gate KILL-FAMILY --json`, the same for `KILL-INHERIT`, `KILL-DISTILL` and the residual's kill gate, and the envelope decision's record; the script derives `HAS_FAMILY`, `HAS_INHERIT`, `HAS_DISTILL` (and its restriction to privileged teachers), `HAS_RESIDUAL`, logs them, and selects the expected outcome of every later step. An undecided kill gate is treated as "not admitted" (fail closed), and the script says so.

**Steps.**
1. `edit`: `frob design edit <reference quadruped> set-param femur_length +8% --json`, then `set-param stance_width +6% --json`; assert a new design identity, the reported effect on both symmetry groups, and the invalidation set.
2. `policy-apply`: `frob policy apply --policy class:quadruped --design <edited> --task <velocity> --json`. With a family policy: exit 0 or 2; the report has `in_distribution` with per-parameter margins, `class_reference`, `return_share_of_reference`, and `rank_agreement` (a record and mode, or `unranked`); the bound policy's manifest names the family policy as donor. Without one: exit 5 naming the decision in force and listing the fallback rungs (baseline controller, sampling controller, specialist).
3. `policy-apply-decline`: applying to an out-of-class design (a biped), to a quadruped with a limb 50% longer, and to one with an extra spine joint: each exit 3 with FROB-REF-534 and its reason; no evaluation job is created. (Skipped with the reason logged if there is no family policy.)
4. `policy-apply-ladder-guard`: `--score-for-ladder` without a rank-agreement record → exit 3, FROB-REF-537; with a record whose mode is `discard-only`, a request to select is refused and a request to discard succeeds.
5. `fine-tune-rung`: `frob policy apply … --fine-tune fraction:0.05 --json` (exit 8, then completion); assert the step budget equals 5% of the class reference steps and the result's manifest names its donor.
6. `inheritance-map`: `frob train inherit --child <edited> --donor <parent policy> --dry-run --json` prints the map (correspondences by transport kind, unmatched roles, depth); a fixture edit with a missing correspondence → exit 3, FROB-REF-531 naming the edit and role.
7. `inherit`: `frob train inherit --child <edited> --donor <parent policy> --compare both --json`, short budget. With inheritance admitted or experimental: the run reports `steps_to_parent_verdict`, `depth`, audit-twin status, `origin: inherited(1)` and the label `experimental` unless the gate has passed for the edit class; both comparison modes are reported separately. With inheritance killed: exit 5 naming the decision; the script then starts the child from the prior or from scratch and asserts `origin` accordingly.
8. `lineage-resets`: a chain of six parameter edits with `--reset-depth 5 --audit-rate 2`: assert the depth sequence, a reset at the sixth step with its reason, at least one audit pair, and `origin` labels in subsequent `frob eval run` outputs; a planted-regression donor triggers a regression reset. (Skipped with the reason if inheritance is killed.)
9. `distill`: baseline teacher: `frob control synth … --json`, `frob train distill --teacher baseline:<id> --student mlp:256,256,128 --task <velocity> --seed 3 --json`, short budget; assert the student's manifest lists its teacher and standing, that evaluation used only the student's channels, and that the shuffled-label control run (`--control shuffled-labels`) scores below the true student. On the restricted branch: the baseline teacher is refused (exit 5) and a privileged teacher is accepted; assert the student's actor schema excludes `critic-only` channels.
10. `residual`: `frob train start --network residual:mlp:128,128 --base-controller <id> --json`, short budget: at step zero the wrapped controller's digests equal the baseline's; the saturation share is reported; the manifest records the base controller and authority. Without the residual admitted: the refusal naming its decision.
11. `family-envelope`: `frob train family --class quadruped --dry-run --json` with no approved envelope → a budget-class refusal naming the open question and its default (no pretraining); with an approved envelope the dry run reports the ceiling and the kill rule it will enforce.
12. `manifests`: for every policy produced above, `frob artifact read <policy_id> --json`: the manifest has schema `frob.policy/1`, names donors and teachers, carries the action law, the reset mode and the group block, and the three no-authorization flags are false.
13. `negative-controls`: (a) a build in which `policy.apply` evaluates before checking membership (an evaluation job appears for the biped: caught); (b) an inherited child whose manifest omits its donor (caught by step 12); (c) a distilled student evaluated on teacher states (refused); (d) a residual with a non-zero output bias at initialization (the digest identity of step 10 fails).
14. `gate-receipts`: append user-side confirming receipts: to GATE-CORTEX-11 the decline clause (every out-of-class design declined) when a family policy exists; to GATE-CORTEX-13 the verb clause (the verb refuses without a mapping table and reports the steps to the parent's requirement verdict); to GATE-CORTEX-14 the shuffled-label control's failure at small scale. The full-scale numeric clauses of those gates are measured by the mechanism beads' own scripts and are not asserted here.

**Scale.** All trainings use short budgets on `batch-cpu` or one GPU and assert mechanics, labels and refusals, not learning thresholds; the script labels every such result `mechanics-only`. It runs in minutes on a GPU.

**Logging (the shared helper of `STD-E2E`).** NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/cortex_amortize/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (the mechanism bead exercised), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed, and `origin` where present), measured values with units and thresholds (return share of the class reference; support margins; fine-tune steps against the 5% budget; steps to the parent's verdict; depth; student and shuffled-control scores), `refusal` events with code, pointer, reason and ranked repairs, digests (design identities, policy identities, inheritance-map digest, the baseline-identity digests of step 10), and on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. The `summary` line lists assertions passed and failed, the branch flags, controls caught and gates touched. No private-scene data is logged.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The script exits 0 on a correct build on every combination of branches it can detect, asserting for each mechanism either its working behaviour or its refusal with the decision named; it exits non-zero if a killed mechanism still runs or an admitted one is refused.
- [ ] Steps 1 to 12 log their commands, exit codes, wall time and assertions; skipped steps log the reason and the decision that caused the skip.
- [ ] Every policy produced carries a manifest naming its donor or teachers; every result carries `origin`; experimental and advisory labels are present where the decisions require them.
- [ ] The four negative controls of step 13 are caught.
- [ ] The user-side confirming receipts of step 14 are appended only when their assertions pass and their controls are caught.
- [ ] Two runs with one seed give identical assertion results and identical compared digests.
- [ ] Cancellation during any training step drains and finalizes with no leaked device resources.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`amortize_branch_detection_table` (the script's branch logic, factored into a small helper tested against all combinations of the four decisions and the envelope state, including "undecided → not admitted"); `amortize_fixture_designs_exist` (the edited quadruped, the biped, the long-limb and extra-joint variants compile and have the expected class and support results).
### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script: `scripts/e2e/cortex_amortize.sh`, as specified; run in CI on `batch-cpu` and on the Linux host with a GPU before the M4 exit review; its log is attached to that review.
### Known answers and negative controls
The planted out-of-class set (biped; limb 50% longer; extra spine joint) with known refusal reasons; the zero-change identity for the residual at initialization (digests equal the baseline's); the depth sequence for k = 5 (reset at the sixth step). Negative controls: the four of step 13. Metamorphic relation: re-describing the edited design (joint order, axis conventions) changes no step's outcome. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; `batch-cpu` and one GPU backend, each within its class.

## Considerations
- The most probable real-world state of M4 is a mixed one (for example: no family policy, inheritance experimental, distillation restricted); the branch-aware design is what keeps this script meaningful there, and its log is the plainest statement of what M4 actually delivered.
- The script deliberately does not re-measure the numeric gate clauses; doing so at small scale would produce numbers that look like evidence and are not.
- If the milestone exits in its variant with no family policy, steps 2 to 5 and 11 assert refusals and fallbacks, and the North-Star scenario is recorded as not demonstrated.
