## Background
PPO under its rule battery is the Solid trainer of FrankenRobotics and the only algorithm admitted at M2. The specification lists soft actor-critic (SAC) among the Frontier learning algorithms: an off-policy method with a stochastic actor, two critics whose minimum forms the target, a learned entropy temperature and a replay store, which reuses each collected transition many times. It is not on the critical path, and its benefit is bounded by training's share of the design loop in the phase ledger: an algorithm that halved training time shortens a loop whose training share is q by at most 1/(1 − q/2).

The Frontier rule applies in full. SAC has a pre-funding pilot (bead BD-324: SAC against PPO on the reference quadruped task at equal wall-clock on an incumbent stack, three seeds, about 10 GPU-hours; falsifier: the arm beats PPO neither in time to the threshold nor in final requirement pass rate) and a kill gate, **KILL-ALTALGO**, decided per algorithm by bead N23b-23: if the falsifier holds, SAC is not built and PPO remains the trainer. This bead is the build bead of that algorithm. It exists only on the funded branch: it starts only when the row `sac` of the decision record is `funded` (a pilot that is `falsified`, `undetermined` or "passed, not funded: ceiling" leaves it closed), and until it lands `train.start --algo sac` answers the capability refusal (process exit code 5) with the pilot's identifier and falsifier.

Why wall-clock and not samples decides: off-policy methods look good per sample and cost more per update; in a simulator that produces hundreds of thousands of environment steps per second, wall-clock is the scarce resource. SAC can pay where environment steps are expensive (heavy scenes, estimated-state tasks, the referee tier), which is where its comparison is reported separately.

What a pass funds, in the plan's words: SAC as a second trainer under the same environment semantics (two flags, final observations, physical discount). The risk this bead is written against is the one PPO's battery exists for: an off-policy learner copied from common practice treats a time limit as a termination, takes the post-reset observation as the successor state, uses one per-step discount and computes its entropy for a random variable that was not sampled. Each of those is a rule with a test here.

Tier Frontier; crate `frob-learn` (modules `offpolicy` and `sac`), battery in `frob-conformance`. Rejected alternatives: a host-side replay buffer (it moves every transition across the device boundary, against the device-residency of the learner); an external SAC through the Gymnasium-compatible adapter as a product trainer (untrusted learner provenance, no rule battery); a tanh-squashed actor as a private action law (the registered laws are the only laws; a third may be registered later under the same interface).

## Technical approach
**Replay storage (DESIGN).** `ReplayStorage` is a ring of transitions allocated once in the device arena under the same lease and epoch rules as rollout storage. Per transition: actor and critic observation tensors; the stored action variable under the registered action law with its per-component tag; the reward as the integral over the control interval; `dt_sim`; `terminated`, `truncated`, `invalid`; the successor observation, which is the *final observation* of the episode when the episode ended in that step (never the post-reset observation); the world address and decision index. Capacity is computed from the device budget the placement planner grants and is printed at admission; a request that does not fit is refused before allocation with FROB-REF-602 (the memory estimate exceeds the device budget at admission, N21-REF; the ring's bytes beside the world batch's). Sizing example (illustrative sizes, DESIGN arithmetic): 48 actor and 20 critic-only channels and 12 actions give (2 × 68 + 12 + 2) × 4 bytes = 600 bytes plus about 30 bytes of tags, flags and address, so 10⁷ transitions need about 6.3 GB of a 24 GB card.

**Minibatches by address.** The indices of update u are drawn from a counter-based stream registered as `replay-sampling`, keyed by (run identity, u), uniformly over valid entries. No host random number generator is involved, so under the deterministic profile the same seed gives the same weights digest twice (class D2).

**Target (two masks, physical discount).** For a sampled transition with successor s′ and a fresh action a′ ~ π(·|s′):

    y = r + γ(Δt)·(1 − terminated)·[min(Q̄₁(s′, a′), Q̄₂(s′, a′)) − α·log π(a′|s′)],   γ(Δt) = exp(−β·Δt),

with Δt the transition's `dt_sim`, β the task's discount rate in 1/s, Q̄ᵢ the target critics and α the temperature. Termination cuts the bootstrap; truncation does not (the successor is the preserved final observation). Critic loss: the mean over valid samples of (Qᵢ(s, a) − y)², i = 1, 2, differentiating the current critic output. Target critics follow by θ̄ ← (1 − τ)·θ̄ + τ·θ after every critic update (DESIGN default τ = 0.005). Critics read the task's `critic-only` channels; the actor never does.

**Actor and temperature (entropy follows the action law).** SAC is admitted only under the action law `clipped-with-endpoint-mass`: the entropy of the latent Gaussian grows without limit as σ grows although the applied action saturates, so entropy maximization under `latent-then-limit` inflates σ without changing behaviour. `train.start --algo sac --action-law latent-then-limit` is answered with the capability refusal naming the admissible law. The actor loss is

    L_π = −α·H(π(·|s)) − E_ε[min(Q₁, Q₂)(s, a(ε))],   a(ε) = limit(μ(s) + σ(s) ⊙ ε),

where H and its gradients in μ and log σ are the closed forms of the registered law (density inside the bounds, point masses at the bounds), and the second term is differentiated along the sample path (the derivative of a(ε) is 1 inside the bounds and 0 at a bound). The noise ε comes from the qualified action sampler by address. The temperature is learned from L_α = α·(H(π) − H̄), with the target entropy H̄ an argument (DESIGN default: per channel, the entropy of a Gaussian whose σ is 10% of the channel's range; tuned on development seeds only).

**Job.** The training job's state machine is the PPO job's (`Collecting → Updating → Publishing`, commit at iteration boundaries): actor weights and the observation normalizer are frozen during a collection; an iteration collects `steps_per_collection` control intervals in every world, appends them to the ring, then runs U updates of batch B. The update-to-data ratio U·B/(worlds × steps) is an argument and is reported; the pilot's tuned value is the default. Observation normalization is global, versioned and invertible, updated between collections. Invalid worlds and padded elements have weight zero in every loss and statistic.

**The rules and their battery (DESIGN names; battery `cortex-offpolicy` in `frob-conformance`, written against a small trait so that it tests any off-policy implementation).** O-1 two masks in the target. O-2 the successor of an ended episode is its final observation. O-3 physical discount per transition. O-4 valid samples only. O-5 the stored action variable and the entropy are those of the registered law. O-6 frozen behaviour during collection. O-7 target-network update exact. O-8 the target uses the minimum of two critics. O-9 minibatch indices are a function of (run, update) only. Each rule has a fixture and a planted defect that must fail it. The battery is a Gauntlet entry, run as a known-answer row of the G1 tier (`eval.run` evaluates a candidate on scenarios and has no `--impl`, `--backend` or `--seed`, VERBS-eval). Its fixtures form one row of the suite's file `crates/frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml`, cited by the provisional tag `offpolicy-01` until the allocation ledger issues its number (a bead never invents one): this bead files the `[[block]]` request `registry = "ka"`, `count = 1`, `owner = "N23a-37"` in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/allocations.toml` (N38a-40) and maps the tag in one commit when the block is issued. The row's truths include the bootstrap pair of KA-576 and the discounts of KA-579 applied to the off-policy target; its entries are per implementation (`sac` here, `td3` added by N23a-38) and per site (`host` for `batch-cpu`, `cuda`, `metal`); its planted defects are its controls in `controls.toml`, under the `gauntlet-mutations` umbrella.

**Surface.** `train.start --algo sac` adds `replay_capacity`, `utd`, `batch`, `tau`, `target_entropy`, `warmup_steps`; MLP networks only (a recurrent policy with replayed hidden state is refused as not admitted). `train.status` adds `replay_fill`, `utd`, `alpha`, the two critics' mean values and the target-critic lag. The manifest records `algo: sac`, the hyperparameters and the action law. The registry row `sac` in `registries/algorithms.toml` moves from `pilot-pending` to `admitted` with the pilot record's identifier when this bead closes.

**Determinism and evidence.** Class D2 per backend under `device-replay`; evidence class `Tested`; every output labelled `frontier`. A run under the labelled nondeterministic profile is class D4 and is re-evaluated before it is cited.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The bead was started with the decision row `sac` of KILL-ALTALGO recorded as `funded`; the registry row cites that record.
- [ ] The battery `cortex-offpolicy` (`FROB_TEST_SEED=7 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- offpolicy-01`, the devices' annex features built) passes for this implementation on `batch-cpu` and on each GPU backend, and with `--features gauntlet-mutations` each of its planted defects is reported `caught`: a single `done` flag in the target; the post-reset observation as successor; one per-step discount; a `Faulted` world that changes a gradient; Gaussian entropy under the clipped law; one critic in the target.
- [ ] On-device backward programs of the three losses match FrankenTorch autograd within 10⁻⁵ relative on 10³ random batches.
- [ ] The same seed gives identical weights digests twice under `device-replay`; minibatch indices are unchanged by a different ring capacity that holds the same entries.
- [ ] Behaviour, not plumbing: on the reference quadruped's flat velocity task SAC attains the registered return threshold under the attainment rule on `batch-cpu` and on one GPU backend; the record is a training gate record with a zero-weight and a random policy failing the same threshold and a replay of the exported checkpoint reproducing the return.
- [ ] The comparison with PPO at equal wall-clock on our engine (five paired seeds; time to attainment and final requirement pass rate; ceiling stated first; A/A and potency arms) is recorded as a method-gate record and reported whichever arm wins; where SAC wins neither measure the capability is listed `admitted, no advantage measured` and is never a default.
- [ ] `frob train start --algo sac --json` is reachable, refuses the latent action law and a recurrent network, refuses a replay request above the device budget before allocation, and is cancellable with no leaked device resources; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-learn` lists the capability, its rules and its kill-gate status.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`target_two_masks` (continuing task cut externally with target value 10 and γ = 0.99 → 9.9; a true deadline with terminal reward 0 → 0; both flags → 0); `target_twin_min_and_entropy` (r = 0.5, γ = 0.99, Q̄ = (1, 3), α = 0.2, log π = −1.5 → y = 0.5 + 0.99 × 1.3 = 1.787; with `terminated`, y = 0.5); `successor_is_final_observation` (an episode that ends in step t stores the final observation, compared bitwise with the environment's `final_obs`); `discount_from_dt_sim` (β = 0.5025 /s: γ = 0.99000 at 20 ms and 0.99499 at 10 ms); `polyak_exact` (θ̄ = 1, θ = 3, τ = 0.005 → 1.010); `entropy_follows_clipped_law` (against the quadrature reference of the action-law bead at a bound; differs from the Gaussian value); `replay_indices_by_address`; `invalid_world_weight_zero`; `frozen_behaviour_in_collection` (an update attempted mid-collection is refused); `latent_law_refused_for_sac`; `capacity_refused_before_allocation`; `critic_gradient_from_current_output`.
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/cortex_offpolicy.sh`, owned by this bead (N23a-38 adds its step `td3`), step `sac`: (1) the battery, `FROB_TEST_SEED=7 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features <cuda|metal> -- offpolicy-01` (`batch-cpu` always and each device whose annex feature is built; a device absent on the host is `Skipped` with the reason, never passed), then the same with `--features gauntlet-mutations` for the six planted defects; from the hash-named receipt under `target/gauntlet/receipts/` the script asserts every `sac` entry `pass` and every control line `caught` with its intended value; (2) `frob train start <reference quadruped> --algo sac --task <flat-velocity> --backend batch-cpu --seed 7 --key e2e-sac-1 --json` (expects exit code 8; the registry's positional design and idempotency flag), `frob job wait`, `frob train status <job> --json`, `frob train export <job> --out target/e2e-artifacts/cortex_offpolicy/sac/ --json` (the row's `out`); (3) the evaluations on the development stream, `frob eval run <reference quadruped> --policy <id> --task <flat-velocity> --stream dev --key e2e-sac-ev --json` and the controls `--policy zero:<id>` and `--policy random:<id>:7` (N23b-15's forms; keys `e2e-sac-ev-zero`, `e2e-sac-ev-random`), and the replay of the exported checkpoint (key `e2e-sac-ev-replay`); (4) the refusals as dry runs, each under its own key: `--action-law latent-then-limit` (`e2e-sac-r1`; expects exit 5, FROB-REF-724 naming the admissible law `clipped-with-endpoint-mass`), `--network gru:64` (`e2e-sac-r2`; expects exit 5, FROB-REF-724, a recurrent network not admitted for `sac`), an oversized `--replay-capacity` (`e2e-sac-r3`; expects FROB-REF-602 with the ring's bytes as `observed` and the device budget as `required`, and no allocation). It sources the shared helper of `STD-E2E` and writes NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/cortex_offpolicy/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: every `cmd` with exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block (class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` lines with units and thresholds (return, pass rate, steps and seconds to attainment, both throughput units, update-to-data ratio, α, replay fill); `refusal` lines with code and repairs; digests compared across the two same-seed runs (the second under key `e2e-sac-2`, so that it computes rather than returning the first one's receipt) with the first differing iteration on failure; a `summary` with assertion counts and `gates: ["KILL-ALTALGO"]` as consulted. A reduced mode (`E2E_SMALL=1`) asserts the mechanics on a short budget and labels itself so.
### Known answers and negative controls
The fixtures above are exact arithmetic: 9.9 and 0 (the bootstrap pair of KA-576), 0.99 and 0.99499 (KA-579), 1.787, 0.5 and 1.010. Negative controls are the six planted defects of the battery, registered in the negative-control registry against the battery's row, and the three gate-record controls (zero-weight, random, replay). ## Considerations
- Kill gate KILL-ALTALGO (trigger: the pilot's falsifier holds for SAC; fallback: SAC is not built and PPO under its rules remains the trainer). A killed row closes this bead `withdrawn`, citing the SAC arm's `[[pilot.result]]` (N41-17, rule 7; where TD3's row is funded the close notes that N23a-38 carries the shared substrate); nothing else in the milestone waits for it.
- Memory: the ring competes with the world batch for the card's memory; the admission estimate prints both, and a ring that does not fit beside the requested worlds is refused, never silently shrunk.
- A deterministic successor action is not used: a′ is sampled, so the sampler's joint-law rule (P-12) applies to the update as well as to collection.
- The actor's information contract holds: rewards live in the store for the critics and are never an actor input unless the task declares them an `actor` channel.
- Performance envelope: more updates per collected step is the method's point and its cost; the learner's share of wall-clock is reported to the phase ledger as for PPO.
