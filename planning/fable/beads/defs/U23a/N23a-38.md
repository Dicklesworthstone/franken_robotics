## Background
The specification lists TD3 (the twin-delayed deterministic policy gradient) among the Frontier learning algorithms, beside PPO, which is Solid and the only trainer admitted at M2. TD3 is off-policy like SAC but learns a *deterministic* actor: two critics whose minimum forms the target, noise added to the target action and clipped (target smoothing), and an actor that is updated less often than the critics. It reuses each transition many times, which matters only where environment steps are expensive; in a simulator that produces hundreds of thousands of environment steps per second, wall-clock is the scarce resource, and that is how it is judged.

The Frontier rule applies. TD3 has its own pre-funding pilot (one of the six arms of bead BD-324: TD3 against PPO on the reference quadruped task at equal wall-clock on an incumbent stack, three seeds, about 10 GPU-hours; falsifier: the arm beats PPO neither in time to the threshold nor in final requirement pass rate) and its own row of the kill gate **KILL-ALTALGO**, decided by bead N23b-23. The decisions are independent per algorithm: killing SAC says nothing about TD3. This bead is TD3's build bead and exists only on the funded branch of its row; until it lands `train.start --algo td3` answers the capability refusal (process exit code 5) with the pilot's identifier and falsifier. The ceiling is stated first: none of the other algorithms is on the critical path, and each is bounded by training's share of the measured design loop.

What a pass funds, in the plan's words: TD3 as a second trainer under the same environment semantics (two flags, final observations, physical discount). It shares the replay storage, the twin critics, the target networks, the addressed minibatch sampling and the off-policy rule battery with SAC; if SAC's row was killed and TD3's was funded, the shared substrate is built by this bead from the specification in N23a-37, and the dependency on N23a-37 is then satisfied by that bead's closure `withdrawn`, citing the SAC arm's `[[pilot.result]]` with verdict `falsifier-holds` (N41-17, rule 7) and noting that the shared substrate is carried by N23a-38.

Tier Frontier; crate `frob-learn` (module `td3` on `offpolicy`). Rejected alternatives: exploration by unaddressed host noise (not reproducible, and outside the sampler's joint-law rule); a deterministic policy treated as equivariant under a task's symmetry group (a deterministic equivariant policy must emit a symmetric action at a symmetric observation, which can be wrong; see Considerations).

## Technical approach
**Actor and behaviour.** The policy is a = limit(μ(s)): the network's mean followed by the deterministic limiter to the channel bounds. During collection the behaviour is a = limit(μ(s) + σ_e ⊙ ε) with ε standard normal noise drawn by address from the qualified action sampler (stream `action-sampling`), so the joint-law rule holds for exploration noise as it does for PPO's samples. σ_e is given per channel as a fraction of the half-range (DESIGN default 0.1). The applied action is what the store keeps. No likelihood is computed anywhere in TD3, so the same-random-variable rule has nothing to bind; the manifest nevertheless states `policy_kind: deterministic` and the exploration law used in training, and loading the checkpoint as a stochastic policy is refused by the manifest check.

**Target (two masks, physical discount, smoothing).** With target actor μ̄ and target critics Q̄₁, Q̄₂:

    ã = limit(μ̄(s′) + clip(σ̃ ⊙ ε′, −c, c)),      y = r + γ(Δt)·(1 − terminated)·min(Q̄₁(s′, ã), Q̄₂(s′, ã)),

with γ(Δt) = exp(−β·Δt) from the transition's `dt_sim`, s′ the preserved final observation when the episode ended, ε′ addressed noise from a stream registered as `target-smoothing` keyed by (run, update, sample), and σ̃, c fractions of the half-range (DESIGN defaults 0.2 and 0.5). Termination cuts the bootstrap; truncation does not. Critic loss: the mean over valid samples of (Qᵢ(s, a) − y)², i = 1, 2.

**Delayed actor update.** The actor is updated once every d critic updates (DESIGN default d = 2) by descending −Q₁(s, limit(μ(s))) over valid samples; the target actor and target critics are updated by θ̄ ← (1 − τ)·θ̄ + τ·θ in the same step (default τ = 0.005). The gradient passes through the limiter with derivative 1 inside the bounds and 0 at a bound, so a saturated channel receives no actor gradient: the saturation share of μ(s) is reported per collection and the status carries the flag `actor-saturated` above a registered share (DESIGN default 20%).

**Rules added to the battery `cortex-offpolicy` (DESIGN names).** T-1 the actor's parameters change only on updates whose index is a multiple of d. T-2 smoothing noise is clipped to ± c before it is added, and the target action is inside the bounds. T-3 the exported policy is deterministic: one observation, one action, on every backend. Rules O-1 to O-4 and O-6 to O-9 of the battery apply unchanged (O-5 is the entropy rule and does not apply). The battery is the Gauntlet's known-answer row that N23a-37 registers (provisional tag `offpolicy-01` until its allocated number is mapped; N38a-40): this bead adds the row's `td3` entries per site and TD3's planted defects as further controls of that row (on the branch where SAC was killed it registers the row and files the block request itself, as N23a-37 specifies). `eval.run` has no `--impl`, `--backend` or `--seed` (VERBS-eval).

**Job and surface.** The state machine, frozen behaviour during collection, global versioned normalization, valid-sample weights and update-to-data ratio are those of the off-policy job. `train.start --algo td3` adds `explore_sigma`, `smooth_sigma`, `smooth_clip`, `policy_delay`, `tau`, and the replay arguments; MLP networks only. `train.status` adds the saturation share and the critics' mean values. The registry row `td3` moves from `pilot-pending` to `admitted` with the pilot record's identifier when this bead closes.

**Determinism and evidence.** All noise is addressed, so the same seed gives the same weights digest twice under `device-replay` (class D2). Evidence class `Tested`; outputs labelled `frontier`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The bead was started with the decision row `td3` of KILL-ALTALGO recorded as `funded`; the registry row cites that record.
- [ ] The battery `cortex-offpolicy` with rules T-1 to T-3 (`FROB_TEST_SEED=7 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- offpolicy-01`, the devices' annex features built) passes for this implementation on `batch-cpu` and on each GPU backend, and with `--features gauntlet-mutations` each planted defect is reported `caught`: an actor updated on every critic update; unclipped smoothing noise; a target action outside the bounds; an export that still adds exploration noise; a single `done` flag in the target; one critic in the target.
- [ ] On-device backward programs of the critic and actor losses match FrankenTorch autograd within 10⁻⁵ relative on 10³ random batches.
- [ ] The same seed gives identical weights digests twice under `device-replay`; exploration and smoothing draws are independent of slot, batch size and shard.
- [ ] Behaviour, not plumbing: on the reference quadruped's flat velocity task TD3 attains the registered return threshold under the attainment rule on `batch-cpu` and on one GPU backend, in a training gate record with zero-weight and random controls failing the threshold and a checkpoint replay reproducing the return.
- [ ] The comparison with PPO at equal wall-clock on our engine (five paired seeds, ceiling stated first, A/A and potency arms) is recorded as a method-gate record and reported whichever arm wins; where TD3 wins neither measure the capability is listed `admitted, no advantage measured` and is never a default.
- [ ] `frob train start --algo td3 --json` is reachable, cancellable with no leaked device resources, and refuses `--tying equivariant` (see Considerations) and a recurrent network; `CONTRACT.md` lists the capability and its kill-gate status.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`td3_target_twin_min` (r = 0.5, γ = 0.99, Q̄ = (1, 3) → y = 0.5 + 0.99 × 1 = 1.49; with `terminated`, y = 0.5; an external cut with target value 10 → 9.9); `smoothing_noise_clipped_then_limited` (normalized bounds [−1, 1], μ̄(s′) = 0.9, noise draw 0.8 with c = 0.5 → 0.9 + 0.5 = 1.4 → target action 1.0); `policy_delay_two` (over four critic updates the actor's parameters change after updates 2 and 4 only); `polyak_exact` (1, 3, τ = 0.005 → 1.010); `actor_gradient_zero_at_bound` (a channel at its bound contributes zero; inside, one); `exploration_noise_by_address` (identical bits alone, in a batch of 8,192, in another slot); `export_is_deterministic`; `deterministic_checkpoint_refuses_stochastic_load`; `saturation_flag_above_registered_share`.

## Considerations
- Kill gate KILL-ALTALGO, row TD3 (trigger: the pilot's falsifier holds; fallback: TD3 is not built and PPO remains the trainer).
- Equivariance holds in law, not pathwise: at an observation fixed by an element of the observation-preserving symmetry group, a deterministic equivariant policy must emit an action fixed by that element, which the two-latch fixture of the control chapter shows can be wrong; the policy classes of this system therefore keep their randomness at fixed points. A TD3 policy has none, so weight tying under the group is refused for `td3` and symmetry enters only as augmentation.
- A deterministic actor that saturates stops learning in the saturated channels; the reported share is the early warning, and scaling position targets sensibly is the repair, not a change to the limiter.
- Evaluation of a TD3 policy uses the deterministic export; the five-seed rule applies to training seeds as for any policy.
