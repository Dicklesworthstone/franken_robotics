## Background
The specification lists evolution strategies among the Frontier learning algorithms, with one sentence of design: they use the sibling simulator's derivative-free optimizers through the airlock, for small policies and for the gains of control rung R1. An evolution strategy needs no gradient and no value function: it perturbs a parameter vector, runs whole episodes with each perturbed copy, and moves the vector toward the perturbations that scored better. It suits a batched simulator in one respect (thousands of worlds can each carry one perturbation) and is bounded in another (the number of parameters it can move usefully is small).

The Frontier rule applies. The method has its own pre-funding pilot (one of the six arms of bead BD-324: a small policy of at most about 10⁴ parameters perturbed by antithetic Gaussian noise, one perturbation per world, rank-shaped returns, and the same method on the gains of a baseline controller; against PPO on the reference quadruped task at equal wall-clock on an incumbent stack, three seeds, about 10 GPU-hours; falsifier: the arm beats PPO neither in time to the threshold nor in final requirement pass rate) and its own row of the kill gate **KILL-ALTALGO**, decided by bead N23b-23. This bead is the build bead and exists only on the funded branch of that row; until it lands `train.start --algo es` answers the capability refusal (process exit code 5) with the pilot's identifier and falsifier. If the row is killed, the decision record states the consequence: the gains of the baseline rung are tuned by the optimizers of the design-optimization subsystem directly, outside Cortex. The ceiling is stated first: none of the other algorithms is on the critical path; each is bounded by training's share of the measured design loop.

Where the optimizers come from. FACT (to be re-read at the pin): the sibling crate `fs-dfo` has CMA-ES with restart schedules. Sibling crates are reached only through the airlock crate `frob-fsim`, whose feature group `optimize` exposes them behind the FrankenRobotics-native trait `Optimizer` (ask for candidates, tell their scores), so that a sibling API change costs one crate. The group is off by default, and its consumer list (a DESIGN list whose change is an architecture decision record) names the design-optimization, task, dynamics, mission and analysis crates, not `frob-learn`. This bead therefore carries the decision record that adds `frob-learn`, behind a feature `es` that is off by default, to that list.

Tier Frontier; crate `frob-learn` (module `es`, feature `es`). Rejected alternatives: writing an optimizer in-house as the main path (the siblings exist and the airlock contains their volatility; an in-house rule is kept only as the fallback below); calling the design-optimization crate from `frob-learn` (an import from a higher layer, which the layer check rejects); evolution strategies for large networks (the dimension limit is part of the admission).

## Technical approach
**Parameters.** `EsParams::Policy(NetworkSpec)`: the flattened weights of an MLP actor with a deterministic head, a = limit(μ(s)); admitted up to a dimension limit (DESIGN default 10⁴ parameters, the pilot's size; a larger request is answered with the capability refusal naming the limit). `EsParams::Gains(ControllerId)`: the gain vector of a baseline controller inside the box its controller record declares.

**One generation.**
1. *Ask.* The driver asks the optimizer for n candidates θ₁ … θ_n (n is an argument; with the fallback rule the candidates are antithetic pairs θ ± σ·εᵢ).
2. *Evaluate.* Candidate i is assigned E worlds (DESIGN default E = 1, the pilot's "one perturbation per world"; larger E trades population for noise). All candidates of a generation run whole episodes in one batch under the candidate's fixed parameters. The two members of an antithetic pair, and every candidate when the optimizer is not antithetic, are evaluated on the *same* world addresses, so that differences between candidates are paired differences on common random inputs.
3. *Fitness.* The mean over the candidate's valid episodes of the episodic return (the task's physical discounting applies: rate terms are integrated over simulated time). Invalid worlds have weight zero; a candidate with no valid episode is told to the optimizer as `Faulted` and takes no part in ranking.
4. *Shape.* Fitness values are replaced by centred ranks in [−½, ½] (the best gets ½, the worst −½, ties share the mean rank), so that the update is invariant under monotone transformations of the return.
5. *Tell.* The shaped scores go back to the optimizer by candidate identity; its state (a stored, hashed object) advances; the new mean is published as the iteration's weights at the commit boundary of the training job.

**The local fallback rule (also the pilot's arm).** Where the `optimize` group is absent or fails admission, or the dimension exceeds what the admitted optimizer accepts, the driver uses the isotropic antithetic estimator: with n/2 pairs and shaped scores u⁺ᵢ, u⁻ᵢ,

    θ ← θ + (η/(n·σ))·Σᵢ (u⁺ᵢ − u⁻ᵢ)·εᵢ,

with step η and perturbation scale σ as arguments. Which rule produced a run is in its manifest (`optimizer: airlock:cma-es@<pin>` or `local:antithetic`); when the group is requested by name and absent, the verb answers FROB-REF-790 (foundation group absent) and does not fall back silently. That answer is the airlock's admission check (N33c-32), driven by the registry: this bead's change registers the predicate `train-es-airlock-optimizer` in `frob_verbs::admission::predicates` (N33c-32's predicate form; it holds for a resolved `train.start` request with `algo = es` and `optimizer = airlock:cma-es`, and for nothing else) and, in the same change, enters the `train.start` row's entry `needs_groups = [{ groups = ["optimize"], when = "train-es-airlock-optimizer" }]` in place of the row's `needs_groups = []` of M2 (N33c-32's lint `needs-groups-known` refuses an entry whose predicate is not registered, so the entry cannot precede the predicate). The entry passes that lint, NJL-01's generator appends FROB-REF-790 to the row's refusal list, and the same change sets the expectation of VERBS-train's test `needs_groups_es` for `train.start` to that entry, with 790 in the generated list.

**Perturbations by address.** Every perturbation component is a counter-based draw from a stream registered as `es-perturbation`, keyed by (run identity, generation, pair index, parameter index); no host random number generator is involved. A generation therefore replays bit-identically, and a perturbation never has to be stored: it is regenerated from its address at the update.

**Job and surface.** One iteration of the training job is one generation; the state machine and commit rule are the PPO job's. `train.start --algo es` adds `population`, `episodes_per_member`, `sigma`, `step`, `optimizer` (`airlock:cma-es` or `local:antithetic`), `params` (`policy` or `controller:<id>`). `train.status` adds the generation index, best and mean fitness of the generation (descriptive), the optimizer's step-size state and the count of faulted candidates. The exported checkpoint is an ordinary policy with `policy_kind: deterministic` and `algo: es` in its manifest; for gains, the output is a controller record with the tuned vector and its provenance. The registry row `es` moves from `pilot-pending` to `admitted` with the pilot record's identifier when this bead closes.

**Determinism and evidence.** Class D2 per backend under the deterministic profile for the evaluator and the fallback rule; the airlock optimizer's own determinism is established by its admission test and recorded with its pin. Evidence class `Tested`; outputs labelled `frontier`.

## Test plan
### Unit tests
`centred_ranks` (fitness (3, 1, 2, 2) → ranks (½, −½, 0, 0)); `antithetic_update_one_pair` (one pair, ε = (1, 0), f⁺ > f⁻, n = 2, η = 0.1, σ = 0.1 → θ moves by (η/(n·σ))·(½ − (−½))·ε = (0.5, 0)); `perturbation_by_address` (same bits alone, in a population of 4,096, on another shard; parameter j unchanged when the dimension grows); `pair_shares_world_addresses`; `faulted_candidate_excluded`; `quadratic_fixture_fallback` (a `synthetic` fixture: f(θ) = −‖θ − θ*‖² in 10 dimensions from θ = 0 with θ* = (1, …, 1), registered seed, population 64, σ and η; the distance to θ* after 200 generations is below a tolerance that is to set at the first run and then frozen, DESIGN proposal 0.05); `quadratic_fixture_airlock` (the same through the airlock optimizer, feature on); `dimension_limit_refused`; `group_absent_is_790_not_fallback`; `es_airlock_predicate_registered` (the predicate holds for `--algo es --optimizer airlock:cma-es`, not for `--optimizer local:antithetic` and not for `--algo ppo`; the row's entry passes `needs-groups-known`); `monotone_transform_invariance` (replacing f by exp(f) leaves every update bit-identical).
### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/cortex_es.sh`: `frob train start <reference quadruped> --algo es --params policy --network mlp:32,32 --optimizer local:antithetic --population 256 --task <flat-velocity> --backend batch-cpu --seed 7 --sync-wait-ms 0 --key e2e-es-1 --json` (expects exit code 8 with the job handle: the bounded wait of 0 ms elapses, where the command line's default would wait to completion; the registry's positional design and idempotency flag), `frob job wait <job> --json` (waits to completion), `frob train status <job> --json`, `frob train export <job> --out target/e2e-artifacts/cortex_es/ --json`, `frob eval run <reference quadruped> --policy <id> --task <flat-velocity> --stream dev --key e2e-es-ev --json`, the controls `--policy zero:<id>` and `--policy random:<id>:7` (N23b-15's forms; keys `e2e-es-ev-zero`, `e2e-es-ev-random`) and the checkpoint replay (key `e2e-es-ev-replay`); the same with `--optimizer airlock:cma-es --key e2e-es-cma` where the group is admitted (otherwise the step asserts FROB-REF-790 and logs `skipped: capability absent` for the run); a gain-vector run with `--params controller:<reference baseline> --key e2e-es-gain`; a refused oversized network (`--network mlp:256,256`, about 8 × 10⁴ parameters against the limit of 10⁴, key `e2e-es-big`; expects exit 5, FROB-REF-724 naming the limit, before any world is created). It sources the shared helper of `STD-E2E` and writes NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/cortex_es/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: every `cmd` with exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block; `metric` lines with units and thresholds (return, pass rate, generations and seconds to attainment, both throughput units, faulted candidates); `refusal` lines with code and repairs; digests of two same-seed runs (the second under key `e2e-es-2`) with the first differing generation on failure; `summary` with assertion counts and `gates: ["KILL-ALTALGO"]` as consulted.
### Known answers and negative controls
The rank and update fixtures are exact arithmetic; the quadratic has a closed-form optimum. Negative controls: a planted evaluator that gives the two members of a pair different worlds (the pairing check fails); a planted perturbation stream advanced by a host counter (the replay digest differs); a planted fallback to the local rule when the group was requested (the 790 test fails); a planted shaping that uses raw returns (the monotone-transform test fails); and the three controls of the gate record. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; each backend against class D2.

## Considerations
- Kill gate KILL-ALTALGO, row evolution strategies (trigger: the pilot's falsifier holds; fallback: the method is not built in Cortex, PPO remains the trainer, and baseline gains are tuned by the design-optimization subsystem's optimizers).
- The `optimize` group adds about 12 crates and 84 thousand lines to the build (FACT from the sibling manifests, to be re-read); that cost is why the feature is off by default and why the local rule exists.
- Whole-episode fitness wastes the per-step information PPO uses; the method is expected to win only for very small parameter vectors (gains, small reflex policies) and where rewards are sparse; the comparison is reported, not assumed.
- A deterministic policy has no randomness at symmetric observations, so weight tying under a task's symmetry group is refused for `es`, as for any deterministic policy class.
- Episodes run to their end before any update, so a generation's wall-clock is set by the longest episode; the status reports it.
