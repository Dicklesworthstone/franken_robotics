## Background

Training is the dominant cost of published robot design loops, and FrankenRobotics does not claim a faster
trainer: its lever is to train fewer designs. Engine and learner throughput are therefore *parity* requirements.
An infinitely fast environment step would speed training by at most about 7.7 times on one incumbent stack and
about 1.4 times on another; the learner is about 13% of training wall-clock on one incumbent stack. If our
training time were much worse than an incumbent's, every other lever would have to absorb the loss, and the
ten-fold test charges it in full.

GATE-CORTEX-05 (performance, M2, TARGET): wall-clock to the return threshold of the reference training gate
(PPO on the reference quadruped's flat velocity task) on one RTX 4090 is at most 1.25 times an incumbent's
wall-clock for the comparable task, re-measured on the same card under the disclosure block. Published context:
about 410 s (read by eye from a published figure, ± 20 s, about 45 s of it compilation), so the planning value
of the limit is 513 s. This is parity, not advantage. It is row 4 of the performance targets: time to the return
threshold, cold start, compilation and admission included, control decimation 10; tolerance at most 1.25 times
the incumbent reproduced on our machine; ambition at most the incumbent's time.

Two rules sharpen "time to a threshold". A recorded score crossing is a descriptive event; attainment needs
error control over every evaluation look that could have produced the crossing (N23b-33); in a parity comparison
both arms are scored by the same rule on the same stream sizes, and the incumbent's published time is context.
And a performance ratio is admitted only as the one-sided 95% lower confidence bound of a paired ratio.

## Scope

In: the measurement protocol for both arms; the paired statistic; the disclosure block; the gate record with
its controls; the reading that the learning-kernel kill gate takes from it; the learner's share for the phase
ledger.
Out: optimizing throughput (BD-217 and the profile-guided work); the PPO trainer (BD-197, BD-198); the
incumbent harness and its measurement (BD-039, BD-040, BD-142); the two-card scaling row (BD-199).

## Technical approach

**Arms.** Ours: `frob train start` on workload Q (reference quadruped, 12 actuated joints, 18 degrees of
freedom, step 2 ms, control decimation 10, 50 Hz control) on tier `gpu-cuda`, one RTX 4090, cold start (kernel
compilation or cache state disclosed, admission, graph capture, training). Incumbent: the pinned incumbent in
its strongest warm or cached configuration, frozen after tuning on development fixtures only, on the comparable
task (differences in robot, terrain, command distribution and rates listed).

**Scoring, identical for both arms.** A fixed development evaluation stream disjoint from training worlds; a
cadence registered before the run (every look kept); `crossing` = the first look whose point score met the
target (descriptive); `attained` = the first look at which the registered procedure rejects "at or below
target": error spent across looks as αₖ = α/[k·(k + 1)] with α = 0.05, and at look k an exact one-sided binomial
bound at level αₖ for a success target, or a confidence sequence on bounded returns for a return target. Time to
threshold is the wall-clock from process start to the end of the attaining look's training segment (evaluation
time is charged). A run that does not attain within its budget is right-censored at the budget; the set of runs
is summarized by a restricted mean. Evaluation uses the same stream sizes for both arms: the incumbent's
exported weights are scored by the same episodes count per look, through its own environment for its arm.

**Statistic.** At least ten pairs; the two arms of a pair run back to back in randomized order on the same host
state. With aᵢ the incumbent's time and bᵢ ours, ℓᵢ = ln(aᵢ/bᵢ), mean ℓ̄ and standard deviation s, the bound is
L = exp(ℓ̄ − t·s/√n), t = 1.833 for n = 10 (the 95th percentile of Student's distribution with 9 degrees of
freedom; for n > 10 the t of n − 1 degrees of freedom). The gate passes when L ≥ 1/1.25 = 0.8, that is, our time
is at most 1.25 times the incumbent's as a lower bound on the ratio, never on the mean. A ratio stated without
this bound is refused (FROB-REF-863, a performance ratio without a paired lower bound; repair: run the pairs).
Censored pairs enter at the budget and are flagged; a gate with a censored run of ours cannot pass.

**Host state of a pair (DESIGN, so that "same host state" is a rule and not a judgement).** Before and after
each arm the harness samples the host's one-minute load average, the GPU utilization and memory held by other
processes on the card, and the card temperature. A pair is discarded when another process held more than 5%
GPU utilization during either arm, or the two arms' mean load averages differ by more than 25%, or the card
started either arm above 80 °C. Discarded pairs are counted, logged with the reason and replaced, so the gate
always rests on at least ten kept pairs; a campaign that discards more than half its pairs is reported as
`host-unstable` and claims nothing. (Reason: `trj` is often heavily loaded; the thresholds are starting values
the Performance lead may tighten, recorded in the gate record.)

**Training profile.** Our arm trains under the default `device-replay` profile (class D2). A `fast-training`
(class D4) variant may be measured as a second, labelled row; its looks are scored only by re-evaluating each
look's weights on the development stream under `device-replay`, because no number observed during a D4 run is
evidence (FROB-REF-861), and the re-evaluation time is charged to its clock.

**Disclosure block (mandatory; a figure without the complete block is refused as a Performance claim,
FROB-REF-862; repair: supply the missing fields).** Hardware and driver; backend, compiler and kernel versions;
world count; time step, substeps and control decimation; solver iterations and tolerance; contact capacity and
mean active contacts per world; sensors enabled; whether the policy and learning are included (yes); scene;
determinism class; warm-up and duration; whether launch graphs were captured; median and 99th-percentile step
time; peak memory; the unit of every rate with the decimation beside it; row capacity and the counts of
`Degraded` and `Faulted` worlds; profile and precision profile; fingerprint digest; placement receipt with the
requested and actual backend per stage, fallback count, transfer bytes and synchronization count; cold or warm
with compile and admission times and cache state; thermal state, soak duration and host load; the number of
pairs, the lower bound and the comparator's revision and configuration; energy where the platform exposes
credible data, and a named gap where it does not, never a zero. (The mixed-batch fields of the block do not
apply to workload Q and are written `n/a`.)

**Comparator state.** Read from the decision on re-measurement (N23b-28): `re-measured` gives a verdict;
`published-context` records our time against 513 s with that label and no verdict.

**Behaviour, not plumbing.** Each of our runs carries the training gate record (N23b-15): the trained policy
attains; a zero-weight and a random policy fail the same threshold; the exported checkpoint replays. A fast run
that learned nothing is not a time to threshold.

**What else reads the measurement.** The learning-kernel kill gate: its second clause fires if this gate is
missed by more than two times after one optimization pass (DESIGN reading: our time above twice the gate's
limit, that is L < 0.4, or above 1,025 s under `published-context`). The phase ledger: the learner's share of
training wall-clock on our stack (policy forward, backward, optimizer) is recorded beside the incumbent's about
13%.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: GATE-CORTEX-05 is recorded with measured values: ten or more paired times, the lower bound L against 0.8, the disclosure block, the comparator state and both arms' attained looks.
- [ ] Both arms are scored by the same attainment rule on the same stream sizes; no arm's time is a first crossing.
- [ ] Under `published-context` the record shows our time against 513 s with the label and no `pass`.
- [ ] Each of our runs has a valid training gate record; a run whose controls do not fail the threshold is excluded and reported.
- [ ] Every pair's host-state samples are in the record; discarded pairs are listed with their reason; a record with an incomplete disclosure block is refused (FROB-REF-862) and a ratio without its bound is refused (FROB-REF-863).
- [ ] The paired-bound known answer holds in the harness (ten pairs of ratio 1.5 give 1.5), and so does its A/A known answer: of 100 simulated A/A campaigns at most 9 clear 1.02.
- [ ] The measurement is passed to the learning-kernel kill gate's record with the reading "missed by more than two times: yes or no".
- [ ] The learner's share of training wall-clock is recorded for the phase ledger.
- [ ] The gate is recorded by a session that did not implement the trainer, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-CORTEX-05 --receipt <hash>`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance training_parity`:
- `paired_bound_known_answer`: ten pairs with aᵢ/bᵢ = 1.5 → L = 1.5.
- `aa_campaigns`: 100 A/A campaigns of ten pairs (`synthetic`: log-ratios drawn from a normal law with mean 0 and standard deviation 0.05 from an addressed stream) → at most 9 bounds L exceed 1.02.
- `bound_arithmetic`: times (incumbent 410 s; ours 480 s) in all ten pairs → ratio 0.854, L = 0.854 ≥ 0.8, pass; ours 540 s → 0.759, fail.
- `censored_cannot_pass`: one of our runs censored at the budget → the gate cannot be `pass`.
- `same_rule_both_arms`: a record whose incumbent arm is scored by first crossing is rejected.
- `kill_reading`: L = 0.39 → "missed by more than two times: yes"; L = 0.45 → "no".
- `disclosure_complete`: a record missing any field of the block (each field removed in turn, energy included) is refused with FROB-REF-862 naming the field; a record whose energy field is `0` where the platform exposes no data is refused the same way.
- `ratio_without_bound_refused`: a record carrying a mean ratio and no paired lower bound → FROB-REF-863.
- `host_state_discard`: a pair with 12% foreign GPU utilization in one arm is discarded and replaced; a campaign with 6 of 11 pairs discarded is `host-unstable` and has no verdict.
- `d4_look_not_evidence`: a `fast-training` row whose look scores come from the training run rather than `device-replay` re-evaluation → FROB-REF-861.
- `published_context_no_verdict`: comparator provenance `published` → status stays `not-run`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/training_time_parity.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/training_time_parity/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. For each pair, in randomized order:
(a) ours: `frob train start quad-ref --task quad-flat-velocity --backend gpu-cuda --devices 0 --seed <s>
--eval-cadence <registered> --key bd218-p<i>-ours --json` (the design positional and `--backend` as the
train.start row has them; `--eval-cadence` is N23b-33's registration; one key per pair, so no pair is answered
from another's record), polled with `frob train status <job> --json` until `attained` or the budget, then `frob
train export <job> --out policies/ --json` and the evaluations the training gate record (N23b-15) needs: the
controls `frob eval run --policy zero:<id> --task quad-flat-velocity --stream dev --tier gpu-cuda --key
bd218-p<i>-zero --json` and the same with `--policy random:<id>:<seed>` (key `bd218-p<i>-random`), and the replay
of the exported checkpoint on the CPU reference tier, `frob eval run --policy <exported checkpoint> --task
quad-flat-velocity --stream dev --tier batch-cpu --key bd218-p<i>-replay --json`; (b) the incumbent through the
incumbent harness with the same seed index
and cadence. `cmd` events carry the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block
(claim Performance, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` events carry each look (score, target,
level αₖ), `crossing_s`, `attained_s`, the pair's ratio and, at the end, L against 0.8, with units; `assert`
events carry the controls' failures and the gate clause; on failure the log names the first pair that broke a
rule and the artifact paths. The `summary` gives assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-CORTEX-05"]`.
A `--smoke` mode runs one pair on `batch-cpu` with a tiny budget and claims no gate.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Harness known answers: ten pairs of ratio exactly 1.5 give a lower bound of 1.5; of 100 A/A campaigns at most 9 clear 1.02.
- Attainment known answers: a policy at true success 0.89 against a 0.90 target with 30 episodes per look crosses within 50 looks with probability 1 − (1 − 0.89³⁰)⁵⁰ = 0.78548, which is why crossing is not used; with all-success looks, look k needs at least 36, 46, 74 and 103 episodes at k = 1, 2, 10 and 50.
- Negative controls: a planted run with a zero-weight policy that "reaches" a mis-set threshold must be excluded by the training gate record; a planted timing that stops the clock at enqueue instead of completion must be caught by the harness's completion rule; a planted record with the decimation omitted must be rejected.
- Determinism: wall-clock is not deterministic and is treated statistically; the trained policy's evaluation is re-run under a D2 profile and is identical on repetition.

## Considerations

- RISK (high): engine throughput or training time lands below the parity band. Early warning: a paired lower bound under 0.67 of the incumbent on a physics row, or this gate above 1.25 times the incumbent. Mitigations: the parity kill gate of the performance chapter, the learning-kernel kill gate, and the profile deciding where effort goes.
- The training machine is often heavily loaded; pairs are back to back and host load is recorded, and a pair run under different load is discarded by rule, not by judgement.
- "Missed by more than two times" has no formal definition in the plan; the reading above is this bead's and is flagged for the gate register's owner.
- The incumbent's figure was read from a plot; treat 410 s as context only.
