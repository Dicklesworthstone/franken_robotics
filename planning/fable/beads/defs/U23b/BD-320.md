## Background

Training one policy per candidate design is the dominant cost of published design loops. The lever of
FrankenRobotics is to train fewer designs, and the largest term of that lever is the *family policy*: one set of
weights per morphology class (quadruped-like legged, multi-legged, biped and humanoid, wheeled, tracked,
wheel-leg hybrid), conditioned on the design, so that most designs of a campaign are scored without training and
a new design starts from a prior. It is deliberately not one universal policy (ADR-CORTEX-05): the evidence
(FACT, source register; every figure is a simulation return, to be re-read before a gate cites it) is

| Setting | Return with no adaptation, as a share of a specialist's | Fine-tuning cost |
|---|---|---|
| Within one class, in a parametric neighbourhood of the trained templates | 70 to 85% | About 2 to 25% of a from-scratch run stopped at matched return |
| Across topology-diverse designs at affordable pretraining | 10 to 40% | A gain of 2 to 3 times at best, and none on flat terrain |
| Outside the training distribution | Fails outright | Not applicable |

and every figure in the first row is for flat or mildly varied ground with fixed actuation. Hence three design
rules: one policy per class; the policy *declines* designs outside its class or pretraining distribution instead
of scoring them (FROB-REF-534); and its score may rank designs only after rank agreement with specialists has
been measured (BD-328).

When it pays (DESIGN arithmetic on those FACTs): with pretraining cost P, specialist cost s per design and
fine-tuning fraction f, the family policy pays after N > P/(s·(1 − f)) designs. At P = 60 GPU-hours and f = 0.1
that is about 590 quadruped designs (s = 410 s) and about 70 to 130 humanoid designs (s = 0.5 to 1 GPU-hour).
The ceiling on the training phase is 1/f, between 4 and 50 times for f between 0.25 and 0.02, and on the whole
cycle whatever share training has in the measured loop.

Tier: Frontier (BET-15). Pilot before funding: N23b-19. Kill gate KILL-FAMILY: at the gate, return with no
adaptation below 70% of specialists on more than half of the held-out set together with fine-tuning above 20% of
specialist steps, or the pilot's falsifier holds; fallback, no family policy: specialists with inheritance from
the nearest parent, and the baseline controller screens. Pretraining does not start without an owner-approved
compute envelope (N23b-24); the fail-closed default is no family policy.

## Scope

In: the `FamilyPolicy` artifact; consumption of a registered pretraining distribution (a `GeneratorId` with a
`DesignSplit` and its three held-out sets) by digest; the compute-envelope check; the pilot check; the

the generator (BD-287), the split type (N17a-19) and the generator-side support report (N17a-20).

## Technical approach

**Artifact (DESIGN sketch, from the plan's type sketch).**

```rust
/// A family policy: one policy for a generator of designs, with one token per link.
pub struct FamilyPolicy {
    manifest: PolicyManifest,        // schema frob.policy/1: network, action law, discount, channels by role,
                                     // observation schema digests, trained_on, group tying
    generator: GeneratorId,          // the content-addressed distribution over designs it was trained on
    split: DesignSplit,              // training designs against held-out designs, orbit-consistent
    envelope: SupportEnvelope,       // the membership rule; a design outside it is declined
    rank_record: Option<RankAgreementId>,   // without one it is not a rung (FROB-REF-537)
}
pub struct SupportEnvelope { class: MorphologyClass, topology_signatures: Vec<TopologySig>,
                             parameter_box: Vec<(ParamPath, Qty, Qty)>, card_families: Vec<CardFamilyId>,
                             payload_range: (Qty, Qty), strata: Vec<StratumId> }
```

**Pretraining distribution, per class.** Designs are drawn from the morphology generator (a probabilistic
grammar over the typed design term; design i of seed s is a pure function of (`GeneratorId`, s, i) with its
derivation and exact log-probability): the class templates; parameter perturbations of them (limb lengths and
masses within ±20 to ±30%, actuator cards within one family, payloads within the task range); and single-limb
edits as a separate, labelled stratum. Three held-out sets are fixed before training by the `DesignSplit`:
parametric designs inside the box; single-limb edits; and out-of-class designs that the policy must decline. The
split groups designs in one orbit of the observation-preserving symmetry group on the same side, so that a
mirrored copy of a training design cannot appear in a test set. A family-policy score is admitted only for
designs absent from the training manifest.

**Pipeline of `train.family` (numbered).**

1. Resolve class, generator, split, task family and budget; bind immutable identities.
2. Envelope check (the admission check of N36-20, with N23b-24's per-run row and token): estimate
   device-hours. A job is refused at admission with FROB-REF-761 (a job exceeds the compute envelope in force,
   exit 4; the estimate, the class needed and OPEN-CORTEX-1 are attached) in two cases: there is no approved
   per-run row that admits the run's scope with a token matching the decision record, or the row's limits do not
   cover the estimate's upper end. Nothing runs. Two other codes have their own sites. FROB-REF-704 (exit 4) is
   raised only by the daemon's scheduler at dispatch, if the approval expired or was withdrawn while the job
   waited. FROB-REF-701 (exit 4) is raised when the estimate's upper end exceeds the job's own `--budget`.
3. Pilot check: the pilot record must exist and not be `falsified`, and KILL-FAMILY must not have fired;
   otherwise the capability refusal FROB-REF-724 (exit 5) is returned with the missing pilot record or the
   fired kill record in `pointer`, and `frob capabilities` reports `train.family` as `killed` with its fallback.
4. Sample the training designs; reject and count designs that fail the design check (a generator above 50%
   rejection is flagged); plan size buckets so that padding waste stays within the limit.
5. Train with PPO under its rule battery on a mixed batch: each world holds one design as a variant inside
   padded capacities; padded tokens and padded degrees of freedom are masked in kernels, rewards and gradient
   accumulation; observation normalization is per token feature, global over all valid samples. Each design is
   presented under random equivalent descriptions (the convention augmentation of N23b-41: axis conventions and
   joint order before canonicalization, mirrored copies under Γ_obs), because a published multi-embodiment
   policy kept 3.6% of its return on re-described robots without it and 80.6% with it (FACT, source register).
6. At checkpoints evaluate on a development subset of the parametric held-out set (never on the test sets).
7. Export: checkpoint, `frob.policy/1` manifest, mapping table, generator and split digests, support envelope.

**Membership rule and the decline.** `envelope.admits(design) -> Result<(), Refusal>`: (a) the design's
topology signature (limb count, limb types, joint classes per limb, in canonical form) is one of the envelope's
signatures; (b) every perturbed parameter lies in its box interval; (c) actuator cards belong to the trained
families and the payload lies in range; (d) the generator can locate the design (a derivation exists). Any
failure refuses with FROB-REF-534 ("a family policy applied outside its class or its pretraining distribution";
repairs, in order: screen with the baseline or the sampling controller; train a specialist), naming the failing
parameter with its value and interval, or the signature and class. Every path that would score a family policy
on a design calls it first: `policy.apply`, evaluation with a family policy, and the ladder's shared-policy rung.
A declined design is never scored and never appears in a ranking with a family-policy score.

**Mapping table.** The manifest's `channels` list roles, not positions: for a family policy each actuated joint
token maps to a role pattern (for example `leg[i].hip`) with unit, sign, bounds and rate; applying the policy to
a design matches channels by role and refuses on any mismatch (FROB-REF-531; INV-CORTEX-04: a checkpoint is
inseparable from its manifest and mapping table, and a policy reaches a design only through that table).
`trained_on` gains `generator`, `split`, `designs`, `strata` and the approval token's hash.

**Verb (`train.family`, run, first milestone M4).** Input: `class`, `generator`, `split`, `task_family`,
`designs` (count), `budget`, `devices`, the determinism profile `--determinism` (the spelling of every verb that
takes one; `--profile` names a step profile; default `device-replay`, class D2; `fast-training` is enabled only
on `train.start`, N35-06, so a family policy is never D4-trained), `approval`, `features`, idempotency key,
bounded wait.
`--features canonical | si | si-norm` selects the token feature map (N23b-40 builds the three): the canonical
dimensionless features, raw SI features, or SI features with per-feature normalization fitted on the
pretraining set; it exists for the arms D, S and N of PILOT-C-21 (N10b-11). Its default is read from that
pilot's record at admission: `canonical` when the verdict is `adopt`, otherwise (`fallback`, `underpowered`,
`not run`, or no record) `si-norm`, the pilot's declared fallback; the manifest's `trained_on` records the map
and the record it was read from. Output: the
`FamilyPolicy` identity, the envelope, counts per stratum, rejection rate, bucket histogram, steps, device-hours
used against the ceiling, development scores with their looks. Evidence: class `Tested`, tier, determinism of
the training profile (D2 under `device-replay`), labels `frontier`.
`--dry-run` trains nothing and needs no approval token. It reports the bucket plan and the parameter count. With
`--emit-tokens` it also reports the static and dynamic token digests per design: N23b-40's tokenizer diagnostic,
which that bead checks through its test entry because this verb is built after it.

**Campaign cost model (the function GB-28's Lane B tests; DESIGN).** `frob_learn::family::cost::plan(P, s, f,
N) -> CostPlan { breakeven_designs, gain, verdict }` with `breakeven_designs = P/(s·(1 − f))`, `gain(N) =
N·s/(P + N·f·s)` (the training-phase saving of a campaign of N designs, tending to 1/f) and `verdict` `pretrain`
when N exceeds the break-even, otherwise `specialists`. With f unknown the function takes the range f ∈ [0.05,
0.2], returns the break-even over the whole range, and returns `pretrain` only when N exceeds the break-even at
every f of the range. This bead owns the function; the cascade's planner and the estimate attached to a
`train.family` refusal call it. `--dry-run` does not print it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition, part 1: the pilot of the family policy (N23b-19) has run and its falsifier outcome is on file; this bead does not start pretraining on a `falsified` or missing record.
- [ ] Seed done-condition, part 2: a design outside the class is declined, not scored: each design of the out-of-class held-out set gets FROB-REF-534 from every scoring path, with the failing signature or parameter named, and no score is written.
- [ ] `frob train family … --json` refuses without an approval token (exit 4) and runs with one; it stops at its device-hour ceiling.
- [ ] The three held-out sets are fixed before training and no orbit straddles the split.
- [ ] The exported artifact carries generator, split, support envelope, manifest and mapping table; loading it without the manifest fails; a role mismatch is refused (FROB-REF-531).
- [ ] Padded tokens never contribute to a loss or statistic (a planted non-finite value in a padded token changes nothing).
- [ ] The policy trains on a mixed batch of at least three shape classes of one morphology class on a device, and on `batch-cpu` as the reference path; cancellation is tested; `CONTRACT.md` lists the capability, its evidence class and its kill-gate status.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-learn family`:
- `membership_inside`: a template with limb length +15% is admitted.
- `membership_parameter_outside`: limb length +45% with a ±30% box → FROB-REF-534 naming the parameter, value and interval.
- `membership_topology_outside`: a hexapod offered to the quadruped class → FROB-REF-534 naming the signature.
- `membership_card_family`: an actuator card of an untrained family → FROB-REF-534.
- `declined_never_scored`: after a decline the result store holds no score for that design and policy.
- `split_orbit_consistency`: a mirrored copy of a training design lands on the training side.
- `envelope_check_blocks`: no token → FROB-REF-761, exit 4, zero device-seconds; a token whose row allows 10 device-hours against an estimate of 12 → FROB-REF-761 with the class needed; a covered estimate with `--budget` below its upper end → FROB-REF-701; a queued job whose approval expires before dispatch → FROB-REF-704 from the scheduler.
- `pilot_falsified_blocks`: a pilot record marked `falsified` → FROB-REF-724, exit 5, the record named in `pointer`.
- `break_even`: P = 216,000 s, s = 410 s, f = 0.1 → N > 585.4; humanoid 66.7 to 133.3.
- `cost_model_verdicts`: the same P, s, f: N = 500 → `specialists`, N = 700 → `pretrain`; gain(600) = 246,000/240,600 = 1.0224; gain = 1 exactly at the break-even N; f unknown over [0.05, 0.2] → break-even 554.6 to 658.5, N = 600 → `specialists` (the range does not pay throughout), N = 700 → `pretrain`.
- `features_default_from_pilot_record`: no PILOT-C-21 record → `si-norm`; a record with verdict `adopt` → `canonical`; `fallback` or `underpowered` → `si-norm`; an explicit `--features si` overrides and is recorded in `trained_on`.
- `manifest_inseparable`: checkpoint without manifest fails to load; channel role mismatch → FROB-REF-531.
- `padded_tokens_masked`: NaN in padded token features leaves every gradient bit-identical.
- `determinism_option`: `--determinism device-replay` is admitted and recorded in `trained_on`; `--determinism fast-training` is FROB-REF-802 (outside the option's domain on this verb); `--profile device-replay` is FROB-REF-802 (unknown parameter).
- `dry_run_emit_tokens`: `--dry-run --emit-tokens` needs no token, uses zero device-seconds, and gives equal digests for a design and its re-described twin and different digests for two different designs.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/train_family.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/train_family/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. With a small synthetic test token on a tiny
generator: (1) `frob phylum generate --generator @fixture:quad-mini --count 24 --seed 1 --split
@fixture:quad-mini-split --json`; (1b) `frob train family --class quadruped --generator @fixture:quad-mini-twins
--split @fixture:quad-mini-twins-split --task-family velocity --designs 2 --dry-run --emit-tokens --key tf-dry-1
--json`, where the fixture generator's designs 0 and 1 are `quad-mini-0` and its re-described twin (relabelled
links, flipped axis signs, exchanged legs in the file), asserting equal static and dynamic token digests for the
two: the verb-level repetition of N23b-40's check; (2) `frob train family --class quadruped --generator <g>
--split <s> --task-family velocity --designs 16 --budget <small> --key tf-1 --json` without a token (FROB-REF-761,
exit 4, zero device-seconds), then with `--approval <token> --key tf-2`; (3) `frob train status`, asserting that the loss has fallen from its start and that the trained
policy beats the zero-weight and random controls on the training designs (N23b-41's smoke, now through the
verb); (4)
`frob eval run <held-out parametric> --policy <family> --task velocity --stream dev --key tf-ev-1 --json` (a
score is returned) and `frob eval run @fixture:hexapod --policy <family> --task velocity --stream dev --key
tf-ev-2 --json` (FROB-REF-534, exit 3, no score; the code is on eval.run's row in VERBS-eval). The same
refusal through `policy.apply` is asserted by that verb's own script and by `cortex_amortize.sh` (N23a-25,
N23a-36), which are built after this bead. `cmd` events carry the exact command, exit code, wall time and evidence block (claim, class, tier,
determinism, identities, seed); `metric` events carry rejection rate, padding waste and device-hours against the
ceiling; `refusal` events carry code and pointer; on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths
are logged. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["KILL-FAMILY"]` as consulted.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-589 (family break-even: about 590 quadruped designs at P = 60 GPU-hours and f = 0.1; 70 to 130 humanoid designs; ceiling 1/f).
- Negative controls: a planted membership rule that admits everything must fail the out-of-class set; a planted scoring path that bypasses the rule must be caught by `declined_never_scored`; zero-weight and random policies must fail the task threshold on training designs (the training gate record applies to pretraining too).
- Metamorphic relation: relabelling the links of a held-out design, or re-describing it with another axis convention, changes neither its membership verdict nor, after mapping back, its actions.
- Determinism lanes: design sampling is exact by address; under the D2 training profile two runs on one machine give identical checkpoints; across worker counts and backends the trained policy's evaluation agrees inside the D3 envelope.

## Considerations

- RISK (high): family policies do not transfer, lose plasticity along lineages, or pretraining is never approved. M4 has a variant with no family policy: the rungs are then the static check, the baseline controller, the sampling controller, gait orbits and per-design specialists inheriting from the nearest parent; the family-policy gate is recorded failed or `not-run` and the ten-fold record states the lost factor.
- Shared controllers pull a search toward designs that are easy to steer; this bias is measured by region in the rank-agreement record, not here.
- A second refusal exists on the generator side, FROB-REF-541 (a design outside the training generator's support, found by the generator's derivation test); the membership rule raises 534 and cites the 541 finding when the generator reports it (N23-REF's division of the two codes).
- Pretraining at 10¹² steps (over a thousand GPU-hours) is out of scope in any envelope.
