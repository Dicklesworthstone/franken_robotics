## Background

FrankenRobotics never scores a design by the most expensive method first. It uses a ladder of rungs: static
checks; a shared policy (the family policy of the design's class with no adaptation, or the synthesized baseline
controller where the class has no family policy); a family policy fine-tuned at a declared fraction of
specialist steps; a specialist trained for the design; paired re-evaluation on the referee; a rare-failure bound.
The ladder is only as good as the agreement between adjacent rungs, and for the case that matters that
agreement has never been published. FACT (source register; to be re-read): no rank agreement between a shared
policy and specialists was found; the nearest figures are a rank correlation of 0.40 overall (0.50, 0.60 and
0.83 on three robots) between a critic and rollouts of the same shared policy, and Kendall τ of 0.236, 0.740 and
0.159 for weight sharing in architecture search on three benchmarks. Cheap rungs are biased as well as noisy:
shared controllers pull a search toward designs that are easy to steer, and early evaluation favours fast
learners.

The rule (INV-CORTEX-06, ADR-CORTEX-05): a rung's score may rank designs only after its rank agreement with the
higher rung has been measured for that class and task family; no rung selects or discards designs without its
rank-agreement record. This is the decisive early experiment of the program (BET-17). Its consequences are
fixed in advance, on the lower 95% bound of Kendall's τ near the top of the ranking: at least 0.7, together with
the winner check (N23b-35), lets the rung select finalists; between 0.5 and 0.7 it discards only, and at least
the top quarter goes up to the fine-tune rung; below 0.5 it is not a rung (FROB-REF-537).

The experiment runs first as a pilot on incumbent tools at M3 (BD-310, pre-registered by BD-145) and here, at
M4, on our engine. Kill gate KILL-RANK: the ladder's rank gate fails for every morphology class tested;
fallback: the shared-policy rung is `uninformative` everywhere, every candidate that survives static analysis is
scored on the higher rung, the ladder's claim shrinks to stopping and elimination among trained candidates, and
the ten-fold record states which factor was lost.

## Scope

In: the calibration experiment on our engine per class and task family, as its capstone: the design set from
the pre-registration, the scoring of the baseline-controller rung, the reuse of the specialists, and the
statistics on every rung pair; the ledger record; the verb between rungs; enforcement of "no record, no rung"
at the allocation site; the three gates' measurements and records; the kill gate's reading for rungs.
Out: the family policy's three scorings per design, the 256 paired episodes, the score table
`frob.rung-scores/1` and the refusal FROB-REF-537 at `frob-learn`'s own rung-scoring entry points (N23a-24, a
dependency whose table this bead reads); the pilot (BD-310); the tier-pair record (BD-265); the winner check's
statistics (N23b-35, used here); the ladder, the conformal layer and the audit lane (BD-329, BD-345); the
ledger's row versions fed by the audit lane (N28b-22); model-form rank robustness (BD-266, N23b-08).

## Technical approach

**The calibration experiment (one per morphology class and task family).**

1. *Designs.* 40 designs of one class, drawn by a keyed stream before any score exists (the pre-registered
   set); the planted winner of the class is among them where one exists.
2. *Three scores per design.* The family policy's scores (a) and (b) and the per-episode pairs of step 3 arrive
   as the table `frob.rung-scores/1` written by N23a-24; this bead scores the baseline controller itself.
   (a) The shared policy on the fast tier with no adaptation: the family policy
   where it exists, and separately the baseline controller. (b) The family policy after a fine-tune at 5% of
   specialist steps. (c) A specialist promoted on the referee (the specialists of the family-policy experiment
   are reused, which cuts the cost from about 45 GPU-hours to about 5).
3. *Per-episode pairing.* For each design, 256 paired episodes of the specialist on the fast tier and on the
   referee at the same world addresses, giving the per-episode correlation ρ_ep.
4. *Statistics, per rung pair and stratum.* Kendall τ on the top band (top m of either rung, m = max(10, 3k)
   for k finalists wanted) with a bootstrap interval (designs resampled with replacement, 2,000 resamples from
   an addressed stream; the lower 95% bound is the 5th percentile); top-10 recall of the lower rung against the
   higher; Spearman ρ over the stratum; the mean signed residual per region of design space (the bias of the
   cheap rung); the conformal margin q̂ at level α with the exchangeability martingale value; ρ_ep; the
   containment rank k_c(m) and the regret of the applied cut.
5. *Record.* One ledger row per (morphology class, task family, rung pair, stratum), keyed also by policy and
   kernel versions, effort profile and battery version, with provenance (calibration experiment or audit-lane
   draws, claim identifier, recording session). Evidence is never pooled across model identities: a change of
   model, kernel version, effort profile or battery version restarts the row.

**Modes, from the record (all conditions on the lower bootstrap bound).**

| Mode | Condition | What the lower rung may do |
|---|---|---|
| `selective` | τ on the top band ≥ 0.7, with the winner check passed; calibration valid (at least 19 audit designs at α = 0.05, martingale below 20); audited miss rate at most α | Select by the conformal survivor rule, advancing max(m, survivors, k_c(m)) |
| `discard-only` | 0.5 ≤ τ < 0.7 | The fixed table: the top third advances, so the top quarter always does; a design within 2·q̂ of the k-th best is never eliminated |
| `uninformative` | τ < 0.5; or the stratum is unmeasured; or the planted winner was lost in this stratum | Nothing. The rung is skipped in this stratum and its budget moves up one rung |

An unmeasured stratum is `uninformative`: the default fails closed. A request that asks a rung to select or
discard without a record that meets the threshold is refused with FROB-REF-537 (repairs: run the rank-agreement
experiment; use the rung as advisory). The baseline controller as a scorer has no exemption. Where ρ_ep is below
0.7 on at least 200 paired addresses, the tier-correction estimator is disabled for the stratum and promotion
uses referee episodes alone.

**Verb `eval.rank-agreement` (run, M4; owner `frob-opt`).** Input: class, task family, rung pair, stratum or
design set, `k`, `m`, budget, idempotency key. Output: the row above with `mode`, intervals and the admissibility
of a requested cut. Evidence: class `Tested`, tier pair, determinism (scores are evaluations under a D2 profile,
whatever profile trained the policies).

**Gates measured.**

- GATE-CORTEX-12 (design comparison): the experiment is run and its consequence applied: τ, top-10 recall and
  intervals are recorded per class and task family, and a rung without a record is refused. The gate passes when
  the record exists and the rule is enforced, whatever τ is.
- GATE-ATEL-07: per morphology class, τ on the top band at least 0.7 for `selective` and at least 0.5 for
  `discard-only`, and ρ_ep at least 0.7, all at the lower bootstrap bound, on 40 designs; a class below 0.5 is
  `uninformative`.
- GATE-REFLEX-04: rank agreement of the baseline rung with trained specialists on 30 to 40 designs per class and
  task family, thresholds fixed before data: a lower 95% bound of at least 0.7 lets the rung select; at least
  0.5 lets it discard the bottom half; below 0.5 it removes outright failures only (`baseline-only: unranked`).
- KILL-RANK for rungs: fires if GATE-ATEL-07 fails for every class tested.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: GATE-CORTEX-12 is recorded (records exist per class and task family; a rung without a record is refused); GATE-ATEL-07 and GATE-REFLEX-04 are recorded with their measured lower bounds and resulting modes; KILL-RANK is decided for rungs and recorded.
- [ ] The design set, strata and thresholds 0.7 and 0.5 are those of the pre-registration; a threshold edited after the first result is refused by the study record.
- [ ] Each record holds τ on the top band with its interval, top-10 recall, Spearman ρ, signed residual per region, q̂ and martingale value, ρ_ep on 256 paired episodes per design, containment rank and regret.
- [ ] FROB-REF-537 fires for: no record; a record below 0.5 asked to discard; a record between 0.5 and 0.7 asked to select.
- [ ] Reports print the mode of every rung and stratum; finalists chosen under any mode other than `selective` are described as "k survivors", never as "the top k".
- [ ] In the variant with no family policy, the baseline rows are measured and the family rows are recorded `not-run`; the gates' rows say so.
- [ ] Negative controls of the record are caught: a planted threshold edit, a planted calibration from survivors only, and the forty-design winner-third fixture.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt rank_agreement`:
- `kendall_tau_small`: scores (1, 2, 3, 4) against (1, 2, 4, 3): one discordant pair of six, τ = (5 − 1)/6 = 2/3.
- `top_band_definition`: k = 3 gives m = max(10, 9) = 10; the band is the union of both rungs' top ten.
- `mode_thresholds`: lower bounds 0.72, 0.61, 0.43 and "no record" → `selective`, `discard-only`, `uninformative`, `uninformative`.
- `selective_cut_below_containment_refused`: with a bound of 0.72 and k_c(1) = 3, a selection that retains 2 is refused with the repair "retain 3", and one that retains 3 is admitted.
- `planted_winner_lost_is_uninformative`: a stratum whose audit shows the planted winner discarded is `uninformative` whatever its τ.
- `ref_537_cases`: the three refusal cases above.
- `bootstrap_reproducible`: the interval is identical on repetition; the lower bound is the 5th percentile.
- `no_pooling_across_identities`: a kernel version change starts a new row.
- `rho_ep_switch`: ρ_ep = 0.65 on 256 × 40 addresses disables the tier correction for the stratum.
- `survivors_calibration_forbidden`: computing q̂ from advanced designs only is refused by the calibration refusal of the ladder.
- Property (INV-CORTEX-06): over random allocation requests, none that selects or discards without a qualifying record is admitted.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/rank_agreement_rungs.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/rank_agreement_rungs/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Steps: (1) the fixture check of N23b-35;
(2) for the calibration set: `frob eval run` for each design under each scorer (`--policy <family>`, `--policy
<family> --fine-tune 0.05`, `--controller baseline`, `--policy <specialist> --tier referee`), then `frob eval
rank-agreement --class quadruped --task-family velocity --rungs R1,R3 --json` and the other rung pairs;
(3) `frob opt cascade --plan-only` on a stratum without a record, asserting FROB-REF-537; (4) the mode printed
in `frob report campaign`. `cmd` events carry the exact command, exit code, wall time and evidence block (claim,
class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` events carry τ with its interval against 0.7 and 0.5,
top-10 recall, ρ_ep against 0.7, containment rank and regret; `assert` events carry the mode and the refusal;
on failure the first rung pair or stratum that disagrees is logged with artifact paths. `summary`: assertions
passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-CORTEX-12", "GATE-ATEL-07", "GATE-REFLEX-04", "KILL-RANK"]`. A `--smoke` mode
uses eight designs on `batch-cpu` and claims no gate.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The forty-design fixture: τ = 41/45 on the true top ten with the winner at lower-rung rank 3 (selection at k = 2 refused, at k = 3 accepted).
- Conformal arithmetic the record relies on: n = 39 and α = 0.05 select the 38th smallest residual; fewer than 19 audit designs cannot support α = 0.05.
- Negative controls: a planted over-scored design must be caught at the next rung; a planted rung that discards with no record must be refused; a threshold changed after data must be refused.
- Potency: a synthetic lower rung equal to the higher rung plus small noise must come out `selective`; an independent one must come out `uninformative`.
- Determinism lanes: statistics are exact functions of the score tables; score tables are class D2 per backend; the record is identical twice on one machine and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- RISK (high): rank agreement between rungs is too low for cheap rungs to eliminate designs; early warning: τ under 0.5 at the lower bound in the pilot, and verdict flips after finalist training. If most designs are then trained, the compute ratio of the ten-fold thesis is near 1, and the record must say so.
- Whether a fine-tune fraction of 5% is the right middle rung, or two are needed, is an open question of the optimization chapter (default: one rung at 5%); each fraction is its own rung with its own row.
- The published within-class figures are for flat or mildly varied ground; agreement on stairs, slopes and wet floors is unknown, so strata follow the task family's terrain set.
- Rank agreement is reported per region because the bias is not uniform; a region where the cheap rung under-scores gets its own margin or refuses.
