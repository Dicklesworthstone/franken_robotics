## Background

"Mean reward over a few seeds" is the usual way a learned policy is reported, and it is fragile: a mean is moved
by one outlier seed, three seeds give no interval, and a number measured on the training scenes or with
privileged state says little about the robot. FrankenRobotics fixes an evaluation protocol for every policy
result that Cortex (the learning subsystem, crate `frob-learn`) reports:

- held-out scenes;
- at least five seeds;
- the interquartile mean with a stratified bootstrap interval;
- pairwise comparisons through an anytime-valid e-process (a betting test whose error rate holds whenever it is
  stopped), never by comparing two means;
- every reported return with its estimated-state twin, the gap being part of the result;
- time to a target success rate and performance at matched compute.

This bead builds the report that carries those quantities. The rules about what a study may and may not do with
them (one study type, pairing and determinism, fixed evaluation episodes, holdout retirement, acceptance, method
gates) are N23b-10.

Tier: Solid. Crate: `frob-learn`, using the batteries of `frob-arena`.

## Scope

In: the aggregate statistics (per-seed scores, interquartile mean, stratified bootstrap); the report schema; the
five-seed rule; the estimated-state twin; the matched-compute fields; the extension of `eval.run` output for
policies and the policy inputs it reads (`--policy`, `--task`, `--split` or `--stream dev`, `--seeds`, `--state`).
Out: protocol guards and refusals (N23b-10); the attainment rule for "time to a target" (N23b-33, which this
report reads); the e-process comparison itself (BD-262); acceptance, which is the requirement verdict in the
blinded qualification lane; checkpoints and their manifest, through which a policy is bound for evaluation
(N23a-13, a dependency); the reference training gate, which uses this report and depends on it (BD-198).

## Technical approach

**Score matrix.** A policy family is trained with S independent training seeds (S ≥ 5). Each trained policy is
evaluated on T strata (scene classes or task variants of the held-out split) with E fixed evaluation episodes per
stratum, at fixed world addresses. The per-seed, per-stratum score x[s][t] is the stratum's primary metric in
physical units, normalized by the stratum's declared scale to a pure number; the requirement pass rate is
reported beside it in its own matrix.

**Interquartile mean.** For n values sorted ascending x₍₁₎ ≤ … ≤ x₍ₙ₎, the interquartile mean is the mean of
the middle half by mass:

    IQM = (2/n) · Σᵢ wᵢ · x₍ᵢ₎,   wᵢ = length of [i − 1, i] ∩ [n/4, 3n/4].

For n divisible by 4 it is the plain mean of the middle n/2 values. The aggregate is the IQM over all S·T
entries of the matrix. Reason: it ignores the best and worst quarter of runs, so one diverged seed or one lucky
seed does not move it, and it still uses half the data (a median uses one or two values).

**Stratified bootstrap interval.** For b = 1 … B (B = 2,000): within each stratum t, resample S seeds with
replacement; recompute the IQM over the resampled matrix. The 95% interval is the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles
of the B values. Resampling indices come from a registered stream keyed by (report identity, b, t), so the
interval is reproducible bit for bit. Strata are never pooled before resampling: a hard stratum keeps its weight.

**Five seeds.** A report with S < 5 is produced with the label `under-seeded`, no interval, and cannot be
attached to a gate record or cited in a comparison; the gate recorder checks the label.

**Estimated-state twin.** Every evaluation runs twice at the same addresses: `--state privileged` (the policy's
observations are built from simulator state through the sensor models only) and `--state estimated` (the same,
with the state estimator in the loop wherever the controller or observation terms consume estimated state). The
report holds both aggregates and `gap = IQM_privileged − IQM_estimated` with its own bootstrap interval. A report
without its twin is labelled `privileged-only` and is not a reportable return.

**Matched compute.** The report states: environment steps and device-seconds of training; time to the task's
target success rate, with both the descriptive `crossing` and the error-controlled `attained` of the attainment
procedure (a crossing alone is never reported as attainment); and performance at matched compute, meaning the
aggregate at a registered compute budget common to the arms being compared. The observation schema digest and
sensor-model identity are part of the report, because more rollouts with an easier sensor model do not establish
a win.

**Schema (`frob.evalreport/1`, DESIGN).**

```json
{ "schema": "frob.evalreport/1", "policy_family": "pf:…", "task": "t:…", "split": {"digest": "b3:…", "kind": "held-out"},
  "seeds": 5, "strata": ["flat", "rough", "stairs"], "episodes_per_stratum": 256,
  "score": {"iqm": "0.812", "ci95": ["0.774", "0.846"], "bootstrap": {"B": 2000, "stream": "eval-bootstrap"}},
  "pass_rate": {"iqm": "0.93", "ci95": ["0.90", "0.95"]},
  "state": {"privileged": "0.812", "estimated": "0.771", "gap": "0.041", "gap_ci95": ["0.020", "0.063"]},
  "compute": {"env_steps": "2.0e8", "device_s": "410", "time_to_target": {"crossing_s": "338", "attained_s": "402"},
              "at_matched_compute": {"budget_device_s": "300", "iqm": "0.74"}},
  "objective": "mean", "observation_schema": "b3:…", "labels": [], "determinism": "D2" }
```

`evidence = { class: "Tested", tier, determinism, seeds, identities }`. Verb surface: `frob eval run --policy
<family> --task <t> --split held-out --seeds 5 --key <k> --json` returns the report; `eval.scorecard` shows the
per-stratum matrix with the outcome columns.

**Inputs this bead adds to `eval.run` (DESIGN; the `eval.run` row of VERBS-eval carries them).** `--policy
<family | checkpoint>` names what is evaluated in place of a controller; the policy is bound to the positional
design by role through N23a-13's matcher (a mismatch is `FROB-REF-531`), and with no positional design it is
evaluated on the design it was trained for (the policy's design parent in the artifact graph, N23a-13). A
*policy family* (DESIGN) is the set of stored policies whose training requests (`frob.train.start.in/1`, with the
idempotency key removed) are equal except for `--seed`; it is named by any one member, and its identity
`pf:<digest>` is the digest of that request with the seed removed, printed in the report as `policy_family`. A
single checkpoint is a family of one;
`--task <t>`; exactly one of `--split held-out | <split id>` (a held-out split of the task's scenes) and `--stream
dev` (the registered development stream of N23b-33, on which attainment looks are evaluated); both, or neither
with `--policy`, is `FROB-REF-802`. That rule is the task form's: with `--policy`, a battery (`--battery <name>`,
in place of `--task` and its split or stream) is also a valid population, the role-matched loading case of
N23-REF (`frob eval run @fixture:hexapod --policy @fixture:quad-specialist --battery smoke --key ref-531 --json`,
which must answer 531); the policy is bound by role as above, the run returns the battery form's outputs
(per-scenario verdicts and the scorecard handle), and the five-seed report is the task form's; `--seeds <S>` (the
number of training seeds of the family to evaluate: the S lowest seeds on file; when fewer are on file the report
evaluates those and records the count, so fewer than five is `under-seeded`);
`--state privileged | estimated` (default: both, issued as the twin at the same addresses; a single value
produces a `privileged-only` or estimated-only report and labels it); `--tier` as in the row; the idempotency
key. The control forms `zero:<spec>` and `random:<spec>:<seed>` of `--policy` are added by N23b-15, which builds
them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob eval run` on a policy family returns a `frob.evalreport/1` report with the interquartile mean, a stratified bootstrap interval, the pass-rate matrix, the estimated-state twin and the compute block.
- [ ] The IQM known answers below hold exactly; the bootstrap interval is identical on a repeated call (addressed resampling).
- [ ] A report with fewer than five seeds carries `under-seeded`, has no interval and is refused by the gate recorder and by `eval.compare`.
- [ ] A report without its estimated-state twin carries `privileged-only` and is not accepted as a reported return.
- [ ] `time_to_target` shows `crossing` and `attained` separately; where the procedure did not attain within the budget the field is `censored` at the budget.
- [ ] Evaluation runs on a split that does not intersect the training manifest; an intersecting split is refused with FROB-REF-502 (an evaluation reuses a scene from the policy's training manifest; exit 3).
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-learn` documents the schema and the labels; the report is reachable through `frob --json`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-learn evalreport`:
- `iqm_divisible`: values 1 … 8 → 4.5 (mean of 3, 4, 5, 6).
- `iqm_fractional`: values (1, 2, 3, 4, 100) → weights (0, 0.75, 1, 0.75, 0); (1.5 + 3 + 3)/2.5 = 3.0, where the mean is 22.
- `iqm_outlier_invariance`: replacing the largest of eight values by 10⁶ leaves the IQM unchanged.
- `bootstrap_stratified`: a matrix with a constant stratum and a varying stratum: every resample keeps the constant stratum constant; pooling strata before resampling (the planted defect) changes the interval and is caught.
- `bootstrap_reproducible`: two computations give identical percentiles; changing the report identity changes the resampling indices.
- `five_seed_rule`: S = 4 → label `under-seeded`, `ci95` absent.
- `twin_gap`: synthetic matrices with IQMs 0.812 and 0.771 give gap 0.041.
- `crossing_not_attained`: a learning curve that crosses the target once and falls back shows a `crossing_s` and `attained: censored`.
- `eval_policy_inputs`: `--split` and `--stream` together, or a task with neither, with `--policy` → FROB-REF-802; `--policy` with `--battery smoke` and no task is admitted and binds by role; a positional design whose roles do not match the policy's mapping table → FROB-REF-531 (N23a-13's matcher), and no positional design → the policy's design parent; `--state privileged` alone → label `privileged-only`.
- `policy_family_resolution`: five stored policies whose training requests differ only in `--seed` (1 … 5) resolve, from any one of them, to one family with one `pf:` identity; a sixth whose request also differs in `--steps` is not a member; `--seeds 3` takes seeds 1, 2 and 3; `--seeds 5` on a family with four members evaluates four and labels the report `under-seeded`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/eval_report.sh` (helper of `STD-E2E`; newline-delimited JSON at
`target/e2e-logs/eval_report/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript). It trains five short seeds of the reference
quadruped's flat velocity task on `batch-cpu` (`frob train start quad-ref --task quad-flat-velocity --backend
batch-cpu --seed <s> --steps <small budget> --key er-tr-<s> --json` for s = 1 … 5, then `frob job wait` and `frob
train export`), then runs `frob eval run --policy <the seed-1 policy, naming its family> --task quad-flat-velocity --split held-out --seeds 5
--tier batch-cpu --key er-ev-1 --json` (the twin, both states), asserts the report's fields, re-runs the call
under a new key (`er-ev-2`, so the second call computes rather than returning the stored receipt) and asserts
identical intervals, runs `--state privileged` alone (key `er-ev-priv`) asserting the `privileged-only` label,
runs the four-seed case (`--seeds 4`, key `er-ev-4`) asserting `under-seeded`, and asserts that `--split
held-out --stream dev` together is refused with FROB-REF-802. Every `cmd` event has the exact command, exit
code, wall time and the evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` events carry
the IQM, the interval and the gap with units; `assert` events carry expected and observed values; on failure the
first differing percentile and the report path are logged. The `summary` gives assertions passed and failed and
`gates: []`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The two IQM cases above (hand-checkable).
- Negative controls: a planted aggregate that reports the plain mean must fail `iqm_fractional` (22 against 3.0); a planted bootstrap that pools strata must fail `bootstrap_stratified`; a planted report that evaluates on a training scene must be refused by the split check; a planted report that prints a point-score crossing as `attained` must fail `crossing_not_attained`.
- Metamorphic relations: permuting seeds or strata leaves the IQM unchanged; an affine change of the score scale maps the IQM and interval by the same map.
- Determinism lanes: the report is byte-identical twice on one machine and across worker counts under a D2 profile; debug and release agree; across backends the aggregates agree inside the D3 envelope, with the class named in the report.

## Considerations

- Five seeds is a floor. The bootstrap interval with five seeds per stratum is coarse and is reported as it is; widening it artificially or narrowing it by pooling is not allowed.
- The IQM discards the tails on purpose. A user who cares about the tail declares the CVaR objective (N23b-03), which is reported beside the IQM, not in its place.
- The estimated-state gap is a property of the estimator and the sensor suite as much as of the policy; a large gap is a finding about the design.
- Training five seeds multiplies training cost by five; for gate measurements this is within the gate's compute class, and for exploratory work the `under-seeded` label keeps the cheaper report honest.
