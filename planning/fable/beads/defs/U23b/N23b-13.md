## Background

The Gymnasium-compatible adapter (BD-301) exists so that rival learning stacks can train in this environment,
which lets the ten-fold test split a difference in training time into an engine part and a learner part (three
of four combinations can be run: ours with ours, theirs with theirs, our engine with their learner). Its gate is
GATE-CORTEX-16 (parity, M3, TARGET): 10³ episodes driven through the adapter and natively have equal digests in
class D2; the final observation is in `info` in every autoreset mode; and an external PPO reaches the threshold
of the reference training gate through it. The first two clauses are measured by BD-301. This bead measures the
third, completes the gate row, and takes the final reading of the kill gate: ✖ KILL-GYMADAPT fires if
GATE-CORTEX-16 fails; fallback, rival stacks drive the environment through the job interface in whole rollouts,
and the ten-fold test records the added latency in its phase ledger (the fallback path is specified in N23b-14).

"The threshold of the reference training gate" is the return threshold that our own PPO must reach on the
reference quadruped's flat velocity task (the training known-answer gate, measured by BD-198), with a zero-weight
policy and a random policy both failing the same threshold. An external learner reaching it through the adapter
shows the adapter delivers a learnable, correct environment, not only matching digests on replayed actions.

## Scope

In: one pinned external PPO implementation in an isolated environment; its training run through the adapter on
the reference task; attainment judged by the error-controlled rule; the behaviour-not-plumbing controls; the
completed gate record; the adapter-side figures of this run that the learner-swap diagnostic starts from
(step 7); the final kill-gate reading.
Out: tuning the external learner beyond its frozen configuration; our own PPO (BD-197, BD-198); the rival arm
of the ten-fold test itself (BD-315), which reuses this setup; the learner-swap diagnostic (N39a-22, which
depends on this bead): its three runs `OO`, `TT` and `OT` on five interleaved seeds under the ten-fold gate's
caps, the split of each `train` phase into environment and learner time from the phase ledger, the
decomposition of a difference into an engine part and a learner part, the task-match check, the whole-rollout
path with its lost factor when the kill gate has fired, and the record under `tenfold/…/diagnostics/`. This
bead runs none of that: it reports what one adapter-driven training shows and hands over the pinned
environment and the reading of the kill gate.

## Technical approach

1. **Environment.** An isolated, recorded Python environment (lock file with hashes) containing the host
   library and one widely used external PPO implementation at the pins the rival freeze names. Nothing from it
   is linked into any `frob` binary.
2. **Configuration.** The external learner's default continuous-control configuration with the minimum changes
   the task needs (observation and action dimensions, number of worlds). The configuration is frozen and hashed
   before the first run. The adapter runs in the autoreset convention that learner expects, with the final
   observation taken from `info` for truncation bootstraps where the learner supports it; the convention and
   whether the learner used `final_obs` are recorded.
3. **Run.** Train on the reference quadruped's flat velocity task through the adapter on one device, three
   seeds, with a development evaluation stream disjoint from training worlds at a cadence registered before the
   run. Evaluation is done natively by `frob eval run` on the exported weights (converted to the `frob.policy/1`
   format through the safetensors interchange with a manifest and mapping table), so that the score does not
   depend on the external stack's own evaluation.
4. **Attainment.** "Reaches the threshold" means `attained` under the error-controlled procedure, not a first
   crossing: looks spend error as αₖ = α/[k·(k + 1)] with α = 0.05, and a look attains when the procedure valid
   for the registered cadence rejects "at or below target". All looks are kept in the record. The clause passes
   when at least two of three seeds attain within the registered budget (DESIGN reading of "an external PPO
   reaches the threshold"; the count per seed is reported either way).
5. **Controls in the record.** A zero-weight policy and a random policy evaluated through the same path both
   fail the threshold; the exported checkpoint replayed natively reproduces the behaviour and its policy head is
   not zero. A record without them is refused (FROB-REF-544). The third control that 544's repair text lists,
   a shuffled-label student, applies only where distillation is involved (§23.23); this run has none, and the
   record states "shuffled-label: not applicable (no distillation)" rather than omitting the field.
6. **Labels.** Every result is `adapter-driven` with untrusted learner provenance; it completes this parity
   gate and feeds the learner-swap diagnostic; it is not evidence for any of our method gates.
7. **Adapter-side figures for the learner swap.** Wall-clock to attainment, environment steps per second, and
   the adapter's overhead per step (time in the Python half and in the copy), recorded beside our own learner's
   figures on the same card; the difference is reported, not gated. These are figures of this gate's three-seed
   run, labelled `adapter-driven`. They are not the learner-swap diagnostic and are never copied into its
   record: N39a-22 runs its own `OT` combination on five seeds under the ten-fold gate's caps and computes the
   engine and learner parts there. What N39a-22 takes from this bead is the pinned external environment (the
   lock file and the frozen configuration), the weights interchange, the per-step overhead as a cross-check
   on its ledger split, and the kill gate's final reading, which decides whether its `OT` run goes through the
   adapter or through whole rollouts.
8. **Completing the gate and the kill reading.** The gate row is `pass` only if clauses 1 and 2 (from BD-301's
   record) and clause 3 pass with all controls caught, recorded by a session that did not implement the adapter.
   If the gate fails after one repair cycle, KILL-GYMADAPT fires: the row is `killed`, the annex is not shipped,
   N23bp1-01 builds the whole-rollout path that N23b-14 specified, BD-315 switches the rival's access path to it,
   and the phase ledger records the added latency (in `wait`, by N39a-22's rule).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The external environment is pinned by a lock file with hashes; the learner's configuration hash is recorded before the first run.
- [ ] Three seeds are trained through the adapter; every evaluation look is in the record; `attained` is reported per seed with the look index and time.
- [ ] Clause 3 is recorded with its measured values and its controls (zero-weight and random policies fail; the exported checkpoint replays; the policy head is not zero).
- [ ] GATE-CORTEX-16 has a completed row citing BD-301's clauses 1 and 2 and this clause, with status `pass`, `fail` or `killed`.
- [ ] KILL-GYMADAPT's final reading is appended to the decision record of N23b-14, and on firing the downstream switch is recorded.
- [ ] Results carry `adapter-driven`; an attempt to attach one to a Cortex method gate is refused.
- [ ] The learner-swap figures are in the record with a disclosure block (hardware, driver, versions, world count, step, decimation, whether policy and learning are included, warm-up, duration).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance gym_gate`: `gate_row_requires_three_clauses` (a row with two clauses cannot be
`pass`); `clause3_two_of_three` (attainment vectors (true, true, false) pass; (true, false, false) fail);
`weights_interchange_roundtrip` (external weights converted to `frob.policy/1` and loaded natively give the same
actions as the external forward pass on 10³ random observations within 10⁻⁵ relative); `adapter_driven_refused_for_method_gate`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/gym_adapter_external_ppo.sh` (helper of `STD-E2E`; newline-delimited JSON at
`target/e2e-logs/gym_adapter_external_ppo/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript). Steps: build the isolated environment
from the lock file; start `frob-gym-worker` for the reference task; run the external learner for each seed;
at each registered look export weights, convert, and run `frob eval run --policy <converted> --task
quad-flat-velocity --stream dev --key gym-ppo-s<seed>-look<k> --json` (the development stream is spelled `--stream
dev`, as in the reference training gate's script; each look has its own idempotency key, so that no look returns an
earlier look's stored receipt); evaluate the zero-weight and random controls through the same command with
`--policy zero:<spec>` and `--policy random:<spec>:<seed>` and keys `gym-ppo-zero` and `gym-ppo-random`; replay the
final converted checkpoint of each seed natively on the CPU reference tier, `frob eval run --policy <converted
final checkpoint> --task quad-flat-velocity --stream dev --tier batch-cpu --key gym-ppo-s<seed>-replay --json`,
and log its head norm; assemble clause 3 as a training-gate record (N23b-15's `TrainingGateRecord`, shuffled-label
`not applicable`) and assert that N23b-15's checker accepts it; run the gate, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test g2_parity -- GATE-CORTEX-16` (a parity gate), whose entries read BD-301's receipts for
clauses 1 and 2 and this record and produce the gate's receipt, and record its value from that receipt with `cargo
run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-CORTEX-16 --receipt <hash>` (the one recorder,
KNOWN_DRIFTS 9, in a session that did not implement the adapter); append the final reading of KILL-GYMADAPT to
`docs/adr/ADR-KILL-GYMADAPT-M3.md` (N23b-14's record, N40-03's form) and run N23b-16's
`scripts/e2e/cortex_kill_gate_records.sh` as a stage, asserting that its KILL-GYMADAPT step passes on the
appended reading. `cmd` events carry the exact command, exit code, wall time and evidence block
(claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed; `adapter-driven`); `metric` events carry each look's score
with its target and level αₖ, steps per second and adapter overhead; `assert` events carry attainment per seed
and each control's failure; on a failed assertion the first failing look and the artifact paths are logged. The
`summary` gives assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-CORTEX-16", "KILL-GYMADAPT"]`. A `--smoke` mode
trains one seed for a small budget and asserts only that the pipeline and the controls run (it claims no gate).

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-590 (training gate controls: zero-weight, random and shuffled-label policies fail the return threshold; an exported all-zero policy head passes a plumbing test and fails this one); CX-257.
- Negative controls: the zero-weight and random policies; a planted adapter that returns post-reset observations with no `final_obs` (the external learner's truncation bootstrap is then wrong, and the parity clause of BD-301 must already have failed); a planted evaluation that uses the external stack's own reported return must be rejected by the record check.
- Attainment control (the counterexample of §23.27): a synthetic look series from a policy whose true success rate is 0.89 against a target of 0.90, 30 episodes per look, 50 looks. A 30-of-30 look has exact one-sided 95% lower bound 0.05^(1/30) = 0.90497 and so crosses the target; one look does this with probability 0.89³⁰ = 0.0303, and the series contains at least one such crossing with probability 1 − (1 − 0.0303)⁵⁰ = 0.78548. The record must show `crossing` at that look and `attained: censored`; at α = 0.05 an all-success look k needs at least 36, 46, 74 and 103 episodes at k = 1, 2, 10 and 50.
- Determinism: the external learner is not deterministic and is labelled so; every score that enters the record is a native re-evaluation under a D2 profile of fixed weights, identical on repetition.

## Considerations

- An external learner may ignore `final_obs` and bootstrap wrongly at truncations; that is the rival's stack behaving as it does elsewhere. The record states whether it used `final_obs`.
- "At least two of three seeds" is this bead's reading of a clause that names no count; the per-seed outcomes are always reported.
- If the external learner fails to learn for reasons unrelated to the adapter (a bad default), one repair cycle allows a second pinned configuration; a third attempt is not allowed without a new registration.
