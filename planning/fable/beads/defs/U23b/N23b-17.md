## Background

An invariant is a property that must hold in every state of the system; a proof obligation is a property with a
named way of discharging it. In FrankenRobotics each one is carried by a test: a property test over generated
cases, an exhaustive check, or a gate. Cortex (the learning subsystem, crate `frob-learn`) states seven
invariants and six proof obligations. Most are properties of mechanisms built by other beads (the vectorized
environment, tensor views, PPO, the multi-device update, the on-device backward kernels). This bead collects all
thirteen into one conformance battery, so that each has a named test with the stated verification, none is lost
between mechanism beads, and a milestone exit can ask one question: does the battery pass?

Terms. `VecEnv` is the vectorized environment with explicit reset; a world ends by termination (success, failure
or an intrinsic mission deadline), truncation (an external collection limit) or invalidity (numerical failure).
A shard is one device's share of a multi-device update. Class D3 is agreement inside a recorded tolerance
envelope together with agreement of task decisions. A `TensorView` is a typed, strided view into a device buffer.

## Scope

In: one test per identifier with the stated statement and verification; the battery target; the registration of
each identifier with the test that carries it.
Out: fixing or building the mechanisms; the known-answer fixtures (N23b-18); the gates that also exercise some
of these properties.

## Technical approach

**Invariants.**

| ID | Statement | Test in this battery | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| INV-CORTEX-01 | The final observation of every ended world is retrievable in every reset mode | Property: random episodes (random lengths, random endings by termination, truncation and invalidity) in `explicit` and in `autoreset: masked`; for every ended world the final observation read from `obs` (explicit) and from `final_obs` (masked) are bit-equal and equal the observation recomputed from the snapshot before the end | BD-192 |
| INV-CORTEX-02 | Behaviour weights and the observation normalizer are frozen during collection | Property: digests of the weights and normalizer before and after each collection are equal; an update attempted mid-collection is refused; two collections from one snapshot are identical | BD-197 |
| INV-CORTEX-03 | A likelihood ratio is computed for the random variable that was sampled | Property: for random policies and both action laws, the stored variable's density under the collection law equals the density the learner evaluates at an unchanged policy (ratio exactly 1); mixing laws is refused (FROB-REF-527) | BD-197, N23b-29 |
| INV-CORTEX-04 | A checkpoint is inseparable from its manifest and mapping table, and a policy reaches a design only through that table | Property: a checkpoint with its manifest stripped fails to load; a checkpoint applied to a design whose channels are permuted by name order still drives the right joints (matching is by role); a mismatch in role, dimension, sign, unit or rate is refused (FROB-REF-531) | BD-198, BD-320, BD-322 |
| INV-CORTEX-05 | Every training gate record contains its negative controls | Property over 10³ generated records: PASS is accepted only for records with all required controls failing the threshold (FROB-REF-544 otherwise) | N23b-15 |
| INV-CORTEX-06 | No rung selects or discards designs without its rank-agreement record | Property over random ladder requests: any allocation that selects or discards in a stratum with no record, or with a record below its threshold, is refused (FROB-REF-537). Registered at M2 as `pending`; active from the pilot record at M3 | BD-328 |
| INV-CORTEX-07 | Invalid and padded samples have weight zero in every loss and statistic, and are counted in every failure report | Property: random batches with random invalid worlds and random padding; every loss, gradient and normalization statistic is unchanged when the invalid and padded entries are overwritten with arbitrary finite or non-finite values; the failure count equals the number of invalid worlds | BD-197, BD-192 |

**Proof obligations.**

| ID | Obligation | Verification, as stated | Test in this battery |
|---|---|---|---|
| PO-CORTEX-01 | The value bootstrap is cut by termination only and the trace by any reset | All eight flag combinations on a three-step fixture against a hand table | The eight combinations of (terminated, truncated, invalid) at the middle step of a three-step fixture, with δₜ = rₜ + γₜ·(1 − terminatedₜ)·V(o′ₜ) − V(oₜ) and Aₜ = δₜ + γₜ·λₜ·(1 − needs_resetₜ)·Aₜ₊₁, `needs_reset` = terminated or truncated or invalid, compared with a table computed by hand in exact rationals; an invalid step has weight zero and ends the trace of the valid samples before it, bootstrapping from the last valid value as at a truncation |
| PO-CORTEX-02 | A frozen policy's likelihood ratio is exactly 1 on any replay of its own collection, under any packing or minibatch order | Property test over 10⁴ random packings | 10⁴ random packings and minibatch orders of random multi-episode collections, feed-forward and recurrent; every ratio equals 1 exactly (bit pattern of 1.0) |
| PO-CORTEX-03 | The update is invariant to the partition into shards inside the D3 envelope, empty shards included | 10³ random partitions | 10³ random partitions of random batches into 1 to 4 shards, at least 50 with an empty shard; gradients and statistics agree with the one-shard update inside the recorded envelope |
| PO-CORTEX-04 | The actor's output does not depend on any channel outside its schema, nor on anything not yet delivered | The causal test; inference with the other channels absent | The causal test on the reference policy with future terrain, hidden friction and undelivered measurements perturbed one at a time; inference with `critic-only` and `evaluation-only` channels physically absent gives identical commands; extended to rewards, flags, masks and `info` fields by the actor view |
| PO-CORTEX-05 | Conversion between stride conventions round-trips or refuses | Exhaustive over two- and three-dimensional views with element strides in −2 to 2 | Exhaustive enumeration: extents 1 to 3 per dimension, origins that keep the view in bounds, element strides in {−2, …, 2}; each view converts and converts back to the same view, or the conversion refuses; no conversion returns a different in-bounds view |
| PO-CORTEX-06 | On-device backward kernels equal the FrankenTorch oracle | The backward-parity gate | 10³ random batches through the on-device backward kernels and through FrankenTorch autograd on the CPU: relative difference within 10⁻⁵ |

**Battery.** `crates/frob-conformance/tests/cortex_invariants.rs`, one `#[test]` per identifier, generated cases
from registered streams with explicit seeds, shrinking on failure, each printing its identifier. A registry
`frob-conformance/registry/obligations.toml` (DESIGN; if the Gauntlet already has one, its rows) maps identifier
→ test name → mechanism bead → status (`active`, `pending` with the milestone at which it becomes active).

**Hand table for PO-CORTEX-01 (the part every implementer gets wrong).** Three steps with rewards (1, 1, 1),
values V(o₀), V(o₁), V(o₂), V(o₃) = (10, 10, 10, 10), γ = 0.99, λ = 0.95, flags at step 1 only. For the flags
at step 1: none → δ₁ = 1 + 9.9 − 10 = 0.9 and the trace continues; truncated only → δ₁ = 0.9 (bootstrap kept,
using the final observation) and the trace stops; terminated (alone or with truncated) → δ₁ = 1 − 10 = −9 and
the trace stops; invalid → step 1 has weight zero, step 0's trace stops and bootstraps from V(o₁).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Thirteen tests exist, one per identifier, each named after its identifier and runnable alone.
- [ ] Each test uses the verification the obligation states (eight combinations; 10⁴ packings; 10³ partitions; exhaustive stride enumeration; 10³ batches at 10⁻⁵).
- [ ] The obligations registry lists all thirteen with a test and a mechanism bead; INV-CORTEX-06 is `pending` until the rank-agreement record exists and `active` afterwards.
- [ ] The battery passes on `batch-cpu`; device-dependent tests (INV-CORTEX-01, -07, PO-CORTEX-03, -06) also pass on each device backend.
- [ ] Each test has a planted defect that it catches (listed below).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test cortex_invariants`:
`inv_cortex_01_final_obs_retrievable`, `inv_cortex_02_frozen_behaviour`, `inv_cortex_03_same_random_variable`,
`inv_cortex_04_checkpoint_manifest_table`, `inv_cortex_05_gate_record_controls`,
`inv_cortex_06_rung_needs_record`, `inv_cortex_07_invalid_padded_zero_weight`,
`po_cortex_01_two_masks_eight_combinations` (asserts δ₁ ∈ {0.9, 0.9, −9, −9} for none, truncated, terminated,
both, and the trace-stop pattern), `po_cortex_02_ratio_one_10e4_packings`, `po_cortex_03_shard_partitions_10e3`,
`po_cortex_04_actor_independence`, `po_cortex_05_stride_roundtrip_exhaustive`, `po_cortex_06_backward_parity`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/cortex_invariants.sh` (helper of `STD-E2E`; newline-delimited JSON at
`target/e2e-logs/cortex_invariants/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript). It runs the battery per tier (`batch-cpu`,
then each device that exists), one `cmd` event per test with the exact command, exit code and wall time, one
`assert` event per identifier with the case counts (for example 10,000 packings), the seed, and on failure the
shrunk counterexample and its replay command; `metric` events for the largest relative difference of
PO-CORTEX-06 against 10⁻⁵ and of PO-CORTEX-03 against the envelope. `summary`: identifiers passed, failed and
pending; `gates: []`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers used: KA-576 (0 and 9.9; termination wins), KA-577 (final observation equal in both modes), KA-585 (packed episodes: ratios 1 and 1; across the reset 1.582459), KA-587 and KA-588 (0.25; 4/3 and 32/9), KA-580 (stride conversion), KA-586 (P-2, P-9, P-10 rows).
- Planted defects, one per test: post-reset observations returned as final (01); normalizer updated during collection (02); density of the latent used for a clipped action (03); channels matched by position (04); recorder that skips the control check (05); ladder that discards without a record (06); padded entries included in a mean (07); a single `done` flag (PO-01); hidden state carried across a reset (PO-02; the ratio reads 1.582459 on the two-episode case); mean of shard means (PO-03); a critic-only channel zeroed instead of absent while the actor reads it (PO-04); verbatim stride copy (PO-05); a backward kernel with a wrong activation derivative (PO-06).
- Determinism lanes: the battery is seed-explicit and identical twice on one machine, across worker counts, and in debug and release on `batch-cpu`; across backends PO-CORTEX-03 and -06 are compared against their envelopes.

## Considerations

- Several of these properties are also checked inside gates of other beads. The duplication is deliberate: a gate measures a threshold once at a milestone; the battery runs on every change.
- PO-CORTEX-02 demands exact equality to 1. That requires the learner to recompute the density by the same arithmetic path as collection for a frozen policy; an implementation that cannot achieve it must explain the difference and may not relax the test silently.
- The stride enumeration is small (a few thousand views) and exhaustive on purpose; a random test would miss the single negative-stride case that has no unsigned form.
