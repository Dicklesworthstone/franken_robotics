## Background

Distillation in this system is one mechanism with four possible teachers: the student is trained by supervised
learning on states the student itself visits, relabelled by the teacher (dataset aggregation). The teachers are
the baseline controller (a warm start for any class that has one; seconds of cost; the student inherits the
baseline's limits), the sampling-based predictive controller (demonstrations for a class that has a cost and no
shaped reward; about 67 GPU-minutes per 4,000 simulated seconds at its cheapest configuration; the teacher is
`model-privileged`), specialists (a family policy distilled from many specialists) and a privileged teacher (a
proprioceptive student from a teacher that reads `critic-only` channels). Behaviour cloning from the baseline
controller is the cheapest warm start: the baseline supplies demonstrations for free.

This is the pre-funding pilot for distillation and behaviour cloning (it serves BET-27, a sampling controller as
fallback). If students stay below 70% of their teachers' requirement pass rate, kill gate KILL-DISTILL fires
(after one repair cycle at the later gate): distillation is limited to the privileged-teacher case and warm
starts come from behaviour cloning only.

## Scope

In: the frozen protocol for two teachers on ten designs; the run on existing tools; the record; the falsifier
reading.
Out: the mechanism and its gate on our engine (BD-323); the sampling-controller pilot itself (the control
chapter); workspace code.

## Technical approach

**Protocol (frozen before the first run).**

1. *Designs.* Ten of the thirty designs of the sampling-controller pilot: the ten that have specialists.
2. *Teachers.* (a) The existing sampling controller of that pilot with its fixed cost per class. (b) A
   hand-written baseline controller for the class (for the legged templates, a trot controller).
3. *Students.* For each design and teacher, one student policy with a proprioceptive observation schema,
   trained by dataset aggregation: roll out the student, relabel the visited states with the teacher's action,
   fit, repeat for a fixed number of rounds. The student's schema is run with the teacher's privileged inputs
   absent.
4. *Evaluation.* Each student is evaluated on its own rollouts with its own observation schema; teacher states
   are never used at evaluation. Reported: the student's requirement pass rate and return as shares of the
   teacher's, with the requirement verdicts of both; the cost of generating the teacher's labels (GPU-minutes).
5. *Falsifier.* Students below 70% of the teacher's requirement pass rate (median over the ten designs, per
   teacher).
6. *Controls.* Shuffled labels: a student trained on teacher labels permuted across states must fail the
   threshold; if it does not, the threshold does not test distillation. Zero-weight and random policies must
   fail it too. Potency: a student trained on its own teacher's exact states with unlimited rounds on one
   design must approach the teacher (the pipeline can transmit behaviour at all).
7. *Ceiling.* A warm start from the baseline is nearly free; the sampling teacher pays only when its
   demonstrations are pooled over a family or where no shaped reward exists. Nothing is claimed on speed.
8. *Fallback and kill gate.* Privileged-teacher distillation only, with behaviour-cloning warm starts;
   KILL-DISTILL.

**Cost.** About 10 GPU-hours beyond the sampling-controller pilot.

**Record.** Protocol hash and pins; a table per teacher with ten rows (student pass rate, teacher pass rate,
share, label cost); the shuffled-label, zero-weight and random results; the falsifier outcome per teacher.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The sampling-controller pilot has run (its record is cited), or this pilot runs teacher (b) only and records teacher (a) as `not-run`.
- [ ] Protocol, design list and round counts are committed before the first run.
- [ ] Twenty students (ten designs by two teachers) are trained and evaluated on their own rollouts; the record holds their shares of the teachers' requirement pass rates.
- [ ] The shuffled-label control is in the record and failed the threshold; zero-weight and random policies failed it.
- [ ] The falsifier is read per teacher against 70%; the outcome is on file.
- [ ] The label cost of the sampling teacher is recorded in GPU-minutes per 4,000 simulated seconds.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance pilots_registry`: `pilot_distill_schema`; `pilot_protocol_hash_frozen`;
`falsifier_reading_distill` (median share 0.82 → `not-falsified`; 0.64 → `falsified`; a record whose
shuffled-label student passed the threshold → `undetermined: control not caught`).

### End-to-end script and logging

`tools/pilots/cortex_distill/run.sh` (development-time; sources the logging helper of `STD-E2E`): log at
`target/e2e-logs/pilot_cortex_distill/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; `cmd` events per aggregation round and
evaluation with exact command, exit code and wall time; `metric` events per student (share against 70%) and for
label cost; `assert` events for the falsifier and each control; `summary` with counts and `gates:
["KILL-DISTILL"]`; validation of the register entry.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-590 (training gate controls: zero-weight, random and shuffled-label policies fail the return threshold); CX-257.
- Published context as a sanity band: the sampling teacher's label cost of about 67 GPU-minutes per 4,000 simulated seconds at the cheapest configuration and the throughput target.
- A student is never evaluated on teacher states: a planted evaluation that does so must report a higher share and be flagged by the record check (the evaluation's state source is recorded).

## Considerations

- A distilled policy takes the weakest standing among its teachers; a student of a `model-privileged` sampling teacher is itself labelled accordingly, and that label travels into the mechanism.
- The pilot's falsifier (70%) is the kill gate's number; the later gate asks for 90% from baseline and specialist teachers and records the sampling teacher's figure with its cost.
- If the sampling controller is removed by its own kill gate, teacher (a) disappears and distillation's value narrows to warm starts and the privileged case.
