## Background

GATE-CORTEX-05 (performance, M2): wall-clock to the return threshold of the reference quadruped's flat velocity
task on one RTX 4090 is at most 1.25 times an incumbent's wall-clock for the comparable task, re-measured on the
same card under the disclosure block. The published context is about 410 s (FACT: read by eye from a published
figure, ± 20 s, of which about 45 s is compilation). This is a parity gate, not an advantage claim.

A ratio against a published number is weak: the published run used its own evaluation rule (a first crossing of
a curve), its own card state and its own task variant. The plan's rule is that the figure re-measured on our
card, under the same attainment procedure and stream sizes as our arm, is the comparator, and the published time
is context.

OPEN-CORTEX-6 asks whether that re-measurement exists before M2. Owner: Spark lead. It blocks citing
GATE-CORTEX-05 as passed. Fail-closed default: the gate is recorded against the published figure with the label
`published-context` until the re-measurement exists. The question is closed by the incumbent parity baseline
gate, whose rows include time to threshold on one card.

## Scope

In: the decision record; the two states of the gate record and how the label is enforced; what the
re-measurement must contain to close the question.
Out: measuring the incumbent (BD-040, BD-142); measuring our arm and recording the gate (BD-218).

## Technical approach

**Two states.**

| State | Comparator | Gate record | May be cited as |
|---|---|---|---|
| `published-context` (default) | 410 s (± 20 s read error), giving a limit of 513 s (1.25 × 410 s) | Our measured time, the limit, the label `published-context`, status `not-run` for the parity claim | A measured training time; never "GATE-CORTEX-05 passed" |
| `re-measured` | The incumbent's time to threshold on the same card, under the disclosure block, scored by the same attainment rule on the same stream sizes, ten pairs | Our time, the paired ratio's one-sided 95% lower bound, status `pass` or `fail` | The gate's verdict |

**What the re-measurement must contain to close the question.** (1) The incumbent at its pinned revision in its
strongest warm configuration, frozen after tuning on development fixtures only. (2) The comparable task
(matched robot, terrain, command distribution, control rate and decimation; differences listed). (3) Cold start
included: compilation and admission inside the time. (4) The evaluation rule of the attainment procedure: a
fixed development stream at a registered cadence, every look kept, `attained` under error control across looks,
non-attainment right-censored at the budget. (5) At least ten pairs with our arm, back to back in randomized
order on the same host state, with the host load recorded. (6) The complete disclosure block. (7) The check
"reproduced within 20% of the published figure, or the difference explained in writing".

**Enforcement.** The gate recorder refuses status `pass` for GATE-CORTEX-05 when the comparator's provenance is
`published`; the label is part of the record's schema, not a comment. BD-218 reads this decision's state before
it writes the gate row.

**Why the default is not "skip the gate".** Our own time is still measured and disclosed at M2 under the
default; only the verdict waits. A kill gate reads the same measurement (the learning-kernel kill gate fires if
the parity gate is missed by more than two times after one optimization pass), and under the default it reads
the published limit: above 1,025 s is a miss by more than two times of the 513 s limit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision record states the state in force and, when `re-measured`, cites the incumbent measurement's record by hash.
- [ ] Under the default, the gate row for GATE-CORTEX-05 carries `published-context`, shows our measured time against 513 s, and cannot be set to `pass`.
- [ ] The seven requirements above are checked against the incumbent record before the state changes; a record missing any one keeps the default.
- [ ] If the incumbent's re-measured time differs from 410 s by more than 20%, the difference is explained in writing in the record.
- [ ] The state is visible on every report that mentions training-time parity.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance cortex_parity_state`: `published_cannot_pass` (a GATE-CORTEX-05 row with
comparator provenance `published` and status `pass` is refused); `limit_arithmetic` (1.25 × 410 = 512.5,
recorded as 513 s; twice that is 1,025 s); `remeasured_requires_seven` (an incumbent record without the
disclosure block, or with fewer than ten pairs, or scored by first crossing, does not change the state);
`twenty_percent_rule` (an incumbent time of 500 s is 22% above 410 s and requires an explanation field).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/training_parity_state.sh` (helper of `STD-E2E`; newline-delimited JSON at
`target/e2e-logs/training_parity_state/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript): loads the decision record, the incumbent
measurement record if present and the gate row, and asserts their consistency (state, label, status, hash
resolution); each assertion is an `assert` event with expected and observed values; `summary` names
`GATE-CORTEX-05` as consulted and `gates: []`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The arithmetic: 513 s limit; 1,025 s for "missed by more than two times".
- Negative controls: a planted gate row that claims `pass` against the published figure; a planted incumbent record scored by first crossing. Both must be rejected.
- The paired-bound rule the re-measurement feeds: ten pairs of constant ratio r return the bound r.

## Considerations

- The incumbent's 410 s was read from a figure; a re-measurement may well differ by more than the read error, in either direction. A faster incumbent tightens the gate, and the plan accepts that.
- The training machine is often heavily loaded; the re-measurement records host load and uses back-to-back pairs so that load affects both arms.
- If the incumbent cannot be made to run the comparable task on our card, the question stays open, the label stays, and the performance chapter's parity baseline gate records why.
