## Background

Rule P-12 of the PPO battery in FrankenRobotics: the joint law of the sampled action equals the law that the
likelihood computation assumes, across the channels of one decision, across consecutive decisions of one world,
and across neighbouring worlds. Marginal checks do not establish it: a sampler whose Box–Muller phase is a
linear function of a counter has correct marginals and makes adjacent channels agree in sign with probability
0.665510 instead of 0.5.

The conformance crate qualifies a sampler against its manifest (the declared joint law, the transform and
bounds, the stream layout; N23b-29) with four statistics over N decisions, and writes a qualification receipt
without which `train.start` refuses the sampler. The gate:

GATE-CORTEX-18 (known answer, M2, TARGET). For action dimensions 2, 12 and 29, N = 10⁵ decisions and 64 worlds:
every channel-pair, consecutive-decision and adjacent-world sign agreement lies in ½ ± 0.005; every off-diagonal
entry of the standardized covariance is below 4/√N in magnitude; the planted linear-phase sampler is rejected
with an agreement of 0.6655 ± 0.005.

Tier: Solid. Fixtures and battery in `frob-conformance`; the sampler is `frob-learn`'s.

## Technical approach

**Statistics, for a sampler with d channels, W worlds and N decisions.** Let z[w][t][j] be the standardized
noise (the sampled latent minus its mean, divided by its standard deviation; for a full-covariance law, the
whitened noise L⁻¹(a − μ)).

1. *Channel pairs.* For every pair j < j′: the frequency over N decisions (world 0) with which z[·][t][j] and
   z[·][t][j′] have the same sign. d·(d − 1)/2 statistics.
2. *Consecutive decisions.* For every channel j: the frequency with which z[0][t][j] and z[0][t + 1][j] have the
   same sign. d statistics.
3. *Adjacent worlds.* For every channel j: the frequency with which z[w][t][j] and z[w + 1][t][j] have the same
   sign, over N samples drawn across the 64 worlds' adjacent pairs. d statistics.
4. *Covariance.* The d × d empirical covariance of z over N decisions; its off-diagonal entries.

Under a product law every agreement frequency has mean ½ and standard deviation ½/√N, which is 0.00158 at
N = 10⁵, so the band ½ ± 0.005 is 3.16 standard deviations; an off-diagonal covariance entry has standard
deviation 1/√N = 0.00316, so 4/√N = 0.01265 is 4 standard deviations.

**How many statistics, and the family-wise rate.** For d = 2, 12 and 29 the sign statistics number
(1 + 2 + 2) + (66 + 12 + 12) + (406 + 29 + 29) = 559, and the covariance entries 1 + 66 + 406 = 473. A single
sign statistic of a correct sampler leaves the band with probability 2·(1 − Φ(3.162)) = 0.00157; if the 559
were independent, at least one would leave it with probability 1 − (1 − 0.00157)⁵⁵⁹ = 0.58, and at least one
covariance entry would exceed its bound with probability 0.03. The plan's instruction is that the number of
pairs tested is recorded so that the family-wise rate is stated. Because draws are addressed, a run is
deterministic: for a fixed seed plan the battery passes or fails forever. DESIGN (flagged for the Cortex lead):

- The seed plan (world addresses, stream keys, decision range) is registered in the battery file before the
  first run and is not changed after a result.
- A statistic outside its band on the registered plan triggers one *confirmation* on a second registered plan
  with N = 10⁶ for that statistic only, where ½ ± 0.005 is 10 standard deviations: a real defect of size 0.005
  or more persists, a 3.2 standard deviation fluctuation does not. The gate fails only if the confirmation is
  also outside the band.
- The record states: the count of statistics (559 and 473), the per-statistic and family-wise false-alarm
  rates, how many statistics went to confirmation, and their confirmed values.

**Marginal checks.** Before the four statistics the battery checks each channel's standardized noise alone:
positive in ½ ± 0.005 of N = 10⁵ draws, mean 0 ± 0.01 and variance 1 ± 0.02. They are reported separately so
that a rejection can be attributed to the joint law.

**The planted sampler (negative control).** The fixture is N33c-37's
`frob_conformance::plants::linear_phase_sampler`
(`crates/frob-conformance/src/plants/linear_phase_sampler.rs`, compiled only into the conformance build and
selectable there as `@fixture:planted-linear-phase`): the sibling trainer's Box–Muller recurrence, whose phase
uniform is a linear function of a 64-bit counter, so that the phase advances by a fixed 0.16724512 of a turn per
draw while the radius uniform is an independent addressed draw. Its recurrence, constants and expected values
are specified and tested there. What this battery relies on, from that bead's table: channels ℓ draws apart
agree in sign with probability 0.66551 at ℓ = 1, 0.33102 at ℓ = 2, 0.00347 at ℓ = 3 and 0.99306 at ℓ = 6;
consecutive decisions of one channel are d draws apart (0.33102 for d = 2, 0.98612 for d = 12, 0.70022 for
d = 29); adjacent worlds agree at ½; every marginal check passes. The battery must reject it on every tested
dimension, with the adjacent-channel agreement measured as 0.6655 ± 0.005: more than 100 standard deviations
from ½ at N = 10⁵. DESIGN (one fixture, one location): an earlier draft of this bead placed a second copy under
`fixtures/cortex/planted_sampler/`; that copy is withdrawn, because two implementations of one negative control
can drift apart and the gates GATE-CORTEX-18, GATE-WS-06 and GATE-WS-07 must all fire the same plant.

**Rejection record.** A failed qualification writes no receipt. It writes a rejection record `{kernel or fixture
digest, manifest digest, dimension, the first failing statistic (kind, channel pair or channel, observed value,
band), seed-plan identity, session}`, which is what the gate rows cite for the plant.

**Receipt.** On a pass the battery writes `{kernel digest, manifest digest, backend, dimensions tested, N, W,
statistics counts, extremes observed, seed-plan identity, session}` to the ledger. The receipt is what
`train.start` looks up. It is invalidated by any change of the kernel or manifest digest.

**The sampler row of GATE-WS-06.** The foundation-fact gate of the suite chapter has four rows, recorded by
N33c-40 at M0 with row 6 (the sibling's sampler) left `due M2`: "the planted sampler is rejected by rule P-12".
That row cannot be completed before this battery exists, and the fixture bead cannot wait for the battery
(this bead depends on it), so the row is completed here: after GATE-CORTEX-18 is measured, the M2 mode
`scripts/e2e/suite_ws06.sh --sampler-row` (the script of N33c-40, a dependency, which runs the same battery commands and the
same admission refusal as this bead's script) is run, and row 6 is written from its receipt with the
rejection record, the measured adjacent-channel agreement against 0.6655 ± 0.005, the passing result of the
qualified sampler as the row's control, and the sibling revision whose constants N33c-37 re-read. The gate
GATE-WS-06 then becomes `pass`.

**Layout.** `crates/frob-conformance/src/batteries/sampler_joint_law.rs` (the statistics, the bands, the
confirmation rule, the receipt and the rejection record, and the entries that `tests/g1_known_answers.rs`
collects under GATE-CORTEX-18); the two battery-control samplers (world-only and decision-only correlation) in
`crates/frob-conformance/src/plants/sampler_battery_controls.rs`, compiled only into the conformance crate; the
seed-plan file `crates/frob-conformance/batteries/sampler_joint_law.toml`, which registers the seed plans and
nothing else (no expected value: those are the registry row's). The planted linear-phase sampler is not in this bead's layout (N33c-37).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance batteries::sampler_joint_law`:
- `planted_marginals_pass`: on the plant of N33c-37 the battery's marginal checks pass: each channel positive in ½ ± 0.005 of draws, mean 0 ± 0.01, variance 1 ± 0.02.
- `planted_rejected_all_dims`: the plant is rejected for d = 2, 12 and 29, naming the first failing statistic; the adjacent-channel statistic is 0.6655 ± 0.005 on each dimension; no receipt is written and the rejection record names the pair, the statistic and the observed value.
- `planted_kinds_match_fixture_table`: the battery's three kinds of statistic on the plant agree within 0.005 with the fixture's expected values (channel lags 1, 2, 3 and 6; consecutive decisions 0.33102, 0.98612 and 0.70022 for d = 2, 12 and 29; adjacent worlds ½).
- The fixture's own arithmetic and direct-count tests (the phase step 0.16724512, the lag table, 66,551 ± 500 adjacent agreements in 10⁵ two-channel draws on three seeds) are N33c-37's and are not repeated here.
- `qualified_passes_bands`: the addressed sampler passes on the registered plan (with confirmations if triggered).
- `band_arithmetic`: ½/√10⁵ = 0.001581; 0.005 is 3.16 of them; 4/√10⁵ = 0.012649.
- `family_count`: 559 sign statistics and 473 covariance entries for d ∈ {2, 12, 29}.
- `confirmation_rule`: a synthetic statistic at 0.5055 on the first plan and 0.5004 at N = 10⁶ passes; 0.5055 then 0.5056 fails.
- `receipt_invalidated_by_digest_change`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/sampler_joint_law.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/sampler_joint_law/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Steps: (1) the gate's entries,
`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test g1_known_answers --
GATE-CORTEX-18` (the `host` and `lockstep` entries on `batch-cpu`, then one entry per device site; a site whose
backend is absent is `Skipped` in the receipt, never a pass; the mutation umbrella does not build in a release
profile, and 10⁵ decisions need none), and the value of GATE-CORTEX-18 recorded from its receipt with `cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-CORTEX-18 --receipt <hash>` (the one recorder, KNOWN_DRIFTS
9; the script never writes the row itself); (2) from that receipt, the entry in which the admission path refuses the
plant: the "not qualified" refusal with no world created; (3) the verb stage, `frob train start <reference
quadruped> --task quad-flat-velocity --backend batch-cpu --budget env_steps=<one collection> --seed <seed> --key
sjl-start --json` with the qualified sampler, asserting it starts and that the envelope names the sampler
manifest. Stage (3) needs the training verbs (N23a-14), which depend on the sampler and do not precede this
bead; until they exist it is logged `skipped: capability absent`, and the training script of N23a-35, which
depends on both beads, runs it. `cmd` events
carry the exact command, exit code, wall time and evidence block; `metric` events carry, per dimension and
kind, the extreme agreement with its band, the largest covariance entry with its bound, and the planted
sampler's agreement with its expected 0.6655 ± 0.005; `assert` events carry pass or fail, with the first
failing statistic (kind, channels, value) on failure; `refusal` events carry the code. The `summary` gives
assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-CORTEX-18"]`. The sampler row of GATE-WS-06 is logged by
`scripts/e2e/suite_ws06.sh --sampler-row` (N33c-40's script), which this bead runs after its own script passes;
that run's `summary` names `gates: [GATE-WS-06 (sampler row), GATE-CORTEX-18]` and its `metric` events carry,
per dimension, every pair's agreement frequency, the band ½ ± 0.005, the number of pairs tested and the planted
value against 0.6655 ± 0.005.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: the planted sampler's 0.665510 adjacent-channel agreement (reported counts 66,552, 66,552 and 66,551 in 100,000 calls per seed); independence gives 0.5 with standard deviation 0.00158 at N = 10⁵.
- Negative control of the gate: the planted linear-phase sampler of N33c-37 (registered there under rule P-12, GATE-CORTEX-18, GATE-WS-06 and the late risk row on the joint law; this bead registers no second control for it).
- Control of the battery itself: a second planted sampler that is correlated only across worlds (the same uniform reused in worlds w and w + 1) must be caught by statistic 3 and not by statistics 1 and 2, showing each statistic sees its own defect; one correlated only across decisions must be caught by statistic 2.
- Determinism lanes: the battery's statistics are exact functions of addressed draws; identical twice on one machine and across worker counts; the sign statistics agree across backends wherever the normal's sign is decided by the same exact uniforms.

## Considerations

- The gate as written tests 559 statistics at 3.2 standard deviations, so a correct sampler would fail a fresh random run more often than not; the registered plan with a confirmation stage is this bead's resolution and is flagged for the Cortex lead. The alternative, widening the band to a family-wise level, would weaken detection of small real defects.
- Sign agreement detects dependence through signs only; a dependence that preserves sign independence (for example a shared magnitude) is the covariance statistic's job, and higher-order dependence is not covered. The battery says what it tests.
- After the sibling's sampler is corrected upstream, the battery is re-run against it; only then may it serve as a comparison baseline.
