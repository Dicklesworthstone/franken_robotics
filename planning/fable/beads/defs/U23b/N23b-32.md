## Background

Rule E-10 of FrankenRobotics says the actor's information contract covers every field an online actor can read:
observation channels, earlier rewards, termination and truncation flags, action masks and each field of `info`,
each with an access class and a delivery instant (types in N23b-31). A rule about information needs a test that
does not read the policy's code. That test is the causal-policy test (`sense.causal-test`): run a world and a
twin that differs only in something the policy cannot yet know; at every decision instant record a digest of the
bytes delivered so far and a digest of the command; wherever the delivered digests of the two runs agree, the
command digests must agree. It already has five planted leaks about observations. This bead adds the four that
rule E-10 names, and the known answer that shows the rule matters:

GATE-CORTEX-19 (known answer, M2; adapter rows at its release, TARGET). Each of the four planted leaks fails
`sense.causal-test` in 100 of 100 seeds; the docking exclusion holds with reward hidden and flips with reward
delivered, on `VecEnv` and on the adapter; a contract change alters the `TaskId` and invalidates the cached
exclusion.

The docking model: three one-second ticks, x₀ = v₀ = 0, acceleration g·u_k during tick k with a hidden gain
g ∈ {1, 2} fixed for the episode, commands bounded by abs(u_k) ≤ U, mission (x₃, v₃) = (1, 0) exactly, and one
informative packet (a reading of v₁) that here arrives after the third command is committed. Exact integration
gives v₃ = g·(u₀ + u₁ + u₂) and x₃ = g·(5u₀/2 + 3u₁/2 + u₂/2). With the packet late a causal controller issues
one command vector in both worlds, the final position scales with g and cannot equal 1 for both gains: every
case is excluded.

## Scope

In: the four planted leaks as fixtures of the causal test; the docking known answer in both contract states on
`VecEnv` (and on the adapter when it exists); the `TaskId` and cache-invalidation check; the gate record; the
script.
Out: the causal test itself (BD-119, BD-215); the contract types (N23b-31); the docking fixture and its exact
kernels (BD-190) and the mission machinery (BD-129); the adapter (BD-301).

## Technical approach

**The four planted leaks.** Each is a planted leak of the kind the causal test already has for observations (the
leak registry of the sensing chapter): a small, real variant of the environment wrapper or task, compiled only
under the Cargo feature `conformance-leaks` and selected with `sense.causal-test --control leak.cortex.N` (N = 1
to 4, in the order of the table). A binary built without that feature does not have the option and refuses it as
an unknown parameter (FROB-REF-802), so a release binary cannot be asked to run a leak. The four are the plants
of the Cortex catalogue's late entry on the actor contract (N23-CX), from which the seeding tool of the
negative-control registry writes their four control records under GATE-CORTEX-19; this bead builds the variants
and writes no registry row by hand.

| Leak | Planted defect | Twin perturbation that exposes it | Expected failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Previous reward wired into the actor | The actor's input gather appends the previous interval's reward although the task leaves reward `trainer-only` | A hidden parameter that changes the reward and no delivered observation (the docking gain g) | Delivered-observation digests equal, command digests differ at the first decision after the first reward |
| An `info` field that carries a hidden parameter | `info["debug_gain"]` is passed to the policy | The same hidden gain | Failure at the first decision |
| An action mask computed from hidden state | A mask that forbids u > ½ when g = 2, computed from simulator truth | The hidden gain | The command differs (or the mask digest differs with equal delivered digests) at the first masked decision |
| A termination flag delivered before its instant | `terminated` for interval t is readable at the decision that precedes its delivery instant | A world that terminates at t against a twin that does not, with identical observations delivered up to that decision | Command digests differ one decision early |

Each leak is run on 100 seeds (seeds vary the twins' other draws); the gate requires failure in 100 of 100. The
honest wrapper must pass the test on the same 100 seeds.

**The docking known answer.** Task `fixture:dock-late-packet` with U = ¾, the packet late.

1. *Reward hidden (the default contract).* The mission query "does a causal policy exist that docks in both
   worlds?" returns **excluded** (an alias-pair witness: the two worlds deliver identical bytes to the actor at
   every decision, and x₃ = g·c cannot equal 1 for both g). On `VecEnv`: the witness policy below is run and
   cannot distinguish the worlds; its commands are equal in both and at most one world docks.
2. *Reward declared and delivered before the second command.* The shaped reward r₁ = −abs(1 − x₁) is declared
   an `actor` channel with a delivery instant before decision 2. With u₀ = ¼, x₁ = g/8: r₁ = −7/8 if g = 1 and
   −3/4 if g = 2. The witness policy reads r₁ and issues (¼, ½, −¾) for g = 1 and (¼, 0, −¼) for g = 2; both
   are within U = ¾ and both reach (1, 0) exactly: x₃ = 1·(5/8 + 3/4 − 3/8) = 1, v₃ = ¼ + ½ − ¾ = 0; and
   x₃ = 2·(5/8 + 0 − 1/8) = 1, v₃ = 2·(¼ + 0 − ¼) = 0. The verdict changes to **feasible**, with that witness.
3. Both runs are required: a harness that reports the same verdict in both is defective, and the gate treats
   equal verdicts as a failure of the harness.
4. Arithmetic is exact (the fixture runs on the exact-integer kernel class, so the equality x₃ = 1 is decided
   exactly on every backend).

**Contract change and the cache.** The two tasks differ only in the contract; their `TaskId`s must differ. The
exclusion is requested, cached, then requested for the second task: the cache must miss and the answer must be
recomputed as feasible. A planted `TaskId` without the contract digest serves the stale exclusion and must be
caught.

**Adapter rows (at the adapter's release; carried by BD-301).** This bead leaves the rows `pending`, and the
adapter bead BD-301, which depends on this one, records them by running this bead's script with its adapter
step enabled. What that step runs is specified here, so that the two halves of the gate use one script and one
set of fixtures. The same two runs are driven through the adapter by a small Python driver: with reward hidden from the policy (the driver passes only `obs` to the policy) the verdict is excluded;
with a driver whose policy consumes the returned reward while the task leaves reward `trainer-only`, the run is
labelled `actor-read-undeclared`, the reward-perturbation probe detects the read, and the run is refused as a
mission answer; with the reward declared, the witness docks both worlds.

**Record.** Per leak: seeds failed of 100, first failing decision and channel. Docking: the two verdicts with
the witness; the two `TaskId`s; cache hit or miss. Recorded per tier (`batch-cpu` first; devices where they
exist).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-CORTEX-19 is recorded with measured values: each of the four planted leaks fails the causal test in 100 of 100 seeds, with the first failing decision instant and channel; the honest wrapper passes on the same seeds.
- [ ] On `VecEnv` the docking exclusion holds with reward hidden and the verdict is feasible with reward delivered, with the witness (¼, ½, −¾) and (¼, 0, −¼) reaching (1, 0) exactly in both worlds.
- [ ] The two contract states have different `TaskId`s and the cached exclusion is not served for the second.
- [ ] The adapter step of the script exists and is skipped with `skipped: capability absent` until the adapter exists; the gate row states "adapter rows pending (BD-301)". BD-301 records the rows by enabling that step; this bead does not wait for it.
- [ ] The harness-defect check exists: equal verdicts in the two runs fail the gate.
- [ ] The four leaks are controls of the gate in the negative-control registry (seeded from the catalogue entry, `feature = "conformance-leaks"`, `target = "sense.causal-test --control leak.cortex.N"`) and are unreachable from release builds: `--control` on a binary without the feature is refused with FROB-REF-802.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --features conformance-leaks actor_contract`:
- `leak_prev_reward_fails`, `leak_info_hidden_param_fails`, `leak_mask_hidden_state_fails`, `leak_early_termination_fails`: each on 100 seeds, 100 failures, with the expected first failing decision.
- `honest_wrapper_passes`: 100 of 100 passes.
- `docking_arithmetic`: exact rationals: r₁ ∈ {−7/8, −3/4}; x₃ and v₃ for both witness sequences equal (1, 0); a common command vector gives x₃ ratios 1 : 2 across gains.
- `verdicts_differ`: excluded with reward hidden; feasible with reward delivered.
- `taskid_differs_and_cache_misses`.
- `same_verdict_is_harness_defect`: a planted harness returning `excluded` twice fails.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/actor_contract.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/actor_contract/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Steps, with a `frob` built with the
`conformance-leaks` feature (on a release binary step 1 is replaced by the assertion that `--control` is refused
with FROB-REF-802, and the leaks are then shown by step 5 alone): (1) for each planted leak N, `frob sense
causal-test <docking fixture design> --task @fixture:dock-late-packet --policy @fixture:leak-probe --control
leak.cortex.<N> --seeds 100 --key leak<N> --json`, asserting exit code 2 with `verdict: "fail"` on every seed and
the expected first failing decision and channel; (2) the honest wrapper, the same call without `--control` and
with `--key honest`, asserting a pass on the same seeds; (3) `frob task show @fixture:dock-late-packet --json`
and the reward-delivered variant, comparing the printed contract digests and `TaskId`s; (4) `frob mission
synthesize <query> --key dock-hidden --json` and `--key dock-delivered` over the two gains on the two tasks (the
M1 verb that returns exclusions and witness controllers), then `frob mission show <query> --answer <answer>
--set excluded --json` and `--set inner`, asserting `excluded` for the first task and the witness for the second,
and that the second answer was not an idempotent replay of the first; (5) the `VecEnv` half of the gate, which
no verb reaches with a hand-written witness policy: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance
--features gauntlet-mutations,conformance-leaks --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-CORTEX-19`, whose entries step
the witness policy through the real `VecEnv` on both tasks and both gains (docking in both worlds only on the
second task), run the four leaks on 100 seeds and apply the gate's other controls; the script asserts on the
receipt, and the value of GATE-CORTEX-19 is recorded from it with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
record_gate -- --gate GATE-CORTEX-19 --receipt <hash>` (the one recorder, KNOWN_DRIFTS 9); (6) the adapter step (`--adapter`), which BD-301 enables at the adapter's release and which logs
`skipped: capability absent` until then.
`cmd` events carry the exact command, exit code, wall time and evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism
`exact`, identities, seed); `metric` events carry failures of 100 per leak (threshold 100); `assert` events
carry verdicts, the witness values and the `TaskId` comparison; on failure the first seed, decision and channel
are logged with the artifact paths. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-CORTEX-19"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The docking family's known answers: with the packet late every case is excluded; with an informative signal delivered before the second command and u₀ = ¼, the two sequences above dock both worlds; exact arithmetic gives no false admissions.
- The late counterexample "a reward handed to the actor is not an observation" (provisional identifier CX-23-L2) is the seed of the four controls.
- Negative controls: the four leaks; the harness that reports one verdict twice; the `TaskId` without the contract digest; a causal test that compares content and ignores delivery instants (it must miss the early-termination leak, so the real test is shown to depend on instants).
- Determinism lanes: digests and verdicts are exact; identical twice on one machine, across worker counts, debug and release, and across backends (the fixture uses exact-integer kernels).

## Considerations

- The gate's 100 of 100 is strict on purpose: a leak is a logical defect, not a statistical one; a leak that fails 99 of 100 seeds indicates a twin perturbation that sometimes fails to expose it, and the fixture is repaired.
- The docking model is a three-tick scalar plant; nothing about a real docking device follows from it. Its value is that both verdicts are provable by hand.
- Externally driven policies cannot be inspected; the reward-perturbation probe detects consumption only if consumption changes the commands, so the label is conservative: a driver that does not declare its policy inputs is treated as having read everything.
