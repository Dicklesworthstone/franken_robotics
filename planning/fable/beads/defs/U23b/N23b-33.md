## Background

Figures of the form "time to a target" appear throughout a training system: the training-time parity gate, the
performance table's time-to-threshold rows, every comparison of learning algorithms. The usual rule stops the
clock at the first evaluation that clears the target. That rule is wrong, and the plan gives the case.

Counterexample. A policy with true success rate 0.89 is evaluated against a target of 0.90 with 30 episodes per
look. A look with 30 successes of 30 has an exact one-sided 95% lower bound of 0.05^(1/30) = 0.90497, above the
target. One look does this with probability 0.89³⁰ = 0.0303. Across 50 independent looks at the unchanged policy
the probability of at least one such crossing is 1 − (1 − 0.0303)⁵⁰ = 0.78548. A rule that stops the clock at
the first crossing reports attainment, about four times in five, for a policy that never attains.

The rule adopted: a recorded score crossing is a descriptive event. Attainment is a claim about the policy's
population performance, and it needs error control over every evaluation look that could have produced the
crossing. Tier: Solid. Owner: `frob-learn`, with the study machinery of the task-and-study crate.

## Scope

In: the registered development stream and cadence; the look table in the run record; the attainment procedure
for success targets and for return targets; the two fields of `train.status`; the final-holdout alternative with
its label; right-censoring and the restricted mean; the parity rule.
Out: the pre-analysis verb and the gate's simulation (N23b-34); the consumers (BD-218, N23b-09, N23b-13, the
pilots).

## Technical approach

**Registration (before the run).** A training run that will report any time to a target registers: the target
(a success rate p₀, or a return level with the return's declared span [a, b]); the total level α (default 0.05);
the development stream (a fixed, addressed sequence of evaluation worlds, disjoint from training worlds by
split); the cadence (when looks happen, in environment steps or wall-clock, and how many episodes nₖ look k
uses). The registration is hashed into the run record; a cadence or target changed after the first look is
refused. Looks use non-overlapping segments of the stream by default.

**The run record holds every look, not only the best.** Per look k: index, wall-clock and step count, nₖ,
successes (or mean return), the level αₖ, the lower bound at that level, `crossed` and `attained`.

**Attainment procedure (default).** Error is allocated across looks as

    αₖ = α / [k·(k + 1)],   k = 1, 2, …,   which sums to α over all k,

so the probability that a policy at or below the target is ever declared attained is at most α, whatever the
dependence between looks and however many looks occur.

- *Success targets.* At look k with x successes of n: an exact one-sided binomial test at level αₖ of "true
  rate ≤ p₀": attained when P(X ≥ x | n, p₀) ≤ αₖ, equivalently when the exact one-sided lower confidence bound
  at level αₖ exceeds p₀. For an all-success look this needs p₀ⁿ ≤ αₖ, that is n ≥ ln(1/αₖ)/ln(1/p₀). With
  p₀ = 0.90 and α = 0.05: 36 episodes at k = 1 (αₖ = 0.025), 46 at k = 2, 74 at k = 10, 103 at k = 50.
- *Return targets.* A confidence sequence on bounded returns from the study machinery, evaluated at level αₖ at
  look k; attained when its lower bound exceeds the target. Reference check (conservative): Hoeffding's bound,
  mean − (b − a)·√(ln(1/αₖ)/(2n)).

**`train.status` (two fields).** `crossing`: the first look whose point score met the target; descriptive.
`attained`: the first look at which the registered procedure rejects "at or below target"; or `censored` with
the budget. DESIGN sketch of the added block:

```json
"target": {"kind": "success", "p0": "0.90", "alpha": "0.05", "registration": "b3:…"},
"looks": [ {"k": 1, "wall_s": "61.0", "steps": "1.2e7", "n": 64, "successes": 55, "alpha_k": "0.025",
            "lower_bound": "0.7498", "crossed": false, "attained": false} ],
"crossing": {"look": 4, "wall_s": "244.0"}, "attained": {"look": 6, "wall_s": "366.0"},
"attainment_reachable_from_look": 1
```

**Reachability.** At registration the procedure reports the first look at which attainment is possible at all
(`attainment_reachable_from_look`, or `never`). A cadence of 30 episodes per look against p₀ = 0.90 gives
`never` (even look 1 needs 36). Such a run is labelled `attainment-unreachable`; it may report crossings and can
never report a time to target, and a gate that needs one refuses the registration.

**The final-holdout alternative.** The checkpoint chosen on the development stream is evaluated once on a final
holdout at level α. That qualifies the chosen checkpoint and leaves its first attainment time unknown; the
reported time is then an upper bound and is labelled `upper-bound`.

**Censoring.** Non-attainment within the training budget B is right-censored at B. A set of runs is summarized
by the restricted mean time E[min(T, B)], estimated by the mean of min(Tᵢ, B), reported with the count censored;
a plain mean over attained runs only is never reported.

**Parity comparisons.** Both arms are scored by the same rule on the same stream sizes. An incumbent's
published time to threshold is context; the figure re-measured under this rule is the comparator.

**Layout.** `crates/frob-learn/src/train/attainment.rs`; the look table in the run record; the fields in the
`train.status` schema.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A run cannot report a time to target without a registration made before its first look; a changed cadence or target is refused.
- [ ] Every look is in the run record; `train.status` shows `crossing` and `attained` separately, each with its look and time, or `censored`.
- [ ] The four episode counts are reproduced exactly: 36, 46, 74 and 103 all-success episodes at looks 1, 2, 10 and 50 for p₀ = 0.90, α = 0.05.
- [ ] A policy at true rate 0.89 simulated over 50 looks is declared attained with probability at most α for any registered cadence (measured by N23b-34's gate); the first-crossing figure is never presented as attainment.
- [ ] A cadence that cannot attain is labelled `attainment-unreachable` at registration.
- [ ] The final-holdout alternative labels its time `upper-bound`; censored runs are summarized by the restricted mean with the censored count.
- [ ] The procedure is the one used by the training-time parity gate for both arms.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-learn train::attainment`:
- `alpha_series`: α₁ = 0.025, α₂ = 0.008333, α₁₀ = 0.0004545, α₅₀ = 0.00001961; the partial sum over 50 looks is 0.05·(1 − 1/51) = 0.04902.
- `all_success_counts`: ⌈ln(1/αₖ)/ln(1/0.9)⌉ = 36, 46, 74, 103 for k = 1, 2, 10, 50 (35.01, 45.44, 73.05 and 102.88 before rounding up).
- `exact_binomial_boundaries`: n = 120, p₀ = 0.90: the least x that attains is 115 at k = 1, 116 at k = 2, 118 at k = 10 and 120 at k = 50.
- `thirty_of_thirty_not_attained`: 30 of 30 at look 1 has P(X ≥ 30 | 0.9) = 0.0424 > 0.025: `crossed` true, `attained` false; its unadjusted 95% lower bound is 0.90497.
- `reachability`: cadence 50 × 30 → `never`; 50 × 36 → look 1 only; 50 × 120 → look 1.
- `crossing_vs_attained_fields`: a synthetic look table yields crossing at look 4 and attained at look 6.
- `censoring_and_restricted_mean`: times (300, 400, censored at 600) → restricted mean 433.3 with one censored.
- `upper_bound_label`: the final-holdout path sets the label.
- `hoeffding_reference`: n = 100, span 1, αₖ = 0.025: half-width √(ln 40/200) = 0.1358.
- `registration_frozen`: editing the cadence after look 1 is refused.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The counterexample's numbers: 0.90497, 0.0303, 0.78548; the four episode counts.
- Negative controls: a planted `train.status` that copies `crossing` into `attained`; a planted procedure that applies level α at every look (no spending), which attains at 30 of 30; a planted record that keeps only the best look. Each must fail its test.
- Metamorphic relation: inserting extra looks at which nothing is evaluated changes no existing αₖ (levels depend on the look index of the registered cadence, so the cadence is part of the registration).
- Determinism: the procedure is an exact function of the look table (class D0 for counts); identical on every run.

## Considerations

- The spending series is conservative for late looks (αₖ falls as 1/k²); a run that attains late needs more episodes per look. The registration may grow nₖ with k; it may not be changed after the fact.
- Error control across looks holds for one run. Choosing the best of several runs is a second multiplicity and is the business of the qualification lane's request index, which uses the same series across requests.
- A time to target is a wall-clock quantity and is not deterministic; the look table's scores are re-computable under a D2 profile.
- The published incumbent time was a first crossing read from a curve; expect the re-measured, error-controlled time to be longer for both arms.
