## Background

FrankenRobotics scores designs on a ladder of rungs, cheap ones first (a shared family policy or a baseline
controller), dear ones last (a specialist policy promoted on the referee). A cheap rung may *select* finalists
only where its rank agreement with the higher rung has been measured: a lower 95% bound of at least 0.7 on
Kendall's τ over the top band admits a rung to selection. Kendall's τ = (concordant − discordant pairs)/C(n, 2)
counts inverted pairs and weighs every pair equally. It does not say that the best design survives a cut.

Counterexample (the reason for this bead). Forty designs. The cheap rung reproduces the true order exactly,
except that the true best is ranked third. Among the true top ten, 2 of 45 pairs are inverted, so
τ = (43 − 2)/45 = 41/45 ≈ 0.911, far above 0.7. A cut that keeps the top 5%, two designs, discards the winner.

Rule. For a rung admitted to selection with m finalists wanted, the number of designs advanced is the largest
of three numbers: m; the survivor count of the conformal margin rule (every design whose lower-rung score is
within 2·q̂ of the k-th best lower-rung score advances, q̂ being the calibrated residual quantile); and the
*containment rank* k_c(m): the smallest k such that, in the calibration experiment and in every audit-lane draw
to date for the class and task family, the higher rung's top m all lie inside the lower rung's top k. The
ledger row gains two fields: `containment_rank(m)`, and the *regret* of a cut, which is the higher-rung score
of the best design the cut discards minus that of the best design it keeps. Positive regret means the winner
was lost. A selection whose retention is below k_c(m) is refused, with k_c(m) as the repair. Where a class has
no calibration, the rung discards by the fixed table and does not select.

Tier: Solid. Owner: `frob-opt`. Verbs: `eval.rank-agreement` reports τ, top-k recall, the containment rank and
the regret of the requested cut; `opt.cascade` reads k_c(m) when it sizes a rung transition.

## Technical approach

**Definitions.** A *paired set* S is a set of n designs each scored on a lower rung (y_lo) and a higher rung
(y_hi), larger is better. Ranks are 1-based within S. Ties in y_lo are broken pessimistically for containment
(a tied design counts at the worst rank of its tie group); ties in y_hi enlarge the top-m set to include every
design tied with the m-th.

- τ on a band B ⊆ S: over the C(|B|, 2) pairs of B, (concordant − discordant)/C(|B|, 2); pairs tied on either
  score count as neither.
- top-k recall at (m, k): |top_m(y_hi) ∩ top_k(y_lo)| / m.
- containment rank on S: k_S(m) = max over d ∈ top_m(y_hi) of rank_lo(d).
- regret of a cut at k on S: max{y_hi(d) : rank_lo(d) > k} − max{y_hi(d) : rank_lo(d) ≤ k}; if nothing is
  discarded the regret is −∞ and is reported as `none`.
- k_c(m) for a class and task family: the largest k_S(m) over the calibration set and every audit-lane draw to
  date. Where a set's size n_S differs from the population size n being cut, its rank is scaled to
  ⌈k_S(m)·n/n_S⌉ (DESIGN reading: the plan defines k_c on sets and applies it to populations of other sizes
  without stating the scaling; a rank quantile is the conservative natural choice, and the question is flagged
  for the owner of the ladder).

**Retention rule.** `advance = max(m, conformal_survivors, k_c(m))`. A request to select with retention below
k_c(m) is refused (the Cortex registry's request `selection-below-containment`, proposed FROB-REF-570 in N23-REF,
raised through its named constant until the allocation ledger fixes the number; exit 3) with `observed` = the
requested retention, `required` = k_c(m), and one repair: retain k_c(m). The neighbouring code FROB-REF-537 is
different. It refuses a rung that has no qualifying rank-agreement record at all, whereas this refusal is for a
rung admitted to selection whose requested cut is too tight. With no calibration record for the class and task family the mode is not `selective` at all, and the
rung discards by the fixed table (successive halving with η = 3: the top ⌈n/3⌉ advance).

**Ledger fields.** `containment_rank: {m: k}` for the values of m in use (at least m = 1 and the campaign's
finalist count) and `regret: {k: value}` for the cut actually applied, per stratum, with the set identities that
produced them. Both are recomputed whenever an audit-lane draw arrives; k_c(m) never decreases within one model
identity (a change of model, kernel version, effort profile or battery version restarts the row).

**Report fields of `eval.rank-agreement`.** For a requested cut (m, k): `tau_band` with its bootstrap interval,
`top_k_recall`, `containment_rank`, `regret`, and `admissible: true|false` with the refusal when false.

**The fixture form of `eval.rank-agreement` (this bead's part of the verb; VERBS-eval's row).** `frob eval
rank-agreement --fixture <name> [--class <c>] [--band <b>] [--m <m> --k <k>] --key <key> --json` reads a
registered synthetic score table and runs nothing: tier `static`, determinism `exact`, every output labelled
`synthetic`; the verb is of kind `run`, so the call carries `--key`. `<name>` resolves to
`crates/frob-opt/fixtures/rank_agreement/<name>.toml`, the directory and the file format that BD-265 registers
(`name`, `synthetic = true`, `class`, the strata, per design and stratum the scores `y_lo` and `y_hi`, the
planted top-weighted τ, the declared `mode` and `mode_reason`). The form serves every file of that directory,
so BD-265's `tier-pair-planted-0.8`, `tier-pair-planted-0.6`, `tier-pair-planted-0.3` and
`tier-pair-winner-lost` are reached through it (N16b-32 steps 3 and 4 call it with `--class quadruped`), and
this bead adds its own file, `forty-design-winner-third.toml`, there. The output is the row's: the record with
τ, the strata, `mode` and `mode_reason` recomputed by BD-265's rule from the table, and, when `--m` and `--k`
are given, the cut's fields above. `--class` is optional with `--fixture` and must equal the file's `class`; a
name with no file, a file that fails the format, or a differing class is FROB-REF-802 with `pointer`
`/fixture` or `/class`. No private fixture path and no second directory exists.

**The allocation entry (DESIGN).** `frob_opt::winner_check::admit_selection(record, m, k, conformal_survivors)
-> Result<Advance, Refusal>` applies the retention rule and is the one place the retention refusal is raised:
`opt.cascade` (BD-352, M4) calls it when it sizes a selecting transition, and until that verb exists the same
function is reached from the user's side through the example program `cargo run -p frob-opt --example
winner_check -- select --fixture <name> --m <m> --k <k> --json`, which prints one envelope and exits with its
code (exit 3 on the refusal). `eval.rank-agreement` itself only reports `admissible`.

**The forty-design fixture (labelled `synthetic`; numbers chosen to realize the plan's case).** Registered as
`crates/frob-opt/fixtures/rank_agreement/forty-design-winner-third.toml` in BD-265's format: one stratum, `class
= "quadruped"` (a label: nothing runs), the forty score pairs below, the planted τ on the true top ten 41/45,
`mode = "selective"` and `mode_reason = "measured"` (the rung is admitted to selection on τ and top-ten recall
1; that it still loses the winner at k = 2 is what the winner check adds). Designs
d₁ … d₄₀ with y_hi(dᵢ) = 41 − i (d₁ is the true best, score 40). y_lo equals y_hi except: y_lo(d₂) = 40,
y_lo(d₃) = 39, y_lo(d₁) = 38. The lower-rung order is d₂, d₃, d₁, d₄, …, d₄₀. Then on the true top ten:
discordant pairs are (d₁, d₂) and (d₁, d₃), τ = (43 − 2)/45 = 41/45; over all forty, τ = (778 − 2)/780 = 776/780.
Top-1 recall at k = 2 is 0. Containment rank for m = 1 is 3. Regret at k = 2 is y_hi(d₁) − y_hi(d₂) = 40 − 39 =
1 > 0; at k = 3 it is y_hi(d₄) − y_hi(d₁) = 37 − 40 = −3.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The library computes τ on a band, top-k recall, containment rank and regret; `eval.rank-agreement` reports them for a requested cut.
- [ ] GATE-CORTEX-21 is measured by its entries in the `g1_known_answers` tier target (the fixture's values through the library and through the verb's fixture form, with the three planted controls caught in the same run) and recorded from that run's receipt by this bead, the gate's one recorder (N23b-16), outside any script and in a session that did not implement the check: on the forty-design fixture the verb returns τ = 41/45 on the true top ten, top-1 recall 0 at k = 2, containment rank 3 for m = 1 and positive regret at k = 2; a request to select with k = 2 is refused and the same request with k = 3 is accepted.
- [ ] The refusal carries the requested retention, k_c(m) and the single repair; its code is `selection-below-containment` (proposed 570), never 537, and the script's `refusal` event carries it.
- [ ] The ledger row holds `containment_rank` and `regret`; an audit-lane draw that raises a set's containment rank raises k_c(m) and never lowers it.
- [ ] With no calibration for a class and task family, a selection request is not served by this rule: the rung discards by the fixed table.
- [ ] `winner_check::admit_selection` returns max(m, conformal survivors, k_c(m)) or the retention refusal, and the example program `winner_check` reaches it from the command line; BD-352's `opt.cascade` calls the same function when it sizes a selecting transition and asserts the refusal through the verb in its own script (nothing here waits for it).
- [ ] The fixture form serves every file of `crates/frob-opt/fixtures/rank_agreement/`: `--fixture tier-pair-winner-lost --class quadruped` returns BD-265's declared mode and reason, `--fixture forty-design-winner-third` the values above; an unknown name, and a `--class` that differs from the file's, are FROB-REF-802.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt winner_check`:
- `fixture_tau_top_ten`: 41/45 as an exact rational.
- `fixture_tau_all`: 776/780.
- `fixture_recall`: top-1 recall at k = 2 is 0; at k = 3 it is 1.
- `fixture_containment`: k_c(1) = 3; k_c(2) = 3; k_c(3) = 3; k_c(4) = 4.
- `fixture_regret`: +1 at k = 2; −3 at k = 3.
- `selection_refused_below_containment`: (m = 1, k = 2) refused with `selection-below-containment` (proposed 570) and required 3; (m = 1, k = 3) accepted; a stratum with no record gives 537, not 570.
- `advance_is_max_of_three`: m = 3, conformal survivors 6, k_c = 4 → 6; m = 3, survivors 2, k_c = 5 → 5.
- `ties_pessimistic`: two designs tied at lower-rung ranks 2 and 3, one of them the true best → containment rank 3.
- `kc_monotone`: sets with containment ranks (3, 2, 5) give k_c = 5; a later set with 4 leaves it at 5.
- `kc_scaling`: k_S = 3 on a set of 40, applied to a population of 120 → ⌈3 × 120/40⌉ = 9.
- `no_calibration_fixed_table`: n = 81 with no record → 27 advance by the fixed table and the mode is not `selective`.
- Property: for random paired sets, cutting at k ≥ k_S(m) always has top-m recall 1 and non-positive regret for m = 1.
- `fixture_form_loads_directory`: the five files of `fixtures/rank_agreement/` load in BD-265's format; `forty-design-winner-third` yields the values of the fixture tests through the verb's handler; a name with no file is 802 (`pointer` `/fixture`), and `--class wheeled` on a `quadruped` file is 802 (`pointer` `/class`).
- `fixture_declared_mode_matches_classifier`: BD-265's mode rule recomputed on `forty-design-winner-third` gives `selective`, `measured` (so the file passes BD-265's registration test, which recomputes every file of the directory).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/winner_check.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/winner_check/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Steps: (1) `frob eval rank-agreement --fixture
forty-design-winner-third --band true-top-10 --m 1 --k 2 --key "$(e2e_key ra-k2)" --json`, asserting τ = 41/45,
recall 0, containment rank 3, regret > 0 and `admissible: false`; (2) the same with `--k 3` and its own key
(`ra-k3`; without a distinct key the call would return step 1's stored result), asserting `admissible: true`
and regret ≤ 0; (3) a selection request at k = 2 through the allocation entry, `cargo run -p frob-opt --example
winner_check -- select --fixture forty-design-winner-third --m 1 --k 2 --json`, asserting exit 3 with the
retention refusal `selection-below-containment`, `observed` 2, `required` 3 and the repair "retain 3"; (4)
the same at k = 3, accepted with `advance` 3; (5) `frob eval rank-agreement --fixture tier-pair-winner-lost
--class quadruped --key "$(e2e_key ra-bd265)" --json`, asserting BD-265's declared mode `uninformative` with
`winner-lost` (the form loads BD-265's directory), and `e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-802 3 --` the verb with
`--fixture no-such-table`; (6) the gate: `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-CORTEX-21 --mutations`, asserting from
the receipt (`e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) the gate's line and the three controls `caught`. The script
records nothing (STD-E2E's linter fails a script that runs `record_gate`): this bead records the gate from
that receipt's hash with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-CORTEX-21 --receipt
<hash>`, outside the script (N38b-19). `opt.cascade` is not called: it lands at M4 (BD-352). `cmd` events carry the exact command, exit code, wall time and evidence block (claim, class,
tier `static`, determinism `exact`, identities, `synthetic`); `metric` events carry τ, recall, containment rank
and regret with their expected values; `refusal` events carry code, observed and required. `summary`:
assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-CORTEX-21"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The fixture's values above; the late counterexample "a rung with τ above 0.7 near the top keeps the winner" (provisional identifier CX-23-L4 in the counterexample registry).
- Negative controls the gate must catch: a planted allocation that admits selection on τ alone (it accepts k = 2 on the fixture); a planted containment rank computed on the lower rung's own top m (it returns 1); a planted regret with the sign reversed.
- Metamorphic relations: relabelling designs changes nothing; a strictly increasing transformation of y_lo changes neither τ, recall nor containment rank; adding a constant to y_hi leaves regret unchanged.
- Determinism: all quantities are exact functions of the two score vectors (class D0); identical on every run and platform.

## Considerations

- A screen that discards the winner is a named high risk of the program; its other mitigations are the audit lane and the planted winner. The containment rank turns the audit lane's findings into a retention floor.
- k_c(m) can only grow as audits accumulate, which makes a long-running class more conservative; that is intended, and a new model identity restarts it.
- The scaling of k_c to populations of a different size is this bead's reading and is the first thing to confirm with the ladder's owner.
- With one calibration of forty designs, k_c(1) is a sample maximum and can be optimistic; the conformal survivor count and the audit lane cover what it misses.
