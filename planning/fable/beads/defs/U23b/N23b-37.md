## Background

✖ KILL-SHORTGRAD governs short-horizon gradient training with design parameters, a Moonshot learning arm in
which policy parameters and a continuous design vector are updated together from analytic gradients of a short
rollout. Trigger: the pilot's falsifier fires, or its gate fails after one repair cycle. Fallback, named in
advance: PPO with the paired finite-difference stencil where that passes its own gate; otherwise derivative-free
search over the design vector. The kill gate has no milestone of its own and is decided at the milestone of the
gate beside it, M6.

The pilot's falsifier: the method does not reach the matched, referee-promoted return in at most 0.35 of the
derivative-free arm's GPU-hours, or the median cosine between the analytic design gradient and the paired
central difference is below 0.5 on more than 40% of monitored windows. The gate (GATE-CORTEX-22, design
comparison, M6): the same comparison on both tasks on our engine: matched referee-promoted return in at most
0.35 of the derivative-free arm's GPU-hours; cosine at least 0.5 on at least 60% of monitored windows; every
reported design re-scored by the ladder, none promoted on a gradient.

This bead takes the first reading (at the pilot) and fixes which fallback is in force. The final reading is
taken with the gate by N23b-39.

## Scope

In: the first reading; the precondition checks; the decision record; the fallback selection; downstream
consequences.
Out: the pilot (N23b-36); the mechanism and the gate (N23b-38, N23b-39).

## Technical approach

1. **Preconditions.** (a) The step-adjoint mechanism exists and its gate passed; if its own kill gate
   (KILL-STEPADJOINT, N16c-28) fired, this decision is recorded with the reason "no gradient source" and the
   fallback is selected. (b) The pilot record (its row in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`) exists
   with its controls caught and one meter row per run.
2. **Rule.** The outcome is named in the four names of the kill-gate record (N40-03): `fired`, `not-fired`,
   `not-reached`, `deferred`. The pilot's result words map to the register's statuses (`falsified` →
   `falsifier-holds`, `not-falsified` → `falsifier-rejected`, `undetermined` → `inconclusive`).
   - Pilot `falsified` → `fired`. N23b-38 and N23b-39, which exist only for the funded mechanism, are closed
     `withdrawn` citing the pilot's `[[pilot.result]]` (verdict `falsifier-holds`; N41-17 rule 7), and the ADR
     lists them "withdrawn before this decision".
   - Pilot `not-falsified` and precondition (a) holds → `not-fired` at this reading, citing the pilot's receipt:
     funded; the gate's clause stays open for the final reading.
   - Pilot `undetermined` → `not-reached` (not funded); a repeat needs a new frozen protocol, and is a `deferred`
     outcome only if an ADR names the later exit and its reason.
   - Pilot closed `withdrawn: not run: no envelope, R17` (N23b-36's not-approved branch, R17 declined) →
     `not-reached` with that reason; the fallback selected in step 3 is in force, and N23b-38 and N23b-39 are
     closed `withdrawn` citing R17 (N44-01 step 5) and listed "withdrawn before this decision". It is never read
     as `not-fired`.
   - Precondition (a) fails → `not-reached` with the reason "no gradient source", whatever the pilot says.
   - On either `not-reached` branch that is not R17's, N23b-38 and N23b-39 close `withdrawn` citing this ADR,
     whose entry in force is `not-reached` (N41-17 rule 5: they exist only on the funded branch).
3. **Fallback selection.** If the paired-stencil mechanism passed its own gate (variance ratio of at least 2 at
   the admitted horizon and sign agreement of at least 70% over 20 replicates on its pilot task; its kill gate
   not fired), the fallback is PPO with the paired stencil for the design gradient. Otherwise the fallback is
   derivative-free search over the design vector (rank-based search through the optimization verbs), with the
   stencil kept only as a sensitivity report.
4. **Record.** `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SHORTGRAD-M6.md` (the program's one directory for decision records) with the
   fields N40-03 requires: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered by the fallback ledger N40-29
   and any amendments; each measured value with its receipt hash beside its threshold (the pilot record's
   identifier and hash; the ratio against 0.35; the share of monitored windows below 0.5 against 40%); the two
   precondition statuses; the outcome and the deciding branch; the fallback in force and its name
   (`ppo-with-stencil` or `derivative-free`); the beads re-read (N23b-38, N23b-39: released, or withdrawn as
   step 2 says); what changes downstream; the recording session, different from the pilot's. Registry: the first
   entry of the `decision` array (`exit = "M6"`, `outcome`, `adr`, `date`, `session`, and `receipts` with the
   pilot result's receipt, at least one for `not-fired`) of the KILL-SHORTGRAD row that
   N40-29 entered in `registries/gates.toml`, and the owner declaration (`[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`, the
   ledger's `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied, not restated) in `frob-learn`'s
   `contract.toml`, which the cross-check N40-30 compares with the ledger. The final reading (N23b-39) appends a
   second entry with its own ADR; this entry is never edited. Both readings fall at M6, so the second entry
   carries `reading = "final"` and its ADR is `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SHORTGRAD-M6-final.md` (N40-03 step 6).
5. **Downstream if it fires or is `not-reached`.** `train.start --algo short-horizon-ac` is refused by the
   dispatch as a capability not in the build: `FROB-REF-724`, reason `killed: KILL-SHORTGRAD` after `fired`
   (`not-linked` when `not-reached`), exit 5; `frob capabilities --json` lists the fallback in force and not the
   method, with the decision record's hash; joint refinement of a finalist and its controller is done by the
   fallback; no report mentions a gradient-trained policy; the phase ledger's refinement stage is unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SHORTGRAD-M6.md` exists with N40-03's required fields: the pilot hash, both measured quantities against their thresholds, both precondition statuses, the outcome (one of N40-03's four) with its branch, and the fallback in force.
- [ ] The KILL-SHORTGRAD row in `registries/gates.toml` carries the first `decision` entry, and `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (N40-30) passes for this gate (owner declaration present, trigger and fallback equal to the ledger's, outcome consistent with the measured values).
- [ ] If `fired` or `not-reached`: the fallback is named in the row and in the printouts, GATE-CORTEX-22 carries the status N40-03's propagation gives it, N23b-38 and N23b-39 are closed `withdrawn` citing the decision that step 2 names (the pilot result, R17, or this ADR), and the verb refuses the algorithm with `FROB-REF-724`.
- [ ] If `not-fired`: the record states that the final reading is owed by N23b-39.
- [ ] The fallback named matches the recorded status of the paired-stencil gate.
- [ ] The recording session differs from the pilot's.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_gates.rs`: `shortgrad_first_reading_branches` (pilot outcomes × adjoint
status → `fired`; `not-fired`; `not-reached` with reason "no gradient source"; `not-reached` with reason
`undetermined`; `not-reached` with reason "not run: no envelope"; every returned outcome is one of N40-03's four,
and an `undetermined` pilot never yields `not-fired`); `shortgrad_killed_capability` (with the outcome `fired`,
`--algo short-horizon-ac` → FROB-REF-724 with reason `killed: KILL-SHORTGRAD`, exit 5; with `not-reached`, reason
`not-linked`); `shortgrad_record_adr_path` (the first entry's ADR is `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SHORTGRAD-M6.md`; a second
entry may be appended, the first never edited); `shortgrad_fallback_selection`
(stencil gate passed → `ppo-with-stencil`; stencil killed → `derivative-free`); `shortgrad_record_requires_pilot_hash`;
`shortgrad_self_recording_refused`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kill_shortgrad_first.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N23b-37 milestone=M6 labels=cortex,kill-gate,moonshot
gates=KILL-SHORTGRAD seed=0 needs=cpu plants=not-fired-with-falsified-pilot`; sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh`
(STD-E2E); NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/kill_shortgrad_first/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript) re-derives the
outcome from the stored inputs: it reads the pilot's row and results in `pilots.toml`, the step-adjoint gate's and
the paired-stencil gate's rows in `registries/gates.toml`, applies the rule, compares the derived outcome and
fallback with the ADR and the `decision` entry, asserts that the pilot hash resolves and that every run of the
pilot has a meter row, runs N40-03's read-only `scripts/e2e/kill_gate_records.sh --live --ids` on KILL-SHORTGRAD
and `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (asserting that no failure names KILL-SHORTGRAD), and,
when the outcome is `fired` or `not-reached`, `e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-724 5 -- frob train start quad-a --task
flat-velocity --algo short-horizon-ac --key ksg-1 --json`. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`, the command, exit code and wall time; `metric` lines for the ratio (threshold 0.35) and the share of
windows below 0.5 (threshold 40%), each with the receipt it came from; the derived and recorded outcomes and
fallbacks; the refusal block; on disagreement the first differing field; `summary` with assertions passed and
failed and `gates: ["KILL-SHORTGRAD"]`. It writes nothing to the registry; the recording session does.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Negative control: a planted pilot record with ratio 0.50 must yield `fired`; a decision entry that says
  `not-fired` beside that pilot (plant `not-fired-with-falsified-pilot`) must make the script fail, and
  `kill_ledger` must report `outcome-contradicts-measurement`.
- Negative control: a planted record with the step-adjoint kill gate fired and a "funded" branch must be rejected.
- The decision is a pure function of three records and is identical on every run.

## Considerations

- Two mechanisms upstream each have their own kill gate (step adjoints; the paired stencil). This decision is the place where their outcomes combine, so that no branch is left without a named fallback.
- The 0.35 threshold demands roughly a three-times saving on the refinement stage alone; given that stage's share of the loop, even a pass moves the end-to-end figure little. The decision record repeats the ceiling so that funding is not mistaken for a speed claim.
