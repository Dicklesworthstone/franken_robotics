## Background

In FrankenRobotics agents are the discrete optimizer over designs, and screening is done by rungs that train
nothing. One stage remains slow under population methods: the joint refinement of a selected design and its
controller. Short-horizon gradient training with design parameters is a Moonshot for that stage: policy
parameters θ and a continuous design vector φ (up to about 20 entries of the per-world parameter row) are
updated together from analytic gradients of a short rollout. The gradient mechanism it consumes is the step
adjoint of the dynamics chapter: at a converged contact step the residual of the convex solve is zero and its
Hessian H is already factored, so by the implicit function theorem the cotangent of the step's inputs is
obtained by solving H·λ = v̄ (two triangular solves) and applying the transposed partial derivative of the
residual; nothing is differentiated through solver iterations.

Why the window is short. At the closed-loop exponent quoted for learned controllers on other systems, 0.16 per
control step, a perturbation grows by e^(0.16·K): 13 at K = 16, 167 at K = 32 and 7.8 × 10⁸ at K = 128. A
gradient through 128 steps is dominated by its variance, and its mean tells the optimizer nothing. The exponent
is a published figure for other systems; the cosine monitor (N23b-39) measures ours.

Standing: Moonshot, off the critical path, not a differentiator (several incumbents differentiate their step,
and a composed incumbent can host this). Funded only after the step-adjoint gate has passed and the pilot
(N23b-36) did not falsify (decision N23b-37). Ceiling: the finalist refinement stage only. Kill gate:
KILL-SHORTGRAD; fallback, PPO with the paired stencil where that passes its own gate, otherwise derivative-free
search over φ.

## Scope

In: the learner: windows, the loss, the reverse sweep, the critic, the parameter updates for θ, ψ and φ, the
storage and memory admission, the verb arguments, the profile label.
Out: the three guards (cosine monitor, step labels, proposals re-scored by the ladder) and the gate (N23b-39);
the step adjoints (BD-422's tasks); the paired stencil (BD-353).

## Technical approach

**Objects.** Policy π_θ (actor), critic V_ψ, design vector φ ∈ box B of the design family (each component a
named lever with bounds; components on a manifold carry their retraction). All worlds of the run share φ and
differ by randomization and scene draws.

**Window loss.** For a window of K control steps, K ∈ {16, 32}, starting from the worlds' current states, with
reparameterized actions aₜ = μ_θ(oₜ) + σ_θ ⊙ zₜ (noise zₜ from the addressed action sampler, held fixed for the
sweep), the loss per world is

    L(θ, φ) = −[ Σ_{t=0}^{K−1} Γₜ·rₜ + Γ_K·V_ψ(o_K) ],

the negated sum of rewards in the window plus the learned value at its end. Γₜ = exp(−β·τₜ) is the physical
discount accumulated over the simulated time τₜ since the window's start (DESIGN: with β = 0 this is the plain
sum the plan writes; using the task's β keeps the objective the same as PPO's). A world that terminates inside
the window contributes rewards up to termination and no tail value; a truncation bootstraps from the final
observation; an invalid world has weight zero.

**Gradient: one reverse sweep through the window.** Forward: run the K control intervals (K × decimation
world-steps), storing the step buffers of every world-step. Reverse, from the window's end to its start: (1)
the critic's input gradient at o_K; (2) for each control interval, backwards: reward terms' transposed
derivatives (straight-line reverse programs derived at build time), the observation and sensor stages, the
policy network's backward pass (accumulating ∂L/∂θ), then for each world-step of the interval the step adjoint
(solve H·λ = v̄ with the stored factor; accumulate state, action and parameter-row cotangents). The result is
∂L/∂θ and ∂L/∂φ per world, summed over valid worlds and divided by the valid count. A step whose solve did not
converge has no derivative: its world is dropped from the batch gradient and counted.

**Critic.** V_ψ is fitted to temporal-difference targets as in on-policy actor-critic training: for each window
the target at its start is the K-step return Σ Γₜ·rₜ + Γ_K·V_ψ⁻(o_K) with a stop-gradient on the bootstrap
(and intermediate n-step targets along the window), under the same two-mask rule as PPO (the value bootstrap is
cut by termination only; the trace by any reset). The value loss differentiates the current output.

**Updates.** θ and ψ by the optimizer kernel (Adam) with global gradient-norm clipping (the gradient can be
large at K = 32: a growth factor of 167 on perturbations). φ by a projected step: φ ← Proj_B(Retr_φ(−η_φ·ĝ_φ))
with ĝ_φ the design gradient after the guards of N23b-39 have filtered it; components on a manifold move along
their retraction; under a symmetry of the closed loop the design gradient is averaged over the
observation-preserving subgroup so that a symmetric design stays symmetric. A change of φ rewrites the parameter
row of every world between windows; it needs no recompilation inside a shape class.

**Memory admission.** Step buffers are stored for every world-step of the window. FACT (arithmetic on the
per-world byte model): at 27 kB per quadruped world, K = 16 and 10 world-steps per interval the store is
160 × 27 kB = 4.32 MB per world and 8.85 GB for 2,048 worlds; at K = 32 it is 8.64 MB per world, 8.85 GB for
1,024 worlds. The cost model (BD-219) prints the figure, the start envelope reports it as `window_store_bytes`, and a job
that does not fit is refused before allocation with `FROB-REF-602` (the interim code N16-REF assigns to a
step-buffer store above the device budget; exit 6; repairs: fewer worlds, a shorter window, the paired
stencil).

**Profile and evidence.** Training with analytic gradients runs under the labelled D4 training profile; its
only output is weights and a design proposal tagged `trained_under: D4`. No number observed during training is
evidence. Every score that enters the ladder is an evaluation under a D2 profile. Paired checks inside the run
(the cosine monitor) pair worlds and therefore run their own forward passes under a D2 physics step, which every
profile keeps.

**Verb.** `frob train start <design> --task <task> --algo short-horizon-ac --design-params <list> [--window
16|32] [--design-lr <η_φ>] [--backend <tier>] [--worlds <n>] [--seed <s>] --key <idempotency key> --json`:
`<list>` names levers of the design; the envelope reports the window in force, `window_store_bytes`, the counts
of valid, dropped and labelled steps, the design proposals so far and the label `gradient: analytic` (or
`gradient: stencil` after the monitor's switch); `frob train status <run>` reports the same per window. Policy
manifests record `algo` and the design lineage. The VERBS-train row lists the value `short-horizon-ac` of
`--algo` and, from M6, `--design-params <levers>`, `--window 16|32` and `--design-lr <rate>` with it, under
"Extended by: N23b-38"; this bead builds them. Until N23b-37 records `not-fired`, the dispatch answers
`--algo short-horizon-ac` with `FROB-REF-724` (reason `not-linked`, exit 5).

**Layout.** `crates/frob-learn/src/shortgrad/` (window, sweep driver, critic targets, design update); reverse
kernels for rewards and observations in the kernel library, derived with instance certificates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Not started unless N23b-37 recorded the outcome `not-fired` at KILL-SHORTGRAD's first reading (`docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SHORTGRAD-M6.md`) and the step-adjoint gate is recorded as passed.
- [ ] On a smooth, contact-free fixture the analytic gradients ∂L/∂θ and ∂L/∂φ agree with central finite differences of L within 10⁻³ relative in `f32` on `batch-cpu` and on a device (the sweep is correct where everything is smooth).
- [ ] The scalar implicit-function known answer is reproduced through the learner's sweep: Φ(v; z) = ½·m·(v − z)² + ½·g_c·v² gives dv⁺/dz = m/(m + g_c) = 1/4 at m = 2, g_c = 6, and 1 with the contact open.
- [ ] A world with a non-converged step is dropped from the batch gradient and counted; an invalid world has weight zero.
- [ ] φ stays inside its box after every update; a manifold component stays on its manifold; a symmetric design stays symmetric under a symmetric closed loop.
- [ ] A window that does not fit in memory is refused before allocation with `FROB-REF-602` (exit 6) and the estimate printed; the 4.32 MB per world figure is reproduced by the cost model.
- [ ] The options `--design-params`, `--window`, `--design-lr` and the value `short-horizon-ac` appear in the VERBS-train row (extended by this bead), and the cluster's schema snapshot is regenerated.
- [ ] The run is labelled D4; its training returns are refused as evidence (FROB-REF-861, a number observed during the run); the policy itself, offered for scoring or citation before a re-evaluation under D2 or D3, is refused with FROB-REF-867 (the division of N23a-18); its policy and design proposals carry `trained_under: D4`.
- [ ] Cancellation at a window boundary leaves a consistent checkpoint; the verb is reachable through `frob --json`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-learn/tests/shortgrad.rs` (`cargo test -p frob-learn --test shortgrad`):
- `window_loss_definition`: rewards (1, 2, 3), tail value 10, Γ ≡ 1 → L = −16; with β such that Γ = (1, 0.99, 0.9801) and Γ₃ = 0.970299 → L = −(1 + 1.98 + 2.9403 + 9.70299) = −15.62329.
- `termination_cuts_tail`: termination at step 2 of 3 → no tail value and no reward after it.
- `sweep_matches_finite_differences_smooth`: a suspended robot tracking joint targets, K = 16: analytic against central differences, relative error ≤ 10⁻³, for θ (sampled directions) and all components of φ.
- `scalar_implicit_function`: 1/4 and 1; at the boundary the step's label is `one-sided`.
- `nonconverged_dropped`: a planted non-converged step removes its world from the gradient and increments the counter.
- `critic_two_masks`: the value target bootstraps through a truncation and not through a termination.
- `design_projection`: a step that would leave the box lands on the bound; an inertia parameter stays on its cone.
- `symmetry_average`: with a mirror-symmetric closed loop the design gradient's antisymmetric part is zero after averaging.
- `memory_estimate`: K = 16, decimation 10, 27 kB per world → 4.32 MB per world; 2,048 worlds → 8.85 GB.
- `growth_factors`: e^(0.16·16) = 12.9; e^(0.16·32) = 167.3.
- `window_store_refused_602`: K = 32, decimation 10, 27 kB per world and a device budget of 16 GiB: 4,096 worlds
  (8.64 MB each, 35.4 GB) are refused with `FROB-REF-602` before any allocation, the payload naming the largest
  world count that fits (1,988); 1,024 worlds (8.85 GB) are admitted.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: the scalar implicit-function case (1/4 and 1); the growth factors; the memory arithmetic.
- A zero gradient is not a stationary point: a 1 kg block on a floor with μ = 0.5 pushed with 4 N for 0.01 s has dv/du = 0 although more force would move it (breakaway 4.905 N; at 6 N, v = 0.01095 m/s and dv/du = 0.01 s/kg). A design update must not treat a `fixed-mode` zero as "no improvement possible"; that is the guards' job, and this learner must pass the step's label through unchanged.
- Negative controls: a planted sweep that omits the step adjoint's parameter-row term (∂L/∂φ wrong on the smooth fixture); a planted learner that keeps non-converged worlds in the mean; a planted profile label of D2 on the training run.
- Determinism: training is D4 by label; the smooth-fixture gradient tests run under D2 and are identical twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- The reverse sweep's cost is hypothesized at about one forward sweep; the step-adjoint gate allows up to twice. If it is much more, the 0.35 threshold of the gate is out of reach and the kill gate decides.
- Storing step buffers bounds the product of worlds and window; at K = 32 the world count halves. The monitor may halve K, which doubles the admissible worlds for the next window.
- The physical discount inside the window is this bead's choice for consistency with the PPO objective; with β = 0 it is exactly the plan's sum.
- A gradient learner can exploit the differentiated model's form; every design it proposes is re-scored by the ordinary ladder and nothing is promoted on a gradient (N23b-39).
