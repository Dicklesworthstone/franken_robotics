## Background

Short-horizon gradient training (N23b-38) updates a policy and a continuous design vector φ from analytic
gradients of a window of K control steps. Analytic gradients through contact are fragile in three known ways:
their variance explodes with the horizon; at a change of contact mode the derivative is one-sided or holds only
for a frozen active set; and a gradient method can find designs that exploit the differentiated model. The plan
makes the method usable where contact changes with three guards, and judges it by a gate:

1. *A cosine monitor.* On a fixed share of windows (DESIGN default 1 in 16) the analytic design gradient is
   compared with the paired central difference on the same world addresses. The running median cosine is logged.
   Below 0.5 the window halves; at K = 8 and still below 0.5 the design update switches to the paired stencil
   and the run is labelled `gradient: stencil`.
2. *Step labels.* A window that contains a step the adjoint labels anything other than `smooth` contributes to
   the policy update and not to the design update, because its design gradient is one-sided or fixed-mode.
3. *Design updates are proposals.* φ moves inside the family's box. Every design the run reports is a typed
   edit sequence, recompiled and scored by the ordinary ladder. No verdict cites a gradient.

GATE-CORTEX-22 (design comparison, M6, TARGET): the pilot's comparison on both tasks: matched referee-promoted
return in at most 0.35 of the derivative-free arm's GPU-hours; cosine at least 0.5 on at least 60% of monitored
windows; every reported design re-scored by the ladder, none promoted on a gradient. Kill gate KILL-SHORTGRAD:
the pilot's falsifier fires, or the gate fails after one repair cycle; fallback, PPO with the paired stencil
where that passes its own gate, otherwise derivative-free search over φ.

Standing: Moonshot; off the critical path.

## Scope

In: the three guards; the gate experiment on our engine (three arms, two tasks); the gate record; the final
reading of the kill gate.
Out: the learner (N23b-38); the step adjoints and their labels (the dynamics chapter); the paired stencil

## Technical approach

**Guard 1: the cosine monitor.**

- Selection: windows are chosen by a keyed stream at rate 1 in 16 before the window runs.
- Measurement: for a monitored window, the paired central difference of the same window loss is computed on the
  same world addresses: for each seed, worlds at φ and at φ ⊕ δ·eᵢ and φ ⊖ δ·eᵢ for each of the P design
  parameters (2P + 1 worlds per seed; 41 for P = 20), every world of a seed sharing every random draw, the
  policy and its action noise held fixed. With g_a the analytic design gradient and g_fd the stencil's,
  cos = (g_a · g_fd)/(‖g_a‖·‖g_fd‖).
- Decision: the running median of the cosine over the last M monitored windows (DESIGN default M = 16). Median
  below 0.5 → the window K halves (32 → 16 → 8) and the median restarts. At K = 8 and a median still below 0.5
  → the design update switches to the paired stencil for the rest of the run, and the run is labelled
  `gradient: stencil`. The policy update keeps its analytic gradient throughout.
- Record: every monitored window's cosine, K, and the label in force; the share of monitored windows with
  cosine at least 0.5 (the gate's 60%).

**Guard 2: step labels.** Each world-step's adjoint carries a label: `smooth` (every contact and joint row is
strictly inside one region of its law, with normalized margin above a guard), `fixed-mode` (derivative taken
with active sets frozen), `one-sided` (a row sits on a boundary). The label of a world's window is the weakest
label on its path. Per world: if the window label is `smooth`, the world contributes to ∂L/∂θ and to ∂L/∂φ;
otherwise it contributes to ∂L/∂θ only. The design gradient is the mean over contributing worlds; if fewer than
a quarter of valid worlds contribute (DESIGN floor), the design update is skipped for that window and counted.

**Guard 3: designs are proposals.** The run's φ is never a result. When the run reports a design (at the end,
or at registered checkpoints) it emits a typed edit sequence from the starting design (one `set-parameter` edit
per changed lever, inside bounds); the edited design is recompiled as a new design identity and scored by the
ordinary ladder: evaluation under a D2 profile, promotion on the referee, the exploitation detectors and the
model-form check where it is a finalist. The policy trained alongside is a D4-trained policy and is re-evaluated
before it is cited. A report produced by this run carries no claim and no verdict; any attempt to promote on the
run's own numbers is refused as a D4 result offered as evidence.

**The gate experiment (the pilot's comparison, on our engine).** One quadruped, 20 design parameters, three
seeds, two tasks (rough ground; stairs). Arms at equal GPU-hours: (a) this method; (b) PPO with a
derivative-free outer loop on φ; (c) PPO with the paired stencil. For each arm and seed: the final design is
emitted as edits, recompiled, and its policy re-evaluated and promoted on the referee. The matched return is
the promoted return arm (b) reaches at its budget; the measure is arm (a)'s GPU-hours to a promoted return at
least that level, as a share of arm (b)'s. Pass: at most 0.35 on both tasks; cosine at least 0.5 on at least
60% of monitored windows; every reported design re-scored by the ladder; none promoted on a gradient. The
record follows the method-gate form (competing arms, a potency control, an A/A arm, the ceiling, the fallback)
and carries the behaviour controls (zero-weight and random policies fail).

**Final reading of KILL-SHORTGRAD.** If the gate fails, one repair cycle is allowed (recorded: what was changed
and why). If it fails again the kill gate fires: the decision record of N23b-37 is completed, the capability is
removed from the verb's algorithm list and the fallback named there is in force.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The monitor runs on 1 window in 16 chosen before the window runs; its record holds every cosine with K; the halving and the switch to the stencil happen exactly at a running median below 0.5, and at K = 8 respectively.
- [ ] A window containing a non-`smooth` step contributes nothing to the design gradient and still contributes to the policy gradient.
- [ ] Every design the run reports is a typed edit sequence that recompiles to a design inside the family's box and is scored by the ladder under a D2 profile; no report of the run carries a verdict.
- [ ] GATE-CORTEX-22 is recorded with measured values: per task, arm (a)'s share of arm (b)'s GPU-hours to the matched referee-promoted return against 0.35; the share of monitored windows with cosine at least 0.5 against 60%; the count of reported designs re-scored (all) and promoted on a gradient (zero).
- [ ] The gate record contains its controls (potency, A/A, zero-weight and random policies) and was recorded by a session that did not implement the learner.
- [ ] KILL-SHORTGRAD's final reading is appended to the decision record; on firing, `train.start --algo short-horizon-ac` answers the capability refusal.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-learn shortgrad::guards`:
- `cosine_definition`: g_a = (1, 0), g_fd = (1, 1) → 0.7071; opposite vectors → −1.
- `monitor_rate`: over 16,000 windows the keyed stream selects 1,000 ± 4 standard deviations (about ± 122); selection does not depend on any gradient value.
- `halving_schedule`: medians (0.7, 0.4) at K = 32 → K = 16; (0.45) → K = 8; (0.3) at K = 8 → `gradient: stencil`.
- `stencil_world_count`: P = 20 → 41 worlds per seed; 64 seeds → 2,624 worlds.
- `label_gating`: a batch with 100 worlds of which 30 have a `one-sided` step: design gradient from 70 worlds, policy gradient from 100.
- `design_update_skipped_below_floor`: 20 of 100 contributing → skipped and counted.
- `proposal_is_edit_sequence`: a φ change of two levers emits two `set-parameter` edits whose application reproduces φ exactly; an out-of-box value cannot be emitted.
- `no_verdict_from_run`: the run's report has an empty claims list; promotion on its numbers is refused.
- `gate_arithmetic`: arm (b) reaches the matched return at 10 GPU-hours and arm (a) at 3.2 → share 0.32, pass; 4.0 → 0.40, fail.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/shortgrad_gate.sh` sources the helper of `STD-E2E` and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/shortgrad_gate/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Per task and seed: arm (a) `frob train start
--algo short-horizon-ac --design-params <20 levers> --json`; arm (b) `frob opt start --family <box> --method
derivative-free` with PPO inside; arm (c) PPO with `frob phylum gradient` for the design step; then for each
arm's reported design: `frob design edit` (the emitted sequence), `frob eval run` under a D2 profile, `frob eval
promote --tier referee`, `frob eval detect`. `cmd` events carry the exact command, exit code, wall time and
evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` events carry GPU-hours per arm to
the matched promoted return (share against 0.35), the monitor's cosine share against 60%, and K over time;
`assert` events carry "every reported design re-scored" and "none promoted on a gradient", with the first
violating design on failure. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-CORTEX-22",
"KILL-SHORTGRAD"]`. A `--smoke` mode runs one task, one seed and a tiny budget on `batch-cpu` and claims no gate.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: growth factors 13, 167 and 7.8 × 10⁸ at K = 16, 32, 128 (the reason for the monitor); the block case in which a zero `fixed-mode` gradient is not a stationary point (1 kg block, μ = 0.5, 4 N for 0.01 s: no motion and dv/du = 0; breakaway at 4.905 N), which the label gate must keep out of the design update.
- Negative controls the gate must catch: a run with the window forced to K = 128 and the monitor off (the late counterexample on long differentiable rollouts: its cosine share falls below 60% once the monitor is turned back on to measure it); a planted learner that feeds `one-sided` windows into the design update; a planted report that promotes the run's own φ without the ladder.
- Potency and A/A controls of the comparison: on a smooth contact-free task arm (a) must beat arm (b) clearly; arm (b) against itself must not differ by the gate's factor.
- Determinism lanes: guard decisions are functions of recorded cosines and labels; the monitor's paired stencil runs under a D2 physics step and is identical twice on one machine; all gate scores are D2 evaluations.

## Considerations

- The monitor costs 2P + 1 worlds per seed on one window in sixteen; at P = 20 and 64 seeds that is 2,624 world-windows per monitored window, a visible overhead that is charged to arm (a) in the gate.
- If most windows on stairs contain a mode change, the design gradient is rarely admitted and the method degenerates to the stencil; that is an honest outcome, labelled `gradient: stencil`, and it likely fails the 0.35 clause.
- Promotion on the referee is what "matched return" means; an arm that wins only on the tier it trained on has not won.
- If the paired stencil's own kill gate fired, arm (c) and the monitor's reference do not exist; the mechanism is then closed by N23b-37.
