## Background

The family policy of FrankenRobotics (BD-320) is a design-conditioned policy for one morphology class. Its
network is a *plain transformer over link tokens*: a shared per-token encoder, a transformer over the tokens
with a tree-distance bias, masked pooling and a per-token decoder, with 1 to 5 million parameters; padding
tokens are masked. "Plain" is a decision: fixed message passing along the tree has lost to plain transformers
in published comparisons, and geometric-algebra layers are slower per step with no demonstrated gain (they are
a Moonshot with their own pilot). This network is also arm A, the control, of that Moonshot's comparison.

A section of the policy bundle must be natural under re-description: for every isomorphism of designs (a
relabelling of links, a change of frame or axis-sign convention, a similarity scaling) the policy of the
re-described design is the re-description of the policy. Canonical tokens (N23b-40) give this at the input; the
network must not break it (no dependence on token position), and training also augments over equivalent
descriptions.

Tier: Frontier; depends on the family-policy pilot.

## Scope

In: the network definition; its permutation equivariance; the actor and critic heads; the CPU path on
FrankenTorch and the on-device path with gradient parity; convention augmentation in training; the
re-description test.
Out: tokens (N23b-40); pretraining, membership and the verb (BD-320); equivariant layers under finite groups
(BD-325, BD-326); multivector layers (BD-439).

## Technical approach

**Architecture (DESIGN).**

1. *Encoder.* A shared MLP maps each token's concatenated static and dynamic features to a vector of width d
   (default d = 192); joint tokens and contact tokens have separate input projections and share everything
   after. Depth and orbit indices enter through learned embeddings added to the vector.
2. *Transformer.* N layers (default 4) of pre-norm multi-head self-attention (default 4 heads) and a feed-forward
   block of width 4·d. The attention logit between tokens i and j is the scaled dot product plus a learned bias
   b[h][D(i, j)], where D is the tree distance between the tokens' links (number of joints on the path, clipped
   at a maximum, with separate buckets for "ancestor", "descendant" and "other branch"), per head h. There is no
   positional encoding: a token's position in the sequence carries no information.
3. *Masks.* Padded tokens are excluded as keys and as queries; their outputs are zeroed and never pooled.
4. *Actor head.* A shared per-token decoder MLP maps each joint token's output to the latent action mean for
   that joint (one value per actuated degree of freedom); contact tokens emit nothing. The log standard
   deviation is a learned per-role parameter (shared across joints of one role pattern), not per position.
   Outputs are mapped to the design's channels by the mapping table (by role).
5. *Critic head.* Masked mean pooling over valid tokens, then an MLP to a scalar value. The critic may read
   `critic-only` token features through a separate encoder input; the actor may not (asymmetric actor-critic).
6. *Size.* With d = 192, 4 layers and 4·d feed-forward: about 4 × (4·d² + 8·d²) = 48·d² ≈ 1.8 million
   parameters, inside the 1 to 5 million range.

**Permutation equivariance.** For any permutation P of tokens (with the tree relation permuted accordingly),
the per-token outputs permute by P and the pooled value is unchanged. This holds by construction (shared
weights, no positional encoding, a bias that depends only on tree distance) and is tested.

**Convention augmentation.** During pretraining each design is presented under random equivalent descriptions:
random joint order and link labels (exercising equivariance numerically), random axis-sign and frame
conventions before canonicalization (exercising the canonical form), and mirrored copies under Γ_obs with the
corresponding action transform (the Solid symmetry mechanism). With canonical tokens most of this is a no-op at
the input; it remains in training as a guard against a canonicalization defect and as the stated remedy from
the published evidence.

**Execution.** CPU: FrankenTorch modules (linear, attention, layer normalization) with autograd, used as the
trainer for small runs and as the gradient oracle. Device: kernels in the shared dialect: batched matrix
products, masked softmax over at most the token capacity, layer normalization, and their backward passes, in
single precision; the optimizer kernel is the existing one. Gradient parity target (the same form as the
backward-parity gate of the MLP): on-device gradients match the oracle within 10⁻⁵ relative on 10³ random
batches of random token sets with random masks.

**Checkpoint.** The manifest's `network` records `{kind: "morphology-transformer", d, layers, heads,
tree_distance_buckets}`; weights travel as safetensors.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The network exists on the CPU and on each device backend; parameter count for the default configuration is within 1 to 5 million and is reported.
- [ ] Permutation equivariance holds to round-off: permuted tokens give permuted actions and an unchanged value.
- [ ] Padded tokens affect nothing: changing a padded token's features or the padding amount changes no valid output bit on the CPU and stays inside the D2 batch-composition rule on devices.
- [ ] The re-description test passes on every design of the test corpus: a random relabelling, frame change and power-of-two scaling leaves the actions unchanged after mapping back, to round-off.
- [ ] On-device gradients match the FrankenTorch oracle within 10⁻⁵ relative on 10³ random batches.
- [ ] The actor head reads no `critic-only` feature (inference with those inputs physically absent gives identical actions).
- [ ] A training smoke on a tiny generator reduces loss and beats the zero-weight and random controls on the training designs.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-learn family::network`:
- `param_count_default`: d = 192, 4 layers → between 1.0 and 5.0 million (about 1.8 million), printed.
- `permutation_equivariance`: 10³ random token sets and permutations; outputs permute, value equal, to 10⁻⁶ relative in `f32` and exactly in structure.
- `no_positional_dependence`: inserting a padded token at a random position changes no valid output.
- `tree_bias_buckets`: tokens at tree distance 1 (parent and child), 2 and "other branch" index distinct bias entries; relabelling keeps the indices.
- `redescription_actions`: for each corpus design, actions before and after re-description agree after mapping back (C-23's test); the planted file-order network fails at the first exchange of two legs.
- `asymmetric_actor_critic`: actor outputs identical with critic-only inputs absent.
- `gradient_parity_oracle`: 10³ random batches, relative difference ≤ 10⁻⁵.
- `logstd_by_role`: the standard deviation of `leg[0].hip` and `leg[3].hip` is one shared parameter.

### End-to-end script and logging

Exercised end to end by `scripts/e2e/train_family.sh` (BD-320). Its own script
`scripts/e2e/family_network_parity.sh` (helper of `STD-E2E`; newline-delimited JSON at
`target/e2e-logs/family_network_parity/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript) runs the gradient-parity and
re-description checks per tier through the conformance test target and a short `frob train family` smoke on
`batch-cpu` and one device; `cmd` events carry the exact command, exit code, wall time and evidence block;
`metric` events carry the largest relative gradient difference against 10⁻⁵ and the largest re-description
action difference; `assert` events carry pass or fail with the first failing batch or design. `summary`: counts
and `gates: []`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The construction's tests: re-description leaves actions unchanged to round-off; a policy that reads tokens in file order fails at the first exchange of two legs.
- Negative controls: a planted positional encoding (must fail `permutation_equivariance` and `redescription_actions`); a planted mask bug that pools padded tokens (must fail `no_positional_dependence`; this is the invariant "a padded element never contributes to a mean"); a planted backward kernel with a wrong softmax derivative (must fail `gradient_parity_oracle`).
- Behaviour controls for the smoke: zero-weight and random policies fail the threshold.
- Determinism lanes: CPU forward and backward are identical twice and across worker counts; device kernels are class D2 per backend with stable reductions; CPU against device inside the recorded envelope.

## Considerations

- Attention over at most a few dozen tokens is small; the kernels are bandwidth-bound like the rest of the learner, and reduced-precision paths are not adopted (the arithmetic is too small to repay the risk).
- If the equivariance kill gate fires, mirrored augmentation under Γ_obs remains (it is the Solid mechanism) and nothing here changes.
- The default sizes are DESIGN starting points; the family-policy experiment may change them, and each change is a new network identity in the manifest.
