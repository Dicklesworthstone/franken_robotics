## Background

Construction C-24 (Moonshot) replaces the plain morphology transformer of a family policy by a network
whose link tokens carry multivector features and whose layers are equivariant under relabelling and under
the rigid motions that preserve gravity. Its gate and kill gate are in the register for milestone M8:

- **GATE-CORTEX-17** (claim: Design comparison; TARGET): "arm B or C is at least 1.3 times faster than arm
  A to the return threshold at equal wall-clock, or better with no adaptation beyond seed spread".
- **KILL-GATREE**: trigger "GATE-CORTEX-17 fails"; fallback "the plain morphology transformer remains;
  `fs-ga` features are not used in policies".

The arms: A, the control, is the plain link-token transformer with heading normalization and convention
augmentation; B is A with a geometric-algebra encoder and decoder only; C is full geometric-algebra message
passing on the kinematic tree. The same embodiment set and the same wall-clock budget (not equal
parameters), three seeds per arm. Measured: time to a return threshold, return with no adaptation on
held-out designs, and retention under re-description. The ceiling, stated before any run, is 0 to 1.3 times
on an amortized pretraining run, so the mechanism is not a source of the ten-fold result and is off every
critical path.

The gate is read twice. The first reading is the pre-funding pilot on existing tools outside the workspace
(N23b-01), taken by the first-reading decision (N23b-02): `falsified` closes the epic on the fallback;
`undetermined` funds nothing. Only on `not-falsified`, with a compute envelope approved, is the mechanism
built in the workspace (N23bx-01) and the gate measured **on our engine**. That final measurement and
reading are named by the epic and by the work package but carried by no task; an epic cannot be depended
on, so the M8 exit review had nothing to cite. This bead is the measurement, the gate record and the final
reading.

DESIGN: the final reading uses the pilot's protocol and reading rule unchanged, so that the two readings
differ only in the stack and the scale. Rejected: equal parameters or equal environment steps as the budget
(geometric-algebra layers ran 1.4 to 7.5 times slower per epoch in published work, so either would
flatter them); scoring time to threshold by first crossing (a first crossing of a noisy curve is
descriptive only; attainment needs error control over every look).

## Scope

In: the frozen protocol; the nine training runs and their evaluations on our engine; the potency, A/A,
zero-weight and random-policy controls; the reading rule; the gate receipt and register row; the final
reading of KILL-GATREE with both branches; the record the exit review cites.

Out: the arms' implementation and layer laws (N23bx-01, SPEC-C-24); the plain transformer, its pretraining
verb and the family-policy experiment (N23b-41, BD-320, BD-321); the attainment procedure (N23b-33); the
pilot and the first reading (N23b-01, N23b-02); device kernels for a winning arm (a later task of the epic).

## Technical approach

**1. Preconditions, checked before compute is spent.** The first reading is `not-falsified`; a class E3
envelope (pretraining of one family policy, approved per run with its ceiling and kill rule written first)
is approved for nine runs; the layer laws and the gravity control pass on the production arms; arm A's
digest equals the plain transformer's. A failed precondition is recorded `not run` with its reason: the
KILL-GATREE row's `decision` array gains `{ outcome = "not-reached", adr = "docs/adr/ADR-KILL-GATREE-M8.md", date,
session, exit = "M8" }` (N40-03: the mechanism was not funded), and the ADR's second section states the failed
precondition as the reason. A `not-falsified` first reading appends no entry (N23b-02), so without this one the M8
due list would show KILL-GATREE missing. GATE-CORTEX-17 stays `not-run`. (When the first reading was `falsified`
or `undetermined`, N23b-02 has appended its entry and this bead appends none.)

**2. Protocol (committed to the gate's protocol file before the first run).**
1. *Embodiment set.* The registered training generator and held-out split of the family-policy experiment
   for one morphology class (quadruped-like legged), the same for every arm.
2. *Arms and seeds.* A, B, C as above; seeds 2416, 2417, 2418 (the pilot's), printed in the log. The plan
   may raise the seed count, never lower it.
3. *Budget.* Equal elapsed training time per arm and seed on the same device, fixed in the envelope; a run
   whose elapsed training time differs from the budget by more than 1% is rejected from the comparison.
   Steps per second and parameter count per arm are recorded in the disclosure block.
4. *Measures.* (a) Time to the registered return threshold, with threshold and evaluation cadence fixed in
   advance and attainment judged with error control over all looks (level αₖ = α/[k·(k + 1)] at look k);
   (b) return with no adaptation on the held-out designs; (c) retention under re-description: the ratio of
   return on equivalently re-described robots (random relabelling of links, change of frame and axis-sign
   convention, power-of-two scaling) to return on the originals. Evaluation worlds are the same for every
   arm, paired by world address; evaluation is under a class D2 profile.
5. *Commands* (run verbs take `--key`; every run below has its own key, `gt-<arm>-<seed>` and suffixes):
   `frob train family --class quadruped --generator <id> --split <id> --task-family <id> --arch
   plain|ga-encoder|ga-tree --seed <s> --approval <token> --key gt-<arm>-<seed> --json` (`--arch` and `--seed`
   from N23bx-01); `frob train status <job> --json` for attainment and the step count at each look; per final
   policy and held-out or re-described design, `frob policy apply <policy> --design <d> --task <id> --key
   gt-<arm>-<seed>-pa-<d> --json` to bind it with no adaptation, then `frob eval run <d> --policy <bound> --task
   <id> --split <held-out split> --seeds <n> --key gt-<arm>-<seed>-ev-<d> --json`, the form BD-321 uses.

**3. Reading rule.** Let ρ_X be the paired ratio time(A)/time(X) for X ∈ {B, C}, with
ℓᵢ = ln(time_A,i / time_X,i) over the seed pairs, mean ℓ̄ and sample standard deviation s. Its one-sided 95%
bounds are exp(ℓ̄ ∓ t·s/√n) with t the 95th percentile of Student's distribution with n − 1 degrees of
freedom (t = 2.920 for n = 3). An arm X is *better with no adaptation beyond seed spread* when the worst of
its seeds has a zero-shot return above the best seed of arm A.

| Result | Condition | GATE-CORTEX-17 | KILL-GATREE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pass | For some X the lower bound of ρ_X is at least 1.3, or X is better with no adaptation beyond seed spread; all controls behave | `pass`, with measured values | Does not fire |
| Fail | For both B and C the upper bound of ρ_X is below 1.3 and neither is better beyond seed spread | `fail` | Fires |
| Undetermined | Anything else: an interval that straddles 1.3; a control failed; a run rejected for budget | `fail`, with the detail `undetermined:` and the reason (the register's statuses, N40-01, have no undetermined pass) | Fires at the M8 decision unless a repeat with more seeds under a new frozen protocol is approved within the envelope; a gate that has not passed does not keep a Moonshot alive |

A measured speed-up between 1.0 and 1.3 is a failure by design: the threshold is the ceiling itself.

**4. Record.** The gate receipt `target/gates/GATE-CORTEX-17.json` and the row in `registries/gates.toml`
hold, for all three arms: times to threshold with their attainment looks, the environment-step count of each
run at its attainment look (`not-attained` with the steps at the budget's end for a run that does not attain;
BET-30's rider counts steps, at 1/1.3 of the plain transformer's, and N00p1-11 reads these counts for it), ρ_B
and ρ_C with both bounds,
zero-shot returns per seed with the seed spread, retention under re-description, steps per second and
parameter counts, the controls' outcomes, commit, hardware fingerprint, seeds and the envelope identifier.
Measured values, never targets. Results are labelled Moonshot.

**5. Final reading of KILL-GATREE (written by a session that did not run the comparison).** It is the second
section, "Final reading (our engine)", appended to the kill-gate ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-GATREE-M8.md` that the
first reading opened (N23b-02); the first section is never edited and its digest in the header must still
match. The section carries N40-03's fields (the trigger as registered and its amendments, each measured value
with its receipt hash beside 1.3, the outcome, the fallback in force and the capability's name, the beads
re-read, the recording session), and the KILL-GATREE row's `decision` array gains this reading's entry
(`fired` or `not-fired`, the ADR path, date, session, `receipts` with the gate receipt, `exit = "M8"`). If it fires:
the `fired` entry is appended and GATE-CORTEX-17 takes `killed` with `detail = "KILL-GATREE: <fallback>"` by N40-03's
propagation (`apply_decisions`); the feature
`moonshot-ga-policy` and its code are removed; no code path feeds `fs-ga` features to a policy; the family
policy's network remains the only tree policy; the policy manifest never carries a continuous-group tying
entry from this mechanism; the epic closes described by its fallback, "the plain morphology transformer";
the fallback ledger of kill gates is updated. If it does not fire: the winning arm stays behind its
feature, a task for its device kernels is created under the epic, and its policies remain Moonshot-labelled
and are evaluated like any policy. On neither branch does anything on a critical path change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The protocol file is committed before the first run and equals the pilot's protocol except for the stack and scale (checked by a diff recorded in the log).
- [ ] Nine runs (three arms × three seeds) completed inside 1% of the wall-clock budget, or the comparison is recorded `not run` or `undetermined` with the rejected runs named.
- [ ] GATE-CORTEX-17 is recorded with measured values for all three arms: times to threshold with attainment looks, environment steps at each run's attainment look (or `not-attained`), zero-shot returns with seed spread, retention under re-description; the zero-weight and random-policy controls are in the record and miss the threshold in every arm.
- [ ] Potency control: an arm A′ identical to A with a planted 1.3 times throughput handicap is detected as slower by the paired lower bound. A/A control: two labellings of arm A do not clear 1.02.
- [ ] Negative control for the kill branch: a planted arm C whose message passing is the identity shows no gain over A and reads as a failure.
- [ ] KILL-GATREE is decided and recorded with the gate's measured values by a session that did not run the comparison, as the second section of `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-GATREE-M8.md` (the first section's digest unchanged) with its entry appended to the row's `decision` array; on the fired branch no build feeds `fs-ga` features to a policy (checked by a dependency and source search) and the capability is named by its fallback.
- [ ] A failed precondition after a `not-falsified` first reading runs nothing, appends the `not-reached` entry at M8 with its reason in the ADR's second section, and leaves GATE-CORTEX-17 `not-run`; the M8 due list shows KILL-GATREE decided.
- [ ] The M8 exit review can cite the receipt and the decision record by hash.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-conformance gate_cortex_17` (the reading rule and the record; real arithmetic on `synthetic` timing tables):
- `paired_ratio_bounds_three_seeds`: times A = (10.0, 10.0, 10.0) h and X = (7.0, 7.1, 6.9) h give ℓ̄ = 0.35674, s = 0.01429, half-width 2.920·0.01429/√3 = 0.02409, bounds exp(0.35674 ∓ 0.02409) = (1.395, 1.463), the upper being exp(0.38083) = 1.46350: pass.
- `between_one_and_ceiling_fails`: X = (8.5, 8.6, 8.4) h gives ℓ̄ = 0.1626, s = 0.0118 and an upper bound of 1.200, below 1.3: fail.
- `straddling_interval_is_undetermined`: X = (7.0, 9.5, 8.0) h.
- `beyond_seed_spread_rule`: A zero-shot returns (0.60, 0.64, 0.62) and X (0.66, 0.70, 0.65) pass (0.65 > 0.64); X (0.66, 0.70, 0.63) do not.
- `budget_rejection`: a run 1.5% over budget is rejected and the comparison becomes `undetermined`.
- `attainment_not_first_crossing`: a curve that crosses the threshold once and falls back is not attained at that look.
- `record_schema_complete`: a receipt missing any arm's field fails validation, including a run without its
  step count at the attainment look (or `not-attained`).
- `steps_at_attainment_look_recorded`: a synthetic run attaining at look 4 with 2.4 × 10⁸ steps records
  2.4 × 10⁸, not the step count of its first crossing at look 2.
- `precondition_failed_not_reached`: a `not-falsified` first reading with no approved envelope yields the M8
  entry `not-reached` and no run; with a `falsified` first reading it appends nothing (N23b-02's entry stands).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/ga_tree_policy.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; seed-explicit; idempotent by idempotency keys; `E2E_QUICK=1` runs the smoke budget and writes no gate record):
1. Preconditions: the first-reading record, the envelope, `cargo test … laws_c24`, `arm_a_is_plain`.
2. The nine `frob train family` runs, then `frob train status` per run for attainment.
3. `frob eval run` on the held-out and re-described sets for each final policy.
4. The controls: A′ with the planted handicap; A/A; identity-message arm C; zero-weight and random policies.
5. The reading rule; the receipt; the register row, recorded from that receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance
   --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-CORTEX-17 --receipt <hash>` (the one recorder, KNOWN_DRIFTS 9).
Logged per STD-E2E: every command with exit code and wall time, the evidence block (claim Design
comparison, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed, the Moonshot label), times to threshold and returns
against their thresholds, environment steps at each attainment look, both bounds of each ratio against 1.3, steps per second and parameter counts,
digests, and a final summary naming GATE-CORTEX-17 and KILL-GATREE with the reading.

### Known answers and negative controls
- The arithmetic of the unit cases above (bounds 1.395 to 1.463; upper bound 1.200; the straddling case has bounds 0.953 and 1.598).
- Behaviour-not-plumbing controls: zero-weight and random policies fail the return threshold in every arm's record.
- Re-description known answer: a policy that reads tokens in file order fails at the first exchange of two legs; arm A with convention augmentation must not.
- Planted failures the reading must catch: the handicapped A′; the identity-message arm C; a threshold chosen after looking at a curve (the registration order check voids the run).
- Determinism: evaluation under class D2, repeated twice with identical digests; training runs are compared by their paired bounds, not by digests.

## Considerations

- Compute is the binding constraint: the envelope is approved per run, and the fail-closed default is that nothing runs and KILL-GATREE gains the `not-reached` entry of step 1.
- Arm A improves with every gain of the plain transformer, which raises the bar for B and C; that is intended.
- "Better beyond seed spread" on three seeds is weak evidence; it is admitted because the register states it, and the record prints all nine values so a reader can judge.
- The decision is asymmetric on purpose: an undetermined Moonshot is ended, because keeping it taxes every later reader of the policy code.
