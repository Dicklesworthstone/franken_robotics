## Background

The requirement language is the acceptance test of the whole system. A mission is accepted or rejected by a
requirement that returns a verdict and a margin (ADR-ARENA-01); adversarial search minimizes that margin; attribution
flips that verdict; the blinded qualification lane counts it. If the evaluator of the language is wrong, every one of
those is wrong in the same direction and nothing downstream can notice. So the language is built with three
independent evaluators of its past-time operators and a fourth, offline, for its future-time operators, and it is not
called complete until they agree on a million random traces per operator, bit for bit.

The language itself is construction C-30: signal temporal logic over typed signals, with unit-normalized atoms
(ψ ≥ 0 with a declared positive scale s of ψ's dimension, robustness ρ = ψ/s), conjunction as minimum, disjunction
as maximum, negation as −ρ, bounded windows on every temporal operator, past-time operators (H, O, S) available
online, and future-time operators (G, F, U) yielding verdicts offline over completed traces.

The pieces are built by the beads this one depends on:

| Piece | Bead |
|---|---|
| Syntax tree, margin semantics, the definitional evaluator, the pre-origin rule, the two soundness laws | N24a-04 |
| S-expression view and grammar | N24a-03 |
| Unit and dimension checker, scales, exact atoms | N24a-05 |
| Clocks, reductions, sample age, exact window arithmetic | N24a-06 |
| Online block-decomposition kernels | N24a-07 |
| The referee's monotonic-deque monitor | N24a-08 |
| Offline two-pass verdict evaluator: reach, undetermined, decided-early | N24a-09 |
| Boundary guard and exact re-evaluation | N24a-10 |
| Verdict record `frob.verdict/1`, purity, joint stage accounting | N24a-11 |
| Labelled examples and the mutation check | N24a-12 |

This bead assembles them behind one crate interface and runs the differential campaign that is the monitor half of
GATE-ARENA-03. The seed done-condition is exactly that: the monitor rows of GATE-ARENA-03, the three evaluators
agreeing bit for bit on 10⁶ random traces per operator.

## Scope

In: the public interface of `frob-spec` for requirements; the random formula and trace generators; the differential
harness; the campaign of 10⁶ traces per operator including multi-rate signals and windows with rounding; the gate
record; the `frob-spec` contract filled in.

Out: the refusal rows of GATE-ARENA-03 (each requirement-language refusal triggered by its planted task file),
which close with the task-file verbs (BD-122); device execution of the online kernels (BD-255); the device half of
the gate, which also waits on OPEN-ARENA-5.

## Technical approach

**1. The crate interface (DESIGN; intent, not signatures).**

```rust
pub fn check(req: &Requirement, task: &Task) -> Result<CheckedRequirement, Refusal>;   // units, clocks, windows, reach
pub fn compile_online(c: &CheckedRequirement) -> OnlineProgram;       // block kernels, with manifest and memory count
pub fn deque_monitor(c: &CheckedRequirement, online: Ident) -> DequeMonitor;           // the referee's monitor
pub fn evaluate(c: &CheckedRequirement, trace: &dyn AtomTrace) -> VerdictRecord;       // offline, two passes
pub fn evaluate_definitional(c: &CheckedRequirement, trace: &dyn AtomTrace) -> DefinitionalResult;   // oracle
pub fn check_examples(c: &CheckedRequirement, examples: &[Example]) -> ExamplesReport; // labels and mutation check
```

`check` runs, in order: the unit checker (FROB-REF-520, -535), the clock and window checker (FROB-REF-536, -523,
-538, -525), the online capacity check (FROB-REF-528) and reach against horizon (FROB-REF-526). It returns the first
refusal of each kind found, all together, so that an author fixes a file in one round.

**2. What must agree with what.**

| Operators | Evaluators compared | Compared values |
|---|---|---|
| H, O, S (and Boolean combinations) | Online block kernel; deque monitor; definitional evaluator | Margin bits, Boolean, binding witness, the `covered` and `pre-origin` flags, at every tick |
| G, F, U | Offline evaluator; definitional evaluator | Margin bits, Boolean, binding witness at tick 0; the three-way outcome on truncated traces |
| Past-time formulas inside a verdict | A past-time signal sampled at the end of the trace against the offline evaluation of the same formula | Bits |

Agreement is identity of bits. Minimum, maximum and negation are exact in floating point, negative zero is
canonicalized at the atom and a NaN marks the world `Faulted`, so there is no tolerance to set.

**3. Generators (seed-explicit, in `frob-spec/tests/support`).**

1. Windows: tick bounds a ≤ b drawn so that a = 0 in half the cases; b ≤ 64 in 90% of cases, b up to 4,096 (the
   online capacity) in 10%.
2. Traces: length between b + 1 and 4·(b + 1) + 64 ticks; atom margins drawn from a mixture: a small integer lattice
   (to force ties), exact zeros (about 1%), ±0, values within 2⁻²¹ of zero, and full-range doubles; resets at random
   ticks for the streaming evaluators.
3. Multi-rate: atoms read signals on clocks 1/500 s, 1/50 s, 1/30 s and 1/10 s (with phases) reduced or held onto a
   requirement clock, so that the reductions and the hold-with-age path are part of what is compared.
4. Rounding: 10% of windows are authored with bounds that are not multiples of the period, half `:round inward`
   and half `:round outward`.
5. Formulas: one operator under test at the root over atoms, plus a campaign of nested formulas to depth 4.
6. Truncation and gaps: for the offline comparison, 10% of traces are cut before the reach and 10% have a missing
   interval; the expected outcome there is the definitional evaluator's unknown, mapped to `undetermined` unless
   decided.

**4. The campaign.** 10⁶ traces per operator for each of H, O, S, G, F, U (the gate's figure), plus 10⁵ nested
formulas. Shards are keyed by (operator, shard index) so the result is independent of worker count. On a mismatch
the harness stops the shard, minimizes the case (shortest trace, smallest window, fewest distinct values) and writes
a regression file with the seed, the formula, the atom arrays and the differing ticks.

**5. The gate record.** A receipt for the monitor rows of GATE-ARENA-03: traces per operator, mismatches (must be 0),
the commit, the hardware fingerprint, the seeds, and the negative controls that were run and caught. The row in the
gate registry is entered by a session that did not implement the evaluators.

**6. Evidence and determinism.** Class Tested; the comparison is exact (D0 for the decision "identical or not").
The proof obligations discharged here: PO-ARENA-01 (the online block kernels, the deque monitor and the definitional
evaluator agree on every past-time operator; Tested, differential, bit for bit) and PO-ARENA-04 (a positive margin
implies the formula holds and a negative margin that it fails; Tested as a law on random traces).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Monitor rows of GATE-ARENA-03: the three evaluators agree bit for bit on 10⁶ random traces per operator,
      including multi-rate signals and windows with rounding (TARGET: zero mismatches). This is the seed
      done-condition.
- [ ] The offline evaluator agrees with the definitional evaluator on 10⁶ traces per future-time operator, including
      truncated and gapped traces.
- [ ] PO-ARENA-04 holds on every (formula, trace) pair of the campaign.
- [ ] Each registered negative control is caught by the campaign (listed below); a campaign run in which a control
      is not caught is a failure of the gate.
- [ ] `frob-spec`'s `CONTRACT.md` has its ten sections filled: purpose, inputs, outputs, invariants (INV-ARENA-01,
      INV-ARENA-02), refusals (520, 523, 525, 526, 528, 535, 536, 538, 539), determinism class, evidence class of
      outputs, validity domain, oracles (the definitional evaluator), known gaps (device half pending).
- [ ] The interface is reachable through `frob task check` and `frob task verdict` once BD-122 lands, with `--json`.
- [ ] A campaign shard can be cancelled and resumed; the final receipt is identical to an uninterrupted run.
- [ ] The receipt records measured values (mismatch count, traces run), not targets.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `differential_past_time_{h,o,s}`: the three-way comparison, 10⁶ traces each, sharded (run as the G5 tier on the
  host; a 10⁴-trace smoke runs with `cargo test -p frob-spec`).
- `differential_future_time_{g,f,u}`: offline against definitional, 10⁶ traces each.
- `differential_nested`: 10⁵ formulas to depth 4.
- `multi_rate_and_rounding_cases_present`: asserts that at least 5% of the campaign's cases exercised each of: a
  held slower-clock signal, a reduced faster-clock signal, inward rounding, outward rounding, a reset, a tie, an
  exact zero.
- `laws_on_campaign`: PO-ARENA-04 over all pairs.
- `minimizer_reduces_planted_mismatch`: a planted off-by-one in the deque's expiry is reduced to a trace of at most
  8 ticks.
- `shard_independence`: the same campaign with 1, 8 and 36 workers produces the same receipt digest.
- `check_reports_all_refusals`: a file with a missing scale and a non-multiple window returns both refusals.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/arena_requirement_pipeline.sh` (assembled by N24a-42) includes the step `gate-arena-03-monitors`, which
runs plain commands: `cargo test -p frob-spec --release -- --ignored differential_` with
`FROB_SPEC_TRACES=1000000` and the campaign seed, then `frob task verdict` on the golden fixture runs. It asserts
zero mismatches per operator and that each negative-control build fails. NDJSON lines (through the STD-E2E helper,
under `target/e2e-logs/arena_requirement_pipeline/`) carry: the command, exit code, wall time, operator, traces run
(unit: traces), mismatches with threshold 0, the campaign seed, the receipt digest, and on failure the regression
file path and the first differing tick with both bit patterns. The final `summary` line names GATE-ARENA-03.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-597: the three requirement evaluators on 10⁶ random traces per operator, including multi-rate signals and
  windows with rounding: agreement bit for bit; tolerance exact; tier G5, host.
- KA-596: a past-time signal sampled at the end of the trace equals the offline evaluation of the same formula.
- KA-594 (normalization, margins 0.2 and 0.3) and KA-615 (boundary comparator) are run as part of the same suite.
- Negative controls, each a build variant registered in the negative-control registry against GATE-ARENA-03:
  1. a monitor that pads a short window (the control of KA-596);
  2. an off-by-one in the deque's expiry test;
  3. an off-by-one in the block kernel's suffix index;
  4. composition of raw margins (CX-259);
  5. the sign of the margin used as the Boolean value;
  6. a window bound converted through floating point.
- Metamorphic relations: MR-19 (unit normalization leaves normalized robustness and the binding atom unchanged) and
  MR-20 (streaming equals offline; G over [0, T] at 0 equals H over [0, T] at T).
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; Linux and macOS.

## Considerations

- OPEN-ARENA-5 (guard band per backend; default 2⁻²¹ with CPU re-evaluation of every flagged atom) blocks only the
  device half of GATE-ARENA-03. The M1 exit needs the host half.
- The campaign's cost is dominated by the definitional evaluator on S and U (cubic in the window for small
  windows). At b ≤ 64 it is minutes on the 36-core machine; the 10% of cases at capacity use short traces.
- Honest limits of the construction: requirements are the acceptance metric and a falsification score; they do not
  replace shaped rewards in training. Margins are comparable across atoms only through declared scales.
- The mission language (`.fmission`) reuses this grammar by reference to task terms (OPEN-AUGUR-1 default), so a
  defect here is also a defect in mission certificates; that is the reason for a million traces and not a thousand.
- What would make us revisit: any disagreement that turns out to be a legitimate difference of exact functions
  (there should be none), or a device family on which minimum and maximum are not bit-exact.
