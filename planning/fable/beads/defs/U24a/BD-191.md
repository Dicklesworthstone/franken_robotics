## Background

An agent debugging a robot reads numbers first: a summary, a failure class, a margin. Some questions are faster to
answer by looking: where exactly did the foot land relative to the wet patch, which link touched the sill, was the
payload already tilted half a second before the fall. `telemetry.stills` gives an agent that can see a picture of a
recorded instant, and its companion `telemetry.inspect-pixel` lets any agent, sighted or not, ask what is under a
pixel. The loop is: look, then ask. (FACT: a sibling project of the suite uses the same loop for garments.) One of
the program's North-Star scenarios states the need directly: a humanoid crosses a commercial kitchen with a wet
patch; telemetry reports slip distance per stance, the friction-utilization margin and the stills at the moment of
the slip, and the agent can ask which link a pixel belongs to.

Three rules shape the verb, each a decision with a rejected alternative.

- *Stills show what physics has.* They are drawn from the collision field with the same ray kernels the sensors
  use, shaded simply, not from the visual mesh. Reason: a robot that trips on nothing must be shown the geometry the
  contact solver saw. Rejected: rendering the artist's mesh, which is prettier and can differ from the collision
  geometry exactly where the failure is. (Decision of the scene subsystem: the visual mesh is read by the viewer
  only; invariant: physics, sensors and stills read only the collision field, the overlays and the maps.)
- *Stills never feed back.* The simulator owns state and presentation reads immutable snapshots. No observation
  depends on presentation state: viewer, stills, telemetry level or wall clock (invariant INV-SENS-01). Drawing a
  still during a run changes no physics digest and no delivered observation.
- *A still is a view with numbers behind it.* Every overlay draws a physical quantity from a named telemetry
  channel with its unit, a numeric legend and the evidence class of the run; anything quantitative is read through
  `telemetry.inspect-pixel` or `telemetry.query`, never estimated from colours.

The seed bead bundles three verbs: `telemetry.stills`, `telemetry.inspect-pixel` and `telemetry.export-html`. This
bead carries `telemetry.stills` and the end-to-end script of all three; pixel inspection is N24a-40 and the
self-contained replay is N24a-41. The seed done-condition, "a pixel maps to the right link in fixtures", is
asserted by this bead's script through N24a-40. The renderer and identifier buffer themselves belong to the sensing
crate (see `needs`); this bead owns the verb: what to draw, when, how it is laid out, stored, labelled and cited.

## Scope

In: the verb (read; second milestone), its schema `frob.telemetry.stills/1`; frame selection (`--at`), including the
three failure frames; view selection; contact-sheet layout and captions; overlays on stills and the per-pixel
overlay value buffer; storage of stills as content-addressed artifacts with their identifier buffers; attachment of
still handles to the first-cause report; privacy and sealing; determinism; `scripts/e2e/telemetry_stills.sh`.

Out: the ray-cast renderer, identifier buffer and lookup function (the sensing crate's bead); the overlay functions
of other subsystems; the live viewer page and the dashboard (seed BD-300); the noninterference battery (seed
BD-215); colour cameras for policies and offline hero renders (Frontier items of the sensing chapter).

## Technical approach

**1. Input.** `frob telemetry stills <run> --world <address> [--episode <n>] [--at <selector>] [--views <list>]
[--overlay <list>] [--size <W>x<H>] [--sheet | --single] [--depth] --json`.

- `--at` selectors: `failure` (default when the episode has a terminal failure), `keyframes` (default otherwise),
  `first-cause`, `decided` (the tick at which the verdict was decided), `binding` (the binding time of the binding
  atom), `event:<class>` (a failure class's first anomaly), `t=<seconds>` (an exact rational), `tick=<n>`.
- `failure` means three frames at −0.5 s, 0 s and +0.2 s around the terminal event (the offsets the failure
  taxonomy fixes for its first-cause report). `keyframes` means, in time order: the start, each monitor's first
  anomaly, the decision tick, the terminal event; at most 4 frames by default, evenly thinned if more, never
  dropping the terminal event.
- `--views`: `orbit`, `top`, `side`, `follow` (robot-follow), `sensor:<name>` (the sensor's own rays and pose).
  Default: `orbit,top,side,follow`.
- `--overlay`: any of `contact-cones`, `film`, `clearance`, `energy`, `actuator`, `balance`, `sensing`; example
  from the specification: `telemetry.stills --overlay contact-cones,film`.

**2. Where a frame comes from.** A still is drawn from a recorded instant, never from a re-simulation. The verb
resolves each requested instant to stored data: a snapshot, or the pose columns of the Kinematics stream for worlds
whose control-rate trace was retained (an address-keyed sample of worlds and every failing world, whose ring buffer
is flushed on failure). If the exact tick is not stored, the nearest stored tick at or before it is drawn and both
`requested_tick` and `drawn_tick` are returned; a +0.2 s frame past the end of the episode is replaced by the last
tick and marked `clamped`. If nothing is stored for the world (it was not in the traced sample and did not fail),
the verb refuses with the sampling rule and the manifest field that would have retained it; it never fabricates a
frame.

**3. Output (`frob.telemetry.stills/1`).** `still_id` (content hash over the run, world, frames, views, overlays,
size and renderer version); `image` (artifact handle, PNG, also a `frob://` resource over MCP); `id_buffer` and
`range_buffer` handles; `tiles`: one entry per tile with view, tick, simulated time as a rational, event name, the
pixel rectangle in the sheet, and the camera pose and intrinsics used; `legend`: per overlay the channel name,
unit, the numeric minimum and maximum mapped to the colour scale, the scale's name and the evidence class; `drawn_from:
"collision-field"`; the privacy mark; the envelope's evidence block.

**4. Contact-sheet layout (DESIGN).** Rows are frames in time order, columns are views; tile size 320 × 240 by
default; at most 16 tiles unless `--max-tiles` raises it; a 2-pixel gutter; a caption strip of fixed height under
each tile in a built-in bitmap font ("t = 7.50 s · failure − 0.5 s · top"). The same captions are in `tiles`, so an
agent that cannot read the image reads the table. The layout is a pure function of the options.

**5. Overlays on stills.** Stills carry the same overlays as the viewer. Each overlay is a pure function of the
snapshot and the recorded channels:

| Overlay | Quantity drawn | Earliest |
|---|---|---|
| `contact-cones` | The cone at each contact (normal, two tangential, spin) with friction utilization ‖f_t‖/(μ·f_n) as colour; the contact wrench as an arrow | M2 |
| `energy` | Power per actuator, losses by kind, dissipation at contacts; the numerical residual as its own bar | M2 |
| `actuator` | Torque and speed utilization against the card; winding temperature | M2 |
| `balance` | Centre of mass, support polygon, margin to tipping | M2 |
| `sensing` | Frusta and rays with occlusion, coloured by mask reason | M2 |
| `film` | Film thickness and effective friction on floors, including film the robot has moved | M3, with the film law |
| `clearance` | Doorways and passages, passable or not, with the bottleneck named | M3, with the clearance complex |

Beside the colour image the verb stores an *overlay value buffer* per overlay: for each pixel that shows an overlay
element, the stored channel value in the channel's own quantization, with the element's telemetry address. This is
what makes "the value under a probe pixel equals the telemetry channel within its quantization" checkable. An
overlay whose channel was not recorded for that world is refused with the list of overlays that are available;
the verb never returns a blank overlay.

**6. Stills in failure reports.** From this bead on, the first-cause report of `telemetry.failures` (and
`explain.run`, which consumes it) attaches the handles of the three failure frames for each failing world it lists,
drawn lazily on first request and cached by `still_id`.

**7. Storage and determinism.** Images and buffers are content-addressed artifacts under the run's retention
class. The PNG encoder is in-tree, writes no timestamp or text chunk, and uses fixed settings, so the same snapshot
and options give the same bytes. Identifier buffers are exact on every backend; shaded colour is in the determinism
class of the ray kernels (bit-identical per backend, and across backends where the kernels attain it).

**8. Privacy and sealing.** A still of a private scene is a private artifact: it carries the private mark, is
excluded from logs and exports unless the caller holds the scope, and its bytes never enter an end-to-end log. A
still of a run in the blinded confirmation lane is a sealed field until the campaign closes: a session without the
administrative capability is refused with FROB-REF-840 (a session without `qual:admin` asks for sealed confirmation
data before close) and the attempt is logged.

**9. Cost.** Stills are drawn on demand: four views at 320 × 240 cost about as much as one depth camera for four
frames. The verb is `read` and synchronous within the bounded wait; a sheet larger than the wait budget returns a
job handle (exit code 8) like any long call.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob telemetry stills <run> --world <w> --at failure --json` returns a three-frame, four-view contact sheet
      with captions, legends, `still_id`, and buffer handles; reachable over MCP as `telemetry_stills` with the
      image as a resource.
- [ ] Seed done-condition: on the fixtures below, a pixel maps to the right link (asserted through
      `telemetry.inspect-pixel`): each of the reference quadruped's named links, the cart's chassis, payload and
      four wheels, the sill fixture, the floor region and a contact disc.
- [ ] A still shows the collision field: a decorative visual part with no collision counterpart is absent; a
      collision-only blocker is present; `drawn_from` says so.
- [ ] The three failure frames are at −0.5 s, 0 s and +0.2 s around the terminal event, with `clamped` set where
      the episode ended earlier.
- [ ] A world with nothing stored is refused with the sampling rule; an overlay without its channel is refused
      with the available list.
- [ ] Two calls with the same options return identical bytes and the same `still_id`.
- [ ] Drawing stills during a run leaves every physics digest and every delivered observation unchanged.
- [ ] A still of a private scene carries the private mark; a sealed run's still is refused with FROB-REF-840.
- [ ] `telemetry.failures` attaches still handles to its first-cause report.
- [ ] The companion verbs exist (N24a-40, N24a-41) and `scripts/e2e/telemetry_stills.sh` passes.

- `failure_frames_offsets`: terminal event at t = 7.5 s on a 1/50 s control clock → frames at ticks 350, 375, 385.
- `failure_frame_clamped`: episode ends at the terminal tick → third frame is the last tick, `clamped = true`.
- `keyframes_thinned_keep_terminal`: 9 candidate frames, limit 4 → 4 frames including start and terminal.
- `nearest_stored_tick_reported`: requested tick 377 with snapshots every 25 ticks → drawn 375, both returned.
- `not_retained_refused`: a world outside the traced sample that did not fail.
- `sheet_layout_pure`: tile rectangles for 3 × 4 at 320 × 240 with 2-pixel gutters equal the expected table.
- `captions_match_tiles_table`.
- `overlay_without_channel_refused_with_list`.
- `overlay_value_buffer_equals_channel`: at a contact with stored friction utilization 0.83 (quantized), every
  pixel of that contact's glyph reads the stored integer.
- `still_id_stable_and_option_sensitive`: same options → same id; a changed overlay list → another id.
- `png_bytes_deterministic`: no timestamp chunk; byte equality across two encodes.

- `first_cause_report_attaches_handles`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/telemetry_stills.sh` (owned by this bead; step groups `stills-*` here, `pixel-*` from N24a-40,
`replay-*` from N24a-41).

1. `setup`: `frob sim run` on `batch-cpu` and on the referee for three fixtures: the reference quadruped on a plane
   with a planted fall; the cart at the door sill with a planted chassis strike; a depth camera facing a glass pane.
2. `stills-failure`: `frob telemetry stills <run> --world <w> --at failure --overlay contact-cones --json`; assert
   the tile table, the three offsets, the legend's channel and unit.
3. `stills-determinism`: the same call again; equal `still_id` and equal image digest.
4. `stills-collision-field`: the fixture with a decorative part; assert its identifier is absent from the buffer.
5. `stills-noninterference`: the quadruped run repeated with a still requested every 100 steps through the daemon;
   `frob telemetry divergence <runA> <runB>` reports none.
6. `stills-refusals`: an untraced world (exit 3 with the sampling rule); `--overlay film` on a scene with no film
   channel (exit 3 with the available list).
7. `pixel-*` and `replay-*` as described in their beads.

The script sources the shared end-to-end helper and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/telemetry_stills/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block
(claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds (tiles returned;
frame offsets in seconds against −0.5, 0, +0.2; pixel identifiers against expected names; mismatches against 0),
the digests compared (`still_id`, image, identifier buffer), and on failure the first differing value and the
artifact handles needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed. Image bytes and
scene geometry are never written to the log; identifiers and digests only.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answer (identifier buffer): on the quadruped fixture each of the 12 named leg links and the base is hit by
  a pixel aimed at its centroid from the `top` or `side` preset; on the cart fixture the six bodies and the sill.
- Known answer (presentation noninterference, KA-505): turning the viewer, stills or the telemetry level on or off
  leaves every physics digest and every delivered observation unchanged.
- Known answer (probe): an overlay value under a probe pixel equals the telemetry channel within its quantization.
- Negative control 1: an identifier buffer built from the visual mesh: the decorative part appears; step
  `stills-collision-field` must fail.
- Negative control 2: a renderer that advances a keyed noise counter: step `stills-noninterference` must fail.
- Negative control 3: failure frames taken at fixed ticks from the episode start instead of around the terminal
  event: `failure_frames_offsets` must fail.
- Negative control 4: a PNG encoder that stamps the wall-clock time: `stills-determinism` must fail.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; identifier buffers across backends (exact); colour
  images per backend, each against its class.

## Considerations

- A still is for understanding and never for measurement; the verb's output says so in its schema description, and
  reports cite channels, not pictures.
- Dependency policy: the PNG writer needs DEFLATE. An in-tree encoder with stored or fixed-Huffman blocks is enough
  (stills are small) and avoids admitting a codec crate; if an admitted codec exists by then, it may be used so
  long as output bytes stay deterministic.
- Retention: stills are derived artifacts and may be collected before their run; a collected still is redrawn on
  request while the run's poses are stored, and otherwise the handle resolves to a tombstone refusal.
- Frame selection defaults are DESIGN proposals of this bead (the specification fixes only the three failure
  offsets and "several views at key frames"); they are options, recorded in `still_id`.
- The live viewer and its overlay table arrive one milestone later; the overlay functions must therefore be usable
  without the page, which this verb proves.
