## Background

Every episode on the batched tiers begins with a reset, and what a reset leaves behind is the first thing a learner
and a paired study see. An episode that starts with a foot 2 mm inside the floor begins with an impulse that no robot
would feel; a paired comparison in which one design starts settled and the other does not compares spawn artifacts.
The remedy is the settled-pose cache: episodes start from precomputed quasi-static poses (the Cortex requirement on
every task's reset term: a settled-pose cache of precomputed quasi-static poses, TARGET 64 per design with
penetration at most 0.5 mm, so that there is no spawn impulse in the first transitions).

The cache core exists from M1 on the referee (N24a-16): keys, the settling procedure, selection by world address,
refusal FROB-REF-529 and the verb `task.settle`. This bead takes it to where training and evaluation happen: states
settled in double precision on `batch-cpu`, loaded into thousands of worlds on every tier through the vectorized
environment's reset, checked at load on that tier, and gated on all 64 states for each reference robot.

The seed done-condition is GATE-ARENA-08: penetration at most 0.5 mm and first impulse within 5% of static support
on all 64 states for each reference robot; a design edit invalidates the cache.

## Scope

In: the settler on `batch-cpu` in `f64` (64 states settle as one batch); conversion of settled states to each
tier's arithmetic and layout; the load path inside `VecEnv::reset`; running N24a-16's load check on each tier with
that tier's own first step; the counts of `Degraded: unsettled` worlds per design and per arm; cache handling for
parameter variants; the gate measurement on the reference robots; the end-to-end script.

Out: the key, criterion, selection rule and refusal, the load check `load_settled` with its `LoadOutcome`
(`Settled` or `Unsettled { support_ratio, penetration }`) and the `Degraded: unsettled` mark (N24a-16, all reused
unchanged: this bead runs them on the batched tiers and adds the per-arm counts); the reset-pose stream and its
address helper `by_reset` (N35-23, N35-24); the vectorized environment's reset
semantics (BD-192); its device-resident views and slot layout on CUDA and Metal, into which the load path writes
(BD-193, with the device steps BD-172 and BD-174); training (BD-198, which depends on this bead).

## Technical approach

**1. Settling on `batch-cpu`.** The 64 spawn draws of a cache are the 64 worlds of one double-precision batch on the
`batch-cpu` tier, stepped with the task's contact law under the strict (transactional, event-located) profile.
The criterion is evaluated per world with a sliding window: base and joint speeds below 10⁻³ in SI units for
0.2 s with maximum penetration at most 0.5 mm (TARGET). Worlds that have settled are frozen by mask; the batch ends
when all 64 are settled or the time limit passes. Every cache built at M1 on the referee is rebuilt here; `settled_on`
in the receipt changes from `referee` to `batch-cpu`, and the two builds are compared (configuration differences
inside the fidelity envelope of the two tiers; a larger difference is reported, not hidden).

**2. A cached state on another tier.** A state is stored in double precision with positions in the anchored
convention (cell index plus offset). Loading into a single-precision tier converts by rounding to nearest, then:

1. writes configuration, zero velocities, actuator rest states, estimator initial state and contact warm starts
   into the world's slots by indexed copy (no host round trip per world on device tiers; the 64 states are resident
   in a small device table per model);
2. calls N24a-16's `load_settled(world, &SettleCache, index)`, whose one step is here the tier's own first step
   (in the tier's precision and kernels), measuring the total contact impulse J and the maximum penetration;
3. `LoadOutcome::Settled` when |support_ratio − 1| ≤ 0.05 with support_ratio = ‖J/h‖ / (m·‖g‖) (N24a-16's
   definition) and the maximum penetration is at most 0.5 mm (the first contact impulse after loading a cached
   state on any tier must lie within 5% of the static support force);
4. on `LoadOutcome::Unsettled { support_ratio, penetration }` the world carries N24a-16's `Degraded: unsettled`
   mark with the two values in its run record. The world keeps running and is counted; it is not silently
   re-rolled, and it is not dropped. Whether a degraded world's episode counts as valid for the task follows the
   task's policy for degraded worlds; beyond that policy it sets the `invalid` mask of the environment (a study
   error with weight zero in policy updates, kept in benchmark and failure counts). On a device tier the first
   step and the measurement of J and penetration run on the device, the reset view returns `support_ratio` and
   penetration for all reset worlds in one batched read (not a per-world round trip), and the outcome is
   N24a-16's criterion applied to those values with N24a-16's thresholds, which are not restated in this crate.

**3. Inside `VecEnv::reset(mask, seeds)`.** For each world in the mask: the pose index comes from the counter-based
stream at the address `by_reset(reset-pose, "world", reset_count)` (N35-24's helper: the `reset-pose` stream, the
reserved whole-world path `world` in place of a joint or body path hash, and the episode-local reset count as the
counter; index = the draw's 32-bit word mod 64, unbiased since 64 divides 2³²), so the index depends on the world
address and the reset count and on nothing about the design; the state is loaded as above;
clocks take a fresh origin, packet queues are emptied, and online requirement monitors have their coverage reset
(their values follow the pre-origin rule until their windows are covered). The reset view reports, per world, the
pose index and the degraded flag. Two designs in a paired study at one address get the same index (the address
names no design path) and each its own state; a pair with either world `Degraded: unsettled` is classified
`unscored` as N24a-16 states.

**4. Variants.** A cache belongs to one `ModelId`. Parameter variants that change mass, geometry or contact
surfaces are different models with their own caches; `task.settle --cohort <cohort>` (an option this bead adds to
the verb's task, `--model` and key inputs; `--model` and `--cohort` are exclusive, both is `FROB-REF-802`) builds
them for a whole cohort as one batch per shape bucket. Per-world randomization that does not change the model (floor friction, a payload shift
within the task's draws) reuses the model's cache and relies on the load check of step 2. Admission of a run whose
cohort contains a model without a cache is refused with FROB-REF-529 naming the models.

**5. Accounting.** Every run receipt and every scorecard built on it carries: resets performed, resets marked
`Degraded: unsettled` (count and fraction, per design), and the distribution of first-impulse ratios. If two arms of
a comparison differ in their unsettled fraction by more than one percentage point of worlds, the comparison reports
it beside the verdict (the same rule the engine applies to capacity-degraded worlds).

**6. The gate measurement.** For each reference robot on file for the archetypes that exist at M2 (the imported
public models serving `quad-mammal`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive` and `mecanum`, and the archetype templates
themselves): build the cache; on every tier present (`batch-cpu` in `f64` and `f32`, CUDA, Metal) load all 64 states
and record penetration and first-impulse ratio; then apply a design edit (a mass change, a geometry change and a
contact-surface change, one at a time) and confirm that the old cache is not served. The gate record carries the
measured maxima per robot and tier, the commit, the hardware fingerprint and the seed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-ARENA-08 (the seed done-condition): for each reference robot, on all 64 states and on every tier present,
      penetration at most 0.5 mm and first contact impulse within 5% of static support (TARGET); a design edit that
      changes mass, geometry or contact surfaces invalidates the cache.
- [ ] `VecEnv::reset` on 4,096 worlds loads states by the index drawn at `by_reset(reset-pose, "world",
      reset_count)` with no per-world host round trip on device tiers; the same addresses give the same indices on
      every tier and for every design paired at the address.
- [ ] A world that fails N24a-16's `load_settled` on its tier is marked `Degraded: unsettled` and appears in the
      receipt's counts per design and per arm; no world is re-rolled or dropped; the batched path calls the same
      function, thresholds and mark as the referee (a source scan finds no second copy of the 0.05 and 0.5 mm thresholds).
- [ ] The negative controls below are caught.
- [ ] A cohort run with one model lacking a cache is refused with FROB-REF-529 naming it.
- [ ] Cancellation during a cohort settle drains and leaves no partial cache visible and no leaked device buffers.
- [ ] The CPU reference path exists (`batch-cpu` `f64`), and device loads are checked against it by a retained
      differential test: loaded configurations equal the CPU values after rounding to the tier's precision.
- [ ] The gate is recorded with measured values by a session that did not implement the capability.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `batch_settle_equals_single_settle`: the 64-world batch settle and 64 single-world settles give identical states
  (batch-composition invariance of the settler).
- `load_roundtrip_f32`: a state loaded into `f32` slots differs from its `f64` source by at most one unit in the last
  place per coordinate in the anchored representation.
- `first_impulse_ratio_formula`: a 12 kg body at rest on a level plane, step h = 2 ms: J = m·g·h = 0.23544 N·s;
  ratio 1; the same with gravity along a 6° slope still gives ratio 1 for the vector impulse.
- `degraded_unsettled_marked_and_counted`: a state perturbed 2 mm into the floor before load → N24a-16's
  `load_settled` returns `Unsettled` with `support_ratio` above 1.05 on the batched tier, the `Degraded: unsettled`
  mark is set, count 1.
- `index_identity_across_tiers`: indices from the address stream are identical on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal.
- `reset_index_by_world_path`: the index is drawn at `by_reset(reset-pose, "world", reset_count)`; two designs at
  one address, one with an added limb and one with its legs reordered, get the same index at every reset count; the
  next reset count gives the index of the next draw; a planted build that keys the draw by a body index gives the
  two designs different indices and fails.
- `cohort_and_model_exclusive`: `task settle <task> --model m --cohort c` is refused with FROB-REF-802.
- `reset_resets_monitor_coverage`: an online H signal returns pre-origin values after reset until covered.
- `variant_models_have_own_caches`: a 10% mass variant has a different key; a friction-only world draw does not.
- `unsettled_fraction_reported_per_arm`.
- `device_load_equals_cpu_rounded` (the retained differential test of the acceptance): on each device tier present,
  the loaded configuration of every one of the 64 states equals, bit for bit, the `f64` CPU state rounded to nearest
  in the tier's precision; a planted truncating conversion fails it.
- `cancel_cohort_settle_clean`: a cohort settle cancelled mid-batch leaves no cache entry visible under any key,
  and the device allocator's live-buffer count returns to its value before the settle.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/gate_arena_08_reset_cache.sh`:

1. `frob task settle <task> --model <robot> --key ga08-settle-<robot> --json` for each reference robot (job
   handle, `frob job wait <job> --sync-wait-ms 30000 --json`, BD-210); assert `settled_on: "batch-cpu"` and 64
   states; then `frob task settle <task> --cohort <three-variant cohort> --key ga08-cohort --json` (one batch per
   shape bucket, three caches) and the same with `--model` added (exit 3, FROB-REF-802).
2. `frob sim run <robot> --task <task> --tier <tier> --worlds 4096 --seconds <one control interval> --telemetry T1
   --seed <seed> --key ga08-load-<robot>-<tier> --json` on each tier present; `frob telemetry query <run> --stream
   contact --json` for the first-step contact impulse and penetration envelope, and the reset view's pose indices
   and `Degraded: unsettled` counts from the run's receipt; assertions against 0.5 mm and 5%, and equal pose indices
   for equal addresses across tiers.
3. Three `frob design edit <scratch copy of robot> set-param … --expected-parent <id> --key ga08-edit-<kind> --json`
   commands (mass, geometry, contact surface), each followed by a run (key `ga08-stale-<kind>`) that must be refused
   with FROB-REF-529, exit 3, until `task settle` is repeated (key `ga08-resettle-<kind>`).
4. The negative-control runs (control builds; keys `ga08-ctl-<n>`), each through `e2e_expect_caught`.
5. The known-answer row through the Gauntlet, `e2e_tier g1_known_answers KA-601` (that is, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed>
   cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- KA-601`; the Gauntlet has no `frob` command), and its
   controls with `e2e_tier g1_known_answers KA-601 --mutations`; the log carries the receipt hash. The script
   writes no gate record (convention 9): this bead, the one that measures GATE-ARENA-08, records it from that
   receipt outside the script with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ARENA-08
   --receipt <hash>`, in a session that did not implement the capability.

Idempotent and seed-explicit. NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/gate_arena_08_reset_cache/` records per step: command,
exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), per robot and tier
the maximum penetration (mm, threshold 0.5) and the worst |ratio − 1| (threshold 0.05), counts of degraded worlds,
cache keys before and after each edit, and on failure the world address and state index of the first offender. The
`summary` line names GATE-ARENA-08.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-601: settled-pose reset cache on all 64 states for each reference robot: penetration at most 0.5 mm; first
  impulse within 5% of static support; tolerance as stated by the gate; tier G1, lockstep. Negative control: a
  design edit must invalidate the cache.
- Negative control 1 (planted defect): a build that keys the cache by design name serves a stale cache after the
  mass edit; step 3 of the script must fail that build.
- Negative control 2: spawning at nominal height without the cache; the first-impulse assertion must fail (this is
  the spawn artifact the mechanism removes).
- Negative control 3: a reset path that re-rolls unsettled worlds until they pass; the degraded count is then zero
  while the planted perturbed state exists, and the accounting test must fail.
- Metamorphic relations: a world's reset state does not depend on batch size, slot or co-tenants (MR-01); permuting
  world indices permutes the pose indices and changes nothing else.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; each backend against its class
  (the index is D0 everywhere; loaded states are D2 per backend).

## Considerations

- OPEN-ARENA-2 (no permissively licensed hexapod reference model): twelve of the eighteen archetypes have no
  reference model on file; for those the gate's "reference robot" is the archetype's own template.
- The 5% bound is tight for single precision on stiff contacts; if a tier cannot meet it for a robot class the
  result is recorded as a failed row with its measured value, and the repair is in the contact conditioning, not in
  the threshold.
- Rebuilding at M2 changes every `SettleKey` whose `settled_on` or kernel versions changed; episodes memoized under
  the M1 keys are not served, by construction.
- The device table of 64 states per model costs memory proportional to models × 64 × state size; for a cohort of 512
  topologies it is reported by the cost model before admission.
- No-GPU variant of M2: the gate then runs on `batch-cpu` only and device rows are recorded `not run` with the
  reason.
