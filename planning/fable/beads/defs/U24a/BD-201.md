## Background

M2 puts the batch under the studio, and the first things to run in a batch are the two families every legged and
wheeled design is judged on first: following a commanded velocity, and getting across terrain.

| Family | Example tasks | First milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Velocity tracking | Commanded planar velocity and yaw rate on flat and rough ground | M2 |
| Terrain traversal | Stairs up and down, gaps, stepping stones, slopes, rubble | M2 |

Every family ships at least one task with a requirement, labelled example traces and a reference-robot baseline
scorecard. This seed bead does two things: it builds the velocity-tracking family, and it closes the M2 set by taking
both families (terrain traversal is N24a-25) through GATE-ARENA-01: every task reproducible inside its determinism
class from its `TaskId`, `SceneId`, `ModelId` and seed, with reference-robot baseline scorecards recorded.

Velocity tracking matters beyond its own scores: it is the task of the training gate (PPO on the reference
quadruped's flat velocity task), of the throughput and training-time parity rows against the incumbent, and of the
requirement reward-term pilot. A defect in how it is specified would contaminate all three. The standard defect is
that "success" is defined by the shaped reward (an exponential kernel of velocity error), so that a change of reward
weights silently changes what success means. Here the reward terms train and a requirement, in physical units,
decides.

## Scope

In: two velocity-tracking tasks (flat and rough ground) for the legged and wheeled reference classes: all nine
task-file sections, the requirement, labelled example traces, baselines; the family's registry record; the M2 run of
the reproducibility gate for both M2 families and the recording of their reference scorecards.

Out: terrain traversal's own content (N24a-25); fixture 2, the split-grip landing (BD-202); the training of a policy
on these tasks (BD-198); the metrics themselves (N24a-17 to N24a-20).

## Technical approach

**1. Tasks.** `velocity-track-flat` and `velocity-track-rough`, each instantiated for the reference quadruped (the
imported public quadruped serving the `quad-mammal` archetype) and for the wheeled references (`diff-drive`,
`mecanum`); the humanoid reference is included where its baseline controller exists. Files under
`assets/tasks/velocity-tracking/`.

**2. The nine sections** (DESIGN; numeric defaults below are this bead's proposals, fixed in the task file before its
labelled examples are recorded and frozen by the `TaskId` afterwards).

- `interface`: mechanical base port, electrical bus; observation layout of proprioception (joint positions and
  velocities, base angular velocity, gravity direction, the command, sample ages) at 50 Hz; action: joint position
  targets through the driver loop (legged) or wheel-rate targets (wheeled), with the registered action law.
- `clocks`: physics 1/500 s, policy 1/50 s, requirement 1/50 s (the clock named `req`, on which the task's
  requirements are evaluated; N24a-06), command 1/50 s.
- `terms`: observation terms with units, noise from sensor cards, delays and declared representations; reward
  terms as rates integrated over simulated time. OPEN-ARENA-1 (owner Arena lead, due M2; recorded by N24c-16 before
  this task file is written) decides which public task definitions to mirror; default: mirror the reward terms of
  the public velocity-tracking tasks and make no claim of score parity. The mirrored terms keep the public task's
  names, signs and functional forms, each written with units as a rate, event or terminal term, and the section
  carries a `source` record (public task, version, licence) that is part of the canonical bytes, so a change of
  source is a new `TaskId`. Termination: `(terminate failure :on (decided tracked violated))`.
- `signals` (truth, physics clock): `lin_err` = ‖v_cmd − v‖ of the base in the yaw frame [m/s], state, `:reduce
  max`; `yaw_err` = |ω_cmd − ω| [rad/s], state, `:reduce max`; `tilt` = max(|roll|, |pitch|) [deg], interval
  extremum, `:reduce max`; `body_strikes` [count], `:reduce last`; `cmd_age` = time since the command last changed
  [s], state, `:reduce last`.
- `requirements`:

  ```lisp
  (requirements
    (atom settling  (<  cmd_age 1 s)                          :scale 1 s)
    (atom lin-ok    (>= (- 0.25 m/s lin_err) 0 m/s)           :scale 0.25 m/s)
    (atom yaw-ok    (>= (- 0.25 rad/s yaw_err) 0 rad/s)       :scale 0.25 rad/s)
    (atom upright   (>= (- 30 deg tilt) 0 deg)                :scale 30 deg)
    (atom no-strike (=  body_strikes 0)                       :exact)
    (online on-track (H (0 s 1 s) (and lin-ok yaw-ok)))
    (verdict tracked (G (0 s 20 s) (and upright no-strike (or settling (and lin-ok yaw-ok))))))
  ```

  Reading: at every requirement tick of a 20 s episode the robot is upright, has not struck its body, and either a
  command changed within the last second or both tracking errors are within bounds. Reach 20 s; horizon 20 s. The
  verdict is labelled `sampled` because the tracking atoms read state-kind channels. `on-track` is the online signal
  used by early-warning displays and by the reward-term pilot.
- `randomization`: floor friction from the interface card's stated uncertainty; actuator parameters from the
  actuator cards; payload mass from the payload card where one is declared; sensor bias from sensor cards; the
  command schedule: piecewise-constant (v_x, v_y, ω_z) redrawn every 4 s from the task's declared command box
  (proposed for the quadruped: v_x ∈ [−1.0, 1.0] m/s, v_y ∈ [−0.5, 0.5] m/s, ω_z ∈ [−1.0, 1.0] rad/s; for
  differential bases v_y = 0). The command stream is keyed by world address and physical time, never by control tick,
  so two designs and two control rates see the same commands.
- `reset`: `(settled-pose :count 64 :speed-below 1e-3 :for 0.2 s :penetration-max 0.5 mm :first-impulse-within 5 %)`.
- `world`: flat ground, or the rough-ground terrain family (band-limited noise) at a declared difficulty, with a
  split by seed and tile set; horizon 20 s.
- `study`: default fixed-n, n = 128 paired worlds, margin 0.05, the paired exact rule, level 0.05. (At n = 128 that
  rule can return all four of its verdicts; at n = 64 it cannot return a practical tie.)

**3. Labelled examples.** At least two satisfying and two violating traces per task, one near the boundary of each
comparator: satisfying traces from the reference robot under its baseline controller; violating traces from planted
conditions (the same controller with halved gains, which lags beyond 0.25 m/s; a push that tips the robot beyond
30°; a body strike on rough ground). The mutation check must leave no vacuous hard-limit atom.

**4. Baselines.** For each task and reference robot: the synthesized baseline controller, the family's baseline
battery (a `smoke`-sized set of held-out worlds), and the scorecard with tracking error (linear and yaw), success
and fall rates, requirement margin with binding atoms, both cost-of-transport figures, Froude number (legged),
actuator utilization, friction utilization, slip distance and tail robustness.

**5. Closing the M2 set.** Run `scripts/e2e/gate_arena_01_reproducibility.sh --family velocity-tracking` and
`--family terrain-traversal` (N24a-24's harness) on every tier present; the script produces the receipt and writes
no gate record. This bead records the M2 part of GATE-ARENA-01 from that receipt, outside any script, with `cargo
run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ARENA-01 --receipt <hash> --part M2`, in a session
that did not implement the tasks (convention 9: one recorder per part; BD-253 records the M3 part and BD-368 the
M4 part the same way).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-ARENA-01 for velocity tracking and terrain traversal (the seed done-condition): each task is reproducible
      inside its determinism class from its `TaskId`, `SceneId`, `ModelId` and seed on every tier present, and the
      reference-robot baseline scorecards are recorded.
- [ ] Both velocity-tracking tasks pass `task.check`: units, clocks, windows, reach against horizon, labelled
      examples, mutation check.
- [ ] The family registry lists both M2 families as `available` (computed).
- [ ] The command schedule is identical for two designs paired on one world address and unchanged when the control
      rate is doubled.
- [ ] A change of reward weights through a training overlay leaves the `TaskId` and every verdict unchanged.
- [ ] Each mirrored task file carries the `source` record of N24c-16's OPEN-ARENA-1 record; every scorecard and report
      on it carries the sentence "reward terms mirror ⟨source and version⟩; no score parity is claimed"; a report
      that states a score ratio against the public task without a Parity record is refused.
- [ ] The negative controls below are caught.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `requirement_parses_and_checks`: five atoms, one online signal, reach 20 s, coverage label `sampled`.
- `settling_window_semantics`: synthetic trace with a command change at 4.0 s and an error of 0.6 m/s until 4.8 s →
  satisfied; the same error persisting until just before 5.2 s → violated. The or-clause takes the larger of its
  two margins, so the binding atom is `settling`, with margin (1 − 1.18)/1 = −0.18 at 5.18 s, the last tick of the
  error: the report reads "the error outlasted the settling allowance by 0.18 s". At exactly 5.0 s the `settling`
  margin is 0 and the strict comparator makes it false.
- `tilt_violation_binds_upright`: tilt 33° at 7 s → violated, margin (30 − 33)/30 = −0.1, binding atom `upright`.
- `command_stream_is_time_keyed`: commands at control rates 50 Hz and 100 Hz are equal at every multiple of 4 s.
- `examples_admitted`: counts, near-boundary examples, kill matrix without vacuous hard-limit atoms.
- `overlay_keeps_task_id`.
- `mirrored_task_has_source_record`: the `terms` section's `source` names task, version and licence equal to the
  OPEN-ARENA-1 record; changing the version changes the `TaskId`.
- `mirrored_report_has_no_parity_sentence`: the baseline scorecard's report contains the sentence with the source
  and version.
- `score_ratio_without_parity_record_refused`: a report stating "x% of the public task's score" with no Parity
  record is refused under the evidence-wording rules.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/task_family_velocity_tracking.sh` (seed 1), for each task and reference robot: `frob task new
assets/tasks/velocity-tracking/<task>.ftask --key vt-new-<task> --json` and `frob task check <TaskId> --json`;
`frob task settle <TaskId> --model <robot> --key vt-settle-<task>-<robot> --json`; `frob eval run <robot> --battery
<baseline battery> --tier referee --key vt-ev-ref-<task>-<robot> --json` and the same with `--tier batch-cpu` and
the key `vt-ev-cpu-<task>-<robot>` (and each device tier present, with its own key); `frob eval scorecard <run>
--json` on each run; then the gate script with `--family` as a stage (its receipt hash is logged; the script
never runs `record_gate`). Asserts the checks, the presence of every metric with unit and n, and the gate result. NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/task_family_velocity_tracking/` with command, exit
code, wall time, evidence block (class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), the scorecard's outcome counts and
key metrics with units, on failure the first differing value with the run and task identities needed to
reproduce, and a final `summary` line with assertions passed and failed and GATE-ARENA-01.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answer for the requirement: the two synthetic traces of `settling_window_semantics` and the tilt case.
- Negative control: a success definition taken from the shaped reward (an exponential kernel of velocity error) is
  planted as an alternative verdict; changing a reward weight changes its "success", and the purity test of the
  verdict record must reject it.
- Negative control: a command stream keyed by control tick; the rate-doubling test must fail.
- Negative control from the training gate that uses this task: a zero-weight policy and a random policy must both
  fail the requirement on the baseline battery (they are recorded here as two of the violating examples'
  generators).
- Metamorphic relations: mirroring the robot and the commands on a symmetric quadruped leaves the scorecard
  unchanged within noise; scaling the unit system leaves margins unchanged.
- Determinism lanes: those of the gate script (twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; each
  backend against its class).

## Considerations

- The thresholds (0.25 m/s, 0.25 rad/s, 30°, the 1 s settling allowance, the 4 s command period, the command box)
  are proposals of this bead, to be reconciled with the public task definitions mirrored under OPEN-ARENA-1 before
  the examples are recorded. They are in the task file, so any change is a new `TaskId`.
- A strict "always within bounds" requirement fails at every command switch; the `settling` atom is the explicit,
  inspectable allowance for that, not a hidden grace period.
- The verdict is `sampled`; a tilt excursion is read from an interval-extremum channel, so it is not missed between
  ticks, but tracking error is a state sample.
- Performance context only: this task carries the parity rows against the incumbent (training time to threshold on
  one RTX 4090 at most 1.25 times the incumbent's); nothing here claims an advantage.
