## Background

M3 moves the robot indoors. Four families arrive together because the first comparison with a rival (the partial
ten-fold gate) needs missions in real kinds of places:

| Family | Example tasks | First milestone | Bead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interior navigation | Point goal through doors and thresholds in each archetype | M3 | this bead |
| Payload transport | Carry or tow a stated mass along a warehouse route, including a ramp | M3 | N24a-26 |
| Low-friction crossing | Cross a kitchen galley with a wet or greasy patch | M3 | N24a-27 |
| Tight-space maneuvering | Turn around, parallel shift and dock in an aisle of stated width | M3 | N24a-28 |

The places are the seven interior archetypes (home, apartment, office, warehouse, manufacturing plant, retail store,
commercial kitchen), each a typed attribute grammar Building → Floors → Rooms → Zones → Fixtures → Clutter with
dimensional constraints (door widths, aisle widths, counter heights, stair geometry) and a seed. Delivery is proxy
first: the structural proxy of each archetype (boxes and fixtures with exact functional clearances, doors, ramps,
thresholds, stairs, film patches, glass and mirror regions and a lighting level) lands at M3, and within M3 the
warehouse and the commercial kitchen come first, because the M3 scenarios need them.

The seed done-condition: GATE-ARENA-01 for these families, with reference-robot scorecards on the warehouse and
kitchen proxies. This bead builds interior navigation and closes the M3 set through the gate.

What interior navigation tests in a robot, by archetype: step and threshold crossing and narrow doors (home);
turning radius and docking at a lift-lobby sill (apartment); movable obstacles, carpet rolling resistance and door
opening (office); long aisles and ramps (warehouse); narrow aisles with movable carts (retail); swing doors and tight
galley turns (kitchen). A door is a one-joint articulated mechanism simulated by the same engine as the robot, with
its own parameter table (hinge axis, leaf width and mass, a closer torque law τ = τ₀ + k·θ + c·θ̇, latch state and
release force, stop angle); pushing it transfers momentum and work.

## Scope

In: point-goal navigation tasks on the warehouse and commercial-kitchen proxies (and, as their proxies land, on the
other five archetypes) for the legged and wheeled reference classes; the requirement; labelled examples; baselines
and scorecards; feasibility tags in the scorecard; the M3 run of the reproducibility gate for all four M3 families.

Out: the other three families' content (N24a-26 to N24a-28); the interior generators and hazard lists (Habitat
beads); clearance complexes and feasibility tags themselves (BD-234); the full batteries and GATE-ARENA-07
(BD-254); verdicts on batched tiers (BD-255).

## Technical approach

**1. Tasks.** `nav-warehouse` and `nav-kitchen` first; `nav-home`, `nav-apartment`, `nav-office`, `nav-plant`,
`nav-retail` when their proxies exist. Each binds a scene family of its archetype with a split grouped by building,
a start region and a point goal taken from the scene pack's route graph, and at least one door and one threshold on
the nominal route.

**2. Requirement** (DESIGN; thresholds are this bead's proposals, frozen by the `TaskId`):

```lisp
(requirements
  (atom upright    (>= (- 30 deg tilt) 0 deg)                 :scale 30 deg)
  (atom clear      (>  clearance_min 0 m)                     :scale 0.05 m)
  (atom no-strike  (=  body_strikes 0)                        :exact)
  (atom near-goal  (>= (- 0.25 m dist_to_goal) 0 m)           :scale 0.25 m)
  (atom settled    (>= (- 0.05 m/s base_speed) 0 m/s)         :scale 0.05 m/s)
  (online arrived  (H (0 s 1 s) (and near-goal settled)))
  (verdict navigated (U (0 s T) (and upright clear no-strike) arrived)))
```

`clearance_min` is the minimum distance between non-contact links and the scene (an interval extremum reduced by
`min`), so a strike between ticks is not missed; links designated to push doors are contact sites and do not count.
`body_strikes` counts contacts by non-designated links with fixtures and structure. The deadline T is declared per
task from the nominal route length L_route and the class's nominal speed v_nom: T = 2·L_route / v_nom rounded up to a
whole second (DESIGN), and the horizon equals T. Hard-limit atoms: `upright`, `clear`, `no-strike`.

**3. Randomization.** From the archetype's hazard registry rows (door closer torque, threshold height, floor
material and its friction card, clutter placement), each row with its provenance class, and from actuator and sensor
cards; every draw bound to a named stream keyed by the physical entity it perturbs (a door's closer by the fixture
identifier, floor friction by tile).

**4. Feasibility.** Generated scenes carry a feasibility tag for the nominal footprint, computed from the clearance
complex: a route witness, an impossibility cut, or unresolved. In the scorecard a scenario with an impossibility cut
for the candidate's footprint is `infeasible-for-candidate` with the bottleneck named (for example a doorway
narrower than the body); one impossible for every body of the class is `infeasible-for-class`. Both stay in the
all-scenario denominator (ADR-ARENA-09). The count of scenes in each feasibility class is part of the split
manifest.

**5. Labelled examples.** Per task at least two satisfying and two violating traces with near-boundary cases:
satisfying from the reference robot under its baseline controller through an open and a closed door; violating from
a shelf strike in an aisle, a stop 0.3 m short of the goal, and a tip at a threshold.

**6. Baselines and scorecards.** On the warehouse and kitchen proxies, for the legged and wheeled reference robots
under their baseline controllers: rows by archetype and route class; success with both denominators; failure-class
breakdown; requirement margin with binding atoms; time to verdict (restricted mean); both cost-of-transport figures;
clearance; fallback count and clean completions.

**7. Closing the M3 set.** `scripts/e2e/gate_arena_01_reproducibility.sh --family <id>` for interior navigation,
payload transport, low-friction crossing and tight-space maneuvering on every tier present; record the gate.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `requirement_checks`: five atoms, reach T, hard-limit set of three, coverage per atom.
- `deadline_from_route`: L_route = 18 m, v_nom = 0.6 m/s → T = 60 s.
- `arrives_and_dwells`: near goal and slow from 41.0 s → satisfied, decided at 42.0 s.
- `stops_short`: 0.30 m from the goal → violated, margin (0.25 − 0.30)/0.25 = −0.2, binding atom `near-goal`.
- `strike_between_ticks_is_seen`: clearance dips to −0.002 m for 4 ms between requirement ticks → violated, binding
  atom `clear`.
- `door_contact_by_designated_link_is_not_a_strike`.
- `infeasible_tag_maps_to_column`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/task_family_interior_navigation.sh`: `frob scene gen` (warehouse and kitchen proxies), `frob scene
accept`, `frob scene split`, `frob task check`, `frob task settle`, `frob eval run`, `frob eval scorecard --json`;
then the gate script per family. Asserts the checks, the scorecard column sums, the planted infeasible doorway's
column, and the gate. NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/task_family_interior_navigation/` with command, exit code, wall
time, evidence block, scene and split identities as digests, outcome counts, feasibility counts and the gate summary.
No geometry of a private scene is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers of the requirement: the three synthetic traces above.
- Adversarial-search known answer reused here: a doorway narrower than the body is `infeasible-for-candidate` with
  the bottleneck; negative control: an oracle that checks a point robot calls it solvable.
- Negative control: a scorer that reads clearance as a state sample at requirement ticks misses the 4 ms strike;
  the planted trace must fail that scorer.
- Negative control: a split keyed by `SceneId` alone accepts a layout and its mirror image across train and test;
  the gate's split step must refuse it.
- Metamorphic relations: a mirrored layout with the mirrored robot gives the mirrored result; a clutter variant of a
  scene belongs to the split group of its base scene.
- Determinism lanes: through the gate script.

## Considerations

- Moving people, pets and vehicles are Frontier scenario dressing and are not part of these tasks; no claim about
  human behaviour is made.
- The deadline rule T = 2·L_route / v_nom is a proposal; a task may declare its own T, and either way it is in the
  task file.
- Optical failure modes (glass, mirrors, steam) matter for navigation under estimated state; scorecards report the
  estimated-state twin beside the truth-state figures.
- The M3 partial ten-fold gate draws its legged and wheeled briefs from these families; nothing here is tuned to it.
