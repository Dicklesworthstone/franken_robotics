## Background

North-Star scenario NS-17, qualification that survives 500 adaptive retries. Before: an agent submits fifty
candidates against the same held-out scenes until one passes; each comparison is valid alone and the campaign is
not. After: `frob eval qualify` pre-commits the candidate, the baseline, the mission and the metrics; submission i
runs on fresh sealed world clusters at level α_i = α / [i·(i + 1)], a series that sums to α; the discovery lane
cannot read those worlds, and only the decision and an aggregate receipt come back until the campaign closes. The
gate behind the story drives the lane with 500 adaptive retries on worlds that carry no signal and requires a
false-promotion rate of at most α, with power preserved on planted gains.

Decision ADR-ARENA-08: discovery and confirmation are separate lanes with separate data rights, because an
optimizing agent that may resubmit to the same held-out worlds will eventually pass by chance.

Why the error rate holds (the argument the gate checks in practice). Because 1/[i·(i + 1)] = 1/i − 1/(i + 1), the
levels sum to α·m/(m + 1) after m requests and to α in the limit (proof obligation PO-ARENA-02). Request i tests a
candidate that the agent chose with everything it had seen before, on clusters that are independent of everything it
had seen before, so conditional on the past the chance of a false promotion at request i is at most α_i. A union
bound over requests gives a campaign-wide chance of any false promotion of at most α, for any number of retries and
any adaptive strategy. The argument needs three things and the lane enforces exactly those: the request is fixed
before the draw, the clusters are fresh, and the level is spent whether or not the agent likes the answer.

This seed bead is the measurement: GATE-QUAL-01.

## Scope

In: the three arms of the gate (no-signal, planted gain, broken-lane control); the exact statistics; the invariant
checks on every campaign's ledger; the end-to-end script; the gate record; the consequence if the gate fails.

Out: the lane (BD-259); the firewall gate GATE-QUAL-02 (the confirmation-firewall bead); offline replay of a
campaign, GATE-QUAL-03 (BD-383); the world families and the adversary (N24a-34).

## Technical approach

**1. The gate as registered (TARGET).** Over 2,000 simulated campaigns on no-signal worlds at α = 0.05, each with
500 adaptive retries by a scripted adversary that sees every receipt and all discovery data, a false promotion
occurs in at most 123 campaigns (an exact one-sided test of a rate at or below 0.05 rejects at the 1% level from
124). A planted true gain of the declared size is promoted within 10 requests in at least 80% of campaigns. Negative
control: the same adversary against reused clusters at a constant level obtains a false promotion in more than 99%
of campaigns. Earliest milestone M3. Claim: Known answer.

**2. Arm A, no signal.** 2,000 campaigns × 500 requests through the real qualification service in `frobd` under the
deterministic simulation runtime, on the family `synthetic:null-pairs/1` (every design has success probability 0.6;
any promotion is false). Each request follows the six steps of the protocol:

1. *Commit*: candidate, baseline, task, claim, study type and rule, tier plan, number of clusters and budget, hashed
   into the ledger with an idempotency key before any confirmation world exists; for a fixed-n study the
   pre-analysis is computed at this request's level and attached.
2. *Spend*: the request receives α_i = α / [i·(i + 1)] and the ledger records it; the index advances for every
   committed request, including ones that end inconclusive or are refused after commitment.
3. *Draw*: fresh world clusters from the sealed key, the campaign identifier, the index and a cluster counter; no
   cluster serves two requests and none has appeared in any discovery dataset.
4. *Run*: candidate and baseline paired on the clusters; the study runs at level α_i with clusters as units.
5. *Answer*: a receipt and nothing else: the decision, the index, the level spent, the number of units used and a
   hash.
6. *Close*: when the campaign closes, the key is revealed and the family is retired into discovery.

Study rule per request (DESIGN for the gate): the fixed-n paired exact rule of fixture 1 (two-sided exact
Clopper–Pearson intervals for the two discordance probabilities at the request's level, promotion iff the lower
bound of their difference exceeds the margin 0.05), with 256 clusters per request, the default family size. A
second pass repeats arm A with the anytime-valid study type, so that both study types are gated.

Statistic: F = the number of campaigns in which at least one request returned `promoted`. Pass iff F ≤ 123. The
threshold is recomputed, not trusted: the script evaluates the exact binomial tail P(X ≥ k) for X ~ Binomial(2000,
0.05) and confirms that k = 124 is the smallest count with tail probability at most 0.01.

**3. Arm B, planted gain.** 2,000 campaigns on `synthetic:planted-gain/1` with the declared discordance
(0.25, 0.02); the agent submits the truly better candidate at each request until promoted. Statistic: the fraction
of campaigns promoted at an index of at most 10. Pass iff it is at least 0.80. Context from the cost of retrying
(known answer KA-621): relative to one test at α = 0.05, request 1 needs about 1.23 times the units, request 10
about 2.57 times, request 100 about 4.08 times and request 500 about 5.15 times; for the rule of fixture 1 a
practical tie first becomes reachable at n = 99 for request 1, n = 178 for request 10 and n = 328 for request 500,
and at 64 pairs request 1 has 478 preferring outcomes of 2,145 where the development level had 521. Retrying is
allowed and is never free.

**4. Arm C, the negative control.** The same adversary against the broken lane (reused clusters, constant level α):
false promotion in more than 99% of 2,000 campaigns. If arm C does not exceed 99% the gate is void, whatever arms A
and B show: a gate that nothing can fail validates nothing.

**5. Invariants checked on every campaign's ledger.**

- INV-QUAL-01: discovery cannot read confirmation worlds (every peek by the adversary is refused with FROB-REF-840
  and logged).
- INV-QUAL-02: confirmation clusters are fresh: no cluster serves two requests, and none is in any discovery dataset
  before the campaign closes.
- INV-QUAL-03: the ledger of levels is append-only, the index advances by one for every committed request, and the
  total spent never exceeds α (after m requests it equals α·m/(m + 1) exactly, in rational arithmetic).
- INV-QUAL-04: a request's commitment precedes the derivation of its clusters in ledger order.
- Metamorphic relation MR-13: the decision of request i is unchanged by later requests (re-running a campaign
  truncated at i reproduces decisions 1…i).

**6. Record.** F, the planted-gain fraction, the control fraction, the per-code refusal counts, the exact threshold
computation, seeds, commit and hardware fingerprint; entered by a session that did not implement the lane.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-QUAL-01 (the seed done-condition): F ≤ 123 of 2,000 no-signal campaigns with 500 adaptive retries each at
      α = 0.05; the planted gain is promoted within 10 requests in at least 80% of campaigns; the broken-lane control
      exceeds 99% (TARGET values; measured values recorded).
- [ ] The four invariants hold in every one of the 4,000 real-lane campaigns; one violation fails the gate.
- [ ] The partial sums of levels equal α·m/(m + 1) exactly for every m up to 500 in every campaign.
- [ ] Both study types pass arm A.
- [ ] Receipts contain only the listed fields; nothing per world is returned.
- [ ] Cancellation and injected crashes between protocol steps leave one request, one level spent and one receipt
      per idempotency key.
- [ ] The gate is reproducible from its seeds: a second run gives the same F.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `binomial_threshold_is_124`: exact tail of Binomial(2000, 0.05): P(X ≥ 124) ≤ 0.01 < P(X ≥ 123).
- `level_series_partial_sums`: exact rationals, m = 1…500; for m = 7: 0.05·7/8; the request level at i = 7 is
  0.05/56.
- `union_bound_holds_on_small_enumeration`: for a 3-request campaign with per-request false-promotion probabilities
  exactly α_i (a synthetic rule with randomized acceptance), the enumerated campaign false-promotion probability is
  at most α·3/4.
- `index_advances_on_refused_after_commit`.
- `decision_of_request_i_independent_of_later_requests`: truncation replay (MR-13).
- `retry_cost_table`: sample-size inflation ln-based figures 1.23, 2.57, 4.08, 5.15 at requests 1, 10, 100, 500 to
  the printed digits, from the ratio of critical values at α_i and at 0.05 for the rule in use.
- `receipt_fields_closed_set`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/gate_qual_01_retries.sh`:

1. Start `frobd` under the simulation runtime; `frob doctor`.
2. Arm A: for each campaign seed, open a campaign (with `qual:admin`), run the adversary's 500 requests through
   `frob eval qualify --json`, read `frob eval ledger --json`, close the campaign.
3. Arm A again with the anytime-valid study type.
4. Arm B on the planted-gain family.
5. Arm C against the broken lane.
6. Compute F, the fractions and the exact threshold; assert.

Seed-explicit, resumable by campaign index, idempotent. Logging through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/gate_qual_01_retries/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it: per campaign one line with the
exact command lines used, exit codes, wall time, the evidence block (marked `synthetic`), the index of the first
promotion (or none), refusal counts by code, the final level spent as an exact rational, and the four invariant
checks; `metric` lines for F against 123, the planted-gain fraction against 0.80 and the control fraction against
0.99; on failure the campaign seed, the request index and the ledger rows needed to reproduce. The final `summary`
line gives assertion counts and names GATE-QUAL-01. No sealed address or outcome is written to the log.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-622: 2,000 simulated campaigns on no-signal worlds at α = 0.05, each with 500 adaptive retries: a false
  promotion in at most 123 campaigns; a planted true gain promoted within 10 requests in at least 80% of campaigns.
  Negative control: reused clusters at a constant level give false promotion in more than 99% of campaigns.
- KA-619 (level series, exact partial sums; negative control: a constant level per request); KA-620 (fourteen null
  submissions at 5% give 51%); KA-621 (cost of a retry).
- CX-267 (resubmit candidates to the same held-out scenes until one passes).
- Metamorphic relation MR-13 (qualification under adaptive retries): the campaign-wide false-promotion rate stays at
  most α for any number of retries and any adaptive strategy; the decision of request i is unchanged by later
  requests; mutation that must break it: reused clusters at a constant level.
- Determinism lanes: the run is repeated on the second machine; F and every ledger digest must be identical (the
  simulation runtime and the integer generators are exact).

## Considerations

- OPEN-ARENA-3 (owner: project lead; blocks GATE-QUAL-01): the size and composition of the sealed world families,
  and who holds `qual:admin`. Fail-closed default: the owner alone opens and closes campaigns; one sealed family per
  task family, sized for 20 requests at 256 clusters; no result is called qualified until a family exists. The
  simulated gate can run on the synthetic families before the decision; real campaigns cannot.
- Stop decision written at the milestone: if GATE-QUAL-01 fails, nothing is called qualified, and confirmation falls
  back to fixed-n exact studies with a hard cap on submissions before any tournament is scheduled.
- RISK-34 (qualification is gamed by adaptive retries or by a leak from sealed worlds; trigger: a receipt field
  outside the listed set, requests without a close) and the pre-mortem's story "qualification was gamed" are what
  this gate, the firewall gates and campaign replay by the offline auditor answer together. Residual trust:
  whoever holds `qual:admin`.
- F is expected well below 100: exact tests are conservative and the levels sum to α·500/501. A value near 123
  would itself be a signal to investigate.
- Cost: 4,000 real-lane campaigns × 500 requests is 2 × 10⁶ requests of synthetic episodes; with 256 clusters each
  about 5 × 10⁸ pair draws: minutes of CPU for the draws, and the service time per request dominates.
