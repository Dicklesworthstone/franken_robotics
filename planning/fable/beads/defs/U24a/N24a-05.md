## Background

The composition rules of the requirement language are minima and maxima. A minimum of a clearance in metres and a
thermal margin in kelvins has no meaning: which one is "smaller" depends on the unit system. Decision ADR-ARENA-03
therefore requires that every atom states an inequality *and* the physical scale that makes its margin dimensionless.
The scale is a judgment ("a clearance shortfall of 0.10 m matters about as much as a thermal shortfall of 10 K") and
it is recorded in the task file, where it can be reviewed and where changing it changes the `TaskId`.

Counterexample for composition without scales (hand-checkable; known answer KA-594, counterexample CX-259). A
clearance margin of 0.02 m and a thermal margin of 3 K compose to a raw minimum of 0.02, so clearance binds. Express
the clearance in millimetres and the raw minimum is 3, so the thermal margin binds. With scales of 0.10 m and 10 K
the margins are 0.2 and 0.3 in either unit system, and clearance binds in both. The binding atom of a requirement is
what an agent acts on (it is the condition the next design edit targets), so it must not depend on the unit system.

This bead is the checker that makes the property true by construction: it rejects an expression that adds or
compares different dimensions, rejects an atom with no scale, verifies that the scale has the dimension of the
compared expression, and produces the normalized, dimensionless atom values that every evaluator consumes.

## Scope

In: dimension inference over `Expr`; the scale rule; `:exact` atoms; conversion of every quantity to canonical SI
values in exact rational arithmetic before any floating-point evaluation; the normalizer that turns an atom into
"dimensionless margin = (difference)/scale"; refusals FROB-REF-520 and FROB-REF-535 with ranked repairs.

Out: clocks and reductions (N24a-06); the margin algebra above the atom (N24a-04); the verb `task.check` that
surfaces these refusals to an agent (BD-122).

## Technical approach

1. **Signals carry units.** A signal is a telemetry channel with a unit, a channel kind and a clock; its unit comes
   from the task's `signals` section and must agree with the channel's registered unit in Chronicle's channel
   schema. A mismatch is FROB-REF-535 with both units printed.
2. **Dimension inference** over the expression grammar, using the dimension algebra of `frob_core::qty` (seven SI
   base exponents plus angle as a tracked pseudo-dimension, so that `deg` and `rad` convert and an angle is not
   silently added to a pure number):
   - `Signal` and `Qty`: their declared dimension.
   - `+`, `-`, `min`, `max`: all operands must have one dimension; the result has it.
   - `*`: exponents add.
   - `abs`: the operand's dimension.
   - `(delay d x)`: d must have dimension time; the result has the dimension of x.
   Any violation is FROB-REF-535 with a pointer to the offending subexpression and the two dimensions.
3. **The comparison.** Both sides of `Cmp` must have one dimension D.
4. **The scale rule.**
   - `:scale s`: s must be a strictly positive quantity of dimension D. A missing scale on a non-exact atom is
     FROB-REF-520. A scale of the wrong dimension is FROB-REF-535. A zero or negative scale is refused as
     FROB-REF-520 (the atom has no usable scale).
   - `:exact`: admitted only when both sides are integer-valued: every signal it reads is registered with an
     integer quantization (a count, an event count, a cumulative integer counter, or a state-kind flag or mode
     index such as fixture 1's `drive_saturated`, which is 0 or 1), and literals are integers. The margin is +1 or
     −1. `:exact` on a real-valued signal is FROB-REF-520 with the repair "declare a scale".
   - A temperature *margin* is a difference, so its scale is written in kelvin; an absolute temperature compared with
     an absolute temperature has dimension temperature and the same rule applies.
5. **Normalization.** Each atom compiles to a pair (difference expression in canonical SI units, scale in the same
   units). Unit conversion factors are exact rationals (1 mm = 1/1000 m; 1 deg = π/180 rad is the one irrational
   factor: for angle atoms both the difference and the scale are converted with the same factor, so the ratio is
   computed in the authored unit and is exact). The margin is difference/scale. Because both are converted by the
   same exact factor, the margin is independent of the authored unit; for power-of-two rescalings it is
   bit-identical, and for other factors it is identical when computed in the canonical unit, which is the only unit
   the evaluator ever sees.
6. **Ranked repairs** attached to each refusal (the envelope's `refusal.repairs`):
   - FROB-REF-520: (1) add `:scale <qty>` of the atom's dimension, with a suggested value equal to the magnitude of
     the atom's threshold literal where one exists (for `(>= (- 5 deg tilt) 0 deg)` suggest `5 deg`); (2) declare
     `:exact` if the signal is an integer count.
   - FROB-REF-535: (1) the conversion that would make the dimensions agree, if the mismatch is a unit of the same
     dimension written inconsistently; (2) otherwise the two dimensions and the subexpression to rewrite.
7. **Outputs.** `CheckedAtom { id, diff: KirExpr /* canonical units */, scale: f64, cmp, exact: bool, reads:
   Vec<SignalId> }`. The dimension check itself is exact arithmetic on integer exponents: determinism class D0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The two-atom example evaluates to margins 0.2 and 0.3 with the clearance binding, whether the clearance is
      authored in metres or in millimetres (KA-594).
- [ ] A planted task file with an atom lacking both scale and `:exact` is refused with FROB-REF-520; one that adds a
      length to a temperature inside an atom, one that compares a length with a time, and one whose scale is in
      seconds for a length atom are each refused with FROB-REF-535 (the unit rows of GATE-ARENA-03).
- [ ] Every atom of fixture 1 checks: `cleared` (scale 0.10 m), `upright` (5 deg), `tilt-ok` (35 deg), `current-ok`
      (6 A), `advancing` (0.002 m), and the two `:exact` atoms `no-strike` and `saturated`.
- [ ] Each refusal carries a pointer, both dimensions, and at least one ranked repair; exit code 3.
- [ ] Rescaling every authored unit of a task by a power of two (lengths × 4, times × 2) leaves every margin
      bit-identical on 10⁴ random traces.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `normalization_example`: atoms `(>= clearance 0 m) :scale 0.10 m` at 0.02 m and `(>= thermal 0 K) :scale 10 K` at
  3 K → 0.2 and 0.3; `min` = 0.2; binding atom `clearance`. Re-authored with `20 mm` and `:scale 100 mm` → the same
  bits.
- `raw_composition_is_unit_dependent` (negative control, kept as a test of the test): the raw minimum is 0.02 in
  metres and 3 in millimetres, and its argmin changes.
- `missing_scale_is_520`, `zero_scale_is_520`, `exact_on_real_signal_is_520`.
- `exact_on_integer_flag_admitted`: `(= drive_saturated 1) :exact` over a 0/1 state flag and `(= strikes 0) :exact`
  over a count both check; `(= tilt 0 deg) :exact` over a real-valued angle is FROB-REF-520.
- `add_length_and_temperature_is_535`, `compare_length_with_time_is_535`, `scale_wrong_dimension_is_535`,
  `delay_with_length_is_535`, `signal_unit_disagrees_with_channel_is_535`.
- `angle_is_tracked`: `(- 5 deg tilt)` with `tilt` in radians checks and converts; `(+ tilt 1)` (angle plus pure
  number) is refused.
- `product_dimensions`: `(* force distance)` has dimension energy; comparing it with a quantity in joules checks.
- `repairs_present`: every refusal above has a non-empty `repairs` list whose first entry parses as a valid edit.
- `power_of_two_rescale_is_bit_identical`: 10⁴ traces.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/spec_units.sh [--seed S]` (owned by this bead; default seed 2405; idempotent; sources the STD-E2E
helper). `task.check` comes after this bead (BD-122), so the script drives the checker through its tests, the
Gauntlet row and one example program on this bead's own dependencies (the task reader of N24a-02). Steps:

1. `unit`: `FROB_TEST_SEED=$SEED cargo test -p frob-spec units::` (the unit tests above, including the power-of-two
   rescaling on 10⁴ traces).
2. `known-answer`: `e2e_tier g1_known_answers KA-594`, then `e2e_tier g1_known_answers KA-594 --mutations` (the
   CX-259 control, raw composition, must be caught); the receipt hash is logged.
3. `planted`: `cargo run -p frob-spec --example unit_check -- <file> --json` on the five planted canonical task files
   under `crates/frob-spec/tests/planted/units/` (no scale and no `:exact` → FROB-REF-520; a length plus a
   temperature, a length compared with a time, a length atom scaled in seconds, a delay given in metres →
   FROB-REF-535): each exits 3 with its code, the pointer, both dimensions and at least one ranked repair. On the
   golden `kitchen-tray.ftask.json` the example exits 0 and prints every atom with its scale in canonical units.

NDJSON under `target/e2e-logs/spec_units/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N24a-05`), `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time, the refusal block (`code`,
`pointer`, `observed`, `required`, `unit`, repairs), the two margins of KA-594 against 0.2 and 0.3 with the binding
atom, and on failure the first differing atom with the file path; a final `summary` line naming GATE-ARENA-03
(its unit rows are exercised here and never recorded here). The same refusals through `frob task check` are the `units-*` steps
of `scripts/e2e/arena_requirement_pipeline.sh` (N24a-42), a script with steps of its own that does not run this
one (its one stage is BD-121's campaign script).

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-594 (normalization): margins 0.2 and 0.3 in either unit system; the clearance binds in metres and millimetres;
  tolerance exact. Its negative control: composition of raw margins (0.02 in metres, 3 with the clearance in
  millimetres).
- CX-259 (compose raw requirement margins with a minimum) seeds a negative-control registry entry attached to
  GATE-ARENA-03: a build of the normalizer that skips the division by the scale must fail `normalization_example`.
- Related construction-level case (C-30): a clearance of 0.02 m and a thermal margin of 15 K have minimum 0.02 in
  metres and kelvin and 15 in millimetres and kelvin; with scales 0.10 m and 10 K the values are 0.2 and 1.5 and
  clearance binds in every unit system. Kept as a second fixture.
- Metamorphic relation MR-19 (unit normalization of requirements): a change of units leaves normalized robustness
  and the binding atom unchanged; the mutation that must break it is composition of raw margins.
- Determinism: dimension checking is D0; the tests run in debug and release with identical refusals.

## Considerations

- A scale is a human judgment. The checker enforces its presence and dimension, never its value; `task.check`
  prints every scale beside its atom so a reviewer sees them together.
- An atom may read several signals on different clocks; this bead does not look at clocks. An atom that passes here
  can still be refused by N24a-06 (FROB-REF-525).
- The degree-to-radian factor is irrational; the design avoids it by forming the ratio before conversion. When one
  difference mixes degrees and radians (as `(- 5 deg tilt)` does when the `tilt` channel is registered in radians,
  the case of `angle_is_tracked`), the channel value is converted into the authored angle unit in double precision,
  one rounding per sample; the unit-rescaling relation then holds to that rounding and not bit for bit (margins
  from telemetry are D2 in either case). The checker records which case applied, and the bit-identity criterion
  above is asserted only on atoms whose conversions are all rational.
- Risk: authors pick scales to make a preferred atom bind. Mitigation is review plus the labelled examples near the
  boundary of each comparator (N24a-12), which pin what the requirement means regardless of scale.
