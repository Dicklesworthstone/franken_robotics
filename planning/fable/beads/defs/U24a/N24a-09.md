## Background

Operators that look forwards (always G, eventually F, until U) cannot be evaluated while an episode runs without
silently truncating their windows, so they are evaluated on a completed trace (ADR-ARENA-04). That evaluation is the
verdict: the unit that scorecards, studies and searches count. This bead builds the production evaluator for it, on
the CPU, in two passes over the recorded trace.

Three behaviours distinguish it from a textbook robustness computation, and each exists because of a specific way
that evaluation harnesses lie:

1. **Reach against horizon.** The reach of a formula, the sum of its nested window ends, is computed statically; a
   task whose episode horizon is shorter than the reach of its verdict is refused at admission (FROB-REF-526). A
   requirement that cannot be decided inside the episode it is attached to would otherwise be reported forever as
   "not violated".
2. **Undetermined, never truncated.** A window that the recorded trace does not cover, because the episode was
   truncated or samples are missing, yields `undetermined` with the reason, never a value from the part that exists
   (INV-ARENA-02). The standard failure is a G over 45 s evaluated on a 30 s trace and reported as satisfied.
3. **Decided early.** A verdict whose truth is already fixed by a prefix, as when an always-clause is violated or an
   until-clause is met, is reported as decided at the tick that fixed it. That is how an environment ends an episode
   on success or on a hard violation without truncating a window: the short trace still carries a complete verdict.

## Scope

In: static reach; refusal FROB-REF-526; the forward pass (atoms and past-time subformulas); the backward pass
(future-time operators); interval semantics for unknown values; the three outcomes `satisfied`, `violated`,
`undetermined` with reason; `decided_at`; the binding atom and time; per-atom minima; agreement with the definitional
evaluator.

Out: the boundary guard and exact re-evaluation at ρ = 0 (N24a-10); the verdict record, the fourth value `invalid`
and storage (N24a-11); the online decision signal used for early termination (N24a-13); the verb (BD-122).

## Technical approach

**Static reach (in ticks of the requirement clock).**

    reach(past-time formula) = 0
    reach(¬φ) = reach(φ);   reach(φ ∧ ψ) = reach(φ ∨ ψ) = max(reach φ, reach ψ)
    reach(G_[a,b] φ) = reach(F_[a,b] φ) = b + reach(φ)
    reach(φ U_[a,b] ψ) = b + max(reach φ, reach ψ)

The verdict is evaluated at tick 0. If reach · T exceeds the task's horizon the task is refused with FROB-REF-526
(equality is admitted: fixture 1 has reach 8 s and horizon 8 s; the kitchen-tray task reach 20 s and horizon 22 s).
Past-time operators add nothing to reach; they look backwards and are governed by the pre-origin rule of the
language. Ranked repairs: (1) lengthen the horizon to the reach, printed in seconds; (2) the window whose end
contributes most, with the bound that would fit.

**Values are intervals.** Each node at each tick carries [lo, hi], the range of margins consistent with what the
trace holds; a known value has lo = hi. A missing atom sample, or any tick after the last recorded tick, is
[−∞, +∞]. Operators act on intervals monotonically: min and max componentwise; negation maps [lo, hi] to [−hi, −lo].
Pre-origin values are known (false, −∞) by the language's rule and are not intervals.

**Pass 1, forward in time.** Normalized atom values are read from the recorded channels through the declared
reductions (N24a-06). Past-time subformulas, including every `online` signal, are evaluated with the streaming
deque monitor (N24a-08), extended to intervals node by node: a minimum or maximum node is run once on its
operands' lo arrays and once on their hi arrays (both are monotone), and a negation node swaps and negates the two.
Negations are evaluated where they stand and are not pushed to the atoms, because the duality between H and O does
not hold at ticks governed by the pre-origin rule. Where the run recorded online signals, pass 1 compares its recomputation with the recording and reports a
mismatch as an integrity fault, not as a verdict.

**Pass 2, backward in time.** Future-time nodes are evaluated from the last tick towards tick 0.

- G_[a,b] and F_[a,b]: a monotonic deque run backwards gives the sliding minimum or maximum over [t + a, t + b] in
  O(1) amortized per tick.
- φ U_[a,b] ψ, by the mirror of the since decomposition (a lattice identity, exact for minima and maxima):

      φ U_[a,b] ψ  =  min( F_[a,b] ψ ,  min( min of ρ(φ, t″) over t″ ∈ [t, t + a) ,  R(t + a) ) ),
      R(s) = max( ρ(ψ, s), min( ρ(φ, s), R(s + 1) ) ),    R = [−∞, +∞] beyond the last recorded tick
      (and −∞ beyond the horizon, where no candidate exists).

- Each is computed on lo and on hi. Total cost O(T) per node; a 600 s window at 50 Hz (30,000 samples) is routine.

**Deciding.** Let [lo, hi] be the verdict's interval at tick 0 and B its Boolean value where defined.

| Condition | Verdict | Reported margin |
|---|---|---|
| lo > 0 | `satisfied` | lo if lo < hi (a guaranteed lower bound), else the exact margin |
| hi < 0 | `violated` | hi if lo < hi (a guaranteed upper bound), else the exact margin |
| lo = hi = 0 | decided by the declared comparators at the boundary (N24a-10) | 0 |
| otherwise | `undetermined` | none; the reason lists the node path, the missing tick range and the cause (`truncated` or `missing-samples`) |

DESIGN (this bead's elaboration): when a verdict is decided on a partial trace the reported margin is the
conservative bound, the one closest to zero that every continuation respects; `coverage.missing_samples` counts the
missing atom samples inside the reach.

**Decided-at.** `decided_at` is the smallest tick d such that the verdict is already decided when every sample after
d is treated as unknown. Decidedness is monotone in d (more data never un-decides a verdict), so d is found by
bisection over prefix lengths with O(log T) interval evaluations. For a fully covered trace whose verdict needed its
whole reach, d is the reach.

**Binding atom, binding time and per-atom minima.** The witness chain of the definitional evaluator is reproduced:
(atom, tick) attaining the reported margin, ties to the earliest tick and then to declaration order. `atoms_min`
records, for each atom, its minimum margin over the ticks the verdict read (for fixture 1's worked verdict:
`cleared` −0.31, `upright` 0.84).

**Purity and determinism.** Inputs: the trace's atom channels and the checked requirement. No reward, no controller
state, no wall-clock time (INV-ARENA-01). Sequential double precision on the CPU: class D2; where every verdict atom
is exact, D0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On fully covered traces the evaluator equals the definitional evaluator bit for bit (margin, Boolean, binding
      witness) on 10⁴ random (formula, trace) pairs per future-time operator; the 10⁶ run is BD-121.
- [ ] A planted task whose horizon is shorter than the reach of its verdict is refused with FROB-REF-526 and the
      repair names the horizon needed (a reach row of GATE-ARENA-03).
- [ ] An episode that ends at 30 s under G over [0, 45 s] with every recorded value positive returns `undetermined`
      with cause `truncated` and ticks 30 s to 45 s named; the same episode with one negative value at 12 s returns
      `violated`, decided at 12 s.
- [ ] A trace with a dropped telemetry interval inside a verdict window returns `undetermined` with cause
      `missing-samples`, unless the verdict is decided regardless of the gap.
- [ ] Fixture 1 shape: on a run that docks at 5.2 s the verdict is `satisfied`, decided at 5.2 s; on a run with a
      chassis strike at 2.0 s it is `violated`, decided at 2.0 s with binding atom `no-strike`; on a run that never
      docks it is `violated`, decided at 8 s.
- [ ] Recomputed online signals equal the recorded ones on the fixture runs.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `reach_rules`: `(U (0 s 8 s) hard dwell)` with `dwell` past-time → 8 s; `(G (0 s 10 s) (F (0 s 5 s) a))` → 15 s;
  `(and (F (0 s 3 s) a) (G (2 s 7 s) b))` → 7 s.
- `horizon_shorter_than_reach_is_526`; `horizon_equal_to_reach_admitted`.
- `truncation_control`: the 30 s / 45 s case above, both variants.
- `interval_arithmetic`: min([1, 3], [−∞, +∞]) = [−∞, 3]; max([1, 3], [−∞, +∞]) = [1, +∞]; ¬[−2, 5] = [−5, 2].
- `until_backward_matches_definition`: 10⁴ random pairs, windows up to [8, 24] ticks.
- `decided_at_always`: G over [0, 9] ticks, values [1, 1, 1, −2, 1, …] → violated, decided at tick 3, reported
  margin −2 (an upper bound that later samples cannot raise).
- `decided_at_eventually`: F over [0, 9], values [−1, −1, 4, …] → satisfied, decided at tick 2, margin 4 (a lower
  bound).
- `decided_at_until`: φ = [1, 1, 1, 1], ψ = [−1, −1, 2, −1], window [0, 3] → satisfied at tick 2 with margin
  min(2, min(1, 1)) = 1.
- `decided_is_monotone`: for 10³ random pairs, decidedness as a function of prefix length never goes from decided
  to undecided.
- `gap_inside_window`: unknown sample at tick 4 under G over [0, 9]; all others positive → `undetermined`; with a
  negative at tick 6 → `violated`.
- `atoms_min_and_witness`: fixture-shaped synthetic trace → expected map and witness.
- `online_recording_mismatch_is_fault`: a recorded online channel altered by one bit → integrity fault.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Construction C-30 truncation control: an episode that ends at 30 s under G over [0, 45 s] returns `undetermined`.
- KA-596 (online and offline agreement), exact; negative control: a monitor that pads a short window.
- Metamorphic relation MR-20: G over [0, T] at tick 0 equals H over [0, T] at tick T on fully covered traces.
- Negative control (seed of a registry entry for GATE-ARENA-03): an evaluator that clamps a future window to the end
  of the trace must fail `truncation_control` by returning `satisfied`.
- Negative control: an evaluator that reports the midpoint of [lo, hi] as the margin of a decided-early verdict must
  fail `decided_at_always`.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; both platforms; bit comparison of the verdict record.

## Considerations

- The reach rule for U adds the window end to the larger operand reach; for a future-time left operand this is
  conservative by one tick and can refuse a task whose horizon is exactly sufficient. The repair message says so.
- The interval treatment evaluates negation in place. A rewrite to negation normal form would be wrong at the start
  of an episode: ¬H is true while H cannot yet hold, whereas O of the negated operand ranges over existing ticks.
- `undetermined` is a real outcome with its own scorecard column; it is never coerced to a failure or a pass.
- A numerical fault during the episode is not this bead's `undetermined`: it becomes the verdict value `invalid`
  (N24a-11), which is counted separately.
- OPEN-NEMESIS-2 concerns margins that are flat until shortly before failure; nothing here smooths a margin.
