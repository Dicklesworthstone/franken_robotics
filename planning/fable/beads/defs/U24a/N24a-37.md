## Background

A design loop that cannot say where the energy went cannot compare two robots on efficiency, and a simulator that
cannot say where energy came from will sooner or later hand a policy energy that no actuator supplied. The record
subsystem (Chronicle, crate `frob-chronicle`) therefore keeps an energy ledger for every world and episode
(goal GOAL-11: energy accounting that closes), and `telemetry.energy` is the read verb through which an agent, a
metric or a gate reads it. The verb is one of the four read verbs bundled with the failure taxonomy (seed BD-094);
the ledger itself (its accumulation on the referee, stored heat, braking modes, the first offending event) is the
work of the referee-ledger bead BD-092, and the batched ledger and the created-energy audit are BD-208. This bead is
the view: the schema, the breakdowns, the rules that keep the view from lying, and the display of the audit flags.

Three rules of the ledger are also rules of the view, and each exists because the shortcut is tempting:

- *The numerical residual is its own line.* It is reported as "integrator and solver" and is never folded into a
  physical term. A view that distributes the residual over friction and damping looks tidier and hides exactly the
  number that says whether the tier can be trusted (invariant INV-CHRON-09: the lines of the energy ledger, including
  the residual line, sum to zero; no physical term absorbs the residual).
- *Regeneration is never netted against draw without saying so.* Bus power is integrated with its positive and
  negative parts separately.
- *Energy per task is printed two ways.* The mean over completed episodes, and the total over all attempts divided
  by completions. A design that succeeds once for 10 J and fails once for 20 J reports 10 J and 30 J; a rival with two
  12 J successes reports 12 J and 12 J; the first figure alone would rank them wrongly.

Rejected alternative: one "efficiency" scalar per run. It cannot be decomposed, cannot show a residual, and invites
the conditional-mean error above.

## Scope

In: the verb `telemetry.energy` (read; M1); its input and output schema `frob.telemetry.energy/1`; the line list;
breakdowns by actuator, joint, limb and stance or swing; the decomposition of cost of transport along the same
lines; the two per-task figures; the residual statement on every figure; the first offending event; the display of
the three created-energy flags; cross-world aggregation that is order-independent; refusals.

Out: accumulating the ledger (BD-092 on the referee, BD-208 on batched tiers); the audit monitors themselves and
the verb `fidelity.energy` (BD-208); the scorecard metrics that consume this verb (N24a-18); the typing of a
comparison as `model-conditional` when its advantage coincides with flagged episodes (the analysis and comparison
beads of the design-studio subsystem).

## Technical approach

**1. The identity the view prints.** For each world and episode, over the queried interval:

```
battery energy out        = Σ over actuators (copper loss + friction loss + gear loss + Δ stored)
                            + mechanical work at joints
mechanical work at joints = Δ kinetic + Δ potential
                            + contact dissipation (normal damping + sliding friction)
                            + joint damping + work on fixtures and payloads
                            + algorithmic exchange + numerical residual
```

Every term is a port quantity: the power crossing a typed port of the composed design (effort × flow), integrated
over the step. Terms are accumulated by the step in double-float (a pair of doubles) and stored on the Energetics
stream. Definitions used by the view:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `battery_out` | Decrease of chemical energy of the pack |
| `copper`, `friction`, `gear` | Actuator losses, per actuator: i²·R, bearing and seal friction, gear mesh loss |
| `stored` | Increase of magnetic, rotor-kinetic, elastic, polarization and thermal stored energy. Heat that is stored in a thermal node is stored energy: it is listed for attribution and is not added again as a loss |
| `heat_to_ambient` | Heat leaving thermal nodes to the surroundings |
| `joint_work` | Mechanical work at the joints, Σ τ·ω·Δt |
| `d_kinetic`, `d_potential` | Change of kinetic and gravitational potential energy of the bodies |
| `contact_normal_damping`, `contact_sliding_friction` | Constitutive dissipation at contacts: ∫ c·ḋ² dt and ∫ f_t·v_slip dt |
| `joint_damping` | Dissipation in joint dampers and limits by their own laws |
| `fixture_payload_work` | Work done on fixtures and payloads (and by moving surfaces on the robot, with its sign) |
| `algorithmic_exchange` | Energy that an impulse-level constraint step removes from, or returns to, the unconstrained velocity predictor. Not heat |
| `numerical_residual` | "Integrator and solver": what remains after every other line |
| `bus_positive`, `bus_negative` | ∫ max(0, Σ vⱼ·iⱼ) dt and ∫ min(0, Σ vⱼ·iⱼ) dt over the drives |
| braking modes | `motoring`, `regeneration_to_battery`, `shunt_dissipation`, `clipped_braking`, as separate lines |

**2. Input.** A run (or a set of runs), an optional world and episode filter, an optional time window `[t0, t1]` in
simulated seconds (exact rationals), `--by actuator|joint|limb|phase` (phase = stance or swing of the limb the joint
belongs to), `--per-task`, `--cot`, a token budget.

**3. Output (`frob.telemetry.energy/1`).** `lines`: each line with value (joules), unit, the count of worlds and
steps it sums over; `closure`: the sum of all lines, which must be zero by construction (see 5); `residual`:
absolute value, relative value r_rel = |numerical_residual| / Σ |line| over the exchanged lines, the tier's bound
(TARGET: 10⁻⁹ relative on the referee, 10⁻³ on single-precision tiers), and `within_bound`; `first_offending`: when
a step's residual exceeds its tolerance, the earliest step and the element (port) whose local balance failed, not
only the episode total; `breakdown`: the requested table; `cot`; `per_task`; `flags`; `frontier`; the envelope's
evidence block.

**4. Breakdowns and cost of transport.** Every breakdown row sums to its parent line exactly (same quantized
integers). Cost of transport is energy / (m·g·d) with m total mass, g the scene's gravity magnitude and d the
distance travelled by the base along its path; it is printed twice, from mechanical work at the joints and from
electrical energy drawn from the battery, and decomposes along the breakdown: for example "38 of 100 J: copper loss
in the knees during stance". A share is always printed as "k of n" with its unit beside any percentage. With d = 0
the cost of transport is `null` with reason `no-distance`, never infinity.

**5. Closure and order independence (DESIGN).** For cross-world and cross-step sums the view does not add floating
values in arrival order. Each stored line value is quantized once to an integer count of a declared quantum
(default 1 nJ, stored as a 128-bit integer in the JSON as a decimal string), and sums are integer sums, so a total
is identical under any permutation of worlds, shard split and merge order (invariant INV-CHRON-06). The
`numerical_residual` line of an aggregate is defined as minus the integer sum of every other line, so `closure` is
exactly zero and the residual carries every rounding; nothing else may absorb it.

**6. Energy per task.** With `--per-task`: `mean_conditional_on_completion` = (Σ energy of completed episodes) /
completions; `total_per_completion` = (Σ energy of all attempts) / completions. Both are printed with their counts
("1 of 2 completed"). With zero completions both are `null` with reason `no-completions`.

**7. Created-energy flags (display).** For each world the view shows the three audit flags and the quantities
behind them; thresholds are TARGET values owned by the audit:

| Flag | Quantity | Raised when |
|---|---|---|
| `energy-created` | r = Δ kinetic + Δ potential − (actuator work + external work − all dissipation) per step; created energy = Σ max(0, r) over a window | Created energy above 1% of the absolute work exchanged in any 0.5 s window on a fast tier; above 10⁻⁶ relative on the referee |
| `contact-active` | Net work a contact does on the bodies over one contact episode, touch to release | Net positive work above the tier's residual bound |
| `cone-excess` | Friction utilization ‖f_t‖ / (μ·f_n) per contact | Above 1.05 for more than 1% of stance time (a pyramidal cone admits up to √2 along its diagonal) |

A flagged episode keeps its result and gains the flag; the view never filters it. At M1 the flags are shown for
referee runs; batched tiers show them once the audit bead lands, and until then the field reads `not-run`.

**8. Two lines for two different things.** `algorithmic_exchange` and constitutive dissipation are printed
separately on every tier. On compliant tiers the algorithmic exchange of a resting contact is zero, because the
contact force is a function of state; the line matters on the referee's impulse step and on a hard-contact batch
law. The view marks which lines may feed a thermal or wear state (constitutive dissipation and electrical loss
only); the binding rule itself is enforced by the ledger.

**9. Refusals and absences.** A figure over an interval that contains dropped telemetry is refused with
FROB-REF-888 (a metric computed over missing samples; repairs, ranked: re-run with the channel retained; accept
`unavailable`); exit code 3. A world whose Energetics stream was not retained returns its per-episode totals (level
T0) and the statement that no per-step breakdown exists.

**10. Evidence and determinism.** Evidence class `Tested`; tier and determinism class inherited from the run; the
frontier the figures were read at is in the envelope. The answer is a pure function of committed records, filters
and budget: identical bytes on repeat.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob telemetry energy <run> --json` prints every line of the identity, the separate `numerical_residual` and
      `algorithmic_exchange` lines, and `closure` equal to zero; reachable over MCP as `telemetry_energy`.
- [ ] INV-CHRON-09 holds on the view for 10³ random recorded runs: lines sum to zero and no physical line changes
      when the residual changes.
- [ ] The two-design example prints 10 J and 30 J against 12 J and 12 J; with zero completions both figures are
      `null` with a reason.
- [ ] Bus energy is printed as separate positive and negative integrals; the four braking modes are separate lines.
- [ ] Breakdowns by actuator, joint, limb and phase each sum exactly to their parent line.
- [ ] A step whose residual exceeds tolerance is reported with the earliest step and the offending element.
- [ ] Totals are bit-identical under 100 permutations of worlds and shard splits.
- [ ] The three flags are displayed with their quantities; a flagged episode is still counted.
- [ ] A window with dropped samples is refused with FROB-REF-888.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-chronicle/tests/energy_view.rs`; `cargo test -p frob-chronicle energy_view`.

- `closure_is_zero`: random ledgers (10³) → `closure` = 0 exactly; `numerical_residual` = −Σ others.
- `residual_not_absorbed` (INV-CHRON-09): perturb the residual of a recorded step by 1 µJ → only the residual line
  changes.
- `energy_per_task_two_ways`: A {10 J completed, 20 J failed}, B {12 J, 12 J completed} → A: 10 J and 30 J; B: 12 J
  and 12 J.
- `per_task_zero_completions_is_null`.
- `bus_parts_separate`: a drive at 50 rad/s and −2 A with k_e = 0.2 V·s/rad, R = 1 Ω for 1 s → terminal voltage 8 V;
  `bus_negative` −16 J; shaft 20 J in; copper 4 J. A blocked winding at 3 A and 3 V for 1 s → 9 J copper,
  bus current 0.375 A on a 24 V bus.
- `stored_heat_not_double_counted`: 5 J of copper loss into a thermal node with no ambient path → `stored` +5 J,
  `heat_to_ambient` 0, closure 0.
- `breakdown_sums_to_parent`: by joint, limb, phase.
- `cot_decomposition`: a synthetic ledger of 100 J with 38 J copper in knees during stance → "38 of 100 J".
- `cot_zero_distance_is_null`.
- `first_offending_event_named`: a sign error planted at one port at step 137 → step 137 and that port.
- `permutation_invariant_totals` (INV-CHRON-06).
- `gap_refused_888`.
- `flags_displayed_not_filtered`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Steps `energy-*` of `scripts/e2e/telemetry_verbs.sh` (owned by N24a-39): `frob sim run --tier referee` on the damped
pendulum, on the two-design energy fixture and on the three resting, sliding and dropping block fixtures; then
`frob telemetry energy <run> --json`, `--by joint`, `--per-task`. Asserts closure, the residual bound, the per-task
pairs and the separate lines. NDJSON lines carry the command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block, each
measured line with unit, the relative residual against its bound, and on failure the first offending step and port.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-598 (energy accounting): 10 J against 12 J conditional on completion; 30 J against 12 J per completion; both
  published. Negative control: dropping failed attempts from the total (a report that prints only the conditional
  mean) must fail `energy_per_task_two_ways`.
- Damped pendulum (synthetic fixture, DESIGN numbers): mass 1 kg on a massless 1 m rod, g = 9.81 m/s², released at
  rest from 60°, viscous joint damping, run until at rest: joint damping dissipates m·g·l·(1 − cos 60°) = 4.905 J;
  Δ potential = −4.905 J; Δ kinetic = 0; relative residual below 10⁻⁹ on the referee. Negative control: a sign
  error seeded at one port is localized to that port.
- KA-611 (power ledger of the cart fixture): 9 W dissipated and 0.375 A from the bus; 8 V, 16 W returned, 20 W from
  the shaft, 4 W in copper.
- Resting block (1 kg on a frictionless stationary support, g = 8 m/s²): algorithmic exchange ½·m·g²·h per second
  of simulated time = 1 J/s at 32 Hz and 0.5 J/s at 64 Hz; constitutive dissipation exactly 0; heat 0. Sliding at
  1 m/s on μ = 0.5: 4 W of friction heat at both rates. A plastic drop arriving at 2 m/s: 2 J of impact dissipation,
  booked once. Negative control: a view that books algorithmic exchange under contact dissipation shows a parked
  block heating at a rate that halves when the rate doubles.
- Created energy: a body bouncing on a stiff penalty contact near the stability limit shows `energy-created` and
  `contact-active`; the same drop under the convex law at the default step shows neither; a block pushed along the
  diagonal of a pyramidal cone shows utilization √2 and `cone-excess`. Negative control: a circular-cone run must
  not be flagged.
- Determinism: bytes compared twice on one machine, across worker counts, debug and release, and across platforms.

## Considerations

- The 10⁻³ bound on single-precision tiers is a TARGET measured by the batched-ledger bead; this verb reports the
  measured residual whatever the bound, and never rounds a residual to zero.
- The quantum of 1 nJ is a DESIGN default; it bounds the rounding carried by the residual line at one quantum per
  stored value and is recorded in the output.
- A cost-of-transport figure inherits the distance definition of the task; where a task defines another distance
  (route length), the metric bead states it and this verb prints the path distance.
- Private runs: figures are not scene data, but world addresses in `first_offending` follow the run's privacy mark.
- What would make us revisit: if double-float accumulation on a device proves too costly at telemetry level T0, the
  ledger owner changes the accumulator; the view's integer aggregation is unaffected.
