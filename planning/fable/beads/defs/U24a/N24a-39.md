## Background

The four read verbs of the telemetry cluster that exist at the first milestone are how every later subsystem and
every agent learns what a run did: `telemetry.summary` (a token-budgeted hierarchical summary), `telemetry.query`
(columns by stream and range, paged against a committed frontier), `telemetry.failures` (failures by class and
kind, with first causes) and `telemetry.energy` (the energy ledger and the created-energy flags). Each has unit
tests beside its code. This bead is the end-to-end test of the capability as a user meets it: the real `frob`
binary, real referee runs on real fixtures, the four verbs called through the command line and through the MCP
server, and assertions on what comes back. It is one capability-level test bead; the mechanism beads (BD-094,
N24a-35, N24a-36, N24a-37) contribute named step groups to its script.

Why an end-to-end test in addition to unit tests: the properties that matter most here are properties of the whole
path. "Counts sum to what was planned" involves the job engine's notion of planned, the chunk store's notion of
committed, and the summary builder. "Each row exactly once under concurrent appends" needs a writer process that is
really writing. "A numerical fault is never counted as a physical failure or dropped from a denominator" has to
hold in the number an agent actually reads.

The script also measures, at its milestone and on the referee, the failure-taxonomy half of gate GATE-CHRON-08
(claim: Known answer): each failure class's monitor fires on its planted fixture, and a numerical fault is never
counted as a physical failure or dropped from a denominator. The other half of that gate (the created-energy audit
flags every planted trampolining and cone-excess exploit and no honest fixture) is measured on batched tiers at the
second milestone by the audit's own bead; this script carries its referee rows.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/telemetry_verbs.sh`; its fixture set under `fixtures/e2e/telemetry/`; the four step groups
`summary-*`, `query-*`, `failures-*`, `energy-*`; a cross-check group `consistency-*`; an MCP group `mcp-*`; the
structured log; negative-control builds; the gate record for the referee rows of GATE-CHRON-08.

Out: stills, pixel inspection and the replay export (their script belongs to BD-191, second milestone);
histograms and first-divergence search (their own verbs and scripts); the world and perception failure classes
(N24a-38 and its script); device rows.

## Technical approach

**1. Fixtures (all `synthetic`, each with its identity recorded in the log).**

| Fixture | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `fail/<class>` for the thirteen classes available at the first milestone: `fall`, `stuck`, `collision`, `self-collision`, `slip`, `torque-saturation`, `speed-saturation`, `thermal-limit`, `limit-impact`, `timeout`, `numerical-fault`, `thermal-runaway`, `brownout` | One planted failure per class, with the expected first-anomaly tick on file |
| `fail/knee-thermal-fall` | A quadruped whose knee winding reaches its temperature limit and which then falls: expected anomalies at ticks (thermal-limit 400, torque-saturation 430, fall 520) |
| `battery/mixed-10` | Ten episodes: six complete and satisfied, two physical failures, two with planted solver exhaustion |
| `job/planned-2048` | A referee batch planned at 2,048 episodes and stopped by budget, with refused and unstarted episodes planted |
| `energy/pendulum` | A 1 kg bob on a massless 1 m rod, g = 9.81 m/s², released from 60°, viscous joint damping, run to rest |
| `energy/two-designs` | Design A: one 10 J success and one 20 J failure; design B: two 12 J successes |
| `energy/block-rest`, `energy/block-slide`, `energy/block-drop` | A 1 kg block under g = 8 m/s²: at rest on a frictionless support at 32 Hz and 64 Hz; sliding at 1 m/s on μ = 0.5; a plastic drop arriving at 2 m/s |
| `energy/penalty-bounce`, `energy/pyramid-diagonal` | A stiff penalty contact near its stability limit; a block pushed along the diagonal of a pyramidal friction cone |

**2. Step groups.** Every step is a `frob … --json` command followed by assertions made with the helper's `assert_*`
functions on the envelope.

1. `setup-*`: `frob doctor --json`; `frob sim run --tier referee` on each fixture with an idempotency key derived
   from the step name; `frob job wait` where the envelope returns a job handle (exit code 8).
2. `summary-*` (N24a-35): `frob telemetry summary <run> --budget 800 --json` on `job/planned-2048`: the estimated
   size is at most 800 tokens; completed + failed + refused + unstarted = 2,048; the progress string has the form
   "k of 2,048 episodes committed"; no percentage without its "k of n"; three handles taken from the result
   (`telemetry.failures`, `telemetry.energy`, `telemetry.query`) are invoked and resolve. Budgets 200 and 3,200:
   the smaller tree is contained in the larger. `--budget 50` exits 3 with the minimum budget in the refusal. A
   second call while a job is still running states a later frontier.
3. `query-*` (N24a-36): while a referee batch is still being written by a second process, `frob telemetry query
   <run> --stream contact --ticks 0:… --page-size 500 --json` is paged to exhaustion; the union of pages equals a
   query issued after the run with the same pin (duplicates 0, missing 0). A cursor reused with a changed filter
   exits 3 with FROB-REF-896. An impulse channel requested with `--reduce mean` exits 3 and lists `sum`. A window
   over a planted dropped interval returns the gap, and an aggregate over it exits 3 with FROB-REF-888.
4. `failures-*` (BD-094): `frob telemetry failures <run> --json` on each `fail/<class>` run: exactly the expected
   class fires, at the expected tick. On `battery/mixed-10`: kinds are physical 2, numerical 2; the
   physical-failure count is 2; every denominator is 10; the two faulted episodes have verdict `invalid`. On
   `fail/knee-thermal-fall`: first cause `thermal-limit`, lead time 120 ticks, labelled `Estimated`; `frob explain
   run <run> --budget 600 --json` names the same first cause and cites the channel.
5. `energy-*` (N24a-37): `frob telemetry energy <run> --json` on `energy/pendulum`: joint damping 4.905 J within
   10⁻⁹ relative, `closure` exactly zero, relative residual below 10⁻⁹. On `energy/two-designs --per-task`: 10 J and
   30 J for A; 12 J and 12 J for B. On the block fixtures: algorithmic exchange 1 J/s at 32 Hz and 0.5 J/s at 64 Hz
   with constitutive dissipation exactly 0; 4 W of sliding heat at both rates within 0.1%; 2 J of impact
   dissipation within 10⁻⁹ J. On `energy/penalty-bounce`: flags `energy-created` and `contact-active`; the same
   drop under the convex law at the default step: neither. On `energy/pyramid-diagonal`: utilization √2 within
   10⁻⁶ and flag `cone-excess`.
6. `consistency-*`: the failure counts in the summary equal those of `telemetry.failures`; the energy totals in the
   summary equal those of `telemetry.energy`; a value read through `telemetry.query` on the Energetics stream sums
   to the ledger line it belongs to. A number shown by two verbs is the same number.
7. `mcp-*`: the same four calls through `frob-mcp` (tools `telemetry_summary`, `telemetry_query`,
   `telemetry_failures`, `telemetry_energy`) return envelopes whose `result` is byte-identical to the command-line
   answers at the same frontier.
8. `determinism-*`: every read is issued twice at one frontier and compared byte for byte; the script is run under
   a debug and a release build of `frob` and the results compared.

**3. Negative-control builds.** The script accepts `--control <name>` and, for each, runs against a build with one
planted defect behind a test-only feature flag and asserts that the named step fails:

| Control | Planted defect | Step that must fail |
|---|---|---|
| `fault-as-fall` | A faulted world is reported as `fall` | `failures-kinds` |
| `fault-filtered` | Faulted worlds are filtered before counting | `failures-denominator` |
| `terminal-as-first-cause` | First cause computed as the terminal class | `failures-first-cause` |
| `summary-completed-only` | Success reported over completed episodes with no mention of unstarted or refused | `summary-counts` |
| `offset-pagination` | Pages by row offset instead of a pinned frontier | `query-pinned` |
| `impulse-mean` | An impulse channel downsampled by averaging | `query-reduce` |
| `conditional-mean-only` | Energy per task printed as the conditional mean alone | `energy-per-task` |
| `residual-absorbed` | The numerical residual folded into sliding friction | `energy-closure` |
| `exchange-as-heat` | Algorithmic exchange booked as contact dissipation | `energy-block-rest` |

**4. Idempotence and seeds.** Every run command carries an explicit seed and an idempotency key; a second execution
of the script reuses the recorded runs (the envelope reports the original receipt) and produces the same
assertions. `--fresh` clears the script's own scratch store first.

**5. The gate record.** On success the script writes the referee rows of GATE-CHRON-08 for the thirteen classes
(per class: fixture identity, expected and measured first-anomaly tick, pass) and the two accounting assertions,
with the commit and the seeds, as a receipt; the registry row is entered by a session that did not implement the
verbs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `scripts/e2e/telemetry_verbs.sh` exits 0 on a correct build and non-zero on any failed assertion; it is
      idempotent and seed-explicit.
- [ ] All eight step groups run on the referee with no GPU present.
- [ ] Each of the nine negative-control builds makes its named step fail, and no other step group.
- [ ] The MCP answers equal the command-line answers byte for byte at the same frontier.
- [ ] The referee rows of GATE-CHRON-08 (failure-taxonomy half, thirteen classes; numerical faults neither counted
      as physical nor dropped) are recorded with measured values.
- [ ] The log is written as specified below and contains no scene data from private packs.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Helpers of the script live in `frob-conformance` and are tested there (`cargo test -p frob-conformance e2e_telemetry`):

- `union_of_pages_equals_pinned_query`: detects one duplicated and one missing row in synthetic page sets.
- `token_estimate_matches_builder`: the helper's estimate (one token per four bytes of UTF-8, rounded up) equals
  the verb's own.
- `counts_identity_checker`: flags 1,312 + 40 + 6 + 689 ≠ 2,048 and accepts 1,312 + 40 + 6 + 690.
- `no_bare_percentage_lint`: flags "92%" with no "k of n" beside it.
- `control_dispatch`: each control name maps to exactly one expected failing step.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/telemetry_verbs.sh [--control <name>] [--fresh] [--group <prefix>]`, as described above. It sources
the shared helper of the end-to-end standard and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/telemetry_verbs/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line
has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (the bead that owns the step group), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`,
`metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim,
class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds (estimated tokens against
the budget; the four counts against planned; duplicates and missing against 0; first-anomaly ticks against
expected; joules and watts against their known answers; the relative residual against its bound), the digests of
answers compared for determinism, and on failure the first differing value and the paths of the run and of the
saved envelope. A final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and names GATE-CHRON-08.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Wording known answer: "1,312 of 2,048 episodes committed".
- Pagination: 10⁴ rows read in pages during concurrent appends, each row visible at the pin once. Control: a query
  ordered by timestamp alone repeats or skips equal-time samples.
- Downsampling: impulses (0.2, 0, 0.1, 0.3) N·s over one window sum to 0.6 N·s. Control: the same train averaged.
- First cause: anomalies at ticks 400, 430, 520 → `thermal-limit`, lead 120 ticks.
- Energy: KA-598 (10 J and 30 J against 12 J and 12 J); the pendulum's 4.905 J; the block rows 1 J/s, 0.5 J/s, 0,
  4 W, 2 J; utilization √2 on the pyramid diagonal.
- Metamorphic relation MR-36: a page sequence pinned to a frontier shows each row once under concurrent appends;
  the mutation that must break it is offset pagination.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; across platforms (answers are pure functions of
  committed records, so bytes are compared).

## Considerations

- The concurrent-append step needs a writer that is really concurrent; the script starts the batch under `frobd`
  where the daemon exists and otherwise as a second `frob` process, and fails loudly if the batch finished before
  the first page was read (the step would otherwise pass vacuously).
- Runtime budget: referee fixtures are small (seconds each); the whole script is meant for every change that
  touches `frob-chronicle`.
- The script is extended, not forked, when device tiers arrive: a `--tier` option re-runs the same groups against
  each tier's class.
- If the thirteen-fixture set proves too slow for every change, the fixtures stay and a `--group` subset runs per
  change with the full set nightly; the gate record always comes from the full set.
