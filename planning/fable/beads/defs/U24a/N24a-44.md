## Background

A requirement is evaluated on its own clock, which is usually slower than physics. Fixture 1 steps physics at
1/2000 s and evaluates its requirement at 1/100 s, so every requirement tick spans 20 physics ticks, and the
fixture's atoms are stated as **interval extrema** over those ticks: `env_min_x` (the rearmost point of the loaded
collision envelope, a minimum), `tilt` (the larger of |roll| and |pitch|, a maximum) and `i_max` (the larger
winding current magnitude, a maximum). The kitchen-tray worked task declares its signals the same way:
`(tilt deg :kind interval-extremum :clock physics :reduce max)`, `(clearance_min m :kind interval-extremum :clock
physics :reduce min)`.

The rule of the requirement language (DESIGN of the plan): a signal on a faster clock enters a requirement through
a declared reduction over each requirement tick, `last`, `min` or `max`; channels of kind interval extremum, such
as the minimum clearance swept during a step, are reduced by the matching extremum; and a requirement on an
interval-extremum channel is labelled `interval` in its verdict, where one on a state-kind channel is labelled
`sampled`, because a verdict on a sampled trace says nothing about the time between samples.

The telemetry schema as drafted has five channel kinds (`state`, `interval-mean`, `impulse`, `event`,
`cumulative-counter`, bead BD-090) and none for an extremum over an interval. The requirement side (the admitted
reductions and the coverage label, bead N24a-06) therefore reads a kind that nothing declares or records. This
bead supplies the missing kind and its producers on the referee. It was found during integration of the Arena
beads: fixture 1 (N24b-05) needs these channels at M1.

Why a kind and not a convention. A `state` channel may be downsampled by a sample; an extremum channel downsampled
by a sample silently loses the spike it exists to keep. The smallest case: tilt is 10° at both ends of a
requirement tick and 36° for one physics tick (0.5 ms) in between. `last` reports 10° and the hard limit of 35° is
never seen; the interval maximum reports 36° and the limit is violated. Rejected alternatives: recording every
physics tick of every such signal at full rate (the requirement would still need a reduction, and the store
would carry 20 times the samples); a `state` channel with a comment (no type prevents the wrong reduction).

## Scope

In: the channel kind `interval-extremum` with its sense (`min` or `max`) in the channel declarations of
`frob-chronicle`; its downsampling rule; the producer contract and the fold in the transactional referee step of
`frob-dyn`; the first channels (`env_min_x`, `tilt`, `i_max`, `clearance_min`, `tray_tilt`, `tray_acc`); what the
coverage label `interval` may claim.

Out: the other five kinds, the `.frun` layout and `downsample` for them (BD-090); the admitted reductions per kind
on the requirement side, the coverage label in a verdict and FROB-REF-525 (N24a-06); `telemetry.query`, which
returns the kind with each column (N24a-36); the same fold in the batched step kernels and on devices (the M2
telemetry bead BD-207 and the batched-step beads; a maximum and a minimum are selects, so the fold is legal under
the lockstep rule); the swept collision detection whose bound the clearance channel records (N16a-08).

## Technical approach

**Declaration (DESIGN).** A sixth kind in the channel-kind table:

| Kind | Example | Correct downsampling | Wrong, and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `interval-extremum` (`min` or `max`) | Minimum clearance swept during a step; peak tilt or peak winding current over a tick | The same extremum over the window: a minimum of minima, a maximum of maxima | A sample, a mean or the opposite extremum loses or hides the excursion the channel exists to keep |

A channel of this kind declares, beside unit, frame, clock and quantization: `sense` (`min` or `max`) and
`coverage`, one of `committed-endpoints` (the extremum over the committed sub-interval endpoints inside the tick)
or `swept` (a bound that holds for every instant of the tick, with its bound side, `lower` for a minimum and
`upper` for a maximum). The value at tick k of the channel's clock is the extremum of the underlying quantity over
the interval (t_{k−1}, t_k]; tick 0 holds the value at the episode origin.

**Producer contract.** A producer registers a fold: an initial value (+∞ for `min`, −∞ for `max`), an update
called at every committed sub-interval endpoint of the step, and a read at the tick boundary that emits the value
and resets the fold. On the transactional referee the sub-intervals are those of event location, so a value at an
impact or at a stick-to-slip transition is folded in; a rejected trial step contributes nothing (the fold is part
of the restore set, so a retry cannot leave a phantom extremum). A producer that declares `swept` supplies a bound
from a swept computation, and the tighter of the swept bound and the endpoint extremum is never reported as
tighter than the swept bound. A channel declared `interval-extremum` with no registered fold is a typed error at
registration.

**First producers (M1, referee).** `tilt` and `tray_tilt`: maximum of the angle of the body's up axis from
vertical, `committed-endpoints`. `i_max`: maximum winding current magnitude over the actuator sub-steps of the
tick, `committed-endpoints`. `tray_acc`: maximum acceleration magnitude of the tray frame. `env_min_x`: minimum
world x over the loaded collision envelope. `clearance_min`: minimum distance between non-contact links and the
scene, `swept` where the transaction's certified collision detection supplies a lower bound and
`committed-endpoints` otherwise; the declaration says which.

**Laws.** (1) Composition: the extremum over a union of adjacent intervals equals the extremum of their extrema, so
downsampling twice equals downsampling once, and a requirement tick's value does not depend on how its physics
ticks were grouped in storage. (2) Refinement: halving the physics step can only move a `committed-endpoints`
maximum up and a minimum down or leave them; a `swept` bound brackets both. (3) Dominance: for every tick,
min-extremum ≤ the state sample at the tick's end ≤ max-extremum of the same quantity.

**What `interval` may claim.** The label means "no committed state inside the tick exceeded the value", and for a
`swept` channel "no instant inside the tick did". It is not a claim about uncommitted trial states. The coverage
field travels with the channel into the verdict record, so a reader can tell the two apart.

Determinism: the fold is order-fixed (sub-intervals in time order), exact for `min` and `max`, class D0 given the
committed states. Evidence: a recorded measurement; class `Tested`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The channel-kind table has six kinds; `downsample` on an `interval-extremum` channel admits only the matching
      extremum, and a mean, a sample or the opposite extremum is a typed error (at compile time where the kind is
      static).
- [ ] The spike case: tilt 10° at both ends of a requirement tick and 36° for one physics tick between them gives
      36° on the `max` channel and 10° under `last` on a state channel; fixture 1's atom `tilt-ok` (limit 35°) is
      violated on the first and not on the second.
- [ ] Fixture 1's channels `env_min_x`, `tilt` and `i_max` are recorded at the requirement clock with kind
      `interval-extremum` over the 20 physics ticks of each requirement tick, and its clock check (N24a-06) passes.
- [ ] A rejected trial step leaves no trace in any fold (planted: a trial state with tilt 80° that is rolled back).
- [ ] `frob telemetry query` returns the kind, sense and coverage of each such column.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-chronicle` lists the kind with its three laws.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `extremum_downsample_matching_only`: (3, 9, 4, 7) as maxima over four ticks downsample to 9; a request for a
  mean does not compile; for the minimum, (3, 9, 4, 7) gives 3.
- `composition_law`: for random sequences of 20 values, the maximum of two 10-tick maxima equals the 20-tick
  maximum; grouped 4 × 5 and 5 × 4 likewise.
- `spike_seen_by_extremum_missed_by_last`: the 10°, 36°, 10° case above.
- `dominance_law`: min channel ≤ end sample ≤ max channel on 10⁴ random ticks.
- `rollback_leaves_no_extremum`: the planted 80° trial state.
- `fold_registered_or_refused`: a channel declared `interval-extremum` with no fold fails registration.
- `swept_never_tighter_than_bound`: an endpoint minimum of 12 mm with a swept lower bound of 9 mm reports 9 mm.

### End-to-end script and logging

Steps `extremum-*` of `scripts/e2e/telemetry_verbs.sh` (N24a-39): a referee run of fixture 1's nominal case;
`frob telemetry query --stream task --channels tilt,i_max,env_min_x --json`, asserting kind, sense, coverage and
100 Hz sampling; a downsampled query at 10 Hz, asserting each value equals the extremum of its ten source values;
and a query that asks for the mean of `tilt`, asserting the refusal. Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes,
wall time, the `evidence` block, the column descriptors, the first differing value on failure.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answer: the spike case (36° against 10°); the composition law on the fixture's 20-tick grouping.
- Negative control: a build in which the kind downsamples by `last` passes every state-channel test and must fail
  `spike_seen_by_extremum_missed_by_last` and the 10 Hz step of the script.
- Negative control: a fold that is not part of the restore set must fail `rollback_leaves_no_extremum`.
- Metamorphic relation: re-chunking the stored column (different chunk boundaries) changes no downsampled value.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine, debug and release, and across worker counts: identical column bytes.

## Considerations

- Cost: one accumulator per channel and world, updated at each committed sub-interval; negligible beside the step.
- On lockstep tiers there is no event location, so `committed-endpoints` covers fewer instants than on the
  referee; the coverage field records the tier's meaning and a comparison across tiers reads it.
- A requirement that needs a guarantee between committed states must read a `swept` channel; where none exists
  the verdict's label stays `interval` with coverage `committed-endpoints`, and the task audit can show the gap.
- If the owners of the ledger schema prefer to carry the kind inside BD-090, this bead reduces to the producers
  and the tests; the kind's rules stay as written here.
