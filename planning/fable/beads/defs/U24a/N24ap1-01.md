## Background

The metric and scorecard beads of the Arena chapter each name a step group of one end-to-end script,
`scripts/e2e/arena_scorecards.sh`: the core verdict metrics (N24a-17), the energy metrics (N24a-18) and the scorecard
accounting (N24a-22), all at the first milestone, then the motion and contact metrics (N24a-19, N24a-20) at the
second and maneuver cost and fallback counts (N24a-21) at the third. The family registry (N24a-23, first milestone)
needs a step that reads it through `frob task list`. The script was carried by the reproducibility-gate bead
N24a-24, which is a second-milestone bead; the first-milestone beads would then have closed with no end-to-end
evidence until a milestone later. This bead is that script, split out so that it exists on the referee at the first
milestone with the first-milestone step groups; the later metric beads add their groups to it and depend on it.

Why an end-to-end script beside the unit tests of each metric: the frauds a scorecard invites are properties of the
whole path. A denominator shrunk by dropping faulted episodes, an energy figure printed without its pair, a "p99"
from ten episodes, a battery shortened under the same name: each must be absent from the JSON an agent actually reads
from `frob eval scorecard`, produced by the real binary from recorded runs, not only from a library function.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/arena_scorecards.sh [--group <prefix>] [--tier <tier>] [--control <name>] [--fresh]`; its fixture
batteries under `fixtures/e2e/arena_scorecards/`; the step groups `families-*` (N24a-23), `metrics-core-*`
(N24a-17), `metrics-energy-*` (N24a-18) and `scorecard-*` (N24a-22); the extension contract by which later beads add
groups; the structured log; the negative-control builds; helper unit tests in `frob-conformance`.

Out: the groups `metrics-motion-*` (N24a-19), `metrics-contact-*` (N24a-20) and `metrics-maneuver-*` (N24a-21), each
added by its own bead, which depends on this one; the reproducibility gate GATE-ARENA-01 and its script
`gate_arena_01_reproducibility.sh` (N24a-24, which from the second milestone also re-runs this script with
`--tier batch-cpu`); the M1 families' settle-run-verdict pipeline (N24c-07); the recording of GATE-ARENA-07 with the
batteries at the third milestone (BD-254); the solvability oracle (BD-271).

## Technical approach

**1. Fixtures** (all labelled `synthetic`; each fixture's identity is written to the log).

| Fixture | Content | Group |
|---|---|---|
| `battery/ten-planted` | Ten episodes of a one-requirement task with horizon 8 s: six `satisfied`, decided at 1, 2, 2, 3, 4 and 5 s; two `violated` (one of class `fall`, one `collision`); one `undetermined` whose last valid time is 6 s; one `invalid` faulted at 3 s | `metrics-core-*` |
| `battery/two-designs` | Design A: one 10 J `satisfied`, one 20 J `violated`; design B: two 12 J `satisfied` (synthetic ledger totals) | `metrics-energy-*` |
| `battery/no-completions` | Two `violated` episodes of 20 J and 5 J | `metrics-energy-*` |
| `ledger/cot-hand` | m = 8.000 kg, g = 9.81 m/s², path length 1.00 m, gross draw 20.000 J, returned 4.304 J (net 15.696 J), joint work 11.772 J | `metrics-energy-*` |
| `battery/twenty-planted` | Twenty scenarios: fourteen `satisfied`, three `violated`, one with a planted numerical fault, one doorway narrower than the body (0.55 m against 0.62 m), one refused at admission | `scorecard-*` |
| `families/incomplete-*` | Three planted family rows marked `available`: no requirement; one satisfying example only; no baseline scorecard | `families-*` |

How the fixtures reach the binary (DESIGN). The fixture batteries are `frob.battery/1` manifests with their events
files (N24b-01); at the start of a run the script copies them, with the registries they need, into a scratch
registries root under `target/e2e-scratch/arena_scorecards/` and passes it with the process option
`--registries-root` (N32ax-03), so the committed registries are never edited and every envelope carries the warning
`registries: override`, which the script asserts.

How the planted outcomes arise (DESIGN). The verdict-bearing scenarios are real referee episodes, not hand-written
records: their worlds fix the outcome by construction (the distance to the goal fixes the decision time; a ledge
on the path produces the `fall`, a post the `collision`; a horizon shorter than the verdict's reach produces the
`refused` scenario with FROB-REF-526; the doorway narrower than the body produces the infeasible one). Two outcomes
cannot be produced by an honest world file and come from a seam compiled only under the test feature that also
builds the negative controls of section 3: the disturbance law `plant:fault@3s` overwrites one state value of the
world with a non-finite number at 3 s of simulated time, so that the real fault path marks the world `Faulted` and
the verdict is `invalid`; `plant:gap@6s` stops the recorder retaining the requirement's channels at 6 s, so that
the real coverage check returns `undetermined` with reason `missing-samples`. A build without the feature refuses
both laws with FROB-REF-724 (not implemented in this build, exit code 5), so the script requires the test-feature
build: its preflight reads `frob capabilities --json` and stops before any step, naming the feature, on a build
without the seam. The energy fixtures (`battery/two-designs`, `battery/no-completions`, `ledger/cot-hand`) are recorded
stores (run records, verdict records and energy-ledger rows written by the real writers from the table's totals),
because no simulated episode totals exactly 10 J; their steps read the recorded run identifiers with `frob eval
scorecard` and `frob telemetry energy` and run no episode; they are labelled `synthetic` and the scorecard's
evidence block must repeat the label.

The doorway's infeasibility witness is, until the solvability oracle exists (BD-271, third milestone), a fixture
record carrying the checked necessary condition "body width 0.62 m > doorway width 0.55 m" with the bottleneck named.
The cross-check against the oracle is not a step of this bead (the oracle comes two milestones later): BD-271
registers the group `scorecard-oracle-*` through the step table of section 4, which obtains the witness from
`frob nemesis oracle` and asserts that it and the fixture record name the same bottleneck.

**2. Step groups.** Every step is a `frob … --json` command followed by assertions made with the helper's `assert_*`
functions on the envelope; runs carry an explicit seed and an idempotency key derived from the step name, and a
returned job handle (exit code 8) is followed by `frob job wait`.

1. `families-*` (N24a-23): `frob task list --json` returns thirteen families; at the first milestone the three M1
   families are `available` and the other ten `planned`; `cargo test -p frob-arena family_completeness` passes; with
   the planted rows loaded each is reported `partial` with its missing item named, for example "baseline scorecard:
   missing for ref:quadruped"; a row whose `status` was hand-edited to `available` is overridden.
2. `metrics-core-*` (N24a-17): `frob eval run` on `battery/ten-planted`, then `frob eval scorecard --json`. Rates:
   success 6 of 10 with interval [0.2623, 0.8785], fall 1 of 10, collision 1 of 10, never 6 of 8; the `invalid` and
   `undetermined` episodes are shown as their own counts. Time to verdict: restricted mean 4.76 s (Kaplan–Meier of
   "not yet satisfied" with the `invalid` episode censored at 3 s and the `undetermined` one at 6 s; S = 0.9, 0.7,
   0.6, 0.48, 0.36 after 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 s; an episode censored at a tick is at risk at that tick), and the
   attainers-only mean 2.833 s appears nowhere. `--quantile 0.99` is answered with exit code 0 and the cell
   `not-observable` at n = 10 with p_max = 0.7411, n_needed = 299 and no value (N24a-17: an answer, not a
   refusal). The binding-atom table lists each atom with its count. A metric over a trace with a planted
   dropped interval exits 3 with FROB-REF-888.
3. `metrics-energy-*` (N24a-18): on `battery/two-designs`, design A shows 10 J conditional and 30 J per completion,
   design B 12 J and 12 J, both figures in both rows; on `battery/no-completions` both figures are `undefined` with
   "0 completions" and 25 J spent; on `ledger/cot-hand` CoT_elec = 0.2000 and CoT_mech = 0.1500 with gross 20.000 J,
   returned 4.304 J and the residual line present; `frob telemetry energy --json` on the same runs reports the same
   totals.
4. `scorecard-*` (N24a-22): on `battery/twenty-planted` the seven columns read satisfied 14, violated 3, undetermined
   0, invalid 1, infeasible-for-candidate 1, infeasible-for-class 0, refused 1, summing to 20; success over all
   scenarios 14 of 20 = 0.700 and over scenarios not shown infeasible 14 of 19 = 0.737, side by side, each with its
   exact interval; no overall-score field; every cell has unit, n and interval; the scorecard cites its `BatteryId`
   and frontier. Step `scorecard-identity`: a re-run with the scratch battery file missing one scenario under the
   same name is refused at load, before any episode, with `battery-manifest-invalid` (kind `no-event`, exit code 3;
   N24b-01), and the refusal's computed `BatteryId` differs from the registered one; after the removal is appended
   to the events file with a reason, the run is admitted, `bound` echoes the new `BatteryId`, a
   `frob.battery.event/1` row precedes the run record, and the scorecard counts 19 scenarios. A fixture witness
   that is a bare "infeasible" label without a checked condition is rejected by the classifier, and the scenario
   falls to its verdict column.
5. `determinism-*`: every read above is issued twice at one frontier and compared byte for byte; the script is run
   under a debug and a release build of `frob` and the results compared.

**3. Negative-control builds.** `--control <name>` runs against a build with one planted defect behind a test-only
feature flag and asserts that the named step fails and no other group does.

| Control | Planted defect | Step that must fail |
|---|---|---|
| `rate-valid-only` | Success computed over episodes that are neither `invalid` nor `undetermined` (6 of 8) | `metrics-core-rates` |
| `attainers-mean` | Time to verdict as the mean over attainers (2.833 s) | `metrics-core-rmt` |
| `quantile-unchecked` | A 99th percentile printed from ten episodes | `metrics-core-quantile` |
| `conditional-only` | Energy per task printed as the conditional mean alone | `metrics-energy-per-task` |
| `drop-invalid` | `invalid` subtracted from the denominators | `scorecard-denominators` |
| `second-denominator-only` | Only the not-shown-infeasible rate reported | `scorecard-denominators` |
| `battery-id-by-name` | Battery identity taken from its name (the shortened file then loads under the old identity and no refusal is raised) | `scorecard-identity` |
| `status-hand-set` | Family status read from the file instead of computed | `families-status` |

**4. Tiers and extension.** `--tier referee` is the default and the only tier at the first milestone; from the
second, N24a-24 runs the script with `--tier batch-cpu`, and every group compares its figures with the referee's
under the tier's determinism class. A later bead adds a group by registering its prefix, fixtures, expected values
and any control in the script's step table; existing groups are not edited, and `--group <prefix>` runs one group
alone. The script is extended, never forked.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `scripts/e2e/arena_scorecards.sh` exits 0 on a correct build and non-zero on any failed assertion; it is
      idempotent (a second execution reuses the recorded runs and makes the same assertions) and seed-explicit.
- [ ] The four first-milestone groups and `determinism-*` run on the referee with no GPU present and assert the
      values of section 2.
- [ ] Each of the eight negative-control builds makes its named step fail, and no other group.
- [ ] The groups of N24a-19, N24a-20 and N24a-21 can be added through the step table without editing an existing
      group, which a shell self-test with a dummy group demonstrates.
- [ ] The log is written as specified below and contains identifiers and digests only, no scene data from private
      packs.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Helpers of the script live in `frob-conformance` and are tested there (`cargo test -p frob-conformance
e2e_arena_scorecards`):

- `restricted_mean_helper`: 4.76 s on the ten-episode fixture; 5.75 s on N24a-17's four-episode case.
- `clopper_pearson_helper`: 6 of 10 → [0.2623, 0.8785]; 14 of 20 → [0.4572, 0.8811]; endpoints rounded outward.
- `column_sum_checker`: flags 14 + 3 + 0 + 1 + 1 + 0 + 0 = 19 ≠ 20 (a dropped refusal) and accepts the full set.
- `k_of_n_lint`: flags "60%" with no "6 of 10" beside it.
- `control_dispatch`: each control name maps to exactly one expected failing step.
- `step_table_extension`: a dummy group registered in the table runs under `--group` and leaves the other groups'
  digests unchanged.

### End-to-end script and logging

The script above. It sources the shared helper of the end-to-end standard and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/arena_scorecards/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line has
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (the bead that owns the step group), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`,
`refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier,
determinism, identities, seed), the fixture identity, measured values with units and their expected values and
tolerances (counts against denominators, rates with intervals, the restricted mean in seconds, joules, the
dimensionless costs of transport, the seven column counts against N), both `BatteryId`s as digests, the digests of
answers compared for determinism, and on failure the first differing field with the run identifier and the saved
envelope's path. A final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed, the groups run, and names GATE-ARENA-07
for the scorecard rows it exercises.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-598: 10 J against 12 J conditional on completion, 30 J against 12 J per completion.
- KA-600 (scorecard accounting): a planted infeasible scenario appears in its column and in the all-scenario
  denominator; columns sum to the battery size; a silently shortened battery changes identity.
- Derived values recomputed by the helpers: restricted mean 4.76 s; Clopper–Pearson intervals above; p_max(10) =
  0.05^(1/10) = 0.7411; costs of transport 15.696/78.48 = 0.2000 and 11.772/78.48 = 0.1500.
- The eight negative-control builds of section 3.
- Determinism lanes: twice at one frontier; debug and release; across platforms (answers are pure functions of
  committed records, so bytes are compared).

## Considerations

- Runtime: the fixtures are a few dozen short referee episodes; the script is meant for every change that touches
  `frob-arena` metrics or scorecards.
- The fixture witness for the doorway stands in for the solvability oracle until the third milestone; it carries a
  checked condition, so it exercises the classifier's real rule ("a failed search, a refusal or a fault is never a
  witness") and is never confused with a bare label.
- Ownership: each step group's `bead` field names the metric bead that specifies its values; a failing group is
  routed to that bead, not to this one, unless the script itself is wrong.
- Private families never enter these fixtures; scorecards over private scenes are exercised by the qualification
  lane's own scripts.
