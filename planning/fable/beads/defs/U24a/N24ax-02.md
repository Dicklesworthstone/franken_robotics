## Background

A task in FrankenRobotics is data: the task file `.ftask` (schema `frob.task/1`, identity `TaskId`) with
nine sections. Its `terms` section holds "observation, action, reward and termination terms, each with
unit, noise, delay and **the representation under which it transforms**". The reason is stated with the
requirement: declaring the representation of each term "is what lets the framework build equivariant
networks, generate mirrored data, and test declared invariances mechanically".

Three groups are in play. Γ_mech is the group of isometries of the body that preserve its mechanical
parameters. Γ_obs ≤ Γ_mech keeps only elements that also preserve the sensors, clocks, payload, **the task
(its goal set, requirement predicates and reward terms, declared by Arena)** and the law of the scenes. The
rule for the task clause is fail-closed: "every observation, action, reward and requirement term of the
task must declare how it transforms; an element under which some term has no declared transformation is
excluded". Weight tying and augmentation additionally need "the task's world and command distributions
invariant in law".

The task-file bead (BD-122) and the schema bead (N24a-02) carry the field name and the examples "trivial,
pseudovector, permutation, invariant"; the term-contract beads of the learning subsystem require the field
to be present. No bead defines the closed vocabulary of kinds, how a kind acts, how an expression built
from terms inherits a kind, how a randomization draw or a goal is declared invariant in law, or the
function that answers "does element g preserve this task?". The symmetry functor (N14a-11), the Sensorium
predicate for the task clause (N20b-01) and result transport across an orbit (N17a-24) each assume it
exists. This bead writes it, in `frob-spec`, which owns the term types.

DESIGN decisions. (1) Kinds are a closed enum checked like units: an expression's kind is inferred, and a
term whose declared kind differs from the inferred one is refused. Rejected: free-text kinds (nothing can
be checked) and per-term hand-written matrices (the permutation depends on the design, which a task must
not name). (2) Absence is legal and means *undeclared*: a task for an asymmetric robot need not declare
anything, and then only the identity preserves it. Rejected: a default of `invariant`, which would admit
every element silently. Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: the kind vocabulary and its action under one group element; the `rep` field on observation, action,
reward and termination terms, on `signals`, and on `randomization` draws; kind inference for expressions and
requirement atoms; invariance in law of a draw; the goal declaration in the `world` section; the function
`preserves_task`; the `symmetry` block of `task.check` and `task.show`.

Out: computing Γ_mech and the induced permutations of a design (the symmetry algorithm and N14a-11);
composing the five clauses into Γ_obs and storing it (N20b-01, BD-241); the scene family's law group
(N19cx-01); building equivariant networks from the declared kinds (N23a-31) and the other requirements of
the term contract (BD-194, N23a-05); transporting results (N17a-24).

## Technical approach

**1. Kinds (DESIGN; `crates/frob-spec/src/rep.rs`).** Let a group element act on space by the orthogonal
matrix P (det P = ±1), on joints by a signed permutation ρ_J, on actuators by a signed permutation ρ_A (the
sign includes gear direction), on end effectors by a permutation π_F, on sensors by a permutation π_S, and
on the rays or pixels of sensor s by π_s.

| Kind (`rep`) | Action | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `invariant` (alias `trivial`) | x ↦ x | height, speed, energy, a margin, a clock phase |
| `pseudoscalar` | x ↦ det(P)·x | signed yaw rate about the vertical; signed lateral curvature |
| `polar(frame)` | v ↦ P·v | linear velocity, specific force, a goal offset, gravity direction |
| `axial(frame)` (alias `pseudovector`) | ω ↦ det(P)·P·ω | angular velocity, a moment, angular momentum |
| `twist(frame)`, `wrench(frame)` | by diag(det(P)·P, P) on (angular, linear) | base twist; a contact wrench |
| `rotation(frame)` | R ↦ P·R·Pᵀ | base orientation |
| `component(kind, axis)` | the stated component of the transformed vector | lateral velocity = `component(polar(base), y)` |
| `joint` | q ↦ ρ_J·q | joint positions, velocities, position targets |
| `actuator` | u ↦ ρ_A·u | torques, currents, temperatures (sign +1 for scalars per actuator) |
| `foot` | by π_F | contact flags, normal loads |
| `sensor` | by π_S | per-sensor health or age |
| `scan(sensor)` | by π_s | lidar ranges, a depth image |
| `indexed(index, kind)` | permutation on the index, `kind` on each entry | foot positions = `indexed(foot, polar(base))` |
| `fixed` | none: the term is tied to something no element moves | distance to the north wall; a one-sided reward |

`frame` is `base` or `world`. The parity rule for improper isometries is the convention card's
T(P) = diag(det(P)·P, P). `fixed` is a declaration, not an omission: it breaks every element whose action
on the term's inputs is not the identity.

**2. Where `rep` is written.** On every observation, action, reward and termination term; on every entry
of `signals` (requirements read signals); on every `randomization` draw (the kind of the drawn quantity).
The field is optional. Absent means *undeclared*.

**3. Inference for expressions.** The requirement grammar admits `+ − * min max abs delay` over signals
and quantities. Rules: a quantity literal is `invariant`; `delay` keeps the kind; `+`, `−`, `min`, `max`
need equal kinds and keep them, except that `min` and `max` are defined only on `invariant`; a product of
two `pseudoscalar`s, and `abs` of one, is `invariant`; `invariant × k` is `k`; a `component` along the
vertical of a `polar` vector is `invariant` and of an `axial` vector is `pseudoscalar` for elements that fix
the vertical. Every combination these rules do not name (a product of two vectors, `abs` of a vector, a sum of
a `polar` and an `axial` term) has no kind and is a mismatch, never a guess. An atom compares an expression with a quantity; its kind is the expression's. A verdict
formula is preserved by g when g maps the set of atoms onto itself (an `invariant` atom to itself; an atom
on an `indexed` signal to the atom on the permuted index) and the formula, with atoms renamed and the
operands of `and`/`or` sorted, is syntactically equal to itself. A reward term must infer `invariant`, or
the reward terms must map onto each other with equal weights. An inferred kind that contradicts the
declared one is a static-admission failure of `task.check`, reported under the dimension refusal
FROB-REF-535 with `pointer` at the term and `detail: representation-mismatch` (DESIGN: no new public code;
a kind error is the same class of defect as a unit error).

**4. Invariance in law of a draw.** A draw declares the kind of its quantity and its distribution. It is
invariant under g when the pushed-forward distribution equals the original: a `pseudoscalar` or a
horizontal `component` drawn from a distribution symmetric about zero (uniform on [−a, a], a centred
normal, a symmetric table) is invariant under a sign flip, and uniform on [0, a] is not; an `indexed`
family of draws is invariant when the entries g exchanges have identical distributions and are independent
or exchangeable; an `invariant` quantity is always invariant; a `polar` planar vector needs a distribution
invariant under P. The comparison is exact on the declared parameters (rationals), never sampled.

**5. Goals.** The `world` section gains, beside the scene family or scene set, `goal`: `from-pack` (the
goal regions of the scene pack, which move with the scene, so the scene family's law group governs),
`command` (the goal is a commanded quantity drawn in `randomization`; rule 4 governs), or `world-fixed` with
a pose (invariant only under elements whose world action fixes that pose).

**6. The check.**

```rust
pub struct ElementAction { pub p: Orthogonal3, pub joints: SignedPerm, pub actuators: SignedPerm,
                           pub feet: Perm, pub sensors: Perm, pub scans: BTreeMap<SensorSlot, Perm> }   // DESIGN
pub enum TaskPreserved { Yes, Broken { path: TermPath, reason: BreakReason }, Undeclared { path: TermPath } }
pub enum BreakReason { FixedTerm, TermSetNotInvariant, FormulaNotInvariant, RewardNotInvariant,
                       DrawNotInvariantInLaw, GoalWorldFixed }
pub fn preserves_task(task: &Task, g: &ElementAction) -> Vec<TaskPreserved>;       // every finding, not the first
pub fn transform_term(term: &Term, g: &ElementAction) -> Option<Term>;             // None when undeclared
```

The caller supplies the permutations (they depend on the design); `frob-spec` never reads a design. The
identity always returns `Yes`. The answers for a set of elements that is a group form a subgroup; the
function does not assume the input is a group.

**7. Surface and identity.** `rep` values and `goal` are part of the canonical JSON, hence of `TaskId`.
`task.check` gains a `symmetry` block: counts of declared, `fixed` and undeclared terms, each inferred kind,
and every mismatch. `task.show --section symmetry` prints the same. With `--element` and a file of
permutations, `task.check` prints `preserves_task` for that element (class `Tested`, tier `static`,
determinism `exact`).

**8. The element file (DESIGN, round 3: the input stage routes a file by its extension and its top-level
`schema` member, N34-24, so the file needs a registered format).** Format `group-element`, schema
`frob.group.element/1`, extension `.json`, owner crate `frob-spec` (reader `rep::read_element`, which returns
the `ElementAction` of section 6): a JSON object with `schema` (the constant above), `name` (free text, not part
of the action), `p` (the 3 × 3 orthogonal matrix, row-major, nine numbers; |PᵀP − I| ≤ 10⁻¹² entrywise and
det P = ±1, else FROB-REF-802 at `/p`), `joints` and `actuators` (signed permutations: entry i is `[j, s]`,
index i goes to index j with sign s ∈ {1, −1}), `feet` and `sensors` (permutations: entry i is j) and `scans`
(an object from sensor slot to a permutation of that sensor's rays or pixels). A permutation that is not a
bijection on 0..n−1 is FROB-REF-802 with the pointer at the entry; an unknown member is FROB-REF-103; an
unknown major version is FROB-REF-891. This bead enters the format's row in `registries/formats.toml` through
the format registry of N34-19 (DESIGN `id = "FMT-GROUP-ELEMENT"`, format `group-element`, schema
`frob.group.element/1`, extension `.json` routed by its `schema` member, owner `frob-spec`, reader
`rep::read_element`; consumer the `--element` input of `task.check`; canonical form canonical JSON with no view;
`decision` = `docs/adr/ADR-WS-06-format-rows.md`, N34-27's decision record, which every row beyond the thirteen
cites), as N24a-13 enters the overlay format's. By N34-27's fail-closed rule `fmt.param-without-row` that row is
what admits the `params` entry `element = "artifact-input<group-element>"` that VERBS-task's `task.check` row
carries, so the path jail's 734 and 828 and the reader codes apply to it as to every file input. The permutations are those a design induces (section 6: the caller supplies them); `frob-spec` checks
only their shape, never a design.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The fifteen kinds (fourteen rows of the table; `twist` and `wrench` share one) parse and round-trip through both views of the task file; an unknown kind is refused with FROB-REF-891 (the vocabulary is closed and grows only by a schema version); `trivial` and `pseudovector` are accepted as aliases and canonicalized.
- [ ] A term with no `rep` is accepted and reported as undeclared; `preserves_task` then returns `Undeclared` for every non-identity element and `Yes` for the identity.
- [ ] A reward declared `invariant` whose expression infers `pseudoscalar` is refused by `task.check` with FROB-REF-535 and `detail: representation-mismatch`.
- [ ] The flat velocity task of the reference quadruped, fully declared, is preserved by the left-right mirror when lateral velocity and yaw-rate commands are drawn symmetrically, and broken with `DrawNotInvariantInLaw` when yaw rate is drawn from [0, 1] rad/s.
- [ ] A requirement with atoms `left_clear` and `right_clear` under `and` is preserved by the mirror; with a weight or a threshold on one side only it is `FormulaNotInvariant`.
- [ ] A task with `goal = world-fixed` is broken with `GoalWorldFixed` under every element that moves the goal pose.
- [ ] Changing any `rep` changes `TaskId`.
- [ ] `task.check` and `task.show` expose the `symmetry` block through `frob --json`; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-spec` lists the kinds and the fail-closed rule.
- [ ] `--element` reads a `frob.group.element/1` file through `rep::read_element`; a malformed element (P not orthogonal, a permutation that is not a bijection) is FROB-REF-802 with the pointer at the offending member, and a file whose `schema` member names another format is refused by the input stage, never parsed as an element.
- [ ] `registries/formats.toml` holds the `group-element` row citing N34-27's decision record, and `cargo xtask check-contracts --json` reports no `fmt.param-without-row` for `task.check`'s `element`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-spec/tests/rep.rs`; real parser and checker; task files labelled `synthetic`.
- `kind_actions_mirror`: under P = diag(1, −1, 1): polar (a, b, c) ↦ (a, −b, c); axial (a, b, c) ↦ (−a, b, −c); pseudoscalar s ↦ −s; rotation R ↦ P·R·P.
- `parity_twice_identity`: every kind's action applied twice under an involution is the identity (property test).
- `joint_signed_permutation`: with ρ_J exchanging left and right hip abduction with sign −1, q = (0.1, −0.2) ↦ (0.2, −0.1).
- `inference_table`: each rule of step 3, including `abs(pseudoscalar) = invariant` and `min` on a non-invariant refused.
- `component_vertical`: the vertical component of a polar vector is invariant; of an axial vector, pseudoscalar.
- `draw_symmetry`: uniform [−0.5, 0.5] invariant; uniform [0, 1] not; per-foot friction with equal ranges invariant under π_F, with a lower left range not.
- `formula_canonical_equality`: `(and a b)` equals `(and b a)`; `(U … a b)` does not equal `(U … b a)`.
- `undeclared_is_fail_closed`, `fixed_breaks_moving_elements`, `identity_always_yes`.
- `answers_form_subgroup`: on C₂ × C₂ (left-right mirror a, front-back mirror b, their product ab), over generated
  synthetic task files with planted one-sided terms, the preserved set is always one of the five subgroups ({e},
  {e, a}, {e, b}, {e, ab}, the whole group) and never, say, {e, a, b}. Known answer: a lateral-velocity term
  declared `fixed` leaves exactly {e, b} (a and ab negate the lateral component, b does not).
- `untyped_product_is_mismatch`: `(* lin_vel_x yaw_rate_vec)` and `(abs lin_vel)` infer no kind and are refused
  with `detail: representation-mismatch`.
- `unknown_kind_refused`: `"rep": "tensor(base)"` is refused with FROB-REF-891 and the pointer at the term;
  `"trivial"` and `"pseudovector(base)"` load and are written back as `invariant` and `axial(base)`, with one
  `TaskId` for the alias and the canonical spelling.
- `element_file_reader`: `quad_mirror_lr.json` (P = diag(1, −1, 1), the hip-abduction pair exchanged with sign
  −1) reads to the `ElementAction` of `joint_signed_permutation`; P = diag(1, −1, 1.001) is 802 at `/p`; a
  `joints` entry naming one index twice is 802 at that entry; `"schema": "frob.group.element/2"` is 891; an extra
  member `"group"` is 103.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Hand case (the mirror of a quadruped, P = diag(1, −1, 1), det P = −1): forward velocity unchanged, lateral velocity negated, yaw rate negated, roll rate negated, pitch rate unchanged; a scorecard metric declared `invariant` unchanged.
- CX-120 (a world slippery on one side only): the task is preserved in law when the side is drawn symmetrically; `preserves_task` says nothing about any single world.
- The two-latch case (two identical latches, exactly one to be closed): the task is preserved by the mirror; that a tied deterministic policy fails on it is a property of the policy class and is not reported as a break of the task.
- Negative controls: a checker that treats absent `rep` as `invariant` (the fail-closed test fails); a parity rule that omits det(P) for axial vectors (the roll-rate row fails); a formula comparison that sorts the operands of `U`.
- Determinism: all outputs exact (D0): twice, debug and release, across platforms.

## Considerations

- The vocabulary must cover what the shipped task families use; a term that fits no kind is declared `fixed` and costs the task its symmetry until a kind is added by a schema version.
- Invariance in law is decided from declared distributions. A draw bound to a card uncertainty inherits the card's interval; two cards that g exchanges must be the same card or have identical intervals.
- A task can be preserved while a controller is not equivariant; consumers that test an interface use prescribed command sequences for that reason.
- The check is per element and takes milliseconds; consumers call it for every element of Γ_mech (orders of two to twelve).
