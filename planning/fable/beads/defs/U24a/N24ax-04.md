## Background

Arena's metric registry (`registries/metrics.toml`, bead N24a-17) holds one record per metric: identifier,
unit, the definition on file, the channels it reads, how one episode's value is formed and how episodes
combine. Every scorecard reads it. It says nothing about whether a metric is a quantity a gradient can be
taken of.

Three mechanisms take gradients of a metric with respect to design parameters, and each is admitted on
**smooth metrics only**:

- the paired finite-difference design gradient of Phylum: "Energy, tracking error and margins are admitted.
  A binary outcome (a fall, a mode switch) has zero gradient almost everywhere and a finite-difference
  variance that grows as 1/δ; it is refused (`FROB-REF-540`)", where δ is the perturbation size;
- the step adjoints of Kinesis, whose guards include "smooth metrics only; a binary outcome is refused" in
  the same way;
- short-horizon gradient training with design parameters (Moonshot), which consumes those adjoints.

FROB-REF-540 is the Phylum refusal "gradient unreliable: non-smooth metric, horizon above the admitted cap,
pairs diverged early, or an uncontrolled determinism class", with the repairs: shorten the horizon; change
the metric; use rank-based search. For its first sub-reason the estimator must know what kind of quantity
the metric is. Without a declaration it would guess from the metric's name or unit, and a guess fails in
both directions: a success rate has the unit of a pure number, as does a normalized tracking score.

DESIGN decision: smoothness is a required attribute of every metric record, chosen from a closed set, and
the admission tests read it. Rejected alternatives: a table inside the estimator (two consumers would keep
two tables, and a new metric would be absent from both); inferring it from `per_episode` (a `count`
reduction is recognizable, a hidden threshold inside a definition is not); numerical probing by perturbing
a parameter and looking for jumps (it costs episodes and cannot prove smoothness). Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: the attribute, its closed value set and the rule for composite metrics; the loader rule that refuses a
record without it; the classification of the fourteen core metrics (the sixteen rows of section 3); the
records this bead adds so that a gradient can name a smooth form where the core metric is not one (section
3a); the query the admission tests call; the column in `eval.scorecard` metadata.

Out: the registry, its loader and the `Metric` trait (N24a-17); each metric's computation (N24a-18 to
N24a-21); the gradient estimators and their other admission tests (N17b-22 and the step-adjoint beads);
the registry row of FROB-REF-540 (N17-REF).

## Technical approach

**1. The attribute (DESIGN).** `smoothness` on each record of `registries/metrics.toml`, one of:

| Value | Meaning | Per-episode form | Gradient admission |
|---|---|---|---|
| `smooth-accumulation` | J(T) = Σ_{t<T} j_t with j_t a continuous function of state and input between events | A sum or a time integral | Admitted |
| `margin` | An extremum over time of a continuous quantity (a minimum clearance, a requirement margin) | A minimum or maximum | Admitted; the gradient is that of the binding instant, one-sided where two instants tie |
| `binary` | An indicator of an outcome: success, fall, collision, a verdict, a mode switch | 0 or 1 | Refused |
| `count` | An integer number of events | A count | Refused |
| `threshold-time` | A time at which a condition first held, possibly censored | A time or `censored` | Refused (the value jumps when an event appears or disappears, and censoring puts a point mass at the horizon) |
| `quantile` | A quantile or a tail mean of another metric over the randomization | Defined only over a batch | Refused for per-pair estimators (a pair of worlds has no quantile); the underlying metric's value applies if the consumer differentiates the per-episode quantity |
| `ratio-of-totals` | A total divided by a total that can be zero or can change by a binary outcome | Defined only over a batch | Refused |

A record also carries `smooth_in`: the list of parameter classes for which the metric is smooth by its
definition (`design-continuous`, `policy`, `none`), and for `quantile` the field `of` naming the underlying
metric. A metric reported in two forms has one record per form, each with its own value.

**2. Loader rule.** A record without `smoothness`, or with a value outside the set, fails to load (a
registry error at build time; the loader is the only constructor of a metric). The value must be
consistent with `per_episode`: `count` with an integer-count reduction; `margin` with a `min` or `max`
reduction over a state or interval-extremum channel; `smooth-accumulation` with a sum or duration-weighted
mean over an interval-mean, impulse or state channel; `binary` with a verdict or termination-class read.
An inconsistent pair fails the same test.

**3. Classification of the core set (normative; each metric's record carries the value).** This bead (M2) writes
the value into every record the registry holds at M2, the records of N24a-17 to N24a-20. Maneuver cost and
fallback count are records of N24a-21 (M3), which registers them with the values below; until then their rows
are the rule that bead follows, and the loader rule of section 2 refuses either record without them.

| Metric | `smoothness` | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Tracking error | `smooth-accumulation` | Root mean square of a continuous error |
| Success, fall and collision rates | `binary` | Per-episode indicators |
| Requirement margin | `margin` | With the binding atom and its time; an `:exact` atom's margin of +1 or −1 is `binary` |
| Time to verdict | `threshold-time` | Right-censored at the horizon |
| Cost of transport, mechanical and electrical | `smooth-accumulation` | Per episode over the distance travelled; undefined at zero distance |
| Energy per completed task, conditional on completion | `ratio-of-totals` | The conditioning event is binary |
| Energy per completed task, all attempts over completions | `ratio-of-totals` | Undefined at zero completions |
| Episode energy (the numerator of the two above) | `smooth-accumulation` | Registered as its own record so that a gradient can name it |
| Froude number | `smooth-accumulation` | v² / (g × leg length) with v a mean speed |
| Actuator utilization | `smooth-accumulation` | A fraction of time at a limit is an integral of an indicator: registered `binary` for the at-limit fraction and `smooth-accumulation` for the mean normalized load |
| Friction utilization | `margin` for its maximum; `quantile` for a distribution summary | Of ‖γₜ‖ / (μ·γₙ) over stance |
| Slip distance | `smooth-accumulation` | A time integral, zero below the normal-force floor |
| Clearance | `margin` | A minimum distance |
| Tail robustness | `quantile` | 10% conditional value at risk, `of` the task score |
| Maneuver cost | `smooth-accumulation` | Path length and time |
| Fallback count | `count` | By kind |

**3a. Records this bead adds (DESIGN identifiers).** Each is a full record (unit, definition on file, channels,
per-episode form, how episodes combine) built by the loader like any other, and each reads channels that the
owning metric bead already records, so it adds no channel:

| Record | Definition | Channels from | `smoothness` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `episode-energy` | Net energy of one episode from start to adjudication (the numerator of both per-completion figures), J | N24a-18 | `smooth-accumulation` |
| `actuator-load-mean` | Per joint, the time mean over the scoring window of the torque magnitude divided by the card's torque limit, dimensionless | N24a-19 | `smooth-accumulation` |
| `friction-utilization-max` | Per episode, the maximum of ‖γₜ‖ / (μ·γₙ) over stance contacts | N24a-20 | `margin` |

The core records keep their identifiers: `actuator-utilization` (the at-limit fractions) is `binary` and
friction utilization's distribution summary is `quantile`.

**4. The query (DESIGN; `crates/frob-arena/src/metrics/smoothness.rs`).**

```rust
pub enum Smoothness { SmoothAccumulation, Margin, Binary, Count, ThresholdTime, Quantile { of: MetricId }, RatioOfTotals }
pub enum GradientAdmission { Admitted { one_sided_at_ties: bool }, Refused { reason: &'static str, repairs: &'static [&'static str] } }
pub fn gradient_admission(metric: MetricId, wrt: ParamClass) -> GradientAdmission;
```

Admitted exactly for `SmoothAccumulation` and `Margin` when `wrt` is in the record's `smooth_in`. A refusal
carries the sub-reason `non-smooth-metric`, the metric's value, and the repairs of FROB-REF-540 in order:
shorten the horizon is omitted (it does not help here), then "change the metric" with the admitted metrics
of the same task listed, then "use rank-based search". The consumer raises FROB-REF-540 with
`pointer = metric`; this bead raises nothing itself.

**5. Surface.** `frob eval scorecard --json` metadata lists each metric's `smoothness`, so an agent can see
before asking which metrics a gradient verb will accept. Determinism exact; no evidence claim.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every record of `registries/metrics.toml` has a `smoothness` from the closed set; removing it from any record fails the registry load test.
- [ ] The consistency rule rejects `smooth-accumulation` on a metric whose `per_episode` is an event count, and `margin` on a sum.
- [ ] `gradient_admission` admits tracking error, both costs of transport, episode energy, slip distance, clearance and the requirement margin for `design-continuous`, and refuses the three rates, time to verdict, both energy-per-completed-task forms and tail robustness, each with `non-smooth-metric` (maneuver cost and fallback count join the same matrix when N24a-21 registers them at M3).
- [ ] The requirement margin of a verdict whose binding atom is `:exact` is refused.
- [ ] The three added records (`episode-energy`, `actuator-load-mean`, `friction-utilization-max`) load, carry their `smoothness`, are admitted for `design-continuous`, and reproduce their known answers.
- [ ] The paired finite-difference estimator's metric admission (when present) calls this query and contains no metric names of its own (checked by a source search in a test).
- [ ] The scorecard metadata shows the attribute; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-arena` lists the values and the admission rule.

- `core_classification_table`: every row above whose record is in the registry, value by value (at M2 all but

- `quantile_names_its_metric`: a `quantile` record without `of` fails to load.
- `refusal_lists_admitted_alternatives`: a refused success rate on the flat velocity task lists tracking error and cost of transport.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/metric_smoothness.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; idempotent):
1. `frob eval scorecard <the reference run of the flat velocity task, BD-201> --json`: assert each metric's `smoothness` in the metadata block.
2. `frob phylum gradient --design <design> --task <task> --metric success-rate --estimate-only --key ms-grad-success --json` (verb `phylum.gradient`, a `run` verb, so it carries a key; the metric admission is the estimator's, N17b-22, which depends on this bead: the script asks `frob capabilities --json` first and, until the estimator is in the build, logs `e2e_skip 'needs N17b-22: phylum.gradient metric admission'`, so this bead closes on step 1 and its unit tests): exit code 3, FROB-REF-540, `pointer = metric`, sub-reason `non-smooth-metric`, zero world-steps.
3. The same with `--metric tracking-error --key ms-grad-tracking`: admitted, the estimate returned with no refusal (`--estimate-only`: the admission runs before any world, and the gradient run itself belongs to the estimator's script).
Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, one `assert` event per metric with
expected and observed value, and the `refusal` event with code, pointer and repairs.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answer (why a binary outcome is refused), reproduced by the unit test `variance_scaling_demo` on `synthetic` data with 10⁵ pairs. Indicator J = 1[θ + ε > 0] with ε uniform on [−1, 1], evaluated at θ = 0 in paired worlds θ ± δ sharing ε: the two worlds differ with probability δ and the central difference is then 1/(2δ), so its mean is 1/2 (the true slope) and its variance is 1/(4δ) − 1/4: 24.75 at δ = 0.01 and 12.25 at δ = 0.02, a ratio of 2.0 ± 0.2 (the variance grows as 1/δ). Quadratic J = (θ + ε)²: the paired central difference is 2·(θ + ε) for every δ, variance 4/3, ratio 1.0 ± 0.05.
- Negative control `guess-by-unit`: a mutant query that admits every dimensionless metric; `admission_matrix` must fail on the success rate.
- Negative control `missing-attribute-defaults-smooth`: a loader that defaults the attribute; `every_metric_declares_smoothness` is replaced by a planted record without the field, which must be refused.
- Determinism: registry load and query are exact, identical in debug and release and across platforms.

## Considerations

- `smooth-accumulation` is a statement about the metric's definition, not about the trajectory: contact changes still make a rollout non-smooth, which is why the estimators keep their own horizon, divergence and step-label tests. This attribute removes one kind of misuse only.
- Margins are admitted with a flag because the minimizing instant can change; consumers that cannot handle one-sided values treat the flag as a refusal.
- New metrics must pick a value; when none fits, the set is extended by a registry schema version, not by `smooth_in = none` on a wrong value.
- The split of actuator and friction utilization into two records each is a DESIGN choice of this bead so that their smooth forms can be differentiated; the scorecard still prints them together.
