## Background

One comparison under an e-process is valid under optional stopping. It does nothing about an agent that submits
fifty candidates to the same held-out scenes until one passes: with independent attempts at level 5% each,
fourteen null submissions give a 51% chance of at least one false promotion. The **blinded qualification lane**
closes that hole, and `eval.qualify` is its verb. A design comparison that is to be reported as a claim goes
through this lane; `eval.compare` on discovery worlds is a development result.

**Lanes.** The discovery lane is everything an agent may read while it designs: training scenes, the
development splits, batteries, the hard-world set, its own runs and telemetry. The confirmation lane is a
service inside the daemon that owns a sealed family of worlds and runs registered requests against it. A
**campaign** is a set of confirmation requests that share a claim family (a task, a baseline class and a
held-out world family) and a total error budget α; all agents on the claim family share its index sequence.

**The protocol for request i of a campaign.**

1. *Commit.* The request names candidate, baseline, task, claim, study type and rule, tier plan, number of
   clusters and budget. It is hashed into the ledger with an idempotency key before any confirmation world
   exists. For a fixed-n study the pre-analysis is computed at this request's level and attached. (N24b-16.)
2. *Spend.* The request receives the level α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)] and the ledger records it. The index advances
   for every committed request, including ones that end inconclusive or are refused after commitment. (N24b-15.)
3. *Draw.* The service derives fresh world clusters from a sealed key, the campaign identifier, the index and a
   cluster counter. No cluster serves two requests and none has appeared in any discovery dataset. (N24b-17.)
4. *Run.* Candidate and baseline run paired on the clusters at the tier plan, with promotion to a higher tier
   by the trust-ladder rule. The study runs at level α_i with clusters as units.
5. *Answer.* The agent receives a receipt and nothing else: the decision, the index, the level spent, the
   number of units used and a hash.
6. *Close.* When the campaign closes, the key is revealed, the clusters and their episodes become readable, and
   the family is retired into discovery. (N24b-17.)

This bead builds steps 4 and 5, the verb, and the composition of all six into one service. Why the error rate
holds: the levels sum to α·m/(m + 1) after m requests; request i tests a candidate chosen with everything seen
before on clusters independent of everything seen before, so conditional on the past the chance of a false
promotion at request i is at most α_i; a union bound gives at most α for the campaign, for any number of
retries and any adaptive strategy. ADR-ARENA-08 (decision): discovery and confirmation are separate lanes with
separate data rights.

## Scope

In: the qualification executor inside the daemon; paired execution on clusters under the tier plan with
promotion; the study at level α_i; the receipt; the verb `eval.qualify` (including `dry`); refusals and exit
codes; cancellation and budget stops; the seed done-condition measured through the verb on the synthetic

500-retry gate at full scale (BD-260); standing, citation and the bundle (N24b-19); offline replay (BD-383).

## Technical approach

**The executor.** A service of the daemon that alone holds the handle to the confirmation store (a separate
root with a separate key) and the sealed key. For a committed request it: (1) re-hashes the request against its
commitment (FROB-REF-841 on a mismatch); (2) asks N24b-17 for the request's clusters; (3) schedules candidate
and baseline on every cluster, paired by cluster and world address, each design from its own settled state,
dispatched into slots of the persistent environments like any other episodes but with outputs written only to
the confirmation store; (4) evaluates requirement verdicts per episode and reduces them to one outcome per
cluster by the map the study declared (clusters are the units of inference); (5) applies the tier plan: a
result from a fast tier is provisional, and the plan's `promote_to` tier re-runs the units the promotion rule
selects (at least every unit whose outcome decides the verdict; the trust-ladder rule and its decision table
are the promotion bead's); (6) runs the study at level α_i: the fixed-n rule once at n complete units, or the
e-process consuming units in address order, never in completion order, so preemption and reordering by the
daemon leave the evidence unchanged; (7) maps the study's verdict to the decision and writes the receipt and
the ledger outcome.

**Decision mapping.** Study verdict `Promote` → `promoted`. `Inferior` or `PracticalTie` → `not-promoted`.
`Inconclusive`, a budget stop, a cancellation, or too many invalid units for the rule to be evaluated on
complete pairs → `inconclusive`. Invalid and unresolved units are counted, never dropped; a preference needs
complete pairs.

**The receipt, schema `frob.qualify.receipt/1`.**

```json
{ "schema": "frob.qualify.receipt/1", "campaign": "q:threshold-delivery/3", "index": 7,
  "alpha_i": "0.05/56", "alpha_spent": "0.05*7/8", "decision": "not-promoted", "units_used": 256,
  "sealed_until_close": ["world addresses", "per-unit outcomes", "telemetry", "stills"],
  "receipt_hash": "b3:90c2…", "authorization": {"execution_authorized": false,
  "hardware_safety_signoff": false, "sim_to_real_certified": false} }
```

Type sketch (DESIGN): `QualifyReceipt { campaign, index, alpha_i: Ratio, alpha_spent: Ratio, decision:
Promoted | NotPromoted | Inconclusive, units_used: u32, receipt_hash: Blake3, no_authorization }`. The receipt
hash covers the request hash, the decision, the level and the Merkle root of the request's cluster digests, so
the offline auditor can later tie the receipt to the revealed worlds. Nothing else is returned: no count of
successes, no interval, no e-value, no address, no failure reason. The envelope's evidence block: class
`Tested`; tier (the promoted tier where promotion ran); determinism class; `claims: ["Design comparison"]`;
`reality: "no-claim"`.

**The verb.** Registry (DESIGN): `eval.qualify`, cluster `eval`, kind `run`, first milestone M3, capabilities
`["qual:run"]`, input schema `frob.eval.qualify.in/1`, output schema `frob.qualify.receipt/1`, refusals
`FROB-REF-840` to `-845`, `-532`, `-533`, `-552`, the D4 refusal, the idempotency refusals and the budget
family. It is a long tool: it takes an idempotency key and a budget, accepts a bounded synchronous wait and
otherwise returns a job handle (exit code 8). Input: `campaign` or claim family, `candidate`, optional
`baseline`, `task`, `claim`, `study`, `tier_plan`, `clusters`, `budget`, `dry`. With `dry: true` it returns the
preview of N24b-16 and commits nothing. Progress events of a running request carry only episodes done of total;
no running count of outcomes.

**Refusals of the lane (meaning; exit code; first repair).**

| Code | Meaning | Exit | First repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| FROB-REF-840 | A session without `qual:admin` asks for sealed confirmation data before close | 5 | Wait for the campaign's close; nothing an agent holds changes this |
| FROB-REF-841 | A confirmation request was not committed first, or differs from its commitment | 3 | Submit the changed request as a new request |
| FROB-REF-842 | The units the request needs at its level exceed its budget; the reachable verdicts are attached | 3 | Raise the budget or change the claim |
| FROB-REF-843 | The candidate's lineage contains data from the sealed family | 3 | Submit a candidate with a clean lineage |
| FROB-REF-844 | A cluster would be used twice | 3 | Read the stored derivation; never reuse a counter |
| FROB-REF-845 | A result is cited as qualified without a promoting receipt | 3 | Cite the result as a development result, or qualify it |

**Failure after commitment.** A cancelled request, a budget stop or an executor fault ends with decision
`inconclusive`, the level stays spent and the clusters stay consumed; cancellation drains (episodes in flight
are discarded, nothing sealed is released, device resources are freed). A transport failure during the wait
leaves the job recoverable by its idempotency key: one request and one index exist, or none.

**What a retry costs.** Relative to a single test at α = 0.05, request 1 needs about 1.23 times the units,
request 10 about 2.57 times, request 100 about 4.08 times and request 500 about 5.15 times. For the rule of
fixture 1 a practical tie first becomes reachable at n = 99 for request 1, n = 178 for request 10 and n = 328
for request 500, and at 64 pairs request 1 has 478 preferring outcomes of 2,145 where the development level had
521. The dry run prints the figures for the caller's own request.

Code: `crates/frob-arena/src/qualify/{executor,receipt}.rs`; verb registration in `frob-verbs`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Through `frob eval qualify` on the synthetic no-signal family with the scripted adversary: a planted true gain of the declared size is promoted within 10 requests in at least 80% of campaigns; the same adversary against reused clusters at a constant level (the mutant lane) obtains a false promotion in more than 99% of campaigns and is thereby caught (the seed done-condition).
- [ ] A request on a physical task (a small sealed family of cart-and-sill conditions registered by the test under a test campaign; the full fixture holdout is bead N24b-10) runs paired on fresh clusters at its tier plan and returns a receipt whose JSON has exactly the listed fields.
- [ ] The receipt's level strings equal α/[i·(i + 1)] and α·i/(i + 1) for its index; `eval.ledger` shows the same.
- [ ] The decision is a pure function of the committed request, the revealed clusters and the outcomes: after close, recomputation at level α_i reproduces it.
- [ ] Preempting and reordering the request's episodes inside the daemon does not change the decision or `units_used`.
- [ ] Cancellation after commitment: outcome `inconclusive`, level spent, no sealed data released, no leaked device resource.
- [ ] Each refusal 840 to 845 is triggered by its planted case with its exit code; the verb is in the registry with schemas and is reachable through the CLI and the MCP surface.
- [ ] `crates/frob-arena/CONTRACT.md` states the lane's inputs, outputs, invariants (INV-QUAL-01 to -04), refusals, determinism class and known gaps.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `receipt_has_only_listed_fields`: schema test; adding any field fails.
- `receipt_levels_for_index`: index 1 → `0.05/2`, `0.05*1/2`; index 7 → `0.05/56`, `0.05*7/8`.
- `decision_mapping`: the four study verdicts and the three failure cases map as specified.
- `clusters_are_units`: 256 clusters of 3 worlds each give `units_used` = 256.
- `evidence_consumed_in_address_order`: an e-process fed the same units in two completion orders returns the same e-value.
- `promotion_before_decision`: with tier plan batch-cpu → referee, a unit whose fast-tier outcome is contradicted on the referee takes the referee's outcome.
- `cancel_after_commit_spends_level`.
- `refusals_840_to_845`: one planted case each (840: `telemetry.query` on the confirmation run from a session without `qual:admin`; 841: tampered request; 842: budget for 64 clusters at α_10; 843: tainted lineage; 844: forced counter reuse; 845: a report citing a discovery comparison as qualified).
- `dry_commits_nothing`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/arena_qualification_lane.sh` (bead N24b-24): the whole protocol from `session open` with
`qual:run`, a dry run, a committed request, the receipt, `eval.ledger`, a refused sealed read, to an admin close
and reveal; and the reduced planted-gain and reused-cluster campaigns through the verb. Logged per STD-E2E:
every command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block, the request hash, ledger sequences, level
strings, receipt fields, refusal codes; a final summary naming GATE-QUAL-01 (power half and control). The log
contains no sealed field before the close step, and the script greps its own log to assert that.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-619 (level series), KA-620 (fourteen nulls: 51%), KA-621 (retry costs; 99, 178, 328; 478), KA-622 (the gate:
at most 123 of 2,000; 80% within 10; control above 99%). CX-267 (resubmission to the same scenes); CX-384 (let
`eval.qualify` say which worlds failed: an agent could search near those worlds, patch for them and resubmit,
and the α_i schedule would then bound an error rate for a procedure that is no longer the one being run).
Negative controls: the reused-cluster constant-level lane; the leaking-receipt lane. Metamorphic (MR-13): the
decision of request i does not depend on requests after it. Determinism lanes: after close, replay of a
request's episodes reproduces digests in class D2; the decision is D0 given the outcomes.

## Considerations

- OPEN-ARENA-3 (owner: project lead; blocks GATE-QUAL-01): who holds `qual:admin` and the sealed families. Fail-closed default: the owner alone opens and closes campaigns; one sealed family per task family, sized for 20 requests at 256 clusters; no result is called qualified until a family exists.
- Fallback if the 500-retry gate fails (BET-21): nothing is called qualified, and confirmation falls back to fixed-n exact studies with a hard cap on submissions per campaign, before any tournament is scheduled.
- The ten-fold tournament's adjudicator runs this same protocol on its own sealed key; the executor therefore takes its key and family from the campaign, never from global configuration.
- Timing is a side channel the lane accepts: `units_used` and compute cost are in the receipt and envelope; per-unit timing is not exposed.
- A request with a sampling-controller arm needs a frozen, non-privileged, latency-measured planner record; a task needs an audit record with no unresolved finding. Both are admission checks owned elsewhere and consulted in N24b-16.
- RISK-34 (qualification gamed by adaptive retries or by a leak from sealed worlds; warning signs: a receipt field outside the listed set, requests without a close): mitigated by this bead with N24b-15 to -18, BD-260, BD-261 and BD-383.
