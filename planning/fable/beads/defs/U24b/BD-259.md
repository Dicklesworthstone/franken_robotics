## Background

One comparison under an e-process is valid under optional stopping. It does nothing about an agent that submits
fifty candidates to the same held-out scenes until one passes: with independent attempts at level 5% each,
fourteen null submissions give a 51% chance of at least one false promotion. The **blinded qualification lane**
closes that hole, and `eval.qualify` is its verb. A design comparison that is to be reported as a claim goes
through this lane; `eval.compare` on discovery worlds is a development result.

**Lanes.** The discovery lane is everything an agent may read while it designs: training scenes, the
development splits, batteries, the hard-world set, its own runs and telemetry. The confirmation lane is a
service inside the daemon that owns a sealed family of worlds and runs registered requests against it. A
**campaign** is a set of confirmation requests that share a claim family (a task, a baseline class and a
held-out world family) and a total error budget α; all agents on the claim family share its index sequence.

**The protocol for request i of a campaign.**

1. *Commit.* The request names candidate, baseline, task, claim, study type and rule, tier plan, number of
   clusters and budget. It is hashed into the ledger with an idempotency key before any confirmation world
   exists. For a fixed-n study the pre-analysis is computed at this request's level and attached. (N24b-16.)
2. *Spend.* The request receives the level α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)] and the ledger records it. The index advances
   for every committed request, including ones that end inconclusive or are refused after commitment. (N24b-15.)
3. *Draw.* The service derives fresh world clusters from a sealed key, the campaign identifier, the index and a
   cluster counter. No cluster serves two requests and none has appeared in any discovery dataset. (N24b-17.)
4. *Run.* Candidate and baseline run paired on the clusters at the tier plan, with promotion to a higher tier
   by the trust-ladder rule. The study runs at level α_i with clusters as units.
5. *Answer.* The agent receives a receipt and nothing else: the decision, the index, the level spent, the
   number of units used and a hash.
6. *Close.* When the campaign closes, the key is revealed, the clusters and their episodes become readable, and
   the family is retired into discovery. (N24b-17.)

This bead builds steps 4 and 5, the verb, and the composition of all six into one service. Why the error rate
holds: the levels sum to α·m/(m + 1) after m requests; request i tests a candidate chosen with everything seen
before on clusters independent of everything seen before, so conditional on the past the chance of a false
promotion at request i is at most α_i; a union bound gives at most α for the campaign, for any number of
retries and any adaptive strategy. ADR-ARENA-08 (decision): discovery and confirmation are separate lanes with
separate data rights.

## Scope

## Technical approach

**The executor.** A service of the daemon that alone holds the handle to the confirmation store (a separate
root with a separate key) and the sealed key. For a committed request it: (1) re-hashes the request against its
commitment (FROB-REF-841 on a mismatch); (2) asks N24b-17 for the request's clusters; (3) schedules candidate
and baseline on every cluster, paired by cluster and world address, each design from its own settled state,
dispatched into slots of the persistent environments like any other episodes but with outputs written only to
the confirmation store; (4) evaluates requirement verdicts per episode and reduces them to one outcome per
cluster by the map the study declared (clusters are the units of inference); (5) applies the tier plan: a
result from a fast tier is provisional, and the plan's `promote_to` tier re-runs the units the promotion rule
selects (at least every unit whose outcome decides the verdict; the trust-ladder rule and its decision table
are the promotion bead's); (6) runs the study at level α_i: the fixed-n rule once at n complete units, or the
e-process consuming units in address order, never in completion order, so preemption and reordering by the
daemon leave the evidence unchanged; (7) maps the study's verdict to the decision and writes the receipt and
the ledger outcome.

**Decision mapping, per registered rule (N24b-06).**

| Rule | `promoted` | `not-promoted` | `inconclusive` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `paired-clopper-pearson/1` (preference) | verdict `Promote` | `Inferior` or `PracticalTie` | `Inconclusive` |
| `one-sided-binomial-bound/1` (absolute) | the exact upper bound on f failing units of n is at most p_max | the bound exceeds p_max | — |
| `paired-total-discordance/1` (equivalence) | the exact upper bound on d discordant pairs of n is at most the margin | the bound exceeds the margin | — |
| anytime-valid e-process | the e-value crosses 1/α_i | — | the request's units are spent without a crossing |

For every rule, a budget stop, a cancellation, or too many invalid units for the rule to be evaluated on
complete units (complete pairs for a paired rule) → `inconclusive`. Invalid and unresolved units are counted,
never dropped; a preference needs complete pairs.

**The receipt, schema `frob.qualify.receipt/1`.**

```json
{ "schema": "frob.qualify.receipt/1", "campaign": "q:threshold-delivery/3", "index": 7,
  "alpha_i": "0.05/56", "alpha_spent": "0.05*7/8", "decision": "not-promoted", "units_used": 256,
  "sealed_until_close": ["world addresses", "per-unit outcomes", "telemetry", "stills"],
  "receipt_hash": "b3:90c2…", "authorization": {"execution_authorized": false,
  "hardware_safety_signoff": false, "sim_to_real_certified": false} }
```

Type sketch (DESIGN): `QualifyReceipt { campaign, index, alpha_i: Ratio, alpha_spent: Ratio, decision:
Promoted | NotPromoted | Inconclusive, units_used: u32, receipt_hash: Blake3, no_authorization }`. The receipt
hash covers the request hash, the decision, the level and the Merkle root of the request's cluster digests, so
the offline auditor can later tie the receipt to the revealed worlds. Nothing else is returned: no count of
successes, no interval, no e-value, no address, no failure reason. The envelope's evidence block: class
`Tested`; tier (the promoted tier where promotion ran); determinism class; `claims: ["Design comparison"]`;
`reality: "no-claim"`.

**The verb.** Registry (VERBS-eval's row): `eval.qualify`, cluster `eval`, kind `run`, first milestone M3,
capabilities `["qual:run"]`, input schema `frob.eval.qualify.in/1`, output schema `frob.qualify.receipt/1`.
Chapter refusals, checked before commitment (a refused request advances no index and spends no level): `-502` (an
`in-sample` input, at the commit step; N24a-31); `-532`; `-533` (a paired study with an arm of an uncontrolled
class, D4 included, from `study::admit`); `-543` and `-556` (the pairing rules, through `study::admit`); `-552`;
`-567` (a hard-world or search-selected world set offered as the confirmation sample: the request may name no
worlds, only the campaign's sealed family; N24b-16); `-817` (sampling-controller episodes offered as the scoring
source; N22a-25); `-825` (a result of class D4 or unrecorded offered as evidence, or a tier plan naming
`fast-training`; N24b-16's admission); `-840` to `-845`; the symbolic campaign-state refusals
`campaign-not-open`, `claim-family-mismatch` and `campaign-request-cap` (N24-REF, exit 3) and the other pending
names of the row, each raised by the bead the row names; and the kind's codes: `-720` (no `qual:run`, exit 5),
`-803` (key reused with a different request, exit 7), `-805` (no key, exit 3) and the budget family (`-701`, exit
4). It is a long tool: it takes an idempotency key and a budget, accepts a bounded synchronous wait
(`sync_wait_ms`) and otherwise returns a job handle (exit code 8). Input: `campaign` or claim family, `design` (the
candidate design; the verb resolves it to `candidate: {design, controller}` with the design's frozen controller
record, which is the long form a caller may give instead, `--candidate design=<d>,controller=<c>`), optional
`baseline` (absent for an absolute claim such as "failure probability of `delivered` at most 0.05"), `task`,
`claim`, optional `study`, `tier_plan`, `clusters` and `budget` (defaults filled by N24b-16 from the claim and the
task's registered rule, and echoed in the commitment), `dry`, and `sampling` (accepted only to be refused unless
`nominal`, N24c-32 and N24c-35). The worked agent session calls it with only `design`, `task`, `claim`,
`idempotency_key` and `sync_wait_ms`, and that call must succeed. With `dry: true` it returns the preview of
N24b-16 and commits nothing. Progress events of a running request carry only episodes done of total; no running
count of outcomes.

**The mission-bound form (§32.16's written session).** In place of `task`, `claim` and the campaign a caller may
give `mission`, a `MissionId` of the mission store (BD-126):

    frob eval qualify carrier-b --mission q:5b1e… --key qual-1 --json

Before commitment the verb reads the stored query and resolves it: `task` is the task whose requirement terms the
mission's acceptance predicates reference, `claim` is that requirement as the claim (the absolute template at the
failure bound 1 − p of the mission's `prob` binder, or the preference template when the query names a
baseline), and the campaign is the open campaign of the resulting claim family (none open: `campaign-not-open`).
The resolved `task` and `claim` enter the commitment exactly as if the caller had given them, beside `mission`,
so the request hash covers all three and the dry run echoes them. DESIGN: a mission whose predicates do not
reference the requirement terms of exactly one task names no claim family and is refused before commitment with
`claim-family-mismatch`, pointer `/mission`. `mission` given together with `task` or `claim` is FROB-REF-802
(exit 3), nothing committed.

**Refusals of the lane (meaning; exit code; first repair).**

| Code | Meaning | Exit | First repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| FROB-REF-840 | A session without `qual:admin` asks for sealed confirmation data before close | 5 | Wait for the campaign's close; nothing an agent holds changes this |
| FROB-REF-841 | A confirmation request was not committed first, or differs from its commitment | 3 | Submit the changed request as a new request |
| FROB-REF-842 | The units the request needs at its level exceed its budget; the reachable verdicts are attached | 3 | Raise the budget or change the claim |
| FROB-REF-843 | The candidate's lineage contains data from the sealed family | 3 | Submit a candidate with a clean lineage |
| FROB-REF-844 | A cluster would be used twice | 3 | Read the stored derivation; never reuse a counter |
| FROB-REF-845 | A result is cited as qualified without a promoting receipt | 3 | Cite the result as a development result, or qualify it |

**Failure after commitment.** A cancelled request, a budget stop or an executor fault ends with decision
`inconclusive`, the level stays spent and the clusters stay consumed; cancellation drains (episodes in flight
are discarded, nothing sealed is released, device resources are freed). A transport failure during the wait
leaves the job recoverable by its idempotency key: one request and one index exist, or none.

**What a retry costs.** Relative to a single test at α = 0.05, request 1 needs about 1.23 times the units,
request 10 about 2.57 times, request 100 about 4.08 times and request 500 about 5.15 times. For the rule of
fixture 1 a practical tie first becomes reachable at n = 99 for request 1, n = 178 for request 10 and n = 328
for request 500, and at 64 pairs request 1 has 478 preferring outcomes of 2,145 where the development level had
521. The dry run prints the figures for the caller's own request.

Code: `crates/frob-arena/src/qualify/{executor,receipt}.rs`; verb registration in `frob-verbs`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Through `frob eval qualify` on the synthetic no-signal family with the scripted adversary: a planted true gain of the declared size is promoted within 10 requests in at least 80% of campaigns; the same adversary against reused clusters at a constant level (the mutant lane, MR-13's `nc-<nnnn>` build of N24b-18) obtains a false promotion in more than 99% of campaigns on N24b-18's `absolute-above` world and is thereby caught (the seed done-condition); on the two paired worlds, where the conservative paired rule cannot reach 99%, the fraction lies inside the exact 99% interval of N24b-18's computed expectation.
- [ ] A request on a physical task (a small sealed family of cart-and-sill conditions registered by the test under a test campaign; the full fixture holdout is bead N24b-10) runs paired on fresh clusters at its tier plan and returns a receipt whose JSON has exactly the listed fields.
- [ ] The receipt's level strings equal α/[i·(i + 1)] and α·i/(i + 1) for its index; `eval.ledger` shows the same.
- [ ] The decision is a pure function of the committed request, the revealed clusters and the outcomes: after close, recomputation at level α_i reproduces it.
- [ ] Preempting and reordering the request's episodes inside the daemon does not change the decision or `units_used`.
- [ ] Cancellation after commitment: outcome `inconclusive`, level spent, no sealed data released, no leaked device resource.
- [ ] Each of the lane's refusals that this bead or its components raise, 841, 842, 843, 844 and 567, is triggered through the verb by its planted case with exit code 3; the verb is in the registry with schemas and all of 840 to 845 in its refusal list, and is reachable through the CLI and the MCP surface. FROB-REF-840 is raised by the firewall (BD-261) and FROB-REF-845 by the standing check (N24b-19), both of which depend on this bead; their planted cases are asserted there and in N24b-24, not here.
- [ ] The worked session's minimal call (`design`, `task`, `claim`, key, `sync_wait_ms`; absolute claim, no baseline) commits, runs and returns a receipt with `index: 1`, `alpha_i: "0.05/2"`, `alpha_spent: "0.05*1/2"` and `units_used: 200`.
- [ ] The mission-bound form `frob eval qualify <design> --mission <MissionId> --key <k> --json` commits a request whose `task`, `claim` and campaign equal those of the same request written out by hand, and whose request hash differs from it only by the recorded `mission`; `--mission` with `--task` or `--claim` is FROB-REF-802 with nothing committed; a mission that names no single task's requirement is `claim-family-mismatch`.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/qualify_executor.sh` passes on a clean checkout at M3 against a real daemon.
- [ ] `crates/frob-arena/CONTRACT.md` states the lane's inputs, outputs, invariants (INV-QUAL-01 to -04), refusals, determinism class and known gaps.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `receipt_has_only_listed_fields`: schema test; adding any field fails.
- `receipt_levels_for_index`: index 1 → `0.05/2`, `0.05*1/2`; index 7 → `0.05/56`, `0.05*7/8`.
- `decision_mapping`: the four paired verdicts and the three failure cases map as specified; absolute rule at n = 200, level 0.05/2, p_max 0.05: f = 1 (bound 0.0275) and f = 3 (0.0432) → `promoted`, f = 4 (0.0504) → `not-promoted`; equivalence at n = 128, level 0.05/2, margin 0.05: d = 0 (0.0284) and d = 1 (0.0428) → `promoted`, d = 2 (0.0553) → `not-promoted`; an e-process whose units are spent without a crossing → `inconclusive`.
- `clusters_are_units`: 256 clusters of 3 worlds each give `units_used` = 256.
- `evidence_consumed_in_address_order`: an e-process fed the same units in two completion orders returns the same e-value.
- `promotion_before_decision`: with tier plan batch-cpu → referee, a unit whose fast-tier outcome is contradicted on the referee takes the referee's outcome.
- `cancel_after_commit_spends_level`.
- `refusals_841_to_844_and_567`: one planted case each through the verb (841: tampered request; 842: budget for 64 clusters at α_10; 843: tainted lineage; 844: forced counter reuse; 567: a request whose `clusters` field names the `hard` battery). The 840 and 845 cases (`telemetry.query` on the confirmation run from a session without `qual:admin`; a report citing a discovery comparison as qualified) are BD-261's and N24b-19's tests.
- `levels_match_d8_oracle`: the receipt's `alpha_i` and `alpha_spent` for indices 1 to 500 equal, as exact rationals, the level series of the statistics oracle library (N12-48, Appendix D.8), which is never regenerated from this implementation.
- `design_resolves_to_candidate`: the minimal call with `design: "quad-tray"` commits a request whose `candidate` is `{design: <its DesignId>, controller: <its frozen ControllerId>}` and whose defaults (rule, clusters, tier plan, budget) are echoed in the commitment.
- `dry_commits_nothing`.
- `mission_form_resolves`: a synthetic mission whose acceptance predicate references the coin task's requirement
  and whose `prob` binder is 0.95 resolves to that task, the absolute claim at failure bound 0.05 and the open
  coin campaign; its commitment equals the hand-written request's apart from `mission`; `mission` with `task` →
  FROB-REF-802; a mission referencing two tasks → `claim-family-mismatch`; no open campaign → `campaign-not-open`.
- `refusals_before_commit_match_registry`: one planted case each for 502, 532, 533, 543, 552, 556 and 825
  through the verb (the checks are N24a-31's and N24b-16's, through `study::admit`): the code, exit 3, no index
  assigned. FROB-REF-817 is raised by N22a-25 and the `sampling` refusals by N24c-32 and N24c-35, which depend on
  this bead; their planted cases are in those beads.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead's own script, `scripts/e2e/qualify_executor.sh [--long]` (header `bead=BD-259 milestone=M3
gates=GATE-QUAL-01`), sources the STD-E2E helper and drives the real daemon, `frob` and the MCP server with this
bead's dependencies only:
1. `sessions`: `frob session open` for `owner` (the holder role of `config/qualification.toml`) and for `agent`
   (`--scopes …,qual:run`), each with its key; the owner opens the synthetic campaign `q:e2e-coin/1` with `frob
   eval ledger --action open --task <coin TaskId> --world-family <family> … --json` (N24b-15; an admin action,
   no key).
2. `minimal_call`: the worked session's call as `agent`, first with `--dry` (preview, nothing committed), then
   committed: receipt fields exactly the listed set, `index` 1, `0.05/2`, `0.05*1/2`, `units_used` 200.
3. `mission_form`: a mission created with `frob mission new … --key "$(e2e_key mis-1)"` (BD-126) and submitted
   with `--mission`; the commitment echoes the resolved task, claim and campaign; `--mission` with `--task` is
   FROB-REF-802.
4. `planted_refusals`: 841, 842, 843, 844 and 567 through the verb by their planted cases (test hooks of a test
   build), each with exit 3 and no index assigned for the pre-commit ones; a cancelled request ends
   `inconclusive` with its level spent (`frob eval ledger` shows the row).
5. `reorder`: the same request replayed under the test hook that preempts and reorders episodes; same decision
   and `units_used`.
6. `simulator`: reduced campaigns through the verb on N24b-18's synthetic family (planted gain: at least 80%
   promoted within 10 requests; with `--long` the reused-cluster constant-level lane above 99% on
   `absolute-above`).
7. `close_and_recompute`: the owner closes the campaign (`frob eval ledger q:e2e-coin/1 --action close --json`,
   no key); the decision of each receipt equals
   a recomputation from the revealed counts at α_i.
Logged per STD-E2E: every command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block, the request hash, ledger
sequences, level strings, receipt fields, refusal codes, campaign statistics against 0.80 and 0.99; a final
summary naming GATE-QUAL-01 (power half and control). The log contains no sealed field before the close step,
and the script scans its own log to assert that. `scripts/e2e/arena_qualification_lane.sh` (N24b-24) composes
this lane with the firewall (BD-261), the standing (N24b-19) and the bundle, and runs this script as a stage.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-619 (level series), KA-620 (fourteen nulls: 51%), KA-621 (retry costs; 99, 178, 328; 478), KA-622 (the gate:
at most 123 of 2,000; 80% within 10; control above 99%). CX-267 (resubmission to the same scenes); CX-384 (let
`eval.qualify` say which worlds failed: an agent could search near those worlds, patch for them and resubmit,
and the α_i schedule would then bound an error rate for a procedure that is no longer the one being run).
Negative controls: the reused-cluster constant-level lane; the leaking-receipt lane. Metamorphic (MR-13): the
decision of request i does not depend on requests after it. Determinism lanes: after close, replay of a
request's episodes reproduces digests in class D2; the decision is D0 given the outcomes.

## Considerations

- OPEN-ARENA-3 (owner: project lead; blocks GATE-QUAL-01): who holds `qual:admin` and the sealed families. Fail-closed default: the owner alone opens and closes campaigns; one sealed family per task family, sized for 20 requests at 256 clusters; no result is called qualified until a family exists.
- Fallback if the 500-retry gate fails (BET-21): nothing is called qualified, and confirmation falls back to fixed-n exact studies with a hard cap on submissions per campaign, before any tournament is scheduled.
- The ten-fold tournament's adjudicator runs this same protocol on its own sealed key; the executor therefore takes its key and family from the campaign, never from global configuration.
- Timing is a side channel the lane accepts: `units_used` and compute cost are in the receipt and envelope; per-unit timing is not exposed.
- A request with a sampling-controller arm needs a frozen, non-privileged, latency-measured planner record; a task needs an audit record with no unresolved finding. Both are admission checks owned elsewhere and consulted in N24b-16.
- RISK-34 (qualification gamed by adaptive retries or by a leak from sealed worlds; warning signs: a receipt field outside the listed set, requests without a close): mitigated by this bead with N24b-15 to -18, BD-260, BD-261 and BD-383.
