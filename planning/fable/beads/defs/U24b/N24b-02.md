## Background

Two of the project's bets are tested on a corpus of designs whose right answers are known in advance. BET-05:
static analysis (`design.check`) prunes bad designs before simulation and does not throw away a winner; its
falsifier is an area under the ROC curve below 0.8 on the corpus, or less than 0.05 above a mass-and-torque
heuristic, or a discarded planted winner. BET-06: a baseline controller synthesized from the model makes most
well-formed designs move; its falsifier is fewer than 80% of corpus designs that pass `design.check` traversing
5 m of flat ground. The corpus is also the *potency control* of every analysis and screen: an experiment that
cannot see a planted effect is silent about a real one.

The **design corpus** is about 200 designs (TARGET) made of three kinds: the design templates (archetypes);
parameter perturbations of them; and designs with planted defects of known kind. The three defect kinds the plan
names are an undersized knee actuator, an inertia that breaks static stability, and a wheelbase too long for an
aisle. The corpus also holds the planted winners used to audit screening (does a cheap screen discard a design
that is truly in the top k?) and the planted single causes used to test failure attribution.

The rule that makes the corpus worth anything: **labels are fixed before any analysis is tuned.** RISK-14 names
the failure: the corpus is tuned to the analyses (labels edited after an analysis changed). Its mitigations are
labels frozen before tuning, a held-out third, and potency controls. This bead builds the registry that enforces
those three; the seed bead BD-248 authors the roughly 200 designs and commits the label hash using it. Rejected
alternative: labels kept beside each analysis's tests. Rejected because each analysis then owns its own answer
key and nothing detects a relabel.

## Scope

In: the corpus manifest and label file formats; the corpus identity; the freeze record and its enforcement; the
split into a tuning part and a held-out third with read rules; the typed description of how each entry was made
(template, perturbation, planted defect, planted winner, planted cause); the loader used by analyses, screens and
gates; its example target `corpus_load` and the harness row `corpus_freeze` of `g1_known_answers`.

Out, with the bead that has it: the content (the seed bead BD-248, which depends on this bead): authoring the
roughly 200 designs, the planting rules and their 25% margin, the derivation of every label, the independent
re-derivation, the freeze commit of corpus version 1 with the check that no read precedes it, and the end-to-end
script on the shipped corpus (`scripts/e2e/design_corpus_freeze.sh`). This bead ships no corpus: its tests and
its end-to-end step run on `synthetic` corpora. Also out: the validation of baseline synthesis and static
analysis on the corpus (BD-249, BD-250); the screening audit, which raises `FROB-REF-566` from `nemesis.audit
prepare` when a planted winner without a rank on record is offered as a plant (N26b-03, N26b-07; applied to the
cascade by BD-345); attribution on planted causes (the Autopsy beads).

## Technical approach

**Files (DESIGN).** `assets/corpus/<version>/corpus.toml` (entries), `assets/corpus/<version>/labels.toml`
(the answer key), designs as `.fbot` files in `designs/` beside them, the label derivations in `derivations/`
and `AUTHORING.md` (the layout BD-248 fills: `assets/corpus/v1/{corpus.toml, labels.toml, designs/*.fbot,
derivations/*.md, AUTHORING.md}`). The derivation files and `AUTHORING.md` are not hashed into `CorpusId`. Code
in `crates/frob-arena/src/corpus/`.

**Entry, schema `frob.corpus/1` (DESIGN).**

```toml
[[entry]]
design = "d:41aa…"                 # DesignId (content hash of the canonical design)
class = "quad-mammal"
origin = { kind = "planted-defect", parent = "d:9f2c…", defect = "undersized-knee-actuator",
           edits = [ { edit = "swap-actuator", args = { joints = "*.knee", to = "qdd-s" } } ] }
split = "tuning"                   # tuning | held-out
```

`origin.kind` is one of `template`, `perturbation` (parent and typed parameter edits with their magnitudes),
`planted-defect` (parent, defect kind, the typed edits that plant it), `planted-winner` (parent and the property
that a cheap screen represents poorly), `planted-cause` (parent and the single cause). A `perturbation` entry
may carry `control = "sound-near-miss"` (an edit that moves toward a planted defect and stops on the safe side
of its threshold; the planted sound designs an analysis must pass). Every non-template entry
is reproducible from its parent by its typed edits; the loader re-applies the edits and checks that the result
hashes to `design` (a corpus entry that cannot be rebuilt is refused).

**Defect kinds (closed list at version 1).** `undersized-knee-actuator` (continuous torque demand in crouch
exceeds the actuator card; expected finding of the actuator-envelope analysis), `inertia-breaks-static-stability`
(a mass or inertia edit that moves the projected centre of mass outside the support polygon in the nominal
stance; expected finding of the static-stability analysis), `wheelbase-too-long-for-aisle` (turning envelope
exceeds the aisle width of the tight-space task; expected finding of the maneuverability analysis). New kinds
append; a kind is never renamed.

**Labels.** `labels.toml` holds, per design: the expected verdict of each analysis that has an answer for it
(`pass`, `fail` with the finding code), the expected simulated outcome class on the reference task
(`traverses-5m`, `fails: <failure class>`), and for planted winners and causes the planted property. The
format also has the fields the content bead BD-248 fills: `margin` (the ratio of the planted quantity to its
threshold: at least 1.25 for a planted defect and at most 0.75 for a `sound-near-miss` control, by that bead's
margin rule); for a planted winner either the rank record (`claim_id` of the full evaluation, the population, k
and the rank) or `rank = "pending"`; for a pending winner, and for one whose `claim_id` does not resolve, the
loader returns the entry with `usable_as_control = false`; for a planted cause the removing intervention and at least two interventions that must not flip the
outcome. Labels are written from the construction of the entry, never from running the analysis under test.

**Freeze.** `frob-arena` computes `LabelHash` = BLAKE3 of the canonical label file and `CorpusId` = BLAKE3 of the
canonical `{entries (sorted by design), LabelHash}`. A ledger record `corpus-frozen {CorpusId, LabelHash, date,
actor}` is written once per version. The loader refuses a corpus whose label file does not hash to the frozen
`LabelHash` for its `CorpusId`. A relabel therefore needs a new version directory and a new freeze record: it
changes the corpus identity, and every result cites the `CorpusId` it ran on.

**The held-out third.** Entries are assigned `split` by a hash of `design` under a seed fixed in the manifest
(so the assignment is reproducible and independent of labels), with one third `held-out`, stratified by `class`
and `origin.kind`. The loader takes a `purpose` argument: `tune` returns only `tuning` entries with labels;
`evaluate` returns all entries with labels and records an `evaluation-read` event naming the analysis version.
An analysis version that has an `evaluation-read` event may not be re-tuned and re-evaluated under the same
version string: the gate harness refuses a second evaluation of a changed analysis that kept its version.

**Composition quotas (DESIGN, checked by the loader as warnings at M3 and as errors at release).** Every
archetype on file appears as a template; each planted-defect kind has at least ten entries across at least two
classes where the kind applies; at least five planted winners and five planted causes when their authors'
beads land. The count target of about 200 is a TARGET.

Determinism: D0 for identities. The registry emits no physical numbers.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob.corpus/1` and the label file format are documented in `crates/frob-arena/CONTRACT.md`; the loader exists with `tune` and `evaluate` purposes.
- [ ] A corpus with an edited label and an unchanged freeze record is refused; a new version with a new freeze record loads and has a different `CorpusId`.
- [ ] Each non-template entry is rebuilt from its parent by its typed edits and matches its `DesignId`; a planted mismatch is refused.
- [ ] The split is one third held out within each stratum (to within one entry) and is reproducible from the seed.
- [ ] `tune` never returns a held-out label (property test); `evaluate` writes an `evaluation-read` event.
- [ ] A second evaluation by a changed analysis under an unchanged version string is refused.
- [ ] The three defect kinds are registered with the analysis each is expected to trip.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/corpus_registry.sh` passes on a clean checkout at M3; the harness row `corpus_freeze` of `g1_known_answers` exists and its relabelled-copy control is caught.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `corpus_label_hash_detects_relabel`: flip one label of a synthetic 12-entry corpus (labelled `synthetic`); load fails with the two hashes printed.
- `corpus_entry_rebuilds_from_parent`: apply `swap-actuator` to the parent, compare `DesignId`; a wrong `to` argument fails.
- `corpus_split_stratified_third`: for a synthetic 90-entry corpus with 3 classes and 3 kinds, each stratum of 10 has 3 or 4 held-out entries, and the assignment is stable under reordering of the manifest.
- `corpus_tune_hides_held_out`: `tune` output contains no entry whose `split` is `held-out`.
- `corpus_evaluation_read_is_recorded`: one event per `(analysis, version, CorpusId)`.
- `corpus_reevaluation_of_changed_analysis_refused`: same version string, different analysis digest, second `evaluate` fails.
- `corpus_id_order_independent`: permuting entries leaves `CorpusId` unchanged.
- `corpus_pending_winner_not_a_control`: a planted-winner label with `rank = "pending"` loads with `usable_as_control = false`; one with a rank record whose `claim_id` does not resolve is treated as pending in the same way (BD-248's rule: a winner whose rank is not on record is not a planted winner).
- `corpus_control_and_margin_fields_round_trip`: an entry with `control = "sound-near-miss"` and a label with `margin` parse and re-serialize to the same canonical bytes; an unknown field is an error.

### End-to-end script and logging
The corpus has no verb of its own at version 1: analyses, screens and gate harnesses read it through the loader.
This bead's own script, `scripts/e2e/corpus_registry.sh` (header `bead=N24b-02 milestone=M3`), sources the
STD-E2E helper and runs on the `synthetic` corpora of the unit tests with this bead's dependencies only:
1. `freeze`: `e2e_tier g1_known_answers corpus_freeze` (the harness row this bead registers): the 12-entry corpus
   loads, its `CorpusId` matches the freeze record in the scratch ledger, and a copy with one label flipped is
   refused with both hashes printed.
2. `loader`: this bead's example target `cargo run -p frob-arena --example corpus_load -- <dir> --purpose
   tune|evaluate`, which prints the `CorpusId`, the counts per `origin.kind` and `split`, and the entries returned:
   on the 90-entry corpus, `tune` returns no held-out entry and each stratum holds 3 or 4 held-out entries;
   `evaluate` writes one `evaluation-read` event; a second `evaluate` with a changed analysis digest under the same
   version string exits 3 with the refusal printed; an entry whose `swap-actuator` edit names the wrong card is
   refused with the two `DesignId`s.
Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, both hashes, the count per `origin.kind` and `split`, the receipt hash
of the tier run, and on failure the first entry that differs. `scripts/e2e/arena_scorecard_batteries.sh`
(N24b-23) runs this script as its step `corpus_freeze` and adds `frob design check` on one planted-defect entry,
to show the entry is an ordinary design reachable from the user's side. The same assertions on the shipped
corpus are the end-to-end script of BD-248 (`scripts/e2e/design_corpus_freeze.sh`), which exists only once the
content does; this script does not wait for it.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Negative control: the relabelled copy (must be refused); a corpus entry whose edits do not reproduce its design (must be refused).
- Potency: each planted-defect kind is tripped by its named analysis on at least one entry when BD-250 runs; this bead only registers the expectation.
- Determinism: `CorpusId` and the split are D0, identical twice and across machines.

## Considerations

- OPEN-ATEL-5 (owner: Arena lead; blocks GATE-ATEL-09 for the affected classes): the planted winner for morphology classes that have no earlier specialist result. Fail-closed default: the control is reported `not-run` and finalists are described as "k survivors".
- The corpus is discovery data. It is never a confirmation sample, and results on it are potency controls, not claims about designs in general.
- Planted defects must be honest physics, not flags: an analysis that keys on the `defect` field instead of the design fails the rebuild-and-relabel control in BD-250.
- Twelve of the eighteen archetypes have no permissively licensed reference robot; corpus templates stand alone for them.
