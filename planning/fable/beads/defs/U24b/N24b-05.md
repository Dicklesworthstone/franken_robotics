## Background

`fixture:cart-sill-dock` compares two front-wheel-drive carts, identical except for the front wheels (60 mm for
design A, 75 mm for design B), on one mission: start at rest, cross a 12 mm door sill, and dock on the run-out,
within 8 simulated seconds. The larger wheel has a better lever at the lip and a smaller tread force for the same
torque; the fixture exists to find which effect dominates once load distribution, motor lag, impact and momentum
are included. Everything is DESIGN: a synthetic fixture with explicit values. Measured profiles replace synthetic
ones only under a new fixture identity.

This bead builds the fixture's model: the two designs, the drive with its two loops, the world, the rules and the
task file whose requirement decides a trial. It produces one settled start state per design and one nominal
episode per design on the referee tier (the CPU tier with event location and a transactional step), with a
verdict. The analytic rows (N24b-04), the limiting rigs and the gate (BD-123) and the development batch (N24b-07)
build on it.

Two findings from an earlier planar pilot of this fixture shape the requirement. FACT (planning archive, sill
pilot audit): the pilot's hand-written scorer had a latched dwell timer and no chassis-strike guard. Written in
the requirement language as below, both defects are unrepresentable: the dwell is a past-time "historically"
operator, false at the tick any condition lapses, and the strike guard is an atom of the until-clause. FACT (same
archive): changing only the winding law's feedforward term from R·i_ref to R·i changed 24 of 156 labels (the
60 mm design from 8 to 18 successes of 78, the 75 mm design from 61 to 67). The winding law is therefore part of
the fixture's constants, and the pilot remains exploratory with its own law and scorer.

## Scope

In: design files for A and B; the motor card and the drive law; the sill scene in two strata; clocks; signals;
the task file with the requirement, stall and hard-limit rules and labelled example traces; the start state; the
energy report per trial; one nominal episode per design on the referee. Out: rigs and GATE-ARENA-04 (BD-123);
the batch and rule (N24b-07, N24b-06); batch tiers (N24b-09).

## Technical approach

**Bodies.** Chassis frame: x forward, y left, z up, origin midway between the axles on the axle line.

| Part | Value |
|---|---|
| Axles and wheels | Axles at x = ±0.16 m (wheelbase 0.32 m); wheel centres at y = ±0.12 m; front-wheel drive, rear wheels passive |
| Chassis | Uniform box 0.40 × 0.20 × 0.08 m, 5.0 kg, centred 50 mm above the axle line (it overhangs each axle by 40 mm) |
| Payload | Uniform box 0.18 × 0.14 × 0.08 m, 2.2 kg, bolted to the chassis top face with its bottom flush, centred at x = δ |
| Wheels | Uniform cylinders 30 mm wide. Rear pair: radius 60 mm, 0.2 kg each, in both designs |
| Design A | Front pair radius 60 mm, 0.2 kg each, spin inertia 3.6 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m². Total 8.000 kg |
| Design B | Front pair radius 75 mm, 0.3125 kg each, spin inertia 8.7890625 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m². Total 8.225 kg. Front axle rides 15 mm higher; chassis pitched up 2.69° |
| Rotor | Additional reflected rotor inertia per driven wheel: zero in the primary fixture |

Files: `assets/fixtures/cart-sill-dock/design-a.fbot`, `design-b.fbot` (every body has a fill; the payload offset
δ is a design lever bound per condition); the loaded collision envelope is the union of chassis, payload and
wheel geometry.

**Drive.** One motor per front wheel, on the wheel shaft.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Supply | Ideal regulated 24 V bus for the 8 s trial; a lossless averaged four-quadrant bridge that absorbs regeneration |
| Motor | k_t = 0.2 N·m/A, k_e = 0.2 V·s/rad, R = 1 Ω, L_w = 10 mH, current limit ±6 A, gear ratio 1 |
| Initial state | Zero current; winding and pack held at 20 °C (no thermal drift of resistance) |
| Shaft loss | Viscous damping 0.002 N·m·s/rad on each of the four wheel shafts |
| Outer loop, 100 Hz | Wheel-rate target 0.30 m/s divided by the loaded radius: 5.0 rad/s for A, 4.0 rad/s for B. Current command i_ref = 2 A·s/rad × (target − sampled rate), clipped to ±6 A, held for 10 ms |
| Inner driver, continuous | Winding voltage v = R·i + k_e·ω + 4 Ω·(i_ref − i), clipped to ±24 V |
| Power ledger | Net DC power Σ v_j·i_j over the two drives; bus current is that sum divided by 24 V; positive and negative parts integrated separately |

Winding dynamics: L_w·di/dt = v − R·i − k_e·ω. Current-limit rule, as numbered steps inside a physics
sub-interval: (1) compute v from the law above and clip to ±24 V; (2) if i = +6 A and the clipped v would raise
the current (v − R·i − k_e·ω > 0), apply v = R·i + k_e·ω, which holds the limit, provided that value lies within
±24 V; symmetrically at −6 A; (3) the instant at which |i| reaches 6 A is located inside the step by the event
locator, not detected at the step's end; (4) if no admissible voltage keeps the winding inside its rating, record
a physical limit violation (the event that the hard-limit atom `current-ok` reads). `drive_saturated` is 1 at a
requirement tick when either current command is clipped at ±6 A.

**World and rules.**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Floor and sill | Level floor. A sill spans x from 0 to 0.10 m across the full lane width, 0.012 m high, in two strata: sharp upper edges, or 2 mm fillets on both upper edges |
| Contact | Coulomb friction μ, one value for all wheel–floor and wheel–sill contacts of a condition, 0.8 nominal; inelastic impact; g = 9.81 m/s² |
| Start | Stationary, centred and parallel to the lane, front axle at x = −0.50 m, settled under gravity, separately for each design |
| Clocks | Physics 1/2000 s; outer loop and requirement clock 1/100 s; atoms are interval extrema over the 20 physics ticks of each requirement tick |
| Docked | The minimum world x of the whole loaded collision envelope exceeds 0.10 m, and roll and pitch stay within 5°, continuously for 0.5 s, with the dwell complete within 8 s of the start. The dwell restarts whenever a condition lapses |
| Hard limits | Roll or pitch beyond 35°; any chassis contact with the sill; winding current beyond the rating by more than 0.01 A. Each is absorbing |
| Stall | The command is saturated and the cart advances less than 2 mm over 0.5 s. A stall names the failure reason; the verdict still comes from the requirement at the horizon |
| Horizon | 8 simulated seconds. Timeout, rollover, strike and stall each carry a reason |

Scene: `assets/fixtures/cart-sill-dock/sill-sharp.fscene` and `sill-fillet.fscene`, analytic overlays (a box; a
box with two quarter-cylinder fillets). Settling: double precision on the referee until base and joint speeds
stay below 10⁻³ in SI units for 0.2 s with maximum penetration at most 0.5 mm (TARGET of the reset cache); the
first contact impulse after loading the state must lie within 5% of the static support force.

**The requirement (S-expression view of the task file's `requirements` section; the JSON form is the same tree).**

```lisp
(requirements
  (atom cleared    (>  (- env_min_x 0.10 m) 0 m)                    :scale 0.10 m)
  (atom upright    (>= (- 5 deg tilt) 0 deg)                        :scale 5 deg)
  (atom tilt-ok    (>= (- 35 deg tilt) 0 deg)                       :scale 35 deg)
  (atom current-ok (>= (- 6.01 A i_max) 0 A)                        :scale 6 A)
  (atom no-strike  (=  strikes 0)                                   :exact)
  (atom saturated  (=  drive_saturated 1)                           :exact)
  (atom advancing  (>= (- x_front (delay 0.5 s x_front)) 0.002 m)   :scale 0.002 m)
  (online dwell    (H (0 s 0.5 s) (and cleared upright)))
  (online stalled  (and (H (0 s 0.5 s) saturated) (not advancing)))
  (verdict docked  (U (0 s 8 s) (and tilt-ok current-ok no-strike) dwell)))
```

Signals: `env_min_x` is the rearmost point of the loaded collision envelope; `tilt` the larger of |roll| and
|pitch|; `i_max` the larger winding current magnitude; each an interval extremum over a requirement tick (the
minimum for `env_min_x`, the maximum for the other two). `strikes` counts chassis–sill contacts (integer,
cumulative). `x_front` is the world x of the front axle (state kind, reduction `last`). Operators: `H` is
"historically" over a closed past window, `U` is "until" over a closed future window; a margin is the atom's
expression divided by its scale, and conjunction takes the minimum. The until-form says that the hard limits
hold at every tick before the dwell completes, and that it completes by 8 s. The reach of the verdict is 8 s, so
the episode horizon must be at least 8 s (a shorter horizon is refused at admission with FROB-REF-526).
`stalled` is an online signal used for the failure reason and for early termination; it never decides a verdict.

**Labelled examples (DESIGN; required for admission by `task.check`, at least two satisfying and two violating,
one near the boundary of each comparator).** Synthetic traces shipped beside the task: `docked-clean`
(satisfied); `docked-after-dwell-restart` (a 0.3 s lapse of `upright`, then 0.5 s of dwell: satisfied, and a
latched timer would have accepted earlier); `stuck-front-lip` (violated, reason `stuck`, `env_min_x` ends at
0.069 m: margin −0.31, binding atom `cleared`); `chassis-strike` (violated at the strike tick); `overcurrent`
(`i_max` = 6.02 A: violated); boundary cases `env-exactly-0.10` (violated, because `cleared` is strict),
`tilt-exactly-5` (satisfied, because `upright` is non-strict) and `current-exactly-6.01` (satisfied).

**Energy.** For every trial: net bus energy integrated from the start to adjudication, with duration, gross
draw and returned energy. Descriptive in version 1 of the fixture; it takes no part in the rule.

**Outputs.** Per design: a settled state; a nominal episode (μ = 0.8, δ = 0, heading 0, sharp lip) stored as
`.frun` with a `frob.verdict/1` object (verdict, margin, binding atom and time, decided-at time, reason,
coverage kind `interval`, boundary flag, per-atom minima, evidence, the three authorization flags all false,
trace digest). Evidence: claim Known answer for the rows, Design comparison for episodes; class `Tested`; tier
`referee`; determinism D2 per platform; label `synthetic`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both designs compile; `frob design check` reports total mass 8.000 kg and 8.225 kg and B's pitch 2.69°.
- [ ] `frob task check` admits the task file: units, clocks, windows, reach (8 s) against horizon (8 s), the eight labelled examples agree with the monitors, and the mutation check passes (reversing a comparator or deleting an atom changes at least one example's label; no hard-limit atom is vacuous).
- [ ] Each design settles within the criterion (penetration ≤ 0.5 mm; first impulse within 5% of static support).
- [ ] A nominal episode per design runs on the referee to adjudication and stores a `frob.verdict/1` whose `coverage.kind` is `interval` and `missing_samples` is 0.
- [ ] The current-limit crossing is an event inside a step: a test trial that saturates shows |i| ≤ 6.01 A at every committed sub-interval.
- [ ] The power ledger closes: bus energy = shaft work + copper loss + inductor energy change, within 10⁻⁶ relative over a trial.
- [ ] Changing the feedforward term to R·i_ref is possible only as a declared fixture variant with its own identity.
- [ ] Cancellation of a running fixture episode drains and leaves no partial verdict.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `f1_designs_mass_and_inertia`: compiled model totals 8.000 and 8.225 kg; front spin inertias 3.6 × 10⁻⁴ and 8.7890625 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m².
- `f1_outer_loop_targets`: 5.0 and 4.0 rad/s; command at zero rate is clipped at 6 A (2 × 5.0 = 10 A before clipping).
- `f1_inner_driver_limit_hold`: at i = 6 A with i_ref = 6 A the applied voltage is R·i + k_e·ω and di/dt = 0; with ω such that this exceeds 24 V a limit violation is recorded.
- `f1_requirement_examples`: the eight labelled traces give their labels; `stuck-front-lip` has margin −0.31 ± 10⁻⁹ and binding atom `cleared`.
- `f1_dwell_does_not_latch`: a trace with dwell conditions true for 0.49 s, false for one tick, true for 0.49 s is `violated` at the horizon.
- `f1_strict_boundary`: `env_min_x` = 0.10 m exactly for the whole dwell is `violated`; 0.10 m + 1 ulp is `satisfied` and carries the `boundary` flag.
- `f1_reach_equals_horizon`: horizon 7.99 s is refused with FROB-REF-526.
- `f1_power_ledger_closure`: closure within 10⁻⁶ relative on the nominal episodes.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/arena_fixture_cart_sill_dock.sh` (bead N24b-20), steps `check_task`, `settle` and `nominal`:
`frob task check`, `frob task settle`, `frob sim run --tier referee --task <TaskId> --design <A|B> --seed 17
--json`, `frob task verdict <run> --json`. Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, wall time, evidence blocks,
the verdict fields, the settle metrics with thresholds (0.5 mm, 5%), energy figures with units, and trace digests.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-602 (masses, inertias, pitch) through the compiled model; the verdict example margin −0.31. Negative
controls: a scorer variant whose dwell is a latched flag, run on the trace of `f1_dwell_does_not_latch` (dwell
conditions true for 0.49 s, false for one tick, true for 0.49 s; label `violated`): the latched variant returns
`satisfied`, disagrees with the label, and is refused by `task.check` with FROB-REF-539; a task variant without
the `no-strike` atom (the mutation check must report that deleting the atom changes the label of `chassis-strike`).
Metamorphic: mirroring the lane (y → −y) leaves the verdict and margin unchanged; refining the physics step with
the requirement clock fixed at 1/100 s keeps the definition of the verdict. Determinism: the nominal episodes
give identical trace digests twice on one machine and across worker counts; debug and release agree in class D2.

## Considerations

- The transactional step's limits (16 dyadic splits, 64 events per base interval, 3 retries; OPEN-KIN-5) apply at the lip; a refused step leaves the trial `invalid`, which is counted, never dropped. The plan's stop decision for M1 names this fixture: if refusals leave it without a completed run, that is reported, not hidden.
- Witness migration at the lip (flat, edge, top) is owned by the contact beads; its tolerances (OPEN-TRAC-5) are decided on a sill fixture at M1.
- Rotor inertia and controller rate are pinned (zero and 100 Hz): a comparison that leaves them unpinned measures loop stability (CX-264). The follow-ups that vary them are N24b-08.
- A hardware counterpart (an instrumented cart with 60 mm and 75 mm wheels) is an optional bench package of the Reality lane; nothing here claims agreement with it.
