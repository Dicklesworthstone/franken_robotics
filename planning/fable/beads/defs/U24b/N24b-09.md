## Background

GATE-ARENA-04 (claim: Known answer; TARGET) reads: fixture 1: every analytic row within tolerance on the referee
and inside the fidelity envelope on the batch tiers; the creep rigs flip at the stated thresholds; both negative
controls are caught. It lands in two halves: M1 on the referee (bead BD-123) and M2 on the batch tiers. The
batch tiers are the throughput tiers: `batch-cpu` (the CPU reference of the batched step, in `f64` and `f32`,
with a `strict` profile that locates events like the referee), the CUDA backend and the Metal backend. They step
many worlds in lockstep, in single precision on devices, and they are where design search actually runs, so a
fixture that passes only on the referee says little about results an agent will see.

This bead lowers the fixture's rigs into batched worlds, runs them on each batch tier, and compares every row
with its referee value against the tier's fidelity envelope. The bead BD-212 (the standard battery frozen, the
fidelity ledger with its frozen envelopes) records the envelope and counts this half of the gate in its
done-condition; this bead supplies the fixture-side work that record needs.

Two things differ from the referee and are the reason this is more than a re-run. First, the creep rigs: on the
fast tiers the friction grid excludes points within 0.01 of a threshold (0.005 on the referee), because
single-precision contact and lockstep stepping blur the threshold. Second, the boundary: an atom is decided on
the device only when its normalized margin lies outside a guard band of 2⁻²¹ around zero; a world with a verdict
atom inside the band is flagged `boundary` and that atom is re-evaluated on the CPU with its declared
comparator. FACT (planning archive, boundary-classifier runs on the Metal GPU and both RTX 4090 cards): on a
dense 65,536-case boundary screen, unguarded single-precision strict comparison gave 452 false admissions and
23,438 false exclusions; with a 2⁻²¹ guard and an exact fallback there were no final errors.

## Scope

In: each rig of the fixture as a batch of worlds (one world per grid point or per row variant); the fast-tier
grid protocol; row-by-row comparison against the referee inside the envelope; the `boundary` flag on devices;
the wheel-loop control on the batched actuator kernels; the batch-tier record of GATE-ARENA-04. Out: the
envelope's definition and freeze (BD-212); the batched step and kernels themselves (their beads); the
development batch on batch tiers (it stays a referee study at M1; a later re-run on batch tiers is a fidelity
variant of N24b-08).

## Technical approach

1. **Lowering.** Each rig's scene and task are compiled for the batched step: the sill as an analytic overlay,
   the cart as a packed model, the drive through the batched actuator kernels (winding current level with the
   current-limit rule), stiction and hard stops through the joint-row kernel. One batch per rig holds all its
   grid points and variants as separate worlds, addressed (17, k, 0).
2. **Tiers and precisions.** `batch-cpu` in `f64` (lockstep and `strict` profiles), `batch-cpu` in `f32`, CUDA
   (`f32`), Metal (`f32`).
3. **Row comparison.** For every analytic row the batch-tier value is compared with the referee value of
   BD-123 and with the analytic value. A row passes when the difference from the referee lies inside the frozen
   fidelity envelope for that quantity and tier. Until the envelope is frozen the provisional bands are (DESIGN
   proposal, replaced by the envelope at freeze): static forces and energies 2%; lip lever 0.2 mm; spectral
   radius 5 × 10⁻³; power-ledger figures 10⁻⁴ relative in `f32`.
4. **Creep rigs, fast-tier protocol (TARGET band).** The grid is the multiples of 0.01 in friction, excluding
   points whose distance to the threshold is less than 0.01. Expected brackets: A sharp (threshold 0.7500) 0.74
   and 0.76; B sharp (0.6459) 0.63 and 0.66; A fillet (0.7332) 0.72 and 0.75; B fillet (0.6351) 0.62 and 0.65;
   rear pull-over of A at δ = 0 (1.0435) 1.03 and 1.06; at δ = +0.04 m (0.8869) 0.87 and 0.90. The outcome must
   be "does not climb" at the lower point and "climbs" at the upper one on every batch tier.
5. **Boundary.** One world per threshold runs exactly at the threshold. On devices it must be flagged
   `boundary` and its verdict atom re-evaluated on the CPU; the test asserts the flag and that the final
   verdict equals the CPU re-evaluation, not the device comparison.
6. **Wheel-loop control on the batched actuator stage.** The `wheel-loop` rig's spectral radius is measured on
   each tier (expected 0.9207 within the band). The registered mutant `euler-current-then-wheel` (explicit Euler
   at a 0.5 ms step, current before wheel speed, expected radius 1.497) is applied to the batched actuator
   kernel in a `mutants` build and must make the row fail on every tier.
7. **Batch composition.** The same rig world run alone, in a batch of 64 and in a batch of 4,096 with unrelated
   co-tenant worlds must give identical digests on one device family (class D2: a world does not depend on the
   batch it ran in).
8. **Record.** The Gauntlet's test target calls `frob task fixture cart-sill-dock --layer rigs --tier <tier>
   --json` for each tier and writes receipts; the GATE-ARENA-04 row gains the batch-tier measurements, per tier,
   with commit, hardware fingerprint and seed.

Evidence: claim Known answer; class `Tested`; tier `batch-cpu`, `cuda` or `metal`; determinism D2 per backend
(D3 across backends: decisions agree and state lies inside the envelope); label `synthetic`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every analytic row lies inside the fidelity envelope on `batch-cpu` (`f64` and `f32`), CUDA and Metal; the measured differences from the referee are recorded per row and tier.
- [ ] All six creep thresholds flip between their fast-tier bracket points on every batch tier.
- [ ] The world at each threshold carries the `boundary` flag on devices and its final verdict equals the CPU re-evaluation.
- [ ] The wheel-loop mutant is caught on every tier.
- [ ] Rig-world digests are identical alone, in a batch of 64 and in a batch of 4,096 on one device family.
- [ ] A retained differential check runs the same rig kernels on the CPU reference and on each device and reports the first divergence, if any.
- [ ] The batch-tier half of GATE-ARENA-04 is recorded with measured values by a session that did not implement the lowering.
- [ ] Cancellation of a batched fixture job drains with no leaked device buffer.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `f1_batch_grid_exclusion_fast_tier`: table test of the six thresholds against the brackets above (for example B sharp excludes 0.64 and 0.65).
- `f1_batch_rows_within_envelope_cpu_f64`, `_cpu_f32`, `_cuda`, `_metal`: per-tier row comparison.
- `f1_batch_creep_brackets_<tier>`: (no, yes) at each bracket pair.
- `f1_batch_boundary_flag_<tier>`: flagged at threshold; unflagged at the bracket points.
- `f1_batch_wheel_loop_radius_<tier>`: 0.9207 within the band; mutant gives a value above 1.
- `f1_batch_composition_invariance`: three batch sizes, equal digests.
- `f1_batch_strict_matches_referee`: on the `strict` profile in `f64`, creep outcomes equal the referee's at the referee's own bracket points (band 0.005).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/arena_fixture_cart_sill_dock.sh` (bead N24b-20), step `rigs_batch`, run once per available tier
with `--tier batch-cpu|cuda|metal`. Logged per STD-E2E: per row the analytic, referee and tier values with
units, the envelope and the pass flag; per grid point the friction, outcome, margin and boundary flag; the
device fingerprint; digests for the composition test; on failure the first differing row or the first divergent
stage with the snapshot path.

## Considerations

- The lockstep tiers do not locate events; the lip is met through the speculative contact margin. A creep outcome that differs from the referee at a bracket point is a fidelity finding, recorded in the fidelity ledger, and the row is then not inside the envelope: the gate fails honestly instead of the band widening after the fact.
- OPEN-ARENA-5 (owner: Spark lead; blocks the device half of the boundary gates): the guard band per backend. Fail-closed default: 2⁻²¹ with CPU re-evaluation of every flagged atom.
- Single-precision contact near the lip is ill-conditioned when the wheel bears on the edge with a small normal load; the conditioning figure of the contact solve is logged with the row.
- If a device backend is unavailable the row is recorded `not run` for that tier, never inferred from another tier.
