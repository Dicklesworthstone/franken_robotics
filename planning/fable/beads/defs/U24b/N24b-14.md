## Background

A **study** is a registered plan to answer one comparative or absolute question. It declares its type when it
is created and keeps it (INV-ARENA-05: a study has one type for its whole life). There are two types.

| | Anytime-valid study | Fixed-n exact study |
|---|---|---|
| Inference | A betting e-process or a confidence sequence, valid at every stopping time | An exact interval or test at a sample size fixed in advance |
| Stopping | When the evidence crosses its threshold or the budget ends; unfinished episodes are cancelled | At n complete units, never earlier |
| Before the first episode | Null hypothesis, effect margin, bound on outcomes, betting rule, level, budget | n, the rule, the level, and a pre-analysis: which verdicts are reachable at that n, and the power at declared alternatives |
| Reports | The e-value, the confidence sequence, pairs used, the stopping reason | Counts, the interval, the verdict, and the pre-analysis hash |
| Interim information | Any; validity does not depend on it | Progress only. Outcomes may be looked at, and the verdict is computed once, at n |
| Typical use | Screening, racing, promotion between tiers, bisection | Registered A/B questions, equivalence claims, qualification of a finalist |

ADR-ARENA-07 (decision): a study is anytime-valid or fixed-n, never both. Reason: each kind of inference is
valid only under its own stopping rule. A fixed-n interval computed at the moment an anytime-valid study stops
has no coverage guarantee, and a fixed-n study that stops when its running p-value first looks good loses its
level. Counterexample for the second (CX-266): an exact one-sided sign test at nominal level 5% on pairs that
favour either design with probability ½, looked at after every pair: after 8 looks the chance of a false
rejection is 13/256 = 5.08%; after 16 it is 8.76%, after 32, 12.9%, and after 64, 17.4%.

The seed bead BD-124 builds the two inference engines on the CPU and the verbs `study.plan`, `study.submit`
and `study.status`. This bead is the admission layer of Arena in front of them: the typed study object, the
checks that run before the first episode, the four rules common to both types, and the refusals. It implements
the study half of construction C-15 (evidence is a test martingale; time-uniform inference and a sealed fixed-n
decision are different procedures, and neither substitutes for the other).

## Scope

In: the `Study` type and its immutability; the per-type declaration checks; refusal of mixtures; the four
common rules (pairing and the unit of inference, determinism class, N-way race level, controller arm); the
peeking known answer as a test; the refusal codes and repairs. Out: the e-process and exact-test engines and
the study verbs (BD-124); the enumerator behind the pre-analysis (N24b-06); device execution of paired
comparisons (BD-262); the qualification lane's use of studies (N24b-16, BD-259).

## Technical approach

**Types (DESIGN sketch; names fix intent).**

```rust
pub enum Study { Anytime(AnytimeStudy), FixedN(FixedNStudy) }
pub struct AnytimeStudy { null: Hypothesis, margin: Qty,
    span: LossSpan,              // bound on the paired difference, fixed before the first observation
    betting_rule: RuleId, level: Ratio, budget: Budget }
pub struct FixedNStudy { n: u32, rule: RuleId, level: Ratio, margin: Qty,
    preanalysis: PreAnalysis }   // computed before the first episode; a study without one is refused
pub struct PreAnalysis { outcomes: u64, reachable: Set<StudyVerdict>, power: Vec<(Alternative, Ratio)>, hash: Blake3 }
pub enum StudyVerdict { Promote, Inferior, PracticalTie, Inconclusive }
pub struct StudyPlan { designs: Vec<DesignId>, scene: SceneRef, task: TaskId, controller_arm: ControllerArm,
    seed_protocol: SeedProtocol, // pairing by (world, replicate); clusters are the unit of inference
    budget: Budget, backend: BackendRequest, study: Study, estimate: CostEstimate }
pub struct PairCounts { a_only: u32, b_only: u32, both: u32, neither: u32, unscored: u32, planned: u32 }
```

The study's type is part of its content address; there is no operation that converts one variant into the
other, and no field of one variant is accepted on the other (INV-ARENA-05, checked by a compile-fail test and a
schema test).

**Admission checks, in order, before any episode is scheduled.**

1. *One type.* The request carries exactly one of the two declarations. A request that carries both, or that
   asks an anytime-valid study for an exact fixed-n interval at its stopping time, or asks a fixed-n study to
   stop on interim evidence, is refused with FROB-REF-552.
2. *Anytime-valid declarations present:* null hypothesis, effect margin, the bound on outcomes (`LossSpan`,
   taken from the task definition and never estimated from the study's own data; a metric without one is
   refused for this study type), betting rule, level, budget.
3. *Fixed-n declarations present:* n, the rule, the level, and the pre-analysis from `eval.preanalyze` at that
   n and level. Missing pre-analysis: FROB-REF-532. A declared verdict that the pre-analysis shows unreachable:
   FROB-REF-532 with the reachable set attached. Fixture 1 shows why: at 64 pairs its rule cannot return one of
   its four verdicts.
4. *Determinism class.* Paired statistics are refused under an uncontrolled determinism class: FROB-REF-533.
   Both arms run at class D2 or better on one backend (bit-reproducible on that device family), or at D3 with
   decision-level equivalence recorded for the task. A D4 run (unspecified determinism, the labelled training
   profile) never enters a comparison.
5. *Unit of inference.* Units are paired by world address. When several episodes share a cluster, such as one
   building with its materials, the cluster is the unit of inference: the study declares `unit: world |
   cluster` and, for clusters, the map from episode outcomes to the unit's outcome, fixed before the first
   episode (DESIGN options: `all`, the unit succeeds when every episode in it is satisfied; `mean`, the mean of
   a bounded outcome, with the span declared). n counts units, not episodes. A study over clustered worlds that
   declares `unit: world` is refused under the same code as a malformed plan (the study-plan schema error).
6. *N-way race.* A race of N candidates that eliminates on single pairwise comparisons spends α/(N − 1) per
   comparison, so that a null race keeps its best candidate with probability at least 1 − α. The admission
   layer computes the per-comparison level and writes it into each pairwise study; a race plan whose pairwise
   studies carry the undivided level is refused.
7. *Controller arm.* A comparison of designs declares its controller arm: `shared` (one controller for both,
   which isolates the change of body); `retuned-equal-resources` (controllers retuned with equal resources,
   which compares the best systems; the resources are recorded per arm); `warm-start` (a candidate that
   inherits weights declares its warm start and receives a matched transfer baseline). A design comparison with
   no declared arm is refused.

**Interim behaviour.** For a fixed-n study `study.status` returns progress (units done of n) and may return
raw outcomes; it returns no interval, test statistic or verdict before n complete units, and the verdict is
computed once. The only early stop is exact curtailment: every admissible completion of the unresolved units
yields the same verdict (function in N24b-06). For an anytime-valid study any interim information may be
shown; when the evidence crosses its threshold or the budget ends, unfinished episodes are cancelled.

**Refusals.**

| Code | Meaning | Ranked repairs | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| FROB-REF-552 | A request mixes study types | Register two studies, one of each type, on disjoint units; or keep the declared type and drop the foreign statistic | 3 |
| FROB-REF-532 | A fixed-n study has no pre-analysis, or names a verdict it cannot reach | Run `eval.preanalyze`; raise n to the first value at which the verdict is reachable; drop the verdict; register the equivalence rule | 3 |
| FROB-REF-533 | Paired statistics requested under an uncontrolled determinism class | Re-run both arms under a D2 profile on one backend; or record decision-level equivalence under D3 for the task; never D4 | 3 |

Code: `crates/frob-arena/src/study/{types,admit}.rs`. Ledger: the admitted study (its content address, type,
declarations, the pre-analysis hash) is recorded before the first episode.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A request with both declarations is refused with FROB-REF-552 (the seed condition of BD-124 that a study declaring both types is refused is exercised here at the admission layer).
- [ ] A request for a fixed-n interval on a stopped anytime-valid study, and a request to stop a fixed-n study on an interim look, are each refused with FROB-REF-552.
- [ ] A fixed-n study without a pre-analysis, and one declaring a practical tie at n = 64 under the fixture-1 rule, are each refused with FROB-REF-532.
- [ ] A paired study with a D4 arm, or with arms on different backends and no D3 equivalence record, is refused with FROB-REF-533.
- [ ] `study.status` on a fixed-n study before n returns no interval and no verdict.
- [ ] A 5-way race at α = 0.05 carries level 0.0125 in each pairwise study.
- [ ] A design comparison without a controller arm is refused; each of the three arms is accepted with its required fields.
- [ ] A study's type cannot be changed after creation (compile-fail and schema tests); the admitted study is in the ledger before its first episode.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `study_one_type_for_life`: compile-fail test that no conversion between variants exists; schema test that a JSON study with both `anytime` and `fixed_n` keys is rejected.
- `study_mixture_refused_552`: the three mixture requests above.
- `study_fixed_n_requires_preanalysis_532`; `study_unreachable_verdict_532` (n = 64, tie declared; at n = 86 admitted).
- `study_determinism_class_533`: (D2, D2, same backend) admitted; (D2, D4) refused; (D2 on CUDA, D2 on Metal) refused without a D3 equivalence record and admitted with one.
- `study_race_level`: N = 2 → α; N = 5 → α/4; N = 9 → α/8, as exact rationals.
- `study_cluster_unit`: 12 episodes in 4 clusters with `all` give n = 4 units; one failed episode fails its unit.
- `study_controller_arm_required`.
- `peeking_inflates_fixed_n`: exact enumeration of the sign test looked at after every pair gives 13/256 after 8 looks, 2869/32768 (8.76%) after 16, 12.95% after 32 and 17.42% after 64.
- `peeking_safe_under_confidence_sequence`: 10⁴ simulated null sequences of 64 pairs under the e-process of the anytime-valid engine: the fraction ever rejecting is at or below 5% (exact one-sided binomial check at the 1% level).
- `status_hides_interim_inference`: the status object of a fixed-n study at 30 of 64 units has no `interval`, `p_value` or `verdict` field.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/arena_study_preanalysis.sh` (bead N24b-21), steps `refuse_mixture`, `refuse_no_preanalysis`,
`refuse_uncontrolled_class`, `race_level` and `status_interim`: `frob study plan …` with each planted request,
asserting the refusal code and exit code 3, and `frob study status` on a running fixed-n study. Logged per
STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, refusal code, pointer and repairs, the admitted study's address and pre-analysis
hash, the evidence block.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-618 (peeking: 13/256 after 8 looks, 8.76%, 12.9%, 17.4%; control: the same data under a confidence sequence
stay below their level). CX-266 (look at a fixed-n test after every pair and stop when it rejects). CX-265.
Negative control for INV-ARENA-05: a build that lets `study.status` return a running p-value for a fixed-n
study; the interim test must fail. Metamorphic: relabelling the two arms mirrors every admitted study and
refusal. Determinism: admission is D0; the same request gives the same study address twice and across machines.

## Considerations

- The peeking numbers depend on the stopping rule "reject when the exact one-sided p-value is at most 5% at any look"; the test fixes that rule and reproduces the fractions exactly.
- For metrics claimed singly across several metrics, the e-value form of the Benjamini–Hochberg rule applies in the comparison engine; this layer only checks that each metric has its own declared span.
- The qualification lane re-uses these checks at the level α_i of a request, with clusters as units (bead N24b-16).
- What would make us revisit: an engine that can provide exact fixed-n inference with a valid group-sequential boundary would be a third declared type with its own stopping rule, never a relaxation of these two.
