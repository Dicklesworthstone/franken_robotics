## Background

An e-process makes one comparison valid under optional stopping. It does nothing about an agent that submits
fifty candidates to the same held-out scenes until one passes. With independent attempts at level 5% each,
fourteen null submissions give a 51% chance of at least one false promotion (1 − 0.95¹⁴), and five hundred give
certainty for practical purposes. The **blinded qualification lane** closes that hole (ADR-ARENA-08: discovery
and confirmation are separate lanes with separate data rights, because an optimizing agent that may resubmit to
the same held-out worlds will eventually pass by chance; BET-21: error control holds across an agent's adaptive
retries).

The lane's accounting unit is the **campaign**: a set of confirmation requests that share a claim family
(a task, a baseline class and a held-out world family) and a total error budget α. All agents working on the
claim family share the campaign and its index sequence. Request i receives the level α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)], and
the index advances for every committed request, including ones that end inconclusive or are refused after
commitment. Because 1/[i·(i + 1)] = 1/i − 1/(i + 1), the levels sum to α·m/(m + 1) after m requests and to α in
the limit. Request i tests a candidate chosen with everything seen before, on clusters independent of
everything seen before, so the conditional chance of a false promotion at request i is at most α_i, and a union
bound over requests gives a campaign-wide chance of any false promotion of at most α, for any number of retries
and any adaptive strategy. The argument needs three things: the request is fixed before the draw, the clusters
are fresh, and the level is spent whether or not the agent likes the answer. This bead builds the third: the
campaign object, the index and the level ledger, and the read verb `eval.ledger`.

Why opening a campaign is privileged: an agent that could open a fresh campaign would reset its index and void
the bound. Opening therefore needs the capability `qual:admin`.

## Scope

In: the campaign record, its two kinds and its lifecycle states; the index sequence and its concurrency rule;
the level series in exact rationals; the append-only ledger (INV-QUAL-03); the wire forms of levels; the
optional hard cap on submissions; the read verb `eval.ledger` with its admin actions `open` and `close`. Out: request commitment (N24b-16); cluster derivation, close-time
reveal and retirement (N24b-17); execution and receipts (BD-259); the capability firewall (BD-261); replay by
the offline auditor (BD-383).

## Technical approach

**Campaign record, schema `frob.qualify.campaign/1` (DESIGN).** `id` (`q:<claim-family-slug>/<serial>`, for
example `q:threshold-delivery/3`); `claim_family` = `{task: TaskId, baseline_class, world_family}`;
`alpha_total` (an exact decimal string, default "0.05"); `state` (`open`, `closed`); `opened_by`, `opened_seq`;
`key_commitment` (set by N24b-17 at opening); `max_requests` (optional hard cap); `family_capacity_clusters`;
`clusters_per_request` and `default_tier_plan` (the defaults a request that names neither receives, N24b-16;
the opener may set them, otherwise `clusters_per_request` comes from the configuration and the tier plan is
`{run: "referee"}`; the worked agent session's campaign `q:kitchen-tray/1` was opened with 200 clusters per
request); `config` (the BLAKE3 hash of `config/qualification.toml` at opening, and its `decided` flag and
`adr`); `predecessor` (the campaign closed before it on the same claim family, or none); `level_schedule`
(`series`, the default, or `bonferroni`, the fallback below); `kind` (below).

**Campaign kinds (DESIGN).** `qualification` (the default, everything in this bead) and `battery`: a sealed
container for objects that no agent token may read before close, such as the answer keys of the diagnosis
battery (N32b-37), held by the confirmation firewall (BD-261). A battery campaign has a label in place of a
claim family (`q:battery-<label>/<serial>`), no index, no levels, no sealed world family and no key derivation;
a qualification request to it is refused with `claim-family-mismatch` (N24b-16), and its close makes its
sealed objects readable and retires nothing. One mechanism serves both, so the firewall's layers, refusal and
audit apply to the battery's objects unchanged; the objects are written through the admin action `seal` and read
through the read-form input `--read-sealed <name>` (MCP `read_sealed`), both of which BD-261 adds to the same verb.

**The lane's configuration (OPEN-ARENA-3, written by N24c-13).** Opening reads `config/qualification.toml`:
`qual_admin_holders` (roles), `families_per_task_family`, `requests_per_family`, `clusters_per_request`,
`decided`, `adr`. A missing or malformed file loads as the fail-closed defaults (holders = owner, one family per
task family, 20 requests, 256 clusters, `decided: false`), never as an open configuration; who may hold
`qual:admin` is enforced by the capability registry from the same file (BD-261). The campaign takes
`family_capacity_clusters` = `requests_per_family` × `clusters_per_request` (5,120 under the defaults) and
records the file's hash, so a later change of the file does not alter an open campaign. An opener-set
`clusters_per_request` changes the capacity accordingly (`requests_per_family` × the campaign's value). With
`decided: false` a campaign may be opened only on a synthetic claim family (the `synthetic` label of the task
family); a real claim family has no sealed family until the decision exists, which is the fail-closed posture
of N24c-13, and opening one is refused with the symbolic `qualification-undecided` (`8xx`, exit 3; requested of
N24-REF; repairs: record the OPEN-ARENA-3 decision, N24c-13; use a synthetic claim family for tests). A battery
campaign needs no sealed world family and may be opened while undecided.

**Lifecycle rules.**

1. `open` requires a session holding `qual:admin`. Without it: FROB-REF-720 (the session token lacks a required
   scope), exit code 5.
2. At most one open campaign per claim family, and at most `families_per_task_family` campaigns open at once
   across the claim families of one task family (one under the default). A second opening while one is open is
   refused with exit code 7 (a conflict with the ledger's state), naming the open campaign, under the symbolic
   name `campaign-already-open` registered by the Arena refusal registry (N24-REF; numbered at implementation,
   tests match the name). After a close the world family is retired (N24b-17), so a new campaign on the same
   task needs a new sealed family and is a new decision by the holder of `qual:admin`: it is recorded with a
   reference to its predecessor (`predecessor`), so the total error budget spent on a claim family over time is
   visible. Opening on a retired family is N24b-17's refusal (FROB-REF-886).
3. `close` requires `qual:admin` (FROB-REF-720 without it), is irreversible, and triggers the reveal and
   retirement of N24b-17.

**Index and levels.** The index i of a request is assigned inside one immediate transaction of the ledger at
commitment (N24b-16): i = (last index of the campaign) + 1. The level is the exact rational
α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)], and the cumulative spend after m requests is α·m/(m + 1). Both are stored as reduced
rationals and printed on the wire in the display forms `"<alpha_total>/<i·(i+1)>"` and
`"<alpha_total>*<m>/<m+1>"`: for request 7 at α = 0.05, `"0.05/56"` and `"0.05*7/8"`.

**The level ledger (INV-QUAL-03: the ledger of levels is append-only, the index advances by one for every
committed request, and the total spent never exceeds α).** Table `qual_levels`: `(campaign, index, alpha_i,
alpha_spent, request_hash, ledger_seq, outcome)`, where `outcome` is filled once: `promoted`, `not-promoted`,
`inconclusive`, `refused-after-commit` or `cancelled`. Rules enforced by the store and re-checked by a
property test: a unique key on (campaign, index); indices contiguous from 1; no update of `alpha_i`,
`request_hash` or `ledger_seq`; no delete; `outcome` written at most once; a row exists for every committed
request whatever its outcome. `ledger_seq` is a string on the wire (integers above 2⁵³ never travel as JSON
numbers).

**Hard cap (the fallback of BET-21, built as a configuration).** A campaign may carry `max_requests`; request
`max_requests + 1` is refused before commitment, with no index assigned and no level spent (exit code 3; the
symbolic `campaign-request-cap` registered by N24-REF). If the lane's adaptive-retry gate fails, nothing is
called qualified and confirmation falls back to fixed-n exact studies with a hard cap on submissions per
campaign; with this field the fallback is a setting, not a rebuild. Under the fallback the level may be the
constant α/max_requests (a Bonferroni split, `level_schedule = "bonferroni"`, allowed only with
`max_requests`), chosen at opening and recorded; its cumulative spend after m requests is α·m/max_requests,
which reaches α at the cap.

**The verb `eval.ledger`.** Registry (VERBS-eval's row): cluster `eval`, kind `read`, first milestone M3,
capabilities `["eval:read"]`, output schema `frob.eval.ledger.out/1`. Input: a campaign id, or a claim family
(`--task`, `--baseline-class`, `--world-family`), which resolves to the family's open campaign, or to its latest
closed one when none is open. Output: `campaign`, `kind`, `state`, `claim_family`, `predecessor`,
`alpha_total`, `level_schedule`, `index` (the last committed), `alpha_spent`, `next_alpha_i`, the rows
`[{index, alpha_i, outcome, receipt_hash, units_used}]`, `max_requests` if set. Nothing sealed: no world
address, no per-unit outcome, no trace reference. After close it also returns the revealed key's identifier
and the location of the cluster derivation. Evidence block: class `Tested` for the arithmetic record, tier
`static`, determinism `exact`.

**Opening and closing: the admin actions of `eval.ledger` (DESIGN, the entry form VERBS-ground fixes for
`ground.band --retire`).** The plan says who may open and close a campaign and gives no surface for doing it.
The entry keeps `kind = "read"` and declares `actions = [{ name = "open", kind = "admin", capabilities =
["eval:read", "qual:admin"] }, { name = "close", kind = "admin", capabilities = ["eval:read", "qual:admin"] }]`
(VERBS-eval's row declares them, with BD-261's third action `seal`). Each action is idempotent and takes its own idempotency key: the same key and body
return the stored campaign record, the same key with a different body is FROB-REF-803 (exit 7). On the command
line:

    frob eval ledger --open --task <TaskId> [--baseline-class <class>] --world-family <family> [--slug <name>]
        [--alpha 0.05] [--clusters-per-request <n>] [--tier-plan '<json>'] [--max-requests <n>
        [--level-schedule bonferroni]] --key <k> --json
    frob eval ledger --open --kind battery --label <name> --key <k> --json
    frob eval ledger <campaign> --close --key <k> --json

`--baseline-class` is absent for an absolute claim family; the slug defaults to the task's short name. Over MCP
the same actions are the input property `action: "open"` or `action: "close"` of the tool `eval_ledger`, with
the same fields. `open` returns the new `frob.qualify.campaign/1` record (its `key_commitment` written by
N24b-17 in the same step); `close` returns the closed record and triggers N24b-17's reveal and retirement.
Refusals: FROB-REF-720 without `qual:admin` (exit 5); `campaign-already-open` (exit 7);
`qualification-undecided` (exit 3); FROB-REF-886 for a retired family (N24b-17); `campaign-not-open` for a
close of a campaign that is not open (exit 3).

**What a retry costs (printed by the verb's help and by dry runs).** The sample size of a test grows with
ln(1/α_i). Relative to a single test at α = 0.05, request 1 needs about 1.23 times the units, request 10 about
2.57 times, request 100 about 4.08 times and request 500 about 5.15 times. Retrying is allowed and is never
free.

Code: `crates/frob-arena/src/qualify/{campaign,levels}.rs`; the service lives inside the daemon.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Opening and closing a campaign succeed with `qual:admin` and are refused without it (FROB-REF-720, exit code 5); a second open campaign on one claim family is refused (exit code 7) and names the open one.
- [ ] Opening reads `config/qualification.toml`: the campaign's `family_capacity_clusters` equals requests × clusters from the file (5,120 under the defaults), its record carries the file's hash, `decided` and `adr`; a missing or malformed file gives the fail-closed defaults; with `decided: false` a real (non-synthetic) claim family cannot be opened (`qualification-undecided`, exit 3).
- [ ] The admin actions `open` and `close` are reachable as CLI flags and as the MCP `action` property with one handler; each is idempotent under its key (same key and body: the stored record; same key, different body: FROB-REF-803, exit 7); a claim-family input resolves to the open campaign, else to the latest closed one, and `predecessor` names the campaign closed before it.
- [ ] A battery campaign opens with a label and no claim family, has no index, levels or world family, refuses a qualification request with `claim-family-mismatch`, and its close retires nothing.
- [ ] A Bonferroni campaign (`max_requests = 4`) spends 0.05/4 per request and exactly α at the cap; `level_schedule = "bonferroni"` without `max_requests` is refused at opening.
- [ ] For m = 1 … 500 the stored cumulative spend equals α·m/(m + 1) as an exact rational and is below α (PO-ARENA-02, exact rational summation).
- [ ] The ledger is append-only: attempts to update a level, delete a row, skip an index or write an outcome twice all fail; a row exists for inconclusive, refused-after-commit and cancelled requests.
- [ ] Under 64 concurrent submissions to one campaign, indices are 1 … 64 with no gap and no duplicate.
- [ ] `frob eval ledger <campaign> --json` returns the index, levels spent and receipts and no sealed field; the wire strings for request 7 are `0.05/56` and `0.05*7/8`.
- [ ] `max_requests = 3` refuses a fourth request before commitment, with no level spent.
- [ ] The verb is in the registry and reachable through the CLI and the MCP surface; cancellation of a read has no side effect.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/qualify_ledger.sh` passes on a clean checkout at M3 against a real daemon.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `level_series_exact_partial_sums`: for α = 1/20 and m = 1 … 500, Σ α/[i·(i + 1)] = α·m/(m + 1) in exact rationals; at m = 7 the sum is 7/160 and α_7 = 1/1120.
- `level_series_never_exceeds_alpha`: property test over random α ∈ (0, 1) as rationals and m up to 10⁴.
- `level_wire_strings`: i = 1 → `0.05/2`, spent `0.05*1/2`; i = 7 → `0.05/56`, `0.05*7/8`; parsing the strings returns the stored rationals.
- `ledger_append_only`: the five forbidden operations fail.
- `ledger_index_advances_for_every_commit`: outcomes (promoted, inconclusive, refused-after-commit, cancelled) each consume one index.
- `campaign_open_requires_admin` (FROB-REF-720, exit 5); `campaign_single_open_per_family` (`campaign-already-open`, exit 7, the open campaign named; the second attempt leaves the index sequence untouched); `campaign_close_irreversible` (a second close under a new key: `campaign-not-open`).
- `campaign_reads_config`: with the default file, capacity 5,120 and the file hash in the record; with `requests_per_family = 10`, `clusters_per_request = 128`, capacity 1,280; with the file deleted, the defaults; with `decided: false`, opening a synthetic claim family succeeds and a real one is refused (`qualification-undecided`, exit 3); editing the file after opening leaves the open campaign's capacity and hash unchanged.
- `ledger_admin_actions_cli_and_mcp`: `--open` and `--close` on the CLI and `action: "open"`/`"close"` over MCP reach one handler and give byte-identical records; `ledger_admin_action_idempotent`: the same key and body twice give one campaign; a changed `--alpha` under the same key is FROB-REF-803, exit 7.
- `ledger_claim_family_input_and_predecessor`: with `q:x/1` closed and `q:x/2` open, the claim family resolves to `q:x/2`, whose `predecessor` is `q:x/1`; after `q:x/2` closes it resolves to `q:x/2`.
- `campaign_battery_kind`: `--kind battery --label diag` opens `q:battery-diag/1` with no index and no levels; a qualification request naming it is refused with `claim-family-mismatch`; close writes no retirement record.
- `campaign_bonferroni_schedule`: `max_requests = 4` with `bonferroni` gives levels 1/80 each at α = 1/20, cumulative m/80, exactly 1/20 at m = 4, and refuses request 5 (`campaign-request-cap`); `bonferroni` without `max_requests` is refused at opening.
- `campaign_families_per_task_family`: with the default of one, a second claim family of the same task family cannot be opened while the first is open.
- `campaign_concurrent_index_assignment`: 64 threads, contiguous indices.
- `campaign_hard_cap`: the cap refuses request max + 1 with nothing spent.
- `retry_cost_ratios`: ln(1/α_i)/ln(20) = 1.231, 2.569, 4.078, 5.150 for i = 1, 10, 100, 500 (± 5 × 10⁻⁴).

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead's own script, `scripts/e2e/qualify_ledger.sh` (header `bead=N24b-15 milestone=M3`), sources the STD-E2E
helper and drives the real daemon, `frob` and the MCP server with this bead's dependencies only (requests are
committed by N24b-16, which depends on this bead, so this script checks the campaign object, the actions and the
read form; the rows after real requests are N24b-24's):
1. `sessions`: `frob session open` for `owner` (the holder role of the default `config/qualification.toml`) and
   for `agent` (`--scopes eval:read,qual:run`), each with its own key.
2. `open_refused`: as `agent`, `frob eval ledger --open --task <synthetic TaskId> --world-family <family> --key
   "$(e2e_key open-agent)" --json`: FROB-REF-720, exit 5, no campaign created.
3. `open`: as `owner`, the same call under `$(e2e_key open)`: the `frob.qualify.campaign/1` record with
   `family_capacity_clusters` 5,120, the file's hash, `decided: false`; the same key and body again return the
   same record; the same key with `--alpha 0.1` is FROB-REF-803, exit 7. The MCP `eval_ledger` call with
   `action: "open"` and the same key returns byte-identical bytes.
4. `refused_opens`: a second open on the same claim family under a new key (`campaign-already-open`, exit 7,
   naming the open campaign, the index sequence untouched); an open on a non-synthetic claim family
   (`qualification-undecided`, exit 3); `--level-schedule bonferroni` without `--max-requests` (refused at opening,
   exit 3).
5. `read`: as `agent`, `frob eval ledger <campaign> --json` and the claim-family form resolve to the same campaign:
   index 0, `next_alpha_i` `0.05/2`, no sealed field; a Bonferroni campaign on a second synthetic family with
   `--max-requests 4` shows `next_alpha_i` `0.05/4`.
6. `battery`: `--open --kind battery --label e2e` as `owner`: `q:battery-e2e/1`, no index, no levels.
7. `close`: as `owner`, `--close` under its key: state `closed`; a second close under a new key
   (`campaign-not-open`, exit 3); the claim-family form now resolves to the closed campaign; `--close` as `agent`
   is FROB-REF-720, exit 5.
8. `known_answers`: `e2e_tier g0_laws KA-619` (the constant-level control caught) and `e2e_tier g1_known_answers
   KA-621`; the receipt hashes logged.
Logged per STD-E2E: command, session, key, exit code, the campaign state, `predecessor`, index, the level
strings, the cumulative spend against α, and a `refusal` event for each refused open and close. No sealed field
can appear in the log because the verb returns none. `scripts/e2e/arena_qualification_lane.sh` (N24b-24) runs
this script as a stage and adds the rows after real requests, the cap and the reveal.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-619 (exact partial sums α·m/(m + 1) for m up to 500; limit α; control: a constant level per request, whose
sum α·m exceeds α at m = 2 and must be caught by the never-exceeds test). KA-620 (fourteen null submissions at a
constant 5%: 1 − 0.95¹⁴ = 0.512). KA-621 (cost ratios). CX-267 (resubmit to the same held-out scenes until one
passes). Negative control for INV-QUAL-03: a build in which a refused-after-commit request returns its index to
the pool; the contiguity-with-outcomes test must fail. Metamorphic: the level of request i does not depend on
the outcomes of requests 1 … i − 1 nor on anything after i. Determinism: D0; identical rationals and strings on
every machine.

## Considerations

- PO-ARENA-02 is a candidate theorem card (T-26: for every n, the partial sum equals α·n/(n + 1) and is below α; the infinite sum is an analytic statement and is not needed). Until a checker accepts it, the class of the statement is `Tested` by exact rational summation.
- OPEN-ARENA-3 (owner: project lead; blocks GATE-QUAL-01). Fail-closed default: the owner alone opens and closes campaigns; one sealed family per task family, sized for 20 requests at 256 clusters; no result is called qualified until a family exists.
- Trust boundary: whoever holds `qual:admin` is trusted. A warning sign of gaming is a campaign with many requests and no close, or successive campaigns on one task; `eval.ledger` exposes both.
- The dashboard's budget pane shows each submission's α_i and the sum spent by reading this verb.
- OPEN-SAFE-4 (owner: project owner): retention of retired confirmation clusters and idempotency keys. Default: keys 30 days, retired clusters sealed indefinitely; the level ledger itself is never collected.
