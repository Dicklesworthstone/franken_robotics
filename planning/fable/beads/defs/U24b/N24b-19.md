## Background

The blinded qualification lane returns a receipt: the decision, the index, the level spent, the units used and
a hash. The plan then fixes what a receipt means for the words people use: **a result is `qualified` only with
a receipt whose decision is `promoted`.** Reports cite the campaign, the index and the level. Citing a
discovery-lane comparison as a qualification is a protocol refusal (FROB-REF-845). And the evidence travels:
the receipts, the ledger of levels and, after close, the cluster derivation are part of the `.frobcert` bundle
and are replayed by the offline auditor `frob-verify`.

Why a word needs a mechanism. Every number that crosses the agent boundary carries a claim product (the kind
of claim, the evidence class set by the weakest input, a tier, a determinism class), and citing a result above
its product is refused. "Qualified" is the strongest standing a design comparison can have in this system; if
it could be attached by a report template, the lane's error control would be decorative. So the standing is
computed from a receipt, never asserted.

What the bundle can and cannot say. ADR-LEMMA-06 (decision): statistical receipts travel in bundles and are
never proofs; an auditor can recompute arithmetic, it cannot attest that episodes ran. The proof-kind registry
has a row for this, `sample-coverage`: the arithmetic of a recorded study (counts, e-values, spent α); never a
proof, class at most `Tested`. This bead writes that evidence into bundles; the readers are in a different crate
with no shared code (the writer is in `frob-lemma`, the readers in `frob-cert`): the in-tree checker of the kind
`sample-coverage` (N12-13, M3), which `prove.bundle` runs before it returns, and the offline replay of whole
campaigns by `frob-verify` (BD-383, M5).

## Scope

In: the standing field and its computation; the citation format; refusal FROB-REF-845 at every place a
standing is rendered or exported; the campaign section of the bundle, before and after close; the writer; the
appending of FROB-REF-845 to `prove.bundle`'s `refusals` list in `registries/verbs.toml`. Out:
the lane and its receipt (BD-259); the offline checker and its mutations (BD-383); the report verbs themselves
(BD-302), which call this bead's check.

## Technical approach

**Standing.** A comparison or acceptance result carries `standing` in its evidence: `provisional` (a fast-tier
result not yet promoted), `established` (promoted on the trust ladder), or `qualified`. The constructor of
`qualified` is private to `frob-arena` and takes a `QualifyReceipt` whose `decision` is `Promoted`, whose
request (looked up by campaign and index) names the same candidate, baseline, task and claim as the result
being labelled, and whose receipt hash verifies. There is no other constructor and no conversion from the
other two values (compile-fail test). The claim product is unchanged by qualification: class stays `Tested`,
`reality` stays `no-claim`; qualification is a statement about error control across retries, not about truth
of the model.

**Citation.** Wherever a qualified result is printed or exported it carries `qualified_by: {campaign, index,
alpha_i, alpha_total, receipt_hash}`, rendered as, for example, "qualified in campaign q:threshold-delivery/3,
request 7, level 0.05/56 of 0.05".

**FROB-REF-845: a result is cited as qualified without a promoting receipt.** Exit code 3. Raised by: the
report verbs when a template or caller marks a result qualified; `prove.bundle` when asked to bundle a result
as qualified; the envelope serializer when a handler sets the standing by any path other than the constructor
(a run-time check that backs the type-level one, for data read back from storage); import of an artifact that
claims the standing without a verifying receipt. Ranked repairs: cite the result as a development result
(discovery lane); submit a committed request with `eval.qualify`; if a receipt exists but is `not-promoted` or
`inconclusive`, cite it as such with its index and level.

**The campaign section of the bundle (DESIGN layout; canonical JSON under the bundle schema `frob.cert/1`).**
One bundle entry of kind `sample-coverage` per cited request, under the answer's Merkle root, plus the bundle's
`lineage.alpha_spend` array. Contents:

| Part | Before close | After close |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign record | id, claim family identifiers, `alpha_total`, the key commitment, state | plus the revealed key |
| Level ledger | For every index 1 … m of the campaign: `alpha_i`, cumulative spend, the request hash, the commit's ledger sequence, the outcome (a prefix of the ledger up to and including the cited request is the minimum; the whole ledger is included when the campaign is closed) | the same |
| The cited request | Its canonical bytes (so the hash can be recomputed), its pre-analysis, the receipt | the same |
| Cluster derivation | The Merkle root of the request's cluster digests and the derivation record's ledger sequence | plus the per-cluster seeds' inputs (index and counters, skips), the cluster digests, the per-unit outcomes and the pair counts |
| Study arithmetic | The rule, n and level | plus the counts (with the accounting identity) or the e-process increments, from which the decision is recomputed |
| Flags | The three authorization flags, all false | the same |

`lineage.alpha_spend` lists, for the cited request, `{campaign, index, alpha_i, alpha_spent}` as exact rational
strings. Numbers that can exceed 2⁵³ (ledger sequences) are strings. The entry's class is `Tested`.

**Writer.** `frob-lemma` assembles the section from plain records handed over by `frob-arena` (the campaign,
ledger rows, request bytes, receipt, and after close the reveal). It is written against the schema and shares
no code with the reader. `prove.bundle … --receipt <hash>` adds the section to a design's bundle; the in-tree
check then runs the `sample-coverage` checker of N12-13 before the bundle is returned (the same rule as for
mission bundles). A bundle written before close is valid and smaller: the checker verifies the levels, the
ledger prefix, the request and receipt hashes and the population, and reports that the decision is not
recomputed because the counts are still sealed. Re-bundling after close adds the reveal, the checker then
recomputes the decision from the revealed counts, and the bundle has a new hash.

**No sealed data before close.** The writer refuses to include any per-cluster datum while the campaign is
open; it can only obtain them through the post-close reveal. A bundle is an export, so the firewall's
sealed-type rule applies to it as to any envelope.

**The registry row of `prove.bundle`.** VERBS-prove's block already names FROB-REF-845 with this bead as its
raiser, but the M0 entry in `registries/verbs.toml` cannot list it: the lint RL-06 (N32a-02) refuses a listed
code that `registries/refusals.toml` does not hold, and N24-REF enters 845 at M1. This bead's change, which
lands the receipt form, therefore appends `FROB-REF-845` to `prove.bundle`'s `refusals` list in
`registries/verbs.toml`, so that the generated help, the MCP tool description and the refusal catalogue show it
from M3 on.

Code: `crates/frob-arena/src/qualify/standing.rs`; `crates/frob-lemma/src/bundle/campaign.rs`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `standing: qualified` can be constructed only from a promoting receipt that matches the result's candidate, baseline, task and claim (compile-fail test for other paths; run-time check for stored data).
- [ ] A report or bundle request that marks a discovery-lane comparison as qualified is refused with FROB-REF-845, exit code 3, with the three repairs.
- [ ] A receipt with decision `not-promoted` or `inconclusive` cannot produce the standing; it can be cited as what it is.
- [ ] Every rendered qualified result carries campaign, index and level.
- [ ] `prove.bundle --receipt` writes the campaign section; before close it contains no per-cluster datum; after close it contains the reveal; both are accepted by the in-tree check.
- [ ] `lineage.alpha_spend` holds exact rational strings equal to the ledger's.
- [ ] Re-serializing the section gives identical bytes; the three authorization flags are present and false.
- [ ] The qualified standing does not change class, tier or the `reality` field of the claim product.
- [ ] `prove.bundle`'s `refusals` list in `registries/verbs.toml` contains `FROB-REF-845` after this change, RL-06 passes, and `frob prove bundle --help --json` lists the code.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/qualify_standing_bundle.sh` passes on a clean checkout at M3 against a real daemon.

### Unit tests
- `qualified_requires_promoting_receipt`: the three decisions; only `Promoted` yields the standing.
- `qualified_requires_matching_request`: a promoting receipt for a different candidate is rejected.
- `qualified_no_other_constructor`: compile-fail tests for struct-literal construction and for conversion from `established`.
- `refuse_845_report_marks_discovery_as_qualified`; `refuse_845_import_without_receipt`.
- `citation_format`: campaign `q:threshold-delivery/3`, index 7 → "level 0.05/56 of 0.05".
- `bundle_campaign_section_open`: contains the ledger prefix 1 … 7, request bytes, receipt, Merkle root; contains no address, no cluster digest list, no outcome vector; N12-13's checker accepts it with the note that the decision is not recomputed.
- `bundle_campaign_section_closed`: additionally the key, counters, digests, outcomes, counts; `a_only + b_only + both + neither + unscored = planned`.
- `bundle_alpha_spend_exact`: `"0.05/56"` and `"0.05*7/8"`.
- `bundle_roundtrip_bytes`.
- `standing_leaves_claim_product`: class `Tested`, `reality: no-claim` before and after.
- `prove_bundle_row_lists_845`: the loaded `prove.bundle` entry's `refusals` contains `FROB-REF-845`, and RL-06 run over `registries/verbs.toml` reports no unregistered code.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead's own script, `scripts/e2e/qualify_standing_bundle.sh` (header `bead=N24b-19 milestone=M3`), sources
the STD-E2E helper and drives the real daemon and `frob` with this bead's dependencies only, as `owner` and
`agent` sessions (`frob session open`, each under its own key):
1. `promote`: as `owner`, a campaign on N24b-18's synthetic family `family:qual-sim-coin@1` (`frob eval ledger
   --open … --key "$(e2e_key open)" --json`); as `agent`, the planted-gain candidate submitted with `frob eval
   qualify <candidate> --task task:qual-sim-coin@1 --claim '…' --baseline <baseline> --key "$(e2e_key q1)"
   --json` (BD-259) until a `promoted` receipt: its hash, index and `alpha_spent` (`0.05*1/2` for request 1)
   logged.
2. `cite_without_receipt`: under keys of their own, (a) step 1's promoting receipt cited for another design,
   `frob prove bundle --design <other> --receipt <hash> --out bad.frobcert --key "$(e2e_key b-845a)" --json`
   (the receipt's request names another candidate), and (b) a design whose discovery-lane comparison the test
   fixture stored with its standing edited to `qualified` on disk, bundled under `$(e2e_key b-845b)` (the
   run-time check refuses it on read): each `e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-845 3`, the three repairs logged, no
   bundle written.
3. `bundle_open`: `frob prove bundle --design <candidate> --receipt <hash> --out open.frobcert --key "$(e2e_key
   b-open)" --json`; `frob prove verify open.frobcert --json` accepts it with the note that the decision is not
   recomputed; a scan of the bundle finds no address, no cluster digest list and no outcome vector.
4. `bundle_closed`: as `owner`, `frob eval ledger <campaign> --close --key "$(e2e_key close)" --json`; then the
   same bundle call under `$(e2e_key b-closed)` (a new bundle with a new hash); `frob prove verify
   closed.frobcert --json` recomputes the decision; the pair-count identity holds.
5. `help`: `frob prove bundle --help --json` lists FROB-REF-845.
6. `determinism`: step 4's bundle call under a new key gives identical bundle bytes.
Logged per STD-E2E: commands, keys, exit codes, the refusal with repairs, bundle hashes, entry counts, the in-tree
check's verdict (with the "decision not recomputed" note before close), and the sealed-value scan of the
pre-close bundle. The report verbs' 845 (`frob report design` over the edited result) is asserted by BD-302,
which depends on this bead. `scripts/e2e/arena_qualification_lane.sh` (N24b-24) runs this script as a stage.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-623 (replay of a campaign; the bundle is its input). Known answer from the worked session: a first request
on 200 clusters at level 0.05/2 that is promoted yields a receipt with `alpha_spent` "0.05*1/2", and the bundle
built from it has an entry that the in-tree check accepts. Negative controls: a stored result whose standing
was edited to `qualified` on disk (the run-time check must refuse it on read); a pre-close bundle into which a
test build injects one cluster digest (the sealed-value scan must find it). Metamorphic: bundling the same
receipt twice gives identical bytes. Determinism: bundle bytes are a deterministic function of their inputs
(class D0), on two hosts and two worker counts.

## Considerations

- The auditor recomputes arithmetic and ordering; it cannot attest that the episodes ran. Reports must not present a campaign bundle under the Proof claim, and the bundle's class says so.
- A citation outlives its campaign: after the family is retired the receipt remains valid evidence of the decision made then; a new claim on new worlds needs a new campaign.
- If the lane's adaptive-retry gate has failed, nothing is called qualified: the constructor is then disabled by configuration and every request for the standing returns FROB-REF-845 with the gate's status as the reason.
- Size: a closed campaign's section with 256 clusters per request is small (digests and one outcome per unit per arm); the size is logged.
