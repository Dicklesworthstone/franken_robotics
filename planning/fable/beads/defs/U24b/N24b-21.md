## Background

A fixed-n study has a frozen rule and therefore a finite set of outcomes; a wanted verdict may not be among
them. The verb `eval.preanalyze` enumerates the reachable verdicts and the power of a fixed-n rule at a given
n and level, and the study admission layer refuses a study that mixes types, lacks a pre-analysis, names an
unreachable verdict or asks for paired statistics under an uncontrolled determinism class. This script drives
both from the user's side with the real `frob` binary. It is the user-side evidence for GATE-ARENA-05 (claim:
Known answer; M1; TARGET: `eval.preanalyze` reproduces the tables of fixture 1 exactly and refuses the
unreachable verdict); the gate's one recorder is N24b-06, which measures it and records it from its known-answer
receipt (convention 9), and this script writes no gate row.

## Scope

In: one script covering the verb `eval.preanalyze` from the command line (the enumeration tables, their
consequences, power, the level dependence, the equivalence and absolute rules, the refusal of an unreachable
declared verdict at the verb, the committed table, hash stability, the two mutant builds) and the admission
layer's refusals provoked through `frob study plan` (mixed types, a missing pre-analysis, an uncontrolled
class), with the race level, one interim status and the peeking figures; its structured log and negative
controls; running the two mechanism scripts it builds on as stages (`scripts/e2e/preanalyze_tables.sh` of N24b-06
and `scripts/e2e/study_admission.sh` of N24b-14).

Out: the enumerator and the verb's definition (N24b-06), the admission layer (N24b-14) and the study engines
(BD-124) themselves; the executors of `study.plan` and `study.status` that steps 8 and 9 drive (N33b-19, a
dependency); the study lifecycle from the user's side, which is the other script,
`scripts/e2e/study_two_types.sh` of N33b-19: a plan's identity and its embedded pre-analysis at n = 64 and
n = 86 (GATE-ATEL-06), a declared tie refused at `study.plan`, submission under an idempotency key, status
polled to the end, `eval.compare` and its five comparison-time refusals. That script asserts no table of
`eval.preanalyze`; it compares hashes with it.

## Technical approach

**Path and invocation.** `scripts/e2e/arena_study_preanalysis.sh [--mutants]`. Plain shell; non-zero exit on a
failed assertion; idempotent (writes only under `target/e2e-logs/arena_study_preanalysis/` and a scratch
store); no randomness except the seeded null simulation of step 9, whose seed is printed.

**Steps.** Every `study.plan`, `study.submit` and `eval.compare` call carries `--key "$(e2e_key <label>)"` with
its own label (they are `mutate` and `run` verbs; convention 5).

0. `stages`: `scripts/e2e/preanalyze_tables.sh` (N24b-06) and `scripts/e2e/study_admission.sh` (N24b-14), each
   run as a stage (with `--mutants` passed through), their log paths, digests and receipt hashes logged.
1. `enumerate`: `frob eval preanalyze --rule paired-clopper-pearson/1 --n N --level 0.05 --margin 0.05 --json`
   for N = 64, 86, 128, 256. Assert outcomes and counts: 2,145 → 521, 521, 0, 1,103; 3,828 → 1,031, 1,031, 1,
   1,765; 8,385 → 2,517, 2,517, 4, 3,347; 33,153 → 11,384, 11,384, 39, 10,346.
2. `consequences`: at N = 64 assert the concordance interval ±0.0662, `min_b_for_prefer` = 15, 17, 19, 21, 23
   for a = 0 … 4, and `first_reachable` for the tie = 86 with the single pair (0, 0).
3. `power`: alternatives (0.17, 0), (0.25, 0.02), (0.40, 0.02) for "prefer B" and (0.02, 0.02), (0, 0) for the
   tie. Assert at N = 64: 0.117, 0.406, 0.962, 0, 0; at 128: 0.775, 0.906, above 0.999, 0.072, 1; at 256:
   0.998, above 0.999, above 0.999, 0.427, 1.
4. `levels`: `--level 0.05/2`, `0.05/110`, `0.05/250500`; assert the tie is first reachable at 99, 178 and 328,
   and that N = 64 at level 0.05/2 has 478 preferring outcomes. Assert each record's `level` field is the
   reduced rational (`1/40`, `1/2200`, `1/5010000`; `1/20` for the step-1 records), that the four hashes at
   N = 64 differ, and that `--level 1/20` reproduces step 1's N = 64 hash byte for byte (the level is part of
   the record N24b-06 defines).
5. `other_rules`: `--rule paired-total-discordance/1 --n 64 --level 0.05` gives 0.0457 for zero discordant
   pairs; `--rule one-sided-binomial-bound/1 --n 200 --level 0.05/2` gives bounds 0.0432 and 0.0504 at 3 and 4
   failures.
6. `refuse_unreachable`: the same call at N = 64 with `--declared-verdicts promote,inferior,practical-tie` (the
   flag and the verdict tokens are those N24b-06 defines); assert
   refusal `FROB-REF-532`, exit code 3, the reachable set and the repair "n = 86" attached.
7. `committed_table`: regenerate `registries/preanalysis/paired-clopper-pearson-1.toml` to a scratch path and
   assert byte equality with the committed file.
8. `refuse_mixture`, `refuse_no_preanalysis`, `refuse_uncontrolled_class`: `frob study plan` with a request
   carrying both type declarations (expect `FROB-REF-552`); a fixed-n plan with no pre-analysis (expect
   `FROB-REF-532`); a paired plan with one arm under the labelled nondeterministic profile (expect
   `FROB-REF-533`). Exit code 3 each.
9. `race_level` and `status_interim`: a 5-way race plan shows level 0.0125 per pairwise study; a fixed-n plan at
   n = 64 is submitted (`frob study submit <plan> --key …`), and `frob study status` polled while units done is
   below 64 (the units done of each poll logged; the first poll follows the submit at once) returns progress and
   no interval or verdict; the status after completion returns the verdict once.
9a. `refuse_admission_declarations`: through `frob study plan`, the three declaration checks of N24b-14's
   `study::admit`: a plan over clustered worlds declaring `unit: world`, a race plan whose pairwise study
   carries the undivided level 0.05, and a design comparison with no `controller_arm` (then one with two
   `ControllerId`s under `shared`); expect FROB-REF-802, exit code 3, and the pointers `/seed_protocol/unit`,
   the pairwise study's `level` (with `required` 1/80), and `/controller_arm`. The same planted mixed-type
   plan as step 8 is also passed to `frob eval compare`, which must answer with the comparison code 553
   where `study plan` answered 552 (one situation, one code per verb, the rule of N24-REF).
9b. `independent_enumeration`: the conformance re-implementation of N24b-06 (double precision, regularized
   incomplete beta, no shared code) prints its tables for N = 64, 86, 128 and 256 at level 0.05 and for N = 64
   at 0.05/2 (N24b-06's example target `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example preanalysis_reference -- --rule
   paired-clopper-pearson/1 --n <N> --margin 0.05 --level <α> --json`, JSON on stdout); the script logs `table_digest` (BLAKE3 of the canonical count table) for the production verb's output and
   for the reference, asserts the two agree count for count, and on a mismatch logs the first differing (N,
   a, b) cell with both verdicts. Theorem card T-26 (CARD-T-26) cites this step and steps `consequences` and
   `refuse_unreachable` of this script's log as its tested evidence.
10. `peeking` (context for the refusal of step 8): `e2e_tier g0_laws KA-618` runs the admission layer's known
    answer; the receipt's measured values are asserted: 13/256 after 8 looks and 17.4% (± 0.05 points) after 64.
11. `hash_stability`: run step 1 twice; assert identical pre-analysis hashes.
12. `negative_controls` (with `--mutants`): a build whose endpoints are rounded inward, and a build using tails
    of α/2 instead of α/4; step 1's assertions must fail under each, and the script records the control as
    caught.

**Logging.** Per the shared helper of STD-E2E: newline-delimited JSON at
`target/e2e-logs/arena_study_preanalysis/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line has
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim Known answer, class Tested, tier static, determinism exact), expected and measured
values per table cell, the pre-analysis hash, and for refusals the code, pointer and ranked repairs. The final
`summary` line gives assertion counts, controls caught and the gate touched (GATE-ARENA-05).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, is executable and passes on a clean checkout at M1.
- [ ] Every table cell of steps 1 to 5 is asserted individually and logged with expected and measured values.
- [ ] The tie request at N = 64 is refused with FROB-REF-532 and the three study-type refusals fire with their codes and exit code 3.
- [ ] The three declaration refusals of `study::admit` fire with FROB-REF-802 and their pointers; the mixed-type plan yields 552 through `study plan` and 553 through `eval compare`.
- [ ] Step `independent_enumeration` logs both table digests, and the production and reference tables agree count for count at every N and level asserted.
- [ ] The committed table equals a fresh generation.
- [ ] With `--mutants`, both planted defects are caught.
- [ ] The script writes no gate row; its `summary` line names GATE-ARENA-05 as touched with the control (the refused tie) caught, and the stage runs of N24b-06's and N24b-14's scripts pass (N24b-06 records the gate from its own receipt).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
A `--list-steps` mode prints the step names; a small test asserts the list so that a dropped step is caught. A
`--selftest` mode alters one expected count (521 to 520) and must make the script fail, showing its assertions
can fail.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. Commands from the user's side: `frob eval preanalyze`, `frob study plan`, `frob study
submit`, `frob study status`, `frob eval compare` (step 9a only); the conformance reference through its example
target (step 9b); `e2e_tier` for KA-618; the two stage scripts. Assertions and log content as above.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-612 (enumeration; control: a tie verdict requested at n = 64 is refused), KA-613 (power), KA-614 (0.0457),
KA-618 (peeking: 13/256 after 8 looks; 8.76%, 12.9%, 17.4%), KA-621 (99, 178, 328; 478). CX-265, CX-266.
Negative controls: the refused tie; the two mutant builds; the self-test. Determinism: output hashes identical
twice, and on both platforms (class D0).

## Considerations

- The whole script is arithmetic and should finish in seconds; N = 256 dominates.
- Rules registered by other subsystems under the same verb (a cadence rule for threshold crossings; the ten-fold study plan) get their own steps in their owners' scripts; this script fails if the verb's rule list loses one of the three rules it tests.
