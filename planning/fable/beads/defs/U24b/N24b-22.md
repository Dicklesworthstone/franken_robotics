## Background

`fixture:quad-split-grip` asks whether a wider sole helps a quadruped arrive on a landing whose surface has a
low-grip strip under one side. Its limiting model predicts a reversal with the sign of the foot's lateral
offset, which a count of contact area cannot produce. This script drives the whole fixture from the user's
side with the real `frob` binary: the limiting model on the host, the plate-drag rig on the referee, the
slip-metric invariance, the development set with its reflected cases, and optionally the held-out factorial
under two contact laws. It is the script from which GATE-ARENA-06 is recorded (claim: Known answer; M2;
TARGET: limiting-model rows within 1% on the referee; slip invariance to 10⁻¹² relative in double precision;
reflected cases mirror inside the determinism class).

## Scope

In: one script, its steps, assertions, negative controls and structured log. Out: the fixture's mechanisms
(BD-202, N24b-11, N24b-12, N24b-13).

## Technical approach

**Path and invocation.** `scripts/e2e/arena_fixture_quad_split_grip.sh [--tier referee|batch-cpu|cuda|metal]
[--holdout] [--mutants]`. Plain shell; non-zero exit on a failed assertion; idempotent (writes only under
`target/e2e-logs/arena_fixture_quad_split_grip/` and a scratch store); seeds and world addresses of every cell
printed in the log.

**Steps.**

1. `register`: `frob task fixture quad-split-grip --layer analytic --tier host --json` on a fresh store; assert
   the pre-run record precedes any episode, and that it holds the frozen `ControllerId` and both sole designs.
2. `limiting_model_host`: assert capacities 0.525 and 0.525 at z = 0; 0.690 and 0.619 at +12 mm; 0.360 and
   0.431 at −12 mm (40 mm sole; 70 mm sole), and yaw moments per newton 2.75 and 4.81 mm; 1.76 and 4.25 mm.
3. `plate_drag_referee`: `--layer rigs --tier referee`; assert the six pull forces (52.5, 52.5, 69.0, 61.93,
   36.0, 43.07 N at a 100 N load) and six guide moments (0.275, 0.481, 0.176, 0.425, 0.176, 0.425 N·m) within
   1%; log the quadrature count used.
4. `slip_invariance`: `--rows slip-invariance`; assert the relative change of the slip distance under
   duplication of one quadrature point with halved weights is at most 10⁻¹².
5. `plate_drag_controls` (with `--mutants`): `--control pyramidal-cone` and `--control
   single-quadrature-point`; the capacity rows must fail (expected errors about 36.6% and, at +12 mm on the
   40 mm sole, 16%); also the slip metric's control (unweighted point speeds) must fail step 4. Each is
   recorded as caught.
6. `check_task` and `settle`: `frob task check`, `frob task settle` for both sole designs; assert admission
   (reach 13 s, horizon 13 s), penetration at most 0.5 mm, first impulse within 5% of static support; assert
   the preflight report shows the two foot assemblies' mass and inertia equal to 10⁻⁹ relative.
7. `development_set`: `--layer batch --set development --tier <t>`; assert 14 verdict objects (two soles ×
   seven cells), each with coverage kind `interval`; log per cell the verdict, margin, binding atom, per-foot
   slip, distance, work, peak torque and falls.
8. `reflection`: the same with `--reflect`; for each split-grip cell assert equal verdict, binding atom and
   decided-at tick with its mirrored counterpart, and continuous quantities within the tier's tolerance.
9. `holdout_register`, `holdout_rigid`, `holdout_compliant_refine`, `decision` (with `--holdout`): assert the
   20 cells, both law identities and the rule are registered before the first held-out episode; run 20 cells
   per sole under the rigid law and under the compliant law with refinement; log the classification vector's
   digest per refinement level; assert the outcome is one of `preferred`, `no-preference`,
   `fidelity-dependent` and that it equals the decision function applied to the logged completion counts.
10. `determinism`: steps 3 and 7 twice; assert identical digests.

**Logging.** Per the shared helper of STD-E2E: newline-delimited JSON at
`target/e2e-logs/arena_fixture_quad_split_grip/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line
has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), expected and measured values with units
and thresholds (1%, 10⁻¹², the reflection tolerance), digests, and on failure the first differing channel and
the `.frun` paths of both members of a pair. The `summary` line gives assertion counts, controls caught and the
gate touched (GATE-ARENA-06). No private-scene data (the fixture is synthetic).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, is executable and passes on a clean checkout at M2 on the referee.
- [ ] Each clause of GATE-ARENA-06 is covered by at least one logged assertion with measured value and threshold: steps 3, 4 and 8.
- [ ] With `--mutants`, the three controls are caught; a control made ineffective fails the script.
- [ ] With `--holdout`, registration precedes the held-out runs and the printed outcome matches the logged counts.
- [ ] Two runs give identical digests for the compared steps.
- [ ] The gate record for GATE-ARENA-06 cites this script's log by path and digest.
- [ ] Cancelling during `development_set` leaves no partial store and no orphaned job.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`--list-steps` prints the step names and a small test asserts the list. `--selftest` alters one expected
capacity (0.690 to 0.700) and must fail.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. Commands from the user's side: `frob task fixture`, `frob task check`, `frob task
settle`. Assertions and log content as above.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-616 (limiting model, 1% on the referee; controls: a pyramidal friction cone; a sole with one quadrature
point), KA-617 (reflected cases), KA-599 (slip invariance; control: unweighted point speeds). CX-268. Negative
controls: the three mutants; the self-test; a planted misplacement of the mirrored strip by 5 mm (with
`--mutants`), which must break step 8. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; with `--tier`, on batch tiers
against the fidelity envelope.

## Considerations

- Runtime: the plate-drag rows are short; the development set is 14 episodes of 13 simulated seconds; the holdout with refinement is the long part and runs behind `--holdout`.
- The outcome of the holdout is not asserted beyond being well-formed; it is a finding, and a `fidelity-dependent` outcome is a legitimate one.
- If the referee cannot express a material boundary inside one footprint, step 3 reports the rig as not expressible and the gate cannot be recorded as passed.
