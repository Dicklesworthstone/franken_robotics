## Background

`fixture:quad-split-grip` asks whether a wider sole helps a quadruped arrive on a landing whose surface has a
low-grip strip under one side. Its limiting model predicts a reversal with the sign of the foot's lateral
offset, which a count of contact area cannot produce. This script drives the whole fixture from the user's
side with the real `frob` binary: the limiting model on the host, the plate-drag rig on the referee, the
slip-metric invariance, the development set with its reflected cases, and optionally the held-out factorial
under two contact laws. It composes the own scripts of the fixture's beads into one run and adds the steps no
single bead owns.

It records no gate. GATE-ARENA-06 (claim: Known answer; M2; TARGET: limiting-model rows within 1% on the
referee; slip invariance to 10⁻¹² relative in double precision; reflected cases mirror inside the determinism
class) is recorded by BD-202, the bead that measures it, from its receipt (convention 9). This script's log is
user-side evidence beside that record.

## Scope

In: one script, its stages and steps, assertions, negative controls and structured log. Out: the fixture's
mechanisms and their own scripts (BD-202, N24b-11, N24b-12, N24b-13), which this script runs as stages.

## Technical approach

**Path and invocation.** `scripts/e2e/arena_fixture_quad_split_grip.sh [--holdout] [--mutants]`. Plain shell;
non-zero exit on a failed assertion; idempotent (writes only under
`target/e2e-logs/arena_fixture_quad_split_grip/` and a scratch store); seeds and world addresses of every cell
printed in the log. Every call of a run verb (`task.fixture`, `task.settle`) carries `--key "$(e2e_key
<label>)"`, and two runs the script compares carry distinct labels (convention 5).

**Stages (the own scripts of the dependencies, each run whole, with `--mutants` passed through; a stage that
fails fails this script).**

0. `model_rigs`: N24b-12's `scripts/e2e/arena_fixture2_model.sh` (the limiting model on the host: capacities
   0.525 and 0.525 at z = 0, 0.690 and 0.619 at +12 mm, 0.360 and 0.431 at −12 mm, yaw moments per newton 2.75
   and 4.81 mm, 1.76 and 4.25 mm; the plate-drag rig on the referee: pull forces 52.5, 52.5, 69.0, 61.93, 36.0,
   43.07 N and guide moments 0.275, 0.481, 0.176, 0.425, 0.176, 0.425 N·m within 1%, with the sample layout of 16
   samples at z = 0 and 18 at ±12 mm; the row `slip-invariance` at 10⁻¹²; with `--mutants` the controls
   `pyramidal-cone` (about 36.6% on every capacity row) and `single-quadrature-point` (16% at +12 mm on the
   40 mm sole) caught).
1. `slip_metric`: N24b-11's `scripts/e2e/arena_slip_metric.sh` (the recorded box drag, the stance channel, and
   with `--mutants` the unweighted-sum control caught).
2. `holdout` (with `--holdout`): N24b-13's `scripts/e2e/arena_fixture2_holdout.sh` (its steps `capacity`,
   `holdout_register`, `holdout_rigid`, `holdout_compliant_refine`, `decision`, `changed_after_registration`).

**Steps of this script (each a `step` value in the log).**

3. `register`: `frob task fixture quad-split-grip --layer analytic --tier host --key "$(e2e_key reg)" --json` on
   a fresh store; assert the pre-run record precedes any episode, and that it holds the frozen `ControllerId`
   and both sole designs.
4. `check_task` and `settle`: `frob task check` and `frob task settle … --key "$(e2e_key settle-<sole>)"` for both
   sole designs; assert admission (reach 13 s, horizon 13 s), penetration at most 0.5 mm, first impulse within 5%
   of static support; assert the preflight report shows the two foot assemblies' mass and inertia equal to 10⁻⁹
   relative; assert the contact-capacity preflight of BD-202 (`frob contact capacity` per sole: the static
   maximum of active samples against the referee's cap of 21) and log the adjudicator it selects (the referee,
   or `batch-cpu` under `strict` while the referee's cap is 21, FD-FSIM-13).
5. `development_set`: `frob task fixture quad-split-grip --layer batch --set development` with the selected
   adjudicator in full (`--tier referee`, or `--tier batch-cpu --profile strict`) and `--key "$(e2e_key dev-1)"`;
   assert 14 verdict objects (two soles × seven cells), each with coverage kind `interval`, every episode on the
   one adjudicator named in step 4, and no contact set truncated (a FROB-REF-721 refusal is a `refusal` event
   counted per sole, and a difference of more than one percentage point between the soles' refusal shares must
   appear as `capacity-biased` on the result); log per cell the verdict, margin, binding atom, per-foot slip,
   distance, work, peak torque and falls.
6. `reflection`: the same with `--reflect` under `--key "$(e2e_key dev-reflect)"`; for each split-grip cell
   assert equal verdict, binding atom and decided-at tick with its mirrored counterpart, and continuous
   quantities within the tier's tolerance.
7. `reflection_control` (with `--mutants`): BD-202's planted asymmetry (the mirrored scene built with the strip
   5 mm misplaced) under its own key; the reflection check of step 6 must fail (`e2e_expect_caught`).
8. `determinism`: step 5 again under `--key "$(e2e_key dev-2)"`; assert identical verdict and trace digests.

**Logging.** Per the shared helper of STD-E2E: newline-delimited JSON at
`target/e2e-logs/arena_fixture_quad_split_grip/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line
has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), expected and measured values with units
and thresholds (1%, 10⁻¹², the reflection tolerance), digests, and on failure the first differing channel and
the `.frun` paths of both members of a pair. Each stage logs under its own script's directory and this log
records the stage's exit code and log path. The `summary` line gives assertion counts, controls caught and the
gate touched (GATE-ARENA-06); the script writes no gate row. No private-scene data (the fixture is synthetic).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, is executable and passes on a clean checkout at M2, its stages included.
- [ ] Each clause of GATE-ARENA-06 is covered by at least one logged assertion with measured value and threshold: the stage `model_rigs` (limiting-model rows, slip invariance) and step `reflection`.
- [ ] Every run-verb call carries `--key`, and compared runs carry distinct keys.
- [ ] With `--mutants`, the four controls are caught (two rig controls and the slip control through the stages, the planted asymmetry in step 7); a control made ineffective fails the script.
- [ ] With `--holdout`, the holdout stage passes: registration precedes the held-out runs and the printed outcome matches the logged counts.
- [ ] Two runs give identical digests for the compared steps.
- [ ] The script writes no gate row, and its `summary` names GATE-ARENA-06 as touched.
- [ ] Cancelling during `development_set` leaves no partial store and no orphaned job.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`--list-steps` prints the stage and step names and a small test asserts the list. `--selftest` alters one
expected value of this script's own steps (the 14 verdict objects to 15) and must fail.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. Commands from the user's side: `frob task fixture`, `frob task check`, `frob task
settle`, `frob contact capacity`, and through the stages the commands of N24b-11's, N24b-12's and N24b-13's
scripts. Assertions and log content as above.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-616 (limiting model, 1% on the referee; controls: a pyramidal friction cone; a sole with one quadrature
point), KA-617 (reflected cases), KA-599 (slip invariance; control: unweighted point speeds). CX-268. Negative
controls: the three mutants (through the stages); the planted asymmetry of step 7; the self-test. Determinism
lane: twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- Runtime: the plate-drag rows are short; the development set is 14 episodes of 13 simulated seconds; the holdout with refinement is the long part and runs behind `--holdout`.
- The outcome of the holdout is not asserted beyond being well-formed; it is a finding, and a `fidelity-dependent` outcome is a legitimate one.
- If the referee cannot express a material boundary inside one footprint, the `model_rigs` stage reports the rig as not expressible and the gate cannot be recorded as passed.
- The development set and the holdout run on one adjudicator each, named in the log; the script has no tier option, because a tier other than the registered adjudicator would break the single-adjudicator rule of BD-202 and N24b-13.
