## Background

A task's terms carry the physics of time and of action limits: a running reward is a rate integrated over
simulated time, the discount is a rate per second, the likelihood of an action is computed for the random
variable that was sampled, and a requirement may shape training only in its past-time, unit-normalized form.
This script shows each of those from the user's side with the real `frob` binary on fixture tasks, at two
control rates, with the tempting wrong form beside the right one. It records the reward row of GATE-CORTEX-06
(the 500 against 1,000 reward fixture is invariant under rate terms) and the P-3 and P-7 rows of GATE-CORTEX-07
(the PPO battery: same random variable; physical discount).

## Scope

In: one script, its fixture tasks, assertions, negative controls and structured log. Out: the term compiler
and discount and action-law functions (BD-194); the environment's other rules (BD-192) and the rest of the PPO
battery (BD-197), which have their own scripts.

## Technical approach

**Path and invocation.** `scripts/e2e/cortex_task_terms.sh [--tier batch-cpu|cuda|metal] [--mutants]`. Plain
shell; non-zero exit on a failed assertion; idempotent (writes only under `target/e2e-logs/cortex_task_terms/`
and a scratch store); seeds printed.

**Fixture tasks** under `assets/fixtures/task-terms/` (labelled `synthetic`): `rate-penalty.ftask` (a
point-mass world that executes a prescribed 10 s motion; one reward term declared `rate` with value −100 per
second; a twin `step-penalty.ftask` with a per-step term of −1); `const-rate.ftask` (a constant reward rate
r = 1 per second with β = 0.5025 per second, long horizon); `clip-law.ftask` (one action channel with unit
variance clipped at +1, two policy means 0 and 1); `mixed-units.ftask` (a clearance atom with scale 0.10 m and
a thermal atom with scale 10 K; a second copy with the clearance written in millimetres); `future-online.ftask`
(an "always" window offered as an online reward).

**Steps.**

1. `check_tasks`: `frob task check` on each fixture task; assert admission for all but `future-online.ftask`.
2. `reward_rate`: `frob sim run` on the two variants of the rate task whose only difference is the control
   clock (periods 1/50 s and 1/100 s; identical physics clock and seed); assert the episode's reward totals are
   equal (−1,000 at both rates, within 10⁻¹² relative in `f64`). Then the per-step twins: assert totals −500
   and −1,000, and log it as the negative form.
3. `reward_substeps`: the rate task with 10 and with 20 physics sub-steps per interval; assert equal totals.
4. `event_once`: a task with one event term and a forced retry of the sub-step containing the event (a test
   hook of the transactional step); assert the event reward is counted once.
5. `discount`: on `const-rate.ftask` at 20 ms and 10 ms control intervals read the discounted return of the
   first state from the learner's evaluation of a fixed zero policy; assert 2.0001 (± 5 × 10⁻⁴) and 1.9951
   (± 5 × 10⁻⁴), against r/β = 1.990; then with a per-step γ = 0.99 override in a test profile assert 2.0 and
   1.0 and log it as the negative form.
6. `action_law`: evaluate the likelihood ratio at the applied action +1 between the two means under each
   registered law; assert 3.15149 (± 10⁻⁵) for `clipped-with-endpoint-mass` and 1.64872 (± 10⁻⁵) for
   `latent-then-limit`.
7. `action_law_mixture`: collect a rollout under one law and start learning under the other with `frob train
   start`; assert refusal `FROB-REF-527`, exit code 3, with the repairs "recollect" and "relabel under the
   registered law".
8. `requirement_terms`: `frob task verdict` on a recorded trace of `mixed-units.ftask` in metres and in
   millimetres; assert margins 0.5 and 1.2 and binding atom `clearance` in both. `frob task check` on
   `future-online.ftask`; assert refusal `FROB-REF-554`, exit code 3.
9. `curriculum_identity`: a curriculum file that edits a requirement scale is rejected as changing the
   `TaskId`; one that edits reward weights leaves the `TaskId` unchanged and changes the schedule identity.
10. `applied_action_logged`: assert telemetry of a run holds both the sampled and the applied action and the
    run manifest names the action law.
11. `negative_controls` (with `--mutants`): a build that integrates rate terms per control step (step 2 must
    fail); a build whose learner uses latent densities for clipped samples without refusing (step 7 must
    fail). Each recorded as caught.
12. `determinism`: steps 2 and 5 twice; assert identical totals and digests; with `--tier`, assert agreement
    with `batch-cpu` inside the tolerance class.

**Logging.** Per the shared helper of STD-E2E: newline-delimited JSON at
`target/e2e-logs/cortex_task_terms/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim,
class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), reward totals with units at each control rate, discount values,
likelihood ratios, margins and binding atoms, refusal codes with repairs, and on failure the first differing
value with the `.frun` path. The `summary` line gives assertion counts, controls caught and the gates touched
(GATE-CORTEX-06 reward row; GATE-CORTEX-07 rows P-3 and P-7).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, is executable and passes on a clean checkout at M2 on `batch-cpu`.
- [ ] The rate-reward totals are equal at 50 Hz and 100 Hz and the per-step totals are 500 and 1,000 in magnitude.
- [ ] The discount values 2.000 and 1.995 (unrounded 2.0001 and 1.9951) hold across control rates; the per-step override gives 2.0 and 1.0.
- [ ] The two action-law ratios are reproduced and the mixture is refused with FROB-REF-527.
- [ ] The mixed-unit fixture binds on the clearance in both unit systems; the future-time online reward is refused with FROB-REF-554.
- [ ] With `--mutants`, both planted defects are caught.
- [ ] The gate records for the named rows cite this script's log by path and digest.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`--list-steps` and its asserting test. `--selftest` changes the expected likelihood ratio to 3.2 and must fail.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. Commands from the user's side: `frob task check`, `frob sim run`, `frob task
verdict`, `frob train start`. Assertions and log content as above.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-579 (γ = 0.99 at 20 ms and 0.99499 at 10 ms; 2.000·r and 1.995·r, near 1.990·r), the reward-rate,
action-law and mixed-unit rows of the Cortex known answers. CX-248. MR-21 (mutation: a per-step penalty, 500
against 1,000). Negative controls: the per-step penalty; the per-step discount; the two mutant builds; the
self-test. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; on device
backends against `batch-cpu` inside class D3.

## Considerations

- The prescribed-motion world keeps the two control rates on identical physics, so the only difference is the control interval; a task whose motion depends on the control rate would confound the fixture.
- The discounted return is read over a horizon long enough that truncation changes it by less than 10⁻⁶ (about 30 s at β = 0.5025 per second), and the fixture states that horizon.
- If the default action law changes (OPEN-CORTEX-2), the script's law names follow the registry; both laws stay tested.
