## Background

A **task family** is a group of tasks that ask one kind of thing of a robot. Arena's rule is that every family
ships at least one task with a requirement, labelled example traces and a reference-robot baseline scorecard, so
that an agent given a new design can run it against something whose answer on a known robot is on file. Thirteen
families are planned across the milestones; three land at M1, all on the CPU, because M1 is a referee studio with
no GPU code:

| Family | Example tasks | Site at M1 |
|---|---|---|
| Stand and recover | Stand for 10 s; recover from a push of stated impulse | `referee` |
| Threshold delivery | A loaded cart crosses a door sill and docks on the run-out (fixture 1) | `referee` |
| Timed docking | Reach a pose with zero velocity by a deadline under a hidden plant gain (the exact mission slice; its boundary case is a known answer) | host, exact arithmetic |

The later families (velocity tracking and terrain traversal at M2, interiors and payload transport at M3, moving
surfaces at M4) reuse the pattern this bead establishes. The bead serves the M1 exit (a fresh agent can run a task
and get a verdict with evidence) and records the referee rows of the task-reproducibility gate GATE-ARENA-01. It
also registers the two tasks of the baseline-controller gate GATE-REFLEX-01 ("each archetype stands for 10 s and
traverses 5 m of flat ground under its baseline controller", M1 on the referee): `stand-10s` and
`traverse-5m-flat`, which the Reflex and Skeleton beads run by these names. DESIGN: the traverse task sits in the
stand-and-recover family because it is the M1 locomotion check of the baseline controllers and asks whether a
design moves, not how well it tracks a speed; commanded-velocity tracking is the velocity-tracking family's, M2.
Crate `frob-arena` (task files and fixtures under `assets/tasks/`); tier Solid.

Why these three: each exercises one thing the requirement language must get right. Standing exercises
always-clauses and hard limits; threshold delivery exercises the until-form with a dwell that cannot latch
(FACT: the hand-written scorer of an earlier planar pilot of this fixture had a latched dwell timer and no
chassis-strike guard; both defects are unrepresentable in the language); timed docking exercises exact
comparison at a boundary, where a non-strict comparator would admit a mission that cannot be completed.

## Scope

In: one `.ftask` per task below, each with requirement, signals, clocks, reset, horizon, at least two satisfied and
two violated example traces with one near the boundary of each comparator; the reference run and reference
scorecard of each; the reproducibility tests.
Out: the cart fixture itself, its analytic layer and creep rigs (BD-123); the development batch (N24b-07) and its
fixed-n rule with the pre-analysis (N24b-06); the docking family as a mission query over (U, ε) cells (BD-129); the docking
fixture on every backend (BD-190); baseline controllers (BD-116, BD-117, BD-118).

## Technical approach

Numbers marked DESIGN below are choices of this bead where the plan fixes only the task's intent; they are written
into the task file and hashed before the first run, after which they cannot move without a new `TaskId`.

**1. Stand and recover.** Bodies: the first archetypes that exist at M1 (`quad-mammal`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`,
`mecanum`), restricted to those whose contact law exists at M1 and whose contact count fits the referee's cap of
21 contacts. Controller: the baseline from `control.synth`. Clocks: physics as the referee's base step, requirement
clock 1/50 s. Signals: `tilt` (larger of |roll| and |pitch|, interval extremum, reduce max), `base_height_ratio`
(base height over its settled height, interval extremum, reduce min), `offsite_contacts` (count of contacts on
bodies other than feet, wheels or tracks, count), `base_speed` (state, reduce max).

- `stand-10s` (horizon 10 s): `(atom upright (>= (- 20 deg tilt) 0 deg) :scale 20 deg)`,
  `(atom height-ok (>= (- base_height_ratio 0.8) 0) :scale 0.2)`, `(atom no-strike (= offsite_contacts 0) :exact)`,
  `(verdict stands (G (0 s 10 s) (and upright height-ok no-strike)))`. The 10 s duration is the plan's; the 20°
  and 0.8 bounds are DESIGN.
- `recover-push` (horizon 10 s): a horizontal impulse J = m·Δv with Δv = 0.3 m/s (DESIGN), m the total mass,
  applied at the base centre of mass at t = 2 s, direction drawn uniformly from the stream `task/push_dir`
  keyed by the world address. Extra atoms `(atom tilt-ok (>= (- 45 deg tilt) 0 deg) :scale 45 deg)` and
  `(atom still (>= (- 0.05 m/s base_speed) 0 m/s) :scale 0.05 m/s)`; online
  `(online settled (H (0 s 2 s) (and upright still)))`;
  `(verdict recovered (U (0 s 10 s) (and tilt-ok no-strike) (and settled (H (0 s 2 s) after-push))))`, where
  `after-push` is the exact atom `(= push_done 1)` on a count signal that becomes 1 at the requirement tick of the
  push (t = 2 s) and stays 1. The inner `H` over `after-push` is required: with a bare `after-push` the until is met
  at t = 2 s by a body that stood still for the two seconds *before* the push, whatever it does afterwards (the
  pre-push loophole, caught by `recover_requires_after_push`). With it, the 2 s settled window lies wholly at or after
  the push tick, so the earliest success is t = 4 s and the body has until 10 s to recover. Reach 10 s (past
  operators add nothing).
- `traverse-5m-flat` (horizon 40 s): 5 m of net displacement on flat ground with no fall. World: a flat plane
  under the default interface card's friction, no hazard; the `randomization` section is empty (no randomized
  field), so the world address selects only the settled start pose from the 64 of the reset cache, as for
  `stand-10s`. Command: forward along the heading at reset, at the speed of the term `cmd_speed` (m/s), whose value
  in the registered task is `nominal`, the baseline controller's nominal speed for its class as its controller
  record states it, echoed in the run header (DESIGN: BET-06 asks whether the baseline moves a design; a variant
  that fixes the term, such as `traverse-5m-flat-0.3` and `-0.6` of the legged row's script, is a different task
  with its own `TaskId`). Extra signal `progress` (net displacement of the base frame's origin along the heading
  at reset since t = 0, state, metres, reduce last). Atoms `tilt-ok` (as in `recover-push`), `no-strike`,
  `(atom not-fallen (>= (- base_height_ratio 0.5) 0) :scale 0.5)` and
  `(atom reached (>= (- progress 5 m) 0 m) :scale 1 m)`;
  `(verdict traversed (U (0 s 40 s) (and tilt-ok not-fallen no-strike) reached))`. Reach 40 s. The 5 m is the
  gate's; the 45° and 0.5 bounds, the 1 m scale and the 40 s horizon (an average of 0.125 m/s suffices) are
  DESIGN. The verdict is decided at the first tick with 5 m covered, so the episode's cost follows the design's
  speed.

**2. Threshold delivery** (`cart-sill-dock`, horizon 8 s, physics 1/2000 s, requirement clock 1/100 s). The cart
is a front-wheel-drive four-wheel cart of 8.000 kg (design A, 60 mm front wheels) or 8.225 kg (design B, 75 mm)
crossing a 12 mm sill that spans x from 0 to 0.10 m, starting stationary with the front axle at x = −0.50 m,
target wheel-rate equivalent to 0.30 m/s. Docked means: the minimum world x of the whole loaded collision
envelope exceeds 0.10 m and roll and pitch stay within 5°, continuously for 0.5 s, with the dwell complete within
8 s; the dwell restarts whenever a condition lapses. Hard limits, each absorbing: roll or pitch beyond 35°; any
chassis contact with the sill; winding current beyond the 6 A rating by more than 0.01 A. Requirement:

```lisp
(requirements
  (atom cleared    (>  (- env_min_x 0.10 m) 0 m)                    :scale 0.10 m)
  (atom upright    (>= (- 5 deg tilt) 0 deg)                        :scale 5 deg)
  (atom tilt-ok    (>= (- 35 deg tilt) 0 deg)                       :scale 35 deg)
  (atom current-ok (>= (- 6.01 A i_max) 0 A)                        :scale 6 A)
  (atom no-strike  (=  strikes 0)                                   :exact)
  (atom saturated  (=  drive_saturated 1)                           :exact)
  (atom advancing  (>= (- x_front (delay 0.5 s x_front)) 0.002 m)   :scale 0.002 m)
  (online dwell    (H (0 s 0.5 s) (and cleared upright)))
  (online stalled  (and (H (0 s 0.5 s) saturated) (not advancing)))
  (verdict docked  (U (0 s 8 s) (and tilt-ok current-ok no-strike) dwell)))
```

The until-form says the hard limits hold at every tick before the dwell completes and that it completes by 8 s;
`H` over 0.5 s is false the tick any condition lapses, so the dwell cannot latch. A stall (command saturated and
less than 2 mm advance over 0.5 s) names the failure reason; the verdict still comes from the requirement at the
horizon. Reference robot: design A with the fixture's own controller (outer loop at 100 Hz, current command
2 A·s/rad times the rate error clipped to ±6 A). Reference condition: friction 0.8, centred payload, sharp lip,
zero heading.

**3. Timed docking** (`timed-docking`). Plant, exact: three ticks of one second from x₀ = v₀ = 0; during tick k the
acceleration is g·u_k with a hidden gain g ∈ {1, 2} fixed for the episode and |u_k| ≤ U; exact zero-order-hold
integration gives v₃ = g·(u₀ + u₁ + u₂) and x₃ = g·(5u₀/2 + 3u₁/2 + u₂/2). The mission is (x₃, v₃) = (1 m, 0)
exactly. The only informative packet is a reading of v₁ = g·u₀ with additive error in [−ε, +ε], delivered before
the second command. Commands, readings and ε are integers in units of 1/64, and positions and velocities are
integers in units of 1/128 (DESIGN: the zero-order hold halves a command once, so for commands k₀/64, k₁/64, k₂/64
the position x₃ = g·(5k₀ + 3k₁ + k₂)/128 and the velocity v₃ = g·2·(k₀ + k₁ + k₂)/128 are integers on the 1/128
grid for every command on the 1/64 grid; on a 1/64 grid of positions an odd sum would not be representable), so
every comparison is of the exact-integer class. Signals `x_err = x₃ − 1` and `v_err = v₃` (count kind, in 1/128
units, so the target is 128) and `reading_late`
(count kind: 1 when the reading was not delivered before the second command; it names the failure reason and is not
an atom of the requirement).
Requirement: `(atom at-pose (= x_err 0) :exact)`, `(atom at-rest (= v_err 0) :exact)`,
`(verdict docked (F (3 s 3 s) (and at-pose at-rest)))`, horizon 3 s, requirement clock 1 s. Worlds of the task:
the six combinations of g ∈ {1, 2} and reading error e ∈ {−ε, 0, +ε}, selected by the world address. Reference
controller (exact witness): probe p = u₀; decide g = 1 if the reading is below 3p/2 and g = 2 otherwise; then
u₁ = 1/g − 2p and u₂ = p − 1/g. Reference parameters U = 3/4, ε = 1/16, p = 1/4: commands (1/4, 1/2, −3/4) for
g = 1 and (1/4, 0, −1/4) for g = 2, both reaching (1, 0). The family's exact boundary is ε < (U + 1/2)/4 with
equality excluded; at U = 3/4 the probe p = 1/4 tolerates ε < 1/8 and p = 5/8 tolerates ε < 5/16. If the reading
arrives after the third command is committed, a causal controller issues one command vector in both gains, and the
final position of a common vector is proportional to g, so it cannot equal 1 m for both gains: the late-packet cell
is excluded for every controller. The reference controller's no-reading branch (DESIGN) commands as for g = 1, so its
three g = 2 worlds end at (x₃, v₃) = (2 m, 0) and are `violated` with reason `reading_late`.

**Labelled traces.** Per task: two satisfied (reference robot, nominal and one perturbed world) and two violated
(stand: a body released at 30° tilt; a design with an undersized actuator that sinks below 0.8 of its height.
Traverse: a controller commanding zero speed, which stands for 40 s with `progress` near 0; a trip with an
off-site contact at t = 6 s. Cart: a chassis strike; a timeout with the cart stalled at the lip. Docking:
ε = 5/16 with p = 5/8 and reading 15/16; a late packet). One trace per comparator within 0.1 of its boundary, as
the example rule requires.

**Reference scorecards.** `frob eval run` then `frob eval scorecard` for each task with its reference robot on the
referee (or the exact host plant for docking), 16 worlds each from run seed 17 (DESIGN), stored under
`assets/tasks/<task>/reference/` with the identities (`TaskId`, `ModelId`, `ControllerId`, tier build) and the
episode digests.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each task is reproducible on the referee from its `TaskId`, `ModelId` and seed: two runs give identical episode digests and verdict records; a changed seed changes the episode digest (the seed's done-condition; for timed docking the site is the exact host plant).
- [ ] Each task file passes `task.check`, including the example stage and the mutation check, with no vacuous hard-limit atom.
- [ ] Reference scorecards are on file for every task and reference robot, with outcome columns summing to the battery size.
- [ ] `stand-10s` is satisfied by each M1 archetype that the standing gate of the baseline controllers covers, and violated by the planted bodies.
- [ ] `traverse-5m-flat` is registered under that name, is satisfied on the referee by the same archetypes under their baseline controllers with `cmd_speed` echoed in the run header, and is violated by its two planted traces.
- [ ] Timed docking: the reference controller docks in all six worlds at U = 3/4, ε = 1/16; at ε = 5/16 with p = 5/8 the reading 15/16 is possible in both gains, which need u₁ = −1/4 and u₁ = −3/4, and the verdict is `violated` in at least one world; with the packet late no controller is satisfied in both gains, and the reference controller's no-reading branch is `violated` (x₃ = 2 m) in the three g = 2 worlds.
- [ ] Cart: the reference condition produces a verdict with binding atom and time; a planted chassis strike is `violated` with reason `strike` even if the dwell later completes.
- [ ] The referee rows of GATE-ARENA-01 are recorded with measured digests.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena families`:
- `m1_tasks_pass_check`: all five task files.
- `stand_requirement_on_synthetic_traces` (`synthetic`): tilt held at 19.9° satisfied; 20.1° at one tick violated with binding atom `upright`.
- `traverse_requirement_on_synthetic_traces` (`synthetic`): `progress` rising at 0.25 m/s with tilt 10° is
  satisfied, decided at t = 20 s; `progress` of 4.95 m at 40 s is violated with binding atom `reached` and margin
  −0.05; tilt of 46° at t = 3 s followed by 5 m at 20 s is violated with binding atom `tilt-ok`; an off-site
  contact at t = 6 s is violated with binding atom `no-strike`.
- `recover_requires_after_push`: a trace that is still for 2 s before the push and falls afterwards is violated; a
  trace that is still before the push, wobbles, and is upright and still from t = 5.0 s is satisfied, decided at
  t = 7.0 s. Control: the formula with a bare `after-push` (no inner `H`) is satisfied at t = 2 s on the falling
  trace and must be caught.
- `cart_dwell_does_not_latch`: cleared for 0.4 s, lapses, cleared again for 0.5 s; decided at the end of the second dwell.
- `cart_strike_is_absorbing`.
- `docking_witness_exact`: commands (1/4, 1/2, −3/4) and (1/4, 0, −1/4), that is (16, 32, −48) and (16, 0, −16)
  in 1/64 units, give (x₃, v₃) = (128, 0) in 1/128 units, which is (1 m, 0); the odd command vector (1, 0, 0)/64
  under g = 1 gives x₃ = 5 in 1/128 units, held exactly.
- `docking_boundary_5_16`: both worlds can read 15/16; required second commands differ by 1/2.
- `docking_late_packet_excluded`: for every common command vector on the 1/64 grid with |u_k| ≤ 3/4, x₃(g = 2) =
  2·x₃(g = 1), so no vector docks in both gains; the reference controller's no-reading branch gives (2 m, 0) in the
  g = 2 worlds, `violated` with reason `reading_late`.
- `seed_changes_digest`, `same_seed_same_digest` for each task.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/arena_m1_families.sh` (bead N24c-07): settle, run, verdict and scorecard for each task through the
`frob` binary; asserts the reproducibility and seed clauses and compares with the reference scorecards. Logs per
the shared helper: commands, exit codes, wall time, evidence block, digests, verdict counts, and on failure the
first differing episode with its `.frun` path.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-615 (boundary comparator at U = 3/4, ε = 5/16: the strict requirement fails; control: a non-strict comparator
admits the equality case). KA-499 and KA-500 (docking commands and the equality reading 15/16). The standing rows
of the baseline-controller gate (each M1 archetype stands for 10 s). Negative controls: a latched dwell timer
passes the lapsing-dwell trace; a scorer without the strike guard passes the strike trace; a docking plant in
binary floating point with a step that is not a dyadic rational misses (1, 0) by rounding and is rejected by the
exact atoms. Metamorphic: the mirrored push on a mirror-symmetric body gives the mirrored result. Determinism:
twice on one machine; debug and release; Linux and macOS (referee class D2; docking class D0).

## Considerations

- The seed text says "on the referee" for all three; timed docking is an equality mission that floating point cannot decide, so it runs as the exact scalar plant on the host and its site is recorded as such. A floating-point referee replica of the slider would need tolerance atoms and would be a different task.
- The DESIGN numbers of the standing tasks should be revisited against the first archetypes' measured behaviour before they are frozen; freezing happens when the task file is first registered.
- OPEN-ARENA-1 (which public task definitions to mirror) concerns the M2 velocity-tracking tasks, not these.
- The cart's result depends on rotor inertia and controller cadence (success fractions 0.093 and 0.780 at 100 Hz with zero rotor inertia against 1.000 and 1.000 at 1 kHz in an exploratory planar sweep); the task pins both (zero added rotor inertia, 100 Hz outer loop) so the reference scorecard is not a statement about loop stability left unpinned.
