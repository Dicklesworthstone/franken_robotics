## Background

The mission-level **verdict** is the unit that scorecards, studies, adversarial searches and attribution count. It
is produced by evaluating a task's verdict formula (a bounded-time temporal-logic formula over unit-normalised
atoms) on a completed episode trace. `task.verdict` is the verb that does this on a recorded run: given a run and a
task it returns a verdict object with schema `frob.verdict/1`, stores it with the episode in the run file `.frun`
and writes a summary to the ledger. Crates: `frob-spec` (verdict type and offline evaluator, from BD-121) and
`frob-arena` (the verb, the binding to runs). Tier: Solid. At M1 it runs on episodes recorded on the referee tier;
at M2 the same verb gains a batch form that evaluates traces recorded on `batch-cpu` and devices on the host
(N24cx-01); verdicts written by the batched tiers with streaming monitors arrive with the M3 bead for batched
requirement verdicts.

Design reasons carried here:

- **Past time online, future time offline (ADR-ARENA-04).** Future-time operators are evaluated on the completed
  trace and are the verdict; an online value of a future-time operator would be a silently truncated window.
- **The declared comparator decides at the boundary, in exact arithmetic (ADR-ARENA-05).** A strict and a
  non-strict inequality differ exactly at zero, and single precision misclassifies near-boundary cases in bulk:
  FACT (boundary-classifier runs recorded at plan time) on a dense 65,536-case screen, unguarded single-precision
  strict comparison gave 452 false admissions and 23,438 false exclusions; with a 2⁻²¹ guard band and an exact
  fallback there were no final errors.
- **A verdict is a pure function of the trace and the `TaskId` (INV-ARENA-01)** and never depends on a reward
  term; **no requirement value is computed from a truncated or incomplete window (INV-ARENA-02)**.

## Scope

In: the verb `task.verdict` (read; M1): its input schema (a task and a recorded run), resolution of the run's
Task-stream channels, the call of the offline evaluator, the output schema that carries the verdict record with
its coverage and boundary labels and decided-early tick, the refusals for unusable inputs (a run that does not
cover the reach, a missing channel, a run of another task), the envelope and exit codes.

Out: the record type `frob.verdict/1` itself, its four values, its storage, the purity test and the joint
reporting of stages (N24a-11, which governs wherever this bead restates them); the evaluators (BD-121); example
checking (N24a-12); the M2 batch form `--worlds all` on batched-tier and device runs, the `task-all` telemetry
selection and the decision record `frob.decisions/1` (N24cx-01, which builds on this verb's input schema and
algorithm unchanged); streaming monitors on devices and tier-written verdicts (BD-255); the failure
taxonomy that names `reason` (BD-094); scorecards (N24c-04).

## Technical approach

**Input** (`frob.task.verdict.in/1`): `run` (a run identifier or `.frun` path), `task` (`TaskId` or name;
default: the `TaskId` the run recorded; required for a trace that records none), optional `requirement` (the
verdict's identifier; default: the task's verdict), optional `worlds` (addresses; default all episodes in the
run), `re_evaluate` (`--re-evaluate`: evaluate a task other than the one the run recorded). The verb has no
evaluator selector: it answers with the offline two-pass evaluator; the agreement of the three evaluators and of
the online kernels with it is a Gauntlet check (GATE-ARENA-03, KA-596), run by `cargo test`, not an option here.

**Algorithm.**
1. Resolve identifiers; refuse if the run's recorded `TaskId` differs from the requested task unless
   `--re-evaluate` is given (a different task may be evaluated on the same trace when every signal it reads was
   recorded; a missing signal refuses, it is never synthesised). A trace that records no `TaskId` (a labelled
   example trace of N24a-12, or a `.frun` whose header has `trace_kind: synthetic` and no `task`, such as the hand
   cases of the task audit, N24c-22 and N24c-27) is evaluated against the named task without `--re-evaluate`,
   under the same signal rule; `task` is then required (FROB-REF-802, pointer `/task`, without it). Its record
   carries the trace's `synthetic` label, is returned and summarised in the ledger by `trace_digest`, and is not
   written into the trace file, which is content-addressed by that digest (DESIGN: such a trace belongs to no run
   and to no task, so there is no recorded task for `--re-evaluate` to override). Before reading any episode, a
   requested verdict whose reach exceeds the run's declared episode horizon is refused with FROB-REF-526 (no
   episode of the run can cover its windows); an episode cut short of the horizon is step 5's `undetermined`.
   An episode assembled from separately recorded episodes (its header lists more than one source episode, or its
   trace has a reset event or a restart of simulated time after its first tick) is refused with
   `stage-concatenation`.
2. For each episode, read the signal columns the requirement names, on their own clocks, from the committed
   telemetry. Apply each signal's declared reduction (`last`, `min`, `max`) over each requirement tick.
3. Evaluate atoms: normalised margin ρ = e/s for an atom with expression e and scale s; an `:exact` atom has
   margin +1 or −1. An atom whose margin lies inside the guard band |ρ| < 2⁻²¹ (TARGET) is flagged `boundary` and
   re-evaluated on the CPU with its declared comparator, in exact rational or integer arithmetic where its inputs
   are of the exact-integer class and in double precision otherwise.
4. Evaluate the verdict formula with the offline two-pass evaluator. Margins compose as: negation −ρ; conjunction
   and disjunction, minimum and maximum; G and F over [a, b], minimum and maximum of ρ(φ, t′) for t′ ∈ [t + a,
   t + b]; φ U over [a, b] ψ, the maximum over t′ ∈ [t + a, t + b] of min(ρ(ψ, t′), min of ρ(φ, t″) for t″ ∈
   [t, t′)); H and O over [a, b], minimum and maximum over [t − b, t − a]; φ S over [a, b] ψ, the maximum over
   t′ ∈ [t − b, t − a] of min(ρ(ψ, t′), min of ρ(φ, t″) for t″ ∈ (t′, t]). ρ > 0 implies the formula holds,
   ρ < 0 that it fails, ρ = 0 decides nothing and the comparator rule of step 3 applies.
5. **Coverage.** If any window the formula needs is not covered by the trace (truncated episode, missing samples,
   dropped telemetry) the verdict is `undetermined` with the reason and the count of missing samples; no value is
   computed from the part that exists.
6. **Decided early.** If a prefix fixes the truth (an always-clause violated, an until-clause met), report
   `decided_at_s` as the tick that fixed it. This is how an environment ends an episode on success or on a hard
   violation without truncating a window.
7. **Numerical faults.** A NaN in any atom, or an episode the tier marked `Faulted`, gives `invalid`: a study
   error, excluded from policy updates and retained in every benchmark and failure count.
8. Write the record into the episode's `.frun` (Task stream) and a summary row to the ledger; return the records.

**The verdict object.**

```json
{ "schema": "frob.verdict/1", "task": "t:cart-sill-dock@6be1…", "requirement": "docked",
  "world": {"run_seed": 17, "world": 41, "episode": 0}, "model": "m:9f2c…", "controller": "c:5e02…",
  "verdict": "violated", "margin": -0.31, "binding_atom": "cleared", "binding_time_s": "8",
  "decided_at_s": "8", "reason": "stuck", "coverage": {"kind": "interval", "missing_samples": 0},
  "boundary": {"flagged": false, "guard": "2^-21"}, "atoms_min": {"cleared": -0.31, "upright": 0.84},
  "evidence": {"class": "Tested", "tier": "referee", "determinism": "D2", "standing": "provisional"},
  "authorization": {"execution_authorized": false, "hardware_safety_signoff": false,
                       "sim_to_real_certified": false},
  "trace_digest": "b3:77a0…" }
```

`verdict` ∈ {`satisfied`, `violated`, `undetermined`, `invalid`}. `binding_atom` is the atom whose margin equals
the formula's margin, with the time at which it bound; it is what an agent acts on. `coverage.kind` is `sampled`
when a deciding atom reads a state-kind channel (the verdict says nothing about the time between samples) and
`interval` when the deciding atoms read interval-extremum channels. `atoms_min` gives each atom's minimum margin
over the episode. `reason` is a failure class from the failure taxonomy when the verdict is `violated`. Times are
exact decimal strings of simulated seconds. The three authorization flags are constants, all false.

**Stages.** A mission with several stages is one requirement over one continuous episode, the stages chained by
until-clauses. The verb refuses to combine verdicts of separately recorded stage episodes into a mission verdict,
because the end state of one need not lie in the entry set of the next (CX-269). Stage pass rates are reported
jointly per world, never as a product.

**The CX-269 fixture** (`synthetic`, exact rationals; the plan states the counterexample in words only, and this
bead supplies its case). `crates/frob-arena/tests/fixtures/cx269/`: a sled on a line with acceleration bounded by
1/2 m/s² and the mission task `cx269-mission.ftask`: reach the gate x ≥ 1 m, then stop in the bay x ∈ [1.4 m,
1.6 m], as one requirement over one episode, `(verdict delivered (F (0 s 8 s) (and in-bay stopped (O (0 s 8 s)
at-gate))))` with `at-gate` (scale 1 m), `in-bay` (|x − 1.5 m| ≤ 0.1 m, scale 0.1 m) and `stopped` (|v| ≤ 0.01 m/s,
scale 0.01 m/s); the task file carries the labelled examples that static admission requires (N24a-12's rule: at
least two of each label, one near the boundary of each comparator, no vacuous hard-limit atom), as sled traces in
exact rationals, so that it passes `task.new` and is resolvable by name. `stage-a.frun`: from rest at x = 0 at the bound, x = t²/4, at the gate at t = 2 s with v = 1 m/s
(stage A satisfied). `stage-b.frun`: stage B's own reset, at rest at the gate, then bang-bang over 1 s each way,
stopped at x = 3/2 m at its t = 2 s (stage B satisfied). `stitched.frun`: the two joined and held at rest to 8 s,
its header listing both source episodes. `continuous.frun`: one 8 s episode, stage A followed by braking at the
bound from v = 1 m/s, which stops at x = 2 m at t = 4 s, 0.4 m past the bay's far edge: `violated`. The end state of A (x = 1 m, v = 1 m/s) is outside
B's entry set (at rest at x = 1 m), and no admissible command stops the sled in the bay from it, so the product
of the two stage verdicts (`satisfied`) is wrong and the one-episode verdict (`violated`) is right. This bead
records the fixture's path in the `case_supplied_by` field of CX-269's entry in
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/counterexamples.toml` and sets its `numeric = true` (N24-CX's entry keeps
`plan_case = "words"`), so that CX-269's control is runnable and is no longer an obligation.

**Plants** (N24-CX's registration table names this bead as the planting bead of three entries; each mutant is
built in the real component and enabled only under `gauntlet-mutations`). `N24c-02:cx-259` (mutation, Cargo
feature `cx-259` of `frob-arena`): step 3 composes the raw expression values with min and max and divides by the
scale afterwards, so the binding atom of the 0.02 m and 3 K case changes with the unit of the clearance.
`N24c-02:cx-260` (mutation, feature `cx-260`): the stage report prints the product of the stage pass rates in
place of the joint count. `N24c-02:cx-269` (planted input, the `synthetic` fixture above): `stitched.frun` offered
for the mission verdict. The battery rows of N24c-10 run the first two through KA-594 and KA-595.

**Refusals.** Each with exit code 3, a `pointer` and ranked repairs.

| Refusal | Trigger | Ranked repairs |
|---|---|---|
| `FROB-REF-888` (a metric over missing samples, owned by Chronicle) | A signal the requirement reads was not recorded in the run; it is never estimated or synthesised | Re-run with the channel recorded; evaluate a requirement whose signals were recorded |
| `FROB-REF-526` (the episode horizon is shorter than the reach of the verdict) | The requested verdict's reach exceeds the run's declared episode horizon (a task evaluated with `--re-evaluate`, or a trace with no recorded task) | Re-run under the requested task, whose horizon covers its reach; evaluate a requirement whose reach fits the run |
| `verdict-task-mismatch` (symbolic, DESIGN; registered in N24-REF, raised by its slug until the allocation ledger of N35-33 numbers it) | The run's recorded `TaskId` differs from the requested task and `--re-evaluate` is absent | Name the run's own task; pass `--re-evaluate` (every signal must have been recorded) |
| `stage-concatenation` (symbolic, DESIGN; registered in N24-REF) | An episode offered for a verdict is assembled from separately recorded episodes: its header lists more than one source episode, or its trace has a reset event or a restart of simulated time after its first tick (CX-269) | Write the mission as one requirement over one episode with the stages chained by until-clauses |

A task named by `TaskId` or name has passed static admission when it was stored (BD-122), so the admission
codes 520 to 539 are not raised by this verb.

Exit code 3 for refusals; 0 when every episode is `satisfied`; 2 when any is `violated`, `undetermined` or
`invalid`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On four synthetic traces of the cart requirement (labelled `synthetic`, built by this bead's tests from the requirement text: a clean docking and a docking after a dwell restart, satisfied; a stuck front lip with `env_min_x` ending at 0.069 m, violated with margin −0.31 and binding atom `cleared`; a chassis strike, violated at the strike tick) the verb returns the four labels, with binding atom and time, and identical bytes on two runs and on Linux and macOS. The fixture's full set of ten labelled examples belongs to the cart fixture (N24b-05) and is checked end to end by N24c-06.
- [ ] INV-ARENA-01: changing every reward term of the task's `terms` section (a different `TaskId` for training, same requirement) leaves verdict, margin and binding atom unchanged for the same trace.
- [ ] INV-ARENA-02: a trace cut at 6 s under the 8 s until yields `undetermined` with reason and missing-sample count, never a verdict from the covered part.
- [ ] A trace with one atom exactly on its boundary carries `boundary.flagged: true` and is decided by the declared comparator: strict fails, non-strict holds.
- [ ] An always-clause violated at tick k reports `decided_at_s` = k·T.
- [ ] A NaN sample yields `invalid`; the record is stored and counted.
- [ ] A run of another task is refused (`verdict-task-mismatch`) without `--re-evaluate` and evaluated with it when every signal was recorded; with one signal missing it is refused with FROB-REF-888; a requested verdict whose reach exceeds the run's declared horizon is refused with FROB-REF-526; the stitched trace of the CX-269 fixture is refused (`stage-concatenation`) and its continuous trace is `violated`. Each refusal carries its first repair as `next_actions[0]`.
- [ ] A `synthetic` trace that records no `TaskId` is evaluated against `--task` without `--re-evaluate`, its record labelled `synthetic` and the trace file unchanged; the same trace without `--task` is FROB-REF-802; a run called without `--task` is evaluated against the `TaskId` it recorded.
- [ ] CX-269's entry in `counterexamples.toml` names the fixture in `case_supplied_by` with `numeric = true`, and after N38b-05's seeding its control is runnable, not an obligation; the plants `cx-259`, `cx-260` and `cx-269` are each caught under `gauntlet-mutations` (the binding atom changes with the unit; the stage report prints 0.9025; the stitched trace is answered with a verdict).
- [ ] The record is stored in the `.frun` and summarised in the ledger; `frob task verdict --json` returns the envelope with the claim product; `CONTRACT.md` updated.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena verdict`:
- `verdict_pure_in_trace_and_task`: same trace, two task files differing only in reward terms; identical verdict bytes apart from `task`.
- `undetermined_on_short_trace`; `undetermined_on_missing_samples` (one dropped chunk inside a window).
- `boundary_strict_vs_nonstrict`: expression value exactly 0 with `>` gives violated, with `>=` satisfied, both flagged.
- `docking_boundary_exact`: the scalar docking case with command limit U = 3/4, read error ε = 5/16, probe 5/8: both hidden-gain worlds can read 15/16; the atom `(> separation 0)` with separation = 5/8 − 2·(5/16) = 0 is violated.
- `decided_early_always`, `decided_early_until`.
- `binding_atom_unit_invariant`: clearance margin 0.02 m (scale 0.10 m) and thermal margin 3 K (scale 10 K): margins 0.2 and 0.3, binding atom `clearance`, also with the trace in millimetres.
- `joint_stage_counts`: 20 worlds, two stages each passing in 19: the joint count is 18 when the failing worlds differ and 19 when they coincide; the verb never emits 18.05.
- `invalid_on_nan_is_counted`.
- `stage_concatenation_refused`: on the CX-269 fixture, `stitched.frun` against `cx269-mission` is refused
  `stage-concatenation` (pointer at the second source episode); `continuous.frun` is `violated` with final
  position 2 m, exact; `stage-a.frun` ends at (x, v) = (1 m, 1 m/s) and `stage-b.frun` starts at (1 m, 0).
- `task_mismatch_needs_re_evaluate`: the cart run evaluated against the kitchen-tray task refuses without the flag;
  the cart run against a second cart task that differs only in the `upright` bound (4° in place of 5°) is evaluated
  with `--re-evaluate`; a task reading an unrecorded channel refuses with FROB-REF-888.
- `reach_beyond_horizon_is_526`: the 8 s cart run with `--re-evaluate` against a cart task whose until window is
  (0 s 10 s) refuses with FROB-REF-526 before any episode is read.
- `synthetic_trace_needs_task_not_re_evaluate`: the CX-269 `continuous.frun` (no recorded `TaskId`) is evaluated
  with `--task cx269-mission` and no `--re-evaluate`; without `--task` it is FROB-REF-802 at `/task`; the trace
  file's digest is unchanged after the call. `default_task_is_recorded`: the cart run with no `--task` gives the
  same record as with `--task cart`.
- `plants_caught` (under `gauntlet-mutations`): with `cx-259` the binding atom of the 0.02 m and 3 K case is
  `thermal` when the clearance is written in millimetres, and `binding_atom_unit_invariant` fails; with `cx-260`
  `joint_stage_counts` fails on the printed 18.05; with the `stage-concatenation` check removed the stitched
  trace returns a verdict and `stage_concatenation_refused` fails.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead's own script, `scripts/e2e/arena_task_verdict.sh` (header `bead=N24c-02 milestone=M1`; convention 11),
drives the real `frob` binary on this bead's `synthetic` CX-269 fixture only: (1) `frob task new
crates/frob-arena/tests/fixtures/cx269/cx269-mission.ftask --name cx269-mission --key tv-new --json`; (2) `frob task
verdict crates/frob-arena/tests/fixtures/cx269/continuous.frun --task cx269-mission --json`: exit 2, `violated`,
the record labelled `synthetic`, the trace file's digest unchanged; (3) the same on `stitched.frun`: exit 3,
`stage-concatenation`, `next_actions[0]` the first repair; (4) `continuous.frun` without `--task`:

The steps on a recorded referee run (`verdict_examples`, `verdict_short_trace`, `verdict_boundary`,
`verdict_task_mismatch`, `verdict_missing_channel`) are in `scripts/e2e/arena_task_file.sh` (N24c-06, which
depends on this bead). Logged per the shared helper: commands, exit codes, wall time, the `evidence` block, the
verdict, margin, binding atom and time against expectations, the trace digest, refusal events with code expected
and observed, and on failure the first differing field with the `.frun` path; the `summary` line gives assertions
passed and failed.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-594 (normalisation: 0.2 and 0.3, clearance binds in both unit systems; control: composition of raw margins),
KA-595 (joint stage accounting 18 or 19 of 20, never 0.95² = 0.9025; control: multiplying stage rates), KA-596
(online and offline agreement; control: a monitor that pads a short window), KA-615 (boundary comparator at
U = 3/4, ε = 5/16; control: a non-strict comparator admits the equality case). Counterexamples CX-259, CX-260,
CX-269, with the plants `N24c-02:cx-259`, `:cx-260` and `:cx-269` above (CX-269's case is this bead's fixture:
stage verdicts satisfied and satisfied, the one-episode verdict violated with the sled stopped 0.4 m past the
bay). Metamorphic: unit rescaling leaves margin and binding atom unchanged; G over [0, T] evaluated at 0 equals
H over [0, T] evaluated at T. Determinism: a verdict at a boundary requires class D0 arithmetic for the deciding
atom; the record's determinism field is the weakest class among the trace and the evaluator.

## Considerations

- A verdict from a fast tier is `provisional` until promoted by the trust ladder; at M1 every verdict is from the referee and still carries its standing field.
- A `sampled` verdict is a statement about sample instants. A requirement about clearance should read an interval-extremum channel; the report tells the author which label the verdict earned.
- The guard band value per backend is OPEN-ARENA-5 (default 2⁻²¹ with CPU re-evaluation of every flagged atom); the experiment bead N24c-15 settles it for devices.
- Requirements about what the robot knew read delivered packets with their timestamps; requirements about what happened read simulation state at committed physics ticks. They are different signals with different names, and the verb never substitutes one for the other.
- Verdict records never contain raw samples, so a verdict on a private scene leaks no scene data.
