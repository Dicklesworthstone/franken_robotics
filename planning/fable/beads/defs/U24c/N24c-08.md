## Background

The Arena chapter states five invariants about tasks, verdicts, scorecards, fixtures and studies, and four proof
obligations about the monitors, the level series, the pre-analysis and the margin. The mechanisms they constrain
are built by several beads (the requirement-language core, the task file, the fixture, the study types, the
verdict and scorecard verbs). This bead is the single place where each identifier is carried as an executable
property test with a planted defect it must catch, so that none of them can be closed by assertion. It builds no
mechanism. Tier: Solid; crates `frob-spec` and `frob-arena` (`tests/` directories); test tier G0 (laws) and G5
(determinism) of the verification programme. The four qualification invariants (INV-QUAL-01 to -04) need the
blinded lane and are tested at M3 by N24c-09.

The rule being applied: for every law, invariant and proof obligation a property test, and for every gate at
least one negative control, because a gate that nothing can fail validates nothing.

## Scope

In: one property test (or differential test) per identifier below, each with its negative control registered in
the negative-control registry of the unit-test conventions; the exact-rational instance check behind PO-ARENA-02.
Out: the mechanisms; the 10⁶-trace gate run of the monitors (the requirement-language core bead measures
GATE-ARENA-03's monitor rows; this bead supplies the property-test form that runs on every change at 10⁴ traces
per operator); the general theorem behind PO-ARENA-02 (theorem card T-26, by induction on n).

## Technical approach

Each row gives the statement, the test, and the planted defect (a build-time feature flag or a test-only mutant)
that must make the test fail.

|---|---|---|---|

| INV-ARENA-03 | Every scenario of a battery appears in exactly one outcome column, and the columns sum to the battery size | 100 random batteries with planted refusals, numerical faults, short traces and (synthetic) infeasibility witnesses: each scenario index appears once; the sum equals the size; both denominators are consistent | A scorecard that drops `invalid` scenarios |
| INV-ARENA-04 | A fixture's constants, seeds, input table and rule are hashed into the ledger before its first run | For fixture 1: the ledger sequence number of the fixture-hash record is lower than that of the first episode of either design; a run offered as a fixture run whose recomputed hash differs is refused with FROB-REF-530; changing one constant (friction range, a mass, the rule's margin) changes the hash | A fixture runner that writes the hash after the first run; an edited constant under the old fixture identity |
| INV-ARENA-05 | A study has one type for its whole life | A study created `fixed-n` cannot be reported, stopped or extended as `anytime` and conversely; every such request is refused with FROB-REF-552; the type field is immutable in the ledger | A study record whose type is rewritten at report time |

**Notes on the mechanisms under test.**

- The **online kernel** for a sliding minimum or maximum over a window of W ticks uses a block decomposition
  that keeps a prefix and a suffix extremum per block of W samples: fixed memory of 2·W values per operator node
  and world, at most three comparisons per sample, no data-dependent control flow. The referee uses the
  monotonic-deque formulation of the same function. The **definitional evaluator** computes each window's extremum
  directly from the table of margins. Minimum, maximum and negation are exact in floating point, so all three
  return identical bits for identical atom values, after negative zero is canonicalised; a NaN marks the world
  `Faulted` and is excluded from the differential.
- The **pre-analysis** classifies every count pair (a, b) with a + b ≤ n, where b is the number of pairs in which
  only the candidate succeeds and a those in which only the baseline does. It forms two-sided 97.5% exact
  Clopper–Pearson intervals [L_b, U_b] and [L_a, U_a] for the two discordance probabilities; [L_b − U_a,
  U_b − L_a] covers their difference with probability at least 95%. Prefer the candidate when L_b − U_a > +0.05;
  inferior when U_b − L_a < −0.05; practical tie when the interval lies inside [−0.05, +0.05]; otherwise
  inconclusive. Expected counts: n = 64: 2,145 pairs, 521 / 521 / 0 / 1,103; n = 86: 3,828, 1,031 / 1,031 / 1 /
  1,765; n = 128: 8,385, 2,517 / 2,517 / 4 / 3,347; n = 256: 33,153, 11,384 / 11,384 / 39 / 10,346.
- The **level series**: request i of a qualification campaign with total budget α is tested at
  α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)]; since 1/[i·(i + 1)] = 1/i − 1/(i + 1) the levels telescope to α·m/(m + 1) after m requests.

- CX-266 (look at a fixed-n test after every pair and stop when it rejects: an exact one-sided sign test at

**Layout.** `crates/frob-spec/tests/laws.rs` (INV-ARENA-01, -02, PO-ARENA-01, -04);
`crates/frob-arena/tests/invariants.rs` (INV-ARENA-03, -04, -05, PO-ARENA-02, -03). Random cases come from a
seeded generator; the seed is printed on failure and the failing case is minimised and saved.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Nine property or differential tests exist, one per identifier, each named after its identifier, and pass.
- [ ] Each planted defect, enabled one at a time, makes its test fail; the ten controls (one per identifier, two for INV-ARENA-04) are entries of the negative-control registry with the test that catches them.
- [ ] PO-ARENA-01 runs at 10⁴ traces per operator on every change and its three evaluators agree bit for bit; the 10⁶-trace run is the gate's.
- [ ] PO-ARENA-03 reproduces the four rows of counts exactly and the perfect-concordance interval at n = 64 of ±(1 − 0.0125^(1/64)) = ±0.0662.
- [ ] PO-ARENA-02 holds in exact rationals for m = 1 … 500.
- [ ] Failures print the seed and a minimised case.
- [ ] Under `gauntlet-mutations` the plants `cx-265` and `cx-266` are each caught by their named tests (the tie at n = 64 admitted; a stop of a fixed-n study accepted), and with the plants off the tests pass; CX-265 and CX-266 are cited with their numbers.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The nine tests of the table: `inv_arena_01_verdict_pure`, `inv_arena_02_no_truncated_window`,
`inv_arena_03_columns_partition`, `inv_arena_04_fixture_hashed_first`, `inv_arena_05_study_type_fixed`,
`po_arena_01_three_evaluators_agree`, `po_arena_02_level_series_exact`, `po_arena_03_preanalysis_exact`,
`po_arena_04_margin_sign_law`; and ten `control_*` tests (INV-ARENA-04 has two), each compiled with its mutant
under `--features gauntlet-mutations`, asserting that the corresponding test fails. Further fixed cases: `tie_first_at_86` (the only tie pair at n = 86 is (0, 0), with
interval ±0.0497); `prefer_needs_15` (with no baseline-only pair a preference needs at least 15 candidate-only
pairs of 64, and 17, 19, 21, 23 with one to four baseline-only pairs); `cx_265_tie_at_64_refused` (a fixed-n plan
at n = 64 declaring `practical-tie` is refused with FROB-REF-532, the reachable set {prefer, inferior,
inconclusive} and the first n at which a tie is reachable, 86, attached; at n = 86 it is admitted).

### End-to-end script and logging
No script of its own. The invariants are exercised from the user's side by `scripts/e2e/arena_task_file.sh`
(INV-ARENA-01, -02 through `task.verdict`) and `scripts/e2e/arena_m1_families.sh` (INV-ARENA-03 through
`eval.scorecard`); those logs record digests and column sums. Test output is captured in the per-bead log with the
seed, the count of cases and the first failing case when one exists.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-596 (online and offline agreement; control: a monitor that pads a short window), KA-597 (three evaluators,
bit for bit), KA-600 (scorecard accounting), KA-612 (rule enumeration; control: a request for a tie verdict at
n = 64 is refused), KA-618 (peeking: an exact one-sided sign test at nominal 5% looked at after every pair
rejects falsely with chance 13/256 = 5.08% after 8 looks, 8.76% after 16, 12.9% after 32, 17.4% after 64;
control: the same data under a confidence sequence stay below their level), KA-619 (level series; control: a
constant level per request). Metamorphic relations: MR-14 (an anytime-valid study's level holds at every stopping
time; a fixed-n study's verdict is computed once), MR-19 (unit normalisation), MR-20 (streaming equals offline).
Determinism lanes: the differential test runs twice on one machine, with one worker and all workers, in debug
and release, and on Linux and macOS; all outputs byte-identical (class D0).

## Considerations

- A property test proves nothing beyond its generator's reach; the generator covers nesting depth to 4, windows to the online capacity of 4,096 ticks and clock ratios including 1/30 s against 1/500 s.
- The independent enumeration must share no code with the enumerator, or the comparison is a self-check; it is written against the statement of the rule only. It is N24b-06's `frob-conformance` lane; a second independent implementation here would add maintenance and no evidence.
- INV-ARENA-05's refusal (FROB-REF-552) and the immutable type field are N24b-14's mechanism (hence the dependency); BD-124 supplies the two engines.
- If a proof checker later accepts theorem card T-26, PO-ARENA-02 becomes class Proved for the general statement and the rational test remains as the instance check.
