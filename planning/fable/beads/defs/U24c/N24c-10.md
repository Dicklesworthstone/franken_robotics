## Background

A known answer is an analytic or synthetic truth the implementation must recover, with a derivation on file. The
Arena chapter lists eleven of them, each paired with a **negative control**: a mutation or planted failure that
must make the row fail. They are the content of the `fixtures` battery, which runs on every change that touches
contact, actuators, clocks or the requirement language. The rule of the verification programme for a row:

1. a derivation on file under `docs/derivations/<fixture>.md` (model, derivation, value with its digits, error
   budget of the test);
2. a tolerance with a reason (`exact` for integers, rationals, Booleans, verdicts and digests; otherwise the
   tolerance of the owning gate);
3. a negative control that is a mutation, not a boundary behaviour;
4. a tier and a site: a row runs first on the weakest site that can decide it, then on every tier its gate names;
5. values are not improved in transit: the registry carries the printed value.

The mechanisms that compute these answers are built by other beads (the fixture, the study types, the
requirement-language core, the verdict and scorecard verbs). This bead makes the eleven rows executable in one
place, registers each control, writes the derivation files that no fixture owns, and makes the battery report
every row against its known answer. A value in the Arena chapter that follows from a stated formula is a derived
value and is recomputed by its test, never copied. Crates `frob-conformance` (registry rows) and `frob-arena`
(the test bodies); tier Solid; claim kind Known answer.

## Scope

In: registry rows and controls for the eleven answers; derivation files for rows 3 and 6 to 11; the wiring that
makes `frob task fixture` and the `fixtures` battery run them; the M1 rows executed, the two M2 rows registered as
`not run` with their reason.
Out: building the fixtures and mechanisms; the chapter-wide known-answer registry bead (which carries all thirty
Appendix M rows KA-594 to KA-623 with tolerance and tier); the late known answers of the traction slice
(N24c-19), the item bank (N24c-32) and the task audit (N24c-28).

**Row 4 values.** Capacity per unit normal load c(z, w) = 0.525 + 0.55·clip(z/w, −½, ½) and yaw moment
0.275·N·(w/4 − z²/w): at z = 0, 0.525 for both soles and 2.75 mm and 4.81 mm per newton; at z = +12 mm, 0.690

**Registration.** Each row becomes an entry of the known-answer registry (identifier, case, truth, tolerance,
tier, site, derivation file, the test that runs it, the control and the test that proves the control fails) and
its control becomes an entry of the negative-control registry. Rows 4 and 5 are registered with status `not run`
and the reason "fixture 2 lands at M2"; they flip to executable when that bead closes. A row whose control is not
registered cannot support a gate.

**Derivation files written here.** `docs/derivations/arena-energy-accounting.md`, `arena-normalization.md`,
`arena-boundary-comparator.md`, `arena-online-offline.md`, `arena-peeking.md` (the exact enumeration of sign-test
paths that gives 13/256), `arena-joint-stages.md`, `arena-level-series.md`. The cart's derivations are under
`docs/derivations/fixture-cart-sill-dock/` and belong to the fixture.

**Battery wiring.** `frob task fixture --battery fixtures --json` reports every registered row against its known
answer with the measured value, tolerance, verdict and the status of its control.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Nine rows (1 to 3 and 6 to 11) execute at M1 and pass within tolerance; rows 4 and 5 are registered `not run` with their reason.
- [ ] Each of the nine controls, enabled one at a time, makes its row fail; a row with its control disabled is reported as unable to support a gate.
- [ ] Every row has a derivation file with model, derivation, digits and error budget.
- [ ] `frob task fixture --battery fixtures --json` lists every row with measured value against expected, and exits 2 when any row fails.
- [ ] The peeking row reproduces 13/256 exactly by enumeration, and 8.76%, 12.9% and 17.4% to the printed digits.
- [ ] Printed values are carried unchanged; any recomputation that disagrees is reported as a discrepancy and does not overwrite the registry.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena known_answers`: one test per row, named `ka_arena_<nn>_<slug>`, and one `control_`
test per row. Examples with values: `ka_arena_03_energy` (10, 12, 30, 12 J); `ka_arena_06_normalization`
(0.2, 0.3; binding atom `clearance` in both unit systems); `ka_arena_09_peeking` (exact rational 13/256);
`ka_arena_10_joint_stages` (18 when the failing worlds differ, 19 when they coincide); `ka_arena_11_levels`
(m = 1 … 500); `ka_arena_02_enumeration` (521, 521, 0, 1,103; tie first at 86; 15 pairs);
`ka_arena_02_equivalence_bound` (zero discordant pairs in 64 give an exact one-sided 95% bound
1 − 0.05^(1/64) = 0.0457); `ka_arena_02_power` (P(prefer) at discordance (0.17, 0), (0.25, 0.02), (0.40, 0.02):
0.117, 0.406, 0.962 at n = 64).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/arena_fixtures_battery.sh` runs `frob task fixture --battery fixtures --json` and, for each row,
re-runs it with the row's control enabled through the conformance harness's mutation switch. It sources the
shared helper and logs per row:
command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, measured value with unit, expected value, tolerance, pass or
fail, control caught or not, and on failure the first differing digit with the derivation file's path. The
summary names GATE-ARENA-04 and GATE-ARENA-05 as touched (their closing measurement belongs to the fixture and
study beads).

### Known answers and negative controls
The rows and controls of the table are the content. Counterexamples behind them: CX-259 (raw margins), CX-260
(multiplying stage rates), CX-261 (energy per completed trial only), CX-262 (explicit Euler on current then
wheel), CX-265 (a fixed-n study can return any of its verdicts), CX-266 (peeking), CX-267 (resubmission).
Metamorphic relations: MR-19, MR-20, MR-27, MR-28. Determinism lanes: exact rows byte-identical twice, across
worker counts, debug and release, Linux and macOS; referee rows inside the referee's class.

## Considerations

- Rows of the wider registry printed with tolerance `to set` or control `none stated` are not eligible to support a gate until fixed (open questions on tolerances and controls of the verification programme); among these eleven every row has both.
- Row 7's device half waits for the guard-band experiment (OPEN-ARENA-5, bead N24c-15).
- The battery must stay fast (minutes) because it runs on every relevant change; the cart's creep rigs dominate.
