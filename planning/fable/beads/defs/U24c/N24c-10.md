## Background

A known answer is an analytic or synthetic truth the implementation must recover, with a derivation on file. The
Arena chapter lists eleven of them, each paired with a **negative control**: a mutation or planted failure that
must make the row fail. They are the content of the `fixtures` battery, which runs on every change that touches
contact, actuators, clocks or the requirement language. The rule of the verification programme for a row:

1. a derivation on file under `docs/derivations/<fixture>.md` (model, derivation, value with its digits, error
   budget of the test);
2. a tolerance with a reason (`exact` for integers, rationals, Booleans, verdicts and digests; otherwise the
   tolerance of the owning gate);
3. a negative control that is a mutation, not a boundary behaviour;
4. a tier and a site: a row runs first on the weakest site that can decide it, then on every tier its gate names;
5. values are not improved in transit: the registry carries the printed value.

The mechanisms that compute these answers are built by other beads (the fixture, the study types, the
requirement-language core, the verdict and scorecard verbs). This bead makes the eleven rows executable in one
place, registers each control, writes the derivation files that no fixture owns, and makes the battery report
every row against its known answer. A value in the Arena chapter that follows from a stated formula is a derived
value and is recomputed by its test, never copied. Crates `frob-conformance` (registry rows) and `frob-arena`
(the test bodies); tier Solid; claim kind Known answer.

## Scope

In: the Gauntlet entries and test bodies for the known-answer records of rows 3 and 6 to 11, with the
front-matter lines of their derivation files; derivation files for rows 3 and 6 to 11; the wiring that makes
`frob task fixture --battery fixtures` run the eleven rows and report each control; the M1 rows executed, the two
M2 rows reported `not run` with their reason. The records themselves (N24-KA) and the controls of rows 1, 2, 3, 6,
7, 8, 9, 10 and 11 (their carrying beads, listed under Registration) are run here, not written again.
Out: building the fixtures and mechanisms; the chapter-wide known-answer registry bead N24-KA (which carries all
thirty Appendix M rows KA-594 to KA-623 with tolerance and tier, and on which this bead depends); the `fixtures`
battery manifest and its resolver (N24b-01, a dependency); the M1 conformance capstone BD-146, which reads this
bead's rows and is not a prerequisite of it; the late
known answers of the traction slice (N24c-19), the item bank (N24c-32), the task audit (N24c-28), the idle arm
(N24c-27) and the exposure-coupling fixture (N24c-38).

This bead is a conformance test bead, not a registry: it executes rows that the registry bead N24-KA carries and
depends on the mechanisms that compute them. The beads that consume its result are the M1 exit review (BD-147),
which reads the `fixtures` battery report, and the M2 rows of fixture 2 that flip rows 4 and 5 to executable.

**Row 1 values (the analytic layer of the cart; r wheel radius, h = 12 mm lip height, x = √(2rh − h²),
tan θ = x/(r − h), L = 0.32 m wheelbase, δ payload offset).** Mass roll-up: 8.000 kg and 8.225 kg; front-wheel
mass ratio 1.5625; spin-inertia ratio (75/60)⁴ = 625/256. Tread force cap 2·k_t·I_max/r with k_t = 0.2 N·m/A and
I_max = 6 A: 40 N and 32 N. Force slope 2·k_t·K_ω/r² with K_ω = 2 A·s/rad: 222.22 and 142.22 N/(m/s); a gain of
3.125 A·s/rad for the larger wheel matches the slopes. Lip lever: 36.000 mm at r = 60 mm, 40.694 mm at 75 mm.
Front lift-off in the creep limit: friction demand μ ≥ tan θ = 0.7500 and 0.6459 (0.7332 and 0.6351 with 2 mm
fillets); drive torque for the smaller wheel at δ = 0: 1.270 N·m of 2.4 N·m available. Rear pull-over in the
creep limit: demand ¾·(32/55 − δ)/(23/55 + δ), which is 24/23 = 1.043 at δ = 0 and 0.887 at δ = +0.04 m; for the
larger wheel at δ = 0 the demand of 34.2 N exceeds the 32 N cap. Creep-feasible set of the smaller wheel: only
δ ≥ 23.0 mm, 1.13% of the development distribution. Energy: raising the front axle by h takes 0.471 J against
0.360 J + 0.018 J of kinetic energy at 0.30 m/s. Wheel-loop one-tick spectral radius: 1.4820 with the feedforward
term R·i_ref, 0.9207 with R·i, 2.6917 for an instantaneous-current approximation (2.7075 without shaft damping);
the mutation gives 1.854 and 1.497. Power ledger: a blocked winding at 3 A and 3 V dissipates 9 W and draws
0.375 A from the 24 V bus; a drive at 50 rad/s and −2 A has terminal voltage 8 V, returns 16 W, takes 20 W from
the shaft and loses 4 W in copper. The creep rigs run at a commanded 0.02 m/s and must flip outcome between the
friction grid points (spacing 0.01) that bracket the threshold, excluding points within 0.005 of it on the
referee; a run exactly at the threshold carries the `boundary` flag.

**Row 4 values.** Capacity per unit normal load c(z, w) = 0.525 + 0.55·clip(z/w, −½, ½) and yaw moment
0.275·N·(w/4 − z²/w): at z = 0, 0.525 for both soles and 2.75 mm and 4.81 mm per newton; at z = +12 mm, 0.690

**Registration.** The eleven rows are groupings of N24-KA's records in
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml`; this bead adds no record and stores no status (N24-KA: the
loader computes eligibility). For each record that rows 3 and 6 to 11 serve (KA-598, KA-594, KA-615, KA-596,
KA-618, KA-595, KA-619) it adds the Gauntlet entry in the test target the record's `tier` names (`g1_known_answers`
for KA-594 to KA-598 and KA-615; `g0_laws` for KA-618 and KA-619), whose `run` calls the `ka_arena_*` body below,
and writes the crate, the test name and this bead's key into the front matter of the record's derivation file
(N24-KA's rule; never a field of the record). Rows 1 and 2 run the entries their carrying beads write (KA-602 to
KA-611: N24b-04 on the host, BD-123 on the referee; KA-612 to KA-614: N24b-06 and BD-124). Each control is one entry
of `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml`, written once by its owner and only referenced here: row 1,
BD-123's `euler-current-then-wheel` and `rear-lip-removed`; row 2, CX-265's plant `N24c-08:cx-265`; row 3, CX-261's
plant `N24c-04:cx-261`; row 6, CX-259's plant `N24c-02:cx-259`; row 7, N24a-10's non-strict comparator and its
unguarded single-precision screen; rows 8, 9 and 11, N24c-08's padded-window monitor, confidence-sequence control
and constant level per request; row 10, CX-260's plant `N24c-02:cx-260`. Rows 4 and 5 (KA-616, KA-599) have no
entry at M1: the battery reports them `not run` with the reason "fixture 2 lands at M2", and their entries are
added by the fixture-2 beads (N24b-12, N24b-11) when fixture 2's rows join the battery (N24b-01's recorded
addition event). A row whose control is not registered cannot support a gate.

**Derivation files written here.** `docs/derivations/arena-energy-accounting.md`, `arena-normalization.md`,
`arena-boundary-comparator.md`, `arena-online-offline.md`, `arena-peeking.md` (the exact enumeration of sign-test
paths that gives 13/256), `arena-joint-stages.md`, `arena-level-series.md`. The cart's derivations are under
`docs/derivations/fixture-cart-sill-dock/` and belong to the fixture.

**Battery wiring.** `frob task fixture --battery fixtures --key <k> --json` reports every registered row against
its known answer with the measured value, tolerance, verdict and the status of its control (`controls: [{name,
caught}]`); `--rows <n> --control <name>` reruns one row under one control, in a build with the `mutants` feature
(a release build answers FROB-REF-724, exit 5, per the `task.fixture` row).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Nine rows (1 to 3 and 6 to 11) execute at M1 and pass within tolerance; rows 4 and 5 are reported `not run` with their reason, and no status is written into a known-answer record.
- [ ] `FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- KA-598` (and likewise KA-594, KA-595, KA-596, KA-615; `--test g0_laws -- KA-619`, `-- KA-618`) runs this bead's entry and passes; each derivation file of rows 3 and 6 to 11 names the test, crate and bead in its front matter.
- [ ] Each of the nine controls, enabled one at a time, makes its row fail; a row with its control disabled is reported as unable to support a gate.
- [ ] Every row has a derivation file with model, derivation, digits and error budget.
- [ ] `frob task fixture --battery fixtures --json` lists every row with measured value against expected, and exits 2 when any row fails.
- [ ] The peeking row reproduces 13/256 exactly by enumeration, and 8.76%, 12.9% and 17.4% to the printed digits.
- [ ] Printed values are carried unchanged; any recomputation that disagrees is reported as a discrepancy and does not overwrite the registry.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena known_answers`: one test per row, named `ka_arena_<nn>_<slug>`, and one `control_`
test per row, built with `--features gauntlet-mutations` and the owning crate's control feature, asserting that
the row fails under it; the Gauntlet form of the same rows is `FROB_TEST_SEED=<s> cargo test -p frob-conformance
--test g1_known_answers -- <KA row>` (G0 rows under `g0_laws`). Examples with values: `ka_arena_03_energy` (10, 12, 30, 12 J); `ka_arena_06_normalization`
(0.2, 0.3; binding atom `clearance` in both unit systems); `ka_arena_09_peeking` (exact rational 13/256);
`ka_arena_10_joint_stages` (18 when the failing worlds differ, 19 when they coincide); `ka_arena_11_levels`
(m = 1 … 500); `ka_arena_02_enumeration` (521, 521, 0, 1,103; tie first at 86; 15 pairs);
`ka_arena_02_equivalence_bound` (zero discordant pairs in 64 give an exact one-sided 95% bound
1 − 0.05^(1/64) = 0.0457); `ka_arena_02_power` (P(prefer) at discordance (0.17, 0), (0.25, 0.02), (0.40, 0.02):
0.117, 0.406, 0.962 at n = 64).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/arena_fixtures_battery.sh` runs `frob task fixture --battery fixtures --key afb-0 --json` and, for
each row with a control, on a build with the `mutants` feature, `frob task fixture --battery fixtures --rows <n>
--control <name> --key afb-<n>-<name> --json` (distinct keys, so no rerun returns a stored receipt), expecting
exit 2 with that row failing and `caught: true`; on a release build it asserts `--control` answers FROB-REF-724,
exit 5. It sources the shared helper of STD-E2E and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/arena_fixtures_battery/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N24c-10`), `step`, `event`, and per row:
command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, measured value with unit, expected value, tolerance, pass or
fail, control caught or not, and on failure the first differing digit with the derivation file's path. The
summary names GATE-ARENA-04 and GATE-ARENA-05 as touched (their closing measurement belongs to the fixture and
study beads); the script writes no gate record.

### Known answers and negative controls
The rows and controls of the table are the content. Counterexamples behind them: CX-259 (raw margins), CX-260
(multiplying stage rates), CX-261 (energy per completed trial only), CX-262 (explicit Euler on current then
wheel), CX-265 (a fixed-n study can return any of its verdicts), CX-266 (peeking), CX-267 (resubmission).
Metamorphic relations: MR-19, MR-20, MR-27, MR-28. Determinism lanes: exact rows byte-identical twice, across
worker counts, debug and release, Linux and macOS; referee rows inside the referee's class.

## Considerations

- Rows of the wider registry printed with tolerance `to set` or control `none stated` are not eligible to support a gate until fixed (open questions on tolerances and controls of the verification programme). Each of the eleven battery rows has a control, but rows 1 and 2 group records that state none (KA-602 to KA-605, KA-608, KA-609, KA-611, KA-613, KA-614): those records stay `obligation: control` in N24-KA (the owed controls are N38b-33's), and a row's control makes only the records it names eligible.
- Row 7's device half waits for the guard-band experiment (OPEN-ARENA-5, bead N24c-15).
- The battery must stay fast (minutes) because it runs on every relevant change; the cart's creep rigs dominate.
