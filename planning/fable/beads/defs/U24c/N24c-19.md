## Background

Fixture 2 (`fixture:quad-split-grip`) tests whether sole width helps on a landing with a low-grip strip. Its
executable known answers come from a **plate-drag rig** that realises the limiting model: one sole plate on a
compliant layer under a centred normal load, guided against yaw, pulled along the material boundary until it
slips; the pull force at slip must equal the model's capacity times the normal load and the guide moment must
equal the model's yaw moment.

A guided plate measures the capacity **with the moment left free** (the guide carries whatever yaw moment
arises). A robot's foot is not guided. The wrench-slice mechanism therefore adds two rows to the fixture: the rig
runs once guided against yaw, as before, and once **free to yaw**, which enforces zero moment physically; and a
**two-point rig** makes the difference hand-checkable. This bead builds those rows and is the end-to-end
measurement of GATE-ARENA-10 (claim kind Known answer, M2):

> The cone program reproduces 52.5 N, 0.55 N·m, 25 N and 40 N to 10⁻⁹ in double precision; the two-point rig
> slips within 2% of each value on the referee and inside the fidelity envelope on the batch tiers; a scorecard
> that ranks the two soles by moment-free capacity when the task commands zero yaw is rejected by `task.check`.

The gate register lists this gate only in its table of gates added after the register was compiled (milestone
M2), without restating the threshold; the defining subsection quoted above governs the threshold.

## Scope

In: the two-point rig and the free-yaw plate rig as registered rows of fixture 2; their constants, hashed into
the fixture before the first run; the script `scripts/e2e/arena_traction_wrench.sh`; the gate record.
Out: the cone program (N24c-17); the metric and the `task.check` rule (N24c-18); the rest of fixture 2 (the
quadruped course, the guided plate rig and the limiting-model table: its seed bead).

## Technical approach

Constants marked DESIGN are choices of this bead where the plan gives only the outcome; they are written into the
fixture manifest and hashed with it.

**Two-point rig (`rig:two-point`).** A rigid bar of mass 10.1937 kg (so that its weight is 100.000 N at
g = 9.81 m/s²; DESIGN) resting on two point feet at lateral offsets y = ±20 mm, centre of mass midway and at
foot height in the model so that a pull at ground level creates no pitching or rolling load transfer; each foot
therefore carries 50 N. Floor: the half-plane y > 0 has μ = 0.8 and the half-plane y < 0 has μ = 0.25 (the
`split` variant), or μ = 0.4 everywhere (the `uniform` variant). A horizontal pull along +x is applied at the
bar centre at ground level and ramped at 1 N/s from zero (DESIGN: quasi-static; the ramp rate is halved once to
confirm the onset does not move by more than 0.5%). Two mountings: `guided` (a prismatic joint along x: yaw,
lateral motion locked; the joint's reaction yaw moment is recorded) and `free` (planar: x, y and yaw free).
**Slip onset** is the first requirement tick at which any contact point's tangential speed has stayed above
1 mm/s for 0.05 s (DESIGN); the pull force at that tick is the measured capacity.

| Variant, mounting | Expected pull at slip | Expected guide yaw moment at slip |
|---|---|---|
| split, guided | 52.5 N (12.5 + 40) | 0.55 N·m ((40 − 12.5) N × 0.020 m) |
| split, free | 25 N (2 × 12.5: the low-grip foot saturates first and the bar begins to yaw about the other) | none (no guide) |
| uniform, guided | 40 N | 0 |
| uniform, free | 40 N | none |

**Plate rig, free to yaw (`rig:plate-drag-free`).** The fixture's plate (80 × 40 mm and 80 × 70 mm, the 80 mm
side along the pull) under a centred normal load N, its centre on the boundary between μ = 0.25 and μ = 0.8
(offset z = 0), mounted free in x, y and yaw, pulled along the boundary as above. Expected: slip at no less than
0.3486·N for both widths (the plan's zero-yaw closed form (μ_L + √(μ_L·μ_H))/2, which assumes longitudinal
traction that never points backward), in fact at no less than 0.3731·N (longitudinal traction of either sign,
N24c-17), and equal within 2% to the cone program's value on the same quadrature with zero yaw moment and zero
net lateral force (the free mounting reacts neither; for this fore-aft symmetric plate the program's optimum
already has zero lateral force). Planning-time estimate of that value, HYPOTHESIS until measured: about 0.51·N for
the 80 × 40 mm plate and 0.49·N for the 80 × 70 mm plate, so at zero offset the free plate also ranks the narrow
sole ahead. The guided plate at z = 0 slips at 0.525·N for both widths (its existing row).

**Fixture discipline.** The two rigs are added to the fixture manifest as rows `wrench-slice/two-point/*` and
`wrench-slice/plate-free/*`; because a fixture is complete before its first run, the manifest hash changes once
when they are added and is then frozen. `frob task fixture quad-split-grip --rows wrench-slice` runs them and
reports each row against its known answer.

**Script `scripts/e2e/arena_traction_wrench.sh`** (sources the shared helper; idempotent; seed-explicit although
the rigs are deterministic; non-zero exit on failure).

| Step | Command | Asserts |
|---|---|---|
| `program_hand_cases` | `frob task fixture quad-split-grip --rows wrench-slice/program --json` | 52.5 N, 0.55 N·m, 25 N, 40 N each within 10⁻⁹ (double precision); solver state `optimal`; residuals logged |
| `rig_split_guided`, `rig_split_free`, `rig_uniform_guided`, `rig_uniform_free` | `frob task fixture quad-split-grip --rows wrench-slice/two-point/<variant> --tier referee --json` | slip force within 2% of 52.5, 25, 40, 40 N; guide moment within 2% of 0.55 N·m and of 0 (absolute 0.011 N·m) |
| `plate_free_40`, `plate_free_70` | `… --rows wrench-slice/plate-free/<width> --tier referee --json` | slip force ≥ 0.3731·N (and so ≥ the plan's 0.3486·N) and within 2% of the program's value for the rig's quadrature; the program's value and the measured one logged for both widths |
| `ramp_halved` | the split, free row at 0.5 N/s | onset within 0.5% of the 1 N/s onset |
| `batch_tiers` | the four two-point rows with `--tier batch-cpu` (and each device tier present) | each slip force inside the frozen fidelity envelope for slip-onset force; tiers absent on the machine logged `not run` |
| `scorecard_two_point` | `frob eval scorecard` on the rig runs | `capacity, moment free` = 52.5 N with 0.55 N·m; `capacity at +x, yaw = 0` = 25 N |
| `check_rejects_scalar_ranking` | `frob task check` on a task that commands zero yaw and ranks by the moment-free capacity | exit 3 with the symbolic refusal `metric-moment-free-under-moment-command` |
| `check_admits_free` | the same ranking with `moments: free` | exit 0 |
| `control_pyramid` | the program rows with the pyramidal-cone mutation enabled through the conformance harness | the single-point diagonal row reports √2 times too much and the step fails as required |

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/arena_traction_wrench/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N24c-19`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block (claim
Known answer, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), each measured force in N and moment in N·m with its
expected value and tolerance, slip-onset time, the fixture hash, and on failure the first row out of tolerance
with the `.frun` path of the rig run. The `summary` line names GATE-ARENA-10.

**Gate record.** On a full pass the measured values (four program values and their errors; four rig forces and
their relative errors; the batch-tier rows; the refusal observed) are written to the gate's receipt.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four program values are within 10⁻⁹ of 52.5 N, 0.55 N·m, 25 N and 40 N.
- [ ] On the referee the two-point rig slips within 2% of each expected force, and the guided split variant reports a guide moment within 2% of 0.55 N·m.
- [ ] The free-yaw plate slips at no less than 0.3486·N for both widths (the plan's bound; 0.3731·N is the sharper bound asserted by the script) and within 2% of the cone program's value.
- [ ] On each batched tier present, the two-point rows lie inside the frozen fidelity envelope; absent tiers are recorded `not run`.
- [ ] The scalar-ranking task is rejected by `task.check`, and admitted when the moment is declared free.
- [ ] The pyramidal-cone control and a control that drops the moment row (25 N becomes 52.5 N) are each caught.
- [ ] The rig constants are in the fixture manifest and its hash is frozen before the first recorded run; GATE-ARENA-10's receipt carries measured values.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena fixture2_wrench`: `two_point_static_loads` (each foot carries 50 N before the pull,
within 0.1%); `slip_onset_detector` on a synthetic speed trace (`synthetic`): 0.9 mm/s for 1 s gives no onset;
1.1 mm/s for 0.04 s then rest gives none; 1.1 mm/s for 0.05 s gives onset at the end of that window;
`fixture_hash_changes_once`: adding the rows changes the manifest hash, re-running does not.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script; its steps, assertions and log fields are the tables above.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers (stated with the mechanism; they carry no Appendix number yet): two-point rig 52.5 N and 0.55 N·m
guided, 25 N free, 40 N both ways on uniform μ = 0.4; strip model at zero offset 0.525·N guided for both widths
and at least 0.3486·N free for both widths. KA-616 is the existing guided-plate table this extends. Negative
controls: the pyramidal friction cone; a sole with one quadrature point (cannot carry a moment, so guided and
free agree and the difference vanishes); the moment row dropped; normal loads altered by a pull applied above
ground level (the rig's load transfer must be zero, checked by `two_point_static_loads` during the pull).
Metamorphic: swapping the two friction half-planes mirrors the guide moment's sign and leaves the forces
unchanged; the mirrored rig returns the mirrored result inside its determinism class. Determinism lanes: twice on
one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; referee against batched tiers by the fidelity envelope.

## Considerations

- A compliant contact law has bounded creep before slip, so the onset definition (speed above 1 mm/s for 0.05 s) matters; it is a fixture constant and is reported with the result.
- If the reference body decision for fixture 2 (OPEN-ARENA-4) changes the sole plate dimensions, the plate rows follow the fixture's plates; the two-point rig is independent of the body.
- The 2% tolerance on the referee is the gate's; it leaves room for the compliant layer's creep and the ramp's finite rate.
- A reversal of ranking between contact laws on these rigs would be reported as a fidelity-dependent ranking, a finding about the tiers.
