## Background

The task audit asks whether a task can be satisfied by a behaviour its author did not intend. To find such
behaviours cheaply it does not use real candidate designs, which are constrained by exactly the limbs, limits and
controllers that keep them honest. It uses **foil bodies**: bodies that are deliberately over-capable or
degenerate, written in the design language, each run in the task's own scene and scored by the task's own
requirement and cost. If a sled can "walk to the goal", the requirement does not require walking.

| Foil | What it is | What it probes |
|---|---|---|
| Free centroidal body | One rigid body with the mass and inertia of the design class, applying bounded wrenches at the contact sites the task designates, inside friction cones; no limbs, no actuator limits | Whether the predicate can be met by forces alone, without the intended mechanism |
| Relaxed family member | The current design with card limits multiplied by 3 (DESIGN) and collision enabled on every link | Behaviours the real design is only barely kept from |
| Degenerate shapes | A sled, a ball, a tumbling box and a single-leg hopper of the class's mass | Sliding, rolling, tumbling and bouncing routes to a satisfied verdict |

A foil is marked `foil: true` and is **never a candidate**: offering one to the optimisation verbs, to a paired
comparison or to the qualification lane is refused. Without that rule the cheapest way to "win" a campaign would
be to submit the sled.

Tier Frontier: this bead is funded only after the audit pilot on ten authored tasks has recorded a result, and it
exists only on the surviving branch of KILL-FOIL (trigger: detection below 60%, or findings on more than 30% of
clean tasks, or the sampling controller on the actual design finds the same loopholes at the same cost, so foil
bodies add nothing; fallback: the in-campaign check on winning episodes only, with `task.audit` running the
actual design alone). Owner: `frob-arena` (library and rule), using the design language of `frob-design`.

## Scope

In: the `foil` marker; the three foil kinds and how each is built for a morphology class and a task; the files
under `assets/foils/`; the candidate-admission refusal; labelling of foil runs.
Out: the search that drives the foils (N24c-24); findings and resolution (N24c-22); intent invariants (N24c-21).

## Technical approach

Parameters marked DESIGN are choices of this bead where the plan gives only the description; each is a field of
the foil's file and so part of its identity.

**The marker.** A top-level field `foil: true` in a design document. Only the value `true` is ever written; a
design without the field is not a foil, so no existing `DesignId` changes. Three things key on it: (1) foil-only
components are admitted only in a design that has it (a lint refuses them elsewhere as an unknown field); (2)
the candidate-admission check refuses it; (3) every run, verdict and scorecard of a foil carries `foil: true` in
its evidence block and cannot be cited as a design result or enter the design corpus, a response-model fit, or a
family-policy training set.

**Foil-only component `wrench-site` (DESIGN).** A force generator at a named site of a body: input a force
vector per control tick; the applied force is the input projected onto the friction cone of the site's contact
with the scene (normal component ≥ 0 and tangential magnitude ≤ μ times it, with μ from the site's interface
card), limited in magnitude to `bound`, and zero unless the site is within the contact distance of a scene
surface. It therefore applies only forces that some contact at that site could apply: the foil is "forces alone,
inside friction cones", not a rocket.

**1. Free centroidal body** (`assets/foils/free-centroidal.fbot.tmpl`). Built per (morphology class, task):
one rigid body whose mass equals the class reference design's total mass and whose inertia equals its composite
inertia at the nominal stance, with a collision Region equal to the class's bounding box; one `wrench-site` at
each contact site the task designates (the feet, wheels or tracks of the class at their nominal stance
positions). `bound` per site: one body weight m·g (DESIGN). No limbs, no actuators, no actuator limits.

**2. Relaxed family member** (a recipe, `assets/foils/relaxed.recipe.json`). Derived from the design under audit
by a deterministic transformation: every actuator card limit (peak and continuous torque, speed, current,
power, bus current) multiplied by 3 (DESIGN); collision enabled on every link with the link's mass Region as its
collision proxy; `foil: true` set. Its controller is the baseline controller re-synthesised for the relaxed
cards. Because it is derived, it is rebuilt when the design under audit changes.

**3. Degenerate shapes** (`assets/foils/sled.fbot.tmpl`, `ball`, `tumbling-box`, `hopper`), each scaled to the
class's mass:

| Shape | Body | Actuation |
|---|---|---|
| Sled | A slab with the class's footprint and a height of one tenth of its length, contact law `skid` on its underside | `wrench-site` at the four underside corners, `bound` m·g/2 each |
| Ball | A sphere whose diameter is the class's body width, rolling contact | A bounded torque about any horizontal axis, up to m·g times the radius |
| Tumbling box | A cuboid with the class's bounding-box proportions, point-foot contacts at its eight corners | `wrench-site` at each corner, `bound` m·g/2 |
| Single-leg hopper | A body of the class's mass on one prismatic leg with a point foot | Leg force up to 3·m·g, stroke one third of the class's leg length; a bounded hip torque |

All shape parameters above are DESIGN. Every foil also admits a bounded initial twist (linear speed up to twice
the task's commanded or nominal speed, angular speed up to 2π rad/s) as part of what the search may choose.

**Instantiation.** `foil.instantiate(kind, class, task, design?) -> Design` resolves a template against the
class reference (mass, inertia, footprint, site positions) and the task (designated sites, interface cards of
the scene), producing an ordinary design document with `foil: true` and a `DesignId`. Instantiation is a pure
function of its inputs.

**Candidate-admission refusal.** The function that admits a candidate for `opt.*`, `eval.compare` and
`eval.qualify` (and any verb that ranks or promotes a design) checks the marker first. The plan assigns no code
number; the refusal is registered as `foil-as-candidate` in the evaluation-protocol family (exit code 3,
`retry: never` for that design; repair: none for the foil; submit a real design). A design derived from a foil
by typed edits keeps the marker unless the foil-only components are removed, in which case it is an ordinary
design and is linted as one.

**Evidence.** Foil runs are class `Tested` on their tier with the `foil` condition flag in the claim product; the
flag propagates to anything computed from them, and a result carrying it is refused wherever a design result is
required.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each foil kind instantiates for the quadruped and the wheeled classes into a design that compiles and runs in a task's scene under the task's requirement.
- [ ] A design with a `wrench-site` and no `foil: true` is refused at load; a design without the marker is byte-identical to before this bead (no `DesignId` moved on the archetypes).
- [ ] The free centroidal body applies no force when its sites are out of contact, and never a force outside the friction cone (property test over random inputs).
- [ ] The relaxed member's card limits are exactly 3 times the design's, and it is rebuilt when the design changes.
- [ ] Offering any foil to `opt.start`, `eval.compare` or `eval.qualify` is refused with `foil-as-candidate`; in `eval.qualify` the refusal precedes commitment.
- [ ] Every foil run's envelope and stored verdict carry the `foil` flag; a scorecard built from foil runs cannot be promoted or cited as a design comparison.
- [ ] The sled instance at a constant 1 m/s through the hand-case goal region reproduces the 0.2 s dwell.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena foils`:
- `instantiate_is_pure`: same inputs, same `DesignId`.
- `free_body_mass_and_inertia_match_class`: equal to the class reference's totals to 10⁻¹² relative.
- `wrench_site_respects_cone`: 10⁴ random inputs; applied tangential ≤ μ × normal; zero when 1 cm above the ground.
- `wrench_site_bound`: inputs above `bound` are scaled to it.
- `relaxed_limits_times_three`; `relaxed_collision_on_every_link`.
- `shape_masses_equal_class_mass`.
- `foil_marker_refuses_candidate` for the three verbs; `qualify_refusal_before_commit`.
- `foil_only_component_needs_marker`.
- `no_design_id_moved`: the archetypes' identities before and after.
- `foil_flag_propagates`: a scorecard over foil runs carries the flag and `eval.promote` refuses it.

### End-to-end script and logging
Steps `foil_instantiate`, `foil_run_hand_case` and `foil_candidate_refused` of `scripts/e2e/arena_task_audit.sh`
(bead N24c-28). Logged: commands, exit codes, wall time, the `evidence` block with the `foil` flag, the foil's
`DesignId`, the measured dwell in the goal region (0.2 s expected), the refusal's symbolic code, and on failure
the first differing value.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answer: the sled hand case (inside x ∈ [4.9, 5.1] m for t ∈ [4.9, 5.1] s at 1 m/s). Negative controls: a
`wrench-site` that ignores the contact test lets the free body fly (it then satisfies an airborne loophole no
contact-driven body could, and the property test catches it); an admission check that tests the design's name
instead of the marker; a relaxed member built from stale cards. Metamorphic: mirroring a foil and the scene
mirrors its trajectory. Determinism: foil runs are ordinary runs and are replayable inside their tier's class.

## Considerations

- A foil that is too capable finds "loopholes" no plausible design could use, which shows up as findings on clean tasks; the kill gate's 30% clean-task bound is the check on that, and the bounds in this bead are the knobs.
- The free centroidal body assumes the class's mass and inertia; for a new class with no reference design it uses the design under audit.
- Foil templates are assets with identities; changing a bound is a new template version, recorded in the audit record so that findings are reproducible.
- If KILL-FOIL fires this bead closes with reason `fallback`, and the library, the `wrench-site` component, the marker and the admission refusal are removed together; the idle arm then runs the actual design only.
