## Background

The task audit runs foil bodies (a free centroidal body, a relaxed family member, a sled, a ball, a tumbling
box, a hopper) in a task's scene under the task's own requirement and looks for ways of satisfying it that the
author did not intend. One optimum is not what is wanted: the best-scoring behaviour of a sled is one loophole,
and a task may have several. The objective of the search is therefore **many distinct high-scoring behaviours**,
kept in a quality-diversity archive indexed by what the body did:

- the fraction of time each site class touches the ground,
- the range of base attitude, ‖log R‖,
- the airborne fraction,
- the sliding fraction (tangential speed at stance contacts above a card threshold).

Two generators fill the archive: the sampling-based predictive controller (which needs no training and no gait:
at each control tick it samples perturbed command sequences, rolls each out on the body's own model inside the
batch, and applies the first command of a weighted average), and rank-based search over open-loop parameters.
`frob-control` and `frob-opt` supply the search machinery; `frob-arena` owns the audit that drives it.

Tier Frontier, funded after the audit pilot (N24c-20) and built before KILL-FOIL is decided, since the gate run
that feeds the kill gate (N24c-25) uses it. The kill gate's third trigger is measured by this bead: if the
sampling controller on the **actual design** finds the same loopholes at the same cost, foil bodies add nothing,
so the search must be able to run on the actual design with an identical budget. That actual-design path is also
what the kill gate's fallback keeps ("`task.audit` runs the actual design alone"), so the generators, archive,
budget and cell-to-finding rule are written against any body, and only the foil-specific inputs are Frontier.

Alternative rejected: maximising the requirement margin alone. It returns the same loophole from every restart
and says nothing about the others.

## Scope

In: the parameterisation of foil behaviour; the two generators; the descriptor computation and binning; the
archive and its tie-breaking; budget accounting; conversion of archive cells into findings; the actual-design
comparator run.
Out: the foils (N24c-23); the finding record, clause proposals and resolution (N24c-22); invariants (N24c-21);
the idle arm (N24c-27); the gate fixture of 30 + 30 tasks (N24c-25).

## Technical approach

Constants marked DESIGN are this bead's choices and are recorded in the audit record.

**1. Behaviour parameters.** For bodies with `wrench-site` inputs: K = 8 spline knots (DESIGN) per input
component over the horizon, plus the bounded initial twist. For the relaxed family member and the actual design:
the spline knots of joint targets that the sampling controller already uses, passed through the actuator model.

**2. Generators.**
- *Sampling controller.* Controller kind `mpc-sample` with the planning configuration fixed for the audit
  (horizon 0.4 to 0.9 s, control at 50 Hz, annealing iterations and sample count as configured; one control tick
  costs N_s·H·N_it model steps for N_s samples, H = horizon over planning step, N_it iterations). Its cost is the
  negative of the task's online success margin plus the task's own cost terms: the audit asks the controller to
  satisfy the requirement by any means the body allows. Perturbations are drawn from counter-based streams keyed
  by (world address, tick, iteration, sample), so a run replays exactly. Planning worlds are forks, and the
  digest of the controlled world must not depend on how many forks ran beside it.
- *Rank-based open-loop search.* A covariance-adaptation evolution strategy with restarts over the parameter
  vector of item 1, objective the verdict margin of the resulting episode, population evaluated as one batch.
  Each restart starts from a point drawn from a stream keyed by (task, foil, restart index).

**3. Descriptors, computed per episode from committed telemetry** by `intent::descriptors` of N24c-21 (one
definition shared with the in-campaign check, which outlives this bead if KILL-FOIL fires). `site_contact`: for
each site class of the task (feet, wheels, tracks, other), the fraction of episode time with at least one contact
of that class.
`attitude_range`: the maximum over the episode of ‖log(R_ref⁻¹·R)‖ for the base, in radians. `airborne`: the
fraction of time with no contact at all. `sliding`: the fraction of time in which some stance contact's
tangential speed exceeds the interface card's sliding threshold.

**4. Archive.** A grid over (site_contact of designated sites, site_contact of other bodies, attitude_range,
airborne, sliding) with 5 bins per axis (fractions in fifths; attitude in [0, π] in fifths; DESIGN), 3,125 cells.
A cell keeps the episode with the highest verdict margin under the total order (margin, then trace digest), so
the archive's content does not depend on arrival order and two partial archives merge commutatively. (The sibling
archive's own rule replaces an elite only on strict improvement, which keeps the first arrival on ties; the
total order removes that dependence.)

**5. Budget.** The audit's budget is at most 5% of the task's reference campaign, counted in simulated model
steps including the sampling controller's planning steps. It is split equally across the bodies selected
(DESIGN), and within a body half to each generator. The search stops at the budget and returns the archive so
far, with the steps actually used. Admission refuses a budget above the caller's remaining budget; a stop
publishes the archive as an audit record only if it completed its allotted steps, otherwise it is marked
`partial` and the task is not "audited".

**6. From cells to findings.** For every occupied cell whose episode's verdict is `satisfied`:
- evaluate every adopted intent invariant on the episode;
- if one is violated, emit a finding with that invariant (the first violated in the fixed order site-only,
  corridor, passive-contact, attitude, airborne, sliding, dwell) and the clause proposal for it;
- if none is violated: for a free centroidal body or a degenerate shape the finding is `unclassified` (the body
  has no intended mechanism, yet satisfied the task and the library has no word for how); for the relaxed member
  and the actual design it is `unclassified` only if its cell is not occupied by the unrelaxed design's baseline
  run (DESIGN).
Findings from different bodies that fall in one cell and violate the same invariant are merged, keeping the
highest margin, so the author sees distinct behaviours.

**7. Actual-design comparator.** With `--arms actual` (its findings carry `arm: actual`, the same name as in the finding schema of N24c-22) the same search, budget and archive run on the design under
audit with the sampling controller. The audit record stores, for each finding, whether the comparator produced a
finding with the same violated invariant in the same or an adjacent cell within the same budget; that table is
what the kill gate reads.

**Evidence and determinism.** Episodes are class `Tested` on their tier, flagged `foil`. Sampling-controller
episodes are audit data only: they are never offered as the scoring source of acceptance, promotion or
qualification (that use has its own refusal). The search is deterministic given (task, body, budget, build): all
draws are addressed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the hand-case task (goal region x ∈ [4.9, 5.1] m, requirement eventually-in-goal within 10 s) the sled's archive contains a satisfied cell with full sliding fraction, and the emitted finding violates `dwell` when the task adopts it.
- [ ] On a task with a planted off-site-contact loophole the archive yields a `site-only` finding; on its clean twin (the invariant already a hard limit) no `site-only` finding is emitted, and any other finding (for example an `unclassified` free-body behaviour) is logged with its cell and body, since the clean-task finding rate is the gate's measurement (N24c-25), not this bead's.
- [ ] The archive is identical for any evaluation order and for one batch or several (merge commutativity), and identical on re-run.
- [ ] Budget accounting counts planning steps; the search never exceeds its budget; an interrupted search is marked `partial`.
- [ ] At least two distinct findings are returned on a task with two planted loopholes (diversity, not one optimum).
- [ ] The comparator run on the actual design produces the same-or-adjacent-cell table for every finding.
- [ ] The controlled world's digest does not change with the sampling controller's fork count.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena audit_search`:
- `descriptors_on_synthetic_traces` (`synthetic`): a trace airborne 30% of the time, sliding 100% of stance, attitude range 0.2 rad lands in the expected cell.
- `archive_total_order`: two episodes of equal margin, either arrival order, same elite (smaller digest wins).
- `archive_merge_commutes`: merge(A, B) = merge(B, A) byte for byte.
- `budget_counts_planning_steps`: N_s = 64, H = 20, N_it = 2 at 50 Hz for 1 s costs 64·20·2·50 = 128,000 steps.
- `cell_to_finding_order`: an episode violating both `corridor` and `attitude` is reported under `corridor`.
- `unclassified_for_foil_only`.
- `merge_findings_same_cell_same_invariant`.
- `search_replays_exactly`: two runs, identical archive digest.
- `two_planted_loopholes_two_findings`.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead's own script, `scripts/e2e/task_audit_search.sh` (the verb, the foils and the invariants are its
dependencies; seed 3): `frob task audit <hand-case TaskId> --arms foil --key tas-foil-1 --json` (exit 8, then `frob
job wait <job> --sync-wait-ms 30000 --json`; exit 2 with the `dwell` finding of the sled), the same under
`tas-foil-2` (identical archive digest), `frob task audit <hand-case TaskId> --arms actual --design <reference
design> --key tas-actual --json` (the comparator table), and `frob task audit <two-loophole fixture TaskId> --arms
foil --key tas-two --json` (two distinct findings). The same cases from the user's side, with resolution and
qualification, are steps `audit_hand_case` and `audit_comparator` of `scripts/e2e/arena_task_audit.sh` (N24c-28).
Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes (8 then completion), wall time, the `evidence` block with the `foil`
flag, budget granted and steps used with their share of the reference campaign (threshold 5%), occupied cells,
satisfied cells, findings by invariant, the archive digest, the comparator table, on failure the first cell whose
elite differs between two runs with both run identities, and a `summary` line naming GATE-ARENA-11 and KILL-FOIL.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answer: the hand case (a sled at a constant 1 m/s is inside the goal region for 0.2 s; verdict satisfied;
the dwell clause of 2 s is violated by it). Negative controls: an archive with one cell returns one loophole on
the two-loophole task; a search that maximises margin without descriptors (same); a budget meter that ignores
planning steps overspends by orders of magnitude and is caught by the accounting test; an archive keeping the
first arrival on ties is order-dependent and fails the merge test. Metamorphic: mirroring the scene and the foil
mirrors the archive's episodes and leaves descriptors unchanged. Determinism: identical archives twice on one
machine and across worker counts; across backends inside the tier's class.

## Considerations

- Cost: the sampling controller is expensive (a published quadruped configuration uses 2,048 samples × 16 steps × 2 iterations = 65,536 model steps per control tick); the audit uses small sample counts on simple foils, and the 5% cap is what bounds it.
- The descriptor grid is coarse on purpose; finer grids fragment one behaviour into many findings. Bin counts are recorded with each audit.
- If the airlock entries for the archive and the optimiser are not admitted at M3, the fallback inside this bead is a plain grid archive and random-restart local search written in `frob-arena`, with the same interface; the gate is measured on whatever shipped.
- If the comparator finds the same loopholes at the same cost on the gate's tasks, KILL-FOIL fires; the fallback branch of N24c-26 removes the foil-specific inputs (the `wrench-site` parameterisation and the foil bodies' runs) and keeps the comparator path (the actual design under the sampling controller, the archive, the budget and the cell-to-finding rule) as the audit's search (N24cp1-01). If the pilot's falsifier holds before this bead starts, nothing of it is built (the plan's pilot consequence: "only the in-campaign check is built") and it closes with reason `fallback`.
