## Background

A paired comparison of two close designs on a battery spends most episodes on items both designs pass or both
fail. The item bank (in `frob-arena`) holds a response model of the items. This bead is the `frob-opt` half that
turns the model into savings without changing what is estimated: a **proposal** that draws discriminating items
more often, and an **importance-weighted estimator** of the contrast under the battery's frozen law p.

For designs A and B with paired differences D_i = Y_B,i − Y_A,i:

    q*(i) ∝ p(i)·√(E_model[D_i²]),      q(i) = (1 − ε)·q*(i) + ε·p(i),      ε = 0.1 (DESIGN),
    Ĉ = (1/n)·Σ_j w(i_j)·D_{i_j},        i_j drawn from q by an addressed stream,   w(i) = p(i)/q(i) ≤ 1/ε.

E_q[w·D] = Σ_i q(i)·(p(i)/q(i))·E[D_i] = Σ_i p(i)·E[D_i]: the estimate is unbiased for the contrast under the
frozen law whether or not the response model is right. The model buys efficiency and nothing else.

Exact arithmetic. Let D be ±1 on a fraction f of items and 0 elsewhere, with mean μ. Uniform sampling has
variance f − μ². Under q* the weight is f on the discriminating items and the variance is f² − μ². At f = 0.1
and μ = 0.05 these are 0.0975 and 0.0075, a ratio of 13. With the mixture at ε = 0.1 the weight on a
discriminating item is 1/(0.9/f + 0.1) = 0.1099 and the variance is 0.1 × 0.1099 − 0.0025 = 0.0085, a ratio of
11.5.

Counterexample: a wrong model costs up to 1/ε. Let the model place q* on a set of items disjoint from the items
that discriminate. Each discriminating item then has q = ε·p and weight 10, the second moment is f × 10 = 1.0,
and the variance is 0.9975 against 0.0975 under uniform sampling: 10.2 times worse, with the estimate still
unbiased. The mixture bounds the damage; it does not remove it. The gate therefore measures realised interval
width and not the model's own forecast.

Tier Frontier: funded after the item-bank pilot (N24c-29) and built before KILL-ITEMBANK is decided, because the
replay gate (N24c-33) runs this proposal and estimator; it stays in service only on the `kept` branch (N24c-34),
and the fallback (N24c-35) replaces the sampling mode. If the pilot is falsified it closes with reason `fallback`
unbuilt. Expected saving with model error, the mixture and replicate noise: 4 to 8 times (HYPOTHESIS, tested by
the replay gate). The saving multiplies with the
tier correction, which lowers the cost of an episode instead of their number.

## Technical approach

**1. Model second moment.** With independent outcomes of the two designs on an item given the model:
success outcomes, π_A = σ(a_iᵀ·θ_A − b_i) and π_B likewise, E_model[D_i²] = π_A·(1 − π_B) + π_B·(1 − π_A);
continuous outcomes, E_model[D_i²] = (a_iᵀ·(θ_B − θ_A))² + 2·s_i². If the model predicts no difference anywhere
(Z = 0 below), q* = p. No per-item floor is applied (DESIGN, changed from a floor of 10⁻⁶ under the square root):
the mixture already keeps q ≥ ε·p > 0 wherever p is positive, and a per-item floor would move the known answers
(on the two-value fixture it turns 3/400 into 759/100,000 = 0.00759 and the wrong-model weight 10 into 9.18). A
`synthetic` fit version may instead carry the test-only model form `second-moment-table` (E_model[D_i²] given per
item for one named pair), which the known-answer fixtures use because no logistic parameters give a second moment
of exactly 0 or 1; it is refused on any battery not labelled `synthetic`. ε must lie in (0, 1]: `--epsilon 0` is
refused as an invalid argument in a study (it removes the defensive bound and can leave q = 0 where p > 0), and is
admitted only by `--explain-variance`, which computes the exact per-draw variance and draws nothing, and in a
study only under the ARENA-I1 plant below (a `gauntlet-mutations` build). A design with fewer than 20
rows in the bank (DESIGN) takes θ equal to the class mean of the fitted θ_d (as the replay gate does for its
held-out candidate; the fit is not constrained to centre θ at 0); when both designs lack rows the proposal
follows only each item's predicted pass rate π for the average design (E_model[D_i²] = 2·π·(1 − π)).

**2. Proposal.** q*(i) = p(i)·√(E_model[D_i²]) / Z, Z the normalising sum; q = (1 − ε)·q* + ε·p. q is computed
once at plan time from a named fit version and the two designs' parameters at that time, written into the study
plan as exact rationals (after rounding q* to a fixed binary precision and renormalising, so that w is
reproducible on every platform), and never updated during the study. Hence w(i) ≤ 1/ε exactly.

**3. Draws.** The j-th item of the study is drawn from q by a counter-based stream keyed by (study identity, j)
with the registered stream name `study/item`. Within the drawn item, the replicate's world address is the next
unused address of that item, the same for both arms (pairing by world address is unchanged). Draw j depends on
nothing else, so consumption in index order makes the estimate independent of scheduling and preemption.

**4. Estimator.** Observation j is X_j = w(i_j)·D_{i_j}. Ĉ is the mean of the X_j. Its variance is estimated by
the sample variance of X_j. The uniform-equivalent count reported to the agent is n_eq = n·V̂_p/V̂_q, where
V̂_q is that sample variance and V̂_p = (1/n)·Σ_j w(i_j)·D_{i_j}² − Ĉ² estimates the variance under p from the
same draws (E_q[w·D²] = E_p[D²]).

**5. Rules** (each a refusal, not a warning).

| Rule | Reason | Refusal |
|---|---|---|
| Anytime studies on discovery worlds only, and only with the variance-adaptive betting sequence; the declared span is the widened one | X_j lies in a span 1/ε times the span declared for D; a range-driven sequence would lose (1/ε)² = 100 in sample size, wiping out the gain | A request with the range-driven sequence, or with the unwidened span, is refused (symbolic `item-bank-needs-variance-adaptive`) |
| Refused for `fixed-n` studies | The exact fixed-n rule counts discordant pairs under the frozen law; weighted draws are not that experiment | Refused as a comparison that changes the sampling law of a fixed-n study (symbolic `item-bank-in-fixed-n`; the gate's refusal clause) |
| Refused inside the blinded qualification lane | Confirmation draws from p on fresh sealed clusters, and the fit never reads a confirmation world | Refused before commitment (symbolic `item-bank-in-qualification`) |
| Not composed with the tier-correction estimator | Promotion pairs are drawn from p; multiplying the two weights would allow 10 × 20 = 200 | Refused (symbolic `item-bank-with-tier-correction`) |
| A rare-failure bound refuses item-bank episodes | A bound accepts only episodes drawn nominally or with recorded bounded weights of its own proposal | The existing refusals of the rare-failure lane apply (a bound over battery-selected or search-selected episodes; episodes not drawn nominally) |

The plan assigns no code numbers to the first four; they are N24-REF rows under the symbolic names above in the
evaluation-protocol family, raised by slug until N35-33's allocation ledger (`registries/refusal_allocations.toml`)
numbers them (exit code 3; repair in each case: run with
`--sampling nominal`). Replicate noise is untouched: variance between seeds inside an item is not reduced by
choosing items, and the report says so.

**6. Verb surface.** `eval.compare A B --task T --battery <b> --sampling item-bank [--epsilon 0.1] [--fit <version>]
--key <k>`, with `--explain-variance` (rule of item 1) and the sequence choice of the study (`--sequence`, whose
range-driven value is refused by rule 1). The `eval.compare` row of VERBS-eval lists these inputs: `battery`,
`sampling` (`nominal`, `item-bank`; `stratified` is N24c-35's), `epsilon` in (0, 1] with default 0.1, `fit`,
`explain-variance` and `sequence`, all implemented here for the item-bank mode. Planning (`study.plan`)
records: the battery version and p, the fit version, ε, q, the widened span, the sequence type. The comparison's
identity includes all of them. Default sampling stays `nominal`.

**7. What an agent reads.** Beside the verdict and the confidence sequence: the episodes used and the
uniform-equivalent count n_eq; the realised maximum weight; and the separating items ranked by discrimination
‖a_i‖ with their difficulty b_i, which says in which capability the two designs differ. Evidence: kind Design
comparison, class `Tested`, standing `provisional` (discovery data; a claim goes through the qualification lane,
which samples nominally).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The two-value fixture reproduces variances 0.0975 (uniform), 0.0075 (q*) and 0.0085 (mixture) in exact rational arithmetic: 39/400, 3/400 and 309/36,400 (first clause of GATE-ARENA-12).
- [ ] Under the disjoint wrong model the mean of Ĉ over 10⁴ replays equals the true contrast 0.05 within three Monte Carlo standard errors, every weight is at most 1/ε, and the second-moment ratio to uniform sampling is at most 1/ε (exactly 10 in the fixture; the variance ratio 10.23 is reported beside it) (second clause).
- [ ] A `fixed-n` request with item-bank sampling is refused (third clause); so are requests inside the qualification lane, with the tier-correction estimator, and with a range-driven sequence.
- [ ] A rare-failure bound offered episodes from an item-bank comparison refuses them.
- [ ] The estimate is identical under any scheduling order, preemption and worker count.
- [ ] The result reports n, n_eq, the maximum weight and the ranked separating items; the study plan records fit version, ε and q.
- [ ] `--sampling nominal` on the same study reproduces the plain paired comparison bit for bit.
- [ ] The plants `N24c-31:arena-i1` (ε = 0 admitted, forecast variance reported) and `N24c-31:arena-i2` (a promotion offered item-bank episodes) are caught by `wrong_model_unbiased` and `refuse_with_tier_correction`, and the controls seeded from ARENA-I1 and ARENA-I2 are reported caught.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-opt item_bank`:
- `exact_fixture_three_variances` (rationals); `exact_fixture_ratios` (13 and 3549/309 = 11.49).
- `wrong_model_unbiased`: 10⁴ replays of n = 100; mean within 3 standard errors of 1/20.
- `wrong_model_second_moment_is_ten_times`: 1 against 1/10.
- `weights_bounded_by_inverse_epsilon`: property over random p and models.
- `q_is_distribution`: sums to 1 exactly; positive wherever p is.
- `second_moment_formulas`: π_A = 0.9, π_B = 0.9 gives 0.18; π_A = 0, π_B = 1 gives 1.
- `draws_addressed`: draw j identical alone and among 10⁴ draws; index order equals completion-order-independent estimate.
- `uniform_equivalent_count`: on the exact fixture with q*, n_eq/n tends to 13.
- `refuse_fixed_n`, `refuse_in_qualification_before_commit`, `refuse_with_tier_correction`, `refuse_range_driven_sequence`, `rare_bound_refuses_item_bank_episodes`.
- `nominal_sampling_is_plain_comparison`.
- `zero_model_difference_floor`: a model predicting no difference anywhere gives q = p.
- `no_per_item_floor`: on the two-value fixture with a `second-moment-table` fit (1 on the discriminating items, 0 elsewhere) the per-draw variance is exactly 3/400 at ε = 0 (explain mode) and 309/36,400 at ε = 0.1; the control with a 10⁻⁶ floor gives 759/100,000 and is rejected.
- `epsilon_zero_refused_in_study`; `table_form_refused_on_real_battery`.
- `sparse_design_takes_class_mean`: a design with 19 rows gets θ equal to the mean of the fitted θ_d, not 0, on a fit whose θ mean is non-zero.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers (stated with the mechanism; no Appendix number yet; N24-KA's late records KA-ARENA-I1 and
KA-ARENA-I2, whose `pending: N24c-31` lines in the derivation files' front matter this bead replaces with the crate
and the tests `exact_fixture_three_variances`, `exact_fixture_ratios`, `wrong_model_unbiased` and
`wrong_model_second_moment_is_ten_times`): 0.0975, 0.0075, 0.0085; ratios 13 and 11.5; wrong-model variance
0.9975; weight bound 10; composed weight 200 if two estimators were multiplied.

Counterexamples (N24-CX's late rows, planted by this bead):
- ARENA-I1 (a good item model makes importance sampling safe by itself: under the disjoint wrong model each
  discriminating item has q = ε·p and weight 10, second moment 1.0, variance 0.9975 against 0.0975, 10.2 times
  worse and still unbiased; with ε = 0 those items are never drawn and the estimate is biased). Kind mutation, gate
  GATE-ARENA-12. Plant `N24c-31:arena-i1`, Cargo feature `arena-i1` of `frob-opt`, built only with
  `--features gauntlet-mutations`: the planner admits ε = 0 in a study and reports the model's forecast variance in
  place of the realised one. Caught by `wrong_model_unbiased` (the mean of Ĉ falls to 0 against 1/20) and by the
  gate's realised-width check, which the forecast no longer matches.
- ARENA-I2 (importance weights from two estimators may be multiplied: item-bank weights bounded by 1/ε = 10 and
  tier-correction weights by 20 compose to 200). Kind planted-input, through `rule_gates.toml` (§24.21). Plant
  `N24c-31:arena-i2`, the `synthetic` fixture `crates/frob-opt/tests/fixtures/arena_i2/`: the episodes of an
  item-bank comparison on the two-value battery, with their recorded weights (at most 10), offered as the pairs of a
  promotion under the tier-correction estimator (weights up to 20). Caught by `refuse_with_tier_correction`: the
  promotion is refused with `item-bank-with-tier-correction` and no composed weight is computed.

Negative controls: ε = 0 (biased under the wrong model: the discriminating items are never drawn); self-normalised
weights (biased at small n); a proposal recomputed from the study's own outcomes without a predictable-weights
argument; a range-driven sequence on the unwidened span (its level fails on the wrong-model fixture). Type-I
check: 10⁴ null comparisons (true contrast zero) with item-bank sampling and optional stopping stay at or below
the level. Metamorphic: permuting item labels changes nothing; scaling D by a constant scales Ĉ. Determinism:
q stored as exact rationals; draws class D0; the estimate inherits the episodes' class.

## Considerations

- The gate text asks for a variance ratio at or below 1/ε under the wrong model, while the fixture's own variance ratio is 10.23; the provable statement is the second-moment bound, which is what is asserted, with the discrepancy recorded.
- Freezing q at plan time gives up adaptivity for a simple validity argument; if the replay gate shows the saving is model-limited, adapting q between rounds with predictable weights is the next step and needs its own test.
- The independence assumption in E_model[D²] ignores common random numbers; that only affects efficiency.
- If the variance-adaptive sequence is not available the mode is not offered at all (fail closed), since the range-driven one makes it harmful.
