## Background

Item-bank sampling is a mode of the paired comparison verb: `eval.compare … --sampling item-bank` draws battery
items from a proposal built on a response model and corrects with importance weights, so that the contrast under
the battery's frozen item law is estimated without bias from fewer episodes. `eval.items` shows the bank and the
weights a comparison used. An agent sees the capability only through those two verbs, so its correctness has to
be shown there, with the real binary, and not only in unit tests of the arithmetic.

GATE-ARENA-12 (claim kind Known answer, first milestone M3) is the known-answer gate of the mechanism:

> The two-value fixture reproduces 0.0975, 0.0075 and 0.0085 in exact arithmetic; under the disjoint wrong model
> the mean of Ĉ over 10⁴ replays equals the true contrast within its Monte Carlo error and the variance ratio
> stays at or below 1/ε; a `fixed-n` request with item-bank sampling is refused.

The gate register lists this gate only in its table of gates added after the register was compiled (milestone
M3), without restating the threshold; the defining subsection quoted above governs it. This bead is the
end-to-end script that measures it from the user's side and exercises every refusal rule of the mode.

The fixture: a battery of 1,000 synthetic items with a uniform law; the paired difference D of two scripted
"designs" is +1 on 75 items, −1 on 25 items and 0 on the other 900, so the discriminating fraction is f = 0.1
and the mean is μ = 0.05. Three samplers: uniform (variance f − μ² = 0.0975), the ideal proposal q* (0.0075) and
the defensive mixture with ε = 0.1 (weight 10/91 on a discriminating item, variance 309/36,400 = 0.0085). A
fourth, the **disjoint wrong model**, puts q* on 100 items none of which discriminates; each discriminating item
then has q = ε·p and weight 10, and the variance is 0.9975.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/arena_item_bank.sh`, the synthetic two-value battery and its two scripted designs (labelled
`synthetic`), assertions, log, gate record.
Out: the replay gate on recorded episodes (N24c-33); the mechanisms (N24c-30, N24c-31).

## Technical approach

**Fixture construction (`scripts/e2e/fixtures/arena_item_bank/`).** A battery manifest of 1,000 items whose
episodes are trivial one-tick tasks with a deterministic outcome per (design, item): design A passes 525 items
and design B passes 575 so that the per-item differences are exactly +1 on 75, −1 on 25, 0 on 900. Three
prepared bank states, loaded as `synthetic` fit versions of the test-only form `second-moment-table` (N24c-31; no
logistic parameters give a second moment of exactly 0 or 1): `ideal` (E_model[D²] is 1 on the 100
discriminating items and 0 elsewhere), `wrong` (1 on 100 non-discriminating items and 0 on all others), and
`flat` (0 everywhere, so q = p). With no per-item floor these give the plan's numbers exactly; a floor of 10⁻⁶
under the square root would give 0.00759 and a wrong-model weight of 9.18 instead.

**Steps and assertions.**

| Step | Command | Asserts |
|---|---|---|
| `items_list` | `frob eval items --battery two-value --class synthetic --fit ideal --json` | exit 0; 1,000 rows with p = 1/1000; fit version present; sorted by discrimination |
| `exact_variances` | `frob eval compare A B --task <T> --battery two-value --sampling item-bank --fit {flat, ideal(ε=0), ideal(ε=0.1)} --explain-variance --key aib-x-<case> --json` | the planner's exact variance per draw equals 39/400, 3/400 and 309/36,400 as rational strings (0.0975, 0.0075, 0.0085) |
| `replay_ideal` | 10⁴ studies of n = 100 draws each with `--fit ideal --epsilon 0.1`, distinct study identities and distinct keys `aib-ideal-<j>` (a repeated key would return the first study's stored receipt) | mean of Ĉ within 3 standard errors of 0.05; sample variance of the per-draw observation within 5% of 0.0085; every weight ≤ 10 |
| `replay_wrong` | the same with `--fit wrong`, keys `aib-wrong-<j>` | mean of Ĉ within 3 standard errors of 0.05 (standard error √(0.9975/10⁶) ≈ 0.001); maximum weight exactly 10; second moment of the observation within 5% of 1.0, at most 1/ε times the uniform second moment 0.1; variance ratio to uniform reported (expected 10.23) |
| `items_for_comparison` | `frob eval items --battery two-value --class synthetic --comparison <id> --json` (`<id>` the stored comparison of one `replay_wrong` study) | q, w and draw counts per item; Σ q = 1; w = p/q; every w ≤ 1/ε |
| `refuse_fixed_n` | `frob eval compare A B --task <T> --battery two-value --fixed-n 64 --rule paired-clopper-pearson/1 --declared-verdicts prefer,inferior,inconclusive --sampling item-bank --key aib-r-fixed --json` (the row's fixed-n block) | exit 3, symbolic refusal `item-bank-in-fixed-n` |
| `refuse_qualify` | `frob eval qualify A --task <T> --claim '<claim>' --sampling item-bank --key aib-r-qual --json` | exit 3 before commitment; the campaign index (`frob eval ledger <campaign> --json`) unchanged |
| `refuse_tier_correction` | `frob eval promote <item-bank result> --tier referee --key aib-r-promote --json` (promotion's tier-correction estimator) | exit 3, `item-bank-with-tier-correction` |
| `refuse_range_sequence` | `frob eval compare … --sampling item-bank --sequence range --key aib-r-range --json` | exit 3, `item-bank-needs-variance-adaptive` |
| `refuse_rare_bound` | `frob nemesis bound A --task <T> --tier batch-cpu --target 1e-3 --mode certify --episodes <run set of the replay_wrong study> --key aib-r-bound --json` | exit 3, FROB-REF-560 (offered episodes drawn neither nominally nor with recorded bounded weights of the bound's own proposal) |
| `refuse_epsilon_zero` | `frob eval compare A B --task <T> --battery two-value --sampling item-bank --fit wrong --epsilon 0 --key aib-r-eps0 --json` | exit 3, invalid argument (ε outside (0, 1]); no draw made |
| `nominal_equals_plain` | the comparison with `--sampling nominal` twice, once on a build flag that disables the item-bank code path, under keys `aib-nom-1` and `aib-nom-2` | identical result digests |
| `schedule_invariance` | one item-bank study run with 1 worker and with all workers, and preempted once, under keys `aib-sched-1`, `aib-sched-all` and `aib-sched-pre` | identical estimate and identical item draw sequence |
| `agent_report` | read the result of one item-bank study | fields `episodes_used`, `uniform_equivalent`, `max_weight`, `separating_items[]` (with ‖a_i‖ and b_i) present; standing `provisional` |

The 10⁴ replays use the daemon's batch submission so the step takes seconds (each episode is one tick).

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/arena_item_bank/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N24c-32`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block (claim,
class, tier, determinism, identities, seed, fit version, ε). `metric` events with value, unit, expected value and
tolerance: the three exact variances (as rational strings), replay means with their standard errors, sample
variances, maximum weights against 1/ε, the second-moment ratio. `refusal` events with code expected and
observed. On failure: the first differing value, the study identity and the item draw sequence digest. The
`summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-ARENA-12"]`.

**Gate record.** The measured values (three exact variances; replay mean and standard error under the wrong
model; maximum weight; second-moment ratio and variance ratio; the refusal observed for the fixed-n request) are
written to the gate's receipt, which a session that did not implement the mechanism records with
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ARENA-12 --receipt <hash>`; the script itself
writes only the receipt. The options `--battery`, `--sampling`, `--epsilon`, `--fit`, `--explain-variance`,
`--sequence` and the fixed-n block of `eval.compare`, `--sampling` of `eval.qualify` (accepted only to be refused),
`--class`, `--fit` and `--comparison` of `eval.items` and `--episodes` of `nemesis.bound` are in their VERBS rows.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 and writes the log, transcript and summary; every step of the table is asserted.
- [ ] The exact variances are reported as 39/400, 3/400 and 309/36,400.
- [ ] Under the wrong model the replay mean is within three standard errors of 0.05 and no weight exceeds 10.
- [ ] The six refusal steps observe their refusals; the qualification refusal leaves the index unchanged.
- [ ] `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE=1` (run the wrong-model replay with ε = 0 on a `gauntlet-mutations` build carrying N24c-31's plant `arena-i1`, since a study otherwise refuses ε = 0) makes the script fail: the estimate collapses to 0 and the unbiasedness assertion reports it.
- [ ] `refuse_epsilon_zero`: `--sampling item-bank --epsilon 0` in a study is refused as an invalid argument; the same value in `--explain-variance` is answered.
- [ ] GATE-ARENA-12's receipt carries the measured values, including the reported variance ratio of the wrong-model case.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
A shell self-test checks the fixture generator: 75 + 25 + 900 items, sum of differences 50, law sums to 1.
Mechanism tests are in N24c-30 and N24c-31.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script; steps, assertions and log fields are given above.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: 0.0975, 0.0075, 0.0085; wrong-model variance 0.9975 and weight 10; true contrast 0.05. Negative
controls: ε = 0 under the wrong model (biased, caught); a planner that reports the model's forecast variance in
place of the exact one under the wrong fit (reports 0.0085 where the truth is 0.9975; the replay's sample
variance catches the difference); a fixed-n study that silently accepts the sampling flag. Determinism lanes:
twice on one machine; one worker and all workers; debug and release; Linux and macOS. Item draws are class D0 and
must be identical in every lane.

## Considerations

- The gate's wording "variance ratio at or below 1/ε" is not met literally by the mechanism's own counterexample (0.9975/0.0975 = 10.23); the script asserts the second-moment bound, which is the provable one, and records the variance ratio. The discrepancy is reported to the plan's owner through the coverage notes.
- The fixture's "designs" are scripted outcome tables, not robots; the gate is about the estimator, and the script says so in its evidence block (`synthetic`).
