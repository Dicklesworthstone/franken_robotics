## Background

Item-bank sampling claims that a response model of battery items lets a close paired comparison reach the same
interval width with several times fewer episodes, without bias. The arithmetic of the idealised case is a known
answer (a ratio of 13, or 11.5 with the defensive mixture, when a tenth of the items discriminate). Whether real
batteries behave like that is not known: with model error, the mixture and replicate noise the expectation is a
factor of 4 to 8 (HYPOTHESIS). The test of the hypothesis is a **replay study**: record a full matrix of
outcomes once, then replay comparisons out of it under item-bank sampling and under nominal sampling, where the
truth is the full matrix's own contrast.

GATE-ARENA-13 (claim kind Design comparison, first milestone M3):

> Replay on a recorded full matrix of about 40 designs × 150 items × 4 replicates (24,000 episodes): for the 20
> closest pairs, episodes to equal interval width lower by a factor of at least 4; coverage within 2 points of
> nominal; item parameters fitted on one family predict held-out designs better than per-item means.

The kill gate KILL-ITEMBANK reads the same three numbers: it fires if the factor is under 2 for the 20 closest
pairs, or coverage falls more than 2 points below nominal, or the held-out prediction is no better than per-item
means; the fallback is stratified sampling by interior archetype with proportional allocation, which needs no
model. This bead records the matrix, runs the replay under a protocol frozen beforehand, and writes the gate
record. It decides nothing.

A replay is used because the gate must measure **realised** interval width, not the model's own forecast of its
variance: a wrong model forecasts a saving it does not deliver.

## Scope

In: recording the matrix; the frozen protocol (pairs, splits, target width, replays); the three measurements;
the gate record; the script.
Out: the mechanism (N24c-30, N24c-31); the known-answer gate (N24c-32); the decision (N24c-34).

## Technical approach

Choices marked DESIGN operationalise the gate's wording and are frozen in a protocol file whose hash is committed
before the matrix is analysed.

**1. The matrix.** One morphology class (the quadruped class of the design corpus). About 40 designs: templates,
parameter perturbations and planted-defect designs from the corpus, chosen by a seeded draw so that both close
and distant pairs occur. About 150 items: the class's `standard` battery items (scene instance, task,
disturbance law, initial-condition family), with the battery's law p. 4 replicates per (design, item) at
distinct world addresses. Total about 24,000 episodes, run once through `eval.run` on a batched tier (the tier
form of N24cp3-01) under a replayable profile (`--determinism reference-replay` on `batch-cpu`), each scored by the task's requirement verdict (success outcome) and its margin (continuous
outcome). Episodes with `invalid` or `undetermined` verdicts are kept and counted; a (design, item) cell with
fewer than 4 valid replicates is reported and its pair comparisons use the valid ones.

**2. Truth.** For designs A and B: C_AB = Σ_i p(i)·(Ȳ_B,i − Ȳ_A,i), with Ȳ the mean over the recorded
replicates. This is the contrast of the finite recorded population; replays sample from that population, so the
truth is exact for the replay.

**3. The 20 closest pairs.** The 20 unordered pairs with the smallest |C_AB| among pairs whose designs both have
complete cells (DESIGN). Also recorded, for context and not for the gate: 20 pairs at the median |C| and the 20
most distant.

**4. Leave-candidate-out fits (DESIGN).** For a replayed comparison "candidate B against baseline A", the bank is
fitted on the matrix rows of every design except B (in real use a new candidate has no rows), θ_B is the class
mean, and q is built from that fit with ε = 0.1. Each closest pair is replayed in both roles.

**5. Replay.** For each (pair, role) and each sampler (item-bank q; nominal p): 500 replays (DESIGN), each drawing
(item, replicate) by an addressed stream, with replacement across items and without reuse of a replicate until
the item's four are exhausted (then with replacement), feeding the variance-adaptive betting confidence sequence
at level α = 0.05 with the widened span for the item-bank arm. Record, per replay, the number of episodes at which
the interval half-width first falls below the target W, where W is the half-width the nominal arm reaches in the
median at n = 300 episodes for that pair (DESIGN: a width both arms can reach inside the recorded data).

**6. Measures.**
- *Episode factor.* Per (pair, role): median episodes to W under p divided by median episodes to W under q. The
  gate statistic F is the geometric mean over the 40 (pair, role) values, reported with the count of values at or
  above 4, at or above 2, and below 1.
- *Coverage.* The fraction of replays, pooled over closest pairs and roles, whose interval at the stopping
  point contains C_AB, for each sampler. Required: item-bank coverage not more than 2 points below nominal
  (≥ 93% at α = 0.05).
- *Held-out prediction.* Split the designs into two families by the corpus's own family labels (for example
  templates and their parameter perturbations against planted-defect designs); fit on one, predict the other,
  and the reverse. Report held-out log loss of the fit against the per-item-mean baseline with a paired
  bootstrap interval over held-out designs. Required: lower log loss with an interval excluding zero in both
  directions.

**7. Thresholds.** Gate passes if F ≥ 4, coverage ≥ 93% and the fit beats per-item means. Kill-gate readings
(recorded): F < 2; coverage < 93%; fit no better than per-item means.

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/arena_gate_itembank.sh`: sources the shared helper; step `record_matrix`
(`frob eval run <design> --battery standard --tier batch-cpu --determinism reference-replay --run-seed <protocol seed>
--key gim-rec-<design> --json` per design, the
battery manifest giving the four replicate world addresses per item; long jobs waited on with `frob job wait`,
resumable because a re-submitted key returns the stored run); step `freeze_protocol`
(checks the protocol hash predates analysis); steps `fit_heldout`, `replay_closest`, `replay_context`; step
`gate_record`. Logs NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/arena_gate_itembank/`: per command the exit code, wall time and
`evidence` block; per (pair, role) the truth, W, median episodes under each sampler, the factor; pooled coverage
per sampler against 93%; held-out log losses and their interval; matrix accounting (episodes run, invalid,
undetermined); and a `summary` with F, coverage, the prediction result and `gates: ["GATE-ARENA-13"]`. On
failure: the pairs with the lowest factors and the replays that missed coverage, with their draw-sequence
digests.

**Gate record.** F with its count breakdown, coverage per sampler, held-out log losses, the matrix's battery
version, fit versions, protocol hash, commit and hardware fingerprint, recorded by a session that did not
implement the estimator with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ARENA-13 --receipt
<hash>`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The matrix is recorded once (about 24,000 episodes), with its accounting, and is reproducible from identities and seeds.
- [ ] The protocol file (pairs rule, W rule, replays, splits, thresholds) is hashed and committed before any replay.
- [ ] F, coverage and held-out prediction are measured as defined and recorded with their breakdowns.
- [ ] The nominal arm's coverage is at or above nominal (a check of the replay machinery itself).
- [ ] Negative controls are caught: with the bank fitted on labels shuffled across items (a wrong model) F falls to about 1 or below while coverage holds; with ε = 0 (on a `gauntlet-mutations` build carrying N24c-31's plant `arena-i1`, since a study otherwise refuses ε = 0) and the shuffled model, the weights are unbounded, the declared span no longer holds, and coverage fails; if it does not, the control is reported as uninformative rather than passed.
- [ ] The gate's receipt is written by a session that did not implement the estimator.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-opt replay`: `truth_is_matrix_contrast` (a 3 × 4 × 2 toy matrix by hand);
`closest_pairs_rule`; `episodes_to_width` on a synthetic stream (`synthetic`) with known variance: nominal needs
about 13 times the episodes of the ideal proposal on the two-value fixture at the same W; `coverage_counter`;
`leave_candidate_out_excludes_rows` (no row of B in the fit's row digest); `protocol_hash_precedes_analysis`.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead's script is `scripts/e2e/arena_gate_itembank.sh`, as specified above.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answer used to validate the replay code before it touches the matrix: the two-value fixture's ratio of 13
(q*) and 11.5 (mixture). Negative controls: the shuffled-label model (efficiency must vanish, unbiasedness and
coverage must not); ε = 0 with the shuffled model (coverage must fail, proving the coverage check can fail); a
replay that measures the model's forecast width in place of realised width (reports a saving under the shuffled
model, and is thereby caught). Determinism: the matrix is recorded under a replayable profile (class D2 on its
backend); replays are addressed draws (class D0), so every number of the gate reproduces on re-run.

## Considerations

- Twenty pairs of one class and one battery are a narrow sample; the gate is a pre-registered threshold on this suite and is reported as such. Extending to wheeled classes repeats the study, it does not reuse this result.
- Replicate noise bounds the factor: with 4 replicates per cell and noisy outcomes, choosing items cannot remove between-seed variance. If F lands between 2 and 4 the gate fails and the kill gate does not fire; the decision bead states what happens then.
- Compute: about 24,000 episodes on a batched tier plus cheap replays; recorded against the M3 envelope.
- The matrix is discovery data and may be reused as the bank's initial rows; it must never be drawn from a sealed family.
- If the item-bank pilot (N24c-29) is falsified, the bank is never built and this bead closes unrun with reason `fallback`; the kill-gate bead then records `fallback` from the pilot alone.
