## Background

Item-bank sampling models the items of a battery so that a close comparison can run mostly on the items that
separate the two designs, with importance weights keeping the estimate unbiased for the battery's frozen law. It
adds a fitted model, a fit version to every comparison's identity, a widened span to the confidence sequence and
five refusal rules. It is worth that only if the measured saving on real recorded outcomes is substantial,
coverage holds, and the model predicts designs it has not seen.

The kill gate KILL-ITEMBANK was written before any measurement:

| Trigger (any one, read at GATE-ARENA-13) | Fallback |
|---|---|
| The factor is under 2 for the 20 closest pairs; or coverage falls more than 2 points below nominal; or the held-out prediction is no better than per-item means | Stratified sampling by interior archetype with proportional allocation, which needs no model |

The kill gate has no milestone or register row of its own; it is decided at the milestone of its gate, M3. Its
inputs are the replay study on a recorded matrix of about 40 designs × 150 items × 4 replicates and, before
that, the paper-scale pilot. This bead applies the triggers, records the outcome and re-reads the dependents.

Note the two thresholds on the factor: the gate GATE-ARENA-13 passes at a factor of at least 4; the kill gate
fires below 2. Expected value 4 to 8 (HYPOTHESIS).

## Scope

In: the decision rule on the replay study's recorded numbers; the record; what is removed or kept; re-reading of
dependents; the instruction to build the fallback when it fires.
Out: the measurements (N24c-29, N24c-33); the fallback's implementation (N24c-35).

## Technical approach

**Inputs** (from the gate record of GATE-ARENA-13 and the pilot record).
- F: the geometric mean over the closest pairs (both roles) of the ratio of median episodes to a common interval
  width, nominal over item-bank.
- cov: the item-bank arm's pooled coverage of the true contrast at nominal 95%.
- pred: whether the fit's held-out log loss beats per-item means in both split directions, with its interval.
- The pilot's outcome (falsified or not).

**Rule.**
1. If the pilot's falsifier held and the item-bank beads were never funded: outcome `fallback`.
2. Trigger 1: F < 2.
3. Trigger 2: cov < 93% (more than 2 points below nominal 95%).
4. Trigger 3: pred is false (no better than per-item means in either direction).
5. Any trigger: outcome `fallback`. None, and the gate passed (F ≥ 4, cov ≥ 93%, pred true): outcome `kept`.
6. None, and the gate did not pass (2 ≤ F < 4 with coverage and prediction holding): outcome `kept-unproven`
   (DESIGN of this bead): the mode stays available behind an explicit flag with its measured factor printed in
   every result, is not the default for any verb, and is not counted as a lever in any speed claim; the gate's
   row stays `not passed`. One repair cycle (refit choices tuned on the pilot matrices, then a re-run on a newly
   recorded matrix) may upgrade it to `kept`; a second miss leaves it `kept-unproven`.

**On `fallback`.**
- The bank, the response-model fit, the proposal and estimator, `eval.items` and the `--sampling item-bank` mode
  close with reason `fallback` and are removed from the surface (the verb `eval.items` is unregistered; the
  sampling flag is refused as not built). The symbolic refusals that guarded the mode go with it.
- The fallback mechanism is built by its own bead (N24c-35): stratified sampling by interior archetype with
  proportional allocation.
- The recorded matrix is kept as discovery data.
- Battery items keep their identities and the battery keeps its law p; nothing about the estimand changes.

**On `kept`.** The mode stays Frontier and opt-in per comparison; the measured F, coverage and prediction gain
are recorded as its expected performance for that class and battery; other classes need their own replay before
the mode is used for them without the `unproven` mark.

**Record.** An architecture decision record under `docs/adr/` with F and its breakdown (how many pairs at or above
4, at or above 2, below 1), cov, pred with its interval, the triggers' truth values, the outcome, the dependents
re-read. The kill gate's row in the gate status file is set to decided with measured values. The bead closes with
reason `decided: kept`, `decided: kept-unproven`, or `fallback: stratified sampling by archetype`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rule is on file before the replay study's numbers are read (commit order checked).
- [ ] The record carries F, cov and pred with breakdowns and intervals, each trigger's truth value, and the outcome.
- [ ] On `fallback`: `eval.items` and `--sampling item-bank` are gone from the registry and refused as not built; the stratified fallback bead is unblocked; no result or document describes item-bank sampling as available.
- [ ] On `kept` or `kept-unproven`: every item-bank result prints the measured factor for its class and battery, or the `unproven` mark.
- [ ] Dependent beads are re-read with their new states recorded.
- [ ] The decision is recorded by a session that implemented neither the estimator nor the replay.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`kill_itembank_rule` (a pure function of (F, cov, pred, pilot)): (5.2, 0.95, true) → kept; (1.8, 0.95, true) →
fallback; (5.2, 0.92, true) → fallback; (5.2, 0.95, false) → fallback; (3.0, 0.95, true) → kept-unproven; pilot
falsified → fallback regardless. On the fallback branch: `item_bank_mode_refused_not_built`,
`eval_items_unregistered`.

### End-to-end script and logging
No script of its own. A `decide` step is appended to the log of `scripts/e2e/arena_gate_itembank.sh`: the command
that evaluates the rule on the recorded numbers, its output and the path of the decision record. On the fallback
branch `scripts/e2e/arena_item_bank.sh` is retired and the stratified-sampling script takes its place.

### Known answers and negative controls
The thresholds are the known answers: 2 and 4 for the factor; 93% for coverage at nominal 95%. Negative control
on the decision: a factor computed from the model's forecast variance in place of realised width is not
accepted as F (the replay study's own control enforces this, and the decision checks that the control was
caught).

## Considerations

- The `kept-unproven` state is this bead's addition for the band between the kill threshold and the gate threshold, which the plan leaves undefined; it keeps a mechanism that is unbiased and somewhat useful without letting it be cited.
- A fallback outcome costs nothing downstream: comparisons default to nominal sampling throughout, and the qualification lane never used the mode.
- If the compute for the 24,000-episode matrix is not approved by M3, the fail-closed outcome is that the mode is not offered (treated as `fallback` for surface purposes) until the study runs.
