## Background

This bead exists only on one branch of a decision. Item-bank sampling (a fitted response model of battery items,
used to importance-sample items for close comparisons) has a kill gate, KILL-ITEMBANK: if on the replay study
the episode-saving factor is under 2 for the 20 closest pairs, or coverage falls more than 2 points below
nominal, or the model's held-out prediction is no better than per-item means, the mechanism is not shipped, and
its named fallback is **stratified sampling by interior archetype with proportional allocation, which needs no
model**. If the kill gate does not fire, this bead closes as not needed.

Why this fallback: batteries at M3 are organised by scene class, and for interior tasks the scene class is the
interior archetype (home, apartment, office, warehouse, commercial kitchen, manufacturing plant, retail store).
Designs often differ by archetype (a wide base fails in the galley kitchen and passes in the warehouse), so the
paired difference varies more between archetypes than within them. Stratifying on the archetype removes the
between-archetype part of the variance with no model, no fit version, no weights above 1 and no extra failure
mode: with proportional allocation the estimator's variance is never larger than under simple sampling. The
saving is modest and bounded by the between-stratum share; that is the honest price of needing no model.

Tier Solid (classical survey sampling). Owner: `frob-opt` for the sampler and estimator, `frob-arena` for the
strata (they come from the battery manifest).

## Scope

In: strata from the battery; proportional allocation; the block design that keeps anytime validity; the
estimator and per-stratum report; the sampling mode of `eval.compare`; the admission rules.
Out: the paired-comparison machinery (its own bead); the item bank (removed on this branch); coverage-driven
selection of which scenes a battery contains (a different mechanism).

## Technical approach

**Strata.** Each battery item carries a scene class in the battery manifest; for interior batteries the scene
class is the interior archetype. Stratum h has weight W_h = Σ_{i ∈ h} p(i), an exact rational, where p is the
battery's frozen item law. Strata with W_h = 0 do not exist; an item with no scene class is a manifest error.

**Estimand.** Unchanged: C = Σ_i p(i)·E[D_i], the contrast under p, with D_i = Y_B,i − Y_A,i the paired
difference on item i (both arms on the same world address).

**Block design (DESIGN of this bead).** Let M be the least common denominator of the W_h. If M ≤ 64, a **block**
is M paired episodes: M·W_h of them from stratum h, each drawn from p restricted to the stratum (probability
p(i)/W_h), by the counter-based stream `study/item` keyed by (study identity, block index, position). The
block's observation is the plain mean of its M differences (proportional allocation is self-weighting). If
M > 64, allocations are rounded to a 64-episode block by largest remainders with at least one episode per
stratum, and the block's observation is X = Σ_h W_h·D̄_h, where D̄_h is the mean of the block's differences in
stratum h; this is unbiased for any allocation that gives every stratum at least one draw.

Properties, each tested as a law:
1. E[X] = C (unbiased), for both forms.
2. X is a convex combination of differences, so it lies in the span declared for D: no widening of the
   confidence sequence's span, unlike item-bank sampling.
3. Blocks are independent and identically distributed, so the block observations feed the same anytime-valid
   betting sequence as nominal sampling, with blocks as the units; stopping is allowed at block boundaries only.
4. Variance identity. With S² the per-draw variance of D under p, S_h² the variance within stratum h and μ_h its
   mean: S² = Σ_h W_h·S_h² + Σ_h W_h·(μ_h − C)². The exact-proportional block mean has variance
   (1/M)·Σ_h W_h·S_h² ≤ S²/M; the saving factor against simple sampling is S²/Σ_h W_h·S_h² ≥ 1.

**Hand cases (exact).**
- Two strata of weight 1/2. In the first, D = +1 on a fifth of the items and 0 otherwise; in the second, D = 0
  everywhere. C = 1/10. Simple sampling: S² = 1/10 − 1/100 = 9/100. Within-stratum: 1/2·(1/5·4/5) + 0 = 2/25.
  Between: 1/2·(1/10)² + 1/2·(1/10)² = 1/100. Saving factor (9/100)/(2/25) = 9/8.
- Strata of weight 1/10 and 9/10; D = +1 everywhere in the first and 0 everywhere in the second. C = 1/10;
  S² = 9/100; within-stratum variance 0: a block of 10 episodes returns exactly 1/10 every time.
- One stratum: the mode reduces to nominal sampling bit for bit.

**Admission rules (fail closed, DESIGN).** The mode is admitted for `anytime` studies on discovery worlds. It is
refused for `fixed-n` studies (the exact rule's enumeration assumes simple sampling of pairs) and inside the
blinded qualification lane (confirmation draws from p on fresh sealed clusters); a rare-failure bound refuses
its episodes as it refuses any episode not drawn nominally; it is not combined with the tier-correction
estimator in one estimate. Refusals use the symbolic name `stratified-sampling-not-admitted` with the reason,
numbered by the refusal registry (exit code 3; repair: `--sampling nominal`).

**Verb surface.** `eval.compare A B --task T --battery <b> --sampling stratified`. The study plan records the
strata, W_h, M and the block size. Default sampling stays `nominal`.

**What an agent reads.** The verdict and confidence sequence; blocks and episodes used; the estimated saving
factor from the realised within- and between-stratum variances; and the per-stratum mean difference D̄_h with
its count, which says in which archetype the two designs differ. Evidence: kind Design comparison, class
`Tested`, standing `provisional`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three hand cases reproduce exactly: saving factor 9/8; zero variance with a constant return of 1/10; identity with nominal sampling for one stratum.
- [ ] Unbiasedness: over 10⁴ replays on a synthetic battery (`synthetic`) with seven strata of unequal weights, the mean estimate is within three standard errors of the true contrast, for both block forms.
- [ ] The variance identity holds on recorded data: realised block variance matches (1/M)·Σ W_h·S_h² within sampling error and never exceeds the nominal variance beyond sampling error.
- [ ] Type-I error at or below α under optional stopping at block boundaries over 10⁴ null comparisons.
- [ ] Requests in a `fixed-n` study, in the qualification lane and to a rare-failure bound are refused.
- [ ] The result reports per-stratum differences and the realised saving factor; the estimate is independent of scheduling order and worker count.
- [ ] On the recorded replay matrix (when it exists) the realised saving factor for the 20 closest pairs is reported as a measurement, with no threshold attached.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-opt stratified`:
- `hand_case_nine_eighths`, `hand_case_zero_variance`, `single_stratum_is_nominal`.
- `weights_exact_rationals_sum_to_one`; `block_allocation_proportional` (M = 10 with weights 1/10, 3/10, 6/10 gives 1, 3, 6).
- `large_denominator_uses_weighted_form`: weights with M = 1,000 round to a 64-episode block with every stratum present; estimator unbiased (10⁴ replays).
- `observation_within_span_of_d`: property test.
- `variance_identity_property`: random strata; total = within + between to 10⁻¹².
- `addressed_draws_block_independent`: block k is identical alone and among 10⁴ blocks.
- `refuse_fixed_n`, `refuse_qualification`, `rare_bound_refuses`.
- `stop_only_at_block_boundary`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/arena_stratified_sampling.sh` (sourcing the shared helper; built on this branch only): a synthetic
seven-archetype battery; `frob eval compare … --sampling stratified --json`; asserts the hand cases through the
verb, the refusals and schedule invariance. Logged: commands, exit codes, wall time, the `evidence` block, strata
weights, block size, the estimate against the true contrast, realised within- and between-stratum variances,
the saving factor, refusal codes, and on failure the first block whose draws differ between two runs.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: the hand cases (9/100, 2/25, 1/100, factor 9/8; the exact 1/10). Negative controls: an estimator
that averages stratum means without weights (biased when weights are unequal: with weights 1/10 and 9/10 and
stratum means 1 and 0 it returns 1/2 where the truth is 1/10); a sampler that stops inside a block (the partial
block is not proportional and the plain mean is biased); strata taken from outcomes instead of the manifest
(post-hoc strata make the estimate depend on the data). Metamorphic: relabelling strata changes nothing;
splitting a stratum into two with the same content never increases variance. Determinism: draws class D0; the
estimate inherits the episodes' class.

## Considerations

- The saving is the between-stratum share of the variance and may be near 1 for designs that differ uniformly across archetypes; it is reported, not promised. The plan's expectation of 4 to 8 was for the item bank and does not transfer.
- This bead is built only if the kill gate fires; if the item bank is kept, the archetype breakdown it would have given agents is already available from scorecards by scene class.
- Admitting the mode in fixed-n studies would need its own exact enumeration under stratified sampling; that is deliberately not attempted.
- If a battery has many tiny strata the block grows; the cap of 64 with the weighted form bounds it.
