## Background

Exposure domains say what kind of entity each randomisation draw belongs to: a `field` is sampled once per world
at stable coordinates; a `process` is exogenous in physical time; an `event` is tied to a declared mission event;
a `private` draw is noise of one port. The coupling test (`task.check --coupling`) checks that a task's draws
behave as declared: per-arm marginals unchanged by the coupling, no change when unused draws, a sensor or a limb
are inserted, no change when the physics step is halved, and correct behaviour under a retimed controller.

GATE-ARENA-14 (claim kinds Known answer and Design comparison; M2 for the fixture, M3 for the battery) is:

> Fixture: a slick strip over x ∈ [2.0, 2.5] m (`field`, per 0.25 m tile), a push at t = 3 s (`process`), a push
> at the first crossing of a gate at x = 5 m (`event`), a baseline controller and one retimed from a 0.5 s to a
> 0.4 s gait period. With 1,000 unused draws inserted, the step halved and a limb added: tile values
> bit-identical; the timed push at 3 s in every arm; the gate push at each arm's crossing; per-arm outcomes
> indistinguishable from independent sampling in a two-sided test at level 0.01 on 4,096 worlds. The tick-keyed
> and footfall-keyed controls are refused. On a held-out slip-and-dock battery the variance of the contrast is at
> most half that of named streams keyed per stream and tick, at equal count.

This bead builds the fixture and the end-to-end script and records the **fixture half** of the gate at M2. The
battery half (the variance clause) is measured at M3 with the coupling-choice bead. The gate has no row in the
central register's tables; its defining subsection governs it.

## Scope

In: the fixture task, scene, arms and controls; `scripts/e2e/arena_exposure_coupling.sh`; the M2 gate record.
Out: the held-out slip-and-dock battery and the variance clause (N24c-40); the mechanisms (N24c-36, N24c-37).

## Technical approach

Constants marked DESIGN complete the gate's description and are frozen in the fixture manifest.

**Fixture `fixture:exposure-coupling` (`assets/fixtures/exposure-coupling/`).**
- *Scene.* A straight lane along +x, 8 m long, 1.5 m wide (DESIGN), nominal friction 0.8. A slick strip over
  x ∈ [2.0, 2.5] m: floor friction is a `field` with a cell size of 0.25 m; the two tiles covering the strip draw
  their friction from a uniform law on [0.15, 0.35] and all other tiles from [0.7, 0.9] (DESIGN ranges, written
  as a card uncertainty). A gate region at x = 5 m with role `region:gate`.
- *Disturbances.* A lateral push of impulse m × 0.3 m/s (m the robot's mass; DESIGN) at t = 3 s, domain
  `process`. A lateral push of the same law at the first crossing of the gate, domain `event`, role
  `region:gate`, event `first-crossing`. Push directions (left or right) are fair draws in their domains.
- *Task.* Walk from x = 0 to x = 6.5 m within 20 s and stand; requirement with upright and no-strike hard limits
  and a 1 s dwell at the goal; physics step 2 ms; requirement clock 1/50 s.
- *Arms.* Arm A: the reference quadruped with its baseline controller at a gait period of 0.5 s. Arm B: the same
  design with the controller retimed to a 0.4 s gait period (a different `ControllerId`). Variants for the
  insertion checks: the task with 1,000 unused registered draws; arm A with an extra unconsumed sensor; arm A
  with a one-joint tail (an added limb).
- *Controls (must be refused).* `control-tick`: the timed push implemented by an adapter keyed by control tick
  1,500 (3.0 s at 2 ms; 1.5 s at 1 ms). `control-footfall`: the timed push keyed by the tenth footfall (5.0 s at
  a 0.5 s gait period; 4.0 s at 0.4 s).

**Script `scripts/e2e/arena_exposure_coupling.sh`** (sources the shared helper; idempotent; run seed 23;
non-zero exit on failure).

| Step | Command | Asserts |
|---|---|---|
| `compile` | `frob task new …/exposure-coupling.ftask --json`; `frob task show --section randomization` | every draw has a domain; an exposure table version is reported; `coupling: untested` |
| `study_refused_untested` | `frob eval compare A B --task exposure-coupling --json` | exit 3, `exposure-table-untested`, `next_actions` names the coupling test |
| `coupling_test` | `frob task check exposure-coupling --coupling --arms A,B --worlds 4096 --json` (wait on the job) | exit 0; four checks `passed`; status `passed` |
| `insertion_values` | read the record | tile-value digests identical across the base run, the 1,000-unused-draws variant, the extra-sensor variant and the added-limb variant |
| `rate_values` | read the record | tile-value digests identical at 2 ms and 1 ms; the timed push at exactly 3 s (rational `3`) in both |
| `retiming_values` | read the record | timed push at 3 s for arm A and arm B; gate push time equals each arm's own recorded first-crossing time (the two differ); gate push magnitude identical across arms for the same world |
| `marginals` | read the record | for each arm, the verdict test and the margin test against independent sampling have p ≥ 0.01 on 4,096 worlds (or pass after the single confirmation run, logged) |
| `control_tick_refused` | `frob task check control-tick --coupling …` | exit 3, `coupling-test-failed`, failed check `rate`, first differing value 3.0 s against 1.5 s |
| `control_footfall_refused` | `frob task check control-footfall --coupling …` | exit 3, `coupling-test-failed`, failed check `retiming`, 5.0 s against 4.0 s |
| `static_order_key_refused` | `frob task new` on a task whose draw declares `:key footfall-index` | exit 3, FROB-REF-556 |
| `study_admitted` | `frob eval compare A B --task exposure-coupling --worlds 64 --json` | admitted (exit 0, 2 or 8); the result lists event times per arm |
| `table_change_resets` | edit one draw's cell size (a new `TaskId`); `frob task show` | `coupling: untested` |

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/arena_exposure_coupling/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N24c-38`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block (claim,
class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). `metric` events: push times in seconds as exact rationals per arm
and step size; first-crossing times per arm; p-values with the 0.01 threshold; counts of worlds. Digests of
tile-value sets for each variant. `refusal` events with code and failed check expected and observed. On failure:
the first differing tile (cell index, both values), or the first world whose push time differs, with the
`.frun` paths. The `summary` names `GATE-ARENA-14 (fixture, M2)`.

**Gate record.** The fixture half's measured values: digest equality across the three insertions and the halved
step; push times; crossing times per arm; the four p-values; the two controls' refusals with their failed checks.
The battery half is left `not run` with its milestone.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 with every step asserted, and writes the log, transcript and summary.
- [ ] Tile values are bit-identical across the base run and the four perturbations (1,000 unused draws, extra sensor, added limb, halved step).
- [ ] The timed push is at 3 s in every arm and at both step sizes; the gate push is at each arm's own crossing with equal magnitude.
- [ ] Both arms pass the marginal test at level 0.01 on 4,096 worlds.
- [ ] The tick-keyed and footfall-keyed controls are refused, each on the check named above; the order-keyed declaration is refused at compile time.
- [ ] A paired study is refused before the test and admitted after it; a table change resets the status.
- [ ] `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE=1` (the script then expects the tick-keyed control to pass) makes it fail with the first differing time logged.
- [ ] The fixture half of GATE-ARENA-14 is recorded with measured values.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
A shell self-test checks the fixture: the strip covers exactly tiles 8 and 9 (x ∈ [2.0, 2.25) and [2.25, 2.5)
at a 0.25 m cell); the gate is at x = 5 m; arm B's `ControllerId` differs from arm A's; the two controls differ
from the fixture only in the push's key. Mechanism tests are in N24c-36 and N24c-37.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script; steps, assertions and log fields are given above.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: tick 1,500 is 3.0 s at a 2 ms step and 1.5 s at 1 ms; the tenth footfall is at 5.0 s under a
0.5 s gait period and 4.0 s under 0.4 s. Negative controls: the two keyed controls; `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE=1`; a
build with a call-order generator (the insertion digests then differ; run in the nightly lane through the
conformance harness's mutation switch). Metamorphic: mirroring the lane and both arms mirrors push directions
and leaves every time unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; one worker and all workers; debug and
release; `batch-cpu` and, when present, each device tier, with digests compared inside each tier's class
(`field` values and push times are class D0 and must be identical on every tier).

## Considerations

- The marginal check is statistical: with two arms and two statistics at level 0.01 there is about a 4% chance that a correct coupling needs the confirmation run, and about 4 in 10⁴ that the step fails spuriously; the log records when the confirmation run was used.
- The check needs 4 × 4,096 episodes of 20 s; on `batch-cpu` this is the script's dominant cost, and `E2E_QUICK=1` runs it at 512 worlds without writing the gate record.
- The fixture uses the reference quadruped; if the fixture-2 body decision introduces a dedicated minimal body, this fixture keeps the reference template, since it tests exposures and not soles.
