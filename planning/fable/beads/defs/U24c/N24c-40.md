## Background

A task's randomisation draws each declare an exposure domain, and the compiler lowers them to an exposure table
that a four-part coupling test must pass before any paired study may use the task. For some draws more than one
legal keying exists: floor friction can be a `field` keyed per tile or per fixture (one value for a whole strip
or mat). Both are legal; they share different amounts of exposure between the two arms of a comparison and so
give different paired correlations ρ and different contrast variances, Var(D) = 2σ²·(1 − ρ) for equal marginal
variances σ².

The **choice among legal couplings** (Frontier) is made on evidence and then frozen: the choice is made on
pilot addresses by the measured variance of the contrast; the table version is then locked; confirmation
addresses are drawn only afterwards; both marginals and the contrast variance are recorded. Locking before
drawing is what keeps the choice from being tuned on the worlds that then judge the designs.

The mechanism's worth is measured by the second half of GATE-ARENA-14 (M3): **on a held-out slip-and-dock
battery the variance of the contrast is at most half that of named streams keyed per stream and tick, at equal
count.** Its kill gate KILL-EXPODOMAIN fires under a factor of 2 on that battery, or if the automatic choice
picks a coupling that fails the test in any pilot; fallback: the keyed streams as they stand, with explicit
per-stream adapters, the four checks kept as a test. This bead builds the choice, the lock, and the battery
measurement. Funded after the coupling-choice pilot. Owner `frob-arena`.

## Scope

In: declaring alternatives; the choice procedure and its record; the lock and its ledger ordering; the held-out
slip-and-dock battery and the variance measurement against the per-stream-and-tick baseline; the M3 gate record.
Out: the domains and compiler (N24c-36); the four checks (N24c-37); the fixture half of the gate (N24c-38); the
decision (N24c-41).

## Technical approach

**1. Declaring alternatives (DESIGN syntax).** A draw may list more than one legal key:

```lisp
(draw strip-friction :from card:rubber-60a/wet-tile:uncertainty :stream world.material
      :domain field :alternatives ((cell 0.25 m) (fixture strip-1)))
```

A task with unresolved alternatives is a **coupling family**: it has no `TaskId` usable by a study until one
alternative per draw is chosen and locked. A draw with one key needs no choice.

**2. Choice procedure** (`frob task check <family> --coupling --choose --arms A,B --pilot-worlds n`).
1. Enumerate candidate tables (the product of alternatives; refused above 16 candidates, DESIGN cap).
2. Run the four-part coupling test on each candidate for the arms. Candidates that fail are excluded and listed
   with the failed check.
3. **Marginal agreement across candidates.** For each arm, compare outcomes between every pair of surviving
   candidates (the same two-sample tests as the coupling test, level 0.01). Candidates whose per-arm marginals
   differ are not alternatives of one coupling but different world models; the procedure then stops with the
   finding `alternatives-change-marginals` and the author must pick the model by hand.
4. On the **pilot addresses** (n worlds from a pilot run seed that is recorded as consumed; default 1,024), run
   both arms under each surviving candidate; compute each arm's outcome variance, the paired correlation ρ̂ and
   Var(D) with a bootstrap interval.
5. Choose the candidate with the smallest Var(D); ties within sampling error go to the candidate that shares
   less (the finer key: `cell` before `fixture`), because it assumes less about the world (DESIGN).
6. Record everything: candidates, test results, both marginals per candidate, ρ̂, Var(D), the choice, the pilot
   seed.

**3. Lock.** The chosen keys are written into the task; the resulting exposure table version and `TaskId` are
registered with status `locked`, and a lock record is appended to the ledger. Any address derivation for a
confirmation or estimation run on this task must have a ledger sequence number above the lock record's; a study
whose addresses were derived before the lock, or that reuses the pilot run seed, is refused (symbolic
`coupling-choice-after-draw`; exit code 3; repair: draw fresh addresses). Re-choosing is a new family version
and a new lock.

**4. The held-out slip-and-dock battery (`battery:slip-and-dock-holdout`).** Tasks that combine a low-friction
crossing with a timed dock under disturbances: a slick patch (`field`), a timed push (`process`), a push at a
gate crossing (`event`), sensor noise (`private`), on held-out interior scenes (galley and warehouse aisle
variants from the held-out side of the scene split). Arm pairs: 10 pairs (DESIGN) of a design and a close
variant or of one design under two controllers, frozen with the battery. Equal count: N worlds per pair
(default 1,024), the same N for both keyings.

**5. Measurement.**
- *Domain arm*: the battery's tasks with their exposure tables (alternatives chosen and locked on pilot
  addresses as above), paired by world address.
- *Baseline arm*: the same tasks with every draw taken from named streams keyed per stream and tick, that is by
  (world address, step, stream identifier) with no domain semantics (a test-only compiler mode that lowers each
  draw to its stream's per-tick sequence).
- For each pair compute Var(D) of the paired outcome difference under each arm of the measurement, at equal
  count, with bootstrap intervals; and each design's marginal success rate under both (they must agree: the
  baseline keying is not illegal for marginals, it merely pairs nothing).
- Gate statistic: R = the pooled ratio Σ_pairs Var_domain(D) / Σ_pairs Var_baseline(D). Required R ≤ 1/2. Also
  reported: the per-pair ratios and the count of pairs at or below 1/2.

**6. Script and record.** `scripts/e2e/arena_gate_coupling_battery.sh` (sources the shared helper): steps
`choose` (on the gate fixture's family and on each battery task with alternatives), `lock`,
`refuse_pre_lock_addresses`, `run_domain`, `run_baseline`, `variance_ratio`, `gate_record`. NDJSON under
`target/e2e-logs/arena_gate_coupling_battery/`: commands, exit codes, wall time, `evidence` blocks; per
candidate its test results, marginals, ρ̂ and Var(D); the choice and the lock's ledger sequence; per pair the two
variances with intervals and the ratio; R against 0.5; a `summary` naming `GATE-ARENA-14 (battery, M3)`. On
failure: the pairs with the highest ratios and the draws whose keying differs between the arms.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the toy of the pilot (one critical foothold, same-cell probability s = 3/4) the procedure measures Var(D) near 1/8 per tile and near 0 per fixture against 1/2 for the baseline, confirms equal marginals and chooses per fixture; with marginals made unequal it stops with `alternatives-change-marginals`.
- [ ] A candidate that fails any of the four checks is never chosen (asserted on a family that includes a tick-keyed alternative).
- [ ] The lock precedes every confirmation address derivation in ledger order; a study on pre-lock addresses or on the pilot seed is refused.
- [ ] Both marginals and the contrast variance are recorded for every candidate.
- [ ] On the held-out slip-and-dock battery R ≤ 1/2 at equal count, with the per-pair breakdown recorded; the battery half of GATE-ARENA-14 is written with measured values.
- [ ] Marginal success rates agree between the domain arm and the baseline arm of the measurement.
- [ ] The measurement's negative control is caught: with the domain arm replaced by the baseline keying under another name, R is about 1 and the gate fails.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena coupling_choice`: `enumerate_candidates_product`; `cap_on_candidates`;
`failed_candidate_excluded`; `marginal_disagreement_stops_choice`; `choose_smallest_variance`;
`tie_goes_to_finer_key`; `lock_record_precedes_addresses`; `pilot_seed_cannot_be_reused`;
`variance_ratio_pooled` (two pairs with variances (0.2, 0.5) and (0.1, 0.1): R = 0.3/0.6 = 1/2);
`toy_exact` (`synthetic`: 1/2, (1 − s)/2, 0 for s = 1/4, 1/2, 3/4).

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead's script is `scripts/e2e/arena_gate_coupling_battery.sh`, as specified above.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: the toy's exact variances; Var(D) = 2σ²·(1 − ρ) with the factor of ten between ρ = 0.50 and
0.95; the two keying counterexamples (tick 1,500 at 3.0 s against 1.5 s; the tenth footfall at 5.0 s against
4.0 s). Negative controls: the renamed-baseline arm (R ≈ 1); a choice made on confirmation addresses (refused by
the lock ordering); a candidate that copies one arm's exposure to the other (smallest variance, different
marginals: must be excluded by step 3, never chosen). Metamorphic: swapping the arms leaves Var(D) and the
choice unchanged. Determinism: draws are class D0; both measurement arms run under a replayable profile so the
variances reproduce on re-run.

## Considerations

- The gain depends on how much exposure a pair of arms physically shares; pairs of very different bodies gain little under any keying. The battery's pairs are close variants on purpose, which is where paired comparison matters, and the report says so.
- Choosing by minimum variance on a finite pilot can pick a candidate by noise; the consequence is efficiency only, because every surviving candidate is legal and marginal-preserving.
- The baseline "per stream and tick" is the keyed-stream scheme without domain semantics; it is a test-only mode and is never offered to studies.
- If the kill gate fires, this bead closes as `fallback`: tasks carry one hand-declared key per draw and explicit per-stream adapters, and only the four checks remain.
- Confirmation in the blinded lane draws its own fresh sealed clusters after the lock by construction; the lock record is part of what the campaign's request commits to.
