## Background

This bead exists only on one branch of a decision. KILL-FOIL (§24.20) reads the gate run on 30 planted-loophole
and 30 clean tasks and fires on any of three triggers: detection below 60% (fewer than 18 of 30 planted tasks
found); findings on more than 30% of clean tasks (more than 9 of 30); or the sampling controller on the actual
design finds the same loopholes at the same cost, so foil bodies add nothing. Its named fallback is **the
in-campaign check on winning episodes only; `task.audit` runs the actual design alone**. The task-audit pilot has
its own, earlier falsifier: if no finding on its ten authored tasks is accepted by an author, **only the
in-campaign check is built**. The decision bead (N24c-26) records which outcome holds; this bead carries out the
`fallback` outcome, so that the decision record stays a decision and the removal is a piece of work with its own
tests.

Two cases reach this bead, and they differ in what exists:

- **Gate-fired fallback.** The foil library (N24c-23) and the audit search (N24c-24) were built for the gate run.
  The foil-specific parts are removed; the search's actual-design path (sampling controller, archive, budget,
  cell-to-finding rule) is kept as the audit's search, as the fallback's words "`task.audit` runs the actual design
  alone" require.
- **Pilot-falsified fallback.** Nothing of the foil library or the search was built (they closed with reason
  `fallback` unbuilt). Nothing is removed; the work is the capability's description, the verb's `arms` surface and
  the end-to-end profile.

On both, the Solid parts are untouched: intent invariants and the in-campaign check (N24c-21), the audit record,
findings, resolution and the qualification refusal for unaudited tasks or open findings (N24c-22), and the idle arm
(N24c-27, which runs the actual design and has its own gate). If the decision is `kept`, this bead closes with
reason `not taken`.

## Scope

In: removal of the foil-specific parts on the gate-fired branch; the `task.audit` arms surface and the refusal of
the foil arm as not built; the capability's description; the default profile of the audit's end-to-end script;
the state of the dependent beads' records.
Out: the decision and its record (N24c-26); every Solid mechanism of the audit (named above), which this bead must
leave byte-for-byte unchanged in behaviour; the M3 count of accepted findings over the real task families, which
is still taken with the remaining arms (by the task-family beads).

## Technical approach

**1. Removal (gate-fired case).**
- `assets/foils/` (templates, the relaxed-member recipe) is removed from the asset tree; their identities stay in
  the content store and in the audit records that cite them, so past findings remain replayable by hash.
- The foil-only design component `wrench-site` is removed from the design language's admitted components. A design
  that still carries it, or the `foil: true` marker, is refused at load as an unknown field (the existing lint), so
  nothing that could be offered as a foil can be built.
- The candidate-admission check for the marker (`foil-as-candidate`) is removed with the marker: there is nothing
  left to refuse. The symbolic refusal stays registered as retired in the Arena refusal table (N24-REF), never
  reused.
- The audit search (N24c-24) loses its foil inputs (the `wrench-site` spline parameterisation and the bounded
  initial twist of foils) and its `--arms foil`; its actual-design path, archive, budget accounting and
  cell-to-finding rule are kept unchanged, and their tests keep passing.

**2. The verb surface (both cases).** `task.audit --arms` accepts `actual`, `idle` and `in-campaign`; `--arms foil`
is refused with the symbolic code `audit-arm-not-built` (exit code 3, `retry: never` in this build; message names
the decision record; repair: use `--arms actual`). The default `arms` is every arm available in the build, so a
caller with no `--arms` gets the fallback's arms without change. On the pilot-falsified branch `--arms actual` is
also absent (not built) and is refused the same way, since the pilot's consequence is that only the in-campaign
check is built beyond the Solid idle arm. `--list-bodies` (N24c-23), accepted only with `--arms foil`, goes with
the foil arm: on the gate-fired branch it is refused with the same `audit-arm-not-built` (it would name the foil
arm), and on the pilot-falsified branch, where N24c-23 was never built, it keeps answering FROB-REF-724, exit 5.

**3. The capability's description.** The `capabilities` output and the verb's help name the fallback: "task audit:
in-campaign check and actual design only" (gate-fired) or "task audit: in-campaign check and idle arm only"
(pilot-falsified). The audit record's `arms` field lists what ran; a record never claims a foil arm.

**4. Budget.** The audit's default budget stays at most 5% of the task's reference campaign, now spent on the
actual design (gate-fired) or on the idle arm (pilot-falsified).

**5. End-to-end profile.** `scripts/e2e/arena_task_audit.sh` (N24c-28) already has a fallback profile
(`AUDIT_FOILS=0`). This bead makes `fallback` the default profile of the build (read from `frob capabilities`, not
from the environment), keeps `AUDIT_FOILS=1` as a refusal check (the script asserts `audit-arm-not-built` instead
of running foil steps), and names the fallback in the script's `summary` line.

**6. Records.** The foil beads' records (N24c-23, N24c-24) are annotated with the decision record's identifier;
the gate fixture of N24c-25 is retained for a future re-run under a recorded change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Gate-fired case: no verb, schema or asset offers a foil; a design carrying `wrench-site` or `foil: true` is refused at load; `assets/foils/` is gone and past audit records still replay their foil episodes by hash from the content store.
- [ ] `task.audit --arms foil` (with or without `--list-bodies`) is refused with `audit-arm-not-built`, naming the decision record; the default arms are the fallback's.
- [ ] The Solid parts are unchanged: the unit tests of N24c-21, N24c-22 and N24c-27 pass unmodified, and the actual-design path of the search passes its own tests (gate-fired case).
- [ ] The capability's status line and the audit record name the fallback; no record claims a foil arm.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/arena_task_audit.sh` runs its fallback profile by default, asserts the refusal when foils are requested, and exits 0.
- [ ] Pilot-falsified case: nothing is removed; `--arms foil` and `--arms actual` are refused as not built; the in-campaign check and the idle arm run.
- [ ] `foil-as-candidate` is marked retired in the refusal table and `audit-arm-not-built` is registered.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena audit_fallback`:
- `foil_arm_not_built_refusal`: `--arms foil` → exit 3 with `audit-arm-not-built`; `--arms foil --list-bodies` → the same refusal, no `DesignId` returned (gate-fired case).
- `audit_runs_actual_design_only`: with no `--arms`, the record lists `actual`, `idle`, `in-campaign` (gate-fired) or `idle`, `in-campaign` (pilot-falsified).
- `wrench_site_refused_at_load`; `foil_marker_refused_at_load`.
- `past_foil_findings_replay_by_hash`: an audit record from before the removal replays its sled finding from the content store.
- `capability_line_names_fallback` (both cases).
- `solid_parts_unchanged`: the identities of the intent-invariant library and the finding schema are equal before and after the removal.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/arena_task_audit.sh` in its fallback profile (N24c-28's script, profile switched by this bead): steps
`fallback_arms_listed`, `foil_arm_refused`, `in_campaign_check`, `idle_hand_case`, `qualify_refused`,
`qualify_admitted`, and `actual_design_audit` on the gate-fired branch. The three steps this bead adds:
`fallback_arms_listed` (`frob capabilities --json`, then `frob task audit <reach-goal-dwell TaskId> --key
fb-audit --json` with no `--arms`; the record's `arms` are the fallback's), `foil_arm_refused` (`frob task audit
<reach-goal TaskId> --arms foil --key fb-foil --json`; exit 3, `audit-arm-not-built` naming the decision record)
and `actual_design_audit` (`frob task audit <reach-goal TaskId> --arms actual --design <d> --key fb-actual --json`,
gate-fired branch only; on the pilot-falsified branch the same call is refused like `--arms foil`). Logged per the
shared helper (NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/arena_task_audit/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`): `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N24cp1-01`), `step`,
`event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, the arms listed by `frob capabilities`, the
refusal's symbolic code observed and expected, the decision record's identifier, and on failure the first step
whose arms or refusal differ. The `summary` line names the profile `fallback` and the case.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: the kill gate's thresholds as recorded by N24c-26 (18 of 30, 9 of 30) appear in the decision record
this bead cites. Negative controls: a build that keeps `wrench-site` admitted (caught by
`wrench_site_refused_at_load`); a removal that also deletes the content-store objects of past foil episodes
(caught by `past_foil_findings_replay_by_hash`); a fallback that drops the in-campaign check or the qualification
refusal (caught by the end-to-end steps); an e2e script that selects its profile from the environment alone
(caught by running it with `AUDIT_FOILS=1` against the fallback build, which must assert the refusal, not pass
foil steps).

## Considerations

- The removal must not reopen the qualification lane to unaudited tasks: the rule "no unaudited task, no open finding" holds on both branches.
- Past findings made with foils remain valid findings of their tasks; accepted clauses stay accepted. Only the ability to make new ones goes.
- A later re-run of the gate (for example with new foil bounds) is a recorded change that reopens the foil beads; this bead's removal is then reverted by that change, not silently.
- Fail-closed: if the decision record is missing, this bead does not start; the build keeps whatever was decided last.
