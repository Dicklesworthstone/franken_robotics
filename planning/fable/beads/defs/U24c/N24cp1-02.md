## Background

This bead exists only on one branch of a decision. The exposure-domain mechanism (§24.22) has every randomisation
draw of a task declare one of four domains (`field`, `process`, `event`, `private`), a compiler that lowers the
declarations to a versioned exposure table, a four-part coupling test that gates paired studies, and a Frontier
choice among legal couplings that is made on pilot addresses and locked before confirmation addresses are drawn.
Its kill gate KILL-EXPODOMAIN fires if the contrast variance on the held-out slip-and-dock battery is not at
least halved against named streams keyed per stream and tick, or if the automatic choice picks a coupling that
fails the test in any pilot. Its named fallback is **the keyed streams of §35.9 as they stand, with explicit
per-stream adapters; the four checks are kept as a test**.

The decision bead (N24c-41) records the outcome and defines what the fallback means. This bead carries it out, so
that the decision stays a decision and the change to the task language and compiler is a piece of work with its
own tests. If the decision is `kept`, this bead closes with reason `not taken`.

## Scope

In: the adapter form in the task's `randomization` section and its admission; replacing the compiler's
automatic lowering by the adapters; withdrawal of `task.check --coupling --choose` and of the lock; the four
checks re-pointed at adapters and still gating paired studies; the end-to-end re-run of the gate fixture with
adapters; migration of existing tasks.
Out: the decision and the fallback's definition (N24c-41); the keyed streams themselves (N35-24) and the stream
registry (N35-23), which are kept as they stand; the four checks' algorithms (N24c-37), which are reused, not
changed.

## Technical approach

**1. The adapter form** (DESIGN reading of "explicit per-stream adapters", as N24c-41 defines it). For each
stream a task draws from, the author writes in `randomization` the mapping from the physical entity to the
stream's key, in the vocabulary of the four domains, with no compiler inference behind it:

```lisp
(randomization
  (adapter scene-field         :key (tile (cell 0.25 m)))         ; friction read where the contact point lies
  (adapter disturbance-process :key (time 3 s))                    ; a push at a physical instant
  (adapter disturbance-event   :key (event region:gate first-crossing))
  (adapter sensor-noise        :key (port imu))
  (draw floor-friction :from interface:rubber-60a/tile#mu :stream scene-field)
  …)
```

The adapters name rows of the stream registry (`registries/streams.toml`, N35-23) by their `name`, which the
fallback keeps as it stands; a name with no row is FROB-REF-103 at the adapter, as it is at a draw's `:stream`
(N24c-36). `:from` keeps BD-200's form (a card field, or a registry row such as N24cp3-02's
`registry:disturbances/push#impulse_per_mass`).

An adapter is ordinary task data and part of the `TaskId`. A draw whose stream has no adapter is refused
(symbolic `stream-adapter-missing`; exit code 3; repair: write the adapter). Keys based on the order of contacts,
footfalls or draws, on a control tick or on a training counter stay refused with FROB-REF-556. Where a task had
`:alternatives`, the author keeps one by hand.

**2. Lowering.** The compiler's automatic lowering of domain declarations is removed; the exposure table is now
produced verbatim from the adapters (one row per draw: stream, key schema, source distribution), with its own
table-format version. The table's bytes remain part of the `TaskId` and its `coupling` status is kept as before.

**3. Withdrawal.** `task.check --coupling --choose` is refused as not built (symbolic `coupling-choice-not-built`,
naming the decision record); lock records already in the ledger stay readable, and studies keyed under a locked
table keep their identities and replay. If the coupling-choice pilot was falsified, `--choose` was never built and
only the refusal is added.

**4. The four checks kept.** `task.check --coupling` runs marginal, insertion, rate and retiming invariance on
adapter-keyed tables exactly as before (N24c-37's algorithms, unchanged), and `study.plan` and `eval.compare`
still refuse a paired study on a task whose table has not passed (`exposure-table-untested`).

**5. Migration.** Every task in the repository whose `randomization` uses domain declarations without adapters
is rewritten mechanically: each declaration becomes the adapter it compiled to under the last `kept` build, so
the drawn values are unchanged for every address (checked by digest). Rewritten tasks get new `TaskId`s with
lineage edges to the old ones.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tasks compile from explicit adapters; a draw with no adapter is refused with `stream-adapter-missing`; an order-keyed or tick-keyed adapter is refused with FROB-REF-556.
- [ ] `task.check --coupling` still runs the four checks on adapter-keyed tables and still gates paired studies.
- [ ] The gate fixture of N24c-38, rewritten with adapters, passes `scripts/e2e/arena_exposure_coupling.sh` unchanged in its assertions, and its tick-keyed and footfall-keyed controls are still refused on the same checks.
- [ ] `--choose` is refused with `coupling-choice-not-built`; earlier lock records and studies still replay.
- [ ] Migrated tasks draw bit-identical values at every address of a 64-address sample, and their lineage edges are recorded.
- [ ] No document or verb help describes automatic lowering or the choice as available; both new symbolic refusals are registered in the Arena refusal table.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena expo_fallback`:
- `adapter_task_compiles`; `adapter_in_task_id` (changing an adapter's cell size changes the `TaskId`).
- `adapter_missing_refused`; `order_keyed_adapter_refused` (FROB-REF-556 for `:key (footfall 10)` and `:key (tick 1500)`).
- `adapter_task_passes_four_checks` on the fixture's task.
- `choose_mode_not_built`; `old_lock_record_readable`.
- `migration_preserves_draws`: for each migrated task, field values per cell, process event times and event magnitudes equal by digest at 64 addresses.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/arena_exposure_coupling.sh` (N24c-38) re-run on this branch with the fixture's task rewritten with
adapters, plus two steps added by this bead: `choose_refused` (`frob task check <family> --coupling --choose
--arms A,B --pilot-worlds 1024 --key cp2-choose --json` → exit 3, `coupling-choice-not-built`, the worded refusal
the `task.check` row lists for `--choose` after this fallback; the action is of kind `run`, so the call carries a
key) and `adapter_missing_refused` (`frob
task new <fixture task with one draw lacking its adapter> --key cp2-missing --json` → exit 3,
`stream-adapter-missing`; the fixture's own `task.new` calls carry keys `cp2-new-<variant>`). Logged per the shared helper (NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/arena_exposure_coupling/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`): `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N24cp1-02`), `step`,
`event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, tile-value digests per variant, push times
per arm and step size as exact rationals, p-values against 0.01, refusal codes expected and observed, the decision
record's identifier, and on failure the first differing tile or push time with the `.frun` paths. The `summary`
names the profile `fallback: adapters`.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: the fixture's (tile values bit-identical under the three insertions and the halved step; the timed
push at 3 s; the gate push at each arm's crossing); the two keying counterexamples (tick 1,500 at 3.0 s against
1.5 s; the tenth footfall at 5.0 s against 4.0 s). Negative controls: an adapter keyed by visiting order
(refused at compile time, and if hidden behind a custom function, caught by checks 3 and 4); a migration that
re-keys a draw (caught by `migration_preserves_draws`); a build that keeps `--choose` reachable (caught by
`choose_mode_not_built`).

## Considerations

- The fallback does not license order-based keys; it only removes inference. Authors now write what the compiler used to infer, and the checks verify it.
- Migration changes `TaskId`s; results under old identities stay valid for their tasks and are not silently re-attributed.
- The measured R and the choice log stay on file in the decision record; a later recorded change may restore the compiler, reverting this bead's withdrawal explicitly.
