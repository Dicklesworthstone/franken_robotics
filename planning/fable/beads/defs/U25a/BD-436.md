## Background

A shared neural network can save training when robots differ. The stronger goal is to tell a design agent
which part of a closed-loop skill can be transferred to another body, and why. Two registered verbs transport,
and their scopes differ. `mission.transport` moves a certified claim through an interface map with a bounded
error (Frontier): its output is a proof object or a counterexample, and nothing in it is learned.
`skill.transport` moves a skill artifact (`.fskill`) to another body with a learned residual (Moonshot). A
skill moved by `skill.transport` carries no certified claim across unless `mission.transport` has issued the
interface certificate for the same pair of bodies. Neither stands in for the other.

**The object.** A skill is represented at its task interface by an observable state, an action or wrench
interface, reachable predecessor and terminal sets, and clock and environment assumptions. A source robot A
and a new embodiment B may realize the same interface through different internal contact modes.

**The relation.** Seek a relation R(x_A, x_B) and a causal refinement map from abstract commands to target
efforts such that: (1) every admitted initial target state is related to an abstract state compatible with its
observable history; (2) for each permitted abstract command, the implementable interface selects one target
command using only information available before disturbances act; (3) for every admitted target disturbance
and every resulting target successor, there is an allowed abstract successor related to it, respecting any
shared disturbance law; (4) observations, task outputs, timing and target progress are preserved. This is an
alternating simulation: the controller chooses, then the environment chooses. A target with successors
{stable, fall} cannot refine an abstract stable-only transition by selecting just its stable branch.
Approximate relations carry error and timing budgets small enough to preserve the mission's acceptance
predicates.

This is the largest step beyond the exact skill layer (which, for one body and contact mode, returns a
feasible allocation or a separating infeasibility witness before any training). It is off the critical path;
nothing before M8 depends on it.

## Technical approach

1. **Short plan first.** The epic opens with a written plan of at most a few pages: planned weeks, the pilot
   and its falsifier, the ceiling, the compute envelope requested, and the stop rule (an epic that has not
   passed its pilot within its planned weeks returns to the register of queued ideas and frees its slot).
2. **The static core [F]** (child; first form at M4). For a carried tray: match tray pose, velocity, allowable
   contact wrench and acceleration limits. A quadruped and a wheeled carrier have fundamentally different
   support transitions, so only the tray stabilization objective and its feedback state are ported, and only
   when the target's reachable wrench set at the tray interface covers the requested one. That coverage test
   is a polytope or cone containment, certified with `lp-primal` and `lp-farkas`; residual motions are
   resolved by whole-body inverse dynamics.
3. **The counterexample output.** At a ramp crest the target may lose lateral authority. The verb returns that
   contact mode, the requested wrench, the attainable set and a rerunnable counterexample, which localizes the
   required retraining or body edit to one mode.
4. **Pilot before funding** (child): the tray interface between one wheeled carrier and one quadruped on the
   CPU referee, with the ramp-crest loss of authority planted. Falsifier: the interface arm needs as many new
   target trials as the conditioned-policy arm, or it reports coverage where the referee shows a fall.
5. **The experiment that decides funding** (GATE-AUGUR-23): three treatments at equal compute and equal source
   data: training from scratch; an ordinary cross-morphology conditioned policy with full transfer learning;
   interface-based transfer with limited adaptation. Record new trials to target, irrecoverable failures, task
   success, contact impulse and energy. Freeze the target morphology and terrain before tuning. A successful
   transfer of a skill segment does not certify the remainder of the mission.
6. **The learned residual [M]** (child, with GATE-REFLEX-09): a frozen, morphology-conditioned residual in task
   space above the exact allocation program, with inputs made dimensionless by the body's mass m₀, a length
   ℓ₀ and gravity g (momentum scale m₀·√(g·ℓ₀), angular-momentum scale m₀·ℓ₀·√(g·ℓ₀)). Pilot: 24 target
   bodies of one class, three treatments at equal compute and source data, about 40 GPU-hours. Both cost
   denominators are reported: the incremental cost per target and the amortized cost with pretraining (a
   pretraining cost P is repaid after P/(c_b − c_t) targets, with c_b and c_t the per-target costs of the
   baseline and of transfer).
7. **Chance claims** are transported only with a coupling budget (child, owned elsewhere); without it the verb
   carries universal claims only.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Owned by the children. The epic requires of them: the {stable, fall} refinement fixture is refused; the tray
coverage test accepts a covered wrench set with an `lp-primal` witness and rejects an uncovered one with an
`lp-farkas` dual; the ramp-crest fixture returns the lost contact mode.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_transport_tray.sh` (owned by the child N25c-04, which first runs N25c-02's
`scripts/e2e/mission_transport_static.sh` as a stage) drives `frob mission transport` on the tray
interface for the wheeled-to-quadruped pair, asserts the certificate or the counterexample, and replays the
counterexample on the referee. It logs the interface definition's digest, the coverage verdict per contact
mode, and the replay address, through the shared helper of STD-E2E. The epic's closing check reads the gate
records and the two ADRs.

### Known answers and negative controls

Skill exact layer, by hand: a planar two-link arm with link lengths 0.4 m and 0.3 m at joint angles 0 and π/2
has tool point (0.4, 0.3) m; a tool force of (10, 0) N needs joint torques (−3, −3) N·m; with each actuator
limited to 2.5 N·m the witness is direction (1, 0), support value 25/3 N against the required 10 N, binding
actuator joint 2. Negative controls: a target with successors {stable, fall} accepted as a refinement; a
coverage report where the referee shows a fall.

## Considerations

- Ceiling, stated before any speed test: the published record for policy inheritance across single-limb
  topology edits is 33 to 100% of from-scratch steps, so the saving on a changed-support transfer may be nil;
  the claim is localization of what must be retrained.
- KILL-AUGUR-TRANSPORT: the interface arm needs as many target trials as the conditioned policy, or reports
  coverage where the referee shows a fall. Fallback: family policies and inheritance; the wrench-coverage test
  is kept as a static analysis. KILL-SKILL: GATE-REFLEX-09 fails. Fallback: keep the exact layer and its
  witnesses; drop the learned residual.
- OPEN-AUGUR-10: no Moonshot slice is funded before its pilot passes; pretraining needs an owner-approved
  envelope with its ceiling and kill rule written first.
