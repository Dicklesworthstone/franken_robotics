## Background

Some missions cannot be guaranteed for every admitted world and are asked for with a probability: "succeed for
at least 95% of manufactured units". A probabilistic statement is three statements in one, and omitting any of
them makes it uncheckable: the **population** the probability is over, the **event** whose probability is
bounded, and the **confidence level** of the evidence. A worst-case bound over an interval of a material
parameter and a probability over manufactured units are different statements and use different binders.

The second hazard is composition. A mission of several stages either preserves the joint law across stages or
allocates failure budgets that add up to the mission allowance. The smallest case that shows why: among 20
equally likely worlds, two stages each pass in 19. If their failed worlds differ, 18 of 20 missions pass. If
they coincide, 19 of 20 pass. Independent stages with those marginal rates give 0.95² = 0.9025. If each stage
instead has conditional success of at least 0.95 at every history that reaches it, the product is a lower
bound. Marginal stage certificates alone do not identify mission reliability, so the subsystem reports joint
episode outcomes when it has them and refuses to multiply marginals without a stated dependence assumption.

DESIGN and its reason: the kernel offers exactly four ways to combine stage evidence, each with its
precondition, and refuses anything else. Rejected alternatives: multiplying stage pass rates by default (true
only under independence, and optimistic or pessimistic otherwise); always using the union bound (sound, and
needlessly weak when joint outcomes are available).

## Scope

In: semantic rules for the `prob` binder; exact evaluation over finite listed populations; the four stage
combinations; the hand-off of empirical cells to the study types; refusals 846 and 855 as raised here.
Out: the study machinery itself (BD-124); sealed confirmation; randomized policies under guaranteed prefixes
(N25a-03); transport of chance claims between systems (a later section's coupling budget).

## Technical approach

1. **The binder.** `Binder::Prob { var, population: PopulationId, at_least: Ratio, confidence: Ratio }`. The
   bound variable's world component must have `kind = "population"` with a named law; a `prob` binder over a
   `bounded-set` component is refused. Missing population, event (the acceptance predicate the probability is
   of) or confidence: `FROB-REF-846`, first repair "the joint-outcome request" (the fields to add, shown
   filled in for the query at hand). Exit 3.
2. **Finite populations, exactly.** When the population is a finite list of worlds with rational weights, the
   probability of the event under a fixed policy is computed by enumeration in exact rationals and compared
   with `at_least` as declared (`≥`). The cell's proof is an enumeration record; its class is `Certified`, and
   `confidence` is 1 by construction.
3. **Stage combination.** For stages S₁ … S_k and a mission allowance δ on the failure probability:

   | Mode | Precondition | Result |
   |---|---|---|
   | `joint` | Whole-mission episodes, or the joint law of stage outcomes, are available | P(all stages pass), exactly or as an estimate with its bound |
   | `additive-budget` | Stage failure budgets δᵢ with Σ δᵢ ≤ δ, each stage certified to fail with probability at most δᵢ | Mission failure at most Σ δᵢ (no dependence assumption is needed) |
   | `conditional-product` | Each stage has conditional success at least pᵢ at every history that reaches it | Mission success at least Π pᵢ |
   | `declared-independence` | The query states `dependence: independent` for the stage outcomes | Π pᵢ, with the assumption printed in the answer and in the certificate |

   Any other request to combine marginal stage rates is refused with 846. The mode used is recorded per cell.
4. **Empirical cells.** With `evidence.profile = "empirical"` a cell's support is a study of one of two types,
   never a mixture: a fixed-n exact study with its reachability pre-analysis (the verdicts the rule can reach
   at the planned n are enumerated before sampling), or an anytime-valid study. The kernel emits a study
   request and receives counts; the cell records `sample_coverage = { successes, n, population, method }` and
   `confidence` in fields separate from its geometry, with class `Estimated`. The bound is recomputed from the
   counts: for a one-sided exact binomial bound at level α with zero failures in n trials, the upper bound on
   the failure probability is 1 − α^(1/n).
5. **Profiles are not mixed inside a cell** (`FROB-REF-855`; repair: split the cell). A probability mass over
   the design domain (for example "what share of the unresolved set") is reported only under a weighting of
   topology classes and continuous parameters that the caller supplied (855; repair: supply the weighting).
6. **Confirmation.** A mission answer that will be cited as qualified is confirmed on sealed worlds through
   the blinded qualification lane; until then an empirical cell is a development result and says so.

Determinism: exact enumeration is D0; empirical cells inherit the determinism class of their study. Evidence
classes as stated; the answer's class is the weakest.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A `prob` binder without population, without event or without confidence is refused with 846 and the
      filled-in joint-outcome request.
- [ ] Twenty-world fixture: `joint` returns 18/20 when the failing worlds differ and 19/20 when they coincide;
      `additive-budget` returns "at least 18/20"; `declared-independence` returns 361/400 with the assumption
      printed; a bare request to multiply 19/20 by 19/20 is refused with 846.
- [ ] `conditional-product` returns a value that is at most the exact joint probability on 1,000 generated
      finite stage trees.
- [ ] Zero failures in 20, 30 and 59 trials at α = 0.05 give failure upper bounds 0.1391, 0.0950 and 0.0495.
- [ ] An empirical cell prints coverage and confidence in separate fields; a cell that mixes an exact proof
      and a sample bound is refused with 855.

- `prob_over_bounded_set_refused`.
- `twenty_worlds_joint`: stage 1 fails in world 7; stage 2 fails in world 12 gives 18/20; stage 2 failing in
  world 7 gives 19/20.

- `independence_must_be_declared`: without the declaration 846; with it 361/400 and the label.

- `empirical_fields_are_separate`; `mixed_profiles_refused_855`; `mass_without_weighting_refused_855`.
- `study_types_never_mixed`: a request for a fixed-n verdict at an anytime-valid stopping time is refused.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_admission.sh` (bead N25a-23) submits the twenty-world mission in its four forms through
`frob mission synthesize` and asserts the three values and the 846 refusal; it logs the combination mode, the
exact fraction, the refusal code and its first repair.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-631 (twenty worlds, two stages each passing 19 of 20: 18/20 if the failures differ, 19/20 if they coincide;
0.9025 only under independence; negative control: multiplying stage rates). CX-276 (both stages succeed 95% of
the time, so the mission succeeds 90.25%). Negative controls registered: a mutant that multiplies marginals by
default; a mutant that defaults a missing confidence to 0.95. Determinism: the enumeration results are exact
and identical across runs and platforms.

## Considerations

- A claim such as "failure under 5%" is unreachable with fewer than 59 failure-free trials at α = 0.05; the
  pre-analysis says so before any world is run, and the request is refused at registration.
- A rare admissible world can be a useful counterexample while carrying negligible operational probability;
  admissibility and priors are separate inputs and are reported separately.
- The union bound is the fail-closed default when a stage's dependence on earlier stages is unknown.
