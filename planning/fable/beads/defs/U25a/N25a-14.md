## Background

The first mission slice is the M1 exit demonstration of the mission subsystem: a fresh agent is given a
mission; through the command line or MCP it changes the allowed motor authority, the read error and the
delivery tick, asks for the feasible sensor–actuator region, and explains which hardware restriction to relax.
Its gate, GATE-AUGUR-07 (TARGET), reads: a fresh agent given only the generated documentation completes 10
scripted docking and stance amendments, names the binding hardware restriction in at least 9, and never
reports an unresolved cell as a verdict; the serial turn count is recorded against a second arm restricted to
the lower verbs. The stop decision attached to it is severe: if a fresh agent cannot complete the amendments
after one repair cycle, the mission chapter is re-planned before physics is added, because the mapping from
sensors and actuators to the query is then the defect.

A gate like this is only as good as its script. The plan fixes the count, the two families and the pass
criteria; it does not list the amendments. This bead fixes them (DESIGN), derives each ground truth by hand in
exact arithmetic, and freezes both before any agent run. The truths are checked by an exact evaluator that is
independent of the product, so that the gate cannot pass by the product agreeing with itself.

The two base missions. **Docking:** a unit cart starts at rest and takes three one-second ticks with
acceleration g·u_k, hidden gain g ∈ {1, 2} fixed per execution, commands bounded by abs(u_k) ≤ U; it must be at
position 1 with velocity 0 after tick 3. The only informative packet is the first velocity reading
v₁ = g·u₀ with additive error in [−ε, +ε]. With p = u₀, the terminal conditions force u₁ = 1/g − 2p and
u₂ = p − 1/g; the bounds for both gains give 1 − U ≤ p ≤ (U + 1/2)/2 on U ∈ [1/2, 1]; the reading sets,
centred at p and 2p, must be disjoint, p > 2ε. With the packet on time a robust causal policy exists exactly
when ε < (U + 1/2)/4, equality excluded; with the packet late none exists. Base values: U = 3/4, ε = 1/8,
packet before the second command. **Stance:** 400 N on four point feet at (±1/3, ±b) m, centre of mass in a
150 mm × 75 mm half-rectangle (first-moment vertices (±60, ±30) N·m), each foot independently commanded in
[0, 160] N; with v = 1/b the family is feasible exactly when 90 + 15·v ≤ 160, that is v ≤ 14/3.

## Scope

In: ten amendment cards (prose briefs shown to the agent), ten sealed truth records, the rubric, the exact
evaluator that validates the truths, and the freeze record. Out: running the agents and recording the gate
(BD-140); the product mechanisms.

## Technical approach

Each card is a short brief in plain language with no tool hint. Each truth record holds: the verdict category
(`attainable`, `not-attainable`, `region-with-unknown`), the accepted names of the binding hardware
restriction, the smallest relaxing changes, and whether the product's answer contains unresolved cells.

| # | Amendment (as briefed) | Truth | Binding restriction (accepted names) | Smallest relaxing change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Docking: map the feasible region over U ∈ [1/2, 1] and ε ∈ [0, 1/2], four cells per axis | Of 16 cells: 7 inner, 4 excluded, 5 unresolved (they straddle ε = (U + 1/2)/4) | The sensor error against motor authority, ε < (U + 1/2)/4 | Split the five straddling cells; nothing to relax |
| A2 | Docking: a cheaper velocity sensor, ε = 5/16, U = 3/4 | Not attainable: equality; both worlds can read 15/16 at p = 5/8 | Sensor read error, or motor authority | ε below 5/16, or U above 3/4; neither threshold is attained |
| A3 | Docking: the packet arrives after the third command is committed | Not attainable for any U and ε: one command vector cannot dock both gains | Delivery time of the velocity packet | Deliver it before the second command |
| A4 | Docking: motor authority reduced to U = 1/2, ε = 1/8 | Attainable with the single probe p = 1/2: commands (1/2, 0, −1/2) for gain 1 and (1/2, −1/2, 0) for gain 2 | Motor authority (zero reserve: the probe range is one point) | None needed; any increase of U restores reserve |
| A5 | Docking: manufacturing spread admits a third gain 33/32; ε = 1/64, U = 3/4 | Exact docking not attainable; least worst-case position error 1/65 m | The read error against the gain spread, or the exact target | A declared tolerance of at least 1/65 m, or ε below 5/512 |
| A6 | Stance: narrow the body to half-width 1/5 m | Excluded: a foot would need 165 N | Foot force rating | Rating 165 N on every foot, or half-width at least 3/14 m |
| A7 | Stance: passive equal-spring suspension, half-width 1/4 m | Excluded: the springs deliver (175, 115, 85, 25) N | Load-sharing authority (passive suspension), or foot rating | Active allocation, or rating 175 N, or half-width at least 1/2 m |
| A8 | Stance: every foot must keep at least 10 N | Minimum half-width becomes 1/4 m, with forces (160, 130, 100, 10) N at the vertex (60, 30) | Foot rating together with the force floor | Not applicable (a new boundary, v ≤ 4) |
| A9 | Stance: foot ++ is derated to 150 N | Minimum half-width becomes 1/4 m (90 + 15·v ≤ 150) | Rating of foot ++ | Restore 160 N, or widen to 1/4 m |
| A10 | Stance with a knee: load 160 N, feet at (±1/5, ±1/v) m, moments (±16, ±12) N·m, ratings 88 N, knee hold (1/10)·f ≤ 4·r with belt ratio r ∈ [1, 5/2] free, v ∈ [4, 12], paving 4 × 3; what is the narrowest body? | Best certified half-width 1/6 m; possibly as narrow as 1/8 m; three cells unresolved; gap 1/24 m | Foot rating 88 N for the ultimate limit v ≤ 8; knee holding torque (belt ratio at least 11/5 at v = 8) | Split cell (6, 8] × [2, 5/2] at r = 11/5 |

Derivations kept with the truth records (exact rationals):
1. A1: with feasibility increasing in U and decreasing in ε, a cell is inner when its worst corner (least U,
   greatest ε) is feasible and excluded when its best corner is infeasible. Rows of ε: [0, 1/8] gives 4 inner
   cells; (1/8, 1/4] gives 3 inner and one unresolved (the cell U ∈ [1/2, 5/8] contains the infeasible point
   (1/2, 1/4)); (1/4, 3/8] gives 4 unresolved; (3/8, 1/2] gives 4 excluded.
2. A2: p ≤ (U + 1/2)/2 = 5/8 and p > 2ε = 5/8 cannot both hold; at p = 5/8 the readings 5/8 + 5/16 and
   5/4 − 5/16 coincide at 15/16.
3. A3: the terminal position of a common command vector scales with g.
4. A4: 1 − U = (U + 1/2)/2 = 1/2 and 1/2 > 2ε = 1/4.
5. A5: the reading (65/64)·p is possible under gains 1 and 33/32 for every first command, so both receive the
   same remaining commands; their final positions are c and 33c/32, and the least worst-case error is 1/65 m at
   c = 64/65. Separating the two gains would need p/32 > 2ε with p ≤ 5/8.
6. A6 to A9: from t = f₊₊ ≥ 90 + 15·v against the rating, with the floor φ adding φ to the left side; under
   equal springs the largest force is 145 + (15/2)·v.
7. A10: every balanced allocation has f₊₊ − f₋₋ = 40 + 6·v; feasible exactly when 40 + 6·v ≤ min(88, 40·r).

**Rubric.** An amendment is *completed* when the agent's final statement gives the correct verdict category.
The binding restriction is *named* when the statement contains one of the accepted names (matched by an
evaluator session that did not build the product, using the truth record). A *false verdict* is any sentence
that presents an unresolved cell as attainable or as unattainable; one such sentence fails the gate. Turn
counts are serial tool calls from the first brief to the final statement.

**Freeze.** The cards and truth records are committed by hash (`gate07.freeze.json`: file digests, date,
authoring session) before any agent run. The truth directory is outside the working directory the agent sees.

**Validation.** `frob_conformance::fixtures::gate07` recomputes every truth with its own exact arithmetic (the
pilot evaluators re-run under BD-041 and BD-042 plus the closed forms above) and fails if a record disagrees.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Ten cards and ten truth records exist; the freeze record lists twenty digests.
- [ ] The exact evaluator reproduces every truth: A1 counts 7, 4, 5; A2 shared reading 15/16; A4 the two
      command sequences reach (1, 0); A5 error 1/65 m; A6 165 N; A7 (175, 115, 85, 25) N; A8 and A9 half-width
      1/4 m; A10 incumbent 1/6 m, lower bound 1/8 m, gap 1/24 m.
- [ ] At least two cards (A1, A10) have answers with unresolved cells, so the "never a verdict" clause is
      exercised.
- [ ] Changing any number in a card or a truth record changes its digest and fails the freeze check.
- [ ] No card names a verb, a flag or a file format.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate07_fixture.rs`: `a01_cell_counts_7_4_5`; `a02_equality_reading_15_16`;
`a03_no_common_vector`; `a04_single_probe_sequences_dock` (v₃ = g·(u₀ + u₁ + u₂) = 0 and
x₃ = g·(5u₀/2 + 3u₁/2 + u₂/2) = 1 for both gains); `a05_worst_error_1_over_65`; `a06_needs_165`;
`a07_passive_175`; `a08_floor_boundary_v4`; `a09_derated_boundary_v4`; `a10_gap_1_over_24`;
`freeze_digests_match`; `cards_contain_no_tool_names` (a word-list scan).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_first_slice.sh` (bead BD-140) consumes this fixture. This bead's own check is
`scripts/e2e/gate07_fixture_check.sh`: it runs the evaluator, verifies the freeze, and logs each truth value
with its derivation line, the digests, and a `summary` naming GATE-AUGUR-07 as "fixture ready", through the
shared helper of STD-E2E.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-624, KA-626, KA-635, KA-636, KA-637, KA-638 supply the values. Negative controls for the rubric, each a
canned transcript the scorer must classify correctly: a transcript that calls the straddling cells of A1
"infeasible" (a false verdict); one that answers A3 with "increase motor authority" (wrong restriction); one
that reports A5 as attainable.

## Considerations

- The list is this bead's DESIGN and is the place to argue with it before the freeze; after the freeze it
  changes only by a dated amendment that precedes every run it applies to.
- The agent under test must not be able to read this bead, the truth records or the product's tests; it is
  given the generated documentation and the cards.
- Accepted names are deliberately broad where two restrictions bind at once (A2, A5, A7): the gate tests
  whether the agent finds a true binding restriction, not whether it guesses the author's preferred word.
