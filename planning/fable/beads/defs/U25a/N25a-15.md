## Background

Monotonicity is the cheapest source of family verdicts in a mission answer and the easiest to misuse. Write
d ≼ d′ for an order on a chart of the design domain (a larger rating, a longer stroke, a wider stance in a
stated coordinate). A verdict proved at one design is carried to its neighbours along that order by the proof
kind `monotone-transport`. The gate for the mechanism, GATE-AUGUR-08 (TARGET), reads: zero false transports on
a planted set of at least 20 non-monotone fixtures (including the widened sole); the falsification harness
finds every planted reversal within 10⁴ ordered pairs.

A Frontier mechanism is funded only after a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier has run. This bead is that
pilot and, at the same time, the frozen corpus the gate will use. It exists before the mechanism so that the
mechanism is born with its negative controls, and so that a hole in the three-part test is found on paper
instead of in a certificate.

The canonical trap, which the corpus must contain: enlarging a convex set of allowable contact forces admits
more loads in a static model, so widening a physical sole helps the static inclusion; the same wider sole may
collide during swing, which is a new branch at the larger design that no branch at the smaller design
simulates. A design that passes the static test and is "monotonically better" is still outside the inner set
until that branch condition is checked.

## Scope

In: 23 non-monotone fixtures in nine families, 10 monotone controls, a generator, exact truth evaluators, the
rule that bounds how rare a planted reversal may be, the pilot run and its record. Out: the proposer and the
falsification harness in `frob-mission` (BD-288); the auditor's checking rule; the gate measurement
(N25a-25).

## Technical approach

**Fixture format.** `{ id, family, chart, parameter, cell, constraints (expression DAG in exact rationals),
claimed_direction, transported_verdict (inclusion upward | exclusion downward), side_conditions_supplied,
truth: { monotone, verdict_map, reversal_pair, pair_measure }, expected: accept | reject, expected_rule }`.
`verdict_map` gives the exact verdict as a function of the parameter (a list of intervals with open and closed
ends). `pair_measure` is the probability that two independent uniform draws from the cell, ordered, form a
reversal.

**The rarity rule (DESIGN).** Every planted reversal has `pair_measure ≥ 1/200`. Then 10⁴ random ordered pairs
miss it with probability at most (1 − 1/200)^10000 < e⁻⁵⁰, so "finds every planted reversal within 10⁴ ordered
pairs" is a property of the harness and not of luck. The generator refuses a fixture below the bound.

**Monotone controls** (each claim is true and must be accepted, so that a checker that rejects everything
cannot pass): three stance cells with inclusion transported upward in the foot rating (the witness allocation
stays realizable; disturbances, observations and branches are unchanged); two stance cells with exclusion
transported downward in the rating (the relaxed feasible set shrinks with the rating); two creep-limit cells
with friction demand μ ≥ tan θ, monotone in μ by construction; one docking cell with inclusion upward in the
actuator bound U at fixed read error; one pusher cell with the per-build design set growing with the upper
command bound; one K1-shaped fixture restricted to a cell that lies entirely on one side of c.

**The pilot.** On the host, with exact evaluators only:
1. Generate all 33 fixtures; verify every `verdict_map` by evaluating the constraints at 10³ rational points.
2. Apply the three-part test as specified (structural sign by outward-rounded enclosure of the partial
   derivative over the whole cell; presence of each required side condition as a sub-certificate; scope
   within one stratum) with a prototype evaluator. Record, per fixture, which part rejects it.
3. Run the ordered-pair search with 10⁴ pairs and fixed seeds on every fixture with a planted reversal; record
   the index of the first reversal found.
Falsifier: a planted non-monotone fixture that no part of the test rejects; or a monotone control that some
part rejects; or a planted reversal not found within 10⁴ pairs. If the falsifier holds, the specification of
the test is repaired before BD-288 is built, and the repair is recorded. Ceiling of benefit, stated before any
speed test: none is claimed; transport is an evidence rule, and its saving is the cells it decides without a
solve, measured later on real families. Fallback if the test cannot be made to reject the corpus: transport is
limited to parameters that enter only as the bound of a provably growing set, and every other cell is decided
on its own.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 23 non-monotone fixtures and 10 controls exist with exact verdict maps; the corpus is hash-committed.
- [ ] Every planted reversal has pair measure at least 1/200, computed exactly and stored.
- [ ] Pilot record: each of the 23 is rejected by a named part of the test; each of the 10 is accepted; every
      planted reversal is found within 10⁴ pairs (the largest first-hit index is stored).
- [ ] The widened-sole fixtures are rejected for the branch condition and not for the sign.
- [ ] The pilot result, including a negative one, is entered in the review log with the fallback decision.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/monotone_corpus.rs`: `k1_band_verdict_maps`; `k2_samples_positive_derivative_negative_between`
(h′ at the five grid points is positive; h′((a + b)/2) is negative); `k3_static_margin_increasing_but_branch_fails`;
`k6_pusher_band` (for u = 1: pass iff g ∈ [1, 5/4]); `k7_stratum_boundary_at_one_quarter`;
`k8_need_is_841_over_800_at_midpoint`; `controls_are_monotone`; `pair_measure_at_least_1_over_200`;
`corpus_digest_matches`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/monotone_corpus_pilot.sh` runs the generator, the three-part prototype and the pair search, and
writes the pilot record. Logging through the helper of STD-E2E to
`target/e2e-logs/monotone_corpus_pilot/<utc>.ndjson`: per fixture its family, the rejecting part, the first
reversal pair and its index, the seed; a `summary` line with counts 23/23 rejected, 10/10 accepted, and the
gate GATE-AUGUR-08 marked "pilot".

### Known answers and negative controls

The corpus is itself the set of negative controls. Values reused: KA-627 and CX-270 (the 841/800 midpoint);
KA-628 (pusher window). A control of the pilot: a deliberately weakened sign test that samples the derivative
at five points accepts the K2 fixtures; the pilot must show that the specified test does not.

## Considerations

- The corpus is synthetic: it tests the logic of the transport rule, not any contact model. The widened sole's
  swing collision is stipulated as a branch condition, not simulated.
- The mechanism's implementers see this corpus; that is intended for registered negative controls. Fresh
  variants (new constants from the same generators, new seeds) are drawn for the gate run so that the
  mechanism cannot be tuned to the listed constants.
- The same band family is the planted fixture for the later rule that forbids filling verdicts between
  evaluated points without a proof of monotonicity.
