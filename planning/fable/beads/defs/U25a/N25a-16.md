## Background

`mission.revalidate` answers: after this edit, which of my verdicts are still proved, which are withdrawn,
what must be rerun and what will it cost. The edit may be to the mission, the design family, an actuator or
material card, a sensor, a clock, or the world. What a design agent does with it: changes a motor or a floor
tolerance without redoing the qualified part of a study. Kind: run. Tier: Frontier; the dependency-keyed part
is Solid. It is the revalidation engine (BD-292) applied to a mission answer, plus two things only a mission
has: polarity rules stated in the vocabulary of worlds, policies and requirements, and optimality obligations.

**The assumption lattice, with polarity.** Assumption sets are ordered by inclusion of admitted worlds and
commands. The feasible family F is antitone in the world set, monotone in the policy class (more information,
earlier packets, more actuator authority) and monotone in the acceptance predicate ordered by implication.
Each transport of a verdict across an edit is marked with its direction:

| Edit | Inclusion (a universal guarantee with the same witness) | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Narrow the disturbance set | Preserved | Must be rechecked |
| Enlarge the disturbance set | Must be rechecked | Preserved when its witness lies in the old set and the policy class and information contract are unchanged |
| Relax an actuator limit | Preserved if the old command is still realizable and the plant and observations still realize the old dynamics | Must be rechecked |
| Relax a requirement | Preserved | Must be rechecked |
| Improve a sensor, or deliver a packet earlier | Preserved if every new observation history is one the old policy was proved on, or the old observation can be reconstructed from the new one | Can be destroyed: an observation-based impossibility may no longer hold |
| Delay a packet across a decision instant | Must be rechecked | Must be rechecked |
| Simultaneous edits to two fields | Joint check; per-field monotonicity rarely proves an interaction | Joint check |

**Feasibility and optimality are separate obligations.** A cost-9 cart may be the certified minimum while a
cost-5 relay is excluded by a doorway. Widen only the relay's doorway. The cart and its complete successful
trace remain valid, and the optimum moves to 5. Before returning a minimum or a complete Pareto set after an
edit, the subsystem revalidates the competing exclusions, the global cost bound, catalog membership and
prices. It preserves the cart's witness and withdraws the stale minimum, including when an amendment adds a
new admissible catalog part.

Rejected alternatives: one "still valid?" flag per answer (an answer survives in parts); trusting the
direction of an edit as described by the caller (the engine classifies the edit from the two documents).

## Scope

In: the verb; six mission polarity rules registered in the engine's rule registry, each with its side
conditions; the witness re-check path; optimality obligations; the result; the implication records handed to
the bundle writer. Out: the engine and its grades 1 and 2 (BD-292); the frozen campaign (N25a-17); the gate
measurement (N25a-26); the kill decision (N25a-19).

## Technical approach

1. **Verb.** `frob mission revalidate a:93f0… --after-edit e:5d1c…` (also `--against q:…`). Registry: cluster
   `mission`, kind `run`, capability `mission:run`, idempotent, first milestone M3. Refusals: 854 (reuse
   outside eligibility), 881 (prefix mismatch), 874 (world scope changed and no revalidation requested), and
   the budget family.
2. **Order of work per decided cell.** (a) Grade 1 and grade 2 from the engine. (b) For a cell not saved by
   those, the **witness re-check**: bind the old witness to the amended query and run the independent checker
   on it; an inclusion whose old witness still verifies is preserved at checker cost, with licence
   `witness-rechecked` (this is a full proof for the new query, obtained without a search; it applies to
   inclusions only). (c) With the flag `frontier-revalidate-implication` on, the polarity rules below.
   (d) Otherwise the cell is withdrawn for the amended mission and listed under `reruns` with a cost estimate
   by budget line.
3. **Rules registered** (name; applies to; side conditions, each an exact check recorded in the licence):
   - `narrow-disturbance`; inclusions; the new world set is contained in the old one.
   - `enlarge-disturbance`; exclusions; the old world set is contained in the new one, the exclusion's
     witness world or load vertex lies in the old set, and the policy-class and observation-contract digests
     are unchanged.
   - `relax-actuator`; inclusions; the new limits contain the old ones, the edit changes no other model field
     (a pure change), and the witness command lies within the new limits.
   - `relax-requirement`; inclusions; every accept atom of the new mission is implied by the old one
     (thresholds moved in the permissive direction, comparators not strengthened).
   - `tighten-actuator`; exclusions; the new limits are contained in the old ones and the edit changes no
     other model field (every command admitted now was admitted before, so no new witness can appear).
   - `improve-observation`; inclusions; every new observation history is one the old policy was proved on (a
     narrower error interval; an earlier arrival that crosses no decision instant), or a declared map
     reconstructs the old observation from the new one. No rule preserves an exclusion under this edit.
   No rule exists for a packet delayed across a decision instant, for an enlarged hypothesis support applied
   to inclusions, or for joint edits on shared support: those are rechecked.
4. **Optimality.** A cost claim's obligations are (i) the incumbent's feasibility, (ii) every exclusion that
   the lower bound rests on, (iii) catalog membership of the incumbent, (iv) prices. Each is graded on its
   own. If (i) survives and any of (ii) to (iv) does not, the result says `feasible: preserved` and
   `optimal: revoked`; the minimum is withdrawn and the claim returned is `feasible-candidate` until the
   competing exclusions are rechecked. An added catalog part or a lowered price always revokes optimality.
5. **Result.** `surviving` (cells with grade, licence and rule name), `withdrawn` (cells with the field that
   caused it and, when known, the two worlds or the new mode that invalidates the old proof), `reruns` (with
   cost estimates), `optimality`, `mask`, and `new_answer`: the address of an answer bound to the amended
   `MissionId` in which surviving cells keep their proofs and withdrawn cells are unresolved. Exit 0 when
   nothing is withdrawn; exit 2 otherwise.
6. **Implication records.** Each grade-3 survival is handed to the bundle writer as an implication record
   (rule name, old and new assumption sets, the order relation, the checks) so that the auditor can re-check
   it; a survival without one is refused by the auditor as reuse without a recorded implication.

Determinism: exact (D0). Evidence: a preserved cell keeps its class; nothing here raises a class.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Cost fixture: the cost-9 cart keeps its witness (`feasible: preserved`) and the minimum is withdrawn
      (`optimal: revoked`) when the relay's doorway is widened; the re-solved minimum is 5. The same happens
      when a cost-7 feasible part is added (new minimum 7).
- [ ] Docking polarity: with the implication flag on, an inclusion at (U, ε) = (3/4, 1/4) survives ε falling
      to 1/8 (`improve-observation`) and U rising to 1 (`relax-actuator`); an exclusion at (3/4, 3/8) survives
      ε rising (`enlarge-disturbance`) and U falling (`tighten-actuator`); each of the four opposite moves is
      rechecked.
- [ ] Adding the gain 33/32 to the hidden set keeps every exclusion and rechecks every inclusion; the
      inclusion at (3/4, 1/64) is withdrawn, with the indistinguishable pair (gains 1 and 33/32, reading
      (65/64)·p) named.
- [ ] Stance: narrowing the load hull to (±50, ±25) N·m preserves the inner cell by rule and sends the
      exclusion to recheck, after which the whole domain v ∈ [2, 6] is inner; enlarging it to (±70, ±35)
      preserves the exclusion and rechecks the inclusion, which shrinks to v ≤ 22/7.
- [ ] With the flag off the same calls return the same final answers through reruns, with zero grade-3
      licences.
- [ ] A simultaneous edit of ratings (160 → 170 N) and hull (to (±70, ±35)) is checked jointly: inner
      v ≤ 26/7.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/revalidate_mission.rs`:
- `doorway_widened_keeps_witness_revokes_minimum` (`synthetic`: cart of width 3/5 m through doorway A of
  width 4/5 m, cost 9; relay of width 7/10 m excluded by doorway B of width 13/20 m, cost 5; B widened to
  3/4 m).
- `catalog_part_added_revokes_minimum`.
- `docking_six_moves` (the two survivals per verdict and the four rechecks).
- `third_gain_rechecks_inclusions_keeps_exclusions`.
- `stance_hull_narrowed`: boundary 75 + (25/2)·v ≤ 160 holds for all v ≤ 6.
- `stance_hull_enlarged`: boundary 105 + (35/2)·v ≤ 160, v ≤ 22/7.
- `stance_joint_edit`: 105 + (35/2)·v ≤ 170, v ≤ 26/7.
- `witness_recheck_preserves_without_search`: ratings 160 → 170 N: the old witness verifies under the new
  query.
- `no_rule_preserves_exclusion_under_better_sensor`; `delay_across_decision_rechecks_both`;
  `mixed_motor_edit_not_a_relaxation`; `flag_off_same_answers`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_revalidate_campaign.sh` (bead N25a-26) calls this verb for every amendment of the frozen
campaign and for the cost fixture, and compares with full recomputation. Logged per call: the classified
edit, each cell's grade, licence and rule, the optimality block, rerun estimates and the digests of the old
and new answers.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-700 (doorway), KA-701 (33/32: 34,590 positive decisions withdrawn on each grid), KA-638 (best worst-case
error 1/65 m). CX-282, CX-314, CX-316. The stance values are recomputed for this bead from t ≥ r + s − W with
r = (W + 3·P_x)/2 and s = (W + v·P_y)/2. Negative controls registered: a mutant `enlarge-disturbance` that
skips the "witness lies in the old set" check; a mutant that preserves an exclusion under an improved sensor;
a mutant that keeps the minimum when a cheaper part is added. MR-11.

## Considerations

- KILL-AUGUR-REUSE: one incorrectly inherited verdict from an implication rule removes that rule; the registry
  status is the switch, and the witness re-check path and grades 1 and 2 remain.
- The second delivery slice is where this could matter at scale: change one measured surface law in a
  warehouse with several routes, body variants and sensor mounts, and recheck only the obligations whose slack
  the changed interval must still fit. A conventional dependency graph, an incremental optimizer and a
  hot-started simulator are eligible comparators, and their fastest correct implementation sets the bar.
- The verb registry lists this verb's first milestone as M4 in one table and the milestone plan delivers it at
  M3; this bead follows the milestone plan and the gate (GATE-AUGUR-12 at M3).
