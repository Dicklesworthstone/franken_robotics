## Background

`mission.transport` asks whether a skill certified on one robot can be carried to another through its task
interface: a relation between the two systems and a causal refinement map such that every admitted target
disturbance leads to a successor that the abstract skill allows. Its Frontier core is static: for a carried
tray, the target's reachable wrench set at the tray interface must cover the requested one, a polytope or
cone containment certified by exact witnesses and duals. Beyond that core the mechanism is Moonshot: whether
transferring through a certified interface with limited adaptation actually saves target-side trials against
simply training a cross-morphology conditioned policy is unknown, and the published record for policy
inheritance across single-limb topology edits (33 to 100% of from-scratch steps) says the saving on a
changed-support transfer may be nil. The claim that remains even then is localization: the verb names the
contact mode where authority is lost, so that retraining or a body edit is confined to that mode.

**KILL-AUGUR-TRANSPORT.** Trigger: the interface arm needs as many target trials as the conditioned policy, or
reports coverage where the referee shows a fall. Fallback, named in advance: family policies and inheritance
(the learning-side mechanisms already built by M4); the wrench-coverage test is kept as a static analysis.
Decided at M8.

The two halves of the trigger are different kinds of failure. The first is economic: the mechanism works and
buys nothing. The second is a soundness failure: a coverage certificate contradicted by the referee is a false
certificate, and it is handled as one whatever this gate decides.

## Scope

In: evaluating both halves of the trigger on the record of the pre-registered experiment; the ADR; applying
the branch. Out: running the pilot and the experiment; the learned residual's own kill gate; the coupling
budget's kill gate.

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** From the record of the three-treatment experiment on the tray interface (one wheeled carrier
   to one quadruped; treatments at equal compute and equal source data: training from scratch; an ordinary
   cross-morphology conditioned policy with full transfer learning; interface-based transfer with limited
   adaptation; target morphology and terrain frozen before tuning): for each treatment and seed, new trials
   to reach the target criterion, irrecoverable failures, task success, contact impulse and energy; and, for
   the interface arm, every coverage verdict with the referee replay of each contact mode, including the
   planted ramp-crest loss of lateral authority.
2. **Half A (economic).** The interface arm "needs as many target trials as the conditioned policy" when the
   paired lower confidence bound on (trials of the conditioned-policy arm) ÷ (trials of the interface arm),
   over seeds paired by address, does not exceed 1 at the pre-registered level. The threshold and the level
   are fixed in the pre-registration, before any run; this bead applies them as written.
3. **Half B (soundness).** Any case in which the verb reported coverage (an accepted certificate that the
   target's wrench set covers the request in a contact mode) and the referee shows a fall in that mode under
   an admitted disturbance. One case fires the half. The planted ramp-crest case must have been reported as a
   loss of authority, with its mode and a replayable counterexample; reporting coverage there fires half B.
4. **The gate fires if A or B holds.**
5. **Branch: fires.** `mission.transport` beyond the static core is parked: its registry row keeps the name
   with status `parked`; the Moonshot feature stays off. Skills are moved between bodies by family policies
   and inheritance along design edits. The wrench-coverage test stays available as a static analysis that
   answers "can this body deliver this task wrench in this contact mode", with its witness or dual. If half B
   fired, the one-false-certificate procedure also runs: the proof kind and checker version involved are
   disabled until the cause is found, stored bundles that used them are withdrawn, and the counterexample
   joins the mutation corpus. A row is added to the register of killed ideas with the measurement that killed
   it and the condition that would reopen it.
6. **Branch: does not fire.** The verb ships at its measured scope: the interface families and contact modes
   of the experiment and nothing broader. A successful transfer of a skill segment does not certify the
   remainder of a mission, and the documentation says so.
7. **The ADR** states the measured values, which half fired (if any), the branch, the state of the two sibling
   kill gates (the learned residual; the coupling budget for chance claims), and that no threshold was
   changed after the first run.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists at M8 with both halves evaluated against the pre-registered record.
- [ ] The gate register row for KILL-AUGUR-TRANSPORT cites the ADR; GATE-AUGUR-23's row is `pass` or
      `killed`, with the fallback named.
- [ ] On the fired branch: `frob mission transport` beyond the static core returns the parked-verb refusal;
      the static coverage analysis still returns its witness or dual on the tray fixture; the register of
      killed ideas has the new row.
- [ ] If half B fired: the false-certificate procedure's four consequences are on record.
- [ ] The decision procedure classifies three synthetic records correctly: interface arm clearly cheaper and
      no false coverage (not fired); equal trial counts (fired by A); one coverage report contradicted by a
      referee fall (fired by B).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_augur_transport.rs`: `cheaper_and_sound_does_not_fire`;
`equal_trials_fires_half_a`; `one_false_coverage_fires_half_b`; `planted_ramp_crest_reported_as_coverage_fires_b`;
`fired_branch_parks_verb_keeps_static_analysis`. The decision is a pure function of the experiment record;
inputs are `synthetic` records in the experiment's schema.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kill_augur_transport.sh` reads the experiment record, runs the decision function, and under the
parked configuration asserts that `frob mission transport` is refused beyond the static core while the
coverage analysis answers. Logging through the helper of STD-E2E: the paired ratio with its bound, the count
of contradicted coverage reports, the outcome, and a `summary` line naming KILL-AUGUR-TRANSPORT.

### Known answers and negative controls

The refinement counterexample that every transport rule must respect: a target with successors
{stable, fall} cannot refine an abstract stable-only transition by selecting just its stable branch. The
three synthetic records are the controls of the decision procedure. Determinism: a pure function of the
record.

## Considerations

- A kill gate is never quietly re-scoped: the trial-count comparison and its level are whatever the
  pre-registration says.
- Parking costs little: nothing before M8 depends on this verb, and the exact skill layer keeps refusing an
  infeasible body before any training.
- No slice of this kind is funded before its pilot passes, and pretraining needs an owner-approved envelope;
  if the pilot is never funded, this decision is recorded as "not run; mechanism parked by default".
