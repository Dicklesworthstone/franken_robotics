## Background

The stance family is the first continuous family of robot bodies with an exact answer: a robot carries 400 N
on four point feet at (±1/3, ±b) m; its centre of mass moves through a rectangle with half-dimensions 150 mm
and 75 mm; each foot delivers any independently commanded normal force from 0 to 160 N; the question is how
narrow the body can be. The answer is half-width b = 3/14 m. Its gate, GATE-AUGUR-02 (claim type Known answer,
M1, TARGET), is measured through the public verb: boundary exactly 3/14 m; four vertex witnesses; dual
(0, 1/2, 1/2) for the excluded cell; 11,520 of 11,520 grid states agree with an independent zonotope test;
lifted and projected formulations agree; the passive-spring amendment withdraws the cell at b = 1/4 m.

A gate measured "through the public verb" cannot be closed by crate-level tests: the registry entry, the
admission path, the driver, the exact reconstruction, the bundle writer, the auditor and the envelope all sit
between the lowering and what a user sees. This script is that measurement, from the user's side, on the real
binaries, and it is also the M1 demonstration of the North-Star scenario "a mission answered over a family".

## Scope

In: one shell script, its fixtures and its log; the tier run whose receipt GATE-AUGUR-02 is recorded from.
Out: the mechanisms it
exercises (their own beads); the mutation corpus in full (BD-136); two-host bundle identity and the audit on a host with no GPU in under 10 s (N25b-06, the script of
GATE-AUGUR-05, which audits the bundles this script's answers produce).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/mission_stance.sh`; plain shell; idempotent (works in a fresh temporary state root);
exits non-zero on the first failed assertion after writing the log. Fixture
`tests/fixtures/mission/stance-family-400N.fmission`: family `template:four-point-stance@1`, topology `rect4`,
ports `foot.pp, foot.pm, foot.mp, foot.mm`, free `v` = 1/half_width with chart `inverse-length` and box
["2", "6"], bound `u` = "3" and `cap` = four times "160" N, world W = "400" N with first-moment hull
(±60, ±30) N·m, observation of W and P before the decision, authority `independent-normal-force`, law class
`policy_class.law: adjustable` (required for an inclusion over a load set, N25e-28; the class whose law object,
`multilinear-vertex`, BD-132's first form of the law check admits), horizon
`static`, accept atoms force balance, moment balance, unilateral and rating, prefix ∃ policy per nominal
design, ∀ world, ∀ execution, objective minimize 1/v with claim `minimum-with-matching-lower-bound`, evidence
`exact`.

Every call of a `run` or `mutate` verb (`mission.new`, `mission.synthesize`, `mission.refute`,
`result.revalidate`) carries an idempotency key `--key ms-<run>-<step>-<n>`, built from the run's timestamp, the
step and a counter (VERBS-mission, VERBS-result: a call without a key is `FROB-REF-805`); two calls the script
compares (step 10's repeat against the first run, the amended answer against a fresh synthesis) never share a
key, since the second would return the first one's stored receipt. The read verbs `mission.check` and
`mission.show` take none. The `cmd` lines of the log carry the keys.

1. Build `frob` and, from a copy of its directory alone on its stable compiler, `frob-verify`.
2. `frob mission new stance-family-400N.fmission --key … --json` then `frob mission check q:… --json`: assert
   domain nonempty, fragment `linear-static`, and the echo "∃ policy per nominal design, ∀ worlds, ∀ executions".
3. `frob mission synthesize q:… --key … --json`. Assert: exit 0; inner cell v ∈ ["2", "14/3"] closed at both ends
   with policy `selectable-per-nominal-design`; excluded cell v ∈ ("14/3", "6"] with binding "rating at
   foot.pp under P = (60, 30) N*m"; no unresolved cell; witnesses for the four vertices with forces
   (160, 130, 110, 0), (130, 160, 0, 110), (110, 0, 160, 130), (0, 110, 130, 160) N at v = 14/3; the dual
   y = ("0", "1/2", "1/2"); `cost_bounds` incumbent "3/14", lower bound "3/14", `attained: true`, gap "0";
   evidence class `Certified`, tier `static`, determinism `exact`; three authorization flags `false`; the
   warning about zero reserve on two constraints; certificate kinds `lp-primal`, `lp-farkas`, `partition`;
   `result.resolution` (N25a-33) met, unresolved measure "0" in the chart `v` against the bound "0", and
   `interior`, `exterior`, `boundary` each discharged by cells (c0; c1; both, nothing unresolved).
4. `frob-verify <bundle>` standalone: accepted; then two corrupted copies (feet reordered with unchanged
   forces; (60, 30) written as the torque where (30, −60, 0) N·m belongs): each rejected. The copies are made
   from this run's bundle by the `frame-unit` operators of the mission mutation library (BD-136,
   `crates/frob-conformance/src/mutate/`), which re-serialize canonically and recompute every hash, so that
   integrity passes and the rejection must come from the typing check: assert FROB-REF-829 (the auditor's code
   for a frame, sign or port-order mismatch; a mission verb would report FROB-REF-850) with the first failing
   rule naming the port order for the first copy and the torque field for the second. A rejection by a hash or
   integrity rule fails the assertion: such a control proves nothing about the witness check.
5. Grid: for u, v ∈ {2, 3, 4} per metre and ratings in {0, 80, 160, 240}⁴ N (2,304 bodies) and the four
   vertices plus their centroid (11,520 states), a point query per state through `frob mission synthesize`
   with `--set` (one key per state); compare each verdict with the independent zonotope test
   (`frob-conformance`). Assert
   11,520 of 11,520 agree; 2,254 feasible states; 114 bodies cover all four vertices.
6. `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test stance_grid`: the lifted and projected formulations agree. This is a
   check whose result is logged. It writes no receipt (not a tier target); the grid clause reaches the gate
   through step 11's tier run.
7. `frob mission refute q:… --cell v=5 --key … --json`: dual (0, 1/2, 1/2); 165 N needed against 160 N; the
   three repairs, each `attained: true`: ratings raised to 165 N on all four feet; half-width widened to 3/14 m
   (v = 14/3); the load hull narrowed to abs(P_y) ≤ 28 N·m (90 + (5/2)·P_y = 160).
8. `frob mission show q:… --answer a:… --set outer --json` (the outer set's definition and cells); point
   queries `frob mission synthesize q:… --design v=4 --key … --json` (inner) and `--design v=5` (excluded);
   `frob mission synthesize q:… --exists --key … --json` (true, with a witness). (`mission.show --at`, the
   stored-set membership query, answers only from M4.)
9. Amendment: `frob mission synthesize stance-family-400N.fmission --set
   policy_class.authority=passive-equal-spring --key … --json` yields a new mission q′ (the law class
   `adjustable` is kept); `frob result revalidate a:… --between q:… q′ --key … --json` (the M1 mission-cell
   form of `result.revalidate`, N25a-13 and N28b-32: `--between` takes two versions of a mission, and
   `--against q′` is the row's equivalent form for a mission-answer target; `mission.revalidate` answers only
   from M3, N25a-16): assert the inner cell is
   invalidated, the exclusion inherited; then the re-solved answer: at v = 4 (b = 1/4 m) excluded with forces
   (175, 115, 85, 25) N against the 160 N rating, and compare with a fresh synthesis of the amended mission.
10. Run steps 3 and 9 twice, with fresh keys, and compare result bytes and bundle digests.
11. **Receipt and record.** The assertions and metrics of steps 2 to 10 carry `--gate GATE-AUGUR-02` in the
    log (boundary, witnesses, dual, grid agreement, counts, lifted-equals-projected, the withdrawal, and the
    two corrupted bundles caught). A log is not a receipt: only a tier run writes one (N38a-02; STD-E2E
    `e2e_tier`). So the script then runs the gate's known-answer entries with their controls,
    `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-AUGUR-02 --mutations`. These are the M1 rows N25d-37 binds: KA-624, and the
    grid clause's KA-625 and KA-658, whose entries are N25c-12's grid code, with the weak-screen control. The
    script asserts with `e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"` that the gate line is `pass` and every control
    `caught`, and logs the receipt's hash. It writes no gate row. A session that implemented none of the
    mechanisms enters the row with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-02
    --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19), citing this run's log beside it; N38b-19 refuses unless the row's controls were
    caught in that run.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a clean checkout and every assertion of steps 2 to 10 is logged as passed.
- [ ] The `g1_known_answers` receipt of step 11 shows GATE-AUGUR-02 `pass` with every control caught. The
      log holds the measured values: boundary "3/14", the four witnesses, the dual, grid agreement
      11,520/11,520, counts 2,254 and 114, lifted-equals-projected, and the withdrawal at b = 1/4 m. The row is
      entered from that receipt through `record_gate` by a session that implemented none of the mechanisms.
- [ ] Every `run` and `mutate` call in the log carries a `--key`, and no two compared calls share one.
- [ ] Both corrupted bundles, re-sealed with consistent hashes, are rejected by the standalone auditor with
      FROB-REF-829 and the typing rule (the gate's negative controls, caught in the same run).
- [ ] The reconstruction counters show zero failures on the family.
- [ ] Two runs give identical result bytes and bundle digests.
- [ ] A planted defect makes the script fail: with the support-polygon-plus-capacity screen substituted for
      the lowering (a test build), step 5 reports 1,596 disagreeing bodies and the script exits non-zero.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own; the script's helper functions for comparing exact-rational JSON fields are tested in the
shared harness. The mechanisms' unit tests are in BD-130, N25a-05, N25a-06, N25a-09, N25a-10, N25a-12 and
N25a-13.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. It sources the shared helper of STD-E2E and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/mission_stance/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines carry the exact command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's
evidence block (class, tier, determinism, mission and answer identities); `assert` lines carry the expected
and observed exact values and, on failure, the first differing field and the paths of the query, answer and
bundle; `metric` lines carry grid agreement counts, reconstruction counters and the checker's wall time;
digests of result and bundle bytes; a final `summary` with assertions passed and failed and the gate touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-624 (b ≥ 3/14 m; (160, 130, 110, 0) N; 165 N at b = 1/5 m), KA-625 (2,254 of 11,520; 114 of 2,304; the
weak screen accepts 1,596), KA-626 and KA-022 (passive springs at b = 1/4 m; the amendment withdraws the inner
set), KA-658 (two exact classifiers agree). Negative controls: the two corrupted bundles; the weak-screen
build; a mutant renderer that prints the excluded cell as closed at 14/3 (caught by the partition check).
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; worker counts 1 and 8.

## Considerations

- The grid loop issues 11,520 point queries; the budget is ten minutes on eight cores (TARGET), inside the
  machine envelope. If it does not fit, the daemon's batch submission is used and the per-state assertion is
  unchanged.
- The law-class result for this family (3/14 m under an adjustable law, 1/2 m under an affine law) is a later
  gate; this script does not assert it.
- OPEN-AUGUR-2 is informed by the reconstruction counters this script records.
