## Background

The stance family is the first continuous family of robot bodies with an exact answer: a robot carries 400 N
on four point feet at (±1/3, ±b) m; its centre of mass moves through a rectangle with half-dimensions 150 mm
and 75 mm; each foot delivers any independently commanded normal force from 0 to 160 N; the question is how
narrow the body can be. The answer is half-width b = 3/14 m. Its gate, GATE-AUGUR-02 (claim type Known answer,
M1, TARGET), is measured through the public verb: boundary exactly 3/14 m; four vertex witnesses; dual
(0, 1/2, 1/2) for the excluded cell; 11,520 of 11,520 grid states agree with an independent zonotope test;
lifted and projected formulations agree; the passive-spring amendment withdraws the cell at b = 1/4 m.

A gate measured "through the public verb" cannot be closed by crate-level tests: the registry entry, the
admission path, the driver, the exact reconstruction, the bundle writer, the auditor and the envelope all sit
between the lowering and what a user sees. This script is that measurement, from the user's side, on the real
binaries, and it is also the M1 demonstration of the North-Star scenario "a mission answered over a family".

## Scope

In: one shell script, its fixtures and its log; the gate record for GATE-AUGUR-02. Out: the mechanisms it
exercises (their own beads); the mutation corpus in full (BD-136); two-host bundle identity (the auditor
gate).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/mission_stance.sh`; plain shell; idempotent (works in a fresh temporary state root);
exits non-zero on the first failed assertion after writing the log. Fixture
`tests/fixtures/mission/stance-family-400N.fmission`: family `template:four-point-stance@1`, topology `rect4`,
ports `foot.pp, foot.pm, foot.mp, foot.mm`, free `v` = 1/half_width with chart `inverse-length` and box
["2", "6"], bound `u` = "3" and `cap` = four times "160" N, world W = "400" N with first-moment hull
(±60, ±30) N·m, observation of W and P before the decision, authority `independent-normal-force`, horizon
`static`, accept atoms force balance, moment balance, unilateral and rating, prefix ∃ policy per nominal
design, ∀ world, ∀ execution, objective minimize 1/v with claim `minimum-with-matching-lower-bound`, evidence
`exact`.

1. Build `frob` and, from a copy of its directory alone on its stable compiler, `frob-verify`.
2. `frob mission new stance-family-400N.fmission --json` then `frob mission check q:… --json`: assert domain
   nonempty, fragment `linear-static`, and the echo "∃ policy per nominal design, ∀ worlds, ∀ executions".
3. `frob mission synthesize q:… --json`. Assert: exit 0; inner cell v ∈ ["2", "14/3"] closed at both ends
   with policy `selectable-per-nominal-design`; excluded cell v ∈ ("14/3", "6"] with binding "rating at
   foot.pp under P = (60, 30) N*m"; no unresolved cell; witnesses for the four vertices with forces
   (160, 130, 110, 0), (130, 160, 0, 110), (110, 0, 160, 130), (0, 110, 130, 160) N at v = 14/3; the dual
   y = ("0", "1/2", "1/2"); `cost_bounds` incumbent "3/14", lower bound "3/14", `attained: true`, gap "0";
   evidence class `Certified`, tier `static`, determinism `exact`; three authorization flags `false`; the
   warning about zero reserve on two constraints; certificate kinds `lp-primal`, `lp-farkas`, `partition`.
4. `frob-verify <bundle>` standalone: accepted; then two corrupted copies (feet reordered with unchanged
   forces; (60, 30) written as the torque where (30, −60, 0) N·m belongs): each rejected.
5. Grid: for u, v ∈ {2, 3, 4} per metre and ratings in {0, 80, 160, 240}⁴ N (2,304 bodies) and the four
   vertices plus their centroid (11,520 states), a point query per state through `frob mission synthesize`
   with `--set`; compare each verdict with the independent zonotope test (`frob-conformance`). Assert
   11,520 of 11,520 agree; 2,254 feasible states; 114 bodies cover all four vertices.
6. `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test stance_grid`: the lifted and projected formulations agree.
7. `frob mission refute q:… --cell v=5 --json`: 165 N needed against 160 N; the three repairs.
8. `frob mission show q:… --answer a:… --set outer`; a point query at v = 4 (inner) and v = 5 (excluded);
   `--exists` (true, with a witness).
9. Amendment: `frob mission synthesize stance-family-400N.fmission --set
   policy_class.authority=passive-equal-spring --json` yields a new mission; `frob result revalidate a:…
   --against q:new --json`: assert the inner cell is invalidated, the exclusion inherited; then the re-solved
   answer: at v = 4 (b = 1/4 m) excluded with forces (175, 115, 85, 25) N against the 160 N rating, and
   compare with a fresh synthesis of the amended mission.
10. Run steps 3 and 9 twice and compare result bytes and bundle digests.
11. Write the gate record for GATE-AUGUR-02 with the measured values and the caught controls.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a clean checkout and every assertion of steps 2 to 10 is logged as passed.
- [ ] The gate record holds measured values: boundary "3/14", the four witnesses, the dual, grid agreement
      11,520/11,520, counts 2,254 and 114, lifted-equals-projected, and the withdrawal at b = 1/4 m.
- [ ] Both corrupted bundles are rejected by the standalone auditor (the gate's negative controls, caught in
      the same run).
- [ ] The reconstruction counters show zero failures on the family.
- [ ] Two runs give identical result bytes and bundle digests.
- [ ] A planted defect makes the script fail: with the support-polygon-plus-capacity screen substituted for
      the lowering (a test build), step 5 reports 1,596 disagreeing bodies and the script exits non-zero.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own; the script's helper functions for comparing exact-rational JSON fields are tested in the
shared harness. The mechanisms' unit tests are in BD-130, N25a-05, N25a-06, N25a-09, N25a-10, N25a-12 and
N25a-13.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. It sources the shared helper of STD-E2E and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/mission_stance/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines carry the exact command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's
evidence block (class, tier, determinism, mission and answer identities); `assert` lines carry the expected
and observed exact values and, on failure, the first differing field and the paths of the query, answer and
bundle; `metric` lines carry grid agreement counts, reconstruction counters and the checker's wall time;
digests of result and bundle bytes; a final `summary` with assertions passed and failed and the gate touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-624 (b ≥ 3/14 m; (160, 130, 110, 0) N; 165 N at b = 1/5 m), KA-625 (2,254 of 11,520; 114 of 2,304; the
weak screen accepts 1,596), KA-626 and KA-022 (passive springs at b = 1/4 m; the amendment withdraws the inner
set), KA-658 (two exact classifiers agree). Negative controls: the two corrupted bundles; the weak-screen
build; a mutant renderer that prints the excluded cell as closed at 14/3 (caught by the partition check).
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; worker counts 1 and 8.

## Considerations

- The grid loop issues 11,520 point queries; the budget is ten minutes on eight cores (TARGET), inside the
  machine envelope. If it does not fit, the daemon's batch submission is used and the per-state assertion is
  unchanged.
- The law-class result for this family (3/14 m under an adjustable law, 1/2 m under an affine law) is a later
  gate; this script does not assert it.
- OPEN-AUGUR-2 is informed by the reconstruction counters this script records.
