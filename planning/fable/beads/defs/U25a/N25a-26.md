## Background

After an edit to a mission, a design family, an actuator or material card, a sensor, a clock or the world, the
studio answers four questions without recomputing everything: which verdicts are still proved, which are
withdrawn, what must be rerun, and what it will cost. One engine does this for every stored verdict (BD-292);
`result.revalidate` exposes it for any result and `mission.revalidate` (N25a-16) applies it to a mission answer
with the mission's polarity rules and its separate feasibility and optimality obligations. Reuse comes in four
grades: (1) identity, the obligation's full key is unchanged; (2) dependency, every changed field lies outside
the obligation's recorded dependency support; (3) implication, a registered polarity rule preserves the
obligation (Frontier, behind the flag `frontier-revalidate-implication`); (4) none, rerun with its cost.

The danger is a stale verdict: a result carried across an edit that in fact invalidated it. A hash identifies;
only an implication licenses skipping a recheck. The plan-time pilot showed a correctly keyed dependency graph
tying a more elaborate rule on a toy fixture, and a key that omitted one dependency inheriting a stale verdict,
so the mechanism is funded only through a frozen campaign with a hard stop (N25a-17): 30 amendments in ten
categories, sealed before either system saw them, with truths from independent full recomputation, and an
incremental incumbent written by a separate session.

Two gates read one run of that campaign, each against the threshold in its own row:

- **GATE-AUGUR-12** (Known answer; M3; TARGET): zero incorrectly inherited verdicts; unnecessary invalidations
  at most 20% of unaffected verdicts; the cost-9 and cost-5 fixture keeps the witness and withdraws the minimum.
- **GATE-ATEL-11** (Known answer; dependency-grade rows due at M3, grades 3 and 4 at M4; TARGET): every decision
  equals full recomputation; zero unsafe reuse; the stale-key mutant is caught; unnecessary invalidations at
  most 20% of unaffected verdicts, counted and reported.

Definitions used by both (fixed by the campaign bead so that the engine's implementers cannot tune them): an
**incorrectly inherited verdict** is a verdict kept across an amendment that the truth withdraws or changes; it
is counted even if a later amendment would restore it. An **unnecessary invalidation** is a verdict in the
truth's `unaffected` set (same verdict after the edit) that the system invalidated. **Unsafe reuse** is an
inherited verdict whose licence does not hold; it is a false accept.

## Scope

In: the script, the four arms, the scoring of every amendment, the public fixtures run beside the sealed set,
the mutant builds, the two gate records and the per-rule table that the kill decision reads. Out: the engine
(BD-292); the mission rules and the verb (N25a-16); the campaign, its truths, its scorer and the incumbent
(N25a-17); the kill decision (N25a-19); any speed claim.

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/mission_revalidate_campaign.sh --campaign DIR [--arms B,C,D] [--controls]`. It is run by the
gate runner's session, which is neither the campaign author nor an implementer; `DIR` is the sealed store.

**Arms.** A: fresh solving of every amended mission in an empty store (the reference decision). B: grades 1 and
2 plus the witness re-check, flag off (the default configuration, and the Solid part). C: the same with
`frontier-revalidate-implication` on. D: the incremental incumbent (a hermetic dependency graph with
demand-driven recomputation and linear-program ranging). All arms start from the same cache state: the base
answers, computed once and copied into each arm's store, with equal digests asserted.

Steps.
1. **Seal.** Verify `campaign30.freeze.json`: 30 record digests, the ten category counts (3, 2, 5, 3, 3, 4, 2, 2,
   4, 2), the author session differing from the implementer sessions, and no earlier gate record in the ledger
   that cites this campaign's digest (a campaign that has been run is spent). Assert the packet-advance
   amendment and the cost amendment are present by category tag. Stop with a failed precondition otherwise.
2. **Base answers.** For each base fixture named by the records (stance, docking, pusher, passive jaw, pad
   family, binary sensor, camera-delivery, the two-doorway cost fixture): `frob mission synthesize
   <base>.fmission --json` (or the fixture's result-producing verb for non-mission verdicts); verify the bundles
   with the standalone `frob-verify`; record answer addresses.
3. **Per amendment and per arm B and C.** Apply the record's edit through the public surface. An edit to a
   mission field: write the amended document (the record gives field, old value, new value), admit it with
   `frob mission new` and `frob mission check` to obtain q′, and call
   `frob mission revalidate a:… --against q:… --json`. An edit to a design, a card or the world: apply it with
   its typed edit verb, which returns the edit address `e:…`, and call
   `frob mission revalidate a:… --after-edit e:… --json`. Stored verdicts that are not mission answers (the
   camera-delivery results) go through `frob result revalidate r:… --after-edit e:… --json`. Record
   `surviving` (cells with grade, licence and rule name), `withdrawn` (with the field that caused it), `reruns`
   with their cost estimates, the `optimality` block, the `mask` and `new_answer`.
4. **Decision after reruns.** Execute the listed reruns (`frob mission synthesize q:… --from <new_answer>`,
   which keeps the surviving cells with their proofs and resumes on the withdrawn ones), giving the arm's final
   answer. In arm A run `frob mission synthesize` on the amended mission in an empty store. Compare the two
   over their common refinement, cell by cell: verdict, closedness of each end, cost bounds and the
   `attained` flag. "Every decision equals full recomputation" is this comparison over all 30.
5. **Score** with the campaign's scorer (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --bin campaign30 -- score`): per
   amendment, per arm, the counts of inherited errors (a `surviving` cell whose truth verdict differs),
   unnecessary invalidations (an `unaffected` cell listed `withdrawn`), and the size of `unaffected`. Pooled
   ratio = Σ unnecessary ÷ Σ unaffected, compared with 1/5 as exact rationals; also reported per category.
6. **Arm D** runs the same 30 through the incumbent's entry point and is scored the same way.
7. **Public fixtures** (exact; run in arms B and C):
   - Cost fixture: a cart of cost 9 through doorway A is the certified minimum while a relay of cost 5 is
     excluded by doorway B; widen B. Expect `feasible: preserved`, `optimal: revoked`, and the re-solved minimum
     5; adding a feasible part of cost 7 instead gives 7.
   - Camera-delivery, four states (initial; a display label renamed; delivery at tick 2; delivery at tick 3;
     decisions commit at ticks 2 and 4): 2, 0, 0, 1 scene-pair evaluations and 1, 0, 0, 1 verdict
     recomputations, each verdict equal to a fresh replay.
   - Docking with the gain 33/32 added to {1, 2}: every exclusion kept, every inclusion rechecked, the
     inclusion at (U, ε) = (3/4, 1/64) withdrawn with the indistinguishable pair named; best worst-case error
     1/65 m.
   - Eligibility: a screen compiled for gains {1, 2} offered for {1, 33/32, 2} is refused with `FROB-REF-854`.
8. **Edit and inverse (MR-10).** For each amendment apply its inverse to the amended state: zero solver calls
   and the original answer address returned.
9. **Licences are checkable.** Each mask and each grade-3 implication record is verified by the standalone
   auditor; the same bundle with one implication record stripped is rejected as reuse without a recorded
   implication (`FROB-REF-835`).
10. **Mutants (`--controls`).** Conformance-only builds, each with one planted defect, on which the main mode
    must exit non-zero: a dependency support with delivery time removed (the stale-key mutant: at least one
    inherited verdict on the campaign, including the packet-advance amendment); a hash match taken as verdict
    transfer; an `enlarge-disturbance` rule that skips the check that the witness lies in the old set; a rule
    that preserves an exclusion under an improved sensor; a build that keeps the minimum when a cheaper part is
    added; a scorer that forgives an inherited error which a later amendment restores.
11. **Costs, as disclosure only.** Per arm and amendment: solver calls, checker seconds and wall time on the
    critical path, and the campaign's dirty fraction. No ratio between arms is printed: a ratio needs a
    registration admitted by the ceiling check, and the stated ceiling here is the dirty fraction.
12. **Records.** Through the gate runner: the row of GATE-AUGUR-12 (inherited errors in arms B and C, pooled
    unnecessary-invalidation ratio, the cost fixture's two flags) and the M3 dependency-grade row of
    GATE-ATEL-11 (decisions equal to recomputation out of 30 in arm B, unsafe reuse 0, stale-key mutant caught,
    the ratio). A per-rule table (rule name, survivals licensed, inherited errors) is written for the kill
    decision. After the records are written the campaign is marked revealed and moved to the development pool.

**Reading the arms.** GATE-AUGUR-12 is read on arm B and on arm C. An inherited verdict in arm B fails the gate.
An inherited verdict in arm C that arm B does not have fires KILL-AUGUR-REUSE for the rule that licensed it:
that rule's registry status becomes `disabled`, arm C is re-run, and the record shows both runs. Arm D is not a
gate arm; it is the comparator that decides whether work on implication rules continues in a category (only
where engine and incumbent are equally correct).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-12: zero incorrectly inherited verdicts on the 30 amendments in the default arm; pooled
      unnecessary invalidations at most 20% of unaffected verdicts; the cost-9 and cost-5 fixture keeps the
      witness and withdraws the minimum. The row carries measured values and thresholds.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-11, M3 row: 30 of 30 decisions equal full recomputation; zero unsafe reuse; the stale-key mutant
      is caught; the unnecessary-invalidation ratio is counted and reported.
- [ ] The camera fixture's evaluation counts are 2, 0, 0, 1 and 1, 0, 0, 1; the third-gain amendment withdraws
      the inclusion and names the pair; refusal 854 is returned for the reused screen.
- [ ] Every inverse edit costs zero solver calls.
- [ ] Every mask and implication record is accepted by the standalone auditor; a stripped record is rejected.
- [ ] Each of the six mutants makes the script exit non-zero.
- [ ] The per-rule table exists and is cited by the kill decision; the campaign is marked revealed.
- [ ] The log holds counts and digests only for sealed records, never their constants.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own: the engine's are in BD-292 (`crates/frob-verbs/tests/revalidate_engine.rs`), the mission
rules' in N25a-16 (`crates/frob-mission/tests/revalidate_mission.rs`), the scorer's in N25a-17
(`crates/frob-conformance/tests/campaign30.rs`). The comparison over a common refinement used in step 4 is a
small library function in `frob-conformance` with its own tests: `common_refinement_of_two_partitions`
([2, 14/3] ∪ (14/3, 6] against [2, 4] ∪ (4, 6] gives three cells with the right closed ends) and
`differing_closed_end_is_a_difference`.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Log `target/e2e-logs/mission_revalidate_campaign/<utc>.ndjson` through the helper of
STD-E2E, with a transcript beside it. Per call a `cmd` line (exact command, exit code, wall time, evidence
block with claim, class, tier, determinism `exact`, mission, answer and edit identities). Per amendment and
arm: the category, the classified edit (component and direction), per-obligation grade and licence, counts of
surviving, withdrawn and rerun cells, solver calls, the reference decision's digest and the arm's, and
`metric` lines for inherited errors (threshold 0) and unnecessary invalidations. On a mismatch: the first
differing cell with both verdicts and the artifact paths. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed, the
pooled ratio against 1/5, the gates GATE-AUGUR-12 and GATE-ATEL-11. Sealed records appear by digest and
category; their constants are not logged.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-699 (camera fixture; control: a key without delivery time keeps a stale promotion). KA-700 (widening the
doorway: the cost-9 cart stays feasible and its optimality is revoked). KA-701 (the gain 33/32: every positive
decision on each test grid is withdrawn, 34,590 of them; control: a hash match taken as verdict transfer).
KA-638 (best worst-case error 1/65 m). CX-282, CX-287, CX-314, CX-316. Development examples with exact
expected values (public): stance hull narrowed to (±50, ±25) N·m, the inner cell survives and the whole domain
v ∈ [2, 6] becomes inner; ratings 160 → 150 N, the exclusion survives and the inclusion shrinks to v ≤ 4; hull
enlarged to (±70, ±35) N·m, inner v ≤ 22/7; authority changed to passive springs, the inner cell is withdrawn.
Metamorphic relations: MR-10 (edit then inverse), MR-11 (polarity directions), MR-12 (incremental equals
full). Negative controls: the six mutants of step 10. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1
and 8 (grades, masks and results are exact functions of the two states, class D0).

## Considerations

- Thirty amendments on small exact fixtures show correctness and not economics. The ceiling stated before any
  speed test is the dirty fraction; exact reuse of rollouts after a mass or geometry edit is at most about 10%
  of compute (HYPOTHESIS until measured on the project's own memo graph).
- One run spends the campaign. A re-run after a repair needs a newly authored and sealed campaign; the repair
  cycle is recorded with both digests.
- KILL-AUGUR-REUSE is standing from M3: one incorrectly inherited verdict from an implication rule removes that
  rule, and reuse falls back to the dependency-keyed memo graph. This script produces the evidence; the
  decision is N25a-19.
- The seed list places the frozen campaign in the M4 conformance bead while both gates are due at M3; the
  campaign is created at M3 and this script runs then. The M4 run (grades 3 and 4 for every verdict kind) uses
  a new campaign.
- RISK-38 (an incomplete memo key serves a stale verdict; high) is the risk this gate exists to bound.
