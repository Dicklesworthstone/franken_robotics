## Background

Augur, the mission-query subsystem (crate `frob-mission`), answers questions about a family of robot designs
by lowering them to small conventional subproblems and checking every result independently. Its standing
decision is **ADR-AUGUR-03, conventional formulations first**: a query is lowered to the most ordinary sound
formulation available, and a specialized solver or kernel is written only after repeated changed queries show,
with measured timings, that the ordinary route is the bottleneck. Reason: in every plan-time pilot the
conventional formulation tied the specialized one. The same discipline applies one level down, to code the
sibling libraries already own: before new mission code is written, the owners worth probing are probed.

The plan records what was found in the simulation sibling (FACT: paths under `frankensim/crates/`, read
2026-11-21 at head `483d09c682f2`; each to be re-read at the pinned commit before a gate cites it). The
signatures quoted in this bead were re-read on 2026-11-22 in a local checkout whose head was `e49c0f7d7657`,
already later than the plan's: a small demonstration that the pin moves.

- `fs-sos` describes polynomial models (univariate `Poly`; multivariate `MPoly` and interval `IPoly`),
  sum-of-squares programs lowered to a block semidefinite program solved by an in-house interior-point method
  with a centred solve that maximizes a uniform Gram margin, Lasserre and Putinar relaxations, and a verifier
  that encloses residuals with interval arithmetic and treats the numerical solve as untrusted (with two cases
  at the pin in which the univariate bound functions return a value that is not a bound).
- `fs-ivl` provides outward-rounded intervals, affine forms and multivariate Taylor models
  (`multi_taylor.rs`: `range_subdomain`, `recenter`) and interval Newton with
  `DEFAULT_MAX_ROOT_BOXES = 65_536` (`newton.rs`).
- `fs-regime` exposes a π-group basis, scaling maps and model admission.
- `fs-voi` exposes ranking-flip probability, expected opportunity loss and `recommend_unknown_resolutions`.
- `fs-eproc` contains sequential evidence routines.
- `fs-checker` re-checks evidence packages outside the solving process through a closed registry of semantic
  witness kinds.
- Missing in the suite: a paving driver, contractors, interval forms of the robot static analyses, a
  trajectory optimizer and any homotopy solver.

All are reached through the airlock crate `frob-fsim` unless the suite chapter lists them as bedrock-direct.
The first two are admitted for mission work by their own bead (the second linear-program solver, `fs-ivl` and
`fs-sos`). This bead does the remaining four and turns every fact above into something that fails when it
stops being true.

Why a bead and not a note: a FACT about a sibling at a commit decays silently when the pin moves. A sentence
in a contract that says "the suite has no paving driver" is how a second paving driver gets written a year
after the owner shipped one, and a sentence that says "`recommend_unknown_resolutions` exists" is how a build
breaks at a milestone exit. Rejected alternatives: re-reading by hand at each gate (not repeatable, and nobody
does it under deadline); admitting all four crates above the airlock now (admission is never inherited and is
done at the milestone of first use, by its own known answer and negative control).

The robotics-specific work that no owner supplies is the mapping from a causal mission and a morphology to
sound small subproblems, and the cross-check from each result back to the mission; every such mapping has a
deliberately invalid fixture (proof obligation PO-AUGUR-01). That is where mission code is written.

## Scope

In: one probe record per crate (`fs-regime`, `fs-voi`, `fs-eproc`, `fs-checker`); executable pin facts for all
six crates named above; the reuse decision per mission mechanism; the register of missing pieces with the
bead that builds each and its switch-over rule. Out: admission above the airlock (the group gates); the
admission of `fs-ivl`, `fs-sos` and the second solver (their own bead); any wrapper beyond the narrow trait a
probe needs; any change in a sibling repository.

## Technical approach

1. **Pin facts as tests** (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/owner_probes_augur.rs`, compiled against the
   pinned siblings through `frob-fsim` features; DESIGN). Each fact is a test that names the path it was read
   from and fails with the message "foundation fact changed: re-read and update the probe record":
   - `fs_ivl_has_range_subdomain_and_recenter` (the two functions resolve and run on x·(1 − x) over [0, 1]);
   - `fs_ivl_default_max_root_boxes_is_65536` (a constant assertion);
   - `fs_sos_verifier_treats_solve_as_untrusted` (a Gram proposal perturbed in one entry is not certified);
   - `fs_sos_has_multivariate_program_and_interval_verifier` (the modules `mpoly`, `sdp`, `program`, `verify`
     and `global` resolve; `verify` proves positive definiteness by interval Cholesky);
   - `fs_regime_pi_groups_exists` (`pi::pi_groups(&[Input]) -> Result<PiBasis, RegimeError>`),
     `fs_voi_recommend_unknown_resolutions_exists`, `fs_voi_expected_opportunity_loss_exists`,
     `fs_eproc_betting_eprocess_exists` (symbol-level, each with one call on a two-line input);
   - `fs_checker_registry_is_closed` (the sibling's semantic registry declares exactly two witness families,
     `frankensim/exact-interval` and `frankensim/bounded-linf-residual`; an evidence package with an unknown
     family is rejected by the sibling's own checker, run as a child process in the test; nothing of it is
     linked).
   A fact that cannot be expressed as a call (a count of lines, a manifest description) is recorded in the
   probe record with its commit and is not asserted.
2. **Probe record per crate** (rows of the foundation ledger with `kind = "probe"`; fields: crate, commit,
   toolchain, library lines, whether it needs `fs-qty`, the airlock group, posture, the mission mechanisms
   that would use it, the probe's known answer and negative control, decision, date).

   | Crate | Size at the pin (FACT) | Reached how | Mission use | Probe known answer | Negative control | Decision recorded |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | `fs-regime` | 2,277 library lines; needs `fs-qty`. `pi::pi_groups` computes the integer nullspace of the 6 × n dimension matrix (SI exponents m, kg, s, K, A, mol) by exact fraction-free elimination over `i128`; `scaling::ScalingMap`; `cards::admit` with a distance to validity | Airlock, group `stats` | Dimensionless groups as the exponent chart of monomial screens and of design charts; refusing a lowering used outside its regime of validity | For the quantities of the leg fixture (load F = 117.72 N, lever arm ℓ = 0.2 m, reduction N = 10, torque τ = 2 N·m, foot speed v = 1.5 m/s, motor speed ω = 80 rad/s) `pi_groups` returns rank 3 and 6 − 3 = 3 groups, and the exponent vector of F·v/(τ·ω) is an integer combination of them; its value is 176.58/160 = 8829/8000 | τ entered as a force (in N): F·v/(τ·ω) then has the dimension of a length and the membership test must fail | Use through the airlock when the first screen needs it; no code written here |
   | `fs-voi` | 1,044 library lines (1,715 with tests); closure 5; needs `fs-qty`. `ranking_flip_probability(chosen, other)` is the probability that `other` is in fact better under Gaussian objectives that are minimized, Φ((mean_chosen − mean_other)/σ) with σ the root sum of squares of the two total standard deviations; `expected_opportunity_loss` integrates by quadrature with 256 panels | Airlock, group `stats` (gate INT-FSIM-14) | Ordering the cell worklist and ranking next tests by expected opportunity loss | Chosen design with mean 0 and total standard deviation 3/5, other design with mean 1 and 4/5: ranking-flip probability Φ(−1) = 0.158655253931457…, to 10⁻¹² | The two arguments swapped return 1 − Φ(−1) ≈ 0.8413 and the test fails; this also pins the minimizing convention | Use for scheduling only; a score never enters a verdict. A mission cost is minimized, so costs are passed unchanged and utilities with their sign reversed |
   | `fs-eproc` | In the airlock group `optimize`. `BettingEProcess::new(null_mean)` is a one-sided betting e-process for H₀: mean ≤ `null_mean` on outcomes in [0, 1], with a predictable plug-in bet λ = clamp((μ̂ − m)/σ̂², 0, (1/2)/max(m, 1 − m)) computed from past data only; also `PairwiseRace`, `GaussianMixtureCs`, `combine_product` | Not reached by `frob-mission` | Empirical cells go through the study types of the task subsystem, which owns the sequential tests | With null mean 1/2 and outcomes (1, 1): the first bet is 0 (no data), so the e-value stays 1; the second bet is 1 (μ̂ = 3/4, σ̂² = 1/16, capped at 1), so the e-value becomes 1 + 1·(1 − 1/2) = 3/2, to 10⁻¹² | A process that bets on the first outcome (λ ≠ 0 before any data) gives an e-value other than 1 after one observation; an outcome outside [0, 1] is refused by the sibling because it voids the guarantee | No direct edge from `frob-mission`; recorded so that none is added later |
   | `fs-checker` | 3,851 library lines; needs `fs-qty`. Its semantic module is a closed registry of two witness families (`frankensim/exact-interval`, `frankensim/bounded-linf-residual`) with size caps (4,096 interval nodes; residual dimension 128; 10⁶ operations); external callbacks authenticate origins and cannot add a family or manufacture a pass | Pattern only; not linked | The model for the offline auditor: a standalone re-check through a closed registry of witness kinds, each recomputed from primitive inputs under bounded work | An evidence package from the sibling's own tests is accepted by its checker run as a child process | The same package with an unknown witness family is rejected | Adopt the pattern (closed registry, bounded work per kind, out-of-process re-check); never a dependency, because the auditor may depend on nothing |

3. **Reuse decisions per mechanism** (written into the crate contract's "oracles and owners" section):
   scheduling and next-test ranking call `fs-voi` behind a trait `OpportunityLoss` and fall back to "largest
   unresolved measure first" when the group is off, with identical verdicts either way (invariant
   INV-AUGUR-10: scheduling heuristics can change the order and cost of work and cannot change any verdict);
   enclosures are proposed by `fs-ivl` and recomputed by the auditor's own arithmetic; polynomial bounds are
   proposed by `fs-sos` and checked exactly; sequential evidence is the task subsystem's; nothing in the
   auditor comes from a sibling.
4. **Register of what the suite lacks**, each with the bead that builds it in this repository and a
   switch-over rule (if the owner gains the piece, the probe test for its absence fails, the in-tree piece is
   compared with the owner's on the fixtures of its gate, and the owner's is preferred when it passes them):

   | Missing piece | Built here by | Absence asserted by |
   |---|---|---|
   | A paving driver | the region-certificate beads (per-constraint pavings) | `fs_ivl_has_no_paving_driver` (no public item matching the driver's role; a recorded search, re-run by script) |
   | Contractors | the same beads | `fs_ivl_has_no_contractors` |
   | Interval forms of the robot static analyses | the region-certificate beads, on top of the static analyses' expression graphs | recorded search |
   | A trajectory optimizer | the trajectory-optimization lane of `frob-synth` | recorded search across the suite's manifests |
   | Any homotopy solver | the continuation lane of `frob-synth` | recorded search |

   The "recorded search" is a script, `scripts/probes/suite_absence.sh`, that greps the pinned sibling
   checkouts for the public names a piece would have (a fixed pattern list per piece) and prints the hits; it
   is evidence for a human decision and not a proof of absence, and the record says so.
5. **Firewall assertions** added to `xtask check-deps`: `frob-mission` has no direct edge to `fs-eproc`,
   `fs-checker`, `fs-voi` or `fs-regime` (the last two only through `frob-fsim` features); `frob-cert` has no
   `fs-*` crate in its graph.
6. **When the pin moves.** The stack lock's update procedure runs this bead's tests; a failing pin fact blocks
   the lock update until the probe record is re-read and re-dated.

Determinism: the probes are exact or to stated tolerance; none produces a claim about a robot.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Four probe records exist in the foundation ledger with commit, toolchain, posture, decision and date.
- [ ] Every fact listed in the Background that can be expressed as a call is a passing test at the pin, and
      each such test fails when its fact is falsified (shown once per test by a planted edit in a scratch copy
      of the sibling: a renamed function, a changed constant).
- [ ] The `fs-voi` probe returns Φ(−1) to 10⁻¹²; the `fs-regime` probe returns a basis of three groups whose
      span contains F·v/(τ·ω); both negative controls are caught.
- [ ] `cargo tree -p frob-mission` shows `fs-voi` and `fs-regime` only with the `stats` feature of
      `frob-fsim`, and never `fs-eproc` or `fs-checker`; `cargo tree -p frob-cert` shows no `fs-*` crate.
- [ ] With the `stats` feature off the stance and pusher answers are byte-identical to those with it on.
- [ ] The register of missing pieces names a building bead for each of the five and the absence script runs
      and prints its hit list.
- [ ] No sibling repository is modified.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/owner_probes_augur.rs`: the eight pin-fact tests of step 1;
`voi_ranking_flip_is_phi_minus_one` (√(9/25 + 16/25) = 1, so the probability is Φ((0 − 1)/1));
`voi_argument_swap_is_caught`; `regime_pi_basis_has_three_members` (dimension matrix of rank 3 over length,
mass and time for six quantities; one possible basis is N, τ/(F·ℓ) and ω·ℓ/v);
`regime_power_ratio_is_in_span` (F·v and τ·ω both have dimension kg·m²·s⁻³; the exponent vector is minus the
sum of the last two basis groups); `regime_wrong_dimension_rejected`;
`eproc_first_bet_is_zero_then_three_halves`; `checker_accepts_own_package_rejects_unknown_family` (child
process).
`crates/frob-mission/tests/scheduling.rs`: `opportunity_loss_order_does_not_change_answer_bytes` (stance and
pusher, feature on and off; property test over 10³ random permutations of the worklist).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/augur_owner_probes.sh` runs the probe tests at the pinned commits, the absence script and the two
`cargo tree` assertions, then the stance query twice (feature on, feature off) and compares answer digests.
Logging through the helper of STD-E2E to `target/e2e-logs/augur_owner_probes/<utc>.ndjson`: per crate a `cmd`
line with the sibling commit and toolchain, an `assert` line per pin fact, `metric` lines for the two known
answers with their tolerances, the hit list of the absence script, the two answer digests, and a `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Φ(−1) = 0.158655253931457 (ranking-flip probability at Δ = 1, s₁ = 3/5, s₂ = 4/5; the form
Φ(−Δ/√(s₁² + s₂²)) is the group gate's known answer). The leg fixture's power ratio F·v/(τ·ω) = 176.58/160 =
8829/8000. The betting e-process at null mean 1/2 after outcomes (1, 1): 3/2. Negative controls: the argument
swap; the wrong dimension; the bet placed before any data; the unknown witness family; the planted edits that falsify each pin fact; a scheduling mutant that drops a cell when its
score is negative (caught by the byte comparison). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1
and 8 for the scheduling test.

## Considerations

- A probe is not an admission. Nothing here licenses use above the airlock; the group gate does, at the
  milestone of first use, at the pin of that day.
- `fs-regime` and `fs-voi` need `fs-qty`, so they are tied to the suite's nightly toolchain; the probes run in
  the lanes that have it and are recorded `not run` elsewhere, never as passed.
- The plan-time pilots used none of the sibling dynamics, contact or sensor code; what they established is
  that individual witness types can be computed by small conventional programs. The integrated interface
  remains a hypothesis until the first mission gate, and these probes do not change that.
- The absence script cannot prove absence. Its value is that the question is asked again, by the same
  patterns, every time the pin moves.
- If an owner ships a paving driver or a trajectory optimizer, the in-tree piece is not deleted on sight: it is
  compared on its gate's fixtures first, and the comparison is recorded.
