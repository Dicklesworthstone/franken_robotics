## Background

A mission answer is strongest when it covers a region of designs and not only the points an agent proposed: a
certified box around a design is a statement about every as-built unit inside it. A region certificate for one
constraint c(θ) ≤ 0 on a box B of design parameters is an outward-rounded enclosure [c](B): if its upper end is at
most 0 the box is feasible for that constraint; if its lower end is above 0 it is infeasible; otherwise it is
undecided and is bisected.

What is deliberately not attempted is paving a joint design box of ten to twenty dimensions. The arithmetic: 10⁴
boxes are 13 bisections; in 20 dimensions that is less than one cut per axis, and uniform boxes have side 0.64 of
the domain (0.41 in 10 dimensions), so a constraint surface that depends on most parameters crosses nearly every
box. The published record agrees (FACT, source register; each figure to be re-read from its primary source before
a gate cites it): interval design of parallel robots is described by its practitioners as exponential branch and
bound suited to small and medium problems, with a worked example of five design parameters; a three-dimensional
dextrous-workspace paving took about five hours and left an error bound of up to a third of the certified volume;
Taylor models delay the growth with dimension very little when dependent intervals lack cancellation structure.

What is planned instead (ADR-AUGUR-06: region evidence is per constraint, with a bounded split set) is
certificates for one constraint over the parameters it depends on, bisected along at most about six of them and
combined by products, intersections and monotonicity. Whether that decides a useful share of a real family is
bet BET-20, and it is a Frontier mechanism, so a paper-scale pilot must run before its work package (mission
queries at scale and region certificates, M4) is funded. This bead is that pilot and the decision it feeds. The
ceiling of benefit is stated before any speed test: none is claimed; pointwise static checks with an existing
library give the same pruning for proposed designs. The value sought is evidence quality and tolerance
robustness.

Killed forms, recorded so that nobody rebuilds them: sampling only inside a certified inner ellipsoid (it cuts
off the feasibility boundary, where optima sit, and misses disconnected islands); a logarithmic change of
variables to linearize posynomial constraints for an integer-linear decision procedure (the logarithm of a
posynomial is a convex log-sum-exp, neither linear nor polynomial); cylindrical algebraic decomposition for
reachable sets (doubly exponential beyond four or five variables).

## Technical approach

1. Protocol frozen before execution (the values below are the DESIGN default; the pilot record fixes them, with
   their digest, before the first run). The box has twelve parameters: three link lengths, two hip offsets,
   three masses, gear ratio, motor mass, body length and body width.

| Parameter | Symbol | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Thigh, shank, foot-link length | ℓ₁, ℓ₂, ℓ₃ | [0.15, 0.30], [0.15, 0.30], [0.02, 0.08] m |
| Hip abduction offset, hip fore-aft offset | d_a, d_h | [0.03, 0.10], [0.00, 0.08] m |
| Body, thigh, shank mass | m_b, m₁, m₂ | [4, 12], [0.3, 1.0], [0.1, 0.5] kg |
| Gear ratio | N | [6, 30] |
| Motor mass | m_m | [0.2, 0.6] kg |
| Body length, body width | L, B | [0.35, 0.70], [0.15, 0.35] m |

   Derived: total mass M = m_b + 4·(m₁ + m₂) + 12·m_m; g = 9.81 m/s²; joint torque available
   τ_av = η·N·k_τ·m_m with η = 0.85 and torque density k_τ = 3 N·m/kg.
   Constraints (each written as c(θ) ≤ 0):
   - Static knee torque in three stance postures p (knee interior angle φ_p = 150°, 110°, 80°; foot load
     F_p = M·g/4, M·g/4 and M·g/3 for the three-leg stance): F_p·ℓ₁·ℓ₂·sin φ_p / √(ℓ₁² + ℓ₂² − 2·ℓ₁·ℓ₂·cos φ_p)
     − τ_av ≤ 0. The fraction is the distance from the knee to the hip–foot line times the load.
   - Static hip abduction torque in the same postures: F_p·d_a − τ_av ≤ 0.
   - Reach to the foothold set: 0.45 − (ℓ₁ + ℓ₂ + ℓ₃) ≤ 0 and |ℓ₁ − ℓ₂| − 0.10 ≤ 0.
   - Self-clearance: 0.02 − (L − 2·d_h − 1.2·(ℓ₁ + ℓ₂)) ≤ 0 (fore and hind feet do not meet) and
     0.5·(ℓ₁ + ℓ₂) − (B + 2·d_a) ≤ 0 (track width against leg length).
   - A mass budget M − 14 ≤ 0, which is monotone in each mass.
2. The pilot program is a small standalone tool with existing libraries, outside the workspace
   (`tools/pilots/region-box/`, with its tool and pin recorded): outward-rounded intervals, affine forms, a
   forward–backward contractor over the expression tree, and a bisection driver with a box counter.
3. Four arms, each with a budget of 10⁴ boxes:
   - Arm 1, plain intervals: bisect the joint 12-parameter box along the widest relative side; a box is inner if
     every constraint's enclosure has upper end at most 0, excluded if some constraint's lower end is above 0.
   - Arm 2, affine forms: the same paving with affine-form enclosures.
   - Arm 3, contractors: the same paving with forward–backward contraction on each constraint before bisecting.
   - Arm 4, per-constraint decomposition: for each constraint, pave only the parameters it reads, bisecting at
     most six of them (the knee-torque constraint reads seven; the six with the largest affine coefficients are
     split and the seventh is enclosed at full range), 10⁴ boxes per constraint. A design is decided inner if it
     lies in every constraint's inner set and decided excluded if it lies in some constraint's excluded set;
     the joint decided share is estimated from 10⁶ seeded sample points and reported with its sampling interval.
   Every arm classifies a box by the three-way verdict of the C-32 contract (rule C-32.R1 of N10c-04: enclosure
   [c](B) = [lo, hi]; hi ≤ 0 feasible for that constraint, lo > 0 infeasible, otherwise undecided and bisected),
   and the box budget is read through its paving arithmetic (C-32.R7: after k bisections in n dimensions a uniform
   box keeps 2^(−k/n) of each side), so the pilot measures the contract the product will implement.
4. Measurements, per arm: the undecided share of the box volume; the inner and excluded shares; per constraint,
   the share of its own sub-box that is decided; and the attribution of decided volume: for each constraint, the
   share of all decided volume that it decides alone. The two numbers the decision reads are (a) the undecided
   share of the best arm and (b) the largest share of decided volume that falls to a single monotone bound (the
   mass budget, or a reach bound).
5. Soundness spot check inside the pilot: 10⁶ exact point evaluations; no point may contradict a box its arm
   decided. One contradiction voids the arm.
6. Falsifier (TARGET, fixed in advance): the best arm leaves more than 90% undecided, or more than 95% of the
   decided volume falls to a single monotone bound. Cost: about two engineer-days and CPU minutes (TARGET).
7. Decision (kill gate KILL-AUGUR-REGION, decided at M3 on this pilot; a second reading is taken at M4 if the
   region gate fails after one repair cycle).
   - Trigger met: region certificates are removed. Family answers use dual witnesses, monotone bounds and
     pointwise static analyses at proposed designs; untested regions are reported as sample coverage, class
     `Estimated`. Downstream: the driver, the combination, the M4 integration and their gate script are closed
     as `superseded` with the ADR's identifier; the weak-variable extension is not built; a request for a region
     certificate is refused with the per-constraint decomposition no longer offered; the enclosure proof kinds
     stay in the registry for their other uses (linkage circuits, tolerance partitions); BET-20's fallback is
     adopted: family answers are reported as tested sample sets with witnesses and make no inner-set claim.
   - Trigger not met: the work package is funded for the arm that won, and the recorded shares become the
     baseline of the M4 gate (the per-constraint arm decides at least 50% of each constraint's sub-box volume
     within 10⁴ boxes; no single monotone bound gives more than 95% of decided volume; zero certified boxes
     contradicted by 10⁶ point evaluations).
8. Record: the pilot's record in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (schema
   `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`, N38a-09: mechanism and tier, protocol digest, falsifier, ceiling "none claimed",
   fallback and kill gate, cost, tool and pin, result). The record already exists as row 3 of the Augur pilot
   rows, id `PILOT-C-32` (N25d-40, a dependency; carrier BD-289), entered without a protocol; this bead stores
   the protocol's digest in it before the first run, which makes it `registered`, and appends the
   `[[pilot.result]]` with the two numbers and the outcome against the falsifier, its receipt committed at
   `receipts/pilots/<hash>.json` (N38a-09, rule 2a); it adds no second record. The decision is
   `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-REGION-M3.md` with the required fields N40-03 lists, recorded by a session that did
   not implement the pilot. With the ADR the kill row KILL-AUGUR-REGION (entered by N40-26 with
   `decided = "M3"` and `reread = ["M4"]`) gains its M3 decision entry (`exit = "M3"`, `outcome`, `adr`,
   `date`, `session`, `receipts`: the pilot result's receipt), and this bead, the deciding bead in N25d-41's
   map, writes the owner declaration that N40-30 compares with the ledger, in `frob-mission`'s `contract.toml`:
   `[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`, the ledger's current `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads`
   copied, not restated, and the `decision` block with each measured value citing its receipt; without it
   `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` fails `owner-declaration-missing` at the M3 exit. The M4 reading of step 7, if
   it is taken, is written by the M4 gate script (N25b-11, after this bead) as `ADR-KILL-AUGUR-REGION-M4.md`. The verdict of BET-20 is its own bead's (BET-20), which reads this
   record and never re-decides it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The protocol (box, constraints, arms, budgets, seeds, falsifier) is on file with its digest dated before
      the first run.
- [ ] The seed's condition: the undecided share of the best arm and the largest share from one monotone bound
      are on file, with per-arm and per-constraint tables.
- [ ] KILL-AUGUR-REGION is decided by `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-REGION-M3.md` before the M4 work package is
      funded, with the trigger evaluated against its two numbers (90% and 95%) and the downstream list applied;
      the pilot's `[[pilot.result]]` is appended to row 3, `PILOT-C-32`, of the Augur pilot records in
      `pilots.toml`, whose protocol digest was stored before the first run.
- [ ] The kill row carries the M3 decision entry, written with the ADR by a session that did not implement the
      pilot, and the owner declaration of KILL-AUGUR-REGION is in `frob-mission`'s `contract.toml`;
      `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` passes for the row.
- [ ] Zero decided boxes are contradicted by the 10⁶ point evaluations in any arm that is reported.
- [ ] Negative controls: (a) a planted constraint that reads all twelve parameters with equal weight
      (Σ θᵢ/widthᵢ ≤ 6 in normalized coordinates) is reported as almost entirely undecided by arms 1 to 3, which
      shows the measurement can see failure; (b) a family in which only the mass budget decides anything trips
      the 95% clause; (c) an arm with outward rounding disabled is caught by the point-evaluation check.
- [ ] The pilot ran outside the workspace with existing tools; its tool pins and its cost are recorded.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In the pilot tool (`tools/pilots/region-box/tests/`): `knee_torque_closed_form` (at ℓ₁ = ℓ₂ = 0.2 m, φ = 110°,
the knee–line distance is 0.2·0.2·sin 110° / √(0.08 − 0.08·cos 110°) = 0.1147 m to four digits);
`enclosure_contains_samples` (property: random points inside a box evaluate inside the box's enclosure);
`contractor_never_loses_solutions` (property: a feasible point survives contraction); `box_budget_respected`;
`volume_accounting_sums_to_one` (inner + excluded + undecided = 1 within the sampling interval);
`monotone_attribution` (on a two-constraint toy where a mass budget decides 60% and a torque bound 40%, the
attribution returns those shares).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_region_box.sh` runs the pilot tool for the four arms with fixed seeds and writes
`target/e2e-logs/pilot_region_box/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript through the shared helper: `start` with the
protocol digest and tool pins; one `metric` line per arm (boxes used, undecided share, inner share, excluded
share), one per constraint (decided share of its sub-box), the two decision numbers against 0.90 and 0.95, the
count of point contradictions against 0; `assert` lines for the negative controls; on a contradiction, the
arm, the box bounds, the enclosure, the contradicting point and its exact value, and the seed and protocol path
needed to reproduce it; a final `summary`. The script
drives no `frob` verb, because a pilot runs before the mechanism exists; the evidence block is recorded as
"pilot; class Tested; synthetic model".

### Known answers and negative controls

The arithmetic known answer of the mechanism: 10⁴ boxes are 13 bisections; a uniform box keeps 0.64 of each side
in 20 dimensions and 0.41 in 10 (2^(−13/20) = 0.637, 2^(−13/10) = 0.406). The stance family is the standing
contrast: a parametric dual with a sign proof covers a whole cell at once, which is why linear static families
need no paving. CX-275 (sample points in a cell fail, so the cell is excluded) is the shortcut the enclosure rule
replaces. Negative controls: the three listed above. Determinism: fixed seeds give identical tables on two runs.

## Considerations

- OPEN-AUGUR-3 (the measured sparsity of the constraint-to-parameter graph, and the coupled dimension that
  linkage closure adds; default: region certificates are refused above six parameters) is measured on the real
  robot classes by a separate bead; the pilot's model is a stand-in and is labelled `synthetic`.
- The pilot's static model says nothing about dynamic performance, run-up, landing or recovery; a region
  certificate covers the static rigid model named in the bundle and nothing else.
- Deciding volume is not the same as saving simulations; the pilot must not be read as a speed result.
- If the falsifier holds, the fallback is the plan, not a failure: BET-20 names it in advance.
