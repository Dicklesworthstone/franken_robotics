## Background

Redesigning the body and designing the world are two sides of one decision problem. Given feasible rivals d_A and
d_B, `mission.separate` searches a physically admitted world family for a world where the predicted preference
changes by a practically important margin, and returns it minimized, with the edit that removes the effect. Its
registered output: an executable, physically admissible pair of worlds in which a design ordering or a diagnosis
changes, minimized, with the edit that removes the effect. What a design agent does with it: learns which terrain
or interior feature exposes a failure and ships it as a regression. The smallest such change is often more
informative than the largest simulated reward: for a threshold, one changed edge radius; for a warehouse, a short
compliant mat under load; for a kitchen, a joint condition of layout, optics and friction near a turn.

The verb is the public face of two pieces built separately: the admissibility contract (what may be asked, which
worlds and edits count, how smallness is ordered) and the separation engine (search, referee reproduction,
minimization, first-cause check) over the adversarial-worlds machinery. This bead adds the verb, the planted
reversals that test it, its end-to-end script, and the record of its gate. Tier: Frontier. It exists only on the
branch where the kill gate KILL-AUGUR-SEPARATE did not fire (trigger: minimization changes the failure mechanism
in more than 20% of planted cases, or a conventional scenario tool with the same generator matches the result at
equal cost; fallback: ship the adversarial search and minimization without the design-pair query and keep
counterexamples as regressions).

The formal object (construction C-18): for closed loops c = (d, π) with a fixed controller, c R w holds when
every admitted execution of c in w is accepted; the separating set is S(c_A, c_B) = { w : c_A R w differs from
c_B R w }; the verb returns the admitted world in S nearest to nominal, where nearest means fewest edits in
authored units and then least normalized physical distance. That world is the dual of the minimal design repair.

## Technical approach

1. Verb (kind `run`; cluster `mission`; MCP tool `mission_separate`). The registry row's form is canonical:
   `frob mission separate q:5b1e --designs d:a3f1,d:b77e --key sep-1 --json`, where `q:` addresses a stored
   separation query of N25b-22 (decision, base world, legality set, smallness, budget, the two frozen policies)
   and `--designs` names the two rivals. The inline form (DESIGN) builds the same query in one call:
   `frob mission separate --a d:… --policy-a p:… --b d:… --policy-b p:… --task t:… ((--requirement <atom> |
   --metric <name>) --margin <value> | --diagnosis <label>) --base-world w:… --legality l:… --key … --json`; the
   three decision flags are the variants of N25b-22's `Decision` (a preference on a requirement or a metric
   with its margin, or a diagnosis that changes), so the registry's input "two designs or two diagnoses" is
   covered. Input schema `frob.mission.separate.in/1`; an idempotency key and a budget are required;
   `--estimate` returns the cost of search, reproduction and minimization separately and runs nothing.
2. Output schema `frob.mission.separate.out/1` (DESIGN), in the standard envelope:
   - `separated: true` with: `worlds` (two `.fmcw` addresses: the minimized separating world and its nearest
     passing neighbour); `edit_list` (each edit with path, from, to, units and the causal path it changes);
     `removing_edit`; `smallness` (edit count, normalized distance); `first_cause` and `failure` (class, binding
     atom, margin) for the losing design, before and after minimization, with `mechanism_preserved`;
     `minimality` (`1-minimal`, `minimum-among-pairs` or `non-minimal(k)`), with the number of tests and the
     seeds failing (for example 61/64); `referee` (reproduced, with the variants tried); `legality` (inside,
     touched bounds with provenance) and, separately, `prior`; `traces` (four replay addresses with state
     digests); `absolute` (each design's own hard-limit verdicts in both worlds, so that a relative reversal is
     never read as either design meeting the mission); `pair` (the pair search's record of the returned
     reversal, carried through from N26a-10 by the engine N25b-23: the preference gap g = S_A − S_B on the
     separating world and the printed reversal score −g, the reversal kind (`verdict` or `margin`), both
     verdicts, the solvability oracle's class for the losing design, and the artifact guard's outcome for each
     design); `search` (per evaluated world its address, the flip probability and rank it had when proposed,
     and its gap; the ranking orders work and is never an input to a verdict or a count);
   - or `separated: false` with `none_found: true`, the budget used and the best reversal score reached. The
     verb never reports "none exists".
   The evidence block has class `Tested`, the tier of the reproduction, the determinism class of that tier, and
   claim Design comparison; the three no-authorization flags are present and false.
3. Job behaviour. A separation is a long query: after the bounded synchronous wait it returns a job handle
   (exit 8); progress reports candidates evaluated, reversals found, and minimization tests done; cancellation
   drains and returns the best reproduced reversal so far with its actual minimality label.
4. Refusals surfaced, as the registry row lists them: FROB-REF-859 (raised at admission by N25b-22: an
   experiment or world edit of the query cannot be realized through the declared interfaces, or does not
   declare the causal path it changes); FROB-REF-360 (passed through from the legality check of BD-270: a world
   or edit lies outside the legality profile, checked per edit and again on the final world; the violated bound
   is named with its provenance and the nearest legal value attached); FROB-REF-362 (passed through from the
   declaration check of a typed edit during search or minimization: `changes` empty or unknown, an aspect whose
   digest changed without its tag, several changes bundled in one edit). All three exit 3. Also the budget
   family when the estimate exceeds the session budget, and the two admission refusals of N25b-22 that the
   allocation ledger numbers (N35x-02's DESIGN slugs, each raised through one named constant of N25b-22, exit
   3): `decision-names-shaping-reward` (family `5xx`: the decision names a shaping reward rather than a
   hard-limit monitor) and `world-edit-scale-undeclared` (family `3xx`: a world edit on a field with no declared
   scale, the field named and no scale invented). This bead prints neither number; the script reads each from
   the ledger by its slug.
5. The ten planted design reversals, authored, validated and sealed by N25bp1-01 (each a pair of designs with
   frozen controllers, a base world in which the stated preference holds, a legality set, and a planted minimal
   edit set hidden among five to eight distractor edits that do not affect the decision; cases 1 and 2 are
   public known answers, the other eight are sealed):

| # | Scene and design pair | Planted minimal edits | Causal path |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Split-grip landing; soles 40 mm and 70 mm | Strip offset +12 mm to −12 mm | material |
| 2 | The same, reflected to the right side | The mirrored offset | material |
| 3 | Door sill; two wheel radii | One changed edge radius of the lip | geometry |
| 4 | Door sill, rear pull-over; two carts | Payload shift from 0 to +0.04 m | actors (payload) |
| 5 | Warehouse aisle; small castor and large wheel | A short compliant mat under load | material |
| 6 | Kitchen turn; two sensor mounts | Layout, lighting and floor friction near the turn | geometry, optics, material |
| 7 | Docking; fast and slow actuator | A camera packet delayed by one control tick | clocks |
| 8 | Corridor; depth camera and lidar base | One light moved (depth dropout) | optics |
| 9 | Door on the route; light and heavy base | Door-closer torque raised | actors |
| 10 | Tiled crossing; two sole laws | A film on the tile and a payload shift | material, actors |

   Each fixture records the losing design's planted failure class and binding atom. Cases 1 to 5 and 7 to 9
   have one planted edit, case 10 two, case 6 three.
6. Gate measurement. For each planted case run the verb and assert: an admissible world pair is returned; the
   minimized edit list has at most three edits and equals the planted set (or a subset that still reverses,
   with continuous values bisected toward nominal); the first cause and failure class are unchanged by
   minimization; the reversal is confirmed on the referee. The gate run reads the sealed population of
   N25bp1-01 at the engine revision the decision (N25b-26) pinned, and the script checks both against the
   freeze record before the first case. The receipt is entered with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
   record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-13 --receipt <hash> --part M3` (N38b-19); the controls of the acceptance
   list run in the same invocation, so that a control that is not caught fails the record.

      `mission.separate` returns an admissible world pair whose minimized edit list has at most 3 edits and

      closes with reason `fallback` and the name of the fallback.
- [ ] Every returned world replays with identical state digests on its originating backend, carries its
      passing neighbour and its solvability evidence, and lists legality and prior in separate fields.
- [ ] The answer includes each design's absolute hard-limit verdicts in both worlds.
- [ ] On a pair of identical designs the verb returns `separated: false` with "none found".
- [ ] The verb is reachable through `frob --json` and the generated tool, accepts `--estimate`, returns exit 8
      with a job handle on a long run, and survives cancellation with a correctly labelled partial result.
- [ ] The answer's `pair` block carries the gap, both verdicts, the reversal kind, the oracle class and both
      guard outcomes, and its `search` block the flip-probability rank of every evaluated world; the script
      logs them per case.
- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-13 is recorded through `record_gate` from the gate run's receipt, with the sealed population's
      freeze digest and the pinned engine revision in the record.
- [ ] Negative controls: (a) a build without the first-cause check (a registered `mutation` control in
      `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml`, a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch of `frob-mission`
      built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-mission/nc-<nnnn>`, numbered at registration by N38b-04)
      passes a planted mechanism-changing case as minimized and the script catches it; (b) a public control
      case whose reversal exists only on the fast tier (`fixtures/separation/controls/fast-tier-only/`, authored
      here, not one of the ten) is dropped at referee reproduction and reported as tier-sensitive; (c) an edit without a declared causal
      path is refused with FROB-REF-859; (d) a query whose legality set is narrowed so that the planted edit of
      case 3 lies outside it, offered that edit, is refused with FROB-REF-360 naming the bound and its
      provenance; (e) an edit that bundles a material and a geometry change under one tag is refused with
      FROB-REF-362 by the declaration check.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/separate_verb.rs` and `crates/frob-verbs/tests/mission_separate.rs`:
- `schema_round_trip`: the output document validates against `frob.mission.separate.out/1`.
- `identical_designs_none_found`.
- `absolute_verdicts_present`.
- `estimate_runs_nothing`.
- `registry_form_equals_inline_form`: `q:` with `--designs` and the inline flags build queries with the same
  digest.
- `refusals_passed_through`: FROB-REF-360 and FROB-REF-362 from the legality and declaration checks reach the
  envelope unchanged, exit 3.
- `cancel_returns_labelled_partial`.
- `planted_case_one_exact`: case 1 returns the single strip-offset edit (+12 mm to −12 mm), the 40 mm sole
  losing on the separating world (limiting-model capacities 0.360 against 0.431, where the base world gives
  0.690 against 0.619), with the reversal kind the fixture records.
- `mirror_case_two`: case 2 returns the mirrored edit (MR: the mirror of case 1's result).
- `distractors_removed`: all distractor edits are absent from the minimized list in the public cases 1 and 2.
  The eight sealed cases never enter a unit test or a visible fixture (N25bp1-01's seal); they are measured
  only by the gate run of the script.
- `pair_block_carried`: on case 1 the `pair` block's gap is negative, its reversal score is −g, and the oracle
  and both guard outcomes are present; a candidate handed to the verb without an oracle or guard record is
  refused by the engine (N25b-23 step 3b) and never reaches the answer.
- `search_rank_is_order_only`: scrambling the flip-probability ranking leaves the returned pair of case 1
  unchanged at full budget (N26a-10's scramble test, through the verb).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_separate.sh`, plain shell over the real `frob` binary, seed-explicit and idempotent. Steps:
admission cases (one well-formed query in the registry form and the same in the inline form, one with an
undeclared causal path, one with a reward-based decision, one edit on a field with no declared scale, one
illegal edit, one bundled edit: controls (c) to (e) and the two ledger-numbered refusals, each through
`e2e_expect_refusal` with its code and exit 3); the freeze-record
and engine-revision check against N25bp1-01's `fixtures/separation/freeze.json`;
`frob mission separate q:<case> --designs d:…,d:… --key sep-<run>-<case> --json` on each of the ten planted
cases (a distinct key per case and per repeat, so that no case is answered from another call's receipt), waiting
with `frob job wait <job> --json`; `frob sim replay <trace> --tier referee --key sep-<run>-<case>-rp-<i> --json`
of the four traces of each result; comparison of the minimized edit list with the planted set; the
identical-designs case; controls (a) and (b); the run's receipt, from which the gate is recorded (step 6; the
script writes no gate row). Sources the helper of STD-E2E. Log:
newline-delimited JSON at `target/e2e-logs/mission_separate/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line has
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence
block; per case the candidate count, the edit lists before and after minimization, the first cause and failure
class before and after, the smallness tuple, the referee variants, the four state digests; from the answer's
`pair` block the reversal gap g (and the reversal score −g), both verdicts and the reversal kind, the oracle
class of the losing design and the guard outcome for both designs; from its `search` block the
flip-probability rank of each evaluated world (these are the only user-side exercise of N26a-10's entry point,
whose bead defers its logging here); for a sealed case the committed log carries digests, counts and pass/fail
only, never the edit values or distractors. `metric` lines `cases_passed` against 10,
`max_edits` against 3 and `mechanism_preserved` against 10; on failure the first differing edit or the first
digest mismatch with the paths of the two `.fmcw` files. A final `summary` names both gates.

### Known answers and negative controls

The limiting model of the split-grip fixture gives the exact reversal behind cases 1 and 2: capacities 0.690
against 0.619 at +12 mm and 0.360 against 0.431 at −12 mm for the 40 mm and 70 mm soles. The pull-over creep
limit behind case 4: friction demand 24/23 = 1.043 at δ = 0 and 0.887 at δ = +0.04 m for cart A. The shrinker's
control: failure exactly when edits 3 and 6 of eight are present returns {3, 6}. CX-268 (more contact area means
more traction). Negative controls as listed. Metamorphic relation: case 2 is the mirror of case 1 and must
return the mirrored result inside the determinism class. Determinism lanes: each result replays twice with
identical digests; the minimized edit lists are identical across worker counts.

## Considerations

- The verb registry's row keeps `mission.separate` at first milestone M4 while its gate is due at M3; the
  registry's early-forms table lets the verb answer from M3 (BD-293, GATE-AUGUR-13), so this bead follows the
  gate register without a mismatch (recorded in the coverage file).
- A separation is evidence about two frozen closed loops in one world. It is `Tested`, it is not a verdict about
  either design's family, and one route through one environment proves nothing universal.
- Minimization cost grows quadratically with the number of edits and each test is 64 paired replays of two
  closed loops; the distractor counts of the fixtures keep the gate inside the default machine envelope.
- The planted cases must not be seen by the engine's authors before the gate run in any form that lets the
  minimal set be hard-coded; they are authored by a separate session and sealed like the amendment campaign
  (N25bp1-01). The decision run and this gate run read one sealed population at one pinned engine revision; an
  engine change between them voids the population and a new one is sealed before the gate is measured.
