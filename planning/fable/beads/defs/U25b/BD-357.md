## Background

At M4 a mission query is asked of large families: thousands of discrete designs, each with continuous parameters,
evaluated in device batches. Three mechanisms decide how much of that family is ever evaluated. The
mission-observable quotient merges designs the mission cannot tell apart. Checked cuts discard subtrees of a
discrete family with a bound that holds for every completion. The cell worklist refines continuous cells and
orders the attempts on each. All three are Frontier, all three are scheduling-or-pruning devices whose danger is
discarding or merging something that mattered, and the kill gate KILL-AUGUR-CUTS covers the first two: one false
cut on the frozen catalog, or less than 70% of evaluation cost saved; or one quotient merge with different
replayed traces. Fallback: cuts off (exhaustive evaluation with the static screen as an ordering heuristic);
quotient off (memoize on full model identity only).

The pieces are built by separate beads. This bead integrates them into `mission.synthesize` on the path that
runs family batches, re-measures the pilot's two numbers on the product itself, and records the final decision of
the kill gate. It serves BET-19 (a query over a design family reaches an accepted answer sooner than
point-by-point evaluation) and the M4 milestone statement "mission queries over design families on the GPU".
What Augur may conclude from a batch is limited by the rules of family execution: candidate screening and
residual evaluation run in bulk, the checker queue runs on the CPU, and its capacity and transfer time count
toward decision time.

No speed is claimed beyond what is measured. Two rules from the scaling limit apply to any ratio this bead
reports: if a fraction f of matched wall time is improvable by a factor r and the method adds relative overhead
h, the speedup is at most 1/(1 − f + f/r + h); and a pruning ratio is never multiplied by a GPU speedup that was
measured on work the pruning already removed.

## Scope

In: the pipeline order inside `mission.synthesize` for a discrete-plus-continuous family; the hand-off between
quotient, cuts, worklist and family batches; the product-path measurement on the frozen catalog; the replay
test's record; the quotient's pilot record `PILOT-QUOTIENT-01` (this bead is its carrier); the answer's report
of what was merged, cut and evaluated; the end-to-end script; the final kill-gate record.
Out: the mechanisms themselves; the cuts pilot (N25b-16); the first reading of the kill gate (N25b-17); device
execution of batches (BD-355); bundles at M4 (BD-364); region certificates (N25b-10, BD-358).

## Technical approach

1. Pipeline for a query whose design domain is a discrete family with continuous parameters:
   a. Admission and the consistency test; compile the mission's lowering, its constraint-to-parameter graph and
      its observable mask.
   b. Family ranking where several archetype families are admitted (orders work only).
   c. Checked cuts by best-first fill of typed holes (if the cuts clause was kept): pruned nodes become excluded
      cells with cut certificates.
   d. Quotient over surviving leaves (if the quotient is on): leaves are grouped by the class key of the
      mission-observable projection; one representative per class is scheduled.
   e. For each representative and its continuous parameters: the cell worklist with its ladder (transport,
      static exclusion, exact inclusion, region certificate, campaign, split). Campaign rungs dispatch family
      batches; strict predicates evaluated in bulk on a device use the exact-integer class or guarded single
      precision with exact fallback.
   f. Every proof goes through the bundle writer and the checker; verdicts of a representative are served to
      its class members with the representative named.
   g. The answer: inner, excluded and unresolved cells partitioning the family (INV-AUGUR-02), with a report.
2. Report fields added to the answer (DESIGN): leaves in the family; leaves removed by cuts, with the number of
   cuts by kind; classes and class-size distribution; representatives evaluated; cells decided by each rung;
   costs by phase (bounds, quotient, proposing, device, checking, transfer); and the statement of what was not
   evaluated and why.
3. Product-path measurement on the frozen catalog of 1,024 leaves (the pilot's catalog and mission, unchanged):
   - run `mission.synthesize` with cuts and quotient off: the exhaustive reference, which must reproduce the
     pilot's ground-truth labels;
   - run with cuts on: count false cuts (leaves under a pruned node that the reference marks feasible) and
     compute evaluation cost saved, 1 − C_cuts / C_exhaustive, with the pilot's cost model;
   - run with the quotient on: compare verdicts leaf by leaf with the reference, and run the replay test on
     sampled class pairs (bit-identical state digests required).
   - the quotient's pilot. This bead is the carrier of `PILOT-QUOTIENT-01`, row 5 of the Augur pilot records in
     `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (schema `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`, entered by N25d-40, whose
     plan-time enumeration has no archived record and is repeated here). Before the ballast family runs it
     commits the frozen protocol (eight equal slugs in 24 collinear slots, the enumeration by (N, Σx, Σx²), the
     replay sampling rate, the falsifier "two merged designs with different replayed traces") with its hash,
     which makes the record `registered`; afterwards it appends one `[[pilot.result]]` with the class count
     against 24,546, the placements against 735,471, the pairs replayed, the mismatches against 0 and the
     receipt. It adds no second record.
4. Final kill-gate record. Cuts clause: zero false cuts and at least 70% saved on the product path, or the
   fallback is adopted now (cuts off). Quotient clause: zero false merges over the replayed pairs, or the
   fallback is adopted (quotient off). The record cites the first reading and the pilot. It is the second entry
   per scope (`half:cuts`, `half:quotient`) at this exit, so by N40-03's rule it carries `reading = "final"` and
   its own decision record, `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-CUTS-M4-final.md`, with its receipt hashes beside the
   trigger's numbers and a citation of N25b-17's `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-CUTS-M4.md`, which is not edited (a
   dated reading appended to that file would fail the register's load with `decision-adr-path`). The two final
   entries are appended to the kill row's `decision` array and to the `decision` block of the owner declaration
   N25b-17 wrote in `frob-mission`'s `contract.toml` (each measured value citing its receipt), so that
   `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` (N40-30) still passes for the row; a trigger met on the
   product path makes the decision in force `fired` for that clause, with N40-03's propagation. The recording
   session is not one that implemented the mechanisms.
5. Fallback paths are real code paths, exercised by the script: cuts off means every leaf is evaluated and the
   static screen only orders them; quotient off means the memo key is the full model identity. This bead owns
   the leaf enumeration the exhaustive path uses (the Cartesian product of each hole's legal fills, legality
   decided by the design language's check), so the path exists on both branches of the kill gate, including
   the one on which N25b-13 closed with reason `fallback`.
   Surface (options of the `mission.synthesize` row of VERBS-mission from M4): `--cuts <on | off>` and
   `--quotient <on | off>`, defaulting to the decision in force for the clause's scope (`on` for `not-fired`,
   `off` before the decision and for `fired`); `--cuts on` or `--quotient on` on a fired clause is refused with
   `FROB-REF-724` (exit 5) naming the ADR, as for any capability whose kill gate fired; the scheduler is chosen
   with N25b-12's `--scheduler`. The answer's report records the settings that ran.
6. Determinism and placement. Verdict labels for the family batch are identical on the CPU reference path and
   on the device backends under the exact-integer or guarded class; a family screen is placed where its data
   lives, and the script records where each phase ran.

Evidence class: cells keep the class of their proofs; anything merged through the quotient is capped at `Tested`
for the merge itself; the answer's class is the weakest.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The seed's condition: false cuts on the frozen catalog and the evaluation cost saved are recorded (from
      the product path), and KILL-AUGUR-CUTS is decided, per clause, in `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-CUTS-M4-final.md`
      (the product-path reading) and in two `reading = "final"` entries of its kill row (`half:cuts`,
      `half:quotient`), citing the measured values with receipt hashes; the register loads with them.
- [ ] `--cuts off --quotient off` evaluates all 1,024 leaves through this bead's enumeration; `--cuts on` on a
      fired cuts clause is refused with `FROB-REF-724` and the ADR named.
- [ ] The exhaustive reference reproduces the pilot's labels on all 1,024 leaves.
- [ ] With cuts on: zero false cuts; cost saved reported against 0.70; every cut's bundle accepted by the
      auditor.
- [ ] With the quotient on: every leaf's verdict equals the reference; the replay test over sampled class pairs
      shows zero digest mismatches; the ballast family returns 24,546 classes from 735,471 placements.
- [ ] `PILOT-QUOTIENT-01` is `registered` with the frozen protocol's hash before the ballast run and carries the
      appended `[[pilot.result]]` with those values; N25d-40's linkage test finds no second record.
- [ ] INV-AUGUR-10: three scheduler settings give identical inner and excluded sets on the catalog.
- [ ] With cuts off and quotient off the answer is unchanged and only cost rises (the fallbacks work).
- [ ] No ratio in the report multiplies a pruning ratio by a device speedup measured on work the pruning
      removed; any quoted ratio carries its measured f, r and h.
- [ ] Cancellation mid-campaign returns only cells with accepted bundles; a later run refines monotonically.
- [ ] Negative controls: a planted wrong bound yields false cuts that the measurement reports; a projection
      that omits the second moment yields a false merge that the replay test reports; a scheduler that marks
      cells decided is caught by the schedule-independence check.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/family_search.rs`; real mechanisms on a 4³ sub-catalog and on the ballast family.
- `pipeline_order`: cuts run before the quotient, the quotient before dispatch; an instrumented run records the
  stages in order.
- `class_members_served_from_representative`.
- `pruned_subtree_is_one_excluded_cell`.
- `fallback_cuts_off_same_answer`, `fallback_quotient_off_same_answer`.
- `report_accounts_for_every_leaf`: removed + merged + evaluated + unresolved equals the family size.
- `no_illegal_ratio`: the report builder refuses to print a combined ratio without f, r and h.
- `leaf_enumeration_complete`: on the 4³ sub-catalog the enumeration yields 64 leaves, each legal by the design
  language's check, none twice; a fill whose port signature does not match is never produced.
- `fired_clause_refuses_on`: with the cuts clause recorded `fired`, `--cuts on` returns `FROB-REF-724` and the
  default resolves to `off`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_family_search.sh`, plain shell over the real `frob` binary, seed-explicit and idempotent.
Steps: `frob mission synthesize catalog-1024.fmission --cuts <on|off> --quotient <on|off> --key
fs-<cuts>-<quotient>-<run> --json` in four configurations (reference `off off`; cuts on; quotient on; both on)
and with three scheduler settings (`--scheduler fifo`, `opportunity-loss`, `hinted --hints <file>`, each with
its own key: compared runs never share a key); `frob mission synthesize ballast-24-8.fmission --quotient on
--key fs-ballast --json`; a multi-family query for the starter ranking; `frob prove bundle a:… --out <dir>
--key fs-bundle --json` and a standalone audit of the cut certificates with `frob-verify <dir>`; the replay
test's record from the quotient-on answers; the fallback configurations. Log: newline-delimited JSON
at `target/e2e-logs/mission_family_search/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block; `metric`
lines for `false_cuts` against 0, `cost_saved` against 0.70, `class_count` against 24,546, `replay_mismatches`
against 0, and phase costs with the placement of each phase; digests of the inner and excluded sets per
configuration; on failure the first leaf whose verdict differs from the reference, with its node, cut or class,
the configuration and seed, and the paths of the two answers and of the cut or class-pair bundle needed to
reproduce it.
The script writes the receipt of the product-path values (`receipts/gates/<hash>.json`) that the final entries
cite; it writes no register entry and no ADR (step 4).
A final `summary` names the kill gate and both clauses.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-648 (ballast: 735,471 placements, 24,546 classes; {0, 3} and {1, 2} must split), KA-643 (shared bus: an
additive peak rule prunes the feasible two-tick case), KA-656 (guarded strict predicate on 65,536 rows: 34,590
admitted, 30,946 excluded of which 1,820 are exact ties; no error after exact fallback). CX-278, CX-284 (strict
predicates compared in unguarded single precision: 452 false admissions and 23,438 false exclusions). Negative
controls as listed. Metamorphic relations: a world of a mixed batch gives the same verdict as the same world
alone; permuting leaves permutes results. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; CPU
reference against each device backend under the exact-integer or guarded class, labels identical.

## Considerations

- The scaling limit is the honest frame for this bead: with f = 0.97, r = 40 and h = 0.02 the ceiling is about
  13.5, and infinite acceleration of 85% of a workflow gives at most 6.7 (illustrative values, not
  measurements). The three terms are measured on the strong baseline's critical path before any family kernel
  is credited.
- KILL-AUGUR-TIE remains in force: if a short conventional program reaches the same checked scope at similar
  turnaround, that program ships behind the same verbs.
- A later audited campaign that finds a discarded feasible design triggers the cuts fallback at that time; the
  kill gate does not expire with this bead.
- Checker capacity is finite and on the CPU; a family answer with tens of thousands of certificates can be
  bounded by audit time, which the report shows as its own phase.
