## Background

Region certificates decide a constraint over a box of design parameters by enclosing it with outward-rounded
arithmetic and bisecting. Bisection is affordable only in a few dimensions, so the whole approach rests on one
structural fact about robots: each lowered constraint g_k reads only a few parameters. The constraint-to-parameter
graph records, for every lowered constraint g_k, the set S_k of parameters it reads.

HYPOTHESIS: legged static analyses are sparse by construction; joint torque, reach and clearance of one leg depend
on roughly three to six parameters of that leg. Closure constraints of linkage legs raise the coupled dimension.
The measured sparsity for the plan's robot classes is open question OPEN-AUGUR-3 (owner: the Augur lead). Its
fail-closed default: region certificates are refused above six parameters. It blocks the region gate at M4
(the per-constraint arm must decide at least half of each constraint's sub-box volume within 10⁴ boxes). A later
extension of the mechanism bounds the number of parameters that are bisected, not the number a constraint reads,
and for that it needs more than incidence: for an affine-form enclosure g(x) ∈ g₀ + Σᵢ aᵢ·εᵢ + r·[−1, 1] with
εᵢ ∈ [−1, 1], parameter i contributes width 2·|aᵢ|, and the graph records |aᵢ| per parameter.

This bead produces the graph for real design families and measures it, so that the question is settled by data
before the product mechanism is built. FACT about the suite (paths under `frankensim/crates/`, read 2026-11-21, to
be re-read at the pinned commit): `fs-ivl` provides outward-rounded intervals, affine forms and multivariate
Taylor models; missing in the suite are a paving driver, contractors and interval forms of the robot static
analyses. The last gap is closed here. Tier: Frontier.

Rejected alternatives: hand-writing an interval version of each analysis (two implementations of every analysis
would drift; and obligation PO-AUGUR-01 already requires an independent second lowering, which should be a
different derivation and not a copy); differentiating numerically to find dependencies (a zero finite difference
at sample points does not show that a parameter is not read).

## Scope

In: an expression-recording scalar that turns a static analysis into a constraint expression over the free
parameters of a design family; the graph (incidence and weights); the measurement over the robot classes; the
report; the decision record for OPEN-AUGUR-3.
Out: the static analyses themselves and their generic-scalar form (the analysis beads); the paving driver
(N25b-09); the combination rule (N25b-10); the paper pilot on a stand-in model (BD-289).

## Technical approach

1. Lowering by recording (DESIGN). A design family from the design language has free parameters with units,
   charts and boxes. Each static analysis that yields a margin (required minus available, written so that
   feasible means g ≤ 0) is executed once with every free parameter replaced by a recording value. The result is
   an expression DAG in the neutral proof vocabulary (nodes: exact constant, typed variable, sum, product,
   quotient, integer power, square root, absolute value, minimum, maximum). Constraints lowered at M3:
   - mass budget: total mass minus the budget (mass roll-up);
   - reach: required foothold or workspace distance against link lengths (workspace analysis), one constraint
     per limb and per declared target;
   - static joint torque against the actuator's continuous envelope at each declared stance posture (actuator
     envelope analysis), one constraint per joint and posture;
   - self-clearance at the declared postures (self-collision and clearance analysis), one per body pair.
   Trigonometric functions of a parameter do not occur at fixed postures (angles are data); a constraint whose
   DAG would need one is recorded as `not-lowerable` with its reason and counted separately.
2. The graph. For each constraint g_k: S_k is the set of parameter leaves reachable from its root. Weights: the
   affine-form evaluation of g_k over the family's box (through the airlock entry for interval arithmetic) gives
   coefficients aᵢ and remainder r; the record holds |aᵢ| per parameter, r, and the normalized share
   |aᵢ| / (Σⱼ |aⱼ| + r). The graph is stored as an artifact (`frob.constraint-graph/1`, DESIGN) addressed by
   hash, keyed by the family identity and the analysis versions, and memoized like any analysis result.
3. Second route (PO-AUGUR-01: each lowering from a design family and a mission to primitive equations is
   correct; verified by a second, independently written lowering for every supported template, compared
   exactly, and one deliberately invalid fixture per lowering). For the template legs the recorded expression is
   compared with a closed form derived by the symbolic derivation path on the same template: at 10³ seeded
   rational points the two must be equal exactly, and S_k must be identical. The invalid fixture: a recording
   run in which the thigh length is bound to its nominal value before recording (so the DAG silently omits it)
   must be caught by the comparison of S_k.
4. Measurement. Families: one per robot class that exists at M3 (the legged, wheeled, tracked and hybrid
   archetypes; at least the quadruped, the humanoid, the differential-drive and mecanum bases, a tracked base
   and one wheel-leg hybrid), each with its template's free parameters at their declared boxes, and one linkage
   leg with a closed chain. Reported per class and per constraint kind: the distribution of |S_k| (minimum,
   median, maximum); the share of constraints with |S_k| ≤ 6; the coupled dimension that the linkage closure
   adds (|S_k| with and without the closure equations substituted); the weak-variable profile (the share of
   total affine width carried by the six heaviest parameters); the count of `not-lowerable` constraints.
5. Decision record for OPEN-AUGUR-3, per class, by the Augur lead:
   - median |S_k| ≤ 6 and at least 90% of constraints within six: the limit of six stands for that class;
   - otherwise, if the six heaviest parameters carry at least 90% of affine width in most constraints: the
     class is a candidate for the bounded-split-set extension and stays refused until that extension passes its
     own gate;
   - otherwise: region certificates stay refused for the class above six parameters (FROB-REF-856: a region
     certificate is requested over more parameters than the admitted limit, or a joint paving is requested;
     first repair: the per-constraint decomposition; exit code 3) and its constraints are checked pointwise.
   Thresholds 90% are DESIGN; they are fixed in the experiment's registration before the measurement.
6. The experiment is registered in the ledger before it runs, with its question, the families, the thresholds
   and what each outcome changes.

Outputs carry evidence class `Tested` (a measurement on stated families). Determinism: the DAGs and S_k are
exact (class D0); weights come from floating-point affine forms and are reproducible on one platform.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For the quadruped template, every lowered constraint's recorded expression equals the second-route closed
      form exactly at 10³ rational points, and the two S_k agree; the invalid fixture is caught.
- [ ] The graph artifact exists for each class named above, with incidence and weights, and is retrievable by
      hash.
- [ ] The report gives, per class and constraint kind, the |S_k| distribution, the share within six, the
      closure's added dimension, the weak-variable profile and the `not-lowerable` count.
- [ ] The decision for OPEN-AUGUR-3 is recorded per class (an ADR or the open-question register), or the default
      "refused above six parameters" is confirmed in force with this report cited.
- [ ] The experiment's registration predates its results.
- [ ] Negative controls: the bound-before-recording fixture; a planted dense constraint (the sum of all
      normalized parameters) is reported with |S_k| equal to the parameter count and a flat weight profile.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/constraint_graph.rs`; real analyses, no mocks.
- `recording_scalar_builds_dag`: (a·b + c)/d recorded with four variables has four leaves and the expected
  digest; evaluating the DAG at rational points equals direct evaluation.
- `support_of_stance_rows`: for the stance family in the chart v = 1/half-width, the moment-balance row reads
  {v} and the force-balance row reads no free parameter.
- `knee_torque_support`: the static knee-torque constraint of the three-joint leg reads the two link lengths,
  the masses above the knee, the gear ratio and the motor's envelope parameters, and does not read the body
  width.
- `weights_sum_to_one`: normalized shares plus the remainder share sum to 1.
- `closure_raises_dimension`: a four-bar leg's foot-position constraint reads more parameters with the closure
  substituted than its open-chain template (synthetic fixture: 4 against 2).
- `invalid_lowering_caught`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/constraint_graph_sparsity.sh` runs the measurement through the example driver of the crate (no verb
exposes the graph by itself at M3; the product reads it inside `mission.synthesize`), with fixed seeds. It logs
through the shared helper to `target/e2e-logs/constraint_graph_sparsity/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `start`
with family identities and analysis versions; per class `metric` lines for median and maximum |S_k|, the share
within six and the weak-variable profile, each against its registered threshold; `assert` lines for the
second-route equality and the two negative controls; the digests of the graph artifacts; a `summary` line.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: in the stance family the only free parameter is v, so every constraint has |S_k| ≤ 1; in the
synthetic 20-parameter case of the weak-variable extension (g₁ = x₁ + δ − 0.8 with δ = 0.01·m² and m the mean of
(2·xᵢ − 1) over the other nineteen parameters) the constraint reads all twenty parameters and x₁ alone carries
the dominant weight, with weak width 0.01; in the dense control g = Σ xᵢ − 10 on [0, 1]²⁰ every parameter carries
equal weight 1. Negative controls: the two listed. Metamorphic relation: renaming or reordering free parameters
permutes S_k and the weights and changes nothing else.

## Considerations

- The hypothesis may fail for classes with heavy coupling through posture and load share; the default then
  stands and region certificates are simply not offered for those classes. That outcome is acceptable and is
  the reason the question has a default.
- A constraint that is `not-lowerable` is never silently dropped from a region answer: its presence makes the
  region answer unresolved for the affected designs.
- The requirement that analyses run over a generic scalar is a real cost on the analysis crate; if it is not
  met for an analysis, that analysis is lowered by the derivation path alone and has no second route, which is
  recorded as an open obligation against PO-AUGUR-01.
- Kill gate that governs the consumers of this data: KILL-AUGUR-REGION (fallback: dual witnesses, monotone
  bounds and pointwise static analyses; untested regions reported as sample coverage, `Estimated`).
