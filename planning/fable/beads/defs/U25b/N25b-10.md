## Background

A design family is constrained by many lowered constraints g_1, …, g_K at once: static joint torque in several
postures, reach, clearance, a mass budget, static equilibrium with ratings. The per-constraint driver produces,
for each g_k, an inner set I_k and an excluded set in the coordinates S_k that the constraint reads. This bead
turns those separate sets into a family answer without ever paving the joint design box, and decides which
conditions should not be paved at all because a cheaper and stronger certificate exists.

The rules of the mechanism, each with its reason:

- Combination by cylinders. A design is in the region answer's inner set when it lies in every cylinder over
  I_k, and outside the outer set when it lies outside some cylinder over the outer set O_k (that is, inside some
  constraint's excluded set). The joint set is represented implicitly, by K membership tests; it is never
  enumerated, because enumerating it is the isotropic paving the design rules out. Its volume, when wanted, is an
  `Estimated` quantity from sampling.
- Monotone bounds first. A mass budget or a rating bound that is monotone in one parameter removes its half
  space with one `monotone-transport` certificate and no bisection.
- Dual witnesses for static conditions. Where a condition is a linear or second-order-cone feasibility problem
  (static equilibrium with ratings, friction cones, torque envelopes), the exclusion is an `lp-farkas` or
  `conic-farkas` certificate. A parametric dual with a sign proof covers a whole cell at once, which is why
  linear static families do not need paving at all.
- Pointwise checks remain. An agent needs a check at the points it proposes. Region certificates are a source of
  evidence quality and of tolerance robustness (a box around a design is a statement about every as-built unit
  inside it). They are not presented as a speed lever, and deciding volume is not the same as saving simulations.

Tier: Frontier; the bet is BET-20 (per-constraint certificates with a split set of about six parameters, composed
by monotonicity, symmetry and witnesses, resolve a useful share of a family; its control: the midpoint
counterexample must be excluded from every inner set). Rejected alternatives are the killed forms of the
mechanism: sampling only inside a certified inner ellipsoid (cuts off the feasibility boundary and misses
disconnected islands); a logarithmic change of variables to linearize posynomial constraints for an
integer-linear decision procedure (the logarithm of a posynomial is a convex log-sum-exp); cylindrical algebraic
decomposition (doubly exponential beyond four or five variables).

## Technical approach

1. Per-constraint routing, in order of cost, for each lowered constraint g_k with support S_k:
   a. Monotone. If g_k has a proved sign of dependence on a parameter over the whole box (the outward-rounded
      enclosure of the partial derivative excludes zero, or the parameter enters only as the bound of a set that
      provably grows), its boundary in that parameter is found by exact corner evaluation, and the half space
      on the failing side is excluded by one `monotone-transport` proof object. A sign that holds at sample
      points is not a proof. Where g_k is monotone in every parameter it reads, no bisection happens at all.
   b. Dual. If the condition is the feasibility of a linear or second-order-cone system in auxiliary variables
      (forces, torques) with parameters entering affinely in a declared chart, exclusion is sought as a
      parametric dual with a sign proof over the cell (`lp-farkas`, `conic-farkas`), and inclusion as an
      affine-in-chart witness (`lp-primal`). Convexity is claimed only in a chart where the constraints are
      affine with design-independent mission vertices.
   c. Paving. Otherwise, if |S_k| is within the admitted limit (six, or the class's decided limit), call the
      driver; else the constraint is checked pointwise only and marks region answers unresolved.
2. Membership oracle (DESIGN):

```rust
pub enum RegionVerdict { Inner(Vec<ProofRef>), Excluded(ProofRef), Unresolved(Vec<ConstraintId>) }
pub fn classify_point(sets: &ConstraintSets, d: &DesignPoint) -> RegionVerdict;
pub fn classify_box(sets: &ConstraintSets, b: &BoxQ) -> RegionVerdict;
```

   `classify_box`: for each constraint, project the box onto S_k. It is inside the cylinder over I_k when the
   projection is covered by certified inner boxes (an exact coverage check of the same form as a `partition`
   proof); it is excluded by g_k when the projection lies inside one certified excluded box or half space. The
   result is `Inner` only if every constraint covers it, with one proof list per constraint; `Excluded` as soon
   as one constraint excludes it; otherwise `Unresolved`, naming the constraints that did not decide. Cost: K
   coverage tests, each over the boxes of one constraint.
3. Cells of the mission answer. The answer still lists cells with verdicts. The cells are the query's own
   boxes: the worklist's cells, and a tolerance box around each proposed design built from the design domain's
   part and fabrication tolerances. A cell that `classify_box` reports `Inner` carries, per constraint, the
   proof references that cover it; an `Excluded` cell carries the one excluding proof. The implicit joint set
   itself is exposed through `mission.show` (the read verb that pages a stored answer by set), which gains a
   membership selector for a point or a box (DESIGN: `--at <design or box>` in its input schema) and answers
   from the stored per-constraint sets without recomputing them.
4. Volume. On request the decided share of the domain is estimated by seeded sampling (10⁶ points by default),
   reported as inner, excluded and unresolved shares with a confidence interval, class `Estimated`, under the
   weighting the caller supplied. It is never folded into the cell geometry and never raises a class.
5. Scope statement, attached to every region answer's assumptions and printed in its report: the certificate
   covers the static rigid model named in the bundle; it says nothing about dynamic performance, run-up,
   landing or recovery.
6. Refusal. FROB-REF-856: a region certificate is requested over more parameters than the admitted limit, or a
   joint paving is requested. Message fields: the constraint, |S_k|, the limit. Ranked repairs: (1) the
   per-constraint decomposition; (2) bind parameters to shrink the support; (3) a pointwise check at the
   proposed designs. Exit code 3.
7. Report per constraint: the route taken (monotone, dual, paving, pointwise), S_k, boxes used, decided share of
   its sub-box, and the share of all decided volume it decides alone; the last figure is what the kill gate's
   "single monotone bound" clause reads.
8. Class. A region cell is `Certified` only if every constraint's proof is accepted by the auditor and every
   input is at least `Certified`; a cell that rests on a sampled quantity is `Estimated`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Stance family: routed to the dual path; no box is paved; the answer equals the exact one (inner
      v ∈ [2, 14/3], excluded (14/3, 6]).
- [ ] A mass budget monotone in three masses is routed to the monotone path with zero bisections: one
      `monotone-transport` proof per mass whose failing half space meets the box (step 1a), each boundary exact
      from corner evaluation, and the query's cells decided by exact corner evaluation or left unresolved.
- [ ] On the 12-parameter static quadruped family, `classify_box` returns `Inner` for a tolerance box around a
      feasible design only when every constraint covers it, and the inner verdict is contradicted by none of
      10⁶ exact point evaluations inside sampled inner boxes.
- [ ] The midpoint counterexample is excluded from every inner set: the designs (e, W) = (0, 1.9) and (0.9, 1)
      on feet (±1 − e, ±1) m capped at 0.5 N are feasible, and no inner cell contains their midpoint
      (0.45, 1.45), which needs 841/800 N from a pair capped at 1 N.
- [ ] A request to pave a constraint that reads seven parameters at the default limit, and a request for a
      joint paving, are each refused with FROB-REF-856 and the per-constraint decomposition as first repair.
- [ ] The volume estimate is labelled `Estimated`, carries its interval and weighting, and does not alter any
      cell's class.
- [ ] Every region answer carries the scope statement.
- [ ] Negative controls: a combination rule that declares a box inner when it is covered by all but one
      constraint is caught by the point evaluations; a combination that intersects boxes of different
      constraints as if they shared parameters (boxes of g₁ in (x, y) intersected with boxes of g₂ in (y, z)
      coordinate by coordinate, ignoring which parameters each reads) is caught by `cylinders_two_constraints`;
      a monotone route that accepts a sampled sign is caught on a
      constraint whose derivative changes sign inside the box (g = (x − 1/2)² − 1/16 on [0, 1], monotone on each
      half and not on the whole: samples taken in (1/2, 1] read "increasing", both corners give g = 3/16 > 0,
      and the route would exclude the box although [1/4, 3/4] is feasible).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/region_combine.rs`; real driver and auditor.
- `cylinders_two_constraints`: g₁(x, y) = x + y − 1 ≤ 0 and g₂(y, z) = y − z ≤ 0 on [0, 1]³: the point
  (0.2, 0.3, 0.9) is inner with one proof per constraint; (0.9, 0.9, 0.95) is excluded by g₁; the joint set is
  never materialized (the test asserts no three-dimensional box list is built).
- `monotone_mass_budget_no_bisection`: m_b + 4·(m₁ + m₂) + 12·m_m ≤ 14 over the pilot's mass ranges (m_b in
  [4, 12], m₁ in [0.3, 1.0], m₂ in [0.1, 0.5], m_m in [0.2, 0.6] kg, BD-289) yields zero boxes and one
  transport proof: only m_b has a failing half space inside its range, m_b > 14 − 4·(0.3 + 0.1) − 12·0.2 = 10;
  the other three would need m₁ > 1.8, m₂ > 1.6 or m_m > 0.7, outside their ranges.
- `monotone_first_no_boxes`: g = m₁ + m₂ + m₃ − 20 ≤ 0: one `monotone-transport` per parameter, zero boxes.
- `cylinder_combination_disjoint_supports`: two constraints on disjoint parameter sets: membership equals the
  product of the per-constraint sets.
- `stance_routed_to_dual`.
- `box_coverage_exact`: a tolerance box straddling two adjacent inner boxes is covered; one that reaches an
  unresolved box is not.
- `midpoint_never_inner`.
- `refusal_856_over_limit`, `refusal_856_joint_paving`.
- `volume_is_estimated`: class and interval present; seeds reproduce the figure.
- `sole_decider_attribution`: on the toy of two constraints the shares are as planted.

### End-to-end script and logging

A region answer exists on the user's side only once BD-358 (which depends on this bead) adds the region mode to
`mission.synthesize`; this bead's acceptance rests on the unit tests above and does not wait on a later script.
It exposes the per-constraint routing report (step 7) as JSON and the `--at` selector of `mission.show`
(registered on that verb's row in VERBS-mission from M4). `scripts/e2e/mission_region_certificates.sh` (bead N25b-11)
then drives it through `frob mission synthesize` and `frob mission show <q> --answer <a> --at <point or box>
--json` on the 12-parameter family and logs the route per constraint, the decided shares, the sole-decider
shares against 0.95, the refusal on an over-wide request, and the membership answers at planted points with
their proof kinds.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-624 (the stance family needs no paving: a parametric dual covers the excluded cell), KA-627 (the midpoint,
841/800 N). CX-270 (the midpoint of two feasible designs is feasible), CX-274 (the support polygon contains the
load and total capacity suffices: of 2,304 bodies in the stance grid that screen accepts 1,596 the exact model
rejects), CX-275 (sample points fail, so the cell is excluded). Negative controls as listed. Metamorphic
relations: adding a constraint can only move cells from inner to unresolved or excluded; permuting constraints
does not change any verdict. Determinism lanes: verdicts for fixed query boxes are identical across worker
counts and across two hosts given the same certified box sets.

## Considerations

- A region certificate is a statement about the declared static model. A design that passes it and is
  "monotonically better" is still outside the inner set of a dynamic mission until its dynamic obligations are
  checked; the widened sole that helps the static test and collides during swing is the standing example.
- The number of inner proofs per cell grows with the number of constraints; the bundle set per cell is one
  coverage proof per constraint, and audit time is reported.
- OPEN-AUGUR-11 (a standard measure on unresolved sets): default none; the caller supplies the weighting for
  any reported mass.
- Kill gate named in advance: KILL-AUGUR-REGION, with its fallback (dual witnesses, monotone bounds and
  pointwise static analyses; untested regions reported as sample coverage, `Estimated`). The monotone and dual
  routes of this bead survive the kill; only the paving route is removed.
