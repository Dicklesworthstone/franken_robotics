## Background

Region certificates are the Frontier mechanism by which a mission answer covers boxes of designs and not only
proposed points: for each static constraint, sub-boxes of the parameters it reads are certified feasible or
infeasible by outward-rounded enclosures, and the family answer is assembled from those per-constraint sets. A
paper-scale pilot at M3 decided whether the mechanism was worth building. GATE-AUGUR-09 (claim Known answer; pilot
at M3, the gate at M4) measures the built mechanism on the same 12-parameter quadruped box: the per-constraint
arm decides at least 50% of each constraint's sub-box volume within 10⁴ boxes; no single monotone bound accounts
for more than 95% of decided volume; and zero certified boxes are contradicted by 10⁶ point evaluations.

This bead is the end-to-end script that takes that measurement through the public verbs and the standalone
auditor, records the gate, and takes the second reading of the kill gate KILL-AUGUR-REGION, whose trigger has two
parts: the pilot's falsifier (read at M3), or this gate failing after one repair cycle (read here). The bet
behind the mechanism is BET-20. No speed is claimed for it; the ceiling of benefit stated before any speed test
is none, because pointwise static checks prune equally for proposed designs.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/mission_region_certificates.sh`; the fixture query; the point-evaluation oracle run; the audit
of emitted boxes; the receipt from which the gate is recorded; the second kill-gate reading with its ADR
(`docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-REGION-M4.md`, whichever way it falls); BET-20's M4 decision record
(`docs/adr/bet-20-m4.md`); the self-test.
Out: the mechanism (the driver, the combination, the M4 integration); the pilot (BD-289); the bounded-split-set
gate, which reuses this fixture with its own thresholds.

## Technical approach

Fixture. `fixtures/missions/quad-static-12.fmission` (DESIGN): the quadruped template with twelve free
parameters (three link lengths, two hip offsets, three masses, gear ratio, motor mass, body length and width) at
the boxes frozen by the pilot record; a static world family; predicates for static joint torque in three stance
postures, reach to a foothold set, self-clearance and a mass budget; the prefix "exists policy per nominal
design, for all worlds, for all executions" with horizon `static`; evidence profile `enclosed`; a box budget of
10⁴ per constraint; response parts including the per-constraint report.

Steps (plain shell, `set -euo pipefail`, shared helper, seed-explicit, idempotent, writes under `target/`):
1. `start`: commit, toolchain, host fingerprint, the pilot record's digest, seeds.
2. `frob mission synthesize quad-static-12.fmission --estimate --json`: assert the estimate separates
   admission, lowering, proposing and checking, and runs nothing.
3. `frob mission synthesize quad-static-12.fmission --key reg-main-<seed> --json` (a job, exit 8 with its
   handle; the script waits with `frob job wait j:… --sync-wait-ms 30000 --json` and, after a dropped
   connection, recovers it with `frob job find --verb mission.synthesize --key reg-main-<seed> --json`).
   Assert `ok: true`, the three authorization flags false, the scope statement ("static rigid model; nothing
   about dynamic performance, run-up, landing or recovery") among the assumptions, and a per-constraint report.
4. From the report, for each constraint: the route (monotone, dual, paving, pointwise), the parameters read,
   boxes used (at most 10⁴), and the decided share of its sub-box. Assert the share is at least 0.50 for every
   constraint routed to paving.
5. Sole-decider attribution: assert that no single monotone bound accounts for more than 0.95 of decided volume.
6. Point-evaluation oracle. An independent evaluator (the conformance crate's exact evaluation of the lowered
   constraints, not the mission crate's code) draws 10⁶ seeded points; for each point inside a certified inner
   box of a constraint it asserts g ≤ 0, and inside a certified excluded box g > 0. Assert zero contradictions.
7. Bundles. `frob prove bundle a:… --out <dir> --key reg-bundle-<seed> --json`; build `frob-verify` from a copy
   of its crate; audit every
   box certificate with a cleared environment; assert every one is accepted and record the kinds
   (`interval-box`, `taylor-box`, `monotone-transport`, `lp-farkas`) and the total audit time.
8. Membership. `frob mission show q:… --answer a:… --at <design> --json` (the `--at` selector of N25b-10) for
   four planted designs: one inside every
   constraint's inner set (expected inner, with one proof list per constraint), one violating the mass budget
   (excluded by the monotone proof), one violating knee torque in the crouch posture (excluded by a box), one in
   an unresolved band (unresolved, with the constraints named). Assert each verdict and that the unresolved one
   is not printed as either verdict.
9. Refusal. `frob mission synthesize quad-static-12.fmission --region joint --key reg-joint --json` (the
   joint paving of all twelve parameters, which BD-358's surface defines as a refused request): assert exit 3
   and FROB-REF-856 with the per-constraint decomposition as the first repair.
10. Budget stop. Re-run with a wall budget that ends mid-paving and a key of its own (`--budget wall_s=<w>
    --key reg-stop-<seed>`): assert that certified boxes are returned, each complete in itself, with exit 2 and
    the flag `resolution-unmet` (the default of the open question on budget stops), and that a later run with
    more budget (`--key reg-more-<seed>`) only moves boxes out of the unresolved set.
11. Gate record and kill reading. Put the measured values in the run's receipt and enter the row with
    `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-09 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19).
    If any clause fails: record `fail`, allow one repair cycle (N40-03's reading: one bounded attempt on the
    mechanism, same fixture and thresholds), re-run; if it fails again the kill gate's second trigger is met
    and the decision `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-REGION-M4.md` (the fields N40-03 requires, recorded by a session
    that did not implement the mechanism) adopts the fallback (region certificates removed; family answers use
    dual witnesses, monotone bounds and pointwise static analyses at proposed designs; untested regions
    reported as sample coverage, `Estimated`), outcome `fired`. A pass is recorded in the same file as outcome
    `not-fired` with the gate's receipt. Either way the kill row gains an entry with `exit = "M4"` (`outcome`,
    `adr`, `date`, `session`, `receipts`) beside BD-289's M3 entry, which is not edited; the script writes the
    receipt and the ADR skeleton, and the entry is written with the ADR by the recording session.
12. BET-20's M4 reading (requested by BET-20 and BD-289, whose acceptance stops at M3). After the gate is
    recorded, `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example bets -- status --json` (N00-28's one verdict rule) gives
    BET-20's verdict through M4; the recording session writes `docs/adr/bet-20-m4.md` with N00-28's fields (the
    statement; each falsifier clause with the measured value, unit, threshold and gate record; the arms'
    results; the controls caught; the verdict; if falsified, the fallback in force, the wordings and goals that
    change and the beads that stop or start), citing `docs/adr/bet-20-m3.md` and the KILL-AUGUR-REGION entries
    of M3 and M4, and updates the bet register's `status` and `decision_record` in the same change. The script
    then re-runs the `bets` example and asserts that the verdict printed and the one in `bet-20-m4.md` agree.

Logging: newline-delimited JSON at `target/e2e-logs/mission_region_certificates/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a
transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25b-11`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code,
wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). `metric` lines per
constraint: `decided_share` against 0.50, `boxes_used` against 10⁴; overall: `max_single_monotone_share` against
0.95, `point_contradictions` against 0, `audit_total_s`. On failure: the first contradicting point with its
coordinates, the box and its certificate path, or the first rejected certificate with its rule. A final `summary`
names both gates, the receipt hash, the kill gate's M4 outcome and BET-20's verdict through M4. Only identifiers, digests and parameter values of the public fixture are logged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-09 (TARGET, M4): at least 50% of each paved constraint's sub-box volume decided within 10⁴
      boxes; no single monotone bound gives more than 95% of decided volume; zero certified boxes contradicted
      by 10⁶ point evaluations. Measured values are recorded.
- [ ] Every emitted box certificate is accepted by the standalone auditor built from an empty environment.
- [ ] The four membership queries return the planted verdicts.
- [ ] The joint-paving request is refused with FROB-REF-856.
- [ ] A budget stop returns only complete certified boxes and later work is monotone (INV-AUGUR-04).
- [ ] KILL-AUGUR-REGION's second reading is on file in `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-REGION-M4.md` with N40-03's
      fields: `not-fired` with the gate's receipt, or `fired` (the fallback adopted) after one repair cycle; the
      kill row carries the M4 entry beside the unedited M3 entry of BD-289.
- [ ] `docs/adr/bet-20-m4.md` is on file with N00-28's fields, cites `docs/adr/bet-20-m3.md`, and states the
      verdict that the `bets` example prints for BET-20 after the M4 gate record.
- [ ] Every run-verb call of the script carries `--key`, and the two compared runs of step 10 carry distinct
      keys.
- [ ] Negative controls (self-test): (a) a build whose enclosures round to nearest produces a certified box that
      the point oracle contradicts at step 6 or the auditor rejects at step 7; (b) a family whose only binding
      constraint is the mass budget fails step 5; (c) a combination build that ignores one constraint returns
      inner for the knee-torque violator at step 8.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`scripts/e2e/tests/mission_region_certificates_selftest.sh` runs the three planted builds and asserts the
failing step and the first differing value in the log. The report parser is tested on a recorded per-constraint
report; the point oracle has a unit test on the unit-disc constraint (points inside certified inner boxes
satisfy x² + y² ≤ 1).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. It runs on the CPU reference path; where the family batch of M4 offers device-resident
screening the script repeats step 3 with the batched backend and asserts identical cell labels under the
exact-integer or guarded class.

### Known answers and negative controls

The pilot's recorded shares (the undecided share of its best arm and the largest share from one monotone bound)
are the baseline the gate is read against. The arithmetic known answer (10⁴ boxes are 13 bisections) explains
the per-constraint budget. CX-275 (sample points fail, so the cell is excluded) and CX-270 (the midpoint of two
feasible designs is feasible) are the shortcuts under guard. Negative controls: the three planted builds.
Determinism lanes: the script twice on one machine gives identical box lists and digests; on the second host
every certificate is accepted, the point oracle of step 6 again finds zero contradictions, and the decided
shares of steps 4 and 5 (exact box volumes) differ from the first host's only through the last boxes decided,
which N25b-09's determinism rule allows across platforms; both hosts' values are logged and each must meet its
threshold.

## Considerations

- OPEN-AUGUR-3: if the measured sparsity puts a constraint above the admitted limit for this class, that
  constraint is routed to pointwise checks and is not counted in the 50% clause; the report says so.
- The gate measures decided volume, which is evidence quality and tolerance robustness. It is not a speed
  result, and it is not presented as one.
- The job can take minutes on the CPU; the script uses the job protocol and survives a killed connection by
  recovering the job with its idempotency key.
- If the kill fires, the fixture and this script are kept: the fallback's sample-coverage report is exercised
  by the same steps with the class `Estimated` expected.
