## Background

A kill gate names its trigger and its fallback in advance and is never quietly re-scoped. Its decision is recorded
as an ADR at the milestone the gate register names; a trigger, a threshold or a fallback changes only by a dated
amendment made before the measurement it governs. A kill gate that fires is not a failure of the program: the
fallback is the plan.

KILL-AUGUR-CUTS guards two Frontier mechanisms of family search that share one risk, discarding a design that
would have worked:

- checked cuts over discrete families (pilot: the frozen 1,024-leaf catalog; ceiling: the statically decidable
  fraction of the catalog);
- the mission-observable quotient (pilot: run as an enumeration; falsifier: two merged designs with different
  replayed traces; ceiling: fixture-dependent, none claimed).

Trigger: one false cut on the frozen catalog, or less than 70% of evaluation cost saved; or one quotient merge
with different replayed traces. Fallback, named in advance: cuts off, with exhaustive evaluation and the static
screen as an ordering heuristic; quotient off, with memoization on full model identity only. Decided at M4.

The two clauses are independent, and the fallback is stated per mechanism, so this bead decides them separately:
the cuts clause from the pilot, before the cuts mechanism is funded; the quotient clause as a reading that stays
open while the quotient is in use, because its trigger is an event (a false merge) and not a pilot number. In the
register's terms (N40-03) the kill row is decided per scope of kind `half`: `half:cuts` and `half:quotient`, each
with its own decision entry and outcome.

## Scope

In: the decision protocol for each clause; the ADR; the downstream list per branch; the standing record for the
quotient clause; the fail-closed posture before the decision.
Out: the pilot that produces the cuts numbers (N25b-16); the mechanisms (N25b-13, N25b-14); the product-path
re-measurement and the final record on both clauses (BD-357).

## Technical approach

1. Inputs for the cuts clause, from the pilot record: the count of false cuts on the frozen catalog; the
   evaluation cost saved; the statically decidable ceiling; the outcomes of the potency controls (the planted
   wrong bound was caught; the all-feasible catalog showed no saving; the A/A run showed none).
2. Admissibility of the measurement. The decision is taken only if the pilot's protocol digest predates its
   results and all potency controls behaved. A pilot whose controls failed is void and is re-run; it is not
   read as a pass.
3. Decision rule, cuts clause:
   - false cuts ≥ 1, or cost saved < 0.70: the trigger is met, outcome `fired` for `half:cuts`.
     Fallback: cuts off; discrete families are evaluated exhaustively, and the static screen is used only to
     order evaluations (which may change cost and can change no verdict).
   - false cuts = 0 and cost saved ≥ 0.70: outcome `not-fired` for `half:cuts`, citing the pilot's receipt; the
     cuts mechanism is funded. The pilot's numbers become the
     baseline that the product-path measurement must reproduce on the same frozen catalog.
   - A special case decided by arithmetic: if the measured ceiling (its cost share, the share of exhaustive cost
     spent on statically infeasible leaves, as N25b-16 records it beside the count fraction) is below 0.70 the
     saving cannot reach the threshold on this catalog; the trigger is met without scoring the cuts arm further.
4. Decision rule, quotient clause (standing from the first use of the quotient): any replay test that finds two
   members of one class with different state digests is a false merge. At M4 the entry for `half:quotient` is
   `not-fired` when no false merge has been confirmed, citing the receipt with the replayed class pairs to date.
   On the first confirmed false merge the
   trigger is met for the quotient: outcome `fired` for `half:quotient` (at M4 as this entry; afterwards as a
   re-opened entry with `reading = "reopened"` and its own ADR, N40-03 step 6); memoization is keyed on
   full model identity only; results served through the affected class are withdrawn for non-representative
   members and re-evaluated. A false merge caused by an implementation defect in the projection (an observable
   the equations read and the mask omitted) is still a trigger; the gate text says "one quotient merge", and
   re-enabling the quotient afterwards requires a dated amendment to the register.
5. Downstream list.
   - Cuts fired: the cuts mechanism bead (N25b-13) closes with reason `fallback` and the fallback's name
     ("cuts off: exhaustive evaluation with the static screen as an ordering heuristic"), and each dependent
     either closes `superseded` with the ADR's identifier or keeps its dependency because it stands on the
     fallback (the close rules of N41-17); the integration bead BD-357 stands on the fallback and proceeds with
     exhaustive evaluation over quotient classes (its own leaf enumeration); answers over discrete families
     list every leaf's own verdict and no pruned-subtree cells; `mission.synthesize --cuts on` is refused; the
     co-design cascade keeps its own screening rules, which have their own audits.
   - Cuts not fired: the mechanism bead becomes ready; the frozen catalog is added to the conformance fixtures.
   - Quotient fired: the projection's report (class counts, splitting observables) is kept as a diagnostic;
     merging is disabled by one registry flag; the family-batch chapter's orbit quotient is unaffected.
   - Either way the worklist, the starter ranking and the mission verbs are unchanged.
6. Fail-closed posture until the cuts clause is decided: no cut is applied on any production path; discrete
   families are evaluated exhaustively. Until a quotient false merge occurs the quotient may be used, with its
   replay sampling on.
7. The ADR: `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-CUTS-M4.md`, one section per scope (`half:cuts`, `half:quotient`). The
   product-path reading of BD-357 is a second entry per scope at the same exit, so by N40-03's rule it carries
   `reading = "final"` and its own file `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-CUTS-M4-final.md`, which cites this one; this
   file is never edited afterwards. The decision entries (`scope`, `outcome`, `adr`, `date`, `session`,
   `receipts`) are written with the ADR by the recording session. It carries the fields N40-03 requires (the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered and
   the amendments applied, none expected; each measured value with its receipt hash beside the trigger's
   number; the outcome per clause; the fallback in force and the name the capability carries from now on; the
   beads re-read, each "stands on the fallback" or "superseded by this ADR"; what changes downstream; the
   session that recorded it, which must not be a session that implemented N25b-13, N25b-14 or the pilot) and
   in addition the pilot record's digest, the ceiling (count fraction and cost share) and the potency
   controls' outcomes. With them the recording session writes the gate's owner declaration, which N40-30
   compares with the ledger (this bead is the deciding bead in N25d-41's map), in `frob-mission`'s
   `contract.toml`: `[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`, the ledger's current `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`,
   `mode` and `reads` copied, not restated, and the `decision` block per scope with each measured value citing
   its receipt; without it `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` fails `owner-declaration-missing`
   at the M4 exit. BD-357's final entries are appended to that block, not to a second declaration.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-CUTS-M4.md` exists with every field N40-03 requires, the cuts clause evaluated
      against its numbers (false cuts against 0; cost saved against 0.70) and the decision stated per clause;
      the recording session is not one that implemented the mechanisms or the pilot.
- [ ] The kill row KILL-AUGUR-CUTS carries one decision entry for `half:cuts` (`fired` or `not-fired`) and one
      for `half:quotient`, each citing the receipt of its measured values (the cuts numbers; the count of
      replayed class pairs and of false merges to date), which the kill row itself does not carry.
- [ ] The owner declaration of KILL-AUGUR-CUTS is in `frob-mission`'s `contract.toml` with both entries, and
      `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` passes for the row.
- [ ] The downstream list is applied: beads on the losing branch are closed with a typed reason that cites the
      ADR, or the mechanism bead is marked ready.
- [ ] A void pilot (controls failed, or protocol dated after results) cannot produce a `not-fired` decision; the
      decision function refuses it.
- [ ] Negative control of the decision function: fed one false cut and a saving of 0.95 it returns `fired`; fed
      zero false cuts and 0.69 it returns `fired`; fed zero and 0.70 it returns `not-fired`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_augur_cuts.rs`:
- `one_false_cut_kills_regardless_of_saving`.
- `saving_threshold_inclusive_at_070`.
- `ceiling_below_threshold_kills_by_arithmetic`: a ceiling cost share of 0.55 gives `fired` without a cuts-arm
  score; a count fraction of 0.75 with a cost share of 0.55 still gives `fired`.
- `void_pilot_refused`.
- `quotient_clause_one_false_merge`: one mismatching pair flips the quotient status and leaves the cuts status
  untouched.
- `clauses_independent`: the four combinations of outcomes produce four distinct register states.
- `entries_load_per_half`: the two entries load with `scope_kind = "half"`; a second M4 entry for `half:cuts`
  without `reading = "final"` fails with `decision-entry-duplicate`, and one whose `adr` is not the `-final`
  path fails with `decision-adr-path` (N40-03's loader, on a scratch register).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kill_augur_cuts_decision.sh` reads the pilot's log and record, recomputes the two numbers and the
ceiling from the per-leaf lines (so that the decision does not trust a summary), applies the rule, and writes the
receipt of the recomputed values (`receipts/gates/<hash>.json`) and the ADR skeleton; it writes no register
entry (step 7). It logs through the shared helper to
`target/e2e-logs/kill_augur_cuts_decision/<utc>.ndjson`: the pilot record's digest, the recomputed numbers
against their thresholds as `metric` lines, the control outcomes as `assert` lines, the decision per clause, and
a `summary`. It runs no simulation.

### Known answers and negative controls

The decision function's truth table above is its known answer. The quotient clause's reference false merge is
the two-slug placement pair with equal Σx and different Σx² ({0, 3} and {1, 2}: Σx = 3; Σx² = 9 and 5), which a
projection that omits the second moment merges and replay distinguishes. The shared-bus fixture (an additive
peak rule prunes the feasible two-tick case) is the reference false cut. Determinism: the script's output is a
function of the pilot's log.

## Considerations

- The threshold on false cuts is zero by design: a cut that removes one feasible design is wrong even when the
  eventual winner survives.
- A `not-fired` decision on the pilot does not end the gate: the integration bead re-measures on the product path,
  and a false cut found there, or in an audited campaign later, triggers the fallback then.
- The cost-saved number depends on how cost is counted; the pilot fixes the cost model in its frozen protocol,
  and this bead recomputes from the per-leaf lines to keep the summary honest.
- The quotient clause interacts with the family-batch chapter's own kill gate on digest classes (one false
  quotient surviving a repair cycle, or less than 5% of dispatches saved). The two are read side by side; the
  stricter outcome governs merging in mission search.
