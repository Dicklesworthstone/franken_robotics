## Background

Revalidation after an edit is the mechanism by which a design study keeps the part of its evidence that an
amendment did not touch. Its promise is correctness first: every verdict it keeps must be one that full
recomputation would also return. Its secondary promise, less work, is unproven: the planning pilot on a
four-state fixture showed that a correctly keyed dependency graph does exactly the work a semantic receipt does,
so there is no reuse advantage to claim there. The opportunity that remains is obtaining a physical
non-dependence or sensitivity certificate cheaply enough to matter.

The plan therefore prescribes a pilot before funding, with a hard stop. Freeze a campaign of 30 amendments before
either system sees it: visual-only, camera-timing, sole geometry, floor law, torque, shared-noise, load-authority
and facility edits; edits that matter to telemetry and not to a decision; and an apparently cosmetic change that
advances a packet by one control tick. A separate engineer implements the incremental incumbent (demand-driven
recomputation with solver sensitivity information). Every decision is replayed independently. Record every
incorrectly inherited success and every unnecessary invalidation; count unsafe reuse as a false accept even if a
later change happens to restore feasibility. Falsifier: any incorrectly inherited verdict. Continue only in cells
where the two systems are equally correct and measured critical-path costs support the registered target.
Ceiling: bounded by the dirty fraction of the campaign; a dependency analysis at plan time puts exact reuse of
rollouts after a mass or geometry edit at no more than about 10% of compute (HYPOTHESIS until measured on the
project's own memo graph), so the benefit lies in analyses and certificates, not in trajectories.

This bead freezes the campaign, builds the incumbent arm and the ground truth, and hands the measurements to the
revalidation gate and to the kill gate KILL-AUGUR-REUSE. Tier: pilot of a Frontier mechanism.

## Scope

In: the campaign (30 amendments with their base answers), frozen and sealed; the ground truth by independent
replay; the incumbent arm, written by a session that does not write the revalidation engine; the measurement
protocol and cost model; the pilot record.
Out: the revalidation engine and the mission layer under test (BD-292, N25b-18); the gate record (N25b-21); the
decision (N25b-20); the campaign's later home in the conformance suite.

## Technical approach

1. Base answers the amendments apply to: the three first-slice mission answers (the stance family, the docking
   family, the pusher battery); a cart delivery answer on the sill fixture (fixture 1); a sole comparison on the
   split-grip landing (fixture 2); the cost-9 cart and cost-5 relay catalog fixture; and the four-state
   camera-delivery fixture of the planning pilot.
2. The 30 amendments (DESIGN composition; the concrete edits are written by the freezing session and sealed):

| Category | Count | Examples of what the edit changes | What a correct system does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visual-only | 3 | A display label, a mesh colour, a viewer camera | Keeps every verdict; zero rechecks |
| Camera timing | 4 | Exposure start, a packet delayed within its slack, a packet delayed across a decision instant, the apparently cosmetic change that advances a packet by one control tick | Keeps the first two; rechecks the last two |
| Sole geometry | 3 | Sole width 40 to 70 mm, sole height, ankle origin | Rechecks contact-dependent verdicts; keeps static ones the sole does not enter |
| Floor law | 4 | Friction on one strip, the strip's offset, a second contact law, a film | Rechecks verdicts whose support contains that surface cell; keeps routes that avoid it |
| Torque | 4 | A pure limit relaxation, a limit tightening, a motor swap that also changes mass and rotor inertia, a derating | Keeps inclusions under the pure relaxation; rechecks the rest |
| Shared noise | 3 | A common noise model, an error radius, error dependence between reads | Rechecks everything that read the model, including implicit readers |
| Load authority | 3 | Independent normal forces to passive equal springs, an added force floor, a removed load observation | Withdraws the stance cell at half-width 1/4 m; rechecks the others |
| Facility | 3 | A doorway widened, a threshold ramp added, a counter moved | Keeps witnesses that do not touch the changed region; rechecks exclusions that used the removed obstacle |
| Telemetry-only | 2 | A telemetry level, a sampling reservoir | Keeps every decision |
| Catalog and price | 1 | A new admissible part of lower cost | Keeps witnesses; revokes the minimum |

   The amendments are frozen (a sealed manifest with a digest) before the revalidation engine, the mission layer
   or the incumbent sees them, and they are released to all arms at once.
3. Ground truth. For each amendment a session that implements none of the arms recomputes the amended answer
   from scratch (admission, search, bundles, checker) and replays every decision. The result is, per verdict of
   the base answer, `unchanged` or `changed` with the new verdict. Unaffected verdicts are those that are
   unchanged.
4. Incumbent arm. A second session implements demand-driven recomputation: a dependency graph over the same
   lowered computations, keyed completely (delivery times and implicit dependencies included), with solver
   sensitivity information (dual values and slack) to skip a recheck when a changed bound still fits. It is the
   strongest conventional rival, and it is eligible to be the shipped implementation if it is as correct and
   cheaper.
5. Arm under test: the project's revalidation engine at the grades available at M3 (identity and dependency),
   run through the engine's own driver; the polarity rules of the mission layer are scored on the same campaign
   by the gate script once that layer exists, with the same ground truth and the same scoring code.
6. Measurements, per amendment and arm, all from the same cache state:
   - incorrectly inherited verdicts: verdicts the arm kept that ground truth marks `changed`. Each is a false
     accept, even if a later amendment in the campaign would restore feasibility;
   - unnecessary invalidations: verdicts the arm rechecked or reran that ground truth marks `unchanged`,
     as a share of unaffected verdicts;
   - critical-path cost: wall and compute seconds from edit to the successor answer, by phase (mapping the
     edit, checks, reruns, bundle writing, checker);
   - the dirty fraction: the share of base obligations whose support contains an edited field.
7. Hard stop and reading. Any incorrectly inherited verdict by the arm under test is the falsifier: the rule
   that licensed it is removed (the kill gate), and work continues only on the remaining rules. Where the two
   arms are equally correct, the costs are compared; implication-grade reuse is pursued only in cells where
   costs support the registered target. A tie in cost is recorded as a tie, and the incumbent's design may ship.
8. Potency controls: (a) a planted stale-key variant of the arm under test (its supports omit delivery time)
   must produce at least one incorrectly inherited verdict on the camera-timing category; (b) an arm that
   reruns everything must score zero incorrect inheritance and 100% unnecessary invalidation; (c) the
   visual-only category must show zero rechecks in a correct arm.
9. Record: a pilot-register row (mechanism "revalidation by implication", tier Frontier, protocol digest,
   falsifier, ceiling as measured dirty fraction, fallback and kill gate, cost, result) and the per-amendment
   table.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The sealed manifest of 30 amendments exists with a digest dated before any arm's first run on it, and
      the sessions that wrote the arms did not write it.
- [ ] Ground truth exists for every base verdict under every amendment, from full recomputation and replay.
- [ ] The incumbent arm exists, was written by a separate session, and its keys include delivery times and the
      implicit dependencies.
- [ ] Per amendment and arm, the four measurements are on file.
- [ ] The three potency controls behave as stated.
- [ ] The pilot record states the measured dirty fraction as the ceiling and the outcome against the falsifier.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/amendment_campaign.rs`: `manifest_has_thirty_with_categories` (counts per category
as in the table; the one-tick packet advance is present); `ground_truth_complete`; `scoring_false_accept` (a
kept verdict that ground truth marks changed scores as an incorrect inheritance even when amendment k + 1
restores it); `scoring_unnecessary_invalidation_share` (3 unnecessary of 15 unaffected is 20%);
`stale_key_variant_is_caught`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_amendment_campaign.sh`: applies each amendment through the real `frob` binary (the engine's
revalidation verb for the arm under test, `frob mission revalidate <answer> --after-edit <edit> --json` where the
mission layer is present; the incumbent's driver for the second arm; a fresh `frob mission synthesize` for ground
truth), then scores. It logs through the shared
helper to `target/e2e-logs/pilot_amendment_campaign/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: per amendment and arm the
command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block, the counts of kept, rechecked and rerun
obligations, incorrect inheritances against 0, the unnecessary-invalidation share, phase costs; `assert` lines
for the controls; digests of every successor answer and of its ground truth; on a mismatch the first verdict
that differs with its cell, both values and the licence under which it was kept. A `summary` gives totals.

### Known answers and negative controls

The four-state camera-delivery fixture is a known answer for both arms: 2, 0, 0 and 1 scene-pair evaluations and
1, 0, 0 and 1 verdict recomputations, every verdict matching fresh replay; the variant that omits delivery time
inherits a stale promotion (CX-287). The docking support enlargement (the gain 33/32 at ε = 1/64 reverses every
positive label; best worst-case error 1/65 m) and the passive-spring amendment at half-width 1/4 m
((175, 115, 85, 25) N against 160 N) are exact withdrawals. Negative controls: the three potency controls.
Determinism: with fixed seeds the per-amendment table is identical on a second run.

## Considerations

- The seal matters more than the size: an engine tuned on the campaign proves nothing. The manifest is held by
  the freezing session and its digest is recorded in the pilot register first.
- "Unnecessary invalidation" has a threshold in the gate (at most 20% of unaffected verdicts) and none in the
  falsifier; a safe system that invalidates too much fails the gate without triggering the kill.
- The incumbent may win. The plan's rule is to ship the generic method when it ties or wins, and the result is
  recorded either way.
- The same campaign is read by the studio's general revalidation gate; the two gates must score from the same
  ground truth.
