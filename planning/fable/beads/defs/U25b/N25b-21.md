## Background

After an edit to a mission, a design family, a card, a sensor, a clock or the world, `mission.revalidate` reports
which verdicts of a mission answer are still proved, which are withdrawn, what must be rerun and what it will
cost. Its correctness condition is simple to state and hard to meet: every verdict it keeps must be one that full
recomputation would also return. GATE-AUGUR-12 (claim Known answer, M3) measures it: revalidation on the frozen
30-amendment campaign yields zero incorrectly inherited verdicts; unnecessary invalidations are at most 20% of
unaffected verdicts; and the cost-9 and cost-5 fixture keeps the witness and withdraws the minimum.

This bead is the end-to-end script that takes that measurement through the public verb and records the gate. It
is also the M3 demonstration of the second delivery slice of the mission chapter: one edit preserves a valid
witness; a second forces withdrawal; a third changes the required instrument; and all three match independent
full recomputation. The same campaign and ground truth are read by the studio's general revalidation gate, so
the scoring code is shared and the two records cannot disagree about what was correct.

The cost-9 and cost-5 fixture: a cost-9 cart is the certified minimum while a cost-5 relay is excluded by a
doorway. Widen only the relay's doorway. The cart and its complete successful trace remain valid, and the optimum
moves to 5. Feasibility and optimality are separate obligations: the witness is kept, the stale minimum is
withdrawn.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/mission_revalidate.sh`; the scoring against the campaign's sealed ground truth; the fixture

## Technical approach

Steps (plain shell, `set -euo pipefail`, shared helper sourced, seed-explicit, idempotent, writes under
`target/`):

1. `start`: commit, toolchain, host, seeds, the campaign manifest's digest and the ground truth's digest; assert
   the manifest digest equals the one in the pilot register (the campaign has not been edited since it was
   frozen).
2. Base answers: produce or load by address the base mission answers the campaign applies to; assert each is
   accepted by the checker and carries a bundle root.
3. For each of the 30 amendments, from the same cache state:
   `frob mission revalidate <base answer> --after-edit <edit> --json`. Assert `ok: true`; read `surviving`,
   `withdrawn`, `split`, `reruns` and the mask; execute the listed reruns; read the successor answer.
4. Score against ground truth, verdict by verdict:
   - an incorrectly inherited verdict is one listed under `surviving` (any licence) whose ground-truth verdict
     differs; assert the total over the campaign is zero;
   - an unnecessary invalidation is a verdict rechecked, rerun or withdrawn whose ground truth is unchanged;
     assert the share over all unaffected verdicts is at most 0.20;
   - the successor answer after the reruns equals the ground-truth answer cell by cell.
5. Category checks: zero rechecks on the visual-only and telemetry-only amendments; a recheck of both
   inclusions and exclusions on the packet delayed across a decision instant and on the one-tick packet
   advance; withdrawal of the stance cell at half-width 1/4 m under the passive-spring amendment with the
   allocation (175, 115, 85, 25) N named.
6. The cost-9 and cost-5 fixture: `frob mission synthesize cart-relay.fmission --json` (minimum 9 with its
   matching lower bound); apply the doorway edit; `frob mission revalidate`: assert the cart's cell is under
   `surviving`, the minimum is under `split` as `optimal: revoked`, and after the recheck the answer's
   `cost_bounds` report incumbent 5 with lower bound 5 and `attained: true`. Then apply the catalog amendment
   (a new admissible part of cost 7 to the original answer) and assert the minimum is revoked again.
7. Mask audit: for three amendments chosen by seed, verify the mask against the recorded dependency supports and
   the edit's field list with the conformance crate's independent checker, without rerunning anything.
8. Withdrawal records: for every withdrawn cell assert a logged withdrawal that names two worlds or a new mode,
   and that `frob evidence trace` on the successor answer resolves it.
9. Stale use: hand the pre-edit docking screen (compiled for gains {1, 2}) to the amended mission with gain
   33/32 added; assert exit 3 and FROB-REF-854 with `mission.revalidate` as the repair.
10. `summary` and gate record with the measured values: incorrectly inherited verdicts (threshold 0), the
    unnecessary-invalidation share (threshold 0.20), and the fixture's outcome.

Logging: newline-delimited JSON at `target/e2e-logs/mission_revalidate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript.
Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25b-21`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time
and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). Per amendment: its
category, counts of obligations by licence (`kept-identity`, `kept-dependency`, `kept-implication` with the rule
name, `recheck`, `rerun`), rerun cost by phase, the digest of the successor answer and of the ground truth.
`metric` lines: `incorrectly_inherited` against 0 and `unnecessary_invalidation_share` against 0.20. On failure:
the first verdict that differs, with its cell, the kept value, the true value, the licence and rule under which
it was kept, and the paths of both answers. A final `summary` names the gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-12 (TARGET, M3): zero incorrectly inherited verdicts on the frozen 30-amendment campaign;
      unnecessary invalidations at most 20% of unaffected verdicts; the cost-9 and cost-5 fixture keeps the
      witness and withdraws the minimum. Measured values are recorded.
- [ ] The campaign manifest's digest equals the frozen one.
- [ ] Every successor answer equals ground truth after its reruns.
- [ ] The mask audit passes on the sampled amendments.
- [ ] The stale-screen use is refused with FROB-REF-854.
- [ ] The log ends with a `summary` line; the script exits non-zero on any failed assertion.
- [ ] Negative controls (self-test): (a) an engine build whose supports omit packet delivery time yields at
      least one incorrectly inherited verdict in the camera-timing category and the script fails at step 4 with
      that verdict logged; (b) a build that reruns everything passes the first clause and fails the 20% clause;
      (c) a build that treats the optimum as one verdict keeps the stale minimum and fails step 6.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`scripts/e2e/tests/mission_revalidate_selftest.sh` runs the script against the three planted builds and asserts
the failing step and the first differing value for each. The scorer (shared with the pilot) has unit tests:
`false_accept_counts_even_if_later_restored`; `share_three_of_fifteen_is_twenty_percent`;
`successor_equals_truth_cellwise`.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. It runs on the CPU; campaign amendments that need device episodes use the tiers the
base answers used, and the determinism class of each comparison is recorded.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-224 (the polarity table: each row gives the stated survival on the docking and stance fixtures), KA-638 (the
gain 33/32 at ε = 1/64: no exact docking; best worst-case error 1/65 m), KA-626 ((175, 115, 85, 25) N against
(155, 135, 105, 5) N at half-width 1/4 m). The four-state camera fixture: 2, 0, 0, 1 pair evaluations and
1, 0, 0, 1 recomputations. CX-282 (a certificate that held for the declared worlds holds when a world is added),
CX-287 (a memo that omits an input). Negative controls: the three planted builds. Metamorphic relation: applying
an amendment and then its inverse returns the original answer's digest. Determinism lanes: the script twice on
one machine and on a second host gives identical successor digests for the exact fixtures.

## Considerations

- One incorrectly inherited verdict here is also the trigger of the kill gate KILL-AUGUR-REUSE when it was
  licensed by an implication rule; the log records the licence and rule so that the decision can be taken from
  the log alone.
- The 20% clause protects usefulness, not safety; failing it does not withdraw anything.
- If the incumbent arm of the pilot is as correct and cheaper, the plan ships it behind the same verb; this
  script then measures that implementation with no change.
- RISK-38 (an incomplete memo key serves a stale verdict) is the risk under measurement; the stale-key build is
  its registered negative control.
