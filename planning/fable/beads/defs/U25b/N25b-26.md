## Background

A kill gate names its trigger and its fallback in advance and is never quietly re-scoped; its decision is recorded
as an ADR at the milestone the gate register names. A kill gate that fires is not a failure of the program: the
fallback is the plan.

KILL-AUGUR-SEPARATE guards the Frontier mechanism "design–world separation": given two feasible designs and a
decision between them, return the smallest admissible world in which the decision reverses, minimized, with the
edit that removes the effect.

- Trigger: minimization changes the failure mechanism in more than 20% of planted cases, or a conventional
  scenario tool with the same generator matches the result at equal cost.
- Fallback, named in advance: ship the adversarial search and minimization without the design-pair query; keep
  counterexamples as regressions.
- Decided at M3.

The two clauses test two different things. The first asks whether the mechanism's own output is trustworthy: a
"minimized separating world" whose failure is of a different kind from the one found is not a smaller version of
the same counterexample, and if that happens often the query's central promise (the smallest world that exposes
this failure) is empty. The second is doctrine D-17 applied here: a specialized method names the generic method
it must beat, and if the generic method ties, the plan says so and ships the generic method. The generic method
is a scenario-based falsification tool that samples worlds from the same generator, evaluates each design alone
with the same monitors, and differences the failure sets.

## Scope

In: the measurement protocol for both clauses; the definition of "equal cost" and of "matches"; the decision
rule; the ADR; the downstream list; the fail-closed posture.
Out: the pilot that supplies the first reading (N25b-25); the engine (N25b-23); the gate on planted reversals
that the verb must pass if the mechanism is kept (BD-293); the shrinker's own kill gate.

## Technical approach

1. Population. The planted cases: reversals whose cause is known by construction. From the pilot: the reversals
   found on its three cases (split-grip stance, one periodic gait, rear-wheel pull-over). From the sealed
   population of N25bp1-01 (a dependency): the ten planted design reversals that the verb's gate also reads,
   each with a known minimal edit set of at most three edits and a known failure class for the losing design,
   validated on the referee and sealed before any engine run. Cases 1 and 2 of that population are public
   known answers and are reported apart from the eight sealed ones.
   The engine is run here before the verb exists (the verb, BD-293, depends on this decision): a conformance
   runner, `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example separation_measure -- <case> --json` (an example target: the crate has no binary, BD-025), calls the engine's entry
   (N25b-23) on each case at a pinned engine revision and writes one envelope per case with the engine's
   recorded cost by phase (search, referee reproduction, minimization, checks), the edit lists before and after
   minimization, the failure class, binding atom and first cause before and after, and receipts. That
   revision is recorded in the ADR; N25bp1-01's one-sealed-run rule then binds the verb's gate run to it.
2. Clause 1, mechanism change. For each planted case, run the engine (or, in the pilot, the manual procedure)
   and compare the failure class and binding requirement atom of the losing design before and after
   minimization, and the first cause (the earliest monitor anomaly before the terminal event). A case counts as
   mechanism-changed when the class or the binding atom differs. The measured share is changed cases over
   planted cases in which a reversal was found. Threshold: more than 20% triggers.
3. Clause 2, the conventional tool at equal cost.
   - Same generator: the comparator draws worlds from the same legality set and primitives, by random and grid
     sampling, with the same monitors and the same frozen controllers.
   - Equal cost: its budget is the engine's recorded total for that case (search, referee reproduction,
     minimization and checks), in wall time and compute seconds on the same host, warm.
   - Matches: within that budget the comparator returns a world in which the decision reverses, reproduced on
     the referee, whose edit count is no larger than the engine's result, and whose failure class equals the
     planted one. A larger or mechanism-changed world is not a match.
   - The clause is met when the comparator matches on every planted case in which the engine succeeded (the
     gate text says "matches the result"); the per-case table is recorded so that a partial tie is visible.
4. Decision rule, in N40-03's outcomes.
   - Clause 1 share above 0.20, or clause 2 met: outcome `fired`, the fallback in force under the name
     "adversarial search and minimization without the design-pair query". The design-pair query is
     not shipped. The adversarial search and the shrinker ship as they are for single designs; every
     counterexample found during the pilot and the measurement is kept as a regression fixture.
   - Otherwise: outcome `not-fired`, citing the measurement's receipt; the mechanism is kept; the verb's gate
     (ten planted reversals, at most three edits each, first cause unchanged, confirmed on the referee) is its
     acceptance.
5. Guard against a hollow pass. For each case the comparator is also run at twice the budget; a case it matches
   only at twice the budget is marked `marginal` and reported separately. A population in which the engine
   itself fails to find the planted reversal in more than half the cases cannot produce `not-fired`: the
   measurement is void, the entry is `deferred` to M4 with that reason, and the default (not shipped, step 7)
   stays.
6. Downstream list.
   - Fired: the verb's bead and the engine's pairing code close with reason `superseded` and the ADR's
     identifier; the admissibility contract stays (it also governs single-design counterexamples); the dual
     query on priced world edits re-bases on single-design counterexamples; `mission.separate` is removed from
     the generated documentation or marked not implemented with exit code 5; the ten-fold strata that would
     have used separation use the adversarial search directly.
   - Not fired: nothing changes except the decision record.
7. Fail-closed posture until the decision: `mission.separate` is not offered to agents; any separation shown in
   a report carries the label "pilot".
8. ADR content, in `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-SEPARATE-M3.md` with the required fields N40-03 lists: the
   per-case table (found, edits, class before and after, comparator result at one and two times the budget,
   costs), the two numbers, the pinned engine revision and the freeze record's digest, the decision, the
   downstream list as applied. The kill row's decision entry (`outcome`, `adr`, `date`, `session`, `receipts`)
   is written with the ADR by a session that implemented neither the engine nor the pilot. That session also
   writes the gate's owner declaration, which N40-30 compares with the ledger (this bead is the deciding bead in
   N25d-41's map), in `frob-mission`'s `contract.toml`: `[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`, the ledger's current
   `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied, not restated, and the `decision` block with each
   measured value citing its receipt; without it `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` fails
   `owner-declaration-missing` at the M3 exit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The per-case table exists for the whole population, from recorded runs with receipts.
- [ ] The mechanism-change share (threshold 0.20) and the comparator's match count are recorded; marginal cases
      are listed.
- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-SEPARATE-M3.md` records the decision at M3 with both clauses evaluated against
      their numbers and the fields N40-03 requires, and the kill row's decision entry has outcome `fired` or
      `not-fired` (`deferred` only for a void measurement), citing the measurement's receipt.
- [ ] The downstream list is applied.
- [ ] The owner declaration of KILL-AUGUR-SEPARATE is in `frob-mission`'s `contract.toml`, and
      `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` passes for the row.
- [ ] Negative controls of the measurement: (a) a planted population in which shrinking is forced to change the
      mechanism in 3 of 10 cases yields a share of 0.30 and the decision `fired`; (b) a comparator capped at
      one sample yields no match and, with a clean clause 1, the decision `not-fired`; (c) a population on which
      the engine finds nothing is declared void and gives `deferred`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_augur_separate.rs`:
- `share_boundary`: 2 changed of 10 is 0.20 and does not trigger ("more than 20%"); 3 of 10 triggers.
- `match_requires_edit_count_and_class`: a comparator world with four edits against the engine's two is not a
  match; a world with the same count and a different failure class is not a match.
- `equal_cost_budget_from_recorded_total`.
- `comparator_must_match_all_to_trigger`: 9 of 10 matches does not meet clause 2; the table still lists them.
- `void_when_engine_finds_less_than_half`.
- `marginal_cases_reported`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kill_augur_separate_decision.sh` checks that N25bp1-01's freeze record predates the first engine
run, runs the engine on each planted case through the conformance runner of step 1, runs the comparator on each
case with the real `frob` binary (the scenario tool is `nemesis.search`'s one-arm campaigns over the case's
legality set, on each design with the same seed so that the drawn worlds are paired: random sampling with `frob
nemesis search <design> --task <task> --legality <set> --method card-uniform --seed <s> --budget cpu_core_s=<b> --key
kas-<run>-<case>-<design>-cu-<budget> --json`, and grid sampling with `--method given --worlds <grid.ndjson>`
(the grid over the set's primitives, written by the script as world documents) under the key
`kas-<run>-<case>-<design>-gv-<budget>`; the engine's recorded total for the case is split between the two
designs and the two modes, then the whole is repeated at twice that budget; every run-verb call carries its own
key, so that the two budgets and the two designs are never answered from one stored receipt), differences the
two designs' failure sets, reproduces every claimed reversal with `frob sim replay <trace> --tier referee --key
kas-<run>-<case>-rp-<n> --json`, builds the table, applies the rule and writes the measurement's receipt
(`receipts/gates/<hash>.json`) and the ADR skeleton; it writes no register entry (step 8). It logs through the
shared helper to `target/e2e-logs/kill_augur_separate_decision/<utc>.ndjson` with
a transcript: command lines, exit codes, wall times and the envelope's evidence block for every evaluation; the
engine revision and the freeze record's digest; per case the engine's and the comparator's edit counts, failure
classes and costs; `metric` lines for the share against 0.20 and for the match count against the population
size; the decision; on an unexpected outcome the case, the step, the two `.fmcw` paths and the seed needed to
reproduce it; a `summary`. For a sealed case the committed log carries digests and counts only.

### Known answers and negative controls

The planted cases have known minimal edit sets by construction; the standing one is the split-grip strip offset
(one edit, from +12 mm to −12 mm, reversing the preference between the 40 mm and 70 mm soles: capacities 0.690
against 0.619 and 0.360 against 0.431). The shrinker's control "failure exactly when edits 3 and 6 are both
present returns {3, 6}" checks the minimization half. Negative controls as listed. Determinism: the table is a
function of recorded runs with fixed seeds.

## Considerations

- "Equal cost" and "matches" are not defined in the plan beyond the sentence of the trigger; the definitions
  above are DESIGN and are fixed in the measurement's registration before any run, so that the gate is not
  re-scoped after the data.
- The comparator is given every advantage the engine has (the generator, the monitors, warm caches). An arm
  against a weak baseline measures the baseline.
- The shrinker has its own kill gate (KILL-MCW, decided by N26a-35, a dependency: on unplanted failures, fewer
  than half of shrunk worlds have at most three edits, or fewer than 90% replay); if that one fires, separation
  loses its minimization and this gate is decided `fired`.
- If the mechanism is killed, nothing downstream on the critical path depends on it.
