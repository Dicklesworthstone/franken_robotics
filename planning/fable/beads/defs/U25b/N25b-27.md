## Background

A design agent should be able to ask whether spending more compute on training with the present sensor suite can
ever satisfy the mission. Sometimes the answer is no for a reason that has nothing to do with the body, the
controller or the amount of compute: two situations the robot cannot tell apart require different actions.

The observation-indistinguishability witness (proof kind `alias-pair`): construct two physically admissible
latent states that require disjoint successful actions. Fix the permissible probe actions, the time available,
the observation histories and the policy's information. For equal prior classes and a fixed observation
experiment with distributions P and Q, the best probability of distinguishing the states is (1 + TV(P, Q))/2,
where TV is the total-variation distance; this caps action success when success requires distinguishing them. If
active probing is allowed, the bound is taken over all permitted probe strategies, or it is labelled as applying
to the specified experiment only. A claimed universal observability bound fails if a permitted policy can perform
a safe probe the model left out.

Finding candidate world pairs is the sensing subsystem's work (`sense.alias`), and so is the exact arithmetic:
the total-variation distance and the enumerator of sensing policies are built once, in `frob-sense` (N20a-40).
This bead is the mission side: turning a witness into a scoped statement about a mission (an exclusion under
every admitted policy, or a bound for one named experiment), comparing sensing policies by their exact success
probabilities under the mission's deadline, and getting the three lessons of the exact fixture into the
answer. Tier: Frontier. Paper pilot: run
(the sensor-aliasing pilot, whose re-run in this repository is a dependency). Ceiling: none claimed; exact Bayes
classification ties. Kill gate: KILL-AUGUR-ALIAS (generated alias witnesses are contradicted by a permitted probe
in more than 5% of audited cases; fallback: witnesses are labelled experiment-specific only and universal
observability claims are refused).

The exact fixture. Two sealed payload classes require disjoint final forces, [2, 4] N and [7, 9] N. A binary
probe reports the correct class with probability 3/4.

| Sensing policy | Success per class | Meets a 90% target |
|---|---|---|
| Blind | 1/2 | No |
| One read | 3/4 | No |
| Three independent reads, majority | 27/32 | No |
| Five independent reads | 459/512 | No |
| Seven independent reads | 3807/4096 | Yes |
| Nine reads sharing one common fault bit | 3/4 | No |
| One read of a distinct 95% modality | 19/20 | Yes |
| Stop at a vote gap of two, unbounded horizon | 9/10, with 16/5 expected reads | Yes, only without a deadline |
| The same rule, forced to guess after six reads | 225/256 | No |

Exact Bayes classification on the full observation sequence attains the same numbers. Three lessons are part of
the specification. First, error dependence, not per-read accuracy, sets the value of redundancy: the common-mode
row is the assumption that reverses the sensor-budget decision. Second, "seven" is a fixed-horizon result and is
not a lower bound on expected cost across adaptive policies: the sequential rule reaches 90% in 3.2 expected
reads. Third, the chance of still waiting after six reads is 27/512, and forcing a fair guess then gives 225/256,
below 90%; so delay is priced and the mission deadline is enforced for every sensing policy that is compared.

## Technical approach

1. Witness object (DESIGN): two admitted worlds; the commitment deadline; the permitted probe set; for the
   deterministic case the common observation history and the two disjoint success sets; for the stochastic case
   the fixed experiment and its two observation laws P and Q as finite distributions with rational
   probabilities; `scope`, one of `all-admitted-probes` or `named-experiment(id)`; the source (structural,
   symmetry, enumerated, searched).
2. Scope rule. `all-admitted-probes` may be written only when the argument covers every permitted probe
   strategy: a structural argument (no information path from the hidden parameter to any delivered packet
   before the deadline), or exhaustive enumeration of the permitted probe strategies on a finite abstraction. A
   witness from search alone is `named-experiment`. Only an `all-admitted-probes` witness supports a statement
   about every admitted policy; a `named-experiment` witness supports a bound for that experiment and is worded
   so.
3. The bound. For equal priors and a fixed experiment, the best probability of acting correctly when success
   requires distinguishing the states is (1 + TV(P, Q))/2 with TV(P, Q) = ½·Σ_o |P(o) − Q(o)|. For n jointly
   independent binary reads of accuracy a the laws are product laws and the value equals the success of the
   likelihood-ratio rule (majority for odd n, majority with a fair tie-break for even n).
4. Exact values. Success probabilities come from the enumerator of `frob-sense` (N20a-40), called through its
   library interface from `crates/frob-mission/src/alias/policies.rs` (DESIGN); `frob-mission` holds no second
   enumerator. This bead passes it: the per-read channel (accuracy, or a declared finite joint law of the read
   errors), the sensing policy and the mission's deadline in reads. The policies it compares are the
   enumerator's: `Fixed(n)`; `Sequential { gap }` (stop when one class leads by `gap`); `SequentialForced { gap,
   max_reads }` (the same, with a fair guess at `max_reads`); `Modality { accuracy }`. What comes back, all
   exact rationals: success probability per class, expected number of reads, the probability of being undecided
   at the deadline (the enumerator sums over the 2ⁿ observation sequences for n binary reads, n at most 20).
5. Deadline rule. Every sensing policy compared for a mission is evaluated under the mission's deadline:
   an unbounded sequential rule is reported with its expected cost and is marked not admissible when the
   mission has a deadline; the forced-guess variant is what is compared.
6. Error dependence. Redundancy (a success probability above single-read accuracy) is credited only when the
   reads' joint law is declared: jointly independent, or a declared finite joint law that the enumerator
   evaluates. With a shared fault bit the value stays at the single-read accuracy for any number of reads. With
   no declared joint law the guaranteed value is the single-read accuracy. The joint law of the read errors is
   read from the card and never assumed (DESIGN, stated in the same words in N20a-40 and N25b-27). The
   declaration is the dependence class of the failure cause on the card (N20a-24): `per-sample` means jointly
   independent reads; `per-region-episode` and `shared-with` mean a common fault, one fault bit shared by the
   reads; with no declaration the guaranteed value is the single-read accuracy for any number of reads. A finite
   joint law given explicitly is enumerated as given. The later card field `error_dependence`
   (`joint-independent`, `shared-fault`, `declared-law`, `pairwise`, `unknown`; bead N25e-34, which lands after
   this one) replaces this reading when it exists and governs from then on: under `pairwise` or `unknown` no
   redundancy is credited.
7. Proof objects. Deterministic witness: an `alias-pair` object of version 1 (two worlds; their observation
   sets up to commitment; their success sets; the scope). Stochastic witness: an `alias-pair` object of version
   2, whose payload also carries the two laws and the claimed bound; its checker (N12x-06, a dependency)
   recomputes TV and the bound exactly. This bead adds the version-2 fields to the `alias-pair` payload type of
   N25b-01 and to the mission writer's mapping (BD-133), in the schema N12x-06 publishes, so that the writer
   reproduces N12x-06's version-2 specimens byte for byte. Class: `Certified` when the worlds are replayed in an
   exact integer or rational class; `Tested` when they run in floating point.
8. Wording in `mission.refute`. With an `all-admitted-probes` witness whose bound is below the requirement: "no
   admitted policy under this observation interface meets the requirement", an exclusion for the cell, with the
   witness. With a `named-experiment` witness: "under experiment E the best achievable success is b", a bound
   attached to an unresolved cell, never an exclusion. In both cases the answer lists the binding fact (which
   observation, which deadline) and the kinds of change that relax it: extract the information, move to acquire
   it, or change the mechanics so that it is unnecessary.

Determinism: exact rational enumeration, class D0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the fixture the mission answer reports every row of the fixture table exactly, with the values taken
      from the enumerator of N20a-40: 1/2, 3/4, 27/32, 459/512, 3807/4096, 3/4 with a common fault bit over nine
      reads, 19/20, 9/10 with 16/5 expected reads, and 225/256 when forced at six with undecided probability
      27/512. (That the enumerator computes them is N20a-40's acceptance.)
- [ ] With a deadline of six reads the answer states that no policy on this probe reaches 90%: six independent
      reads give 459/512 (equal to five), TV = 203/256 and (1 + TV)/2 = 459/512.
- [ ] A witness from search alone cannot carry the scope `all-admitted-probes`; attempting it is refused.
- [ ] With a `named-experiment` witness `mission.refute` returns a bound on an unresolved cell and no exclusion.
- [ ] A permitted safe probe omitted from the model defeats a universal claim: on a planted case where a
      permitted probe separates the two worlds, the enumeration over permitted probes finds it and the witness
      is rejected.
- [ ] Reads with no declared joint law are credited only single-read accuracy.
- [ ] The stochastic witnesses emitted on the fixture are written as version-2 `alias-pair` bundles that N12x-06's
      checker accepts, and the writer reproduces N12x-06's version-2 specimens (`specimens/alias-pair/v2/`) byte
      for byte; version-1 bundles are unchanged.
- [ ] The unbounded sequential rule is reported with its expected cost and marked not admissible under a
      deadline.
- [ ] Negative controls: a build that passes shared-fault reads to the enumerator as independent returns a value
      above 3/4 for nine reads and fails the fixture; a build that reports the fixed-horizon "seven" as a lower
      bound on expected reads is caught by the sequential row (16/5 < 7).

## Considerations

- A witness ends a futile policy search; it does not speed a search that can succeed. No speed is claimed.
- The universal scope is the dangerous one: a probe the model left out makes a universal bound false, which is
  what the kill gate audits. Until that audit has a record, universal wording is available only for structural
  and enumerated witnesses.
- A pairwise-independent joint law can have the same accuracy and pair tables as an independent one and a lower
  best success (55/64 against 3807/4096 for seven reads in a known construction); this is why "pairwise" is not
  accepted as a declaration of independence.
- The standing refusal on training against a mission with a standing witness belongs to the learning verbs; this
  bead supplies the witness they attach.
