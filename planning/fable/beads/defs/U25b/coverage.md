## (e) Contradictions, gaps and what was done

1. Boundary with U12. The Lemma spine is split: this unit writes the seeds BD-021, BD-026, BD-027, BD-133 and
   BD-136; U12 wrote the registry, schema, report, family-certificate and gate-record beads around them. Each
   seed here states its scope against those keys in `needs` and `related`. Overlap that should be reconciled by
   links, not by dropping either side: schema fields (BD-027, BD-133 against N12-03) and independence checks
   (BD-136 against N12-09).
2. Two refusal vocabularies for one event. The Lemma table has FROB-REF-823, -826, -829 and the mission table
   has FROB-REF-848, -850, -851, -852, -881 for overlapping causes. Carried as a mapping in BD-133: the auditor
   and `prove.verify` report the Lemma code with the rule identifier; mission verbs report the mission code.
   Two mutations of §25.5.3 have no dedicated mission code: "a cell whose closed end was certified only as open"
   is mapped to FROB-REF-826, and a missing side condition on a proof that is not a transport to FROB-REF-829
   (FROB-REF-852 is worded for transports).
3. GATE-WS-03 is the done-condition of the M0 seed BD-026, while the gate register dates it M1 and one of its
   four clauses needs the M1 conformance corpus. Three clauses are measured at M0 (N25b-05) and the row stays
   `not run` until the fourth is measured at M1.
4. GATE-LEMMA-00 names T-04 "for the M0 kernels", but no M0 seed derives a kernel whose program is the adjoint
   action. BD-021 adds a three-assignment program `K-ad-se3` to the derivation list (DESIGN).
5. "Constructing Proved evidence outside frob-lemma fails to compile" cannot be enforced by Rust visibility
   alone when the evidence type lives in `frob-core`. BD-021 specifies type privacy plus one doorway guarded by
   a denied lint and a workspace scan, and states the acceptance test so that another sealing mechanism chosen
   by the owner of `frob-core` (BD-005) satisfies it unchanged.
6. Milestones. The verb registry dates `mission.separate` and `mission.revalidate` M4; the gate register dates
   GATE-AUGUR-13 and GATE-AUGUR-12 M3, and seed BD-293 is M3. The gate register governs: BD-293 and N25b-18
   deliver at M3.
7. Seeds of other units inside this unit's subsections. BD-358 (region certificates, U25e) and BD-292
   (revalidation engine, U25a) carry GATE-AUGUR-09 and GATE-AUGUR-12 in their done-conditions, while §25.6 and
   §25.8 are this unit's. This unit wrote the granular mechanism, pilot, decision and gate-script beads
   (N25b-08 to N25b-11; N25b-18 to N25b-21) and made the gate scripts depend on those seeds. Some overlap with
   the seed bodies is likely.
8. The 30-amendment campaign is needed at M3 by GATE-AUGUR-12 (and by the studio's gate, part of which is dated
   M1), while seed BD-377 places it in conformance at M4. N25b-19 freezes it at M3; BD-377 is named as its later
   home. The M1 part of the studio's gate is outside this unit.
9. Kill-gate terms the plan leaves undefined were fixed as DESIGN inside the decision beads, to be registered
   before measurement: "equal cost" and "matches" for KILL-AUGUR-SEPARATE (N25b-26); the audited population,
   sample size and the meaning of "contradicted by a permitted probe" for KILL-AUGUR-ALIAS (N25b-29); the cost
   model and the per-clause reading for KILL-AUGUR-CUTS (N25b-16, N25b-17); the confirmation window and the
   cause analysis for KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT (N25b-03, N25b-04); per-rule removal for KILL-AUGUR-REUSE (N25b-20).
10. Region answers are implicit (K membership tests, never an enumerated joint set), while an answer is a list
    of cells that partition the domain. Resolved in N25b-10: the answer's cells are the query's own boxes,
    classified through a membership oracle, and `mission.show` gains a membership selector (DESIGN).

    affine form or a Taylor model cannot be emitted as `interval-box`; N25b-09 emits it as `taylor-box` or

13. The stochastic indistinguishability bound needs the two observation laws in the `alias-pair` payload, which

14. Fixture details absent from the plan were fixed as DESIGN defaults to be frozen by the bead that runs them:
