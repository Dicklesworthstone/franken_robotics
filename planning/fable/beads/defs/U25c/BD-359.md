## Background

An engineer asks: can replacing this sole cause a formerly successful delivery to fail? The two success sets are
expensive to compute and may be mostly unknown. Ask directly for the relation.

For two fixed causal controllers, a regression is a shared world input in which A succeeds and B fails. Build
the product of the two systems driven by the same exogenous scene, payload and clock law, and ask whether the
terminal pair (A succeeds, B fails) = (1, 0) is reachable. Unreachability establishes dominance on a cell without
either robot having a guarantee. This is construction C-40 of the mathematical spine, and it replaces two habits:
computing two success sets and comparing them, and pairing by seed while assuming the worlds match.

The hand-checkable example. One measured surface parameter z ranges over [0, 1]. A completes when z ≥ 3/5 and B
when z ≥ 2/5. Each has both successful and failing worlds; neither has a guarantee. A separate interval for each
binary output allows all four pairs. The coupled constraint eliminates (1, 0): the replacement loses no previous
success and adds the interval [2/5, 3/5). Under a uniform law the gain is 1/5; without a law the interval of
newly covered surfaces is still an engineering answer.

Tier: Frontier. The pilot (N25c-24) runs first. The seed's done-condition is GATE-AUGUR-16 and the decision of
KILL-AUGUR-DOMINANCE; the gate is measured by N25c-26 and the decision recorded by N25c-27, both of which depend
on this bead.

## Technical approach

**Query (DESIGN).** A `.fmission` with a `comparison` block: a baseline design and controller (A), a replacement
design and controller (B), the shared inputs (scene, payload, clock law, and each declared disturbance with its
index: space, time or unit), and one acceptance predicate evaluated per robot. The prefix is for all worlds, for
all executions; there is no existential policy binder because both controllers are fixed. Default surface:
`frob mission refute dominance.fmission --design d:B --policy p:B --baseline d:A --baseline-policy p:A --key
dom-1 --json` (a run verb: every call carries an idempotency key; the options `--baseline`, `--baseline-policy`
and `--paired-plan` are this bead's, registered in the `mission.refute` row of VERBS-mission from M4).

**Outcomes.**

- Unreachability of (1, 0) over an enclosure of every admitted joint execution, with progress and nonempty
  executions, establishes dominance on the cell: proof kind `product-unreachable`, class `Certified`.
- A concrete admissible paired trace that reaches (1, 0) is a counterexample: proof kind `trace-witness`
  (a replay address, a digest and the monitor verdict), class `Tested`.
- An outer enclosure that intersects (1, 0) triggers a witness search or an unresolved verdict.

**Joint coverage is checked explicitly.** The diagonal pairs (0, 0) and (1, 1) project onto both full marginal
outcome sets and hide (1, 0) when the two internal branch choices can vary independently. Only the declared
shared inputs and disturbances are coupled; every other admissible branch combination is retained for a
worst-case assertion. The proof object therefore lists, per cell, the branch set of A, the branch set of B, and
for each compatible pair its joint label; the checker confirms that the listed pairs are all the pairs the
declared coupling allows.

**Algorithm.**

1. Admit: both controllers are fixed and causal on their own observation contracts; a controller retuned or
   trained per body is refused for this query with `FROB-REF-881` (exit 3; the comparison's prefix binds no
   policy, and a controller chosen per body is an existential over policies, a different prefix; `pointer` at
   the controller's entry in the `comparison` block), with the repair "state its information and budget
   contract and ask a mission query with an existential policy".
2. Build the product: state (x_A, x_B, shared world state), one clock law, shared inputs fed to both plants,
   independent inputs duplicated, a terminal monitor on the pair of outcomes.
3. Partition the initial and parameter domain into boxes. The domain is split where a mode transition could
   break relational invariance (the boundaries at which either robot's branch set changes).
4. On each box, enclose each robot's outcome per branch (exact case analysis where the outcome maps are rational
   piecewise-affine; outward-rounded interval enclosure otherwise). Form the joint outcome set over compatible
   branch pairs.
5. If the set excludes (1, 0), and executions are nonempty and progress to the horizon on every branch: emit
   `product-unreachable` for the box.
6. If it contains (1, 0): search the box for a concrete paired execution (sample, then refine); on success
   replay it on the event-located tier and emit `trace-witness`; otherwise mark the box unresolved with the
   reason, or split.
7. A numerical branch that cannot be enclosed goes to per-case replay; it never closes a box.
8. Assemble the answer: dominated cells, regression cells with witnesses, unresolved cells, and the set on which
   B gains (cells with label (0, 1)).

**Absolute verdicts.** A relative zero-loss assertion does not establish that either robot meets the absolute
mission. The verb therefore emits the per-design absolute hard-limit verdicts beside the paired verdict, with
the required telemetry and energy accounting, even when the relation was proved cheaply.

**Labels for paired studies.** A closed box supplies the joint label of every paired draw that falls in it,
without replay. The labels are fed to the frozen paired rule of the comparison studies; draws in unresolved
boxes are replayed. The ceiling of this use is the fraction of paired draws whose joint label can be closed
without replay, measured on the pilot before any claim.

The hand-off (DESIGN) is a per-address outcome table in address order: one row per paired draw with its world
address, its joint label and its source (`closed` with the box identity and bundle digest, or `replayed` with
both trace digests). A study's statistic is a pure function of the per-address outcomes taken in address order
(INV-ATEL-02), so the frozen paired rule of the registered plan (the study engines of BD-124, on devices BD-262)
is evaluated on this table as it would be on a table produced by replay: `frob mission refute … --paired-plan
<plan>` returns the table's digest, the closed fraction and the paired verdict. Reason: `study.submit` runs both
arms itself and takes no supplied outcomes, so without this table the closed labels could not reach the rule.

**Evidence.** `Certified` for closed boxes under the declared model; `Tested` for witnesses. The answer's class
is the weakest among its cells. Determinism: exact on the rational fragment; outward-rounded otherwise.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/dominance.rs` (exact rationals, the real `product-unreachable` checker in process, no
mocks):

- `scalar_dominance` (KA-641, KA-189): A iff z ≥ 3/5, B iff z ≥ 2/5. Expected cells: [0, 2/5) label (0, 0);
  [2/5, 3/5) label (0, 1); [3/5, 1] label (1, 1); (1, 0) unreachable; gain measure 1/5 under a uniform law.
- `separate_enclosure_rejected` (CX-277, CX-094): marginal outcome sets {0, 1} and {0, 1} admit all four pairs;
  a certificate built from them is not `product-unreachable`.
- `tied_branches_rejected`: a product reaching only (0, 0) and (1, 1) whose branches are not coupled by a
  declared shared input fails joint coverage.
- `reversed_roles`: exchanging A and B on the scalar fixture gives a regression with witness z = 1/2.
- `piecewise_affine_exact` (T-32): outcome maps with rational breakpoints; boxes close exactly at breakpoints.
- `progress_required`: a branch that deadlocks before the horizon cannot count as a success for either robot.
- `unenclosable_branch_replayed`: a branch flagged as numerically unenclosed leaves its box unresolved.
- `label_table_matches_replay`: on the scalar fixture with 1,000 seeded draws of z, the outcome table built from
  closed labels equals the table built by replaying every draw row for row, and the frozen paired rule returns
  the same verdict on both; flipping one closed label changes the table digest and is caught by the replay
  comparison.
- `cancel_keeps_closed_boxes`: a run cancelled mid-partition returns only boxes closed with their proof objects
  or witnesses, each complete in itself, and every other box unresolved; no unresolved box is reported as
  dominated.
- `card_t32_live_cells` (CARD-T-32's live output): the three cells of the scalar fixture and the cells of a
  two-branch product built by this bead pass the card's checks (a) joint coverage, (b) the outcome pair per
  piece, (c) no (success, failure) piece and (d) the progress and nonempty-execution declarations, through
  N12-17's checker in process; (e) the answer carries both absolute verdicts; the same cells with one compatible
  pair removed from the joint branch table fail (a).
- `retuned_controller_refused`: a `comparison` block whose B controller is declared retuned per body is refused
  with `FROB-REF-881` and the repair text; with the controller fixed the same query is admitted.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-641: coupled A/B on z ∈ [0, 1]: (1, 0) unreachable; B gains on [2/5, 3/5).
- CX-277: each projection of a product covers its marginal outcomes, so the dominance check may enclose them
  separately: false.
- Negative control: one mislabelled joint outcome planted in a closed box (a label flipped in the branch table)
  must be caught by replay and by the checker.
- Metamorphic: relabelling branches or reordering boxes changes nothing; refining the partition never changes a
  closed box's label.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; bundle bytes identical across hosts.

## Considerations

- Kill gate KILL-AUGUR-DOMINANCE (M4). Trigger: one mislabelled joint outcome, or a conventional reachability
  tool closes the same cells at similar cost. Fallback: paired comparison by replay under the comparison
  studies; the product lowering is kept only as the rule for space-indexed shared inputs.
- Sound enclosures through contact-rich full-order dynamics do not exist in the suite; the mechanism closes
  cells on quasi-static, finite and low-dimensional transitions and replays the rest.
- The plan names no verb for this query; it is routed through `mission.refute` (DESIGN), whose row registers
  `--baseline`, `--baseline-policy` and `--paired-plan` from M4 for this bead.
