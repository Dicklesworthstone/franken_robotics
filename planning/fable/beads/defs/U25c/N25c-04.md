## Background

GATE-AUGUR-23 is the convergence gate of skill transport by interface. Claim: Design comparison. Milestone M8.
Threshold (TARGET): the three-treatment experiment on the tray interface, pre-registered; the planted ramp-crest
loss of authority is reported with its mode and a replayable counterexample.

This is the experiment that decides funding for the Moonshot part of `mission.transport`. A shared neural network
can save training when robots differ; the question here is whether interface-based transfer saves more, and
whether it can say which part of the skill does not carry over. The pilot (N25c-01) ran the same design at paper
scale to decide whether the static core was worth building. This bead runs it as a registered gate measurement
through the public verbs, on the released surface, with confirmation on sealed worlds.

It is a design comparison, so its rules are those of every such claim: the comparison arms get equal compute and
equal source data, the fixture is frozen before tuning, the outcome measures are fixed in advance, and the result
is published whatever it is.

## Scope

In: the registration, the three arms, the measures, the planted failure check, the gate record. Out: building the
verb (N25c-02, N25c-03); the kill decision (N25c-05); the `skill.transport` half of the experiment's reporting
(the Reflex chapter's gate GATE-REFLEX-09).

## Technical approach

**Arms.** At equal compute and equal source data:

1. training from scratch on the target;
2. an ordinary cross-morphology conditioned policy with full transfer learning (the family-policy lane);
3. interface-based transfer with limited adaptation: `frob mission transport` gates each contact mode; the tray
   stabilization objective and its feedback state are ported in covered modes; adaptation is allowed only in the
   modes the verb names as lost.

**Fixture.** The tray interface between one wheeled carrier and one quadruped, on the terrain with the ramp crest
at which the target loses lateral authority. The target morphology and the terrain are frozen before any tuning.
Confirmation episodes are drawn from sealed world clusters of the blinded qualification lane, which no arm's
development has read.

**Measures, per arm.** New trials to target; irrecoverable failures; task success; contact impulse; energy. Both
denominators are reported: incremental cost per target, and amortized cost including pretraining.

**Procedure.**

0. Compute envelope. Before the first run above E0, file N44-01's reserved row R22 in
   `registries/compute_envelope.toml` (WP-79): the two training arms (1 and 2) of GATE-AUGUR-23 and arm 3's
   limited adaptation, which is given the same per-arm compute, the class that covers the budget the
   registration states for them, scope `gate:GATE-AUGUR-23`. Every training and qualification job of the three
   arms cites that scope with `--envelope-scope gate:GATE-AUGUR-23`; a job that cites none falls to `adhoc`,
   whose class in force is E0, and is refused above it. The owner's decision is recorded in the row before any
   arm starts; silence is treated as declined. If R22 is not approved, arms 1 and 2 and arm 3's adaptation are
   recorded `not run` with the envelope reason, GATE-AUGUR-23 is `not-run` (never `pass`), and the interface map
   is not claimed on the gate; arm 3's static query (step 2), which needs no training, still runs and its result
   is reported.
1. Register: fixture identity, seeds, compute per arm (with the R22 row's identity), the stopping rule for
   "trials to target", the definitions of the five measures, the falsifier and the gate text. The registration
   hash is recorded before any run.
2. Run arm 3's static query: `frob mission transport tray.fskill --from d:carrier --to d:quad --interface
   tray.json --key at-tray-static-<seed> --json`. Assert the answer is `LostAuthority` at the crest mode with the requested wrench, the
   support value and a replay address; replay the address on the referee and assert the loss occurs.
3. Run the three arms to their stopping rule; store per-arm records with replay addresses for every failure.
4. Evaluate the falsifier: arm 3 needs as many new target trials as arm 2, or arm 3 reported coverage in a mode
   where the referee shows a fall.
5. Measure the gate: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --
   GATE-AUGUR-23` against the stored arm records (with `--features gauntlet-mutations` for the two negative
   controls) writes the receipt: measured trial counts per arm (or `not run` with the envelope reason), the planted
   mode found or not, the counterexample's address, the registration hash, the R22 row and its decision, commit,
   hardware fingerprint and seeds. The script stops there and never writes the gate record (one recorder per
   gate). The recording session, which did not implement the verb, enters the row with `cargo run -p
   frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-23 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19); with R22 declined
   the row it enters is `not-run`.

**What the gate does not establish.** A successful transfer of a skill segment does not certify the remainder of
the mission. No chance claim crosses the interface without a coupling budget. Speed and physical claims are
reported separately; no reality claim is made.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The registration exists with its hash and predates every run; the fixture is frozen before tuning.
- [ ] `frob mission transport` reports the planted crest loss with its contact mode and a counterexample that replays on the referee (the gate's second clause).
- [ ] All three arms ran at equal compute and equal source data, and the five measures are recorded for each with both denominators.
- [ ] N44-01's reserved row R22 is filed before the first run above E0 and its decision recorded before any arm trains; every training and qualification job cites `--envelope-scope gate:GATE-AUGUR-23`; if R22 is declined (or unanswered) arms 1 and 2 and arm 3's adaptation are `not run` with the envelope reason and the gate is `not-run`, never `pass`.
- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-23 has a row in `registries/gates.toml`, entered by `record_gate` from the script's receipt, with measured values, or the status `fail` with the reason, or `not-run` with the envelope reason; a negative control was caught (below).
- [ ] The evidence class of every reported number is stated; the run was recorded by a session that did not implement the verb.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `registration_freezes_fixture`: a change to the terrain or the target design after registration changes the
  fixture identity and the run refuses to continue.
- `arms_equal_compute`: the harness refuses to compare arms whose recorded compute differs by more than the
  registered tolerance.
- `trials_to_target_rule`: on a synthetic learning curve that crosses the threshold at trial 37 and dips below
  it at trial 40, the stopping rule returns the registered answer (crossing is not attainment unless held for
  the registered window).
- `r22_declined_is_not_run`: with the R22 row declined, or filed with no decision, the harness records arms 1 and
  2 and arm 3's adaptation `not run` with the envelope reason and the gate status `not-run`; no input yields
  `pass` without the three arms' trial records; with R22 approved the arms proceed.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_transport_tray.sh`. It first runs `scripts/e2e/mission_transport_static.sh` (N25c-02) as the
self-check of the verb and stops if that fails. Commands, each run verb with its own key (`--key at-<arm>-<seed>`,
distinct per arm, seed and step, so that no arm's call returns another's receipt): `frob mission transport …
--key … --json`; `frob prove verify <bundle> --json`; `frob sim replay --run <run> --world <w> --key … --json` for
the counterexample; `frob train start … --envelope-scope gate:GATE-AUGUR-23 --key … --json` and `frob eval
qualify … --envelope-scope gate:GATE-AUGUR-23 --key … --json` for the arms' training and confirmation (skipped
with `e2e_skip 'R22 not approved: training arms not run'` when the envelope row is declined); then the
gate test of step 5, which writes the receipt (the script runs no `record_gate`). It asserts the R22 row's
presence and decision, the planted mode, the replayed loss, equal compute, and the falsifier outcome. It sources the shared
end-to-end helper and writes NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/mission_transport_tray/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript
beside it. Each line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the
envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and
thresholds (trial counts per arm; support value against requested wrench), digests of the bundle and of the
replayed trace, and on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths. The summary line gives
assertions passed and failed and names GATE-AUGUR-23. No private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answer: the planted crest mode is the only lost mode; the two-link coverage case (25/3 N against 10 N)
  is run first as a self-check of the verb.
- Negative control 1: the fixture without the crest; the verb must report coverage in every mode and the referee
  must show no loss.
- Negative control 2: a mutated verb that drops the contact-cone rows reports coverage at the crest; the replay
  step must contradict it and the script must fail.
- Determinism lanes: the static query twice on one machine and across worker counts gives identical bundle bytes;
  training arms are compared under their own determinism class, with seeds explicit.

## Considerations

- Compute: arms 1 and 2 train policies and arm 3 adapts in its lost modes at the same per-arm compute; their
  envelope is N44-01's reserved row R22 (step 0), approved by the owner before the run and cited by each job as
  `--envelope-scope gate:GATE-AUGUR-23`; a job above the class in force for its scope is refused before it starts
  (`FROB-REF-761`). If not approved the training is `not run` and the gate is `not-run`.
- Ceiling: the published record for inheritance across single-limb topology edits is 33–100% of from-scratch
  steps (FACT from the literature register, to be re-read), so the saving may be nil.
- Kill gate: KILL-AUGUR-TRANSPORT reads this bead's result (N25c-05). Fallback: family policies and inheritance;
  the wrench-coverage test stays as a static analysis.
- This experiment also feeds the kill gate of `skill.transport`; the two decisions are separate.
