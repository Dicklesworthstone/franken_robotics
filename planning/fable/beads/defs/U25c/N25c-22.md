## Background

GATE-AUGUR-03 (claim: Known answer; milestone M1; threshold TARGET): the docking family through the public
verbs: 561 of 561 cells, nine boundary cells excluded, all late-packet cells excluded, the equality reading
15/16 exported, the three-gain amendment withdraws every positive cell and reports 1/65 m.

"Through the public verbs" is the point. The mechanism beads (BD-129, N25c-20, N25c-21) have unit tests against
the same numbers; this bead proves that a caller with only the `frob` binary gets them, with an evidence block
on every answer and a bundle that an independent checker accepts. It is the M1 exit demonstration for the
mission chapter and the first slice an implementer can finish: two gains, two packet timings, one actuator
bound family, one observation-noise family; translate the query to a conventional scenario-tree linear program;
export the exact success region and the equality counterexample; check both outside the process; mutate the
packet timestamp and rerun. The compulsory interfaces it exercises are a causal history, a feasible controller,
two indistinguishable worlds, an exact physical parameter chart, and an independent checker. The verb also
returns what additional measurement or actuator authority would restore feasibility.

A gate passes only with a recorded measurement against its threshold and at least one caught negative control.

## Scope

In: the mission files of the fixture; the script; its assertions and logging; the gate record of GATE-AUGUR-03;
the planted negative controls. Out: the mechanisms (BD-129, N25c-20, N25c-21); the M1 exit demonstration of the
slice, which replays the worked example from a demo file, sends the rival's bundle through the same checker and
cites this gate's record (N25d-27, which depends on this bead); the docking rows of the known-answer battery
(N25d-37); the fresh-agent test of the slice (BD-140, GATE-AUGUR-07); checker independence and the mutation
corpus (BD-136); device runs.

## Technical approach

**Fixture files (checked in under `fixtures/missions/docking/`).** The first two are BD-129's (it writes and
admits them); this bead writes the others.

- `docking.fmission` (BD-129): the two-gain family on the grid U = 1/2 + i/32 (i = 0…16), ε = j/64 (j = 0…32),
  packet on time, exact target (1, 0), prefix exists policy, for all worlds, for all executions, evidence `exact`.
- `docking-late.fmission` (BD-129): the same with the packet's arrival after the third command.
- `docking-point.fmission`: the single cell U = 3/4, ε = 5/16.
- `docking-minU.fmission`: the on-time two-gain mission at ε = 5/16 with design domain U ∈ [1/2, 1] and
  `objective = { minimize: U, claim: minimum-with-matching-lower-bound }` (N25a-10's unattained infimum).
- `docking-catalog.fmission`: the same with U drawn from the catalog {1/2, 5/8, 3/4, 7/8, 1}.
- `docking-3gain.edit`: the amendment that sets the hidden support to {1, 33/32, 2}.
- `docking-3gain-point.fmission`: KA-638's point, U = 3/4, ε = 1/64, g ∈ {1, 33/32, 2}, packet on time, exact
  target.

**Steps.**

0. Stages: run `scripts/e2e/mission_docking_lowerings.sh` (N25c-20) and `scripts/e2e/mission_docking_amendment.sh`
   (N25c-21), the mechanism beads' own scripts, each with its own log; a failed stage fails this script. (BD-129's
   own script drives the lowering through cargo and is not a stage of this one, which needs only the `frob`
   binary.)
1. `frob mission check docking.fmission --json`: admitted; the quantifier order is echoed; record `MissionId`.
2. `frob mission synthesize docking.fmission --key "$(e2e_key syn-game-cold)" --json`: assert 344 inner cells,
   217 excluded cells, 0 unresolved; assert the nine boundary cells (i, j) = (0, 16), (2, 17), (4, 18), (6, 19),
   (8, 20), (10, 21), (12, 22), (14, 23), (16, 24) are excluded; assert `evidence.class = Certified`,
   `determinism = exact`, the three no-authorization flags `false`. Then compare the answer's 561 labels, in
   N20b-13's row-major order (i outer, j inner), with N20b-13's committed label vector
   (`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/docking/`): `e2e_assert_digest_eq` on the two BLAKE3 digests, both logged;
   on a mismatch the first differing cell (i, j) with both labels. N20b-13's script stops at the Gauntlet rows;
   this step is the public-verb comparison of its committed label vector.
3. `frob mission show q:<step 1's MissionId> --answer a:<step 2> --set inner --limit 50 --json` (`mission.show`
   takes the stored mission identity, not a `.fmission` path), following the
   returned `cursor` with `--cursor` until the set is exhausted, and the same with `--set excluded` (a read verb;
   the paging options are the generated `--limit` and `--cursor`): the union has 561 cells with disjoint
   interiors and stated boundary ownership (INV-AUGUR-02), and the page count with limit 50 is logged.
4. `frob mission synthesize docking.fmission --lowering scenario-tree --key "$(e2e_key syn-tree-cold)" --json`:
   the competing answer; assert cell-by-cell equality with step 2. Each lowering is run a second time, warm, with
   its own key (`syn-game-warm`, `syn-tree-warm`, so that neither returns the cold run's stored receipt), and the
   cold and warm wall times are logged as `metric` lines with the answer's phase ledger: the timings of N25c-20's
   first reading of KILL-AUGUR-TIE.
5. `frob mission refute docking-point.fmission --key "$(e2e_key refute-point)" --json`: assert `mode = scope`, the exported common reading
   15/16, the two required second commands −1/4 and −3/4, the binding constraint (separation of the reading
   sets), and the repair `U > 3/4` with `attained: false`.
5a. `frob mission synthesize docking-minU.fmission --json` (N25a-10's unattained infimum): assert
   `cost_bounds.attained = false`, lower bound 3/4, `candidate_cost_gap` with open threshold 3/4, candidate
   U = 1 (the cheapest closed point of the inner cell (3/4, 1]) and gap 1/4, the claim returned as
   `feasible-candidate`, and the warning "objective: claimed minimum not established; reported as
   feasible-candidate with gap …".
5b. `frob mission synthesize docking-catalog.fmission --json`: assert minimum U = 7/8 with `attained: true`
   and gap 0, and the entries 1/2, 5/8 and 3/4 named as excluded (3/4 by the strict comparator: 5/16 is not
   below (3/4 + 1/2)/4 = 5/16).
6. `frob mission synthesize docking-late.fmission --json`: assert 561 excluded cells, each with an `alias-pair`
   proof whose scope is "all admitted policies"; then the same with `--lowering scenario-tree`: assert
   cell-by-cell equality (the 17 common-command programs, which with the 34 extrema of step 4 are the 51
   linear programs of KA-657).
7. Mutate the packet timestamp back to on time (an edit of one field) and rerun: the answer of step 2 returns
   and the `MissionId` equals that of step 1.
8. `frob mission synthesize docking-3gain-point.fmission --json`: assert "no exact docking" with an
   `alias-pair` proof for g = 1 and g = 33/32, and best worst-case error 1/65 m at c = 64/65. Then apply
   `docking-3gain.edit` to `docking.fmission` and withdraw from the stored two-gain answer of step 2:
   `frob result revalidate a:<step 2> --against q:<amended family> --json`: exit 2, every cell invalidated with
   the reason "reads world.hidden.g", none inherited (M1 grades, N25a-13); `frob mission synthesize q:<amended
   family> --from a:<revalidated answer> --json` (the mission positional is required): assert that the 327 previously positive cells at ε ≥ 1/64 are withdrawn, each
   naming g = 1, g = 33/32 and the common reading (65/64)·p, the 17 cells at ε = 0 stay inner, and the 217
   excluded cells stay excluded.
9. `frob prove bundle a:<step 2> --out two-gain/ --json`, then offer that bundle as the two-gain screen:
   `frob mission synthesize docking.fmission --set world.hidden.g='{1, 33/32, 2}' --reuse two-gain/ --json`:
   assert refusal `FROB-REF-854`, exit 3, pointer `/world/hidden/g`, first repair revalidation with the ready
   command `frob result revalidate a:… --against q:…`; run that command and assert that it answers (exit 2, the
   invalidated list of step 8), so the first repair handed to an M1 caller works. Control: the same `--reuse`
   on the unamended `docking.fmission` is accepted (exit 0, the answer of step 2).
10. `frob prove bundle` and `frob prove verify` for the answers of steps 2, 4, 5, 6 and 8: every bundle
    accepted; total checker time recorded.
11. Negative controls (below): each planted defect must flip at least one assertion.
12. The script writes its receipt (the measured values, the commit, the seed (none: exact), the hardware
    fingerprint, the controls caught) and prints its hash; it never writes the gate record. The recording
    session, which did not implement the mechanisms, enters the row with `cargo run -p frob-conformance
    --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-03 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19), the one recorder the gate
    register names for GATE-AUGUR-03.

**Idempotency keys.** Every call of a run verb (`mission.synthesize`, `mission.refute`, `result.revalidate`,
`prove.bundle`) carries `--key "$(e2e_key <label>)"`, and no two calls the script compares share one: besides
the labels above, `syn-minu` and `syn-catalog` (steps 5a and 5b), `syn-late-game` and `syn-late-tree` (step 6),
`syn-retimed` (step 7, which must not return step 2's receipt, so its answer is recomputed and compared),
`syn-3gain-point`, `reval-3gain` and `syn-from-reval` (step 8), `bundle-two-gain`, `reuse-3gain`,
`reval-repair` (the repair command of step 9, run with the key appended by the script) and `reuse-2gain`
(step 9), and `bundle-<step>` (step 10). `mission.check`, `mission.show` and `prove.verify` are read verbs and
take none.

**Logging.** The script sources the shared helper of the end-to-end harness and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/mission_docking_family/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it.
Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`),
the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
identities, seed), measured values with units and their thresholds (inner 344, excluded 217, boundary 9, late
561, reading 15/16, open threshold 3/4 with gap 1/4, catalog minimum 7/8, error 1/65 m, withdrawn 327, kept
17), the cold and warm wall times of both lowerings, the withdrawal count with the first withdrawn cell and its
counterexample, the refusal event with code, pointer and repairs, digests where state is compared (answer and
bundle digests of the two lowerings; the 561-label digest beside N20b-13's committed one), the two stages' log
paths and summaries, and on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. A final
`summary` line gives counts of assertions passed and failed and the gates touched. The script is idempotent,
takes no seed (exact arithmetic) and exits non-zero on any failed assertion.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script-level self-tests (run with `--self-test`, no `frob` needed):

- `expected_counts_table`: the script's embedded expectations satisfy 344 + 217 = 561 and list nine boundary
  cells with 2·j = 32 + i.
- `log_schema`: a dry run emits lines that validate against the harness's log schema, including the summary.
- `exit_code_on_failure`: a forced failed assertion yields a non-zero exit and a `first_difference` field.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; its path, commands, assertions and log fields are given above.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-635 (on-time witnesses), KA-636 (late packet), KA-637 (family and grid; the strict comparator at
  U = 3/4, ε = 5/16 behind steps 5, 5a and 5b), KA-638 (third gain), KA-657 (scenario-tree agreement).
- Negative control 1, non-strict comparator: a build flag that evaluates the boundary with ≤ admits the nine
  boundary cells; the count assertion (344) must fail with 353.
- Negative control 2, clairvoyant evidence: a per-world command table submitted for the late-packet mission must
  be refused with `FROB-REF-881` (evidence for a different quantifier prefix).
- Negative control 3, stale screen: step 9's reuse must be refused; a build with the eligibility check disabled
  returns 344 positive labels for the three-gain mission and the assertion must fail.
- Negative control 4, reordered quantifiers: the bundle of step 2 with the prefix reordered and hashes made
  consistent is rejected by `frob prove verify`.
- Metamorphic: running the script with the grid enumerated in reverse order gives identical answer digests.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; two hosts; CLI and MCP.
