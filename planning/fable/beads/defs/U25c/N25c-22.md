## Background

GATE-AUGUR-03 (claim: Known answer; milestone M1; threshold TARGET): the docking family through the public
verbs: 561 of 561 cells, nine boundary cells excluded, all late-packet cells excluded, the equality reading
15/16 exported, the three-gain amendment withdraws every positive cell and reports 1/65 m.

"Through the public verbs" is the point. The mechanism beads (BD-129, N25c-20, N25c-21) have unit tests against
the same numbers; this bead proves that a caller with only the `frob` binary gets them, with an evidence block
on every answer and a bundle that an independent checker accepts. It is the M1 exit demonstration for the
mission chapter and the first slice an implementer can finish: two gains, two packet timings, one actuator
bound family, one observation-noise family; translate the query to a conventional scenario-tree linear program;
export the exact success region and the equality counterexample; check both outside the process; mutate the
packet timestamp and rerun. The compulsory interfaces it exercises are a causal history, a feasible controller,
two indistinguishable worlds, an exact physical parameter chart, and an independent checker. The verb also
returns what additional measurement or actuator authority would restore feasibility.

A gate passes only with a recorded measurement against its threshold and at least one caught negative control.

## Scope

In: the mission files of the fixture; the script; its assertions and logging; the gate record of GATE-AUGUR-03;
the planted negative controls. Out: the mechanisms (BD-129, N25c-20, N25c-21); the M1 exit demonstration of the
slice, which replays the worked example from a demo file, sends the rival's bundle through the same checker and
cites this gate's record (N25d-27, which depends on this bead); the docking rows of the known-answer battery
(N25d-37); the fresh-agent test of the slice (BD-140, GATE-AUGUR-07); checker independence and the mutation
corpus (BD-136); device runs.

## Technical approach

**Fixture files (checked in under `fixtures/missions/docking/`).**

- `docking.fmission`: the two-gain family on the grid U = 1/2 + i/32 (i = 0…16), ε = j/64 (j = 0…32), packet on
  time, exact target (1, 0), prefix exists policy, for all worlds, for all executions, evidence `exact`.
- `docking-late.fmission`: the same with the packet's arrival after the third command.
- `docking-point.fmission`: the single cell U = 3/4, ε = 5/16.
- `docking-3gain.edit`: the amendment that sets the hidden support to {1, 33/32, 2}.

**Steps.**

1. `frob mission check docking.fmission --json`: admitted; the quantifier order is echoed; record `MissionId`.
2. `frob mission synthesize docking.fmission --json`: assert 344 inner cells, 217 excluded cells, 0 unresolved;
   assert the nine boundary cells (i, j) = (0, 16), (2, 17), (4, 18), (6, 19), (8, 20), (10, 21), (12, 22),
   (14, 23), (16, 24) are excluded; assert `evidence.class = Certified`, `determinism = exact`, the three
   no-authorization flags `false`.
3. `frob mission show <answer> --set inner --page-size 50` and `--set excluded`: paged listing; the union has
   561 cells with disjoint interiors and stated boundary ownership (INV-AUGUR-02).
4. `frob mission synthesize docking.fmission --lowering scenario-tree --json`: the competing answer; assert
   cell-by-cell equality with step 2.
5. `frob mission refute docking-point.fmission --json`: assert the exported common reading 15/16, the two
   required second commands −1/4 and −3/4, the binding constraint (separation of the reading sets), and the
   repair `U > 3/4` with `attained: false`.
6. `frob mission synthesize docking-late.fmission --json`: assert 561 excluded cells, each with an `alias-pair`
   proof whose scope is "all admitted policies".
7. Mutate the packet timestamp back to on time (an edit of one field) and rerun: the answer of step 2 returns
   and the `MissionId` equals that of step 1.
8. Apply `docking-3gain.edit`; `frob mission synthesize` on the amended mission: assert "no exact docking",
   best worst-case error 1/65 m, and that every previously positive cell at ε ≥ 1/64 is withdrawn with its
   counterexample.
9. Attempt to reuse the two-gain screen on the three-gain mission: assert refusal `FROB-REF-854`, exit 3, first
   repair `mission.revalidate`.
10. `frob prove bundle` and `frob prove verify` for the answers of steps 2, 4, 5, 6 and 8: every bundle
    accepted; total checker time recorded.
11. Negative controls (below): each planted defect must flip at least one assertion.
12. Write the gate row for GATE-AUGUR-03 with the measured values, the commit, the seed (none: exact), the
    hardware fingerprint and the receipt hash.

**Logging.** The script sources the shared helper of the end-to-end harness and writes newline-delimited JSON to
`target/e2e-logs/mission_docking_family/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a human-readable transcript beside it.
Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`),
the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
identities, seed), measured values with units and their thresholds (inner 344, excluded 217, boundary 9, late
561, reading 15/16, error 1/65 m), digests where state is compared (answer and bundle digests of the two
lowerings), and on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. A final
`summary` line gives counts of assertions passed and failed and the gates touched. The script is idempotent,
takes no seed (exact arithmetic) and exits non-zero on any failed assertion.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script-level self-tests (run with `--self-test`, no `frob` needed):

- `expected_counts_table`: the script's embedded expectations satisfy 344 + 217 = 561 and list nine boundary
  cells with 2·j = 32 + i.
- `log_schema`: a dry run emits lines that validate against the harness's log schema, including the summary.
- `exit_code_on_failure`: a forced failed assertion yields a non-zero exit and a `first_difference` field.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; its path, commands, assertions and log fields are given above.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-635 (on-time witnesses), KA-636 (late packet), KA-637 (family and grid), KA-638 (third gain), KA-657
  (scenario-tree agreement).
- Negative control 1, non-strict comparator: a build flag that evaluates the boundary with ≤ admits the nine
  boundary cells; the count assertion (344) must fail with 353.
- Negative control 2, clairvoyant evidence: a per-world command table submitted for the late-packet mission must
  be refused with `FROB-REF-881` (evidence for a different quantifier prefix).
- Negative control 3, stale screen: step 9's reuse must be refused; a build with the eligibility check disabled
  returns 344 positive labels for the three-gain mission and the assertion must fail.
- Negative control 4, reordered quantifiers: the bundle of step 2 with the prefix reordered and hashes made
  consistent is rejected by `frob prove verify`.
- Metamorphic: running the script with the grid enumerated in reverse order gives identical answer digests.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; two hosts; CLI and MCP.
