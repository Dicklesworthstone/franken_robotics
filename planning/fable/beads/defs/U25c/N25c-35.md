## Background

Facility co-design and multi-robot handoff is a Moonshot with an unknown ceiling. Its kill gate and its stop
rule are the two controls that keep it from consuming the program.

KILL-AUGUR-FACILITY, decided at M8:

- **Trigger.** The acceptance fixture fails to discriminate two strategies or misses a seeded failure after one
  repair cycle.
- **Fallback.** Single-robot missions with priced world edits from the retrofit catalogue; multi-robot
  composition is parked.

The stop rule, which applies even when the kill gate does not fire: expansion to a multi-room shift is funded
only after the fixture discriminates at least two feasible engineering strategies and recovers each seeded
failure. If the fixture fails, the work reduces to the failed subtask, and until that subtask is revalidated its
uncertainty propagates through the handoff contract.

The delivery order states the same decision from the other side: if the slice's test fails, reduce to the failed
subtask. A kill gate that fires is not a failure of the program: the fallback is the plan, and the fallback here
is already useful (an editable world priced against an editable robot, for one robot).

## Scope

In: reading the gate row of GATE-AUGUR-24 and the pilot record; applying the trigger with its single repair
cycle; applying the stop rule; the ADR; re-reading dependents; stating what is funded next. Out: producing the
evidence (N25c-34, N25c-28); the multi-room commission itself (not yet split into beads).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** The gate row of GATE-AUGUR-24: number of feasible strategies discriminated, the list of seeded
   failures with "recovered" or "missed" and their withdrawals and minimized scenarios, the optimality gaps of
   unresolved subproblems; the count of repair cycles used; the pilot record.
2. **Trigger.** Fires when, after at most one repair cycle, fewer than two feasible strategies are
   discriminated, or any of the four seeded failures (protruding payload corner, common loss of traction, skewed
   handoff clock, supply change) is missed. A second repair cycle is not available: a second failure fires the
   gate.
3. **Stop rule.** If the trigger did not fire and all four failures were recovered and two strategies were
   discriminated, the multi-room shift may be proposed to the owner with its plan, pilot and envelope. Otherwise
   nothing beyond the fixture is funded.
4. **Reduction on failure.** Name the failed subtask (site compilation, composition, pricing, operation over
   time, counterfactual reporting). Until that subtask is revalidated, every answer that crosses a handoff
   carries its uncertainty: cells that depend on it are unresolved, with the subtask named in their explanation.
5. **Record.** An ADR with the measured values, the decision, the repair cycle's description, commit and date;
   written by a session that did not implement the mechanisms. `registries/gates.toml`: `pass` or
   `killed: fallback adopted`.

**If it fires.** The epic closes with the reason `fallback`. Multi-robot composition (N25c-30's several decision
makers and cross-robot ledgers) is parked behind its feature flag; `.fmission` keeps accepting one decision
maker. What stays: site compilation, the priced world-edit grammar for a single robot, counterfactual outputs
for a single robot, and the retrofit catalogue's own verb. The Moonshot slot is freed.

**If it does not fire.** The epic may continue to the flagship commission: a partial 3D survey of a stockroom, a
retail aisle and a commercial kitchen; a shared doorway and an unloading counter as bottlenecks; the
five-objective Pareto set; then the transfer tests (a dry warehouse loading bay, a furnished apartment, sloping
outdoor service terrain) with new geometry, measurements and holdouts. Each is split into beads at that point,
with its own pilot and acceptance, and each is described no more broadly than the fixture that qualified it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists, cites the gate row and the pilot's protocol hash, and states the trigger's evaluation with numbers and the repair cycle used.
- [ ] The KILL-AUGUR-FACILITY row of `registries/gates.toml` has its status and receipt.
- [ ] The stop rule's outcome is recorded: funded (with the owner's approval and envelope) or not.
- [ ] Dependents are re-read: feature flags and the decision-maker limit of `.fmission` match the decision.
- [ ] On failure, the failed subtask is named and affected cells carry it in their explanations.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `two_strategies_four_failures`: a record with 2 strategies and 4 of 4 recovered does not fire; the stop rule
  permits a proposal.
- `one_missed_failure`: 2 strategies, 3 of 4 recovered, one repair cycle already used: fires.
- `one_strategy`: 1 strategy, 4 of 4 recovered: fires.
- `repair_cycle_counted`: a record that claims a second repair cycle is treated as a fired trigger.
- `not_run_is_not_pass`: a gate row with status `not run` cannot produce `pass`.
- `trigger_text_unchanged`: the tool refuses if the trigger or fallback text differs from the register's.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/decide_kill_augur_facility.sh`: reads the records through `frob evidence trace`, evaluates the
trigger and the stop rule, prints the decision. NDJSON: one line per clause (strategies, each failure, repair
cycles) with measured values and verdicts; a summary naming the gate; non-zero exit when an input is missing or
its evidence closure dangles.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The synthetic records above have known verdicts.
- Negative control: a trigger amended after the fixture's protocol hash date is rejected.
- Negative control: a decision recorded by an implementing session is rejected by the close rule.

## Considerations

- An epic that has not passed its pilot within its planned weeks returns to the register of deferred ideas and
  frees its slot, independently of this gate.
- Funding per epic, and any envelope above the default, is the owner's decision.
- Priority is one step above the Moonshot default because the decision gates everything after the fixture.
