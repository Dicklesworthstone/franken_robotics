## Background

The geometric-phase epic proposes an inverse query: given a desired displacement, find the cheapest periodic
change of shape that produces it. The forward half (holonomy of a connection; curvature as a first-order
predictor) is established mathematics for principally kinematic systems. Whether the inverse query earns its
place against plain trajectory optimization is not known. The program requires a paper-scale pilot with a
falsifier before the work is funded.

- **Pilot.** The differential-drive loop and a three-link kinematic crawler on the CPU referee.
- **Falsifier.** The reversal residual or the chart-change residual exceeds tolerance, or trajectory
  optimization finds every proposed gait at lower total cost.
- **Ceiling.** Exact only for principally kinematic systems; for dynamic walking it is a diagnostic.

The pilot has two halves. The first checks that the mathematics, as it will be implemented, reproduces a closed
form and survives its two consistency tests (reverse the loop; change the chart). The second checks value: the
gaits the connection proposes must not all be found more cheaply by the comparison arm.

## Scope

In: the two fixtures; a prototype holonomy integrator and curvature proposer (standalone, small); referee
rollouts of the same loops; the reversal and chart-change residuals; the trajectory-optimization arm on the same
loops with its cost accounting; the falsifier verdict. Out: the product connection and its admission (N25c-43);
the inverse query (N25c-44); the gate (N25c-45); the kill decision and the record of the atlas's funding rule
(N25c-46); the earlier pilot of construction C-08 at M3 (N10a-16, record `PILOT-C-08`, a dependency): the
planar three-link floating body and the wheeled snake against direct collocation, judged on held-out loops by
rank agreement (Kendall τ at least 0.8) and a drift budget of 10%, which decided whether analysis A-09 is used
as a design oracle and took a first reading of the atlas condition. This pilot does not repeat that
comparison. It reuses that pilot's wheeled snake as fixture 2 (the same three segments of 0.3 m, a passive
wheel set at each midpoint, the same shape coordinates) and starts its prototype from that pilot's holonomy
arm; what it adds is the differential-drive closed form, the two residuals at their tolerances, the referee,
and the total-cost comparison.

## Technical approach

**Fixture 1: differential drive (closed form).** Two wheels of radius ρ at y = ±w, a free caster, ideal
rolling. Shape variables: the wheel angles (φ_L, φ_R). Body twist in axes x forward, y left:
v_x = ρ·(φ̇_L + φ̇_R)/2, v_y = 0, ω = ρ·(φ̇_R − φ̇_L)/(2w). The square loop (left wheel forward by δ, right wheel
forward by δ, left wheel back by δ, right wheel back by δ) is integrated as four exact pivots: about the right
wheel by −θ, about the left wheel by +θ, about the right wheel by +θ, about the left wheel by −θ, with
θ = ρ·δ/(2w). Result: zero net yaw, zero net advance, sideways displacement of magnitude 4w·(1 − cos θ), toward
−y for this loop order and toward +y for the reversed loop. Leading order: ρ²·δ²/(2w), the Lie bracket of the
two wheel fields.

**Fixture 2: three-link kinematic crawler** (the wheeled snake of N10a-16; segment length 0.3 m). Three rigid
links in a planar chain, each with a passive wheel set that forbids lateral slip at its midpoint; two joint
angles (α₁, α₂) are the shape. The three no-slip
constraints are Pfaffian: W_g(r)·ξ + W_s(r)·ṙ = 0 with W_g a 3 × 3 matrix. Where W_g is invertible the
connection is A(r) = W_g(r)⁻¹·W_s(r). Loops: circles of several radii around several centres in the (α₁, α₂)
plane, kept away from det W_g = 0.

**Measurements.**

1. *Closed form.* Fixture 1's integrated holonomy against 4w·(1 − cos θ) for a grid of (ρ, w, δ).
2. *Reversal residual.* For each loop γ, the composition of the holonomy of γ and of γ traversed backward must
   be the identity; the residual is its distance from the identity in metres (and radians).
3. *Chart-change residual.* Recompute the same physical gait with the body frame attached to a different link
   (crawler) or shifted along the axle (differential drive) and with re-parameterized shape coordinates;
   transform back; the residual is the difference of the predicted physical motion.
4. *Referee agreement.* The same loops executed by joint or wheel commands on the CPU referee with ideal-rolling
   contact; displacement per cycle against the integrated holonomy.
5. *Curvature as proposer.* For small loops, the first-order prediction −(D𝔸)·area against the integrated
   holonomy, with the loop doubled, reversed and enlarged until the approximation breaks; the breaking radius is
   recorded.
6. *Comparison arm.* Direct collocation and trajectory optimization over the same loop family, asked for the
   same displacement targets with zero net yaw, at the same actuator costs. Both arms are charged all their
   compute and engineering time. Scores: cycles per metre, energy, total cost to obtain each gait, and the time
   to recover a new gait after the wheel spacing is changed.

**Tolerances (registered before the run; the gate's TARGET values are the reference).** Closed form within 10⁻⁹
(relative, in exact-pivot arithmetic); reversal and chart-change residuals below 10⁻⁸ m.

**Comparison arm (DESIGN).** The constructive lane `refine` of the mission verbs (trajectory optimization with
design variables), `frob mission synthesize <fixture> --strategy refine --key ph-cmp-<i> --json`, charged its
whole compute; the same lane is the gate's comparison arm (N25c-45), so the pilot and the gate compare the same
two routes. N10a-16's collocation arm is not re-run.

N38a-09) already holds row 16 of the Augur pilot rows as two records, entered without a protocol (status

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `diff_drive_closed_form`: ρ = 0.05 m, w = 0.2 m, δ = 1 rad: θ = 0.125 rad; poses after the four pivots
  (0.0249349, −0.0015605, −0.125), (0.0498699, −0.0031209, 0), (0.0249349, −0.0046814, 0.125),
  (0, −0.0062419, 0); magnitude 0.0062419 m; leading order 0.00625 m.
- `reversed_loop_opposite`: the reversed loop ends at (0, +0.0062419, 0).
- `leading_order_ratio`: for δ → 0 the ratio of the exact value to ρ²·δ²/(2w) tends to 1 (at δ = 0.1 rad the
  ratio is 1 − θ²/12 to first order, with θ = 0.0125).
- `fixed_steering_no_holonomy`: a loop in which only one wheel moves forward and back returns to the start.
- `crawler_rank_drop`: at a shape where det W_g = 0 the prototype reports a rank drop and does not invert.
- `sign_control`: the curvature formula with the bracket term's sign flipped fails the small-loop comparison by
  an error of order one.
- `holonomy_pilot_registered_before_result` (in N38a-09's `crates/frob-conformance/tests/pilot_register.rs`):
  the result's date and ledger sequence follow the registration and its `protocol_blake3` equals the registered
  hash.
- `holonomy_gated_beads` (same file, scratch records): with 16a `falsifier-rejected` and 16b `registered`,
  `fundable_bead` answers `Fundable` for N25c-43 and `Blocked{registered}` for N25c-47; with 16a
  `falsifier-holds`, `Blocked{falsifier-holds}` for N25c-43.

on failure the loop and the first value out of tolerance; summary naming KILL-AUGUR-PHASE.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-649: the differential-drive loop. CX-030: the flipped bracket sign.
- Negative control: a lateral-slip mutation of the crawler's wheels (one wheel set allowed to slip); the
  kinematic prediction must disagree with the referee and the gait must be withdrawn, not kept.
- Negative control: a skid-steer base given to the prototype must be refused (its no-slip constraints force
  ω = 0), not assigned a yaw connection.
- Determinism: the prototype is deterministic; referee rollouts repeat with the same seeds.

## Considerations

- If trajectory optimization finds every proposed gait at lower total cost, the pilot is falsified even when the
  mathematics checks out: the fallback keeps A-09 as a forward diagnostic and parks the inverse query.
- Poor shape coordinates make curvature maps misleading; the pilot records results in the given coordinates and
  in minimum-perturbation coordinates when they are available.
- CPU only; a few minutes of compute.
