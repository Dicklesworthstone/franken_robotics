## Background

Locomotion by shape change is governed by a connection: a rule that says how much the body's pose moves for a
small change of shape. On a principal bundle of poses over shapes, with pose g ∈ SE(2) and shape r, the
reconstruction equation is g⁻¹·ġ = −A(r)·ṙ. The net motion of a closed shape loop is the holonomy of A.

The forward analysis A-09 already computes connection curvature for kinematic shape-changers. The inverse query
of the geometric-phase epic needs more from the connection than a curvature map: it needs to know where the
connection is valid at all. A connection belongs to one contact stratum. A loop in shape space that crosses slip
or lift-off cannot borrow its old connection. A base that turns by scrubbing has no no-slip connection for yaw.
This bead builds the connection as a qualified object: derived on a declared stratum, admitted by checks, and
refused outside its scope.

Tier: Frontier on kinematic fixtures. Part of the M6 epic; starts only after the pilot (N25c-42).

## Scope

In: derivation of A(r) from Pfaffian rolling constraints; the alternative of a learned connection and its
labelling; stratum admission (no-slip, fixed rank, quasi-static); chart and body-frame invariance as a test; the
refusals (rank drop, skid-steer, stratum crossing, rank-only displacement) under the code of
`connection-outside-stratum`; the connection object and its evidence; the design fixtures of its own end-to-end
script (`fixtures/designs/holonomy/`) and that script. Out: the curvature
formula and its sign law (the spine's specification bead and BD-243); the growth-vector analysis (BD-106); the
inverse query (N25c-44).

## Technical approach

**Derivation.** On a stratum with constraint rows W_g(r)·ξ + W_s(r)·ṙ = 0, where ξ = g⁻¹·ġ is the body twist
(v_x, v_y, ω) and rank W_g = dim G = 3, the connection is A(r) = W_g(r)⁻¹·W_s(r). For the differential drive
with wheels at y = ±w, radius ρ and shape (φ_L, φ_R): ξ = (ρ·(φ̇_L + φ̇_R)/2, 0, ρ·(φ̇_R − φ̇_L)/(2w)), so A is
constant and its curvature is the bracket term alone.

When the constraints do not determine ξ (rank W_g < 3) the system is not principally kinematic on that stratum:
the reconstruction needs the momentum term and this object does not apply.

**Learned connections.** Where the force model, not ideal rolling, determines the quasi-static motion, A may be
fitted from referee rollouts of small shape motions. A fitted connection is labelled `Tested`, carries its fit
residual and its operating region, and is never used for a `Certified` statement.

**Stratum admission (all three, checked and recorded).**

1. *No-slip.* The contact model on the stratum is ideal rolling, or the slip model has been separately qualified
   for it; a lateral-slip margin is evaluated on the referee over the operating region.
2. *Fixed rank.* rank W_g(r) = 3 with a conditioning margin over the declared shape region; the region is cut
   where the margin fails.
3. *Quasi-static.* The declared speed range keeps inertial terms below the registered fraction of contact
   forces; otherwise the connection is a diagnostic only.

**Connection object (DESIGN).**

```rust
pub struct Connection { design: DesignId, stratum: StratumId, shape: Vec<ShapeVar>, region: ShapeRegion,
                        source: ConnectionSource,              // Pfaffian | Fitted { residual, runs }
                        a: ConnectionKernel,                   // A(r) as a derived kernel with derivatives
                        admission: AdmissionRecord, class: EvidenceClass }
pub struct AdmissionRecord { no_slip: Margin, rank_margin: Margin, quasi_static: Margin }
```

**Chart invariance (a test, not an assumption).** A change of shape coordinates or of body frame changes the
representation of A. For any loop, the motion predicted in chart 1 and the motion predicted in chart 2 and
transformed back must agree as physical motions. The chart-change residual is reported in metres.

**Refusals.** All four are the refusal `connection-outside-stratum` (row 6 of N25e-45: family `1xx`, number
assigned by N35x-02's run at M0, carried by the constant
`frob_core::refusal::augur_late::CONNECTION_OUTSIDE_STRATUM`; exit 3, `retry: after-change`), told apart by
`detail`. The number exists before this bead lands, so no interim code is ever emitted (N25e-45). The fifth
kind of the row, `over-determined`, is BD-243's from M3; `rank-drop` is also raised by BD-243 from M3, and this
bead keeps that emit site's code and detail.

- *Skid-steer* (`detail: component-removed`). With fore and aft axles at ±a, exact lateral no-slip requires both
  v_y + a·ω = 0 and v_y − a·ω = 0, which forces ω = 0 for a > 0. The no-slip connection for such a base has no
  yaw component; a request that needs yaw from it is refused, pointer the removed component and the constraint
  pair, first repair "a separately qualified slip model".
- *Rank drop* (`detail: rank-drop`). At shapes where W_g loses rank the connection is undefined; requests there
  are refused, pointer the shape and the missing direction, first repair "restrict the loop to the admitted
  stratum, or choose shape variables and wheels whose rows give full rank". For the crawler this is every shape
  with α₁ = α₂: the links are chords of one circle and the three wheel axles meet at its centre.
- *Stratum crossing* (`detail: stratum-crossing`). A shape loop that crosses a contact-mode boundary (slip,
  lift-off) cannot use one connection; the request is split at the boundary and needs hybrid reset maps and
  fresh rank checks on each side, or it is refused, pointer the loop segment and the stratum predicate that fails
  there, first repair "split the loop at the mode boundary and supply reset maps with a fresh rank check on each
  side".
- *Bracket rank as reachability* (`detail: rank-only-displacement`, CX-027, CX-281). A request to certify a
  finite displacement from accessibility rank alone is refused: a Lie-bracket direction is infinitesimal and
  depends on drift and admissible inputs; first repair "request the integrated holonomy of a finite loop as the
  witness".

**Surface.** `analyze.curvature` (the A-09 verb of the `analyze` family, BD-243) reports the connection, its
admission record and its curvature; `frob mission synthesize` (N25c-44) consumes the object. The stratum is the
verb's `--stratum <rolling[:<wheel>,…]|flight>` input (default `rolling` for a design that declares wheel
constraints; the result echoes it). The verb's row lists `connection-outside-stratum` by the number the ledger
assigns, raised through `CONNECTION_OUTSIDE_STRATUM` with no interim, and its five kinds: `rank-drop` and
`over-determined` from M3 (BD-243), and `stratum-crossing`, `component-removed` and `rank-only-displacement` from
M6 (this bead). Evidence: `Certified` for Pfaffian
connections on exact kinematic fixtures (the derived kernel carries an instance certificate); `Tested` for
fitted ones.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `diff_drive_connection`: A's columns map φ̇_L and φ̇_R to (ρ/2, 0, −ρ/(2w)) and (ρ/2, 0, +ρ/(2w)) (up to the
  sign convention of the reconstruction equation).
- `bracket_gives_sideways`: the bracket of the two wheel fields is a pure lateral translation of magnitude
  ρ²/(2w) per unit shape area.
- `skid_steer_refused` (KA-650, KA-063): axles at ±a; the constraint pair forces ω = 0; a yaw request is refused.
- `rank_drop_refused`: the crawler (segments 0.3 m) at α₁ = α₂ = 0.3 rad has det W_g = 0 (the three axles meet
  at one point) and is refused with `detail: rank-drop`; at (0.3, −0.3) rad det W_g ≠ 0 and the connection is
  issued.
- `refusal_rows`: each of the four kinds is emitted with the constant's code, exit 3, `retry: after-change`, its
  `detail`, its pointer and the first repair named above; no code other than the constant's is reachable from
  these emit sites.
- `chart_change_invariance`: body frame moved from the axle centre to a point 0.1 m forward; the predicted
  physical motion of the square loop is unchanged within 10⁻⁸ m.
- `stratum_crossing_split`: a loop that lifts one wheel midway is not integrated with one connection.
- `one_sided_input` (KA-651, CX-281): ẋ = u with u ∈ [0, 1]: rank one, and negative x is refused as a certified
  target with `detail: rank-only-displacement`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/analyze_connection.sh`, owned by this bead and built on its dependencies only (header `# e2e:
bead=N25c-43 milestone=M6 labels=augur,frontier gates=GATE-AUGUR-20 seed=43 needs=cpu
plants=bracket-sign,skidsteer-admitted`); N25c-45's gate script runs it as a stage. Design fixtures (DESIGN
names, under `fixtures/designs/holonomy/`, delivered here): `diffdrive-r05-w20` (ρ = 0.05 m, w = 0.2 m),
`crawler3-l03` (posture α₁ = 0.3, α₂ = −0.3 rad), `crawler3-l03-arc` (posture α₁ = α₂ = 0.3 rad),
`skidsteer-a015` (axles at ±0.15 m). `analyze.curvature` is a `read` verb, so no call carries a key. Steps:

1. `admit-diffdrive`: `frob analyze curvature diffdrive-r05-w20 --shape wheel_l,wheel_r --loop rect:1,1 --json`,
   exit 0: the admission record's three margins, source Pfaffian, class `Certified`, the constant connection,
   the loop's ordered-product displacement within 10⁻⁹ (relative) of (0, −0.0062419 m, 0) beside the
   leading-order 0.00625 m, and the chart-change residual below 10⁻⁸ m.
2. `admit-crawler`: `frob analyze curvature crawler3-l03 --shape alpha1,alpha2 --loop rect:0.05,0.05 --json`,
   exit 0: admission record, chart-change residual below 10⁻⁸ m with the body frame on another link.
3. `refuse-rank-drop`: the same call on `crawler3-l03-arc`: exit 3, the constant's code, `detail: rank-drop`,
   pointer the shape (0.3, 0.3) rad.
4. `refuse-skidsteer`: `frob analyze curvature skidsteer-a015 --shape wheels_l,wheels_r --loop rect:1,1 --json`:
   exit 3, `detail: component-removed`, the constraint pair v_y ± a·ω = 0 in the pointer, first repair "a
   separately qualified slip model"; no yaw connection in the output.

Every step runs on the default stratum `rolling` (all four fixtures declare wheel constraints), and each
asserts that the result echoes `rolling`. The stratum-crossing and rank-only kinds are reached through the unit
tests (`stratum_crossing_split`, `one_sided_input`) and through N25c-44's query (its lift-off and one-sided
fixtures). It sources the STD-E2E
helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/analyze_connection/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command,
exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities); the three
margins; source and class; displacements and residuals against their tolerances; per refusal its code, `detail`,
pointer and first repair; output digests; on failure the first value out of tolerance and the fixture path;
`summary` with assertions passed and failed. Negative controls: a build with the bracket sign flipped fails
step 1 by an error of order one; a build that admits the skid-steer connection fails step 4.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-649 (through the connection), KA-650 (skid-steer: ω = 0), KA-651 (one-sided input).
- CX-028: ideal rolling applied to a skid-steer base; under the shortcut the base cannot yaw at all; the
  negative control is admitting the connection for it.
- CX-030: the curvature with +[𝔸ᵢ, 𝔸ⱼ]; must fail the loop test by an error of order one.
- Negative control: a mecanum base with misaligned rollers; the curvature check passes and must not be reported
  as a layout test (that defect is a rank drop reported by the growth vector).
- Determinism: derived kernels are deterministic; exact fixtures give identical bytes across hosts.

## Considerations

- Curvature in poor coordinates misleads; minimum-perturbation coordinates are computed for the connection
  before any curvature map is shown as displacement per cycle.
- The refusal `connection-outside-stratum` is row 6 of N25e-45 (message, pointer, the five `detail` kinds,
  ranked repairs, exit 3); its number is assigned by N35x-02's allocation run at M0, before this bead lands, and
  is read only through the constant. An emit site that spells a number, or falls back to a generic
  model-admission code, fails N25e-45's `sixth_row_loads` check.
- Exists only while the epic is alive (KILL-AUGUR-PHASE); the forward diagnostic A-09 survives either way.
