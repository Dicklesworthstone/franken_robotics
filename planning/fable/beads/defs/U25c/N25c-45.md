## Background

GATE-AUGUR-20 (claim: Known answer; milestone M6; threshold TARGET): holonomy: differential-drive loop within
10⁻⁹ of 4w·(1 − cos θ); loop-reversal and chart-change residuals below 10⁻⁸ m; the skid-steer and slip mutations
refused or withdrawn; one newly proposed gait admitted by a full referee rollout.

The gate has four clauses, and each guards a different way the geometric-phase query could be wrong. The closed
form checks the group integration. The two residuals check that the result is a physical statement and not an
artifact of loop direction or coordinates. The mutations check the limits that are part of the specification: a
connection must not be issued for a base that turns by scrubbing, and a gait must be withdrawn when the wheels
actually slip. The last clause checks value: at least one gait that the query proposed, which was not supplied
to it, is confirmed by an independent full rollout.

This bead also runs the plan's reference cases and the comparison arm, and records the scores that the kill
decision reads.

## Scope

In: the reference cases; the script, its assertions and logging; the comparison arm's run and scores; the gate
row. Out: the mechanisms (N25c-43, N25c-44); the pilot's prototype (N25c-42); the kill decision (N25c-46).

## Technical approach

**Reference cases.**

1. A fixed steering configuration (no holonomy): a loop that moves one input out and back along the same path
   encloses no area and returns the body to its start.
2. A loop and its reverse: holonomies are group inverses; the composition is the identity.
3. A joint-axis reflection: mirroring the mechanism and the loop mirrors the displacement (lateral component
   changes sign, forward component is kept, yaw changes sign).
4. A lateral-slip counterexample that must withdraw a gait: the same loop on wheels with a finite lateral
   friction limit low enough to slip; the kinematic prediction and the rollout disagree and the gait is
   withdrawn.

**Fixtures.** The design fixtures `diffdrive-r05-w20`, `crawler3-l03` and `skidsteer-a015` are N25c-43's;
`gait-diffdrive.fmission` (ρ = 0.05 m, w = 0.2 m, sideways target, zero yaw), `gait-crawler.fmission` (the
three-link kinematic crawler, a forward target) and `gait-skidsteer.fmission` (a fixed-axis four-wheel base
asked for yaw without slip) are N25c-44's. This bead adds the slip variants of the first two (a finite lateral
friction limit low enough to slip), the mirrored variants of reference case 3, and `diffdrive-r05-w20-fwd` (the
same design with its body frame 0.1 m forward).

**Steps.**

0. Stages: `scripts/e2e/analyze_connection.sh` (N25c-43) and `scripts/e2e/mission_holonomy_query.sh` (N25c-44)
   run first (KNOWN_DRIFTS 11); their `summary` lines must report no failed assertion, and their log paths are
   cited in this log.
1. `frob analyze curvature diffdrive-r05-w20 --shape wheel_l,wheel_r --loop rect:1,1 --json` (the A-09 verb;
   `read`, no key) on the differential drive: assert the admission record (no-slip, rank, quasi-static) and the
   constant connection.
2. Closed form: the same call with `--loop rect:<δ>,<δ>` for δ ∈ {0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 6} rad, reading the loop's
   ordered-product displacement; assert each sideways displacement is within 10⁻⁹ (relative) of 4w·(1 − cos θ),
   with zero net yaw and advance (for δ = 1 rad: 0.0062419 m).
3. Reversal: for every loop of steps 2 and 5, compose with the reversed loop; assert the residual is below
   10⁻⁸ m (and the yaw residual below the registered angle tolerance).
4. Chart change: repeat step 2 on `diffdrive-r05-w20-fwd` (body frame moved 0.1 m forward), transform the
   displacement back to the axle centre, and assert the difference is below 10⁻⁸ m; assert the chart-change
   residual the connection reports for shape coordinates (φ_L + φ_R, φ_R − φ_L) is below 10⁻⁸ m. Repeat on the
   crawler with the body frame on another link.
5. `frob mission synthesize gait-diffdrive.fmission --key hol-syn-1 --json` and `…gait-crawler.fmission`
   (`hol-syn-2`): record the proposed gaits; assert zero net yaw, the target within tolerance, the clearance
   verdict.
6. Full referee rollout of one newly proposed gait: the answer's `rollout` (the replay address of the full
   referee rollout that N25c-44 step 6 ran, ideal-rolling contact on the event-located CPU tier) is re-executed
   from its address with `frob sim replay --run <r> --world <w> --capture t2 --key hol-roll-1 --json` and must
   reproduce bitwise; assert the net displacement measured from the replayed trace meets the target within the
   query's tolerance. "Newly proposed" means the loop was produced by the query, not supplied in the fixture
   file.
7. Skid-steer mutation: `frob mission synthesize gait-skidsteer.fmission --key hol-syn-3 --json`: assert
   refusal or `NoneExists` with the constraint pair v_y ± a·ω = 0 as the reason; assert no yaw connection is
   issued.
8. Slip mutation: run the gait of step 6 on the slip variant; assert the rollout misses the target and that the
   studio withdraws the gait (a logged withdrawal naming the slip event), and that the kinematic claim is
   retired for that operating region.
9. Reference cases 1 and 3.
10. Comparison arm: direct collocation and trajectory optimization over the same loops and targets at the same
    actuator costs, through the constructive lane `refine` (trajectory optimization with design variables):
    `frob mission synthesize <fixture> --strategy refine --key hol-cmp-<i> --json` (DESIGN: the product lane,
    so that the kill decision compares two product paths; the pilot's standalone program is not reused).
    Record for both arms:
    cycles per metre, the computed group displacement, the ordered-cycle reversal residual, operating-region
    coverage, energy, total cost per gait found, and the time to recover a new gait after changing the wheel
    spacing from w = 0.2 m to 0.25 m.
11. Write the gate receipt (the four clauses with measured values, the comparison arm's scores, commit, seeds,
    hardware fingerprint) and print its hash; the script runs no `record_gate` and never writes the gate record
    (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9). The recording session, which did not implement the mechanisms, enters the row with `cargo
    run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-20 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19, the gate
    recorder, KNOWN_DRIFTS 1).

**Logging.** Header `# e2e: bead=N25c-45 milestone=M6 labels=augur,frontier,gate gates=GATE-AUGUR-20 seed=20
needs=cpu plants=bracket-sign,skidsteer-admitted,curvature-integral,keep-slipped-gait`. Every repeat that a
determinism lane compares carries its own key. The script sources the shared end-to-end helper and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/mission_holonomy/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier,
determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds (closed-form error against 10⁻⁹;
residuals against 10⁻⁸ m; rollout displacement against the target tolerance), digests of answers and rollouts,
and on failure the first differing value and artifact paths. The summary line counts assertions and names
GATE-AUGUR-20.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script self-tests (`--self-test`): `closed_form_table` (the five expected values: θ = ρ·δ/(2w) and
4w·(1 − cos θ) for δ = 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 6 rad, namely 6.2499×10⁻⁵, 1.5620×10⁻³, 6.2419×10⁻³, 2.4870×10⁻²,
2.1465×10⁻¹ m); `log_schema` (a dry run validates); `newly_proposed_check` (a loop identical to one in the
fixture file is rejected as not newly proposed).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; path, commands, assertions and log fields are above.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-649 (the loop), KA-650 (skid-steer: ω = 0), KA-651 (one-sided input) as a self-check of the refusal path.
- Negative control 1 (CX-030): a build with the bracket sign flipped in the curvature; the small-loop proposals
  point the wrong way and step 5's target assertion fails by an error of order one.
- Negative control 2 (CX-028): a build that admits the no-slip connection for the skid-steer base; step 7 fails.
- Negative control 3: a build that integrates large loops by the curvature integral instead of the ordered
  product; step 2 fails at δ = 6 rad (0.225 m against 0.21465 m).
- Negative control 4: a build that keeps the gait after the slip mutation; step 8 fails.
- Determinism lanes: steps 1 to 5 twice on one machine and across worker counts with identical bytes; rollouts
  under the referee's class with seeds explicit.
