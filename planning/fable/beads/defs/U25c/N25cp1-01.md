## Background

The facility epic has two stages in the mission chapter's delivery order (slice 5, M8): return an engineer's
Pareto set for the two-carrier brownfield handoff, then commission the multi-room shift. The first stage is the
acceptance fixture (N25c-34) and its decision (N25c-35). The second is the flagship query of §25.17:

> An engineer provides a partial 3D survey of a stockroom, a retail aisle and a commercial kitchen; a catalog of
> body modules; permissible room changes; pack temperatures; and delivery windows. A shared doorway and an
> unloading counter are the bottlenecks. The query asks for a Pareto set over body cost, facility-change cost,
> energy per delivered item, worst admitted delivery time, and route availability after one module or charger
> drops out. The answer can be one modified carrier, two simpler robots with a handoff, a small threshold
> retrofit, or a finding that every allowed alternative leaves a mission unmet.

The stop rule governs it: expansion to a multi-room shift is funded only after the fixture discriminates at least
two feasible engineering strategies and recovers each seeded failure. When N25c-35 records that the stop rule
passed, the multi-room shift "may be proposed to the owner with its plan, pilot and envelope". The program's rule
for a Moonshot is that a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier runs before any of its work is funded. This bead is
that proposal: the short plan, the paper-scale pilot of the three-room shift, and the owner's recorded decision.
It was split out because the epic carried the commission as "not yet split", so no bead held it and the feature
could be lost.

## Scope

In: the short plan of the multi-room shift (scope, pilot, falsifier, ceiling, kill gate, acceptance story,
compute envelope); the paper-scale instance of the three-room commission, frozen in a hashed protocol; exact
enumeration of its discrete schedules with tabulated continuous subproblems (no product code); the falsifier
verdict; the growth measurement from the one-door fixture to three rooms; the proposal to the owner and the
recorded decision. Out: the commission through the public verbs (N25cp1-02); the three transfer tests
(N25cp1-03); the fixture, its gate and the stop rule (N25c-28, N25c-34, N25c-35); every product mechanism
(N25c-29 to N25c-33).

## Technical approach

**Precondition.** The bead starts only if the KILL-AUGUR-FACILITY row of `registries/gates.toml` carries the
decision outcome `not-fired` and the stop-rule section of N25c-35's ADR
(`docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-FACILITY-M8.md`) records the stop rule as passed. If the outcome is `fired` or
`not-reached` (N40-03's closed set; `deferred` is not available at M8), or the stop rule did not pass, this bead
closes with that ADR's reason and nothing below runs.

**The short plan (written first, hashed).** Scope (three rooms, two bottlenecks, the five objectives); the pilot
below; the falsifier; the ceiling (unknown, stated as such); the kill gate it inherits (KILL-AUGUR-FACILITY's
reduction rule); the acceptance story of N25cp1-02 and N25cp1-03; the compute envelope, with any excess over the
default marked for the owner's approval.

**The paper instance (DESIGN, synthetic, frozen in the protocol; values recorded there).** It extends the
fixture of N25c-28 to three rooms and keeps its constants where they overlap:

- Rooms: a stockroom with a cold room, a retail aisle, a commercial kitchen. One shared doorway (clear width
  0.62 m, the fixture's door) between the stockroom and the corridor; one unloading counter in the kitchen; a
  12 mm sill at the kitchen entrance.
- Robots: carrier C1 (loaded width 0.66 m, wheels cross at most 10 mm) and carrier C2 (loaded width 0.50 m,
  wheels cross 20 mm), as in the fixture, with C2's payload limited to one pack.
- Deliveries per slot: one chilled pack to the kitchen counter and one ambient tote to the aisle.
- Pack temperatures (DESIGN, fail-closed form): each pack class has a maximum time outside the cold room; the
  chilled class is 90 s. The atom is a time atom on the schedule and needs no thermal model; a payload thermal
  owner model, when one exists, may replace it with a time-at-temperature interval read as N25c-32 reads every
  owner model.
- Delivery windows: the chilled pack within 120 s and the tote within 150 s of the slot start.
- Legs on a dry floor: cold room to doorway 10 s; doorway to kitchen counter 40 s; doorway to aisle 30 s; kitchen
  to aisle 25 s; a handoff at the doorway 15 s. Wet floor: legs × 6/5, the handoff unchanged.
- Edits: the fixture's narrow bracket (body cost 30, loaded width 0.60 m) and widened door (facility cost 50); the
  ramp strip over the sill (facility cost 40) and larger wheels (body cost 120), the prices of N25c-31's sill
  fixture.

**Method at paper scale.** Enumerate the discrete schedules (which robot carries which item, in what order,
through which bottleneck intervals); evaluate each against the tabulated legs for both material states; mark a
schedule unresolved when a subproblem is declared timed out; for every excluded strategy record its minimal
obstruction; report the nondominated set over (body cost, facility cost, worst delivery time) with energy and
drop-one availability as tabulated values.

**Measurements.** Discrete schedules enumerated; strategies discriminated (feasible and nondominated); which of
the four outcome forms the instance yields; wall time; the growth factor of the schedule count from the
one-door fixture (N25c-28) to this instance.

**Falsifier.** The enumeration cannot discriminate two feasible strategies on the three-room instance, or the
schedule count exceeds the envelope so that the product search could not be run within it.

**Proposal and decision.** The short plan, the pilot record and the envelope go to the owner; the decision
(funded, with envelope, or not) is recorded beside N25c-35's ADR. Funding of an epic and any envelope above the
default are the owner's (N25d-49 sets the order and envelope of Augur's Moonshot slices under the cap of four).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The short plan and the protocol (instance, legs, edits, prices, falsifier) are hashed before any run.
- [ ] The enumeration reproduces the derived answers below exactly and reports the nondominated strategies with their obstructions.
- [ ] The falsifier verdict, the schedule count, the growth factor and the wall time are written to the review log.
- [ ] The owner's decision (funded with envelope, or not) is on record; N25cp1-02 cannot start without a funded record.
- [ ] If N25c-35 did not pass the stop rule, this bead is closed with that reason and nothing else in it ran.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `chilled_pack_atom` (derived here): direct by C1, 10 + 40 = 50 s dry and 60 s wet; relay through the doorway,
  10 + 15 + 40 = 65 s dry and 12 + 15 + 48 = 75 s wet: both within 90 s. A relay that waits 30 s at the shared
  counter in the wet state reaches 105 s and is excluded with the pack atom as its obstruction.
- `kitchen_sill`: C1 (10 mm) excluded at the 12 mm sill; with the ramp strip admitted (40) or larger wheels (120);
  C2 (20 mm) crosses.
- `doorway_shared`: two robots in the doorway in overlapping half-open intervals refused; touching intervals
  compose.
- `windows`: a schedule that delivers the chilled pack at 125 s is excluded by the 120 s window.
- `outcome_forms`: the protocol lists brief variants (permitted edits, robots present, material states), each
  with its outcome form computed by hand and recorded before the run, at least one per form (one modified
  carrier; two robots with a handoff; a threshold retrofit; an unmet finding); the enumeration returns each
  recorded form. Fixed case: every edit forbidden and C2 absent is unmet, with the obstruction C1's loaded width
  0.66 m against the 0.62 m door and the least relaxation named.
- `timeout_is_unresolved`: a subproblem declared timed out leaves its schedule unresolved, never excluded.
- `protocol_frozen`: a change to any constant after the hash refuses the run.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_facility_shift_paper.sh`: runs the standalone enumeration with `python3 -B` (standard library
only) or its Rust equivalent, compares the output with the pinned expected file, prints the falsifier verdict
and the growth factor. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/pilot_facility_shift_paper/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line carries `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code and wall time; `metric` lines for the schedule count, the
strategies discriminated, each strategy's (body cost, facility cost, worst time) and obstruction, the growth
factor; the protocol hash and the expected file's digest; on failure the first differing strategy or count; a
summary naming KILL-AUGUR-FACILITY as informed.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: the derived cases above; the fixture's own table (N25c-28) reproduced as the one-door
  sub-instance.
- Negative control: an enumeration that treats the doorway as two independent resources admits a schedule with
  both robots in it at once and must be caught.
- Negative control: an additive peak rule used as a prune removes a feasible schedule and must be flagged.
- Determinism: exact integer and rational arithmetic; identical output on every host.

## Considerations

- Ceiling: unknown. The growth factor is the first number that says whether the product search can scale to a
  shift; a factor that exceeds the envelope is itself the falsifier.
- The instance is synthetic and states no site; a real survey enters only in N25cp1-02, with its privacy mark.
- If the falsifier holds, the work reduces to the failed subtask under N25c-35's reduction rule and the
  commission is not funded.
- Counts toward the cap of four active Moonshot items while it runs.
