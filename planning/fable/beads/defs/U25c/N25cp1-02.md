## Background

The flagship query of the facility epic is a brownfield logistics commission: a partial 3D survey of a
stockroom, a retail aisle and a commercial kitchen; a catalog of body modules; permissible room changes; pack
temperatures; and delivery windows, with a shared doorway and an unloading counter as the bottlenecks. The answer
is a Pareto set over body cost, facility-change cost, energy per delivered item, worst admitted delivery time,
and route availability after one module or charger drops out, and it can take one of four forms: one modified
carrier, two simpler robots with a handoff, a small threshold retrofit, or a finding that every allowed
alternative leaves a mission unmet.

The slice's test in the delivery order states what must be shown: a modified site, a modified body and a relay
policy are compared under one mission; withheld evidence overturns or supports a named prediction; speed and
physical claims are reported separately. The decision if it fails: reduce to the failed subtask; until it is
revalidated its uncertainty propagates through the handoff contract.

The mechanisms exist (N25c-29 to N25c-33) and were qualified on the two-carrier fixture (N25c-34). This bead runs
them on the commission, after the stop rule passed (N25c-35) and the owner funded the shift on the pilot's record
(N25cp1-01). It was split out because the epic carried the commission as "not yet split" and no bead held it.

## Scope

In: the commission brief as checked-in files (site, catalogue, permitted room changes, pack classes, delivery
windows), frozen and hashed; the pack-temperature atoms in the mission; the Pareto query with all four
counterfactual outputs; drop-one availability for every module and charger; named predictions registered before
withheld evidence is opened; the comparator arm; the end-to-end script and its record. Out: the mechanisms
(N25c-29 to N25c-33); the fleet scene (N25cx-01); decoupled stepping for large rosters (N16c-20); the transfer
tests (N25cp1-03); real-place ingestion (BD-398, used through N25c-29).

## Technical approach

**Precondition.** N25cp1-01 records the owner's decision as funded, with its envelope. Otherwise this bead closes
with that reason.

**The brief (checked in under `fixtures/missions/facility-shift/`, hashed before any run).**

- Site: the three-room site of the pilot, authored as a scene (all dimensions marked as authored), compiled by
  N25c-29 into a world family; where the owner supplies a real partial survey instead, it is ingested through
  N25c-29's path, carries its privacy mark, stays outside the repository, and nothing from it is logged.
- Fleet: a fleet scene (N25cx-01) with the robots the strategies need, one decision maker per robot.
- Catalogue: body modules and the retrofit catalogue's kits (BD-369); permitted room changes as the brief's edit
  permissions (N25c-31).
- Pack classes (DESIGN, fail-closed): each class has a maximum time outside the cold room, a time atom on the
  schedule; where a versioned payload thermal owner model exists, its time-at-temperature interval replaces the
  bound and enters the dependency support (the rule of N25c-32). The commission never infers a pack temperature
  itself.
- Delivery windows per destination; two material states (dry, wet); the operating-state block of N25c-32 with
  the hot restart and the charger-loss replay.

**Steps of the commission.** Every run and mutate call (`scene.compile`, `mission.new`, `mission.synthesize`,
`prove.bundle`, `mission.revalidate`, `eval.qualify`, and the edits of step 7) carries its own idempotency key (`--key sh-<step>-<n>`);
the drop-one re-answers of step 4 carry one distinct key each, so none returns another's stored receipt.

1. `frob scene compile <site> --name facility-shift --key sh-1-1 --json`, `frob mission new --from-scene
   s:facility-shift --key sh-1-2 --json`, `frob mission check q:<id> --json`: the brief admitted; the
   `MissionId` recorded.
2. Register the named predictions before any answer is read: for each strategy the pilot found nondominated, the
   predicted worst delivery time interval and the predicted residual quantity; for each withheld item (a held-back
   doorway width measurement, the kitchen's wet-floor friction card, a sealed world cluster of the blinded lane
   BD-259) the outcome it is predicted to support. Registration is a record with a timestamp before the reveal.
3. `frob mission synthesize q:<id> --key sh-3-1 --json` with `objective.claim: pareto` over the five objectives:
   strategies with objective bounds; the outcome form; a disposition for every omitted option; minimal
   obstructions; per-budget repairs with gaps; the smallest survey that settles each tie (a request through
   `scene.probe-request`, N19c-05); an acceptance mission per survivor.
4. Drop-one availability: for every module and every charger in turn, re-answer with it removed; record which
   strategies remain available.
5. `frob prove bundle a:<answer> --out <scratch>/bundle --key sh-5-1 --json` and `frob prove verify
   <scratch>/bundle --json` on the answer.
6. Dispatch each survivor's acceptance mission to the simulated qualification lane (the blinded lane of BD-259,
   which returns a decision and nothing per world), in the row's mission-bound form: `frob eval qualify <survivor
   design> --mission q:<acceptance mission> --key sh-6-<n> --json`. Assert that the prediction's ledger sequence
   precedes the receipt's and that the receipt's decision is the one the registered interval implies (an
   interval wholly inside the requirement predicts acceptance, one wholly outside predicts rejection, one that
   straddles predicts neither and is recorded as untested by this lane).
7. Reveal the withheld evidence one item at a time. The doorway measurement and the wet-floor friction card each
   enter as one edit, followed by `frob mission revalidate a:<answer> --after-edit e:<reveal> --key sh-7-<n>
   --json` on the latest answer (the row takes exactly one change); the sealed cluster's outcome is the receipt
   of step 6 and is read, not revalidated. Record every registered prediction as supported or overturned with
   the evidence that decided it.
8. Comparator arm: the neutral export of N25c-31 (same actions, costs, mission) given to conventional
   mixed-integer scheduling, motion-planning and robust-optimization tools with the same sub-solvers; record its
   strategies, exclusions and time.
9. Report speed and physical claims separately: verdicts and their evidence classes in one table, wall times and
   the comparator's times in another. No reality claim is made.

**Reduction on failure.** If a step fails, name the failed subtask (site compilation, composition, pricing,
operation over time, counterfactual reporting); cells that depend on it are reported unresolved with the subtask
in their explanation, and the record says so.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The brief is checked in (or, for a real survey, held outside the repository with its mark) and its hash predates every run.
- [ ] The answer states its outcome form; each strategy's five objective values are bounds; every omitted option has a disposition; with an unresolved option the claim is `pareto-candidates`.
- [ ] Pack-temperature atoms are in the mission and in each survivor's dependency support; the pack fixture below excludes the schedule it must.
- [ ] Drop-one availability is recorded for every module and charger.
- [ ] Every registered prediction predates the reveal and is recorded as supported or overturned.
- [ ] The comparator arm ran on the same export; speed and physical claims are reported separately.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/mission_facility_shift.sh` passes with the logging below; the recording session did not implement the mechanisms.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script self-tests (`--self-test`, no `frob` needed) and checks on the checked-in brief:

- `brief_hash_stable`: the brief's files hash to the recorded value; a changed window or pack limit changes it.
- `pack_atom_fixture` (the pilot's derived case): the relay that waits 30 s at the counter in the wet state reaches
  105 s against the chilled class's 90 s and must be excluded with the pack atom as obstruction; the direct and
  plain relay schedules (60 s and 75 s wet) are not excluded by it.
- `prediction_before_reveal`: a prediction whose timestamp follows the reveal of its evidence is rejected.
- `drop_one_complete`: the availability table has one row per module and per charger.
- `log_schema`: dry-run lines validate against the STD-E2E schema, summary present.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_facility_shift.sh`: first runs `scripts/e2e/mission_facility_fixture.sh` (N25c-34) as a
self-check and stops if it fails; then steps 1 to 9 above. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/mission_facility_shift/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line carries `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class,
tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` lines for each strategy's five objective bounds, worst delivery
time against each window, pack exposure against each class limit, the drop-one availability table, the
comparator's results and times; each prediction with its registration time, its evidence and its verdict;
digests of the brief, answers, bundles and minimized scenarios; on failure the first differing value and the
artifact paths; a summary line with assertions passed and failed. No private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: the fixture's table through the self-check; the pilot's derived cases.
- Negative control: a mutant that drops the pack atoms admits the waiting relay and must be caught.
- Negative control: a weights-only Pareto mutant omits an unsupported nondominated strategy and must fail the
  disposition check.
- Negative control: a prediction edited after its reveal is rejected.
- Negative control: a doorway treated as per-room rather than shared admits two robots in it at once; the
  composition check must refuse.
- Determinism lanes: exact parts identical twice on one machine and across worker counts; referee replays under
  the referee's class.

## Considerations

- Ceiling: unknown. The commission qualifies exactly the three rooms, two bottlenecks and pack classes it
  contains; no release cell is described more broadly.
- Compute: the envelope recorded by N25cp1-01; a run beyond it is not shrunk but scheduled under an approved
  budget.
- A real survey is private data; answers derived from it inherit its mark (N37-17).
- KILL-AUGUR-FACILITY's reduction rule applies to every failure here.
