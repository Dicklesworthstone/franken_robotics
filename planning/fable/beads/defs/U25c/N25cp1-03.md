## Background

The flagship query of the facility epic names three transfer tests: a dry warehouse loading bay, a furnished
apartment and sloping outdoor service terrain, "with new geometry, measurements and holdouts". They ask whether
the facility method, frozen after the multi-room commission (N25cp1-02), answers correctly on places it was not
developed on. Each site changes something the commission did not exercise: the loading bay has one material
state and a dock gap; the apartment has movable furniture, rugs and narrow doorways; the outdoor terrain has
slope in the gravity frame, mixed surfaces and changing light.

The rule that governs every claim here: a release cell is described no more broadly than the fixture that
qualified it. A transfer test that passes widens the qualified set by exactly that site; one that fails names
its failed subtask. This bead was split out because the epic carried the transfer tests as "not yet split" and no
bead held them.

## Scope

In: the three sites (new geometry, new measured cards, holdouts sealed before the run); the method frozen at the
commission's commit and parameters; registered predictions per site; the reveal of holdouts; the per-site record;
the end-to-end script. Out: the commission (N25cp1-02); the mechanisms (N25c-29 to N25c-33); ingestion
(BD-398); the blinded lane (BD-259); measurement requests (N19c-05).

## Technical approach

**Precondition.** N25cp1-02 has a recorded run. A site is run only with the method frozen: the commit, the
compiler and search parameters and the catalogue version of that run. A change to any of them between sites
refuses the run.

**Sites (each either a real place supplied by the owner and ingested through BD-398 with its privacy mark, or an
externally authored synthetic site that development has not read).**

| Site | What is new | Material states | Holdouts sealed before the run |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dry warehouse loading bay | dock leveller gap and plate, pallet-jack traffic as a shared occupancy resource | one (dry) | a measured gap width; a sealed world cluster of traffic timings |
| Furnished apartment | movable furniture with admitted placements, rugs as friction patches with edges, narrow doorways | dry, plus a rug edge | measured doorway widths; a furniture placement drawn after the answer |
| Sloping outdoor service terrain | slope in the gravity frame, gravel and grass patches, lighting as a latent state | dry and wet | a measured slope; wet-grass friction card; a sealed world cluster of lighting |

Holdouts are sealed with the blinded lane's sealed clusters (BD-259) or, for a measurement, held by a session
that does not run the query, and opened only after the site's predictions are registered.

**Per site.**

1. Compile the site (N25c-29) and write the mission (the commission's deliveries adapted to the site's
   destinations and windows, recorded in the protocol before the run): `frob scene compile <site> --name
   tr-<site> --key tr-<site>-1 --json`, `frob mission new --from-scene s:tr-<site> --key tr-<site>-1b --json`,
   `frob mission check q:<id> --json`.
2. Register the predictions: the outcome form, each survivor's worst delivery time interval, the residuals and
   the outcome each holdout is predicted to support.
3. `frob mission synthesize q:<site query> --key tr-<site>-3 --json` with the claim `pareto`; `frob prove bundle
   a:<answer> --out <scratch>/<site> --key tr-<site>-3b --json` and `frob prove verify <scratch>/<site> --json`.
4. Open the holdouts one at a time. A measurement, a card or a furniture placement drawn after the answer enters
   as one edit, followed by `frob mission revalidate a:<answer> --after-edit e:<holdout> --key tr-<site>-4-<n>
   --json` (one distinct key per holdout). A sealed world cluster (traffic timings, lighting) is never opened
   into the query: each survivor is qualified on it through the blinded lane, `frob eval qualify <survivor
   design> --mission q:<site query> --key tr-<site>-4q-<n> --json`, whose receipt carries a decision and nothing
   per world. Record each prediction as supported or overturned.
5. Record the site: qualified (every prediction supported, every certificate accepted) or failed with its failed
   subtask named.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All three sites ran with the method frozen at the commission's commit and parameters; a changed parameter is refused.
- [ ] Every holdout was sealed before, and opened after, the site's registered predictions.
- [ ] Each site's record lists every prediction as supported or overturned, with its evidence.
- [ ] A failed site names its failed subtask; a passed site widens the qualified set by that site only.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/mission_facility_transfer.sh` passes with the logging below; no private-scene data is logged or stored in the repository.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `method_frozen`: a run with a parameter different from the commission's is refused, naming the parameter.
- `holdout_after_registration`: an answer computed after a holdout was opened is rejected for that site; a
  sealed-cluster prediction whose ledger sequence follows its qualification receipt is rejected.
- `dock_gap` (synthetic): a 25 mm leveller gap against wheels that cross at most 20 mm is excluded with the gap as
  obstruction; a 15 mm gap is admitted.
- `slope_braking` (synthetic, derived here): braking at 1.5 m/s² downhill on a 6° slope needs
  μ ≥ (1.5 + 9.81·sin 6°)/(9.81·cos 6°) = 2.5254/9.7563 = 0.2589; on a flat floor 1.5/9.81 = 0.1529. With the
  friction card [0.25, 0.35] the flat case is inner and the slope case is unresolved, with friction as the
  residual at threshold 0.2589 and one measurement request.
- `movable_furniture`: a chair with three admitted placements; a route is certified only if it is clear for all
  three; a route clear for two is unresolved, never inner.
- `rug_edge_patch`: a rug edge is a friction patch boundary and a height step; a route crossing it carries both
  in its dependency support.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_facility_transfer.sh --site <loading-bay|apartment|outdoor>` (or all three in order): for
each site the five steps above. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/mission_facility_transfer/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line carries `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block; the frozen
commit and parameter digest; each prediction with its registration time, the holdout that decided it and its
verdict; residuals with thresholds; digests of site models, answers and bundles; on failure the first differing
value and the artifact paths; a summary line per site and overall. No private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: the derived slope, dock-gap and furniture cases above.
- Negative control: a compiler that ignores slope (gravity frame dropped) declares the slope case inner and must
  be caught.
- Negative control: a holdout opened before registration invalidates that site's record.
- Negative control: furniture fixed at one placement certifies a route that another placement blocks.
- Determinism: exact parts identical twice on one machine; referee replays under the referee's class.

## Considerations

- A failed transfer test is a finding, not a defect of the test: it names what the commission did not cover.
- Real places are private data; every derived answer inherits the mark (N37-17) and stays outside the repository.
- KILL-AUGUR-FACILITY's reduction rule applies: a failed subtask is revalidated before any answer that crosses
  it is reported as more than unresolved.
- Compute: within the envelope recorded by N25cp1-01.
