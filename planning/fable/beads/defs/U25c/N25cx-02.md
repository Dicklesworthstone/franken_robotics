## Background

`experiment.suggest` is the studio's answer to "what should I find out next": given competing designs,
unresolved cells of a mission answer or competing explanations, it returns information-gathering proposals,
each with a testable prediction and a cost. It is advisory and never decides validity (architecture decision
ADR-ATEL-07). Its full form, proposals ranked by decision value per unit cost with a stop rule, is a
fourth-milestone (M4) capability, and so is the generator that synthesizes new probes.

Two things in the plan need a form of the verb one milestone earlier.

- The milestone list for M3 names, among mission queries under amendment, "**the expert grid behind
  `frob experiment suggest`**". The expert grid is a programmed, hand-declared list of candidate experiments;
  it is also the named fallback of the kill gate on the probe generator (KILL-AUGUR-PROBE, decided at M3:
  if the generator never beats the programmed expert plus direct measurement, `experiment.suggest` ships
  the expert grid and the direct measurement).
- The agent diagnosis battery (GATE-TETHER-15, M3) hands a fresh agent thirty wrong-setup tasks. Five are
  *inconclusive*: the evidence supplied cannot decide between two causes, and the only correct answer is
  `unknown`, **with the experiment from `experiment.suggest` that would decide**. One invented cause on an
  inconclusive task fails the arm. An agent cannot give that answer if the verb does not exist until M4.

The beads that carry the verb (its proposal records on the mission side, its ranking on the studio side)
are both M4. This bead is the M3 form: the verb restricted to the expert grid, ordered by cost, with the
direct test always present. It adds no new mathematics; the selection rule is the one `mission.distinguish`
already uses at M3 on hand-declared candidates. Tier: Solid (a lookup with a soundness rule), by contrast
with the Frontier generator and ranking.

Terms. A *cause* is a named explanation of an observed failure. A *reading set* is the closed set of values
an experiment may return under one cause, over its nuisance ranges and measurement error. An experiment
*separates* two causes when their reading sets are disjoint. The *direct test* measures the target itself at
mission conditions; it is the stronger comparator and is always offered.

Why the direct test is never optional (the plan's normative lesson): a probe generated for three admitted
material laws chose the right gripper pad in 675 of 675 in-class cases and was wrong in 225 of 225 cases of a
withheld fourth law whose early trace is identical; measuring the candidate pad at mission dwell was right in
900 of 900. An in-class-perfect experiment cannot expose a cause outside the class, so every proposal states
the class it is conditional on.

Rejected alternatives: letting the battery's agent compose its own discriminating experiment (that is the
skill the M4 generator automates; at M3 the battery tests restraint, not invention); shipping the M4 verb
early without ranking (the unranked list of every possible action is noise; the grid is curated);
answering "the cause is X" when existing evidence already separates (the verb would be deciding validity).

## Scope

In: the grid registry and its row format; the M3 cause vocabulary; prediction evaluation against supplied
evidence; the separation test; ordering by cost; the direct test; the no-separator answer; the verb's M3
surface and schema (a subset of the M4 proposal record); refusals; fixtures for the five inconclusive tasks
and the pad fixture.

Out: synthesis of new probes and the experiment grammar (the M4 probe compiler); ranking by value of
information and the STOP entry (BD-351); proposal kinds `split-cell` and `rerun-higher-tier` driven by cell
explanations (N25c-18, which extends this bead's record); registration of predictions before a run (BD-344;
at M3 a proposal carries its prediction and nothing is registered); the battery, its sealed key and scorer
(N32b-37).

## Technical approach

Layout (DESIGN): `registries/experiments/expert-grid.toml`; `crates/frob-mission/src/suggest/grid.rs`;
verb handler `crates/frob-verbs/src/verbs/experiment_suggest.rs`.

**1. Grid row.**

```toml
[[experiment]]
id        = "analytic-friction-vs-tread-cap"
kind      = "analysis"                    # "analysis" | "check" | "sim" | "referee" | "probe" | "direct"
command   = "frob task fixture {fixture} --layer analytic --rows friction-demand,tread-force-cap --key {key} --json"
reads     = ["friction_demand_ok", "tread_force_cap_ok"]
cost      = { sims = 0, wall = "seconds" }
[[experiment.predicts]]
cause = "insufficient-friction"; reading = { friction_demand_ok = false, tread_force_cap_ok = true }
[[experiment.predicts]]
cause = "low-current-limit";     reading = { friction_demand_ok = true,  tread_force_cap_ok = false }
```

A reading is categorical (a set of allowed values per observable) or an interval with its error bound. A
row lists every cause it has a prediction for; it says nothing about the others. A row whose command is a run
verb carries the placeholder `{key}`, which the caller fills with an idempotency key of its own when it runs
the proposal; the verb never invents one.

**2. Cause vocabulary at M3** (closed; DESIGN, taken from the battery's classes): `units-error`,
`insufficient-friction`, `low-current-limit`, `visual-collision-mismatch`, `delayed-observations`; and, for a
mission answer, the mechanism identifiers of its mechanism set (material laws and the like).

**3. The initial grid** (the minimal evidence the battery's own table names, one row each, plus
confirmations):

| Row | Reads | Separates |
|---|---|---|
| Mass roll-up against the declared budget (`design.check`) | Budget ratio | `units-error` from each other cause |
| Friction-demand and tread-force-cap rows of the analytic layer | Two booleans | `insufficient-friction` from `low-current-limit` |
| One `sim.run` with the stall reason read from telemetry | `traction-limited` or `current-limited` | The same pair, when both analytic rows are marginal |
| Collision-to-visual deviation of `scene.accept` | Deviation against the pack threshold | `visual-collision-mismatch` from each other cause |
| `sense.causal-test` and the sample-age field | Pass or fail; age against the decision deadline | `delayed-observations` from each other cause |
| Hand-declared pad probes (the candidates of `mission.distinguish`) | Normalized load at (r, t) | Material mechanisms |
| Direct test: apply the typed repair of one cause and re-run the failing task on the same addresses | Requirement verdict | Any pair (cost: at least one run per cause) |

**4. Selection** (the rule of `mission.distinguish`, on grid rows).
1. Inputs: the causes (`--between a b [c…]`, or the mechanism set of the mission answer given as the
   positional `a:…`, the row's form), the evidence already held (`--evidence` identifiers: check reports, runs,
   verdicts), a budget.
2. For each row with a prediction for at least two of the causes, instantiate its reading sets on the
   fixture named by the evidence (nuisance ranges and error bounds included).
3. If the evidence already contains a row's reading and it lies in exactly one cause's set, the proposal
   list is empty with `status: "existing-evidence-separates"` and a pointer to that evidence. The verb does
   not name the cause.
4. Keep rows whose reading sets are pairwise disjoint for the causes given; order by (`sims`, wall class,
   the row's cost in seconds where it states one). Rows of equal cost are ordered by the tie-break of
   `mission.distinguish` (BD-291): the largest gap between the nearest reading sets by default, the smallest
   radius under `--tie-break smallest-radius`; row id breaks any remaining tie. The first is the suggestion;
   the rest follow. `--tie-break largest-gap|smallest-radius` (default `largest-gap`) is this bead's option, the
   CLI form of BD-291's mission field `candidates.tie_break` (`"smallest:radius"`), which `--between` has no
   mission to carry; it is on no row of VERBS-experiment yet and is requested of it. With a positional answer
   whose mission sets `candidates.tie_break`, giving `--tie-break` as well is 802 when the two differ.
5. Append the direct test, always, with its cost.
6. If no row separates: `status: "no-separating-experiment-in-grid"`, the direct test alone, and for each
   pair the two overlapping reading sets as the witness that the grid cannot decide.
7. A cause outside the vocabulary is refused (FROB-REF-802, arguments fail the schema), listing the
   vocabulary.

**5. Record** (the M4 proposal record with fewer kinds; field names identical so that M4 only adds):
`{ kind, action, command, cost, predictions: [(cause, reading set)], relevance: {between}, condition:
[causes assumed], ranking: "cost-only" }`. Every list carries the label `unranked-by-value`.

**6. Surface.** `frob experiment suggest --between insufficient-friction low-current-limit --evidence
v:1c0e… --json`, or `frob experiment suggest a:93f0… --grid expert --json`. Kind `read`: it runs nothing.
Registry entry: the row of VERBS-experiment, a dependency, which registers `experiment.suggest` at M3 (owner
`frob-mission`) with `first_milestone` kept at M4; from M3 `frob capabilities` lists it as implemented with
`form: "expert-grid"`, and this bead's handler serves that form. No `forms` field exists in the entry schema
and this bead adds none (NJL-03's rule for early forms). The ranked form answers `FROB-REF-724` naming the form
until BD-351 lands at M4. Citing a proposal as a verdict is refused downstream with FROB-REF-872.
Determinism D0. Evidence class of the envelope: that of the evidence read; proposals are advisory.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For each of the five inconclusive battery tasks (development copies), the verb returns as first
      proposal the experiment the answer key names, with disjoint predictions for the two seeded causes.
- [ ] On the pad fixture with mechanisms V, P and C the cheapest separating probes are at 125 ms (cost
      0.375 s): r = 2 with reading sets [0.8737, 0.9362] for V and C and [0.9520, 0.9982] for P (gap 0.0158,
      the probe the pilot recorded) and r = 3 with [0.9674, 1.0102] for P (gap 0.0312). The grid returns
      r = 3 under the default largest-gap tie-break and r = 2 under `--tie-break smallest-radius`, and the
      direct test at 1.25 s. With the withheld law D added to the admitted set, no 125 ms probe separates (the
      reading sets of P and D coincide for every exposure up to a quarter of the time constant) and the first
      proposal moves to an exposure of 0.5 s (cost 0.75 s), where r = 3/2, 2 and 3 separate P from the rest
      with gaps 0.0129, 0.0662 and 0.1090: r = 3 by default, r = 3/2 under smallest radius (at r = 2 the sets
      are [0.8737, 0.9362] for P, [0.6858, 0.8074] for D and [0.6305, 0.7351] for V and C), still ahead of
      the direct test.
- [ ] Every non-empty answer contains the direct test; every proposal lists the causes it is conditional on.
- [ ] No output field names a cause as the conclusion; with separating evidence already present the status
      is `existing-evidence-separates` and the list is empty.
- [ ] Order is by cost: with the analytic row and the one-simulation row both separating, the analytic row
      is first.
- [ ] A cause outside the vocabulary is refused with FROB-REF-802 and the vocabulary; a downstream citation
      of a proposal as a verdict is refused with FROB-REF-872.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob --json` and the MCP tool `experiment_suggest`; one envelope; the list is
      identical on two runs; `frob capabilities` lists the verb with `form: "expert-grid"` and the ranked call
      (`--study`) answers `FROB-REF-724` naming the form.
- [ ] Planted defects are each caught: `overlapping-sets-accepted`; `direct-test-omitted`;
      `order-by-row-id`; `names-the-cause`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-mission --test suggest_grid`:

- `grid_loads_and_rows_have_two_predictions`.
- `friction_vs_current_analytic_row_first`: fixture 1 with friction below the creep threshold of the lip and
  an adequate driver → the analytic row separates; cost 0 simulations.
- `marginal_rows_fall_to_sim`: both analytic rows marginal (inside their error bounds) → the `sim.run` row
  with stall reasons `traction-limited` against `current-limited` is first.
- `units_error_by_mass_rollup`: a 0.24 m link entered as 240 with metres declared → budget ratio set
  disjoint from every other cause's.
- `delayed_observation_by_causal_test`.
- `pad_expert_grid_r2_125ms`: reading sets as above; nearest ends 0.0158 apart at r = 2 and 0.0312 at
  r = 3; the default tie-break returns r = 3, `smallest-radius` returns r = 2.
- `pad_law_d_moves_to_half_second`: with D admitted, the first proposal is at 0.5 s: r = 3 by default,
  r = 3/2 under `smallest-radius`.
- `pad_law_d_not_separated_from_p_early`: at r = 2, t = 125 ms the sets of P and D coincide.
- `existing_evidence_separates_returns_empty`.
- `no_separator_returns_witness_sets`.
- `direct_test_always_last_and_present`.
- `unknown_cause_refused_802`; `record_fields_subset_of_m4_schema`.
- `tie_break_conflicts_with_mission_802`: a positional answer whose mission sets `candidates.tie_break =
  "smallest:radius"` with `--tie-break largest-gap` is 802; with `--tie-break smallest-radius` it answers r = 2.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/experiment_suggest_grid.sh --seed <s>`: for each of the five development copies of the
inconclusive tasks: (1) run the commands that produce the task's supplied evidence (`frob design check
<design> --json`, `frob task fixture <fixture> --layer analytic --key es-<run>-<task>-ev --json`); (2) `frob
experiment suggest --between <a> <b> --evidence … --json`; (3) assert the first proposal's row id, the disjoint
predictions and the presence of the direct test; (4) execute the proposal's `command` with `{key}` filled as
`es-<run>-<task>-x` and assert that its reading falls in exactly one predicted set (the seeded one, known to the
script, not to the verb); then the pad fixture: `frob mission distinguish <pad fixture> --key es-<run>-pad
--json` (BD-291) for the answer, and `frob experiment suggest a:<pad answer> --grid expert --json` twice, the
second with `--tie-break smallest-radius`, asserting r = 3 and r = 2; then the two refusals, the 724 of the
ranked form, and `frob capabilities --json` for the `expert-grid` form. NDJSON per STD-E2E to `target/e2e-logs/experiment_suggest_grid/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a
transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence
block, the causes, each proposal's row id, cost and reading sets, the reading obtained and the set it fell
in, and on failure the first proposal whose sets overlap or the missing direct test. Sealed battery tasks
are never used: development copies only. The `summary` line lists
`gates: ["GATE-TETHER-15", "GATE-AUGUR-11"]` as touched; neither is recorded here.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The pad table: archived trace alone refuses all 225 cases per law; the programmed expert on V, P, C is
  correct in 225 of 225 for each and wrong in 225 of 225 on D, at 0.375 s; the direct measurement is correct
  in 900 of 900 at 1.25 s.
- The battery's minimal-evidence table (wrong units: the mass roll-up; insufficient friction: the
  friction-demand row, no rollout; low current limit: the tread-force cap row and one `sim.run`; hidden
  mismatch: the collision-to-visual deviation; delayed observations: `sense.causal-test` and sample age).
- Negative controls: the four planted defects.
- Determinism: twice on one machine, debug and release; the list is D0.

## Considerations

- The grid is curated and small. A pair of causes with no row is answered honestly
  (`no-separating-experiment-in-grid` and the direct test); adding a row is data entry with a fixture.
- The cause vocabulary is closed at M3 so that the verb cannot be used to launder a free-text guess into a
  "cause"; M4 replaces it with mechanism sets attached to cells.
- If KILL-AUGUR-PROBE fires, this bead's form is what `experiment.suggest` remains for probes; the M4 beads
  then add ranking and the other proposal kinds on top of it.
- The battery's sealed answer key must not be readable through this verb: the grid holds predictions per
  cause class, never per task.
