## (e) Contradictions, gaps and what was done

1. **Three-law probe: r = 2 or r = 3.** §25.14 records the probe generated for {V, P, C} as r = 2 at 125 ms
   (nearest ends 0.0158 apart). Recomputing the candidate grid shows r = 3 at 125 ms also separates, at the same
   cost, with gap 0.0312. §25.38 adopts "among probes of equal cost choose the largest gap", which returns r = 3.
   Both score 675 of 675 in class and 0 of 225 on the withheld law. BD-291 and N25c-13 assert the candidate list
   and both tie-breaks; the gate's counts are unaffected.
2. **"32 distinct inequalities" of the projected stance form.** There are 48 raw rows; 32 after removing rows
   repeated across load vertices within one generator pair (the plan's count); 28 after also merging four
   capacity-free rows that coincide across pairs. N25c-10 states the rule under which it counts 32.
3. **"The three-gain amendment withdraws every positive cell."** On the 561-cell grid the alias between gains 1
   and 33/32 holds for every probe only when ε ≥ 1/64. The 17 cells with ε = 0 keep exact docking; 327 of the 344
   admitted cells are withdrawn. N25c-21 reports per cell and N25c-22 asserts the withdrawal for ε ≥ 1/64.
4. **The 225 cases per law** are described (25 pairs of q and τ, three probe errors, three archived errors) and
   not listed. N25c-13 uses a 5 × 5 grid with errors {−0.02, 0, +0.02}, which reproduces every count of §25.14 and
   §25.38; the reproduction bundle's list governs if it differs.
5. **No verb is named for the dominance query (§25.16).** BD-359 routes it through `mission.refute` with a
   baseline pair, marked as a DESIGN default to be confirmed in the M4 verb registry.
6. **No verb or refusal code is named for the inverse holonomy query and its admission (§25.19).** N25c-44 uses
   `mission.synthesize` and `mission.refute`; N25c-43 uses the generic model-admission refusal until a code is
   assigned.
7. **`scene.probe-request` is M5 in the verb registry, while seed BD-291 (M3) names it.** The request record is
   emitted inside unresolved mission answers from M3 (BD-291, N25c-18); the read verb is N25c-19 at M5.
8. **`experiment.suggest` has three homes**: registered by §25.3.1 (unit U25a's subsection), specified in §25.14
   (this unit), ranked in §28.16 (BD-351, unit U28a). N25c-18 carries the Augur side (proposal records,
   per-mechanism predictions, decision relevance, the direct measurement).
9. **`mission.revalidate` is M4 in the verb registry but appears in M1 amendment examples.** The M1 scripts of
   this unit (N25c-11, N25c-22) compare answers cell by cell and switch to the verb when it lands.
10. **Seed done-conditions "KILL-… decided" on mechanism seeds (BD-291, BD-359, BD-363).** The decision needs the
    mechanism's results, so it is a separate bead that depends on the seed (N25c-16, N25c-27, N25c-40); each seed's
    acceptance criteria say so. For BD-359 and the Moonshot mechanisms the paper-scale pilot is a prerequisite of
    the mechanism bead.
