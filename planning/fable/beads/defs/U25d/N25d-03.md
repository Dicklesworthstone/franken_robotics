## Background

GATE-AUGUR-21 (claim: Known answer; milestone M6; TARGET): the symmetry–event identity holds to finite-difference
error on the mirrored landing and fails by a margin of at least 10 times that error on each of the four asymmetric
mutations. The identity is D h(x⁺)·S_e(x⁻) = S_{h·e}(h·x⁻)·D h(x⁻) for a body symmetry h, a transversal contact
event e and its saltation matrix S (the first-order map across the event including the event-time shift). The
check that evaluates it is N25d-02. This bead supplies the fixture the gate is measured on, the mutations that must
make it fail, the three further cases the rules require, and the script that records the gate.

A gate that nothing can fail validates nothing, so the mutations are registered controls: each must be caught in
the same run in which the gate passes. A fixture is complete before its first run: every mass, constant, seed and
rule is fixed at registration and a later change is a new fixture identity.

## Scope

In: `fixture:mirrored-landing` and its variants as files under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/augur/`; the
derivation note; the script; the gate record. Out: the check (N25d-02); the pilot (N25d-01); the GPU amortization
experiment (N25d-04); the kill decision (N25d-05).

## Technical approach

**The fixture (synthetic; values are DESIGN choices of this bead, frozen at registration).**
- Body: a rigid trunk carrying a tray, mass 12 kg, a uniform box 0.50 m (x, forward) × 0.30 m (y, left) × 0.10 m
  (z, up). Four legs with hips at (±0.20, ±0.12, −0.05) m; each leg is one prismatic joint along the leg axis,
  nominal length 0.30 m, lower-leg mass 0.5 kg, a point foot of radius 0.02 m, an ankle load cell, and an active
  damper: force −k·(ℓ − ℓ₀) − c·ℓ̇ with k = 8,000 N/m and c = 120 N·s/m, where the damping term uses the ankle's
  own load packet sampled at 1 kHz and delivered after 1 ms.
- Ground: flat and rigid on the CPU referee with located events. One sole law per foot (friction 0.8, plastic
  normal impact); left and right sole laws are separate parameters with equal values.
- Initial state: at rest, lowest foot 0.05 m above ground, roll 2° and pitch 3°, so the four touchdowns are
  isolated and their order is recorded at registration.
- The mirror h: reflection in the sagittal plane, P = diag(1, −1, 1); it maps each left leg, contact, load packet
  and sole law to its right counterpart. The mirrored landing starts from h applied to the initial state, on the
  same model: the image event is evaluated with the image foot's own sole law, which in the base fixture equals
  the source foot's.
- Observables: tray roll (rad) and the four ankle loads (N), read 0.20 s after the last touchdown.
- Perturbation: a one-sided change of the first-landing leg's length rate before its touchdown.

**The four mutations (each breaks the symmetry of exactly one thing).**

| Id | Mutation | What breaks |
|---|---|---|
| M-payload | A 1.0 kg point mass fixed to the tray at (0, +0.10, +0.05) m | Mass distribution |
| M-latency | The left ankle packets are delivered after 2 ms, the right after 1 ms | The information path |
| M-graze | The first-landing foot gains a toe point 0.06 m ahead of the foot that grazes the ground during the landing (transversality number below the threshold) | Event regularity |
| M-simul | The same-side rear leg is shortened so that it touches down at the same instant as the first foot, a pair not tied by the mirror | Event order |

For M-graze and M-simul the check routes by its rules (unsupported at grazing; ambiguous order). The gate still
needs a margin on each mutation, so for these two the script also evaluates the residual with one-sided relocated
differences on the supported side and requires it to exceed 10 times the error estimate; the routed status is
logged beside it.

**Three rule cases (pass cases with stated expectations).**
- S-slide: friction 0.2 on all feet so that each impact slides; the identity holds between mirrored sliding
  impacts, and the normal-to-tangential cross term of S is non-zero and present in the returned matrix.
- S-stick: the invariant sticking event: zero roll, so a left–right pair touches down together and sticks; the
  mirror maps the event to itself and S commutes with D h.
- S-pronk: zero roll and zero pitch: all four feet together; admitted as one coupled impact, not refused.

**Measurement.** For each case the script obtains from the check the residual norm ρ, the error estimate η, the
status and the per-observable shares. Pass conditions: symmetric fixture ρ ≤ η; each mutation ρ ≥ 10·η;
S-slide, S-stick, S-pronk status `Holds` with the stated properties.

**Derivation note.** `docs/derivations/augur-mirrored-landing.md` states the model, the mirror's signed
permutation on every state and channel, the expected sign pattern of D h, and the error budget (step sizes ε and
ε/2, round-off bound of the referee in double precision).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The fixture, its eight variants (four mutations, three rule cases, and the unequal-sole control variant)
      and the derivation note are committed; the fixture identity (hash) is printed by `frob task fixture`.
- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-21 is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with measured ρ, η and ρ/η per case, the commit, the
      hardware fingerprint and the seed, by a session that did not implement N25d-02.
- [ ] Each of the four mutations is caught with margin at least 10 in the same run.
- [ ] Two mutant builds are caught: one that carries the source foot's sole law onto the image foot (on the
      variant with unequal sole laws, friction 0.8 left and 0.5 right, the correct build reports `Broken` and the
      mutant reports `Holds`); and one that uses fixed-time differences in place of relocated ones.
- [ ] S-slide returns a matrix whose cross term matches relocated differences; S-pronk is not refused.
- [ ] Re-running the script gives identical digests and an identical gate record (referee, exact class).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/augur_mirrored_landing.rs`:
- `fixture_is_mirror_symmetric`: the symmetry analysis returns the mirror for the base fixture and not for
  M-payload.
- `event_order_is_registered`: the touchdown order of the base fixture equals the registered order; the mirrored
  landing has the mirrored order.
- `mutations_change_one_thing`: each mutation's design or task diff against the base has exactly one entry.
- `latency_mutation_changes_packets_only`: M-latency leaves `ModelId` unchanged and changes the observation
  contract digest.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/augur_symmetry_at_contact.sh`:
1. `frob task fixture fixture:mirrored-landing --variant <v> --json` for each variant;
2. `frob sim run --tier referee --fixture … --seed 1 --json` for the landing and its mirror image;
3. `frob analyze symmetry <design> --symmetry-events <run> --json`;
4. asserts as above.
Log (`target/e2e-logs/augur_symmetry_at_contact/<utc>.ndjson`): per step the command, exit code, wall time and
evidence block; per case `metric` lines for ρ, η, ρ/η with thresholds 1 and 10, the transversality number, the
status, the touchdown order and the state digests; `refusal` lines for `FROB-REF-263`; on failure the first
differing observable and the replay addresses; a `summary` with the gate touched.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-094 and CX-042 as the hand case behind the gate. Controls: the four mutations and the two mutant builds.
Metamorphic relation: applying the mirror twice returns the base landing bit for bit. Determinism lanes: twice
on one machine; debug and release; the referee only (no device tier is claimed by this gate).

## Considerations

- The gate text asks for a failing margin on all four mutations, while the rules route two of them; the script
  records both the route and a one-sided residual so that neither reading is lost.
- The closed-loop damper is there because a latency mutation is meaningless on a passive body.
- If the pilot's falsifier held, this bead is not started and the epic goes to its kill decision.
