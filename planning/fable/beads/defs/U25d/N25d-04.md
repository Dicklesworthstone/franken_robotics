## Background

The symmetry–event check (N25d-02) decides, at one contact event, whether a body symmetry h commutes with the
event: D h(x⁺)·S_e(x⁻) = S_{h·e}(h·x⁻)·D h(x⁻), with S the saltation matrix across the event. When it holds, a
derivative computed at one event can be transported to its mirror image. The Moonshot question of the epic is
whether that buys time where it matters: **does checking the symmetry mask and the event map once per design
family reduce the time to decide a design amendment on either GPU family?**

"Symmetry mask": for each member of a family batch, which group elements are symmetries of that member.
"Event map": for each event class of the family, which events correspond under each group element and whether the
identity holds there. A family whose members share topology and symmetry can share one mask and one map, checked
once, and then compute tangent work for one representative of each orbit of events.

The ceiling is known before any run: at most the group order (2 for a mirror, 4 for a fore-aft and left-right
symmetric quadruped) on the tangent work of symmetric events, and tangent work is only a fraction of the time to a
decision. That fraction is measured first. The general bound for any partial speed-up applies: if a fraction f of
matched wall time is improvable by a factor r and the method adds relative overhead h, the speed-up is at most
1 / (1 − f + f/r + h). With r = 4 and f = 0.2 the ceiling is 1.18 before overhead. This is why the item is
Moonshot, position 10 in the Moonshot queue, and why its kill gate names "no reduction" as a trigger.

## Scope

In: the measurement of f on the strong baseline; the amortized arm; the challenger arm; the paired comparison; the
retirement rule for bifurcating modes; the record. Out: the check itself (N25d-02); the gate fixture (N25d-03);
gradients through contact as a mechanism (the separate epic on step adjoints); the kill decision (N25d-05).

## Technical approach

**Step 0, the ceiling (blocking).** On the strong baseline, take the phase breakdown of one amended-mission
decision that uses event derivatives (a gait-gradient step inside a design campaign on a symmetric quadruped
family): tangent work on symmetric events, other tangent work, forward simulation, checking, transfers, authoring.
Record f (the share of tangent work on symmetric events), r (the group order of the family) and the predicted
overhead h of building the mask and map. The kill gate asks for a reduction in decision time on at least one GPU
family, that is a paired lower bound above 1 against the stronger of the two other arms (DESIGN reading of
"reduction": against both the challenger B the chapter names and the fallback C that ships otherwise, so per
device the deciding number is the smaller of the lower bounds of B/A and C/A). If 1 / (1 − f + f/r + h) is at or below 1, or below whatever larger
ratio the experiment registers as its claim, the experiment is refused by the ceiling-before-speed check and the
kill gate is decided on that record alone.

**Arms (equal sealed costs; the same family batch, seeds and addresses).**
- A, amortized: mask and event map computed once per family; tangent work for orbit representatives only;
  transported derivatives elsewhere, each under a licence from the check.
- B, challenger: an event-aware differentiable simulator path with graph automorphisms for deduplication and a
  finite-difference fallback, given the same caches and the same sealed costs. It is tuned by a competent engineer
  on development fixtures and then frozen.
- C, fallback: per-event finite differences with no symmetry (what ships if the kill gate fires).

**Measured quantity.** Time to decide a design amendment (from the amendment to an accepted verdict, including
checker time and transfers), paired by world address and seed across arms; reported as a lower confidence bound on
the paired ratio B/A and C/A, never a ratio of means. Run natively on each device family (both RTX 4090 on the
Linux host; the M5 Ultra under Metal), each completing the closed loop on its own; accepted outcomes must agree
between arms within the determinism class.

**Potency controls.** A planted effect: a family in which all events are symmetric and tangent work dominates must
show a ratio near r; an A/A campaign must not clear 1.02.

**Retirement rule.** Where mode bifurcations dominate (the event map differs between members, or the check
returns `Ambiguous` or `Unsupported` on more than half the events of a family), the event telemetry is kept as a
diagnostic and the assumption of amortized tangent work is retired for that family: arm A is not offered there.

**When the experiment does not run.** It runs only if GATE-AUGUR-21 passed (N25d-03) and the owner funded the
slice (N25d-49, OPEN-AUGUR-10). Otherwise this bead spends no GPU time, its pilot record is set to status
`withdrawn` (the register's status for a pilot that will not run), and the bead closes with N41-17's typed reason
`withdrawn` and the recorded reason (rule 2): `withdrawn: not funded: OPEN-AUGUR-10` (N25d-49: never passed, never
killed); `withdrawn: not run: no envelope, R<n>` (N44-01) for a run the owner did not approve; the falsified
pilot's `[[pilot.result]]` (rule 7); or `withdrawn: not run: GATE-AUGUR-21 fail` citing the gate record. A ceiling
at or below 1 closes it `withdrawn: not run: ceiling`, citing the step-0 record, which the kill decision reads.
Each such close is an input of N25d-05, never a gap that blocks it.

**Record.** The pilot record is `PILOT-SYMEVENT-02` in the pilot register
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` (schema `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`, N38a-09), entered at M0 by N25d-40
as its row 17b (amortized use, tier `M`, `bead = "N25d-04"`, `work_package = "BD-426"`, `fallback = { ships =
"Per-event finite differences; symmetry is used only on smooth kernels", kill_gate = "KILL-AUGUR-SYMEVENT" }`,
`integrated_gate = "GATE-AUGUR-21"`). This bead registers that record and adds no second one: before step 0 it
commits the frozen protocol at `docs/pilots/PILOT-SYMEVENT-02.md` (the arms, the paired measure, the potency
controls and the claimed ratio) and stores its BLAKE3 hash, freeze date and ledger sequence in the record's
`protocol` table (status `registered`), with `cost` and `tool` filled; results are appended as
`[[pilot.result]]` under that hash with their committed receipts. Beside it, a method-experiment record
(N38a-07): f, r, h, the ceiling, both ratios with intervals per device, the potency controls, the share of events
with a licence, and the retirement decisions per family. A close without a run sets `PILOT-SYMEVENT-02` to
`withdrawn` with the close reason; the record is never deleted.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] f, r and h are measured on the strong baseline and the ceiling is recorded before any arm is timed.
- [ ] The experiment is registered with its six parts; an experiment whose ceiling is below its target is refused
      and recorded as such.
- [ ] Paired lower bounds of B/A and C/A are reported per device family with the disclosure block (hardware
      fingerprint, load, versions, cold and warm separated).
- [ ] The planted-effect family shows a ratio within 20% of r; the A/A campaign stays below 1.02.
- [ ] Accepted outcomes are identical across arms for every paired world, or the differing pairs are listed.
- [ ] The result, whichever way it goes, is in the pilot register and feeds KILL-AUGUR-SYMEVENT.
- [ ] The owner's funding decision (OPEN-AUGUR-10) is on record before GPU time is spent; unfunded or not
      reached, the bead closes `withdrawn` with the recorded reason (`not funded`, `not run: …`) citing that record,
      and no arm is timed.
- [ ] The deciding number per device is the smaller of the lower bounds of B/A and C/A, and it is logged.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `ceiling_formula`: f = 0.2, r = 4, h = 0 gives 1.1765; f = 0.85, r = ∞ gives 6.67; f = 0.97, r = 40, h = 0.02
  gives 13.5 (the illustrative values of the bound).
- `orbit_representatives`: for the group C₂ × C₂ acting on four feet, one representative event per orbit; the mask
  of a member with a one-sided payload drops the mirror.
- `transport_requires_licence`: arm A refuses to transport at an event without a `Holds` finding.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/augur_symmetry_amortization.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N25d-04 milestone=M6 labels=augur,moonshot
gates=KILL-AUGUR-SYMEVENT seed=4 needs=cuda,metal plants=transport-without-licence`; sources
`scripts/e2e/lib.sh`; exits 77 with the logged close reason when the slice is not funded or not run) runs the
three arms through `frob gait gradient <orbit> --params … --metrics … --tier <gpu-cuda|gpu-metal|batch-cpu> --key
amz-<arm>-<tier>-<pair> --json` and the campaign verbs on each tier (every timed run its own key, so no pair
returns a stored receipt), and logs on every line `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command,
exit code, wall time and evidence block, and per pair: world address, seed, arm, wall time by stage, transfers,
checker time, the number of events, licences used, decision digests; `metric` lines for f, r, h, the ceiling and
the paired lower bounds with their thresholds; a `summary` naming the kill gate touched.

### Known answers and negative controls
The planted-effect family and the A/A campaign; a mutant arm A that transports without a licence must be caught
by an outcome mismatch on the M-payload family of N25d-03. Determinism lanes: each arm twice on one machine;
across the two device families under their class.

## Considerations

- Budget: this is a device experiment on both machines and exceeds the default envelope if run at campaign size;
  it names a reduced family (64 designs, 8 worlds each) and needs the owner's approval for more.
- Tangent work on the compliant tiers has no saltation matrix (the step map is continuous), so the experiment's
  events are those of the event-located route; if that route is not available on devices, f on devices is zero and
  the kill gate fires on the ceiling alone.
- A specialized kernel is never written before the phase breakdown shows where the time goes.
- Priority 2, not the Moonshot default 4: the kill decision N25d-05 (priority 2) waits on this bead's close, and
  in the common case that close is quick (`withdrawn: not funded`, or `withdrawn: not run: ceiling` after step 0). The GPU campaign
  itself still waits on the owner's funding and the queue's slot; the priority orders the close, not the spend.
