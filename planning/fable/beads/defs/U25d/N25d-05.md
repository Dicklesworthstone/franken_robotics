## Background

A kill gate names, in advance, the condition under which a mechanism is abandoned and the fallback that then
ships. It is never quietly re-scoped: its trigger, threshold and fallback change only by a dated amendment made
before the measurement it governs, and its decision is recorded as an ADR at its milestone. A kill gate that fires
is not a failure of the program; the fallback is the plan.

KILL-AUGUR-SYMEVENT governs the symmetry–event identity (epic BD-426). For a body symmetry h and a contact event
e with saltation matrix S (the first-order map across the event), the identity is
D h(x⁺)·S_e(x⁻) = S_{h·e}(h·x⁻)·D h(x⁻). It has a Frontier use, as a test that a symmetry may transport a
derivative across an event, and a Moonshot use, amortizing tangent work across a design family.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Trigger | The identity cannot separate the symmetric fixture from its mutations, or amortization shows no reduction in decision time on either GPU |
| Fallback, named in advance | Per-event finite differences; symmetry is used only on smooth kernels |
| Decided | M6, recorded as an ADR |
| Decides on | The pilot's falsifier (N25d-01), GATE-AUGUR-21 (N25d-03), the amortization experiment (N25d-04) |

## Scope

In: evaluating the trigger on measured values; the ADR; the registry update; re-reading dependents. Out: producing
the measurements (N25d-01, -03, -04).

## Technical approach

**The two halves decide separately**, because the trigger is a disjunction over two different uses.

1. *The test half.* Inputs: the pilot's residual ρ and error estimate η on the symmetric fixture and on the
   asymmetric-payload mutation; the gate's ρ/η on the fixture and on each of the four mutations. The test half
   fires if the pilot's falsifier holds (the identity holds on the asymmetric-payload mutation, or fails on the
   symmetric fixture beyond the finite-difference error), or if GATE-AUGUR-21 fails: any mutation with ρ < 10·η,
   or the symmetric fixture with ρ > η.
2. *The amortization half.* Inputs: f, r, h and the ceiling 1 / (1 − f + f/r + h) from the strong baseline; the
   paired lower bounds of the time-to-decision ratios on CUDA and on Metal, per device the smaller of the lower
   bounds of B/A (the challenger) and C/A (the fallback), as N25d-04 logs it. It fires if the ceiling is at or
   below 1, or if that deciding lower bound is at or below 1 on both device families. If the owner did not fund
   the experiment, the half is `not-reached` (N40-03; N25d-49: an unfunded experiment is never recorded as passed
   and never as killed), not fired.
3. *The row's outcome* (N40-03 names). `fired` if either half fires (the trigger is a disjunction).
   `not-fired` if both halves were evaluated and neither fires. `deferred` if the test half does not fire and the
   amortization half is `not-reached`: the capability stays partial (the check and its local licences ship; the
   family mask and event map do not exist and are not described), the amortization read stays `not-run`, and the
   decision is re-taken at the first exit after OPEN-AUGUR-10 funds the experiment, re-read at every exit until
   then. A measurement bead closed `withdrawn` is an input of this rule, with the reason its close cites (N41-17
   rule 2): N25d-03 citing the pilot's `[[pilot.result]]` when the falsifier held (rule 7, which also closes
   N25d-02 and N25d-04); N25d-04 citing the unfunded item (`not funded`, N25d-49), the envelope row that was not
   approved, the failed gate, or its ceiling record (`not run: ceiling`).

**Outcomes and what changes downstream.**

| Outcome | Record | Downstream |
|---|---|---|
| Neither half fires | ADR "kept"; outcome `not-fired`; GATE-AUGUR-21 `pass` | The check stays; transported derivatives are offered under licences; the family mask and event map are enabled behind the epic's feature flag |
| Test half does not fire; amortization not funded | ADR "test kept, amortization not reached"; outcome `deferred` | The check stays; local transports under licences are offered; no family mask or event map exists or is described; the Moonshot slot is not consumed |
| Only the amortization half fires | ADR "test kept, amortization killed"; outcome `fired`, status `killed`, fallback in force | The check stays as a diagnostic that reports findings; no tangent work is skipped; the Moonshot slot is freed |
| The test half fires | ADR "killed"; outcome `fired`, status `killed`, fallback adopted | The module `symmetry_event` is removed or disabled; `gait.gradient` and every consumer use per-event finite differences; symmetry is used only where kernels are smooth (step equivariance between events); `FROB-REF-852` is raised for any attempted transport across an event; the amortization experiment is not run |

**The fallback as work.** Per-event finite differences already exist (the `finite-difference` derivative label
with events re-located on both sides). The fallback needs no new code; it needs the removal of licences from
consumers and a test that no derivative crosses an event by symmetry.

**Registry.** The kill record of KILL-AUGUR-SYMEVENT (entered by the fallback ledger, N40-26, in the shape of
N40-03) gets its `decision` (outcome, ADR, date, session; `pass` is not an outcome of a kill gate), the measured
values and, if fired, status `killed` with the fallback in force. The bead closes with a typed reason of N41-17's
set: `pass` (`not-fired`, citing this bead's decision test and its output) or `fallback` (either half fired, with
the ADR and the fallback's name: per-event finite differences when the test half fired; the check kept as a
diagnostic with no amortized use when only the amortization half fired); on `deferred` it stays open with the
deferred entry recorded and closes when the decision is re-taken. It also writes
the gate's owner declaration, the block N40-30 compares with the ledger: `[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`, the
ledger's current `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied, not restated, and the `decision`
block (outcome, ADR, measured values each citing its receipt), in `frob-mission`'s `contract.toml`; N25d-41's map
names this bead as the deciding bead. The ADR is `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-SYMEVENT-M6.md` (KNOWN_DRIFTS 4) with the
fields N40-03 requires: the kill gate, milestone M6, the trigger as registered and any amendments applied, the
measured values with their receipts, the outcome, the fallback and the capability's new name, the beads re-read,
the downstream changes, and the recording session.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The trigger is evaluated on recorded measurements only; every input is cited by its ledger identifier.
- [ ] The decision is `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-SYMEVENT-M6.md`, with N40-03's fields, stating the two halves
      separately, with ρ, η, ρ/η, f, r, h, the ceiling and the paired lower bounds.
- [ ] The registry row is updated by a session that did not implement the measured work; `cargo test -p
      frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` passes for KILL-AUGUR-SYMEVENT.
- [ ] If the test half fired: a test shows that no consumer transports a derivative across an event, and the
      capability is described everywhere by the name of the fallback.
- [ ] Dependents are re-read: the M6 exit review (BD-427) finds the ADR; any bead that needed the killed
      capability is closed `superseded` with the ADR identifier.
- [ ] Negative control of the decision rule: fed a synthetic record in which one mutation has ρ = 5·η, the rule
      returns "test half fires".

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `rule_is_pure`: the decision is a function of the record; reordering inputs changes nothing.
- `rule_test_half`: (ρ/η symmetric, mutations) = (0.8; 14, 22, 31, 12) gives "not fired"; (0.8; 14, 5, 31, 12)
  gives "fired"; (1.6; …) gives "fired".
- `rule_amortization_half`: lower bounds (1.07, 0.98) give "not fired"; (0.99, 1.00) give "fired"; ceiling 0.97
  gives "fired" without timings; on both devices B/A 0.97 with C/A 1.30 gives "fired" (the stronger arm decides).
- `rule_unfunded_defers`: a clean test half with the amortization experiment `not funded` gives `deferred`,
  never `fired` and never `not-fired`; the same record with a failed gate gives `fired`.
- `rule_outcome_names`: every outcome the rule returns is one of N40-03's four.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_augur_symevent.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N25d-05 milestone=M6 labels=augur,kill-gate
gates=KILL-AUGUR-SYMEVENT,GATE-AUGUR-21 seed=0 needs=cpu plants=mutation-at-5-eta,unfunded-record`; sources
`scripts/e2e/lib.sh`) reads the three records (or their `withdrawn` closes with the cited reason), applies
the rule, and logs each input with its ledger identifier and threshold (1 and 10 for ρ/η; 1 for the ceiling and
the deciding lower bounds), the outcome of each half, the row's outcome and the record it proposes. It re-runs the
rule on the synthetic record with one mutation at ρ = 5·η and requires "test half fires", and on the unfunded
record and requires `deferred`. After the recording session has written the decision, step `ledger` runs `cargo
test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger`. Every line carries the `STD-E2E` fields (`ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time); a `summary` names the kill gate. It writes nothing to the registry
itself; the recording session does.

### Known answers and negative controls
The synthetic records above. The decision is reproducible from the logged inputs alone.

## Considerations

- A decision by default is not allowed: an unfunded experiment is recorded as unfunded (`not-reached`), not as
  passed and not as killed.
- The thresholds are those of the gate register; where the chapter and the register differ the register governs
  (they agree here).
