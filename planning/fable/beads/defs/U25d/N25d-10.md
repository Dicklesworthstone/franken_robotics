## Background

GATE-AUGUR-17 (claim: Known answer; milestone M4; TARGET) covers the constructive lanes of Augur, the lanes that
propose candidate designs for a mission query: "the co-design fixed point equals the GB-03 closed form and
rejects the additive-peak rule on the bus fixture; the three-pose dyad and both circuit fixtures exact; zero
certified linkages change circuit on the referee across 240 instances." This bead measures the first clause, the
co-design rows, through the public verb, and at the same time exercises the contract every lane shares: a
candidate has a label and no verdict. The linkage rows are measured by a sibling script (N25d-19); the gate's
PASS is recorded when both have run.

The two fixtures. (1) The mass spiral (worked card GB-03): actuator mass m_a = τ_req/ρ_τ, required torque
τ_req = c·m·g·L, total mass m = m_f + n·m_a, so m = m_f/(1 − κ) with loop gain κ = n·c·g·L/ρ_τ, closing only if
κ < 1. (2) The two-finger bus: two fingers each need one powered tick and the bus powers one finger per tick; a
deadline of one tick has zero schedules, two ticks have two schedules, local motor limits are identical in both
cases, and a rule that adds peak demands prunes the feasible case.

## Scope

In: fixture files; the script; the mutants; the gate record for the co-design rows. Out: the lane (BD-360); the
linkage rows (N25d-19); the continuous-resource and Pareto-coverage acceptance tests that belong to
GATE-AUGUR-35 (N25e-44).

## Technical approach

**Fixtures (under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/augur/codesign/`).**
- `mass-spiral.fmission` with its catalog: n = 12 joints, c = 1/4, L = 3/10 m, ρ_τ = 15 N·m/kg,
  g = 981/100 m/s², structure m_f = 6 kg; expected fixed point 30000/2057 kg (κ = 2943/5000).
- `mass-spiral-long-leg.fmission`: L = 13/25 m, κ = 38259/37500 > 1; expected: no candidate, outcome
  `LeavesBound`.
- `mass-spiral-catalogue.fmission`: the step-function catalogue (10 N·m at 0.6 kg, 20 at 1.2, 40 at 2.6) with
  structures 6, 6.5, 13 and 24 kg; expected sizes 1, 2, 3 and `LeavesBound`, totals 13.2, 20.9 and 44.2 kg.
- `two-finger-bus.fmission` at deadlines of one and two ticks; expected 0 and 2 schedules; the design is kept at
  two ticks.
- Derivation notes in `docs/derivations/augur-codesign-*.md`.

**Script steps.**
1. `frob mission check <fixture> --json` (admission, quantifier prefix echoed).
2. `frob mission synthesize <fixture> --strategy codesign --estimate --json`, then the same without
   `--estimate`.
3. Assert on the result: the antichain, the total mass as an exact rational string, the outcome, the trace
   length; for every candidate `label == "candidate"` and `verdict` null or naming a checked cell; the evidence
   block's class; the `authorization` flags all false.
4. `frob mission synthesize two-finger-bus.fmission --strategy codesign --json` at both deadlines; assert the
   schedule counts and that no exclusion cites a peak sum.
5. Contract checks of the dispatch: an unknown strategy (`--strategy anneal`) exits 3 with a schema refusal;
   `--strategy continuation` without its feature exits 5.
6. Cancellation: start a long catalogue query, cancel, assert the drained answer keeps completed candidates and
   reports the rest as not started.
7. Mutants (built from the registered control list, each run through steps 2 to 4 and required to fail):
   `increment-stop` (stops when the increment is below 10 g; returns less than the fixed point),
   `additive-peak` (prunes the two-tick bus case), `declared-monotone-prune` (prunes with an unchecked
   relation), `candidate-into-inner` (copies a candidate into `inner`; the answer validator must reject it).

**What is recorded.** The gate's co-design rows in `registries/gates.toml`: measured fixed point (exact string
and its difference from the closed form, required to be 0), the bus counts, the list of controls caught, commit,
hardware fingerprint, seed, by a session that did not implement BD-360.

failure the first differing value and the answer address; a `summary` with assertions passed and failed and
the gate touched. Lines produced for the dispatch contract carry bead N25d-06 in the `bead` field.
