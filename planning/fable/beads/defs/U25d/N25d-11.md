## Background

A kill gate states in advance when a mechanism is abandoned and what ships in its place; its decision is an ADR
at its milestone and its terms change only by a dated amendment made before the measurement. KILL-SYNTH-CODESIGN
governs the Frontier part of the monotone co-design lane (strategy `codesign` of `mission.synthesize` in the
crate `frob-synth`): using antichains of minimal resources over part catalogs to prune and rank design variants.
The Solid part, deciding whether a sizing loop closes on a catalog, is not at stake.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Trigger | The lane mis-ranks more than 20% of top designs against the referee, or prunes one independently feasible design |
| Fallback, named in advance | Restrict to the closure analysis A-10 for actuator, battery and compute selection |
| Decided | M4, recorded as an ADR |
| Decides on | The pilot's 20-variant sweep (N25d-09); the co-design rows of GATE-AUGUR-17 (N25d-10); the lane's record inside the first co-design campaigns |

Why the trigger is asymmetric. One falsely pruned feasible design fires the gate, whatever the ranking looks
like, because a prune is wrong if any discarded design is independently feasible under the same mission, even
when the eventual winner survives. Ranking is tolerated up to one error in five among top designs, because a
ranking only orders work.

## Scope

In: the decision rule on recorded values; the campaign audit that supplies post-pilot evidence; the ADR; the
registry update; the re-reading of dependents; the definition of the fallback as work. Out: producing the pilot
and gate measurements.

## Technical approach

**Inputs.**
1. From the pilot: the number of pruned variants later found feasible by the full check on the referee; the
   share of the top five whose order differs from the referee's.
2. From the gate script: whether the co-design rows passed with their controls caught.
3. From product use, where a co-design campaign that uses the lane has run by the M4 exit review (DESIGN: an
   audit, so that the decision does not rest on 20 variants alone; it does not delay the decision if no
   campaign has run): every pruned catalog option in a random sample of at least 30 prunes is re-evaluated in
   full (static analyses, then the referee), and the top eight candidates are re-ranked on the referee. Counts
   are recorded as (false prunes, sampled prunes) and (mis-ranked, top designs).

**Rule (a pure function of the record).**
- Fire if false prunes ≥ 1 in the pilot or in the audit.
- Fire if the mis-ranked share of top designs exceeds 0.20 in the pilot (more than 1 of 5) or in the audit
  (more than 1 of 8, since 2/8 = 0.25).
- Otherwise keep (outcome `not-fired`), and record the measured counts.
- The co-design rows of GATE-AUGUR-17 are not a trigger (DESIGN, reason: the ledger's trigger, N40-26, has the two
  clauses above and nothing else, and N40-30 refuses a decision whose rule departs from it; and a wrong fixed
  point is a defect of the Solid closure, which is also the fallback, so firing would not help). If those rows
  have not passed by the decision point after one repair cycle, the outcome is `deferred` (N40-03), the defect is
  filed against BD-360 with its first differing value, and the decision is re-run when the rows pass.
- Pilot branch (rule 7 of the lifecycle rules, N41-17). If the pilot's falsifier held, the session that records
  the funding decision on its result closes BD-360 and N25d-10 `withdrawn`, citing `PILOT-CODESIGN-01` and its
  `[[pilot.result]]`; this bead then becomes ready and applies the two clauses to the pilot's counts alone (there
  are no gate rows and no campaigns, so the deferral clause above does not apply), and its ADR lists the two as
  "withdrawn before this decision". The fallback is then built as described below.

**Outcomes and downstream.**

| Outcome | Record | Downstream |
|---|---|---|
| Kept | ADR with the counts; outcome `not-fired` | `--strategy codesign` returns antichains; starter-design ranking across families may use them |
| Fired | ADR; outcome `fired`, status `killed`, fallback adopted | The strategy returns closure results only: per catalog chain, the least element that closes or `LeavesBound`. No antichain is used to prune or rank; every surviving option is evaluated by the ordinary analyses. Candidate ranking moves to the evaluation ladder. The cascade's stage that consumed antichain rankings is re-read and takes the unranked set |

**The fallback as work.** The closure analysis exists (A-10 in `design.check`). The restricted strategy needs:
(1) a switch in `frob-synth::codesign` that disables `Min` over incomparable vectors and iterates one chain at
a time; (2) removal of prune decisions from the result (`explanations[]` says "ranking withdrawn by
KILL-SYNTH-CODESIGN"); (3) the tests of the Pareto assertions removed and replaced by a test that no option is
dropped without a full evaluation. Estimated at one to two days; it is part of this bead if the gate fires.

**Record.** The decision ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SYNTH-CODESIGN-M4.md` (every kill-gate decision is
`docs/adr/ADR-<gate-name>-<milestone>.md`) with the fields N40-03 requires: the kill gate; the milestone; the
trigger as registered and the amendments applied; each measured value with its receipt hash beside the
trigger's number (false prunes against 0, and the mis-ranked share against 0.20, for the pilot and the audit,
with their counts); the outcome and, for `deferred`, the later exit and the reason; the fallback now in force
and the capability's name from now on ("co-design closure (A-10)"); the beads re-read (BD-360, the cascade's
consuming stage, the starter-design ranking), each "stands on the fallback" or "superseded by this ADR"; what
changes downstream; commit, hardware fingerprint and seeds; the recording session, which did not implement the
lane or run the measurements. The kill row of KILL-SYNTH-CODESIGN in `registries/gates.toml` (`kind = "kill"`)
receives `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session }`; a re-run after a deferral appends a second entry with
its `exit` (N40-03).

**Registry and closing.** The kill-gate row in `registries/gates.toml` gets status, measured counts and the ADR
identifier; this bead closes with the typed reason `pass` (kept, citing this bead's decision test and its
output) or `fallback` (with the ADR and the fallback's name; N41-17); on `deferred` it stays open with the
deferred entry recorded and closes when the re-run decision is taken. BD-360 is not
held open for this decision and is not reopened by it: it closed on its own acceptance before the measurements
this bead reads (it is their dependency), and the ADR's re-read entry for it states whether it stands on the
fallback (rule 5 of N41-17; the restricted strategy is this bead's own work). It also writes the gate's owner declaration, the block N40-30
compares with the ledger: `[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`, the ledger's current `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`,
`mode` and `reads` copied, not restated, and the `decision` block (outcome, ADR, measured values each citing its
receipt); N25d-41's map names this bead as the deciding bead.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rule is applied to recorded values only, each cited by ledger identifier; the audit sample is drawn
      by address hash, not by hand.
- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SYNTH-CODESIGN-M4.md` exists with every field N40-03 requires and states false prunes
      and mis-ranked shares for the pilot and the audit, and the outcome (with the later exit and reason if
      `deferred`); the kill row carries `decision` with the outcome, the ADR path, the date and the session,
      and the register's load checks accept it.
- [ ] If fired: the restricted strategy is in force, its test passes, and the capability is described as
      "co-design closure (A-10)" in the status table and the release cells.
- [ ] Dependents re-read: the M4 exit review finds the ADR; the cascade and the starter-design ranking record
      which form of the lane they stand on.
- [ ] Negative control of the rule: a synthetic record with one false prune and perfect ranking returns "fire".
- [ ] In the pilot branch, BD-360 and N25d-10 are closed `withdrawn` citing the pilot record before this bead
      starts, and the outcome is taken on the pilot's counts, never `deferred`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `rule_one_false_prune_fires`; `rule_ranking_two_of_five_fires`; `rule_one_of_five_keeps`;
  `rule_two_of_eight_fires`; `rule_one_of_eight_keeps`; `rule_gate_rows_unpassed_defers` (perfect pilot counts
  with the gate rows failing → `deferred`, never `fired`); `pilot_branch_decides_on_pilot` (a `falsifier-holds`
  pilot with BD-360 and N25d-10 closed `withdrawn` → `fired`, never `deferred`; with either still open the
  decision tool refuses with "gate bead not withdrawn"); `rule_is_pure` (input order irrelevant);
  `rule_matches_ledger_trigger` (the rule's two clauses and thresholds 1 and 0.20 equal the ledger row's);
  `deferred_names_later_exit` (a `deferred` entry without a later exit and a reason is rejected);
  `adr_fields_complete` (an ADR missing any N40-03 field, or a kill row whose `decision.adr` names a missing
  file, is rejected by the register check).
- `fallback_no_drop_without_evaluation`: in restricted mode every catalog option of the bus and mass-spiral
  fixtures has an evaluation record or appears in the answer.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_synth_codesign.sh` reads the pilot record, the gate record and the audit records through
`frob evidence trace`, applies the rule and logs each input with its ledger identifier and threshold (0 false
prunes; 0.20 mis-ranked share), the outcome and the proposed registry row; it then re-runs it on the synthetic
one-false-prune record and requires `fire`, and asserts that the kill row's `decision` and the ADR state the
same outcome. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/kill_synth_codesign/<utc>.ndjson` with
a transcript: every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall
time and the evidence block of the record read; one `metric` line per clause with the measured counts, the
threshold and the verdict, with the record digests; a `summary` names the kill gate; non-zero exit on missing
inputs or dangling evidence. The recording session writes the registry.

### Known answers and negative controls
The synthetic records of the unit tests; CX-278 as the reason a peak-sum prune counts as a false prune.

## Considerations

- A tie with a conventional dependency graph on correctness does not fire the gate; the gate is about wrong
  prunes and wrong rankings, not about speed.
- The audit costs referee time; it is budgeted inside the campaigns it audits.
