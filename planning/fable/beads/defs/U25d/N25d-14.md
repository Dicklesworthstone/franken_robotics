## Background

GATE-AUGUR-18 (claim: Design comparison; milestone M4; TARGET): "Refinement: of 20 trajectory-optimized
candidates per mission, at least 10 pass the referee route and beat the nominal design on held-out terrain, each
solved within 10 minutes on 8 threads." The mechanism under test is the strategy `refine` of
`mission.synthesize` (BD-362): a nonlinear program over a motion and a vector of continuous design parameters,
solved on the CPU, whose output is a candidate design with no verdict.

Why the gate is a comparison on the referee and not a property of the optimizer. An optimized plan assumes
rigid contact and ideal actuators, and nothing published says how often an optimized design survives an
independent closed-loop evaluation. So the gate judges the candidates the way any design is judged: under a
controller, on the slowest and most trusted simulation tier (the CPU referee with located contact events),
against the design they started from, on terrain the optimizer never saw.

## Scope

In: the three missions as fixtures; the candidate generation through the public verb; the referee evaluation;
the paired comparison; controls; the gate's tier entry and receipt; the gate record (this bead is the gate's one
recorder). Out: the lane, its fixtures and its own script (BD-362; this script runs it as its first stage); the
transcription and its script (N25d-13); the pilot with an external optimizer (N25d-12); the kill decision
(N25d-15).

## Technical approach

**Missions (frozen as `.fmission` and `.ftask` fixtures before the run).** On the reference 12-degree-of-freedom
quadruped: a trot at 1 m/s; a 0.3 m step up; carrying a 5 kg payload. Each names its motion, its fixed contact
schedule, its free parameters θ (between 4 and 17, with boxes) and its metric (cost of transport for the trot
and the payload mission; time and peak torque margin for the step).

**Stage 1, the lane contract.** BD-362's `scripts/e2e/synth_refine_lane.sh` (which runs N25d-06's and N25d-13's
scripts first): the gear-ratio solve from ratio 1 (`gear-ratio`) and from ratio 4 (`gear-ratio-from-4`), each
from its nominal start with no `--seed` (ratio 2 within 10⁻⁶, cost 192, sensitivity
near zero, label `candidate`, null verdict, `fixed-mode` on every sensitivity); the boxed fixture [3, 5] (active
bound reported); the 18-parameter and free-schedule queries (exit 3, `FROB-REF-857` with
`detail: synthesis-out-of-scope`, pointer, first repair); a 1 s budget (no candidate, `BudgetExhausted`, no
exclusion); a cancellation mid-solve (nothing partial published). A failed stage stops the script before the gate
solves.

**Candidates.** For each mission, 20 starts: the nominal design perturbed inside the boxes by address-seeded
draws. `frob mission synthesize <m>.fmission --strategy refine --threads 8 --budget wall_s=600 --seed <i>
--key refine-<m>-<i>-<run> --json` per start (`--threads` and `--seed` are the lane options BD-362 defines). A
start that returns no candidate counts as a failed candidate; its explanation is logged.

**Evaluation ("the referee route").** Each candidate and the nominal design get the baseline controller
synthesized for their class by one rule, with no per-design tuning (`frob control synth <design>
--key ctl-<design>-<run> --json`). Both run on the CPU referee under the mission's requirement on held-out
terrain: the battery `fixtures/augur/refine/<m>-held-out` holds instances from the terrain generator at the
mission's difficulty with seeds reserved for this gate and absent from every optimizer input, frozen before the
first solve; `frob eval run <design> --battery <m>-held-out --tier referee --key ev-<m>-<design>-<run> --json`.
Worlds are paired by address and seed between a candidate and the nominal design.

**Per-candidate verdict.** Pass = (a) the requirement holds on the held-out set at the mission's declared rate,
hard limits never violated; and (b) the candidate beats nominal: the lower confidence bound of the paired
difference in the mission metric is above zero, by the fixed-n exact study with its reachability pre-analysis
(the study type is declared and never mixed). Before the first solve, `frob eval preanalyze --rule <rule> --n <n>
--margin 0 --level 0.05 --json` (a read verb; the rule and n, the held-out battery's pair count, frozen with the
protocol) prints the reachable verdicts and the pre-analysis hash, which are committed. Per candidate, `frob eval
compare <nominal> <candidate> --task <m> --battery <m>-held-out --fixed-n <n> --rule <rule> --margin 0 --level
0.05 --declared-verdicts <the reachable verdicts> --key cmp-<m>-<i>-<run> --json` runs the paired fixed-n study
on the battery's worlds (`--battery` draws the pairs from the battery's items; the fixed-n block makes it the
exact study, not the anytime default); the candidate, the second arm, beats nominal when its verdict is `promote`
(prefer B, N24b-06). `study.plan` is not used: its manifest names a task and no battery, so it cannot draw the
held-out worlds. And (c) its solve took at most 600 s on 8 threads.

**Idempotency keys.** Every run-verb call carries `--key` and no two compared calls share one (a shared key
returns the first call's stored receipt): one key per start, per controller synthesis, per evaluation and per
comparison, each with the run's identifier (`eval preanalyze` is a read verb and takes none); the nominal design's evaluation key differs from every
candidate's. A resumed run reuses the same keys on purpose, which is how completed work is reused by address.

**Gate decision.** For each of the three missions, at least 10 of 20 candidates pass. The measured counts, the
solve-time quantiles and the paired bounds are recorded, not the targets.

**Receipt and record.** The gate's tier entry (DESIGN: written by this bead in `g1_known_answers`, tagged
GATE-AUGUR-18, a through-verb entry like N28c-16's; reason: its checks are exact re-derivations of counts from
stored answers and its controls have known outcomes, while G2 holds agreement with an external oracle, which this
gate is not; N25d-41 copies this tier into the gate record) re-reads every solve, evaluation and comparison of
this run by its key, so the stored answers are returned and nothing is recomputed;
it checks each answer's digest against the one the script logged, recomputes the per-candidate verdicts and the
per-mission counts from them, and runs the four controls below in the same run. The script runs it with `e2e_tier
g1_known_answers GATE-AUGUR-18 --mutations` (the STD-E2E helper for `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-AUGUR-18`), asserts that the
receipt's counts equal the
script's and that its `controls` list all four as `caught`, and logs the receipt's hash. The script never runs
`record_gate`: the recording session, which did not implement the lane, enters the gate from that receipt with
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-18 --receipt <hash>`.

**Controls (each must be caught in the same run).**
- A/A: the nominal design against itself must not beat nominal.
- Potency: a planted candidate whose θ is known to be better on a closed form (the gear-ratio fixture at ratio
  2 against nominal 1) must beat nominal; a planted worse one (ratio 4 against nominal 2) must not.
- Leak: a build that draws held-out terrain seeds from the optimizer's seed range is detected by the
  disjointness check.
- Tuning: a build that tunes the controller for candidates only is detected by the controller-rule digest
  differing between the two arms.

Each is registered in `controls.toml` against GATE-AUGUR-18 before the tier entry is written (kind `a-a` for the
A/A, `planted-design` for the two potency designs, `mutation` with its own feature for the leak and tuning
builds), and this bead appends the four numbers to the gate's `controls`, as N25d-41 states.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Mission fixtures, starts, held-out seeds, the fixed-n rule and n, and the pre-analysis record (reachable
      verdicts and hash) are committed before the first solve.
- [ ] 60 solves and their evaluations are recorded; no start is dropped silently.
- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-18 is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` from the receipt of the tier run, with per-mission pass
      counts, solve-time quantiles, the commit, the hardware fingerprint and the seeds, by a session that did not
      implement the lane, through `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-18
      --receipt <hash>`; the script itself writes no record.
- [ ] BD-362's stage passes before the gate solves start.
- [ ] All four controls are caught in the same tier run, and the receipt lists them.
- [ ] The claim recorded is `design-comparison`; nothing in the record is worded as a speed or fidelity claim.
- [ ] The script is idempotent and resumable: completed solves and evaluations are reused by address.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/augur_refine_gate.rs`: `held_out_seeds_disjoint`; `controller_rule_same_for_both_arms`;
`paired_bound_constant_difference` (ten pairs with a constant difference return it); `verdict_requires_all_three`
(a candidate that beats nominal but took 601 s fails); `reachability_table_present` (every verdict the fixed-n
rule can return at the planned n is listed); `keys_distinct_across_compared_calls` (the script's key generator
never gives two starts, two evaluations or a candidate and the nominal design one key, and gives a resumed run the
same keys); `held_out_battery_frozen` (the battery's digest is committed before the first solve and a changed
battery fails the run).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/augur_synth_refine.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N25d-14 milestone=M4 labels=augur,frontier,gate
gates=GATE-AUGUR-18`): stage 1, then the commands above for every start, the evaluations and comparisons, and the
tier run. Log at `target/e2e-logs/augur_synth_refine/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it, through the STD-E2E
helper (every line `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25d-14`, or the stage's bead on a stage's lines), `step`, `event`): a
`stage` line with the stage's script, log path and summary;
per solve the command, key, exit code, wall time, threads, seed,
problem identity and size (knots, variables, constraints, non-zeros), assembly time, iterations, the
certificate residuals; per evaluation the evidence block, the
requirement verdict and the world addresses; per comparison the paired difference, its bound and threshold 0;
`metric` lines per mission for the pass count against 10 of 20 and the slowest solve against 600 s; control
outcomes; the `cmd` line of `e2e_tier` with its `receipt`; on failure the first failing candidate with its
replay addresses; a `summary` naming the gate, controls caught and the receipt hash.

### Known answers and negative controls
The closed-form potency fixtures (ratio 2 with cost 192 against ratio 1 with cost 300). The four controls
above. Determinism lanes: referee evaluations twice on one machine give identical digests; solves repeat
bit for bit on one machine at a fixed thread count; decisions are compared across debug and release.

## Considerations

- Compute: 60 solves of up to 10 minutes on 8 threads is at most 10 hours of wall time on one machine; referee
  evaluation of 63 designs on a held-out battery is the larger cost and is budgeted with the owner if it
  exceeds the default envelope.
- If the pilot's falsifier held, this bead is not started and the kill gate decides on the pilot: the funding
  review closes it `withdrawn` with the pilot record (PILOT-TRAJOPT-01, `falsifier-holds`) as its reason,
  which lets N25d-15 become ready, and N25d-15's ADR re-reads it as `superseded`.
- The gate does not say the lane is better than an external optimizer; parity is the stated ceiling.
