## Background

Step 1 of bounded exact linkage synthesis (the linkage lane of Augur's constructive crate `frob-synth`). A dyad
is a single link joining a fixed pivot O on the frame to a moving pivot on a body. If the body must pass through
three prescribed planar poses, the moving pivot occupies three positions P₁, P₂, P₃, and a rigid link can hold
it only if those three points lie on a circle about O. Eliminating the unknown crank radius from
‖Pᵢ − O‖² = ‖P₁ − O‖² leaves a linear system: the fixed pivot is the circumcentre of the three positions,

  2·(Pᵢ − P₁)·O = ‖Pᵢ‖² − ‖P₁‖²,   i = 2, 3,

a rational 2×2 system when the positions are rational. Two dyads guiding the same body form a four-bar linkage
that visits the three poses. With a catalog of lug positions and keep-in regions for pivots, the admissible
dyads can be enumerated exactly, with no polynomial solver and no floating point.

This step is deliberately modest. Three-position synthesis has textbook closed forms, so no speed is claimed;
what the lane adds is exactness (every accept or reject is a rational comparison) and the circuit certificate of
step 3 (N25d-18), without which a set of dyads is only a root of the synthesis equations and not a mechanism.

## Scope

In: the pose and catalog inputs; the exact solve; the rank split; keep-in and length filters; pairing dyads
into four-bars; the enumeration order; output records; the example program `dyad_enumerate` and its own
end-to-end script. Out: five-pose and template systems through the
derivation subprocess, the strategy surface and the `.fmodule` output (BD-361); circuit, branch, clearance and
tolerance certificates (N25d-18).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-synth/src/linkage/dyad.rs` (DESIGN). All arithmetic is over ℚ with the workspace's exact
rational type; no `f64` appears in this module.

**Inputs.**
- Three planar poses of the moving body, each a rotation given as a rational point (c, s) on the unit circle
  (c² + s² = 1 exactly; produced from a rational half-angle parameter t as c = (1 − t²)/(1 + t²),
  s = 2t/(1 + t²)) and a rational translation d. A pose that is not exactly a rotation is refused at
  admission as an invalid query field: `FROB-REF-868`, exit 3, pointer `/design_domain/linkage/poses/<i>`,
  `observed` c² + s², `required` 1, repair "give the rotation by its rational half-angle t" (the block is BD-361's).
- A catalog of candidate moving-lug positions a_j in the body frame. (Reading of "lug-pair catalog" adopted
  here, DESIGN: a lug pair is the pair of lugs one link joins; the catalog lists, per moving lug, the link
  centre distances that can be bought or made, each as a rational interval of squared length, or `any`.)
- Keep-in regions: a convex polygon with rational vertices for fixed pivots in the frame, and one for moving
  pivots in the body frame.

**Per moving lug a.**
1. Positions Pᵢ = Rᵢ·a + dᵢ, exact.
2. Matrix A = 2·[P₂ − P₁; P₃ − P₁] (rows), right side b = (‖P₂‖² − ‖P₁‖², ‖P₃‖² − ‖P₁‖²),
   det A = 4·((P₂ − P₁) × (P₃ − P₁)).
3. Rank split.
   - Rank 2 (det ≠ 0): O = A⁻¹·b, radius² = ‖P₁ − O‖². One dyad.
   - Rank 1 with an inconsistent system (three distinct collinear positions): no finite fixed pivot. The lug
     is reported `degenerate: collinear` and skipped (the plan: collinear positions are reported as
     degenerate). The fixed pivot would be at infinity: a slider, which is a different template that this lane
     does not offer. A query whose lug catalog leaves no other dyad (for example one that names exactly this
     lug) is answered, not refused: zero candidates, the step completed (not failed; the verb, BD-361, maps
     this to exit 2: the lane completed and accepted none), and an
     `explanations[]` entry `degenerate: collinear` carrying its exact witness (det A = 0 and the inconsistent
     right side, as rational strings), the lug and the poses, with `next` naming a slider template or another
     lug. It is never an exclusion of the cell and never a pivot (DESIGN: the case is a mathematical outcome of
     a valid query, so it takes no refusal code; this replaces an earlier uncoded "refused").
   - Rank 1 with a consistent system (two of the three positions coincide): a line of admissible pivots (the
     perpendicular bisector of the two distinct positions); reported as a one-parameter family, intersected
     with the keep-in polygon to a segment.
   - Rank 0 (all three positions coincide: the lug is the pole of the motion): every O in the keep-in region
     is admissible.
4. Filters, all exact: a in the moving keep-in polygon; O in the fixed keep-in polygon (signed-area tests
   with rational cross products; boundary inclusion is declared, closed by default); radius² inside a
   catalogued squared-length interval; radius² > 0 (a zero-length link is refused).

**Pairing.** Admissible dyads are paired into four-bars: two distinct moving lugs a, b with pivots O_a, O_b;
the coupler length is ‖a − b‖ and the ground length ‖O_a − O_b‖. Pairs with O_a = O_b or a = b are skipped.
Enumeration is in catalog order (lexicographic in lug indices) so that output is deterministic and budgets cut
at a reproducible point.

**Output.** `DyadSet { poses, dyads: Vec<Dyad>, families: Vec<PivotFamily>, degenerate: Vec<LugId> }` and an
iterator of `FourBarCandidate { lugs, pivots, squared_lengths }` handed to step 3. Counts and the first few
rejections per filter are reported for explanation. Class of each record: exact (rational identities that the
offline auditor can recompute); determinism D0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] P₁ = (0, 0), P₂ = (2, 0), P₃ = (0, 2) give O = (1, 1) and radius² = 2, as exact rationals.
- [ ] Collinear positions give determinant zero and the `degenerate: collinear` outcome, never a pivot; a catalog
      that leaves only that lug completes with zero four-bar candidates and the explanation with its witness.
- [ ] A pose that is not exactly a rotation is refused with `FROB-REF-868` and the pointer to the pose.
- [ ] The coincident-positions cases return a family, not a refusal and not an arbitrary point.
- [ ] No floating-point type appears in the module (checked by a lint in its tests).
- [ ] Keep-in tests are exact on boundary points, and the boundary rule (closed) is documented.
- [ ] Enumeration over a catalog of 40 lugs returns the same ordered list on every run and thread count.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/synth_linkage_dyad.sh` passes on the fixtures below (the dyad known answers are measured
      through the public verb later, by BD-361's and N25d-19's scripts).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-synth/tests/dyad.rs`:
- `dyad_known_answer`: (0, 0), (2, 0), (0, 2) → O = (1, 1), radius² = 2.
- `dyad_unit_circle`: (1, 0), (0, 1), (−1, 0) → O = (0, 0), radius² = 1.
- `dyad_collinear`: (0, 0), (1, 0), (2, 0) → det 0, `degenerate: collinear`.
- `dyad_two_coincident`: (0, 0), (0, 0), (2, 0) → the family x = 1.
- `dyad_pole`: three equal positions → every keep-in point.
- `pose_must_be_rotation`: (c, s) = (3/5, 4/5) accepted; (3/5, 3/5) refused with `FROB-REF-868`, `observed`
  18/25 and the pointer to the pose.
- `collinear_only_lug_completes_empty`: a catalog of the single collinear lug yields zero four-bar candidates,
  status completed (not failed), the explanation `degenerate: collinear` with det A = 0 as a string, and no
  exclusion.
- `size_beyond_exact_type_refused`: coordinates whose composed numerators exceed the 4,096-bit coefficient cap
  are refused with `FROB-REF-857` naming that cap, never rounded.
- `keepin_boundary_closed`: a pivot exactly on an edge is inside; just outside by 1/10⁹ is outside.
- `length_interval_filter`: radius² = 2 passes [49/25, 51/25] and fails [9/4, 5/2].
- Property test: for random rational centres and radii and three rational points on that circle (generated
  from rational half-angle parameters), the solve returns the centre and radius² exactly.
- Property test: the returned O is equidistant from the three positions (‖Pᵢ − O‖² equal as rationals).

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-653 (pivot (1, 1), radius² = 2). Planted defects the tests must catch: a solve in `f64` that returns
(1.0000000000000002, 1) on a scaled instance (exact comparison fails); a keep-in test with an open boundary;
a collinear instance that returns a huge finite pivot. Metamorphic relations: translating all poses by a
rational vector translates O by the same vector; applying a rational rotation to all poses rotates O.
Determinism: twice on one machine, across worker counts, debug and release, identical bytes.

## Considerations

- A lug at the pole of the displacement is common in symmetric tasks; treating it as an error would lose valid
  designs.
- Rational coordinates grow under composition of poses; size limits are those of the exact type and the
  coefficient cap of the symbolic lane (4,096 bits), and an instance beyond them is refused with
  `FROB-REF-857` naming the cap, not rounded.
- The reading of "lug-pair catalog" is a DESIGN interpretation; if the catalog owner defines it differently the
  filter in step 4 changes and nothing else.
