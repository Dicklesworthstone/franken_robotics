## Background

Step 3 of bounded exact linkage synthesis, and the reason the lane exists. **A root of the synthesis equations
is not a mechanism.** Take ground pivots O₂ = (0, 0) and O₄ = (4, 0), crank O₂A of length 1, coupler AB of
length 4 and rocker O₄B of length 3: a crank-rocker. The distance from A to O₄ ranges over [3, 5] and never
reaches 1 or 7, so A, B and O₄ are never collinear and the sign of (B − A) × (O₄ − A) is constant on each
assembly circuit. At crank angle 0 the closure equations give B = (11/3, ±√80/3); at crank angle π they give
B = (11/5, ±12/5). A design whose precision poses are (crank 0, B above) and (crank π, B below) satisfies every
synthesis equation and cannot be driven from one pose to the other without disassembly. The circuit certificate
is the constant non-zero sign, proved over the crank interval by enclosure.

The certificate has four parts: circuit (all target poses on one connected component, visited in order), branch
(the loop-closure Jacobian stays non-singular along the way, with the reciprocal of its determinant bounded),
clearance (no self-collision, and the required reach), and tolerance (all of this for every as-built unit in a
box around the nominal dimensions). Output kinds: `interval-box` and `partition` from the closed proof-kind
registry, so the independent auditor `frob-verify` can re-check them with its own outward-rounded arithmetic.

Rejected: sampling the driving angle densely (a sign change between samples is missed; samples are not a proof
over an interval); accepting numerical continuation of the closure as evidence of a circuit.

## Scope

In: the constraint expressions; the driving interval and its partition; enclosures over the tolerance box;
the verdict per candidate; the proof objects; the defect reports. Out: generating candidates (N25d-17, BD-361);
writing bundles (the certificate writer) and checking them (the auditor); the module output (BD-361).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-synth/src/linkage/circuit.rs` (DESIGN). Proposals use the interval and Taylor-model
arithmetic admitted through the airlock; nothing here is trusted by the auditor, which recomputes each box.

**Setting for a planar four-bar** with ground pivots O₂, O₄, crank length r, coupler length c, rocker length ρ,
driving angle s (crank angle), A(s) = O₂ + r·(cos s, sin s), and B the intersection of the circle of radius c
about A with the circle of radius ρ about O₄, on a chosen side σ ∈ {+, −}.
- Loop-closure Jacobian determinant with respect to the passive angles: Δ(s) = (B − A) × (O₄ − A), with
  Δ² = ((c + ρ)² − d²)·(d² − (c − ρ)²)/4 and d² = ‖A(s) − O₄‖². For the fixture: d² = 17 − 8·cos s and
  Δ² = 16·(4 + cos s)·(2 − cos s), from 80 at s = 0 to 144 at s = π, so |Δ| ≥ √80 on the whole revolution.
- Side: the sign of Δ. It is the assembly mode.

**Algorithm, per candidate and tolerance box T (each dimension nominal ± tolerance).**
1. Poses to parameters. Each precision pose i has a driving value sᵢ (an exact root isolated by interval
   contraction to a box [sᵢ]) and a side σᵢ.
2. Circuit pre-check. All σᵢ must be equal; otherwise report `circuit-defect: poses on different assembly
   circuits`, naming the poses, and stop. No certificate can exist (Δ would have to change sign).
3. Order. The sᵢ must be monotone along the chosen direction of drive, their isolating boxes disjoint.
4. Partition the driving interval [min sᵢ, max sᵢ] into pieces. On each piece × T, enclose Δ with outward
   rounding. A piece passes when the enclosure excludes zero with the sign σ; a piece that straddles zero is
   bisected until a floor width, then the candidate is reported `branch-unresolved` (not a defect: unknown).
   Record m = the smallest |Δ| lower end over all pieces; the certified bound on 1/|Δ| is 1/m.
5. Clearance and reach, on the same pieces × T: for each pair of links that may collide (declared by the
   template: crank with rocker, coupler with frame hardware), enclose the signed separation of their capsule
   geometry and require its lower end above the declared margin; enclose the output point and require the
   reach targets inside it.
6. Emit proof objects: one `interval-box` per piece and per constraint (expression digest, box, claimed
   enclosure, expected verdict "holds"), and one `partition` object stating that the pieces cover the driving
   interval exactly with disjoint interiors and stated end ownership. The tolerance box is part of every
   `interval-box`, so the statement covers every as-built unit in T.
7. Verdict for the candidate: `certified` (all pieces pass), `circuit-defect`, `branch-defect` (an enclosure
   proves that Δ² is not positive on some piece: a branch point or a loss of closure lies on the path),
   `collision`, `reach-short`, or `unresolved` (budget or floor width reached).

**Split set.** An `interval-box` certificate bisects at most six parameters. Here bisection is along s only;
the dimensions in T are enclosed at full tolerance range, which is narrow. A template with more than six
bisected parameters is outside the kind's scope and is refused.

**Class.** `certified` is a claim about the linkage's own geometry: class `Certified` once the auditor accepts
the bundle. It is not a mission verdict; the linkage is still a candidate (INV-AUGUR-11).

**Other templates.** For a five-bar the driving space is two-dimensional and the partition is a paving of a
rectangle with the same per-piece rule; the determinant is that of the 2×2 passive Jacobian of the loop.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The crank-rocker (ground 4, crank 1, coupler 4, rocker 3): with poses (0, above) and (π, above) the
      certificate is issued over [0, π] with m ≥ √80 − (rounding), both poses on the circuit with Δ < 0; with
      poses (0, above) and (π, below) the result is `circuit-defect`.
- [ ] The second circuit fixture (ground 4, crank 1, coupler 7/2, rocker 3; 1 + 4 ≤ 7/2 + 3, so the crank turns
      fully and the two circuits are disjoint): B = (73/24, ±√4655/24) at crank angle 0; |Δ| ≥ √4655/8 on the
      revolution; a candidate with poses on different circuits is rejected.
- [ ] Every issued certificate is accepted by `frob-verify`; each registered mutation of it is rejected.
- [ ] A tolerance box is part of every box certificate; widening the tolerance until Δ's enclosure touches
      zero turns the verdict to `unresolved`, never to `certified`.
- [ ] A straddling piece is never reported as a defect or as a pass.
- [ ] The circuit rows of GATE-AUGUR-17 are measured through the public verb by N25d-19.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-synth/tests/circuit.rs` (real interval arithmetic, real auditor):
- `crank_rocker_positions`: B = (11/3, ±√80/3) at 0; (11/5, ±12/5) at π (the second exactly rational).
- `crank_rocker_delta_formula`: Δ² = 16·(4 + cos s)·(2 − cos s); 80 at 0, 144 at π; sign negative for B above.
- `circuit_defect_detected`: sides (+, −) → `circuit-defect` before any enclosure.
- `certificate_same_side`: sides (−, −) → `certified`, m within 10⁻⁶ of √80.
- `grashof_fixture`: the 4, 1, 7/2, 3 linkage as above.
- `non_grashof_branch_defect`: ground 4, crank 3, coupler 2, rocker 2 cannot turn fully: d² = 25 − 24·cos s
  must stay at or below (c + ρ)² = 16, so the crank locks at cos s = 3/8 (s ≈ 1.186 rad). A candidate whose
  driving interval is [1/2, 3/2] rad is reported `branch-defect`: on the piece [1.3, 1.5] the enclosure of Δ²
  is negative (d² ≥ 18.5 there), so the closure is lost on the path.
- `tolerance_box_carried`: lengths ± 1/5000 m still certify; ± 1/2 m does not.
- `straddle_is_unresolved`.
- Property test: for random Grashof crank-rockers with rational lengths the enclosure of Δ² contains the closed
  form at 1,000 sampled angles.

### End-to-end script and logging
Exercised through `scripts/e2e/augur_synth_linkage.sh` (N25d-19): per candidate the verdict, m, the number of
pieces, the certificate kinds, the auditor's verdict and the defect report.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-654 (B = (11/5, ±12/5) at crank angle π; the two signs are different circuits); CX-280. Mutations the
auditor must reject: a piece removed from the partition; a piece whose claimed enclosure is narrower than the
true one; the tolerance box dropped; an end of the driving interval opened. Planted defect in the proposer: a
build that checks only the pose equations certifies the (0 above, π below) design and is caught. Determinism:
proof-object bytes identical twice on one machine, across worker counts, debug and release.

## Considerations

- Clearance uses the same swept-geometry primitives as the self-collision analysis; a linkage that certifies
  kinematically and collides is `collision`, with the piece and the pair named.
- Dynamic effects (inertia through a near-singular pose, backlash) are outside the certificate; the bound 1/m
  tells the designer how close to singular the path comes.
