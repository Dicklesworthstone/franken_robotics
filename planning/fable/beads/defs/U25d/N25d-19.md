## Background

GATE-AUGUR-17 (claim: Known answer; milestone M4; TARGET): "Constructive lanes: the co-design fixed point
equals the GB-03 closed form and rejects the additive-peak rule on the bus fixture; the three-pose dyad and
both circuit fixtures exact; zero certified linkages change circuit on the referee across 240 instances." The
co-design clause is measured by the sibling script (N25d-10). This bead measures the linkage clauses through
the public verb and records the gate once both scripts have run.

The mechanism: the strategy `linkage` of `mission.synthesize` (BD-361) builds planar linkages from precision
poses in exact arithmetic, and certifies for each that all poses lie on one assembly circuit, traversed in
order without crossing a branch point (N25d-18). The last clause of the gate tests the certificate against
physics as the program's most trusted simulator computes it: a linkage that the lane certified is assembled as
a closed chain and driven through its poses on the CPU referee; it must not bind and must not end on the other
circuit.

## Scope

In: the 240 frozen instances (the pilot's set, re-used by hash); the rig fixture and the referee drive; the four
mutants as the rows' registered controls; the metamorphic relations and determinism lanes; the linkage part of the
gate's tier entry and the tier run over both halves; the gate record (this bead is the gate's one recorder). Out:
the lane, its exact fixtures and its own script (BD-361); the dyad and circuit scripts (N25d-17, N25d-18); the
co-design rows (N25d-10); the pilot; the kill decision.

## Technical approach

**Exact fixtures.** BD-361's table, under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/augur/linkage/`: `dyad-3pose` (pivot
(1, 1), radius² = 2), `dyad-collinear` (exit 2, `degenerate: collinear`), `pose-not-rotation` (868), the two
circuit linkages with their pass and defect cases (`circuit-a-*`: B = (11/3, √80/3) and (11/5, 12/5), m = √80;
`circuit-b-*`: B = (73/24, ±√4655/24), m = √4655/8), `caps-9var` (857), `burmester-5pose-planted`. "Both circuit
fixtures" is read as the two linkages, each with a pass case and a defect case. This bead adds the metamorphic
variants `dyad-3pose-moved` (all poses under the rational rigid motion: rotation by half-angle t = 1/2, then
translation (3, −1)) and `circuit-a-same-moved`, and the rig fixture `linkage-rig` (frame fixed, a
position-controlled drive on the crank, the declared stall criterion and constraint-force bound).

**The 240 instances.** The pilot's frozen set (three-pose dyads with a lug catalog and keep-in regions; three
strata of 80; a quarter with planted circuit or branch defects), identified by its hash.

**Script steps.**
1. Stages, in order, each stopping the script on failure: N25d-17's `scripts/e2e/synth_linkage_dyad.sh`, N25d-18's
   `scripts/e2e/synth_linkage_circuit.sh` and BD-361's `scripts/e2e/synth_linkage_lane.sh` (which runs the exact
   fixtures through the public verb with `frob prove verify`, `frob-verify` and `frob module check`, and the 857
   and 868 refusals). The lane stage's asserts on the fixture table are the measured values of the dyad and
   circuit rows.
2. Metamorphic: `dyad-3pose-moved` and `circuit-a-same-moved` with keys `lk-mr-<fixture>-<run>`; pivots move by the
   same rigid motion and verdicts are unchanged; one instance of the 240 with its catalog relabelled gives the
   same certified set.
3. For each of the 240 instances: synthesize (`--key lk-inst-<i>-<run>`); for every certified linkage, import
   the module into `linkage-rig`, `frob sim run <rig> --scene <pose drive> --tier referee --key
   lk-ref-<i>-<c>-<run> --json` driving through the poses in order (one key per instance and candidate c);
   classify `moves`, `binds` (drive stall or constraint force above the declared bound) or `changes circuit` (the
   sign of the closure determinant at the end differs from the certified one, read from telemetry).
4. Determinism lanes: the exact fixtures and the first 24 instances again with second `<run>` suffixes (twice on
   one machine; one and eight workers; debug and release); identical module and certificate digests (exact
   class) and identical referee digests.
5. The gate's tier entry (DESIGN: this bead adds the linkage rows to the `g1_known_answers` entry tagged
   GATE-AUGUR-17 that N25d-10 wrote for the co-design rows; they call `frob_synth::linkage` on the exact fixtures
   and drive the 240 instances' certified linkages on the referee through the library): `e2e_tier
   g1_known_answers GATE-AUGUR-17 --mutations`, the STD-E2E helper for `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p
   frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-AUGUR-17`. One run covers both
   halves, so the control runner (N38b-04) requires N25d-10's four mutants and this bead's four caught in the same
   run: `no-circuit-check` (emits the defect designs), `sampled-sign` (checks the sign at 16 sample angles; a
   planted instance with a narrow sign change between samples passes it and then changes circuit on the
   referee), `truncate-caps` (drops the ninth variable), `open-interval` (a certificate whose partition omits an
   end point; the auditor rejects it). The script asserts that the receipt's counts equal steps 1 and 3 (and
   N25d-10's co-design values) and that its `controls` list all eight as `caught`.

**Idempotency keys.** Every run-verb call carries `--key`, and no two calls the script compares share one (a
shared key returns the first call's stored receipt); the determinism lane's second run uses a second `<run>`
suffix so that it recomputes. `frob prove verify` and `frob module check` are read verbs and take none.

**Recording the gate.** GATE-AUGUR-17 has one row, two halves and one recorder, this bead. The script never runs
`record_gate` (a script produces a receipt and never records a value). The recording session, which did not
implement BD-360 or BD-361, reads the hash of step 5's receipt from the log (the gate report row of
`scripts/e2e/run-all.sh --gate GATE-AUGUR-17`, which runs N25d-10's script and this one, lists it) and enters the
gate with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-17 --receipt <hash>`.

**Gate decision for these rows.** All exact fixtures match; zero of the certified linkages among the 240 are
`binds` or `changes circuit`; every planted defect was refused a certificate; all mutants are caught.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three stages pass; through BD-361's stage every row of the exact fixture table matches exactly.
- [ ] Across the 240 instances the count of certified linkages that bind or change circuit is 0, and the count
      of certified linkages is reported per stratum (a gate that certifies nothing is recorded as vacuous and
      does not pass).
- [ ] Every certificate is accepted by `frob-verify` on a host with no GPU; the four mutants of this bead and the
      four of N25d-10 are caught in the one tier run, and the receipt lists them.
- [ ] The metamorphic variants move pivots by the stated rigid motion with unchanged verdicts; the determinism
      lanes give identical digests.
- [ ] `FROB-REF-857` is returned for the caps fixture with its pointer and first repair; `FROB-REF-868` for the
      pose that is not a rotation; the collinear dyad returns exit 2 with the `degenerate: collinear`
      explanation and no candidate.
- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-17 is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` from the receipt of step 5, with the measured counts of
      both halves, the commit, the hardware fingerprint and the seeds, by a session that did not implement either
      lane; the script itself writes no record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/augur_linkage_gate.rs`: the moved fixtures equal the rigid motion of the
originals, exactly; the instance set's hash equals the pilot's; the planted-defect quarter is labelled; the
vacuity check trips on an empty certified set; `linkage-rig` declares its stall criterion and force bound; the four
mutants are registered in `controls.toml` against GATE-AUGUR-17 with their plants and per-control features; the
script's key generator gives distinct keys to every compared call and each determinism lane.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/augur_synth_linkage.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N25d-19 milestone=M4 labels=augur,frontier,gate
gates=GATE-AUGUR-17`). Through the STD-E2E helper, log at `target/e2e-logs/augur_synth_linkage/<utc>.ndjson` with
a transcript beside it; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25d-19`, or the stage's bead on a stage's
lines), `step`, `event`; a `stage` line per stage with its script, log path and summary; each command with its
key, exit code and wall time; the evidence block; `assert` lines with exact expected and observed strings;
per instance: stratum, planted flag, dyad counts per filter, verdict, m, pieces, auditor verdict, referee
outcome with the replay address; `refusal` lines with code, pointer and repair; `metric` lines for wrong
certificates (threshold 0), certified count, and referee wall time; the child-process request and reply
digests; the `cmd` line of `e2e_tier` with its `receipt`; per control its id and `caught`; on failure the first
differing value and the artifacts to reproduce; a `summary` naming the gate, controls caught and the receipt
hash.

referee digests identical run to run.
