## Background

GATE-AUGUR-17 (claim: Known answer; milestone M4; TARGET): "Constructive lanes: the co-design fixed point
equals the GB-03 closed form and rejects the additive-peak rule on the bus fixture; the three-pose dyad and
both circuit fixtures exact; zero certified linkages change circuit on the referee across 240 instances." The
co-design clause is measured by the sibling script (N25d-10). This bead measures the linkage clauses through
the public verb and records the gate once both scripts have run.

The mechanism: the strategy `linkage` of `mission.synthesize` (BD-361) builds planar linkages from precision
poses in exact arithmetic, and certifies for each that all poses lie on one assembly circuit, traversed in
order without crossing a branch point (N25d-18). The last clause of the gate tests the certificate against
physics as the program's most trusted simulator computes it: a linkage that the lane certified is assembled as
a closed chain and driven through its poses on the CPU referee; it must not bind and must not end on the other
circuit.

## Scope

In: fixtures; the 240 frozen instances (the pilot's set, re-used by hash); the script; the referee drive; the
mutants; the refusal row for the symbolic caps; the gate record. Out: the lane; the pilot; the kill decision.

## Technical approach

**Exact fixtures (`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/augur/linkage/`).**

| Fixture | Input | Expected, exact |
|---|---|---|
| `dyad-3pose` | Moving-pivot positions (0, 0), (2, 0), (0, 2) | Fixed pivot (1, 1); radius² = 2 |
| `dyad-collinear` | Positions (0, 0), (1, 0), (2, 0) | Determinant 0; refusal `degenerate: collinear`; no pivot |
| `circuit-a-same` | Ground pivots (0, 0), (4, 0); crank 1, coupler 4, rocker 3; poses (crank 0, B above), (crank π, B above) | Certified on [0, π]; B = (11/3, √80/3) and (11/5, 12/5); determinant bound m = √80 within rounding |
| `circuit-a-defect` | The same linkage; poses (crank 0, B above), (crank π, B below) | `circuit-defect`: the two signs are different circuits; no candidate |
| `circuit-b-same`, `circuit-b-defect` | Ground 4, crank 1, coupler 7/2, rocker 3 (Grashof: 1 + 4 ≤ 7/2 + 3) | B = (73/24, ±√4655/24) at crank 0; certified with m = √4655/8 within rounding for same-side poses; rejected for opposite sides |
| `caps-9var` | A template instance with 9 unknowns | `FROB-REF-857`, exit 3, repair naming a smaller template or the continuation lane |

"Both circuit fixtures" is read as the two linkages above, each with a pass case and a defect case.

**The 240 instances.** The pilot's frozen set (three-pose dyads with a lug catalog and keep-in regions; three
strata of 80; a quarter with planted circuit or branch defects), identified by its hash.

**Script steps.**
1. For each exact fixture: `frob mission synthesize <fixture>.fmission --strategy linkage --json`; compare
   pivots, radii and B coordinates as exact strings (algebraic values by their defining polynomial and an
   enclosure), the verdict, the certificate kinds (`interval-box`, `partition`), and that every candidate has
   `label == "candidate"` and a null verdict.
2. `frob prove verify <bundle> --json` and the standalone `frob-verify` on each emitted certificate; both accept.
3. `frob module check <linkage>.fmodule --json`: mobility 1, ports and contract present, digest matches the one
   the certificates name.
4. For each of the 240 instances: synthesize; for every certified linkage, import the module into a test rig
   design (frame fixed, a position-controlled drive on the crank), `frob sim run --tier referee` driving
   through the poses in order; classify `moves`, `binds` (drive stall or constraint force above the declared
   bound) or `changes circuit` (the sign of the closure determinant at the end differs from the certified one,
   read from telemetry).
5. Mutants, each required to fail the script: `no-circuit-check` (emits the defect designs), `sampled-sign`
   (checks the sign at 16 sample angles; a planted instance with a narrow sign change between samples passes
   it and then changes circuit on the referee), `truncate-caps` (drops the ninth variable), `open-interval`
   (a certificate whose partition omits an end point; the auditor rejects it).

**Gate decision for these rows.** All exact fixtures match; zero of the certified linkages among the 240 are
`binds` or `changes circuit`; every planted defect was refused a certificate; all mutants are caught.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every row of the fixture table matches exactly.
- [ ] Across the 240 instances the count of certified linkages that bind or change circuit is 0, and the count
      of certified linkages is reported per stratum (a gate that certifies nothing is recorded as vacuous and
      does not pass).
- [ ] Every certificate is accepted by `frob-verify` on a host with no GPU; the four mutants are caught.
- [ ] `FROB-REF-857` is returned for the caps fixture with its pointer and first repair.
- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-17 is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with the measured counts from this script and from
      N25d-10, the commit, the hardware fingerprint and the seeds, by a session that did not implement the lane.

with exit code and wall time; the evidence block; `assert` lines with exact expected and observed strings;

certificates (threshold 0), certified count, and referee wall time; the child-process request and reply
digests; per mutant the assertion that caught it; on failure the first differing value and the artifacts to
reproduce; a `summary` naming the gate.

machine; one and eight workers; debug and release; identical module and certificate digests (exact class);
referee digests identical run to run.
