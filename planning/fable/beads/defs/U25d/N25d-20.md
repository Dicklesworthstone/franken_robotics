## Background

A kill gate fixes in advance the condition under which a mechanism is dropped and what ships in its place.
KILL-SYNTH-LINKAGE governs bounded exact linkage synthesis (the strategy `linkage` of `mission.synthesize`
in the crate `frob-synth`): planar linkages built from precision poses in exact arithmetic, each certified to
move through its poses on one assembly circuit.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Trigger | A certified linkage binds or changes circuit on the referee, or more than 25% of template instances exceed the caps |
| Fallback, named in advance | Multi-start numerical optimization of linkage dimensions with the same circuit and clearance check, classed `Tested` |
| Decided | M4, recorded as an ADR |
| Decides on | The pilot's 240 instances (N25d-16); the linkage rows of GATE-AUGUR-17 (N25d-19); the admission state of the symbolic sibling at M4 |

Two different failures are named. The first is a false certificate about a mechanism: the lane's only claim is
its certificate, so one certified linkage that binds or changes circuit when driven on the most trusted
simulator ends the claim. The second is irrelevance: if more than a quarter of the problems the lane is meant
for do not fit inside the symbolic solver's caps (8 variables, 64 equations, degree 256, zero-dimensional
systems only), the exact route is not the useful route.

A third way to the fallback is outside the trigger and is an interim posture, not a kill (DESIGN, from the lane's
own switch-over rule in BD-361 and the program rule that every sibling is the program's own, so a sibling defect
is upstream work and no milestone waits on it): if the symbolic sibling is not admitted at M4, the numerical lane
ships under its own name until the sibling passes admission, and then the exact lane switches over.

## Scope

In: the rule; the ADR; the registry update; re-reading dependents; the specification of the fallback lane, built
as part of this bead if the gate fires. Out: the measurements.

## Technical approach

**Rule (a pure function of the record).**
1. Fire if any certified linkage in the pilot or in the gate run is `binds` or `changes circuit` on the referee,
   unless the cause is shown to be the referee rig (a mis-specified stall bound) and the rerun is clean; one
   repair cycle is allowed and recorded.
2. Fire if the share of template instances outside the caps exceeds 0.25 in the pilot's census.
3. If the symbolic sibling is not admitted at M4 and rules 1 and 2 cannot be evaluated on the product lane, the
   outcome is `deferred` (N40-03), not `fired`: the numerical lane below ships as the interim posture, labelled
   "interim: exact lane awaiting the symbolic sibling's admission", the admission defect is filed as an upstream
   work item in the sibling's repository, and the decision is re-run when the sibling passes admission. The
   ADR names the later exit N40-03 requires of a `deferred` outcome (M5, the next exit, and each later exit
   until the sibling is admitted) with the reason. The pilot's verdict (rules 1 and 2 on the pilot's record)
   still fires the gate if it applies.
4. Otherwise keep (outcome `not-fired`).

**Readiness when the pilot fires (rule 7 of the lifecycle rules, N41-17).** If the pilot's falsifier held, the
linkage mechanism is not funded (N38a-09), so the beads that exist only for it can never become ready: the session
that records the funding decision on the pilot's result (`fundable(WP-67)` = `Blocked{falsifier-holds}`) closes
N25d-17, N25d-18, BD-361 and N25d-19 `withdrawn`, citing the pilot record `PILOT-C-34` and its
`[[pilot.result]]`; that makes this bead ready, its rule fires on the pilot's record, and its ADR lists the four
as "withdrawn before this decision". The numerical lane below uses a circuit pre-check and a determinant
enclosure; in this branch N25d-18 was never built, so the fallback work of this bead builds those two checks to
N25d-18's specification and carries its tests `circuit_defect_detected`, `certificate_same_side` and
`straddle_is_unresolved`. In the gate branch N25d-18 exists and the fallback reuses it ("stands on the
fallback").

When rule 1 fires because the certificate rule itself is wrong (not an implementation defect), the consequence
of one false certificate also applies: the proof kind and checker version involved are disabled until the cause
is found, stored bundles that used them are withdrawn, and the counterexample becomes a permanent fixture of
the mutation corpus.

**Outcomes and downstream.**

| Outcome | Record | Downstream |
|---|---|---|
| Kept | ADR with counts; outcome `not-fired` | `--strategy linkage` ships with `Certified` construction claims; the continuation epic (BD-437) may build on it |
| Deferred (sibling not admitted) | ADR; outcome `deferred`, with the upstream work item | `--strategy linkage` routes to the numerical lane, every linkage `Tested`, the answer says "interim"; switch-over to the exact lane when the sibling is admitted, with the gate's linkage rows re-run then |
| Fired | ADR; outcome `fired`, status `killed`, fallback adopted | `--strategy linkage` routes to the numerical lane below; every linkage is `Tested`; the subprocess solver is not called; the continuation epic is re-read: its fallback "the exact lane plus multi-start recovery" loses the exact lane and stands on multi-start recovery alone |

**The fallback lane (specified here so it can be built without further design).**
- Variables: the linkage dimensions of a template (pivot coordinates and link lengths), boxed by the keep-in
  regions and the catalog lengths.
- Objective: the sum of squared pose errors at the precision poses (loop-closure residuals with the body at each
  pose), plus nothing else.
- Method: 20 starts per instance by address-seeded sampling inside the boxes; a local least-squares solve from
  each (the sibling's optimizers through the airlock, or Gauss–Newton with a step limit); a start is kept when
  its residual is below a declared tolerance (TARGET: 10⁻⁹ m per pose).
- Checks, unchanged from the exact lane: the circuit pre-check (all poses on one side of the closure
  determinant), the enclosure of the determinant along the driving interval over the tolerance box, clearance
  and reach. The enclosures are still computed, but because the dimensions come from a floating-point solve
  the poses are met only to the tolerance; the statement is therefore "a linkage that passes within 10⁻⁹ m of
  its poses on one circuit", and its class is `Tested`.
- Labelling: candidates carry `label: candidate`, class `Tested`, and the assumption "numerical dimensions;
  completeness not claimed" (the lane says nothing about solutions it did not find).
- Output: the same `.fmodule` shape, without `Certified` guarantees.
The same lane serves as the multi-start recovery named in the fallback of the continuation kill gate.

**Record.** The decision ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SYNTH-LINKAGE-M4.md` (every kill-gate decision is
`docs/adr/ADR-<gate-name>-<milestone>.md`; a re-run after a deferral writes `ADR-KILL-SYNTH-LINKAGE-<exit>.md`)
with the fields N40-03 requires: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered and the amendments
applied; each measured value with its receipt hash beside the trigger's number (wrong certificates against 0;
the outside-caps share against 0.25, with its count of 240); the sibling's admission state; the outcome and, for
`deferred`, the later exit and the reason; the fallback now in force and the capability's name from now on
("numerical linkage optimization (Tested)", or "interim: exact lane awaiting the symbolic sibling's admission");
the beads re-read, each "stands on the fallback" or "superseded by this ADR"; what changes downstream; commit,
hardware fingerprint and seeds; the recording session, which did not implement the lane or run the
measurements. The kill row of KILL-SYNTH-LINKAGE in `registries/gates.toml` (`kind = "kill"`) receives
`decision = { outcome, adr, date, session }`; a re-run after a deferral appends a second entry with its `exit`,
and the earlier entry is kept (N40-03).

**Registry and closing.** The kill-gate row gets status, counts and the ADR identifier; this bead closes with the
typed reason `pass` (kept, citing this bead's decision test and its output) or `fallback` (with the ADR and the
fallback's name; N41-17); on `deferred` it stays open with the deferred entry recorded and closes when the
re-run decision is taken. BD-361 is not held open for this decision and is not reopened by it: it closed on its
own acceptance before the measurements this bead reads (it is their dependency), and the ADR's re-read entry for it
states whether it stands on the fallback (rule 5 of N41-17; the fallback lane is this bead's own work). It also
writes the gate's owner declaration, the block N40-30 compares with the ledger: `[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`,
the ledger's current `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied, not restated, and the `decision`
block (outcome, ADR, measured values each citing its receipt); N25d-41's map names this bead as the deciding bead.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `rule_one_circuit_change_fires`; `rule_caps_share`: 60 of 240 keeps (0.25 is not "more than"), 61 fires;
  `rule_sibling_not_admitted_defers` (a clean pilot with the sibling not admitted → `deferred`, never `fired`);
  `rule_sibling_not_admitted_pilot_falsified_fires`; `rule_is_pure`; `rule_matches_ledger_trigger` (the two
  clauses and thresholds 0 and 0.25 equal the ledger row's, N40-26, and the owner declaration's trigger text is
  byte-equal to it); `deferred_names_later_exit` (a `deferred` entry without a later exit and a reason is
  rejected by the register check); `pilot_branch_needs_withdrawn_gate_bead` (a `falsifier-holds` pilot with
  N25d-19 still open makes the decision tool refuse with "gate bead not withdrawn").
- Fallback (only if built): `numeric_dyad_fixture` (positions (0, 0), (2, 0), (0, 2): pivot within 10⁻⁹ of
  (1, 1)); `numeric_circuit_precheck` (the two-circuit design is refused); `numeric_class_is_tested`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_synth_linkage.sh` reads the pilot record, the gate record and the admission record through
`frob evidence trace`, applies the rule and logs each input with its ledger identifier and threshold (0 wrong
certificates; 0.25 outside caps), the outcome and the proposed registry row; it re-runs the rule on the
synthetic 239-clean-one-change record and requires `fire`; it asserts that the kill row's `decision` and the ADR
state the same outcome. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/kill_synth_linkage/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time and the evidence block of the record read; one
`metric` line per clause with the measured value, threshold and verdict and the record digests; a `summary`
names the kill gate; non-zero exit on missing inputs or dangling evidence. If the fallback is in force (fired or deferred), `scripts/e2e/augur_synth_linkage.sh` is re-run in fallback
mode and must pass with its exactness assertions replaced by tolerance assertions and class `Tested`.

### Known answers and negative controls

under both outcomes.

## Considerations

- The fallback keeps the valuable half (the circuit and clearance check) and gives up exactness and the
  `Certified` class; that is the honest description, and the status table must use it.
- A kill here does not fail the milestone: nothing on the critical path needs exact linkages.
