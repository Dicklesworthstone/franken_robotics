## Background

A kill gate fixes in advance when a mechanism is dropped and what ships instead; its decision is an ADR at its
milestone. KILL-SYNTH-CONT governs the Moonshot continuation lane of Augur's constructive crate `frob-synth`
(`--strategy continuation`): all isolated solutions of a mechanism family by parameter homotopy from one
generic solve, each root certified by an exact α-test, the set labelled `Complete` or `Partial`.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Trigger | More than 0.1% lost paths, an unflagged lost path, or a missed known count |
| Fallback, named in advance | The exact lane plus multi-start CPU recovery; every result labelled `Partial` |
| Decided | M8, recorded as the ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-SYNTH-CONT-M8.md` with the fields the kill-gate record bead N40-03 requires |
| Decides on | The pilot on Stewart–Gough forward kinematics (N25d-21); GATE-AUGUR-22 (N25d-24); the state of KILL-SYNTH-LINKAGE (N25d-20), which fixes what "the exact lane" means |

The three triggers are three ways the lane's only distinctive claim, completeness, can be false or
unsupported. Many lost paths: the count cannot be trusted. One unflagged lost path: the accounting itself is
unsound, so no label can be trusted. A missed known count (fewer than 40 certified roots on a generic
Stewart–Gough instance; fewer than 16 for general 6R inverse kinematics if that family is run): the engine
does not find what is known to exist.

## Scope

In: the rule; the ADR; the registry update; re-reading dependents; the fallback as work. Out: the measurements.

## Technical approach

**Inputs.** From the pilot and the gate run: paths tracked and lost (targets and monodromy loops separately),
the flagged list, the accounting identity (tracked = regular + singular + at-infinity + failed), certified
counts per parameter set, labels, the trace residuals, and the outcome of the planted controls (a removed start
solution must give `Partial(39, 1)`).

**Rule (a pure function of the record).**
1. Fire if the lost-path share over all tracked paths exceeds 0.001 after one repair cycle.
2. Fire if any lost path is absent from the flagged list, or the accounting identity fails on any run.
3. Fire if any generic parameter set of a family with a known count (the planted controls excluded) certifies
   fewer roots than the known count, whatever its label: a missed known count is a certified count below 40 on a
   generic Stewart–Gough instance (N10c-05), and a `Partial` label records the miss but does not repair it. A
   `Complete` label on such a set also meets rule 2's unsoundness.
4. Fire if the pilot's falsifier held (a lost path the trace test did not flag, or `Complete` on a run that
   missed a root); the product lane is then not built.
5. If the owner did not fund the epic (OPEN-AUGUR-10), the outcome is `not-reached` (N40-03), recorded as
   "not funded", never `fired` (DESIGN, reason: a kill records that a measured mechanism failed its trigger; an
   unfunded mechanism was never measured, and recording it as killed would lose it from the queue of parked
   ideas). Nothing of the lane is built while the gate is not reached; the decision is taken if the epic is
   funded later.
6. Otherwise keep (outcome `not-fired`).

**Outcomes and downstream.**

| Outcome | Record | Downstream |
|---|---|---|
| Kept | ADR with counts; outcome `not-fired` | The strategy ships behind its feature flag; `Complete` labels may be issued; Study-quadric synthesis, queued behind this epic, may take the freed slot for its own pilot |
| Not funded | ADR; outcome `not-reached` | The strategy is not offered (`FROB-REF-724`, exit 5, naming the unfunded epic and the fallback); `FROB-REF-857`'s second repair names multi-start recovery labelled `Partial` where the numerical linkage lane exists, and is otherwise omitted; the epic returns to the parked register with its pilot record kept |
| Fired | ADR; outcome `fired`, status `killed`, fallback adopted | The strategy routes to the fallback; `Complete` is removed from the label set; every result is `Partial`; `FROB-REF-857`'s second repair reads "multi-start recovery, labelled `Partial`"; the Moonshot slot is freed; Study-quadric synthesis is not started |

**The fallback as work.** "The exact lane plus multi-start CPU recovery; every result labelled `Partial`":
(1) inside the symbolic caps, the exact lane answers as before (if KILL-SYNTH-LINKAGE fired, this half is
absent and the fallback is multi-start recovery alone, as that gate's ADR says); (2) beyond the caps,
multi-start recovery: many address-seeded starts in the unknowns' boxes, a local Newton or least-squares solve
from each, deduplication of converged points by distance, α-certification of each distinct point with the
existing certifier (a certified root is still a true root); (3) the label is always `Partial(c, 0)` with the
note that completeness is not claimed and the number of starts used; (4) such a result can support inclusion
of the designs found and never an exclusion. The numerical multi-start lane specified for the linkage kill
gate is reused; what this bead adds is the routing, the label change and the tests.

**Registry and closing.** The kill-gate row gets status, measured values and the ADR identifier, and its kill row
receives `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session }` (N40-03). This bead closes with a typed reason of N41-17's
set: `pass` (kept, outcome `not-fired`, citing this bead's decision test and its output), `fallback` (fired, with
the ADR and the fallback's name), or `withdrawn: not funded: OPEN-AUGUR-10` (outcome `not-reached`, citing the
ADR and N25d-49's decision; if the epic is funded later, the decision is taken again as a new entry of the kill
row). The epic closes on the capability or on the fallback by name. It also writes the gate's owner declaration,
the block N40-30 compares with the ledger: `[[gate]]` with `role = "owner"`, the ledger's current `trigger`,
`fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied, not restated, and the `decision` block (outcome, ADR, measured
values each citing its receipt), in `frob-synth`'s `contract.toml` (the place N25d-41 names for the chapter's
declarations); N25d-41's map names this bead as the deciding bead.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rule is applied to recorded values, each cited by ledger identifier; a repair cycle, if used, is
      recorded.
- [ ] The ADR states the lost share (targets and monodromy separately), flagged and unflagged counts, certified
      counts against known counts, control outcomes, the funding state, and the outcome.
- [ ] If fired: no code path can emit `Complete`; multi-start recovery on the two-conic family at p = (5, 2)
      finds the four roots (1, 2), (2, 1), (−1, −2), (−2, −1) from 64 starts and labels them `Partial(4, 0)`;
      the status table names the fallback.
- [ ] Dependents re-read: the epic, the M8 exit review and the queued Study-quadric item find the ADR.
- [ ] Negative control of the rule: a synthetic record with 4,000 tracked paths, 3 lost and all flagged keeps
      (0.075%); with 5 lost it fires (0.125%); with 1 lost and unflagged it fires.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `rule_lost_share`; `rule_unflagged_fires`; `rule_accounting_identity`; `rule_missed_count` (39 certified
  under a `Complete` label fires; 39 under `Partial(39, 1)` on one of the ten generic gate sets fires; 39 under
  `Partial(39, 1)` on the planted `drop-start` control does not, since that is the control behaving correctly);
  `rule_not_funded_is_not_reached` (never `fired`); `rule_is_pure`.
- Fallback (only if built): `multistart_two_conics`; `fallback_never_complete`; `fallback_never_excludes`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/kill_synth_cont.sh` reads the pilot and gate records through `frob evidence trace`, applies the
rule and logs each input with its ledger identifier and threshold (0.001 lost share; 0 unflagged; known counts
40 and 16), the outcome and the proposed registry row; it re-runs the rule on the three synthetic records of the
acceptance criteria and requires keep, fire, fire; it asserts that the kill row's `decision` and the ADR state
the same outcome. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/kill_synth_cont/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript: every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time
and the evidence block of the record read, with the records' content hashes and the ADR path; one `metric` line
per clause with the measured value, threshold and verdict; on a mismatch between the recomputed outcome and the
ADR, the first differing input with its ledger identifier; a `summary` names the kill gate; non-zero exit on
missing inputs or dangling evidence. In fallback mode `scripts/e2e/augur_synth_continuation.sh` is re-run with label assertions changed to
`Partial`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-655 for the known counts; the synthetic records above.

## Considerations

- The lane is off the critical path; a kill here changes nothing in M0 to M7.
- The 0.1% threshold applies to the whole of what is tracked; on 400 target paths alone it allows none.
