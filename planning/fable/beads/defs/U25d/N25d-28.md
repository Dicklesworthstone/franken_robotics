## Background

Slice 2 of Augur's delivery order asks whether a mission answer survives amendment in the way the semantics
promise. The slice: amend the stance, material and load-sharing assumptions through one schema. The evidence
that buys the next investment: one edit preserves a valid witness; a second forces withdrawal; a third changes
the required instrument; all three match independent full recomputation. The decision if it fails: if
uncertainty in the physical contract dominates, spend the next increment on measurement, or settle for a
narrower envelope.

Why these three. They are the three things an amendment can do to evidence, and each has a rule. A hash
identifies an object; only an implication licenses skipping a recheck. Assumption sets are ordered, and each
transport of a verdict across an edit has a direction: narrowing the disturbance set preserves an inclusion and
requires an exclusion to be rechecked; changing the authority that realizes a witness invalidates the witness;
enlarging the set of admitted mechanisms can leave a probe unable to separate them. Feasibility and optimality
are separate obligations: a feasible witness can survive an edit that moves the optimum.

The fixtures are the two exact ones of the chapter. The stance family: a robot carries 400 N on four point
feet at (±1/3, ±b) m with 160 N ratings, its centre of mass anywhere in a 150 mm by 75 mm half-dimension
rectangle; with u = 3 and v = 1/b the vertex (60, 30) N·m is feasible exactly when v ≤ 14/3, so the minimum
half-width is 3/14 m. The pad probe: four synthetic relaxation laws V, P, C and a withheld law D; a probe
generated for {V, P, C} (radius ratio 2 at 125 ms) is right in 675 of 675 admitted cases and wrong in 225 of
225 cases of D, whose early trace equals P's; the direct measurement at mission dwell is right in 900 of 900.

## Scope

In: the three amendments as typed edits; the revalidation run and the independent full recomputation for each;
the comparison; the slice record. Out: the revalidation engine (BD-292); the stance and probe mechanisms
(BD-130, BD-291); the warehouse surface-law experiment (N25d-29).

## Technical approach

Each amendment is one typed edit to a `.fmission` (or to a card it references), applied with `--after-edit`,
followed by (a) `frob mission revalidate <answer> --after-edit <edit>` and (b) a fresh
`frob mission synthesize` (or `distinguish`) of the amended mission in a clean store. The two must agree cell
by cell, proof by proof in verdict and class. The agreement is the test.

**Amendment 1: a witness is kept, an exclusion is rechecked, a minimum moves.** Narrow the payload envelope
along x from 150 mm to 120 mm (first moment hull (±48, ±30) N·m in place of (±60, ±30)).
- Polarity: narrowing the disturbance set preserves inclusions and requires exclusions to be rechecked.
- Expected from revalidation: the inner cell v ∈ [2, 14/3] survives with its witnesses untouched (no recheck:
  the old load vertices cover the new hull); the excluded cell (14/3, 6] is rechecked.
- Expected new answer: with r = (400 + 3·48)/2 = 272 and s = 200 + 15·v the binding pair becomes
  72 + 15·v ≤ 160, so the boundary moves to v = 88/15, that is b = 15/88 m. Cells: inner [2, 88/15], excluded
  (88/15, 6]. At v = 88/15 the vertex (48, 30) is met by forces (160, 112, 128, 0) N.
- Optimality: the old minimum 3/14 m is withdrawn (it is still feasible and is no longer the minimum); the new
  certified minimum is 15/88 m with a matching lower bound.

**Amendment 2: a cell is withdrawn.** Change the authority model from `independent-normal-force` to
`passive-equal-spring` at half-width 1/4 m (v = 4).
- Under independent forces the allocation (155, 135, 105, 5) N balances the vertex (60, 30) inside the 160 N
  ratings. Four equal springs on rigid planar ground add the compatibility equation f₊₊ + f₋₋ = f₊₋ + f₋₊ and
  give (175, 115, 85, 25) N: 15 N over the rating, with every foot still loaded.
- Expected: revalidation withdraws the inner cell that contains v = 4 with a logged withdrawal naming its
  counterexample (the vertex and the passive forces); the full recomputation under the passive authority
  excludes it. The old certificate offered for the amended query is rejected by the checker (it assumes an
  authority the design no longer declares).

**Amendment 3: the required instrument changes.** Admit law D into the mechanism set of the pad mission.
- Before: `mission.distinguish` returns the three-law probe (radius ratio 2 at 125 ms, cost 0.375 s) as the
  cheapest separating intervention, with the direct measurement listed.
- After: the three-law probe no longer separates decisions (D follows P until a quarter of the time constant
  and implies the opposite pad). Expected: the previous probe verdicts are withdrawn as conditional on a
  mechanism set that no longer holds; the returned instrument is one that separates all four laws, or, where
  none of the offered probes does, the direct measurement at mission dwell (1.25 s with the archived reading).
  The script accepts either and records which; it fails if the three-law probe is still returned as sufficient.

**Record.** For each amendment: cells preserved, withdrawn, rechecked and recomputed; agreement with the full
recomputation (required: exact); the cost of revalidation against the cost of recomputation (reported, no
threshold); and the slice's status for the register. If the three amendments cannot be expressed as edits to
one schema, that is itself the failure the slice's decision speaks to and is recorded as such.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All three amendments are expressed as typed edits to the same mission schema; no fixture is rewritten by
      hand between runs.
- [ ] Amendment 1: inner cell preserved without recheck; boundary 15/88 m exact; minimum 3/14 m withdrawn and
      15/88 m certified; equals full recomputation.
- [ ] Amendment 2: the cell at v = 4 is withdrawn with its counterexample (175, 115, 85, 25) N; the stale
      certificate is rejected; equals full recomputation.
- [ ] Amendment 3: the three-law probe is not returned as sufficient; the returned instrument decides all
      admitted laws; equals full recomputation.
- [ ] Zero incorrectly inherited verdicts across the three; every withdrawal is logged with its reason.
- [ ] Negative controls: a revalidation build that keys on hashes only re-derives everything (no preserved
      cell) and is flagged as providing no reuse; a build that inherits exclusions under a narrowed disturbance
      set keeps the stale boundary 3/14 and fails amendment 1; a build that ignores the authority field keeps
      the cell in amendment 2.
- [ ] The slice record is written for the M3 exit review.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/augur_slice2.rs`: `narrowed_hull_boundary` (88/15; forces (160, 112, 128, 0)
sum to 400 and meet both moments: (160 + 112 − 128 − 0)/3 = 48 and (160 − 112 + 128 − 0)·(15/88) = 30);
`passive_spring_forces` (t = 175 from 2t − 150 = 550 − 2t); `probe_reversal_counts` (675 of 675; 0 of 225);
`polarity_table_rows` (narrow disturbance: inclusion preserved, exclusion rechecked).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/augur_slice2_amendments.sh`. Log: per amendment the edit, both commands with exit codes and wall
times, the evidence blocks, per cell the old verdict, the revalidated verdict and the recomputed verdict,
withdrawal records with counterexamples, the checker's rejection of each stale bundle with its first failing
rule, cost of both routes; on failure the first cell that differs; a `summary` with the slice status.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-624 (boundary 3/14 m; forces (160, 130, 110, 0)), KA-626 (passive springs), KA-639 (probe counts); CX-273
(a feasible allocation exists, so the body is admitted), CX-279 (the probe separates every admitted mechanism,
so its verdict is safe). The three control builds above. Determinism: answers and bundles identical twice on
one machine and across worker counts (exact class).

## Considerations

- The new boundary 15/88 m is derived here from the fixture's own inequality and gets a derivation file before
  the script may support the slice record.
- Reuse cost is reported, not gated: a correctly keyed dependency graph is expected to tie on these toys.
- If the physical contract's uncertainty dominates (the amendments mostly end in unresolved cells), the slice's
  pre-declared decision applies: measurement first, or a narrower envelope.
