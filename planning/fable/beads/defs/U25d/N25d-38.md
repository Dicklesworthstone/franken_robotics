## Background

The Augur chapter (mission queries over families of designs) lists 33 known answers: fixtures whose truth is
exact under a stated model and hand-checkable. The M1 rows are in the sibling battery N25d-37. This bead
carries the fifteen rows whose mechanisms arrive at M3 and M4: sensing limits (alias witnesses), a body that
removes the need for a sensor (the passive jaw), probe and instrument design, additive pad families, product
dominance, joint resource contracts, label queries, the mission-observable quotient, the constructive lanes,
and strict predicates on devices.

Rules for a row (from the verification program): a derivation on file; a tolerance with a reason; a registered
negative control (a row printed with `none stated` cannot support a gate until one is registered); a tier and
a site. Printed values are not improved in transit. Two rows (633, 634) are first asserted at M3 by the alias
and jaw gate's own bead; they are re-asserted here so that the battery is complete in one script.

## Scope

In: fixtures, derivation notes, registry entries, controls and the script for the fifteen rows. Out: the
mechanisms and their gates' measurement beads.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every row has a fixture, an `expect.json`, a derivation note and a registered control; no registry row
      carries `none stated`.
- [ ] Every value in the table is asserted through the public verbs (`mission.refute`, `mission.distinguish`,
      `mission.synthesize` with its strategies, its label queries (BD-363, with N25c-36's encoder premise for
      KA-646), `--quotient on` for KA-648 (BD-357) and the device screen of BD-355; the pad family of KA-640
      through N25c-14's query), exactly or to the printed digits where the table prints decimals.
- [ ] Each control is caught in the same run.
- [ ] KA-656 gives identical decision bytes on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal, with zero final errors against exact
      CPU arithmetic, and the unguarded build shows 452 and 23,438.
- [ ] The M3 rows (633, 634) were already asserted by their gate's bead; this script re-asserts them.
- [ ] The battery is cited as fixture source by the measurement beads of the gates these rows support.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/augur_ka_m4.rs`: one first-principles test per row in exact rationals or exact
enumeration: `ka_633_majority` (27/32, 459/512, 3807/4096; sequential rule 9/10 with 16/5 expected reads;
27/512 still undecided after six reads and 225/256 when forced), `ka_634_jaw_bounds` (28/17; 4/3),
`ka_639_forces`, `ka_640_eccentric`, `ka_641_joint_outcomes`, `ka_643_schedules`, `ka_644_triple_empty`
(x + y ≥ 4/3 against x + y ≤ 1), `ka_645_fixed_map_needs_three`, `ka_646_shared_record`, `ka_647_widths`,
`ka_648_classes` (735,471 and 24,546), `ka_652_mean_square_radius`, `ka_653_circumcentre`, `ka_654_closure`,
`ka_656_tallies`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/augur_known_answers_m4.sh`: per row, first the battery entry (`FROB_TEST_SEED=1 cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- KA-6nn`; KA-656 also under `--test g5_determinism -- KA-656` on each
host and device), then the row's public-verb command with its comparison against `expect.json` (every run verb
with its own key, `--key ka-6nn-<run>`, so a row's re-run and its control never return each other's receipt),
then its control (`FROB_TEST_SEED=1 cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test
g1_known_answers -- KA-6nn`, which must fail on the planted defect; for KA-652 and KA-653 the additional
controls as well). Logging through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/augur_known_answers_m4/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line carries
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25d-38`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`); `cmd`
lines the KA number, the exact command, exit code, wall time and the evidence block (class, tier, determinism,
identities); `assert` lines expected and observed values as exact strings (decimals to the printed digits), the
control and its outcome; digests per backend for KA-656; on failure the first differing value with the fixture
path; a `summary` with rows passed, failed, controls caught and gates touched.

### Known answers and negative controls
The rows above. Counterexamples behind the controls: CX-271 (pairwise compatible variants), CX-277 (separate
projections), CX-278 (per-component limits and the additive peak rule), CX-279 (probe), CX-280 (a root is not
a mechanism), CX-284 (unguarded single precision), CX-286 (repeated reads). Determinism lanes: exact rows
twice (the second run with its own keys) and debug against release through the verbs; 1, 2 and 8 workers in
process in `ka_m4_workers_lane` (STD-TEST's `testkit::det::lane`), since no mission verb carries a worker
option; KA-656 across backends by digest.

## Considerations

- The probe forces are printed to two decimals; their tolerance is "to the printed digits" and the derivation
  note gives more digits and the rounding.
- Rows whose mechanism was withdrawn by its kill gate keep their fixtures: the truth of a known answer does
  not depend on the mechanism, and the fallback must reproduce it or state that it cannot.
