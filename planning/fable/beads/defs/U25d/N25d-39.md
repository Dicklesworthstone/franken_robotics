## Background

The Augur chapter (mission queries over families of designs) lists 33 known answers, registered as 35 rows of
the program's known-answer suite. The M1 rows are in N25d-37 and the M3 and M4 rows in N25d-38. This bead
carries the last five, whose mechanisms are Frontier and Moonshot epics of M6 and M8: locomotion by geometric
phase (the holonomy query), admission of a no-slip connection, the rule that a Lie-bracket direction is not a
reachable direction, the information fixture of a two-robot handoff, and the solution counts used by the
continuation lane.

Rules for a row: a derivation on file; a tolerance with a reason (one of these rows is printed with the
tolerance `to set`, and is not eligible to pass until its derivation file states one); a registered negative
control (three of these rows are printed with `none stated`); a tier and a site. The epics' own gate beads
assert these rows when they land (the holonomy rows at M6); this battery completes the registry and re-asserts
them in one script at M8.

## Scope

In: fixtures, derivation notes, registry entries, tolerances and controls for the five rows; the script. Out:
the mechanisms and their gates.

## Technical approach

| KA | Fixture | Exact answer | Exercises | Tolerance | Negative control (registered) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KA-649 | Differential-drive loop: two wheels of radius ρ at half-track w with a free caster, ideal rolling; the square loop (left wheel forward by angle δ, right forward by δ, left back by δ, right back by δ) | Sideways displacement 4w·(1 − cos θ) with θ = ρ·δ/(2w); zero net yaw and zero net advance; the reversed loop gives the opposite displacement; leading order ρ²·δ²/(2w) | Holonomy query | *Set here, as N25-KA proposes*: absolute error at most 10⁻⁹ m on the sideways displacement on the host, and loop-reversal and chart-change residuals below 10⁻⁸ m on the referee, the thresholds of the holonomy gate | *Registered here (none was stated), the two controls N25-KA proposes*: a loop integrator that ignores the orientation of the loop, which the reversed loop must catch (it must give the opposite sign); and the leading-order formula ρ²·δ²/(2w) used as the truth: at θ = 1 rad it gives 2w·θ² = 2w against 4w·(1 − cos 1) = 1.8388·w, an 8.8% error the row must catch |
| KA-650 | Skid-steer base (four fixed-axis wheels, fore and aft axles at ±a) under a no-slip connection | ω = 0: exact lateral no-slip requires v_y + a·ω = 0 and v_y − a·ω = 0, which forces ω = 0 for a > 0; its yaw needs a separately qualified slip model | Connection admission | exact | Admitting the connection for a skid-steer base (a build that returns a yaw-producing gait under no-slip) |
| KA-651 | ẋ = u with u ∈ [0, 1] | Bracket rank one; negative x is unreachable from zero | Finite witness rule | exact | Bracket rank read as reachability |
| KA-642 | Handoff bit: robot A sees the payload bit; robot B must choose its matching latch before observing A | A pooled-history planner wins both cases; every deterministic local choice for B loses one; delivering the bit to B by its commitment restores both | Decision makers | exact (verdict) | A late bit: delivery after B's commitment must leave the mission infeasible |
| KA-655 | Stewart–Gough forward kinematics; general 6R inverse kinematics | 40 and 16 solutions (FACT, literature; to be re-read from the primary source) | Continuation labels | exact (count) | *Registered here*: one start solution removed from the generic set; the run must be labelled `Partial(39, 1)` and must not report 40 |

**Derivations the notes must contain.** For KA-649: with the right wheel held, advancing the left wheel by
angle δ rolls an arc ρ·δ and rotates the body by θ = ρ·δ/(2w) about the right wheel's contact; composing the
four moves on SE(2) in order gives a pure translation perpendicular to the heading of magnitude
4w·(1 − cos θ); expanding gives 2w·θ² = ρ²·δ²/(2w), the Lie bracket of the two wheel fields; reversing the
order of the four moves negates it. Numeric instance for the script: ρ = 1/10 m, w = 1/4 m, δ = 5/2 rad gives
θ = 1/2 rad and a displacement of 1 − cos(1/2) = 0.122417… m (4w = 1). For KA-650: the two lateral constraints
subtract to 2a·ω = 0. For KA-651: the reachable set from 0 is x ≥ 0 at every time, although the single input
field spans the line. For KA-642: enumerate B's two deterministic choices against the two bits. For KA-655:
the source of each count, re-read and cited.

**Sites.** KA-649 on host and referee (the closed form on host; the same loop rolled on the CPU referee within
the gate's tolerance); the others on host; KA-642 also through the auditor (a finite game and an alias pair);
KA-655 at G2 on host through the continuation lane.

**Registry.** Entries in `known_answers.toml` with the tolerances and controls above; the row for KA-649 loses
its `to set` marker only when its derivation note is committed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Five fixtures, `expect.json` files and derivation notes exist; KA-649's tolerance is stated in its note.
- [ ] No registry row carries `none stated` or `to set` after this bead.
- [ ] Each row is asserted through the public surface of its epic (`mission.synthesize` or `mission.refute` with
      the holonomy objective; connection admission; the handoff mission; `--strategy continuation`), and each
      control is caught.
- [ ] A row whose epic was killed or not funded is recorded `not run` with the ADR's identifier, never passed;
      its host-only closed-form check still runs.
- [ ] The count 40 (and 16) is confirmed from a primary source or the row is blocked.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/augur_ka_late.rs` (host, first principles): `ka_649_square_loop` (compose four
SE(2) motions exactly in symbolic cos and sin of θ: translation (0, ±4w·(1 − cos θ)) up to the heading
convention, rotation identity; numeric check at θ = 1/2); `ka_649_reverse_negates`; `ka_649_small_angle_is_not_exact`
(8.8% at θ = 1); `ka_649_orientation_blind_integrator_caught` (the mutant gives the same sign for the
reversed loop and fails `ka_649_reverse_negates`); `ka_650_no_slip_forces_zero_yaw`; `ka_651_reachable_set_nonnegative`;
`ka_642_local_choices_lose_one`; `ka_655_counts_cited`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/augur_known_answers_late.sh [--milestone M6|M8]` (the form of N25d-38's script): per row, first the
battery entry (`FROB_TEST_SEED=1 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- KA-6nn`; KA-655 under
`--test g2_parity`, its site being G2; KA-649's referee roll also under `--test g5_determinism -- KA-649`), then
the row's public-verb command named in its fixture's `expect.json`, with its comparison (every run verb with its
own key, `--key ka-6nn-<run>`, so that a row's re-run and its control never return each other's receipt), then
its control (`FROB_TEST_SEED=1 cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test <tier> --
KA-6nn`, which must fail on the planted defect). A row whose mechanism's verb does not exist in the build (its
epic not funded, or withdrawn by its kill gate) is logged `pending` with the ADR that says so, never passed; its
battery entry and control still run. Logging through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/augur_known_answers_late/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line carries
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25d-39`), `step` (the KA number and stage), `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`,
`refusal`, `end`); `cmd` lines the exact command, exit code, wall time and the evidence block (claim Known answer,
class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `assert` lines expected and observed values as exact strings
(decimals to the printed digits) with the tolerance, and the control and its outcome; for KA-649 the reversal and
chart-change residuals and the referee digests of the two rolls; for KA-655 the label and the certified and lost
counts; on failure the first differing value with the fixture path; a `summary` with rows passed, pending,
not-run and failed, controls caught, and the gates touched (GATE-AUGUR-22).

### Known answers and negative controls
The five rows. Counterexample behind KA-651's control: CX-281 (a Lie-bracket direction is reachable). The
controls registered here close the `none stated` cells of KA-649 (two: the orientation-blind integrator and the
small-angle truth) and KA-655 and give KA-642 its late-bit mutation. Determinism lanes: host rows twice, debug and release; the referee roll of KA-649 twice with
identical digests.

## Considerations

- The heading convention decides the sign and the axis name of the sideways displacement; the derivation note
  fixes the frame so that "sideways" is unambiguous.
- These rows sit behind epics that may be killed. Their closed forms remain true and remain in the registry.
- The holonomy gate also needs one newly proposed gait admitted by a full referee rollout; that is not a known
  answer and is not in this battery.
