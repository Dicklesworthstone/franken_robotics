## Background

The program funds no Frontier [F] or Moonshot [M] mechanism before a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier has
run: small enough for a CPU in a day, registered before it runs, with an outcome not known in advance, and
with the ceiling of the benefit stated before any speed test. The pilot register
(`frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`) holds one record per mechanism: the mechanism and tier, the protocol
frozen before execution, the falsifier, the computed ceiling, the fallback and kill gate, the cost, the tool
and its pin, and then the result. A work package whose pilot's falsifier holds is not funded; its fallback
ships. A bead for an [F] or [M] mechanism is not ready until its pilot has a recorded result.

Augur (mission queries and inverse design) is almost entirely Frontier and Moonshot, so its rows are many. Some
pilots were run at plan time and exist as small programs in the planning reproduction archive; "run" in the
table below means that paper-scale pilot exists, and its integrated counterpart is still required by the gate
named. The others are still to run. Every plan-time pilot's strong baseline tied or won, which is why most
ceilings read "none claimed".

This bead enters the rows. It runs nothing; the carriers do.

## Scope

In: 21 rows from the chapter's table and one row this drafting adds (the two small exact constructions, for
which the chapter names no pilot); the linkage test that ties mechanism beads to pilot rows. Out: running any
pilot; the register's schema and the funding gate (see `needs`); the kill decisions.

**Record fields (as the register's owner defines them; values this bead supplies).** `mechanism`, `tier`,
`section`, `protocol` (text and hash; for the plan-time pilots, the path inside the reproduction archive),
`falsifier`, `ceiling`, `fallback`, `kill_gate`, `cost`, `tool` and `pin`, `status`, `result`, `carrier_beads`,
`integrated_gate`. For the plan-time pilots the `result` is first filled from the recorded values, marked
`recorded, not re-run`, and overwritten by the re-run beads at M0.

**Linkage test.** `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0 -- registry::augur_pilots`:
1. All 22 rows are present once, each with a non-empty falsifier, ceiling and fallback (a row whose ceiling is
   unknown says `unknown` explicitly, as rows 13 and 15 do).
2. Every kill gate named exists in the gate registry.
3. Every bead of an [F] or [M] Augur mechanism names a pilot row; a mechanism bead whose row has no result is
   reported `not ready` by the status generator.
4. A row with status `run (paper-scale)` names its integrated gate, and that gate's status is not inferred
   from the pilot.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The 22 rows are in `pilots.toml` with the texts above; the registry check passes.
- [ ] No row's result is a product measurement; the plan-time rows are marked `recorded, not re-run` until
      BD-041, BD-042 and BD-043 overwrite them.
- [ ] The status generator prints an [F] or [M] Augur mechanism as `not ready` while its pilot row has no
      result (GATE-GAUNT-08's rule, exercised on these rows).
- [ ] Negative controls: a row with an empty falsifier fails the registry check; a mechanism bead with no pilot
      row fails it; a pilot result copied into a gate's `measured` field is rejected.
- [ ] Row 22 is marked as added in drafting, so that the chapter's owner can confirm or remove it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`registry::augur_pilots` as specified: `rows_complete` (22), `falsifier_ceiling_fallback_present`,
`kill_gates_exist`, `mechanism_beads_have_rows`, `pilot_result_is_not_gate_measurement`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/augur_pilot_register.sh`: runs the registry test and `frob doctor --registries --json` (the
status generator's view); logs per row its status, kill gate, carrier and integrated gate, each check's
outcome, and a `summary`. It is re-run at each funding decision of an Augur work package.

### Known answers and negative controls
The three planted registry defects above. The recorded plan-time values carried into the rows: ratios 1.345
warm and 1.088 cold with ceiling 1.46; six of six corrupted certificates rejected; 735,471 placements in
24,546 classes; probe ceilings 8, 3.33 and 1.

## Considerations

- The probe ceiling: with setup h, common validation cost v and engineering cost e per decision, the ratio of
  direct to probe cost is (h + 1 + v + e_direct)/(h + 1/8 + v + e_probe): at most 8 at zero overhead, 3.33 at
  h = 0.25 s, and 1 against a programmed expert who chooses the same schedule.
- The owner decides which Moonshot rows are funded and in what order, under the cap of four active Moonshot
  items (decision bead N25d-49).
- A pilot result changes a mechanism's tier and scope in its bead; a negative result is recorded, not hidden.
