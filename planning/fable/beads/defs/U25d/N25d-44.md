## Background

A mission answer is a pair of design sets, an inner set I of designs proved to work and an outer set O outside
which designs are proved not to work, with unresolved cells between. A cell enters I by the second of four
proof rules, **constructive inclusion**: supply one causal controller, an admissible contact schedule, event
guards, and *a sound reachable tube or an exact witness for the whole cell*; check actuator bounds, material
domains and the mission predicate over the enclosure; at an uncertain mode boundary, branch conservatively or
return unresolved. A reachable tube is a set-valued enclosure of every state the closed loop can reach over
the horizon, for every admitted world and every admitted branch of set-valued contact.

FACT at plan time: exact witnesses exist for static, quasi-static and finite-game cells. Sound reachable tubes
for contact-rich full-order cells exist in no sibling library. What the suite has (`frankensim/crates/`, to be
re-read at the pinned commit): `fs-ivl` with outward-rounded intervals, affine forms, multivariate Taylor
models and interval Newton; `fs-sos` with sum-of-squares programs and a verifier, whose region-of-attraction
routine uses a fixed quadratic Lyapunov function with a largest example of two states. Published scale
(FACT, literature, to be re-read): sum-of-squares certificates reach about five to ten states; a diagonally
dominant relaxation reached 30 states only for balancing with pinned feet and no input limits.

OPEN-AUGUR-5 is therefore open by necessity. Owner: the Augur lead. **Fail-closed default: constructive
inclusion is limited to static, quasi-static, finite-game and low-dimensional smooth cells; contact-rich
cells stay unresolved, with `Tested` trajectories attached.** Revisited at M4, when family queries run at
scale and the cost of the default can be counted. The default removes verdicts and adds none: a referee
trajectory can only remove a cell from I (it hunts counterexamples); it never puts one in.

## Scope

In: the census of unresolved cells by cause on the M3 and M4 mission fixtures; the re-survey of owners at the
pinned commits; the options with their costs; the decision rule; the ADR and register row. Out: building any
tube machinery (if the decision widens inclusion, that is a new work package with its own pilot and kill gate,
not this bead); reachable-set tables for shields (BD-440).

## Technical approach

**1. Census (`docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-AUGUR-5.census.json`, DESIGN path, beside the decision record it feeds: every
decision record and its inputs live in `docs/adr/`).** For every stored mission answer of the
M3 and M4 gates (stance, docking, pusher, alias and jaw, separation, dominance, labels, the 1,024-design
family batch, the region-certificate box), list each unresolved cell with its explanation and compute:
- U = unresolved cells; U_tube = those whose only explanation is `ModeSequenceUnenclosed` (the mode sequence
  of the cell's executions cannot be enclosed) and that carry at least one `Tested` trajectory;
- by family: U_tube / U, and the measure of those cells under the query's own `resolution` measure;
- D_small = the number of U_tube cells whose guard-ambiguous subsystem has at most four reduced states (a
  threshold crossing, a tray, braking on mixed tiles, a door pinch zone are the plan's examples).

**2. Re-survey.** At the pinned commit of each sibling, search for: validated integration (Taylor-model
flowpipes), hybrid reachability with guard enclosure, invariant-set or barrier certificates above two states.
Record path, function, largest demonstrated state dimension, and whether the result is a certificate that a
checker written against the standard library could recompute (a result that only its producer can verify
changes nothing: proofs are checked by an independent program).

**3. Options.**

| Option | What it admits | What it needs | Cost and risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (default) | Inclusion on static, quasi-static, finite-game and low-dimensional smooth cells | Nothing new | Contact-rich cells stay unresolved; answers on dynamic missions are mostly `Tested` |
| B | Inclusion on cells whose ambiguity is confined to a subsystem of at most four reduced states, by a mode-indexed table of cells K_m with one common safe action per cell, satisfying R_mn((K_m ⊕ E) ∩ G_mn, D) ⊆ K_n ⊖ (−E) for every enabled guard G_mn (R_mn the reset, D the disturbance set, E the sensing-error set, ⊖ erosion) | A proof kind `tube-inclusion` in the closed registry with a checking rule; a proof, not an assumption, that the full body reduces to those states on the cell | The reduction is an assumption watched by monitors and is not proved; without that proof the cell is at best `Tested`. The table is a Moonshot item due at M8 |
| C | Inclusion by a full-order enclosure from a sibling | A sibling that provides one, admitted through the airlock, with a checkable certificate | None exists at plan time |

**4. Decision rule (DESIGN proposal, fixed before the census is read).** Keep A unless both hold: (i) the
re-survey finds an owner, or option B's reduction proof exists for at least one fixture, with a certificate
the independent checker can recompute; (ii) U_tube / U ≥ 25% on at least one family due at M4, or D_small
covers at least 25% of U_tube. If only (ii) holds, the outcome is `keep-default, cost recorded`: the share is
written into the M4 exit review as the capability the default removed. If both hold, the outcome is
`fund-pilot`: a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier (one cell included by the tube and refuted by a referee
trajectory inside its stated scope) is written before any work is funded.

**5. Records.** `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-AUGUR-5.md` (census digest, survey table, outcome, next review date:
M8, when the shield tables' pilot has run). The register row (deadline M4) is set to `decided` with this ADR
whatever the outcome (or `defaulted`, through N41-03's `default` command, when the ADR is recorded as "default at
deadline"); under `keep-default` and `keep-default, cost recorded` the ADR states that the default
stays in force and the capability it removes (the underlying capability is still missing). A row left `open`
would read `silent` in N41-03's due query at M4 and block the exit. The M8 review, if it changes the outcome,
writes a new ADR that supersedes this one from its date (N41-03: a later answer does not rewrite history).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The census covers every stored answer of the gates listed and reports U, U_tube, D_small per family with
      the measure used.
- [ ] The survey table lists every sibling crate searched, with the pinned commit, and states for each finding
      whether an independent checker could recompute it.
- [ ] The rule's outcome is reproduced from the census and survey by the script; the ADR exists before the M4
      exit review and is signed by the Augur lead or recorded as "default at deadline".
- [ ] No answer in the census has a contact-rich cell in I without an accepted certificate (zero cells whose
      only support is a `trace-witness`).
- [ ] Negative controls of the rule: the five synthetic records below give the stated outcomes.
- [ ] The census is taken after the answers it counts exist: the beads that store them (stance, docking,
      pusher, alias and jaw, separation, family batch, region certificates, dominance, labels) are
      dependencies, because a decision reads measurements and a census of missing answers would read as zero.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/open_questions.rs`:
- `oa5_keep_default`: U = 400, U_tube = 40 (10%), D_small = 0, no owner → `keep-default`.
- `oa5_dsmall_branch`: U = 400, U_tube = 40 (10%), D_small = 10 (25% of U_tube), no owner → `keep-default, cost
  recorded` (condition (ii) through its second branch).
- `oa5_cost_recorded`: U = 400, U_tube = 160 (40%), no owner → `keep-default, cost recorded`.
- `oa5_fund_pilot`: U = 400, U_tube = 160, owner with checkable certificate → `fund-pilot`.
- `oa5_owner_without_checkable_certificate`: same counts, owner whose result only its producer can verify →
  `keep-default, cost recorded`.
- `oa5_census_classifier`: a cell with explanations {`ModeSequenceUnenclosed`, `BudgetExhausted`} is not
  counted in U_tube (the cause is not the tube alone).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/open_augur_5.sh`: lists stored answers with `frob mission show q:<mission> --answer a:<answer> --set
unresolved --json`, following the returned `cursor` until the set is exhausted (a read verb: no key; the mission is
the positional argument and the answer is named by `--answer`, as VERBS-mission's `mission.show` row reads), builds the census, applies the rule and logs per family U, U_tube, D_small with the measure
and its unit, each survey finding, the outcome and a `summary`; it also asserts on every answer that no cell of
class `Certified` in `inner` rests on a `trace-witness` (the default in force), and that the census digest in the
ADR equals the digest of `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-AUGUR-5.census.json`. Seeds: none. It sources the STD-E2E helper and
writes `target/e2e-logs/open_augur_5/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N25d-44`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `end`); `cmd` lines the exact
command, exit code, wall time and the evidence block of the answer read; `metric` lines U, U_tube, U_tube/U and
D_small per family against the thresholds of 25%; `assert` lines observed and expected, and on failure the answer
address and the offending cell.

### Known answers and negative controls
The five synthetic records of the unit tests. Planted failure: an answer file (synthetic, in a scratch store)
whose inner cell on a contact-rich family cites only a `trace-witness`; the script must flag it, and its bundle
must be refused as a kind used outside its scope, in the code of the surface that checks it (BD-133, step 7):
`FROB-REF-823` from `frob prove verify` and the standalone `frob-verify`, `FROB-REF-848` from a mission verb
offered it as evidence (a proof kind is unknown, at an unknown version, disabled, or used outside its scope; first
repair: the kinds admitted for this claim).

## Considerations

- This default is the largest single limit on what a mission answer can certify, and the plan says so in its
  own "honest limits": the plan-time pilots are scalar or static. Reports must print it, not bury it.
- A relaxation that drops contact (rule 1, exclusion) is sound without tubes; the asymmetry is real:
  exclusions of contact-rich designs can be certified, inclusions cannot.
- Low-dimensional smooth cells are admitted by the default; "low-dimensional" is bounded in practice by what
  interval and Taylor-model enclosures can decide with the checker's own arithmetic.
- What would make us revisit before M8: a sibling release with validated hybrid reachability; or a kill-gate
  decision that makes dynamic inclusion the only remaining source of value.
