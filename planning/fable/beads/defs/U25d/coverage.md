## (e) Contradictions, gaps and what was done

1. **GATE-AUGUR-07's claim.** "Performance" in the chapter's table, "Design comparison" in the program
   register. N25d-41 follows the register.
2. **GATE-AUGUR-09's milestone.** M4 in the chapter, "M3 (pilot); M4" in the register. N25d-41 follows the
   register.
3. **Kill-gate milestones.** The chapter's kill table gives none; N25d-41 and the five kill-decision beads use
   the register's.
4. **Refusal block.** §25.24 says the block 840 to 859 is used, yet six of its twenty codes lie outside it (868,
   874, 877, 881, 884, 887). N25d-31 carries all twenty as printed. Appendix M and Appendix N cite
   FROB-REF-826 for the prefix mismatch on the pusher where §25.24 defines FROB-REF-881; N25d-31 treats 826 as
   the auditor's own rejection code and 881 as the mission code: the verb surface reports whichever layer
   detected the condition and carries the other in `refusal.related` (a DESIGN default until the registry
   owners rule).
5. **Mass placement and actuator-loss robustness** are tagged [F] and have no pilot, ceiling or kill gate in
   §25.27. N25d-07 is their pilot; N25d-40 adds a row marked "added in drafting" with a DESIGN falsifier and
   the fallback named; no new kill-gate identifier was invented.
6. **The symmetry-event finding has no verb** in the verb registry. N25d-02 proposes an option on
   `analyze.symmetry` and a block in the result of `gait.gradient` as a DESIGN default and asks the registry's
   owner to confirm.
7. **No thresholds for the open questions.** §25.28 says "decide at GATE-AUGUR-02 from the reconstruction
   failure rate" and "revisit at M4" without numbers. N25d-43 and N25d-44 propose decision rules (1% of
   attempts or any unrecovered boundary failure; 25% of unresolved cells) labelled DESIGN, amendable by the
   owner before the measurement and not after. N25d-42 does the same for a re-open trigger (20% orphan tasks).
8. **OPEN-AUGUR-3 and OPEN-AUGUR-8 are listed in §25.28 but their mechanisms are elsewhere.** Units U25b and
   U25e each wrote an experiment bead for OPEN-AUGUR-3, and U25b a decision bead for OPEN-AUGUR-8. This unit
   wrote no third copy: N25d-48 registers both rows and tests both defaults from outside. The lead should
   merge the two OPEN-AUGUR-3 experiment beads.
9. **OPEN-AUGUR-8 says unreviewed bundles "say so in their flags"** while the authorization object must hold
   exactly three flags. N25d-48 tests both facts and accepts the marker beside that object, as the bundle
   unit placed it.
10. **Owners missing in the chapter.** §25.28 names no owner for OPEN-AUGUR-2, -5, -8, -9, -11; §42.3 does
    (Augur lead; Lemma lead for -8). The beads use §42.3. §42.2 lists OPEN-AUGUR-10 as needed by "M3; M6" in a
    row shared with another question, §42.3 as "M6 and M8 epics"; N25d-49 asks at the M5 and M7 exits.
11. **When a Moonshot is "active".** The doctrine says an item is active from the funding of its pilot; the
    default of OPEN-PROG-5 says none is active before its pilot passes. N25d-49 reads them together: funding a
    pilot takes a slot under the cap of four; the slice's own work is funded only after the pilot passes.
12. **OPEN-AUGUR-10's scope.** The question names the Moonshot slices of §25.17 to §25.20; the M8 milestone
    asks for "funding per epic". N25d-49 keeps one ledger for all six Augur Moonshot items, which adds skill
    transport and continuation (the continuation pilot N25d-21 already depended on it).
13. **Inline predicate atoms.** The schema's example prints atoms as Unicode text and its second example uses
    bare identifiers; the plan does not say how an inline atom is parsed. N25d-42 states the default so that
    it can be tested (one comparison, no temporal token; anything temporal by reference) and leaves the
    spelling to the schema bead.
14. **Carriers named in N25d-41** for KILL-AUGUR-TIE and KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT are the seed beads; other units
    have since written decision beads for both. The register row should name those when the lead resolves keys.
15. **The reproduction archive on disk differs from its recorded digest** (a later revision with follow-up
    experiments). BD-043 re-runs the revision on disk and records the follow-up experiments as additional rows,

16. **Titles.** Several titles run past the charter's "about 110 characters" (the longest is 158). They were
    left as drafted; the eight beads of the second sitting are between 106 and 123.
