## Background

A design studio's conclusions are only as usable as the way they are written down. The `report` cluster writes
evidence packages: a directory with an index that states what was found about a design, a run, a mission answer or
a campaign, and for every statement what supports it. The pattern is the evidence package of the simulation
sibling (FACT, to be re-read at the pinned commit: FrankenSim's `fs-package` writes such packages and `fs-checker`
re-checks them outside the solving process). Owner: Atelier, crate `frob-opt`, which holds comparison, lineage,
optimization, the ladder, the cascade, revalidation, experiment suggestion, registered predictions and reports.
Tier Solid [S]. Registry: `report.design` (read, M3), `report.run` and `report.mission` (read, M3),
`report.campaign` (read, M4, with a first form at M3 for the partial ten-fold gate).

The governing rule: a report is generated from the artifact graph and prints nothing it cannot resolve. The
artifact graph ties every statement to what supports it through four identifiers: `ClaimId` (the hash of claim
type, subject identities, statement and scope), `EvidenceId` (the hash of kind and payload hash; kinds are episode
set, chunk range, snapshot, certificate, receipt, ledger row, analysis result), `DecisionPolicyId` (a dotted rule
name and version) and `TraceId` (the execution that produced an evidence item). Edges: a claim is supported by
evidence, decided by a decision policy, and may depend on other claims; evidence is produced by a trace and may
derive from other evidence; the weakest input bounds the claim. `evidence.trace <claim_id>` returns the closure. A
claim whose closure contains a dangling or tombstoned node prints `evidence: unavailable` and cannot be cited
(`FROB-REF-869`).

Why this bead sits with the mission chapter's late rules: several of them create conditions that a report must
carry. A sweep with a non-monotone outcome makes dependent results `model-conditional` until model-form variants
agree; a mission answer may hold unresolved cells; a bracket label says a design is controller-limited, not
inferior. A report that printed such results without their conditions would undo the rules. Rejected
alternatives: free-form report templates filled from logs (numbers with no evidence path); a report that
summarizes only winners (a losing experiment must be reproducible as readily as a winning one).

## Scope

In: the four verbs, the package layout, the statement model and its mandatory fields, the resolution rule, the
refusals, determinism of bytes, the M3 form of `report.campaign` (the phase ledger read from the phase
attribution of N28a-03, and the list of fallbacks that fired). Out: the artifact graph and `evidence.trace`
(BD-095); the scorecards and batteries (BD-254); the promotion rule and labels (BD-263); the qualified standing
and its check (N24b-19, which this bead calls); the full campaign content at M4 (BD-352, with the decision

options `--arms B,W,F` and `--ablate` (N39b-46, which depends on this bead and computes them in

depends on this bead and on the hazard registries of BD-227: the flag `--claims-realism` and, for the claim, the
collection of the manifests of cited scenes and of the `assumed` hazard rows that the cited runs' tasks drew); the
scene section's line that prints the scene's generator identity and never a novelty statement, with the lint over
the report templates (NEG-27, which depends on this bead and adds the line through the typed-statement interface
where a template's scene section lacks it); explanations (`explain.*`); the
reuse section of `report.campaign` and the refusal of a reuse figure without its column name (N28c-44, which
depends on this bead and adds both to the generator built here: the table of the three columns per edit
class, the typed reuse value that carries its column and regime, the three closed routes to an unnamed or
combined figure, the refusal's message and repairs, the wording rules, and the option `--reuse` of
`design.diff`). This bead states the rule once, in step 4, because the plan places it among the report rules;
it specifies and tests none of it.

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-opt/src/report/`; verb definitions generated from the verb registry.

1. Package layout (DESIGN): a directory `report-<kind>-<id>/` with `index.html` (self-contained, no network
   references), `report.json` (the machine-readable form, schema `frob.report/1`), `claims/` (one file per
   statement with its closure summary), `certificates/` (referenced `.frobcert` bundles by digest),
   `MANIFEST` (digests of every file). The package is itself an artifact with an identity; exporting it attaches
   the three no-authorization flags, all `false` (`execution_authorized`, `hardware_safety_signoff`,
   `sim_to_real_certified`).
2. Statement model. Every statement in a report carries: its claim type (exactly one of Proof, Known answer,
   Parity, Fidelity, Design comparison, Reality, Performance, or none for descriptive rows); its `claim_id`;
   the boundary of its claim product (the weakest input: evidence class, tier, determinism class, with the
   input that set it); the mode of every ladder rung that selected it; its rank-robustness type
   (`model-robust`, `model-conditional` or `unchecked`); its qualification receipt if it has one; its result
   label (`provisional`, `established` with the adjudicator, `provisional: tier-sensitive`, `model-conditional`,
   `energy-created`, `capacity-biased`); the union of its condition flags; the `reality:` line (`no-claim`
   unless calibration supplies a band); and the no-authorization flags.
3. Resolution rule. Before a number is printed its `claim_id` is traced; every number in a report resolves to
   its evidence through `evidence.trace`. A statement whose closure is dangling or tombstoned is printed as
   `evidence: unavailable` with no number, and the report's summary counts such statements; a request to cite
   one is refused with `FROB-REF-869`.
4. Conditions travel. A `model-conditional` result is printed with its condition (the variant that flips it and
   both orders of the ranking); a request to print it without its condition, for example a compact table mode
   that drops condition columns, is refused with `FROB-REF-557` (a `model-conditional` result offered as a
   design comparison without its condition; repair: run the rank-robustness analysis, report the condition).
   A result that is not `established` is never printed as established (`FROB-REF-820`). A statement printed as
   `qualified` is checked through N24b-19's function: a discovery-lane comparison marked `qualified` without a
   qualification receipt is refused with `FROB-REF-845` (exit 3) and the receipt, when present, is printed by
   its identifier. A reuse figure printed without its column name is refused in a report; that refusal is
   built and tested by N28c-44.
5. `frob report design <id>` writes the evidence package of a design: the design and its lineage, every
   analysis, scorecards, comparisons with their verdicts, certificates, device receipts and replay addresses.
6. `frob report run <id>`: the evidence package of a run: the run's identity and model, tier and profile, the
   requirement verdicts, the failure taxonomy entry with first cause, the energy ledger summary, telemetry
   digests, replay address, and any promotion or tier-correction record that cites the run.
7. `frob report mission <answer-id>`: the evidence package of a mission answer: the canonical query with its
   quantifier prefix echoed; inner, excluded and unresolved cells, each with topology, chart, units, bounds
   with open or closed ends, assumptions and policy scope; witnesses; cost bounds with `attained` and gap; the
   certificate bundle with the checker's verdict; the next test; the assumptions; the claim product. An
   unresolved cell is printed as unresolved with its explanations and is never printed as either verdict.
   Late-rule fields are printed where present: law class and law of each inner cell; claim kinds and coupling
   budgets of transported claims; point statuses of sweeps (`evaluated`, `proved`, `unresolved`) and any
   non-monotone record; bracket ends and labels (a `controller-limited` design is never described as inferior);
   split sets and weak widths; sizing brackets and `lower-bound-only` labels; Pareto dispositions and coverage
   obligations; scope markings of nuisance parameters.
8. `frob report campaign <id>` adds the phase ledger, the screening audit, the planted-winner outcome and the
   list of fallbacks that fired; it prints the three reuse columns per edit class (mass, mount, controller-only,
   mission-only, topology), never summed (the reuse section is N28c-44's, added to this verb at M3);
   `--arms B,W,F` prints T, C, r_T and r_C with intervals and
   `--ablate {information, controller, stepping}` prints an ablation with its bound (both options are
   N39b-46's). This bead builds the verb at M3 with the phase ledger (per phase: exclusive wall seconds,
   device-seconds and the share of the campaign, read from the phase records of N28a-03) and the fallbacks that
   fired, and with section slots that print `not yet available at this milestone` (never an empty or zero
   value) for the screening audit and the planted winner until their M4 beads fill them; the cascade bead
   completes it at M4.
9. Determinism. Reports are deterministic: the same graph produces the same bytes. No wall-clock time, host
   name or trace identifier enters the package content (they identify executions and never enter a content
   hash); ordering is canonical (by claim identifier). Class D0.
10. Completeness. A losing experiment is reported with the same completeness as a winning one, so that it can
    be reproduced as readily: the same sections, the same evidence links, the replay addresses.
11. Envelope. Each verb returns the package digest, path, statement count, unavailable count and the weakest
    claim product among statements; `--estimate` returns the cost without writing; pagination applies to
    `report.json` when listed through the surface.
12. Inputs this bead accepts, as the rows of VERBS-report list them (the registry of record):
    - `--cite <id>` on all four verbs (a parameter marked `accepts = "verdict"`): names a result or claim as
      evidence for the report's statements. The cited identifier is resolved through one function,
      `report::resolve_cited(id)`, which applies in order: a closure that is dangling or tombstoned →
      `FROB-REF-869`; then N16b-04's `cite_as_established`, the only source of an `EstablishedRef`, which returns
      exactly one code for a result that is not `established`: `FROB-REF-822` for a `capacity-biased` comparison
      cited as a verdict (N18b-22 step 4: "any verb asked to cite the comparison as a verdict, a report
      included"), `FROB-REF-557` for a `model-conditional` ranking cited as a design comparison without its
      condition, and `FROB-REF-820` for every other non-established result (repairs: promote; run the settling
      experiment); then a standing `qualified` with no promoting receipt → `FROB-REF-845` (N24b-19's check). N28c-12, which depends on this bead, wraps the same function with the advisory-type
      guard (`FROB-REF-872`); a result listed without `--cite` is printed with its own label and is never
      refused for being provisional.
    - `--compact` on `report.campaign` (and the compact table mode of `report.design`): drops columns, and a
      `model-conditional` statement whose condition columns would be dropped is refused with `FROB-REF-557`.
    - `--claim reality|performance` on `report.run`: the claim the report is asked to make. `reality` routes
      every result of the run that rests on sensing through N20b-18's `require_reality_claimable(deps)`; an
      `assumed` sensor or optical card field in the dependency set is `FROB-REF-447`, exit 3, with the offending
      field and the repairs of N20b-18 (record a datasheet or measurement; drop the claim), and an admitted
      request still prints `reality: no-claim` unless calibration supplies a band. `performance` attaches the
      Performance claim type only within the run's claim product; a statement asked to stand above it is
      `FROB-REF-819` (a result cited above its claim product: a kind not in the result's `evidence.claims`,
      N00-25's rule), raised by this handler, not by the export door. Before attaching it, and for any statement
      that cites a device throughput, the handler calls N21c-34's predicate
      `frob_core::decisions::exploratory_blocks(claim_kind, receipt)`: while the ADR-SPARK-01 row of
      `registries/decisions.toml` is not `ratified` (OPEN-SPARK-1 open), a Performance or Parity claim resting on a
      device receipt is refused with `FROB-REF-819`, exit 3, `pointer` `evidence.device.exploratory`, `observed`
      naming OPEN-SPARK-1 and the row's status; a `batch-cpu` receipt, or a `ratified` row, is admitted. N21c-34
      proves the predicate through a test verb and leaves the assertion on `report.run` to this bead.
    - `--claims-realism` is not this bead's: N19a-39 adds it with `FROB-REF-312`; until then the option answers
      `FROB-REF-724` naming that bead, as the registry's late-extension rule requires.
    The codes these raise (447, 557, 819, 820, 822, 845, 869) are in the rows' refusal lists; a verb that raised
    an unlisted code would fail the registry's conformance test.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every number in a report resolves to its evidence through `evidence.trace`: on a fixture graph, each printed number's `claim_id` returns a closure whose hashes recompute (checked for 100% of numbers in four fixture reports).
- [ ] A `model-conditional` result printed without its condition is refused (`FROB-REF-557`), and with its condition it is printed with both orders of the ranking.
- [ ] A statement with a dangling or tombstoned closure prints `evidence: unavailable` and no number; citing it is refused with `FROB-REF-869`.
- [ ] The four verbs are reachable through `frob --json` and the MCP surface with documented schemas; each package carries the three no-authorization flags, all `false`, and the `reality:` line on every statement.
- [ ] Byte determinism: two generations from the same graph, on two hosts and two worker counts, give identical package digests.
- [ ] `report.mission` never prints an unresolved cell as a verdict (asserted on the docking and stance answers with a budget stop), echoes the quantifier prefix, and prints open or closed ends as certified.
- [ ] A non-`established` result presented as established is refused (`FROB-REF-820`). (The companion rule, that a reuse figure without its column name is refused, is accepted in N28c-44 with its three planted routes.)
- [ ] `--cite` of a provisional result is `FROB-REF-820`, of a `capacity-biased` comparison `FROB-REF-822` (never 820 or 557 as well), of a dangling claim `FROB-REF-869`, of a discovery comparison marked `qualified` without a receipt `FROB-REF-845`, each exit 3; all four go through `report::resolve_cited`, whose non-established branch is N16b-04's `cite_as_established`.
- [ ] `frob report run <run> --claim performance --json` on a run whose receipt is a device receipt is refused with `FROB-REF-819`, exit 3, `pointer` `evidence.device.exploratory` and `observed` naming OPEN-SPARK-1 and the status, while the ADR-SPARK-01 row is `unratified` (or `ratified-amended`); the same request on a `batch-cpu` receipt, or with the row `ratified`, is admitted.
- [ ] `frob report run <run> --claim reality --json` on a sensing run whose cards hold an `assumed` optical field is refused with `FROB-REF-447`, exit 3, naming the field; on the all-measured catalog it is admitted with `reality: no-claim`.
- [ ] Every option a row of VERBS-report lists for these verbs is accepted by the generated schema, and `--claims-realism` answers `FROB-REF-724` naming N19a-39 until that bead lands.
- [ ] The generator admits a section contributed by another bead without change to the statement model: the reuse section of N28c-44 is added through the same typed-statement interface as every other section.
- [ ] A losing comparison's report has the same sections and evidence links as a winning one (structural diff of `report.json` keys is empty).
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-opt` documents the package layout and the statement model.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/report_evidence_packages.sh`: builds a small fixture session through real verbs (a design check, a
referee run, a paired comparison with a model-form check that plants a `model-conditional` ranking, the stance and
docking mission answers with a budget stop; every `run` verb with its own `--key`, so that no step returns an
earlier step's stored receipt), then runs `frob report design`, `frob report run`,
`frob report mission` and `frob report campaign` with `--json`. For every number in each `report.json` it calls
`frob evidence trace <claim_id> --json` and asserts a resolvable closure. It deletes one evidence chunk (through
retention with a tombstone) and asserts `evidence: unavailable`; requests `frob report campaign <id> --compact
--json` on the planted ranking and asserts exit 3 with `FROB-REF-557`; calls `frob report run <run> --cite <id>`
with a provisional result (820), a tombstoned claim (869), a stored comparison edited to `qualified` (845)
and a stored comparison carrying N18b-22's `capacity-biased` label (822), asserting exit 3 and the code each;
calls `frob report run <device run> --claim performance --json` with a scratch decisions registry whose
ADR-SPARK-01 row is `unratified` (819, pointer `evidence.device.exploratory`) and again with the row `ratified`
(admitted; where no device is present the run is made on the `batch-cpu` backend posing as a device tier in the
conformance profile, as N21c-34's script does, and marked `no-device`); calls `frob report run <a depth-camera run on the shipped default cards>
--claim reality --json` (447, the assumed field named) and the same on the all-measured catalog (admitted,
`reality: no-claim`); calls `frob report run <run> --claims-realism --json` and asserts `FROB-REF-724` naming
N19a-39 while that bead is not served; regenerates each package and compares digests. Logs through the STD-E2E helper
to `target/e2e-logs/report_evidence_packages/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`
(`BD-302`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block; `metric` lines
with numbers printed, numbers resolved (threshold: equal), unavailable statements, package digests; `refusal`
lines with code, pointer and repairs; on failure the first number without a resolvable closure, with its JSON
path. Logs contain no private-scene data.

### Known answers and negative controls

The stance answer's printed numbers (boundary 3/14 m; forces (160, 130, 110, 0) N at the (60, 30) N·m vertex; 165 N
needed at 1/5 m) and the docking answer's (the boundary ε < (U + 1/2)/4; the equality reading 15/16) must each
trace to their certificates. Negative controls: a report generator that reads a number from a log line instead of
the graph (the planted number has no `claim_id` and the test fails); a compact mode that drops conditions; a
package that embeds the generation time (digest differs between runs). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine;
across worker counts; debug and release; the Mac and the Linux host (class D0).

## Considerations

- The seed's done-condition is the first two acceptance criteria. GATE-ATEL-16 (the cascade end to end, whose
  report prints every mode, every fallback and the phase ledger) is measured by the cascade bead at M4 and uses
  this bead's `report.campaign`.
- Retention can delete the interval that explains a decision (RISK-39); decision-relevant chunks are pinned,
  and a report over a collected object says `evidence: unavailable` and does not guess.
- Privacy: a report over a private scene carries the private mark and its export follows the licence joins of
  the safety chapter; the package never inlines private-scene payloads, only digests.
- Size: a campaign report can reference thousands of claims; `index.html` paginates and `report.json` is the
  complete form.
- Graph position (DESIGN): about twenty beads build on this one, so it carries only the generator, the statement
  model and the four base verbs, and waits on nothing they do not need. Every extension (the reuse section,
  N28c-44; the arm and ablation options, N39b-46; the realism-claim wiring, N19a-39; the decision sections,
  N28c-19; the cascade, BD-352) is a separate bead that depends on this one and adds a section through the
  typed-statement interface.
