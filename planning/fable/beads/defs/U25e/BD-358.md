## Background

Augur answers a question about a family of designs with an inner set (every member carries a checked reason why it
works) and an outer set (everything outside carries a checked reason why it cannot). For static conditions that are
neither linear nor monotone, the reason for a whole box of designs is a region certificate: an outward-rounded
enclosure of one constraint c(θ) ≤ 0 over a box B. If the enclosure's upper end is at most 0 the box is feasible
for that constraint; if its lower end is above 0 the box is infeasible; otherwise it is undecided and is split.

What is not attempted, and why. Paving a joint design box of ten to twenty dimensions with certified boxes is out
of reach: 10⁴ boxes are 13 bisections; in 20 dimensions that is less than one cut per axis, and uniform boxes have
side 0.64 of the domain (0.41 in 10 dimensions), so a constraint surface that depends on most parameters crosses
nearly every box. The published record agrees (FACT, source register; to be re-read before a gate cites it):
interval design of parallel robots is described by its practitioners as exponential branch and bound suited to
small and medium problems, with a worked example of five design parameters; a three-dimensional dextrous-workspace
paving took about five hours and left an error bound of up to a third of the certified volume; Taylor models delay
the growth with dimension very little when dependent intervals lack cancellation structure.

What is built (ADR-AUGUR-06: region evidence is per constraint, with a bounded split set). Certificates for one
constraint over a box of the parameters that constraint depends on, bisected along at most about six of them,
combined by products, intersections and monotonicity. The reading "a certificate's box has at most six parameters"
is too narrow: what is bounded is the number of parameters bisected, and the weak-variable extension (N25e-24)
handles constraints that read more. This bead is the base mechanism at M4, after its pilot (BD-289) has decided
KILL-AUGUR-REGION. Tier Frontier [F]; bet BET-20; construction C-32.

Killed forms, not built: sampling only inside a certified inner ellipsoid (it cuts off the feasibility boundary,
where optima sit, and misses disconnected islands); a logarithmic change of variables to linearize posynomial
constraints for an integer-linear decision procedure (the logarithm of a posynomial is a convex log-sum-exp);
cylindrical algebraic decomposition for reachable sets (doubly exponential beyond four or five variables).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-mission/src/region/` (DESIGN), shared by the component beads. Enclosures come from interval,
affine-form and Taylor-model arithmetic behind the airlock (FACT, sibling `frankensim`, to be re-read at the
pinned commit: `fs-ivl` provides outward-rounded intervals, affine forms, multivariate Taylor models with
`range_subdomain` and `recenter`, and interval Newton with `DEFAULT_MAX_ROOT_BOXES = 65_536`; it has no paving
driver and no contractors, which are new work in N25b-09).

**What the parts deliver (their contracts, as this bead consumes them; the specification of each is in its own
bead and is not repeated here).**

1. Constraint-to-parameter graph (N25b-08). Each lowered constraint g_k lists the parameters S_k it reads, from
   its expression DAG. The graph is an output (`result.region.graph`) and the input of every later step.
2. Routing and combination (N25b-10). Monotone bounds first: a constraint monotone in one parameter over the
   cell removes its half space with one `monotone-transport` certificate and no bisection. Dual witnesses for
   static conditions: `lp-farkas` or `conic-farkas`, a parametric dual with a sign proof covering a whole cell,
   so linear static families need no paving. Only the remaining constraints are paved. The family answer is
   assembled by cylinders over the per-constraint sets, represented implicitly by membership tests and never
   enumerated; its volume, when wanted, is an `Estimated` quantity from sampling. A constraint with more than
   six parameters and no weak-variable treatment, and a joint paving of the whole design box, are refused with
   `FROB-REF-856` (repair: the per-constraint decomposition, or pointwise checks). Pointwise checks at an
   agent's proposed design remain. Every certificate carries the scope: the static rigid model named in the
   bundle; nothing about dynamic performance, run-up, landing or recovery.
3. Paving (N25b-09). `PaveRequest → Paving` per constraint in the coordinates S_k: inner boxes, excluded boxes
   and unresolved boxes (reason `BudgetExhausted` or `StraddlesThreshold`), each decided box with an
   `interval-box` or `taylor-box` proof object (expression DAG digest, box, claimed enclosure); the cheaper
   enclosure is tried first; the budget is 10⁴ boxes per constraint (DESIGN, the box count of the pilot);
   bisection is confined to at most six parameters of S_k.

**What this bead builds.**

4. The region mode of the verb. A mission query whose evidence profile is `enclosed` asks for region
   certificates; `mission.synthesize` then runs, per constraint, the routing of N25b-10 and, for the paved ones,
   the driver of N25b-09, charges proposing and checking to their budget lines, and assembles the answer. On the
   command line the mode and its two limits have spellings (DESIGN): `frob mission synthesize <mission>
   --region per-constraint [--split-cap 6] [--boxes 10000] --key <k> --json`. `--region per-constraint` is the only region
   mode and is implied by the `enclosed` profile; `--boxes` and `--split-cap` override the query's box budget
   and split cap for that call and are recorded in the answer's request digest. `--region joint` does not exist
   as a mode: asking for it is the joint-paving request that N25b-10 refuses.
5. The result block `result.region`: the graph; per constraint the route taken, the arithmetic used (interval,
   affine, Taylor with its order), boxes spent, decided volume share split into inner and excluded, unresolved
   share, and the share of decided volume owed to a single monotone bound; and the proof references. It is the
   per-constraint report of N25b-10 and the paving report of N25b-09 joined on the constraint identifier, with
   nothing recomputed. Evidence: boxes `Certified`; sampled volumes `Estimated`.
6. Bulk proposing. Enclosure proposals for many boxes may be screened in bulk on the device classes admitted for
   family queries (exact-integer or guarded single precision with exact fallback; BD-355): the device returns
   candidate verdicts per box, and each candidate then goes through the driver's reproducibility rule on the
   CPU, so every certificate is still checked on the CPU with the checker's own outward-rounded arithmetic
   (propose anywhere, check on the CPU). A device proposal can change which boxes are tried first and never
   which boxes are certified: the certified set for a fixed box order is the same with and without the device.
7. Mutation test of box certificates. From the certified boxes of the gate fixture, 200 mutants are produced by
   three operators (an endpoint moved outward past the decided region; the claimed enclosure narrowed; a
   parameter dropped from the box), with hashes recomputed; the auditor must reject every one. The mutants come
   from the one mutation generator (BD-136), whose `interval-box` operators already enlarge the box and narrow
   the claimed enclosure; this bead adds to its operator table "drop a parameter from the box" and the same
   three operators for `taylor-box`, and writes no generator of its own.
8. Cancellation and budget stops across the parts: a cancelled or budget-stopped synthesis returns the boxes
   certified so far, each complete in itself, and the unresolved remainder; proposals in flight are discarded;
   no unresolved box is printed as a verdict.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-09 (TARGET; pilot reading at M3, gate at M4): on the 12-parameter quadruped box (three link lengths, two hip offsets, three masses, gear ratio, motor mass, body length and width; static joint torque in three stance postures, reach to a foothold set, self-clearance) the per-constraint arm decides at least 50% of each constraint's sub-box volume within 10⁴ boxes; no single monotone bound gives more than 95% of decided volume; zero certified boxes contradicted by 10⁶ point evaluations. Recorded with the measured shares per constraint. The measurement is taken and the gate recorded by the gate script of N25b-11 on the integrated verb of this bead; this bead closes when its own tests below pass on that fixture, and the gate's value is on record when N25b-11 closes.
- [ ] Every decided box carries a proof object that the auditor's checker accepts (`interval-box` of BD-132, `taylor-box` of N12-16, both dependencies, called in process by the tests); 200 mutated boxes (an endpoint moved, an enclosure narrowed, a parameter dropped from the box) are rejected. The `taylor-box` bundles are written by the M4 writer BD-364, which depends on this bead, so the standalone `frob-verify` run over the written bundles is N25b-11's (step 7) and BD-364's to assert.
- [ ] Through the integrated verb, the behaviours the components specify hold end to end: a joint paving request and a constraint over more than the split cap without weak-variable treatment are refused with `FROB-REF-856` and the repair "per-constraint decomposition" (N25b-10); monotone constraints are discharged before any bisection, with zero boxes spent on them (N25b-10); combination by cylinders agrees with direct evaluation on 10⁶ sampled designs, no sampled point being in the combined inner set and infeasible, or outside the outer set and feasible (N25b-10).
- [ ] `result.region` carries, per constraint, the route, the arithmetic used, boxes spent, the inner, excluded and unresolved shares and the single-monotone share, and these equal the component reports they are joined from.
- [ ] `--boxes` and `--split-cap` override the query's values and appear in the request digest; `--region joint` is refused with `FROB-REF-856`.
- [ ] With bulk proposing on a device and without it, the certified box set for the gate fixture is identical.
- [ ] A budget stop returns the certified boxes and the unresolved remainder; no unresolved box is printed as a verdict.
- [ ] CPU reference path; reachable through `frob --json`; cancellation drains (checked boxes kept, proposals in flight discarded); `CONTRACT.md` updated.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/region.rs`, the integration tests of this bead, through `mission.synthesize` with the
real driver, the real combination and the real auditor:
- `region_mode_end_to_end_disc`: a one-constraint query g = x² + y² − 1 ≤ 0 on [−2, 2]² with 10⁴ boxes returns a
  `result.region` whose decided share is at least 95% of the domain and whose every box is accepted by the
  auditor (the driver's own test of the disc is `disc_paving_sound_and_mostly_decided` in N25b-09).
- `region_block_joins_component_reports`: on a two-constraint query (one monotone, one paved) the block's
  route, boxes spent and shares equal the reports of N25b-10 and N25b-09 for the same run.
- `cli_overrides_recorded`: `--boxes 500 --split-cap 4` change the run and the request digest;
  `--region joint` → `FROB-REF-856`.
- `box_mutants_rejected`: 200 mutants of certified boxes by the three operators, zero accepted.
- `bulk_proposals_do_not_change_certified_set`: device proposals on and off, identical certified boxes.
- `budget_stop_keeps_certified`: a box budget ending mid-paving returns complete certified boxes and the
  unresolved remainder, and no unresolved box as a verdict.
- `cancel_discards_in_flight_proposals`.
The tests of the parts are in the component beads and were moved there from this bead where they were first
written: `monotone_first_no_boxes` (g = m₁ + m₂ + m₃ − 20 ≤ 0: one `monotone-transport` per parameter, zero
boxes), `cylinder_combination` (there `cylinder_combination_disjoint_supports`), `split_cap_refusal` and
`joint_paving_refusal` (there `refusal_856_over_limit` and `refusal_856_joint_paving`) in N25b-10; `contractor_peels_slab`
(g = x − 0.3 ≤ 0 on [0, 1]), `taylor_beats_interval_on_dependency` (g = x·(1 − x) − 0.3 ≤ 0 on [0, 1]) and the
property test `no_certified_box_contradicted` in N25b-09.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/mission_region_mode.sh` (this bead's own, on small fixtures, built only on its dependencies; N25b-11's
gate script below may run it as a stage). Fixtures (this bead's, `fixtures/augur/region-mode/`): `disc.fmission`
(g = x² + y² − 1 ≤ 0 on [−2, 2]², box budget 10⁴), `two-constraint.fmission` (one constraint monotone over the
cell, one paved) and `wide7.fmission` (a constraint reading seven parameters with no weak-variable treatment).
Steps: (1) `frob mission synthesize disc.fmission --region per-constraint --key rm-<run>-disc --json`: decided
share at least 95%, and `result.region` with the route, the arithmetic, boxes spent and the shares; (2)
`two-constraint.fmission` (`rm-<run>-two`): zero boxes spent on the monotone constraint, with its
`monotone-transport` reference, and the paved constraint's report; (3) `disc.fmission --boxes 500 --split-cap 4`
(`rm-<run>-ovr`): a request digest that differs from step 1's; (4) `disc.fmission --boxes 100` (`rm-<run>-stop`):
the certified boxes and the unresolved remainder, and no unresolved box printed as a verdict; (5) through
`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-856 3`, `disc.fmission --region joint` (`rm-<run>-joint`) and `wide7.fmission`
(`rm-<run>-wide`), each with the repair "per-constraint decomposition"; (6) `frob prove bundle a:<answer of step 1>
--out <dir> --key rm-<run>-disc-b --json` and the standalone `frob-verify <dir>/<bundle>.frobcert --json` under
`env -i` on its `interval-box` certificates, all accepted (the `taylor-box` bundles are BD-364's); (7) the
script's own exact evaluator at 10⁶ points of the disc's domain against the certified boxes of step 1: zero
contradictions. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/mission_region_mode/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`BD-358`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence
block; per constraint the route, the arithmetic, boxes spent, the inner, excluded and unresolved shares and the
single-monotone share; auditor verdicts with bundle digests; refusals with code and repairs; the contradiction
count against 0; a `summary` with assertions passed and failed. It measures none of GATE-AUGUR-09's clauses and
records nothing.

One script measures this gate: `scripts/e2e/mission_region_certificates.sh`, built by N25b-11, on the fixture
`fixtures/missions/quad-static-12.fmission` (an earlier name of this bead's own, `augur_region_certificates.sh`
on `fixtures/augur/quad12/static.fmission`, is not created: one gate has one script and one fixture). It drives
this bead from the user's side: `frob mission synthesize` on the 12-parameter family (the query carries the box

`target/e2e-logs/mission_region_certificates/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`,

(threshold 10⁴), contradictions (threshold 0); on failure the first contradicted box with the contradicting
point and the bundle path. The `summary` names GATE-AUGUR-09.

### Known answers and negative controls

The arithmetic of the barrier (13 bisections; side 0.64 in 20 dimensions and 0.41 in 10) as a documented check of
the box-count report. Negative controls of this bead: a certificate with a narrowed enclosure (the checker must
reject it; one of the 200 mutants); a `result.region` block whose shares are recomputed and disagree with the
component reports; a bulk-proposing path that certifies a box on the device's word (the certified set then
differs from the CPU-only run and the auditor rejects a box). Controls of the parts, kept in their beads: a
driver that marks a straddling box inner, which the point-evaluation audit must contradict (N25b-09); a
combination that intersects boxes of different constraints as if they shared parameters (N25b-10). Related
counterexample: CX-275 (failing sample points do not exclude their cell). Determinism lanes: twice on one
machine; across worker counts (the box queue is processed in a canonical order, so the box set is identical);
debug and release; proposals on a device and on the CPU give the same certified set.

## Considerations

- KILL-AUGUR-REGION is decided at the pilot (more than 90% undecided in the best arm, or more than 95% of the
  decided volume from one monotone bound) and again if this gate fails after one repair cycle. Fallback: region
  certificates are removed; family answers use dual witnesses, monotone bounds and pointwise static analyses at
  proposed designs; untested regions are reported as sample coverage, `Estimated`. This bead exists only on the
  branch where the pilot did not fire.
- OPEN-AUGUR-3 (N25e-27) measures the sparsity that the whole approach presumes; until it reports, region
  certificates are refused above six parameters.
- Ceiling, stated before any speed test: none is claimed; pointwise static checks with an existing library give
  the same pruning for proposed designs.
- Checker time is part of decision time and has its own budget line; 10⁴ boxes per constraint must check within
  the query's `checker_s`.
