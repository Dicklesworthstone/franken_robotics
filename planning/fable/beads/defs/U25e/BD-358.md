## Background

Augur answers a question about a family of designs with an inner set (every member carries a checked reason why it
works) and an outer set (everything outside carries a checked reason why it cannot). For static conditions that are
neither linear nor monotone, the reason for a whole box of designs is a region certificate: an outward-rounded
enclosure of one constraint c(θ) ≤ 0 over a box B. If the enclosure's upper end is at most 0 the box is feasible
for that constraint; if its lower end is above 0 the box is infeasible; otherwise it is undecided and is split.

What is not attempted, and why. Paving a joint design box of ten to twenty dimensions with certified boxes is out
of reach: 10⁴ boxes are 13 bisections; in 20 dimensions that is less than one cut per axis, and uniform boxes have
side 0.64 of the domain (0.41 in 10 dimensions), so a constraint surface that depends on most parameters crosses
nearly every box. The published record agrees (FACT, source register; to be re-read before a gate cites it):
interval design of parallel robots is described by its practitioners as exponential branch and bound suited to
small and medium problems, with a worked example of five design parameters; a three-dimensional dextrous-workspace
paving took about five hours and left an error bound of up to a third of the certified volume; Taylor models delay
the growth with dimension very little when dependent intervals lack cancellation structure.

What is built (ADR-AUGUR-06: region evidence is per constraint, with a bounded split set). Certificates for one
constraint over a box of the parameters that constraint depends on, bisected along at most about six of them,
combined by products, intersections and monotonicity. The reading "a certificate's box has at most six parameters"
is too narrow: what is bounded is the number of parameters bisected, and the weak-variable extension (N25e-24)
handles constraints that read more. This bead is the base mechanism at M4, after its pilot (BD-289) has decided
KILL-AUGUR-REGION. Tier Frontier [F]; bet BET-20; construction C-32.

Killed forms, not built: sampling only inside a certified inner ellipsoid (it cuts off the feasibility boundary,
where optima sit, and misses disconnected islands); a logarithmic change of variables to linearize posynomial
constraints for an integer-linear decision procedure (the logarithm of a posynomial is a convex log-sum-exp);
cylindrical algebraic decomposition for reachable sets (doubly exponential beyond four or five variables).

## Scope

In: the constraint-to-parameter graph, the per-constraint paving driver with contractors, the box certificates,
combination by cylinders, the monotone-first rule, dual witnesses for static conditions, the refusal of joint
paving, the gate measurement. Out: the weak-variable enclosure and split-set choice (N25e-24); the pilot (BD-289);
the checkers (`interval-box` in BD-132; `taylor-box` in U12's bead); bundles re-checked on a second machine
(BD-364).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-mission/src/region/` (DESIGN). Enclosures come from interval, affine-form and Taylor-model
arithmetic behind the airlock (FACT, sibling `frankensim`, to be re-read at the pinned commit: `fs-ivl` provides
outward-rounded intervals, affine forms, multivariate Taylor models with `range_subdomain` and `recenter`, and
interval Newton with `DEFAULT_MAX_ROOT_BOXES = 65_536`; it has no paving driver and no contractors, which are new
work here).

1. Constraint-to-parameter graph. Each lowered constraint g_k lists the parameters S_k it reads, from its
   expression DAG. The graph is an output (`result.region.graph`) and the input of every later step.
2. Monotone bounds first. A constraint that is monotone in one parameter over the cell (a mass budget, a rating
   bound) removes its half space with one `monotone-transport` certificate and no bisection: the sign of the
   partial derivative is enclosed over the whole cell and must exclude zero.
3. Dual witnesses for static conditions. Where a condition is a linear or second-order-cone feasibility problem
   (static equilibrium with ratings, friction cones, torque envelopes), exclusion is an `lp-farkas` or
   `conic-farkas` certificate; a parametric dual with a sign proof covers a whole cell at once, which is why
   linear static families need no paving at all.
4. Per-constraint paving. For each remaining g_k, in the coordinates S_k: a queue of boxes; enclose g_k over the
   box (plain interval, affine form, Taylor model: try the cheaper first); upper end ≤ 0 → inner box; lower end
   > 0 → outer (excluded) box; otherwise apply a forward–backward contractor (propagate the constraint
   g_k ≤ 0 and its negation backward through the expression DAG to shrink the box toward the undecided part,
   peeling decided slabs), then bisect the widest contributing parameter. Each decided box carries an
   `interval-box` or `taylor-box` proof object: expression DAG digest, box, claimed enclosure. Budget: 10⁴
   boxes per constraint (DESIGN, the box count of the pilot); a constraint that exhausts it keeps its
   undecided boxes as `unresolved` with reason `BudgetExhausted` or `StraddlesThreshold`.
5. Split set. Bisection is confined to at most six parameters of S_k (`--split-cap`, default 6). A constraint
   with more than six parameters and no weak-variable treatment is refused a region certificate
   (`FROB-REF-856`; repair: the per-constraint decomposition, or pointwise checks). A request for a joint
   paving of the whole design box is refused with the same code.
6. Combination. A design is in the region answer's inner set when it lies in every cylinder over the inner
   boxes I_k, and outside the outer set when it lies outside some cylinder over O_k (inside an excluded box of
   some constraint). The joint set is represented implicitly by k membership tests and is never enumerated. Its
   volume, when wanted, is an `Estimated` quantity from sampling.
7. Pointwise checks remain: an agent's proposed design is checked at its point regardless. Region certificates
   are a source of evidence quality and tolerance robustness (a box around a design is a statement about every
   as-built unit inside it). They are not presented as a speed lever.
8. Scope carried in every certificate: the static rigid model named in the bundle; nothing about dynamic
   performance, run-up, landing or recovery.
9. Bulk proposing. Enclosure proposals for many boxes may be screened in bulk on the device classes admitted for
   family queries (exact-integer or guarded single precision with exact fallback); every certificate is still
   checked on the CPU with the checker's own outward-rounded arithmetic (propose anywhere, check on the CPU).

Verb surface: `frob mission synthesize <mission> --region per-constraint [--split-cap 6] [--boxes 10000] --json`
adds `result.region`: the graph; per constraint the arithmetic used, boxes spent, decided volume share (inner,
excluded), unresolved share, the share of decided volume owed to a single monotone bound; and the proof
references. Evidence: boxes `Certified`; sampled volumes `Estimated`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-09 (TARGET; pilot reading at M3, gate at M4): on the 12-parameter quadruped box (three link lengths, two hip offsets, three masses, gear ratio, motor mass, body length and width; static joint torque in three stance postures, reach to a foothold set, self-clearance) the per-constraint arm decides at least 50% of each constraint's sub-box volume within 10⁴ boxes; no single monotone bound gives more than 95% of decided volume; zero certified boxes contradicted by 10⁶ point evaluations. Recorded with the measured shares per constraint.
- [ ] Every decided box carries a proof object that `frob-verify` accepts; 200 mutated boxes (an endpoint moved, an enclosure narrowed, a parameter dropped from the box) are rejected.
- [ ] A joint paving request and a constraint over more than the split cap without weak-variable treatment are refused with `FROB-REF-856` and the repair "per-constraint decomposition".
- [ ] Monotone constraints are discharged before any bisection (asserted: zero boxes spent on them).
- [ ] Combination by cylinders agrees with direct evaluation on 10⁶ sampled designs: no sampled point is in the combined inner set and infeasible, or outside the outer set and feasible.
- [ ] A budget stop returns the certified boxes and the unresolved remainder; no unresolved box is printed as a verdict.
- [ ] CPU reference path; reachable through `frob --json`; cancellation drains (checked boxes kept, proposals in flight discarded); `CONTRACT.md` updated.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/region.rs`: `single_constraint_disc` (g = x² + y² − 1 ≤ 0 on [−2, 2]²: with 10⁴ boxes
the decided area is at least 95% of the domain, inner boxes lie inside the disc and excluded boxes outside,
verified by exact rational corner checks); `monotone_first_no_boxes` (g = m₁ + m₂ + m₃ − 20 ≤ 0: one
`monotone-transport` per parameter, zero boxes); `contractor_peels_slab` (g = x − 0.3 ≤ 0 on [0, 1]: two boxes, the
cut at 0.3, no bisection); `cylinder_combination` (two constraints on disjoint parameter sets: membership equals
the product of the per-constraint sets); `split_cap_refusal`; `joint_paving_refusal`;
`budget_stop_keeps_certified`; `taylor_beats_interval_on_dependency` (g = x·(1 − x) − 0.3 ≤ 0 on [0, 1]: the plain
interval enclosure [−0.3, 0.7] decides nothing at the root box and the Taylor or affine form reaches the true range
[−0.3, −0.05], deciding it in one box); property test `no_certified_box_contradicted` (random polynomial
constraints of degree at most 4 in at most 6 variables; 10⁴ sampled points per decided box).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/augur_region_certificates.sh`: runs `frob mission synthesize fixtures/augur/quad12/static.fmission
--region per-constraint --boxes 10000 --json`, then `frob prove bundle` and `frob-verify` under `env -i` on every
box certificate, then 10⁶ point evaluations with `frob design check` in batch against the certified boxes. Asserts
the three clauses of the gate. Logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/augur_region_certificates/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`
(`BD-358`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block; `metric` lines
per constraint with decided share (threshold 0.50), largest single-monotone share (threshold 0.95), boxes spent
(threshold 10⁴), contradictions (threshold 0); on failure the first contradicted box with the contradicting point
and the bundle path. The `summary` names GATE-AUGUR-09.

### Known answers and negative controls

The arithmetic of the barrier (13 bisections; side 0.64 in 20 dimensions and 0.41 in 10) as a documented check of
the box-count report. The disc and the quadratic x·(1 − x) fixtures above. Negative controls: a driver that marks
a straddling box inner (the point-evaluation audit must find a contradiction); a certificate with a narrowed
enclosure (the checker must reject it); a combination that intersects boxes of different constraints as if they
shared parameters. Related counterexample: CX-275 (failing sample points do not exclude their cell). Determinism
lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts (the box queue is processed in a canonical order, so the box
set is identical); debug and release; proposals on a device and on the CPU give the same certified set.

## Considerations

- KILL-AUGUR-REGION is decided at the pilot (more than 90% undecided in the best arm, or more than 95% of the
  decided volume from one monotone bound) and again if this gate fails after one repair cycle. Fallback: region
  certificates are removed; family answers use dual witnesses, monotone bounds and pointwise static analyses at
  proposed designs; untested regions are reported as sample coverage, `Estimated`. This bead exists only on the
  branch where the pilot did not fire.
- OPEN-AUGUR-3 (N25e-27) measures the sparsity that the whole approach presumes; until it reports, region
  certificates are refused above six parameters.
- Ceiling, stated before any speed test: none is claimed; pointwise static checks with an existing library give
  the same pruning for proposed designs.
- Checker time is part of decision time and has its own budget line; 10⁴ boxes per constraint must check within
  the query's `checker_s`.
