## Background

The rule under test: no verb infers a verdict at an unevaluated parameter value from its neighbours unless a
checked monotonicity argument is attached, and a sweep whose verdict changes more than once is recorded as a
non-monotone outcome and makes dependent results `model-conditional`. The rule exists because a real sweep showed
success, timeout, success at three adjacent friction values, so that "fill above the first success" would have
invented an outcome.

GATE-AUGUR-26 (claim: Known answer; M3; thresholds TARGET): on the banded fixture the detector flags the
non-monotone outcome in 100 of 100 seeds, no verdict is filled, and the mission answer leaves the band
`unresolved`; on the creep rig the licensed fill equals the exhaustive grid at every point; a mutant that fills
above the first success is caught on both.

Two fixtures are needed, one where a fill is provably wrong and one where it is provably right. The first is new: a
planted fixture with a provable band, in which a cart on a compliant sill mount passes below and above a resonance
band of mount stiffness and fails inside it, so the grid shows pass, fail, pass. The second exists: the creep-limit
rig of the cart-sill fixture, where success is the friction demand μ ≥ tan θ and is monotone in μ by construction.
Tier Solid [S]. Rejected alternative: testing the rule only on recorded pilot data (the pilot's scorer has known
defects and its physical reading is unresolved; a gate needs a fixture whose truth is a theorem).

## Scope

In: the banded fixture (`fixture:sill-mount-band`, class `synthetic`), its closed form and its referee model; the
creep-rig sweep harness; `scripts/e2e/augur_no_monotone_fill.sh`; the gate record. Out: the rule (N25e-07), the
detector (N25e-08), the cart-sill fixture and its creep rigs (BD-123, known answers KA-606 and KA-607).

## Technical approach

Banded fixture (DESIGN; every number is an exact consequence of the stated model). The sill is a plate of mass
m_s = 1 kg on a mount of stiffness k and damping c = 20 N·s/m, with one vertical degree of freedom. The creeping
cart's driven wheels load it with F(t) = F₀·sin(ω·t), F₀ = 10 N, ω = 100 rad/s. The requirement, on a 1 ms clock,
is |x(t)| ≤ x_max = 2 mm for t in [2 s, 3 s] (twenty time constants 2·m_s/c = 0.1 s after the start). The
steady-state amplitude of the forced oscillator is

  X(k) = F₀ / √((k − m_s·ω²)² + (c·ω)²),

so the requirement fails exactly when (k − 10⁴)² < (F₀/x_max)² − (c·ω)² = 5000² − 2000² = 2.1 × 10⁷, that is for
k in (10⁴ − √(2.1 × 10⁷), 10⁴ + √(2.1 × 10⁷)) = (5417.4, 14582.6) N/m. The grid k ∈ {2000, 4000, …, 20000} N/m gives
pass, pass, fail × 5, pass × 3: two changes. Every grid point is at least 582 N/m from a band edge (6000 − 5417.4
and 14582.6 − 14000 are both 582.6 N/m). Seeds vary the
forcing phase and jitter each grid point by at most ±200 N/m; the band survives every seed. The fixture ships the
analytic verdict (exact inequality in rationals, with 2.1 × 10⁷ compared without a square root) and a referee model
(a prismatic joint with a spring-damper, physics step 0.5 ms). Files: `fixtures/augur/sill-mount-band/` with the
`.fbot`, the `.ftask`, the `.fmission` that sweeps k (with a top-level field `seed`, so that `--set seed=<s>` is
a one-field amendment and each seed is its own `MissionId`), and `truth.json` (pinned verdict per grid point per
seed). The band's known answer is N25-KA's row `augur-late-03` (§25.30, GATE-AUGUR-26) in
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml`, inside N25-KA's block of 18 requested of N38a-40; this
bead cites the tag until the block is issued and its number then, never one invented here.

Creep rig. From the cart-sill fixture (wheelbase 0.32 m, 12 mm sharp lip, commanded speed 0.02 m/s): the front
lift-off rig has friction demand μ ≥ tan θ = x/(r − h), x = √(2rh − h²): 0.7500 for wheel A (radius r = 60 mm) and
0.6459 for wheel B (r = 75 mm). Grid: μ from 0.50 to 1.00 in steps of 0.01, excluding points within 0.005 of the threshold on the
referee. The exhaustive grid is run once and pinned as `truth.json`. The licensed run evaluates the two grid points
that bracket the threshold and fills the rest under the creep licence.

Script steps (`scripts/e2e/augur_no_monotone_fill.sh`; plain shell, seed-explicit, idempotent):
1. `start`: commit, host, seeds 0..99.
2. Banded fixture, per seed: `frob phylum sweep --designs <workload> --task
   sill-mount-band --worlds-per-design 1 --tier referee --seed <s> --key nf-<run>-<s>-ph --json` over the full k
   grid, then `frob mission synthesize fixtures/augur/sill-mount-band/band.fmission --set seed=<s> --key
   nf-<run>-<s>-ms --json` with only k ∈ {2000, 10000, 18000} evaluated, then `frob nemesis search <design>
   --task sill-mount-band --legality <profile> --budget worlds=10 --key nf-<run>-<s>-ns --json` over the same
   parameter (a run verb: one key per call and per seed, so that no seed is answered from another's receipt;
   `<workload>` is the workload reference the fixture ships for its ten k-grid designs). Assert: the detector classification is `non-monotone`
   with two changes on the full sweep; in the mission answer every unevaluated k has status `unresolved`; the
   cells of the affected interval are `unresolved`; no point has a verdict without `evaluated` or `proved`
   status; the answer's conditions include `model-conditional` with the record identifier; the fidelity ledger
   has the variant request. Count seeds flagged; the threshold is 100 of 100.
3. Compare every evaluated verdict with `truth.json` (exact match required).
4. Creep rig: run the licensed sweep, `frob mission synthesize creep-<wheel>.fmission --fill licensed --key
   nf-<run>-creep-<wheel> --json` for wheels A and B; assert every grid point's verdict equals the pinned
   exhaustive grid; assert every filled point has a `monotone-transport` proof; `frob prove bundle a:<answer>
   --out <dir> --key nf-<run>-creep-<wheel>-b --json` and the standalone `frob-verify <dir>/<bundle>.frobcert
   --json` on each proof under `env -i`, asserting `accept`.
5. Unlicensed request: `frob mission synthesize band.fmission --set seed=0 --fill licensed --key
   nf-<run>-unlic --json` through `e2e_expect_refusal`; assert exit 3 with `FROB-REF-852` and the three repairs.
6. Mutant: the fill-above-first-success mutant is a registered mutation control in
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` (a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch of `frob-mission`,
   built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-mission/nc-<nnnn>`, numbered at registration by N38b-04, the
   same control N25e-07's script runs). Rerun steps 2 and 4 on that build, with keys `nf-<run>-mut-…`. Assert
   the script's own checks fail on both fixtures: on the band, k = 6000 is reported pass against a pinned fail;
   on the creep rig, filled points carry no accepted proof. The mutant run must exit non-zero, and the wrapper
   records "mutant caught on both".
7. `summary` with the receipt hash. The script never records a value (STD-E2E's `self-recording` lint): the
   gate's measured counts are recorded from that receipt by a session that did not implement N25e-07 or N25e-08,
   with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-26 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19),
   which checks that the control of step 6 is listed as caught in the same run.

Logging (STD-E2E): `target/e2e-logs/augur_no_monotone_fill/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` plus a transcript. Every line has
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25e-09`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's
`evidence` block. `metric` lines carry the seed, the grid, the verdict sequence, the change count, the count of
unresolved points and the flagged-seed tally against its threshold. `refusal` lines carry code, pointer and repairs.
On failure: the first seed and grid point that differ from `truth.json`, with replay addresses.

### Known answers and negative controls

Band (5417.4, 14582.6) N/m; amplitudes 10/√(6000² + 2000²) = 1.58 mm at k = 4000 and 16000, and 10/2000 = 5.00 mm
at resonance. Creep thresholds 0.7500 and 0.6459 (KA-606, KA-607). The band, the licensed creep fill and the
refused unlicensed fill are N25-KA's `augur-late-03`, cited by tag until N25-KA's block is issued. Negative
controls: the fill mutant (step 6); a detector that counts one change (N25e-08's mutant) run through step 2 must
drop the flagged tally to 0 of 100. Metamorphic relation: scaling F₀ and x_max by one factor leaves every verdict
unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; the referee model on
the CPU only (the fast tiers are not needed for this gate).

## Considerations

- The band fixture is a planted truth about a linear oscillator. It certifies the rule, not any claim that real
  sills resonate.
- Grid points near a band edge would make the referee verdict step-sensitive; the 582 N/m margin and the
  ±200 N/m jitter keep them at least 382 N/m apart, and a seed that lands within 100 N/m of an edge is a fixture
  error.
- The variants requested by the detector agree on this fixture, so the `model-conditional` label clears after
  they run; the unevaluated points stay `unresolved` regardless, which is the behaviour the gate asserts.
