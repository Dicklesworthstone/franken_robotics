## Background

The monomial screen (N25e-10) excludes a design family when a conjunction of monomial constraints
c·θ₁^a₁ ⋯ θₙ^aₙ ≤ 1 is contradictory, with a certificate of nonnegative integers λ_k such that the exponents cancel
and Π c_k^λ_k > 1. Real sizing constraints are often sums of monomials: a torque budget with a gravity term and an
inertial term, a bus current that adds several motors. A constraint P(θ) = Σ_j m_j(θ) ≤ 1 with several monomial terms
is not linear in the exponent coordinates y = ln θ: its logarithm is a convex log-sum-exp. This bead adds the two
monomial necessary conditions that let such a constraint join the screen soundly, wires the screen into the family
search, and gives `mission.refute` the report an engineer acts on. Tier Solid [S].

The relaxations only ever exclude. The smallest case that shows the limit of the simplest one: with two terms
m₁ = m₂ = 0.6 each term passes and the sum is 1.2, so a term-wise screen is not a decision. Rejected alternatives:
treating the term-wise test as a feasibility test (false, by that case); optimizing the weights of the mean
inequality per cell with a nonlinear solver (unnecessary: any positive weights are sound, and two default choices
decide the fixtures; a better weight is only ever a stronger exclusion).

## Scope

In: the term-wise and weighted-mean conditions, default weights, the screen as the static-exclusion step for a
cell and for the whole positive orthant, `mission.refute` for a family with the binding pair and the smallest
change of a constant, the control witness. Out: the core screen and certificate (N25e-10); the corpus and gate
script (N25e-12); per-constraint paving (BD-358).

## Technical approach

1. Sums of monomials. For P(θ) = Σ_j m_j(θ) ≤ 1 with m_j = c_j·θ^a_j, two necessary conditions are monomial and
   may join the system:
   - each term alone: m_j ≤ 1;
   - a weighted mean inequality: for positive rational weights w_j with Σ w_j = 1 and common denominator D,
     Σ_j m_j ≥ Π_j (m_j/w_j)^w_j, so P ≤ 1 implies Π_j (m_j/w_j)^(D·w_j) ≤ 1. This is one monomial constraint with
     coefficient Π_j (c_j/w_j)^(D·w_j) and exponent row Σ_j D·w_j·a_j.
   Any positive weights are sound. Default weights (DESIGN): equal weights, and in addition the term shares
   m_j/P evaluated at the centre of the cell, rounded to a common denominator D of at most 16: integer
   numerators n_j ≥ 1 with Σ n_j = D, chosen to minimize the largest deviation |n_j/D − share_j|, ties to the
   smaller D. For the whole orthant (no cell) only equal weights are used unless the caller supplies weights
   with `--weights`.
2. Soundness note carried in the result: over a cell with fixed weights the relaxation is an outer
   approximation, so an undecided cell says nothing. The screen only excludes. Inclusion, and cells the screen
   does not decide, go to the per-constraint lane of region evidence.
3. Worklist. In the cell loop the screen is the first static exclusion tried after transports from decided
   neighbours: lower the cell's monomial and posynomial constraints, add the box bounds, add the relaxation
   rows, run N25e-10. An excluded cell leaves the outer set with its `monomial-product` proof. The same call
   with no box bounds tests the whole positive orthant; a certificate there excludes every cell at once and
   the search stops with an empty outer set under the declared static model.
4. Each derived row records its provenance (`term-of` or `mean-of` with the weights), so that the checker can
   recompute the derived coefficient and exponent row from the original posynomial in the bundle's primitive
   equations. A derived row whose recomputation differs is a rejected certificate.
5. `mission.refute <family.fmission>` for a family returns, when the screen finds a certificate: the excluded
   scope (cell or orthant); the multipliers and the product; the binding constraints (λ_k > 0) by name, with
   their physical meaning; and the smallest change of a constant that removes the contradiction. For a constant
   s that enters the binding coefficients as c_k ∝ s^(e_k), the product scales as s^(Σ λ_k·e_k); with product ρ
   the factor needed is ρ^(−1/Σ λ_k·e_k). Constants are ranked by the smallest relative change and returned as
   typed edits (a rating, a requirement, a card). The report also states the combined quantity when one
   exists. Where the infimum is not attained by a catalog part, the answer is an open threshold with any
   feasible priced candidate and its gap.
6. When no certificate exists `mission.refute` says the screen does not exclude the family, and, where a point
   query is cheap, returns a pointwise witness that satisfies every constraint (an `lp-primal` style point
   checked exactly). A failed screen is never reported as feasibility of the family.
7. Scope and ceiling in every answer: the exclusion covers the static rigid model named in the bundle and says
   nothing about dynamic performance; no speed claim is made.

Result fields (`result.screen`): `scope`, `rows[]` (with provenance), `multipliers`, `product`, `binding[]`,
`repairs[]` (constant, current value, required value, relative change), `witness` (when not excluded).
Evidence: class `Certified`, tier `static`, determinism `exact`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The two-term case (0.6, 0.6) is excluded by equal weights: 4·m₁·m₂ = 1.44 > 1.
- [ ] The two-term case (0.9, 0.15), sum 1.05, is not excluded at equal weights (4 × 0.135 = 0.54) and is excluded at weights (6/7, 1/7), where the relaxed product is (1.05)⁷ ≈ 1.407 > 1; the centre-share default finds (6/7, 1/7).
- [ ] The term-wise rows alone exclude neither case (each term is at most 1), and the result says the term-wise screen is not a decision.
- [ ] `mission.refute` on the leg family returns λ = (1, 1), product 8829/8000, the binding pair (holding torque, no-load speed) and the repair "motor corner power τ·ω above 176.58 W" with the single-constant alternatives ranked.
- [ ] The control (τ = 2.5 N·m) returns the witness ℓ = 0.2 m, N = 10 with constraint values 0.94176 and 0.9375, and no exclusion.
- [ ] A cell excluded by the screen carries a proof that `frob-verify` accepts; a derived row with altered weights is rejected.
- [ ] A cell the screen does not decide is passed to the next worklist step unchanged; no verdict is recorded for it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/monomial_sums.rs`: `termwise_passes_both_cases`; `equal_weights_excludes_0_6_0_6`
(coefficient (0.6/½)·(0.6/½) = 1.44 with D = 2); `equal_weights_misses_0_9_0_15`; `share_weights_exclude_0_9_0_15`
(D = 7: (0.9/(6/7))⁶·(0.15/(1/7)) = (21/20)⁷ = 1801088541/1280000000); `weights_rounded_to_small_denominator`
(shares (6/7, 1/7) are kept exactly with D = 7; shares (0.83, 0.17) round to (5/6, 1/6) with D = 6); `mean_row_exponents` (row = Σ D·w_j·a_j);
`weights_soundness_property` (for random positive θ and random positive weights, Σ m_j ≥ Π (m_j/w_j)^w_j, checked
in exact rationals after raising to D); `orthant_exclusion_empties_outer`; `refute_smallest_constant_change` (leg
fixture: factors 1.103625 on τ or ω, or 1/1.103625 on the mass or the speed: τ to 2.20725 N·m, ω to 88.29 rad/s,
m to 10.873 kg, v to 1.3591 m/s); `no_certificate_is_not_feasibility`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/augur_monomial_screen.sh` (N25e-12) runs `frob mission refute` and `frob mission synthesize` on the
fixtures and the generated corpus and logs rows, multipliers, products and repairs with the STD-E2E helper.

### Known answers and negative controls

The two-term cases above; the leg fixture and its control. Negative controls: a mutant that treats "every term at
most 1" as inclusion (must be caught by the (0.6, 0.6) case, whose true sum is 1.2); a mutant that uses negative or
zero weights (caught by the soundness property test); a mutant that excludes on a float product (caught by
N25e-10's differential test). Metamorphic relations: rescaling a parameter's unit (θ → 1000·θ with coefficients
adjusted) leaves every verdict and λ unchanged; permuting constraints permutes λ and leaves the product unchanged.
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; identical bundles.

## Considerations

- The relaxation's strength depends on the weights; cells near the boundary of the true feasible set stay
  undecided and that is correct behaviour, not a defect to tune away.
- The repair report ranks constants by relative change, which is unit-free; the cost of each change belongs to
  the caller's catalog and is not invented here.
- Binding pairs are an explanation device: an agent reads "holding torque against no-load speed" and edits the
  motor, the reduction family or the requirement.
