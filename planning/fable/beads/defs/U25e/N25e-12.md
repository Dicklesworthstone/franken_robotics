## Background

The monomial screen excludes a whole design family when a conjunction of static sizing relations of the form
c·θ₁^a₁ ⋯ θₙ^aₙ ≤ 1 is contradictory. Its certificate is a list of nonnegative integers λ_k, not all zero, with
Σ_k λ_k·a_k = 0 and Π_k c_k^λ_k > 1, checked in integer arithmetic by the offline auditor `frob-verify`. The search
for λ is a floating-point linear program and is not trusted; only the certificate is.

GATE-AUGUR-27 (claim: Known answer; M3; thresholds TARGET): on 1,000 generated monomial systems of at most 12
parameters and 24 constraints with planted status, no feasible system is excluded; every infeasible system whose
contradiction has a product of at least 1.01 and fits the size caps is excluded; every certificate is accepted by
`frob-verify`, and each of 200 mutated certificates (one multiplier or one coefficient changed) is rejected. The leg
fixture returns λ = (1, 1) and 8829/8000; its control returns the witness; the two-term case (0.9, 0.15) is excluded
at weights (6/7, 1/7) and not at equal weights.

This bead owns the generator, the pinned corpus, the mutation set and the script that records the gate. The corpus
is a pinned oracle: planted status and mutants are produced by an independent reference in `frob-conformance` and
never regenerated from the candidate screen. Tier Solid [S].

## Scope

In: the generator and reference checker in `frob-conformance`, the corpus on disk, the mutants, the script, the
gate record. Out: the screen (N25e-10, N25e-11) and the checker (N12-14).

## Technical approach

Generator (`crates/frob-conformance/src/gen/monomial_systems.rs`; seed-explicit; exact rationals; DESIGN):
1. Sizes: n ∈ [1, 12] parameters, K ∈ [2, 24] constraints, exponents in [−3, 3], coefficients with numerators and
   denominators at most 10⁴.
2. Planted feasible systems (500): draw a point θ* with rational coordinates in [1/10, 10]; draw exponent rows;
   set each coefficient so the constraint's value at θ* is a rational in [0.5, 1.0] (the upper end 1.0 is used
   in 50 systems, so that tight feasible systems are present). Planted status `feasible`, with θ* stored as the
   witness.
3. Planted infeasible systems (500): draw λ* with entries in [0, 8] and at least two nonzeros; draw exponent rows
   and repair the last nonzero row so Σ λ*_k·a_k = 0 (rejection-sample until integer); draw coefficients, then
   scale one so the product Π c_k^λ*_k equals a target ρ. Targets: 350 systems with ρ ∈ [1.01, 4]; 100 with
   ρ ∈ (1, 1.01); 50 whose smallest certificate exceeds the caps. For these 50 the entries of λ* in [0, 8] do
   not apply: λ* is primitive (gcd 1) with one entry in [65, 128], K ≤ n + 1, and the exponent rows have rank
   K − 1, so the left null space is one-dimensional, every certificate is a positive integer multiple of λ*, and
   none fits the multiplier cap of 64 (the reference checker confirms the rank; a system with a smaller
   certificate would be excluded legitimately and is redrawn). Planted status `infeasible` with ρ, λ* and a
   `within-caps` flag.
4. Reference checker (independent code, exact): verifies each planted witness and each planted λ*; classifies
   every candidate certificate as valid or invalid.
5. Mutants (200): from 100 emitted certificates, one mutant with a multiplier changed by ±1 and one with a
   coefficient in the certificate payload changed (numerator ±1). The reference checker confirms each mutant
   is invalid (exponents no longer cancel, product not above 1, or payload no longer matching the primitive
   equations); a mutant that is still valid is discarded, logged as equivalent, and replaced.
6. Corpus layout: `fixtures/augur/monomial/systems/<id>.fmission` (the system as a family query: parameters
   with positive ranges, the monomial constraints as exact strings), `…/systems/<id>.truth.json` (planted status,
   θ* or λ* and ρ, the `within-caps` flag; read only by the script and the reference, never by the product),
   `…/mutants/<id>.frobcert`, `…/expected.toml`. Corpus digest pinned in the repository.

Script (`scripts/e2e/augur_monomial_screen.sh`; plain shell, idempotent):
1. `start`: commit, host, corpus digest (must equal the pinned digest).
2. For each system: `frob mission refute fixtures/augur/monomial/systems/<id>.fmission --screen monomial --key
   ms-<run>-<id> --json` (a run verb: one key per system, so that no system is answered from another's
   receipt). Record `result.screen.scope`, `multipliers`, `product`, exit code, `evidence`.
3. Assertions on the tallies: feasible systems excluded = 0 (threshold 0); infeasible systems with ρ ≥ 1.01 and
   `within-caps` excluded = 350 of 350; systems beyond caps report `beyond-caps` with no certificate; the 100
   systems with ρ ∈ (1, 1.01) may or may not be excluded, and any exclusion there must still verify.
4. For every exclusion: `frob prove bundle a:<answer> --out <dir> --key ms-<run>-<id>-b --json`, then the
   standalone `frob-verify <dir>/<bundle>.frobcert --json` under `env -i`; assert `accept`. Cross-check the
   certificate with the reference checker.
5. Mutants: `frob-verify <mutant>.frobcert --json` on each of the 200; assert `reject` with a rule of the `monomial-product` area or the
   query-mismatch rule; threshold 200 of 200.
6. Fixtures by name: the leg family (assert λ = (1, 1), product "8829/8000", binding pair, repair 176.58 W); its
   control with τ = 5/2 (assert no exclusion and the witness ℓ = 1/5, N = 10 with values 2943/3125 and 15/16);
   the two-term case (0.9, 0.15) with `--weights equal` (assert no exclusion) and with default or explicit
   (6/7, 1/7) weights (assert exclusion); the two-term case (0.6, 0.6) (assert exclusion at equal weights).
7. Negative control of the gate: the build of the screen with the exponent check disabled, a registered
   mutation control in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` (a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch of
   `frob-mission`, built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-mission/nc-<nnnn>`, numbered at registration
   by N38b-04), rerun on steps 2 to 4 with keys `ms-<run>-mut-<id>`; the script's checks must fail.
8. `summary` with the receipt hash. The script never records a value (STD-E2E's `self-recording` lint): the
   tallies are recorded from that receipt by a session that did not implement N25e-10 or N25e-11, with `cargo run
   -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-27 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19), which checks
   that the control of step 7 is listed as caught in the same run.

Logging (STD-E2E): `target/e2e-logs/augur_monomial_screen/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line:
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25e-12`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence`
block. `metric` lines: system id, planted status, ρ, observed verdict, λ, product (exact strings), with thresholds on
the tallies. `assert` lines for bundles: bundle digest, expected and observed verdict and rule. On failure: the
first system whose verdict contradicts its planted status, with the path of the system file and the bundle.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-27 (TARGET, M3) recorded through `record_gate` (N38b-19) with tallies: 0 feasible systems excluded; 350 of 350 in-cap infeasible systems with product at least 1.01 excluded; every certificate accepted by `frob-verify`; 200 of 200 mutated certificates rejected; the leg fixture, its control and both two-term cases as stated.
- [ ] The script exits 0 from a clean checkout on a host with no GPU, and non-zero on any failed assertion.
- [ ] The corpus digest is pinned and checked; the generator reproduces the corpus byte for byte from its seeds.
- [ ] The reference checker in `frob-conformance` shares no source file with `frob-mission` or `frob-cert`.
- [ ] Negative control of the gate: a build of the screen with the exponent check disabled excludes at least one feasible system or emits a certificate `frob-verify` rejects, and the script fails.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/monomial_gen.rs`: `planted_feasible_witness_checks` (every θ* satisfies its system
exactly); `planted_infeasible_lambda_checks` (Σ λ*·a = 0 and product = ρ exactly); `mutants_are_invalid` (all 200
judged invalid by the reference); `corpus_is_reproducible` (two generations give identical bytes);
`size_bounds` (n ≤ 12, K ≤ 24); `tight_feasible_present` (50 systems with a constraint equal to 1 at θ*);
`beyond_caps_have_no_small_certificate` (each of the 50 has exponent rank K − 1 and a primitive λ* with an entry
above 64).
Script self-tests in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `monomial_screen_selftest_planted_wrong.sh` (flip one planted status and
expect a failure naming the system); `monomial_screen_selftest_permissive_verify.sh` (an auditor that skips the
product comparison accepts a mutant, and the script fails).

### Known answers and negative controls

Leg: coefficients 2943/50 and 3/160, rows (1, −1) and (−1, 1), λ = (1, 1), product 8829/8000 = 1.103625; control
product 8829/10000 with witness values 0.94176 and 0.9375. Two-term: (0.6, 0.6) gives 1.44 at equal weights;

feasible systems (a screen that treats "at most 1" as "below 1" errs here), the disabled-exponent-check build, the
self-tests. The leg fixture and its control are N25-KA's late row `augur-late-04` and the two-term cases its
`augur-late-05`, cited by the KA numbers N38a-40 issues to those slugs from N25-KA's block (a tag in a test name
or derivation file fails `provisional-tag-past-due`). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and
release; the Mac and the Linux host; identical tallies and bundle digests (class D0).

## Considerations

- The clause "every infeasible system … is excluded" is a completeness requirement on the search and the
  reconstruction; a miss is a gate failure with no kill gate behind it, so it blocks the M3 exit until repaired.
- Systems with ρ just above 1 are where floating-point search loses the certificate; they are reported and not
  gated, by the gate's own 1.01 clause.
- The 200 mutants also feed the checker-independence campaign of M3 (BD-294), which needs at least 200 mutated
  witnesses per proof kind.
