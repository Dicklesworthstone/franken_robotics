## Background

A kill gate names its trigger and its fallback in advance; its decision is recorded as an architecture decision
record at its milestone, and a fired kill gate means the fallback is the plan.

KILL-AUGUR-BRACKET governs the dominance stop. A utility bracket gives a design d a certified upper end Ū_d on the
task utility any admitted causal controller can reach and an achieved lower end L_d. If Ū_d < L_c for a competitor
c, the ladder stops spending training, fine-tuning and planner budget on d in that comparison. The stop is the only
part of the mechanism that removes work, so it is the part that can do harm: a false stop discards a design that
could have won, and nothing downstream would notice.

- Trigger: one false stop on the solvable family or in an audited campaign (the screening audit samples dominated
  designs and trains them); or less than 3 times lower controller effort at matched decisions; or the label
  changes the agent's next action in under a tenth of comparisons.
- Fallback: brackets are printed as advisory labels, no stop is automatic, and designs leave the ladder only by
  the ladder's own rules.

The register gives the gate no milestone of its own; it is decided at M4, the milestone of GATE-AUGUR-28.

## Scope

In: reading the three measurements, recording the decision, switching the default if the gate fires. Out: producing
the measurements (N25e-13, N25e-16).

## Technical approach

1. Inputs, all from recorded runs:
   - false stops on the solvable ramp-and-carrier family (N25e-16 Part A; threshold: none);
   - false stops in the audited campaign: dominated designs sampled by the screening audit and trained like
     survivors; a sampled design that reaches its competitor's lower end is a false stop (threshold: none). With
     no false stop among m audited dominated designs the rate of false stops is at most 1 − δ^(1/m) at
     confidence 1 − δ (9.5% at m = 30 and δ = 0.05); the record states m and that bound;
   - the ratio of controller effort per accepted decision, static filters plus ladder against the same with
     brackets, at matched decisions, with its 95% paired lower bound (threshold: at least 3);
   - the share of comparisons in which the label changed the agent's next action (threshold: at least one
     tenth).
2. Repair rule. A false stop caused by an implementation defect (a relaxation that omitted a declared resource
   and was not refused, a level taken at α instead of α/(2M)) gets one repair cycle and a full re-run of both
   parts. A false stop that survives, or one caused by the rule itself, fires the gate.
3. Record: `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-BRACKET.md` with the trigger as written, the four measured values, commit,
   receipts, the decision (`pass` or `killed: fallback adopted`) and date; the gate row in
   `registries/gates.toml` cites it and resolves in the artifact graph.
4. If fired, mechanical changes:
   - the default of `eval.compare` and of the ladder becomes `--bracket advisory`; the automatic stop is
     removed from the ladder's allocation path and the code path is kept behind a test-only flag for the
     mutation corpus;
   - `body-limited` exclusions are unaffected (they are ordinary rule-1 exclusions with certificates);
   - the `controller-limited` flag continues to be printed and continues to feed the controller-budget
     escalation, which has its own gate;
   - campaign reports print brackets as labels with the note "advisory: no design was stopped on a bracket";
   - the false-stop counterexample, if any, becomes a permanent fixture of the negative-control registry.
5. If not fired: the status table records the measured ratio, x (share dominated before training), y (bracket
   cost share) and the audit bound; the mechanism stays Frontier and is described no more broadly than the
   brief class that qualified it (energy-, torque-, reach- or deadline-limited briefs).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists at the M4 exit with all four measurements, each traceable through `evidence.trace` to its run.
- [ ] The gate row has status `pass` or `killed: fallback adopted`, citing commit, command, output and receipt hash.
- [ ] If fired: `eval.compare` defaults to advisory, the ladder issues no automatic stop (asserted by a test on the allocation ledger), and reports carry the advisory note.
- [ ] The decision session did not implement N25e-14 or N25e-15.
- [ ] A change to trigger, threshold or fallback appears only as a dated amendment made before the measurement.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/bracket_fallback.rs`: `advisory_default_issues_no_stop` (with the fallback configuration, a
dominated design keeps its allocation and the label is still printed); `audit_bound_arithmetic` (m = 30, δ = 0.05
gives 1 − 0.05^(1/30) = 0.0950). A registry check verifies the gate row cites an existing ADR.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/augur_kill_bracket_fallback.sh`: in the fallback profile, runs `frob eval compare … --json` on the
solvable family's worked scenario, asserts the labels are present, asserts no stop in the allocation ledger and the
advisory note in `frob report campaign`. Logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/augur_kill_bracket_fallback/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` (command, exit code, labels, ledger rows,
`summary` naming KILL-AUGUR-BRACKET). The script also runs once in a dry-run profile at M4 whatever the decision,
to prove the fallback branch works before it is needed.

### Known answers and negative controls

The worked case (sound bound 0.622 against a competitor at 0.60: no stop). Negative control of the decision
procedure: a dry run fed a planted false stop must produce the record `fired` and switch the dry-run profile to
advisory. Second control: an A/A comparison (no brackets in either arm) must yield a ratio whose interval contains
1, so a ratio of 3 cannot arise from noise in the accounting.

## Considerations

- The third trigger is behavioural: if agents ignore the label, the mechanism costs programs and changes
  nothing, and is demoted even if sound.
- A pass at one brief class is not a licence for another; vacuous bounds on contact-rich briefs are expected and
  are reported as uninformative, not as failures of this gate.
- Owner of the decision: the Atelier lead with the Augur lead; confirmed at the M4 exit review.
