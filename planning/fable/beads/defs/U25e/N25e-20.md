## Background

`frob-synth` holds the constructive lanes of Augur: they propose candidate designs, and a candidate has no verdict
until `frob-mission` and the checker give it one (INV-AUGUR-11). Four lanes exist (`codesign`, `refine`, `linkage`,
`continuation`). This bead adds a fifth, `placement`, tier Frontier [F], hypothesis H-28.

Idea. For a fixed topology with fixed joint axes and link lengths, the inverse dynamics of a prescribed motion is
linear in the inertial parameters (N25e-19 supplies the regressor Y). At knot k of a motion (q_k, q̇_k, q̈_k) with
active contacts C_k,

  Y(q_k, q̇_k, q̈_k)·π = Sᵀ·τ_k + Σ_{c ∈ C_k} J_c(q_k)ᵀ·λ_{c,k},

with π the stacked inertial parameters, S the selection of actuated coordinates, τ_k the joint torques and λ_{c,k}
the contact forces. Everything unknown enters linearly or through convex cones, so placement and sizing for that
motion is one cone program. The lane takes a small library of nominal motions for the archetype (from the gait
atlas, the skill artifacts and centroidal planning) and solves one program per motion and per catalog choice.

What the certificate says, and what it does not. The statement excluded is the pair (design cell, motion). A design
that completes the mission by a different motion is untouched. The lane therefore proposes candidates and prunes
motions; it removes a cell from the outer set only when the mission itself prescribes the motion (a declared lift
profile, a commanded acceleration). Ceiling, stated before any test: the lane decides only the share of a design
decision that is placement and sizing for known motions; a conventional optimizer over a dynamics library can pose
the same program, so this is a convenience of the typed core and not a differentiator. Rejected alternatives:
making gear ratio a continuous variable of the energy objective (not convex, limit 2 below); making a module's
position a variable (it enters quadratically, limit 1); making link lengths variables (Y depends on them
nonlinearly, limit 3).

## Scope

In: the program, its three outcomes and certificates, the three limits with their handling, the scope rule, the
screening signal, the strategy flag. Out: the regressor (N25e-19); the 30-design comparison and gate script
(N25e-21); the kill decision (N25e-22); the existing static mass-placement construction of §25.21 (N25d-08, which
stays the fallback) and the task-wrench analysis A-24 (BD-348).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-synth/src/placement/` (DESIGN). One program per (motion, catalog choice), on the CPU, solved by
the one cone solver (three end states: `optimal`, `infeasible` with a dual certificate, `unconverged`).

Variables.
- Inertia: π = π_fixed + Σ_s m_s·φ_s with m_s ≥ 0, where φ_s is the parameter vector of a unit mass at an admissible
  site s (battery cells, compute, ballast), with a total-mass equality per movable component; and, for bodies
  whose distribution is free, a pseudo-inertia in the positive-semidefinite cone with the mass-support bound
  (mass inside a ball of centre o and radius ρ satisfies ‖c − o‖² + tr(I_C)/(2m) ≤ ρ², linear in the
  pseudo-inertia).
- Contact forces λ_{c,k} inside the friction cone of the scene's interface card at that contact.
- Joint torques τ_k, tied to the others by the knot equation, under the actuator cards at fixed gear ratios N:
  peak |τ_{j,k}| ≤ η·N·τ_peak; thermal ‖τ_j‖₂ ≤ √K·η·N·τ_cont over the K knots; voltage
  R·|τ_{j,k}|/(η·N·k_t) + k_e·N·|q̇_{j,k}| ≤ V_bus, linear because q̇ is data. (η transmission efficiency, τ_peak and
  τ_cont the motor's peak and continuous torque, R winding resistance, k_t torque constant, k_e back-EMF
  constant, V_bus bus voltage.)
- Objective: electrical energy Σ_j Σ_k [R·(τ_{j,k}/(η·N·k_t))² + τ_{j,k}·q̇_{j,k}]·Δt, a convex quadratic, in
  epigraph form.

Outcomes.
1. A placement and sizing that realizes the motion at least energy: an `lp-primal` style witness (site masses,
   forces, torques) checked exactly against every equality and bound after rational reconstruction.
2. A lower bound on electrical energy for that motion: the dual value, verified in exact rationals.
3. A dual certificate that no placement and no admissible contact forces realize the motion: `lp-farkas` when
   the active rows are linear, `conic-farkas` when a friction or thermal cone is active.
`unconverged` or a failed reconstruction leaves the pair (cell, motion) unresolved; it is never an exclusion.

Three limits, each with its smallest case.
1. A continuous site enters quadratically. A mass m at position x contributes m·x to the first moments and m·x²
   to the second. The program is linear in masses at fixed sites, not in a position. A splittable mass (cells
   along a rail) is exact. For one unsplittable module the program is a relaxation whose infeasibility still
   excludes every single-site placement; a fractional optimum is rounded (candidate sites: the heaviest site
   and its neighbours) and re-solved with the module fixed, and the answer reports the gap.
2. Energy is not jointly convex in torque and gear ratio. With N a variable the peak, thermal and voltage limits
   stay convex (the voltage limit times N reads c₁·|τ| + k_e·|q̇|·N² ≤ V_bus·N), so feasibility over a continuous
   ratio is one cone program. The copper-loss term g(τ, N) = τ²/N² is not convex: g(0, 1) = 0, g(2, 3) = 4/9 and
   at the midpoint g(1, 2) = 1/4 > 2/9. Minimum energy therefore enumerates catalog ratios, one program each. A
   request for jointly optimal energy and ratio over a continuous ratio is routed to enumeration and the answer
   says so.
3. Link lengths are not variables. They are enumerated on the cells of the query.

Scope rule and screening signal. Each result is tagged `scope: (cell, motion)`. An exclusion is forwarded to
`frob-mission` as a cell exclusion only if the mission declares the motion as prescribed. Otherwise the count of
library motions a design can realize is returned as a screening signal: it may discard under the rules of the
evaluation ladder and may not establish a comparison.

Input block (`design_domain.placement` of the `.fmission` schema, BD-126's row for this lane, distinct from its
`mass_placement` block, which targets a pseudo-inertia and has no motion or movable component). `components[]`, each
movable component with its name, mass, `splittable` (cells on a rail) or not (one module), and its admissible
`sites[]`; optional `free_bodies[]`, bodies whose distribution is free, each with its `support` in the form of
`mass_placement.support`; `motions`, either `library: <id>` (the archetype's nominal motions) or `prescribed:
[<motion ref>]` (the mission's declared motions, the only ones whose exclusion becomes a cell exclusion);
`catalog`, the motor and ratio rows by card reference. Every number is an exact string; the block is semantic
and enters `MissionId`; a malformed block is `FROB-REF-868` with its pointer.

Verb. `frob mission synthesize <mission> --strategy placement [--motions <library>] [--ratios catalog|continuous]
[--objective energy|first-moment:<component>] --key <key> --json` (a run verb: every call carries its
idempotency key). `--motions`, `--ratios` and `--objective` are lane options (DESIGN), on `mission.synthesize`'s
row in VERBS-mission with `placement`; `--motions` overrides the block's library. `--objective` defaults to `energy`; `first-moment:<component>`
maximizes the named movable component's first moment along its rail, the linear objective that finds a placement
boundary (the cart's 35/654 m). `--ratios` defaults to `catalog`; `continuous` makes the gear ratio a variable,
which is admitted for feasibility and for a linear objective (one cone program, limit 2) and, with
`--objective energy`, is routed to enumeration over the catalog ratios with `routed: enumeration` in the result.
It returns per (cell, motion, catalog choice): outcome, placement, energy and its lower bound, binding rows, the
rounding gap, the certificate reference and the scope tag; plus `realizable_motions` per design. Candidates carry
the label `candidate`. Evidence: witnesses and duals `Certified` for the stated program; class of the candidate
itself: none until verified.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Cart known answer through the verb: boundary 35/654 m in exact rationals at a = 3 m/s²; largest acceleration 981/320 m/s²; at 4 m/s² infeasible for every placement with the traction inequality as the dual, and the dual accepted by `frob-verify`.
- [ ] The all-wheel-drive variant of the same motion is feasible, and the answer for the rear-drive cart carries `scope: (cell, motion)` and does not exclude the cart from the outer set unless the mission prescribes the 4 m/s² motion.
- [ ] A request for jointly optimal energy and ratio is routed to enumeration, one program per catalog ratio, with the routing stated in the result.
- [ ] A single unsplittable module is handled by relaxation, rounding and re-solve; the rounded solution is verified feasible exactly or reported infeasible with the relaxation's bound.
- [ ] Every solve returns one of the three outcomes or `unresolved`; a solver failure is never an exclusion.
- [ ] `realizable_motions` carries the evidence label `screening` (rule 4 of the analyses: it may discard under the ladder's rules and may not establish a comparison), so the comparison verbs reject it as comparison evidence exactly as they reject every screening-labelled result; a test offers it to `eval.compare` and asserts the rejection.
- [ ] CPU reference path only; reachable through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp`; cancellation drains per program; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-synth` documents the lane.
- [ ] The `design_domain.placement` block parses and enters `MissionId`; only a motion listed under `prescribed` can turn an exclusion into a cell exclusion.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-synth/tests/placement.rs`: `cart_boundary` (15 kg at 0.5 m, 5 kg battery on a rail, wheelbase 1 m,
centre of mass at floor height, μ = 1/2, g = 981/100: N_r = g·[7.5 + 5·(1 − x_b)], feasible at a = 3 iff x_b ≤ 35/654);
`cart_infeasible_4` (dual on μ·N_r ≥ 80 N); `cart_awd_feasible`; `energy_lower_bound_matches_primal` (strong
duality on a two-joint fixture within 10⁻⁹, then exact verification of both); `voltage_row_linear_in_torque`;
`thermal_row_is_soc`; `ratio_enumeration_routing`; `copper_loss_nonconvex` (1/4 > 2/9); `single_module_rounding`
(a three-site rail where the relaxation splits the mass 50/50 and both single sites are feasible at higher
energy); `single_module_relaxation_excludes` (relaxation infeasible implies every single site infeasible, checked
by enumeration); `scope_tag_not_cell_exclusion`; `unconverged_is_unresolved`; `mass_support_bound_row` (1 kg in a
ball of radius 1/10 m with principal moments 1/125 kg·m² each needs a mean squared radius of 3/250 m² against
1/100 m² available: no placement); `placement_block_parses` (the cart written as a `design_domain.placement`
block with a splittable 5 kg battery on 101 rail sites parses and canonicalizes; a component with no sites is
`FROB-REF-868`); `prescribed_motion_only_excludes_cell` (the 4 m/s² motion listed under `prescribed` gives a
cell exclusion; under `library` it gives `scope: (cell, motion)` only).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/synth_placement_lane.sh` (this bead's own; the gate script `scripts/e2e/augur_placement_lane.sh` of
N25e-21 later adds the regressor stage and the 30-design comparison). Fixtures (this bead's,
`fixtures/augur/placement-lane/`): the rear-drive cart of `cart_boundary` (15 kg at 0.5 m, a 5 kg battery on a rail
of 101 sites, wheelbase 1 m, μ = 1/2) as `cart-a3.fmission` (the 3 m/s² motion), `cart-a4.fmission` (4 m/s² under
`library`), `cart-a4-prescribed.fmission` (the same motion under `prescribed`), `cart-awd-a4.fmission` (all wheels
driven) and `cart-nosites.fmission` (a component with no sites); `two-joint.fmission` (energy over the catalog
ratios). Steps: (1) `frob mission synthesize cart-a3.fmission --strategy placement --key pl-<run>-a3 --json`
(feasible) and with `--objective first-moment:battery` (`pl-<run>-a3-fm`): the boundary "35/654" m; (2)
`cart-a4.fmission` (`pl-<run>-a4`): infeasible for every placement with the traction row as the dual, `scope:
(cell, motion)` and no outer-set exclusion; `frob prove bundle a:<answer> --out <dir> --key pl-<run>-a4-b --json`
and the standalone `frob-verify <dir>/<bundle>.frobcert --json` under `env -i` (accept); (3)
`cart-a4-prescribed.fmission` (`pl-<run>-a4p`): a cell exclusion; (4) `cart-awd-a4.fmission` (`pl-<run>-awd`):
feasible; (5) `two-joint.fmission --ratios continuous --objective energy` (`pl-<run>-route`): `routed:
enumeration`, one program per catalog ratio; (6) `cart-nosites.fmission` (`pl-<run>-nosites`) through
`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-868 3`, the pointer at the component. Every solve ends in one of the three end
states or `unresolved`.
It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/synth_placement_lane/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N25e-20`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block; per call the key, the outcome, the
scope, the route and the exact values as strings; auditor verdicts with bundle digests; refusals;
a `summary` with assertions passed and failed naming GATE-AUGUR-29. Every run-verb call carries its own key, so no
answer is returned from another call's receipt; the script records no gate value (STD-E2E's `self-recording` lint).

### Known answers and negative controls

Cart: 35/654 m (53.5 mm); 3.066 m/s²; infeasible at 4 m/s²; feasible with all wheels driven (μ·g = 4.905 m/s²).
Mass placement: the 1 kg ball case above (KA-652). The cart is N25-KA's late row `augur-late-07` and the
copper-loss midpoint its `augur-late-08`, cited by the KA numbers N38a-40 issues to those slugs from N25-KA's block
(a tag in a test name or derivation file fails `provisional-tag-past-due`). Negative controls: a lane that reports the cart excluded (cell
exclusion) for the 4 m/s² motion when the mission does not prescribe it; a lane that optimizes energy over a
continuous ratio and returns a non-optimal stationary point on a fixture where enumeration finds a lower energy; a
lane that drops the friction cone. Determinism lanes: certificates identical twice on one machine and across two
solvers after exact reconstruction; debug and release.

## Considerations

- KILL-SYNTH-REGRESSOR (N25e-22): the design-comparison part of the gate fails, or more than 15% of designs
  infeasible on every library motion still pass the mission in closed loop (then the motion-count screening
  signal is withdrawn first). Fallback: the static mass-placement program and the task-wrench analysis as they
  stand; candidates start from class templates.
- The motion library bounds what the lane can see; a design that needs a motion outside the library looks
  infeasible everywhere, which is why the share of such designs that still pass the mission is reported.
- Rigid contact and ideal actuators beyond the card limits are assumed; closed-loop evaluation decides.
- Program size: knots × (joints + 3·contacts) plus sites; hundreds to a few thousand variables per program.
