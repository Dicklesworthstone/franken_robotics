## Background

The placement lane of `frob-synth` solves, for a prescribed motion, one cone program over masses at admissible
sites, contact forces and joint torques, because inverse dynamics is linear in the inertial parameters
(Y(q, q̇, q̈)·π = Sᵀ·τ + Σ J_cᵀ·λ). It returns a placement and sizing that realizes the motion at least electrical
energy, a lower bound on that energy, or a dual certificate that no placement realizes the motion. The mechanism is
Frontier, and its value is uncertain by its own account: it decides only the placement-and-sizing share of a design
decision and only for motions it was given.

GATE-AUGUR-29 (claims: Known answer and Design comparison; M4; thresholds TARGET): the cart, boundary 35/654 m in
exact rationals, the dual at 4 m/s² accepted by `frob-verify`. The regressor identity Y·π equals the derived inverse
dynamics within 10⁻¹² relative on 10⁴ random states per reference robot. A request for jointly optimal energy and
ratio is routed to enumeration. On 30 designs and two missions, candidates initialized by the lane beat their class
templates by at least 15% in electrical cost of transport in closed-loop evaluation at a 95% paired lower bound, or
start within 10% of the optimum that forward co-design reaches; the share of designs infeasible on every library
motion that still pass the mission is reported.

The last clause is the honest one: a design the lane calls infeasible on every motion it knows may still pass the
mission by a motion outside the library, and the gate requires that share to be measured. This bead owns the script
and the experiment protocol. Rejected alternative: scoring the lane by the program's own energy objective (that is
the optimizer grading itself; only closed-loop evaluation on the mission counts).

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/augur_placement_lane.sh`; the pre-registered comparison on 30 designs and two missions; the gate
record. Out: the lane and the regressor (N25e-20, N25e-19); the kill decision (N25e-22).

## Technical approach

Part A, known answers (CPU, exact).
1. Regressor identity: for each reference robot, `frob analyze regressor-identity --robot <id> --states 10000
   --seed 7 --json`; assert the maximum relative error is at most 10⁻¹².
2. Cart: `frob mission synthesize fixtures/augur/cart-placement/accel3.fmission --strategy placement
   --key pl-cart3-<run> --json`; assert feasibility; again with `--objective first-moment:battery` and
   `--key pl-cart3-fm-<run>`, assert the boundary x_b = "35/654" m. `…/accel4.fmission` (`--key pl-cart4-<run>`):
   assert the outcome is the dual certificate with the traction inequality binding, the scope tag
   `(cell, motion)`, and no cell exclusion; `frob prove bundle <answer> --key pl-bundle4-<run> --json` and
   `frob-verify` under `env -i` accept the dual. `…/accel4-awd.fmission` (`--key pl-awd-<run>`): assert feasible.
3. Routing: `frob mission synthesize … --strategy placement --ratios continuous --objective energy
   --key pl-route-<run> --json`; assert the result says `routed: enumeration` and lists one program per catalog
   ratio.

Idempotency keys. Every run-verb call carries `--key` and no two calls the script compares share one (a shared
key returns the first call's stored receipt): one key per fixture and objective in Part A, and in Part B one
per design, mission and arm for synthesis, controller synthesis, evaluation and comparison. The determinism
lane's second run of Part A uses its own `<run>` suffix. `frob analyze regressor-identity`, `frob phylum
generate` and `frob prove verify` are read verbs and take none.

Part B, design comparison (pre-registered in `docs/prereg/augur_placement_lane.md` before any run).
1. Designs: 30 designs drawn by the morphology generator from at least two archetype classes (legged and wheeled),
   each with a movable battery or ballast and a catalog of motors and ratios; the draw's seed and split are
   frozen (`frob phylum generate --generator <gen> --count 30 --seed <seed> --json`, its output digest in the
   pre-registration).
2. Missions: two, with requirement verdicts and the electrical cost-of-transport metric (electrical energy per
   unit weight per unit distance), for example velocity tracking on flat ground and payload transport up a ramp.
3. Arms per design: T, the class template's placement and sizing; P, the candidate initialized by the lane (best
   placement and catalog choice over the library motions, `frob mission synthesize <d>.fmission --strategy
   placement --key pl-P-<d>-<m>-<run> --json`); F, the optimum reached by forward co-design from the template
   under the campaign's budget (the reference; DESIGN reading of "forward co-design" for a fixed topology:
   the optimization chapter's lever search over the same placement and sizing levers, started from the
   template, `frob opt start --task <m> --design <template of d> --levers <placement and sizing levers>
   --budget <declared> --key pl-F-<d>-<m>-<run> --json`).
4. Evaluation: closed loop, the same controller acquisition rule for every arm (`frob control synth <arm design>
   --key pl-ctl-<d>-<arm>-<run> --json`), paired on common world addresses, on a determinism-controlled profile
   (`frob eval run <arm design> --battery <m> --key pl-ev-<d>-<m>-<arm>-<run> --json`). Metric per design and
   mission: electrical cost of transport among episodes that satisfy the requirement, with both per-completion
   figures published.
5. Statistics: the paired relative improvement of P over T across designs and missions, with a 95% paired lower
   bound (the anytime-valid paired comparison, `frob eval compare <T> <P> --task <m> --key pl-cmp-<d>-<m>-<run>
   --json`, or a fixed-n study through `frob study plan` and `frob study submit --key …`, declared in advance in
   the pre-registration, never mixed). Pass
   condition (either): lower bound of the improvement at least 15%; or P's cost within 10% of F's on the
   designs where F converged.
6. Screening audit: for every design the lane reports infeasible on every library motion, run the mission in
   closed loop anyway. Report the share that pass. More than 15% is a kill trigger (the motion-count screening
   signal is withdrawn first).
7. Ceiling before speed: no speed claim is made; the record states the share of each design decision that was
   placement and sizing.

Script: Part A always; Part B in a smoke form (3 designs, 1 mission) by default and in full with `--campaign`
under a job handle whose result is read back for the gate record. The gate is recorded from a full run by a
session that did not implement N25e-19 or N25e-20, through `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate
-- --gate GATE-AUGUR-29 --receipt <hash>`; a smoke run never records it.

Logging (STD-E2E): `target/e2e-logs/augur_placement_lane/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line:
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25e-21`), `step`, `event`, exact command, key, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block. `metric` lines: robot and maximum regressor error against 10⁻¹²; the cart's exact values as strings; per
design and mission the three arms' cost of transport with units, the paired improvement and its lower bound
against 15%, the ratio to F against 1.10, and the share of infeasible-everywhere designs that pass against 15%. On
failure: the first failing robot, state seed or design, with replay addresses. No private-scene data.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script self-tests in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `placement_selftest_cart_boundary.sh` (a fixture with the battery mass
changed to 6 kg must fail the boundary assertion); `placement_selftest_unpaired.sh` (arms on different world
addresses are refused); `placement_selftest_share.sh` (a synthetic result with 5 of 20 infeasible-everywhere
designs passing reports 25% and flags the kill trigger). Analysis tests in
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/placement_analysis.rs`: `paired_improvement_lower_bound` on synthetic data with a
planted 20% improvement; `within_ten_percent_of_reference`; `smoke_run_never_records` (a 3-design run's report
is marked `small-only` and the recorder refuses it); `keys_distinct_per_arm` (the key generator never gives two
arms, designs or missions one key).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; steps and logging as above.

### Known answers and negative controls

Cart: 35/654 m; infeasible at 4 m/s² with the traction dual; feasible with all wheels driven (N25-KA's
`augur-late-07`, cited by tag until N38a-40 issues N25-KA's block). Regressor: 10⁻¹².
Negative controls: an A/A run of Part B (T against T) whose improvement interval must contain zero; a planted lane
that returns the template unchanged (improvement zero, the gate's first clause must fail); a regressor with a
flipped column (Part A must fail). Determinism lanes: Part A twice on one machine, debug and release, both hosts;
Part B under the declared determinism class with paired addresses.

## Considerations

- KILL-SYNTH-REGRESSOR (N25e-22) reads this record: failure of the comparison part, or more than 15% of
  infeasible-everywhere designs passing.
- Cost of transport is only defined on completing episodes; a design that completes less often can look
  cheaper, so both per-completion figures and the completion rates are in the record.
- Forward co-design may not converge on some designs within budget; those are excluded from the 10% clause and
  listed.
