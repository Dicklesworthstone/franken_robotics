## Background

Region certificates decide one constraint over a box of design parameters by outward-rounded enclosure and
bisection. Bisection cannot be spread over many parameters: 10⁴ boxes are 13 bisections, less than one cut per axis
in 20 dimensions. The first reading of the governing decision (ADR-AUGUR-06) was therefore that a certificate's box
has at most about six parameters. That reading is too narrow. What is bounded is the number of parameters that are
bisected, not the number the constraint reads. A constraint may depend on every parameter of a family and still be
decided over most of the box, when most of that dependence is weak.

Definitions. For a lowered constraint g(x) ≤ 0 on a box B ⊂ ℝⁿ, an affine-form enclosure gives
g(x) ∈ g₀ + Σᵢ aᵢ·εᵢ + r·[−1, 1] with εᵢ ∈ [−1, 1]. Parameter i contributes width 2·|aᵢ| and the nonlinear remainder
contributes 2·r. Order the parameters by |aᵢ|. The split set S is the leading part of that order, capped at six. The
other parameters are weak, and the weak width is ω = 2·(Σ_{i ∉ S} |aᵢ| + r) with every weak parameter at its full
range. Bisect only inside S, enclose the weak parameters at full range, and stop splitting a sub-box when the width
contributed by S falls below ω, because then no further split inside S can decide it.

The mechanism is Frontier [F]. Its supporting HYPOTHESIS: for the static constraints of legged and wheeled classes,
the parameters of other limbs and of distal bodies enter one leg's torque, reach and clearance constraints weakly,
through posture and load share. Before product work, a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier: this bead. Rejected
alternative: piloting directly on the 12-parameter quadruped family (its truth is unknown, so an unsound driver
could look good there; the pilot needs a case with an exact answer and a case built to make the rule fail safely).

## Scope

In: a standalone prototype of the driver on two analytic constraints systems, with exact expected output. Out: the
product driver and verbs (N25e-24); the family comparison (N25e-25); the kill decision (N25e-26).

## Technical approach

Location (DESIGN): `crates/frob-conformance/pilots/weak_variable/`; affine forms from the admitted interval
library through the airlock, or a 200-line exact affine-form evaluator for these polynomial cases.

Case 1, the known answer. On [0, 1]²⁰ let m = (1/19)·Σ_{i=2..20} (2·xᵢ − 1) and δ = 0.01·m², so 0 ≤ δ ≤ 0.01. Require
g₁ = x₁ + δ − 0.8 ≤ 0 and g₂ = 0.2 + δ − x₁ ≤ 0. Both constraints read all 20 parameters and neither is monotone in x₂
to x₂₀. At the root box the affine form of δ has zero linear coefficients (m ranges over [−1, 1], m² over [0, 1])
and remainder 0.005, so the ranking puts x₁ first with a₁ = ½ and S = {x₁}; the weak width is 0.01. Expected
output, in exact rationals:

| Slab of x₁ | Verdict | By |
|---|---|---|
| [0, 1/5) | excluded | g₂ (lower end above 0 for every weak value) |
| [1/5, 21/100) | unresolved, reason `weak-variable width` | g₂ straddles |
| [21/100, 79/100] | inner for both | both upper ends at most 0 |
| (79/100, 4/5] | unresolved, reason `weak-variable width` | g₁ straddles |
| (4/5, 1] | excluded | g₁ |

Five slabs decide 0.98 of the volume: 0.58 inner and 0.40 excluded; 0.02 unresolved. An isotropic paving of the
same box with the budget of 10⁴ boxes (13 bisections, less than one cut per axis) decides almost none of it; the
pilot runs that arm too and records its decided share.

Case 2, the negative control. g = Σ_{i=1..20} xᵢ − 10 ≤ 0 on the same box. Every parameter contributes the same
width, 1. With any six parameters in S the weak width is 14 and the sum over S ranges over [0, 6]. An inner cell
would need that sum to be at most −4 and an excluded cell would need it above 10; neither can occur. The stopping
rule fires at once, since 6 < 14. The mechanism must return `unresolved` for the whole box after one enclosure and
no bisection, although half the volume is feasible by symmetry. A run that bisects here burns budget, and a run
that returns an inner or excluded cell is unsound. The exact answer to this constraint belongs to the monotone
rule. That is the point of the control: the split-set rule is for dependence that is weak, not for strong
dependence spread evenly.

Procedure: implement the queue algorithm (enclose with weak parameters at full range; decide, cut or bisect inside
S; stop when the S-width falls below ω; budget 10⁴ boxes per constraint), run both cases, compare with the tables,
and audit every decided slab with 10⁶ random points evaluated in exact rationals.

Falsifier (fixed before the run): any decided cell contradicted by a point; Case 1 not reproduced (shares 0.58,
0.40, 0.02); Case 2 spending more than one enclosure evaluation or returning any decided cell. Ceiling, stated
first: no speed claim; decided volume is evidence quality and tolerance robustness, and the number that matters is
candidate evaluations avoided.

Pilot record (N38a-09). Before the first run this bead writes the protocol (both cases, the expected tables, the
queue algorithm and its stopping rule, the budget, the audit's point count and seed, the falsifier, the ceiling) to
`docs/pilots/PILOT-WEAKVAR-01.md` and the record `[[pilot]]` `PILOT-WEAKVAR-01` (DESIGN identifier) in
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`, schema `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`: `mechanism` weak-variable
enclosure with a bounded split set; `tier = "F"`; `owner = "§25.34"`; `bead = "N25e-23"`; `work_package =
"WP-66"`; `protocol = { path, blake3, frozen, ledger_seq }`; `falsifier` as above, with its counts (zero
contradicting points of 10⁶; one enclosure evaluation and zero bisections on Case 2); `ceiling = { statement =
"candidate evaluations avoided", value = "none on speed", basis = "decided volume is evidence quality, not time" }`;
`fallback = { ships = "ADR-AUGUR-06 as first written: no region certificate above six parameters, pointwise
checks", kill_gate = "KILL-WEAKVAR" }`; `cost`; `tool` (site `host`); `status = "registered"`; `integrated_gate =
"GATE-AUGUR-30"`; `gated_beads = ["N25e-24", "N25e-25"]` (the product driver and its gate script, which exist only

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/pilots/weak_variable/tests.rs`: `delta_range` (0 ≤ δ ≤ 1/100, attained at m = 0 and
m = ±1); `affine_form_of_delta` (centre 0.005, zero linear part, remainder 0.005); `slab_boundaries_exact`
(g₂'s upper end 0.2 + 0.01 − x₁ ≤ 0 iff x₁ ≥ 21/100; lower end 0.2 − x₁ > 0 iff x₁ < 1/5; symmetric for g₁);
`shares_sum_to_one`; `control_one_enclosure` (enclosure counter equals 1); `control_no_decided_cell`;
`stop_rule_fires_when_s_width_below_omega` (6 < 14).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/augur_pilot_weak_variable.sh` asserts that `PILOT-WEAKVAR-01` is registered and that the protocol file
hashes to its registered digest, runs the pilot binary, asserts the criteria, appends the `[[pilot.result]]`, and
logs through the STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/augur_pilot_weak_variable/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`, `script`,
`bead` (`N25e-23`), `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time; `metric` lines with slab boundaries as exact
strings, the three shares, S, ω, enclosure and bisection counters with their thresholds; on failure the first
contradicting point. The `summary` names GATE-AUGUR-30 (pilot reading).

### Known answers and negative controls

Case 1 and Case 2 as above (no Appendix M number exists yet: they are registered by tag as `augur-late-09` and
`augur-late-10` in N25-KA until N38a-40 issues their numbers). Additional negative controls: a driver that keeps
bisecting below ω (budget counter must exceed the expected count and fail); a driver that encloses weak parameters
at their midpoint instead of full range (it "decides" the slabs [1/5, 21/100) and (79/100, 4/5] and the point audit
must contradict it with m = ±1). Metamorphic relations: permuting x₂ … x₂₀ changes nothing; replacing 0.01 by 0.02
moves the inner slab to [0.22, 0.78] and the shares to 0.56, 0.40, 0.04. Determinism: class D0.

## Considerations

- The pilot's Case 1 is built to succeed; its value is in pinning the exact slab logic, the open and closed
  ends, and the stopping rule. Whether real constraints have weak dependence is measured by OPEN-AUGUR-3
  (N25e-27) and by the family comparison (N25e-25).
- Exact slab boundaries need a threshold cut along the leading parameter, not midpoint bisection; with midpoint
  bisection the unresolved share exceeds 0.02. The pilot records both variants so the product driver's choice
  is informed.
