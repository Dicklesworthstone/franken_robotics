## Background

Region certificates (BD-358) decide one static constraint over a box of design parameters by outward-rounded
enclosure, with bisection confined to about six parameters. The decision behind that cap (ADR-AUGUR-06) was first
read as "a certificate's box has at most about six parameters", which would refuse every constraint that reads more
(`FROB-REF-856`). This bead corrects the reading and extends the mechanism: what is bounded is the number of
parameters that are bisected, not the number the constraint reads. A constraint may depend on every parameter of a
family and still be decided over most of the box, when most of that dependence is weak. Tier Frontier [F]; the
pilot N25e-23 must have reproduced its two cases first.

HYPOTHESIS that makes this worth building: for the static constraints of legged and wheeled classes, the
parameters of other limbs and of distal bodies enter one leg's torque, reach and clearance constraints weakly,
through posture and load share. OPEN-AUGUR-3 measures the constraint-to-parameter graph and, for this mechanism,
records |aᵢ| per parameter and not only incidence (N25e-27). Ceiling: no speed claim. Decided volume is evidence
quality and tolerance robustness; the number that matters is candidate evaluations avoided. Rejected alternatives:
raising the split cap (the bisection arithmetic does not change: 13 cuts are 13 cuts); fixing weak parameters at
nominal values (unsound: a certificate must hold for every value in the box).

## Scope

In: the affine-form ranking, the choice of S, the queue algorithm with its stopping rule, storage of per-constraint
sets over the whole box, the `--split-cap` flag and the report fields on `mission.refute` and `mission.synthesize`.
Out: the base paving, contractors and combination (BD-358 with its parts N25b-09 and N25b-10); the checkers
(`interval-box` in BD-132, `taylor-box` in N12-16); the family comparison (N25e-25).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-mission/src/region/weak.rs` (DESIGN). Enclosures come from the interval and Taylor-model
arithmetic behind the airlock; certificates are of the existing kinds.

Definitions. For a lowered constraint g(x) ≤ 0 on a box B ⊂ ℝⁿ, an affine-form enclosure gives
g(x) ∈ g₀ + Σᵢ aᵢ·εᵢ + r·[−1, 1] with εᵢ ∈ [−1, 1]. Parameter i contributes width 2·|aᵢ| and the nonlinear remainder
contributes 2·r. Order the parameters by |aᵢ| (ties by parameter index). The split set S is the leading part of
that order, capped at six (the cap of ADR-AUGUR-06; `--split-cap k`). The other parameters are weak, and the weak
width is ω = 2·(Σ_{i ∉ S} |aᵢ| + r) with every weak parameter at its full range.

Choice of S (DESIGN, within the latitude the mechanism leaves to `frob-mission`): S is the shortest leading prefix,
of length at most k, whose weak width ω is at most one quarter of the width 2·Σ_{i ∈ S} |aᵢ| it contributes itself;
if no prefix of length at most k qualifies, S is the first k parameters. The enclosure library must use a
dependency-aware square (x² over [−1, 1] is [0, 1], not [−1, 1]); the known answer's weak width of 0.01 depends on
it, and a library without it doubles that width.

Algorithm. A queue holds sub-boxes that differ from B only in the coordinates of S.
1. Enclose g over the sub-box with every weak parameter at full range. Upper end at most 0: inner. Lower end
   above 0: excluded. Either result is an `interval-box` or `taylor-box` certificate whose box is the full
   n-dimensional sub-box, with S recorded. The checker's work is linear in n per box.
2. Otherwise cut. First try a threshold cut along the leading parameter of S (DESIGN refinement, a
   one-dimensional contractor): from the affine form on the sub-box, compute the sub-interval of that parameter
   on which the upper end is at most 0 and the sub-interval on which the lower end is above 0, with the other
   parameters at full range; if either is nonempty, split there and re-enclose each piece (a piece is certified
   only by its own enclosure, never by the linearization). If no threshold cut exists, bisect along the
   parameter of S with the largest current contribution. The enclosure is linearized again on each sub-box, so
   the aᵢ are local and the order may change.
3. Stop splitting a sub-box when the width contributed by S falls below ω. No further split inside S can decide
   it, because the weak parameters alone straddle the threshold. The cell is `unresolved` with the reason
   `weak-variable width`, and the report names the weak parameter with the largest |aᵢ| as the next candidate.
4. The budget is the box count of the base mechanism, per constraint (10⁴ boxes).
5. Open and closed ends: a slab is inner where the upper end is ≤ 0 (closed at equality) and excluded where the
   lower end is > 0 (open at equality); adjacent decided boxes with one verdict are merged into slabs for the
   report and stay separate certificates in the bundle, tied by a `partition`.

Combination across constraints is unchanged: a design is inner when it lies in every constraint's inner set. Each
set is now stored over the whole box with its split set recorded.

Verbs. `mission.refute` and `mission.synthesize` take `--split-cap k` (default 6), and `mission.refute` gains the
region spellings BD-358 gives `mission.synthesize` (`--region per-constraint`, `--boxes`), so that
`frob mission refute <mission> --region per-constraint --split-cap 6 --key <k> --json` runs this driver (both are
run verbs and take the idempotency key; VERBS-mission carries `--split-cap` on both rows and `--region`,
`--boxes` on `mission.refute`, from M4). Both report, per
constraint: S, ω, the three volume shares (inner, excluded, unresolved), and the driver's counters
(`enclosures`, `bisections`, `threshold_cuts`, `boxes`), with unresolved cells carrying their reason and next
candidate parameter. A constraint that reads more than k parameters is no longer refused outright; `FROB-REF-856`
("a region certificate is requested over more parameters than the admitted limit, or a joint paving is
requested") remains for a joint paving request and for `--split-cap` above 6, the bisection limit of
ADR-AUGUR-06. The flag's schema states only its minimum, 1 (VERBS-mission): `--split-cap 0` fails the schema
(`FROB-REF-802`), and a cap of 7 or more passes the schema and is refused by the handler with the domain refusal
856 naming the bisection limit (a schema maximum of 6 would turn it into 802, which names no limit and no
repair).

Explanation type: `Explanation::StraddlesThreshold` gains the detail `weak-variable width { omega, next: ParamRef }`
in the answer's unresolved cells. Evidence: decided boxes `Certified`; shares are exact volumes of box unions.
Determinism class D0 for the box set (canonical queue order).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The 20-parameter case returns five slabs with shares 0.58 (inner), 0.40 (excluded) and 0.02 (unresolved), S = {x₁} and ω = 0.01 for both constraints, and `frob-verify` accepts every certificate.
- [ ] The negative control g = Σ xᵢ − 10 ≤ 0 on [0, 1]²⁰ returns `unresolved` for the whole box with one enclosure evaluation and no bisection, reason `weak-variable width`.
- [ ] Every certificate's box is the full n-dimensional sub-box with its split set recorded; a certificate that fixes a weak parameter at a point is rejected by the checker.
- [ ] `--split-cap k` is honoured for k from 1 to 6 on both `mission.refute` and `mission.synthesize` (the flag BD-358 introduced for synthesis, extended here); k above 6 is refused with `FROB-REF-856` and k = 0 with `FROB-REF-802`; the report lists S, ω and the three shares per constraint.
- [ ] No certified box is contradicted by 10⁶ sampled points on the pilot cases and on the 12-parameter family.
- [ ] A cell stopped by rule 3 is never reported as inner or excluded, and its next candidate parameter is named.
- [ ] CPU reference path; reachable through `frob --json`; cancellation drains; `CONTRACT.md` updated.

### Known answers and negative controls

The 20-parameter case (five slabs; 0.58, 0.40, 0.02) and the evenly-spread control (unresolved after one
enclosure). Negative controls: enclosing weak parameters at their midpoint (the point audit contradicts it at
m = ±1); continuing to bisect below ω (bisection counter exceeds the bound); returning inner for half of the
control's box (unsound; audit contradicts). No Appendix M number exists for these rows: they are N25-KA's late
rows `augur-late-09` and `augur-late-10`, cited by the KA numbers N38a-40 issues to those slugs from N25-KA's
block (a tag in a test name or derivation file fails `provisional-tag-past-due`). Metamorphic relations:
permuting weak parameters changes nothing; scaling the weak coefficient from 0.01 to 0.02 gives shares 0.56, 0.40,
0.04. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release; identical box sets and
bundle digests.

## Considerations

- KILL-WEAKVAR (N25e-26): the family comparison fails, or more than 90% of the volume of constraints that read
  more than six parameters stays unresolved at budget on the reference families. Fallback: ADR-AUGUR-06 as first
  written: a constraint that reads more than six parameters receives no region certificate and is checked
  pointwise.
- The mechanism decides volume only where the weak width is small against the constraint's margin; constraints
  with strong dependence spread evenly stop at once, by design, and go to the monotone rule or to pointwise
  checks.
- The threshold cut is an efficiency device; soundness rests only on the per-piece enclosure and the checker.
- Checker cost grows linearly with n per box; 10⁴ boxes of 20 parameters must fit the query's checker budget.
