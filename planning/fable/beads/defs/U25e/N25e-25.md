## Background

Weak-variable enclosure extends region certificates from constraints that read at most about six design
parameters to constraints that read any number, by bisecting only a split set S of at most six parameters and
enclosing the others (the weak parameters) at full range. A sub-box stops being split when the width contributed by
S falls below the weak width ω, because the weak parameters alone then straddle the threshold.

GATE-AUGUR-30 (claims: Known answer and Design comparison; M4; thresholds TARGET): the 20-parameter case returns
five slabs with shares 0.58, 0.40 and 0.02, and `frob-verify` accepts every certificate. The negative control
returns `unresolved` for the whole box with one enclosure evaluation and no bisection. On the 12-parameter static
quadruped family, against per-constraint decomposition restricted to constraints that read at most six parameters
and at the same box budget: at least 10 percentage points more decided volume, or at least 10% more candidate
evaluations avoided, with no certificate rejected.

The comparison's baseline is the mechanism as first decided: constraints that read more than six parameters get no
region certificate and are checked pointwise. "Candidate evaluations avoided" is the number that matters, because
decided volume is only useful where candidates fall. This bead owns the script and the comparison protocol.
Rejected alternative: comparing against an isotropic paving (a straw man; it decides almost nothing in 12
dimensions).

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/augur_weak_variable.sh`; the frozen candidate stream; the two-arm comparison; the gate record.
Out: the driver (N25e-24); the kill decision (N25e-26).

## Technical approach

Part A, known answers through the public verb, on N25e-24's fixtures (`W=fixtures/augur/weak-variable`).
1. `frob mission refute $W/slabs20.fmission --region per-constraint --split-cap 6 --key weak-known-<run>
   --json`. Assert
   five slabs with boundaries "1/5", "21/100", "79/100", "4/5"; verdicts excluded, unresolved, inner, unresolved,
   excluded; shares "29/50", "2/5", "1/50"; S = {x₁} and ω = "1/100" for both constraints.
2. `frob prove bundle a:<answer of step 1> --out <dir> --key weak-bundle-<run> --json`, then the standalone
   `frob-verify <dir>/<bundle>.frobcert --json` under `env -i` on every certificate; assert `accept`.
3. `frob mission refute $W/sum20.fmission --region per-constraint --split-cap 6 --key weak-control-<run> --json`
   (the constraint Σ xᵢ − 10 ≤ 0). Assert one cell, `unresolved`, reason `weak-variable width`; read the driver's
   counters from the result (`enclosures: 1`, `bisections: 0`).
4. Point audit: 10⁶ seeded points evaluated in exact rationals against the decided slabs; assert zero
   contradictions.
5. Negative control of the gate: the driver that encloses the weak parameters at their midpoint instead of their
   full range, a registered mutation control in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` (a `cfg(feature =
   "nc-<nnnn>")` branch of `frob-mission`, built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-mission/nc-<nnnn>`,
   numbered at registration by N38b-04), rerun on steps 1 and 4 with keys `weak-mut-<step>-<run>`; the point
   audit must find contradictions (at m = ±1, where the true δ is 1/100) and the script's checks fail.

Part B, design comparison on the 12-parameter static quadruped family (protocol pre-registered in
`docs/prereg/augur_weak_variable.md` before the first run).
1. Arms at the same box budget (10⁴ boxes per constraint): arm R, per-constraint decomposition restricted to
   constraints that read at most six parameters (the others are checked pointwise only); arm W, the same with
   weak-variable enclosure for constraints that read more.
2. Decided volume: for each arm, the volume of the design box that is decided (inner for every constraint, or
   excluded by some constraint), estimated on a frozen sample of 10⁶ points with an exact membership test per
   point (class `Estimated`, with its binomial interval). Metric 1: the difference in percentage points.
3. Candidate evaluations avoided: a frozen stream of 10,000 candidate designs (from the morphology generator,
   `frob phylum generate` of BD-287, restricted to the family, seed pinned). A candidate is avoided when it falls in an excluded cell (no
   pointwise static check and no simulation is needed) or in an inner cell for the static constraints (the
   static check is skipped). Metric 2: the relative difference in avoided candidates between arms.
4. Pass: metric 1 at least 10 points, or metric 2 at least 10%, with no certificate rejected by `frob-verify`
   in either arm and zero contradictions in a point audit of arm W's boxes.
5. Kill-gate input: the share of the volume of constraints that read more than six parameters that stays
   unresolved at budget in arm W.

Script: Part A always; Part B fully (it is CPU-only and bounded by the box budget).

Idempotency keys: every `frob mission refute` call carries its own key (`--key weak-<known|control>-<run>` in
Part A; `--key weak-<arm R|W>-<constraint>-<run>` per arm in Part B, so the two arms, which are compared, never
return each other's receipts), and `frob prove bundle` carries `--key weak-bundle-<run>`; `frob phylum generate`
and `frob-verify` are reads. The script ends with a `summary` carrying the receipt hash and never records a value
(STD-E2E's `self-recording` lint); the gate, which the register holds as one part covering both halves, is
recorded once from the receipt of a full run by a session that did not implement N25e-24, with `cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-30 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19), which refuses the
record unless the control of Part A step 5 is listed as caught in the same run.

Logging (STD-E2E): `target/e2e-logs/augur_weak_variable/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N25e-25`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block.
`metric` lines: slab boundaries and shares as exact strings; S, ω; enclosure and bisection counters against their
thresholds (1 and 0 for the control); per arm decided volume with its interval, avoided candidates, the two
differences against their thresholds (10 points; 10%), rejected certificates (threshold 0), the unresolved share of
wide constraints (kill threshold 90%). On failure: the first contradicting point or rejected certificate with its
path.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script self-tests in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `weak_selftest_share.sh` (a result with shares 0.56, 0.40, 0.04 fails
Part A and reports the expected shares); `weak_selftest_control_bisects.sh` (a result with `bisections: 3` on the
control fails); `weak_selftest_budget_mismatch.sh` (arms with different box budgets are refused). Analysis tests in
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/weak_analysis.rs`: `decided_volume_estimator` (on the known answer the sampled
decided share is 0.98 within its interval); `avoided_count` on a synthetic stream.

identical because seeds are pinned).

## Considerations

- KILL-WEAKVAR fires if the comparison fails or if more than 90% of the volume of wide constraints stays
  unresolved at budget; the fallback is to refuse region certificates above six parameters and check pointwise.
- If the family's constraints turn out to read few parameters each, arm W equals arm R and the comparison fails
  honestly: the mechanism is then unneeded for that family. OPEN-AUGUR-3's measurement (N25e-27) predicts which.
- Decided volume far from where candidates fall avoids nothing; that is why the gate accepts either metric and
  records both.
