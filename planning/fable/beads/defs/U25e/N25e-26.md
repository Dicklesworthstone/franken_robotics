## Background

A kill gate names its trigger and its fallback in advance; its decision is an architecture decision record at its
milestone, and a fired gate means the fallback is the plan.

KILL-WEAKVAR governs weak-variable enclosure: region certificates for constraints that read more than six design
parameters, obtained by bisecting only a split set of at most six parameters and enclosing the rest at full range.
The governing decision (ADR-AUGUR-06: region evidence is per constraint, with a bounded split set) was first read
as refusing any constraint that reads more than six parameters. The extension rests on a hypothesis: that most of
the dependence of a leg's static constraints on other limbs and distal bodies is weak.

- Trigger: the comparison of GATE-AUGUR-30 fails (on the 12-parameter static quadruped family, at the same box
  budget, weak-variable enclosure yields neither at least 10 percentage points more decided volume nor at least
  10% more candidate evaluations avoided than per-constraint decomposition restricted to constraints that read at
  most six parameters, or a certificate is rejected); or more than 90% of the volume of constraints that read
  more than six parameters stays unresolved at budget on the reference families.
- Fallback: ADR-AUGUR-06 as first written: a constraint that reads more than six parameters receives no region
  certificate and is checked pointwise.

The register gives the gate no milestone of its own; it is decided at M4 with GATE-AUGUR-30.

## Scope

In: reading the record of N25e-25 and the sparsity measurement of N25e-27, the decision, its record and the switch.
Out: the measurements.

## Technical approach

1. Inputs: from N25e-25, the two comparison metrics with their intervals, the count of rejected certificates,
   the point-audit contradictions and the unresolved share of wide constraints in the weak-variable arm; from
   N25e-27, the distribution of per-parameter contributions |aᵢ| on the reference families (context for the
   decision, not a trigger).
2. Repair rule. A rejected certificate or an audit contradiction is a soundness defect: it gets one repair cycle
   and a full re-run; if it recurs, the gate fires and the false certificate procedure applies (the proof kind
   and checker version are disabled until the cause is found, stored bundles are withdrawn). A failed
   comparison gets one repair cycle limited to the choice of the split set and the enclosure arithmetic; the
   box budget and the baseline are not touched.
3. Record: `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-WEAKVAR.md` with the trigger as written, measured values, commit, receipts, the
   decision (`pass` or `killed: fallback adopted`) and date; the gate row in `registries/gates.toml` cites it
   and resolves in the artifact graph.
4. If fired, mechanical changes:
   - `mission.refute` and `mission.synthesize` refuse a region certificate for a constraint that reads more
     parameters than the split cap, with `FROB-REF-856` and the repair "per-constraint decomposition, or
     pointwise checks"; the weak-variable path is removed from the worklist;
   - such constraints are checked pointwise at proposed designs, and untested regions are reported as sample
     coverage, class `Estimated`;
   - `--split-cap` remains as the cap of the base mechanism; the report fields S and ω are dropped from the
     result schema at the next schema version and kept readable in stored answers;
   - stored bundles whose boxes relied on weak-variable enclosure stay valid (their certificates are sound
     per box); they are simply no longer produced.
5. If not fired: the status table records both metrics and the unresolved share; the mechanism stays Frontier
   and is described for "static constraints with weak cross-limb dependence on the reference families", no more
   broadly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists at the M4 exit with every measured value traceable through `evidence.trace`.
- [ ] The gate row has status `pass` or `killed: fallback adopted`, citing commit, command, output and receipt hash.
- [ ] If fired: a constraint over seven parameters is refused a region certificate with `FROB-REF-856` (test), and the worklist no longer schedules weak-variable enclosure.
- [ ] The decision session did not implement N25e-24.
- [ ] A change to trigger, threshold or fallback appears only as a dated amendment made before the measurement.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/region_weak_fallback.rs`: `wide_constraint_refused_in_fallback` (with the fallback
configuration the 20-parameter known answer is refused with `FROB-REF-856` and the pointwise repair);
`narrow_constraint_unaffected` (a three-parameter constraint is certified as before). A registry check verifies the
gate row cites an existing ADR.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/augur_kill_weakvar_fallback.sh`: in the fallback profile, runs `frob mission refute
fixtures/augur/weak20/known.fmission --region per-constraint --json` and asserts exit 3 with `FROB-REF-856`; runs a
pointwise `frob design check` on three points of the same family and asserts verdicts. Runs once in a dry-run
profile at M4 whatever the decision. Logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/augur_kill_weakvar_fallback/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` (command, exit code, refusal block, `summary`
naming KILL-WEAKVAR).

### Known answers and negative controls

Trigger arithmetic: a decided-volume difference of 9 points and an avoided-candidates difference of 8% fails; 12
points passes alone; 4 points with 15% avoided passes. An unresolved share of 91% of wide-constraint volume fires
the second clause. A dry run fed a planted failing comparison must record `killed: fallback adopted`. Negative
control: the A/A comparison of N25e-25 must fail the comparison clause (both differences zero).

## Considerations

- The fallback costs nothing already built: the base mechanism (BD-358) never depended on the extension.
- The second clause can fire even when the comparison passes (a few constraints decide a lot while most wide
  ones stay unresolved); the gate then fires, by its wording, and the passing families are noted for a later
  amendment.
- Owner of the decision: the Augur lead; confirmed at the M4 exit review.
