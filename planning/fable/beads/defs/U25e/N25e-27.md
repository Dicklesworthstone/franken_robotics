## Background

Region certificates decide one static constraint over a box of design parameters, bisecting at most about six
parameters. The approach presumes that static constraints are sparse: that joint torque, reach and clearance of one
leg depend on roughly three to six parameters of that leg. That is a HYPOTHESIS, not a measurement, and closure
constraints of linkage legs raise the coupled dimension. The open question OPEN-AUGUR-3 asks for the measurement:
the sparsity of the constraint-to-parameter graph for the program's robot classes, and the coupled dimension that
linkage closure adds. Its owner is the Augur lead, it blocks GATE-AUGUR-09 (region certificates on the 12-parameter
box; pilot at M3, gate at M4), and its fail-closed default is: region certificates are refused above six
parameters.

The weak-variable extension changed what must be measured. Incidence alone (which parameters a constraint reads)
no longer decides whether a constraint can be certified; what matters is how strongly each parameter enters. For a
lowered constraint g(x) ≤ 0 on a box, an affine-form enclosure gives g(x) ∈ g₀ + Σᵢ aᵢ·εᵢ + r·[−1, 1] with
εᵢ ∈ [−1, 1]; parameter i contributes width 2·|aᵢ| and the nonlinear remainder 2·r. OPEN-AUGUR-3 therefore now
records |aᵢ| per parameter, not only incidence. This bead is the experiment that answers the question, so that
it is settled by data before the product mechanism is built. It is an experiment bead and not a design
decision: the default holds until its report is on file. Rejected alternative: answering from the expressions'
syntax alone (a parameter can appear in an expression and contribute nothing measurable, or appear once and
dominate).

There is one experiment for OPEN-AUGUR-3, this one. The lowering of the static analyses to constraint
expressions and the graph artifact are a mechanism of their own (N25b-08), which an earlier draft combined with
a second copy of this experiment; the measurement over classes, the decision rule and the registration that it
carried are folded in here.

## Scope

In: the families measured and the registration of the experiment before it runs; the measurement over those
families (incidence statistics, strengths on the root box and on sub-boxes, the effective split size, the
closure comparison, the interaction graph); the report; the recommendation to the owner with its rule; the ADR;
the check that the default is in force until then.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- the expression-recording scalar, the lowering of the mass, reach, torque and clearance constraints, the graph
  artifact `frob.constraint-graph/1` with S_k and the root-box weights, its `affine_on(sub_box)` evaluation,
  the second independent route with its exact comparison (PO-AUGUR-01), the planted dense constraint and the
  driver that prints a family's graph: N25b-08, on which this bead depends. This bead lowers nothing itself:
  every number it reports is computed from that artifact;
- the paving driver (N25b-09, BD-358), the weak-variable driver (N25e-24), the pilot's four-arm comparison
  (BD-289).

## Technical approach

Tool. The measurement functions are in `crates/frob-mission/src/region/measure.rs`, with enclosures from the
admitted interval library through the graph's `affine_on`. They are reachable two ways:
- the verb `analyze.constraint-graph` (MCP tool `analyze_constraint_graph`; owner `frob-mission`; capability
  `analyze:read`; output schema `frob.analyze.constraint-graph.out/1`), which this bead implements to the row
  of NJLx-51, a dependency: `frob analyze constraint-graph <family.fmission> --json` returns the artifact
  `frob.constraint-graph/1` of N25b-08 with the measurements of steps 3, 4 and 6 added (S_k and |S_k|; the
  components; on the root box and the 64 sub-boxes |aᵢ| per parameter, r, the ranked order and s_k; the
  three summary shares). Evidence: incidence exact; strengths class `Tested` as a description of the stated
  boxes; nothing returned is a certificate. Exit 0. Its only refusal is `FROB-REF-868` (the `.fmission` file is
  not valid), raised by the mission reader. Before M3 the row answers `FROB-REF-724`, exit 5, as NJLx-51 states.
  (An earlier draft of this bead called the tool "not a verb"; NJLx-51 later registered it, for reachability
  through `frob --json` and MCP, and that registration governs. N25b-08 keeps its example driver as the crate's
  own tool and adds no verb; the verb is this bead's and wraps N25b-08's artifact.)
- the driver of N25b-08, `cargo run -p frob-mission --example constraint_graph -- <family.fmission> --json`,
  given the option `--measure`, which prints the same measurements plus the experiment-only outputs of steps 5
  and 6 (the closure comparison, the treewidth bound, the weak-variable profile, the `not-lowerable` count).

1. Families (TARGET list; each a parameter box with its lowered static constraints). One per robot class that
   exists at M3 (the legged, wheeled, tracked and hybrid archetypes), and at least:
   - the 12-parameter quadruped box of the region pilot (three link lengths, two hip offsets, three masses, gear
     ratio, motor mass, body length and width; static joint torque in three stance postures, reach to a foothold
     set, self-clearance);
   - one wheeled family (a differential-drive carrier: wheel radius, track, wheelbase, three masses, gear ratio,
     payload offset; static traction, tip-over margin, sill lever), and the mecanum base;
   - one biped or humanoid stance family;
   - a tracked base and one wheel-leg hybrid, where their templates exist by M3;
   - one linkage-leg family with a closed chain (to measure what closure adds).
   Each family uses its template's free parameters at their declared boxes.
2. Registration. The experiment is registered in the method-experiment register before it runs
   (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/experiments.toml`, schema `frob.gauntlet.experiment/1`, N38a-07, with its
   six parts), with its question, the families, the thresholds of the decision rule below and what each outcome
   changes. A threshold changed after the first measurement is refused.
3. Incidence (read from the graph artifact). For each constraint g_k, the set S_k of parameters in its expression
   DAG; |S_k|; the bipartite graph; its connected components; the treewidth upper bound of the parameter
   interaction graph (two parameters interact when one constraint reads both).
4. Strength. On the root box and on a fixed set of 64 sub-boxes (a 2-level dyadic refinement along the three
   leading parameters), evaluate the affine form of each g_k and record |aᵢ| per parameter, the remainder r,
   the ranked order, the smallest prefix whose complement has weak width at most a quarter of the prefix width
   (the effective split size s_k), and the ratio ω/margin where ω is the weak width and margin is the distance of
   g₀ from zero.
5. Closure. For the linkage family, repeat with the closure constraint eliminated symbolically where the
   elimination exists, and with it kept as an extra constraint (both forms come from N25b-08); report the change
   in |S_k| and s_k (the coupled dimension that closure adds).
6. Summary statistics per family and per constraint kind: the distribution of |S_k| (minimum, median, maximum)
   and of s_k; the share of constraints with |S_k| ≤ 6; the share with |S_k| > 6 but s_k ≤ 6 (the constraints
   only the weak-variable extension can certify); the share with s_k > 6 (no region certificate under either
   rule); the weak-variable profile (the share of total affine width carried by the six heaviest parameters);
   the count of `not-lowerable` constraints.
7. Report `docs/experiments/open-augur-3.md` and a machine-readable `registries/experiments/open-augur-3.json`,
   with a recommendation for the owner per family: keep the default (refuse above six parameters), or admit
   weak-variable enclosure for the measured families, with the evidence.

Decision rule proposed to the owner, per family (DESIGN; the owner decides; the thresholds are fixed in the
registration before the measurement). Two drafts proposed two rules: one read incidence (median |S_k| at most
6 and at least 90% of constraints within six) and the share of affine width carried by the six heaviest
parameters; the other read the effective split size (at least half of the constraints with s_k ≤ 6). They are
one rule in three branches:
1. Median |S_k| ≤ 6 and at least 90% of constraints with |S_k| ≤ 6: the limit of six stands for that family and
   suffices; per-constraint region certificates cover it.
2. Otherwise, if at least half of the family's constraints have s_k ≤ 6: the recommendation is to admit
   weak-variable enclosure for the family. The family stays refused above six parameters until the owner's
   decision is on record and that extension has passed its own gate (GATE-AUGUR-30, with KILL-WEAKVAR). The
   criterion is s_k and not the six-heaviest share, because s_k is the size of the split set the weak-variable
   driver would bisect, so the rule predicts what that driver can decide; the six-heaviest share is printed
   beside it.
3. Otherwise: the default stays for that family above six parameters (`FROB-REF-856`: a region certificate is
   requested over more parameters than the admitted limit, or a joint paving is requested; first repair: the
   per-constraint decomposition; exit code 3) and its wide constraints are checked pointwise.
Until the report exists and the decision is recorded, `FROB-REF-856` is returned for any region certificate
over more than six parameters.

Evidence class: the incidence graph is exact (class D0); the strengths are enclosure outputs on stated boxes
(`Tested` as a description of those boxes; nothing here is a certificate) and are reproducible on one platform.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The driver with `--measure` returns, for each family, the incidence graph, |S_k|, the ranked |aᵢ| per constraint, r, s_k and ω on the root box and the 64 sub-boxes.
- [ ] The report covers every family of the list that exists at M3 (at least the quadruped box, a wheeled family, a biped or humanoid stance family and the linkage leg), states per family and constraint kind the |S_k| distribution, the three shares, the weak-variable profile and the `not-lowerable` count, and states the coupled dimension that linkage closure adds.
- [ ] On the analytic fixtures the tool reproduces known values: for g₁ = x₁ + 0.01·m² − 0.8 on [0, 1]²⁰, |S| = 20, a₁ = ½, every other aᵢ = 0, r = 0.005, s = 1; for g = Σ xᵢ − 10, every aᵢ = ½ and s exceeds 6.
- [ ] The experiment's registration, with the thresholds of the rule, predates its results.
- [ ] The recommendation per family and the owner's decision are recorded as `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-AUGUR-3.md` (or the default "refused above six parameters" is confirmed in force with this report cited); the fail-closed default is in force until then (test: a seven-parameter region request is refused with `FROB-REF-856` when the ADR is absent).
- [ ] The measurement is on file before the M3 pilot reading of GATE-AUGUR-09 is recorded.
- [ ] `frob analyze constraint-graph` matches its registry row of NJLx-51 (inputs, output schema, exit 0, `FROB-REF-868` on an invalid `.fmission`), and its registry example `frob analyze constraint-graph quad-12.fmission --json` runs from M3 with the values the driver's `--measure` prints for the same family; no other verb is added by this bead.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/constraint_graph.rs` (shared with N25b-08, whose tests are those of the lowering and
of incidence): `weak20_strengths` (the values above); `even_spread_strengths`; `effective_split_size_prefix_rule`
(a = (0.5, 0.2, 0.01, 0.01), r = 0: s = 2 because the prefix of length 1 has width 1.0 and complement width
0.44, which is above a quarter, and the prefix of length 2 has width 1.4 and complement width 0.04);
`closure_adds_dimension` (a planar four-bar leg: the closure constraint couples the two link lengths the
open-chain constraint reads separately); `components_and_treewidth_bound` on a hand-built graph;
`three_shares_partition` (the three shares of a family sum to 1); `decision_rule_branches` (synthetic families:
median 4 with 95% within six → branch 1; median 9 with 60% of constraints at s_k ≤ 6 → branch 2; median 9 with
30% → branch 3; a threshold edited after registration → refused); `default_refusal_without_adr`;
`verb_matches_driver` (the verb and the driver's `--measure` give identical S_k, |aᵢ|, r and s_k on the weak20
fixture); `verb_invalid_fmission_868`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/augur_constraint_graph.sh`: runs `frob analyze constraint-graph <family> --json` on the two analytic
fixtures and on every family and asserts the analytic values through the verb; runs the driver with `--measure`
on the families with fixed seeds for the experiment-only outputs; asserts that verb and driver agree on the shared
fields; writes the report inputs. Logs through the STD-E2E
helper to `target/e2e-logs/augur_constraint_graph/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`, `script`,
`bead` (`N25e-27`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block;
`start` with family identities and analysis versions; `metric` lines per constraint with |S_k|, s_k, the top
three |aᵢ|, r and ω; per family the median and maximum |S_k|, the three shares and the weak-variable profile,
each against its registered threshold; the branch of the rule each family falls in; the digests of the graph
artifacts read; the `summary` names OPEN-AUGUR-3 and GATE-AUGUR-09 (blocked until the report is on file).

### Known answers and negative controls

The two analytic fixtures. Negative controls: a tool that reports incidence as strength (every incident parameter
given equal weight) must fail `weak20_strengths`; a tool that ranks on the root box only must miss the change of
order on a constraint whose dominant parameter changes across the box (planted fixture g = x₁·x₂ on [0, 1]² shifted
so the leading parameter differs between two sub-boxes). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and
release; identical JSON.

## Considerations

- The answer may be unwelcome: if most constraints of real families have strong dependence on many parameters,
  both region mechanisms decide little, KILL-AUGUR-REGION or KILL-WEAKVAR fires, and family answers rest on
  dual witnesses, monotone bounds and pointwise checks. Knowing this at M3 is the purpose of the experiment.
- Strengths depend on the box: a parameter that is weak on a small box can be strong on a large one. The report
  therefore gives values on the families' declared boxes and on their sub-boxes, and makes no claim beyond them.
- Owner: the Augur lead. Fail-closed default until decided: region certificates refused above six parameters.
- The thresholds (90%, one half, the quarter in the prefix rule) are DESIGN and are part of the registration;
  they are proposals to the owner, who may decide otherwise in the ADR.
