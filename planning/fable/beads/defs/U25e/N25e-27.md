## Background

Region certificates decide one static constraint over a box of design parameters, bisecting at most about six
parameters. The approach presumes that static constraints are sparse: that joint torque, reach and clearance of one
leg depend on roughly three to six parameters of that leg. That is a HYPOTHESIS, not a measurement, and closure
constraints of linkage legs raise the coupled dimension. The open question OPEN-AUGUR-3 asks for the measurement:
the sparsity of the constraint-to-parameter graph for the program's robot classes, and the coupled dimension that
linkage closure adds. Its owner is the Augur lead, it blocks GATE-AUGUR-09 (region certificates on the 12-parameter
box), and its fail-closed default is: region certificates are refused above six parameters.

The weak-variable extension changed what must be measured. Incidence alone (which parameters a constraint reads)
no longer decides whether a constraint can be certified; what matters is how strongly each parameter enters. For a
lowered constraint g(x) ≤ 0 on a box, an affine-form enclosure gives g(x) ∈ g₀ + Σᵢ aᵢ·εᵢ + r·[−1, 1] with
εᵢ ∈ [−1, 1]; parameter i contributes width 2·|aᵢ| and the nonlinear remainder 2·r. OPEN-AUGUR-3 therefore now
records |aᵢ| per parameter, not only incidence. This bead is the experiment that answers the question. It is an
experiment bead and not a design decision: the default holds until its report is on file. Rejected alternative:
answering from the expressions' syntax alone (a parameter can appear in an expression and contribute nothing
measurable, or appear once and dominate).

## Scope

In: the measurement tool, the families measured, the report and the recommendation to the owner. Out: the paving
driver (BD-358), the weak-variable driver (N25e-24), the pilot's four-arm comparison (BD-289).

## Technical approach

Tool (DESIGN): `frob analyze constraint-graph <family.fmission> --json`, implemented in
`crates/frob-mission/src/region/graph.rs` with enclosures from the admitted interval library.

1. Families (TARGET list; each a parameter box with its lowered static constraints): the 12-parameter quadruped
   box of the region pilot (three link lengths, two hip offsets, three masses, gear ratio, motor mass, body
   length and width; static joint torque in three stance postures, reach to a foothold set, self-clearance);
   one wheeled family (a differential-drive carrier: wheel radius, track, wheelbase, three masses, gear ratio,
   payload offset; static traction, tip-over margin, sill lever); one biped or humanoid stance family; and one
   linkage-leg family with a closed chain (to measure what closure adds).
2. Incidence. For each constraint g_k, the set S_k of parameters in its expression DAG; |S_k|; the bipartite
   graph; its connected components; the treewidth upper bound of the parameter interaction graph (two
   parameters interact when one constraint reads both).
3. Strength. On the root box and on a fixed set of 64 sub-boxes (a 2-level dyadic refinement along the three
   leading parameters), evaluate the affine form of each g_k and record |aᵢ| per parameter, the remainder r,
   the ranked order, the smallest prefix whose complement has weak width at most a quarter of the prefix width
   (the effective split size s_k), and the ratio ω/margin where margin is the distance of g₀ from zero.
4. Closure. For the linkage family, repeat with the closure constraint eliminated symbolically where the
   elimination exists, and with it kept as an extra constraint; report the change in |S_k| and s_k (the coupled
   dimension that closure adds).
5. Summary statistics per family: the distribution of |S_k| and of s_k; the share of constraints with |S_k| ≤ 6;
   the share with |S_k| > 6 but s_k ≤ 6 (the constraints only the weak-variable extension can certify); the
   share with s_k > 6 (no region certificate under either rule).
6. Report `docs/experiments/open-augur-3.md` and a machine-readable `registries/experiments/open-augur-3.json`,
   with a recommendation for the owner: keep the default (refuse above six parameters), or admit weak-variable
   enclosure for the measured families, with the evidence.

Decision rule proposed to the owner (DESIGN; the owner decides): the default is lifted for a family when at least
half of its constraints have s_k ≤ 6; otherwise the default stays for that family and its wide constraints are
checked pointwise. Until the report exists, `FROB-REF-856` is returned for any region certificate over more than
six parameters.

Evidence class: the incidence graph is exact; the strengths are enclosure outputs on stated boxes (`Tested` as a
description of those boxes; nothing here is a certificate).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob analyze constraint-graph` returns, for each family, the incidence graph, |S_k|, the ranked |aᵢ| per constraint, r, s_k and ω on the root box and the 64 sub-boxes.
- [ ] The report covers the four families, states the three shares per family, and states the coupled dimension that linkage closure adds.
- [ ] On the analytic fixtures the tool reproduces known values: for g₁ = x₁ + 0.01·m² − 0.8 on [0, 1]²⁰, |S| = 20, a₁ = ½, every other aᵢ = 0, r = 0.005, s = 1; for g = Σ xᵢ − 10, every aᵢ = ½ and s exceeds 6.
- [ ] The recommendation and the owner's decision are recorded as an ADR; the fail-closed default is in force until then (test: a seven-parameter region request is refused with `FROB-REF-856` when the ADR is absent).
- [ ] The measurement is on file before the M3 pilot reading of GATE-AUGUR-09 is recorded.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-mission/tests/constraint_graph.rs`: `incidence_from_dag` (a constraint that mentions a parameter only
inside a term multiplied by zero after simplification is not incident); `weak20_strengths` (the values above);
`even_spread_strengths`; `effective_split_size_prefix_rule` (a = (0.5, 0.2, 0.01, 0.01), r = 0: s = 2 because the
prefix of length 1 has width 1.0 and complement width 0.44, which is above a quarter, and the prefix of length 2
has width 1.4 and complement width 0.04); `closure_adds_dimension` (a planar four-bar leg: the closure constraint
couples the two link lengths the open-chain constraint reads separately); `components_and_treewidth_bound` on a
hand-built graph.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/augur_constraint_graph.sh`: runs the verb on the four families and the two analytic fixtures, asserts
the analytic values, writes the report inputs. Logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/augur_constraint_graph/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`
(`N25e-27`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block; `metric` lines
per constraint with |S_k|, s_k, the top three |aᵢ|, r and ω; the per-family shares; the `summary` names
OPEN-AUGUR-3 and GATE-AUGUR-09 (blocked until the report is on file).

### Known answers and negative controls

The two analytic fixtures. Negative controls: a tool that reports incidence as strength (every incident parameter
given equal weight) must fail `weak20_strengths`; a tool that ranks on the root box only must miss the change of
order on a constraint whose dominant parameter changes across the box (planted fixture g = x₁·x₂ on [0, 1]² shifted
so the leading parameter differs between two sub-boxes). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and
release; identical JSON.

## Considerations

- The answer may be unwelcome: if most constraints of real families have strong dependence on many parameters,
  both region mechanisms decide little, KILL-AUGUR-REGION or KILL-WEAKVAR fires, and family answers rest on
  dual witnesses, monotone bounds and pointwise checks. Knowing this at M3 is the purpose of the experiment.
- Strengths depend on the box: a parameter that is weak on a small box can be strong on a large one. The report
  therefore gives values on the families' declared boxes and on their sub-boxes, and makes no claim beyond them.
- Owner: the Augur lead. Fail-closed default until decided: region certificates refused above six parameters.
