## Background

The controller class is part of a family answer. A cell of designs is certified for a declared class of control
laws, and the certificate carries the law: one feasible command per vertex of a load set proves that a feasible
command exists for each load, and does not prove that a controller of the declared class can produce them. On the
stance family (400 N on four point feet at (±1/3, ±b) m, ratings of 160 N, load moments P with abs(P_x) ≤ 60 N·m and
abs(P_y) ≤ 30 N·m) the smallest admissible half-width is 3/14 m for an adjustable law (any function of the observed
load), 1/2 m for an affine law, and no half-width at all for a constant command.

GATE-AUGUR-31 (claim: Known answer; M1; thresholds TARGET): through the public verb the stance family returns
3/14 m under `adjustable` with the bilinear law, 1/2 m under `affine` with the symmetric law and a matching dual,
and an exclusion for every b under `constant`. The bilinear law evaluated on a 121 × 61 grid of loads meets every
balance equation and rating in exact rationals. The four vertex witnesses relabelled `affine` are rejected by
`frob-verify`, and so is a certificate with no law object.

This bead is the script that records the gate. It is part of the first mission slice at M1 and runs on the CPU
with exact arithmetic. Rejected alternative: asserting on the library API (the gate says "through the public
verb", because a correct lowering behind a wrong surface is still a wrong answer to the caller).

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/augur_law_class.sh`; the `passive.fmission` query; the grid evaluation; the two rejected
certificates; the registered control; the gate record. Out: the lowering and the other stance-law queries
(N25e-28) and the checker rules (N25e-29).

## Technical approach

Fixtures (`fixtures/augur/stance-law/`): N25e-28's `adjustable.fmission`, `affine.fmission`, `constant.fmission`,
identical to the stance query (free parameter v = 1/half-width on the box [2, 6] in the inverse-length chart, bound
u = 1/half-length = 3, ratings (160, 160, 160, 160) N, load hull (±60, ±30) N·m, objective "minimize 1/v" with claim
`minimum-with-matching-lower-bound`, evidence profile `exact`) except for `policy_class.law`, and its
`nolaw.fmission` with the field omitted; plus this bead's `passive.fmission`, the affine query with the authority
model `passive-equal-spring` of BD-130.

Steps (plain shell, idempotent):
1. `start`: commit, host.
2. `frob mission synthesize fixtures/augur/stance-law/adjustable.fmission --key law-<run>-adjustable --json`.
   Assert exit 0;
   `cost_bounds.incumbent` = "3/14", `lower_bound` = "3/14", `attained: true`; the inner cell v ∈ [2, 14/3]; the
   witness law kind `multilinear-vertex` with the four vertex allocations (160, 130, 110, 0), (130, 160, 0, 110),
   (110, 0, 160, 130), (0, 110, 130, 160) N at v = 14/3; `evidence.class` `Certified`.
3. Grid check of the bilinear law, done by the script's own exact-rational helper (not by the product): for
   P_x ∈ {−60, −59, …, 60} and P_y ∈ {−30, …, 30} (121 × 61 = 7,381 loads) compute
   f(P) = Σ_σ ¼·(1 + σ_x·P_x/60)·(1 + σ_y·P_y/30)·f_σ and assert Σ fᵢ = 400, Σ xᵢ·fᵢ = P_x, Σ yᵢ·fᵢ = P_y with
   feet at (±1/3, ±3/14) m, and 0 ≤ fᵢ ≤ 160, all exactly. Assert f(0, 0) = (100, 100, 100, 100).
4. `frob mission synthesize …/affine.fmission --key law-<run>-affine --json`. Assert incumbent and lower bound "1/2"; the law kind
   `affine` with f₀ = (100, 100, 100, 100) and A rows (±3/4, ±1/2) in N per N·m, value (160, 130, 70, 40) N at the
   vertex (60, 30); the excluded cell's proof is an `lp-farkas` dual (the matching dual).
5. `frob mission refute …/affine.fmission --set design_domain.free.v=4 --key law-<run>-affine-v4 --json` (b = 1/4
   m). Assert an exclusion
   whose dual sums the four own-vertex rating rows (700 N required against 640 N), and two repairs: widen to
   1/2 m; or change `policy_class.law` to a law that is not affine.
6. `frob mission synthesize …/constant.fmission --key law-<run>-constant --json`. Assert the whole domain excluded
   (empty outer set) with an `lp-farkas` proof. Then `frob mission synthesize …/passive.fmission --key
   law-<run>-passive --json`: assert incumbent and lower
   bound "1/2", equal to step 4's minimum (the consistency check between the class rule and the authority rule).
7. `frob mission synthesize …/nolaw.fmission --key law-<run>-nolaw --json` through `e2e_expect_refusal`. Assert
   exit 3 and the refusal "inclusion over a disturbance set without a declared law class" with its three ranked
   repairs, pointer `policy_class.law`, and the code and `detail` read from row 2 of N25e-45's
   `fixtures/augur/late-refusals/expected.toml` (the row the ledger holds: the interim `FROB-REF-868` with
   `detail: law-class-missing`), never typed into the script.
8. Bundles: `frob prove bundle a:<answer> --out <dir> --key law-<run>-<step>-b --json` for steps 2, 4 and 6 (one key
   per bundle: every run verb of this script carries its own key, so no step is answered from another's stored
   receipt); `frob-verify` under `env -i`; assert `accept`.
9. Rejected certificates, made from step 2's bundle by the mission mutation library (BD-136,
   `crates/frob-conformance/src/mutate/`), which re-serializes canonically and recomputes every hash so that
   integrity passes and the rejection must come from a law rule: (a) `reject-law.affine-relabel.frobcert`:
   `law_class`, `law.kind` and the embedded query's `policy_class.law` set to `affine` (so rule 2,
   `law.class-kind`, passes), the four vertex commands kept, and f₀ and A fitted through three of the four
   vertices (N25e-29's specimen); (b) `reject-law.present.frobcert`: the `law` object removed. Assert that
   `frob-verify` rejects (a) with first failing rule `law.affine` (rule 4: at the fourth vertex the fit misses,
   160 + 0 ≠ 130 + 110 on the first foot) and (b) with `law.present` (rule 1), each with `FROB-REF-829`. A
   rejection by a hash or integrity rule, or by `law.class-kind` for (a), fails the assertion: such a control
   proves nothing about the law check.
10. Negative control of the gate: the `affine` lowering replaced by the per-vertex lowering of `adjustable` (the
    class ignored), a registered mutation control in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` (a
    `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch of `frob-mission`, built with `--features
    gauntlet-mutations,frob-mission/nc-<nnnn>`, numbered at registration by N38b-04), rerun on step 4 with the key
    `law-<run>-mut-affine`; the step must fail (the minimum returned is "3/14", not "1/2").
11. `summary` with the receipt hash. The script never records a value (STD-E2E's `self-recording` lint):
    GATE-AUGUR-31 is recorded from that receipt by a session that did not implement N25e-28 or N25e-29, with
    `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-31 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19),
    which refuses the record unless the control of step 10 is listed as caught in the same run.

Logging (STD-E2E): `target/e2e-logs/augur_law_class/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N25e-30`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block
(claim, class, tier, determinism, identities). `metric` lines: the three minima as exact strings against their
expected values; the grid tally (7,381 of 7,381 required); `assert` lines for bundles with digest, expected and
observed verdict and rule; the `refusal` line for step 7. On failure: the first load of the grid that violates an
equation or rating, with the computed forces.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-31 (TARGET, M1) recorded: 3/14 m under `adjustable` with the bilinear law; 1/2 m under `affine` with the symmetric law and a matching dual; an exclusion for every b under `constant`; 7,381 of 7,381 grid loads meet every balance equation and rating in exact rationals; the relabelled witnesses and the certificate with no law object rejected by `frob-verify`.
- [ ] The script exits 0 from a clean checkout on a host with no GPU and non-zero on any failed assertion; total run time under 60 s (TARGET).
- [ ] Bundle digests are identical across two hosts.
- [ ] The query with no law class is refused with ranked repairs, and nothing is published for it.
- [ ] Negative control: a build whose checker skips the law rules accepts the relabelled certificate, and the script fails.

release; the Mac and the Linux host; class D0.

## Considerations

- The stance gate of the first slice (boundary 3/14 m, four vertex witnesses, the passive-spring amendment) is a
  different gate measured by another bead; this script reuses its fixture family and adds the class dimension.
- The affine minimum equals the passive-suspension answer; a later amendment that declares
  `passive-equal-spring` authority must return 1/2 m as well, and the script asserts that equality as a
  consistency check between the two rules.
- The rows of this subsection are N25-KA's late row `augur-late-11` (the stance family under three law classes),
  cited by the KA number N38a-40 issues to that slug (a tag in a test name or derivation file fails
  `provisional-tag-past-due`); the values are stated here in full.
