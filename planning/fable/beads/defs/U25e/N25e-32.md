## Background

The docking game with a continuous hidden gain (three one-second ticks from rest, acceleration g·u_k, gain g
anywhere in [1, 2], commands bounded by U = 3/4, one velocity reading r = g·p + e with abs(e) ≤ ε = 1/64 arriving
before the second command, exact rest required at the end, guarantee abs(x₃ − 1) ≤ δ) has the least guaranteed
tolerance δ* = (√1745 − 41)/32 m ≈ 24.1624 mm, the positive root of 16·δ² + 41·δ − 1 = 0 (N25e-31). The optimum is
irrational. It is returned as the root of its polynomial with a rational enclosure, bracketed by two rational
certificates: an inclusion above it and an exclusion below it. This bead builds those certificates, the witness
policy with its exhaustive exact check on the raw dynamics, and the refusal that keeps a screen compiled for two
gains from being reused on the interval. Tier Solid [S].

Notation from the solution: p = u₀ is the probe; c = 2p + u₁; rest forces u₂ = −p − u₁, and then x₃ = g·c with c
confined to [2p − U, p + U]. A reading r admits the gains [a, b], a = max(1, (r − ε)/p), b = min(2, (r + ε)/p), and
the tolerance requires (1 − δ)/a ≤ c ≤ (1 + δ)/b. Rejected alternative: certifying by a dense grid of gains (a grid
is evidence of class `Tested` at best; the certificate must cover the continuum).

## Scope

In: the two rational certificates and the same two at the ends of the root's enclosure; the witness policy; the
exhaustive rational check; the grid refutation at δ = 1/50; the eligibility refusal. Out: the solver and the
algebraic answer (N25e-31); the gate script (N25e-33); the checkers themselves (BD-132).

## Technical approach

1. Witness policy (the inner cell's controller). After the reading r, compute [a, b]; choose
   c in [max((1 − δ)/a, 2p − U), min((1 + δ)/b, p + U)] (DESIGN: the midpoint, for determinism); command
   u₁ = c − 2p and u₂ = −p − u₁. The policy is a function of the reading only and uses it after it arrives.
2. Inclusion certificate at δ = 1/40 with p = 5/8. Facts: ε/(p + ε) = 1/41 ≤ 1/40; 2p − U = 1/2 ≤ 41/80 = (1 + δ)/2;
   p + U = 11/8 ≥ 39/40 = 1 − δ. The reading interval [p − ε, 2p + ε] = [39/64, 81/64] is partitioned at p + ε = 41/64
   and 2p − ε = 79/64. On the first piece a = 1 and b = (r + ε)/p; on the second a = (r − ε)/p and b = (r + ε)/p; on
   the third a = (r − ε)/p and b = 2. On each piece the bounds of the interval for c are monotone in r, so
   nonemptiness is checked at the ends of the piece. Kind: `partition` over `interval-box` pieces, each piece
   carrying the two end checks and the monotonicity sign; plus the endpoint licence that x₃ = g·c is increasing
   in g for c > 0.
3. Exclusion certificate at δ = 1/50. The polynomial is negative there: 16/2500 + 41/50 − 1 = −0.1736. Pieces of the
   probe axis:
   - every p in (0, 49/64) fails the ambiguity condition through an alias pair: the worlds g = 1 with e = +ε and
     g = min(2, 1 + 2ε/p) with the error that gives the same reading share the reading p + ε and need disjoint
     values of c (the second needs c ≤ (1 + δ)/g₂ < 1 − δ, which holds exactly when p < ε·(1 − δ)/δ = 49/64);
   - every p above 63/100 fails the reach condition at the high gain: in the world g = 2, c ≥ 2p − U > (1 + δ)/2;
   - p ≤ 0 fails as c ≤ U = 3/4 < 1 − δ in the world g = 1.
   Since 63/100 < 49/64, the pieces cover (0, 3/4] and the nonpositive probes. Kind: `alias-pair`, with a
   `partition` of the probe axis; the two reach pieces carry the single-world bound as a Farkas-style
   sub-certificate (DESIGN detail: the failing fact on those pieces is actuator reach in one world, not an
   alias, and the bundle says so).
4. Enclosure ends. The same two certificate shapes are emitted at the rational ends [δ_lo, δ_hi] of the root's
   enclosure (width ≤ 10⁻⁹ m): inclusion at δ_hi with a rational probe in
   [ε·(1 − δ_hi)/δ_hi, (2U + 1 + δ_hi)/4]; exclusion at δ_lo, where ε·(1 − δ_lo)/δ_lo > (2U + 1 + δ_lo)/4 makes the
   two failing pieces overlap. The checker also verifies the polynomial's sign at both ends (negative at δ_lo,
   positive at δ_hi), which isolates the positive root.
5. Exhaustive exact check on the raw dynamics (`frob-conformance`, exact rationals). The raw plant is
   v_{k+1} = v_k + g·u_k, x_{k+1} = x_k + v_k + g·u_k/2. Run the witness policy at δ = 1/40 and p = 5/8 for 2,049 gains
   g = 1 + i/2048 and 65 errors e = −ε + j·ε/32: 133,185 cases; each must end with v₃ = 0 exactly, commands within
   the bound, and abs(x₃ − 1) ≤ 1/40. At δ = 1/50, for each probe on a grid of 95 values (DESIGN: p = k/128,
   k = 1, …, 95; the count 95 is the recorded one, the spacing is this bead's choice), the same policy form fails
   on at least one case. These runs are the audit of the certificates, class `Tested`; they are not the proof.
6. Eligibility. A screen, classifier or certificate compiled for the gain set {1, 2} is bound to that support.
   Offering it for the interval [1, 2] is refused with `FROB-REF-854` (reused outside its eligibility: support,
   deadline, error units or tolerance changed); first repair: `mission.revalidate`. The two-gain positive labels
   are wrong on the interval: exact docking is impossible there.

Proof objects are plain data from `frob-mission`; `frob-lemma` writes the bundles; `frob-verify` checks them with
its own rational arithmetic. Determinism D0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] δ = 1/40 is included with p = 5/8: the `partition` of the reading axis at 41/64 and 79/64 with three `interval-box` pieces is accepted by `frob-verify`.
- [ ] δ = 1/50 is excluded with the alias pair and the partition of the probe axis (pieces p ≤ 0, (0, 49/64), (63/100, 3/4]); accepted by `frob-verify`.
- [ ] The certificates at the enclosure's two ends are accepted, and the polynomial's signs at the ends are checked.
- [ ] The witness policy passes the 133,185 exact cases on the raw dynamics; the count and the maximum of abs(x₃ − 1) (at most 1/40) are recorded.
- [ ] At δ = 1/50 no probe of the 95-value grid passes all cases.
- [ ] A screen compiled for the gain set {1, 2} is refused on the interval [1, 2] with `FROB-REF-854` and the repair `mission.revalidate`.
- [ ] Mutations rejected by `frob-verify`: the reading partition with a gap; the probe partition with the alias piece shortened to (0, 5/8), which leaves (5/8, 63/100] uncovered; an alias pair whose two worlds do not share a reading; the exclusion relabelled δ = 1/40 with its pieces unchanged; the inclusion at δ = 1/40 with the probe changed to p = 1/2 (it fails the ambiguity condition, which needs p ≥ 39/64).

### Known answers and negative controls

Inclusion at 1/40 with p = 5/8 (1/41 ≤ 1/40; 1/2 ≤ 41/80; 11/8 ≥ 39/40); exclusion at 1/50 (ambiguity needs
p ≥ 49/64, reach needs p ≤ 63/100); 133,185 cases; 95 probes. Related rows: KA-638 and CX-282 (a certificate that
held for the declared worlds does not hold when a world is added: with a third gain 33/32 at ε = 1/64 no exact
docking exists, best worst-case error 1/65 m). Negative controls: a policy that picks c outside the interval; an
inclusion offered at δ = 1/50 (must be rejected); the two-gain screen on the interval. Determinism lanes: twice on
one machine; debug and release; both hosts; identical bundles.

## Considerations

- The case counts are audits with fixed grids; a different grid is a different audit, and the gate's numbers
  refer to these grids.
- The exclusion's reach pieces are not alias pairs; labelling the whole exclusion `alias-pair` would be a kind
  used outside its scope (`FROB-REF-848`), so the bundle composes kinds under one `partition`.
- FACT (recorded plan-time pilot, to be re-run before a gate cites it): a solver on the raw three-tick dynamics
  excluded 20 mm, and independent interval checks confirmed inclusion at the threshold.
