## Background

The docking family is the first mission slice an implementer can finish: three one-second ticks, a hidden gain,
one velocity packet. With the gain anywhere in the interval [1, 2], command bound U = 3/4 and read error ε = 1/64,
exact docking is impossible and the right question is the least position tolerance δ that one causal policy can
guarantee for every gain and every read error, with exact rest at the end. The answer is
δ* = (√1745 − 41)/32 m, about 24.1624 mm, the positive root of 16·δ² + 41·δ − 1 = 0, attained with the probe
p* = (39 + √1745)/128. A 20 mm requirement is infeasible; a 25 mm requirement is met with the rational probe 5/8.

GATE-AUGUR-32 (claim: Known answer; M1; thresholds TARGET): through the public verbs, δ = 1/50 is excluded with the
alias pair and the partition of the probe axis; δ = 1/40 is included with p = 5/8; the minimum is returned as the
root of 16·δ² + 41·δ − 1 with an enclosure no wider than 10⁻⁹ m and `attained: true`. The witness policy passes the
133,185 exact cases on the raw dynamics. A screen compiled for the gain set {1, 2} is refused on the interval
[1, 2].

This bead is the script that records the gate, on the CPU in exact arithmetic. The fixture checks three things at
once: that a minimum that is not rational is returned as what it is; that a closed tolerance is reported as
attained; and that a continuum of hidden parameters is settled by endpoint reasoning with a certificate, not by
sampling gains. Rejected alternative: asserting the decimal 24.1624 mm (a decimal assertion would pass a solver
that never found the polynomial).

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/augur_docking_tolerance.sh`; the query files; the gate record. Out: the solver (N25e-31), the
certificates and the exhaustive check (N25e-32).

## Technical approach

Fixtures (`fixtures/augur/docking-gain-interval/`): `least-tolerance.fmission` (world: gain as a bounded set
[1, 2], `persistence: fixed-per-execution`; read error bounded-set [−1/64, 1/64]; observation: velocity reading
arriving before the second decision; policy class causal; quantifiers exists policy, forall world, forall
execution; predicates: rest at tick 3 as an equality, abs(x₃ − 1) ≤ δ non-strict; objective minimize δ with claim
`minimum-with-matching-lower-bound`); `tol-1-40.fmission` and `tol-1-50.fmission` with δ bound; and
`screen-two-gain.frobcert`, a stored positive screen compiled for the gain set {1, 2}.

Steps (plain shell, idempotent):
1. `start`: commit, host.
2. `frob mission synthesize …/least-tolerance.fmission --enclosure 1e-9 --key dock-<run>-least --json`. Assert
   exit 0;
   `cost_bounds.optimum.polynomial` equals ["-1", "41", "16"] (lowest degree first) up to a positive rational
   factor (the helper normalizes to primitive integer coefficients); `cost_bounds.attained` is `true`;
   `gap` ≤ 10⁻⁹; the enclosure's ends bracket the root (the script's exact helper evaluates the polynomial:
   negative at the lower end, positive at the upper); `evidence.class` `Certified`, determinism `exact`.
3. `frob mission synthesize …/tol-1-40.fmission --key dock-<run>-tol40 --json`. Assert an inner answer whose witness has probe "5/8", and
   proof kinds `partition` and `interval-box`.
4. `frob mission refute …/tol-1-50.fmission --key dock-<run>-tol50 --json`. Assert an exclusion whose proofs are a `partition` of the
   probe axis with an `alias-pair` on the piece (0, 49/64) and reach sub-certificates on p ≤ 0 and (63/100, 3/4];
   the binding conditions named are ambiguity and reach at the high gain.
5. `frob prove bundle a:<answer> --out <dir> --key dock-<run>-<step>-b --json` for steps 2 to 4 (one key per
   bundle); `frob-verify` under `env -i`; assert `accept` for each.
6. Exhaustive check: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --bin docking-interval-exhaustive -- --answer <a:… of step
   3> --json` (the raw-dynamics harness of N25e-32, which reads the witness policy's parameters δ = 1/40 and
   p = 5/8 from the product's answer and simulates the raw tick equations in exact rationals). Assert
   `cases: 133185`, `passed: 133185`, final velocity exactly zero in every case, maximum abs(x₃ − 1) at most
   "1/40". Then `-- --delta 1/50 --probe-grid 95`; assert `probes_passing_all: 0`.
7. Eligibility (N25c-21's `--reuse` check): `frob mission synthesize …/least-tolerance.fmission --reuse
   …/screen-two-gain.frobcert --key dock-<run>-reuse --json` (a key distinct from step 2's, since the two runs
   are compared: with step 2's key the call would return step 2's stored receipt instead of being refused). Assert exit 3, code `FROB-REF-854`, pointer `/world/hidden/g` with
   observed `{1, 2}` and required `[1, 2]`, nothing published, and first repair revalidation
   (`mission.revalidate`), whose ready command in an M1 build is `frob result revalidate a:… --against q:…`
   (the verb that answers before M3).
8. Sampling is not the proof: assert that no proof object in the bundles of steps 2 to 4 is a `trace-witness`
   or a `sample-coverage`, and that the endpoint licence appears among the side conditions.
9. `summary` with the receipt hash. The script never records a value (STD-E2E's `self-recording` lint):
   GATE-AUGUR-32 is recorded from that receipt by a session that did not implement N25e-31 or N25e-32, with
   `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-32 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19).

Logging (STD-E2E): `target/e2e-logs/augur_docking_tolerance/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line:
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25e-33`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence`
block. `metric` lines: the polynomial's coefficients, the enclosure's ends and width against 10⁻⁹ m, `attained`,
the case tallies against 133,185 and 0; `assert` lines for bundles with digest, verdict and rule; the `refusal`
line for step 7 with code, pointer and repairs. On failure: the first failing gain and error of the exhaustive
check with the computed trajectory, or the first rejected bundle with its path.

## Considerations

- This gate joins the M1 exit demonstration of the mission chapter (the docking slice driven by a fresh agent);
  an agent that changes ε or U should see the polynomial change by the parametric form
  δ² + (2U + 1 + 4ε)·δ − 4ε = 0, and the script's optional `--amend` mode checks one such amendment
  (U = 1, ε = 1/32: δ* = (√657 − 25)/16 ≈ 0.0395).
- The enclosure width drives certificate size; 10⁻⁹ m needs rationals of about 30 bits, well inside the limits.
