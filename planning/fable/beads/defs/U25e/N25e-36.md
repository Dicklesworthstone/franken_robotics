## Background

A sensor budget must state the joint law of its read errors, not their pairwise tables. Seven reads of a hidden
binary class, each right with probability 3/4, give a best success of 3807/4096 when the errors are jointly
independent, 3/4 when one fault bit is shared by all reads, and 55/64 under a constructed law that is pairwise
independent and not jointly independent (the number K of wrong reads is 1, 2, 3 or 4 with probabilities
(43, 3, 9, 9)/64, placed uniformly among the seven slots: each read is wrong with probability 1/4 and each pair is
wrong together with probability 1/16, exactly as for independent reads). A requirement of 0.9 is met by the first
law and missed by the third, with the same accuracy and the same pair tables.

GATE-AUGUR-33 (claim: Known answer; thresholds TARGET) has two parts with two milestones. The error-law rows, due
at M3: enumeration of the 128 histories returns 55/64 for the pairwise law, 3807/4096 for the independent law and
3/4 for the shared fault, exactly; a redundancy claim on a card marked `pairwise` is refused. The label rows, due at
M4, are measured by N25e-37. This bead is the script for the first part. Rejected alternative: one script for both
parts at M4 (the M3 exit needs its part recorded at M3).

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/augur_error_law.sh`; four sensor cards; the mission that asks for 0.9; the gate record for the
error-law rows. Out: the mechanism (N25e-34); the label rows (N25e-37).

## Technical approach

Fixtures (`fixtures/augur/error-law/`): four cards for one binary probe with single-read accuracy 3/4:
`independent.fcard` (`error_dependence: joint-independent`), `shared.fcard` (`shared-fault`),
`pairwise-law.fcard` (`declared-law` referencing `pairwise.errorlaw.json`, the K-law above), `pairwise-only.fcard`
(`pairwise`, no joint law); a mission `payload-class.fmission` (two sealed payload classes requiring disjoint final
forces, seven reads before commitment, required success 9/10); a design `probe-bot.fdesign` carrying the one binary
probe, named `probe`, whose card `--card` binds (both verbs take a design as well as the mission); and `truth.json` with the three
exact values.

Steps (plain shell, idempotent):
1. `start`: commit, host.
2. For each of the first three cards: `frob sense alias fixtures/augur/error-law/probe-bot.fdesign --mission
   fixtures/augur/error-law/payload-class.fmission --card probe=<card> --reads 7 --key el-<card> --json` (the
   `<sensor>=<card>` form VERBS-sense registers; `sense.alias` is a
   `run` verb: without `--key` it is `FROB-REF-805`). Assert `redundancy.guaranteed` equals "3807/4096", "3/4" and "55/64" respectively;
   `redundancy.decoder` is `enumeration` with `histories: 128` for the independent and declared laws;
   `evidence.class` `Certified`, determinism `exact`.
3. The script's own exact helper recomputes the three values from first principles (the product law; the shared
   fault; the K-law with uniform placement: Σ over histories of the larger of the two class likelihoods, halved)
   and compares with the verb's output and with `truth.json`.
4. Marginal check of the declared law: from the result of step 2 for `pairwise-law.fcard`, read
   `redundancy.law_marginals`; assert the per-read error probability is "1/4" for each of the seven reads and
   the joint error probability is "1/16" for each of the 21 pairs.
5. Decision: `frob mission refute fixtures/augur/error-law/payload-class.fmission --design
   fixtures/augur/error-law/probe-bot.fdesign --card <card> --key el-ref-<card> --json` for the three cards (the
   bare form VERBS-mission registers on `mission.refute`). Assert the
   requirement 9/10 is met under `independent` (3807/4096 ≥ 9/10) and missed under `shared` and under the
   pairwise law (55/64 < 9/10), each with the binding assumption named (the error law).
6. Refusal: `frob sense alias … --card probe=pairwise-only.fcard --reads 7 --claim-redundancy --key el-claim
   --json`.
   Assert exit 3 with the refusal code and detail N25e-34 emits (`FROB-REF-846`, `detail: joint-error-law-missing`,
   or the number the ledger assigns through N25e-45), not merely exit 3 (a missing key would also exit 3, with 805),
   the refusal "redundancy credited without a joint error law" with its three repairs, and that the guaranteed
   value in the refusal's `observed` field is "3/4".
7. `summary` with the receipt hash. The script never records a value (STD-E2E's `self-recording` lint): the
   error-law rows of GATE-AUGUR-33 (M3) are recorded from that receipt by a session that did not implement
   N25e-34, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-33 --receipt <hash>
   --part M3` (N38b-19).

Logging (STD-E2E): `target/e2e-logs/augur_error_law/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N25e-36`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence`
block. `metric` lines: card, law, guaranteed success as an exact string against its expected value, histories
enumerated; the `refusal` line with code, detail, pointer and repairs. On failure: the first card whose value
differs, with both values.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-33, error-law rows (TARGET, M3) recorded: 55/64 for the pairwise law, 3807/4096 for the independent law and 3/4 for the shared fault, exactly, by enumeration of the 128 histories; the redundancy claim on the `pairwise` card refused.
- [ ] The declared law's marginals are confirmed (1/4 per read, 1/16 per pair).
- [ ] The 0.9 requirement is met under the independent law and missed under the other two, through the public verb.
- [ ] The script exits 0 from a clean checkout on a host with no GPU and non-zero on any failed assertion.
- [ ] Negative control: a build of A-17 that computes redundancy from per-read accuracy returns 3807/4096 for the pairwise law, and the script fails at step 2.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script self-tests in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `error_law_selftest_helper.sh` (the helper returns 27/32 for three
independent reads and 459/512 for five, the published rows of the base fixture);
`error_law_selftest_wrong_truth.sh` (`truth.json` altered to 54/64 fails with both values printed);
`error_law_selftest_no_refusal.sh` (a build that credits redundancy on the `pairwise` card fails step 6).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; steps and logging as above.

### Known answers and negative controls

3807/4096 ≈ 0.9294; 3/4; 55/64 ≈ 0.8594 (46/64 decided with certainty plus half of 18/64). Base rows: KA-633;
CX-286; KA-683; CX-307. Negative controls: the per-read-accuracy analysis; the missing refusal; a declared law
whose probabilities sum to 63/64 (must be rejected at card admission). The seven-read row (3807/4096, 3/4,
55/64) is N25-KA's `augur-late-13`, cited by tag until N38a-40 issues N25-KA's block. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; both hosts; class D0.

## Considerations

- The gate's M4 half (label rows) is recorded separately; the M3 exit needs only this half.
- The K-law is a constructed adversary; the script asserts arithmetic, not anything about a physical sensor.
