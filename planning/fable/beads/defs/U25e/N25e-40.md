## Background

Two additions to probe design are proposed as Frontier work: a rule that nuisance parameters shared in the world
stay shared in every bound that compares quantities, and a known answer that a probe separating four admitted
relaxation laws exists at a longer dwell than the three-law probe. Both rest on numbers that were derived during
planning: a margin table computed on a 201 × 201 grid of the nuisance box, and a recorded pilot (FACT, to be
re-run: an enclosure of the delayed law's row reported at least 4.07 N with the parameters shared and −0.0776 N with
independent extremes). The program rule is that a recorded plan-time result is re-run before a gate cites it, and
that Frontier work has a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier before it is funded. This bead is both for this
subsection: it reproduces the numbers with a standalone exact program and an independent enclosure, and fixes them
as the pinned truth for the product beads.

The model. A compact pad (radius ratio r = 1, initial normal load 120 N) and a broad pad (r = 2, 100 N), Coulomb
coefficient 0.5, handover at T = 1 s. Normalized load R(r, t) = q + (1 − q)·exp(−t/(τ·f(r))), q ∈ [0.18, 0.22],
τ ∈ [0.9, 1.1] s. Laws: V, f = 1; P, f = r²; C, f = min(r, 1)²; D, for r ≥ 1 the exponent is
−[k·min(s, 1/4) + (1 + (1 − k)/3)·max(s − 1/4, 0)] with s = t/τ and k = 1/r², and for r < 1 as P. Margin of the compact
pad over the broad pad: m(q, τ) = 60·R(1, T; q, τ) − 50·R(2, T; q, τ) newtons. Rejected alternative: citing the
planning numbers unchanged (they were produced by throwaway scripts; a gate needs a pinned, reproducible source).

## Scope

In: a standalone program for the margin table (grid and enclosure), the four-law probe table, the 900-case
enumeration, the short-dwell non-existence, and the pinned truth files. Out: the product mechanisms (N25e-38,
N25e-39); the gate script (N25e-41); the base three-law pilot re-run (the plan-time pilots bead).

## Technical approach

Location (DESIGN): `crates/frob-conformance/pilots/shared_nuisance/`; double precision for grids, with an
independent outward-rounded interval evaluation (the admitted interval library, or a 150-line directed-rounding
evaluator for exp on these arguments) for the enclosures.

1. Margin table, grid. Evaluate m on the 201 × 201 grid of the box for each law; record the minimum, its
   location and the maximum. Expected: V and C minimum 4.4994 N at (0.18, 0.9 s); D minimum 4.7240 N at
   (0.18, 1.1 s); P minimum −13.0598 N at (0.18, 0.9 s) and maximum −10.0162 N (the broad pad wins everywhere, by at
   least 10.02 N).
2. Margin table, independent extremes. Minimize 60·R(1, T) and maximize 50·R(2, T) separately over the box and
   subtract. Expected: 0.2836 N (V and C), −0.0776 N (D), −15.0751 N (P). Under D the independent bound changes
   sign while the shared margin exceeds 4.7 N over the whole box.
3. Enclosures. For each law an outward-rounded lower bound of the shared margin by subdividing the (q, τ) box
   until the bound is within 10⁻³ N of the grid minimum, and the same for the independent form. The recorded
   pilot's weaker enclosure (at least 4.07 N shared for D) must be consistent with, and is superseded by, the
   new bound.
4. Probe table. For r ∈ {1/4, 1/2, 1, 3/2, 2, 3} and t ∈ {1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1, 2} s compute the least reading under
   P and the greatest under V, C, D over the box (and the reverse), and the gap after both read errors
   (difference − 0.04). Expected separating candidates at the least cost (0.75 s): (3/2, 1/2 s) with 0.8206,
   0.7677, gap 0.0129; (2, 1/2 s) with 0.8937, 0.7874, gap 0.0662; (3, 1/2 s) with 0.9509, 0.8019, gap 0.1090. No
   candidate with t ≤ 1/4 s separates P from D.
5. Case enumeration. 225 cases per law (25 pairs of q and τ, three probe errors, three errors on the archived
   reading), four laws: each of the three probes decides 900 of 900 correctly; the three-law probe (r = 2 at
   125 ms) decides 675 of 675 for V, P, C and 0 of 225 for D.
6. Output: `fixtures/augur/pad-relaxation/truth.json` (the pinned numbers with their grid and tolerance) and
   `docs/pilots/shared_nuisance.md` (falsifier outcome and ceiling).

Falsifier (fixed before the run): any table entry differing from the expected value by more than 10⁻³ N (margins)
or 10⁻⁴ (readings); a separating candidate at t ≤ 1/4 s; fewer than 900 of 900 for any of the three probes; or an
enclosure that contradicts the grid. Ceiling, stated first: 5/3 in modeled exposure time against the direct
measurement at mission dwell (1.25 s against 0.75 s), and 1 against a programmed expert given the same four laws.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The margin table reproduces: shared 4.50, 4.72, −13.06 N (P's largest margin −10.02 N); independent 0.28, −0.078, −15.08 N; each within 10⁻³ N of the values above, by grid and by enclosure.
- [ ] The probe table reproduces to four digits, with gaps 0.0129, 0.0662 and 0.1090 at cost 0.75 s, and no separating probe at any dwell of 1/4 s or less.
- [ ] Each of the three probes decides 900 of 900 cases; the three-law probe decides 675 of 675 and 0 of 225.
- [ ] `truth.json` is written with a digest pinned in the repository; the report states the falsifier, its outcome and the ceiling.
- [ ] The program's output is identical across two runs and agrees across the two hosts to the stated tolerances.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/pilots/shared_nuisance/tests.rs`: `laws_coincide_at_r1` (all four laws give the same
R(1, t) for every q, τ and t); `d_follows_p_until_quarter` (for r ≥ 1 and t/τ ≤ 1/4 the D and P readings are equal);
`d_reaches_v_at_s1` (at t/τ = 1 the D exponent equals −1 for every r ≥ 1: k/4 + (1 + (1 − k)/3)·(3/4) = 1);
`nominal_loads` (q = 0.20, τ = 1 s: 29.66 N compact; 24.72 N broad under V; 41.15 N broad under P);
`margin_minima_at_corners`; `independent_extremes_sign_change_for_d`; `probe_gaps`; `cases_900`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/augur_pilot_shared_nuisance.sh` runs the pilot binary, compares with the expected values and logs
through the STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/augur_pilot_shared_nuisance/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a
transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25e-40`), `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time; `metric` lines per
law with shared and independent margins in newtons and their tolerances, per probe the two readings, the gap and
the cost, the case tallies; on failure the first differing entry. The `summary` names GATE-AUGUR-34 (pilot
reading).

### Known answers and negative controls

The margin table, the probe table and the tallies above (base registry rows KA-639 and CX-279; no Appendix M
number exists yet for the new rows). Negative controls: an evaluator that treats D as P for all times (it finds
no separating probe at 1/2 s and must fail the probe table); a margin computed with τ shared and q independent (it
gives an intermediate value and must fail both columns). Metamorphic relations: scaling both initial loads by one
factor scales every margin by it and leaves the probe table unchanged; widening the τ interval can only lower the
shared minima. Determinism: grid results in double precision to the stated tolerances; enclosures identical.

## Considerations

- Check of the D law's definition while implementing: at s = 1 the exponent is −[k/4 + (1 + (1 − k)/3)·(3/4)] =
  −[k/4 + 3/4 + (1 − k)/4] = −1, the V value, for every k; this identity is a unit test because a misread
  bracket changes every D number.
- The pilot's pass does not show that scoped evaluation matters on real contracts; it shows the rule is
  necessary on this fixture and pins the numbers the gate cites.
- If the falsifier holds (the numbers do not reproduce), the gate's table is corrected by a dated amendment
  before any product bead cites it.
