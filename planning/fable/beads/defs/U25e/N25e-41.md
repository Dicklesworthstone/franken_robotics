## Background

Two rules were added to probe and instrument design. First: nuisance parameters that two quantities share in the
world stay shared in every bound that compares them; a refutation assembled from independent extremes of a shared
parameter is refused, because an extreme point of an independent box is not a world. Second: with the delayed
relaxation law D admitted to the mechanism set, `mission.distinguish` finds a probe that separates all four laws
at a dwell of 1/2 s, and none at 1/4 s or less.

GATE-AUGUR-34 (claim: Known answer; M4; thresholds TARGET): the margin table reproduces to 10⁻³ N, and a refutation
built from the independent extremes under law D is refused. With four laws admitted `mission.distinguish` returns a
probe at t = 1/2 s with r = 3 and a gap of at least 0.10, decides 900 of 900 cases, and returns no probe at any
dwell of 1/4 s or less.

The same subsection amends an existing kill gate. KILL-AUGUR-PROBE (trigger: the generator never beats the
programmed expert plus direct measurement at matched correctness on the coupled-layout experiment, or issues one
false lower bound; fallback: `experiment.suggest` ships the expert grid and the direct measurement, and the
experiment grammar is parked) gains one trigger: a certificate or a counterexample that depends on unshared values
of a parameter declared shared passes the checker. Its fallback is unchanged. This bead is the script that records
the gate and measures the added trigger; the kill decision itself stays with the probe-design work. Rejected
alternative: testing the scope rule only inside the proposer (the added trigger is about the checker).

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/augur_shared_nuisance.sh`; the seeded corpus of unshared-witness bundles for the checker; the
case harness `pad-probe-cases` (a binary of `frob-conformance` that reads a probe and its threshold from a product
answer and enumerates the 225 cases per law of N25e-40, independently of the product); the registered control;
the gate record; the trigger reading for clause C of KILL-AUGUR-PROBE, and, if a bundle is accepted, the M4
decision entry and ADR that N25c-16's rule calls for. Out: the mechanisms, their options `--scope ignore` and
`--witness independent-extremes` and their fixture queries (N25e-38, N25e-39); the pinned truth (N25e-40); the
M3 kill decision (N25c-16).

## Technical approach

Fixtures: N25e-38's `fixtures/augur/pad-relaxation-shared/` (`S` below): `margin-<law>.fmission` for V, C, D, P
(the compact-against-broad margin at the handover with q and τ scoped to the pad pair of one trial) and
`margin-D-noscope.fmission` (scope removed); N25e-39's `fixtures/augur/probe-four-laws/` (`Q` below):
`four.fmission` (mechanism set {V, P, C, D}, the candidate grid, read error ±0.02, cost 0.25 s + t),
`four-quarter.fmission` (dwell bounded at 1/4 s) and `three.fmission` ({V, P, C}); N25e-40's
`fixtures/augur/pad-relaxation/truth.json`. This bead's corpus `fixtures/augur/pad-relaxation/unshared/`: 200
seeded bundles, each a counterexample or an enclosure certificate whose witness assigns two different values of q
or τ to the two pads of one trial (generated by an independent tool in `frob-conformance`; each confirmed invalid
by the reference).

Steps (plain shell, idempotent):
1. `start`: commit, host, digest of `truth.json`.
2. Margin table: `frob mission distinguish $S/margin-<law>.fmission --key nuis-margin-<law>-shared-<run> --json`
   for the four laws. Assert the least shared margin within 10⁻³ N of 4.50, 4.50, 4.72 and −13.06 N (and P's
   largest margin −10.02 N), and `scope: shared` on q and τ. With `--scope ignore` (independent evaluation; key
   `nuis-margin-<law>-ignore-<run>`) assert 0.28, 0.28, −0.078 and −15.08 N and the marking `scope: undeclared`.
3. Refused refutation: `frob mission refute $S/margin-D-noscope.fmission --witness independent-extremes --key
   nuis-refute-D-<run> --json` through `e2e_expect_refusal` with the code, `detail: unshared-witness`, pointer and
   the three ranked repairs read from row 4 of N25e-45's `fixtures/augur/late-refusals/expected.toml` (the interim
   `FROB-REF-850` while the ledger holds the row, exit 3), never typed into the script; assert the refusal
   "witness assembled from unshared values of a shared parameter" with its pointer at q or τ. Also assert that
   the scoped query under D (`frob mission refute $S/margin-D.fmission --key nuis-scoped-D-<run> --json`) reports
   the compact pad ahead by at least 4.7 N over the whole box, with no counterexample.
4. Four laws: `frob mission distinguish $Q/four.fmission --key nuis-four-<run> --json`. Assert the chosen probe has r = "3",
   t = "1/2" s, gap ≥ 0.10, cost 0.75 s; the alternatives at equal cost are listed with gaps 0.0129 and 0.0662;
   the condition names {V, P, C, D}; the direct measurement (1.25 s) is in the proposal list.
5. Cases: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --bin pad-probe-cases -- --answer <a:… of step 4> --json`; assert 900 of
   900 decided correctly (four laws, 225 cases each).
6. Short dwell: `frob mission distinguish $Q/four-quarter.fmission --key nuis-quarter-<run> --json`. Assert no
   probe is returned, and that the answer carries the witness mechanisms P and D with opposing margins.
7. Three laws: `frob mission distinguish $Q/three.fmission --key nuis-three-<run> --json`; assert a probe at
   125 ms, r = 3 under the largest-gap tie-break
   (gap 0.0312) with r = 2 (gap 0.0158, the probe the plan-time pilot recorded) listed as the equal-cost
   alternative, and, through the case harness on law D, 225 of 225 wrong for either: the earlier result stands
   and the answer is marked conditional.
8. `frob experiment suggest a:<answer of step 4> --json` (a read verb): assert the ranking contains the probe and
   the direct measurement, each with a prediction per mechanism and its cost, and that no entry is marked as a
   verdict.
9. Checker trigger: `frob-verify` under `env -i` on each of the 200 bundles of the unshared corpus. Assert 200
   rejections with the scope rule. Any acceptance is recorded as the added trigger of KILL-AUGUR-PROBE, with
   the bundle path, and fails the script.
10. Negative control of the gate: the auditor without the scope rule, a registered mutation control in
    `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` (a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch of `frob-cert`, from
    which a `frob-verify` is built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-cert/nc-<nnnn>`, numbered at
    registration by N38b-04), rerun on step 9; the step must fail (unshared bundles accepted). The control's
    acceptances are logged as the control's, never as a clause C reading.
11. `summary` with the receipt hash and the clause C reading (rejected of 200, any accepted bundle path). The
    script never records a value (STD-E2E's `self-recording` lint): GATE-AUGUR-34 is recorded from that receipt
    by a session that did not implement N25e-38 or N25e-39, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
    record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-34 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19), which refuses the record unless the
    control of step 10 is listed as caught in the same run. The clause C reading stays on file in that receipt,
    which N25c-16's ADR names; a clean battery adds nothing to the M3 entry. If one bundle is accepted, the
    decision is re-opened by N25c-16's rule (N40-03; N40-26's note on this gate): a new ADR
    `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-PROBE-M4.md` and a new entry in the kill row's `decision` array (`exit = "M4"`,
    `outcome = "fired"`, the same fallback, the ADR path, date, session and this run's receipt hash), written by
    a session that did not run this measurement; the M3 entry and its ADR are kept and never edited, and clauses
    A and B are not re-evaluated.

Idempotency keys: every run-verb call carries its own key, distinct wherever two answers are compared:
`--key nuis-margin-<law>-<shared|ignore>-<run>` for the eight margin queries of step 2 (the shared and the
`--scope ignore` forms of one law must not return each other's receipt), `nuis-refute-D-<run>` (step 3, and
`nuis-scoped-D-<run>` for its scoped companion), `nuis-four-<run>`, `nuis-quarter-<run>`, `nuis-three-<run>`
(steps 4, 6 and 7). `frob experiment suggest`, `frob-verify` and the case harness are reads.

Logging (STD-E2E): `target/e2e-logs/augur_shared_nuisance/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line:
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25e-41`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence`
block. `metric` lines: law, shared and independent margins in newtons against their expected values and the
10⁻³ N tolerance; the probe (r, t), gap against 0.10, cost; case tallies against 900; unshared bundles rejected
against 200. `refusal` lines with code, detail, pointer and repairs. On failure: the first differing margin, the
first misdecided case with its (law, q, τ, errors), or the first accepted unshared bundle.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-34 (TARGET, M4) recorded: margin table to 10⁻³ N; the refutation from independent extremes under law D refused; with four laws admitted a probe at t = 1/2 s with r = 3 and a gap of at least 0.10; 900 of 900 cases; no probe at any dwell of 1/4 s or less.
- [ ] The added trigger of KILL-AUGUR-PROBE is measured: 200 of 200 unshared-witness bundles rejected by `frob-verify`; the reading is on file for the kill decision.
- [ ] The three-law probe's 225 reversals on the withheld law are reproduced, and the four-law answer is marked conditional on its mechanism set.
- [ ] The script exits 0 from a clean checkout on a host with no GPU and non-zero on any failed assertion.
- [ ] Negative controls: a build with the smallest-radius tie-break returns r = 3/2 and fails step 4; the registered auditor build without the scope rule accepts unshared bundles, and step 10 catches it in the same run as the recorded receipt.
- [ ] If a bundle of the corpus is accepted, `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-AUGUR-PROBE-M4.md` and an `exit = "M4"` entry with outcome `fired` exist, written by a session that did not run the measurement, and the M3 entry is unchanged; the register's load checks (`decision-adr-path`, `decision-entry-duplicate`) accept them.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; steps and logging as above.

### Known answers and negative controls

Margins: 4.50 N at (0.18, 0.9 s) for V and C; 4.72 N at (0.18, 1.1 s) for D; −13.06 N for P; independent 0.28,
−0.078, −15.08 N. Probe: r = 3 at 1/2 s, readings 0.9509 against 0.8019, gap 0.1090, cost 0.75 s; ratio 5/3 to the
direct measurement and 1 to the programmed expert. Base rows: KA-639, KA-640, CX-279. Negative controls as in the
acceptance criteria, plus an A/A check of the case harness (a probe that reads nothing decides half the cases).
No Appendix M number exists for the new rows: they are N25-KA's late rows `augur-late-15` and `augur-late-16`,
cited by the KA numbers N38a-40 issues to those slugs (a tag in a test name or derivation file fails
`provisional-tag-past-due`). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and
release; both hosts; identical answers and verdicts.

## Considerations

- The kill gate KILL-AUGUR-PROBE keeps its original triggers and fallback; this bead only supplies the reading
  for the added one. If an unshared bundle is ever accepted, the false-certificate procedure also applies.
- Ceiling stated before any speed test: 5/3 in modeled exposure time on this fixture, and 1 against a
  programmed expert given the same four laws; the script records both and asserts neither as a gain.
- A fifth law outside the admitted set can reverse the four-law probe; the answer's condition and the direct
  measurement in the proposal list are the protection, and the script asserts both are present.
