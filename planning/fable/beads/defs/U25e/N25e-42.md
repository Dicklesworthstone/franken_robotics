## Background

The co-design lane of `frob-synth` treats components as feasibility relations between a poset of delivered
functionality and a poset of required resources. Carrying a payload for a shift requires torque, electrical supply
and heat rejection; adding battery capacity adds mass that flows back into the torque requirement. In the monotone
fragment this feedback is a least fixed point, found by Kleene iteration x₀ = ⊥, x_{k+1} = F(x_k). On finite catalog
chains the iteration terminates at exact equality. On a continuous resource it does not terminate, and the
tempting stopping rule, "stop when the increment is small", returns a design that does not carry itself.

The smallest case. A pack of mass m needs a pack of mass F(m) = 1 + 0.99·m kilograms to carry itself and the
payload. Kleene iteration from zero gives x_k = 100·(1 − 0.99ᵏ). After 527 iterations the iterate is about 99.5 kg
and the next step adds 5 g. The least feasible mass is 100 kg. A stop on "increment below 10 g" returns a pack half
a kilogram too light, and F(99.5) = 99.505 > 99.5 shows that it does not carry itself.

The rule (tier Solid [S]; owner `frob-synth`, with `frob-algebra` for the order checks; verb `mission.synthesize`):
a sizing answer on a continuous resource is accepted on a witness, never on a small increment. Rejected
alternatives: a tighter increment threshold (the error is the increment divided by 1 − q, unbounded as the loop
gain q approaches one, so no fixed threshold is safe); extrapolating the sequence (Aitken or Richardson) and
returning the extrapolated value unchecked (an estimate is not a witness; it may be used as the proposal for U and
must then pass the check).

## Scope

In: the bracket for continuous resources, the upper witness and its check, the lower bound, the width, the
contraction-based proposal, the label `lower-bound-only` and the refusal. Out: the finite-chain criterion and the
antichain machinery (the co-design lane, BD-360); Pareto coverage (N25e-43); the gate script (N25e-44).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-synth/src/codesign/witness.rs` (DESIGN). F is the monotone map of the co-design loop on a
product of resource posets (reals with the usual order per coordinate, product order).

1. On a finite catalog chain the existing criterion stands: the iteration stops at exact equality
   x_{k+1} = x_k.
2. On a continuous resource the lane returns a bracket [x_k, U]:
   - Upper witness. A value U with F(U) ≤ U in the product order. Because F is monotone, every Kleene iterate
     from the bottom stays below U (x₀ = ⊥ ≤ U, and x_k ≤ U implies x_{k+1} = F(x_k) ≤ F(U) ≤ U), so the least
     fixed point is at most U and U is feasible: resources U cover what a design with resources U requires.
     The check is one evaluation of F, in exact rationals where F is rational, otherwise outward-rounded with
     the comparison F(U)'s upper end ≤ U.
   - Lower bound. Any Kleene iterate from the bottom is at most the least fixed point.
   - Width. The answer states U − x_k per coordinate.
3. Proposing U. Where a contraction constant q < 1 has been proved in a stated norm (‖F(x) − F(y)‖ ≤ q·‖x − y‖ on
   the region), the distance from x_{k+1} to the fixed point is at most q·‖x_{k+1} − x_k‖/(1 − q), which proposes
   U = x_{k+1} + q·‖x_{k+1} − x_k‖/(1 − q) (per coordinate, in the norm's unit ball); the proposal is still
   checked by F(U) ≤ U. Without a proved q, proposals come from bounded doubling of the last increment (at most
   a declared number of trials) or from a caller-supplied candidate; each is checked the same way.
4. Stopping. The iteration stops when a checked witness exists and the width is within the requested tolerance,
   or when the iterate leaves the admitted resource bound (an exclusion of the catalog under the stated
   relations, as before), or on budget.
5. Labels and refusal. A sizing answer without an upper witness is labelled `lower-bound-only` and cannot be
   returned as a design: "sizing returned without a feasibility witness". The refusal has no registry number
   yet; until the ledger assigns the row that N25e-45 (a dependency) specifies, it is emitted as `FROB-REF-853`
   (a minimum claimed without its matching bound) with `detail: sizing-witness-missing`, through the constant of
   `frob_core::refusal::augur_late`; pointer at the resource; `retry` `after-change` (N25e-45's row 5); ranked
   repairs: supply or search for a witness U; prove a contraction constant; choose from a finite catalog; accept
   `lower-bound-only` as a bound and not as a design. Exit 3 when a design was requested; exit 2 when a bound
   was requested and returned.
6. Options (DESIGN) on `mission.synthesize --strategy codesign`: `--tolerance <width>` (per continuous
   resource, in its unit) is the requested bracket width of step 4; `--candidate <resource>=<value>` supplies a
   caller's value, which is checked like any proposal of step 3: if F(value) ≤ value it is a witness and may be
   returned as the design, otherwise, when a design was requested, the answer is the refusal of step 5 with
   F(value) and the comparison in its `observed` field. Both options are on `mission.synthesize`'s row in
   VERBS-mission with `codesign`, and every call carries its idempotency key (`--key`), as on
   any run verb.
7. Result fields (`result.sizing`): `lower` (the iterate), `upper` (the witness), `width`, `iterations`,
   `witness_check` (F(U) and the comparison, exact strings), `contraction` (q, norm, proof reference) when
   used, `label` (`bracket` or `lower-bound-only`).
8. Proof object. The witness is an `lp-primal` style point certificate: the checker re-evaluates F(U) from the
   primitive equations in the bundle and verifies F(U) ≤ U; monotonicity of F is a recorded side condition
   (each component map's order and monotonicity are stated conditions of the lane).

In the fixture q = 0.99: from 99.5 kg the bound is 0.99 × 0.005/0.01 = 0.495 kg above 99.505 kg, which gives
100 kg, and F(100) = 100. Evidence: `Certified` for the witness check; determinism D0 for rational F.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the scalar pack F(m) = 1 + 0.99·m the lane returns a bracket whose upper end passes F(U) ≤ U and lies within 1 g of 100 kg.
- [ ] The increment-stopping mutant (stop when the increment is below 10 g) returns about 99.5 kg; offered as a design it is refused, because F(99.5) = 99.505 > 99.5.
- [ ] An answer with no witness is labelled `lower-bound-only` and cannot be exported as a design; the refusal lists the four repairs.
- [ ] On a finite catalog chain the iteration still stops at exact equality and returns the same antichain as before (regression).
- [ ] The witness certificate is accepted by `frob-verify`; a witness lowered by 1 g (U = 99.999) is rejected, since F(99.999) = 99.99901 > 99.999.
- [ ] A map that is not monotone is refused before iteration (the witness argument needs monotonicity).
- [ ] Reachable through `frob mission synthesize --strategy codesign --json`; `CONTRACT.md` documents the bracket.

## Considerations

- As the loop gain approaches one the least fixed point runs away (a/(1 − q)); the closure analysis refuses a
  design whose loop gain is at or above one, and this lane returns `lower-bound-only` until a witness is found
  inside the admitted bound.
- Monotonicity and Scott continuity of each component map are stated conditions of the lane; a surrogate fitted
  from simulation makes the result `Estimated`, and the witness check then certifies only the surrogate's
  inequality.
- The width is information for the caller: a 1 g bracket on a 100 kg pack is tight; the gate asks for exactly
  that on the fixture.
