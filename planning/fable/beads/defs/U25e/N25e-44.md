## Background

Two acceptance rules were added to the co-design lane of `frob-synth`. A sizing answer on a continuous resource is
accepted on a witness, never on a small increment: the lane returns a bracket whose upper end U satisfies
F(U) ≤ U for the monotone loop map F, and an answer without such a witness is labelled `lower-bound-only` and
cannot be returned as a design. A complete Pareto claim over discrete builds is accepted on a disposition for
every omitted option (`dominated-by`, `infeasible`, or `unresolved`, the last downgrading the claim to
`pareto-candidates`), never on a sweep of weights.

GATE-AUGUR-35 (claim: Known answer; M4; thresholds TARGET): on the scalar pack the lane returns a bracket whose
upper end passes F(U) ≤ U and lies within 1 g of 100 kg; the increment-stopping mutant returns 99.5 kg and is
refused. On the three-build catalog all three builds are returned with dispositions; a weights-only mutant omits
(4, 4) and fails; the budget query returns (4, 4). On 20 generated catalogs of at most 200 options the returned set
equals brute-force dominance filtering.

The two fixtures: the pack that needs F(m) = 1 + 0.99·m kilograms of pack to carry itself and the payload (least
feasible mass 100 kg; Kleene iterate after 527 steps about 99.5 kg with a next increment of 5 g); and three
feasible builds with (body cost, facility cost) = (0, 6), (4, 4), (6, 0). This bead is the script that records the
gate. Rejected alternative: accepting "within 1 g of 100 kg" for the lower iterate (the gate's point is the
upper witness; an iterate close to the fixed point from below is still infeasible).

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/augur_sizing_pareto.sh`; the 20 generated catalogs with brute-force truth and the
withheld-exclusion variant; the two registered controls rerun; the gate record. Out: the mechanisms, their fixtures
and the registration of their controls (N25e-42, N25e-43).

## Technical approach

Fixtures: N25e-42's `fixtures/augur/sizing-witness/pack.fmission` (one continuous resource, pack mass; loop map
F(m) = 1 + (99/100)·m with the payload term 1 kg; objective: least pack mass; contraction constant 99/100 declared
with its one-line proof); N25e-43's `fixtures/augur/pareto-coverage/` (`P` below): `three-builds.fmission` (a
catalog of three feasible builds with objective vectors (0, 6), (4, 4), (6, 0), claim `pareto`) and
`three-builds-budget4.fmission` (minimize body cost subject to facility cost ≤ 4). This bead's
(`fixtures/augur/sizing-pareto/`, `G` below): `gen/<i>.fmission`, i = 0..19 (catalogs of 20 to 200 options, two or
three integer objectives with ties, a random subset infeasible with a stored exclusion; seed pinned), with
`truth.json` from an independent brute-force filter in `frob-conformance`; `gen/unresolved.fmission` (catalog 0
with one stored exclusion withheld).

Steps (plain shell, idempotent):
1. `start`: commit, host, generator seed.
2. Pack: `frob mission synthesize fixtures/augur/sizing-witness/pack.fmission --strategy codesign --tolerance 1e-3
   --key sp-pack-<run> --json` (tolerance in kilograms). Assert `result.sizing.label` is `bracket`; `upper` within 0.001 kg of 100 kg; the recorded
   `witness_check` shows F(U) ≤ U; the script's exact helper recomputes 1 + (99/100)·U and confirms the
   inequality; `lower` ≤ 100 ≤ `upper`.
3. `frob prove bundle a:<answer of step 2> --out <dir> --key sp-bundle-pack-<run> --json`, then the standalone
   `frob-verify <dir>/<bundle>.frobcert --json` under `env -i` on the witness certificate; assert `accept`.
4. Increment-stopping mutant (the registered mutation control of N25e-42's script, a `cfg(feature =
   "nc-<nnnn>")` branch of `frob-synth` built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-synth/nc-<nnnn>`,
   numbered at registration by N38b-04; threshold 5 g): step 2's call on that build with `--key
   sp-mut-inc-<run>`; assert the mutant's value is about 99.5 kg (between 99.49 and 99.51); submit it as a
   design through `frob mission synthesize fixtures/augur/sizing-witness/pack.fmission --strategy codesign
   --candidate m=<value> --key sp-cand-<run> --json` (the product build) through `e2e_expect_refusal` with the
   code, `detail: sizing-witness-missing`, pointer and the four ranked repairs read from row 5 of N25e-45's
   `fixtures/augur/late-refusals/expected.toml` (the interim `FROB-REF-853` while the ledger holds the row, exit
   3), never typed into the script; assert the refusal "sizing returned without a feasibility witness" and that
   the helper confirms F(value) > value (99.505 against 99.5 at the nominal point).
5. Three builds: `frob mission synthesize $P/three-builds.fmission --key sp-3b-<run> --json`. Assert claim
   `pareto`, the set {(0, 6), (4, 4), (6, 0)}, and an empty omitted list (so no disposition is missing).
6. Weights-only mutant (the registered mutation control of N25e-43's script, a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")`
   branch of `frob-synth` built the same way): step 5's call on that build with `--key sp-mut-w-<run>`; assert
   its answer omits (4, 4); assert the product's own checker rejects the claim (an omitted option with no
   disposition); the wrapper records "mutant fails".
7. Budget query: `frob mission synthesize $P/three-builds-budget4.fmission --key sp-b4-<run> --json`; assert the
   returned build is (4, 4) with a matching lower bound.
8. Generated catalogs: `frob mission synthesize $G/gen/<i>.fmission --key sp-gen-<i>-<run> --json` for
   i = 0..19; assert the returned set equals `truth.json` and that every omitted option has a disposition whose
   kind matches the truth (dominated with a valid dominator, or infeasible with its exclusion). Tally 20 of 20.
   `frob prove bundle a:<answer> --out <dir> --key sp-bundle-gen-<i>-<run> --json` and `frob-verify` under
   `env -i` on each claim's bundle.
9. Unresolved option: `frob mission synthesize $G/gen/unresolved.fmission --key sp-unres-<run> --json`; assert
   the claim is `pareto-candidates` and the coverage obligation names the option.
10. `summary` with the receipt hash. The script never records a value (STD-E2E's `self-recording` lint): the
    gate is recorded from that receipt by a session that did not implement N25e-42 or N25e-43, with
    `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUGUR-35 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19),
    which refuses the record unless the controls of steps 4 and 6 are listed as caught in the same run.

Idempotency keys. Every run-verb call carries `--key` and no two calls the script compares share one (a shared
key returns the first call's stored receipt): the weights-only mutant (`sp-mut-w-<run>`), the budget query
(`sp-b4-<run>`), each generated catalog (`sp-gen-<i>-<run>`), the withheld-exclusion re-run (`sp-unres-<run>`,
which must not return the full run's receipt) and each bundle; the determinism lane's second run uses its own
`<run>` suffix. `frob-verify` and the `cargo tree` check of the considerations are not verbs and take none.

Logging (STD-E2E): `target/e2e-logs/augur_sizing_pareto/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line:
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N25e-44`), `step`, `event`, exact command, key, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block. `metric` lines: the bracket's ends in kilograms, the distance of the upper end from 100 kg against the
0.001 kg threshold, the witness check as exact strings, the mutant's value; for catalogs the set sizes, the
disposition counts by kind and the tally against 20. `refusal` lines with code, detail, pointer and repairs. On
failure: the first catalog and option whose disposition differs from the truth.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUGUR-35 (TARGET, M4) recorded: a bracket whose upper end passes F(U) ≤ U and lies within 1 g of 100 kg; the increment-stopping mutant returns 99.5 kg and is refused; all three builds returned with dispositions; the weights-only mutant omits (4, 4) and fails; the budget query returns (4, 4); 20 of 20 generated catalogs equal brute-force dominance filtering.
- [ ] The witness and the Pareto claims are accepted by `frob-verify` in an empty environment.
- [ ] A withheld exclusion downgrades the claim to `pareto-candidates` with its coverage obligation.
- [ ] The script exits 0 from a clean checkout on a host with no GPU and non-zero on any failed assertion.
- [ ] Results are identical across two runs and two hosts.

## Test plan

Script self-tests in `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `sizing_selftest_helper.sh` (the helper evaluates F(100) = 100,
F(99.5) = 99.505 and F(99.999) = 99.99901 exactly); `sizing_selftest_lower_only.sh` (an answer whose `upper` is
absent fails step 2); `pareto_selftest_truth.sh` (a truth set with one option removed fails and names the option).

`ties_kept`; `generated_catalog_reproducible`; `keys_distinct` (the script's key generator gives the
withheld-exclusion re-run and the determinism lane's second run keys of their own).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; steps and logging as above.

### Known answers and negative controls

Pack: x_k = 100·(1 − 0.99ᵏ); after 527 iterations about 99.5 kg, next step 5 g; least feasible mass 100 kg;
contraction bound 0.99 × 0.005/0.01 = 0.495 kg above 99.505 kg gives 100 kg, and F(100) = 100. Catalog: (0, 6),
(4, 4), (6, 0), none dominated; (4, 4) is best for no weights (the conditions 4w₁ ≤ 2w₂ and 4w₂ ≤ 2w₁ force both to
zero); a facility budget of four selects it. Negative controls: the two mutants; the withheld exclusion; a
witness check in floating point. The pack row and the catalog row are N25-KA's late rows `augur-late-17` and
`augur-late-18`, cited by the KA numbers N38a-40 issues to those slugs (a tag in a test name or derivation file
fails `provisional-tag-past-due`). Determinism lanes: twice on one
machine; debug and release; both hosts; class D0.

## Considerations

- The mutants are registered mutation controls: a build with an `nc-*` feature under the release profile is a
  compile error (N38b-04's mutation guard), and the script checks with `cargo tree -e features` that the
  product binary it measures carries none before it writes its receipt.
- The pack fixture's loop gain of 0.99 is deliberately close to one; the closure analysis refuses designs at or
  above one, and the fixture sits just inside.
