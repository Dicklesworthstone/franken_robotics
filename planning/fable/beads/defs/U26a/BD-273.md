## Background

A binary failure has zero gradient almost everywhere: a search that only sees "failed" or "passed" is blind
until it stumbles on a failure. The search therefore minimizes a number that is defined on every episode: the
**requirement margin**. A task's requirement is a bounded-time temporal formula over atoms (comparisons of
signals, each with a scale of the signal's dimension); every formula has a truth value and a margin ρ, a
dimensionless number whose sign agrees with the truth value away from the boundary: an atom e ≥ 0 with scale s
has margin e/s, conjunction and "always" take the minimum, disjunction and "eventually" the maximum. The margin
of an episode's verdict is its distance from violating the requirement. A world whose margin is negative, after
the boundary check, is a candidate failure. Minimizing a robustness margin is established falsification practice
with a standing tool competition (FACT from a search summary, to be confirmed at the source). The falsification
score is thus: the margin of the verdict over an episode, minimized over worlds.

This bead is the campaign: it runs search methods against that score inside the legality profile, always beside
card-uniform randomization at equal budget, passes every candidate failure through the solvability oracle and the
artifact guard, and returns classified, promoted failures. It is Frontier: the gain over randomization on robots
in interiors is unmeasured. Bet BET-22 rides on it, with a kill gate named in advance.

**Ceiling, stated before any speed test.** HYPOTHESIS: for failures of nominal probability near 3 × 10⁻⁵ the
search reaches a first failure with at least 3 times fewer simulated steps than card-uniform randomization. A
planning recomputation that charges the oracle and the shrinking puts the ratio near 6; published measurements
of adaptive stress testing range from no gain over naive sampling on a policy-in-the-loop task to 2 to 20 times
in a driving study (FACT, source register). The ceiling of this lever on the cost of one found failure is
therefore under ten, and its effect on the time to a design decision is bounded by the share of that time spent
finding failures, which the phase ledger measures. No larger claim is made anywhere.

Rejected: searching on the binary outcome; searching without a baseline arm; reporting the lowest-margin world
as the result (ADR-NEMESIS-04: every failure is shrunk before it is reported as a counterexample).

## Technical approach

1. **Score.** `score::falsification(verdict) -> Score`: the verdict object of an episode carries `verdict`
   (`satisfied`, `violated`, `undetermined`, `invalid`), `margin`, `binding_atom`, `binding_time_s`, per-atom
   minima and a `boundary` flag. An atom is decided on a device only when its normalized margin lies outside a
   guard band of 2⁻²¹ around zero (TARGET); a flagged world is re-evaluated on the CPU with the declared
   comparator, exactly where the inputs allow. Candidate failure: `violated` after that re-evaluation.
   `invalid` (a numerical fault) and `undetermined` (a window the trace does not cover) are never failures; they
   are counted and reported. Methods receive the margin; an `invalid` world is given the margin +∞ so that no
   method is rewarded for crashing the solver.
2. **Request.** Candidate (design, controller, policy), task, legality profile, method, budget; optional mission
   query (the search is then confined to the mission's declared world family intersected with the profile),
   seed, tier (default: the fastest admitted tier), idempotency key, synchronous wait.
   `--method`: `auto` (default), `card-uniform`, `cross-entropy`, `descent`, `grammar`, `splitting`, `given`,
   and `exploration` (DESIGN, requested of VERBS-nemesis: the exploration lane as one search arm, the arm that
   GATE-NEMESIS-05 measures, N26a-31: splitting and cross-entropy only, half of the search arm each, on the one
   shared archive with the restart rule of step 3; a two-mode trap with one static and one per-step mode needs
   both on one archive, and `auto` would also spend its share on grammar and descent. It needs what
   `--method splitting` needs: FROB-REF-569 on a task with no per-step randomness after the fork, FROB-REF-724
   in a build without the splitting explorer or after KILL-RARE).
   Options, with the spellings this bead fixes; every one is on the `nemesis.search` row of VERBS-nemesis, which
   this bead depends on and which is the authority for their spelling and domain (convention 12):
   - `--worlds <file>`: required with `--method given` and refused with any other method (FROB-REF-802, the
     schema refusal): the newline-delimited file of world documents the `given` arm of N26a-07 reads.
   - `--require-cited` (the demand BD-270 defines): a world that touches a bound of provenance `assumed` is
     refused with FROB-REF-361. A `given` document is refused individually; the methods' own proposals are
     confined to quantities whose bounds are `cited` or `card` (DESIGN: the third repair of 361 applied by the
     search itself), and a request that leaves no searchable quantity is refused with FROB-REF-361 before any
     episode.
   - `--seed <u64str>` (the campaign seed, an unsigned 64-bit integer carried as a decimal string as the row's
     schema type says; default 0, echoed in the result), `--tier <tier>`, `--mission <query>`.
   - `--step-plan <plan>`: the step plan of the campaign's own evaluations on the request's tier (default: the
     tier's production plan). A plan that carries a conformance-only option of the planted-exploit bead N16b-15
     (`penetration-recovery`, or `pyramid4` as the base cone) is admitted only by a binary built with the
     `conformance` feature, for a session holding the conformance scope, exactly as that bead's guard states for
     every verb; the result then prints the option's label, and the artifact guard's tier-up and variant replays
     always run under the production plan of their tier, which is how a planted artifact is removed.
   - `--first` (driver step 7).
   With `--method card-uniform`, as under the configuration `baseline-only`, the campaign has one arm: the
   baseline receives the whole budget and the result reports `baseline_arm` and `steps.baseline` only.
   `--method given` is also a one-arm campaign (DESIGN): the caller's worlds are evaluated in file order within
   the budget and nothing else is drawn; the result says so with `baseline_arm: null` and the reason
   `given-worlds-only`. Reason: `given` is itself an arm of the baseline kind (N26a-07), used to evaluate
   planted, hand-written or scenario-language worlds; a card-uniform arm drawn beside a hundred planted worlds
   would mix unrelated failures into their counts, and beside a planted artifact it would run under the
   artifact's step plan. The rule that every campaign carries a baseline at equal budget is a rule about the
   search methods, and a scenario-language arm is compared with a separate card-uniform campaign at the same
   budget (N26a-11).
3. **Driver (numbered).**
   1. Resolve identities; check the legality profile exists; refuse a confirmation run or an address in the
      confirmation namespace (the sealed-read refusal FROB-REF-840 of the qualification lane).
   2. Split the budget: half to the baseline arm (card-uniform), half to the search arm (a one-arm campaign,
      under `--method card-uniform` or `baseline-only`, gives the baseline all of it); inside each, reserve
      20% (TARGET) for oracle and guard runs.
   3. `auto` runs a fixed table of shares of the search arm (DESIGN; a table, not an adaptive controller):
      cross-entropy 40%, grammar mutation 30%, descent 15%, splitting 15% where the task has per-step
      disturbance streams and the splitting explorer is in the build (else its share goes to cross-entropy).
      The splitting explorer is BD-277, a later seed that registers itself as a `SearchMethod` in this portfolio
      (this bead does not wait for it); until it lands, and after KILL-RARE removes it, `--method splitting`
      returns FROB-REF-724 (not implemented in this build, exit 5) and `auto` uses the table without it. All
      methods share one failure archive (N26a-09): the driver restarts the cross-entropy sampler from an
      occupied archive cell other than the one it has collapsed onto, and routes a grammar offspring's handoff
      request (discrete structure frozen, margin within 0.2 of zero) to descent for one local run charged to
      the same arm.
   4. Loop rounds: each method proposes; the driver checks legality on the final world (illegal proposals are
      counted, not run); worlds are evaluated on the fast tier in batches; outcomes return to the methods.
   5. Every evaluated world is recorded with its address and the law that proposed it (the discovery record),
      so a later importance-weighted estimate remains possible and so that the world can never be offered as a
      confirmation sample (INV-NEMESIS-06; refusal FROB-REF-567 at the lane that would misuse it).
   6. Each candidate failure goes to the oracle (class `solvable-causal`, `plant-feasible`, `not-excluded` or
      `infeasible-for-candidate`) and to the artifact guard (replay one tier up, numerical variants, energy
      audit). A failure that passes both is **promoted**. `infeasible-for-candidate` worlds are counted in the
      scorecard's infeasible column and offered to the retrofit query; `not-excluded` are reported and counted
      neither way; `artifact-candidate` worlds are excluded and written to the fidelity ledger.
   7. Stop on budget, on cancellation (drain running batches, finalize, publish nothing partial as a result), or
      when asked to stop at the first promoted failure (`--first`): each arm stops at its own first promoted
      failure or at the end of its half of the budget, so both arms' steps to their first failure are measured
      (an arm that reaches its budget is reported censored).
   8. **The solvable share** (the second clause of GATE-NEMESIS-02; DESIGN reading, because promoted failures are
      solvable by definition and the clause would otherwise be vacuous): among the search arm's candidate
      failures that the guard did not label `artifact-candidate`, the share whose oracle class is
      `solvable-causal` or `plant-feasible`; the denominator also counts `not-excluded` and
      `infeasible-for-candidate`. The result prints numerator and denominator.
4. **Result (output schema `frob.nemesis.search.out/1`, input `frob.nemesis.search.in/1`: the names the verb
   registry VERBS-nemesis gives by rule).**
   ```json
   { "worlds_searched": 4096, "failures_promoted": 3, "classes": { "slip": 2, "timeout": 1 },
     "baseline_arm": { "method": "random within the profile", "failures_at_equal_budget": 1 },
     "worst": { "world": "w:9d04…", "run": "r:19e2…", "requirement": "delivered", "binding_atom": "upright",
                "margin": -0.42, "edits": 7 },
     "artifact_guard": "all three reproduce on the referee",
     "failures": [ { "world": "w:…", "oracle": "solvable-causal", "class": "slip", "binding_atom": "upright",
                     "margin": -0.42, "law": "search:grammar", "legality": {…}, "prior": {…} } ],
     "not_counted": { "infeasible_for_candidate": 2, "not_excluded": 1, "artifact_candidate": 1,
                      "invalid": 0, "refused_illegal": 14 },
     "solvable_share": { "solvable": 3, "classified": 5 },
     "steps": { "search": {…per phase…}, "baseline": {…} }, "certificate": false }
   ```
   Exit 2 when at least one promoted failure exists, 0 when none (with the plain statement that finding none is
   not a certificate of anything), 8 while the job runs. Evidence block: class `Tested`, the tier that promoted
   the failures, determinism class of the run, `claims: []`, `reality: no-claim`. Failures are paginated.
   Under `--method given` the result also carries `refused`: one entry per document that was turned away,
   `{line, code, pointer, bound, observed, required}` with code FROB-REF-360, -361 or -362, because the caller
   wrote those worlds and must be told which were refused and why; the verb still completes (a refused document
   is not a refusal of the request). Proposals of the search methods are only counted
   (`not_counted.refused_illegal`). Each arm's entry under `steps` holds its charged steps per phase and, with
   `--first`, `to_first_promoted` and `censored`.
5. **The `unresolved` rule.** For any swept parameter the result marks every value that was neither evaluated nor
   covered by a proof as `unresolved`; no verdict is interpolated between evaluated values. Verdict sign changes
   along a sorted sweep are reported to the sign-change detector; more than one change is a non-monotone outcome
   and results that depend on that interval are typed `model-conditional` until step-halving and stiffness
   variants agree.
6. **Surface.** Verb `nemesis.search`, kind run, first milestone M3; CLI `frob nemesis search <design> --task …
   --legality … [--method M] [--worlds FILE] [--require-cited] [--first] [--seed S] [--tier T] [--mission Q]
   [--step-plan P] --budget gpu_s=240 --key nem-1 --json`; MCP tool `nemesis_search` in the task cluster `attack`;
   scope `nemesis:run`; idempotent by key; a long verb (bounded wait, then a job handle with progress in
   worlds). Search runs on a fast tier; nothing it finds is a counterexample until the oracle and the guard have
   passed it.
7. **Gate.** GATE-NEMESIS-02 (Design comparison; TARGET): on planted traps of nominal probability at most
   3 × 10⁻⁵, in 30 paired repetitions, the search reaches a first promoted failure with at least 3 times fewer
   simulated steps than card-uniform randomization at a 95% lower bound, and at least 70% of reported
   counterexamples are `solvable-causal` or `plant-feasible`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition, GATE-NEMESIS-02, in the part this bead delivers: every quantity the gate and
      KILL-NEMESIS read is in each campaign's result (per arm `to_first_promoted` and `censored` under
      `--first`, steps charged per phase, and `solvable_share` with numerator and denominator), checked by
      `first_stops`, `budget_split_and_reserve` and `solvable_share_counts`. The measurement belongs to N26a-11, which
      depends on this bead: the experiment registered as a method experiment with its ceiling, arms, potency
      control and fallback before the first run, 30 paired repetitions, and the gate's one record entered from
      its script's receipt with `record_gate` by a session that did not implement the search (no script writes
      a gate record); N26a-12 decides KILL-NEMESIS from that record. This bead waits on neither (convention 7).
- [ ] `--method given` without `--worlds`, and `--worlds` with another method, are FROB-REF-802; a `given` run
      lists every refused document under `refused` with its line, code and bound and completes; with
      `--require-cited` a `given` document touching an `assumed` bound is listed with FROB-REF-361 and no
      proposal of a search method touches one.
- [ ] `--step-plan` naming a plan with `pyramid4` as base cone or `penetration-recovery` is refused by a default
      build and by a session without the conformance scope, and is admitted, with the option's label printed in
      the result, by the `conformance` build under that scope; the guard's replays of such a campaign run under
      the production plan.
- [ ] `--method card-uniform` runs one arm with the whole budget; two such campaigns with different seeds draw
      different worlds (what the A/A control of N26a-11 pairs).
- [ ] Every campaign of a search method carries the baseline arm's count at equal budget, and its two arms'
      charged steps differ by at most one batch; a `given` campaign evaluates the caller's worlds only and
      reports `baseline_arm: null` with the reason `given-worlds-only`.
- [ ] No world outside the legality profile is simulated; no candidate failure is counted before the oracle and
      the guard; `infeasible-for-candidate`, `not-excluded` and `artifact-candidate` worlds appear only under
      `not_counted`.
- [ ] An `invalid` world is never reported as a failure and never lowers a method's score.
- [ ] A campaign that finds nothing exits 0 with `certificate: false`; the same request replayed with the same
      key returns the stored envelope (`idempotent_replay: true`).
- [ ] Every evaluated world has a discovery record with address and proposing law, found by origin `nemesis`
      (the record the qualification lane reads to refuse such a world with FROB-REF-567; that refusal is the
      lane's, BD-259, and is exercised from the user's side by N26b-14).
- [ ] With `nemesis.search.methods = baseline-only` (N26a-07's switch) a campaign on a planted trap of
      probability 10⁻² returns a promoted failure classified by the oracle and passed by the guard, with
      `method: card-uniform` and no search method constructed.
- [ ] Unevaluated sweep values are `unresolved`; a planted non-monotone sweep is reported with its sign changes.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob --json` and `frob-mcp` with documented schemas; cancellation tested (request,
      drain, finalize, no leaked device resources); `CONTRACT.md` updated; a CPU reference path exists
      (`--tier batch-cpu`).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-nemesis/tests/search_driver.rs` (methods and oracle real; margins from `synthetic` closed forms where
stated):
- `score_sign_and_boundary`: margins −0.31, 0, +0.84 map to failure, boundary re-evaluation, pass; a margin of
  2⁻²² is flagged and re-evaluated.
- `invalid_is_not_failure`: a faulted world gets score +∞ and appears under `not_counted.invalid`.
- `budget_split_and_reserve`: budget 10⁶ steps → 5 × 10⁵ per arm, of which 10⁵ reserved.
- `auto_shares`: 40, 30, 15, 15 with per-step disturbances and the splitting explorer registered; 55, 30, 15, 0
  without per-step disturbances or without the explorer; `--method splitting` without the explorer → 724.
  `exploration_arm`: in this bead's build (no explorer registered) `--method exploration` → 724 naming the
  splitting explorer, zero episodes; the half-and-half split on one archive is asserted where the explorer
  registers itself (BD-277, `registers_with_search_portfolio`), so this bead does not wait for it.
- `pipeline_order`: a candidate failure is not in `failures` until both downstream verdicts exist.
- `first_stops`: `--first` stops each arm at its own first promoted failure and reports steps to it per arm; an
  arm that finds none is reported censored at its half of the budget.
- `given_needs_worlds_file`: the two FROB-REF-802 cases; `given_refused_documents_listed`: a file of five
  documents, two outside the profile and one with an undeclared path, completes with `worlds_searched: 2` and
  three `refused` entries (360, 360, 362) carrying their line numbers and bounds.
- `require_cited_confines_search`: on a profile with one `card` and one `assumed` bound, no proposal of any
  method edits the `assumed` quantity; with only `assumed` bounds the request is refused with 361 and zero
  episodes.
- `step_plan_guarded`: the three cases of the acceptance line (default build; conformance build without the
  scope; conformance build with it), on the diagonal-push fixture of N16b-15.
- `card_uniform_single_arm`: `--method card-uniform` charges the whole budget to the baseline arm and reports
  no `steps.search`; seeds 1 and 2 give different proposal digests. `given_single_arm`: a file of three worlds
  gives `worlds_searched: 3`, `baseline_arm: null` with its reason, and no nominal draw in the ledger.
- `solvable_share_counts`: candidate failures classified (solvable-causal 2, plant-feasible 1, not-excluded 1,
  infeasible 1, artifact 1) → share 3/5; the artifact is in neither term.
- `fallback_pipeline_complete`: under `baseline-only`, a planted trap of probability 10⁻² (`synthetic` margin on
  a real candidate) yields a promoted failure with oracle class and guard outcome attached.
- `auto_two_modes_with_archive` (`synthetic`: failure when z₁ > 4 or z₂ > 4): with the shared archive and the
  restart rule, both modes are found in at least 80 of 100 seeded runs; with the restart rule off, fewer (the
  difference is recorded and must be positive).
- `handoff_routed_to_descent`: a grammar offspring at margin 0.15 produces one descent run on its continuous
  quantities, charged to the search arm; one at margin 0.25 produces none.
- `unresolved_values`: a sweep evaluated at 3 of 7 grid values reports 4 `unresolved`.
- `discovery_records_written`: one per evaluated world, with law.
- `cancel_drains`: cancellation mid-round leaves no running batch and publishes no result.
- `replay_bitwise`: two runs with one seed give identical world lists and digests on one backend.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/nemesis_search.sh` (N26a-11) drives `frob nemesis search` on planted traps and logs per arm the
steps to the first promoted failure, the classes, the not-counted buckets and the gate metrics with thresholds.
`scripts/e2e/nemesis_loop.sh` (N26a-20) runs the search inside the whole attack loop.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-661 (a planted solvable trap of known nominal probability is found with a causal witness; control:
card-uniform randomization at the same budget). CX-289 (any world that defeats the design is a counterexample:
the narrow doorway must land in `not_counted`). Potency control of the gate: a planted trap of probability 10⁻²
must be found by both arms within their budgets; an A/A campaign (baseline against baseline, different seeds)
must not clear 1.02 at the paired lower bound (the pass rule of N38a-08's A/A routine, the program's rule for
every method experiment). Metamorphic: permuting world slots or changing batch size changes no result. Determinism lanes:
twice on one machine, across worker counts, debug and release, each against the determinism class of its tier.

## Considerations

- KILL-NEMESIS. Trigger: GATE-NEMESIS-02 fails after one repair cycle. Fallback: the search methods are removed;
  Nemesis keeps the legality profile, the oracle, the guard and shrinking, applied to failures that card
  randomization finds (N26a-07). Decided at M3 (N26a-12). Downstream: `nemesis.search` then reports
  `method: card-uniform` only; the design-pair query and splitting inside search are withdrawn.
- A search can find the simulator's weaknesses faster than the design's. The guard is the answer, and its
  rejections are findings in their own right (fidelity ledger).
- An agent could use search results to tune a design to the search. That is permitted: the results are discovery
  data. What is refused is citing them, or the worlds, as confirmation.
- Budget is charged before work is dispatched; exhaustion ends with the worlds evaluated so far and the plain
  statement of how many were searched.
- The second baseline (a scenario-language sampler with the same generator and monitors) is admissible and is
  run where the tool exists; otherwise it is recorded `not run`.
