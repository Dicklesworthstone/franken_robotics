## Background

GATE-NEMESIS-02 is the gate on which the adversarial search lives or dies (claim: Design comparison, since it
compares a method with a competing arm; earliest milestone M3; TARGET): on planted traps of nominal probability
at most 3 × 10⁻⁵, in 30 paired repetitions, the search reaches a first promoted failure with at least 3 times
fewer simulated steps than card-uniform randomization at a 95% lower bound, and at least 70% of reported
counterexamples are `solvable-causal` or `plant-feasible`. If it fails after one repair cycle, kill gate
KILL-NEMESIS removes the search methods.

A gate of this kind is a method experiment, and the program admits a method experiment only if six things are
written down before its first run: the ceiling (the largest effect it could show), the competing arms, a potency
control (a planted effect it must detect and an A/A arm it must not), a paired lower bound as the reported
quantity, the pre-declared fallback, and a reachability pre-analysis (that the threshold can be crossed at the
planned sample size under the declared alternative). A test whose ceiling is below its success threshold cannot
confirm anything. This bead writes those six down, runs the experiment from the user's side on the real binary,
and records the gate.

## Scope

In: the registration record; the script; the measurement and its statistics; the gate's receipt; the gate's
two control records that no catalogue entry or known-answer row states; the logs.
Out: the search and its methods (BD-273, N26a-07 to N26a-09); the planted traps (N26a-04); the decision on the
kill gate (N26a-12).

## Technical approach

1. **Registration (before any run; hashed into the ledger).**
   - *Ceiling:* under ten on the cost of one found failure (published gains 2 to 20 times, and none on one
     policy-in-the-loop task; a planning recomputation charging the oracle and shrinking gives about 6). The
     threshold 3 is below the ceiling, so the test can confirm. On the time to a design decision the ceiling is
     the share of that time spent finding failures, taken from the phase ledger of the baseline arm.
   - *Arms:* (1) `nemesis.search --method auto`; (2) card-uniform randomization at equal budget, which is also
     the mechanism's own fallback; (3) where a scenario-language tool is installed, its sampler with the same
     generator and monitors through `--method given`; otherwise recorded `not run`.
   - *Potency control:* a planted trap of probability 10⁻² that both arms must find; an A/A run (arm 2 against
     arm 2 with different seeds) whose paired lower bound must not clear 1.02 (the pass rule of the A/A routine
     of N38a-08, the program's rule for every method experiment: an A/A campaign that clears 1.02 means a broken
     harness, and the experiment is void until it is fixed).
   - *Reported quantity:* the one-sided 95% lower confidence bound on the paired ratio R = (steps of arm 2 to
     its first promoted failure in the trap) ÷ (steps of arm 1), pairs formed by trap and seed.
   - *Fallback:* KILL-NEMESIS, as stated.
   - *Reachability:* under the declared alternative R = 6 with the pair-to-pair spread seen in the pilot
     (N26a-06), the probability that the lower bound exceeds 3 at 30 pairs is computed and recorded; if it is
     below 0.8 the experiment is not run as planned and the plan is revised by a recorded decision.
2. **Design.** 30 paired repetitions over the T3 traps of the planted registry (probability 3 × 10⁻⁵, trigger
   box z₁ > 2.3263 and z₂ > 2.7478), ten traps across two interiors, three seeds each. "Found" is a promoted
   failure inside the trap's box with the trap's failure class and binding atom. Steps are simulated steps
   charged to the arm, including its oracle and guard runs. Arm 2 is capped at 10⁵ worlds per repetition (three
   times its expected 33,333) and a capped run enters at the cap, flagged censored, which lowers R. One campaign
   measures both arms of a pair (BD-273 runs the baseline arm beside the search arm, each on half of the
   budget, and with `--first` reports each arm's steps to its own first promoted failure), so the budget B of
   step 3 is the number of steps at which one arm's half, after its 20% reserve for oracle and guard runs,
   affords the baseline's cap of 10⁵ worlds; the two arms of a pair then share seed, tier and budget by
   construction.
3. **Script steps.**
   1. `start`: build, registry digest, registration hash, seeds, tier, hardware fingerprint.
   2. Potency: one campaign (`--method auto --first`) on the 10⁻² potency trap of each interior (the T2
      records of N26a-04); assert both arms find it. A/A (DESIGN: run on the potency traps, so that the harness
      check costs thousands of worlds and not millions): 30 pairs of one-arm campaigns `--method card-uniform
      --first`, the two campaigns of a pair on one trap with different `--seed` values and different keys; the
      ratio of their steps to the first promoted failure goes to the A/A routine of N38a-08; assert the lower
      bound does not clear 1.02.
   3. For each of the 30 pairs, one campaign: `frob nemesis search <candidate> --task <task> --legality
      <profile> --method auto --first --budget steps=<B> --seed <s> --key <trap>-<s> --json` (a key per pair;
      no two campaigns of the script share one); wait on the job handle; read from the one result each arm's
      `to_first_promoted` and `censored` under `steps.search` and `steps.baseline`, and the first promoted
      failure of each arm; assert the failure lies in the box (the "found" test of N26a-04 item 5, applied to
      the failure's world by that bead's registry tool: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
      nemesis_planted -- contains --trap <id> --world <w:…>`, which prints the world's two germ values, the
      box and the verdict).
   4. Compute R per pair and the lower bound (the paired-lower-bound routine of N38a-08, on log ratios);
      compute the solvable share as BD-273 defines it: over the search arm's candidate failures that the guard
      did not label `artifact-candidate`, the share classified `solvable-causal` or `plant-feasible` (the
      denominator also counts `not-excluded` and `infeasible-for-candidate`), summed over all 30 pairs.
   5. `metric` lines, tagged `--gate GATE-NEMESIS-02`: `ratio_lower_95 = x` against `≥ 3`; `solvable_share = y`
      against `≥ 0.70`; the potency and A/A outcomes of step 2 are logged as the gate's caught controls.
   6. The fallback branch: in a scratch configuration with `nemesis.search.methods = baseline-only` (N26a-07's
      switch), three of the 30 pairs are rerun as one-arm campaigns, `frob nemesis search <candidate> --task
      <task> --legality <profile> --method card-uniform --first --budget steps=<B> --seed <s> --key
      <trap>-<s>-fb --json` (`card-uniform` is a method the `baseline-only` portfolio holds; the method is
      named because N26a-12 has every other value of `--method` answer FROB-REF-724 under the switch, and steps
      2 to 5, which need two arms, are not rerun under it). Assert for each: the result reports
      `method: card-uniform`, `steps.baseline` and no `steps.search`, and a promoted failure inside the trap's
      box, or the arm `censored` at the whole budget (logged; at most one of the three may be censored).
      (The design-pair entry point, N26a-10, is exercised by `scripts/e2e/mission_separate.sh`, BD-293;
      this gate does not wait on the mission subsystem.)
   7. Rerun `scripts/e2e/scene_feature_law.sh` (N19ax-04) and require that its step 5 (`frob nemesis search` on
      the add-film variant: `prior.defined` true, the film term's mass ratio 1/3) runs and passes; a `skip` there
      fails this step, because `nemesis.search` and the density bead (N26a-03) now exist.
   8. Write the run's hash-named receipt (item 5); `summary`.
4. **Logging.** `target/e2e-logs/nemesis_search/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript, through the shared helper. Every
   line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N26a-11`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
   evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), and for `metric` events the value, unit
   and threshold. Per repetition: arm, trap, seed, steps per phase, censoring flag, first failure's world
   identity, class, atom, oracle class, guard outcome, legality and prior blocks, and the campaign's per-mode
   table (failure class by binding atom with counts, from the result's `classes` and `failures`: the
   catalogue's view, N26a-27). On failure: the first pair
   whose search arm did not find the trap, with the campaign identity needed to replay it. Summary: assertions
   passed and failed, `gates: ["GATE-NEMESIS-02"]`.
5. **Gate receipt, and who records.** The script never writes `registries/gates.toml` (no script and no verb
   writes a gate record). Its receipt holds the measured lower bound, the solvable share, the censored count,
   the potency and A/A outcomes, the registry digest, the registration hash, commit, fingerprint and seeds. The
   gate's record is entered from that receipt by a session that did not implement the search: `cargo run -p
   frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-NEMESIS-02 --receipt <hash>` (the recorder of
   N38b-19). A fail is recorded as a fail; one repair cycle is allowed before KILL-NEMESIS is read, and the
   second run is a new receipt and a new record, not an overwrite.
6. **Control records of the gate** (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml`, BD-025's schema; one
   plant, one record). The gate register's row for GATE-NEMESIS-02 (N26b-18) names three controls. The
   baseline arm at equal budget is the stated control cell of KA-661 (N26-KA, a dependency; the cell names
   GATE-NEMESIS-02, so the seeded record carries this gate beside the row's owner gate), so its record is
   the one the seeding tool of N38b-05 derives from that row: this bead lists it and writes no second one
   (BD-025's reuse check). The potency control and the A/A arm are stated by no catalogue entry and no
   known-answer row, and this bead, which measures the gate, appends one record for each at the next free
   `NC-` number under BD-025's rules, both with `seed = "GATE-NEMESIS-02"`, `gate = ["GATE-NEMESIS-02"]`,
   `target = "scripts/e2e/nemesis_search.sh step 2"` and `fixture` the two T2 records with `role = "potency"`
   of the GATE-NEMESIS-02 interiors in N26a-04's planted registry
   (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/planted/nemesis/`):
   - the potency control, `kind = "planted-world"`: `defect` "a trap box of nominal probability 10⁻² planted
     in each interior, common enough that every arm must find it"; `expect` "both arms of one `--method auto
     --first` campaign report a promoted failure inside the box; an arm that reports none voids the run";
   - the A/A arm, `kind = "a-a"`: `defect` "both arms of a pair are card-uniform randomization (two one-arm
     campaigns with different seeds on one potency trap), so the true ratio is 1"; `expect` "the paired lower
     bound over the 30 pairs does not clear 1.02; a bound above 1.02 voids the experiment".
   The receipt's control lines carry the three `NC-` identifiers with the outcomes of step 2 and of the
   baseline arm, from which the recorder writes each record's `last_seen`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The registration record with all six parts exists and predates the first measured run.
- [ ] The potency control passes: both arms find the 10⁻² trap; the A/A lower bound does not clear 1.02.
- [ ] `controls.toml` holds one `GATE-NEMESIS-02`-seeded record for the potency control (`planted-world`) and
      one for the A/A arm (`a-a`), each naming the T2 potency records as its `fixture`; both pass BD-025's
      loader validation; no second record exists for the baseline arm, whose record is the one seeded from
      KA-661; the receipt carries a control line for each of the three.
- [ ] The script's receipt carries GATE-NEMESIS-02's measured values (TARGET thresholds: lower bound at least 3;
      solvable share at least 70%), pass or fail, and `record_gate` accepts it from a session that did not
      implement the search; the script itself writes no gate record.
- [ ] No two campaigns of the script share an idempotency key, and each pair's two arms come from one campaign
      (asserted from the job identities in the log).
- [ ] Ten synthetic pairs with constant ratio 4 fed to the statistics step return a lower bound of 4.
- [ ] The fallback-branch step (step 6) produces promoted failures with `method: card-uniform` from one-arm
      campaigns under `nemesis.search.methods = baseline-only`.
- [ ] Step 7 runs step 5 of `scripts/e2e/scene_feature_law.sh` un-skipped and it passes (the request of the
      scene-feature-law bead to the density bead, carried here because the density bead closes before the verb).
- [ ] Logs contain one line per command and the per-repetition fields; the script exits non-zero when an
      assertion fails. A gate metric that misses its threshold is such an assertion (the shared helper asserts
      every metric that carries a threshold), so a failed gate makes the script exit 1; it still writes its
      receipt first, so that the fail is recorded as a fail and KILL-NEMESIS can read it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`scripts/e2e/tests/nemesis_search_stats.sh`: feeds recorded step counts to the statistics step: constant ratio 4
→ bound 4; a baseline censored at 10⁸ steps in every pair with the search at 10⁷ steps → R = 10 per pair,
bound 10; a mixed set with one
search arm that never finds the trap → that pair enters with R below 1 and the bound drops accordingly.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-661 (the planted solvable trap is found with a causal witness; the baseline at equal budget is its control).
Negative controls: the A/A arm; a search arm silently given twice the budget (the equal-steps assertion fails);
a "found" rule that accepts any failure anywhere (the in-box assertion fails on the baseline's unplanted
failures). Determinism lanes: the 30-pair run repeated on one machine gives identical step counts on one
backend; across backends the gate is re-measured, not compared bit for bit.

## Considerations

- Wall time: 30 capped baseline repetitions are up to 3 × 10⁶ worlds; on the fast tier this is minutes to
  hours depending on measured throughput, and the script prints an estimate first.
- The lower bound is on a ratio of costs to the first failure, which is heavy-tailed (geometric for the
  baseline); the bound routine works on log ratios, and its coverage on geometric data is part of its own test.
- A pass says the search finds planted traps of this kind faster. It does not say that unplanted failures of
  real designs are found faster; the share of design-loop time spent finding failures is reported from the phase
  ledger so the effect on decisions is not overstated.
