## Background

The artifact guard exists to stop failures and witnesses that the simulator invented. Its gate
(GATE-NEMESIS-06) needs both kinds of plant: failures that exist only because of a known simulator artifact,
which must all be labelled `artifact-candidate`, and failures with a physical cause, of which at least 95 in 100
must survive. Without the second kind a guard that rejects everything would pass. Two artifacts are named as the
seeds of the negative controls:

- **Box-cone traction.** A friction cone linearized as a box (a four-sided pyramid aligned with the tangent
  axes) grants √2 times the friction coefficient along its diagonals, 41% more than the true cone.
- **Energy-pumping penetration recovery.** A position-level correction that removes a penetration depth d in one
  step of length h gives the body a velocity d/h from nothing. With m = 1 kg, d = 1 mm and h = 2 ms that is
  0.5 m/s and 0.125 J per event; a hopping body gains height from the solver. The compliant contact law on the
  same penetration at stiffness 10⁵ N/m stores ½·k·d² = 0.05 J and returns less.

Both are deliberate, labelled defects that can be switched on in a non-production step plan. The same two
mechanisms are the planted exploits of the created-energy audit; this bead reuses the mechanisms and builds
failing worlds and false witnesses from them.

## Scope

In: 100 planted artifact failures (50 per artifact), 20 planted false witnesses, 100 planted genuine failures,
their truth records, the freeze.
Out: the guard (BD-272); the script (N26a-16); the cone kind and the recovery mode themselves, which are the two
labelled solver mutations that N16b-15 (M2, a dependency) builds once as conformance-only step-plan options and
registers as controls: `pyramid4` (the four-sided pyramid cone) and `penetration-recovery` (the position-level
correction), with its hopper and diagonal-push fixtures. This bead adds no mutation and no second copy of
either; its artifact step plans select those options, and its reference instances are further instances of
those fixtures' families. Also out: traps for the search and the oracle (N26a-04).

## Technical approach

1. **Registry.** `crates/frob-conformance/registry/planted/nemesis_guard/`: one record per plant with `id`,
   `kind` (`artifact-failure` | `false-witness` | `genuine-failure`), `artifact` (`box-cone` | `penetration-pump`
   | none), the world, the candidate, the task and requirement, the step plan that carries the artifact, and
   `truth`: the expected guard status and, for artifacts, the set of checks that must remove the failure. The
   two values of `artifact` name families of plants; the step plan of a `box-cone` record carries the option
   `pyramid4` and that of a `penetration-pump` record the option `penetration-recovery`, the names under which
   N16b-15 registers the mutations (one to one; a test asserts the mapping).
2. **Box-cone failures (50).** Family: a body that must slide along a diagonal to satisfy its requirement.
   Reference instance: a 10 kg block on a floor of friction 0.5, pushed by a fixture along the tangent diagonal
   with 60 N, requirement "delivered to the far mark within 5 s". True cone limit 0.5 × 10 × 9.81 = 49.05 N: it
   slides and is delivered. Box cone limit √2 × 49.05 = 69.37 N: it sticks and "delivered" is violated
   (failure class `stuck`). The 50 instances vary mass (5 to 20 kg), friction (0.3 to 0.7), push direction
   within ±10° of the diagonal and push force at 1.15 to 1.30 times μ·m·g (inside the window between the two
   limits, whose ratio is 1.414), plus a robot variant (a skid-steer turn that needs lateral slip).
3. **Penetration-pump failures (50).** Family: a hopping or bouncing body under the position-level recovery
   mode at a step near the stability limit, requirement "apex height stays below a ceiling" or "upright through
   ten hops". Each event injects ½·m·(d/h)² (0.125 J for the reference instance); height grows until the
   requirement is violated. Instances vary mass, drop height, step (1 to 4 ms) and contact stiffness.
4. **False witnesses (20).** Runs that satisfy a requirement only through an artifact, for the guard's witness
   role: ten that hold a payload on a slope with tan θ = 0.6 at friction 0.5 (the true cone needs 0.6; the box
   cone's diagonal grants 0.707); ten that clear a ledge by pumped hop height.
5. **Genuine failures (100).** Worlds in which the candidate fails for a physical reason that does not depend on
   the cone linearization, the recovery mode, the step, the iteration count, the tolerance or the choice between
   the two compliant laws. Chosen with margin: a slip on a film patch under a push where the friction demand
   exceeds supply by at least 30%; a stall at a threshold where required torque exceeds the card's peak by at
   least 30%; a tip under a payload shift where the centre of pressure leaves the support by at least 20 mm; a
   late recovery under a sensor latency twice the controller's tolerance. 25 of each. The margins are what make
   "survives every variant" a fair expectation; the gate still allows 5 in 100 to be lost.
6. **Truth by measurement, then frozen.** For each artifact plant, the first measurement on the referee and on
   `batch-cpu` records which checks remove the failure (tier-up with the production cone; `law-2`; the energy
   audit's flag). That set is written to `truth` and frozen; thereafter the guard must report at least those
   removals. For each genuine plant, a referee run and the four variant runs confirm the failure persists before
   the plant is admitted to the registry (a plant that does not persist is not genuine and is dropped).
7. **Hand-checkable invariants of the plants**, asserted by tests so that a registry edit cannot drift: for every
   box-cone instance, μ·m·g < push < √2·μ·m·g; for every pump instance, the per-event energy ½·m·(d/h)² exceeds
   the compliant law's stored ½·k·d².
8. **Freeze.** The registry digest is recorded before BD-272's gate is measured.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 100 artifact failures, 20 false witnesses and 100 genuine failures validate against the record schema.
- [ ] Every box-cone instance satisfies μ·m·g < push < √2·μ·m·g; every pump instance satisfies the energy
      inequality; both asserted by test.
- [ ] Every artifact failure fails under its artifact step plan and passes under the production plan on the
      referee (asserted once at registration, recorded per plant).
- [ ] Every genuine failure fails on the referee and under all four numerical variants at registration.
- [ ] `truth.removed_by` is recorded for every artifact plant and the registry is frozen before the guard's
      gate.
- [ ] The registry is listed in the conformance planted-defect index so that the "every gate has a registered
      negative control" check finds it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/nemesis_guard_planted.rs`:
- `reference_values`: 49.05 N and 69.37 N; 0.5 m/s, 0.125 J and 0.05 J; √2 × 0.5 = 0.7071 > 0.6 > 0.5.
- `box_cone_window_all_instances`; `pump_energy_all_instances`.
- `artifact_plan_fails_production_passes` (sampled 10 per artifact, real runs on the CPU reference tier).
- `genuine_persists_under_variants` (sampled 10).
- `registry_counts_and_schema`; `truth_frozen_hash`.
- `artifact_maps_to_registered_option`: every `box-cone` record's step plan carries `pyramid4` and every
  `penetration-pump` record's carries `penetration-recovery`, and both options resolve to the control records
  of N16b-15.

### End-to-end script and logging
Consumed by `scripts/e2e/nemesis_guard.sh` (N26a-16). This bead's own script,
`scripts/e2e/nemesis_guard_register.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; seed-explicit; idempotent: a rerun on a
frozen registry verifies instead of writing), performs the first measurements through `frob sim run --json`
and `frob sim replay --tier … --json` and writes `target/e2e-logs/nemesis_guard_register/<utc>.ndjson` plus a
transcript. Both are run verbs: every call carries its own `--key` (plant identifier, tier and variant), so a
rerun returns the stored receipts and no two measurements are one request. The artifact step plans are
requested as conformance requests: the script needs the binary built with the `conformance` feature and a
session holding the conformance scope, without which N16b-15's guard refuses the two options (the script then
exits 77, skipped as a whole, never passed). Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N26a-15`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit
code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block. Per plant: the step plan and its option (`pyramid4`,
`penetration-recovery` or none), verdict, class, atom, failure time and energy readout on each tier and variant,
with `metric` lines for the hand-checkable window (push against μ·m·g and √2·μ·m·g, in N) and the per-event
energy against ½·k·d² (in J); the removal set written to the registry; the registry digest at freeze. Assertions:
every artifact plant fails under its artifact plan and passes under the production plan; every genuine plant
fails on the referee and under all four variants. On failure: the first plant that does not behave as its kind
says, with its record path and seeds. A final `summary` gives counts per kind and assertions passed and failed.
Planted control: a copy of the registry with one genuine plant whose margin is set to zero (demand equal to
supply) must be dropped by the persistence check.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-662: the planted artifacts are labelled `artifact-candidate` with the variant that removed each; the control
is that a genuine planted failure survives every variant. CX-295: a failure found under one contact law is not
thereby a failure of the design. The plants are themselves the negative controls of GATE-NEMESIS-06; a registry
with no genuine failures (so that "reject everything" passes) is caught by the count test.

## Considerations

- The artifact step plans are non-production and labelled; no evaluation or training run may select them, and a
  result produced under one cannot be promoted.
- The window between μ and √2·μ is where the box-cone plant lives; near its edges solver tolerance decides the
  outcome, so instances sit at least 10% inside.
- If the production contact law changes, registration is rerun and the registry gets a new version; recorded
  gate values that used the old one are void.
- The corpus is discovery data and is never a confirmation sample.
