## Background

GATE-NEMESIS-04 (known-answer claim; earliest milestone M3; TARGET) has three rows: over 10⁴ synthetic studies
with known p the 95% upper bound covers p in at least 95%; the defensive mixture covers under a mis-centred
proposal; non-nominal episodes are refused. It is the gate that says the certification lane tells the truth: a
bound that does not cover is worse than no bound, and a bound that accepts selected episodes is not a bound.
The verification program lists the same check under "certifying the certifiers": for rare-event bounds, the
Gaussian tail 3.167 × 10⁻⁵ and the random-walk probability 1.504 × 10⁻⁴ must lie inside the confidence sequence
in at least the stated share of repetitions, with every weight at most 1/ε.

`nemesis.bound` is the user-visible capability: modes `certify`, `refute` and `estimate` for a failure
probability against a target. This script drives it on the real binary, on fixtures whose truth is known in
closed form, and writes the receipt from which the gate is recorded.

## Scope

In: the script; the synthetic-rate fixtures; the coverage campaign; the refusal probes; the gate record; logs.
Out: the estimators and the verb (BD-276 and its component beads); exploration's gate (N26a-31).

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures (all labelled `synthetic`; each is a real task run by the real engine, with a failure predicate
   that is a declared function of randomized germs so that p is exact).**
   - R1: one-sided Gaussian tail, failure when z₁ > 4: p = 3.167 × 10⁻⁵.
   - R2: two-sided tail, failure when |z₁| > 4: p = 6.334 × 10⁻⁵ (for the mis-centred proposal).
   - R3: Bernoulli-rate tasks with p ∈ {5 × 10⁻³, 10⁻³} for the fixed-n coverage campaign.
   - R4: the random walk (up-probability 0.4, start level 1, level 20 before 0): p = 1.504 × 10⁻⁴, as a task
     with per-step randomness.
2. **Steps.** Conventions for every call: `frob nemesis bound` takes `--tier batch-cpu` explicitly (the verb has
   no tier default: the scope is part of the claim) and a distinct idempotency key `--key nb-<step>-<i>`, so that
   two runs the script compares are two runs and not one stored receipt; options are only those on the verb's
   row in VERBS-nemesis.
   1. `start`: build, seeds, tier, hardware fingerprint.
   2. Cost table: `frob nemesis bound <c> --task R3 --tier batch-cpu --target 1e-4 --mode certify --estimate
      --key nb-2-1 --json` (and 1e-5, 1e-6): assert required episodes 29,956, 299,572 and 2,995,731 at δ = 0.05,
      and 76,959 with `--delta 1/2200` (0.05/110, the level of the tenth qualification request); assert the
      estimate also names the sequence's clean count and the design a budget buys (N26a-22: 92,872 and
      `sequential` at 10⁻⁴ with a budget of 10⁵ episodes; `fixed-n` at 10⁻⁵ whatever the budget).
   3. Budget refusal: a certify request for 10⁻⁵ with budget for 100,000 episodes: assert `FROB-REF-760`,
      exit 4, `reachable_bound` ≈ 2.996 × 10⁻⁵.
   4. Row 1, coverage. (a) Fixed-n part, at the planned (n, p) pairs (1,000, 5 × 10⁻³) and (5,000, 10⁻³), whose
      exact coverages by enumeration are 0.9599 and 0.9596: the verb has no option that runs many studies of a
      chosen size, so the 10⁴-study campaign per pair is BD-276's crate-level campaign (`gate_rows`), run through
      the Gauntlet runner by the gate's name: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
      g1_known_answers -- GATE-NEMESIS-04`; the script reads the receipt and asserts that the count of studies
      whose one-sided 95% upper bound is at least p is at least 9,500 per pair. KA-141 is a G0 row (SPEC-C-27),
      so the random-walk fixture R4 runs under the laws target: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p
      frob-conformance --test g0_laws -- KA-141` (crude and bounded-weight bounds bracket 1.504 × 10⁻⁴ in at
      least 1 − δ of 10⁴ repetitions; fork control caught). (b) Sequential part, from the user's
      side: 1,000 calls `frob nemesis bound <c> --task R3-1e-3 --tier batch-cpu --target 1e-3 --mode certify
      --budget episodes=10000 --key nb-4b-<i> --json` (each a sequential study to a horizon of 10,000 with
      stopping); assert the true p lies inside the reported interval in at least 950 of 1,000. (c) Parity: 20
      of the crate-level studies are repeated through the binary with the same addresses and give the same
      counts and bounds bit for bit.
   5. Row 2, mis-centred proposal: 1,000 studies on R2 in mode `estimate` with shift +4 only and ε = 1/10
      (`--shift 4 --epsilon 1/10`, distinct keys): assert the confidence sequence covers 6.334 × 10⁻⁵ in at least
      950; assert every recorded weight is at most 10. Control: the same campaign with the nominal share removed
      is a proposal law a normal build cannot construct, so it runs as the registered negative control of
      KA-665 (a G0 row, N26-KA): `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0_laws
      --features gauntlet-mutations -- KA-665`; assert the control is reported caught with coverage below 50%, which shows
      the fixture has teeth; assert also that the release binary refuses `--epsilon 0` at admission with
      FROB-REF-563, exit 3, zero episodes drawn (N26a-24: a mixture without its nominal share is outside the
      admitted law).
   6. KA-665: mode `estimate` on R1 with shift 4, ε = 1/10: assert 3.167 × 10⁻⁵ inside the interval and maximum
      weight ≤ 10.
   7. Refute: mode `refute` on R1 against p⋆ = 10⁻⁵: assert outcome `refuted` (exit 2) and log the draws used;
      against p⋆ = 10⁻⁴: assert not refuted.
   8. Certify: mode `certify` on a rate task with p = 0 (a predicate that never fires) against p⋆ = 10⁻³: with
      `--budget episodes=2995` assert design `fixed-n` and `certified` (exit 0) at 2,995 = `required(10⁻³, 0.05)`
      episodes; with `--budget episodes=10000` assert design `sequential` and `certified` at the first batch
      boundary at or after the sequence's clean count (planning value 8,062); on R3 with p = 5 × 10⁻³ against
      10⁻³ and the larger budget: assert `refuted`.
   9. Row 3, refusals: offer through `--episodes` (a) a tilted set (from a `nemesis.search` cross-entropy
      campaign), (b) a search-selected set, (c) fork children, (d) a battery-selected run (a run of a covering
      battery from `nemesis.cover`, BD-278), (e) a hard-world-set run (`nemesis.hardset run`, BD-275): assert
      `FROB-REF-560` each, exit 3, no estimate in the envelope; twin: a fresh nominal set under a new commitment
      is admitted. Offer a proposal fitted on the run's own addresses: `FROB-REF-563`. Change one comparator of
      the requirement after a commitment and continue: `FROB-REF-568`. `--mode certify --proposal <id>`: assert
      exit 3 with slot `certify-by-importance-sampling` and zero episodes drawn. A certify request on a
      static-only variant of R4 under the `table` policy: assert the bound completes by nominal sampling with
      `FROB-REF-569` in `fallback.trigger`.
   10. Policy: three synthetic finalists through the full decision policy (N26a-26), logging transitions.
   11. Scope: assert every result envelope has the scope line, tier, randomization and predicate identities and
       evidence class `Tested`.
   12. `metric` lines and gate record; `summary`.
3. **Logging.** `target/e2e-logs/nemesis_bound/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript, via the shared helper. Every line:
   `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N26a-30`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block
   (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). Metrics with value, unit and threshold:
   `coverage_fixed_n[(n,p)]`, `coverage_sequential`, `coverage_mixture_miscentred`, `coverage_bare_proposal`
   (control), `max_weight` against 1/ε, `refusals_ok`. Per bounded-weight batch: n, failures under the proposal,
   max and mean weight, log-wealth, interval. On failure: the first study whose bound missed, with its seed and
   counts. Summary: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-NEMESIS-04"]`.
4. **Gate record.** The three rows' measured values, with the exact coverages of the planned pairs printed
   beside the Monte Carlo counts, commit, fingerprint, seeds, in the script's receipt, which hashes the log and
   the Gauntlet receipts of steps 4 and 5; the gate's record is entered from it by a session that did not
   implement the capability, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate
   GATE-NEMESIS-04 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19; no script writes a gate record).
5. **Runtime.** The fixed-n campaign is 10⁴ × 6,000 synthetic episodes of a trivial task: minutes on the CPU.
   The 2,000 user-side calls of steps 4(b) and 5 are each a few thousand trivial episodes; the script runs them
   through one `frobd` so that process start-up is paid once.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `scripts/e2e/nemesis_bound.sh` exits 0 on a correct build and non-zero on any failed assertion.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-04 is recorded with measured values for all three rows (TARGET: coverage at least 95% over
      10⁴ studies; the mixture covers under the mis-centred proposal; non-nominal episodes refused).
- [ ] The control of row 2 (a proposal without its nominal share) fails to cover, and the script requires that.
- [ ] The cost table values and the FROB-REF-760 refusal with its reachable bound are asserted.
- [ ] FROB-REF-560, -563 and -568 are each produced by their probe with exit 3 and no estimate, and each
      refusal's admissible twin is admitted; the certify-with-proposal slot and the 569 fallback record are
      asserted.
- [ ] This bead is GATE-NEMESIS-04's one recorder (the gate register, N26b-18): the record is entered from the
      script's receipt with `record_gate` by a session that did not implement the capability, and the script
      itself writes no gate record (BD-276 measures the rows at crate level and depends on no script).
- [ ] Every result has the scope line and class `Tested`; none says `Certified`.
- [ ] The log has per-study and per-batch fields and a final summary line.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`scripts/e2e/tests/nemesis_bound_selftest.sh`: runs the coverage step against a build with the test feature
`nemesis-bound-inward-rounding` (bounds rounded the wrong way and the zero-failure count taken from
⌊ln(1/δ)/p⋆⌋ − 2) and requires the cost-table assertion to fail; runs the refusal step against
`nemesis-lane-open` (the admission check disabled) and requires the script to fail on the first accepted tilted
set.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-664 (29,956; 299,572; 2,995,731; and 76,959 for the tenth qualification request; control: the undercount
29,955, one below the exact value (NEG-04, CX-400; the count rounded down from ln(1/δ)/p⋆, 29,957, errs on the
safe side and is withdrawn)), KA-665 (Gaussian tail inside the confidence sequence; weights at most 10; control: a
mixture without its nominal share on a mis-centred proposal), KA-141 (bounds bracket a closed-form probability
in at least 1 − δ of 10⁴ repetitions), KA-139. CX-290, CX-291, CX-294 (the hard-world set offered as nominal
draws). Determinism lanes: the campaign run twice on one machine gives identical counts; across worker counts
identical; on the second platform the Bernoulli-rate fixtures give equal counts (their draws are exact integer
arithmetic), and the Gaussian fixtures are compared under the determinism class of normal variates (bitwise per
backend; across backends only where the cross-backend gate has passed, otherwise the gate rows are re-measured).

## Considerations

- Reaching "at least 9,500 of 10⁴" for a bound whose true coverage is exactly 95% would be a coin toss. The
  planned pairs have exact coverage near 0.96, so the criterion is reached with probability above 0.9999 for a
  correct implementation and is still failed by a bound that under-covers by one percentage point.
- The synthetic tasks exercise the whole path (sampler, engine, monitor, admission, estimator, envelope) but not
  robot physics; the physical fixtures of the exploration gate do that.
- The test-only "bare proposal" law exists only behind a feature flag in test builds; a normal build cannot
  construct it, and the script asserts that too.
