## Background

GATE-NEMESIS-05 (claim: Design comparison, a method against a competing arm; earliest milestone M3; TARGET): on
planted failures of probability 10⁻⁴ and 10⁻⁵, one with two separate modes, at equal episode budget and 30
repetitions, exploration reaches failures with at least 10 times fewer episodes than crude sampling at 10⁻⁵, and
both modes are found in at least 80% of repetitions. It is the product-scale repeat of the exploration pilot,
and it feeds kill gate KILL-RARE.

Exploration here means the two methods of the exploration lane: multilevel splitting on forks (snapshots of
worlds that reached a distress level, continued under fresh per-step disturbances) and cross-entropy tilting of
static parameters. Neither issues a bound; they issue failures and rates labelled `Estimated`. This script
measures what they are for: finding rare failures sooner, without collapsing onto one failure mode.

As a method experiment it must be registered before its first run with six parts: a ceiling, competing arms, a
potency control, a paired lower bound as the reported quantity, a pre-declared fallback and a reachability
pre-analysis.

## Scope

In: the registration; the script; the measurement; the gate record; the logs.
Out: splitting, tilting and the distress score (BD-277, N26a-08, N26a-29); the planted fixtures (N26a-04); the
bound's gate (N26a-30); the kill decision (N26a-32).

## Technical approach

1. **Registration (hashed into the ledger before any run).**
   - *Ceiling:* published gains 2 to 20 times (and about 5 times in mean-square error on one policy-in-the-loop
     task); the TARGET of 10 to 100 times at 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻⁵ holds only with an informative score. The threshold
     10 is inside that range, so the test can confirm. On the cost of a certified statement the ceiling is
     about 3 (a clean bound at 10⁻⁵ costs about 3 × 10⁵ nominal episodes whatever the explorer does), and that
     is the figure quoted for decisions.
   - *Arms:* (1) exploration: `nemesis.search --method splitting` with tilting on the static germs, sharing one
     failure archive; (2) crude nominal sampling at equal episode budget, which is also the fallback; (3) the
     defensive mixture in `estimate` mode with a proposal from a separate pilot, as a second comparison.
   - *Potency control:* a planted failure of probability 10⁻² that every arm must find; an A/A run (crude
     against crude) whose lower bound must be below 1.2.
   - *Reported quantity:* the one-sided 95% lower confidence bound on the paired ratio (crude
     episode-equivalents to the first promoted planted failure ÷ exploration's), per fixture.
   - *Fallback:* KILL-RARE (crude nominal sampling with the binomial bound alone; splitting removed; tilting
     kept only inside `nemesis.search`).
   - *Reachability:* under the declared alternative (ratio 30) with the spread seen in the pilot, the
     probability that the lower bound exceeds 10 at 30 pairs is computed and recorded; below 0.8 the plan is
     revised by a recorded decision before running.
2. **Fixtures.** From the planted registry: T4 (probability 10⁻⁴; box z₁ > 2.3263 and z₂ > 2.3263) and the
   two-mode T5 (probability 10⁻⁵; mode A z₁ > 4.4172, a slip binding `upright`; mode B z₂ > 4.4172, a torque
   saturation binding `delivered`), on the quadruped task with per-step push disturbances, so that forks carry
   fresh randomness and part of each trigger accumulates during the episode. "Found" is a promoted failure
   inside a planted box with the box's class and atom.
3. **Budget and accounting.** An episode-equivalent is the simulated steps of one full nominal episode; fork
   fragments, level pilots, oracle and guard runs are charged by their steps to the arm that caused them. Per
   repetition: 3 × 10⁴ episode-equivalents on T4 and 10⁵ on T5, equal for all arms; a run that does not find the
   failure is censored at its budget.
4. **Steps.**
   1. `start`; potency control; A/A.
   2. Level pilot and freeze: assert the level record's hash is in the ledger before stage 0; assert the
      per-step stream check passes (and that the same request on a static-only variant of the task is refused
      with `FROB-REF-569`, splitting requested where no randomness enters after the fork).
   3. For each of 30 paired repetitions per fixture: run arm 1 and arm 2 (and arm 3) with paired seeds; record
      episode-equivalents to the first promoted planted failure, the modes found, the Good–Turing unseen mass
      at the end, per-level fork fractions, duplicates detected (must be zero).
   4. Statistics: the paired lower bound of the ratio at 10⁻⁵ and at 10⁻⁴; the share of T5 repetitions in which
      both modes were found by arm 1.
   5. Label checks: every rate printed by arm 1 carries `Estimated` and "not a bound"; offering arm 1's
      episodes to `frob nemesis bound --episodes` returns `FROB-REF-560`.
   6. Coverage reading for KILL-RARE: for every arm that offers an interval as a bound (arm 2's exact binomial
      interval and arm 3's confidence sequence) the share of repetitions whose interval covers the planted
      probability interval.
   7. `metric` lines: `gain_lower_95_at_1e-5` against `≥ 10`; `both_modes_share` against `≥ 0.80`;
      `interval_coverage[arm]` (read by the kill gate against 0.85); gate record; `summary`.
5. **Logging.** `target/e2e-logs/nemesis_explore/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript, through the shared helper. Every
   line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N26a-31`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence
   block. Per repetition and arm: fixture, seed, episode-equivalents per phase, censoring flag, first failure's
   world identity with fork path, class, atom, oracle class, guard status, modes found; for splitting the level
   table (trials, hits, fraction per level), snapshots taken, duplicates; the unseen-mass value with its
   interval. On failure: the first repetition in which a planted mode was missed, with what is needed to replay
   it. Summary: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-NEMESIS-05"]`.
6. **Gate record.** Both measured values, the censored counts, the registry digest, the level record hashes, the
   registration hash, commit, fingerprint and seeds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The registration record with all six parts predates the first measured run.
- [ ] The potency control and the A/A check pass.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-05 is recorded with measured values, pass or fail (TARGET: at least 10 times fewer episodes
      than crude sampling at 10⁻⁵ at a 95% lower bound; both modes found in at least 80% of repetitions).
- [ ] The three quantities KILL-RARE reads (gain at 10⁻⁵, coverage of each interval offered as a bound, share of
      repetitions missing a planted mode) are in the record.
- [ ] No duplicate children are detected in any repetition; the static-only request is refused with
      FROB-REF-569.
- [ ] Every exploration rate is labelled `Estimated`; exploration episodes are refused by the bound.
- [ ] The gate record names the requirement classes excluded from splitting, read from the decision record of
      the open question on flat margins (N26b-20): either the classes for which the default stands (no
      splitting, crude sampling) or the score admitted for a class. The open question blocks this gate, so the
      record is not written before that decision exists.
- [ ] The script exits non-zero when an assertion fails and zero when the gate is measured.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`scripts/e2e/tests/nemesis_explore_stats.sh`: recorded episode counts through the statistics step: constant
ratio 12 over ten pairs → bound 12; a set where mode B is never found → `both_modes_share` = 0; a set with all
crude runs censored → conservative ratios at the cap.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-666 (the random-walk level probability, run as the script's first sanity step: the splitting estimate of
1.504 × 10⁻⁴ within its group spread; a walk with no noise after the fork is refused). KA-667 (the unseen-mass
monitor, logged). KA-140. CX-292. Negative controls: the A/A arm; a build with the test feature
`nemesis-fork-same-key` (children share the parent's streams: the duplicate check must abort with FROB-REF-569
and the script requires that); an exploration arm that finds only mode A (the both-modes row fails).
Determinism lanes: one repetition replayed on one machine gives identical fork paths and digests; the gate is
re-measured, not compared bit for bit, across backends.

## Considerations

- Wall time: 30 repetitions × (3 × 10⁴ + 10⁵) episode-equivalents per arm is about 4 × 10⁶ per arm; hours on a
  device at measured throughput, a day on CPU cores. The script prints an estimate and supports resuming by
  idempotency keys.
- OPEN-NEMESIS-2: if the requirement's margin is flat until shortly before failure the level pilot declares the
  score `flat` and the gate cannot be measured on that requirement; the fixtures use a requirement with a graded
  margin, and the script reports the declaration if it occurs.
- A pass at 10 times on planted fixtures is about discovery speed. The record prints the certification ceiling
  (about 3) beside it so the two are not confused.
