## Background

GATE-NEMESIS-03 (known-answer claim; earliest milestone M3; TARGET): planted causes recovered exactly in at least
95 of 100 trials; every `.fmcw` replays with identical digests in 100 of 100; on unplanted failures the share of
worlds with at most three edits is recorded. The third row is a measurement without a threshold: it is the number
that kill gate KILL-MCW reads (on unplanted failures fewer than half of shrunk worlds have at most three edits,
or fewer than 90% replay).

The capability behind it is `nemesis.shrink`: it shrinks a promoted failure and writes a `.fmcw`, the minimal
counterexample world file. Shrinking is delta debugging over the failing world's edit list, under a test relation
asked on 64 paired seeds (a subset fails when the world built from it is legal, replays deterministically,
violates the same requirement atom with the same failure class, and does so on at least 48 of 64 seeds); the
result is 1-minimal (removing any single edit makes the failure disappear), continuous edits are then moved
toward nominal by bisection, and when the original list has at most 12 edits every one- and two-edit subset is
also tested so that the result is the minimum among pairs. A file that does not replay with identical digests on
its originating backend is invalid.

This script measures the gate from the user's side on the real binary and builds the one fixture set the gate
needs that nothing else provides: worlds with a planted cause hidden among inert edits.

## Scope

In: the planted-cause registry (100 trials); the unplanted-failure sample; the script; the gate record; logs.
Out: the shrinker and its components (BD-274, N26a-17, N26a-18, N26a-19); the kill decision (N26a-35).

## Technical approach

1. **Planted-cause registry (DESIGN; `crates/frob-conformance/registry/planted/nemesis_shrink/`).** 100 trials.
   Each trial is a failing world built from a solvable trap of the planted registry (N26a-04) whose mechanism
   needs exactly a known set of edits (the cause), plus inert legal edits that do not affect the mechanism:
   - cause sizes 1, 2 and 3 (34, 33 and 33 trials);
   - total edits per world drawn from 8 to 32 (so 5 to 31 inert edits), in a seeded random order;
   - inert edits come from classes that cannot touch the mechanism by construction (an optics edit in a world
     whose failure is a slip with a proprioceptive controller; a fixture edit in an unvisited room), and each
     trial is checked at registration: the cause alone fails on at least 60 of 64 seeds; every proper subset of
     the cause fails on at most 4 of 64; the inert edits alone fail on 0 of 64.
   - 5 further trials, outside the 100, contain a **masked** second cause (a second grease patch under a mat):
     the full world fails by the planted cause, and a subset that drops the mat can fail by the second patch.
     Failure is then not monotone in the edit set; the asserted result is any failing set of the same mechanism
     whose every single removal passes, and the result must carry no distance-to-boundary claim.
   - 5 further trials, outside the 100, plant two disjoint causes of sizes 2 and 3 in the order of the
     order-dependence example, to show the pair check from the user's side.
   Truth per trial: the cause's edit set. The registry is frozen and hashed before the shrinker's first measured
   run.
2. **Unplanted failures.** At least 60 promoted failures (TARGET) found by `frob nemesis search` on reference
   robots with their baseline controllers in generated interiors of at least three archetypes, with no planted
   trap, taken in the order found (no selection by size or outcome). The sample's campaign identities are
   recorded so the measurement can be reproduced.
3. **Steps.** Every run-verb call (`nemesis search`, `nemesis shrink`, `sim replay`) carries a distinct `--key
   ns-<step>-<trial>`; the two replays of step 8 (original and altered copy) carry different keys, so the second
   is a run and not the first one's stored receipt.
   1. `start`: build, registry digests, seeds, originating backend, hardware fingerprint.
   2. Planted causes: for each of 100 trials, `frob nemesis shrink <world> --key <k> --json` (exit 8 then 2).
      Assert the result's edit set equals the planted cause (paths equal; continuous values at or between the
      planted threshold and the planted value after bisection); `minimality` is `1-minimal`;
      `neighbours_passing` has one identity per edit; `seeds_failing` at least 48 of 64. Metric
      `planted_recovered = k of 100` against `≥ 95`.
   3. Replay: for every `.fmcw` written in step 2 and step 5, `frob sim replay <fmcw> --json`: assert identical
      state digests on the originating backend. Metric `fmcw_replay = k of n` against `n of n` (the gate says
      100 of 100).
   4. Order dependence: on the 5 two-cause trials assert the result is the two-edit cause and the label is
      "1-minimal; minimum among pairs" (`pairs_exhausted: true`); with `--no-pair-check` assert the three-edit
      cause is returned and the label is "1-minimal" only.
   5. Unplanted failures: shrink each; record edits before and after, tests run, seeds failing, label; compute
      the share with at most three edits and the share that replay. Metrics
      `unplanted_at_most_three = s` (recorded; no threshold in the gate) and `unplanted_replay = r`.
   6. Refusals: shrink an artifact candidate and a failure whose recorded digest was altered: assert
      `FROB-REF-564` (shrinking requested for a failure that does not replay, or that the guard labelled an
      artifact candidate), exit 3, for each.
   7. Different mechanism: one trial whose reduced world fails by another atom: assert the separate finding is
      recorded with its own classification and the shrink's result keeps the original atom and class.
   8. Validation: feed each written file to the reader; assert it validates; alter one edit value in a copy and
      assert `frob sim replay` reports the first differing step and the file is declared invalid.
   9. Write the receipt the gate is recorded from (item 5); `summary`.
4. **Logging.** `target/e2e-logs/nemesis_shrink/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript, via the shared helper. Every
   line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N26a-34`), `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence
   block. Per shrink: trial id, edits before, the planted cause, edits after, tests run (distinct subsets), the
   outcome of each test in order (subset, k of 64, cache hit), bisection steps per edit, pair-check count, label,
   seeds failing, the `.fmcw` world identity and content hash, the digest comparison. On failure: the first
   trial whose result differs from its cause, with the trace needed to replay the shrink. Summary: assertions
   passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-NEMESIS-03"]`.
5. **Gate record.** `planted_recovered`, `fmcw_replay`, `unplanted_at_most_three`, `unplanted_replay`, sample
   sizes, registry digests, commit, fingerprint, seeds, in the script's receipt; the gate's record is entered
   from it by a session that did not implement the capability, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
   record_gate -- --gate GATE-NEMESIS-03 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19; no script writes a gate record).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The planted-cause registry has 100 trials with sizes 1, 2 and 3 among 8 to 32 edits, every trial passed
      its registration checks, and the registry was frozen before the first measured shrink.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/nemesis_shrink.sh` exits 0 on a correct build and non-zero on any failed assertion.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-03 is recorded with measured values: planted causes recovered exactly (TARGET at least 95 of
      100); every `.fmcw` replays with identical digests (TARGET 100 of 100); the share of unplanted failures
      with at most three edits (recorded, with its sample size).
- [ ] The share of unplanted shrunk worlds that replay is recorded for the kill gate.
- [ ] The order-dependence trials show the three-edit result without the pair check and the two-edit result with
      it.
- [ ] FROB-REF-564 is produced for an artifact candidate and for a failure that does not replay.
- [ ] The log has the per-shrink fields above and a final summary line.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-663 (shrinking planted causes of size 1, 2 and 3 among 8 to 32 edits returns the planted set exactly;
control: the order-dependence example, {c, d, e} before the pair check and {a, b} after it, at most 78 tests of
one- and two-edit subsets for 12 edits). KA-142 (edits 3 and 6 of eight). KA-144 (a stored `.fmcw` reproduces its
state hash). CX-288, CX-067. INV-NEMESIS-04 (a `.fmcw` replays with identical digests on its originating
backend), PO-NEMESIS-01 (1-minimal under its test relation). Metamorphic relation: removing any edit of a
1-minimal world removes the failure (asserted against the passing neighbours). Negative controls: the
first-round-only shrinker; a shrinker on one seed instead of 64 (a seed-fragile planted world then yields a
wrong cause). Determinism lanes: a shrink replayed on one machine gives the same trace, the same result and the
same file bytes.

## Considerations

- Cost: a shrink of a 32-edit world takes up to a few hundred tests of 64 episodes each; at least 170 shrinks (100
  planted, 5 masked, 5 two-cause, 60 or more unplanted) are of the order of 10⁶ episodes on the fast tier. The
  script supports resuming by idempotency keys and prints an estimate first.
- "Recovered exactly" is strict; a result that contains the cause plus one inert edit is a miss. The 95-of-100
  threshold absorbs seed-fragile trials, not sloppy minimization.
- The unplanted sample is whatever the search finds; if the search is removed by its kill gate the sample comes
  from card-uniform randomization, and the record says which.
