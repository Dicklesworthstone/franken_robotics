## Background

The screening audit runs a random sample of each stratum of discarded designs at the next stage (the audit lane,
N26b-01). This bead turns those results into the number the audit exists to produce: a **false-discard rate per
stratum with an exact binomial bound**. A *false discard* is a discarded design that the next stage shows to be a
top-k design: the screen threw away something the campaign wanted to keep.

The bound is exact arithmetic, not an approximation. With no top-k design among m audited discards, the
false-discard rate of the stratum is at most 1 − δ^(1/m) at confidence 1 − δ: 9.5% at m = 30 and 5.0% at m = 59
for δ = 0.05. The approximation ln(1/δ)/m (10.0% at m = 30) is **not used**: it is looser, and the plan's rule is
that a number which follows from a stated formula is computed from that formula. The reading of these numbers
matters as much as the numbers: thirty clean audited discards do not show the screen is sound; they only bound
the rate of discarded winners at 9.5%.

The bound is a statistical statement of class `Tested` at confidence 1 − δ. It is never `Certified`, a word
reserved for certificates that a checker accepted.

Tier Solid: an exact binomial bound.

## Scope

In: `audit::false_discard` (the predicate), `audit::bound` (the exact one-sided upper bound for any count of
misses), the stratum verdict, the stage-level stratified bound, the sample-size helper.
Out: the lane (N26b-01); the planted winner (N26b-03); the record, the campaign label and the bypass (N26b-04).

## Technical approach

1. **False discard.** For an audited discard d with next-stage score y_d at the fixed sample size `n_audit`, on
   the same world addresses as the survivors: d is a false discard when y_d is at least the k-th best next-stage
   score among the stage's comparison population (default: the survivors of the stage; k = `top_k` of the plan,
   the number of designs the campaign means to keep). A tie counts as a false discard (fail closed). Where a
   survivor's evaluation stopped before `n_audit` episodes, its score on the first `n_audit` paired addresses is
   used when it exists; otherwise its final score, and the comparison is flagged `unpaired`.
2. **The bound.** For a stratum with m audited discards of which k are false discards, the upper bound
   U(k, m, δ) on the stratum's false-discard rate p is the largest p not rejected at level δ:
   - k = 0: U = 1 − δ^(1/m).
   - 0 < k < m: U is the root in p of Σ_{i=0..k} C(m, i)·pⁱ·(1 − p)^(m−i) = δ (the Clopper–Pearson bound), found
     by bisection to an absolute width of 10⁻¹² and returned as the upper end.
   - k = m: U = 1.
   - Census: when the whole stratum was audited (m = n_s) the rate is known: U = k/n_s, flagged `census`.
   The result is rounded upward (the next representable number above the computed value), so a printed bound is
   never below the exact one. The audit samples without replacement from a finite stratum; the binomial bound is
   conservative for that case, because C(n − D, m)/C(n, m) ≤ (1 − D/n)^m for D false discards among n.
3. **Reference values (δ = 0.05).**

   | m | k | Audited rate k/m | Bound U |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 30 | 0 | 0 | 0.095034 (printed 9.5%) |
   | 59 | 0 | 0 | 0.049508 (printed 5.0%) |
   | 19 | 0 | 0 | 0.145869 |
   | 10 | 0 | 0 | 0.258866 |
   | 100 | 0 | 0 | 0.029513 |
   | 30 | 1 | 0.0333 | 0.148596 |
   | 30 | 2 | 0.0667 | 0.195326 |
   | 59 | 2 | 0.0339 | 0.102889 |
   | 100 | 5 | 0.0500 | 0.102253 |

   `audit::audits_needed(τ, δ, k)` returns the smallest m whose bound is at most τ: with no miss, ⌈ln δ/ln(1 − τ)⌉,
   which is 29 for 10%, 59 for 5%, 149 for 2% and 299 for 1%; with one miss, 46 for 10% and 93 for 5%.
4. **The stratum verdict (DESIGN reading of "an audited miss rate above the declared tolerance").** With the
   declared tolerance τ (default 0.05, the campaign's α; fixed in the plan before the stage runs):
   - `screen-biased`: the audited miss rate k/m exceeds τ. This is the plan's trigger, read on the measured rate.
   - `passed`: k/m ≤ τ and U ≤ τ.
   - `bound-above-tolerance`: k/m ≤ τ but U > τ. The stratum is not labelled biased; its claim is the bound,
     and the verdict carries `audits_needed`.
   - `void`: an audit episode of the stratum was cancelled or re-weighted, or the plan was not committed before
     the scores existed.
   Whatever the verdict, every false discard found is listed with its design, both scores and its rank at the
   next stage (`discarded_winners`); each is a finding, and each continues in the campaign as a survivor.
5. **The stage-level bound.** Over the S audited strata, with n_s discards in stratum s and N_d in all, the
   stage's overall false-discard rate is at most Σ_s (n_s/N_d)·U(k_s, m_s, δ/S) at confidence 1 − δ (each
   stratum at level δ/S, combined by the union bound). It is reported as `overall.rate_upper_bound` with its
   method named; it is conservative by construction and is not a substitute for the per-stratum rows.
6. **Output.** `StratumAudit { key, discards: n_s, audited: m, false_discards: k, rate: k/m, upper_bound: U,
   delta, tolerance, verdict, audits_needed, discarded_winners, flags }`. Counts are exact; the bound is a
   double rounded upward; class `Tested` at confidence 1 − δ.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `bound(0, 30, 0.05)` is 0.095034 and `bound(0, 59, 0.05)` is 0.049508 to six decimals, printed as 9.5% and
      5.0%; the function never returns the approximation 0.099858 for (0, 30).
- [ ] Every row of the reference table reproduces to six decimals; for k ≥ 1 the binomial tail at the returned
      bound is at most δ and at the bound minus 10⁻⁹ exceeds δ (checked in exact rational arithmetic).
- [ ] `audits_needed` returns 29, 59, 149, 299 (no miss) and 46, 93 (one miss) for the tolerances above.
- [ ] Verdicts at τ = 0.05: (k, m) = (0, 59) `passed`; (0, 30) `bound-above-tolerance` with `audits_needed` 59;
      (1, 30) `bound-above-tolerance` with one discarded winner listed; (2, 30) and (1, 19) `screen-biased`;
      (3, 60), exactly at the tolerance, is not `screen-biased`.
- [ ] A census stratum of 12 discards with 1 false discard reports the rate 1/12 as its bound, flagged `census`.
- [ ] The stage-level bound for strata (n, m, k) = (200, 30, 0), (300, 30, 0), (100, 19, 1), (400, 59, 0) is
      0.125782 (per-stratum bounds 0.135901, 0.135901, 0.291999, 0.071581 at level 0.0125).
- [ ] A tie with the k-th best score counts as a false discard.

- `rounded_upward`: the returned double is at least the exact value on 1,000 random (k, m).
- `monotone_in_k_and_m`: U rises with k and falls with m (property).

- `stage_bound_worked` (0.125782); `tie_is_false_discard`; `unpaired_flag`.
- `conservative_without_replacement`: for (n, D, m) = (100, 20, 19), exact hypergeometric coverage of the bound
  is 0.9912, above 0.95 (enumerated in the test).

### End-to-end script and logging

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-668 (no top-k design among 30 audited discards bounds the false-discard rate at 9.5% with 95% confidence, and
59 bound it at 5.0%) and its twin KA-706 on the cascade's side. CX-293 (thirty clean audited discards only bound
the rate at 9.5%; they do not validate the screen). Planted defects the tests must catch: a bound that reports
the audited rate k/m (on (100, 20, 19) it covers the planted 20% in 56% of draws; the coverage test of the
gate fails); the approximation ln(1/δ)/m; a two-sided interval's upper end at level δ (too small); a verdict that
calls (0, 30) `passed` at τ = 0.05. Determinism: counts exact; bounds identical across platforms to the last bit
(the bisection is on a fixed grid of iterations).

## Considerations

- The plan does not say whether "miss rate above the tolerance" reads the measured rate or the bound. Reading the
  bound would label every small stratum biased (30 clean audits give 9.5% against a 5% tolerance), which would
  make the label meaningless; reading the rate alone would call 30 clean audits a pass. The three-valued verdict
  keeps both facts visible. A different reading is a one-line change in `verdict` and a new policy version.
- A single discarded winner is a serious finding whatever the stratum verdict: the falsifier of the static-pruning
  bet (BET-05) is "the blinded audit of discarded strata finds a discarded design in the true top-k".
- "Top-k" at the next stage is itself measured with noise at fixed n; the predicate is a comparison of point
  scores on paired addresses, and the record keeps both scores so a reader can see the margin.
- Per-stratum bounds are not simultaneous; the stage-level bound is.
