## Background

Sampling the discarded strata (N26b-01, N26b-02) measures how often a screen discards a good design. It cannot
show that the screen *can* keep a good design of a kind it handles badly, because such designs may be absent from
the sample. The second half of the screening audit is therefore a potency control: **plant a winner.** A design
whose rank at the final stage is known from an earlier full evaluation, and which the screen represents poorly
(a gait outside the gait atlas, a body far from the policy family's training set), enters the population under a
blinded identity. It must survive every screen. A missed planted winner labels the campaign's results
`screen-biased` and bypasses the stage for the affected strata until it is re-qualified.

Two conditions make the control mean something. The winner's rank must be *on record from a full evaluation*: a
design somebody believes to be good is not a known winner, and a plant without a record is refused
(FROB-REF-566). And the plant must be *blinded*: neither the screen nor an agent steering the campaign may be
able to treat it specially.

The design corpus (about 200 designs with labels frozen before any analysis is tuned) holds planted winners for
this purpose. The co-design cascade applies the same control to its screening stage and seals the plant's
identity in the campaign manifest; Nemesis owns the mechanism.

Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: `audit::plant`: admission of a plant (the rank record, the poor-representation declaration), the sealed
commitment and the reveal, blind insertion into a population, tracking the plant through the stages, the outcome
record; refusal FROB-REF-566.
Out: the lane and the bound (N26b-01, N26b-02); the label and bypass mechanics (N26b-04); the verb (N26b-05); the
corpus and its frozen labels (BD-248); what the cascade does with a missed plant (BD-345).

## Technical approach

1. **Types (DESIGN sketch).**
   ```rust
   pub struct RankRecord {            // read from the ledger; never supplied by the caller as a bare number
       evaluation: EvaluationId, population: PopulationId, stage: StageId,   // must be the final stage
       rank: u32, of: u32,
       identities: EvalIdentities,    // task family and version, robot class, tier, kernel versions
       ledger_seq: u64,
   }
   pub struct Plant { design: DesignId, rank: RankRecord, represented_poorly: PoorlyRepresented, salt: [u8; 32] }
   pub enum PoorlyRepresented { GaitOutsideAtlas, BodyFarFromFamilyTrainingSet, RareMorphologyClass, Other(String) }
   pub struct PlantCommitment(Blake3);     // BLAKE3(salt ‖ design ‖ evaluation ‖ represented_poorly)
   pub enum PlantStatus { Survived, Missed { stage, stratum, score, cut }, NotRun { reason }, CommitmentMismatch }
   ```
2. **Admission of a plant (numbered).**
   1. Look up the rank record by design identity. It must exist, belong to a *full* evaluation (the final stage
      of a campaign: a specialist promoted on the referee), and carry a rank inside the top k the audited
      campaign means to keep. Otherwise refuse with **FROB-REF-566**: "a planted winner's rank is not on record
      from a full evaluation". Envelope: `pointer` the design; `observed` the best record found (none, or a
      record from an earlier stage, or a rank outside the top k); `required` "a final-stage rank within the top
      k"; `retry: after-change`. Ranked repairs: (1) run the full evaluation of the proposed design first, then
      plant it; (2) choose one of the design corpus's planted winners that has a record; (3) run the audit
      without a plant: the control is then reported `not-run` and the campaign's finalists are described as "k
      survivors". Exit code 3.
   2. The record's identities must match the audited campaign's task family, robot class and final tier. A
      record from another task or class is not a record for this campaign (same refusal, `observed` naming the
      mismatched identity).
   3. `represented_poorly` is required. A plant the screen represents well is not a potency control; where the
      screen offers a representation diagnostic (distance to the family policy's training set, atlas
      membership), the diagnostic value is stored and a plant inside the well-represented region raises the
      warning `plant-well-represented`.
3. **Sealing.** The commitment is written into the audit plan before the stage runs (and so into the campaign
   manifest hash). The salt and the design identity stay in a sealed field readable only with the campaign's
   administration scope until the campaign closes.
4. **Blind insertion.** The plant joins the population as an ordinary candidate: the same record shape as any
   other design, no corpus label, no provenance field that differs in kind from its neighbours', and a position
   set by the population's keyed order. Jobs that evaluate it are byte-identical in form to any other. The plant
   is not exempt from the keyed audit draw: if it is discarded and drawn, the lane finds it as a false discard as
   well.
5. **Tracking.** After every audited stage, `plant::survived(stage, decisions)` reads the sealed identity and
   records whether the plant advanced. "Must survive every screen" is evaluated per stage; the first stage that
   discards it yields `Missed { stage, stratum, score, cut }`, where the stratum is the one the plant fell into
   (reason, family, band).
6. **Reveal.** At the close of the campaign (or when the last screening stage has been audited) the salt and
   identity are revealed and checked against the commitment. A mismatch is `CommitmentMismatch` and voids the
   control. The outcome is written to the audit record and printed by the campaign report.
7. **Status semantics.** `Survived`: the plant passed every audited screen. `Missed`: a potency failure of the
   stage that discarded it. `NotRun`: no plant was available (no earlier full evaluation exists for the class,
   or the control was omitted); this is a result, it is carried by the campaign's results, and it never counts
   as a pass.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A plant with no rank record, with a record from a non-final stage, with a rank outside the top k, or with a
      record of another task or robot class is refused with FROB-REF-566, exit 3, with the three ranked repairs
      and `observed` naming what was found.
- [ ] A plant with a valid record and a declaration is admitted; its commitment is in the audit plan before the
      stage's first score.
- [ ] The population entry and the job specification of the plant are indistinguishable in form from other
      candidates' (a structural-equality test with the identity fields masked).
- [ ] On a stage that discards the plant the outcome is `Missed` with the stage, the stratum, the plant's score
      and the cut; on stages that advance it the outcome is `Survived`.
- [ ] A reveal that does not hash to the commitment gives `CommitmentMismatch`.
- [ ] An audit run without a plant reports `NotRun` with a reason; no code path reads `NotRun` as a pass.
- [ ] A missing `represented_poorly` is rejected as a schema error (the request fails its schema; exit 3).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-nemesis/tests/audit_plant.rs` (synthetic ledger rows and stage records, labelled `synthetic`):
- `refused_566_no_record`; `refused_566_stage_not_final`; `refused_566_rank_outside_top_k` (rank 7 with k = 3);
  `refused_566_identity_mismatch` (a record of another task family).
- `admitted_with_record` (rank 2 of 81 at the final stage, k = 3).
- `commitment_binds_identity`: changing the design, the evaluation or the salt changes the commitment.
- `blind_insertion_structural_equality`.
- `missed_reports_stage_and_stratum`: plant score 0.41 against a cut of 0.66 in family `hexapod-radial`, band
  `Middle` (d = 0.25/0.52 = 0.48 with y_min = 0.14).
- `survived_every_stage`; `reveal_mismatch`; `not_run_is_not_pass`.
- `plant_can_be_drawn_into_lane`: a discarded plant with a priority below its stratum's quota appears among the
  audited discards and is counted there as a false discard.

### End-to-end script and logging
Through `frob nemesis audit` in `scripts/e2e/nemesis_audit.sh` (N26b-07). Logged: the commitment hash, the
admission check with the rank record's evaluation identity, per stage whether the plant advanced (after the
reveal only), the reveal check, the status, and for a refusal the FROB-REF-566 envelope. The plant's identity
never appears in a log line before the reveal step.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-668: a planted winner removed by a deliberately biased screen is reported; control: a screen with no bias
passes. Planted defects the tests must catch: an admission that accepts a caller-supplied rank number with no
ledger record; a tracker that reports `Survived` when the plant was discarded at an early stage and re-entered
through the audit lane (survival is judged on the screen's own decision); a `NotRun` treated as a pass.
Determinism: commitments and statuses are exact.

## Considerations

- OPEN-ATEL-5 (owner: Arena lead): the planted winner for morphology classes that have no earlier specialist
  result. Default: the control is reported `not-run` and finalists are "k survivors"; it blocks the cascade's
  audit gate for those classes, not this mechanism.
- A design identity is a content hash, so "blinded" cannot mean a false identity; it means unlabelled and
  uncorrelated with position. An agent that has memorized the corpus could still recognize a plant; the corpus
  label file is therefore kept in a sealed field, and the campaign's agent-facing surface never lists it.
- One plant tests one weakness. A campaign may plant several (one per poorly represented kind); each has its own
  commitment and outcome.
- A plant that survives says the screen kept this design, not that it keeps every design like it; the sampled
  strata remain the measurement of rate.
