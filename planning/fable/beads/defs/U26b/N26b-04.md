## Background

The screening audit produces two kinds of evidence about a screening stage of a design campaign: a false-discard
rate per stratum of discarded designs, with an exact binomial bound (N26b-01, N26b-02), and the fate of a planted
winner (N26b-03). This bead is what happens next. The plan's rule: **a missed planted winner, or an audited miss
rate above the declared tolerance, labels the campaign's results `screen-biased` and bypasses the stage for the
affected strata until it is re-qualified.** And a second duty: the audit lane is the only calibration source for
the rank-agreement measurement between rungs, because it supplies designs that were not selected by the scores
being calibrated.

Three things must therefore exist as durable, queryable objects, not as log lines: the audit record itself (so a
report can print it and a reader can recompute it); the label and the bypass (so the cascade can refuse to trust
a stage, and so a later campaign inherits the distrust until the stage is re-qualified); and the calibration
rows. Nemesis writes them; the cascade and the rank-agreement ledger read them. The cascade's own gate
(GATE-ATEL-09) is the statement that it does not trust a rung whose audit is missing or failed: a stage with no
audit record, a missed planted winner or an audited miss rate above its tolerance has its rung set to
`uninformative` in the affected strata and its results labelled `screen-biased`.

Tier Solid: bookkeeping.

## Scope

In: the record type and canonical schema `frob.nemesis.audit/1`; the campaign label; the bypass list and its
query; the re-qualification rule and event; the calibration export; artifact-graph edges; what a report reads.
Out: computing the lane, the bounds and the plant outcome (N26b-01 to N26b-03); the verb (N26b-05); the mode
automaton of rungs and the re-opening of discarded designs (the cascade, BD-345 and BD-329); the report verb
(BD-302).

## Technical approach

1. **The record (canonical JSON; its identity `au:` is the hash of the canonical bytes).**
   ```json
   { "schema": "frob.nemesis.audit/1", "id": "au:7c1e…", "campaign": "cmp:…", "stage": 3,
     "screen": {"id": "scr:family-zero-shot", "version": 4},
     "plan": {"commitment": "b3:90aa…", "committed_before_scores": true, "stream": "nemesis-audit"},
     "share_of_spend": "1/10", "spend": {"stage_gpu_s": 9000, "audit_gpu_s": 900},
     "delta": "0.05", "tolerance": "0.05", "top_k": 3, "n_audit": 64,
     "strata": [ {"key": {"reason": "score-below-cut", "family": "hexapod-radial", "band": "near"},
                  "discards": 400, "audited": 36, "false_discards": 0, "rate": "0/36", "upper_bound": 0.0799,
                  "verdict": "bound-above-tolerance", "audits_needed": 59, "discarded_winners": []} ],
     "merged": [], "overall": {"rate_upper_bound": 0.1258, "method": "stratified; level delta/S per stratum"},
     "planted_winner": {"commitment": "b3:11f0…", "status": "missed", "stage": 3,
                        "stratum": {"reason": "score-below-cut", "family": "hexapod-radial", "band": "middle"},
                        "known_rank": "2 of 81", "rank_record": "ev:…", "score": 0.41, "cut": 0.66},
     "label": "screen-biased",
     "bypass": [ {"stage": 3, "screen": "scr:family-zero-shot@4", "reason": "missed-planted-winner",
                  "strata": [{"family": "hexapod-radial"}]} ],
     "calibration": {"rows": 90, "artifact": "b3:5d02…"},
     "evidence": {"class": "Tested", "confidence": "0.95", "origin": "campaign"},
     "policy": {"id": "nemesis.audit.verdict", "version": 1} }
   ```
   Rates are exact fractions as strings; bounds are doubles rounded upward. A record built from a synthetic
   stage carries `"origin": "synthetic"` and can label nothing outside its own test campaign.
2. **The label.** `label = "screen-biased"` when any of: the planted winner's status is `missed`; some stratum's
   verdict is `screen-biased` (audited miss rate above the tolerance). The label is attached, in the ledger, to
   the campaign and the stage; every result of the campaign that depends on the stage in an affected stratum
   carries it in its evidence block, and `report.campaign` prints the screening audit, the planted-winner
   outcome and the label. A campaign report without an audit record for a screening stage prints
   `screening audit: not run` for that stage; it never prints nothing.
3. **Affected strata.** For a `screen-biased` stratum verdict: that stratum (after any merge). For a missed
   plant: every stratum of the plant's design family at that stage (the screen has shown it cannot keep a
   winner of that family). For a `void` stratum: that stratum, with reason `audit-void`.
4. **Bypass.** `Bypass { stage, screen (identity and version), strata, reason, since: ledger_seq, lifted:
   Option<Requalification> }`. While a bypass is in force the stage neither selects nor discards in those strata:
   designs there skip the stage and its budget moves up one stage. `audit::bypass_in_force(screen, stratum) ->
   Option<Bypass>` is the query the cascade calls before trusting a stage; it also answers `Some` with reason
   `audit-missing` for a screen identity that has no audit record at all in the campaign. Bypasses persist
   across campaigns for the same screen identity and version: a later campaign starts with them in force.
5. **Re-qualification (DESIGN).** A bypass is lifted only by a recorded event
   `Requalification { audit: AuditId, ledger_seq }` naming a later audit of the same screen identity whose
   verdict is `passed` in every stratum the bypass names, and, where the bypass reason was a missed plant, whose
   planted winner belongs to the same design family and has status `survived`. A `bound-above-tolerance`
   verdict does not re-qualify. A new version of the screen does not inherit a qualification and does not lift
   a bypass by itself: it starts unaudited.
6. **Calibration export.** `audit::calibration_rows(record) -> Vec<CalRow>` with
   `CalRow { design, stratum, lower_score: Option<f64>, higher_score: f64, n, addresses, inclusion: Presample |
   StratumQuota }`, stored as an artifact and offered to the rank-agreement ledger. Rows with `inclusion:
   Presample` were drawn uniformly from the whole population before any score and are exchangeable with it;
   `StratumQuota` rows are uniform within their stratum only and carry the stratum's sampling fraction so a
   consumer can weight them. No row is ever built from a design that entered the next stage because the screen
   advanced it and that was not in the pre-sample.
7. **Graph and retention.** Artifact-graph edges: audit plan → lane → audited episodes → audit record → label
   and bypass events; the record resolves from any campaign result that carries the label. Audit records and
   bypass events follow the ledger's retention; a bypass in force pins its audit record.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The example record round-trips to identical canonical bytes; unknown fields are rejected.
- [ ] `label` is `screen-biased` exactly when a plant is `missed` or a stratum verdict is `screen-biased`; a
      record whose strata are all `passed` or `bound-above-tolerance` with a surviving plant has no label.
- [ ] After a missed plant in family F at stage 3, `bypass_in_force` returns the bypass for every stratum of F at
      stage 3 and `None` for other families.
- [ ] A screen identity with no audit record in the campaign yields a bypass with reason `audit-missing`.
- [ ] A later audit that passes in the named strata with a surviving plant of family F lifts the bypass by a
      recorded event; a later audit with `bound-above-tolerance` does not; a new screen version starts unaudited.
- [ ] A bypass persists into a second campaign that uses the same screen identity and version.
- [ ] Calibration rows exist for every lane member that finished, with the inclusion kind and, for quota rows,
      the sampling fraction; a survivor outside the pre-sample never appears.
- [ ] A synthetic record cannot create a bypass for a real screen identity.

- `bypass_persists_across_campaigns`.
- `calibration_rows_inclusion`: 90 lane members → 90 rows, 9 `Presample` survivors, 81 discards of which the
  pre-sampled ones are marked `Presample`; quota rows carry fractions 36/400, 27/300, 14/150, 4/50.
- `survivor_outside_presample_excluded`.
- `synthetic_origin_isolated`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/nemesis_audit.sh` (N26b-07) reads the record back with `frob artifact read`, asserts the label, the
bypass list and the calibration artifact, and logs the record identity, the label, each bypass with its strata
and reason, and each re-qualification event.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-668 (a planted winner removed by a deliberately biased screen is reported; a screen with no bias passes). The
cascade's gate reads this record: a stage with no audit record, a missed planted winner or a miss rate above
tolerance must lead to `uninformative` and `screen-biased`. Planted defects the tests must catch: a label
computed from the stage-level bound alone (a biased single stratum is averaged away); a bypass keyed by campaign
instead of by screen identity (it would not persist); calibration rows built from all survivors (the exclusion
test). Determinism: record bytes and identities are exact.

## Considerations

- The label is strong on purpose: it marks results, not the screen's code. Removing it requires a
  re-qualification on record, never an edit.
- Bypassing a stage costs compute (designs go up one stage unscreened); that cost is the measured price of a
  screen that failed its audit and is charged in the campaign's phase ledger.
- Atelier's refusals at the cascade boundary are distinct codes of its own block: selecting in a stratum whose
  mode is not `selective` (FROB-REF-545) and calibration from survivors or a cancelled audit episode
  (FROB-REF-546). This bead supplies the facts those refusals test.
- RISK-37 (a screen discards the winner; High): the earliest signals are exactly two fields of this record, a
  discarded design in the true top band and a planted winner missing from the finalists.
