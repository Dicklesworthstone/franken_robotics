## Background

GATE-NEMESIS-07 (claim: Known answer; earliest milestone M4; TARGET): **screening audit: a planted winner removed
by a biased screen is reported in 100 of 100 runs; the stratified bound covers the planted false-discard rate in
at least 95% of 1,000 synthetic campaigns.** The gate register notes that this and the cascade's gate
GATE-ATEL-09 are one mechanism, owned by Nemesis, with the exact bound 1 − δ^(1/m); the cascade's gate is the
separate statement that it does not trust a rung whose audit is missing or failed.

What is being protected: a screening stage of a design campaign can throw away the winner and nothing downstream
will notice, because a comparison among survivors is silent about what was discarded. The audit samples the
discarded strata by a keyed draw fixed before any score exists, runs the sample at the next stage at a fixed
size, bounds the false-discard rate per stratum exactly (no top-k design among 30 audited discards bounds it at
9.5% with 95% confidence; 59 bound it at 5.0%), and plants a winner of known rank that must survive every screen.
A missed planted winner, or an audited miss rate above the declared tolerance, labels the campaign's results
`screen-biased` and bypasses the stage for the affected strata until it is re-qualified.

This script measures the gate from the user's side: the real `frob` binary, the verb `nemesis.audit`, planted
fixtures whose truth is known by construction.

## Scope

In: the script, its assertions, its logs, the gate record with measured values, the self-test against the
registered mutants.
Out: the mechanism and the verb (N26b-01 to N26b-05); the fixtures (N26b-06); the cascade's application and its
gate (BD-345).

## Technical approach

1. **Surface exercised.** `frob nemesis audit prepare …`, `frob nemesis audit run … [--stage-record F --outcomes
   G] [--reveal R]`, `frob artifact read <au:…>`, and the fixture generator of the conformance crate
   (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --bin nemesis-audit-fixtures -- --seed S --kind biased|unbiased|coverage
   --out DIR`).
2. **Steps.**
   1. `start`: build identity, registry identity and hash of the planted fixtures, the seed lists, δ = 0.05,
      tolerance 0.05, hardware fingerprint.
   2. *Biased screen, 100 runs.* For each seed: generate the stage record and outcome table; run the audit
      offline with the reveal. Assert: exit 2; `planted_winner.status` is `missed` with the stage and a stratum
      of family F₄; `label` is `screen-biased`; `bypass` names every stratum of F₄ at that stage; the plant's
      `known_rank` is `2 of 81`. Metric `planted_winner_reported = k of 100` against `100 of 100`.
   3. *Unbiased screen, 100 runs.* Assert: `planted_winner.status` is `survived`; no label; no bypass with
      reason `missed-planted-winner`. Metric `unbiased_passes = k of 100` against `100 of 100`.
   4. *Coverage, 1,000 campaigns.* For each seed: generate, audit offline. For each of the four strata compare
      `upper_bound` with the planted rate D_s/n_s; compare `overall.rate_upper_bound` with Σ D_s / Σ n_s.
      Metrics: `stratum_rows_covered = a of 4,000` against `≥ 95%`; `campaigns_covered = b of 1,000` against
      `≥ 950`. Also assert, per stratum, that `false_discards` never exceeds the planted D_s and that every
      listed discarded winner is a planted one.
   5. *Known-answer rows.* On three hand-built stage records: 30 audited discards with no false discard →
      `upper_bound` 0.0950 (printed 9.5%), verdict `bound-above-tolerance`, `audits_needed` 59; 59 audited with
      none → 0.0495 (printed 5.0%), `passed`; 30 audited with two → rate 2/30, verdict `screen-biased`.
   6. *The commitment.* Take one stage record, move its plan commitment after the first score record, run:
      assert `status: not-run` with reason `scores-exist-before-commitment` and that no label or bypass was
      written. `prepare` on a live scratch campaign before its stage runs, then `run`: assert
      `committed_before_scores: true`.
   7. *The plant's record.* `prepare` with a plant that has no final-stage rank on record: assert `FROB-REF-566`
      (a planted winner's rank is not on record from a full evaluation), exit 3, three ranked repairs, `retry:
      after-change`. With a rank outside the top k: the same code, `observed` naming the rank.
   8. *Re-qualification.* After a biased-screen audit, run a passing audit of the same screen identity with a
      surviving F₄ plant and enough audited discards for `passed`: assert the bypass is lifted by a recorded
      event; repeat with a `bound-above-tolerance` audit: assert it is not.
   9. *Never cancelled.* On a live scratch campaign with a race enabled at the next stage, assert from the
      episode records that every lane member reached `n_audit` episodes and none carries a stop by the race.
   10. *Calibration rows.* Assert the artifact exists, has one row per finished lane member, and contains no
       survivor outside the pre-sample.
   11. Write the gate record; `summary`.
3. **Self-test (the gate can fail).** `scripts/e2e/tests/nemesis_audit_selftest.sh` runs the script against
   builds with each registered mutant and requires a non-zero exit with the named row failing:
   `nemesis-audit-mut-survivors-only` → step 2; `nemesis-audit-mut-point-estimate` → step 4 (expected stratum
   coverage about 55%); `nemesis-audit-mut-post-score-draw` → step 6; `nemesis-audit-mut-approximation` →
   step 5 (10.0% where 9.5% is expected).
4. **Logging.** The script sources the shared helper of the end-to-end harness and writes
   `target/e2e-logs/nemesis_audit/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript beside it. Every line: `ts`,
   `script`, `bead` (`N26b-07`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the
   exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
   identities, seed). Per audit: the fixture seed and kind, the plan commitment and whether it preceded the
   scores, per stratum n_s, audited m, false discards k, k/m, the upper bound with δ and τ, the verdict and the
   planted truth beside it; the plant's status and stratum; the label; each bypass; the audit record identity.
   On failure: the first seed and stratum whose bound lies below its planted rate (or the first run whose plant
   went unreported), with the paths of the stage record and outcome table. Final `summary`: assertions passed
   and failed; `gates: ["GATE-NEMESIS-07"]`. Fixtures are synthetic; no private-scene data can appear.
5. **Gate record.** `planted_winner_reported`, `unbiased_passes`, `stratum_rows_covered`, `campaigns_covered`,
   the expected coverage of the study from the fixture manifest (0.9952 per stratum row), the registry hash,
   commit, command, hardware fingerprint and seeds, as the row of GATE-NEMESIS-07 in `registries/gates.toml`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `scripts/e2e/nemesis_audit.sh` exits 0 on a correct build and non-zero on any failed assertion; it is
      idempotent and seed-explicit.
- [ ] The planted winner removed by the biased screen is reported in 100 of 100 runs (TARGET of the row); the
      unbiased screen passes in 100 of 100.
- [ ] The per-stratum bound covers the planted false-discard rate in at least 95% of the 4,000 stratum rows and
      the stage-level bound covers the planted overall rate in at least 950 of 1,000 campaigns (TARGET of the
      row); both measured shares are recorded.
- [ ] The three known-answer rows print 9.5%, 5.0% and the `screen-biased` verdict.
- [ ] FROB-REF-566 is produced for a plant without a full-evaluation rank on record.
- [ ] The self-test shows each of the four mutants failing its named step.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-07 is recorded with measured values, commit, fingerprint and seeds.
- [ ] The log has the per-audit fields above and a final summary line.

## Test plan

`scripts/e2e/tests/nemesis_audit_selftest.sh` (the four mutants, as above) and a shell-lint pass of the script.
The script's parsing helpers have a fixture test: a recorded envelope with a known record yields the expected
metric lines.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-668 (the screening audit: a planted winner removed by a deliberately biased screen is reported; no top-k
design among 30 audited discards bounds the false-discard rate at 9.5% with 95% confidence, and 59 bound it at
5.0%; control: a screen with no bias passes). KA-706 (the same bounds as the cascade states them). CX-293 (a
comparison among survivors validates the screen: it does not). The four registered mutants are the gate's
negative controls. Determinism lanes: the offline audits are exact; the script run twice on one machine, and
with 1 and 8 workers, produces identical metric lines and audit record identities; the live steps (6, 9) inherit
the determinism class of their tier and compare only counts and flags.

## Considerations

- 1,200 offline audits are arithmetic on small tables: seconds in total. The two live steps need a scratch
  campaign with a next stage; where the campaign machinery is absent the steps are recorded `not run` with the
  reason and the gate is not recorded as passed.
- The coverage threshold is met with a wide margin by construction of the study (expected 99.5% of stratum
  rows); a result near 95% is itself a sign of a defect and the script warns below 97%.
- The cascade's gate GATE-ATEL-09 (rung set to `uninformative`, results labelled, no audit episode cancelled, a
  planted unsound prune caught) is measured elsewhere on a seeded cascade population; this script does not
  claim it.
