## Background

Counterfactual debugging forks a failed simulated episode shortly before the failure, applies a typed
intervention `do(factor := value)` and records whether the failure still happens. Causes interact, so single
interventions are not enough: a robot that falls because the floor is greasy *and* a knee is torque-limited may
be rescued by removing either factor, or only by removing both. For a factor list N of size m, the **value of a
coalition** S ⊆ N is v(S), the rescue rate when all of S are applied together.

**ADR-AUTOPSY-05. Contrasts first; Shapley values are a summary on a budget.** The table of what each
intervention and each pair did is the result. A Shapley summary is computed only when that table does not already
answer the question. The reasons: a full coalition table over ten factors would take 2¹⁰ = 1,024 coalitions and
leave no budget for a single replicate, so the stability the method depends on could not be measured; and a
Shapley value can be negative and never establishes that a factor is necessary. The rejected alternative,
coalition-value attribution as the primary output, is recorded in the decision-core register as rejected and
kept as a budgeted description only.

**The budget (DESIGN).** At most 12 factors, all singles and all pairs. That is 12 + 66 = 78 coalitions; with 8
replicates each, 64 placebo forks and the null fork it comes to 78 × 8 + 64 + 1 = 689 forks, and the series at
relative 10⁻⁷ adds 8 forks for each coalition that rescues. With forks of two or three seconds this is a small
batch on any tier (689 forks × 2 s ≈ 1,400 world-seconds).

This bead builds the coalition layer: which coalitions run, the table of their values and tests, the pair
interactions, the inclusion-minimal rescuing coalitions that the labels `joint` and `alternative` and the repair
search read, and the rule that decides whether a Shapley summary is computed. It carries the gate for all of it.
Crate `frob-autopsy`. Tier: Solid. It serves decision core DC-06 (which cause, and which repair edit, ranks first
after a failure) and the bet that interventional autopsy is stable enough to act on.

## Technical approach

1. **Enumeration** `plan_coalitions(factors, mode)`:

   | Mode | Coalitions | Count for m factors |
   |---|---|---|
   | `singles` | every single factor | m |
   | `pairs` (default) | singles and all unordered pairs | m·(m + 1)/2 |
   | `all` | every non-empty subset; admitted only for m ≤ 5 | 2^m − 1 (31 at m = 5; 32 with ∅) |
   | `sampled` | singles, pairs and the prefixes of T sampled permutations | as scheduled by the summary's budget |

   Order is canonical: by size, then by the factors' canonical strings. With more than 12 factors the request is
   not silently trimmed: singles run for all, pairs run among the 12 factors with the largest single-factor
   contrasts (ties by canonical string), and the attribution carries `pairs: top-12-by-single-contrast`.
2. **Admissibility of a coalition.** The union of the members' changes is classified as one change set (its
   class is the maximum of its members'). A coalition is excluded, with a reason on the table, when it contains
   an intervention that was refused (a structural change at a mid-episode tick, FROB-REF-581), when two members
   set the same factor to different values, or when the union changes a mechanism that neither member declares.
3. **Fork count** (a derived value with a known-answer test):
   forks = 1 (null) + P (placebo) + R·(number of admissible coalitions) + R₇·(number of coalitions that rescue),
   with defaults P = 64, R = 8, R₇ = 8. Checks: m = 12, pairs: 1 + 64 + 8·78 = 689 before the 10⁻⁷ series. m = 10:
   55 coalitions, 505. m = 3: 6 coalitions, 113; with three rescuing coalitions, 137. The count is returned by the
   plan before anything runs, with its cost estimate.
4. **Values.** v(S) = rescued_S / R, an exact rational; v(∅) = ρ₀ = placebo flips / P. A coalition's contrast is
   v(S) − ρ₀. Replicate 0 of each coalition is its unperturbed fork and replicates 1 to R − 1 carry independent
   perturbations at relative 10⁻¹²; the flip rate at each size is read against replicate 0.
5. **Test of a coalition.** The same exact one-sided test used for single factors (Fisher's exact test of
   rescued_S of R against placebo flips of P), at level α divided by the number of coalitions of that size that
   ran: singles at α/m, pairs at α/(number of admissible pairs). DESIGN: sizes are separate families, each
   controlled at α; the attribution prints both levels. A coalition *rescues* under the criterion used for
   singles: contrast at least 0.5, tested above the placebo, flip rate at most 0.1 at 10⁻¹².
6. **Pair interaction.** For each pair,
   I(i, j) = v({i, j}) − v({i}) − v({j}) + v(∅),
   exact, printed to two decimals. Reading: +1 when the factors rescue only together (0, 0, 1 with ρ₀ = 0); −1
   when each rescues alone (1, 1, 1); −1 also when one rescues alone and adding the other cancels the rescue (1,
   0, 0), which is why the interaction is printed beside the three rates and never alone.
7. **Minimal rescuing coalitions.** `minimal_rescuing(table)` returns the rescuing coalitions that contain no
   rescuing proper subset. These are what the labels read: one rescuing single is `but-for`; several rescuing
   singles are `alternative`; no rescuing single and a rescuing pair makes that pair's members `joint`.
8. **The Shapley trigger.** `--shapley auto` (default): the summary is computed only when no single factor and no
   pair rescues. Then, if m ≤ 5, the remaining coalitions up to all 2^m are scheduled and the exact summary is
   computed; otherwise the sampled summary is scheduled inside `--shapley-budget` forks (DESIGN default 512).
   `--shapley never` suppresses it. `--shapley force` computes it on request, with the added line "the coalition
   table already answers the question" whenever a single or a pair rescues. No label is derived from it.
9. **Types and wire form.**

   ```rust
   pub struct CoalitionTable { factors: Vec<FactorPath>, placebo: Count, mode: CoalitionMode,
                               entries: BTreeMap<Coalition, CoalitionEntry>, levels: Levels }
   pub struct CoalitionEntry { rescued: Count, flips_1e12: Count, series_1e7: Option<SeriesCounts>,
                               p_value: BigRatio, tested: bool, rescues: bool, class: ForkClass,
                               excluded: Option<Exclusion> }
   ```
   In the attribution: `"pairs": [ {"do": ["film := dry", "torque_limit := off"], "rescued": "8/8",
   "interaction": -0.34} ]` and, when computed, `"shapley": { "method": "exact", "values": […], "sum": "…",
   "note": "…" }`.
10. **Gate measurement.** On the 100 planted cases with ten candidate factors each (55 coalitions, 505 forks per
    case before the second series) the attribution's outcome label and its minimal rescuing coalitions are
    compared with the registry's label and repair sets. On the three host tables the exact values, the labels and
    the identities are checked. Record: correct of 100 by kind, the misses with their tables, identities checked
    and failed, the negative-value case, commit, fingerprint, seeds.

Module: `crates/frob-autopsy/src/coalition.rs`. Determinism: enumeration and arithmetic are class D0; children
have the class of their tier (D2 required).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition, GATE-AUTOPSY-04 (TARGET), recorded with measured values: planted joint and
      alternative causes are labelled correctly in at least 95 of 100; the Shapley identities (efficiency, dummy,
      symmetry) hold exactly on every table computed; the negative-value case is reproduced: (½, −½), sum 0.
- [ ] The fork-count function returns 689, 505, 113 and 137 for the cases above, and the plan's count equals the
      number of forks actually run in every test.
- [ ] A coalition containing a refused intervention is excluded with its reason and the rest of the table is
      still produced.
- [ ] With `--shapley auto` no summary appears whenever a single or a pair rescues; one appears on a planted
      three-factor case in which only the triple rescues.
- [ ] Pair interactions equal the stated formula exactly on integer counts, including a non-zero v(∅).
- [ ] No label of an attribution changes when the Shapley summary is switched on or off.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob autopsy intervene … --coalitions pairs --json` and `frob autopsy attribute … --json`
      with the `pairs` block validating against `frob.autopsy.attribution/1`; cancellation of the coalition batch
      is drained and leaves no child slot occupied; `CONTRACT.md` documents the budget and the trigger.

## Considerations

- KILL-AUTOPSY (decided at M3): if planted single-cause accuracy is below 90% after one repair cycle, or the top
  factor changes in more than 30% of cases at 10⁻⁷, the fallback is single-factor paired re-runs from the episode
  start with the first-anomaly ordering labelled `Estimated`, and coalitions and Shapley summaries are removed.
  This bead is on the branch that the kill removes.
- OPEN-AUTOPSY-2: whether one-at-a-time contrasts suffice, which would make pairs optional (owner: Autopsy lead;
  decided by the planted single-cause gate, which records the agreement between ten one-at-a-time forks and the
  full coalition table). Default until decided: singles and pairs.
- A rescue at a fork is not a minimal repair in any stronger sense than the table shows, and it is not evidence
  that an edited design passes the task; that takes a re-run from the episode start and then a study.
- Cost on the CPU tier: one case of the gate is about 505 forks of 2 s; the 100 cases are about 10⁵
  world-seconds: about two minutes at 1,000 world-seconds per second and about 17 minutes at 100.
