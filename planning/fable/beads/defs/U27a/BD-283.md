## Background

Counterfactual debugging tests a proposed cause by restoring a failed episode to a moment before the failure,
changing one thing and running forward. For a change to the world or to what the robot senses, that is the right
experiment. For a change to the robot itself it is not. A different gear ratio applied one second before a
thermal trip acts on a motor that was heated by the old ratio's currents. Winding temperature, battery state of
charge, wear, estimator bias, a policy's hidden state and the contact memory all carry the history of the old
design, and none has a canonical image under the edit.

**ADR-AUTOPSY-03. Design-parameter interventions re-run from the episode start.** History-dependent state would
otherwise answer a different question. The rejected alternative is to fork anyway and trust the result. The
counterexample that rejects it is hand-checkable: one thermal node with time constant 60 s, ambient 25 °C, a trip
at 115 °C, a steady-state rise of 192 K at gear ratio 12 and 108 K at 16, and a 45 s task. At ratio 12 the motor
trips at 60·ln(192/102) = 37.95 s. Ratio 16 from the episode start peaks at 82.0 °C and passes with 33 K in
hand. Ratio 16 applied at 36.95 s finds the winding at 113.3 °C and still heading for 133 °C, and trips at
42.4 s. The fork is not wrong about the experiment it ran; it ran the wrong experiment.

DESIGN: an intervention classified as structural or design-parameter is never applied to a mid-episode state
(INV-AUTOPSY-03: no structural or design-parameter intervention starts from a mid-episode state, except in the
labelled diagnostic mode). The verb `autopsy.rerun` does the right experiment instead. It is also the tool for
one label of the attribution table: `precedes-snapshot` (no intervention at the fork tick rescues, and the same
change applied from the episode start does), and it is what remains of the subsystem if its kill gate fires
(single-factor paired re-runs from the episode start).

Crate `frob-autopsy`, layer 5. Tier: Solid. Serves decision core DC-06 and scenario NS-16, in which the
gear-ratio intervention is re-run from the episode start because winding temperature depends on the whole history.

## Technical approach

**Surface.** `autopsy.rerun`, kind `run`, first milestone M3, scope `autopsy:run`.

```text
frob autopsy rerun <run> ( --edit '<typed design edit>' | --do '<factor> := <value>' )
                   [--worlds failing[,sample:<n>] | --worlds <list>] [--controller keep|resynth|mpc|family:<id>]
                   [--placebo on|off] [--replicates <r>] [--tier <tier>] --key <idempotency key> --json
```

`--key` is required, as for every `run` verb (a call without it is FROB-REF-805); two re-runs a script compares
carry distinct keys.

**Steps.**

1. **Apply the edit.** A typed design edit is applied to the run's design with the expected parent, giving a new
   `DesignId` and `ModelId` and a reset cache of its own: the settled states of the edited model are computed (or
   found by content hash), 64 quasi-static poses settled in double precision until base and joint speeds stay
   below 10⁻³ in SI units for 0.2 s with penetration at most 0.5 mm. A state is never transported from one
   design to another; the initial equilibrium is recomputed for the edited design. With `--do`, a world or
   diagnostic intervention is applied from tick 0 instead and the model is unchanged.
2. **Choose the controller** by the eligibility rule: keep it when the model is unchanged; re-synthesize the
   baseline for the edited model; a family policy if the edited design is in its class; the sampling controller;
   a specialist policy trained for the original body alone is refused for design factors (FROB-REF-587). If the
   controller kind changes, the original design is re-run under the new kind too and the failing set is
   re-established.
3. **Re-run the failing worlds from the start on the same world addresses.** Scene, disturbances and noise are
   paired because every draw is a counter-based stream keyed by address and by the physical entity it perturbs
   (a gust by time, floor friction by tile, a sensor's noise by the hash of its port name); events that depend
   on the robot's actions keep their own logically keyed streams, so an action-dependent event in one arm does
   not shift any draw in the other. The pose index of the reset is drawn by address, so both designs start from
   the same spawn specification, each in its own settled state. Both arms run on the same tier and step profile;
   if the recorded run is on that tier its episodes are reused, otherwise the original design is re-run there.
4. **Re-run a sample of worlds that passed**, because a repair can break what worked. `sample:<n>` takes the n
   passing worlds with the smallest address hashes under a stream identifier of its own (default n = 64), a pure
   function of the run manifest. That stream is registered by this bead as a row appended to
   `registries/streams.toml` (N35-23: later rows are appended by their owners; name `autopsy-rerun-sample`,
   domain `engine`, entity the hash of the run manifest, tick the world index, identifier the next free one in
   the lock file), so the selection is reproducible by name and cannot collide with another subsystem's draws.
   When fewer than n worlds of the recorded run passed, all of them are taken: nothing is refused and no world
   is drawn twice. The report states both numbers, `worlds.sampled_passing` (the count taken, min(n, passing))
   and `worlds.passing_available` (the count that passed in the recorded run), and carries the flag
   `sample-short` when the first is below n, so that "63 of 64 still pass" is never read from a sample of five.
   With no passing world the step is skipped, `sampled_passing` is 0 and the `pass_to_*` counts are 0 of 0.
   (The default n = 64 meets this whenever a failure is common, which is the usual case for a debugged run.)
5. **Placebo re-run** (default on). The unedited design is re-run on the failing worlds with one coordinate of
   its settled initial state perturbed at relative 10⁻¹² by the keyed generator the fork placebo uses (N27b-03:
   coordinate, sign and a size factor on [1, 2) drawn by address). The fraction that no longer fails
   is the placebo rate of the re-run, ρ₀. A whole episode is a long horizon, so this rate can be far from zero;
   reporting it is what keeps a re-run's rescue count from being read as a cause on its own (ADR-AUTOPSY-01: a
   cause is what an intervention removes, net of a placebo).
6. **Report the paired outcomes, labelled `Estimated`.**

   ```json
   { "schema": "frob.autopsy.rerun/1", "run": "r:19e2…", "edit": "regear *.knee 16",
     "design": {"from": "d:41aa…", "to": "d:8e77…"}, "model": {"from": "m:9f2c…", "to": "m:c3d0…"},
     "reset_cache": {"key": "b3:…", "settled": 64},
     "controller": {"arm": "resynthesized-per-design", "from": "c:5e02…", "to": "c:77e1…"},
     "worlds": {"failing": 23, "sampled_passing": 64, "passing_available": 4073},
     "paired": {"fail_to_pass": 17, "fail_to_fail": 6, "pass_to_pass": 63, "pass_to_fail": 1},
     "new_failures": [{"world": 4410, "class": "timeout"}],
     "placebo": {"n": 23, "flips": 1, "rate": 0.043},
     "contrast": 0.70, "test": {"kind": "fixed", "p": "…", "level": "…", "tested": true},
     "label": "contributing",
     "evidence": {"class": "Estimated", "scope": "cause in the model at this tier, for the listed edit"},
     "next_actions": [ {"command": "frob eval compare quad-a quad-a@regear --task kitchen-tray",
                        "why": "A verdict on the two designs comes from a study, not from the autopsy."} ] }
   ```
   (The numbers are illustrative; the shape is fixed.) Rules for the label, the same table the fork contrasts
   use: `chaotic: unattributable` when ρ₀ is above 0.2; otherwise the edit is tested above the placebo by the
   exact one-sided test of fail-to-pass of n against placebo flips of n; `contributing` when tested; `none`
   otherwise. `but-for` additionally needs a flip rate of at most 0.1 at 10⁻¹² among replicates of the edited
   design, which exist only with `--replicates r` (r perturbed re-runs of the edited design per failing world);
   without replicates the strongest label a re-run can carry is `contributing`. With `--placebo off` the counts
   are reported and the label is absent.
7. **The record for the label table.** `RerunOutcome { change, rescued: (k, n), placebo: (f, n), tested,
   broke: (j, s) }` is stored against the fork handle when the re-run was started from an attribution, so that
   an attribution in which nothing rescues at the fork tick and the same change rescues from the start can be
   labelled `precedes-snapshot`.
8. **Hand-off.** The pair of designs goes to `eval.compare` for a verdict under one of the two study types (an
   anytime-valid e-process, or a fixed-n exact study with its pre-analysis), with the controller arm declared.
   The re-run prints the command as its first next action and never prints a verdict on the task itself.

**Refusals.**

| Code | Trigger | Ranked repairs | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| FROB-REF-587 | A design-parameter re-run with a policy that cannot act on the edited body without retraining | `--controller resynth`; a family policy whose class contains both designs; `--controller mpc`; retrain or inherit, then `eval.compare` (the order of the registry row, N27-REF) | 3 |
| FROB-REF-562 | The recorded run is below determinism class D2 on the tier the arms would run on, so the arms cannot be paired exactly | `--tier batch-cpu` (both arms re-run there from the start; the re-run's form of the row's first repair, teleport to `batch-cpu`); re-record the episode under `device-replay` on a family with a current batch-independence record; `frob spark check --sweeps all` to renew the record (N35-REF's order) | 3 |
| FROB-REF-840 | The run is a sealed confirmation run | None from a discovery session | 3 |
| FROB-REF-529 | The edited model has no complete settled-pose cache for the task: fewer than 64 of its spawn draws came to rest in step 1, so no paired reset exists (the reset cache's refusal, raised by every verb that starts episodes) | Fix the spawn draws the settle finding names (it lists the failed draws and their reasons); then re-run, which settles the edited model again | 3 |
| `design.edit`'s chapter codes | The typed edit of step 1 fails its precondition table: FROB-REF-101, -102, -103, -105, -106, -107, -108, -111, -114, -121, -123, -125, -126 (the row of `design.edit` in VERBS-design, entered in this verb's row explicitly) | The repairs of each code's registry row | 3 |

A world that loads a settled state and fails the tier's own settle check on its first step carries the mark
`Degraded: unsettled` and is counted per arm (the batched reset cache, BD-195); it is never dropped, and a pair
with either world so marked is classified `unscored` (as N24a-16 states) and reported beside the paired counts,
not inside them. A mid-episode request for the same edit
made to the fork verb is FROB-REF-581, and its first repair is this verb.

**Gate measurement (GATE-AUTOPSY-06)** on `fixture:thermal-regear`: design A (ratio 12) fails at 37.95 s; the
checkpoint at 36.95 s holds 113.3 °C; the mid-episode request `gear_ratio := 16` is refused; the diagnostic
forced fork trips at 42.4 s; `autopsy.rerun --edit 'regear joint[0] 16'` passes with a peak of 82.0 °C (the
spelling of N27a-17's script, whose fixture joint is `joint[0]`).

Module `crates/frob-autopsy/src/rerun.rs`. Determinism: each arm has the class of its tier (D2 required);
selection, pairing and statistics are class D0. Evidence: the report is class `Estimated`, reality `no-claim`,
with the three no-authorization constants false.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition, GATE-AUTOPSY-06 (TARGET), recorded with measured values: the thermal counterexample's
      four numbers within 0.1 s and 0.1 K (37.95 s, 113.3 °C, 42.4 s, 82.0 °C); the mid-episode request is
      refused (FROB-REF-581, earliest valid fork tick 0); the re-run passes.
- [ ] Pairing: for every failing world the two arms read identical exogenous draws (digests of the scene,
      disturbance and noise streams equal), while the reset states differ and each is a settled state of its
      own design.
- [ ] The edited design starts from its own reset cache: no state field of the original design's episodes is
      read by the edited arm (store instrumentation), which is INV-AUTOPSY-03 for this verb.
- [ ] The sample of passing worlds is a function of the manifest: identical across slot orders and shard counts.
- [ ] `sample:<n>` with fewer than n passing worlds takes every passing world, reports `sampled_passing` equal
      to `passing_available` with the flag `sample-short`, and its `pass_to_pass` and `pass_to_fail` counts sum
      to `sampled_passing`; with no passing world the counts are 0 of 0 and the verb still completes.
- [ ] A specialist policy with a design edit is refused with FROB-REF-587 and the ranked repairs.
- [ ] The report always contains the four paired counts, the placebo block (unless switched off), the label rule
      applied, class `Estimated`, and `eval.compare` as the first next action; it never contains a task verdict.
- [ ] `--do` with a world intervention from tick 0 returns a `RerunOutcome`, and an attribution in which nothing
      rescued at the fork tick becomes `precedes-snapshot` when that outcome rescues.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob --json` and the agent surface with a documented schema and exit codes; one
      idempotency key yields one job and one charge; cancellation drains and leaves no device resource held;
      `CONTRACT.md` documents the verb.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-autopsy/tests/rerun.rs`:
- `thermal_rerun_passes`: on the fixture, the edited arm's peak is 82.0 °C ± 0.1 K at 45 s; verdict satisfied.
- `thermal_original_fails_at_37_95`; `thermal_mid_episode_refused_581`.
- `paired_draws_equal`: on a quadruped run with pushes and sensor noise, the two arms' draw digests per stream
  are equal on 23 failing worlds.
- `action_dependent_events_do_not_shift_draws`: a slip-triggered event in one arm only; the push at 8.0 s is
  identical in both.
- `edited_arm_reads_no_parent_state`.
- `own_reset_cache`: the edited model's cache key differs; both arms' first contact impulse is within 5% of the
  static support force.
- `passing_sample_is_address_pure` (property): the same 64 worlds under 100 slot orders and shard counts; the
  stream used is the registered `autopsy-rerun-sample` row and the registry loader accepts the appended row.
- `passing_sample_short`: a recorded run with 60 failing and 4 passing worlds and `--worlds failing,sample:64`:
  the 4 are taken, `sampled_passing` = `passing_available` = 4, flag `sample-short`, pass counts sum to 4; the
  same run with every world failing: `sampled_passing` = 0, counts 0 of 0, no flag of failure.
- `paired_table_counts`: 23 failing and 64 passing: 17 + 6 = 23 and 63 + 1 = 64; contrast = 17/23 − 1/23 = 16/23.
- `label_rules`: placebo 6 of 23 → `chaotic: unattributable`; 17 of 23 against 1 of 23 → tested, `contributing`;
  with 8 replicates and no flips → `but-for`; `--placebo off` → no label.
- `rerun_outcome_feeds_precedes_snapshot`.
- `sealed_run_refused_840`; `below_d2_refused_562`; `specialist_refused_587`; `missing_key_refused_805`.
- `unsettled_edit_refused_529`: an edit that lowers the stance height until 3 of the 64 spawn draws strike the
  floor → FROB-REF-529, exit 3, the three failed draws and their reasons in `observed`, no episode stepped.
- `design_edit_refusal_passed_through`: on a `synthetic` design whose knee gear ratio is a lever bounded to
  [6, 30], `--edit 'regear *.knee 400'` → FROB-REF-111 with `design.edit`'s pointer and repairs, exit 3, no
  settle attempted.
- `no_task_verdict_in_report`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-675 (the thermal re-run: 37.95 s; 113.3 °C at the fork; a trip at 42.4 s in the forced fork; 82.0 °C peak
from the start; tolerance 0.1 s and 0.1 K; control: a tool that reports the forced fork's result as the answer to
the design question). CX-298, CX-071. The exact one-sided test on the illustrative counts: 17 of 23 against 1 of
23. Planted defects the tests must catch: an implementation that restores the 36.95 s checkpoint under the
edited model and continues (it reports a trip at 42.4 s and fails `thermal_rerun_passes`); one that reuses the
original design's settled pose (the first-impulse check fails on a mass edit); one that pairs by seed with a
sequential stream (the push differs between arms when the edited design draws one more value). Metamorphic
relation: re-running with an edit and then with its inverse returns the original `DesignId` and the original
outcomes bit for bit. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; each tier against itself.

## Considerations

- A re-run on the failing worlds is not a verdict on the task: the failing worlds are a selected sample, and
  regression to the mean alone rescues some of them. The placebo re-run measures that floor on the unedited
  design, and the sampled passing worlds show the other direction. A study decides.
- KILL-AUTOPSY's fallback is this verb used alone: single-factor paired re-runs from the episode start, with
  the first-anomaly ordering labelled `Estimated`. Nothing in this bead depends on coalitions.
- Cost: one re-run is a full episode per world and arm; 23 failing and 64 passing worlds of 20 s with a placebo
  arm are about 2,200 world-seconds, against a fork's 2 s per child. The estimate is printed before admission.
- A forced fork of a design parameter remains available in the labelled diagnostic mode because it is
  occasionally the question someone means to ask; its result never substitutes for this verb's.
- The same edit may change the reset pose enough that a world's early contacts differ; that is part of the
  effect of the edit and is why pairing is by address and physical entity, not by trajectory.
