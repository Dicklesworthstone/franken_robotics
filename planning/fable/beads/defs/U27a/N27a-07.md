## Background

The fork classifier decides, before any state is restored, which of five classes a requested change belongs to
and the earliest tick at which it may be applied. A classifier that is wrong in the permissive direction lets a
run proceed from a state the change has invalidated, and the result looks like any other run. The protection is a
fixed catalogue of (intervention, state) pairs with the right answer written down beforehand, and two refusals
that must come back with the right fields.

Two gates read this catalogue, one from each chapter that shares the mechanism. The gate register records that
they are one set of five classes, defined once and implemented once, with one refusal code for a warm-start graft.

| Gate | Claim | Threshold (TARGET) | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| GATE-AUTOPSY-02 | Known answer | 40 catalogued pairs of intervention and state are classified correctly; the warm-start graft and a mid-episode gear change are refused with the right fields | M2 |
| GATE-CHRON-05 | Known answer | The five classes are assigned correctly on 100 planted change sets; the warm-start graft is refused 100 of 100; the empty intervention equals replay on 1,000 worlds | M2 |

The plan fixes the number 40 and the two named refusals; it does not list the pairs. The list below is this
bead's DESIGN, eight pairs per class, chosen so that every row of the classifier's rule table and every liveness
case is hit at least once, and one more row (E9) for the classifier's `actuator-limit` kind. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the catalogue as a registry file with recorded states; the generator of the 100 planted change sets; the two
named refusals; the gate tests and records, including the entry that measures GATE-CHRON-05's third clause
("the empty intervention equals replay on 1,000 worlds") on N27a-06's population; the planted classifier
defects; the `controls` and `tier` fields of both gates' rows (N40-02: Autopsy and Chronicle have no register
bead of their own, so the measuring bead fills them).
Out: the classifier (N27a-02); the population of failing worlds, its committed manifest and GATE-AUTOPSY-01
(N27a-06); the end-to-end script (N27a-08).

## Technical approach

**States.** Each pair names a recorded episode and a tick. Two recorded episodes serve all 41: (Q) the reference
quadruped walking across a floor with a low-friction patch, a mat and a door, under a recurrent policy, with an
IMU, encoders and a depth camera, failing by a slip-and-fall; (C) the loaded cart of the sill fixture
(`fixture:cart-sill-dock`, 60 mm front wheels) at the tick its front wheels are on the lip of the 12 mm sill.
Both are recorded on the CPU tier and their episode files are pinned by hash in the registry.

The forcing fields in the last column are the members each list must contain. The exact expected list of every
row is stored in the registry file and is the classifier's fixed order (N27a-02: payload, actuator thermal,
actuator electrical, battery, wear, controller, policy history, filter, packet queues, contact memory,
proprioception history, sensor histories, settled pose) restricted to the fields that are live in the pinned
checkpoint's manifest; `catalogue_rows` compares it as an ordered list, so a row whose list differs only in order
fails.

Row count. The rows C1 to S8 are the forty pairs of the gate, eight per class. Row E9 was added with the
classifier's `actuator-limit` kind, under the rule in Considerations that a new changeable field brings a
catalogue row in the same change; the catalogue therefore holds 41 rows, and the gate passes only when all 41
are correct (the register's threshold of 40 is a floor, not a sample).

**The two named refusals.**

- *Mid-episode gear change* (row S1 without classify-only): FROB-REF-581 with `earliest_valid_fork_tick: 0` and
  forcing fields that include the actuator thermal state, the policy history and the contact memory.
- *Warm-start graft* (case G1): on episode C at the lip, a request to run the 75 mm design from the 60 mm run's
  checkpoint, keeping its contact warm start (`--change 'model.wheel[front].radius := 75 mm' --reuse
  checkpoint.contact.memory` at the lip tick). Expected: FROB-REF-582, a structured refusal and not a run,
  naming the contact memory as the field that forces a restart. Its control (KA-676): the same edit at tick 0
  without `--reuse` is classified `structural` and accepted, so the refusal is specific to the graft.

**The 100 planted change sets for GATE-CHRON-05.** Generated from the catalogue by a seeded generator: 20 per
class, each a catalogue row with its numeric value redrawn inside the field's legal range and, for 40 of the 100,
combined with a second change of an equal or lower class, so that the set rule (the class of a set is the maximum
of its members) is exercised. The expected class of each set is computed from the catalogue rows by the set rule,
not by running the classifier. The graft is requested 100 times with redrawn wheel radii between 65 and 90 mm and
fork ticks across the lip crossing; each must be FROB-REF-582.

**Planted classifier defects** (registered as negative controls; each must make the gate test fail): design
changes mapped to exogenous; liveness ignored (every surface change exogenous); the set rule taking the minimum;
the forcing-field list returned empty; the graft allowed when the radii differ by less than 10%. Each is a row
of `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` of kind `mutation`, naming both gates, and a
`cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch at the classifier's site in `frob-chronicle` (the registry's mutation
convention); none is a private flag of this bead.

**The third clause of GATE-CHRON-05** (DESIGN: measured here, not cited, because the recorder enters a part only
from the lines of the receipts it is given, N38b-19, and N27a-06's run writes lines for GATE-AUTOPSY-01 only). An
entry `GATE-CHRON-05` of the tier target `g5_determinism` drives N27a-06's committed population manifest on
`batch-cpu` in double precision: for each of the 1,000 failing worlds a null fork (empty change set, shared
streams) one second before the failure, run to the end of the parent's record, its chained digest compared with
the recorded one at every tick. Clause criterion: 1,000 of 1,000 bitwise. Its negative control is a sixth row of
`controls.toml` of kind `mutation`, a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch at the indexed copy of `frob-chronicle`
that steps one tick past the checkpoint before copying; it must take the count below 1,000.

**Running and recording the gates.** The catalogue clauses of both gates are entries of the tier target
`g1_known_answers` that call the functions of `fork_classification.rs`. GATE-AUTOPSY-02 is run as
`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations --
GATE-AUTOPSY-02`, which catches the five controls in the same run, and its row is entered from that run's
receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUTOPSY-02 --receipt <hash>`.
GATE-CHRON-05 sits in two tiers and is measured by one run of each, from one commit: the same `g1_known_answers`
command with `-- GATE-CHRON-05`, and `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g5_determinism
--features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-CHRON-05`; its row is entered with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
record_gate -- --gate GATE-CHRON-05 --receipt <g1 hash> --receipt <g5 hash>` (N38b-19's receipt set, tier
order). Both rows are entered by a session that did not implement the work.

**The chapter fields of the two rows** (N40-02, rule 8). This bead fills, in the same change that registers its
controls: GATE-AUTOPSY-02 `tier = ["G1:host"]` and `controls` the identifiers of the five classifier controls;
GATE-CHRON-05 `tier = ["G1:host", "G5:batch-cpu"]` and `controls` those five and the sixth. An empty `controls`
list forbids a pass, so neither row can be recorded before these fields are filled.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-AUTOPSY-02 recorded PASS with measured values: 41 of 41 rows classified with the expected class,
      earliest valid tick, forcing fields and labels; the warm-start graft refused with FROB-REF-582 naming the
      contact memory; the mid-episode gear change refused with FROB-REF-581, tick 0 and the three named fields.
- [ ] GATE-CHRON-05 recorded PASS with measured values from its two receipts: 100 of 100 planted change sets
      assigned the expected class; the graft refused 100 of 100; the empty intervention bitwise equal to the
      recorded episode on 1,000 of 1,000 worlds of N27a-06's population on `batch-cpu`; the sixth control takes
      that count below 1,000.
- [ ] `registries/gates.toml` carries the `tier` and `controls` fields stated above for both rows, and every
      identifier in `controls` resolves in `controls.toml`.
- [ ] The registry file validates against its schema; each episode file's hash matches; a change to a recorded
      episode changes the catalogue identity.
- [ ] Each of the five planted classifier defects makes at least one catalogue assertion fail.
- [ ] No catalogue case restores state when run with classify-only (store read counter zero).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/fork_classification.rs`:
- `catalogue_rows`: one assertion per row (41) on class, earliest valid tick, forcing fields (as an ordered list),
  labels and refusal code; the test prints a table of expected against observed.
- `gear_change_refused_with_fields`: `earliest_valid_fork_tick == 0`; `forced_by ⊇ {actuator thermal state,
  policy history, contact memory}`.
- `graft_refused_582_names_contact_memory`; `graft_control_tick_zero_accepted`.
- `planted_sets_100`: seed 1; expected classes from the set rule; all 100 equal.
- `graft_100_of_100`.
- `defect_*` (five tests): the classifier is built with one planted defect (`--features
  gauntlet-mutations,frob-chronicle/nc-<nnnn>`, the control's own mutation feature) and the catalogue must
  fail, at the row the control's `expect` field names.
- `catalogue_schema` and `episode_hashes`.
- The `g5_determinism` entry `GATE-CHRON-05`: 1,000 null forks of the manifest's worlds on `batch-cpu` in double
  precision, each bitwise at every tick; built with the sixth control's feature, the entry reports at least one
  `mismatch` at the first tick and fails.

### End-to-end script and logging
The catalogue is driven from the user's side by `scripts/e2e/run_fork.sh` (N27a-08), which loops the 41 rows and
the two named refusals through `frob run fork … --classify-only --json` and logs expected against observed for
each row, the refusal blocks, and the metrics `pairs_correct` (threshold 41, all rows), `planted_sets_correct` (100) and
`graft_refused` (100).

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-676: warm-start graft across a design edit, refused with FROB-REF-582 (exact verdict). KA-716's negative
control: the 60 mm warm start grafted onto the 75 mm wheel. CX-321: "graft a warm start across a design edit in a
fork". CX-298 and CX-071: "apply a design edit at a mid-episode fork to see whether it prevents the failure" is
the shortcut that row S1 refuses. Facts of the cart fixture used by rows C and G1: front wheels of radius 60 mm
(design A) and 75 mm (design B), a sill 12 mm high, lip lever √(2rh − h²) = 36.000 mm at r = 60 mm and 40.694 mm
at r = 75 mm, so the contact point on the lip, and with it every stored contact frame and warm start, differs
between the designs. Determinism lanes: the catalogue test gives identical output twice, in debug and release and
on both operating systems (the classifier is exact).

## Considerations

- The catalogue is frozen with a hash; adding a changeable field to the registry of fork fields requires adding
  a catalogue row in the same change, and the count in the gate record then exceeds 40.
- Rows K5 and I8 encode this program's reading of a case the plan leaves open (a surface touched or a region
  sensed earlier and not now). If that reading is changed, these two rows change with it and nothing else does.
- The expected values are written from the rule table, never captured from the classifier's output.
