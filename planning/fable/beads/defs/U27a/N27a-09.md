## Background

Comparing two runs (a fork with its parent, one tier with another, one backend with another) starts with the
first place they differ. Every attribution reads that answer, so the Autopsy chapter restates the algorithm that
the Chronicle subsystem owns and the verb `telemetry.divergence` runs, and gives it a gate of its own. This bead
is that gate: a battery that plants divergences and checks the algorithm finds them, including the one mistake
that is easy to make.

**The algorithm under test** (the contract; the implementation is the first-divergence search bead, BD-206).

```text
first_divergence(A, B, mode):
  align the committed ticks and events of A and B by stable identifiers; a variable with no counterpart
    is listed as unpaired and is never matched by position
  if mode is exact:
      c_t = H(c_{t-1}, d_t) is the chained digest of the per-tick state digests d_t, stored at checkpoints
      bisect over checkpoints for the least k with c_k(A) ≠ c_k(B)
      replay both runs from checkpoint k − 1 and compare d_t tick by tick: the first unequal tick is t1
  if mode is tolerance, with a scale per class of variable:
      for each block of ticks, in chronological order:
          [lo, hi] = bounds on the block maximum of the scaled error
          hi ≤ 1 : the block agrees; go on
          lo > 1 : a mismatch lies inside; descend into the block
          otherwise : unresolved; refine this block before looking at any later block
      where samples are missing, report the earliest possible interval and the coverage gap
  at t1, re-run the tick with per-stage digests or residuals: the first stage over tolerance is s1
  report t1, s1, the variables that differ under their physical names and units, and the first tick at
    which a requirement atom or a task decision differs
```

**The trap.** Bisection is valid only on a predicate that is monotone and decided. "Some tick up to t differs"
is monotone. "The states differ at tick t" is not, because an error can vanish and return. Counterexample: two
traces of eight ticks that differ at ticks 1 and 5 and agree elsewhere. A midpoint search on the pointwise
predicate probes tick 3 or 4, sees agreement, moves right and reports tick 5. The chained digest reports tick 1,
because each chain value covers the whole prefix. The same trap applies to bisecting a design lineage, where the
predicate must be "the outcome has changed by this edit" and each step must be decided by a study.

**Rules for reading the result**, which the battery also checks in the report: the first recorded discrepancy is
labelled with its temporal resolution; the order in which solver stages report residuals inside a tick is not
evidence of a physical cause; for a fork against its parent the first divergent tick is the intervention tick or
later; when topology differs between the runs, only shared interfaces and task observables are compared (a leg
that exists in one run has no counterpart in the other).

Gate row (claim: Known answer; milestone M2; TARGET): **GATE-AUTOPSY-07. First divergence: planted divergences
found at the right tick and stage in 100 of 100 in exact mode; the pointwise mutant is caught; tolerance mode
reports gaps.** Tier: Solid.

## Scope

Two gates measure this one mechanism, and the fixtures are built once. Chronicle's gate bead (N29a-21,
GATE-CHRON-06, a dependency) builds and exports them from the test-support module
`frob_conformance::divergence_fixtures`: the two-tick pair builder with its shape check, the eight-tick minimal
digest sequences, the pointwise-bisection mutant, the gap-skipping mutant, and the loose-bound and
missing-samples fixtures. This bead runs those and compares the outcomes with its own row; it writes no second
mutant and no second fixture. The plant hook of the step is N29a-25's, also a dependency.

In: the planted-divergence battery of 100 single-plant trials for tick and stage; the run of the shared two-tick
cases, the pointwise mutant and the tolerance-mode cases (loose bound, missing samples) against this gate's
row; the fork-against-parent, changed-topology and agreement-soundness cases; the property tests for
PO-AUTOPSY-02 and PO-AUTOPSY-03; the monotonicity derivation on file; the end-to-end script; the gate record of
GATE-AUTOPSY-07.
Out: the algorithm's implementation and the verb (BD-206); the construction of the shared fixtures and mutants,
the chunk-final-digest mutant, and Chronicle's own gate for the same mechanism (N29a-21: GATE-CHRON-06,
recorded from that chapter's side); how an attribution uses the result (N27a-13); the
independent M3 cross-checks of PO-AUTOPSY-02 and PO-AUTOPSY-03 on synthetic traces against a linear scan, and
the registration of the two obligations in the Autopsy invariant suite (N27b-20, which cites the derivation
file written here and writes no second one).

## Technical approach

1. **Fault injection for planting.** A test-only hook of the step, compiled only under the conformance feature,
   adds one unit in the last place to one named output of one named stage (kernel) at one named tick of one
   world (the hook is N29a-25's; this battery arms it and builds none). Run A is the unmodified run; run B is
   the same address with the hook armed.
2. **Exact-mode battery.** 100 trials on the CPU tier (50 in each precision), each with a tick t drawn uniformly
   from the episode and a stage s drawn uniformly from the ten stages of the batched step, both by seed.
   `first_divergence(A, B, exact)` must return t1 = t and s1 = s. Ten of the trials place t on a checkpoint
   boundary and ten on the tick after one, the two off-by-one positions of the bisection.
3. **Two-tick case.** Eight-tick traces that differ at ticks 1 and 5 and agree elsewhere. Expected: tick 1. Both
   forms of the case come from the shared fixtures of N29a-21: the minimal instance as host digest sequences
   (the executable counterexample of the derivation in step 8), and pairs built on a real step. One
   construction for the real-step pairs (DESIGN, N29a-21's, with its shape check that asserts unequal digests
   at the first tick, equal digests between, unequal at the second before a pair is used): the first plant is
   on a stage output that is recorded but not fed back, so the state digests are equal again afterwards, and
   the second plant is on a state variable. A pair made by arming the hook and then trying to cancel the first
   fault with a second one is not used, because nothing guarantees the cancellation is exact in the state.
4. **The pointwise mutant.** A second implementation, kept in the test tree (once: the mutant of N29a-21's
   fixture module), bisects on "the state digests differ at tick t". On the two-tick case it must report
   tick 5. The battery asserts that the mutant is wrong there, so the case stays a live negative control, and
   asserts that the production function is not the mutant by running both on the 100 two-tick pairs of the
   shared trial generator (seeds 0 to 99, random first ticks).
5. **Fork against parent.** Fork a world at tick f with a setpoint change that takes effect at f + 3; expected
   t1 = the first tick the changed setpoint is read, never earlier than f; the report includes the order in which
   classes of variable leave tolerance and the first tick at which a requirement atom or task decision differs.
6. **Tolerance mode.** The loose-bound and missing-samples fixtures are the shared ones of N29a-21 (the same
   block with lo = 0.6 and hi = 1.4; the same removed samples at ticks 40 to 59 with the mismatch at 47; the
   same gap-skipping mutant, which reports tick 60); this battery asserts on them what its own row needs.
   - *Loose bound.* A block whose stored envelope gives hi = 1.4 and whose stored samples give lo = 0.6: the
     function must return `unresolved` for the block, refine it before any later block, and then return the right
     interval. A later block with lo > 1 must not be reported first.
   - *Missing samples.* A trace with samples absent for ticks 40 to 59 and a true first mismatch at tick 47
     that persists to the end of the trace (ticks up to 39 are certified as agreeing):
     expected output is the earliest possible interval [40, 60] with the coverage gap [40, 59], not a tick.
     Tick 60 is the first proven mismatch and belongs to the interval: had every missing tick agreed, it
     would be the first divergence, and the record cannot exclude that (the rule of the tolerance bead,
     N29a-20: from the first tick of the earliest block not certified as agreeing to the last tick of the
     block in which a mismatch is first proven). The negative control is a tool that skips the gap and
     reports the first mismatch among retained samples, tick 60; an answer of [40, 59] is also wrong, since
     it excludes a possible first divergence.
   - *Agreement soundness.* Whenever a block is reported as agreeing, the true maximum scaled error over the
     block, computed from the full state of both runs, is at most 1.
7. **Unpaired variables and topology.** Run A is a quadruped; run B the same body with a tail added. Expected:
   comparison on shared interfaces and task observables only; the tail's variables listed as unpaired; no match
   by position.
8. **Proof obligations as tests.**
   - **PO-AUTOPSY-02.** `first_divergence` in exact mode returns the least tick at which the prefixes differ.
     Class sought: Tested, with planted divergences; the monotonicity argument is on file in
     `docs/derivations/first-divergence-monotonicity.md`, written by this bead and the only derivation file for
     this obligation. The file states and proves, for the chained digest c_t = H(c_{t−1}, d_t) over per-tick
     state digests d_t and the predicate P(k): c_k(A) ≠ c_k(B):
     - *Monotone.* c_k covers ticks 0 to k. If c_k(A) ≠ c_k(B) then the inputs of H at k + 1 differ, so
       c_{k+1}(A) ≠ c_{k+1}(B) unless H collides (probability about 2⁻¹²⁸ for the 128-bit digest, the only gap
       in the argument, stated in the file). So P(k) ⇒ P(k′) for k′ > k.
     - *Decided.* Each P(k) is a comparison of two stored values.
     - *Correct.* If c_k(A) = c_k(B) then, barring a collision, c_{k−1} and d_k agree, and by induction the
       prefixes agree through k. Bisection over checkpoints therefore finds the least checkpoint k with P(k);
       the first unequal tick lies after checkpoint k − 1 and at or before checkpoint k, and comparing d_t tick
       by tick from checkpoint k − 1 finds it. That tick is the least tick at which the prefixes differ.
     - *The trap.* The pointwise predicate Q(t): d_t(A) ≠ d_t(B) is not monotone. On two traces of eight ticks
       (0 to 7) that differ at ticks 1 and 5, a midpoint search on Q probes tick 3 (agree), moves right, probes
       5 (differ), probes 4 (agree), and reports 5; on P it probes 3 (differ), 1 (differ), 0 (agree), and
       reports 1. The file's counterexample is executable as the test `two_ticks_reports_first`.
     - The same rule is recorded for bisecting a design lineage: the predicate must be "the outcome has changed
       by this edit" and each step must be decided by a study.
   - **PO-AUTOPSY-03.** In tolerance mode, an interval reported as agreeing has a true maximum scaled error of
     at most 1. Class sought: Tested with outward-rounded block bounds: the envelope's upper bound is rounded up
     and the sample-based lower bound rounded down when they are computed in floating point.
9. **Record.** GATE-AUTOPSY-07 with: exact trials passed of 100 (tick and stage separately), mutant caught
   (count of two-tick cases on which it is wrong), tolerance cases passed, commit, fingerprint, seeds. The
   gate is an entry of the tier target `g1_known_answers` that calls the battery's functions: it is run as
   `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features
   gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-AUTOPSY-07` (the pointwise and gap-skipping mutants are its controls, caught
   in the same run), and its row is entered from that run's receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance
   --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-AUTOPSY-07 --receipt <hash>` by a session that did not implement
   the work.

      the right stage in exact mode; the pointwise mutant reports the later tick on the two-tick case and is
      thereby caught; tolerance mode returns the interval [40, 60] and the gap [40, 59] on the missing-samples

      exists and is cited by the test.
- [ ] No reported t1 is later than the true first divergence on any exact trial (the Chronicle obligation

- [ ] The report of every trial names variables by physical name and unit and carries its temporal resolution.
- [ ] The changed-topology case lists the unpaired variables and matches nothing by position.
- [ ] The script exits non-zero on any miss and logs the planted and the reported tick and stage.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-chronicle/tests/first_divergence_gate.rs` for the tests that need no shared fixture, and
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/g1_first_divergence_autopsy.rs` for those that use the fixtures and mutants of
N29a-21 (`two_ticks_reports_first`, `pointwise_mutant_reports_5`, the three tolerance tests and
`gap_skipping_tool_reports_later`): the fixture module belongs to the conformance crate, and no other crate
depends on that crate.
- `planted_tick_and_stage_100` (seeded): equality of (t1, s1) with the planted pair in every trial.
- `checkpoint_boundary_cases`: t on a checkpoint, t one after.
- `two_ticks_reports_first`: differ at 1 and 5 of 8; expected 1.
- `pointwise_mutant_reports_5`: the mutant's answer on the same traces is 5.
- `po_autopsy_02_least_tick` (property, 1,000 random pairs of traces with one to three planted differences):
  the result equals the minimum planted tick.
- `fork_divergence_not_before_fork_tick`.
- `tolerance_unresolved_then_refined`; `tolerance_refines_chronologically`: with an unresolved block at ticks 20
  to 39 and a proven mismatch at ticks 60 to 79, the reported interval lies in 20 to 39 when the truth does.
- `tolerance_gap_interval`: expected interval [40, 60] with `coverage_gaps: [[40, 59]]` and resolution
  `interval`; an implementation that answers [40, 59] fails.
- `gap_skipping_tool_reports_later`: the control implementation returns tick 60.
- `po_autopsy_03_agreeing_blocks_sound` (property, 1,000 random blocks): reported agreement implies a true
  maximum scaled error ≤ 1; with inward rounding planted, the property fails.
- `unpaired_variables_listed`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gate_autopsy_07.sh`. From the user's side, with a `frob` binary built with the `conformance`
feature (the plant option exists only in that build and is not in the verb registry, N29a-25): record run A
with `frob sim run … --key ga07-<seed>-a`; record run B at the same addresses with `frob sim run …
--conformance-plant <plant file> --key ga07-<seed>-b-<trial>` (a plant file `frob.conformance-plant/1` naming
the trial's tick, stage, output and world; the two runs carry distinct keys, and the plant changes the step
plan identity, so B is never answered from A's receipt); `frob telemetry divergence <A> <B> --world <w>
--mode exact --json`; then a fork (`frob run fork … --change … --key ga07-<seed>-fork`) and its parent; then
the tolerance cases with `--mode tolerance` on runs whose telemetry level leaves a gap. Asserts as in the
acceptance list; the missing-samples case asserts the interval [40, 60] and the gap [40, 59]. Log (newline-delimited JSON at `target/e2e-logs/gate_autopsy_07/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`
plus a transcript): `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N27a-09`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time,
the envelope's evidence block, and per trial `planted_tick`, `planted_stage`, `reported_tick`, `reported_stage`,
`checkpoints_probed`, `ticks_replayed`, chain digests of both runs at the bracketing checkpoints; for tolerance
trials the block bounds `[lo, hi]`, the interval and the gap. Metrics: `exact_hits` (threshold 100),
`mutant_caught` (threshold 1), `tolerance_cases_passed`. A final `summary` line with `gates:
["GATE-AUTOPSY-07"]`. On failure: the first differing value and the two run identities and episode paths.

## Considerations

- The same mechanism has two gates (this one and Chronicle's GATE-CHRON-06: the two-tick case 100 of 100, a loose
  bound returning `unresolved` then the right interval, a gap returning an interval). The fixtures are shared and
  each gate is compared with its own row: they are built in N29a-21 and imported here, so a change to a fixture
  is made there and both rows are re-measured.
- Lineage bisection has the same trap with a different predicate. Its counterexample: outcomes along six
  versions are pass, fail, pass, pass, pass, fail; the midpoint passes, the search goes right and reports the
  last edit while the first edit also changed the outcome. That mechanism returns a pair of adjacent versions
  labelled `a-change` unless the prefix has been scanned; nothing here implements it.
- Stage order inside a tick is an order of computation. The battery's report text must say "first stage over
  tolerance", never "cause".
