## Background

Two properties of the counterfactual debugger are visible to a user only through the labels it prints. First,
that it tells causes that must be repaired together (`joint`: no single intervention removes the failure and a
pair does) from causes of which either may be repaired (`alternative`: several single interventions each remove
it), a distinction a Shapley summary cannot make since both give (½, ½). Second, that it names nothing when
nothing can be attributed: when a round-off-scale placebo flips the outcome in more than a fifth of forks
(`chaotic: unattributable`), and when the cause is not among the declared factors (`none`).

Both are gated on planted failures whose truth was fixed before any attribution ran:

| Gate | Claim | Threshold (TARGET) | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| GATE-AUTOPSY-04 | Known answer | Planted joint and alternative causes are labelled correctly in at least 95 of 100; the Shapley identities hold exactly; the negative-value case is reproduced | M3 |
| GATE-AUTOPSY-05 | Known answer | Abstention: a planted chaotic failure and a planted no-cause failure name no factor in 100 of 100 | M3 |

The crate-level gate tests call the library. This bead is the script that reaches the same answers through the
`frob` binary, as an agent would, and the recording of the two gate rows from the receipts of their tier runs.
Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/autopsy_coalitions_abstention.sh`, its assertions, its log, and the recording of the two gate
rows.
Out: the planted registries (N27a-12, N27a-14); the mechanisms (BD-281, BD-282 and the attribution verb); the
single-cause gate's script; the refusal script; the session-transcript script.

## Technical approach

The script sources the shared end-to-end helper; takes `--seed` (default 1), `--tier` (default the CPU tier in
double precision), `--subset <n>` for a fast lane (default: every case), and `--registry-dir`; opens a session
with scopes `sim:run` and `autopsy:run`; derives idempotency keys from the seed, the case identifier and the
step, and passes one as `--key` on every call of a `run` verb. The `frob` binary is the conformance build (the
crates' `conformance` feature on): the simulated chaotic cases need the probe atom of N27a-14 and the host
cases need the host-fixture handles of N27b-03; neither exists in a release build. The script checks this
first, with `frob autopsy intervene fixture:host/joint-pair --plan-only --key <k> --json`, and stops with a
message naming the missing build feature when the handle is refused as unknown.

For every case of the two registries (100 joint and alternative cases with 10 A/A controls; 100 chaotic cases
with the knife-edge fixture, 100 no-cause cases, 30 controls; the three host tables):

1. **Parent.** For a simulated case, run or find the failing episode from the case's identities and address
   (`frob sim run`) and assert the recorded failure matches the registry (class, binding atom, failure time).
   A host case (the 50 doubling-map cases, the 10 five-iterate controls, the three host tables) has no
   recorded run: its fork handle is the host-fixture handle `fixture:host/<name>:<case>` of N27b-03, which the
   Autopsy verbs accept in the conformance build, and steps 1 and 2 are skipped for it (the handle's null fork
   is checked by the verb itself).
2. **Fork point.** `frob run fork <run> --world <w> --before failure --dt 1.0s --json`. Assert `null_replay` is
   `bitwise`.
3. **Interventions.** `frob autopsy intervene <fork> --placebo 64 --replicates 8 --coalitions pairs --do … (one
   per declared factor) --json`; wait on the job if exit 8. Assert `forks_run` equals the planned count: with
   ten factors 1 + 64 + 8 × 55 = 505, plus 8 for each coalition that changed the outcome (at least one of its
   8 children passed where the parent failed: the run layer's trigger for the 10⁻⁷ series, BD-279).
4. **Attribution.** `frob autopsy attribute <fork> --json` (and `--shapley force` on the host tables). Read the
   outcome label, the per-factor labels, `top_factor`, `named_set`, the pairs with interactions, the placebo
   block, the Shapley block, the scope and the evidence block.
5. **Assertions by kind.**
   - *Joint*: outcome `joint`; both planted factors labelled `joint`; no distractor named; the minimal rescuing
     coalition is the planted pair; the pair's interaction is positive.
   - *Alternative*: outcome `alternative`; both planted factors labelled `alternative`; each planted single
     rescues; no distractor named.
   - *A/A control* (a failing world whose adverse factor is not declared, N27a-12): outcome `none`; no rescuing
     single or pair; nothing named in any of the four places; ρ₀ ≤ 0.2.
   - *Chaotic*: outcome `chaotic: unattributable`; every factor `none`; `top_factor` absent; `named_set` empty;
     the first next action is not a repair edit; the heuristic ordering is attached and labelled `Estimated`;
     the contrasts are still printed.
   - *No-cause*: outcome `none`; nothing named in any of the four places.
   - *Abstention controls*: the real factor is `but-for` in all 30 (five-iterate map; planted factor added to
     the list; the standing robot's declared torque cap), and ρ₀ = 0 on the five-iterate map and the standing
     robot.
   - *Host tables*: joint pair → labels `joint`, Shapley (1/2, 1/2); alternative pair → `alternative`, (1/2,
     1/2); negative-value table → factor 1 `but-for`, Shapley (1/2, −1/2) with sum 0 equal to v(N) − v(∅).
   - *Every case*: evidence class `Estimated`; `scope` lists the model, the tier, the precision and all declared
     factors; the Shapley block, when present, satisfies sum = v(N) − v(∅) as exact fractions.
   - *Divergence block* (every simulated case; N27a-13): for the first rescuing child of the case (or, where
     nothing rescues, the first flipped placebo child), run `frob telemetry divergence <child> <parent> --json`
     and assert that its first divergent tick equals the `divergence.first` of that child's contrast in the
     attribution, and that it is at or after the fork tick. Log both ticks, the class order and the first
     decision tick.
6. **Gate metrics.** `joint_alternative_correct` of 100 (threshold 95); `shapley_identities_exact` (count of
   tables checked, failures must be 0); `negative_value_reproduced` (boolean); `chaotic_named_nothing` of 100
   (threshold 100); `no_cause_named_nothing` of 100 (threshold 100); `controls_not_abstained` of 30 (threshold
   30); `aa_named_nothing` of 10 (threshold 10); `divergence_block_agrees` (count of simulated cases checked,
   failures must be 0). These go to the script's `summary` line with the commit, the hardware fingerprint and
   the seed. The script writes no gate status (a test never does). The rows of GATE-AUTOPSY-04 and
   GATE-AUTOPSY-05 are entered from the receipts of the gates' tier runs, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p
   frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-AUTOPSY-04` (likewise
   `GATE-AUTOPSY-05`): entries that drive the same registries through the same verbs and keys, so that they
   read the attributions this script stored and buy no fork, and that plant the gates' registered controls in
   the same run (for 04: labels taken from Shapley values, pairs tested at the singles' level, v(∅) left out
   of the interaction; for 05: name the largest contrast regardless of ρ₀, name the nearest declared factor,
   always decline). Each row is recorded with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate
   <id> --receipt <hash>` by a session that did not implement the coalition layer or the decision model
   (N38b-19). The script runs the two tier entries as its last step and logs the receipt hashes.
7. **Self-test mode** (`--self-test`): runs four cases against a binary built with one registered negative
   control at a time (label from Shapley values; name the largest contrast regardless of ρ₀; name the nearest
   declared factor; always decline) and asserts that the corresponding assertion fails.

Structured log: newline-delimited JSON at `target/e2e-logs/autopsy_coalitions_abstention/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`
with a human-readable transcript beside it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N27a-18`), `step`, `event`
(`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`). `cmd` lines: the exact command line, exit code, wall
time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). Per case, one `assert`
line per check with expected and observed values, and one `metric` line with: case identifier and kind, factors,
coalitions planned and run, forks run against planned, placebo flips of 64 and ρ₀, each planted factor's rescued
count, p-value and level, pair interactions, outcome label expected and observed, `top_factor`, `named_set`,
Shapley values as fractions with their sum. Digests: the parent's chain digest and the null fork's. On failure:
the first differing value (which label, which place a name appeared) and the run, fork and registry identifiers
needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line: assertions passed and failed, cases by kind, `gates:
["GATE-AUTOPSY-04", "GATE-AUTOPSY-05"]`. The log holds identities, counts and labels only; no scene content.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on the full registries on the CPU tier in double precision, with the eight metrics at
      or above their thresholds, and non-zero when any threshold is missed.
- [ ] On every simulated case the divergence block of the attribution agrees with `frob telemetry divergence`.
- [ ] Both tier entries produce receipts whose figures equal the script's `summary`, with their controls
      caught, and both rows recorded from them hold measured values, not targets.
- [ ] Every host case is reached through its `fixture:host/…` handle on the conformance build; on a release
      build the script stops at its first check with the missing-feature message and runs no case.
- [ ] `forks_run` equals the planned count on every case.
- [ ] A chaotic case in which a declared factor shows a rescue count of 8 of 8 is logged with that count and
      with nothing named.
- [ ] `--self-test` shows each of the four negative controls caught.
- [ ] A second invocation with the same seed creates no new jobs and logs identical labels, counts and digests.
- [ ] `--subset 10` runs in minutes on the CPU tier and is the lane used on every change to the crate.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Shell-level tests of the helpers: `planned_forks 10 pairs` = 505 and `planned_forks 12 pairs` = 689;
`named_anywhere` detects a name in each of the four places (label, `top_factor`, `named_set`, first repair
action) on canned envelopes; `fraction_sum_equals` compares exact fractions ("1/2", "-1/2" sum to "0").

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script; commands, assertions and log fields are in the technical approach.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-673 (labels `joint` and `alternative`; Shapley values (½, ½) in both; repair sets {1, 2}, and {1} or {2};
control: a summary that reports only Shapley values cannot tell them apart). KA-674 ((½, −½); the values sum to
0 exactly). KA-672 (the doubling map on its fortieth iterate: about half of placebo forks flip; label `chaotic:
unattributable`; control: five iterates attribute normally). KA-147. KA-670's control (a failure planted through
a factor outside the list names no factor). The derived fork counts 505 and 689. CX-296, CX-297, CX-300.
Determinism lanes: the script twice on one machine with identical logs apart from timestamps and wall times;
with 1 and with all workers; debug and release binaries; where a device backend is present, a 10-case subset on
the device against its own record, with attribution still in double precision on the CPU tier.

## Considerations

- Cost: about 280 simulated cases at about 505 forks of 2 s each are 2.8 × 10⁵ world-seconds: about five minutes
  at 1,000 world-seconds per second and under an hour at 100. The host cases cost nothing.
- The script reads names in four places because a name can leak through a repair suggestion even when the label
  table is right.
- If the subsystem's kill gate fires, coalitions are removed and GATE-AUTOPSY-04's half of this script is
  retired with a recorded reason; the abstention half stays.
