## Background

A closed-loop legged robot in contact amplifies tiny differences. Restore a failing world, change anything at
all, and some outcomes change; that is sensitivity, not cause. Autopsy therefore gives every intervention its
meaning through three kinds of fork from the same checkpoint:

- **The null fork** applies the empty intervention. It must reproduce the parent bit for bit on the originating
  profile (invariant INV-AUTOPSY-01). If it does not, the record is unsound and nothing is attributed
  (FROB-REF-583).
- **A placebo fork** applies a change no physical cause could depend on: one state coordinate perturbed by a
  relative 10⁻¹². The **placebo flip rate** ρ₀ is the fraction of placebo forks whose verdict differs from the
  parent's. It measures how much of an apparent rescue is mere sensitivity.
- **Replicates** of an intervention apply the same change with independent perturbations of the same size. A
  second series at a relative 10⁻⁷, the scale of single-precision round-off, is run for every intervention that
  changes the outcome.

This bead builds the perturbation generator and the counting that all three rest on, and the measurement of how
fast a placebo fork separates from its parent. The counterexample it exists for (CX-300, "any flip under an
intervention is attribution"): a doubling map x ← 2x mod 1 with failure defined on its fortieth iterate amplifies
a 10⁻¹² placebo by 2⁴⁰ ≈ 1.1 × 10¹², about half of placebo forks flip, and every intervention would look like a
cause. Tier Solid; crate `frob-autopsy`, layer 5; serves construction C-29 (the placebo fork is "an intervention
with no mechanistic route to the outcome") and bet BET-23.

Random streams in this system are counter-based and keyed by logical address `(WorldAddr, step, stream_id)`;
there is no generator state. By default a child reads the same streams as its parent, so the intervention is the
only difference. A replicate changes one declared thing more: a state perturbation of stated size, or the key of
one named stream.

## Scope

In: child addressing for the fork series; the perturbation (coordinate, sign and size by keyed draw); the
representability rule; replicate-by-rekey; verdict comparison and flip counting, including `unknown` outcomes;
the null-fork check and FROB-REF-583; the separation trace of a placebo fork and the growth figure G(W); a
`ForkTarget` trait so that host fixtures run through the same code.
Out: choosing how many forks to run and running them in a batch (BD-279); the statistics on the counts (N27b-05);
the admission rule that reads ρ₀ and G(W) in the single-precision lane (N27b-23); snapshots, restore and the fork
path encoding (the fork service, BD-205).

## Technical approach

1. **Series and child addresses (DESIGN).** A child's address is the parent's `WorldAddr` extended by a fork
   path `(fork_tick, change_set_hash, series, index)`. Series: `null` (one child, empty change set); `placebo`
   with its size tag (`placebo@1e-12`, `placebo@1e-7`); `rep@1e-12` and `rep@1e-7` (replicates of one coalition,
   keyed by its change-set hash). Replicate index 0 of `rep@1e-12` is the **unperturbed fork** of the
   intervention; indices 1 … R − 1 are perturbed. All R indices of `rep@1e-7` are perturbed. (The budget
   arithmetic of the attribution, 78 coalitions × 8 + 64 + 1 = 689 forks, fixes that the unperturbed fork is one
   of the R.)
2. **The perturbation.** For a perturbed child, three exact draws from the registered stream `autopsy.perturb`
   keyed by `(child address, fork_tick)`: a coordinate index j among the robot's physical state coordinates
   (generalized positions and velocities; never clocks, queues, counters or integer state), a sign σ ∈ {−1, +1},
   and a size factor u uniform on [1, 2). The applied change is δ = σ·u·ε·s_j, with ε ∈ {10⁻¹², 10⁻⁷} and s_j the
   physical scale of the coordinate's class (DESIGN defaults: 1 m, 1 rad, 1 m/s, 1 rad/s, taken from the scale
   table tolerance comparisons already use). Orientation coordinates are perturbed in the tangent space (a
   rotation by δ about a drawn body axis) so the state stays on its manifold. Reason for a size *range*: with one
   fixed size, the amplified shift on the doubling map is 10⁻¹² · 2⁴⁰ mod 1 = 0.0995 for every fork and only about
   20% flip, on the threshold; sizes on [1, 2)·ε spread the shift over the circle and give 0.45 to 0.51.
3. **Representability.** After applying δ, the stored coordinate must differ in bits from the original. In single
   precision a 10⁻¹² change is below round-off and is a no-op; the generator then returns `NotRepresentable` and
   the series is recorded as *not run at this precision*, never as zero flips. Where ε·s_j is below one unit in
   the last place of the stored value at 10⁻⁷, the applied size is raised to one unit in the last place and the
   applied relative size is recorded. A silent no-op perturbation would make ρ₀ = 0 and hide chaos; a unit test
   plants exactly that defect.
4. **Replicate by re-keying.** `ReplicateBy::Rekey(stream_id)`: instead of a state perturbation, the child reads
   one named stream (for example one sensor's noise) under a key extended by the replicate index; every other
   stream is the parent's. Used where a state perturbation is not wanted (an exact-integer state).
5. **Verdicts and flips.** Each child runs to the fork horizon and yields a requirement verdict: `violated` (with
   failure class and binding atom), `satisfied`, or `unknown` (a numerical outcome). Counting rules:
   - *rescued*: `satisfied`. A child that is `violated` by a different atom or class is not rescued and is counted
     under `other_failure`.
   - *flip* (placebo, and perturbation flip rate): the verdict differs from the reference verdict (the parent's
     for placebo forks; the unperturbed fork's for replicates).
   - `unknown` is fail-closed: counted as a flip and as not rescued, kept in every denominator, and reported in
     its own count.
   ρ₀ = placebo flips / placebo forks. Flip rate of an intervention at ε = perturbed replicates at ε whose verdict
   differs from the unperturbed fork's, divided by R (index 0 agrees with itself by definition at 10⁻¹²).
6. **The null fork.** Child `null` runs in the same batch as the other children. Its chained state digest at
   every committed tick from the fork tick to the parent's last tick must equal the parent's. On mismatch:
   **FROB-REF-583** (the null fork does not reproduce its parent), exit 3, nothing else in the request is reported
   as a result; `observed` carries the first divergent tick and stage from the first-divergence search;
   `retry: after-change`; ranked repairs: (1) `frob telemetry divergence <parent> <null fork>` to name the first
   divergent tick, stage and variables; (2) fork from another stored snapshot, or by replay from the world
   address, to rule out a damaged checkpoint; (3) re-record the run under a replay profile and file the
   determinism violation with both digests.
7. **Separation and growth.** For placebo child k at checkpoint tick t, d_k(t) is the largest scaled difference
   from the parent over shared physical state variables (difference divided by the class scale). G_k(W) =
   max over t in [fork tick, fork tick + W] of d_k(t) / d_k(fork tick). Reported: `separation_growth` =
   median_k G_k(horizon), and `separation_growth_max` = max_k G_k(horizon) (the conservative figure admission
   rules read). For a linear system with exponent λ per step the value after n steps is e^(λ·n).
8. **`ForkTarget` trait (DESIGN).** `restore(checkpoint) → state`, `apply(change_set)`, `perturb(j, δ)`,
   `run(horizon) → (verdict, digest chain, checkpointed states)`. Implemented by the Chronicle fork service and by
   host fixtures in `crates/frob-autopsy/src/fixtures/` (each labelled `synthetic`): the doubling map in 64-bit
   fixed point (exact), the linear map x ← 1.1735·x, the inverted pendulum, the one-node thermal model.
9. **Module layout.** `crates/frob-autopsy/src/perturb.rs`, `flip.rs`, `nullfork.rs`, `separation.rs`,
   `fixtures/`. Determinism: draws are class D0; a child inherits the class of its tier (D2 required for
   attribution). Evidence: counts are facts about replayable forks, class Tested on their tier.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] INV-AUTOPSY-01: the null fork has identical digests on the originating profile on every fixture; a
      checkpoint with its packet queue stripped does not replay and is refused with FROB-REF-583 and the first
      divergent tick.
- [ ] A placebo fork differs from the restored checkpoint in exactly one coordinate (or one rotation), by a
      relative size in [ε, 2ε), with the drawn sign; the same child address gives the same perturbation on every
      run and backend.
- [ ] Doubling map, fortieth iterate, 64 placebo forks at 10⁻¹²: ρ₀ is between 0.3 and 0.7; over five iterates
      ρ₀ = 0.
- [ ] Linear map x ← 1.1735·x: G(40 steps) is within 1% of 602 (1.1735⁴⁰ = 601.6; e^(0.16·40) = 601.8).
- [ ] In single precision the `@1e-12` series reports `NotRepresentable`; the `@1e-7` series changes bits on
      every child.
- [ ] `unknown` children are counted as flips and as not rescued, and appear in their own count.
- [ ] Replicate-by-rekey changes one stream and no other draw (digest of every other stream's draws unchanged).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-autopsy/tests/perturb.rs`, `nullfork.rs`, `separation.rs`:
- `perturb_is_one_coordinate`: restored state vs perturbed state differ in one index; |δ|/s_j ∈ [ε, 2ε).
- `perturb_is_keyed`: same address → same (j, σ, u); two addresses differing only in replicate index → independent
  draws (chi-square over 10⁴ draws for uniform j and fair sign).
- `no_op_is_refused` : ε = 10⁻¹² on an `f32` state returns `NotRepresentable`.
- `ulp_floor_f32`: a coordinate of value 3.0 in `f32` at ε = 10⁻⁷ moves by one unit in the last place
  (2.38 × 10⁻⁷) and records the applied size.
- `null_fork_bitwise` (fixture 2, the quadruped on the split-grip landing, one failing world): identical digests.
- `null_fork_stripped_queue_refused` (KA-671 control): packet queue removed from the checkpoint → FROB-REF-583.
- `doubling_map_chaotic` (KA-672, `synthetic`): ρ₀ ∈ [0.3, 0.7] at 40 iterates; `doubling_map_five_iterates`:
  ρ₀ = 0 (amplification 32 gives a shift of at most 6.4 × 10⁻¹¹).
- `linear_map_growth` (`synthetic`): G(20) = 24.5, G(40) = 602, G(80) = 3.6 × 10⁵, each within 1%.
- `unknown_is_fail_closed`: a child that ends in a numerical fault counts as a flip.
- `flip_rate_reference`: index 0 is unperturbed and its verdict is the reference.
- `rekey_changes_one_stream`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/autopsy_intervene.sh` (N27b-04) runs null, placebo and replicate forks through
`frob autopsy intervene` and logs, per series: forks run, the applied relative size range, flips, unknowns, ρ₀,
`separation_growth` and its maximum, and for the null fork the two chain digests compared.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-671 (null fork: identical digests; control: stripped packet queue refused). KA-672 and CX-300 (doubling map:
`chaotic` at forty iterates, normal at five). The linear-map growth of 602 at 40 steps. KA-145 (the empty
intervention replays the recorded failure bit for bit). Negative controls: a generator whose perturbation is a
no-op (ρ₀ = 0 on the doubling map: the test fails); a fixed perturbation size (ρ₀ ≈ 0.20 on the doubling map: the
band test fails); a null fork compared by final state only (the stripped-queue control passes wrongly).
Metamorphic: a placebo flips nothing on a non-chaotic case (the five-iterate map; a quadruped standing still).
Determinism lanes: placebo outcomes identical twice on one machine, across worker counts, alone and in a batch of
512 children; debug and release on `batch-cpu`.

## Considerations

- The 10⁻¹² and 10⁻⁷ sizes and the counts are defaults under open question OPEN-AUTOPSY-1 (owner: Autopsy lead;
  decided by the planted-cause pilot; default 8 replicates at 10⁻¹² and 8 at 10⁻⁷ for interventions that change
  the outcome).
- The coordinate set is the robot's state. Perturbing a scene object the robot never touches would be a placebo
  that cannot flip anything, and would understate ρ₀.
- G(W) is a finite-size growth figure, not a Lyapunov exponent; it saturates once separations reach the size of
  the attractor. That is the regime the report needs to show.
- A counter-based stream keyed by address means two forks of the same child address are the same experiment;
  re-running a request never draws "fresh" perturbations, which keeps a transcript replayable.
