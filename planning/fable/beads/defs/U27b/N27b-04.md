## Background

`autopsy.intervene` is the verb that runs counterfactual experiments on a failed simulated world: the null fork
(the empty intervention), placebo forks (one state coordinate perturbed by a relative 10⁻¹²), replicates of each
intervention, and coalitions. Everything Autopsy later says about causes rests on two properties of this verb
that can only be shown on a running system: the starting point of every fork is exact, and a change that may not
be applied to a mid-episode state is refused with the fields an agent needs to recover. The seed condition of
the verb is: *a placebo fork equals replay on 1,000 worlds; a mid-episode gear change is refused with the right
fields.*

"A placebo fork equals replay" needs a precise reading, because a placebo fork is perturbed on purpose and its
digest after the fork tick differs from the parent's. The reading used here, and asserted below: (1) the null
fork, run through this verb, has digests identical to replay of the parent on the originating backend; (2) every
placebo child is bit-identical to the replayed state at the fork tick before its perturbation is applied, and
after it differs in exactly one coordinate by the recorded size; (3) a placebo with the perturbation size forced
to zero is bit-identical to the null fork for its whole length.

This script also re-reads, through the Autopsy verb at M3, the two fork gates first recorded at M2 by the fork
service: GATE-AUTOPSY-01 (null forks of 1,000 failing worlds replay with identical digests on the originating
backend; a world forked alone and in a batch of 512 children gives identical digests) and the refusal clause of
GATE-AUTOPSY-02 (the warm-start graft and a mid-episode gear change are refused with the right fields). The M2
measurement of record belongs to the fork service's bead; this is the regression reading on the surface an agent
uses. Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/autopsy_intervene.sh`, its fixtures, assertions and logs; the M3 regression reading of
GATE-AUTOPSY-01 and of the refusal clause of GATE-AUTOPSY-02; the share of the 1,000 recorded failures whose
placebo flip rate is above 0.2 (the second falsifier of BET-23, which no other bead measures).
Out: the M2 measurements of record: GATE-AUTOPSY-01 on 1,000 failing worlds per backend with its committed
population manifest (N27a-06), and GATE-AUTOPSY-02 with the 40 catalogued classification pairs and the two
named refusals (N27a-07); this script re-reads them at M3 through `autopsy.intervene` and writes no gate row
of its own. Also out: attribution (N27b-09); the other refusals (N27b-19).

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures.** (a) A recorded evaluation of the reference quadruped with at least 1,000 failing worlds, on
   `batch-cpu` and on each available device tier: the population manifest committed by the M2 gate bead N27a-06
   (the same world addresses, so that the regression rows compare like with like); only if that manifest is
   absent for a tier does the script run an evaluation with an explicit seed, in `eval.run`'s single-task form
   (`frob eval run <quadruped> --task <task> --worlds <n> --run-seed 20270301 --tier <tier> --key
   e2e-eval-<tier> --json`, with n chosen to yield at least 1,000 failures and logged; no `--battery`, beside
   which `--run-seed` and `--worlds` are FROB-REF-802). (b) The docking cart of fixture 1 at its sill, in the 60 mm and 75 mm
   wheel designs, for the warm-start graft. (c) Fixture 2, the quadruped on the split-grip landing, for the
   sequential request. All fixtures are generated; none is a private scene.
2. **Steps** (each a `frob … --json` command; the script sources the shared end-to-end helper):
   1. `frob run fork <run> --world <w> --before failure --dt 1.0s --key e2e-fork-<w> --json` for each of the 1,000 worlds, in
      batches; assert `null_replay: "bitwise"`.
   2. `frob autopsy intervene <fork> --placebo 64 --replicates 8 --key e2e-null-<w> --json` with no
      `--do` (null and placebo only); assert `forks_run = 65`, the null child's final chain digest equals the
      parent's, and from the ledger that each placebo child's pre-perturbation digest at the fork tick equals the
      parent's and that exactly one coordinate differs, with applied relative size in [10⁻¹², 2 × 10⁻¹²).
      From the same results, over the worlds read in double precision: `share_rho0_above_0.2` = the worlds
      with more than 12 placebo flips of 64, divided by the worlds read. It is recorded against 0.5, the
      threshold of BET-23's clause "the placebo flip rate exceeds 0.2 on more than half of the reference
      failures"; it is no part of either gate, and the kill decision (N27b-11) and the bet (BET-23) read it
      from this log.
   3. The zero-size placebo. The verb has no option for the perturbation size (a test-only flag on a product
      verb would be a surface no registry row describes), so the reading is made one level down, on the same
      code: `cargo test -p frob-autopsy --test intervene -- zero_size_placebo_equals_null` (BD-279), which
      calls the generator with ε = 0 on four placebo children of a real fork and requires their chain digests
      to equal the null fork's.
   4. Alone and in a batch: world w₀ forked with 1 child and with 512 children (two requests with keys
      `e2e-alone-<w₀>` and `e2e-batch-<w₀>`, each under its own scratch state root, `FROB_STATE` pointed at a
      copy of the root that holds the parent run, as the M2 gate script does: in one store the second request
      would read child 0 by address from the first and the comparison would be empty); child 0's digests in
      the two stores are equal.
   5. Counts: three interventions with `--coalitions pairs` on one world; assert `forks_run` equals 113 plus 8
      per coalition reported as having changed the outcome.
   6. The gear change: `--do 'model.joint[*.knee].gear_ratio := 16'` beside one admissible intervention; assert
      exit 2, `refused[0].code = "FROB-REF-581"`, `earliest_valid_fork_tick = 0`, and `forced_by` ⊇ {actuator
      thermal state, policy history, contact memory}; assert the `next_actions` entry is a `frob autopsy rerun`
      command. The same intervention alone: exit 3 with the same fields in `refusal`.
   7. The graft, requested as the fork service's own script and catalogue request it (N27a-07, N27a-08): on
      fixture 1, `frob run fork <C> --world <w> --at <lip tick> --change 'model.wheel[front].radius := 75 mm'
      --reuse checkpoint.contact.memory --key e2e-graft --json`, where C is the 60 mm run; assert exit 3 and
      FROB-REF-582 naming the contact memory as the field that forces a restart. Then the same graft through
      the Autopsy verb, `frob autopsy intervene <fork at the lip> --do 'model.wheel[front].radius := 75 mm'
      --reuse checkpoint.contact.memory --key e2e-graft-aut --json`: the same code and field (exit 3, the only
      intervention). Control: the same `--change` at tick 0 without `--reuse` and with `--classify-only` is
      classified `structural` and accepted, so the refusal is specific to the graft.
   8. Sequential mode (N27bp2-01): on a failing split-grip world of fixture 2 (the quadruped on the split-grip landing,
      whose all-dry case passes), `--test sequential --max-pairs 64` with
      `--do 'scene./floor/patch[strip]/friction := 0.8'` (the all-dry surface) and one decoy that does not
      rescue (the friction of a patch off the route); assert the
      ledger header says `test: sequential`, each factor reports its rounds and its e-value, the rescuer closes
      as tested at the round where E_n first reaches m/α, and a second execution returns the stored answer;
      then a request for the same two factors as a fixed table on the same fork (`frob autopsy intervene <fork>
      --test fixed --do … --do … --key e2e-seq-as-fixed --json`) is refused with FROB-REF-553 before any fork
      runs, with the stored type and its ledger in `observed` (the sequential mode, N27bp2-01). The read verb
      has no option that could ask for the other reading.
   9. Parent integrity: chunk hashes of the parent run before step 1 and after step 8 are equal.
3. **Idempotent and seed-explicit.** Every `run` call carries an idempotency key (`--key`) derived from the
   script name, the step and the world; a second execution of the script returns stored answers
   (`idempotent_replay: true`) and the same assertions pass.
4. **A regression reading, not a record.** The script writes no gate status and no gate row (a test never
   does; the rows of GATE-AUTOPSY-01 and GATE-AUTOPSY-02 are entered by N27a-06 and N27a-07 from the receipts
   of their tier runs). Its `summary` line carries the M3 figures (worlds replayed, digest mismatches, refusal
   field checks, `share_rho0_above_0.2`) with the commit, the hardware fingerprint and the seed, and the exit
   review (N27b-30) compares them with the recorded rows. Its last step runs the two gates' tier entries with
   their registered controls, `e2e_tier g5_determinism GATE-AUTOPSY-01 --mutations` and `e2e_tier
   g1_known_answers GATE-AUTOPSY-02 --mutations`, and asserts from each `E2E_LAST_RECEIPT` that every control
   is `caught`; it logs the receipt hashes and records nothing from them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 1,000 of 1,000 null forks have identical digests on the originating backend, per backend available.
- [ ] 64,000 of 64,000 placebo children restore bit for bit and differ in one coordinate by the recorded size.
- [ ] Zero-size placebo children equal the null fork (the crate test of step 3 passes inside the script).
- [ ] Alone and in a batch of 512, digests are equal across the two state roots.
- [ ] `share_rho0_above_0.2` is logged with its numerator and denominator against 0.5.
- [ ] The gear change is refused with code, earliest valid tick 0 and the three forcing fields; exit 2 in a mixed
      request and 3 alone.
- [ ] The warm-start graft is refused with FROB-REF-582 and names the contact memory, through `run.fork` and
      through `autopsy.intervene`; its tick-0 control is accepted.
- [ ] The sequential request stops each factor at the round its e-process dictates and replays to the same
      rounds; requesting the same factors as a fixed table on that fork is refused with FROB-REF-553.
- [ ] The receipts of the two tier entries run by the script show every registered control of GATE-AUTOPSY-01
      and GATE-AUTOPSY-02 `caught`, among them the two build-level defects of the test plan.
- [ ] The script exits non-zero on any failed assertion, is idempotent, and writes its log and transcript.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own: the script drives the real `frob` binary. The helper's own tests belong to the end-to-end
harness. A shell-level self-test `scripts/e2e/autopsy_intervene.sh --self-test` runs steps 2 to 6 on eight
worlds in under two minutes for use on every change.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/autopsy_intervene.sh`. Log: `target/e2e-logs/autopsy_intervene/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript
beside it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N27b-04`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall
time, the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). `metric` lines:
`null_digest_mismatches` (value, unit 1, threshold 0), `placebo_restore_mismatches` (threshold 0),
`placebo_coordinates_changed` (threshold exactly 1 per child), `applied_relative_size_min/max` (thresholds
10⁻¹² and 2 × 10⁻¹²), `forks_run` against the planned count, `batch_digest_equal`, `share_rho0_above_0.2`
(value with its two counts, unit 1, threshold 0.5 of the bet's clause), and for the sequential
request `rounds_to_close` and `e_value` per factor against the threshold m/α. `refusal` lines: code,
pointer, `earliest_valid_fork_tick`, `forced_by`. On failure: the first world and tick at which digests differ,
both digests, and the ledger and fork identifiers needed to reproduce. Final `summary`: assertions passed and
failed, gates touched (GATE-AUTOPSY-01, GATE-AUTOPSY-02). No scene geometry or state values are logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-671: null fork, identical digests; negative control run by the script: a checkpoint with its packet queue
stripped (a conformance fixture) must be refused, not replayed: with FROB-REF-580 at classification when the strip
leaves a contract field missing, and with FROB-REF-583 (first divergent tick attached) when the strip is disguised
and only the null fork can catch it; both variants are run. KA-676: the graft is refused with FROB-REF-582.
Build-level defects (a null fork that no longer reproduces its parent bit for bit; a structural change let
through at a mid-episode tick) cannot be planted in the `frob` binary the script drives, which carries no
mutation feature (N38b-04): they are the gates' registered mutation controls, the fork that steps one tick past
its checkpoint of GATE-AUTOPSY-01 (N27a-06) and design changes mapped to exogenous of GATE-AUTOPSY-02 (N27a-07),
caught in the two tier entries of the script's last step and asserted from their receipts. Determinism lanes: the script is run twice on one machine and
with 1 and 16 workers; the measured digests are equal across the lanes.

## Considerations

- 1,000 worlds × 65 forks of about two seconds is 130,000 world-seconds: minutes on `batch-cpu`, less on a
  device. The self-test keeps the per-change cost small.
- If a device family has not attained class D2 for batch independence, the alone-versus-batch step is expected to
  be refused by the licence check (FROB-REF-562) on that family; the script records the refusal and does not
  count it as a failure of this verb.
- The regression figures do not replace the M2 gate records; if they disagree with them, that is a finding to
  file against the fork service.
- The placebo share is a property of the recorded population and of the fork lead (1 s here). If it is above
  one half, the kill decision's ADR records it and the lead ladder is re-examined at 0.5 s and 0.25 s; this
  script reports the figure and decides nothing.
