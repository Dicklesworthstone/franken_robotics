## Background

The output of an attribution is a table of what each intervention and each pair did, and a label per factor that
says what the table supports. The labels are the part an agent acts on, so their conditions, their order of
precedence and their spelling have to be fixed in one place. This bead is that place: a pure function from the
statistics of N27b-05 (and, where they exist, the coalition table and the episode-start result) to labels, a
ranking and a named set.

The labels and their conditions (thresholds are TARGETs):

| Label | Condition |
|---|---|
| `but-for` | One factor with contrast at least 0.5, tested above the placebo, and a flip rate of at most 0.1 at 10⁻¹² |
| `contributing` | Tested above the placebo with a smaller contrast |
| `joint` | No single factor rescues and a coalition does |
| `alternative` | Several single factors each rescue |
| `precedes-snapshot` | No intervention at the fork tick rescues, and the same change applied from the episode start does |
| `precision-sensitive` | The failure is not reproduced in double precision and is not admitted to the single-precision lane |
| `chaotic: unattributable` | ρ₀ above 0.2. No factor is named |

Three more spellings appear on the wire: `none` for a factor that is not named; `marginal` for a failure whose
verdict moves under perturbations at round-off scale on the originating tier (a verdict that moves under a
relative change of 10⁻⁵ in state also moves under sensor noise and friction scatter two orders larger, so the
right output is the label, not more digits); and `tier-specific: <variant>` for a failure that a numerical
variant removes. For a marginal failure, or for causes that interact, the report names a set of factors and not
a top factor.

Why labels and not scores: a single ranked score (a Shapley value, a contrast) hides the distinction an agent
needs for repair. Two factors that rescue only together and two that each rescue alone have the same summary
values and opposite repair sets. Tier Solid; crate `frob-autopsy`; construction C-29; decision core DC-06.

## Scope

In: the label function with its precedence; the rescue criterion; per-factor labels; the attribution-level
outcome; the ranking rule; the top factor and the named set; stability of the top factor between the 10⁻¹² and
10⁻⁷ series; wire spellings and the extended `CauseLabel` type.
Out: the statistics (N27b-05); coalition values (BD-281); the abstention decision as a policy with fallbacks
(N27b-12, BD-282); deciding `precision-sensitive`, `marginal` and `tier-specific` (N27b-07, N27b-24, N27b-25:
this bead only places them in the precedence order).

## Technical approach

1. **Type (the program's sketch, extended by the two late labels; DESIGN).**
   ```rust
   pub enum CauseLabel { ButFor, Contributing, Joint, Alternative, PrecedesSnapshot, PrecisionSensitive,
                         ChaoticUnattributable, Marginal, TierSpecific(VariantTag), None }
   pub struct Labelling { per_factor: Vec<(FactorPath, CauseLabel)>, outcome: CauseLabel,
                          ranking: Vec<FactorPath>, top_factor: Option<FactorPath>,
                          named_set: Vec<FactorPath>, top_factor_stable_1e7: Option<bool> }
   ```
2. **The rescue criterion.** A factor or coalition *rescues* when its contrast is at least 1/2, it is tested above
   the placebo, and its flip rate at 10⁻¹² is at most 1/10. All three comparisons are on exact rationals. With
   R = 8 the flip-rate clause means zero flips (1/8 exceeds 1/10), hence all eight replicates rescued; with
   R = 16 it admits one flip. In the single-precision lane the flip-rate clause is read at 10⁻⁷, the only series
   that exists there. Because replicate 0 of the 10⁻¹² series is the unperturbed fork, the number of flipped
   replicates equals the rescue count when the unperturbed fork still fails and R minus the rescue count when it
   is rescued; a table that violates this identity is malformed.
3. **Precedence (first match decides the outcome; DESIGN).**
   1. The record is unsound (null fork mismatch): no labelling exists; the caller has already refused.
   2. `precision-sensitive` or `marginal`, as decided by the precision procedure or the lane: every factor
      `none`; no contrast is named.
   3. `tier-specific: <variant>`: the numerical variant that rescued is the only named item.
   4. `chaotic: unattributable` when 5·f > P (ρ₀ above 0.2): every factor `none`. The contrasts are still
      printed, so a reader sees what was measured.
   5. Otherwise, with S = the single factors that rescue:
      - |S| = 1: that factor is `but-for`; outcome `but-for`.
      - |S| ≥ 2: each member is `alternative`; outcome `alternative`.
      - |S| = 0 and some coalition rescues: the members of each inclusion-minimal rescuing coalition are
        `joint`; outcome `joint`. If coalitions were not run, the outcome cannot be `joint`; the labelling
        records `coalitions: not-run` and the caller offers `--coalitions pairs` as the next action.
      - Every other factor that is tested above the placebo is `contributing`; the rest are `none`.
      - |S| = 0, no coalition rescues, and an episode-start application of one of the changes rescues: outcome
        `precedes-snapshot`. If no episode-start result exists the outcome is `contributing` or `none` and the
        caller offers an earlier fork (the lead ladder 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2 s) or a re-run from the start.
   A factor whose contrast is at least 1/2 but whose flip rate at 10⁻¹² exceeds 1/10 is `contributing`, not
   `but-for`: it beat the placebo but the fork itself is unstable.
4. **Ranking.** Factors are ordered by: rescues (yes before no); contrast, descending; flip rate at 10⁻⁷,
   ascending (`null` last); flip rate at 10⁻¹², ascending; canonical factor string. `top_factor` is present only
   when the outcome is `but-for`. `named_set` is every factor whose label is not `none`. The gate phrase "the
   planted factor ranks first" reads `ranking[0]` and requires its label not to be `none`.
5. **Stability of the top factor at 10⁻⁷.** The ranking is recomputed with each factor's rescue rate taken from
   its 10⁻⁷ series (a factor with no second series keeps its 10⁻¹² rate). `top_factor_stable_1e7` is whether
   `ranking[0]` is the same. This is the per-failure quantity behind "the top factor changes in at most 30% of
   cases under 10⁻⁷ perturbation".
6. **Diagnostic and forced factors.** A diagnostic factor (for example `torque_limit := off`) is labelled like
   any other and shows the class `diagnostic`; it is never offered as a repair. A `diagnostic-forced` child is
   never labelled.
7. **Wire spellings.** `"but-for"`, `"contributing"`, `"joint"`, `"alternative"`, `"precedes-snapshot"`,
   `"precision-sensitive"`, `"chaotic: unattributable"`, `"marginal"`, `"tier-specific: half-step"` (and the other
   variant tags), `"none"`. A serializer test pins each string.
8. **Layout.** `crates/frob-autopsy/src/label.rs`; determinism class D0 (a pure function of integer counts).
   Evidence class of a labelling: Estimated (an attribution drawn from contrasts), whatever the class of the
   contrasts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The shape of the session example is reproduced on a consistent count table: rescue counts 8/8, 3/8 and 0/8
      with 2 placebo flips of 64 and m = 3, where the second factor's unperturbed fork still fails (so its flip
      rate at 10⁻¹² is 3/8), give `but-for`, `contributing`, `none`; `top_factor` is the first.
- [ ] Two factors at 8/8 with no flips give `alternative` for both, no `top_factor`, and a named set of two;
      8/8 beside 7/8 (one flipped replicate, flip rate 1/8) gives `but-for` and `contributing`.
- [ ] Consistency is enforced on input: a count table in which the flip rate at 10⁻¹² is zero and the rescue
      count is neither 0 nor R is rejected as malformed (all replicates agree with the unperturbed fork, so they
      share one verdict).
- [ ] Planted joint pair (each single 0 of 8, the pair 8 of 8) gives `joint` for both members; planted
      alternative pair (each single 8 of 8) gives `alternative`.
- [ ] 13 placebo flips of 64 give `chaotic: unattributable` and every factor `none`, whatever the rescue counts.
- [ ] A factor with contrast 0.9 and one flipped replicate at 10⁻¹² is `contributing`.
- [ ] `precedes-snapshot` is produced only when an episode-start result is supplied and rescues.
- [ ] The label function is total: every input in a generated space of count tables yields exactly one outcome
      (property test), and the outcome is invariant under permutation of the factor list.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-autopsy/tests/label.rs`:
- `session_example`, `alternative_example`, `strong_and_nearly_strong` (the acceptance cases, values as stated).
- `malformed_counts_rejected`: (rescued 7/8, flip rate 0 at 10⁻¹²) and (rescued 3/8, flip rate 1/8) are both
  rejected: with replicate 0 unperturbed, the flip count must equal either the rescue count or R minus it.
- `joint_pair`, `alternative_pair` (KA-673; `synthetic` count tables).
- `chaotic_overrides_everything`: f = 13, P = 64, k = 8 of 8 → `chaotic: unattributable`.
- `unstable_but_strong_is_contributing`.
- `precision_sensitive_overrides_chaotic`; `tier_specific_names_variant`.
- `precedes_snapshot_needs_start_result`.
- `joint_needs_coalitions`: singles all `none`, coalitions not run → outcome `none` with `coalitions: not-run`.
- `ranking_tie_break`: equal contrasts ordered by flip rate at 10⁻⁷, then 10⁻¹², then path.
- `top_factor_stability`: a factor at 8/8 (10⁻¹²) and 2/8 (10⁻⁷) beside one at 6/8 and 6/8 → top changes.
- `total_and_permutation_invariant` (property, 10⁴ tables).
- `wire_spellings` (golden strings).
- `cluster_card`: 164, 38 and 12 of 200 → `but-for` (contrast 0.76) and `contributing` (0.13).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/autopsy_attribute_planted.sh` (N27b-09) and `scripts/e2e/autopsy_session.sh` (N27b-18) assert
labels from the user's side and log, per attribution, the outcome, the ranking, the named set, `top_factor` and
`top_factor_stable_1e7`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-673 (joint and alternative pairs: labels `joint` and `alternative`, with identical Shapley values (½, ½), so a
summary that reports only those values cannot tell them apart). KA-672 (the doubling map: `chaotic:
unattributable`). KA-146 (the construction's version of the same pair of cases; its text calls the either-alone
case two `but-for` causes; under the Autopsy specification's finer table each is `alternative`, and the test asserts both that
each meets the rescue criterion alone and that the label is `alternative`). CX-296, CX-297. Negative controls: a
label function that takes the factor with the largest Shapley value as `but-for` (fails the joint/alternative
pair); one that skips the chaos clause (names a factor on the doubling map); one that ignores the flip-rate
clause. Determinism: a pure function; identical on every platform.

## Considerations

- The thresholds 0.5, 0.1 and 0.2 are TARGETs measured against planted causes by GATE-AUTOPSY-03 and
  GATE-AUTOPSY-05; they live in the decision policy record, and changing one is a new policy version.
- `alternative` with a negative pair interaction is the expected signature (either factor already rescues, so
  the pair adds nothing); the interaction term is reported by the coalition bead, not interpreted here.
- If KILL-AUTOPSY fires, `joint` becomes unreachable (no coalitions) and the label set shrinks; the function
  must then never emit it, which the `joint_needs_coalitions` test already covers.
