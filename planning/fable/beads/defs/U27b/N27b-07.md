## Background

An attribution compares the outcomes of forks that differ by one intervention. It is meaningful only if the
arithmetic of the simulator is not itself a larger perturbation than the placebo. FACT (source register; to be
re-read from the primary source before a gate cites it): learned continuous-control policies in closed loop have
measured maximal Lyapunov exponents of 0.03 to 0.16 per control step on walker and cheetah tasks, and a
perturbation of 5 × 10⁻⁴ degrees in a torso angle produces significant divergence after 50 steps. The uncontrolled
systems are not chaotic; the sensitivity comes from the policy with contact. At 0.16 per step over 80 steps (2 s
at that benchmark's 25 ms control period) a perturbation grows by about 3.6 × 10⁵. Single-precision round-off of
6 × 10⁻⁸ then reaches about 2% of the state scale, enough to flip a marginal failure; double-precision round-off
of 10⁻¹⁶ stays near 4 × 10⁻¹¹.

**ADR-AUTOPSY-04. Attribution reports a flip rate at round-off scale; double precision is the default for one
failure.** Closed-loop legged control amplifies round-off enough to flip marginal outcomes in single precision
over long windows. Single precision on the originating tier is admitted only by a measured rule (the
single-precision lane, a separate Frontier mechanism). Rejected alternatives: (a) attribute on whatever tier
recorded the failure, with no measurement of sensitivity, which names causes of round-off; (b) require double
precision with no exception, which leaves a failure that exists only on the fast tier unattributable and treats
a requirement of convenience as if it were a requirement of the evidence.

The tiers: `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal` (single precision, throughput), `batch-cpu` (the CPU reference for the
batched algorithm, in `f64` or `f32`), `referee` (double precision, the most trusted). A snapshot is bound to
its numerical profile: restoring it under another profile is a comparison replay, not a continuation.

Tier Solid; crate `frob-autopsy`, layer 5; decision core DC-06; risk RISK-50.

## Scope

In: choosing where an attribution runs from the precision of the recorded failure; the three-step procedure for a
single-precision failure; the per-world decision-level equivalence check; the route record; the refusal
FROB-REF-584 and the hand-off to the artifact guard; the `double_precision` field that `run.fork` reports; the
reporting duties of every attribution (flip rates at both scales, growth of placebo separation).
Out: the single-precision lane itself (admission N27b-23, execution N27b-24, numerical-variant interventions
N27b-25); the licence check on determinism class at admission (FROB-REF-562, the determinism-licence bead
BD-269); the population-level equivalence test used for promotion (BD-177 supplies the per-decision comparison
this bead calls).

## Technical approach

1. **Default.** Attribution runs on `batch-cpu` in double precision, or on the referee. A failure recorded there
   needs no routing: the forks run on the recording profile, and the null fork must be bitwise.
2. **A failure recorded on a single-precision tier** goes through three steps.
   1. *Null fork on the originating backend.* Replay from the checkpoint with the empty intervention and require
      identical digests (INV-AUTOPSY-01). A mismatch ends everything with FROB-REF-583.
   2. *The same checkpoint in double precision on `batch-cpu`.* The checkpoint is widened exactly and run to the
      fork horizon as a comparison replay. The per-world decision-level equivalence check compares it with the
      recorded failure:
      | Compared | Agreement means |
      |---|---|
      | Requirement verdict | Both `violated` |
      | Failure class | The same class (for example `fall`, `slip`, `thermal-limit`) |
      | Binding atom | The same requirement atom |
      | Failure time | The difference lies inside the fidelity ledger's recorded envelope for "time of first failure" of the stratum (for example a fall at 11.31 s against 11.30 s) |
      A double-precision run that ends in a numerical outcome, or a stratum with no recorded envelope, makes the
      check `unresolved`, which is treated as *not equivalent* (fail-closed) with the reason recorded.
      The double-precision run is then repeated once: the two double-precision runs must have identical digests,
      which is the null-fork property on the attribution profile.
   3. *Route.* Equivalent: attribute in double precision; the forks start from the widened checkpoint and the
      double-precision run is their parent. Not equivalent: the failure goes to the single-precision lane with
      the numerical-variant interventions first. Only if that lane does not admit it is the failure labelled
      `precision-sensitive`, attribution refused (FROB-REF-584) and the world passed to the artifact guard.
3. **Route record (DESIGN).**
   ```rust
   pub enum PrecisionRoute {
       Native { tier, precision },                                 // recorded in f64; nothing to do
       F64 { equivalence: Equivalence, parent_f64: EpisodeId },    // step 3, equivalent
       Lane { reason: NotEquivalent, admission: LaneAdmission },   // handed to the lane
       PrecisionSensitive { reason: NotEquivalent, lane: LaneAdmission /* not admitted */ },
   }
   pub struct Equivalence { verdict: bool, class: bool, atom: bool, time_delta: Qty, envelope: Qty,
                            status: Equivalent | NotEquivalent | Unresolved(Reason) }
   ```
   The route is stored with the fork handle and printed by `run.fork` as `"double_precision":
   "decision-equivalent"`, `"not-equivalent"` or `"unresolved"`, and by every attribution as `tier` and
   `precision`.
4. **FROB-REF-584** (attribution requested for a failure that neither lane admits, or under an uncontrolled
   determinism class). Raised when the route is `PrecisionSensitive`, and when a fork set that an attribution
   would read was itself run under class D4 or an unrecorded class (for example a child whose step plan includes
   a numerical variant with no current determinism record). Envelope: `pointer` = the fork; `observed` = the
   equivalence table of step 2 and the lane's admission figures (ρ₀ at 10⁻⁷, G(W)·u, the windows tried);
   `required` = "decision-equivalent in double precision, or admitted to the single-precision lane";
   `affected_claims` = the attribution; `retry: after-change`; exit 3. Ranked repairs: (1) fork closer to the
   failure (`frob run fork <run> --world <w> --before failure --dt 0.5s`, then 0.25 s) and try again; (2) read
   the artifact guard's verdict for the world, since a failure that double precision does not reproduce is an
   artifact candidate; (3) re-record the episode from its start on `batch-cpu` in double precision and attribute
   the failure there if it occurs; (4) use the first-anomaly ordering of `frob explain run`, labelled
   `Estimated`.
5. **Hand-off.** On `PrecisionSensitive` the world is submitted to the artifact guard as a failing run; the
   guard's label (`artifact-candidate` with the variant that removed the failure, or `Passed`) is attached to
   the refusal as `observed.guard`, and the guard writes its own record of a tier-sensitive case.
6. **What every attribution reports**, so that a reader sees how far the result is from the regime where nothing
   can be attributed: the flip rate of each intervention at 10⁻¹² and at 10⁻⁷; the placebo flip rate; and the
   growth of the separation between the placebo forks and the parent over the fork horizon.
7. **Layout.** `crates/frob-autopsy/src/precision.rs`. Determinism: the comparison is a decision-level
   comparison between two profiles (class D3 in kind), each side replayable under its own class D2. Evidence of
   the route record: Tested.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A failure recorded on `batch-cpu` in `f64` routes `Native` and runs no comparison replay.
- [ ] A single-precision failure that double precision reproduces routes `F64`, with the four comparisons and the
      time difference against its envelope in the record; two double-precision runs have identical digests.
- [ ] Each of the four ways of differing (verdict, class, atom, time outside the envelope) routes away from
      `F64`; a numerical outcome or a missing envelope gives `unresolved` and is treated as not equivalent.
- [ ] KA-679: a marginal failure built to flip under a 10⁻⁷ perturbation and not under 10⁻¹², taken from a
      single-precision run, ends `precision-sensitive` with FROB-REF-584 once the lane does not admit it; the
      same failure with a wide margin attributes normally in double precision.
- [ ] FROB-REF-584 carries the fields of step 4, is reachable through `frob autopsy attribute --json` and
      `frob autopsy intervene --json`, and the refused world has an artifact-guard record.
- [ ] Until the single-precision lane exists, a not-equivalent failure is refused with FROB-REF-584 directly and
      the refusal says the lane is not built in this build.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-679 with its control (the same failure with a wide margin attributes normally). The arithmetic of the
Background as checked values: e^(0.16·80) = 3.62 × 10⁵; 6 × 10⁻⁸ × 3.62 × 10⁵ = 0.0217; 10⁻¹⁶ × 3.62 × 10⁵ =
3.6 × 10⁻¹¹. Negative controls: a route that compares final states within a tolerance instead of decisions (the
smallest case: one run ending at 1.0000001 m and the other at 0.9999999 m against x ≥ 1 m have a state error of
2 × 10⁻⁷ m and different verdicts; the test must route away from `F64`); a route that treats `unresolved` as
equivalent. Determinism lanes: the route record is identical twice on one machine and across worker counts; the
double-precision parent is bitwise on repeat (G5).

## Considerations

- The check is per world, on one failure. It is not the population test used to promote a tier (which bounds
  the rate of discordant decisions over hundreds of worlds); it borrows that test's notion of a decision and of
  the failure-time envelope.
- A failure reproduced in double precision can still be marginal: its 10⁻¹² placebo may be clean while its 10⁻⁷
  series flips. That is why the 10⁻⁷ series is reported in every attribution, and why the label `marginal`
  exists.
- If KILL-ENSEMBLE fires, step 3 reverts to the decision as first written: attribution in double precision
  only, and a failure not reproduced there goes to the artifact guard unattributed.
- OPEN-AUTOPSY-3 (owner: Cortex lead; blocks nothing): the Lyapunov exponents of our own controllers and
  policies on our tiers are unmeasured. Default: fork leads of at most 2 s, with the growth of placebo
  separation reported in every attribution.
