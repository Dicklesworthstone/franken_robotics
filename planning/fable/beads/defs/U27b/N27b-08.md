## Background

An attribution method is judged on failures whose cause is known because it was put there. The known answer of
the Autopsy specification (KA-670): **planted single causes: 200 failures in five classes (friction drop, torque cap, payload
shift, latency, terrain step), ten candidate factors each. The planted factor ranks first with label `but-for`.
Negative control: a failure planted through a factor outside the list names no factor.** The same suite is read
by the gate that decides whether Autopsy's coalition machinery survives (GATE-AUTOPSY-03, with KILL-AUTOPSY), by
the gate of the single-precision lane, and at M4 by the comparison of agents with and without Autopsy.

The planted causes are part of the design corpus: about 200 designs with labels fixed before any analysis is
tuned, planted defects, planted winners for the screening audit and planted single causes for attribution. This
bead does not invent labels. It turns the corpus entries into recorded failing episodes with fork handles and a
manifest, checks by an independent route that each plant really is a single cause, and provides the harness that
any attribution arm is run through. The independence matters: an oracle is never regenerated from the candidate,
so the label "this factor is the cause" comes from construction plus from-the-start runs, never from forks.

Tier Solid. Crates `frob-autopsy` (harness) and `frob-conformance` (suite manifest). Serves BET-23 and DC-06.

## Scope

In: the suite manifest; realization of each planted failure as a recorded episode on the tiers that need it; the
single-cause check; the ten-candidate list per failure; the out-of-list control set; the harness API with its
arms (full table, one-at-a-time, heuristic ordering); the stability protocols; the suite report.
Out: the corpus entries and their label file (BD-248); attribution itself (BD-280); the script that records the
gate (N27b-09); the planted joint, alternative, chaotic and no-cause sets of the coalition and abstention gates
(seed beads BD-281 and BD-282); the pilot at paper scale (N27b-10).

## Technical approach

1. **Manifest** (schema `frob.autopsy.planted/1`, DESIGN; `frob-conformance/registry/autopsy_planted.toml`).
   Per failure: identifier; corpus entry and label-file hash; design, controller or policy, task, scene, world
   address; cause class; the planted change as a canonical `do(…)` string in the direction that *plants* it and
   its inverse (the rescuing intervention); margin stratum; the ten candidate factors, of which exactly one is
   the inverse of the plant; the fork lead (1.0 s by default); expected failure class and binding atom.
2. **The five classes** (40 failures each; magnitudes are examples of the kind, the values are the corpus's):
   | Class | The plant | Rescuing intervention | Typical decoys |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Friction drop | A floor patch on the route lowered, for example 0.8 → 0.25 | `scene./…/friction := nominal` | Friction of patches off the route; a film elsewhere |
   | Torque cap | One joint's torque limit lowered, for example to 60% | `model.joint[<j>].torque_limit := nominal` | Limits of other joints; speed limits |
   | Payload shift | A payload displaced laterally or shifted at a stated time | `disturbance.payload_shift := 0` | Pushes at other times; payload mass |
   | Latency | A sensor latency raised, for example IMU 0 → 40 ms | `sense.imu.latency := nominal` | Latency of other sensors; noise levels |
   | Terrain step | A step or sill raised, for example by 30 mm | `scene./…/step_height := nominal` | Other geometry on and off the route |
   Each list of ten holds the rescuer and nine decoys: at least two of the same class at another place or joint,
   and at least one from each other class, so a method that names "the friction factor" by class alone is wrong.
3. **Realization.** For each failure the harness runs the planted world from its start on `batch-cpu` in double
   precision under the replay profile and records it; for the single-precision lane it also records it on each
   available single-precision tier. It then makes the fork handle (`run.fork --before failure --dt 1.0s`).
   Realization is cached by content address; the suite is built once per engine identity.
4. **The single-cause check** (independent of forks). From the episode start on the same world address:
   (a) the baseline world without the plant passes on at least 60 of 64 paired seeds; (b) the planted world
   fails with the expected class and atom on at least 60 of 64; (c) the planted world with each decoy alone set
   to its rescuing value still fails on at least 48 of 64. A plant that does not meet (a) to (c) is excluded with
   its reason, the exclusion is counted, and the suite is admitted for the gate only with at least 200 failures
   and all five classes at 40 (a short suite is a refusal to run the gate, never a silent denominator change).
5. **The out-of-list control.** A further 20 failures (four per class) whose candidate list omits the rescuer and
   holds ten decoys. Expected: no factor named.
6. **Harness API (DESIGN).**
   ```rust
   pub trait AttributionArm { fn attribute(&self, case: &PlantedCase) -> ArmResult; }   // top factor or abstain
   pub struct SuiteReport { per_class: [ClassReport; 5], accuracy: Ratio, abstained: u32, wrong: Vec<Confusion>,
                            top_change_rate_1e7: Ratio, oat_agreement: Option<Ratio>, control_named: u32 }
   ```
   Arms provided: `FullTable` (singles, pairs when available, 8 replicates, 64 placebo forks), `OneAtATime`
   (ten unperturbed forks, one per factor; its top factor is the single rescued factor, or none if zero or
   several rescue), `Heuristic` (the first-anomaly ordering mapped to the candidate nearest in mechanism).
   *Accuracy* = cases whose rank-one factor is the rescuer with a label other than `none`, over all cases; an
   abstention counts as not correct.
7. **Stability protocols.** `series` (product gate): a case's top factor *changes* when the ranking recomputed
   from the 10⁻⁷ series has a different rank-one factor. `snapshots:k` (pilot protocol): the whole attribution
   is repeated from k checkpoints perturbed at 10⁻⁷ (and at 10⁻¹²); a (case, perturbation) pair counts as changed
   when its rank-one factor differs from the unperturbed one; the rate is over all pairs, and the per-case rate
   (any change) is reported beside it. The protocol is named in every report.
8. **Layout.** `crates/frob-autopsy/src/planted/` (harness), `crates/frob-conformance/registry/` (manifest),
   `crates/frob-autopsy/tests/planted_suite.rs`. Determinism: realization under class D2; the manifest and
   reports are exact. Nothing in the suite is a private scene.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The manifest holds 200 admitted failures, 40 per class, each with ten candidates of which exactly one is
      the rescuer, and 20 out-of-list controls; the label-file hash in the manifest equals the corpus's committed
      hash.
- [ ] Every admitted failure passes the single-cause check (a) to (c); exclusions are listed with reasons.
- [ ] Every failure has a fork handle with `null_replay: bitwise` on its recording tier.
- [ ] The harness runs the three arms and prints accuracy, abstentions, the confusion list, the top-change rate
      under both stability protocols and the one-at-a-time agreement.
- [ ] Potency of the harness: an arm that always names the rescuer scores 200 of 200; an arm that names a random
      candidate scores about 20 of 200 (between 8 and 35); an arm that names the first decoy scores 0.
- [ ] A suite with fewer than 200 admitted failures refuses to produce a gate figure.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-autopsy/tests/planted_suite.rs`:
- `manifest_well_formed`: counts, one rescuer per list, class mix of decoys, hash equality.
- `single_cause_check_catches_double_plant` (`synthetic` manifest row with two plants): excluded under (c).
- `single_cause_check_catches_weak_plant`: a plant failing on 40 of 64 seeds is excluded under (b).
- `harness_oracle_arm_scores_all`, `harness_random_arm_scores_near_chance`, `harness_decoy_arm_scores_zero`.
- `abstention_counts_as_incorrect`.
- `stability_series_protocol` and `stability_snapshots_protocol` on a `synthetic` case whose top factor flips
  under 10⁻⁷ in 3 of 20 snapshots → 0.15 over pairs.
- `short_suite_refuses_gate`.
- `out_of_list_control_shape`: no control list contains its rescuer.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/autopsy_attribute_planted.sh` (N27b-09) realizes the suite if absent and logs per failure: class,
stratum, the three single-cause counts, the fork handle, and per arm the top factor, label and correctness.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-670 and KA-148. The out-of-list set is the registered negative control of the gate. Further planted defects the
harness must expose: a label file edited after the hash was committed (hash mismatch stops the run); a candidate
list that leaks the answer by position (the harness shuffles candidates by a keyed permutation and the oracle arm
is the only one allowed to read the label). Metamorphic: permuting a candidate list permutes results and changes
no accuracy. Determinism: the suite's recorded digests are identical on rebuild at one engine identity.

## Considerations

- Margin strata are frozen in the corpus before any attribution is tuned. The gate reads the overall accuracy;
  the report prints it per stratum, so a method that only works on wide-margin failures is visible.
- The suite uses one robot class by default (the reference quadruped under its baseline controller and under
  the trained velocity policy). Extending it to wheeled robots is cheap once the corpus has the entries.
- The same suite is read again by the single-precision lane and by the agent comparison at M4; it must not be
  tuned between those readings. Any change to the manifest is a new suite identity.
- 200 failures × (689 forks of about two seconds) is about 2.8 × 10⁵ world-seconds for the full-table arm:
  minutes to an hour on `batch-cpu`.
