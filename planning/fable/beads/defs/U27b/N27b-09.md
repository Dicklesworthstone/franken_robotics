## Background

GATE-AUTOPSY-03 is the gate on which Autopsy's central bet (BET-23: interventional autopsy is stable enough to
act on) is read at M3, and whose failure triggers the kill gate KILL-AUTOPSY. Its criterion (claim: Known
answer; thresholds are TARGETs until measured):

> Planted single causes: the planted factor ranks first in at least 90% of 200 failures in double precision, and
> in the single-precision lane for windows of at most 1 s, and the top factor changes in at most 30% of cases
> under 10⁻⁷ perturbation; the agreement between ten one-at-a-time forks and the full coalition table is
> recorded.

The clause about the single-precision lane was added to the gate when the lane was introduced. A gate passes
only with a recorded measurement against its threshold, and every convergence gate has a registered negative
control that it catches; here the control is a failure planted through a factor outside the candidate list,
which must name no factor. This bead is the script that measures the gate from the user's side, on the real
binary, and writes the record. Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/autopsy_attribute_planted.sh`; the three double-precision figures; the one-at-a-time agreement
record; joining the lane clause measured by the lane's own script; the negative control; the gate row; the
statement of what counts as one repair cycle.
Out: the suite (N27b-08); the verbs (BD-279, BD-280); the coalition table (BD-281); the lane's measurement
(N27b-28); the kill decision (N27b-11); the abstention gate on chaotic and no-cause failures (seed bead BD-282).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** The planted suite of 200 failures and 20 out-of-list controls, realized on `batch-cpu` in double
   precision; seeds and world addresses come from the manifest. The script realizes the suite if it is absent.
2. **Per failure** (all commands with `--json`, idempotency keys derived from the script name, the step and the
   failure identifier):
   1. `frob run fork <run> --world <w> --before failure --dt 1.0s` → the fork handle; assert `null_replay:
      "bitwise"`.
   2. `frob autopsy intervene <fork> --placebo 64 --replicates 8 --coalitions pairs --do <c1> … --do <c10>`
      (ten candidates: 55 coalitions; 1 + 64 + 440 = 505 forks before the second series).
   3. `frob autopsy attribute <fork>` → the record; read `ranking[0]`, its label, `top_factor_stable_1e7`, the
      placebo rate.
   4. The one-at-a-time arm: `frob autopsy intervene <fork> --placebo 0 --replicates 1 --coalitions none` with
      the same ten candidates under a second idempotency key (ten forks), then `frob autopsy attribute`; its top
      factor is the single rescued factor, or none.
3. **Figures.**
   - *Accuracy* = failures whose `ranking[0]` is the planted rescuer with a label other than `none`, over 200.
     Threshold: at least 180.
   - *Top-factor change at 10⁻⁷* = failures with `top_factor_stable_1e7 = false`, over the failures that have a
     top factor at 10⁻¹². Threshold: at most 30%.
   - *One-at-a-time agreement* = failures where the ten-fork arm and the full table name the same top factor (or
     both name none), over 200. Recorded with a one-sided 95% exact lower bound; no threshold. This figure
     answers whether ten forks answer as well as several hundred.
   - *Negative control*: the 20 out-of-list failures; factors named must be 0 of 20.
   - *Lane clause*: read from the latest record of the lane script (N27b-28): accuracy of the lane on the same
     suite for windows of at most 1 s, threshold 90%. If the lane's kill gate has fired, the clause is recorded
     as `not applicable: KILL-ENSEMBLE` with the ADR's identifier. If the lane has not been measured yet, the
     clause is `pending`.
4. **Verdict and record.** The double-precision clauses hold when accuracy ≥ 180/200, change rate ≤ 30%, the
   control names nothing and the agreement is recorded. The gate's status is `pass` only when those hold and the
   lane clause is `pass` or `not applicable`; with the lane clause `pending` the measured values are written and
   the status stays `not run` until the lane script completes the row; any failed clause makes it `fail`. The
   kill decision reads the double-precision figures and does not wait for the lane. The row written to the gate
   ledger holds the measured values, the commit, the hardware fingerprint, the seeds, the suite identity and
   label-file hash, the claim identifiers of the 200 attributions, and the receipt hash of this script's log.
5. **Repair cycle.** A first `fail` opens exactly one repair cycle: defects found in the implementation may be
   fixed and the script re-run once on the unchanged suite. Thresholds, the suite and the candidate lists may not
   change inside the cycle. The second result is final and is what the kill decision reads.
6. **Self-test mode.** `--self-test` runs eight failures and two controls for use on every change, without
   writing a gate row.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script produces the five figures with their denominators and writes one gate row per run.
- [ ] The negative control is exercised on every run and the row states that it was caught (0 of 20 named).
- [ ] A planted regression is caught by the script: with a build flag that makes the attribution skip the
      placebo subtraction, the control names factors and the script fails; with a flag that randomizes the
      ranking, accuracy falls below 180 and the script fails.
- [ ] Re-running the script returns stored answers for every `run` verb and identical figures.
- [ ] The script exits non-zero on `fail`, and the row's status is `fail`, never absent.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own beyond `--self-test`; the figures' arithmetic is covered by the harness tests of the suite.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/autopsy_attribute_planted.sh`. Log `target/e2e-logs/autopsy_attribute_planted/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript beside it. Every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N27b-09`), `step`, `event`, the exact command,
exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). Per
failure, `metric` lines: `forks_run`; `placebo_rate` (threshold 0.2); for each candidate `rescued`, `contrast`,
`flip_rate_1e-12`, `flip_rate_1e-7`, `p_value` and `level`; `rank_one`, `rank_one_label`, `planted`, `correct`;
`top_factor_stable_1e7`; `oat_top`, `oat_agrees`. `assert` lines for the null replay and the schema. On an
incorrect case: the planted factor, the named factor, both rows of the table and the fork and ledger identifiers
needed to replay it. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `accuracy` with threshold 0.90;
`top_change_rate` with threshold 0.30; `oat_agreement` with its lower bound; `control_named` with threshold 0;
`lane_clause`; gates touched (GATE-AUTOPSY-03). No scene content is logged; the suite is generated.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-670 (the planted factor ranks first with label `but-for`; at least 90%); its control (a factor outside the
list names nothing). KA-148. Planted failures of the *script* (run with `--negative-controls`): the two build
flags of the acceptance criteria; a suite manifest whose label hash does not match (the script must stop before
running a fork). Determinism lanes: the script is run twice on one machine and at two worker counts; the 200
records are byte-identical across the lanes (each is integer arithmetic over forks of class D2).

## Considerations

- Cost: 200 × 505 forks of about two seconds, plus second series and the ten-fork arm, is about 2.1 × 10⁵
  world-seconds: a few minutes to half an hour on `batch-cpu` at 100 to 1,000 world-seconds per second.
- OPEN-AUTOPSY-2 (whether one-at-a-time contrasts suffice, making pairs optional) is decided from the agreement
  figure this script records; the decision itself is a separate bead (N27b-13).
- If KILL-AUTOPSY fires, this script is kept as the regression test of the fallback: the same suite read by
  single-factor paired re-runs from the episode start.
- The gate measures accuracy on planted causes. It says nothing about how often real failures have a single
  removable cause; the share of reference failures with a placebo rate above 0.2 is the separate falsifier of
  the bet and is reported by the abstention gate.
