## Background

The whole point of Autopsy for an agent is one short session: **read the heuristic, fork, intervene, attribute,
re-run**, then let a study decide. Each move is one verb with a bounded output and a `next_actions` list that
names the next move; the session is recorded as a transcript (`frob.autopsy.transcript/1`) that replays every
fork from its address. The program's demonstration of this is a quadruped carrying a tray across a generated
kitchen with a wet patch on the floor, falling at a slip, and being debugged in six calls.

This bead is the end-to-end script that runs that session on the real binary, on a failure whose cause is
planted (so the right answer is known), and then checks the two conditions the workflow's seed bead closes on:
*a recorded transcript replays to the same repair*, and *citing an attribution as established is refused
(FROB-REF-880)*. It is one script per capability, not per mechanism: the capability is the session. Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/autopsy_session.sh`: the planted kitchen failure, the seven calls, the assertions on shape and
content, the transcript replay and comparison, the citation refusal, the surface-parity leg (CLI against MCP),
the ghost manifest check, and the logs.
Out: the 200-failure accuracy gate (N27b-09); the eight-refusal sweep (N27b-19); the agent comparison at M4 that
measures repair turns (seed bead BD-375), which reuses this script's fixture.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixture (generated, seed-explicit, not private).** Scene: the kitchen archetype at scene seed 20270317 with
   one film patch `galley-2` on the route (film `grease`), all else dry. Robot: the reference quadruped template
   with a tray payload under its baseline controller. Task `kitchen-tray`: carry the tray across; requirement
   atoms `upright` and `delivered`. The plant: the film patch. Planted-cause check (from the episode start, on
   64 paired seeds): with the film the candidate fails on at least 60; with the patch dry it passes on at least
   60. The recorded evaluation is the film arm of those 64 paired seeds, recorded on a single-precision tier
   where one is present and on `batch-cpu` otherwise. The script picks the first world in address order that
   fails with the film and passes with the patch dry in the paired check (a world whose failure the plant
   causes, so that `but-for` is the expected label at the fork).
2. **The session** (every command with `--json`; every `run` call with `--key sess-<step>`):
   | Step | Command | Asserted |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | `frob explain run <run> --budget 600` | Bounded output; a first anomaly labelled `Estimated`; `next_actions` offers `frob run fork` |
   | 2 | `frob run fork <run> --world <w> --before failure --dt 1.0s` | A fork handle; class `continuation`; `null_replay: "bitwise"`; on a single-precision tier `double_precision: "decision-equivalent"` |
   | 3 | `frob autopsy intervene <fork> --placebo 64 --replicates 8 --coalitions pairs --do 'scene./floor/patch[galley-2]/film := dry' --do 'model.joint[*.knee].torque_limit := off' --do 'sense.imu.latency := 0 s' --do 'model.joint[*.knee].gear_ratio := 16'` | Exit 2; three interventions ran; `gear_ratio` refused with FROB-REF-581, `earliest_valid_fork_tick: 0`, a non-empty `forced_by`; `forks_run` = 113 + 8 per coalition that changed the outcome |
   | 4 | `frob autopsy attribute <fork>` | Schema `frob.autopsy.attribution/1`; `null_replay: "bitwise"`; placebo rate at most 0.2; the film factor is `ranking[0]` with label `but-for`; latency is `none`; `evidence.class = "Estimated"` with the fixed scope text; `next_actions` contains an `autopsy rerun` for the gear edit |
   | 5 | `frob autopsy repair <fork>` | `repair_sets` = [[film := dry]], labelled 1-minimal; no diagnostic factor in a repair set; `forks_bought = 0` |
   | 6 | `frob autopsy rerun <run> --edit 'regear *.knee 16' --worlds failing,sample:<k>`, with k = min(64, passing worlds of the recorded run) computed by the script (`--worlds failing` alone when k = 0) | A new design identity with its own reset cache; paired counts on the same world addresses for the failing worlds and for exactly k sampled passing worlds; the placebo re-run's rate; label and class `Estimated` |
   | 7 | `frob eval compare <design> <design>@regear --task kitchen-tray` | A study verdict (`settled`, `tied` or `inconclusive`); the script asserts the verdict's presence and type, not its direction |
   The numbers of the program's illustrative transcript (3 of 8, 17 of 23, 63 of 64) are not asserted; the shape
   is.
3. **Transcript.** `frob autopsy show <session> --format full` validates against the schema and has one entry
   per step in order; `--budget 600` returns one line per move within budget.
4. **Replay to the same repair.** `frob autopsy show <session> --format script > $TMP/replay.sh`; the script is
   executed in namespace `replay-<utc>` against an empty store for forks (nothing copied);
   `frob autopsy show <session> --compare <replayed session>` must return `identical: true` for the fork,
   intervene, attribute and repair entries, and the two repair sets must be equal.
5. **Citation.** Through the report path, the session's attribution is cited as an established cause, and then
   as a cause in the physical robot; both must be refused with FROB-REF-880, exit 3, with the three ranked
   repairs; citing it as described prints the permitted wording.
6. **Surface parity.** Steps 2 to 5 are repeated through `frob-mcp` with fresh idempotency keys (the same
   request content under a second key denotes the same intervention set and buys no fork: its children are
   read by address, so `forks_run`, the `ledger` identifier and the whole `result` are equal, BD-279), each tool in a
   task cluster that holds it (NJL-05): `run_fork` after `session_focus {cluster: "repair"}`;
   `autopsy_intervene` and `autopsy_attribute` after `session_focus {cluster: "diagnose"}`; `autopsy_repair`
   after `session_focus {cluster: "repair"}`. Each envelope's `result` digest equals the CLI's.
7. **Ghost manifest.** `frob autopsy show <session> --format ghost` validates; the null ghost's deviation from
   the parent is exactly zero.
8. **Fallback toolset.** `--toolset fallback` (used if the kill gate fires) skips steps 3 to 5's coalition parts
   and asserts the single-factor path: `explain`, `rerun` per candidate edit, `compare`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All seven steps return the asserted shape; the planted film factor is named `but-for` and is the single
      repair set.
- [ ] The gear-ratio intervention is refused at the fork and tested from the episode start.
- [ ] The replayed transcript is identical for the four compared moves and yields the same repair.
- [ ] Both citation roles are refused with FROB-REF-880.
- [ ] CLI and MCP agree on every compared envelope.
- [ ] The script is idempotent (a second run returns stored answers and passes), seed-explicit, exits non-zero on
      any failed assertion, and writes its log and transcript.
- [ ] Total serial calls for the diagnosis (steps 1 to 5) is five; each `next_actions` list names the command the
      script issues next (asserted by string match on the verb).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own. `--self-test` runs steps 1 to 5 on a reduced fixture (the split-grip landing of fixture 2 with
its low-grip strip as the planted cause) in under three minutes.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/autopsy_session.sh`. Log `target/e2e-logs/autopsy_session/<utc>.ndjson` with a human-readable
transcript beside it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N27b-18`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`,
`metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command, exit code, wall time, and the envelope's evidence block (claim,
class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). `metric` lines: `placebo_rate` (threshold 0.2); per factor
`rescued`, `contrast`, both flip rates and the label; `forks_run` against the plan; `forks_bought` by repair
(threshold 0); the paired counts of the re-run with `sampled_passing` against k and the re-run's placebo
rate; `show_tokens` against the budget; `replay_identical`;
`mcp_cli_digest_equal`; `null_ghost_max_deviation` (threshold 0). `refusal` lines: code, pointer, the fields of
FROB-REF-581 and FROB-REF-880. On failure: the first differing transcript entry and field, both digests, and the
fork, ledger and session identifiers needed to reproduce. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed; gates
touched: none (the session is a capability test; its fixture feeds GATE-AUTOPSY-08 at M4). The kitchen is
generated; no private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
The planted cause is the known answer: the film patch is the rescuer; the IMU latency is a decoy that must be
`none`. Negative controls (run with `--negative-controls`, each expected to make an assertion fail): the patch
dry in the recorded run (no failure to debug: the script must stop at step 1 with "nothing failed", not invent a
cause); a replay store in which one fork's verdict is altered (the comparison must name the entry); a build flag
that lets `gear_ratio` through at the fork. Determinism lanes: the session twice on one machine; with 1 and 16
workers; CLI against MCP; on `batch-cpu` across the two platforms.

## Considerations

- Cost: one evaluation of 64 worlds (the film arm; its dry arm is the planted-cause check), 113 to 161 forks of
  about two seconds, one paired re-run from the start of at most the 64 recorded worlds (at least 60 failing and
  the few that passed) with its placebo re-run of the failing ones, one small study: minutes on any tier.
- With the film failing on at least 60 of 64 seeds, few passing worlds exist, so the script asks for exactly
  the k that exist; the default `sample:64` of the re-run verb with fewer passing worlds than asked is the
  re-run verb's own case (BD-283), not exercised here.
- The script asserts that a study is *reached*, not what it concludes; a design verdict from this fixture would
  be an accident of one scene.
- This script is the fixture of the M4 agent comparison (agents with Autopsy against agents with logs and
  `explain` alone on the planted-defect designs); it must not be tuned to make any agent arm look better, and a
  change to its fixture is a new fixture identity.
- The kitchen scene needs the M3 scene generator; until then `--self-test` on fixture 2 is the regression test.
