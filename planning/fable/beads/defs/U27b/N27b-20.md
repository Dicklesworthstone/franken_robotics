## Background

Autopsy states six invariants and three proof obligations. Each is already tested where its mechanism is built.
This bead exists because invariants are properties of *artifacts*, and a property tested only inside the module
that is supposed to maintain it is tested by the party with the least reason to find a fault. It builds one
auditor that reads any fork ledger, attribution, repair result and transcript the system has produced and checks
all six invariants on them, a set of mutants the auditor must reject, and independent cross-checks of the three
proof obligations, one of which requires a written derivation: that file is written with the first-divergence
gate at M2 (N27a-09) and is checked and cited here.

The invariants:

- **INV-AUTOPSY-01.** The empty intervention replays its parent bit for bit on the originating profile;
  otherwise nothing is attributed.
- **INV-AUTOPSY-02.** Every intervention changes one declared mechanism, and its model difference is stored with
  the fork.
- **INV-AUTOPSY-03.** No structural or design-parameter intervention starts from a mid-episode state, except in
  the labelled diagnostic mode.
- **INV-AUTOPSY-04.** A fork never mutates its parent; a child has its own address and shares the prefix by
  reference.
- **INV-AUTOPSY-05.** A factor is named only when its contrast is tested above the placebo at a stated level and
  the placebo flip rate is at most 0.2.
- **INV-AUTOPSY-06.** Every attribution carries its scope: the model, the tier, the precision and the factor
  list.

The proof obligations:

| Obligation | Statement | Class sought |
|---|---|---|
| PO-AUTOPSY-01 | The Shapley summary satisfies efficiency, the dummy property and symmetry on the computed coalition table | Tested in exact rational arithmetic on integer counts |
| PO-AUTOPSY-02 | `first_divergence` in exact mode returns the least tick at which the prefixes differ | Tested with planted divergences; the monotonicity argument is on file in `docs/derivations/` |
| PO-AUTOPSY-03 | In tolerance mode, an interval reported as agreeing has a true maximum scaled error of at most 1 | Tested with outward-rounded block bounds |

Tier Solid. Crates `frob-autopsy` (auditor) and `frob-conformance` (suite registration). A gate that nothing can
fail validates nothing; hence the mutants.

## Scope

In: `audit::check(artifacts) → Vec<Violation>`; the mutant set; independent cross-checks for the three
obligations (a second Shapley routine; a linear scan against the divergence search on synthetic traces);
the check that the derivation file for PO-AUTOPSY-02 exists, states its collision gap and has an executable
counterexample; registration of each invariant and obligation in the conformance suite with its negative
control; a hook that runs the auditor over every artifact the end-to-end scripts produce (the helper library
`scripts/e2e/lib/autopsy.sh` with `autopsy_audit`, and the example target `autopsy_audit` it calls).
Out: maintaining the invariants (the mechanism beads); implementing Shapley values and their own law tests
(BD-281, N27a-11) and the divergence search (BD-206); the M2 gate battery of the divergence search on real runs
with fault injection, its property tests of PO-AUTOPSY-02 and PO-AUTOPSY-03, and the derivation file
`docs/derivations/first-divergence-monotonicity.md` itself (N27a-09, GATE-AUTOPSY-07).

## Technical approach

1. **The auditor.** Input: a fork ledger, and optionally the attribution, repair result and transcript that cite
   it, with read access to the store. Output: violations, each with the invariant, the artifact, the row and the
   observed value. Checks:
   | Invariant | Check on the artifact |
   |---|---|
   | 01 | The ledger's null row has a final chain digest equal to the parent's at the same tick, and the auditor recomputes that by replaying the null fork from its address; if they differ, any attribution citing the ledger is a violation |
   | 02 | Every row's change set has a stored model difference; recomputing the difference from the parent identities gives the stored hash; its mechanism set equals the declared mechanism (a coalition: the union of its members') |
   | 03 | Every row whose class is structural or design-parameter has fork tick 0, or carries the label `diagnostic-forced`; no `diagnostic-forced` row is cited by a label or a repair set |
   | 04 | The parent's chunk hashes at audit time equal those recorded in the ledger header; every child address is the parent address extended by a fork path and differs from it; the child's prefix chunks are the parent's (same content hashes, no second copy stored) |
   | 05 | For every factor with a label other than `none`: recompute the exact test from the ledger's counts and confirm it passes at the level recorded for its family (a single factor: the level divided by the header's `family_size`, which the auditor recomputes as the number of distinct single factors in the fork's intervention sets up to that one, BD-279; a member of a `joint` coalition: the coalition's test at the level divided by the number of admissible coalitions of its size, as the coalition table records), and that 5·flips ≤ placebo forks. For a ledger whose header declares the sequential test (N27bp2-01): recompute E_n = ∏(1 + ½·(X_i − Y_i)) round by round from the stored pairs and confirm that a factor recorded as tested crossed m/α at its recorded round and not before, and that a factor still open at the stop is not named |
   | 06 | The attribution, repair result and transcript each carry a scope whose model, tier and precision equal the ledger's and whose factor list equals the declared list |
   The auditor recomputes from counts and addresses; it calls none of the code paths that produced the labels
   (it has its own thirty-line exact test), so a shared defect cannot hide.
2. **Mutants** (each a real artifact altered by a test helper; the auditor must report exactly the stated
   violation): a null row with one bit of its digest flipped (01); a row without a model difference, and a row
   whose difference touches a second mechanism (02); a gear-ratio row at a mid-episode tick without the label
   (03); a parent chunk rewritten after the fork, and a child whose prefix was copied (04); a factor labelled
   `contributing` at 3 of 8 against 2 of 64 with twelve factors (p = 0.0084 above the level 0.0042), a
   `but-for` with 13 placebo flips of 64, a sequential ledger whose single factor is recorded as closed at
   round 7 when its eight clean pairs give E_7 = 1.5⁷ ≈ 17.1 < 20 = m/α, and a third one-factor set on a fork
   whose header says `family_size: 1` where the fork's sets hold three distinct factors (05); an attribution
   whose scope omits the precision (06).
3. **PO-AUTOPSY-01 cross-check.** An independent exact-rational Shapley routine (direct evaluation of
   φᵢ = Σ over S ⊆ N∖{i} of |S|!·(m − |S| − 1)!/m! · [v(S ∪ {i}) − v(S)] over integer rescue counts with a common
   denominator) is compared with the summary's values on every coalition table the suite produces with m ≤ 5,
   and the three laws are asserted exactly: *efficiency* Σ φᵢ = v(N) − v(∅); *dummy* (a factor with
   v(S ∪ {i}) = v(S) for all S has φᵢ = 0); *symmetry* (two factors interchangeable in v have equal values). Hand
   cases: the joint and the alternative two-factor tables both give (½, ½); the table v(∅) = 0, v({1}) = 1,
   v({2}) = 0, v({1, 2}) = 0 gives (½, −½), summing to 0.
4. **PO-AUTOPSY-02 cross-check and derivation.** The derivation file is
   `docs/derivations/first-divergence-monotonicity.md`, written by N27a-09; this bead writes no second file. It
   checks that the file states and proves the following, and files a change against N27a-09 if a part is missing:
   - Chained digest c_t = H(c_{t−1}, d_t) over per-tick state digests d_t. Predicate P(k): c_k(A) ≠ c_k(B).
   - *Monotone:* if c_k(A) ≠ c_k(B) then the inputs of H at k + 1 differ, so c_{k+1}(A) ≠ c_{k+1}(B) unless H
     collides (probability about 2⁻¹²⁸ for the 128-bit digest). So P(k) ⇒ P(k′) for k′ > k.
   - *Decided:* each P(k) is a comparison of two stored values.
   - *Correct:* if c_k(A) = c_k(B) then, barring a collision, c_{k−1} and d_k agree, and by induction the
     prefixes agree through k. Bisection over checkpoints therefore finds the least checkpoint k with P(k); the
     first unequal tick lies after checkpoint k − 1 and at or before checkpoint k, and comparing d_t tick by
     tick from checkpoint k − 1 finds it. That tick is the least tick at which the prefixes differ.
   - *The trap:* the pointwise predicate Q(t): d_t(A) ≠ d_t(B) is not monotone, because an error can vanish and
     return. Two traces of eight ticks (0 to 7) that differ at ticks 1 and 5: a midpoint search on Q probes tick
     3 (agree), moves right, probes 5 (differ), probes 4 (agree), and reports 5. On P it probes 3 (differ), 1
     (differ), 0 (agree), and reports 1.
   The same rule is recorded for bisecting a design lineage: the predicate must be "the outcome has changed by
   this edit" and each step must be decided by a study.
   Test: 1,000 pairs of synthetic traces with planted divergence sets (including sets where the difference
   vanishes and returns); an independent linear scan gives the truth; the search must agree in every case.
5. **PO-AUTOPSY-03 cross-check.** For tolerance mode, 1,000 synthetic trace pairs with known pointwise scaled
   errors and randomly missing samples; for every interval the search reports as agreeing, the true maximum
   scaled error (computed from the full traces in exact rational arithmetic) is at most 1. Block bounds are
   checked to be outward-rounded: the reported upper bound is never below the true block maximum. Where samples
   are missing the report must be the earliest possible interval and the coverage gap, never a tick.
6. **Suite registration and hook.** Each invariant and obligation is a row of the conformance suite with tier
   (G0 for the Shapley laws; G1 for planted divergences; G4 for the stripped-queue chaos case; G5 for the null
   fork), its test and its registered negative control (the mutant). The hook is this bead's own end-to-end
   helper library, `scripts/e2e/lib/autopsy.sh` (sourced after the shared `lib.sh`, whose `e2e_` prefix is
   reserved, STD-E2E): its function `autopsy_audit <dir>` runs the auditor over every ledger a script produced,
   through the example target `cargo run -p frob-autopsy --example autopsy_audit -- <dir> --json` (DESIGN: the
   auditor is a library function that no verb exposes), logs through the shared helper's functions and fails
   the script on any violation.
7. **Layout.** `crates/frob-autopsy/src/audit.rs`; `crates/frob-autopsy/tests/invariants.rs`, `po.rs`. The
   derivation it cites is `docs/derivations/first-divergence-monotonicity.md` (N27a-09).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The auditor reports no violation on the clean real artifacts of `audit_clean_artifacts` and
      `audit_clean_sequential`, and the helper step `autopsy_audit` fails a run over a directory that holds a
      mutant. Its run over every ledger, attribution, repair result and transcript the Autopsy end-to-end
      scripts produce is made by the exit review (N27b-30, step 5), which depends on this bead and on those
      scripts.
- [ ] Each mutant of step 2 is reported with exactly its invariant; no mutant passes.
- [ ] The three Shapley laws hold exactly on every table with m ≤ 5 in the suite, and the hand cases give
      (½, ½), (½, ½) and (½, −½).
- [ ] Exact-mode divergence equals the linear scan on 1,000 planted pairs, including the eight-tick case (tick
      1); the pointwise midpoint mutant is caught (it reports tick 5).
- [ ] No interval reported as agreeing in tolerance mode has a true maximum scaled error above 1; gaps are
      reported as intervals with their coverage.
- [ ] The derivation file of N27a-09 exists, contains the four parts of step 4 and the statement of the
      collision gap, is cited by the known-answer row for first divergence, and its counterexample is executable
      as a test here (`eight_tick_trap`).
- [ ] All nine identifiers are rows of the conformance suite with a negative control each.

- `auditor_exact_test_matches_stats` (10⁴ random count tables against the statistics module).
- `audit_helper_step`: the `autopsy_audit` step over a directory with one clean ledger and one mutant exits

`crates/frob-autopsy/tests/po.rs`:
- `shapley_efficiency_dummy_symmetry` (exact rationals; 1,000 random integer tables for m = 2 … 5).
- `shapley_hand_cases`.
- `first_divergence_exact_vs_scan` (1,000 pairs); `eight_tick_trap` (ticks 1 and 5 → 1);
  `pointwise_midpoint_mutant_reports_5`.
- `tolerance_agreeing_intervals_sound` (1,000 pairs); `block_bounds_outward`; `gap_reports_interval_not_tick`.

### End-to-end script and logging
No script of its own (the auditor is reached by no `frob` command). It provides `autopsy_audit` in its helper
library `scripts/e2e/lib/autopsy.sh`, which the exit review (N27b-30,
step 5) runs over the ledgers of `scripts/e2e/autopsy_intervene.sh`, `autopsy_attribute_planted.sh`,
`autopsy_session.sh` and `autopsy_lane_f32.sh` (none of those scripts waits on this bead); it logs one `assert`
line per invariant per ledger and, on a violation, the invariant, the row and the observed value with the
ledger hash.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-677 (two traces of eight ticks that differ at ticks 1 and 5: tick 1 by the chained digest; control: the
pointwise midpoint search reports tick 5). KA-678 (tolerance mode with a coverage gap: the earliest possible
interval and the gap; control: a tool that skips the gap reports a later tick). KA-673 and KA-674 (the Shapley
hand cases; the values sum to v(N) − v(∅) exactly). KA-671 (null fork). CX-296, CX-297, CX-299. The mutants are
the registered negative controls. Determinism: the auditor is exact arithmetic over stored artifacts and replays
of class D2; its verdicts are identical on every platform.

## Considerations

- The auditor replays the null fork, so auditing costs one fork per ledger; `--no-replay` skips that and reports
  invariant 01 as `not rechecked`, never as passed.
- Collision probability of the 128-bit digest is the only gap in the monotonicity argument; the derivation
  states it rather than hiding it. The host-side BLAKE3 hashes of snapshots are an independent check at
  checkpoints.
- If KILL-AUTOPSY fires, PO-AUTOPSY-01 has no mechanism left and its row is closed with that reason; the other
  eight remain.
