## Background

A value in the Autopsy specification that follows from a stated formula is a derived value and a known answer; every
other number carries its label. The Autopsy specification lists ten known answers (KA-670 to KA-679 in the program's
known-answer suite), each with a negative control, and the single-precision lane adds two more that have no
number in the suite yet. A known answer does its job only when four things exist together: a fixture that
realizes the case, a derivation on file, a test at the right tier that compares against the derivation and not
against a recording of the candidate, and a registered negative control shown to fail. This bead is the wiring
that guarantees all four for every Autopsy answer, holds the host fixtures to their derivations, and makes the
suite fail if any row is unbound.

## Scope

In: the binding of the twelve answers (the ten numbered rows and the two lane rows) to code: a runner entry
per row and site in the conformance crate's tier targets, and the chapter's binding table that names, for each
row, the tests and the negative control that discharge it; the statement of each host fixture's parameters and
derived values, and the tests that hold the five fixtures to them; the derivation files no other bead writes;
the suite run and its completeness check.
Out: the registry rows themselves, which are `[[ka]]` records of the suite's one file
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml` entered by N27-KA (this bead writes no registry row and
no registry file, and adds no key to a row: the row format is N38a-12's); the control records of
`controls.toml` (issued by the seeding tool of N38b-05 from the rows' control texts); the related rows of
other chapters and the derived values (N27-KA); building the host fixtures (the doubling map, the linear
map, the pendulum and the thermal node are built with the `ForkTarget` trait by N27b-03, the marginal threshold
block by N27b-07, because their own tests and the lane's admission tests need them before this suite exists);
the thermal derivation and its simulated realization (N27a-16); the first-divergence monotonicity derivation
(N27a-09); the tests of KA-673 to KA-678 inside their mechanism beads (bound here by name); the 200-failure
suite (N27b-08); gate records.

## Technical approach

1. **Suite rows and how they are bound.** The rows are the `[[ka]]` records that N27-KA enters in
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml` (schema N38a-12: `id`, `section`, `fixture`,
   `same_as`, `case`, `truth`, `tolerance`, `tier` with `sites`, `control`, `derivation`, `label`, `due`,
   `printed`, `slug`). A row carries no test name and no bead: the suite binds a row to code in two ways, and
   this bead supplies both for the twelve rows of its table.
   - *Runner entries.* For each row and each of its sites, an entry `ka:<id>@<site>` in the tier target
     (N38a-13: `g1_known_answers` for the G1 rows, `g5_determinism` for KA-671) whose function computes the
     observed value: through the Autopsy verbs on the row's fixture where the answer is user-visible (the host
     fixtures by their `fixture:host/…` handles, N27b-03; the simulated ones by their runs), directly in exact
     arithmetic for the pure tables. The runner compares with `assert_ka` under the row's tolerance and runs
     the row's registered controls in the same run. A row is run as `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p
     frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- KA-672`, and the chapter as `…
     -- ch:27`.
   - *The binding table* (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/ka_autopsy_bindings.rs`, data in the test, not in a
     registry): per row the runner entry, the mechanism beads' tests that cite the row, the control test, and
     the state of each binding (`bound`, `pending: <bead>`, `not applicable: <ADR>`). Where a mechanism
     bead's test is a row's own test, this bead adds beside it a test under the suite's naming convention that
     calls the same function (`ka_671_null_fork_bitwise` beside `null_fork_bitwise`: STD-TEST's `ka_<nnn>_`
     prefix), so that the suite's lint finds a developer-level test for the row and the names the mechanism
     beads and their logs cite stay as they are.
   This bead changes no truth or tolerance. The two lane answers are the rows with `slug = "lane-1"` and
   `"lane-2"`; their numbers come from the block N27-KA requests in the allocation ledger (N38a-40), and the
   entries and test names here take the numbers in the same commit that replaces the tags.
2. **Host fixtures and the values they must reproduce** (in `crates/frob-autopsy/src/fixtures/`, each labelled
   `synthetic`, each a `ForkTarget`). The first three and the thermal node are built by N27b-03, the threshold
   block by N27b-07; this bead is where their parameters and derived values are stated once and tested:
   - *Doubling map.* State x in 64-bit fixed point on [0, 1); step x ← 2x mod 1 (a left shift, exact); failure
     iff the fortieth iterate is at least ½. Perturbation: size u·10⁻¹² with u in [1, 2) and a drawn sign. The
     amplified shift 2⁴⁰·u·10⁻¹² lies in [1.0995, 2.199), so it wraps the unit interval and about half of
     placebo forks flip. With five iterates the shift is at most 6.4 × 10⁻¹¹ and nothing flips unless x₅ lies
     that close to ½, or to 0 (mod 1), where a negative shift wraps past 1 (the fixture's starting points
     exclude both bands).
   - *Linear map.* x ← 1.1735·x in `f64`; no verdict needed for the growth answer; G(n) = 1.1735ⁿ: 24.5 at 20,
     601.6 at 40 (the answer 602 is e^(0.16·40) = 601.8), 3.62 × 10⁵ at 80.
   - *Pendulum.* An inverted pendulum θ̈ = (g/l)·sin θ with g/l = 9.81 s⁻², released from rest at
     θ₀ = 5 × 10⁻⁸ rad, integrated with a fixed step; verdict "falls to the positive side" when θ first exceeds
     +0.5 rad before it falls below −0.5 rad. Its perturbable coordinate set is {θ} alone (N27b-03). A
     perturbation of σ·u·10⁻⁷ rad with u in [1, 2) makes θ₀ negative exactly when σ = −1; a perturbation at
     10⁻¹² never does.
   - *Marginal threshold failure.* A block decelerating to rest at x_f against the success predicate x ≥ 1 m,
     with its initial speed chosen (and recorded) so that x_f is one unit in the last place below 1 in single
     precision (1 − 2⁻²⁴ m ≈ 1 − 5.96 × 10⁻⁸ m, the closest `f32` value below 1) and 2 × 10⁻⁸ m above 1 in
     double precision (N27b-07); the wide-margin variant ends at 0.95 m.
   - *Thermal node.* T′ = (T_amb + ΔT_ss(r) − T)/τ with τ = 60 s, T_amb = 25 °C, ΔT_ss = 192 K·(12/r)², trip at
     115 °C, task length 45 s; exact solution T(t) = T_amb + ΔT_ss·(1 − e^(−t/τ)) from rest.
3. **Derivations**, one per row. Two are written by other beads and are bound here, not written twice. The
   thermal derivation is `docs/derivations/thermal-regear.md` (N27a-16); its reference constants are the ones
   the host thermal node is held to: trip time 60·ln(192/102) = 37.951 s; winding at the fork tick 36.95 s =
   25 + 192·(1 − e^(−36.95/60)) = 113.283 °C; the forced fork heads for 133 °C and trips after a further
   60·ln((133 − 113.283)/(133 − 115)) = 5.465 s, at 42.415 s; from the start at ratio 16 the peak at 45 s is
   25 + 108·(1 − e^(−0.75)) = 81.984 °C, 33 K in hand. The eight-tick trace and the monotonicity argument are
   `docs/derivations/first-divergence-monotonicity.md` (N27a-09). The others are written here as
   `docs/derivations/autopsy-*.md`: the doubling-map shift; the linear-map growth; the pendulum's sign
   argument; the two-factor Shapley tables; the growth arithmetic (e^(0.16·n) = 24.5, 602, 3.6 × 10⁵ at
   n = 20, 40, 80).
4. **Binding.** KA-670 → `planted_single_causes_gate` and `out_of_list_cause_names_nothing` (BD-280, N27b-08).
   KA-671 → `null_fork_bitwise`, control `null_fork_stripped_queue_refused` (N27b-03). KA-672 →
   `doubling_map_chaotic`, control `doubling_map_five_iterates` (N27b-03), and the label test through the
   attribution. KA-673, KA-674 → the coalition bead's label and Shapley tests (BD-281, N27a-11), cross-checked
   by `shapley_hand_cases` (N27b-20) and the repair tests (N27b-14). KA-675 → the re-run bead's thermal tests
   (BD-283, on the fixture of N27a-16), with the host thermal node of N27b-03 held to N27a-16's derivation by
   `thermal_closed_form` here. KA-676 → `warm_start_graft_refused_582` (BD-279) and the fork service's own
   test `wheel_graft_refused_582` (N27a-02). KA-677, KA-678 → the divergence bead's tests (BD-206) and the gate
   battery's `two_ticks_reports_first` and `tolerance_gap_interval` (N27a-09), cross-checked by `eight_tick_trap`
   and `gap_reports_interval_not_tick` (N27b-20). KA-679 → `marginal_failure_precision_sensitive` (the route
   and FROB-REF-584 in a build without the lane), control `wide_margin_attributes_normally` (N27b-07), and
   `ka679_through_lane` (the label through the lane's real admission, N27b-24). `lane-1` → `linear_map_growth`
   (N27b-03). `lane-2` → `pendulum_angle_placebo` (N27b-03) for ρ₀, and the lane's admission tests
   `pendulum_marginal` and `pendulum_stable_at_1e12` (N27b-23) for the label and the control.
   Bindings to the lane beads (N27b-23, N27b-24), which are Frontier work under KILL-ENSEMBLE and are not
   prerequisites of this suite, are written in the binding table as `pending: <bead>` until that bead closes,
   and as `not applicable: KILL-ENSEMBLE` with the ADR's identifier if the lane is withdrawn (N27b-29; the two
   lane rows' numbers are then recorded `void` in their block by N27-KA's owner, N38a-40's rule). The exit
   review (N27b-30) reads the table and accepts neither `pending` nor a missing binding.
5. **Completeness check.** A test in `frob-conformance` loads the rows through the test kit's loader and the
   binding table, and fails if any of the twelve rows lacks a derivation file whose front matter lists it, a
   runner entry for one of its sites, a resolvable test name in the binding table or a control; and if any
   test named in the table was not executed in the suite run. A binding marked `pending: <bead>` passes only
   while that bead is open in the tracker, and `not applicable: KILL-ENSEMBLE` only with a recorded ADR. The
   check also asserts that the fixtures' expected values are read from the derivation constants, never from a
   stored output of the candidate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The twelve rows of N27-KA load from the suite's one file; each has a derivation file, a runner entry at
      its tier for each site, and its negative control (lane bindings `pending` or `not applicable` as step 4
      allows); the completeness check passes, fails when a binding is removed, and fails on a `pending`
      binding whose bead is closed. This bead adds no file under `registry/` and no key to a row.
- [ ] The five host fixtures exist as `ForkTarget`s and reproduce their derived values: doubling map ρ₀ in
      [0.3, 0.7] at forty iterates and 0 at five; linear map growth within 1% of 602 at 40 steps; pendulum
      ρ₀ in [0.25, 0.75] at 10⁻⁷ and 0 at 10⁻¹²; thermal 37.95 s, 113.3 °C, 42.4 s and 82.0 °C within 0.1 s
      and 0.1 K.
- [ ] Every stated negative control is executed and shown to fail in the way the row says.
- [ ] The host rows run under `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers
      --features gauntlet-mutations -- ch:27` in under five minutes on a laptop, each control caught in the
      same run; the chapter's meta-checks run under `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
      ka_autopsy_bindings`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-autopsy/tests/fixtures.rs`: `doubling_map_exact_shift` (the map is a 64-bit shift; 2⁴⁰ = 
1,099,511,627,776); `linear_map_values`; `pendulum_sign_decides` (σ = −1 at 10⁻⁷ falls negative; σ = ±1 at
10⁻¹² falls positive); `threshold_fixture_precision_split` (`f32` one unit in the last place below 1, `f64`
2 × 10⁻⁸ m above); `thermal_closed_form` (the four numbers).
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/ka_autopsy_bindings.rs`: `rows_complete` (twelve rows, each with derivation,
runner entry, table entry and control), `rows_bound_tests_ran`, `pending_binding_needs_open_bead`,
`expected_values_from_derivations`, `control_fails_as_stated` (one sub-test per row with a control),
`no_private_registry_file` (no `.toml` under a directory named `known_answers`; no key outside N38a-12's
schema on an Autopsy row). The runner entries themselves live in the tier targets `g1_known_answers` and
`g5_determinism`, twelve rows by their sites.

### End-to-end script and logging
No script of its own. The gate scripts log the KA identifiers they touch on their `assert` lines
(`ka: "KA-670"` and so on), and the suite's own run writes one line per row: `id`, `tier`, `site`, measured
value, truth, tolerance, pass or fail, and the control's outcome.

### Known answers and negative controls
The twelve rows above with their controls are the content. One finding recorded with the fixtures: with a
perturbation of one fixed size, the doubling map's amplified shift is 10⁻¹²·2⁴⁰ mod 1 = 0.0995 for every fork
and only about 20% of placebo forks flip, which sits on the 0.2 threshold; "about half" requires sizes spread
over [1, 2)·10⁻¹², which is the generator's rule. Metamorphic relations bound here: the null fork is the
original; a placebo flips nothing on a non-chaotic case. Determinism lanes for the host rows: twice on one
machine, debug and release, both platforms (exact or `f64` with deterministic elementary functions).

## Considerations

- The thermal fixture is a model with an exact solution, not the actuator thermal network; it tests the logic of
  "fork versus re-run", which is what the answer is about. The re-run bead also runs the case on the real
  thermal model.
- The pendulum's integrator is fixed-step and its verdict depends only on the sign of θ₀, so the answer is
  independent of step size; the test runs two step sizes to show it.
- Numbers for the two lane answers come from the block N27-KA requests in the allocation ledger (N38a-40);
  until it is issued the tags `lane-1` and `lane-2` are the identifiers in logs and test names, and a tag left
  after the rows' due milestone fails that ledger's check.
