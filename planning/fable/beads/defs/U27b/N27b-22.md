## Background

By default Autopsy attributes one failure in double precision on the CPU, because closed-loop legged control
amplifies round-off. The **single-precision lane** is a Frontier mechanism that would let attribution run on the
tier where the failure happened, in single precision, when a measured rule says the result can be trusted. Its
argument is arithmetic on a published exponent: FACT (source register; to be re-read before a gate cites it), at
0.16 per control step the growth factor e^(0.16·n) is 24.5 at n = 20, 602 at n = 40 and 3.6 × 10⁵ at n = 80.
Single-precision round-off of 6 × 10⁻⁸ therefore reaches 1.5 × 10⁻⁶ of state scale after 20 steps, 3.6 × 10⁻⁵
after 40 and 2% after 80; at that benchmark's 25 ms period these are fork windows of 0.5 s, 1 s and 2 s.
**HYPOTHESIS: the exponent of our closed loops is of the same order.** It was measured on walker and cheetah
benchmarks under learned policies; nobody has measured trained quadruped policies in any engine that could be
found (open question OPEN-AUTOPSY-3). If our loops are much more sensitive, the lane admits almost nothing and
is not worth building; if they are much less, even 2 s windows are safe.

Every Frontier mechanism needs a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier before it is funded, small enough to run on
a CPU in a day, registered before it runs, with an outcome not known in advance, and the ceiling of its benefit
is computed before any speed test. This is that pilot. Its falsifier is taken from the lane's kill gate
(KILL-ENSEMBLE: agreement with double precision below 90%; or fewer than half of the reference failures
admitted at any window of 0.5 s or more; or a bulk speedup below 3).

## Scope

In: the frozen protocol, the pilot program (outside the product workspace), the registered record (falsifier,
ceiling, fallback, kill gate, cost, tool and pin), the run, the result, and its consequences for funding the
lane and for OPEN-AUTOPSY-3.
Out: the lane itself (N27b-23 to N27b-27), its product-scale gate (N27b-28) and its kill decision (N27b-29).

## Technical approach

1. **Register before running** (`frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`): mechanism "attribution in single
   precision on the originating tier" (Frontier; Autopsy); protocol hash; and:
   - *Falsifier.* (a) Fewer than half of the reference failures are admitted at W = 0.5 s (admission: placebo
     flip rate ρ₀ ≤ 0.1 at a relative 10⁻⁷ over 32 placebo forks, and G(W)·u ≤ 10⁻⁴ with u = 6 × 10⁻⁸); or
     (b) on the admitted planted failures the single-precision top factor equals the double-precision top factor
     in fewer than 90% of cases; or (c) the computed ceiling of the bulk speedup is below 3.
   - *Ceiling, computed first.* For the bulk workload of 200 failures with pair interactions (55 interventions ×
     32 replicates × 2 s = 7.0 × 10⁵ world-seconds): T_cpu = 7.0 × 10⁵ / r_cpu and T_dev = 7.0 × 10⁵ / r_dev +
     T_fixed, where r is world-seconds per second on the fork workload and T_fixed is what both arms share and
     the device does not speed up (restoring checkpoints, compiling variants, admission, reading results).
     Ceiling = T_cpu / T_dev. With the TARGET figures r_dev = 4,000 and r_cpu between 1,000 and 100 this is
     between 4 and 40 before T_fixed (about 3 minutes against 12 minutes to 2 hours); the record states the
     figures used and their source, and if the ceiling is below 3 the speed clause is declared unreachable and
     the lane is funded, if at all, only for its second benefit.
   - *Second benefit (not a speed).* Attribution of tier-specific failures, which the double-precision rule
     cannot attribute at all. One failure with ten factors and 16 replicates is 160 forks of 2 s, 320
     world-seconds: seconds on either tier, so for a single failure the lane saves nothing.
   - *Fallback.* The decision as first written: attribution in double precision only, and a failure not
     reproduced there goes to the artifact guard unattributed.
   - *Cost.* Under one day on a 32-core machine (about 50 failures × 3 windows × 33 forks, plus 40 planted
     failures × 10 factors × 16 replicates in two precisions).
2. **Protocol (frozen by hash).**
   1. *Arithmetic controls first.* The linear map x ← 1.1735·x (exponent ln 1.1735 = 0.16 per step): the
      measured growth after 40 steps must be 602 within 1%. The uncontrolled pendulum released 5 × 10⁻⁸ rad
      from upright with the verdict "falls to the positive side": at a placebo of 10⁻⁷ rad ρ₀ must be about one
      half and the case must not be admitted; at 10⁻¹² the side must be stable.
   2. *Reference failures.* 50 failing episodes of the quadruped under the trained policy (and 50 of the
      humanoid if a policy exists), recorded in single precision, drawn by address from one evaluation, not
      selected by outcome margin.
   3. *Growth.* For each failure and each W ∈ {0.5 s, 1 s, 2 s}: restore at W before the failure; run the parent
      and 32 placebo forks perturbed at a relative 10⁻⁷ (one coordinate, drawn sign, size factor in [1, 2));
      record ρ₀ and, per fork, the ratio of the largest scaled separation over the window to the initial one;
      G(W) is the maximum over forks, with the median beside it. Exponent estimate λ̂ = ln G(W) / (control steps
      in W).
   4. *Admission.* Per failure and window: admitted iff ρ₀ ≤ 0.1 and G(W)·6 × 10⁻⁸ ≤ 10⁻⁴ (that is, G(W) ≤
      1,667). Report the admitted share per W.
   5. *Agreement.* 40 planted single-cause failures (8 per class, ten candidates each) with W = 1 s: attribute
      in single precision with 16 replicates per intervention at 10⁻⁷ and 32 placebo forks, and in double
      precision with the default counts; compare the top factor, its label and the sign of each contrast on the
      admitted cases.
3. **Reading the result.**
   | Outcome | Consequence |
   |---|---|
   | At least half admitted at some W ≥ 0.5 s, agreement ≥ 90%, ceiling ≥ 3 | The lane is funded; the default fork lead for the lane is the largest W with at least half admitted |
   | Fewer than half admitted at 0.5 s | Falsifier (a): the lane is not funded; KILL-ENSEMBLE is recorded as fired at the pilot |
   | Agreement below 90% | Falsifier (b): not funded |
   | Ceiling below 3 | The speed clause is unreachable; the owner decides whether the tier-specific benefit alone funds a reduced lane, and the gate's speed clause is amended before any measurement or the lane is not funded |
   | λ̂ well below 0.03 per step | Evidence that leads beyond 2 s are usable; recorded against OPEN-AUTOPSY-3 |
4. **Expectation stated in advance (HYPOTHESIS, to be confronted with the result).** At 0.16 per step and a
   25 ms control period, G reaches 1,667 after 46 steps: windows up to about 1.16 s would be admitted and 2 s
   would not. At 0.03 per step the bound is 247 steps, about 6 s.
5. **Record.** Result, positive or negative, into the pilot register and the review log; figures obtained on the
   development oracle are labelled `development-oracle`, support no gate, and are re-measured on our tiers by
   the lane's gate (which records G(W) for the reference quadruped and humanoid policies at 0.5 s, 1 s and 2 s).
   The pilot program lives under `tools/pilots/autopsy_growth/` with its pin and has no product dependency.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot record with protocol hash, falsifier, ceiling (with the throughput figures and their source),
      fallback, kill gate, cost, tool and pin exists before the first run.
- [ ] The two arithmetic controls behave as stated (602 within 1%; pendulum not admitted at 10⁻⁷, stable at
      10⁻¹²); a pilot whose controls fail is void and is not read.
- [ ] G(W), its median, λ̂, ρ₀ and the admitted share are reported for each W with denominators, per robot.
- [ ] Agreement of top factor, label and contrast signs is reported on the admitted planted cases with its
      one-sided 95% exact lower bound.
- [ ] The outcome row of step 3 that applies is recorded, and the lane's beads are released or withdrawn to
      match; OPEN-AUTOPSY-3's status line is updated with λ̂.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
In the pilot's directory (no product crates): `linear_map_growth_602`; `pendulum_flip_half_at_1e7` and
`pendulum_stable_at_1e12`; `admission_rule_boundaries` (ρ₀ = 3/32 admitted, 4/32 not; G = 1,666 admitted, 1,667.5
not); `ceiling_formula` (r_dev = 4,000, r_cpu = 1,000, T_fixed = 0 → 4.0; T_fixed = 60 s → 704/(176 + 60) =
2.98, below 3); `lambda_estimate` (G = 602 over 40 steps → 0.16).

### End-to-end script and logging
`tools/pilots/autopsy_growth/run.sh` (a pilot script, outside `scripts/e2e/`). Newline-delimited JSON in the
end-to-end line format: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N27b-22`), `step`, `event`, command, exit code, wall time, tool
and pin, precision, seed; `metric` lines with thresholds: `linear_map_G40` (602 ± 1%), `pendulum_rho0_1e-7`
(above 0.1), per failure and window `rho0` (0.1), `G_max` (1,667), `G_median`, `lambda_hat`; per window
`admitted_share` (0.5); `top_factor_agreement` (0.90) with its lower bound; `ceiling` (3). Final `summary`: the
verdict against each falsifier clause.

### Known answers and negative controls
The linear map (growth 602 after 40 steps, within 1%) and the pendulum (ρ₀ about one half at 10⁻⁷, so not
admitted and labelled `marginal`; stable at 10⁻¹², which is why the 10⁻¹² series alone would have hidden the
problem). The growth arithmetic 24.5, 602, 3.6 × 10⁵ and the reach of round-off 1.5 × 10⁻⁶, 3.6 × 10⁻⁵, 2%.
Negative controls: an A/A arm (double precision against double precision must agree on every top factor); a
perturbation generator that is a no-op in single precision (ρ₀ = 0 on the pendulum: the control fails and voids
the run). Determinism: each precision arm is run twice; a tool that cannot repeat a fork exactly in single
precision is unfit and the pilot is deferred to `batch-cpu` in `f32`.

## Considerations

- "State scale" in the admission rule is the physical scale of each coordinate's class (1 m, 1 rad, 1 m/s,
  1 rad/s by default); the pilot records the scales it used, since G(W)·u is only meaningful against them.
- Failures drawn by address include marginal ones; that is intended. A verdict that moves under a relative
  change of 10⁻⁵ in state also moves under sensor noise and friction scatter two orders larger. It is marginal in
  any arithmetic, and the right output is the label `marginal`, not more digits.
- A ceiling just above 3 with realistic fixed costs is a warning that the speed clause has little power; the
  record says so in advance rather than after a failed test.
- The pilot does not measure tier-specific failures (it has no second engine tier); that benefit is tested only
  by the product gate's planted solver artifact.
