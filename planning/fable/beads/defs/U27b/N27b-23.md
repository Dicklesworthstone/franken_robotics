## Background

The single-precision lane lets an attribution run on the tier where the failure happened instead of being moved
to double precision on the CPU. It amends the decision that double precision is required: double precision
remains the default for one failure, where it costs little, but it is not a requirement of the evidence. What
the evidence supports is a flip rate measured under perturbations at or above round-off scale, and that
measurement can be made on the tier where the failure happened.

Admission is the measurement. Before any intervention is run in single precision, the lane runs placebo forks at
the scale of single-precision round-off and asks two questions: does the verdict itself survive such nudges
(the placebo flip rate), and does round-off stay small against the state over the fork window (the growth of
separation)? Only a failure that passes both is attributed in the lane. The rule (thresholds are DESIGN defaults
and TARGETs of the lane's gate): **ρ₀ ≤ 0.1 and G(W)·u ≤ 10⁻⁴ of state scale, with u = 6 × 10⁻⁸.**

Why it is admissible, as arithmetic on the published exponent of 0.16 per control step (FACT about its source;
HYPOTHESIS for our loops, which the pilot and the gate measure): growth is 24.5 after 20 steps, 602 after 40 and
3.6 × 10⁵ after 80, so round-off of 6 × 10⁻⁸ reaches 1.5 × 10⁻⁶ of state scale after 20 steps, 3.6 × 10⁻⁵ after
40 and 2% after 80: fork windows of 0.5 s, 1 s and 2 s at a 25 ms period. The first two pass 10⁻⁴; the third
does not. A verdict that moves under a relative change of 10⁻⁵ in state also moves under sensor noise and
friction scatter two orders larger; it is marginal in any arithmetic, and the right output is the label
`marginal`, not more digits.

Tier Frontier (a narrowed form of attribution on the originating tier, admitted by a measured rule). Crate
`frob-autopsy`. Pilot: N27b-22. Kill gate: KILL-ENSEMBLE, with fallback double precision only.

## Scope

In: the admission procedure for one failure and one window; exact evaluation of the two clauses; the search for
the longest admissible window; the lower bound on the window; the admission record; the labels `marginal` and
the reason codes; cost of admission.
Out: the growth and flip primitives (N27b-03); running interventions in the lane and choosing lanes (`--lane`,
N27b-24); numerical-variant interventions (N27b-25); the cross-check against double precision (N27b-26).

## Technical approach

1. **Procedure for a failure recorded on a single-precision tier, at window W.**
   1. The null fork on the originating profile must give identical digests (unchanged; INV-AUTOPSY-01;
      FROB-REF-583 otherwise).
   2. R₀ placebo forks at a relative 10⁻⁷ on the originating tier over W (DESIGN default R₀ = 32): each perturbs
      one state coordinate by σ·s·10⁻⁷ times a size factor in [1, 2), raised to one unit in the last place where
      that is larger. Record the placebo flip rate ρ₀ and, per fork, the growth of its separation from the
      parent over the window.
   3. **Admission.** `admitted = (10·flips ≤ R₀) ∧ (G(W)·u ≤ 10⁻⁴)`, where G(W) is the maximum over the R₀ forks
      of [largest scaled separation over the window ÷ initial scaled separation], evaluated with an
      outward-rounded upper bound, and u = 6 × 10⁻⁸. At R₀ = 32 the first clause admits at most 3 flips; the
      second admits G(W) up to 1,666.
2. **Admission record (DESIGN).**
   ```rust
   pub struct LaneAdmission { tier: Tier, window: Qty, r0: u32, flips: u32, unknown: u32,
                              g_max: f64, g_median: f64, g_times_u: f64, lambda_envelope: f64,
                              admitted: bool, reason: Admitted | PlaceboFlips | Growth | Both,
                              windows_tried: Vec<(Qty, bool, Reason)>, w_min: Qty }
   ```
   `lambda_envelope = ln(g_max) / (control steps in the window)` is reported as a description. It is the upper
   envelope of the worst fork, what the growth clause sees, and is never called λ̂: the divergence rate λ̂ (the
   median least-squares slope of ln δ(t)) is a different estimator, defined in the pilot (N27b-22) and measured
   on our tiers by the pairing report (N35-08), and the two are kept apart (DESIGN, as stated in both).
3. **Not admitted: the longest window that passes.** Candidate windows are the fork-lead ladder below the
   requested one, in descending order (2 s, 1 s, 0.5 s, 0.25 s). The first that passes is taken; each trial costs
   R₀ + 1 forks of its own length (forks from different ticks are different experiments; nothing is reused).
   Because G(W) is a maximum over the window it does not decrease as W grows, so once the growth clause passes
   at a window it passes at every shorter one; the flip clause is re-measured at each.
4. **The lower bound on the window.** A fork must start before the failing trajectory has parted from the one
   that does not fail; a fork that starts later tests nothing (its honest label would be `precedes-snapshot`).
   The bound is W_min = failure time − t_b, where t_b is the earlier of: the first decision-relevant divergence
   reported by the first-divergence search between the recorded run and its double-precision comparison replay,
   where that replay exists and does not fail; and the first-anomaly time of the heuristic ordering. Windows
   shorter than W_min are not tried. (In the worked session the first anomaly is 0.2 s before the fall, so the
   0.25 s rung is the shortest usable.)
5. **Outcomes.**
   | Result | What follows |
   |---|---|
   | Admitted at the requested W | The lane attributes at W |
   | Admitted at a shorter W′ ≥ W_min | The lane attributes at W′; the record shows the windows tried |
   | No window passes; the flip clause failed at the shortest; double precision reproduces the failure | Outcome `marginal`; no factor is named in the lane; under `auto` the request escalates to double precision |
   | No window passes; only the growth clause failed; double precision reproduces the failure | Reason `growth`; escalate to double precision |
   | No window passes and double precision does not reproduce the failure (either clause) | `precision-sensitive`; attribution refused (FROB-REF-584); the world goes to the artifact guard. This row takes precedence over the two above: a failure that only the single-precision run shows is never called `marginal` (plan: only when the lane is also not admitted is it labelled `precision-sensitive`) |
6. **Scales.** "State scale" is the physical scale of each coordinate's class (the table tolerance comparisons
   use; defaults 1 m, 1 rad, 1 m/s, 1 rad/s). The scales used are stored in the record. Separation is computed
   in double precision from the stored single-precision states (subtraction of two stored values is exact).
7. **Cost.** 33 forks of W seconds per trial: at W = 1 s on a device, a fraction of a second. For a cluster,
   admission for all failures runs as one batch.
8. **Layout.** `crates/frob-autopsy/src/lane/admit.rs`, `lane/window.rs`. Determinism: every fork is class D2
   on its tier; the record is identical on repeat on one device family. Evidence: the record is `Tested` on its
   tier.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Linear map x ← 1.1735·x (exponent 0.16 per step): measured G after 40 steps is within 1% of 602; the
      growth clause passes at 40 steps (602 × 6 × 10⁻⁸ = 3.6 × 10⁻⁵) and fails at 80 (0.022); the window search
      returns the 40-step window when 80 was requested.
- [ ] Pendulum released 5 × 10⁻⁸ rad from upright, verdict "falls to the positive side": ρ₀ at 10⁻⁷ is about one
      half, the case is not admitted at any window, and the outcome is `marginal`; at a placebo of 10⁻¹² the side
      is stable (shown as the reason the 10⁻¹² series alone would have hidden the problem).
- [ ] Boundary behaviour is exact: 3 flips of 32 admitted and 4 not; G = 1,666 admitted and G = 1,667 not.
- [ ] Windows below W_min are never tried, and the record lists every window tried with its reason.
- [ ] A `NotRepresentable` or no-op perturbation is never counted as a non-flip (the admission fails closed with
      the reason recorded).
- [ ] `unknown` outcomes among placebo forks count as flips.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob autopsy intervene --lane tier-f32 --plan-only --json`, which prints the admission
      record without running interventions.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-autopsy/tests/lane_admit.rs` (host fixtures `synthetic`; real forks on fixture 2 in `f32` on
`batch-cpu`):
- `linear_map_admission`: G(20) = 24.5, G(40) = 602, G(80) = 3.6 × 10⁵ within 1%; admitted at 20 and 40, not at
  80; search from 80 returns 40.
- `pendulum_marginal`: ρ₀ in [0.25, 0.75] at 32 forks; not admitted at 2, 1, 0.5, 0.25 s; outcome `marginal`.
- `pendulum_stable_at_1e12` (double precision): zero flips.
- `not_reproduced_is_precision_sensitive`: the marginal threshold block of KA-679 (fails in `f32`, passes in
  `f64`) with its flip clause failing at every window → `precision-sensitive` and FROB-REF-584, never `marginal`.
- `flip_clause_boundary`, `growth_clause_boundary`, `growth_upper_bound_is_outward`.
- `window_ladder_order` and `w_min_respected` (first anomaly 0.3 s before failure → 0.25 s never tried).
- `growth_monotone_in_window` (property over 200 synthetic traces).
- `unknown_counts_as_flip`; `noop_perturbation_fails_closed`.
- `quadruped_wide_margin_admitted` (fixture 2 with a wide-margin failure at W = 0.5 s); the record's fields are
  all present.
- `record_deterministic` (twice; across worker counts).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/autopsy_lane_f32.sh` (N27b-28) logs the admission record of every failure and window: `rho0`
(threshold 0.1), `g_max` and `g_times_u` (threshold 10⁻⁴), `g_median`, `lambda_envelope`, `admitted`, `reason`,
`windows_tried`, `w_min`.

### Known answers and negative controls
The two known answers of the lane: (i) the linear map, growth 602 after 40 steps, G within 1%; (ii) the
pendulum, not admitted, `marginal`, stable at 10⁻¹². The reach-of-round-off arithmetic (1.5 × 10⁻⁶, 3.6 × 10⁻⁵,
2%). Negative controls: an admission that uses the median growth instead of the maximum (admits a case whose
worst fork exceeds the bound: a `synthetic` set with one fast-growing fork); an admission that reads the 10⁻¹²
series (admits the pendulum); a search that reuses the long window's forks for a short window. Determinism
lanes: the admission record twice on one device, across worker counts, alone and in a batch.

## Considerations

- KILL-ENSEMBLE triggers that this mechanism feeds: fewer than half of the reference failures admitted at any
  window of 0.5 s or more. If it fires, admission is removed with the lane.
- The lane exists on one branch of the other kill gate as well: if KILL-AUTOPSY fires, fork-based attribution
  is withdrawn and the lane with it (its own kill gate is then recorded as moot). This bead, the first of the
  lane, therefore depends on that decision (N27b-11) and starts only when it is recorded as not fired; the
  decision reads the double-precision clauses of GATE-AUTOPSY-03 and waits for nothing in the lane.
- The thresholds 0.1, 10⁻⁴ and R₀ = 32 are DESIGN defaults kept in the lane's policy record; the pilot's figures
  may move them before the gate is first measured, by a dated amendment and never after.
- OPEN-AUTOPSY-3: `lambda_envelope` values accumulated here are upper envelopes of our own loops' sensitivity;
  they are exported to the ledger, labelled as envelopes, so the default fork lead can be revisited beside the
  λ̂ of record from the pairing report (N35-08).
- Admission is per failure. A cluster's admitted share is a ledger figure, not a licence to attribute the
  unadmitted members in the lane.
