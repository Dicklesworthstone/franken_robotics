## Background

The single-precision lane attributes failures on the tier where they happened, admitted by a measured rule. A
measured admission rule is still a rule that could be wrong: the growth threshold could be too loose for some
class of motion, or a tier could carry a defect that the placebo forks share with the intervention forks. The
lane therefore carries **a cross-check that can fail**: one attribution in k (DESIGN default k = 8), and every
attribution that enters a report, is repeated in double precision on `batch-cpu`; the top factor, its label and
the sign of each contrast are compared, and the agreement rate is a ledger figure.

The agreement rate is also what the lane's kill gate reads: KILL-ENSEMBLE fires when agreement with double
precision is below 90% (or fewer than half of the reference failures are admitted at any window of 0.5 s or
more, or the bulk speedup is below 3). And the lane's gate requires that on the planted-cause suite with windows
of at most 1 s the lane names the same top factor as double precision in at least 95% of admitted cases. A check
that samples one in eight and reports its own rate is how the lane stays honest after the gate has passed.

Tier Frontier (part of the lane). Crate `frob-autopsy`. Pilot: N27b-22.

## Scope

In: selecting which lane attributions are cross-checked; running the double-precision repeat; the comparison
rule; the agreement ledger and its strata; the rule that a lane attribution enters a report only with a
cross-check; what happens on disagreement; the alarm that the kill gate reads.
Out: the lane (N27b-24) and the double-precision path (BD-279, BD-280) that this bead calls; the gate script
(N27b-28); the kill decision (N27b-29).

## Technical approach

1. **Selection (deterministic, not chosen by outcome).** A lane attribution with identifier hash h is
   cross-checked when `h mod k = 0` (k = 8 by default), computed from the attribution's content-independent
   identity (fork address and request hash, never its result). In addition every lane attribution that a report
   is about to print is cross-checked on demand if it has not been. Selection by address means the sampled set
   cannot drift toward easy cases, and a replay selects the same ones.
2. **The repeat.** The failure's checkpoint is taken through the precision route: widened, replayed in double
   precision on `batch-cpu`, compared at decision level. Then:
   - *Decision-equivalent:* the same declared factors (and coalitions) are run in double precision with the
     default counts (8 replicates at 10⁻¹², 64 placebo forks, the 10⁻⁷ series for whatever changes the outcome)
     at the same fork tick the lane used, and attributed.
   - *Not equivalent:* the failure exists on the fast tier only. The cross-check is recorded as
     `not comparable: tier-only failure` and is counted in its own column, not as agreement or disagreement. (A
     lane attribution of such a failure already carries `tier-specific` or the condition `tier-sensitive`.)
3. **Comparison.** For a comparable pair (lane L, double precision D):
   | Compared | Agreement means |
   |---|---|
   | Top factor | `ranking[0]` of L equals `ranking[0]` of D, or both name no factor |
   | Label | The label of that factor is the same, and the outcomes are the same |
   | Sign of each contrast | For every factor run in both, sign(contrast_L) = sign(contrast_D), where a contrast that is not tested above the placebo in either is treated as zero and agrees with zero or with an untested contrast of either sign |
   `agree = top ∧ label ∧ signs`. Each component is recorded separately. The gate's 95% clause reads the
   top-factor component; the kill gate's 90% reads `top`, with the other two reported beside it.
4. **Agreement ledger** (schema `frob.autopsy.crosscheck/1`, DESIGN). One row per cross-check: lane attribution,
   double-precision attribution, stratum (robot class, task family, tier, window), the three components,
   `comparable`, both placebo rates, the lane's admission figures. Aggregates per stratum and overall: counts,
   the agreement rate with its one-sided 95% exact (Clopper–Pearson) lower bound, the share not comparable. The
   figures are read by `frob autopsy show --format crosscheck` (a view of the ledger; a format this bead adds
   to the read verb `autopsy.show` of BD-284, carried by the verb's registry row, VERBS-autopsy, from this
   bead on; its `result` is the aggregate table with handles to the rows) and by reports.
5. **Reports.** A lane attribution may be printed by a report only if its cross-check exists and `agree` is
   true, or it is `not comparable` and printed with its tier-only wording. If `agree` is false the report prints
   the double-precision attribution and notes the disagreement; the lane attribution is kept and marked
   `superseded-by: <double-precision attribution>`.
6. **On disagreement.** The pair is written to the ledger with the first differing component; the failure's
   lane attribution gains the flag `crosscheck: disagree`; `next_actions` on it point to the double-precision
   result. Nothing is averaged or reconciled.
7. **Alarm.** When the overall top-factor agreement rate, over at least 40 comparable cross-checks, falls below
   0.90 (35 or fewer agreements of 40; the kill gate's own figure, read as a rate), the lane's adoption status is
   set to `suspended`: `auto` stops choosing
   `tier-f32`, forced `--lane tier-f32` returns its result with the flag `lane suspended`, and the kill
   decision is put on the agenda with the ledger attached. The alarm is a holding action, not the kill decision.
8. **Cost.** One double-precision attribution per eight lane attributions: for ten factors with pairs, 505 forks
   of two seconds on `batch-cpu` per check, so the cross-check adds about one eighth of the double-precision
   cost to a bulk job. This overhead is charged to the lane's arm when its speedup is measured.
9. **Layout.** `crates/frob-autopsy/src/lane/crosscheck.rs`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Selection is one in eight by identity and is reproduced exactly on replay; over a fixed set of 800 lane
      identities the selected count lies between 70 and 130 (a hash selects a binomial count: mean 100, standard
      deviation about 9.4) and is the same on every replay.
- [ ] Every lane attribution that a report prints has a cross-check; a report attempted without one triggers the
      check before printing.
- [ ] The comparison's three components are computed as the table states; a planted disagreement (a lane
      attribution with its ranking reversed by a test hook) is recorded as `disagree` with component `top`.
- [ ] A tier-only failure is recorded `not comparable` and enters neither count.
- [ ] The ledger's aggregate equals the recount from its rows; the lower bound is the exact one-sided bound
      (for 38 agreements of 40: 0.851; for 40 of 40: 0.928).
- [ ] The alarm suspends `auto` when the agreement rate over at least 40 comparable checks is below 0.90 (36 of
      40 does not trigger it; 35 of 40 does), and never before 40 checks.
- [ ] The cross-check's forks and wall time are charged to the lane's arm in the job's cost record, which is
      the figure the gate script (N27b-28, which depends on this bead) reads for that arm.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-autopsy/tests/lane_crosscheck.rs` (real attributions on fixture 2 in `f32` and `f64` on
`batch-cpu`; `synthetic` attribution records where stated):
- `selection_one_in_eight_by_identity` (800 fixed identities: count in [70, 130], identical twice);
  `selection_independent_of_result` (the same identity with two different
  results is selected or not identically).
- `compare_components`: (same top, same label, same signs) → agree; (same top, label `but-for` against
  `contributing`) → label disagrees; (untested contrast of −0.03 against +0.02) → signs agree.
- `tier_only_not_comparable`.
- `report_requires_crosscheck` (real report generator).
- `disagreement_supersedes`.
- `ledger_recount`; `clopper_pearson_lower_bounds` (38/40 → 0.851; 40/40 → 0.928; 95/100 → 0.898).
- `alarm_threshold_and_minimum_count` (36/40 no alarm; 35/40 alarm; 30/39 no alarm yet).
- `wide_margin_failure_agrees` (real: lane and double precision on a wide-margin planted failure).
- `crosscheck_cost_charged_to_lane`: in a job of sixteen lane attributions at k = 8, the forks and wall time
  of every double-precision repeat the selection chose are recorded under the lane's arm.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/autopsy_lane_f32.sh` (N27b-28) cross-checks every admitted case of the planted suite for the gate
measurement (no input sets k, so the script runs each double-precision repeat itself with `--lane f64` and
compares by the rule of step 3) and one in eight of the bulk job, and logs per pair: identities, `comparable`, `top`, `label`,
`signs`, both placebo rates; and the aggregate with its lower bound against 0.95 (gate) and 0.90 (kill).

### Known answers and negative controls
The exact lower bounds above. Negative controls: a cross-check that selects by result (for example only
attributions labelled `but-for`) is caught by `selection_independent_of_result`; one that counts tier-only
failures as agreement inflates the rate and is caught by `tier_only_not_comparable`; an A/A arm (double
precision against double precision) must agree in every case, and a harness in which it does not is broken.
Determinism: the selection and the comparison are exact; both attributions are replayable under class D2.

## Considerations

- A one-in-eight sample bounds drift slowly: forty comparable checks take 320 lane attributions. The gate
  measures agreement on the whole planted suite first; the sample is for what comes after.
- Agreement with double precision is not correctness. Both paths share the model, the contact formulation and
  the factor list; the cross-check detects arithmetic sensitivity, which is the lane's specific risk.
- If KILL-ENSEMBLE fires, the ledger is kept as the record of why, and the cross-check is removed with the lane.
