## Background

The single-precision lane (attribution on the originating tier in single precision, admitted by a measured
rule) is a Frontier mechanism, and a Frontier capability is implemented only when its gate has a recorded pass
with measured values and its kill-gate decision is on record. Its gate, defined with the lane (claim: Known
answer for its first four clauses; the last clause is a comparison of methods measured as a registered
experiment; first milestone M3; thresholds are TARGETs until measured):

> **GATE-AUTOPSY-09.** The linear map and the pendulum control behave as stated. G(W) is recorded for the
> reference quadruped and humanoid policies at 0.5 s, 1 s and 2 s. On the planted-cause suite of GATE-AUTOPSY-03
> with windows of at most 1 s, the lane names the same top factor as double precision in at least 95% of
> admitted cases. A planted solver artifact that double precision does not reproduce is labelled
> `tier-specific` with the right variant. Bulk attribution of 200 failures is at least 3 times faster in wall
> time than on `batch-cpu`.

The lane also amended GATE-AUTOPSY-03, which now requires the planted factor to rank first in at least 90% of
failures "in the single-precision lane for windows of at most 1 s" as well as in double precision. This script
measures all five clauses of the first gate and the lane clause of the second, on a real device, from the
user's side, and produces the receipt from which the GATE-AUTOPSY-09 row is recorded and in which the lane
clause is read by GATE-AUTOPSY-03's one recorder, N27b-09. Its figures are exactly what the kill gate KILL-ENSEMBLE reads (agreement
with double precision below 90%; or fewer than half of the reference failures admitted at any window of 0.5 s or
more; or a bulk speedup below 3).

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/autopsy_lane_f32.sh`; the five clauses; the lane clause of GATE-AUTOPSY-03; the registered
negative controls; the GATE-AUTOPSY-09 row, and the lane clause in the receipt that GATE-AUTOPSY-03's recorder
reads; the pre-registration of how ambiguous clauses are read.
Out: the mechanisms (N27b-23 to N27b-27); the kill decision (N27b-29); the double-precision clauses of
GATE-AUTOPSY-03 (N27b-09).

## Technical approach

1. **Pre-registration (written into the script's header and the experiment registry before the first run).**
   - *Admitted cases* are planted failures admitted at a window of at most 1 s (1 s, else 0.5 s, else 0.25 s).
   - *Lane accuracy for GATE-AUTOPSY-03* is the share of admitted cases whose rank-one factor is the planted
     rescuer with a label other than `none`. The share over all 200 failures (counting unadmitted cases as not
     correct) is recorded beside it, so the stricter reading is on file.
   - *Agreement* is the top-factor component of the cross-check's comparison rule (N27b-26, step 3) applied to
     every admitted case, not to its one-in-eight sample: no verb input sets the sampling rate, so the script
     runs the double-precision repeat of each admitted case itself (`--lane f64` on the same fork), over
     comparable pairs (a case double precision does not reproduce is refused with FROB-REF-584 and counted not
     comparable).
   - *Bulk speedup* is the one-sided 95% lower confidence bound on the paired ratio of wall times over ten
     blocks of 20 failures; the cross-check at one in eight is charged to the lane's arm.
2. **Steps.**
   | Step | What runs | Figure and threshold |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | The two host known answers (`cargo test -p frob-autopsy --test lane_admit -- linear_map_admission pendulum_marginal pendulum_stable_at_1e12`), then the same two cases through the verb on the conformance build of `frob`, by their host-fixture handles (N27b-03): `frob autopsy intervene fixture:host/linear-map --lane tier-f32 --plan-only --key <k> --json` and the same with `fixture:host/upright-pendulum` | Linear map: G after 40 steps within 1% of 602. Pendulum: ρ₀ at 10⁻⁷ above 0.1 (about one half), not admitted, outcome `marginal`; stable at 10⁻¹² |
   | 2 | For the quadruped and the humanoid policy: 50 failing worlds each, drawn by address from one evaluation on the device; `frob run fork <run> --world <w> --before failure --dt <0.5s, 1.0s, 2.0s> --key <k> --json`; `frob autopsy intervene <fork> --lane tier-f32 --plan-only --key <k> --json` for each | Recorded per robot and window: G(W) maximum and median, `lambda_envelope` (ln G / steps, an upper envelope, never called λ̂; the divergence rate λ̂ of record is the pairing report's, N35-08), ρ₀, the admitted share. No threshold on G; the admitted share at windows of 0.5 s or more is the kill gate's second figure (at least one half) |
   | 3 | The planted suite recorded on the device; per failure, after `frob run fork <run> --world <w> --before failure --dt 1.0s --key <k> --json`, `frob autopsy intervene <fork> --do <candidate> …` (the ten candidates) `--lane tier-f32 --key <k> --json` and `frob autopsy attribute <fork> --lane tier-f32 --json`; then, for every admitted case, `frob autopsy intervene <fork> --do … --lane f64 --key <k2> --json` and `frob autopsy attribute <fork> --lane f64 --json`, compared by the cross-check's rule | Lane accuracy on admitted cases at least 90% (clause of GATE-AUTOPSY-03); top-factor agreement with double precision at least 95% of admitted, comparable cases; the out-of-list controls name nothing |
   | 4 | The planted solver artifact (the under-converged contact solve on the block-on-slope fixture) through `frob run fork <run> --world <w> --before failure --dt 1.0s --key <k> --json`, `frob autopsy intervene <fork> --lane auto --key <k> --json`, `frob autopsy attribute <fork> --lane auto --json` | `double_precision: "not-equivalent"`; outcome `tier-specific: iterations-x2`; an artifact-guard record attached |
   | 5 | The registered speed experiment: the 200 planted failures in ten blocks, each attributed with pairs by arm A (`--lane auto` on the device) and arm B (double precision on `batch-cpu`, all cores) | Ceiling computed and stored first; lower bound of the paired wall-time ratio at least 3; A/A below 1.02; the planted slowdown detected; disclosure block attached |
3. **Records.** The script measures and writes no gate status (a test never does); every `run` call in it
   carries `--key`, and two arms that are compared carry different keys. Its `summary` holds all five clauses'
   measured values. The row is entered from the receipt of the gate's tier run, which the script runs as its
   last step (`e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-AUTOPSY-09 --mutations`, that is `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo
   test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-AUTOPSY-09`) and
   whose hash it logs. The entries drive the same fixtures, suite and keys through the same verbs (so they read
   what this script stored and buy no fork), take the fifth clause from the registered experiment's record,
   write the lane clause of GATE-AUTOPSY-03 (lane accuracy on admitted cases and over all 200) into the same
   receipt, and plant the registered controls in the same run (admission bypassed, a mutation; the pendulum;
   the out-of-list set; the A/A speed arm); the script asserts from `E2E_LAST_RECEIPT` that each is `caught`.
   The line of GATE-AUTOPSY-09 is `pass` only if every thresholded clause passes and the recorded-only clauses
   are present. Its row is recorded with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate
   GATE-AUTOPSY-09 --receipt <hash>` by a session that did not implement the lane (N38b-19), and cites the
   commit, the hardware fingerprint, the seeds, the suite identity, the claim identifiers of the attributions
   and the receipt hash. This bead records no row of GATE-AUTOPSY-03: that gate has one recorder, N27b-09,
   whose entry reads the lane clause from this receipt and whose line is `pass` only when its double-precision
   clauses and the lane clause both pass.
4. **Repair cycle.** A first `fail` opens one repair cycle on the unchanged suite, thresholds and
   pre-registration; the second result is final and is what the kill decision reads.
5. **Devices.** The script runs once per available device family and its receipt holds one sub-record per family; the gate
   passes on a family by family basis, and a family that lacks a class-D2 record is recorded as `not run:
   no determinism licence`, which is not a pass.
6. **Self-test.** `--self-test` runs step 1 and eight failures of step 3 on `batch-cpu` in `f32` (the lane is
   defined for any single-precision originating tier), without the tier entries and so without a receipt.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All five clauses are measured with denominators; the receipt of the tier run holds them per device
      family and the GATE-AUTOPSY-09 row recorded from it holds measured values, not targets.
- [ ] The lane clause of GATE-AUTOPSY-03 is in the receipt with both denominators (admitted; all 200), where
      that gate's recorder (N27b-09) reads it; this bead records no GATE-AUTOPSY-03 row.
- [ ] The registered negative controls are exercised and caught on every run: the pendulum is not admitted; the
      out-of-list failures name nothing; the A/A speed arm stays below 1.02; the admission-bypass mutation (the
      pendulum is then attributed) is caught in the tier run, as its receipt shows. Under `--verify-plants`
      each of the script's declared plants makes it exit 1 with a failed assertion that names the plant.
- [ ] The speed experiment's six parts are in the ledger with a timestamp before the first block ran, and the
      ceiling is at least 3 or the clause is recorded as unreachable with the stored ceiling (which is a `fail`
      of the clause, to be read by the kill decision).
- [ ] The three figures the kill gate reads (agreement, admitted share, speed bound) appear in the `summary`
      line with their thresholds.
- [ ] The script is idempotent, seed-explicit, exits non-zero on `fail`, and never logs scene content.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
None of its own beyond `--self-test`; arithmetic of the bounds is tested in the cross-check and bulk beads.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/autopsy_lane_f32.sh`. Log `target/e2e-logs/autopsy_lane_f32/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside
it. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N27b-28`), `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the
envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), and the device family. `metric`
lines with units and thresholds: `linear_map_G40` (602 ± 1%); `pendulum_rho0_1e-7` (above 0.1) and
`pendulum_admitted` (false); per robot and window `G_max`, `G_median`, `lambda_envelope`, `rho0`, `admitted_share`
(0.5 at windows of 0.5 s or more); per planted failure `admitted`, `window`, `rank_one`, `correct`,
`crosscheck_top`, `crosscheck_label`, `crosscheck_signs`; `lane_accuracy_admitted` (0.90),
`lane_accuracy_all200`, `agreement_top` (0.95) with its lower bound; `tier_specific_label` (expected
`tier-specific: iterations-x2`); `ceiling` (3), per block `wall_s_lane`, `wall_s_cpu`, `ratio`,
`speedup_lower_bound` (3), `aa_ratio` (below 1.02). On failure: the first case that disagrees with both
attributions' tables and the fork, ledger and admission identifiers needed to replay it. Final `summary`:
assertions passed and failed; the three kill-gate figures; gates touched (GATE-AUTOPSY-09, GATE-AUTOPSY-03).

### Known answers and negative controls
The lane's known answers: (i) x ← 1.1735·x has exponent ln 1.1735 = 0.16 per step; after 40 steps a perturbation
has grown by 602 and the measured G must match within 1%; (ii) the pendulum released 5 × 10⁻⁸ rad from upright
with no control and the verdict "falls to the positive side": a placebo of 10⁻⁷ rad flips the side whenever its
sign is negative, so ρ₀ is about one half, the lane must not admit the case and the label is `marginal`; at a
placebo of 10⁻¹² the side is stable. KA-670 through the lane. The planted solver artifact. Admission bypassed
is the gate's registered mutation control, planted by the tier run (no product binary carries a mutation
feature, N38b-04). Planted failures of the script (header `plants=self-crosscheck,no-ceiling`, injected under
`E2E_PLANT_FAILURE`): `self-crosscheck`, the double-precision repeat replaced by the lane attribution itself
(agreement 100% by construction: the script detects identical ledger hashes and fails); `no-ceiling`, the speed
experiment's record presented without its stored ceiling (in a scratch copy, never the committed register),
which the register's admission check refuses with FROB-REF-866, so no block runs and the speed clause fails.
Determinism lanes: steps 1 to 4 twice on one device and at two batch packings; lane
ledgers identical.

## Considerations

- The gate names two claims. Its first four clauses are known answers and recorded figures; the fifth is a
  comparison of methods and is measured under the experiment form (ceiling, arms, potency control, paired lower
  bound, fallback, reachability). The row records the governing claim as Known answer and carries the
  experiment's identifier for the fifth clause.
- G(W) for the humanoid needs a humanoid policy on the device at M3. If none exists, that sub-clause is recorded
  as `not run` with the reason and the gate cannot pass until it is; it is not silently dropped.
- If the measured ceiling is below 3 the speed clause fails by arithmetic, and the kill decision (N27b-29)
  takes it from there; the script does not retune block sizes or counts to move the ratio.
- The figures of step 2 are the first measurements of OPEN-AUTOPSY-3 on our own tiers (owner: Cortex lead;
  default until then: leads of at most 2 s with the growth of placebo separation reported in every
  attribution).
