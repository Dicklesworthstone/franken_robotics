## Background

Four static analyses were added to the studio after its first formulation, each from a demonstrated failure of
a plausible shortcut, and each needing no simulation:

| ID | Analysis | Question | Mathematics | In `check` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-20 | Energy-budget feasibility | Can the mission's mandatory work be paid from the declared energy and power budget? Can the body deliver more energy into a contact than the safety budget allows? | A lower bound on mandatory work (potential rise, unavoidable dissipation) against stored, injected and approach kinetic energy | ✓ when a budget is bound |
| A-21 | Deadline and latency closure | Does the last informative observation arrive before the decision it must inform, on every admitted schedule? | The seven instants of the clock contract composed along the sense–decide–actuate path in exact rational time | ✓ |
| A-22 | Sampled-loop stability | Is each actuator's current and speed loop stable at the declared control period, feedforward law and reflected inertia? | The one-tick map of the sampled-data loop with the hold modelled exactly; its spectral radius | ✓ |
| A-23 | Shared-bus schedule | Can all consumers on a shared electrical bus meet their demands by their deadlines, jointly? | Joint time-indexed feasibility over the bus contract; never a sum of peaks | ✓ |

Each analysis is its own bead (N28a-07, N28a-08, N28a-09, N28a-10). This bead is the integration: it registers
the four with the preflight runner so that `frob design check` runs them, shows that the two-second bound of
`check` survives, and records the first rows of the gate GATE-ATEL-05, which is due in parts: the rows for A-20
to A-23 at the first studio milestone (M1), the rest (A-15 to A-19, A-24 to A-26) at M4. The gate's form: each
analysis passes its known answers, its negative control fails, and the analyses marked ✓ keep `design check`
under 2 s.

Seed done-condition: GATE-ATEL-05 rows for A-20 to A-23; the additive peak rule, which falsely prunes the
two-finger bus, is caught.

## Scope

In: registration of the four analyses in the stage runner, their `check` policy (which findings refuse, with
which codes), the binding rule for "✓ when bound", the timing measurement, the gate rows with measured values,
the prune-soundness hook. Out: the analyses' mathematics (their beads); the remaining rows of the gate
(BD-348); the stage runner (N28ap1-01); the assembly of the real stage-1-to-6 analyses and the archetype
templates (BD-110, BD-109); the memo graph (BD-111). Order of work: the registration is written against the
preflight staging contract (N14b-10: the stage table, statuses, options and budgets) and runs in the stage
runner N28ap1-01, which was split from BD-110 in round 1 so that it needs no real analysis; both are
dependencies, so the lines that run the four analyses inside `design.check` close here. BD-110 and BD-109 are
higher-numbered seeds: the measurement of `design check` on the archetype templates with the four analyses on
is made by N28a-29's script (which depends on this bead and on BD-110) and written into the same gate rows.

## Technical approach

1. **Stage placement, order and binding come from the second stage table (N14bx-01), not from this bead.**
   That table is the compiled constant `CHECK_ANALYSES` beside `STAGES` in
   `crates/frob-analyze/src/preflight/stages.rs`; for these four it says: all run in stage 6, "statics and
   sizing", after the actuator-envelope and bus analyses whose results they read, in the order A-23 (after
   A-11's bus feasibility, which it refines), A-22, A-21, A-20 (then A-24 when bound, BD-348). This bead
   registers the four analyses' executors under their `AnalysisId`s and obtains every placement decision by
   calling N14bx-01's `plan_check(req, landed)`, `stop_after(plan, refused_at, all)` and `exit_for(outcome)`; it
   writes no order, binding or exit rule of its own (a second, hand-written order is the contract violation
   that N14bx-01's `runner_order_from_constants` test catches). Each analysis has its own timing line. A request
   with `--stages` that excludes stage 6 marks them `not-run: excluded`.
2. **Binding (as the table states it).** A-20 joins only when the request names a budget or a task segment with
   a rise (`--budget`, `--task`); unbound, it is listed as `not-run: unbound` with the flag that would bind it.
   A-21 runs on every chain the task declares and, with no task, reports worst-case ages only. A-22 runs on
   every actuated joint with a controller card. A-23 runs on every shared bus with two or more consumers.
3. **Refusal policy inside `check`**, as the policy rows of `registries/preflight.toml` (N14bx-01, held to the
   constants by a build-time test) give it; the finding kinds this bead's executors emit carry exactly those
   names, with the decision, joint or bus as the finding's subject:

   | Finding kind | Severity in `check` | Code |
   |---|---|---|
   | `A-20.energy`, `A-20.power` (an `outer` exclusion) | Refusing | FROB-REF-115 (a design budget exceeded: energy or peak power) |
   | `A-20.source-unbound` (an exclusion attempted with a declared source unbound) | Refusing | FROB-REF-551 |
   | `A-20.contact-energy` | High, not refusing | — |
   | `A-21.late` (subject: the decision) | High, not refusing (no code is registered) | — |
   | `A-21.stale` (subject: the decision; served only by a sample older than a declared freshness bound) | Low, reported (N14bx-01 policy row) | — |
   | `A-22.unstable`, `A-22.tier-unstable` (subject: the joint) | High, not refusing (no code is registered) | — |
   | `A-22.slow` (subject: the joint; contraction too slow for a declared settling deadline) | Low, reported (N14bx-01 policy row) | — |
   | `A-23.bus-infeasible` (subject: the bus, with the binding ticks) | Refusing | FROB-REF-133 |
   | `A-23.tight` (subject: the bus; few feasible schedules, with the count) | Info, reported (N14bx-01 policy row) | — |

   A High finding gives exit 2 (by `exit_for`) and appears first in `next_actions`. The stage-6 budget is the
   revised one of N14bx-01: 755 ms with A-23, A-22 and A-21 always bound (600 ms for A-08, A-10, A-11 and the
   beam rung, plus 155 ms), 875 ms when A-20 and A-24 are bound.
4. **Timing.** The gate measures the 95th percentile of total `check` time over 200 runs per template, with an
   empty memo graph, with and without the four analyses, and each analysis's own median and 95th percentile.
   Budgets (TARGET): A-20 20 ms, A-21 50 ms, A-22 5 ms, A-23 100 ms; total `check` under 2 s.
5. **Gate rows.** For each analysis one row in the gate register: `gate = "GATE-ATEL-05"`, `part = "A-2x"`,
   the known answer's measured value against its truth, the negative control's identifier and `caught: true`,
   the timing, commit, hardware fingerprint and seed. A row carries measured values, not targets. This bead is
   the recorder of these four parts: each value is recorded from the Gauntlet run's receipt with `cargo run -p
   frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ATEL-05 --receipt <hash>` (no script writes a row).
   The sub-part "`design check` under 2 s with the four on, on the archetype templates" needs the templates
   (BD-109) and the assembled stages (BD-110), higher seeds this bead cannot wait for; it is measured and
   recorded by N28a-29, the bead the gate register names for it.
6. **Prune-soundness hook.** Every exclusion issued by these analyses inside a campaign is logged with the
   rule's identifier (`A-20.energy`, `A-23.bus-infeasible`) so the cascade's audit can sample pruned designs
   and run them; one feasible pruned design suspends that rule for the campaign and files the case as a
   negative-control seed. This bead supplies the log record and the `suspended` switch per rule.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob design check` on a design with a shared bus, a rate loop and a declared observation chain runs
      A-21, A-22 and A-23 and lists A-20 as `not-run: unbound`; with `--budget` it runs A-20.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-05, part A-20: 0.94176 J against 0.90 J excludes; 0.05 J of approach energy removes the
      exclusion; the control "exclusion issued with approach energy unbound" is caught (551).
- [ ] Part A-21: packet at tick 1, decisions at ticks 2 and 4; delays to ticks 2 and 3 change the answer only at
      the second delay and only for the first decision; the content-only check is caught.
- [ ] Part A-22: spectral radii 1.4820 and 0.9207; the instantaneous-current model (2.6917 for both) is caught.
- [ ] Part A-23: zero schedules at a one-tick deadline, exactly two at two ticks; the additive-peak rule, which
      falsely prunes the two-finger bus, is caught.
- [ ] The per-analysis times are recorded against 100, 5, 50 and 20 ms (A-23, A-22, A-21, A-20) on this bead's
      fixtures run through the runner, 200 runs each with an empty memo graph; that `design check` stays under
      2 s at the 95th percentile on the first four archetypes with the four analyses on, and stage 6 within its
      revised budget (755 ms unbound, 875 ms with A-20 and A-24 bound), is measured by step 9 of N28a-29 and
      recorded by that bead into GATE-ATEL-05's M1 part with `record_gate`.
- [ ] The four gate rows exist with measured values; the remaining parts of the gate are listed as
      `not-yet-due (M4)`.
- [ ] A rule marked `suspended` no longer refuses and its findings are downgraded to Info for the campaign.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-analyze/tests/check_a20_a23.rs`:
- `stage6_order_and_timing_lines`: four timing lines in the order `plan_check` gives (A-23, A-22, A-21, A-20);
  the order is read from N14bx-01's constants, and a planted local order in this bead's registration fails
  N14bx-01's `runner_order_from_constants`.
- `finding_kinds_match_policy_rows`: every finding kind the four executors emit has a row in
  `registries/preflight.toml` with the severity and code of the table above.
- `a20_unbound_listed_not_run`, `a20_bound_runs`.
- `policy_table`: each finding maps to the severity and code above.
- `bus_infeasible_stops_stage6_133`; `loop_unstable_is_exit_2_not_3`.
- `suspended_rule_downgrades`.
- `gate_rows_have_measured_values`: a row with a target copied into `measured` is rejected by the register
  check (a control on the record itself).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/check_a20_a23.sh`, this bead's own script (convention 11; N28a-29's `atelier_check.sh` runs it
as a stage and adds the 2 s run on the archetype templates). It sources the STD-E2E helper, stores the four
known-answer fixtures with `frob design new a2x-<fixture> --file <copy> --key "$(e2e_key new-<fixture>)"`
(N14a-21), runs each through `frob design check <design> --stages 6 --json` (with `--budget stored=0.90J
--task sill` for A-20; without them A-20 is `not-run: unbound` with the binding flag named) and through each
`frob analyze` verb, asserts the stage-6 order A-23, A-22, A-21, A-20 from the timing lines and the policy
outcomes of the table above (the infeasible bus exit 3 with 133; the unstable loop exit 2, never 3), then
runs the four negative-control mutants through the Gauntlet in the form
ADR-GAUNT-01 fixes (BD-025; there is no `frob conformance` command, and BD-025's lint `no_conformance_command`
fails a script that keeps the literal form): `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features
gauntlet-mutations --test <tier> -- GATE-ATEL-05`, with `<tier>` the test target named by the gate record's
`tier` field (written by N28c-25); the M4 parts report `not-yet-due`. NDJSON at
`target/e2e-logs/check_a20_a23/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; lines with the STD-E2E fields (`ts`, `script`,
`bead` = BD-108, `step` = `a20_a23`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block); per part the measured value, the truth, the tolerance, the control's identifier
and whether it was caught, the receipt hash, the 95th-percentile times against their budgets; on failure the
first differing value with the fixture path; and a `summary` line naming the gate.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-684, KA-685, KA-686, KA-687 with the values above; negative controls CX-306, CX-310, CX-308, CX-303. The
gate fails if any control passes unnoticed. Metamorphic: adding an unrelated sensor changes none of the four
results (their read sets do not include it), which the memo test of BD-111 also checks. Determinism: A-20,
A-21, A-23 are D0; A-22 is double precision and deterministic on one platform.

## Considerations

- Two of the four findings have no refusal code in the registry (A-21, A-22). Fail-closed default: they are
  High findings and block nothing by themselves; the loop-order rule lets a simulation proceed. Whether they
  should refuse is an open point for the refusal registry's owner; if codes are assigned, the change is a
  policy-row edit in N14bx-01's table, never a local promotion here.
- Stage placement was first chosen here as a DESIGN default before the second stage table existed; that table
  (N14bx-01) now owns it and agrees (stage 6; A-23, A-22, A-21, A-20), and this bead cites it.
- KILL-STATIC (M3) applies to all refusing uses here: if it fires, refusals become advisory lints.
- The timing gate uses an empty memo graph on purpose; the warm figures belong to the memo-graph gate.
