## Background

`frob design check` is the first stage of the studio loop and the cheapest: static analysis in seconds, before
any controller is synthesized or any world is stepped. It is also rung R0 of the evaluation ladder: it may
refuse a design or exclude it with a typed bound, and it never ranks survivors. Its purpose is feedback an agent
can act on at once: each refusal carries ranked repairs expressed as typed edits, which become the envelope's
`next_actions`.

The preflight has eight stages. Stage 1 is the model compile; stages 2 to 8 call the studio's analyses and live
in `frob-analyze`. The machine that executes them (the runner, policy file, aggregation, repair ranking,
budgets with `undecided`, stage records, the verb with its schema and exit codes) is N28ap1-01, a component
this bead depends on. This bead is the capstone of stages 1 to 6 (stages 7, place, and 8, information, need
scenes and alias search and land at M3 in BD-244): it puts the real analyses of stages 2 to 6 into the runner
with their policy rows, proves through the binary that each stage refuses what it owns, and measures the two
gates the seed names. The stage table is one artifact, the staging contract of N14b-10 (the typed constant
`STAGES`); its first six rows are reproduced here because the gate is measured against them, and where this
copy and that constant ever differ the constant governs:

The figures are budgets per stage for any archetype on the reference host with an empty memo graph. They are
not predictions and are not added into a headline. The one total committed to is that stages 1 to 6 complete
within 2 s at the 95th percentile on the archetypes; a stage that misses its own budget is a finding even when
the total passes.

Seed done-condition: GATE-ATEL-02 for the included analyses; GATE-SKEL-04 for stages 1 to 6.

## Scope

In: registering the real analyses of stages 2 to 6 (A-02, which wraps N13-04's solid-fill plausibility verdict; A-01,
A-06 at the reference pose, A-13; A-07 and stage 4's dispatch; A-03 with the symmetry assertion and the

`registries/preflight.toml`;
the cross-stage legs on the binary that need the real analyses (each stage refusing what it owns, the repairs
that repair, the archetypes clean); the measurement of GATE-ATEL-02 (M1 part) and of GATE-SKEL-04 for stages 1
to 6, with their gate rows.

Out, with the bead that has it: the runner, policy file mechanism, aggregation, repair ranking, budgets,
stage records and the verb surface (N28ap1-01), including the verb's `estimate_only` path (VERBS-design gives
`design.check` `estimable = true`: `--estimate` returns `plan_check`'s plan priced by the stage and row budgets
and runs nothing) and its refusal list of 31 codes; the staging contract (N14b-10) and the second table with
`plan_check`, `stop_after`, `exit_for` (N14bx-01); the analyses themselves (BD-100 to BD-106, N13-04, N14a-10,
N14a-11, N14b-11, N15b-01 and their owners); A-20 to A-23 and their GATE-ATEL-05 rows (BD-108); the seeded-defect corpus
and the valid-design generator (N14b-18); stages 7 and 8 (BD-244); the memo graph (BD-111); the remaining
analyses of GATE-ATEL-02 at M3 and M4 (A-04 by BD-332; A-05, A-09, A-14 full forms by their seeds).

## Technical approach

1. **Assembly.** Each analysis of stages 2 to 6 registers its executor under its `AnalysisId` through the
   analysis contract (N28a-04); the runner (N28ap1-01) finds it by the stage table. For every finding kind these
   analyses emit, one policy row in `registries/preflight.toml` says refusing (with the stage's owned code) or
   reported; the runner's build-time test holds the rows to `STAGES` and to each analysis's declared
   `finding_kinds()` (N28a-04). The point-foot biped's `A-08.no-static-stability` is reported (Info), never
   refusing; the parallel-roller mecanum's `A-07.motion-missing` refuses with 118 at stage 4. N15b-01's
   `wiring::check_joins` is registered at stage 1 (its kinds refuse with 101 or 114) and
   `wiring::check_structure` at stage 5 (its kinds refuse with 108); N13-04's plausibility verdict reaches
   stage 2 through A-02's kind `A-02.<body>.solid-fill` (BD-100), whose policy row is refusing with 168. The conformance run over the real stage 1 to 6 analyses (the
   archetypes, the first-rejection fixtures and the corpus) fails on any finding that carries the run-time note
   `policy-missing`.
2. **Cross-stage legs.** Through `frob --json design check` on real fixtures, each stage refuses what it owns,
   with exit 3, the stage, the code, the pointer and a first repair that, applied, clears the refusal:
   - the eight first-rejection fixtures of N14b-18, one per owning stage and code;
   - stage 3, A-13: `quad-mammal` with the hip adduction range widened until the thigh sweeps through the body
     is refused at stage 3 with FROB-REF-123, exit 3, and a `set-limits` repair that restores the declared range
     (the `design.check` leg moved here from BD-104, which owns the swept-chart mathematics);
   - stage 5, A-03: `frob design check --stages 5` on the forward-lidar `quad-mammal` with `assert_obs: C2xC2`
     is refused with FROB-REF-109, exit 3, with BD-102's symmetry report (Γ_mech of order 4, Γ_obs of order 2,
     the lidar named as the breaker) in `next_actions` (the leg moved here from BD-102);
   - the point-foot biped template: exit 2, `A-08.no-static-stability`, never 3;
   - the parallel-roller mecanum: exit 3 at stage 4 with FROB-REF-118 and a roller-angle repair;
   - stage 2, N13-04: the torso box 0.42 × 0.20 × 0.10 m at 2,700 kg/m³ written `solid` in a design with
     `mass_max` 14 kg (m_solid 22.68 kg against the bound 3.5 kg) refused at stage 2 with FROB-REF-168, exit 3,
     first repair `{shell: t}`;
   - stage 1 and stage 5, N15b-01: a port joined to a port of the wrong kind refused at stage 1 with
     FROB-REF-101, and one reversed bus port refused at stage 5 with FROB-REF-108, each exit 3.
3. **Timing measurement (GATE-ATEL-02, M1 part).** Per archetype (`quad-mammal`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`,
   `mecanum`), 200 cold runs (empty memo graph, fresh process state for caches), recording median and 95th
   percentile of every stage and of the total, with the commit, hardware fingerprint and seed. A stage over its
   budget at the 95th percentile produces a `budget-miss` entry in the gate row even if the total is under 2 s.
   Stage 5 is recorded against 150 ms on each of the four archetypes (the timing leg moved here from BD-102).
   Each included analysis (A-01, A-02, A-03, A-06 at the reference pose, A-07, A-08, A-10, A-11, A-13) passes its
   known-answer tests through `check`. The row carries measured values, never targets.
4. **Defect measurement (GATE-SKEL-04, stages 1 to 6).** Run the seeded-defect corpus of N14b-18 (100 defects,
   each labelled with its owning stage and code, and the generator of 10³ valid designs): every defect must be
   refused; at least 95 of 100 by the stage and code that own them; zero refusals on the archetypes present and
   on the 10³ generated valid designs. Each misattributed defect is listed with the stage and code it got.
5. **Gate rows.** This bead is the one recorder of both gates' M1 parts (stages 1 to 6): each run's receipt is
   recorded with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate <id> --receipt <hash>` into
   `registries/gates.toml`, in the record form of N40-01, under the Atelier rows of N28c-25 (no script writes a
   gate row itself): `gate`, `part` (`M1`), measured values with units, thresholds, `budget-miss`
   entries, the controls and whether each was caught, commit, hardware fingerprint, seed; the parts due later
   are `not-yet-due (M3)` or `(M4)`.
6. **Documentation.** `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-analyze` prints the stage table (from `STAGES`), the policy rows of
   this bead and the gate results' location.

      percentiles are in the gate row, stage 5 within 150 ms on each; budget misses are listed.

      zero refusals on the four archetypes and on 10³ random valid designs.
- [ ] Every refusal carries a code from the stage's owned list, a field pointer and at least one typed-edit
      repair, or an explicit `no_repair_reason`.
- [ ] `next_actions` are runnable: for each of the eight first-rejection fixtures, applying the first action and
      re-running the check clears that refusal.
- [ ] The widened hip adduction on `quad-mammal` is refused at stage 3 with FROB-REF-123, exit 3, and a
      `set-limits` repair; `--stages 5` with `assert_obs: C2xC2` on the forward-lidar `quad-mammal` is refused
      with FROB-REF-109, exit 3, with BD-102's report in `next_actions`.
- [ ] The point-foot biped is reported and not refused (exit 2); the parallel-roller mecanum is refused at stage
      4 (exit 3, 118).
- [ ] Every finding kind of stages 1 to 6 has a policy row; the runner's agreement test passes; the conformance
      run over the real analyses reports no finding with the note `policy-missing`.
- [ ] N15b-01's `check_joins` (stage 1: 101, 114) and `check_structure` (stage 5: 108) are registered and

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-analyze/tests/preflight_assembled.rs`, `cargo test -p frob-analyze preflight_assembled`:
- `every_stage_refuses_its_own`: the eight first-rejection fixtures give the expected stage, code and pointer.
- `first_repair_clears`: applying the first `next_action` of each clears its refusal.
- `a13_hip_adduction_123`, `a03_assert_obs_109_with_report`.
- `point_foot_exit_2`, `parallel_roller_mecanum_118`.
- `policy_rows_cover_stage_1_to_6_kinds`: every finding kind the registered analyses declare has a row.
- `no_policy_missing_in_conformance`: over the archetypes, the first-rejection fixtures and the corpus, no
  finding carries `policy-missing`; with one policy row removed on a scratch copy of the policy file, the run
  fails naming the kind.
- `stage_entry_points_registered`: `solid_torso_168_stage_2` (22.68 kg against 3.5 kg), `wrong_kind_join_101_stage_1`,
  `reversed_bus_port_108_stage_5`; each code is raised at its stage and at no other.
- `archetypes_clean`: the four archetypes give exit 0 or 2 and no refusal.
- `gate_row_has_measured_values`: a row with a target copied into `measured` is rejected by the register check.
- `misattribution_listed`: a planted corpus label with the wrong stage is reported as misattributed, not
  silently counted.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/atelier_check.sh` (bead N28a-29) drives the real `frob` binary: checks the four templates (exit
0 or 2), the eight first-rejection fixtures (exit 3, expected code and stage), the A-13 and A-03 legs above,
applies the first `next_action` and re-checks, then runs the two gate measurements in the Gauntlet's one run
form (ADR-GAUNT-01, BD-025; there is no `frob conformance` command, and BD-025's lint `no_conformance_command`
fails a script that writes one): `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers
--features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-ATEL-02` and the same with `-- GATE-SKEL-04` (both gates are of claim
class known answer, so the record's `tier` field names `g1_known_answers`; the M1 part, stages 1 to 6, is the
part the records hold at M1). The receipts' hashes are logged.
Logged per call (STD-E2E NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/atelier_check/<utc>.ndjson`): command line, exit code, wall
time, per-stage status and time against budget, the refusal code and pointer, the first three repairs, the
evidence block, the defect identifier and its expected stage and code; per gate a `metric` line per stage with
median and 95th percentile in milliseconds against the budget and the count of refused, correctly attributed
and false-refused designs; on failure the first defect or archetype that differs and the paths to reproduce; a
summary with assertions passed and failed and the gates touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers by analysis: Sarrus, Bennett and four-bar mobility 1 (KA-037, KA-038); the growth vectors and the
parallel-roller mecanum (KA-058, KA-268, KA-681); the stance values (KA-682); the symmetry of the quadruped
with a forward lidar (KA-680); the mass fixtures of the inertia construction; the co-design and envelope
fixtures of the actuator beads; KA-291 for the corpus. Negative controls: the 100 seeded defects; a mutant
policy row that makes `A-08.no-static-stability` refusing (the point-foot biped then exits 3); a mutant that
drops the A-13 analysis from stage 3 (the hip-adduction leg passes, which the test detects); a timing record
that copies the target into `measured`. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug
and release; across platforms (D0 analyses byte-identical).

## Considerations

- KILL-STATIC (decided at M3 on the design corpus, N28a-22, measured by BD-250): if static verdicts predict
  simulated failure no better than a mass-and-torque heuristic, `design check` verdicts are downgraded to
  advisory lints and the pruning claim is withdrawn. The policy rows of this bead are where that switch lives.
- The measured ceiling of static checks as a pruning lever is modest (pruning trivial failures cuts trainings by
  at most about 1.19 times; FACT, source register); their value is feedback in seconds and typed repairs.
- Targets for a warm check after a one-parameter edit belong to the memo-graph gate, not to this bead.
- An analysis added later must fit its stage's budget or become a separate verb; the gate re-measures the total
  whenever a stage's analysis list changes.
- The timing figures depend on the reference host; the row records its fingerprint so that a later run on
  another host is never compared without saying so.
