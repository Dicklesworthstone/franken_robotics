## Background

The studio runs static analyses before any simulation and lets them refuse a design. That is only justified if
their verdicts say something about what a simulation would show. The bet (BET-05): static analysis prunes bad
designs before simulation and does not throw away a winner. Its measured ceiling as a pruning lever is modest
(in published loops under 2% of morphology proposals fail validation before training and about 16% of trained
candidates are degenerate, so pruning trivial failures cuts trainings by at most about 1.19 times; FACT, source
register); its main value is feedback in seconds. The bet has a falsifier fixed in advance: on the design
corpus, `design check` verdicts predict simulated failure no better than a mass-and-torque heuristic (area
under the ROC curve below 0.8, or less than 0.05 above the heuristic); or the audit of discarded strata finds a
discarded design in the true top-k; or the planted winner is discarded.

This bead runs that measurement at the third milestone, once the design corpus exists, and records the gate
GATE-ATEL-01: area under the ROC curve at least 0.8 and at least 0.05 above the heuristic baseline at a 95%
paired lower bound; planted defective designs flagged and a planted sound design passed. The decision that
follows from a failure (KILL-STATIC: verdicts downgraded to advisory lints, the pruning claim withdrawn) is its
own bead, N28a-22, which reads this bead's record.

The corpus (built by another bead): about 200 designs made of templates, parameter perturbations of them, and
designs with planted defects of known kind (an undersized knee actuator, an inertia that breaks static
stability, a wheelbase too long for an aisle), with planted winners for auditing screens. Its labels are fixed
before any analysis is tuned; a relabel changes the corpus identity.

Seed done-condition: GATE-ATEL-01; KILL-STATIC decided (the decision is recorded by N28a-22 from this
measurement).

## Scope

In: the pre-registration, the score definition, the heuristic arm, the split, the ROC computation with paired
bounds, the potency controls, the discard audit on the corpus, the gate row, the measurement record for the
kill decision. Out: building or labelling the corpus (BD-248); the analyses (their beads; stages 1 to 6 through BD-110,
stages 7 and 8 through BD-244, both dependencies because the check score reads every stage the corpus task
binds); the kill decision (N28a-22); the campaign-time screening audit (BD-345).

## Technical approach

1. **Pre-registration (a fixed-n study record, committed before any corpus design is checked).** The file
   `registrations/atel-01.toml`, committed, whose BLAKE3 digest every receipt of the gate carries. It holds:
   the corpus identity (label-file hash); the split: a held-out third chosen by a keyed stream over corpus ids,
   stratified by archetype and defect kind, the other two thirds available for tuning analysis thresholds; the
   outcome: the corpus's frozen binary label "fails its task family in simulation" (failure of the requirement
   on a majority of the family's standard conditions); the two scores below; the thresholds 0.8 and 0.05; the
   bootstrap seed and count; the top-k for the discard audit (k = 10 per archetype family). It also holds the
   six parts that N28c-25's check `gates_atelier_form` requires of a method gate before its first run, and
   refuses the run without: (i) the arms: the check score, and the mass-and-torque heuristic as the strongest
   conventional alternative at equal resources (both read the same design files and nothing else); (ii) the
   potency controls and the clean case of step 6; (iii) the decision statistic, the paired lower bound of Δ,
   never a ratio of means; (iv) the fallback, KILL-STATIC (verdicts downgraded to advisory lints, the pruning
   claim withdrawn); (v) the ceiling, computed before the run: pruning trivial failures cuts trainings by at
   most 1/(1 − 0.16) ≈ 1.19 times (KA-015); (vi) the pre-analysis of this fixed-n study: from the frozen
   held-out class counts (n_fail, n_pass) alone, whether the pass rule is reachable at all (the bootstrap
   lower bound of Δ on a best-case table, check score separating perfectly and heuristic at AUC 0.70, must
   reach 0.05; if it does not, the record says `pass unreachable at this corpus size` before the run and the
   corpus bead is asked to grow it) and the power at the declared alternative AUC_check = 0.90, AUC_heur = 0.70
   (2,000 seeded binormal draws of the held-out table with score correlation 0.5; DESIGN values, reported and
   never a pass condition). A threshold or score edited after the first receipt is refused
   (`changed-without-amendment`, N40-03's amendment rule).
2. **The check score.** For each design run `frob design check --all` (stages 1 to 6, plus stages 7 and 8 where
   the corpus task binds a scene) and take s_check = the minimum normalized margin over all findings (negative
   when a requirement is violated), with designs that fail stage 1 (no model) scored −∞. Lower score predicts
   failure. The score uses margins, not the refuse-or-pass bit, so the ROC curve is not a single point.
3. **The heuristic arm (the mass-and-torque heuristic), fixed in advance.** s_heur = min over actuated joints j
   of τ_cont,j·N_j·η_j/(m·g·r_j), with τ_cont the card's continuous torque, N the gear ratio, η the forward
   efficiency, m the total mass, g gravity, r_j the horizontal lever from the joint to its limb's contact at
   the reference stance (the wheel radius for a driven wheel). It is what an engineer would compute on an
   envelope; it reads no geometry beyond levers and no dynamics.
4. **Measurement on the held-out third.** AUC of each score against the label by the rank-sum statistic (ties
   at half weight). The paired difference Δ = AUC_check − AUC_heur with a 95% one-sided lower bound from 2,000
   seeded bootstrap resamples of designs (both scores resampled together). Also reported: AUC on the tuning
   two thirds (to show the optimism gap), per-archetype AUCs, and the confusion table of the refuse-or-pass
   bit.
5. **Pass rule.** AUC_check ≥ 0.8 on the held-out third, and the lower bound of Δ ≥ 0.05. Both numbers and
   their intervals are recorded whatever the outcome.
6. **Potency controls.** Every planted-defect design whose defect kind a stage claims to cover must be flagged
   by a finding of that kind (flagged, not merely scored low); the planted sound designs (templates with no
   defect) must pass with no refusing finding. Either failing fails the gate regardless of AUC.
7. **Discard audit on the corpus.** Among designs `check` would refuse, count those in the true top-k of their
   family by simulated performance (from the labels); and check that every planted winner in the corpus is
   passed. With no top-k design among m audited discards the rate of discarded winners is at most
   1 − δ^(1/m) at confidence 1 − δ (9.5% at m = 30 and δ = 0.05). One discarded true top-k design, or a
   discarded planted winner, is recorded as a falsifier of BET-05 and passed to the kill decision.
8. **Record.** The gate row (measured AUCs, Δ and its bound, control outcomes, corpus identity, commit,
   hardware fingerprint, seed) in the gate register; a `frob.beadlog/1` entry; the per-design table as an
   artifact for the kill decision and for later tuning.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pre-registration is in the ledger before the first corpus check (ordering asserted); an attempt to
      change a threshold afterwards is refused.
- [ ] The registration holds the six method-gate parts (arms, potency and clean controls, paired lower bound,
      fallback, the ceiling 1.19×, the reachability pre-analysis with the power at the declared alternative);
      `gates_atelier_form` passes on it and fails on a copy without the ceiling.
- [ ] The gate is run only in the cargo form of ADR-GAUNT-01 and recorded by this bead with `record_gate` from
      the run's receipt, never by the script (convention 9); the script contains no `frob conformance` command
      (`no_conformance_command` passes).
- [ ] The measurement runs end to end on the corpus and reports AUC_check, AUC_heur, Δ with its 95% lower
      bound, per-archetype values and the confusion table, on the held-out third only for the pass rule.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-01 is recorded with measured values: pass iff AUC_check ≥ 0.8, the lower bound of Δ ≥ 0.05,
      every covered planted defect flagged, every planted sound design passed.
- [ ] The discard audit reports m, the number of true top-k designs among discards, the bound, and the planted
      winners' outcomes.
- [ ] On a synthetic corpus with a planted score of known AUC 0.90 and a heuristic of known AUC 0.70 the
      pipeline returns values within 0.02 of the planted ones and passes; with the check score replaced by the
      heuristic it reports Δ = 0 and fails.
- [ ] The record needed by the KILL-STATIC decision exists whether the gate passed or failed.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/static_validation.rs`:
- `auc_rank_sum_exact`: scores (0.1, 0.4, 0.35, 0.8) with labels (fail, fail, pass, pass): AUC = 0.75.
- `auc_ties_half_weight`: scores (0.2, 0.5, 0.5, 0.9) with labels (fail, fail, pass, pass): AUC = 3.5/4 =
  0.875; two stage-1 failures both scored −∞ against each other count one half.
- `paired_bootstrap_seeded`: same seed, same bound; `paired_bound_below_point`.
- `split_is_keyed_and_stratified`: the held-out third has each archetype and defect kind in proportion.
- `score_min_margin`: findings with margins (+0.4, −0.31, +0.05) give −0.31; a stage-1 failure gives −∞.
- `heuristic_formula`: τ_cont = 10 N·m, N = 6, η = 0.9, m = 12 kg, r = 0.25 m ⇒ 54/29.43 = 1.835.
- `potency_controls_enforced`: a run in which one planted defect is not flagged fails even with AUC 0.95.
- `discard_bound`: m = 30, δ = 0.05 ⇒ 0.0950.
- `preregistration_frozen`: a registration whose threshold 0.8 is edited to 0.75 after the first receipt is
  refused, naming the field; a registration committed after the first corpus check is refused (ledger order).
- `method_gate_six_parts`: a registration lacking the ceiling, or the pre-analysis, fails `gates_atelier_form`
  (N28c-25) naming the missing part, and the gate entry does not run.
- `preanalysis_seeded`: the reachability verdict and the power estimate are identical for the same seed and
  class counts.
- `synthetic_corpus_known_auc` (the fifth acceptance item).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/atelier_static_validation.sh` (header: `bead=BD-250 milestone=M3 labels=atelier,gate,verb:design.check
gates=GATE-ATEL-01 seed=<seed> needs=cpu plants=heuristic-as-check,unflagged-defect,late-registration`). There
is no `frob conformance` command (ADR-GAUNT-01, BD-025's lint `no_conformance_command`). Steps:
1. `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example registry_report -- gates --chapter 28 --milestone M3 --json` (N28c-25):
   assert that GATE-ATEL-01's six method-gate parts are registered and that the digest of
   `registrations/atel-01.toml` equals the registered one.
2. From the user's side, for every held-out corpus design (a seeded sample of 12 in small mode), `frob design
   check <design> --all --task <corpus task> --json` on the real binary; log id, split, the minimum normalized
   margin, the refuse-or-pass bit, exit code (0, 2 or 3) and the finding kinds; the script's s_check must equal
   the per-design table entry the gate run writes (CLI path against library path).
3. The gate: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test <tier named by GATE-ATEL-01's record
   in registries/gates.toml> -- GATE-ATEL-01`, which checks every corpus design through the same analyses and
   writes the per-design table and a hash-named receipt; then the same with `--features gauntlet-mutations` for
   the controls (each must fail its row).
4. Log the receipt hash of step 3 with the measured values. The script writes no gate row (convention 9: a
   script never writes one): this bead, which the gate register names for GATE-ATEL-01, records it from that
   receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ATEL-01 --receipt <hash>`,
   by a session that did not write the analyses, and the script's next run reads the row back through step
   1's `registry_report` and asserts it equals the logged values.
It sources the shared helper of STD-E2E and writes `target/e2e-logs/atelier_static_validation/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript beside it; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit
code, wall time and, for `frob` calls, the envelope's `evidence` block (class, tier `static`, determinism D0,
design identity, seed). Then `metric` events for AUC_check (threshold 0.8), the lower bound of Δ (threshold
0.05), AUC_heur, the optimism gap, controls (all flagged; all sound passed), the discard audit (m, top-k
discards, the bound); the receipt hash and the registration digest; and a `summary` naming GATE-ATEL-01. On
failure it names the planted design that was not flagged or the bound that was missed, with the per-design
table path. In small mode the gate is not recorded and the log says nothing is claimable.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the AUC and bound arithmetic above; the synthetic corpus with planted AUCs; KA-015 (the ceiling
1/(1 − 0.16) ≈ 1.19). Negative controls (the gate's potency controls, registered in
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` with numbers from the allocation of N38a-40): planted defective
designs that must be flagged; a planted sound design that must pass; the heuristic-as-check mutant that must fail
the Δ rule; a planted defect whose stage finding is suppressed (`unflagged-defect`), which must fail the gate even
with AUC above 0.8; a registration committed after the first check (`late-registration`), which must stop the
run. Determinism: `check` is exact, the bootstrap is seeded; the gate row is identical across runs on one corpus
identity, twice on one machine and in debug and release.

## Considerations

- RISK-14: the corpus is tuned to the analyses, or the analyses to the corpus. Countermeasures in this bead:
  labels frozen before tuning, a held-out third, potency controls. The optimism gap between the two splits is
  reported so a reader can see how much tuning mattered.
- Reading of the threshold: "at a 95% paired lower bound" is applied to the difference from the heuristic; the
  0.8 threshold is applied to the point estimate, and the lower bound of AUC_check is recorded beside it. If
  the stricter reading (both at the lower bound) is wanted, the row already holds the number.
- Labels come from simulation with baseline controllers; a design that fails because its controller is poor is
  a labelled failure the static analysis could not have predicted. The per-archetype table and the share of
  failures the taxonomy classes as control-caused are reported so that this confound is visible.
- Compute: about 200 designs checked in seconds each; the simulations behind the labels are the corpus bead's.
