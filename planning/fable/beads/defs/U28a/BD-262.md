## Background

"Mean reward over five seeds" is how designs are usually compared, and it is wrong twice: the seeds are not
paired, and the experimenter peeks. The studio compares two designs on paired episodes (the same world
addresses, so the same scenes, disturbances and randomization draws) and, in the study type `anytime`, with a
test that stays valid however often it is looked at.

**The statistic.** For metric M with a declared bound on its paired difference (the span), let dᵢ be the
candidate's value minus the baseline's at address i and d̃ᵢ the difference shifted by the margin and scaled by
the span into [−1, 1]. A betting e-process Eₙ = ∏ᵢ (1 + λᵢ·d̃ᵢ), with each bet λᵢ chosen from earlier
outcomes only, is a nonnegative supermartingale under the null hypothesis that the candidate is no better, so
by Ville's inequality the chance it ever exceeds 1/α is at most α. The comparison can therefore stop the moment
the evidence is clear, cancel the losing arm's unfinished episodes, and keep its error rate. For a standardized
paired effect δ the process crosses 1/α after about 2·ln(1/α)/δ² pairs (about 24 pairs at δ = 0.5 and
α = 0.05), so clear differences are cheap and null differences cost the budget.

**Why address order.** Episodes are consumed in address order, never in completion order, so preemption and
reordering by the daemon leave the e-value unchanged. If the statistic consumed episodes as they finished, the
scheduler would be choosing the sample, and a device that finishes short (failed) episodes first would bias the
test. The invariant (INV-ATEL-02): a study's statistic is a pure function of the per-address outcomes taken in
address order; it does not depend on completion order, preemption, co-tenancy, shard count or memo hits.

A first, CPU-only form of both study types exists from the first studio milestone (BD-124). This bead puts the
anytime comparison on the batched device tiers, where thousands of worlds run at once and cancellation saves
real compute, and closes the comparison row of the gate GATE-ATEL-04.

Seed done-condition: type-I error at or below α under optional stopping over 10⁴ null comparisons (row of
GATE-ATEL-04); a D4 arm in a paired comparison is refused.

## Scope

In: the device execution of an anytime paired study (both arms on common addresses), the address-ordered
consumer, the stopping rule, cancellation, the confidence sequence, the one-call verb `eval.compare`, the null
calibration. Out: pairing admission and the fixed-n rule (N28a-18, N28a-19); several metrics and N-way rules
(N28a-20); the comparison record and `study.compare` (N28a-21); `explain.compare` (N28a-17); promotion to the
referee and the `provisional` label's removal (BD-263); racing inside optimization (BD-341).

## Technical approach

1. **Verb.** `frob eval compare A B --task T [--scene S] [--metric M…] [--margin m] [--level α] [--budget …]
   [--max-pairs n] [--backend <placement> | --backend <i>=<placement> …] [--state privileged|estimated] --key K
   --json`: the one-call form that plans and submits an anytime-valid paired study with defaults (two-phase
   equivalents: `study.plan` then `study.submit`). Kind `run`; idempotency key and budget required (a keyless
   call is `FROB-REF-805`; two runs a test compares carry distinct keys, or the second returns the first one's
   stored receipt); bounded synchronous wait; exit 8 with a job handle while running; progress events carry
   `{pairs_consumed, pairs_planned}`. Phase `promotion` (`evaluate` in the phase ledger). The registry row
   (VERBS-eval) is the authority on the inputs and codes; this bead implements the device form and the inputs
   `metric`, `margin`, `max_pairs` and `backend`, and the CPU form and the study-type blocks are N33b-19's.
   - *`backend`, both forms.* One placement for every arm (`batch-cpu`, `strict:gpu-cuda`, …; `referee` is the
     M1 default), or per arm `--backend <i>=<placement>`, repeated, with `i` the arm's position from 1 (DESIGN
     of the row: N35-08's per-arm placement under the one input that names a placement). An arm not named takes
     the single placement given, else the default. An index outside 1..(number of arms), an index named twice,
     or a placement outside the grammar of `sim.run`'s `--tier` is `FROB-REF-802` (pointer `/backend/<i>`)
     before any plan is written. Per-arm placement changes the execution of step 3 and is judged by the
     determinism licence (561 below), never silently re-placed.
   - *`state`.* Both arms run under the one state named (§22.3; DESIGN default `privileged`, the estimated twin a
     second comparison under its own key). This bead adds it to the shared plan path, so it holds on the CPU
     form too, and passes it to each arm's episode admission, whose check (N22a-03, the owner of `--state` on
     runs) raises `FROB-REF-464` before any episode when a design lacks a sensor its filter needs. No other bead
     adds it to this row; its callers are M4 (N20a-36, N20b-37).
   - *Inputs and codes added by others, which the device form passes through unchanged to the shared admission
     and plan so that each applies here exactly as on the CPU form:* `controller_arm` (a field of `study.plan`,
     N33b-19, M1); `pairing` (N35-07, M2: `Asked`, default `exact`); `auto_causal` (N20b-12, M2, with its pending
     `causal-receipt-missing`); `claim` (N28a-20, M3); `FROB-REF-502` (N24a-31, an `in-sample` result as an arm);
     `FROB-REF-561` (N35-07's licence check, run after `pairing::admit`, for the licence-only cases: an arm of
     unrecorded class, a pair on different device families demanded `exact` without class D1, a D3 pair
     demanded `exact`); `FROB-REF-843` (N24c-30, an item-bank refit over a sealed row); `FROB-REF-890`
     (N33c-31, arms under different stack receipts). This bead raises none of them itself; its test
     `extender_codes_reach_device_form` checks that each landed one is raised on a device backend exactly as on
     the CPU form.
2. **Plan.** Resolve designs, task, scene and controller arm to immutable identities; draw the address list
   (world, replicate) from the plan key; run the pairing admission (six rules; refusals 542, 543, 553, 555,
   556); run the study-admission check shared with the CPU form (N24c-37): a paired study on a task whose
   exposure table's coupling-test status is not `passed` for the arms' morphology class and tier family is
   refused (symbolic `exposure-table-untested`, exit 3, `retry: after-change`; repair: `frob task check
   --coupling <task> --pair A B`, the command given in `next_actions`); bind each design's settled pose; write the plan, the span, the betting rule and the address list to
   the ledger before any episode.
3. **Execution.** With one placement, both arms are submitted as slots of the same shape class or bucket on one
   device family under a D2 profile, each world keyed by its address (randomness by address; a world's
   trajectory does not depend on the batch it ran in). With per-arm placement, each arm runs on its own
   placement on the same address list; the pair is `paired-by-address` (the realized correlation reported,
   N35-08) unless both arms' records are class D1, and a request that demands `exact` of such a pair is refused
   by the licence (561) before any episode. Chunks publish per-address outcomes with digests.
4. **Address-ordered consumer.** Outcomes land in a buffer indexed by address position. The e-process advances
   only over the contiguous prefix for which both arms' outcomes are present (or the pair is `unscored`, which
   contributes factor 1 and is counted). After each advance: if Eₙ ≥ 1/α (candidate better), or the mirrored
   process for "candidate worse" crosses, or the confidence sequence lies inside ±margin, stop at index n.
5. **Stopping and cancellation.** On a stop at index n the job cancels every episode with address position
   beyond n through the job protocol (request, drain, finalize; no leaked device resources). Outcomes that had
   already completed beyond n are stored and flagged `not-consumed`; they never enter the statistic. If the
   budget or `--max-pairs` ends first, the verdict is `inconclusive` with the evidence so far.
6. **Bets (DESIGN default, a versioned rule id).** λᵢ = clip(μ̂ᵢ₋₁/(σ̂²ᵢ₋₁ + μ̂²ᵢ₋₁), 0, ½), with μ̂ and σ̂² the
   running mean and variance of earlier d̃ (initialized at λ = ¼ for the first pair). Any predictable rule
   keeps validity; the rule id is in the plan so a replay recomputes the same bets.
7. **Confidence sequence.** For a grid of candidate effects θ, run the same betting process against the null
   "mean difference = θ"; the sequence at time n is the set of θ not rejected at 1/α. Reported as an interval
   in the metric's units, valid at every stopping time.
8. **Result (minimal; the full record is N28a-21).** Per metric: disposition, e-value, confidence sequence,
   pairs used with the accounting identity, the stopping reason (`threshold`, `tie`, `budget`), tier, the
   pairing label (`paired-exact` or `paired-by-address` with the realized pair correlation), and `provisional`
   when the tier is a fast tier.
9. **Memo.** Episodes already in the memo graph for the same full identity and address are served, bit
   identical, and the e-value is the same as without them.
10. **Class.** The statistic is D0 given the outcomes (integer or rational success bits; for real-valued
    metrics the products are accumulated in a fixed order in double precision, which is deterministic on one
    platform). Evidence class `Tested`; claim kind none (a development result) until qualified.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-ATEL-04, comparison row: over 10⁴ simulated null comparisons with optional stopping (stop at first
      crossing, horizon 512 pairs), the fraction that declare the candidate better is at or below α = 0.05
      (recorded with its exact binomial upper bound), for the success-bit metric and for one real-valued metric
      with a declared span.
- [ ] A comparison with one arm recorded at class D4 is refused (FROB-REF-542) before any episode runs.
- [ ] For a planted true difference (success 0.80 against 0.50 on 512 addresses) the study stops early,
      cancels the remaining episodes, and reports pairs used below 150; the cancelled episodes release their
      device slots (no leaked resources).
- [ ] Random completion orders, preemption at snapshot boundaries, co-tenant worlds and 1, 2 and 4 shards give
      the identical e-value, stopping index and verdict (100 schedules of 1,000 episodes).
- [ ] A partly memoized comparison reaches the identical e-value.
- [ ] A metric with no declared span is refused for this study type (543).
- [ ] A paired study on a task whose exposure table has not passed the coupling test is refused
      (`exposure-table-untested`) on the device form exactly as on the CPU form, before any episode runs.
- [ ] Outcomes completed beyond the stopping index are stored as `not-consumed` and excluded.
- [ ] The verb is reachable through `frob --json` and MCP; exit 8 and job polling work; cancelling the job
      publishes no partial verdict.
- [ ] Per-arm `--backend 1=<p> --backend 2=<p>` with equal placements gives the identical e-value, stopping
      index and verdict as `--backend <p>`; with arms on different device families it is `paired-by-address`
      under `--pairing by-address` and `FROB-REF-561` under `--pairing exact` (without class D1), before any
      episode; `--backend 3=<p>` on two arms is 802.
- [ ] `--state estimated` on a design without the sensor its filter needs is `FROB-REF-464` before any episode,
      on the device form as on the CPU form; each landed extender's code (502, 561, 843, 890) is raised on a
      device backend exactly as on the CPU form.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/compare_anytime.rs`:
- `null_type_one_error`: 10⁴ null streams (paired success bits with equal success 0.6 and correlation 0.5).
- `null_type_one_error_real_metric`: differences uniform on [−1, 1].
- `power_planted_difference`: median pairs to a decision under 0.80 against 0.50.
- `address_order_invariance`: 100 random permutations of completion give one e-value trace.
- `prefix_only_consumption`: with address 3 missing, nothing beyond 2 is consumed although 4 to 100 are done.
- `not_consumed_beyond_stop`.
- `bets_are_predictable`: λᵢ is unchanged by altering outcome i or any later outcome.
- `confidence_sequence_contains_truth`: coverage of the true effect at every n ≤ 512 in at least 95% of 10³
  runs.
- `unscored_pair_factor_one`.
- `memoized_prefix_same_evalue`.
- `d4_refused_before_plan_commit`.
- `study_refused_untested_on_device`: on a device backend, a paired study on a task whose exposure table is
  `untested` (and one whose table `failed`) is refused with `exposure-table-untested` before any episode is
  submitted, through the same admission function the CPU form calls; after `task check --coupling` passes it
  is admitted, and a new table version refuses again.
- `per_arm_backend_parse`: `1=batch-cpu,2=batch-cpu` equals the single form's plan bytes; an unnamed arm takes
  the single placement; index 0, index 3 on two arms, an index named twice and an unknown placement are 802
  with the pointer `/backend/<i>`.
- `per_arm_cross_family_licence`: arms on two device families with `--pairing exact` and D2 records are 561
  (after `pairing::admit` has passed them); with `--pairing by-address` they are admitted and labelled
  `paired-by-address`; with D1 records `exact` is admitted.
- `state_passed_to_both_arms`: both arms' episode manifests carry the one state; `estimated` on a design with
  no IMU is 464 from the run admission before any episode is submitted.
- `extender_codes_reach_device_form`: for each landed extender (502, 561, 843, 890; one fixture each, shared
  with the CPU form's tests) the device backend returns the same code, pointer and exit as the CPU form; an
  extender not yet landed is listed `not-yet-due`, never passed.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-088 (empirical type-I error of the betting e-process at or below α over 10⁴ comparisons with optional
stopping). Negative controls: a mutant consumer that takes episodes in completion order with failures finishing
first (its null error exceeds α; the test must detect it); a mutant bet that peeks at the current outcome (not
predictable; caught by `bets_are_predictable` and by inflated null error); a D4 arm. Metamorphic: swapping the
arms swaps the direction of the verdict and leaves the pairs used unchanged; adding a third unrelated design to
the batch changes nothing. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker and shard counts; debug and
release; across backends inside their classes (bit-identical under D2 on one family; decision-equal under D3).

## Considerations

- One comparison is valid under optional stopping. It does nothing about an agent that submits fifty candidates
  to the same held-out worlds until one passes; that is the job of the blinded qualification lane, with fresh
  sealed world clusters and the error budget α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)] for the i-th submission. A result of this verb
  on discovery worlds is a development result.
- The betting rule trades power for robustness; the default caps bets at ½ so that one adverse pair cannot
  erase the evidence. Changing it is a new rule version.
- Under class D3 the paired test remains valid as statistics and is labelled; if uncontrolled arithmetic lowers
  the correlation between arms from 0.95 to 0.50 a comparison needs (1 − 0.50)/(1 − 0.95) = 10 times the
  episodes (illustrative), which is why D2 is the default.
- OPEN-ATEL-3 (owner: suite lead): whether the sibling e-process and race crates pass admission on the project
  toolchain. Fail-closed default: local implementations checked against the same known answers.
- Contact capacity can bias a comparison between morphologies with different contact counts; a study whose arms
  differ by more than one percentage point in degraded or capacity-refused episodes is labelled
  `capacity-biased` by the contact subsystem's rule and returns no winner.
