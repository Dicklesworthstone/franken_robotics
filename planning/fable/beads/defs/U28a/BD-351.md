## Background

When several finalists are close, or a mission answer has unresolved cells, the next unit of budget can go to
more cheap episodes, a referee batch, a fine-tune, a bench probe, a measurement of an actuator-card field, or
splitting a cell. `frob experiment suggest <designs or answer> --budget …` returns a ranked list of such
information-gathering proposals, each with a testable prediction. It is advisory. It never decides validity:
ADR-ATEL-07, parametric models rank experiments; distribution-free tests issue verdicts, so a wrong value model
costs efficiency and never validity.

The verb is registered once, by the mission subsystem, which owns it and its probe generator. This bead is the
studio's part: the ranking by decision value, which is also layer 3 of the evaluation ladder's allocation rule
(the finalist stage, decision core DC-05). Two kill gates bear on the verb and do not overlap:
KILL-AUGUR-PROBE governs the probe generator, and KILL-VOI governs the ranking.

Four rules define the ranking.

- **Ranking by decision value.** Each proposal is an action with a cost and a variance component it reduces
  (numerical, statistical or model). Its value is the reduction in expected opportunity loss of the current
  choice per unit cost, and the list ends with STOP when nothing clears the price. (FACT: the sibling
  value-of-information crate has these routines and a knowledge-gradient routine over a finite Gaussian menu;
  its own comparison test is a three-design fixture, so no saving is measured.)
- **Partition by decision, not by parameter.** For a measurement, the compatible physical mechanisms are
  partitioned by the design decision they imply; the cheapest realizable intervention whose possible readings
  separate incompatible decisions is proposed; where observation sets still overlap, two witness mechanisms
  with opposing design margins are returned; when all mechanisms imply the same choice, identification is
  skipped. The identifiability analysis A-25 checks that the proposed excitation can identify what it claims.
- **Measuring the target directly is always on the list.** Counterexample: a short probe synthesized for three
  admitted material laws chose the right gripper pad in 675 of 675 in-class cases; a fourth, delayed law
  followed the same early trace and reversed all 225 held-out decisions; measuring the candidate pad at mission
  dwell was right in 900 of 900 (FACT, executed plan-time pilot). An in-class-perfect probe cannot expose
  structural error, so every suggestion states its model class and lists the direct measurement as a
  comparator with its cost.
- **Every suggestion registers its prediction.** The predicted outcome interval of the chosen experiment is
  registered before it runs. The calibration of those intervals is the fallback trigger of DC-05.

What the evidence supports: information-gain selection against a capable agent is unsupported and plausibly
below 1.5 times (a language-model tuner beat random search more often than Gaussian-process optimization at 10
and at 30 evaluations, and model-based search lost to a genetic algorithm on a robot co-design benchmark;
FACT, source register). Layer 3 is therefore first a stop rule and a budget allocator.

Seed done-condition: GATE-ATEL-15; GATE-AUGUR-11; KILL-VOI decided. The decision is recorded by N28c-17 from
the replay's measurement; it depends on this bead, and this bead does not depend on it (a mechanism does not
depend on the decision about it).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** Finalist designs with their stored comparisons, or a mission answer with unresolved cells; the
   remaining budget and the budget price λ (value per GPU-second equivalent below which nothing is bought); the
   allowed experiments; the exchange rate between GPU-seconds and real experiment cost (OPEN-ATEL-1; with no
   rate declared, real experiments are listed unranked).
2. **Posterior.** For leader ℓ and each rival r: Δ_r ~ N(m_r, s_r²), with m_r the current paired estimate and
   s_r² the sum of variance components read from the ledgers: sampling (from the comparison), tier bias (from
   the tier-correction ledger; absent a measured correlation, the fidelity envelope), reality gap (only when a
   calibrated cell exists; otherwise excluded and the proposal list says the reality component is unpriced).
3. **Menu.** Each experiment e has a cost c_e and removes variance s̃_e² from one or more Δ_r: n more fast-tier
   pairs (sampling component only: posterior variance 1/(1/σ_s² + n/σ_pair²)); a referee batch of n pairs
   (observes Δ with variance σ_ref²/n, shrinking total variance); a fine-tune of a finalist (moves it up one
   rung; variance model from the rank-agreement ledger's residuals); a bench probe or card-field measurement
   (model component; from the probe generator, with its admitted mechanism set); a cell split (for mission
   answers; value from the cell's share of the unresolved measure).
4. **Value.** Two finalists: EVSI_e = s̃_e·[φ(z) − z·Φ(−z)], z = |m|/s̃_e. More than two: the knowledge
   gradient, the expected reduction in the opportunity loss E[max_i μ_i] − max_i E[μ_i] of the chosen design
   over the finite Gaussian menu (closed form by the piecewise-linear maximum; at most 64 candidates in the
   budgeted mode). Value is exactly zero when no outcome of e can change the choice.
5. **Ranking and stop.** Sort by EVSI_e/c_e; emit proposals whose ratio is at least λ; then the marker `STOP`
   with the price and the best rejected ratio. If the first entry is STOP the answer is "decide now".
6. **Measurement proposals (partition by decision).** From the probe generator: the admitted mechanism set
   ℳ, partitioned by implied decision. One block → `identification-not-needed`. Otherwise the cheapest
   intervention whose reading sets are disjoint between blocks over the admitted nuisance intervals and read
   error; if reading sets overlap, the two witness mechanisms with opposing design margins. Each such proposal
   carries `model_class: ℳ` and the statement that its verdict is conditional on ℳ. A-25 is run on the
   proposed excitation; a proposal that fails it is dropped with the reason.
7. **Direct measurement.** For every measurement-type proposal the list also contains "measure the target at
   mission conditions" with its cost, marked as the stronger comparator, whatever its rank.
8. **Predictions.** For each proposal, per mechanism or per outcome, an 80% predicted interval; when the
   caller runs a proposal through its `next_actions` command, the prediction is registered (hashed and frozen)
   before the run starts and scored after. DC-05's monitor reads the coverage of the last 30: outside 65 to
   92% the core falls back to the fixed order check, simulate, train, referee.
9. **Finalist layer of the ladder.** When the population layer hands over (at most 64 survivors), the cascade
   calls this ranking in a loop: buy the top proposal, update the posterior from the outcome, repeat until
   STOP or budget. The handover is one way; this layer orders work, and every verdict still comes from the
   promotion rule and the comparison rules.
10. **Output.** `proposals[{kind, target, cost, component, evsi, ratio, prediction, model_class, command}]`,
    `direct_measurement`, `stop {price, best_rejected_ratio}`, `posterior` summary; `evidence.class:
    Estimated`; `claims: []`. No field of the output is a verdict type (INV-ATEL-12: a value-of-information
    quantity never appears in a verdict field); a consumer that cites one as a verdict is refused with
    **FROB-REF-872** (repair: run the comparison). Kind `read`; phase `review`.
11. **KILL-VOI fallback form.** Value of information is a stop rule only: proposals are listed unranked by
    value (in the fixed order), and STOP is still computed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Worked example: m = 0.03, sampling variance 0.03², tier-bias variance 0.04², per-pair noise 0.3 (fast)
      and 0.1 (referee): 200 fast-tier pairs are worth 0.0013 for 1 GPU-second; 8 referee pairs 0.0055 for 27;
      the fast batch ranks first (sixfold per unit cost); after it a second fast batch is worth about 10⁻⁵ and
      the referee batch ranks first; at m = 0.15 the list is STOP.
- [ ] Zero-value fixture: where no outcome can change the choice the value is exactly 0 and the proposal is
      absent (GATE-ATEL-15, second clause).
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-15: on the offline replay over the ladder log (the harness of N28b-18, re-run at M4 on a log
      of our own engine with our own scorers), the knowledge-gradient chooser needs at most 1/1.5 of the agent
      chooser's evaluations to identify a true top-3 design at 95% confidence. The deciding statistic is the
      one-sided 95% paired lower bound on the ratio (agent ÷ knowledge gradient) over 100 replays, which must
      be at least 1.5 (the reading the decision bead N28c-17 applies); the point ratio of restricted means and
      the medians with their interval are recorded beside it.
- [ ] `ValueModel` known answers on the branch selected by N28c-28's decision (the `fs-voi` airlock branch if
      its first-use admission by N33b-13 passed, else the local closed form): 0.00131 and 0.00550 within 1% on
      the worked menu, and exactly 0 on the no-change fixture; where both branches exist, their values agree
      within 10⁻¹² relative on the same inputs (N28c-28 defines the trait and no longer runs these at M1).
- [ ] Measurement proposals handed to the ranking (records of `frob.proposal/1`, labelled `synthetic`) are ranked
      by value per cost beside simulation proposals with variance component `Model`, keep their `model_class`
      and `comparator` fields unchanged, and a `DirectMeasurement` entry is never dropped by the price cut.
- [ ] Under a DC-05 decision in its fallback state (the coverage trigger or KILL-VOI, set on the core record),
      the list comes back in the fixed order check, simulate, train, referee with STOP still computed.
- [ ] No verdict-typed field appears in the output schema; citing a proposal's value as a verdict is refused
      (872).
- [ ] The rows these checks cannot reach at this bead's close are recorded by the beads that depend on it:
      GATE-AUGUR-11 through this verb (friction probe; pad fixture 675 of 675 in class, zero unconditional
      verdicts on the withheld law, 900 of 900 for the direct measurement) and the zero-value clause by N28c-16;
      the prediction ordering by N28c-14; the coverage trigger by N28c-15; KILL-VOI by N28c-17.
- [ ] With no exchange rate declared, real experiments appear unranked with that reason.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/suggest.rs`:
- `evsi_values`: 0.00131, 0.00550 (0.0054965), 1.0 × 10⁻⁵; `evsi_zero_when_no_change_possible`; monotonicity in s̃ and |m|;
  all run through the `ValueModel` trait on the selected branch.
- `value_branches_agree` (only where both the airlock and the local branch are built): equal within 10⁻¹²
  relative on the worked menu, the second fast batch and the no-change fixture.
- `posterior_update_fast_then_referee`: after the fast batch the variance is 0.0003 (sampling) + 0.0016 (tier
  bias), so tier bias dominates and the referee batch ranks first.
- `ranking_by_ratio_and_stop`; `stop_first_when_m_large`.
- `knowledge_gradient_three_designs`: against brute-force Monte Carlo of the opportunity-loss reduction within
  10⁻³; reduces to the two-design closed form.
- `measurement_records_ranked_with_model_component`: committed `synthetic` records of the friction-law probe
  (readings 0.5 and 0.7 at 1.0 m/s) and its `DirectMeasurement` comparator are ranked by value per cost; both
  keep `model_class` and `comparator`; the comparator survives a price above its ratio.
- `fallback_state_fixed_order`: with the DC-05 record in its fallback state, four proposals come back as check,
  simulate, train, referee regardless of value, STOP still computed.
- `voi_not_a_verdict_872`.
- `unranked_fallback_form`.
(The partition, skip, A-25, omitted-law and comparator rules of measurement proposals are tested in N28c-13 and
N25c-18; prediction ordering in N28c-14; the coverage trigger in N28c-15.)

### End-to-end script and logging

The verb's fixture-level script is `scripts/e2e/experiment_suggest.sh` (bead N28c-16: ranking values, STOP,
the zero-value clause, comparators, the advisory wall, the fallback forms). This bead's own script covers what
that one does not, the finalist loop on a real population and the gate rows.
`scripts/e2e/atelier_suggest.sh`: on three finalists of the seeded population with stored comparisons,
`frob experiment suggest d1 d2 d3 --budget gpu_s=600 --json`; runs the top proposal through its command (a run
verb, with a `--key` of its own per round); re-suggests; continues to STOP; then the replay row of GATE-ATEL-15
through `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test <tier named by GATE-ATEL-15's record in
registries/gates.toml> -- GATE-ATEL-15` on the M4 ladder log, whose hash-named receipt the script logs. The
script writes no gate row (convention 9): the gate register names one recorder for GATE-ATEL-15, N28c-16,
which records the replay clause from this receipt and the zero-value clause from its own with `cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ATEL-15 --receipt <hash>`. (GATE-AUGUR-11 through the
verb is N28c-16's step 5.) Sourcing the shared helper of STD-E2E, it logs per round: the posterior (m, variance components), each
proposal's cost, value and ratio against the price, and the selected `ValueModel` branch; the replay's
evaluations-to-top-3 per chooser and the paired lower bound against 1.5. It writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/atelier_suggest/<utc>.ndjson`; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step` (`suggest`,
`run`, `re-suggest`, `stop`, `gate`), `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's
`evidence` block (class `Estimated`, `claims: []`); on failure the first differing value and the ledger frontier
and paths to reproduce; a final `summary` line naming GATE-ATEL-15 with pass or fail.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the value arithmetic (on the selected `ValueModel` branch, and branch agreement where both
exist); the friction-probe fixture's records (0.5 against 0.9 at 1.5 m/s; 0.5 against 0.7 at 1.0 m/s) as ranked
inputs. The pad counts 675/675, 225 reversed, 900/900 and CX-317 (an in-class-perfect probe is safe to act on:
the withheld law must not receive an unconditional verdict) are asserted by N28c-13 and, through this verb, by
N28c-16. Negative controls here: expected information gain as the ranking (a registered mutant that buys an
experiment which cannot change the choice; caught by the zero-value fixture); a ranking that drops the direct
measurement below the price cut. Determinism: the ranking is a pure function of
ledger state at a pinned frontier and the request; the agent arm of the replay is recorded data.

## Considerations

- KILL-VOI: GATE-ATEL-15 fails. Fallback, named in advance: value of information is a stop rule only;
  `experiment.suggest` lists proposals unranked by value. The ceiling was stated first: a factor of 2 to 5 from
  stopping and elimination when candidates are separated, at the finalist stage only.
- KILL-AUGUR-PROBE (decided at M3 with the probe pilot): if it fired, the proposals of kind "probe" are the
  programmed expert grid and the direct measurement, and the generator's rows of GATE-AUGUR-11 are recorded
  against that form.
- A probe certificate is conditional on its mechanism set; a stopping rule derived from a probe is not used on
  an uncharacterized material.
- OPEN-ATEL-1 (exchange rate) and OPEN-ATEL-3 (sibling admission): fail-closed defaults as stated.
