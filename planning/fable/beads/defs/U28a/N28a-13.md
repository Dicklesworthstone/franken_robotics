## Background

A-25 answers: would a proposed experiment determine the card or scene parameters on which the pending decision
depends? Before an agent (or the studio's own experiment suggestion) spends a bench measurement or a batch of
referee runs to "identify friction", it should know whether those runs can say anything about friction at all.

The demonstrated failure, identifying friction from a convenient run: with drawbar force
F = min(k_t·I/r, μ·N), torque constant k_t = 0.2 N·m/A and wheel radius r = 0.06 m, runs at 1 A and 2 A under
normal loads of 30 N and 40 N give 3.33 N and 6.67 N for μ = 0.4 and for μ = 0.8 alike, because both runs are
torque-limited; at 3 A under 10 N they give 4 N against 8 N. A fit to the first two runs reports some μ and
labels it a measurement. The analysis must report zero sensitivity to μ for the first two runs and ask for an
excitation in the traction-limited regime.

The mathematics: the rank of the sensitivity of predicted readings to the parameters over the proposed
excitation, and coverage of each regime of a switching law. Tier: Solid for closed-form reading maps and regime
coverage; Frontier where readings come from simulation (piloted and gated by the calibration subsystem). It is
a separate verb, not part of `check`.

## Scope

In: the reading map and its Jacobian, scaling, the rank and conditioning tests, alias detection, regime
enumeration and coverage for switching laws, decision relevance, findings with suggested excitations. Out: any
fit (this analysis needs no data and never returns one); Fisher information with shared instrument error,
profile likelihood and greedy experiment selection (BD-390); probe synthesis (BD-291); ranking experiments by
value (BD-351).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** The model and its cards; the parameter set θ in question (card fields, scene fields), each with
   a prior interval or standard deviation; the proposed excitation: a list of runs, each with its commanded
   inputs and conditions and the readings it would record (instrument, channel, instants); optionally the
   pending decision as a partition of parameter space (which values imply which design choice).
2. **Reading map.** y(θ) = the vector of predicted readings over all runs. For closed-form laws (static force
   balances, card envelopes, the drawbar law above) it is evaluated symbolically; for dynamic readings it is
   evaluated on the referee tier in double precision (Frontier path).
3. **Regimes of switching laws.** A law built from `min`, `max`, saturations, stick-or-slip and limit
   predicates is declared with its regime predicates. For each run the analysis evaluates which regime is
   active over the prior range of θ: `always`, `never` or `mixed`. A parameter that appears only in regimes
   that are `never` active in every run has zero sensitivity by construction; this is reported without
   differentiating. A run that is `mixed` is flagged: its sensitivity depends on which side of the switch the
   true parameter lies.
4. **Jacobian.** J = ∂y/∂θ at the prior mean and at eight prior draws (TARGET), by automatic differentiation
   for closed forms and central differences otherwise (a difference that straddles a regime switch is not
   used; the two one-sided values are reported instead). Columns are scaled by the prior standard deviations
   and rows by the instrument's stated error, so J is dimensionless.
5. **Tests (thresholds are TARGETs recorded in the contract).**
   - *Rank*: a singular direction of the scaled J with σ_min/σ_max below 10⁻⁶ at every evaluation point is
     structurally unidentified in this excitation; the direction (a combination of parameters) is reported.
   - *Per-parameter*: a column of zero norm ⇒ `zero-sensitivity`.
   - *Aliasing*: two columns with cosine of magnitude at least 0.95 ⇒ reported as an aliased combination, with
     the combination that is identified.
   - *Precision (advisory here)*: the bound √((JᵀJ)⁻¹)_ii against half the prior standard deviation.
6. **Decision relevance.** When the decision partition is given: if every parameter value in the prior implies
   the same choice, the result is `identification-not-needed`; otherwise only the directions that separate
   blocks of the partition are required to be identified, and an unidentified direction inside one block is
   reported as harmless.
7. **Findings and suggestions.** `A-25.<param>.unidentified` (High) with the regime that carries the parameter
   and the cheapest excitation from the registered experiment grammar that enters it (for friction: sled drag
   at two normal loads, or an incline to first slip; for the example: a run at 3 A under 10 N).
   `A-25.<a>~<b>.aliased` with what separates them. Known structural aliases are seeded: armature and link
   inertia about a joint moving alone; Coulomb friction and a constant bias torque in one-directional motion;
   viscous friction and back-EMF damping under voltage drive; torque constant and the scale of inertia and
   friction with commanded current and encoder only; actuator delay and clock offset; friction coefficient and
   the torque limit (the example); static friction in a run that never dwells at zero velocity; contact
   stiffness and damping from one drop height.
8. **Class.** Closed-form path: exact rationals where the law is rational, else double; evidence `Tested`. The
   simulated path is labelled a screening signal until the calibration subsystem's gate passes. The verb is
   `analyze.identifiability`; exit 2 when anything is unidentified.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The drawbar fixture: runs (1 A, 30 N) and (2 A, 40 N) give F = 3.33 N and 6.67 N for μ = 0.4 and for
      μ = 0.8; the analysis reports `zero-sensitivity` for μ, regime `torque-limited: always`, and suggests an
      excitation in the traction-limited regime; adding the run (3 A, 10 N), which gives 4 N against 8 N, makes
      μ identified.
- [ ] A fit-like mutant that returns a value of μ from the first two runs is contradicted by the analysis
      (negative control).
- [ ] Each of the eight seeded structural aliases is detected on its smallest case and its separating
      experiment is named.
- [ ] With a decision partition in which both friction values imply the same wheel, the result is
      `identification-not-needed`.
- [ ] A `mixed` regime run is flagged and no central difference across the switch is used.
- [ ] Contract conformance (declared reads; derived memo key); the verb is schema-valid; the analysis needs no
      data and returns no fit.

`crates/frob-analyze/tests/a25_identifiability.rs`:
- `drawbar_values`: 0.2·1/0.06 = 3.333 N; 0.2·2/0.06 = 6.667 N; μ·N = 12, 16, 24, 32 N in those runs; at 3 A:
  10 N against μ·N = 4 N and 8 N.
- `mu_zero_sensitivity_first_two_runs`; `mu_identified_with_third_run`.
- `regime_always_never_mixed`: a run at 3 A under 20 N is `mixed` over μ ∈ [0.4, 0.8] (μ·N from 8 to 16 N
  against 10 N).
- `alias_armature_link_inertia`: a joint moving alone sees only I_link + N²·J_r; the singular direction is
  (1, −1/N²) in scaled units.
- `alias_coulomb_bias_one_direction`; `alias_viscous_backemf_voltage_drive`.
- `decision_partition_same_choice`.
- `no_difference_across_switch`.
- `rank_threshold`: σ_min/σ_max of 10⁻⁷ is unidentified; 10⁻³ is identified.

### End-to-end script and logging

Logged: per parameter the column norm, the regime table per run, the singular values, the suggested
excitation.

### Known answers and negative controls

the closed-form path is exact; the simulated path is deterministic on one platform.

## Considerations

- A probe that is perfect inside its assumed law class can be wrong outside it: in one executed study a probe
  built for three material laws classified 675 of 675 cases inside the class and 0 of 225 under an omitted
  delayed law, while measuring the target quantity directly was right in 900 of 900 (FACT, plan-time pilot).
  A-25 checks identifiability within the declared law class and says so; it cannot detect a missing law.
- RISK-42 (a model fits nominal motion for the wrong physical reason) is what the alias list and the energy
  ledger on real logs address together.
- The calibration subsystem's rule is that a fit on an unidentifiable combination is refused, never regularized
  quietly; this analysis is the check that refusal calls.
