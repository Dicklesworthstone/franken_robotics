## Background

After a simulation, `frob explain run` assembles a budgeted explanation: the failure taxonomy with first-cause
ordering, the energy ledger, the saturation report by joint, friction utilization and slip, the gap between
estimated and privileged state, and a ranked list of typed edits predicted to help, which becomes the
envelope's `next_actions`. That verb exists from the first studio milestone (bead BD-112). Four additions make
the explanation harder to misread; this bead delivers the one that needs comparisons, and completes the three
that waited for mechanisms of the second and third milestones.

- **Description and cause are different outputs.** The first-cause ordering is a description: it orders
  anomalies by time and is labelled `Estimated`. A causal statement ("torque saturation caused the fall") comes
  only from a controlled intervention, which is the fork machinery of the counterfactual debugger under its
  decision rule. `explain` offers that as a `next_action`; it does not imply it.
- **Explaining a comparison.** `frob explain compare <comparison>` clusters the paired-episode differences by
  contact signature and scene region and names the two or three regimes in which the candidate gained or
  regressed, within a token budget. A paired difference is attributed to a regime only if both arms' traces for
  that world address are present; the address-keyed telemetry sample guarantees that the same worlds were
  traced for both arms.
- **Gait by symmetry mode** (delivered with `explain.run` at M1): joint trajectories projected onto the
  isotypic components of the observation-preserving symmetry subgroup, with the energy per component; a
  readability aid that makes no claim about performance.
- **The first physical consequence of an edit.** When a run follows an edit, `explain` leads with the change
  the agent made, the first event at which the two lineages' traces part, and the evidence for the comparison.

Why regimes: a single paired effect of +0.12 hides that the candidate won every sharp-lip crossing and lost
every low-friction one. An agent that sees the two regimes knows what to edit next; one that sees the average
does not. Rejected: per-episode listings (unaffordable in tokens and unread); clustering on learned embeddings
(not reproducible, not explainable); attributing differences from one arm's trace only (the other arm may have
met a different regime in the same world).

## Scope

In: `explain.compare` with its regime key, clustering, budget and output; and for `explain.run`: next actions
ranked from the sensitivity analysis A-16, the first-divergence lead after an edit, the intervention offer.
Out: `explain.run` and `explain.design` themselves (BD-112); the comparison (BD-262) and its record (N28a-21);
first-divergence search (BD-206); sensitivity (BD-243); interventions (BD-279); the regime atlas with validity
labels (A-26, N28a-16), which this report feeds and does not require.

## Technical approach

**`frob explain compare <comparison> [--metric M] [--budget N] [--regimes k] --json`** (kind `read`, cluster
`explain`, first milestone M3, phase `review`).

1. Load the comparison record; refuse a sealed study (840). For each address in address order, take the pair
   only if both arms have a stored trace at that address (the reservoir is keyed by address hash, so a traced
   address is traced for both arms; a pair where one trace was collected by retention is `untraced`). Report
   `traced_pairs`, `untraced_pairs`, `planned`.
2. **Regime key per traced pair (DESIGN).** At the first divergent task event between the arms on that address
   (or, when both arms end alike, at the tick of largest metric divergence), record:
   - *contact signature*: the sorted set of active contact sites with, per site, stick or slip, and the
     per-joint saturation flags (torque, speed, thermal), each as observed in the arm that did worse;
   - *scene region*: the scene's region identifier at the robot's base position at that tick (regions come
     from the scene pack: a sill lip, a wet patch, a doorway, open floor), or a coarse grid cell where the scene
     declares none.
   The key is the pair (contact signature, scene region), a small canonical string.
3. **Clustering.** Group pairs by key. While there are more than k groups (default 3), merge the smallest group
   into the nearest by signature overlap (Jaccard on contact sites, ties by key bytes); groups below a minimum
   size (default 5 pairs) go to `other`. Deterministic: a pure function of the traced pairs.
4. **Per regime:** number of pairs; candidate wins, losses and ties; mean paired difference with a bootstrap
   interval (2,000 seeded resamples), labelled `Estimated` and descriptive; the dominant failure class of the
   losing arm; the binding requirement atom (the atom with the smallest robustness margin at the verdict in the
   losing arm); one representative address (the median absolute difference) with drill-down handles for both
   arms (`telemetry.query`, stills).
5. **Sentence per regime**, generated from a fixed template, for example "B gained in 21 pairs at `sill-lip`
   with front wheels slipping (+0.43, interval +0.21 to +0.62); A's binding atom was `cleared`."
6. **Budget.** Token budget as for `explain.run` (bytes of the serialized section divided by four, rounded up;
   default 600); regimes in order of absolute total contribution to the effect; omitted regimes listed by name
   with handles; nothing cut silently.
7. **Next actions.** `frob analyze sensitivity` on the losing design for the binding atom; the fork that would
   test the described mechanism (`autopsy.intervene`), offered and not implied; from M4, `analyze.regimes`.
8. **Envelope.** `evidence.class: Estimated`, `claims: []`; the comparison's own standing and `provisional`
   flag are echoed, so an explanation of a development result cannot read as a qualified claim.

**Completions of `explain.run`.**
9. *Sensitivity-ranked edits.* When A-16 results exist for the model and the failing metric, the ranked list of
   typed edits is computed from them: for each lever with a significant sensitivity, the edit that moves it in
   the improving direction by its declared step, with the predicted change of the metric, ordered by predicted
   gain per unit of the scarcest budget; edits whose sensitivity estimate is a screening signal are labelled
   so. Without A-16 results the M1 behaviour stays (repairs of matching design findings).
10. *First divergence lead.* When the run's design is a child of a design with a run on the same addresses,
    the explanation leads with the edit (from the lineage edge), then the first event at which the two
    lineages' traces part on the earliest differing address (tick, stream, event), then the paired evidence if
    a comparison exists.
11. *Intervention offer.* The first-cause section ends with the `autopsy.intervene` command that would test
    the described cause by a controlled fork with a placebo; the wording stays descriptive.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the planted two-regime comparison (candidate better on 20 traced `sill-lip` pairs by +0.5, worse on 10
      traced `wet-patch` pairs by −0.4, equal elsewhere), `explain.compare` names exactly those two regimes
      with the right signs and counts, within a 600-token budget.
- [ ] A pair with one arm's trace missing is counted `untraced` and attributed to no regime; with all traces
      missing the verb reports that nothing can be attributed and how to record traces.
- [ ] The output is byte-identical on repeat and independent of the order in which traces were stored.
- [ ] The text contains no causal verb (word list test) and carries `Estimated`; the intervention is among the
      next actions.
- [ ] `explain.run` on a failing run with A-16 results lists edits in the order of predicted gain; on a run
      that follows an edit it leads with the edit and the first divergent event.
- [ ] A sealed study is refused (840); an unpromoted comparison's explanation echoes `provisional`.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob --json` and MCP; schema `frob.explain.compare/1`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `regime_key_canonical`: the same contacts in another order give one key.
- `two_planted_regimes_recovered`.
- `merge_smallest_until_k`; `small_groups_to_other`.
- `untraced_pairs_excluded_and_counted`.
- `budget_respected_and_omissions_listed` at 200, 600 and 2,000 tokens.
- `no_causal_wording`.
- `sensitivity_ranking_orders_edits`: sensitivities (−0.8 per kg on shank mass, +0.3 per unit gear ratio) and
  budgets give the expected order.
- `first_divergence_lead_present_after_edit`; `absent_without_parent_run`.
- `deterministic_bytes`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/atelier_compare.sh` (bead N28a-30): after the cart comparison with telemetry at the sampled level,
runs `frob explain compare <id> --budget 600 --json` and `frob explain run <run-of-child> --json`; asserts the
regime list is non-empty, counts sum to the traced pairs, the token count is within budget, and the handles
resolve. Logged: regimes with counts, effects and intervals, traced and untraced pairs, tokens used, the first
divergent event.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: the planted two-regime fixture. Negative controls: a mutant that attributes from one arm's trace
only (it assigns untraced pairs to regimes; caught by the count identity); a mutant that reports the average
only (fails the regime assertion); a description that uses "caused" (word-list test). Determinism: byte
identical across runs and platforms for the same stored comparison.

## Considerations

- The regime report is a description. Whether its regions predict where a gradient or a ranking is trustworthy
  is the question the regime atlas (A-26) asks, with its own pilot and kill gate; if that gate fires, this
  report remains, with no validity label.
- Retention can delete traces a comparison needs; decision-relevant chunks are pinned by the record-keeping
  subsystem, and this verb reports a collected trace as `untraced`.
- Privacy: a comparison in a private scene inherits the private mark; region identifiers of a private scene are
  not written to end-to-end logs.
