## Background

A paired comparison of two designs can be declared as a fixed-n exact study: the sample size and the decision
rule are frozen before any episode runs, and the verdict is computed once, at n complete pairs. It is the study
type for registered A/B questions, equivalence claims and qualification of a finalist. This bead fixes the rule
as the studio uses it, the table of which verdicts the rule can reach at a given n, and the one condition under
which a fixed-n study may stop early; and it discharges the proof obligation attached to the rule.

**The rule (frozen before sampling).** n pairs; b = the number of pairs in which the candidate succeeds and the
baseline fails; a = the reverse; the effect estimate is (b − a)/n. Form exact Clopper–Pearson intervals
[L_b, U_b] and [L_a, U_a] for the two discordant probabilities, each two-sided at 97.5% for a study at level
α = 0.05 (in general each at 1 − α/2, that is α/4 per tail), and subtract opposite endpoints: the interval
[L_b − U_a, U_b − L_a] covers the difference of the two probabilities with probability at least 1 − α.

| Verdict | Condition (margin m, default 0.05) |
|---|---|
| Promote the candidate | L_b − U_a > +m, and hard feasibility holds (the candidate violates no hard limit in a condition where the baseline does not) |
| Inferior | U_b − L_a < −m |
| Practical tie | The interval lies inside [−m, +m] |
| Inconclusive | Otherwise |

**Why a reachability table.** At n = 64 with a five-point margin the rule cannot return one of its four
verdicts: even perfect concordance (a = b = 0) leaves the interval [−0.0662, +0.0662], because the upper
endpoint for zero events in 64 at 1.25% per tail is 1 − 0.0125^(1/64) = 0.0662. A study that plans to conclude
"tie" at n = 64 has planned something impossible, and it should learn that before spending its budget.

**Why curtailment must be exact.** A fixed-n study that stops when its running result first looks good loses
its level: an exact one-sided sign test at nominal 5% looked at after every pair has a false-rejection chance of
13/256 = 5.08% after 8 looks, 8.76% after 16, 12.9% after 32 and 17.4% after 64. The only early stop that
changes nothing is one where the remaining pairs cannot change the verdict. No e-process may stop a fixed-n
study.

## Scope

In: the comparison layer's obligations on the fixed-n rule, in `frob-opt`: the decision of each verdict
inequality certified at the margin; the corner form of the exact-curtailment test with its tie scan, with its
hard-feasibility and unscored conditions, checked against brute force over every completion; the label
`curtailed-exact` with the pairs saved; the closed form for the first n at which a tie is reachable, at the
development level and at qualification levels, as reported in the repair of refusal 555; the worked results;
the standalone zero-discordance bound as a known answer; the peeking counterexample kept as a test; and proof
obligation PO-ATEL-02 (simultaneous coverage of at least 95% on the grid of true discordance probabilities;
the reachability report equal to an independent enumeration).

One implementation of the rule (DESIGN, the same statement in BD-124: two implementations of one frozen rule
in two crates could disagree at a margin, and a study and its comparison would then report different verdicts
for the same counts). The rule `paired-clopper-pearson/1` (endpoints, the verdict function, the enumerator, the
power sums, the function `curtail`) is implemented once, in `frob-arena` (`study/{clopper_pearson,rule,
preanalysis}.rs`, bead N24b-06, a dependency of BD-124), and the fixed-n engine of BD-124 runs it. This bead
writes no second copy of those functions in `frob-opt`: steps 1 to 4 below state what the rule computes so
that the obligations can be checked, the tests of this bead call the `frob-arena` functions, and the only
function this bead adds is the corner test with its tie scan (step 5), which must equal `curtail` on every
state.

Out, and where it is: the study types, study registration, the fixed-n engine as a study (the pre-analysis
attached at creation, progress only before n, the verdict once at n, the stop and cancellation on a curtailed
verdict), `study.plan` and refusal 532 (BD-124, on which this bead depends); the rule's implementation, the
committed tables and the verb `eval.preanalyze` (N24b-06); the refusal 555 itself (N28a-18); running episodes
(BD-262); the qualification lane (BD-259).

## Technical approach

1. **Endpoints.** For count k of n and tail probability t = α/4: L = 0 if k = 0, else the p solving
   P(X ≥ k; n, p) = t; U = 1 if k = n, else the p solving P(X ≤ k; n, p) = t. Computed by bisection on the
   exact rational binomial tail, which is monotone in p, on the dyadic grid p = j/2⁴⁸ of the rule's
   implementation (a bracket [lo, hi] of width 2⁻⁴⁸; the lower endpoint is reported rounded down and the upper
   rounded up, so every reported interval contains the true one); the double value is reported, and the
   brackets are kept.
2. **Certified verdict.** Each inequality of the table is decided from brackets (for example "L_b − U_a > m" is
   certain when lo(L_b) − hi(U_a) > m and certainly false when hi(L_b) − lo(U_a) ≤ m); if a bracket straddles
   the margin, bisect further on the exact tail until it does not (DESIGN, the same statement in BD-124: the
   reachability report must be exact, so a comparison the grid leaves undecided is refined until it is decided
   and is never settled by the rounding). The classification of an outcome is therefore exact, not a
   floating-point accident. At the sizes of the known answers below no bracket straddles the margin, so the
   refined and the unrefined classification give the same counts; the test `no_straddle_at_known_answers`
   asserts it.
3. **Enumeration (`preanalyze`).** Classify every count pair (a, b) with a + b ≤ n: C(n + 2, 2) outcomes.
   Return the count per verdict, the reachable set, `least_b_at_a` (the smallest b that promotes for
   a = 0, 1, 2, …), the interval at perfect concordance, and, for each declared alternative (p_b, p_a), the
   power: the trinomial probability of the promote region (and of the tie region), summed exactly. The result
   is hashed; the hash is the `preanalysis` field of the plan.
4. **First n at which a verdict is reachable.** For a tie: the smallest n with 1 − t^(1/n) ≤ m, that is
   n = ⌈ln t / ln(1 − m)⌉ with t = α/4. Reported in the repair of refusal 555.
5. **Exact curtailment.** At an interim state with a, b counted and r pairs unresolved, every admissible
   completion is (a + i, b + j) with i, j ≥ 0 and i + j ≤ r. Because L_b − U_a rises with b and falls with a
   (and U_b − L_a likewise), the promote and inferior regions are monotone and their tests need corners; the
   tie region is not monotone (it needs both a lower and an upper condition, which move in opposite directions
   along each axis), so the test for "no completion is a tie" is a scan, not a corner:
   - every completion promotes iff (a + r, b) promotes, and no remaining pair can change hard feasibility
     (true only when r = 0 or the task declares no hard limit);
   - every completion is inferior iff (a, b + r) is inferior;
   - every completion is a tie iff the lower condition L_b − U_a ≥ −m holds at (a + r, b) and the upper
     condition U_b − L_a ≤ m holds at (a, b + r);
   - every completion is inconclusive iff (a, b + r) does not promote, (a + r, b) is not inferior, and no
     completion is a tie. The last clause is the tie scan: for each i = 0 … r the completions (a + i, b + j)
     that meet the lower condition are those with j ≥ j_lo(i) and those that meet the upper condition are those
     with j ≤ j_hi(i), both thresholds non-decreasing in i, so a tie is reachable iff some i has
     max(0, j_lo(i)) ≤ min(r − i, j_hi(i)); two monotone pointers make it O(r). Testing only (a, b) is wrong:
     at n = 256 (α = 0.05, m = 0.05; 39 tie outcomes, among them (5, 6), (6, 5) and (6, 6)) the state
     (a, b) = (0, 6) with r = 5 is not a tie, (0, 11) does not promote and (5, 6) is not inferior, yet the
     completion (5, 6) is a tie, so the study must not stop as inconclusive; 230 interim states at n = 256 would
     stop wrongly under the (a, b)-only test, and the first n at which that test errs is 156 (76 states); below
     156 the tie outcomes are too few for it to matter.
   If one of these holds, the study stops, cancels the unresolved episodes, and reports the verdict with the
   label `curtailed-exact`, the pairs saved and the interim counts; otherwise it continues. (The stop and the
   cancellation are carried out by the fixed-n engine of BD-124 on the rule's function `curtail`; the corner
   test `fixed_n::curtail_corners` of this bead is the form the comparison reports and logs, and it is required
   to return the same answer as `curtail` on every state.) Unscored pairs
   reduce the pairs that can still become discordant and are part of r until resolved.
6. **Standalone equivalence bound.** A claim of equivalence may register, before the data, the test that
   bounds the difference by the total discordance probability: zero discordant pairs in n give the exact
   one-sided bound 1 − δ^(1/n) at confidence 1 − δ. It is a different registered rule
   (`paired-total-discordance/1`, the name under which the rule's implementation registers it), never applied
   after the fact to a study planned under the paired rule.
7. **Class.** D0 for the classification and counts; power values in double precision with error below 10⁻¹².

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] n = 64, m = 0.05, α = 0.05: 2,145 count pairs; 521 promote, 521 inferior, 0 ties, 1,103 inconclusive;
      perfect concordance gives [−0.0662, +0.0662]; with a = 0 promotion needs b ≥ 15, and 17, 19, 21, 23 with
      one to four baseline-only pairs.
- [ ] n = 86: 3,828 pairs; 1,031, 1,031, 1 tie (only (0, 0)), 1,765. n = 128: 8,385; 2,517, 2,517, 4, 3,347.
      n = 256: 33,153; 11,384, 11,384, 39, 10,346.
- [ ] Power at n = 64: 0.117 at discordance (0.17, 0), 0.406 at (0.25, 0.02), 0.962 at (0.40, 0.02); P(tie) = 0.
- [ ] First reachable tie: n = 86 at α = 0.05; n = 99 at α/2 (request 1 of a qualification campaign); 178 at
      α/110 (request 10); 328 at α/250,500 (request 500). At n = 64 and level α/2 there are 478 promoting
      outcomes.
- [ ] Curtailment: (a, b) = (10, 10) after 60 of 64 pairs stops as inconclusive; (0, 50) after 50 stops as
      promote when the task has no hard limit and does not stop when it has; (50, 0) after 50 stops as
      inferior; (0, 22) after 40 does not stop. At n = 256, (0, 6) with 5 unresolved does not stop (the
      completion (5, 6) is a tie). The corner test with its tie scan equals brute-force enumeration over every
      completion for every interim state at n ≤ 32, and for every interim state at n = 256 against the
      enumeration's verdict table (no tie is reachable at n ≤ 32, and an (a, b)-only tie clause first errs at
      n = 156, so the non-monotone tie region is exercised only by the second comparison).
- [ ] Zero discordances in 64 pairs: bound 0.04573.
- [ ] PO-ATEL-02: on the grid of true discordance probabilities (p_a, p_b) in multiples of 1/20 with
      p_a + p_b ≤ 1, the exact coverage of [L_b − U_a, U_b − L_a] at n = 64 is at least 0.95 at every point
      (the minimum on the half-grid up to 0.5 is about 0.983); the reachability report equals an independent
      enumeration.
- [ ] The table for the first fixture's rule is regenerated in CI and compared byte for byte.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-693 (≡ KA-612): the enumeration at 64 and 86; KA-694: 0.04573. Negative controls: a study that declares
"tie" reachable at n = 64 (refused by the pairing admission with 555, tested there against this table); a
mutant curtailment that stops on the running verdict (it stops (0, 22) at 40 pairs, which the brute-force
comparison catches); a mutant whose inconclusive test checks only (a, b) for a tie (it stops (0, 6) with 5
unresolved at n = 256, which the tie-scan test catches); a mutant that uses 95% two-sided intervals for each probability (the counts change, the
enumeration test fails). Determinism: exact; identical on every platform.

## Considerations

- The 64 conditions of the first fixture are a development batch: they can prefer a design or call it inferior,
  they cannot declare a tie, and their power for a modest effect is low (0.117 at the reference effect). A
  holdout for a claim is sized from the power table and run through the qualification lane at its own level.
- Units: where several episodes share a cluster (one building), the cluster is the unit and n counts clusters.
- A fixed-n interval computed at the moment an anytime study stops has no coverage guarantee; the type system
  keeps the two apart, and the refusal for trying is the pairing bead's.
- Retrying is allowed and never free: the needed sample grows with ln(1/α_i).
