## Background

A capability counts as implemented only when it is reachable through `frob --json` with a documented schema and
exit code, its known answers pass against a pinned oracle, and its gate has a recorded pass with measured
values. Unit tests beside the code cannot show the first of these. This bead is the end-to-end script for the
studio's first user-visible capability: static checking of a design from the command line, as an agent would
drive it. One script per capability, not one per mechanism: it exercises `design.check` stages 1 to 6, every
`analyze` verb that exists at the first studio milestone, the rule that a refused design cannot be simulated
without a recorded override, and the phase records, and it performs the gate measurements that are made on a
running system.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures directory, its assertions and log; a self-test of the script. Out: the mechanisms;
the conformance runner itself (the gauntlet beads); the M3 and M4 parts of the same gates.

## Technical approach

**Path.** `scripts/e2e/atelier_check.sh`; plain shell; sources the shared helper of STD-E2E; exits non-zero on
any failed assertion; idempotent (works in a scratch namespace it creates and removes); seed-explicit
(`FROB_E2E_SEED`, default 17, passed to every generator).

**Sections (DESIGN).** `--section m1|m3|m4|all` (default: every section whose milestone the build has reached).
This bead writes the frame and the M1 section below and owns the script (convention 11); later beads add their
steps to their section without changing the M1 steps: stages 7 and 8 and A-14 at M3 (their beads); at M4 the
GATE-ATEL-05 completion (BD-348), whose section runs as stages the own scripts of A-24 (N28ap1-02,
`analyze_wrench.sh`), A-25 (N28a-13, `analyze_identifiability.sh`) and A-26 (N28a-16, `analyze_regimes.sh`). A
section whose milestone the build has not reached logs its steps as `not-yet-due` with the milestone.

**Fixtures** (`scripts/e2e/fixtures/atelier/`, all `synthetic` except the four templates, which are used by
name): the top-heavy quadruped; the synthetic point-foot biped (the `biped-point` template is M3); the stance
fixtures at b = 3/14, 1/5 and 1/4 m; the two-design cart of KA-602; N14a-13's box-slab body and massless chain;
Sarrus, Bennett, four-bar, the synthetic Ackermann steering linkage (the `ackermann` template is M3) and the
perturbed Bennett; `diff-drive`, `mecanum` and the parallel-roller mecanum; the two-mass model; the sill cart
with its budget; the three packet-timing variants; the wheel loop with two feedforward laws; the two-finger bus
with one-tick and two-tick deadlines; the eight first-rejection designs; `quad-lidar-assert-c2xc2` (`quad-mammal`
with one forward lidar and the design field `assert_obs: "C2xC2"`). The analysis beads' own fixtures (BD-100,
BD-101, BD-106) are reused by path, not copied.

**Steps.**
1. `frob doctor --json` and `frob capabilities --json`: record the build; list the 26 analysis verbs of the
   cluster `analyze` with kind, milestone and implementation status (there is no `frob analyze --list`, N28a-05).
   Assert 26 entries.
1a. Verb sweep (the end-to-end leg of N28a-05): for every verb step 1 lists as implemented, `frob analyze
   <name> quad-mammal --json` and the same on `mecanum`; assert a schema-valid envelope against the verb's
   `frob.analyze.<name>.out/1`, exit 0 or 2 (3 only for a missing binding, which the envelope names as the
   first repair; never 3 on a finding), the evidence block, and the memo status of a second identical call
   (`hit`, identical result bytes); for every verb listed as not implemented, exit 5 with `FROB-REF-724` and
   its milestone.
2. Templates: `frob design check <t> --json` for the four templates. Assert exit 0 or 2, six stage entries,
   every stage within its budget, total under 2 s, envelope schema-valid, evidence block present.
3. Known answers through verbs: run as stages the analysis beads' own scripts (convention 11:
   `analyze_mass_stability.sh` of BD-100, `analyze_mobility.sh` of BD-101, `analyze_maneuverability.sh` of
   BD-106, `analyze_modes.sh` of BD-107, `check_a20_a23.sh` of BD-108, `analyze_energy_budget.sh` of N28a-07,
   `analyze_deadlines.sh` of N28a-08, `analyze_loop_stability.sh` of N28a-09, `analyze_bus_schedule.sh` of
   N28a-10), each failing this script when it fails, then `frob analyze mobility|mass|stability|
   maneuverability|modes|energy-budget|deadlines|loop-stability|bus-schedule … --json` on the templates and on
   the fixtures those scripts leave to this one (the steps they log `e2e_skip 'needs BD-110'`). Assert the
   values: mobility 1, 1, 1, 1 for
   the Ackermann steering linkage and 0 for the perturbed Bennett; 8.000 and 8.225 kg; forces (160, 130, 110, 0) N
   at 3/14 m, infeasible at 1/5 m with the dual witness f₊₊ − f₋₋ = 165 N, (155, 135, 105, 5) N active (++
   range [150, 160] N) and (175, 115, 85, 25) N passive at 1/4 m; growth vectors (2, 3), (3) and (2, 3)-for-(3); modes at 10
   and 14.142 rad/s; 0.94176 J with and without 0.05 J; the three packet verdicts; 1.4820 and 0.9207; schedule
   counts 0 and 2.
4. Refusals: the eight first-rejection designs, the box-slab body (106, stage 2) and the massless chain (104,
   stage 2, the joint named), the top-heavy quadruped (124), the parallel-roller mecanum (118), the infeasible
   bus (133), the perturbed Bennett (110). Assert exit 3, the expected code and stage,
   a non-empty repair list. Two legs that BD-110 assembles: `quad-mammal` with its hip adduction range widened
   until the limbs sweep through each other (A-13) refused at stage 3 with FROB-REF-123, exit 3, first repair a
   `set-limits` edit; and `frob design check <forward-lidar quad-mammal> --stages 5 --json` on the design whose
   `symmetry` block asserts `assert_obs: "C2xC2"` (the assertion is a field of the design, not an option of the
   verb; fixture `quad-lidar-assert-c2xc2`, KA-276's design) (A-03) refused with FROB-REF-109,
   exit 3, with the symmetry report of BD-102 (mechanical group C₂ × C₂, observation-preserving C₂, breaker the
   lidar mount) in `next_actions`.
4a. Runner options (N28ap1-01): `frob design check <t> --all --json` (every stage runs past a refusal, exit
   still 3); `--stages 4-5` on a design whose stage-2 record exists (stages 1 to 3 taken from their records,
   which the log shows as read) and on one that stage 2 refuses (exit 3 from the record, nothing recomputed); a
   1 ms `--time-budget` for stage 8 (no effect on stages 1 to 6); `--estimate` (the planned stages with their
   budgets, exit 0, no stage record written). Assert the stage list and order equal N14bx-01's `plan_check` for
   each request.
5. Repair loop: for three of them (124, 118, 110) apply the first `next_action` exactly as printed (a
   `frob design edit … --expected-parent <checked DesignId>` command), appending only `--key ac-rep-<code>-<seed>`
   because the printed command carries none and `design.edit` is a `mutate` verb (805 without a key), then
   re-check; assert the refusal is gone and the new version's `DesignId` differs.
6. Loop order: `frob sim run <top-heavy> --tier referee --seconds 2 --key ac-loop-1-<seed> --json` on the
   refused top-heavy design (assert exit 3 with code 124); again with `--override check --override-reason e2e
   --key ac-loop-2-<seed>` (a distinct key, or the first call's stored answer would return; assert it runs and
   the envelope carries `stage-override:check` and an empty `claims`); `frob explain run <run> --json` on it
   (assert the condition is echoed).
7. Not refused: the point-foot biped (assert exit 2 with `A-08.no-static-stability`, never 3).
8. Phases (N28a-03): `frob session show --phases --json` in the script's session; write its raw-record lines
   to `<log dir>/intervals.ndjson` and run `frob-tenfold phase attribute <log dir>/intervals.ndjson --t0
   <opened_ms> --stop <now_ms>` (N39a-23); assert phases `author`, `check`, `synthesize`, `screen`, `diagnose`
   present and the exclusive sum within 2% of the session clock.
9. Gates, run in the form ADR-GAUNT-01 fixes (BD-025: there is no `frob conformance` command, verb or binary,
   and BD-025's lint `no_conformance_command` fails this script if the literal form appears in it):
   `FROB_TEST_SEED=$FROB_E2E_SEED cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test <tier> --
   <gate>`, with `<tier>` the test target named by the gate record's `tier` field (written by N28c-25 for the
   Atelier gates), for GATE-ATEL-02 (its M1 part: 200 cold runs per M1 archetype, total under 2 s and every
   stage within its budget at the 95th percentile, stage 5 within 150 ms), GATE-ATEL-05 (its M1 parts A-20 to
   A-23, including `design check` under 2 s with the four analyses on and stage 6 within 755 ms unbound and
   875 ms bound; the M4 parts report `not-yet-due`), GATE-SKEL-04 (stages 1 to 6 on the first-rejection corpus
   of N14b-18), and the chapter's negative-control registry, whose nine shortcut rows (CX-302 to CX-310) run
   first as a stage, N28a-11's own `atelier_shortcuts.sh` (convention 11), failing this script when it fails.
   Assert each run passes with every control caught
   and writes its receipt; copy the measured values and the receipt hashes into the log. The script writes no
   gate row (convention 9). This bead records one part, the one the gate register names it for and no lower
   bead can measure: GATE-ATEL-05's M1 timing on the archetype templates (`design check` under 2 s at the 95th
   percentile with A-20 to A-23 on; stage 6 within 755 ms unbound and 875 ms bound), with `cargo run -p
   frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ATEL-05 --receipt <hash>`. GATE-ATEL-02's and
   GATE-SKEL-04's M1 rows are recorded by BD-110 and GATE-ATEL-05's four analysis parts by BD-108; this run
   re-measures them on the integrated build and fails if a value differs from the recorded row beyond the
   row's stated tolerance.
10. Determinism: repeat step 2 with `FROB_WORKERS=1` and `=8`; assert identical result bytes (the `cost` block
    excluded).
11. Cancellation: start `frob design check` on the largest template with `--time-budget` large, cancel it,
    assert no partial stage record exists for that request.

**Log.** NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/atelier_check/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript beside it. Every
line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the
exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism,
identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds (per-stage milliseconds against budgets; each
known answer against its truth and tolerance), digests where bytes are compared, and on failure the first
differing value and the fixture path that reproduces it. A final `summary` line gives assertions passed and
failed and the gates touched. No private-scene data can appear: every fixture is synthetic.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script passes on a clean checkout at the first studio milestone and fails (non-zero) when any one of
      the planted regressions of its self-test is active.
- [ ] Every step logs at least one `assert` event; the summary counts match the events.
- [ ] The three gates' measured values appear in the log with thresholds; GATE-ATEL-05's M1 template-timing
      part is recorded by this bead with `record_gate` from its receipt, and the other parts agree with the rows
      BD-110 and BD-108 recorded.
- [ ] Each analysis bead's own script listed in step 3 runs as a stage and passes.
- [ ] Two consecutive runs with the same seed produce logs that differ only in timestamps, wall times and
      costs.
- [ ] The script is listed in the `fixtures` battery so it runs on every change that touches the analyses, the
      design language or the verb registry.
- [ ] `--section m1` runs the steps above; at M1 `--section m3` and `--section m4` log each of their steps as
      `not-yet-due` with its milestone and exit 0, so the later beads' steps are visible before they land.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`scripts/e2e/tests/atelier_check_selftest.sh` runs the script against a build with one planted regression at a
time and expects failure at the named step: (a) the stance analysis replaced by the polygon-and-capacity
screen (step 9); (b) `sim.run` not consulting the stage record (step 6); (c) a stage's budget set to 1 ms (step
2, budget miss); (d) the bus analysis replaced by sum-of-peaks (step 3); (e) a log line missing its `evidence`
block (the helper's schema check); (f) a runner that ignores `plan_check` and runs stage 6 in registration order
(step 4a); (g) `frob capabilities` listing 25 analysis verbs (step 1); (h) an analysis verb that returns exit 3
on a finding (step 1a); (i) the A-02 massless chain reported as a High finding instead of refusing with 104
(step 4).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; see above. Runtime target under 3 minutes on the reference host (TARGET), dominated by
the 200-run timing measurement of GATE-ATEL-02.

### Known answers and negative controls

All M1 known answers of the studio (KA-680 to KA-687, the mobility and growth-vector rows, KA-291) and their
controls (CX-302 to CX-310 as bound at M1). The self-test's planted regressions are the controls on the script.
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release builds give the same D0
values.

## Considerations

- The script measures timing on whatever host runs it; the gate rows are only written from the reference host
  (the log records the hardware fingerprint, and the register refuses a row from another fingerprint).
- When stages 7 and 8, the remaining analyses and the remaining archetypes land, they extend this script; the
  step list is kept in the script's header so the M3 and M4 parts are visible as `not-yet-due`.
