## Background

Comparing two designs is the studio capability agents use most and misuse most easily. Its unit tests check the
statistics; this script checks the capability as a user meets it: plan a study, see its cost and its reachable
verdicts, be refused for each kind of dishonest pairing, run the comparison on a batched tier with early
stopping, read the stored record, and get an explanation by regime. It also performs the measurements of the
two comparison gates that are made on a running system. One script for the capability; the mechanisms have
their own beads.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures, assertions, log and self-test. Out: the mechanisms; promotion to the referee and
qualification (their own end-to-end scripts); bisect (the lineage script, N28b-06).

## Technical approach

**Path.** `scripts/e2e/atelier_compare.sh`; plain shell over `frob` (and `frobd` when the daemon tier is
selected); sources the shared helper of STD-E2E; idempotent in a scratch namespace; `FROB_E2E_SEED` default 17.
Backend from `FROB_E2E_BACKEND` (default `batch-cpu`; device rows run when a device is present and are logged
as `not run` otherwise, never as passed); every `frob eval compare` call below carries `--backend
"$FROB_E2E_BACKEND"` (eval.compare's placement input), so the backend a run used is the one selected.

**Fixtures (synthetic).** Cart designs A (60 mm front wheels) and B (75 mm); the cart task; a pair of scripted
outcome streams for the statistic-only steps (a null stream, a stream with success 0.80 against 0.50, a stream
that reaches (a, b) = (10, 10) at 60 pairs, a two-regime stream); six invalid plans (the unreachable tie of
step 2 and the five of step 3, two of them N28a-18's planted inputs, used by path).

**Steps.**
0. Stages (convention 11: the mechanism beads' own scripts, each failing this script when it fails):
   N28a-18's `pairing_discipline.sh` (the admission and the refusal clause of GATE-ATEL-06), N28a-19's
   `fixed_n_rule.sh` (the rule's tables and curtailment), BD-262's `eval_compare_devices.sh` (the comparison on
   devices and the comparison row of GATE-ATEL-04). The steps below are the capability story on top of them.
1. `frob eval preanalyze --rule paired-clopper-pearson/1 --n 64 --margin 0.05 --level 0.05 --json` and `--n 86`
   (the rule is a required input of the fixed-n mode): assert 2,145 = 521 + 521 + 0 + 1,103 and 3,828 = 1,031 +
   1,031 + 1 + 1,765; log the pre-analysis hashes.
2. A fixed-n study of A against B at n = 64 that names "tie" among its verdicts (`--fixed-n 64 --rule
   paired-clopper-pearson/1 --margin 0.05 --declared-verdicts promote,inferior,inconclusive,practical-tie`; the
   rule is a field of the fixed-n block, VERBS-eval, and without it the request is 802, not 555), submitted twice, once per layer (the layer
   rule of N24-REF and N28-REF: one situation, one code per verb): through `frob eval compare cart-a cart-b --task
   cart-sill-dock … --key ac-inv-555 --json`, assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-555` and that the repair reports n = 86;
   through `frob study plan`, assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-532`, and the same repair. Without "tie", through `frob
   study plan`: assert a plan id, a cost estimate with interval, the reachable set, and no episode created.
3. Five more invalid requests, each through `frob eval compare` (key `ac-inv-<code>`, with `-epoch` and
   `-draw` suffixes for the two 556 cases) and through `frob study plan`: both study types (553 through
   `eval.compare`, 552 through `study.plan`); a metric with no span under `anytime` (543 on both); seeds keyed by
   (world, epoch), N28a-18's planted input `pair-world-epoch` (CX-311; 556 on both); a material field indexed by
   draw order, its planted input `pair-by-draw-index` (CX-312; 556 on both); one arm on the labelled D4 training
   profile (542 through `eval.compare`, 533 through `study.plan`). Assert exit 3 and the code of each layer, and
   that no verb answers the other layer's twin. The five `eval.compare` refusals (553, 555, 542, 543, 556) are
   the planted violations of GATE-ATEL-06.
4. `frob eval compare cart-a cart-b --task cart-sill-dock --max-pairs 256 --key ac-cmp-1 --json` (anytime;
   then `frob job wait` on exit 8). Assert: exit 0 or 2; a disposition among the five; e-value and confidence
   sequence present; counts satisfy the accounting identity; `provisional: true`; `standing:
   development-result`; if it stopped early, cancel receipts for the unconsumed addresses.
5. Schedule fuzz: rerun step 4 under key `ac-cmp-2` with `FROB_SCHEDULE_FUZZ=1` (random preemption and chunk
   order) and under keys `ac-cmp-3`, `ac-cmp-4` with 1 and 4 shards (distinct keys, or a rerun would return the
   first run's stored receipt). Assert identical e-value, stopping index, verdict and outcome-table digest.
6. Memo: rerun under key `ac-cmp-5` with the memo graph warm. Assert identical e-value and a non-zero count of
   served episodes.
7. Fixed-n with curtailment: `frob study submit <plan of step 2> --key ac-sub-1 --json` on the scripted
   (10, 10)-at-60 stream. Assert the stop label `curtailed-exact`, verdict `inconclusive`, four pairs saved.
8. `frob study compare <id> --json` for both studies: validate against `frob.comparison/1`; assert the absolute
   block for both arms and the first-divergence field.
9. Two metrics: `frob eval compare cart-a cart-b --task cart-sill-dock --metric success --metric
   energy-per-completion --claim named --key ac-multi --json`; assert per-metric e-values and an e-BH set equal
   to the script's recomputation from the reported e-values.
10. `frob explain compare <id> --budget 600 --json` on the two-regime stream's study: assert two regimes with
    the planted signs, traced plus untraced pairs equal to planned, tokens within budget.
11. Sealed read: `frob study compare` on a sealed fixture study without the admin scope: assert exit 5, 840
    (N24-REF: the one Arena code with exit 5); and on an identifier no study has: 802 with pointer `study`, exit
    3 (N32a-13).
12. Gates, in the cargo form of ADR-GAUNT-01 (BD-025; there is no `frob conformance` command, and the lint
    `no_conformance_command` fails a script that names one): `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p
    frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-ATEL-06` (the tier its record names for this Known-answer
    gate; enumeration at 64 and 86 exact; each of the five refusals 100 of 100), then the same with `--features
    gauntlet-mutations` (the mutant admission control must be caught); and `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p
    frob-conformance --test <tier named by GATE-ATEL-04's record in registries/gates.toml> -- GATE-ATEL-04`
    for the comparison row (10⁴ null comparisons; rate at or below α with its exact binomial upper bound). Each
    run writes a hash-named receipt; assert pass with controls caught, and copy the measured values and receipt
    hashes into the log. This script writes no gate row and this bead records none (convention 9): the gate
    register's recorders (N28c-25) are N28a-18 for GATE-ATEL-06's one part (M1: the enumeration clause BD-124
    measures and the refusal clause, from one receipt) and BD-262 for GATE-ATEL-04's M3 part (the comparison
    row), each with `record_gate` from its own receipt; this run re-measures on the integrated build and fails if
    a value differs from the recorded row beyond its stated tolerance.

**Log.** NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/atelier_compare/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` plus a transcript. Every line:
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and thresholds
(null rejection rate against α with its binomial bound; pairs used; device seconds saved by cancellation;
counts), digests where state is compared, and on failure the first differing value (for step 5: the first
address at which the e-value traces part) and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line
gives assertions passed and failed and the gates touched. No private-scene data: all fixtures are synthetic.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script passes at the third milestone on `batch-cpu`, and on each present device family, and exits
      non-zero under each planted regression of its self-test.
- [ ] Steps 5 and 6 demonstrate INV-ATEL-02 on the running system: identical statistic under reordering,
      preemption, sharding and memo hits.
- [ ] The gate measurements in the log equal the rows their recorders wrote to the gate register (within each
      row's tolerance); the script writes none.
- [ ] Each stage of step 0 passes, and a failing stage fails this script.
- [ ] Two runs with one seed give logs that differ only in timestamps, wall times and costs.
- [ ] The script joins the `fixtures` battery for changes to `frob-opt`, the study layer or the job protocol.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`scripts/e2e/tests/atelier_compare_selftest.sh`, one planted regression per run: (a) the consumer takes
episodes in completion order (step 5 fails); (b) numerical faults dropped from `planned` (step 4, identity);
(c) the pre-analysis computed with 95% intervals per probability (step 1); (d) the D4 check removed (step 3);
(e) `explain.compare` attributing from one arm's trace (step 10, count identity).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Runtime target under 10 minutes on `batch-cpu` (TARGET), dominated by the 10⁴ null
comparisons, which run on scripted outcome streams and need no physics.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-693, KA-694, KA-088; the worked study result (19, 2, 40, 3 of 64; interval [0.0554, 0.4392]). Negative
controls: the six invalid plans; the self-test regressions; CX-311 and CX-312 through step 3. Determinism
lanes: twice on one machine; across shard counts; across backends inside their classes (the device rows compare
decisions, not bits, against `batch-cpu` unless bit equality has been established for the family).

## Considerations

- The cart comparison's real outcome is not asserted (which wheel wins is what the fixture exists to find); the
  script asserts the form and the invariants of the result, and the scripted streams carry the exact
  expectations.
- The device rows need the batch-composition invariance gate to be current for the family; otherwise the
  pairing admission labels or refuses the study and the script logs that outcome as the expected one.
