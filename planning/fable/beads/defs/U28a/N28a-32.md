## Background

The co-design cascade is the studio's largest user-visible capability: one verb that takes a brief and a
population of designs through deduplication, screening, racing, robustness checks and qualification, and
returns finalists with evidence. Its acceptance gate is, by definition, an end-to-end run on a seeded
population in which every stage's control runs and the report prints every mode, every fallback and the phase
ledger. This bead is that script. It also carries the running-system part of the calibrated ladder's gate
(layer 2 against the fixed table on the same population and addresses), because the two share the population.

## Scope

In: the seeded population as a fixture, the script, its assertions and log, its self-test. Out: the cascade and
its stages (BD-352 and the beads it calls); the conformance crate's ownership of the population after M4
(BD-377).

## Technical approach

**The seeded population (`synthetic`; built by `scripts/e2e/fixtures/atelier/make_population.sh` from seed
17).** 27 designs in the quadruped class by default (81 with `FROB_E2E_POPULATION=81`), drawn by keyed typed
edits from the template, plus planted members:
- two designs that are mirror images under the observation-preserving group (must merge at stage 1), and two
  that the mechanical group identifies and a forward sensor distinguishes (must split);
- a design carrying the two-finger shared bus with a two-tick deadline (feasible; a planted additive-peak
  prune would discard it);
- a planted winner with a known top-k result from a stored specialist-and-referee evaluation, in a form the
  shared-policy rung scores poorly; its identity sealed in the manifest;
- a design that profits from diagonal traction on a pyramidal friction cone and one that profits from energy
  created by an over-soft contact (for stage 5);
- a pair of identical designs under two names for the no-signal qualification control;
- a set of nine identical candidates for the null-race control.

**Path.** `scripts/e2e/atelier_cascade.sh`; plain shell over `frob` and `frobd`; sources the shared helper of
STD-E2E; idempotent in a scratch namespace; seed-explicit.

**Steps.**
1. Build the population; `frob opt cascade --spec <task> --population <set> --budget … --estimate --json`:
   assert a per-stage cost model and no physics run.
2. `frob opt cascade … --allocation fixed --json` (exit 8, then poll with `frob job wait`). Assert, from the
   result and the job's progress events: denominators at every stage; stage 1 merged the mergeable pair and
   split the other; the bracket ledger was written before the first score; modes printed per stratum; the
   planted winner among the finalists; the two exploit designs typed `model-conditional`; stage 5 spend at
   most 10% of the total; the no-signal pair not promoted; all six controls `passed`.
3. Same population and addresses with `--allocation conformal`: assert the audit list precedes scores, no
   audit episode was cancelled, survivors against 3k, and collect coverage, recall and GPU-seconds for the
   comparison with step 2 (the running-system rows of the calibrated-ladder gate).
4. Sabotage runs (each a separate campaign id): (a) the screen biased against the planted winner: assert
   `screen-biased`, the rung `uninformative` in that stratum, and the campaign re-opened; (b) stage 5 skipped
   by flag: assert the gate check reports the exploit design as an unflagged winner and fails; (c) the planted
   additive-peak prune enabled: assert the prune-soundness audit finds the feasible bus design, suspends the
   rule and files the case; (d) no planted winner supplied: assert the control is `not-run` and the report
   says "survivors".
5. Cancel and resume: cancel the step-2 campaign (new key) during stage 4, resume it, and assert finalists and
   digests equal step 2's.
6. Honest ending: a population in which every design violates a hard limit: assert exit 2 and "every admitted
   alternative fails".
7. `frob report campaign <id>` twice: assert identical bytes; assert it contains every rung's mode, the
   fallbacks that fired, the screening audit with its bound (for m audited discards, 1 − 0.05^(1/m)), the
   planted-winner outcome and the phase ledger whose exclusive phases sum to the campaign clock within 2%.
8. Archive merge: two agents' archives from two half-campaigns merged in both orders through
   `frob opt archive` exports: assert identical bytes.
9. Gates: `frob conformance run --gate GATE-ATEL-16 --json`; `--gate GATE-ATEL-08 --json`. Assert pass with
   controls caught; copy measured values into the log.

**Log.** NDJSON at `target/e2e-logs/atelier_cascade/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line has
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and
thresholds (per stage: designs in and out, spend against budget, control outcome; coverage against
1 − α − 0.03; recall of both rules; the cost ratio with its paired bound against 1; stage-5 share against
10%), digests where state is compared (resumed against uninterrupted; report regenerations; both merge
orders), and on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. A final
`summary` line gives assertions passed and failed and the gates touched. The planted winner's identity is
logged only after the campaign closes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script passes at the fourth milestone on `batch-cpu` with the 27-design population and, where the
      compute class allows, with 81 designs on a device; it exits non-zero under each planted regression of
      its self-test.
- [ ] All six stage controls are observed `passed` in step 2 and each sabotage of step 4 is observed caught.
- [ ] The measured values of both gates in the log equal the rows written to the gate register.
- [ ] Two runs with one seed give logs that differ only in timestamps, wall times and costs.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`scripts/e2e/tests/atelier_cascade_selftest.sh`, one planted regression per run: (a) audit episodes
cancellable by the race (step 3); (b) stage 1 deduplicating under the mechanical group (step 2, the pair that
must split); (c) the report printing "top k" under `discard-only` (step 7); (d) the resume path re-drawing
addresses (step 5); (e) a control silently skipped and reported `passed` (step 4d expects `not-run`).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Runtime target: under 45 minutes on `batch-cpu` for 27 designs with reduced episode
counts (TARGET); the 81-design device run is sized by the campaign's compute class.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-697 (stage-1 counts on the counting fixture, run as a side check), KA-692, KA-706; the halving counts
27 → 9 → 3. Negative controls: the four sabotage campaigns and the self-test regressions. Determinism lanes:
twice on one machine; resumed against uninterrupted; across shard counts; under schedule fuzz; across backends
inside their classes.

## Considerations

- The planted winner needs an earlier specialist-and-referee result; for classes without one the control is
  `not-run` by the open question's default, and the script's step 4d is the test of that path.
- The population is small enough that conformal calibration can fall below 19 audit designs in a stratum; the
  script therefore uses one stratum for the layer-2 comparison, and logs the calibration count.
- Stage 6 uses a scratch qualification campaign with its own sealed clusters; nothing from it is readable by
  the script before close, and the script asserts that an early read is refused.
