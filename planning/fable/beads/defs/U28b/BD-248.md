## Background

Several of the project's bets are tested on a set of designs whose right answers are known before any analysis
runs. BET-05: static analysis (`design.check`) prunes bad designs before simulation and does not throw away a
winner; it is falsified if, on the corpus, check verdicts predict simulated failure no better than a
mass-and-torque heuristic (area under the ROC curve below 0.8, or less than 0.05 above the heuristic), or the
blinded audit of discards finds a discarded design in the true top k, or the planted winner is discarded
(GATE-ATEL-01, kill gate KILL-STATIC). BET-06: a baseline controller synthesized from the model moves most
well-formed designs (GATE-REFLEX-02). The corpus is also the *potency control* for every analysis and screen:
an experiment that cannot see a planted effect says nothing about a real one. Three later consumers need
particular entries: the screening audit needs *planted winners* (designs known to belong in the top k that a
cheap screen represents poorly), the attribution gate needs *planted single causes* (failures with exactly one
cause), and the agent comparisons count turns to a fix on *planted-defect* designs.

The rule that gives the corpus its value is: **labels are fixed before any analysis is tuned.** RISK-14 names
the failure this prevents (the corpus tuned to the analyses: labels edited after an analysis changed) and its
mitigations: labels frozen before tuning, a held-out third, potency controls. The Arena unit's registry bead
supplies the mechanism (label hash, corpus identity, freeze record, split, loader). This bead is the content:
authoring the designs, deriving every label from the construction, and committing the freeze. Tier: Solid.
Seed done-condition: the hash of the label file is committed before any analysis is tuned; a relabel changes
the corpus identity.

Rejected alternatives: labels produced by running the analyses (circular: the analysis grades itself); a corpus
of randomly generated designs only (no known answers, so no potency control); labels kept with each analysis's
tests (nothing detects a relabel).

## Scope

In: the content of the corpus: the list of entries and their typed-edit constructions; the planting rules with
margins; the label derivations; the independent re-derivation; the act of freezing corpus version 1 (the commit,
its order, and the check that no read precedes it) and the rule for later versions; sealing material for
planted winners; the end-to-end script on the shipped corpus.

Out, with the bead that has it: the registry (N24b-02, a dependency of this bead): the formats of `corpus.toml`
and `labels.toml` (schema `frob.corpus/1`, including the fields this bead's content needs: the `control` mark
on a perturbation, the `margin` of a label, a winner's rank record or `rank: pending`, a cause's removing and
inert interventions), the definitions of `LabelHash` and `CorpusId`, the `corpus-frozen` record and the loader's
refusal of a relabelled corpus, the split into a tuning part and a held-out third, the `tune` and `evaluate`
purposes, and the rebuild of an entry from its parent. This bead populates that registry and uses its freeze;
it defines no format and no hash. Also out: the validations that consume the corpus (BD-249, BD-250); the
screening audit and its blinding of planted winners (N26b-03, N26b-05; applied to the cascade by BD-345);
attribution and the realization of planted causes as the 200-failure suite (N27b-08, BD-280); the design
benchmark's briefs (N28b-10).

## Technical approach

**1. Composition (DESIGN; the total of about 200 is a TARGET).** Eighteen archetypes exist after BD-246:
`quad-mammal`, `quad-sprawl`, `hexapod-radial`, `octopod`, `biped-point`, `humanoid`, `diff-drive`,
`ackermann`, `mecanum`, `omni-3`, `swerve-4`, `tracked-flipper`, `wheel-leg`, `tri-wheel-stair`,
`rocker-bogie`, `mobile-manipulator`, `snake-crawler`, `balancer-2w`.

| Origin kind | Count | How made | What is known in advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| `template` | 18 | One per archetype, unmodified | Passes `design.check`; traverses 5 m of flat ground under the baseline controller where the class has one |
| `perturbation` | 90 | Five per archetype: typed parameter edits inside the template's declared lever ranges (link lengths ±10 to 20%, mass moved between links, gear ratio, wheel radius, track width) | Sound by construction, each with its margin to every planted threshold recorded |
| `perturbation`, marked `control: sound-near-miss` | 12 | Edits that move toward a planted defect and stop at least 25% on the safe side of its threshold | The planted sound designs that an analysis must pass |
| `planted-defect` | 54 | 18 per kind, across at least six classes each (see 2) | The defect kind and the analysis expected to report it |
| `planted-winner` | 10 | See 3 | Top-k membership in its class population from a full evaluation |
| `planted-cause` | 20 | See 4 | The single cause and the intervention that removes the failure |

Every non-template entry is its parent plus a list of typed edits, so the registry can rebuild it and compare
the `DesignId`. No entry is authored by editing a file by hand.

**2. Planted defects (closed list of kinds at version 1).**

- `undersized-knee-actuator` (legged classes): the knee actuator is swapped for a smaller card or regeared so
  that the continuous torque needed to hold the nominal crouch exceeds the card's continuous rating. Expected
  finding: the actuator-envelope analysis (A-11).
- `inertia-breaks-static-stability` (all classes): a mass or payload edit moves the projected centre of mass
  outside the support region of the nominal stance, or reduces the tip-over angle below the task's slope.
  Expected finding: static stability (A-08).
- `wheelbase-too-long-for-aisle` (wheeled, tracked and hybrid classes): the wheelbase or track length is
  lengthened until the turning footprint exceeds the aisle width of the tight-space task. Expected finding:
  maneuverability (A-07).

*The margin rule (DESIGN).* A planted defect violates its threshold by at least 25% (for example the demanded
knee torque is at least 1.25 times the continuous rating), and a `sound-near-miss` control stays at least 25%
inside it. The reason is that a label must not depend on an analysis's tolerance or on which load-sharing
model it assumes: inside a ±25% band the entry would test tuning, not potency. The margin of each entry is a
field of its label.

**3. Planted winners.** A planted winner is a design that (a) a full evaluation (a specialist policy promoted
on the referee, on the class's reference task) placed in the top k of a recorded population of its class, with
k and the population identity on file, and (b) the cheap screens represent poorly. Two constructions are used:
an unusual gait (for example a quadruped whose best gait is a bound when the family policy's training set is
dominated by trots) and a body far from the family policy's training generator (few examples of its morphology
in the training set). The label holds the evaluation's `claim_id`, the population, k and the rank. A winner
whose rank is not on record is not a planted winner: an audit that is offered one is refused with
`FROB-REF-566` (a planted winner's rank is not on record from a full evaluation). At corpus version 1 (M3)
winners are registered for the classes that have a full evaluation; the others are entered as `rank: pending`
and are not usable as controls (OPEN-ATEL-5: the control is then reported `not-run` and finalists are
described as "k survivors"). Adding ranks later is a new corpus version with a new identity. For use in a
campaign a winner is given a blinded identity; the mapping is sealed in the campaign manifest hash and
revealed at close (the screening-audit beads own that mechanism).

**4. Planted single causes.** Twenty (design, task, seed set) entries in which a failure on the reference
task has exactly one cause, planted so that one typed intervention removes it and the others do not. The
classes are the five of the attribution known answer (KA-670), four entries per class, on the reference
quadruped under its baseline controller by default:

| Class | The plant (example magnitude; the entry's value is its own) | Removing intervention |
|---|---|---|
| Friction drop | A floor patch on the route lowered, 0.8 → 0.25 | `scene./…/friction := nominal` |
| Torque cap | One joint's torque limit lowered to 60% | `model.joint[<j>].torque_limit := nominal` |
| Payload shift | A payload displaced laterally, or shifted at a stated time | `disturbance.payload_shift := 0` |
| Latency | One sensor's latency raised, IMU 0 → 40 ms | `sense.imu.latency := nominal` |
| Terrain step | A step or sill on the route raised by 30 mm | `scene./…/step_height := nominal` |

Each label names the class, the plant as a canonical `do(…)` string and its inverse (the removing
intervention), two interventions that must not flip the outcome (a placebo and an unrelated mechanism), a
margin stratum (`narrow` or `wide`, from the derivation's margin to the failure threshold), and a seed set of
16 world addresses, so that each class offers 64 candidate failures from which the attribution suite (N27b-08)
keeps the 40 its single-cause check admits and draws its four out-of-list controls. The suite's ten-candidate
lists and its realization as recorded episodes are N27b-08's; the corpus fixes the plants, before any
attribution method is tuned.

*Every intervention of a cause is admissible at a mid-episode fork.* The attribution suite forks each failure
about 1 s before it and applies candidates from the fork tick, and it excludes a case whose remover or decoys
the fork service classifies `structural` at the fork tick (N27b-08's check (d), reason `not-forkable`).
Therefore the removing intervention and the two must-not-flip interventions of every cause are each a scheduled
disturbance, a scene property, a sensor-link value or a torque-limit row; none is a design or structural edit
(a payload mass, a gear ratio, an actuator card, a sensor mount, a speed limit that exists only as a design
parameter), which a fork refuses with `FROB-REF-581` and which could neither rescue nor rank. The torque-cap
remover `model.joint[<j>].torque_limit := nominal` is the diagnostic row `torque-limit-set` of N27b-01 (the
limit row takes its nominal value from the fork tick, labelled `diagnostic`, `realizable: false`); it is
admissible at a fork only because the fork classifier (N27a-02) classifies the limit row as exogenous from the
fork tick. The buildable form of the same change (a typed `set-param` on the actuator's limit, or
`swap-actuator`) is a different question, asked from the episode start by `autopsy.rerun`, and is not a corpus
intervention. Each cause's derivation file names the family of each of its three interventions.

**5. Labels, derived from the construction.** For every entry, `labels.toml` holds: the expected verdict of
each analysis that has an answer for it (`pass`, or `fail` with the finding code); the expected simulated
outcome class on the reference task (`traverses-5m` or `fails: <failure class>`); the margin; and for winners
and causes the planted property. Each label is computed by a closed-form or hand calculation written in
`assets/corpus/v1/derivations/<entry>.md` (for the knee: the static joint torque of the crouch pose from link
masses and lengths against the card's continuous rating; for stability: the projected centre of mass against
the support polygon with per-contact force limits; for the aisle: the swept footprint of a turn in place or
of the minimum-radius turn against the aisle width). No label is produced by running the analysis it will be
used to judge.

**6. Independent re-derivation.** A second session that has not seen `labels.toml` recomputes every label from
the derivation files and the designs. Disagreements are resolved by correcting the derivation or by removing
the entry, never by running the analysis under test; the count of disagreements and removals is recorded.

**7. The freeze.** Order of operations, enforced by the registry (N24b-02, which defines both hashes and the
record) and checked here: (a) designs and labels
committed; (b) re-derivation agrees; (c) `LabelHash` (BLAKE3 of the canonical label file) and `CorpusId`
(BLAKE3 of the sorted entries and `LabelHash`) computed and the `corpus-frozen` record written; (d) only then
may any analysis read the corpus for tuning. The freeze commit is the first commit in which the label file
exists on the main line, and the freeze record's date precedes the first `evaluation-read` and the first
`tune` read in the ledger. A relabel is a new version directory (`assets/corpus/v2/`), a new `LabelHash`, a new
`CorpusId` and a new freeze record; results cite the `CorpusId` they ran on, so old results stay attached to
the old labels.

**8. Files.** `assets/corpus/v1/{corpus.toml, labels.toml, designs/*.fbot, derivations/*.md, AUTHORING.md}`.
Determinism: identities are D0. The corpus emits no physical measurement of its own.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: the `corpus-frozen` record with `LabelHash` and `CorpusId` is committed before any
      analysis reads the corpus; the ledger shows no `tune` or `evaluation-read` event older than the freeze.
- [ ] Seed done-condition: changing one label and reloading under the old freeze record is refused; the same
      change in a new version directory yields a different `CorpusId`.
- [ ] About 200 entries with the counts of the composition table; every archetype appears as a template; each
      defect kind has 18 entries across at least six classes.
- [ ] Every non-template entry rebuilds from its parent by its typed edits to the recorded `DesignId`.
- [ ] Every planted defect violates its threshold by at least 25%; every `sound-near-miss` control is at least
      25% inside it; margins are recorded.
- [ ] Every label has a derivation file; the independent re-derivation agrees on 100% of retained entries and
      its disagreement count is on record.
- [ ] Each planted winner cites the `claim_id` of its full evaluation with population, k and rank, or is
      marked `rank: pending` and loads with `usable_as_control = false` (the screening audit's refusal of such a
      plant, `FROB-REF-566` from `nemesis.audit prepare`, is tested by its own beads, N26b-03 and N26b-07, at M4).
- [ ] Each planted cause names its class (one of the five of KA-670, four entries each), one removing
      intervention, two that must not flip the outcome, a margin stratum and 16 world addresses; all three
      interventions are of a fork-admissible family (none a design edit).
- [ ] One third of entries, stratified by class and origin kind, is held out by the registry's split.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-arena --test corpus_content` (run against the shipped corpus, no mocks):

- `corpus_counts_match_table`: counts per origin kind equal 18, 102 (90 + 12), 54, 10, 20 (total 204);
  planted causes 4 per class in each of the five classes.
- `corpus_every_entry_rebuilds`: re-apply typed edits to each parent; all `DesignId`s match.
- `corpus_defect_margins`: for each planted defect, the recorded ratio (demand ÷ rating, footprint ÷ aisle,
  required tip angle ÷ available) is at least 1.25; for each near-miss control at most 0.75.
- `corpus_labels_have_derivations`: one derivation file per entry, non-empty, naming its formula.
- `corpus_winner_needs_rank_record`: a winner without a resolving `claim_id` is `pending`, and the loader
  (N24b-02) returns it with `usable_as_control = false`; every winner with a rank record resolves its `claim_id`
  and has a rank of at most k.
- `corpus_cause_has_one_remover`: exactly one intervention is listed as removing, and at least two as inert;
  the removing intervention is the inverse of the plant string; each entry has 16 distinct world addresses.
- `corpus_cause_interventions_forkable`: every removing and must-not-flip intervention parses as a typed
  assignment whose path lies in one of the four fork-admissible families (`disturbance.…`, `scene./…`,
  `sense.<sensor>.…`, `model.joint[<j>].torque_limit`) and none as a typed design edit; a `synthetic` cause whose
  remover is `model.joint[<j>].gear_ratio := nominal` fails the test naming the entry. (The live classification
  at each fork tick is N27b-08's check (d).)
- `corpus_freeze_precedes_reads`: the freeze record's sequence number is lower than every corpus read event.
- `corpus_relabel_changes_identity`: on a copy, flip one label in a new version directory; `CorpusId` differs.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/design_corpus_freeze.sh` sources the shared helper of STD-E2E. Steps: `frob design check
<entry> --json` on one template, one planted defect of each kind and one near-miss control (the corpus entries
are ordinary designs reachable from the user's side); `cargo test -p frob-arena --test corpus_content`, which
loads the shipped corpus through the registry's loader and asserts the `CorpusId` against the freeze record in
the ledger; the same target on a copy with one label flipped, which must be refused; the counts per origin kind
and split that the target prints, compared with the composition table. Log `target/e2e-logs/design_corpus_freeze/<utc>.ndjson` and a
transcript: commands, exit codes, wall time, the envelope's evidence block, both hashes, counts with expected
values, and on failure the first entry that failed to rebuild or the first label without a derivation. Final
`summary` line. The script asserts that no label text is written to the log for held-out entries.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers are the labels themselves: each planted defect is expected to be reported by its named
  analysis (A-11, A-08, A-07) when BD-250 runs, and each near-miss control to pass.
- Negative controls: the relabelled copy (refused); an entry whose edits do not reproduce its design
  (refused); a planted winner with no rank record (loaded with `usable_as_control = false`, never offered as a
control); a "defect" encoded only as a
  metadata flag with no change to the design (the rebuild check shows the design equals its parent, and the
  entry is rejected), which keeps planted defects honest physics.
- Determinism: `CorpusId`, the split and every rebuilt `DesignId` are identical twice and across machines.

## Considerations

- RISK-14 (owner: Arena lead): the corpus tuned to the analyses. Signal: labels edited after an analysis
  changed. This bead's freeze order and the registry's refusal are the countermeasure.
- OPEN-ATEL-5 (owner: Arena lead; blocks GATE-ATEL-09 for the affected classes): planted winners for classes
  with no earlier specialist result. Fail-closed default: `not-run` for the control, "k survivors" in reports.
- The corpus is discovery data and a potency control. It is never a confirmation sample, and a result on it
  is not a claim about designs in general.
- Twelve of the eighteen archetypes have no permissively licensed reference robot; their templates stand
  alone, and no imported model enters the corpus without its licence record.
- The margin rule makes the corpus easy on purpose near the thresholds. Sensitivity near a threshold is a
  different question and belongs to the analyses' own known answers.
- Revisit the composition if the held-out third has fewer than three entries in any stratum that a gate reads.
