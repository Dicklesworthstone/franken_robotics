## Background

The co-design cascade spends cheap stages to discard designs before expensive ones. A screen can throw away
the winner and nothing downstream will notice: an e-value between the eighth and ninth survivors says nothing
about a design discarded at the first rung (counterexample CX-293). RISK-37 ("a screen discards the winner",
rated high, owner Atelier lead) is the risk; this bead is its countermeasure inside the cascade. Tier: Solid.

Three audits make the loss observable. The first two are *the* screening audit, a single mechanism owned by
the adversarial subsystem (verb `nemesis.audit`, gate GATE-NEMESIS-07; lane N26b-01, bounds and verdicts
N26b-02, plant N26b-03, record `frob.nemesis.audit/1` with its label and bypass N26b-04, verb N26b-05, gate
recorded by N26b-07): a stratified sample of discarded designs that is evaluated anyway, and a planted winner
that must reach the finalists. This bead
applies that audit to each stage of the cascade and defines no second mechanism, verb or bound. The third
audit, prune soundness, is Atelier's own (N28b-30). What this bead adds is the consequence: **Atelier does not
trust a rung without a passed audit** (GATE-ATEL-09). A rung whose audit is missing, whose planted winner was
lost, or whose audited miss rate exceeds its tolerance is set to `uninformative` in the affected strata, its
results are labelled `screen-biased`, and the campaign re-opens.

Terms. A *rung* is one evaluation method of the ladder (static check, shared policy, fine-tuned policy,
specialist, referee, rare-failure bound). A *stratum* is the unit in which rank agreement is measured (default:
one per morphology class). A rung's *mode* in a stratum is `selective`, `discard-only` or `uninformative`
(N28b-23). The *audit lane* is the keyed draw of designs that go up a rung regardless of score (N28b-13).

Rejected alternatives: auditing only the final ranking (silent about discards); calibrating on survivors
(biased toward agreement; refused as `FROB-REF-546`); a second audit mechanism private to Atelier (two bounds
for one question would drift apart; the plan fixes one owner).

Seed done-condition: GATE-ATEL-09; GATE-NEMESIS-07.

## Scope

In: the audit plan in the campaign manifest; the per-stage application; blinding of audit jobs; the trust rule
and its labels, including the cascade's answer to the audit verdict `bound-above-tolerance`, which the audit
leaves to this bead; the re-opening procedure; the handling of a control that cannot run; the planted-campaign
battery that measures GATE-ATEL-09; reading GATE-NEMESIS-07 as recorded by N26b-07 (the seed's done-condition
names both gates; this bead does not measure the second).

Out: the audit's lane, bounds, verdicts, the planted winner's sealing, the record, its label and the bypass
(N26b-01 to N26b-04); the verb (N26b-05); the GATE-NEMESIS-07 measurement (N26b-07); the keyed draw (N28b-13);
the prune-soundness sampler (N28b-30); the mode automaton (N28b-23); the ledger rows the audit feeds
(N28b-22); the cascade orchestrator (BD-352); the corpus's planted winners (BD-248).

## Technical approach

**1. The audit plan (part of the campaign manifest, fixed before the first score).** For every screening stage
of the cascade (stage 1 deduplicate and check, stage 2 reuse and synthesize where it excludes, stage 3 screen,
stage 4 race) the manifest lists: the strata, formed as stage × reason for discard × morphology class × archive
cell block; the audit rate per stratum (DESIGN default: a tenth of the rung's spend, the audit lane's share);
the fixed episode count n per audited design; the confidence 1 − δ (default δ = 0.05) and the tolerance τ on
the audited miss rate (default α = 0.05); the lane's merge floor `m_min`, which the cascade sets to the
smallest m whose clean bound meets the tolerance, ⌈ln δ / ln(1 − τ)⌉ (59 at the defaults), so that the lane
(N26b-01) merges a small stratum upward (score band first, then reason, leaving the morphology class) from its
quota counts before any score exists, never after an outcome is seen; k, the number of finalists wanted; and
the hash that seals the planted
winner's identity. The controller obtains each stage's plan and seal by `frob nemesis audit prepare <campaign>
--stage <n> [--share 1/10] [--n-audit N] [--delta 0.05] [--tolerance 0.05] [--top-k K] [--plant <design>
--poorly <kind>] --key <k>` (N26b-05; a plant whose rank is not on record is refused there with FROB-REF-566),
and writes the returned commitments into the manifest. The manifest hash is written to the ledger before any
design is scored. A rate changed after that point is a different campaign.

**2. Blinding.** An audit design and a survivor are the same job type with the same budget. The evaluating job
receives a design, addresses and a budget and nothing that says which lane it serves; lane membership is a
function of the audit key that only the campaign controller evaluates. The planted winner enters under a
blinded identity (a fresh `DesignId` of a re-described but equivalent term is not used: the design is the
design; what is hidden is the *fact* that it is the plant, which lives only in the sealed manifest field).

**3. Per-stage application.** Before a stage starts, the controller asks `audit::bypass_in_force(screen,
stratum)` (N26b-04) for every stratum: a bypass in force, from this campaign or from an earlier one that used
the same screen identity and version, or the answer `audit-missing` for a screen identity with no audit
record, makes the stage's rung `uninformative` there before it runs. When a stage closes, the controller calls
`frob nemesis audit run <campaign> --stage <n> --key <k>` (the default action; `--campaign <id>` is the same
argument). The returned record (schema `frob.nemesis.audit/1`, inside the envelope `frob.nemesis.audit.out/1`)
gives, per stratum: m (audited discards that completed), x (audited discards that belong in the true top k at
the higher rung), the exact upper bound U on the rate of discarded winners at confidence 1 − δ, the verdict
(`passed`: x/m ≤ τ and U ≤ τ; `screen-biased`: x/m > τ; `bound-above-tolerance`: x/m ≤ τ and U > τ, with
`audits_needed`; `void`: an audit episode was cancelled or re-weighted, or the plan was committed after scores),
and the planted winner's status (`sealed` until the reveal is checked, then `survived`, `missed` with its stage
and stratum, `not-run`, or `commitment-mismatch`), with the record's `label` and `bypass` list. With x = 0 the
bound is 1 − δ^(1/m): 9.5% at m = 30 and 5.0% at m = 59 for δ = 0.05. The approximation ln(1/δ)/m (10.0% at
m = 30) is not used. The record's calibration rows (`Presample` and `StratumQuota` inclusion) are written to the
rank-agreement ledger as its continuous source of calibration rows.

**4. The trust rule.** For each (stage, stratum) at stage close:

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| No audit record (`audit-missing`), a stratum verdict `void`, or a plant status `commitment-mismatch` | The stage's rung is `uninformative` in the stratum; results that depended on it are labelled `screen-biased` (the plan treats a stage whose audit is missing as one that lost its plant) |
| Plant status `missed` | Results labelled `screen-biased` (the record's own label); the offending rung (the one that discarded it) is `uninformative` in every stratum of the plant's design family at that stage (the record's bypass); the campaign re-opens |
| Stratum verdict `screen-biased` (audited miss rate x/m above the tolerance τ) | Same as the row above, for that stratum |
| Stratum verdict `bound-above-tolerance` (no evidence of bias, but U > τ) | The rung is `uninformative` in that (already merged) stratum and re-opens its discards, with **no** `screen-biased` label; the report prints `audit insufficient: U at m, audits_needed` |
| A feasible design found among sampled prunes (N28b-30) | The pruning rule is suspended for the campaign; the case is filed as a negative-control seed |
| Stratum verdict `passed` and plant `survived` (or `not-run` under 6) | The rung keeps the mode the rank-agreement ledger gives it |

DESIGN for the `bound-above-tolerance` row, which the audit leaves to the cascade: the rule fails closed
because the plan's rule is that no rung is trusted without a *passed* audit, and the label stays off because
the audit found no discarded winner above the tolerance and a label would assert a bias that was not
observed. Any discarded winner the audit lists continues in the campaign as a survivor whatever the verdict.

`uninformative` here is the mode of the rank-agreement ledger: the rung is skipped in that stratum, its budget
moves up one rung, and designs it eliminated earlier in the campaign are re-opened. A bypass that the record
puts in force is lifted only by a re-qualifying audit of the same screen identity and version (N26b-04); a
`bound-above-tolerance` verdict never re-qualifies.

**5. Re-opening (deterministic).** The designs the rung discarded in the affected strata are re-queued at the
next rung in address order, under the remaining budget. If the budget cannot cover them, the campaign ends
with the honest disposition "inconclusive at this budget", still labelled `screen-biased`. Nothing is
silently truncated.

**6. A control that cannot run.** If no planted winner with a recorded rank exists for a morphology class
(OPEN-ATEL-5), the planted-winner control is reported `not-run` for that class, the result carries that, and
finalists are described as "k survivors", never as "the top k". The stratified audit of discards still runs;
`not-run` for the plant is not treated as a missing audit.

**7. Audit episodes are never touched.** No audit-lane episode is cancelled, re-weighted or selected after any
score for its design exists (INV-ATEL-03). A request that would cancel one (a race's elimination, a budget
rebalance) is refused with `FROB-REF-546` (calibration from survivors, or an audit episode cancelled or
re-weighted; repair: redraw the audit lane by key; exit 3).

**8. Records.** Each application writes a `core_decision`-style evidence record: campaign, stage, stratum,
audit record identifier, the finding, the action, the mode before and after, the decision-policy identifier
(`atelier.screen-audit.trust`, version 1). Reports print the audit outcome and the planted-winner outcome for
every stage (the campaign report bead reads these records). Determinism: the rule is a pure function of the
audit record and the manifest (D0).

**9. Measuring GATE-ATEL-09.** A battery of 100 planted campaigns on a `synthetic` scorer (scores are a pure
function of design and address, so a campaign costs milliseconds; the cascade controller, the audit and the
trust rule are the real code). Plants, distributed over the hundred: a stage whose audit record is deleted; a
biased screen that removes the planted winner; a screen whose discards contain true top-k designs at a rate
above tolerance; a pruning rule that excludes a feasible design. Clean campaigns are interleaved as the pass
case.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-ATEL-09 (TARGET): in 100 of 100 planted campaigns a stage with no audit record, a missed planted
      winner or an audited miss rate above its tolerance has its rung set to `uninformative` in the affected
      strata and its results labelled `screen-biased`; a planted unsound prune is caught in 100 of 100; no
      audit episode is ever cancelled. Measured counts are in the receipt of `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test
      -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-ATEL-09`, and the gate is recorded from it with the
      `record_gate` example.
- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-07 (TARGET), measured and recorded by N26b-07 and read here (its row in
      `registries/gates.toml` resolves with PASS): a planted winner removed by a biased screen is reported in
      100 of 100 runs; the stratified bound covers the planted false-discard rate in at least 95% of 1,000
      synthetic campaigns.
- [ ] A stratum with verdict `bound-above-tolerance` after the upward merge is `uninformative` with no
      `screen-biased` label; a bypass in force from an earlier campaign on the same screen identity and
      version makes the rung `uninformative` before the stage runs.
- [ ] Clean campaigns in the battery are not labelled `screen-biased` (the rule does not fire on nothing).
- [ ] The audit plan and the planted winner's seal are in the manifest hash before the first score; a rate
      edited afterwards is rejected as a different campaign.
- [ ] An evaluating job's inputs are byte-identical for an audit design and a survivor of the same design.
- [ ] Re-opening re-queues exactly the discards of the affected strata (for a missed plant, every stratum of
      its design family at that stage), in address order.
- [ ] With no planted winner on record the control prints `not-run` and reports say "k survivors".
- [ ] A cancellation aimed at an audit episode returns `FROB-REF-546`.
- [ ] Every application has an evidence record with the decision-policy identifier and resolves through
      `evidence.trace`; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-opt` lists the rule, its labels and refusals.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt --test screen_trust`:

- `missing_audit_sets_uninformative`: delete the audit record of stage 3 for stratum `quad-mammal`; mode
  becomes `uninformative` there and stays unchanged in `hexapod-radial`.
- `lost_plant_labels_screen_biased`: the plant (family `hexapod-radial`) is discarded at stage 3 (status
  `missed`); results carry `screen-biased`; the stage-3 rung is `uninformative` in every `hexapod-radial`
  stratum of stage 3 and unchanged in other families; the re-open queue equals those strata's discards.
- `miss_rate_over_tolerance`: m = 30, x = 3, τ = 0.05 → verdict `screen-biased`, rung `uninformative`, label
  set; m = 59, x = 0 → bound 0.0495 ≤ 0.05, `passed`, mode unchanged; m = 30, x = 0 → bound 0.0950 > 0.05,
  `bound-above-tolerance`: the rung is `uninformative` and no label is set; m = 30, x = 1 →
  `bound-above-tolerance`, the listed winner continues as a survivor.
- `merge_floor_from_tolerance`: the plan's `m_min` is 59 at τ = δ = 0.05 and 29 at τ = 0.10; two discard
  strata of one class with quotas 30 and 29 are merged by the lane into one of 59 before any score, and a
  merge requested after the stage's outcomes exist is refused as a changed campaign.
- `bypass_from_earlier_campaign`: a bypass recorded on screen `scr:family-zero-shot@4` in campaign 1 makes
  that rung `uninformative` in campaign 2 before stage 3 runs; version 5 of the screen starts unaudited
  (`audit-missing`).
- `void_and_commitment_mismatch_as_missing`: both make the rung `uninformative` and set `screen-biased`.
- `bound_values`: 1 − 0.05^(1/30) = 0.0950 and 1 − 0.05^(1/59) = 0.0495 to four places; ln(20)/30 = 0.0999 is
  not the value returned.
- `not_run_plant_is_not_missing_audit`; `reopen_order_is_address_order`;
  `reopen_over_budget_ends_inconclusive_screen_biased`.
- `audit_job_inputs_identical`: serialize the job request for one design in both lanes; bytes equal.
- `cancel_audit_episode_refused_546`.
- Property test for INV-ATEL-03: over 1,000 random campaign schedules with races and budget rebalances, no
  audit episode's state is ever `cancelled` and no audit weight differs from 1.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/screening_audit.sh` is specified in N28b-31 and drives `frob opt cascade` and `frob nemesis
audit` on the planted and clean seeded populations (the cascade orchestrator BD-352 depends on this bead, so
the user-side script lives in the later bead and names this one). This bead's own gate run is the GATE-ATEL-09
entry of the known-answer tier target, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
g1_known_answers -- GATE-ATEL-09` (with `--features gauntlet-mutations` for the negative controls below),
invoked by that script as its `gates` step. It runs the 100 planted campaigns and logs, per campaign: the plant
kind, the stage and stratum affected, the audit record (m, x, bound, tolerance), the mode before and after, the
label, the count of re-opened designs, and the count of cancelled audit episodes against 0. Structured log per
STD-E2E with the gate's measured values against 100 of 100. The test writes the receipt; the gate value is
recorded from it by this bead with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ATEL-09
--receipt <hash>`, never by a script.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-706 / KA-668: no top-k design among 30 audited discards bounds the false-discard rate at 9.5% with 95%
  confidence; 59 bound it at 5.0%. A planted winner removed by a deliberately biased screen is reported;
  negative control: a screen with no bias passes.
- CX-293: a comparison among survivors does not validate the screen; the battery includes a campaign whose
  survivor comparison is decisive while its screen discarded the plant, and the campaign must still be
  labelled `screen-biased`.
- Negative controls for the gate itself: the trust rule disabled (a planted campaign then reports the top k
  with no label, and the gate test must fail); the audit calibrated on survivors (refused, `FROB-REF-546`).
- Metamorphic: permuting the order in which strata are closed leaves modes and labels unchanged.
- Determinism: identical records twice, across worker counts and under random preemption of non-audit jobs.

## Considerations

- OPEN-ATEL-5 (owner: Arena lead; blocks GATE-ATEL-09 for the affected classes). Fail-closed default as in 6.
- Cost: the audit lane's tenth of rung spend is the price of the control and is charged to the campaign; the
  campaign report states it. With m below 59 per stratum the default tolerance cannot be supported, so small
  strata are merged upward to the morphology class before the audit is declared too small.
- A lost plant in one stratum says nothing about the others; the rule is per stratum on purpose.
- KILL-STATIC and KILL-RANK change which rungs exist; the trust rule is unchanged and applies to whatever
  rungs remain. If the screening audit itself fails GATE-NEMESIS-07, no rung may discard and every candidate
  that survives static analysis is scored on the higher rung.
- Interaction with the decision cores: audit designs run at fixed n and are never stopped by the promotion
  core; the interference test "no audit episode was stopped by an e-process" belongs to the composition bead.
- Privacy: audit records hold design identities and scores, no scene data.
