## Background

A design comparison is about an order, and an order can be an artifact of the model: of the compliance of the
contact law, of the shape of the friction cone, of a penalty stiffness, of the time step, or of solver
residual. A fast contact model can also hand a policy energy that no actuator supplied. Analysis A-19 asks:
does the ranking of the finalists survive a change of contact law, cone, stiffness, step or solver tolerance,
and did any finalist profit from energy the simulator created? It is stage 5 of the six-stage co-design
cascade, between the race that produces finalists and the sealed qualification. Tier: Solid for the controls,
Frontier where the hard-contact batch law is the arm. It serves North-Star scenario NS-09 (a design whose rank
among finalists changes under another admissible contact law is labelled model-conditional and may not spend
prototype budget) and guards RISK-05 (the convex contact model's creep misleads wheel and stair results) and
RISK-47 (search exploits a model-form artifact).

One mechanism, one verb: the model-form rank check is registered as `fidelity.modelform` and implemented once
(BD-266). That check uses the fidelity chapter's criterion: top-k overlap of at least k − 1 under every variant
and a clean energy audit. The cascade is stricter, because its output is a ranked recommendation that may
spend prototype budget: the top-k *set* and the *order of the top two* must survive every variant, and no
finalist's advantage may coincide with created energy or cone excess. This bead adds that rule, the
per-finalist record, the consequences inside the cascade, and the stage's built-in control. It writes no verb
by hand: it registers A-19 as an `Analysis` of `frob-analyze`, a read view over the stored stage-5 record, from
which N28a-05's generator emits the row `analyze.rank-robustness` (section 8).

Rejected alternatives: one robustness score (the consumer needs to know which variant flipped the order,
because that names the experiment that would settle it); skipping the stage when the budget is tight (the same
campaign with stage 5 skipped reports the exploit as the winner, which is the stage's negative control);
typing only single policies (designs are compared, so the ranking is what must survive).

Seed done-condition: GATE-ATEL-10.

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs.** From stage 4: the ranked finalists (top k, DESIGN default k = 8, and the runners-up ranks
k + 1 to 2k), their paired episodes with world addresses, the base step plan, and the campaign's compute to
date. From the energy ledger: per-episode flags `energy-created` (created energy above 1% of the absolute work
exchanged in any 0.5 s window on a fast tier, above 10⁻⁶ relative on the referee), `contact-active` (a contact
returned more work than it absorbed) and `cone-excess` (friction utilization ‖f_t‖/(μ·f_n) above 1.05 for more
than 1% of stance time; a pyramidal cone admits up to √2 along its diagonal).

**2. Variants (each changes one thing; an unadmitted arm is `not-run` with its reason).**
(a) the alternative contact law, where a second law is admitted on the tier (the hard-contact batch law where
available); (b) the circular (elliptic) cone against a four-sided pyramid of the same μ; (c) contact stiffness
at one half and at twice its value, dissipation time held; (d) half the step, physical parameters held;
(e) one tenth of the solver tolerance.

**3. Procedure (numbered).**
1. Call the model-form check on the finalists with the stage budget (step 6). It returns per variant the
   re-scored order of the 2k designs and the top-k overlap, and the energy-audit summary.
2. Strict set test: for every admitted variant the top-k set equals the base top-k set (overlap = k).
3. Top-two test: for every admitted variant the designs ranked first and second are the same, in the same
   order.
4. Flag-coincidence test, per finalist f and per design g ranked below it among the 2k: recompute the paired
   comparison of f against g on the addresses where neither arm's episode carries any of the three flags.
   The advantage of f over g *coincides with flagged episodes* when the comparison was settled in f's favour
   on all addresses and is not settled in f's favour on the unflagged addresses (it reverses, ties, or
   becomes inconclusive with at least half of the pairs remaining). If fewer than half of the pairs remain
   unflagged, the advantage is counted as coinciding (the result rests mostly on flagged episodes).
5. Type the campaign ranking: `model-robust { variants, top_k_overlap, energy_audit }` when tests 2, 3 and 4
   all pass on every admitted variant; otherwise `model-conditional { flipped_by, implicated }`.
6. Budget: the stage may spend at most a tenth of the campaign's compute (TARGET). The model-form check sizes
   episodes per design per arm from that share and drops arms in its fixed order when the floor does not fit;
   dropped arms are `not-run: budget`.

**4. The per-finalist record (DESIGN).** The plan says each finalist is typed. Every finalist's result carries
the campaign ranking's type, and in addition `implicated: [reason]` with reasons `left-top-k under <variant>`,
`entered-top-k only under <variant>`, `top-two order flipped with <design> under <variant>` and
`advantage over <design> coincides with <flag>`. A finalist with an empty list in a `model-conditional`
campaign is still reported under the campaign's condition, and the report can say which finalists caused it.
A preference between two designs is stated only if it holds under both qualified contact laws; with one law
admitted, pairwise preferences are printed with "one contact law".

**5. `unchecked` and `not-run`.** A comparison's rank-robustness field takes one of three values:
`model-robust`, `model-conditional`, `unchecked`. It is `unchecked` when stage 5 did not run or no variant arm
was admitted. A stage that cannot run its control (no second contact law admitted, no planted exploit
available) reports the control as `not-run`, and the result carries that; `unchecked` and `not-run` are never
rendered as `model-robust`.

*The stage's negative control as a mutant.* In a conformance build (feature `gauntlet-mutations`) the mutant
`skip-stage5` replaces this stage by a no-op that writes no stage-5 record, so the ranking is `unchecked` and
the planted exploit is reported as the winner; GATE-ATEL-10's known-answer entry must fail under it. The
mutant's row is in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` (the negative control of GATE-ATEL-10).
Scripts reach it as `frob opt cascade … --mutant skip-stage5`, a conformance-build-only input loaded as every
`--mutant` is (the one loader of N21b-42 with the mutants of N35-15: the receipt and envelope carry
`conformance_mutant`, the ledger stores the run as non-citable, a release build answers 802); the input is on
VERBS-opt's row of `opt.cascade` (`conformance_only = ["mutant"]`) and is implemented by the cascade verb's bead
BD-352, which depends on this bead.

**6. Consequences of `model-conditional`.** (i) The report shows both orders as a fidelity-dependent ranking,
with the variant that flipped it. (ii) The result cannot be reported as a design comparison without its
condition: an attempt (a report, export or claim that drops the variant that flips it) is refused with
`FROB-REF-557` (repair: run A-19 and report the condition; exit 3). (iii) It cannot spend prototype budget: a
`model-conditional` result offered for promotion, as input to a build packet or for prototype budget is
refused with `FROB-REF-830` (repair: resolve the variant that flips the ranking; report both rankings; exit 3).
The plan's row for 557 names the build packet too; the refusal registry (N28-REF) and the citation rule of the
trust labels (N16b-04) fix one code per request, and the spend case is 830, the code GATE-CORTEX-15 requires;
this bead raises neither code itself, it sets the condition that the citation check reads. (iv) The condition
`model-conditional` joins the claim product's condition flags by union and cannot be dropped by any consumer.
(v) `next_actions` names the cheapest settling experiment (the same finalists on the
referee for a law flip; a step-refinement study for a step flip; a contact-parameter identification for a
stiffness flip; the offending contact mode and world for an energy or cone flag).

**7. Records and determinism.** One evidence record per campaign for the stage (inputs' identities, variant
step-plan identities, overlaps, top-two per variant, flag counts per finalist, the type, the spend). The type
is a deterministic function of the scores and flags. Output claim kind: Design comparison, class Tested.
Layer: `frob-opt::cascade::stage5`, calling `frob-opt::modelform`.

**8. Registration as analysis A-19 (the row `analyze.rank-robustness`).** A-19 is registered in `frob-analyze`
as an `Analysis` under the contract of N28a-04 (`crates/frob-analyze/src/analyses/a19_rank_robustness.rs`). It
is a read view over the stage-5 evidence record of section 7: it never re-runs the model-form check or the
stage, and it adds no second implementation of the rule. N28a-05's generator emits `analyze.rank-robustness`
from this registration, equal to the row VERBS-analyze has on file (`read`, registered tier S, first milestone
M4, `analyze:read`, `frob.analyze.rank-robustness.in/1` and `.out/1`, owner `frob-analyze`, example `frob
analyze rank-robustness camp:12 --json`). The registration (DESIGN where the row leaves the spelling to the
implementing bead):

- `ID = "A-19"`, `VERSION = 1` (raised by any change to the strict rule, the flag-coincidence test or the record
  layout); `tier()` Solid (the row prints `S/F`; mixed rows are registered `S`, the hard-contact arm being one
  variant); `screening()` false; `encloses()` empty and `bound` never set; never in `check` (it reads runs).
- Inputs: the campaign (positional, `camp:<id>`), or `--finalists <id>` (a stored stage-4 finalist set, whose
  stage-5 record is looked up by its identity); optional `--variants law,cone,stiffness,step,tolerance` (a subset
  of the five arms of section 2 to print; the type is always computed over every admitted arm). Without either
  binding the verb answers exit 3 naming the missing campaign as the first repair (N28a-05's rule).
- `reads()`: the stage-5 record and the energy-ledger flag counts it cites, by content hash; the memo key is that
  hash, the version and the parameters, so a new stage-5 record is a new key.
- `run`: `AnalysisResult.output` = `{ campaign, rule: "cascade-strict", type, variants: [{ variant, admitted |
  not-run(reason), order (the 2k designs), top_k_overlap, top_two }], flipped_by, finalists: [{ design,
  implicated: [reason], flags: { energy_created, contact_active, cone_excess } }], spend_share, control }`, with
  `type` one of `model-robust`, `model-conditional`, `unchecked` (section 5) and `rule` saying which wording of
  model-robust produced it (the general verb `fidelity.modelform` reports its own). Evidence: the claim product
  of the runs read, with the `model-conditional` condition flag carried as section 6 (iv) requires.
- Findings: `A-19.model-conditional` (High: N28a-04's `finding_kinds()` admits Info, Low and High and never
  `Refusing`, since policy decides a refusing severity and A-19 never joins `design.check`; the finding names the
  condition that a consumer's citation check reads, and the consumer raises `FROB-REF-557` when it presents the
  result as a design comparison without it; repairs: the settling experiments of section 6 (v) as
  `next_actions`) and `A-19.unchecked` (Info; no stage-5 record, or no arm admitted; repair: `frob opt cascade
  --stage 5 --campaign <id> --key <k>`). The verb reports a finding with exit 2 and never refuses on it.
- Declared refusals: none. The generator appends `FROB-REF-551`, the row's only code: `FROB-REF-557` is raised
  by the verbs that present the ranking (`report.design`, `report.campaign`; N28-REF's row for 557 names them and
  excludes this view) and `FROB-REF-830` by the receiving verb of a spend.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-ATEL-10 (TARGET): every planted cone-excess and energy-pumping exploit is typed
      `model-conditional`; honest winners are typed `model-robust`; the stage costs at most 10% of campaign
      compute. Measured by the GATE-ATEL-10 entry of the known-answer tier target, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed>
      cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-ATEL-10` (with `--features
      gauntlet-mutations` for the skipped-stage control), on stage-4 fixtures: finalist sets built from the
      planted exploits and planted rankings of N16b-15 (pyramid-cone winner, penetration-recovery winner) and
      from an honest winner, each with the campaign compute of stages 1 to 4 recorded beside it, run through
      the real model-form check and this stage on the tier; the receipt holds the counts and the measured
      share, and this bead records the gate from it with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate
      -- --gate GATE-ATEL-10 --receipt <hash>`. The user-side confirmation through `opt.cascade` is the
      `stage5` step of N28b-28.
- [ ] Strictness: a ranking whose top-k overlap is k − 1 under one variant is `model-robust` for
      `fidelity.modelform` and `model-conditional` for the cascade, with that variant in `flipped_by`.
- [ ] A swap of ranks 1 and 2 under any admitted variant gives `model-conditional` even when the set is
      unchanged.
- [ ] A finalist whose advantage disappears on unflagged addresses is listed as implicated with the flag.
- [ ] `unchecked` when the stage is skipped; `not-run` arms listed with reasons; neither printed as robust.
- [ ] Through the citation check, a conditional stage-5 result cited as a design comparison without its
      condition returns `FROB-REF-557`, and the same result offered for promotion returns `FROB-REF-830` (one
      code per request, never both).
- [ ] Negative control caught: the same planted campaign with stage 5 skipped reports the exploit as winner.
- [ ] The stage writes its evidence record, and the condition appears in the envelope's claim product.
- [ ] A-19 is registered with identifier, version, reads, tier, findings and declared refusals; `check-generated`
      passes with the generated `analyze.rank-robustness` row equal to the row on file (refusal 551 alone,
      neither 557 nor 830); `frob analyze rank-robustness <campaign> --json` returns the stored stage-5 type, variants and
      implicated finalists without starting a job, exit 2 with `A-19.model-conditional` on a conditional
      campaign and exit 0 on a robust one.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt --test cascade_stage5`:

- `strict_set_rule`: k = 8, overlaps (8, 7, 8, 8, 8) → `model-conditional { flipped_by: [variant 2] }`;
  (8, 8, 8, 8, 8) with stable top two → `model-robust`.
- `top_two_flip`: overlaps all 8, ranks 1 and 2 exchanged under `stiffness ×2` → conditional, both designs
  implicated with reason `top-two order flipped`.
- `flag_coincidence`: finalist f beats g on 64 paired addresses, 40 to 24; 20 of f's wins are on addresses
  flagged `cone-excess`; on the 44 unflagged addresses the count is 20 to 24: advantage coincides, f
  implicated.
- `mostly_flagged_counts_as_coinciding`: 40 of 64 addresses flagged → coinciding regardless of the remainder.
- `honest_winner_unaffected_by_unrelated_flags`: flags on a design outside the 2k do not implicate anyone.
- `unchecked_when_skipped`; `not_run_arm_reported`; `one_law_pairwise_wording`.
- `conditional_offered_for_spend_830` (promotion and a build-packet request object, through N16b-04's check);
  `conditional_without_condition_refused_557`; `one_code_per_request`.
- `condition_joins_claim_product`: combining a conditional result with a robust one yields the condition.
- `budget_share_at_most_tenth`: campaign cost 10,000 units → stage spend ≤ 1,000 in the planned schedule.

`cargo test -p frob-analyze --test a19_registration`:

- `a19_generates_row_on_file`: the generator emits `analyze.rank-robustness` equal field for field to the row in
  `registries/verbs.toml` (refusals: 551 alone); a scratch registration that declares 557, and one that declares
  830, each fail `check-generated` naming the field.
- `a19_reads_stored_record_only`: on the stored record of the `strict_set_rule` campaign the output's type,
  `flipped_by` and overlaps equal the record's; the model-form check's call counter stays 0.
- `a19_unchecked_without_record`: a campaign with stage 5 skipped → type `unchecked`, finding `A-19.unchecked`,
  exit 2; no campaign bound → exit 3 naming the missing binding.
- `a19_finding_severity_high`: the conditional campaign's finding `A-19.model-conditional` has severity High
  (never `Refusing`), `finding_kinds()` declares it at High, and the verb exits 2, not 3.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/cascade_stage5_modelform.sh` is written by this bead and run as the `stage5` step of the cascade
end-to-end script (N28b-28), because it drives `frob opt cascade`, whose verb (BD-352) depends on this bead;
this bead's own gate pass is the known-answer tier entry above. The script sources the shared helper of STD-E2E. It
runs `frob opt cascade` on three seeded populations (one with the planted pyramid-cone winner, one with the
planted penetration-recovery winner, one clean), then the first again with `--mutant skip-stage5` in the
conformance build, each call with its own key (`--key s5-cone`, `s5-pen`, `s5-clean`, `s5-skip`); then
`frob report campaign`, the same report in a form that drops the condition columns, `frob eval promote` on
the conditional result, and `frob analyze rank-robustness <campaign> --json` on each campaign (the A-19 view:
the same type as the cascade's envelope, exit 2 with `A-19.model-conditional` on the conditional ones, exit 0 on
the clean one, `unchecked` on the stage-skipped one, and no job started). Asserts the type per campaign, the variant in `flipped_by`, the implicated finalist,
the measured stage share against 0.10, the refusals 557 (condition dropped) and 830 (promotion) with exit 3,
and that the stage-skipped run names the plant as winner and prints `unchecked`. Log:
`target/e2e-logs/cascade_stage5_modelform/<utc>.ndjson` with transcript: commands, exit codes, wall time, the
evidence block with its condition flags, per-variant overlaps and top-two, flag counts, the spend share with
its threshold, and on failure the first design whose rank crossed the top-k boundary. Final `summary` line
naming GATE-ATEL-10.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-692: a finalist whose advantage comes from diagonal traction on a pyramidal cone (up to √2·μ, 41% more
  than the cone) is typed `model-conditional`. Negative control: the same campaign with stage 5 skipped
  reports it as the winner.
- KA-353: the planted pyramid-cone winner and the planted penetration-recovery winner return
  `model-conditional`; a genuine winner returns `model-robust`; cost at most 10% of the campaign's compute.
- Hand check for the energy exploit: a position-level penetration correction that removes a depth of 1 mm in
  one 2 ms step gives a 1 kg body 0.5 m/s and 0.125 J per event from nothing; the planted hopping policy that
  uses it must carry `energy-created` and its design must be implicated.
- Metamorphic relation MR-39: a ranking survives the variants or is typed `model-conditional`; relabelling
  designs or permuting the order of variants leaves the type unchanged.
- Determinism: identical types twice, across worker counts, and with the variant arms run in any order.

## Considerations

- Three wordings of `model-robust` exist in the plan (overlap at least k − 1; the set and its order within
  noise; the set and the order of the top two). This bead implements the third for the cascade and leaves the
  first to the general verb; the two can disagree on the same ranking by design, and the output says which
  rule produced the label.
- A cone flip is observable only if the pyramid arm is admitted; otherwise the planted cone exploit must be
  caught by the `cone-excess` monitor through the coincidence test, which is why that test exists.
- A high share of `model-conditional` finalists per campaign is a warning about the contact law; the share is
  recorded per campaign for the fidelity ledger's owners.
- The 10% share is a TARGET; when the floor of episodes does not fit, arms are dropped and named, never
  silently skipped.
- `analyze.rank-robustness` and `fidelity.modelform` are not two spellings of one verb: the registry
  (VERBS-analyze) settles that the `analyze` verb is the finding-shaped read view (section 8) with its own
  schema, over the record the cascade wrote under the strict rule, while `fidelity.modelform` runs the general
  check under the k − 1 rule.
