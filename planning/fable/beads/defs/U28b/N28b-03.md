## Background

`design.bisect` finds, in a lineage of typed edits, an edit that changed whether a task succeeds. Two things
are promised about it. Gate GATE-ATEL-04 (its bisect row, due at M4): `design bisect` finds the planted edit
in at least 95 of 100 trials. Proof obligation PO-ATEL-08: `design.bisect` errs with probability at most α and
never reports `first-change` wrongly; it is discharged by the union-bound derivation and by 100 planted
lineages, 20 of them non-monotone. The comparison half of GATE-ATEL-04 (type-I error at or below α over 10⁴
null comparisons, due at M3) belongs to the comparison beads and is not measured here.

A gate that nothing can fail validates nothing, so the battery is built around plants: lineages in which the
edits that change the outcome are known because they were put there, and mutants of the search that must be
caught. The non-monotone lineages matter most: they are the case in which a naive binary search returns a
confident wrong answer (outcomes pass, fail, pass, pass, pass, fail; the search reports the last edit while
the first also changed the outcome). Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the generator of planted lineages and its frozen instance; the scoring rule for "found"; the run on the
real verb; a `synthetic` Bernoulli lane for the error rate; the mutants; the gate record.

Out: the search rules (N28b-02); the verb (BD-343); the comparison engine and its own null calibration
(BD-262).

## Technical approach

**1. The lineage generator (DESIGN).** `frob-conformance::fixtures::planted_lineage(seed)` builds a lineage
from a template with a declared task and predicate (default: `quad-mammal` and `diff-drive` on "stand and
recover" and "threshold delivery", success threshold as in the task file, margin δ = 0.1). A lineage has L
edits, L drawn from {8, 12, 16, 24, 32} by the seed. Edits are of two kinds:

- *inert* edits: renames, a colour, a display label, parameter moves of at most 1% on levers that the
  generator has verified (on the referee, at fixed n = 256 paired episodes) to leave the success rate within
  δ/4 of the parent's;
- *planted* edits, each with a verified effect of at least 3δ on the success rate: a break (for example the
  knee actuator swapped for a card with half the continuous torque; the drive wheel radius cut so the sill is
  not climbed) or its repair (the inverse edit, or a regear that restores the margin).

A *monotone* lineage has one planted break at a position drawn uniformly. A *non-monotone* lineage has three
planted edits: break, repair, break, at increasing positions, so its endpoints still differ. The frozen
instance `assets/fixtures/lineages/planted-v1/` holds 100 lineages (80 monotone, 20 non-monotone) with the
manifest: seed, template, task, L, planted positions and kinds, the verification record of every edit's
effect, and a hash committed before the battery is first run against the verb.

**2. Scoring a trial.** A trial runs `frob design bisect --good v₀ --bad v_L --predicate … --alpha 0.05`.

| Lineage | Run | Counted as found when |
|---|---|---|
| Monotone | default | The returned edit is the planted break and the label is `a-change` |
| Monotone | `--scan-prefix` | The returned edit is the planted break and the label is `first-change` |
| Non-monotone | default | The returned edit is one of the three planted edits, label `a-change`, `other_changes_possible = true` |
| Non-monotone | `--scan-prefix` | The returned edit is the first planted edit, label `first-change` |

A result with label `bracket` (an inconclusive probe) is not found and not wrong; it is counted separately. A
returned edit that is an inert edit is *wrong*. A `first-change` label on any edit other than the first planted
one is a *wrong first-change*; the tolerance for those is zero.

**3. The gate run.** All 100 lineages in the default mode, and again with `--scan-prefix`. The gate value is
the number found in the default mode (threshold: at least 95 of 100). Recorded beside it: found with scan,
brackets, wrong edits, wrong first-changes (must be 0), probes used against ⌈log₂ L⌉, pairs per probe, and
GPU- or CPU-seconds. Tier of the probes: `batch-cpu` where the task runs there, the referee otherwise; the
record names it. Each run is seed-explicit and reuses stored episodes through the memo graph on a second pass
(the second pass must reach identical e-values).

**4. The error-rate lane (`synthetic`).** The battery above has no null. To check "errs with probability at
most α" against something that can fail, a second lane replaces the simulator by Bernoulli outcomes: each
version has a success probability, inert edits leave it unchanged, and probes draw paired outcomes by address
from a keyed stream. 10⁴ lineages of L = 16 with one break of size exactly 3δ are bisected; the frequency of
wrong edits (an inert edit returned) must be at most α = 0.05 within the exact binomial acceptance bound for
10⁴ trials (at most 551 wrong, the 0.99 quantile of Binomial(10⁴, 0.05)). This lane runs the real search and
the real e-process code; only the episode source is synthetic, and it is labelled so.

**5. Mutants that must be caught (negative controls).**

| Mutant | Expected effect on the battery |
|---|---|
| Per-probe level α instead of α/P | Wrong-edit frequency in the synthetic lane rises above the acceptance bound |
| The `a-change` result relabelled `first-change` | Wrong first-changes on the non-monotone lineages (expected 20 of 20 in default mode minus those where the last break happens to be first) |
| Probes consume episodes in completion order | E-values differ between two runs with shuffled completion; the determinism assertion fails |
| The midpoint computed on the full path after each step (off-by-one bracket) | Found count falls below 95 on the monotone lineages |

**6. Outputs.** A gate receipt for the bisect row of GATE-ATEL-04 (commit, hardware fingerprint, seed,
measured counts), and a PO-ATEL-08 record citing the derivation file and both lanes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The frozen instance exists with 80 monotone and 20 non-monotone lineages and a committed manifest hash;
      every edit's verified effect is on record (inert within δ/4, planted at least 3δ).
- [ ] Bisect row of GATE-ATEL-04 (TARGET): the planted edit is found in at least 95 of 100 trials; the
      receipt holds the measured count, brackets and wrong edits.
- [ ] Zero wrong `first-change` labels over all 200 runs (default and scan).
- [ ] On the 20 non-monotone lineages the default result is `a-change` with `other_changes_possible = true`.
- [ ] Synthetic lane: wrong-edit frequency at most the exact binomial acceptance bound for α = 0.05.
- [ ] Each of the four mutants fails the battery in the way listed.
- [ ] A second pass served from the memo graph reaches identical e-values and labels.
- [ ] PO-ATEL-08 is recorded as discharged with the derivation, the counts and the mutants.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test planted_lineage`:

- `generator_is_seed_deterministic`: the same seed yields the same lineage bytes.
- `planted_positions_respect_kind`: monotone has one planted edit; non-monotone has three in increasing
  order with kinds break, repair, break.
- `manifest_hash_detects_change`: editing one planted position changes the manifest hash and the loader
  refuses the instance.
- `scoring_table`: synthetic results are scored as the table in 2 says (an inert edit returned is wrong; a
  bracket is neither).
- `binomial_bound`: the 0.99 quantile of Binomial(10⁴, 0.05) is 551 (computed exactly in integers).
- `bernoulli_lane_paired_by_address`: two probes of the same version pair read the same draws.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/bisect_planted_lineages.sh` sources the shared helper of STD-E2E. It loads the frozen instance,
runs `frob design bisect … --json` on every lineage in both modes through the real binary, scores each trial,
runs the synthetic lane and the four mutants (built as test-only features of the conformance crate), and
writes the gate receipt. Log `target/e2e-logs/bisect_planted_lineages/<utc>.ndjson` with transcript: per trial
the command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block, L, planted positions, each probe's version, outcome,
e-value and pair counts, α per probe, the returned edit and label, the score; per lane the counts against
thresholds (found ≥ 95; wrong first-change = 0; synthetic wrong ≤ 551); per mutant the assertion that failed.
On failure: the first lineage whose result differs from its plant, with the artifact paths to replay it. Final
`summary` line naming GATE-ATEL-04.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-702 / CX-313 are the smallest non-monotone lineage (six versions; pass, fail, pass, pass, pass, fail); it
  is lineage 0 of the frozen instance, with its expected probes (versions 2, 3, 4) asserted exactly.
- Negative controls: the four mutants; a lineage with no planted edit (endpoints equal), which must return
  `no-change`.
- Metamorphic: inserting an inert edit anywhere in a monotone lineage does not change which planted edit is
  returned; reversing `good` and `bad` with the predicate negated returns the same edit.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; `batch-cpu` and referee probes each against
  their determinism class.

## Considerations

- Effects of at least 3δ make the plants unambiguous; the battery tests the search and its error accounting,
  not the comparison's power at the margin, which the comparison gate covers.
- The 95-of-100 threshold allows brackets from inconclusive probes at the default budget; if brackets exceed
  five, the budget per probe, not the rule, is the first thing to examine.
- Cost: about ⌈log₂ L⌉ ≤ 5 probes per trial in default mode and up to L in scan mode; the scan pass dominates
  and may be run on `batch-cpu` overnight. The estimate is printed before the run.
- The frozen instance is discovery data for the verb's developers after its first use; a new instance (new
  seeds, new hash) is generated for each milestone's gate run.
