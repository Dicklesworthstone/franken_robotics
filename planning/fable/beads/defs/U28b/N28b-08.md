## Background

Inside one generation of an optimization, candidates are compared by e-processes and the dominated ones are
cancelled mid-evaluation, so compute follows the survivors. Whether that saves anything depends on how well
separated the candidates are. FACT (the sibling's own battery, `frankensim/crates/fs-race/tests/battery.rs`
and its `CONTRACT.md`): it asserts a saving of at least 2 on a separated field and below 1.5 on an inseparable
one, with 11.7 and 1.03 measured on its fixtures. The plan therefore expects two to five times fewer
evaluations when candidates are separated and nothing otherwise, and marks this a HYPOTHESIS for robot design
populations, "settled per campaign by the saving the race ledger records". This bead builds that ledger.
Tier: Solid.

Why a ledger and not a claim: the ten-fold thesis counts on fewer evaluations per decision, and a factor that
is asserted from a sibling's toy fixture and never measured on robot populations is exactly the kind of false
progress the plan guards against. The ledger turns the factor into a measured, per-campaign number with a
stated baseline, and the studio registers its own prediction of the saving before the race so that its
expectations are scored.

Terms. A *race* over N candidates proceeds in rounds; rounds are the only clock; in each round every survivor
consumes one observation (one paired episode at the next world address) in index order. A candidate's
elimination evidence is the average of its pairwise e-processes against all original opponents (the mixture
construction), and it is eliminated when that mixture reaches 1/α, so the true best is eliminated with
probability at most α. The *no-stopping baseline* is the same field evaluated with every candidate run to the
round cap.

## Scope

In: the race ledger record `frob.race-ledger/1` and the module that folds it from the race's round events; the
saving and its denominators; the separation diagnostic; accounting of episodes cancelled part-way; the memo and
audit-lane accounting rules; the registered prediction of the saving; the campaign roll-up and the optional
block `race_ledger` of the `opt.status` output that carries it; the end-to-end script of the optimization
campaign (`opt.start`, `opt.status`, `opt.archive`). This bead is the only place where the ledger record and the
saving are specified.

Out, and where it is: the race algorithm and its error guarantee, the round events (one per round: the round,
the survivors, the eliminated candidates with their mixture value and the threshold) and the per-generation
counts of evaluations started, completed and cancelled that this bead reads, and the verbs `opt.start` and
`opt.status` with their parameters and the rest of the status output (BD-341, on which this bead depends;
BD-262 for the paired comparison); the null-race control of the cascade (N28b-27); the archive (BD-342); the
optimizers (N28b-07); registered predictions as a mechanism (BD-344).

## Technical approach

**1. The record (`frob.race-ledger/1`, one per race, content-addressed, cited from the campaign's run row and
as an evidence item of the artifact graph).**

```json
{ "campaign": "…", "generation": 7, "stratum": "quad-mammal", "alpha": "1/20",
  "candidates": 27, "round_cap": 64,
  "planned_obs": 1728,            // candidates × round_cap: the no-stopping baseline
  "consumed_obs": 612,            // observations that entered an e-process
  "partial_obs_cost": { "gpu_s": 31.5 },   // work spent on episodes cancelled before they completed
  "memo_gpu_s_avoided": 0.0,      // recompute cost of memo-served observations, credited to the memo graph
  "saving_obs": "1728/612",       // exact ratio; printed as 2.82
  "saving_gpu_s": "…",            // baseline GPU-seconds ÷ (consumed + partial) GPU-seconds
  "rounds": [ { "round": 9, "survivors": 19, "eliminated": ["d:…", …] } ],
  "eliminations": [ { "design": "d:…", "round": 9, "mixture_e": "23.4", "threshold": "20" } ],
  "separation": { "top_gap_over_noise": 1.9, "median_gap_over_noise": 0.6 },
  "winner": "d:…", "stopped_by": "all-but-k eliminated | round cap | budget | cancelled",
  "prediction": { "id": "p:…", "saving_interval": ["2.0", "5.0"], "confidence": "0.8" } }
```

**2. Definitions.**
- `planned_obs` = N × round cap. It is fixed in the race's manifest before the first round.
- `consumed_obs` = Σ over candidates of the rounds each survived.
- `saving_obs` = planned ÷ consumed, an exact rational.
- `saving_gpu_s` = baseline GPU-seconds ÷ (completed GPU-seconds + partial GPU-seconds). The baseline is, per
  candidate, round cap × that candidate's mean cost per completed observation (observations a stopped
  candidate never ran have no measured cost, so its own mean stands in for them). Completed GPU-seconds are
  measured, except that a memo-served observation is charged its recorded recompute cost on both sides, so a
  memo hit changes neither saving; the seconds the memo graph actually avoided are reported apart as
  `memo_gpu_s_avoided` and credited to the memo graph, not to the race. Partial GPU-seconds are the work of
  episodes cancelled before they completed. `saving_gpu_s` is the number a speed claim may cite. Charging
  partial work can only lower it; with equal cost per observation across candidates it is at most
  `saving_obs`, but with unequal costs it may exceed `saving_obs` (an expensive candidate stopped early), and
  the record says which candidates' costs drove the difference.
- Separation (a description, class `Estimated`): for the final scores, the gap between the best and the
  second-best candidate, and between the best and the median, each divided by the per-pair noise standard
  deviation. It explains a saving; it does not justify one.

**3. Accounting rules.** Observations are counted by world address, in address order, never by completion
order, so a race replayed under a different schedule has an identical ledger apart from the measured
GPU-seconds (INV-ATEL-02: a study's statistic is a pure function of the per-address outcomes taken in address
order). An observation served from the memo graph is counted as consumed (it entered the e-process) and is
marked `memo`, with zero GPU-seconds; the baseline charges the same observation its recorded recompute cost,
so memo hits do not inflate the saving. Audit-lane designs inside a generation are excluded from the race and
from both sides of the ratio: they run at fixed n and are never cancelled.

**4. The registered prediction.** Before round 1 the studio registers its expected saving for the race as an
interval with a confidence (default: [2, 5] at 0.8 when the previous generation's separation was above 1,
[1, 1.5] otherwise; both DESIGN defaults recorded in the policy; the first generation of a campaign, with no
previous separation, uses [1, 1.5]). It is registered through BD-344's primitive under the studio predictor
`studio:saving` (the name N28c-14 fixes; N28c-14, which depends on this bead, adds that predictor's versions and
refits and reports it beside the studio's other predictors), with the race as its target and `saving_obs` as its
outcome. The prediction is hashed and frozen before the race starts and scored afterwards with the interval
score, like any registered prediction. The campaign report prints the calibration of these predictions.

**5. Campaign roll-up.** `opt.status` prints, with denominators: races run, total planned and consumed
observations, the pooled saving (a ratio of sums, not a mean of ratios), the share of races whose saving was
below 1.5, and the prediction coverage so far. These fields, with the identities of the ledger records, are the
optional block `race_ledger` of the output schema `frob.opt.status.out/1`; the verb and the rest of its output
are BD-341's, and the block is absent in a build without this bead. The roll-up is what the phase ledger of the
ten-fold test reads for the phase "simulation" of a campaign.

**6. Where it is written.** The race engine of BD-341 emits round events; `frob-opt::race::ledger`
(`crates/frob-opt/src/race/ledger.rs`; one layout for the crate, DESIGN, the same in BD-341 and N28b-07) folds
them into the record when the race ends or is cancelled (a cancelled race writes a ledger with
`stopped_by: cancelled` and what was consumed). The saving of this record is the one BD-341's background
describes as "the N·T evaluations of a non-racing generation divided by the evaluations used": planned ÷
consumed, with N the candidates of the generation (`--population`) and T the round cap (`rounds` of `--race`).
Determinism: every field except measured GPU-seconds is D0 given the outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every race of a campaign has a ledger record that resolves through `evidence.trace`.
- [ ] On the separated synthetic field the recorded `saving_obs` is at least 2; on the inseparable field it is
      below 1.5; both are asserted by the test and printed with their denominators.
- [ ] The partial cost of cancelled episodes is included in `saving_gpu_s`; on the equal-cost fixture
      `saving_gpu_s` is strictly below `saving_obs` whenever partial work was charged; memo hits change
      neither saving and are reported as `memo_gpu_s_avoided`.
- [ ] The ledger is identical (apart from measured seconds) under random preemption and reordering.
- [ ] Memo-served observations are marked and do not change the saving.
- [ ] No audit-lane design appears in any race's candidates.
- [ ] The saving prediction is registered before round 1 and scored after the race.
- [ ] `opt.status` prints the pooled saving as a ratio of sums with both sums.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/opt_race_campaign.sh` passes.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt --test race_ledger` (the real race engine on `synthetic` Gaussian fields):

- `separated_field_saving`: 27 candidates with means spaced 0.5 noise standard deviations apart, cap 64,
  α = 0.05: saving at least 2 (the measured value is recorded, not asserted beyond the floor).
- `inseparable_field_saving`: 27 identical candidates: saving below 1.5, and the winner is reported with
  `stopped_by: round cap`.
- `saving_definitions`: planned 1,728 and consumed 612 give the exact ratio 1728/612 = 48/17.
- `partial_cost_charged`: an episode cancelled at 40% of its length adds 0.4 of its GPU-seconds.
- `memo_hits_neutral`: replacing half the observations by memo hits leaves `saving_obs` and `saving_gpu_s`
  unchanged and moves their recompute cost into `memo_gpu_s_avoided`.
- `baseline_cost_per_candidate`: two candidates of costs 1 and 3 GPU-seconds per observation, cap 64, the
  expensive one stopped after 4 rounds and the cheap one run to the cap: baseline 256, completed 76,
  `saving_gpu_s` = 64/19 against `saving_obs` = 128/68 = 32/17.
- `ledger_schedule_invariant`: 100 random completion orders, identical records.
- `audit_designs_excluded`.
- `prediction_frozen_before_round_one`: a prediction registered after the first observation is refused.
- `pooled_saving_ratio_of_sums`: races (1728/612) and (1728/1700) pool to 3456/2312, not to the mean of the
  two ratios.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/opt_race_campaign.sh` sources the shared helper of STD-E2E and runs, on `batch-cpu` with
`--seed 5`: `frob opt start --design templates/diff-drive --levers wheel_radius,track,gear,mass_shift --method
cma-es --generations 3 --population 27 --race alpha=0.05,rounds=64 --task threshold-delivery --budget
gpu_s=1800 --seed 5 --sync-wait-ms 0 --key orc-1 --json` (returns a job at once, exit 8: the command line
waits to completion unless `--sync-wait-ms` bounds the wait, and the script needs the handle while the campaign
runs; then `frob job wait <job> --json` at the end); `frob opt status <campaign>
--json` during and after; `frob opt archive --campaign <campaign> --json` (the registry's spelling: no action
word); a kill of the worker during generation 2 and a restart; a second run with the same seed under a new key
`orc-2` (the same key would return the first run's stored receipt instead of running). Asserts: a
ledger per race with planned ≥ consumed; status progress with denominators; the archive non-empty and every
elite marked `provisional` where the rung is not `selective`; after the restart the same candidates and
e-values as an uninterrupted run; the second run's ledgers equal the first's apart from seconds. Log
`target/e2e-logs/opt_race_campaign/<utc>.ndjson` with transcript: commands, exit codes, wall time, evidence
blocks, per-race planned and consumed observations and savings, the optimizer state hash per generation,
prediction identifiers, digests of the two runs' ledgers, and on failure the first differing elimination.
Final `summary` line.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The sibling battery's figures (FACT): a saving of at least 2 asserted and 11.7 measured on its separated
  field; 1.03 on its inseparable field. Our separated and inseparable fixtures reproduce the two regimes.
- PO-ATEL-04 (in a race the true best is eliminated with probability at most α) is discharged by the race's
  own beads with a null race of 81 over 512 replays; this bead adds the ledger-side control that a null race's
  saving stays below 1.5.
- Negative controls: a ledger that counts observations in completion order (differs between two schedules and
  fails `ledger_schedule_invariant`); a ledger that omits partial costs (on the equal-cost fixture
  `saving_gpu_s` then equals `saving_obs` although partial work was done, which the acceptance check rejects,
  and `partial_cost_charged` fails); a ledger that charges memo hits zero seconds (`memo_hits_neutral` fails);
  a race that eliminates on a single pairwise crossing
  without the α/(N − 1) correction (the null field then loses its best more often than α; the cascade control
  catches it).
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts 1 and 8; `batch-cpu` and a GPU backend each
  against its class.

## Considerations

- The saving on robot populations is a HYPOTHESIS until campaigns have been recorded; no report may quote a
  typical saving before the ledger holds at least the campaigns of the surface A/B and the cascade gate.
- A saving near 1 is a result, not a failure of the race: it says the candidates are not separated at this
  rung, which is information the ladder's mode logic and the agent can use.
- The ledger is small (kilobytes per race) and is pinned with its campaign's evidence closure.
- If the sibling race is not admitted (OPEN-ATEL-3), the local race writes the same record.
