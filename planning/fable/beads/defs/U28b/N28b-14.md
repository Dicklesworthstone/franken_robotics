## Background

A cheap rung of the ladder scores a design with a shared policy; a dearer rung scores it with a policy
trained for it. Allocation needs to know how far the cheap score can be from the dear one, with a stated miss
rate and without assuming a model of the error. Split-conformal prediction gives exactly that from the audit
lane's designs, which ran both rungs and were drawn before any score existed (construction C-26). With n
audit designs, the residuals are rᵢ = |y_hi,i − ĝ(y_lo,i)|, where ĝ is a monotone map fitted on a disjoint part
of the audit set, and the margin q̂ is the ⌈(n + 1)(1 − α)⌉-th smallest residual. For a design exchangeable with
the audit set the residual is at most q̂ with probability at least 1 − α. Tier: Frontier (it is the heart of
the calibrated layer of the ladder; the fixed halving table is its fallback).

What the guarantee is, stated without inflation: coverage of at least 1 − α on average over calibration draws,
for designs exchangeable with the audit set. It moves from one calibration draw to another: with n = 39 the
coverage averages 0.95 and falls below 0.885 in one calibration draw in twenty (arithmetic from the Beta law
of split-conformal coverage). Two designs separated by more than twice the margin at the lower rung are
misordered at the higher rung with probability at most 2α. Top-k recall is *measured* by the audit lane and is
never claimed from the theory.

Rejected alternatives: a Gaussian model of the residual (the cheap rung is biased, not only noisy, and a
wrong model would understate the margin); calibrating on the designs that advanced (biased toward agreement;
the under-coverage is demonstrated in the tests); a learned correction of the fast score used as the screen
(it hides exactly the artifacts the trust ladder exists to expose).

## Scope

In: the estimator (ĝ, residuals, the index rule, q̂); the validity conditions; the split of the audit set; the
record a margin is stored as; identity scoping; the coverage law and its simulation; the misordering bound.

Out: the audit draw (N28b-13); the survivor set, trust check and truncation (N28b-15); the exchangeability
alarm (N28b-16); per-region margins (N28b-24); the compiled DC-03 table and mode automaton (BD-349, N28b-23).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs.** For one (rung pair, stratum, identity scope): the published audit pairs (y_lo,i, y_hi,i) with
their `audit-draw` references, in the order of the audit key h(d). Scores are on the task's unit scale (each
divided by the metric's declared span), so margins are comparable across tasks. Identity scope: the two
scorers' identities, kernel versions, effort profile and battery version. Evidence and calibration are never
pooled across model identities (INV-ATEL-05); a change of any of these resets the calibration, and a request
that mixes two scopes is refused with `FROB-REF-548` (pooling evidence or calibration across two model
identities; repair: restart the ledger rows in the new identity; exit 3).

**2. The split (keyed, score-independent; DESIGN).** Order the audit designs by h(d). When the audit set has
at least 38 designs, odd positions form the fit part F and even positions the calibration part C. When it has
fewer, ĝ is taken from the class-level calibration experiment for the same identity scope (its designs are
disjoint from the campaign's audit draws) and every audit design is in C; if no such ĝ exists, ĝ is the
identity on unit-scaled scores and the record says `g_hat: identity`. In every case F and C are disjoint.

**3. The monotone map.** ĝ is the isotonic regression of y_hi on y_lo over F (pool-adjacent-violators), made
a function by linear interpolation between the block means and constant extension beyond the extreme knots.
It is stored as its knot list. Monotonicity is what lets a margin on the mapped scale be applied to lower-rung
ranks.

**4. Residuals and the index rule.** For i ∈ C: rᵢ = |y_hi,i − ĝ(y_lo,i)|. With n = |C| and
k = ⌈(n + 1)(1 − α)⌉: if k > n the calibration is *invalid* (not enough points) and no margin is issued;
otherwise q̂ is the k-th smallest residual (ties resolved by position; the value is what matters). At α = 0.05
validity needs n ≥ 19: n = 19 gives k = 19; n = 18 gives k = 19 > 18. At n = 39, k = 38.

**5. The record (DESIGN, schema `frob.conformal-margin/1`).**
`{ rung_pair, stratum, scope, alpha, n, k, q_hat, g_hat: { kind: "isotonic" | "class-level" | "identity",
knots }, fit_n, audit_draws: [EvidenceId], generation, valid: bool, reason? }`. It is an evidence item; an
`AllocationDecision` that uses a margin cites it. A margin is never edited: a new generation writes a new
record.

**6. The coverage law (what PO-ATEL-03 checks).** For exchangeable residuals with no ties, the coverage of a
margin computed from n calibration points is distributed Beta(k, n + 1 − k) over calibration draws. Its mean
is k/(n + 1) ≥ 1 − α. At n = 39, α = 0.05: Beta(38, 2), mean 0.95, 5th percentile 0.884. The implementation
exposes `coverage_law(n, alpha) -> (mean, quantile(p))` in exact or high-precision arithmetic so reports can
print the spread next to every margin ("coverage 0.95 on average; below 0.885 in one draw in twenty").

**7. The misordering bound.** For designs a and b exchangeable with the audit set, each residual exceeds q̂
with probability at most α, so both are within q̂ with probability at least 1 − 2α; then
ĝ(y_lo,a) − ĝ(y_lo,b) > 2·q̂ implies y_hi,a > y_hi,b. The derivation is written in
`docs/derivations/po-atel-03.md` with the coverage law.

**8. What is refused.** A calibration set containing any design that is not in an `audit-draw` record for the
same rung pair, stratum and generation range, or any re-weighted residual: `FROB-REF-546` (calibration from
survivors, or an audit episode cancelled or re-weighted; repair: redraw the audit lane by key). A set that
mixes identity scopes: `FROB-REF-548`.

**9. Layout and determinism.** `crates/frob-opt/src/ladder/conformal.rs`. The estimator is a pure function of
the ordered audit pairs (D0 given the scores).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Index rule: n = 39, α = 0.05 selects the 38th smallest residual; n = 19 the 19th; n = 18 is invalid and
      issues no margin.
- [ ] PO-ATEL-03: over 10⁴ simulated calibration draws at n = 39 with exchangeable residuals, mean coverage is
      within 0.005 of 0.95 and the 5th percentile is within 0.01 of 0.884.
- [ ] Misordering: on a synthetic population, pairs separated by more than 2·q̂ on the mapped scale are
      misordered at the higher rung in at most 2α of cases, on average over calibration draws.
- [ ] The survivor-only negative control under-covers (mean coverage below 0.90 on the fixture) and the
      estimator refuses that input with `FROB-REF-546`.
- [ ] ĝ is monotone, fitted on a part disjoint from the calibration part, and stored as knots; with fewer
      than 38 audit designs the fallback order (class-level, identity) is followed and recorded.
- [ ] Mixed identity scopes are refused with `FROB-REF-548`.
- [ ] Every margin record cites its audit draws and resolves through `evidence.trace`.
- [ ] The derivation file exists and is cited by the proof obligation's record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt --test conformal_margin`:

- `index_rule_table`: (n, α) → k: (39, 0.05) → 38; (19, 0.05) → 19; (18, 0.05) → invalid; (99, 0.05) → 95;
  (20, 0.10) → 19.
- `margin_is_order_statistic`: residuals 0.001·i for i = 1…39 give q̂ = 0.038.
- `isotonic_fit_monotone`: on (y_lo, y_hi) = ((0.1, 0.2), (0.2, 0.15), (0.3, 0.4)) the fit pools the first two
  to 0.175 and is non-decreasing.
- `split_is_keyed_and_disjoint`: fit and calibration parts depend only on h(d) and share no design.
- `coverage_law_values`: Beta(38, 2): mean 0.95; quantile(0.05) = 0.8840 to four places.
- `coverage_simulation`: 10⁴ draws, exchangeable residuals; thresholds of the acceptance list.
- `survivor_calibration_undercovers` (`synthetic` fixture): y_lo uniform on [0, 1], y_hi = y_lo +
  0.05·(1.5 − y_lo)·z with z standard normal, population 600; calibrating on 39 audit designs covers about
  0.95 of the population; calibrating on 39 of the top third by y_lo covers about 0.83. The test asserts the
  first is at least 0.94 and the second at most 0.90 over 2,000 repetitions.
- `non_audit_design_refused_546`; `mixed_scope_refused_548`; `invalid_below_19`.
- `misordering_bound`: Monte Carlo on the fixture above with the audit calibration.
- `agrees_with_airlock_oracle` (only when the sibling is admitted): the same q̂ index as `fs-surrogate`'s
  split-conformal band on the residual list.

### End-to-end script and logging

Step `calibration` of `scripts/e2e/cascade_seeded_population.sh` (bead N28b-28): after the screening stage of
`frob opt cascade` on the seeded population, read the margin records with `frob evidence trace`; assert n, k
and q̂ against a recomputation from the published audit pairs by an independent script (plain sort and
index), the validity flags per stratum, and the printed coverage spread. Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit
codes, wall time, evidence blocks, n, k, q̂ with units (unit-scaled score), the coverage law's mean and 5th
percentile, and on failure the first stratum whose recomputed margin differs.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-696: conformal margin at n = 39, α = 0.05 is the 38th ordered residual (exact index). Negative control:
  calibration from survivors only.
- Worked example used downstream: q̂ = 0.04 with a third-best lower-rung score of 0.74 puts the cut at 0.66.
- Further arithmetic from the construction: fewer than 19 audit designs cannot support α = 0.05, because
  ⌈(n + 1)·0.95⌉ then exceeds n.
- Metamorphic: applying one strictly increasing map to all y_lo leaves q̂ unchanged when ĝ is refitted;
  permuting the calibration designs leaves q̂ unchanged.
- Determinism: bit-identical margins twice and across worker counts for the same score inputs.

## Considerations

- Exchangeability is an assumption about how audit designs relate to the designs the margin is applied to.
  It holds by construction for designs of the same generation and stratum (the audit set is a uniform sample
  of them). It fails when the optimizer moves on; the alarm bead exists for that, and a margin is always
  printed with the generation it was calibrated in.
- Conformal coverage moves by several points at twenty calibration designs; small strata get wide margins,
  which is the honest outcome, and the trust check then skips the rung.
- A margin is a statement about scores, not about success probability; it enters no verdict field.
- If the adaptive layer is killed, this estimator is still used to *measure* (margins are recorded in the
  rank-agreement ledger); it simply stops deciding who advances.
