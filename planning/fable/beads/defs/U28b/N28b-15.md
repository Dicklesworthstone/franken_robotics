## Background

At a rung transition the ladder must decide which designs go up. The fixed table (successive halving: the top
third by the lower rung's score) is the baseline and the fallback. The adaptive rule of the calibrated layer
uses the conformal margin instead: a rung cannot separate designs that are closer than twice its own error,
so everything inside that margin goes up, and only what is clearly below is discarded. When that leaves few
designs, the rung has done its job; when it leaves many, the rung is not separating the top band and should
not be trusted there. And when the population has shrunk to a set of finalists, allocation passes, one way,
to a different rule that ranks *experiments* by value of information. This bead is those three decisions as
runtime rules. Tier: Frontier (decision core DC-02, adaptive part). The asymmetry that governs all of them
(decision ADR-ATEL-03): eliminating the best design costs far more than a wasted higher-rung episode.

Rejected alternatives: a fixed top fraction sized from rank correlation alone (a rung with Kendall τ of 0.91
on the top ten can still rank the true best third, and a cut that keeps two designs discards the winner);
bandit allocation (regret is the wrong objective for identifying the best design); letting both the survivor
rule and the value-of-information rule issue requests for the same designs (two controllers on one quantity).

## Scope

In: the survivor rule; its combination with the containment rank; the trust check and its three responses;

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs at a transition (lo → hi) in a stratum.** The lower-rung scores of the population; the mode of
the rung pair in the stratum; a valid margin record (q̂, ĝ, α, n) or none; k, the number of finalists wanted;
the containment rank k_c(k) from the rank-agreement record where one exists; the remaining rung budget.

**2. The survivor rule (mode `selective`).** Work on the mapped scale s_i = ĝ(y_lo,i) (ĝ is monotone, so
ranks are those of the lower rung). Let s_(k) be the k-th best mapped score. The survivor set is

    V = { i : s_i ≥ s_(k) − 2·q̂ }.

The number advanced is the largest of three numbers: k; |V|; and k_c(k), the smallest retention at which, in
the calibration experiment and every audit draw to date for the class and task family, the higher rung's top
k all lay inside the lower rung's top k_c. When k_c(k) exceeds |V| the set is extended by lower-rung rank to
k_c(k). A selection whose retention is below k_c(k) is refused by the winner check with k_c(k) as the repair.
Audit-lane designs advance regardless and are not counted in these numbers.

**3. The trust check.** If |V| ≤ 3k the rung separates the top band and is trusted there: V advances. If
|V| > 3k, responses are tried in this order (DESIGN order; the plan lists the three):
1. *Buy more lower-rung episodes* for the designs in V and for the audit designs (the same number for both,
   so the audit set stays exchangeable with them), recalibrate, and re-apply the rule. Taken when the
   estimated cost fits the rung's remaining budget and the residual's sampling share (from the paired
   variance of lower-rung episodes) is large enough for doubling the episodes to bring |V| to 3k or less.
2. *Skip the rung for the top band:* all of V advances, and the decision records that the rung was not used
   to choose among them. Taken when the higher rung's cost for |V| designs fits the budget.
3. *Truncate* to 3k by lower-rung score and label the campaign `budget-truncated`. The label travels on every
   result of the campaign and is printed in its report; finalists are then "k survivors", not "the top k".
The second consecutive generation in which |V| > 3k is a fallback trigger of the decision core: the stratum
reverts to the fixed table.

**4. The fixed table, and the other modes.** Layer 1 of the allocation rule is successive halving with η = 3
inside each stratum: at each rung transition the top ⌈n/3⌉ designs by the lower rung's score advance, on a
schedule written to the ledger before the campaign starts (FACT: `fs-race::successive_halving` returns a
`BracketLedger`; its kills are rank-based and carry no e-process guarantee). The table is the baseline every
adaptive rule is measured against, on the same population and the same addresses, in GPU-seconds to the same
accepted decision and in audited top-k recall, and it is the fallback of every trigger in the chapter. The
seed bead for the ladder builds it; this rule calls it. `discard-only`: the fixed table decides (the top
⌈n/3⌉ advance, so the top quarter always does), with one protection: where a margin exists, a design within 2·q̂ of the k-th best is never eliminated.
`uninformative`: nothing is decided at this rung; it is skipped in the stratum and its budget moves up one
rung. A request that asks a rung to select in a stratum whose mode is not `selective` is refused with
`FROB-REF-545` (repairs: run the audit lane; or accept "k survivors"; exit 3). The check is N28b-23's, called
here, with the registry's division: 545 when a rank-agreement record exists and its mode is `discard-only` or
`uninformative`; a stratum with no record at all answers the learning chapter's `FROB-REF-537`, never 545.

**5. Worked case (the known answer).** n = 39 audit residuals, α = 0.05, q̂ = 0.04 on a unit-scaled score;
k = 3; the third-best lower-rung score is 0.74; the cut is 0.74 − 0.08 = 0.66; six designs lie at or above it;
3k = 9, so the rung is trusted and six advance. With q̂ = 0.10 the cut is 0.54, about twenty designs survive,
more than 9, and the trust check fails.

**6. The one-way handover.** When the survivors of the last population rung in a stratum number at most 64
(DESIGN: the budgeted mode of the value-of-information core, "64 candidates, closed form only"), the
controller writes a *handover marker*: `{ campaign, stratum, generation, designs, frontier }`. From that
record on:
- the finalist layer (experiments bought in order of value per cost until none clears the budget price) may
  issue requests for those designs;
- the population layer may not: any allocation request from the survivor rule or the fixed table that names
  a handed-over design is rejected inside the controller and counted as an interference violation
  (INV-ATEL-04: no design receives allocation requests from both the population layer and the finalist
  layer);
- the handover is never reversed within a campaign. A design re-opened by an audit after handover is scored
  through the audit lane, not by the population layer.
The finalist layer orders work only. Every verdict still comes from the promotion core and the comparison
rules; a value-of-information quantity never appears in a verdict field (INV-ATEL-12), and citing one as a
verdict is refused with `FROB-REF-872` (repair: run the comparison; exit 3). Both are the advisory type wall
of N28c-12, a dependency, and this bead adds no second check: every quantity the finalist layer hands on is an
`Advisory<T>`, which the verdict writer cannot take, the layer's tables are stored with result kind `advisory`,
and 872 is raised by `frob_verbs::require_establishable` when such an identifier is given to a parameter marked
`accepts = "verdict"`.

**7. The record.** Each transition writes an `AllocationDecision` (the plan's type sketch, fields normative):
`{ campaign, stratum, from, to, rule: FixedTable { eta } | ConformalSurvivors { alpha, q_hat, audit_n }, mode,
advanced, discarded, audit_lane, rank_record: Option<RankAgreementId>, policy: DecisionPolicyId, spend }`,
plus (DESIGN additions) `cut`, `survivors`, `containment_rank`, `trust: "trusted" | "bought-episodes" |
"skipped-top-band" | "budget-truncated"`, and, while the rule runs in shadow, `baseline_action` (what the
fixed table would have done). A rung without a rank record may not discard. The action is a pure function of
the table version, the ledger state at a pinned frontier and addresses; it reads no clock (INV-ATEL-06).

**8. Layout.** `crates/frob-opt/src/ladder/{survivors.rs, trust.rs, handover.rs}`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The worked case returns cut 0.66 and six survivors, `trusted`; with q̂ = 0.10 the trust check fails and
      the response order of step 3 is followed and recorded.
- [ ] The number advanced is max(k, |V|, k_c(k)); a retention below k_c(k) is refused with k_c(k) as repair.
- [ ] `budget-truncated` appears on every result and in the report of a truncated campaign.
- [ ] In `discard-only`, no design within 2·q̂ of the k-th best is eliminated; in `uninformative` nothing is
      eliminated and the budget moves up.
- [ ] `FROB-REF-545` fires when a rung is asked to select outside `selective`.
- [ ] After a handover marker, no population-layer request names a handed-over design in any replayed
      campaign of the test suite (INV-ATEL-04), and the marker is never reversed.
- [ ] No value-of-information quantity appears in a verdict field; `FROB-REF-872` fires on citation.
- [ ] Every transition writes an `AllocationDecision` with its policy identifier and, in shadow, the baseline
      action; the record resolves through `evidence.trace`.
- [ ] A planted over-scored design (lower-rung score inflated by 0.2) advances and is eliminated at the next
      rung, with its residual recorded as an exceedance.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt --test survivors`:

- `worked_cut_066`: scores with third-best 0.74, q̂ = 0.04, k = 3 → cut 0.66; the fixture's six designs at or
  above 0.66 advance.
- `margin_010_fails_trust`: same scores, q̂ = 0.10 → cut 0.54; 20 survivors > 9.
- `advance_is_max_of_three`: k = 3, |V| = 6, k_c = 8 → 8 advance; k_c = 4 → 6.
- `trust_response_order`: budget allows doubling episodes → response 1; not that but the higher rung for |V| →
  response 2; neither → truncation to 9 with the label.
- `second_generation_over_3k_triggers_fallback`.
- `discard_only_protects_margin_band`: 81 designs, top 27 advance by the table; a design ranked 30th within
  2·q̂ of the k-th best also advances.
- `uninformative_moves_budget_up`; `select_outside_selective_refused_545`.
- `handover_at_64`: 65 survivors → no marker; 64 → marker written.
- `no_dual_requests_after_handover`: property test over 1,000 random campaign traces (INV-ATEL-04).
- `voi_not_in_verdict_872`: a `synthetic` value-of-information table for the handed-over designs, built with
  N28c-12's `Advisory` constructors and stored with result kind `advisory` (the ranking itself is BD-351's and is
  not needed here): no `AllocationDecision` or verdict record of the trace holds an advisory value, and
  `require_establishable("result", <the table's id>)` returns `FROB-REF-872` with first repair "run the
  comparison".
- `audit_designs_not_counted`; `decision_reads_no_clock` (the function has no time argument; a lint forbids
  wall-clock reads in the module).
- `planted_overscored_caught_next_rung`.
- `agrees_with_pilot_reference`: on the pilot's frozen log (N28b-18), every transition decision of this rule
  (cut, |V|, trust response, advanced set) equals that of the pilot's reference implementation
  `chooser_replay/survivors_ref.rs` wherever the reference applies (no containment rank, no handover), so the
  pilot's KILL-LADDER evidence was produced by the rule that ships.

### End-to-end script and logging

Step `survivors` of `scripts/e2e/cascade_seeded_population.sh` (bead N28b-28): on the seeded population
(81 designs, three rungs, k = 3) run `frob opt cascade --json` with the adaptive layer in shadow and then as
default; assert per transition the cut, |V| against 3k, the trust response, the advanced set, the handover
marker when survivors reach 64 or fewer, and the absence of population-layer requests afterwards; then ask for
selection in a `discard-only` stratum (expect `FROB-REF-545`, exit 3). Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit
codes, wall time, evidence blocks, each `AllocationDecision` with its measured numbers and thresholds, both
actions in shadow, and on failure the first design whose advancement differs from the expectation.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-696 and the worked case: n = 39, α = 0.05, the 38th residual, say 0.04; third-best lower-rung score
  0.74; cut 0.66.
- The winner-check counterexample (forty designs; the cheap rung reproduces the true order except that the
  true best is ranked third; τ = 41/45 ≈ 0.911 on the true top ten; a cut that keeps two designs discards the
  winner): with k = 1 the containment rank is 3 and this rule advances at least 3.
- Negative controls: a rule that advances only the top k (loses the planted over-scored design's rival and
  fails the recall assertion of the gate bead); a controller that lets the fixed table re-rank handed-over
  finalists (caught by the INV-ATEL-04 property test).
- Determinism: decisions identical twice, across worker counts, and under random preemption and reordering of
  episode completion (the inputs are per-address outcomes in address order).

## Considerations

- KILL-LADDER (decided at M4): if the adaptive layer does not reach halving's audited top-k recall at fewer
  GPU-seconds after one repair cycle, this rule stays in the code as a shadow measurement and the fixed table
  allocates.
- The factor 2 and the bound 3k are the plan's; the response order of the trust check and the 64-design
  handover are DESIGN defaults recorded in the decision policy. Changing one is a new policy version.
- A wide margin is information: it says the cheap rung cannot tell the top designs apart. The report prints
  the cut, the band size and the response so that an agent sees why many designs were trained.
- The share of a population that must leave the first rung is expected to be about the top quarter, not the
  top twentieth (HYPOTHESIS inferred from published neighbours; settled by the rank-agreement gate).
