## Background

The conformal margin of a rung pair is valid for designs that are exchangeable with the audit set: roughly,
designs that could have been drawn in any order from the same population. An optimizer does not stand still.
As a campaign moves toward a new region of design space (longer legs, heavier payloads, a different gait), the
cheap rung's error there may be larger than where it was calibrated, and a margin computed earlier then
covers less than it promises. The plan reconciles conformal exchangeability with drift by construction: an
alarm ends the regime and calibration restarts inside the new one. The alarm is a test martingale on the
audit designs' conformal p-values. At a value of 20, which is 1/α at α = 0.05, the stratum's calibration is
void, the stratum reverts to the fixed table, and calibration restarts inside the new regime. Tier: Frontier.

Why a test martingale: it is valid at every moment without a planned sample size (by Ville's inequality a
nonnegative martingale started at 1 exceeds 1/α with probability at most α, ever), so the alarm can be checked
after every audit design without inflating its false-alarm rate; and it needs no model of the drift.

Rejected alternatives (the plan's own list for the same decision in another core): kernel two-sample and
optimal-transport drift tests (no power at twenty scalar residuals); a fixed re-calibration schedule (either
too slow to catch a drift or wasteful when there is none); comparing recent and old mean residuals with a
t-test at each generation (repeated testing with no error control).

## Scope

In: the sequence order of audit residuals; conformal p-values with keyed tie-breaking; the martingale; the
alarm threshold and what happens at it; the restart; the record; the measured false-alarm rate and detection
delay.

Out: the margin and the residuals themselves (N28b-14); mode transitions and hysteresis after a restart
(N28b-23); the fixed table (N28a-33); the assembly of layer 2 (BD-329); the reality band's use of the same
alarm.

## Technical approach

**1. The sequence.** For one (rung pair, stratum, identity scope), audit residuals are ordered by
(generation, h(d)), with h the audit key. The order is fixed by identities before any score exists, so it
cannot be chosen to hide or to manufacture an alarm. Residuals r₁, r₂, … are those of the calibration record
of each generation, computed with the ĝ in force when the design was scored.

**2. Conformal p-values.** For the t-th residual,

    p_t = ( #{ j ≤ t : r_j > r_t } + θ_t · #{ j ≤ t : r_j = r_t } ) / t ,

with θ_t a uniform draw on [0, 1) from the addressed stream keyed by (campaign seed, stratum, rung pair, t,
"conformal-tiebreak"). If the residuals are exchangeable the p_t are independent and uniform on [0, 1]. A
drift toward larger residuals makes recent p-values small. The stream is a row of the one stream registry
`registries/streams.toml`, which holds no such row among its first 23, so this bead appends it as the registry's
rule for later rows requires (N35-23: appended by the owner, next free identifier, never a literal identifier
in source): name `conformal-tiebreak`, domain `engine`, entity the hash of campaign, stratum and rung pair,
tick the residual index t, component `part`, owner `frob-opt`, status `active`. Appending a row changes no draw
of an existing stream, and the draw is read through the generated constant.

**3. The martingale (DESIGN).** A mixture of power martingales over a fixed grid:

    M_t = (1/9) · Σ_{ε ∈ {0.1, 0.2, …, 0.9}}  Π_{s ≤ t}  ε · p_s^(ε − 1) .

Each factor ε·p^(ε − 1) has expectation 1 under a uniform p, so each product is a nonnegative martingale
started at 1, and so is their average. M grows when p-values concentrate near 0 (residuals growing), which is
the direction that breaks coverage; p-values concentrating near 1 (residuals shrinking) make the margin
conservative and need no alarm. M is computed in logarithms in `f64` (log-sum-exp over the nine components);
p-values are floored at 2⁻⁵² so a zero cannot produce an infinity. The value is stored after every audit
design with the index t.

**4. The alarm.** When ln M_t ≥ ln 20: the stratum's calibration is *void*. In the same ledger transaction:
(a) a `calibration-void` evidence record is written (rung pair, stratum, scope, generation, t, M_t, the
margin record it voids); (b) the stratum reverts to layer 1 (the fixed halving table; the mode becomes
`discard-only` as the decision core's fallback); (c) calibration restarts inside the new regime: the residual
sequence, ĝ's fit set and the martingale are reset, and only audit designs of the next generation onward
enter the new calibration. A voided margin is never used again, and designs already advanced under it are
not recalled (they went up, which is the safe direction); designs *discarded* under it in the generation that
raised the alarm are re-opened through the audit lane.

**5. Error rate and power (TARGET figures from a simulation of this construction; re-measured by the tests).**
Under exchangeability the alarm fires at any time in a campaign with probability at most 1/20 by Ville's
inequality (the simulation gives about 0.02 over 400 audit designs). When the residual scale triples after 40
audit designs, the alarm fires within 120 further audit designs in about 97% of runs, with a median delay of
about 26 designs; when it doubles, in about 70%. A doubling that is missed leaves a margin too small by the
same factor for some generations; the audited miss rate (a separate fallback trigger) is the second line of
defence.

**6. Interaction rules.** The alarm reads only audit-lane residuals, never survivors' (which were selected by
score). It changes state only at a generation boundary as far as *modes* are concerned (the mode automaton
applies hysteresis), but the void itself is recorded at once so that no further decision in the current
generation uses the margin: transitions still pending in that generation use the fixed table. A change of
identity scope (new policy version, kernel version, effort profile, battery version) resets the sequence
without an alarm, because evidence is never pooled across identities.

**7. Layout and determinism.** `crates/frob-opt/src/ladder/exchangeability.rs`: a pure function from the
ordered residuals and the tie-break stream to (p_t, ln M_t). Determinism D0 given the residuals; the logarithm
is evaluated with the workspace's deterministic math routines so the alarm index is the same on every
platform, and the test suite pins the alarm index of fixed sequences.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Null calibration: over 10⁴ seeded sequences of 400 exchangeable residuals the alarm fires in at most 5%
      (inside the exact binomial acceptance bound for 0.05).
- [ ] Synthetic drift trips the alarm: with the residual scale tripled after 40 audit designs, the alarm
      fires within 120 further designs in at least 90% of 1,000 seeded runs.
- [ ] At the alarm the margin is voided, the stratum uses the fixed table for every later transition, the
      residual sequence and martingale restart, and discards of the alarm's generation are re-opened.
- [ ] A voided margin record is never cited by a later allocation decision: after the alarm the module's
      query for the margin in force returns `void` for the stratum until a new valid calibration exists (the
      property this bead tests; the campaign-level check on real allocation decisions is the `drift` step of
      N28b-28, which runs after the ladder beads of M4).
- [ ] The p-value order is by (generation, audit key); permuting arrival or completion order leaves every p_t
      and M_t unchanged.
- [ ] A change of identity scope resets the sequence and writes no `calibration-void` record.
- [ ] The alarm index of three pinned sequences is identical on macOS and Linux hosts, debug and release.
- [ ] Each alarm writes its `calibration-void` evidence record, which resolves through `evidence.trace` and is
      the record the campaign report's list of fallbacks that fired is built from (the report sections are
      N28c-19's, M4; that the report lists the alarm is asserted by the `drift` step of N28b-28, not here).
- [ ] `registries/streams.toml` holds the row `conformal-tiebreak`, the stream registry's loader accepts it,
      and the pinned vectors of the existing streams are unchanged.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt --test exchangeability_alarm`:

- `p_value_definition`: residuals (0.3, 0.1, 0.5) give p = (θ₁·1/1, (1 + θ₂)/2, θ₃/3); with the pinned
  tie-break stream the three values are asserted exactly.
- `martingale_starts_at_one_and_factor_mean_is_one`: numerical integral of ε·p^(ε − 1) over [0, 1] equals 1
  for each ε of the grid (closed form: 1).
- `null_false_alarm_rate`: 10⁴ sequences × 400 points → frequency ≤ the 0.99 quantile of
  Binomial(10⁴, 0.05)/10⁴ = 0.0551.
- `drift_detection`: scale × 3 after point 40 → detection within 120 points in ≥ 90% of 1,000 runs; the median
  delay is recorded.
- `shrinking_residuals_do_not_alarm`: scale × 1/3 after point 40 → alarm frequency no higher than the null's.
- `alarm_voids_and_restarts`: after the alarm, the next margin is computed only from later generations and
  needs at least 19 points to be valid.
- `pending_transitions_use_fixed_table`; `discards_of_alarm_generation_reopened`.
- `scope_change_resets_silently`.
- `order_invariance`: shuffled completion orders, identical (p_t, ln M_t).
- `floor_prevents_infinity`: a p-value of exactly 0 from the formula is floored and ln M stays finite.
- `survivor_residuals_refused_546`: a residual without an `audit-draw` citation is refused.
- `drift_below_survivors_caught_by_audit_only`: the lower-half drift fixture of the negative controls; the
  audit-fed alarm meets the detection threshold and the survivor-fed mutant does not.

### End-to-end script and logging

Step `drift` of `scripts/e2e/cascade_seeded_population.sh` (bead N28b-28): run `frob opt cascade` on the
seeded population with its planted drift (from generation 4 the generator moves to longer limbs, where the
synthetic R1 scorer's error is three times larger); assert that a `calibration-void` record appears for the
affected stratum, that later transitions in it cite the fixed table, that the report lists the fallback, and
that the clean stratum raises no alarm. Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, wall time, evidence blocks,
ln M per audit design against ln 20 = 2.9957, the alarm's generation and index, and on failure the first
decision after the alarm that still cites the voided margin.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The threshold: 20 = 1/α at α = 0.05; ln 20 = 2.9957.
- The construction's test (C-26): synthetic drift trips the exchangeability alarm; the gate that measures the
  adaptive layer against the fixed table requires the same on a campaign.
- Negative controls: a martingale fed survivors' residuals instead of audit residuals. The module refuses
  such input (every residual must cite an `audit-draw` record; otherwise `FROB-REF-546`), and the reason is
  shown on a fixture: from generation 4 the rung's error triples only among designs in the lower half of the
  lower-rung scores, where survivors never lie and where a discarded winner would hide; the audit-fed alarm
  fires within 120 designs in at least 90% of runs, while the survivor-fed mutant stays at the null rate. On a
  stationary population neither alarms beyond the null bound, so the survivor mutant is not caught by false
  alarms but by missed drift. An alarm that resets ln M to 0 without voiding the margin (the "never cited
  again" assertion fails).
- Metamorphic: multiplying every residual by a positive constant leaves every p_t and M_t unchanged.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; across the two host platforms.

## Considerations

- The grid of ε and the mixture are DESIGN choices recorded in the decision policy; changing them is a new
  policy version and re-runs the calibration tests.
- The alarm has limited power against a slow drift (a doubling is caught only about seven times in ten at
  these sizes). It is one of several triggers; the audited miss rate and the 3k trust check also end adaptive
  allocation in a stratum.
- A stratum that alarms repeatedly never accumulates two consecutive valid generations and so stays on the
  fixed table: the system degrades to its baseline, which costs compute and never validity.
- The same alarm guards the reality band of the calibration subsystem; a shared module avoids two
  implementations of one inequality.
