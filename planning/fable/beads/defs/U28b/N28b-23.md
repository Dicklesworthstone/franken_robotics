## Background

Decision ADR-ATEL-04: a cheap rung discards; it does not select. A rung may choose finalists only in the
strata where the rank-agreement ledger has measured that it can, because no published measurement of rank
agreement between a shared policy and trained specialists exists and the nearest neighbours are weak. The
ledger's measurements therefore need consequences that are mechanical, fixed before data, and impossible to
argue with after the fact. Each rung pair in each stratum is in one of three modes. The thresholds 0.5 and
0.7 are the kill thresholds of the calibration experiment (the same numbers the fidelity, control and
learning chapters use for their rank checks), fixed before data in the pre-registration. All conditions are
on the *lower bootstrap bound*.

| Mode | Condition | What the lower rung may do |
|---|---|---|
| `selective` | τ on the top band ≥ 0.7, with the winner check passed; calibration valid (n ≥ 19, martingale below 20); audited miss rate at most α | The conformal survivor rule |
| `discard-only` | 0.5 ≤ τ < 0.7; or a fallback trigger of the allocation cores fired | The fixed table: the top third advances, so the top quarter always does. Where a margin exists, a design within 2·q̂ of the k-th best is never eliminated. The audit lane keeps running |
| `uninformative` | τ < 0.5; or the stratum is unmeasured; or the planted winner was lost in this stratum | Nothing. The rung is skipped in this stratum, its budget moves up one rung, and designs it eliminated earlier in the campaign are re-opened through the audit lane |

An unmeasured stratum is `uninformative`: the default fails closed. This bead turns the table into an
automaton with hysteresis (decision core DC-03's "mode automaton": a mode changes only at a generation
boundary, and a stratum needs two consecutive generations of valid calibration to become `selective`), fixes
what reports may say, and wires the program-level kill switch. Tier: Frontier.

Decision ADR-ATEL-06 applies: every adaptive decision is compiled ahead of time into a table or automaton
with a version; the runtime evaluates tables; no decision reads the wall clock, device load or arrival
order. Rejected alternatives: modes recomputed after every audit design (a rung would flap between rules
inside one generation, and a replay under a different schedule would allocate differently); thresholds on
the point estimate (with forty designs the interval is wide, and a point estimate of 0.7 is compatible with
a rung that is close to useless).

## Scope

In: the automaton (state, inputs, transition function, hysteresis); its evaluation at generation boundaries;
behaviour inside a generation when a margin is voided; the fallback triggers it reads; refusal
`FROB-REF-545`; the wording rule; the KILL-RANK switch; re-opening on a downgrade to `uninformative`; state
storage and evidence records.

Out: the ledger rows (N28b-22); the alarm (N28b-16); the survivor rule (N28b-15); the trust rule that sets
the audit flags (BD-345); compilation to the registry artifact (BD-349); the calibration experiment and
GATE-ATEL-07's measurement (BD-328).

## Technical approach

**1. State.** Per (rung pair, stratum, identity scope): `mode ∈ {uninformative, discard-only, selective}` and
`valid_generations: u8` (consecutive generations in which every `selective` condition held). Initial state:
`uninformative`, 0. The state is stored in the ledger with the generation at which it was last evaluated.

**2. Inputs at the boundary after generation g** (all read at one pinned ledger frontier): the latest
rank-agreement row version for the key (τ lower bound L, or `insufficient`, or no row); `calibration_valid`
(at least 19 residuals and no `calibration-void` record in g); `miss_rate_ok` (the audited miss rate's bound
at most α); `winner_check_passed`; `triggers` (the set of fallback triggers that fired during g: fewer than
19 calibration points; martingale at or above 20; audited miss rate above α; survivors above 3k for two
generations; a change of identity or scope); `plant_lost` and `audit_missing` for the stratum (from the
screening audit); and `kill_rank` (the program ruling).

**3. Transition function (first row that applies).**

| # | Condition | Next mode | `valid_generations` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `kill_rank` and the lower rung is the shared-policy rung | `uninformative` | 0 |
| 2 | No row, or τ `insufficient`, or L < 0.5, or `plant_lost`, or `audit_missing` | `uninformative` | 0 |
| 3 | L < 0.7, or `triggers` non-empty, or not `calibration_valid`, or not `miss_rate_ok`, or not `winner_check_passed` | `discard-only` | 0 |
| 4 | Otherwise, and `valid_generations` + 1 ≥ 2 | `selective` | incremented (saturating) |
| 5 | Otherwise | `discard-only` | incremented |

So an upgrade to `selective` needs two consecutive clean generations; a downgrade is immediate at the next
boundary. A stratum set to `uninformative` because its planted winner was lost stays there for the rest of
the campaign (the stage is bypassed until it is re-qualified by a later campaign's passed audit).

**4. Inside a generation.** The mode does not change inside a generation (the automaton's obligation). Two
things can still happen at once and are not mode changes: a voided margin (the alarm) makes the survivor rule
unavailable, so pending transitions in a `selective` stratum use the fixed table for the rest of the
generation; and a change of identity scope makes the key itself new, which reads as unmeasured.

**5. On a downgrade to `uninformative`.** The rung is skipped in the stratum from the next transition on; its
unspent budget is added to the next rung's; the designs it eliminated earlier in the campaign in that stratum
are re-opened through the audit lane (scored at the higher rung at the fixed n, in audit-key order).

**6. Refusal.** A request that asks a rung to *select* in a stratum whose mode is not `selective` (for
example `opt.cascade --finalists-from R1`) is refused with `FROB-REF-545` (repairs, in order: run the audit
lane, that is, let calibration accumulate; or accept "k survivors"; exit 3). A request to select or discard
with no record at all is the owner's `FROB-REF-537`.

**7. The wording rule.** Every campaign report prints the mode of every rung and stratum. Finalists chosen
under any mode other than `selective` are described as "k survivors", never as "the top k". The rule is
enforced where results are typed: a result set carries `selection_basis: "top-k" | "survivors"`, computed
from the modes of every rung that reduced it, and the report generator has no other source for the phrase.

**8. KILL-RANK.** If GATE-ATEL-07 fails for every morphology class tested, the program ruling sets
`kill_rank`. Then the shared-policy rung is `uninformative` everywhere, every candidate that survives static
analysis is scored on the higher rung, the plan's benefit claim for the ladder shrinks to stopping and
elimination among trained candidates, and the ten-fold record states which factor of the thesis was lost.
The switch is a field of the registry record of the core; flipping it is a new decision-policy version.

**9. Records.** Each evaluation writes a `core_decision` record: key, generation, the inputs' identifiers
(row version, margin record, audit record), the previous and next state, the table version, the frontier,
and the decision-policy identifier. The action is a pure function of the table version, the ledger state at
the pinned frontier and addresses; it reads no clock (INV-ATEL-06).

**10. Layout.** `crates/frob-opt/src/ladder/modes.rs`; the transition table is data
(`registries/cores/DC-03.modes.toml`, DESIGN name) loaded and hash-checked at start.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The transition function matches the table on an exhaustive enumeration of its boolean inputs and the
      three τ bands (all combinations, generated).
- [ ] Two consecutive clean generations are required for `selective`; one dirty generation returns the
      stratum to `discard-only` or `uninformative` at the next boundary; no mode changes inside a generation
      in any replayed campaign of the suite.
- [ ] An unmeasured stratum is `uninformative`; a lost planted winner makes its stratum `uninformative` for
      the rest of the campaign and re-opens that rung's discards.
- [ ] A voided margin inside a generation switches pending transitions to the fixed table without changing
      the stored mode.
- [ ] `FROB-REF-545` fires for a selecting request outside `selective`.
- [ ] `selection_basis` is `survivors` whenever any reducing rung was not `selective`, and reports print
      "k survivors" in that case and the mode table in every case.
- [ ] With `kill_rank` set, every shared-policy rung is `uninformative` and the estimate costs every design
      at the higher rung.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-07's consequence is applied from recorded rows: on planted populations with known τ on each
      side of 0.7 and 0.5 the mode assigned is the one the thresholds prescribe.
- [ ] Every evaluation writes its `core_decision` record; the module contains no clock read (lint).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt --test rung_modes`:

- `exhaustive_transition_table`: 3 τ bands × 2⁷ flag combinations × 3 prior modes × 2 counter values.
- `two_clean_generations`: inputs clean at g = 1, 2 → modes (`discard-only`, `selective`).
- `dirty_generation_resets`: clean, clean, trigger, clean, clean → (D, S, D, D, S).
- `lower_bound_not_point`: point τ = 0.74 with lower bound 0.61 → `discard-only`.
- `band_edges`: L = 0.70 → eligible for `selective`; L = 0.6999 → `discard-only`; L = 0.50 → `discard-only`;
  L = 0.4999 → `uninformative`.
- `unmeasured_is_uninformative`; `insufficient_tau_is_uninformative`.
- `plant_lost_sticks_for_campaign`; `uninformative_reopens_discards`; `budget_moves_up`.
- `void_inside_generation_uses_table_keeps_mode`.
- `select_outside_selective_refused_545`.
- `selection_basis_from_modes`: rungs (S, S) → `top-k`; (S, D) → `survivors`.
- `kill_rank_forces_uninformative_r1_only` (the fine-tune rung pair keeps its own mode).
- `no_clock_dependency`: evaluating at different wall times and under shuffled completion orders gives the
  same state.

### End-to-end script and logging

Step `modes` of `scripts/e2e/cascade_seeded_population.sh` (bead N28b-28): three seeded populations whose
synthetic first-rung scorer is built to have τ on the top band of about 0.85, 0.6 and 0.3 against the
specialist rung; `frob opt cascade` on each for three generations; asserts the mode sequence per stratum
(for example D, S, S for the first), the refusal 545 on a forced selection in the second, the skipped rung
and re-opened discards in the third, and the report's wording. Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, wall
time, evidence blocks, per generation the row version, L against 0.7 and 0.5, the flags, the state before and
after, and on failure the first generation whose mode differs from the transition table.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The Gauntlet's check of this ledger: planted populations with known top-weighted rank correlation on each
  side of each threshold in force (0.7 and 0.5), and a planted winner the cheap rung represents poorly; the
  measured τ covers the planted value in its bootstrap interval; the mode assigned is the one the thresholds
  prescribe; a lost planted winner stops the rung from discarding in that stratum.
- Negative controls: an automaton without hysteresis (it enters `selective` after one clean generation; the
  `two_clean_generations` test fails); a report generator that prints "top 3" from a `discard-only` rung
  (rejected, because the phrase is derived from `selection_basis`); a threshold changed after the first
  result (refused by the study record of the pre-registration).
- Determinism: the state sequence is identical twice, across worker counts and under random preemption.

## Considerations

- The verification chapter's summary row for this ledger says an unmeasured stratum is `discard-only`, while
  the chapter that owns the modes says `uninformative` and calls it the fail-closed default. This bead
  follows the owning chapter: with no record a rung may not discard. The conformance check must be written
  to the same reading.
- Hysteresis costs one generation of adaptive allocation per stratum; the alternative costs trust in a rule
  that flaps.
- RISK-27 (rank agreement between rungs is too low for cheap rungs to eliminate designs; high; owner Atelier
  lead): this automaton is the mechanism by which that risk degrades the system gracefully to
  `discard-only` and `uninformative` instead of silently mis-selecting.
- OPEN-ATEL-2 (stratum definition) changes how many automata exist, not how one behaves.
