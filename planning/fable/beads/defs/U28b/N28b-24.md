## Background

Cheap rungs are biased as well as noisy. A population scored under one shared controller drifts toward designs
that controller steers easily, and early evaluation underestimates slow learners. A single margin for a whole
stratum treats that bias as if it were noise: it is too wide where the shared policy works well and too
narrow exactly where it fails, for example on bodies that are hard to steer with a shared controller and
would be good with a trained one. Those are the designs a screen throws away by mistake.

The rank-agreement section gives low agreement four consequences. Two are carried by the mode automaton (the
report says so; the program is told through KILL-RANK). This bead carries the other two:

- **Bias is handled by region.** A region where the cheap rung systematically under-scores gets its own
  margin by per-bucket calibration; a bucket below the calibration floor refuses instead of extrapolating
  (FACT: `fs-eproc::hardening::MondrianConformal` implements this refusal).
- **The tier correction switches off.** Where ρ_ep, the per-episode correlation between the fast tier and
  the referee on paired addresses, is below 0.7 on at least 200 paired addresses, the tier-correction
  estimator is disabled for that stratum and promotion uses referee episodes alone.

Tier: Frontier. Rejected alternatives: a learned correction of the cheap score by region (it hides exactly
the artifacts the trust ladder exists to expose); widening the single margin until it covers the worst
region (the rung then separates nothing anywhere); extrapolating a margin to a bucket with three audit
designs (the guarantee does not exist there, and a number would be read as if it did).

## Scope

In: regions and how they are fixed; the signed residual and the per-region bias measure in the ledger row;
per-bucket margins and their refusal; how the survivor rule uses them; the ρ_ep switch for the tier
correction and what promotion does when it is off.

Out: the stratum-level margin (N28b-14); the survivor rule's other parts (N28b-15); the tier-correction
estimator (BD-264) and promotion (BD-263); controller-budget curves; modes (N28b-23).

## Technical approach

**1. Regions (buckets).** A region is a block of the stratum's design space defined in the campaign manifest
before any design is scored: by default the blocks of the quality-diversity archive's descriptor grid (mass,
speed, energy per metre, footprint) coarsened to at most eight blocks per stratum (DESIGN), or any partition
declared on design fields (leg count, limb-length ratio, payload class). Membership is a function of the
design alone, never of a score, so each bucket's audit designs are a uniform sample of that bucket's
population and the conformal guarantee holds within the bucket.

**2. The bias measure.** For an audit design i with mapped lower score ĝ(y_lo,i): the *signed* residual
e_i = y_hi,i − ĝ(y_lo,i). Positive means the cheap rung under-scored the design. The ledger row stores, per
region: the count, the mean of e and a bootstrap 95% interval (designs resampled within the region, addressed
stream). A region is flagged `under-scored` when the interval's lower end is above zero and `over-scored`
when its upper end is below zero. The flags are descriptions (class `Estimated`); the margins below are what
act.

**3. Per-bucket margins.** For bucket b with n_b calibration residuals r = |e|:
q̂_b = the ⌈(n_b + 1)(1 − α)⌉-th smallest, valid only if that index is at most n_b (n_b ≥ 19 at α = 0.05). A
bucket below the floor has *no margin*: the estimator returns a typed refusal for that bucket (it does not
borrow the stratum's margin and does not extrapolate from neighbours). Per-bucket coverage then holds for
designs exchangeable with their own bucket's audit designs.

**4. Use in the survivor rule.** With per-bucket margins in force (a stratum is switched to them when any
region is flagged `under-scored` and at least two buckets are valid; DESIGN rule), design i of bucket b(i)
advances when

    s_i ≥ s_(k) − ( q̂_b(i) + q̂_b(k) ),

where s is the mapped lower score and b(k) is the bucket of the k-th best design. With one common margin this
is the 2·q̂ rule. The reasoning is the same: each of the two designs is within its own bucket's margin of its
higher-rung score with probability at least 1 − α, so a pair separated by more than the sum of the two
margins is misordered with probability at most 2α. A design in a bucket with no margin is never eliminated
by the rung: it advances, and the decision records `bucket-unqualified`. The trust check (at most 3k
survivors) is applied to the resulting set as usual; unqualified buckets count toward it, which is how a
population dominated by unmeasured regions ends on the fixed table.

**5. Record.** The margin record gains `buckets: [ { region, n, k, q_hat | refused: "below-floor",
mean_signed, interval, flag } ]`; an allocation decision made with per-bucket margins cites it and lists the
bucket of every design at the cut.

**6. The tier-correction switch.** The specialist → referee row of the ledger carries ρ_ep, the correlation
of per-episode outcomes between the fast tier and the referee at the same world addresses, with the number
of paired addresses. Rule, per stratum and identity scope:

| ρ_ep evidence | Tier-correction estimator | Promotion uses |
|---|---|---|
| At least 200 paired addresses and ρ_ep ≥ 0.7 (lower bootstrap bound) | Enabled | The corrected estimate, with the referee audit sample |
| At least 200 paired addresses and ρ_ep < 0.7 | Disabled for the stratum | Referee episodes alone |
| Fewer than 200 paired addresses | Not enabled (insufficient) | Referee episodes alone |

A request for a tier-corrected estimate where the switch is off is refused by the estimator's owner with
`FROB-REF-811` (a tier-corrected estimate across identities, with no audited address, or below the
effective-sample floor; repair: referee episodes alone). The reason for the threshold is arithmetic: pairing
with the fast tier scales the variance of the referee estimate by 1 − ρ², a saving of 1.96 times at ρ = 0.7,
5.3 at 0.9 and 10 at 0.95; below 0.7 the saving is under two and the estimator's assumptions are the larger
risk. The switch state is written to the ledger at each generation boundary and read at a pinned frontier.

**7. Layout and determinism.** `crates/frob-opt/src/ladder/{regions.rs, mondrian.rs, tier_switch.rs}`. All
three are pure functions of ledger rows at a frontier (D0 given the scores).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Regions are fixed in the manifest before scoring; a region defined on a score is rejected.
- [ ] The ledger row holds the mean signed residual per region with its interval and flag.
- [ ] A bucket with fewer than 19 calibration residuals returns a refusal, not a margin; its designs are
      never eliminated by the rung and are recorded `bucket-unqualified`.
- [ ] On the planted-bias population (below) the single-margin rule discards the planted good design in the
      under-scored region and the per-bucket rule advances it.
- [ ] Per-bucket held-out coverage is at least 1 − α − 0.03 in every valid bucket of the planted population.
- [ ] The tier-correction switch follows the table: off below 0.7, off below 200 paired addresses, on
      otherwise; with it off, promotion's evidence record shows referee episodes only.
- [ ] The local estimator and the sibling's (when admitted) agree on margins and on the refusal.
- [ ] Every decision that used per-bucket margins cites the bucket record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt --test region_bias`:

- `signed_residual_flag`: a region with e = (+0.08, +0.11, +0.06, +0.09, +0.10, +0.07, +0.12, +0.08) is
  flagged `under-scored`; a region with mixed signs and mean near zero is not flagged.
- `bucket_floor_refuses`: n_b = 18 → refusal `below-floor`; n_b = 19 → the 19th residual.
- `sum_of_margins_rule`: s_(k) = 0.74 in a bucket with q̂ = 0.03; a design at 0.62 in a bucket with q̂ = 0.10
  advances (0.62 ≥ 0.74 − 0.13 = 0.61); the same design under a common margin of 0.04 does not
  (0.62 < 0.66).
- `common_margin_reduces_to_2q`.
- `unqualified_bucket_never_eliminated`.
- `planted_bias_population` (`synthetic`): two regions of 60 designs; in region B the lower rung under-scores
  by 0.10 on average; a design in B with true higher-rung rank 2 has lower-rung rank 15; the single-margin
  rule (q̂ ≈ 0.05) discards it, the per-bucket rule advances it.
- `region_on_score_rejected`.
- `tier_switch_table`: (ρ_ep lower bound, addresses) = (0.75, 256) → on; (0.65, 256) → off; (0.80, 150) →
  off (insufficient).
- `pairing_factor`: 1/(1 − 0.7²) = 1.96.

### End-to-end script and logging

Step `region_bias` of `scripts/e2e/cascade_seeded_population.sh` (bead N28b-28): on the seeded population
with a planted hard-to-steer region, `frob opt cascade` with the stratum-level margin and again with
per-bucket margins; asserts the flag on the planted region, the bucket refusals for small buckets, that the
planted design survives only under per-bucket margins, and the tier-switch state shown in the promotion
records of the finalists. Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, wall time, evidence blocks, per region the
count, mean signed residual with interval, the margin or refusal, the cut applied to each boundary design,
ρ_ep with its address count against 0.7 and 200, and on failure the first design eliminated from an
unqualified bucket.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The pairing arithmetic of the plan: 1.96, 5.3 and 10 at ρ = 0.7, 0.9 and 0.95.
- The floor: fewer than 19 calibration designs cannot support α = 0.05.
- Negative controls: extrapolating a neighbour's margin into a small bucket (the refusal test fails);
  defining regions after scoring so that the planted design's region looks well calibrated (rejected at
  manifest admission); enabling the tier correction at ρ_ep = 0.5 (the switch test fails, and the
  estimator's own gate shows a planted between-tier reversal being established).
- Determinism: identical records twice, across worker counts.

## Considerations

- Buckets divide a small audit set into smaller ones; with a tenth of rung spend on audits, most campaigns
  will qualify only a few buckets. The design accepts that: an unqualified bucket's designs go up, which
  costs compute and loses nothing.
- Region definitions are a place where an analyst could tune after the fact; fixing them in the manifest
  hash before scoring is the guard, and a change of regions is a new campaign.
- KILL-DR (the tier-correction kill gate, decided with its own gate) fires if ρ_ep is below 0.7 on 200
  paired addresses in every stratum tested; this switch is the per-stratum form of the same rule and keeps
  recording ρ_ep either way.
- Controller-budget curves attack the same bias per design instead of per region; where both exist the
  per-design curve escalates a design before the rung may discard it, and the region margin still applies to
  whatever is compared afterwards.
