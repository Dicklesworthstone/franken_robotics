## Background

The first two stages of the cascade remove work before any simulation is bought. Stage 1 (deduplicate and
check) keeps one canonical representative per orbit under the observation-preserving symmetry subgroup,
merges designs with equal mission-observable digests, and runs `design.check` with the bound analyses. Stage
2 (reuse and synthesize) serves memo hits for analyses, controllers, step plans and episodes, reuses module
contracts where an edit is local, and runs bounded exact linkage synthesis when the brief asks for a
mechanism. Both stages can be wrong in two directions, and the directions are not symmetric: failing to
merge or to reuse wastes compute; merging two designs the mission can tell apart, or reusing a verdict an
edit invalidated, is a false result. Each stage therefore carries a two-sided control built into the
campaign. Stage 1: two designs that must merge and two that must split. Stage 2: a mirror swap must reuse; a
shared-bus edit must invalidate. Tier: Solid.

The plan is plain about how much stage 1 is worth. Ten leg types on six slots with dihedral symmetry of order
12 give (10⁶ + 2·10 + 2·10² + 10³ + 3·10⁴ + 3·10³)/12 = 86,185 representatives, an 11.6-fold reduction; with
only a sagittal mirror left, because a sensor broke the rest, (10⁶ + 10³)/2 = 500,500, a 2.0-fold reduction
(exact counts, by the orbit-counting lemma). The stage is worth having and is not a large factor once sensors
break the symmetry. That is also why the must-split control matters: the tempting error is to count with the
mechanical group when a front-only sensor is fitted, which merges designs the closed loop distinguishes.

## Scope

In: the two controls as `StageControl` implementations; their planted items and how they are built for a
given population; the expected outcomes; the action on each kind of failure; reuse receipts; the reported
deduplication factor.

Out: the quotient and the digest (BD-285); the memo store and revalidation (BD-111, BD-292, the memo beads of
this unit); the digest's store-policy rules (N28b-41); linkage synthesis; the framework (N28b-25).

## Technical approach

**1. Stage 1 control: `dedup-merge-split`.** Planned before any design is checked; items are added to the
population under sealed identities.

| Item | Construction | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| Must-merge pair (orbit) | A design from the population and its image under a non-trivial element of the observation-preserving subgroup of its chassis and task (for example the left–right mirror of a leg-type assignment) | One representative; the other mapped to it with the recorded group element |
| Must-merge pair (digest) | Two designs that differ only in a field the mission's equations do not read (a ballast slug moved between two placements with equal count, first and second moments) | Equal mission-observable digests; evaluated once |
| Must-split pair (symmetry) | Two designs related by an element of the mechanical group that is *not* in the observation-preserving subgroup (the sensor breaks it) | Two representatives |
| Must-split pair (digest) | Two placements with equal first moment and different second moment (slots {1, 4} and {2, 3}: Σx = 5 for both, Σx² = 17 and 13) | Different digests; two classes |

Evaluation at stage close: every must-merge pair merged and every must-split pair kept apart. Actions:
- a must-split pair merged (a false equivalence) → the control *fails*: the quotient that merged it (orbit or
  digest) is disabled for the campaign, deduplication falls back to canonical form and content hash only,
  every class that quotient formed in this campaign is split and its members re-admitted, and the case is
  filed as a negative-control seed;
- a must-merge pair not merged → the control is recorded `failed: inefficient` (no result is wrong); the
  campaign continues and the report states the lost factor.
When the chassis has only the trivial observation-preserving subgroup the orbit items cannot be built; that
half is `not-run: trivial-symmetry` and the digest half still runs.

**2. The reported factor.** Stage 1 records, per stratum: designs in, orbit representatives, digest classes,
designs out, and the factor as an exact ratio. The factor is reported, not promised; no estimate of it is
printed before the stage has run except the orbit count from the counting formula for enumerated families.

**3. Stage 2 control: `reuse-invalidate`.**

| Item | Construction | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| Mirror swap | A design already evaluated in the campaign with two identical modules exchanged across the mirror plane (a leaf swap that leaves the compiled model's observables unchanged up to the recorded symmetry) | Reuse: its analyses, controller and episodes are served (memo hits or grade-1 and grade-2 revalidation), with zero new episodes |
| Shared-bus edit | The same design with an edit that touches the shared electrical bus (a lower bus current limit, or one more consumer on the bus) | Invalidation: every verdict and analysis whose support includes the bus (the joint bus schedule, actuator envelopes under load, episodes) is recomputed; none is served |

The second item encodes the structural rule from the module contracts: swapping a leaf module preserves its
siblings' local certificates, while an edit that touches a shared battery bus, a shared thermal sink, a
cross-branch kinematic loop or base-coupled contact invalidates global ones. Evaluation: zero new episodes
for the mirror swap; zero served results among bus-dependent items for the bus edit. Actions:
- a bus-dependent result served after the bus edit (unsafe reuse, a false accept even if a later change
  restores feasibility) → the control *fails*: the memo node kind or reuse rule that served it is stopped for
  the campaign (the store's stop-on-mismatch latch for a node kind; the registry's `disabled` status for a
  polarity rule), everything it served in this campaign is recomputed, and the case is filed;
- the mirror swap not reused → `failed: inefficient`; reported, nothing recomputed twice.

**4. Reuse receipts.** Every item stage 2 serves instead of computing is listed in the stage's output as a
receipt: `{ design, asset: analysis | controller | step-plan | episode-set | verdict, key, grade: 1 | 2 | 3,
licence, cost_avoided }`. Grade 1 is identity (the full key is unchanged), grade 2 dependency (every changed
field lies outside the recorded support), grade 3 implication (a named polarity rule). Every reused verdict
carries a grade and a licence (INV-ATEL-07). `cost_avoided` is the node's recorded recompute cost; the
campaign report sums it in the "exact reuse" and "evidence carried forward" columns, never as one number.

**5. Layout and determinism.** `crates/frob-opt/src/cascade/{stage1_control.rs, stage2_control.rs}`. Both
controls are pure functions of the manifest and the ledger (D0).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the seeded population the four stage-1 items give the expected outcomes and the control record is
      `passed`.
- [ ] With the quotient replaced by one under the mechanical group, the must-split symmetry pair is merged,
      the control fails, the quotient is disabled for the campaign and its classes are re-split.
- [ ] With the digest computed without second moments, the must-split digest pair is merged and the control
      fails the same way.
- [ ] The orbit counts 86,185 and 500,500 are reproduced by the counting formula used in the stage's report
      for the enumerated ten-types-on-six-slots family.
- [ ] The mirror swap is served with zero new episodes; the shared-bus edit serves no bus-dependent result.
- [ ] With the bus removed from one analysis's recorded support (a planted mutant), the stage-2 control
      fails, the node kind is stopped for the campaign and the served items are recomputed.
- [ ] Every served item has a receipt with grade and licence; `cost_avoided` is reported by column.
- [ ] Inefficient failures (not merged, not reused) are reported with the lost factor and do not label the
      results as wrong.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt --test cascade_stage12_controls`:

- `burnside_counts`: k = 10 types on six slots under the dihedral group of order 12 → 86,185; under the
  sagittal mirror alone → 500,500; with k = 3 → 92, checked against brute-force enumeration.
- `must_merge_orbit_pair`; `must_merge_digest_pair`.
- `must_split_sensor_breaks_symmetry`: with one forward lidar the mechanical group has four elements and the
  observation-preserving subgroup two; the pair related by a broken element stays split.
- `must_split_second_moment`: placements {1, 4} and {2, 3} have Σx = 5 and Σx² = 17 and 13.
- `false_equivalence_disables_quotient`; `inefficient_merge_reported_not_failed_wrong`.
- `mirror_swap_zero_new_episodes`.
- `bus_edit_recomputes_bus_dependents`: the joint bus schedule analysis and the episodes are recomputed; a
  mass-only roll-up that does not read the bus is still served.
- `unsafe_reuse_stops_node_kind`.
- `receipt_has_grade_and_licence` (property: no receipt without both).
- `trivial_symmetry_half_not_run`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Steps `stage1` and `stage2` of `scripts/e2e/cascade_seeded_population.sh` (bead N28b-28): `frob opt cascade`
on the seeded population, then `frob report campaign --json`; asserts the two control records, the factor
with its numerator and denominator, the receipts' grades, zero new episodes for the mirror swap (from the
job records), and, in a second run with the planted mutants enabled one at a time, the failure actions.
Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, wall time, evidence blocks, designs in and out per stratum, each
control item's hash, expected and observed outcome, receipts by grade with cost avoided, and on failure the
first pair whose outcome differs from its plan.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-697: stage 1 orbit representatives: 86,185 and 500,500 (exact counts). Negative control: deduplication
  under the mechanical group when a sensor breaks it (CX-305: a quadruped with one forward lidar has the
  four-element group mechanically and a two-element group in closed loop).
- KA-698: 735,471 ballast placements give 24,546 classes. Negative control: two placements with equal first
  moment and different second moment merged.
- CX-303 is the standing example for the bus rule: two fingers each need one powered tick and the bus powers
  one finger per tick; with a one-tick deadline no joint schedule exists and with two ticks exactly two
  exist, so a bus edit changes feasibility that no per-module summary can see.
- Metamorphic: relabelling slots by an element of the observation-preserving subgroup leaves the set of
  representatives unchanged.
- Determinism: identical control outcomes twice and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- BET-31 (campaign quotienting) is falsified by one false equivalence; its fallback, deduplication by
  canonical form and content hash only, is the action this control takes inside a campaign. The program-level
  kill decision belongs to the quotient's owner.
- The mirror-swap item requires a design with two identical modules across a mirror plane; populations
  without one report `not-run: no-mirror-pair` for that half.
- These controls add four to six designs to a population; their cost is negligible beside the stages they
  protect.
- RISK-38 (an incomplete memo key serves a stale verdict) is the risk behind stage 2's control; the shadow
  recomputation of the memo store is the continuous defence and this control is the per-campaign one.
