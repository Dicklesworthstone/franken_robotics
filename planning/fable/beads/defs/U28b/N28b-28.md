## Background

`frob opt cascade --spec <mission or task> --budget …` runs the six-stage co-design cascade from a
specification to a ranked, typed result set, streaming progress with denominators. It is orchestration over
the verbs of the studio and is judged as such. This bead is its end-to-end test and the measurement of its
gate. GATE-ATEL-16 (known answer; earliest M4): the cascade end to end on a seeded population: stage controls
all run; the report prints every mode, every fallback and the phase ledger; merge of two agents' archives in
both orders gives identical bytes.

The gate is deliberately about honesty of the record and not about speed. A cascade that returned a
plausible ranking while a control was silently skipped, a rung was trusted without an audit, or a fallback
fired unreported would pass any performance test and still be worthless as evidence. The seeded population
is built so that every such omission shows up as a missing or wrong line in the output. It carries
North-Star scenario NS-06 at reduced scale.

This is the only end-to-end bead of the cascade (the key N28a-32 and its script name `atelier_cascade.sh`
were merged into it). One script, `cascade_seeded_population.sh`, carries every step.

## Scope

In: one script, `scripts/e2e/cascade_seeded_population.sh`, with the steps that the mechanism beads of this
unit name; the content the seeded population must have; the reduced-scale population as a `synthetic`
fixture and its builder `scripts/e2e/fixtures/atelier/make_population.sh`; the assertions on `opt.cascade`,
`opt.status`, `opt.archive` and `report.campaign` output; the sabotage campaigns, the cancel-and-resume step
and the honest-ending step; the script's self-test; the gate record.

Out: registering the gate-scale population with its stored full evaluation as conformance data (BD-377,
which takes the reduced fixture over into the conformance crate after M4); the cascade itself and its stages
(BD-352 and the beads it calls, among them BD-345 and N28b-12 to N28b-27, whose steps this script hosts and
which are reached through BD-352 in the graph); the verb's own script `scripts/e2e/opt_cascade.sh` (BD-352),
the screening-audit script (N28b-31) and the stage-5 script (BD-346), which this script invokes as stages and
whose checks it does not repeat; the prediction controls (N28c-18), the report sections (N28c-19) and the
report-clause campaigns (N28c-20), whose controls registered against GATE-ATEL-16 run in this bead's gate run;
the paired gate of the conformal layer, GATE-ATEL-08, with its coverage,
recall and cost-ratio rows (N28b-19), which runs on the same population and addresses.

## Technical approach

**1. The seeded population (requirements on the fixture).** Reduced scale for the script: 81 designs in
three morphology classes (27 each of `quad-mammal`, `hexapod-radial`, `diff-drive` variants), three rungs,
k = 3 finalists per class, on `batch-cpu`; gate scale for the recorded run: the population BD-377 registers.
The reduced population is built by `scripts/e2e/fixtures/atelier/make_population.sh` from seed 17 by keyed
typed edits from the class templates; its default form for continuous integration is the 27 designs of the
quadruped class alone (halving counts 27 → 9 → 3), and `FROB_E2E_POPULATION=81` builds all three classes.
Scorers at reduced scale are *fixture scorers*: registered controllers whose scores are a fixed, seeded
function of the design and the world address composed with the real simulation of a short task, so that rank
agreement between rungs is known by construction. They are labelled `synthetic` in every record; the
cascade, ledgers, controls and report are the real code. Plants, each sealed in the fixture manifest:

| Plant | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Three strata with top-band τ of about 0.85, 0.6 and 0.3 between the first rung and the specialist rung | Modes `selective`, `discard-only`, `uninformative` |
| A planted winner per class that the first rung under-scores | Screening audit; survives or the stratum is `uninformative` |
| A planted over-scored design | Caught at the next rung |
| A drift from generation 4 in one stratum (first-rung error tripled) | The exchangeability alarm and reversion to the fixed table |
| A hard-to-steer region in one class | Per-bucket margins |
| Must-merge and must-split pairs; a mirror swap and a shared-bus edit | Stage 1 and 2 controls |
| A pyramid-cone exploit among one class's finalists | Stage 5 types the ranking `model-conditional` |
| An inert-edit clone of the reference | Stage 6 no-signal candidate |
| One class with a family policy, one without, and one design the baseline cannot synthesize | Scorer admission |
| A design carrying the two-finger shared bus with a two-tick deadline (feasible; a planted additive-peak prune would discard it) | Prune soundness |
| A design that profits from energy created by an over-soft contact, beside the pyramid-cone exploit | Stage 5, second exploit |
| A set of nine identical candidates | Stage 4 null-race control |

The planted winner has a known top-k result from a stored specialist-and-referee evaluation and is in a form
the shared-policy rung scores poorly; its identity is sealed in the manifest and logged only after the
campaign closes. The must-merge pair are mirror images under the observation-preserving group; the
must-split pair are identified by the mechanical group and distinguished by a forward sensor. The no-signal
candidate may equally be a pair of identical designs under two names.

**2. Steps.** Each step logs under the bead that owns its mechanism. Every `frob opt cascade` call carries its
own idempotency key: `csp-estimate`, `csp-run`, `csp-replay-w8`, `csp-replay-preempt`, `csp-fixed`,
`csp-framework-1` to `csp-framework-4`, `csp-merge-a`, `csp-merge-b`, `csp-sabotage-a` to `csp-sabotage-d`,
`csp-honest`, `csp-fin-r1` for the selecting request of `modes`, and `csp-cancel` for the cancelled-and-resumed
campaign; every `frob eval rank-agreement` call of `rank_ledger` and `modes` carries `csp-ra-<stratum>`. Steps that compare two runs (`run` with
`replay`, `run` with `fixed_table`, `fixed_table` with `cancel_resume`) would otherwise receive the first run's
stored receipt, or a key conflict (`FROB-REF-803`), instead of a second run. The records `eval.rank-agreement`
writes in `rank_ledger` hold the same measures as the audit-lane rows (that step's differential), so the
campaigns that follow read the same modes; the selecting request of `modes` is `--plan-only` and writes nothing.

| Step | Commands | Asserts |
|---|---|---|
| `opt_cascade` | Invokes `scripts/e2e/opt_cascade.sh` (owned by BD-352, in its own scratch root: `--plan-only` with every rung `uninformative`, `--finalists-from R1` with no record (537), the unranked "k survivors" report, N22a-17's consume stage, promote-only tier-pair charging, and the codes the cascade passes on, 546, 548, 549, with 704 for a campaign without an approved envelope) | The stage exits 0 with zero failed assertions; its `summary` line and log path are recorded |
| `estimate` | `frob opt cascade … --estimate --json` | Five costs, headroom, modes, labels; zero dispatches |
| `scorers` | (from the estimate) | Admitted scorer per rung and class with reasons |
| `run` | `frob opt cascade --spec <task> --population seeded-81 --budget … --seed 3 --key csp-run --json` (exit 8, then `frob job wait`); `frob opt status` while running | Progress events carry denominators; the job completes with a `Ranked` disposition and exit 2 (labels present) |
| `stage1`, `stage2` | `frob evidence trace` on the control records | Outcomes per N28b-26; factor with numerator and denominator; receipts with grades |
| `audit_lane`, `calibration`, `survivors`, `region_bias`, `drift` | `frob evidence trace` on decisions, margins, rows | Per N28b-13 to N28b-16 and N28b-24 |
| `rank_ledger` | `frob evidence trace <claim> --json` on each allocation decision's `rank_record`; then, per stratum, `frob eval rank-agreement --designs <the stratum's audited set> --pair R1,R3 --class <class> --task <task> --key csp-ra-<stratum> --json` (BD-328) on the same pairs | Per N28b-22 (row versions, sources, n_designs, measures equal to a recomputation from the published audit pairs, every `rank_record` resolves; after the checkpoint swap the stratum reads as unmeasured and a selecting request is 537); the rung-pair differential: the audit-lane row's measures equal those `eval.rank-agreement` reports on the same pairs |
| `modes` | `frob evidence trace` on the `core_decision` records; on the `discard-only` stratum, the record `eval.rank-agreement` wrote in `rank_ledger` (BD-328's calibration-experiment record, lower bound between 0.5 and 0.7), then `frob opt cascade --spec <task> --population seeded-81 --seed 3 --plan-only --finalists-from R1 --key csp-fin-r1 --json` and `frob report campaign <id> --json` | Per N28b-23: the three modes in the three strata in every generation, upgrades only after two clean generations; on BD-328's `discard-only` record the selecting request is refused with `FROB-REF-545`, exit 3, its two repairs in order, and the report says "3 survivors", never "top 3" |
| `stage3` | Invokes `scripts/e2e/screening_audit.sh` | Audit records per stage; planted winner outcome |
| `stage4`, `stage6` | Control records | Null-race count within its bound; no-signal candidate not promoted; alpha spend at most α |
| `stage5` | Invokes `scripts/e2e/cascade_stage5_modelform.sh` (written by BD-346) | The exploit class typed `model-conditional`; others `model-robust`; the stage's share of spend at most 0.10; a report form that drops the condition refused with `FROB-REF-557` and `frob eval promote` of the conditional finalist refused with `FROB-REF-830`, each exit 3 and one code per request (BD-346) |
| `framework` | Four small populations; one run with the second contact law withheld | The four dispositions; `not-run` carried on the result |
| `report` | `frob report campaign <id> --json` and the HTML form | See 3 |
| `merge` | Two sessions run the cascade's first two generations on disjoint halves of the population; in two stores `frob phylum map --merge <A> <B> --json` and `frob phylum map --merge <B> <A> --json` (the merge belongs to `phylum.map`; `opt.archive` is a read of one campaign's elites, `--campaign`, `--top`, `--stratum`), then `frob opt archive --campaign <id> --json` in each store | Identical `MapId` and bytes of the merged archive, identical lineage links of its elites |
| `replay` | The whole `run` step again with another worker count and with random preemption | Identical decisions, modes, labels and e-values |
| `fixed_table` | `frob opt cascade --spec <task> --population seeded-81 --budget … --seed 3 --allocation fixed --key csp-fixed --json` on the same population and addresses | Denominators at every stage; the bracket ledger written before the first score; modes printed per stratum; the planted winner among the finalists; both exploit designs typed `model-conditional`; stage 5 spend at most 10% of the total; the no-signal pair not promoted; all six controls `passed` |
| `sabotage` | Four separate campaign ids: (a) the screen biased against the planted winner; (b) stage 5 skipped by the conformance-build mutant (`--mutant skip-stage5`, defined by BD-346; a release build answers it with 802); (c) the planted additive-peak prune enabled; (d) no planted winner supplied | (a) `screen-biased`, the rung `uninformative` in that stratum, the campaign re-opened; (b) the gate check reports the exploit design as an unflagged winner and fails; (c) the prune-soundness audit finds the feasible bus design, suspends the rule and files the case; (d) the control is `not-run` and the report says "survivors" |
| `cancel_resume` | Cancel the `fixed_table` campaign (new key) during stage 4, then resume it | Finalists and digests equal the uninterrupted run's |
| `honest_ending` | A population in which every design violates a hard limit | Exit 2 and "every admitted alternative fails" |
| `gate_query` | There is no `frob gates` or `frob conformance` command (ADR-GAUNT-01, BD-025's lint `no_conformance_command`). In the gate-scale invocation the run is `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test <tier named by GATE-ATEL-16's record in registries/gates.toml> --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-ATEL-16`, which runs the gate's registered controls in the same run and writes the hash-named receipt; in both forms the script then reads the row of GATE-ATEL-16 in `registries/gates.toml` and its receipt read-only | In the gate-scale invocation (`FROB_E2E_POPULATION=gate`), the row this run wrote (section 4) reads back with exactly this run's measured values; in the reduced form, the row is read and checked against its gate-scale receipt (hash, controls run, report keys, both digests), and the reduced run's own values are logged beside it without being compared to it, since the populations differ. The gate-scale campaign is not re-run inside the reduced script |

The `--allocation conformal` run on the same population and addresses (audit list before scores, no audit
episode cancelled, survivors against 3k, coverage, recall and GPU-seconds against the `fixed_table` run) is
the running-system part of GATE-ATEL-08 and belongs to N28b-19; this script asserts only the per-mechanism
lines of the `audit_lane`, `calibration` and `survivors` steps.

**3. What the report must print (the gate's second clause).** For the campaign: the disposition; per stage
the control, its outcome and action; per rung pair and stratum the mode in every generation; every fallback
that fired, with its trigger (alarm, trust check, audit failure, missing calibration, null-race failure) and
the generation; the audit lane's size and cost; the planted-winner outcome; the screening audit's bound per
stratum; the phase ledger (authoring, check, synthesis, simulation, training, promotion, review, wait) with
costs and the controls' cost as separate lines, its exclusive phases summing to the campaign clock within 2%; the reuse receipts by column; finalists described as "k
survivors" wherever any reducing rung was not `selective`; each finalist's rank-robustness type and
qualification receipt; the claim type, `claim_id` and weakest input of every statement; the no-authorization
flags. Reports are deterministic: the same artifact graph produces the same bytes, which the script checks by
generating the report twice. The script holds a list of required report keys and fails on any that is absent.

**4. The gate record.** GATE-ATEL-16 is recorded from the gate-scale run with: the count of stage controls
run (six of six, or the registered reason for each `not-run`), the completeness check of the report's keys
(with the report-clause measurements of N28c-20's winning and losing campaigns), the two archive digests, and
the replay agreement. The receipt shows caught every control registered in `controls.toml` against
GATE-ATEL-16, because N38b-19 refuses a receipt without them: the stage controls' plants, N28c-18's prediction
controls (a prediction accepted after its outcome, a report that omits missed predictions, a hash taken after
the run starts, the saving registered at close, pooled prediction versions), N28c-19's report-section controls
(an empty `fallbacks_fired` omitted, "top 3" under `discard-only`, summed reuse columns, discarded designs'
replay addresses dropped, a controller ceiling in the verdict column) and N28c-20's report-clause controls (a
number read from a log line, an omitted `fallbacks_fired` section, a package embedding its generation time, a
losing report without replay addresses, a compact mode that drops conditions). The row is written by the
Gauntlet's recorder from the run's receipt, `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-ATEL-16 --receipt <hash>`
(N38b-19), never by hand and never by a `frob` command. The reduced-scale script is the regression that runs in
continuous integration.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `scripts/e2e/cascade_seeded_population.sh` exists, sources the shared helper, drives the real binary
      through every step of the table and exits non-zero on any failed assertion.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-16 (TARGET): on the seeded population all stage controls run; the report prints every mode,
      every fallback and the phase ledger; the merge of two agents' archives in both orders gives identical
      bytes. Recorded with measured values (controls run, report keys present, both digests) from a receipt
      in which every control registered against the gate, N28c-18's, N28c-19's and N28c-20's among them, is
      `caught`.
- [ ] The `opt_cascade` stage runs BD-352's script and fails when it fails; no step repeats its checks.
- [ ] Each plant produces its expected line: three modes in three strata; the alarm in the drift stratum; the
      over-scored design eliminated at the next rung; `model-conditional` for the exploit class; the planted
      winner's outcome; the no-signal candidate rejected.
- [ ] Planted omissions are caught: with a control's record deleted, a fallback's report line suppressed, or
      a mode omitted from the report (test-only switches), the script fails at the step that owns it.
- [ ] The replay under another worker count and under preemption gives identical decisions and e-values.
- [ ] The report regenerates to identical bytes.
- [ ] The log has the fields below, a bead attribution per step and a final `summary` line naming the gate.
- [ ] The script passes at the fourth milestone on `batch-cpu` with the 27-design population and, where the
      compute class allows, with 81 designs on a device; all six stage controls are observed `passed` in the
      `fixed_table` step and each sabotage is observed caught.
- [ ] In the gate-scale invocation the measured values of the gate in the log equal the row written to the
      gate register; in the reduced form the row is read and checked against its receipt.
- [ ] It exits non-zero under each planted regression of its self-test; two runs with one seed give logs
      that differ only in timestamps, wall times and costs.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

A shell lint and `--list-steps`; the script is the test. The required-report-keys list lives beside the
script and is itself checked against the report schema so the two cannot drift.

`scripts/e2e/tests/cascade_seeded_population_selftest.sh`, one planted regression per run: (a) audit
episodes cancellable by the race (caught at `audit_lane`); (b) stage 1 deduplicating under the mechanical
group (caught at `stage1`, the pair that must split); (c) the report printing "top k" under `discard-only`
(caught at `report`); (d) the resume path re-drawing addresses (caught at `cancel_resume`); (e) a control
silently skipped and reported `passed` (sabotage (d) expects `not-run`).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/cascade_seeded_population.sh` writes `target/e2e-logs/cascade_seeded_population/<utc>.ndjson`
and a human-readable transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`,
`assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
`evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), measured values with units and their
thresholds (per stage: designs in and out, spend against budget, control outcome; survivors against 3k; ln M against ln 20; coverage against 0.92; null-race eliminations against
38; alpha spend against α; stage-5 share against 0.10), digests where state is compared (archives, lineages,
reports, replays), and on failure the first differing value and the artifact paths needed to reproduce (the
campaign's store is kept on failure). The final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed per step
and the gates touched (GATE-ATEL-16; and, by invocation, GATE-ATEL-09 and GATE-ATEL-10). No private-scene
data is logged; the fixture uses generated scenes only. Runtime target: under 45 minutes on `batch-cpu` for
the 27-design form with reduced episode counts (TARGET); the 81-design device run is sized by the campaign's
compute class.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-696 (the 38th residual at n = 39), KA-697 (86,185 and 500,500 representatives; the stage-1 counts on
  the counting fixture, run as a side check), KA-692 (the cone exploit typed `model-conditional`) and KA-706
  are asserted at the steps that own them; the halving counts 27 → 9 → 3 in the 27-design form.
- Negative controls of the script itself: the three planted omissions of the acceptance list; a cascade
  build with the trust rule disabled (step `stage3` fails); a report that calls survivors "the top 3" in the
  `discard-only` stratum (step `report` fails).
- Metamorphic: permuting the population's order, or renaming designs, leaves every decision unchanged.
- The four sabotage campaigns and the five self-test regressions are negative controls as well.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1 and 8; with random preemption; resumed against
  uninterrupted; across shard counts; under schedule fuzz; the gate-scale run on a GPU backend against its
  determinism class, with decision-level agreement with `batch-cpu` recorded.

## Considerations

- The reduced scale keeps the script to minutes on `batch-cpu`; the gate-scale run is a campaign of compute
  class E2 and needs the owner's approval, with the reduced run as the fallback evidence if it is withheld.
- Fixture scorers make rank agreement controllable; they say nothing about real rank agreement, and every
  record they touch says `synthetic`. The real-scorer run of the cascade is the gate-scale run.
- The script is long because the capability is. It is organized so that `--only <step>` runs one step
  against a stored campaign, which is how a single mechanism's regression is debugged.
- If a kill gate has fired (KILL-LADDER, KILL-RANK, KILL-VOI), the expected lines change (fixed table
  everywhere; `uninformative` first rung; unranked suggestions) and the script reads the registry state to
  choose its expectations; it never treats a fired kill gate as a failure.
- The planted winner needs an earlier specialist-and-referee result; for classes without one the control is
  `not-run` by the open question's default (OPEN-ATEL-5), and sabotage (d) is the test of that path.
- The 27-design form is small enough that conformal calibration can fall below 19 audit designs in a
  stratum; it therefore uses one stratum wherever a margin is needed, and logs the calibration count.
- Stage 6 uses a scratch qualification campaign with its own sealed clusters; nothing from it is readable by
  the script before close, and the script asserts that an early read is refused.
