## Background

The cascade spends cheap stages to discard designs before expensive ones. A comparison among survivors says
nothing about what was discarded: an e-value between the eighth and ninth finalists is silent about the
design a screen threw away at the first rung (CX-293). The adversarial subsystem therefore audits every
screening stage, in two ways, and owns the audit: its verb is `nemesis.audit` (kind `run`, first milestone
M4), its gate is GATE-NEMESIS-07, and the cascade applies it stage by stage without restating it. There is
one screening audit in the system; the registry holds no second name for it. Tier: Solid.

1. **Sampling discarded strata.** Before any score exists, a keyed random draw assigns a share of the
   population to the audit lane (10% of the stage's spend, TARGET). Audited designs run at the next stage at
   a fixed sample size as if they had survived, and are never cancelled by a race. Discards are stratified by
   reason for discard, design family and score band. The result is a false-discard rate per stratum with an
   exact binomial bound.
2. **Planting a winner.** A design whose rank at the final stage is known from an earlier full evaluation,
   and which the screen represents poorly (a gait outside the atlas, a body far from the policy family's
   training set), enters the population under a blinded identity. It must survive every screen.

A missed planted winner, or an audited miss rate above the declared tolerance, labels the campaign's results
`screen-biased` and bypasses the stage for the affected strata until it is re-qualified. The audit lane is
also the only calibration source for the rank-agreement measurement between rungs, because it supplies
designs that were not selected by the scores being calibrated.

Rejected alternatives: auditing a sample chosen after scoring (the sample can then depend on the scores
through any code path that sees them); the approximation ln(1/δ)/m for the bound (10.0% at m = 30 where the
exact value is 9.5%; the exact form costs nothing); a planted winner whose "known rank" is a guess (refused).

## Scope

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs.** A campaign, a stage, and the campaign manifest's audit plan (strata definition, audit share,
fixed n, δ, tolerance, k, the planted winner's seal). From the ledger: the stage's `audit-draw` record, the
population admitted to the stage, the stage's decisions (who advanced, who was discarded and for which
reason), and the next stage's scores of every design that ran there (survivors and audited designs).

**2. Post-stratification.** The audit draw is a uniform sample of the stage's population made before scores
existed, so within any subset defined afterwards it is a uniform sample of that subset. Audited *discards*
are grouped by (reason for discard, design family or morphology class, score band, and, where the campaign
uses it, archive cell block). Reasons are the stage's own typed outcomes (excluded by a named analysis;
below the cut; raced out; pruned by a contract). Score bands are the terciles of the lower-rung score among
discards (DESIGN).

**3. The false-discard bound (per stratum and pooled).** Let m be the audited discards of the stratum that
completed the next stage, and x the number of them that fall in the top k of the next stage's scores, taken
over all designs that ran there. The one-sided upper confidence bound on the stratum's rate of discarded
winners at confidence 1 − δ is the exact binomial (Clopper–Pearson) bound: the p solving
Σ_{i ≤ x} C(m, i)·pⁱ·(1 − p)^(m − i) = δ. For x = 0 it is 1 − δ^(1/m): 9.5% at m = 30 and 5.0% at m = 59 for
δ = 0.05. The stratum passes when the bound is at most the declared tolerance. A stratum with too few audited
discards to support the tolerance (for tolerance 0.05 and δ = 0.05, fewer than 59 with x = 0) is reported
`under-sampled` with the bound it does support; the cascade's trust rule decides what follows.

**4. The planted winner.**
- *Eligibility.* The design must carry a recorded full evaluation (a specialist promoted on the referee)
  that placed it in the top k of a named population of its class; the record's `claim_id` is checked. If the
  rank is not on record the audit refuses with `FROB-REF-566` (a planted winner's rank is not on record from
  a full evaluation; repair: run the full evaluation, or report the control `not-run`; exit 3).
- *Seal.* Before the campaign starts the controller draws a salt and writes
  seal = BLAKE3(salt ‖ DesignId ‖ campaign) into the campaign manifest, whose hash is committed before any
  score. Nothing else in the manifest or the population marks the design.
- *Blinded entry.* The design enters the population as an ordinary member through the ordinary generator
  path. No job, scorer or agent-facing listing can tell it from the others; only the controller holds the
  salt.
- *Reveal.* At campaign close the salt is published, anyone can check the seal against the population, and
  the audit record states the winner's fate: `reached-finalists`, or `lost at stage s by <reason>`.
- A campaign with no eligible winner for a class reports `not-run` for that class's plant.

**5. The record (`frob.audit/1`).** `{ campaign, stage, plan_hash, strata: [ { key, discards, audited: m,
top_k_found: x, bound, delta, tolerance, status: "passed" | "failed" | "under-sampled" } ], pooled: {…},
planted_winner: { seal, status: "reached-finalists" | "lost" | "not-run", stage?, reason?, revealed: bool },
label: null | "screen-biased", bypass: [strata], requalified_by: null | campaign }`. It is an evidence item
with a claim of kind Design comparison, class Tested, scoped to the tier, the population and the generator
identity.

**6. Label and re-qualification.** `screen-biased` is set when any stratum's status is `failed` or the
planted winner is `lost`. The stage is bypassed for the listed strata until re-qualified: a later audit of
the same stage, scorer identities and stratum passes with its planted winner reaching the finalists; the
passing record names the record it re-qualifies.

**7. The verb.** `frob nemesis audit --campaign <id> --stage <n> [--reveal] --json`; input schema
`frob.nemesis.audit.in/1`, output `frob.audit/1` in the envelope. Kind `run` (it may need to wait for
audited designs to finish the next stage; it then returns a job, exit 8). Exit 0 when every stratum passed
and the plant reached the finalists; 2 when anything is `failed`, `under-sampled`, `lost` or `not-run`; 3 on
a refusal. Layer: `frob-nemesis`, reading Chronicle; it runs no simulation of its own.

**8. Measuring GATE-NEMESIS-07.** Two synthetic batteries on fixture scorers: (a) 100 runs in which a
deliberately biased screen (it under-scores one design family by a fixed offset) removes the planted winner;
the audit must report the loss in 100 of 100, and an unbiased screen must pass; (b) 1,000 synthetic
campaigns with a planted false-discard rate per stratum (true top-k designs placed among discards at known
rates between 0 and 20%); the stratified bound must cover the planted rate in at least 95%.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-NEMESIS-07 (TARGET): a planted winner removed by a biased screen is reported in 100 of 100 runs;
      the stratified bound covers the planted false-discard rate in at least 95% of 1,000 synthetic
      campaigns. Measured counts are in the gate receipt.
- [ ] A screen with no bias passes (the negative control of the known answer).
- [ ] Bounds: m = 30, x = 0 → 0.0950; m = 59, x = 0 → 0.0495; m = 30, x = 1 → 0.1486; to four places.
- [ ] A planted winner without a resolving full-evaluation record is refused with `FROB-REF-566`.
- [ ] The seal is in the manifest hash before the first score; the reveal verifies; before the reveal no
      output of any verb identifies the plant (checked by scanning every envelope of the campaign for the
      plant's identity in a distinguishing position).
- [ ] The audit uses only designs in the stage's `audit-draw` record; any other design in its input is
      rejected.
- [ ] An under-sampled stratum is reported as such with the bound it supports, never as passed.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob --json` and the MCP tool `nemesis_audit`; cancellation of the waiting job leaves
      no partial record; `frob-nemesis/CONTRACT.md` documents the bound, the seal and the label.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-nemesis --test screening_audit`:

- `bound_zero_found`: (m, δ) = (30, 0.05) → 0.09503; (59, 0.05) → 0.04951.
- `bound_one_found`: (30, 1, 0.05) → 0.1486 (Clopper–Pearson upper, one-sided).
- `approximation_not_used`: the returned value at m = 30 is not ln(20)/30 = 0.0999.
- `post_stratification_uniform`: within each post-stratum the audited designs' keys are uniform (the 10⁶-draw
  inclusion test of the audit lane, conditioned on stratum).
- `top_k_membership_from_next_stage_scores`.
- `seal_verifies_and_hides`: the seal checks with the salt; without it, 2²⁰ candidate identities yield no
  match advantage (the seal is a salted hash).
- `planted_winner_without_rank_refused_566`.
- `lost_plant_sets_label_and_bypass`; `failed_stratum_sets_label`; `requalification_clears_bypass`.
- `under_sampled_reported`: m = 30, x = 0, tolerance 0.05 → `under-sampled`, bound 0.095.
- `non_audit_design_rejected`.
- `coverage_simulation`: 1,000 synthetic campaigns → coverage of the planted rate ≥ 0.95.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/screening_audit.sh` (bead N28b-31) drives `frob opt cascade` and `frob nemesis audit` on clean
and planted seeded populations. This bead's gate batteries are run by `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
gate_nemesis_07`, invoked as a step of that script, logging per run the screen's bias, the plant's fate as
reported against its true fate, per stratum m, x, the bound and the planted rate, and the coverage tally
against 0.95; structured log per STD-E2E with the gate named in the `summary` line.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-668 / KA-706: a planted winner removed by a deliberately biased screen is reported; no top-k design
  among 30 audited discards bounds the false-discard rate at 9.5% with 95% confidence, and 59 bound it at
  5.0%. Negative control: a screen with no bias passes.
- CX-293: a comparison among survivors does not validate the screen.
- Negative controls for the audit itself: an audit whose sample is drawn among discards after scoring by a
  rule correlated with the score (coverage of the planted rate falls below 95% in battery (b), which the gate
  test must detect); a plant marked in the population listing (the envelope scan fails).
- Determinism: the record is a pure function of the ledger at a frontier; identical twice and across worker
  counts.

## Considerations

- RISK-37 (a screen discards the winner; high; owner Atelier lead): this audit and the trust rule that
  consumes it are the named mitigations.
- The bound is a statement about the audited strata on this population, on the stated tier. It is never a
  claim about designs in general, and with few audited discards it is loose; the report prints m beside it.
- OPEN-ATEL-5: classes with no earlier specialist result have no eligible planted winner; the control is
  `not-run` there.
- The planted winner costs one design's evaluation through the cascade; the audited discards cost the audit
  lane's tenth. Both are charged to the campaign.
