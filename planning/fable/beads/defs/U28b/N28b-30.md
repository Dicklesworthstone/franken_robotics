## Background

The cheapest stage of the cascade excludes designs by static analysis and by module contracts before any
simulation: "this stance cannot hold the payload", "this bus cannot power these actuators", "this body
cannot pay for the climb". An exclusion is the strongest thing a cheap analysis can say, and a wrong one is
invisible: the excluded design is never simulated, so nothing contradicts the analysis. The plan's third
screening audit, Atelier's own, makes it visible. **Prune soundness:** an exclusion made by an analysis or a
contract is wrong if any discarded design is independently feasible under the same mission, even when the
eventual winner survives. Pruned designs are sampled and run at the shared-policy rung or above. One feasible
pruned design suspends that pruning rule for the campaign and files the case as a negative-control seed.
Tier: Solid.

The standing example is the shared bus. Two fingers each need one powered tick; the bus powers one finger
per tick. With a one-tick deadline no joint schedule exists; with two ticks exactly two exist. A rule that
adds both peak currents rejects the two-tick design, which is feasible. The correct analysis keeps the joint
time-indexed contract, and a prune is rejected if any discarded design is feasible under the same mission.
Two more plausible prunes that are wrong, from the same list: a stance screen made of the support polygon
plus total actuator capacity accepts 1,596 bodies of 11,520 that the exact classifier rejects (the opposite
error, and equally a reason to audit); and a quasi-static energy exclusion without approach energy excludes a
crossing that 0.05 J of approach kinetic energy makes possible (an 8 kg body on a 12 mm rise needs
8 × 9.81 × 0.012 = 0.94176 J against a 0.90 J budget).

This bead serves BET-05 (static analysis prunes bad designs before simulation and does not throw away a
winner) by testing the second half of the bet inside every campaign.

## Technical approach

**1. Pruning rules.** Every exclusion at the static stage names the rule that made it: `(analysis or
contract identifier, version, bound type, authority model)`, for example `(A-23 shared-bus schedule, 2,
exact, joint time-indexed)` or `(A-20 energy budget, 1, outer, quasi-static with approach energy)`. Only
`exact` and `outer` results may exclude (an `outer` result says everything it reports infeasible is
infeasible, for the model it encloses). An exclusion from an analysis whose model does not enclose a
declared state or energy source is refused at source with `FROB-REF-551` (repairs: bind the missing source;
or accept `unresolved`), so it never becomes a prune.

**2. The sample.** At stage close, for each rule that excluded at least one design in a stratum, the
controller takes the s excluded designs with the smallest audit key
h(d) = Philox(campaign seed; DesignId; rule identifier; "prune-audit"), with
s = min(excluded, max(5, ⌈excluded/10⌉)) (DESIGN default, a manifest field). The key depends on identities
only, so the sample cannot be steered.

**3. Running a pruned design.** Each sampled design is run at the first rung that admits a scorer for it
(shared policy, baseline or sampling controller; the scorer-admission order), on the stage's fixed address
schedule at a fixed n, under the mission's task and requirement. This is the one sanctioned exception to
"no stage runs on a design the previous stage refused": the request carries the override `prune-audit:<rule>`
and that override is recorded on every result of the run (INV-ATEL-01). These runs never enter a ranking and
are never cancelled.

**4. "Independently feasible".** An exclusion claims that no witness can exist. A sampled design contradicts
it when (a) at least one of its episodes satisfies the mission's requirement, and (b) that episode's world
address is replayed on the referee and the requirement is still satisfied there (so the witness is not an
artifact of a fast tier). The confirmed episode is a witness trace. A design that merely scores well without
satisfying the requirement is not a contradiction.

**5. On a confirmed witness.** In one ledger transaction: the rule is *suspended for the campaign* (it makes
no further exclusions; every design it excluded in this campaign is re-admitted to the stage that follows,
in audit-key order, budget permitting, otherwise the campaign's disposition says so); a `prune-unsound`
evidence record is written (rule, design, mission, the witness trace's run and address, the analysis's
finding that claimed infeasibility); the case is filed as a negative-control seed in the conformance
registry (a fixture consisting of the design, the mission and the expected verdict "feasible", which the
analysis must not exclude from then on); and the stage-1 control record lists the failure. The campaign's
results are not labelled `screen-biased` for this alone when the re-admitted designs have all been
evaluated; they are when the budget prevented it.

**6. With no witness found.** For each rule, with m sampled designs run and none feasible, the rate of
wrongly pruned designs among that rule's exclusions is at most 1 − δ^(1/m) at confidence 1 − δ (45.1% at
m = 5 and 9.5% at m = 30 for δ = 0.05; the bound is loose at small m and is printed as such). The audit is a
potency check with a stated reach, not a proof of soundness.

**7. The planted unsound prune (for GATE-ATEL-09).** The conformance fixture adds a deliberately wrong rule
to a seeded campaign: the additive-peak bus rule (it sums peak currents and compares with the bus limit),
together with the two-finger design at a two-tick deadline, which that rule excludes and which is feasible
(exactly two joint schedules exist). The audit must sample it (the fixture makes it the only exclusion of
that rule), run it, confirm the witness on the referee, suspend the rule and file the case, in 100 of 100
planted campaigns.

**8. Layout and determinism.** `crates/frob-opt/src/cascade/prune_audit.rs`. The sample and the actions are
pure functions of the ledger and the manifest (D0); the runs have their episodes' determinism class.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every static exclusion in a campaign names its rule, bound type and authority model; an `inner` result
      never excludes.
- [ ] The sample per rule follows the size rule and is identical under any ordering of the population.
- [ ] Sampled pruned designs run with the `prune-audit` override recorded on every result and never enter a
      ranking.
- [ ] A witness is counted only after its referee replay satisfies the requirement.
- [ ] On a confirmed witness the rule is suspended, its exclusions are re-admitted, the evidence record and
      the negative-control seed exist, and the stage-1 control shows the failure.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-09, prune clause (TARGET): a planted unsound prune is caught in 100 of 100 planted
      campaigns (the count is recorded in the gate receipt written by BD-345).
- [ ] A sound rule on the same fixtures (the joint time-indexed bus contract at a one-tick deadline) is not
      suspended in any clean campaign.
- [ ] The bound for the no-witness case is printed with m.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-opt --test prune_audit`:

- `two_finger_bus_counts`: one powered tick each, one finger per tick: zero joint schedules at a one-tick
  deadline, exactly two at two ticks (exhaustive enumeration).
- `additive_peak_rule_wrongly_excludes`: the planted rule excludes the two-tick design; the audit confirms
  the witness; the rule is suspended.
- `joint_contract_not_suspended`: the correct rule excludes the one-tick design and no witness exists.
- `energy_exclusion_with_and_without_approach`: 0.94176 J against 0.90 J excludes; with 0.05 J of approach
  kinetic energy bound, the analysis does not exclude; an exclusion issued with approach energy unbound is
  refused at source (`FROB-REF-551`) and never reaches the prune audit.
- `sample_size_rule`: excluded = 3 → 3; 40 → 5; 200 → 20.
- `sample_is_keyed`: permutation-invariant.
- `witness_requires_referee_replay`: a fast-tier success that fails on the referee is not a witness.
- `suspension_readmits_in_key_order`; `override_recorded_on_results`.
- `inner_bound_cannot_exclude`.
- `no_witness_bound`: m = 5 → 1 − 0.05^(1/5) = 0.4507; m = 30 → 0.0950.

### End-to-end script and logging

Step `prune` of `scripts/e2e/screening_audit.sh` (bead N28b-31): a seeded campaign with the planted
additive-peak rule enabled through the conformance fixture and one without; `frob opt cascade`, then
`frob evidence trace` on the `prune-unsound` record and `frob report campaign`; asserts the sampled design,
the witness's referee replay, the suspension, the re-admission count and the filed seed; in the clean
campaign asserts no suspension. Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, wall time, evidence blocks, per
rule excluded and sampled counts, per sampled design the requirement verdicts on the fast tier and the
referee, the action, and on failure the first sampled design whose outcome differs from the plan.

### Known answers and negative controls

- CX-303 / KA-687 (A-23): two fingers on one bus: zero schedules at a one-tick deadline, exactly two at two
  ticks; the additive-peak rule rejects the feasible two-tick design.
- CX-306 / KA-684 (A-20): 0.94176 J against a 0.90 J budget excludes the passive crossing; 0.05 J of
  approach kinetic energy removes the exclusion; negative control: an exclusion issued with approach energy
  unbound.
- CX-302 / KA-682 (A-08): the polygon-and-total-capacity screen accepts 1,596 of 11,520 bodies that the
  exact classifier rejects: the mirror-image error, listed so that the audit is not mistaken for a complete
  check of an analysis (it tests exclusions only; wrong acceptances are caught downstream by simulation).
- Negative control for the audit: with the audit's referee replay disabled, a planted fast-tier artifact
  (a design that "succeeds" only through created energy) would suspend a sound rule; the test asserts that
  with the replay enabled it does not.
- Determinism: identical samples and actions twice and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- The correct bound for m = 5 is 45%, which shows how little five samples prove; the default sample grows
  with the number of exclusions, and a rule that excludes many designs is audited more heavily.
- Suspending a rule can flood later stages with designs; the cost estimate printed after a suspension shows
  the new plan, and the campaign may end "inconclusive at this budget".
- KILL-STATIC (static-analysis verdicts downgraded to advisory lints, decided on the corpus at M3) removes
  exclusions altogether; this audit then has nothing to sample and reports `not-run: no-exclusions`.
- A filed negative-control seed is a permanent test: the analysis that produced the wrong exclusion cannot
  be re-enabled for that mission class until it passes the seed.
