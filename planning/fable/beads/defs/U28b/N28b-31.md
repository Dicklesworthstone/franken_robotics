## Background

A screen can throw away the winner and nothing downstream will notice. Three audits make the loss
observable: the stratified audit of discards and the planted winner (one mechanism, verb `nemesis.audit`),
and Atelier's own prune-soundness audit. On top of them sits the rule that Atelier does not trust a rung
without a passed audit. Each has unit tests in its own bead. This bead is the script that shows the whole
capability from the user's side on a running system: a clean campaign whose audits pass, a campaign whose
screen is biased and is caught, a campaign whose audit record is missing, a campaign with an unsound prune,
and the refusals. It also runs the two gate batteries and writes their receipts' inputs. The gates:

- GATE-ATEL-09 (known answer; M4): in 100 of 100 planted campaigns a stage with no audit record, a missed
  planted winner or an audited miss rate above its tolerance has its rung set to `uninformative` in the
  affected strata and its results labelled `screen-biased`; a planted unsound prune is caught in 100 of 100;
  no audit episode is ever cancelled.
- GATE-NEMESIS-07 (known answer; M4): a planted winner removed by a biased screen is reported in 100 of 100
  runs; the stratified bound covers the planted false-discard rate in at least 95% of 1,000 synthetic
  campaigns.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/screening_audit.sh`, its steps, assertions and log; invocation of the two gate batteries.

Out: the mechanisms (N28b-13, N28b-29, N28b-30, BD-345); the cascade's own end-to-end script (N28b-28), which
calls this one as its `stage3` step.

## Technical approach

The script is plain shell over the real `frob` binary, idempotent in a temporary store, seed-explicit
(`--seed 13`), on `batch-cpu` with the reduced seeded population (81 designs, three classes, fixture scorers
labelled `synthetic`).

| Step | What it runs | What it asserts |
|---|---|---|
| `clean` | `frob opt cascade` on the clean population; `frob nemesis audit --campaign C --stage 3 --json` | Exit 0; every stratum `passed` or `under-sampled` with its bound printed; the planted winner `reached-finalists`; no label; modes unchanged by the audit |
| `seal` | Reads the campaign manifest before and after `--reveal` | The seal is present before the first score; no envelope of the run identifies the plant before the reveal; the reveal verifies |
| `biased` | The same campaign with the fixture's biased screen (one design family under-scored at the first rung) | The audit reports the plant `lost at stage 3`; results carry `screen-biased`; the stage-3 rung is `uninformative` in the plant's stratum; that stratum's discards are re-opened in audit-key order; other strata untouched; exit 2 |
| `miss_rate` | A population whose discards contain true top-k designs at a planted rate of 15% in one stratum | That stratum's bound exceeds the tolerance; same actions as `biased` for that stratum |
| `missing` | The clean campaign with the stage-3 audit record removed by the conformance fixture before the trust rule runs | The rung is `uninformative` in every stratum of the stage and results are `screen-biased` |
| `prune` | A campaign with the planted additive-peak bus rule and the two-finger design | The pruned design is sampled, runs under the `prune-audit` override, its witness replays on the referee, the rule is suspended, its exclusions are re-admitted, and the negative-control seed is filed |
| `refusals` | A job cancellation aimed at one audit episode; an audit request with a planted winner that has no rank record | `FROB-REF-546` with exit 3; `FROB-REF-566` with exit 3 |
| `not_run` | A class with no eligible planted winner | The plant's control is `not-run`; the report says "k survivors" for that class |
| `report` | `frob report campaign` on the `biased` campaign | The screening audit per stage and stratum (m, x, bound, tolerance), the planted-winner outcome, the label and the list of fallbacks that fired are printed |
| `gates` | `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test gate_atel_09` and `--test gate_nemesis_07` | 100 of 100 planted campaigns handled; 100 of 100 prunes caught; zero cancelled audit episodes; 100 of 100 lost plants reported; coverage of the planted rate at least 95% of 1,000 |

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, sources the shared helper, runs every step against the real binary and exits
      non-zero on any failed assertion.
- [ ] Each step asserts the values in the table, including exit codes and refusal codes.
- [ ] The `gates` step produces the measured counts that the gate receipts of BD-345 and N28b-29 cite.
- [ ] Planted omissions are caught: with the trust rule disabled, with the audit drawn after scoring, and
      with audit episodes made cancellable (test-only switches), the script fails at `biased`, `gates` and
      `refusals` respectively.
- [ ] The script is idempotent and logs as specified.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own beyond a shell lint and `--list-steps`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/screening_audit.sh` writes `target/e2e-logs/screening_audit/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every
line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (BD-345, N28b-29 or N28b-30 by step), `step`, `event`, the exact command line,
exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block, measured values with units and thresholds (per
stratum m, x, the bound against the tolerance; re-opened design count; cancelled audit episodes against 0;
gate tallies against 100, 100, 100 and 950), digests of the audit records, and on failure the first stratum
or campaign whose outcome differs from its plan with the store path to reproduce it. The final `summary`
line gives assertions passed and failed and names GATE-ATEL-09 and GATE-NEMESIS-07. The seal's salt is never
written to a log before the reveal step.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-706 / KA-668: 30 clean audited discards bound the false-discard rate at 9.5% with 95% confidence and 59
  at 5.0%; a biased screen's removal of the planted winner is reported; an unbiased screen passes.
- CX-293: a decisive comparison among survivors does not clear a campaign whose screen lost the plant; the
  `biased` step checks that the label is set even though the finalists' comparison settled.
- The three planted omissions are the script's own negative controls.
- Determinism: the script is run twice and the audit records' digests must agree; worker counts 1 and 8.

## Considerations

- The reduced population gives small strata, so several will print `under-sampled`; that is the correct
  output and the script asserts it instead of hiding it by lowering the tolerance.
- The `missing` step needs the conformance fixture to delete a ledger record between stages; it is a
  test-only capability that the release binary refuses.
- If KILL-STATIC has fired there are no static exclusions and the `prune` step expects
  `not-run: no-exclusions`.
