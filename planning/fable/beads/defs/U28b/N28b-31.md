## Background

A screen can throw away the winner and nothing downstream will notice. Three audits make the loss
observable: the stratified audit of discards and the planted winner (one mechanism, verb `nemesis.audit`),
and Atelier's own prune-soundness audit. On top of them sits the rule that Atelier does not trust a rung
without a passed audit. Each has unit tests in its own bead. This bead is the script that shows the whole
capability from the user's side on a running system: a clean campaign whose audits pass, a campaign whose
screen is biased and is caught, a campaign whose audit record is missing, a campaign with an unsound prune,
and the refusals. It also runs the two gate batteries and writes their receipts' inputs. The gates:

- GATE-ATEL-09 (known answer; M4): in 100 of 100 planted campaigns a stage with no audit record, a missed
  planted winner or an audited miss rate above its tolerance has its rung set to `uninformative` in the
  affected strata and its results labelled `screen-biased`; a planted unsound prune is caught in 100 of 100;
  no audit episode is ever cancelled.
- GATE-NEMESIS-07 (known answer; M4): a planted winner removed by a biased screen is reported in 100 of 100
  runs; the stratified bound covers the planted false-discard rate in at least 95% of 1,000 synthetic
  campaigns.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/screening_audit.sh`, its steps, assertions and log: what the cascade does with a screening
audit (the trust rule, the label, the re-opening of discards, the prune audit, the cancellation refusal); the
battery of GATE-ATEL-09; the invocation of the audit's own script as one step.

The audit's vocabulary is that of its record `frob.nemesis.audit/1` (N26b-02, N26b-04): stratum verdicts
`passed`, `screen-biased`, `bound-above-tolerance`, `void`; plant status `sealed`, `survived`, `missed`,
`not-run`, `commitment-mismatch`; and `audit-missing` for a stage with no record. The verb is
`frob nemesis audit` with the actions `prepare` (`--action prepare`) and `run` (the default, which takes no
`--action`; NJL-01's action rule); `--campaign` is accepted as an alias of the positional campaign. What the cascade does with each outcome is BD-345's trust rule.

Out, with the bead that has it: the end-to-end test of the audit itself and the record of GATE-NEMESIS-07, that
is the planted winner reported in 100 of 100 biased runs, the unbiased screen passing in 100 of 100, the
coverage of the planted false-discard rate over 1,000 campaigns, the known-answer rows 9.5% and 5.0%, the
commitment before scores and the refusal `FROB-REF-566` (N26b-07, `scripts/e2e/nemesis_audit.sh`, a dependency).
This script was drafted with a second measurement of that gate (`--test gate_nemesis_07`); one measurement and
one gate row remain, there, and this script calls that one. Also out: the mechanisms (N28b-13; the audit's
lane, bound, plant, record and verb, N26b-01 to N26b-05, which superseded N28b-29; N28b-30; BD-345); the
cascade's own end-to-end script (N28b-28), which calls this one as its `stage3` step.

## Technical approach

The script is plain shell over the real `frob` binary, idempotent in a temporary store, seed-explicit
(`--seed 13`), on `batch-cpu` with the reduced seeded population (81 designs, three classes, fixture scorers
labelled `synthetic`). Every `frob opt cascade` and `frob nemesis audit` call carries its own idempotency key
(`--key sa-<step>`, for example `sa-clean`, `sa-biased`, `sa-miss-rate`, `sa-missing`, `sa-prune`,
`sa-not-run`): the `missing` step re-runs the clean campaign's request and would otherwise receive the clean
step's stored receipt instead of a new run.

| Step | What it runs | What it asserts |
|---|---|---|
| `clean` | `frob opt cascade` on the clean population; `frob nemesis audit C --stage 3 --key k --json` | Every stratum `passed`, or `bound-above-tolerance` with its bound U and `audits_needed` printed; the plant `survived`; no `screen-biased` label; modes unchanged in `passed` strata; in a `bound-above-tolerance` stratum the rung is `uninformative` with its discards re-opened, no label, and the report line "audit insufficient" (BD-345); exit 0 when every stratum passed, 2 otherwise |
| `seal` | Reads the campaign manifest before and after `--reveal` | The seal is present before the first score; no envelope of the run identifies the plant before the reveal; the reveal verifies |
| `biased` | The same campaign with the fixture's biased screen (one design family under-scored at the first rung) | The audit reports plant status `missed` at stage 3; results carry `screen-biased`; the stage-3 rung is `uninformative` in every stratum of the plant's design family and a bypass is recorded for the screen; those strata's discards are re-opened in audit-key order; strata of other families untouched; exit 2 |
| `miss_rate` | A population whose discards contain true top-k designs at a planted rate of 15% in one stratum | That stratum's verdict is `screen-biased` (observed x/m above the tolerance); the same actions as `biased`, for that stratum only |
| `missing` | The clean campaign with the stage-3 audit record removed by the conformance fixture before the trust rule runs | `audit::bypass_in_force` returns `audit-missing`; the rung is `uninformative` in every stratum of the stage and results are `screen-biased` |
| `prune` | A campaign with the planted additive-peak bus rule (CX-303's plant `a23-additive-peak`: the binary built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-analyze/a23-additive-peak`, one control per build, N28b-30) and the two-finger design; `scripts/e2e/prune_audit.sh` (N28b-30) runs first as a child | The pruned design is sampled, runs under the `prune-audit` override, its witness replays on the referee, the rule is suspended, its exclusions are re-admitted, and the negative-control seed is filed |
| `refusals` | A job cancellation aimed at one audit episode | `FROB-REF-546` with exit 3. (The refusal `FROB-REF-566`, a planted winner with no rank record, is asserted by step 7 of `nemesis_audit.sh`, N26b-07, and is not repeated here) |
| `not_run` | A class with no eligible planted winner | Plant status `not-run`; the stage control is `not-run: no-planted-winner`; the report says "k survivors" for that class |
| `report` | `frob report campaign` on the `biased` campaign | The screening audit per stage and stratum (m, x, bound, tolerance, verdict), the plant status, the label, the bypass and the list of fallbacks that fired are printed |
| `gates` | `FROB_TEST_SEED=13 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-ATEL-09` (the GATE-ATEL-09 entry of the known-answer tier target, with its registered controls run in the same receipt, the form BD-345 records from; there is no per-gate target) | 100 of 100 planted campaigns handled; 100 of 100 prunes caught; zero cancelled audit episodes; the receipt's hash is logged, and the script writes no gate record (BD-345 records the value from that receipt with the `record_gate` example) |
| `audit_gate` | `scripts/e2e/nemesis_audit.sh` (N26b-07), run as a child with the same build and seed list | It exits 0 and its summary names GATE-NEMESIS-07 with 100 of 100 lost plants reported and the planted rate covered in at least 950 of 1,000 campaigns. This script copies those tallies into its own log as context and records nothing for that gate |

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, sources the shared helper, runs every step against the real binary and exits
      non-zero on any failed assertion.
- [ ] Each step asserts the values in the table, including exit codes and refusal codes.
- [ ] The `gates` step produces the measured counts that the gate receipt of BD-345 cites for
      GATE-ATEL-09; the `audit_gate` step shows the audit's own script passing on the same build; the row of
      GATE-NEMESIS-07 is written only by N26b-07.
- [ ] Planted omissions are caught: with the trust rule disabled, with the audit drawn after scoring, and
      with audit episodes made cancellable (test-only switches), the script fails at `biased`, `gates` and
      `refusals` respectively.
- [ ] The script is idempotent and logs as specified.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own beyond a shell lint and `--list-steps`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/screening_audit.sh` writes `target/e2e-logs/screening_audit/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every
line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (BD-345, N26b-05 or N28b-30 by step), `step`, `event`, the exact command line,
exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block, measured values with units and thresholds (per
stratum m, x, the bound against the tolerance; re-opened design count; cancelled audit episodes against 0;
gate tallies against 100 and 100 for GATE-ATEL-09, and the child script's tallies against 100 and 950 as
context), digests of the audit records, and on failure the first stratum or campaign whose outcome differs
from its plan with the store path to reproduce it. The final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed
and names GATE-ATEL-09 as the gate touched; GATE-NEMESIS-07 is named only as read from the child script. The seal's salt is never
written to a log before the reveal step.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-706 / KA-668: 30 clean audited discards bound the false-discard rate at 9.5% with 95% confidence and 59
  at 5.0%; a biased screen's removal of the planted winner is reported; an unbiased screen passes.
- CX-293: a decisive comparison among survivors does not clear a campaign whose screen lost the plant; the
  `biased` step checks that the label is set even though the finalists' comparison settled.
- The three planted omissions are the script's own negative controls.
- Determinism: the script is run twice and the audit records' digests must agree; worker counts 1 and 8.

## Considerations

- The reduced population gives small strata, so several will print `bound-above-tolerance` (the lane merges
  strata upward before any score, but 81 designs cannot supply 59 audits per stratum); that is the correct
  output, with its consequence (the rung `uninformative` there, no label), and the script asserts it instead
  of hiding it by lowering the tolerance.
- The `missing` step needs the conformance fixture to delete a ledger record between stages; it is a
  test-only capability that the release binary refuses.
- If KILL-STATIC has fired there are no static exclusions and the `prune` step expects
  `not-run: no-exclusions`.
