## Background

Revalidation decides which stored verdicts survive an edit. Getting it wrong in one direction wastes compute
(an unnecessary invalidation); getting it wrong in the other reports a verdict that is no longer true (unsafe
reuse, a false accept). GATE-ATEL-11 (known answer) measures both on a campaign the implementers never saw:
revalidation on the frozen 30-amendment campaign: every decision equals full recomputation; zero unsafe
reuse; the stale-key mutant is caught; unnecessary invalidations are at most 20% of unaffected verdicts,
counted and reported. The gate has rows at three milestones: the dependency grades on mission cells at the
first, the dependency grades in general at the third, and the full gate at the fourth. This bead is the
fourth-milestone run, on our own engine, across every verdict kind the studio stores by then, and it is also
the pilot with a hard stop that the plan requires for grade 3:

"A campaign of 30 amendments is frozen before any implementation sees it: visual-only edits, camera timing,
sole geometry, floor law, torque, shared noise, load authority, facility edits, and one apparently cosmetic
change that advances a packet by one control tick. A separate session implements the incremental dependency
graph. Fresh solving, grades 1 and 2, and grade 3 are run from the same cache state and dirty fraction;
every decision is replayed; every incorrectly inherited success and every unnecessary invalidation is
recorded. KILL-REUSE fires on any unsafe reuse, or if grade 3 saves less than a fifth of the recheck cost
that grade 2 leaves (TARGET). Fallback: grades 1 and 2 only."

The plan's prior, from the kept negative result, is that grade 3 will show little or no advantage over a
correctly keyed dependency graph. The run exists to find out on a richer plant, at a fixed price, with the
stop rule written first.

## Scope

In: the content rules of the M4 campaign; the four arms and the common starting state; the measurements; the
stale-key mutant; the gate record; the cost figures handed to the KILL-REUSE decision.

Out: the engine and rules (BD-292, BD-347, N28b-34); authoring procedure and sealing mechanics (the M3
campaign bead, repeated here by reference to its method); the decision (N28b-36).

## Technical approach

**1. The campaign (a new sealed set for M4).** Thirty amendments, authored by a session that implements
neither the engine nor the incumbent, each with a truth recomputed from scratch by exact or independently
replayed evaluation, sealed by hash before any arm runs, and revealed only after the receipt is written. A
campaign used for an earlier gate run is public and is used here only as development material. Composition
(the plan's categories; counts are DESIGN):

| Category | Count | Verdict kinds exercised |
|---|---|---|
| Visual-only edits (names, labels, colours no sensor reads) | 3 | all kinds; must inherit with zero evaluations |
| Camera and packet timing, including one apparently cosmetic change that advances a packet by one control tick | 5 | mission inclusions and exclusions; design comparisons whose controller reads the packet |
| Sole and contact geometry | 3 | static stability findings; comparisons; mission cells |
| Floor and material law | 3 | comparisons (model-form change: no pure direction); energy findings |
| Torque and actuator limits, one of them a motor swap that changes torque and mass together | 4 | actuator-envelope findings; inclusions; a mixed change |
| Shared noise (independent reads to one shared fault bit) | 2 | observability findings; alias exclusions |
| Load authority (independent forces against passive springs) | 2 | stance inclusions and exclusions (policy class shrunk and enlarged) |
| Facility and world edits (a doorway widened; a catalog part added; an obstacle removed) | 5 | feasibility against optimality; route findings |
| Hypothesis support (a gain added close to an existing one; a gain removed) | 2 | inclusions withdrawn; exclusions kept |
| Controller-only and reward-only edits | 1 | every model analysis must be reused |

Each amendment record lists the edit, the affected and unaffected verdicts by truth, and the expected
status of every obligation.

**2. The four arms, from one starting state.** Before each amendment the store is restored to the same
snapshot (the base designs, missions, scenes and all their stored verdicts, analyses and episodes), so every
arm sees the same cache state and the same dirty fraction.

| Arm | What decides | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| F: fresh solving | Everything recomputed; no reuse | The reference for decisions and for cost |
| G12: grades 1 and 2 | Identity and dependency support | The Solid default |
| G123: grades 1 to 3 | Adds the sixteen polarity rules, flag on, rules enabled in a scratch registry | The Frontier candidate |
| I: incremental incumbent | A hermetic dependency graph keyed by field, written by a separate session | The comparator: does anything beat a well-keyed graph? |

**3. Measurements (per amendment and pooled).**
- *Decision equality:* every verdict's status after the amendment in arms G12, G123 and I equals arm F's
  (and the sealed truth).
- *Unsafe reuse:* the number of verdicts an arm kept that the truth withdraws (an incorrectly inherited
  success). Tolerance: zero, in every arm that is to remain enabled.
- *Unnecessary invalidations:* verdicts an arm reran whose result is identical to the stored one, as a share
  of the truly unaffected verdicts. Threshold: at most 20%.
- *Recheck cost:* the measured cost (CPU-seconds and GPU-seconds, intervals over three repetitions) of the
  reruns each arm performed. Let C₂ be the pooled recheck cost of G12 and C₃ that of G123. The saving of
  grade 3 is (C₂ − C₃)/C₂.
- *The stale-key mutant:* an engine variant whose key or support omits delivery time is run on the timing
  amendments; the gate requires that the campaign's comparison with arm F catches it (it keeps a stale
  promotion where delivery is delayed across a decision instant).

**4. Replay.** Every decision of every arm is replayed: for a preserved verdict the licence is checked
offline (masks against supports; rule names against the registry), and for a rerun the new evidence is
recomputed twice and compared.

**5. The record.** A gate receipt for GATE-ATEL-11 (commit, hardware fingerprint, seed, the campaign's
hash): per arm the counts of equal decisions out of the total, unsafe reuses, unnecessary invalidations with
their share, and costs; the mutant's detection; and for KILL-REUSE: C₂, C₃, the saving with its interval, and
the list of rules that fired with the count of obligations each preserved. The campaign is then revealed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The M4 campaign's hash is committed before any arm runs; its authoring session implemented neither the
      engine nor the incumbent; truths were recomputed independently.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-11 (TARGET) is recorded with measured values: every decision of arm G12 equals full
      recomputation; zero unsafe reuse in G12; the stale-key mutant is caught; unnecessary invalidations at
      most 20% of unaffected verdicts, counted and reported.
- [ ] The same four numbers are recorded for arm G123 and arm I.
- [ ] All arms started every amendment from the same store snapshot (snapshot hash logged per run).
- [ ] C₂, C₃ and the grade-3 saving are recorded with intervals, and the per-rule preservation counts.
- [ ] Every preserved verdict's licence re-checks offline; every rerun reproduces.
- [ ] The controller-only amendment shows exact reuse of every model analysis in G12; the mass-changing
      motor swap is classified `Mixed` and sent to grade 4 in G123.
- [ ] The inputs of the KILL-REUSE decision are filed.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test gate_atel_11` (the scoring code on a public development campaign of
six amendments with known truths):

- `decision_equality_scoring`; `unsafe_reuse_counts_even_if_later_restored` (an inherited success that a
  later amendment happens to make true again is still counted).
- `unnecessary_invalidation_share`: 3 of 20 unaffected verdicts rerun identically → 15%, pass; 5 of 20 → 25%,
  fail.
- `grade3_saving`: C₂ = 400 s, C₃ = 340 s → 15% < 20% → reported as below the kill threshold.
- `snapshot_restored_between_amendments`: store digest equal before every amendment in every arm.
- `stale_key_mutant_caught_on_tick3_delay`: the mutant keeps the first decision's promotion when delivery
  moves from tick 1 to tick 3; scoring flags one unsafe reuse.
- `licence_offline_check`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/revalidate_frozen_campaign.sh` sources the shared helper of STD-E2E. On the public development
campaign it runs the four arms through the real binary (`frob result revalidate … --grades 2`, `--grades 3`
with the flag, the fresh-solve path with `--no-memo`, and the incumbent harness), restoring the store
snapshot between amendments, then the scoring and the mutant run. The sealed gate run uses
`scripts/exp/revalidate_frozen_campaign_run.sh` with the same steps. Log
`target/e2e-logs/revalidate_frozen_campaign/<utc>.ndjson` with transcript: per amendment and arm the command,
exit code, wall time, the evidence block, the snapshot digest, changed fields, per obligation the grade,
licence and status, solver and episode calls, measured rerun cost with units, the reference decision, and
flags for unsafe reuse and unnecessary invalidation; pooled counts against their thresholds (unsafe = 0;
unnecessary ≤ 20%); C₂, C₃ and the saving against 1/5; and on failure the first verdict whose status differs
from arm F with the paths to replay it. Final `summary` line naming GATE-ATEL-11.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-699 (camera fixture): 2, 0, 0, 1 scene-pair evaluations and 1, 0, 0, 1 verdict recomputations across
  the four states, each verdict equal to a fresh replay; the key without delivery time keeps a stale
  promotion (CX-315).
- KA-700 and KA-701 as development amendments (doorway; hair-widening of the hypothesis support).
- Negative controls: the stale-key mutant; an arm that inherits on hash equality alone (must show unsafe
  reuse on the hypothesis-support amendments); an arm that invalidates everything on any edit (decision
  equality holds, unnecessary invalidations are near 100% and the gate fails on that clause).
- Proof obligation PO-ATEL-07 (incremental evaluation with the memo graph equals full recomputation) is
  exercised here on the campaign in addition to its random-edit battery.
- Determinism: arm decisions are D0; costs are measured three times and reported as intervals.

## Considerations

- KILL-REUSE (decided in N28b-36): any unsafe reuse in this gate, or grade 3 saving less than a fifth of the
  recheck cost grade 2 leaves. Fallback: grades 1 and 2 only.
- Thirty amendments are few; one category can dominate the cost totals. The record therefore gives the
  saving per category as well as pooled, and the decision reads both.
- The incumbent arm keeps the comparison honest: if a plain dependency graph matches grade 3's saving, the
  implication machinery has no measured value even where it is safe.
- A sealed campaign is spent by one gate run. A repair cycle after a failure needs a newly authored campaign.
- RISK-38 (an incomplete memo key serves a stale verdict): this gate and the shadow recomputation of the memo
  store are its two named countermeasures.
