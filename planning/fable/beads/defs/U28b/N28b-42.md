## Background

At the second milestone the memo graph starts serving *episodes*: the full record of a world's trajectory,
keyed by its full identity (model, scene, task, controller or policy, world address, tier, kernel versions,
effort profile, device family), served only within same-backend D2 (or D1 where the device family holds it)
and never across a D3 boundary. Episodes are where a stale hit would do the most damage, because every
comparison, scorecard and verdict is folded from them. GATE-ATEL-12's episode part (M2) therefore repeats
the analysis part's obligation one level down: PO-ATEL-07 (incremental evaluation with the memo graph equals
full recomputation, over random edit sequences, with shadow recomputation in production), and the tripwire
(a shadow mismatch stops serving within one hit). This bead is that battery and the episode steps of the
memo end-to-end script. Tier: Solid.

The seed bead that memoizes episodes owns the mechanism and its own done-condition (an episode recorded on
another fingerprint is not served; the eviction oracle). This bead owns the evidence that the mechanism
never changes an answer.

## Scope

In: the episode lane of the edit-sequence battery; what is compared; the expected reuse pattern per edit
kind; class-boundary controls; the shadow tripwire on a planted nondeterministic kernel; the partly memoized
comparison; the `episodes` step of `scripts/e2e/memo_graph.sh`; the M2 gate record.

Out: episode memoization itself and the eviction oracle (BD-220); replay and digests (BD-203); the policy
(N28b-38); the analysis part (N28b-40); the nine interference tests on a replayed campaign (BD-350).

## Technical approach

**1. The lane.** The generator of the analysis part is reused with the same seeds. After every step of a
sequence two things are run on the reference task of the template (stand and recover for legged templates,
threshold delivery for wheeled ones): `sim.run` of 16 worlds for a 2 s horizon at telemetry level T0 with
digests, and a paired comparison of the current version against the previous one on those 16 addresses. Two
lanes, as before: *incremental* (one persistent store, mode `episodes`) and *fresh* (a new store per step,
serving off). Sizes (DESIGN split of the obligation's 10⁴ sequences; the analysis lane already runs all of
them): seeds 0 to 999 on `batch-cpu` in `f64` and in `f32`; seeds 0 to 99 on each GPU backend present on the
machine.

**2. What is compared after every step.** For each world: the chained state digest of every chunk and the
T0 metrics (integers, so exact); for the comparison: the canonical bytes of its result (statistic, confidence
sequence or interval, pair counts, verdict). Equality is required bit for bit *within a backend* (each
backend's incremental lane against its own fresh lane): that is the determinism class in which hits are
served. Across backends nothing is served, and nothing is compared here.

**3. Expected reuse pattern (asserted per step from the incremental lane's counters).**

| Step kind | Model analyses | Controller | Episodes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rename or display-only edit | Served | Served | Served (all 16) |
| Controller-only or reward-only edit | Served | Recomputed if the task interface changed, else served | Recomputed |
| Scene-only edit | Served | Served | Recomputed |
| Mass or geometry edit | Recomputed where read | Recomputed | Recomputed: a mass or geometry edit reuses no rollout |
| Revisit of an earlier version (edit then inverse) | Served | Served | Served (all 16) |

A step that serves an episode it should not (a mass edit with a served rollout) is a failure even if the
digests happen to agree, because it means a key input is missing.

**4. Class-boundary controls.**
- An episode recorded under one device fingerprint is requested under another (the conformance fixture
  presents a second fingerprint): it must be recomputed.
- An episode recorded on `batch-cpu` is requested on a GPU backend, and the reverse: recomputed (never across
  a D3 boundary).
- An episode recorded under the labelled D4 training profile is requested: there is no node (D4 results are
  not memoized).
- A changed kernel version or effort profile: recomputed.

**5. The tripwire.** With the determinism fixture's planted nondeterministic reduction compiled into one
kernel, episodes are run until a shadowed hit (one in 64 by key hash) is recomputed; the digests differ; the
episode kind's latch must be set in the same transaction, the next candidate episode hit must be computed,
and a determinism-violation record with both digests and both executions' fingerprints must exist. Analyses
keep being served.

**6. The partly memoized comparison.** A comparison of 256 pairs is computed three ways: all episodes fresh;
arm A fully served; a keyed random half of both arms served. Its canonical result must be byte-identical in
the three, and identical again when completion order is shuffled by random preemption (the statistic is a
pure function of per-address outcomes in address order).

**7. The gate record (episode part, M2).** Sequences and steps compared per backend with zero differences;
the reuse-pattern assertions; the four class-boundary controls; the tripwire; the three-way comparison;
commit, hardware fingerprints, seeds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] PO-ATEL-07, episode part: for seeds 0 to 999 on `batch-cpu` (both precisions) and 0 to 99 on each GPU
      backend, the incremental and fresh lanes agree bit for bit after every step; counts are recorded.
- [ ] The reuse pattern of the table holds at every step; no rollout is served after a mass or geometry edit.
- [ ] All four class-boundary controls recompute; none serves.
- [ ] The planted nondeterministic kernel trips the shadow comparison, the episode kind stops serving within
      one hit, and the violation record exists.
- [ ] The partly memoized comparison is byte-identical to the fresh one in all three forms and under
      shuffled completion.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-12's episode part is recorded with measured values; together with the analysis part the gate
      row is complete.
- [ ] Mutants are caught: an episode key without the world's delivery instants; without the effort profile;
      without the device family.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test memo_equivalence_episodes`:

- `episode_lane_smoke`: seeds 0 to 19 on `batch-cpu` in the default profile; the full ranges under the gate
  profile.
- `reuse_pattern_by_edit_kind`: one sequence per row of the table, counters asserted.
- `fingerprint_boundary_recomputes`; `backend_boundary_recomputes`; `d4_not_memoized`;
  `kernel_version_change_recomputes`; `effort_profile_change_recomputes`.
- `shadow_trips_on_nondeterministic_kernel`; `latch_within_one_hit`.
- `comparison_three_way_identical`; `comparison_identical_under_preemption`.
- `mutant_key_without_delivery_instants_caught` (a delayed packet then serves the old episode; the lanes
  differ); `mutant_key_without_effort_profile_caught`; `mutant_key_without_device_family_caught`.
- `revisit_serves_all`: an edit and its inverse → 16 of 16 episodes served, digests equal to the first run.

### End-to-end script and logging

Step `episodes` of `scripts/e2e/memo_graph.sh` (bead N28b-40): `frob sim run quad-a --tier batch-cpu --worlds
16 --horizon 2s --seed 17 --json` twice (second: 16 served); a rename then a run (16 served); a payload-mass
edit then a run (0 served); the inverse edit then a run (16 served, digests equal to the first run's); a run
with `--no-memo` (0 served, digests equal); the same on a GPU backend where present, with one cross-backend
request (0 served). Logged per STD-E2E: commands, exit codes, wall time, evidence blocks with determinism
class and fingerprint, per run the served and computed episode counts, per world the final chained digest
for the served and recomputed forms, counters from `frob store status`, and on failure the first world and
chunk whose digest differs with the artifact paths to replay it. Final `summary` line naming GATE-ATEL-12
(episode part).

### Known answers and negative controls

- The plan's statements of where reuse is and is not, as the table's rows.
- KA-699 / CX-315 at the episode level: delaying a camera packet from tick 1 to tick 3 must miss the episode
  key; the mutant key without delivery instants serves the stale episode.
- INV-ATEL-08 (a memo hit is bit-identical to a recomputation inside its determinism class) and INV-ATEL-02
  (a study's statistic does not depend on memo hits): the lanes and the three-way comparison are their tests.
- Negative controls: the three key mutants; the nondeterministic kernel; serving across fingerprints.
- Determinism lanes: each backend twice; worker counts 1 and 8; debug and release on `batch-cpu`; platforms
  compared only within a class that the device family holds.

## Considerations

- Cost: about 10⁴ steps × 16 worlds × 2 s on `batch-cpu` per precision in the gate profile; hours, not days.
  The GPU lanes are small on purpose: the property is about keys and serving rules, which are
  backend-independent, and the per-backend digest equality is covered by the replay gates.
- The ceiling on what this reuse is worth is stated by the plan: after a dynamics-affecting edit at most
  about a tenth of compute. The battery proves safety; it makes no speed claim.
- If the episode kind's latch is ever set in production, campaigns continue by recomputing; the cost shows
  in the reuse ledger's first column falling to the analyses' share.
- RISK-38 is the risk this battery addresses at the level where it would hurt most.
