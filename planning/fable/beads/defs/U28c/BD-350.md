## Background

Eleven decision cores (DC-01 to DC-11) steer the design studio: compiled decision rules for promotion, ladder
advancement, survivor margins, rare-failure bounds, experiment choice, failure attribution, reality bands, solver
effort, job dispatch, battery selection and kernel rewrites. Several touch the same quantity. The plan's
composition rule is: where two controllers touch the same quantity, one rule assigns it and one test shows the
rule holds. There are nine such pairs and therefore nine interference tests. Gate GATE-ATEL-14 (claim kind: Known
answer; milestone M4): the nine interference tests pass on a replayed campaign.

What "interference" means here: the output of one core (a verdict, a digest, a survivor set) changes because of
something another core did that it had no right to influence: the dispatcher changing an e-value by reordering
episodes; an e-process cancelling an audit episode and biasing calibration; a solver-effort rule reacting to queue
load and changing a digest. Each such effect would make results depend on timing or load, which destroys replay,
and with it the evidence ledger. The governing asymmetry (ADR-ATEL-03): reporting an artifact-dependent result as
established, or eliminating the best design, costs far more than a wasted episode.

This is the seed bead for the composition of cores. Its done-condition is GATE-ATEL-14. It also discharges two
proof obligations by experiment: PO-ATEL-09 (scheduling does not change evidence: random preemption, reordering
and co-tenancy over 1,000 episodes in 100 schedules; digests and verdicts identical) and PO-ATEL-04 (in a race the
true best is eliminated with probability at most α: a null race of 81 over 512 replays within the exact binomial
acceptance bound). Tier: Solid [S]; the cores under test are a mix of Solid and Frontier, and a Frontier core that
is on its fallback is tested on its fallback.

## Scope

In: the nine tests, each with its rule, its procedure, its pass condition and its planted violation; the
end-to-end script; the gate record.
Out: the composition registry, static check, fixture and replay driver (N28c-06); the cores (BD-309, BD-349); the
cascade end to end (BD-352, GATE-ATEL-16); the per-core rows of GATE-ATEL-13.

## Technical approach

Test suite `crates/frob-conformance/tests/core_interference.rs`, driven over the replayed-campaign fixture: part A
is `campaign-81` (81 designs, three strata, rungs R1 to R3 with 32 addresses, synthetic latent-quality outcomes
keyed by address, k = 3 finalists) and its null variant; part B is the real `batch-cpu` mini-campaign (9 designs,
32 addresses, class D2) and a 1,000-world run. A "schedule" controls completion order, preemption points,
co-tenancy and worker count; "replay" re-drives the cores with no change to which episodes exist.

Additional structural assertions run with the suite: the one-way pipeline DC-04 → DC-06 → DC-10 (in the replayed
campaign no incidence-matrix update is read by a rare-failure bound or an attribution within the same campaign);
the exchangeability alarm ends a regime (after a planted drift in the audit residuals trips the martingale at 20,
calibration rows from before the alarm are not pooled with rows after it); and no value-of-information quantity
appears in a verdict field of any record produced in the replay (INV-ATEL-12).

**Pass rule for the gate.** All nine tests pass on the replayed campaign, each planted violation is caught by its
test, and the measured values (counts, digests, bounds) are recorded. A test whose planted violation is not caught
counts as failed.

**Evidence.** The gate row records, per test: schedules or replays run, mismatches found (0 required), the first
mismatch if any (world address, tick or decision index), and the digests compared. Claim kind Known answer, class
Tested, determinism D0 for part A and D2 for part B.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-ATEL-14 (seed done-condition): all nine interference tests pass on the replayed campaign and the gate
      row records the measured values.
- [ ] IT-3: 1,000 episodes × 100 schedules, zero digest mismatches, zero e-value differences, zero verdict
      differences (PO-ATEL-09 discharged, with the count recorded).
- [ ] IT-8: best eliminated in at most 42 of 512 null replays at α = 0.05 (PO-ATEL-04 discharged; observed count
      recorded).
- [ ] IT-5: `FROB-REF-548` fires on each of the three pooling attempts; 1,000 of 1,000 digests unchanged after the
      class-E rewrite.
- [ ] IT-6: `FROB-REF-549` fires on battery-selected and on search-selected episode sets.
- [ ] IT-2: zero cancelled audit episodes; the planted cancelling race raises `FROB-REF-546`.
- [ ] Each of the nine planted violations (and the reassociation variant of IT-5) makes its test fail.
- [ ] The three structural assertions hold on the replayed campaign.
- [ ] The suite is registered in the composition registry (each quantity's `test` identifier resolves).
- [ ] The gate is recorded by a session that did not implement the cores or this suite.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The nine tests are the unit tests (`it1_one_way_handover`, `it2_audit_never_cancelled`,
`it3_schedule_invariance`, `it4_profile_ignores_queue`, `it5a_pooling_refused`, `it5b_class_e_keeps_digests`,
`it6_bound_refuses_selected`, `it7_conditions_equal_draw`, `it8_null_race_keeps_best`,
`it9_memo_invisible`), plus one `*_planted` companion for each that builds the violation behind the `conformance`
feature and asserts the test fails, and `pipeline_no_back_flow`, `alarm_ends_regime`, `no_voi_in_verdict_fields`.
Expected values are those in the table: 0 mismatches; at most 42 of 512; 1,000 of 1,000 digests.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/decision_core_interference.sh`: starts a private `frobd`, loads the fixture, and drives the real
binaries: `frob opt cascade --spec conformance/cores/campaign-81/spec.toml --replay --schedule <i> --json` for the
decision-plane tests; `frob eval compare` with and without `--no-memo` for IT-9; `frob nemesis bound` for IT-6;
`frob eval promote` and `frob eval rank-agreement` with mixed-identity inputs for IT-5a; `frob sim run` before and
after the rewrite for IT-5b; a saturating job set for IT-4. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/decision_core_interference/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`BD-350`), `step` (`IT-1` to `IT-9`), `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and
the envelope's `evidence` block; `metric` lines carry schedules run, mismatches (threshold 0), eliminations of the
best (threshold 42), digests kept (threshold 1,000); `refusal` lines carry code, pointer and repairs for
`FROB-REF-546`, `-548`, `-549`; `digest` fields carry the compared state digests; on failure the first differing
world address, tick and both digests, and the schedule file needed to reproduce. The final `summary` gives
assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-ATEL-14"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: the binomial bound 42 for (512, 0.05, 0.001); the pre-registered draw's first 20 conditions
  (committed with the fixture); the 1,000 world digests of the pinned run.
- Negative controls: the ten planted violations of the table (registered in the negative-control registry against
  GATE-ATEL-14).
- Metamorphic relations: slot permutation and shard count leave IT-3's outputs unchanged; adding a tenth,
  unrelated job to the queue leaves IT-4's digests unchanged.
- Determinism lanes: part A twice on one machine and on both hosts (D0); part B across worker counts and shard
  counts on one backend (D2); debug and release.

## Considerations

- Part B at M4 runs on `batch-cpu` and on one GPU backend; a D3 pair of backends is not compared bit for bit:
  paired statistics across backends are refused unless decision-level equivalence is recorded for the task.
- IT-7 uses a synthetic real-batch fixture, because no calibration data is admitted before the reality lane
  opens; the test is repeated on the synthetic rehearsal dataset when it exists.
- IT-8's bound is a test of the harness's error rate, not a proof; the derivation that the mixture construction
  keeps the best with probability at least 1 − α is on file with the proof-obligation ledger.
- Cost: IT-3 is 100,000 episode replays of a small design; under 30 minutes on the CPU of the M5 Ultra (TARGET).
- A failure here blocks the M4 exit: GATE-ATEL-14 has no kill gate. The remedy is to put the offending core on
  its fallback, which by construction is a fixed table that interferes with nothing, and re-run.
