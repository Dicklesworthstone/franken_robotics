## Background

The evaluation ladder scores a population of robot designs on six rungs of rising cost (static analysis; a shared
family policy on a fast tier; a fine-tuned policy; a specialist; the referee; a rare-failure bound), and two
decision cores decide who climbs. DC-02 decides which designs advance at a rung transition. DC-03 decides how wide
the survivor margin is for a rung pair and stratum, and which mode the rung is in. A decision core is a compiled
decision rule with a table, a loss model, obligations, a deterministic fallback and a budgeted mode, kept as a
record under `registries/cores/`. Gate GATE-ATEL-13 requires of each core: its table regenerates bit-identically;
its null calibration holds; its fallback is demonstrated by forcing each trigger; its shadow run records both
actions. The rows for DC-01, DC-09 and DC-10 are due at M3; this bead measures the M4 rows for DC-02 and DC-03.

The governing asymmetry (ADR-ATEL-03): eliminating a true top-k design costs far more than the rung cost of
promoting a poor one, so successive halving is the fixed baseline and the fallback, and every adaptive rule ships
in shadow behind it. ADR-ATEL-04: a cheap rung discards; it does not select, except in strata where rank agreement
has been measured. The adaptive parts are Frontier [F]: if their calibration fails this gate after one repair
cycle, the cores stay on their fallback and the registry says so (that is also the fallback of kill gate
KILL-LADDER).

## Technical approach

**The rules under test.**

*Layer 1, the fixed table (the fallback).* Successive halving with η = 3 inside each stratum: at each rung
transition the top ⌈n/3⌉ designs by the lower rung's score advance, on a schedule written to the ledger before the
campaign starts.

*Layer 2, conformal survivors with an audit lane.* For each rung pair and stratum:
1. Audit lane: a tenth of the rung's spend goes to designs drawn by a keyed random stream before any score exists;
   each is evaluated at the higher rung at a fixed n and is never cancelled. It is the only source of calibration.
2. Calibration: with n audit designs, residuals rᵢ = |y_hi,i − ĝ(y_lo,i)|, ĝ a monotone map fitted on a disjoint
   part of the audit set; the margin q̂ is the ⌈(n + 1)(1 − α)⌉-th smallest residual. At α = 0.05 this needs
   n ≥ 19.
3. Survivor set: every design whose lower-rung score is within 2·q̂ of the k-th best lower-rung score advances (k
   = finalists wanted).
4. Trust check: at most 3k survivors, or else buy more lower-rung episodes, or skip the rung for the top band, or
   truncate to 3k by lower-rung score and label the campaign `budget-truncated`.
5. Alarm: a test martingale on the audit designs' conformal p-values; at a value of 20 (1/α at α = 0.05) the
   stratum's calibration is void, the stratum reverts to layer 1, and calibration restarts inside the new regime.

*DC-02 record.* Artifacts: the bracket schedule of the fixed table; per rung pair and stratum the survivor rule
with its margin; the one-way handover marker to DC-05 (once the survivors of the last population rung number at
most 64, allocation passes one way to the value-of-information core); the label `budget-truncated`. Loss model:
eliminating a true top-k design against the rung cost of promoting a poor one. Obligations: coverage at least
1 − α for designs exchangeable with the audit set, on average over calibration draws; two designs separated by
more than the margin at the lower rung are misordered at the higher rung with probability at most 2α; top-k
recall is measured by the audit lane and never claimed from the theory; no design is served by two layers.
Fallback: halving at η = 3. Triggers: `under-19-calibration-points`; `martingale-at-or-above-20`;
`audited-miss-rate-above-alpha`; `survivors-above-3k-for-two-generations`; `identity-or-scope-changed`. Budgeted
mode: audit lane at a tenth of rung spend; survivors capped at 3k, then truncation by lower-rung score with the
label.

*DC-03 record.* Artifacts: the table of margins q̂ by (rung pair, stratum, α); the keyed list of audit draws; the
exchangeability martingale; the mode automaton with hysteresis: a mode changes only at a generation boundary, and
a stratum needs two consecutive generations of valid calibration to become `selective`. Modes (all conditions on
the lower bootstrap bound of Kendall τ on the top band): `selective` when τ ≥ 0.7 with the winner check passed,
calibration valid (n ≥ 19, martingale below 20) and audited miss rate at most α → the conformal survivor rule;
`discard-only` when 0.5 ≤ τ < 0.7, or a fallback trigger fired → the fixed table, and a design within 2·q̂ of the
k-th best is never eliminated where a margin exists; `uninformative` when τ < 0.5, or the stratum is unmeasured,
or the planted winner was lost → the rung is skipped in this stratum, its budget moves up one rung, and designs it
eliminated earlier are re-opened through the audit lane. Loss model: as DC-02, plus the cost of the audit lane.
Obligations: coverage at least 1 − α; exchangeability of the audit draws by construction (drawn by key before
scores exist); a pair separated by the margin is misordered with probability at most 2α; the automaton never
changes inside a generation. Fallback: `discard-only` with the fixed table; triggers as DC-02. Budgeted mode: the
audit lane's tenth of rung spend; when that buys fewer than 19 audit designs in a stratum, strata are merged
upward to the morphology class before the stratum falls back. Failure modes, each detected by the martingale or
the calibration floor: calibration from survivors only; drift of the optimizer toward a region the audit set does
not cover; starved strata.

**The four clauses, as tests.**

1. *Regeneration.* The committed artifacts are: the halving bracket schedule (for n in 1..=4,096: advance count
   ⌈n/3⌉ per transition); the conformal rank table (for n in 1..=1,024 and α in {1/20, 1/10}: the rank
   ⌈(n + 1)(1 − α)⌉, or `none` when it exceeds n); the mode automaton's transition table (state × generation-end
   observation → state). `xtask check-generated` regenerates each and compares bytes.
2. *Null calibration (PO-ATEL-03).* Split-conformal coverage holds for exchangeable audit designs and its spread
   is the Beta law. Simulation: 10⁴ calibration draws of n = 39 exchangeable residuals (continuous, i.i.d.); for
   each draw compute q̂ (the 38th smallest) and the exact conditional coverage of a fresh residual; assert the mean
   coverage is 0.950 ± 0.002 and the 5th percentile of coverage is 0.884 ± 0.003 (Beta(38, 2): mean 38/40; its 5th
   percentile is 0.8840; the plan prints "below 0.885 in one draw in twenty"). Misordering: on 10⁵ simulated pairs
   separated by more than 2·q̂ at the lower rung, the fraction misordered at the higher rung is at most 2α = 0.10.
3. *Forced fallbacks.* Each of the five triggers is forced by constructing its condition on the replayed-campaign
   fixture: 18 audit designs in a stratum (after upward merge is disabled for the test); a planted drift in audit
   residuals that drives the martingale past 20; a planted audited miss rate of 0.12 at α = 0.05; survivors of 11
   and 12 with k = 3 in two consecutive generations; a kernel-version change between generations. In every case
   the decision record shows `rule = Fallback { trigger }` and the advancing set equals the top ⌈n/3⌉.
4. *Shadow records.* With both cores in `shadow`, a replay of the fixture campaign records, per rung transition
   and stratum, the conformal survivor set and the halving set; the applied set is halving's. The record count
   equals the number of (transition, stratum, generation) triples.

**Budgeted-mode checks.** Upward merge: strata with 7, 8 and 9 audit designs merge to one class-level calibration
of 24 before any falls back. Truncation: 14 survivors with k = 3 and no budget for more episodes are truncated to
9 by lower-rung score and the campaign is labelled `budget-truncated`.

**Hysteresis checks.** A stratum with valid calibration in generation 4 only is not `selective` in generation 5;
with valid calibration in generations 4 and 5 it is `selective` from generation 6; a trigger in the middle of
generation 7 changes the mode at the boundary to generation 8 and not before.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three artifacts regenerate bit-identically on both hosts, in debug and release.
- [ ] Conformal rank known answers: n = 39, α = 0.05 → rank 38; n = 19 → 19; n = 18 → `none` (falls back).
- [ ] PO-ATEL-03: mean coverage 0.950 ± 0.002 and 5th percentile 0.884 ± 0.003 over 10⁴ draws at n = 39; the
      measured values are recorded.
- [ ] Misordering frequency at most 0.10 for pairs separated by more than the margin.
- [ ] Each of the five triggers, forced, yields the halving set and a fallback record naming the trigger.
- [ ] Worked example: residuals with 38th smallest 0.04 and third-best lower-rung score 0.74 give the cut 0.66; on
      the worked 81-score fixture (built with exactly six lower-rung scores at or above 0.66) six designs survive.
- [ ] Shadow replay: both sets recorded for 100% of decisions; applied equals halving.
- [ ] Hysteresis and budgeted-mode checks pass as stated.
- [ ] The four clauses for DC-02 and DC-03 are registered as entries under GATE-ATEL-13 in the tier target
      `g1_known_answers` (the gate record's `tier`), with the negative controls below behind
      `gauntlet-mutations`; `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers
      --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-ATEL-13` passes with their measured values in the receipt and every
      control `caught`. This bead writes no gate row: the M4 part of GATE-ATEL-13 has one recorder, N28cp3-01.
      If calibration fails after one repair cycle, both records are set to `fallback-only`, and the gate's
      `killed: fallback adopted` status follows from KILL-LADDER's decision (derived by N40-03, never written).

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/cores_dc02_dc03.rs`: `halving_schedule_81` (81 → 27 → 9 → 3); `rank_table_points` (the
three known answers); `margin_is_38th_of_39`; `cut_is_kth_best_minus_2q` (0.74 − 2 × 0.04 = 0.66);

`trigger_miss_rate`, `trigger_survivors_3k_twice`, `trigger_identity_change`; `merge_upward_before_fallback`;
`truncate_and_label`; `mode_needs_two_generations`; `mode_changes_only_at_boundary`;
`uninformative_reopens_discards` (designs eliminated earlier re-enter through the audit lane);
`shadow_records_both_sets`; `handover_marker_at_64` (65 survivors: population layer; 64: marker set, one way).

as stages beside the tier run and records the M4 part.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-696: conformal margin at n = 39, α = 0.05 is the 38th ordered residual (exact index).
- Ladder cost arithmetic used as a fixture check: 81 designs with rungs of 10 s, 3 min and 30 min cost 40.5 h if
  all are trained; halving at η = 3 costs 13.5 min + 81 min + 270 min ≈ 6.1 h; with six survivors and three
  finalists 13.5 min + 18 min + 90 min ≈ 2.0 h, about 2.2 h with a tenth added for the audit lane; with q̂ = 0.10
  about twenty survive, more than 3k = 9.
- Negative controls that must fail: calibration from survivors only (on a fixture where the residual correlates
  with the cheap score it under-covers: measured coverage below 0.90; and the build must raise `FROB-REF-546`); an
  audit episode cancelled by an e-process; a mode change inside a generation; a rung asked to select in a stratum
  whose mode is not `selective` (must raise `FROB-REF-545`).
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1 and 36; debug and release; both hosts (D0 for tables
  and decisions on the fixture).

## Considerations

- The coverage test is about the rule, on exchangeable synthetic residuals; whether robot populations are
  exchangeable with their audit sets is measured by GATE-ATEL-08 (held-out coverage at least 1 − α − 0.03 per
  stratum), not here.
- OPEN-ATEL-2 (the definition of a stratum) changes how many audit designs a stratum receives; the tests are
  written against a stratum key and do not assume a definition.
- The threshold pair 0.7 and 0.5 is fixed before data and is not tuned by this bead.
- A risk named in advance: RISK-27 (rank agreement too low for cheap rungs to eliminate designs) makes
  `uninformative` the common mode; the cores must then cost nothing more than skipping the rung.
