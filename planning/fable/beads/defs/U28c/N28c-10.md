## Background

Two decision cores of the design studio govern what is said about failures. DC-04 decides whether to certify,
refute or stop on a failure-rate bound for a finalist design (family: rare-event simulation; owner `frob-nemesis`;
timescale per finalist; Solid for the bound, Frontier for splitting). DC-06 decides which cause, and which repair
edit, ranks first after a failure (family: counterfactual forks with a placebo; owner `frob-autopsy`; timescale
per failure cluster; Solid). A decision core is a compiled decision rule with a table, a loss model, obligations,
a deterministic fallback and a budgeted mode, registered under `registries/cores/`; the mechanisms belong to their
own subsystems, and the studio fixes the decision rule. Gate GATE-ATEL-13 requires that each core's table
regenerates bit-identically, its null calibration holds, its fallback is demonstrated by forcing each trigger, and
its shadow run records both actions. This bead measures the M4 rows for DC-04 and DC-06.

The loss asymmetries that shape both rules: for DC-04, false certification far outweighs false refutation; for
DC-06, naming a cause in a chaotic regime sends the agent after noise, which costs more than saying nothing.
Rejected for these cores: extreme-value extrapolation of failure tails (checking a fitted tail at 10⁻⁶ takes the
same 3 × 10⁶ nominal episodes that certify directly); defensive-mixture importance sampling as the certified bound
(its floor is ln(1/δ)/(β·n) for nominal share β, never below crude sampling on the nominal share; it survives for
refutation and for tightening an estimate); coalition-value attribution as the primary output (values can be
negative and cannot establish necessity); copy-on-write device state for forks (wrong bottleneck: 8,192 worlds at
30 kB is 0.25 GB); subset simulation by Markov chains (deferred unless forks carry no fresh randomness).

## Scope

In: conformance tests and their GATE-ATEL-13 tier entries for DC-04 and DC-06, with the rule text they test.
Out: recording the M4 part of the gate (N28cp3-01, its one recorder); the mechanisms (`nemesis.bound`, splitting,

## Technical approach

**DC-04 record, as tested.**
- Artifacts: the certification table n = ⌈ln(1/δ)/p*⌉ (the number of independent nominal episodes with zero
  failures that bounds the failure rate by p* at confidence 1 − δ; the exact binomial count is
  ⌈ln δ/ln(1 − p*)⌉, which is never larger); a sequential binomial bound that ends `certified`, `refuted` or
  `exhausted`; a splitting schedule on a distress score, with fixed levels and child world addresses for forks; a
  failure-mode catalogue with a Good–Turing monitor of unseen mass (modes seen once divided by failures seen); a
  truncated-weight lower bound for refutation.
- Loss model and split of budget: explore first, to a fifth of the budget or until unseen mass is at most 0.05;
  refute if the lower bound exceeds p*; spend the rest on nominal sampling, for finalists only.
- Obligations: the bound holds at confidence 1 − δ for independent nominal episodes with a frozen failure
  predicate, and is scoped to its tier; splitting supplies discovery and `Estimated` rates only; failures are
  replayed one tier up; a bound refuses episodes selected by a battery or by a search (`FROB-REF-549`).
- Fallback: crude Monte Carlo alone. Triggers, three, as the record of the owning subsystem (N26a-26) lists
  them: `score-uninformative` (under 1% of forks reach the next level in the first exploration round);
  `forks-without-fresh-randomness` (splitting refuses with the adversarial chapter's FROB-REF-569);
  `null-self-test-failed` (a failed null self-test of the bound in this session: the owner's fail-closed
  addition, with the same fallback).
- Budgeted mode: exploration at most a fifth; on exhaustion a refusal that carries the bound reached.
- Known answers (illustrative throughput). At δ = 0.05, bounds of 10⁻⁴, 10⁻⁵ and 10⁻⁶ need 29,958, 299,574 and
  2,995,733 episodes by the table formula (exact binomial: 29,956, 299,572 and 2,995,731), about 3 × 10⁴, 3 × 10⁵
  and 3 × 10⁶: 75 s, 12.5 min and 2.1 h at 400 episodes per second. Splitting to 10⁻⁶ with L = 6 levels of
  conditional probability p₀ = 0.1 and N = 10⁴ trajectories per level uses 6 × 10⁴ fragments at a coefficient of
  variation near √(L·(1 − p₀)/(p₀·N)) = 7.3%. If throughput is a tenth of that figure, 10⁻⁶ costs 21 h: certify
  10⁻⁵ and report 10⁻⁶ as `Estimated`.

**DC-06 record, as tested.**
- Artifacts: typed interventions (`do(torque limit off)`, `do(friction := nominal)`,
  `do(estimator := privileged)`), each one declared mechanism wide; a fork ledger; a placebo flip rate ρ₀ from an
  intervention that should not matter; a probability of necessity per cause; minimal repair sets by delta
  debugging; the labels `but-for`, `joint` and `chaotic: unattributable`. The output is which repair edit ranks
  first.
- Loss model: a wrong first repair costs an agent turn and its compute; a cause named in a chaotic regime costs
  more than no answer.
- Obligations: the empty intervention replays the failure bit for bit, so the starting point is exact; each
  intervention changes one declared mechanism, checked by model diff; a cause is named only when its flip rate
  beats ρ₀ under a sequential test of the kind DC-01 uses, at a level divided by the number of factors;
  design-parameter interventions re-run from the episode start, because winding temperature and the policy's own
  history differ, while environment and controller-state interventions may fork from a snapshot under the fork
  classification of the record subsystem; attribution arithmetic runs in double precision.
- Fallback: first-anomaly ordering, labelled `Estimated`. Triggers, three, as the record of the owning subsystem
  (N27b-12) lists them, each with its delivery: `replay-mismatch` (the null replay differs: refusal FROB-REF-583,
  whose repairs include `frob explain run <run> --budget 600`); `double-precision-equivalence-failed` (the
  attribution arithmetic fails its double-precision equivalence and no lane is admitted: refusal FROB-REF-584,
  same repair; the owner's fail-closed addition); `placebo-rate-above-0.2` (a result with outcome `chaotic:
  unattributable`, every factor `none`, and the first-anomaly ordering inline under a `fallback` block).
- Budgeted mode: 12 interventions, singles and pairs, about 700 forks (12 singles + 66 pairs = 78 coalitions; at 8
  replicates each, with 64 placebo forks and the null fork, 689 forks). A coalition-value summary is computed only
  when no single or pair flips the outcome, and is labelled a description.
- Known answers (illustrative). Of 200 failing worlds re-run from snapshots, removing the torque limit prevents
  164, nominal friction prevents 38 and a placebo nudge prevents 12: rescue rates 0.82, 0.19 and 0.06; net of
  placebo 0.76 and 0.13. The first is `but-for` (a contrast of at least 0.5). If the placebo rate is near a
  cause's rate (30 of 200 against 38), nothing is attributed. If only the pair prevents the failure, the cause is
  `joint`.

**The four clauses, as tests.**
1. *Regeneration.* DC-04: the certification table for δ ∈ {1/20, 1/100} and p* ∈ {10⁻², …, 10⁻⁶}; the budget-split
   guard (a fifth; unseen mass 0.05); the splitting level schedule. DC-06: the decision table (contrast, placebo
   rate, test outcome) → label and rank; the coalition enumeration order for 12 factors. All under
   `xtask check-generated`.
2. *Null calibration.* DC-04: on a known-probability fixture (a biased lattice random walk whose probability of
   hitting a barrier before a deadline has a closed form; three settings near 10⁻², 10⁻³ and 10⁻⁴), over 10⁴
   repetitions the certified bound exceeds the truth in at least 1 − δ of repetitions that certify, and the
   refutation lower bound lies below the truth in at least 1 − δ of repetitions that refute (within the exact
   binomial acceptance bound). DC-06: on a null fixture in which no intervention changes the failure probability
   (every factor's rescue count is drawn from the placebo's distribution), over 10⁴ simulated attribution tables
   of 12 factors a cause is named in at most α = 0.05 of tables.
3. *Forced fallbacks.* DC-04: a distress score that is constant (0% of forks reach the next level) forces crude
   sampling; a fork set whose children share the parent's streams is detected as duplicates and forces crude
   sampling; a bound whose null self-test is made to fail (the conformance build's planted self-test fixture)
   forces crude sampling. DC-06: a planted replay mismatch of the empty intervention (one changed bit in the
   snapshot) gives FROB-REF-583; a planted single-precision attribution path that disagrees with the
   double-precision recomputation gives FROB-REF-584; a placebo flip rate of 0.25 gives `chaotic:
   unattributable` with the first-anomaly ordering, `Estimated`. Each yields the fallback action and a record
   naming the trigger.
4. *Shadow records.* Each core in shadow records the table action and the fallback action per decision on the
   replayed-campaign fixture (finalists for DC-04; failure clusters for DC-06) and applies the fallback.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Artifacts of both cores regenerate bit-identically on both hosts.
- [ ] DC-04 table known answers: 29,958, 299,574, 2,995,733 by the table formula and 29,956, 299,572, 2,995,731
      exactly; coefficient of variation 0.0735 for (L, p₀, N) = (6, 0.1, 10⁴); 6 × 10⁴ fragments.
- [ ] DC-04 null calibration on the three random-walk settings within the acceptance bounds; values recorded.
- [ ] DC-04 budget split: on a fixture budget of 10⁵ episodes exploration stops at 2 × 10⁴ or at unseen mass 0.05,
      whichever comes first; on exhaustion the refusal carries the bound reached.
- [ ] Battery-selected and search-selected episode sets never reach a bound: requested through the studio they
      are refused with `FROB-REF-549`, offered to `nemesis.bound` directly with `FROB-REF-560`.
- [ ] DC-06 worked table: contrasts 0.76 and 0.13; the first labelled `but-for`; with placebo 30 of 200 against a
      factor at 38 of 200 nothing is named; a pair-only rescue is labelled `joint`.
- [ ] DC-06 null: a cause is named in at most 5% of 10⁴ null tables (within the acceptance bound).
- [ ] DC-06 budget: 78 coalitions, 689 forks; the coalition-value summary appears only when no single or pair
      flips, and is labelled a description.
- [ ] The six forced triggers (three of DC-04, three of DC-06) give the fallback action in the delivery stated
      and a record naming the trigger.
- [ ] Shadow records for 100% of decisions; applied actions are the fallbacks'.
- [ ] The four clauses for DC-04 and DC-06 are registered as entries under GATE-ATEL-13 in the tier target
      `g1_known_answers` (the gate record's `tier`), with the negative controls below behind
      `gauntlet-mutations`; `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers
      --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-ATEL-13` passes with their measured values in the receipt and every
      control `caught`. This bead writes no gate row: the M4 part of GATE-ATEL-13 has one recorder, N28cp3-01.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cores_dc04.rs`: `certification_counts`; `splitting_cv`; `budget_split_fifth`;
`unseen_mass_good_turing` (7 modes seen once among 140 failures → 0.05, exploration stops); `random_walk_bracket`
(10⁴ repetitions per setting, seeded); `trigger_score_uninformative`; `trigger_duplicate_forks`;
`bound_scoped_to_tier` (a bound computed on the fast tier is not accepted as a referee bound);
`refuses_selected_episodes` (549 through the studio, 560 at the bound); `trigger_null_self_test`;
`slow_throughput_recommendation` (at 40 episodes per second the 10⁻⁶ plan is 20.8 h
and the policy recommends certifying 10⁻⁵).
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cores_dc06.rs`: `contrast_arithmetic`; `but_for_threshold`;
`placebo_near_cause_names_nothing`; `pair_only_is_joint`; `null_tables_rarely_name`; `fork_budget_689`;
`trigger_replay_mismatch` (FROB-REF-583); `trigger_precision_equivalence` (FROB-REF-584);
`trigger_placebo_above_0_2`; `design_parameter_reruns_from_start` (a mass edit is
rejected as a snapshot fork); `double_precision_attribution` (the attribution path uses 64-bit arithmetic);
`coalition_summary_only_as_description`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/decision_cores_m4.sh`, phases `dc-04` and `dc-06` (the script is shared by the four M4 row beads;
each phase runs alone with `--phase dc-NN`; whichever row bead lands first creates it from the STD-E2E template):
`cargo xtask check-generated`; `frob cores show dc-04 --rows --json` and `frob cores show dc-06 --rows --json`;
`frob nemesis bound --mode certify` on the random-walk fixture scene (three settings, seeded, each call with its
own key `--key dc04-rw-<setting>-$FROB_E2E_SEED`: `nemesis.bound` is a `run` verb); the cascade's bound request
(the rare-failure bound of stage 6 of BD-352, a dependency for this path only) and a direct `frob nemesis bound`
on the fixture's battery-selected episodes (549 and 560; keys `dc04-sel-<path>-$FROB_E2E_SEED`); `frob autopsy
attribute` on a planted single-cause failure, on a pair-only failure and on the chaotic fixture; the six
forced-trigger runs; the shadow replays on the replayed-campaign fixture. Log:
`target/e2e-logs/decision_cores_m4/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`
(`N28c-10`), `step` (`dc-04.*`, `dc-06.*`), `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the
envelope's `evidence` block (claim Known answer, class Tested, determinism D0 for tables and D2 for fork runs,
seed); `metric` lines carry episode counts against the table values, bracket frequencies against 1 − δ with
their acceptance bounds, the coefficient of variation against 0.0735, contrasts against 0.76 and 0.13, the
null naming rate against 0.05, the fork count against 689; `refusal` lines carry 549, 560, 583 and 584 with
pointer and repairs; `digest` fields carry the table hashes and the replayed failure digests; on failure the
first differing count or label with the fixture path and seed; a `summary` line naming GATE-ATEL-13 (DC-04,
DC-06). The phases write no gate row; N28cp3-01's phase `gate` runs them as stages beside the tier run and records
the M4 part.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The counts, the coefficient of variation, the fork budget and the contrast arithmetic above.
- Negative controls that must fail: a bound computed over battery-selected episodes; a fitted-tail extrapolation
  offered as certification; a fork without fresh randomness counted as an independent trial; a cause named with
  the placebo rate at 0.25; an intervention that changes two mechanisms (caught by model diff); a Shapley-style
  summary presented as necessity.
- Metamorphic relations: relabelling factors permutes the DC-06 table and changes no label; doubling every
  episode count at fixed rates leaves the DC-04 action unchanged once certified.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; both hosts; debug and release; fork-based tests at class D2 on one
  backend.

## Considerations

- A certified rate of 10⁻⁶ exceeds the default compute envelope; where the owner has not approved it, the policy
  certifies 10⁻⁵ and reports 10⁻⁶ as `Estimated`, and this bead tests that degraded path.
- Forks find failures much sooner than waiting (published adaptive stress testing reports 2 to 20 times fewer
  episodes; 10 to 100 times at rates of 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻⁵ is a TARGET), but proving absence has a fixed price that no
  sampler discounts without assuming where failures live; the tests keep discovery and certification apart.
- DC-06's placebo threshold of 0.2 is a DESIGN default recorded in the core's record; changing it is a new
  version of the decision policy.
