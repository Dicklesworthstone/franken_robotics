## Background

`frob experiment suggest <designs or answer> --budget …` returns a ranked list of information-gathering proposals,
each with a testable prediction: more pairs at a rung, a referee batch, a fine-tune, a probe on a bench, a
measurement of a card field, a cell of a mission answer to split. It is advisory. It never decides validity
(ADR-ATEL-07: parametric models rank experiments; distribution-free tests issue verdicts). The verb is registered
once, by the mission-query subsystem, which owns it; the design studio (Atelier) is a consumer and specifies the
ranking by decision value. Two kill gates bear on it and do not overlap: KILL-AUGUR-PROBE governs the probe
generator, and KILL-VOI governs the ranking.

The ranking of simulation proposals by value of information is the seed bead for experiment suggestion (BD-351).
This bead carries what the studio requires of *measurement* proposals, where the danger is different. A
measurement is tempting to choose by what it tells you about a parameter. The plan's rule is to choose it by what
it can change in the design decision:

- **Partition by decision, not by parameter.** For a measurement, the compatible physical mechanisms are
  partitioned by the design decision they imply; the cheapest realizable intervention whose possible readings
  separate incompatible decisions is proposed; where observation sets still overlap, two witness mechanisms with
  opposing design margins are returned; when all mechanisms imply the same choice, identification is skipped. The
  identifiability analysis A-25 checks that the proposed excitation can identify what it claims to.
- **Measuring the target directly is always on the list.** Counterexample (CX-317; FACT from an executed planning
  pilot, re-run in the repository before a gate cites it): a short probe synthesized for three admitted material
  laws chose the right gripper pad in 675 of 675 in-class cases; a fourth, delayed law followed the same early
  trace and reversed all 225 held-out decisions; measuring the candidate pad at mission dwell was right in 900 of
  900. An in-class-perfect probe cannot expose structural error, so every suggestion states its model class and
  lists the direct measurement as a comparator with its cost.

Tier: Frontier [F] for probe proposals (their pilot is the material-probe pilot re-run, and their kill gate is
KILL-AUGUR-PROBE); the comparator rule and the model-class statement are Solid and survive every kill branch.
Rejected alternative: ranking measurements by expected information gain about parameters (it buys knowledge that
cannot change the choice).

## Scope

In: the proposal record; how measurement proposals are assembled from the partition machinery; the A-25 gate on
each; the mandatory direct-measurement comparator; the model-class statement; the skip rule; the two-witness
output; how these enter the ranked list beside simulation proposals.
Out: the value-of-information arithmetic and the STOP rule (BD-351; the DC-05 table); the partition algorithm and
experiment grammar themselves (BD-291); A-25 (BD-348); registering predictions (N28c-14); the verb's registry
entry (the mission-query subsystem).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-opt/src/suggest/measure.rs`, behind the feature `frontier-suggest` (off by default).

1. **Proposal record (DESIGN), schema `frob.proposal/1`.**
   ```rust
   pub struct Proposal {
       id: ProposalId,
       kind: ProposalKind,           // MorePairs { rung, n } | RefereeBatch { n } | FineTune { fraction }
                                     // | BenchProbe { experiment } | CardField { card, field }
                                     // | SplitCell { answer, cell } | DirectMeasurement { target, conditions }
       cost: Cost,                   // GPU-second equivalents at the declared exchange rate, plus declared real cost
       reduces: VarianceComponent,   // Numerical | Statistical | Model
       value: Advisory<f64>,         // reduction in expected opportunity loss of the current choice
       value_per_cost: Advisory<f64>,
       prediction: PredictionRef,    // the registered outcome interval, per mechanism where mechanisms differ
       model_class: ModelClass,      // the set of admitted mechanisms the proposal is conditional on
       comparator: Option<ProposalId>,   // the direct measurement, for every measurement proposal
       identifiability: Option<A25Result>,
       decision_partition: Option<Partition>,   // blocks of mechanisms by implied design decision
   }
   ```
   `value` and `value_per_cost` are advisory quantities and cannot enter a verdict field.
2. **Assembling a measurement proposal.** Inputs: the pending decision (which of the candidate designs or cells is
   chosen), the set ℳ of mechanisms compatible with archived observations and their uncertainty, the realizable
   interventions with costs (fixture compliance, pad radius, sensor position, command waveform, exposure time,
   preload), the admitted nuisance intervals and the reading error bound.
   1. Partition ℳ by the design decision each mechanism implies.
   2. If there is one block: emit no identification proposal; record `skipped: all mechanisms imply the same
      choice` in the answer's notes.
   3. Otherwise, among interventions in order of cost, take the first whose sets of possible readings, over the
      nuisance intervals and the reading error, are disjoint closed sets for every pair of mechanisms in
      different blocks. Emit it as `BenchProbe` or `CardField` with, per block, the predicted reading interval
      and the decision that reading implies (the measurement contract).
   4. If no intervention separates all blocks: emit a proposal of kind `BenchProbe` marked `partial`, and attach
      two witness mechanisms from different blocks whose reading sets overlap, with their opposing design
      margins, so the agent sees which two worlds it cannot yet tell apart.
   5. If the observations fit no admitted mechanism: report a discrepancy; do not pick the nearest mechanism.
3. **The A-25 gate.** Each measurement proposal is passed to the identifiability analysis with its excitation; the
   proposal is kept only if the sensitivity of the predicted readings to the quantity it claims to resolve is
   non-zero over the excitation and every regime of a switching law it relies on is covered. A proposal that
   fails is replaced by A-25's suggested excitation (for example "an excitation in the traction-limited regime")
   or dropped with the reason.
4. **The direct-measurement comparator (mandatory).** For every measurement proposal the list also contains a
   `DirectMeasurement` proposal: measure the target itself at mission conditions (the candidate part at mission
   dwell, speed and load), with its cost. The probe's `comparator` field names it. The list is refused internally
   (a programming error, tested) if a measurement proposal has no comparator. The direct measurement is listed
   even when its cost is higher than every probe and even when its value per cost ranks last.
5. **The model-class statement.** Every proposal's `model_class` lists the mechanisms it is conditional on, and
   the text form says so: "conditional on the admitted laws {…}; a law outside this class can defeat this probe;
   comparator: direct measurement at mission conditions, cost …".
6. **Ranking.** Measurement proposals enter the same ranked list as simulation proposals, by value per cost, with
   value computed as the reduction in expected opportunity loss of the current choice when the mechanism block is
   resolved (the variance component is `Model`). The list ends with STOP when nothing clears the budget price. If
   KILL-VOI has fired, the proposals are listed unranked by value and value is a stop rule only.
7. **Output.** Within the `experiment.suggest` envelope: `proposals[]` (ranked), each with kind, cost, the
   `advisory` block (value, value per cost, model), prediction reference, model class, comparator, partition
   summary and A-25 result; `stop: bool`; `notes[]` (skips, discrepancies). Evidence class `Estimated`,
   `claims: []`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Friction-law fixture: with laws μ₁(v) = 0.5 and μ₂(v) = 0.3 + 0.4·v (v in m/s), which agree at 0.5 m/s and
      imply opposite designs at the mission speed 1.5 m/s (0.5 against 0.9), the proposed probe is at 1.0 m/s
      (readings 0.5 or 0.7) when the reading error bound is below 0.1; with error 0.1 or more no single probe at
      1.0 m/s is proposed and two witness mechanisms are returned; a probe at 0.5 m/s is never proposed.
- [ ] Every measurement proposal in every test output has a `DirectMeasurement` comparator with a cost, and a
      non-empty model class.
- [ ] Skip rule: when both laws imply the same pad, no identification proposal is emitted and the note is present.
- [ ] A-25 gate: on the drawbar fixture (F = min(k_t·I/r, μ·N), k_t = 0.2 N·m/A, r = 0.06 m) a proposal to
      identify μ from runs at 1 A and 2 A under 30 N and 40 N is rejected (zero sensitivity: 3.33 N and 6.67 N for
      μ = 0.4 and for μ = 0.8) and replaced by an excitation in the traction-limited regime (3 A under 10 N: 4 N
      against 8 N).
- [ ] Material-pad fixture (the pilot's four laws): the suggestion output for the three admitted laws lists the
      synthesized probe with model class {V, P, C} and the direct measurement at mission dwell; a harness that
      follows the probe scores 675 of 675 in class and 0 of 225 on the withheld law D, and one that follows the
      direct measurement scores 900 of 900.
- [ ] A discrepancy (observations fitting no admitted mechanism) is reported and no proposal is fabricated.
- [ ] No proposal value appears in a verdict field; citing a proposal as a verdict is refused (`FROB-REF-872`).
- [ ] With the feature off, or after KILL-AUGUR-PROBE fires, the verb lists the programmed expert grid and the
      direct measurement only, and still states the model class.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/suggest_measure.rs`:
- `partition_two_blocks_friction_laws`; `cheapest_separating_probe_is_1_0_mps`; `probe_at_0_5_separates_nothing`;
  `error_0_1_returns_two_witnesses` (witness margins have opposite signs);
- `single_block_skips_identification`;
- `comparator_always_present` (property test over 1,000 random mechanism sets and intervention menus);
- `comparator_listed_even_if_dearest`;
- `a25_rejects_motor_limited_runs` and `a25_suggests_traction_limited`;
- `model_class_text_names_laws`;
- `discrepancy_not_nearest`;
- `omitted_mechanism_control`: a third law equal to μ₁ up to 1.0 m/s and departing later defeats the 1.0 m/s
  probe; the output's model class shows it was conditional on two laws, and the comparator is unaffected;
- `pad_fixture_scores` (675/675, 0/225, 900/900 by replaying the committed 900 cases).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/experiment_suggest.sh` (N28c-16) runs `frob experiment suggest` on the friction-law and pad fixtures
and asserts the comparator, the model class and the A-25 replacement from the user's side; it logs each
proposal's kind, cost, advisory value and comparator identifier, and the scores of the three replay harnesses with
their expected values.

### Known answers and negative controls

- CX-317 (675 of 675 in class; all 225 held-out decisions reversed by the fourth law; direct measurement 900 of
  900) and CX-309 / KA-688 (the drawbar fixture values) with the numbers stated above.
- Negative controls that must fail: a suggestion list without the direct measurement; a probe proposal that
  omits its model class; a proposal to identify μ from the motor-limited runs that passes A-25; a ranking by
  parameter information gain (on the single-block fixture it proposes an identification the decision cannot use).
- Metamorphic relation: adding a mechanism that implies the same decision as an existing block does not change
  the proposed probe unless its reading set overlaps another block's.
- Determinism lanes: the proposal list is byte-identical twice on one machine and across hosts at a pinned ledger
  frontier (interval arithmetic for reading sets is outward-rounded and deterministic).

## Considerations

- The pilot evidence is unflattering and is kept: the programmed expert tied the generator and direct measurement
  beat both on the withheld law. What this bead ships is therefore a discipline (state the class, always offer
  the direct measurement) more than a generator.
- Real-experiment costs and the exchange rate between GPU-seconds and bench time are OPEN-ATEL-1; until decided,
  measurement proposals carry `cost.real: undeclared` and are listed after simulation proposals of equal value.
- A direct measurement can be impossible before a part exists; the proposal then says `not yet realizable` with
  the earliest stage at which it becomes so, and stays on the list.
- Interaction with calibration against reality: a probe's calibrated result may be reused for a family only
  within the scope the reality subsystem declares.
