## Background

`frob experiment suggest <designs or answer> --budget …` is how an agent asks the design studio where the next
unit of budget should go. It returns a ranked list of information-gathering proposals, each with a testable
prediction: more pairs at a rung, a referee batch, a fine-tune, a probe on a bench, a measurement of a card field,
a cell of a mission answer to split. Each proposal is an action with a cost and a variance component it reduces
(numerical, statistical or model); its value is the reduction in expected opportunity loss of the current choice
per unit cost, and the list ends with STOP when nothing clears the price. The verb is advisory and never decides
validity. It is registered by the mission-query subsystem (kind `read`, first milestone M4, cluster `experiment`);
the studio specifies the ranking.

Gate GATE-ATEL-15 (claim kind: Design comparison; M4) has two clauses: in the offline replay of a logged
population (64 designs × 3 rungs × 32 addresses, four choosers replayed 100 times with no further simulation) the
knowledge-gradient chooser is at least 1.5 times better than the agent chooser in evaluations to a true top-3; and
the value is exactly zero on a fixture where no outcome can change the choice. The first clause is measured by the
replay experiment that belongs to the seed bead for experiment suggestion. This bead is the end-to-end script for
the verb: it measures the second clause through the real binary and asserts every user-visible rule of the
suggestion contract, including the planted violations. Tier: Frontier [F] for the ranking; the script also runs
with the ranking killed (unranked list, stop rule only) and asserts that form.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures, assertions and logging; the record of the zero-value clause.
Out: the ranking implementation (BD-351), measurement proposals (N28c-13), the prediction hooks (N28c-14), the
advisory type wall (N28c-12), the replay experiment and the kill decision (BD-351, N28c-17).

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/experiment_suggest.sh`: plain shell, sources the STD-E2E helper, idempotent, seed-explicit
(`FROB_E2E_SEED`), non-zero exit on any failed assertion. Fixtures under `conformance/suggest/`:

- `two-finalists/`: a ledger snapshot with two finalist designs whose posterior difference has mean m = 0.03,
  sampling variance 0.03² and tier-bias variance 0.04² (rows labelled `synthetic`), per-pair noise 0.3 on the fast
  tier and 0.1 on the referee, and a declared exchange rate of 30 core-seconds per GPU-second.
- `decided/`: the same with m = 0.15.
- `no-change/`: one finalist only; and a pair with zero removable variance.
- `friction-laws/` and `pad-laws/`: the measurement fixtures (two friction laws that agree at 0.5 m/s and differ
  at 1.5 m/s; the four material laws V, P, C and the withheld D with their 900 committed cases).
- `drawbar/`: the identifiability fixture F = min(k_t·I/r, μ·N), k_t = 0.2 N·m/A, r = 0.06 m.

Steps (each a `cmd` line, followed by `assert` lines):
1. `frob experiment suggest --fixture two-finalists --budget gpu-s=600 --json` → exit 0; `evidence.class ==
   "Estimated"`, `claims == []`; proposals ranked by `advisory.value_per_cost` descending; the first is "200 more
   fast-tier pairs" with value 0.0013 (± 1%) at cost 1; "8 referee pairs" has value 0.0055 (± 1%) at cost 27; ratio
   of value per cost 6.4 ± 0.1; the list ends with a STOP entry only if nothing clears the price (here it does not
   end with STOP at a price of 10⁻⁴ per GPU-second).
2. Buy the first proposal: `frob eval compare … --from-proposal <id> --json` on the fixture's recorded outcomes;
   then `frob experiment suggest` again → the referee batch is now first and a second fast batch is worth about
   10⁻⁵ (± 20%).
3. `frob experiment suggest --fixture decided --budget gpu-s=600 --json` → the first entry is STOP with reason
   "no experiment can change the choice at this price"; the largest value in the list is below 2 × 10⁻⁵.
4. Zero-value clause of GATE-ATEL-15: `frob experiment suggest --fixture no-change …` (both variants) → every
   proposal's `advisory.value` is exactly the JSON number 0, and the answer is STOP. The measured values are
   written to the gate row as the second clause.
5. Measurement proposals: on `friction-laws` assert the probe at 1.0 m/s, its two predicted readings (0.5, 0.7),
   its model class naming both laws, and a `DirectMeasurement` comparator with a cost; with the reading error set
   to 0.1 assert two witness mechanisms with opposite margins. On `pad-laws` assert the comparator "measure the
   candidate pad at mission dwell" and run the three replay harnesses (`frob-conformance run suggest.pad-replay`):
   675/675 in class, 0/225 on law D for the probe, 900/900 for the direct measurement.
6. A-25 gate on `drawbar`: the proposal to identify μ from the 1 A and 2 A runs is absent; the suggested
   excitation is in the traction-limited regime (3 A under 10 N).
7. Prediction registration: for the proposal bought in step 2, `frob predict report --predictor studio:suggest
   --json` shows one prediction registered before the first outcome row (sequence numbers compared) and scored.
8. Advisory wall: `frob eval promote <proposal-id> --json` and `frob report design <design> --cite <proposal-id>
   --json` → exit 3, `FROB-REF-872`, first repair "run the comparison".
9. Fallback forms: with the DC-05 coverage fixture at 17 of 30, the list is in the fixed order check, simulate,
   train, referee and says why; with the conformance flag that simulates the kill decision
   (`--core dc-05=fallback`), proposals are listed unranked by value with the stop rule still applied.
10. Budget and estimate: `--estimate` returns a cost interval and runs nothing; a budget of `gpu-s=0` returns STOP
    with reason `budget`; 65 candidate experiments → 64 evaluated, the omitted one named.
11. Determinism: steps 1, 3 and 5 repeated; envelopes compared byte for byte after removing `cost.wall_ms`.
12. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; gates touched: `GATE-ATEL-15 (zero-value clause)`.

**Logging.** `target/e2e-logs/experiment_suggest/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` plus a transcript. Every line: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N28c-16`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), exact
command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities,
seed); `metric` lines with each proposal's value, cost and value per cost against expected values and tolerances,
the replay harness scores against 675, 0 and 900, and token count of the answer; `refusal` lines with code,
pointer and repairs; digests of the envelopes compared in step 11; on failure the first differing value and the
fixture path. Final `summary` line. No private-scene data.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a correct tree and non-zero when any one planted defect below is compiled in.
- [ ] Known values asserted within tolerance: 0.0013, 0.0055, ratio 6.4, second batch 10⁻⁵, decided fixture below
      2 × 10⁻⁵ and STOP.
- [ ] Zero-value clause: exactly 0 on both no-change fixtures, recorded in the gate row with the claim identifier
      of the run.
- [ ] Every measurement proposal seen by the script has a comparator and a model class.
- [ ] `FROB-REF-872` on both citation attempts.
- [ ] The prediction of a bought proposal precedes its outcome in the ledger.
- [ ] The fixed-order and unranked forms are asserted.
- [ ] Two runs with the same seed give identical `assert` and `metric` values.
- [ ] The log and transcript exist at the stated path with a `summary` line naming the gate.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The value-of-information arithmetic: EVSI = s̃·[φ(z) − z·Φ(−z)], z = |m|/s̃; a measurement of variance v on a
  component of prior variance w removes w²/(w + v). Fast batch: w = 0.0009, v = 0.09/200, s̃ = 0.0245, EVSI
  0.00131. Referee: w = 0.0025, v = 0.01/8, s̃ = 0.0408, EVSI 0.00550. Perfect information at m = 0.15:
  1.9 × 10⁻⁵.
- CX-317 scores (675/675; 0/225; 900/900).
- Planted defects the script must catch (registered against GATE-ATEL-15): a chooser that ranks by expected
  information gain (assigns positive value on the decided and no-change fixtures); a list without the direct
  measurement; a suggestion whose prediction is registered after its outcome; a build in which a proposal
  identifier is accepted by `eval.promote`; a value computed in single precision that prints a non-zero residue
  on the no-change fixture.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1 and 36; the Mac and the Linux host (the suggestion
  list is a pure function of the fixture ledger at its frontier).

## Considerations

- The fixtures are synthetic ledgers; nothing in this script supports a claim about savings on robot populations.
  That claim, if any, comes from the replay experiment and the kill decision.
- The script must pass in both branches of KILL-VOI; step 9 keeps the unranked branch exercised so that the
  fallback is not first run on the day it is needed.
- Runtime budget: under 3 minutes on the CPU tier (TARGET).
