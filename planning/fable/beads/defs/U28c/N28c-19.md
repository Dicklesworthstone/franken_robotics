## Background

A report in the design studio is an evidence package generated from the artifact graph (the graph that ties every
claim to its evidence, to the decision rule that decided it and to the execution that produced it). It prints
nothing it cannot resolve. Each statement carries its claim type, its claim identifier, the boundary of its claim
product (the weakest input), the mode of every ladder rung that selected it, its rank-robustness type, its
qualification receipt if it has one, and the machine-readable no-authorization flags. `frob report campaign <id>`
adds the phase ledger, the screening audit, the planted-winner outcome and the list of fallbacks that fired.
Reports are deterministic: the same graph produces the same bytes. A losing experiment is reported with the same
completeness as a winning one, so that it can be reproduced as readily.

The verbs and the package format are the seed bead for reports (first form at M3), and the co-design cascade bead
completes the campaign content at M4. This bead supplies the part of the campaign report that comes from the
decision plane and that only the studio can state honestly: which mode each rung was in when it selected, which
adaptive rules were acting and which had fallen back, which stage controls ran, and the labels and wording that
follow. The reason it needs its own bead is that these sections are where a campaign report most easily overstates
itself. The plan's rules:

- Every campaign report prints the mode of every rung and stratum (`selective`, `discard-only`, `uninformative`).
  Finalists chosen under any mode other than `selective` are described as "k survivors", never as "the top k".
- A stage that cannot run its control (no planted winner available, no second contact law admitted) reports the
  control as `not-run`, and the result carries that.
- "Every admitted alternative fails", "tied within the margin" and "inconclusive at this budget" are results.
- A `model-conditional` finalist is shown with both orders as a fidelity-dependent ranking; offered without its
  condition it is refused (`FROB-REF-557`).
- A `controller-limited` design has the flag printed beside its rank and is never described as inferior.
- A reuse figure printed without its column name is refused in a report.

Gate GATE-ATEL-16 (Known answer; M4) includes the clause "the report prints every mode, every fallback and the
phase ledger"; this bead implements what that clause checks. Tier: Solid [S].

## Scope

In: the campaign report's decision sections and their data sources; label propagation; the wording rules as
checks on generated text; completeness on honest endings; determinism of these sections.
Out: the report verbs, package layout, statement model and resolution rule (BD-302); running the cascade and its
controls (BD-352, BD-345, BD-346); the reuse ledger's numbers (N28c-42, with the report's column refusal in
N28c-44); the budget-curve flag's computation (N28c-37 at cascade stage 3, a dependency because the finalists
section prints it); the studio's predictions and their scores (N28c-14, a dependency because the `predictions`
section prints them).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-opt/src/report/campaign.rs`; every value is read through the artifact graph at the campaign's
closing ledger frontier.

1. **Sections added to `report.json` (`frob.report/1`, kind `campaign`) and to `index.html`.**
   1. `phase_ledger`: per phase (`author`, `propose`, `check`, `synthesize`, `compile`, `screen`, `train`,
      `evaluate`, `qualify`, `certify`, `diagnose`, `wait`, `adjudicate`) the exclusive seconds and the resource
      seconds (device-seconds, core-seconds), cold and warm runs apart; the closure figure; `ledger-open` when
      more than 2% is unattributed.
   2. `ladder_modes`: one row per (rung pair, stratum): mode; the lower bootstrap bound of Kendall τ on the top
      band; the conformal margin q̂ and audit count n; the exchangeability martingale's value; the
      rank-agreement record identifier; `unmeasured` where no record exists (which is `uninformative`).
   3. `decision_cores`: per core that decided anything in the campaign: adoption status, policy name and
      version, table hash, decisions by rule (table, fallback, budget), and for shadow or canary the agreement
      and disagreement counts.
   4. `fallbacks_fired`: the list of (core, trigger, count, first decision record) from the decision records;
      empty list printed as "none fired", never omitted.
   5. `stage_controls`: for each of the six cascade stages its control and status: stage 1 "two designs that
      must merge and two that must split"; stage 2 "a mirror swap must reuse; a shared-bus edit must invalidate";
      stage 3 "the blinded stratified audit of discards and the planted winner"; stage 4 "a null race keeps the
      best with probability at least 1 − α"; stage 5 "planted cone and energy-pumping exploits must be caught";
      stage 6 "false-promotion rate at most α under adaptive retries on no-signal worlds". Status is `passed`,
      `failed` or `not-run` with the reason.
   6. `screening_audit`: audited discards m per stratum and reason; true top-k designs found among them; the
      bound on the rate of discarded winners 1 − δ^(1/m) at confidence 1 − δ (9.5% at m = 30 and δ = 0.05; 5.0% at
      m = 59); the planted winner's sealed identity (revealed at close) and whether it reached the finalists;
      prune-soundness samples and any suspended pruning rule.
   7. `finalists`: each with rank or survivor index, the paired verdict and its statistic, the absolute
      hard-limit verdict beside it, result label (`provisional`, `established` with adjudicator,
      `provisional: tier-sensitive`), rank-robustness type (`model-robust {variants, top-k overlap, energy
      audit}` or `model-conditional` with both orders), and flags `controller-limited`, `budget-truncated`,
      `screen-biased`.
   8. `reuse`: the three columns per edit class, read from the reuse ledger, never summed.
   9. `predictions`: the studio's registered predictions for the campaign (expected saving, suggested-experiment
      outcomes) with their scores.
   10. `ending`: one of `ranked-result`, `every-admitted-alternative-fails`, `tied-within-margin`,
       `inconclusive-at-budget`, with the denominators.
2. **Label propagation.** A campaign whose planted winner was lost, or whose audit is missing, carries
   `screen-biased` on every result from the affected strata. `budget-truncated` is carried by every finalist of a
   truncated stratum. A `not-run` control is carried as a condition on the campaign's claim product.
3. **Wording rules, enforced on the generated text and JSON by `report::lint` before a package is written.**
   - R1: the phrases "top k", "top-k", "the best k" and "winner" may describe finalists only if every rung and
     stratum that selected them was `selective`; otherwise the generator writes "k survivors" and the lint fails
     a template that does otherwise.
   - R2: within the row or sentence of a `controller-limited` design, the words "inferior", "worse", "loses" and
     "dominated" are forbidden; the flag text is "controller-limited: the estimated controller ceiling exceeds
     the measured score by more than the decision margin".
   - R3: a number tagged as a reuse share must have a column name (`exact-reuse`, `evidence-carried-forward`,
     `reacquisition-avoided`) and a regime (`cold` or `warm`); the three are never added in any cell.
   - R4: a `model-conditional` finalist's row must contain its condition and both orders; otherwise
     `FROB-REF-557`.
   - R5: advisory quantities (values of information, controller ceilings) appear only in sections marked
     advisory and never in a statement with a claim type.
   - R6: every section of the list in step 1 is present in every campaign report; a section with nothing to say
     says so explicitly.
4. **Completeness of losing campaigns.** The section list and key set of `report.json` are identical for a
   campaign that ends `ranked-result` and for one that ends otherwise; replay addresses and evidence links are
   present for discarded designs as for finalists.
5. **Determinism.** Rows are ordered by canonical keys (stratum key, rung pair, core identifier, design
   identifier); no wall-clock value enters these sections except durations, which are ledger values. Class D0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the seeded cascade population the campaign report contains all ten sections; `ladder_modes` has a row for
      every rung pair and stratum; `fallbacks_fired` equals a direct query of the decision records; the phase
      ledger closes within 2% (the clause of GATE-ATEL-16 this bead serves).
- [ ] Finalists selected through a `discard-only` or `uninformative` stratum are described as "k survivors"; the
      lint rejects a template with "top 3" in that case and accepts it when all selecting strata were
      `selective`.
- [ ] A campaign with no planted winner available prints the stage-3 control as `not-run` with the reason, and
      the condition is on the campaign's claim product.
- [ ] A `controller-limited` row with the word "inferior" fails the lint; a reuse share without column and regime
      fails; a `model-conditional` row without its condition is refused with `FROB-REF-557`.
- [ ] Screening-audit arithmetic: m = 30, δ = 0.05 prints 9.5%; m = 59 prints 5.0%.
- [ ] A campaign that ends "every admitted alternative fails" has the same `report.json` key set as a ranked one.
- [ ] The sections are byte-identical across two generations from the same graph, on both hosts.
- [ ] Every number in these sections has a claim identifier or an evidence identifier that resolves through
      `frob evidence trace`.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/report_campaign_atelier.sh --phase sections` passes at this bead's close, with its log.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead adds the phase `sections` to `scripts/e2e/report_campaign_atelier.sh` (the script belongs to N28c-20,
which depends on this bead; each phase runs alone with `--phase <name>`, and the first bead to land creates the
script from the STD-E2E template), so the decision sections are checked through the real binary at this bead's
close, on a small campaign this bead commits under `conformance/campaigns/sections-9/` (9 designs, the
population set `sections-9`, in three
strata, one measured `selective`, one `discard-only`, one unmeasured; a manifest that forces three core triggers;
no planted winner for one morphology class; one finalist flagged `controller-limited` by the stage-3 rule):
1. `frob opt cascade --spec conformance/campaigns/sections-9/spec.toml --population sections-9 --budget
   cpu_core_s=1800 --seed 9 --key sec9-1 --json` (the registry form of the verb, VERBS-opt: the brief by
   `--spec`, the candidates by `--population`, a budget in the registered units and an idempotency key; exit 8,
   then `frob job wait`), then
   `frob report campaign <id> --json` → all ten sections present in `report.json` and `index.html`;
   `ladder_modes` has a row per (rung pair, stratum) with one `unmeasured`; `fallbacks_fired` equals
   `frob cores show dc-03 --decisions <id> --json` and the other cores' listings (three entries); the phase
   ledger closes within 2%; stage 3 prints `not-run` with the reason, and the condition is on the campaign's
   claim product; the finalists of the non-`selective` strata are "k survivors"; the audit bound is printed
   with m and δ.
2. `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example atelier_harness -- report.lint --templates conformance/report-lint/
   --json` (the conformance crate has no binary, ADR-GAUNT-01 of BD-025; the studio's parameterized harnesses
   are subcommands of its example target `atelier_harness`, which the first bead to need it creates and to
   which this bead adds `report.lint`, a wrapper over `report::lint`) → each planted template is
   rejected with its rule named (R1 "top 3" under `discard-only`; R2 "inferior" in a `controller-limited` row;
   R3 a reuse share without column or regime, and a cell summing two columns; R4 a `model-conditional` row
   without its condition, refused with `FROB-REF-557`; R5 a controller ceiling in a claim statement; R6 an
   omitted empty section), and the clean twin of each is accepted.
3. Every number of the ten sections resolves through `frob evidence trace <id> --json` (numbers printed equal
   numbers resolved).
4. The report regenerated twice from the same graph gives identical section bytes.
Lines go to `target/e2e-logs/report_campaign_atelier/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with `bead` `N28c-19`, `step`
`sections.*`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block; `metric`
lines carry the section list, the counts of modes by kind, fallbacks listed against fallbacks recorded, the
phase-ledger closure against 2%, the audit bound against its expected value, numbers printed against numbers
resolved; per lint rule an `assert` with the template, the expected and observed outcome; `refusal` lines with
code, pointer and repairs; package digests as `digest` fields; on failure the JSON path of the first missing
section or unresolved number; a `summary` line. N28c-20 then runs the winning and losing campaigns of the
gate-scale fixtures.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-706: no top-k design among 30 audited discards bounds the false-discard rate at 9.5% with 95% confidence;
  59 bound it at 5.0%.
- Negative controls that must fail (registered against GATE-ATEL-16): a report generator that omits
  `fallbacks_fired` when the list is empty; one that prints "top 3" under `discard-only`; one that sums the three
  reuse columns; one that drops discarded designs' replay addresses from a losing campaign; one that prints a
  controller ceiling in the finalists' verdict column.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1 and 36; the Mac and the Linux host (D0).

## Considerations

- The wording lint is a guard on templates and generated strings, not a language model; it works on a closed
  vocabulary and structured rows, and its word lists live in the crate's contract so that changing them is
  reviewed.
- A report over a campaign whose evidence was partly collected by retention prints `evidence: unavailable` for
  the affected statements (the report bead's rule); these sections follow the same rule and never substitute a
  cached number.
- Size: a campaign can have hundreds of strata; `index.html` paginates the modes table and `report.json` is the
  complete form.
- OPEN-ATEL-5 (the planted winner for morphology classes with no earlier specialist result) makes `not-run` a
  common status at first; the report must make that visible, not routine.
