## Background

The design studio's chapter ends its mechanisms with a table of known answers and negative controls: for each
subject (an analysis, the fixed-n rule, a decision core, deduplication, revalidation, bisect) one case whose truth
is known by derivation or exact enumeration, and one negative control that must fail. A known-answer test shows
the implementation recovers an analytic or synthetic truth; the negative control shows the test can fail (a gate
that nothing can fail validates nothing). The mechanism beads each test their own rows. This bead is the battery
that carries the table as a whole: it registers every row as a named known-answer fixture with a pinned expected
value and tolerance, attaches the row's negative control to the negative-control registry, runs all rows in one
suite, and reports, per row, value, tolerance, and whether the control was caught. It is how the milestone exit
reviews see at a glance that no row of the table was dropped.

Rows are due at different milestones (static analyses at M1; decision cores DC-01, DC-09, DC-10 and dependency
revalidation at M3; the rest at M4), so the battery is built in three stages, each landing at its milestone so
that the M1 and M3 exits have the report too: N28cp1-01 (M1) builds the suite, the staging rule, the report and
the nine M1 rows; N28cp1-02 (M3) adds ten rows; this bead adds the nine M4 rows and the whole-table check. A row
whose mechanism does not exist yet is reported `not run`, which is not a pass, and the battery's milestone-M4
form requires every row. Tier: Solid [S].

## Scope

In: the fixtures, test functions and controls of the nine rows due at M4 (KA-688, KA-690, KA-691, KA-692, KA-696,
KA-697, KA-702, KA-705 and the registry row KA-706); the M4 form of the battery, in which every row of the table
and both additional registry rows must pass with their controls caught; the phase `m4` of
`scripts/e2e/atelier_known_answers.sh` and the full run of the script.
Out: the suite, the row record, the staging rule, the report and the M1 rows (N28cp1-01); the M3 rows and the
KA-703 control (N28cp1-02); implementing the mechanisms; the registries themselves (the known-answer registry
N28-KA; the counterexample registry N28-CX); the per-mechanism test files.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The nine M4 rows exist as fixtures with pinned expected values and tolerances and agree with the known-answer
      registry number for number; with them, all twenty-five table rows and the two additional registry rows exist.
- [ ] With `FROB_MILESTONE=M4` every row passes and every control is caught, including the KA-694 and KA-703
      controls defined by the earlier stages; no row is `not run` and none is still `to-be-rerun`.
- [ ] The report lists every row with its gate and owner; a missing row fails the generated-artifact check.
- [ ] The arithmetic of the M4 rows recomputes in exact arithmetic inside the suite, independent of the component
      under test: 86,185 and 500,500; 3.33 N and 6.67 N, 4 N and 8 N; the 38th of 39 residuals; 1 and 0 for the two
      f32 groupings; 9.5% at m = 30 and 5.0% at m = 59 for KA-706.
- [ ] Every control is recorded `caught` in the negative-control registry with session and date.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/atelier_known_answers.sh` passes in full (phases `m1`, `m3` and `m4`), with its log.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The battery is the test suite. Independent arithmetic checks added by this stage: `burnside_dihedral_six_slots`
((10⁶ + 20 + 200 + 1,000 + 30,000 + 3,000)/12 = 86,185); `mirror_only_count` (500,500); `drawbar_forces`
(0.2·1/0.06 = 3.33 N and 0.2·2/0.06 = 6.67 N, both below μ·N for μ = 0.4 and 0.8 at 30 N and 40 N; 3 A under 10 N
gives min(10, 4) = 4 N and min(10, 8) = 8 N); `conformal_index` (⌈(39 + 1)(1 − 0.05)⌉ = 38); `f32_regrouping`
((10⁸ + (−10⁸)) + 1 = 1 and 10⁸ + (−10⁸ + 1) = 0 in binary32); `audit_bound_values` (1 − 0.05^(1/30) = 0.0950 and
1 − 0.05^(1/59) = 0.0495); `m4_form_no_not_run` (a scratch set with one M4 row unimplemented fails the M4 form).
The independent checks of the M1 and M3 rows are in their stages.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/atelier_known_answers.sh` (created by N28cp1-01 with phase `m1`; N28cp1-02 adds `m3`). This bead
adds the phase `m4` and runs the script whole with `FROB_MILESTONE=M4`. Phase `m4`: the suite with
`FROB_MILESTONE=M4`; `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example registry_report -- ka --chapter 28 --json` (the
report of N28cp1-01: the `ka` subcommand of the conformance crate's example target `registry_report`, the crate
having no binary under ADR-GAUNT-01 of BD-025; all twenty-seven rows pass, none `not run`); and from the user's
side `frob design bisect --good v0 --bad v5 --predicate succeeds --task <the fixture's task> --key
ka702-$FROB_E2E_SEED --json` on the six-version lineage pass, fail, pass, pass, pass, fail (an `a-change` at the
last edit, and `FROB-REF-550` on the same request with `--require first-change` and no scan, under its own key
`ka702-fc-$FROB_E2E_SEED`; `design.bisect` is a `run` verb, and without a key the dispatcher's 805 would answer
first, KA-702), `frob analyze identifiability` on the drawbar fixture (the motor-limited runs
rejected and the traction-limited replacement, KA-688), `frob analyze wrench` on the infeasible task wrench (a dual
vector that re-checks in rational arithmetic, KA-690), `frob analyze regimes` on the run with one planted mode
switch (two regions split at the switch tick, KA-691). The earlier phases use `frob analyze symmetry` (KA-680),
`frob analyze bus-schedule` (KA-687), `frob eval preanalyze` (KA-693), `frob cores show dc-01 --rows` (KA-695) and
`frob nemesis cover` (KA-704). It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/atelier_known_answers/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead` (`N28c-24` for this phase), `step` (`m4.*`), `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and, for
verb calls, the envelope's `evidence` block; per row a `metric` line with measured, expected, tolerance and unit;
an `assert` line per control (`caught: true`); `refusal` lines with code, pointer and repairs for the 550 check;
on failure the row, both values and the fixture path; a `summary` with rows passed, failed and `not run`, which
must be 27, 0 and 0.

### Known answers and negative controls

The table is the content. Determinism lanes: exact rows are identical on both hosts and in debug and release;
KA-686 and KA-703 are compared after rounding to the printed digits; KA-691 and KA-692 run on the referee and
lockstep tiers as their registry rows state.

## Considerations

- A control must be a defect whose value is clearly outside the row's tolerance (the KA-703 control of
  N28cp1-02 is the E[S]² variant for that reason).
- The plan prints the 5th-percentile coverage for KA-696's companion statistic as 0.885 in one place and 0.884
  in another; the exact value is 0.8840 and that is what the conformal test uses.
- Several subjects' fixtures are owned by other subsystems (clearance for A-18, wrench attainability for A-24,
  the docking fixture for KA-701); the battery calls their components and does not copy their fixtures.
- A row whose expected value changes is a change to a known answer and needs the derivation on file updated
  first; the suite's fixtures are never regenerated from the implementation.
