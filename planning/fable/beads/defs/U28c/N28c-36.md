## Background

Controller-budget curves extrapolate, for each robot design, the score it would reach with unlimited controller
effort: the ceiling J_d∞ of the saturating law J_d(b) = J_d∞ − a_d·(c(b)/c(b₀))^(−γ) fitted to scores at three
controller budgets. The estimate steers which designs get more controller effort. An estimate that steers
allocation needs an honest interval, and the plan is explicit about which interval that is: "Its interval is not
the standard error of the fit: it is the conformal margin of the ladder for the predictor ĝ = J_d∞, calibrated on
audit-lane designs that were scored at the specialist rung. Where no calibration exists the interval is the
metric's declared span, and the rule reduces to the fixed table."

Why not the fit's standard error: with three budgets the three-point form has no residual at all, and the
least-squares form has one degree of freedom; either would give an interval that reflects sampling noise in three
numbers and says nothing about whether a saturating law describes this body. A conformal margin makes no
assumption about the law. It asks only how far the predictor has been from the truth on designs exchangeable with
this one, where the truth is a specialist's score. The audit lane supplies those designs: they are drawn by a
keyed random stream before any score exists, run at the higher rung at a fixed n, and are never cancelled, so
they are exchangeable with the population by construction. Calibrating on the designs that advanced is biased
toward agreement and is forbidden. Tier: Frontier [F].

## Scope

In: the calibration set and its admissibility; the margin; the interval; the floor and the span fallback; the
exchangeability alarm; per-stratum bookkeeping; the synthetic calibration harness used by the known-answer gate.
Out: the fit (N28c-35); the escalation rule that consumes the interval (N28c-37); the audit lane itself and the
ladder's own margins (the ladder beads).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-opt/src/budget_curve/interval.rs`, feature `frontier-budget-curve`.

1. **Calibration record.** Key: (morphology class, task family, controller family, budget ladder, estimator
   identifier and version, stratum, model identities). Members: audit-lane designs i with a `Fit` status and a
   specialist-rung score y_i (the mean score of a policy trained or inherited for that design alone, on the same
   addresses). Residual rᵢ = |yᵢ − J_i∞|. Audit designs whose fit was refused are excluded from the residual set
   and counted (`unfit_in_audit`); they need no interval because refused designs always escalate.
2. **Margin.** With n residuals, q̂ is the ⌈(n + 1)(1 − α)⌉-th smallest (α = 0.05 by default). The index exists
   only for n ≥ 19 at α = 0.05; with fewer the record has no margin.
3. **Interval.** For a design d with a `Fit` status in a stratum that has a margin:
   [J_d∞ − q̂, J_d∞ + q̂], intersected with the metric's declared span. Guarantee: for a design exchangeable with
   the calibration set, its specialist score lies within q̂ of J_d∞ with probability at least 1 − α, on average
   over calibration draws (with n = 39 coverage averages 0.95 and falls below 0.884 in one draw in twenty).
4. **Fallbacks, fail-closed.**
   - No calibration record, or n < 19, or the alarm has fired, or the identities differ (a new kernel version,
     effort profile, estimator version or ladder): the interval is the metric's declared span. The escalation
     rule then gets no information from the curve and reduces to the ladder's fixed table.
   - Strata are merged upward to the morphology class before a stratum falls back (the same budgeted mode as the
     survivor-margin core), and the merge is recorded.
5. **Exchangeability alarm.** The conformal p-values of successive audit designs feed the same test martingale
   the ladder uses; at a value of 20 (1/α at α = 0.05) the stratum's calibration is void, intervals revert to the
   span, and calibration restarts inside the new regime with residuals observed after the alarm only.
6. **No pooling across identities.** Residuals computed under one estimator version, ladder, kernel version or
   effort profile are never pooled with another's (the pooling refusal of the studio applies); a change resets
   the record.
7. **Output.** `CeilingInterval { lo, hi, source: Conformal { q_hat, n, alpha, record } | DeclaredSpan { reason },
   stratum }`, both ends advisory quantities. The envelope of any verb that prints J∞ prints the interval and its
   source beside it.
8. **Synthetic calibration harness** (used by the known-answer gate before any specialist exists).
   `frob_conformance::budget_curve::synthetic(seed)` draws designs from the law with planted
   (J∞ ~ U[0.5, 0.95], a ~ U[0.05, 0.40], γ ~ U[0.3, 1.5]), an effort ratio of 4, three levels, 8 worlds, and
   paired noise (per-world score noise with standard deviation 0.03, a shared per-world component of standard
   deviation 0.05 common to all levels, so that increments are less noisy than levels); it scores a calibration
   set of 100 synthetic audit designs against their planted J∞ and tests coverage on 1,000 fresh designs. The
   harness labels everything `synthetic`. A proportion of threshold designs (scores flat then jumping) is mixed
   in to check that refusals are excluded from coverage accounting and counted separately.

Determinism: the margin is an order statistic of ledger rows at a pinned frontier (class D0 given the rows).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Margin index: n = 39, α = 0.05 → the 38th smallest residual; n = 19 → the 19th; n = 18 → no margin and the
      interval is the declared span with reason `under-19-calibration-points`.
- [ ] On the synthetic harness, with 100 calibration designs and 1,000 test designs, the interval covers the
      planted J∞ in at least 92% of test designs with a `Fit` status (1 − α − 0.03 at α = 0.05); measured coverage
      is recorded.
- [ ] Refused fits are excluded from the coverage denominator and reported as a count; they are never assigned an
      interval other than the span.
- [ ] A calibration set built from survivors only (planted: residuals from designs that advanced) under-covers on
      the synthetic fixture where the residual correlates with the cheap score (measured coverage below 0.90),
      and the build refuses such a set (`FROB-REF-546`).
- [ ] A planted drift (the planted γ of new designs shifts from 0.8 to 0.3 after the 40th audit design) trips the
      alarm within 20 further audit designs in at least 95 of 100 seeded runs; after the alarm the interval is
      the span until 19 new residuals exist.
- [ ] Residuals under two estimator versions are never pooled (the attempt is refused with `FROB-REF-548`).
- [ ] Every printed J∞ carries its interval and the interval's source.
- [ ] The interval is intersected with the declared span and both ends are advisory types.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/budget_curve_interval.rs`: `margin_index_points` (39 → 38; 19 → 19; 18 → none);
`interval_is_j_inf_plus_minus_q` (J∞ = 0.726, q̂ = 0.05 → [0.676, 0.776]); `intersect_with_span` (J∞ = 0.97,
q̂ = 0.05 on a unit score → [0.92, 1.0]); `span_fallback_reasons` (no record; under 19; alarm; identity change);
`merge_upward_before_fallback`; `unfit_audit_designs_excluded_and_counted`; `survivors_only_rejected`;
`no_pooling_across_estimator_versions`; `alarm_resets_calibration`; `synthetic_coverage_at_least_0_92`
(seeded; 10 repetitions, each above the threshold within the binomial tolerance for 1,000 trials);
`coverage_beta_spread` (over 10³ calibration draws of n = 39 the 5th percentile of coverage is 0.884 ± 0.005).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/budget_curve.sh` (N28c-39) runs the synthetic harness through
`frob-conformance run budget-curve.coverage --designs 1000 --calibration 100 --seed <seed> --json` and the verb
`frob eval compare --budget-curve` on fixtures with and without a calibration record; for this bead it logs the
margin, n, α, measured coverage against 0.92, the refused count, the interval source per design, and the alarm
state.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-696 (the 38th ordered residual at n = 39, α = 0.05) and the floor n ≥ 19.
- Negative controls that must fail: the standard error of the fit used as the interval (on the synthetic fixture
  with threshold designs and misspecified rates its coverage falls far below 0.92); calibration from survivors
  only; a margin extrapolated to a stratum with 12 residuals.
- Determinism lanes: the margin and intervals are identical twice on one machine and on both hosts for the same
  ledger frontier.

## Considerations

- The guarantee is about the specialist's score, not about a metaphysical ceiling: a specialist with a limited
  training budget defines the truth the interval covers. The oracle of the design-comparison gate adds the
  sampling controller at 8,192 samples as a second reference for the same reason.
- Before M4 no audit-lane specialists exist on real populations; on real designs the interval is therefore the
  declared span and the rule is inert, which is the intended fail-closed behaviour. The synthetic harness is
  what the M3 known-answer gate measures.
- Coverage is on average over calibration draws and moves by several points at small n; a report must not
  present 95% as a guarantee for one calibration set.
- OPEN-ATEL-2 (the definition of a stratum) determines how quickly a stratum reaches 19 residuals.
