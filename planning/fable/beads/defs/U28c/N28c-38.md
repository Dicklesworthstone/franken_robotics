## Background

`frob eval compare A B --task T` is the studio's paired comparison: both designs meet the same scenes,
disturbances and randomization draws, and the verdict comes from a declared study (anytime-valid or fixed-n). A
comparison run under a cheap scorer has a blind spot: it compares body × controller at one controller budget. The
plan's worked case shows why that matters: design A scores 0.61, 0.70, 0.72 at three budgets and design B scores
0.55, 0.68, 0.76; the fixed-budget verdict at the base budget prefers A by 0.06 and at the top budget prefers B by
0.04; the curves cross between the second and third budgets; A is saturated and B is limited by its controller.

The plan gives the surface for seeing this: "`eval.compare --budget-curve` returns, per design, the scores by
budget, J∞ with its interval, the saturation index and the flag", and "`explain.compare` prints both curves".
This bead is that surface. It adds nothing to the verdict: the measured contrast at the highest budget reached
goes through the ordinary comparison path unchanged and is the only quantity that can become `established`; the
curve quantities are advisory, typed so they cannot enter a verdict field. Tier: Frontier [F], behind a feature;
with the feature off the option does not exist.

## Scope

In: the option on `eval.compare`; what is run; the output block; the crossing indicator; the `explain.compare`
view; refusals and exit codes; token budgets.
Out: the budget coordinate, rows, fit and interval (their beads); the escalation rule in the cascade (N28c-37);
the comparison's own statistics (the comparison beads).

## Technical approach

1. **Registry.** `eval.compare` (kind `run`, takes an idempotency key and a budget, accepts the bounded
   synchronous wait and otherwise returns a job handle with exit 8) gains the option `--budget-curve` and
   `--ladder <id>` (default: the registered default ladder of each design's controller kind; both designs must
   use the same ladder). The output schema gains an optional `advisory.budget_curve` object. `explain.compare`
   (kind `read`) gains the curves section. Both changes are generated from the verb registry; the capability is
   listed as Frontier.
2. **What runs.** For each design and each ladder level, the comparison's world addresses are evaluated under the
   controller at that level (the sweep of the budget coordinate, on identical addresses for both designs and all
   levels). Episodes already in the memo graph are served; `--estimate` returns the cost of the missing ones.
   Under the sampling controller the default screening allowance applies: three episodes per design per level,
   or up to the break-even count of the class for a paired screening comparison (30 for the quadruped setting and
   60 for the humanoid at a 20 ms planning step), and the result is labelled `screening: sampling-control`.
3. **Output (within `frob.envelope/1`).**
   - `result.verdict` and its statistic: unchanged; computed at the highest budget both designs reached, by the
     declared study type, with the accounting identity and the tier label as for any comparison. Under the
     sampling controller it remains a screening result and is not acceptance evidence.
   - `result.advisory.budget_curve`:
     ```json
     { "ladder": "mpc-sample.ns-x4", "efforts": ["2048","8192","32768"], "unit": "model-steps/tick",
       "designs": [ { "design": "d:…", "scores": [0.61, 0.70, 0.72], "worlds_used": 8,
                      "increments": [ {"delta": 0.09, "se": 0.012, "dof": 7}, {"delta": 0.02, "se": 0.004, "dof": 7} ],
                      "fit": { "status": "fit", "estimator": ["three-point", 1], "rho": 0.222 },
                      "j_inf": 0.726, "interval": { "lo": 0.676, "hi": 0.776,
                                                    "source": "conformal", "q_hat": 0.05, "n": 39, "alpha": 0.05 },
                      "saturation": 0.95, "flag": "saturated" },
                    { "design": "d:…", "scores": [0.55, 0.68, 0.76], "…": "…",
                      "j_inf": 0.888, "saturation": 0.62, "flag": "controller-limited" } ],
       "crossing": { "contrast_at_base": 0.06, "contrast_at_top": -0.04, "sign_change": true,
                     "significant": { "base": true, "top": false }, "between_levels": [1, 2] } }
     ```
     The subtree is marked advisory in the schema (`x-frob-advisory`). Where no calibration exists `interval.source`
     is `declared-span` with the reason. A refused fit prints `fit.status: "curve-unfit"` with its reason, no
     `j_inf`, and the flag `curve-unfit`.
   - `evidence`: the claim product of the comparison as usual; the advisory block is class `Estimated` and adds no
     claim.
   - `next_actions`: when a design is `controller-limited` or `curve-unfit`, the first suggestion is to evaluate
     it at the next rung (a fine-tune or a specialist) with the pre-filled command; when the curves cross, a
     suggestion to compare at the top budget with more pairs.
4. **Crossing indicator.** From the per-world paired differences of the two designs at the base and top levels:
   `sign_change` is true when the mean contrasts have opposite signs; `significant` reports, per level, whether
   the contrast exceeds two paired standard errors; `between_levels` names the adjacent levels between which the
   sign of the contrast changes. It is a description, not a verdict.
5. **`explain.compare`.** For a comparison that has budget rows, the explanation adds a section "Controller
   budget": a two-row table of scores by level for each design, each design's J∞ with interval and source, the
   saturation index, the flag, and one sentence stating whether the ordering depends on budget ("A leads by 0.06
   at the base budget; B leads by 0.04 at the top budget; B is controller-limited"). Token budget for the
   section: at most 250 tokens (TARGET). The wording rule applies: a `controller-limited` design is never
   described as inferior.
6. **Refusals and exits.** The option inherits the comparison's refusals (a paired study under an uncontrolled
   determinism class; a pairing keyed by event order; an anytime study on a metric with no declared span).
   Additional schema refusals: the two designs' controllers are on different ladders; a ladder with fewer than
   three levels. Citing any value of the advisory block as a verdict is refused (`FROB-REF-872`). With the feature
   off the option is refused as a capability not implemented (exit 5).
7. **Cancellation.** The sweep is a job; cancellation drains at chunk boundaries; completed levels remain as rows
   and memoized episodes; a cancelled sweep publishes no advisory block for incomplete curves.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob eval compare A B --task T --budget-curve --json` on the worked fixture returns the block above:
      J∞ 0.726 and 0.888, saturation 0.95 and 0.62, flags `saturated` and `controller-limited`, crossing between
      levels 1 and 2 with contrasts +0.06 and −0.04.
- [ ] The verdict fields are byte-identical to those of the same comparison run at the top budget without the
      option (the option changes nothing in the verdict).
- [ ] On the threshold fixture (0.20, 0.20, 0.80) the design prints `curve-unfit` with reason `flat-start`, no
      J∞, and the next action suggests the next rung.
- [ ] Without a calibration record the interval's source is `declared-span`; with one it is `conformal` with q̂, n
      and α.
- [ ] All levels of both designs ran on the same address list (asserted from the rows); a repeated call serves
      every episode from the memo graph.
- [ ] `frob explain compare <comparison> --json` contains the "Controller budget" section within 250 tokens and
      passes the wording rule.
- [ ] `FROB-REF-872` on citing `j_inf`; schema refusal on mismatched ladders; exit 5 with the feature off.
- [ ] The verb is reachable through `frob --json` and the MCP surface with the updated schema; cancellation is
      tested (request, drain, finalize; no leaked device resources).

`crates/frob-verbs/tests/eval_compare_budget_curve.rs`: `block_matches_worked_case`;
`verdict_unchanged_by_option` (digest of the verdict subtree equal with and without the option);
`advisory_subtree_marked`; `unfit_prints_reason_no_j_inf`; `interval_source_declared_span_when_uncalibrated`;
`crossing_indicator_fields` (+0.06, −0.04, sign change, levels [1, 2]); `no_crossing_on_parallel_curves`;
`different_ladders_refused`; `two_level_ladder_refused`; `feature_off_exit_5`; `citing_j_inf_is_872`;
`screening_label_under_sampling_control`; `explain_section_within_token_budget`;
`explain_never_says_inferior_for_controller_limited`; `cancel_leaves_complete_levels_only`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/budget_curve.sh` (N28c-39) is the script for this capability; it runs the verb on the worked,
threshold and uncalibrated fixtures and on a real three-level sweep of the reference quadruped template, then
`frob explain compare`. Logged for this bead: the exact commands, exit codes, wall times, the envelope's
`evidence` block, each printed value with its expected value and tolerance, the verdict-subtree digests with and
without the option, the refusal lines, and the token count of the explanation section.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The worked case values (0.726, 0.888, 0.95, 0.62; contrasts +0.06 and −0.04) and the threshold refusal.
- Negative controls that must fail: an implementation that reports the verdict at the base budget when higher
  levels exist; one that puts J∞ into the verdict's `statistic`; one that runs levels on different address sets;
  one whose explanation calls the controller-limited design "worse".
- Determinism lanes: the envelope (after removing `cost.wall_ms`) is byte-identical twice on one machine and
  across worker counts at class D2.

## Considerations

- Results under the sampling controller carry `model-privileged` unless the planning model was drawn
  independently of the plant's randomization; the block repeats that flag so a reader does not mistake a
  clairvoyant planner's ceiling for a deployable controller's.
- The option multiplies screening cost by the ladder (1 + 4 + 16 = 21 units of the base level for the default
  ratio); `--estimate` must show that before anything runs, and session budgets apply.
- For learned-policy ladders the fit is unavailable until the base effort question is settled; the block then
  prints scores by level and `fit.status: "curve-unfit"` with reason `effort-undefined`, which is still useful
  as a description.
- If KILL-BUDGETCURVE fires, the option remains as a descriptive view (scores by budget and the saturation index
  as a description) and the flag is no longer used for allocation.
