## Background

Controller-budget curves are a Frontier mechanism of the design studio: each design is scored at three controller
budgets on shared world addresses; a saturating law J_d(b) = J_d∞ − a_d·(c(b)/c(b₀))^(−γ) gives an estimate J_d∞
of the score at unlimited controller effort; a conformal margin calibrated on audit designs gives its interval;
the saturation index s_d = (J(b_max) − J(b₀))/(J_d∞ − J(b₀)) says how much of the predicted gain is already
realized; and designs whose curves the law cannot describe are refused (`curve-unfit`) instead of extrapolated.
The user-visible surface is `frob eval compare A B --task T --budget-curve`.

Gate GATE-ATEL-17 (claim kind: Known answer; first milestone M3): on 10³ synthetic designs drawn from the law with
planted (J∞, a, γ) and paired noise, the calibrated interval covers the planted J∞ at a rate of at least
1 − α − 0.03; the worked case returns 0.726, 0.888, 0.95 and 0.62; the threshold case is refused. This bead is the
end-to-end script for the capability and the measurement of that gate. It is a known-answer gate on purpose: it
shows the arithmetic and the refusals are right before any claim is made that the mechanism helps; whether it
helps is the separate design-comparison gate at M4, with its kill gate.

## Scope

In: the script, fixtures, assertions, logging and the gate record.
Out: the mechanism beads under test; the oracle experiment (N28c-40); the pilot (N28c-32); the cascade
integration script.

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/budget_curve.sh`: plain shell over the real `frob` binary (built with the `frontier-budget-curve`
feature) and a private `frobd` on the CPU tier; sources the STD-E2E helper; idempotent; seed-explicit
(`FROB_E2E_SEED`). Fixtures under `conformance/budget-curve/`:

- `worked/`: a recorded ledger with two designs, 8 shared world addresses and three levels of the ladder
  `mpc-sample.ns-x4`, whose per-world scores average to (0.61, 0.70, 0.72) and (0.55, 0.68, 0.76); rows labelled
  `synthetic`.
- `threshold/`: one design averaging (0.20, 0.20, 0.80).
- `calibrated/`: `worked/` plus a calibration record of 39 synthetic audit designs with 38th-smallest residual
  0.05.
- A real fixture: the reference quadruped template and one ±10% variant, the flat velocity task, 3 addresses.

Steps:
1. `start`: commit, toolchain, host fingerprint, seed, feature set.
2. Worked case: `frob eval compare d:A d:B --task flat-velocity --budget-curve --fixture worked --json` → assert
   per design: scores by budget; ρ 0.222 and 0.615; `j_inf` 0.726 and 0.888 (three decimals); `saturation` 0.95
   and 0.62 (two decimals); flags; `crossing.contrast_at_base` +0.06, `contrast_at_top` −0.04,
   `between_levels` [1, 2]. These four numbers are the second clause of the gate.
3. Verdict untouched: run the same comparison at the top level without the option; assert the digest of the
   `verdict` subtree equals that of step 2.
4. Threshold case: `… --fixture threshold …` → `fit.status == "curve-unfit"`, reason `flat-start`, no `j_inf`
   key, flag `curve-unfit`, a next action proposing the next rung. This is the third clause of the gate.
5. Interval source: on `worked/` the interval's source is `declared-span`; on `calibrated/` it is `conformal`
   with `q_hat` 0.05, `n` 39, `alpha` 0.05, and A's interval is [0.676, 0.776].
6. Synthetic coverage (first clause of the gate):
   `frob-conformance run budget-curve.coverage --designs 1000 --calibration 100 --alpha 0.05 --seed $SEED --json`
   draws designs from the law with planted J∞ in [0.5, 0.95], a in [0.05, 0.40], γ in [0.3, 1.5], an effort
   ratio of 4, 8 worlds and paired noise, fits each through the same library code the verb uses, calibrates on
   100 and tests on 1,000. Assert: coverage of the planted J∞ among fitted designs ≥ 0.92; refused share
   reported; the planted threshold designs mixed into the draw (5%) are all refused. Repeat with three seeds; all
   three must pass; the minimum is the recorded value.
7. Real sweep: `frob eval compare quad-a quad-a-v1 --task flat-velocity --budget-curve --addresses 3 --json` →
   exit 0, 2 or 8 then completion; assert 18 rows (2 designs × 3 levels × 3 addresses) on one address list, the
   result label `screening: sampling-control`, the flag `model-privileged`, a `fit` object for each design
   (whatever its status), and that a second identical call is served entirely from the memo graph
   (`cost.gpu_s == 0`, `cost.cpu_core_s` below 1).
8. Controller identity: `frob control show` for the three controllers of `quad-a` → equal family identifier,
   efforts 2,048, 8,192 and 32,768 model steps per tick.
9. Advisory wall: `frob eval promote <the comparison's j_inf reference>` and `frob report design quad-a --cite
   <same>` → exit 3, `FROB-REF-872`.
10. Explanation: `frob explain compare <comparison of step 2> --json` → the "Controller budget" section is
    present, at most 250 tokens, and contains no forbidden word in the controller-limited design's sentence.
11. Refusal paths: mismatched ladders → schema refusal (exit 3); the binary built without the feature → exit 5.
12. Cancellation: start the real sweep, cancel after the first level completes; assert the job drains, rows
    exist for the completed level only, and no advisory block is published.
13. Determinism: steps 2, 4 and 5 twice; envelopes equal byte for byte after removing `cost.wall_ms`; step 7's
    world digests equal across `--workers 1` and `--workers 36`.
14. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; gates touched: `GATE-ATEL-17`; write the gate row: coverage
    (minimum over seeds) against 0.92, the four worked numbers, the refusal.

**Logging.** `target/e2e-logs/budget_curve/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript beside it. Every line: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N28c-39`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), exact
command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities,
seed); `metric` lines with each asserted value, its expected value, tolerance and unit (coverage against 0.92;
J∞ and s; efforts in model-steps/tick; row counts; token counts); `refusal` lines with code, pointer and repairs;
`digest` fields for verdict subtrees, address lists and world digests; on failure the first differing value and
the fixture or ledger path needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line. No private-scene data.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The gate's known answers: ρ_A = 0.02/0.09 = 0.222, J_A∞ = 0.61 + 0.09/0.778 = 0.726, s_A = 0.11/0.116 = 0.95;
  ρ_B = 0.08/0.13 = 0.615, J_B∞ = 0.55 + 0.13/0.385 = 0.888, s_B = 0.21/0.338 = 0.62; the threshold case
  (0.20, 0.20, 0.80), whose two lower budgets would extrapolate to 0.20, wrong by at least 0.60.
- Planted defects the script must catch (registered against GATE-ATEL-17): a fit that returns J∞ = 0.20 for the
  threshold case; an interval taken from the fit's standard error (synthetic coverage falls below 0.92); a
  calibration from survivors only; levels run on different address sets; J∞ written into the verdict.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1 and 36; debug and release; the Mac and the Linux host
  for the fixture steps (identical envelopes).

## Considerations

- The synthetic designs are drawn from the law itself, so coverage here says the calibration procedure is
  implemented correctly; it says nothing about whether real designs follow the law. The pilot's refused share and
  the M4 oracle experiment address that.
- The real sweep costs 2 designs × 3 addresses × (64 + 256 + 1,024) samples per tick on the CPU tier; budget
  under 20 minutes on the large workstation's CPU (TARGET). The script accepts `--skip-real` for quick local
  runs, and the gate record requires the full run.
- If the pilot's falsifier holds and the mechanism is not built, this script and its gate are recorded as
  `killed: fallback adopted` under KILL-BUDGETCURVE, and only the descriptive saturation index remains.
