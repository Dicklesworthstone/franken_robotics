## Background

Controller-budget curves are a Frontier mechanism of the design studio: each design is scored at three controller
budgets on shared world addresses; a saturating law J_d(b) = J_d∞ − a_d·(c(b)/c(b₀))^(−γ) gives an estimate J_d∞
of the score at unlimited controller effort; a conformal margin calibrated on audit designs gives its interval;
the saturation index s_d = (J(b_max) − J(b₀))/(J_d∞ − J(b₀)) says how much of the predicted gain is already
realized; and designs whose curves the law cannot describe are refused (`curve-unfit`) instead of extrapolated.
The user-visible surface is `frob eval compare A B --task T --budget-curve`.

Gate GATE-ATEL-17 (claim kind: Known answer; first milestone M3): on 10³ synthetic designs drawn from the law with
planted (J∞, a, γ) and paired noise, the calibrated interval covers the planted J∞ at a rate of at least
1 − α − 0.03; the worked case returns 0.726, 0.888, 0.95 and 0.62; the threshold case is refused. This bead is the
end-to-end script for the capability and the measurement of that gate. It is a known-answer gate on purpose: it
shows the arithmetic and the refusals are right before any claim is made that the mechanism helps; whether it
helps is the separate design-comparison gate at M4, with its kill gate.

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/budget_curve.sh`: plain shell over the real `frob` binary (built with the `frontier-budget-curve`
feature) and a private `frobd` on the CPU tier; sources the STD-E2E helper; idempotent; seed-explicit
(`FROB_E2E_SEED`). Fixtures under `conformance/budget-curve/`:

- `worked/`: a recorded ledger with two designs, 8 shared world addresses and three levels of the ladder
  `mpc-sample.ns-x4`, whose per-world scores average to (0.61, 0.70, 0.72) and (0.55, 0.68, 0.76); rows labelled
  `synthetic`.
- `threshold/`: `worked/` with design B replaced by the threshold design, which averages (0.20, 0.20, 0.80); a
  comparison needs two designs, so the verb is called on `d:A d:B` in every store.
- `calibrated/`: `worked/` plus a calibration record of 39 synthetic audit designs with 38th-smallest residual
  0.05.
- A real fixture: the reference quadruped template and one ±10% variant, the flat velocity task, 3 addresses.

Each recorded fixture is a store: a step that names one empties the run's state root (`FROB_STATE`, which
`e2e_init` exports) and restores the store into it, so the verb serves every episode from the memo graph
(`frob` reads no `FROB_HOME` and has no `--root`, STD-E2E's isolation rule); no product verb takes a fixture
option. Step 8 belongs to phase `ladder`, step 6 to
phase `coverage`, steps 2 to 5, 7 and 9 to 12 to phase `verb`, and steps 1, 13, 14 and 15 to this bead's phase `gate`
(the phases `rows` and `fit` of N28c-34 and N28c-35 run their own harness steps and have no numbered step here);
`--phase gate` runs the whole script.

Steps:
1. `start`: commit, toolchain, host fingerprint, seed, feature set.
2. Worked case (store `worked/`): `frob eval compare d:A d:B --task flat-velocity --budget-curve --key bc-w-1 --json` → assert
   per design: scores by budget; ρ 0.222 and 0.615; `j_inf` 0.726 and 0.888 (three decimals); `saturation` 0.95
   and 0.62 (two decimals); flags; `crossing.contrast_at_base` +0.06, `contrast_at_top` −0.04,
   `between_levels` [1, 2]. These four numbers are the second clause of the gate.
3. Verdict untouched: run the same comparison at the top level without the option; assert the digest of the
   `verdict` subtree equals that of step 2.
4. Threshold case (store `threshold/`): the command of step 2 with `--key bc-t-1` → for design B
   `fit.status == "curve-unfit"`, reason `flat-start`, no `j_inf` key, flag `curve-unfit`, a next action
   proposing the next rung; design A is fitted as in step 2. This is the third clause of the gate.
5. Interval source: on `worked/` the interval's source is `declared-span`; on `calibrated/` it is `conformal`
   with `q_hat` 0.05, `n` 39, `alpha` 0.05, and A's interval is [0.676, 0.776].
6. Synthetic coverage (first clause of the gate):
   `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example atelier_harness -- budget-curve.coverage --designs 1000 --calibration
   100 --draws 10 --alpha 0.05 --seed "$s" --json` (the harness of N28c-36: a subcommand of the conformance
   crate's example target, the crate having no binary under ADR-GAUNT-01 of BD-025) draws designs from the law with planted J∞ in [0.5, 0.95], a in [0.05, 0.40], γ in [0.3, 1.5], an
   effort ratio of 4, 8 worlds and paired noise, fits each through the same library code the verb uses, and tests
   1,000 designs against ten independent calibration sets of 100. Assert: the coverage of the planted J∞ among
   fitted designs, averaged over the ten calibration draws, is ≥ 0.92; per-draw coverages and the refused share
   reported; the planted threshold designs mixed into the draw (5%) are all refused (by `flat-start`, or by
   `accelerating` when noise lifts Δ₁ above its band). Run for three seeds `s`: `$FROB_E2E_SEED`,
   `$FROB_E2E_SEED + 1` and `$FROB_E2E_SEED + 2`, each logged; all three must pass; the minimum is the recorded
   value. A single calibration draw of 100 is not the unit of the check: its coverage is
   Beta(96, 5), below 0.92 about one time in ten, so three single draws would fail a correct tree about one run
   in four; the ten-draw mean fails it with probability about 10⁻³ per seed.
7. Real sweep: `frob eval compare quad-a quad-a-v1 --task flat-velocity --budget-curve --key bc-r-1 --json`
   (three addresses: the sampling controller's default screening allowance per design and level, N28c-38;
   `eval.compare` has no address-list option, and both designs and all levels share the comparison's own world
   sample) → exit 0, 2 or 8 then completion; assert 18 rows (2 designs × 3 levels × 3 addresses) on one address list, the
   result label `screening: sampling-control`, the flag `model-privileged`, a `fit` object for each design
   (whatever its status), and that a second identical call is served entirely from the memo graph
   (`cost.gpu_s == 0`, `cost.cpu_core_s` below 1).
8. Controller identity: `frob control show` for the three controllers of `quad-a` → equal family identifier,
   efforts 2,048, 8,192 and 32,768 model steps per tick.
9. Advisory wall: `frob eval promote <the comparison's j_inf reference> --key bc-adv-$FROB_E2E_SEED --json`
   (`eval.promote` is a `run` verb: without a key the dispatcher's `FROB-REF-805` would answer before 872) and
   `frob report design quad-a --cite <same> --json` → exit 3, `FROB-REF-872`.
10. Explanation: `frob explain compare <comparison of step 2> --json` → the "Controller budget" section is
    present, at most 250 tokens, and contains no forbidden word in the controller-limited design's sentence.
11. Refusal paths: mismatched ladders → schema refusal (exit 3); the binary built without the feature → exit 5.
12. Cancellation: with the run's state root emptied (step 7's rows and memoized episodes would otherwise serve
    every level at once, and its key would return its stored result), start the real sweep under a key of its
    own (`frob eval compare quad-a quad-a-v1 --task flat-velocity --budget-curve --key
    bc-cancel-$FROB_E2E_SEED --json`, exit 8 with a job handle) and cancel it with `frob job cancel <job> --json`
    once the first level's rows exist; assert the job drains, rows exist for the completed level only, and no
    advisory block is published.
13. Determinism: steps 2, 4 and 5 twice; envelopes equal byte for byte after removing `cost.wall_ms`; step 7's
    world digests equal with the private daemon's worker pool configured to 1 and to 36 workers (BD-160's pool
    configuration, set when the script starts `frobd`; `eval.compare` takes no worker option).
14. Gate receipt: `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-ATEL-17 --mutations` (the tier the gate record's `tier` field
    names for this Known-answer gate). This bead registers the gate's entries there: the worked case and the
    threshold refusal through the fit library on the `worked/` and `threshold/` rows, and the synthetic coverage
    through the library behind `budget-curve.coverage` at the three seeds, with the planted defects below as its
    controls behind `gauntlet-mutations`. Assert from the receipt: the gate line `pass`, coverage (minimum over
    seeds) at least 0.92, the four worked numbers and the refusal equal to steps 2, 4 and 6, every control
    `caught`; log the receipt hash. The script writes no gate row.
15. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; gates touched: `GATE-ATEL-17`; the receipt hash.

**Recording (one recorder).** This bead is GATE-ATEL-17's one recorder. A session that did not implement the
mechanism enters it from the receipt of step 14 with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate --
--gate GATE-ATEL-17 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19), which refuses unless every control was caught in that run.

**Logging.** `target/e2e-logs/budget_curve/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript beside it. Every line: `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N28c-39`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), exact
command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities,
seed); `metric` lines with each asserted value, its expected value, tolerance and unit (coverage against 0.92;
J∞ and s; efforts in model-steps/tick; row counts; token counts); `refusal` lines with code, pointer and repairs;
`digest` fields for verdict subtrees, address lists and world digests; on failure the first differing value and
the fixture or ledger path needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line. No private-scene data.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The script's three checks that a shell script cannot make are script checks (STD-E2E: each a function in
`crates/frob-conformance/src/e2e/checks/<name>.rs`, registered in `checks/mod.rs`, standard library and the
tool's JSON reader only), called from the script with `e2e_check <name>`: `rounding` (steps 2 and 5: a value
equals its expected value to the printed digits), `coverage-threshold` (step 6: the coverage against 0.92) and
`key-absent` (step 4: `j_inf` absent from the threshold design's block). Their tests, in the target `e2e_scripts`
(`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test e2e_scripts`): `rounding_accepts_printed_digits` (0.725714 passes as
0.726) and `rounding_rejects_off_by_one_digit` (0.7249 fails); `coverage_threshold_boundary` (0.919 fails, 0.920
passes); `key_absent_fails_on_null_or_number` (`j_inf` present with value null, or with a number, fails).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script, as specified.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The gate's known answers: ρ_A = 0.02/0.09 = 0.222, J_A∞ = 0.61 + 0.09/0.778 = 0.726, s_A = 0.11/0.116 = 0.95;
  ρ_B = 0.08/0.13 = 0.615, J_B∞ = 0.55 + 0.13/0.385 = 0.888, s_B = 0.21/0.338 = 0.62; the threshold case
  (0.20, 0.20, 0.80), whose two lower budgets would extrapolate to 0.20, wrong by at least 0.60.
- Planted defects the script must catch (registered against GATE-ATEL-17): a fit that returns J∞ = 0.20 for the
  threshold case; an interval taken from the fit's standard error (synthetic coverage falls below 0.92); a
  calibration from survivors only; levels run on different address sets; J∞ written into the verdict.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; worker counts 1 and 36; debug and release; the Mac and the Linux host
  for the fixture steps (identical envelopes).

## Considerations

- The synthetic designs are drawn from the law itself, so coverage here says the calibration procedure is
  implemented correctly; it says nothing about whether real designs follow the law. The pilot's refused share and
  the M4 oracle experiment address that.
- The real sweep costs 2 designs × 3 addresses × (64 + 256 + 1,024) samples per tick on the CPU tier; budget
  under 20 minutes on the large workstation's CPU (TARGET). The script accepts `--skip-real` for quick local
  runs, and the gate record requires the full run.
- If the pilot's falsifier holds and the mechanism is not built, this bead is closed as not built and its gate's
  status becomes `killed: fallback adopted` through N40-03's propagation from KILL-BUDGETCURVE's decision
  (N28c-41), never written by hand; only the descriptive saturation index remains.
