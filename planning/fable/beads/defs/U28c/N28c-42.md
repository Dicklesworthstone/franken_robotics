## Background

"Reuse after an edit" has meant three different things in design-automation claims, and adding them, or reporting
one under another's name, overstates what a cache does. The design studio's plan-time evidence is blunt:
rollouts and training are at least nine tenths of per-design compute and depend on every mass and geometry
parameter, so exact reuse after a dynamics-affecting edit is at most about a tenth of compute (the hypothesis of
large incremental-evaluation savings was refuted for exact reuse). What survives is a memo graph as hygiene,
dependency-directed revalidation as an evidence rule, and warm starts, which are a different quantity that
belongs to inheritance and must be counted once. The reuse ledger keeps the three apart:

| Column | Definition | What it is evidence of |
|---|---|---|
| Exact reuse | Compute whose result is taken unchanged from the memo graph because no dependency changed: analyses, step plans, kernels, scene compiles, rollouts of untouched designs | The memo graph |
| Evidence carried forward | Verdicts that `result.revalidate` keeps without recomputation, each with the dependency receipt that justifies it | The revalidation rule |
| Reacquisition avoided | Training or optimization steps not spent because a child started from a parent's policy, orbit or solution, measured against a from-scratch control stopped at the same return | Inheritance and warm starts |

Each column is a share of one declared estimand: **total work per accepted child decision**, in GPU-seconds and
CPU-seconds, priced cold and warm separately. The third column is counted here and nowhere else; the phase ledger
of the ten-fold test does not credit it again to inheritance. A reuse figure printed without its column name is
refused in a report. Owner: the record subsystem (`frob-chronicle`) keeps the ledger and `frob-opt` runs the
from-scratch control. Tier: Solid [S]. Gate GATE-ATEL-19 (M3) is measured on this ledger by its end-to-end bead.

Why cold and warm apart: a kernel compile of 95.91 s beside 240 s of training is 28.6% of a cold run, and the
cached 0.89 s is 0.37% of a warm one (FACT from the source register, re-read before a gate cites it), so a cold
figure flatters a cache. Why the third column is separate: if a warm start avoids a fraction a of training, the
third column is 0.9·a (72% at a = 0.8); an edit "reuses 70% of prior work" only through the third column, and
only if inheritance holds for that class of edit.

## Scope

In: the estimand; the accounting rules; the row schema and table; the attribution of every avoided second to
exactly one column; cold and warm pricing; the identity check; edit-class tagging; the type-level rule that the
columns are never summed; the queries used by verbs and reports.
Out: running the from-scratch control (N28c-43); the verbs and report sections (N28c-44); the frozen lineage and
the gate measurement (N28c-45); the memo graph and revalidation themselves.

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-chronicle/src/reuse_ledger.rs`.

1. **The unit of account.** An *accepted child decision* is the completed decision about a child design produced
   by one edit of a parent (a typed edit with an expected parent): its check, controller synthesis or training,
   evaluation, and the comparison or requirement verdict that was accepted for it. The ledger has one record per
   (lineage, edit, regime, resource), where regime ∈ {`cold`, `warm`} and resource ∈ {`gpu_s`, `cpu_core_s`}.
2. **Quantities, in seconds.**
   - `recomputed`: compute actually spent to reach the child's decision (measured, from job receipts).
   - `exact`: for every memo node served as a hit during the child's decision, the node's recorded recompute
     cost (measured when the node was first computed, in the same regime), summed by node kind: subterm compile,
     analysis, controller synthesis, step plan, kernel binary, scene compile, episode, comparison or summary
     (derived nodes are costed by their own combination cost only, so episodes are not counted twice).
   - `carried`: for every verdict obligation that revalidation kept without recomputation under a dependency
     licence (grade 2) or an implication licence (grade 3), the rerun cost that revalidation reports for that
     obligation, with the licence's receipt identifier. An obligation kept by identity (grade 1) is a memo hit
     and is counted under `exact`, never here.
   - `reacquisition_avoided`: (steps of the from-scratch control stopped at the same return − steps the
     warm-started child spent) × the measured cost per step, for training and optimization started from a
     parent's policy, orbit or solution; zero when there was no warm start; present only when a control was run,
     otherwise `not-measured` (never estimated).
   - `total`: the measured cost of deciding the child with nothing reused (the from-scratch decision control:
     no memo serving, no carried verdicts, no warm start), where it was run; otherwise the sum of the four
     quantities above, labelled `total: reconstructed`.
3. **Single attribution.** Every avoided second belongs to exactly one column. Precedence when two mechanisms
   could claim it: `exact` first (a memo hit is the strongest licence), then `carried`, then
   `reacquisition_avoided`. A training run that was both warm-started and partly served from memo contributes
   its memo-served episodes to `exact` and only the remaining avoided steps to the third column.
4. **Regimes.** `cold`: every cache empty at the start of the control (kernel binary cache, step-plan capture,
   scene compile cache, memo store); costs include compilation and capture. `warm`: process and binary caches
   populated by an unrelated prior run of the same shape class; the memo store empty of this lineage's results
   for the control arm. Each record is priced in its own regime: node costs are the costs observed in that
   regime (a kernel binary costs its compile time cold and its cache-lookup time warm). The two regimes are
   never averaged.
5. **Shares and the identity.** `share(column) = column / total` per regime and resource. Identity (the ledger's
   own check, and a clause of the gate): where the from-scratch decision control was run,
   |exact + carried + reacquisition_avoided + recomputed − total| ≤ 0.02 × total. The residual is stored. A
   record that fails the identity is flagged `ledger-open` and is excluded from any aggregate.
6. **Edit classes.** Each record is tagged with the class of its edit: `mass`, `mount`, `controller-only`,
   `mission-only` or `topology`, derived from the typed edit's declared effect (an edit that changes any mass,
   inertia or geometry parameter is `mass` or, if it moves an attachment or sensor frame, `mount`; an edit to the
   controller, reward or task interface only is `controller-only`; a scene, requirement or mission edit is
   `mission-only`; adding or removing a limb, joint or actuator family is `topology`). Mixed edits are tagged
   with the first class in the order topology, mass, mount, controller-only, mission-only, and marked `mixed`.
7. **Row (DESIGN), schema `frob.reuse-ledger/1`.**
   ```rust
   pub struct ReuseRecord {
       lineage: LineageId, edit: EditId, parent: DesignId, child: DesignId,
       class: EditClass, mixed: bool, regime: Regime, resource: Resource,
       recomputed: Seconds,
       exact: Tagged<ExactReuse, Seconds>,          exact_by_node_kind: Vec<(NodeKind, Seconds)>,
       carried: Tagged<CarriedForward, Seconds>,    carried_by_grade: Vec<(u8, Seconds)>, receipts: Vec<EvidenceId>,
       reacquisition_avoided: Measured<Tagged<Reacquisition, Seconds>>,   // Measured::NotMeasured without a control
       total: TotalSource,                          // Measured(Seconds, control evidence) | Reconstructed(Seconds)
       identity_residual: Option<Ratio>, status: RecordStatus,           // Closed | LedgerOpen | NoControl
   }
   ```
8. **Never summed.** The three column types carry distinct marker types and implement no addition with each
   other; no public function returns their sum or a combined "reuse" share; the identity check adds raw seconds
   inside the module only. Queries return `ReuseColumns { exact, carried, reacquisition_avoided }` per regime and
   resource, and aggregates per edit class return the three means separately with their counts. A compile-fail
   test pins the missing addition.
9. **Queries.** `record(edit, regime, resource)`; `by_class(lineage or campaign, regime, resource) ->
   [(EditClass, ReuseColumns, n_records, n_with_control)]`; `open_records(...)`.
10. **Consistency with the phase ledger.** The phase ledger records seconds actually spent per phase; it has no
    "saved by inheritance" entry. A check asserts that no phase-ledger field of a run references avoided training
    time, so the third column exists only here.

Determinism: records are derived from receipts and ledger rows at a pinned frontier; arithmetic in integer
microseconds, summed in canonical order (class D0 given the rows).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For an edit with all three kinds of reuse on a fixture lineage, the record holds the four quantities, the
      breakdowns and the residual, in both regimes and for both resources.
- [ ] Arithmetic fixtures reproduce: kernel compile 95.91 s with training 240 s → exact share 28.6% cold; cache
      lookup 0.89 s with training 240 s → 0.37% warm; rollouts and training at 0.9 of total with a warm start
      avoiding a = 0.8 → third column 72%; a dynamics-affecting edit with no warm start → exact share at most
      10%.
- [ ] Single attribution: on a fixture where a grade-1 kept obligation is also a memo hit, its cost appears under
      `exact` only; total attributed seconds equal the sum of distinct avoided seconds.
- [ ] Identity: a record whose quantities sum to within 2% of the measured total is `Closed`; one that misses by
      3% is `LedgerOpen` and absent from aggregates.
- [ ] Without a control, `reacquisition_avoided` is `NotMeasured` and the total is `Reconstructed`; no estimate is
      substituted.
- [ ] No API returns a sum of the three columns (compile-fail test); aggregates per class return three separate
      figures with counts.
- [ ] Cold and warm records are stored and returned separately; a query without a regime is a schema error.
- [ ] The phase-ledger consistency check passes and fails on a planted "saved by inheritance" phase entry.

- `regimes_never_averaged`;
- `edit_class_tagging` (five single-class edits and one mixed edit tagged `topology`, `mixed: true`);

- `phase_ledger_has_no_saved_training`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reuse_ledger.sh` (N28c-45) drives a frozen lineage of 30 edits through the real verbs and reads the
ledger back. Lines logged for this bead: per edit, regime and resource the four quantities with unit seconds,
the total and its source, the residual against the 2% threshold, the record status; per class the three means
with counts; digests of the ledger rows.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers: 28.6% cold against 0.37% warm for the same cache; 0.9·a = 72% at a = 0.8; exact reuse after a
  dynamics-affecting edit at most about one tenth.
- Negative controls that must fail: a ledger that reports one combined "reuse" share; one that counts a warm
  start under exact reuse; one that prices the kernel cache at its cold cost in the warm regime; one that fills
  the third column from an estimate when no control was run; one that credits avoided training in the phase
  ledger as well.
- Determinism lanes: records identical twice on one machine and across worker counts (integer microseconds).

## Considerations

- The identity can only be checked where the from-scratch decision control was run; elsewhere totals are
  reconstructed and labelled. Reports must print how many records per class had a control.
- Node recompute costs drift as kernels get faster; a record prices nodes at the cost observed when they were
  computed in that regime, and a kernel-version change starts new costs (evidence is never pooled across
  identities).
- The ledger is not a source of the ten-fold claim. Its purpose is to stop a reuse figure from being read as
  more than it is; the exact column is expected to be small after dynamics-affecting edits and total after
  controller-only, reward-only or scene-only edits for model analyses.
- RISK-38 (an incomplete memo key serves a stale verdict) is not detected here; the shadow recompute detects it.
  A stale hit would nevertheless appear as exact reuse, which is one more reason the exact column is reported
  beside the shadow-mismatch count.
