## Background

The reuse ledger of the design studio reports three kinds of reuse apart, as shares of the total work per accepted
child decision: exact reuse (memo hits), evidence carried forward (verdicts kept by revalidation), and
reacquisition avoided: training or optimization steps not spent because a child started from a parent's policy,
orbit or solution, "measured against a from-scratch control stopped at the same return". The record subsystem
keeps the ledger; `frob-opt` runs the from-scratch control. This bead is that control.

Two controls are needed, and both are measurements, not estimates:

1. The **matched-return training control**: what would it have cost to reach the same return without the warm
   start? Without it the third column is a guess, and it is the column through which large reuse figures arrive
   (if a warm start avoids a fraction a of training, the third column is 0.9·a, 72% at a = 0.8).
2. The **no-reuse decision control**: what does the whole child decision cost with nothing reused? It supplies the
   measured total against which the ledger's identity is checked (the three columns plus recomputed work sum to
   the measured total within 2%).

Published figures (FACT, other engines, to be re-read before any gate cites them) show how much the answer varies
by edit: transfer from a class prior to a similar body costs about 2 to 25% of an early-stopped from-scratch run;
the one parent-to-child study reports about 50%; after a single-limb topology edit the cost is 33 to 100% and
there is no gain on flat terrain; no source measures a small typed parameter edit. So the control is run per edit
class, and the gate requires it for at least one edit per class. Tier: Solid [S] (the control is plain
measurement; the warm-start mechanisms it measures have their own tiers).

## Scope

In: the two control protocols; matched-return stopping; censoring; seeds; what counts as from scratch; cost per
step; the records written to the reuse ledger; cancellation; the options that `train.start`'s registry row
(VERBS-train) lists as this bead's extension: `--from-scratch`, `--no-carry`, `--no-memo` and
`--match-return <run>` with `--seeds`.
Out: the ledger itself (N28c-42); warm-start mechanisms (PPO initialization from a parent's weights, inheritance
through mapping tables, orbit continuation, solution warm starts in controller synthesis); the verbs that print
the columns (N28c-44).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-opt/src/reuse/control.rs`; controls run as ordinary jobs under the daemon with their own
budget line, phase `train` or `evaluate` in the phase ledger, and the tag `control: from-scratch`.

1. **Matched-return training control.**
   1. Input: an edit parent → child whose child training or optimization was warm-started; the child's training
      receipt (steps spent S_warm, the return curve, the return R* at which the child's decision was accepted,
      the evaluation addresses, the seed).
   2. From-scratch definition, recorded on the control: the same learner, hyperparameters, network and
      environment as the child's run, initialized without the parent's artifact. Where the child's class has a
      class prior that a fresh child would also use, the control starts from that prior and is labelled
      `from: class-prior`; otherwise `from: random-init`. The label is part of the ledger record, because the
      two give different third columns.
   3. Run the control with k seeds (default k = 3; the gate requires at least one edit per class to have a
      control, and the seed count is recorded), evaluating at the same cadence and on the same addresses as the
      child's run, on a D2 evaluation profile.
   4. Stop each seed at the first evaluation at which its return reaches R* (matched return), recording steps
      S_scratch. If a seed has not reached R* at the cap of max(4 × S_warm, the class's default training budget),
      it is right-censored at the cap.
   5. Result: avoided steps = median over seeds of (S_scratch − S_warm). If any seed is censored the result is a
      lower bound and is labelled `censored-lower-bound`. If the median is negative (the warm start was slower
      than starting fresh), the avoided steps are recorded as 0 with the flag `negative-transfer` and the
      negative value kept in the detail.
   6. Cost: avoided seconds = avoided steps × the measured cost per training step of the control run (GPU-seconds
      and CPU core-seconds separately, learner time included), in the regime of the record.
   7. The same protocol applies to optimization warm starts (controller synthesis or trajectory optimization
      started from the parent's solution): "return" is the objective value at acceptance and "steps" are solver
      iterations.
2. **No-reuse decision control.** Re-run the child's whole decision (check, synthesis or training, evaluation,
   comparison) with memo serving disabled (`--no-memo`), revalidation told to recheck everything
   (`--no-carry`), and no warm start (`--from-scratch`), in the requested regime:
   - `cold`: the control job starts in a fresh daemon state root with empty kernel binary cache, scene cache and
     memo store;
   - `warm`: caches populated by an unrelated prior run of the same shape class; memo store empty of this
     lineage.
   Its measured GPU-seconds and CPU core-seconds are the ledger's `total` for that edit and regime. The control's
   verdict must equal the reused decision's verdict (same e-value trajectory at the same addresses under class
   D2); a difference is a determinism or stale-reuse defect and is filed, and the ledger record is `LedgerOpen`.
3. **Which edits get a control.** By default one edit per class per lineage or campaign, chosen by a keyed draw
   before any reuse figure is computed (so the controlled edits are not picked for looking good), plus any edit
   the caller names. The share of records with a control is printed wherever the columns are printed.
4. **Records written.** For each controlled edit: the control's evidence identifiers, `S_warm`, the per-seed
   `S_scratch` with censoring flags, the matched return R*, the from-scratch label, the cost per step, and the
   resulting `reacquisition_avoided` and `total` values, passed to the reuse ledger.
5. **Budget and cancellation.** A control is refused at admission if its estimate exceeds the session budget;
   the ledger then keeps `NotMeasured`. Cancellation drains at chunk boundaries; a cancelled control writes
   nothing to the ledger.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Matched-return control on a fixture learner with a known learning curve (a synthetic task whose return
      after s steps is 1 − exp(−s/τ) with τ = 1,000, evaluated every 100 steps; warm start equivalent to a head
      start of 1,200 steps; R* = 0.80): from scratch reaches 0.80 at step 1,700 (first evaluation at or above
      −τ·ln 0.2 = 1,609); the warm child reaches it at step 500; avoided steps 1,200.
- [ ] Censoring: with R* unreachable within the cap, the result is labelled `censored-lower-bound` with avoided
      ≥ cap − S_warm.
- [ ] Negative transfer: a warm start that is slower yields avoided = 0 with `negative-transfer`.
- [ ] The from-scratch label (`class-prior` or `random-init`) is recorded and printed.
- [ ] The no-reuse decision control's verdict equals the reused decision's verdict on the fixture lineage, and
      its measured seconds become `total` in the ledger for the stated regime.
- [ ] Controlled edits are selected by a keyed draw recorded before any reuse figure exists (ledger sequence
      order asserted).
- [ ] Without a control the ledger shows `NotMeasured`; no code path estimates the third column.
- [ ] Controls appear in the phase ledger under their real phases with the control tag, and are excluded from the
      child's own `recomputed` seconds.
- [ ] Cancellation of a running control leaves no ledger write and no leaked device resources.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-opt/tests/reuse_control.rs`: `matched_return_first_crossing` (the synthetic curve: 1,700 and 500 →
1,200 avoided); `median_over_three_seeds` (S_scratch 1,600, 1,700, 1,900 → median 1,700);
`censored_seed_gives_lower_bound`; `negative_transfer_flagged`; `class_prior_label`;
`cost_per_step_from_control_run`; `keyed_selection_precedes_figures`; `decision_control_verdict_equal`;
`decision_control_cold_starts_empty` (the control's receipts show a kernel compile, not a cache hit);
`warm_control_has_cache_hits_for_binaries_only`; `control_not_counted_as_recomputed`;
`optimization_warm_start_same_protocol` (iterations in place of steps).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reuse_ledger.sh`, phase `controls` (the shared script is described in N28c-42; the user route
`design.diff --reuse --run-control` lands later with N28c-44, so this phase drives the controls directly). On the
three edits of phase `ledger` it runs the no-reuse decision control in both regimes through `cargo run -p frob-opt
--example reuse_decision_control -- --edit <id> --regime cold|warm --json` (DESIGN, an example target over this
bead's `control.rs` that submits the control as ordinary daemon jobs; `frob-conformance` has no binary to run,
ADR-GAUNT-01 in BD-025), asserting the verdict equals the reused
decision's (digests), the cold control's receipts show a kernel compile and the warm one's a cache lookup, the
measured seconds become `total`, and the identity closes within 2%; runs the matched-return control on the synthetic
learner fixture through `frob train start <child> --task synthetic-curve --algo ppo --from-scratch --match-return
<child-run> --seeds 3 --key … --json` (this bead's extension of `train.start`), asserting 1,700 and 500 steps and
1,200 avoided, and on a censored and a negative-transfer fixture; checks that the keyed selection of controlled edits
precedes any figure in ledger order; and cancels one running control, asserting no ledger write. It sources the
STD-E2E helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/reuse_ledger/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line has
`ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N28c-43`), `step` (`controls`), `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the
envelope's `evidence` block; `metric` lines carry, per controlled edit, the matched return, per-seed steps with
censoring flags, avoided steps and seconds and the from-scratch label, and the decision control's seconds per regime
and resource; `digest` fields carry both verdicts; on failure the edit, the seed and the receipts; a phase `summary`
line.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answer: the synthetic learning curve above (1,609 → first evaluation 1,700; head start 1,200 → 500).
- Arithmetic link to the ledger: with training at 0.9 of total and a = 0.8 avoided, the third column is 72%.
- Negative controls that must fail: a control stopped at matched steps instead of matched return; a control that
  inherits the parent's weights by accident (the from-scratch run shows the parent's artifact in its inputs); a
  decision control that still serves memo hits (its receipts show zero compute for analyses); controlled edits
  chosen after the figures were computed.
- Determinism lanes: evaluation at D2 gives identical matched steps for a fixed seed twice on one machine;
  training itself runs under the labelled training profile and only its D2 evaluations decide the stop.

## Considerations

- At M3 the warm starts available are solution warm starts in controller synthesis; the verb registry has no M3
  verb that starts a child's policy from a parent's weights (`train.inherit`, BD-322, is M4). Policy warm starts,
  inheritance through mapping tables and orbit continuation therefore arrive at M4 and use the same protocol;
  their gate (inheritance) reads steps to the parent's requirement verdict, which is the same matched-return
  idea. The training control is built and tested at M3 on the synthetic learner so that it is ready then.
- Inheritance biases search toward bodies near the parent, and long lineages lose plasticity; a lineage that
  resets to the class prior at a scheduled depth has, at that edit, no warm start and therefore a third column of
  zero. The ledger must show that honestly.
- Controls cost real compute (a from-scratch training per controlled edit and seed). The default of one edit per
  class keeps this bounded; reports state the coverage of controls so that an uncontrolled figure is not read as
  measured.
- A control is evidence about one edit; generalizing to a class is a statement about the class's mean with its
  count, not a property of the mechanism.
