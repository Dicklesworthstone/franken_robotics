## Background

The design studio reports three kinds of reuse after an edit, apart and never summed: exact reuse (compute taken
unchanged from the memo graph because no dependency changed), evidence carried forward (verdicts that
revalidation keeps without recomputation, each with its dependency receipt), and reacquisition avoided (training
or optimization steps not spent because a child started from a parent's policy, orbit or solution, measured
against a from-scratch control stopped at the same return). Each is a share of total work per accepted child
decision, in GPU-seconds and CPU-seconds, priced cold and warm separately. The surfaces are `frob design diff A B
--reuse` and the reuse section of `frob report campaign`, per edit class: mass, mount, controller-only,
mission-only, topology.

Gate GATE-ATEL-19 (first milestone M3; its pass condition is a known answer, and its timing columns follow the
performance disclosure rules): on one frozen lineage of 30 edits spanning the five classes, the three columns are
reported cold and warm, with the from-scratch control run for at least one edit per class. The columns plus
recomputed work sum to the measured total within 2%. A controller-only edit shows exact reuse of every model
analysis, and a mass edit shows exact reuse of none of its rollouts. This bead builds the frozen lineage, is the
end-to-end script for the capability, and records the gate.

The two structural facts in the gate are the honest core of the reuse story. Rollouts and training are at least
nine tenths of per-design compute and depend on every mass and geometry parameter, so after a mass edit no
rollout is reusable bit for bit and exact reuse is at most about a tenth; after a controller-only edit every
model analysis is reusable, because none of the fields those analyses read has changed. A ledger that showed
otherwise would be measuring a stale key or a mislabelled column.

## Scope

In: the frozen lineage fixture; the script; its assertions; the logging; the gate record.
Out: the ledger, the controls and the verbs (their beads); the 30-amendment campaign of the revalidation gate (a
different fixture with a different purpose, although three edits are shared by design).

## Technical approach

**The frozen lineage** `conformance/lineages/reuse-30/`: a chain of 30 typed edits starting from the reference
quadruped template with its synthesized baseline controller and a small trained policy; six edits per class,
interleaved by a fixed order; the manifest (edit list with expected parents, the task, the 64 evaluation
addresses, seeds, which edits get controls) is hashed and committed before the ledger implementation is run on
it. Edits (DESIGN; each is a typed edit with an idempotency key):

| Class | The six edits (illustrative list, frozen in the manifest) | Expected structural effect |
|---|---|---|
| mass | torso mass +5%; shank density −10%; battery mass +0.4 kg; payload 2 kg added; thigh shell thickness +0.5 mm; foot mass +20% | Every rollout invalid; model analyses that read mass recomputed; scene compile and kernels reused |
| mount | lidar mount moved 30 mm forward; IMU mount rotated 5°; battery mount lowered 20 mm; camera mount raised 40 mm; payload mount shifted 25 mm aft; foot pad offset 5 mm | Sensor and attachment frames change; analyses reading only untouched fields reused |
| controller-only | stance gain +10%; swing height −15%; reward weight on energy doubled; command filter cutoff 12 Hz → 9 Hz; policy observation history 3 → 5; action scale −10% | Model identity unchanged: every model analysis reused; rollouts recomputed |
| mission-only | payload requirement 2 kg → 2.5 kg; speed threshold 0.8 → 0.9 m/s; scene variant with a 20 mm sill; floor friction interval narrowed; time limit 20 s → 16 s; a second waypoint added | Model analyses and controller synthesis reused; verdicts rechecked per polarity |
| topology | a tail link added; a toe joint added to each foot; the tail removed; a wheel swapped for a foot on one leg; a spine joint added; the spine joint removed | New shape class: nearly nothing reused; a new step plan |

**The script** `scripts/e2e/reuse_ledger.sh`: plain shell over the real `frob` binary and `frobd`; sources the
STD-E2E helper; idempotent; seed-explicit. Steps:

1. `start`: commit, toolchain, host fingerprint, seed; verify the manifest hash equals the committed hash.
2. Build the lineage, warm regime: for each edit in order, `frob design edit <parent> <edit> --expected-parent …
   --idempotency-key … --json`; then the child's decision: `frob design check`, `frob control synth` (or, for the
   edits marked for training, `frob train start --warm-start <parent-policy>` to the matched return), `frob eval
   compare <parent> <child> --task quad-flat --json` on the 64 addresses. All with memo serving and revalidation
   on. Record job receipts.
3. For each edit: `frob design diff <parent> <child> --reuse --regime warm --json`; assert the three named
   columns, regime, resources, seconds, total source, residual.
4. Controls, at least one edit per class (the manifest's keyed selection: edits 3, 9, 14, 20 and 27; plus the
   training control for the edits that were warm-started): `frob design diff <parent> <child> --reuse
   --run-control --regime warm --json` (wait for the job); then the same with `--regime cold`, which the daemon
   runs in a fresh state root with empty caches. Assert each controlled record's status is `closed` and
   |exact + carried + reacquisition avoided + recomputed − total| ≤ 2% of total, for GPU-seconds and for CPU
   core-seconds, in both regimes.
5. Structural assertions (the two facts of the gate):
   - for each of the six controller-only edits: the set of model-analysis nodes evaluated for the child equals
     the set served from the memo graph (zero analysis nodes recomputed; `exact_reuse.by_node_kind.analysis`
     covers all of them);
   - for each of the six mass edits: zero episode nodes served from the memo graph for the child's rollouts
     (`exact_reuse.by_node_kind.episode` is absent or 0 seconds).
6. Further assertions, logged as expectations with their measured values (not gate thresholds): mass edits'
   exact share at most 0.10 in the warm regime; mission-only edits reuse controller synthesis; topology edits
   show a new step plan; for the cold control of one edit the kernel-binary node's share is larger cold than
   warm (the "a cold figure flatters a cache" effect; the reference values are 28.6% against 0.37% for a 95.91 s
   compile and a 0.89 s cached load beside 240 s of training).
7. Evidence carried forward: for the mission-only edits that narrow an interval or relax a requirement, assert
   at least one verdict obligation is kept under a dependency licence with a receipt identifier, and that its
   seconds appear under `evidence_carried_forward` and not under `exact_reuse`.
8. Third column: for each warm-started edit with a training control, assert `reacquisition_avoided` is measured,
   carries the from-scratch label, and equals (control steps at matched return − child steps) × cost per step
   from the control's receipts (recomputed independently by the script from the receipts).
9. Campaign view: `frob report campaign <lineage-campaign> --json`; assert the reuse section has five class rows
   per regime and resource with counts and control counts, the estimand line and the non-additivity line; assert
   no cell equals the sum of the three columns.
10. Refusals: the three planted routes to an unnamed or combined reuse figure (a bare percentage placeholder; the
    `--reuse-summary total` option; a design-report citation without a column) → each exit 3 with the stated
    repairs.
11. Determinism and disclosure: the ledger rows and both verbs' outputs are byte-identical across two reads at
    one frontier; every timing figure's envelope carries the disclosure block (host fingerprint, regime, cache
    state).
12. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; gates touched: `GATE-ATEL-19`; write the gate row: records per class
    and regime, controls per class, the maximum identity residual, the two structural counts.

**Logging.** `target/e2e-logs/reuse_ledger/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`,
`bead` (`N28c-45`), `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, the envelope's `evidence` block
(claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `metric` lines per edit, regime and resource: total,
recomputed, the three columns in seconds and as shares, the residual against 0.02; per controlled edit: matched
return, control steps, child steps, avoided steps; structural counts (analysis nodes recomputed for
controller-only edits: threshold 0; episode nodes served for mass edits: threshold 0); `refusal` lines for step
10; `digest` fields for the manifest, ledger rows and report section; on failure the first edit whose record is
open, with its quantities and the receipts needed to reproduce. Final `summary`. No private-scene data.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The lineage manifest is committed with its hash before the ledger code runs on it; the script verifies the
      hash.
- [ ] All 30 edits have ledger records in the warm regime and, for the controlled edits, in the cold regime, for
      both resources, with the three columns under their names.
- [ ] From-scratch controls exist for at least one edit per class (five or more), each with a closed identity
      within 2% in both regimes; the maximum residual is recorded.
- [ ] Controller-only edits: zero model-analysis nodes recomputed (six of six). Mass edits: zero rollout episodes
      served from the memo graph (six of six).
- [ ] The third column is `not-measured` wherever no control ran, and measured with its label where one did.
- [ ] The report's reuse section and `design.diff --reuse` agree with the ledger rows; nothing is summed.
- [ ] The three refusal routes fire.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-19 is recorded with measured values by a session that did not implement the ledger; timing
      figures carry their disclosure blocks.
- [ ] With any one planted defect below, the script exits non-zero and names the symptom.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script, as specified.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answers of the gate: zero model analyses recomputed after a controller-only edit; zero rollouts reused
  after a mass edit; identity within 2%. Reference arithmetic the ledger must be able to reproduce on its unit
  fixtures: 28.6% cold against 0.37% warm for one cache; 0.9·a = 72% at a = 0.8; exact reuse after a
  dynamics-affecting edit at most about a tenth.
- Planted defects the script must catch (registered against GATE-ATEL-19): a memo key for episodes that omits a
  mass field (a mass edit then shows reused rollouts: a stale hit); an analysis key that includes a controller
  field (a controller-only edit then recomputes analyses); a ledger that counts a warm start under exact reuse; a
  report cell with the sum of the columns; a third column filled without a control; a cold control run with a
  warm kernel cache (its kernel node shows a lookup time, not a compile).
- Counterexample behind the first two controls: a key that omits a dependency keeps a stale result (the
  four-state camera fixture, in which a key without delivery time keeps a stale promotion).
- Determinism lanes: the lineage build twice on one machine gives identical child digests and identical ledger
  rows except measured seconds; structural counts are identical across worker counts; the Mac and the Linux host
  each record their own timing figures with their own disclosure blocks.

## Considerations

- Runtime: 30 child decisions on a small quadruped (64 addresses each), a handful of warm-started trainings and
  their from-scratch controls, and five cold controls that each pay a kernel compile; budget under 3 hours on one
  GPU plus the workstation's CPU (TARGET, measured and recorded).
- Timing shares vary by host; the gate's pass conditions are the identity and the two structural facts, which do
  not. The shares themselves are reported, not thresholded.
- Three edits are shared with the frozen 30-amendment campaign of the revalidation gate so that the two fixtures
  can be cross-checked; the lists are otherwise independent, and neither may be edited after its hash is
  committed.
- At M3 warm starts are limited to weight reuse where spaces are unchanged and solver warm starts; the lineage's
  topology edits therefore have no warm start and a third column of `not-measured` or zero until inheritance
  through mapping tables exists at M4, when the gate is re-run and the row extended.
