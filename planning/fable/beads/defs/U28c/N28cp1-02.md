## Background

The design studio's table of known answers and negative controls is carried by one battery, built in three
stages so that each milestone exit has the whole-table report for the rows due by then (the first stage,
N28cp1-01, built the suite, the staging rule, the report and the M1 rows). A known-answer row shows the
implementation recovers an analytic or synthetic truth; its negative control shows the test can fail.

At M3 the chapter's milestone plan brings the decision cores DC-01, DC-09 and DC-10 of GATE-ATEL-13, the
dependency grades of GATE-ATEL-11 (revalidation), A-09 connection curvature, A-17 observability as a redundancy
score, A-18 route clearance, and campaign deduplication. This bead adds the ten rows due then, with their
controls, including the control the battery defines for KA-703, whose registry entry states none. The rows are
recorded by their owning beads at their own gates; the battery is the cross-check that none was dropped. Tier:
Solid [S].

## Scope

In: the fixtures and test functions of KA-681 (its A-09 part), KA-683, KA-689, KA-695, KA-698, KA-699, KA-700,
KA-701, KA-703 and KA-704; their controls; the KA-703 control defined here; the independent arithmetic of these
rows; the phase `m3` of `scripts/e2e/atelier_known_answers.sh`.
Out: the suite, staging and report (N28cp1-01); the M4 rows and the whole-table acceptance (N28c-24); the
mechanisms (BD-120, BD-233, BD-243, BD-285, BD-292, BD-309).

Independent arithmetic inside the suite, in exact arithmetic and independent of the component under test:
`majority_of_seven` (3807/4096); `fcfs_wait_formula` (λ·E[S²]/(2(1 − ρ)) with λ = 1/270, E[S²] = 324,090,
ρ = 0.7 → 2,000.6); `wrong_second_moment_control` (E[S]² = 35,721 gives 220.5 s, outside the tolerance);
`coverage_certificate_sum` (31 + 22 + 14 + 9 + 6 + 4 = 86; bound 98); `dc01_rows` (the integer inequality
19^(n−k) ≥ 100·14^(n−k)·6^k first holds at (16, 0) and at (22, 1)). Rows adopted from planning pilots (KA-698,
KA-699, KA-701) carry `to-be-rerun: true` until re-executed in this repository and re-pinned.

Determinism: exact rows are class D0; KA-703 is compared after rounding to the printed digits.

- [ ] With `FROB_MILESTONE=M3` the M1 and M3 rows pass and every control of both stages is caught, including the

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The rows' test functions `ka_681_curvature`, `ka_683_majority`, `ka_689_doorway`, `ka_695_dc01_table`,
`ka_698_ballast_classes`, `ka_699_camera_states`, `ka_700_doorway_optimality`, `ka_701_docking_support`,
`ka_703_queue_waits`, `ka_704_certificate` and their `_control` companions; the independent arithmetic above;
`ka_703_preempt_control_too_weak` (documents why the control is the E[S]² variant: dropping the short-class load
from the preempt-resume denominator gives 0.167 s against 0.172 s, inside the printed precision, so it is not used).

### End-to-end script and logging

Phase `m3` of `scripts/e2e/atelier_known_answers.sh` (the script belongs to N28cp1-01): `cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test ka_atelier` with `FROB_MILESTONE=M3`; `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
registry_report -- ka --chapter 28 --json` (the report of N28cp1-01: the `ka` subcommand of the conformance
crate's example target `registry_report`, the crate having no binary under ADR-GAUNT-01 of BD-025; rows of M1
and M3 pass, M4 rows `not run`); from the user's side `frob cores show dc-01 --rows --json` (rows
(16, 0) and (22, 1), KA-695), `frob nemesis cover` on the worked instance (86 of 100 and the bound 98, KA-704),
`frob result revalidate` on the four camera states (2, 0, 0, 1 and 1, 0, 0, 1, KA-699), `frob analyze route` on the
narrow and the widened doorway (the cut and the witness path, KA-689). It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/atelier_known_answers/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`; every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`
(`N28cp1-02`), `step` (`m3.*`), `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and, for verb calls, the
envelope's `evidence` block; per row a `metric` line with measured, expected, tolerance and unit; an `assert` line
per control (`caught: true`); on failure the row, both values and the fixture path; a `summary` with rows passed,
failed and `not run`.

### Known answers and negative controls

The rows above are the content, with KA-695 and KA-704 traced to their derivations (the DC-01 inequality; the
greedy certificate of PO-ATEL-05). Determinism lanes: exact rows identical on both hosts and in debug and release.

## Considerations

- KA-701 and the docking fixture belong to the mission-query subsystem; the battery calls the revalidation engine
  on that fixture and does not copy it.
- A control must lie clearly outside the row's tolerance; the KA-703 control differs from 2,000.6 s by an order
  of magnitude.
- A row whose expected value changes is a change to a known answer and needs the derivation on file updated first.
- Runtime: the M3 rows are exact computations, small revalidations and one cover; under five minutes on the CPU
  tier (TARGET).
