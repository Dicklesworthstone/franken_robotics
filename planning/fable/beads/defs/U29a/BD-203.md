## Background

One of the project's goals is that any world is reproducible: re-run alone on the CPU from its address, bit for
bit where the device reaches the bitwise class, and independent of the batch it ran in. A world is determined
by its address `(run_seed, world, episode)`, its model, scene, task and policy identities, and its kernel
versions. `frob sim replay <episode>` reproduces it. This is what makes a failure in world 1,337 of 4,096 on a
GPU something an agent can pick up and examine, and it is the base on which teleport, forks, the first-
divergence search, the background referee and memoized episodes all stand.

Reproduction needs three things, delivered at milestone M2 by three beads:
1. a cheap, exact way to say "these two states are the same bits" on every backend: the per-tick **state
   digest** and the per-world **digest chain**, computed in-kernel and emitted at the commit fence (N29ap1-01);
2. stored states to start from: **snapshots**, copied at the commit fence by `K-snapshot`, checked against the
   device digest on the host and stored by the T3 schedule (N29ap1-02), in the container `frob.snapshot/1`
   (N29a-18);
3. an engine and a verb that re-run a world and compare: **replay** (this bead, the capstone of the three: its
   done-condition is the gate).

**ADR-CHRON-05: digests are integer mixes computed in-kernel; BLAKE3 is used on the host.** Reason: integer
arithmetic is exact on every backend. The device digest is a 128-bit integer mix of the raw bit patterns of a
world's canonical state slabs; it is a tripwire, not an identity. Identities in the record are BLAKE3 hashes
computed by the host over committed snapshots.

A world's trajectory must not depend on the batch it ran in. Replay of one world alone, in a batch of different
size, and among different co-tenants must give the recorded digests on the same backend. Seed done-condition:
GATE-CHRON-01. Tier Solid.

Terms. *Profile identity*: the BLAKE3 of the precision profile, contact law, step profile, step size, kernel
versions and shape-class identity a snapshot is bound to (N29a-18). *Class*: the determinism class of §35 (D0
exact, D1 bitwise across backends, D2 bitwise per backend and independent of batch, slot, co-tenants and
capacity, D3 tolerance). *Capture*: what a replay is asked to record beyond the comparison.

Rejected alternatives: replay that compares only the final digest (misses a divergence that heals, the two-tick
case of the divergence bead); replay from stored per-tick state (cost); a replay engine per backend (three
engines would drift; one engine drives any backend's step).

Polishing round 1 split this seed: the digest wiring is N29ap1-01 and the snapshot kernel with its host side and
the T3 schedule is N29ap1-02, so that work needing only digests or only snapshots does not wait for replay.

## Scope

In: the replay engine; the start selection; alone, other-batch-size and co-tenant compositions; the comparison
at every recorded point; the report; captures; the verb `sim.replay` without `--tier`, on the recorded backend
and profile; its refusals; cancellation of a replay job; the property test of INV-CHRON-08 through replay.
Out: the digest and chain (N29ap1-01); `K-snapshot`, the host side of snapshots, `EpisodeId` and the T3 schedule
(N29ap1-02); the container and FROB-REF-894's check (N29a-18); the mix function (N21a-23); the fence, retries and
leases (N21b-07, N21b-11); the ring (N29a-11); the capture kind `failure` and the ring's `reconstruct` markers
(N29ap1-03, which adds them to this engine and verb); teleport, that is `--tier` (BD-204); forks (BD-205); the
divergence search between two runs (BD-206); the gate's measurement and its script (N29a-19); the `<file.fmcw>`
input form (N26a-19 at M3, which resolves a counterexample-world file to an address and calls this engine).

## Technical approach

**1. The replay engine.** `replay(target, opts)`:
1. Resolve the episode to its run manifest and identities; check that kernels, model, scene, task and policy
   objects with those identities are present (a missing identity is a refusal naming it: FROB-REF-802 with the
   pointer to the identity and the repair "restore the object or re-run the compile that produced it").
2. Choose the start: the latest stored snapshot at or before `from` whose profile identity equals the replay's,
   else tick zero by address (from the settled-pose cache, N24a-16). A snapshot of another profile offered as a
   continuation is refused by the container with FROB-REF-894 (repair: request a comparison replay).
3. Build an environment for the world: alone (batch of one), or as `opts` asks (`batch_size`, `co_tenants`
   drawn by seed from other addresses of the same run), in the recorded shape class or any class that admits
   the model.
4. Restore (the container's restore, with the digest chain resumed from the header) and step to `to` (default:
   the episode's terminal tick), computing d_t and x_t (N29ap1-01).
5. Compare at every recorded point: chunk-end d and x for all worlds; per-tick values where recorded (ring and
   traced worlds). Stop at the first mismatch or at the first refusal raised by the step.
6. Return `ReplayReport { class, from, to, start: snapshot hash | tick-zero, compared_points, result: Bitwise |
   Mismatch { chunk, tick, expected, observed } | Refused { code, tick }, captured }`, where `captured` lists what
   `opts.capture` asked for: per-tick digests (`digests`), a T2 window (`t2`) or snapshots at given ticks
   (`snapshots`). The kind `failure` (the capture a failure ring would have produced, for a world that held no
   ring) is added to this list by N29ap1-03.
A mismatch writes a row to the divergence ledger (address, tick, both digests, backend, kernel versions).

**2. The verb `sim.replay`** (kind `run`; M2; capability `sim:run`; schemas `frob.sim.replay.in/1`,
`frob.sim.replay.out/1`; the registry row is VERBS-sim's, owner `frob-chronicle`; its chapter refusals are the
row's: 601, 606, 840 and 894, raised by this bead's handler, and 222 (teleport, BD-204), 247 (`--require
bit-equal`, N35-07) and 791 (`referee-worker-mismatch`, the producer worker of N28b-05, M3), raised by the
extensions that bring them).
`frob sim replay <episode> | --run <r> --world <w> [--episode <e>] [--from <tick>|--from-snapshot <hash>]
[--to <tick>] [--alone | --batch-size <n> | --co-tenants <seed>] [--capture digests|t2|snapshots|failure]
[--tier <t>] --key <k> --json` (`failure` from N29ap1-03). Without `--tier` the replay runs on the recorded backend and profile; `--tier` is
BD-204's. Envelope: `result` is the report; `evidence` has class `Tested`, the tier, the determinism class that
applied (`D2` within a family), identities and seed. Exit codes: 0 for `Bitwise`; 2 for a mismatch (a finding:
the first differing chunk and tick with both digests, and the divergence-ledger row); 3 for FROB-REF-894 and
FROB-REF-802 (a missing identity or an invalid argument); 5 for FROB-REF-840 (a sealed confirmation run is
outside the session's scopes, N24-REF); 6 for FROB-REF-601
(the recorded backend is unavailable; the repair names `--tier batch-cpu`) and FROB-REF-606 (the device's
fingerprint differs from the one recorded with the run, so a bitwise comparison is not valid evidence; repairs:
`frob spark fingerprint`, `frob spark check`, or teleport with `--tier batch-cpu`). Before N26a-19 exists, a
`.fmcw` argument is refused with FROB-REF-724 (the reader is not linked in this build).

**3. Jobs.** A replay is a job like any `run` verb: one idempotency key yields one job; cancellation drains at
the next device chunk boundary, leaks no device resource and reports the last compared tick. On a device the job
runs under the device job protocol of BD-210 (device workers, lease, drain and release); this bead adds only
what a replay reports on cancel.

**4. Layout.** `crates/frob-chronicle/src/replay.rs`; `crates/frob-verbs/src/verbs/sim_replay.rs`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three compositions of the gate hold on what this bead can run itself: 200 worlds of a `batch-cpu` run
      (both precisions), and of a run on each device backend present, replay alone, in a batch of another size
      and among other co-tenants with digests identical to the record at every recorded point. The seed's
      done-condition, GATE-CHRON-01 (TARGET: 1,000 random worlds from a GPU run replay with identical digests on
      the same backend, and inside the class on `batch-cpu`, in the same three compositions), is measured and
      recorded by N29a-19, which depends on this bead and on teleport (BD-204). This bead closes on its own
      criteria and does not wait for that record.
- [ ] INV-CHRON-08: a world's digests do not depend on batch size, slot or co-tenants (property test through
      replay on `batch-cpu` in both precisions; the device lanes are the gate's).
- [ ] A replay from a stored snapshot and a replay from tick zero agree with each other and with the record.
- [ ] `frob sim replay` on an unfaulted world returns `Bitwise`, exit 0; with one planted bit flip in a stored
      chunk digest it returns the mismatch with chunk and tick, exit 2, and a divergence-ledger row.
- [ ] `--capture digests`, `t2` and `snapshots` return what they name: per-tick digests equal to the record's
      where it has them, the T2 rows of the requested window, and snapshots whose recomputed digests equal the
      replay's d_t.
- [ ] A replay stops at the first refusal raised by the step and reports its code and tick.
- [ ] Cancellation of a replay job drains and leaks no device resource; one key yields one job.
- [ ] The verb is reachable through `frob --json` and the MCP tool `sim_replay`; FROB-REF-840, -894, -601, -606
      and a missing identity each give their code and exit.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `replay_alone_equals_recorded`, `replay_other_batch_size`, `replay_co_tenants` (`batch-cpu`, single and
  double precision, 200 worlds each; the same three on each device backend present, skipped with a logged
  reason where there is none).
- `replay_start_selection`: with snapshots at ticks 0, 256, 512 and `--from 600`, the start is 512; with the
  512 snapshot bound to another profile, the start is 256.
- `snapshot_and_tick_zero_agree`: the same world from tick zero and from the 512 snapshot gives equal d and x
  from tick 512 on.
- `replay_mismatch_report_fields`: chunk, tick, expected and observed digests; divergence-ledger row.
- `replay_stops_at_first_refusal`: a world that faults at tick 77 reports `Refused { code, tick: 77 }`.
- `capture_kinds`: on a traced world, `digests` equals the recorded per-tick digests, `t2` equals the recorded T2
  rows of the window byte for byte, and `snapshots` at ticks 300 and 400 restore to states whose d equals the
  record's (the ring comparison `capture_failure_equals_ring` is N29ap1-03's).
- `missing_identity_refused`; `sealed_840` (exit 5); `profile_mismatch_894`; `backend_unavailable_601`;
  `fingerprint_changed_606`; `fmcw_before_m3_724`.
- `replay_cancel_drains`: a cancel after the first device chunk reports the last compared tick and frees every
  slab lease.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/sim_replay.sh` (owned by N29a-19): a device run, then `frob sim replay` for sampled worlds in the
three compositions, a planted mismatch, a capture, and the refusals. This bead's step group is `replay-*`; its
lines carry the world address, start tick and source (snapshot hash or tick zero), compared points, the
determinism class, the first mismatch with both digests, and the divergence-ledger row identifier. The step
groups `digest-*` (N29ap1-01), `snapshot-store-*` (N29ap1-02) and `snapshot-*` (N29a-18) run before it in the
same script. `sim.run` answers on the batched tiers only after this bead (N16b-25 depends on it), so until
N29a-19 runs the script, the runs this bead replays are recorded by the conformance harness through the job
protocol of BD-210, and the verb is called on those runs from the command line (`frob sim replay --run <r>
--world <w> --alone --key rp-<w> --json`: exit 0, `result: Bitwise`).

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-716, replay half: the empty intervention replays bit for bit (exact; G5 · all tiers).
- Negative controls: a replay that compares only the final digest (misses a divergence that heals; the two-tick
  case of the divergence bead); a start selection that ignores the profile identity (the 894 test fails); a
  co-tenant composition drawn from the slot rather than the address (the co-tenant test fails).
- Metamorphic: splitting a rollout at a snapshot and resuming equals running it whole; permuting world indices
  permutes results and changes nothing else (MR-01).
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release on `batch-cpu`; each backend
  against its own record (class D2); across backends only through the teleport bead.

## Considerations

- KILL-D1 (decided at M2): if a device family cannot hold bit equality with `batch-cpu`, that family is class D2
  for itself and D3 against others. Replay on the same family is unaffected.
- Memoized episodes, the background referee and the fork service depend on this bead or on its components; each
  reads snapshots through the container and digests through the record, never device slabs directly.
- The collision budget of the mix is about 2⁻¹²⁸ per comparison for independent states; it is stated with every
  `Bitwise` verdict as "digests equal", not as "states proved equal".
- Cost: a replay of one world alone costs about one world-episode on its backend plus the restore; the replay of
  1,000 worlds for the gate runs as batches of the recorded composition and of the two others.
