## Background

"Where do these two computations part?" is asked constantly: a replay against its record, a fork against its
parent, one tier against another, one backend against another, two designs of one lineage. Everything that
follows (an attribution, a bug report, an explanation of why B beat A) starts from the first place they differ.
`frob telemetry divergence <a> <b>` answers it. The record subsystem (Chronicle) owns the algorithm; the
counterfactual-debugging subsystem restates it because every attribution reads its output, and registers no
divergence verb of its own.

The algorithm has eight parts:
1. *Align.* Committed ticks and events of both runs; canonical variables by stable identifier (link, joint,
   contact feature pair, channel).
2. *Exact classes.* Compare the chain of state digests. Because each chain value covers the whole prefix, "the
   prefixes agree up to chunk i" is monotone in i and binary search over chunks is valid. Inside the first
   unequal chunk the per-step digests are compared in order, and the first unequal step is re-run with
   per-stage digests.
3. *Tolerance class.* Envelopes give an upper bound and samples a lower bound on the largest scaled difference
   over an interval; unresolved intervals are refined chronologically.
4. *Never bisect on an undecided or pointwise predicate.*
5. *Missing samples.* The answer is the earliest possible interval and a coverage gap, not a tick.
6. *Stage.* Within the first supported interval the algorithm refines to the first stage over tolerance.
7. *Changed topology.* Two designs with different bodies are compared on shared interfaces and task observables
   only. A new leg has no counterpart.
8. *Decisions.* Beside the state divergence, the first tick at which a task decision differs (a contact-mode
   sequence, a termination, a success) is reported.

**The trap behind rule 4.** Bisection is valid only on a predicate that is monotone and decided. "Some tick up
to t differs" is monotone. "The states differ at tick t" is not, because an error can vanish and return.
Counterexample: two traces of eight ticks that differ at ticks 1 and 5 and agree at ticks 2 to 4. A midpoint
search on "tick k differs" probes tick 3 or 4, sees agreement, goes right and reports tick 5. The chained
digest reports tick 1.

This bead builds parts 1, 2, 4, 6, 7 and 8, the report and the verb; the tolerance class and missing samples
(parts 3 and 5) are N29a-20. Seed done-condition: GATE-CHRON-06; GATE-AUTOPSY-07. Tier Solid.

## Technical approach

**1. Interface across layers (DESIGN).** The request and report types and the trait `DivergenceSearch { fn
first_divergence(&self, a: RunRef, b: RunRef, opts) -> Result<DivergenceReport, Refusal> }` live in
`frob-core` (layer 0). `frob-chronicle` (layer 6) implements the trait; `frob-verbs` hands the implementation
to crates of lower layers that need it (the attribution crate is layer 5 and cannot link the record crate).
The implementation exposes its two entry points, `exact` and `tolerance`, and its block bounds to tests.

**2. Align.**
1. Episodes: by world address for a replay, a tier pair or a backend pair; by (parent address, fork path) for
   a fork against its parent; by address for two designs evaluated on common addresses.
2. Ticks: the committed control ticks present in both; a tick present in one only is a coverage difference.
3. Variables: by stable identifier (link path, joint path, contact feature pair, channel name), never by
   position. A variable with no counterpart is listed as `unpaired` and never matched.
4. Decide the comparable set: if model identities are equal, all canonical state; otherwise the shared
   interfaces (the base, links and joints whose paths and types are equal in both models) and the task
   observables (the Task stream's channels).
5. Chunk lengths: the two runs may have been recorded with different chunk lengths. `chunk_len` is a field of
   each run's manifest (a power of two from 64 to 1,024 control ticks, 256 by default; the chunk-length
   decision N29b-18 added it as an input of `sim.run`, `--chunk-len`). A reader never assumes a value and
   never pairs chunk index with chunk index: everything is aligned on ticks.

**3. Exact search** (both runs in an exact class against each other: the same backend and profile, or a pair
with the bitwise class attained; and equal model identities).
1. Chunk level: let P(i) = [x_i(A) ≠ x_i(B)], with x_i the digest-chain value at the end of chunk i. P is
   monotone in i. Binary search for the least i with P(i); if none, the runs agree on the compared range.
   When the chunk lengths differ (L_A ≠ L_B), "chunk i" is a block of the **coarser** partition, L = max(L_A,
   L_B) ticks long: the chain is per tick (x_t folds x_{t−1} and d_t), so its value at a tick does not depend
   on how a run was cut into chunks, and because every admitted length is a power of two each coarse boundary
   is also a chunk end of the finer run, where its record holds the chain value. P(i) compares the two chain
   values at the last tick of coarse block i. (Were a length ever admitted that is not a power of two, the
   common boundaries would be the multiples of the least common multiple; the search takes its boundaries
   from the two manifests and assumes neither value.)
2. Tick level: inside chunk i (the coarse block, when the lengths differ) compare the per-tick digests d_t in
   order. They come from the record where it
   has them (ring-holding and traced worlds); otherwise both runs are replayed from the snapshot at the end of
   chunk i − 1 (or from tick zero) with per-tick capture. The first unequal tick is t₁. A linear scan in order
   is used, not a bisection on d_t.
3. Stage level: re-run tick t₁ on both sides from the common state at t₁ − 1 with per-stage digests; the first
   differing (substep, stage, kernel) is s₁. For a fork against its parent the first divergent tick is the
   intervention tick or later, and the stage step compares from the parent's state with and without the
   change.

**4. Decision divergence.** Independently of states: scan the Task stream and T1 events of both runs in tick
order and report the first tick at which any of these differs: the contact-mode sequence (the ordered list of
make and break events by feature pair), a termination (and its cause), a success predicate, a requirement
atom's truth value, a failure class. This is what the decision-level equivalence test reads.

**5. Order of leaving agreement.** For a fork against its parent and for tolerance comparisons the report lists
the classes of variable in the order in which they first differ (for example: contact impulses at tick 412,
joint velocities at 412, base pose at 413, a termination at 520).

**6. Report (`frob.divergence/1`, the core type of item 1; the verb's output schema
`frob.telemetry.divergence.out/1` carries it as `result`).** `{ a, b, mode: "exact" | "tolerance", compared: {
ticks: [from, to],
variables: n, unpaired: [names] }, agree: bool, first_state_divergence: null | { tick, resolution: "tick" |
"interval", interval: [lo, hi] | null, substep, stage, kernel, variables: [ { name, unit, a, b } ] },
first_decision_divergence: null | { tick, what, a, b }, order_of_departure: [ { class, tick } ],
coverage_gaps: [ { from, to, run, reason } ], frontier: [seq_a, seq_b], caveat }`. `caveat` is the fixed
sentence: "This is where the two computations part. It is not a statement about cause, and the order in which
solver stages report residuals is not evidence of a first physical cause." The first recorded discrepancy is
labelled with its temporal resolution.

**7. The verb** (`telemetry.divergence`, kind `read`, M2, capability `telemetry:read`; replays it needs for
per-tick capture run on the CPU tier in process, or as a job when a device is required, in which case the verb
returns exit 8 with a job handle and is re-read afterwards). `frob telemetry divergence <a> <b> [--world <w>]
[--mode exact|tolerance] [--from <tick>] [--to <tick>] [--scales <profile>] [--no-replay] --json`. `--no-replay`
forbids every replay the search would otherwise run (per-tick capture here, block refinement in tolerance mode):
the answer then comes from stored data alone and is a chunk-resolution interval or `unresolved` where the
record cannot decide, never a guessed tick. Mode defaults to exact
when both runs are in an exact class against each other and to tolerance otherwise; asking for exact mode on a
pair that is not is refused (FROB-REF-802 with the pair's classes named; repair: `--mode tolerance`). Refusals:
FROB-REF-840 (a sealed confirmation run; exit 5, outside the session's scopes, N24-REF), FROB-REF-888 where a
requested range has no samples at all (exit 3),
FROB-REF-894 (a per-tick capture replay would restore a snapshot of another numerical profile as a
continuation; repair: a comparison replay, which this verb then runs in tolerance mode; exit 3), FROB-REF-802
(exit 3). Evidence: *pass-through* (the claim product of the two records; the report adds no claim, and its
`caveat` says so). Exit 0 when the runs agree on the compared range; exit 2 when they part or when coverage gaps
limit the answer. The registry row (VERBS-telemetry) records this verb as `read` with a static cost model; the
job case above (DESIGN: a capture that needs a device is a job) is the only exception and is stated in the row's
cost model.

**8. Layout.** `crates/frob-core/src/divergence.rs` (types, trait); `crates/frob-chronicle/src/divergence/
{align.rs, exact.rs, decisions.rs, report.rs}`; `crates/frob-verbs/src/verbs/telemetry_divergence.rs`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `two_tick_case_reports_tick_1` (KA-717): eight ticks, differences at 1 and 5, equal at 2 to 4.
- `pointwise_mutant_reports_tick_5`: the midpoint search on "tick k differs", kept in the test tree, returns 5
  on the same case; the production function is asserted different from it on 100 two-tick cases with random
  first ticks.
- `chunk_bisection_is_on_chain`: P(i) is evaluated on chain values; with a divergence in chunk 3 of 16 the
  search visits at most 4 chunk indices.
- `tick_scan_is_linear_in_order`.
- `boundary_cases`: divergence at the first tick of a chunk; at the last tick; at tick 0; none.
- `divergence_across_different_len`: two recordings of one deterministic episode (same address, backend and
  profile, 600 control ticks) at `chunk_len` 128 and 256 report no divergence (`agree: true`, exit 0), in
  both argument orders; with one unit in the last place planted at tick 300 of the 128 recording, and again
  with it planted in the 256 recording instead, the report names tick 300 in both cases (tick 300 lies in
  the coarse block [256, 511], which is scanned from tick 256); the chunk-level search compares chain values
  at ticks 255 and 511 only, and at the last tick 599 for the final partial block; a planted aligner that
  pairs chunk index with chunk index reports a divergence on the unplanted pair and fails the test.
- `stage_named`: a plant in stage 7 at substep 3 is reported as (3, 7, its kernel).
- `per_tick_from_replay_when_absent`: a world with no ring is replayed for the chunk and gives the same t₁.
- `align_by_identifier_not_position`: channels listed in different orders pair correctly; a renamed channel is
  unpaired.
- `changed_topology_shared_only`.
- `decision_divergence_first`: equal states until tick 300, a termination at 280 in one run through a
  different threshold in the task: decision divergence at 280, state divergence null in range.
- `order_of_departure`.
- `exact_mode_refused_for_tolerance_pair`.
- `capture_replay_profile_894`: per-tick capture for a run whose only snapshot before the chunk is bound to
  another profile is refused with 894 in exact mode and answered in tolerance mode when asked.

`scripts/e2e/telemetry_divergence.sh` (owned by N29a-21). This bead's step group `exact-*`: a `batch-cpu` run
and a second run of the same address with one unit in the last place planted at (tick 137, substep 4, stage 6)

Lines carry both run identifiers, the mode, the compared range, the reported tick, stage and kernel against the plant, both
digests, both frontiers and the caveat's presence.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-717: traces that differ at ticks 1 and 5 report tick 1 (exact; G1 · host). Negative control: midpoint
  search on a pointwise predicate reports tick 5. CX-320 is the same case in the counterexample catalogue.
- Further mutants: a search that trusts the final digest only; an aligner that matches by position (the tail
  case pairs a tail link with a leg link); a report without the caveat (schema check).
- Property: for random divergence sets (a random subset of ticks at which runs differ), the reported tick is
  the minimum of the set.
- Determinism: the report is a pure function of the two records and the options; bytes compared on repeat.

## Considerations

- The digest chain makes the exact search correct up to the collision budget of a 128-bit mix; the report says
  "digests first differ at", and a pair with equal chains is reported as "digests equal on the compared range".
- Comparing two designs of one lineage is always a tolerance or decision comparison in practice: their states
  differ from the first tick the edit takes effect, and the useful outputs are the order of departure and the
  first decision divergence.
- The same trap applies to bisecting a lineage of designs: the predicate there must be "the outcome has
  changed by this edit", and each step must be decided by a study. That rule belongs to the lineage-bisect bead
  and cites this counterexample.
- Per-tick capture by replay needs the kernels and identities of both runs to be present; an evicted object
  turns the answer into a chunk-resolution interval with the reason, not a guess.
