## Background

The energy ledger attributes every joule that enters a rollout to where it went: actuator losses, stored
energy, mechanical work, contact and joint dissipation, work on fixtures, and a numerical residual on its own
line. On the referee it is computed in double precision on the host. On the batched tiers (`batch-cpu`, CUDA,
Metal) the step runs in single precision for thousands of worlds, state stays on the device, and the ledger
must be accumulated inside the step by a kernel: `K-ledger-step`, the last kernel of stage 9 ("integrate on the
group; accumulate the energy ledger").

Two difficulties are specific to these tiers. First, precision: energy differences are small differences of
large numbers, and a running sum of 10,000 single-precision terms loses the digits the ledger is about. Every
term is therefore accumulated in double-float (a pair of single-precision numbers carrying about 48 bits) on
the device. Second, the fast contact model: a fast contact model can hand a policy energy that no actuator
supplied, and the ledger is what makes that measurable. The residual is therefore kept with its sign, with the
positive part separate.

**ADR-CHRON-08: the numerical residual is its own line, and created energy is audited.** The integrator's and
the solver's energy error is never folded into a physical term.

This bead delivers the kernel, the accumulators, their emission and the closure gates on single-precision
tiers. The three audit monitors and the verb `fidelity.energy` are N29a-22. Seed done-condition:
single-precision rows of GATE-CHRON-03 and GATE-SINEW-02; GATE-CHRON-08; GATE-FID-06. Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: the kernel's inputs from stages 6 to 9; the per-step discrete terms; double-float accumulation; the
per-world and per-port accumulators; the signed residual and its positive part by contact mode; emission at T0
and T2; host conversion to the ledger's integer quanta; closure measurement in single precision; the
single-precision row of the port balance.
Out: the ledger's lines, identities, breakdowns and per-task figures as defined on the referee (BD-092, reused
unchanged); the monitors, flags and `fidelity.energy` (N29a-22); the actuator account's own kernels; the
algorithmic-exchange split (another unit); the gate record of GATE-FID-06 (dynamics unit).

## Technical approach

**1. Manifest.** `K-ledger-step`: class `float` with `f32dd` accumulators under the IEEE-safe math mode; reads
the world's own rows of: pre-step and post-step velocities and configuration (for kinetic and potential
energy), the mass-matrix action already formed in stage 6, actuator port lines from the actuator kernels,
solved contact impulses, gaps and slip velocities and joint-row impulses from stage 8, external and fixture
wrenches; writes the world's ledger accumulators. No cross-world access. Parallel shape: worlds × joints and
worlds × rows.

**2. Per-step discrete terms** (the same definitions as on the referee, evaluated with the step's own discrete
quantities so that the kinetic balance closes algebraically up to rounding):
- `d_kinetic` = ½·v₊ᵀ·M(q₊)·v₊ − ½·vᵀ·M(q)·v, from the two committed states;
- `d_potential` = U(q₊) − U(q) (gravity, and springs of the model);
- `joint_work` per actuated joint = τ·h·v̄ with v̄ = (v₊ + v)/2 on that coordinate;
- `contact_sliding_friction` per contact = f_t·v_slip·h at the solved slip velocity; `contact_normal_damping`
  = c·ḋ²·h for the compliant law; energy stored in contact compliance (½·k·d² for the convex compliant law) is
  a *stored* line, not dissipation and not creation;
- `joint_damping`, limit, stiction and backlash rows by their own laws;
- `fixture_payload_work` and work by prescribed surfaces, with sign;
- the actuator lines (copper, friction, gear, stored by store, heat to ambient, the four braking modes, bus
  power in positive and negative parts) taken from the actuator account;
- `numerical_residual` r = `d_kinetic` + `d_potential` − (`joint_work` + external work − all dissipation) −
  Δ(contact compliance energy).

**3. Accumulation.** Each line has a per-world `f32dd` accumulator updated by the error-free two-sum; products
that enter a line (τ·v̄, f_t·v_slip) are formed with the split product so that the low part is kept. No fused
multiply-add is used; the start-up canary of the double-float kernels must have passed on the device
(FROB-REF-609 otherwise: run without double-float kernels, labelled, and file the compiler evidence). In
addition to the signed residual accumulator there is `created` = Σ max(0, r), kept separately and tagged by the
contact mode in which it arose (no contact, sticking, sliding, impact or limit): five accumulators per world.

**4. Per-port accumulators.** For traced worlds (T2) the kernel also accumulates per joint and per contact
site, which is what breakdowns by joint, limb and stance or swing need. Untraced worlds carry per-world totals
only (T0): about 30 lines × 8 bytes = 240 bytes per world.

**5. Emission.** At each logical chunk boundary the T0 statistics kernel copies the per-world totals into the
statistics ring (BD-207). The per-step residual r and the per-step absolute work exchanged w = |joint work| +
|external work| + |dissipation| are written at T2 for traced worlds and into a short per-world window buffer
for all worlds (the last 0.5 s of r and w at control rate), which the audit monitors read.

**6. Host side.** After the drain, each double-float total is converted to the ledger's integer quantum (1 nJ):
the pair (hi, lo) is widened exactly to double precision, summed and rounded half-even. Cross-world and
cross-shard sums are integer sums (the evidence-aggregate types), so a total has the same bits under any
permutation of worlds, shard split and merge order. The stored `numerical_residual` total is minus the integer
sum of the other lines.

**7. Closure in single precision.** The same two figures as on the referee: the closure defect ε (independent
accumulation of both sides of the identity, relative to the absolute work exchanged) and the residual share ρ.
Targets: ε below 10⁻³ relative for the whole ledger on single-precision tiers; the per-episode port balance of
each actuator element below 10⁻⁴ relative. These are different scopes: the looser bound on the whole ledger
does not relax the bound on the port balance.

**8. Layout.** `crates/frob-kernels/src/dynamics/k_ledger_step.rs` and its dialect twin;
`crates/frob-chronicle/src/energy/batched.rs`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition, GATE-CHRON-03 single-precision row (TARGET): on the damped pendulum and on the
      reference quadruped standing and trotting for 20 s, the ledger closes below 10⁻³ relative on `batch-cpu`
      in single precision and on each admitted device; the pendulum's lines match the hand calculation within
      that bound. Both ε and ρ are recorded.
- [ ] Seed done-condition, GATE-SINEW-02 single-precision row (TARGET): the port energy balance of every
      actuator element closes below 10⁻⁴ relative per episode on the same runs.
- [ ] Seed done-condition, GATE-CHRON-08 and GATE-FID-06: this bead supplies the signed residual, the positive
      part by contact mode and the window buffer on which the audit is computed; N29a-22 records the energy
      half of GATE-CHRON-08, and the dynamics unit records GATE-FID-06, both against this kernel.
- [ ] INV-CHRON-09 on batched tiers: the stored lines sum to zero in integers and no physical line absorbs the
      residual (a planted fold of r into sliding friction is caught by the pendulum fixture, which has no
      friction).
- [ ] Ledger totals of a run are byte-identical under 100 permutations of world order and under a split
      across two shards.
- [ ] The kernel is bit-identical between `batch-cpu` and each device that has attained the bitwise class, and
      inside the recorded envelope otherwise; its CPU-versus-device check is current before it enters a scored
      plan.
- [ ] Physics digests are unchanged by the presence of the kernel's telemetry writes (it reads state and writes
      only ledger and statistics slabs).
- [ ] With a plain single-precision accumulator substituted (mutant), the long-accumulation test fails (the
      total stalls at 2,048 J of a true 4,000 J), so the double-float path is shown to be load-bearing.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `pendulum_f32_closure` (KA-707 on batched tiers): 1 kg, 1 m, 9.81 m/s², damping 0.5 N·m·s/rad, 60°, 60 s:
  `d_potential` within 10⁻³ relative of −4.905 J; ε < 10⁻³.
- `double_float_accumulates_small_terms`: 4·10⁷ additions of 10⁻⁴ J (true total 4,000 J): a plain `f32`
  accumulator stops growing at 2,048 J, where its spacing is 2.44·10⁻⁴ J and half of that exceeds the
  increment; the `f32dd` accumulator reaches 4,000 J within 10⁻⁶ relative.
- `kinetic_balance_algebraic`: for a constant-inertia body the sum of v̄·impulse over forces equals
  `d_kinetic` to rounding.
- `compliance_energy_is_storage`: a mass pressed 1 mm into a contact of stiffness 10⁵ N/m stores 0.05 J, and
  releasing it returns at most that; `created` stays 0.
- `created_tagged_by_mode`: a planted positive residual during sliding lands in the `sliding` accumulator only.
- `per_port_sums_to_world_total` (traced worlds).
- `integer_totals_permutation_invariant`.
- `residual_not_folded` (the planted fold).
- `canary_required_609`: with the double-float canary failing in a test build, the plan is refused or runs
  labelled without the double-float kernels.
- `port_balance_f32`: a geared motor under a voltage step for 1 s: element balance below 10⁻⁴ relative.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/energy_ledger_batched.sh`: for `batch-cpu` and each present device: `frob sim run --tier <t>
--fixture pendulum-damped --worlds 256 --seconds 60 --precision f32 --json` and `frob sim run --tier <t>
--workload Q --worlds 4096 --seconds 20 --json`; then `frob telemetry energy <run> --json` and `frob telemetry
energy <run> --by actuator --json`; assert the hand values, ε against 10⁻³, each actuator's port balance
against 10⁻⁴, byte-equal totals for a second run with worlds in another slot order, and equal physics digests
with ledger emission on and off. Log to `target/e2e-logs/energy_ledger_batched/<utc>.ndjson`: per line `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block with the placement
receipt; `metric` lines with every line's value in joules, ε, ρ and the worst port balance against their
thresholds; on failure the first offending step and port and the run identifier. The `summary` line names
GATE-CHRON-03 (single-precision row) and GATE-SINEW-02 (single-precision row).

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-707 at the single-precision tolerance; KA-295 (the port energy balance closes each step to a recorded
  residual: 10⁻⁴ relative per episode on single-precision tiers; control: stored heat also counted as a loss
  produces a false defect).
- KA-354 as input to the audit: a position-level penetration correction that removes d = 1 mm in one step at
  m = 1 kg and h = 2 ms gives the body d/h = 0.5 m/s and 0.125 J per event from nothing; the convex compliant
  law on the same penetration at k = 10⁵ N/m stores 0.05 J, returns less and creates none.
- Negative controls: the plain accumulator; the fold of the residual into friction; compliance energy booked
  as creation (an honest bounce is then flagged); a sign error at one port (localized to that port).
- Metamorphic: permuting world indices permutes results and changes nothing else; halving the time step moves
  the residual line by the integrator's order.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one device; across worker counts on `batch-cpu`; debug and release; across
  backends against the class.

## Considerations

- The thresholds are TARGETs; the created-energy rule's numbers are re-baselined once at M2 with the frozen
  fidelity envelopes, and that one permitted re-baseline is written down.
- On the compliant tiers the algorithmic exchange of a resting contact is zero because the contact force is a
  function of state; the reserved line matters on the hard-contact batch law, which arrives later.
- Cost: one pass over joints and rows per physics step; it is part of the step in every configuration of the
  telemetry cost gate, since it is accounting of the physics and not optional telemetry.
- Several later beads read this kernel's output: the exploitation detectors, the model-form rank check, the
  energy-budget safety tier and the synthetic rehearsal of calibration.
