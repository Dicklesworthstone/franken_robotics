## Background

Two mechanisms of the record subsystem (Chronicle) claim that what they produce does not depend on scheduling:
the choice of which worlds are traced is a pure function of addresses, and distributional summaries are integer
histograms merged by addition. This bead measures both claims on real backends:

> GATE-CHRON-07 (Known answer; M2; TARGET): sampling and histograms: INV-CHRON-05 and -06 hold over 100
> permutations, shard splits and co-tenancies on `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal; weighted estimates from the
> traced stratum recover a planted population mean inside the exact interval.

- INV-CHRON-05: the set of traced worlds is a pure function of the run manifest and the addresses.
- INV-CHRON-06: every evidence aggregate has the same bits under any permutation of worlds, shard split and
  merge order.

"Co-tenancy" means the job's worlds share a batch with worlds of other jobs. "Shard split" means the job's
worlds are divided among several independent batches (on two cards, or several batches on one device).
"Permutation" means the same worlds occupy different slots. A world's address `(run_seed, world, episode)` is
the same in every variation; only where and beside whom it runs changes.

This is a test bead: it builds the variation generator, the comparisons, the planted population, the negative
controls, the script and the gate record. Tier Solid; verification tier G5 (determinism), all tiers.

## Scope

In: the variation matrix; the reference job; the three comparisons (traced sets, aggregate bytes, weighted
estimates); mutants; the script `telemetry_sampling_histograms.sh`; the gate row per backend.
Out: the mechanisms (N29a-12, N29a-13, N29a-14, BD-207, N29a-15); the general batch-composition gate over
physics digests (BD-181); the cost gate (N29a-17).

## Technical approach

**1. Reference job.** The reference quadruped on rough ground under its baseline controller, 1,024 worlds × 2
episodes of 4 s (200 control ticks), seed 20261122, `trace.rate = 1/16` (so about 128 traced addresses of
2,048), three histogrammed channels (knee torque, friction utilization, base height) with run-level and
per-world histograms, envelopes on, and a *planted population*: a per-world scalar y_w ∈ {0, 1} recorded only at
T2, set to 1 when the Philox draw of a dedicated stream `gate-planted` for the world's address is below 3/8.
The true count M of ones among the 2,048 addresses is computed by the script from the addresses alone.

**2. Variation matrix, per backend.** 100 variations drawn by seed, each a triple:
- a permutation of the slot order of the job's worlds (uniform);
- a shard split: 1, 2, 3, 4 or 8 batches with random assignment of worlds to batches (on CUDA with two cards,
  splits across cards are included; on Metal and `batch-cpu`, several batches in sequence);
- a co-tenancy: 0, 256 or 1,024 foreign worlds of another seed and another task in the same batch, at a batch
  capacity of 2,048 or 4,096.
Variation 0 is the plain run (identity permutation, one shard, no co-tenants).

**3. Comparison A, traced sets (INV-CHRON-05).** For each variation read the list of traced addresses
(`frob telemetry query --traced-worlds`) and compare its sorted digest with (a) variation 0 and (b) the set the
script computes from the manifest with an independent reference implementation of the hash. Failing worlds are
compared separately (the failing set must also be equal, since trajectories are equal).

**4. Comparison B, aggregates (INV-CHRON-06).** For each variation compare the canonical bytes of: the three
run-level histograms; every per-world T0 histogram; every per-chunk envelope; the T0 counts. All must equal
variation 0. Across backends the same integers are compared: integer aggregates of *identical inputs* are class
D0; where a device does not attain bitwise equality with `batch-cpu` for the floating state, the inputs differ
and the cross-backend comparison is made on a fixed, replayed input corpus instead (the T2 values recorded on
`batch-cpu` are fed through each backend's quantizer and `K-x-hist`), which isolates the aggregate from the
physics.

**5. Comparison C, weighted estimates.** For each variation compute Ŷ_total = Σ_{failing} y + 16·Σ_{sampled,
non-failing} y from the traced worlds and check that it is identical in every variation (same sample). Then,
over 1,000 further run seeds of a reduced job (same addresses, physics shortened to one tick, since y depends
only on the address), check that the sampled count X of ones among non-failing worlds lies inside the exact
central binomial interval for (M′, 1/16) at error 10⁻⁶ per seed, where M′ is the number of ones among
non-failing worlds, and that the mean of Ŷ_total over seeds is within 3 standard errors of the planted total.
"Exact interval" is computed from the binomial distribution in exact rational arithmetic, not from a normal
approximation.

**6. Negative controls, each run on `batch-cpu` and required to fail its comparison:**
- arrival-order reservoir sampling in place of the address hash (traced sets differ between variations);
- a compactor quantile sketch in place of the histogram (medians differ under permutation; the three-sample
  streams 1, 2, 3 and 3, 2, 1 give 1 and 2);
- a floating-point sum accumulator in the run-level histogram's count path;
- a hash that mixes in the slot index;
- weights applied uniformly to failing worlds as well (the estimate's mean leaves the interval).

**7. Gate record.** Per backend one row with: variations run, variations with equal traced sets, variations
with equal aggregate bytes, corpus words differing across backends (threshold 0), seeds inside the binomial
interval of 1,000, mutants caught of 5, the commit, the fingerprint digest of the device, the base seed. A
backend that is absent or not admitted is recorded `not run` with the reason; under the no-GPU variant of the
milestone the gate is recorded for `batch-cpu` alone and says so.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On each of `batch-cpu`, CUDA and Metal: 100 of 100 variations have traced sets equal to variation 0 and
      to the independent computation from the manifest.
- [ ] On each backend: 100 of 100 variations have byte-equal run-level histograms, per-world histograms,
      envelopes and counts.
- [ ] The replayed-corpus comparison shows zero differing words between backends for the quantizer and the
      histogram kernel.
- [ ] The weighted estimate is identical across the 100 variations, and over 1,000 seeds the sampled count lies
      inside the exact binomial interval in every seed, with the mean within 3 standard errors of the planted
      total.
- [ ] All five mutants are caught.
- [ ] Physics run digests are equal across the 100 variations on each backend (a precondition, measured by the
      batch-composition harness and re-read here; a failure there is reported as that gate's failure, not this
      one's).
- [ ] The script exits non-zero below any threshold and names the first failing variation with its seed,
      permutation digest, shard split and co-tenancy.
- [ ] The gate rows cite claim identifiers that resolve in the artifact graph.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In `crates/frob-conformance/tests/g5_sampling_histograms.rs`:
- `variation_is_seeded`: the same seed gives the same triple.
- `reference_hash_agrees_with_library` (three frozen vectors) and `reference_hash_is_independent` (it shares
  no code with the library; a repository check on the source file).
- `planted_population_count`: M computed from addresses equals the count read from an all-traced run.
- `binomial_interval_exact`: for (M′, p) = (700, 1/16) the central interval at error 10⁻⁶ computed in rationals
  matches a table committed with the test.
- `comparator_fires_on_one_bit`: flipping one count in one histogram of one variation fails comparison B.
- `each_mutant_fails` (five cases).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/telemetry_sampling_histograms.sh` (this bead owns it; step groups `sample-*`, `hist-core-*`,
`kernel-*` and `verb-*` come from N29a-12, N29a-13, N29a-14 and N29a-15):
1. For each present backend: run variation 0 with `frob sim run --tier <t> --worlds 1024 --episodes 2
   --seconds 4 --telemetry T2 --trace-rate 1/16 --seed 20261122 --key v0 --json`.
2. Run variations 1 to 99 through the batch-composition harness (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g5 --
   GATE-CHRON-07 --backend <t> --variations 100`), which drives the same binary with slot maps, shard maps and
   co-tenant jobs, and read its receipt.
3. Fetch and compare aggregates with `frob telemetry histogram` and `frob telemetry query --aggregate`.
4. The replayed-corpus comparison with `frob spark check --kernel K-x-hist`.
5. The estimate lane and the five mutants.
It sources the shared helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/telemetry_sampling_histograms/<utc>.ndjson` and a
transcript. Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time and the
evidence block with the placement receipt. `metric` lines carry, per backend, the four counts with thresholds
(100, 100, 0, 1,000). `assert` lines carry both digests. On failure: the variation's seed and description, the
first differing address or bin with both values, and the paths of both runs' stores. The `summary` line lists
assertions and names GATE-CHRON-07.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-713 (the traced set is identical under 100 slot assignments and shard counts; control: arrival-order
  reservoir sampling gives different sets) and KA-714 (counts identical under 100 permutations and shard
  splits, on every backend; control: the capacity-two compactor gives medians 1 and 2).
- CX-319 and CX-325 from the counterexample catalogue are the first two mutants.
- Metamorphic relation MR-32 is this gate stated as a relation.
- Determinism lanes: the gate itself is the across-composition lane; it is also run twice on one machine with
  equal outcome vectors, and in debug and release on `batch-cpu`.

## Considerations

- Compute: about 100 runs of 2,048 world-episodes of 4 s per backend; on a device this is minutes and fits the
  default envelope of one device and one hour; on `batch-cpu` it is sized to eight cores.
- If a device family holds only tolerance agreement with `batch-cpu` (the kill gate on bitwise equality fired),
  this gate is unaffected within the family (comparisons A and B are per backend) and the cross-backend
  statement rests on the replayed corpus.
- The co-tenancy lane before the daemon's dispatcher exists uses the harness's direct slot maps; it is re-run
  through the dispatcher when that lands, by the dispatcher's own gate.
