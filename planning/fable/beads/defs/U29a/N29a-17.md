## Background

Telemetry is only "always on" if it is cheap. The record subsystem (Chronicle) keeps level T0 (per-episode
metrics, energy-ledger totals, integer histograms, per-chunk envelopes, state digests) and T1 (events) for
every world, always, and level T2 (control-rate traces) for an address-keyed sample. The claim that this is
affordable is a Performance claim with its own gate:

> GATE-CHRON-13 (Performance; M2; TARGET): T0 and T1 on every world cost at most 3% of step time, and T2 on a
> 1% sample at most 2% more, on the reference quadruped workload with the disclosure block.

A Performance claim in this project is a recorded run on named hardware carrying a complete disclosure block;
a figure without the block is refused as a Performance claim (FROB-REF-862). A ratio is admitted only as a
paired lower bound, never as a ratio of two means (FROB-REF-863 otherwise). And the method rules of the
performance program apply: account for the whole workflow (transfers, synchronization and telemetry stay in the
denominator), timers cover completion and never enqueue alone, measure paired and interleaved on a quiet host.

This is a test bead: it defines the three configurations, the measurement, the statistics, the disclosure, the
script and the gate record. It optimizes nothing; if the gate fails, the levers are named in Considerations and
each is a separate, measured change. Tier Solid.

## Scope

In: the workload and the three telemetry configurations; the "bare" configuration's definition; the paired
measurement protocol; the bound computation; the disclosure block; digests as the behaviour proof; the script
`telemetry_cost.sh`; the gate rows per device family and for `batch-cpu`.
Out: the performance harness itself (BD-216); throughput parity rows of the engine (BD-217); the telemetry
mechanisms (BD-207, N29a-11, N29a-14).

## Technical approach

**1. Workload.** Reference workload Q: the reference quadruped with 12 actuated joints and 16 foot samples on
rough ground, 18 degrees of freedom, 13 bodies, contact capacity 32, row capacity 160, step h = 2 ms, four
warm-started Newton iterations, control decimation 10 (50 Hz control), 8,192 worlds, the baseline controller in
the loop and no learning. The unit is seconds of wall-clock per environment step (one control interval for all
worlds), measured to completion.

**2. Configurations.**
- **B (bare):** a measurement-only build flag disables every `stat`-class kernel and the host drain: no
  histograms, envelopes, events, per-tick digests and digest chain (N29ap1-01), failure monitors (N29b-05) or
  ring. The commit fence and the chunk structure stay. B is never a shippable configuration and its results are never evidence of anything but
  cost.
- **T01:** T0 and T1 on every world as shipped: metrics, envelopes on the default channel set, run-level and
  per-world histograms, per-control-tick state digest and digest chain, the T1 event buffer, the failure
  monitors in the step (the kernel `K-x-monitor` of N29b-05 with its first-anomaly latches and the events it
  emits: they are part of what T0 and T1 cost on every world, and that bead records the kernel's own cost as
  one line of this budget), the drain and chunk publication, and the energy-ledger totals' transfer (the ledger kernel itself is part of the step in
  all three configurations, since it is physics accounting; only its emission is telemetry).
- **T012:** T01 plus T2 on `trace.rate = 1/100` and the rings of N29a-11 at their defaults for the traced
  worlds.

**3. Protocol.** After warm-up (kernels cached, launch graphs captured, 200 environment steps discarded), run
R = 30 blocks. A block is three segments of 500 environment steps, one per configuration, in a seeded and
balanced order: each configuration takes each position ten times (interleaving removes drift from thermal
state and host load). Each segment's time is the completion
time of its last device chunk minus the start of its first, including host drain and publication. On the Mac
the run starts after a thermal soak; on the shared Linux host the load average is recorded per block.

**4. Statistics.** For block i let t_B, t_01, t_012 be the three segment times. Paired overheads: a_i = t_01 /
t_B − 1 and b_i = (t_012 − t_01) / t_B. The gate value for each is the one-sided 95% upper confidence bound of
the mean over the 30 blocks (Student-t on the paired values; the paired-bound function of the performance
harness). Thresholds: bound(a) ≤ 0.03 and bound(b) ≤ 0.02. The medians and the 99th-percentile step times are
reported beside them.

**5. Behaviour proof.** The state at the end of each 500-step segment must be identical in B, T01 and T012 for
the same seed (telemetry never feeds state). Because B computes no in-kernel digest, the comparison uses one
host-side digest per segment: after the segment's last device chunk completes, and outside the timed interval,
the committed state slabs are read back once and hashed with the host reference of the state digest
(`frob_chronicle::digest::reference`, N29ap1-01), in all three configurations alike. In T01 and T012 the
in-kernel d at the last tick must also equal that host digest. A difference voids the measurement and is
reported as a defect of the telemetry path.

**6. Disclosure block (every field required).** Hardware and driver; backend, compiler and kernel versions;
world count; time step, substeps and control decimation; solver iterations and tolerance; contact capacity and
mean active contacts per world; sensors enabled; whether the policy and learning are included; scene;
determinism class; warm-up and duration; whether launch graphs were captured; median and 99th-percentile step
time; peak memory; the unit of every rate and the decimation beside it; row capacity and the counts of worlds
marked `Degraded` and `Faulted`; the profile and precision profile; the fingerprint digest; the placement
receipt with requested and actual backend per stage, fallback count, transfer bytes and synchronization count;
cold or warm, with compile and admission times and the cache state; thermal state and soak duration; host
load; for a ratio the number of pairs, the bound, and the comparator's configuration (here, configuration B);
telemetry-specific additions: channel set, quanta, histogram parameters, trace rate, ring depth and reservoir
size, logical chunk length, device chunk length, bytes drained per environment step.

**7. Gate record.** One row per (device family, hardware): bound(a), bound(b), medians, pairs, digests equal
(yes or no), the disclosure block's identifier, commit, fingerprint. `batch-cpu` is recorded as a row of its
own (24 threads on the Mac) for information; the gate's verdict is taken on each admitted device family, and
on `batch-cpu` alone under the no-GPU variant of the milestone. Each row is entered from the receipt of the
gate's tier run (step 2 of the script) with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate
GATE-CHRON-13 --receipt <hash>`, by a session that did not implement the work.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On one RTX 4090 (`gpu-cuda`) and on the M5 Ultra (`gpu-metal`), each where admitted: the 95% upper bound
      of the T0 + T1 overhead is at most 3% and of the additional T2 overhead at most 2%, with 30 pairs.
- [ ] The physics run digests of B, T01 and T012 are equal for every block.
- [ ] Every recorded figure carries the complete disclosure block; a record with one field removed is refused
      with FROB-REF-862 (planted).
- [ ] The overhead is reported as a paired bound; an attempt to record the ratio of two means is refused with
      FROB-REF-863 (planted).
- [ ] Timers cover completion: a variant that stops the clock at enqueue reports a smaller time and is rejected
      by the harness's completion check (planted).
- [ ] The script records `not run` with the reason for an absent or unadmitted device family, and fails only
      on a measured value above its threshold or a digest mismatch.
- [ ] The measurement stays inside the compute envelope in force and records it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In `crates/frob-conformance/tests/perf_telemetry_cost.rs`:
- `paired_bound_known_values`: for 30 synthetic pairs with overhead exactly 2.0% and standard deviation 0.5%,
  the bound is 2.0% + t₀.₉₅,₂₉·0.5%/√30 = 2.0% + 1.699 × 0.0913% = 2.155%.
- `block_order_is_seeded_and_balanced`: over 30 blocks each configuration appears in each position 10 times.
- `segment_time_uses_completion`: on a recorded receipt stream of a real `batch-cpu` segment, re-timed so that
  every enqueue instant precedes its completion by 5 ms (a data fixture labelled `synthetic`; the timing
  function under test is the real one), the measured time uses completion.
- `t01_includes_failure_monitors`: the step plan of configuration T01 contains `K-x-monitor` and that of B
  does not; a T01 plan built without it is rejected as not the shipped configuration.
- `disclosure_block_complete`: the schema lists every field above; removing each in turn is rejected.
- `digest_mismatch_voids_measurement`.
- `end_digest_outside_timer`: the read-back and host digest of the segment's end state are excluded from the
  segment time (a variant that includes them reports a larger B time and is rejected).
- `bare_flag_not_in_release_profile`: the measurement-only flag cannot be enabled in a build that serves verbs.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/telemetry_cost.sh`:
1. `frob spark fingerprint --json`; `frob sim estimate` for workload Q at 8,192 worlds (memory and time
   interval, to confirm the envelope).
2. The gate's tier run on the family's site, `FROB_TEST_SEED=20261122 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
   <tier> --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-CHRON-13 @<site>` (`<tier>` is the test target, one of the six,
   named by the `tier` field of the gate's record; `<site>` is `cuda`, `metal`, or `lockstep` for `batch-cpu`;
   the entry fixes the 30 blocks and runs the planted controls of step 4 in the same run; the harness drives the
   real engine through the same step plan `frob sim run` uses), then read its receipt.
3. `frob sim run --tier <family> --workload Q --worlds 8192 --seconds 10 --telemetry T2 --trace-rate 1/100
   --key tc-<family>-confirm --json` once as the user-visible confirmation that the shipped configuration is the measured T012, and
   compare its placement receipt and channel set with the disclosure block.
4. The three planted controls (the missing disclosure field refused with FROB-REF-862, the ratio of means with
   FROB-REF-863, the enqueue-only timer rejected by the completion check), each read from that receipt with its
   outcome `caught`.
At M3 the peak capture (N29ax-02) adds the step group `peaks-*`: a second set of 30 balanced blocks over B, T01
and T01+peaks, with its own bound and an added row of the gate.
It sources the shared helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/telemetry_cost/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every
line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time and the evidence block
(claim `Performance`, class `Tested`, tier, determinism class, fingerprint, seed). `metric` lines carry
bound(a) with threshold 0.03 and bound(b) with threshold 0.02, medians and 99th percentiles in milliseconds per
environment step, bytes drained per step, and per-block digests. On failure: the block and configuration of
the first digest mismatch, or the measured bound with the per-kernel time shares of the `stat` kernels from the
profiling mode, and the path of the disclosure block. The `summary` line names GATE-CHRON-13.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Known answer for the statistic: the worked bound above (2.155%).
- Negative controls: the missing disclosure field; the ratio of means; the enqueue-only timer; a "bare"
  configuration that also disables the commit fence (the harness checks that B's synchronization count per
  chunk equals T01's).
- Determinism lane: digests equal across the three configurations (class D2 on the device).
- The measurement is repeated at 1, 8, 64, 256 and 2,048 worlds for the calibration curve the method requires;
  those rows are recorded and are not gated.

## Considerations

- All thresholds are TARGETs. If a bound fails, levers in order, one at a time, each with identical physics
  digests: digest cadence (every control tick, not every physics step); envelope channel set; per-world
  histograms on fewer channels; atomic contention in concentrated run-level bins (replace by per-world partial
  counts and the fixed integer reduction); drain batching. Each is recorded as its own measured change.
- On Metal completion is synchronous and one command buffer is one device chunk, so drain time adds directly to
  the step; on CUDA the drain overlaps the next chunk on another host thread. The two families are therefore
  recorded separately and neither result transfers.
- The shared Linux host is often heavily loaded; blocks whose load average exceeds a recorded limit are kept in
  the record and marked, not dropped.
- A telemetry cost that passes at 8,192 worlds says nothing about 256 or 524,288; the calibration curve is the
  honest statement.
