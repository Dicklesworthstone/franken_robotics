## Background

The first-divergence search answers "where do these two computations part" for replays, tiers, backends, forks
and designs. Its correctness has three faces, and the gate names one case for each:

> GATE-CHRON-06 (Known answer; M2; TARGET): first divergence: the two-tick case reports the earlier tick 100 of
> 100; a tolerance case with a loose bound returns `unresolved` and then the right interval after refinement; a
> trace with missing samples returns an interval and a gap.

- *The two-tick case* guards the exact search against the bisection trap. Two traces differ at an early tick,
  agree again for a while, and differ again later. A search that bisects on the pointwise predicate "the states
  differ at tick t" probes a tick in the agreeing stretch, moves right and reports the later tick. The chained
  digest reports the earlier one. Smallest instance: eight ticks, differences at ticks 1 and 5, agreement at
  ticks 2 to 4; the wrong answer is 5 and the right answer is 1.
- *The loose bound* guards the tolerance search against premature answers: when the stored envelopes cannot
  certify agreement and the stored samples cannot prove a mismatch, the honest answer is `unresolved`, and
  refinement must examine that block before any later one.
- *Missing samples* guard against pretending to a resolution the record does not have: the answer is the
  earliest possible interval and the coverage gap, not a tick.

This is a test bead with the end-to-end script for the verb `telemetry.divergence`. The other subsystem that
reads the algorithm's output keeps its own battery over the same code; this bead records Chronicle's row. Tier
Solid; verification tier G1.

## Technical approach

**1. How a two-tick pair is built on a real step.** Run A: an unmodified `batch-cpu` episode of the reference
quadruped (single precision, `lockstep` profile, 400 control ticks). Run B: the same address with the
conformance fault hook armed twice. At tick t_a the hook adds one unit in the last place to one output of
stage 10 that is *recorded but not fed back* into the next tick's dynamics for the fixture's controller: the
held action of a channel the baseline controller overwrites at its next update. The state digest at t_a
therefore differs and the digests at the following ticks are equal again. At tick t_b > t_a + 2 the hook is
armed on a state variable (a joint velocity), after which the runs differ for good. This reproduces "differ at
t_a, agree in between, differ from t_b" with real kernels; the script asserts the shape itself (digests unequal
at t_a, equal on (t_a, t_b), unequal at t_b) before using the pair, so that a fixture that does not have the
shape cannot pass the gate by accident.

**2. The 100 two-tick trials.** For seeds 0 to 99: draw t_a uniformly in [1, 300], the gap g in [3, 60], t_b =
t_a + g, and the world. Ten trials force t_a onto the first tick of a logical chunk and ten onto the last tick
of one, with t_b in a later chunk; ten keep both ticks inside one chunk. Expected answer in exact mode: t₁ =
t_a, with the stage of the first plant. Measured value: (trials with t₁ = t_a, 100).
The 400-tick episode has two logical chunks, [0, 255] and [256, 399], so the boundary groups are: ten trials
with t_a = 256, the first tick of the second chunk (the chain enters it from its carried value; t_b lies in the
same chunk), and ten with t_a = 255, the last tick of the first chunk, with t_b in the second chunk.

**3. Mutant searches** (kept in the test tree; each must be *wrong* on the trials stated, which keeps the
cases live as negative controls):
- pointwise midpoint bisection on per-tick digests (the mutant's rule, fixed so that the counts below can be
  recomputed: search over the ticks lo = 0 to hi = last tick; probe mid = ⌊(lo + hi)/2⌋; if the digests differ
  at mid then hi = mid, else lo = mid + 1; report lo): its probes converge on the boundary at t_b, and it
  reports t_a only when a probe happens to land exactly on t_a. By exhaustive count over the draw ranges of
  the 400-tick episode that is 720 of the 17,400 free (t_a, g) pairs, about 4%; never for t_a = 255; and for
  t_a = 256 only when g ≤ 6 (the probes are 199, 299, 249, 274, 262 and then 256). The battery asserts that
  it is wrong on at least 90 of the 100 trials, and on the eight-tick minimal case it probes 3, 5, 4 and
  reports tick 5;
- chunk-level comparison of final digests only: reports the chunk of t_b when t_a and t_b lie in different
  chunks and the chunk-end digest after t_a happens to be equal; it is wrong on those trials because the
  chunk-end *state digest* is equal while the *chain* differs. This mutant shows why the search uses the chain
  value and not the state digest at chunk ends.

**4. Tolerance fixtures.**
- *Loose bound.* Pair: single precision against double precision of the same address (a comparison pair, so
  exact mode is refused and tolerance mode is used). A block is prepared in which the base-height envelopes of
  the two runs overlap loosely (the script widens run B's stored envelope row through the conformance harness
  so that hi = 1.4) and the only common stored samples give lo = 0.6. Call 1 with `--no-replay`: the result
  must be `unresolved` with that block's interval and bounds [0.6, 1.4]. Call 2 with replay allowed and a
  5 mm base offset planted at tick 200 (one `shift` plant of N29a-25 on the base's horizontal position, in the
  same plant file as the run's other plants, so replay reproduces it) inside a *later* part of the same block:
  the result must be
  tick 200, and the log must show that the unresolved block was replayed before any later block was read. A
  third pair with a later block whose lo > 1 and an earlier unresolved block that truly agrees must report the
  later block's mismatch only after the earlier block has been refined to agreement.
- *Missing samples.* Run B's T2 samples for ticks 40 to 59 are removed and replaced by a gap record
  (`ring-overflow`), and a persistent mismatch is planted at tick 47 with replay disabled (the kernels'
  identities are made unavailable to the call, as after eviction). Expected: `resolution: "interval"`,
  interval [40, 60] (tick 60, the first proven mismatch, is included because the record cannot exclude that
  every missing tick agreed; N29a-20 §5), one coverage gap [40, 59], no tick. Mutants: a search that skips gaps
  reports tick 60; one that reports the gap alone ([40, 59]) excludes a possible answer.

**5. Gate record.** The row holds: two-tick trials correct of 100; pointwise mutant wrong on k of 100; chain
mutant wrong on its trials; the three tolerance outcomes (unresolved, then tick 200; ordering respected;
interval and gap); the commit, host fingerprint and base seed. The row is entered from the receipt of the
gate's tier run (step 3 of the script) with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate
GATE-CHRON-06 --receipt <hash>`, by a session that did not implement the work.

**6. The query time at the decided chunk length, re-measured end to end.** The decision record on the chunk
length L (`docs/adr/ADR-CHRON-13.md`, written at M1 by the decision bead N29b-18) chose L under four
conditions, of which condition (d) is "a first-divergence query on a 1,000-chunk episode returns in under 1 s
on the host". At M1 neither the digest chain nor this verb existed, so (d) was *computed* from measured parts
(⌈log₂ 1,000⌉ = 10 point lookups of a chunk row in the real ledger plus L per-step digest comparisons) and the
record marks it `computed; re-measured by N29a-21`. This bead makes the measurement:
1. Fixture: one world of workload Q on `batch-cpu` (single precision, `lockstep` profile) run for 1,000 × L
   control ticks at the decided L of the record (256 by default: 256,000 ticks, 5,120 s of simulated time at
   50 Hz), telemetry T0 with the digest chain, twice: run A unmodified and run B with one `ulp` plant on a
   state variable at a tick inside chunk 700.
2. Measurement: the wall time of `frob telemetry divergence <A> <B> --world <w> --json` in exact mode, end to
   end (the process start, the bisection over the chain, the replay of the bracketed chunk on both sides,
   the stage refinement and the envelope), once cold (the first call after the store is opened) and 20 times
   warm; the answer must be the planted tick and stage every time.
3. Bound (TARGET, the decision's own): the cold call and the warm 95th percentile are each under 1 s on the
   host.
4. Record: the two figures, L, the host fingerprint and the commit are appended to `docs/adr/ADR-CHRON-13.md`
   under "condition (d), re-measured end to end at M2", and the mark becomes `re-measured: holds` or
   `re-measured: fails`. A failing figure does not change GATE-CHRON-06's row; it reopens the decision: the
   record's rule is applied again with the measured value (the largest L that satisfies all four
   conditions) as a dated amendment by the decision's owner, and until then L stays as decided.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The two-tick case reports the earlier tick in 100 of 100 trials, with the stage of the first plant.
- [ ] The pointwise mutant is wrong on at least 90 of the 100 trials; the chunk-final-digest mutant is wrong on
      every trial it applies to.
- [ ] The loose-bound call without replay returns `unresolved` with bounds [0.6, 1.4]; with replay it returns
      tick 200, and the unresolved block is refined before any later block.
- [ ] The missing-samples case returns the interval [40, 60] and its gap [40, 59], and no tick; the
      gap-skipping mutant reports tick 60 and is caught, and the gap-only mutant ([40, 59]) is caught.
- [ ] Every pair's shape is asserted before use (unequal, equal, unequal digests for two-tick pairs).
- [ ] Every report carries the caveat that it states where the computations part and not why.
- [ ] The script exits non-zero below any threshold and names the first failing seed with t_a, t_b and the
      reported tick.
- [ ] The gate row's claim identifiers resolve in the artifact graph.
- [ ] The first-divergence query on the 1,000-chunk pair at the decided chunk length returns the planted tick
      and stage, cold and warm, with both wall times measured against the 1 s bound; `docs/adr/ADR-CHRON-13.md`
      carries the appended figures and condition (d) is marked `re-measured: holds` or `re-measured: fails`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In `crates/frob-conformance/tests/g1_first_divergence.rs`:
- `eight_tick_minimal_case`: host-only digest sequences differing at 1 and 5: production returns 1, the
  pointwise mutant returns 5.
- `pair_shape_checker`: accepts (≠, =, =, ≠) and rejects (≠, ≠, ≠) and (=, =, ≠).
- `trial_generator_is_seeded`: seeds 0 to 99 reproduce; the three boundary groups have ten trials each, with
  t_a = 256, t_a = 255 (t_b ≥ 258) and both ticks in one chunk.
- `mutant_wrong_rate`: on the generated trials the pointwise mutant's wrong count is at least 90.
- `chain_vs_state_digest_at_chunk_end`: a pair equal in state at a chunk end and different in chain.
- `loose_bound_fixture_bounds`: the prepared block has lo = 0.6 and hi = 1.4 exactly in rationals.
- `gap_fixture`: after removal, ticks 40 to 59 have no samples and one gap record.
- `pointwise_mutant_exhaustive_count`: over every (t_a, g) with t_a in [1, 300] and g in [3, 60] on 400 ticks
  the mutant reports t_a in exactly 720 of 17,400 cases; for t_a = 255 in none; for t_a = 256 exactly for
  g = 3, 4, 5, 6 (host-only, on synthetic digest sequences labelled `synthetic`).
- `thousand_chunk_fixture_shape`: the timing fixture has exactly 1,000 logical chunks at the decided L and its
  plant lies in chunk 700; a fixture with fewer chunks is rejected before any time is measured.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/telemetry_divergence.sh` (this bead owns it; step groups `exact-*` and `tolerance-*` come from
BD-206 and N29a-20):
1. Build run A: `frob sim run --tier batch-cpu --workload Q --worlds 16 --seconds 8 --telemetry T2 --trace-rate
   1/1 --seed 20261122 --key A --json`.
2. For three hand-picked trials build run B with the hook (the same request with `--conformance-plant <file>`
   under the key `B-<seed>`, a `frob.conformance-plant/1` file of N29a-25 with an `ulp` section holding the two
   plants at t_a and t_b; available only in a build with the conformance feature) and call `frob telemetry divergence <A> <B> --world <w> --json`;
   assert exit 2, `mode: "exact"`, the tick, substep, stage and kernel, and the presence of the caveat.
3. The remaining trials through the gate's tier run, `FROB_TEST_SEED=20261122 cargo test -p frob-conformance
   --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-CHRON-06` (the entry runs the 100 trials
   and the mutants in one run), reading its receipt.
4. The same pair unplanted (run A against a second run of A's request under the key `A2`; the key `A` again
   would return A's stored receipt): exit 0, `agree: true`.
5. The tolerance cases with `--mode tolerance`, `--no-replay` (BD-206's option) and then with replay.
6. A refusal: exact mode requested for the single-against-double pair must exit 3 and name both classes.
7. The query time at the decided chunk length (technical approach, part 6): build the 1,000-chunk pair with
   two `frob sim run` calls under the keys `A1000` and `B1000` (B with `--conformance-plant`), call `frob
   telemetry divergence <A1000> <B1000> --world <w> --json` 21 times, and log the metrics
   `divergence_query_cold_ms` and `divergence_query_warm_p95_ms` against the threshold 1,000 ms, with the
   chunk length, the chunk count and the number of ledger lookups and replayed ticks the report states.
It sources the shared helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/telemetry_divergence/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript.
Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time and the evidence
block of the verb's envelope. `metric` lines carry (correct, 100), the mutants' wrong counts, and for
tolerance cases lo, hi and the order of blocks refined. `assert` lines carry the planted and reported
coordinates and both digests. On failure: the seed, t_a, t_b, the reported tick, the run identifiers and the
store paths. The `summary` line names GATE-CHRON-06.

## Considerations

- The recorded-but-not-fed-back channel used for the first plant must exist in the fixture's controller; the
  shape check guarantees the fixture is what it claims. If a future controller reads the held action, the
  fixture switches to another such channel, and the change is a fixture identity change.
- Widening a stored envelope and removing samples are done through the conformance harness on a copy of the
  run; the original run's chunks are never modified (their keys map to one content hash for the life of the
  run).
- This gate and the other subsystem's gate overlap by design: two subsystems depend on the mechanism and each
  records the cases it relies on.
