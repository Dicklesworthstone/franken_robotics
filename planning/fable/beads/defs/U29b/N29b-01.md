## Background

The energy ledger of the record subsystem (crate `frob-chronicle`) attributes every joule of an episode to a
store, a loss or an export, with the numerical residual on its own line (goal GOAL-11; it is the port structure of
the design, construction C-12, read at run time). Its two identities, per world and episode:

```
battery energy out        = Σ actuators (copper loss + friction loss + gear loss + Δ stored) + mechanical work at joints
mechanical work at joints = Δ kinetic + Δ potential + contact dissipation (normal damping + sliding friction)
                            + joint damping + work on fixtures and payloads + numerical residual
```

Seed bead BD-092 builds the ledger on the referee: the lines, per-step accumulation, breakdowns by joint, by limb
and by stance and swing, the decomposition of cost of transport, and the three refinements (bus power integrated
with its positive and negative parts separately; energy per task reported two ways; every energy figure names the
residual it was computed with). It fixes the stored quantum (1 nJ integers), the type `EnergyFigure { value,
unit, residual_abs, residual_share, frontier }` by which an energy number leaves the crate, and the absolute
work exchanged W_abs = Σ over steps of (the absolute values of joint work and external work, plus dissipation).

This bead is the sub-task that follows a core mechanism: its edge cases, its refusals and the laws its figures
must obey. Each item below is a place where a correct ledger can still print a misleading figure, and where the
plan's sentences leave a decision open. The plan's own example of what the breakdown must support is "38% of the
energy went to copper loss in the knees during stance"; its example of the per-task trap is a design that
succeeds once for 10 J and fails once for 20 J (10 J and 30 J) against a rival with two 12 J successes (12 J and
12 J), where the first figure alone ranks them wrongly.

Decisions here are DESIGN and are stated with the alternative that was rejected.

## Scope

In: the stance-or-swing rule at event boundaries and for bodies without legs; the partition law for breakdowns
and how it is guaranteed; negative cells; cost-of-transport corner cases; per-actuator signed parts and bus
circulation; the channel-kind rule for downsampling energy quantities; per-task figures with no completion and
with numerical or budget outcomes; the residual of an aggregate; the tests and fixtures for all of these.

Out: the lines, the accumulators, the three refinements in their plain form, the first offending event and the
referee gate (BD-092); the batched kernel (BD-208); the verb `telemetry.energy` (bead N24a-37 of unit U24a); the
lines `algorithmic exchange` (N29b-22); the audit monitors (N29b-02; bead N29a-22 of unit U29a).

## Technical approach

**1. Stance or swing, exactly.** BD-092's rule: a limb is in stance during a step when one of its contact sites
carries normal load at the committed state. Refinements:
- *At events.* The referee's transactional step splits a base interval at located touchdown and lift-off events,
  and the active contact set is fixed inside each sub-interval. Phase is assigned per sub-interval, so the
  boundary between swing and stance terms is the event time, not the step boundary. On a fixed-step tier it is
  the step boundary; the tier's result says which.
- *By contact, not by intent.* A foot dragging during a nominal swing is in stance. Rejected: the commanded gait
  phase, because it attributes real contact losses to "swing" and makes the phase table depend on the controller.
- *No legs.* A limb whose contact sites are wheels or tracks is in stance whenever it is in contact; its report
  prints the stance share and no swing row, and says so rather than inventing a swing phase.
- *No limb.* Terms owned by the base link, by links in no named limb (`trunk`), by fixtures, and the residual
  have phase `not applicable`.

**2. The partition law and how it is guaranteed.** Law: for every line, the by-joint table, the by-limb table and
the by-limb-and-phase table each sum to the line total, as integers of the ledger's quantum. Guarantee (DESIGN):
terms are accumulated at the finest key (line, element, phase) only; every coarser figure is defined as the sum
of the quantized finest cells; the line total used by the stored ledger is the same sum. Rejected: one
accumulator per axis, because three independently rounded sums of the same terms disagree in the last digits and
the report then contradicts itself (a registered negative control). Cross-world tables are integer sums of
per-world quantized cells, so they have the same bits under any permutation of worlds, shard split or merge
order (invariant INV-CHRON-06; decision ADR-CHRON-03: evidence aggregates are integers).

**3. Negative cells.** A link losing kinetic energy, a descending body's potential change and regeneration give
negative cells. Every table prints, beside the net, the sum of its positive cells and the sum of its negative
cells, so that a large cancellation is visible. Percentages are stated with their base ("38% of battery energy
out") and are computed from the base line's own total, never from a sum of absolute values.

**4. Cost of transport.** COT = E / (m·g·d), with E a line total (battery energy out for the electrical figure,
joint work for the mechanical one). Shares of the cells of the identity whose left side is E sum to COT, by the
partition law. d = 0 (the robot did not move, or returned to its start under a net-displacement metric) gives
`unavailable: zero distance` with E still printed; never infinity. An episode that did not complete still has a
COT over the distance it covered, and the figure carries the episode's outcome.

**5. Signed parts per actuator, and bus circulation.** BD-092 integrates the bus power P = Σⱼ vⱼ·iⱼ into
`bus_positive` and `bus_negative`. Added here, per actuator j and per integration interval of length h:
`draw_j += h·max(0, vⱼ·iⱼ)`, `regen_j += h·max(0, −vⱼ·iⱼ)`, and the derived
`circulation = Σⱼ regen_j − abs(bus_negative) ≥ 0`: energy that one joint returned and another consumed on the
same bus without reaching the battery. Reason: Σⱼ max(0, −pⱼ) and max(0, −Σⱼ pⱼ) differ whenever one joint brakes
while another motors, so without the third number the per-actuator table and the bus table appear to disagree.
The split is always made at the finest interval the ledger integrates and is never recomputed from a coarser
net. Consistency checked (not owned) here: the braking-mode lines that carry energy (regeneration accepted by
the battery, shunt dissipation) sum to `bus_negative` within the residual bound; clipped braking carries no
energy and is a duration.

**6. Channel kinds of energy quantities.** The signed parts, the circulation and every ledger accumulator are
*cumulative counters*: downsampled by taking the last value. Signed power is an *interval mean*: downsampled by
a duration-weighted mean. A positive or negative part is never derived from a downsampled mean. Smallest case:
P = +10 W for 0.5 s then −10 W for 0.5 s: draw 5 J, regeneration 5 J, net 0; the one-second mean is 0, and a
split computed from it reports 0 and 0.

**7. Per-task figures: the corner cases.** Over a set S of episodes at a pinned frontier, with C the completed
episodes, F the physical failures, and X = S ∖ (C ∪ F) the episodes whose outcome kind is numerical, budget or
protocol:

```
energy per completed episode  = (1/|C|) · Σ_{i∈C} Eᵢ
energy per completion         = (Σ_{i∈C∪F} Eᵢ) / |C|
```

- |C| = 0: both are `unavailable: no completion`; the total over attempts is printed. Never 0, never infinity.
- Episodes in X are in neither numerator. Their count and their energy are printed beside the figures
  (`excluded: { numerical, budget, protocol, energy }`). Reason: a numerical outcome leaves the verdict unknown,
  so its partial energy belongs to neither reading; hiding it would let a design that drives the solver to
  faults shed its expensive episodes. A figure that omits what it excluded is refused on the evidence path
  (`FROB-REF-865`, a summary statistic that omits what it excluded; exit 3). Rejected: counting a faulted
  episode's energy as an attempt (its energy is truncated at an arbitrary tick).
- Each figure prints its numerator and denominator ("30 J = 30 J over 1 completion, 2 attempts").
- Sums over episodes are integer sums of quantized values.

**8. The residual of an aggregate.** For a figure over many episodes, `residual_abs` is the sum of the absolute
per-episode residuals and `residual_share` is the largest per-episode share (residual over that episode's
W_abs), with the address of the episode that set it; a mean of shares would let one bad episode hide. A figure
whose share exceeds the tier's bound (10⁻⁹ on the referee, 10⁻³ on single-precision tiers, TARGET) is printed
and marked `residual-over-bound` with the first offending step and element; it is not suppressed.

**9. Layout.** Additions to the modules BD-092 creates (`energy/breakdown.rs`, `energy/per_task.rs`) plus
`energy/{phase.rs, signed.rs}`; tests `crates/frob-chronicle/tests/energy_edge_cases.rs`; fixture
`tests/fixtures/energy/ledger-quad-38.json` (`synthetic`). Determinism: accumulators as BD-092; derived figures
exact integers. Evidence: fixture results are Known answers of class Tested.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the synthetic fixture `ledger-quad-38` the by-limb-and-phase table reports copper loss in the knees
      during stance as 38 J of 100 J battery energy out, and every table sums to its line total in integers.
- [ ] On the referee, a term in the sub-interval before a located touchdown is booked to swing and the one after
      it to stance; a wheeled fixture prints stance only.
- [ ] The per-axis-accumulator mutant fails the partition test in single precision.
- [ ] The two-joint fixture (+8 W motoring, −3 W braking for 1 s) gives bus draw 5 J, bus regeneration 0 J,
      per-actuator draw 8 J and regeneration 3 J, circulation 3 J.
- [ ] The square-wave fixture gives 5 J, 5 J and net 0 at 1 kHz and after downsampling the counters to 1 Hz.
- [ ] Per-task figures: {10 J success, 20 J failure} gives 10 J and 30 J; with two added numerical faults of 7 J
      each the figures are unchanged and the exclusion block shows 2 episodes and 14 J; removing the block is
      refused with FROB-REF-865; a set with no completion prints `unavailable: no completion`.
- [ ] An aggregate's residual names its worst episode; a zero-distance episode prints COT `unavailable`.
- [ ] Cross-world tables are byte-identical under 100 permutations of worlds and 10 shard splits.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `quad38_known_table` (`synthetic`): battery out 100 J = copper 50 (knees stance 38, knees swing 4, hips stance
  6, hips swing 2) + actuator friction 6 + gear 9 + Δ stored 5 + joint work 30; joint work 30 = Δ kinetic 2 +
  Δ potential 8 + normal damping 4 + sliding friction 6 + joint damping 7 + fixtures 3 + residual 0. Both
  identities close; the 38% statement is produced.
- `cot_decomposition`: m = 10 kg, g = 9.81 m/s², d = 20 m → m·g·d = 1,962 J; COT = 100/1,962 = 0.050968; the
  knee-stance copper share is 38/1,962 = 0.019368; shares sum to COT.
- `phase_at_located_event`; `phase_by_contact_not_command` (a foot that slips off a step early);
  `wheeled_robot_stance_only`; `trunk_has_no_phase`.
- `partition_law_random`: 10³ seeded cell sets, every grouping sums to the line total.
- `negative_cells_gross_sums`: cells +5 J and −3 J → net 2, positive 5, negative −3.
- `bus_circulation_two_joints`: 5, 0, 8, 3, 3 as above. `bus_sign_split_square_wave`: 5, 5, 0.
- `split_not_recomputed_from_mean`: the mean-power channel type has no method that yields a part.
- `per_task_two_figures`: (10, 30) and (12, 12) joules, exact; the conditional mean ranks the first design
  better, the per-completion figure ranks the second better.
- `per_task_no_completion`; `per_task_exclusions_listed`; `per_task_exclusion_block_required_865`.
- `aggregate_residual_worst_episode`: shares 10⁻¹², 3·10⁻¹⁰, 10⁻¹¹ → reported 3·10⁻¹⁰ with that address.
- `zero_distance_cot_unavailable`.
- Property: per-task figures and all tables invariant under permutation of episodes and worlds.

## Considerations

- This bead overlaps BD-092 by design: BD-092 states the breakdowns and the three refinements; this bead holds
  the cases the statements leave open. If the two are merged, the content of items 1 to 8 goes into BD-092's
  technical approach unchanged.
- The exclusion rule of item 7 is a choice the plan does not spell out; it follows the rule that a numerical
  outcome is neither a physical failure nor dropped from a denominator. If the owner prefers that a faulted
  episode's energy count as an attempt, only the numerator of the second figure changes, and the exclusion block
  already prints what is needed to recompute it.
- Memory on batched tiers grows with cells × worlds. BD-208 may keep limb-and-phase cells for every world and
  per-joint cells only for traced worlds; the finer table is then `unavailable` for untraced worlds and is never
  estimated (a metric over missing samples is refused, FROB-REF-888).
- Heat held in a thermal node is stored energy; loss lines are listed for attribution and not added again
  (BD-092's rule; the tables here inherit it).
