## Background

A fast contact model can hand a policy energy that no actuator supplied: a penalty spring integrated near its
stability limit returns more than it absorbed; a position-level penetration correction that removes a depth d in
one step gives a body the velocity d/h from nothing (m = 1 kg, d = 1 mm, h = 2 ms: 0.5 m/s and 0.125 J per event).
A learning or search process finds such sources and builds a "better" gait on them. The energy ledger of the
record subsystem (crate `frob-chronicle`) makes this measurable, and three monitors watch for it in every world.
This bead is the first monitor, and the only one that owns a result label.

Decision ADR-CHRON-08: the numerical residual is its own ledger line, and created energy is audited. The
integrator's and the solver's energy error is never folded into a physical term, and energy that appears from
nowhere is a finding about the simulator, reported against the result that benefited.

Ownership rule that this bead implements: the flag `energy-created`, **with this window and this denominator, is
the one flag of the created-energy audit**. The dynamics subsystem applies it (it accumulates the per-step
quantities and attaches the consequences to results) and defines no other label or threshold.

This bead is the host reference evaluator: a pure function of the ledger's per-interval sums that runs on referee
runs at the first milestone. The monitors on the batched tiers, the verb `fidelity.energy` and the gate's energy
half arrive at the second milestone (seed BD-208 for the kernel; bead N29a-22 of unit U29a for the three
monitors); they use the rule fixed here, and this evaluator is the oracle their in-kernel form is checked
against. The reason it exists a milestone earlier: the certifier self-test of the verification subsystem needs it
at the first milestone (the stiff penalty bounce must raise `energy-created` on the referee as part of
GATE-CHRON-03's record), and the referee threshold has to be exercised before any fast tier exists.

Rejected alternatives: a whole-episode ratio (a burst of created energy is diluted by a long honest episode);
tumbling windows (a burst that straddles a boundary is halved, see the hand case below); a threshold on the signed
residual (dissipation errors and creation errors cancel).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs per integration interval k (from the ledger).**

```
r_k = ΔE_kinetic,k + ΔE_potential,k − ( W_actuators,k + W_external,k − D_k )
c_k = max(0, r_k)                                             created energy of the interval
a_k = Σ_ports |W_port,k| + D_k                                absolute work exchanged (the ledger's W_abs, per interval)
```

ΔE_potential includes gravity and every modelled elastic store, including energy held in contact compliance (for
a linear compliant contact with penetration d and stiffness k, ½·k·d², which can return at most that): storage is
not creation, in the same way that heat held in a modelled thermal mass is stored energy. W_external includes work
by prescribed surfaces and external forces. D_k ≥ 0 is all modelled *constitutive* dissipation of the interval
(contact normal damping, sliding friction, joint damping, stiction and backlash rows). The stage-level
"algorithmic exchange" of an impulse step (N29b-22) is not in D and does not enter r: its two sides cancel inside
every step.

**2. Per-control-interval sums.** For control interval j (the run's control period T_c): C_j = Σ_{k∈j} c_k and
A_j = Σ_{k∈j} a_k, double-float. These two sequences, per world, are all the evaluator reads; they are part of the
energetics stream and are cumulative-counter channels (downsampled by last value, never averaged).

**3. The window.** n_w = ⌈0.5 s / T_c⌉ control intervals (25 at 50 Hz). Windows are half-open in time, (a, b],
and slide by one control interval. For every start index s with s + n_w ≤ n: C(s) = Σ_{j=s}^{s+n_w−1} C_j and
A(s) likewise, each recomputed as a fixed-order sum over the window (no running add-and-subtract, which drifts).
An episode shorter than 0.5 s has one window, the whole episode. Windows do not cross an episode reset.

**4. The rule.**

```
flag energy-created  iff  ∃ s :  C(s) > θ · A(s)        when A(s) ≥ n_w quanta
                                 C(s) > E_floor         when A(s) < n_w quanta   (almost nothing was exchanged)
θ = 0.01   on a fast tier  (the lockstep profile on batch-cpu, gpu-cuda, gpu-metal)          TARGET
θ = 10⁻⁶   on the referee and on the strict profile (the transactional profiles)             TARGET
E_floor = the tier's absolute residual bound: 10⁻⁹ J in f64, 10⁻⁶ J in f32   (DESIGN)
```

The comparison is made on the ledger's integer quanta (1 nJ): `100·C > A` on a fast tier and `10⁶·C > A` on the
referee, so the flag is a pure integer predicate and is identical under any world order. The small-denominator
branch exists so that a body at rest with nothing exchanged is neither flagged by rounding nor exempt from real
creation.

"10⁻⁶ relative on the referee" is read as the same window and the same denominator with θ = 10⁻⁶ (DESIGN reading;
the plan states the number and the word "relative" and nothing else). Both thresholds are re-baselined once, at
the second milestone, together with the frozen fidelity envelopes; after that a change is a dated decision record.

**5. The record.** Per (world, episode): `flagged: bool`; `created_total_J`; `worst_window { start_tick,
created_J, exchanged_J, ratio }` (the window with the largest ratio, earliest on ties); `first_window_tick` (start
of the first window over the threshold); `created_by_mode[]` when the step supplies the tag. The flag is a bit, so
its run-level aggregate is a bitwise or and a count, both exact integers (ADR-CHRON-03). The first crossing is
written as a level-T1 event.

**6. What the flag does.** A flagged episode **keeps its result and gains the flag**: no metric is altered, no
episode is dropped, and the flag joins the episode's record. Consequences are applied downstream and stated here
so the record can be read alone: a result with a flagged input carries the condition `energy-created` in its claim
product and cannot be labelled `established`; a ranking that contains one is `model-conditional`; a design or
policy whose advantage in a comparison coincides with flagged episodes is typed `model-conditional` by the
rank-robustness analysis A-19; the exploitation detectors of the learning subsystem read the same flag.

**7. No switch.** The monitor cannot be disabled by a run option. The only build without it is the registered
negative control "audit switched off", compiled under the `conformance` feature.

**8. Layout.** `crates/frob-chronicle/src/energy/audit/created.rs` (the same module the second-milestone monitor
bead extends with the in-kernel counterpart's host side); fixtures under
`fixtures/chronicle/energy-audit/`; determinism class of the evaluator: a deterministic function of its two input
sequences (D2 with them). Evidence: the flag is a finding with claim Known answer, class Tested, on fixtures.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The window arithmetic passes its four hand cases (unit tests below) in `f64` and `f32`.
- [ ] On the referee, the stiff penalty bounce fixture raises `energy-created`; the same drop under the convex
      compliant law at the default step does not (known answer KA-709, first half).
- [ ] On the referee, the resting block, the driven sliding block and the plastic drop of N29b-23 raise no flag at
      32, 64 and 2,000 Hz.
- [ ] A flagged episode's metrics are byte-identical to the same episode evaluated by the mutant with the monitor
      removed; only the flag record differs.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: a tumbling-window evaluator; a signed-residual evaluator; a whole-episode ratio;
      contact elastic energy counted as creation.
- [ ] The evaluator is a pure function: the same two sequences give the same record on every platform, and a
      property test over 10⁴ random sequences agrees with a brute-force oracle.
- [ ] The certifier self-test row "energy audit: stiff penalty bounce; a sign error at one port → `energy-created`
      raised; the error localized to its port" is recorded with GATE-CHRON-03's referee record (BD-092 owns the
      gate; this bead supplies the first half of the row).

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`crates/frob-chronicle/tests/energy_created.rs`)

- `window_burst_over_threshold`: T_c = 0.02 s, n_w = 25, A_j = 1 J for all j, C_10 = 0.3 J, others 0 → a window
  holds 0.3/25 = 0.012 > 0.01 → flagged; worst ratio 0.012.
- `window_burst_under_threshold`: C_10 = 0.2 J → 0.008 → not flagged.
- `sliding_not_tumbling`: C_24 = C_25 = 0.15 J → windows [0, 24] and [25, 49] each hold 0.006; the sliding window
  [1, 25] holds 0.3/25 = 0.012 → flagged. A tumbling evaluator returns not flagged and must fail this test.
- `signed_residual_cancels`: r alternating +0.03 J and −0.03 J per interval with A_j = 1 J → Σ r = 0 over any even
  span, yet every 25-interval window holds 12 or 13 positive intervals, C(s) = 0.36 J or 0.39 J, ratio 0.0144 or
  0.0156 → flagged. An evaluator that sums the signed residual returns about 0 and must fail this test.
- `referee_threshold`: C(s)/A(s) = 2·10⁻⁶ flagged under θ = 10⁻⁶ and not under 0.01.
- `small_denominator`: A(s) = 0 with C(s) = 10⁻³ J → flagged; C(s) = 0 → not flagged; A(s) of 10 quanta with
  C(s) of 5 quanta → not flagged (below the floor); no division anywhere.
- `integer_predicate`: C = 1,000,001 nJ, A = 100,000,000 nJ → 100·C exceeds A → flagged; C = 1,000,000 nJ → not
  flagged (equality is not excess).
- `short_episode_single_window`; `no_window_across_reset`.
- `compliance_is_storage`: a contact compressing by d = 1 mm at k = 10⁵ N/m holds 0.05 J in ΔE_potential; r = 0.
- `worst_window_earliest_on_tie`.
- Property `matches_bruteforce`: 10⁴ seeded sequences of length up to 500 against an O(n·n_w) oracle.

### End-to-end script and logging

Step group `audit-referee-*` added to `scripts/e2e/energy_ledger_referee.sh` (the first-milestone script of seed
BD-092): `frob sim run --tier referee` on the penalty-bounce fixture and on the convex-law drop, then
`frob telemetry energy <run> --json`. Asserts the flag on the first and its absence on the second, the worst
window fields, and that both episodes report their apex heights unchanged by the audit. NDJSON lines: command, exit
code, wall time, evidence block (with the condition `energy-created` on the flagged run), `created_J`,
`exchanged_J`, `ratio` against the threshold in force with its unit, the first-crossing tick; on failure the
window that decided and the run identifier.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-709: the stiff penalty bounce raises `energy-created` (and `contact-active`, N29b-03); the convex law at the
  default step raises neither. Tolerance: exact (flags). Fixture (the one bead N29a-22 pins, so that both
  milestones use the same input): a 1 kg body dropped from 0.2 m on a stiff penalty contact of k = 10⁶ N/m with
  no damping, stepped at h = 2 ms, where h·√(k/m) = 2 is the explicit stability limit. Hand check of the
  mechanism: a step that starts with penetration d = v·h at approach speed v ends with speed
  −v + (h·√(k/m))²·v = 3·v upward (gravity's g·h neglected), so the body leaves faster than it arrived and its
  apex height grows.
- KA-354 (owned by the dynamics chapter, cited): the position-level correction gains 0.125 J per event at m = 1 kg,
  d = 1 mm, h = 2 ms and raises this flag within one episode.
- Negative control named by the plan: the audit switched off, in which the bouncing policy wins the comparison
  (exercised end to end in N29b-06).
- Further negative controls: the three wrong evaluators above; a sign error seeded at one port, which must move r
  and be localized to that port (KA-707's control, with BD-092).
- Metamorphic: scaling every energy of an episode by a positive constant leaves the flag unchanged when A(s) > 0;
  appending honest intervals never clears a flag.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; across platforms on the pinned sequences.

## Considerations

- Failure story 5 of the risk chapter's premortem (policies exploited contact compliance and promotions kept
  failing): its earliest signals are the promotion failure rate, the count of `energy-created` flags and the share
  of finalists typed `model-conditional`; this monitor is where the second is produced.
- The 1% threshold is a TARGET fixed before measurement; an honest fast tier must sit well under it, and the
  re-baseline at the second milestone records the measured honest distribution beside the threshold.
- The window is in simulated time. A run at a 100 Hz control rate has n_w = 50; a control period that does not
  divide 0.5 s rounds the window up, never down.
- Performance: the host evaluator is O(n·n_w) per world on traced data; the in-kernel form keeps a ring of n_w
  pairs per world (BD-208) and must agree with this evaluator on every traced world.
- Revisit if honest fixtures flag on single precision: first check that compliance storage and prescribed-surface
  work are in the residual, then re-baseline once; a second change needs a decision record.
