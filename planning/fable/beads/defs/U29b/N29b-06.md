## Background

GATE-CHRON-08 is a gate of the record subsystem (crate `frob-chronicle`), claim kind Known answer, decided at the
second milestone. Its pass criterion (TARGET, governed by the gate register): **the created-energy audit flags
every planted trampolining and cone-excess exploit and no honest fixture. Each failure class's monitor fires on its
planted fixture, and a numerical fault is never counted as a physical failure or dropped from a denominator.**

The gate has two halves with different mechanisms, written by three drafting units, and it needs one record:

| Half | Tier and milestone | Mechanism | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy audit | Batched tiers, second milestone | The three monitors and `fidelity.energy` (bead N29a-22); the kernel (seed BD-208); the contact oracles (N29b-03) | `scripts/e2e/energy_audit.sh` of bead N29a-22 |
| Failure taxonomy | Referee, first milestone | Classes, kinds, first cause, `telemetry.failures` (seed BD-094) | `scripts/e2e/telemetry_verbs.sh` of bead N24a-39, whose receipt is the referee rows this bead's record cites |
| Failure taxonomy | Batched tiers, second milestone | First-anomaly latches, ring flush, kind counts (N29b-05) | **this bead** |
| Consequence of a flag in a comparison | Second milestone | Flags on the episode record and the flag join (N29b-04) | **this bead** |

So this bead is the end-to-end script for what the other scripts leave out, and the place where the gate's row is
assembled: it runs the energy-half script, the referee script and the batched failure script as stages, runs the
one control that only shows up at the level of a comparison (with the audit switched off, a policy that bounces
on a stiff penalty contact wins), and ends with the gate's tier run, in which every registered control of the
gate must be caught and whose one receipt this bead records as the gate's one part (the script itself records
nothing).

Two rules of the verification subsystem bind it. A gate that nothing can fail validates nothing: a run in which a
registered control was not caught is a FAIL whatever the passing assertions say. And no self-certification: the
PASS is recorded by a fresh-context session that did not implement the monitors.

## Scope

In: `scripts/e2e/gate_chron_08.sh`; its own fixture, the comparison pair `fixtures/e2e/chron08/bounce-pair.json`,
and the gate's fixture list `fixtures/e2e/chron08/list.toml` (which names the batches of N29b-05); step groups
`energy-half`, `failures-referee`, `failures-batched`, `audit-join-*`, `controls-*`, `receipt`; the entries this
bead registers against the gate in the `g1_known_answers` tier table (item 8); the structured log; recording the
gate's M2 part in `registries/gates.toml` from the tier receipt; naming the recorder of each later wave of the
fixture list (item 10).

Out: the energy-half fixtures and assertions (bead N29a-22's script); the failure batches and their script
(N29b-05's `failures_batched.sh`); monitors, latches and plants (their beads);
the referee rows themselves (N24a-39's receipt, which this bead's `referee-rows` entry verifies and cites); the
later waves' fixtures and their recordings (the beads named in item 10); GATE-FID-06's battery measurement (bead
N16b-33 of unit U16b).

## Technical approach

**1. Tiers.** `batch-cpu` in `f64` and `f32`, `gpu-cuda`, `gpu-metal`. A device absent on the host is logged
`not-run` with the reason; the gate is PASS only when every tier admitted at the milestone was measured.

**2. `energy-half` and `failures-referee` (two scripts as stages).** `energy-half` runs
`scripts/e2e/energy_audit.sh` with the same seed and tier list, reads its summary line (planted exploits flagged
of planted; honest fixtures flagged of honest; controls caught) and fails if that script failed. No fixture is
duplicated here. `failures-referee` runs `scripts/e2e/telemetry_verbs.sh` (bead N24a-39; it runs BD-094's
`telemetry_failures.sh` as a stage) with the same seed, fails if that script failed, and reads from its log the
hash and the path of the referee receipt that script writes: the referee rows of the failure-taxonomy half for
the thirteen classes of the second milestone (per class the fixture identity, the expected and the measured
first-anomaly tick and `pass`), the two accounting assertions, the commit and the seeds. Running it here, and not
reading an older log, makes the referee rows a measurement at the commit the gate is recorded at.

**3. `failures-batched` (N29b-05's script as a stage).** Runs `scripts/e2e/failures_batched.sh` (bead N29b-05,
which owns the registered fixtures `chron08/<name>` below) with the same seed and tier list, reads its summary
line and fails if that script failed; no fixture is duplicated here. What the stage asserts:

| Fixture | Content | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| One batch per failure class available at the second milestone | 64 worlds, 8 addresses carrying that class's planted failure (the first-milestone fixture of the class, batched) | That class's first-anomaly latch fires on 8 of 8 planted and 0 of 56 unplanted worlds; other classes 0 unless the fixture declares them |
| `fault-batch` | 64 worlds, 4 with planted solver exhaustion | Kind `numerical` 4 of 64; the physical-failure count excludes them; every denominator is 64; their rings were flushed |
| `untraced-failure` | A batch in which the planted failing worlds are outside the traced sample | First cause still reported for them (from latches), labelled `Estimated` |

Per fixture and lane (`cpu64`, `cpu32`, `cuda`, `metal`) that script runs `frob sim run --tier <t> [--precision
<p>] --worlds 64 --fixture chron08/<f> --seed <s> --key fb-<f>-<l> --json` (its determinism repeats with their
own keys), then `frob telemetry failures <run> --json`.
The summary's per-class counts, kind split and denominators are copied into this script's `metric` lines.

**4. `audit-join-*` (the comparison-level control).** A fixed-n paired study on `batch-cpu`: arm A is the policy
that bounces on the stiff penalty contact (the trampolining fixture of the energy half, as a hop-height task),
arm B an honest policy under the convex law. With the production build: the comparison's envelope carries the
condition `energy-created`, its label is not `established`, and the flag join (read through the comparison's
claim, below) shows arm A flagged on the addresses where it wins. With the conformance build
"audit switched off": arm A is reported the clean winner, and the assertion on the condition fails, which is the
required outcome for that control. Commands (the M2 study verbs; `eval.compare` arrives at M3): `frob study plan
fixtures/e2e/chron08/bounce-pair.json --json`, then `frob study submit <plan> --key c08-join --json`, whose
completed study record carries the envelope with its conditions, its claim identifier and `audit_join_ref`, then
`frob evidence trace <clm:…> --json` on that claim (`evidence.trace` takes a claim identifier, never an evidence
identifier: the closure lists the join as the evidence item of kind `analysis result` that `audit_join_ref`
names, with its payload hash) and `frob artifact read b3:<payload hash> --json` for the join's rows; the
production submission is repeated with `--key c08-join-r2` (the determinism lane:
the same condition set and flag join); in the conformance build the same with `--key c08-join-audit-off`, so that
the runs a test compares never share a receipt.

**5. `controls-*`.** The controls of the failure and comparison halves, each run and each required to be caught
(the first by this script, the other four by N29b-05's script in the `failures-batched` stage, whose summary
reports them):

| Control | Defect | Caught by |
|---|---|---|
| audit-off | Monitors return clear flags (conformance build) | The comparison's envelope lacks the condition; arm A is the clean winner |
| fault-as-fall | A faulted world is reported as `fall` | The kind split on `fault-batch` |
| fault-filtered | Faulted worlds are removed before counting | The denominator on `fault-batch` |
| latch-last-writer | A latch updated by store instead of integer minimum | Latches differ under a permuted batch |
| flush-physical-only | The ring is flushed for physical failures only | The faulted worlds of `fault-batch` have no trace |

The script then reads the control registry and asserts, on the receipt of the tier run of item 8, that *every*
control registered against GATE-CHRON-08 (including those owned by the energy-half script: the tumbling window,
the folded residual, per-step passivity, the utilization threshold of 1.0, and N16b-15's three solver mutations
registered against this gate, M1 `penetration-recovery`, M2 `pyramid4` and M3 `explicit-penalty`) has the
outcome `caught` there: the tier runner runs every registered control of a gate in the run that measures it.

**6. Pass rule.** PASS iff the energy half passed; the referee receipt verifies (thirteen class rows and both
accounting assertions); every failure class fires 8 of 8 and 0 of 56 on every measured
tier; `fault-batch` satisfies its three identities; the comparison carries its condition; and every registered
control was caught.

**7. Logging.** The script sources the shared helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/gate_chron_08/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead` ("N29b-06"), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command
line, exit code, wall time, and the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities,
seed, conditions). `metric` lines carry each measured value with unit and threshold: per-class counts against
8 of 64; kind counts; denominators; latch-against-host mismatches (threshold 0); the energy half's three counts
as read from its summary; the comparison's condition set and label; controls caught of controls registered.
Where state is compared the line carries digests. On failure: the first differing value, the fixture, the world
address and class, and the artifact paths needed to reproduce (the run identifier and the `.frun` export). A
final `summary` line gives assertions passed and failed, tiers measured, and `gates: ["GATE-CHRON-08"]`. All
fixtures are synthetic or from the public battery; no private-scene data can be logged.

**8. `receipt`, then the recording.** The script writes no gate record (one recorder per gate, N38b-19; a log
line that runs the recorder fails the harness lint `self-recording`). Its last step group runs the gate's tier
run through the shared helper, `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-CHRON-08 --mutations`, that is
`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations --
GATE-CHRON-08`, with `FROB_CHRON08_REFEREE` exported as the path of the referee receipt that stage
`failures-referee` produced. The tier runner (N38a-02) selects every entry registered against the gate and due at
the milestone, on every site present, and runs every registered control of the gate in the same run. The entries
have three origins:
- the energy half: the known answers KA-709 and KA-710 and the honest fixtures, entries of the beads that own
  them (N29a-22, N29b-02, N29b-03);
- the batched failure half, registered here (DESIGN: one entry per row of `list.toml` and site, generated from
  the list, so that a later wave adds a row and no code): each batch of N29b-05 on `lockstep` (`batch-cpu`, one
  entry per precision), `cuda` and `metal`, with `fault-batch`, `untraced-failure` and the comparison pair; they
  call the latches of N29b-05 and the flag join of N29b-04 in process and assert what items 3 and 4 assert;
- the referee rows, registered here as the entry `chron08:referee-rows@host` (DESIGN: a tier receipt has no
  field for another receipt, so the citation is an entry whose measurement is the verification). It loads the
  file `FROB_CHRON08_REFEREE` names, recomputes its BLAKE3 hash against its name, requires its commit to be an
  ancestor of `HEAD` and each of the thirteen class rows and both accounting assertions to be `pass`, and reports
  as measured values the receipt's hash, "13 of 13" and "2 of 2". An unset variable, or a file that is missing,
  altered, from a foreign commit or holding a failing row, fails the entry with the cause named.

The receipt's GATE-CHRON-08 line is `pass` only when every entry passed and every registered control is `caught`
(the test kit's rule; a device site that is absent is counted as skipped, never as passed). The script asserts
that line and logs the receipt hash. Then, as this bead's recording step and from a session that did not
implement the monitors: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-CHRON-08 --receipt
<hash> --part M2`, which writes `status = "pass"`, `measured = "exploits flagged <a> of <a>; honest fixtures
flagged 0 of <b>; failure classes <c> of <c> on <sites> (referee rows from <referee receipt hash>); numerical
kept apart 4 of 4; controls caught <k> of <k>"`, the control identifiers, the claim identifier, the evidence and
`recorded = { session, implemented_by }` with the two different. The part rests on that one receipt, whose
entries hold the energy half, the batched failure half and the verified referee receipt, so one trace reaches
all three.

**9. Idempotence and budget.** Seed-explicit; re-running against the same store returns stored answers by
idempotency key. Batches of 64 worlds keep the script inside the machine envelope (8 cores, 16 GiB).

**10. Later waves of the fixture list, and who records them (DESIGN).** The ten classes of the second group that
the second milestone lacks extend `list.toml` when their monitors land, each with its planted fixture and clean
control. The register keeps the gate's one part (the plan decides GATE-CHRON-08 at M2 only), and a wave is entered
as a re-recording of that part from the receipt of a tier run of the *whole* list as it then stands (the wave's
bead runs this script again as a stage of its own: it reads the list, runs its three stage scripts and the same
`g1_known_answers -- GATE-CHRON-08` run with the referee receipt exported, so every earlier class is proved again
and a regression fails the part), with `record_gate -- --gate GATE-CHRON-08 --receipt <hash> --part M2` from a
session that did not
implement that wave's monitor. One recorder per wave, each the bead that switches its classes to `available`:

| Wave | Classes | Milestone | Recorder |
|---|---|---|---|
| M2 | the eleven mechanical classes, `thermal-runaway`, `brownout` | M2 | this bead |
| 1 | `film-slip`, `perception-dropout`, `localization-alias`, `observation-alias`, `timing-race`, `pinch`, `snag` | M3 | N24a-38 |
| 2 | `gait-instability` | M4 | N22ap2-03 |
| 3 | `model-drift` | M5 | BD-394 |
| 4 | `slosh-tip` | M6 | N19b-13 |

Each wave's `measured` names the classes it covers and its receipt; the earlier recordings stay readable in the
lines the recorder appends at every recording (the control history and the recording bead's log) and in the
registry's commits. Rejected: a part per wave, which would add milestone parts to a gate the register lists at M2 alone
and leave a part `not-run` for good whenever a class's feature is killed; and re-recording by this bead, which
is closed at M2. A wave whose bead does not land leaves the list, and the part, as the last wave left them.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The script's testable part is its assertion library, `crates/frob-conformance/tests/chron08_asserts.rs`:
`assert_k_of_n`, `assert_kind_split`, `assert_condition_present` and `assert_control_caught` against canned
envelopes, including the inverted case (a control that passes must make `assert_control_caught` fail) and a
canned energy-half summary with one honest fixture flagged (must fail). The check of the entry
`chron08:referee-rows@host`, in the same file on referee receipts written by the test (real files, BLAKE3
recomputed): `referee_rows_pass` (thirteen passing rows and two passing assertions → the entry passes and
reports the hash, "13 of 13" and "2 of 2"); `referee_rows_unset` and `referee_rows_missing` (no variable; no
file → fail, the cause named); `referee_rows_altered` (one byte changed → fail, hash mismatch);
`referee_rows_failing` (one class row `fail` → fail, the class named); `referee_rows_foreign_commit` (a commit
that is not an ancestor of `HEAD` → fail). The list: `list_wave_field` (`list.toml` parses, thirteen classes at
wave M2, and a row without `wave` is rejected); `entries_from_list` (a row added to a scratch copy of the list
yields one more entry per site with no change of code).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. `--list` prints the fixture, tier and control matrix without running, so that the exit
review can compare it with the gate text; the second-milestone exit review re-runs it from a clean checkout.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Gate text of GATE-CHRON-08, both sentences.
- KA-709's negative control (the audit switched off: the bouncing policy wins the comparison), exercised here at
  the level of a comparison; KA-709 and KA-710 themselves are asserted by the energy-half script.
- The five controls of the table; three are named by the plan (audit switched off; a numerical fault counted as a
  physical failure; a numerical fault dropped from a denominator).
- CX-150 as arithmetic for the fault batch: 2 successes in 2 valid trials with 98 unresolved has a complete-case
  rate of 100% and a true rate anywhere in [2%, 100%].
- Determinism lanes: each batched fixture is run twice and with two batch sizes (in N29b-05's stage, whose latch
  digests this script copies); latches, kind counts and flags are compared exactly; the comparison pair is
  submitted twice under distinct keys in the production build and gives the same condition set.

## Considerations

- Two other scripts exist around this gate: `scripts/e2e/energy_audit.sh` (the energy half of this gate, bead
  N29a-22, the only owner of that name) and `scripts/e2e/created_energy_gate.sh` (GATE-FID-06, bead N16b-33,
  renamed from `energy_audit.sh` to end a name collision; its log directory followed). This script calls the
  first and never the second; the two gates share fixtures but not records.
- The gate row is written through the gate recorder (N38b-19), which checks that every control registered
  against the gate was caught in the same run and that the recording session is not the implementing one.
- The ten later world and perception failure classes join the list when their features exist (third milestone and
  after), recorded by the beads of item 10's table; this bead's script reads `list.toml` and so runs them too once
  they are in it.
- The audit's thresholds are TARGETs re-baselined once at this milestone together with the fidelity envelopes; a
  re-baseline is written down before the gate is recorded, never after.
- Device rows need the hardware; a host with one device records it and marks the other `not-run`, and the exit
  review decides whether the milestone's admitted tier list was covered.
