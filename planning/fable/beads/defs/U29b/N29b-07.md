## Background

Promotion says whether a fast-tier result is faithful to the referee (the most trusted tier). It does not say what
the number would have been on the referee. The **tier-correction ledger** supplies that number from the pairs that
promotion already pays for, with an estimator that stays unbiased when the fast tier is wrong (construction C-25:
fidelity tiers are coupled estimators; the same world address run on two tiers is a coupled pair). The estimator
and its use inside `eval.promote` are seed bead BD-264. The record subsystem (crate `frob-chronicle`) owns the
ledger: the stores behind the three ledgers of disagreement (fidelity, rank agreement, tier correction) are
Chronicle's, and their rows are evidence that other subsystems cite by frontier.

This bead builds the store at the second milestone, one milestone before the estimator, for three reasons the
plan states. (1) Paired data already exists then: the fidelity ledger's battery pairs are paid for by its own
gate, and the per-episode correlation ρ_ep must be measured on 200 paired addresses before the correction is
funded (the pilot). (2) The referee does not reach every scenario, so the ledger must record, per stratum, the
share of battery scenarios the referee completes and the wall-clock of a promotion, and both figures are reported
at the second-milestone exit. FACT (re-read at the pinned commit before a gate cites it): the sibling simulation
library's articulated contact step admits at most 21 contacts and its event solve has no persistent time stepper
(`frankensim/crates/fs-mbd/src/articulated_contacts/step.rs`; `fs-contact/src/sustained/solve.rs`,
`MAX_CONTACTS = 21`). (3) The kill gate KILL-DR is decided on ρ_ep, and its fallback says "the ledger keeps
recording ρ_ep": the recording must not depend on the estimator being switched on.

The ceiling, stated before any measurement: the saving in referee episodes from pairing is 1/(1 − ρ_ep²), that is
1.96 at ρ_ep = 0.7, 5.3 at 0.9, 10 at 0.95 and 25 at 0.98 (arithmetic). Paired worlds decouple under contact, and
published cross-fidelity correlation becomes neutral at large dynamics gaps (FACT, source register), so the plan
expects about two until a higher ρ_ep is measured. The correction is adopted because it is nearly free, not
because it is a large factor.

Design decision (DESIGN): rows are derived from **exact integer moments** of quantized outcomes. Rejected: running
floating-point means and variances (their bits depend on the order pairs arrive, which violates the rule that an
evidence aggregate has the same bits under any permutation, shard split and merge order, INV-CHRON-06); a sketch.

## Scope

In: the stratum key and its identifier; the moment table and its accumulation; the derived row with every measure
the plan lists; pair sources and de-duplication by address; referee-reach measures and the promotion wall-clock
record; the frontier and estimator version on every row; read API for the estimator and for reports; the
"keep recording when the correction is off" rule; tests.

Out: the estimator, its confidence sequence, budget and switch-off rule (BD-264); audit-round records and the
ordering invariant (N29b-08); the decision of the kill gate (bead N16b-10 of unit U16b); the multilevel and

## Technical approach

**1. Stratum key.** `(model class, scene class, task, quantity, tier pair, kernel versions, effort profile,
battery version)`, serialized as canonical JSON and hashed: `StratumId = BLAKE3("tier-correction/1" ‖ canonical
key)`. A pair may be added to a stratum only if all eight components match; pairs from two model, kernel or
battery identities never share a row (the read-side refusal is FROB-REF-811, raised in N29b-08). The second
component of the tier pair is the pair's recorded adjudicator: where the fidelity ledger adjudicates a scenario
the referee refuses with the `strict` profile of `batch-cpu` (BD-212 records `adjudicator = batch-cpu:strict` on
such a pair), the pair belongs to the stratum of that tier pair and never pools with referee-adjudicated pairs; a
pair with no adjudicated outcome (a numerical fault on the slow side) is not a pair and is counted as unpaired by
cause.

**2. Pair record.** `{ stratum, address, y_fast_q, y_ref_q, source, round }` where `address` is the world address
(the same scene draw, disturbances, randomization and policy on both tiers), `y_*_q` are the two outcomes
quantized to the quantity's declared quantum as `i64` (round half even; a binary outcome is 0 or 1), `source ∈
{battery, promotion, background}`, and `round` is the audit round for promotion pairs. Insert is idempotent on
(stratum, address, source-identity): the same pair inserted twice is one row; a second insert with different
values is a determinism conflict and is refused.

**3. Moments (exact).** Per stratum, as `i128` sums over its pairs: n, Σf, Σr, Σf², Σr², Σf·r (f = y_fast_q,
r = y_ref_q). Integer addition is commutative and associative, so the six numbers are independent of insertion
order. They are updated in the same ledger transaction that inserts the pair.

**4. Derived row (schema `frob.tier-correction.row/1`), computed from the moments in `f64` in one fixed
expression order.**

```
mean_f = Σf/n          var_f = (Σf² − (Σf)²/n)/(n − 1)          likewise mean_r, var_r
cov    = (Σf·r − Σf·Σr/n)/(n − 1)
mean_d = mean_r − mean_f        var_d = var_r + var_f − 2·cov          (d = referee − fast)
ρ_ep   = cov / √(var_f · var_r)                                        (for binary outcomes this is the φ coefficient)
β      = cov / var_f            c = mean_r − β·mean_f                  (the affine prediction m = c + β·Y_fast)
saving_ceiling = 1/(1 − ρ_ep²)
```

Stored with: `n_pairs`; the audit probabilities used (their distinct values and counts); the effective sample
size reported by the last promotion; `referee_complete_share`; `estimator_version`; the ledger frontier (the
commit counter at which the row was computed); and, for ρ_ep, a 95% interval (Fisher z, labelled Estimated). A
stratum with n < 2 or var_f = 0 has ρ_ep and β `unavailable`, and the prediction map reverts to the identity
(β = 1, c = 0), which is also the rule for a new stratum.

**5. Referee reach.** Per stratum: `referee_attempted`, `referee_completed`, and the counts of each capacity or
numerical refusal that stopped the rest (for example the referee's contact-capacity refusal), giving
`referee_complete_share = completed / attempted` printed as "k of n". A promotion in a stratum the referee cannot
complete returns `provisional: referee-unavailable`; the store exposes the predicate
`referee_available(stratum)` (share above zero and at least one completed pair).

**6. Promotion wall-clock.** Each promotion writes `{ stratum, referee_episodes, wall_s, cpu_core_s }`. Before
promotions exist (this milestone), a *dry promotion* is recorded per stratum of the standard battery: the referee
half of an audit set of 64 addresses (DESIGN size, the 95th-percentile target of referee episodes per established
verdict), and the row stores the measured seconds and the projection to 16 and 256 episodes, labelled Estimated.
How it is taken (DESIGN): from referee episodes the battery measurement has already run, with no extra episode.
When the referee half of a battery run commits its pairs, the store takes the stratum's first 64
referee-completed addresses in address order (a stratum with fewer uses all it has and stores the count), sums
the referee wall-clock recorded for each (BD-213's coverage campaign, `eval.run … --record coverage`, records
wall-clock per scenario and per tier; a referee world runs on one core) as `cpu_core_s`, divides that sum by
the cores the run used for `wall_s`, and projects both in proportion to the count. A stratum whose referee
episodes were run without that record has `promotion_wall_clock: unavailable` with the next action naming the
campaign; a figure is never estimated from another stratum. Rejected: a separately timed run of 64 referee
episodes, which would need an entry point that no verb has before `eval.promote` and would repeat episodes the
battery has paid for. Both reach figures are printed in the second-milestone exit report.

**7. Reads.** `row(stratum, frontier)` returns the row as of a pinned frontier, so a verdict can cite exactly what
it read; `prediction_map(stratum, frontier)` returns (c, β, hash); `strata_below(ρ, n_min)` lists strata whose
ρ_ep is under a threshold on at least n_min pairs (the kill gate's question with ρ = 0.7 and n_min = 200). No new
verb: each derived row is written to the artifact graph (BD-095) as an evidence item of kind `ledger row` under a
claim of class Tested, and rows surface through promotion receipts, through the receipt of a battery run whose
pairs update a row, and through `frob evidence trace <clm:…>` on such a claim (`evidence.trace` takes a claim
identifier, never an evidence identifier). The run's receipt carries the identifiers in the `fidelity` block of
`eval.run`'s output, as `tier_correction: [{ stratum, claim }]`, one element per row its pairs updated, empty
when no row is updated (a member of the block that N16bp2-01 owns and VERBS-eval lists, filled by this store's
import, which returns it to the verb's handler). The row's bytes are the payload of the claim's evidence item and are read with
`frob artifact read b3:<payload hash>`.

**8. Recording is unconditional.** Battery and background pairs update the moments whether or not the correction
is enabled for the stratum or globally. After a KILL-DR decision of "killed", promotion reports the label only,
from referee episodes alone, and this store keeps recording ρ_ep.

**9. Layout.** `crates/frob-chronicle/src/ledgers/tier_correction/{key.rs, pairs.rs, moments.rs, row.rs,
reach.rs}`; tables `tier_correction` (rows), `tier_pair` (pairs), `tier_moment` (moments). Determinism: moments
are exact (class D0); derived figures are a deterministic function of them. Evidence class of a row: Tested.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Rows computed from pairs inserted in 100 random orders, and through 10 different shard splits and merges,
      are byte-identical.
- [ ] The hand fixture below reproduces mean, variance, covariance, ρ_ep, β and c to 12 significant digits, and
      `saving_ceiling` prints 1.961, 5.263, 10.26 and 25.25 for ρ_ep = 0.7, 0.9, 0.95 and 0.98.
- [ ] A pair whose stratum key differs in any component cannot enter a row; a duplicate pair is one row; a
      conflicting duplicate is refused.
- [ ] `referee_complete_share` and the dry-promotion wall-clock are recorded for every stratum of the standard
      battery and appear in the second-milestone exit report with their denominators.
- [ ] With the correction switched off, inserting 200 battery pairs still updates ρ_ep.
- [ ] ρ_ep per stratum on at least 200 paired addresses is available to the KILL-DR decision by a single read at
      a pinned frontier.
- [ ] A new stratum returns the identity prediction map.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`crates/frob-chronicle/tests/tier_correction_store.rs`)

- `moments_hand_case`: pairs (f, r) = (1, 2), (2, 3), (3, 5), (4, 4): n = 4, Σf = 10, Σr = 14, Σf² = 30,
  Σr² = 54, Σfr = 39 → mean_f = 2.5, mean_r = 3.5, var_f = 5/3, var_r = 5/3, cov = 4/3, ρ_ep = 0.8, β = 0.8,
  c = 1.5, mean_d = 1.0, var_d = 2/3 (check: the differences are 1, 1, 2, 0), saving ceiling 1/(1 − 0.64) = 2.778.
- `order_independence`: 10³ seeded permutations of 500 pairs → identical moments and row bytes.
- `binary_phi`: 2×2 counts (both succeed 50, fast only 10, referee only 5, neither 35) → φ = (50·35 − 10·5) /
  √(60·40·55·45) = 1,700/2,437.2 = 0.6975.
- `ceiling_values`: 1/(1 − 0.49) = 1.9608; 1/(1 − 0.81) = 5.2632; 1/(1 − 0.9025) = 10.256; 1/(1 − 0.9604) = 25.25.
- `stratum_key_strict`: changing the battery version yields a different `StratumId`.
- `duplicate_pair_idempotent`; `conflicting_pair_refused`.
- `degenerate_stratum_identity_map`: n = 1, or all fast outcomes equal → ρ_ep `unavailable`, map (0, 1).
- `reach_counts`: 40 attempted, 31 completed, 9 capacity refusals → "31 of 40", share 0.775.
- `dry_promotion_from_recorded_episodes`: a stratum with 80 referee-completed addresses of 2.5 s recorded
  wall-clock each, run on 8 cores → the first 64 in address order: `cpu_core_s` 160, `wall_s` 20; projections
  40 and 5 s for 16 episodes, 640 and 80 s for 256. A stratum with 40 completed addresses stores the count 40,
  100 and 12.5 s, and the same two projections.
- `dry_promotion_unavailable_without_record`: referee episodes with no recorded wall-clock →
  `promotion_wall_clock: unavailable` naming the coverage campaign; no figure is copied from another stratum.
- `frontier_pinned_read`: a row read at frontier F is unchanged by pairs committed after F.
- `segment_source_rejected`: a pair tagged as a segment result cannot be constructed (no such `source`).
- `row_written_as_claim`: after the hand case's four pairs commit, the row's claim resolves in the artifact graph
  and its closure holds one evidence item of kind `ledger row` whose payload hash is the row's bytes.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/chronicle_tier_ledger.sh` (header `bead=N29b-07 milestone=M2 gates=KILL-DR seed=29`): runs the
quadruped's `smoke` battery (the per-change battery of the fidelity measurement, N24b-01) on both tiers, at the
same world addresses by construction, since a battery's items carry their own addresses (the battery form takes
neither a run seed nor a world count, VERBS-eval): `frob eval run --class quadruped --battery smoke --tier batch-cpu
--key ctl-cpu --json`, then `frob eval run --class quadruped --battery smoke --tier referee --adjudicate
strict-fallback --record coverage --key ctl-ref --json` (distinct keys, so that neither run
receives the other's stored receipt; the second is also BD-213's coverage campaign, whose per-scenario
wall-clock feeds the dry promotion of item 6). Pairs are written by the battery runs, not by a read: the pairing
hook of the fidelity ledger commits the pair rows as the second run completes (its `fidelity` block reports
`pairs_written`, N16bp2-01; BD-212), and this store imports them as source `battery`. The script then reads the
fidelity strata (`frob fidelity ledger --class quadruped --tier-pair batch-cpu,referee --battery smoke --json`:
the stratum keys and n per stratum of the battery the two runs used; without `--battery` the read would answer for
the class's frozen battery, whose strata the stratum key keeps apart), and this store's row of one stratum through the claim identifier that the referee run's
`fidelity.tier_correction` lists (`frob evidence trace <clm:…> --json`, then `frob artifact read b3:<payload
hash> --json` for the row's bytes); it then repeats the import with the pairs in reversed order into a scratch
store and asserts the row's bytes are equal.
Asserts the row's presence, n equal to the fidelity stratum's n, the share as "k of n", that ρ_ep is finite or
explicitly `unavailable`, and the dry-promotion figures with their episode count. NDJSON
lines per the shared helper: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, n_pairs, ρ_ep with its interval,
`saving_ceiling`, the reach share, wall seconds with unit, the frontier; a `summary` line naming KILL-DR as the
gate whose measurement was recorded.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The hand case and the φ case above; the four ceiling values (the plan prints them rounded as 1.96, 5.3, 10, 25).
- Negative controls: floating-point running moments (caught by `order_independence` in `f32`-sized quanta);
  pooling across kernel versions (caught by `stratum_key_strict`); a store that stops recording when the
  correction is off (caught by the unconditional-recording test).
- Metamorphic: an affine change of the fast outcome's unit (f → a·f + b, a > 0) leaves ρ_ep unchanged and maps
  (c, β) to (c − β·b/a, β/a).
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release.

## Considerations

- KILL-DR (decided at the third milestone; decision record in bead N16b-10 of unit U16b). Trigger: ρ_ep below 0.7
  on 200 paired addresses in every stratum tested, or GATE-CHRON-09 coverage fails after one repair cycle.
  Fallback: promotion reports the label only, from referee episodes alone; the ledger keeps recording ρ_ep. This
  bead supplies the first measurement and implements the last clause.
- Quantization: the quantum of a quantity bounds the accuracy of its moments; it is declared per quantity (the
  channel's quantum) and recorded in the row.
- `i128` head-room: 10⁹ pairs of 32-bit quantized outcomes give sums of squares below 2⁹⁴.
- The reach figures decide how often promotion can run at all; a stratum with share zero is a statement about the
  referee's capacity, not about the design, and reports must say `referee-unavailable` and name the adjudicating
  profile instead.
- The background sampler (tier Frontier; its mechanism belongs to the dynamics chapter, seed BD-214, with its own
  pilot): idle CPU cores re-run address-sampled fast-tier worlds on the referee from their snapshots and write
  rows into the fidelity and tier-correction ledgers. At the planned throughputs it covers on the order of 0.1 to
  2.4% of GPU world-seconds if the referee runs at 0.2 to 1.0 times real time per core on 24 cores (HYPOTHESIS;
  the speed is measured at the second milestone), and it reports the coverage it measured. It is a correctness
  guard and a free source of pilot variances, not a speed lever. For this store it is the pair source
  `background`, whose pairs carry their recorded inclusion probabilities.
- Segments are a different quantity (tier Frontier): restarting one-second segments on the referee from fast-tier
  snapshots shows where in an episode the tiers part; it estimates per-segment fidelity, not the episode-level
  outcome, and is never used for promotion. Falsifier: if segment disagreement does not predict episode-level
  exceedance better than chance on the standard battery, the mode is removed; the ceiling of its benefit is
  diagnostic time, not referee cost. Store rule that follows: a segment result can never be inserted as a pair of
  this ledger (the `source` enumeration has no segment member; segment rows live under their own quantity in the
  fidelity ledger).
- Privacy: pairs carry world addresses, not scene content; rows inherit the privacy class of their scenes through
  the stratum's scene class.
