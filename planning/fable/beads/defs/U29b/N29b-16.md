## Background

A proof obligation is a claim about a mechanism that the plan commits to discharge by a named method: a written
derivation, an exhaustive check, a statistical test with stated bounds, a simulation campaign, or a gate. The
record subsystem (crate `frob-chronicle`) has seven. They sit between invariants (properties tested on reachable
states) and gates (thresholds measured on a running system): each one says *why* a mechanism is entitled to the
property the invariants then check.

| ID | Obligation | How it is discharged |
|---|---|---|
| PO-CHRON-01 | The publishing transaction preserves INV-CHRON-01 to -03 under every fault of the simulation fault menu. | Deterministic simulation over 10⁵ schedules; every failing schedule minimized and kept. |
| PO-CHRON-02 | The tier-correction estimator is unbiased, and equals the plain mean when every address is audited. | A written derivation; exact equality at n = N; simulation with a deliberately wrong prediction. |
| PO-CHRON-03 | The k smallest address hashes are a uniform sample, and per-shard samples merge exactly. | Derivation; inclusion frequencies over 10⁶ draws inside exact binomial bounds; merge equality on random shard splits. |
| PO-CHRON-04 | A histogram bin is at most 2⁻ˢ of its lower edge wide, and merge is associative and commutative. | Exhaustive check over all 16-bit magnitudes; property test of merges. |
| PO-CHRON-05 | The first-divergence algorithm never reports a tick later than the true first divergence on exact classes. | Planted divergences at one tick and at two ticks, 100 trials each. |
| PO-CHRON-06 | The in-kernel digest distinguishes states that differ in any recorded bit, to the collision budget of a 128-bit mix. | Avalanche tests at the verification subsystem's law tier (G0); identical output on every backend for a pinned corpus. |
| PO-CHRON-07 | The energy ledger closes. | GATE-CHRON-03. |

The mechanisms belong to other beads. This bead owns the *discharges*: the three derivations as documents, the
tests that are specific to an obligation (the exhaustive bin check, the inclusion-frequency test, the avalanche
test, the planted-divergence trials), and one discharge record per obligation that names its evidence. Without
it, an obligation is a sentence that a mechanism bead may or may not have honoured.

## Technical approach

**PO-CHRON-01.** Record regenerated by the release lane of N29b-11: the fault menu (a crash at every await point
of the publish and submit paths; a torn ledger write; duplicate and reordered delivery; delay; lease expiry and
takeover by a second writer; device loss; a full disk; a clock jump; cancellation at every boundary; a client
that disconnects and reconnects by submission key), schedules run (TARGET 10⁵ per release), await-point coverage,
violations (expected 0) and the list of minimized regressions.

**PO-CHRON-02 (record created here, due M3; filled by N29b-08 and BD-264).** With Zᵢ = mᵢ + Aᵢ·(Y_ref,i − mᵢ)/pᵢ
and everything except Aᵢ fixed before the draw, E[Aᵢ/pᵢ] = 1, so E[Zᵢ] = E[Y_ref,i] for any mᵢ; with every
pᵢ = 1, Zᵢ = Y_ref,i. The record names three parts: the derivation `docs/proofs/chronicle/po-chron-02.md` and the
exact cases (full audit equals the plain mean; exact enumeration with a prediction off by a constant), both
N29b-08's; and the simulation, BD-264's, run on the real estimator: 10⁴ rounds with the prediction replaced by a
constant, by its negation and by noise leave the mean of θ̂ unchanged within three standard errors. Until both
land the row is `open` with `due = "M3"`.

**PO-CHRON-03 (derivation written here).** Setting: each world address w has
h(w) = Philox(key = run_seed, counter = (world, episode), stream_id = "telemetry-sample") as u64; fixed rate p
traces w iff h(w) < ⌊p·2⁶⁴⌋; fixed size k traces the k worlds with the smallest h(w), ties broken by address
order.
1. *Uniformity.* Model h as a random function with distinct values on the N admitted addresses (the probability
   of any tie is below N²/2⁶⁵, and ties are broken deterministically). The values are exchangeable, so the set of
   the k smallest is equally likely to be any k-subset: a uniform sample without replacement, inclusion
   probability k/N. For the fixed rate the inclusion probability is ⌊p·2⁶⁴⌋/2⁶⁴, within 2⁻⁶⁴ of p, independently
   per address.
2. *Exact merge.* For disjoint shards A and B, let S_k(X) be the k smallest of X. Claim: S_k(A ∪ B) =
   S_k(S_k(A) ∪ S_k(B)). If x ∈ S_k(A ∪ B) and x ∈ A, fewer than k elements of A ∪ B are smaller than x, hence
   fewer than k elements of A, so x ∈ S_k(A); likewise for B. So S_k(A ∪ B) ⊆ S_k(A) ∪ S_k(B), and taking the k
   smallest of the right side returns it. The operation is commutative and associative because set union and
   "k smallest under a total order" are.
3. *Weights.* Population estimates weight traced worlds by 1/p (fixed rate) or N/k (fixed size); failing worlds
   are always traced and form a stratum of weight one.
Tests: `inclusion_frequencies` (N = 1,000 addresses, k = 100, 10⁶ run seeds: each address's inclusion count is
Binomial(10⁶, 0.1), mean 100,000, standard deviation 300; all 1,000 counts must lie inside the exact binomial
interval at family-wise level 10⁻³); `merge_equals_global` (10⁴ random shard splits into 1 to 16 shards: merged
sample equals the unsharded one exactly); `rate_inclusion` (p = 0.01 over 10⁶ addresses: count inside exact
binomial bounds).

**PO-CHRON-04 (argument and exhaustive check written here).** Quantized magnitude |q|, parameter s. For
|q| < 2ˢ the bin holds exactly one integer. For |q| ≥ 2ˢ let e = ⌊log₂|q|⌋ and sub-bucket index
i = (|q| >> (e − s)) & (2ˢ − 1); the bin covers [2ᵉ + i·2^(e−s), 2ᵉ + (i + 1)·2^(e−s)), of width 2^(e−s), with
lower edge at least 2ᵉ, so width ≤ 2⁻ˢ × lower edge. With m magnitude bits there are 2ˢ exact bins and (m − s)
octaves of 2ˢ sub-buckets: (m − s + 1)·2ˢ bins per sign (640 for m = 24, s = 5, relative resolution 3.1%; 112
for m = 16, s = 3). Merge is integer addition of counts, which is commutative and associative; the 16-bit
saturating counts of per-world histograms merge by min(a + b, 65,535), which is also commutative and associative
on non-negative integers. Tests: `bins_exhaustive_16bit` (every magnitude 0 to 65,535, for s = 3 and s = 5: the
bin index is non-decreasing in |q|; the value lies in its bin; the bin holds one integer or its width is at most
2⁻ˢ of its lower edge; the bin count equals the formula); `merge_laws` (10⁴ random triples of histograms:
(a ⊕ b) ⊕ c = a ⊕ (b ⊕ c), a ⊕ b = b ⊕ a, including saturation).

**PO-CHRON-05.** On exact classes the comparison runs along the chain of state digests, where each chain value
covers the whole prefix; so "the prefixes agree up to chunk i" is monotone in i, a binary search over chunks
returns the first unequal chunk, and a linear scan of per-step digests inside it returns the first unequal step.
A later tick could be reported only if two unequal prefixes had equal chain digests (a collision). Tests:
`planted_one_tick` (100 trials, a divergence planted at a random tick: reported tick equals it);
`planted_two_ticks` (100 trials, divergences at ticks a < b: reported a). Pinned case: traces that differ at
ticks 1 and 5 and agree at ticks 2 to 4 report tick 1, where a midpoint search on the pointwise predicate "tick k
differs" probes tick 3, sees agreement, goes right and reports tick 5.

**PO-CHRON-06.** Tests at the law tier: `digest_single_bit_sensitivity` (for a pinned corpus of 10³ states,
flipping any one recorded bit changes the 128-bit digest: zero misses over all flips); `digest_avalanche` (over
10⁵ random single-bit flips the mean number of changed output bits is 64 ± 0.5 and no output bit changes with
frequency outside [0.49, 0.51]); `digest_cross_backend` (the corpus's digests are bit-identical on `batch-cpu`,
CUDA and Metal; integer arithmetic is exact on every backend); collision budget stated in the record: for n
recorded states the chance of any collision is at most n²/2¹²⁹.

**PO-CHRON-07.** The record cites GATE-CHRON-03's row: residual below 10⁻⁹ relative on the referee and 10⁻³ on
single-precision tiers; a damped pendulum's ledger matches the hand calculation. The referee part of the row is
recorded at M1 by BD-092 and the single-precision part at M2 by BD-208; the obligation is `discharged` only
when the record cites the claim identifiers of both parts.

**Discharge records and ledger.** Each `po-chron-0N.md` has: statement; method; the commit and command that
produced the evidence; the claim identifier of the result; status. The ledger is the program's one obligations
file, `crates/frob-conformance/registry/obligations.toml`, whose schema, loader and row checks are N28cp2-01's (a
dependency; the plan names no file): this bead appends its seven rows in that shared row schema and makes no
second file. One `[[obligation]]` row per obligation: `id`, `statement`, `mechanism` (the beads), `derivation`
(path or `none`), `discharge` (test names and acceptance bounds), `due` (milestone), `status` (`open`,
`derivation-on-file`, `discharged`, `failed`), `record` (the `po-chron-0N.md` path), `claim_ids` (of the
discharging runs), `gate` (where the obligation is a gate, as PO-CHRON-07), `recorded_by`. The conformance
check `obligations_chronicle` fails when an obligation due at or before the current milestone is not
`discharged`, a `discharged` row has no resolving claim identifier, or a named derivation path is missing.

      -03, -04, -05, -06 and -07 are `discharged` with resolving claims, and PO-CHRON-02 is `open` with

      not resolve, and on a missing derivation path.
- [ ] `bins_exhaustive_16bit` passes for s = 3 and s = 5 and its planted variant (sub-bucket index taken from the
      wrong shift) fails.
- [ ] `inclusion_frequencies`, `merge_equals_global` and `rate_inclusion` pass; the planted arrival-order
      reservoir fails `merge_equals_global`.
- [ ] `planted_one_tick` and `planted_two_ticks` report the true first tick 100 of 100; the planted midpoint
      search on a pointwise predicate fails the two-tick test.
- [ ] `digest_single_bit_sensitivity` has zero misses; `digest_cross_backend` is bit-identical on every admitted
      backend; a planted digest that omits one state field fails the sensitivity test.
- [ ] The two derivations written here (PO-03, -04) are readable without the plan and reviewed by a session
      that did not write them (PO-02's is N29b-08's, under the same rule).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Listed with each obligation above; all under `crates/frob-conformance/tests/po_chron/`. Additional pinned cases:
`bins_formula_counts` (640 and 112); `saturating_merge` (65,000 + 1,000 = 65,535 in either order and grouping);
`k_smallest_merge_small` (A = {5, 1, 9}, B = {4, 7, 2}, k = 2: S₂(A) = {1, 5}, S₂(B) = {2, 4}, merged {1, 2},
equal to S₂(A ∪ B)).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/chronicle_proof_obligations.sh` runs the test group (`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p
frob-conformance --test po_chron --features gauntlet-mutations`) and one user-side check per obligation where a
verb exists, every run under its own key:
- PO-CHRON-03: `frob sim run <reference quadruped> --tier batch-cpu --worlds 16 --seconds 2 --telemetry T2
  --trace-size 4 --seed <seed> --workers 1 --key po3-w1 --json` and the same request with `--workers 4 --key
  po3-w4` (two partitions of the worlds among publishing workers; the worker count is an operator-only input
  outside every identity, so one key would return the first run's stored receipt); `frob telemetry query <run>
  --traced-worlds --json` lists the same four worlds for both.
- PO-CHRON-04: `frob telemetry histogram <run> --channel <a declared channel> --quantile 0.95 --json` on the
  first of those runs returns an interval whose width is at most 2⁻ˢ of its lower bound.
- PO-CHRON-05: the first request repeated in the conformance build with the two-tick plant of N29a-25
  (`--conformance-plant <file>`, built by `two_tick_pair`: one unit in the last place at tick 1, restored, and
  again at tick 5; `--key po5-plant`), then `frob telemetry divergence <run> <planted run> --json` reports
  tick 1.

NDJSON lines per the shared helper: command,
exit code, wall time, evidence block, per-obligation status and due milestone, sample sizes, the measured
statistics with their bounds (avalanche mean against 64 ± 0.5; inclusion counts against their binomial
interval); on failure the obligation, the first counterexample, the seed and the command that replays it. A
`summary` line counts obligations discharged of seven and names any still `open`.

## Considerations

- The uniformity argument models the counter-based generator as a random function. That is an assumption about
  the generator, stated in the record; the frequency test is its empirical check, not a proof.
- PO-CHRON-05 holds "on exact classes". In tolerance mode the algorithm returns an interval or `unresolved`; no
  obligation claims a tick there.
- The 10⁶-seed frequency test costs 10⁹ hash evaluations; it runs in the nightly lane, with 10⁴ seeds per
  change.
