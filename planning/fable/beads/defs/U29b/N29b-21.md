## Background

An impulse-level step applies forces, then constraints, then drift. Between the first two stages the bodies have
a velocity that no physical instant has: the *unconstrained predictor*. A block resting on a support acquires the
free velocity g·h and the support impulse removes it; the kinetic energy that passes through the predictor per
step is ½·m·(g·h)², first order in h, and it is neither work nor heat. The plan's rule (tier Solid) is that this
energy is a separate ledger line, the *algorithmic exchange*, accumulated separately from *constitutive
dissipation* for each contact row and each joint-limit row, and that only constitutive dissipation may feed a
thermal or a wear state. Worked case of the defect: booked as contact dissipation, the predictor energy warms
the tyres of a parked robot at a rate set by the step size (1 J/s at 32 Hz and 0.5 J/s at 64 Hz for a 1 kg block
under g = 8 m/s²).

Ownership: `frob-contact` and `frob-dyn` own the two quantities; `frob-chronicle` owns the ledger line and the
invariant. The dynamics subsystem (crate `frob-dyn`) owns the step, so it is the only place that knows the
predictor. This bead makes the step expose it, proves and tests the cancellation that makes the line honest, and
applies the split to the rows that are the dynamics subsystem's own: joint limits and hard stops, joint damping,
and the stiction and backlash rows assembled from the actuator subsystem's descriptors. Contact rows are N29b-20;
the ledger line is N29b-22.

The plan gives the concept and the laws; the formulas are this bead's DESIGN, chosen so that the plan's numbers
come out exactly and the two sides of the exchange cancel identically in every step. Rejected: exposing only the
net exchange (identically zero, shows nothing); recomputing the predictor in the ledger from telemetry (the
ledger would then re-implement the step and could disagree with it).

## Scope

In: the three row velocities (u⁻, u*, u⁺) for every constraint row of an interval; the rows' algorithmic
exchange Xᵢ = λᵢ·½·(uᵢ* − uᵢ⁻), computed by the step for every impulse-level row (contact rows included); the
predictor-side exchange and the cancellation identity; events; the energy split for joint-limit and hard-stop
rows; dissipation of joint damping, stiction and backlash rows by their laws; inclusion in the transaction's
restore set; the per-interval record types for the ledger (`PredictorEnergy` and `RowEnergy`, both defined here
because this bead comes first: N29b-20 depends on it and fills `RowEnergy` for contact rows); the script
`scripts/e2e/predictor_exchange.sh` with its two fixtures.

Out: contact rows' dissipation laws (N29b-20); ledger lines, telemetry and the binding rule (N29b-22); the
batched stage-9 kernel (N29b-24); the row laws themselves (seed BD-084 on the referee).

## Technical approach

**1. The interval, as the step executes it.** v⁻ at the start; forces: v* = v⁻ + h·M⁻¹·f, where f collects every
force that is a function of state (gravity, bias, actuator torques, springs, dampers, state-function rows as
solved); constraints: v⁺ = v* + M⁻¹·Jᵀ·λ over the impulse-level rows; drift with v⁺. The step already computes
all three velocities; this bead retains v* until the interval's energy record is built. For each impulse-level
row i it exposes uᵢ⁻ = Jᵢ·v⁻, uᵢ* = Jᵢ·v*, uᵢ⁺ = Jᵢ·v⁺ (plus the prescribed-surface term where there is one),
with Jᵢ the row Jacobian the solve used.

**2. Predictor-side exchange and the identity.** With T(v) = ½·vᵀ·M·v:

```
X_pred = [T(v*) − T(v⁻)] − h·fᵀ·½·(v⁻ + v⁺)        kinetic energy the force stage put into the predictor, minus
                                                    the whole-interval discrete work of the same forces
       = h·fᵀ·½·(v* − v⁺)
Σᵢ Xᵢ  = Σᵢ λᵢ·½·(uᵢ* − uᵢ⁻) = ½·(Jᵀλ)ᵀ·(v* − v⁻)   the rows' algorithmic exchange (this bead, for every row)
X_pred + Σᵢ Xᵢ = 0     in every interval, for every state
```

Proof: M·(v* − v⁻) = h·f and M·(v⁺ − v*) = Jᵀλ, so X_pred = ½·(v* − v⁻)ᵀ·M·(v* − v⁺) = −½·(v* − v⁻)ᵀ·Jᵀλ = −Σᵢ Xᵢ.
Both sides equal ±½·(v* − v⁻)ᵀ·Jᵀλ: the exchange is the predictor's velocity change paired with the constraint
impulse. This is what "on a resting body the algorithmic work of gravity and the algorithmic removal by the
support cancel exactly in every step, and the identity is checked with both shown" means in formulas. The step
computes X_pred from its own quantities, independently of the rows' Xᵢ, so the check is a real check.

X_pred is attributed to its sources for display: X_pred = Σ_sources h·f_sᵀ·½·(v* − v⁺) over gravity, actuators,
passive forces and state-function rows.

**3. Events.** At a located event the impulse is solved on the common pre-event state with no force stage before
it: v* = v⁻, so X_pred = 0 and every Xᵢ = 0. Event impulses are entirely physical; their loss is the restitution
loss of their law.

**4. Joint-limit and hard-stop rows (impulse-level on the referee).** A row on joint velocity q̇ⱼ with impulse λ:
S = λ·½·(q̇* + q̇⁺), W = λ·½·(q̇⁻ + q̇⁺), X = λ·½·(q̇* − q̇⁻). Constitutive dissipation: restitution loss at a
located limit impact, D_e = ½·I_eff·(1 − e²)·ω², with ω the pre-impact joint speed into the stop, e the card's
stop restitution and I_eff = 1/(Jⱼ·M⁻¹·Jⱼᵀ) the effective inertia at the joint; zero while the joint rests on the
stop.

**5. Rows whose force is a function of state (no algorithmic exchange; dissipation by their own laws).**

| Row | Stored energy | Constitutive dissipation per interval |
|---|---|---|
| Joint damping (viscous, coefficient b) | none | D = b·(q̇⁺)²·h |
| Stiction on the convex tiers (potential ℓ(v) = h·τ_b·ψ_ε(v), ψ_ε(v) = v²/(2ε) for abs(v) ≤ ε and abs(v) − ε/2 otherwise) | none | D = (impulse magnitude)·abs(q̇⁺) = h·τ_b·abs(ψ_ε′(q̇⁺))·abs(q̇⁺) |
| Stiction on the referee (set-valued, ε = 0) | none | D = h·τ_b·abs(q̇⁺) when sliding; 0 when stuck |
| Backlash on the convex tiers (compliant gap rows on δ = θ/N − q with tooth stiffness k_t and a dissipation time) | ½·k_t·p² with p the gap penetration | D = c_t·ṗ²·h, c_t = k_t × dissipation time |
| Backlash on the referee (hard gap rows, card restitution, zero by default) | none | Restitution loss ½·I_eff·(1 − e²)·δ̇² at the located closure; these rows are impulse-level and also carry X by item 4 |
| Compliant joint limit on the convex tiers | ½·k·p² | c·ṗ²·h |

The stiction bound τ_b is evaluated at the start of the interval (τ_b = τ_c + (τ_s − τ_c)·σ(v⁻/v_s) +
c_load·abs(τ_transmitted⁻), with Coulomb level τ_c, breakaway level τ_s, Stribeck velocity v_s and the card's
shape σ), so D uses the same bound the solve used.

**6. Record.** Per interval: `PredictorEnergy { by_source: [(SourceId, f64)], total: f64 }` and one `RowEnergy`
per impulse-level or joint row. Both types are defined here, in `frob_core::energy` (DESIGN: the one crate that
`frob-dyn`, `frob-contact` and `frob-chronicle` all name, so no crate depends on a later bead's crate for a
record type); the fields are those sketched in N29b-20 item 7 (feature or joint identity, row kind, row class,
S, W, X, the dissipation components, stored-energy change, remainder). This bead fills X for every impulse-level
row and every field for joint rows; N29b-20 fills S, W, D, ΔU and the remainder for contact rows and asserts
that its own X equals the step's. `check_exchange(interval)` asserts abs(X_pred + Σ Xᵢ) ≤ tol·scale with
tol = 10⁻¹² in `f64`, scale = abs(X_pred) + Σ abs(Xᵢ) + 1 J·10⁻³ (DESIGN floor, so a resting interval with both
sides near zero is not judged relative to zero); a failure marks the interval's energy record `inconsistent`
and is a check of the transactional step (it joins the "energy-ledger residual" check before commit). It joins
as a second condition under check 5 of the commit checks (N16a-08, a dependency): this bead registers it
through that bead's `CommitCheck` registration, it reports its own `CheckResult` (measured abs(X_pred + Σ Xᵢ) in
joules, threshold tol·scale, locus the interval), and it is not a ninth check. A failure therefore discards the
attempt and sends the transaction to its retry ladder like any other failed check.

**7. Restore set.** The retained v* and the interval's energy records are candidate state: built beside the
committed state, published at commit, discarded on a retry. The ledger's accumulators are already in the restore
set; nothing here may survive a rejected attempt, or a retried interval would double-count.

**8. Layout.** `crates/frob-dyn/src/step/{predictor.rs, row_energy.rs}`; tests
`crates/frob-dyn/tests/predictor_exchange.rs`. Determinism: class D2 with the step.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Resting block (1 kg, g = 8 m/s², impulse step): X_pred = +½·m·(g·h)² from the gravity source and the
      support row's X = −½·m·(g·h)², at 32, 64 and 2,000 Hz; the sum is 0 within 10⁻¹⁵ J.
- [ ] `check_exchange` holds on every committed interval of the first-milestone referee battery (count of
      inconsistent intervals: 0), and fails on a planted step that solves constraints against a different
      predictor than the one it records.
- [ ] Event intervals report X_pred = 0 and all Xᵢ = 0.
- [ ] A joint held on a hard stop by 4 N·m with I_eff = 0.5 kg·m²: X = −16·h² J per interval (−0.5 J/s at 32 Hz,
      −0.25 J/s at 64 Hz), D = 0; an impact into the stop at ω = 2 rad/s with e = 0: D_e = 1 J booked once.
- [ ] Joint damping, stiction and backlash rows report dissipation by the table and never an algorithmic
      exchange, except hard backlash and hard stops on the referee.
- [ ] A forced retry of an interval leaves the ledger identical to a clean run of the same plan (no double
      counting).
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/predictor_exchange.sh` exits 0 on a correct build with its four controls caught; its log
      passes the shared lint.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `predictor_exchange_resting`: v⁻ = 0, f = −m·g, λ = m·g·h → X_pred = ½·m·g²·h² (0.03125 J at h = 1/32);
  Σ Xᵢ = −0.03125 J.
- `predictor_exchange_driven_slide`: push 4 N, friction −4 N, gravity and support: X_pred = 8·h² (push) +
  32·h² (gravity) = 40·h²; rows −8·h² and −32·h²; sum 0. At h = 1/32: 0.0390625 J.
- `identity_random`: 10⁴ seeded articulated states with 0 to 12 impulse-level rows: abs(X_pred + Σ Xᵢ) ≤ 10⁻¹²
  relative.
- `free_flight_no_exchange`: no rows → X_pred = 0 (v⁺ = v*).
- `event_has_no_exchange`.
- `hard_stop_resting`: τ = 4 N·m, I_eff = 0.5 kg·m²: q̇* = τ·h/I_eff = 8·h, λ = −4·h, X = −4·h·½·8·h = −16·h².
- `hard_stop_impact`: ω = 2 rad/s, e = 0, I_eff = 0.5 → D_e = ½·0.5·4 = 1 J.
- `viscous_damping_power`: b = 0.1 N·m·s/rad at q̇ = 2 rad/s → 0.4 W (D = 0.4·h).
- `stiction_sliding_power`: τ_b = 0.2 N·m sliding at 3 rad/s, far outside ε → 0.6 W; stuck (q̇⁺ = 0) → 0.
- `stiction_creep_inside_kink`: ε = 10⁻³ rad/s, holding τ = 0.1 N·m against τ_b = 0.2: creep q̇ = ε·τ/τ_b =
  5·10⁻⁴ rad/s, D = 0.1 × 5·10⁻⁴ = 5·10⁻⁵ W (the stated cost of the convex form, booked as dissipation because
  the law has a dissipation function there).
- `backlash_compliant_storage`: k_t = 10⁴ N·m/rad, p = 10⁻³ rad → 0.005 J stored.
- `retry_no_double_count`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/predictor_exchange.sh` (owned here and built on this bead's dependencies only; header
`bead=N29b-21 milestone=M1 seed=0`). It uses two fixtures registered in N24b-03's registry
(`assets/fixtures/predictor/<name>/fixture.toml`, kind `synthetic`): `predictor/rest-block` (the 1 kg block at
rest on a frictionless stationary support under g = 8 m/s²) and `predictor/hard-stop` (one joint held on a hard
stop by 4 N·m, I_eff = 0.5 kg·m²). Steps:
1. `values`: `FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-dyn --test predictor_exchange`, with `FROB_TEST_EVIDENCE_DIR`
   set to the run's work directory; from the evidence lines the script logs X_pred and Σ Xᵢ of the resting block
   at 32, 64 and 2,000 Hz against ±½·m·(g·h)² and their sum against 10⁻¹⁵ J, the hard stop's −16·h² and 1 J,
   and the number of states `identity_random` checked.
2. `runs`: `frob sim run --tier referee --fixture predictor/<name> --step <h> --seconds 4 --seed 0 --key
   pe-<name>-<rate> --json` for both fixtures at h = 1/32 and 1/64 s: exit 0, every interval committed and no
   world faulted. `check_exchange` is a condition of commit check 5, so a committed run is one whose every
   interval passed it.
3. `controls`: the four negative controls below through `e2e_expect_caught`, each the build with its planted
   defect (the control's `nc-` feature of `frob-dyn`) running the test that must fail it (`identity_random`,
   `event_has_no_exchange`, `viscous_damping_power`, `retry_no_double_count`).

NDJSON lines per the shared helper: command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block of each run, each exchange
in joules per interval with its expected value, their sum against its threshold, the count of inconsistent
intervals (expected 0), each control with `caught`; on failure the first interval whose identity fails, with
X_pred and Σ Xᵢ; a final `summary`.

Reading the two entries from the user's side (predictor side and constraint side of equal magnitude and opposite
sign, in joules per second, summing to zero within 10⁻¹²) needs the ledger line of N29b-22 and the verb
`telemetry.energy` (N24a-37), both later than this bead. Those are the steps `predictor-*` of
`scripts/e2e/gate_chron_14.sh`; they belong to that script's bead, N29b-23, which depends on this bead and on
both and runs this script as a stage.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Plan: resting block, algorithmic exchange 1 J/s at 32 Hz and 0.5 J/s at 64 Hz.
- Derived here: the hard-stop values (−0.5 and −0.25 J/s; 1 J impact); the driven slide's 40·h².
- Negative controls: the recorded predictor differs from the solved one (identity fails); an event solved after
  a force stage (events then show an exchange); joint damping booked as algorithmic exchange; a retry that keeps
  the rejected attempt's record.
- Metamorphic: the identity holds under any change of generalized coordinates (it is written in terms of M, f,
  J and λ only); halving h halves every exchange per second.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; forced-retry digest equals the clean digest.

## Considerations

- The identity is exact for the first-order kick, contact, drift structure. A step with a different structure (a
  variational integrator on the referee is a later, Frontier item) defines its own predictor, or has none; the
  record then carries zero entries and says which structure produced it.
- On the lockstep tiers with a compliant law there are no impulse-level rows and the predictor is the solved
  velocity; N29b-24 carries that case and the hard-contact batch law.
- Source attribution of X_pred is for reading, not for closure; only its total enters the check.
- Cost: one extra Jacobian-vector product per impulse-level row and interval on the referee, negligible beside
  the solve.
