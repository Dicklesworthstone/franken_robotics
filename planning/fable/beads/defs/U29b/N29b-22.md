## Background

The energy ledger of the record subsystem (crate `frob-chronicle`) attributes every joule of an episode to a
store, a loss or an export, with the numerical residual on its own line. A late addition to the plan (tier Solid)
adds a second non-physical line and a rule about what may consume the ledger's heat.

**What it is.** A separate ledger line for the energy that an impulse-level contact step exchanges with the
velocity predictor, and a rule that only constitutive dissipation feeds a thermal or a wear state.

**Why.** A 1 kg block rests on a frictionless stationary support under g = 8 m/s². A step that applies forces,
then contact, then drift gives the block a free velocity g·h and removes it with the support impulse. The kinetic
energy removed per step is ½·m·(g·h)², that is ½·m·g²·h per second of simulated time: 1 J at 32 Hz and 0.5 J at
64 Hz. The block has zero displacement and the support produces no heat. The quantity is first order in h and
vanishes under refinement. Booked as contact dissipation, it warms the tyres of a parked robot at a rate set by
the step size. A design loop that reads tyre temperature, film state or wear would then be optimizing against
the step size.

The two quantities are produced per row by the contact and dynamics subsystems (N29b-20 and N29b-21): the
*algorithmic exchange* (what a constraint impulse removes from, or returns to, the unconstrained predictor) and
the *constitutive dissipation* (computed from a law with a dissipation function: sliding friction as the integral
of f_t·v_slip at the solved slip velocity; normal damping of the compliant law as the integral of c·ḋ²;
restitution loss ½·m_eff·(1 − e²)·v_n² at a located impact; joint damping; stiction and backlash rows by their
own laws). This bead is Chronicle's part: the ledger line, the identity shown with both sides, the invariant on
what a physical state may be fed from, the refusal that enforces it, and what the energy view prints.

Rejected alternatives: leaving the exchange inside the numerical residual (the residual would then be large and
step-dependent on every resting contact, and would hide real residuals); a documentation rule for model authors
(the defect is silent and plausible; it has to be a refusal).

## Technical approach

**1. Line kinds.** Every line of the ledger's registry has a kind, and the kind is what the binding rule reads:

| Kind | Lines |
|---|---|
| `store` | Δ kinetic; Δ potential (gravity and every elastic store, contact compliance included); Δ stored in an actuator (magnetic, rotor-kinetic, elastic, polarization, thermal) |
| `work` | Mechanical work at joints; work on fixtures and payloads; boundary work of prescribed surfaces; battery energy out |
| `constitutive-dissipation` | Contact sliding friction; contact normal damping; restitution loss at located impacts; joint damping; stiction; backlash; rolling and torsional friction where a law has them |
| `electrical-loss` | Copper loss; iron loss; gear and actuator friction losses of the actuator model; shunt dissipation |
| `algorithmic` | Algorithmic exchange, constraint side (by row); algorithmic exchange, predictor side (by source) |
| `residual` | Numerical residual ("integrator and solver"), including per-row discretization remainders |

**2. The line.** Per (world, episode), double-float accumulators: `alg_constraint[row class or site]` = Σ Xᵢ over
intervals and impulse-level rows (negative when energy is removed from the predictor), and
`alg_predictor[source]` = Σ X_pred by source (gravity, actuators, passive forces, state-function rows). The
energy view prints both, per second and in total, and their sum. Known display for the resting block at 32 Hz:
predictor side +1 J/s (gravity), constraint side −1 J/s (support), net 0.

**3. The identity, with both shown.** The mechanical identity of the ledger is unchanged in its physical terms
and gains the pair:

```
mechanical work at joints = Δ kinetic + Δ potential + constitutive dissipation (by component)
                            + work on fixtures and payloads
                            + [ algorithmic exchange: predictor side + constraint side ]      (the bracket is 0)
                            + numerical residual
```

Checks, per committed interval and per episode: (a) the bracket is zero within 10⁻¹² relative to the gross
exchange in `f64` (10⁻⁵ in `f32`); a non-zero bracket is reported as `exchange-inconsistent` and added to the
residual line, never to a physical line; (b) the whole identity closes to the tier's residual bound
(invariant INV-CHRON-09: the lines of the energy ledger, including the residual line, sum to zero; no physical
term absorbs the residual). The created-energy audit's residual (N29b-02) is unaffected by the pair, because the
bracket is zero and dissipation is constitutive.

**4. The binding rule (invariant, DESIGN name INV-CHRON-14 proposed; the plan states it without a number).** A
thermal state (winding, housing, tyre, film) and a wear state take input only from lines of kind
`constitutive-dissipation` and `electrical-loss`. Mechanism: every thermal or wear state in a compiled model
declares its heat or wear inputs as *bindings* to ledger channels: `{ state: StateId, source: LineId, element:
ElementId, gain }` (for example the tyre node of wheel 2 ← contact sliding friction of site 2, gain 0.5 for the
share entering the tyre). `frob-chronicle::energy::bindings::validate(model)` runs at run admission, before any
buffer is allocated, and rejects a binding whose source line has kind `algorithmic` or `residual`, or kind
`store` or `work` (a store is not heat; work is not heat until a law dissipates it). It also rejects a binding to
a stage-level quantity that is not a ledger line at all (the "energy removed by the solve").

**5. The refusal.** Words of the plan: *non-physical energy bound to a physical state*. Trigger: `validate`
finds an inadmissible binding. The refusal block names the state, the source line and its kind, and lists the
admissible lines for that state. Ranked repairs: 1. bind the state to the constitutive line of the same element
(for a tyre: sliding friction and rolling resistance of its contact site); 2. remove the binding. `retry:
after-change`. No code is assigned by the plan; until the registry's owner assigns one the mechanism raises it
under the name `non-physical-energy-binding` in the `8xx` block's pending list (the registry bead N29-REF carries
the request), and this bead does not close on a pending code.

**6. Runtime guard.** Admission-time validation covers declared bindings. A second, cheap guard covers code
paths: the function that advances a thermal or wear state takes its heat input as a value of type
`ConstitutiveHeat`, constructible only from lines of the two admitted kinds (a sealed constructor in the ledger),
so a kernel or a model extension cannot pass an algorithmic or residual accumulator by mistake. A compile-fail
test pins it on the host; on device tiers the step plan's wiring table is checked against the same rule.

**7. Telemetry.** Energetics stream: two cumulative-counter channels per world for the pair's totals at level
T0, and per-row and per-source detail at level T2 for traced worlds. Channel kind `cumulative counter`
(downsampled by last value). Both carry `non_physical: true` in their channel declaration, which is what the
binding validator and any downstream consumer key on.

**8. What the energy view prints on every tier.** The two lines separately, always. On tiers whose contact rows
are all state-function rows (the compliant law) the constraint side is zero and so is the predictor side, and the
view prints zeros with the note `no impulse-level rows`. The line matters on the referee's impulse step and on
the hard-contact batch law.

**9. Layout.** `crates/frob-chronicle/src/energy/{lines.rs, algorithmic.rs, bindings.rs}`; schema additions to
`frob.telemetry.energy/1` (fields `algorithmic.predictor`, `algorithmic.constraint`, `algorithmic.net`).
Determinism: with the step (D2). Evidence: line values are Known-answer class Tested on the fixtures.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the referee resting-block fixture the energy view prints predictor side +1 J/s and constraint side
      −1 J/s at 32 Hz, +0.5 and −0.5 at 64 Hz, net 0, constitutive dissipation 0, and the identity closes within
      the referee's bound with both lines shown.
- [ ] A model whose tyre or film node is bound to the algorithmic line, to the residual line, or to a
      stage-level solve energy is refused at admission with the refusal described above, before any buffer is
      allocated; the same model bound to sliding friction is admitted.
- [ ] `ConstitutiveHeat` cannot be constructed from an `algorithmic` or `residual` line (compile-fail tests).
- [ ] The identity check reports `exchange-inconsistent` on a planted step whose two sides differ, and the
      difference lands in the residual line.
- [ ] The two lines appear in `frob telemetry energy --json` on every tier that exists at the milestone, with
      zeros and the note where there are no impulse-level rows.
- [ ] The created-energy flags of the three fixtures are unchanged by the presence of the lines.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-chronicle` lists the line kinds, the binding rule and the refusal.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`crates/frob-chronicle/tests/algorithmic_line.rs`, `…/bindings.rs`)

- `line_kinds_table`: every registered line has exactly one kind; the two algorithmic lines and the residual are
  `non_physical`.
- `resting_block_pair`: per-interval inputs X = −0.03125 J and X_pred = +0.03125 J for 32 intervals → −1 J and
  +1 J per second, net 0.
- `identity_with_pair_closes`: the three fixtures' per-interval records (resting, driven slide at 4 W, plastic
  impact of 2 J) close with zero residual.
- `bracket_nonzero_goes_to_residual`: X = −0.03125, X_pred = +0.03000 → `exchange-inconsistent`, 0.00125 J on
  the residual line, physical lines unchanged.
- `binding_admissible`: tyre ← sliding friction; winding ← copper loss; housing ← gear loss: admitted.
- `binding_refused_algorithmic`, `binding_refused_residual`, `binding_refused_store` (film ← Δ potential),
  `binding_refused_stage_energy`: each refused with the state, the line, its kind and the admissible list.
- `validate_before_allocation`: the refusal is returned with no device or host buffer created.
- Compile-fail: `constitutive_heat_from_algorithmic`, `constitutive_heat_from_residual`.
- `compliant_tier_prints_zero_pair`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Plan: resting block, algorithmic exchange 1 J/s at 32 Hz and 0.5 J/s at 64 Hz, constitutive dissipation 0,
  heat 0; sliding at 1 m/s on μ = 0.5, 4 W of friction heat at both rates; a plastic drop arriving at 2 m/s, 2 J
  booked once at the located event.
- Negative control of the gate: a mutant that feeds algorithmic exchange into the film temperature; its
  temperature rise differs between 32 and 64 Hz by a factor of 2 (N29b-23 runs it; this bead's validator is what
  the mutant bypasses).
- Further negative controls: the pair folded into the residual (the residual then reads 1 J/s on a resting block
  and the referee's closure bound fails); the pair folded into contact dissipation (heat becomes rate-dependent).
- Metamorphic: halving the step halves both sides of the pair and changes no constitutive line.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release.

## Considerations

- The invariant has no identifier in the plan's table of thirteen; INV-CHRON-14 is proposed here and reported in
  the unit's coverage notes so the registry's owner can adopt or renumber it.
- Thermal nodes for tyres and films arrive with their features at later milestones (the film-friction law is a
  third-milestone seed). The validator is generic over states, so those nodes are covered when they appear; at
  the first milestone it protects windings, housings and the fixture's test node.
- Electrical loss is admitted as a heat source because it is itself constitutive (i²·R and the card's loss laws),
  owned by the actuator model's ledger.
- A share (`gain`) below 1 must leave the rest somewhere: bindings of one source line must sum to at most 1, and
  the unbound share is heat to ambient. The validator checks the sum.
- Revisit if a tier introduces a step without a predictor: the pair is then identically zero and the line kinds
  and binding rule are unchanged.
