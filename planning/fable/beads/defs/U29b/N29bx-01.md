## Background

The record subsystem (Chronicle) lists thirteen numbered invariants, INV-CHRON-01 to INV-CHRON-13, and the
project's testing rule is that every invariant has a registered property test with a planted violation it
must catch. A later section of the same chapter adds a fourteenth invariant and states it in a sentence
without a number:

> A thermal state (winding, housing, tyre, film) and a wear state take input only from constitutive
> dissipation lines and from electrical loss.

The reason for the invariant is a counterexample. A 1 kg block rests on a frictionless stationary support
under g = 8 m/s². A step that applies forces, then contact, then drift gives the block a free velocity g·h
and removes it with the support impulse. The kinetic energy removed per step is ½·m·(g·h)², which is
½·m·g²·h per second of simulated time: 1 J/s at 32 Hz and 0.5 J/s at 64 Hz. The block does not move and the
support produces no heat. That quantity is an artefact of the algorithm (the *algorithmic exchange* between
the velocity predictor and the constraint impulse); it is first order in the step h and vanishes under
refinement. Booked as contact dissipation it would warm the tyres of a parked robot at a rate set by the
step size. *Constitutive dissipation*, by contrast, comes from a law with a dissipation function: sliding
friction ∫ f_t·v_slip, normal damping ∫ c·ḋ², restitution loss at a located impact, joint damping.

The mechanism that enforces the rule exists as a bead (the ledger line and the binding validator, N29b-22),
and that bead had to invent a name for the invariant ("INV-CHRON-14, proposed"), because identifiers are
stable handles that tests, registries and gate records cite, and an unnumbered invariant cannot be cited.
The suite that registers Chronicle's invariants as property tests covers 01 to 13 only. This bead assigns
the number, enters the invariant in the invariant register, and adds the fourteenth property-test module
with its planted violation, so that the invariant is enforced by the conformance harness and not only by
the mechanism bead that happens to implement it. Tier: Solid.

DESIGN: the identifier is **INV-CHRON-14**, the next free number of the chapter's series. Identifier
prefixes are stable and numbers are never reused; adding one is recorded as an addition to the chapter's
invariant table (thirteen rows in the plan, fourteen in the register), with this bead as the record.

## Scope

In: the register row; the module `inv_14` of the Chronicle invariant suite (a static property over binding
tables and a dynamic property over runs); its planted violations; staging by milestone; the note of the
addition.

Out: the ledger line, the binding validator, the sealed `ConstitutiveHeat` type and the refusal (N29b-22);
the gate script of GATE-CHRON-14 on the referee (N29b-23, which depends on this bead and runs this bead's script

allocation ledger under the slug `non-physical-energy-bound` in family `1xx` (N35x-02) and is entered in the
registry by N29-REF.

## Technical approach

Layout (DESIGN, the register format N21c-27 also uses): `registries/invariants.toml` (section `chronicle`);
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/inv_chron/inv_14.rs`. This bead lands at the first milestone, before the
chapter's suite (N29b-15, second milestone): it creates the `inv_chron` test target with this one module and
the staging rule's `not exercised` reporting, and N29b-15 adds modules 01 to 13 and the summary around it.

1. **Register row** (fields as the register's other sections use them: `id`, `statement`, `verification`,
   `test`, `negative_control`, `tier`, `first_milestone`, `owner_bead`):
   `id = "INV-CHRON-14"`; `statement` = the sentence quoted above; `verification = "binding validation at
   admission; sealed heat type; step-rate invariance of thermal and wear states on the three fixtures"`;
   `test = "inv_chron::inv_14"`; `negative_control = "algorithmic exchange bound to the film temperature"`;
   `tier = "G0 · host for the static property; G1 · referee at M1, batch tiers at M2"`;
   `first_milestone = "M1"`; `owner_bead = "N29b-22"`.
2. **Static property.** Generate compiled-model binding tables at random: states of the four thermal kinds
   and the wear kind, sources drawn from every ledger line kind (`constitutive-dissipation`,
   `electrical-loss`, `algorithmic`, `residual`, `store`, `work`) and from non-ledger stage quantities.
   Assert: the validator accepts a table if and only if every source kind is `constitutive-dissipation` or
   `electrical-loss`; a rejected table is rejected before any buffer is allocated; the refusal names the
   first offending state, its source and the source's kind.
3. **Dynamic property (step-rate invariance).** For each fixture below, run at 32, 64 and 2,000 Hz on the
   referee (M1) with one thermal node bound to the constitutive line of the contact. Assert that the heat
   input integrated by the node equals the constitutive line's integral at every rate, and that the node's
   temperature rise agrees across rates within the fixture's tolerance. The invariant fails exactly when a
   state's input depends on h in the way the algorithmic exchange does.
   | Fixture | Constitutive heat (truth) | Algorithmic exchange |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 kg block at rest, g = 8 m/s², frictionless support | 0, exactly | 1 J/s at 32 Hz; 0.5 J/s at 64 Hz |
   | The same block sliding at 1 m/s on μ = 0.5 | μ·m·g·v = 4 W, within 0.1% | as at rest |
   | Plastic drop arriving at 2 m/s | ½·m·v² = 2 J, within 10⁻⁹ J, booked once at the located event | none after the event |
4. **Planted violations (each must fail the module).** The first is the gate's own mutant and keeps its one
   control (seeded from N29-CX's entry C1 by the seeding tool; no second row); the other three are new
   `[[control]]` rows in `controls.toml` with `seed = "INV-CHRON-14"`, `gate = ["GATE-CHRON-14"]`, `kind =
   "mutation"` and a feature each, in the form N29b-15 uses for the other invariants.
   - `bind-algorithmic-to-film` (feature `mutant-chron-exchange-heat`): the film node's source is the
     algorithmic line with validation bypassed. Caught by the dynamic property: the temperature rise at 32 Hz
     is twice that at 64 Hz.
   - `bind-residual-to-winding`: caught by the static property.
   - `validator-accepts-store`: a validator that admits a `store` line (a store is not heat). Caught by the
     if-and-only-if assertion.
   - `heat-from-stage-energy`: a state advanced from "the energy removed by the solve" through an unsealed
     constructor. Caught by the compile-fail test listed with the module.
5. **Staging.** The module follows the Chronicle invariant suite's staging rule (stated here because this
   bead comes first; N29b-15 applies the same rule to 01 to 13): mandatory on the host and the referee at M1;
   the batch-tier rows (single precision, the hard-contact batch law where admitted) report `not exercised
   (due M2)` until N29b-24 turns them on. A module or row still `not exercised` after its milestone fails the
   suite.
6. **Record of the addition.** The register row carries `added = "INV-CHRON-14 is not in the chapter's table
   of thirteen; the sentence is the chapter's own"`. Every bead and test that wrote "proposed" may cite the
   identifier without the qualifier once this bead is closed.

Determinism: the static property is D0; the dynamic property inherits the referee's class.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test inv_chron inv_14` (real validator, real ledger, the referee):

- `register_row_present_and_unique`.
- `static_iff_admissible_kinds`: 10⁴ seeded tables; acceptance ⇔ all sources admissible.
- `refusal_names_first_offender`: a table with offenders at positions 3 and 7 names position 3.
- `rest_block_heat_is_zero_at_all_rates`: constitutive heat exactly 0 at 32, 64 and 2,000 Hz while the
  algorithmic line reads 1, 0.5 and 0.016 J/s (½·1·8²·h).
- `slide_heat_4w`; `impact_heat_2j_once`.
- `temperature_rise_rate_invariant`: film node on the sliding fixture: rises at the three rates agree
  within 0.1%.
- `planted_*`: four tests, each enabling one violation and asserting the module's check fails.
- `staging_reports_not_exercised_until_m2`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/chronicle_inv14.sh` (this bead's; the gate script `scripts/e2e/gate_chron_14.sh` of N29b-23,
which depends on this bead, runs it as its step group `inv-14-suite`): run the `inv_chron` target for module 14
and assert pass, run it with `--plant bind-algorithmic-to-film` and assert failure with the factor-of-two
message, then `frob sim run --tier referee` on a copy of `rest-block` whose film node is bound to the
algorithmic line and assert the admission refusal (exit 3, the slug `non-physical-energy-bound`). It logs per
STD-E2E: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time, the
evidence block, per fixture and rate the constitutive heat and the algorithmic exchange with their truths
and tolerances, the temperature rises compared, the number of tables tested, and on failure the first
offending binding or the first rate pair that disagrees. The `summary` line lists
`gates: ["GATE-CHRON-14"]` as touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Resting block: algorithmic exchange 1 J/s at 32 Hz and 0.5 J/s at 64 Hz; constitutive dissipation 0;
  heat 0. Sliding at 1 m/s on μ = 0.5 under g = 8 m/s²: 4 W at both rates. Plastic drop at 2 m/s: 2 J,
  booked once.
- The gate's mutant: algorithmic exchange fed into the film temperature is caught because its temperature
  rise differs between 32 and 64 Hz by a factor of 2.
- Negative controls: the four planted violations above.
- Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release.

## Considerations

- Wear states are Moonshot work of a later milestone; the static property already generates them so that
  the rule holds from the day one exists.
- On the compliant tiers a resting contact's algorithmic exchange is zero (the force is a function of
  state), so the dynamic property is vacuous there; the module says `vacuous: no impulse-level rows` and
  does not count it as exercised.
- If the plan's table is later renumbered, the register is the authority: numbers are never reused.
