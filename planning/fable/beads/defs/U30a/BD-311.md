## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) owns the Reality claim. Nothing in the project has been compared with a physical
robot, so every report prints `reality: no-claim`. One cheap comparison is nevertheless available long before any
bench data: for a reference robot (an imported public model used as a parity fixture and benchmark baseline),
makers and papers publish figures such as a top speed, a cost of transport, a payload, a step height cleared or
a battery endurance. Simulating the same robot under the stated conditions and comparing is informative: a gross
disagreement points at a wrong card or a wrong import. It is scheduled by the conformance harness at M3 as one of
the program's mitigations for two risks: "calibration data never arrived" (RISK-43) and "common-mode model error:
every tier shares the same guessed cards" (RISK-32).

The comparison must not be mistaken for calibration. **Agreement with a published real-robot figure is Parity,
never Reality** (invariant INV-GAUNT-03; the claim table says the same in its Parity row). The reasons:
published test conditions are loose (surface, temperature, payload, firmware and controller are rarely all
stated); the figure is its authors' claim under their conditions; nothing was frozen before it was read; and no
scope (cell) can be formed from it. So the status is `published-anchor`, the report line names the source, and
**no band is issued**.

Rejected alternatives: treating a published figure as a one-point calibration set (it has no exchangeable
draws, so a conformal band from it is meaningless); tuning a card until the anchor matches (the anchor is then a
fit, and the disagreement it could have revealed is hidden); omitting the comparison because it proves nothing
(it is the only contact with reality the first releases have, and it is honest when labelled).

FACT about inputs: the reference-robot figures collected at planning time are at search-summary level and are
listed among things "not verified, and not relied on". Each must be re-read from its primary source before use.

## Scope

In: the fields the comparison adds to the anchor schedule and its admission rule; the comparison run; the
Parity record; the report line and the no-band rule; the finding filed on disagreement. Out: the base format
and loader of the anchor schedule `registry/anchors.toml` (N38b-01, at M1; a dependency), and the entry of the
M3 records with the check of their stored results (N38bp1-01); the `reality:` line on every report and its
negative battery (BD-139); the typed Parity record and oracle hygiene (N38a-05); bands (N30a-36 onward); the
share of guessed card fields printed beside the line (N30a-03); the end-to-end script (N30a-04).

## Technical approach

1. **Anchor registry.** There is one file, `crates/frob-conformance/registry/anchors.toml`, the conformance
   harness's anchor schedule. Its base record and loader are N38b-01's (one `[[anchor]]` per figure: `id`;
   `robot`, a reference robot with a public model; `quantity`; `published` = value, unit, source, date read
   and the conditions as stated by the source; `fixture`, the scenario that reproduces the stated conditions;
   `tolerance`; `due = "M3"`; `result`, filled by this bead's comparison; a record without source or
   conditions is rejected). This bead does not define a second format: it extends that record and its loader
   with the fields the comparison needs (DESIGN: one artifact, one format; the schedule existed first, at M1,
   so the comparison conforms to it). Added fields: `robot.model` (the `ModelId` of the import);
   `quantity.metric` (the metric definition of the task subsystem the quantity maps to); `published.spread`
   (if the source states one); `published.locator` (the page, table or figure inside the source);
   `read_level` (`primary` or `search-summary`); `conditions_unstated` (the conditions the simulation needs
   and the source does not give, each with the plausible range the comparison will sweep); `licence` (of the
   reference model and of quoting the figure); `captured_by` (a script under `tools/oracle/` that records the
   entry and its date). The `id` is the anchor key printed in the report line (for example
   `anchor:quad-ref-topspeed`). `tolerance` is the half-width allowed on the published side: the source's
   stated spread, or half a unit of the last printed digit when it states none.
   Admission: an entry with `read_level = "search-summary"`, with no `published.locator`, or with a quantity
   that has no metric definition is refused. An anchor is never regenerated from the candidate and a pin
   moves only by a recorded event (the oracle rules of the conformance harness).
2. **The comparison** (a library function `frob_conformance::anchors::run(id, seed)`, with `admit(entry)`
   beside it. The conformance crate has test targets and no binary, so the two entry points are: the M3
   battery, which calls `run` for every scheduled record from the Parity tier,
   `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g2_parity -- anchor`; and, for a script or a
   person, the example target `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example anchors -- admit <entry>` and
   `… --example anchors -- run <id> [--seed S] [--json]`. An example target is neither a verb nor an `xtask`
   subcommand, the same form the gate recorder uses.) The steps of `run`:
   a. Build the scene and task from the record's `fixture` and the conditions the source states (the
      conditions of the `published` block); for each condition in `conditions_unstated` take its declared range.
   b. Simulate on the referee tier, and on the fastest tier that the fidelity ledger (BD-212: the frozen standard
      battery with per-quantity disagreement envelopes) promotes for this quantity, over a Latin-hypercube sweep
      of the unstated conditions and over the card fields' stated uncertainty (64 points by default, DESIGN).
   c. Result: the simulated quantity as an interval [min, max] with its median over the sweep; the published
      value; the gap, which is the signed difference between the published value and the nearest end of the
      interval (zero if inside); the relative difference; the conditions matched and assumed; the share of
      guessed card fields. The result is written into the record's `result` field.
   d. Outcome label (a finding, never a gate verdict): `inside-sweep` when |gap| ≤ `tolerance`, otherwise
      `outside-sweep(gap)`.
3. **The record.** A Parity record (claim kind Parity; evidence class Estimated because conditions are
   assumed; status `published-anchor`; tier and determinism class of the run; the anchor entry's hash) written
   to the ledger and attached to the reference robot's artifact graph.
4. **The line.** Every report statement that cites the comparison prints
   `reality: published-anchor [source=<anchor key>]`, followed by one sentence generated from the record:
   "Compared with a published figure (<citation>: <published value> <unit>); simulated <min> to <max> <unit>
   over <k> assumed conditions; <outcome>; published conditions are incomplete; this is a Parity result and
   says nothing about a band.", where <outcome> is `inside-sweep` or `outside-sweep by <gap> <unit>`. DESIGN:
   the sentence carries the interval and the outcome because a reader must see whether the two figures
   agreed, and a fixed wording such as "matches <source>" would be false on `outside-sweep`; this bead owns
   the sentence, and the M3 check of N38bp1-01 asserts this form. Statements of the same report that do not
   cite it keep `reality: no-claim`.
5. **No band, no card change.** A published figure is not an admitted dataset, so the reference robot has no
   measured unit in the Reality lane. A band, or a cell status, requested or cited for it is refused with
   `FROB-REF-895` (a band or Reality status for a design with no admitted dataset of origin `real` naming a
   physical unit of it; repair: `reality: no-claim` with the simulation's own interval; exit 3). At M3 the
   refusal comes from the reality guard of BD-139 (`reality::guard::check`, present from M1); from M5 the band
   verb returns the same code, because "the design has no built unit" is the first check of its authorization
   order (N30a-37), ahead of the uncalibrated-cell check (889). The comparison writes no card version: a
   published figure cannot give a card field the provenance `measured`, and no randomization width changes.
6. **Disagreement.** `outside-sweep` files a finding with both sides' pins. The model is not tuned toward the
   anchor until the disagreement has an explanation in a derivation file under `docs/derivations/`; if a card
   is later changed for a stated reason (a cited data sheet), that anchor is marked `consulted` for the new
   card version and may no longer be cited as independent of it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A report on a reference robot with an admitted anchor prints `reality: published-anchor` with its source,
      and issues no band (the seed bead's condition): a band or `calibrated` status cited for that design is
      refused by the reality guard with `FROB-REF-895` at M3 (unit test `band_after_anchor_refused_895`, and from
      the user's side N30a-04). From M5 `ground.band` for that design returns the same code; that call is a
      stage of N30a-04's script which the M5 band script N30a-39 runs, since this bead cannot wait on the band
      verb.
- [ ] The record's claim kind is Parity and no path can relabel it Reality: the status has no transition into
      any cell state (tested against the transition table of N30a-02).
- [ ] An anchor at `search-summary` level, or without a page or table reference, is refused at admission.
- [ ] The comparison reports an interval over the unstated conditions, not a single number, and lists every
      assumed condition.
- [ ] A planted disagreement (an anchor value moved outside the sweep by more than its tolerance) produces the
      `outside-sweep` finding with the gap, and no card version is written.
- [ ] `admit` and `run` work end to end on the synthetic point-mass anchor (planted inside and outside the
      sweep), through the example target and through the `g2_parity` battery. Entering the M3 records (at least
      one per reference robot that has a primary-source figure, a robot with none recorded as "no anchor"; a
      DESIGN target, since the plan fixes no count) and checking their stored results is N38bp1-01's, which
      depends on this bead.
- [ ] The report sentence names the citation, the published value, the simulated interval, the count of
      assumed conditions and the outcome label.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/anchors.rs`: `search_summary_anchor_refused`; `anchor_without_locator_refused`;
`anchor_hash_changes_with_value`; `sweep_interval_contains_every_run` (property);
`inside_and_outside_sweep_labels` (interval [0.82, 1.10], tolerance 0.05: published 0.95 is `inside-sweep`
with gap 0; published 1.13 has gap +0.03 and is `inside-sweep`; published 1.30 is `outside-sweep(+0.20)`);
`base_record_fields_load_unchanged` (a record with only N38b-01's base fields still loads, and is refused at
admission for its missing `read_level` and locator, not at load); `record_kind_is_parity_class_estimated`; `no_card_version_written`.
`crates/frob-ground/tests/anchor_status.rs`: `published_anchor_has_no_cell_transition`;
`band_after_anchor_refused_895` (a `calibrated` status cited for the anchored design is refused by the guard,
with the simulation's own interval in the repair); `line_prints_source`
(`reality: published-anchor [source=anchor:quad-ref-topspeed]`); `sentence_names_outcome` (on the interval
[0.82, 1.10] with one assumed condition, published 0.95 prints "simulated 0.82 to 1.10 … over 1 assumed
conditions; inside-sweep", published 1.30 prints "outside-sweep by +0.20").

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reality_anchor.sh` (bead N30a-04): admits a synthetic-fixture anchor and one real registry entry,
runs the comparison, generates the design report, asserts the line and the refused band. Logged: the anchor key
and hash, the interval, the published value, the gap, the outcome label, the evidence block (claim kind Parity,
class Estimated, tier, determinism, seed) and the refusal payload of the band request.

### Known answers and negative controls

No Appendix-M row. Fixture truth (`synthetic`, built for the test): a point-mass cart whose top speed on a
declared slope has a closed form; the "published" value is planted inside and then outside the sweep.
Negative controls: a report generator that prints `reality: calibrated` for an anchor comparison (must be
caught by the reader's check that a `calibrated` status names a score event); an admission path that accepts a
`search-summary` anchor; a comparison that tunes a card field to close the gap (caught by
`no_card_version_written`). Counterexample CX-335 applies: tier agreement on the reference robot is not
evidence that its cards are right. Determinism: the sweep is drawn by address from a recorded seed; twice on one
machine gives identical records.

## Considerations

- The value of an anchor is small and one-sided: a large gap is informative, a small one is weak evidence.
  The report sentence says so, and the share of guessed card fields (N30a-03) is printed beside it.
- Licence: quoting a published figure with citation is expected to be unproblematic; reference models carry
  their own licences (Appendix G), and an anchor whose model licence forbids redistribution is kept out of any
  replication kit.
- If no primary-source figure can be re-read for any reference robot, the bead closes on that record ("no
  anchor admitted", reports stay `no-claim`); that is a legitimate outcome and blocks nothing.
- An incumbent simulator that has itself been compared with hardware does not transfer that agreement: agreement
  with it is also Parity.
