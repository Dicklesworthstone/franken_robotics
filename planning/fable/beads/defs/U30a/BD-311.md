## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) owns the Reality claim. Nothing in the project has been compared with a physical
robot, so every report prints `reality: no-claim`. One cheap comparison is nevertheless available long before any
bench data: for a reference robot (an imported public model used as a parity fixture and benchmark baseline),
makers and papers publish figures such as a top speed, a cost of transport, a payload, a step height cleared or
a battery endurance. Simulating the same robot under the stated conditions and comparing is informative: a gross
disagreement points at a wrong card or a wrong import. It is scheduled by the conformance harness at M3 as one of
the program's mitigations for two risks: "calibration data never arrived" (RISK-43) and "common-mode model error:
every tier shares the same guessed cards" (RISK-32).

The comparison must not be mistaken for calibration. **Agreement with a published real-robot figure is Parity,
never Reality** (invariant INV-GAUNT-03; the claim table says the same in its Parity row). The reasons:
published test conditions are loose (surface, temperature, payload, firmware and controller are rarely all
stated); the figure is its authors' claim under their conditions; nothing was frozen before it was read; and no
scope (cell) can be formed from it. So the status is `published-anchor`, the report line names the source, and
**no band is issued**.

Rejected alternatives: treating a published figure as a one-point calibration set (it has no exchangeable
draws, so a conformal band from it is meaningless); tuning a card until the anchor matches (the anchor is then a
fit, and the disagreement it could have revealed is hidden); omitting the comparison because it proves nothing
(it is the only contact with reality the first releases have, and it is honest when labelled).

FACT about inputs: the reference-robot figures collected at planning time are at search-summary level and are
listed among things "not verified, and not relied on". Each must be re-read from its primary source before use.

## Scope

In: the anchor registry and its admission rule; the comparison run; the Parity record; the report line and the
no-band rule; the finding filed on disagreement. Out: the `reality:` line on every report and its negative
battery (BD-139); the typed Parity record and oracle hygiene (N38a-05); bands (N30a-36 onward); the share of
guessed card fields printed beside the line (N30a-03); the end-to-end script (N30a-04).

## Technical approach

1. **Anchor registry** `crates/frob-conformance/registry/anchors.toml` (DESIGN), one entry per figure:
   `key`; `robot` (reference-robot identity and the `ModelId` of its import); `quantity` (name, unit, and the
   metric definition of the task subsystem it maps to); `value` and, if stated, its spread; `source` (authors,
   title, venue or URL, and the page, table or figure); `read_on`; `read_level` (`primary` or
   `search-summary`); `conditions_stated` (key-value: surface, speed, payload, temperature, slope, controller);
   `conditions_unstated` (the list of conditions the simulation needs and the source does not give, each with
   the plausible range the comparison will sweep); `licence` (of the reference model and of quoting the
   figure); `captured_by` (a script under `tools/oracle/` that records the entry and its date).
   Admission: an entry with `read_level = "search-summary"`, with no page or table reference, or with a
   quantity that has no metric definition is refused. An anchor is never regenerated from the candidate and a
   pin moves only by a recorded event (the oracle rules of the conformance harness).
2. **The comparison** (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --bin anchors -- run <key>`; scheduled in the M3 battery).
   a. Build the scene and task from `conditions_stated`; for each unstated condition take its declared range.
   b. Simulate on the referee tier, and on the fastest tier that the fidelity ledger (BD-212: the frozen standard
      battery with per-quantity disagreement envelopes) promotes for this quantity, over a Latin-hypercube sweep
      of the unstated conditions and over the card fields' stated uncertainty (64 points by default, DESIGN).
   c. Result: the simulated quantity as an interval [min, max] with its median over the sweep; the published
      value; the signed difference between the published value and the nearest end of the interval (zero if
      inside); the relative difference; the conditions matched and assumed; the share of guessed card fields.
   d. Outcome label (a finding, never a gate verdict): `inside-sweep` or `outside-sweep(gap)`.
3. **The record.** A Parity record (claim kind Parity; evidence class Estimated because conditions are
   assumed; status `published-anchor`; tier and determinism class of the run; the anchor entry's hash) written
   to the ledger and attached to the reference robot's artifact graph.
4. **The line.** Every report statement that cites the comparison prints
   `reality: published-anchor [source=<anchor key>]`, followed by one sentence generated from the record:
   "Compared with a published figure (<citation>); published conditions are incomplete; this is a Parity
   result and says nothing about a band." Statements of the same report that do not cite it keep
   `reality: no-claim`.
5. **No band, no card change.** A band request for the reference robot is refused as for any uncalibrated cell
   (`FROB-REF-889`: a band for an uncalibrated cell, or with too few calibration points; repair: samples
   present and samples needed; exit 3). The comparison writes no card version: a published figure cannot give a
   card field the provenance `measured`, and no randomization width changes.
6. **Disagreement.** `outside-sweep` files a finding with both sides' pins. The model is not tuned toward the
   anchor until the disagreement has an explanation in a derivation file under `docs/derivations/`; if a card
   is later changed for a stated reason (a cited data sheet), that anchor is marked `consulted` for the new
   card version and may no longer be cited as independent of it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A report on a reference robot with an admitted anchor prints `reality: published-anchor` with its source,
      and issues no band (the seed bead's condition): `ground.band` for that design returns `FROB-REF-889`.
- [ ] The record's claim kind is Parity and no path can relabel it Reality: the status has no transition into
      any cell state (tested against the transition table of N30a-02).
- [ ] An anchor at `search-summary` level, or without a page or table reference, is refused at admission.
- [ ] The comparison reports an interval over the unstated conditions, not a single number, and lists every
      assumed condition.
- [ ] A planted disagreement (an anchor value moved outside the sweep) produces the `outside-sweep` finding
      with the gap, and no card version is written.
- [ ] At least one anchor per reference robot that has a primary-source figure is admitted and run at the M3
      exit; a robot with none is recorded as "no anchor" (DESIGN target, since the plan fixes no count).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/anchors.rs`: `search_summary_anchor_refused`; `anchor_without_locator_refused`;
`anchor_hash_changes_with_value`; `sweep_interval_contains_every_run` (property);
`inside_and_outside_sweep_labels` (interval [0.82, 1.10]: published 0.95 is `inside-sweep`; published 1.30 is
`outside-sweep(+0.20)`); `record_kind_is_parity_class_estimated`; `no_card_version_written`.
`crates/frob-ground/tests/anchor_status.rs`: `published_anchor_has_no_cell_transition`;
`band_request_after_anchor_refused_889`; `line_prints_source`
(`reality: published-anchor [source=anchor:quad-ref-topspeed]`).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reality_anchor.sh` (bead N30a-04): admits a synthetic-fixture anchor and one real registry entry,
runs the comparison, generates the design report, asserts the line and the refused band. Logged: the anchor key
and hash, the interval, the published value, the gap, the outcome label, the evidence block (claim kind Parity,
class Estimated, tier, determinism, seed) and the refusal payload of the band request.

### Known answers and negative controls

No Appendix-M row. Fixture truth (`synthetic`, built for the test): a point-mass cart whose top speed on a
declared slope has a closed form; the "published" value is planted inside and then outside the sweep.
Negative controls: a report generator that prints `reality: calibrated` for an anchor comparison (must be
caught by the reader's check that a `calibrated` status names a score event); an admission path that accepts a
`search-summary` anchor; a comparison that tunes a card field to close the gap (caught by
`no_card_version_written`). Counterexample CX-335 applies: tier agreement on the reference robot is not
evidence that its cards are right. Determinism: the sweep is drawn by address from a recorded seed; twice on one
machine gives identical records.

## Considerations

- The value of an anchor is small and one-sided: a large gap is informative, a small one is weak evidence.
  The report sentence says so, and the share of guessed card fields (N30a-03) is printed beside it.
- Licence: quoting a published figure with citation is expected to be unproblematic; reference models carry
  their own licences (Appendix G), and an anchor whose model licence forbids redistribution is kept out of any
  replication kit.
- If no primary-source figure can be re-read for any reference robot, the bead closes on that record ("no
  anchor admitted", reports stay `no-claim`); that is a legitimate outcome and blocks nothing.
- An incumbent simulator that has itself been compared with hardware does not transfer that agreement: agreement
  with it is also Parity.
