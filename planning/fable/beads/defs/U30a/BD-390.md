## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) fits model parameters (actuator friction, armature, delay, contact friction and so
on) to measured logs. Before any fit, step 2 of its procedure asks whether the planned experiment can determine
those parameters at all. Decision ADR-GROUND-04: identifiability is analysed before any fit, and a fit on an
unidentifiable combination is refused, never regularized quietly, because a regularized fit returns the prior
and labels it a measurement. The standing example: with drawbar force F = min(k_t·I/r, μ·N), runs that stay
torque-limited carry no information about the friction coefficient μ, yet a least-squares routine will happily
report one.

`ground.excite` is the verb for this step. It takes a design, a card set and a description of the rig, and
returns an identifiability report and an excitation plan. **It needs no data and it never returns a fit.** It
builds on analysis A-25 of the studio's analysis crate (sensitivity rank over a proposed excitation, with
coverage of each regime of a switching law, in closed form where the reading map allows), and adds what
calibration needs: instrument covariance with shared calibration error, a precision test against the prior, a
correlation test for aliases, a profile likelihood for finalists, a grammar of bench experiments and a greedy
selection under a time budget. It serves the program risk "a model fits nominal motion for the wrong physical
reason" (RISK-42) and the capability "experiments chosen by the decision they could change".

Tier: Frontier. The seed done-condition is GATE-GROUND-03. The gate register places it at M5 (the chapter says
M4; a gate cannot be due before its mechanism lands; it may be recorded at M4).

## Scope

In: the Fisher analysis and three of its four checks; assembly of the report; the verb. Out, each in its own
bead and a dependency of this one: the eight structural-alias fixtures and the fit guard (N30a-15); the
experiment grammar (N30a-16); greedy selection and decision relevance (N30a-17); the profile-likelihood check
and the model-class caution (N30a-18); the end-to-end script (N30a-19). Out: any fit (BD-391).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** `frob ground excite --design d:… --cards <set> --rig rig.toml --params <names|stage> [--budget-s
   B] [--decision partition.toml] [--seed S] --json`. The rig file (`frob.ground.rig/1`, DESIGN) lists
   instruments with `sigma`, `bias_bound`, `calibration`, `instant` and `clock` (the dataset manifest's fields),
   the clocks and the shared marks available, known payloads, which experiments of the grammar the rig can run
   and its safety envelope. Each parameter θ_i has a prior mean and standard deviation σ_i from its card field.
2. **Predicted measurements.** For a candidate experiment e, y_e(θ) is the vector of noise-free readings the
   rig's instruments would record, computed by simulating the experiment on the referee tier in double
   precision through the sensor models, at the instants the instruments declare.
3. **Jacobian.** J_e = ∂y_e/∂θ at the prior mean and at eight prior draws (TARGET; draws by address from the
   seed). Central differences with step 10⁻³·σ_i (DESIGN) on the referee; where the regime enumeration of A-25
   shows a switch between the two evaluation points, the two one-sided differences are reported and no
   central difference across the switch is used. Closed-form reading maps use exact derivatives.
4. **Scaling and Fisher information.** R is the instrument covariance including shared calibration error
   (N30a-08). With D = diag(σ_i), the whitened scaled Jacobian is J̃ = R^(−1/2)·J·D, and
   F = Jᵀ·R⁻¹·J in scaled parameters is J̃ᵀ·J̃, dimensionless. For a plan (a set of experiments) F is the sum
   over its experiments.
5. **The four checks** (thresholds are TARGETs, recorded in the contract).

   | Check | Rule | Outcome |
   |---|---|---|
   | Rank | A singular direction of the scaled J with σ_min/σ_max below 10⁻⁶ | Structurally unidentified in this experiment |
   | Precision | The Cramér–Rao bound √((F⁻¹)_ii) is at most half the prior standard deviation (0.5 in scaled units) | Parameter i is identified by the experiment |
   | Aliasing | A pair with correlation of magnitude at least 0.95 in F⁻¹ | Reported as an aliased combination, with the combination that is identified |
   | Profile | A profile likelihood on the synthetic twin of the experiment, for finalists | A flat profile overrides a good local bound (N30a-18) |

   Details. Rank: singular value decomposition of J̃; a direction below threshold at all nine evaluation points
   is `structurally-unidentified`; below at some is `locally-unidentified(k of 9)` and is treated as
   unidentified by the fit guard unless the profile check clears it. On a rank-deficient F the bound is
   computed on the identified subspace (pseudo-inverse); a parameter with a component in the null space has an
   infinite bound. Aliasing: ρ_ij = (F⁻¹)_ij/√((F⁻¹)_ii·(F⁻¹)_jj); for a flagged pair the report gives the
   well-determined combination (the eigenvector of the pair's 2×2 information block with the large eigenvalue,
   rescaled to physical units, for example "I_link + N²·J_r") and the poorly determined one.
6. **The report** (schema `frob.ground.excite/1`, DESIGN): per parameter: prior standard deviation, predicted
   bound, `identified | not-identified | structurally-unidentified | locally-unidentified`; the singular
   values; unidentified directions as combinations; aliased pairs with ρ, the identified combination and **the
   experiment that would separate each** (from the alias table of N30a-15 and by trying each grammar
   experiment as an addition); the selected plan in order with durations and the objective after each pick
   (N30a-17); the cautions in force (N30a-18); and the recommendation: a plan, "measure the decision quantity
   directly", or "measure nothing".
7. **Verb properties.** Kind `read`; cluster `ground`; long runs take the bounded synchronous wait and
   otherwise return a job handle (exit 8). Exit 0 when every requested parameter is identified by the plan, 2
   when anything stays unidentified or aliased (a finding, with repairs). Evidence: class Estimated, tier
   referee, determinism per platform; `reality: no-claim`. Nothing is written to any card.
8. **The guard interface.** `excite::assess(param_set, excitation) -> Assessment` is the function the
   identification verb calls on the excitation actually present in a dataset; a parameter set that fails is
   refused there with `FROB-REF-879` (a fit on a parameter set that fails identifiability; repair offered: the
   aliased combination and the separating experiment; exit 3).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-GROUND-03 (TARGET): the eight structural aliases are flagged, 8 of 8, each with its identified
      combination and separating experiment, and no fit is issued on an aliased set (recorded with measured
      values by N30a-19).
- [ ] The drawbar case: for runs (1 A, 30 N) and (2 A, 40 N) the column of μ in J is zero and μ is
      `structurally-unidentified`; adding (3 A, 10 N) makes μ identified.
- [ ] On a well-excited single-joint plan every requested parameter has a predicted bound at most half its
      prior standard deviation, and the later fit on rehearsal data achieves errors consistent with the bound.
- [ ] The verb returns no posterior, writes no card, and its schema has no field that could hold a fitted value.
- [ ] J agrees with exact derivatives on the closed-form fixtures to 10⁻⁶ relative; no central difference
      straddles a regime switch.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob ground excite --json` and the agent surface; cancellation mid-run leaves no
      partial report; the crate contract documents thresholds 10⁻⁶, 0.5 and 0.95 as TARGETs.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/excite_analysis.rs`: `fisher_is_dimensionless_under_unit_change` (metres to
millimetres leaves F unchanged); `rank_threshold` (σ_min/σ_max = 10⁻⁷ flagged, 10⁻³ not);
`precision_check_half_prior` (one parameter, one reading with sensitivity 1 and σ = 0.4·σ_prior: identified;
σ = 0.6·σ_prior: not); `alias_correlation_threshold` (two columns with cosine 0.99 flagged, 0.5 not, and the
identified combination returned is their sum direction); `pseudo_inverse_bound_on_identified_subspace`;
`shared_calibration_lowers_information` (the same J with and without a common-mode group);
`locally_unidentified_counts_points`; `no_difference_across_switch`; `report_has_no_fit_fields`;
`plan_information_is_sum_of_experiments`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_excite.sh` (bead N30a-19): `frob ground excite` on the eight alias rigs, the drawbar rig
and a well-excited rig. Logged per run: singular values, per-parameter bounds against 0.5, alias pairs with ρ,
the separating experiments, the plan, the exit code and the evidence block.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-722 (the eight aliases, 8 of 8, with the identified combination named; control: a fit request on each is
refused) and KA-723 (3.33 N and 6.67 N at 1 A and 2 A under 30 N and 40 N for μ = 0.4 and 0.8; 4 N against 8 N
at 3 A under 10 N; control: the torque-limited data alone yields no μ). Counterexamples CX-328 (a good local
fit identifies its parameters) and CX-329 (friction can be identified from any traction run). Negative
controls registered: an analysis with the rank check removed; an `identify` build that ignores the guard.
Metamorphic: permuting parameters permutes the report; duplicating an experiment doubles F. Determinism: twice
on one machine bit for bit; across machines inside the referee's recorded tolerance.

## Considerations

- The analysis is local (a Jacobian at nine points) and assumes the declared law class. A flat profile
  overrides a good local bound, and a probe that is perfect inside its law class can be wrong outside it; both
  are handled in N30a-18 and the rehearsal always contains an omitted-law control.
- Kill gate KILL-GROUND (decided at M5 if the identification gate fails after one repair cycle): no
  identification; a card field then changes only by direct bench measurement of that field. `ground.excite`
  remains useful in that fallback as the tool that says which direct measurement is needed.
- The chapter's verb table cites a refusal number (842) that the refusal registry assigns to another subsystem;
  this bead uses the registry's code for the fit guard (879) and the schema refusal for malformed input.
- Cost: nine Jacobian evaluations of p parameters over the candidate experiments on the referee; seconds to
  minutes for a single joint, which is why the verb may return a job handle.
