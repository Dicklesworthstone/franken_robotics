## Background

Every simulation tier (the referee, the CPU batch tier, both GPU tiers) reads the same actuator card. If the card
is wrong, all tiers are wrong together, and their agreement says nothing about it. Fidelity results (a fast tier
agrees with a trusted one) and Parity results (agreement with an oracle) therefore cannot show that the card
describes a real motor. Through the fourth milestone the catalogue holds generic classes only: small, medium and
large quasi-direct-drive, hobby servo, strain-wave and hub motor, all marked `generic`; real parts enter only
with a cited data sheet (open question OPEN-SINEW-1).

Every card field carries a provenance, one of `datasheet` (with a citation), `measured`, `generic`, `assumed`, and
an uncertainty. The decision here (DESIGN): a report that supports a benchmark claim prints **the share of card
fields whose provenance is `generic` or `assumed`**, because it is the one number that tells a reader how much of
the model was guessed. It is the leading indicator of the program risk "common-mode model error: tiers agree with
each other and disagree with reality, because they share cards" (RISK-32, rated High, owner: the actuator lead).

Rejected alternatives: a qualitative label ("generic model"), which hides whether 5% or 95% of the fields were
guessed; printing provenance only in the card listing (`actuator.list` already does), which a reader of a
benchmark report never opens.

## Scope

In: the count over the cards bound into a model; the report line and envelope field; the rule for which reports
carry it; the trend record. Out: per-field provenance storage (BD-069); randomization over the stated
uncertainty and the `card-conditional` label of the sensitivity analysis; the refusal of verdicts on `assumed`
fields under `--strict-data` (the actuator and analysis beads); the `reality:` line itself (BD-139, N30a-02).

## Technical approach

1. **The count.** `frob_ground::guess_share(model: &Model) -> GuessShare` walks every card bound into the
   model identity (actuator, transmission, battery, interface, material, soil, optical, sensor, payload: the
   closed list of card kinds) and every field that carries a provenance. Result (DESIGN):
   `GuessShare { total, generic, assumed, datasheet, measured_admitted, measured_candidate, by_kind: Map<CardKind,
   Counts>, by_joint: Vec<(JointId, Counts)> }`. The headline share is (generic + assumed)/total as an exact
   rational, printed as a percentage with one decimal. A field with provenance `measured` whose card version has
   not been admitted by a scored prediction counts as `measured_candidate`, and is reported in its own column:
   it is neither a guess nor evidence yet.
2. **Weighting.** The headline is unweighted by design (one field, one count): a weighting by sensitivity would
   itself be a model output. The per-kind and per-joint breakdown lets a reader see where the guesses sit.
3. **The line.** Directly under the `reality:` line:
   `cards: guessed=<g>/<N> (<pct>%) [generic=<a> assumed=<b> datasheet=<c> measured=<d> candidate=<e>]`.
   The envelope carries the same numbers in `evidence.cards`.
4. **Which reports.** Every report whose statements include a claim of kind Design comparison, Fidelity, Parity
   or Performance about a design (design, run, campaign and mission reports; scorecards; the design benchmark;
   the published-anchor comparison of BD-311). The report generator refuses to render such a statement when
   the model's share cannot be computed (a card missing a provenance is already refused at card admission).
5. **Trend.** The share of each benchmark design is written to the ledger with the benchmark's identity, so that
   the risk register's indicator ("the share of `assumed` and `generic` card fields in benchmark designs") is a
   query, not a manual count.
6. **Determinism and class.** Exact counting, class D0; evidence class `Tested` is not claimed: the line states
   provenance, not correctness.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For a fixture model with three cards whose fields are 10 `generic`, 4 `assumed`, 5 `datasheet` and 1
      `measured` (admitted), the line reads `cards: guessed=14/20 (70.0%) [generic=10 assumed=4 datasheet=5
      measured=1 candidate=0]`.
- [ ] Binding a candidate card version in which 3 formerly `generic` fields are `measured` gives
      `guessed=11/20 (55.0%)` with `candidate=3` and `measured=1`.
- [ ] Every report kind listed in step 4 prints the line; a report generator mutant that drops it fails the
      report-schema test.
- [ ] For a starter-catalogue design in which every card field is `generic`, the share is 100.0%, and the report
      says so in words: "every card parameter in this model is a generic class value".
- [ ] The ledger query returns the share for each design of the design benchmark.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/guess_share.rs`: `counts_three_card_fixture` (the 14/20 case); `candidate_counted_apart`
(the 11/20 case); `share_is_exact_rational` (1/3 prints as 33.3%, and the rational is 1/3);
`empty_model_has_zero_total_and_no_line` (a model with no cards prints `cards: none`); `by_kind_sums_to_total`
(property over random models); `line_roundtrip` (print then parse).
`crates/frob-chronicle/tests/report_lines.rs` (the report generator's crate): `benchmark_report_has_cards_line`;
`report_without_design_claim_omits_line`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reality_anchor.sh` (bead N30a-04) generates a design report and a benchmark scorecard for a
starter-catalogue design and asserts both lines. Logged: the five counts, the share as a rational and as a
percentage, and the report identity.

### Known answers and negative controls

Counterexample CX-335 (agreement between tiers shows the model is right: every tier consumes the same actuator
card, so Fidelity and Parity results say nothing about whether the card describes a real motor) and CX-143 (the
same statement for the referee and the fast tiers). The test built from them: a fixture in which the referee
and the batch tier agree to 10⁻⁹ on a model whose cards are 100% `generic` must still print 100% guessed and
`reality: no-claim`. Negative controls: a counter that treats `generic` as `datasheet` (the 14/20 test fails);
a report generator that drops the line. Determinism: D0; twice, debug and release.

## Considerations

- The plan says "a report that supports a benchmark claim"; the list in step 4 is this bead's reading of it
  (DESIGN) and errs toward printing the line more often.
- Three defences exist against a wrong card and none is a proof: randomization draws each field from its stated
  uncertainty; a verdict that changes sign inside that band is labelled card-conditional; under `--strict-data`
  a verdict resting on an `assumed` field is refused. The share printed here is the summary of how much those
  defences are carrying.
- The number can be gamed by citing a data sheet for a field the data sheet does not state; card admission
  requires the citation, and the published-anchor comparison (BD-311) and bench identification are the checks
  that reach further.
