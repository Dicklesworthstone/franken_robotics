## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) owns the Reality claim, and until measured data exists every report prints
`reality: no-claim`. Two things short of measurement are shown to a reader from the third milestone: a
comparison of a simulated quantity with a figure published for the same reference robot, recorded as a Parity
result with status `published-anchor` (never as Reality, because published test conditions are loose), and the
share of card fields whose provenance is `generic` or `assumed` (how much of the model was guessed). This script
is the user-side proof that both are printed correctly, that the anchor's source is named, and that no reality
band can be obtained from an anchor.

## Scope

In: the script, its synthetic anchor fixture, its assertions and log. Out: the comparison (BD-311); the share
(N30a-03); the `reality:` line on every report kind and its 50-misuse battery (BD-139).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/reality_anchor.sh [--seed S] [--controls] [--stage <name>]`, plain shell over the `frob`
binary and the
conformance crate's example target `anchors` (the crate has no binary; BD-311 states the two entry points),
working under `target/e2e-work/reality_anchor/`, idempotent and seed-explicit (default
seed 7). `--stage <name>` runs the fixture steps and one named stage only (`no-band` is the one a later
script calls).

1. **Fixture.** A point-mass cart design on a 5% slope whose top speed has a closed form from its card
   (`synthetic` anchor `anchor:test-cart-topspeed`, read level `primary`, with a planted "published" value and
   one unstated condition, the payload, swept from 0 to 2 kg). Also one entry of the real registry if any is
   admitted (otherwise that branch is logged `not run: no primary-source anchor`).
2. **Admission.** `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example anchors -- admit <entry>`: assert success for the
   fixture; assert refusal for a copy with `read_level = "search-summary"` and for a copy with no page or table
   reference.
3. **Run.** `… --example anchors -- run anchor:test-cart-topspeed --seed S --json`: assert claim kind `parity`, class
   `Estimated`, status `published-anchor`, an interval with min ≤ median ≤ max that contains the closed-form
   value at payload 1 kg, the list of assumed conditions (`payload_kg`), and outcome `inside-sweep`.
4. **Report.** `frob report design test-cart --json`: assert the statement citing the comparison prints
   `reality: published-anchor [source=anchor:test-cart-topspeed]` and BD-311's sentence with the citation,
   the published value, the simulated interval, one assumed condition and the outcome `inside-sweep`; every
   other statement prints `reality: no-claim`; the report prints
   `cards: guessed=<g>/<N> (<pct>%) […]` with the counts the script computes itself from `frob actuator show`.
5. **No band.** A published figure is not an admitted dataset, so the design has no measured unit and the
   refusal is `FROB-REF-895` (a band for a design with no built and measured unit; repair: `reality: no-claim`
   with the simulation's own interval), the first check of the band authorization order, ahead of 889. At M3:
   take the report's anchor statement, edit it to read `reality: calibrated [cell=… n=20 level=0.90 band=…
   dataset=…]`, plant it in the scratch store as an envelope offered as a citation with the misuse planter of
   BD-139's battery (N30b-01; the form of its row 2), and cite it: `frob report design test-cart --cite
   <planted id> --json`. Every report renderer runs the reality guard of BD-139 on a cited input: assert exit
   3 and `FROB-REF-895`. (An earlier text read the edited statement back with `frob artifact read`, which takes
   a stored digest and cannot be handed edited content.) Assert also that the unedited report contains no
   `band=` field anywhere. Stage `no-band` (M5): `frob ground band --design test-cart --task-family <the cart's
   task family> --metric top_speed_m_s --level 0.90 --json` (the verb's design form with every required
   input, so that the answer is the authorization order's and not a schema refusal): assert exit 3,
   `FROB-REF-895`, and a repair carrying the simulation's own interval. Until the band verb is in the build
   (`frob capabilities --json` lists it absent) the stage is logged `not run` with that reason; from M5 the
   band script N30a-39 runs it (`scripts/e2e/reality_anchor.sh --stage no-band`), so this bead never waits
   on the band verb.
6. **Disagreement.** Re-admit the anchor with the published value moved 20% above the sweep maximum; assert
   outcome `outside-sweep` with the gap, a filed finding, and that `frob actuator show` returns the same card
   identities as before (no card version written).
7. **Controls (`--controls`).** Conformance-only builds on which the script must exit non-zero: a report
   generator that prints `reality: calibrated` for the anchor statement; a report generator that omits the
   `cards:` line; an admission that accepts `search-summary`.
8. **Determinism.** Steps 3 and 4 twice on fresh stores give byte-identical records and report bytes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All eight steps pass on both development machines; exit code 0, and non-zero under each control build.
- [ ] The report line carries the status and the source exactly as specified, and no `band=` field appears.
- [ ] The guessed-field counts in the report equal the script's independent count.
- [ ] A `calibrated` status planted on the anchor statement and cited from the design report is refused by the
      reality guard with 895 at M3; stage `no-band` exists and, while the band verb is absent from the build,
      logs `not run` with its reason (its M5 run belongs to N30a-39).
- [ ] The log holds every command, exit code, evidence block and measured value named below, and a `summary`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own (BD-311 and N30a-03 hold them). The fixture generator has one test in the conformance crate:
`anchor_fixture_closed_form` (top speed from the card's torque-speed line and the slope's resistance equals the
script's expected value to 10⁻⁹).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Log `target/e2e-logs/reality_anchor/<utc>.ndjson` through the helper of `STD-E2E`,
with a transcript beside it. Per command a `cmd` line (exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's
evidence block: claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). `metric` lines: interval min, median, max
with unit; published value; gap; outcome label; the provenance counts and the share as a rational. A
`refusal` line for the planted status and for the band request with code, pointer, observed and required values
and the first repair. On failure:
the first differing value and the paths of the anchor entry, the record and the report. Final `summary`:
assertions passed and failed, steps `not run`, gates touched (none).

### Known answers and negative controls

The closed-form top speed of the fixture; the planted inside and outside values. Negative controls: the three
control builds of step 7. Counterexample CX-335 (tier agreement is not evidence about the card) is exercised by
asserting that the referee and batch-tier runs of the fixture agree while the report still prints 100% guessed
fields and no Reality status. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release.

## Considerations

- The real-registry branch depends on someone re-reading a figure from its primary source; the script does not
  fail when none exists, because "no anchor admitted" is a legitimate recorded state.
- The script must not print any text of a licensed source beyond its citation.
- When bands exist (M5), stage `no-band`, run by N30a-39, is the regression test that an anchor never licenses
  one.
- The reality guard's codes are raised by report renderers and artifact readers of every cluster, but the verb
  rows of those readers (here `report.design`) do not yet list 895; requested of NJL-01 as a field-driven
  append, so that the refusal list rule holds.
