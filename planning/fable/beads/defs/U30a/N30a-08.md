## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) admits real data (logs from a robot or a bench rig, single bench measurements,
scans) and fits model parameters to it. Every fit and every identifiability analysis needs the covariance R of
the measurement errors. The admission rule makes measurement uncertainty a required field: a dataset manifest
(`frob.ground.dataset/1`) lists each instrument with its quantity, unit, per-sample standard deviation `sigma`,
a bound on its systematic error `bias_bound`, the instant its timestamp denotes, its clock, and the identity of
its calibration, for example:

```toml
[[instrument]]
name = "encoder-knee-L"; quantity = "angle"; unit = "rad"; sigma = 1.5e-4; bias_bound = 3.0e-4
instant = "sample"; clock = "mcu"; calibration = "cal-2027-02"
```

The point that is easy to get wrong: **samples that share a calibration share an error**. A thousand encoder
samples that share one bias do not count as a thousand independent measurements; averaging them shrinks the
per-sample noise and leaves the bias. A fit that treats R as diagonal reports an uncertainty that falls like
1/√n without limit, and a card written from it claims more than was measured. The sibling estimator
`fs-assimilate` models this as a declared common-mode source (module `groups`), so repeated samples cannot push
uncertainty below the common-mode floor; Ground must build those groups from the manifest.

Rejected alternatives: a diagonal R with inflated sigma (wrong shape: it under-weights single clean samples and
still over-counts long traces); estimating the bias as a free parameter in every fit (it aliases with the
constant terms the fit is after, such as a bias torque).

## Scope

In: the instrument record as a typed measurement model; construction of R with common-mode groups; the
contribution of time-alignment uncertainty; single bench measurements as observations; unit and dimension
checks. Out: refusing a manifest that lacks the fields (BD-387, refusal 873); clock fitting (N30a-11); the
estimators (N30a-05); the discrepancy floor from model form, which is a different floor (N30a-29).

## Technical approach

1. **Types** (DESIGN).
   ```rust
   pub struct Instrument { name, quantity: QuantityKind, unit: Unit, sigma: Qty, bias_bound: Qty,
                           instant: Instant, clock: ClockName, calibration: CalibrationId,
                           resolution: Option<Qty>, bias_sigma: Option<Qty> }
   pub struct MeasurementRow { instrument: InstrumentRef, t_src: RawTime, value: Qty, window: Option<WindowRef> }
   pub struct NoiseModel { diag: Vec<f64>, groups: Vec<CommonMode { members: Vec<RowIdx>, sigma_c: f64,
                           source: CalibrationId }> }
   ```
2. **Error model.** A reading is y_k = h_k(θ) + e_k + c_g(k), with e_k independent, variance σ_k², and c_g one
   draw per calibration identity g shared by every row measured through that calibration. Then
   R = diag(σ_k²) + Σ_g σ_c,g²·1_g·1_gᵀ, where 1_g is the indicator vector of group g. Rows of two different
   instruments that name the same `calibration` are in one group (two encoders zeroed on one fixture).
3. **From the manifest to numbers.** σ_k = `sigma`, plus quantization q²/12 added in variance when a
   `resolution` q is declared. σ_c = `bias_sigma` when the manifest declares it (minor version 1 of the schema,
   N30ax-01; the validator already requires bias_sigma ≤ bias_bound), otherwise σ_c = `bias_bound`/√3 for
   Gaussian machinery (the standard deviation of an error uniform on ±bound; DESIGN), while interval and
   worst-case computations use the bound itself in both cases. A `bias_bound`
   of zero must be written explicitly; a missing one was already refused at admission.
4. **Windows.** When a trace enters a fit as windows of summary features (the estimator admits at most 256
   rows), a feature that is a mean of m samples of one instrument has independent variance σ²/m and keeps the
   full σ_c² in its group: var = σ²/m + σ_c². A feature that is a difference of two samples of one instrument
   (a position change) cancels the common-mode term and has variance 2·σ²; the builder derives each feature's
   group coefficients from its linear weights on the raw samples (coefficient = Σ weights), so that
   cancellation is computed, not assumed.
5. **Time-alignment uncertainty.** For a channel sampled in time whose aligned instant has uncertainty σ_t (from
   the clock fit), the builder adds (ẏ·σ_t)² to the row's variance, with ẏ the local slope of the measured
   signal, and adds one common-mode group per clock for the shared part of the alignment error (the offset is
   shared by every sample on that clock).
6. **Bench measurements** (`kind = "bench"`): one row per measurement of one quantity (a mass, a length, a
   period), with the same fields. They enter the same estimators as direct observations of one parameter.
7. **Hand-off.** `NoiseModel::to_assimilate()` emits the `groups` declaration (at most 64 shared parameters; more
   is a typed error naming the cap) and the diagonal; `NoiseModel::dense()` returns R for the Fisher analysis
   (BD-390), with R⁻¹ applied through the Woodbury identity so that a long trace does not need a dense inverse.
8. **Dimension checks.** `quantity` and `unit` must agree with the dimension of the model output the row is
   compared with; a mismatch is a typed refusal at model-binding time (an angle compared with a current).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For n samples with per-sample σ sharing one calibration with common-mode σ_c, the variance of their mean
      computed from R is σ²/n + σ_c², exactly (tested at n = 1, 10, 1,000).
- [ ] A difference of two samples of one instrument has variance 2·σ² with no common-mode term.
- [ ] Two instruments naming one `calibration` are correlated through R; two naming different ones are not.
- [ ] `to_assimilate()` reproduces the dense R on random small problems (differential test against the dense
      form to 10⁻¹²), and refuses 65 groups with the cap named.
- [ ] A row whose unit dimension does not match the model output is refused before any fit.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/instrument.rs`: `mean_of_n_has_floor` (σ = 1.5·10⁻⁴ rad, σ_c = 3.0·10⁻⁴/√3 =
1.732·10⁻⁴ rad: at n = 1,000 the standard deviation of the mean is 1.733·10⁻⁴ rad, not 4.7·10⁻⁶);
`difference_cancels_common_mode`; `bias_sigma_overrides_uniform_rule` (bias_bound 3.0·10⁻⁴ rad with
bias_sigma 1.0·10⁻⁴ rad gives σ_c = 1.0·10⁻⁴ rad, while the worst-case interval still uses ±3.0·10⁻⁴ rad);
`shared_calibration_correlates_instruments` (correlation
σ_c²/(σ² + σ_c²) = 0.571 between single samples); `quantization_adds_q2_over_12`;
`window_feature_group_coefficient_is_weight_sum`; `time_uncertainty_term` (slope 2 rad/s, σ_t = 50 µs adds
(10⁻⁴ rad)²); `woodbury_matches_dense` (property); `groups_cap_64`; `dimension_mismatch_refused`;
`bench_row_is_direct_observation`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Exercised by `scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh` (N30a-26): the synthetic rig's encoder samples share one planted
bias; the script asserts that the reported posterior standard deviation of the bias-torque term does not fall
below the planted floor as the trace length is doubled twice. Logged as `metric` lines: n, the reported
standard deviation and the floor.

### Known answers and negative controls

The closed forms above are the known answers. Negative control: a noise model built with the groups dropped
(diagonal R) must fail `mean_of_n_has_floor` and, end to end, must make the bias-torque uncertainty fall below
the planted floor (the script's control build). Determinism: exact arithmetic for group structure; double
precision elsewhere, identical twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- σ_c = bound/√3 is a modelling choice and only the default. An operator who knows the bias distribution
  states `bias_sigma` directly (step 3); if both are present the manifest validator requires
  bias_sigma ≤ bias_bound.
- A calibration identity that changes between sessions is itself a scope-change trigger for a calibrated cell
  ("an instrument's calibration identity changes"); the identity carried here is what that trigger compares.
- The common-mode floor here is instrument error. The discrepancy floor of a card (variance that validation
  residuals attribute to model form) is separate and is applied after scoring; the two are never merged.
- The timestamp instant is carried because a fit that compares a model's value at the sample instant with a
  reading stamped at arrival absorbs transport latency into actuator delay.
