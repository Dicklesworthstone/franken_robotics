## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) calibrates simulation against measured reality through a fixed procedure of ten
steps. Each step has one verb, one output, and one state it **fails closed to**: what holds if the step refuses
or cannot run. The order is not a convention: each stage's error aliases into the next (a clock error becomes an
actuator delay; an actuator error becomes a contact parameter), and a comparison made before its prediction was
frozen is a fit with an unknown error rate. If real traces are unavailable, the release ships simulation and
known-answer results with the Reality field open; that is the expected state of the first release.

| Step | What happens | Verb | Output | Fails closed to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Admit data, assign the split, seal validation and holdout | `ground.ingest` | Dataset id | Refusal |
| 1 | Fit clocks | `ground.ingest` | Affine maps with uncertainty | Refusal; no time-based score |
| 2 | Analyse identifiability, design excitation | `ground.excite` | Experiment plan, aliased combinations | No fit |
| 3 | Identify actuators | `ground.identify --stage actuators` | Candidate posterior | Prior card |
| 4 | Identify contact and surface, then inertia and geometry, then sensors | `ground.identify --stage …` | Candidate posterior | Prior card |
| 5 | Write candidate card versions | `ground.identify` | `.fcard` versions | — |
| 6 | Freeze a prediction for a planned trial | `ground.predict` | Prediction record | — |
| 7 | Score on sealed data, retire the holdout, admit or reject the posterior | `ground.score` | Score, coverage, admitted cards | Candidate stays candidate |
| 8 | Issue or refuse a band for the cell | `ground.band` | Band or refusal | `no-claim` |
| 9 | Monitor drift, retire | Automatic on ingest | Monitor state | `retired` |
| 10 | Feed discrepancy back | Automatic | Reality-gap variance per cell; regression cases | — |

Without a shared record of where a cell stands, each verb would re-derive its preconditions, and one of them
would eventually let a step run out of order. This bead is that record, and it owns step 10, which no verb owns.

## Scope

In: the per-cell procedure state derived from the ledger; the precondition check every Ground verb calls; the
fail-closed outcome of each step; step 10 (the reality-gap variance per cell and the regression cases). Out: the
mechanisms of steps 0 to 9 (their own beads); the experiment table that reads σ_r²; the status line (N30a-02).

## Technical approach

1. **State** (DESIGN). `ProcedureState { cell: CellId, lineage: ModelLineage, steps: [StepState; 11] }`, with
   `StepState = NotRun | Done { artifact, ledger_seq } | FailedClosed { refusal, fallback } | Stale { cause }`.
   It is not stored: `procedure::state(cell, lineage)` folds the ledger's Ground events (`ground.sealed`,
   `ground.clock`, `ground.excite`, `ground.candidate`, `ground.frozen`, `ground.scored`, `ground.band`,
   `ground.status`, `ground.feedback`) in sequence order, so it cannot disagree with the record.
2. **Preconditions** (`procedure::require(cell, step) -> Result<Inputs, Refusal>`):
   - step 1 needs step 0; steps 3 and 4 need step 1 done for every clock their traces use (or the data is
     scored on time-free metrics only) and a step-2 report for the stage's parameter set with no unresolved
     alias among the parameters to be fitted (otherwise refusal 879, raised by the fit guard);
   - step 4's sub-stages run in order (contact and surface, inertia and geometry, sensors), and each needs the
     previous stage either done or explicitly declared "prior kept" in the call;
   - step 6 needs the cards it names to exist (candidate or prior) and the cell to be declared;
   - step 7 needs a step-6 record whose ledger sequence precedes the read (refusal 883 otherwise) and an unspent
     holdout (refusal 886 otherwise);
   - step 8 needs step 7 done with coverage for this cell and lineage, and no retirement since (refusals 889,
     897); step 9 runs only for cells whose step 8 is done.
3. **Fail-closed outcomes**, asserted by test for every row: a refused admission leaves no dataset; a refused
   clock fit leaves the dataset admitted and marks time-based metrics unavailable for scoring; a failed
   identifiability check leaves no fit; a refused or failed identification leaves the prior card in force; a
   prediction that did not cover leaves the candidate a candidate (it is then rejected, never half-admitted); a
   refused band leaves `reality: no-claim`; a fired monitor leaves `retired`.
4. **Staleness.** A new card version (a new model identity) makes steps 6 to 9 of the old lineage `Stale` for
   the new one: evidence attached to the old identity does not carry over. A scope change makes step 8 and 9
   `Stale` with the cause.
5. **Step 10a: the reality-gap variance σ_r² per cell and metric.** After each scoring event, from the scored
   pairs (ŷ_i, y_i) of the cell: σ_r² = max(0, mean((y_i − ŷ_i)²) − σ_inst² − σ_sim²), where σ_inst² is the
   instrument variance of the metric (N30a-08) and σ_sim² the sampling variance of the simulated prediction; it
   is reported with its count n and stays labelled `candidate` until n reaches the band's minimum count. For a
   cell with no scored pair, σ_r² is a declared prior per task family with provenance `assumed` (the plan's
   illustration uses σ_r = 0.10 for an uncalibrated cell against 0.02 for a calibrated one, in normalized score
   units). The value is written as ledger event `ground.feedback` and exposed to the experiment-value model as
   the third variance component of a score difference (sampling, tier bias, reality gap). It never enters a
   verdict.
6. **Step 10b: regression cases.** Each of the following is written to the divergence ledger with kind
   `reality`, the scene, task, recorded conditions draw, model identity, world address of the simulated side,
   the dataset and chunk hashes of the real side, and expected against observed: a scored trial outside its
   registered interval; a pair of designs whose real ranking reversed the simulated one (`ranking-falsified`);
   a fit marked `wrong-reason`; every retirement with its trigger. A case from a private dataset stores hashes
   and the `CellId` only. Later studies and the conformance battery replay the simulated side of every case.
7. **Surface.** `frob ground band --status --json` lists, per cell and lineage, the eleven step states with
   artifacts, the current reality status and σ_r² with n. (The cluster is at its limit of seven verbs, so the
   listing lives in the read verb.)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For a synthetic cell taken through all ten steps, `state` returns `Done` for steps 0 to 10 with ledger
      sequences strictly increasing in the order 0, 1, 2, 3–5, 6, 7, 8.
- [ ] Each out-of-order call is refused by `require` with the code listed in step 2 above, and the refusal
      names the missing step.
- [ ] Each fail-closed row holds on a planted failure (seven cases).
- [ ] A new card version marks the old lineage's steps 6 to 9 stale for the new lineage.
- [ ] σ_r² matches the hand value on a fixture (below), and a cell with no scored pair reports the `assumed`
      prior, labelled as such.
- [ ] Each of the four kinds of regression case appears in the divergence ledger and replays.

`private_case_stores_hashes_only`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_procedure.sh` (bead N30a-10) drives all ten steps on the synthetic rehearsal and each
out-of-order call, and logs per step the verb, the artifact identity, the ledger sequence and the fail-closed
state where one was planted.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-734 (read before freeze, refit without a new version, holdout reuse: each refused) is reached through
`require`. Negative controls: a `require` that skips the step-2 check (the alias test fails); a state function
that reads a cached copy and not the ledger (a planted cache that disagrees must be detected). Determinism:
class D0 for the state; σ_r² in double precision, identical twice.

## Considerations

- The reality-gap variance estimator is DESIGN: the plan names the quantity and its use, not its formula.
  Because the planner that reads it never issues a verdict, a wrong value costs efficiency and never validity.
- "Ground work beyond the rehearsal waits if no dataset has been admitted": with no dataset the state of every
  cell is step 0 `NotRun`, reports print `no-claim`, and nothing is blocked.
- Kill gates decided at M5 change which steps exist: KILL-CLOCK removes time-based scoring (step 1 fails closed
  for everyone); KILL-GROUND removes steps 3 to 5 except direct bench measurement of a field; KILL-BAND removes
  step 8 and leaves the raw residual table. The state type keeps the rows and marks them `FailedClosed` with
  the fallback's name.
