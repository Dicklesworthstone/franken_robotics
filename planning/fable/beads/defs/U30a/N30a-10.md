## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) calibrates simulation against measured reality in ten steps: (0) admit data and
seal the validation and holdout parts; (1) fit clocks; (2) analyse identifiability and design excitation; (3)
identify actuators; (4) identify contact and surface, then inertia and geometry, then sensors; (5) write
candidate card versions; (6) freeze a prediction; (7) score on sealed data, retire the holdout, admit or reject
the posterior; (8) issue or refuse a band for the cell; (9) monitor drift and retire; (10) feed discrepancy back
(a reality-gap variance per cell and regression cases). Each step fails closed: to a refusal, to "no time-based
score", to "no fit", to the prior card, to a candidate that stays a candidate, to `reality: no-claim`, to
`retired`. No real data is needed to test this: a truth simulator with planted parameters stands in for a
robot, and everything it emits is labelled `synthetic`.

The gate scripts of the chapter each test one mechanism in depth. This script tests what none of them does: the
order itself, from the user's side, through the seven verbs `ground.ingest`, `excite`, `identify`, `predict`,
`score`, `band` (and `kit` is left to its own script).

## Scope

In: the script; the in-order walk; the out-of-order calls; one planted failure per step; the step-10 outputs.
Out: the statistical gates (clock recovery rates, parameter recovery, band coverage, drift power), which have
their own scripts.
The run of the whole procedure over every fixture of the rehearsal with one assertion per known-answer row,
through all seven verbs and over MCP, is `scripts/e2e/ground_rehearsal.sh` (N30b-31). This script asserts
order, the refusal of out-of-order calls and the fail-closed outcome of each step, and does not repeat the
per-row values.

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/ground_procedure.sh [--seed S] [--controls]`; `frobd` on an empty store; work under
`target/e2e-work/ground_procedure/`; default seed 11.

1. **Rehearsal data.** The rehearsal generator's entry point (BD-388),
   `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example ground_rehearsal -- gen --fixture R-04 --seed S --out <dir>`,
   writes the single-joint rig dataset: two calibration days, one validation day, one holdout day, clocks
   with two marks bracketing each trial, origin `synthetic`. (`ground.kit` has two registered forms, `build`
   and `check`; neither emits rehearsal data on its own, so the script does not go through the kit.)
2. **In order.** Run and assert exit 0 (or 8 followed by a completed job) for, in the registry's forms (each
   `run` and `mutate` call with its own key, KNOWN_DRIFTS 5; identities are read from the previous envelope):
   `frob ground ingest <bundle> --design <rig design> --task-family tf:none --key gp-in-<S> --json`;
   `frob ground excite <rig design> --cards <set> --rig <rig> --params stage:actuators --json`;
   `frob ground identify <dataset> --stage actuators --key gp-id-<S> --json`;
   `frob ground predict --dataset <dataset> --part validation --cell <cell> --model <candidate model>
   --scene <scene> --task <task> --metric <metric>:0.90 --decision "<decision>" --study fixed-n:20
   --key gp-pr-<S> --json`; `frob ground score <prediction> --key gp-sc-<S> --json`;
   `frob ground band --cell <cell> --metric <metric> --level 0.90 --json`; then
   `frob ground band --status --cell <cell> --json`.
   The band step issues a finite band only from at least 19 scored residuals of the unit (N30a-36's
   `within-units` minimum; at n = 20 and 90% the index is 19): the validation day must therefore hold at
   least 19 trials, and the script asserts the scored count before calling `band`. BD-388 registers R-04's
   trial counts per synthetic day, and its validation day holds the 20 trials of the `fixed-n:20` study; with
   fewer, the expected outcome is 889 and the script fails naming the count, not the band.
   Assert after each: the step's artifact exists; the envelope's evidence block says class `Estimated` and
   `reality: synthetic` (never `calibrated`, because the data is synthetic); the ledger sequence of each step
   is greater than the previous step's.
3. **Status listing.** `frob ground band --status --json` shows steps 0 to 8 `Done` with their artifacts, step 9
   with both monitors initialised, and step 10 with a reality-gap variance, its n and its label.
4. **Out of order** (each on a fresh store with only the stated steps done): `identify` before `ingest`
   (the schema refusal `FROB-REF-802`, pointer `dataset`: the call names a dataset identity that was never
   admitted; DESIGN: `ground.identify` lists 879 and 814 only, and an identity that does not resolve is refused
   by the schema layer on every verb, as an unknown card is at `ground.predict`); `identify` on a dataset
   whose clock has one mark (the `ingest` of that dataset completes with exit 2 and reports the clock step
   refused with `FROB-REF-876` and time-based metrics unavailable; `identify --stage actuators` on its
   time-stamped traces is then refused with `FROB-REF-879`, pointer `stage`, naming `clocks`);
   `identify` on a parameter set the `excite` report marks aliased (`FROB-REF-879`); `score`
   with no frozen prediction (`FROB-REF-883`); `score` twice on one holdout (`FROB-REF-886`); `band` before
   `score` (`FROB-REF-889`); `band` after a planted firmware change (`FROB-REF-897` with the reason). Assert
   for each: exit 3, the code, a first repair, and that no artifact of a later step was written.
5. **Fail closed.** After each refusal of step 4, assert the state: the prior card identity is still the one
   bound into the model; the training manifest's randomization widths are byte-identical to before; the report
   of the design prints `reality: no-claim` or `reality: synthetic`.
6. **Feedback.** After a scored trial planted outside its interval, assert one regression case in the divergence
   ledger with kind `reality`, and that its simulated side replays to the recorded digest:
   `frob sim replay --run <run> --world <w> --key gp-rp-<S> --json` on the world address the case records
   (BD-203's verb, `run` kind; an earlier text named `telemetry.divergence`, which compares two runs and does
   not replay one).
7. **Controls (`--controls`).** Conformance builds on which the script must exit non-zero: a `score` that
   accepts a prediction frozen after the read; an `identify` that ignores the alias report; a `band` that
   ignores retirement.
8. **Determinism.** Steps 1 to 3 twice with one seed: identical dataset identity, card identities, prediction
   record hash, band value and status listing bytes (modulo timestamps, which the helper masks).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The in-order walk completes and every envelope prints class `Estimated` and a `reality:` status of
      `synthetic` or `no-claim`.
- [ ] All seven out-of-order calls are refused with the stated codes and leave no later artifact.
- [ ] The fail-closed assertions of step 5 hold after every refusal.
- [ ] One regression case is written and replays.
- [ ] Each control build makes the script exit non-zero; two runs with one seed agree.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own; the state and precondition tests are in N30a-09.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Log `target/e2e-logs/ground_procedure/<utc>.ndjson` through the helper of `STD-E2E`,
with a transcript beside it. Per command a `cmd` line: exact command, exit code, wall time, the evidence block
(claim, class, tier, determinism, dataset, cell and model identities, seed) and the `reality` text. Per step a
`metric` line with the step number, artifact identity and ledger sequence. Per refusal a `refusal` line with
code, pointer, observed, required and first repair. `assert` lines for each fail-closed check with the digest
compared. On failure: the first step whose state differs and the store path. Final `summary`: assertions
passed and failed, steps `not run`, gates touched (none). No sample values from any dataset enter the log.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-734 (the three protocol mutations are refused) is asserted here in passing and in depth by
`ground_protocol.sh`. Negative controls: the three builds of step 7. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine;
debug and release; in-process airlock and worker airlock with equal digests.

## Considerations

- The script depends on seven other beads being built; while any is missing its steps are logged `not run`
  with the missing verb named, and the script still asserts everything that exists.
- If a kill gate has fired, the corresponding step is expected to fail closed for everyone; the script reads
  the recorded decision and asserts the fallback's behaviour instead (for example: after KILL-BAND, `band`
  returns `no-claim` with the raw residual table).
- The M5 milestone lists the seven Ground verbs "exercised first on the synthetic rehearsal"; this script is
  that exercise for the order of the verbs.
