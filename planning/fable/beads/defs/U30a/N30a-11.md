## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) compares simulated with measured traces, and a real dataset has several clocks: a
microcontroller stamps encoder samples, a logging computer stamps commands, a camera stamps frames. Step 1 of
the calibration procedure maps each source clock to one reference clock. The map is
t_ref = a + (1 + s)·t_src, with offset a and skew s. There are two unknowns, so **one shared pulse fixes only
one combination of them**. Skew is not negligible: at 100 parts per million, two clocks that coincide at the
start differ by 0.8 ms after 8 s (FACT, arithmetic), which is most of a tick at 1 kHz and enough to move a
packet across a decision instant of the simulator's clock contract. An unfitted or badly fitted clock turns
into a false actuator delay at the next step, so clocks come first in the fixed identification order.

A *mark* is an event visible on both clocks (a shared hardware pulse, a sync line edge), recorded as a pair
(source timestamp, reference timestamp) with a stated timing error σ_m. The dataset manifest lists each clock
with its nominal rate and its marks; raw source timestamps are retained.

Rejected alternatives: assuming zero skew and fitting an offset only (wrong by 0.8 ms in 8 s at 100 ppm);
fitting the clock by cross-correlating command and response (that estimates offset plus actuator delay, see
N30a-13); fitting to the event being scored (moves the whole prediction error into the clock, see N30a-13).

Tier: Frontier (no pilot is named for it; its gate is decided on synthetic data). The seed bead BD-389 closes
the gate and decides the kill gate KILL-CLOCK; this bead is the estimator.

## Scope

In: the affine fit and its covariance; the two-mark closed forms; the piecewise-affine fit; adequacy testing;
refusal 876 for under-determination; the fit artifact. Out: use of the fit to align instants and the
inside-the-bracket rule (N30a-12); the two misuse detections (N30a-13); the gate measurement (N30a-14).

## Technical approach

1. **Data.** For clock c: marks (x_i, τ_i), i = 1..m, with x_i the source timestamp, τ_i the reference
   timestamp and weight w_i = 1/σ_m,i². One clock per dataset is declared the reference.
2. **Affine fit (weighted least squares), in centred form** to keep conditioning: x̄ = Σw_i·x_i/Σw_i,
   τ̄ = Σw_i·τ_i/Σw_i, S_xx = Σw_i·(x_i − x̄)², S_xτ = Σw_i·(x_i − x̄)·(τ_i − τ̄).
   Rate r̂ = S_xτ/S_xx; skew ŝ = r̂ − 1; aligned time t̂_ref(x) = τ̄ + r̂·(x − x̄); offset â = τ̄ − r̂·x̄.
   Variances: var(τ̄) = 1/Σw_i; var(ŝ) = 1/S_xx; the two are uncorrelated in centred form, so
   σ_t(x)² = 1/Σw_i + (x − x̄)²/S_xx.
3. **Two marks (PO-GROUND-02).** For marks at t₁ < t₂ with independent timing error σ_m each:
   - skew uncertainty σ_s = √2·σ_m/(t₂ − t₁);
   - uncertainty of an aligned instant σ_t(t)² = σ_m²·[(t₂ − t)² + (t − t₁)²]/(t₂ − t₁)², which is σ_m² at
     either mark, σ_m²/2 at the midpoint, at most σ_m² inside the bracket, and grows quadratically outside it.
   These are step 2 with m = 2 and equal weights (S_xx = (t₂ − t₁)²/2·σ_m⁻²). With σ_m = 50 µs and marks 10 s
   apart, σ_s is 7.1 ppm (FACT, arithmetic). The derivation is written to `docs/derivations/ground_clock.md`.
4. **Requirements and refusal.** The affine model needs m ≥ 2 marks at distinct instants that bracket the
   trial. With fewer, or with all marks at one instant, the fit is refused with `FROB-REF-876` (clock alignment
   under-determined, or a scored instant outside the bracket; repair offered: the marks needed; exit code 3).
   The refusal's payload states marks present, marks needed and the bracket required to cover the trial.
5. **Adequacy.** With m > 2 the weighted residual sum χ² = Σw_i·(τ_i − t̂_ref(x_i))² has m − 2 degrees of
   freedom. If χ² exceeds the 99.9% quantile of its distribution (DESIGN threshold, recorded in the contract),
   the constant-skew model is rejected for this clock and the piecewise model is tried.
6. **Piecewise affine (skew can change during the trial).** Knots are placed at declared instants (the manifest
   may state them, for example a thermal transient; otherwise at interior marks chosen by the adequacy test).
   The model is continuous and piecewise linear with K segments and K + 1 parameters, fitted by weighted least
   squares. It needs at least K + 1 marks with every segment containing or bounded by marks at both ends;
   fewer is refusal 876 ("a trial with fewer marks than the model needs"). The fit reports its largest
   residual; with exactly K + 1 marks the residuals are zero by construction and the artifact says "no
   redundancy: residual not informative".
7. **Artifact** (DESIGN). `ClockFit { clock, reference, model: Affine { a, s, cov } | Piecewise { knots, values,
   cov }, marks_used, bracket: [x_first, x_last], sigma_m, chi2, dof, max_residual }`, content-addressed, with
   ledger event `ground.clock`. Aligned times are derived data; the raw timestamps stay in the dataset.
8. **Arithmetic.** Raw timestamps are integers in the device's ticks. The fit converts to seconds after
   centring, in double precision; class: deterministic per platform (no elementary functions beyond square
   root), identical twice on one machine.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the planted case (skew 100 ppm, offset 3.7 ms, two marks 10 s apart, jitter 50 µs) a single fit
      reports σ_s = 7.07 ppm to three digits.
- [ ] Monte Carlo over mark jitter (10⁴ draws) gives an empirical standard deviation of ŝ within 3% of σ_s and
      of the aligned instant within 3% of σ_t at t = t₁, the midpoint, t₂ and t₂ + 5 s (PO-GROUND-02).
- [ ] One mark, or two marks at one instant, is refused with 876 and the payload names the marks needed.
- [ ] With five marks and a planted change of skew at mid-trial, the affine model fails the adequacy test and
      the two-segment model is selected and reports its largest residual; with only three marks the
      three-parameter model reports "no redundancy".
- [ ] The m-mark formulas reduce to the two-mark closed forms (tested symbolically at m = 2, numerically at
      m = 2 with unequal weights against a direct 2×2 solve).

`centred_form_is_stable_at_large_timestamps` (timestamps near 10¹⁵ ticks).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_clock.sh` (bead N30a-14) runs `frob ground ingest` on rehearsal bundles and measures the
recovery rate over 1,000 resamples. It logs, per resample batch, the recovered skew, σ_s, the coverage of
aligned instants, and the refusal payloads of the one-mark control.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-721: skew within 3·σ_s = 21 ppm of the planted 100 ppm and aligned instants covering the truth in at least
99% of 1,000 resamples; controls: one pulse only is refused; an offset fitted to the scored event is detected
(N30a-13). Negative controls registered: a fit that assumes s = 0 (fails exact recovery and coverage at
t = 10 s by 1 ms); a covariance that omits the (x − x̄)² term (fails the outside-bracket variance test).
Metamorphic: re-expressing all timestamps in another unit leaves ŝ unchanged; adding a constant to every source
timestamp changes only â. Determinism: twice on one machine bit for bit; debug and release.

## Considerations

- Kill gate KILL-CLOCK (decided at M5): if the clock-recovery gate or the freeze-before-read gate fails, real
  logs are scored on time-free metrics only (success, energy, peak values), and no crossing time, latency or
  delay is reported. The fit artifact then exists for diagnosis only.
- The stated σ_m is trusted as declared; with m > 2 the residuals check it, with m = 2 nothing does. The
  artifact says which case holds.
- Marks must be independent. Two marks taken from one pulse train with a shared trigger error are one group;
  the noise-model builder (N30a-08) carries that as a common-mode term per clock.
- Open point: the plan does not say how knots are chosen when the manifest declares none; the rule in step 6
  is DESIGN and is recorded in the calibration artifact so that a reader can see it.
