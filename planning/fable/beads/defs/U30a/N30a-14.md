## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) maps each source clock of a real dataset to a reference clock by
t_ref = a + (1 + s)·t_src, fitted by weighted least squares over hardware marks that bracket the trial. No real
data is needed to decide whether the fit is correct: the synthetic rehearsal's truth simulator emits logs with
a planted skew and offset and jittered marks, all labelled `synthetic`.

**GATE-GROUND-02** (claim kind: known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): clock recovery as planted (skew 100 ppm,
offset 3.7 ms, mark jitter 50 µs, marks 10 s apart: recovered skew within 3·σ_s = 21 ppm, where
σ_s = √2·σ_m/(t₂ − t₁) = 7.07 ppm, and aligned instants covering the truth) in at least 99% of 1,000 resamples;
both negative controls caught: one pulse only is refused, and an offset fitted to the scored event is detected.

The gate feeds a kill gate. **KILL-CLOCK** (decided at M5): if GATE-GROUND-02 or the freeze-before-read gate
GATE-GROUND-05 fails, real logs are scored on time-free metrics only (success, energy, peak values), and no
crossing time, latency or delay is reported.

## Scope

In: the script, its resampling protocol, assertions, controls and log; the gate record. Out: the estimator and
rules (N30a-11 to N30a-13); the kill-gate decision itself.

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/ground_clock.sh [--seed S] [--resamples 1000] [--controls]`, plain shell over `frob` and
the rehearsal generator, working under `target/e2e-work/ground_clock/`; default seed 2.

1. **Emit.** The rehearsal generator writes R = 1,000 single-clock-pair bundles (kept small: marks, one encoder
   channel and one command channel over a 10 s trial at 1 kHz), each with planted s = 100·10⁻⁶, a = 3.7 ms,
   two marks at trial start and end with independent Gaussian jitter of 50 µs drawn by address from the seed,
   and 20 ground-truth probe instants spread uniformly inside the bracket whose true reference times the
   generator records in a side file the script reads (never the verb).
2. **Fit.** For each bundle: `frob ground ingest <bundle> --design <rig design> --task-family tf:none --key
   gc-<S>-<ρ> --json` (a distinct key per resample, so that no call returns another's stored receipt). From
   the envelope read the clock artifact: ŝ, â, σ_s, the bracket.
3. **Recovery criteria per resample.** (i) |ŝ − 100 ppm| ≤ 3·σ_s (21.2 ppm). (ii) At the registered scored
   instant t* = 4.212 s, the true reference time lies in [t̂ − 3·σ_t(t*), t̂ + 3·σ_t(t*)] with σ_t from the
   fit. A resample succeeds when (i) and (ii) both hold. The 20 probe instants are evaluated the same way and
   logged as per-probe coverage; they are information, not the gate (see Considerations: simultaneous coverage
   over the whole bracket has probability about 0.990 for a correct fit, which would put the 99% threshold at
   the mean of an honest implementation).
4. **Gate arithmetic.** rate = successes/R. Assert rate ≥ 0.99. Also record: the empirical standard deviation
   of ŝ against 7.07 ppm (expected within 5% at R = 1,000); the mean of σ_s reported by the fits (expected
   7.07 ppm, since σ_m is declared); the per-probe coverage.
5. **Control A: one pulse only.** Bundles with a single mark: `ingest` admits the dataset and reports the clock
   step refused with `FROB-REF-876` (repair: the marks needed, "one more mark bracketing the trial"). The call
   exits 2 (completed with findings) and the refusal object is in `clocks[0].refused`, the integrated
   behaviour BD-389 fixes; a subsequent time-based `score` is refused outright (exit 3, 876); a time-free
   metric still scores.
6. **Control B: offset fitted to the scored event.** A prediction frozen for the crossing time at 4.212 s; the
   truth simulator's log shows 4.180 s; a conformance tool rewrites the clock artifact's offset by 32 ms.
   `frob ground score` must refuse with 876 and the label `clock-not-from-marks`. With the untouched artifact
   the score reports a crossing-time error of 32 ms ± its alignment uncertainty.
7. **Further assertions carried for the rules.** A scored instant 0.5 s after the last mark is refused (876);
   a packet-before-decision query on a pair 60 µs apart across two clocks answers `undecided`; the
   delay-against-offset case (delay 8 ms, offset −8 ms, no shared mark) reports "delay plus offset = 0" and
   leaves the card's delay unchanged.
8. **Negative-control builds (`--controls`).** The script must exit non-zero on: a fit that assumes zero skew
   (rate collapses: the error at 10 s is 1 ms against 3·σ_t of 150 µs); a fit whose covariance omits the skew
   term (probe coverage near the bracket ends stays fine, the outside-bracket assertion and the
   standard-deviation check fail); a `score` that skips artifact verification (control B is not refused).
9. **Determinism.** The whole run twice with one seed: identical per-resample ŝ and identical rate.
10. **Folded from the superseded bead N30b-32 (its clock half).** The fixtures are R-01 (clock) and R-05
    (delay against offset) of the rehearsal catalogue (BD-388). Where this bead describes a bundle or a
    population differently, the catalogue's registered values are the ones generated.
    Single-fit assertions on R-01 before the battery: `clocks[0]` has `skew_ppm` within 21 of 100, `offset_s`
    within 3·σ_t of 0.0037, `sigma_skew_ppm` = 7.07 to three digits and the bracket [0, 10] s; on R-05,
    `delay_plus_offset = 0` within its interval, and `frob ground identify <R-05 dataset> --stage actuators
    --params delay --key gc-d-<S> --json` is refused with `FROB-REF-879` (the card delay update of KA-725's
    control) while `frob actuator show` returns the same card identity before and after. The battery is also a row of the conformance crate's known-answer tier, run in the one
    form that crate has (test targets, no binary):
    `FROB_TEST_SEED=<S> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-GROUND-02`, which drives
    the `ground.ingest` handler per resample exactly as an agent would call it and writes the hash-named
    receipt that the gate record cites. The script sources
    `scripts/e2e/lib.sh`, records `e2e_metric clock_pass <count> resamples >= 990 --gate GATE-GROUND-02`, and
    ends with `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-02`. The `smoke` battery runs the single fits and the controls only; `full`
    runs everything. On a second platform the counts are recorded and compared, not asserted equal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Recovery rate at least 0.99 over 1,000 resamples (TARGET), recorded with the measured value.
- [ ] Empirical standard deviation of ŝ within 5% of 7.07 ppm; mean probe coverage at least 0.99.
- [ ] Control A refused with 876; control B refused with 876 and its label; both recorded as caught.
- [ ] The three rule assertions of step 7 hold.
- [ ] Each control build of step 8 makes the script exit non-zero.
- [ ] Rows KA-721 and KA-725 of the Ground known-answer registry (N30-KA) are bound by this bead: it adds their
      runner entries `ka:KA-721@referee` (the resampled clock battery on R-01, asserting the `synthetic` origin)
      and `ka:KA-725@host` (R-05's "delay plus offset = 0" and the refused card delay update), N38a-13, and
      names in each row's derivation front matter the test that checks it (crate, path and name). The
      known-answer run reports both rows `pass` with their controls caught, and each is logged as an `assert`
      line carrying its KA number, measured value, truth and tolerance.
- [ ] The script writes no gate record (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9). This bead is the gate's one recorder: from the receipt
      of a passing run, a session that did not implement the measured beads enters GATE-GROUND-02 in
      `registries/gates.toml` with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate
      GATE-GROUND-02 --receipt <hash>`. The record holds the measured rate and the count against 990 with the
      expected count, R, the seed, the commit and the machine, and is what the KILL-CLOCK decision reads.
- [ ] Two runs with the same seed give identical counts on one machine.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own beyond a test of the generator's side file: `clock_truth_side_file_matches_planted_map`
(true reference time = 0.0037 + 1.0001·t_src to 10⁻¹²).
Folded from N30b-32: `crates/frob-conformance/tests/ground_batteries_identify.rs`: `clock_pass_rule` (a
resample with skew error 22 ppm fails; with 20 ppm and the registered instant covered it passes).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Log `target/e2e-logs/ground_clock/<utc>.ndjson` through the helper of `STD-E2E`, with
a transcript. Per `ingest` batch of 100 resamples one `cmd` line with the command template, exit codes, wall
time and the evidence block (class `Estimated`, tier referee, determinism, dataset identities, seed;
`reality: synthetic`). `metric` lines: rate with threshold 0.99; standard deviation of ŝ in ppm with expected
7.07; mean σ_s; per-probe coverage; the 32 ms case's reported error and its σ. `refusal` lines for both
controls with code, label and repair. On failure: the first failing resample's index, its ŝ, σ_s, the probe
that missed, and the bundle path. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed and the gate touched
(GATE-GROUND-02) with pass or fail.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-721 (the gate's case) and KA-725 (delay against offset). Counterexamples CX-326 and CX-327. Why 99% is the
right threshold: both gate criteria of step 3 are functions of the two mark errors e₁, e₂ only. The skew clause
is |e₂ − e₁| ≤ 3·√2·σ_m (probability 0.9973); coverage at t* = 4.212 s is |0.5788·e₁ + 0.4212·e₂| ≤ 3·σ_t(t*)
(probability 0.9973); the two are correlated at −0.16, so an honest implementation passes a resample with
probability 0.9946 (numerical integration; expected count 994.6 of 1,000, and the count falls below 990 with
probability 0.022). A fit that under-reports its variance by a factor of 1.3 passes with probability 0.983 and
falls below 99% (the control builds demonstrate it). Negative controls: the two of the gate and
the three builds of step 8. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; both development
machines, compared inside the double-precision tolerance.

## Considerations

- 1,000 ingests of small bundles cost a few minutes of CPU; the script batches them through one daemon.
- The reading of "aligned instants cover truth" decides whether the gate is passable (the design choice of
  the seed bead BD-389, adopted here so that the two agree). With Gaussian jitter a correct fit passes one
  resample with probability 0.9973 on the skew clause alone, 0.9945 with coverage at one registered instant,
  and 0.990 if coverage is required simultaneously at every instant of the bracket. At 0.990 the count falls
  below 990 of 1,000 in 42% of runs of a correct implementation (P(X ≤ 989 | 1,000, 0.990) = 0.417); at 0.9945
  in about 2.5% (0.0249; with the unrounded 0.9946 of the numerical integration above, 0.022). The gate therefore
  asserts coverage at the registered instant, the seed is registered before the first run and never changed
  after a failure, and the expected count (about 994.5) is logged beside the measured one. A reading below 990
  on the pinned seed triggers one pre-registered run at R = 10,000 before any conclusion, and three further
  seeds are run and logged as information (DESIGN; to be confirmed by the Ground lead, since the plan fixes
  R = 1,000).
- The gate is a known-answer gate on synthetic data. Passing it supports no statement about any real clock; the
  log's every line says `synthetic`.
- A failing rate is a record, not a blocked milestone: the fallback (time-free metrics only) ships under its
  own name.
