## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) refuses to fit a parameter that the data cannot determine (decision ADR-GROUND-04:
a fit on an unidentifiable combination is refused, never regularized quietly, because a regularized fit returns
the prior and labels it a measurement). Some unidentifiable combinations are not accidents of one dataset: they
are structural, known in advance, and recur on every bench. Eight are listed below with the smallest case that
shows each and what separates the pair. They are planted in the gate GATE-GROUND-03 (all eight flagged, and no
fit issued on an aliased set) and they seed the negative-control registry: each is a planted defect that a
correct analysis must catch and a careless fit would not.

| # | Aliased quantities | Smallest case | What separates them |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Armature N²·J_r and link inertia about the joint axis | A joint moving alone: only I_link + N²·J_r appears | A payload of known inertia, or a second joint moving |
| 2 | Coulomb friction and a constant bias torque | Motion in one direction: τ_c·sgn(q̇) + τ_b is one number | Reversals |
| 3 | Viscous friction and back-EMF damping | Voltage drive: b + k_t·k_e/R is one number | A current measurement, or current-mode drive |
| 4 | Torque constant and the scale of inertia and friction | Commanded current and encoder only: all four are known up to one factor | A known payload, or a torque sensor |
| 5 | Actuator delay and clock offset | Command and response on two clocks with no shared mark: only delay plus offset appears | A shared hardware mark |
| 6 | Friction coefficient and the torque limit | F = min(k_t·I/r, μ·N) with k_t = 0.2 N·m/A and r = 0.06 m: at 1 A and 2 A under 30 N and 40 N the drawbar force is 3.33 N and 6.67 N for μ = 0.4 and for μ = 0.8; at 3 A under 10 N it is 4 N and 8 N | Exciting the traction-limited regime as well as the torque-limited one |
| 7 | Static friction and everything else | A run that never dwells at zero velocity carries no information on breakaway | Slow torque ramps from rest, both directions |
| 8 | Contact stiffness and contact damping | One drop height gives one rebound ratio | Two drop heights, or a force trace |

Symbols: N gear ratio; J_r rotor inertia; I_link link inertia about the joint axis; τ_c Coulomb friction; τ_b a
constant bias torque; b viscous coefficient; k_t torque constant; k_e back-EMF constant; R winding resistance;
μ friction coefficient; N (in F) normal load; r wheel radius; I (in F) current.

## Scope

In: one executable fixture per alias (a reading map, a smallest excitation, the expected flag, the expected
identified combination, a separating excitation), each verified in closed form; registration of the eight as
negative controls. Out: the general analysis and the fit guard that refuses a fit on an aliased set with 879
(BD-390, which depends on this bead and catches the eight controls: the guard is the analysis run on recorded
data, so it lives with the analysis, and this bead can be finished first); the grammar that supplies
separating experiments (N30a-16); delay-plus-offset typing (N30a-13).

## Technical approach

1. **Fixture form** (DESIGN). `crates/frob-ground/src/excite/aliases.rs` exports
   `AliasFixture { id, params, reading_map, minimal_excitation, expected_null_direction,
   identified_combination, separators: Vec<Excitation> }`, each with a closed-form reading map so that the
   expected answer is exact and independent of the simulator; each also has a referee-simulated twin used by
   the end-to-end script.
2. **The eight reading maps** (noise-free; the null direction is in parameters scaled by their priors, and is
   the expected smallest singular direction).
   1. τ = (I_link + N²·J_r)·q̈. Null: δI_link = −N²·δJ_r. Identified: I_link + N²·J_r.
   2. For q̇ > 0 throughout: τ = b·q̇ + τ_c + τ_b. Null: δτ_c = −δτ_b. Identified: τ_c + τ_b. Separator: a
      trajectory with q̇ of both signs, where τ = b·q̇ + τ_c·sgn(q̇) + τ_b.
   3. Voltage drive at steady speed, with i = (V − k_e·N·q̇)/R: N·k_t·V/R = (b + N²·k_t·k_e/R)·q̇ (per unit
      efficiency). Null: δb = −δ(N²·k_t·k_e/R). Identified: the sum (the plan writes it b + k_t·k_e/R at the
      motor shaft). Separator: a current channel, or current-mode drive.
   4. Current drive, encoder only: I·q̈ + b·q̇ + τ_c·sgn(q̇) = N·k_t·i. Scaling (I, b, τ_c, k_t) by one factor
      λ leaves q(t) unchanged. Null (in log-parameters): (1, 1, 1, 1). Identified: the three ratios I/k_t,
      b/k_t, τ_c/k_t. Separator: a payload of known inertia ΔI (then (I + ΔI)/k_t and I/k_t give k_t), or a
      torque sensor.
   5. Response = f(command(t − d)) logged with a clock offset o on the command side: only d + o appears.
      Null: δd = −δo. Identified: d + o. Separator: two shared marks (a linked clock pair).
   6. F = min(k_t·I/r, μ·N). In runs (1 A, 30 N), (2 A, 40 N): F = 3.333, 6.667 N and ∂F/∂μ = 0 (μ·N = 12, 16 N
      at μ = 0.4; 24, 32 N at μ = 0.8). Null: the μ axis. Separator: (3 A, 10 N), where k_t·I/r = 10 N and
      F = μ·N = 4 N or 8 N, so ∂F/∂μ = 10 N.
   7. A trajectory with |q̇| ≥ v_min > 0 throughout: the stiction row is never active and τ_s enters only
      through the Stribeck term [τ_c + (τ_s − τ_c)·exp(−(q̇/v_s)²)], which is below the noise when v_min is
      several v_s. Null: the τ_s axis (and v_s). Separator: slow torque ramps from rest in both directions.
   8. One reading, the rebound ratio h₁/h₀ of one drop, for two parameters (stiffness k, damping c): rank at
      most 1. Identified: the one combination that sets the ratio. Separators: a contact-force trace (duration
      and peak give two numbers), or a second drop height where the contact law's restitution depends on
      impact speed.
3. **Expected analysis output per fixture** (the contract BD-390's analysis is tested against): the alias is
   flagged (rank or correlation check), the report names the identified combination in physical symbols, and
   names at least one separator from the table.
4. **Closed-form verification, here.** For each fixture the test computes the Jacobian of its closed-form
   reading map in prior-scaled parameters and its singular value decomposition by plain linear algebra (no
   Ground analysis code): on the smallest case the smallest singular direction equals
   `expected_null_direction` and σ_min/σ_max is below 10⁻⁶ (exactly zero for aliases 1 to 6 and 8; below the
   threshold for alias 7 at v_min = 5·v_s); with each listed separator added, the rank is recomputed and
   recorded. The same cases go through the analysis crate's identifiability analysis A-25
   (`fixtures_agree_with_a25`), which predates Ground's own.
5. **Controls.** The eight fixtures are the planted inputs of KA-722's control ("a fit request on each
   aliased set is refused", N30-KA). That control is one record in the one controls file
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` (one plant, one control, N38b-05's rule), issued by
   N38b-05's seeding tool from the control text: an `NC-` identifier, `seed = "KA-722"`, `kind =
   "planted-input"`, `fixture` the index file `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/ground/aliases.toml` (written
   by this bead; it lists the eight fixture files `alias-<n>.toml` beside it with their hashes), and, because
   the expected catch is the product's own refusal, `removed_check = "frob_ground::excite::guard"` (BD-025's
   rule that a control is a defect, not a boundary: the control run on a build without the guard must return
   a fit on each of the eight, and the test must catch every one). This bead writes the nine files and no
   control record (KNOWN_DRIFTS 2; an earlier text registered eight private identifiers `ground.alias.<n>`,
   which the loader rejects). The expected catch is refusal 879 from the fit guard of BD-390, whose tests
   consume these fixtures.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For each of the eight fixtures, in closed form: the smallest singular direction of the smallest case
      equals the expected null direction, the identified combination is the expected one, and each fixture
      lists at least one separator (8 of 8); A-25 gives the same flags on the same cases.
- [ ] Each fixture has a referee-simulated twin whose noise-free readings agree with the closed-form reading
      map within the referee's stated tolerance for that scene (recorded per twin; the twins of the contact
      aliases 6 to 8 are compared in steady state), so the end-to-end script runs the same aliases through the
      binary.
- [ ] With the separator added, fixtures 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 become full rank in closed form; for fixtures 1, 3
      and 8 the test records, per listed separator, the rank it finds, so that no report can claim a
      separation the rank test does not show.
- [ ] The eight fixture files `alias-1.toml` to `alias-8.toml` exist with a `synthetic` kind header, and the
      index `aliases.toml` lists all eight with their hashes; after N38b-05's seeding tool runs, KA-722's one
      control record names the index, with `removed_check` set and 879 as the expected catch on each of the
      eight (caught by BD-390's guard tests and by N30a-19).
- [ ] The drawbar numbers print as 3.33 N, 6.67 N, 4 N and 8 N.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/aliases.rs`: `alias_1_null_direction` ((1, −1/N²) in physical units for N = 9);
`alias_2_one_direction_then_reversal`; `alias_3_voltage_then_current_channel`;
`alias_4_scale_family_invariance` (λ = 1.3 leaves the encoder trace bitwise equal in the closed form) and
`alias_4_known_payload_separates`; `alias_5_delay_plus_offset`; `alias_6_drawbar_values` (0.2·1/0.06 = 3.333;
0.2·2/0.06 = 6.667; min(10, 4) = 4; min(10, 8) = 8; ∂F/∂μ = 0, 0, 10); `alias_7_no_dwell_no_breakaway`;
`alias_8_one_ratio_rank_one`; `separators_rank_recorded` (2, 4, 5, 6, 7 full rank; 1, 3, 8 recorded);
`referee_twins_match_closed_form` (inside each twin's recorded tolerance); `control_fixtures_written` (eight
files, each naming its alias, the smallest case and the expected refusal 879, with the `synthetic` header, and
the index listing exactly those eight with matching hashes);
`fixtures_agree_with_a25` (the same eight cases through the analysis crate give the same flags). The guard
tests that consume these fixtures (`guard_refuses_each_879`, `guard_combination_writes_no_card`,
`guard_has_no_bypass`, `unidirectional_tau_c_refused`) are BD-390's.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_excite.sh` (bead N30a-19) runs `frob ground excite` on each fixture's rig file and
`frob ground identify` on each fixture's synthetic dataset. Logged per fixture: the flag, ρ or the singular
ratio, the identified combination text, the separators named, the refusal code of the fit request.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-722 (8 of 8 flagged with the identified combination named; control: a fit request on each aliased set is
refused), KA-723 (the drawbar forces; control: the torque-limited data alone yields no μ), KA-725 (delay plus
offset). Counterexamples CX-328 (a good local fit identifies its parameters) and CX-329 (friction can be
identified from any traction run). Negative controls of this bead: a fixture whose reading map drops the
sgn(q̇) factor of alias 2 (its reversal separator then shows no rank gain and `separators_rank_recorded`
fails); a twin whose referee scene differs from the closed form (the twin test fails). The controls of the
guard itself (a ridge-regularized solver in place of the refusal; an analysis of the planned instead of the
recorded excitation) are BD-390's. Determinism: closed-form fixtures are exact; referee twins are bitwise on
one machine.

## Considerations

- Two of the plan's separators need care. For alias 1, a payload of known inertia adds a known constant to
  the same sum and by itself fixes the torque scale (alias 4), not the split between rotor and link; the split
  needs the link-side inertia to vary independently (a second joint moving changes I_link(q) while N²·J_r
  stays). For alias 8, a second drop height separates stiffness from damping only if restitution depends on
  impact speed in the contact law in force. The fixtures test what the analysis actually finds for each
  listed separator and record it; the table is carried as the plan states it.
- Alias 7 is a matter of degree (the Stribeck tail is small, not zero); the fixture uses v_min = 5·v_s so that
  the sensitivity is below the 10⁻⁶ rank threshold relative to the largest (exp(−25) ≈ 1.4·10⁻¹¹). That
  holds for the card shape `stribeck_shape = "gaussian"`, which the fixture's card states; under the
  `lorentzian` shape the tail at 5·v_s is 1/26, far above the threshold, and the alias is then only partial
  (reported by the precision check, not the rank check).
- The guard lives in BD-390, not here: it runs BD-390's analysis on recorded data, while BD-390 needs these
  fixtures to be tested, so this bead is finished first. The fixtures are the contract; the guard is the
  analysis run on recorded data.
- These eight are the known ones. The general analysis exists because a new rig will have a ninth; one is
  already recorded (exposure delay against mount translation).
