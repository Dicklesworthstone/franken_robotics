## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) analyses, before any fit, whether a planned experiment can determine the
parameters to be identified, and designs the excitation. The verb is `ground.excite` (kind read): it takes a
design, a card set and a rig description, needs no data, and returns an identifiability report and an
experiment plan, never a fit. A fit requested on a parameter set that fails identifiability is refused with
`FROB-REF-879`, with the aliased combination and the separating experiment as repairs.

**GATE-GROUND-03** (claim kind: known answer; TARGET): the eight structural aliases are flagged, 8 of 8, and no
fit is issued on an aliased set. The gate register places it at M5 and notes that it may be recorded at M4 (the
chapter tags it M4; a gate cannot be due before its mechanism lands). The eight: armature against link inertia
on a joint moving alone; Coulomb friction against a constant bias torque in one-directional motion; viscous
friction against back-EMF damping under voltage drive; torque constant against the scale of inertia and
friction with commanded current and encoder only; actuator delay against clock offset with no shared mark;
friction coefficient against the torque limit; static friction in a run that never dwells at zero velocity;
contact stiffness against damping from one drop height.

## Scope

In: the script, its rigs and datasets, assertions, controls, log and the gate record. Out: the mechanisms
(BD-390, N30a-15 to N30a-18).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/ground_excite.sh [--seed S] [--controls]`, plain shell over `frob`, working under
`target/e2e-work/ground_excite/`; default seed 3. Fixtures come from the alias module's generator
(`cargo run -p frob-conformance --bin ground-alias -- emit`): for each alias n = 1..8 a rig file
`alias-n.rig.toml` restricted to the smallest case, a second rig file with the separator available, and a
small synthetic dataset recorded on the smallest case (origin `synthetic`).

1. **Flagging (8 runs).** `frob ground excite --design … --cards … --rig alias-n.rig.toml --params <pair>
   --json`. Assert: exit 2 (completed with findings); the pair appears under `aliased_pairs` or
   `unidentified_directions`; `identified_combination` equals the expected text for that fixture (for
   example `I_link + N²·J_r`, `τ_c + τ_b`, `delay + offset`); at least one `separating_experiment` is named;
   the result holds no posterior field; evidence class `Estimated`, `reality: no-claim`.
2. **Drawbar numbers (alias 6).** The report's predicted readings for (1 A, 30 N) and (2 A, 40 N) are 3.33 N
   and 6.67 N at μ = 0.4 and at μ = 0.8, and the sensitivity to μ is zero; with (3 A, 10 N) in the rig the
   readings are 4 N and 8 N and μ is `identified`.
3. **No fit on an aliased set (8 runs).** `frob ground ingest` the fixture dataset, then
   `frob ground identify --stage actuators --params <pair> --json` (or the guard through the conformance
   binary): assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-879`, repairs naming the combination and the separator, and that
   `frob actuator show` returns the same card identities before and after. Then with `--combination`: assert
   the combination is returned as a derived quantity and no card version was written.
4. **Separation.** With `alias-n.sep.rig.toml`, re-run step 1 for aliases 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 and assert the pair
   is now identified; for aliases 1, 3 and 8 record what the analysis reports for each listed separator and
   assert only that the report and the rank test agree.
5. **A plan that identifies.** On the single-joint stand rig with a 30-minute budget:
   `frob ground excite --params stage:actuators --budget-s 1800 --json`. Assert: every actuator parameter of
   the stage has a predicted bound at most half its prior standard deviation; no alias remains; the plan's
   total duration is within budget; the picks are logged in order with their gains.
6. **Profile and cautions.** The regime-edge rig (3 A under 14 N, force noise 1 N): μ is
   `not-identified (profile)` although the local precision check passes. The stand plan without a slow ramp
   lists the Stribeck variant as `silent`; with the ramp, `detectable`. With a decision partition in which
   both friction values imply the same wheel: outcome `measure-nothing`.
7. **Controls (`--controls`).** Conformance builds on which the script must exit non-zero: an analysis without
   the rank check (step 1 fails for alias 6); an `identify` that regularizes instead of refusing (step 3
   returns a value); a selector that ignores the time budget (step 5).
8. **Determinism.** Steps 1 and 5 twice: byte-identical reports.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 8 of 8 aliases flagged with the expected identified combination and a separator (the gate's first half).
- [ ] 8 of 8 fit requests refused with 879 and no card version written (the gate's second half).
- [ ] The drawbar values print as 3.33 N, 6.67 N, 4 N and 8 N.
- [ ] The stand plan identifies every stage parameter within budget; the profile, omitted-law and
      measure-nothing cases behave as stated.
- [ ] Each control build makes the script exit non-zero; two runs agree byte for byte.
- [ ] The gate record `GATE-GROUND-03` is written with the two counts (flagged of 8, refused of 8), the
      thresholds in force (10⁻⁶, 0.5, 0.95), the seed, the commit and the machine.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own; the generator has `alias_rigs_are_minimal` (removing the separator's instrument from the
second rig file reproduces the first).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Log `target/e2e-logs/ground_excite/<utc>.ndjson` through the helper of `STD-E2E`,
with a transcript. Per command a `cmd` line (exact command, exit code, wall time, evidence block: claim,
class, tier, determinism, identities, seed). Per alias a `metric` line: the singular ratio or ρ against its
threshold, the identified combination, the separators. Per fit request a `refusal` line: code, combination,
first repair, card identities before and after. For the plan: picks, durations, gains, bounds against 0.5.
On failure: the first alias whose outcome differs, with the rig and report paths. Final `summary`: assertions
passed and failed, steps `not run`, gate touched (GATE-GROUND-03) with its two counts.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-722 (8 of 8 flagged; control: a fit request on each aliased set is refused) and KA-723 (the drawbar
forces; control: the torque-limited data alone yields no μ). Counterexamples CX-328 and CX-329. Negative
controls: the three builds of step 7, and the eight fixtures themselves, which are registered as negative
controls `ground.alias.1` to `ground.alias.8`. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release;
both development machines inside the referee's recorded tolerance.

## Considerations

- A ninth alias (a range sensor's exposure delay against a forward offset of its mount on a straight pass) has
  its own fixture and its own gate, so that the count of eight in this gate is unchanged; this script does not
  assert it.
- If `ground.identify` is not yet built when the gate is first recorded (at M4), the record says that the
  refusals were asserted through the guard function and not through the verb, and the gate is re-recorded at
  M5 through the verb.
- All data is `synthetic`; passing the gate supports no statement about any real rig.
