## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) fits model parameters to admitted logs and writes them as new card versions.
Decision ADR-GROUND-03 fixes the order: clocks, actuators, contact and surface, inertia and geometry, sensors.
The reason is twofold: the literature places the simulation-to-reality gap in the actuator, and each stage's
error aliases into the next.

**Why actuators first** (FACT, source register). Identifying armature, joint friction and delay gave 46% more
travel distance and 75% less rotational deviation in one 2026 study (https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10351); static
friction is commonly missing from randomization; joint friction and actuator delay were called particularly
critical for a point-foot biped. Simulation agrees in kind: in a planar threshold-crossing pilot, changing one
winding feed-forward term from R·i_ref to R·i changed 24 of 156 outcome labels, and success fractions of 0.093
and 0.780 at a 100 Hz loop with no rotor inertia became 1.000 and 1.000 at 1 kHz or with rotor inertia added. A
contact parameter fitted before the actuator is right absorbs the actuator's error and then predicts the wrong
thing on the next surface.

Rejected alternative: one simultaneous fit of every parameter of the robot on whole-body logs. It has the best
fit residual and the worst transfer, because compensating errors are free to hide in each other.

Tier: Frontier. The seed bead BD-391 owns the verb, the gate GATE-GROUND-04 and the kill gate KILL-GROUND; this
bead is the staging and the model that the three estimator passes (N30a-21 to N30a-23) fit.

## Scope

In: the stage list and its gating; the per-joint model and parameter vector; how earlier stages' uncertainty
enters later stages; the direct-measurement path; what each stage reads and writes. Out: the estimator passes;
the calibration record (N30a-24); stages 2 to 4 in detail (N30a-25); card writing (N30a-27).

## Technical approach

1. **Stages.** (0) Clocks (done at ingest). (1) Actuators, joint by joint, unloaded on a stand where possible,
   in this order within the stage: delay and lag; static, Coulomb and viscous friction with the Stribeck
   speed; armature; the scale of the torque constant; then the torque–speed envelope and thermal constants of
   actuator level L2. (2) Contact and surface: friction per material pair under at least two normal loads,
   dissipation, rolling resistance. (3) Inertia and geometry, from scale and pendulum measurements and from
   as-built scans. (4) Sensors: bias, noise, latency. Stage names on the verb: `--stage actuators | contact |
   inertia | sensors`.
2. **The joint model at actuator level L1.** For one joint,

   (I(q) + J_a)·q̈ + b·q̇ + τ_f(q̇) + g(q) = N·η·k_t·i(t − d),
   τ_f(q̇) = [τ_c + (τ_s − τ_c)·exp(−(q̇/v_s)²)]·sgn(q̇) while moving,

   with the joint-level stiction row of the referee's solve holding q̇ = 0 while the applied torque is inside
   ±τ_s. Symbols: q joint position; I(q) link inertia about the joint axis; J_a armature (rotor inertia
   reflected through the gear, N²·J_rotor); b viscous coefficient; τ_c Coulomb friction; τ_s static
   (breakaway) friction; v_s Stribeck speed; g(q) gravity torque; N gear ratio; η efficiency; k_t torque
   constant; i winding current; d pure delay; and a first-order lag τ_lag between commanded and delivered
   current, τ_lag·di/dt = i_cmd(t − d) − i. The parameter vector is
   θ = (J_a, b, τ_c, τ_s, v_s, d, τ_lag, k_t scale), where "k_t scale" multiplies the card's torque constant.
   The model is evaluated by the referee tier with the candidate card bound in, never by a re-implementation.
   **The Stribeck shape.** The exponential above is one of the two shapes an actuator card may state:
   `stribeck_shape = "gaussian"` gives exp(−(q̇/v_s)²), and `"lorentzian"`, the default on batched tiers, gives
   1/(1 + (q̇/v_s)²). The shape is a card field and is never fitted; the referee uses whichever the bound card
   states, the identified v_s belongs to that shape, and the calibration record names it. The two differ
   where it matters here: at 5·v_s the Stribeck excess is e⁻²⁵ ≈ 10⁻¹¹ of (τ_s − τ_c) under `gaussian` and
   1/26 ≈ 3.8% under `lorentzian`. Every fixture and closed-form test of this chapter therefore states
   `gaussian` on its card, and pass A's velocity mask (N30a-21) is "five Stribeck speeds" for `gaussian` and
   the speed at which the excess is below 1% of (τ_s − τ_c), 10·v_s, for `lorentzian` (DESIGN).
3. **Gating** (through the procedure state, N30a-09). A stage runs only if (a) every clock its traces use has
   a fit, or the stage uses time-free readings only; (b) the fit guard (`excite::guard`, BD-390) passes on the excitation
   actually recorded, for the parameters requested; (c) the previous stage is done for this cell and lineage,
   or the call says `--keep-prior <stage>`, which is recorded in the calibration record. A refused stage
   leaves the prior card in force (the step fails closed to the prior card). Codes: (a) is refused with
   `FROB-REF-879`, pointer `stage`, naming `clocks` and each clock without a fit (clocks are stage 0 of the
   order, and the verb lists no clock code), repairs "record the marks the clock fit needs and ingest again"
   and "restrict the request to time-free readings"; (b) is refused with
   `FROB-REF-879` and the guard's repairs; (c) is refused with `FROB-REF-879`, pointer `stage`, naming the
   missing stage, repairs "run that stage first" and "`--keep-prior <stage>`" (an earlier stage left
   unidentified is an alias source for this one: its error is absorbed by the later parameters, which is the
   reason for the order). N30a-25 raises the same refusal for stages 2 to 4.
4. **Carrying earlier uncertainty forward.** A later stage holds earlier stages' parameters at their posterior
   means and declares them as consider parameters with their posterior covariance (the estimator's shared-
   parameter mechanism, at most 64). The later posterior therefore cannot be narrower than the earlier
   stage's uncertainty allows, and the later stage never moves an earlier stage's value.
5. **Joint by joint, then together.** Stage 1 fits each joint from its own stand data. A whole-robot
   output-error pass (N30a-22) may follow as a consistency check: it re-estimates nothing, and reports the
   misfit of the assembled posteriors on whole-robot calibration logs.
6. **Direct measurement.** A bench dataset whose instrument measures one card field directly (a mass on a
   scale, a winding resistance, a gear ratio) is fitted trivially: the field's posterior is the measurement
   with its instrument uncertainty, through the same card writer. This path needs no stage gating beyond
   admission, and it is the only way a card field changes if the kill gate fires.
7. **The L2 quantities of stage 1** (fitted last, with the L1 posterior as consider parameters; they need a
   bus-voltage channel and temperature channels, and are skipped with a recorded reason when the rig has
   none). *Torque–speed envelope*, from the stall and speed sweep at two bus voltages: the envelope is
   i ≤ min(I_max, (V_lim − k_e·N·ω)/R). At stall the terminal voltage and current give R = v/i. On the
   voltage-limited branch i is linear in ω with slope −k_e·N/R and intercept V_lim/R, so with R known the
   slope gives k_e·N and the intercept gives V_lim; the two bus voltages give V_lim as a function of bus
   voltage (the modulation factor of a three-phase drive) and check that the slope does not depend on it;
   the plateau gives I_max. A weighted least-squares fit of the branches warm starts an output-error fit of
   (R, k_e, I_max, V_lim per bus voltage). *Thermal constants*, from the thermal soak at fixed current:
   the two-node network (winding, housing) has steady winding rise P·(1/g_wh + 1/g_ha) for dissipated power
   P = R·i², and two time constants set by the capacitances c_winding and c_housing; (g_wh, g_ha, c_winding,
   c_housing) are fitted by output error on the winding and case temperature traces through the same
   estimator as pass B. The guard applies as everywhere: a sweep with no stall point and no terminal-voltage
   channel shows only the ratios V_lim/R and k_e·N/R, and a soak with only a case sensor cannot separate the
   winding-to-housing conductance from the winding's capacitance.
8. **Outputs per stage:** a candidate posterior (mean, joint covariance, delay profile), a calibration record,
   candidate card versions. Evidence class Estimated; status `candidate(cell)`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `identify --stage contact` on a cell whose actuator stage is neither done nor declared kept is refused
      with `FROB-REF-879`, pointer `stage`, naming the missing stage; with `--keep-prior actuators` it runs and
      the record says so.
- [ ] A stage whose parameter set fails the guard on the recorded excitation is refused with 879 and the prior
      card identity is unchanged.
- [ ] The model evaluated through the referee reproduces a closed-form constant-velocity case: at steady
      q̇ = 1 rad/s with b = 0.02, τ_c = 0.05, τ_s = 0.08, v_s = 0.1 and `stribeck_shape = "gaussian"` (so the
      Stribeck term is 0.03·e⁻¹⁰⁰ ≈ 0), g = 0: N·η·k_t·i = 0.07 N·m. With `lorentzian` on the same card the
      term is 0.03/101 and the balance is 0.070297 N·m: the shape comes from the card.
- [ ] A later stage's posterior variance for a parameter correlated with an earlier one is not smaller than
      the consider-parameter bound (tested on a two-stage linear fixture).
- [ ] A direct bench measurement of a mass writes a candidate card version in which only that field changed.
- [ ] L2: on a synthetic sweep at 24 V and 36 V with planted R = 0.5 Ω, k_e·N = 0.3 V·s/rad and I_max = 10 A,
      the three are recovered within 5% (stall at 5 V and 10 A gives R); with no stall point and no
      terminal-voltage channel the fit of R is refused as aliased with V_lim and k_e. A
      soak at P = 20 W with planted g_wh = 2 W/K and g_ha = 1 W/K gives a steady winding rise of 30 K and
      recovers both conductances within 10% when winding and case temperatures are both logged.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/identify_staging.rs`: `stage_order_enforced`; `clock_without_fit_refused_879`
(a dataset whose `mcu` clock has one mark: `--stage actuators` on its time-stamped traces is refused with
pointer `stage` naming `clocks`; a request restricted to time-free readings runs); `keep_prior_is_recorded`;
`guard_called_on_recorded_excitation`; `refused_stage_leaves_prior_card`;
`steady_state_torque_balance` (0.07 N·m case); `stribeck_shape_follows_card` (0.070297 N·m under
`lorentzian`); `stiction_row_holds_below_tau_s` (applied 0.06 N·m with
τ_s = 0.08: q̇ = 0 for the whole run); `breakaway_at_tau_s` (a ramp of 0.01 N·m/s starts motion at 8.0 s within
one control period); `lag_and_delay_are_distinct` (a step in i_cmd: zero response before d, then
1 − e^(−t/τ_lag)); `consider_parameters_bound_later_stage`; `later_stage_never_moves_earlier_value`;
`direct_measurement_changes_one_field`; `envelope_stall_gives_resistance` (5 V at 10 A: 0.5 Ω);
`envelope_slope_gives_back_emf_constant`; `envelope_two_voltages_give_vlim_and_same_slope` (the
voltage-limited lines at 24 V and 36 V are 12 V/0.5 Ω = 24 A apart at equal speed);
`envelope_ratios_only_refused_879`; `thermal_soak_steady_rise_30K`; `thermal_case_sensor_only_flags_alias`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh` (bead N30a-26) runs the stages in order on the synthetic rehearsal and each
out-of-order call. Logged per stage: the parameters requested, the guard's assessment, the stage state before
and after, and the artifact identities.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-724 (J_a, b, τ_c, τ_s, v_s and d recovered within 10% on a single-joint rig, delay within one grid step;
control: unidirectional data, τ_c refused) is the stage-1 known answer; its estimator is in the pass beads.
Negative controls: a staging that lets `contact` run on a prior actuator card without saying so (the planted
actuator error must then show up as a wrong friction coefficient, and the test asserts the refusal instead);
a later stage that re-fits an earlier parameter. Determinism: referee evaluations bitwise on one machine.

## Considerations

- Kill gate KILL-GROUND (decided at M5; trigger: the identification gate fails after one repair cycle).
  Fallback: no identification. Ground keeps admission, clocks, frozen predictions and bands on direct
  measurements with prior cards; a card field changes only by direct bench measurement of that field. Step 6
  is that fallback, built from the start.
- The plan lists L2 quantities (torque–speed envelope, thermal constants) inside stage 1; they need a voltage
  channel and temperature sensors, and are fitted after the L1 vector with the L1 values as consider
  parameters.
- "Unloaded on a stand where possible": on an assembled robot, link inertia and armature alias on a joint
  moving alone; the guard will say so and the separating experiment is a second joint moving.
- The riemannian treatment of inertial parameters on the positive-definite cone is recorded as a Frontier
  option for stage 3 and is not required here.
