## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) identifies actuator parameters from logs in three passes. This is the first:
**Pass A, linear.** Away from sticking and saturation the per-joint model

(I(q) + J_a)·q̈ + b·q̇ + τ_f(q̇) + g(q) = N·η·k_t·i(t − d)

is linear in (I + J_a, b, τ_c, bias): with the joint moving well above the Stribeck speed, friction is
τ_c·sgn(q̇), and a constant bias torque τ_b absorbs offsets. A least-squares regressor fit on band-limited
signals gives a warm start for the output-error pass and a first residual plot. (Symbols: q joint position;
I(q) link inertia about the axis; J_a armature; b viscous coefficient; τ_c Coulomb friction; g(q) gravity
torque; N gear ratio; η efficiency; k_t torque constant; i current; d delay.)

Pass A is deliberately not the estimator of record. Least squares on measured regressors is biased (the
regressors q̇ and q̈ are themselves noisy and filtered), it cannot see stiction, the Stribeck speed, delay or
lag, and it ignores shared calibration error. Its jobs are to start pass B close to the answer, so that the
iterated update converges inside its iteration cap, and to show a person where the linear model fails before
any nonlinear machinery hides it.

Rejected alternative: skipping to output error from the prior. An output-error cost is not convex in the
parameters, and the estimator's iteration cap (256) is better spent refining than recovering what one linear
solve gives (HYPOTHESIS; the identification pilot records pass B's iteration count with and without the warm
start, which settles it).

## Scope

In: signal conditioning, sample selection, the regressor, the weighted solve and its diagnostics, the residual
plot data, the hand-off. Out: the stiction and Stribeck parameters, delay and lag (pass C, N30a-23); the
Gaussian update with common-mode error (pass B, N30a-22); writing anything to a card (N30a-27).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** Calibration-split traces of one joint: encoder q and winding current i (and bus voltage if
   present), each with aligned instants (N30a-12); the prior card (k_t, N, η, limits); the model's gravity
   torque g(q) for the stand configuration; a starting delay d₀ from the lag diagnostic (N30a-13), rounded to
   whole samples.
2. **Band-limiting.** One zero-phase low-pass filter (forward-backward fourth-order Butterworth; cut-off f_c,
   default one fifth of the Nyquist frequency or half the lowest declared structural resonance, whichever is
   lower; DESIGN) is applied to q and to i. Velocity and acceleration come from the filtered q by a
   Savitzky–Golay differentiator of matched bandwidth. The same filter on both sides of the equation keeps the
   regression unbiased by the filter itself. The filter definition is written to the calibration record.
3. **Motor-side torque.** τ_m,k = N·η·k_t·i(t_k − d₀) − g(q_k), using the prior k_t (its scale is identified
   later and is aliased with the scale of the other terms when only current and encoder are available).
4. **Sample selection.** Keep sample k only if: |q̇_k| ≥ v_min (default 5 times the prior Stribeck speed, and at
   least 3 standard deviations of the velocity noise); the current is below 98% of its limit and no voltage
   saturation flag is set; k is at least one filter length from a trace edge, from a reversal and from a
   sticking interval. The record stores the kept fraction per trace.
5. **Regressor and solve.** Rows Φ_k = [q̈_k, q̇_k, sgn(q̇_k), 1], target τ_m,k, unknown
   θ_A = (I + J_a, b, τ_c, τ_b). Columns are scaled to unit norm; the weighted least-squares problem (weights
   from the diagonal of the instrument covariance mapped through step 3) is solved by Householder QR.
   Reported: θ_A, its covariance σ̂²·(ΦᵀWΦ)⁻¹ with σ̂² the residual variance, the scaled condition number, and
   the kept sample count.
6. **Rank handling.** If the selected samples have one sign of velocity, the third and fourth columns are
   identical: the solve returns the identified combination τ_c + τ_b and marks τ_c and τ_b individually
   `aliased` (structural alias 2); it does not regularize. If the acceleration column has negligible norm
   (constant-speed data), I + J_a is marked unidentified. Only the sum I + J_a is ever returned (alias 1);
   a split into link inertia and armature uses the model's I_link as a declared input with its uncertainty.
7. **Residual plot data.** r_k = τ_m,k − Φ_k·θ_A, binned three ways: against time (drift, temperature),
   against velocity (20 bins per sign; a low-speed bump is the Stribeck effect), against position (36 bins;
   position-dependent friction or a gravity-model error). Each bin stores mean, standard error and count. The
   artifact is the binned data plus a rendered still for the agent surface.
8. **Hand-off.** `PassA { theta, cov, cond, kept, filter, residual_bins, flags }` goes into the calibration
   record as the first entry of `passes` and into pass B as its starting point.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On an analytic trajectory (a two-tone sinusoid with both velocity signs, exact derivatives, no noise) with
      planted I + J_a = 0.012 kg·m², b = 0.02 N·m·s/rad, τ_c = 0.05 N·m, τ_b = 0.01 N·m, the four values are
      recovered to 10⁻⁹ relative.
- [ ] On the referee-simulated stand trace at 1 kHz with the rehearsal's encoder and current noise, each of the
      four is within 5% of truth (a warm-start tolerance; the 10% gate is pass B's).
- [ ] Unidirectional data returns τ_c + τ_b and flags the pair; no individual value is reported.
- [ ] Samples below v_min, at saturation and near reversals are excluded, and the kept fraction is recorded.
- [ ] With a planted Stribeck term the velocity-binned residual shows a low-speed bump whose sign and size
      match the planted (τ_s − τ_c) within its standard error, while θ_A stays within 5%.
- [ ] Moving the starting delay d₀ by 5 samples changes I + J_a by less than its reported standard deviation
      on the multisine trace (the pass is insensitive to small delay error by construction of the band).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/pass_a.rs`: `exact_recovery_analytic`; `noisy_recovery_within_5pct`;
`one_direction_returns_combination`; `constant_speed_marks_inertia_unidentified`;
`selection_excludes_slow_saturated_and_edge_samples` (a trace with planted counts of each);
`same_filter_both_sides_is_unbiased` (filtering only the regressors biases I + J_a by more than 3%; filtering
both does not); `condition_number_reported`; `residual_bins_show_planted_stribeck`;
`residual_bins_show_gravity_model_error` (a 5% error in the mass moment appears as a sinusoid against
position); `weights_follow_instrument_sigma`; `delay_start_insensitivity`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Part of `scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh` (N30a-26). The script reads the calibration record after
`frob ground identify --stage actuators` and logs pass A's four values with their standard deviations, the
condition number, the kept fraction and the largest binned residual with its bin.

### Known answers and negative controls

The planted values above; KA-724's control (unidirectional data: τ_c is refused) is first visible here as the
returned combination. Negative controls: a solver that adds a ridge term to resolve the collinear columns
(the test asserts that no individual τ_c is returned); a selection that keeps low-speed samples (the planted
Stribeck term then biases τ_c by more than 10% and the 5% test fails). Determinism: double precision, QR with a
fixed column order, bitwise twice on one machine; debug and release.

## Considerations

- Errors-in-variables bias grows as the band widens; f_c is a trade between bias and information. The record
  stores f_c so that pass B's independence from it can be checked (pass B does not use filtered regressors).
- The torque constant is taken from the prior card here. If it is wrong by a factor λ, all four values come out
  scaled by λ: the scale alias (alias 4), which a known payload or a torque sensor resolves in pass B.
- A joint with heavy backlash or a compliant transmission (actuator level L3) violates the rigid model; the
  position-binned residual shows it, and the right response is a different model level, not a better fit.
