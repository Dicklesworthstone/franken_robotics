## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) identifies actuator parameters in three passes. **Pass B, output error**, is the
estimator of record for the smooth parameters. The joint, or the whole robot, is simulated with candidate
parameters θ and compared with the measured encoder, current and torque traces. The Gaussian update is the
iterated Gauss–Newton Kalman update of the sibling crate `fs-assimilate` (`nonlinear::iterated`), with model
Jacobians from the referee tier; outliers go through its Huber-loss fit; a shared encoder bias enters as a
common-mode source through its `groups` module, so a thousand samples that share one bias do not count as a
thousand measurements. Because that update admits at most 256 rows (and at most 256 iterations and 65,536
model evaluations; FACT), traces enter as **windows of summary features**, and the window definition is part
of the calibration record.

Why output error and not equation error: comparing simulated with measured outputs uses the measurements only
as measurements (no differentiated, filtered regressors), so it is unbiased under output noise, and it uses the
real simulator, so the identified card is the one that makes this simulator match, including its own stiction
row and delay handling.

Rejected alternatives: feeding raw samples in chunks of 256 as if chunks were independent (they share the
bias and the trajectory, so the posterior would be far too narrow); writing a new estimator here (the sibling's
has receipts, a line search and a robust loss, and decision ADR-GROUND-08 says estimators are reached through
the airlock as whole jobs).

## Scope

In: the parameter block of this pass; simulation of the experiment with candidate θ; the window definition and
feature rows; the noise model per row; the update loop, its caps and convergence; robust loss; misfit before
and after; the posterior. Out: delay, static friction and Stribeck speed (pass C, N30a-23); the receipt and
energy cross-check (N30a-24); card writing (N30a-27).

## Technical approach

1. **Parameters.** θ_B = (J_a or I + J_a, b, τ_c, τ_lag, bias torque, and the k_t scale only when the fit guard
   reports it identified: a known payload or a torque sensor is present). Delay d, static friction τ_s and
   Stribeck speed v_s are held at their current values (prior, or pass C's latest). Prior: Gaussian, mean and
   standard deviation from the card fields; start: pass A's values for the parameters it estimated.
2. **Forward model h(θ).** Replay the logged command of each calibration trace through the referee with the
   candidate card bound in (level L1, the stiction row active), on the stand scene of the experiment, and read
   the simulated instruments at the instants the real ones declare. One evaluation of h is one batch of
   referee runs (one per trace).
3. **Windows** (`WindowDef`, DESIGN; stored in the record). Each trace is cut into windows of fixed length
   (default 0.1 s). Per window and channel the features are: the mean, the least-squares slope, and for an
   encoder the increment (end minus start). Windows containing a saturation flag or a clock gap are dropped
   and listed. The row count is channels × features × windows summed over traces and must be ≤ 256; if it is
   not, the window length is doubled until it is, and the final length is recorded. Features of the measured
   and the simulated traces are computed by the same code.
4. **Row noise.** From the instrument model (N30a-08): a mean of m samples has variance σ²/m plus the full
   common-mode term; an increment cancels the common-mode term; slopes get the least-squares slope variance.
   Common-mode groups (one per calibration identity, one per clock for alignment error) are declared to the
   estimator. Consider parameters from earlier stages enter as further shared parameters (at most 64 in all).
5. **The update** (through `IteratedJob`, N30a-05). With prior (θ₀, P₀), rows y, covariance R:
   at iterate θ_j compute H_j = ∂h/∂θ by central differences on the referee (step 10⁻³ of each prior standard
   deviation); K_j = P₀·H_jᵀ·(H_j·P₀·H_jᵀ + R)⁻¹; proposal θ⁺ = θ₀ + K_j·[y − h(θ_j) − H_j·(θ₀ − θ_j)]; an
   Armijo line search between θ_j and θ⁺ on the objective
   J(θ) = ½·(y − h(θ))ᵀ·R⁻¹·(y − h(θ)) + ½·(θ − θ₀)ᵀ·P₀⁻¹·(θ − θ₀). Stop when the relative decrease of J is
   below 10⁻⁸ or the scaled step norm is below 10⁻⁶. Posterior covariance P = (P₀⁻¹ + Hᵀ·R⁻¹·H)⁻¹ at the
   final iterate. Budget: each iteration costs 2·p + 1 evaluations plus line-search trials; the loop stops and
   the stage fails closed (prior card kept, a rejected record written) if 256 iterations or 65,536 evaluations
   are reached without convergence.
6. **Robust loss.** Rows whose standardized residual exceeds the Huber constant (1.345, DESIGN) are
   down-weighted by the sibling's Huber fit; the record lists them with their windows. More than 10% of rows
   down-weighted marks the fit `model-suspect`.
7. **Misfit.** Reported before (at θ₀) and after (at the posterior mean): χ² = (y − h)ᵀ·R⁻¹·(y − h), its
   degrees of freedom (rows minus fitted parameters), and the reduced value. A reduced χ² far above 1 after
   the fit is evidence of model-form error and feeds the discrepancy floor later; it does not widen P here.
8. **Whole-robot variant.** The same loop with all joints' posteriors assembled and every joint simulated,
   used as a consistency check after the joint-by-joint fits: it reports misfit only and re-estimates nothing.
9. **Interim posture.** The windowing exists because of the 256-row cap. Upstream work item `FD-FSIM-23` (a
   windowed or streaming entry beyond 256 rows) would remove it; the switch-over test is to re-fit one
   recorded dataset both ways and compare posteriors.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For a forward model that is linear in θ, one iteration reproduces the closed-form Kalman posterior
      (mean and covariance to 10⁻¹⁰).
- [ ] On the synthetic single-joint rig with the designed excitation, J_a, b and τ_c are each within 10% of
      the planted values (the smooth part of the actuator-recovery known answer), and the posterior's
      standard deviations cover the errors (|error| ≤ 3·σ for each).
- [ ] Doubling the trace length twice does not push the posterior standard deviation of the bias torque below
      the planted common-mode floor.
- [ ] Ten planted corrupted windows (a 5σ encoder glitch) are listed as down-weighted and move no parameter by
      more than its standard deviation.
- [ ] A dataset that would give 400 rows is re-windowed to at most 256 and the record states the final
      window length; no call to the estimator exceeds a cap.
- [ ] Non-convergence inside the caps leaves the prior card in force and writes a record with status rejected.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/pass_b.rs`: `linear_model_one_iteration_is_kalman`; `recovers_smooth_parameters_10pct`
(planted J_a = 4·10⁻⁴ kg·m², b = 0.02, τ_c = 0.05; seeded noise); `posterior_covers_error`;
`common_mode_floor_survives_longer_trace`; `huber_lists_planted_outliers`; `row_cap_rewindows`;
`features_same_code_for_real_and_simulated`; `increment_feature_cancels_bias`;
`kt_scale_fitted_only_when_guard_allows`; `nonconvergence_fails_closed`; `misfit_before_exceeds_after`;
`evaluation_budget_accounting` (iterations × (2p + 1) plus line-search trials never exceeds 65,536);
`whole_robot_variant_estimates_nothing`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Part of `scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh` (N30a-26). Logged from the calibration record: the window length and
row count, iterations and evaluations used against their caps, χ² before and after with degrees of freedom,
the down-weighted rows, each parameter's posterior mean, standard deviation and error against the planted
value, and the airlock mode (in process or worker), with equal digests asserted across the two modes.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-724 (each planted parameter within 10%). Negative controls: chunked raw samples treated as independent (the
bias-floor test fails); a diagonal R (same); Jacobians taken from a re-implemented joint model instead of the
referee (a planted difference in the stiction handling makes τ_c miss by more than 10%). Metamorphic: scaling a
channel's unit together with its σ leaves the posterior unchanged; permuting traces leaves it unchanged.
Determinism: referee and estimator bitwise twice on one machine; across machines inside the recorded tolerance.

## Considerations

- Windows of means and slopes discard high-frequency content; the designed excitation (multisine lines below
  the window rate) keeps the information in band. The plan's rule is only that the window definition be
  recorded; the feature set here is DESIGN and is what the pilot evaluates.
- Evaluation cost: p = 6 parameters, 13 evaluations per iteration, each a batch of short referee runs; seconds
  to minutes per joint, a whole job for the airlock.
- The Gaussian posterior is local. Parameters with non-smooth effect are kept out of this pass on purpose.
