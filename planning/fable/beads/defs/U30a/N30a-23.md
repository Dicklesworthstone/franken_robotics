## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) identifies actuator parameters in three passes. Passes A and B handle parameters
whose effect on the output is smooth. **Pass C handles the nonsmooth ones.**

- **Delay is profiled, not differentiated.** The simulator's time is a rational lattice, a delay moves a
  command across sample boundaries, and the output is a staircase in the delay: a finite-difference Jacobian
  is zero or a spike. So the other parameters are fitted at each delay on a grid of rational fractions of the
  control period, and the result is the profile, its minimum, and the set of delays inside the 95%
  likelihood-ratio bound (TARGET).
- **Static friction and the Stribeck speed are estimated from the ramp experiments by the ensemble lane**, run
  as batched rollouts with per-world parameter variants, because the stick–slip likelihood has no useful
  Jacobian: whether and when a joint breaks away is a threshold event.

The ensemble lane is the sibling crate `fs-assimilate`'s deterministic serial ensemble square-root filter
(`nonlinear::ensemble`, behind feature `ensemble`). FACT: it is feature-gated and claims no cross-ISA
determinism. Per-world parameter variants are the batched engine's mechanism for running many worlds that
share a model and differ in a parameter row, which is exactly an ensemble.

Rejected alternatives: smoothing the stick–slip transition so that a Jacobian exists (the fitted parameters
would then belong to the smoothed model, not the simulator's stiction row); treating delay as a continuous
parameter with interpolated commands (the identified value would not be one the simulator can represent).

## Scope

In: the delay grid, the profile and its likelihood-ratio set; the ensemble estimation of τ_s and v_s; how the
three passes alternate; the combined posterior. Out: whether a lag may be called a delay (the `Delay` against
`DelayPlusOffset` types, N30a-13); the smooth update (N30a-22); the record (N30a-24).

## Technical approach

1. **Delay grid.** d_j = j·T_c/G for j = 0..J, with T_c the controller's period (a rational from the clock
   contract), G = 4 (DESIGN: quarter-period resolution) and J·T_c/G covering the prior mean ± 4 prior standard
   deviations, at least 0 to 5·T_c. Every d_j is on the simulator's lattice, so each is exactly representable.
2. **Profile.** For each d_j, run pass B with d fixed at d_j (warm-started from the neighbouring grid value)
   and record PL(d_j) = 2·J*(d_j), twice the minimized objective. Results: the table {(d_j, PL)}; the
   minimizer d̂; the set D₉₅ = {d_j : PL(d_j) − PL(d̂) ≤ 3.84} (the 95% point of χ² with one degree of
   freedom). Grid values are independent jobs and may run concurrently.
3. **Reported delay.** d̂ with D₉₅, typed `Delay` when the command and response clocks are linked by marks and
   `DelayPlusOffset` otherwise (N30a-13). If D₉₅ reaches an end of the grid the profile is flat on that side
   and the delay is reported unidentified. Smooth parameters are reported at d̂, and each one's variance is
   increased by the square of half its range across D₉₅ (DESIGN: uncertainty in the delay is not free).
4. **Ensemble for (τ_s, v_s).**
   1. Members: M = 64 (DESIGN) parameter pairs drawn from the prior by a Latin hypercube keyed by world
      address; all other parameters at their current estimates.
   2. Forecast: one batched run of every torque-ramp trace (slow ramps from rest, both directions) with one
      world per member, as per-world parameter variants on the CPU batch tier or the referee.
   3. Observations (features with no smooth dependence on parameters): per ramp, the commanded torque at
      first motion (the first instant |q̇| exceeds three standard deviations of velocity noise), and the mean
      speed over each of four intervals after breakaway. Their noise comes from the instrument model.
   4. Update: `EnsembleJob` (N30a-05) assimilates the observations serially with the square-root update,
      using ensemble cross-covariances between parameters and predicted observations.
   5. Iterate forecast and update (at most 8 rounds) until the ensemble mean moves by less than 0.1 of the
      ensemble standard deviation in both parameters. Posterior: ensemble mean and 2×2 covariance.
   The guard has already refused τ_s when no trace dwells at zero velocity (structural alias 7).
5. **Alternation.** (i) lag diagnostic gives a starting delay; (ii) pass A; (iii) delay profile with pass B
   inside; (iv) ensemble for (τ_s, v_s) at d̂; (v) a final pass B at d̂ with (τ_s, v_s) at the ensemble mean.
   One repeat of (iii) to (v) is run if d̂ changed by more than one grid step after (iv).
6. **Combined posterior.** Mean: the final pass B values, d̂, the ensemble mean. Covariance: pass B's block
   (inflated per step 3), the ensemble's 2×2 block, and zero cross-terms between the blocks, flagged
   `cross-block covariance not estimated` in the record (DESIGN; a known gap).
7. **Determinism and fingerprint.** Grid and profile: deterministic per platform. Ensemble results are
   recorded with the machine fingerprint and are not compared across machines; upstream work item
   `FD-FSIM-24` (a cross-ISA statement for the ensemble lane) would lift this, and its switch-over test is to
   re-run the rehearsal's replay known answers on a second machine and record the comparison.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Planted delay 8 ms at a 1 kHz controller with linked clocks: d̂ is within one grid step (0.25 ms) of
      8 ms and D₉₅ contains 8 ms.
- [ ] Planted τ_s = 0.08 N·m and v_s = 0.10 rad/s on a single-joint rig with ramps in both directions: both
      recovered within 10%, and the ensemble standard deviations cover the errors within 3σ.
- [ ] The profile of a delay that the data cannot constrain (constant command) is flat and the delay is
      reported unidentified, with no value written.
- [ ] With unlinked clocks the result type is `DelayPlusOffset` and the card delay is unchanged.
- [ ] Two runs on one machine give bit-identical profiles and ensemble posteriors; the record carries the
      machine fingerprint for the ensemble part.
- [ ] Smooth parameters' reported variances are not smaller than pass B's at d̂.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/pass_c.rs`: `grid_values_are_on_lattice` (T_c = 1/1000 s, G = 4: every d_j is a
multiple of 1/4000 s exactly); `profile_minimum_at_planted_delay`; `lr_set_contains_truth_and_uses_3p84`;
`flat_profile_is_unidentified`; `variance_inflation_from_delay_set`; `ensemble_recovers_tau_s_and_v_s`;
`ensemble_members_by_address_are_reproducible`; `breakaway_feature_definition` (a ramp of 0.01 N·m/s with
τ_s = 0.08 gives first motion at 0.080 ± 0.001 N·m in the simulated trace); `ensemble_stops_on_small_move`;
`no_dwell_no_tau_s` (the guard's refusal is honoured: the ensemble is not run);
`alternation_repeats_once_on_delay_change`; `cross_block_flag_present`; `fingerprint_recorded`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Part of `scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh` (N30a-26). Logged: the delay grid step, the profile table (delay,
PL), d̂ and D₉₅; the ensemble size, rounds, and per-round mean and standard deviation of τ_s and v_s; the
machine fingerprint; errors against the planted values.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-724 (τ_s, v_s and d among the six recovered parameters: each within 10%, delay within one grid step).
KA-725 (delay plus offset when no mark is shared). Negative controls: a delay estimated by a finite-difference
Jacobian (on the staircase it either stalls at the start value or jumps; the planted 8 ms is missed and the
test must fail); an ensemble whose members are drawn in arrival order rather than by address (two runs
differ; the reproducibility test fails); a smoothed stiction model in the forecast (τ_s misses by more than
10%). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; in-process and worker airlock with equal digests; no
cross-machine bit comparison for the ensemble part.

## Considerations

- Cost: about 21 grid values × a pass B each, plus up to 8 ensemble rounds of 64 worlds over the ramp traces.
  Minutes per joint on the CPU batch tier; the verb returns a job handle.
- The ensemble approximates a non-Gaussian posterior by a Gaussian; a bimodal breakaway (direction-dependent
  stiction) shows up as a wide, poorly converging ensemble, and the record flags non-convergence after 8
  rounds, in which case the stage fails closed for those two parameters only.
- Direction-dependent static friction is not in the L1 model; if the two directions' breakaway torques differ
  by more than their uncertainty the record says so and the fit is marked `model-suspect`.
- G = 4 and M = 64 are DESIGN defaults; both are recorded so that the gate's "one grid step" has a definite
  meaning.
