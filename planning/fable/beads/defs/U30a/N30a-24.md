## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) writes a **calibration record** for every identification run: the receipt that
lets a later reader see what was fitted, from what, how well, and whether the fit can be trusted for a physical
reason. The record holds the prior, the windows, each pass, the posterior (mean, joint covariance, delay
profile), misfit before and after, and the autocorrelation of residuals at control-rate lags.

It also holds one independent cross-check: **the energy ledger evaluated on the real log**. A model can fit
nominal motion for the wrong physical reason, with current draw and mechanical work disagreeing while speed
looks right (program risk RISK-42, owner: Ground lead). The standard case: a torque constant that is too high
by some factor and friction that is too high by the same torque amount reproduce the measured speed exactly
under commanded current; the fit is excellent and the motor it describes does not exist. Energy is the
bookkeeping that such a pair cannot satisfy on both the electrical and the mechanical side. A fit whose
electrical-to-mechanical energy residual exceeds the instrument bound is marked `wrong-reason` and cannot be
admitted.

The energy ledger is the simulator's own accounting identity, per world and episode:
battery energy out = Σ over actuators (copper loss + friction loss + gear loss + Δ stored) + mechanical work at
joints; mechanical work at joints = Δ kinetic + Δ potential + contact dissipation + joint damping + work on
fixtures and payloads + numerical residual. Every term is a port quantity.

## Scope

In: the record's type, schema and immutability; residual diagnostics; the energy cross-check and its bound;
the labels. Out: the passes that fill it (N30a-21 to N30a-23); the discrepancy floor (N30a-29); card versions
(N30a-27); admission (N30a-28).

## Technical approach

1. **Type** (the plan's sketch, DESIGN):
   ```rust
   pub struct CalibrationRecord {
       dataset: DatasetId,                 // a .frun bundle with licence, rights and splits
       cell: CellKey,                      // one robot calibrates one robot
       prior: CardVersion,
       windows: WindowDef,                 // traces enter as windows of summary features
       passes: Vec<Pass>,                  // A linear; B output error; C nonsmooth parameters, delay profiled
       posterior: Posterior,               // mean, joint covariance, delay profile with its 95% set
       misfit: (f64, f64),                 // before and after
       residual_autocorrelation: Vec<f64>, // at control-rate lags
       energy_check: EnergyCheck,          // the energy ledger evaluated on the real log
       discrepancy_floor: Variance,        // never reported below the model-form variance
       status: CardStatus,                 // Candidate until a frozen prediction covers on sealed data
       labels: Vec<GroundLabel>,           // e.g. WrongReason, RankingFalsified
   }
   pub enum CardStatus { Candidate, Admitted, Rejected }
   ```
   Schema `frob.ground.calibration/1`. The record is a content-addressed artifact and is never rewritten.
   `status` and `discrepancy_floor` are the values at writing time (`Candidate`, not yet estimated); later
   changes are ledger events that reference the record (`ground.status`, `ground.floor`), and
   `CalibrationRecord::current(ledger)` returns the view with them applied. Further labels (DESIGN):
   `ModelSuspect`, `ResidualsCorrelated`, `EnergyUnchecked`, `CrossBlockNotEstimated`, `KeptPrior(stage)`.
2. **Residual autocorrelation.** From pass B's final residuals on the un-windowed encoder trace (simulated
   minus measured at the posterior mean), resampled at the control rate: ρ(k) for lags k = 1..20 control
   periods. The record stores the 20 values and the Ljung–Box statistic Q = n·(n + 2)·Σ ρ(k)²/(n − k). If Q
   exceeds the 99% point of χ² with 20 degrees of freedom (37.57) the record gets `ResidualsCorrelated`:
   structure the model does not explain remains at control-rate time scales. It is information for the
   reader and for the discrepancy floor; it does not block.
3. **Energy cross-check.** Over each calibration trace with a bus-voltage and a current channel:
   - measured electrical energy E_el = ∫ v·i dt (trapezoid on the aligned samples; positive and negative
     parts kept separately, so regeneration is visible);
   - the model's account of where it went, evaluated with the fitted card **on the measured motion and
     current**: copper loss ∫ R·i² dt; gear loss ∫ (1 − η)·|N·k_t·i·q̇| dt; friction loss
     ∫ (b·q̇² + |τ_f(q̇)·q̇|) dt; Δ kinetic = ½·(I + J_a)·(q̇_end² − q̇_start²); Δ potential from g(q); work on a
     known payload; Δ stored magnetic energy ½·L·i² where the card states L;
   - residual r_E = E_el − (sum of the model's terms), and the relative residual r_E/∫|v·i| dt.
   **Instrument bound** B_E: the uncertainty of r_E from the instruments alone. Noise terms are propagated to
   a variance (each integral is a weighted sum of samples); bias bounds are propagated as intervals (a
   current bias δ_i shifts E_el by at most δ_i·∫|v| dt, and so on); B_E = 3·√(noise variance) + the sum of
   the absolute bias contributions. If |r_E| > B_E on the pooled calibration traces, the record gets
   `WrongReason`, `energy_check = Failed { r_E, B_E, largest_term }`, and the candidate **cannot be admitted**:
   scoring refuses to admit a record carrying the label, whatever its prediction error.
4. **When the check cannot run.** With no voltage channel, E_el is not measured:
   `energy_check = NotEvaluable { missing: "bus voltage" }` and the label `EnergyUnchecked`. The candidate may
   proceed to validation, the label is printed with the card version and in reports, and under `--strict-data`
   an `EnergyUnchecked` card version cannot be admitted (DESIGN: the plan defines the failing case only; the
   fail-closed reading is available by flag and the default is to show the gap).
5. **Why it catches the compensating pair.** With k_t too high by a factor λ and friction raised to match the
   speed, the model's mechanical account uses the same measured motion, but its electrical account changes:
   the back-EMF power k_e·N·q̇·i (k_e is tied to k_t by the card's power-invariance check) and the friction
   loss are both too large, so the model says more electrical energy was needed than was measured.
6. **Printing.** `frob ground identify --json` returns the record's identity and a summary: misfit before and
   after, labels, energy residual against bound, Q.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The compensating-error case: truth k_t and friction; a fit forced to k_t·1.25 with friction raised so
      that the simulated speed matches within noise. The record carries `WrongReason`, and an attempt to
      admit it is refused.
- [ ] The honest fit of the same data has |r_E| ≤ B_E and no label.
- [ ] With the voltage channel removed: `EnergyUnchecked`; admissible by default, not under `--strict-data`.
- [ ] White residuals give Q below 37.57; residuals with a planted 5 Hz unmodelled oscillation give
      `ResidualsCorrelated`.
- [ ] The record's bytes never change after writing; `current(ledger)` reflects a later admission event.
- [ ] The record contains the window definition, every pass, the delay profile and its 95% set, and both
      misfit values.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-727 (wrong reason: speed matched by a compensating error in k_t and friction; the energy cross-check marks
`wrong-reason`; the plan states no control; DESIGN control: the same fit with the check disabled is offered
for admission and the test must fail). Counterexample CX-332 (matching the observed speed validates the
model). Negative controls: an energy account evaluated on the simulated motion instead of the measured one
(the compensating pair then balances by construction and the label is missed); a bound that omits bias terms
(the honest fit is falsely flagged on a rig with a known current offset). Determinism: integrals in double
precision with a fixed summation order, bitwise twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- The bound trusts the declared instrument uncertainties. An optimistic manifest makes honest fits fail the
  check; that is the right direction of error, and the record shows which term dominates.
- On a whole robot in contact, contact dissipation is not measured, so the cross-check is defined for
  stand experiments and for free-swing traces; for contact-rich logs it is `NotEvaluable` with the reason.
- Temperature: copper loss uses R at the logged winding temperature when a temperature channel exists, else
  the card's reference value with its temperature coefficient as an added interval.
- The plan's sketch places `status` inside the record; the immutable-record-plus-events reading is DESIGN and
  keeps the invariant that a card and its receipt are never mutated.
