## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) identifies model parameters from admitted data in a fixed order (decision
ADR-GROUND-03): clocks, actuators, contact and surface, inertia and geometry, sensors. Each stage's error
aliases into the next, so each stage is fitted with the earlier stages' results held as known-with-uncertainty.
The actuator stage has its own beads. This bead is the three stages after it, as the plan states them:

- **(2) Contact and surface:** friction per material pair under at least two normal loads, dissipation, rolling
  resistance.
- **(3) Inertia and geometry:** from scale and pendulum measurements and from as-built scans.
- **(4) Sensors:** bias, noise, latency.

Why this order among the three: a friction coefficient read from a drive test contains whatever actuator
torque error was left (so actuators come first); an inertia read from a dynamic test on a surface contains the
contact's dissipation; a sensor latency read against the robot's motion contains actuator delay and the
motion model. Direct bench measurements (a scale, a pendulum) depend on none of these and may be admitted at
any time through the direct-measurement path.

Tier: Frontier. No separate pilot is named for these stages; the identification pilot covers one joint, and
the gate GATE-GROUND-04 is stated on actuator parameters. These stages are therefore held to the same
machinery and to their own closed-form known answers below.

## Scope

In: per stage, the parameters, the experiments that inform them, the closed-form warm starts, the output-error
fit, the guards, and where the posterior is written. Out: staging and gating rules (N30a-20); the estimator
(N30a-22, reused); scan registration (N30a-45 to N30a-47); the card writer (N30a-27).

## Technical approach

1. **Stage 2a: friction per material pair.** Experiments: tilt to first slip; sled drag at two or more normal
   loads; a drive test that reaches the traction-limited regime. Warm starts: μ_s = tan θ_slip;
   kinetic friction from the drag line F = μ_k·N_load + F₀ fitted over the loads (two loads separate μ_k from a
   load-independent offset F₀). Fit: output error on the referee with the pair's contact model, with the
   actuator posteriors as consider parameters when a driven test is used. Guards: fewer than two normal loads
   is refused (the offset aliases with μ); a driven test that never leaves the torque-limited regime yields
   no μ (structural alias 6: with F = min(k_t·I/r, μ·N) the sensitivity to μ is zero).
2. **Stage 2b: dissipation.** Experiment: drops from two heights with a force or acceleration trace. Readings:
   contact duration, peak force, rebound ratio. Parameters: the contact law's stiffness and dissipation for
   the pair. Guard: one drop height with the rebound ratio only is one number for two parameters (alias 8).
3. **Stage 2c: rolling resistance.** Experiment: coast-down of the wheeled body on the surface with the drive
   disengaged or commanded to zero torque. Reading: deceleration against speed; the intercept gives the
   rolling-resistance coefficient c_rr = a₀/g after subtracting the drivetrain's identified friction torque
   reflected to the wheel (from stage 1, as a consider parameter), the slope gives a speed-dependent term.
4. **Stage 3: inertia and geometry.** Direct measurements: mass from a scale; centre of mass from a two-support
   balance (distance from support 1: x_c = L·W₂/(W₁ + W₂), with support spacing L and readings W₁, W₂);
   inertia about an axis from a bifilar pendulum (I = m·g·d²·T²/(16·π²·L_f), filament separation d, filament
   length L_f, period T). Geometry deltas and volume from as-built scans. Dynamic refinement (optional): an
   output-error fit of link inertial parameters on free-swing traces. Physical-consistency guard on every
   posterior mean: mass positive; the inertia tensor positive definite with principal moments satisfying the
   triangle inequalities; centre of mass inside the part's bounding hull. A posterior that violates any is
   not written; the stage fails closed to the prior with the violated condition named.
5. **Stage 4: sensors.** Bias: the mean of a stationary segment against a known reference (a gyro at rest
   reads its bias; an accelerometer at rest reads gravity in its frame; an encoder against an index mark).
   Noise: the sample variance of the same segment, and its autocorrelation to check the white-noise
   assumption. Latency: the step-with-shared-mark experiment, by the same profiling as actuator delay, typed
   `Delay` only when the clocks are linked (otherwise "latency plus offset", and the sensor card is not
   updated). The identified actuator delay is a consider parameter, so that sensor latency does not absorb it.
6. **Common rules.** Each stage calls the fit guard on the recorded excitation; writes a calibration record
   with its passes, misfit before and after, and residual diagnostics; produces candidate card versions
   (material, surface, payload or sensor cards, or `.fbot` mass-property parameters) with provenance
   `measured`; and leaves randomization widths unchanged until admission.
7. **Folded from the superseded bead N30b-05.**
   - *Stage gate refusal.* A stage runs only when the cell holds a calibration record of the stage before it;
     otherwise the request is refused with `FROB-REF-879`, pointer `stage` (the staging rule is N30a-20's;
     this is its code and payload). Each stage's record carries the field `stage` and names the earlier
     stages' records as inputs.
   - *Uncertainties of the closed forms.* Incline: σ_μ = σ_θ/cos² θ_slip. Drop: the rebound ratio is
     e = √(h_rebound/h_drop), and for a linear spring and damper the damping ratio is
     ζ = −ln e/√(π² + ln² e); a force trace gives the contact duration and so the natural frequency.
     Coast-down: a = −c_rr·g − (b/m)·v.
   - *Source per field.* Every stage reports, per field, whether the value came from a direct measurement,
     from an output-error fit, or stayed at the prior; nothing is regularized toward the prior without being
     reported as prior. Direct measurements are linear Gaussian observations fused through the sibling's
     sequential update behind its dimensionally typed front door (N30a-05), so units are checked.
   - *Condition-keyed cards.* Interface cards are keyed by material pair and condition: a friction value
     measured dry does not change the wet card's content address.
   - *Sensors.* The instrument's shared calibration error enters the bias estimate as a common-mode source. A
     latency step with no shared mark is refused with 879 and reports "latency plus clock offset". A range or
     vision sensor's exposure delay against its mount offset follows the ninth alias (N30b-29). The stage
     supplies bias, noise and latency fields and leaves geometry between sensors to the sensing subsystem's
     extrinsic calibration.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Incline: a planted μ_s = 0.40 gives first slip at 21.8° and is recovered within 2%.
- [ ] Sled: planted μ_k = 0.40 and F₀ = 0.5 N read 4.5 N at 10 N and 8.5 N at 20 N; both recovered; with one
      load the fit is refused with 879 and the combination "μ_k·N + F₀ at the tested load" is named.
- [ ] A torque-limited drive dataset yields no μ (refused); adding the traction-limited run identifies it.
- [ ] Balance: L = 0.50 m, W₁ = 12 N, W₂ = 8 N gives x_c = 0.20 m. Pendulum: m = 2 kg, d = 0.2 m, L_f = 1 m,
      T = 1.5 s gives I = 0.01118 kg·m².
- [ ] A fitted inertia with a negative principal moment, or moments (1, 1, 3) that violate the triangle
      inequality, is not written and the stage fails closed.
- [ ] Sensors: a planted gyro bias of 0.01 rad/s and noise density recovered within their standard errors; a
      planted latency of 4 ms recovered within one grid step with linked clocks and reported as "latency plus
      offset" without them.
- [ ] Running stage 2 on a prior (unidentified) actuator card with a planted 20% torque-constant error biases
      μ; the staging rule refuses that call unless `--keep-prior actuators` is given, and then records it.
- [ ] `--stage contact` without a stage-1 record in the cell is refused with `FROB-REF-879`, pointer `stage`;
      with it, the stage writes a record whose inputs name the stage-1 record.
- [ ] One drop height with the rebound ratio only, and a latency step with no shared mark, are each refused
      with 879, each naming the identified combination and the separating experiment.
- [ ] A dry-surface measurement leaves the wet interface card's content address unchanged; every written field
      carries `measured`, the dataset hash, the cell and status `candidate`, and fields that stayed at the
      prior say so.
- [ ] A period entered without a unit is refused by the typed front door.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-723 (the drawbar forces; the torque-limited data alone yields no μ). The closed forms above. Negative
controls: a stage-2 fit that ignores the offset F₀ (reads μ_k = 0.45 at 10 N); a stage-3 writer without the
cone guard; a latency estimator that does not carry actuator delay (the planted 8 ms actuator delay appears in
the sensor's latency). Determinism: closed forms exact or double precision; referee fits bitwise on one machine.
Folded from N30b-05. Further planted failures: a stage-2 fit run with a deliberately wrong actuator card and
no stage gate (the friction estimate absorbs the error: the control shows the reason for the order; it is
case CX-G-08 of the chapter's counterexample registry); a sled fit that reports μ_k from one load.
Counterexample CX-329 and aliases 6, 7 and 8 of CX-328. Metamorphic relations: adding a constant to both sled
forces changes F₀ only; doubling both drop heights with the same rebound ratio leaves ζ unchanged.

## Considerations

- Friction "per material pair" means the result belongs to a pair and a surface condition; it is written to
  the pair's card and scoped by the cell's surface class. A dry result is never used for a wet floor.
- The plan gives each of these stages one line. The experiments, warm starts and guards here are DESIGN, built
  from the excitation grammar's rows; they should be confirmed by the pilot's extension to a cart before the
  stages are relied on.
- Treating inertial parameters on the positive-definite cone (a geometry-aware fit) is a recorded Frontier
  option for stage 3; the guard here is the minimum that keeps a non-physical body out of the model.
- If the kill gate KILL-GROUND fires, only the direct measurements of this bead survive (scale, balance,
  pendulum, incline, each a direct measurement of one field).
- These stages have no gate of their own; they are exercised by the rehearsal's rows and by bench packages B
  and D if those ever run. Their absence from a calibration is visible: fields stay `generic` or `assumed` and
  the guess share says so.
- A bifilar pendulum measures one axis per hang; three hangs give the diagonal, and products of inertia need
  the as-built route or oblique hangs. The record states which components were measured.
