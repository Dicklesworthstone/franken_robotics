## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) identifies actuator parameters from logs in a fixed order and in three passes
(a linear regressor fit; an output-error fit on the referee through the sibling's iterated Gauss–Newton Kalman
update; a delay profile and an ensemble estimate of static friction and Stribeck speed), and writes the
posterior as candidate card versions. Whether this works is decided without hardware, on the synthetic
rehearsal: a truth simulator with planted parameters emits logs through the sensor models, labelled
`synthetic`.

**GATE-GROUND-04** (claim kind: known answer; milestone M5; TARGET), three parts: (a) planted actuator
parameters within 10% on at least 95 of 100 synthetic rigs; (b) identified cards cut held-out trajectory error
by at least 30% against prior cards; (c) the omitted-law candidate is rejected in at least 90 of 100.

**KILL-GROUND** (decided at M5): if this gate fails after one repair cycle, there is no identification. Ground
keeps admission, clocks, frozen predictions and bands on direct measurements with prior cards, and a card
field changes only by direct bench measurement of that field.

## Scope

In: the script, the rig population, the three measurements, the associated known answers and controls, the log
and the gate record; the stage-3 per-chart inertia leg that N13-30 and BD-391 assign to this script. Out: the
mechanisms (BD-391 and its parts; the charts, N13-30); the kill-gate decision (N30b-17).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh [--seed S] [--rigs 100] [--controls] [--quick] [--stage <name>]`
(stages of step 11; without `--stage` every step runs), plain shell over
`frob`, `frobd` and the rehearsal generator, under `target/e2e-work/ground_identify/`; default seed 4.
`--quick` runs 10 rigs for development and never records the gate.

1. **Population A (recovery and error cut).** 100 single-joint stand rigs, the population of fixture R-04 of
   the rehearsal catalogue (BD-388; step 9 gives its values). Per rig, planted
   θ = (J_a, b, τ_c, τ_s, v_s, d) drawn by address (each parameter log-uniform in [0.7, 1.4] times the
   nominal; delay on the 1/8 ms grid in [1, 5] ms), a prior card whose fields are off by factors in
   [0.5, 2.0] with their stated uncertainty, clocks with planted skew and two
   shared marks, encoder and current channels with the rehearsal's noise and one shared encoder bias. Data:
   the excitation plan returned by `frob ground excite <rig design> --cards <prior set> --rig <rig>
   --params stage:actuators --budget-s 1800 --json` for that rig, simulated by the truth simulator; split by
   day into calibration, validation and holdout; validation and holdout sealed.
2. **Per rig i,** in the registry's forms, each `run` and `mutate` call with a key derived from the seed and
   the rig index (KNOWN_DRIFTS 5; this is what makes the battery resumable, step 9):
   `frob ground ingest <bundle> --design <rig design> --task-family tf:none --key gi-in-<S>-<i> --json`; the
   `excite` call of step 1; `frob ground identify <dataset> --stage actuators --params <the parameter list
   the excite report prints, passed on unchanged> --key gi-id-<S>-<i> --json` (BD-391's `--params` names:
   `armature`, `viscous`, `coulomb`, `static`, `stribeck_speed`, `delay`, `lag`, `kt_scale`; a long verb, so
   exit 8 is followed by a wait on the job handle); then for part (b) two predictions of the validation
   trajectory (a command sequence not in the calibration split), one with the prior card and one with the
   candidate card, both frozen with `frob ground predict --dataset <dataset> --part validation --cell <cell>
   --model <the model with the prior cards | with the candidate cards> --scene <stand scene> --task
   <validation trajectory> --metric encoder_rms_rad:0.90 --decision "<decision>" --study fixed-n:1 --key
   gi-pr-<S>-<i>-<prior|cand> --json` before `frob ground score <prediction> --key gi-sc-<S>-<i>-<prior|cand>
   --json` opens the validation part.
   From each calibration record assert: it names the card's `stribeck_shape` (`gaussian` on every rig of
   R-04, which pins it; the shape is a card field and is never fitted, N30a-20); pass B's experiments were
   assimilated one call per experiment in ascending order of experiment identifier, the recorded order
   equal to the sorted identifiers; and the posterior standard deviation of the bias torque is at or above
   the planted common-mode floor of the shared encoder bias across the whole sequence (N30a-22).
3. **Part (a).** A rig passes when each of J_a, b, τ_c, τ_s and v_s is within 10% of its planted value and the
   delay is within one grid step. Assert passes ≥ 95 of 100. Also log, per parameter, the fraction within 10%
   and the fraction of errors inside 3 posterior standard deviations (expected near 0.997; below 0.95 is a
   finding that the posterior is too narrow).
4. **Part (b).** Per rig, e_prior and e_cand: the root-mean-square encoder error over the validation
   trajectory, as scored. The gate statistic is 1 − (Σ e_cand)/(Σ e_prior) over the 100 rigs; assert ≥ 0.30.
   Logged as well: the median per-rig reduction and the count of rigs where the candidate was worse.
5. **Population B (omitted law).** 100 rigs of fixture R-06, whose truth has a Stribeck term while the fit
   class is restricted to τ_s = τ_c (Coulomb plus viscous only). Per rig: identify in that class, freeze a
   prediction for a validation trajectory that dwells at low speed, score. The candidate is *rejected* when
   the registered interval does not cover. Assert rejections ≥ 90 of 100. The clause is measured by KA-726's
   runner entry (step 9), where the conformance harness restricts the class (DESIGN, as with the chart
   override of step 10). `ground.identify` has no option that selects a law class (VERBS-ground lists none;
   an earlier text called `--law-class coulomb-viscous`, which KNOWN_DRIFTS 8 forbids), so the verb-path leg
   of this population runs only once the registry row lists such an option (request to VERBS-ground and
   BD-391); until then it is logged `not run: ground.identify has no law-class option`, never as a pass.
   Control built into the gate's known answer: the same fit scored on its own calibration data passes,
   which shows why sealing matters; it is run only inside KA-726's runner entry, never through the verb,
   which cannot read calibration data as if it were sealed.
6. **Further known answers asserted once each.** Unidirectional data: `frob ground identify <unidirectional
   dataset> --stage actuators --params coulomb --key gi-u-<S> --json` is refused with `FROB-REF-879` (KA-724's
   control). Wrong reason (R-07): a candidate forced to a compensating pair (torque constant and friction) is
   labelled `wrong-reason` and cannot be admitted. Shared bias: doubling the trace does not push the
   bias-torque uncertainty below the planted floor. Stage order: `frob ground identify <dataset> --stage
   contact --key gi-o-<S> --json` on a cell with no actuator record is refused with `FROB-REF-879`, pointer
   `stage`. Card
   immutability: the prior card's content address is unchanged after `identify`, and each candidate is a new
   version with provenance `measured`, the dataset hash and the cell.
7. **Controls (`--controls`).** Builds on which the script must exit non-zero: an estimator with a diagonal
   noise model; a delay fitted by finite differences; a score path that reads calibration data in place of
   the sealed part (part (c) then accepts the omitted-law candidates); an `identify` that mutates the prior
   card in place.
8. **Determinism.** Ten rigs of population A twice with one seed: identical posteriors bit for bit on one
   machine; the airlock in process and through the worker gives equal digests; the ensemble part's machine
   fingerprint is logged and no cross-machine bit comparison is made.
9. **Folded from the superseded bead N30b-32.** The rig populations are those of the rehearsal catalogue
   (BD-388): fixture R-04 for population A (nominal rig: J_a = 6.0 × 10⁻³ kg·m², b = 0.050 N·m·s/rad,
   τ_c = 0.30 N·m, τ_s = 0.45 N·m, v_s = 0.10 rad/s, d = 2.625 ms on a 1/8 ms grid; 100 rigs with each
   parameter log-uniform in [0.7, 1.4] × nominal, delay on the grid in [1, 5] ms, prior cards off by factors
   in [0.5, 2.0]), R-06 for the omitted law and R-07 for the wrong reason. Where this bead describes a bundle
   or a population differently, the catalogue's registered values are the ones generated.
   A single nominal rig is taken through `excite`, `identify --stage actuators` (passes A, B, C) and the
   later stages before the battery, asserting: each of the five parameters within 10% and the delay minimizer
   within one grid step; misfit after below misfit before; the energy verdict `consistent`; prior card content
   addresses unchanged; candidate versions written; the training manifest's randomization digest unchanged.
   The battery is the gate's run in the conformance crate's known-answer tier, the one form that crate has
   (test targets, no binary; BD-025, KNOWN_DRIFTS 1; an earlier text called a `frob-conformance run` binary):
   `FROB_TEST_SEED=<S> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-GROUND-04`, which runs
   the runner entries of KA-724 (R-04, 100 rigs), KA-726 (R-06) and KA-727 (R-07) and the held-out clause,
   driving the `ground.identify` handler per rig exactly as an agent would call it, and writes the hash-named
   receipt that the gate record cites. Its three counts equal those of the verb path of steps 1 to 4 on the
   same seeds and fixture hashes on one machine. The verb path is run as daemon jobs: the script waits on
   each job handle and survives exit code 8; partway through population A it kills `frobd`, restarts it and
   re-issues every rig's calls with the same keys, and asserts that finished rigs return their stored
   receipts without a second calibration record (counted from the store's write log), that the interrupted
   job resumes from the committed frontier of the job protocol, and that the final counts equal an
   uninterrupted run's. Metrics: `e2e_metric rigs_within_10pct <k₁> rigs >= 95`;
   `e2e_metric heldout_error_ratio <ρ> 1 <= 0.70` (the sum over rigs of the held-out error with identified
   cards over the sum with prior cards: a ratio of sums, not a mean of ratios);
   `e2e_metric omitted_law_rejected <k₃> rigs >= 90`; each with `--gate GATE-GROUND-04`; then
   `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-04`, which declares the gate touched and records nothing. No sealed chunk is read by any `identify` call (asserted from the store's read
   log). Permuting the rig order leaves every rig's result and the three counts unchanged. The `smoke` battery
   runs the single rig and the controls only. A second run after a declared repair uses the same seeds and
   fixture hashes, and the log records both.
10. **Stage 3 inertia leg, per chart (carried here for N13-30, whose own script defers it to this one).** Not
   a clause of GATE-GROUND-04; it is the ordinary-run reading of pilots N13-29 and N10a-13. Fixture: the
   planted one-link set of N10a-13 (mass 1.2 kg, centre of mass (0.02, −0.01, 0.15) m, principal moments about
   the centre of mass (0.012, 0.011, 0.003) kg·m² under that bead's fixed rotation), driven by its known wrench
   schedule on the truth simulator for 10 s at 200 Hz, emitted as a rehearsal bundle of origin `synthetic`.
   Stage 3 runs with `--keep-prior actuators --keep-prior contact` (the bundle has no actuator or contact
   excitation; both keeps are recorded in the calibration record). With the feature `frontier-affine-inertia`
   built: `frob ground identify <dataset> --stage inertia --keep-prior actuators --keep-prior contact --key
   gi-in3-<S> --json` once through the verb, which uses the chart recorded for the pilots in
   `frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`, and once per chart through a harness of the conformance crate,
   `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example ground_inertia_chart -- fit --dataset <dataset> --chart
   affine-invariant|log-cholesky --seed <S> --json`, declared by this bead under BD-025's rule (a thin parser
   that calls N30a-25's inertia fit with N13-30's `InertiaStep` in the named chart; an earlier text called a
   `frob-conformance run` binary, which does not exist). DESIGN: the chart override exists only in that
   harness; the verb has no chart argument because the chart is a recorded pilot decision, not a user choice.
   Without the feature: one run, recorded as
   `euclidean-projected`. Per chart assert: the calibration record names the chart; the count of infeasible
   iterates (pseudo-inertia failing the positivity check before any projection) is 0 for `affine-invariant`
   and `log-cholesky` and is logged, without a threshold, for `euclidean-projected`; the posterior satisfies
   N30a-25's physical-consistency guard. Log per chart the recovery error: the affine-invariant distance to
   the planted pseudo-inertia (dimensionless), the mass error in kg, the centre-of-mass error in m and the
   largest principal-moment error in kg·m². The verb run's record is bit-identical to the harness run of
   the same chart and seed.
11. **Stages carried for the mechanism beads that name this script as theirs** (KNOWN_DRIFTS 11: one owning
   script, named in each of them; each stage is a `--stage <name>` of this script and logs its own lines):
   - `pass-a` (N30a-21): from the nominal rig's calibration record, pass A's four values with their standard
     deviations, the condition number, the kept fraction and the largest binned residual with its bin.
   - `pass-b` (N30a-22, N30a-08): window length and row count per experiment, iterations and evaluations
     against their caps, χ² before and after with degrees of freedom, the down-weighted rows; the bias-torque
     standard deviation against the planted floor as the trace length is doubled twice (`metric` lines n,
     reported σ, floor).
   - `pass-c` (N30a-23): the delay grid step, the profile table, d̂ and its 95% set; ensemble size, rounds and
     per-round mean and standard deviation of τ_s and v_s; the machine fingerprint.
   - `energy` (N30a-24): on R-07 and on the nominal rig, E_el, each model term, r_E, B_E and the label, and
     the refusal of the wrong-reason admission attempt.
   - `stages` (N30a-20, N30a-25): after the actuator stage, `frob ground identify <cart dataset> --stage
     contact`, `--stage inertia` and `--stage sensors` (keys `gi-s2-<S>`, `gi-s3-<S>`, `gi-s4-<S>`) on the
     cart rehearsal, each stage's parameters, errors against planted values, guard results and record
     identities, and each out-of-order call refused with 879, pointer `stage`.
   - `cards` (N30a-27): `frob actuator show <prior> --json` before and after, the field-by-field diff of the
     two versions, the prior and new `CardId`, the new `ModelId`, the identified fields with provenance and
     the largest off-diagonal correlation.
   - `widths` (N30a-28): the three randomization plan identifiers (before `identify`, after `identify`,
     after `score`) through N30a-28's `frob sim run … --task` form, expected equal, equal, different.
   - `class` (N30a-30): three rigs of population A declared three units of one part class with planted
     unit-to-unit spread; after their versions are admitted, `frob actuator show <class card> --json`: k, the
     spread, its degrees of freedom, the class mean with its standard deviation, and the predictive
     uncertainty against the planted spread.
   - `airlock` (N30a-05, N30a-06): the nominal rig once with `FROB_AIRLOCK=inproc` and once with `worker`,
     equal result digests, the mode, the sibling commit from the stack lock and each receipt's identity.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Part (a): at least 95 of 100 rigs recover all six parameters within tolerance (TARGET), measured value
      recorded.
- [ ] Part (b): pooled held-out trajectory error reduced by at least 30% against prior cards (TARGET).
- [ ] Part (c): the omitted-law candidate rejected on at least 90 of 100 rigs (TARGET); the
      calibration-data control passes, as it should.
- [ ] The five single assertions of step 6 hold.
- [ ] Every calibration record of population A names `stribeck_shape = "gaussian"`, records its experiments
      assimilated in ascending identifier order, and keeps the bias-torque standard deviation at or above
      the planted common-mode floor; the `identify` calls pass the excite report's `--params` list unchanged.
- [ ] Each control build makes the script exit non-zero.
- [ ] Each stage of step 11 runs and logs its lines; the class card of three units has `spread_dof = 2`, and
      the in-process and worker airlock runs give equal digests.
- [ ] The script writes no gate record (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9). This bead is the gate's one recorder: from the
      receipt of a passing gate run, a session that did not implement the measured beads enters
      GATE-GROUND-04 with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-GROUND-04
      --receipt <hash>`, holding the three measured values, the seed, the commit, the machine and the airlock
      mode; it is the record the KILL-GROUND decision reads. A failing value is recorded as such.
- [ ] The verb path's three counts equal the gate run's on the same seeds on one machine (population B's verb
      leg is `not run` until the registry lists a law-class option, and the omitted-law count is then the
      gate run's alone).
- [ ] The 100-rig battery resumes after a kill of the daemon without re-identifying finished rigs, and the
      final counts equal an uninterrupted run's.
- [ ] No sealed chunk is read by any `identify` call; two runs with the same seeds give identical counts on
      one machine.
- [ ] Stage 3 inertia leg (step 10): with the feature built, each of `affine-invariant` and `log-cholesky`
      names itself in the calibration record with 0 infeasible iterates and a logged recovery error; the
      verb's run equals the `ground_inertia_chart` harness run of the recorded chart bit for bit; without the
      feature the `euclidean-projected` run is recorded as such with its infeasible count.
- [ ] Rows KA-724, KA-726 and KA-727 of the Ground known-answer registry (N30-KA) are bound by this bead: it
      adds their runner entries `ka:KA-724@referee`, `ka:KA-726@referee` and `ka:KA-727@referee` (N38a-13;
      the R-04 battery with the unidirectional control, the R-06 omitted-law battery with the
      calibration-data control, the R-07 wrong-reason fit with the energy check disabled as its DESIGN
      control) and names, in each row's derivation front matter, the test that checks it (crate, path and
      name). The known-answer run reports the three rows `pass` with their controls caught; each is logged as
      an `assert` line with its KA number.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own. The population generator has `rig_population_reproducible_by_seed` and
`planted_ranges_cover_classes`.
Folded from N30b-32: `crates/frob-conformance/tests/ground_batteries_identify.rs`: `ratio_is_sum_over_sum`;
`rig_within_rule_needs_all_five_and_delay`; `battery_resumes_from_frontier` (rig keys derived from seed and
index: a battery interrupted after k rigs and re-run issues no new identification for the first k).
The `ground_inertia_chart` harness: `crates/frob-conformance/tests/ground_inertia_chart_args.rs`:
`chart_names_parse` (`affine-invariant`, `log-cholesky`; anything else exits 2 naming the accepted words);
`harness_equals_verb_for_recorded_chart` (same dataset and seed: identical calibration record bytes).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Log `target/e2e-logs/ground_identify/<utc>.ndjson` through the helper of `STD-E2E`,
with a transcript. Per rig one `metric` line: rig index, each parameter's planted value, posterior mean,
standard deviation and relative error; delay and its 95% set; iterations and evaluations used; e_prior and
e_cand; pass or fail. Per command template a `cmd` line with exit codes, wall time and the evidence block
(class `Estimated`, tier referee, determinism, dataset, cell and model identities, seed; `reality: synthetic`).
`metric` lines for the three gate statistics with thresholds 95 rigs, a ratio of 0.70 (a cut of at least
0.30) and 90 rigs. `refusal` lines for step 6. Per chart of step 10 one `metric` line: chart, feature built
or not, infeasible iterates (threshold 0 for the two cone charts, none for the projected one), the four
recovery errors with units, the calibration record's identity and its digest. On
failure: the first failing rig, its calibration record's identity and the paths of its dataset and record.
Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed, the gate touched and its three values.

Folded from N30b-32. Further control builds: the regularized fit that returns a value on an aliased member;

KA-727 (KA-725, delay against offset, is cleared by `ground_clock.sh`, N30a-14); counterexamples CX-328, CX-331
and CX-332. Step 10's control: a build whose cone chart steps as 𝖯 + V (N10a-13's planted arm-A defect) must
show infeasible iterates under `affine-invariant`, and the script must exit non-zero.

## Considerations

- Cost: 200 rigs, each a plan, three passes and two predictions: hours of CPU. The script fans rigs out as
  daemon jobs and is resumable (each rig's result is keyed by seed and index).
- "One repair cycle": if the first full run fails, one round of fixes is allowed and the second run decides
  the kill gate. Both runs are recorded.
- The 10% tolerance on v_s is the hardest row: the Stribeck speed is weakly determined when ramps are fast.
  The excitation plan is part of what is being tested, so the script uses the plan `ground.excite` returns,
  not a hand-made one.
- Everything is `synthetic`. Passing shows the loop is sound on the model class it was given and nothing
  about any real actuator.
