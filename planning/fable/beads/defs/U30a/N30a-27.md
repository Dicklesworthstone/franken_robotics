## Background

An actuator card is immutable, content-addressed data: torque constant, back-EMF constant, resistance, rotor
inertia, gear ratio, Coulomb, viscous and Stribeck friction, limits, thermal constants, latency and more.
Every field carries a provenance (`datasheet` with a citation, `measured`, `generic`, `assumed`) and an
uncertainty. Ground (crate `frob-ground`) is the only source of `measured` fields: step 5 of its procedure
writes the posterior of an identification as **a new card version**. Decision ADR-GROUND-06: a posterior is a
new card version with provenance `measured`; cards are never mutated; a new card version is a new `ModelId`,
and evidence attached to the old identity does not carry over (the design-data immutability invariant, and the
model-identity rule of the studio's decision cores).

What is written: every identified field gets provenance `measured`, the dataset hash, the cell and its marginal
uncertainty. The card also stores **the joint covariance of the identified fields**. Armature and friction
estimates are correlated, and randomization that samples the marginals independently is wider than the
evidence in some directions and narrower in others; correlated draws use the sibling routine
`fs-uq::product_copula` (FACT).

Rejected alternatives: editing the card in place (every earlier result would silently change meaning); storing
only marginal uncertainties (smaller files, wrong randomization); keeping the posterior outside the card in a
side table (a model built from the card would then not carry its own evidence).

## Scope

In: the mapping from the identified parameter vector to card fields; the version writer; the covariance block;
the checks a posterior must pass to be written; the correlated sampler; lineage and ledger event; and, in the
randomization compiler of BD-200 (which draws each entry independently and names "correlation inside one card"
as its point of revisit), the lint against independent sampling of a card with a covariance block (N15ax-03
assigns correlated sampling and the lint here). Out: when the new version's width may be used, and the trait
`CardWidthSource` through which the layer-4 compiler reaches this sampler without depending on `frob-ground`
(N30a-28, which depends on this bead); the discrepancy floor (N30a-29); class cards (N30a-30); the card schema
itself, the covariance block's format and `actuator.check` (BD-069, N15ax-03).

## Technical approach

1. **Field mapping** (actuator stage; field names are the card schema's, BD-069; parameter names are the
   stage-1 names that `ground.identify --params` accepts and the excite report prints, BD-391 step 3, which
   names this bead as the map's owner). The one table, `card::STAGE1_FIELD_MAP`:

   | `--params` name | Symbol | Card field | Rule |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `armature` | J_a | `j_rotor` | J_a/N², gear ratio N from the card; when only I + J_a was identified, the split uses the model's link inertia and the record says so |
   | `viscous` | b | `viscous` | as identified |
   | `coulomb` | τ_c | `coulomb` | as identified |
   | `static` | τ_s | `breakaway` | as identified |
   | `stribeck_speed` | v_s | `stribeck_velocity` | as identified, under the prior card's `stribeck_shape` |
   | `delay` | d | driver `latency` | **only** from a value of type `Delay` (never from "delay plus offset", N30a-13) |
   | `lag` | τ_lag | driver current-loop bandwidth | 1/(2·π·τ_lag) |
   | `kt_scale` | k_t scale | `kt`, and `ke` | both moved by the same factor, so that the two stay consistent in power-invariant coordinates |

   A parameter name outside the table is a programming error caught by the map's exhaustive test, not a
   runtime choice. `stribeck_shape` (`lorentzian` or `gaussian`) is not a parameter and is copied unchanged
   from the prior card: the shape is never fitted (N30a-20), the referee evaluated the fit with the prior
   card's shape, and v_s means a different speed under the other shape (DESIGN: writing a v_s fitted under
   one shape onto a card of the other would silently change the friction curve). Contact, inertia and sensor
   stages map to their cards' fields in the same way, each through its own table.
2. **Writer.** `card::write_version(prior, posterior, record, cell, dataset) -> Result<CardVersion, Refusal>`:
   1. copy the prior card;
   2. for each identified field: value = posterior mean; provenance =
      `measured { dataset: <hash>, cell: <CellId>, record: <calibration record id> }`; uncertainty = the
      posterior's marginal standard deviation;
   3. every other field keeps its value, provenance and uncertainty byte for byte;
   4. add the block `covariance`: `fields` (the identified fields' card paths, for example
      `friction.coulomb`, sorted ascending: the canonical order of the block's format, N15ax-03) and `lower`,
      the lower triangle of their covariance row by row in the products of the fields' card units, each
      diagonal entry equal to the square of that field's σ (its `unc` written as kind `normal`); add
      `delay_set` (the grid delays inside the 95% likelihood-ratio bound, the field's `unc` the interval from
      its smallest to its largest member) when a delay was identified;
   5. set `parent = <prior CardId>`; canonicalize; hash. The result has a new `CardId`.
3. **Checks before writing.** The covariance must be symmetric positive semi-definite (smallest eigenvalue
   ≥ −10⁻¹² of the largest). The new card must pass the card admission checks (torque and back-EMF constants
   agree in power-invariant coordinates within the stated uncertainty; every loss term nonnegative in all
   four quadrants; stated peak and continuous torques reachable inside the card's own limits). A failed
   check writes nothing, and the stage fails closed to the prior card. A posterior whose record carries
   `WrongReason` passes these checks like any other: it is written as a candidate so that it can be inspected,
   its label travels with it, and it can never be admitted.
4. **Identity consequences.** A model bound to the new version has a new `ModelId`. Nothing keyed by the old
   `ModelId` is inherited: verdicts, fidelity-ledger entries, bands, frozen predictions and monitors stay with
   the old identity, and the revalidation engine lists them as stale for the new one.
5. **Ledger.** Event `ground.candidate { prior, version, record, cell, dataset }`. The status of the new
   version is `candidate` (N30a-28).
6. **Correlated draws.** `card::sampler(version) -> CardSampler` exposes the identified block as a Gaussian
   copula: marginals from each field's mean and standard deviation (truncated where a field has a physical
   bound, for example friction ≥ 0), correlation matrix from the covariance block. Randomization calls it
   with a world address; it delegates to `CopulaJob` (N30a-06), so draws are by address and identical in any
   batch. Fields outside the block are drawn independently as before. Layering (DESIGN): this bead adds to
   `frob-actuate` (layer 2, the card crate) the small trait `CorrelatedDraw { fn fields(&self) ->
   &[FieldPath]; fn draw(&self, address: WorldAddress) -> Vec<Quantity> }`, and `CardSampler` implements it;
   the randomization compiler (`frob-learn`, layer 4) receives a `Box<dyn CorrelatedDraw>` through N30a-28's
   `CardWidthSource` and never names `frob-ground` (layer 5), which `xtask check-layers` would refuse.
7. **Listing.** `frob actuator show <version>` prints the identified fields with provenance, the correlation
   matrix, the parent, the record and the status.
8. **Folded from the superseded bead N30b-04.** The writer is also reachable as
   `card_update::write_candidate(record)`, which calls `write_version` once per card touched by a calibration
   record. The delay field is excluded from the `[covariance]` block: it carries `delay_set`. A field
   identified from a dataset of origin `synthetic` carries `origin = "synthetic"` beside the dataset hash and
   counts as guessed in the share of guessed fields printed on reports, so the rehearsal can never lower that
   share. Independent marginal sampling of a card that has a covariance block is a lint error in the
   randomization compiler. The store refuses a write whose content address exists with other bytes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] INV-GROUND-02: the prior card's content address is the same before and after `ground.identify`; the
      posterior is a new version whose identified fields carry provenance `measured`, the dataset hash and
      the cell.
- [ ] Only identified fields differ between prior and new version (a field-by-field diff), plus the covariance
      block, `delay_set` and `parent`.
- [ ] A model bound to the new version has a different `ModelId`, and a verdict attached to the old model is
      reported stale for the new one.
- [ ] A posterior with a negative Coulomb friction mean, or one that breaks the card's power-invariance
      check, is not written.
- [ ] Draws from the sampler reproduce the stored correlation (target −0.9: sample correlation within 0.01 at
      10⁵ draws), and for two fields with equal standard deviation σ and correlation −0.9 the variance of
      their sum is 0.2·σ² (independent sampling would give 2·σ²) and of their difference 3.8·σ².
- [ ] A "delay plus offset" value cannot reach the `latency` field (compile-time, by type).
- [ ] Each of the eight stage-1 parameter names writes exactly the card field of the table, and
      `stribeck_shape` of the new version equals the prior's on both a `gaussian` and a `lorentzian` prior.
- [ ] A card version identified on the rehearsal counts as guessed in the guess share.
- [ ] Independent sampling of a card with a covariance block is rejected by the randomization compiler's lint.
- [ ] A write whose content address already exists with different bytes is refused by the store.
- [ ] CX-G-09's plant `N30a-27:cx-<nnn>` (feature `cx-<nnn>`: draws from independent marginals) builds under
      `gauntlet-mutations`, and `sum_and_difference_variances` fails on that build (2·σ² in place of 0.2·σ²);
      the entry carries `case_supplied_by` naming this bead's fixture and `numeric = true`.

### Known answers and negative controls

The variance identities above (exact for a bivariate normal). Negative controls: a writer that edits the
prior's file in place (the content-address test fails); a sampler that ignores the covariance block (the
sum-variance test reads 2·σ²); a writer that drops the cell from the provenance. Invariant INV-GROUND-02
("a card is never mutated; a posterior is a new version with provenance and dataset hash"; tested by content
addresses before and after `ground.identify`). Determinism: canonical bytes and hash are class D0; draws by
address are identical twice and across batch compositions.
Folded from N30b-04: case CX-G-09 of the chapter's counterexample registry (independent marginals), whose
plant `N30a-27:cx-<nnn>` (`<nnn>` the number issued to the tag CX-G-09, which cites it until then) this bead
builds: the Cargo feature `cx-<nnn>` of `frob-ground` (compiled only under `gauntlet-mutations`, never in a
release profile) makes `card::sampler` ignore the stored covariance block and draw each identified field from
its marginal alone. On the case of two fields with equal σ and correlation −0.9 the draws' correlation reads
0 and the variance of the sum 2·σ² in place of 0.2·σ² (the difference 2·σ² in place of 3.8·σ²);
`sampler_reproduces_correlation` and `sum_and_difference_variances` fail on that build, and GATE-GROUND-04's
gate run (N30a-26) runs it as a control. CX-G-09 is stated in words: the bead writes the case as the
synthetic fixture `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/ground/cx-<nnn>.toml` (the two fields, σ, the
correlation and the four variances), records it in the entry of
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/counterexamples.toml` as `case_supplied_by = "N30a-27:
fixtures/ground/cx-<nnn>.toml"` with `numeric = true` (`plan_case` left `"words"`), and re-runs N38b-05's
seeding tool, so that the control turns from `obligation` to `runnable` under its NC number.
Metamorphic relation: permuting the order in which the writer receives the identified fields yields identical
card bytes (the block is sorted) and so no change in any draw. Across platforms the written cards have
identical bytes. Negative control for the shape rule: a writer that sets `stribeck_shape` to `gaussian` on a
`lorentzian` prior is caught by `only_identified_fields_change`.

## Considerations

- Cross-block covariance between the smooth parameters and the ensemble-estimated pair (τ_s, v_s) is not
  estimated and is stored as zero with a flag; randomization then treats the two blocks as independent, which
  is wider than the truth in some directions. The flag is printed by `actuator show`.
- A Gaussian copula with truncated marginals is an approximation near a bound; for a friction mean within two
  standard deviations of zero the sampler's realized correlation is checked and reported.
- The share of guessed card fields printed on reports (N30a-03) counts fields of a candidate version in their
  own column, not as measured.
- Licence and privacy: a card version inherits the private mark of the dataset it was fitted from, and such a
  version cannot enter a replication kit.
