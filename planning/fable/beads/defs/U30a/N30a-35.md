## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) compares simulation with real trials under a protocol: a prediction record is
frozen in the ledger before the sealed trial data can be read; `ground.score` opens the sealed part once,
scores with a proper rule, retires the holdout and admits or rejects the candidate card version; a refit after
seeing validation data needs a new prediction version and fresh sealed data. The verbs are `ground.predict`
(kind mutate) and `ground.score` (kind run).

**GATE-GROUND-05** (claim kind: known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): the freeze-before-read order holds for
every trial, and the three protocol mutations are refused: a read before freeze, a refit without a new
version, and reuse of a holdout. Together with the clock gate it feeds the kill gate **KILL-CLOCK**: if either
fails, real logs are scored on time-free metrics only, and no crossing time, latency or delay is reported.

## Scope

In: the script, its rehearsal data, the order check over every trial, the three mutations, the associated
scoring and admission assertions, controls, log and gate record. Out: the mechanisms (N30a-31 to N30a-34;
N30a-28, N30a-29; the admission script N25a-29 covers sealed-read routes at ingest).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/ground_protocol.sh [--seed S] [--controls]`, plain shell over `frob` and `frobd`, under
`target/e2e-work/ground_protocol/`; default seed 5. Data: the rehearsal's instrumented-cart data (a cart
crossing a sill; origin `synthetic`; 20 trials per day) as two bundles of one cell: bundle 1, split by day
into two calibration days, one validation day and one holdout day (validation and holdout sealed); bundle 2,
admitted after step 1, one sealed holdout day, the "fresh sealed data" for a second attempt. (A prediction
names its dataset and part, `--part validation|holdout`, and a score spends the whole part, so the two
holdout days are two datasets; an earlier text scored `--part holdout-1` and `holdout-2`, options
`ground.score` does not have.) Every call is in the registry's form; each `run` and `mutate` call carries
its own key (KNOWN_DRIFTS 5), and a call repeated on purpose takes a fresh key so that it is a new attempt,
not the stored receipt of the first.

1. **Honest pass.** `frob ground ingest <bundle 1> --design <cart design> --task-family <sill task family>
   --key gpr-in1-<S> --json`; `frob ground identify <ds-1> --stage actuators --key gpr-id-<S> --json`;
   `frob ground predict --dataset <ds-1> --part validation --cell <cell> --model <model with the candidate
   card> --scene <sill scene> --task <sill crossing> --metric crossing_time_s:0.90 --metric
   peak_current_A:0.90 --decision "<decision>" --study fixed-n:20 --key gpr-pv-<S> --json` (the record's
   tolerances are derived with units, N30a-31); `frob ground score <validation prediction> --key gpr-sv-<S>
   --json`; then a second frozen prediction, the same form with `--part holdout --key gpr-ph-<S>`, scored
   with `frob ground score <holdout prediction> --key gpr-sh-<S> --json` (the spent holdout goes to the
   calibration pool, N30a-34's default).
2. **Order over every trial.** From `frob evidence trace <score claim> --json` collect, for each of the 60
   sealed trials scored, the sequence of its prediction's `ground.frozen` event, of the sealed commitment at
   admission and of the read. Assert commitment < frozen < read for all; the gate statistic is the count of
   trials satisfying it over the count scored (TARGET: all).
3. **Mutation 1: read before freeze.** On a fresh store: `score` with no prediction; a read of a sealed chunk
   by every other route (artifact read, run export, telemetry query, viewer endpoint, direct store call); and
   a prediction frozen after a refused attempt, which must not license that attempt retroactively. Assert
   `FROB-REF-883`, exit 3, repair "register a new version", no chunk bytes in any response.
4. **Mutation 2: refit without a new version.** After step 1's validation score, run `frob ground identify
   <ds-1> --stage actuators --windows <changed definition> --key gpr-rf-<S> --json` (a refit); `predict`
   for the refit without `--supersedes` (a version 1) is refused; `score` of a refit's version-1 record
   on any part is refused with 883; `score` of a correct version-2 record (`--supersedes <step 1's record>`)
   on ds-1's spent validation part is refused with 886; after `frob ground ingest <bundle 2> … --key
   gpr-in2-<S> --json`, version 2 for `--dataset <ds-2> --part holdout` succeeds and runs at the level the
   attempt counter assigns from
   the series α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)]: the claim's scored attempts so far are step 1's validation (attempt 1,
   α/2 = 0.05 for α = 0.10) and holdout-1 (attempt 2, α/6 = 0.016667), so version 2 is attempt 3 at
   α/12 = 0.008333. The log shows each attempt's index and level, and the script asserts the three values.
5. **Mutation 3: holdout reuse.** A score of ds-1's holdout a second time for the same cell and lineage
   (`frob ground score <holdout prediction> --key gpr-sh2-<S> --json`, a fresh key): `FROB-REF-886`, repair
   "acquire fresh sealed data". The same chunks re-admitted under a new dataset name and declared `holdout`:
   886 at admission (N30a-34's admission guard), nothing sealed; the same chunks declared `calibration`:
   admitted.
6. **Tampering.** One byte of a frozen record's body changed in the store: `score` refuses with 883.
7. **Carried assertions.** Interval-score arithmetic on three planted outcomes ([4.05, 4.42] at 0.90: 0.37,
   1.97, 1.37). A claim of "under 5%" at n = 20, declared at nominal α = 0.10 so that its first attempt runs
   at α/2 = 0.05 (N30a-34), refused at registration with "n ≥ 59, or claim under 13.9%".
   A unitless tolerance refused. The training randomization plan identifier (N30a-28's `frob sim run …
   --task` form) equal before and after `identify`, and changed after the admitting `score`. The
   discrepancy-floor event (N30a-29) present after `score`: per
   channel σ_mf with its row count and standard error, per identified field posterior σ, floor σ and reported
   σ, all logged with units; assert reported σ ≥ posterior σ and reported σ ≥ floor σ for every field, and
   σ_mf > 0 on at least one channel (the cart fixture carries the rehearsal's planted model-form difference, a
   contact-dissipation variant the fit class lacks, so a floor of zero everywhere is a finding that fails the
   step). A planted reversed ranking returns `ranking-falsified`, a narrowed cell with no band, and
   one regression case. An omitted-law candidate is rejected at validation.
8. **Controls (`--controls`).** Builds on which the script must exit non-zero: a store with the sealed-area
   guard disabled; a `score` that compares wall-clock timestamps instead of ledger sequences; a spent set
   keyed by dataset name; an admission that accepts a prediction frozen after the read.
9. **Determinism.** Step 1 twice: identical record hashes, score reports and status events (modulo timestamps).
10. **Folded from the superseded bead N30b-33.** Fixtures of the rehearsal catalogue (BD-388) beside the
    instrumented cart: R-04 (nominal rig), R-06 (omitted law), R-12 (reachability) and R-14 (protocol). The
    list of read paths attacked in mutation 1 is generated from the verb registry, so that every verb whose
    output could include chunk bytes is attacked without anyone remembering to add it: `frob artifact read
    <chunk-digest>`, `frob run export <run>`, `frob telemetry query …` over the sealed trials, the HTML replay
    export, and `frob ground score` with a prediction identifier that does not exist. After a prediction is
    frozen, the read event's ledger sequence is larger than that of every refused attempt (the attempts were
    logged, not served). Mutation 2 also covers a "version 1" registered with changed intervals under the old
    identifier; after the accepted version 2 the old version is still listed as scored and the new one has
    `supersedes` set. Mutation 3 also covers a new dataset that declares the spent chunk as its holdout (886
    at admission). A study declaring both types is refused at registration.
    *Crash lane.* With the fault-injection build the process is killed at each await point between the spend
    event and the score record; after restart, `frob job find` by idempotency key and a re-issued call show
    the holdout spent exactly once, a score equal to the uninterrupted one, and no sealed byte returned by any
    call that has no spend event.
    *Plant switches.* With `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE=sealed-guard-off` the read-before-freeze step fails and names
    the path; with `spent-table-by-version` the reuse-after-refit attack succeeds and the script fails; with
    `record-rewrite-allowed` the refit step fails.
    The script records `e2e_metric order_violations <count> trials == 0` and
    `e2e_metric mutations_refused <count> mutations == 3`, both with `--gate GATE-GROUND-05`, then
    `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-05`. A lint over the log checks that only identifiers, counts and digests are
    present: no sealed sample value appears anywhere in it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The order commitment < frozen < read holds for every scored trial (60 of 60), measured and recorded.
- [ ] Each of the three mutations is refused with its code (883, 883 or 886 by path, 886) and leaves no score
      report, no status change and no spent-set entry.
- [ ] The tamper case is refused; the carried assertions of step 7 hold.
- [ ] Each control build makes the script exit non-zero.
- [ ] The script writes no gate record (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9). This bead is the gate's one recorder: from the
      receipt of the gate run, passing or not (`FROB_TEST_SEED=<S> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
      g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-GROUND-05`, which runs every control registered
      for the gate in the same run: the controls of KA-732 and KA-734, CX-331's plant `N30b-06:cx-331`,
      CX-333's `N30a-33:cx-333` and CX-G-06's `BD-387:cx-<nnn>`, each of which the receipt must show
      `caught`), a session that did not implement the measured beads enters GATE-GROUND-05 with
      `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-GROUND-05 --receipt <hash>`, holding
      the order count, the three mutation outcomes, the read paths attacked (count), the crash points
      exercised (count), the seed, the commit and the machine; it is the record the KILL-CLOCK decision reads.
      A failing value is recorded `fail` (with every control `caught`), and this bead then closes `recorded`
      on that record (N41-17 rule 2; KILL-CLOCK's trigger names no repair cycle, so the one measurement
      decides), which is what makes the KILL-CLOCK decision bead N30b-19 ready.
- [ ] Each plant switch makes the script exit non-zero and name the step.
- [ ] No sealed sample value appears anywhere in the log.
- [ ] Rows KA-732 and KA-734 of the Ground known-answer registry (N30-KA) are bound by this bead: it adds
      their runner entries `ka:KA-732@host` (the reachability bound 1 − 0.05^(1/20) = 13.9% and the refused
      "under 5%" claim at n = 20) and `ka:KA-734@daemon` (the three protocol mutations through the daemon,
      with its DESIGN control, a store build with the sealed-area guard disabled), N38a-13, and names, in
      each row's derivation front matter, the test that checks it (crate, path and name). The known-answer

hash prefixes.

## Considerations

- The gate's first half is a universal statement ("every trial"); the script proves it for the trials it
  scores, and the unit-level guard (a read path that cannot be taken without a frozen record) is what makes it
  hold elsewhere. Both are cited in the gate record.
- A read route added to the system later must be added to mutation 1; a route missing from the list is how a
  leak would go unseen.
- If KILL-CLOCK fires, this protocol remains in force for time-free metrics.
- A failure of this gate fires KILL-CLOCK as registered, and the kill decision adds a fail-closed suspension
  of admission until the invariant's own test passes again (N30b-19).
