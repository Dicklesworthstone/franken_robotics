## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) compares simulation with real trials under a protocol: a prediction record is
frozen in the ledger before the sealed trial data can be read; `ground.score` opens the sealed part once,
scores with a proper rule, retires the holdout and admits or rejects the candidate card version; a refit after
seeing validation data needs a new prediction version and fresh sealed data. The verbs are `ground.predict`
(kind mutate) and `ground.score` (kind run).

**GATE-GROUND-05** (claim kind: known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): the freeze-before-read order holds for
every trial, and the three protocol mutations are refused: a read before freeze, a refit without a new
version, and reuse of a holdout. Together with the clock gate it feeds the kill gate **KILL-CLOCK**: if either
fails, real logs are scored on time-free metrics only, and no crossing time, latency or delay is reported.

## Scope

In: the script, its rehearsal data, the order check over every trial, the three mutations, the associated
scoring and admission assertions, controls, log and gate record. Out: the mechanisms (N30a-31 to N30a-34;
N30a-28, N30a-29; the admission script N25a-29 covers sealed-read routes at ingest).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/ground_protocol.sh [--seed S] [--controls]`, plain shell over `frob` and `frobd`, under
`target/e2e-work/ground_protocol/`; default seed 5. Data: the rehearsal's instrumented-cart dataset (a cart
crossing a sill; origin `synthetic`), split by day into two calibration days, one validation day and two
holdout days (the second is the "fresh sealed data" for a second attempt), validation and holdout sealed, 20
trials per day.

1. **Honest pass.** `ingest`; `identify --stage actuators`; `predict` (metrics `crossing_time_s` and
   `peak_current_A` at level 0.90, tolerances with units, study fixed-n with n = 20); `score --part
   validation`; `score --part holdout-1 --then calibration` with a second frozen prediction.
2. **Order over every trial.** From `frob evidence trace` (or the ledger query) collect, for each of the 60
   sealed trials scored, the sequence of its prediction's `ground.frozen` event, of the sealed commitment at
   admission and of the read. Assert commitment < frozen < read for all; the gate statistic is the count of
   trials satisfying it over the count scored (TARGET: all).
3. **Mutation 1: read before freeze.** On a fresh store: `score` with no prediction; a read of a sealed chunk
   by every other route (artifact read, run export, telemetry query, viewer endpoint, direct store call); and
   a prediction frozen after a refused attempt, which must not license that attempt retroactively. Assert
   `FROB-REF-883`, exit 3, repair "register a new version", no chunk bytes in any response.
4. **Mutation 2: refit without a new version.** After step 1's validation score, run `identify` again with a
   changed window (a refit); `predict` with `version = 1` is refused; `score` of the refit's version-1 record
   on any part is refused with 883; `score` of a correct version-2 record on the spent validation part is
   refused with 886; version 2 on holdout-2 succeeds and runs at the second level of the series
   α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)] (0.016667 for α = 0.10), which the log shows.
5. **Mutation 3: holdout reuse.** `score --part holdout-1` a second time for the same cell and lineage:
   `FROB-REF-886`, repair "acquire fresh sealed data". The same chunks re-admitted under a new dataset name:
   still 886.
6. **Tampering.** One byte of a frozen record's body changed in the store: `score` refuses with 883.
7. **Carried assertions.** Interval-score arithmetic on three planted outcomes ([4.05, 4.42] at 0.90: 0.37,
   1.97, 1.37). A claim of "under 5%" at n = 20 refused at registration with "n ≥ 59, or claim under 13.9%".
   A unitless tolerance refused. The resolved training manifest's hash equal before and after `identify`, and
   changed after the admitting `score`. The discrepancy-floor event present after `score`, with reported
   σ ≥ posterior σ. A planted reversed ranking returns `ranking-falsified`, a narrowed cell with no band, and
   one regression case. An omitted-law candidate is rejected at validation.
8. **Controls (`--controls`).** Builds on which the script must exit non-zero: a store with the sealed-area
   guard disabled; a `score` that compares wall-clock timestamps instead of ledger sequences; a spent set
   keyed by dataset name; an admission that accepts a prediction frozen after the read.
9. **Determinism.** Step 1 twice: identical record hashes, score reports and status events (modulo timestamps).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The order commitment < frozen < read holds for every scored trial (60 of 60), measured and recorded.
- [ ] Each of the three mutations is refused with its code (883, 883 or 886 by path, 886) and leaves no score
      report, no status change and no spent-set entry.
- [ ] The tamper case is refused; the carried assertions of step 7 hold.
- [ ] Each control build makes the script exit non-zero.
- [ ] The gate record `GATE-GROUND-05` is written with the order count, the three mutation outcomes, the
      seed, the commit and the machine, and is the record the KILL-CLOCK decision reads.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own; see N30a-31 to N30a-34.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Log `target/e2e-logs/ground_protocol/<utc>.ndjson` through the helper of `STD-E2E`,
with a transcript. Per command a `cmd` line (exact command, exit code, wall time, evidence block: claim,
class `Estimated`, tier, determinism, dataset, cell, model and prediction identities, seed;
`reality: synthetic`). Per scored trial a `metric` line with the three ledger sequences. Per mutation a
`refusal` line with code, pointer, first repair and a digest proving that no artifact changed. `metric` lines
for the level of each attempt, the three interval scores, the manifest hashes, the floor numbers. On failure:
the first trial whose order fails, with its event identities. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed,
gate touched (GATE-GROUND-05) with the count. No sample of sealed data enters the log; chunk references are
hash prefixes.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-734 (the three mutations refused), KA-732 (reachability), KA-726 (omitted law rejected at validation; the
same fit on its own calibration data passes, shown through the conformance binary). Counterexamples CX-331 and
CX-333. Negative controls: the four builds of step 8. Chaos lane (G4): the daemon killed between `scored` and
`spent`; after recovery either both events exist or neither. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug
and release.

## Considerations

- The gate's first half is a universal statement ("every trial"); the script proves it for the trials it
  scores, and the unit-level guard (a read path that cannot be taken without a frozen record) is what makes it
  hold elsewhere. Both are cited in the gate record.
- A read route added to the system later must be added to mutation 1; a route missing from the list is how a
  leak would go unseen.
- If KILL-CLOCK fires, this protocol remains in force for time-free metrics.
