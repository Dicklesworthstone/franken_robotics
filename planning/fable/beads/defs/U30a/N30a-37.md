## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) issues a conformal reality band for a simulated metric inside a calibrated cell.
The guarantee behind the band (coverage at least 1 − α) holds for one kind of new observation only: a draw
exchangeable with the calibration draws. Everything in this bead follows from taking that condition
literally.

- **The exchangeable unit is the robot, not the episode.** Twenty episodes on one machine are one cluster. For
  the same unit in the same cell, episodes are the draws. For a new unit of the same design, units are the
  draws, so a band across units needs at least 9 calibrated units at 90% and is refused below that.
- **A design that was never built has no reality band.** An unbuilt design is not a draw exchangeable with any
  calibration draw, and the distance between it and the calibrated ones cannot be measured. (The known bound
  on the coverage gap under shift needs that distance, so nothing is issued.)
- **Rule 1 of the plan:** a band is issued per cell, and refused for an uncalibrated cell, for n below the
  minimum, for a new unit when too few units are calibrated, for any unbuilt design, for data pooled across
  cells or `ModelId`s, and for synthetic data mixed with real.
- **Counterexample: pooling hides a failing regime.** Pooled calibration at a 90% target covers 20 of 20
  episodes in one regime and 16 of 20 in a shifted one. The total is 36 of 40, nominal overall and 80% where it
  matters (FACT: the sibling's test `fs-eproc/tests/conformal_shift_receipt.rs`). This is why the cell is the
  bucket.

Rejected alternatives: counting episodes as independent whatever robot produced them (the commonest way to
turn 1 unit into "n = 200"); a band for a "similar" design with an inflation factor (the factor would be a
guess presented as a guarantee).

## Scope

In: band scopes (within units, across units); the authorization decision in a fixed order with its refusals;
the handling of origin; the pooling fixture. Out: the quantile (N30a-36); printing and budgets (N30a-38);
retirement (N30a-43, refusal 897 is raised there and checked here).

## Technical approach

1. **Band scopes** (DESIGN names). `within-units`: the target is a future episode of a unit that is in the
   cell and was calibrated; the draws are that unit's episodes; the pool is per unit, and the minimum count
   (19) applies per unit. `across-units`: the target is a unit of the same design that was not calibrated,
   under the same controller, firmware, surface, task family and envelope; the draws are units; the pool holds
   **one residual per calibrated unit**, the statistic registered in the prediction (`keyed-pick`, the
   default: one pair per unit chosen by a keyed draw, step 7; `first-trial`: the unit's first registered
   trial; or `unit-mean`: the unit's mean outcome against the predicted mean), and the band's statement is
   about that same statistic for the new unit.
2. **Minimum for `across-units`.** k calibrated units, each admitted in the cell; a finite band needs
   k ≥ ⌈(1 − α)/α⌉: 9 at 90%, 19 at 95%, 99 at 99%. Below that the request is refused (889) with units
   present and units needed. The quantile of N30a-36 is called with this value as its `min_count` (and with
   19 for `within-units`).
3. **Authorization** (`band::authorize(request) -> Result<Authorized, Refusal>`), checks in this order, first
   failure wins:
   1. The design has no unit in any admitted dataset of the request's origin → `FROB-REF-895` (a band for a
      design that was never built; repair offered: `reality: no-claim`, with the simulation's own interval).
      For a request on real data this means no admitted dataset of origin `real` names a physical unit of the
      design (the same test as the reality guard of BD-139: an unbuilt design, an edited child of a built one, a
      generated family, a design with synthetic data only, a design known only from a published figure); for a
      request on a synthetic pool (the rehearsal) it means no synthetic dataset names a unit of the design, so
      the rehearsal's "unbuilt design" (no dataset at all) is refused while its synthetic cells can be given a
      `synthetic` band (step 4). The refusal payload carries the simulation's own predictive interval,
      labelled as simulation-only.
   2. The request's conditions fall outside every calibrated cell's envelope, or name a controller, firmware,
      surface or task family with no calibrated cell → `FROB-REF-889` (a band for an uncalibrated cell, or
      with too few calibration points; repair: samples present and samples needed).
   3. The cell's calibration is retired → `FROB-REF-897` (use of a retired calibration; repair: the retirement
      reason; recalibrate).
   4. The request's `ModelId` is not the one the pool was scored with (pools are keyed by cell, `ModelId`
      and origin, N30a-07) → 889 for that model identity, with samples present 0 (a new card version is a
      new model identity, and evidence does not carry over, even inside one lineage).
   5. Scope: target unit in the cell → `within-units`, needs n_unit ≥ 19, else 889; target unit not in the
      cell → `across-units`, needs k as in step 2, else 889.
   6. The request asks for residuals from more than one cell, more than one `ModelId`, or of both origins to be
      combined → `FROB-REF-814` (pooling across cells, across `ModelId`s, or of synthetic with real data;
      repair: separate the pools). The pool type already makes this impossible to do by accident; this branch
      answers an explicit request.
   All refusals exit with code 3 and leave `reality: no-claim`.
4. **Origin.** A pool is of one origin. A pool of `synthetic` residuals may be given a band for testing; its
   status is `synthetic`, its line reads `reality: synthetic`, and no path promotes it (invariant
   INV-GROUND-06: data of origin `synthetic` never yields a status other than `synthetic`).
5. **The pooling fixture.** `tests/fixtures/pooled_regimes.toml`: two cells that differ in one envelope
   dimension, with constructed calibration and test residual lists such that a single pooled quantile at
   α = 0.10 covers 20 of 20 test episodes of the first and 16 of 20 of the second (36 of 40), while per-cell
   quantiles cover at least 18 of 20 in each. It is used three ways: as the demonstration; as the test that
   pooling is refused through every public path; as a registered negative control.
6. **The shift bound, stated and not used.** For weighted conformal prediction the coverage gap under
   distribution shift is bounded by Σ w_i·d_TV(Z, Z_i)/(1 + Σ w_i), where d_TV is the total-variation distance
   between the test point's distribution and calibration point i's and w_i are the weights
   (https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.13415). For an unbuilt design the distance cannot be measured, so nothing is
   issued; the bound is recorded in the derivation file so that nobody proposes a "nearby design" band
   without saying what distance they assume.
7. **The per-unit statistic, settled (folded from the superseded bead N30b-07).** Three values may be
   registered in the prediction: `keyed-pick` (the default when none is registered: one pair per unit selected
   by a counter-based draw keyed by (cell, metric, unit), so that the pick is a function of identities, is the
   same under any ingestion order or batch composition, and cannot be steered; no parameter selects the
   episode), `first-trial` and `unit-mean`. Reusing all episodes of all units would treat dependent residuals
   as independent draws; one residual per unit keeps the coverage argument intact. Every band request and
   record carries its scope; in records and in the guard table of the decision core the two scopes are also
   written `draw = "episode"` (`within-units`) and `draw = "unit"` (`across-units`). A request for a unit that
   is in no calibrated cell is an `across-units` request whatever the caller wrote. With nine units at level
   0.90 the index is ⌈10 × 0.9⌉ = 9.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Band refusal known answers: an unbuilt design (895), a cell with 8 points (889: 8 present, 19 needed) and
      a new unit with 3 units calibrated (889: 3 present, 9 needed at 90%) are each refused.
- [ ] Twenty episodes on one unit authorize `within-units` for that unit and do not authorize any band for a
      second unit; the refusal says the units are the draws.
- [ ] Nine admitted units at 90% authorize `across-units` with one residual per unit; eight do not.
- [ ] The pooled fixture cannot be evaluated as one pool through any public function or verb (814), and its
      counts are 20 of 20, 16 of 20 and 36 of 40 when forced through KA-736's runner entry
      (`FROB_TEST_SEED=<S> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0_laws -- KA-736`, bound by N30a-39; the
      conformance crate has test targets and no binary).
- [ ] A band request for a model identity one card version later than the scored one is refused.
- [ ] A synthetic pool yields a band whose status is `synthetic`; mixing one real residual into it is refused.
- [ ] The per-unit pick is identical under any ordering of ingestion and any batch composition, and no
      parameter can override it.
- [ ] The clause "zero bands for the short cell" of the band-coverage gate includes the three-unit case.

## Considerations

- The plan's rule 1 cites refusal numbers (846, 850) that the refusal registry assigns to another subsystem;
  the chapter's own refusal table and the registry agree on 889, 895 and 814, which are used here.
- `across-units` assumes the calibrated units are exchangeable with the new one (same design revision, same
  build process). A change of supplier or build lot is a scope question for the operator; the cell key has no
  field for it yet, and the default is to treat a declared lot change as a new design revision.
- No public paired simulation-and-reality dataset with enough robots per class to test exchangeability across
  units was identified (open question OPEN-GROUND-1; default: a second-simulator stand-in, labelled
  `synthetic`). The across-units branch is therefore exercised on synthetic units only until data exists.
- With per-unit pools, a cell of several units needs 19 episodes on each unit for within-units bands; that is
  the cost of not pretending a second robot is the first.
- A rehearsal with nine or more synthetic units exercises the across-units path; no real program in sight has
  nine instrumented units, so on real data this path will mostly refuse. That is the intended reading of the
  evidence, and open question OPEN-GROUND-1 records that no public paired dataset with enough robots per class
  was identified.
