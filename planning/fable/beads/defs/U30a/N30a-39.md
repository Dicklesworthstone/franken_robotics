## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) issues a conformal reality band for a simulated metric inside a calibrated cell:
the half-width q̂ is the ⌈(n+1)·(1−α)⌉-th smallest of n calibration residuals between simulated predictions
and real outcomes, with a minimum of 19 calibration points, and it is refused everywhere else. Conditional on
a calibration set of size n, realized coverage follows Beta(n + 1 − l, l) with l = ⌊(n+1)·α⌋; at n = 20 and a
0.90 target that is Beta(19, 2): mean 19/21 = 0.905, and a probability of 0.069 that coverage is below 0.80.

**GATE-GROUND-06** (claim kind: known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): mean in-cell coverage within 0.01 of
19/21; the fraction of resamples below 0.80 within 0.03 of 0.069; zero bands for the shifted cell, the short
cell or the unbuilt design. It supports bet BET-28 (calibration bands reach their stated coverage within one
calibrated robot and task family) and feeds the kill gate **KILL-BAND**: if this gate or the drift gate fails,
bands are withdrawn and reports print `reality: no-claim` with the raw residual table.

No hardware is involved: a second simulator with a perturbed contact model stands in for reality, and every
artifact is labelled `synthetic`.

## Scope

In: the script, its stand-in for reality, the resampling protocol, the refusal cases, controls, log and gate
record. Out: the mechanisms (N30a-36 to N30a-38); the drift half of the pilot (N30a-44); the kill decision.

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/ground_band.sh [--seed S] [--resamples 1000] [--controls] [--quick]`, plain shell over
`frob`, `frobd` and the rehearsal generator, under `target/e2e-work/ground_band/`; default seed 6. `--quick`
uses 50 resamples and never records the gate.

1. **Stand-in for reality.** The truth simulator is the referee with the rehearsal's perturbed contact model
   (friction and dissipation changed, and one difference of model form). Task family A: the instrumented cart
   crossing a sill; metric: crossing time. Family B: the same cart on a surface of a different class (a
   shifted regime). The simulated side uses the unperturbed model with admitted cards.
2. **Per resample ρ = 1..R.** A cell of one unit with 20 calibration trials and conditions drawn by address
   from (seed, ρ): `frob ground ingest` (sealed), `frob ground predict` for the 20 trials,
   `frob ground score`, `frob ground band --metric crossing_time_s --level 0.90 --json` → q̂_ρ. Realized
   coverage c_ρ is computed by the script on 2,000 fresh in-cell test trials whose real outcomes the
   generator writes to a side file (the verbs never see them): the fraction with |y − ŷ| ≤ q̂_ρ.
3. **Gate statistics.** Mean of c_ρ against 19/21 = 0.9048 (tolerance 0.01); the fraction of ρ with c_ρ < 0.80
   against 0.069 (tolerance 0.03). Also logged: the standard deviation of c_ρ against 0.063, and the
   Kolmogorov distance between the empirical distribution of c_ρ and Beta(19, 2).
4. **Zero bands where none is due.** Each must be refused, and the count of bands issued across them must be 0:
   - the shifted cell: a band requested for family B with only family A data (`FROB-REF-889`); B episodes
     offered as calibration data for A's cell are rejected by the pool (`FROB-REF-814`, with the differing
     component named);
   - the short cell: 8 calibration points (889: 8 present, 19 needed);
   - the unbuilt design: a design variant with no admitted unit (`FROB-REF-895`, and the payload carries the
     simulation's own interval);
   - a new unit with 3 units calibrated (889: 3 present, 9 needed at 90%).
5. **Carried assertions.** The pooled-regime demonstration through the conformance binary: 20 of 20, 16 of 20,
   36 of 40. Index arithmetic through the verb: level 0.95 at n = 20 returns the largest residual; level 0.96
   returns `band unbounded` and leaves the status `candidate`. Every envelope prints `reality: synthetic`
   (the data is synthetic) and class `Estimated`. A scorecard of five banded metrics reserves 0.02 each. The
   rendered statement contains n, the level and the coverage sentence. A cell with planted low correlation
   carries the real-only recommendation.
6. **Controls (`--controls`).** Builds on which the script must exit non-zero: the index ⌈n·(1−α)⌉ (mean
   coverage reads 0.857); a pool that ignores the cell key (a band is issued for the shifted cell); a band
   verb without the minimum-count guard (a band for the 8-point cell); a quantile computed from the
   calibration fit's own residuals in place of scored predictions.
7. **Determinism.** Fifty resamples twice with one seed: identical q̂_ρ and c_ρ.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Mean in-cell coverage within 0.01 of 19/21 over 1,000 resamples (TARGET), measured value recorded.
- [ ] Fraction of resamples below 0.80 within 0.03 of 0.069 (TARGET), measured value recorded.
- [ ] Zero bands issued for the shifted cell, the short cell and the unbuilt design (and the three-unit case),
      each refused with its code.
- [ ] The carried assertions of step 5 hold.
- [ ] Each control build makes the script exit non-zero.
- [ ] The gate record `GATE-GROUND-06` holds the two statistics, R, the count of wrongly issued bands (0), the
      seed, the commit and the machine, and is what the KILL-BAND decision and bet BET-28 read.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own. The side-file generator has `test_outcomes_are_in_cell` and
`test_outcomes_never_enter_a_dataset`.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Log `target/e2e-logs/ground_band/<utc>.ndjson` through the helper of `STD-E2E`, with
a transcript. Per resample a `metric` line: ρ, q̂_ρ with unit, c_ρ. Per command template a `cmd` line with exit
codes, wall time and the evidence block (class `Estimated`, tier referee, determinism, cell, dataset and model
identities, seed; `reality: synthetic`). `metric` lines for the gate statistics with their thresholds, the
standard deviation and the Kolmogorov distance. Per refusal a `refusal` line: code, observed and required
counts, first repair. On failure: the resamples furthest from expectation with their residual lists' hashes.
Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed, gate touched (GATE-GROUND-06) with both values.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-728 (mean coverage 19/21; fraction below 0.80 about 0.069), KA-729 (the refusals), KA-736 (36 of 40).
Counterexample CX-330. Expected sampling error at R = 1,000: the mean's standard error is 0.063/√1000 = 0.002
(tolerance 0.01 is five standard errors); the fraction's is √(0.069·0.931/1000) = 0.008 (tolerance 0.03 is
nearly four). Negative controls: the four builds of step 6. Metamorphic: scaling the metric's unit leaves
every c_ρ unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release on 50 resamples.

## Considerations

- 1,000 resamples × 20 scored trials go through the real verbs; the script batches them as daemon jobs and is
  resumable by (seed, ρ). The 2,000 test trials per resample are simulated by the generator directly.
- The gate checks the law, not the stand-in: any continuous residual distribution gives Beta(19, 2), so the
  perturbed contact model's details do not matter to the expected values, only to q̂'s size.
- Passing shows that the band arithmetic and the refusals are right on exchangeable synthetic draws. It is
  not evidence about any real robot, and the first real cell is a separate, import-lane gate.
