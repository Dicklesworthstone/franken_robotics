## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) retires a calibration when one of four triggers fires: a scope change, rank
drift, miss counting, or expiry. This bead is what happens next, and how the state is shown.

The plan's statement: on retirement the status becomes `retired(cell, reason)`, reports revert to
`reality: no-claim`, and verdicts that cited the band are marked stale through the studio's
dependency-directed revalidation. The alarm ends the regime, and calibration restarts inside the new one.

And about the display: two facts about power are printed by `ground.band --status` so that a quiet monitor is
not misread. The rank monitor's slack is δ = max(1/√n, 0.02), 0.224 at n = 20, so at n = 20 it sees only
gross shifts. For the miss monitor, the null miss rate p₀(n) is about 0.22 at n = 20; a miss multiplies the
evidence by 2.27 and a cover by 0.641, so four misses in a row retire the calibration and three do not.

A retirement that changed a status word and nothing else would be worse than none: scorecards and verdicts
computed last week would still cite the band. Hence the propagation. And a monitor that reads "e = 0.4, no
alarm" at n = 20 means "no gross failure seen", not "the band is fine"; hence the printed power.

## Scope

In: the retirement transaction; propagation to reports and to results that cited the band; refusal 897;
restart of calibration in a new epoch; manual retirement under a capability; the `--status` listing's content.
Out: the triggers (N30a-40 to N30a-42) and the false-coverage flag (N30a-38), whose events this bead acts
on; the status type (N30a-02); card widths after retirement (N30a-28 defines the rule; this bead emits the
event it reacts to).

## Technical approach

1. **The retirement transaction** (`drift::retire(calibration, reasons) -> Retirement`), atomic in the ledger:
   1. `status::advance(calibrated, Retire(reason))` → `retired(cell, reason)`; event `ground.status`.
   2. Event `ground.retired { band, cell, lineage, reasons, headline, step, monitor_values }`.
   3. Both monitors of the band are closed (their final e-values are in the event).
   4. An invalidation of the band artifact is submitted to the revalidation engine: every stored result
      whose claim product or evidence closure cites the band is marked `stale` with the cause "reality band
      retired: <reason>". Dependency-directed means only dependents are touched; results that never cited
      the band are unchanged, byte for byte.
   5. A regression case is written to the divergence ledger (retirement with its trigger).
   Retiring an already retired calibration is a no-op that returns the first retirement.
   Besides the four triggers of N30a-40 to N30a-42, the transaction reacts to the scorecard's false-coverage
   flag: a `ground.flagged` event written by the e-BH rule of N30a-38 retires that claim's band with reason
   `coverage-flag` (DESIGN, the "a flag retires the band" of N30a-38, which only flags).
2. **Reports.** A statement that cited the band is re-rendered as
   `reality: no-claim [reason=retired:<headline> cell=<id>]` with its simulated value and no band. Report
   bytes are regenerated from the artifact graph, so an old report file is not edited; the next
   `report.design` shows the reverted line, and `evidence.trace` on the old statement shows it stale.
3. **Use of a retired calibration.** Any band request for the cell and lineage, any attempt to cite the band
   in a new result, and any scorecard reservation against it is refused with `FROB-REF-897` (use of a retired
   calibration; repair offered: the retirement reason; recalibrate; exit code 3). The payload carries the
   reasons, the step, and what a recalibration needs (the minimum count for the level).
4. **Restart.** Calibration restarts inside the new regime as a new *epoch* of the cell (or as a new cell when
   the retirement was a scope change that altered the key). An epoch has its own pool, band and monitors.
   Only data acquired after the retirement (by the dataset's acquisition date, and admitted after the
   retirement's ledger sequence) may enter the new epoch's pool; residuals of the old epoch stay in the
   regression set and are never mixed in (the pool guard treats the epoch as part of the pool's key).
5. **Manual retirement.** `frob ground band --action retire --cell <id> --reason "<text>" --json` (the
   registry's `retire` action of the `read` verb, selected by `--action` alone and of kind `admin`, so it
   takes no idempotency key and `--key` on it is 802, VERBS-ground and NJL-01) requires the session
   capability `ground:retire`; without it the capability refusal of the verb layer applies (exit 5). It
   produces `RetireReason::Manual` with the text and the session's identity. A repeat on a cell already
   retired returns the stored retirement record with `already: retired` and writes nothing. There is no
   un-retire.
6. **The status listing** (`frob ground band --status [--cell <id>] --json`, read-only). Per cell and lineage:
   - the reality status and line; n, level, q̂ with unit; the coverage law's mean and standard deviation;
   - the sim–real correlation with its interval, and the real-only recommendation if in force (both computed
     by N30a-38);
   - rank monitor: E⁺, E⁻, E, threshold 1/α_d, and the note "slack δ = <δ>; at this n it sees only shifts that
     move the mean normalized rank beyond <½ + δ>";
   - miss monitor: misses, covers, E, threshold, and the note "null miss rate p₀ = <p₀>; a miss multiplies
     evidence by <a>, a cover by <b>; <k> straight misses retire; it gains evidence only when the true miss
     rate exceeds <p*>", followed by the power to retire within 20 episodes at miss rates 0.6, 0.7 and 0.8 from
     a fresh start and from the monitor's current e-value (`drift::miss_power`, N30a-42);
   - expiry: days and operating hours remaining against the effective limits, their `source` (default or the
     decision record) and the `policy_version` stored at issue (N30a-40);
   - the last scope check and its result; the procedure's step states; the reality-gap variance with its n;
   - for a retired calibration: the reasons, the step, and the count of results marked stale.
   Once per listing: the ceiling statement on the value of pairing real trials with simulation.
7. **Folded from the superseded bead N30b-10.** The transaction is idempotent by cell and trigger, the manual
   form included (it is idempotent by its effect and takes no key, step 5). After it commits: a consumer
   that asks for the cell's bands is
   refused with 897; the `admitted` status of the cell's cards is no longer usable and new training manifests
   resolve to the prior widths (N30a-28, step 6); a later citation of a stale item is refused until it is
   revalidated. A report generated before the retirement is not rewritten; it is stale, and a reader who
   resolves its citations sees that. Pairs of the retired cell never enter the pool of the restarted one
   (814), and the retired data moves to the regression set. Stale marking can be wide: the revalidation engine
   reports the count and the cost before anything is rerun.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a planted alarm the cell's status is `retired(cell, reason)`, and the design report prints
      `reality: no-claim [reason=retired:… cell=…]` for the statement that had the band.
- [ ] A verdict registered with the band in its evidence closure is shown stale by `evidence.trace` with the
      cause; a verdict that did not cite the band is byte-identical before and after.
- [ ] A band request after retirement is refused with 897 carrying the reason; so is a scorecard reservation.
- [ ] A new epoch accepts only data acquired after the retirement; an old-epoch residual pushed to the new pool
      is refused.
- [ ] `--action retire` without `ground:retire` is refused; with it, the retirement is recorded with the
      session's identity, and a repeat returns the stored record with `already: retired`; `--key` on the
      action is refused with 802; there is no verb that reverses it.
- [ ] `--status` at n = 20 prints δ = 0.224, p₀ = 0.22, factors 2.27 and 0.641, "4 straight misses retire",
      the miss monitor's power (fresh start 0.918, 0.990, 1.000 at 0.6, 0.7, 0.8; after ten straight covers
      0.427, 0.779, 0.969) and the expiry countdown with the limits' source; at n = 200 it prints δ = 0.071 and
      p₀ = 0.14.
- [ ] The retirement transaction is atomic under a crash injected after each of its five steps.
- [ ] A training manifest compiled after a retirement has the prior widths.
- [ ] A `ground.flagged` event for a claim retires that claim's band with reason `coverage-flag`; other
      claims' bands on the same scorecard are untouched.
- [ ] No pair of the retired cell or epoch appears in the pool of the restarted one.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_drift.sh` (bead N30a-44): after each kind of trigger the script runs
`frob report design <design> --json`, `frob evidence trace <claim> --json` on the statement of the verdict
that cited the band and on that of one that did not (a retirement is not an edit, so `result.revalidate`,
whose change is an edit, a `--since` identity, a version pair or an amendment, has no form for it; the
engine's stale mark is read from the trace, step 2), `frob ground band --cell <c> --metric <m> --level 0.90
--json` and `frob ground band --status --cell <c> --json`. Logged:
the status before and after, the reality line, the stale-result count, the 897 payload, the status listing's
power notes, and the ledger sequences of the retirement's events.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-730 (the planted drift retires the calibration) and KA-731 (the printed miss-monitor arithmetic).
Negative controls: a retirement that changes the status and submits no invalidation (the stale-verdict test
fails); a report renderer that keeps printing the cached band; a status listing that omits the power notes; a
new epoch that inherits the old pool. Chaos (tier G4): the crash-injection test. Determinism: class D0 for the
transaction and the listing's text.
Folded from N30b-10: planted failure: a retirement that updates the status and skips stale marking (the stale
list is empty: caught). Chaos lane: a kill at each step of the transaction leaves either no retirement or a
complete one.

## Considerations

- Retirement is conservative by design: a false retirement costs a recalibration (some real episodes); a
  missed one costs results that claim a coverage they do not have. The loss model behind α_d says the second
  is far more expensive.
- Marking results stale does not delete them; a stale verdict can be revalidated once a new band exists, or
  kept as a simulation-only result with `reality: no-claim`.
- If the kill gate KILL-BAND fires, every band is withdrawn through this same transaction with reason
  "bands withdrawn", and reports print `reality: no-claim` with the raw residual table.
- The listing is long. It is paginated like any long answer and never cut silently.
- A maintenance event that nobody declared cannot be detected by the deterministic triggers; the monitors are
  the backstop, and their power at small n is printed.
