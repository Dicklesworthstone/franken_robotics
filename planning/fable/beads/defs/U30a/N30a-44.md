## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) retires a calibration, and with it the cell's reality band, when new episodes
stop being exchangeable with the calibration episodes. Four triggers exist: a scope change (controller or
firmware identity, a replaced or re-lubricated part, the surface class, a recorded condition leaving the
envelope, a changed model identity including a kernel change that alters bits, an instrument's calibration
identity), which fires immediately and deterministically; a rank-drift e-process on each new residual's rank
in the frozen calibration residuals; a miss-counting e-process on band misses against the null miss rate
p₀(n); and expiry by age or operating hours. The statistical monitors alarm at an e-value of 1/α_d, α_d = 0.05.

**GATE-GROUND-07** (claim kind: known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): the planted drift retires the calibration
within 20 episodes in at least 90% of resamples; false retirement at most 5% over 10⁴ null runs; every scope
change fires. With the band-coverage gate it feeds the kill gate **KILL-BAND**: if either fails, bands are
withdrawn and reports print `reality: no-claim` with the raw residual table.

Everything runs on the synthetic rehearsal: a truth simulator stands in for the robot, and all data is
labelled `synthetic`.

## Scope

In: the script, its three batteries (planted drift, null runs, scope changes), expiry cases, the effects of
retirement seen from the user's side, controls, log and gate record. Out: the mechanisms (N30a-40 to N30a-43).

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/ground_drift.sh [--seed S] [--resamples 1000] [--null-runs 10000] [--controls] [--quick]`,
plain shell over `frob`, `frobd` and the rehearsal generator, under `target/e2e-work/ground_drift/`; default
seed 8. `--quick` uses 50 resamples and 500 null runs and never records the gate.

1. **Calibrated cells.** For each resample: a one-unit cell of the instrumented cart taken through ingest,
   predict, score and band at level 0.90 with n = 20 (as in the band script), giving a frozen residual list,
   q̂, and two fresh monitors.
2. **Battery A: planted drift.** The truth simulator's joint friction is raised by 30% after episode k (k
   drawn in 5..15 by address). Monitoring episodes are then admitted one at a time with
   `frob ground ingest <episode bundle> --design <cart design> --task-family <sill task family> --key
   gd-a-<S>-<r>-<t> --json` (a key per resample r and episode t, KNOWN_DRIFTS 5; each admission feeds the
   monitors automatically). For each resample record the number of
   post-change episodes until the status becomes `retired`, and which monitor fired. Gate statistic: the
   fraction of resamples retired within 20 post-change episodes; assert ≥ 0.90. Also logged: the median
   detection delay, the split between rank and miss alarms, and the fraction of post-change episodes that
   missed the band (the effect size the 30% change actually produced on the metric).
3. **Battery B: null runs.** 10⁴ runs with no change: a calibrated cell and 200 further exchangeable
   monitoring episodes each. To keep 2 × 10⁶ ingests affordable, this battery drives the monitors through
   KA-730's runner entry in the conformance crate (step 9; the crate has test targets and no binary, BD-025;
   an earlier text called a "conformance binary") on residual streams produced by the same generator (the
   code path from residual to retirement is the production one; only the verb layer is bypassed), and a
   subset of 200 runs goes through `frob ground ingest` end to end and must agree bit for bit with the
   runner's trajectory digests for the same streams, which its receipt carries per run. Gate statistic: the
   fraction of runs with any retirement; assert ≤ 0.05.
4. **Battery C: every scope change fires.** On separate calibrated cells, one planted change each, admitted
   through `frob ground ingest` or requested through `frob ground band`: controller hash; firmware `2.4.1` to
   `2.4.2`; a part replaced; a part re-lubricated; surface dry to wet; an episode at 26 °C against [18, 24];
   an instrument calibration identity; a new card version; a kernel build with a different replay digest.
   Assert for each: retirement in the same call, the reason naming the component, exit code 2 on ingest
   (completed with findings, §32.4: the data is admitted and the retirement is the finding; 0 would mean
   nothing to flag) and `FROB-REF-897` on the next band request.
   Gate statistic: fired count over 9; assert 9 of 9. Counter-case: a dataset from another unit of the same
   design retires nothing.
5. **Expiry.** With the ledger date advanced 91 days: retired by age; with a manifest reporting 201 operating
   hours since calibration: retired by hours; at 89 days and 199 hours: not retired. The limits are the
   defaults of `registries/ground/expiry.toml` (N30a-40) and the log prints their `source` and
   `policy_version`; until the decision OPEN-GROUND-3 is recorded (N30b-27) the source reads `default`.
6. **Effects.** After one retirement of each kind: the design report prints
   `reality: no-claim [reason=retired:…]`; `frob evidence trace <claim> --json` shows the verdict that cited
   the band as stale and the one that did not untouched (N30a-43's forms); `frob ground band --status`
   prints the reasons and, for a
   live n = 20 cell, the power notes (δ = 0.224; p₀ = 0.22; factors 2.27 and 0.641; four straight misses
   retire; power from a fresh start and from the current e-value). A scripted sequence of misses reproduces
   11.7 after three and 26.7 after four.
7. **Controls (`--controls`).** Builds on which the script must exit non-zero: a miss monitor with the nominal
   null 0.10 (battery B exceeds 5%); a rank monitor with no slack (battery B); monitors that are never fed on
   ingest (battery A never retires); a scope check that ignores firmware (battery C reads 8 of 9); a
   retirement that submits no invalidation (step 6).
8. **Determinism.** Fifty resamples of battery A twice with one seed: identical detection delays and e-value
   trajectories. The same fifty once with `FROB_AIRLOCK=inproc` and once with `FROB_AIRLOCK=worker` (the
   drift and conformal calls of N30a-06 in process and through the worker): equal trajectory digests; the log
   carries the mode, the sibling commit and the feature set.
9. **Folded from the superseded bead N30b-34 (its drift half).**
   - *Obligations first.* N30b-24's test target, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<S> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
     ground_obligations` (the form that crate has; an earlier text called a `frob-conformance run` binary,
     which the `no_conformance_command` lint refuses), runs before the batteries; from its receipt the script
     logs each proof obligation's measured value against its acceptance bound and the union
     false-alarm fraction of the two monitors, and records the decision-policy version in force for the rest
     of the run. If the union exceeds 5%, the pre-declared half-level adjustment (alarm threshold 40 for each
     monitor, a new decision-policy version) applies before the gate is measured. Both drift clauses are
     measured under one decision-policy version.
   - *Fixtures.* R-10 (a calibration of 20 episodes, 10 clean episodes, then joint friction raised by 30% from
     episode 31, with residual noise registered so that the post-drift miss probability is at least 0.8) and
     R-11 (the miss arithmetic) of the rehearsal catalogue (BD-388). The gate is recorded on the registered
     fixture; battery A's variant with the change drawn in 5..15 is run and logged as information. The
     registered miss probability is what makes the 90% clause reachable. From a fresh start (e-value 1) the
     miss monitor at n = 20 retires within 20 episodes with probability 0.70, 0.92 and 0.99 at post-drift miss
     probabilities 0.5, 0.6 and 0.7 (exact first-passage values 0.702, 0.918 and 0.990). But the monitor is
     not fresh at the change: the ten clean episodes multiply its e-value by 0.641 per cover, and averaged over
     what an honest band's clean phase does (miss rate drawn from Beta(2, 19)) the power is only 0.586, 0.863
     and 0.983 at 0.6, 0.7 and 0.8 (after ten straight covers: 0.427, 0.779, 0.969); these are the figures
     N30a-42 tests. A registered 0.7 would therefore give about 0.86 and fail the clause for a reason of power,
     not of correctness; 0.8 gives 0.98. The script logs, per resample, the e-value at the change and the
     computed power from it, and the mean of that power over the resamples beside the measured fraction. The null battery's horizon is 200 monitoring episodes per run, the longer of the two horizons that
     were drafted (a longer horizon can only raise the false-retirement fraction), stopped at the first
     crossing.
   - *Batteries as conformance runs.* The gate's run in the conformance crate's law tier (both of its rows,
     KA-730 and KA-731, are G0), `FROB_TEST_SEED=<S> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g0_laws --features
     gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-GROUND-07`, which runs KA-730's runner entry (the drift battery on R-10, 1,000
     resamples, and its control, the null battery of 10⁴ runs) and KA-731's (the miss arithmetic on R-11, and
     its DESIGN control, the monitor with p₀ = 0.10), runs every control registered for the gate in the same
     run (KNOWN_DRIFTS 1; with CX-G-12's plant `BD-394:cx-<nnn>`, each of which the receipt must show
     `caught`, N38b-19), and writes the hash-named receipt the gate record cites. From it:
     `e2e_metric retired_within_20 <v> 1 >= 0.90`;
     `e2e_metric false_retirement <v> 1 <= 0.05` with the per-monitor fractions logged; and from battery C
     `e2e_metric scope_changes_fired <count> changes == 9`; each with `--gate GATE-GROUND-07`; then
     `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-07`, which declares the gate touched and records nothing. The verb-path runs
     (battery A's resamples, battery B's 200-run subset) go through `frobd` as jobs.
   - *The decision core.* `frob cores show dc-07 --json`; one cell is walked through uncalibrated,
     calibrated-exchangeable, calibrated-shifted and retired, logging each decision record, and each fallback
     trigger is forced (fewer than 19 calibration points; a monitor at or above 20; an audited miss rate above
     its bound; a scope change).
   - *The planner rows.* `frob experiment suggest … --json` for the cell before and after calibration: the
     real-batch rows (k, s̃, value), the simulation row, and which is larger in each state.
   - *Plant switches.* With `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE` set to `monitor-resets`, `threshold-10` or
     `scope-ignores-instrument` the script exits non-zero and names the clause.
   - If KILL-BAND has fired, the script asserts the fallback (residual tables, no bands, no monitors) and
     still runs the obligation step and the scope-change battery, which the fallback keeps.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Battery A: retired within 20 episodes in at least 90% of 1,000 resamples (TARGET), measured value
      recorded.
- [ ] Battery B: false retirement at most 5% over 10⁴ null runs (TARGET), measured value recorded; the 200
      end-to-end runs agree bit for bit with the runner entry's trajectories.
- [ ] Battery C: 9 of 9 scope changes fire with the right reason; the other-unit counter-case retires nothing.
- [ ] Expiry and effects behave as stated; the miss arithmetic prints 11.7 and 26.7.
- [ ] Each control build makes the script exit non-zero; the in-process and worker runs give equal digests.
- [ ] Rows KA-730 and KA-731 of the Ground known-answer registry (N30-KA) are bound by this bead: it adds their
      runner entries `ka:KA-730@referee` (the R-10 drift battery with the 10⁴-run null battery as its control)
      and `ka:KA-731@host` (the miss arithmetic 26.7 and 11.7, with its DESIGN control, a monitor using
      p₀ = 0.10 that retires after two misses at e = 25), N38a-13, and names, in each row's derivation front
      matter, the test that checks it (crate, path and name). The known-answer run reports both rows `pass`
      with their controls caught; each is logged as an `assert` line with its KA number.
- [ ] The script writes no gate record (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9). This bead is the gate's one recorder: from the
      receipt of the gate run of step 9, passing or not, a session that did not implement the measured beads
      enters GATE-GROUND-07 with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-GROUND-07
      --receipt <hash>`, holding the three statistics, the resample and run counts, the decision-policy
      version under which both drift clauses were measured, the seed, the commit and the machine; it is what
      the KILL-BAND decision reads. A failing value is recorded `fail` (with every control `caught`), and this
      bead then closes `recorded` on that record (N41-17 rule 2; KILL-BAND's trigger names no repair cycle),
      which is what makes the KILL-BAND decision bead N30b-18 ready.
- [ ] The decision core's walk logs one decision record per state and each forced fallback; the planner rows
      are logged before and after calibration.
- [ ] Each plant switch makes the script exit non-zero and name the clause.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Log `target/e2e-logs/ground_drift/<utc>.ndjson` through the helper of `STD-E2E`, with
a transcript. Battery A: per resample a `metric` line (k, detection delay, monitor that fired, final E⁺, E⁻
and miss e-value). Battery B: per 1,000 runs a `metric` line with the running alarm fraction, split by
monitor. Battery C and expiry: per case a `cmd` line (exact command, exit code, wall time, evidence block,
`reality` text before and after) and a `refusal` line for the follow-up band request. `metric` lines for the
three gate statistics with thresholds 0.90, 0.05 and 9 of 9. On failure: the resamples not retired in time
with their e-value trajectories and residual lists' hashes. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed,
gate touched (GATE-GROUND-07) with its three values.

## Considerations

- Whether a 30% friction increase is detected within 20 episodes at n = 20 depends on how much it moves the
  banded metric relative to q̂. The monitors gain evidence only under gross under-coverage (a true miss rate
  above about 0.35 for the miss monitor). If the planted change produces a smaller effect on the chosen
  metric, the gate fails for a reason that is about power, not about a bug; the log's effect-size line makes
  that visible, and the response (a more sensitive metric such as peak current, a larger n) is a design
  decision for the Ground lead, recorded with the gate.
- Battery B's bypass of the verb layer is a cost decision; the 200 end-to-end runs are the check that the
  bypass changes nothing.
- A passing gate says the monitors behave as specified on synthetic drift. It is not evidence that real
  drift looks like a friction step.
