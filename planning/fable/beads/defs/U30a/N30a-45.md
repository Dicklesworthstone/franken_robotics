## Background

A built part is one more representation of the design. Ground (crate `frob-ground`) can ingest a scan of a
built part, align it to the design, and turn the differences into model parameters, so that the question "does
the unit we actually built still pass?" can be put to the simulator. This bead is the front of that loop:
admitting the scan and registering it.

The plan's steps: (1) the build packet declares datums and at least three non-collinear fiducials per part at
design time; that is what makes registration well-posed. (2) A scan with fiducial correspondences is admitted
like any dataset. (3) The sibling routine `fs-asbuilt::rigid3::register3` aligns scan to design in closed
form. The similarity variant reports scale with a standard error and a unit-suspicion diagnostic: a scale near
25.4 or 0.001 is a unit error, not shrinkage. `estimate_calibrated_rigid3` returns a six-parameter pose
covariance.

Two cautions from the plan. If the registration residual exceeds the deviation under review, the signal is
below the noise and no deviation is reported (the sibling's `well_posed` screen). And the residual is a fit
diagnostic, not a transform uncertainty: a small residual on three fiducials says the three points fit, not
that the pose is known that well. Because the sibling does not search for correspondences (FACT: it registers
with supplied correspondences only; there is no closest-point matching), Ground accepts only scans with
fiducials, or scans registered by an identified external tool whose identity is recorded as provenance
(refusal 812 otherwise).

Rejected alternatives: iterative closest-point registration written here (it converges to wrong local optima
on symmetric parts, and its uncertainty is hard to state; upstream work item `FD-FSIM-25` tracks a
correspondence-search lane in the sibling); trusting the scanner's own "aligned" export with no identity.

## Scope

In: the scan dataset's extra manifest fields; the two admissible routes (fiducials; identified external tool);
the registration jobs; pose covariance; scale and unit suspicion; the well-posedness and noise screens; the
registration artifact. Out: deviation fields and propagation (N30a-46); the as-built model variant and the
tolerance audit (N30a-47); the generic admission rules (BD-387).

## Technical approach

1. **Scan dataset** (`kind = "scan"` in `frob.ground.dataset/1`; the `[scan]`, `[[fiducial]]` and
   `[registration]` fields are defined, validated for shape and hashed by the schema's minor version 1,
   N30ax-01, a dependency, which keeps the manifest one definition; this bead reads them and adds the checks
   that need the build packet):
   `[scan] part = "<design part id>"; unit_serial = "unit-07"; length_unit = "mm"; packet = "<build packet
   hash>"`; the scanner as an `[[instrument]]` with per-point `sigma` and `bias_bound`;
   `[[fiducial]] id = "F1"; measured = [x, y, z]; sigma = 0.02` for each fiducial found in the scan, whose
   `id` must exist in the packet's fiducial list for that part; the point cloud as chunks (at most 10⁶ points
   per registration job). Licence, rights and provenance are required as for any dataset; a scan of a part is
   admitted with origin `real`, a nominal or generated cloud with origin `synthetic`.
2. **Route A: fiducials.** Requirements checked before any sibling call: at least three fiducials matched to
   packet fiducials; non-collinear (the sibling's test, and a local pre-check: the area of the largest
   triangle they span is at least 10⁻³ of the squared largest distance, DESIGN). Fewer than three, or
   collinear ones, is refused with `FROB-REF-812` (an as-built registration that is ill-posed or below its
   noise screen; repair offered: fiducials needed; residual against deviation; exit code 3).
3. **Route B: an identified external tool.** The manifest carries `[registration] tool = "<name>"; version =
   "<v>"; digest = "<hash of the executable or its release>"; transform = [...]; stated_sigma = {rotation_rad,
   translation}`. The identity is recorded as provenance on everything derived. A pre-registered scan with no
   tool identity, or with no stated uncertainty, is refused with 812.
4. **Registration (route A).** `Register3Job { pairs, weights = 1/σ², kind: Rigid }` → rotation R and
   translation t in closed form (the weighted orthogonal Procrustes solution), the fiducial residuals and
   their root mean square. `estimate_calibrated_rigid3` → the 6×6 covariance of the pose (three rotation
   components, three translation components) from the declared fiducial uncertainties.
5. **Scale and unit suspicion.** A second job with `kind: Similarity` returns the scale s with its standard
   error. If |s − 1| ≤ 3·se the scale is consistent with 1. If s is within 1% of 25.4, 1/25.4, 0.001 or 1,000
   (the plan names 25.4 and 0.001; the reciprocals are added, DESIGN), the sibling's unit-suspicion
   diagnostic is raised and the registration is refused with 812 and the repair "declare the scan's length
   unit: observed scale <s> ± <se>". Any other significant scale (for example 0.995 on a printed part) is
   reported as shrinkage with its standard error and carried into the as-built parameters.
6. **Screens.** (a) Well-posedness as in step 2. (b) Noise: `WellPosedJob { residual_rms, deviation_under_
   review }`; when the registration residual exceeds the deviation under review (the tolerance being audited,
   or the smallest deviation the caller asks to resolve), the signal is below the noise: no deviation is
   reported for that feature and the result says so (812 when the whole request is below the screen).
7. **What is reported about uncertainty.** The pose covariance is the transform's uncertainty. The fiducial
   residual is printed separately, labelled "fit diagnostic", and no interface presents it as ± on the pose.
8. **Artifact.** `frob.ground.registration/1`: scan and packet identities, route, fiducials used, R and t,
   pose covariance, scale with standard error and the suspicion flag, residual RMS, screens passed, sibling
   receipts. Class Estimated; deterministic (closed form; bitwise twice on one machine).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A planted rotation of 10° about z and translation (1, 2, 3) mm with fiducial noise σ = 0.02 mm on six
      fiducials is recovered with every pose component inside 3 standard deviations of the reported
      covariance; over 10³ noise draws the mean squared Mahalanobis distance of the pose error under the
      reported covariance is within 10% of 6 (5.5 standard errors) and each component's normalized error has
      variance within 0.15 of 1 (3.4 standard errors; a 10% window would be 2.2 standard errors, and a
      correct covariance would fail one of six components on about 14% of seeds). The covariance is
      calibrated.
- [ ] Two fiducials: refused with 812; three collinear fiducials: refused with 812; a fiducial id absent from
      the packet's fiducial list for the part: refused with 812 naming the id (this check is made here, where
      the packet is read; N30ax-01 checks only the manifest's shape).
- [ ] A cloud exported in inches against a design in millimetres (scale 25.4) raises unit suspicion and is
      refused; a scale of 0.995 ± 0.0005 is reported as shrinkage and not flagged.
- [ ] With a residual of 0.4 mm and a deviation under review of 0.3 mm, no deviation is reported and the
      result names the screen; with a residual of 0.05 mm the deviation is reported.
- [ ] A pre-registered scan without a tool identity is refused; with one, the identity appears in the
      provenance of the registration artifact.
- [ ] No output field or message presents the fiducial residual as the pose's uncertainty.

`rigid_recovery_inside_covariance`; `pose_covariance_is_calibrated_mc` (10³ draws, seeded);
`two_fiducials_refused_812`; `collinear_fiducials_refused_812`; `fiducial_id_must_exist_in_packet`;
`scale_25_4_unit_suspicion`; `scale_0_001_unit_suspicion`; `reciprocal_scales_flagged`;
`shrinkage_0_995_reported_not_flagged`; `noise_screen_blocks_deviation_below_residual`;
`residual_never_labelled_uncertainty` (a schema test on the artifact and the envelope);
`external_tool_identity_required`; `external_tool_provenance_carried`; `point_cap_1e6_prechecked`;
`weights_follow_fiducial_sigma`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_asbuilt.sh` (bead N30a-48): `frob ground ingest` of a synthetic scan of one link.
Logged: fiducials matched, R and t against the planted pose with their standard deviations, the scale with
its standard error, the residual RMS, each screen's outcome, and the refusal payloads for the two-fiducial and
inch-unit controls.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-733 (as-built registration: a planted rotation and translation recovered inside the stated uncertainty;
controls: two fiducials refused; scale 25.4 raises unit suspicion). Negative controls: a registration that
reports the residual RMS as the translation uncertainty (the calibration test fails: three well-fitting
fiducials give a tiny residual and a large true pose error at the far end of the part); a unit check that
silently rescales by 25.4; a route that accepts a scan with neither fiducials nor a tool identity.
Determinism: closed-form jobs, bitwise twice; in process and through the worker with equal digests.

## Considerations

- Kill gate KILL-ASBUILT (decided at M5): if the as-built gate fails, scans are not ingested, and as-built
  data enters as scalar bench measurements (mass, lengths), each a direct measurement of one card field.
- The interim posture for correspondence search is that fiducials are placed at design time; the switch-over
  test for the upstream item is to re-run the as-built known answers without fiducials.
- Fiducials placed close together give a well-fitting registration with a large lever-arm error far from
  them; the pose covariance shows it, and the packet's fiducial placement rule (spread across the part) is
  the design-side remedy.
- A scan of a part made by a third party may carry licence terms; the scan's licence joins the packet's, and a
  non-redistributable scan stays out of any replication kit.
