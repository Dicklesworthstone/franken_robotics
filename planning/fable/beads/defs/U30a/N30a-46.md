## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) ingests a scan of a built part and registers it to the design (N30a-45). This
bead turns the registered scan into numbers an engineer and the simulator can use, in the two steps the plan
gives:

- **Step 4.** The sibling's `field` module computes signed deviation, wall thickness and form fields from
  supplied surface correspondences.
- **Step 5.** The sibling's `propagate` module carries pose covariance into link length, axis offset and axis
  parallelism with their cross-correlation, and downgrades a term to unknown when its sigma-point check shows
  the linearization is poor.

Why the second step matters: a robot's kinematics do not depend on how far each surface point is from nominal;
they depend on a few derived quantities, the distance between joint axes, their offset and their parallelism.
Those are functions of the registration, whose uncertainty is a 6×6 pose covariance, and two of them are
correlated through it (a tilt of the scan changes both an axis angle and a length). Reporting each with an
independent error bar would be wrong in both directions. And some of them are badly nonlinear exactly where
they matter (an angle between two nearly parallel axes), so a first-order error bar can be confidently wrong;
the sigma-point check exists to say "unknown" instead.

FACT (sibling capabilities): fields come from supplied correspondences (deviation, thickness, form and
roughness); propagation handles at most 64 correlated quantities of interest; a separate module gives
simultaneous decision bounds that need no Gaussian assumption. The sibling does not read density or porosity
and has no mass properties.

## Scope

In: surface correspondences and their provenance; the three fields with per-point and pose-borne uncertainty;
feature fits (axes, planes) on the scan; the quantities of interest and their propagated covariance; the
sigma-point downgrade; simultaneous bounds for several features. Out: registration (N30a-45); converting
deltas into model parameters and mass properties, and the tolerance audit (N30a-47).

## Technical approach

1. **Surface correspondences.** The sibling needs pairs (scan point p_i, nominal surface point s_i with unit
   normal n_i). Two sources, each recorded as provenance: (a) an identified external tool supplies them with
   the scan; (b) Ground generates them: each registered scan point is projected onto the design's nominal
   surface with the workspace's signed-distance query on the part's Region (the foot point and the gradient
   give s_i and n_i). Points farther than a gate distance (default 5 times the scanner's σ plus the allocated
   tolerance; DESIGN) from any surface are left unpaired and counted. This is a projection after a well-posed
   registration, not a correspondence search used to register.
2. **Deviation field.** `FieldJob::Deviation` → d_i = n_i·(p_i − s_i), positive outward. Per-point variance
   var(d_i) = σ_scan² + J_i·Σ_pose·J_iᵀ with J_i = [(s_i × n_i)ᵀ, n_iᵀ] (the sensitivity of the normal
   deviation to a small rotation about the origin and a translation) and Σ_pose the registration's covariance.
   The pose term is common to all points of a patch (it does not average away); patch means are reported with
   the independent part reduced by the point count and the pose part kept whole.
3. **Wall thickness.** `FieldJob::Thickness` on paired inner and outer correspondences along the nominal
   normal: thickness = nominal thickness + d_outer − d_inner at each paired location. The pose term cancels to
   first order for opposing faces of one wall, so thickness is known better than either face's position; the
   job reports it with its own variance.
4. **Form.** `FieldJob::Form` per functional feature: flatness (the range of residuals about the best-fit
   plane), cylindricity (about the best-fit cylinder), each with the fitted feature's parameters.
5. **Feature fits** (Ground's, DESIGN): the axis of a bore by a least-squares cylinder fit to its paired
   points; a mounting face by a least-squares plane; each returns the feature in the design frame with a
   covariance from the point noise.
6. **Quantities of interest.** From the fitted features: link length (the distance between the two joint
   axes' reference points), axis offset (the shortest distance between two axis lines), axis parallelism (the
   angle between two axis directions), and any feature the tolerance allocation lists (foot or tool position
   error, closure error of a loop, wheel radius). Each is a function q_k(features, pose).
7. **Propagation.** `PropagateJob { q: at most 64 functions, pose covariance, feature covariances }` → the
   vector of q_k, its covariance with cross-correlations, and per quantity a sigma-point check: q_k is
   evaluated at the sigma points of the input distribution; if the sigma-point mean or variance differs from
   the linearized one beyond the sibling's threshold, that quantity's uncertainty is downgraded to `unknown`
   (its value is still printed, flagged). More than 64 quantities are split into jobs by part, never
   truncated.
8. **Simultaneous bounds.** When several features are judged together (a tolerance audit of m features), the
   sibling's `uncertainty` module supplies simultaneous decision bounds that need no Gaussian assumption, so
   that "all m in tolerance" is a statement with a stated family-wise level, not m separate ones.
9. **Artifact.** `frob.ground.asbuilt_fields/1`: the correspondence source and counts (paired, unpaired);
   per-patch deviation statistics; thickness per wall; form per feature; the quantities of interest with
   covariance and downgrade flags. Class Estimated; closed-form given its inputs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A planted wall deviation of +0.3 mm on one face (500 points, scanner σ = 0.05 mm) is recovered: the
      patch mean deviation is within its stated uncertainty of 0.300 mm; the wall thickness is recovered as
      nominal + 0.3 mm within its uncertainty.
- [ ] The pose term does not average away: doubling the point count halves the independent variance and
      leaves the pose-borne variance of the patch mean unchanged.
- [ ] Thickness uncertainty is smaller than either face's position uncertainty on the same wall.
- [ ] Planted link-length error of +0.2 mm and axis tilt of 0.1° are recovered within the propagated
      covariance, and the reported correlation between the two is non-zero with the sign of the planted
      geometry.
- [ ] The angle between two nominally parallel axes with a planted tilt smaller than its standard deviation
      is flagged `unknown` by the sigma-point check; with a tilt of ten standard deviations it is not.
- [ ] 65 quantities are split into two jobs; no call exceeds the cap; unpaired points are counted and reported.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/asbuilt_fields.rs`: `projection_pairs_on_plane_and_cylinder` (foot points and
normals exact on analytic Regions); `gate_distance_leaves_outliers_unpaired`; `deviation_sign_outward`;
`planted_wall_deviation_0p3mm`; `pose_term_is_common_mode` (patch-mean variance against point count);
`thickness_cancels_pose_first_order`; `flatness_of_planted_bow` (a 0.1 mm bow on a 100 mm face reads 0.1 mm
within noise); `cylinder_axis_fit_recovers_tilt`; `link_length_and_tilt_covariance`
(a rotation uncertainty of 10⁻³ rad about an axis 150 mm from the far bore gives a 0.15 mm contribution to
that bore's position); `parallelism_near_zero_downgraded_unknown`; `parallelism_large_tilt_not_downgraded`;
`qoi_cap_64_split`; `simultaneous_bounds_wider_than_marginal`; `fields_artifact_roundtrip`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_asbuilt.sh` (bead N30a-48). Logged: paired and unpaired point counts; per patch the mean
deviation with its independent and pose-borne standard deviations; thickness per wall; the quantities of
interest with standard deviations, correlations and downgrade flags, each against its planted value.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-733 (a planted rotation and translation; a 0.3 mm wall deviation; recovered inside the stated
uncertainty). Negative controls: deviations reported with point noise only (the pose-term test fails: the
patch mean's error bar shrinks without limit); a propagation that drops cross-correlations (the
length-and-tilt test fails); a propagation without the sigma-point check (the near-zero angle gets a
confident, wrong error bar and the test must catch it); correspondences generated before registration.
Determinism: closed forms; projection uses the exact or double-precision distance query; bitwise twice on one
machine; in process and through the worker with equal digests.

## Considerations

- Projection onto the nominal surface mis-pairs points near edges and thin features; the gate distance and
  the unpaired count are the visible defence, and features within a few σ of an edge are excluded from patch
  statistics (recorded).
- Roughness fields exist in the sibling; nothing in the model consumes them yet, so they are not computed by
  default.
- The sibling reads no density or porosity: internal voids of a printed part are invisible to a surface scan.
  Mass is therefore taken from a scale (N30a-47), never inferred from the scan alone.
- If the kill gate KILL-ASBUILT fires, none of this runs; lengths then enter as scalar bench measurements.
