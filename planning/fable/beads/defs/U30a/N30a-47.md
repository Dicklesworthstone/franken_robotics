## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) ingests a scan of a built part, registers it, and computes deviation fields and
derived quantities (link length, axis offset, axis parallelism) with their covariance (N30a-45, N30a-46).
This bead closes the loop the plan describes in three steps:

- **Step 6.** Ground converts geometric deltas into posteriors on `.fbot` parameters (the design's own
  parameters: lengths, offsets, axis directions), and converts volume and a scale reading into mass, centre of
  mass and inertia through the sibling `fs-mbd-material`, which compiles geometry and card density into the
  existing spatial inertia (FACT). Measured mass against computed volume gives the effective density of a
  printed part.
- **Step 7.** The result is an as-built model variant with provenance `as-built`, hence a new `ModelId`.
  Per-world model variants hold the as-designed and as-built robots in one batch, so "does the built unit
  still pass?" is one run.
- **Step 8.** Each measured feature is compared with its allocated tolerance and sensitivity.

The value: tolerance questions stop being a matter of opinion. A link printed 0.4 mm long and 30% light is
either inside what the requirement can bear or not, and the simulator can say which, under the same task and
the same seeds as the nominal design. Because the as-built robot is a new model identity (decision
ADR-GROUND-06), nothing proved or tested about the as-designed robot is silently attributed to it.

## Scope

In: the mapping from measured quantities to design parameters; mass properties and effective density; the
variant, its provenance and identity; the paired batch run; the tolerance audit. Out: registration and fields
(N30a-45, N30a-46); tolerance allocation itself (BD-406); identification of inertia by pendulum (N30a-25),
whose result takes precedence here when present.

## Technical approach

1. **Geometry to parameters.** Each quantity of interest from N30a-46 that corresponds to a declared design
   parameter (a link length, an axis offset, a joint-axis direction, a wheel radius) gives that parameter a
   posterior: mean = the measured value; covariance = the propagated block, with cross-correlations kept. A
   quantity whose uncertainty was downgraded to `unknown` updates nothing: the parameter keeps its nominal
   value with its allocated tolerance as its width, and the variant records the gap. A measured similarity
   scale (shrinkage) is applied to the part's lengths before feature deltas, never both.
2. **Mass properties.** Inputs: a scale reading m with its instrument uncertainty (a bench dataset); the
   as-built volume V (nominal volume from the design's Regions, corrected by s³ for a measured scale s and by
   wall area times mean thickness deviation for shells), with its uncertainty. Effective density
   ρ_eff = m/V, relative variance (σ_m/m)² + (σ_V/V)². `fs-mbd-material` then compiles the as-built geometry
   with ρ_eff into the part's spatial inertia, giving centre of mass and inertia tensor. Provenance per field:
   mass `measured`; density `as-built (mass over volume)`; centre of mass and inertia
   `as-built (derived, uniform density assumed)`. If a balance or pendulum measurement exists for the part,
   it replaces the derived value for that field. The inertia must pass the physical-consistency guard
   (positive definite, triangle inequalities, centre of mass inside the hull).
3. **The variant.** `asbuilt::variant(design, unit, measurements) -> ModelVariant`: the as-designed model with
   a parameter row replaced by the posteriors' means, each field carrying provenance
   `as-built { scan, registration, unit }` and its uncertainty. It is a parameter variant of the same
   topology (the batched engine's first variant level), it is content-addressed, and its `ModelId` differs
   from the as-designed one. Results, verdicts, certificates and bands of the as-designed model are not
   inherited.
4. **One batch.** `frob ground ingest <scan> … --emit-variant` returns the variant's identity; any study verb
   that accepts variants (`sim.run`, `eval`, a task verdict) runs the as-designed and the as-built model as
   two parameter rows over the same world addresses. The answer to "does the built unit still pass?" is the
   pair of verdicts and, for each requirement, the paired difference with its interval. Evidence: class of
   the weakest input (the variant is Estimated); `reality: no-claim` (a measured geometry inside a simulator
   is still a simulation).
5. **Sampling the as-built uncertainty.** The variant's posterior covariance can be randomized over like a
   card's (correlated draws by address), so the paired run can report how often the built unit passes within
   its measurement uncertainty.
6. **Tolerance audit.** For each feature i in the packet's tolerance list with allocated tolerance t_i,
   sensitivity s_i and class: measured deviation δ_i with uncertainty u_i (simultaneous bound factor k_s from
   N30a-46). Verdict: `in` if |δ_i| + k_s·u_i ≤ t_i; `out` if |δ_i| − k_s·u_i > t_i; `undecided` otherwise;
   `not-resolved` if the feature failed the noise screen. Budget use: with the allocation's coverage factor k
   (tolerance = k standard deviations; k = 3 in the allocation's convention) and variance budget B,
   used = Σ_i s_i²·(δ_i/k)²/B. The audit artifact `frob.ground.tolerance_audit/1` lists every feature, the
   verdicts, the budget used, and for each `out` feature the requirement it threatens (through its
   sensitivity) and whether the paired batch run confirms a failure.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-GROUND-08, second clause: the as-built variant has a `ModelId` different from the as-designed
      model, and a verdict of the as-designed model is not listed for it.
- [ ] A planted link-length error of +0.2 mm becomes the variant's length parameter within its uncertainty;
      an `unknown` parallelism updates no parameter and is listed as a gap.
- [ ] Effective density: nominal volume 120 cm³, card density 1.24 g/cm³ (nominal mass 148.8 g), measured mass
      104.2 g gives ρ_eff = 0.868 g/cm³ (70% of solid), and the variant's mass is 104.2 g.
- [ ] The as-designed and as-built robots run in one batch over the same world addresses, and the result
      prints both verdicts and the paired differences; on a fixture where the built link is long enough to
      miss a clearance requirement, the as-built verdict fails while the as-designed passes.
- [ ] Tolerance audit on the allocation fixture (sensitivities 1 and 4, tolerances 16.0 µm and 6.35 µm, budget
      √B = 10 µm, k = 3): measured deviations 12 µm and 5 µm with negligible uncertainty are both `in` and use
      (4² + 16·1.667²)/100 = 0.604 of the budget; a deviation of 8 µm on the second feature is `out`.
- [ ] A derived inertia that violates the consistency guard is not written.

`audit_in_out_undecided` (|δ| = 15 µm, u = 0.5 µm, k_s = 2.5, t = 16 µm: 16.25 > 16 gives `undecided`);
`audit_budget_use_0p604`; `audit_not_resolved_below_noise_screen`; `variant_provenance_fields`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_asbuilt.sh` (bead N30a-48): the synthetic link's scan and scale reading through
`frob ground ingest --emit-variant`, then a paired task verdict. Logged: each updated parameter with nominal,
measured and uncertainty; mass, effective density, centre of mass and inertia with provenance; both
`ModelId`s; both verdicts and paired differences; the audit table with verdicts and budget used.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-733 (the as-built registration and the 0.3 mm wall deviation recovered inside stated uncertainty) feeds
this bead's inputs; the worked cases above are its own. Negative controls: a variant written under the
as-designed `ModelId` (the identity test fails, and the gate's second clause with it); mass properties
computed from the nominal card density when a scale reading exists (the density test fails); an audit that
ignores measurement uncertainty and calls 15 µm against 16 µm `in`. Determinism: parameter mapping and audit
are exact given their inputs; the paired run has the determinism class of its tier; twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- Kill gate KILL-ASBUILT (decided at M5; trigger: the as-built gate fails): scans are not ingested, and
  as-built data enters as scalar bench measurements (mass, lengths), each a direct measurement of one card
  field. Steps 2 (with a scale reading alone), 3, 4 and 6 still work from scalar inputs.
- Uniform effective density is an assumption a printed part with sparse infill violates: its inertia about
  some axes is higher than a uniform body's. The provenance label says so; the pendulum is the remedy.
- A tolerance's sensitivity may come from an exact kinematic derivative (class Certified in exact arithmetic)
  or from a simulated sensitivity (class Estimated); the audit inherits the weaker class of what it uses.
- A zero first-order sensitivity is not insensitivity (a quantity quadratic in the deviation); such features
  are routed by the allocation to a band-extreme check, and the audit reports them through the paired batch
  run, not through s_i.
