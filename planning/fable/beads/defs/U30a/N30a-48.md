## Background

Ground (crate `frob-ground`) treats a built part as one more representation of the design. A scan with
declared fiducials is admitted like any dataset; the sibling's closed-form registration aligns it to the
design and returns a pose covariance; deviation, thickness and form fields are computed from surface
correspondences; pose covariance is carried into link length, axis offset and parallelism; geometry deltas and
a scale reading become parameters and mass properties of an as-built model variant with its own `ModelId`; and
each measured feature is compared with its allocated tolerance. The as-designed and as-built robots then run
in one batch.

**GATE-GROUND-08** (claim kind: known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): the as-built known answers of the
rehearsal (a planted rotation and translation, and a 0.3 mm wall deviation, recovered inside the stated
uncertainty; two fiducials refused; a scale of 25.4 raising unit suspicion), and the as-built variant has a
new `ModelId`. It feeds the kill gate **KILL-ASBUILT**: if it fails, scans are not ingested, and as-built data
enters as scalar bench measurements (mass, lengths), each a direct measurement of one card field.

No part is built or scanned: the scan is a synthetic point cloud of one link, generated from the design with a
planted pose, planted deviations and scanner noise, and labelled `synthetic`.

## Scope

In: the script, its synthetic scan generator, the assertions for each step of the loop, the refusal controls,
the log and the gate record. Out: the mechanisms (N30a-45 to N30a-47); the kill decision.

## Technical approach

Script `scripts/e2e/ground_asbuilt.sh [--seed S] [--draws 200] [--controls]`, plain shell over `frob`, the
rehearsal generator and the conformance crate's test targets (the crate has no binary, BD-025; an earlier
text called a "conformance binary"), under `target/e2e-work/ground_asbuilt/`; default seed 9. Every `run`
and `mutate` call carries its own key (KNOWN_DRIFTS 5), distinct per draw.

1. **Fixture.** Fixture R-13 of the rehearsal catalogue (BD-388) with its registered extensions (step 11): a
   link of the rehearsal's cart with two bores (joint axes 150 mm apart), a wall of nominal 2.5 mm, four
   fiducials declared in a build packet exported by `frob foundry export` (BD-408, on which N30a-45 and
   N30a-47 depend, so the real export exists when this script runs; the script asserts the packet's
   fiducial-list hash), and an allocated tolerance list with sensitivities. The generator is BD-388's
   rehearsal generator, `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example ground_rehearsal -- gen --fixture R-13
   --seed S --out <dir>` (an earlier text named a `ground-synth` command, which no bead declares); it
   samples 200,000 surface points, applies: a wall deviation of +0.3 mm over one
   patch of one face; a link-length error of +0.2 mm; an axis tilt of 0.1°; R-13's rigid pose (rotation 2°
   about the axis (1, 2, 2)/3, translation (3.0, −1.5, 0.8) mm); scanner noise σ = 0.05 mm per point and
   σ = 0.02 mm per fiducial; and writes a scan dataset (`kind = "scan"`, origin `synthetic`, length unit mm)
   plus a bench dataset with a scale reading of 104.2 g. The planted values go to a side file the verbs never
   read.
2. **Admit and register.** The bench dataset first, `frob ground ingest bench.frun --design <cart design>
   --task-family tf:none --key ga-bn-<S>-<d> --json` (the scale reading the variant's mass properties read,
   N30a-47 step 2), then the scan, `frob ground ingest scan.frun --design <cart design> --task-family tf:none
   --emit-variant --key ga-in-<S>-<d> --json` (`--emit-variant` and the `variant` output are on
   `ground.ingest`'s registry row, N30a-47). Assert: exit 0;
   the registration artifact's rotation and translation are within 3 reported standard deviations of the
   planted pose, component by component; the similarity scale is consistent with 1; the residual is printed
   as a fit diagnostic and passes the noise screen for a deviation under review of 0.3 mm.
3. **Coverage of the stated uncertainty over draws.** Repeat steps 1 and 2 for D = 200 noise draws (seeds
   derived by address). For each of: the six pose components, the wall deviation, the wall thickness, the
   link length and the axis tilt, count the draws in which the planted value lies within 3 stated standard
   deviations. Assert each count is at least 0.97·D (expected 0.997; "recovered inside the stated
   uncertainty" made measurable, DESIGN), and that the normalized errors' variance is between 0.7 and 1.3.
4. **Fields and quantities.** From the fields artifact: the planted face's mean deviation is 0.300 mm within
   its uncertainty and other faces' are zero within theirs; thickness is nominal + 0.3 mm on that wall; the
   link length is nominal + 0.2 mm; the tilt is 0.1°; the length–tilt correlation is non-zero; a second link
   with no planted tilt reports its parallelism `unknown` (the sigma-point downgrade).
5. **Variant and identity.** The envelope's `variant` names the as-built variant. Assert: its `ModelId`
   differs from the as-designed model's; its length parameter equals the measured value; its mass is 104.2 g
   with effective density 0.868 g/cm³; provenance on each changed field reads `as-built`; and, after step 6,
   `frob result show <as-built verdict> --json` gives a claim product naming the variant's `ModelId` and
   inputs that cite no verdict of the as-designed model (`result.revalidate` needs a change, an edit or a
   version pair, and is not the form for this check).
6. **One batch.** A task verdict on the clearance fixture with both variants over the same world addresses:
   both verdicts are printed with paired differences; with the planted +0.2 mm both pass; with a second scan
   planted at +1.5 mm the as-built unit fails and the as-designed one passes.
7. **Tolerance audit.** The audit artifact lists every toleranced feature with `in`, `out`, `undecided` or
   `not-resolved`; the planted features have the expected verdicts; the budget used is printed.
8. **Refusal controls of the gate.** A scan with two fiducials: `FROB-REF-812`, repair "fiducials needed". A
   scan whose coordinates were exported in inches (scale 25.4 against the design): unit suspicion raised and
   812 with the observed scale. Also: three collinear fiducials (812); a pre-registered scan with no tool
   identity (812); a deviation under review of 0.01 mm against a residual of 0.03 mm ("below the noise
   screen", no deviation reported).
9. **Controls (`--controls`).** Builds on which the script must exit non-zero: a registration that reports
   the residual as the pose uncertainty (step 3's counts collapse); a unit check that rescales silently; a
   variant written under the as-designed `ModelId`; deviations reported without the pose term.
10. **Determinism.** Steps 2 to 7 twice with one seed: identical artifacts; in-process and worker airlock
    modes with equal digests.
11. **Folded from the superseded bead N30b-35.** The base fixture is R-13 of the rehearsal catalogue (BD-388):
    one link with four fiducials; planted rotation 2° about (1, 2, 2)/3 and translation (3.0, −1.5, 0.8) mm;
    fiducial noise 0.02 mm; a wall of nominal 2.5 mm with a planted +0.3 mm deviation over one patch; a
    two-fiducial copy and a copy scaled by 25.4. Step 1 uses R-13's pose, fiducials and wall; the further
    planted values of step 1 (the two-bore link geometry, the link-length error, the axis tilt, the scale
    reading, the second scan at +1.5 mm) are registered with the generator as extensions of R-13.
    A second, joint reading of "inside the stated uncertainty" is asserted beside step 3's per-quantity counts.
    It is the reading registered with the pilot (N30b-16): the Mahalanobis distance of the planted pose from
    the estimate under the stated 6 × 6 covariance is at most 12.59 (the 95% quantile of χ² with six degrees
    of freedom) in at least 180 of 200 noise seeds, and the patch-mean deviation's 95% interval contains
    0.3 mm in at least 180 of 200. It is measured by the gate's run in the conformance crate's known-answer
    tier, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<S> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features
    gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-GROUND-08` (the one form that crate has; an earlier text called a
    `frob-conformance run` binary, which the `no_conformance_command` lint refuses), which runs KA-733's
    runner entry over the 200 seeds with its two controls and the variant clause (a new `ModelId` for the
    as-built variant), runs every control registered for the gate in the same run (KNOWN_DRIFTS 1; with
    CX-G-03's and CX-G-04's plants `N30a-45:cx-<nnn>`, each of which the receipt must show `caught`,
    N38b-19), and writes the hash-named receipt the gate record cites; from it
    `e2e_metric pose_covered <count> seeds >= 180` and
    `e2e_metric deviation_covered <count> seeds >= 180`, both `--gate GATE-GROUND-08`, then
    `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-08`, which declares the gate touched and records nothing. Both readings must hold.
    The residual is reported under the name `registration_residual` and nowhere as an uncertainty. On a
    variant with a 0.02 mm planted deviation no deviation is reported and the output states the resolvable
    size. A query for the as-designed identity's fidelity records returns none for the as-built identity. The
    audit lists a feature without allocation as `not-allocated`. Plant switches: with `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE` set
    to `residual-as-uncertainty`, `two-fiducials-ok`, `silent-rescale` or `variant-reuses-id` the script exits
    non-zero and names the step. Metamorphic relation checked in the script: applying a known rigid motion to
    the scan bundle composes with the recovered transform and leaves the deviation field unchanged within
    noise. If KILL-ASBUILT has fired, the script asserts the fallback: the scan is refused, and a bench bundle
    with a mass and two lengths produces a variant with a new identity.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The planted rotation, translation and 0.3 mm wall deviation are recovered inside the stated uncertainty
      (step 2 on the base draw; at least 97% of 200 draws per quantity in step 3).
- [ ] Two fiducials are refused; a scale of 25.4 raises unit suspicion and is refused.
- [ ] The as-built variant has a new `ModelId` and inherits no result of the as-designed model, in the gate
      run and through the verb (`--emit-variant`).
- [ ] The paired batch prints both verdicts; the +1.5 mm case separates them.
- [ ] The tolerance audit and the remaining refusals behave as stated.
- [ ] Each control build makes the script exit non-zero; two runs agree.
- [ ] The script writes no gate record (KNOWN_DRIFTS 9). This bead is the gate's one recorder: from the
      receipt of the gate run of step 11, passing or not, a session that did not implement the measured beads
      enters GATE-GROUND-08 with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-GROUND-08
      --receipt <hash>`, holding the per-quantity coverage counts, the two refusal outcomes, both `ModelId`s,
      the seed, the commit and the machine; it is what the KILL-ASBUILT decision reads. A failing value is
      recorded `fail` (with every control `caught`), and this bead then closes `recorded` on that record
      (N41-17 rule 2; KILL-ASBUILT's trigger names no repair cycle), which is what makes the KILL-ASBUILT
      decision bead N30b-20 ready.
- [ ] The joint reading holds: the planted pose inside the stated 95% region in at least 180 of 200 seeds, and
      the deviation interval containing 0.3 mm in at least 180 of 200.
- [ ] Each plant switch makes the script exit non-zero and name the step; every envelope has
      `evidence.reality = "synthetic"`.
- [ ] Row KA-733 of the Ground known-answer registry (N30-KA) is bound by this bead: it adds the runner entry
      `ka:KA-733@host` (N38a-13; the R-13 registration and wall deviation with the two-fiducial and 25.4-scale
      controls) and names, in the row's derivation front matter, the test that checks it (crate, path and
      name). The known-answer run reports the row `pass` with both controls caught; it is logged as an
      `assert` line with its KA number.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own. The scan generator has `planted_pose_roundtrip` (generate, inverse-transform, compare with
the nominal surface to 10⁻⁹ before noise) and `inch_export_is_exactly_25_4`.
Folded from N30b-35: `crates/frob-conformance/tests/ground_batteries_asbuilt.rs`: `chi2_6dof_95_is_12_59`;
`mahalanobis_of_planted_pose_noise_free_is_zero`; `coverage_rule_180_of_200`; `scaled_fixture_scale_is_25_4`.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Log `target/e2e-logs/ground_asbuilt/<utc>.ndjson` through the helper of `STD-E2E`,
with a transcript. Per command a `cmd` line (exact command, exit code, wall time, evidence block: claim,
class `Estimated`, tier, determinism, scan, packet, design and model identities, seed; `reality: synthetic`
or `no-claim`). `metric` lines: each recovered quantity with its planted value, estimate, stated standard
deviation and normalized error; coverage counts over the draws against 0.97·D; mass and effective density;
both `ModelId`s; both verdicts with paired differences; the audit's verdict counts and budget used.
`refusal` lines for step 8 with code, observed values (fiducial count, scale, residual against deviation)
and first repair. On failure: the first quantity outside its uncertainty, with the scan and artifact paths.
Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed, gate touched (GATE-GROUND-08).

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-733 (a planted rotation and translation; a 0.3 mm wall deviation; recovered inside the stated uncertainty;
controls: two fiducials refused; scale 25.4 raises unit suspicion). Negative controls: the four builds of
step 9. Metamorphic: applying a second known rigid motion to the scan changes R and t by exactly that motion

twice on one machine; debug and release; both development machines inside double-precision tolerance.
Folded from N30b-35: KA-733; cases CX-G-03 and CX-G-04 of the chapter's counterexample registry.

## Considerations

- "Inside the stated uncertainty" is made a counted statement over 200 draws so that the gate can fail for
  an over-confident covariance and not only for a wrong mean. The 97% line and the variance window are DESIGN
  and are recorded with the gate.
- The packet is the manufacturing subsystem's own export, never a hand-written stand-in: BD-408 precedes this
  script through N30a-45 and N30a-47, and the fiducial-id check against the packet is made at registration
  (N30a-45).
- Upstream work item `FD-FSIM-25` (a correspondence-search lane in the sibling) has as its switch-over test a
  re-run of these known answers without fiducials; the script has a `--no-fiducials` mode reserved for it,
  which today must end in refusal 812.
- All data is synthetic. A passing gate shows the as-built loop is sound on generated clouds; it says nothing
  about any scanner.
- The single-case assertion of step 2 can fail by chance on a few per cent of seeds, so the gate rests on the
  counted statements and step 2 uses the registered seed.
