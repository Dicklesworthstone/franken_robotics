## Background

Ground is the subsystem that admits measurements of real hardware and decides what, if anything, a
simulated result may claim about reality. A real dataset is an episode bundle (`.frun`) whose manifest has
schema `frob.ground.dataset/1`. The manifest as specified holds six blocks: `[dataset]` (identity, origin,
kind, licence, acquisition and rights), `[rig]` (units by serial, controller, firmware, surface, envelope of
conditions), `[[instrument]]` (name, quantity, unit, `sigma`, `bias_bound`, the instant its timestamp
denotes, its clock, its calibration identity), `[[clock]]` (name, nominal rate, a list of mark names) and
`[split]`. Its admission bead implements exactly that.

Four components of the same subsystem were specified against data the manifest does not carry:

| Component | What the plan requires of it | What it must read from the manifest |
|---|---|---|
| Clock fitting and its two misuse guards | The clock is never fitted to the event being scored; command-to-response correlation is not a clock fit; without a shared hardware mark the result is "delay plus offset"; if skew can change during the trial the fit is piecewise affine over more marks | For every mark: what kind of thing it is, which clocks saw it, and its timing error σ_m; for every clock, whether it is declared piecewise and where its knots are |
| Deterministic retirement triggers | A calibration retires at once when a part is replaced or re-lubricated, and expires by age or operating hours (default 90 days or 200 operating hours, whichever comes first, and always on part replacement) | A maintenance log per unit and an operating-hours counter per unit |
| As-built scans | A scan is admitted with fiducial correspondences, or registered by an identified external tool whose identity is recorded as provenance; a scale near 25.4 or 0.001 is a unit error | The part scanned, the scan's length unit, the fiducials found, or the external registration with the tool's identity and stated uncertainty |
| Instruments as measurement models | Per-sample noise and a shared calibration error form the covariance R | Optionally the quantization step and, where known, the standard deviation of the bias |

Each of those beads declared the fields it needs as its own DESIGN note. A manifest is one hashed document
with one validator: if four beads each add fields in their own module, the schema that is hashed, the schema
that is published and the schema a replication kit carries can differ. This bead is the single definition
of the added fields. Tier: Solid (schema, validation and admission rules; the consumers are Frontier).

The format rule that governs the change: a schema name carries a major version; adding optional fields
keeps it (a minor version); a reader refuses an unknown major version or an unknown required field and
never parses on a best-effort basis. All additions here are optional at the level of the schema, so the
name stays `frob.ground.dataset/1` and the minor number becomes 1. Whether a field is *required for a use*
is an admission rule, stated below per field.

Rejected alternatives: a side file per component next to the manifest (not covered by the dataset hash, so
a maintenance entry could be added or removed after sealing); a new major version (strands the rehearsal
datasets already written); free-form `extra` tables (a field nobody validates is a field nobody can trust).

## Scope

In: the added blocks and fields, their types, units and validation; which uses require which fields and the
refusal when one is missing; canonical form and hashing of the additions; the reader's behaviour on minor 0
manifests; fixtures; the published schema.

Out: the base manifest, identity, cell key, splits and sealing (BD-387); what the consumers compute from
the fields (N30a-11 the piecewise clock fit, N30a-13 clock guards, N30a-40 triggers, N30a-45 registration,
N30a-08 covariance); the expiry
limits and their owner decision (the OPEN-GROUND-3 decision bead); the build packet's fiducial list (the
packet bead of the manufacturing subsystem).

## Technical approach

Layout (DESIGN): `crates/frob-ground/src/manifest/{marks.rs, maintenance.rs, scan.rs, instrument_ext.rs}`
beside the base parser; schema file `registries/schemas/frob.ground.dataset.1.json` gains the definitions.

**1. Marks.** The base form `marks = ["pulse-0", "pulse-1"]` on a clock stays valid. Each mark may be
described once, at top level:

```toml
[[mark]]
id      = "pulse-0"
kind    = "hardware-pulse"        # "hardware-pulse" | "sync-edge" | "protocol-sync"
clocks  = ["mcu", "logger"]       # every clock that timestamped this mark; must list it in its `marks`
sigma_s = 5.0e-5                  # timing error of the mark on each clock, seconds (σ_m)
```

Rules: the kind list is closed; a task event (a contact, a threshold crossing, the start of motion) is not
a kind, and a `[[mark]]` whose `kind` is anything else is refused. A mark named by a clock with no
`[[mark]]` entry is `unclassified`: it is kept and hashed, it does not count toward the m ≥ 2 marks a clock
fit needs, and it never makes two clocks "share a hardware mark". `protocol-sync` requires `sigma_s`
(its error is usually far larger than a pulse's). A mark is *shared* by two clocks when both are in its
`clocks`.

**1b. Clock model** (two optional fields of each `[[clock]]` entry, inside the hashed manifest; the clock-fit
bead N30a-11 reads them and this bead is their one definition):

```toml
[[clock]]
name = "mcu"; nominal_hz = 1000; marks = ["pulse-0", "pulse-1", "pulse-2"]
model = "piecewise"               # "affine" | "piecewise"; default "affine"
knots = [41250000]                # raw source timestamps, in the clock's ticks; only with "piecewise"
```

Rules: `model` is one of the two words (default `affine` when absent); `knots` is allowed only with
`model = "piecewise"` and, when present, is a strictly increasing list of integers inside the span of the
clock's marks; without `knots` a piecewise clock takes its interior marks as knots (N30a-11). A clock
declared `piecewise` skips the affine attempt. Canonical form: `knots` in ascending order as written.

**2. Maintenance and operating hours.**

```toml
[[rig.maintenance]]
unit = "unit-07"; part = "knee-L gearbox"; action = "replaced"; on = "2027-04-02"   # "replaced" | "re-lubricated"
[rig.operating_hours]
"unit-07" = 412.5                 # the unit's counter at acquisition, hours; non-decreasing across datasets
[rig.trials]
attempted = 64; counter_source = "operator-log"     # the operator's trial counter that `attempts = "all"` is checked against
```

Rules: `unit` must be in `rig.units`; `on` is a date not after `dataset.acquired.on`; an empty log is
written explicitly as `maintenance = []` under `[rig]` (absence and "nothing was done" are different
statements). `rig.trials.attempted` commits, inside the hashed manifest, the value of the operator's trial
counter that ships with the bundle and against which base admission already checks `attempts = "all"`; the
two numbers must be equal, and the number of episode rows must equal both, or admission is refused. Hours are optional: without them the hours limit is `unknown`, the age limit alone applies and
the finding `hours-unknown` is recorded. A counter lower than the same unit's counter in an earlier admitted
dataset is refused.

**3. Scan block** (only for `dataset.kind = "scan"`).

```toml
[scan]
part = "thigh-L"; unit_serial = "unit-07"; length_unit = "mm"; packet = "b3:…"     # build packet hash
[[fiducial]]
id = "F1"; measured = [12.004, 80.117, 3.250]; sigma = 0.02                          # in `length_unit`
[registration]                    # route B only: an identified external tool
tool = "name"; version = "v"; digest = "b3:…"; transform = [ … 12 numbers, row-major 3×4 … ]
stated_sigma = { rotation_rad = 2.0e-4, translation = 0.03 }
```

Rules: `length_unit` is required and is one of `m`, `mm`, `in` (it is never inferred); exactly one route is
present: at least one `[[fiducial]]`, or `[registration]`; `packet` names a build-packet hash and fiducial
`id`s are unique within the scan; `transform` must be a rigid transform to 10⁻⁹ (orthonormal rotation,
determinant +1); `[registration]` without `digest` or without `stated_sigma` is refused. The check that each
fiducial `id` exists in the packet's fiducial list for `part` reads the build packet (BD-408, the
manufacturing subsystem, late in M5) and is made at registration by N30a-45, which depends on it, so that this
validator, which the clock and drift beads also need, does not wait on the manufacturing chain.

**4. Instrument additions.** `resolution` (optional; the quantization step, in the instrument's unit, > 0)
and `bias_sigma` (optional; the standard deviation of the shared calibration error). If both `bias_sigma`
and `bias_bound` are present, `bias_sigma ≤ bias_bound`. `bias_bound` stays required.

**5. Requirement by use** (the admission rule for a dataset that lacks a field):

| Use | Fields required | Missing → |
|---|---|---|
| Any admission | The base fields | FROB-REF-873 (a dataset lacking licence, provenance, rights or measurement uncertainty), unchanged |
| A dataset whose declared cell overlaps a calibrated cell in design and units | `rig.maintenance` (possibly `[]`) and `rig.trials` | FROB-REF-873, the missing fields named (provenance of the rig's condition) |
| A clock fit used in scoring | At least two classified marks that bracket the trial, per fitted clock (K + 1 for a clock declared `piecewise` with K segments, N30a-11) | FROB-REF-876 (clock alignment under-determined), listing the marks needed |
| A result of type `Delay` (not "delay plus offset") | As many marks of kind `hardware-pulse` or `sync-edge` as the clock model needs (two for an affine fit), each shared by the command clock and the response clock, or a chain of clocks so linked (the clock graph of N30a-13) | No refusal: the result type is `DelayPlusOffset` and the card's delay is not updated |
| Admission of a scan | The scan block with one route | FROB-REF-812 (an as-built registration that is ill-posed or below its noise screen), repair: fiducials needed |

Codes of the other rules (DESIGN, so that each refusal test asserts a code): a failure of a shape rule of
steps 1, 1b, 2 and 4 (a word outside a closed list, as a task-event mark kind or an unknown `model`; `knots`
without `piecewise`, out of order or outside the marks' span; a maintenance `unit` not in `rig.units`;
`resolution ≤ 0`; an unknown table or field) is BD-387's schema refusal `FROB-REF-802`, its pointer the
offending field; a rule that compares values (a maintenance `on` after `dataset.acquired.on`; `bias_sigma >
bias_bound`; an hours counter lower than an earlier admitted dataset's; `rig.trials.attempted` against the
counter and the episode rows) is `FROB-REF-873`, naming the fields; every rule of the scan block (step 3) is
812.

**6. Canonical form and identity.** The added tables enter the canonical manifest bytes in a fixed order
(`mark` by id, `rig.maintenance` by (unit, on, part), `fiducial` by id), so `dataset.id` covers them. A
maintenance entry added after admission is therefore a different dataset, and sealed commitments made
against the old identity do not transfer.

**7. Reading minor 0.** A manifest with none of the additions is read as before; every mark is
`unclassified`, hours are `unknown`, and the uses of step 5 that need more are refused or downgraded as
stated. Nothing is defaulted to a favourable value.

**8. Surface.** `frob ground ingest` reports under `result.manifest`: `minor`, counts of classified marks
per kind, `maintenance_entries`, `hours` per unit or `unknown`, and for scans the route. `frob schema
frob.ground.dataset/1` prints the schema with the additions. Determinism D0; admission creates no claim.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four consumer beads' fixtures parse with this one validator, and the published schema contains
      every field they read: mark `kind`, `clocks`, `sigma_s`; clock `model` and `knots`; `rig.maintenance`,
      `rig.operating_hours`, `rig.trials`; `[scan]`, `[[fiducial]]`, `[registration]`; `resolution`,
      `bias_sigma`.
- [ ] `knots` without `model = "piecewise"`, a non-increasing `knots` list, a knot outside the span of the
      clock's marks, and an unknown `model` word are each refused naming the field; a clock with no `model`
      reads as `affine`; two manifests that differ only in one knot have different `dataset.id`.
- [ ] A `[[mark]]` with `kind = "contact-made"` is refused at admission; a bare mark name is accepted as
      `unclassified` and does not count toward a fit.
- [ ] Two datasets that differ only by one maintenance entry have different `dataset.id`.
- [ ] A dataset for a calibrated cell without `rig.maintenance` is refused with FROB-REF-873 naming the
      field; with `maintenance = []` it is admitted.
- [ ] A decreasing operating-hours counter for a unit is refused; a missing one yields `hours-unknown`.
- [ ] `rig.trials.attempted = 64` against an operator trial counter of 64 and 64 episode rows is admitted; a
      counter of 65, or 63 episode rows, is refused naming the two numbers that differ.
- [ ] A scan with no `length_unit`, with both routes, with a duplicated fiducial id or no packet hash, or with
      a non-rigid `transform` is refused with FROB-REF-812 and the offending field (an id absent from the
      packet is refused at registration, N30a-45).
- [ ] `bias_sigma > bias_bound` is refused; `resolution ≤ 0` is refused.
- [ ] A minor 0 manifest (the base example) is admitted unchanged and yields the same `dataset.id` as
      before this bead.
- [ ] The reader refuses an unknown top-level table and an unknown field inside a known table.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-ground --test manifest_minor1` (the real parser and validator on fixture manifests):

- `base_example_id_unchanged`: the base manifest's identity equals the golden value recorded from BD-387.
- `mark_kinds_closed`; `task_event_mark_refused`; `unclassified_mark_not_counted` (two bare marks → a
  scoring fit is refused with FROB-REF-876 and lists "two classified marks bracketing the trial").
- `shared_mark_detection`: a `hardware-pulse` with `clocks = ["cmd", "enc"]` makes the pair shared; two
  marks each seen by one clock do not.
- `protocol_sync_needs_sigma`.
- `clock_model_defaults_affine`; `knots_only_with_piecewise`; `knots_strictly_increasing_inside_marks`
  (knots [5, 3] refused; a knot after the last mark refused); `knots_change_identity`.
- `maintenance_changes_identity`; `maintenance_unit_must_exist`; `maintenance_date_not_after_acquisition`;
  `explicit_empty_log_differs_from_absent`.
- `hours_monotone_per_unit`: 412.5 h then 400.0 h for `unit-07` → refused; `hours_absent_is_unknown`.
- `trials_attempted_matches_counter_and_rows` (64, 64, 64 admitted; 64 against a counter of 65, and 64 against
  63 rows, refused with both numbers in the payload).
- `scan_requires_length_unit`; `scan_exactly_one_route`; `fiducial_ids_unique_and_packet_named`;
  `transform_must_be_rigid` (a rotation scaled by 1.001 is refused); `registration_needs_digest_and_sigma`.
- `bias_sigma_le_bound`; `resolution_positive`.
- `canonical_order_independent_of_file_order`: shuffled tables give the same bytes and the same id.
- `unknown_field_refused`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The clock facts the mark fields serve: with σ_m = 50 µs and two marks 10 s apart the skew uncertainty is
  √2·σ_m/(t₂ − t₁) = 7.1 ppm; a delay of 8 ms with an offset of −8 ms and no shared mark is reported as
  "delay plus offset = 0".
- The expiry defaults the hours field serves: 90 days or 200 operating hours, whichever comes first.
- Unit suspicion the `length_unit` field serves: a fitted scale near 25.4 or 0.001 is a unit error.
- Negative controls (each must fail a test): a validator that accepts any string as a mark kind; a
  canonical form that omits `rig.maintenance` (identity unchanged by an added entry); a reader that infers
  `length_unit` from the cloud's extent; a reader that ignores unknown fields.
- Determinism: canonical bytes and ids equal on Linux and macOS, debug and release.

## Considerations

- An undeclared part replacement cannot be detected from the manifest; the statistical drift monitors are
  the backstop and are weak at small n. Requiring the log for calibrated cells makes the omission a false
  statement by the operator instead of a silent gap.
- A replication kit carries manifests; private datasets never enter a kit, and the added fields change
  nothing about that rule.
- If the kill gate on clock fitting fires, no delay is ever reported; the mark kinds remain useful as
  guards.
- Should a later consumer need a required field, that is a major version with a registered migration.
