## Background

Identification is the step of Ground's procedure that turns an admitted log into estimates of the parameters
the simulator reads from its cards. Ground is the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality
(crate `frob-ground`, layer 5); it reads files and writes files and never touches a device.

**Why actuators first.** Recent sim-to-real studies agree on where the gap lives. Identifying armature, joint
friction and delay gave 46% more travel distance and 75% less rotational deviation in one 2026 study
(https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10351); static friction is commonly missing from randomization; joint friction and
actuator delay were called particularly critical for a point-foot biped (FACT, source register). Simulation
agrees in kind: in a planar threshold-crossing pilot, changing one winding feed-forward term from R·i_ref to R·i
changed 24 of 156 outcome labels, and success fractions of 0.093 and 0.780 at a 100 Hz loop with no rotor
inertia became 1.000 and 1.000 at 1 kHz or with rotor inertia added (FACT). A contact parameter fitted before
the actuator is right absorbs the actuator's error and then predicts the wrong thing on the next surface.

Decisions that govern this bead. **ADR-GROUND-03:** the identification order is fixed: (0) clocks, (1)
actuators, (2) contact and surface, (3) inertia and geometry, (4) sensors, because the literature places the gap
in the actuator and each stage's error aliases into the next. **ADR-GROUND-04:** identifiability is analysed
before any fit, and a fit on an unidentifiable combination is refused, never regularized quietly, because a
regularized fit returns the prior and labels it a measurement. **ADR-GROUND-08:** sibling estimators are reached
through the airlock (the one crate, `frob-fsim`, through which FrankenSim is consumed) as whole jobs, and every
Ground output has evidence class Estimated, because the siblings expose estimated candidates only and require
an external authenticated authority for more.

What the sibling provides (FACT, FrankenSim at commit `483d09c682`, `crates/fs-assimilate`): an iterated
Gauss–Newton Kalman update with Armijo line search, a correlated-noise variant and a Huber-loss fit
(`nonlinear::iterated`; at most 256 rows, 256 iterations, 65,536 evaluations); shared calibration error as a
declared common-mode source so that repeated samples cannot push uncertainty below the common-mode floor
(`groups`; at most 64 shared parameters). It has no notion of joints, actuators or trajectories, no regressor
construction and no delay estimation: those are this crate's.

Tier: Frontier. Pilot: N30b-15. Kill gate KILL-GROUND, named in advance: if GATE-GROUND-04 fails after one repair
cycle there is no identification; a card field then changes only by direct bench measurement of that field.

## Scope

This bead is the capstone of identification and card updates: it owns the verb `ground.identify`, runs the
parts in order and closes GATE-GROUND-04. The parts are built in their own beads, which are dependencies of
this one:

- staging: the fixed order of stages, the per-joint L1 model and stage gating: N30a-20;
- pass A (band-limited linear least squares): N30a-21; pass B (output error on the referee over windowed
  features): N30a-22; pass C (the delay profile and the stick–slip ensemble): N30a-23;
- the calibration record, residual autocorrelation and the energy cross-check: N30a-24;
- stages 2 to 4 (contact and surface, inertia and geometry, sensors): N30a-25;
- card updates from logs: the new candidate card version with its covariance (N30a-27). The later steps of a
  card update happen at scoring time or after it and are related beads, not dependencies: admission and the
  width interface (N30a-28), the discrepancy floor (N30a-29, computed only by scoring), and unit and class
  cards (N30a-30, updated from admitted units). Identification writes candidates only, with the status
  `Candidate` and the floor `not-estimated` of N30a-24's record.

In this bead: the verb, its arguments, envelope and job behaviour; the pre-checks before any fit (clock fits
present, the identifiability report of BD-390 consulted, the fit guard); the sequencing of the passes and the
hand-offs between them; the windowing contract as the verb exposes it; the definition of the 100-rig battery
that the gate script runs. The Technical approach below states the integrated behaviour the parts must show
together; each part's own bead is where its internals are specified and built.

Out: the identifiability analysis itself (BD-390); scoring and admission at scoring time (N30b-06); the gate
script (`scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh`, N30a-26, which depends on this bead); the kill decision (N30b-17).

## Technical approach

1. **Model, per joint at actuator level L1.**
   (I(q) + J_a)·q̈ + b·q̇ + τ_f(q̇) + g(q) = N·η·k_t·i(t − d),
   τ_f(q̇) = [τ_c + (τ_s − τ_c)·exp(−(q̇/v_s)²)]·sgn(q̇) while moving,
   with the joint stiction row holding q̇ = 0 while the applied torque is inside ±τ_s. Symbols: q joint angle;
   I(q) link inertia about the joint axis; J_a armature (rotor inertia reflected through the gear, N²·J_r); b
   viscous coefficient; τ_c Coulomb torque; τ_s static (breakaway) torque; v_s Stribeck speed; g(q) gravity
   torque; N gear ratio; η efficiency; k_t torque constant; i winding current; d actuator delay. The first-order
   lag has time constant τ_lag. Parameter vector θ = (J_a, b, τ_c, τ_s, v_s, d, τ_lag, k_t scale). The
   exponential is the card's Stribeck shape `gaussian`; a card whose `stribeck_shape` is `lorentzian` (the
   default on batched tiers) has 1/(1 + (q̇/v_s)²) in its place. The shape is a card field, not a fitted
   parameter: the referee evaluates whichever the bound card states, and the record names it (N30a-20).
2. **Order inside stage 1.** Joint by joint, unloaded on a stand where possible: delay and lag; static, Coulomb
   and viscous friction with the Stribeck speed; armature; the scale of the torque constant; then the
   torque–speed envelope and the thermal constants of level L2.
3. **Verb.** `frob ground identify <dataset> [--stage actuators|contact|inertia|sensors] [--params <names>]
   [--combination] [--keep-prior <stage>] [--law-class <class>] [--joints …] [--windows <def | raw>]
   [--cell <cell>] --key <k> --json`;
   kind `run` (the idempotency key `--key`, without which the call is FROB-REF-805; budget; bounded
   synchronous wait, otherwise a job handle with exit 8); capability
   `ground:run`, the scope the verb registry gives it; schemas `frob.ground.identify.in/1` and `.out/1`.
   Arguments, as the parts use them (the synopsis is the union, so that no part's flag is missing from the
   schema): the dataset is positional and names its own cell, so `--cell` is an optional cross-check that
   must equal it; without `--stage` the verb runs the earliest stage of the fixed order that has no record
   for this cell and lineage (DESIGN: the registry's example `frob ground identify ds:stand-A-01 --key gi-1
   --json` names none); `--params` restricts the fit to named parameters of the stage, with the names the
   identifiability report of `ground.excite` prints, so that a plan's list can be passed on unchanged (for
   stage 1, DESIGN: `armature`, `viscous`, `coulomb`, `static`, `stribeck_speed`, `delay`, `lag`, `kt_scale`
   for J_a, b, τ_c, τ_s, v_s, d, τ_lag and the k_t scale; the card writer N30a-27 maps them to card fields);
   `--combination` asks for the identified combination of an aliased set in place of its members (BD-390);
   `--keep-prior <stage>` declares an earlier stage kept at its prior (N30a-20); `--law-class <class>` restricts
   the fit to a law class of N30a-18's `registries/ground/omitted_laws.toml` (for example `coulomb-viscous`:
   Coulomb plus viscous friction, τ_s = τ_c, no Stribeck term, so τ_s and v_s leave θ), a class that registry
   does not list is refused with FROB-REF-802 before any fit, without the option the fit uses the stage's full
   law class, and the class fitted is named in the calibration record's `conditional_on` (N30a-18's spelling);
   `--windows <def>` is the windowed route of item 6, and `--windows raw` sends the raw rows through the
   streaming entry of `fs-assimilate` that FD-FSIM-23 adds (M5, DESIGN of that bead): at a pin whose airlock has
   no streaming entry it answers FROB-REF-724 naming FD-FSIM-23, exit 5, before any fit, and the calibration
   record's field `route` reads `windowed` or `streaming`. Result: calibration record
   identifier; candidate card version identifiers; per parameter the posterior mean, standard deviation and
   provenance; the combinations refused as unidentified, each with the
   identified combination and the separating experiment; misfit before and after; labels. Refusals:
   FROB-REF-879 (a fit on a parameter set that fails identifiability), FROB-REF-814 (pooling across cells, across
   model identities, or of synthetic with real data).
4. **Pre-checks, in order.** (a) Only the calibration split is readable; a sealed part is never opened here.
   (b) Every instrument used has a clock fit. A channel on a clock without one may contribute time-free
   features only (levels at rest, steady-state means, peak values); a stage whose requested parameters need
   that channel's time-stamped trace is refused with 879, pointer `stage`, naming `clocks` (N30a-20). (c) The
   identifiability analysis is recomputed for the experiments actually recorded, not the planned ones;
   parameters that fail are removed from θ and reported; a request that names one explicitly is refused with
   879. (d) Rows from more than one cell, model identity or origin are refused with 814. (e) A later stage with
   no record of the earlier stage in this cell is refused with 879, pointer `stage`: its parameters are aliased
   with the earlier stage's unfitted error.
5. **Pass A, linear.** Away from sticking and saturation the model is linear in (I + J_a, b, τ_c, bias):
   N·η·k_t·i(t − d₀) − g(q) = (I + J_a)·q̈ + b·q̇ + τ_c·sgn(q̇) + bias, with d₀ the prior delay. Signals are
   band-limited with one zero-phase low-pass filter applied to both sides, so the linear relation is preserved;
   q̇ and q̈ come from the filtered angle. Samples with |q̇| below five prior Stribeck speeds or with |i| within
   2% of the current limit are masked. Weighted least squares gives a warm start and a first residual plot
   (residual against time, velocity and position). The filter, the mask and the solve are N30a-21's, and
   these are its defaults.
6. **Pass B, output error.** The joint, or the whole robot, is simulated on the referee with candidate θ and
   compared with the measured encoder, current and torque traces.
   - Traces enter as **windows of summary features**, because the sibling's update admits at most 256 rows. A
     window definition (N30a-22's `WindowDef`: the window length of each experiment, the features per channel,
     which are the window mean, the least-squares slope and, for an encoder, the increment, and the row cap of
     256 per experiment) is fixed before the fit and stored in the calibration record with `route =
     "windowed"`; `--windows <def>` sets the length. Experiments are assimilated one after another in a fixed order of experiment identifier, the
     posterior of one being the prior of the next, so the cap binds each experiment and not the dataset; the
     shared calibration biases are carried in that posterior, so the common-mode floor holds across the whole
     sequence (N30a-22).
   - The Gaussian update is `fs-assimilate::nonlinear::iterated`, with model Jacobians of the features with
     respect to θ taken on the referee (parameters scaled by their prior standard deviations).
   - Outliers go through its Huber-loss fit.
   - A shared encoder bias enters as a common-mode source through `groups`, so a thousand samples that share one
     bias do not count as a thousand measurements.
   - Instrument covariance R comes from the manifest's `sigma` and `bias_bound`, never from the residuals.
7. **Handoff.** Delay, static friction and the Stribeck speed are not fitted here: delay is held on a grid value
   and profiled, and the two stick–slip parameters go to the ensemble lane (N30a-23). The calibration record is
   assembled and the energy cross-check run by N30a-24; candidate card versions are written by N30a-27.
8. **Jobs.** The sibling is called through the `Assimilation` trait of the airlock as whole jobs of seconds to
   minutes; under the worker-process fallback of the airlock the same jobs cross a process boundary unchanged.
9. **Gate battery** (defined here; run by the gate script's bead N30a-26 as the `GATE-GROUND-04` entry of the
   conformance crate's known-answer tier, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<S> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
   g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-GROUND-04`; fixture R-04 of the rehearsal: 100 synthetic rigs with planted θ and prior cards off by
   factors of 0.5 to 2.0). Clause 1: every one of J_a, b, τ_c, τ_s,
   v_s within 10% of truth and the delay within one grid step, on at least 95 of 100 rigs. Clause 2: summed over
   the rigs, the root-mean-square held-out trajectory error with identified cards is at most 0.70 of that with
   prior cards (both measured through frozen predictions on the sealed validation day). Clause 3: on the
   omitted-law variant (fixture R-06: truth has a Stribeck term the fit class lacks, the fit restricted with
   `--law-class coulomb-viscous`) the candidate is rejected at validation on at least 90 of 100 rigs.
10. **Stage 3 inertia steps** (`--stage inertia`, the fit itself in N30a-25). With the feature
   `frontier-affine-inertia` built, every step of the inertial-parameter fit goes through N13-30's
   `InertiaStep` on the 4×4 pseudo-inertia matrix, with the chart (`AffineInvariant`, or `LogCholesky` if the
   pilots selected the fallback) taken from the decision recorded for pilots N13-29 and N10a-13 in
   `frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`. Without the feature the fit is Euclidean and each iterate is
   projected onto the pseudo-inertia cone (arm B of N10a-13). The calibration record names the chart used and
   counts the infeasible iterates (iterates whose pseudo-inertia fails the positivity check before any
   projection), so the pilot's comparison can be read from ordinary runs.

Determinism: Passes A and B are class D2 on one machine fingerprint. Evidence class Estimated; status `candidate`
until scored; `synthetic` on the rehearsal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-GROUND-04 (TARGET; seed done-condition), the battery of item 9: at least 95 of 100 rigs within 10%;
      held-out error cut by at least 30% against prior cards; the omitted-law candidate rejected on at least 90
      of 100. Clauses 2 and 3 need frozen predictions scored on sealed data (`ground.predict`, `ground.score`),
      which land after this bead, so the gate is measured from the user's side, and its receipt and record
      written, by N30a-26 (one recorder per gate), which depends on this bead; the KILL-GROUND decision
      (N30b-17) reads that record. This bead closes on the criteria below, whichever way that measurement
      falls; its own recovery check is `single_rig_recovery_within_10_percent`.
- [ ] Unidirectional data: the request for τ_c is refused with FROB-REF-879 and the result names the identified
      combination τ_c·sgn(q̇) + τ_b and the separating experiment (reversals).
- [ ] A batch that mixes two cells, two model identities or both origins is refused with FROB-REF-814.
- [ ] `--stage contact` with no actuator record in the cell is refused.
- [ ] No sealed chunk is read by this verb (the store's read log shows none).
- [ ] The window definition appears in the calibration record with `route = "windowed"`, and changing it changes
      the record's identity.
- [ ] `--law-class coulomb-viscous` fits without τ_s and v_s and the record's `conditional_on` names
      `coulomb-viscous`; `--law-class no-such-class` is refused with FROB-REF-802 before any fit; at a pin with no
      streaming entry `--windows raw` is refused with FROB-REF-724 naming FD-FSIM-23, exit 5, and no record is
      written.
- [ ] CX-G-07's plant is built and its catalogue entry records the case (Known answers below).
- [ ] Pass A reproduces planted (I + J_a, b, τ_c) within 2% on noise-free data; with a shared encoder bias of
      3 × 10⁻⁴ rad the posterior standard deviation of the bias stays at the common-mode floor however many
      windows are used.
- [ ] Stage 3 inertia leg: on the rehearsal's planted one-link set, with the feature built, each chart records in
      the calibration record its chart name, its infeasible-iterate count (zero for the geodesic and
      log-Cholesky charts) and its recovery error against the planted inertial parameters; without the feature
      the projected Euclidean fit runs and is recorded as such.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob ground identify --json` and the MCP tool `ground_identify`; a call without
      `--key` is refused with FROB-REF-805; cancellation mid-job leaves no candidate card and no partial record.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh` (bead N30a-26, which owns the script and depends on this bead) runs ingest,
excite and identify on the rig population through the binary, every `ingest` and `identify` call with its own
`--key`, and logs, per rig, each parameter's estimate against truth, the relative error against 10%, misfit
before and after, and the three gate counts with their thresholds. With `frontier-affine-inertia` built it
also carries N13-30's per-chart leg (that bead's own script defers it here): `frob ground identify --stage
inertia` on the planted one-link set under each chart, logging per chart the infeasible iterates (expected
zero for the geodesic and log-Cholesky charts) and the recovery error against the planted values, with units.

## Considerations

- The 256-row cap forces windowing, and windowing discards information. Upstream work item FD-FSIM-23 asks the
  sibling for a windowed or streaming entry; interim posture: summary-feature windows with the definition in
  the record (`--windows raw` is refused with 724 until the entry is pinned); switch-over: re-fit one recorded
  dataset both ways (`--windows <def>` and `--windows raw`) and compare posteriors.
- A probe that is perfect inside its assumed law class can be wrong outside it (675 of 675 in class, 0 of 225
  under an omitted delayed law, FACT). The omitted-law clause of the gate keeps that failure measured.
- Risk RISK-42: a model fits nominal motion for the wrong physical reason; its countermeasure is the energy
  cross-check of N30a-24.
- Identification of a whole robot reuses the per-joint model with the robot's own I(q) and g(q); on the stand
  these are constants, which is why the stand is preferred.
- Budget: a 100-rig battery is hours of CPU; it runs in the `full` battery, and one rig runs in `smoke`.
