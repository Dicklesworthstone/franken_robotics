## Background

A comparison between simulation and reality means something only if the prediction existed before the data
could be read. Otherwise the comparison is a fit, and its error rate is unknown. That is decision
**ADR-GROUND-05**: a prediction is frozen before its trial data is readable; a refit opens a new prediction
version; a scored holdout is retired. Ground (crate `frob-ground`, layer 5), the subsystem that compares
simulation with measured reality, enforces it in two halves. Admission seals the validation and holdout parts of
every dataset at the moment it enters and commits their hashes to the ledger. This bead is the other half: the
record that says, before the seal is opened, what the simulator expects to see, how the comparison will be
scored and what will be concluded from each possible outcome.

The record extends the studio's registered predictions (a prediction hashed and frozen before a run and scored
afterwards with a proper scoring rule) with what a real trial needs: the cell it belongs to, the trial
conditions drawn in advance, tolerances that come from instruments, and a declared study.

Rejected alternatives: freezing by convention ("we did not look"); a single unitless tolerance for every
metric; deciding the number of trials after seeing some of them; quietly replacing a prediction that failed.

Tier: Frontier. The kill gate that covers this protocol is KILL-CLOCK (its trigger includes a failure of
GATE-GROUND-05): real logs would then be scored on time-free metrics only.

## Scope

This bead is the capstone of frozen predictions: it owns the verb `ground.predict` and, with the verb
`ground.score` (N30b-06), closes GATE-GROUND-05. The parts are built in their own beads, which are
dependencies of this one:

- the prediction record `frob.ground.prediction/1`, its validation, tolerance registration, freezing and
  tamper detection: N30a-31;
- the scoring functions (interval score, bias, coverage, residual structure, `ranking-falsified`): N30a-32;
- the declared study of a real trial and its reachability pre-analysis: N30a-33;
- prediction versions after a refit, the holdout spent once per cell and lineage, error spending: N30a-34.

In this bead: the verb `ground.predict` (kind mutate), its arguments and envelope; the per-cell precondition
check of the procedure (N30a-09) at registration; the conditions draw by address; the order of the freeze in
the ledger as the verb guarantees it; the listing of versions. The Technical approach below states the
integrated behaviour the parts must show together; each part's own bead is where its internals are specified
and built.

Out: opening sealed data, scoring, holdout retirement and admission as a verb (N30b-06, which depends on this
bead); the sealed store and its read guard (BD-387); the gate script (`scripts/e2e/ground_protocol.sh`,
N30a-35, which depends on this bead).

## Technical approach

1. **Record** (DESIGN):

   ```toml
   # frob.ground.prediction/1
   [prediction]
   version = 1; cell = "…"; model = "ModelId"; cards = ["…"]; scene = "SceneId"; task = "TaskId"
   conditions_draw = "philox:seed=…"       # trial conditions are drawn and recorded before the trial
   world_addr      = "…"                   # the simulated side is replayable at its recorded determinism class
   metrics = [ { name = "crossing_time_s", interval = [4.05, 4.42], level = 0.90 },
               { name = "peak_current_A",  interval = [4.8, 6.1],   level = 0.90 } ]
   scoring   = "interval-score"
   decision  = "B beats A by at least 5 points"
   tolerance = "from instrument sigma and the decision margin"
   study     = { type = "fixed-n", n = 20, reachable = ["promote", "inconclusive"] }
   frozen_at = "ledger-seq:…"              # precedes the first read of the sealed chunk
   ```
   The schema is N30a-31's, and its typed form is the one that bead states: per metric `unit`, `source`
   (`caller` or `simulated`) and `tolerance = { value, unit, instrument_sigma, margin }`; `lineage`; and
   `parent`, the previous version, if any (the verb's option `--supersedes <id>` is what sets `parent`; the
   record has no second field of that name). Three more fields the verb fills and the scorer reads: `sealed =
   [chunk-hash commitments this record predicts]`; `clock_requirements` (which metrics are time-based and which
   clock fits they need); `origin` of the dataset.
2. **Verb.** `frob ground predict --dataset <id> --part validation|holdout --cell <cell> --model <id> --scene
   <id> --task <id> --metric <name>:<level>… --decision <spec> --study fixed-n:<n>|anytime [--supersedes <id>]
   --key <k> --json`; kind `mutate` (the idempotency key is required: without it the call is FROB-REF-805; the
   expected parent is the superseded version); capability
   `ground:write`; schemas `frob.ground.predict.in/1` and `.out/1`. `<spec>` is the text form of the record's
   typed `decision` (N30a-31), as in the registry's example `--decision "crossing_time_s below 4.5"`. Result:
   the record identifier, its hash, the
   ledger sequence, the drawn conditions, the intervals, the pre-analysis and `reality` (unchanged by
   registration). Refusals: FROB-REF-883 (a read of sealed data with no frozen prediction, or a prediction
   changed after freezing); FROB-REF-897 (use of a retired calibration: step 4); consumed codes FROB-REF-532
   (a fixed-n study that names a verdict it cannot reach),
   FROB-REF-552 (mixed study types), FROB-REF-102 (a missing or mixed unit).
3. **Conditions.** Trial conditions (payload, speed, surface state within the cell's envelope, start pose) are
   drawn from a counter-based stream keyed by the cell, the dataset part and the trial index, and written into
   the record. The operator runs the trial under those conditions. Nobody, including the experiment planner,
   chooses conditions after seeing anything: at scoring, the recorded conditions must equal the draw within the
   condition instruments' tolerance.
4. **Intervals.** The simulated side runs at `world_addr` with the cell's cards: `admitted` versions where the
   cell has them, prior versions otherwise (a `candidate` version may be named explicitly, which is how a
   candidate is put to its test). The status of each card version bound in `--model` is read with N30a-28's
   `card::status(version, ledger)`. Each metric's interval at its level is the central interval of the simulated
   metric over correlated draws from the cards' stated uncertainty, passed through the sensor models so that it
   predicts the measured value. In a calibrated cell the band half-width is added on both sides (the cell's
   band record and its half-width at the metric's level are N30a-36's).
   *The computation* (this verb's; the record of N30a-31 stores what it is handed and computes nothing):
   simulate the task in the scene under each drawn condition with the bound cards, over 256 correlated draws
   of the cards' identified block, drawn by address through N30a-27's `card::sampler`, and the simulator's
   sensor noise. The interval of a metric at level 1 − α is its central (α/2, 1 − α/2) sample quantile
   interval over those draws, written with `source = "simulated"`; the record gets `world_addr` and the
   digest of the simulated side's replay, so that a scorer can replay the side at its recorded determinism
   class. `ground.predict` has no input for a caller's interval (its row takes `--metric <name>:<level>`), so
   every interval this verb writes is `simulated`; `source = "caller"` is for records built through the typed
   interface.
   *A retired cell.* In a cell whose status is `retired(cell, reason)` the admitted versions of that
   calibration are no longer usable (N30a-43) and no band is added: the binding falls back to the prior
   versions, as in an uncalibrated cell, which is how recalibration starts. A request whose `--model` binds an
   admitted version of the retired calibration is refused with FROB-REF-897 (use of a retired calibration,
   N30-REF's row, which names a prediction among the uses), exit 3, with the retirement reason and its ledger
   sequence in the payload and the repairs in the registry's order: recalibrate inside the new regime; report
   `reality: no-claim`. The cell's status is read through the reality guard (BD-139).
5. **Tolerances are registered** and are derived from measurement uncertainty and the decision margin. For each
   metric the record stores the propagated instrument uncertainty u (from `sigma` and `bias_bound` of the
   instruments the metric uses) with its unit, and the decision margin Δ. If 2·u exceeds Δ (N30a-31's
   registration rule, under which the metric is registered `unresolvable`) the trial cannot resolve
   the decision and the pre-analysis lists `inconclusive` as the only reachable verdict. One unitless epsilon
   across angles, currents and temperatures is refused (FROB-REF-102).
6. **Scoring is proper and declared.** `interval-score`: S = (u − l) + (2/α)·(l − y)₊ + (2/α)·(y − u)₊ for an
   interval [l, u] at level 1 − α and outcome y; (x)₊ is max(x, 0). It is reported later with bias, coverage and
   residual structure against condition.
7. **The study type is declared:** fixed-n exact with a pre-analysis of reachable verdicts, or anytime-valid,
   never both. For real trials n is small and the pre-analysis decides whether the trial is worth running. With
   zero failures in n trials the one-sided 95% upper bound on the failure probability is 1 − 0.05^(1/n): 13.9%
   at n = 20, 9.5% at n = 30, 4.95% at n = 59. A claim of "under 5%" is unreachable below 59 failure-free trials
   (FACT, arithmetic), and registering it at a smaller n is refused, with the first n at which it becomes
   reachable in the payload. These figures are at the level 0.05 the attempt runs at: the first attempt of a
   claim runs at α₁ = α/2 of its nominal α (N30a-34's series α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)]), so the 59-trial threshold
   is that of a claim declared at nominal α = 0.10, the registered fixture of KA-732; declared at nominal
   0.05 its first attempt runs at 0.025 and needs ⌈ln 0.025/ln 0.95⌉ = 72 failure-free trials.
8. **Freezing.** The canonical record is hashed and appended to the ledger; `frozen_at` is that sequence
   number. The sealed store mints a score token only for a record whose sequence precedes every read of the
   chunks in `sealed` (invariant INV-GROUND-03: a prediction's ledger sequence precedes the first read of the
   sealed chunk it predicts). After freezing no field can change: a write to the record's address with other
   bytes is refused with 883.
9. **A refit opens a new version.** A posterior changed after validation data was seen needs version 2 of the
   prediction and fresh sealed data; a version that names sealed chunks already read is refused with 883. A
   published failure keeps its original threshold: the superseded version stays in the ledger, scored and
   visible.
10. **Prediction ledger.** The record is also entered as a registered prediction of the studio, so that the
    calibration of Ground's own intervals appears in `predict.report`.

Determinism: the conditions draw is class D0; the simulated side has the class of its tier, recorded. Evidence
class Estimated; registration creates no Reality status.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-GROUND-05, first clause, as far as this bead can show it: every record the verb registers on the
      rehearsal's protocol bundle carries `frozen_at`, a ledger sequence, and at registration the ledger holds
      no read event of the chunks in its `sealed`; zero violations over the records registered. The clause
      itself (the freeze precedes the first read, over every scored trial) needs `ground.score` (N30b-06) and
      is measured by the gate run of N30a-35; both depend on this bead, and the gate row, recorded from that
      run's receipt, is no condition of closing it.
- [ ] A frozen record cannot be altered: a changed interval, metric, tolerance, decision or study under the
      same version is refused with FROB-REF-883.
- [ ] A new version that names already-read sealed chunks is refused with 883; one that names fresh chunks is
      accepted and carries the earlier version in `parent` (set by `--supersedes`).
- [ ] A registered claim of "failure rate under 5%" at n = 20, declared at nominal α = 0.10 (first attempt at
      0.05), is refused at registration with FROB-REF-532 and the payload states n = 59; at n = 59 it is
      accepted.
- [ ] Computed intervals are reproducible from `world_addr` (the same replay digest twice) and contain the
      stated share of the 256 simulated draws; every interval the verb writes has `source = "simulated"`.
- [ ] A request whose model binds an admitted card version of a retired calibration is refused with
      FROB-REF-897 and the retirement reason; the same request with the prior versions is accepted and adds
      no band.
- [ ] A study declaring both types is refused; a tolerance with no unit is refused.
- [ ] The drawn conditions are a pure function of (cell, part, trial index) and appear in the record before any
      sealed read.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob ground predict --json` and the MCP tool `ground_predict`; a retried call with the
      same idempotency key returns the stored record and does not write a second ledger event.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_predict.sh` (this bead's own, built only on its dependencies; it sources the STD-E2E
helper; header `bead=BD-392 milestone=M5 labels=ground,verb:ground.predict seed=1 needs=cpu,mcp`). Steps:
(1) `frob ground ingest <the rehearsal's actuator-rig bundle R-04> --design <rig design> --task-family tf:none
--key "$(e2e_key ingest)" --json` (BD-387; a `mutate` verb, so with its key), asserting the sealed commitment
of the validation and holdout parts; (2) `frob ground predict --dataset <dataset> --part validation --cell
<cell> --model <the rig's model with prior cards> --scene <id> --task <id> --metric <name>:0.90 --decision
<spec> --study fixed-n:20 --key "$(e2e_key predict)" --json`, asserting the record identifier and hash, the
ledger sequence `frozen_at`, the drawn conditions, an interval per metric with `source = "simulated"`, the
pre-analysis, `reality` unchanged by the registration and `evidence.class = "Estimated"`, and that the ledger
holds no read event of the record's `sealed` chunks; (3) the same call again on purpose under the same key
(`--key "$(e2e_key predict --again)"`): the stored record is returned and the ledger holds one freeze event;
(4) a second record for `--part holdout` under its own label: its conditions differ from step 2's and both
are reproduced by a second registration of the same trial index (a pure function of cell, part and index);
(5) refusals: the claim "failure rate under 5%" under `--study fixed-n:20` at nominal α = 0.10
(`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-532 3`, the payload's n asserted 59), a level outside (0, 1) in `--metric`
(`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-802 3`), the registry's retired option `--trial x`
(`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-802 3`) and a call without `--key` (`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-805 3`);
(6) step 2's request once more as the MCP tool `ground_predict` with its own `idempotency_key`, the two
envelopes agreeing on `ok`, `exit` and the record's fields other than its identifier. Logs to
`target/e2e-logs/ground_predict/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: per line `ts`, `script`, `bead` = BD-392,
`step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; per `cmd` the envelope's evidence block
(claim, class `Estimated`, tier, determinism, the dataset, cell, model and prediction identities, seed;
`reality: synthetic`); per record its hash, `frozen_at`, the interval of each metric with its unit, level and
`source`, and the replay digest; per refusal the code, pointer, observed, required and the ranked repairs; on
failure the first differing field with its expected value and the path of the saved envelope; a final
`summary` with assertions passed and failed. No sample of sealed data enters the log. The protocol as a whole
is N30a-35's: `scripts/e2e/ground_protocol.sh` (which owns that script, depends on this bead and on
`ground.score`, and is GATE-GROUND-05's recorder) registers predictions for every rehearsal trial, logs each
record's identifier, hash and ledger sequence, then attempts the three protocol mutations and logs the
refusal of each with its code and repairs.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-732 (zero failures in 20 trials: upper bound 13.9%; "under 5%" unreachable; the registered claim at n = 20 is
refused at registration) and KA-734 (protocol: read before freeze; refit without a new version; holdout reuse:
each refused). CX-333, CX-331. Planted failures: a registrar without the pre-analysis (accepts "under 5%" at
n = 20); a store that lets a record be rewritten in place; a conditions draw keyed by wall-clock time.
Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; identical record hashes across platforms for the
same inputs.

## Considerations

- A prediction interval that is very wide always covers. Coverage alone is therefore not the score; the
  interval score penalizes width, and both are reported.
- The first prediction in an uncalibrated cell has no band to widen it; it is expected to miss more often than
  its level says, and that miss is the information a calibration starts from.
- The record contains identities and numbers, no raw data, so it can travel in a replication kit even when the
  dataset cannot.
- Repeated confirmation attempts spend error: the level of the i-th attempt is set at scoring (N30b-06).
