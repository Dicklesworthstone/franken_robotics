## Background

A reality band is the one quantitative statement Ground makes about how far a simulated number may be from the
measured one. Ground is the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality (crate `frob-ground`). The
band exists only inside a **cell**, the scope key of every Reality result: (design, set of physical units,
controller identity, firmware, surface class, task family, envelope box over the recorded conditions).
Decision **ADR-GROUND-02:** the unit of calibration is the cell, and a band belongs to exactly one cell,
because coverage holds for exchangeable draws only and pooling hides a failing regime.

The method is split conformal prediction, chosen because its guarantee does not depend on the simulator being
good: marginal coverage of at least 1 − α holds for any simulator quality, provided test draws are exchangeable
with the calibration draws. A poor simulator gives a wide band, not a wrong one (FACT: the coverage law of
https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511).

This mechanism is the narrowed survivor of a stronger idea. The strong form, "with 20 paired real episodes,
bands reach nominal coverage on held-out real episodes for a robot class", was refuted: coverage is marginal
and holds only inside the exchangeable set that was calibrated, and nothing follows for a design with no
calibration data. What survives is a band per calibrated cell and a refusal everywhere else. It is the subject
of bet BET-28 (calibration bands reach their stated coverage within one calibrated robot and task family),
whose falsifiers are: coverage misses nominal by more than the finite-sample allowance; a band issued after the
exchangeability alarm fired; a band issued for a design never built.

Tier: Frontier. Pilot: N30b-14. Kill gate KILL-BAND, named in advance: if GATE-GROUND-06 or GATE-GROUND-07 fails,
bands are withdrawn and reports print `reality: no-claim` with the raw residual table.

## Technical approach

1. **Calibration pairs.** Inside one cell a pair (ŷ_i, y_i) is a simulated prediction and the real outcome under
   the same registered conditions. Pairs come from scored trials and from later episodes ingested into a
   calibrated cell, predicted by the fixed model. A pair enters the pool only if (a) its conditions were as
   drawn, and (b) its episode was not used to identify any card bound in the model (split conformal: residuals
   on fitting data are optimistic). The pool is keyed by (cell, metric, model identity); all three must match.
2. **Residual.** Two-sided: r_i = |y_i − ŷ_i|. One-sided, for the statement "real is at least sim minus q̂" on a
   performance metric: r_i = ŷ_i − y_i.
3. **Half-width.** With n pairs and level 1 − α, q̂ is the ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉-th smallest residual, and the band
   is infinite when that index exceeds n. The implementation is `fs-eproc::hardening::MondrianConformal` with the
   cell as the bucket and a minimum calibration count of 19 (TARGET; the sibling's own fixture uses 19, FACT).
   The sibling refuses a bucket below its minimum and returns an infinite band past the index (FACT); buckets
   are opaque strings to it, so the cell key, its hashing and every scope rule are this crate's.
4. **What the evidence supports** (FACT, arithmetic from the coverage law).
   - Conditional on the calibration set, realized coverage is distributed Beta(n + 1 − l, l) with
     l = ⌊(n + 1)·α⌋.

     | n | Target | Mean coverage | Standard deviation | P(coverage < 0.80) |
     |---|---|---|---|---|
     | 20 | 0.90 | 0.905 | 0.063 | 0.069 |
     | 200 | 0.90 | 0.900 | 0.021 | below 0.001 |
   - A finite band needs n ≥ (1 − α)/α: 9 at 90%, 19 at 95%, 99 at 99%. At n = 20 a 95% band is the largest
     residual seen, and any level above 20/21 = 95.2% is unbounded.
   - The known bound on the coverage gap under shift is Σ w_i·d_TV(Z, Z_i)/(1 + Σ w_i)
     (https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.13415; w_i weights on calibration points, d_TV total-variation distance between
     the test point's law and the i-th point's). For an unbuilt design that distance cannot be measured, so
     nothing is issued.
5. **Rules.**
   1. A band is issued per cell, and refused for an uncalibrated cell or n below the minimum (FROB-REF-889), for
      a new unit when too few units are calibrated (889), for any unbuilt design (FROB-REF-895), for data pooled
      across cells or model identities and for synthetic data mixed with real (FROB-REF-814), and for a retired
      cell (FROB-REF-897).
   2. A simulated metric in a calibrated cell is reported as [ŷ − q̂, ŷ + q̂] with n, the level, and the
      sentence "coverage is marginal over calibration draws; at this n realized coverage varies by about ±s",
      where s = √(a·b/((a + b)²·(a + b + 1))) with a = n + 1 − l and b = l (0.063 at n = 20, 0.021 at n = 200,
      level 0.90).
   3. A band never raises an evidence class. It is Estimated, and in a claim product the weakest input wins.
   4. Many banded metrics on one scorecard spend an explicit false-coverage budget (N30a-37 and N30a-38).
6. **Band record and status.** `frob.ground.band/1`: cell, metric, model identity, sidedness, n, level, index,
   q̂ with unit, the pool hash (the ordered residuals' identifiers), the dataset identifiers, the sibling's
   receipt hash. Written when scoring changes the pool. The cell's status becomes `calibrated(cell, n, level)`
   when a frozen prediction has been scored on sealed data and a finite band exists; the line on reports reads
   `reality: calibrated [cell=… n=… level=… band=<q̂><unit> dataset=…]`. On the rehearsal the status is
   `synthetic`, whatever the counts.
7. **Verb.** `ground.band`, kind `read`; capability `ground:read`; schemas `frob.ground.band.in/1`, `.out/1`.
   - `frob ground band (--cell <c> | --design <built design> --task-family <tf>) --metric <m> --level <1−α>
     [--sided two|lower] [--for <result>] --json`: the band, or the refusal with samples present and samples
     needed. The metric is named with its unit (`crossing_time_s`) and the level is an exact decimal. With
     `--design` and `--task-family` the cell is found by the authorization order of N30a-37. `--for <result>`
     names a stored result (a run, a campaign row or a score): the request's design, model identity, target
     unit and recorded conditions are taken from it, the same authorization order applies (a result outside
     every calibrated envelope, or of another model identity, is refused with 889), and the answer adds the
     result's value ŷ and the interval [ŷ − q̂, ŷ + q̂] (or [ŷ − q̂, ∞) for `--sided lower`).
   - `--list`: cells with status, n per metric, levels available, coverage accounting (pairs in, pairs
     excluded and why, bands issued and refused).
   - `--status [--cell <c>]`: monitor states and their power facts (BD-394), and the per-cell procedure state
     with the reality-gap variance (N30a-09); without `--cell`, every cell.
   - `--action retire --cell <c> --reason <text>` (the registry's action `retire`, selected by the one input
     property `action`; over MCP `action: "retire"`): of kind `admin`, needs the capability `ground:retire`
     besides `ground:read` (without it, FROB-REF-720, exit 5), and takes no idempotency key: no form of
     `ground.band` has one, and `--key` is FROB-REF-802. It is idempotent by its effect: a retire of a cell
     already retired returns the stored retirement record with `already: retired` and writes nothing. It is
     the one form that changes state (N30a-40 and N30a-43).
8. **Gate battery** (defined here; run by the gate script's bead N30a-39 as the `GATE-GROUND-06` entry of the
   conformance crate's tier targets, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<S> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test <tier>
   [--features gauntlet-mutations] -- GATE-GROUND-06`, with
   the tier each known-answer row names). On fixture R-08 of the rehearsal (exchangeable
   residuals from a registered continuous law, n = 20, target 0.90, 1,000 resamples, conditional coverage
   computed exactly as F(q̂)): the mean and the fraction of resamples below 0.80. On R-09 and the pooled
   fixture: zero bands for the shifted cell, the short cell and the unbuilt design.

Determinism: levels are exact decimal strings parsed as rationals and the index ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉ is computed in
integer arithmetic (class D0), because a floating-point ceiling can be off by one where the product is an
integer, as at (19, 0.95) and (99, 0.99); the order statistic over double-precision residuals is
class D2. Evidence class Estimated; claim kind Reality on real data, Known answer on the rehearsal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-GROUND-06 (TARGET; seed done-condition), the battery of item 8: mean in-cell coverage within 0.01 of
      19/21 = 0.905; fraction of resamples below 0.80 within 0.03 of 0.069; zero bands for the shifted cell, the
      short cell or the unbuilt design. The gate is measured from the user's side, and its receipt and record
      written, by N30a-39 (one recorder per gate), which depends on this bead; the KILL-BAND decision (N30b-18)
      reads that record. This bead closes on the criteria below, whichever way that measurement falls.
- [ ] The index table reproduces: (n, level) = (20, 0.90) → 19; (20, 0.95) → 20; (20, 0.99) → infinite;
      (19, 0.95) → 19; (99, 0.99) → 99; (98, 0.99) → infinite.
- [ ] A cell with 8 pairs, a new unit with 3 units calibrated and an unbuilt design are refused with 889, 889
      and 895; the 889 payloads state samples present and needed (8 and 19; 3 and 9).
- [ ] The pooled fixture reproduces 20 of 20 and 16 of 20 under a mutant keyed by design only, and no pooled
      band is issued by the real build (814).
- [ ] An in-sample pair (an episode used to identify a bound card) is excluded from the pool and counted in the
      coverage accounting.
- [ ] Every issued band prints n, the level and the coverage sentence with the right s; its envelope class is
      Estimated.
- [ ] Reachable through `frob ground band --json` and the MCP tool `ground_band`; the three read forms change
      no stored state (store digest equal before and after).
- [ ] `frob ground band --action retire --cell <c> --reason <text> --json` without `ground:retire` is refused with
      720; with it the cell is retired, and a repeat returns `already: retired` with the store digest unchanged;
      the same call with `--key k` is refused with 802.
- [ ] CX-330's plant `BD-393:cx-330` builds under `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-ground/cx-330`, and on that
      build the pooled fixture gives a band with held-out coverage 16 of 20 in the shifted regime (the control
      is caught).

## Considerations

- With 1,000 resamples the standard error of the mean coverage is 0.002 and of the fraction below 0.80 is
  0.008, so the gate's tolerances are about five and four standard errors: a correct build passes reliably and
  the mis-indexed mutant fails.
- The verb is registered as `read`, and its action `retire` mutates. The registry entry (VERBS-ground) keeps
  kind `read` and declares `actions = [{ name = "retire", kind = "admin", capabilities = ["ground:read",
  "ground:retire"] }]`; an `admin` action has no idempotency key and is safe to repeat by its effect, and the
  generated surfaces show it as a separate, capability-gated group.
- Ceiling, stated before any test: Ground has no lever on time or compute; its only possible saving, on
  real-trial count, is bounded near 1.3 (N30a-37 and N30a-38).
- Open question OPEN-GROUND-5 (who could promote a sibling's estimated candidate): default nobody; every band
  stays Estimated.
