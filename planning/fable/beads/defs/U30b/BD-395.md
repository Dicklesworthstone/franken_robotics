## Background

A built part is one more representation of the design. When a link has been printed or machined, a scan of it
says how the real part differs from the model the simulator used: its pose relative to its datums, its wall
thickness, its form. Ground (crate `frob-ground`, the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality)
admits such scans as datasets, registers them to the design, and reports deviations with their uncertainty.
The later half of the loop, turning those deviations into a new model variant and auditing tolerances, is in
N30b-11. Scans of real places belong to another subsystem; Ground handles robots, parts and rigs.

What makes the problem well-posed is decided at design time, not at scan time: the build packet declares
datums and at least three non-collinear fiducials per part, so correspondences between scan and design are
known. That matters because the sibling library registers with supplied correspondences and never searches for
them.

What the sibling provides (FACT, FrankenSim `crates/fs-asbuilt`, about 10,800 lines): three-dimensional
weighted rigid and similarity registration with known correspondences, a scale estimate with standard error
and a unit-suspicion diagnostic, and a calibrated six-parameter pose covariance (`rigid3`); datum-priority
3-2-1 registration (`datum`); deviation, thickness, form and roughness fields from supplied correspondences
(`field`); pose covariance carried into correlated quantities of interest, at most 64, with a sigma-point check
that downgrades to unknown (`propagate`); simultaneous decision bounds that need no Gaussian assumption
(`uncertainty`); a signal-versus-noise screen (`well_posed`). Missing: correspondences are never discovered
(no closest-point matching); no reading of density or porosity; no mass properties; no promotion beyond an
estimated candidate.

Two errors this bead exists to prevent. A registration residual is a fit diagnostic, not a transform
uncertainty: reporting the residual as "the accuracy of the alignment" is wrong in both directions. And a
scale estimate near 25.4 or 0.001 is a unit error, not shrinkage.

Tier: Frontier. Pilot: N30b-16. Kill gate KILL-ASBUILT, named in advance: if GATE-GROUND-08 fails, scans are
not ingested, and as-built data enters as scalar bench measurements (mass, lengths), each a direct measurement
of one card field.

## Scope

In: admission of scans; fiducial checks; rigid and similarity registration; pose covariance; deviation,
thickness and form fields; propagation into kinematic quantities; the noise screen; the unit-suspicion rule;
the as-built record. Out: posteriors on design parameters, mass properties, the as-built model variant and the
tolerance audit (N30b-11); the build packet (the manufacturing subsystem); the kill decision (N30b-20).

## Technical approach

1. **Admission.** A scan is a dataset like any other: a `.frun` bundle with manifest `frob.ground.dataset/1`,
   `kind = "scan"`, licence, rights, the scanner as an instrument with `sigma` and `bias_bound`, and the cell.
   It adds a fiducial table (fiducial identifier, measured position, per-axis σ), optional surface
   correspondences (scan point to design surface point), the declared length unit, and either the fiducials or
   the identity of an external registration tool (name, version, hash of its output). A scan with neither is
   refused with FROB-REF-812 (an as-built registration that is ill-posed or below its noise screen).
2. **Fiducial check.** At least three fiducial correspondences per part, not collinear: the ratio of the
   smallest to the largest singular value of the centred fiducial positions in their best-fit plane must exceed
   10⁻³ (TARGET). Otherwise 812, with the fiducials needed as the first repair.
3. **Registration.** `fs-asbuilt::rigid3::register3` aligns scan to design in closed form with weights from the
   fiducial σ. The similarity variant is always run beside it and reports scale with a standard error and the
   unit-suspicion diagnostic. `estimate_calibrated_rigid3` returns a six-parameter pose covariance (three
   rotation, three translation). Where the packet declares a datum priority, `datum` performs the 3-2-1
   registration instead and both transforms are recorded.
4. **Unit suspicion.** A scale within 2% (TARGET) of 25.4, 1/25.4, 1000 or 0.001 raises `unit-suspicion`. No
   deviation and no posterior is produced from such a scan (a request for one is refused with 812, repair
   "re-export in the declared unit"); the registration record is kept so the finding is visible.
5. **Fields.** `field` computes signed deviation, wall thickness and form fields from the supplied surface
   correspondences, stored as chunked columns referenced by the record.
6. **Propagation.** `propagate` carries the pose covariance into link length, axis offset and axis parallelism
   with their cross-correlation, and downgrades a term to `unknown` when its sigma-point check shows the
   linearization is poor. At most 64 quantities per call; more are split by part.
7. **Noise screen.** For each deviation under review, if the registration residual is at or above the
   deviation, the signal is below the noise and no deviation is reported (`well_posed` false); the output
   states the smallest deviation resolvable at this residual. Decision bounds over several deviations at once
   use the sibling's simultaneous bounds.
8. **Record.** `frob.ground.asbuilt/1`: part and design identities, scan dataset, fiducials used, rigid
   transform, similarity scale ± standard error, flags, pose covariance, residual statistics, per-deviation
   screen results, field references, propagated quantities with covariance and downgrades, the external tool's
   identity if any, the sibling receipt hashes. Ledger event `ground.asbuilt.registered`.
9. **Verb.** `frob ground ingest <scan-bundle> --design <id> --part <part> --json` (the scan form of
   `ground.ingest`, first due at M5). Result adds `registration` (transform, scale, flags), `screen`,
   `quantities`. Refusals: FROB-REF-873 (a dataset lacking licence, provenance, rights or measurement
   uncertainty), FROB-REF-812.

Determinism: closed-form registration in double precision, class D2. Evidence class Estimated (the sibling emits
estimated candidates only); status `synthetic` on the rehearsal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-GROUND-08, known-answer part (TARGET): on the rehearsal's link (planted rotation 2° about
      (1, 2, 2)/3, translation (3.0, −1.5, 0.8) mm, fiducial noise 0.02 mm, a planted +0.3 mm wall deviation)
      the pose and the deviation are recovered inside the stated uncertainty; with two fiducials the scan is
      refused; the copy scaled by 25.4 raises unit suspicion. Measured values recorded.
- [ ] Noise-free, the transform is recovered to 10⁻¹² and the scale is 1 to 10⁻¹².
- [ ] Three collinear fiducials are refused with 812; three in general position are accepted.
- [ ] A 0.3 mm deviation with a registration residual of 0.5 mm is not reported, and the output states the
      resolvable deviation.
- [ ] The residual is never printed under a name that suggests transform accuracy; the pose covariance is.
- [ ] A scan with no fiducials and no external tool identity is refused; with a tool identity it is admitted
      and the identity is in the record's provenance.
- [ ] The measurement is handed to the KILL-ASBUILT decision (N30b-20).
- [ ] Reachable through `frob ground ingest --json`; cancellation leaves no partial record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/asbuilt.rs` (the sibling is the real one; clouds are `synthetic`):
`register_recovers_planted_pose_noise_free`; `pose_within_covariance_with_noise` (1,000 noise seeds: the
planted pose inside the 95% ellipsoid in at least 93%); `two_fiducials_refused_812`;
`collinear_fiducials_refused_812`; `scale_25_4_raises_unit_suspicion`; `scale_0_001_raises_unit_suspicion`;
`scale_1_02_is_not_unit_suspicion_boundary` (2% edge); `no_deviation_from_unit_suspect_scan`;
`wall_deviation_recovered` (0.3 mm within its interval); `noise_screen_blocks_small_deviation`;
`propagate_link_length_and_axis_offset` (hand case: a 0.1° rotation about an axis perpendicular to a 0.25 m
link moves its far end by 0.436 mm); `sigma_point_downgrade_to_unknown` (a planted large-rotation case);
`datum_321_recorded_beside_rigid`; `external_tool_identity_in_provenance`; `more_than_64_quantities_split`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_asbuilt.sh` (bead N30b-35) ingests the rehearsal scans through the binary and logs the
recovered transform against the planted one with the covariance, the scale and its flag, each screen result
with residual and deviation, the refusals with code and repairs, and `synthetic: true`.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-733 (a planted rotation and translation and a 0.3 mm wall deviation recovered inside the stated
uncertainty; two fiducials refused; scale 25.4 raises unit suspicion). CX-G-03, CX-G-04. Planted failures: a
build that reports the residual as the uncertainty (coverage of the planted pose collapses); a build that
accepts two fiducials by assuming no rotation about their line; a build that silently rescales by 1/25.4.
Metamorphic relations: applying a known rigid motion to the scan composes with the recovered transform;
scaling all coordinates and the declared unit together changes nothing. Determinism lanes: twice on one
machine; debug and release; across platforms in class D3.

## Considerations

- Upstream work item FD-FSIM-25 asks the sibling for a correspondence-search lane. Interim posture: datums and
  fiducials are placed at design time so correspondences are known. Switch-over: re-run the as-built known
  answers without fiducials.
- The sibling reads no density or porosity. Mass properties come from a scale reading and computed volume
  (N30b-11), never from the scan.
- A scan of a part made in a private facility inherits the private mark like any dataset.
- Point clouds are large (up to 10⁶ points per call in the sibling). Fields are stored as chunks; the record
  holds references.
