## Background

Every other gate of Ground (the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality) is a Known-answer
gate decided on a synthetic rehearsal. One gate is different. GATE-GROUND-10 is the only gate in the subsystem
whose claim kind is Reality: the first real cell, with a frozen prediction scored on sealed data and a band
issued. It is an import lane: it blocks nothing, it is tied to no milestone, and its resting state is
`no-claim`. A program rule explains the form: a gate needs only what a simulator has, so a gate that needs a
physical board or a bench measurement is written as an import lane with a recorded `no-claim` until the data
exists.

The M5 exit is defined in part by this bead: "the Reality lane has a record or a recorded no-claim". Where no
data was admitted, the line reads `no-claim`, and that is recorded as the state and not as a pass. The
pre-mortem of the program lists "calibration data never arrived" as a story with a countermeasure, which is
this lane: a synthetic rehearsal, frozen predictions, a published-anchor comparison at M3, bench packages that
may be sourced from M0, and a resting state that blocks nothing. The residual is stated plainly: the Reality
claim stays empty.

When data does arrive, this bead is also where the bet behind the subsystem is read. BET-28: within one
calibrated robot and task family, conformal bands on the gap between simulation and measurement reach their
stated coverage. Its falsifier: on held-out real episodes in the family, empirical coverage misses nominal by
more than the finite-sample allowance (about six points at n = 20); or the exchangeability alarm fires and a
band is issued anyway; or a band is issued for a design that was never built. Its arms: uncalibrated simulation
with randomization ranges; measuring the target directly. Its control: a fixture with an omitted law that
passes every in-family residual check and reverses the decision must trigger the alarm or a refusal. Its
fallback: `reality: no-claim`.

The North-Star scenario this serves: bench measurements of one quadruped on one floor are imported with their
provenance; predictions for the held-out episodes are frozen before the comparison; Ground reports a cost of
transport of 0.62 with a band of [0.55, 0.71] at 90% nominal coverage from 20 calibration episodes and states
that coverage at this sample size varies by about six points; the same question about a design that was never
built returns a refusal that names the missing calibrated family (illustrative numbers).

## Scope

In: the record's two forms and their content; the procedure by which the first real cell is taken through the
ten steps; the reading of the bet; the gate row; the script that produces the no-claim form. Out: every
mechanism the procedure uses (the beads in `deps`); the optional bench packages that might supply data
(N30b-38 to N30b-40); the decision on which dataset is admitted (N30b-25).

## Technical approach

1. **Two forms of one record**, `docs/delivery/reality-lane.toml`, schema `frob.ground.lane/1`, cited by the
   row of GATE-GROUND-10 in `registries/gates.toml`. The conformance crate has no binary and no
   `frob-conformance record …` command, so the record is written by the lane's own code and the row is
   entered by the gate recorder, in two steps:
   - *Write.* The library function `frob_conformance::ground_lane::write(store, out)` assembles form A or
     form B from the Ground records of a store and writes the TOML file. It is called by the gate's entry in
     a tier target, the one form in which a gate is run: `FROB_TEST_SEED=1 cargo test -p frob-conformance
     --test g2_parity -- GATE-GROUND-10`, against the state root in force (the one the end-to-end helper
     exports, in a script). The run writes the tier's hash-named receipt under `target/gauntlet/receipts/`,
     whose line for GATE-GROUND-10 says `pass` (form A) or `no-claim` with the reason (form B). DESIGN: the
     plan names no tier for a Reality gate; G2 is chosen because it is the tier of comparisons with a
     reference outside the program (pinned oracles, incumbent simulators, the published anchors of this
     subsystem), and a real dataset is such a reference. Rejected: a test target of the lane's own (an earlier
     text of this bead ran `--test ground_lane -- GATE-GROUND-10`; only the six tier targets run a gate and
     write a receipt the recorder accepts, and the script helper `e2e_tier` takes no other target).
   - *Record.* A session that did not implement the subsystem enters the row with the recorder's example
     target, `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-GROUND-10 --receipt <hash>`
     (N38b-19), which accepts the status `no-claim` only for a gate of kind `reality` and stores the reason
     as the row's `detail`. The receipt line `no-claim: <reason>` is written by this entry alone; that the
     tier runner and the recorder admit it for a `reality` gate is requested of N38a-02 and N38b-19.
   - **Form A, a real cell.** Fields: dataset identifier, licence class, origin (must be `real`), the cell key;
     clock-fit identifiers; calibration record identifiers by stage; candidate and admitted card versions;
     prediction identifiers with their freeze sequences; the sealed-read sequences; the score record;
     the band record (metric, n, level, q̂ with unit, draw type); monitor records armed, with their power
     facts; the expiry date and hours limit; the kit identifier, or the reason no kit could be built; the
     reality line as printed.
   - **Form B, a recorded no-claim.** Fields: `state = "no-claim"`; `reason`, one of `no-dataset-admitted`,
     `dataset-admitted-below-floor` (with n present and needed), `band-refused` (with the refusal code),
     `candidate-rejected` (with the score), `kill-fallback` (with the kill gate), `retired` (with the reason);
     the open questions at their defaults; what would change the state (the cheapest next acquisition, from
     the real-episode planner); the result of the rehearsal gates at the same commit, labelled `synthetic`.
2. **Gate row** (statuses of the gate registry, N40-01). Status `pass` only with form A whose dataset origin is
   `real`. While the dataset question is undecided the row reads `blocked` with detail `OPEN-GROUND-1`; once it
   is recorded, form B gives the terminal status `no-claim` (a Reality gate's resting state, not a pass) with
   `detail` set to the reason (`no-dataset-admitted` when no dataset exists). The rehearsal can never satisfy
   this gate, and three checks say so: the lane writer never writes form A for a dataset of origin
   `synthetic` (it writes form B, with the rehearsal's gate results labelled `synthetic`); the gate recorder
   refuses synthetic evidence for a `reality` gate with its reason `origin-not-admissible` (N38b-19); and a
   synthetic set relabelled `real` is stopped earlier, at admission, by its chunk hashes (FROB-REF-814).
3. **Procedure for the first real cell** (run once data is admitted; each step is a verb of the subsystem):
   0. `ground.ingest`: admit, assign the split, seal validation and holdout;
   1. clocks fitted from shared marks, inside ingest;
   2. `ground.excite`: identifiability of the recorded experiments; aliased combinations named;
   3. `ground.identify --stage actuators`;
   4. `ground.identify` for contact and surface, inertia and geometry, sensors, as the data allows;
   5. candidate card versions written;
   6. `ground.predict`: predictions frozen for the sealed trials;
   7. `ground.score`: sealed data opened once, scored, holdout retired, posterior admitted or rejected;
   8. `ground.band`: a band issued or refused for the cell;
   9. monitors armed and fed by every later ingest;
   10. discrepancy fed back: reality-gap variance for the cell, regression cases.
   Each step fails closed (refusal; no time-based score; no fit; prior card; candidate stays candidate;
   `no-claim`; `retired`), and the step at which the procedure stopped becomes the reason of form B.
4. **Reading the bet** (only in form A). Recorded beside the band: held-out coverage as a count against the
   finite-sample allowance (at n = 20 and 90% nominal, realized coverage has standard deviation 0.063 and falls
   below 0.80 with probability 0.069); whether any monitor fired before issue; the two arms on the same
   held-out episodes (interval width and coverage of uncalibrated simulation with randomization ranges; the
   direct measurement's interval); and the omitted-law control's outcome on the rehearsal at the same commit.
5. **Who records.** A session that did not implement the subsystem writes the record and the row, as for every
   gate.

Evidence: form A is a Reality claim of class Estimated, valid only inside its cell. Form B is a statement of
state.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-GROUND-10 (seed done-condition): a frozen prediction scored on sealed data with a band issued
      (form A), or a recorded `no-claim` with its reason (form B). At the M5 exit one of the two exists and the
      gate row cites it.
- [ ] In a store with no dataset, the lane writer (the `GATE-GROUND-10` entry of the conformance tier target
      `g2_parity`) writes form B with reason `no-dataset-admitted`, the defaults of the five open questions
      and the rehearsal gate results, and the run's receipt has the gate line `no-claim` with that reason;
      recorded from that receipt, the row reads status `no-claim` with `detail = "no-dataset-admitted"`
      (`gate_row_status_from_form`, through the recorder's library function on a scratch register).
- [ ] No script, document or test of this bead contains the command `frob-conformance record` (the lint of
      the conformance crate refuses it), and this bead's script runs no recorder (the harness lint
      `self-recording` passes on its log).
- [ ] The lane writer never writes form A for a dataset of origin `synthetic`, and a hand-made form-A receipt
      on synthetic evidence is refused by the gate recorder with `origin-not-admissible`.
- [ ] Form A validates only if the freeze sequence of every prediction precedes its sealed-read sequence, the
      band's n is at least 19 and the cell of every listed record is the same.
- [ ] Form B's reason matches the step at which the procedure stopped on six planted stops (one per reason).
- [ ] The record is cited by the M5 exit review, and the README's status table shows the lane's state as
      `no-claim` or names its cell, never "passed" for form B.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/ground_lane.rs`: `form_b_no_dataset`; `form_b_each_reason` (six planted stops);
`form_a_requires_real_origin`; `form_a_synthetic_never_written` (a store holding only the rehearsal yields
form B); `synthetic_receipt_refused_origin_not_admissible` (through the recorder's library function);
`relabelled_synthetic_stopped_at_admission_814`; `receipt_line_matches_form` (A → `pass`; B → `no-claim` with
the reason); `form_a_freeze_before_read_checked`;
`form_a_single_cell_checked`; `gate_row_status_from_form` (A → `pass`; B → `no-claim` with its reason;
undecided dataset question → `blocked` with detail `OPEN-GROUND-1`); `status_table_never_says_pass_for_no_claim`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_first_record.sh`, header `bead=BD-397 milestone=M5 labels=ground,e2e,gate
gates=GATE-GROUND-10 seed=1 needs=cpu plants=form-a-on-synthetic,pass-for-form-b`. It sources
`scripts/e2e/lib.sh`, runs in the run's own state root and never runs the gate recorder: a script produces a
receipt, and the row is entered afterwards by a recording session (the two recorder behaviours this lane
relies on, `no-claim` with its detail and the refusal `origin-not-admissible`, are asserted by the unit tests
above through the recorder's library function on a scratch register). Steps: (0) `questions`: print the state
of OPEN-GROUND-1, -2 and -3 from the open-questions registry and, where no decision record exists, assert each
default (the stand-in labelled `synthetic`; no bench hardware; 90 days or 200 hours); (1) `empty`: from the
empty store, run the lane's tier entry, `e2e_tier g2_parity GATE-GROUND-10`: assert form B with reason
`no-dataset-admitted` and, with `e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`, the receipt's gate line `no-claim` with
that reason, never `pass`; (2) `rehearsal`: ingest the rehearsal with `frob ground ingest … --key "$(e2e_key
ingest-<n>)"` and run the ten-step procedure through the seven verbs, every `run` and `mutate` call with its
own key: assert `reality: synthetic` on every output; (3) `still-b`: run the tier entry again: assert that it
still writes form B, that the rehearsal's gate results in it are labelled `synthetic`, and that the receipt
line is still `no-claim`; (4) `relabel`: a rehearsal bundle relabelled `real` with its id recomputed is
refused at admission (`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-814 3`), so no form A can be built on it; (5) `real`: if
the environment variable `GROUND_REAL_DATASET` names an admitted real bundle, run the procedure on it and the
tier entry, asserting form A and the receipt line `pass`; otherwise `e2e_skip` with the reason "no real
dataset admitted". The two plants: a lane writer that writes form A for a synthetic dataset (step 3 fails),
and a writer whose receipt line says `pass` for form B (step 1 fails). Log
`target/e2e-logs/ground_first_record/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it: per command a `cmd`
line (exact command, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block and `reality` text, and for the tier
runs the receipt hash); `assert` lines for the form, the reason and the receipt line, each with expected and
observed; a `refusal` line for step 4 with its code and repairs; the identifiers and hashes written (record,
receipt); on failure the first differing field and the paths of the record and the receipt; a final `summary`
with assertions passed, failed and skipped, `gates: ["GATE-GROUND-10"]` and the state. No content of a real
dataset is ever written to the log; identifiers and counts only.

### Known answers and negative controls

Negative controls: a lane writer that writes form A for a synthetic dataset; a recorder build with the
origin check removed (`synthetic_receipt_refused_origin_not_admissible` then records a pass and fails); a
status generator that prints "pass" for form B; a form A assembled from records of two cells. The omitted-law control of the bet (a probe correct in 675 of
675 in-class cases and 0 of 225 on the omitted law) is run on the rehearsal and its outcome is part of both
forms.

## Considerations

- Open questions bearing on this record. OPEN-GROUND-1: which public paired simulation-and-reality dataset, if
  any, has enough robots per class to test exchangeability across units; none was identified; default, the
  second-simulator stand-in labelled `synthetic`. OPEN-GROUND-2: whether any bench hardware is acquired;
  default none. OPEN-GROUND-3: expiry by age and hours; default 90 days or 200 operating hours. All three have
  decision beads; this record lists their state.
- Ground work beyond the rehearsal waits if no dataset has been admitted. That is a stop rule for effort, not
  a failure of the milestone.
- One real cell supports one sentence about one robot on one surface in one task family. The record's first
  line says so.
- If a kill gate of the subsystem has fired, form A can still exist in reduced form (for example bands on
  direct measurements with prior cards, or time-free metrics only); the record names the fallbacks in force.
