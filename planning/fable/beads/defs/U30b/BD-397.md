## Background

Every other gate of Ground (the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality) is a Known-answer
gate decided on a synthetic rehearsal. One gate is different. GATE-GROUND-10 is the only gate in the subsystem
whose claim kind is Reality: the first real cell, with a frozen prediction scored on sealed data and a band
issued. It is an import lane: it blocks nothing, it is tied to no milestone, and its resting state is
`no-claim`. A program rule explains the form: a gate needs only what a simulator has, so a gate that needs a
physical board or a bench measurement is written as an import lane with a recorded `no-claim` until the data
exists.

The M5 exit is defined in part by this bead: "the Reality lane has a record or a recorded no-claim". Where no
data was admitted, the line reads `no-claim`, and that is recorded as the state and not as a pass. The
pre-mortem of the program lists "calibration data never arrived" as a story with a countermeasure, which is
this lane: a synthetic rehearsal, frozen predictions, a published-anchor comparison at M3, bench packages that
may be sourced from M0, and a resting state that blocks nothing. The residual is stated plainly: the Reality
claim stays empty.

When data does arrive, this bead is also where the bet behind the subsystem is read. BET-28: within one
calibrated robot and task family, conformal bands on the gap between simulation and measurement reach their
stated coverage. Its falsifier: on held-out real episodes in the family, empirical coverage misses nominal by
more than the finite-sample allowance (about six points at n = 20); or the exchangeability alarm fires and a
band is issued anyway; or a band is issued for a design that was never built. Its arms: uncalibrated simulation
with randomization ranges; measuring the target directly. Its control: a fixture with an omitted law that
passes every in-family residual check and reverses the decision must trigger the alarm or a refusal. Its
fallback: `reality: no-claim`.

The North-Star scenario this serves: bench measurements of one quadruped on one floor are imported with their
provenance; predictions for the held-out episodes are frozen before the comparison; Ground reports a cost of
transport of 0.62 with a band of [0.55, 0.71] at 90% nominal coverage from 20 calibration episodes and states
that coverage at this sample size varies by about six points; the same question about a design that was never
built returns a refusal that names the missing calibrated family (illustrative numbers).

## Scope

In: the record's two forms and their content; the procedure by which the first real cell is taken through the
ten steps; the reading of the bet; the gate row; the script that produces the no-claim form. Out: every
mechanism the procedure uses (the beads in `deps`); the optional bench packages that might supply data
(N30b-38 to N30b-40); the decision on which dataset is admitted (N30b-25).

## Technical approach

1. **Two forms of one record**, `docs/delivery/reality-lane.toml`, schema `frob.ground.lane/1`, written by
   `frob-conformance record ground.lane` and cited by the row of GATE-GROUND-10 in `registries/gates.toml`.
   - **Form A, a real cell.** Fields: dataset identifier, licence class, origin (must be `real`), the cell key;
     clock-fit identifiers; calibration record identifiers by stage; candidate and admitted card versions;
     prediction identifiers with their freeze sequences; the sealed-read sequences; the score record;
     the band record (metric, n, level, q̂ with unit, draw type); monitor records armed, with their power
     facts; the expiry date and hours limit; the kit identifier, or the reason no kit could be built; the
     reality line as printed.
   - **Form B, a recorded no-claim.** Fields: `state = "no-claim"`; `reason`, one of `no-dataset-admitted`,
     `dataset-admitted-below-floor` (with n present and needed), `band-refused` (with the refusal code),
     `candidate-rejected` (with the score), `kill-fallback` (with the kill gate), `retired` (with the reason);
     the open questions at their defaults; what would change the state (the cheapest next acquisition, from
     the real-episode planner); the result of the rehearsal gates at the same commit, labelled `synthetic`.
2. **Gate row.** Status `pass` only with form A whose dataset origin is `real`. With form B the status is
   `not run` with `state = "no-claim"` and the reason, or `blocked: OPEN-GROUND-1` while the dataset question
   is undecided. A form-A record whose dataset is `synthetic` is refused by the recorder (FROB-REF-814): the
   rehearsal can never satisfy this gate.
3. **Procedure for the first real cell** (run once data is admitted; each step is a verb of the subsystem):
   0. `ground.ingest`: admit, assign the split, seal validation and holdout;
   1. clocks fitted from shared marks, inside ingest;
   2. `ground.excite`: identifiability of the recorded experiments; aliased combinations named;
   3. `ground.identify --stage actuators`;
   4. `ground.identify` for contact and surface, inertia and geometry, sensors, as the data allows;
   5. candidate card versions written;
   6. `ground.predict`: predictions frozen for the sealed trials;
   7. `ground.score`: sealed data opened once, scored, holdout retired, posterior admitted or rejected;
   8. `ground.band`: a band issued or refused for the cell;
   9. monitors armed and fed by every later ingest;
   10. discrepancy fed back: reality-gap variance for the cell, regression cases.
   Each step fails closed (refusal; no time-based score; no fit; prior card; candidate stays candidate;
   `no-claim`; `retired`), and the step at which the procedure stopped becomes the reason of form B.
4. **Reading the bet** (only in form A). Recorded beside the band: held-out coverage as a count against the
   finite-sample allowance (at n = 20 and 90% nominal, realized coverage has standard deviation 0.063 and falls
   below 0.80 with probability 0.069); whether any monitor fired before issue; the two arms on the same
   held-out episodes (interval width and coverage of uncalibrated simulation with randomization ranges; the
   direct measurement's interval); and the omitted-law control's outcome on the rehearsal at the same commit.
5. **Who records.** A session that did not implement the subsystem writes the record and the row, as for every
   gate.

Evidence: form A is a Reality claim of class Estimated, valid only inside its cell. Form B is a statement of
state.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-GROUND-10 (seed done-condition): a frozen prediction scored on sealed data with a band issued
      (form A), or a recorded `no-claim` with its reason (form B). At the M5 exit one of the two exists and the
      gate row cites it.
- [ ] In a store with no dataset, `frob-conformance record ground.lane` writes form B with reason
      `no-dataset-admitted`, the defaults of the five open questions and the rehearsal gate results.
- [ ] The recorder refuses form A for a dataset of origin `synthetic`.
- [ ] Form A validates only if the freeze sequence of every prediction precedes its sealed-read sequence, the
      band's n is at least 19 and the cell of every listed record is the same.
- [ ] Form B's reason matches the step at which the procedure stopped on six planted stops (one per reason).
- [ ] The record is cited by the M5 exit review, and the README's status table shows the lane's state as
      `no-claim` or names its cell, never "passed" for form B.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/ground_lane.rs`: `form_b_no_dataset`; `form_b_each_reason` (six planted stops);
`form_a_requires_real_origin`; `form_a_synthetic_refused_814`; `form_a_freeze_before_read_checked`;
`form_a_single_cell_checked`; `gate_row_status_from_form` (A → pass; B → not run with state; undecided dataset
question → blocked); `status_table_never_says_pass_for_no_claim`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_first_record.sh`, header `bead=BD-397 milestone=M5 labels=ground,e2e,gate seed=1`. It
sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh`, starts from an empty store, writes form B and asserts its reason; then ingests the
rehearsal, runs the ten-step procedure and asserts that the recorder still refuses form A (origin synthetic)
while printing `reality: synthetic` on every output. If the environment variable `GROUND_REAL_DATASET` names an
admitted real bundle, it runs the procedure on it and writes form A. Log:
`target/e2e-logs/ground_first_record/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`, with per step the command, exit code, the
evidence block, the identifiers written, and a summary with `gates: ["GATE-GROUND-10"]` and the state. No
content of a real dataset is ever written to the log; identifiers and counts only.

### Known answers and negative controls

Negative controls: a recorder that accepts a synthetic form A; a status generator that prints "pass" for form
B; a form A assembled from records of two cells. The omitted-law control of the bet (a probe correct in 675 of
675 in-class cases and 0 of 225 on the omitted law) is run on the rehearsal and its outcome is part of both
forms.

## Considerations

- Open questions bearing on this record. OPEN-GROUND-1: which public paired simulation-and-reality dataset, if
  any, has enough robots per class to test exchangeability across units; none was identified; default, the
  second-simulator stand-in labelled `synthetic`. OPEN-GROUND-2: whether any bench hardware is acquired;
  default none. OPEN-GROUND-3: expiry by age and hours; default 90 days or 200 operating hours. All three have
  decision beads; this record lists their state.
- Ground work beyond the rehearsal waits if no dataset has been admitted. That is a stop rule for effort, not
  a failure of the milestone.
- One real cell supports one sentence about one robot on one surface in one task family. The record's first
  line says so.
- If a kill gate of the subsystem has fired, form A can still exist in reduced form (for example bands on
  direct measurements with prior cards, or time-free metrics only); the record names the fallbacks in force.
