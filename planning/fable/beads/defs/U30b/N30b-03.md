## Background

When Ground identifies actuator parameters from a log it must leave a receipt from which a third party can see
what was fitted, on what, from which prior, and how well, without rerunning anything. That receipt is the
**calibration record**. It is what a card version cites as its origin, what a replication kit ships, and what a
reviewer reads when a band is questioned.

The record also carries the one independent cross-check of the fit. A model can fit nominal motion for the
wrong physical reason: current draw and mechanical work disagree while speed looks right. The standard example
is a torque constant that is 20% too high compensated by friction that is 20% too high; steady speed is matched
exactly and every trajectory residual is small. The energy ledger sees it, because the two errors do not cancel
in energy: the model then claims more friction dissipation than the electrical input can pay for. Ground
therefore evaluates the program's energy ledger (the per-step account that electrical energy in equals
mechanical work out plus copper, friction and gear losses plus the change in stored energy, with the numerical
residual reported as its own line) on the measured log. A fit whose electrical-to-mechanical energy residual
exceeds the instrument bound is marked `wrong-reason` and cannot be admitted. This is the countermeasure named
for risk RISK-42 (a model fits nominal motion for the wrong physical reason).

Rejected alternatives: trusting a small trajectory misfit (it is exactly what the compensated fit achieves);
adding energy as one more term in the fit's objective (the check then stops being independent of the fit).

## Scope

In: the record's schema, identity and ledger event; misfit and residual autocorrelation; the sibling receipts it
cites; the energy cross-check, its instrument bound and the label; the rule that a `wrong-reason` record cannot
be admitted. Out: the passes that fill it (BD-391, N30b-02); card versions written from it (N30b-04); admission
at scoring (N30b-06), which consults the label.

## Technical approach

1. **Type** (DESIGN sketch; fixes intent, not signatures):

   ```rust
   /// What `ground.identify` writes. A fit whose electrical-to-mechanical energy residual exceeds
   /// the instrument bound is marked wrong-reason and cannot be admitted.
   pub struct CalibrationRecord {
       dataset: DatasetId,                // a `.frun` bundle with licence, rights and splits
       cell: CellKey,                     // unit, surface, envelope: one robot calibrates one robot
       prior: CardVersion,
       windows: WindowDef,                // traces enter as windows of summary features
       passes: Vec<Pass>,                 // A linear; B output error; C nonsmooth parameters, delay profiled
       posterior: Posterior,              // mean, joint covariance, delay profile with its 95% set
       misfit: (f64, f64),                // before and after
       residual_autocorrelation: Vec<f64>,     // at control-rate lags
       energy_check: EnergyCheck,         // the energy ledger evaluated on the real log
       discrepancy_floor: Variance,       // never reported below the model-form variance
       status: CardStatus,                // Candidate until a frozen prediction covers on sealed data
       labels: Vec<GroundLabel>,          // e.g. WrongReason, RankingFalsified
   }
   pub enum CardStatus { Candidate, Admitted, Rejected }
   ```
   Serialized as `frob.ground.calibration/1` in canonical form; its identifier is the BLAKE3 hash of that form.
   Also stored: the stage, the clock-fit identifiers used, the identifiability report identifier, the hashes of
   the sibling's audit receipts (contraction receipt, standardized-innovation gate, robust-fit audit), the stack
   pin, the machine fingerprint, and `origin` of the dataset.
2. **Misfit.** ½·rᵀ·R⁻¹·r over the pass-B feature residuals r with instrument covariance R, once with the prior
   parameters and once with the posterior.
3. **Residual autocorrelation.** For each measured channel, the sample autocorrelation ρ(k) of the residuals at
   lags k = 1 … 32 control periods (TARGET). Any |ρ(k)| above 2/√n (n residuals) raises the finding
   `residual-structure`: the model leaves something periodic at the control rate unexplained. A finding, not a
   refusal.
4. **Energy cross-check.** Over each recorded experiment of duration T:
   - electrical energy in, measured: E_el = ∫ v·i dt (bus or winding voltage v, current i);
   - model-side account with the fitted parameters on the measured motion: mechanical work delivered
     W = ∫ τ_load·q̇ dt (τ_load from the torque sensor when present, otherwise from the known payload and
     gravity), copper loss ∫ i²·R dt, friction dissipation ∫ τ_f(q̇)·q̇ dt + ∫ b·q̇² dt, gear loss, and the change
     in stored energy Δ[½·(I + J_a)·q̇²] + Δ[½·L·i²] + stored heat is counted once, as stored energy;
   - residual ρ_E = E_el − (W + losses + Δ stored);
   - instrument bound B_E = ∫ (|i|·β_v + |v|·β_i) dt + 3·√(Σ (i²·σ_v² + v²·σ_i²)·Δt²) + the same two terms for
     the mechanical channels, with β the `bias_bound` and σ the `sigma` of each instrument in the manifest.
   - `EnergyCheck { e_el, model_total, residual, bound, per_term, verdict }` with verdict `consistent` when
     |ρ_E| ≤ B_E, `wrong-reason` otherwise, and `not-evaluable` when the dataset has no voltage or bus-power
     channel.
5. **Consequences.** `wrong-reason` adds the label `WrongReason`; admission at scoring refuses to move such a
   record's cards to `admitted` whatever the score says, and the cards stay `candidate`. `not-evaluable` adds
   the flag `energy-unchecked`, which travels on the card fields and is printed wherever they are cited.
6. **Ledger.** Event `ground.calibration.recorded` with the record identifier, dataset, cell and stage. The
   record is immutable; a refit is a new record that names its predecessor.

Determinism: assembling the record and the energy check are class D2. Evidence class Estimated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every field of the type is present in a record written by `ground.identify` on the rehearsal, and the
      record's identifier recomputes from its canonical bytes.
- [ ] On the wrong-reason fixture (steady speed matched by a torque-constant scale of 1.20 with friction raised
      to compensate) the verdict is `wrong-reason` and a later covering score does not admit the cards.
- [ ] On the nominal rig the verdict is `consistent`, with |ρ_E| below the bound on every experiment.
- [ ] A dataset with no voltage channel gives `not-evaluable` and the `energy-unchecked` flag, never
      `consistent`.
- [ ] Changing the window definition, the prior or one sibling receipt changes the record identifier.
- [ ] The record resolves in the artifact graph from any card version written from it.
- [ ] Contributes to GATE-GROUND-04: the wrong-reason row of the rehearsal is asserted here.

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/calibration_record.rs`: `record_roundtrip_canonical`; `identifier_changes_with_windows`;
`misfit_before_exceeds_after_on_planted_rig`; `autocorrelation_white_noise_within_bound`;
`autocorrelation_flags_planted_ripple` (a 1 kHz-locked ripple added to the encoder);
`energy_balance_hand_case` (a 10 s steady run at 2 A and 6 V with R = 1 Ω: E_el = 120 J, copper 40 J, friction
dissipation 80 J, residual 0);
`energy_compensated_fit_flagged` (the same run with friction 20% high: model total 136 J, residual −16 J, bound
about 2.4 J at 1% bias on current and voltage: `wrong-reason`);
`instrument_bound_scales_with_bias`; `no_voltage_is_not_evaluable`; `stored_heat_counted_once`;
`wrong_reason_blocks_admission` (with a covering score planted).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh` (bead N30b-32) prints for each rig the record identifier, misfit before and
after, the largest autocorrelation with its bound, and the energy residual against the instrument bound with
the verdict; on the wrong-reason fixture it asserts the label and the refused admission.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-727 (the compensated fit is marked `wrong-reason`). CX-332. Planted failures: a build in which admission
ignores the label (the compensated fit is admitted and the gate fails); an energy account that adds stored heat
both as loss and as stored energy (the nominal rig then shows a false residual); a bound computed from the
residual itself. Metamorphic relation: scaling every instrument's bias bound by two doubles the bias part of
B_E and leaves the residual unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release.

## Considerations

- DESIGN choice on `not-evaluable`: admission still rests on the sealed score, and the flag is printed. The
  stricter alternative (no admission without an electrical power channel) would make a stand that measures only
  current and encoder unusable; it is recorded here so the choice can be revisited when real data exists.
- The bound uses the worst case for bias and three standard deviations for noise; it is deliberately wide, so a
  `wrong-reason` label means a disagreement the instruments cannot explain.
- Mechanical work needs a known load: on a stand the payload is known; on a whole robot contact work is not
  measured, and the check is made per joint on unloaded segments only, the rest being `not-evaluable`.
- The record contains no raw samples, only identifiers, parameters and summaries, so it inherits the dataset's
  private mark by lineage and adds no private content of its own.
