## Background

An actuator card is immutable, content-addressed data: torque constant, resistance, rotor inertia, friction
terms, limits, thermal constants and more, where **every field carries a provenance** (`datasheet` with a
citation, `measured`, `generic` or `assumed`) and an uncertainty. Every simulation tier reads the same card, so
agreement between tiers says nothing about whether the card describes a real motor; the only evidence about the
card itself is measurement. This bead is where a measurement becomes a card.

Three decisions govern it. **ADR-GROUND-06:** a posterior is a new card version with provenance `measured`.
Cards are never mutated. A new card version is a new `ModelId` (the identity of the compiled model), and
evidence attached to the old identity does not carry over. **ADR-GROUND-07:** randomization width derived from a
card narrows only after validation, never from a fit alone, because shrinking a card without evidence
overstates what is known. **ADR-GROUND-02:** the unit of calibration is the cell (physical units, controller,
firmware, surface, task family, envelope), so one robot calibrates one robot.

Rejected alternatives: editing the card in place (every earlier result would silently change meaning);
storing only marginal uncertainties (armature and friction estimates are correlated, and randomization that
samples the marginals independently is wider than the evidence in some directions and narrower in others);
letting more samples shrink the posterior without limit (sampling error shrinks, model-form error does not);
treating one measured unit as the part class (one robot calibrates one robot).

## Scope

In: writing candidate card versions from a calibration record; per-field provenance, dataset hash, cell and
marginal uncertainty; the joint covariance block and correlated draws; the candidate and admitted states and
the width rule for randomization; the discrepancy floor; per-unit posteriors and hierarchical part-class cards.
Out: producing the posterior (BD-391, N30b-02); the decision to admit, which scoring takes (N30b-06); the card
schema itself (BD-069); stage-specific fields of interface, material and sensor cards (N30b-05, which uses this
bead's writer).

## Technical approach

1. **Writer.** `frob_ground::card_update::write_candidate(record) → Vec<CardVersionId>`. For each card touched:
   copy the prior version; for every identified field set the value to the posterior mean, the uncertainty to
   the reported standard deviation (step 4), the provenance to `measured`, and attach `dataset` (hash), `cell`
   (key hash), `calibration` (record identifier) and `state = "candidate"`. Fields not identified keep their
   prior value and provenance. For a dataset of origin `synthetic` the field carries `origin = "synthetic"`
   beside the hash and counts as guessed in the share of guessed fields printed on reports.
2. **Joint covariance.** The card stores, beside the fields, one block `covariance = { fields = [...], matrix =
   [...] }` over the identified fields of that card (delay excluded: it carries its profile set). Correlated
   draws for randomization and for uncertainty propagation use a Gaussian copula over the stated marginals
   (`fs-uq::product_copula`, FACT); independent marginal sampling of a card that has a covariance block is a
   lint error in the randomization compiler.
3. **Candidate, then admitted.** `ground.identify` writes `candidate`. A version becomes `admitted` only when
   `ground.score` has scored a frozen prediction made with it on sealed data and the registered interval
   covered. Admission writes a further version that differs only in `state` and in the score's identifier.
   Randomization for training keeps the **prior** width until then: the randomization compiler, asked for the
   width of a field, returns the prior card's uncertainty unless the version is `admitted` and its cell is not
   retired (invariant INV-GROUND-05: randomization width from a card does not narrow without an admitted
   validation).
4. **Discrepancy floor.** The posterior variance of a field is never reported below the variance that
   validation residuals attribute to model form. The floor is the common-mode term of
   `fs-assimilate::groups`, estimated on validation data and stored in the calibration record:
   reported variance = max(posterior variance, floor). Before any validation exists the floor is the prior's
   model-form term from the card class (so a candidate is never tighter than the class admits). More samples
   shrink sampling error and leave the floor.
5. **Unit and class.** A per-unit posterior describes that unit's left knee and is keyed by the unit in its
   cell. A part-class card is hierarchical: a class mean μ and a unit-to-unit spread s². With k units measured,
   each with estimate θ̂_u and reported variance v_u: μ̂ is the precision-weighted mean of the θ̂_u; the spread is
   estimated on k − 1 degrees of freedom as ŝ² = max(0, Σ (θ̂_u − μ̂)²/(k − 1) − mean(v_u)). With one unit
   measured the spread stays at its prior. A draw for an unmeasured unit of the class uses variance
   ŝ² + (variance of μ̂).
6. **Identity.** A new card version has a new content address, so every model that binds it has a new
   `ModelId`; nothing attached to the old identity (fidelity records, verdicts, bands) is copied. The
   revalidation engine is told which fields changed.
7. **Invariant INV-GROUND-02** (a card is never mutated; a posterior is a new version with provenance and
   dataset hash): the writer takes the prior by reference and returns new objects; the store refuses a write
   whose address exists with other bytes.

Determinism: class D0 given the record (pure construction). Evidence class of every written field: Estimated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After `ground.identify`, the prior card's content address is unchanged and still resolves; each new
      version carries `measured`, the dataset hash, the cell, the record identifier and `state = "candidate"`.
- [ ] A candidate posterior leaves the training manifest unchanged: the randomization block compiled before
      and after identification has identical bytes; after admission the width of the admitted fields narrows.
- [ ] The reported variance of a field equals the floor when the posterior variance is below it, on a fixture
      with 1,000 windows sharing one bias.
- [ ] Correlated draws reproduce the stored correlation of armature and viscous friction within sampling error
      on 10⁴ draws; independent sampling of that card is rejected by the lint.
- [ ] With one unit the class spread equals its prior; with five planted units the estimated spread is within
      its own interval of the planted one.
- [ ] A model compiled with the new version has a different `ModelId`, and a band attached to the old identity
      is not offered for the new one.
- [ ] A card version identified on the rehearsal counts as guessed in the guess share.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/card_update.rs`: `prior_address_unchanged`; `fields_marked_measured_with_dataset`;
`unidentified_fields_keep_provenance`; `covariance_block_roundtrip`; `copula_draws_match_correlation` (planted
correlation −0.6 between J_a and b; sample correlation within 0.03 on 10⁴ draws);
`independent_sampling_is_lint_error`; `candidate_keeps_prior_width`; `admitted_narrows_width`;
`retired_cell_reverts_to_prior_width`; `floor_applies` (posterior variance 10⁻⁸, floor 10⁻⁶: reported 10⁻⁶);
`one_unit_spread_is_prior`; `k_units_spread_on_k_minus_1` (k = 5, planted spread 0.02 N·m on τ_c);
`spread_never_negative`; `new_version_new_model_id`; `store_refuses_address_collision`;
`synthetic_origin_counts_as_guessed`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh` (bead N30b-32) records the content address of every bound card before and
after `frob ground identify`, asserts equality of the old addresses, lists the new versions with their states,
and compares the training manifest's randomization digest before and after (digest equality is the assertion
for INV-GROUND-05).

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-724 (the recovered values are what the candidate cards hold). CX-G-09 (independent marginals), CX-G-10 (the
floor). Planted failures: a writer that overwrites the prior in place (content addresses differ before and
after: caught); a randomization compiler that reads `candidate` widths (the manifest digest changes: caught);
a floor of zero (the bias fixture reports an impossible precision). Metamorphic relation: permuting the order
of identified fields permutes the covariance block and changes no draw's joint law. Determinism lanes: twice on
one machine; debug and release; across platforms identical bytes for the written cards.

## Considerations

- The share of guessed fields falls only for cards identified on data of origin `real`; the rehearsal can never
  lower it.
- A class card built from two units has one degree of freedom for its spread; the interval is wide and is
  printed. The report says how many units stand behind a class card.
- Admission is per cell. The same card version may be `admitted` in one cell and unknown in another; consumers
  ask with a cell.
- If the kill gate on identification fires (identification withdrawn), this writer remains for direct bench
  measurements: a field then changes only by a direct measurement of that field, written with the same
  provenance and state rules.
