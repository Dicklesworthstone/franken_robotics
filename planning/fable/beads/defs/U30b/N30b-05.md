## Background

Ground identifies a robot in a fixed order (decision ADR-GROUND-03): (0) clocks, (1) actuators, (2) contact and
surface, (3) inertia and geometry, (4) sensors. The order is fixed because each stage's error aliases into the
next: a contact parameter fitted before the actuator is right absorbs the actuator's error and then predicts
the wrong thing on the next surface. Stage 1 and the verb are in BD-391. This bead supplies the three later
stages of `ground.identify --stage contact|inertia|sensors`. They are simpler than stage 1, because most of
their experiments measure the target almost directly, and that is deliberate: where measuring the decision
quantity directly costs about what probing a parameter costs, Ground recommends the direct measurement.

Where the results go: friction, dissipation and rolling resistance are fields of **interface cards**, keyed by
material pair and condition; mass, centre of mass and inertia are parameters of the design file and of material
cards (density); bias, noise and latency are fields of **sensor cards**. All are written as new card versions
with provenance `measured`, state `candidate`, through the writer of N30b-04, and all fail closed to the prior
card.

Rejected alternative: one joint fit of everything from whole-robot logs. It is exactly the fit in which the
eight structural aliases live, and its posterior is dominated by whichever stage has the weakest prior.

## Scope

In: measurement models and estimators for the three stages; the stage gate; refusals for the aliases that
belong to these stages; candidate versions of interface, material and sensor cards. Out: the identifiability
analysis and the experiment grammar (BD-390); the as-built route to geometry (BD-395, N30b-11); the
exposure-delay alias on a straight pass (N30b-29); the pose-graph calibration of sensor extrinsics, which
belongs to the sensing subsystem.

## Technical approach

1. **Stage gate.** A stage runs only when the cell holds a calibration record of the stage before it; otherwise
   the request is refused with FROB-REF-879, pointer `stage`. Each stage writes its own calibration record
   (schema `frob.ground.calibration/1`, field `stage`), with the earlier stages' records as inputs.
2. **Stage 2, contact and surface.**
   - *Incline to first slip:* static friction μ_s = tan θ_slip, with σ_μ = σ_θ/cos² θ_slip.
   - *Sled drag at two normal loads:* F = μ_k·N + F₀; from loads N₁ ≠ N₂, μ_k = (F₂ − F₁)/(N₂ − N₁) and
     F₀ = F₁ − μ_k·N₁. At least two normal loads are required per material pair; one load is refused with
     FROB-REF-879 (μ_k and F₀ are one number).
   - *Driven traction runs:* with drawbar force F = min(k_t·I/r, μ·N), runs in the torque-limited regime carry
     no information on μ (zero sensitivity); a value of μ from such runs alone is refused with 879 and the
     repair "excite the traction-limited regime" (structural alias 6).
   - *Drop from two heights:* rebound ratio e = √(h_rebound/h_drop). One drop height gives one rebound ratio, so
     contact stiffness and damping are aliased (alias 8); two heights, or a force trace (contact duration gives
     the natural frequency), separate them. For a linear spring and damper, ζ = −ln e/√(π² + ln² e).
   - *Coast-down:* rolling resistance from the velocity-independent part of the deceleration, a = −C_rr·g −
     (b/m)·v.
   - *Whole-robot refinement:* with actuators identified, an output-error fit (pass B of BD-391) over contact
     parameters only, actuator fields held at their stage-1 posterior with its covariance carried as a shared
     nuisance.
3. **Stage 3, inertia and geometry.** Mass from a scale. Centre of mass from a two-support balance:
   x = W₂·L/(W₁ + W₂) from support 1, with support readings W₁, W₂ and span L. Inertia about the vertical axis
   through the centre of mass from a bifilar pendulum: I = m·g·d²·T²/(16·π²·ℓ), with filament separation d,
   filament length ℓ and period T. Link lengths and axis offsets from as-built scans. Each is a linear or
   linearized Gaussian observation fused with `fs-assimilate`'s sequential Kalman update through its
   dimensionally typed front door; the result is a posterior on design parameters with units checked.
4. **Stage 4, sensors.** Bias: mean of readings over a declared stationary interval, with the instrument's
   shared calibration error entered as a common-mode source. Noise: the standard deviation over the same
   interval and its spectrum at the sensor rate. Latency: from a step with a shared hardware mark; without the
   mark only "latency plus clock offset" is identified and the card's latency is not updated (879). A range or
   vision sensor's exposure delay against its mount offset follows the ninth alias (N30b-29).
5. **Estimator rule.** Direct measurements go through the linear path; nothing in these stages is regularized
   toward the prior without being reported as prior. Every stage reports, per field, whether the value came
   from a direct measurement, from an output-error fit, or stayed at the prior.
6. **Cards.** Candidate versions through `card_update::write_candidate`; interface cards are keyed by material
   pair and condition (a friction value measured dry does not update the wet card).

Determinism: class D2. Evidence class Estimated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `--stage contact` without a stage-1 record in the cell is refused; with it, the stage writes a record
      whose inputs name the stage-1 record.
- [ ] The hand cases below reproduce to the printed digits through the verb on `synthetic` bench bundles.
- [ ] One normal load, one drop height, torque-limited runs only, and a latency step with no shared mark are
      each refused with FROB-REF-879, each naming the identified combination and the separating experiment.
- [ ] A dry-surface measurement leaves the wet interface card's content address unchanged.
- [ ] Every written field carries `measured`, the dataset hash, the cell and state `candidate`; fields that
      stayed at the prior say so.
- [ ] Units are checked: a period entered in milliseconds without a unit is refused by the typed front door.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/stages.rs`: `incline_slip` (θ = 21.80°: μ_s = 0.400);
`sled_two_loads` (N = 30 and 40 N, F = 12.5 and 16.5 N: μ_k = 0.400, F₀ = 0.5 N);
`sled_one_load_refused_879`; `torque_limited_runs_refuse_mu` (1 A and 2 A under 30 N and 40 N: 3.33 N and 6.67 N
for μ = 0.4 and 0.8; refused), `traction_limited_run_separates` (3 A under 10 N: 4 N against 8 N);
`rebound_ratio` (0.100 m drop, 0.049 m rebound: e = 0.700, ζ = 0.1128); `one_drop_height_refused_879`;
`two_support_balance` (12 N and 8 N over 0.5 m: 0.200 m); `bifilar_inertia` (m = 2 kg, d = 0.2 m, ℓ = 1.0 m,
T = 1.2 s, g = 9.80665 m/s²: I = 7.154 × 10⁻³ kg·m²); `sensor_bias_keeps_common_mode_floor`;
`latency_without_mark_reports_sum`; `stage_gate_enforced`; `condition_keyed_cards`;
`provenance_reports_prior_fields`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh` (bead N30b-32) runs the three stages after stage 1 on the rehearsal's bench
bundles and logs each field's value, source (direct, fit or prior), interval and state, and each refusal with
its combination and separating experiment.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-723 (friction against the torque limit: 3.33 N and 6.67 N indistinguishable; 4 N and 8 N separated).
CX-329; aliases 6, 7 and 8 of CX-328; CX-G-08 (order). Planted failures: a stage-2 fit run with a deliberately
wrong actuator card and no stage gate (the friction estimate absorbs the error: the control shows the reason
for the order); a sled fit that reports μ_k from one load. Metamorphic relations: adding a constant to both sled
forces changes F₀ only; doubling both drop heights with the same rebound ratio leaves ζ unchanged. Determinism
lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release.

## Considerations

- These stages have no gate of their own; they are exercised by the rehearsal's rows and by bench packages B
  and D if those ever run. Their absence from a calibration is visible: fields stay `generic` or `assumed` and
  the guess share says so.
- Friction is a property of a pair and a condition. A single coefficient "for the robot" is not a card field.
- A bifilar pendulum measures one axis per hang; three hangs give the diagonal, and products of inertia need
  the as-built route or oblique hangs. The record states which components were measured.
- Stage 4 does not replace the sensing subsystem's own calibration of extrinsics; it supplies bias, noise and
  latency fields and defers geometry between sensors.
