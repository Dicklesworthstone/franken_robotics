## Background

A conformal reality band (BD-393) covers at its stated level only for draws that are exchangeable with the
draws it was calibrated on. Everything in this bead follows from taking that condition seriously. A band lives
in a cell: (design, set of physical units, controller, firmware, surface class, task family, envelope box).

**The exchangeable unit is the robot, not the episode.** Twenty episodes on one machine are one cluster: they
share that machine's bearings, its encoder offsets, its particular motor. For the same unit in the same cell,
episodes are the draws. For a new unit of the same design, units are the draws. So a band that is meant to hold
for a unit nobody has measured needs calibrated units, not more episodes: at least 9 at 90%, 19 at 95%, 99 at
99%, and it is refused below that.

**Paired simulation buys little hardware time.** One study reports savings of 20 to 25% of hardware evaluation
effort for similar bounds, and that with low correlation between simulation and reality, real-only evaluation
gives the tightest bounds (https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04354). The ceiling of the benefit on real-trial count is
therefore about 1.3 (1/(1 − 0.25) = 1.33), stated before any test. Ground is an honesty mechanism: it has no
lever on time or compute.

**Many bands on one page.** A scorecard with twenty banded metrics, each at 90%, is expected to contain two
that miss. A reader who is not told reads all twenty as covered.

Rejected alternatives: counting episodes as draws for a new unit (the band would be calibrated on one machine's
idiosyncrasies and under-cover on the next); a per-metric level with no page-level accounting; hiding a low
sim-to-real correlation instead of recommending that the simulator be left out.

## Scope

In: the draw type of a band (episode or unit) and its minimum counts; the construction of an across-unit band;
the false-coverage budget of a scorecard; the correlation report and the real-only recommendation; the stated
ceiling. Out: the per-cell band itself and its verb (BD-393); the guard table that encodes the counts
(N30b-08); the experiment planner that prices real episodes (N30b-09).

## Technical approach

1. **Draw type.** Every band request and record carries `draw = "episode" | "unit"`.
   - `episode`: the cell's `units` component is one unit; the statement is about a further episode of that
     unit. Minimum count 19 pairs (TARGET).
   - `unit`: the statement is about an episode of a unit of this design that is not among the calibrated ones,
     in the same controller, firmware, surface, task family and envelope. The cell's `units` component is the
     set of calibrated units, and the minimum is ⌈(1 − α)/α⌉ units.
   A request for a unit that is not in any calibrated cell is a `unit` request, whatever the caller wrote.
2. **Across-unit band (DESIGN).** With k calibrated units, each holding several pairs, one pair per unit is
   selected by a counter-based draw keyed by (cell, metric, unit), giving k residuals that are exchangeable
   across units. q̂ is the ⌈(k + 1)·(1 − α)⌉-th smallest of them, infinite past k. Reason: reusing all episodes
   of all units would treat dependent residuals as independent draws; one keyed pick per unit keeps the
   coverage argument intact and cannot be steered, because the pick is a function of identities.
3. **Refusal.** Fewer units than the minimum: FROB-REF-889 (a band for an uncalibrated cell, or with too few
   calibration points) with units present and units needed; the first repair states the number of further
   units to calibrate.
4. **False-coverage budget.** A scorecard that will carry m banded metrics declares a budget α_F (default 0.10,
   TARGET) before any band is read. Each claim reserves its share up front through the sibling's
   `admission_alpha` (default equal shares α_F/m) and its band is issued at level 1 − α_k. A share too small
   for a finite band at the cell's n (α_k < 1/(n + 1)) gives an unbounded band, shown as unbounded, never as a
   narrower one. At n = 20 and α_F = 0.10 at most two metrics can carry finite bands under equal shares
   (m ≤ α_F·(n + 1) = 2.1). As later episodes arrive, each claim's misses become an e-value and violations are
   flagged by the e-Benjamini–Hochberg procedure through `fcr_flag`; a flagged claim is printed as flagged on
   every later rendering of the scorecard.
5. **Correlation report.** For each cell and metric the Pearson correlation ρ between ŷ_i and y_i over the pool,
   with its interval by the Fisher transform (z = atanh ρ, standard error 1/√(n − 3)). Printed by
   `ground.band --list` and `--status`. When ρ is below 0.5 (TARGET) the output carries the recommendation
   `real-only evaluation` for that metric: the simulated side adds width, not information, and the direct
   measurement is the better statement.
6. **Ceiling.** Every report that states a saving in real trials from pairing with simulation prints the
   ceiling 1.3 beside it and the measured ρ; a claimed saving above the ceiling is refused by the report lint.

Determinism: the keyed pick and the budget arithmetic are class D0; ρ is class D2. Evidence class Estimated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A band for a new unit with 3 units calibrated is refused with FROB-REF-889, payload "units present 3,
      units needed 9" at level 0.90; with 9 units it is issued, and its index is ⌈10 × 0.9⌉ = 9.
- [ ] Twenty episodes of one unit do not yield a band for another unit (the request is reclassified `unit`
      and refused).
- [ ] The per-unit pick is identical under any ordering of ingestion and any batch composition.
- [ ] A scorecard with five banded metrics at α_F = 0.10 and n = 20 shows five unbounded bands and says why;
      with two metrics it shows two finite bands at level 0.95.
- [ ] A planted claim that misses on later data is flagged by `fcr_flag`, and the flag persists.
- [ ] On a fixture with planted ρ = 0.3 the output recommends real-only evaluation; at ρ = 0.8 it does not.
- [ ] A report asserting a 2× saving in real trials from pairing is refused by the lint.
- [ ] Contributes to GATE-GROUND-06: the clause "zero bands for the short cell" includes the three-unit case.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/band_scope.rs`: `draw_type_from_request`; `new_unit_reclassified_as_unit_draw`;
`unit_minimums` (9, 19, 99 at 0.90, 0.95, 0.99); `three_units_refused_889`; `nine_units_index_9`;
`pick_per_unit_is_keyed` (permutation invariance); `pick_cannot_be_overridden` (no parameter selects the
episode); `budget_equal_shares` (m = 5, α_F = 0.10: α_k = 0.02, level 0.98, needs n ≥ 49);
`max_finite_metrics_at_n20_is_two`; `unbounded_is_reported_not_narrowed`; `fcr_flag_on_planted_miss`;
`fisher_interval` (ρ = 0.5, n = 20: z = 0.549, standard error 0.243, interval about [0.07, 0.77]);
`real_only_below_half`; `saving_above_ceiling_refused`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_band.sh` (bead N30b-34) requests a band for a new unit with three and with nine calibrated
units, builds a five-metric scorecard, and logs for each the draw type, counts present and needed, the reserved
shares, the levels issued, the flags and the measured ρ with its interval and the recommendation.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-729 (a new unit with 3 units calibrated: refused). CX-G-02. Planted failures: a build that counts episodes
as draws for a new unit (a band is issued from one unit's 20 episodes: caught); a scorecard that issues all
five metrics at 0.90 (page-level false coverage about 0.41 if independent, against a budget of 0.10); a
per-unit pick taken as the smallest residual (bands shrink, coverage across units falls). Metamorphic relation:
renaming units changes which pair is picked and never the count rules. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine;
debug and release.

## Considerations

- A rehearsal with nine or more synthetic units exercises the across-unit path; no real program in sight has
  nine instrumented units, so on real data this path will mostly refuse. That is the intended reading of the
  evidence, and open question OPEN-GROUND-1 records that no public paired dataset with enough robots per class
  was identified.
- The page-level arithmetic (1 − 0.9⁵ = 0.41) assumes independence only to show the size of the problem; the
  e-BH flag is valid under arbitrary dependence.
- A correlation near zero does not make the band wrong; it makes it as wide as the outcome's own spread. The
  recommendation says so in those words.
