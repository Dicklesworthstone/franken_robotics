## Background

Registering a scan to its design (BD-395) gives a transform, deviation fields and a few kinematic quantities
with their covariance. That is not yet something the simulator can run. This bead closes the as-built loop: it
turns measured geometry and a scale reading into posteriors on design parameters, compiles a model variant of
the robot as it was actually built, and audits each measured feature against the tolerance it was allocated.
The question it answers in one run is "does the built unit still pass?".

Principles carried from the rest of Ground (crate `frob-ground`, the subsystem that compares simulation with
measured reality). A posterior is a new version, never a mutation, and a new version is a new `ModelId`, so
evidence attached to the as-designed identity does not carry over (decision ADR-GROUND-06). One robot
calibrates one robot: the as-built variant describes that unit. The sibling library reads no density or
porosity, so mass properties are measured, not inferred from appearance.

Sibling fact (FACT, FrankenSim `crates/fs-mbd-material`, about 26,000 lines): it compiles mass, centre of mass
and spatial inertia from geometry volume moments and card density, with sources retained. Geometry coverage and
rigidity are the caller's obligations, and material uncertainty is retained, not turned into an inertia bound.

Rejected alternatives: editing the design file to match the scan (the design is what was intended; the built
part is a different object with its own identity); taking density from the material card for a printed part
(infill and porosity make the effective density of a printed part an unknown that only a scale resolves).

## Scope

In: geometric deltas as posteriors on `.fbot` parameters; mass, centre of mass and inertia from volume and a
scale reading; effective density; the as-built model variant and its identity; the one-batch comparison of
as-designed and as-built; the tolerance audit. Out: registration and fields (BD-395); tolerance allocation
itself (the manufacturing subsystem); scalar bench measurements of inertia by pendulum (N30b-05, which is also
the fallback route if the scan lane is withdrawn).

## Technical approach

1. **Geometric posteriors.** Each propagated quantity from the as-built record (link length, axis offset, axis
   parallelism) maps to a named design parameter of the `.fbot` file. The posterior on the parameter is
   Gaussian with the measured value as mean and the propagated covariance, including cross-correlations between
   parameters of one part. A quantity that was downgraded to `unknown` by the sigma-point check produces no
   posterior; the parameter stays at its designed value with its allocated tolerance as uncertainty, and the
   record says so.
2. **Mass properties.** Volume V and volume moments come from the registered as-built geometry (the design
   geometry displaced by the measured deviation fields where they exist, the design geometry otherwise). A
   scale reading gives mass m with its instrument uncertainty. Effective density ρ_eff = m/V, with
   σ_ρ/ρ = √((σ_m/m)² + (σ_V/V)²). `fs-mbd-material` compiles V, the moments and ρ_eff into mass, centre of
   mass and spatial inertia. For a printed part ρ_eff replaces the card density for that unit and the material
   card gains a candidate field `effective_density` with provenance `measured`, the dataset hash and the cell.
   Without a scale reading, mass properties stay as designed and are marked `assumed`.
3. **Physical consistency.** The compiled inertia must lie in the inertia cone (the pseudo-inertia is positive
   definite and the mass-support bound holds for the part's envelope); a result that fails is not written, and
   the offending measurement is reported.
4. **The as-built variant.** `frob_ground::asbuilt::variant(design, records) → ModelVariant`: the design with
   the geometric posteriors' means and the measured mass properties, provenance `as-built`, the as-built
   records as inputs, and therefore a new `ModelId`. Uncertainty is carried as a covariance block usable by
   correlated draws (N30b-04). The variant is a candidate in Ground's sense: it is admitted to narrow anything
   only after a frozen prediction made with it has been scored.
5. **One run.** Per-world model variants hold the as-designed and as-built robots in one batch: slot A the
   as-designed model, slot B the as-built variant, same task, same world addresses, so every episode is a pair.
   The output is the requirement verdict for each and the paired difference per metric.
6. **Tolerance audit.** Each measured feature is compared with its allocated tolerance t and sensitivity s from
   the tolerance allocation: status `within` if |deviation| + 2·σ ≤ t, `outside` if |deviation| − 2·σ > t,
   `undecided` otherwise (2σ, TARGET); the audit also prints the feature's first-order contribution s·deviation
   to its quantity of interest and the sum over features against the specification margin. A feature whose
   sensitivity was zero or unknown at allocation is listed `not-allocated`, never `within`.
7. **Record.** The as-built record gains `posteriors`, `mass_properties`, `variant` (the new model identity) and
   `audit`. The audit is also written as a table an operator can read: feature, nominal, measured, tolerance,
   status, contribution.

Determinism: class D2 for the compilation; the paired run has the class of its tier. Evidence class Estimated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-GROUND-08, identity part (TARGET): the as-built variant has a new `ModelId`, different from the
      as-designed one, and no fidelity record, verdict or band of the as-designed identity is offered for it.
- [ ] On the rehearsal's link the planted length change and wall deviation appear as posteriors whose intervals
      contain the planted values; the paired run completes with both variants in one batch.
- [ ] A planted mass of 0.412 kg on a computed volume of 3.60 × 10⁻⁴ m³ gives ρ_eff = 1,144 kg/m³, and the
      compiled mass equals the scale reading to 10⁻⁹ relative.
- [ ] A quantity downgraded to `unknown` yields no posterior and is reported as designed-with-tolerance.
- [ ] The audit marks a planted 0.3 mm deviation against a 0.2 mm tolerance `outside`, a 0.1 mm deviation
      `within`, and one at the edge `undecided`; a zero-sensitivity feature is `not-allocated`.
- [ ] An inertia that would leave the inertia cone is not written.
- [ ] Without a scale reading, mass properties are unchanged and marked `assumed`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/asbuilt_model.rs`: `length_posterior_from_propagated_quantity`;
`cross_correlation_carried`; `unknown_downgrade_gives_no_posterior`; `effective_density_hand_case` (0.412 kg,
3.60 × 10⁻⁴ m³: 1,144.4 kg/m³; with 1 g and 1% volume uncertainty the relative uncertainty is 1.03%);
`compiled_mass_equals_scale`; `inertia_cone_violation_not_written`; `variant_has_new_model_id`;
`old_identity_evidence_not_offered`; `paired_batch_shares_addresses`; `audit_status_table` (the four cases
above); `audit_contribution_sum` (two features with sensitivities 1 and 4 and deviations 10 µm and 5 µm:
contributions 10 µm and 20 µm); `no_scale_marks_assumed`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_asbuilt.sh` (bead N30b-35) builds the as-built variant from the rehearsal scan and scale
reading, prints both model identities and asserts they differ, runs the paired batch on the referee, and logs
the per-metric paired difference, both requirement verdicts and the audit table.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-733 (the 0.3 mm wall deviation, here as an audit row). Planted failures: a variant builder that reuses the
as-designed `ModelId` (the gate fails on identity); density taken from the card for a printed part (the
compiled mass differs from the scale reading); an audit that reports `within` for a feature with no allocation.
Metamorphic relation: registering the same scan after a rigid motion of the scan gives the same posteriors and
the same variant identity. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release.

## Considerations

- If the kill gate on scans fires, items 2 and 6 survive in reduced form: mass and lengths enter as scalar bench
  measurements, each a direct measurement of one field, and the variant is built from those alone.
- The as-built variant of one unit says nothing about the next unit; a part-class statement needs several
  units and the hierarchical card of N30b-04.
- Rigidity is assumed by the sibling. A part that visibly flexes under its own weight on the scanner is outside
  this lane, and the scan protocol of the bench package says how the part is supported.
- The paired run is cheap; the informative part is the audit table, because it tells the operator which feature
  to remake.
