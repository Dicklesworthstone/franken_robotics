## Background

Every Frontier mechanism needs a paper-scale pilot with a falsifier before its work is funded: small, run with
existing tools outside the workspace, registered before it runs, with an outcome not known in advance. A work
package whose pilot's falsifier holds is not funded and its fallback ships.

This is the pilot for as-built ingestion in Ground, the subsystem that compares simulation with measured
reality. The mechanism admits a scan of a built part, registers it to the design from fiducial correspondences
in closed form, reports a pose with a six-parameter covariance, computes deviation and thickness fields, and
refuses to report a deviation that is below the registration's own noise. Everything it claims about accuracy
rests on the sibling library's stated uncertainty being honest: if the planted truth falls outside the stated
uncertainty more often than the uncertainty says, the downstream tolerance audit would mark good parts bad and
bad parts good. The pilot tests exactly that, on a synthetic cloud where the truth is known. It needs no
hardware and costs hours of CPU and one to two engineer-days.

## Scope

In: the protocol, frozen before execution; the falsifier in operational form; the ceiling; the fallback; the
record. Out: the production mechanism (BD-395, N30a-47), which depends on this bead.

## Technical approach

1. **Program.** Standalone, under `pilots/ground-asbuilt/`, calling `fs-asbuilt` at its recorded pin on a cloud
   sampled from one link of a reference design. No production crate depends on it.
2. **Planted case** (registered): a link 0.25 m long with four fiducials in general position; planted rotation
   2° about the axis (1, 2, 2)/3 and translation (3.0, −1.5, 0.8) mm; fiducial measurement noise 0.02 mm per
   axis; surface points sampled at 10⁵ with noise 0.05 mm; one wall of nominal thickness 2.5 mm with a planted
   +0.3 mm deviation over a 20 mm × 20 mm patch.
3. **Runs.** (a) 200 noise seeds of the planted case: rigid registration, pose covariance, deviation field,
   propagated link length. (b) The same with two fiducials. (c) The same with all coordinates multiplied by
   25.4. (d) The planted deviation reduced to 0.02 mm, below the registration residual.
4. **Falsifier, operational** (holds → as-built ingestion is not funded as specified): the planted values fall
   outside the stated uncertainty, meaning any of:
   - in run (a) the planted pose lies outside the stated 95% region in more than 20 of 200 seeds (10%);
   - in run (a) the stated interval of the wall deviation excludes 0.3 mm in more than 20 of 200 seeds;
   - run (b) returns a pose; run (c) returns a deviation without a unit-suspicion flag; run (d) reports a
     deviation.
5. **Ceiling, computed before the run.** No speed or compute benefit is claimed; the mechanism's value is a
   model variant of the built unit and a tolerance audit.
6. **Fallback and kill gate.** KILL-ASBUILT: scans are not ingested; as-built data enters as scalar bench
   measurements (mass, lengths), each a direct measurement of one card field.
7. **Record.** One entry in `frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` with mechanism and tier, protocol hash,
   falsifier, ceiling, fallback and kill gate, cost, tools and pins, then the result: the two coverage counts
   with their binomial intervals, the three control outcomes, the verdict.

Everything the pilot produces is labelled `synthetic`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The protocol entry exists with its hash before any run.
- [ ] The result entry holds the pose coverage count and the deviation coverage count over 200 seeds, and the
      outcomes of runs (b), (c) and (d).
- [ ] The verdict is recorded whichever way it falls; if the falsifier holds, BD-395 is not started as
      specified and the review log states the revision or the adoption of the fallback.
- [ ] The pilot re-runs from the registry entry and reproduces its counts exactly on the same machine.
- [ ] Noise-free, the planted transform is recovered to 10⁻¹² (a sanity row, not part of the falsifier).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In the pilot's own test module: `cloud_generator_plants_pose` (the generated fiducials map back under the
planted inverse to 10⁻¹²); `patch_deviation_is_0_3_mm`; `two_fiducial_variant_has_two`;
`scaled_variant_scale_is_25_4`; `rotation_matrix_is_orthonormal`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/pilot_ground_asbuilt.sh`, header `bead=N30b-16 milestone=M5 labels=ground,pilot seed=1`: runs the
pilot from its registry entry and logs to `target/e2e-logs/pilot_ground_asbuilt/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` the
protocol hash, the pose coverage count against 180 of 200, the deviation coverage count against 180 of 200, the
outcome of each control run, and a summary with the verdict. It exits zero when the measurement is complete,
whatever the verdict.

### Known answers and negative controls

Controls that show the pilot can fail: a variant that reports the registration residual as the pose uncertainty
(coverage of the planted pose collapses and the first clause trips); a variant whose deviation interval ignores
the pose covariance (the second clause trips); a variant that rescales by 1/25.4 without a flag (the third
clause trips).

## Considerations

- A 95% region covering 90% of the time passes this pilot by design: the threshold leaves room for the
  linearization the sibling uses, and the result records the measured rate so that a systematic shortfall is
  visible even when it passes.
- The pilot supplies correspondences, as the sibling requires. Real scans without fiducials are out of scope
  until the upstream correspondence-search work item lands.
- A synthetic cloud has none of a real scanner's structured errors (occlusion, specular dropout, thermal
  drift). The pilot shows the arithmetic is honest, not that a scanner is.
