## Background

A kill gate names its trigger and its fallback in advance and is never quietly re-scoped. Its decision is
recorded as an architecture decision record at its milestone. A kill gate that fires is not a failure of the
program: the fallback is the plan, and the capability is described by the fallback's name from then on.

KILL-ASBUILT governs the scan lane of Ground, the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality: a
scan of a built part is admitted with fiducial correspondences, registered to the design in closed form with a
pose covariance, turned into deviation fields and posteriors on design parameters, and compiled into an
as-built model variant with its own identity.

- **Trigger:** GATE-GROUND-08 fails. GATE-GROUND-08 (Known answer; TARGET): the as-built known answers of the
  rehearsal (a planted rotation and translation and a 0.3 mm wall deviation recovered inside the stated
  uncertainty; two fiducials: refused; scale 25.4: unit suspicion raised); the as-built variant has a new
  `ModelId`.
- **Fallback:** scans are not ingested. As-built data enters as scalar bench measurements (mass, lengths), each
  a direct measurement of one card field.
- **Decided at:** M5.

Why the fallback is acceptable: a mass from a scale and a length from a caliper are direct measurements with
instrument uncertainties that need no registration to be trusted. They lose the deviation fields and the
correlation between features, and keep what a model of rigid bodies mostly needs.

## Scope

In: how the measurement is read; the decision; what changes on each branch; the posture until decided; the
record. Out: running the battery (N30a-48); the mechanisms.

## Technical approach

1. **Measurement read.** The record of GATE-GROUND-08, entered by its one recorder (N30a-48) from the receipt
   of the gate's tier run, and read here, never written: coverage of the planted pose by the stated 95% region over
   the registered noise seeds; whether the stated interval of the wall deviation contains 0.3 mm; the
   two-fiducial refusal; the unit-suspicion flag on the scaled copy; inequality of the as-designed and as-built
   model identities; each with its caught controls.
2. **Decision rule.** Not fired only if every part holds: the planted pose inside the stated 95% region in at
   least 90% of seeds (the registered operational reading of "inside the stated uncertainty", the same as the
   pilot's); the deviation interval contains the planted value in at least 90% of seeds; the two-fiducial scan
   refused; unit suspicion raised; the identities differ. The gate row also carries the battery's
   per-quantity reading (N30a-48 step 3: each pose component, the wall deviation, the thickness, the link
   length and the tilt inside 3 stated standard deviations in at least 0.97·D of D = 200 draws, normalized-error
   variance between 0.7 and 1.3), which N30a-48 makes part of the gate ("both readings must hold"); since the
   trigger is the gate failing, a failed per-quantity clause also fires. No repair cycle is granted: the first
   complete recorded measurement decides.
3. **Not fired.** The `decision` entry of the KILL-ASBUILT row has outcome `not-fired` and cites the gate's
   receipt; GATE-GROUND-08 reads `pass` in its own record; scan ingestion is described as implemented
   (Frontier) with its known gaps (correspondences supplied, never discovered).
4. **Fired** (outcome `fired`; GATE-GROUND-08, recorded `fail`, then reads `killed` with `detail =
   "KILL-ASBUILT: <fallback>"`, printed "killed: fallback adopted", through the register's `apply_decisions`,
   the one place a gate becomes `killed`). Downstream changes:
   - `ground.ingest` refuses `kind = "scan"` with FROB-REF-812 and the repair "enter scalar bench measurements
     of mass and lengths".
   - A bench dataset (`kind = "bench"`) may carry scalar features: mass by scale, lengths and offsets by
     caliper or gauge, each with its instrument `sigma` and `bias_bound`, each updating one named design
     parameter or card field through the direct-measurement stage of identification.
   - The as-built variant is still built, from scalars only: measured mass with designed geometry gives the
     effective density; measured lengths replace designed lengths; no covariance between features is claimed.
   - The tolerance audit runs on the measured scalars; features with no scalar measurement are listed
     `not-measured`.
   - Deviation, thickness and form fields are closed as not built; the upstream work item for a
     correspondence-search lane loses its consumer and is marked so.
   - The inventory rows that name scan ingestion for robots and parts are described as "scalar as-built
     measurements".
5. **Until decided (fail-closed default).** Scans are ingested only from the synthetic rehearsal; no as-built
   variant from a scan is used in any result of origin `real`.
6. **Record.** The decision ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-ASBUILT-M5.md` (the one form of a kill-gate decision; an
   earlier text of this bead named `docs/adr/KILL-ASBUILT.md`), with the required fields: the kill gate; the
   milestone; the trigger as registered and any amendments applied; each measured value with its receipt hash
   beside the trigger's number; the outcome; the fallback in force and the name the capability carries from
   then on; the beads re-read (each "stands on the fallback", "superseded by this ADR" or "withdrawn before
   this decision"); the downstream list of item 4 as executed; and the recording session, which did not
   implement the scan lane. The kill row's entry `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }` in
   `registries/gates.toml`, with an outcome from the closed set: `not-fired` (the gate's receipt cited),
   `fired`, `deferred` (with `deferred_to` and the reason, when the measurement is not complete at the M5
   exit) or `not-reached` (only if the scan lane was never funded, its pilot's falsifier having held). This
   bead writes that entry and the ADR; it never writes GATE-GROUND-08's row, and no status `killed`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-ASBUILT-M5.md` exists at the M5 exit with the required fields of item 6 and each part
      evaluated, and the KILL-ASBUILT row's `decision` entry cites it with an outcome of the closed set; an
      ADR at any other path fails the register's load (`decision-adr-path`).
- [ ] The decision can be recorded whichever way the measurement falls: nothing this bead depends on needs
      the gate to pass in order to close (BD-395 closes on its own criteria; N30a-48 closes `pass` or
      `recorded`).
- [ ] If fired: `frob ground ingest` of a scan bundle is refused with FROB-REF-812 and the stated repair; a bench
      bundle with a mass and two lengths produces an as-built variant with a new `ModelId` and an audit that
      lists unmeasured features as `not-measured`; GATE-GROUND-08 prints "killed: fallback adopted" from
      `apply_decisions`, its stored row still `fail`.
- [ ] If not fired: GATE-GROUND-08 is `pass` with measured values and caught controls, and the entry cites its
      receipt.
- [ ] No amendment to trigger, threshold or fallback is dated after the first measurement.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_asbuilt.rs`: `decision_table` (pose coverage 180 of 200 passes, 179 fails;
deviation coverage likewise; an accepted two-fiducial scan fails; a missing unit-suspicion flag fails; equal
identities fail; joint readings passing with one per-quantity count at 193 of 200 (below 0.97·200 = 194)
fails); `scalar_fallback_builds_variant`; `row_without_caught_controls_is_not_a_measurement`;
`outcome_from_closed_set` (every part holds → `not-fired` with the receipt; any part fails → `fired`; "passed"
and "killed: fallback adopted" are rejected as outcomes); `adr_path_is_the_registered_form`
(`docs/adr/KILL-ASBUILT.md` is refused `decision-adr-path`).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kill_asbuilt.sh`, header `bead=N30b-20 milestone=M5 labels=ground,kill-gate
gates=KILL-ASBUILT,GATE-GROUND-08 seed=1 plants=residual-as-uncertainty,uncaught-control`: sources
`scripts/e2e/lib.sh`, reads the gate record, recomputes each part from the receipt it cites, and prints the
outcome the rule gives. It reads only: it writes no register row and no ADR, which are the recording
session's. The two plants are the negative controls below, on scratch copies of the receipt. Log:
`target/e2e-logs/kill_asbuilt/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: each command with its exit code and
wall time; the two coverage counts against their thresholds with the receipt hash; the per-quantity counts
against 194 and variances against [0.7, 1.3]; the three boolean parts; the control results; on failure the
first part that could not be evaluated and the receipt path; and a summary with the trigger evaluation and
`gates: ["KILL-ASBUILT", "GATE-GROUND-08"]`. It exits zero when the decision can be made, whatever it is.

### Known answers and negative controls

Negative controls for the decision: a row whose coverage was computed against the registration residual instead
of the stated covariance (rejected: the row names the uncertainty source); a row with the residual-as-uncertainty
control not caught (rejected).

## Considerations

- "Inside the stated uncertainty" needs an operational reading before the measurement; the 90% of seeds reading
  is registered with the pilot and reused here, so the two cannot drift apart.
- The scan lane's value is largest for printed parts, whose form errors are spatially structured. The scalar
  fallback sees none of that structure; the tolerance audit says which features it could not see.
- Scans of places are another subsystem's lane with its own kill gate; this decision does not touch it.
