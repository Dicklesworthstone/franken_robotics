## Background

A proof obligation is a mathematical statement that a mechanism's correctness rests on. Ground, the subsystem
that compares simulation with measured reality, has three. None of them can be discharged by a certificate
checker: each is a statement about probabilities under sampling assumptions, and the program's rule is that
such a statement is a test with a derivation on file, never something presented as proved or certified. ("An
e-process or a conformal margin delivers its error rate" is on the program's list of obligations that no
checker decides; what it is instead is a null calibration over 10⁴ simulated studies together with an
exchangeability alarm.) The first obligation's combinatorial core was named as a candidate for a theorem card;
this bead records why it stays a test and what would be needed to make its finite part a card.

The obligations:

- **PO-GROUND-01.** The quantile index ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉ and the infinite-band rule give coverage at least
  1 − α for exchangeable draws.
- **PO-GROUND-02.** The two-mark clock formulas: for marks at t₁ < t₂ with independent timing error σ_m each,
  skew uncertainty σ_s = √2·σ_m/(t₂ − t₁) and aligned-instant uncertainty
  σ_t(t)² = σ_m²·[(t₂ − t)² + (t − t₁)²]/(t₂ − t₁)².
- **PO-GROUND-03.** Each drift monitor's false-alarm rate is at most α_d under optional stopping.

## Scope

In: for each obligation, the written derivation, the simulation that discharges it, the acceptance bound with
its statistical reasoning, and the discharge record. Out: the mechanisms (BD-389, BD-393, BD-394); the gates'
own batteries, which use the same reference values.
The mechanism beads carry each obligation as a unit-level property test beside the code and write its
derivation file (PO-GROUND-01 in N30a-36, PO-GROUND-02 in N30a-11, PO-GROUND-03 in N30a-41 and N30a-42). This
bead is the discharge record over all three: it fixes the acceptance bounds with their statistical reasoning,
runs the batteries at full size as its own test target `ground_obligations` (item 2), measures the union
false-alarm fraction of the two monitors, and records the result once per decision-policy version.

## Technical approach

1. **Derivations on file.** `docs/derivations/ground/po-ground-0{1,2,3}.md`, each self-contained:
   - *01.* With n + 1 exchangeable residuals, the rank of the test residual among them is uniform on
     {1, …, n + 1} (ties broken at random). The test residual is at most the k-th smallest calibration residual
     exactly when its rank is at most k, which has probability k/(n + 1). With k = ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉ ≤ n this is
     at least 1 − α; when k > n the band is infinite and coverage is 1. Conditional on the calibration set,
     coverage is Beta(k, n + 1 − k), which is Beta(n + 1 − l, l) with l = ⌊(n + 1)·α⌋, because
     ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉ = n + 1 − ⌊(n + 1)·α⌋.
   - *02.* The aligned instant is the interpolation t̂(t) = w₁·y₁ + w₂·y₂ with w₁ = (t₂ − t)/(t₂ − t₁),
     w₂ = (t − t₁)/(t₂ − t₁) and y_i the reference readings of the marks, so Var t̂ = σ_m²·(w₁² + w₂²); inside
     the bracket w₁, w₂ ∈ [0, 1] with sum one, so the variance is at most σ_m² and equals σ_m²/2 at the
     midpoint; outside it grows quadratically. The skew estimate is (y₂ − y₁)/(t₂ − t₁) − 1 with variance
     2·σ_m²/(t₂ − t₁)².
   - *03.* The miss process multiplies by p₁/p₀ on a miss and (1 − p₁)/(1 − p₀) on a cover; for any conditional
     miss probability p ≤ p₀ and p₁ > p₀ each factor has conditional expectation
     p·p₁/p₀ + (1 − p)·(1 − p₁)/(1 − p₀) ≤ 1, so the process is a nonnegative supermartingale started at one
     and, by Ville's inequality, exceeds 1/α_d at any time with probability at most α_d. The rank process is
     the sibling's betting e-process; its validity under uniform normalized ranks is stated from the sibling's
     contract, and the average of its two directions is again an e-process.
2. **Discharge by simulation.** The batteries are the tests of this bead's test target in the conformance
   crate, run at full size as `FROB_TEST_SEED=<s> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test ground_obligations`
   (`tests/ground_obligations.rs`, a declared test target of that crate; the form N30a-44's script calls). The
   run writes the three discharge records of item 3 to `target/gauntlet/obligations/` (DESIGN), which are its
   receipt: the files a script reads the measured values, the bounds and the union fraction from. The
   conformance crate has no binary named `frob-conformance` and no `run` subcommand; an earlier text of this
   bead wrote `frob-conformance run ground.obligations`, which means the command above. A test target of its
   own and not a tier entry, because a discharge is a record of class Tested about an obligation, not the
   measurement of a gate: the gates the obligations support are measured by their own tier entries. Seeds
   are explicit.
   - *01.* 10⁴ exchangeable resamples at each of (n, 1 − α) ∈ {(20, 0.90), (20, 0.95), (19, 0.95), (200, 0.90)},
     residuals from three registered laws (half-normal, lognormal, a discrete law with ties): the fraction of
     test residuals covered. Acceptance: at least 1 − α − 3·√(α·(1 − α)/10⁴) (for α = 0.10: at least 0.891), and
     the mean within 4 standard errors of k/(n + 1) (19/21 = 0.9048 at (20, 0.90)). At (20, 0.99) every band is
     infinite.
   - *02.* 10⁵ draws of mark jitter at σ_m = 50 µs and marks 10 s apart: the sample standard deviation of the
     skew estimate against 7.071 ppm and of the aligned instant at t ∈ {0, 2.5, 5, 10, 15} s against 50.00,
     39.53, 35.36, 50.00 and 79.06 µs. Acceptance: each within 1% relative (the standard error of a standard
     deviation over 10⁵ draws is 0.22%).
   - *03.* 10⁴ null runs per monitor, each a fresh calibration of n = 20 exchangeable residuals followed by up
     to 1,000 exchangeable episodes, **stopped adversarially**: the run stops at the first time the e-value
     reaches 20, which is the stopping rule most favourable to a false alarm. Reported: the false-alarm
     fraction of the rank monitor, of the miss monitor (with the true conditional miss rate clamped to p₀, the
     boundary of its null, and separately with miss rates drawn from the Beta law), and of their union.
     Acceptance: each monitor at most 0.05 + 3·√(0.05·0.95/10⁴) = 0.0565; the union is reported against 0.05,
     and a union above 0.05 triggers the pre-declared half-level adjustment of the drift mechanism before the
     drift gate is measured.
3. **Discharge record.** `frob.ground.obligation/1`, one per obligation: the derivation file's hash, the
   battery command, seeds, measured values with their acceptance bounds, the verdict and the commit. Cited by
   the decision core's record and by the rows of the gates the obligation supports.
4. **Why not a theorem card.** The finite statement of obligation 01 ("for every permutation-invariant law on
   n + 1 points, P(rank ≤ k) = k/(n + 1)") is a counting fact that an exhaustive check can confirm for small n
   (all (n + 1)! orderings for n ≤ 8), and the battery includes that enumeration as an exact row. Coverage in
   the running system also needs exchangeability to hold, which no checker decides. The obligation is
   therefore recorded as `Tested`, and no bundle presents it as certified.

Determinism: the batteries are seed-explicit and class D2. Evidence class of the discharge: Tested.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/ground_obligations.rs`: `rank_uniform_exhaustive_n_le_8` (exact rationals);
`coverage_lower_bound_20_090`; `coverage_with_ties_randomized_break`; `infinite_band_at_20_099`;
`clock_sd_table` (the six values); `miss_factor_supermartingale_identity` (expectation exactly one at p = p₀,
below one for p < p₀, in exact rationals for p₀ = 11/50, p₁ = 1/2); `null_runs_rank_monitor`;
`null_runs_miss_monitor_boundary`; `null_runs_union_reported`; `adversarial_stop_is_first_crossing`;
`run_writes_three_discharge_records` (after the target runs, `target/gauntlet/obligations/` holds one
`frob.ground.obligation/1` record per obligation, each with its derivation hash, seeds, measured values and
bounds; with a planted missed bound the record's verdict is `fail` and the target exits non-zero).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_drift.sh` (bead N30a-44, which owns the script and depends on this bead) runs
`FROB_TEST_SEED=<S> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test ground_obligations` before the band and drift
batteries and, from the discharge records the run writes, logs each measured value against its acceptance
bound, the seeds, the derivation hashes, and the union false-alarm fraction with the policy version it
implies. The five planted defects below are run by the same target, one per run, each as its own Cargo feature
of `frob-ground` under the umbrella (`--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-ground/<feature>`, never in a default or
release build: `po01-index-n`, `po01-ties-first`, `po02-product-numerator`, `po03-threshold-10`,
`po03-restart-each-look`), each required to make its battery miss a bound.

### Known answers and negative controls

Reference values: 19/21 = 0.9048; σ_s = 7.071 ppm; σ_t at the midpoint 35.36 µs; (0.5/0.22)·0.22 +
(0.5/0.78)·0.78 = 1. Planted defects: a quantile index ⌈n·(1 − α)⌉ (coverage about 0.857 at (20, 0.90): below
the bound); deterministic tie-breaking that always ranks the test residual first among ties (coverage on the
discrete law rises above nominal, which the two-sided check on the mean catches); a clock formula with
(t₂ − t)·(t − t₁) in the numerator; a monitor with threshold 10 (false-alarm fraction about double); a monitor
that restarts its e-value at each look.

## Considerations

- The adversarial-stopping horizon is finite (1,000 episodes). The bound holds for every horizon; a longer one
  only moves the measured fraction closer to the bound, and the horizon is in the record.
- Obligation 03 is stated per monitor. The drift gate's 5% is on retirement by either monitor; this bead is
  where the union is first measured, before that gate.
- Ties matter in practice: residuals of a metric recorded at coarse resolution tie often. The mechanism breaks
  ties by a keyed draw, and the derivation says so.
