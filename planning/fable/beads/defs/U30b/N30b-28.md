## Background

Ground is the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality. Its estimators come from sibling
libraries, reached through the one crate by which the sibling is consumed. Those libraries are explicit about
what they return: estimated candidates. The assimilation crate has no promotion to a validated state and calls
that "a separate admission act requiring calibrated data and an external authenticated authority" (FACT); the
as-built crate ships a verifier that grants no evidence beyond an estimated candidate (FACT). Decision
**ADR-GROUND-08** follows: every Ground output has evidence class Estimated.

The program has four evidence classes, ordered Proved, Certified, Tested, Estimated from strongest to weakest.
Only a Proof claim can be Proved or Certified; the other six kinds of claim, Reality among them, are Tested or
Estimated however much evidence they rest on. So the ceiling for a Reality result is Tested, and OPEN-GROUND-5
asks whether anything of Ground's can ever reach it: **who is the authenticated authority that could promote a
sibling's estimated candidate.**

- **Fail-closed default:** nobody; every Ground output stays Estimated.
- **Owner:** the lead of the proofs and certificates subsystem. **Blocks:** any Ground output above Estimated,
  and nothing else. **Decided at:** M5 or later; no milestone needs it.

## Scope

In: what a promotion would mean, the conditions any authority would have to satisfy, the options, the decision
and its record, how the default is enforced meanwhile. Out: any implementation of a promotion path (it does not
exist on the default branch and gets its own beads if the decision creates it).

## Technical approach

1. **What promotion would be.** Raising the class of a Ground result from Estimated to Tested: for example a
   band whose coverage was confirmed by a registered, fixed-n study on fresh real units, or a card field
   confirmed by a second, independent bench measurement. It would never be Certified or Proved: those belong to
   Proof claims set by a checker.
2. **Conditions any authority must meet** (DESIGN; they restate the program's rules for every class change):
   - the class is set by the component that produced the evidence, never by relabelling: a promotion is a new
     result with its own receipt, produced by a registered study on data the estimate never saw;
   - the authority is authenticated: its act is a signed ledger event naming the study, the data and the level;
   - no self-certification: the authority is not the session or the party that produced the estimate;
   - the claim product still takes the weakest input, so a promoted band attached to a simulated metric that
     rests on an `assumed` card field leaves the metric Estimated;
   - the three no-authorization flags stay false: a promoted Reality result authorizes nothing on hardware.
3. **Options.**
   - (a) Nobody (default). The surface fixes `evidence.class = "Estimated"` in the output schema of every
     `ground` verb.
   - (b) The qualification lane as the authority: a blinded, pre-committed, fixed-n confirmation on fresh real
     units, spending α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)] per attempt, whose receipt is the promoting act. Requires real units in
     number (the reachability arithmetic applies: zero failures in 20 trials bound a rate at 13.9%, and 59 are
     needed for 5%).
   - (c) An external party (a laboratory or a neutral adjudicator) whose signed measurement record is imported
     as data with its own provenance. The import is still data; the class it supports is decided by the study
     that consumes it.
4. **Decision.** The owner chooses (a), (b) or (c), or (a) with a named condition under which the question
   reopens (for example "when nine units of one class exist"). Under (b) or (c) new beads are created for the
   promotion path; none is written now.
5. **Enforcement of the default.** Three independent guards: the output schemas of the cluster fix the class;
   the claim-product type has no setter and no constructor that takes a class; and a conformance test offers a
   Ground result with a hand-edited class of `Tested` to every consuming verb and expects the refusal for a
   result presented above its class.
6. **Record.** `docs/adr/OPEN-GROUND-5.md` with the option, the conditions accepted, the owner's sign-off and
   the date; the open-questions registry row updated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Until a decision other than (a) is recorded, no artifact produced by any `ground` verb has a class other
      than Estimated (a sweep over the rehearsal's outputs).
- [ ] A Ground envelope edited to say `Tested` is refused by every consuming verb with the refusal for a result
      presented above the class of its weakest input.
- [ ] The decision record exists when the question is closed, in one of the stated forms, signed by the owner
      named for it.
- [ ] If (b) or (c) is chosen, the record lists the conditions of item 2 as accepted, and new beads exist for
      the promotion path before any promotion is attempted.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-verbs/tests/ground_class_fixed.rs`: `all_ground_output_schemas_fix_estimated`;
`edited_class_refused_on_consume` (one test per consuming verb: reports, comparison, promotion, the build
packet); `combine_with_ground_input_is_estimated` (a Certified kernel identity combined with a band gives
Estimated). Compile-fail: `no_constructor_takes_a_class`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_rehearsal.sh` (bead N30b-31) asserts `evidence.class == "Estimated"` on every envelope of
the seven verbs and logs the class with each command; `scripts/e2e/ground_refusals.sh` (bead N30b-37) includes
the edited-class attempt.

### Known answers and negative controls

Reference values for option (b): 13.9% at n = 20, 9.5% at n = 30, 4.95% at n = 59; attempt levels 0.025,
0.00833, 0.00417 for α = 0.05. Negative control: a schema for `ground.band` whose class is an open enumeration
(the fixed-class test fails).

## Considerations

- The default costs nothing today: no Reality result exists to promote. The question matters on the day a band
  on real data is cited in a decision that would have needed `Tested`.
- "Authenticated authority" must not become a role an agent can grant itself; any option other than (a) puts
  the act in the ledger with an identity that the capability system issues outside agent sessions.
- Promotion of the sibling's candidate inside the sibling is the sibling's business; this decision is about
  what this program's envelope says.
