## Background

Ground, the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality, analyses identifiability before any fit
and refuses a fit on a combination the data cannot separate. Eight structural aliases are known in advance and
are planted in the gate for that analysis. This bead adds a ninth, kept as its own fixture so that the count of
that gate is unchanged. It is Solid tier: a small, exact fact about geometry and time.

**The alias.** A range sensor with fixed orientation approaches a wall at coordinate L along a straight line at
speed v. With an exposure delay δ (the sensor reports a range that was true δ later than its timestamp says, or
earlier, according to the declared instant) and a forward mount offset b (the sensor sits b ahead of where the
model places it), its reading at time t is

    L − v·(t + δ) − b.

Only v·δ + b appears. At +2 m/s a delay of 20 ms and an offset of 40 mm give the same 101 samples of a
one-second pass at 100 Hz. **A synchronized clock does not separate a sensor's exposure delay from a
translation of its mount along the direction of travel.** This is the spatial counterpart of the
delay-against-offset alias of clock fitting (a delay of 8 ms with a clock offset of −8 ms produces the same
logs as neither), and the same alias binds the extrinsic graph of sensor calibration when a faulty edge is a
time offset on one pair.

Why it matters to a design agent: a mount position is a design variable and an exposure delay is a card field.
A fit that silently assigns the combined effect to one of them moves the sensor in the model, or rewrites the
card, on evidence that supports neither.

Rejected alternative: resolving the alias with a prior ("delays are usually small, so call it offset"). That is
a regularized fit that returns the prior and labels it a measurement.

## Scope

In: the detection rule in `ground.excite`; the ranked separating experiments with their predicted separation;
the behaviour of a fit requested on aliased data; the refusal of card and mount updates; the link to the
sensor-calibration pose graph. Out: the fixture and the gate measurement (N30b-30); the eight aliases and the
analysis machinery (BD-390); the pose graph itself (the sensing subsystem).

## Technical approach

1. **Detection** (in the identifiability report of `ground.excite`, as a named structural check that runs
   beside the numerical ones). For each range, depth or lidar sensor and each planned or recorded pass, compute
   the velocity component along the sensor's measurement axis, v_a. If every pass has the same v_a (within the
   velocity instrument's tolerance), the pair (exposure delay δ, mount offset b along the axis) is flagged
   `aliased`, with the identified combination `v_a·δ + b` and its value where data exists. The report names
   the pair's members `exposure_delay` (δ) and `mount_offset_axial` (b) (DESIGN: the names of the sensor stage
   that `ground.identify --stage sensors --params <names>` accepts, since that option takes the names this
   report prints). The numerical check agrees: the Jacobian columns for δ and b are proportional (∂/∂δ = −v_a, ∂/∂b = −1 for every sample), so the
   scaled Jacobian has a singular direction and their correlation is 1.
2. **Separating experiments, proposed in order of the separation they give** (for the stated case, δ = 20 ms
   against b = 40 mm at +2 m/s):
   - the same pass reversed without rotating the robot: at v_a = −2 m/s the two explanations differ by
     |(−2)·0.020 − 0.040| = 80 mm;
   - a second speed: at +1 m/s they differ by |1·0.020 − 0.040| = 20 mm;
   - an independent exposure mark on the shared hardware line used for clock marks, which measures δ directly.
   In general, for two passes with axial velocities v₁ ≠ v₂ and identified combinations c₁, c₂:
   δ = (c₁ − c₂)/(v₁ − v₂) and b = c₁ − v₁·δ; the predicted separation between the pure-delay and pure-offset
   explanations on the second pass is |v₂ − v₁|·δ₀ for a hypothesized pure delay δ₀. The plan lists each
   proposal with that number and its duration, so the larger separation is visibly the better buy.
3. **A fit requested on data with one axial velocity** returns the identified combination v_a·δ + b with its
   interval and updates neither the card's delay nor the mount transform. A request that names δ or b
   explicitly (`--params exposure_delay`, `--params mount_offset_axial`) is refused with FROB-REF-879 (a fit on a parameter set that fails identifiability), whose payload
   is the combination and whose repairs are the three experiments above, in that order.
4. **With two axial velocities** the sensor stage of identification solves the two-by-two system above, with
   uncertainty propagated from the range noise and the velocity uncertainty:
   σ_δ² = (σ_c₁² + σ_c₂²)/(v₁ − v₂)² (plus the velocity term), σ_b from b = c₁ − v₁·δ. Candidate versions of the
   sensor card (delay) and of the mount parameter are written through the card writer.
5. **Pose-graph link.** When sensor-to-sensor calibration reports an inconsistent loop and the faulty edge is a
   time offset on one pair, the report cites this alias: a time offset on a pair observed at one relative
   velocity is indistinguishable from a translation along that velocity, and the same separating experiments
   apply.
6. **Report text.** The identified combination is printed in the units of length with the pass velocity, in
   the fixed form "delay times speed plus offset = 40 mm".

Determinism: the detection and the two-by-two solve are exact given exact inputs (class D0 in rationals, D2 in
double precision). Evidence class Estimated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `ground.excite` flags the pair when every planned pass has the same axial velocity and does not flag it
      when the plan contains a reversed pass or a second speed.
- [ ] The proposals are listed in the order reversed pass, second speed, exposure mark, with predicted
      separations 80 mm and 20 mm for the stated case, exact to 10⁻⁹.
- [ ] A fit on the single pass reports "delay times speed plus offset = 40 mm" and leaves the card's delay and
      the mount transform's content addresses unchanged.
- [ ] A request for δ alone from the single pass (`frob ground identify <dataset> --stage sensors --params
      exposure_delay --key <k> --json`) is refused with FROB-REF-879 and the three repairs.
- [ ] With the reversed pass added, the pure-delay truth returns δ = 20 ms with b = 0 and the pure-offset truth
      returns b = 40 mm with δ = 0, each within its interval (the gate measurement is N30b-30).
- [ ] The count of the eight-alias gate is unchanged: the ninth alias is a separate named check with its own
      fixture and gate.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-ground/tests/alias_exposure.rs`: `combination_is_v_delta_plus_b` (v = 2, δ = 1/50, b = 0 and
v = 2, δ = 0, b = 1/25 both give 1/25 m exactly in rationals); `jacobian_columns_proportional`;
`flag_when_all_passes_share_axial_velocity`; `no_flag_with_reversed_pass`; `no_flag_with_second_speed`;
`separation_reversed_is_80_mm`; `separation_second_speed_is_20_mm`; `proposals_ordered_by_separation`;
`single_pass_fit_returns_combination_only`; `explicit_delta_request_refused_879`;
`two_pass_solve_pure_delay` (c₁ = 0.040 m at +2 m/s and c₂ = −0.040 m at −2 m/s give δ = 0.020 s, b = 0);
`two_pass_solve_pure_offset` (c₁ = c₂ = 0.040 m give δ = 0, b = 0.040 m); `two_pass_solve_mixed` (c₁ = 0.080 m
and c₂ = 0.000 m give δ = 0.020 s and b = 0.040 m); `perpendicular_motion_has_zero_axial_velocity` (a pass across the sensor's axis carries no
information on δ, and b along the axis is then directly observed).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ground_alias9.sh` (bead N30b-30) drives `frob ground excite` and `frob ground identify --stage
sensors` on the rehearsal's passes and logs the flag, the proposals with their separations, the reported
combination, the unchanged content addresses and the refusal.

### Known answers and negative controls

The known answer is the late row of the Ground known-answer registry N30-KA for GATE-GROUND-11, cited by its
tag `ground-alias9` until the registry's number block is issued and by its number afterwards (no number is
typed here); this bead is the row's mechanism and N30b-30 binds it. The row's values: v·δ + b = 40 mm for both
explanations at +2 m/s; 80 mm apart reversed; 20 mm apart at +1 m/s. The row's control text is this bead's two
negative controls, from which the registry's seeding tool issues the control records (this bead writes none):
a fit that splits the combination by a prior (it reports a delay from one pass: caught by the
unchanged-address assertion and the refusal test); a detector that compares speeds instead of axial
velocities (a reversed pass at the same speed is then wrongly treated as the same velocity and the pair stays
flagged: caught by `no_flag_with_reversed_pass`).

## Considerations

- The reversed pass must not rotate the robot: turning around and driving back presents the same axial
  velocity to the sensor, and separates nothing. The plan states this in the proposal text.
- Only the offset component along the direction of travel is aliased with delay. Lateral mount errors are
  separated by any pass; the report names the component.
- If time-based comparison with real logs has been withdrawn by its kill gate, the flag is still raised and no
  delay or offset is updated from any pass.
- A full-pose version of the alias (delay against a rigid motion along the instantaneous twist) exists for a
  turning robot; the straight pass is the hand-checkable case, and the detector's rule on axial velocity is
  stated for it.
