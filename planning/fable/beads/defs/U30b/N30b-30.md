## Background

GATE-GROUND-11 is the gate of the ninth structural alias in Ground, the subsystem that compares simulation with
measured reality. The alias: a range sensor approaching a wall at coordinate L at speed v, with an exposure
delay δ and a forward mount offset b, reads L − v·(t + δ) − b, so only v·δ + b appears; a synchronized clock
does not separate the delay from the translation. The mechanism (the flag in `ground.excite`, the ranked
separating experiments, the fit that returns only the combination) is in N30b-29.

The gate (claim kind: Known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): in the synthetic rehearsal, the two explanations
produce bit-equal samples on the +2 m/s pass and the fit reports "delay times speed plus offset = 40 mm" with
no card update. With the reversed pass added, both the 20 ms delay and a planted 40 mm offset are recovered
within their intervals, and the predicted separation is 80 mm to 10⁻⁹. A card delay update from the single pass
is refused.

It has no kill gate, so a failure blocks the M5 exit. This bead is the fixture's exact definition and the
script that measures the gate through the binary.

## Scope

In: the exact definition of fixture R-15; the script; the reading of the gate's clauses; the gate row's record.
Out: the alias mechanism (N30b-29); the rehearsal generator at large (BD-388), which hosts this fixture.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixture R-15, exactly.** A robot drives straight toward a wall at L = 5 m. A range sensor on its axis
   samples at 100 Hz for one second: 101 samples at t_k = k/100 s, k = 0 … 100. Four datasets, all of origin
   `synthetic`, noise-free for the bit-equality clause and with range noise σ = 1 mm for the recovery clause:
   - `X-fwd`: v = +2 m/s, truth δ = 20 ms, b = 0;
   - `Y-fwd`: v = +2 m/s, truth δ = 0, b = 40 mm;
   - `X-rev` and `Y-rev`: the same two truths on the reversed pass, v = −2 m/s, robot not rotated;
   - plus `X-slow` and `Y-slow` at v = +1 m/s for the second-speed proposal.
   Readings are generated in exact rational arithmetic as L − v·t_k − (v·δ + b), with v·δ + b = 1/25 m in both
   forward datasets, then converted to floating point once. (Evaluated naively in double precision as
   L − v·(t + δ) − b, the two forward datasets agree bit for bit on only about half of the samples; the
   fixture's definition removes that accident so that the clause tests the alias and not rounding.)
2. **Clauses and how each is measured.**
   - *Bit-equal samples:* the sample columns of `X-fwd` and `Y-fwd` have equal BLAKE3 digests (noise-free).
   - *The reported combination:* `frob ground identify --stage sensors` on `X-fwd` alone, and on `Y-fwd` alone,
     each returns `identified_combination = {name: "delay times speed plus offset", value: "40 mm"}` and no
     candidate version of the sensor card or of the mount parameter; the content addresses of both are equal
     before and after.
   - *Refusal:* the same call with `--field exposure_delay` is refused with FROB-REF-879 (a fit on a parameter
     set that fails identifiability), with the three separating experiments as repairs.
   - *Recovery:* with `X-fwd` and `X-rev` ingested together, δ is recovered with 20 ms inside its stated
     interval and b with 0 inside its interval; with `Y-fwd` and `Y-rev`, b is recovered with 40 mm inside its
     interval and δ with 0 inside its interval (noisy datasets; 100 noise seeds; both truths inside their 95%
     intervals in at least 90 of 100).
   - *Predicted separation:* `frob ground excite` on a plan holding only the forward pass proposes the reversed
     pass first with predicted separation 80 mm, equal to 0.08 m to 10⁻⁹, and the second speed next with 20 mm.
3. **Script.** `scripts/e2e/ground_alias9.sh`, header `bead=N30b-30 milestone=M5 labels=ground,e2e,gate seed=1`.
   It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` and runs, in a temporary store: generate R-15
   (`frob-conformance gen ground.rehearsal --fixture R-15 --seed 1`); ingest; `frob ground excite --json` on the
   forward-only plan; `frob ground identify --stage sensors --json` on each forward dataset; the refused call
   (`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-879 3 -- …`); ingest the reversed passes and identify again; the 100-seed
   recovery battery through `frob-conformance run ground.alias9 --json`. Controls with `e2e_expect_caught`.
4. **Log.** `target/e2e-logs/ground_alias9/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript: per step the command, exit
   code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block (class Estimated, reality `synthetic`, determinism, seed);
   the two sample digests; the combination with its unit; the content addresses before and after; the refusal's
   code, pointer and repairs; δ and b with their intervals per truth; the separation with its error against
   10⁻⁹; the recovery counts against 90 of 100; a summary with assertions passed and failed and
   `gates: ["GATE-GROUND-11"]`. On failure: the first differing sample index with both values, or the first
   seed whose interval missed.
5. **Gate row.** A record appended for GATE-GROUND-11 with the commit, fingerprint, seed, the measured values
   and the receipt hash, by a session that did not implement N30b-29.

### Known answers and negative controls

Values: v·δ + b = 40 mm; 80 mm; 20 mm; 101 samples; recovery δ = 20 ms (truth X), b = 40 mm (truth Y). Negative
controls: a generator using the naive double form (the bit-equality clause fails and the log names the first
differing index); an identifier build that splits the combination by a prior on delay (a card version appears
after the single pass: caught); a reversed pass produced by rotating the robot (axial velocity unchanged: the
recovery clause fails because the system is singular, and the script reports the flag still raised).
Metamorphic relations: adding a constant to L changes every sample by that constant and nothing else; scaling v
and 1/δ together leaves the forward combination unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and
release; macOS and Linux with identical digests.

## Considerations

- The gate text names "both the 20 ms delay and a planted 40 mm offset"; this bead reads that as two truths,
  each recovered when it is the one planted, which is what makes the forward combination 40 mm in both. A
  single truth holding both would give a forward combination of 80 mm.
- The recovery clause needs noise to have intervals at all; the bit-equality clause needs none. Hence two
  variants of each dataset, both registered with the fixture.
- Runtime is seconds; the script belongs in the `smoke` battery from M5.
