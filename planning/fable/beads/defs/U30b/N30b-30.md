## Background

GATE-GROUND-11 is the gate of the ninth structural alias in Ground, the subsystem that compares simulation with
measured reality. The alias: a range sensor approaching a wall at coordinate L at speed v, with an exposure
delay δ and a forward mount offset b, reads L − v·(t + δ) − b, so only v·δ + b appears; a synchronized clock
does not separate the delay from the translation. The mechanism (the flag in `ground.excite`, the ranked
separating experiments, the fit that returns only the combination) is in N30b-29.

The gate (claim kind: Known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): in the synthetic rehearsal, the two explanations
produce bit-equal samples on the +2 m/s pass and the fit reports "delay times speed plus offset = 40 mm" with
no card update. With the reversed pass added, both the 20 ms delay and a planted 40 mm offset are recovered
within their intervals, and the predicted separation is 80 mm to 10⁻⁹. A card delay update from the single pass
is refused.

It has no kill gate, so a failure blocks the M5 exit. This bead is the fixture's exact definition and the
script that measures the gate through the binary.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixture R-15, exactly.** A robot drives straight toward a wall at L = 5 m. A range sensor on its axis
   samples at 100 Hz for one second: 101 samples at t_k = k/100 s, k = 0 … 100. Six datasets, all of origin
   `synthetic`, noise-free for the bit-equality clause and with range noise σ = 1 mm for the recovery clause:
   - `X-fwd`: v = +2 m/s, truth δ = 20 ms, b = 0;
   - `Y-fwd`: v = +2 m/s, truth δ = 0, b = 40 mm;
   - `X-rev` and `Y-rev`: the same two truths on the reversed pass, v = −2 m/s, robot not rotated;
   - plus `X-slow` and `Y-slow` at v = +1 m/s for the second-speed proposal.
   Readings are generated in exact rational arithmetic as L − v·t_k − (v·δ + b), with v·δ + b = 1/25 m in both
   forward datasets, then converted to floating point once. (Evaluated naively in double precision as
   L − v·(t + δ) − b, the two forward datasets agree bit for bit on 96 of the 101 samples with t_k = k/100,
   and on 97 with t_k = k·0.01, not on all; the fixture's definition removes that accident so that the clause
   tests the alias and not rounding.)
2. **Clauses and how each is measured.**
   - *Bit-equal samples:* the sample columns of `X-fwd` and `Y-fwd` have equal BLAKE3 digests (noise-free).
   - *The reported combination:* `frob ground identify --stage sensors` on `X-fwd` alone, and on `Y-fwd` alone,
     each returns `identified_combination = {name: "delay times speed plus offset", value: "40 mm"}` and no
     candidate version of the sensor card or of the mount parameter; the content addresses of both are equal
     before and after.
   - *Refusal:* the same call with `--params exposure_delay` (the option and the name N30b-29 and the verb's
     registry row define; an earlier text wrote `--field`, which is an unknown parameter) is refused with
     FROB-REF-879 (a fit on a parameter set that fails identifiability), with the three separating experiments
     as repairs.
   - *Recovery:* with `X-fwd` and `X-rev` ingested together, δ is recovered with 20 ms inside its stated
     interval and b with 0 inside its interval; with `Y-fwd` and `Y-rev`, b is recovered with 40 mm inside its
     interval and δ with 0 inside its interval (noisy datasets; 100 noise seeds). Counted per parameter and
     per truth (four counts), each truth inside its 95% interval in at least 88 of 100 seeds: a correct build
     fails one count with probability 0.0015 and any of the four with about 0.006. (A joint "both inside in
     at least 90 of 100" would be a coin flip: at equal noise on the two passes the estimates of δ and b are
     uncorrelated, joint coverage is 0.9025, and P(X ≤ 89) = 0.38.) The joint count is logged beside them.
   - *Predicted separation:* `frob ground excite` on a plan holding only the forward pass proposes the reversed
     pass first with predicted separation 80 mm, equal to 0.08 m to 10⁻⁹, and the second speed next with 20 mm.
3. **Script.** `scripts/e2e/ground_alias9.sh`, header `bead=N30b-30 milestone=M5 labels=ground,e2e,gate
   gates=GATE-GROUND-11 seed=1 needs=cpu plants=naive-double-generator,prior-split-fit,rotated-reverse-pass`.
   It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` and runs, in the run's own state root:
   - `generate`: R-15 through the rehearsal generator's example target, `cargo run -p frob-conformance
     --example ground_rehearsal -- gen --fixture R-15 --seed 1 --out $D` (BD-388; there is no binary named
     `frob-conformance`, which an earlier text of this bead called);
   - `ingest`: `frob ground ingest $D/R-15/<dataset> --design <d> --task-family <tf> --key "$(e2e_key
     ingest-<dataset>)" --json` for the forward datasets;
   - `excite`: `frob ground excite <d> --cards <sensor card set> --rig $D/R-15/rig-forward.toml --json`, the rig
     file listing the forward pass only;
   - `identify`: `frob ground identify <dataset> --stage sensors --key "$(e2e_key id-<dataset>)" --json` on
     each forward dataset;
   - `refusal`: `e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-879 3 -- frob ground identify <dataset> --stage sensors --params
     exposure_delay --key "$(e2e_key id-refused)" --json`;
   - `reversed`: ingest the reversed passes and identify again, each call with its own key;
   - `receipt`: the gate's tier entry through the shared helper, `e2e_tier g1_known_answers GATE-GROUND-11
     --mutations` (that is `FROB_TEST_SEED=1 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features
     gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-GROUND-11`), which runs the 100-seed recovery battery and the gate's controls
     and writes the hash-named receipt. The entry is this bead's: `ka:ground-alias9@referee` (the binding rule
     of N38a-13; the tag is replaced by the row's number in the one commit in which N30-KA's block is issued),
     driving `ground.ingest` and `ground.identify` per seed exactly as an agent would call them and asserting
     the datasets' `synthetic` origin; this bead also names the entry's test in the row's derivation front
     matter. The script asserts on the receipt (`e2e_get --file "$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) that the gate line is
     `pass` and every control `caught`, logs the receipt hash and ends with `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-11`. No
     `frob-conformance run …` command exists, and the script runs no recorder.
   Controls with `e2e_expect_caught`, and the three header plants through `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE`.
4. **Log.** `target/e2e-logs/ground_alias9/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript: per step the command, exit
   code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block (class Estimated, reality `synthetic`, determinism, seed);
   the two sample digests; the combination with its unit; the content addresses before and after; the refusal's
   code, pointer and repairs; δ and b with their intervals per truth; the separation with its error against
   10⁻⁹; the four recovery counts against 88 of 100, and the joint count; a summary with assertions passed and failed and
   `gates: ["GATE-GROUND-11"]`. On failure: the first differing sample index with both values, or the first
   seed whose interval missed.
5. **Gate row.** This bead is the gate's one recorder: from the receipt of step `receipt`, a session that
   implemented neither N30b-29 nor this bead enters GATE-GROUND-11 in `registries/gates.toml` with `cargo run
   -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-GROUND-11 --receipt <hash>`; the record then holds
   the commit, fingerprint, seed, the measured values and the receipt hash. The script writes no gate row.

### Known answers and negative controls

The gate's known answer is the late row of N30-KA (tag `ground-alias9` until its number is issued), bound by
this bead. Values: v·δ + b = 40 mm; 80 mm; 20 mm; 101 samples; recovery δ = 20 ms (truth X), b = 40 mm (truth
Y). The row's two registered controls are N30b-29's (the prior-split fit; speeds compared in place of axial
velocities). Negative controls of this script, its three header plants: a generator using the naive double form (the bit-equality clause fails and the log names the first
differing index); an identifier build that splits the combination by a prior on delay (a card version appears
after the single pass: caught); a reversed pass produced by rotating the robot (axial velocity unchanged: the
recovery clause fails because the system is singular, and the script reports the flag still raised).
Metamorphic relations: adding a constant to L changes every sample by that constant and nothing else; scaling v
and 1/δ together leaves the forward combination unchanged. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and
release; macOS and Linux with identical digests.

## Considerations

- The gate text names "both the 20 ms delay and a planted 40 mm offset"; this bead reads that as two truths,
  each recovered when it is the one planted, which is what makes the forward combination 40 mm in both. A
  single truth holding both would give a forward combination of 80 mm.
- The recovery clause needs noise to have intervals at all; the bit-equality clause needs none. Hence two
  variants of each dataset, both registered with the fixture.
- Runtime is seconds; the script belongs in the `smoke` battery from M5.
