## Background

Ground, the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality, is a procedure of ten steps: admit data
and seal splits; fit clocks; analyse identifiability and design excitation; identify actuators; identify
contact, inertia and sensors; write candidate cards; freeze predictions; score on sealed data, retire the
holdout and admit or reject; issue or refuse a band; monitor drift and retire; feed discrepancy back. Each step
has a verb, an output and a state it fails closed to. With no real data, the only way to know that the steps
work together is to run them, in order, on the synthetic rehearsal: a truth simulator with planted parameters
whose every output is labelled `synthetic`.

The per-gate batteries (clock, identification, protocol, bands and drift, as-built, kit) measure statistical
thresholds one mechanism at a time. This script is different: it is the user's path. One agent-like session
takes one rig from a bundle on disk to a band and a kit, and one assertion per known-answer row checks that
the planted truth came out. It closes the done-condition of the rehearsal's seed bead (the known answers
reproduce; every output prints `synthetic`) and it is the demonstration the M5 milestone names: the seven
`ground` verbs exercised first on the synthetic rehearsal.

## Scope

In: the script; its fixtures (generated, not stored); its assertions per row; its log. Out: the statistical
batteries (N30b-32 to N30b-36); the refusal battery (N30b-37); the ninth-alias gate (N30b-30).

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/ground_rehearsal.sh`, header `bead=N30b-31 milestone=M5 labels=ground,e2e seed=1`. It sources
`scripts/e2e/lib.sh`, uses a fresh temporary store and is idempotent.

| Step | Commands (all with `--json`) | Asserts (planted value, tolerance) | Row |
|---|---|---|---|
| `generate` | `frob-conformance gen ground.rehearsal --fixture all --seed 1 --out $D` | Fifteen fixtures; every manifest `origin = "synthetic"` | — |
| `ingest` | `frob ground ingest $D/R-01 …` for each bundle | Dataset ids stable; validation and holdout sealed; clocks fitted: skew within 21 ppm of 100 ppm, offset near 3.7 ms; the one-pulse variant refused with FROB-REF-876 | KA-721 |
| `excite` | `frob ground excite --design … --cards … --rig $D/R-02/rig.toml` | The eight structural aliases flagged, 8 of 8, each with its identified combination; friction against the torque limit: 3.33 N and 6.67 N indistinguishable, 4 N against 8 N separated | KA-722, KA-723 |
| `identify` | `frob ground identify $R04 --cell … --stage actuators`; then `contact`, `inertia`, `sensors` | J_a, b, τ_c, τ_s, v_s within 10%; delay within one grid step; unidirectional variant: τ_c refused with FROB-REF-879; delay 8 ms with offset −8 ms and no mark: "delay plus offset = 0", card delay untouched; wrong-reason variant labelled `wrong-reason` | KA-724, KA-725, KA-727 |
| `predict` | `frob ground predict --dataset $R04 --part validation …`; also over MCP (`ground_predict`) | Record frozen; ledger sequence returned; a claim of "under 5%" at n = 20 refused, with n = 59 in the payload | KA-732 |
| `score` | `frob ground score <prediction>`; also over MCP (`ground_score`) | Freeze sequence precedes read sequence; candidate admitted on the nominal rig; the omitted-law candidate rejected; read-before-freeze, refit-without-version and holdout-reuse each refused | KA-726, KA-734 |
| `band` | `frob ground band --cell … --metric … --level 0.90`; `--list`; `--status`; also over MCP (`ground_band`) | A band with n = 20 and index 19; refusals for the unbuilt design, the 8-point cell and the new unit with 3 units; pooled control counts 20 of 20 and 16 of 20 under the mutant only; miss arithmetic 26.7 and 11.7 in the status output | KA-728, KA-729, KA-731, KA-736 |
| `drift` | `frob ground ingest` of the drift fixture, episode by episode | Retirement within 20 episodes after the planted change; status `retired`; a later band request refused with FROB-REF-897 | KA-730 |
| `asbuilt` | `frob ground ingest $D/R-13/scan …` | Pose and the 0.3 mm deviation inside stated uncertainty; two fiducials refused; scale 25.4 flagged; the as-built `ModelId` differs | KA-733 |
| `kit` | `frob ground kit build --result <score> --out $K`; `frob ground kit check $K --store $EMPTY` | Kit builds; check passes on the same machine by digest | — |
| `sweep` | Re-read every envelope written above | `evidence.reality == "synthetic"` and `evidence.class == "Estimated"` on every one; the line `reality: synthetic dataset=…` in every human rendering | — |

Log: `target/e2e-logs/ground_rehearsal/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block
(claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); each assertion logs the planted value (read from the truth
file by the harness, never by `frob`), the recovered value, the tolerance and the unit; each refusal logs code,
pointer, observed, required and repairs; digests are logged where state is compared (content addresses of
cards before and after, the randomization block, dataset ids). On failure the line names the first differing
value and the paths of the bundle and truth file needed to reproduce. The final `summary` gives assertions
passed and failed and the gates touched. Every line carries `synthetic: true`.

The script touches the known-answer rows of every Ground gate from the second to the ninth; it writes a gate
record only for the eight-alias gate's re-assertion, and leaves the statistical thresholds to the batteries.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a correct build: every row of the table asserts, and the sweep finds no envelope
      with a status other than `synthetic` or a class other than Estimated.
- [ ] All seven verbs are called; `ground_predict`, `ground_score` and `ground_band` are also called through
      `frob-mcp` and return envelopes equal to the CLI's apart from transport fields.
- [ ] With `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE` set to each of: `truth-in-bundle`, `score-reads-calibration`,
      `band-keyed-by-design`, `monitor-resets`, `asbuilt-residual-as-uncertainty`, the script exits non-zero and
      names the step.
- [ ] Two runs with one seed produce logs that differ only in timestamps and wall times.
- [ ] Runtime under 30 minutes on the CPU with the single nominal rig (TARGET); the 100-rig population is not
      part of this script.
- [ ] The seed bead of the rehearsal cites this script's log in its close reason.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/ground_rehearsal_table.rs`: `rows_cover_ka_721_to_734_and_736` (each
known-answer identifier appears in exactly one step); `truth_read_only_by_harness`;
`plants_registered_for_verify_plants`.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; see the technical approach. `scripts/e2e/run-all.sh --milestone M5 --label ground`
runs it with the six batteries, and `--verify-plants` checks each plant.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-721 to KA-734 and KA-736, with the values in the table. CX-326 to CX-334 as the plants and refusals named.
Metamorphic check inside the script: rerunning `identify` on the same record with the experiments listed in
reverse order yields posteriors that agree within their own standard errors. Determinism lanes: twice on one
machine; debug and release; the other platform in class D3 (record, compare inside the envelope).

## Considerations

- The script proves a bounded loop is sound and nothing more; its last log line says so in words, and the
  Reality claim stays `no-claim`.
- Order matters and is part of what is tested: running `band` before `score`, or `identify --stage contact`
  before actuators, must be refused, and the script includes those two out-of-order calls.
- If a kill gate of the subsystem has fired, the corresponding step asserts the fallback's behaviour instead
  (for example time-free metrics only), keyed on the kill gate's row in the registry.
