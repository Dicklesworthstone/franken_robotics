## Background

Two gates of Ground, the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality, are statistical statements
about its first two stages, and both are measured on the synthetic rehearsal (a truth simulator with planted
parameters, labelled `synthetic`).

GATE-GROUND-02 (Known answer; TARGET): clock recovery in at least 99% of 1,000 resamples (planted skew 100 ppm,
offset 3.7 ms, mark jitter 50 µs, marks 10 s apart: skew within 3·σ_s = 21 ppm and the scored instant covered);
both negative controls caught (one pulse only: refused; an offset fitted to the scored event: detected).

GATE-GROUND-04 (Known answer; TARGET): planted actuator parameters within 10% on at least 95 of 100 synthetic
rigs; identified cards cut held-out trajectory error by at least 30% against prior cards; the omitted-law
candidate is rejected in at least 90 of 100.

They share a script because they share a dataset family and an order: clocks are stage 0 of the fixed
identification order, and a clock error left in becomes a delay error and then a friction error. The script
measures the two gates from the user's side and writes the records that two kill decisions read (KILL-CLOCK
for the first, KILL-GROUND, after one repair cycle, for the second).

## Scope

In: the script, its batteries, the reading of each clause, its log and the gate records. Out: the mechanisms
(BD-389, BD-391, N30b-02 to N30b-05); scoring (N30b-06), which the second and third clauses of the
identification gate call; the kill decisions.

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/ground_identify.sh`, header `bead=N30b-32 milestone=M5 labels=ground,e2e,gate seed=1`. It sources
`scripts/e2e/lib.sh`; seeds for every battery are fixed in the script and logged.

1. **Step `clock-single`.** Generate R-01 and R-05; `frob ground ingest … --json` on each. Assert: `clocks[0]`
   has `skew_ppm` within 21 of 100, `offset_s` within 3·σ_t of 0.0037, `sigma_skew_ppm` = 7.07 to three digits,
   the bracket [0, 10] s. On R-05 (no shared mark): `delay_plus_offset = 0` within its interval, and
   `frob ground identify … --field delay` is refused with FROB-REF-879.
2. **Step `clock-battery`.** `frob-conformance run ground.clock --resamples 1000 --seed 1 --json` (which drives
   `ground.ingest` per resample). Per resample: pass when |skew − 100 ppm| ≤ 21 ppm and the registered scored
   instant t* = 4.212 s has |aligned − truth| ≤ 3·σ_t(t*). `e2e_metric clock_pass <count> resamples >= 990
   --gate GATE-GROUND-02`, with the expected count (about 994.5) logged beside it.
3. **Step `clock-controls`.** One-pulse bundle: `e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-876 3 -- frob ground ingest …`.
   Event-fitted offset: `e2e_expect_caught NC-GROUND-0326 -- …` (the detector reports the 32 ms disagreement
   between the offset aligning 4.212 s with 4.180 s and the mark-based offset).
4. **Step `identify-single`.** The nominal rig of R-04 through `excite`, `identify --stage actuators` (passes A,
   B, C), and the later stages. Assert each of J_a, b, τ_c, τ_s, v_s within 10% of truth, the delay minimizer
   within one grid step, misfit after below misfit before, the energy verdict `consistent`, prior card content
   addresses unchanged, candidate versions written, the training manifest's randomization digest unchanged.
5. **Step `identify-battery`.** `frob-conformance run ground.identify --rigs 100 --seed 1 --json`, as a job
   (the script waits on the job handle and survives exit code 8). Three metrics:
   `rigs_within_10pct <k₁> rigs >= 95`; `heldout_error_ratio <ρ> 1 <= 0.70` (sum over rigs of the
   root-mean-square held-out trajectory error with identified cards over that with prior cards, both through
   frozen predictions scored on the sealed validation day); `omitted_law_rejected <k₃> rigs >= 90`; each with
   `--gate GATE-GROUND-04`.
6. **Step `identify-controls`.** `e2e_expect_caught` for: the regularized fit that returns a value on an aliased
   member; the scorer that reads the calibration split (the omitted-law candidate is then admitted); admission
   without the energy check (the wrong-reason fixture is admitted); the writer that mutates a card in place.
7. **Step `record`.** `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-02`; `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-04`.

Log: `target/e2e-logs/ground_identify/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Per command: the command line,
exit code, wall time, the evidence block (class Estimated, reality `synthetic`, determinism class, model and
dataset identities, seed). Per rig: each parameter's estimate, truth, relative error and the 10% threshold;
the delay in grid steps; misfit before and after; the energy residual against the instrument bound; held-out
error with prior and with identified cards. Per battery: the count against its threshold. On failure: the
first rig and parameter out of tolerance with the calibration record's path. Summary: assertions passed and
failed and `gates: ["GATE-GROUND-02", "GATE-GROUND-04"]`. All lines carry `synthetic: true`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 when both gates pass and non-zero when any clause or control fails; in both cases the
      measured values are in the log and in the gate records.
- [ ] The gate records hold: 990-threshold count with seed and expected count; k₁, ρ, k₃ with seeds; the list
      of controls caught.
- [ ] The 100-rig battery resumes after a kill of the daemon without re-identifying finished rigs (the job
      protocol's committed frontier), and the final counts equal an uninterrupted run's.
- [ ] No sealed chunk is read by any `identify` call (asserted from the store's read log).
- [ ] Two runs with the same seeds give identical counts on one machine.
- [ ] The records are written by a session that did not implement the measured beads.

## Considerations

- The clock gate's count is close to its threshold by construction (expected 994.5 against 990); the seed is
  fixed before the first run and never changed after a failure. The log's expected-count line exists so a
  reader can judge a near miss.
- The identification gate allows one repair cycle; a second run of this script after a declared repair uses
  the same seeds and fixture hashes, and the log records both.
- Cost: the 100-rig battery is hours of CPU. The `smoke` battery runs steps 1, 3 and 4 only; the `full`
  battery runs everything.
