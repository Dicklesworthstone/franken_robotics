## Background

Two gates decide whether Ground, the subsystem that compares simulation with measured reality, may issue
reality bands at all. Both are measured on the synthetic rehearsal, and both feed one kill decision
(KILL-BAND: if either fails, bands are withdrawn and reports print `reality: no-claim` with the raw residual
table).

GATE-GROUND-06 (Known answer; TARGET): mean in-cell coverage within 0.01 of 19/21; fraction of resamples below
0.80 within 0.03 of 0.069; zero bands for the shifted cell, the short cell or the unbuilt design.

GATE-GROUND-07 (Known answer; TARGET): the planted drift retires the calibration within 20 episodes in at least
90% of resamples; false retirement at most 5% over 10⁴ null runs; every scope change fires.

The reference arithmetic (FACT): at n = 20 and a 90% target the half-width is the 19th smallest residual, and
conditional coverage is Beta(19, 2), with mean 0.9048, standard deviation 0.0626 and probability 0.0692 of
falling below 0.80. The miss monitor at n = 20 uses p₀ = 0.22 and p₁ = 0.5: four straight misses give 26.7 and
retire; three give 11.7 and do not.

## Scope

In: the script; the coverage battery; the refusal cases; the drift and null batteries; the scope-change and
expiry cases; the decision-core walk; the planner rows; the log and the two gate records. Out: the mechanisms
(BD-393, N30b-07 to N30b-10, BD-394); the proof-obligation batteries' definitions (N30b-24), which this script
runs first.

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/ground_band.sh`, header `bead=N30b-34 milestone=M5 labels=ground,e2e,gate seed=1`. It sources
`scripts/e2e/lib.sh`; every battery's seed is fixed in the script and logged.

1. **Step `obligations`.** `frob-conformance run ground.obligations --json`: logs each proof obligation's
   measured value against its acceptance bound and the union false-alarm fraction of the two monitors, and
   records the decision-policy version in force for the rest of the run.
2. **Step `band-single`.** On the nominal rehearsal cell after scoring: `frob ground band --cell … --metric …
   --level 0.90 --json`. Assert n = 20, index 19, q̂ equal to the 19th smallest residual recomputed by the
   script from the pool listing, the coverage sentence with ±0.063, class Estimated, reality `synthetic`. At
   level 0.95: index 20 (the largest residual). At level 0.99: an infinite band, not a refusal.
3. **Step `band-battery`.** `frob-conformance run ground.band --resamples 1000 --seed 1 --json` on fixture R-08.
   `e2e_metric mean_coverage <v> 1 >= 0.8948 --gate GATE-GROUND-06` and `<= 0.9148`;
   `e2e_metric frac_below_080 <v> 1 >= 0.039 --gate GATE-GROUND-06` and `<= 0.099`; the standard errors (about
   0.002 and 0.008) logged beside them.
4. **Step `band-refusals`.** On R-09: the unbuilt design (`e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-895 3`); the cell with 8
   points (889, payload present 8, needed 19); the new unit with 3 units calibrated (889, present 3, needed 9).
   The shifted cell: a band request for it with no pairs of its own is refused (889), and an attempt to compute
   one from the pooled set is refused (814). `e2e_metric forbidden_bands <count> bands == 0 --gate
   GATE-GROUND-06`. Control: `e2e_expect_caught NC-GROUND-0330 -- …` (the pool keyed by design only issues the
   pooled band and held-out coverage is 16 of 20 in the shifted regime, 36 of 40 overall).
5. **Step `scope-rules`.** A five-metric scorecard at budget 0.10 and n = 20 shows five unbounded bands; a
   two-metric one shows two finite bands at level 0.95. The correlation report prints ρ with its interval, and
   the planted low-correlation cell carries the recommendation `real-only evaluation`.
6. **Step `core`.** `frob cores show dc-07 --json`; walk one cell through uncalibrated, calibrated-exchangeable,
   calibrated-shifted, retired, logging each decision record; force each fallback trigger.
7. **Step `drift-single`.** Ingest fixture R-10 episode by episode; after each, `frob ground band --status`:
   log both e-values against 20. Assert retirement within 20 episodes of the planted change, the reason, the
   status `retired`, the reality line `no-claim`, a band request refused with FROB-REF-897, dependants listed
   stale.
8. **Step `drift-battery`.** `frob-conformance run ground.drift --resamples 1000 --seed 1 --json`:
   `e2e_metric retired_within_20 <v> 1 >= 0.90 --gate GATE-GROUND-07`. `frob-conformance run ground.null --runs
   10000 --seed 1 --json` (fresh calibration of 20, then 200 exchangeable episodes, stopped at the first
   crossing): `e2e_metric false_retirement <v> 1 <= 0.05 --gate GATE-GROUND-07`, with the per-monitor fractions
   logged.
9. **Step `scope-change`.** Seven planted changes, one per cell component (controller, firmware, unit set and
   part replacement, surface, envelope, model identity, instrument calibration): each retires at the first
   ingest or query that shows it. `e2e_metric scope_changes_fired <count> changes == 7 --gate GATE-GROUND-07`.
   Expiry at 91 days and at 201 hours of recorded time.
10. **Step `planner`.** `frob experiment suggest … --json` for the cell before and after calibration: log the
    real-batch rows (k, s̃, value), the simulation row, and which is larger in each state.
11. **Step `record`.** `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-06`; `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-07`.

Log: `target/e2e-logs/ground_band/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Per command: command line, exit
code, wall time, evidence block (class, reality `synthetic`, determinism, identities, seed). Per battery: the
measured value, its threshold, its standard error and the seed. Per refusal: code, samples present and needed,
repairs. Per drift episode: residual rank, miss or cover, both e-values. Per scope change: component, old and
new value, retirement sequence. On failure: the first resample or episode at fault with the pool hash and the
monitor record's path. Summary: assertions passed and failed and `gates: ["GATE-GROUND-06", "GATE-GROUND-07"]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 when every clause of both gates passes, non-zero otherwise; either way all measured
      values are in the log and the gate records.
- [ ] The gate records hold the six measured quantities (mean coverage, fraction below 0.80, forbidden bands,
      retirement fraction, null false-retirement fraction, scope changes fired) with seeds, standard errors and
      the decision-policy version.
- [ ] With `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE` set to `index-n-times`, `pool-by-design`, `min-count-off`, `monitor-resets`,
      `threshold-10` or `scope-ignores-instrument`, the script exits non-zero and names the clause.
- [ ] Both drift clauses are measured under one decision-policy version.
- [ ] The read forms of `ground.band` change no stored state (store digest equal before and after steps 2 and
      5).
- [ ] The records are written by a session that did not implement the measured beads.

## Considerations

- The coverage tolerances are about five and four standard errors, so a correct build passes with wide margin;
  the drift clause's power depends on the fixture's registered post-drift miss probability (at least 0.7,
  giving power about 0.99), and the null clause is first checked by the obligation battery so that the alarm
  level is settled before this gate is measured.
- Cost: 10⁴ null runs of 220 episodes are cheap (residuals only, no physics); the drift battery reuses one set
  of simulated episodes with resampled noise. The whole script is under an hour of CPU (TARGET).
- If KILL-BAND has fired, the script asserts the fallback (residual tables, no bands, no monitors) and still
  runs steps 1 and 9, which the fallback keeps.
