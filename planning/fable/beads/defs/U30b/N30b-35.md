## Background

GATE-GROUND-08 is the gate of as-built ingestion in Ground, the subsystem that compares simulation with
measured reality. The lane admits a scan of a built part with fiducial correspondences, registers it to the
design in closed form with a pose covariance, computes deviation fields, converts the deviations and a scale
reading into posteriors and mass properties, and compiles an as-built model variant that runs beside the
as-designed one. The gate (claim kind: Known answer; TARGET): the as-built known answers of the rehearsal (a
planted rotation and translation and a 0.3 mm wall deviation recovered inside the stated uncertainty; two
fiducials: refused; scale 25.4: unit suspicion raised); the as-built variant has a new `ModelId`.

Its record is read by the kill decision KILL-ASBUILT (fallback: scans are not ingested; as-built data enters as
scalar bench measurements of mass and lengths). The script measures it from the user's side on fixture R-13 of
the rehearsal: one link with four fiducials, planted rotation 2° about (1, 2, 2)/3, translation
(3.0, −1.5, 0.8) mm, fiducial noise 0.02 mm, a wall of nominal 2.5 mm with a planted +0.3 mm deviation over one
patch, a planted scale reading, plus a two-fiducial copy and a copy scaled by 25.4.

## Scope

In: the script; the single case; the coverage battery over noise seeds; the two refusals; the identity check;
the paired run; the audit; the log and the gate record. Out: the mechanisms (BD-395, N30b-11).

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/ground_asbuilt.sh`, header `bead=N30b-35 milestone=M5 labels=ground,e2e,gate seed=1`. It sources
`scripts/e2e/lib.sh` and works in a fresh store.

1. **Step `register`.** `frob ground ingest $D/R-13/scan --design … --part link-3 --json`. Assert: the recovered
   rotation and translation with their 6 × 6 covariance; the planted pose inside the stated 95% region (the
   script computes the Mahalanobis distance of the planted pose from the estimate and compares it with the
   χ² quantile for six degrees of freedom, 12.59); the similarity scale 1 within three standard errors; no
   unit-suspicion flag; the residual reported under the name `registration_residual` and nowhere as an
   uncertainty.
2. **Step `deviation`.** The wall-deviation field's patch mean with its interval contains 0.3 mm; the noise
   screen marks it resolvable. On the variant with a 0.02 mm planted deviation, no deviation is reported and
   the output states the resolvable size.
3. **Step `coverage-battery`.** `frob-conformance run ground.asbuilt --seeds 200 --seed 1 --json`:
   `e2e_metric pose_covered <count> seeds >= 180 --gate GATE-GROUND-08`;
   `e2e_metric deviation_covered <count> seeds >= 180 --gate GATE-GROUND-08`.
4. **Step `refusals`.** The two-fiducial copy: `e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-812 3 -- frob ground ingest …`, with
   the fiducials needed as first repair. Three collinear fiducials: 812. The copy scaled by 25.4: the envelope
   carries `unit_suspicion: true` with scale 25.4 ± its standard error, and a request for a deviation from it
   is refused with 812 and the repair "re-export in the declared unit". A scan with neither fiducials nor an
   external tool identity: 812.
5. **Step `variant`.** Build the as-built variant; read both model identities from the envelope's `bound`
   block; `e2e_assert_eq` they differ; assert that a query for the as-designed identity's fidelity records
   returns none for the as-built identity. Effective density from the scale reading and computed volume equals
   the planted value within its interval; compiled mass equals the scale reading.
6. **Step `paired-run`.** `frob sim run` with the two variants in one batch on the same world addresses; log
   each variant's requirement verdict and the paired difference per metric.
7. **Step `audit`.** The tolerance audit table lists the planted wall feature as `outside` against its 0.2 mm
   allocation, the others `within`, and one feature without allocation as `not-allocated`.
8. **Step `controls`.** `e2e_expect_caught` for: residual reported as uncertainty (pose coverage collapses in
   the battery); two fiducials accepted; silent rescale by 1/25.4; a variant builder that reuses the
   as-designed identity.
9. **Step `record`.** `e2e_gate GATE-GROUND-08`.

Log: `target/e2e-logs/ground_asbuilt/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Per command: command line, exit
code, wall time, evidence block (class Estimated, reality `synthetic`, determinism, identities, seed). For the
registration: estimated and planted rotation vector and translation, the Mahalanobis distance against 12.59,
the scale with its standard error. For the battery: the two counts against 180. For refusals: code, fiducials
found, residual, deviation, scale, repairs. For the variant: both identities and the density. On failure: the
first seed whose region missed, with the scan bundle's path. Summary: assertions passed and failed and
`gates: ["GATE-GROUND-08"]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 when every part of the gate holds and non-zero otherwise, with measured values in the
      log and the gate record either way.
- [ ] The gate record holds: the two coverage counts of 200; the two-fiducial refusal; the unit-suspicion flag;
      both model identities; the controls caught.
- [ ] With `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE` set to `residual-as-uncertainty`, `two-fiducials-ok`, `silent-rescale` or
      `variant-reuses-id`, the script exits non-zero and names the step.
- [ ] Every envelope has `evidence.reality = "synthetic"`.
- [ ] The record is written by a session that did not implement the measured beads.

## Considerations

- "Inside the stated uncertainty" is read as coverage of the 95% region in at least 90% of 200 noise seeds, the
  reading registered with the pilot; the single-case assertion of step 1 can fail by chance in 5% of seeds, so
  the gate rests on the battery and step 1 uses the registered seed.
- If KILL-ASBUILT has fired, the script asserts the fallback: the scan is refused and a bench bundle with a mass
  and two lengths produces a variant with a new identity.
- Runtime is minutes; the 200-seed battery dominates.
