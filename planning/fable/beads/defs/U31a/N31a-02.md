## Background

Foundry is an output-quality subsystem, not a speed lever. That sentence is a commitment with numbers behind it,
and this bead turns it into things a program can check, so that no report, README table or gate record can
drift into claiming more.

The numbers (FACT from the source register; **each is to be re-read from its primary source before a gate cites
it**):

- **Savings against engineered parts are roughly 7 to 30% of link mass.** Reported cases: an industrial forearm
  7%, an exoskeleton arm 10.4%, a hydraulic leg unit 24.5%, and two links of a table-tennis arm 25% and 29%
  (2.27 → 1.70 kg and 0.84 → 0.60 kg; https://arxiv.org/html/2606.26643v1). That last study optimized under
  natural-frequency constraints only, evaluated stiffness, stress and fatigue afterwards, and excluded two links
  as already near their limits. Against primitive shapes the saving is larger and means nothing, because nobody
  ships a solid capsule.
- **Link structure is a small share of a robot.** In one 15 kg quadruped the four legs weigh about 4 kg (27%)
  and the twelve actuators about 6 kg (40%) (https://www.atlantis-press.com/article/15203.pdf). Seven percent
  off the whole leg mass is about 2% of the robot, and thirty percent about 8%, before shafts, gears and
  bearings inside the leg are excluded. The expectation is 2 to 8% of robot mass on this one quadruped, the only
  one with a published mass breakdown; there is no figure for humanoids.
- **It lengthens the loop.** Each design gains finite-element solves per link, and three-dimensional
  optimization of 12 to 20 links is CPU-hours. Nothing here shortens a decision.
- **It is established practice.** Feeding multibody loads into topology optimization is routine in commercial
  tools, and the cited arm study used one. No novelty is claimed.
- **Link fatigue is a safeguard, not an observed failure mode.** Published fatigue analyses of legged robots
  concern gears; a bounded search found no report of a link failing by fatigue. The check exists because an
  optimizer that removes material can create the problem.

**Ceiling, stated before any test.** With link structure a fraction f_L of robot mass and a link-mass saving s,
the direct ceiling on robot-mass saving is f_L·s ≤ 0.27 × 0.30 ≈ 0.08. The mass spiral (total mass
m = m_f/(1 − κ) with loop gain κ of actuator mass on total mass) multiplies a structural saving by 1/(1 − κ)
only if actuators are then re-selected from a discrete catalogue, which is a separate decision with its own
revalidation (HYPOTHESIS; the realized multiplier is measured by bead N31a-25).

This serves the bet "generative structure" (BET-29): links optimized under simulated load histories are lighter
than engineered parts at equal margin; it improves the output and is not counted in the ten-fold thesis. The
program's hygiene rule "the ceiling is computed before any test" applies: a result above its ceiling is an
error in the measurement, not a success.

## Scope

In: the ceiling figures as typed figures of their sources in the project's source register, with a re-read
record for each; `ceiling.rs`, which loads them, computes and prints the ceiling for a design; the report line;
a wording lint over Foundry's user-facing strings and schemas.
Out: the saving itself (the stage-2 comparison of GATE-FOUNDRY-05, seed beads BD-403 and BD-404); the realized
mass-spiral multiplier (N31a-25); the source register's schema, loader and citation lint (NAB-09).

## Technical approach

1. **Re-read record, in the source register.** The figures live in the project's source register
   `registries/sources.toml` (schema `frob.sources/1`, bead NAB-09), not in a file of this crate, so that one
   register answers "was this read, when, and may a gate cite it". The arm study
   (https://arxiv.org/html/2606.26643v1) and the quadruped mass breakdown
   (https://www.atlantis-press.com/article/15203.pdf) are two `[[source]]` entries (kind `public`, topic
   `foundry-ceiling`; the other three reported cases are entries of their own sources, or carry
   `unlocated = true` until located), and each figure is a typed entry of a reading's `figures` list
   (`{ name, value, unit, how = "raw-text", uncertainty }`) with the quoted sentence or table cell in the
   reading's `covers`. Figures: forearm 7%; exoskeleton arm 10.4%; hydraulic leg 24.5%; table-tennis links 25%
   and 29% with the four masses; quadruped legs 4 kg of 15 kg and actuators 6 kg of 15 kg; the statement that
   the arm study constrained natural frequency only and excluded two links. A re-read appends a
   `[[source.reread]]` record (date, session, at_version, result `confirmed`, `corrected`, `not-found` or
   `changed`, note) and never edits the reading. `ceiling.rs` loads the figures through the register's loader
   by source id and figure name and holds no constants of its own. A figure whose latest re-read is
   `corrected` or `changed` takes the re-read value and the difference is entered in the review log; a figure
   whose source is `not-found`, or whose computed flag `reread_before_gate` is true, keeps its value with the
   label `FACT to recheck` and may not be cited by a gate record.
2. **The ceiling function.** `ceiling(design) -> CeilingReport`:
   - f_L = (sum of masses of links whose fill is structural: shell, lattice or solid, excluding actuator,
     battery, sensor and payload bodies) ÷ total mass, from the model's mass roll-up;
   - s_ref = [s_lo, s_hi], the reported range, computed from the register's figures and never typed in:
     s_lo is the smallest of the five reported-case savings (forearm 7%, exoskeleton arm 10.4%, hydraulic leg
     24.5%, table-tennis links 25% and 29%) and s_hi the largest of them rounded up to the next multiple of
     5% (DESIGN: the source range reads "roughly 7 to 30%", and rounding up keeps the ceiling above every
     reported case). Today s_ref = [0.07, 0.30];
   - direct ceiling range = [f_L·s_lo, f_L·s_hi];
   - `spiral_multiplier`: `not-applied` unless an outer actuator re-selection step has been run and
     revalidated (N31a-25), in which case the realized value is printed beside 1/(1 − κ).
   Example: f_L = 0.27 gives [0.019, 0.081].
3. **The report line.** `CeilingReport::line()` renders `ceiling: robot-mass saving at most <f_L·s_hi as
   percent> (link structure is <f_L> of mass; reported link savings <s_lo> to <s_hi>%)`, and the one saving
   formatter `format_saving(ceiling, saving, baseline)` always emits that line before the measured saving. The
   consumers call that formatter and nothing else: the envelope of `foundry.optimize` (BD-403, which depends on
   this bead) and the `structure/` and `baseline.json` records of a packet (N31a-43); each asserts the order at
   its own surface. A measured robot-mass saving above the upper ceiling is flagged `above-ceiling: check the
   baseline` (the usual cause is a baseline that is a primitive solid, not an engineered part).
4. **Baseline rule.** A saving is always stated against the beam-rung section sized to the same envelope cases
   (the engineered baseline), never against the design's primitive Region. `ceiling.rs` refuses to format a
   saving whose baseline record is not a beam-rung result.
5. **Wording lint.** A test scans Foundry's schema descriptions, message catalogue and `CONTRACT.md` for the
   stems `faster`, `speed`, `sooner`, `accelerat`, `time to decision` and for the word `life` used as a result
   noun (ADR-FOUNDRY-06 forbids it; `service duration` and `utilization` are the permitted terms). Allowed
   occurrences are listed with a reason in the test.
6. **Determinism and class.** The ceiling is exact rational arithmetic on masses (D0); its class is that of the
   mass roll-up's weakest input.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every figure above is a typed figure of a source in `registries/sources.toml` with at least one `[[source.reread]]` record carrying a result; none is blank, and `ceiling.rs` contains no literal for any of them.
- [ ] A figure whose `reread_before_gate` flag is true is printed `FACT to recheck`, and a gate record that cites it is rejected.
- [ ] `ceiling()` on a synthetic design with link masses 4.05 kg of 15 kg returns f_L = 0.27 and the range [0.0189, 0.081].
- [ ] `format_saving` emits the ceiling line before the saving on every path, and no other public function formats a saving (the verb-level and packet-level checks of the same order are BD-403's and N31a-43's).
- [ ] A planted `corrected` re-read of the forearm figure to 8% moves s_lo to 0.08 and the lower ceiling at f_L = 0.27 to 0.0216; a planted largest case of 31% moves s_hi to 0.35.
- [ ] A saving computed against a primitive solid baseline is refused by the formatter; against a beam-rung baseline it prints.
- [ ] A planted saving of 12% of robot mass at f_L = 0.27 is flagged `above-ceiling`.
- [ ] The wording lint passes, and fails on a planted schema description containing "faster design decisions" and on a planted result field named `fatigue_life_hours`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry ceiling`:
- `ceiling_range_quadruped`: f_L = 27/100 → [189/10000, 81/1000] exactly.
- `ceiling_excludes_actuator_bodies`: a link body tagged as an actuator housing is not counted in f_L.
- `saving_needs_beam_baseline` (planted primitive baseline refused).
- `above_ceiling_flag`.
- `spiral_not_applied_by_default`: the multiplier field reads `not-applied` with no outer step on record.
- `wording_lint_clean`, `wording_lint_catches_speed_claim`, `wording_lint_catches_life_noun`.
- `source_record_complete`: every figure name resolves in the source register and has a re-read result.
- `corrected_reread_replaces_value`: a planted `corrected` re-read of the forearm figure to 8% moves the lower ceiling to 0.27 × 0.08 = 0.0216 and logs the difference.
- `s_hi_rounds_up_to_5_percent`: largest case 29% → s_hi = 0.30; a planted 31% → 0.35; a planted 30% → 0.30.
- `ceiling_line_precedes_saving`: `format_saving` output on a beam-rung baseline begins with the ceiling line; a planted formatter that appends the line after the saving is caught.
- `unreread_figure_not_citable` (planted: a gate record citing a figure whose `reread_before_gate` is true is rejected).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_ceiling.sh` (this bead's own, built only on its dependencies; it sources the STD-E2E
helper library). Steps: (1) `cargo test -p frob-foundry -- ceiling` through `e2e_cmd` (the known answers, the
planted defects and the wording lint); (2) `cargo run -p frob-foundry --example ceiling -- --json`, an example
program of this bead over the synthetic design of the acceptance criteria (link masses 4.05 kg of 15 kg) with a
beam-rung baseline: `e2e_assert_rational` on f_L = 27/100 and the range [189/10000, 81/1000], and an assertion
that the first line of the formatted saving is the ceiling line; (3) the same example with the planted saving
of 12% asserts `above-ceiling`, and with a primitive-solid baseline asserts that the formatter refuses (exit 3);
(4) the example's source listing: for each figure its source key, value and latest re-read result from
`registries/sources.toml`, asserted non-blank, each `FACT to recheck` figure logged. No `frob` verb is called,
so the evidence block is recorded as absent with reason `no-verb`. Logs to
`target/e2e-logs/foundry_ceiling/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: per line `ts`, `script`, `bead` = N31a-02,
`step`, `event`, the command, exit code and wall time; `metric` lines for f_L, the lower and upper ceiling and
the planted saving against the upper ceiling; on failure the first differing value; a final `summary`. The
verb-level check is a step of `scripts/e2e/foundry_structure_loop.sh` (N31a-54), which runs this script as a
stage: its first assertion after a run is that the envelope's `result.ceiling` precedes `result.saving` in the
transcript and that `saving.robot_mass_fraction ≤ ceiling.upper`, logged with `event: "metric"`, the two values
and their units.

### Known answers and negative controls
Arithmetic known answers: 0.27 × 0.30 = 0.081; 0.27 × 0.07 = 0.0189; table-tennis links (2.27 − 1.70)/2.27 =
25.1% and (0.84 − 0.60)/0.84 = 28.6%. Negative controls: the primitive-baseline saving, the above-ceiling
saving, the two planted wordings. Metamorphic: scaling every mass by a constant leaves f_L unchanged.

## Considerations

- The quadruped breakdown is one robot. For any other class the ceiling uses the design's own f_L and prints
  that the reported saving range comes from other machines.
- If the re-read finds the range materially different, BET-29's falsifier (optimized part less than 7% lighter
  than the engineered part at equal stiffness and fatigue margin) is unchanged; only the printed range moves.
- This bead adds no gate. It exists so that the gate records of GATE-FOUNDRY-05 cite re-read figures.
