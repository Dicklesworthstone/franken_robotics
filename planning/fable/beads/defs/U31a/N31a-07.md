## Background

A structural load case needs every force on the link at one moment: the wrench at each port and the inertial
body load. **The load on a link at one instant** is the wrench at each port (joint interfaces and, for a foot or
wheel, the contact patch) in the link frame, together with the rigid-body acceleration field

  a(x) = a₀ + α × x + ω × (ω × x),  with gravity folded into a₀,

where x is a point of the link in the link frame, a₀ the acceleration of the link-frame origin minus the
gravity vector expressed in the link frame, α the angular acceleration and ω the angular velocity. A rigid link
of density ρ then carries the body load −ρ·a(x) per unit volume (d'Alembert), and the whole set is in
equilibrium.

**The free-body check** is that port wrenches and the body load −ρ·a(x) sum to zero in force and moment. It is
the test that telemetry, frames, sign conventions and mass properties agree: a wrong frame, a reaction applied
with the wrong sign, a missing contact, or mass properties from another model revision all show up as a
residual. Its residual is recorded per sample; a sample above tolerance is dropped and counted, and more than
0.1% dropped (TARGET) refuses the campaign (FROB-REF-167: a load case that is not one instant, or a campaign
failing the free-body check; repair offered: the offending samples and residuals).

Gate (TARGET): GATE-FOUNDRY-02, claim Known answer, required by M5 and recordable at M4. This bead measures its
first clause: free-body residual at most 10⁻⁶ relative on the referee and 10⁻³ on single-precision tiers.

## Scope

In: the `LinkLoad` record; closed-form resultants of the body load; the residual and its scaling; the drop
rule and the campaign refusal; off-port contacts; the residual statistics in the verb's output.
Out: the trace reader (N31a-06); conversion of a wrench to tractions (N31a-08); unit loads (N31a-09); choice of
envelope cases and the refusal of assembled cases (N31a-10).

## Technical approach

1. **Record** (`loads/sample.rs`, DESIGN):
   ```rust
   pub struct LinkLoad {
       addr: WorldAddr, tick: u64, side: JumpSide,        // one instant of one episode
       ports: SmallVec<[(PortId, Wrench); 4]>,            // wrench ON the link, about the port origin r_p, link frame
       a0: Vec3, alpha: Vec3, omega: Vec3,                // body field; a0 = accel of frame origin − gravity
       model: ModelId,
   }
   ```
   A `LinkLoad` has no constructor from parts taken at different (addr, tick): it is built only by the reader
   from one stream record (the type-level half of INV-FOUNDRY-02). The mass properties used in step 2 must
   come from the same `ModelId` as the run; a mismatch is checked before any residual is computed and refuses
   the campaign with FROB-REF-167 (DESIGN: a load case not of one model is not one instant of the run), with
   JSON pointer `model`, both identities in the payload, `retry: after-change` and exit code 3.
2. **Resultants of the body load.** With mass m, centre of mass c and inertia I_O about the link-frame origin:
   - a_c = a₀ + α × c + ω × (ω × c);
   - F_b = −m·a_c;
   - M_b = −(m·c × a₀ + I_O·α + ω × (I_O·ω)), about the origin.
   These follow from integrating −ρ·a(x) and x × (−ρ·a(x)) over the link.
3. **Residual.** r_F = Σ_p f_p + F_b; r_M = Σ_p (r_p × f_p + m_p) + M_b. Scales: S_F = Σ_p ‖f_p‖ + ‖F_b‖;
   S_M = Σ_p ‖r_p × f_p + m_p‖ + ‖M_b‖. Relative residual ε = max(‖r_F‖/S_F, ‖r_M‖/S_M). Floors (DESIGN):
   when S_F < 10⁻³ N and S_M < 10⁻⁴ N·m the sample is `unloaded` and passes with ε = 0.
4. **Tolerance by tier.** ε_tol = 10⁻⁶ for the referee (`f64`) and 10⁻³ for single-precision tiers; the tier
   comes from the run's evidence. Both are TARGETs.
5. **Drop rule.** A sample with ε > ε_tol is dropped from load extraction for that link and counted. If the
   dropped share over a link exceeds 0.1% of its samples the campaign is refused for that link with
   FROB-REF-167. Payload: link, share dropped, the 20 worst samples (address, tick, r_F, r_M, ε), the median
   residual wrench of the dropped set and its line of action (which usually points at an unaccounted contact or
   a frame error), and `retry: after-change`. Exit code 3.
6. **Off-port contacts (DESIGN).** A contact wrench on a link that has no declared contact-patch port (a knee
   striking the ground) enters the free-body sum, so the check still closes, but the sample is tagged
   `off-port` and excluded from that link's envelope cases and spectra, because the unit-load basis has no
   coordinate for it. Off-port samples count toward the same 0.1% budget. When off-port samples are what
   exceed it, the refusal's repairs follow the order of FROB-REF-167's row in N31-REF: the row's repair 1
   (rebuild a case from one sample) does not apply to a campaign failure and is left out; the row's repair 2
   is rendered as the change to the port map it points at, "declare a contact port on link L", and is the
   first; then the row's repair 3, "re-run the campaign on the referee for the named worlds"; then the row's
   repair 4 in its off-port form, "the task makes unplanned contact: review the gait".
7. **Statistics in the result of `foundry.loads`:** per link the maximum, 99.9th percentile and median of ε,
   the dropped and off-port counts and shares, and the tolerance in force.
8. **Precision.** The residual is evaluated in `f64` whatever the tier, with wrenches widened exactly; cross
   products use fused operations only where the deterministic profile allows.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-02, first clause: on the reference quadruped's campaign of the script below the maximum relative residual is at most 10⁻⁶ on the referee and at most 10⁻³ on each single-precision tier, with the measured values logged per tier (the gate's value is recorded from the receipt of N31a-53's script, the one recorder of the gate; convention 9).
- [ ] A link spun at ω = (0, 0, 10) rad/s about an axis 0.1 m from its centre of mass with m = 2 kg needs a port force of magnitude m·ω²·d = 20 N toward the axis; the check closes to rounding.
- [ ] A static link of 2 kg held at one port under gravity 9.80665 m/s² needs a port force of 19.6133 N upward; the check closes.
- [ ] A campaign with a planted sign flip on one joint's reaction is refused with FROB-REF-167 and the payload names that link.
- [ ] A campaign whose mass properties come from a different `ModelId` than the run is refused with FROB-REF-167 (pointer `model`, exit code 3) before any residual is computed.
- [ ] Dropped share exactly at 0.1% passes; one more dropped sample refuses.
- [ ] An off-port contact is counted and excluded, and the refusal lists the port-declaration repair first, then the re-run on the referee for the named worlds, then "review the gait" (FROB-REF-167's row order, step 6).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry loads::freebody`:
- `static_hang_closes`: m = 2 kg, c = (0, 0, −0.1) m, port at origin, a₀ = (0, 0, 9.80665) (gravity folded, link at rest): port force (0, 0, 19.6133) N, port moment 0; residual below 10⁻¹².
- `pure_spin_closes`: the centripetal case above; also the moment balance with a non-principal inertia I_O = diag(0.02, 0.03, 0.01) + product term I_xy = 0.004 kg·m², where ω × (I_O·ω) ≠ 0.
- `angular_accel_closes`: α = (5, 0, 0) rad/s², I_O = diag(0.02, 0.03, 0.01), c = 0: port moment (0.1, 0, 0) N·m.
- `two_port_link_closes`: wrenches at r = (0, 0, 0) and (0, 0, −0.25) m on an accelerating link, generated from the rigid-body equations with a seeded draw; 10³ cases; residual below 10⁻¹⁰.
- `relative_scale_and_floor`; `unloaded_sample_passes`.
- `drop_budget_boundary` (1,000,000 samples: 1,000 dropped passes; 1,001 refuses).
- `sign_flip_refused_167`; `wrong_frame_refused_167` (a wrench left in the world frame); `stale_model_refused_167` (pointer `model`; no residual computed).
- `off_port_contact_tagged_and_excluded`; `off_port_repairs_in_row_order`: with the off-port share at 0.2% the
  refusal is FROB-REF-167 and its repairs are, in order, "declare a contact port on link L", the re-run on the
  referee, "review the gait" (no "rebuild the case from one sample").
- `f32_tier_uses_1e_3`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `forget-gravity` (a₀ without gravity), `drop-omega-term` (omits ω × (I·ω)), `moment-about-wrong-point` (omits r_p × f_p).

### Known answers and negative controls
Hand values: 2 kg × 9.80665 = 19.6133 N; 2 × 10² × 0.1 = 20 N; I·α = 0.02 × 5 = 0.1 N·m. Negative controls: the
three planted defects and the sign flip. Metamorphic: rotating the link frame by any rotation leaves ε
unchanged; scaling all forces and the mass by k leaves ε unchanged. Determinism: residuals are identical twice
on one machine and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- KILL-LOADS: if this gate fails after one repair cycle, load cases are declared by hand from statics with a
  stated dynamic factor and spectra and fatigue read `no-claim` (N31a-13).
- The interval-mean wrench and the tick-mean acceleration balance only if the stepper reports both from the
  same discrete momentum update. If a tier reports accelerations from a different stage of its step, the
  residual sits near the tier's step error and the 10⁻³ bound is what catches it.
- Cable forces at cable ports are not in the sum (cables are `no-claim` in this chapter); a design whose cable
  loads matter will fail this check, which is the right outcome.
- Flexible links violate the rigid-body field; the structure loop assumes links rigid at the rate of load
  extraction, and the frequency-separation rule (N31a-12) is where that assumption is tested.
