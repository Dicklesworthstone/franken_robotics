## Background

A load campaign holds millions of samples, and stress must be known at many places for each. Solving a
finite-element problem per sample is out of the question. The structural model is linear, so stress anywhere
is linear in the *load coordinates* of the link, and a handful of solves suffices.

**Unit loads.** The load coordinates are six per port, except the port used as a statically determinate 3-2-1
restraint, plus twelve for the body field: three for a₀ (the acceleration of the link-frame origin with gravity
folded in), three for the angular acceleration α, and six for the products ω_i·ω_j of the angular velocity.
The products appear because the centripetal term is ω × (ω × x) = (ω·ωᵀ − ‖ω‖²·I)·x, which is linear in
ω_x², ω_y², ω_z², ω_x·ω_y, ω_y·ω_z, ω_z·ω_x. A two-port link has 6 + 12 = 18 coordinates and a three-port link
12 + 12 = 24 (FACT, arithmetic). **One solve per coordinate gives a stress influence matrix, and the stress
history at any gauge over a whole campaign is then a matrix product.**

Two products come out of it. *Spectra* drive fatigue: signed stress histories at gauges, in episode order. The
*governing instants* of each gauge (the samples of largest stress at that gauge) join the envelope cases at
check time.

Proof obligation PO-FOUNDRY-01: the unit-load superposition reproduces a direct solve of any campaign sample;
tested on 10³ random samples with relative difference at most 10⁻⁹ on the referee. It is a clause of
GATE-FOUNDRY-02 ("superposition equals direct solves").

## Scope

In: the coordinate map from a `LinkLoad` to ℓ ∈ ℝⁿ; the unit cases with their equilibrating restraint-port
load; the influence matrix for elements and for gauges; the superposition test; streaming computation of gauge
histories and their storage as turning points; governing instants per gauge.
Out: traction patterns (N31a-08); envelope cases by direction (N31a-10); where gauges are placed and how cycles
are counted (N31a-21); the transient path for impact windows (N31a-12).

## Technical approach

1. **Coordinates** (`loads/unit.rs`). For a link with ports p₀ (the restraint port; DESIGN: the proximal joint
   port) and p₁ … p_{P−1}:
   ℓ = [f(p₁), m(p₁), …, f(p_{P−1}), m(p_{P−1}), a₀, α, q], with
   q = (ω_x², ω_y², ω_z², ω_x·ω_y, ω_y·ω_z, ω_z·ω_x); n = 6·(P − 1) + 12.
   The wrench at p₀ is not a coordinate: equilibrium determines it, and the free-body check (N31a-07) has
   already confirmed the recorded one agrees.
2. **Unit case j.** Apply coordinate j with value one in its SI unit: a traction pattern at its port, or the
   volume load −ρ·a_j(x) for a body coordinate (a_j(x) = e_k, e_k × x, or Q_j·x with Q_j the matrix of the
   product term). Add at p₀ the traction pattern of the equilibrating wrench W₀ = −(resultant of the applied
   load), so the applied set is self-equilibrated. Remove rigid-body modes with a 3-2-1 restraint on three
   non-collinear nodes of the p₀ boss: three displacement components fixed at the first node, two at the
   second, one at the third. With a self-equilibrated load the six reactions vanish; their largest magnitude
   relative to the applied load is recorded per case and must be at most 10⁻⁹.
3. **Solves.** One stiffness factorization per geometry and mesh, n right-hand sides, as one sibling job
   (N31a-04). Output per case: nodal displacements and element stress tensors (six components, element
   centroid; DESIGN) in the link frame.
4. **Influence matrix.** S ∈ ℝ^(6E × n): column j holds the stresses of unit case j over E elements. For a
   gauge g (an element or surface location with a plane normal n_g), the gauge row is
   h_g[j] = n_gᵀ·σ_j(g)·n_g, so the signed gauge stress of a sample is s_g = h_g·ℓ. Memory: 6·E·n doubles; for
   E = 2 × 10⁵ and n = 18 that is about 173 MB (DESIGN arithmetic), within the job's envelope; the gauge
   matrix H (G × n) is tiny.
5. **Superposition test (PO-FOUNDRY-01).** Draw 10³ samples from the campaign by address hash (a registered
   stream, so the draw is reproducible). For each, compute σ_sup = S·ℓ and σ_dir from a direct solve with the
   sample's full load (all port tractions, the body field, the same restraint). Assert
   ‖σ_sup − σ_dir‖_∞ ≤ 10⁻⁹·‖σ_dir‖_∞ on the referee lane (`f64`, direct sparse solve). The direct solves reuse
   the factorization, so the test costs 10³ back-substitutions.
6. **Spectra, streamed.** For each chunk of campaign samples in duty order (episode order, tick order; both
   sides of a jump in order), compute the G × chunk block H·[ℓ(t)] and hand each gauge's values to its
   consumer (the cycle counter of N31a-21). Samples dropped by the free-body check or tagged off-port are
   skipped and counted per gauge. Histories are never materialized whole: with 10⁷ samples and 300 gauges they
   would be 3 × 10⁹ values.
7. **Stored spectrum.** Per gauge, the sequence of turning points (local extrema after merging monotone runs),
   with each point's world address and tick, in `spectrum/<class>/<gauge>.tp` (DESIGN). Turning points are
   lossless for rainflow counting and are what a later reader, the referee rescaling rule and the lifecycle
   crate consume. Also stored: skipped-sample count, the influence-matrix hash and the campaign identity.
8. **Governing instants.** While streaming, keep per gauge the sample of largest positive and largest negative
   s_g (ties: earliest in duty order). Each is a real instant with its address and tick, and is added to the
   case list of the stress check (N31a-19) with the tag `gauge-governing`.
9. **Class and labels.** All outputs Estimated. The linear model's validity (small strain, linear elasticity,
   links rigid at the sampling rate) is stated in the record; the `model-conditional` label of a sample is
   carried to every gauge value it produces.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A two-port link has 18 coordinates and a three-port link 24; the coordinate order is fixed and documented.
- [ ] Every unit case's restraint reactions are at most 10⁻⁹ of the applied load; a planted non-equilibrated unit case (the p₀ pattern omitted) is caught by this check.
- [ ] PO-FOUNDRY-01 / GATE-FOUNDRY-02: on 10³ random campaign samples the superposed stress field equals the direct solve within 10⁻⁹ relative on the referee; the measured maximum is recorded.
- [ ] The centripetal products are exact: for ω = (1, 2, 3) rad/s the superposed body load equals the direct ω × (ω × x) field at 100 random points to rounding.
- [ ] A gauge history computed by streaming equals the history computed by per-sample superposition on a 10⁴-sample subset, bit for bit.
- [ ] Turning-point storage reproduces the rainflow count of the full history on the known-answer sequence.
- [ ] Each governing instant replays to its sample.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry loads::unit loads::influence loads::spectra`:
- `coordinate_count_18_and_24`.
- `centripetal_is_linear_in_products`: ω = (1, 2, 3): q = (1, 4, 9, 2, 6, 3); at x = (0.1, 0, 0), ω × (ω × x) = (ω·x)·ω − ‖ω‖²·x = (0.1, 0.2, 0.3) − (1.4, 0, 0) = (−1.3, 0.2, 0.3) m/s².
- `unit_cases_self_equilibrated` (reactions ≤ 10⁻⁹); `missing_equilibrating_pattern_caught` (planted).
- `superposition_equals_direct_1e_9` on a `synthetic` tube link with two ports (10³ samples).
- `cantilever_by_unit_loads`: beam 0.20 m × 20 × 10 mm fixed at p₀ pattern, 50 N transverse at p₁: root bending stress from S·ℓ within 5% of 30 MPa outside the no-claim zone extrapolated by the beam formula at the sampled section (stress 6·P·(L − x)/(b·h²) at distance x from the root).
- `gauge_row_is_normal_stress`: for a uniaxial unit stress field the row equals cos²θ of the angle between the gauge normal and the load axis.
- `streaming_equals_batch_bitwise`.
- `turning_points_lossless_for_rainflow`: sequence −2, 1, −3, 5, −1, 3, −4, 4, −2 with an inserted monotone point (0 between −3 and 5) stores nine points and counts ranges 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 with counts 0.5, 1.5, 0.5, 1.0, 0.5.
- `dropped_samples_skipped_and_counted`.
- `governing_instant_is_argmax_with_tie_rule`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `swap-two-product-columns` (ω_x·ω_y with ω_y·ω_z), `gravity-not-folded` (a₀ without gravity in the coordinate map), `unsorted-chunks` (spectra fed out of duty order; caught by the turning-point comparison).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_loads.sh` (N31a-53) asserts `result.superposition.max_rel ≤ 1e-9` with the sample count
1,000, and logs n, E, solve time, matrix bytes, reactions maximum, and per gauge the number of turning points
and skipped samples.

### Known answers and negative controls
The centripetal vector (−1.3, 0.2, 0.3); the cantilever (KA-737: root stress 30 MPa); the rainflow sequence
(KA-742). Negative controls: the three planted defects and the missing equilibrating pattern. Metamorphic:
scaling every load coordinate by k scales every stress by k; the gauge history is invariant under re-chunking.
Determinism: the influence matrix and spectra are bitwise identical twice on one machine and across chunk
sizes; across airlock modes the bytes are equal.

## Considerations

- The 10⁻⁹ tolerance presumes a direct solve in `f64`. If the sibling job uses an iterative solver the
  tolerance is unreachable, and the gate must fail visibly; the fix is the solver setting, not the tolerance.
- Superposition holds only while stiffness does not depend on load. Stress stiffening near buckling violates
  it, which is one reason the buckling factor must be at least 2 (N31a-20).
- The influence matrix belongs to one geometry and mesh. Every loop iteration that changes geometry rebuilds
  it; the record carries the mesh hash so a stale matrix cannot be applied.
- KILL-LOADS fallback: with hand-declared cases there are no spectra and fatigue reads `no-claim`.
