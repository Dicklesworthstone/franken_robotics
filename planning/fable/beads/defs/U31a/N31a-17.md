## Background

A topology optimizer returns a density field on a grid. A part is a surface. The step between them decides
whether what gets checked and exported is the thing that was optimized.

**Extraction.** The surface is the level ½ of the projected density. Smoothing may change volume by at most 1%
(TARGET) and is always followed by the independent check.

The second sentence is ADR-FOUNDRY-05 at work: the optimizer never checks its own result, because its stress
aggregate is not a bound and because thresholding changes the structure. A thin member at density 0.55 becomes
solid; one at 0.45 disappears. So stress, buckling, modes and fatigue are evaluated on a separately generated
body-fitted tetrahedral mesh of the extracted surface: a different discretization from the optimizer's cut
cells. The independence is of mesh and formulation, not of codebase, since the optimization sibling itself
depends on the solid-mechanics sibling. Invariant INV-FOUNDRY-03: no geometry enters a packet without passing
the independent check on a mesh the optimizer did not use; the packet manifest cites the check's mesh hash.

The meshing sibling provides incremental Delaunay tetrahedralization on exact predicates with quality
refinement and audits, and constrained recovery and labelled volumetric meshing of piecewise-linear complexes.
Its documentation lists full boundary conformity as successor scope (upstream item FD-FSIM-30). Interim
posture: conformity is measured per mesh and recorded with the structural evidence. Switch-over: replace the
measurement with the stated bound where it applies. **OPEN-FOUNDRY-1** asks whether it meshes an arbitrary
optimized surface at all; the fail-closed default, implemented here, is that if it does not, the independent
check is a second cut-cell solve at a different resolution and background offset, and results are labelled
`check: same-family`. The question blocks GATE-FOUNDRY-04, whose second clause is "the extracted surface
meshes and passes the mesh audit on 20 of 20 fixtures".

## Scope

In: iso-surface extraction; exact re-insertion of port bosses; removal of disconnected fragments; smoothing
under the volume budget; surface validity; body-fitted meshing with audit; the conformity measurement; the
mesh hash; the `same-family` fallback trigger.
Out: the optimization (N31a-15); the solves on the mesh (N31a-19 to N31a-21); the second cut-cell solve used by
the fallback (N31a-19); export formats and decimation (N31a-34, N31a-35).

## Technical approach

1. **Iso-surface** (`topo/extract.rs`). Extract the level set {ρ̃ = ½} of the projected density on the
   optimizer's grid by a deterministic contouring of each cell (fixed case table, fixed vertex order), giving a
   closed triangle mesh. Record the volume V₀ enclosed.
2. **Bosses.** The frozen port bosses are united with the extracted solid using their exact declared geometry,
   so interface faces, bores and patches are the designed surfaces and not staircase approximations. Interface
   triangles are tagged; later stages may not move them.
3. **Fragments.** Connected components not attached to a boss are removed; closed internal cavities are kept
   and counted (they matter for manufacturability). Volume removed is charged to the budget of step 4.
4. **Smoothing.** Volume-preserving smoothing (alternating shrink and inflate passes with fixed weights and a
   fixed pass count; DESIGN) of non-interface vertices. After each pass pair compute V; the accepted result is
   the last one with |V − V₀|/V₀ ≤ 1% (fragments included). If even one pass pair exceeds the budget the
   unsmoothed surface is used and the record says so. Members thinner than the minimum length scale after
   smoothing are a finding, since the optimizer was supposed to forbid them.
5. **Validity and export-exact vertices.** Vertex coordinates are expressed in the part frame (origin at the
   centre of the part's bounding box, axes of the link frame) and rounded to values exactly representable in
   single precision, so that the polyhedron that is meshed, checked and weighed is one that every export
   format can carry without change (the export bead refuses a surface without this property). The surface
   must then be watertight, manifold and consistently oriented, verified through the sibling's quarantine
   path; a failure is repaired only by re-extraction at a finer grid, never by ad hoc stitching. The surface
   triangle count is kept at or below the STEP export bound of 285,712 triangles for a closed surface, by
   choosing the extraction grid accordingly, so that decimation is not needed after the check.
6. **Body-fitted mesh.** Tetrahedralize the interior with constrained boundary recovery; target edge length
   from the minimum length scale (at least three elements across the thinnest member; DESIGN); run the
   sibling's quality refinement and audit (no inverted element; dihedral angles within the audit's bounds).
7. **Conformity measurement.** Record: the two-sided Hausdorff distance between the mesh boundary and the
   input surface (limit: 0.05 of the minimum length scale, the conformity bound registered by the
   OPEN-FOUNDRY-1 experiment N31b-24, so that the experiment, this stage and the gate battery apply one
   number), the relative volume difference (limit 0.1%),
   and whether every tagged interface face is represented exactly. A mesh outside the limits is rejected and
   regenerated once at a finer target; a second failure triggers step 9.
8. **Hash.** `mesh_hash` = BLAKE3 over canonical vertex coordinates and element connectivity. It is written
   into every check result and into the packet manifest (INV-FOUNDRY-03). `optimizer_grid_hash` is recorded
   beside it; the two are different objects by construction, and a test asserts the check never receives the
   optimizer's grid.
9. **Fallback (OPEN-FOUNDRY-1 default).** If meshing fails twice, the stage returns `MeshOutcome::SameFamily`
   with a second cut-cell discretization request: 1.5 times finer than the optimizer's grid (spacing 2/3 of
   it) and the background origin shifted by half an optimizer cell along each axis (DESIGN constants, those
   of the OPEN-FOUNDRY-1 record of N31b-24, fixed so results are reproducible; with this ratio and shift no
   node of the second grid coincides with a node of the first, since h/2 + k·(2h/3) = m·h has no integer
   solution).
   Everything computed from it carries `StructLabel::CheckSameFamily`, printed as `check: same-family`.
10. **Mesh refinement on request.** The stress check asks for refined meshes; refinement is done by this stage
    (uniform or by an element indicator), re-audited, and gets its own hash and conformity record.
11. **Class.** Estimated. Determinism: same bits twice on one machine.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The whole stage (extract, unite bosses, remove fragments, smooth, validate, mesh, audit, measure conformity, hash) is callable on a density-field fixture file and returns one record per fixture: audit result, Hausdorff distance and volume difference against their limits, interface exactness, `mesh_hash`, and whether the `same-family` fallback was taken. This is the entry the battery N31a-18 (which depends on this bead) runs over its 20 extraction fixtures; the 20-of-20 measurement of GATE-FOUNDRY-04's second clause is N31a-18's battery, and the gate row is recorded by N31a-54 from that battery's receipt. This bead's own fixtures (the sphere, the KA-744 tube, the fragment-and-cavity part, the recovery-defeating part) pass through the entry here.
- [ ] Extraction of a sampled sphere of radius 20 mm on a 1 mm grid encloses a volume within 1% of 3.351 × 10⁻⁵ m³; the smoothed surface stays within 1% of the extracted volume.
- [ ] Plain shrinking smoothing (a planted defect) exceeds the 1% budget within a few passes and is rejected by the guard.
- [ ] Boss interface faces are geometrically exact after extraction and smoothing (zero deviation at tagged vertices).
- [ ] A floating fragment in a fixture is removed and its volume charged to the budget; an internal cavity is kept and counted.
- [ ] A mesh with a planted boundary deviation above 0.05 of the length scale fails the conformity measurement; one at 0.04 passes.
- [ ] A fixture built to defeat boundary recovery yields the fallback, and every downstream number is labelled `check: same-family`.
- [ ] The check stage never receives the optimizer's grid: passing it is a type error.

### Known answers and negative controls
Sphere volume 3.351 × 10⁻⁵ m³. The mass-properties tube (KA-744: mass 0.0950 kg for ρ = 2,700 kg/m³, radii 15
and 13 mm, length 0.20 m) is extracted from an analytic density field and its enclosed volume must be within
1% of 3.5186 × 10⁻⁵ m³ (so the mass computed from it by N31a-23 is within 1% of 0.0950 kg); its control is
that the volume enclosure contains the exact volume. Negative controls: the three planted defects.
Metamorphic: translating the density field by whole cells translates the surface and changes no volume;
refining the grid moves the surface by less than one coarse cell. Determinism: surface and mesh bytes are
identical twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- Extraction at exactly ½ is a convention; what matters is that the same convention is used when the optimizer
  reports volume and when the surface is cut, and that the check decides.
- Smoothing rounds re-entrant corners and so hides stress concentrations less than it removes staircase
  artefacts; the 1% budget is what keeps it from quietly thinning members.
- If the fallback is taken often, ADR-FOUNDRY-05's independence is weakened to "same family, different grid".
  The label keeps that visible, and the rate of fallback is part of the GATE-FOUNDRY-04 record.
- Mesh size drives the cost of every later solve; the target of three elements across the thinnest member is a
  floor, and refinement is driven by the stress check.
