## Background

GATE-FOUNDRY-04 (claim: Known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): **two-bar compliance within 10% and bar angle
within 5°; the extracted surface meshes and passes the mesh audit on 20 of 20 fixtures.**

The first clause anchors the topology rung to a problem whose optimum is known in closed form. A load P acts
at distance L from a wall in a design domain of height 2L. The least-volume structure at a fixed stress is two
straight bars from the wall to the load point at ±45°. Derivation: in a symmetric two-bar truss whose bars make
the angle θ with the wall normal, each bar carries the force P/(2·sin θ) over the length L/cos θ; at stress σ
its volume is
P·L/(σ·sin θ·cos θ), proportional to 1/(sin θ·cos θ) and least at θ = 45°, where it equals 2·P·L/σ_allow. At
45° each bar has length √2·L and force P/√2, and with total volume V the bar area is A = V/(2·√2·L); the
compliance (load times deflection at the load point) is Σ N²·l/(E·A) = 4·P²·L²/(E·V). The same anchor serves
the independent check, which is why it is called out as one of the two known answers that tie both
discretizations together.

The second clause is an admission test of the path from density field to analysis mesh, on shapes that
resemble what the optimizer produces. It is also where the open question about the meshing sibling
(OPEN-FOUNDRY-1) gets its evidence.

A gate that nothing can fail validates nothing, so each clause has planted failures.

## Scope

In: the two-bar fixture in two forms (thin slab and full three-dimensional); the measurement of bar angle and
compliance; the twenty extraction fixtures; the planted failures; the gate record.
Out: the mechanisms under test (N31a-15, N31a-17); the user-side script (N31a-54), which re-measures the gate
through the `frob` binary.

## Technical approach

1. **Two-bar fixture** (`crates/frob-foundry/tests/fixtures/twobar/`, `synthetic`). L = 0.10 m; domain
   0.10 × 0.20 × 0.01 m (a slab, so the problem is effectively planar) with the wall face fully supported;
   load P = 1,000 N in the slab plane, perpendicular to the wall normal, at mid-height of the free edge on a
   frozen boss of 8 mm; E = 69 GPa, ν = 0.33; target volume fraction 12%, i.e. V = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ m³; minimum length
   scale 4 mm. Hand values: compliance 4·P²·L²/(E·V) = 4 × 10⁶ × 10⁻²/(6.9 × 10¹⁰ × 2.4 × 10⁻⁵) = 0.02415 J; at
   σ_allow = 138 MPa the least volume would be 2·P·L/σ_allow = 1.449 × 10⁻⁶ m³ (bar area 5.12 mm², bar force
   707.1 N), which is too thin to resolve and is checked only as arithmetic.
2. **Measurements.**
   - *Bar angle*: threshold the result, split it into upper and lower halves at the line through the load
     point along the wall normal, and take the
     principal axis of each half's material (largest eigenvector of the second-moment matrix of solid cell
     centres, excluding the boss and a 10 mm band at the wall). The angle to the wall normal must be
     45° ± 5° for each bar.
   - *Compliance*: from the independent check's displacement at the load boss on the body-fitted mesh of the
     extracted part, P·δ, compared with 4·P²·L²/(E·V) evaluated at the *extracted* volume V; within 10%.
   - Both drivers of the topology rung are measured.
3. **Three-dimensional variant.** Domain 0.10 × 0.20 × 0.04 m with the load spread over a boss through the
   depth; the same angle test in the projection; compliance within 10% of the planar formula. It exists to
   exercise the octree lane; it is reported and is not a second threshold.
4. **Twenty extraction fixtures** (density fields, analytic or frozen from optimizer runs; each with its
   expected topology):
   two-bar slab; two-bar three-dimensional; a straight tube (the mass-properties tube: radii 15 and 13 mm,
   length 0.20 m); a tapered tube; a cantilever with three internal holes; an L-bracket with a re-entrant
   corner; a three-port Y link; a link with a through keep-out for a cable; a link with an actuator pocket;
   a lattice-like member field at the minimum length scale; a shell with a closed internal cavity; a part with
   a floating fragment; a part with a member at density 0.5 ± 0.05 (threshold sensitivity); a curved banana
   link; a thin plate with a hole; a torsion box; a part touching the keep-in boundary; a part with two
   bosses 5 mm apart; a coarse-grid part (two cells across the thinnest member); and the frozen coarse result
   of the reference quadruped's lower-leg class.
   For each: extract, smooth, validate, mesh, audit, measure conformity. Pass = audit passed and conformity
   within limits on the body-fitted path (a fixture that needed the `same-family` fallback counts as a
   failure for this gate and is reported by name).
5. **Planted failures (registered in the negative-control registry).**
   - `twobar-swap-support-and-load`: the support moved to the load boss and the load to the wall. The result
     is not two bars at 45° and the test must fail on angle or compliance.
   - `twobar-single-load-direction-flipped-sign-in-sensitivity`: the optimizer piles material away from the
     load path; compliance misses by more than 10%.
   - `extract-threshold-0.4`: volume differs from the optimizer's by more than 1%.
   - `mesh-audit-disabled`: a fixture with an intentionally inverted element passes meshing; the battery's own
     re-audit must catch it.
6. **Gate record.** Angle of each bar, compliance ratio for each driver, the 20 fixture outcomes with
   conformity numbers, the count on the body-fitted path, commit, stack-lock digest, hardware fingerprint.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both drivers produce two bars at 45° ± 5° on the slab fixture; the measured angles are recorded.
- [ ] Compliance of the extracted part is within 10% of 4·P²·L²/(E·V) at its own extracted volume, for both drivers; the ratios are recorded.
- [ ] 20 of 20 extraction fixtures mesh on the body-fitted path and pass the audit and the conformity limits.
- [ ] Each of the four planted failures makes the battery fail at the named assertion.
- [ ] The gate record for GATE-FOUNDRY-04 is written with measured values, not targets.
- [ ] If any fixture takes the `same-family` fallback, the gate is recorded as not passed, the fixture is named, and the record is attached to the OPEN-FOUNDRY-1 decision.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry --test topology_battery` (feature `structure`; long tests are tagged and run in the
gate lane):
- `twobar_hand_values`: 0.02415 J at V = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ m³; least volume 1.449 × 10⁻⁶ m³ at 138 MPa; bar force 707.1 N; bar area 5.12 mm² and stress 138.0 MPa.
- `twobar_volume_is_least_at_45_degrees`: 1/(sin θ·cos θ) at 35°, 45°, 55° is 2.128, 2.000, 2.128.
- `principal_axis_angle_measure`: on an analytic bar field at 40° the measure returns 40° ± 0.5°.
- `twobar_driver_a`, `twobar_driver_b`: angle and compliance assertions.
- `extraction_fixture_<name>` × 20.
- `planted_<name>` × 4, each asserting failure at the named step.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_structure_loop.sh` (bead N31a-54) runs the two-bar fixture through
`frob foundry optimize --fixture twobar --rung topology --json` followed by `frob foundry check`, and asserts
the two thresholds from the envelopes; it logs both bar angles, the compliance ratio with its 10% threshold,
and the 20-fixture table from `cargo test` output parsed into `metric` lines.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-739 (two-bar optimum: bars at 45°; compliance 4·P²·L²/(E·V); minimum volume 2·P·L/σ_allow; control:
swapping the support and the load port is caught). KA-168 (construction C-35: volume at fixed stress
proportional to 1/(sin θ·cos θ), least at 45°). KA-744 (the tube) as an extraction fixture. Negative controls:
the four planted failures. Metamorphic: mirroring the slab about the load line leaves both angles unchanged;
doubling P leaves the layout unchanged and multiplies compliance by four. Determinism: fixture outcomes are
identical twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- The closed form is a truss result; finite bars with a boss and a clamped wall differ from it, which is why
  the tolerance is 10% and why compliance is compared at the extracted volume.
- The 12% volume fraction is a DESIGN choice that makes the bars about 8 mm wide, resolvable at a 4 mm length
  scale. A much lower fraction would test grid resolution, not the method.
- The fixture list is frozen once the gate is first recorded; adding a fixture afterwards is a recorded change,
  and removing one needs a reason on file.
