## Background

ADR-FOUNDRY-05: **the optimizer never checks its own result. Stress, buckling, modes and fatigue are evaluated
on a separately generated body-fitted mesh.** Reason: the optimizer's stress aggregate is not a bound on the
maximum, and thresholding a density field changes the structure. The rejected alternative, accepting the
optimizer's final stress field, would let the quantity being minimized against grade itself.

The check runs on a body-fitted tetrahedral mesh of the extracted surface, a different discretization from the
optimizer's cut cells. The optimization sibling depends on the solid-mechanics sibling, so the independence is
of mesh and formulation, not of codebase; the cantilever and two-bar known answers anchor both paths.

This bead is the first of the check's four parts. **Stress**: every envelope case, plus the instants that
govern each gauge under the influence matrix of the final geometry. Utilization is u = σ_vm/(S_y/SF) per
element, with σ_vm the von Mises stress, S_y the yield strength from the material card and SF the declared
safety factor. The mesh is refined until the peak moves by under 5% (TARGET). A peak at a re-entrant corner
that does not converge is reported as `singular: fillet required`, with the radius as the repair. That last
rule exists because linear elasticity predicts unbounded stress at a sharp internal corner: refining the mesh
there produces ever larger numbers, none of which is a stress the part would see, and the honest output is the
geometric repair.

Every number is class Estimated, and a structural margin enters a claim product as its weakest input.

Refusal FROB-REF-261: an optimized part that fails the independent check; repair offered: the governing case
and location, and a tighter cap or length scale. Invariant INV-FOUNDRY-03: no geometry enters a packet without
passing this check on a mesh the optimizer did not use; the manifest cites the check's mesh hash.

## Scope

In: the case list; the linear solves; utilization per element with the interface no-claim mask; the refinement
loop and its stopping rule; detection and reporting of singular corners; the result record with mesh hash; the
refusal for optimized parts and findings for other geometry; the `same-family` variant.
Out: buckling and modes (N31a-20); fatigue (N31a-21); mesh generation and refinement mechanics (N31a-17);
traction patterns (N31a-08); card validity (N31a-22); manufacturability (N31a-26), which shares the verb
`foundry.check`.

## Technical approach

1. **Case list** (`check/stress.rs`): every envelope case of the class (all instances, all contact variants,
   each a real instant); the two governing instants of every gauge from the influence matrix built on *this*
   geometry; the peak instants of any transient windows (the cases tagged `transient` by the impact-window
   path of N31a-12, which also attaches `model-conditional` to cases whose peak moves by more than 20% across
   contact variants). Duplicates by (address, tick) are merged. Declared cases are accepted with their label.
2. **Solves.** One factorization per mesh; one right-hand side per case, assembled from port traction patterns
   and the body load with the 3-2-1 restraint on the proximal boss and the self-equilibrium check on reactions
   (at most 10⁻⁹ of the applied load). Whole job through the sibling wrapper.
3. **Utilization.** Per element e and case c: u_ec = σ_vm(e, c)/(S_y/SF). Elements inside the interface
   no-claim zone (within one boss diameter of an interface) are excluded and their share reported. The peak
   u* = max u_ec with its element, surface point, case, instant and world address. Margin = 1/u* − 1.
4. **Refinement.** Level 0 is the mesh from extraction. At each level, refine elements whose utilization is in
   the top 5% or within two element layers of the peak (DESIGN), re-audit, re-solve. Stop when
   |u*_k − u*_{k−1}|/u*_k < 0.05, or after 4 levels (DESIGN cap). The history (level, elements, u*, location) is
   recorded.
5. **Singular corners.** If the cap is reached without convergence, examine the peak location: if it lies on a
   concave surface edge whose local radius of curvature is below the local element size at every level, and
   u* grew monotonically, the result for that location is `singular: fillet required`. The repair is a radius:
   the smallest r in the stock of tool radii of the declared process (or the minimum printable radius) for
   which the estimated peak u_nom·(1 + 2·√(d/r)) ≤ 1, where u_nom is the utilization one member depth away and
   d the member depth at the corner (DESIGN heuristic, class Estimated, printed as a suggestion to re-run
   with). The singular location is excluded from u* and listed; the part does not pass until a run with the
   fillet converges. A non-converging peak that is not at a concave edge is an error in the model (usually a
   point load or a bad restraint) and fails the job with the location.
6. **Outcome.**
   - Geometry with provenance `optimized` (from the topology rung): u* > 1 in any case, or an unresolved
     singular location → FROB-REF-261. Payload: class, governing case with address and tick, location, u*,
     refinement history, and repairs ranked: (1) re-run the rung with stress cap multiplied by 0.95/u*;
     (2) increase the minimum length scale by one stock step; (3) the fillet radius where singular. Exit 3.
     Inside the structure loop the first repair is applied once automatically.
   - Other geometry (a hand-built part, a beam-rung section, the fixtures): the same numbers as findings;
     exit 2 when u* > 1, exit 0 otherwise.
7. **Result record** (`frob.foundry.check.stress/1`): mesh hash at each level, `optimizer_grid_hash` for
   reference, case count, u*, margin, governing case, labels inherited from the governing case
   (`model-conditional`, `provisional`, `declared`), no-claim share, the utilization field's hash, cost. With
   the meshing fallback in force the solves run on the second cut-cell discretization and every number carries
   `check: same-family`.
8. **Verb.** `foundry.check <geometry or design+class> [--cases <set>] [--stress|--modes|--all] --key <k>`
   (kind `run`, so every call carries its idempotency key), or with `--fixture <name>` (the cluster's
   named-fixture input, N31a-51) in place of the positional argument and `--load <N>` to set the magnitude
   in newtons of the fixture's single declared load case (DESIGN: the known-answer fixtures are linear, so
   the halving and planted-load controls need no second fixture). The stress part is always run first because
   buckling and fatigue reuse its factorization and influence matrix. The options `--fixture`, `--load`,
   `--cases`, `--stress`, `--modes` and `--all` are on VERBS-foundry's `foundry.check` row with this bead.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-01 through this path: the cantilever (L = 0.20 m, section 20 × 10 mm, E = 69 GPa, tip load 50 N) gives tip deflection within 2% of 1.159 mm and root bending stress within 5% of 30 MPa after refinement.
- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-01: the planted overstressed link (the same beam with a root section 4 mm deep, 80 N, yield 240 MPa, SF = 1) is located at the root with utilization 1.25 ± 5%; the unthinned beam passes (utilization 0.20 at 80 N).
- [ ] Refinement stops when the peak moves by under 5%; the history is in the record.
- [ ] An L-bracket fixture with a sharp internal corner returns `singular: fillet required` with a radius; the same bracket with that fillet converges.
- [ ] An optimized part with u* = 1.12 returns FROB-REF-261 with the three ranked repairs; the same numbers on a hand-built part are findings with exit 2.
- [ ] Elements within one boss diameter of an interface never govern.
- [ ] The record carries the mesh hash, and the hash differs from the optimizer's grid hash.
- [ ] A governing case labelled `model-conditional` gives a margin labelled `model-conditional`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry check::stress` (feature `structure`):
- `cantilever_deflection_and_stress`: 1.159 mm (shear adds about 0.2%: with G = E/(2·(1 + ν)) and shear factor 5/6 the shear deflection is 2.3 µm) and 30 MPa by linear extrapolation of the top-fibre stress to the root from sections at least one depth away; 28.5 MPa at 10 mm from the root.
- `planted_overstress_located`: 6 × 80 × 0.2/(0.02 × 0.004²) = 300 MPa; u = 1.25 at the root; unthinned 48 MPa, u = 0.20.
- `halving_load_halves_stress_and_deflection`.
- `refinement_stops_under_5_percent`; `refinement_cap_is_four_levels`.
- `reentrant_corner_is_singular`; `fillet_radius_suggestion_from_stock`; `fillet_rerun_converges`.
- `nonconvergence_away_from_corner_is_model_error`.
- `optimized_failure_refuses_261`; `handbuilt_failure_is_finding_exit_2`.
- `no_claim_zone_excluded_from_peak`.
- `labels_inherited_from_governing_case`.
- `mesh_hash_recorded_and_differs_from_grid_hash`.
- `same_family_label_when_fallback`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `unsigned-load-direction` (absolute value of port forces: the cantilever still passes but a combined bending and axial fixture fails its hand value), `include-interface-zone-in-peak`, `stop-refinement-after-level-0`, `utilization-against-ultimate-not-yield`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_stress_check.sh` (this bead's own, built only on its dependencies; it sources the STD-E2E
helper library). Steps: (1) `cargo test -p frob-foundry --features structure -- check::stress` through
`e2e_cmd` (including the four planted defects); (2) `cargo run -p frob-foundry --features structure --example
stress_check -- --fixture cantilever --load 50 --json`, an example program of this bead running the check on a
fixture's geometry and declared load case: tip deflection within 2% of 1.159 mm and root stress within 5% of
30 MPa (`e2e_assert_close`), the refinement history ending under 5%, a mesh hash different from the grid
hash; at `--load 25` both halve; (3) `--fixture cantilever-thinned --load 80`: utilization 1.25 ± 5% at the
root, exit 2 (a hand-built part); the unthinned beam at 80 N: 0.20, exit 0; (4) `l-bracket-sharp`: `singular:
fillet required` with a radius, and `l-bracket-filleted` converges; (5) `neck-optimized` (provenance
`optimized`, u* = 1.12): exit 3 and FROB-REF-261 with the registry's three repairs in order (stress cap ×
0.95/u*, minimum length scale up one stock step, fillet radius), read from the printed refusal. No `frob` verb
is called, so the evidence block is recorded as absent with reason `no-verb`. Logs to
`target/e2e-logs/foundry_stress_check/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: per line `ts`, `script`, `bead` =
N31a-19, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code and wall time; metrics `tip_deflection_mm` (1.159, 2%),
`root_stress_mpa` (30, 5%), `utilization_planted` (1.25, 5%), refinement levels, last peak change, mesh hashes
and no-claim share; on failure the first differing value and the path of the utilization field; a final
`summary`. The verb-level steps are in `scripts/e2e/foundry_check.sh` (N31a-55), which runs this script as a
stage: `frob foundry check --fixture cantilever --key "$(e2e_key cantilever)" --json`, `--fixture
cantilever-thinned --load 80 --key "$(e2e_key cantilever-thinned-80)"`, and `frob foundry check --fixture
neck-optimized --key "$(e2e_key neck-optimized)" --json` asserting exit 3 and FROB-REF-261.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-737 (cantilever: 1.159 mm; 30 MPa; shear adds about 0.2%; control: halving the load halves both, and a
millimetre-for-metre unit error is caught by dimension typing). KA-738 (planted overstressed link: 300 MPa,
utilization 1.25 at the root; control: the unthinned beam passes). Negative controls: the four planted
defects. Metamorphic: scaling all loads by k scales u by k; a rigid motion of part and loads changes no u;
refining a converged mesh once more changes u* by less than 5%. Determinism: twice on one machine; equal bytes
in both airlock modes.

## Considerations

- This gate is also carried by the beam-rung bead (BD-401) for the closed forms and the finite-element form of
  the structural-margin analysis. Here the same fixtures run through the check as applied to arbitrary
  geometry; a disagreement between the two paths is itself a finding.
- Yield-based utilization with von Mises is right for ductile metals. For brittle or strongly anisotropic
  printed material the card must name another criterion; without one the stress check on that material is
  refused through the card rule (FROB-REF-163).
- Refinement near the peak can chase a different peak; the history records location at each level so that a
  wandering peak is visible.
- Cost: one factorization per refinement level and tens of right-hand sides; seconds to minutes per class.
  It runs as a worker job.
