## Background

Link fatigue is a safeguard, not an observed failure mode: published fatigue analyses of legged robots concern
gears, and a bounded search found no report of a link failing by fatigue. The check exists because an
optimizer that removes material can create the problem.

The mathematics is construction C-35, part 2. Stress histories at gauges are reduced to cycles by rainflow
counting, and damage accumulates by the linear (Palmgren–Miner) rule D = Σ nᵢ/N(Sᵢ), with N(S) from the
material's stress-life curve (Basquin: N = (S/S₀)⁻ᵐ); failure is predicted at D = 1. The rule is linear, so it
ignores load order; the residue of open half-cycles is carried between windows. Rare overloads dominate: with
m = 3, 10⁶ cycles at an amplitude of life 2 × 10⁶ give D = 0.5, and 10³ further cycles at five times the
amplitude (life 16,000) add 0.0625, so 0.1% of the cycles carry 11% of the damage.

**The check.** Gauges sit at the 16 highest-stress locations per unit load (TARGET) and at declared notches.
Signed stress on each gauge plane feeds the sibling's streaming counter (`RainflowMiner`) across the campaign.
With damage D accumulated over H_c campaign hours, utilization for a service duration H is
U_f = (D/H_c)·H against a limit of 1 divided by the card's scatter factor. Five rules:

- Signed stress only. Unsigned von Mises is never fed to a cycle counter.
- Residues are carried between episodes and `finish` is called once.
- The mean-stress policy is named in the result.
- The curve must be valid for the material state, the process and the surface finish. Otherwise the result is
  `fatigue: no-claim` (FROB-REF-163).
- Cycles that span tasks (a loaded leg, then an unloaded one) are counted, because the campaign's duty
  sequence is part of the history.

ADR-FOUNDRY-06: fatigue is reported as utilization under a named empirical law with card provenance, class
Estimated; the word "life" is not used for a result. The sibling states the same limit (its module calls
itself an empirical baseline, "not a validated component-life claim"), and curves for printed and cast
material are model claims. The rejected alternative, a predicted life in hours, invites reading an empirical
extrapolation as a warranty.

Gate (TARGET): GATE-FOUNDRY-03, Known answer, required at M5 and recordable at M4: fatigue block and rainflow
sequence exact to 10⁻¹² relative; the window-split mutation reproduces its 10.7% and is caught.

## Scope

In: gauge placement and gauge planes; the signed-stress type; the streaming count with persisted residues;
damage, utilization and the limit; per-instance and per-variant combination; the result record; the two known
answers and their mutations.
Out: gauge histories themselves (N31a-09); the card's validity decision (N31a-22); crack growth and
strain-life (available in the sibling, not used by this chapter); long-horizon damage accumulation under
changing wear state (the lifecycle crate, N31a-48).

## Technical approach

1. **Gauge placement** (`check/gauges.rs`). For each unit load j of the class, take the 16 elements with the
   largest von Mises stress per unit load on the checked geometry, outside the interface no-claim zones,
   preferring surface elements (cracks start at surfaces; DESIGN). For each selected element and each unit
   load that selected it, the gauge plane normal is the direction of the principal stress of largest
   magnitude of that unit stress. Duplicates (same element, normals within 5°) merge. A two-port link has at
   most 16 × 18 = 288 gauges before merging. Declared notches add gauges at their declared location and
   normal with their notch factor K_f multiplying the gauge stress.
2. **Signed gauge stress.** s_g = K_f·n_gᵀ·σ·n_g, the normal stress on the gauge plane; positive is tension.
   It has the type `SignedStress`, which only the gauge projection constructs. The counter wrapper takes
   `SignedStress` and nothing else; `EquivalentStress` (von Mises) has no conversion to it. A second, run-time
   guard refuses a history whose provenance is not a gauge plane.
3. **Counting** (`check/fatigue.rs`). One sibling counter per (gauge, instance, variant), created once, fed the
   gauge's turning points in duty order across all episodes of the campaign, and finished exactly once at the
   end of the whole observation. Between job chunks and across a resumed job, the counter's residue (the stack
   of unclosed reversals) is serialized into the stage record and restored: a resume is not a new window.
   Cycles are counted by the four-point rainflow rule; at `finish` the remaining reversals count as half
   cycles.
4. **Damage.** For a cycle of stress range Δ and mean s̄: amplitude S_a = Δ/2; the card's named mean-stress
   correction converts (S_a, s̄) to an equivalent amplitude at the curve's reference stress ratio; N comes from
   the curve; damage adds count/N. The cut-off policy below the endurance limit (none, a knee with a second
   slope, or a hard cut-off) is part of the named law.
5. **Utilization.** U_f = (D/H_c)·H, with H_c the campaign hours and H the declared service duration of the
   design (a required input; there is no default). Limit: 1/scatter factor from the card. The gauge passes
   when U_f ≤ 1/scatter. The class result is the largest U_f over gauges, over instances of the class (never
   concatenated across instances) and over contact variants, with the governing gauge, instance and variant
   named. The sibling's critical-plane utilization on closed windows is computed as a second number and
   reported beside it.
6. **No-claim.** If the card's curve is missing or not valid for the part's material state, process and
   surface finish, or the load set is declared rather than recorded, the result is `fatigue: no-claim` with
   the reason; nothing numeric is printed. If the caller required a fatigue result (a packet that would record
   a fatigue pass) the outcome is refusal FROB-REF-163 naming the missing field.
7. **Stabilization warning.** The damage rate D/H_c is also computed on each half of the campaign; if the two
   halves differ by more than 5% (the pilot criterion of the construction), the result carries
   `damage rate not stabilized` with both values: a short campaign does not establish a rate.
8. **Result record** (`frob.foundry.check.fatigue/1`): the law (curve identity, exponent, reference ratio,
   mean-stress policy, cut-off, scatter), card provenance, H_c, H, per governing gauge: location, plane, K_f,
   cycle histogram (range and mean bins), D, U_f; counts of skipped samples; labels (`model-conditional`,
   `provisional`); class Estimated. Field names use `utilization` and `service_duration`, never `life`.
9. **Failure.** U_f above the limit on an optimized part is FROB-REF-261 with the governing gauge and the
   repairs of the stress check; on other geometry a finding.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-03, fatigue block: with N(S) = 10⁶·(100 MPa/S)³, fully reversed, 2,000 cycles at 200 MPa, 50,000 at 100 MPa and 400,000 at 50 MPa give damage 0.016 + 0.050 + 0.050 = 0.116 per block, exact to 10⁻¹² relative, and 8.62 blocks to a sum of 1.
- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-03, rainflow sequence: −2, 1, −3, 5, −1, 3, −4, 4, −2 in units of 40 MPa with no mean-stress correction gives ranges 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 with counts 0.5, 1.5, 0.5, 1.0, 0.5 and damage 8.752 × 10⁻⁶, exact to 10⁻¹² relative.
- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-03, mutation: splitting after the fifth point and finishing each window gives 7.816 × 10⁻⁶, 10.7% low, and the test that compares windowed against whole-history counts catches it.
- [ ] Feeding the sequence in two chunks with the residue carried and one `finish` gives exactly the whole-history result (metamorphic relation MR-34).
- [ ] Unsigned stress cannot be passed to the counter (compile-fail), and a history tagged as von Mises is refused at run time.
- [ ] A card whose curve is not valid for the part's process returns `fatigue: no-claim`; when a fatigue pass is required the outcome is FROB-REF-163.
- [ ] For a mirrored class the result is the maximum over instances; the concatenating mutation is caught.
- [ ] No output field or message uses the word "life" for a result.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry check::fatigue check::gauges` (feature `structure`):
- `fatigue_block_0_116`: N(200) = 125,000; N(100) = 10⁶; N(50) = 8 × 10⁶; D = 0.116; 1/0.116 = 8.62.
- `rainflow_sequence_counts_and_damage`: amplitudes are half the range times 40 MPa: 60, 80, 120, 160, 180 MPa; Σ count·(S_a/100)³ = 0.108 + 0.768 + 0.864 + 4.096 + 2.916 = 8.752, so D = 8.752 × 10⁻⁶.
- `window_split_mutation_is_10_7_percent_low`: windows (points 1 to 5) and (points 5 to 9), each finished: ranges 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 with counts 0.5, 1.0, 1.0, 0.5, 1.0; D = 7.816 × 10⁻⁶; (8.752 − 7.816)/8.752 = 10.7%.
- `chunked_with_carried_residue_equals_whole` at every split point of the sequence (eight splits).
- `residue_survives_serialization` (resume in the middle of the sequence).
- `rare_overload_share`: 0.5 + 0.0625; the overload share is 11.1%.
- `history_0_10_0_in_two_windows_is_one_cycle`.
- `unsigned_stress_changes_the_count`: the fatigue block fed as |σ| gives 0.029 per block instead of 0.116; the typed interface makes this unreachable, and the test exercises it through a test-only bypass to prove the detector fires.
- `gauge_count_and_merge`: 16 per unit load, at most 288 for 18 coordinates; duplicates merged.
- `gauge_plane_is_principal_direction`; `notch_factor_multiplies`.
- `utilization_formula`: D = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ over H_c = 5.556 h and H = 2,000 h give U_f = 4.32 × 10⁻³; with scatter 4 the limit is 0.25.
- `no_claim_on_invalid_curve`; `required_fatigue_refuses_163`.
- `max_over_instances`; `max_over_variants_with_label`.
- `unstabilized_rate_flagged`.
- `no_life_noun_in_schema`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `finish-per-episode` (the registered window-split mutation), `feed-von-mises`, `concatenate-instances`, `amplitude-equals-range` (forgets the factor ½ and over-counts damage eightfold at m = 3).

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-741 (fatigue block: 0.116 per block; 8.62 blocks; control: unsigned stress changes the count and is
refused). KA-742 and CX-338 (rainflow sequence; the split loses the range-9 half cycle: 7.816 × 10⁻⁶, 10.7%
low). CX-339 (unsigned von Mises fed to a counter). KA-169 (rare overloads: 0.5 and 0.0625). KA-170 (history
0, 10, 0 in two windows: one full cycle; a counter that drops residues counts none). Metamorphic MR-34
(windows with carried residues equal the whole history). Determinism: counts and damage are exact rational
functions of the inputs in these fixtures and identical everywhere.

## Considerations

- Every number is Estimated however exact the counting: the curve is empirical, Miner's rule ignores load
  order, and the campaign is a sample of duty. The exactness gate is about the arithmetic, not the physics.
- OPEN-FOUNDRY-4: stress-life data for printed material by process and orientation is not chosen; the default
  is `fatigue: no-claim` for printed parts, which blocks the fatigue leg of GATE-FOUNDRY-05 for printed
  fixtures only.
- A tier bias of 10% in loads changes damage by 1.1⁵ = 1.61 at m = 5 and 1.1¹⁰ = 2.59 at m = 10; this is why
  spectra are rescaled to the referee's range by a rule of unit U31b before damage is summed for a packet.
- Under KILL-LOADS there are no spectra and this check reads `no-claim`.
