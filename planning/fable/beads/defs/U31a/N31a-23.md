## Background

A design in this system does not state inertia; it states shapes, fills and materials, and mass properties are
computed from them. Before the structure loop, a link is a primitive Region (a capsule, a box, a tube) with a
fill. After sizing or optimization the link has a real geometry, and its mass, centre of mass and inertia are
different. If the model keeps the old numbers, every later simulation and every later load is for a robot that
is not the one being built.

So: **the checked geometry and its material card go through the mass-properties sibling, and the resulting
mass, centre of mass and inertia replace the link's primitive Region by a typed edit. That is a new
`DesignId`. The baseline controller is re-synthesized, the campaign re-runs on the same world addresses, and
loads are re-extracted.**

A *typed edit* is the only way a design changes: each edit has preconditions, an inverse where one exists, a
declared effect on the symmetry groups, and a scope of invalidation. Using one here means the lineage graph
records that this design descends from that one by a structure run, the invalidation machinery recomputes
exactly what depends on the link's inertia, and the change can be undone.

Invariant INV-FOUNDRY-04: mass properties in the packet equal those of the model the evidence was computed
with; tested by recomputing them from the exported geometry. This bead is where that equality starts: the
numbers fed back are computed from the same geometry object that will later be exported.

Only geometry that passed the independent check is fed back (the "checked geometry"); feeding back a part that
failed would spend a campaign on a design that cannot be kept.

## Scope

In: mass properties of a checked candidate (optimized solid, plate, or beam section); their uncertainty; the
typed edit and its payload; class-wide application through the symmetry constructor; the mass-support
condition; the attachment of the made geometry to the body; the vector x_k handed to the convergence test.
Out: the convergence test (N31a-24); controller re-synthesis and the campaign re-run (N31a-05); the packet's
`mass.json` and the cross-export identity at 10⁻⁶ (N31a-40); the typed-edit machinery itself (BD-058).

## Technical approach

1. **Mass properties** (`feedback/massprops.rs`). Input: the candidate's solid and the material card's density
   with its relative uncertainty.
   - Optimized or plate geometry: sum the volume moments of the tetrahedra of the check's level-0 body-fitted
     mesh through the mass-properties sibling (it provides exact moments of a tetrahedron under a rigid
     transform). This is exact for the polyhedron the mesh represents.
   - Beam section: the sibling's hollow-cylinder or cuboid primitive with the section's dimensions.
   Output in the link frame: mass m, centre of mass c, inertia about c as a symmetric tensor, its principal
   moments (I₁ ≥ I₂ ≥ I₃) and axes; the volume with the geometry sibling's outward-rounded enclosure; relative
   uncertainty of m and of the inertia entries equal to the density's (geometry is taken as exact; material
   uncertainty is retained, not turned into a bound on inertia); sources (geometry hash, mesh hash, card hash).
2. **The edit** (`feedback/edit.rs`). One typed edit per link class, applied under the class's symmetry
   constructor so that the symmetry groups are preserved:
   `set-fill <body> declared { mass, com, inertia, provenance: { source: "foundry", rung: "beam" | "topology",
   geometry: <hash>, check_mesh: <hash>, card: <hash>, loop_iteration: k } }`.
   The body's Region is unchanged and continues to serve collision and the fast tiers; its role as the mass
   support is what the next step tests. The edit's invalidation scope is the body's inertia and whole-robot
   sums; its inverse restores the previous fill. The made geometry is attached to the body as
   `made_geometry: <hash>` for later checks and export; the attachment is part of the design term, so the
   design identity changes with the geometry even when mass properties happen to coincide.
3. **Mass support.** Declared mass properties must be those of some body inside the Region: for a bounding
   sphere of centre o and radius R, ‖c − o‖² + tr(I_c)/(2·m) ≤ R². The design language runs this test on
   every declared fill and refuses on failure. If the keep-in envelope was larger than the primitive Region
   and the optimized part uses the extra room, the edit set also contains a `reshape` of the collision Region
   to the fitted primitive of the new geometry (primitive fitting with its recorded error), and the result
   says that the self-collision and clearance analysis must be re-run; the structure loop does not proceed on
   a design whose reshaped Region fails it.
4. **Result of applying the edits.** A new `DesignId` d_{k+1} and, after compilation, a new `ModelId`. The
   lineage record carries the labels of the run (`exploratory`, `model-conditional`, `check: same-family`,
   `loads: declared`).
5. **The iterate vector.** x_k collects, per link class in a fixed order, (m, I₁, I₂, I₃). It is written into
   the loop record with units, and is the input of the convergence test.
6. **Actuators untouched.** The edit set never contains an actuator, gear or battery change; a proposal that
   does is rejected here (ADR-FOUNDRY-07: actuator cards are held fixed inside the structure loop).
7. **Class and determinism.** Estimated. The moments are sums in a fixed element order with compensated
   summation; identical bits twice on one machine.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tube known answer (ρ = 2,700 kg/m³, radii 15 and 13 mm, length 0.20 m): mass 0.0950 kg, axial inertia 1.87 × 10⁻⁵ kg·m², transverse inertia 3.26 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m², from the primitive path exactly to the printed digits and from a 256-segment tetrahedral mesh within 0.1%.
- [ ] The volume enclosure reported for the tube mesh contains the mesh's exact polyhedral volume, and that volume is within 0.1% of 3.5186 × 10⁻⁵ m³.
- [ ] Applying the edit yields a new `DesignId`; applying its inverse restores the original identity.
- [ ] The edit applied to a mirrored class keeps both symmetry groups; applied to one limb only (a planted misuse) it is reported as breaking the symmetry.
- [ ] Declared properties that violate the mass-support bound are refused by the design language, and the edit set then includes the `reshape` with its fit error.
- [ ] An edit proposal that includes `swap-actuator` is rejected by this stage.
- [ ] Geometry that has not passed the independent check cannot be fed back (the function takes a `Checked<Geometry>`).
- [ ] The relative uncertainty of mass equals the card's density uncertainty.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry feedback::massprops feedback::edit`:
- `tube_mass_properties`: V = π·(0.015² − 0.013²)·0.20 = 3.5186 × 10⁻⁵ m³; m = 0.09500 kg; axial ½·m·(r_o² + r_i²) = 1.8716 × 10⁻⁵ kg·m²; transverse about the centroid (m/12)·(3·(r_o² + r_i²) + L²) = 3.2602 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m².
- `tet_mesh_tube_within_0_1_percent`; `enclosure_contains_polyhedral_volume`.
- `solid_box_inertia`: 0.1 × 0.04 × 0.02 m at 2,700 kg/m³: m = 0.216 kg; I about the long axis (m/12)·(0.04² + 0.02²) = 3.6 × 10⁻⁵ kg·m².
- `rigid_transform_of_inertia` (parallel-axis check against a shifted box).
- `principal_moments_sorted_and_axes_orthonormal`.
- `edit_creates_new_design_id`; `inverse_restores_identity`.
- `class_edit_preserves_symmetry`; `single_limb_edit_reports_break`.
- `mass_support_violation_adds_reshape`.
- `unchecked_geometry_is_a_type_error` (compile-fail).
- `actuator_change_rejected`.
- `uncertainty_propagates_from_density`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `inertia-about-origin-not-com` (the tube's transverse value comes out wrong when the link frame is at an end), `density-from-default` (ignores the card), `feed-back-unchecked-geometry`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_structure_loop.sh` (N31a-54) logs per iteration and class the fed-back (m, c, I₁, I₂, I₃)
with uncertainty, the old and new design identities, and asserts that `frob design show <new>` reports the
`declared` fill with provenance `foundry` and the attached geometry hash.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-744 (mass properties of a tube: 0.0950 kg; 1.87 × 10⁻⁵ and 3.26 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m²; control: the volume
enclosure must contain the exact volume). Negative controls: the three planted defects. Metamorphic: doubling
density doubles mass and every inertia entry and leaves c unchanged; a rigid motion of the geometry moves c
and rotates the inertia tensor accordingly, with principal moments unchanged; scaling lengths by s scales mass
by s³ and inertia by s⁵. Determinism: identical bits twice on one machine; the primitive path is exact
rational where inputs are.

## Considerations

- Keeping the primitive Region for collision means fast tiers collide with the old shape. That is acceptable
  while the optimized part stays inside it; the mass-support test is what detects when it does not.
- Mass properties of a polyhedral mesh of a smooth part are slightly low; the 0.1% figure for the tube is the
  faceting error at 256 segments and shrinks with refinement.
- A new design has no qualification verdict. The loop's output is therefore not packet-ready until
  re-qualified (bead N31a-03).
- KILL-TOPOPT fallback: the beam rung supplies mass and inertia feedback through the same edit with
  `rung: "beam"`.
