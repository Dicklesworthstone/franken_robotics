## Background

The structure loop feeds each iteration's link masses and inertias back into the model and re-extracts loads.
**Why it can fail to converge.** Structural mass of a link sized to a stress cap grows with the loads, and the
loads grow with total mass. In the linear model, link mass S and other mass M₀ satisfy

  S = A + λ·(M₀ + S),

with fixed point S* = (A + λ·M₀)/(1 − λ) and error contracting by λ per iteration. The actuator mass spiral
has the same form with gain κ (total mass m = m_f/(1 − κ)). If both run in one loop the gain is about κ + λ: at
κ = 0.59 and λ = 0.15 it is 0.74, and five iterations contract the first step only to 0.74⁴ = 0.30 of itself
(FACT, arithmetic). That is the reason actuator cards are held fixed inside the loop (ADR-FOUNDRY-07). For the
structure loop alone, with A = 0.5 kg, λ = 0.15 and M₀ = 10 kg, S* = 2.35 kg and four contractions leave
0.15⁴ = 5 × 10⁻⁴ of the first step.

A loop that is merely stopped after a fixed number of passes reports whatever it had. This bead is the test
that decides, from the iterates themselves, whether the loop converged, diverged, cycles or ran out of budget,
and that bounds the remaining error.

The map G from one iterate to the next is deterministic only because the campaign reuses its world addresses.
With fresh random worlds each iteration, sampling noise sets a floor above 2% and the test cannot pass.

Gate (TARGET): GATE-FOUNDRY-06, Known answer, M5: planted-gain loop: fixed point within 10⁻⁶, λ̂ within 0.01;
divergence and cycling refused. Proof obligation PO-FOUNDRY-03: the a-posteriori bound holds for a
contraction (derivation on file; the planted-gain fixture). Refusal FROB-REF-260: a loads–mass loop that
diverges, cycles or exceeds its iteration budget; repair offered: λ̂, the last iterates, and the beam rung.

## Scope

In: the norm; the contraction estimate; the four convergence conditions; the iteration budget; the Aitken
proposal; the divergence and cycling detectors; the under-relaxed step; the refusal payload; the derivation
of the bound; the planted fixtures.
Out: producing iterates (N31a-05, N31a-23); the independent check that is the fourth condition (N31a-19 to
N31a-21); the outer actuator step (N31a-25).

## Technical approach

1. **Iterates and norm** (`feedback/converge.rs`). x_k collects link masses and principal inertias after
   iteration k (four numbers per link class); Δ_k = x_k − x_{k−1}. Because entries have different units, the
   norm at iteration k is the largest componentwise relative magnitude with the weights of x_k:
   ‖v‖_k = max_i |v_i|/|x_{k,i}| (DESIGN). In this norm "relative change" is ‖Δ_k‖_k itself and ‖x_k‖_k = 1.
2. **Estimated contraction.** λ̂_k = ‖Δ_k‖_k/‖Δ_{k−1}‖_k, reported every iteration from k = 2: numerator and
   denominator use the same weights x_k, so that λ̂ is exactly the gain of a scalar linear map (with the
   weights of x_{k−1} in the denominator the planted loop would give λ̂_2 = (0.15/2.85)/(1.0/3.0) = 0.158, not
   0.15). Zero rule: G is deterministic, so ‖Δ_{k−1}‖ = 0 means x_{k−1} is a fixed point and ‖Δ_k‖ = 0; λ̂_k
   is then reported as 0 and the bound as 0. This is the common case on the beam rung, whose stock steps
   make the map piecewise constant. ‖Δ_{k−1}‖ = 0 with ‖Δ_k‖ ≠ 0 means the map is not deterministic (fresh
   worlds, a changed card): the loop stops with the error `map-not-deterministic` naming the component.
3. **Converged** when all four hold (all TARGET):
   1. relative change at most 2% for two successive iterations: ‖Δ_k‖ ≤ 0.02 and ‖Δ_{k−1}‖ ≤ 0.02;
   2. the a-posteriori bound λ̂_k/(1 − λ̂_k)·‖Δ_k‖ is at most 2% of ‖x_k‖ (which requires λ̂_k < 1);
   3. the load envelope moved by at most 5%: over all envelope directions, |p_k − p_{k−1}|/|p_{k−1}| ≤ 0.05
      for the projections p of the governing cases;
   4. the final geometry passes the independent check under the loads it itself generates.
4. **Budget.** Five iterations. Reaching the budget without convergence → FROB-REF-260 with reason
   `budget-exhausted`.
5. **Aitken extrapolation.** From three successive plain iterates, x̃ = x_k − Δ_k²/(Δ_k − Δ_{k−1})
   componentwise may propose the next iterate, which is then evaluated like any other: it becomes the mass
   properties of the next design, the campaign runs, and G(x̃) is computed. A component is not extrapolated
   when |Δ_k − Δ_{k−1}| < 10⁻¹²·|x_k| or when the proposal is non-positive or outside the stock range; it
   then takes x_k. For a linear map the proposal is the fixed point.
   - *When the driver proposes* (DESIGN; the plan says only that Aitken "may propose"). After iteration
     k ≥ 2, the next evaluated design is the proposal only when plain iteration is predicted to miss the 2%
     change within the budget: λ̂_k defined with 0 < λ̂_k < 0.9, no sign alternation on the governing
     component, and λ̂_k^(B − k)·‖Δ_k‖_k > 0.02 with B = 5. At most one proposal per run. Otherwise iteration
     is plain. The policy is a library setting (`ProposalPolicy::Auto`, the default, or `Off`), not a verb
     option.
   - *Counting.* An iteration is one campaign. With a proposal, iteration k+1 runs the campaign on the design
     with mass properties x̃ and yields x_{k+1} = G(x̃), and its change is the residual at the evaluated
     point, Δ_{k+1} = x_{k+1} − x̃ (for a plain step the evaluated point is x_k, so this is the ordinary Δ).
     λ̂ is not formed across a proposal (`n/a` at k+1, which also breaks the "two successive" of the
     divergence and cycling tests); it resumes at k+2.
   - The record marks every proposal and carries `aitken_used`.
   - *The gate fixture evaluates no proposal.* On the planted-gain loop λ̂_2 = 0.15 and ‖Δ_2‖ = 0.15/2.85 =
     5.26%, so 0.15³ × 5.26% = 0.018% < 2%: iteration is plain, the iterates of GATE-FOUNDRY-06 are those of
     the map, and `aitken_used` is false. On a slow loop (A = 0.6 kg, M₀ = 12 kg, λ = 0.6, start 4.0 kg) the
     plain iterates are 10.2, 13.92, 16.152, 17.4912, 18.29472 with a change of 4.39% at iteration 5
     (budget exhausted); with the policy, λ̂_2 = 0.6 and 0.6³ × (3.72/13.92) = 5.77% > 2%, so iteration 3
     evaluates x̃ = 13.92 − 3.72²/(3.72 − 6.2) = 19.5 kg, the fixed point (A + λ·M₀)/(1 − λ) = 7.8/0.4;
     G(19.5) = 19.5 gives Δ_3 = 0 and Δ_4 = 0, and the loop converges at iteration 4.
6. **Diverged** when λ̂ ≥ 1 on two successive iterations → FROB-REF-260, reason `diverged`, with the beam rung
   as the repair.
7. **Cycling** when Δ alternates in sign on the governing component with λ̂ within 0.1 of 1 on two successive
   iterations, which a discrete stock size produces: one under-relaxed step x ← x + ½·(G(x) − x) is tried; if
   the next Δ still alternates → FROB-REF-260, reason `cycling`, naming the two states.
   **Precedence (DESIGN).** A two-size cycle has λ̂ = 1 exactly, which also meets the divergence condition, so
   the cycling test is applied first on every iteration: alternation in sign with λ̂ within 0.1 of 1 on two
   successive iterations is `cycling` (and gets its under-relaxed step); `diverged` is reported only when
   λ̂ ≥ 1 on two successive iterations without that alternation-within-band pattern (monotone growth, or
   alternation with λ̂ > 1.1, an oscillation that grows).
8. **Refusal payload.** Reason; the λ̂ history; the last three iterates with units; the governing component
   (link class and which of mass or inertia); for `cycling` the two stock sizes; repairs ranked: (1) accept
   the beam rung's section for the governing class and re-run; (2) for cycling, fix the heavier of the two
   sizes by a lock and re-run; (3) reduce the load by a design change named by the governing envelope case.
   Exit code 3; `retry: after-change`.
9. **Derivation (PO-FOUNDRY-03), filed as `docs/derivations/foundry/aposteriori_bound.md`.** If G is a
   contraction with constant λ < 1 in the norm, then for the fixed point x*:
   ‖x_k − x*‖ ≤ Σ_{j ≥ 1} ‖x_{k+j} − x_{k+j−1}‖ ≤ Σ_{j ≥ 1} λʲ·‖Δ_k‖ = λ/(1 − λ)·‖Δ_k‖.
   The test uses λ̂ in place of λ, so the bound is an estimate that holds when λ̂ is not below the true
   constant; for a linear scalar map it holds with equality.
10. **Outputs.** Per iteration: x_k, Δ_k, λ̂_k, the bound, the envelope movement, the decision; at the end the
    outcome and, when converged, the fixed-point estimate (the Aitken value of the last three iterates) with
    the bound as its error estimate. All class Estimated except on the exact planted fixture.
11. **Arithmetic.** The test itself is pure and runs in exact rationals when the iterates are rational (the
    planted fixture), so the gate's 10⁻⁶ is tested without floating-point doubt (class D0 there).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-06: on the planted-gain loop (M₀ = 12 kg, A = 0.6 kg, λ = 0.15, start 4.0 kg) the run evaluates no Aitken proposal and its iterates are 3.0, 2.85, 2.8275, 2.824125 (the map's fifth iterate, 2.82361875, is never computed by the run because convergence comes first; KA-743 lists it as a value of the map, and the unit test computes it from the map directly); the reported fixed point (the Aitken value of the last three iterates 2.85, 2.8275, 2.824125) is within 10⁻⁶ of 48/17 = 2.8235294 kg; λ̂ is within 0.01 of 0.15 at every iteration (exactly 0.15 with the same weights in numerator and denominator).
- [ ] The same loop is declared converged at iteration 4 (changes 0.80% and 0.12%; bound 0.021%), given envelope movement under 5% and a passing check.
- [ ] On the slow loop of step 5 (λ = 0.6) plain iteration (`ProposalPolicy::Off`) ends `budget-exhausted` with a change of 4.39% at iteration 5, and the default policy evaluates one proposal (19.5 kg) at iteration 3 and converges at iteration 4.
- [ ] A loop whose change becomes exactly zero reports λ̂ = 0 and bound 0 (the zero rule), and a zero change followed by a non-zero one stops with `map-not-deterministic`.
- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-06: λ = 1.1 is refused as divergent within three iterations (iterates 18.2, 33.82, 51.002; λ̂ = 1.1 twice).
- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-06: a planted two-size cycle (masses alternating 2.6 and 3.0 kg) is detected, one under-relaxed step is tried, and the loop is refused with reason `cycling` naming both sizes (never `diverged`, though λ̂ = 1; an alternating oscillation with λ̂ = 1.5 is `diverged`).
- [ ] PO-FOUNDRY-03: on the planted-gain loop the true error ‖x_k − x*‖ is at most the bound at every k ≥ 2 (with equality to rounding); on a random set of 10³ linear contractions in up to eight dimensions the bound with the true λ holds every time.
- [ ] A loop that satisfies conditions 1 to 3 but fails the independent check is not converged.
- [ ] The Aitken value of the planted loop's first three iterates (4.0, 3.0, 2.85) equals 48/17 exactly in rational arithmetic; a proposal the driver evaluates (the slow loop) is marked as a proposal in the record and is never reported as a result without its evaluation.
- [ ] With fresh random worlds per iteration (planted configuration) on a noisy `synthetic` map with 3% noise, the test does not report convergence.

- `envelope_movement_condition`.
- `vector_case_governing_component`: three classes with gains 0.1, 0.15, 0.3: λ̂ tends to 0.3 and the governing component is named.

## Considerations

- The linear model is a picture of behaviour near the fixed point. Real maps are piecewise constant in places
  (stock sizes) and nonlinear in others; the detectors are written against the iterates, not the model.
- Aitken can propose a poor iterate when the map is not close to linear; because every proposal is evaluated,
  the cost of a bad proposal is one iteration, never a wrong answer.
- The 2%, 5% and five-iteration numbers are TARGETs; the stage-2 pilot's falsifier includes "mass still
  changing by more than 2% between iterations four and five".
- KILL-TOPOPT lists "no convergence" as one of its three triggers; this test produces that fact.
