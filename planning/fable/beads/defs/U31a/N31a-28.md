## Background

Two more manufacturability features are measured by Foundry because no sibling measures them. The fabrication
sibling holds limits for overhang angle and tool radius but "does not measure geometry"; geometric evaluators
for overhang and tool access there are upstream work item FD-FSIM-29. Interim posture: `frob-foundry` owns the
evaluators and reports `review: no-claim`. Switch-over rule: a differential test against the upstream
evaluators, then deletion of the local ones.

**Tool access** (milling): visibility of each surface point along the declared setup directions for the stated
tool radius and reach; the limit is the setup list on the process card. A milled surface must be reachable by a
cutter of finite length and non-zero radius arriving from one of the directions in which the part will be
fixtured. A pocket under a lip, a hole deeper than the cutter, a face that points away from every setup:
none can be cut.

**Overhang angle** (additive): the angle between the surface normal and the build direction; the limit is the
sibling's overhang angle. A layer-by-layer process cannot deposit material over nothing; a surface that leans
out further than the limit needs support, and a flat ceiling is the worst case.

Both are post-extraction checks. Inside the optimization, additive parts are steered by an overhang filter
(another bead); tool access has no usable derivative and is only checked.

## Scope

In: the two evaluators; their violation regions, measured values and repairs; setup-direction and
build-direction suggestions; the differential-test harness for the switch-over.
Out: corner radius and voids (N31a-27); the overhang filter inside the optimization (N31a-29); the limit
comparison and the report (N31a-26); the fixture battery (N31a-30).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** The part's surface triangles with outward normals and its signed distance field φ (negative
   inside) at spacing h (as for the corner evaluator); from the process card: `setups` (unit vectors d
   pointing from the part toward where the tool comes from), `tool_radii_m`, `tool_reach_m`,
   `cone_half_angle_deg`; `build_directions`, `overhang_max_deg`, `max_bridge_m`.
2. **Tool access** (`fab/access.rs`). A surface sample p with normal n is *accessible in setup d with cutter
   radius r* when all three hold:
   1. facing: n·d ≥ 0 (a face that leans away from the tool is an undercut; a wall parallel to the tool axis
      is cut by the cutter's flank and is allowed);
   2. clearance: the cutter's swept cylinder does not gouge the part: for s from 0 to the reach,
      φ(p + r·n_⊥ + s·d) ≥ r − 2·h, where n_⊥ is n with its component along d removed and renormalized (for a
      face normal to d the cutter axis passes through p). The march uses steps of h;
   3. reach: the distance from p along d to the top of the part's stock bounding box is at most
      `tool_reach_m`.
   For `cnc-5axis`, d ranges over a fixed set of 32 directions (a deterministic spherical spiral) inside the
   cone of each setup, and the sample is accessible if any direction works. A sample is *accessible* if some
   setup and the largest cutter that fits (trying radii from largest to smallest) reach it; the smallest
   radius needed is recorded, since a region that only the smallest cutter reaches is slow to machine.
3. **Access violations.** Connected regions of inaccessible samples with: area, centroid, which of the three
   conditions failed for the best setup (`undercut`, `gouge`, `reach` with the shortfall in millimetres), and a
   repair: the best additional setup direction from the candidate set {±x, ±y, ±z and the eight body
   diagonals} by newly accessible area, or for `reach` the tool length that would suffice (target value and
   difference in the limit check's format).
4. **Overhang** (`fab/overhang.rs`). For build direction b and a downward-facing facet (n·b < 0), the overhang
   angle is β = asin(−n·b): 0° for a vertical wall, 90° for a horizontal ceiling. A facet violates when
   β > `overhang_max_deg` and it is not on the build plate (its lowest vertex is more than one layer above the
   part's minimum along b). A flat ceiling whose shortest span is at most `max_bridge_m` is exempt when the
   card allows bridging.
5. **Overhang violations.** Connected violating regions with: area, maximum β, centroid, limit, difference
   β − limit, repair target (the limit). Suggestions: the build direction among the card's list and the six
   axis directions that minimizes violating area (reported with its area), and the support volume implied by
   the present direction (the volume under violating facets down to the first surface or the plate; printed
   as an estimate, class Estimated). `sls` has no overhang limit and this evaluator is not run for it.
6. **Class and authority.** Estimated; `review: no-claim`. Sample density: every triangle centroid plus edge
   midpoints for triangles larger than h² (DESIGN); the sampled area share is reported.
7. **Differential harness for the switch-over** (`tests/fab_differential.rs`, ignored, tagged FD-FSIM-29): runs
   both evaluators and the upstream ones on the fixture battery and requires equal violating regions within a
   band of 2·h and equal measured angles within 1°.
8. **Determinism.** Fixed direction sets and march steps; findings sorted by location.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An open pocket 20 mm deep with setups {+z} and reach 40 mm is fully accessible; the same pocket 60 mm deep is flagged `reach` with a shortfall of 20 mm and the repair target 60 mm.
- [ ] A slot under a 5 mm lip is flagged `undercut` with setup {+z}; adding the side setup along which the slot is open makes it accessible, and the suggestion names that direction.
- [ ] A hole of diameter 4 mm is not accessible to a 3 mm-radius cutter and is accessible to the 1.5 mm one; the smallest radius needed is recorded.
- [ ] `cnc-5axis` with a 30° cone reaches a face tilted 20° past vertical that `cnc-3axis` flags.
- [ ] Sphere of radius 10 mm built along +z with a 45° limit: violating area 2·π·R²·(1 − cos 45°) = 184.0 mm² within 3% (excluding the plate contact).
- [ ] A horizontal shelf's underside has β = 90° and violates; a 45° chamfer is at the limit and passes; a wall leaning 30° passes.
- [ ] A 3 mm bridge is exempt on a card with `max_bridge_m = 0.005` and violates on a card with zero.
- [ ] The best alternative build direction is reported for a part that prints cleanly on its side.
- [ ] `sls` parts are not given an overhang check.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry fab::access fab::overhang`:
- `open_pocket_accessible`; `deep_pocket_reach_shortfall_20mm`.
- `lip_undercut_flagged`; `side_setup_fixes_undercut`; `suggested_setup_is_best_by_area`.
- `small_hole_needs_small_cutter`.
- `five_axis_cone_reaches_tilted_face`.
- `wall_parallel_to_tool_is_accessible`.
- `overhang_angle_definition`: n = (0, 0, −1), b = (0, 0, 1): β = 90°; n = (1, 0, 0): β = 0°; n = (0.7071, 0, −0.7071): β = 45°.
- `sphere_overhang_area`: 2·π·(10 mm)²·0.29289 = 184.0 mm².
- `shelf_violates`; `chamfer_at_limit_passes`; `plate_contact_exempt`.
- `bridge_exemption`.
- `best_build_direction_suggested`.
- `support_volume_estimate` for the shelf: shelf area × height above the plate.
- Planted defects, each caught: `overhang-sign-flipped` (flags upward faces; the sphere's violating region moves to the top), `access-ignores-reach`, `access-ignores-cutter-radius` (treats the tool as a ray; the 4 mm hole passes with the 3 mm cutter), `facing-test-strict` (rejects walls parallel to the tool).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_check.sh` (bead N31a-55): `frob foundry check --fixture deep-pocket --fab --process
cnc-3axis --json`, `--fixture lip-slot`, `--fixture shelf --process slm`, `--fixture sphere-r10 --process fdm`;
asserts feature, failed condition, measured value and repair for each; logs violating area, sampled area
share, the suggested setup or build direction and evaluation time.

### Known answers and negative controls
Sphere overhang area 184.0 mm² (the spherical cap within 45° of straight down); the angle definition cases;
the reach shortfall of 20 mm. Negative controls: the four planted defects and clean fixtures. Metamorphic:
reversing the build direction maps the violating set to that of the mirrored part; adding a setup never
removes accessibility; lengthening the tool never creates a violation. Determinism: identical findings twice
on one machine.

## Considerations

- Real machining access depends on the holder, the fixture and the path planner; this is a geometric
  necessary condition, not a toolpath. That is why the report says `review: no-claim` and why a quotation
  from a shop is an import lane.
- The overhang rule ignores support that the part itself provides beyond the first layer below, thermal
  distortion and residual stress; it is the standard first filter.
- A mechanical pass per part says nothing about assembly: a two-part bracket whose second fastener sits behind
  the first part passes tool access as parts and cannot be assembled. That case belongs to the packet-review
  record (a bead of unit U31b).
- KILL-FAB fallback: findings become advisory lints.
