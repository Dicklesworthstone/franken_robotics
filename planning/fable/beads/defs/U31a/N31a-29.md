## Background

Manufacturability can be enforced at two moments. A constraint that has a usable derivative can be active
inside the optimization, so the optimizer never proposes what cannot be made. One that has none can only be
checked afterwards, and a failure costs a re-run.

The plan's split: **inside the optimization only constraints with usable derivatives are active: length scale,
envelope, symmetry, and for additive parts an overhang filter. The others are checked after extraction. A
violation returns the repair from `check_all` (target value and difference) and triggers a re-run with a
tightened length scale.**

The sibling optimizer provides a minimum length scale through its robust erode/dilate formulation and nothing
else process-specific (FACT: "no process constraints beyond length scale"). Envelope is the design region.
Symmetry is the sibling's quotient chart. The overhang filter is therefore Foundry's to supply, and whether it
can be supplied *inside* a sibling job depends on whether the job's filter stack admits an external stage.

## Scope

In: mapping process cards to the optimizer's length scale; the symmetry and envelope hand-off; the overhang
filter (mathematics, derivative, placement); the fail-closed posture when the filter cannot be placed inside
the job; the post-extraction check loop and its re-run policy.
Out: the optimization formulation (N31a-15); the region (N31a-14); the evaluators (N31a-27, N31a-28); the
report (N31a-26).

## Technical approach

1. **Length scale from the process** (`fab/inopt.rs`). The optimizer's minimum length scale is the largest of:
   the card's `min_feature_size`; for milling, twice the smallest cutter radius (so that an internal slot is
   at least one cutter wide and internal corners can take a cutter-radius fillet); for sheet parts, the larger
   of stock thickness and kerf diameter; the designer's declared minimum. Both solid and void length scales
   are set (the robust formulation enforces both through its eroded and dilated designs).
2. **Envelope.** The region record is the optimization domain; keep-outs are void, bosses are frozen solid.
   Nothing further is needed inside the job.
3. **Symmetry.** For a mirrored link class the quotient chart makes left and right one design. A process may
   add a symmetry of its own: a part milled from two opposite setups is often designed mirror-symmetric about
   the mid-plane so that both setups are the same program; when the designer declares it, the plane is passed
   as an additional quotient and the load set is symmetrized by union (each case and its mirror image).
4. **Overhang filter** (additive processes with an overhang limit; DESIGN, a layerwise support filter). With
   the grid's third axis along the build direction and cubic cells (which makes the self-supporting angle
   45°; for another limit the support stencil is widened or narrowed accordingly), the printable density ξ of
   a cell in layer k is
   - ξ(i, j, 1) = ρ(i, j, 1) on the build plate;
   - Ξ(i, j, k) = smax over the supporting cells of layer k − 1 (the cell below and its four neighbours);
   - ξ(i, j, k) = smin(ρ(i, j, k), Ξ(i, j, k));
   with smin(a, b) = ½·(a + b − √((a − b)² + ε) + √ε), ε = 10⁻⁴, and smax the P-norm
   (Σ ξᴾ)^(1/Q), P = 40, Q = P + ln(n_s)/ln(ρ₀) with n_s = 5 supporting cells and ρ₀ = 0.5, so that a uniform
   field of density ρ₀ is reproduced. A cell cannot be denser than what supports it. The derivative with
   respect to ρ is obtained by one reverse sweep through the layers (top to bottom), at linear cost.
   The filter is inserted after the length-scale filter and before the projection.
5. **Where the filter runs.**
   - If the airlock's optimization job accepts an external differentiable stage (value and vector-Jacobian
     product callbacks crossing as whole-field exchanges per iteration), the filter runs inside the job.
   - Otherwise (the fail-closed default until the capability probe passes): the optimization runs without it,
     the result is labelled `overhang: post-check only`, and overhang is handled by step 6. This is recorded
     as a gap against the sibling; no gate depends on the in-job form.
6. **Post-extraction check loop** (`fab/inopt.rs::repair_rerun`). After extraction and before the independent
   structural check is accepted, run the manufacturability checks of the part's process. On violation:
   1. collect the repairs from the limit check (target value and difference per finding);
   2. choose the re-run change: for thickness and corner-radius findings, a tightened length scale
      L′ = max(1.25·L, the largest repair target expressed as a length); for overhang findings, first the
      best alternative build direction reported by the evaluator, then the tightened length scale; for
      tool-access findings, an added setup if the card allows one, otherwise the tightened length scale; for
      enclosed voids, the void is added to the region as a keep-out connected to the nearest outside face
      (which turns a cavity into a pocket);
   3. re-run the optimization for that class with the change, at most twice (DESIGN cap);
   4. if findings remain, stop and return the part with its findings; the structure loop then uses the last
      candidate that passes its process checks, or the beam section when none does, and says so.
   Every re-run is recorded with its cause, the change and the mass it cost.
7. **Mass cost of manufacturability.** The record reports the mass of the unconstrained optimum (when
   available), the mass with in-job constraints, and the mass after re-runs, so the price of each process rule
   is visible.
8. **Class.** Estimated. Determinism: the filter is a fixed-order sweep in `f64`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For `cnc-3axis` with cutters {1.5, 3, 5} mm and `min_feature_size` 1.5 mm the optimizer's length scale is 3.0 mm; for `slm` with 0.8 mm it is 0.8 mm unless the designer declares more.
- [ ] Overhang filter values: a column of density 1 over a plate cell of density 1 stays 1; a cell of density 1 over supporting cells all of density 0 becomes at most 0.05; a uniform field of 0.5 is reproduced within 10⁻³.
- [ ] The filter's reverse-sweep derivative agrees with central finite differences to 10⁻⁵ relative on a 16 × 16 × 16 random field.
- [ ] On the shelf fixture optimized for `slm`, the in-job filter (or, in the fallback posture, the re-run) yields a part whose violating overhang area is zero or within the bridge allowance; the unfiltered result violates.
- [ ] In the fallback posture the result carries `overhang: post-check only`.
- [ ] A thickness violation triggers one re-run with L′ = max(1.25·L, target) and the re-run's part passes thickness.
- [ ] After two unsuccessful re-runs the part is returned with findings and the loop's fallback choice is recorded.
- [ ] An enclosed void found in a milled part is converted to a keep-out reaching the outside, and the re-run has no enclosed void.
- [ ] The mass cost of each rule is reported.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry fab::inopt`:
- `length_scale_from_card` (the two cases above, plus sheet: stock 2 mm, kerf radius 0.2 mm gives 2.0 mm).
- `smin_smax_values`: smin(1, 0) ≤ 0.005 + tolerance (½·(1 − √(1 + 10⁻⁴) + 0.01) = 0.00498); smax of five cells at 0.5 is 0.5 within 10⁻³; smax of (1, 0, 0, 0, 0) is at least 0.95.
- `supported_column_is_kept`; `unsupported_cell_is_removed`.
- `filter_gradient_matches_finite_differences`.
- `filter_layer_order_is_build_direction` (rotating the build direction rotates the result).
- `fallback_label_when_stage_not_admitted`.
- `rerun_policy_thickness`; `rerun_policy_overhang_tries_direction_first`; `rerun_policy_access_adds_setup`; `rerun_cap_two`.
- `void_becomes_keepout_to_outside`.
- `symmetrized_load_set_is_union_with_mirror`.
- `mass_cost_report`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `filter-sweep-upside-down` (supports from the layer above), `rerun-loosens-length-scale`, `gradient-forward-sweep` (the derivative swept in the wrong direction fails the finite-difference test).

### End-to-end script and logging
A step of `scripts/e2e/foundry_structure_loop.sh` (bead N31a-54): `frob foundry optimize <shelf fixture>
--process slm --exploratory --json`, asserting either zero violating overhang area or the `post-check only`
label with a recorded re-run; a `cnc-3axis` fixture designed to produce a thin web, asserting one re-run with
the tightened length scale. Logged: length scales before and after, causes of re-runs, mass after each,
filter placement (`in-job` or `post-check`).

### Known answers and negative controls
The smin and smax values above; the self-supporting angle of 45° for cubic cells with a five-cell support
stencil. Negative controls: the three planted defects. Metamorphic: applying the filter twice equals applying
it once on a field that is already printable (idempotence within 10⁻³); increasing any density never decreases
any printable density. Determinism: identical bits twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- The in-job overhang filter needs a capability the airlock may not have (an external differentiable stage in
  a sibling job). No upstream work item covers it yet; the gap table of the sibling adapter bead records it,
  and the post-check posture means no milestone waits for it.
- A tightened length scale costs mass, and two re-runs at 1.25 each raise the length scale by 56%. The
  mass-cost report keeps the consequence visible, and a designer may prefer another process.
- Tool access has no derivative and is the constraint most likely to exhaust the re-runs on a three-axis
  process; the honest outcome is then a part with findings or the beam section.
- KILL-FAB fallback: with findings advisory, the re-run loop is disabled and findings are only reported.
