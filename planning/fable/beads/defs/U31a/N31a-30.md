## Background

GATE-FOUNDRY-07 (claim: Known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): **planted violations of wall, corner radius, tool
access, overhang and undercut each detected with location and repair; clean parts pass; 20 of 20.**

The manufacturability evaluators are geometric code written in this project because no sibling measures these
features. Geometric code fails quietly: an evaluator that flags nothing passes every clean part. The gate
therefore pairs planted violations, each with a known location and magnitude, with clean parts that must
produce no finding, and it requires the repair (the target value and the difference) as well as the detection.
The evaluators themselves are mutated to show the battery can fail.

The decision that hangs on it is KILL-FAB: if this gate fails, manufacturability findings become advisory
lints and a packet records no manufacturability pass.

## Scope

In: the twenty fixtures with their expected findings; the matching rule between an expected and an actual
finding; the evaluator mutants; the battery receipt.
Out: the evaluators and the dispatcher (N31a-26 to N31a-28); the re-run policy (N31a-29); the user-side script
(N31a-55), which re-measures the gate through the `frob` binary, and its recording session, which records the
GATE-FOUNDRY-07 row with `record_gate` citing this battery's receipt (one row, written once; convention 9).

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures** (`crates/frob-foundry/tests/fixtures/fab/`, all `synthetic`, built from exact primitives by a
   generator so that every planted dimension is known; each has a process card and an expectation file).

   | # | Fixture | Process | Planted violation | Expected finding |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | Bracket with a 1.0 mm web | `cnc-3axis`, feature minimum 1.5 mm | wall | thickness 1.0 mm at the web; target 1.5; difference 0.5 mm |
   | 2 | Lattice strut 0.5 mm | `slm`, minimum 0.8 mm | wall | thickness 0.5 mm; target 0.8; difference 0.3 mm |
   | 3 | Sheet part with a 1.2 mm bridge between holes | `sheet`, stock 2 mm | wall | thickness 1.2 mm; target 2.0 mm |
   | 4 | Pocket with 2.0 mm internal fillets | `cnc-3axis`, smallest cutter radius 3.0 mm | corner radius | radius 2.0 mm at four corners; target 3.0; difference 1.0 mm; secondary: `gouge` at the same four corners |
   | 5 | Pocket with sharp internal corners | `cnc-3axis`, cutter radius 1.5 mm | corner radius | `r < 2h` at four corners; target 1.5 mm; secondary: `gouge` at the same four corners |
   | 6 | Pocket 10 mm wide with 1.2 mm internal corner radii | `cnc-5axis`, cutter radius 1.5 mm | corner radius | radius 1.2 mm at four corners; target 1.5; difference 0.3 mm; secondary: `gouge` at the same corners (tilting within the cone does not cure it) |
   | 7 | Blind pocket 60 mm deep | `cnc-3axis`, reach 40 mm | tool access | `reach`, shortfall 20 mm at the pocket floor |
   | 8 | Hole of diameter 2 mm | `cnc-3axis`, smallest cutter radius 1.5 mm | tool access | `gouge` in the bore; secondary: corner radius 1.0 mm on the bore wall, target 1.5 |
   | 9 | Face on the underside, setups {+z} only | `cnc-3axis` | tool access | `undercut` on the lower face; suggested setup −z |
   | 10 | Horizontal shelf | `slm`, limit 45° | overhang | β = 90° on the underside; difference 45° |
   | 11 | Wall leaning 60° from vertical | `fdm`, limit 45° | overhang | β = 60°; difference 15° |
   | 12 | Sphere of radius 10 mm | `fdm`, limit 45° | overhang | violating area 184.0 mm² around the bottom |
   | 13 | T-slot under a 5 mm lip, setups {+z} | `cnc-3axis` | undercut | `undercut` under the lip; suggested side setup |
   | 14 | Dovetail with 10° negative walls | `cnc-3axis`, setups {+z} | undercut | `undercut` on both dovetail walls |
   | 15 | Moulded cup with a 3° reverse draft band | card with draft minimum 2° and a demould direction | undercut | draft undercut flagged on the band |
   | 16 | Clean milled bracket (fillets 3.5 mm, walls 3 mm, two setups) | `cnc-3axis` | none | no finding; `pass: true` |
   | 17 | Clean printed link (all overhangs at most 40°, walls 1.2 mm) | `slm` | none | no finding |
   | 18 | Clean sheet plate | `sheet` | none | no finding |
   | 19 | Clean five-axis part with a tilted face | `cnc-5axis`, cone 30° | none | no finding |
   | 20 | Clean printed part with a drained cavity (4 mm drain, minimum 3 mm) | `sls` | none | no finding |

   Rows 1 to 3 are wall, 4 to 6 corner radius, 7 to 9 tool access, 10 to 12 overhang, 13 to 15 undercut, 16 to
   20 clean: three per violation kind and five clean parts. Cutter sizes on the cards are radii.

   **Secondary findings.** A concave corner tighter than the cutter is seen by two evaluators, because the
   tangent-ball cross-check of the corner evaluator (N31a-27) and the swept-cylinder clearance of the access
   evaluator (N31a-28) are the same geometric test there; likewise a bore narrower than the cutter is both a
   gouge and a concave radius below the cutter's. The table lists the second finding as *secondary*: it must be
   present and co-located, it does not count toward its kind's three, and it is in the expectation file so
   that it is not an unexpected finding.

   **Clean margins.** Apart from its planted violation and the secondary findings it causes, each fixture is
   clean for its card by construction: the generator fillets every other internal edge at a radius exceeding
   the card's smallest cutter radius (on a `sheet` card, its `kerf_radius_m`, the limit of the sheet corner
   check of N31a-27) by at least 4·h (h of the default resolution), keeps every other wall
   thicker than the card's minimum by at least 4·h, keeps every other downward face within the overhang limit
   less 5°, and declares setups that reach every other face. Rows 16 to 20 check that such margins raise no
   finding.
2. **Matching rule.** An expected finding is *detected with location and repair* when an actual finding has
   the same feature and (where stated) failed condition; its location lies inside the fixture's declared
   violation region dilated by 2·h; its measured value is within the fixture's tolerance (± h for lengths, ± 2°
   for angles, ± 3% for areas); and its repair target equals the card's limit with the difference equal to
   limit minus measured within the same tolerance. A secondary finding is matched by feature, condition and
   location only (its region within 2·h of the primary's). A fixture passes when every expected finding,
   primary and secondary, is matched and there is no finding outside its expectation file. A clean fixture
   passes when it has no finding at all.
3. **Evaluator mutants** (registered negative controls; the battery must drop below 20 of 20 under each):
   `fab-mut-thickness-uses-bounding-box`, `fab-mut-curvature-sign`, `fab-mut-access-ignores-reach`,
   `fab-mut-access-ray-not-cylinder`, `fab-mut-overhang-sign`, `fab-mut-facing-accepts-undercut`,
   `fab-mut-flag-everything` (fails the clean parts), `fab-mut-flag-nothing` (fails the planted ones),
   `fab-mut-repair-target-is-measured` (detects but returns a useless repair).
4. **Resolution.** The battery runs at the evaluators' default grid rule and again at half the spacing; both
   must pass, and measured values must move toward the planted dimensions.
5. **Battery receipt.** Per fixture: pass or fail, each expected finding with planted and measured values, the
   location error, the repair; the total out of 20; per-kind detection counts (wall, corner radius, tool
   access, overhang, undercut, clean); evaluator versions; commit and hardware fingerprint. The battery is
   bound into the Gauntlet as the G1 row of GATE-FOUNDRY-07, so it runs as `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test
   -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-FOUNDRY-07` (the nine mutants of step 3, registered in
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml`, run with `--features gauntlet-mutations` through the
   control runner of N38b-04), and its receipt carries those values with the nine controls caught in the same
   run. This bead records nothing: the GATE-FOUNDRY-07 row is recorded once by N31a-55's recording session,
   a session that did not implement the evaluators, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate
   -- --gate GATE-FOUNDRY-07 --receipt <hash>`, citing this receipt by hash together with its own script's
   receipt (N38b-19: the recorder refuses a receipt whose controls were not caught in the same run).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 20 of 20 fixtures pass at the default resolution and at half spacing.
- [ ] Each of the five violation kinds is detected on its three fixtures with location inside the planted region and the repair's target and difference correct.
- [ ] The five clean fixtures produce no finding.
- [ ] Each of the nine evaluator mutants reduces the count below 20 and the failing fixtures are the expected ones (including fixtures that fail through a missing or extra secondary finding).
- [ ] The secondary findings of rows 4, 5, 6 and 8 are present and co-located within 2·h, and no fixture has a finding outside its expectation file.
- [ ] The battery's receipt (the G1 row of GATE-FOUNDRY-07) holds measured values, not targets, in the form `record_gate` reads, with per-kind detection counts and the nine mutants caught in the same run; this bead writes no gate row (N31a-55's recording session records it, citing this receipt).
- [ ] The fixture generator is deterministic: regenerated fixtures hash identically.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry --test fab_battery` (the gate lane):
- `fixture_<nn>` × 20, each asserting the matching rule.
- `battery_count_is_twenty_of_twenty`.
- `mutant_<name>_is_caught` × 9, each asserting the count and the set of failing fixtures.
- `half_spacing_also_passes`; `measured_values_converge`.
- `generator_is_deterministic`.
- `matching_rule_rejects_wrong_location`: a finding of the right feature 10 mm away from the planted region does not match.
- `matching_rule_rejects_missing_repair`.
- `secondary_must_be_colocated` (a planted `gouge` 10 mm from the corner on row 4 does not match) and `unexpected_finding_fails_fixture` (a planted extra overhang finding on row 1 fails it).
- `clean_margins_hold`: on each planted fixture, every finding lies inside the planted region dilated by 2·h.
- Gauntlet binding: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-FOUNDRY-07` passes, and with `--features gauntlet-mutations` the control runner reports each of the nine mutants `caught`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_fab_battery.sh` (this bead's own, built only on its dependencies; it sources the STD-E2E
helper library). Steps: (1) `cargo test -p frob-foundry --test fab_battery` through `e2e_cmd`; (2) `e2e_tier
g1_known_answers GATE-FOUNDRY-07`, which logs the receipt hash; from the receipt (`e2e_get --file
"$E2E_LAST_RECEIPT"`) one `assert` line per expected finding (planted value, measured value, tolerance,
location error), the per-kind detection counts, and a `metric` line `fab_fixtures_passed` against 20 with
`--gate GATE-FOUNDRY-07`, at the default resolution and at half spacing; (3) `e2e_tier g1_known_answers
GATE-FOUNDRY-07 --mutations`: each of the nine evaluator mutants read from the receipt's `controls` as
`caught`, with its failing fixtures, one `assert` of kind `negative-control` each. The script writes no gate
record (convention 9). No `frob` verb is called, so the evidence block is recorded as absent with reason
`no-verb`. Logs to `target/e2e-logs/foundry_fab_battery/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: per line `ts`,
`script`, `bead` = N31a-30, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time and `receipt`; on failure the
first unmatched expected finding or the first unexpected one; a final `summary` naming GATE-FOUNDRY-07.
`scripts/e2e/foundry_check.sh` (N31a-55) runs this script as a stage and re-measures the battery through the
binary: the twenty fixtures through `frob foundry check --fab --fixture <name> --process <p> --key "$(e2e_key
fab-<name>)" --json` (a `run` verb, so each call carries a key), with the same matching rule applied to the
envelopes.

### Known answers and negative controls
Planted dimensions are known by construction (the table). The sphere's violating area 2·π·R²·(1 − cos 45°) =
184.0 mm² for R = 10 mm. Negative controls: the nine mutants. Metamorphic: every fixture rotated by 90° about
an axis, with its card's directions rotated too, yields the same findings in the rotated frame. Determinism:
the battery's findings are identical twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- "Undercut" in the gate's list is covered in two ways, because the plan's table has it under draft (moulded
  parts) while the six declarable processes have no moulded one: rows 13 and 14 are milling undercuts found by
  the tool-access evaluator, and row 15 is a draft undercut found by the sibling's draft measure on a card
  that declares a demould direction.
- The fixtures are primitives on purpose: an optimized part with a violation somewhere is not a known answer.
  Optimized parts are exercised by the structure-loop script.
- The fixture list is frozen when the gate is first recorded; later additions are recorded changes.
- A failure here after one repair cycle triggers KILL-FAB; the record then carries the per-kind detection
  counts so that the advisory lints can say which kinds are reliable.
