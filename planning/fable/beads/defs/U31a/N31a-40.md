## Background

Two rows of the packet's content table are carried here.

**Geometry.** The four exports per made part; datum scheme and at least three non-collinear fiducials per
part. Rule: datums and fiducials are placed at design time so that the as-built scan registers.

**Mass properties.** Mass, centre of mass and inertia per part and for the assembly, recomputed from the
exported geometry. Rule: they must equal the model's values within 10⁻⁶ relative (TARGET), which is the
cross-export identity.

Why fiducials are designed in. The as-built sibling registers a scan to the design by a closed-form rigid fit
with supplied correspondences; it does not search for correspondences (a search lane is upstream item
FD-FSIM-25, whose interim posture is exactly this: datums and fiducials are placed at design time so
correspondences are known). A part without identifiable reference points cannot be compared with its scan, and
then nothing can be said about whether the built part is the designed one.

Why the identity is tested. Invariant INV-FOUNDRY-04: mass properties in the packet equal those of the model
the evidence was computed with; tested by recomputing them from the exported geometry. If a packet's geometry
weighs something other than the simulated link, every structural and control result in the packet is about a
different machine. The third clause of GATE-FOUNDRY-09 is this test: mass properties from exported geometry
within 10⁻⁶ of the model.

## Scope

In: the per-part geometry record (files, counts, part frame); the datum scheme; fiducial placement and its
non-collinearity test; `mass.json`; recomputation of mass properties from each of the four exported files; the
comparison rule and its failure report; the assembly roll-up with uncertainty.
Out: writing the geometry files (N31a-34, N31a-35); the model's mass properties and the feedback edit
(N31a-23); as-built registration (Ground); the same identity extended to URDF and MJCF exports and the
unit-error diagnosis (unit U31b).

## Technical approach

1. **Geometry record** (`packet/geometry.rs`; one `geometry/<part>.json` per made part, schema
   `frob.foundry.geometry/1`): the four files with hash, triangle and vertex counts; the part frame and its
   transform to the link frame; `units: "metre"`; the STEP statements (faceted, no units entity, no
   conformance claim); the datum scheme; the fiducials.
2. **Datum scheme** (DESIGN). Three datum features define a reference frame a shop and a scanner can both
   find: *A*, the interface face of the proximal port (a plane: fixes three degrees of freedom); *B*, the axis
   of the proximal bore or, where there is none, a second face (fixes two); *C*, the centre of the distal
   port's interface or a fiducial (fixes the last). The record gives each feature's kind, its defining
   geometry in the part frame, and the resulting datum frame. Parts with one port use two faces and a
   fiducial. Every toleranced feature in `tolerances.json` refers to this frame.
3. **Fiducials** (DESIGN). A fiducial is a small conical dimple (default 1.5 mm across, 60° cone) at a
   recorded position and normal. Candidate sites: surface points on frozen bosses and on the thickest members,
   excluding interface surfaces, no-claim zones, gauge locations and any region the part's process cannot
   reach. Selection: start with the two candidates farthest apart, then add the candidate that maximizes the
   area of the triangle (then, for a fourth, the volume of the tetrahedron); default four fiducials, minimum
   three.
   - *Non-collinearity test*: for the chosen points, the smallest altitude of the triangle formed by the best
     three must be at least 10% of the part's bounding-box diagonal (DESIGN threshold). Fewer than three sites,
     or a failed test, blocks the packet with the finding `fiducials: not registrable` and the repair "add a
     fiducial pad" at the best available site.
   - Dimples are part of the exported geometry (so the scanner sees them) and are added before the
     independent check; their volume is below 10⁻⁶ of any link's and they sit outside stressed regions.
4. **Recomputation** (`packet/mass.rs`). For each made part and each of its four exported files: read the file
   back through the quarantine path; compute volume, first and second moments of the closed triangle surface
   exactly for the polyhedron (signed tetrahedra to the origin, in compensated summation); multiply by the
   card density; transform to the link frame. Because all four files hold the same export-exact polyhedron,
   the four results agree with each other to rounding.
5. **Comparison with the model.** For each part compare each export's result with the mass properties the
   model holds for that link (the values of the feedback edit):
   - mass: |m − m_model|/m_model;
   - centre of mass: ‖c − c_model‖ ÷ the part's bounding-box diagonal;
   - inertia: ‖I − I_model‖_F/‖I_model‖_F (Frobenius norm, about the centre of mass, link axes).
   All three must be at most 10⁻⁶. A failure names the part, the file, the quantity and both values, blocks
   the packet, and prints the ratio of volumes, because a ratio near 10⁹ or 10⁻⁹ means a unit error rather
   than a geometry error.
6. **`mass.json`** (`frob.foundry.mass/1`): per made part: mass, centre of mass, inertia tensor, each with
   relative uncertainty (from the density) and class Estimated, plus the four per-export differences; per
   purchased part: mass and, where the card has them, centre of mass and inertia, with card provenance; the
   assembly at the nominal pose: total mass, centre of mass and inertia about it, with uncertainty combined in
   quadrature over independent parts; the comparison of the assembly totals with the model's.
7. **Class and determinism.** Estimated for values; the comparison itself is exact arithmetic on recorded
   numbers. Recomputation is deterministic (fixed triangle order).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-09, third clause: for every made part of the reference packet, mass, centre of mass and inertia recomputed from each of the four exports are within 10⁻⁶ relative of the model's values; the largest difference is recorded.
- [ ] The tube (ρ = 2,700 kg/m³, radii 15 and 13 mm, length 0.20 m) is reproduced from each geometry export: the four recomputed masses agree with each other to 10⁻¹² and with the model's value for that mesh to 10⁻⁶; against the analytic 0.0950 kg the faceted mesh is within 0.1%.
- [ ] A planted mismatch (the model's link mass changed by 10⁻⁵ relative after export) blocks the packet and names the part and the quantity.
- [ ] A surface file scaled by 1,000 (millimetres read as metres) gives a volume ratio of 10⁹, which the failure report prints.
- [ ] Every made part has a datum scheme and at least three fiducials passing the non-collinearity test; a part offered only two sites is blocked with `fiducials: not registrable`.
- [ ] Three collinear fiducials (a planted fixture) fail the test.
- [ ] Fiducial dimples appear in all four exports at the recorded positions.
- [ ] `mass.json` validates and its assembly mass equals the parts-list roll-up and the model within 10⁻⁶.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry packet::geometry packet::mass`:
- `polyhedron_moments_cube`: a cube of side 0.1 m at 2,700 kg/m³: 2.7 kg; inertia about each central axis m·a²/6 = 4.5 × 10⁻³ kg·m².
- `polyhedron_moments_tetrahedron`: vertices at the origin and the three unit points scaled by 0.1 m: volume 1.6667 × 10⁻⁴ m³; centroid (0.025, 0.025, 0.025) m.
- `four_exports_agree_to_rounding`.
- `tube_from_each_export`.
- `planted_model_mismatch_blocks`; `unit_scale_reports_1e9`.
- `comparison_norms`: a rotated inertia tensor with equal principal moments is not equal in the Frobenius comparison (the frame matters).
- `datum_frame_from_face_axis_point` (orthonormal, right-handed, origin on A).
- `fiducial_selection_maximizes_triangle`; `non_collinearity_altitude_rule`; `two_sites_blocked`; `collinear_three_blocked`.
- `dimples_in_all_exports`.
- `assembly_rollup_with_uncertainty`: two parts of 1 kg ± 1% and 3 kg ± 1% give 4 kg ± 0.79% (quadrature: √(0.01² + 0.03²)/4).
- Planted defects, each caught: `recompute-from-source-mesh-not-file` (never reads the exported file, so a corrupted export passes), `compare-inertia-about-different-points`, `fiducials-on-interface-face`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_export_packet.sh` (bead N31a-57): after export, runs `frob foundry check <packet> --json`
and asserts `cross_export.max_rel ≤ 1e-6` with the per-part table; replaces one STL in a scratch copy by a
copy scaled by 1,000 (fixing the manifest hash so that only this check can catch it) and asserts the failure
with ratio 10⁹; logs per part and format the three differences and the fiducial triangle's altitude ratio.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-744 (the tube: 0.0950 kg; 1.87 × 10⁻⁵ and 3.26 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m²; control: the volume enclosure must contain the
exact volume). Cube 2.7 kg and 4.5 × 10⁻³ kg·m²; tetrahedron volume a³/6. Negative controls: the three planted
defects, the scaled file and the collinear fiducials. Metamorphic: a rigid motion of the part frame changes no
comparison result; mirroring a part (a left-hand instance) negates the off-diagonal inertia terms that involve
the mirrored axis and leaves mass and principal moments unchanged. Determinism: recomputed values are bitwise
identical twice.

## Considerations

- KILL-PACKET (M5): GATE-FOUNDRY-09 failing after one repair cycle reduces the packet to a parts list, mass
  properties and STL; the identity is then tested on STL alone.
- The identity is tight because the export-exact rule makes it cheap; a looser tolerance would hide a part
  that was decimated, smoothed or re-meshed after the check.
- Dimples are a manufacturing instruction and a metrology aid; on some processes they are marked, not cut.
  The record gives position and normal, and the process decides how.
- A real scan compares against this geometry with a registration residual; if the residual exceeds the
  deviation under review, no deviation is reported. That logic lives with the as-built ingestion.
