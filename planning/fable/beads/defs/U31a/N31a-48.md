## Background

Lifecycle compression advances a slow state z by an averaged rate ⟨g⟩ estimated from short simulations. This
bead defines the four families of slow state, the law that gives each one's rate, and how each feeds back
into the fast model:

| Slow state | Law (empirical; coefficients from cards) | Feeds back into |
|---|---|---|
| Backlash β per joint | Archard volume k·F_n·s/H mapped to recession by a card geometry factor | The backlash rows of the joint solve |
| Fatigue damage per gauge | Rainflow with Miner's sum, residues carried across windows; cycles between duty modes counted at the macro level | A threshold only |
| Winding insulation | An Arrhenius rate in winding temperature | A threshold only |
| Cell capacity and resistance | Calendar fade in √t plus fade per unit of charge throughput | Bus voltage sag in the actuator model |

Every law is empirical. The siblings supply little: the contact sibling has an Archard wear volume advanced
from caller-supplied sliding distance and compressive load, with no recession geometry and no map from volume
to backlash; the port-Hamiltonian sibling has a finite state-of-charge battery with no capacity fade or
resistance growth; a search of the sibling workspace found no cell-ageing law and no insulation-life law
(FACT). The laws are therefore written here, with coefficients from cards.

**Counterexample: the law of the average is not the average of the law (CX-344).** With the common halving of
insulation life per 10 K, a duty that spends half its time at T_ref and half at T_ref + 20 K consumes life at
2.5/τ_ref. Evaluating the law at the mean temperature gives 2.0/τ_ref and overstates service duration by 25%.
Micro windows therefore average the rate, never the input.

**Accelerated estimates can also change the failure mechanism; each law carries a validity domain and refuses
outside it** (FROB-REF-831: a lifecycle law outside its validity domain, or cited above class Estimated;
repair offered: the domain and the card needed).

OPEN-FOUNDRY-5 (wear, insulation and cell-fade coefficients with provenance; owner: Sinew lead; blocks
GATE-FOUNDRY-12): default generic cards marked `assumed`, which `--strict-data` refuses. KILL-SINEW-AGE: if
the battery ageing law fails its pilot, or no card with ageing coefficients better than `assumed` exists when
this work is funded, f_Q and f_R are user-set constants per scenario with no evolution law.

## Scope

In: the four laws as implementations of the slow-law interface; their card fields; the rate integrals over a
micro window; the feedback of each slow state into the model variant; fatigue across windows and duty modes;
validity domains and the refusal; the class rule.
Out: the averaging core (N31a-47); triggers for full resolution and rare overloads (N31a-49); thresholds,
times and outputs (N31a-50); the fast models of backlash, thermal and battery (the Sinew subsystem).

## Technical approach

1. **Backlash wear** (`laws/wear.rs`). Archard's law: worn volume rate V̇ = k·F_n·ṡ/H, with wear coefficient
   k (dimensionless), normal load F_n at the wearing contact, sliding speed ṡ and hardness H. For a geared
   joint the card supplies the reduction from joint quantities: F_n = |τ|/r_eff for transmitted torque τ and
   effective radius r_eff, ṡ = σ·r_eff·|θ̇| for slip fraction σ, and a geometry factor γ (radians of backlash
   per cubic metre worn). Hence β̇ = γ·(k·σ/H)·|τ·θ̇| = c_w·|τ·θ̇|, with c_w a card coefficient: backlash
   growth is proportional to transmitted mechanical work rate. The window integrates |τ·θ̇| per joint from the
   traces (the sibling's wear routine advances the volume from the supplied sliding distance and load).
   Feedback: β sets the dead-zone width of the joint's backlash row in the model variant.
   Domain: contact pressure, sliding speed, lubrication regime and temperature ranges from the card.
2. **Fatigue damage** (`laws/fatigue.rs`). Slow state: damage sum D per gauge (gauges and influence matrix
   from Foundry's records for the current geometry). Within a window the gauge stress history is counted by
   the same streaming counter, and the *closed* cycles give the window's damage; the rate is damage per unit
   time. Two things cross window boundaries: the counter's residue, which is kept per gauge for the whole
   run and never finished per window; and **cycles between duty modes**, counted at the macro level: each
   window contributes its (minimum, maximum) gauge stress, the sequence of these extremes over the duty
   profile is itself rainflow-counted once per duty period, and those slow, large cycles (a loaded leg, then
   an unloaded one) add damage at the rate of the duty period. Feedback: none into the model; a threshold
   only. Domain: that of the stress-life curve (material state, process, finish; cycle range).
3. **Winding insulation** (`laws/insulation.rs`). Life-consumption rate
   r(T) = (1/τ_ref)·2^((T − T_ref)/ΔT_half) for winding temperature T, with τ_ref the life at T_ref and
   ΔT_half the halving interval from the card (10 K is the common rule; an activation-energy form
   exp(−E_a/(k_B·T)) is the alternative the card may name). Slow state: consumed fraction Λ with Λ̇ = r(T(t)).
   The window integrates r over the winding-temperature trace of each actuator. Feedback: a threshold only
   (Λ = 1). Domain: the temperature range of the insulation class; above its limit the law refuses, because
   failure there is not gradual ageing.
4. **Cell capacity and resistance** (`laws/cell.rs`). Capacity fade f_Q = a_cal·√t + a_cyc·N_eq, with t
   calendar time, N_eq equivalent full cycles (charge throughput ÷ twice the capacity) and card coefficients
   a_cal, a_cyc; resistance growth f_R by the same form with its own coefficients. In rate form for the macro
   stepper: the cyclic part's rate is a_cyc × (throughput rate), averaged over the window; the calendar part
   is advanced analytically over each macro step (it does not depend on the fast state). Feedback: f_Q and
   f_R set the battery's capacity and internal resistance in the model variant, which changes bus voltage sag
   and, through it, actuator limits. Domain: temperature, state-of-charge window and current-rate ranges of
   the card.
5. **Validity domains** (`domain.rs`). Each law declares ranges for the quantities it was fitted on. During a
   window the law tracks the share of time each quantity spends outside its range. A share above 1% (DESIGN)
   → FROB-REF-831 for that law. Payload: law, card, quantity, range, observed extremes and share, and the
   repairs: (1) a card whose domain covers the observed range (named when the catalogue has one); (2) the
   design change that brings the quantity inside (for example a lower duty); (3) run without this law, with
   its state reported `no-claim`. Exit code 3.
6. **Class rule.** Every rate, state and time is Estimated. A request, a report template or a downstream
   claim product that would cite a lifecycle number as Tested, Certified or Proved is refused with the same
   code, reason `cited above Estimated`; in practice the type ceiling makes this unreachable inside the
   workspace, and the check exists for imported or hand-edited records.
7. **Coefficients and provenance.** Cards carry c_w; τ_ref, T_ref, ΔT_half; a_cal, a_cyc and the resistance
   pair; each with provenance. The generic catalogue's values are marked `assumed`; `--strict-data` refuses
   them (the card rule's refusal). Under KILL-SINEW-AGE the cell law is disabled and f_Q, f_R are constants
   supplied per scenario; outputs then say `cell ageing: user-set constants`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Rate averaging: halving per 10 K, half the time at T_ref and half at T_ref + 20 K: the window's rate is 2.5/τ_ref exactly; the input-averaging mutation gives 2.0/τ_ref and is caught.
- [ ] Wear feedback: with β̇ = ε·(c₀ + c₁·β) the compressed run reproduces β(t) = (β₀ + c₀/c₁)·exp(ε·c₁·t) − c₀/c₁ within 5% at a compression of at least 50.
- [ ] Cell fade: 1% per √month calendar and 0.5% per 100 cycles give 3% + 1.5% = 4.5% after 9 months and 300 cycles.
- [ ] Fatigue across windows: a duty of two modes whose gauge stress alternates between window envelopes [0, 40] MPa and [100, 140] MPa is credited with the 140 MPa-range cycle per duty period at the macro level, which per-window counting alone misses.
- [ ] The fatigue residue is carried across windows: the nine-point rainflow sequence split over two windows gives the whole-history count.
- [ ] A winding temperature above the insulation class limit for more than 1% of a window returns FROB-REF-831 with the range, the observed maximum and the three repairs.
- [ ] Under `--strict-data` an `assumed` coefficient is refused; without it results carry the card field as the weakest input.
- [ ] With the cell law disabled by the kill switch, f_Q and f_R stay at their scenario constants and the output says so.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-lifecycle laws domain`:
- `arrhenius_two_level_duty`: (1 + 4)/2 = 2.5; at the mean temperature 2^(10/10) = 2.0; ratio 1.25.
- `insulation_rate_values`: r(T_ref + 10 K) = 2/τ_ref; r(T_ref − 10 K) = 0.5/τ_ref.
- `wear_rate_is_work_proportional`: c_w = 10⁻⁹ rad/J, |τ·θ̇| averaging 50 W: β̇ = 5 × 10⁻⁸ rad/s, 1.8 × 10⁻⁴ rad per hour.
- `wear_feedback_closed_form`: c₀ = 1, c₁ = 2, β₀ = 0, ε·t = 1: β = 0.5·(e² − 1) = 3.195.
- `cell_fade_9_months_300_cycles`: 0.01 × √9 + 0.005 × 3 = 0.045.
- `equivalent_cycles_from_throughput`: 20 Ah through a 5 Ah cell is 2 equivalent full cycles.
- `macro_level_mode_cycle_counted`; `window_residue_carried`.
- `domain_share_threshold`; `outside_domain_refuses_831`; `cited_above_estimated_refuses_831`.
- `strict_data_refuses_assumed_coefficients`.
- `backlash_enters_model_variant`: the variant's backlash row width equals β.
- `cell_states_enter_model_variant`: Q = Q₀·(1 − f_Q); R₀ = R₀₀·(1 + f_R).
- `kill_switch_freezes_cell_states`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `law-at-mean-temperature` (the registered shortcut), `finish-counter-per-window`, `calendar-fade-linear-in-t` (gives 9% instead of 3% at nine months), `wear-from-mean-torque-times-mean-speed` (the product of averages, not the average of the product).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_lifecycle.sh` (bead N31a-59): `frob foundry lifecycle --fixture two-temperature-duty`,
`--fixture wear-feedback`, `--fixture cell-fade`, each asserting its closed form; a fixture that overheats a
winding, asserting exit 3 and FROB-REF-831; logs per law the rate with half-width, the domain shares, the card
and provenance, and the class.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-751 (wear feedback: β(t) = (β₀ + c₀/c₁)·exp(ε·c₁·t) − c₀/c₁; its control, a planted gait collapse at β*
that must force full resolution before β*, is carried by bead N31a-49). KA-752 (rate averaging: 2.5/τ_ref, not
2.0/τ_ref; control: averaging the input is caught). KA-753 (cell fade: 3% + 1.5% = 4.5%; the plan states no
control; registered here: the linear-in-time calendar mutation). CX-344 (evaluate a life law at the average
condition). Negative controls: the four planted defects. Metamorphic: doubling every coefficient of a law
doubles its rate; permuting the order of modes within a duty period changes neither the mean rates nor the
macro-level cycle count's damage for a two-mode duty. Determinism: laws are deterministic functions of the
window traces.

## Considerations

- All four laws are extrapolations from accelerated tests in the literature; the validity domain is the only
  protection against applying one where the mechanism differs, and it is only as good as the card.
- Wear changes backlash, backlash changes impacts, impacts change wear: the feedback can run away, which is
  the planted collapse case and the reason for the full-resolution trigger.
- Cycles between duty modes are easy to lose and can dominate fatigue damage; counting them at the macro
  level is what keeps the compressed run from being systematically optimistic.
- Nothing here predicts when a particular robot fails. Outputs are time to a threshold under a law, with an
  interval, class Estimated.
