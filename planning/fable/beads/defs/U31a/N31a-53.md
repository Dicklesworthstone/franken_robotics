## Background

GATE-FOUNDRY-02 (claim: Known answer; required at M5, recordable at M4; TARGET): **free-body residual at most
10⁻⁶ relative on the referee and 10⁻³ on single-precision tiers; superposition equals direct solves
(PO-FOUNDRY-01); the assembled-peak and equal-limb mutations refused.**

The capability under test is the verb `foundry.loads`: run or read a load campaign and return envelope cases,
spectra and the free-body residual. A load campaign is the qualified task set plus push-recovery and drop
tasks, run with level-T2 telemetry on every world at fixed world addresses. The gate matters beyond itself:
KILL-LOADS is triggered if it fails after one repair cycle, and the fallback is hand-declared load cases from
statics with a stated dynamic factor, with spectra and fatigue reading `no-claim`.

This bead is the end-to-end script that measures the gate from the user's side, on the real `frob` binary,
real fixtures and real campaigns, with structured logs from which the gate record is written.

## Scope

In: the script, its fixtures, assertions, logs, the gate record, and its self-test against registered
mutants.
Out: the mechanisms (N31a-06 to N31a-13 and the seed bead BD-400, which created this script's first steps;
this bead governs its final form); the referee re-extraction steps (N31b-35 and its script
`foundry_loads_reextract.sh`, measured for GATE-FOUNDRY-14 by N31b-37); the kill-gate decision KILL-LOADS
(N31b-16, which cites this bead's record).

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures.** `quad-ref` (the reference quadruped of the design corpus, qualified); `quad-ref-offset` (the
   same with a 2 kg payload 0.10 m to the left); `span-two-instants` (`synthetic`: a simply supported span
   with a load at L/3 at one tick and at 2L/3 at the next); `soft-link` (`synthetic`: a link whose first
   natural frequency is 250 Hz under loads with a 60 Hz band); a small campaign specification `camp-small`
   (walk, trot, one push, one drop; 16 worlds; fixed addresses; seed stated in the file).
2. **Steps** (each step logs `cmd`, then `assert` or `metric` lines). `foundry.loads` and `sim.replay` are
   `run` verbs: every call below carries `--key "$(foundry_key <label>)"`, the cluster preamble's name for the
   harness's `e2e_key` (N31a-51 step 9; one label per call, written `--key <k>` below; a call
   without one is refused with FROB-REF-805, and two runs a step compares carry distinct keys).
   `foundry.size` is a `read` verb and takes none.
   1. `start`: build identity, stack-lock digest, airlock mode, hardware fingerprint; preamble of the verb
      cluster (seven verbs listed; `sync_wait_ms=1` returns exit 8 with a job handle).
   1b. *Mechanism stages.* Each mechanism bead owns its own script and this script runs it as a stage: the
      own scripts of the mechanism beads this bead depends on, `foundry_campaign.sh` (N31a-06),
      `foundry_impact_loads.sh` (N31a-12) and `foundry_declared_loads.sh` (N31a-13), are run as child scripts,
      each writing its own log. One `assert` line per stage records its exit code and the pass and fail
      counts of its `summary` line, with the path of its log; a failing stage fails this script. The steps
      below are the verb-level measurements of the gate and are not replaced by the stages.
   2. *Campaign on the referee.* `frob foundry loads quad-ref --campaign camp-small --tier referee --key <k>
      --json`. Assert exit 0 or 2; T2 coverage 100% of the listed channels; `free_body.max_rel ≤ 1e-6` per
      link; dropped share ≤ 0.001. Metrics: residual quantiles per link, H_c, storage bytes against the
      estimate.
   3. *Campaign in single precision.* The same with `--tier batch-cpu --precision f32` (runnable on every
      host; `batch-cpu` runs `f64` unless `--precision f32` is given, and the residual tolerance follows the
      precision the run's evidence records, not the tier's name), and with `--tier gpu-metal` on the M5 Ultra
      or `--tier gpu-cuda` where present (both `f32`). Assert `max_rel ≤ 1e-3` and that each evidence block
      records `f32`; a GPU tier absent from the host is logged as `not-measured` with its reason.
   4. *Superposition.* Assert `superposition.samples == 1000` and `superposition.max_rel ≤ 1e-9` on the
      referee run.
   5. *Envelope and replay.* Assert at most 2n cases per link class with n = 18 or 24; pick three cases and
      run `frob sim replay --run <r> --world <w> --episode <e> --to <tick> --capture digests --key <k> --json`
      (the replay address each case stores maps onto these fields); assert digest equality with each case's
      `trace_digest`.
   6. *Assembled-peak mutation.* `frob foundry loads --fixture span-two-instants --key <k> --json`: assert two cases,
      each with mid-span moment P·L/6. Write a load file with per-channel peaks (the helper
      `mutate assemble-peaks`), feed it to `frob foundry size --fixture span-two-instants --loads <file>
      --json`: assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-167`, and that the payload names two different ticks.
   7. *Equal-limb mutation.* On `quad-ref-offset` (`frob foundry loads quad-ref-offset --campaign camp-small
      --key <k> --json`): assert the reported left-to-right peak ratio for the lower-leg class exceeds 1.05
      and the governing vertical-force case comes from a left leg. Restrict the load set to one leg
      (`mutate one-limb`), feed it to `frob foundry size quad-ref-offset --loads <file> --json`: assert exit 3,
      `FROB-REF-169`, naming the three missing limbs.
   8. *Telemetry gap.* Copy the run's store to a scratch directory, delete one T2 chunk, re-read the campaign
      from the copy (`foundry.loads` in read mode on the stored campaign id, with the scratch store and its
      own key): assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-169`, the world and tick interval in the payload, the re-run command
      as first repair.
   9. *Variants and separation.* `--variants all` on the drop task: assert four variant runs, a movement
      figure per direction, and a decision on `model-conditional` for the foot class. On `soft-link`: assert
      exit 3 and `FROB-REF-262` without `--transient impact-windows`, and completion with it.
   10. *Declared loads.* `frob foundry loads quad-ref --declared statics --key <k> --json`: assert every case
       passes equilibrium at 10⁻⁹, the label `loads: declared`, the factor and its source; then
       `frob foundry size quad-ref --loads <file> --json`: assert `fatigue: "no-claim"`.
   10b. *The KILL-LOADS switch (N31a-13).* In a scratch configuration with the KILL-LOADS switch set,
       `frob foundry loads quad-ref --campaign camp-small --key <k> --json`: assert exit 5, `FROB-REF-724`, and
       `--declared statics` as the first offered repair; then the same request with `--declared statics`
       completes. Log the envelope's code and repair.
   11. *Determinism lanes.* Repeat step 2 twice under two distinct keys (a repeat under the same key would
       return the stored receipt; `idempotent_replay: false` is logged): identical trace digests, case files
       and spectra hashes; repeat with a different worker count: identical.
   12. Write the gate receipt; the value is recorded with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
       record_gate -- --gate GATE-FOUNDRY-02 --receipt <hash>` by a session that did not implement the load
       extraction (N38b-19); `summary`.
3. **Gate record fields.** `free_body_max_rel_referee`, `free_body_max_rel_f32`, `superposition_max_rel`,
   `assembled_peak_refused` (true/false), `equal_limb_refused` (true/false), commit, stack-lock digest,
   hardware fingerprint, seed. The KILL-LOADS decision reads this record and the count of repair cycles.
4. **Self-test** (`scripts/e2e/tests/foundry_loads_selftest.sh`): runs the script against builds with each
   registered mutant (the `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch its mechanism bead writes, with its row in
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` by the field set of N31-CX, built with `--features
   gauntlet-mutations,frob-foundry/nc-<nnnn>`, never a release build) and requires a non-zero exit at the
   named step: `forget-gravity` → step 2;
   `drop-omega-term` → step 2; `swap-two-product-columns` → step 4; `assemble-peaks` accepted by the reader →
   step 6; `assume-equal-limbs` → step 7; `pad-gap-with-last-value` → step 8; `include-mean-in-power` → step 9.
5. **Logging.** The script sources the shared helper of the end-to-end harness and writes
   `target/e2e-logs/foundry_loads/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`,
   `script`, `bead` (`N31a-53`), `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the
   exact command line, exit code, wall time, and the envelope's `evidence` block (claim, class, tier,
   determinism, identities, seed). Metrics carry value, unit and threshold. Digest comparisons log both
   digests. On failure: the first differing value, the link, world address and tick of the worst sample, and
   the paths of the run store and case files needed to reproduce. Final `summary`: assertions passed and
   failed; `gates: ["GATE-FOUNDRY-02"]`. Campaign traces are private-scene data when the scene is private:
   only identities, counts and digests are logged.
6. **Idempotence and seeds.** Every campaign is identified by its specification hash and addresses. Each run
   of the script has its own state root (STD-E2E), so a re-run computes again; it sends the same keys, because
   a `foundry_key` label carries no run identifier, which is what lets the logs of two runs be compared line
   for line; scratch directories are removed at `end`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a conforming build and writes the NDJSON log, the transcript and the gate record with measured values.
- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-02's five clauses are each asserted by a named step (2, 3, 4, 6, 7) with the thresholds 10⁻⁶, 10⁻³ and 10⁻⁹.
- [ ] Steps 6 to 9 assert the refusal codes FROB-REF-167, -169 and -262 with their pointers and first repairs; step 10b asserts FROB-REF-724 (exit 5) with `--declared statics` offered under the KILL-LOADS switch.
- [ ] Every `foundry.loads` and `sim.replay` call carries `--key`, compared runs carry distinct keys, and every option used appears in its verb's registry row (KNOWN_DRIFTS 8; the rows of `foundry.loads` and `foundry.size` in VERBS-foundry and of `sim.replay` in VERBS-sim list them).
- [ ] The single-precision clause is measured with `--precision f32` on `batch-cpu` and on any GPU tier present, and each run's evidence records `f32`.
- [ ] The self-test fails at the named step for each of the seven mutants.
- [ ] Two consecutive runs produce identical measured values and digests (the determinism lane), on one machine.
- [ ] No log line contains scene geometry or trace samples.
- [ ] The script's `summary` line names the gate, and the KILL-LOADS decision bead can be closed by citing the record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The script's helpers have their own tests under `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `mutate_assemble_peaks_produces_no_real_instant`
(the mutated file's case matches no tick); `mutate_one_limb_drops_three_instances`; `gate_record_schema_valid`;
`log_lines_have_required_fields` (a run's NDJSON is validated against the harness schema).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_loads.sh`, as specified above; logs under `target/e2e-logs/foundry_loads/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-740 (assembled peaks: P·L/6 per instant; assembled P·L/3 or 0; the assembling mutation is refused). KA-754
(amplification at most 1.042 at a ratio of five), asserted in step 9's metrics. CX-336 and CX-337 are the two
shortcuts refused in steps 6 and 7. The offset-payload statics (left feet 44.95 N, right 38.41 N) as a sanity
metric of the fixture. Negative controls: the seven mutants. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; across
worker counts; referee against single-precision tier each within its own threshold.

## Considerations

- The referee campaign is slow; `camp-small` is sized to finish in minutes on the CPU. The full reference
  campaign of the stage-2 pilot is run by the structure-loop script, not here.
- If the gate fails, the script's log is the evidence for the one permitted repair cycle; a second failure
  triggers KILL-LOADS and step 10 becomes the capability.
- Steps that need the `structure` group are skipped with a logged reason when the binary lacks it, and the
  gate is then recorded as not measured, never as passed.
- The `foundry.loads` options this script uses (`--tier`, `--precision f32|f64`, `--fixture`, `--variants`,
  `--transient impact-windows`, `--declared statics`) are defined by the mechanism beads BD-400, N31a-12 and
  N31a-13, and `foundry.size --loads <file>` by BD-401; the rows of VERBS-foundry list each of them with the
  bead that fixes its spelling, and the `foundry.loads` row states that the appended FROB-REF-724 (exit 5) is
  the KILL-LOADS answer with `--declared statics` as its repair, which step 10b asserts.
