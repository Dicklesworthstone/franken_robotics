## Background

Three gates are measured on the running structure loop, the body of the verb `foundry.optimize` (topology
optimization of link classes with the loads–mass loop):

- GATE-FOUNDRY-04 (Known answer, M5): two-bar compliance within 10% and bar angle within 5°; the extracted
  surface meshes and passes the mesh audit on 20 of 20 fixtures.
- GATE-FOUNDRY-05 (Design comparison, M5): the stage-2 pilot: saving at least 7% (the kill threshold, as in
  the generative-structure bet) with 15% the target; the convergence test passes within five iterations;
  coarse solve at most 120 s per link class.
- GATE-FOUNDRY-06 (Known answer, M5): planted-gain loop: fixed point within 10⁻⁶, λ̂ within 0.01; divergence
  and cycling refused.

KILL-TOPOPT hangs on the second: if GATE-FOUNDRY-05 fails (saving under 7%, no convergence, or over budget),
beam-rung sizing supplies mass and inertia feedback, three-dimensional optimization becomes an offline option
or is dropped, and the structural-margin analysis is unaffected.

The saving is always against the engineered baseline (the beam-rung section sized to the same envelope
cases), and is printed after its ceiling: link structure is about 27% of robot mass on the one quadruped with
a published breakdown, and reported link savings are 7 to 30%, so at most about 8% of robot mass. This script
is where that discipline is checked from the outside.

## Scope

In: the script, fixtures, assertions, logs, three gate records, the self-test.
Out: the mechanisms; the pilot's registration and verdict and the kill-gate decision (unit U31b); the
independent check's own script (N31a-55), which this one calls for two steps.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures.** `planted-gain`, `planted-gain-divergent`, `planted-two-size` (`synthetic` rungs exposing the
   linear loads–mass law S = A + λ·(M₀ + S)); `twobar` (the slab fixture: L = 0.10 m, P = 1,000 N, 12% volume);
   `quad-ref` qualified, with campaign `camp-pilot` (the qualified task set plus push recovery and drops;
   1,000 episodes; fixed addresses); `quad-unqualified`; `shank-blocked` (a knee actuator keep-out severing
   the shank region); `shelf-slm`; `thin-web-cnc`; `bracket-plate`; `knee-torque-step` and
   `knee-torque-nostep` (the two outer-step fixtures).
2. **Steps.**
   1. `start` and the cluster preamble.
   2. *Planted gain (GATE-FOUNDRY-06).* `frob foundry optimize --fixture planted-gain --json`: assert iterates
      3.0, 2.85, 2.8275, 2.824125; `fixed_point` within 10⁻⁶ of 2.8235294 kg; every λ̂ within 0.01 of 0.15;
      outcome `converged` within five iterations.
   3. *Divergence and cycling (GATE-FOUNDRY-06).* The two mutant fixtures: assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-260`,
      reasons `diverged` (within three iterations) and `cycling` (after one under-relaxed step), the beam
      rung as first repair.
   4. *Two-bar (GATE-FOUNDRY-04).* `frob foundry optimize --fixture twobar --rung topology --exploratory
      --json`, then `frob foundry check` on the extracted part: assert both bar angles within 45° ± 5° and
      compliance within 10% of 4·P²·L²/(E·V) at the extracted volume, for both drivers.
   5. *Extraction fixtures (GATE-FOUNDRY-04).* Run the battery lane (`cargo test … topology_battery`) and
      parse its report: assert 20 of 20 meshed and audited on the body-fitted path; log each fixture's
      conformity numbers.
   6. *Admission.* `quad-unqualified`: assert exit 3 and `FROB-REF-166`; with `--exploratory` assert
      completion and the label; then `frob foundry export --packet` on the result: assert the refusal.
   7. *Region refusal.* `shank-blocked`: assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-164`, first repair naming the actuator
      housing.
   8. *Stage-2 comparison (GATE-FOUNDRY-05).* `frob foundry optimize quad-ref --campaign camp-pilot --rung
      topology --class lower-leg --json` with `sync_wait_ms` short (assert exit 8), then `job.wait`. Assert:
      the ceiling line precedes the saving; `saving.link_mass_fraction ≥ 0.07` against the beam baseline at
      equal first bending frequency (within 2%) and fatigue utilization no higher than the baseline's;
      outcome `converged` within five iterations with mass change between iterations four and five at most
      2%; coarse solve time per link class ≤ 120 s. Metrics: saving (with 0.15 as the target), robot-mass
      saving against the ceiling, per-iteration x_k, Δ_k, λ̂_k, bound, envelope movement, stage timings.
   9. *Process constraints.* `shelf-slm`: assert zero violating overhang area or the label
      `overhang: post-check only` with a recorded re-run. `thin-web-cnc`: assert one re-run with a tightened
      length scale. `bracket-plate --plate`: assert the plate label and three-dimensional check results.
   10. *Outer step.* `--outer actuators` on the two torque fixtures: assert multiplier greater than 1 with
       the step listed, and exactly 1 with `no-step-available`.
   11. *Resume and cancel.* Kill the job of step 8 after its third stage (`FROB_TEST_KILL_AT`), resume, and
       assert the same final record hash as the uninterrupted run; cancel a fresh job mid-campaign and assert
       no new design in the lineage.
   12. *Determinism lanes.* Steps 2 and 4 twice: identical records; step 2 in debug and release builds:
       identical (it is exact arithmetic).
   13. Write three gate records; `summary`.
3. **Gate records.** GATE-FOUNDRY-04: both angles, both compliance ratios, 20-fixture table. GATE-FOUNDRY-05:
   saving against the beam baseline with the baseline's identity, frequency and fatigue equality checks,
   iterations to convergence, mass change four to five, coarse time per class, and a field for each of the
   three kill triggers (`saving_under_7_percent`, `no_convergence`, `over_budget`). GATE-FOUNDRY-06: fixed
   point error, λ̂ errors, the two refusals. Each with commit, stack-lock digest, hardware fingerprint, seed.
4. **Self-test** (`scripts/e2e/tests/foundry_structure_loop_selftest.sh`): mutants and the step that must
   fail: `stop-after-five-and-report` → 3; `one-iteration-under-2-percent-suffices` → 2;
   `fresh-worlds-each-iteration` → 8; `twobar-swap-support-and-load` → 4; `extract-threshold-0.4` → 5;
   `saving-against-primitive-baseline` → 8 (the formatter refuses); `admit-any-verdict` → 6;
   `connectivity-without-erosion` → 7.
5. **Logging.** Shared helper; `target/e2e-logs/foundry_structure_loop/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a
   transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31a-54`), `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall
   time, the envelope's `evidence` block. Metrics with unit and threshold. On failure: the first differing
   value (for the planted loop, the first iterate that differs; for the comparison, which of the three
   triggers), and the paths of the loop record, the candidate geometry and the meshes. `summary`: assertions
   passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-FOUNDRY-04", "GATE-FOUNDRY-05", "GATE-FOUNDRY-06"]`.
6. **Runtime.** Steps 2 to 7 and 9 to 12 run in minutes. Step 8 is long (a 1,000-episode campaign per
   iteration, four contact variants, up to five iterations): it is tagged `gate-lane`, runs when the gate is
   recorded, and in routine runs is replaced by `camp-small` with the thresholds asserted only as recorded
   metrics.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a conforming build and writes its log, transcript and three gate records with measured values.
- [ ] Each clause of GATE-FOUNDRY-04, -05 and -06 is asserted by a named step with its threshold (5°, 10%, 20 of 20; 7% with 15% as target, five iterations, 120 s; 10⁻⁶, 0.01, two refusals).
- [ ] The ceiling line precedes every saving in the transcript, and the saving's baseline is a beam-rung record.
- [ ] Refusals FROB-REF-166, -164 and -260 are asserted with pointers and first repairs.
- [ ] The self-test fails at the named step for each of the eight mutants.
- [ ] Resume after a kill gives the same record hash; cancellation leaves no new design.
- [ ] The GATE-FOUNDRY-05 record carries the three kill-trigger fields, so the KILL-TOPOPT decision can cite it.

## Considerations

- A saving between 7% and 15% passes the gate and misses the target; the record says which, and the bet's
  wording follows the number.
- The comparison is at equal first bending frequency and equal fatigue utilization; on printed fixtures the
  fatigue leg reads `no-claim` until stress-life data exists, and the record then says the comparison was at
  equal frequency and stress only.
- If the kill fires, steps 4, 5, 8 and 9 are retired with the rung and the script's remaining steps (beam rung
  feedback through the same loop) are the capability's test.
