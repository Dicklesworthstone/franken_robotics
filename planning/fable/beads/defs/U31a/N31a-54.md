## Background

Three gates concern the running structure loop, the body of the verb `foundry.optimize` (topology
optimization of link classes with the loads–mass loop). This script measures the first and the third and
exercises the second on one campaign:

- GATE-FOUNDRY-04 (Known answer, M5): two-bar compliance within 10% and bar angle within 5°; the extracted
  surface meshes and passes the mesh audit on 20 of 20 fixtures.
- GATE-FOUNDRY-05 (Design comparison, M5): the stage-2 pilot: saving at least 7% (the kill threshold, as in
  the generative-structure bet) with 15% the target; the convergence test passes within five iterations;
  coarse solve at most 120 s per link class.
- GATE-FOUNDRY-06 (Known answer, M5): planted-gain loop: fixed point within 10⁻⁶, λ̂ within 0.01; divergence
  and cycling refused.

KILL-TOPOPT hangs on the second: if GATE-FOUNDRY-05 fails (saving under 7%, no convergence, or over budget),
beam-rung sizing supplies mass and inertia feedback, three-dimensional optimization becomes an offline option
or is dropped, and the structural-margin analysis is unaffected.

The saving is always against the engineered baseline (the beam-rung section sized to the same envelope
cases), and is printed after its ceiling: link structure is about 27% of robot mass on the one quadruped with
a published breakdown, and reported link savings are 7 to 30%, so at most about 8% of robot mass. This script
is where that discipline is checked from the outside.

Who records GATE-FOUNDRY-05. It is a design-comparison gate, and the gate register admits such a result only
as a registered method experiment (a ceiling first, competing arms, potency and A/A controls, a paired lower
bound, a pre-declared fallback, a reachability pre-analysis). That measurement is bead N31b-13: five
campaigns on disjoint address sets and a one-sided lower bound on the mean saving. This script runs the same
comparison once, on one campaign, as the functional test of the verb's loop: it asserts that the loop runs,
converges, pairs its campaigns and reports a saving against the right baseline after its ceiling, and it
logs the saving, the iteration count and the coarse-solve time as metrics beside their thresholds. It does
not write the GATE-FOUNDRY-05 row, and a single-campaign saving above 7% is not the gate. N31b-13 depends on
this bead.

## Scope

In: the script of the verb `foundry.optimize`; fixtures; assertions; logs; the gate records of GATE-FOUNDRY-04
and GATE-FOUNDRY-06; the single-campaign loop record on the stage-2 fixture (step 8); the self-test.
Out: the mechanisms; the registered measurement of GATE-FOUNDRY-05 with its gate row and the three
kill-trigger fields (N31b-13, which depends on this bead); the pilot's registration and verdict (N31b-06,
N31b-07); the KILL-TOPOPT decision (N31b-15), which reads N31b-13's row; the independent check's own script
(N31a-55), which this one calls for two steps.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures.** `planted-gain`, `planted-gain-divergent`, `planted-two-size` (`synthetic` rungs exposing the
   linear loads–mass law S = A + λ·(M₀ + S)); `twobar` (the slab fixture: L = 0.10 m, P = 1,000 N, 12% volume);
   `quad-ref` qualified, with campaign `camp-pilot` (the qualified task set plus push recovery and drops;
   1,000 episodes; fixed addresses); `quad-unqualified`; `shank-blocked` (a knee actuator keep-out severing
   the shank region); `shelf-slm`; `thin-web-cnc`; `bracket-plate`; `knee-torque-step` and
   `knee-torque-nostep` (the two outer-step fixtures).
2. **Steps.** Every call of a `run` verb below (`optimize`, `check`, `export`) carries the idempotency key the
   cluster preamble issues for it (`--key "$(foundry_key <label>)"`, its name for the harness's `e2e_key`, one label per call; N31a-51 step 9),
   omitted here for brevity; a call without one is refused with FROB-REF-805.
   1. `start` and the cluster preamble.
   1b. *Mechanism stages.* Each mechanism bead owns its own script and this script runs it as a stage: the
      own scripts of the mechanism beads this bead depends on, `foundry_ceiling.sh` (N31a-02),
      `foundry_admission.sh` (N31a-03), `foundry_loop_machine.sh` (N31a-05), `foundry_topology_rung.sh`
      (N31a-15), `foundry_plate.sh` (N31a-16), `foundry_extraction.sh` (N31a-17), `foundry_topology_battery.sh`
      (N31a-18), `foundry_mass_feedback.sh` (N31a-23), `foundry_convergence.sh` (N31a-24),
      `foundry_outer_actuators.sh` (N31a-25), `foundry_process_constraints.sh` (N31a-29) and
      `foundry_beam_rung.sh` (N31a-60, with N31a-61's part 2), are run as child
      scripts, each writing its own log. One `assert` line per stage records its exit code and the pass and
      fail counts of its `summary` line, with the path of its log; a failing stage fails this script. The
      steps below are the verb-level measurements and are not replaced by the stages.
   2. *Planted gain (GATE-FOUNDRY-06).* `frob foundry optimize --fixture planted-gain --loop --json`: assert iterates
      3.0, 2.85, 2.8275, 2.824125 (the run's four; KA-743's fifth value, 2.82361875, is the map's and is not
      computed by a run that converges at iteration 4) and `aitken_used: false` (N31a-24's proposal policy
      evaluates no proposal on this fixture); `fixed_point` within 10⁻⁶ of 2.8235294 kg; every λ̂ within 0.01
      of 0.15; outcome `converged` at iteration 4, within five.
   3. *Divergence and cycling (GATE-FOUNDRY-06).* The two mutant fixtures, with `--loop`: assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-260`,
      reasons `diverged` (within three iterations) and `cycling` (after one under-relaxed step), the beam
      rung as first repair.
   4. *Two-bar (GATE-FOUNDRY-04).* `frob foundry optimize --fixture twobar --rung topology --exploratory
      --json`, then `frob foundry check` on the extracted part: assert both bar angles within 45° ± 5° and
      compliance within 10% of 4·P²·L²/(E·V) at the extracted volume, for both drivers.
   5. *Extraction fixtures (GATE-FOUNDRY-04).* Run the battery of N31a-18 as its Gauntlet row,
      `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-FOUNDRY-04`, and
      again with `--features gauntlet-mutations` for its four planted failures, and parse its receipt: assert
      20 of 20 meshed and audited on the body-fitted path, within the conformity limits of N31b-24's record
      (Hausdorff distance at most 0.05 of the minimum length scale, volume within 0.1%), no fixture on the
      `same-family` fallback, and each planted failure `caught` in the same run; log each fixture's conformity
      numbers.
   6. *Admission.* `frob foundry optimize quad-unqualified --links lower-leg --json`: assert exit 3 and
      `FROB-REF-166`; with `--exploratory` assert completion, the `exploratory` label on the result and on
      its loop record, and log the result design's identity. The packet-side consequence (an exploratory
      result cannot enter a packet: `foundry.export` refuses it with FROB-REF-166 and writes no directory) is
      asserted by the packet bead BD-408, whose script is given this step's result as a fixture; this bead
      does not depend on the packet writer and does not call `foundry.export`.
   7. *Region refusal.* `shank-blocked`: assert exit 3, `FROB-REF-164`, first repair naming the actuator
      housing.
   8. *Stage-2 comparison, one campaign (the clauses of GATE-FOUNDRY-05 as metrics).* `frob foundry optimize
      quad-ref --campaign camp-pilot --rung topology --links lower-leg --loop --json` with `sync_wait_ms` short
      (assert exit 8), then `job.wait`. Assert the functional properties: the ceiling line precedes the
      saving; the saving is stated against the beam baseline's continuous-wall reference and is counted only
      under the baseline bead's equal-margin rule (first bending frequency at least 0.98 of the baseline's,
      fatigue utilization no higher than the baseline's; otherwise `not-counted` with its reason); every
      iteration re-uses the campaign's world addresses; the outcome is `converged` or a typed refusal. Record
      as metrics, each beside its gate threshold and without failing the script on the threshold:
      `saving.link_mass_fraction` (0.07, with 0.15 as the target), iterations to convergence (five), mass
      change between iterations four and five (2%), coarse solve time per link class (120 s); also the
      robot-mass saving against the ceiling, per-iteration x_k, Δ_k, λ̂_k, bound, envelope movement and stage
      timings. The registered five-pair measurement that decides the gate is N31b-13.
   9. *Process constraints.* `shelf-slm`: assert zero violating overhang area or the label
      `overhang: post-check only` with a recorded re-run. `thin-web-cnc`: assert one re-run with a tightened
      length scale. `bracket-plate --plate`: assert the plate label and three-dimensional check results.
   10. *Outer step (N31a-25).* `frob foundry optimize --fixture knee-torque-step --loop --outer actuators
       --json` and the same with `--fixture knee-torque-nostep` (each its own key): assert multiplier greater
       than 1 with the step listed, and exactly 1 with `no-step-available`.
   10b. *The loop on the beam rung (the KILL-TOPOPT fallback; N31a-61 step 11).* `frob foundry optimize
       quad-ref --campaign camp-small --rung beam --loop --json`: assert one `frob.foundry.beam/1` record per
       beam-like class (stock section, continuous wall no heavier, fatigue outcome), the result naming the
       rung, and a loop outcome of `converged` or `FROB-REF-260` with reason `cycling` (stock steps make the
       map piecewise constant); then the same request in a binary built without the topology rung, or with
       the KILL-TOPOPT switch set and no `--rung`: the loop runs on the beam rung and says so.
   11. *Resume and cancel.* Kill the job of step 8 after its third stage (`FROB_TEST_KILL_AT`), resume, and
       assert the same final record hash as the uninterrupted run; cancel a fresh job mid-campaign and assert
       no new design in the lineage.
   12. *Determinism lanes.* Steps 2 and 4 twice, under two distinct keys (the same key would return the first
       run's stored receipt): identical records; step 2 in debug and release builds: identical (it is exact
       arithmetic).
   13. Write the two gate receipts and the loop record of step 8; `summary`. The gate values are recorded by
       a session that did not implement the mechanism (N38b-19), with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
       record_gate -- --gate GATE-FOUNDRY-04 --receipt <hash>` (from this script's step 4 receipt together
       with N31a-18's battery receipt; N31b-12 names this script for the row) and `--gate GATE-FOUNDRY-06
       --receipt <hash>`.
3. **Gate receipts.** GATE-FOUNDRY-04: both angles, both compliance ratios, 20-fixture table (from the battery
   receipt, cited by its hash). GATE-FOUNDRY-06: fixed point error, λ̂ errors, the two refusals. Each with
   commit, stack-lock digest, hardware fingerprint, seed. **Loop record of step 8** (not a gate row): saving against the beam baseline with the baseline's
   identity, the frequency and fatigue equal-margin checks, iterations to convergence, mass change four to
   five, coarse time per class. The GATE-FOUNDRY-05 row, with a field for each of the three kill triggers
   (`saving_under_7_percent`, `no_convergence`, `over_budget`), is written by N31b-13 from its five pairs.
4. **Self-test** (`scripts/e2e/tests/foundry_structure_loop_selftest.sh`): runs the script on builds with
   each mutant (a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch of its mechanism bead with its row in
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml`, built with `--features
   gauntlet-mutations,frob-foundry/nc-<nnnn>`, never a release build). Mutants and the step that must
   fail: `stop-after-five-and-report` → 3; `one-iteration-under-2-percent-suffices` → 2;
   `fresh-worlds-each-iteration` → 8; `twobar-swap-support-and-load` → 4; `extract-threshold-0.4` → 5;
   `saving-against-primitive-baseline` → 8 (the formatter refuses); `admit-any-verdict` → 6;
   `connectivity-without-erosion` → 7.
5. **Logging.** Shared helper; `target/e2e-logs/foundry_structure_loop/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a
   transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31a-54`), `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall
   time, the envelope's `evidence` block. Metrics with unit and threshold. On failure: the first differing
   value (for the planted loop, the first iterate that differs; for the comparison, which of the three
   triggers), and the paths of the loop record, the candidate geometry and the meshes. `summary`: assertions
   passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-FOUNDRY-04", "GATE-FOUNDRY-06"]`.
6. **Runtime.** Steps 2 to 7 and 9 to 12 (with 10b) run in minutes. Step 8 is long (a 1,000-episode campaign per
   iteration, four contact variants, up to five iterations): it is tagged `gate-lane`, runs before the
   registered measurement of N31b-13, and in routine runs is replaced by `camp-small`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a conforming build and writes its log, transcript, the two gate receipts and the loop record of step 8 with measured values; GATE-FOUNDRY-04 and -06 are recorded with `record_gate` from those receipts (and, for -04, N31a-18's battery receipt) by a session that did not implement the mechanisms.
- [ ] Every `run` call carries `--key`, runs a step compares carry distinct keys, and every option used appears in the verb's row of VERBS-foundry (`--fixture`, `--loop`, `--rung`, `--outer actuators`, `--plate`, `--links`, `--campaign`, `--exploratory` on `foundry.optimize` are all in the row).
- [ ] Each clause of GATE-FOUNDRY-04 and -06 is asserted by a named step with its threshold (5°, 10%, 20 of 20; 10⁻⁶, 0.01, two refusals); the three clauses of GATE-FOUNDRY-05 are logged as metrics beside their thresholds (7% with 15% as target, five iterations, 120 s) and are decided by N31b-13.
- [ ] The ceiling line precedes every saving in the transcript, and the saving's baseline is a beam-rung record.
- [ ] Refusals FROB-REF-166, -164 and -260 are asserted with pointers and first repairs.
- [ ] The self-test fails at the named step for each of the eight mutants.
- [ ] Resume after a kill gives the same record hash; cancellation leaves no new design.
- [ ] The loop runs on the beam rung when asked and when the topology rung is unavailable, and the result names the rung (step 10b).
- [ ] The loop record of step 8 is in the form N31b-13 reads (baseline identity, equal-margin checks, iterations, coarse time), and this script writes no GATE-FOUNDRY-05 row.

### Known answers and negative controls

first four; fixed point 2.8235 kg; λ̂ = 0.15; controls: λ = 1.1

twice on one machine; debug and release for the exact fixture; across worker counts for the campaign.

## Considerations

- A saving between 7% and 15% would pass the gate and miss the target; the loop record prints both
  thresholds beside the number, and the verdict and the bet's wording follow N31b-13's registered bound.
- The comparison is at equal first bending frequency and equal fatigue utilization; on printed fixtures the
  fatigue leg reads `no-claim` until stress-life data exists, and the record then says the comparison was at
  equal frequency and stress only.
- If the kill fires, steps 4, 5, 8 and 9 are retired with the rung and the script's remaining steps (beam rung
  feedback through the same loop) are the capability's test.
