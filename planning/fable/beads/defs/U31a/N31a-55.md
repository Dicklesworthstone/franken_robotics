## Background

The verb `foundry.check` is Foundry's independent check: stress, buckling, modal, fatigue and
manufacturability evaluation of a geometry, on a body-fitted mesh that no optimizer produced. Three gates are
measured on it:

- GATE-FOUNDRY-01 (Known answer; required at M5, recordable at M4): cantilever deflection within 2% and stress
  within 5% after refinement; the planted overstress located with utilization 1.25 ± 5%.
- GATE-FOUNDRY-03 (Known answer; M5, recordable at M4): fatigue block and rainflow sequence exact to 10⁻¹²
  relative; the window-split mutation reproduces its 10.7% and is caught.
- GATE-FOUNDRY-07 (Known answer; M5): planted violations of wall, corner radius, tool access, overhang and
  undercut each detected with location and repair; clean parts pass; 20 of 20.

KILL-FAB is triggered if the third fails: manufacturability findings become advisory lints and a packet
records no manufacturability pass.

GATE-FOUNDRY-01 is also carried by the beam-rung bead's own script for the closed forms and the
finite-element form of the structural-margin analysis; here the same fixtures go through the check as it is
applied to arbitrary geometry, so the two paths anchor each other.

## Scope

In: the script, fixtures, assertions, logs, three gate records, the self-test.
Out: the mechanisms; the beam rung's script (`scripts/e2e/foundry_size.sh`, seed bead BD-401); the packet
pre-review steps (unit U31b); the kill-gate decision (unit U31b).

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures.** `cantilever` (L = 0.20 m, section 20 × 10 mm, E = 69 GPa, ν = 0.33, yield 240 MPa, tip load
   50 N); `cantilever-thinned` (root section 4 mm deep; 80 N); `euler-tube` (pinned tube, mean radius 10 mm,
   wall 1 mm, length 0.5 m); `l-bracket-sharp` and `l-bracket-filleted`; `neck-optimized` (an optimized part
   with a thin neck, provenance `optimized`); `fatigue-block`; `rainflow-sequence`; `printed-link` (process
   `slm`); the twenty manufacturability fixtures of the battery; cards `al6061-t6` with and without its yield
   field, and a rates card.
2. **Steps.**
   1. `start`; cluster preamble; assert that every result's stack-lock digest and airlock mode equal those of
      `frob doctor --json`.
   2. *Cantilever (GATE-FOUNDRY-01).* `frob foundry check --fixture cantilever --json`: assert tip deflection
      within 2% of 1.159 mm and root bending stress within 5% of 30 MPa after refinement; the refinement
      history ends with a peak change under 5%; mesh hash present. Then `--load 25`: both halve.
   3. *Planted overstress (GATE-FOUNDRY-01).* `--fixture cantilever-thinned --load 80`: assert stress within
      5% of 300 MPa, utilization 1.25 ± 5%, location `root`, exit 2; the unthinned beam at 80 N: utilization
      0.20, exit 0.
   4. *Buckling and modes.* `--fixture euler-tube --load 4000`: λ₁ within 3% of 2.14, pass; `--load 5000`:
      1.71, finding. `--fixture cantilever --modes`: f₁ within 3% of 204 Hz.
   5. *Singular corner and optimized failure.* `l-bracket-sharp`: `singular: fillet required` with a radius;
      `l-bracket-filleted`: converges. `neck-optimized`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-261`, three ranked repairs.
   6. *Fatigue block (GATE-FOUNDRY-03).* `--fixture fatigue-block --json`: damage per block equals 0.116 with
      relative error at most 10⁻¹²; blocks to a sum of one 8.62.
   7. *Rainflow sequence (GATE-FOUNDRY-03).* `--fixture rainflow-sequence`: ranges 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 with counts
      0.5, 1.5, 0.5, 1.0, 0.5; damage 8.752 × 10⁻⁶ at 10⁻¹² relative. The same sequence as a campaign of two
      episodes split after the fifth point: identical damage (residue carried).
   8. *Window-split mutation (GATE-FOUNDRY-03).* Run the helper `mutate finish-per-episode` (a test-only
      counter mode that finishes each episode): assert damage 7.816 × 10⁻⁶, a deficit of 10.7% ± 0.1 point,
      and that the windowed-against-whole comparison reports the mismatch (exit non-zero from the comparison
      tool).
   9. *Cards.* The cantilever with the card stripped of `yield_Pa`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-163`, pointer
      `structure.yield_Pa`. `printed-link`: `fatigue: "no-claim"` with reason `process`; with
      `--require fatigue`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-163`.
   10. *Manufacturability battery (GATE-FOUNDRY-07).* For each of the twenty fixtures:
       `frob foundry check --fixture <name> --fab --process <card> --json`; apply the matching rule (feature,
       location inside the planted region dilated by two grid cells, measured value within tolerance, repair
       target and difference); assert 20 of 20. Then at half grid spacing: 20 of 20.
   11. *Cost fields.* A clean bracket with and without the rates card: cost and carbon absent, then present
       with relative uncertainty.
   12. *Determinism lanes.* Steps 2, 6 and 7 twice: identical values and mesh hashes; steps 6 and 7 in debug
       and release: identical.
   13. Write three gate records; `summary`.
3. **Gate records.** GATE-FOUNDRY-01: deflection and stress errors, planted utilization and location,
   refinement levels. GATE-FOUNDRY-03: the two damages with relative errors, the split value and its deficit,
   `mutation_caught`. GATE-FOUNDRY-07: per fixture pass or fail with planted and measured values and location
   error; the total; per-kind detection counts (wall, corner radius, tool access, overhang, undercut, clean).
4. **Self-test** (`scripts/e2e/tests/foundry_check_selftest.sh`): mutants and the step that must fail:
   `utilization-against-ultimate-not-yield` → 3; `stop-refinement-after-level-0` → 2;
   `accept-smallest-absolute-eigenvalue` → 4; `finish-per-episode` in the production counter → 7;
   `amplitude-equals-range` → 6; `default-yield-when-missing` → 9; and each of the nine evaluator mutants of
   the manufacturability battery → 10.
5. **Logging.** Shared helper; `target/e2e-logs/foundry_check/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every
   line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31a-55`), `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall time, the
   envelope's `evidence` block. Metrics: `tip_deflection_mm` (1.159, 2%), `root_stress_mpa` (30, 5%),
   `utilization_planted` (1.25, 5%), `damage_block` (0.116, 10⁻¹²), `damage_sequence` (8.752 × 10⁻⁶, 10⁻¹²),
   `split_deficit_percent` (10.7), `fab_fixtures_passed` (20 of 20), one `assert` line per expected
   manufacturability finding with planted value, measured value, tolerance and location error. On failure:
   the first differing value and the paths of the mesh, the utilization field and the finding list.
   `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-FOUNDRY-01", "GATE-FOUNDRY-03",
   "GATE-FOUNDRY-07"]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a conforming build and writes its log, transcript and three gate records with measured values.
- [ ] Each clause of GATE-FOUNDRY-01, -03 and -07 is asserted by a named step with its threshold.
- [ ] The window-split mutation reproduces 7.816 × 10⁻⁶ (10.7% low) and is reported as caught.
- [ ] Refusals FROB-REF-163 and -261 are asserted with pointers and first repairs.
- [ ] The self-test fails at the named step for each of its fifteen mutants.
- [ ] The GATE-FOUNDRY-07 record carries per-kind detection counts, so the KILL-FAB decision can cite it.
- [ ] Without the `structure` group the structural steps are logged as not measured and the gates are not recorded as passed.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-737 (cantilever: 1.159 mm; 30 MPa; halving the load halves both). KA-738 (planted overstress: 300 MPa;
1.25 at the root; the unthinned beam passes). KA-741 (fatigue block: 0.116; 8.62 blocks). KA-742 and CX-338
(rainflow sequence: 8.752 × 10⁻⁶; split 7.816 × 10⁻⁶, 10.7% low). CX-339 (unsigned stress) through the typed
interface's unit test. Euler load 8,558 N and first mode 204 Hz as additional registered answers. Negative
controls: the fifteen mutants. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release for the exact
fatigue fixtures; both airlock modes for the cantilever.

## Considerations

- The fatigue exactness is about arithmetic: every fatigue number is still class Estimated, and the script
  asserts the class as well as the value.
- Finite-element steps depend on the sibling at the pinned commit; a pin move re-runs this script before
  anything else in Foundry.
- If KILL-FAB fires, step 10 remains as a regression test of the advisory lints and the gate is recorded as
  failed with the fallback's name.
