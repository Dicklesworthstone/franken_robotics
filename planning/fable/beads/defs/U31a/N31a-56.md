## Background

GATE-FOUNDRY-08 (claim: Known answer; milestone M5; TARGET): **the worked allocation to 10⁻⁹; the
zero-sensitivity and quadratic cases handled as specified; a correlated stack equals its hand value.**

The capability under test is the verb `foundry.tolerance` (kind `read`): sensitivity-driven allocation,
stacks and the band-extreme check. The worked allocation: two features with sensitivities 1 and 4, equal cost
coefficients, coverage factor k = 3 and a budget of √B = 10 µm receive 16.0 µm and 6.35 µm, where equal
tolerances meeting the same budget would be 7.28 µm each, and the allocation costs 19.9% less under the 1/t
cost model. "As specified" for the two awkward cases means: a feature with zero first-order sensitivity is
never loosened without limit; it is refused by the first-order allocator (FROB-REF-165) and routed to the
band-extreme check; for q(x) = x² at nominal zero that check fails at a loose tolerance and the feature
receives a worst-case box.

KILL-TOLERANCE is triggered if this gate fails, or if band-extreme checks fail on more than a fifth of
features in the pilot; the fallback is worst-case boxes per interface.

## Scope

In: the script, fixtures, assertions, logs, the gate record, the self-test.
Out: the mechanisms (N31a-31 to N31a-33; seed bead BD-406); the pilot and the kill-gate decision (unit U31b).

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures.** `worked-case` (two features, sensitivities 1 and 4, equal cost, k = 3, √B = 10 µm);
   `cost-1-and-8` (equal sensitivity, cost weights 1 and 8, statistical limit 0.1 mm); `correlated-pair`
   (a = (1, 1), σ = (3, 4) µm, correlation 0.5); `zero-sensitivity` (a three-feature stack whose third feature
   has sensitivity exactly zero at the nominal pose); `quadratic` (q = x²/L, L = 1 mm, margin 10 µm);
   `two-gap-clearance` (gaps 0.30 mm ± 0.10 and 0.35 mm ± 0.20); `two-link` (the planar arm with lengths 0.30
   and 0.25 m at 30° and 60°); `linkage-8` (the pilot's planar linkage with eight toleranced features);
   `unknown-sim-sensitivity` (a simulated metric whose sensitivity estimate has standard error above half
   its magnitude).
2. **Steps.**
   1. `start`; cluster preamble (the verb is `read`: assert it returns within the call and creates no job).
   2. *Worked allocation.* `frob foundry tolerance --fixture worked-case --json`: assert the two tolerances
      equal the closed form k·√B·(c_i/s_i²)^(1/3)/√(Σ (c_j·s_j)^(2/3)) to 10⁻⁹ relative (15.9904 µm and
      6.3458 µm; printed 16.0 and 6.35); budget met to 10⁻¹²; equal-tolerance value 7.276 µm; saving 19.9%
      with the words "under the 1/t cost model".
   3. *Second closed form.* `cost-1-and-8`: 0.0447 mm and 0.0894 mm, ratio 1 : 2.
   4. *Correlated stack.* `correlated-pair`: standard deviation 6.083 µm (variance 37 µm²); with correlation 0
      and 1: 5.000 and 7.000 µm.
   5. *Zero sensitivity.* `zero-sensitivity`: assert exit 2, the third feature listed with reason
      `zero-sensitivity` and action `worst-case-box`, a finite tolerance, and the other two allocated by the
      closed form against the full budget. With `--method statistical`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-165`, the feature
      named, first repair the worst-case-box command.
   6. *Quadratic.* `quadratic --method statistical`: exit 3 and `FROB-REF-165`. Without the method flag:
      exit 2; the band-extreme check at the process default tolerance (200 µm) recorded as failed (40 µm
      against a 10 µm margin); worst-case box 100 µm ± 1%.
   7. *Clearance.* `two-gap-clearance`: minimum clearance at the population extreme 0.15 mm, from the second
      gap.
   8. *Sensitivities and classes.* `two-link --qoi tool-position`: sensitivities 1, 1 and 0.25 m/rad with
      their directions, class Certified, governing pose recorded. `unknown-sim-sensitivity`: exit 3,
      `FROB-REF-165`, the number of extra episodes as a repair.
   9. *Pilot linkage.* `linkage-8`: assert the allocation meets its budget; run the held-out check
      `frob foundry tolerance --fixture linkage-8 --verify monte-carlo --samples 100000 --seed <s>` and log
      the coverage of the specification against its target; log `band_extreme_failed_share` against 0.2.
   10. *Report.* Assert `tolerances.json` written by `--out` validates, every feature has action, sensitivity
       and class, and the header carries the not-a-drawing statement and the four flags.
   11. *Determinism lanes.* Steps 2 to 4 twice and in debug and release: identical to the last bit for the
       rational parts and within the stated enclosure for cube roots.
   12. Write the gate record; `summary`.
3. **Gate record.** `worked_rel_err` (two values against 10⁻⁹), `zero_sensitivity_handled`,
   `quadratic_handled` (the three observations: refusal under the method flag, band-extreme failure, box
   value), `correlated_stack_value` against 6.083 µm, plus for the kill gate `pilot_coverage` and
   `band_extreme_failed_share`; commit, hardware fingerprint, seed.
4. **Self-test** (`scripts/e2e/tests/foundry_tolerance_selftest.sh`): mutants and failing steps:
   `exponent-one-half-for-statistical` → 2; `forget-coverage-factor` → 2 (5.33 µm and 2.12 µm);
   `ignore-declared-correlation` → 4; `loosen-zero-sensitivity-to-process-max` → 5;
   `band-check-on-linear-model` → 6; `clearance-by-variance-stack` → 7; `float-pass-labelled-certified` → 8.
5. **Logging.** Shared helper; `target/e2e-logs/foundry_tolerance/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript.
   Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31a-56`), `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall time, the
   envelope's `evidence` block. Metrics per feature: tolerance, sensitivity, class, cost, group, path taken;
   per quantity: budget, statistical stack, worst-case stack, slack; discrepancies at band extremes;
   enclosures and bisection counts. On failure: the first differing value with the closed-form reference and
   the path of the allocation record. `summary`: assertions passed and failed;
   `gates: ["GATE-FOUNDRY-08"]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a conforming build and writes its log, transcript and gate record with measured values.
- [ ] The three clauses of GATE-FOUNDRY-08 are asserted by steps 2, 5 and 6, and 4, with thresholds 10⁻⁹ and the hand values.
- [ ] FROB-REF-165 is asserted in its three variants (zero sensitivity under the statistical method, the quadratic case, an unknown simulated sensitivity) with pointers and first repairs.
- [ ] The self-test fails at the named step for each of the seven mutants.
- [ ] The record carries `pilot_coverage` and `band_extreme_failed_share`, so the KILL-TOLERANCE decision can cite it.
- [ ] Two consecutive runs give identical values.
- [ ] Every result's evidence block shows the weakest class among the sensitivities it used.

## Test plan

reference values agree with an independent exact-rational computation of the cube-root enclosure);
`monte_carlo_verifier_is_seeded` (the same seed gives the same coverage); `gate_record_schema_valid`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_tolerance.sh`, as specified; logs under `target/e2e-logs/foundry_tolerance/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-745 (worked case: 16.0 µm and 6.35 µm; 19.9% cheaper under the 1/t model; control: q = x² refused, then
fails the band-extreme check). KA-171 (cost weights 1 and 8: 0.0447 mm and 0.0894 mm). CX-340 (a zero
first-order sensitivity is not insensitivity). Hand values: correlated stack 6.083 µm; clearance 0.15 mm;
quadratic box 100 µm. Negative controls: the seven mutants. Determinism lanes: twice on one machine; debug and
release; across platforms for the rational fixtures.

## Considerations

- The held-out Monte Carlo of step 9 belongs to the pilot's falsifier; it is run here so that the number is
  re-measured whenever the allocation code changes, and is logged as a metric rather than a gate clause.
- The saving figure is under an assumed cost model; the script asserts the qualifying words are present, since
  a bare "19.9% cheaper" would overstate what is known.
- If the kill fires, steps 5 to 7 become the capability (worst-case boxes) and steps 2 to 4 are retired with
  the statistical allocation.
