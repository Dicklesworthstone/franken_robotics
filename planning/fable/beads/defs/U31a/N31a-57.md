## Background

Two gates are measured on the verb `foundry.export`, which writes geometry and the build packet:

- GATE-FOUNDRY-09 (Known answer, M5): tetrahedron round trip bit-exact; open shell and over-bound parts
  refused; mass properties from exported geometry within 10⁻⁶ of the model.
- GATE-FOUNDRY-10 (Known answer, M5): packet schema-valid, every hash matching, flags present and false,
  cross-consistency of parts, geometry, nets and schedule; a mutated flag rejected by `frob-verify`; a writer
  killed at any file boundary leaves no packet.

KILL-PACKET is triggered if either fails after one repair cycle; the fallback is a packet reduced to a parts
list, mass properties and STL, with STEP and harness deferred.

The packet this script exports is the packet of North-Star scenario NS-20, "a build packet": from a qualified
design and task, Foundry emits a `.fbuild` packet with a bill of materials, faceted solid geometry, a tolerance stack that
closes, fatigue margins computed from the simulated load histories, and one re-optimized link, with a claim
product of class Estimated and every file carrying the three program-wide no-authorization flags and the
packet flag `fabrication_released`, all false. This script asserts the content of that packet on the single verb
`foundry.export` (step 13); the scenario as a chain of the six verbs, from telemetry to a verified packet, and
its demonstration manifest are the North-Star bead NS-20, which depends on this bead and reads its records (the
Foundry chapter's draft of that chain, N31b-14, is superseded by NS-20).

## Scope

In: the script of the verb `foundry.export`; fixtures; assertions; logs; the two gate records (GATE-FOUNDRY-09
and -10); the packet-content record of step 13; the self-test.
Out: the mechanisms (N31a-34 to N31a-43; the auditor's checker N31b-44; seed beads BD-407 and BD-408); the
NS-20 pipeline script over all six verbs and the NS-20 demonstration manifest (the North-Star bead NS-20,
which reuses the content record of step 13 and re-measures only what the chain itself establishes); the auditor's own mutation
script (N31b-44); the pre-review items M2 to M6 and the review-record steps (N31b-32, N31b-33, measured by
N31b-34; this script uses only the reader verification of `foundry.check <packet>`, item M1 of N31b-32, which
is why that bead is a dependency); the
KILL-PACKET decision (N31b-19), which reads this script's two gate records.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures.** `tetrahedron`; `tetrahedron-open` (one face removed); `sphere-285714` (a closed mesh of
   285,714 triangles) and `sphere-285712`; `tube` (ρ = 2,700 kg/m³, radii 15 and 13 mm, length 0.20 m, 256
   segments); `harness-24v` (copper, 1 m each way, 0.5 mm², 10 A, 24 V); `two-finger` (the bus fixture);
   `quad-ref-final` (the reference quadruped after its structure loop, re-qualified, with one re-optimized
   link class); `quad-ref-final-unqualified` (the same design without its new verdict);
   `quad-ref-final-provisional` (one envelope case of `structure/` labelled `provisional`);
   `quad-ref-final-exploratory` (`synthetic`: the same design with one link class's structure record produced
   by `foundry.optimize --exploratory`, the label carried in the record; the fixture BD-408's script uses for
   the same case); the ranking
   `box-cone-exploit` (16 qualified finalists, k = 8, whose order flips under the circular cone: the exploit's
   winner and the honest winner, both qualified, the structure loop's successor of the exploit's winner, and a
   `tier-sensitive` selection record on a third finalist); `quad-ref-final-research-only` (the same design with
   a `research-only` asset in its lineage) and `quad-ref-final-attribution` (with an `attribution` asset); a
   scratch area under the session's export root and one outside it.
   Every `frob foundry export`, `frob foundry check` and `frob fidelity modelform` call below carries `--key
   "$(e2e_key <label>)"` (STD-E2E), one label per call, since the three verbs are `run`; where two runs are
   compared (steps 2, 4 and 15) each run has its own label, or the second would return the first one's stored
   envelope and prove nothing.
   `--fixture` is the cluster's named-fixture input (N31a-51).
2. **Steps.**
   1. `start`; cluster preamble.
   1b. *Mechanism stages.* Each mechanism bead owns its own script and this script runs it as a stage: the
      own scripts of the mechanism beads this bead depends on that have one, `foundry_admission.sh` (N31a-03),
      `foundry_mesh_export.sh` (N31a-34), `foundry_step_export.sh` (N31a-35), `foundry_packet_container.sh`
      (N31a-36), `foundry_packet_flags.sh` (N31a-37), `foundry_parts_list.sh` (N31a-39),
      `foundry_packet_geometry.sh` (N31a-40), `foundry_harness.sh` (N31a-41), `foundry_packet_assemble.sh`
      (N31a-43) and `foundry_selection_manifest.sh` (N31ax-01), are run as child scripts, each writing its own
      log. One `assert` line per stage records its exit code and the pass and
      fail counts of its `summary` line, with the path of its log; a failing stage fails this script. The
      steps below are the verb-level measurements of the gates and are not replaced by the stages.
   2. *STEP round trip (GATE-FOUNDRY-09).* `frob foundry export --fixture tetrahedron --geometry-only --format
      step --json` (the loose-geometry mode of N31a-34): assert 18 instances, `replay: "bit-exact"`, and that an independent decode equals the
      canonical mesh; export twice under two keys and assert identical file hashes.
   3. *Refused exports (GATE-FOUNDRY-09).* `tetrahedron-open`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-824`, the open edges in the
      payload. `sphere-285714`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-824`, N = 1,000,003 against 10⁶ and the bound 285,712 in
      the payload. `sphere-285712`: exports (N = 999,996).
   4. *Other formats.* STL, 3MF and GLB of the tetrahedron and the tube: byte-identical across two runs;
      re-import equals the canonical mesh; a loose export has its sidecar with four false flags.
   5. *Packet export (NS-20).* `frob foundry export quad-ref-final --packet out/quad-a --sync-wait-ms 0
      --json` (exit 8 with a job handle), then `frob job wait`. Assert the envelope's packet identity, class Estimated with
      its weakest input named, `authorization` flags false, and next actions.
   6. *Schema and hashes (GATE-FOUNDRY-10).* `frob foundry check out/quad-a.fbuild --json` (reader) and
      `frob-verify --packet out/quad-a.fbuild --json` (auditor, run from an empty directory with a cleared
      environment): both accept; every listed file's hash and size match; no unlisted file.
   7. *Flags (GATE-FOUNDRY-10).* A JSON query over every JSON file: four flags present and false. In a
      scratch copy set `fabrication_released` to true: reader and auditor both refuse with `FROB-REF-860`;
      remove the key: both refuse; a request carrying the key (through the MCP tool `foundry_export`, with
      its own `idempotency_key` from `e2e_key`, so that the flag is the request's only fault): exit 3,
      `FROB-REF-860` (not the pipeline's unknown-parameter 802 and not 805; N31a-37), `repairs` empty.
   8. *Cross-export identity (GATE-FOUNDRY-09).* From the reader's report: for every made part and each of
      the four files, mass, centre of mass and inertia within 10⁻⁶ relative of the model (a mirrored-hand
      geometry against the reflected tensor); log the largest difference. The tube fixture: four exports agree with each other to 10⁻¹² and with 0.0950 kg within
      0.1%. Scale one STL by 1,000 in a scratch copy (the helper fixes its manifest hash and re-issues the
      auditor's bundle): the check fails and prints a volume ratio of 10⁹.
   9. *Cross-consistency (GATE-FOUNDRY-10).* Assert the ten consistency rows pass on the packet; then for
      each row its planted inconsistency, and the three plants on rows that compare sections built in
      parallel (a joint re-bound after the parts list was built; a schedule `cable` that is no net of the
      harness; a chiral class without mirrored geometry), re-run the export on a `frob` built with `--features
      gauntlet-mutations` under `FROB_TEST_PACKET_PLANT=<plant>` (the assembler's plant hook, N31a-43; the
      verb has no input through which a section could be planted), and assert each is blocked with exit 3 and the refusal `packet-inconsistent` (row 10
      of N31b-45, held with no fitting interim: no code, `detail: "packet-inconsistent"`) naming the row and
      entry, the re-run command as first repair, and no packet directory created.
   10. *Atomicity (GATE-FOUNDRY-10).* For each boundary n of the writer's N + 3 (the listed files in path
       order, `manifest.json`, `certs/packet.frobcert`, the rename; N31a-36 step 9): run the export with
       `FROB_TEST_KILL_AT=n` on a `frob` built with `--features gauntlet-mutations` (the hook exists only in
       that build), each under its own label; assert that the target directory either does not
       exist or opens as a complete packet, and that no partial packet exists under the target name; log the
       observed state per boundary.
   11. *Section known answers.* `harness-24v`: 0.0688 Ω, 0.688 V (2.9%), 6.9 W, 20 A/mm²; slack fixture:
       36 mm. `two-finger`: the additive-peak prune is rejected at a two-tick deadline with a witness.
       `--priced` without a catalogue: exit 3, `FROB-REF-818`; default `prices: null`.
   12. *Admission.* `quad-ref-final-unqualified`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-166`, re-qualification as first repair;
       `quad-ref-final-exploratory` (an exploratory record among the inputs): refused (166, pointer at the
       record);
       `quad-ref-final-provisional`: exit 3, `provisional-load-case-in-packet` under its interim code
       `FROB-REF-820`, pointer at the case; no packet directory after any of the three.
   12b. *Selection (`selection-1` to `selection-6`; specified by N31ax-01).* (1) `frob fidelity modelform
       --ranking @fixture:box-cone-exploit --k 8 --key "$(e2e_key sel-mf-1)" --json` (a `run` verb of the
       fidelity cluster):
       verdict `model-conditional`, `flipped_by: ["cone"]`, exit 2 (the settling run of (3) and the re-check
       after it take new keys); (2) `frob foundry export <exploit winner> --packet out/sel --json`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-830`,
       both orders and the flipping variant in `observed`, no packet directory; the structure loop's successor
       of the exploit winner: the same, with `ranked_ancestor` naming the original; (3) apply
       `refusal.repairs[0]` (the settling run on the referee) and re-run the check: `model-robust`; (4) export
       the honest winner: exit 0, `selection.status: "model-robust"` with both record identifiers, and export
       the exploit winner again: still exit 3, `FROB-REF-830` (a later ranking that does not hold it in its top
       k resolves nothing for it); (5) the finalist with a `tier-sensitive` record: exit 3, `FROB-REF-819`;
       (6) `frob-verify` on the honest winner's packet: accepted, and on a scratch copy with `selection.status`
       removed: refused naming the field. The same request through the MCP tool `foundry_export` returns the
       same refusal envelope.
   12c. *Export door (N37-18, through the writer of N31a-36).* `--packet` naming a directory outside the
       session's export root: exit 5, `FROB-REF-734`, nothing written; `quad-ref-final-research-only`: exit 5,
       `FROB-REF-733`, the asset's lineage path in the payload, no packet directory; `quad-ref-final-attribution`:
       exit 0, a `NOTICES` file listed in the manifest with its hash, and the reader and the auditor accept it.
   13. *Packet content (the content NS-20 names).* On the packet of step 5 assert: a parts list with every joint's actuator accounted
       for; four geometry files per made part; `tolerances.json` whose stacks meet their budgets; fatigue
       entries numeric where the cards allow and `no-claim` with a reason otherwise; `structure/baseline.json`
       reporting the re-optimized link's saving against its beam-rung section with the ceiling line first.
   14. *Reduced profile.* `--profile reduced`: exactly the parts list, mass properties and STL files; reader
       and auditor accept; the manifest lists what is deferred.
   15. *Determinism lanes.* Step 5 twice into two directories: identical packet identity; on a second machine
       or container image: identical identity.
   16. Write the receipts of the two gates and the packet-content record; the gate values are recorded with
       `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-FOUNDRY-09 --receipt <hash>` (and
       `GATE-FOUNDRY-10`) by a session that did not implement the mechanisms (N38b-19); `summary`.
3. **Gate records.** GATE-FOUNDRY-09: instance count and replay result, the two refusals, the largest
   cross-export difference with its part and quantity. GATE-FOUNDRY-10: schema result, hash table summary,
   flag checks by reader and auditor, the ten consistency rows, the kill table (boundary → observed state).
   Packet content: packet identity, class, the content assertions of step 13, the saving figure with its
   baseline; NS-20 cites this record in its demonstration.
4. **Self-test** (`scripts/e2e/tests/foundry_export_packet_selftest.sh`): mutants and failing steps:
   `export-b1-not-b2` → 2; `count-points-per-triangle-corner` → 2; `round-at-export-silently` → 8;
   `recompute-from-source-mesh-not-file` → 8; `lenient-deserializer` → 7; `accept-flag-absent` (auditor) → 7;
   `manifest-first` → 10; `rename-file-by-file` → 10; `write-then-check` → 9; `additive-peak-prune` → 11;
   `one-way-length` → 11; `skip-selection-check` → 12b; `accept-provisional-case` → 12;
   `schedule-trusts-parts-list` (the parallel-section rows skipped) → 9.
5. **Logging.** Shared helper; `target/e2e-logs/foundry_export_packet/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a
   transcript. Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31a-57`), `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall
   time, the envelope's `evidence` block. Digests are logged wherever state is compared (file hashes, packet
   identities, mesh hashes). On failure: the first differing value (the file and byte offset for hash
   mismatches; the part, file and quantity for identity failures; the row and entry for consistency) and the
   paths of the packet and scratch copies. `summary`: assertions passed and failed;
   `gates: ["GATE-FOUNDRY-09", "GATE-FOUNDRY-10"]`. Packets may hold private-scene load data: logs carry
   identities, hashes and counts only.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a conforming build and writes its log, transcript, two gate records and the packet-content record with measured values.
- [ ] Each clause of GATE-FOUNDRY-09 and -10 is asserted by a named step (2, 3, 8; 6, 7, 9, 10).
- [ ] The auditor runs from an empty directory with a cleared environment and accepts the packet; both reader and auditor reject the flag mutations with FROB-REF-860.
- [ ] No packet exists under the target name after any killed or blocked export.
- [ ] Refusals FROB-REF-824, -860, -818, -166, -820 (interim, `provisional-load-case-in-packet`), -830, -819, `packet-inconsistent` (held, `detail` only) and the export door's -734 and -733 (exit 5) are asserted with pointers and first repairs (none for 860), through the shared `assert_refusal` helper; an `attribution` design's packet carries a listed `NOTICES` file.
- [ ] The selection steps show the exploit's winner refused before and after the settling run and the honest winner exported with `selection.status: "model-robust"`.
- [ ] The self-test fails at the named step for each of the fourteen mutants.
- [ ] Two exports of the same inputs have the same packet identity, on one machine and across machines.
- [ ] The records let the KILL-PACKET decision (N31b-19) be closed by citation, and the packet-content record is in the form the NS-20 pipeline (NS-20) cites; the gate values are recorded through `record_gate` by a non-implementing session.

## Considerations

- The NS-20 scenario's "18% lighter" is an illustration; the script records the measured saving and asserts
  only that it is reported against the beam baseline after its ceiling.
- The kill lane and the consistency plants depend on test hooks in the writer (N31a-36) and the assembler
  (N31a-43), compiled only in the `conformance` feature that `gauntlet-mutations` enables; the steps that use
  them run a `frob` built with that feature, and the script asserts that the product binary ignores both
  variables (an export under `FROB_TEST_KILL_AT=1` completes and writes the whole packet).
- If the kill fires, step 14 becomes the capability and steps 2, 3 and 11's harness parts are retired with
  the deferred sections.
