## Background

GATE-FOUNDRY-11 (claim: Known answer [M]; milestone M8; TARGET): **the fixed-point cases of the known-answer
table; 10⁶ vectors bit-equal.**

The capability is the Moonshot option `--with-firmware` of the verb `foundry.export`, under the feature
`firmware-export`: export of a joint-level controller as source for a `no_std`, heap-free, unsafe-free crate
in fixed-point arithmetic behind a caller-owned bus trait, with Q-format declarations, overflow certificates,
quantization bounds, a test-vector manifest and the no-authorization flags. Its ceiling is stated in advance:
no speed claim; the benefit is that the controller evaluated in simulation is, integer for integer, the
arithmetic that would be shipped.

The fixed-point cases: in Q15, (−1)·(−1) is 0x8000·0x8000 = 0x40000000, which shifts to 0x8000 and reads as −1;
the negation of −32768 overflows; an integrator adding 400 counts per tick from zero overflows sixteen bits at
tick 82, and without a clamp the one-step query returns the state 32,400 at once. Each counterexample must be
found and the clamped controller certified; a widened input range must break a certificate.

KILL-FIRMWARE: if the gate fails, the export is controller parameters, Q formats and the test-vector manifest
only; no code.

The export's claim is narrow, and the script records it that way: no intermediate overflows on the declared
input ranges (one instance certificate per controller, class Certified), and the exported arithmetic equals the
simulated arithmetic integer for integer on tested vectors (class Tested, never called equivalence). This bead is
the end-to-end measurement of the gate through the released verb and the discharge record of proof obligation
PO-FOUNDRY-04 (for each exported controller, no intermediate overflows on the declared input ranges). It runs
only if the pilot (N31b-10) was positive and the epic was funded; otherwise it is closed as `not run: pilot
negative` and the gate row says the same.

One script, one bead: the gate's measurement was drafted twice (here and as N31b-20). N31b-20 is superseded by
this bead and its content is folded in: two more fixtures, the composition of the vector set, the artifact scan,
the budget clause, the PO-FOUNDRY-04 row, the conformance unit tests and three negative controls.

## Scope

In: the script, fixtures, the vector comparison, certificate replay, assertions, logs, the gate receipt and its recording (the row
of GATE-FOUNDRY-11), the PO-FOUNDRY-04 ledger row, the conformance unit tests, the self-test.
Out: the mechanisms (N31a-44 to N31a-46; the theorem card's certificate format, CARD-T-14); the pilot
(N31b-10); the budget experiment (N31b-29); the kill-gate decision (N31b-21), which reads this bead's record.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures.** `knee-pi-unclamped` and `knee-pi-clamped` (a Q15 proportional–integral joint loop; integrator
   increment up to 400 counts per tick; clamp at ±32,000); `q15-product-full` and `q15-product-symmetric`
   (ranges [−32768, 32767] and [−32767, 32767]); `q15-negate`; `correlated-difference` (a·x − a·y with
   |x − y| ≤ 2⁻⁴); `whole-body-controller` (outside the exportable set); `quad-ref-final` for the in-loop
   run; a tiny budget configuration (`bv_budget_ms = 1`); `pd-loop` and `energy-monitor` (two further
   exportable kinds: a proportional–derivative loop and the energy-budget monitor, both expected to certify).
   All are Q15 in 16-bit words and live under `frob-conformance/fixtures/foundry/firmware/`; the clamped loop
   declares its inputs in [−0.49, 0.49]. (The superseded draft named the first four `pi-unclamped`,
   `pi-clamped`, `mul-full-range` and `neg-full-range`; the names here are the ones used.)
2. **Steps.** Every export below is `frob foundry export quad-ref-final --packet <scratch>/fw-<step>-<n>
   --with-firmware --controller <fixture> --key "$(e2e_key fw-<step>-<n>)" --json`, the form of the
   `foundry.export` row in VERBS-foundry (`foundry.export` is a run verb: one distinct label per call, written
   with STD-E2E's `e2e_key`, so no call receives another's stored receipt; the packet directory lies under the
   session's export root). The fixture controllers are declared on
   the qualified fixture design `quad-ref-final`, so the packet precondition FROB-REF-166 does not intervene. Step 13 adds N31a-46's `--firmware
   params-only`. The stages of the mechanism beads are marked: `scope`, `emission` and `packet independence`
   (N31a-44: steps 2, 9, 12), `obligations` (N31a-45: steps 3 to 8), `vectors` (N31a-46: steps 10, 11, 13).
   1. `start`; assert the binary has the feature (`frob doctor --json`), otherwise log `not measured` and exit
      with the harness's skip code; record the `fln` checker's identity and commit.
   1b. *Mechanism stages.* Each mechanism bead owns its own script and this script runs it as a stage: the
      own scripts of the mechanism beads this bead depends on, `foundry_firmware_emit.sh` (N31a-44),
      `foundry_firmware_obligations.sh` (N31a-45) and `foundry_firmware_vectors.sh` (N31a-46), are run as
      child scripts, each writing its own log. One `assert` line per stage records its exit code and the pass
      and fail counts of its `summary` line, with the path of its log; a failing stage fails this script. The
      steps below are the verb-level measurements of the gate and are not replaced by the stages.
   2. *Scope.* `--controller whole-body-controller` (label `fw-2-1`): exit 5, no code, `detail:
      "firmware-controller-not-exportable"` (held, row 11 of N31b-45; N31a-44), naming the first construct
      outside the exportable set.
   3. *Q15 product.* `q15-product-full`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-827`, status `refuted`, counterexample
      (−32768, −32768) with the offending value 32,768 against the range; no `src/` written.
      `q15-product-symmetric`: certified.
   4. *Negation.* `q15-negate`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-827`, counterexample −32768.
   5. *Integrator.* `knee-pi-unclamped`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-827`; the step obligation's witness is a state in
      [32,368, 32,767] whose successor overflows; under the reachability relation (state a multiple of 400
      from zero) it equals 32,400; a direct run of the integer program from zero overflows at tick 82.
      `knee-pi-clamped`: base and step certified; certificates replayed by the checker.
   6. *Widened range.* Re-check the clamped controller's certificates after declaring the increment range as
      ±800: the certificate check fails on the range digest; re-running the step query with the formats held
      is refuted (s + u reaches ±32,800 before the clamp; the witness re-executes to the overflow), and a full
      re-export assigns the sum a 32-bit word and certifies anew with a new range digest (N31a-45); both
      outcomes are asserted and logged. Then re-declare the inputs of the clamped controller as [−1, 1): at least one
      obligation becomes satisfiable and the export refuses.
   7. *Correlated term.* `correlated-difference`: certified by a bit-vector query in Q1.15, with the log
      showing that interval analysis alone asked for one more integer bit.
   8. *Undecided.* `knee-pi-clamped` with the tiny budget: exit 3, `FROB-REF-827`, status `undecided`, the
      larger-budget repair listed last; no `src/` written. On the full run, log the largest query time
      against the per-obligation budget fixed by the budget decision N31b-29 (2 s by default); an undecided
      obligation is a refusal and fails the certified clause for that fixture. `pd-loop` and `energy-monitor`
      export with every obligation decided.
   9. *Emission.* The certified export's file set; `cargo build` of the generated crate for the host and a
      `cargo check` for a bare-metal target; the denylist lint result; the `AUTHORIZATION` constant and the
      manifest flags all false; the three no-claims present (`timing` among them). Artifact scan: the crate
      has `#![no_std]` and `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, no allocation, no register access, no bus driver and no
      stop-circuit code (a source scan against the deny list; `scan_findings` must be 0). Every certificate is
      replayed with the independent checker.
   10. *Vectors.* Build and run the generated vector harness: assert `mismatches == 0` over `1000000`
       records; assert the manifest hash; regenerate the vectors from the seed and assert the same hash. The
       set is drawn from a registered seed: uniformly random within the declared ranges, plus all range
       corners and the sequences that drive the integrator to its clamp. The crate and the exact-integer
       kernel in the simulator must agree bit for bit on every output and on every state word. The
       quantization bound must contain the measured difference from the real-valued controller on the vectors.
   11. *In-loop.* Run the fixed-point controller as an exact-integer kernel on a small task set of
       `quad-ref-final` twice (and on a device backend where present): equal digests of per-tick integer
       outputs; log the paired closed-loop difference against the real-valued controller with its interval.
   12. *Packet independence.* Export the packet with and without `--with-firmware` (labels `fw-12-1` and
       `fw-12-2`, into two directories): the packet without it is accepted by reader and auditor (`frob-verify`),
       and no file outside `firmware/` differs between the two except `manifest.json` (its file table) and the
       bundle `certs/packet.frobcert`, which embeds the manifest's bytes.
   13. *Fallback.* `--firmware params-only`: parameters, Q-format table and vectors present; no `src/`; the
       manifest says code is withheld.
   13b. *Refusal binding of FROB-REF-827 (N31-REF; the feature lane of the battery N31ap1-51).* Every 827
       refusal of steps 3 to 8 is asserted through the shared `assert_refusal` helper (N31a-52). The
       repair-replay step for 827: apply the first repair of `q15-product-full` in its range alternative (the
       registry row's order puts the counterexample first, and that one repair names a saturating product at
       the named point or the input range narrowed to the largest safe range, [−32767, 32767], which is the
       declaration of `q15-product-symmetric`; N31a-45 step 7) and re-export under a new key:
       certified, exit 0; the second repair, the wider format named in the payload, is applied the same way
       and also certifies. Then run `scripts/e2e/foundry_refusal_coverage.sh` over this
       run's logs and the default-lane logs with the feature list of this binary: 827 must be present.
   14. Write the gate receipt; `summary`. The script writes no gate row itself (one recorder per gate): the
       register names this bead for GATE-FOUNDRY-11 (N31b-12), and the value is recorded from the receipt, by
       a session that did not implement the exporter, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example
       record_gate -- --gate GATE-FOUNDRY-11 --receipt <hash>` (the gate recorder N38b-19).
3. **Gate receipt.** The three cases' outcomes (counterexample values; certified variants), the widened-range
   result, vector count and mismatches, manifest hash, cross-run digest equality, the host toolchain identity,
   the checker identity, per-obligation solver times against the 2 s budget; per fixture the verdict,
   certificate digests and replay results; the artifact scan; commit, hardware fingerprint, seed. A
   measurement that completes with a failing clause still writes its receipt, with status `fail` and the
   failing clause named, and that receipt is recorded like a passing one (it is what the kill decision reads);
   the script exits non-zero; a measurement that could not be completed writes no receipt and no row. The PO-FOUNDRY-04 row of the obligation ledger (N31b-02) is updated with
   the certificate identities, each with its controller, its ranges and its word length.
4. **Self-test** (`scripts/e2e/tests/foundry_firmware_selftest.sh`): mutants and failing steps:
   `skip-step-obligation` → 5; `treat-timeout-as-safe` → 8; `report-unverified-model` → 3;
   `emit-before-certification` → 3 (source files exist after a refusal); `emit-float-gain` → 9;
   `planted-shift-miscompile` → 10; `vectors-from-the-emitted-code` → 10 (the miscompile no longer
   mismatches, which the self-test detects by requiring a failure).
5. **Logging.** Shared helper; `target/e2e-logs/foundry_firmware/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript.
   Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31a-58`), `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall time, the
   envelope's `evidence` block (class Certified only on the certificate list; Tested for the in-loop
   comparison; Estimated elsewhere). Per obligation: intermediate, format, method, status, solver time and
   threshold, certificate hash. Per fixture: `obligations_total`, `decided_unsat`, `refuted`, `undecided` (0
   required for certified fixtures), `query_ms_max` against the budget, `certificates_replayed`; then `vectors`
   (1,000,000), `mismatches` (0) and `scan_findings` (0). On failure: the first mismatching vector's index, inputs and both outputs, or
   the counterexample that did not re-execute, and the path of the generated crate. `summary`: assertions
   passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-FOUNDRY-11"]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a conforming build with the feature and the checker present, and writes its log, transcript and gate receipt with measured values, from which GATE-FOUNDRY-11 is recorded through `record_gate` by a non-implementing session; without them it records `not measured`.
- [ ] Both clauses of GATE-FOUNDRY-11 are asserted: the three fixed-point cases (steps 3 to 5, with the certified variants) and 10⁶ bit-equal vectors (step 10).
- [ ] A widened input range breaks a certificate (step 6).
- [ ] FROB-REF-827 is asserted in both variants (`refuted` with a counterexample; `undecided`), and no source is emitted in either; the binding test `ref_827_fixed_point_obligation` passes and its first repair, replayed, certifies.
- [ ] The widened increment range is refuted with the formats held and re-certified by a full re-export at a 32-bit sum.
- [ ] The generated crate builds for the host and checks for a bare-metal target with no dependencies; its flags are false and the three no-claims are present.
- [ ] The self-test fails at the named step for each of the seven mutants.
- [ ] The packet without firmware is unaffected by the feature (step 12), and both packets pass the offline auditor.
- [ ] `pd-loop` and `energy-monitor` certify with no undecided obligation; the largest query time is recorded against the budget.
- [ ] 10⁶ vectors are bit-equal between the host-built crate and the integer kernel, including all state words; the quantization bound contains the measured difference.
- [ ] The artifact scan passes (`scan_findings` 0) and every certificate replays with the independent checker.
- [ ] Certificates are recorded as class Certified and vectors as class Tested; nothing is recorded as Proved or as equivalence.
- [ ] The gate row (written by `record_gate` from the receipt) and the PO-FOUNDRY-04 row hold measured values; a completed measurement is recorded whatever the outcome.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Helper tests under `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `direct_integer_run_overflows_at_tick_82` (400 × 81 = 32,400 fits;
400 × 82 = 32,800 does not); `witness_validator_accepts_any_overflowing_state`; `gate_receipt_schema_valid`.

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_foundry_11.rs`: `witness_equivalence_class` (any state from 32,368 to
32,767 counts as the integrator finding); `vector_set_contains_corners_and_clamp_sequences`;
`mismatch_report_names_first_vector`; `deny_list_scan_catches_volatile_write` (a planted `write_volatile`);
`clause_table` (the gate passes only if every clause passes and no obligation is undecided).

`cargo test -p frob-foundry --features moonshot-firmware-export refusals`: the binding test N31-REF names for
this code, `ref_827_fixed_point_obligation` (the Q15 product of −1 and −1 refuted with its counterexample, and
the tiny-budget obligation `undecided`; code, exit 3, pointer at the intermediate, repairs in the stated order,
the larger budget last and only for `undecided`).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_firmware.sh`, as specified; logs under `target/e2e-logs/foundry_firmware/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-749 (fixed point, Q15: (−1)·(−1) reads as −1; negation of −32768 overflows; the integrator overflows at
tick 82 and the one-step query returns 32,400; the clamped controller is certified; control: a widened input
range breaks a certificate). CX-342 and CX-343 are the two beliefs refuted in steps 3 to 5. Negative controls:
the seven mutants; a generated crate with one coefficient altered after export (the vector comparison must
report a mismatch); a proof stream with one record altered (the checker must reject); a harness that compares
only outputs and not state words, against a controller whose state diverges before its output does (the full
comparison must catch what the weak one misses). Determinism lanes: vectors and emitted source byte-identical
twice; integer outputs equal across backends; verdicts and counterexample classes identical across runs and
backends (query times are not); solver witnesses validated by re-execution, not by equality.

## Considerations

- The script needs a Linux host with the bit-vector checker; on other hosts steps 3 to 8 are logged as not
  measured and the gate is not recorded.
- Nothing here touches hardware. Timing, stack depth and behaviour on a board stay `no-claim`, and the script
  asserts that no output states an execution time.
- If the kill fires, step 13 is the capability and the gate is recorded as failed with the fallback's name.
- 10⁶ agreeing vectors are a test. The export's claim about all inputs is the overflow certificate only.
- A certificate covers one controller, one set of ranges and one word length; the row records all three.
- The gate runs on a host where the sibling checker is admitted; no device and no board is involved, and no timing is measured.
