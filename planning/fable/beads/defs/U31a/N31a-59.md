## Background

GATE-FOUNDRY-12 (claim: Fidelity [M]; milestone M6 by the gate register; TARGET): **a 100-hour reference
(1.8 × 10⁸ steps) compressed at least 50-fold in steps with at most 5% error on every slow state; the planted
backlash-induced gait collapse caught.**

The capability is the verb `foundry.lifecycle` under the feature `lifecycle`: it advances slow wear and ageing
states over many hours of duty without simulating every step, by averaging rates over short windows at frozen
slow state and taking macro steps, returning to full resolution when the gait's stability margin, the macro
error or the time-scale separation says it must. Its outputs are times to first threshold with intervals,
aged model variants and a front of mass against service duration, all class Estimated. Its ceiling is stated
in advance: the ratio is a count of simulated steps, not wall time and not field experience.

KILL-LIFECYCLE: if the gate fails, the fallback is aged model variants at hand-specified wear states, with no
time axis and no compression claim.

## Scope

In: the script, fixtures, the stored reference, assertions, logs, the gate record, the self-test.
Out: the mechanisms (N31a-47 to N31a-50); the pilot and the kill-gate decision (unit U31b).

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures.** Host-level toys: `averaged-growth` (ż = ε·a·x², x = sin ωt; ε = 10⁻⁴, a = 2, ω = 2π rad/s,
   T = 1,000 s); `wear-feedback` (β̇ = ε·(c₀ + c₁·β) with c₀ = 1, c₁ = 2, β₀ = 0, to ε·t = 1);
   `two-temperature-duty` (half the time at T_ref, half at T_ref + 20 K, halving per 10 K); `cell-fade`
   (1% per √month, 0.5% per 100 cycles; 9 months, 300 cycles); `planted-collapse` (multiplier
   ρ(β) = 0.6 + κ·β reaching 1 at β* = 0.02 rad, with c₀ = 10⁻³, c₁ = 50, ε = 10⁻⁴). Robot-level:
   `gate-duty-100h` (the reference quadruped on a trot duty with wear, fatigue, insulation and cell laws on
   cards chosen so that at least one slow state moves by more than 10% of its threshold in 100 hours), with
   its stored reference run (1.8 × 10⁸ steps at 500 Hz, digest recorded); `winding-overheat` (a duty that
   exceeds the insulation class limit); `quad-ref` for aged variants; three stock walls for the front.
2. **Steps.**
   1. `start`; assert the binary has the feature, otherwise log `not measured` and exit with the harness's
      skip code; cluster preamble.
   2. *Averaged growth.* `frob foundry lifecycle --fixture averaged-growth --json`: z(T) within 5% of
      ε·a·T/2 = 0.1; compression ratio at least 50, printed with the words "ratio of simulated steps".
   3. *Wear feedback.* `--fixture wear-feedback`: β within 5% of 0.5·(e² − 1) = 3.195 at ε·t = 1.
   4. *Rate averaging.* `--fixture two-temperature-duty`: consumption rate 2.5/τ_ref; the helper
      `mutate law-at-mean-temperature` gives 2.0/τ_ref and the comparison reports the 25% overstatement of
      service duration.
   5. *Cell fade.* `--fixture cell-fade`: capacity fade 4.5% (3% calendar + 1.5% cyclic). Under the
      KILL-SINEW-AGE switch: constants reported and the label `cell ageing: user-set constants`.
   6. *Planted collapse (GATE-FOUNDRY-12).* `--fixture planted-collapse`: the mode switches to `full` before
      β = 0.02 rad; the trigger named is the multiplier; the reported collapse time is within 5% of
      ln(1001)/0.005 = 1,381.8 s.
   7. *Gate run (GATE-FOUNDRY-12).* `frob foundry lifecycle --fixture gate-duty-100h --horizon-h 100 --json`
      with a short `sync_wait_ms` (exit 8), then `job.wait`. Compare with the stored reference at 25, 50, 75
      and 100 hours: relative error of every slow state at most 5%; simulated steps at most 3.6 × 10⁶ (ratio
      at least 50); log each state's movement relative to its threshold and assert that at least one exceeds
      10%.
   8. *Reference integrity.* Assert the stored reference's digest; re-run a 1-hour slice of the reference
      directly and assert it equals the stored slice's digest (the reference is real, not regenerated from
      the candidate).
   9. *Validity domain.* `--fixture winding-overheat`: exit 3, `FROB-REF-831`, the quantity, range, observed
      maximum and share in the payload; three repairs. With `--strict-data` on generic cards: the card
      refusal.
   10. *Outputs.* On `quad-ref` for 1,000 hours: the threshold table with intervals; the first threshold
       named with its law and card; thresholds beyond the horizon reported as `> horizon`; four aged
       variants; `frob eval run --variants <set>` on fixed addresses returning per-variant results side by
       side. With three stock walls: three (mass, time) points with intervals and the non-dominated marks.
   11. *Rare overloads.* The same run with and without `--rare-rates`: the label `rare overloads: not
       included` in the second; a larger fatigue rate in the first.
   12. *Fallback.* `--mode hand-specified` with supplied wear states: variants returned; no time axis; no
       ratio.
   13. *Determinism lanes.* Steps 2 to 6 twice: identical records; step 7 twice on one backend: identical.
   14. Write the gate record; `summary`.
3. **Gate record.** Simulated steps and ratio; per slow state the relative error at the four checkpoints and
   the movement relative to threshold; the collapse result (switch point, trigger, time error); the reference
   digest; step accounting split by mode; commit, hardware fingerprint, seed.
4. **Self-test** (`scripts/e2e/tests/foundry_lifecycle_selftest.sh`): mutants and failing steps:
   `average-inputs-then-apply-law` → 4; `calendar-fade-linear-in-t` → 5; `ignore-multiplier` → 6;
   `drop-rejected-steps-from-count` → 7 (the ratio no longer matches the independent step count from the
   job's telemetry); `finish-counter-per-window` → 7 (fatigue error above 5%); `rare-term-silently-zero` → 11;
   `report-ratio-as-speedup` → 2 (the wording check).
5. **Logging.** Shared helper; `target/e2e-logs/foundry_lifecycle/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript.
   Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31a-59`), `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall time, the
   envelope's `evidence` block (class Estimated throughout). Per macro step: slow states, H, the error
   estimate, the multiplier, triggers, mode. Metrics with units and thresholds (5%, 50, 3.6 × 10⁶, 10%). On
   failure: the slow state and checkpoint with the largest error, both values, and the paths of the run
   record and the reference. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-FOUNDRY-12"]`.
6. **Runtime.** The stored reference is produced once per gate recording by a dedicated job (hours on the CPU
   tiers) and is not regenerated by this script; step 7's compressed run is minutes. Step 8 keeps the stored
   reference honest.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a conforming build with the feature and writes its log, transcript and gate record with measured values; without the feature it records `not measured`.
- [ ] Both clauses of GATE-FOUNDRY-12 are asserted: compression at least 50 with at most 5% error on every slow state at four checkpoints (step 7), and the planted collapse caught (step 6).
- [ ] At least one slow state of the gate fixture moves more than 10% of its threshold, and the record shows every state's movement.
- [ ] The four closed-form fixtures meet their values (0.1; 3.195; 2.5/τ_ref; 4.5%).
- [ ] FROB-REF-831 is asserted with its payload and repairs.
- [ ] The self-test fails at the named step for each of the seven mutants.
- [ ] Every printed ratio carries its qualifying words and no output calls it a speed-up.
- [ ] The reference digest check and the 1-hour slice pass.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_lifecycle.sh`, as specified; logs under `target/e2e-logs/foundry_lifecycle/`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-750 (averaged growth: ε·a·T/2). KA-751 (wear feedback closed form; control: the planted collapse must
force full resolution before β*). KA-752 (2.5/τ_ref, not 2.0/τ_ref). KA-753 (cell fade 4.5%). CX-344 (a law
evaluated at the average condition). Arithmetic: 1.8 × 10⁸ ÷ 50 = 3.6 × 10⁶. Negative controls: the seven
mutants. Determinism lanes: twice on one backend; the toys also in debug and release.

## Considerations

- OPEN-FOUNDRY-5 blocks the gate: without coefficients of known provenance the gate fixture runs on generic
  cards marked `assumed`, and the record says so; under `--strict-data` it does not run.
- A fidelity gate against a reference built from the same laws tests the averaging, not the laws. The record's
  wording keeps that distinction: "compressed run agrees with reference", never "predicts service duration".
- If the kill fires, step 12 is the capability and the gate is recorded as failed with the fallback's name.
