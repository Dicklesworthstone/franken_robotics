## Background

GATE-FOUNDRY-12 (claim: Fidelity [M]; milestone M6 by the gate register; TARGET): **a 100-hour reference
(1.8 × 10⁸ steps) compressed at least 50-fold in steps with at most 5% error on every slow state; the planted
backlash-induced gait collapse caught.**

The capability is the verb `foundry.lifecycle` under the feature `lifecycle`: it advances slow wear and ageing
states over many hours of duty without simulating every step, by averaging rates over short windows at frozen
slow state and taking macro steps, returning to full resolution when the gait's stability margin, the macro
error or the time-scale separation says it must. Its outputs are times to first threshold with intervals,
aged model variants and a front of mass against service duration, all class Estimated. Its ceiling is stated
in advance: the ratio is a count of simulated steps, not wall time and not field experience.

KILL-LIFECYCLE: if the gate fails, the fallback is aged model variants at hand-specified wear states, with no
time axis and no compression claim.

The chapter labelled the gate's row "Performance of the method"; the gate register reads it as a Fidelity claim
(a compressed run agrees with a 100-hour reference within a stated error) and places it at M6 with the
lifecycle epic. This bead is the end-to-end measurement of that gate through the released verb. It runs only if
the pilot (N31b-11) was positive and the epic was funded.

One script, one bead: the gate's measurement was drafted twice (here and as N31b-22). N31b-22 is superseded by
this bead and its content is folded in: the reference recorded hourly, the error metric with its registered
floor, the robot-level planted collapse, the synthetic cards and their label, the potency control and the
conformance unit tests.

## Scope

In: the script, fixtures, the stored reference run, the compressed run, the planted-collapse variant, the
comparison, the known answers of the method on robot-free fixtures, assertions, logs, the gate receipt and its
recording (the row of GATE-FOUNDRY-12), the conformance unit tests, the self-test.
Out: the mechanisms (N31a-47 to N31a-50); the pilot (N31b-11); coefficient data (N31b-28); the kill-gate
decision (N31b-23), which reads this bead's record.

## Technical approach

1. **Fixtures.** Host-level toys: `averaged-growth` (ż = ε·a·x², x = sin ωt; ε = 10⁻⁴, a = 2, ω = 2π rad/s,
   T = 1,000 s); `wear-feedback` (β̇ = ε·(c₀ + c₁·β) with c₀ = 1, c₁ = 2, β₀ = 0, to ε·t = 1);
   `two-temperature-duty` (half the time at T_ref, half at T_ref + 20 K, halving per 10 K); `cell-fade`
   (1% per √month, 0.5% per 100 cycles; 9 months, 300 cycles); `planted-collapse` (multiplier
   ρ(β) = 0.6 + κ·β reaching 1 at β* = 0.02 rad, with c₀ = 10⁻³, c₁ = 50, ε = 10⁻⁴). Robot-level:
   `gate-duty-100h` (the reference quadruped on a trot duty with wear, fatigue, insulation and cell laws on
   cards chosen so that at least one slow state moves by more than 10% of its threshold in 100 hours), with
   its stored reference run (1.8 × 10⁸ steps at 500 Hz, digest recorded); `winding-overheat` (a duty that
   exceeds the insulation class limit; its fixture catalogue also holds a card of a higher insulation class
   whose domain covers the duty); `quad-ref` for aged variants; three stock walls for the front.
   The gate fixture in detail: the reference quadruped trotting on flat ground under its baseline controller,
   with a duty profile registered in advance (DESIGN: continuous trot with a registered payload schedule). Its
   wear, insulation and cell-fade cards are *synthetic* cards registered for the gate, with coefficients scaled
   so that each slow-state family moves by a measurable fraction (at least 10% of its threshold) within 100
   hours; they are labelled `synthetic` and every result of this bead prints that label. Slow states
   compared: backlash β of each actuated joint; damage at each fatigue gauge; consumed insulation life per
   winding; capacity fade f_Q and resistance growth f_R. `gate-duty-100h-collapse` is the planted variant: the
   wear coefficient of one knee is raised so that backlash reaches a registered value β* at which the trot's
   orbit loses stability (a Floquet multiplier reaches the unit circle) inside the 100 hours.
   **The reference** is a full simulation of the 100 hours with every slow law integrated at full resolution
   (wear volume advanced from the recorded sliding distance and load each step; rainflow fed every sample
   with residues carried; insulation rate evaluated at each step's winding temperature; cell states advanced
   from current), recorded hourly. It runs once per backend used for the gate and is stored by identity.
2. **Steps.**
   1. `start`; assert the binary has the feature, otherwise log `not measured` and exit with the harness's
      skip code (77); cluster preamble (N31a-51). Every `frob foundry lifecycle` and `frob eval run` call below
      passes `--key "$(foundry_key <step>)"`, the preamble's key; a repeat that a step compares with an earlier
      run uses its own step name, so it computes rather than returning the stored envelope.
   2. *Averaged growth.* `frob foundry lifecycle --fixture averaged-growth --json`: z(T) within 5% of
      ε·a·T/2 = 0.1; compression ratio at least 50, printed with the words "ratio of simulated steps".
   3. *Wear feedback.* `--fixture wear-feedback`: β within 5% of 0.5·(e² − 1) = 3.195 at ε·t = 1.
   4. *Rate averaging.* `--fixture two-temperature-duty`: consumption rate 2.5/τ_ref; the helper
      `mutate law-at-mean-temperature` gives 2.0/τ_ref and the comparison reports the 25% overstatement of
      service duration.
   5. *Cell fade.* `--fixture cell-fade`: capacity fade 4.5% (3% calendar + 1.5% cyclic). Under the
      KILL-SINEW-AGE switch: constants reported and the label `cell ageing: user-set constants`.
   6. *Planted collapse (GATE-FOUNDRY-12).* `--fixture planted-collapse`: the mode switches to `full` before
      β = 0.02 rad; the trigger named is the multiplier; the reported collapse time is within 5% of
      ln(1001)/0.005 = 1,381.8 s.
   7. *Gate run (GATE-FOUNDRY-12).* `frob foundry lifecycle --fixture gate-duty-100h --horizon-h 100 --json`
      with a short `sync_wait_ms` (exit 8), then `job.wait`, on the same backend and duty profile as the
      reference. The envelope reports every slow state hourly, the macro steps taken and rejected, the micro
      windows with their settling residuals, every return to full resolution with its trigger, and the total
      of simulated fast steps (all of them: settling, averaging, rejected steps, full-resolution stretches).
      Compare with the stored reference at every hourly mark (the record tabulates 25, 50, 75 and 100 hours):
      for every slow state the difference is at most 5%, measured relative to the reference's change from its
      initial value, with an absolute floor registered in advance for states that barely move; simulated steps
      at most 3.6 × 10⁶ (ratio at least 50); log each state's movement relative to its threshold and assert
      that every family exceeds 10% (a state that does not is reported and judged against the floor).
   7b. *Planted collapse on the robot (GATE-FOUNDRY-12).* `--fixture gate-duty-100h-collapse`: the compressed
      run must return to full resolution before β* is reached and must report the collapse (the gait's
      failure and its time) within 5% of the reference's time. A run that macro-steps across β* fails the
      clause even if its final slow states are within tolerance. Log `beta_at_return` against β* and both
      collapse times.
   7c. *Potency.* The gate run at a requested 500-fold compression must exceed 5% error or refuse its step
      size; a harness in which it passes cannot see a wrong answer.
   8. *Reference integrity.* Assert the stored reference's digest; re-run a 1-hour slice of the reference
      directly and assert it equals the stored slice's digest (the reference is real, not regenerated from
      the candidate).
   9. *Validity domain and refusals (the feature lane of the battery N31ap1-51).* `--fixture winding-overheat`:
      exit 3, `FROB-REF-831`, the quantity, range, observed maximum and share in the payload; the four repairs
      in N31a-48's order (the registry row's two first: a card whose domain covers the range, then the run
      restricted to the domain; then the design change; then the run without the law), the first naming the
      fixture catalogue's higher insulation-class card; replay that repair under a new key: the run
      completes. A run record edited to cite a lifecycle number as Tested, offered to a claim product: exit 3,
      `FROB-REF-831` with reason `cited above Estimated`. With `--strict-data` on generic cards: exit 3,
      `FROB-REF-113` naming the `assumed` card field. Each through the shared `assert_refusal` helper
      (N31a-52); then `scripts/e2e/foundry_refusal_coverage.sh` over this run's logs and the default-lane logs
      with this binary's feature list: 831 must be present.
   10. *Outputs.* On `quad-ref` for 1,000 hours: the threshold table with intervals; the first threshold
       named with its law and card; thresholds beyond the horizon reported as `> horizon`; four aged
       variants; `frob eval run quad-ref --variants <set>` (the input N31a-50 adds) on fixed addresses
       returning per-variant results side by side. With three stock walls: three (mass, time) points with
       intervals and the non-dominated marks.
   11. *Rare overloads.* The same run with and without `--rare-rates`: the label `rare overloads: not
       included` in the second; a larger fatigue rate in the first.
   12. *Fallback.* `--mode hand-specified --wear-states <file>` with supplied wear states: variants returned;
       no time axis; no ratio. With the KILL-LIFECYCLE decision `fired` in a scratch gate register,
       `e2e_expect_refusal FROB-REF-724 5` on a time-axis request, asserting status `killed`, reason
       `KILL-LIFECYCLE` and the repair `--mode hand-specified` (N31a-50).
   12b. *The cluster contract on this verb (the lifecycle lane of N31ap3-51).* `foundry_cluster_checks
       foundry.lifecycle` from that bead's library, with the quick call `frob foundry lifecycle --fixture
       averaged-growth` and the long call of step 7: the job handle's progress is in macro steps with a
       denominator; a repeat under the same key replays; the same key with a changed horizon exits 7;
       `estimate_only` creates no job; a cancelled run stops at a macro-step boundary, names the last
       committed macro step and leaves no variant set and no run record; the envelope is class Estimated;
       the CLI and the MCP tool `foundry_lifecycle` agree; the verb's two worked examples run as printed.
   13. *Determinism lanes.* Steps 2 to 6 twice: identical records; step 7 twice on one backend: identical.
       Each repeat carries its own key (`foundry_key determinism-<step>`) and asserts `idempotent_replay:
       false`, so the comparison is between two computations, never a run and its stored receipt.
   14. Write the gate receipt (the compressed run's envelope with step 7b's and the controls' results);
       `summary`. The script writes no gate row itself (one recorder per gate): the register names this bead
       for GATE-FOUNDRY-12 (N31b-12), and the value is recorded from the receipt, by a session that did not
       implement the lifecycle crate, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate
       GATE-FOUNDRY-12 --receipt <hash>` (the gate recorder N38b-19).
3. **Gate receipt.** Steps of both runs and the ratio; per slow state the relative error at the four tabulated
   checkpoints, the largest error per slow-state family over all hourly marks, and the movement relative to
   threshold; the collapse results (toy: switch point, trigger, time error; robot: β at return, both collapse
   times); control results; the cards' `synthetic` label; the reference digest; step accounting split by mode;
   commit, backend, hardware fingerprint, seeds. A measurement that completes with a failing clause still
   writes its receipt, with status `fail` and the failing clause named, and that receipt is recorded like a
   passing one (it is what the kill decision reads); the script exits non-zero; a measurement that could not
   be completed writes no receipt and no row.
4. **Self-test** (`scripts/e2e/tests/foundry_lifecycle_selftest.sh`): mutants and failing steps:
   `average-inputs-then-apply-law` → 4; `calendar-fade-linear-in-t` → 5; `ignore-multiplier` → 6;
   `drop-rejected-steps-from-count` → 7 (the ratio no longer matches the independent step count from the
   job's telemetry); `finish-counter-per-window` → 7 (fatigue error above 5%); `rare-term-silently-zero` → 11;
   `report-ratio-as-speedup` → 2 (the wording check); `no-floquet-trigger` → 7b (the run steps across β*).
   The mutants `average-inputs-then-apply-law` (it must miss the insulation state by 20%: 2.0 against 2.5) and
   `finish-counter-per-window` (it must lose the half cycles that span windows) are also run on the gate
   fixture's insulation and fatigue families, where each must push that family's error above 5%.
5. **Logging.** Header `# e2e: bead=N31a-59 milestone=M6 labels=foundry,frob-lifecycle,moonshot
   gates=GATE-FOUNDRY-12,KILL-LIFECYCLE seed=12 needs=cpu plants=<the eight mutants of the self-test>`; sources
   `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E); `target/e2e-logs/foundry_lifecycle/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript.
   Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31a-59`), `step`, `event`, command line, exit code, wall time, the
   envelope's `evidence` block (class Estimated throughout). Per macro step: slow states, H, the error
   estimate, the multiplier, triggers, mode. Metrics with units and thresholds (5%, 50, 3.6 × 10⁶, 10%):
   `steps_reference`, `steps_compressed`, `compression`, per family `error_max`, `macro_steps`,
   `macro_rejected`, `returns_to_full_resolution` with triggers, `collapse_time_reference_h`,
   `collapse_time_compressed_h`, `beta_at_return` against β*, control results. On
   failure: the slow state and checkpoint with the largest error, both values, and the paths of the run
   record and the reference. `summary`: assertions passed and failed; `gates: ["GATE-FOUNDRY-12"]`.
6. **Runtime.** The stored reference is produced once per gate recording by a dedicated job (hours on the CPU
   tiers) and is not regenerated by this script; step 7's compressed run is minutes. Step 8 keeps the stored
   reference honest. **Envelope.** Hours of wall time exceed the default E0 (one hour), so before the first
   reference job this bead files its reserved row R24 in `registries/compute_envelope.toml` (N44-01's
   reservation table names this bead for R24: `packages = ["WP-75"]`, stated in WP-75's Envelope bullet in the same commit) with the job's estimate
   per backend: class E1 for `gate:GATE-FOUNDRY-12` while one reference run is at most 2 hours and all backends
   together at most 12 device-hours, otherwise the class that covers the estimate. Not-approved fallback:
   GATE-FOUNDRY-12 stays `not-run`, never `pass`, no compression figure is printed, and KILL-LIFECYCLE (N31b-23)
   records `deferred` with the reason `no envelope`. A reference job above the class in force is refused before
   it starts (`FROB-REF-761`).

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-750 (averaged growth: ε·a·T/2). KA-751 (wear feedback closed form; control: the planted collapse must
force full resolution before β*). KA-752 (2.5/τ_ref, not 2.0/τ_ref). KA-753 (cell fade 4.5%). CX-344 (a law

the potency run. Determinism lanes: reference and compressed runs are each identical twice on one backend
(class D2); the toys also in debug and release; across backends the comparison is a tolerance comparison and
is reported separately.

## Considerations

- OPEN-FOUNDRY-5 (wear, insulation and cell-fade coefficients with provenance) is read as follows, for its
  owner to confirm (N31b-28): the gate is a claim about the method and is measured on registered `synthetic`
  cards; what the missing data blocks is any output about a real design, which runs on generic cards marked
  `assumed`, says so, and does not run under `--strict-data`. Passing the gate says the method reproduces a
  reference under given laws; it says nothing about real service duration.
- The reference is the most expensive single run in Foundry; it is run once per backend and reused.
- If the actuator chapter's ageing kill gate fired, the cell states are user-set constants: the cell family is
  then excluded from the error clause and the row says so.
- Averaging can fail quietly near a gait bifurcation; the collapse clause is the gate's guard against a result
  that is accurate on average and wrong where it matters.
- A fidelity gate against a reference built from the same laws tests the averaging, not the laws. The record's
  wording keeps that distinction: "compressed run agrees with reference", never "predicts service duration".
- If the kill fires, step 12 is the capability and the gate is recorded as failed with the fallback's name.
