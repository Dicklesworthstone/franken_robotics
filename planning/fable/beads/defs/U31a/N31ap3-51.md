## Background

Foundry (structure and manufacture) is reached through seven verbs of one registry cluster: `foundry.loads`,
`foundry.size`, `foundry.optimize`, `foundry.check`, `foundry.tolerance`, `foundry.export` and
`foundry.lifecycle`. Two are `read` verbs (`size`, `tolerance`: they return facts, create no job and write
nothing); five are `run` verbs (they spend compute, take an idempotency key and a budget, wait synchronously
for at most `sync_wait_ms` and otherwise return a job handle with exit code 8). The plan's rule for the
cluster is one sentence: **long verbs take the bounded synchronous wait and otherwise return a job handle.**

Bead N31a-51 states the contract every handler of the cluster implements: exit codes, the envelope's
evidence and conditions, the job handle with a progress denominator in a unit per verb, idempotent replay,
the cost estimate and admission, cancellation at a boundary per verb, the named-fixture input, two worked
examples per verb, and the refusal a verb gives when the airlock's `structure` feature group is not in the
binary. The handlers are built by the capstone beads BD-400 to BD-408, which depend on N31a-51. **A contract
that no test drives through the real handlers is a table.** This bead is that test, for the six verbs whose
handlers exist at M5; it was split from N31a-51 in polishing round 3, because a test through the handlers
cannot belong to a bead the handlers wait on (the same reason the refusal battery N31ap1-51 was split from
N31a-52). `foundry.lifecycle` is a Moonshot whose handler lands at M6 under its own feature: its share of
these checks runs in its feature lane, by the lifecycle script N31a-59, with this bead's library.

What the registry's own conformance script already proves (`scripts/e2e/verbs_foundry.sh` of VERBS-foundry):
the rows exist on both surfaces, each registry example answers, a misspelt parameter is FROB-REF-802 and a
missing key FROB-REF-805. It does not exercise what is specific to this cluster's handlers: units of
progress, boundaries of cancellation, the class ceiling, the fixtures, the examples. That is the gap closed
here.

## Scope

In: a shell library with one function that runs the contract checks on one verb; this bead's script, which
runs it on the six M5 verbs; the Rust tests of the same contract in process; the execution of the twelve
worked examples of those verbs; CLI and MCP envelope equality on real results; the FROB-REF-790 lane on a
binary built without the `structure` group; three negative controls.
Out: the contract itself, the named-fixture resolver, the script preamble and the text of the examples
(N31a-51); the handlers (BD-400 to BD-408); the refusal payloads and the refusal battery (N31a-52,
N31ap1-51); the rows and their surface parity (VERBS-foundry); the lifecycle verb's run of the library
(N31a-59, which depends on this bead); the completeness check of the M5 verb set (BD-409, which cites this
bead's log).

## Technical approach

1. **Calls used** (every fixture is one a capstone bead registers in the cluster's fixture index and uses in
   its own script; `run` calls carry `--key "$(foundry_key <label>)"`, a new label for every call):

   | Verb | Quick call (finishes inside the default wait) | Long call (outlives `--sync-wait-ms 1`) | Progress unit; cancellation boundary |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `foundry.loads` | `frob foundry loads quad-ref --declared statics` | `frob foundry loads quad-ref --campaign camp-small` | episodes; kernel batch |
   | `foundry.size` (read) | `frob foundry size --fixture cantilever` | none | none |
   | `foundry.optimize` | `frob foundry optimize --fixture planted-gain --loop` | `frob foundry optimize --fixture twobar --rung topology --mode coarse --exploratory` | loop stages out of iterations × 8; stage or sibling-job boundary |
   | `foundry.check` | `frob foundry check --structure --fixture fatigue-block` | `frob foundry check --fixture cantilever --all` | cases; sibling-job boundary |
   | `foundry.tolerance` (read) | `frob foundry tolerance --fixture worked-case` | none | none |
   | `foundry.export` | `frob foundry export --fixture tetrahedron --geometry-only --format stl` | `frob foundry export quad-ref-final --packet <export root>/cluster-<n>` | files; file boundary |

   The last column is read from `CLUSTER_CONTRACT` (N31a-51), not typed again in the tests.
2. **The library** `scripts/e2e/lib/foundry_cluster.sh` (sourced after the STD-E2E helper and the preamble
   library of N31a-51). `foundry_cluster_checks <verb>` runs, as logged steps, on the calls of step 1:
   1. *Wait and handle* (`run` verbs). The long call with `--sync-wait-ms 1` (the CLI spelling of
      `sync_wait_ms`, NJL-01): exit 8; the handle has `id`,
      `state`, `poll_after_ms`, `last_committed` and `progress` with a non-zero denominator in the verb's
      unit; `frob job wait` then returns a result whose digest equals that of the same call made under
      another key with a wait long enough to finish.
   2. *Read verbs.* The quick call returns within the call; the job list, the ledger's row count and the
      store's digest are the same before and after.
   3. *Replay and conflict.* The quick call repeated under the same key (`foundry_key … --again`) returns the
      stored envelope with `idempotent_replay: true` and does no work (the job list and the ledger's row
      count are unchanged by the repeat); the same key with one changed input exits 7 (FROB-REF-803).
   4. *Estimate and admission.* The long call with `--estimate` (the CLI spelling of `estimate_only`,
      NJL-01) returns a cost estimate (CPU core-seconds and storage, with its interval) and creates no job;
      the long call with `--budget cpu_core_s=<n>` below the estimate is refused at admission with exit 4 (a code of the budget family, 701 to 704) and nothing
      is published: no job, no stage record, no file.
   5. *Cancellation.* The long call with `--sync-wait-ms 0`, then `frob job cancel <id>` at once and `frob job
      wait <id>`: the state is `cancelled`, the acknowledgement names the last committed unit in the verb's
      unit, and nothing partial exists (campaign: no case file beyond the last committed batch; loop: no
      stage record beyond the last committed stage and no new design in the lineage; check: no result
      record; export: no directory under the target name). If the job finished before the cancel landed the
      step is repeated under a new key, at most three times; three finishes in a row fail the step as
      `cancel-never-landed`, which is a defect of the fixture's size, never a pass.
   6. *Evidence.* On the quick call's envelope: class Estimated (the one exception, the firmware instance
      certificates inside an export's result, cannot occur at M5), `reality: no-claim`, `claims` empty, the
      three flags false on `foundry.export`, `bound` naming every identity the call resolved, and
      `next_actions` holding a command that parses against the generated CLI.
   7. *Fixture.* For the verbs whose quick call uses `--fixture`: the same content passed positionally (the
      object the index records for the name, addressed by its content hash) gives an envelope equal on
      `result` and `evidence`, and the fixture call's `bound` names the fixture and its hash.
   8. *Surfaces.* The quick call as the MCP tool of the row, sent with the MCP caller that the registry's
      conformance library uses (NJL-02, `verbs_lib.sh`), under its own key: the two envelopes agree on
      `verb`, `ok`, `exit`, `result`, `refusal`, `evidence`, `bound`, `warnings` and `next_actions`.
   9. *Examples.* The verb's two worked examples from the generated documentation, run as printed: the
      success example exits 0 or 2; the refusal example is checked with `assert_refusal` (N31a-52) for its
      code, pointer and first repair.
3. **Conditions.** Three labels must travel into the envelope's `evidence.conditions`: `exploratory` (the long
   optimize call's result, and a `foundry.check` of that result); `loads: declared` (the quick loads call's
   load set read by `foundry.size --loads <file>`); `model-conditional` (a `synthetic` load file whose
   governing case carries the label, read by `foundry.size --loads <file>`).
4. **The 790 lane.** A second `frob` binary is built without the airlock feature `frob-fsim/structure`
   (N33a-11's group; the lane first asserts with `frob capabilities --json` that the group is listed as not
   compiled, the report N33c-32 defines). On it: every call of `foundry.check` and
   `foundry.tolerance` answers FROB-REF-790, exit 5, naming the group and the rebuild as repair;
   `foundry.size --fixture cantilever` answers (the beam form needs no group) and the same with `--fe` is
   refused with 790; `frob foundry optimize quad-ref --campaign camp-small --loop` (no `--rung`; the
   planted-gain fixture has a synthetic rung and would not show the default) runs on the beam rung, its
   result naming the rung with the reason `feature-absent`, and the same with `--rung topology` is refused
   with 790; `foundry.loads` and `foundry.export` never answer 790 (their quick calls complete). One envelope per
   call on the CLI and on MCP.
5. **Rust tests** (`crates/frob-foundry/tests/cluster_contract.rs`): the same checks in process through the
   verb layer with the real handlers and a real daemon job queue on a temporary store, so that a failure
   is located without the shell.
6. **Lifecycle lane.** N31a-59 calls `foundry_cluster_checks foundry.lifecycle` with the quick call
   `frob foundry lifecycle --fixture averaged-growth` and the long call `frob foundry lifecycle --fixture
   gate-duty-100h --horizon-h 100` (progress in macro steps; cancellation at a macro-step boundary), and runs
   the two lifecycle examples; this bead ships the function and a `CLUSTER_CONTRACT` row to read, nothing
   that needs the Moonshot feature.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For each of the four M5 `run` verbs, `sync_wait_ms=1` on the long call exits 8 with a job handle whose progress denominator is in the verb's unit, and `frob job wait` returns the result of an uninterrupted call (equal digests).
- [ ] The two `read` verbs create no job and change neither the ledger's row count nor the store's digest.
- [ ] A repeat under the same key returns the stored envelope with `idempotent_replay: true` and does no work; the same key with changed content exits 7.
- [ ] `estimate_only` returns an estimate and creates no job; a budget below it is refused at admission with exit 4 and nothing published.
- [ ] Cancellation of each `run` verb's long call ends in `cancelled` with the last committed unit named and leaves nothing partial: no stage record beyond it, no new design, no packet directory.
- [ ] Every envelope of the six verbs has class Estimated, `reality: no-claim` and the conditions of its inputs (`exploratory`, `loads: declared`, `model-conditional` each shown once).
- [ ] A named fixture and its content passed positionally give equal results, and `bound` names the fixture.
- [ ] The quick call of each of the six verbs gives equal envelopes on the CLI and as its MCP tool.
- [ ] The twelve worked examples of the six verbs run as printed: six results, six refusals with their first repairs.
- [ ] On the binary without the `structure` group: 790 (exit 5) on every call of `foundry.check` and `foundry.tolerance`, on `foundry.size --fe` and on `foundry.optimize --rung topology`; the beam forms of `size` and `optimize` answer; `loads` and `export` never answer 790.
- [ ] The three planted defects are each caught at the named step.
- [ ] `foundry_cluster_checks` is callable for `foundry.lifecycle` by N31a-59 without change to the library.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry --test cluster_contract` (real handlers, real job queue, temporary store; fixtures
of the index, labelled `synthetic` where they are):
- `sync_wait_then_job_handle` × 4; `progress_has_denominator` × 4 (unit and total read against `CLUSTER_CONTRACT`).
- `read_verbs_create_no_job_and_write_nothing` × 2.
- `idempotent_replay` × 4; `key_conflict_exit_7`.
- `estimate_only_creates_no_job` × 4; `over_budget_refused_at_admission_exit_4`.
- `cancel_leaves_nothing` × 4 (one per `run` verb, at its boundary).
- `envelope_evidence_estimated` × 6; `conditions_propagate_to_envelope` (`exploratory`, `loads: declared`, `model-conditional`).
- `fixture_equals_positional` (the old `fixture_option_resolves_by_index` on a real handler); `bound_names_fixture`.
- `mcp_and_cli_envelopes_equal` × 6.
- `examples_execute` (12 examples).
- `group_absent_refuses_790` (the four rows, with the two predicate forms answering on their beam paths), built only in the lane without the group.
- Planted defects, each a `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch of the handler concerned with its row in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/controls.toml` (numbers from N38a-40), built with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-foundry/nc-<nnnn>` and never in a release build: `read-verb-that-writes` (`foundry.size` creating a ledger row: step 2), `job-progress-without-total` (a handle with no denominator: step 1), `cancel-publishes-partial` (the loop writing its stage record after the cancel was acknowledged: step 5).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_cluster_conformance.sh` (header `bead=N31ap3-51 milestone=M5
labels=foundry,tether,verb:foundry.* gates= seed=1 needs=cpu,mcp,frobd`; no `plants` key, since the three
planted defects are compiled mutations run as negative controls in every run, not failures the script injects
under `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE`, which `run-all.sh --verify-plants` would require to fail the script): `start`; the preamble of
N31a-51; `foundry_cluster_checks` for `foundry.loads`, `foundry.size`, `foundry.optimize`, `foundry.check`,
`foundry.tolerance` and `foundry.export`; the three conditions of step 3; the 790 lane of step 4 when the
second binary is present (its path in `FROB_E2E_BIN_NO_STRUCTURE`, DESIGN: a variable of this script alone;
absent, the lane is an `e2e_skip` with its reason and the summary says so); the three planted defects through `e2e_expect_caught`, each running its named step on a `frob` built
with `--features gauntlet-mutations,frob-foundry/nc-<nnnn>` (an absent build is an `e2e_skip` with its reason,
and the summary says so); `summary`. NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/foundry_cluster_conformance/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N31ap3-51`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the
envelope's `evidence` block; per verb and check an `assert` with observed and expected values (progress unit
and total, both result digests, `idempotent_replay`, job counters before and after, ledger rows and store
digest before and after, the cancelled job's last committed unit and the list of paths checked for absence);
metrics `wait_to_handle_ms` and `cancel_latency_ms` with their units; refusals with code, pointer and first
repair; on failure the verb, the check, the first differing field with both values and the paths of the two
saved envelopes; a `summary` line with assertions passed and failed, verbs covered (6 of 6), plants caught
(3 of 3), the 790 lane's status and `gates: []`.

### Known answers and negative controls
No numeric known answer: the expected values are the units and boundaries of `CLUSTER_CONTRACT` and the exit
codes of the contract (0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8). Negative controls: the three planted defects; a result digest
compared against itself (the uninterrupted call must run under another key, which the script asserts by
`idempotent_replay: false`). Determinism: the script's assertions are equal on two runs; timings are logged
and not compared.

## Considerations

- Kill gates change which calls exist, not the checks. KILL-LOADS fired: the long loads call answers
  FROB-REF-724 with `--declared statics` offered, and the wait and cancellation checks of `foundry.loads` are
  recorded `not applicable: KILL-LOADS`. KILL-TOPOPT fired: the long optimize call is `--rung beam --loop` on
  the reference quadruped's small campaign. KILL-PACKET fired: the long export call adds `--profile reduced`.
- The long calls are the slow part (a 16-world campaign, one coarse solve, one packet): minutes on the CPU.
  Nothing here needs a GPU.
- A cancellation test races the job by nature; the retry rule of step 5 makes a lost race visible instead of
  silently passing, and the fixture sizes are this bead's to keep large enough.
- The 790 lane needs a second build. Where the build farm does not provide it the lane is skipped and
  logged, and BD-409's completeness check treats the skip as not measured.
- Private-scene data: the checks log identities, digests and counts only.
