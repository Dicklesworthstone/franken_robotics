## Background

The claim rules of the project say that conditions travel: flags such as `tier-sensitive`,
`controller-sensitive` and `model-conditional` are joined by union along every chain of evidence and cannot
be dropped by a consumer, and **a model-conditional result may not spend prototype budget**. A ranking of
finalist designs is typed `model-conditional` by the model-form rank check (verb `fidelity.modelform`, and
stage 5 of the co-design cascade) when the order of the finalists changes under another admissible modelling
choice: the alternative contact law, the circular against the pyramidal friction cone, contact stiffness at
half and at twice its value, half the step, a tenth of the solver tolerance, or when a finalist's advantage
coincides with energy the simulator created. The plan states the consequences three times in the same words:
such a result cannot be promoted to `established`, cannot authorize a build packet, and cannot spend
prototype budget; an attempt is refused with FROB-REF-830 ("a `model-conditional` result offered for
promotion, build or prototype budget"; ranked repair: resolve the variant that flips the ranking; report
both rankings). The cascade's text is explicit about what "spend" means: *it cannot spend prototype budget,
so a build-packet request is refused.*

The refusal has three raising sites. Promotion is carried by the rank check and the promotion verb. The
generic test "is this result good enough for this use" lives in the claim-product admission (`admit(use,
product)`), which already has a row for the use `prototype-spend` whose forbidden conditions are
`model-conditional` and `tier-sensitive`. **Nothing in the manufacturing subsystem (Foundry) calls it.** The
packet exporter (`foundry.export`) checks that the design holds a qualification verdict (FROB-REF-166), and a
qualification verdict is about one design on sealed tasks: it says nothing about whether the choice of this
design over its rivals was an artifact of a modelling choice. Without this bead the North-Star scenario "the
exploit is caught" ends one step early: the exploit is labelled, and the design it favoured can still be
sent to a machine shop.

Terms. A *build packet* (`.fbuild`, schema `frob.build/1`) is the directory of geometry, parts, tolerances,
mass properties, harness, schedule and evidence that a shop could build from; it always carries four
no-authorization flags, all false. A *selection record* is a stored result of the model-form rank check
(schema `frob.modelform/1`: the ranking, k, the variants with their top-k overlap, the energy audit, the
verdict `model-robust` or `model-conditional`, `flipped_by`). The *structure loop* changes link mass and
inertia through a typed edit, so the design that is exported usually has a different `DesignId` from the
design that was ranked. Tier: Frontier (the packet is Frontier; the rule itself is a claim rule).

Rejected alternatives: printing the label on the packet and exporting anyway (a consumer has then dropped a
condition by acting on it); refusing only when the caller passes the ranking explicitly (the refusal would be
avoided by not mentioning the ranking); a separate "prototype budget" account with its own verb (the plan
names no such object; the packet request is the act of spending, and any later verb that commits prototype
money registers the same precondition).

## Scope

In: the precondition of a packet request that finds the selection records of the design and applies the
`prototype-spend` admission to them; the lineage walk from the exported design to the ranked design; the
resolution rule; the refusal payload; the manifest field that records what was checked; the same check for
`tier-sensitive`, which the same admission row forbids; the rule on that field in the two packet checkers:
the packet reader of N31a-36 (in `frob-foundry`) and the manifest checker of `frob-verify` (N31b-44, in
`frob-cert`), each rejecting a manifest whose `selection.status` is absent or has a value outside the two,
with a specimen manifest per case.

Out: the rank check and its label (BD-266, BD-346); the claim-product admission table and its codes
(N37-26); the qualification precondition and the hook this bead plugs into (N31a-03); refusal at promotion
(the rank check's own acceptance); the packet container and assembler (N31a-36, N31a-43), which runs this
precondition in its stated order; the through-the-binary assertions, which need the export verb that comes
after this bead (the steps `selection-*` of N31a-57).

## Technical approach

Layout (DESIGN): `crates/frob-foundry/src/admission/selection.rs`; registered through the precondition hook
that packet admission exposes (`fn(&PacketInputs) -> Result<(), Refusal>`).

1. **Find the ranked ancestor.** From the packet's `DesignId` walk the lineage graph backwards across edges
   whose kind is the structure loop's inertia-feedback edit or `migrate` (edits that do not change which
   candidate this is) until a design is reached that appears in some stored ranking, or the walk ends. Any
   other edit kind stops the walk: a design changed by hand after the ranking is a new candidate.
2. **Collect selection records.** All `frob.modelform/1` results in the ledger, not withdrawn, whose ranking
   contains the ranked ancestor among its top k. Also the comparison verdicts that prefer the ancestor over
   another finalist and carry conditions.
3. **Resolve.** A `model-conditional` record R is *resolved* when a later record R′ exists over a ranking
   whose top k contains the same design, with verdict `model-robust`, in which every variant named in
   R.`flipped_by` was admitted and run (an arm listed `not-run` resolves nothing), and whose energy audit is
   clean if R was flagged by the audit. Later is by ledger commit order, never by wall-clock time.
4. **Admit.** For each unresolved record call `admit(Use::PrototypeSpend, product)` with the record's claim
   product. A forbidden condition returns its own code: `model-conditional` → FROB-REF-830;
   `tier-sensitive` → FROB-REF-819 (a result cited above its claim product), since no narrower code exists.
   The first failure in record order is returned; all failing records are listed in the payload.
5. **Refusal payload (exit code 3, `retry: after-change`).** `code: FROB-REF-830`; `pointer`: the selection
   record's identifier; `observed`: `{verdict: "model-conditional", flipped_by, order_base, order_variant,
   design, ranked_ancestor}` (both rankings are always shown); `required`: "a model-robust ranking over the
   finalists, or no ranking at all"; `repairs`, ranked: (1) resolve the variant that flips the ranking, with
   the exact command of the settling experiment the record names in its `next_actions` (for a law flip the
   same finalists on the referee; for a step flip a step-refinement study; for a stiffness flip a
   contact-parameter identification; for an energy flag the offending contact mode and world); (2) report
   both rankings (`report.campaign`), which is a comparison report and not a packet.
6. **No ranking at all.** A design that was never ranked has nothing conditional; the precondition passes
   and the manifest records `selection: {status: "none-recorded"}`. The qualification precondition still
   applies. This is stated in the packet so that a reader does not take silence for robustness.
7. **Manifest.** `manifest.json` gains `selection: {status: "model-robust" | "none-recorded", records:
   [ids], frontier}`: the records read and the ledger frontier they were read at. No packet is written with
   any other status: the type has two values.
8. **`--exploratory` and geometry-only export.** Unchanged in what they allow (loose geometry, labelled);
   the envelope's `evidence.conditions` carries `model-conditional` by union, so the label still travels.
9. **Evidence.** The precondition is a deterministic function of ledger records (D0). The packet stays
   class `Estimated` at most, as every Foundry output does.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Through the admission hook, the packet precondition on a qualified design whose ranking is
      `model-conditional` returns FROB-REF-830 (exit 3 when rendered) with both orders and the flipping
      variant in the payload. (Through `frob foundry export --packet`, with no packet directory written, it is
      asserted by N31a-57's steps `selection-*`.)
- [ ] The same request after a later `model-robust` check that ran the flipping variant passes, and the
      manifest block it returns lists both records with `status: "model-robust"`.
- [ ] A later check in which the flipping arm is `not-run` does not resolve; the refusal stands.
- [ ] The design produced by the structure loop from a conditionally ranked design is refused the same way
      (`ranked_ancestor` names the original); a design changed by a hand edit after the ranking is not.
- [ ] A never-ranked, qualified design exports with `selection.status: "none-recorded"`.
- [ ] A `tier-sensitive` selection record is refused with FROB-REF-819.
- [ ] Planted defects are each caught: `skip-selection-check`; `resolve-by-any-later-record`;
      `lineage-walk-stops-at-first-edit`; `wall-clock-order`.
- [ ] The reader and `frob-verify` reject a manifest whose `selection.status` is absent or has another value
      (the specimen manifests), and accept the two allowed values.
- [ ] The refusal is in the verb's registered refusal list (830 and 819 in the `foundry.export` row of VERBS-foundry, repeated by N31a-51); its
      reachability through `frob --json` and MCP is asserted by N31a-57.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Counterexample this protects (CX-258): a ranking found under a pyramidal (box) friction cone is not a
  ranking; a planted box-cone exploit flips a planted ranking and is typed `model-conditional`, while an
  honest policy on the circular cone is not flagged. Here: the exploit's design cannot reach a packet; the
  honest design can.
- Threshold used: top-k overlap at least k − 1 under every admitted variant, with a clean energy audit.
- Negative controls: the four planted defects of the acceptance list.
- Determinism: the precondition's outcome and the manifest's `selection` block are compared exactly across
  two runs and across debug and release.

## Considerations

- The rank check may be demoted to a periodic audit (overlap at least 7 of 8 in 95% of 20 campaigns with no
  energy flag). A campaign that was not audited has no selection record: `none-recorded`, stated as such.
- The lineage walk rule (which edit kinds preserve "the same candidate") is a DESIGN choice; widening it is
  safe (more refusals), narrowing it needs a reason.
- GATE-FOUNDRY-10's packet fixtures include one conditional selection so that the gate exercises the
  refusal; the gate's script (N31a-57) owns the fixture list and carries the steps.
- No real-world authorization is implied by passing: the four flags stay false.
