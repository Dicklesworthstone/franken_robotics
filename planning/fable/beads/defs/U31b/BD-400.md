## Background

Foundry is the subsystem (crates `frob-foundry` and `frob-lifecycle`, layer 5) that turns a qualified design
into something that could be built. It improves the output and is not a speed lever: no part of the program's
ten-fold thesis rests on it, and its topology rung, finite-element checks and build packet run on qualified
finalists only. Its first step is the *structure loop*: load cases from telemetry → sizing → topology
optimization → an independent check → mass and inertia fed back into the model → repeat until converged.

This bead is the first box of that loop. A link cannot be sized against "the worst torque" and "the worst
foot force" taken separately: structure fails under a combination of loads that occurred together. The
simulator already records what happened: Chronicle's control-rate traces (telemetry level T2) hold joint
constraint wrenches, contact wrenches and body twists. This bead reads them and produces, per link, *envelope
cases* (real instants that are extreme in some direction of load space, used for sizing and optimization) and
*spectra* (signed stress histories at gauges in episode order, used for fatigue).

Decisions that bind it. ADR-FOUNDRY-03: a load case is one instant of one episode; peaks from different
instants are never assembled, because the assembled case can be unsafe (counterexample below). ADR-FOUNDRY-04:
symmetric limbs share one optimization only after their load histories are transformed into one frame and
unioned, because an offset payload loads nominally identical limbs unequally. Alternatives rejected: static
load cases declared by hand with a dynamic factor (kept only as the fallback of KILL-LOADS, because it ignores
what the missions actually did); per-channel peak envelopes (unsafe); interpolating across telemetry gaps
(invents loads). Tier: Frontier [F]; its pilot (N31b-06) must have recorded its verdict first.

FACT (FrankenSim at commit `483d09c682`, paths under `frankensim/crates/`): `fs-solid` provides small-strain
elasticity on body-fitted tetrahedra (`linear3`), consistent surface and volume loads (`loads3`), load
histories sampled to quasi-static cases (`loads3::history`) and sparse average-acceleration dynamics with an
energy check (`loads3::transient`). It has no path from telemetry to load cases, no multibody interface, and
does not turn a port wrench into interface tractions. Those are this bead's own code. `fs-solid` depends on
`fs-qty` and is reached through the airlock; Foundry jobs are whole jobs of seconds to minutes, so the
worker-process route suits them.

## Technical approach

Module layout (DESIGN): `crates/frob-foundry/src/loads/{campaign,sample,freebody,coords,influence,envelope,
spectrum,interface,variants}.rs`.

**1. The load campaign.** The qualified task set plus push-recovery and drop tasks, run with T2 on every world
at fixed world addresses, so the campaign is reproducible and is paired across loop iterations (BD-404 depends
on this). Record (DESIGN): `LoadCampaign { id, design: DesignId, model: ModelId, tasks, addresses:
Vec<WorldAddr>, tier, profile, contact_variant, hours: H_c, samples, dropped }`. `foundry.loads` either runs
the campaign (a job) or reads an existing one by identity.

**2. The load on a link at one instant.** For each port p (a joint interface, or for a foot or wheel the
contact patch): the wrench (f_p, m_p) in the link frame at the port origin r_p. The rigid-body acceleration
field is a(x) = a₀ + α × x + ω × (ω × x), with gravity folded into a₀ (a₀ is the acceleration of the link-frame
origin minus g), α the angular acceleration and ω the angular velocity. Since ω × (ω × x) = (ω·ωᵀ − ‖ω‖²·I)·x,
the field is linear in twelve numbers: a₀ (3), α (3) and the six products ω_i·ω_j, i ≤ j.

**3. Free-body check.** Port wrenches and the body load −ρ·a(x) sum to zero in force and moment. With m the
link mass, c its centre of mass, I_c its inertia about c and a_c = a(c):
residual force R_f = Σ_p f_p − m·a_c; residual moment (about the link origin)
R_m = Σ_p (r_p × f_p + m_p) − (c × m·a_c + I_c·α + ω × I_c·ω).
Relative residual ε = max(‖R_f‖/F*, ‖R_m‖/(F*·ℓ)), with F* = max(Σ_p ‖f_p‖, m·‖a₀‖) and ℓ the largest port
distance from the origin (DESIGN scaling). ε is recorded per sample. Tolerance: 10⁻⁶ on the referee, 10⁻³ on
single-precision tiers (TARGET, the gate's numbers). A sample above tolerance is dropped and counted; more
than 0.1% dropped (TARGET) refuses the campaign with FROB-REF-167 and the offending samples.

**4. Load coordinates and unit loads.** The structural model is linear, so stress anywhere is linear in the
load coordinates: six per port except the port used as a statically determinate 3-2-1 restraint (its wrench
follows from equilibrium), plus twelve for the body field. A two-port link has 18 coordinates and a three-port
link 24. One finite-element solve per coordinate (through the airlock) gives a *stress influence matrix* S
(gauge planes × coordinates); the signed stress history at any gauge over a whole campaign is the matrix
product S·L with L the campaign's coordinate history. Proof obligation PO-FOUNDRY-01: superposition reproduces
a direct solve of any campaign sample (10³ random samples, relative difference at most 10⁻⁹ on the referee).

**5. Envelope cases.** For a fixed, deterministic set of directions d_k in scaled load space, the case is the
campaign sample that maximizes d_k·(L/s). Default direction set (DESIGN): ± each coordinate axis (36 for a
two-port link, 48 for three ports), plus any directions declared in the campaign record. Scaling s_j (DESIGN):
the largest absolute entry of column j of S when S exists, otherwise the 99.9th percentile of |L_j| over the
campaign; s is stored. Ties resolve to the smallest (world address, episode, tick). Every case is a real
instant with all ports and the body field from the same sample, and it stores that sample's reference
(INV-FOUNDRY-02: each case replays to its sample).

**6. Spectra.** Signed stress histories at gauges, in episode order, from S·L. Episode order follows the
campaign's duty sequence; boundaries between episodes are kept as markers and not as resets (BD-402 carries
rainflow residues across them).

**7. Rules.**
1. *Time-coherent cases only.* A case that mixes instants is refused (FROB-REF-167). Counterexample: a simply
   supported span L carries P at L/3 at one instant and P at 2L/3 at another; the mid-span moment is P·L/6
   each time; the assembled case gives P·L/3, and with the second load of opposite sign it gives 0 while each
   real case gives ±P·L/6.
2. *Symmetric limbs are unioned, not assumed equal.* Each limb's history is transformed into the canonical
   limb frame: under a mirror with matrix P (det P = −1) forces transform as polar vectors, f ↦ P·f, and
   moments as axial ones, m ↦ det(P)·P·m. The cases of all limbs of a class are unioned.
3. *Impact loads carry a model-form label.* Peak contact forces depend on contact stiffness. The campaign is
   re-extracted with stiffness halved and doubled and under the alternative contact law. The envelope over the
   variants is the load set, and a peak that moves by more than 20% (TARGET) marks the case
   `model-conditional`, a label the structural margin inherits.
4. *Quasi-static superposition needs separation of frequencies.* It is used only when the link's first
   natural frequency f₁ is at least five times the highest frequency carrying 99% of load power (TARGET; the
   dynamic amplification 1/(1 − (f/f₁)²) is then at most 1.042). Otherwise impact windows go through the
   transient path of `fs-solid`; FROB-REF-262 if that path is not enabled.
5. *A port wrench becomes tractions by a declared interface pattern* on the port boss: a traction field affine
   in position whose resultant force and moment equal the wrench exactly (PO-FOUNDRY-02). Construction
   (DESIGN): on the boss patch Γ of area A, centroid x̄ and second-moment tensor J = ∫_Γ (x − x̄)(x − x̄)ᵀ dA,
   t(x) = f/A + w × (x − x̄) with w = (tr(J)·I − J)⁻¹·m_c and m_c the moment about x̄. Stress within one boss
   diameter of an interface is `no-claim`; bolted joints and press fits are out of scope.
6. *Missing samples refuse the metric* (FROB-REF-169). Nothing is interpolated across a telemetry gap.

**8. The verb.** `foundry.loads` (kind `run`; registry first milestone M4, delivered with Foundry at M5; long
runs take the bounded synchronous wait and otherwise return a job handle, exit 8). Inputs: design, campaign
definition or identity, link selector, variants, `--transient`. Output (schema `frob.foundry.loads/1`, name
DESIGN) per link class: envelope cases (sample reference with world address, episode and tick; coordinates;
direction; variant; labels), spectra handles with H_c, free-body statistics (maximum, 99th percentile, dropped
fraction), the frequency-separation ratio, and the scale vector. Evidence: class Estimated (INV-FOUNDRY-06);
tier and determinism class of the campaign; claims empty. Recorded in the ledger: campaign identity, the
direction set, the scale vector, per-case sample references, residual statistics.

**9. Refusals.** FROB-REF-167 (a load case that is not one instant, or a campaign failing the free-body check;
repair: the offending samples and residuals); FROB-REF-169 (telemetry without the traces or samples a load
metric needs; repair: the telemetry level and worlds required); FROB-REF-262 (quasi-static superposition where
the frequency separation fails; repair: enable the transient path for impact windows). All exit 3.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-02 (TARGET): free-body residual at most 10⁻⁶ relative on the referee and 10⁻³ on single-precision tiers over the gate campaign; superposition equals direct solves on 10³ random samples to 10⁻⁹ relative (PO-FOUNDRY-01); the assembled-peak and equal-limb mutations are refused. Measured values are recorded in the gate row.
- [ ] Every envelope case replays to its sample: `frob sim replay` at the stored address and tick reproduces the port wrenches bit for bit on the same backend (INV-FOUNDRY-02).
- [ ] The traction pattern returns the port wrench to rounding (PO-FOUNDRY-02).
- [ ] A campaign with more than 0.1% dropped samples is refused with FROB-REF-167; a telemetry gap gives FROB-REF-169; a failed frequency separation gives FROB-REF-262 unless `--transient` is set.
- [ ] A peak that moves by more than 20% across the stiffness and contact-law variants is labelled `model-conditional` and the label reaches the structural margin.
- [ ] `foundry.loads` is reachable through `frob --json` with schema and exit codes; outputs carry class Estimated; cancellation of a campaign job leaves no partial campaign record.
- [ ] The gate receipt is handed to the kill-gate decision N31b-16 (the seed's "KILL-LOADS decided" is discharged by that bead).
- [ ] Standing conditions: the code is in `frob-foundry`; its `CONTRACT.md` covers this module in the ten sections (purpose, inputs, outputs, invariants, refusals, determinism class, evidence class of outputs, validity domain, oracles, known gaps); known answers run against the hand derivations, never against stored outputs of this code; every output carries its claim product and the no-authorization flags; the computation is CPU-only and claims no device path; the gate row carries measured values with commit, hardware fingerprint and seed.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry loads`:
- `coordinate_count`: two ports → 18; three ports → 24.
- `body_field_is_linear_in_twelve`: for random a₀, α, ω and ten points x, the field rebuilt from the twelve coordinates equals a₀ + α × x + ω × (ω × x) to 10⁻¹⁵ relative.
- `free_body_static_link`: a 2 kg link at rest with two ports each carrying 9.81 N upward: R_f = 0, R_m = 0.
- `free_body_spinning_link`: a uniform rod of 1 kg and 0.4 m spinning at 10 rad/s about one end: port force m·ω²·L/2 = 20 N toward the axis; residual below 10⁻¹².
- `envelope_case_is_one_sample`; `direction_set_is_deterministic`; `tie_breaks_to_smallest_address`.
- `assembled_peaks_refused`: P = 90 N, L = 0.6 m: 9 N·m per instant; the assembled 18 N·m case is refused with FROB-REF-167.
- `mirror_transform`: mirror y ↦ −y maps f = (1, 2, 3) to (1, −2, 3) and m = (4, 5, 6) to (−4, 5, −6).
- `offset_payload_limbs_unequal` (synthetic): 133.33 N against 116.67 N by side; the union contains both.
- `dropped_fraction_threshold`: 1 dropped in 1,000 is accepted; 2 in 1,000 is refused.
- `frequency_separation`: ratio 5 gives amplification 1.0417 and passes; ratio 4.9 gives FROB-REF-262.
- `model_conditional_threshold`: a peak moving 21% is labelled; 19% is not.
- `traction_returns_wrench` (PO-FOUNDRY-02): random wrenches on an annular boss; resultant force and moment within 10⁻¹² relative.
- `superposition_equals_direct` (PO-FOUNDRY-01, feature `structure`): 10³ random samples, relative difference at most 10⁻⁹.
- `telemetry_gap_refuses_169`; `no_interpolation_across_gap`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `assemble-peaks`, `assume-equal-limbs`, `moments-as-polar` (mirror applied to moments without the determinant), `drop-omega-products` (centrifugal term omitted from the body field).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_loads.sh`: (1) `frob foundry loads --fixture span-two-instants --json` and assert two
cases of P·L/6; (2) the same with the `assemble-peaks` build, assert exit 3 and FROB-REF-167; (3) `frob
foundry loads quad-ref --campaign gate-foundry-02 --tier referee --json` and assert the residual bound 10⁻⁶,
then `--tier gpu` with 10⁻³; (4) `--check-superposition 1000` and assert 10⁻⁹; (5) a campaign with a planted
telemetry gap, assert FROB-REF-169; (6) for three envelope cases run `frob sim replay` at the stored address
and compare wrench digests. Logs per STD-E2E to `target/e2e-logs/foundry_loads/`: per step the command, exit
code, wall time, evidence block; metrics `free_body_residual_max` (threshold 1e-6 or 1e-3),
`dropped_fraction` (0.001), `superposition_rel_diff_max` (1e-9), `cases`, `model_conditional_cases`; the
summary names GATE-FOUNDRY-02.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-740 (assembled peaks: P·L/6 per instant; assembled P·L/3 or 0; control: the assembling mutation is
refused). KA-754 (with f₁ at least five times the loaded frequency the amplification is at most 1.042).
Counterexamples CX-336 and CX-337. Metamorphic: scaling every recorded wrench and the body field by k scales
every coordinate by k and leaves the chosen samples unchanged; relabelling limbs by a symmetry of the robot
permutes the cases and leaves the union unchanged; splitting the campaign into shards and merging gives the
same cases. Determinism: twice on one machine identical; identical across worker counts; cases from a
single-precision tier are compared with the referee inside the stated residual, not bit for bit.

## Considerations

- KILL-LOADS (decided at M5; the chapter allowed M4): GATE-FOUNDRY-02 fails after one repair cycle. Fallback: load cases are declared by hand from statics with a stated dynamic factor; spectra and fatigue read `no-claim`. The `statics` load set of the beam rung is that path.
- A load campaign runs on a fast tier, so every case is `provisional` until re-extracted on the referee (N31b-35). This bead must store, per case, everything that re-extraction needs: world address, episode, tick and direction.
- Cost: T2 on every world is far more telemetry than a normal run keeps (T2 is normally an address-keyed sample). The campaign record states its telemetry volume; the campaign is sized to the device budget.
- The `model-conditional` label here is the model-form label of the trust ladder. DESIGN reading: a labelled case may enter a packet with the label printed, because the load set is already the envelope over the variants; it may not support a claim stronger than Estimated.
- Results hold for the load envelope they were given: an edit that changes mass, geometry or the task set invalidates the cases.
