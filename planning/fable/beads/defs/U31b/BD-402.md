## Background

Foundry's structure loop sizes and optimizes links from simulated load cases. This bead is the loop's
independent check: before any geometry is fed back into the model or written into a build packet, it is
checked for stress, buckling, natural frequency and cumulative fatigue damage by a computation the optimizer
did not perform.

Why a separate check (ADR-FOUNDRY-05): the optimizer never checks its own result. The optimizer's stress
measure is a relaxed aggregate that is not a bound on the maximum, and thresholding the density field into a
surface changes the structure. So stress, buckling, modes and fatigue are evaluated on a separately generated
body-fitted tetrahedral mesh of the extracted surface, a different discretization from the optimizer's cut
cells. The independence is of mesh and formulation, not of codebase: FrankenSim's `fs-topopt` depends on
`fs-solid`, so both paths share a solver family, and the cantilever and two-bar known answers anchor both.

Why fatigue is worded carefully (ADR-FOUNDRY-06): fatigue is reported as *utilization* under a named empirical
law with card provenance, class Estimated. The word "life" is not used for a result. The sibling library says
the same of itself: its fatigue module is an empirical baseline, "not a validated component-life claim", and
its observers are elastic, small-strain and rate-independent ("time labels do not turn the quasistatic solve
into dynamics"). Published fatigue analyses of legged robots concern gears; no report of a link failing by
fatigue was found. The check exists because an optimizer that removes material can create the problem.

FACT (FrankenSim at commit `483d09c682`): `fs-solid` has small-strain elasticity on body-fitted tetrahedra with
consistent stiffness and mass (`linear3`), tensile assessment (`strength`), buckling pencils with eigenvalue
derivatives (`stability`), and fatigue observation with fixed-plane stress-life gauges whose rainflow residues
persist across calls and a critical-plane utilization on closed windows (`fatigue`). `fs-material` has
streaming rainflow with Palmgren–Miner damage (`RainflowMiner`), stress-life curves with an explicit reference
stress ratio (`SnCurve`) and named mean-stress corrections; unclosed reversals survive across input blocks and
`finish` is called once, at the end of the whole observation. Gauges are placed by the caller: placing them is
this bead's work. `fs-mesh` has Delaunay tetrahedralization with quality refinement and audits; its
documentation lists full boundary conformity as successor scope.

Tier: Frontier [F]. Serves bet BET-29 (generative structure) and analysis A-15 (structural margin).

## Technical approach

Modules (DESIGN): `crates/frob-foundry/src/check/{mesh,stress,buckling,modes,fatigue,gauges,record}.rs`.

**0. Mesh.** A body-fitted tetrahedral mesh of the extracted surface through `fs-mesh`, with its audit. The
mesh hash is stored in the result and later cited by the packet manifest (INV-FOUNDRY-03: no geometry enters a
packet without passing the independent check on a mesh the optimizer did not use). Boundary conformity is
measured per mesh (largest distance from mesh boundary to surface) and recorded with the evidence. If meshing
fails, the fail-closed default of OPEN-FOUNDRY-1 applies: a second cut-cell solve at a different resolution
and background offset, with every result labelled `check: same-family`.

**1. Stress.** Solved for every envelope case, plus the instants that govern each gauge under the stress
influence matrix of the final geometry. Utilization per element u = σ_vm/(S_y/SF), with σ_vm the von Mises
stress, S_y the card's yield strength and SF the declared safety factor. The mesh is refined until the peak
moves by under 5% between refinements (TARGET). A peak at a re-entrant corner that does not converge is
reported as `singular: fillet required`, with the fillet radius as the repair, and is not reported as a
number.

**2. Buckling.** The first positive load factor from `stability` (the smallest positive eigenvalue of the
pencil of elastic and geometric stiffness) is at least 2 (TARGET) in every envelope case.

**3. Modes.** The first natural frequency with the link's end masses clears its floor: the actuator bandwidth
requirement from the modes analysis A-12.

**4. Cumulative fatigue damage.**
- *Gauges.* At the 16 highest-stress locations per unit load (TARGET), deduplicated, and at declared notches.
  Each gauge carries a set of planes (DESIGN: for a surface gauge, in-plane directions at 10° steps; the plane
  with the largest damage governs).
- *History.* The signed normal stress on each gauge plane, from the influence matrix times the campaign's load
  coordinates, in episode order.
- *Counting.* `RainflowMiner` across the whole campaign. Damage D = Σ nᵢ/N(Sᵢ) over counted cycles (linear
  Palmgren–Miner rule), with N(S) from the card's stress-life curve at its reference stress ratio and the
  named mean-stress correction.
- *Utilization.* With D accumulated over H_c campaign hours, utilization for a service duration H is
  U_f = (D/H_c)·H, against a limit of 1 divided by the card's scatter factor.
- *Rules.* (a) Signed stress only: unsigned von Mises is never fed to a cycle counter; the spectrum type has no
  constructor from an unsigned equivalent stress. (b) Residues are carried between episodes and `finish` is
  called once, at the end of the campaign. (c) The mean-stress policy is named in the result. (d) The curve
  must be valid for the material state, the process and the surface finish; otherwise the result is
  `fatigue: no-claim` (FROB-REF-163). (e) Cycles that span tasks (a loaded leg, then an unloaded one) are
  counted, because the campaign's duty sequence is part of the history.

**5. Class.** Every number here is Estimated. A structural margin enters a claim product as its weakest input
and inherits the labels of its governing load case (`model-conditional`, `provisional`, `tier-sensitive`).

**6. Result record** (DESIGN): `StructureCheck { geometry: Blake3, mesh: Blake3, conformity, card, cases:
[{case, u_peak, location, converged | singular}], buckling: [{case, factor}], modes: [f₁, …], fatigue:
[{gauge, plane, damage, hours_campaign, utilization(H), law, mean_stress_policy, curve}] | NoClaim(reason),
labels, class: Estimated }`. A part fails when any u_peak > 1, any buckling factor < 2, f₁ below its floor, or
any fatigue utilization above its limit: refusal FROB-REF-261 when the caller is the optimizer or the packet
writer (repair: the governing case and location; a tighter stress cap or a larger length scale), and a finding
with exit 2 when the caller asked for a report.

**7. Surface.** Library entry `check_structure(geometry, card, load_set, service_hours) → StructureCheck`, and
the structural half of the verb `foundry.check` (kind `run`, M5; refusals 163 and 261). A long check returns a
job handle.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-03 (TARGET): the fatigue block gives damage 0.116 per block and 8.62 blocks to a sum of 1, and the rainflow sequence gives ranges 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 with counts 0.5, 1.5, 0.5, 1.0, 0.5 and damage 8.752 × 10⁻⁶, each exact to 10⁻¹² relative; the window-split mutation reproduces 7.816 × 10⁻⁶ (10.7% low) and is caught.
- [ ] Feeding unsigned stress is refused at the type boundary (it does not compile) and at the verb boundary (a planted unsigned history is rejected).
- [ ] The cantilever and planted-overstress fixtures pass through this check with the same numbers as the beam form: 30 MPa within 5% after refinement; utilization 1.25 ± 5% at the root, reported as FROB-REF-261 to the optimizer.
- [ ] A buckling factor below 2, a first frequency below its floor and a fatigue utilization above its limit each fail the part with the governing case and location.
- [ ] A card whose curve is not valid for the material state, process or finish yields `fatigue: no-claim` (FROB-REF-163) while stress, buckling and modes still answer.
- [ ] Every result carries the mesh hash, the mean-stress policy, the law's name and class Estimated; the mesh is not the optimizer's.
- [ ] Cancellation of a running check leaves no partial result record.
- [ ] Standing conditions: the code is in `frob-foundry`; its `CONTRACT.md` covers this module in the ten sections (purpose, inputs, outputs, invariants, refusals, determinism class, evidence class of outputs, validity domain, oracles, known gaps); known answers run against the hand derivations, never against stored outputs of this code; every output carries its claim product and the no-authorization flags; the computation is CPU-only and claims no device path; the gate row carries measured values with commit, hardware fingerprint and seed.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry check`:
- `rainflow_reference_sequence`: −2, 1, −3, 5, −1, 3, −4, 4, −2 in units of 40 MPa, N(S) = 10⁶·(100 MPa/S)³ on amplitude: counts as above; Σ count × range³ = 1,094; damage 1,094/1.25 × 10⁸ = 8.752 × 10⁻⁶.
- `fatigue_block`: 2,000 cycles at 200 MPa (N = 125,000) + 50,000 at 100 MPa (N = 10⁶) + 400,000 at 50 MPa (N = 8 × 10⁶) = 0.016 + 0.050 + 0.050 = 0.116; 1/0.116 = 8.6207 blocks.
- `residue_carried_across_windows`: the same sequence fed in two blocks without `finish` between them equals the whole-history count (metamorphic relation MR-34).
- `window_split_mutation_caught`: finishing each window gives Σ = 977 and 7.816 × 10⁻⁶; the test asserts the 10.7% deficit and that the gate fails on it.
- `residue_minimal_case`: history 0, 10, 0 split into [0, 10] and [10, 0] holds one full cycle of range 10; a counter that drops residues counts none.
- `rare_overloads_dominate`: exponent 3; 10⁶ cycles at a life of 2 × 10⁶ give 0.5; 10³ cycles at five times the amplitude (life 16,000) add 0.0625.
- `cross_task_cycle_counted`: 50 MPa held through one task, 0 through the next: one half cycle of range 50 MPa exists only if episodes are not reset.
- `service_utilization`: D = 0.002 over H_c = 10 h, H = 2,000 h, scatter factor 2: (0.002/10)·2,000 = 0.4 against a limit of 0.5: passes; H = 3,000 h gives 0.6: fails.
- `unsigned_history_rejected`; `mean_stress_policy_is_named`; `no_claim_when_curve_invalid_for_finish`.
- `buckling_floor`: pinned tube r = 10 mm, t = 1 mm, L = 0.5 m, E = 69 GPa: critical load 8,558 N; at 4,000 N the factor is 2.14 (pass), at 5,000 N it is 1.71 (fail).
- `first_mode_cantilever`: 20 × 10 mm aluminium bar (ρ = 2,700 kg/m³, E = 69 GPa), L = 0.20 m: first bending frequency 204.2 Hz in closed form; the mesh result is within 3%.
- `refinement_stops_under_5_percent`; `reentrant_corner_reports_fillet`; `gauges_at_16_highest_per_unit_load`; `mesh_hash_recorded_and_differs_from_optimizer_grid`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `finish-per-window`, `unsigned-von-mises`, `drop-mean-stress-policy`, `reuse-optimizer-mesh`.

`scripts/e2e/foundry_check_structure.sh`: (1) `frob foundry check --structure --fixture fatigue-block --json`

(8.752e-6), `split_deficit` (0.107), `u_peak`, `buckling_factor_min` (threshold 2), `f1_hz` and its floor,
`fatigue_utilization_max`, `refinement_levels`, `mesh_hash`; the summary names GATE-FOUNDRY-03.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-741 (fatigue block; control: unsigned stress changes the count and is refused). KA-742 (rainflow sequence;
control: the window split gives 7.816 × 10⁻⁶). KA-737 and KA-738 re-run through this path. The two
construction-level rows: the residue case and the rare-overload case. Counterexamples CX-338, CX-339.
Metamorphic: scaling all stresses by k scales damage by kᵐ; repeating the campaign twice doubles the damage
plus the cycles closed across the junction; permuting episodes may change the count (the duty sequence is
part of the history) and the test asserts that the code does not hide this. Determinism: counting is exact on
the stored histories; solves are identical twice on one machine and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- OPEN-FOUNDRY-4: there is no stress-life data for printed material by process and orientation. Default: `fatigue: no-claim` for printed parts. It blocks the fatigue leg of the stage-2 comparison for printed fixtures only (N31b-27).
- OPEN-FOUNDRY-1 blocks nothing here by itself: the same-family fallback keeps the check running, with its label.
- The linear rule ignores load order and the observers are elastic: a utilization is a screening number under a named law, never a prediction of when a part breaks.
- Rare overloads dominate damage, so a campaign that never contains them supports nothing; overload rates from adversarial search enter only in the lifecycle work.
- A fast-tier bias of 10% in load becomes a factor of 1.61 in damage at exponent 5; spectra are therefore rescaled to referee ranges before a packet is written (N31b-36).
- Budget: the check is a whole job of seconds to minutes per link and runs through the worker process.
