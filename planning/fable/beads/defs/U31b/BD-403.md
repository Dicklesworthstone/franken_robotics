## Background

Foundry's structure loop has two rungs by decision (ADR-FOUNDRY-02): closed-form beam sizing by default and as
fallback, and three-dimensional topology optimization for finalists. This bead is the second rung: given a
link's design region and its envelope load cases, find the lightest material layout that meets stress,
stiffness, frequency and manufacturing constraints, and extract its surface.

What it is worth, stated before any test. Reported savings against engineered parts are roughly 7 to 30% of
link mass (an industrial forearm 7%, an exoskeleton arm 10.4%, a hydraulic leg unit 24.5%, two links of a
table-tennis arm 25% and 29%); against primitive shapes the saving is larger and means nothing, because nobody
ships a solid capsule. Link structure is a small share of a robot: in one 15 kg quadruped the legs are about
27% of the mass. The direct ceiling is therefore f_L·s ≤ 0.27 × 0.30 ≈ 8% of robot mass, and the expectation
is 2 to 8%. Each design gains finite-element solves per link, and three-dimensional optimization of 12 to 20
links is CPU-hours: this rung lengthens the loop and shortens no decision. Feeding multibody loads into
topology optimization is established practice; no novelty is claimed. These facts come from the plan's source
register and are re-read from their primary sources before a gate cites them.

Consequence (ADR-FOUNDRY-01): optimization runs after the design decision, on qualified finalists. An
exploratory run on an unqualified design is allowed, is labelled, and cannot enter a build packet.
ADR-FOUNDRY-04: mirror-image and symmetric limbs share one optimization only after their load histories are
transformed into one frame and unioned.

FACT (FrankenSim at commit `483d09c682`). `fs-topopt` (about 40,000 lines): density optimization with
Helmholtz filtering, Heaviside projection, continuation and chain-rule sensitivities checked against finite
differences; optimality-criteria drivers for independent multiple loads (`multi_load`); a stress-constrained
formulation with relaxation and a p-norm aggregate (`stress`); eigenfrequency objectives (`eigenfreq`,
`modal`); a robust erode/dilate formulation that enforces a minimum length scale (`robust`);
buckling-constrained modulus allocation (`buckling`). Behind feature `cutfem-marquee`, tagged Frontier
upstream: three-dimensional optimization on cut cells (`sdf3`) with body loads (`sdf3::body`), a relaxed stress
measure and a sampled peak bound (`sdf3::stress`), minimum volume under a stress cap (`sdf3::design`), modal
design (`sdf3::modal`) and goal-driven refinement. Its documentation says that cut volumes, discrete
sensitivities and the stress aggregate are not continuum certificates and that the aggregate is not a bound on
the maximum. `fs-topols` (about 15,700 lines): level-set optimization in two dimensions; its sampled stress is
plane strain, and a thin plate is a plane-stress problem. `fs-geocon`: symmetry by quotient chart
(`QuotientChart`), keep-in and keep-out envelopes (`envelope_violation`), a smoothed volume with its shape
derivative. Missing upstream: load-case extraction, ports, interface tractions, process constraints beyond
length scale, and surface extraction for export. Those are Foundry's own code.

Tier: Frontier [F]; bet BET-29; construction C-35. Both pilot stages must have recorded their verdicts.

## Technical approach

Modules (DESIGN): `crates/frob-foundry/src/topopt/{region,problem,drivers,bisect,classes,plate,extract,
budget}.rs`.

**1. The mathematics** (construction C-35). Densities ρ_e ∈ [ρ_min, 1] on the background mesh give stiffness
K(ρ) = Σ ρ_eᵖ·K_e with penalization p = 3 (DESIGN). For a load F with K(ρ)·U = F the compliance is c = FᵀU and
its sensitivity is ∂c/∂ρ_e = −p·ρ_eᵖ⁻¹·u_eᵀ·K_e·u_e, with no extra solve because the problem is self-adjoint. A
density filter fixes the length scale; a Heaviside projection sharpens the field; continuation raises the
projection sharpness in stages.

**2. Design region.** The keep-in envelope of the link, minus the volumes swept by neighbouring bodies over
the joint ranges (the swept charts of the self-collision analysis A-13), minus actuator, bearing and cable
keep-outs, with port bosses frozen solid. If the region is empty, or the frozen bosses are not connected
inside it, refuse with FROB-REF-164 and name the blocking keep-outs.

**3. Formulation.** Minimize projected volume subject to: (a) a stress cap S_y/SF on the relaxed stress
measure (`sdf3::design`); (b) a stiffness cap per envelope case from the control budget (a compliance or port
deflection limit); (c) a first-mode floor above actuator bandwidth (analysis A-12; `sdf3::modal`); (d) a
minimum length scale (`robust`); (e) the process constraints that have usable derivatives: envelope, symmetry
and, for additive parts, an overhang filter. Load cases are the envelope cases of the link class (BD-400), each
with its port tractions and body load.

**4. Outer bisection.** Where the sibling driver takes compliance at fixed volume (`multi_load`), an outer
bisection on volume fraction v finds the lightest feasible design: (i) solve at v_hi = 1 restricted to the
region; if infeasible, refuse; (ii) set v_lo to the frozen-boss fraction; (iii) solve at the midpoint and test
constraints (a) to (e); (iv) keep the feasible half; (v) stop when v_hi − v_lo ≤ 0.02 (DESIGN) and return the
design at v_hi. Feasibility here is on the optimizer's own measures and is provisional by construction: the
verdict belongs to the independent check.

**5. Link classes.** Mirror-image links are one problem through `fs-geocon::QuotientChart`; the class's load
set is the union of the limbs' cases in the canonical frame. A symmetric quadruped has three to five classes.

**6. Plate parts.** Shape in two dimensions through `fs-topols` (compliance descent with volume control); its
plane-strain sampled stress is not relied on; the part is extruded and checked in three dimensions by the
independent solver.

**7. Extraction.** The surface is the level ½ of the projected density. Smoothing may change volume by at
most 1% (TARGET); a smoothing pass that exceeds it is reduced. Extraction is always followed by the
independent check on a body-fitted mesh (BD-402); the optimizer never checks its own result (ADR-FOUNDRY-05).
A failure there returns FROB-REF-261 with the governing case and location and triggers one re-run with a
tighter cap or a larger length scale.

**8. Budgets (TARGET, unmeasured).** At most 120 s per unique link class for the coarse solve and 10 minutes
for a finalist solve on the CPU cores of the M5 Ultra reference machine. Each run reports wall time, core
seconds, solve count and resolution, so the gate reads measured values.

**9. The verb.** `foundry.optimize` (kind `run`, M5; a job with the bounded synchronous wait). Inputs: design,
link classes, the load campaign, mode (`coarse` or `finalist`), process per part, `--exploratory`, and
`--loop` (the loads–mass loop of BD-404). Output per class: volume and mass against the beam-rung baseline
(saving in percent), the constraint values, the extracted geometry (content hash), the independent-check
record, timings, labels. Refusals: FROB-REF-166 (no qualification verdict; repair: qualify, or `--exploratory`,
labelled and barred from packets), FROB-REF-164, FROB-REF-261, and FROB-REF-260 from the loop. Evidence:
class Estimated; determinism: identical twice on one machine; tier of the load campaign.

**Upstream work items, with interim posture and switch-over rule** (work in the FrankenSim repository under
its own rules; none blocks this bead). FD-FSIM-28, a stable ungated surface for `fs-topopt::sdf3`: interim, it
is reached behind its feature through the airlock, pinned by commit; switch-over, remove the feature gate
from the airlock entry. FD-FSIM-27, a plane-stress option in `fs-topols`: interim, three-dimensional runs and
the three-dimensional check for plates; switch-over, add the plane-stress known answer. FD-FSIM-30, a
statement of boundary conformity in `fs-mesh`: interim, conformity is measured per mesh and recorded.

- [ ] Smoothing never changes volume by more than 1%; every extracted geometry carries an independent-check record.
- [ ] Every run reports wall time against the 120 s and 10 minute budgets (the values GATE-FOUNDRY-05 reads).
- [ ] The seed's remaining conditions are discharged by dependents of this bead: GATE-FOUNDRY-05 is measured by N31b-13 and KILL-TOPOPT is decided by N31b-15.
- [ ] Cancellation of an optimization job leaves no partial geometry in the store.
- [ ] Standing conditions: the code is in `frob-foundry`; its `CONTRACT.md` covers this module in the ten sections (purpose, inputs, outputs, invariants, refusals, determinism class, evidence class of outputs, validity domain, oracles, known gaps); known answers run against the hand derivations, never against stored outputs of this code; every output carries its claim product and the no-authorization flags; the computation is CPU-only and claims no device path; the gate row carries measured values with commit, hardware fingerprint and seed.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry topopt`:
- `two_bar_closed_form`: P = 1,000 N, L = 0.1 m, σ_allow = 100 MPa, E = 69 GPa: minimum volume 2·P·L/σ_allow = 2.0 × 10⁻⁶ m³; compliance at that volume 4·P²·L²/(E·V) = 0.2899 N·m.
- `two_bar_volume_against_angle`: volume ∝ 1/(sin θ·cos θ): 2.000 at 45°, 2.309 at 30° and at 60°.
- `compliance_sensitivity_two_springs`: springs in series with k_e = ρ_e³·k₀, k₀ = 1,000 N/m, F = 10 N, ρ₁ = 0.5: ∂c/∂ρ₁ = −3·F²/(ρ₁⁴·k₀) = −4.8, equal to the finite difference to 10⁻⁶.
- `region_empty_refuses_164`; `ports_disconnected_refuses_164`; `bosses_frozen_solid`.
- `bisection_returns_lightest_feasible`: synthetic monotone feasibility with threshold 0.37 on [0.10, 1.00] returns a fraction in [0.37, 0.39].
- `mirror_class_is_one_problem`; `class_load_set_is_union`.
- `smoothing_volume_limit`: 0.9% accepted, 1.1% reduced.
- `unqualified_refused_166`; `exploratory_is_labelled`.
- `timing_is_reported`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `swap-support-and-load`, `optimizer-checks-itself` (the relaxed stress measure used as the verdict lets the planted overstress through), `unfrozen-boss`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_optimize.sh`: (1) `frob foundry optimize --fixture two-bar --mode coarse --json`, assert
compliance and angle; (2) the swapped-port fixture, assert the failure; (3) the 20 extraction fixtures, assert
20 of 20 mesh audits; (4) an unqualified design, assert exit 3 and FROB-REF-166, then `--exploratory` and
assert the label; (5) a region with a keep-out through the load path, assert FROB-REF-164. Logs per STD-E2E to
`target/e2e-logs/foundry_optimize/`: metrics `compliance_rel_err` (0.10), `bar_angle_deg` (45 ± 5),
`mesh_audits_passed` (20 of 20), `coarse_wall_s` (120), `volume_change_smoothing` (0.01); the summary names
GATE-FOUNDRY-04.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-739 (two-bar optimum: bars at 45°; compliance 4·P²·L²/(E·V); minimum volume 2·P·L/σ_allow; control:
swapping the support and the load port is caught). The construction-level two-bar row (volume ∝
1/(sin θ·cos θ), least at 45°). The planted overstressed link (utilization 1.25) as the control for the rule
that the optimizer does not check itself. Metamorphic: scaling all loads by k scales the minimum volume by k
at fixed allowable stress; mirroring the region and loads mirrors the result. Determinism: identical twice on
one machine and across worker counts for one resolution.

## Considerations

- KILL-TOPOPT (decided at M5): GATE-FOUNDRY-05 fails (saving under 7%, no convergence, or over budget). Fallback: beam-rung sizing supplies mass and inertia feedback; three-dimensional optimization becomes an offline option or is dropped; the structural-margin analysis is unaffected.
- OPEN-FOUNDRY-1 blocks GATE-FOUNDRY-04's meshing clause: whether `fs-mesh` conforms to an arbitrary optimized surface is unmeasured. Default: a second cut-cell solve at another resolution and offset, labelled `check: same-family`.
- The three-dimensional lane is feature-gated and tagged Frontier upstream; a change there is absorbed at the airlock, pinned by commit.
- The mass spiral can multiply a structural saving by 1/(1 − κ) only if actuators are then re-selected, which is a separate outer decision with full revalidation (ADR-FOUNDRY-07); this bead claims no such multiplier.
- A structural result holds for the load envelope it was given; an edit that changes the envelope invalidates it.
