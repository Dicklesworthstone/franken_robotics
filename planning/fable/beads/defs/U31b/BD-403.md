## Background

Foundry's structure loop has two rungs by decision (ADR-FOUNDRY-02): closed-form beam sizing by default and as
fallback, and three-dimensional topology optimization for finalists. This bead is the second rung: given a
link's design region and its envelope load cases, find the lightest material layout that meets stress,
stiffness, frequency and manufacturing constraints, and extract its surface.

What it is worth, stated before any test. Reported savings against engineered parts are roughly 7 to 30% of
link mass (an industrial forearm 7%, an exoskeleton arm 10.4%, a hydraulic leg unit 24.5%, two links of a
table-tennis arm 25% and 29%); against primitive shapes the saving is larger and means nothing, because nobody
ships a solid capsule. Link structure is a small share of a robot: in one 15 kg quadruped the legs are about
27% of the mass. The direct ceiling is therefore f_L·s ≤ 0.27 × 0.30 ≈ 8% of robot mass, and the expectation
is 2 to 8%. Each design gains finite-element solves per link, and three-dimensional optimization of 12 to 20
links is CPU-hours: this rung lengthens the loop and shortens no decision. Feeding multibody loads into
topology optimization is established practice; no novelty is claimed. These facts come from the plan's source
register and are re-read from their primary sources before a gate cites them.

Consequence (ADR-FOUNDRY-01): optimization runs after the design decision, on qualified finalists. An
exploratory run on an unqualified design is allowed, is labelled, and cannot enter a build packet.
ADR-FOUNDRY-04: mirror-image and symmetric limbs share one optimization only after their load histories are
transformed into one frame and unioned.

FACT (FrankenSim at commit `483d09c682`). `fs-topopt` (about 40,000 lines): density optimization with
Helmholtz filtering, Heaviside projection, continuation and chain-rule sensitivities checked against finite
differences; optimality-criteria drivers for independent multiple loads (`multi_load`); a stress-constrained
formulation with relaxation and a p-norm aggregate (`stress`); eigenfrequency objectives (`eigenfreq`,
`modal`); a robust erode/dilate formulation that enforces a minimum length scale (`robust`);
buckling-constrained modulus allocation (`buckling`). Behind feature `cutfem-marquee`, tagged Frontier
upstream: three-dimensional optimization on cut cells (`sdf3`) with body loads (`sdf3::body`), a relaxed stress
measure and a sampled peak bound (`sdf3::stress`), minimum volume under a stress cap (`sdf3::design`), modal
design (`sdf3::modal`) and goal-driven refinement. Its documentation says that cut volumes, discrete
sensitivities and the stress aggregate are not continuum certificates and that the aggregate is not a bound on
the maximum. `fs-topols` (about 15,700 lines): level-set optimization in two dimensions; its sampled stress is
plane strain, and a thin plate is a plane-stress problem. `fs-geocon`: symmetry by quotient chart
(`QuotientChart`), keep-in and keep-out envelopes (`envelope_violation`), a smoothed volume with its shape
derivative. Missing upstream: load-case extraction, ports, interface tractions, process constraints beyond
length scale, and surface extraction for export. Those are Foundry's own code.

Tier: Frontier [F]; bet BET-29; construction C-35. Both pilot stages must have recorded their verdicts.

## Technical approach

Modules (the crate's module map, N31a-01): `crates/frob-foundry/src/topo/{region,formulate,bisect,classes,plate,
extract}.rs`; the drivers and the budget instrumentation of N31a-15 sit in `formulate`.

**1. The mathematics** (construction C-35). Densities ρ_e ∈ [ρ_min, 1] on the background mesh give stiffness
K(ρ) = Σ ρ_eᵖ·K_e with penalization p = 3 (DESIGN). For a load F with K(ρ)·U = F the compliance is c = FᵀU and
its sensitivity is ∂c/∂ρ_e = −p·ρ_eᵖ⁻¹·u_eᵀ·K_e·u_e, with no extra solve because the problem is self-adjoint. A
density filter fixes the length scale; a Heaviside projection sharpens the field; continuation raises the
projection sharpness in stages.

**2. Design region.** The keep-in envelope of the link, minus the volumes swept by neighbouring bodies over
the joint ranges (the swept charts of the self-collision analysis A-13), minus actuator, bearing and cable
keep-outs, with port bosses frozen solid. If the region is empty, or the frozen bosses are not connected
inside it, refuse with FROB-REF-164 and name the blocking keep-outs.

**3. Formulation.** Minimize projected volume subject to: (a) a stress cap S_y/SF on the relaxed stress
measure (`sdf3::design`); (b) a stiffness cap per envelope case from the control budget (a compliance or port
deflection limit); (c) a first-mode floor above actuator bandwidth (analysis A-12; `sdf3::modal`); (d) a
minimum length scale (`robust`); (e) the process constraints that have usable derivatives: envelope, symmetry
and, for additive parts, an overhang filter. Load cases are the envelope cases of the link class (BD-400), each
with its port tractions and body load.

**4. Outer bisection** (N31a-15 governs). Where the sibling driver takes compliance at fixed volume
(`multi_load`), an outer bisection on volume fraction v finds the lightest feasible design: (i) solve at
v_hi = 1 restricted to the region; if the stress cap fails there, the class has no feasible design in its
region: the result is the finding `infeasible-in-envelope` with the governing case and ranked repairs, and the
loop keeps the beam section for that class; (ii) set v_lo to the frozen-boss fraction; (iii) solve at the
midpoint and test constraints (a) to (e); (iv) keep the feasible half; (v) stop when v_hi − v_lo ≤ 0.01 of the
region volume (at most ⌈log₂ 100⌉ = 7 solves) and return the design at v_hi. Feasibility is assumed monotone in
v (HYPOTHESIS); a feasible point below an infeasible one sets the flag `non-monotone` and widens the search by
one solve on each side. Feasibility here is on the optimizer's own measures and is provisional by
construction: the verdict belongs to the independent check.

**5. Link classes.** Mirror-image links are one problem through `fs-geocon::QuotientChart`; the class's load
set is the union of the limbs' cases in the canonical frame. A symmetric quadruped has three to five classes.

**6. Plate parts.** Shape in two dimensions through `fs-topols` (compliance descent with volume control); its
plane-strain sampled stress is not relied on; the part is extruded and checked in three dimensions by the
independent solver.

**7. Extraction.** The surface is the level ½ of the projected density. Smoothing may change volume by at
most 1% (TARGET); a smoothing pass that exceeds it is reduced. Extraction is always followed by the
independent check on a body-fitted mesh (BD-402); the optimizer never checks its own result (ADR-FOUNDRY-05).
A failure there returns FROB-REF-261 with the governing case and location and triggers one re-run with a
tighter cap or a larger length scale.

**8. Budgets (TARGET, unmeasured).** At most 120 s per unique link class for the coarse solve and 10 minutes
for a finalist solve on the CPU cores of the M5 Ultra reference machine. Each run reports wall time, core
seconds, solve count and resolution, so the gate reads measured values.

**9. The verb.** `foundry.optimize` (kind `run`, M5; a job with the bounded synchronous wait; exit 8 with a job
handle when the wait ends first). Inputs (schema `frob.foundry.optimize.in/1`; flag spellings fixed here, and
the registry row VERBS-foundry carries each): `<design>` positional, or `--fixture <name>` for a
registered known-answer fixture (`twobar`, `planted-gain`, the extraction battery of N31a-18) in its place;
link classes (`--links <class>[,<class>…]`, the flag of the verb registry's example; default all classes); the
load campaign (`--campaign <spec|id>`) or a declared load set (`--loads <file>`); `--rung beam|topology` (default
`topology` when the structural feature group is compiled and KILL-TOPOPT is not in force, otherwise `beam`;
`beam` selects the KILL-TOPOPT fallback rung explicitly; a defaulted choice is named in the result with its
reason); `--mode coarse|finalist` (default `coarse`); `--plate` (shape a plate part in two dimensions,
N31a-16); process per part (`--process`); `--exploratory`; `--strict-data` (refuse a required material-card field
of provenance `assumed`, N31a-22); `--loop` (the loads–mass loop of BD-404) and the
idempotency key `--key` (FROB-REF-805 without it). Output (schema `frob.foundry.optimize.out/1`): first the
ceiling line of N31a-02 (`ceiling: robot-mass saving at most …`), then per class: volume and mass against the
beam-rung baseline (saving in percent, printed through N31a-02's `format_saving`, never against the design's
primitive Region), the rung used, the constraint values, the extracted geometry (content hash), the
independent-check record, timings, labels. Refusals, the list of the verb registry row: FROB-REF-166 (no qualification verdict;
repair: qualify, or `--exploratory`, labelled and barred from packets), FROB-REF-164, FROB-REF-261, FROB-REF-260
from the loop, and FROB-REF-167, -169 and -262 from the campaigns the loop runs or re-extracts, and FROB-REF-113
under `--strict-data` (N31a-22); all exit 3. Evidence:
class Estimated; determinism: identical twice on one machine; tier of the load campaign.
Airlock requirement: the topology rung runs on `fs-topopt` and `fs-topols`, in the `structure` feature group, so
the verb row carries `needs_groups = [{ groups = ["structure"], when = "foundry-rung-topology" }]` (N33c-32's
argument-dependent form), and this bead registers the predicate `foundry-rung-topology` in
`frob_verbs::admission::predicates`, so that N33c-32's lint `needs-groups-known` accepts the row. The predicate
holds when the resolved request names `--rung topology` explicitly, and never for a defaulted rung: the default is
`topology` only when the group is compiled and admitted at this pin (N33c-32's presence table) and KILL-TOPOPT is
not in force, and otherwise resolves to `beam` with the reason `feature-absent` or `kill-topopt`, so a request
without `--rung` is never refused for the group. An explicit `--rung topology` in a binary without the group is
refused at admission, before any job or budget, with the appended FROB-REF-790 (`foundation-group-absent`, exit 5,
`required: "structure"`).

**Upstream work items, with interim posture and switch-over rule** (work in the FrankenSim repository under
its own rules; none blocks this bead). FD-FSIM-28, a stable ungated surface for `fs-topopt::sdf3`: interim, it
is reached behind its feature through the airlock, pinned by commit; switch-over, remove the feature gate
from the airlock entry. FD-FSIM-27, a plane-stress option in `fs-topols`: interim, plates are shaped in two
dimensions under plane strain, no two-dimensional number is used as evidence, and every stress, stiffness and
buckling figure comes from the three-dimensional check of the extruded part (N31a-16); switch-over, add the
plane-stress known answer. FD-FSIM-30, a
statement of boundary conformity in `fs-mesh`: interim, conformity is measured per mesh and recorded.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-04 (TARGET): on the two-bar fixture the compliance is within 10% of 4·P²·L²/(E·V) and the bar angle within 5° of 45°; the extracted surface meshes and passes the mesh audit on 20 of 20 fixtures (or the same-family fallback of OPEN-FOUNDRY-1 is in force and labelled).
- [ ] Swapping the support and the load port on the two-bar fixture is caught.
- [ ] An empty region and disconnected ports each return FROB-REF-164 with the blocking keep-outs; an unqualified design returns FROB-REF-166 at the verb (exit 3, both ranked repairs); `--exploratory` runs, and its records carry the `exploratory` label that N31a-03's packet precondition refuses (the packet writer's own refusal is the export beads').
- [ ] `--rung beam` runs the beam rung and reports `rung: beam`; with no `--rung`, the feature group off or KILL-TOPOPT in force selects `beam` and the result names the reason; `--rung topology` with the feature group off is refused with FROB-REF-790 (the airlock group is absent), exit 5, through the registered predicate `foundry-rung-topology`, which the row's `needs_groups` names and the lint `needs-groups-known` accepts.
- [ ] The envelope prints the ceiling line before any saving, and every saving is stated against the beam-rung section sized to the same envelope cases.
- [ ] A class whose stress cap fails at full volume returns the finding `infeasible-in-envelope` with its governing case, and the result keeps the beam section for that class.
- [ ] Port bosses stay solid in every result; mirror-image links produce one geometry and its mirror.
- [ ] Smoothing never changes volume by more than 1%; every extracted geometry carries an independent-check record.
- [ ] Every run reports wall time against the 120 s and 10 minute budgets (the values GATE-FOUNDRY-05 reads).
- [ ] The seed's remaining conditions are discharged by dependents of this bead: GATE-FOUNDRY-05 is measured by N31b-13 and KILL-TOPOPT is decided by N31b-15.
- [ ] Cancellation of an optimization job leaves no partial geometry in the store.
- [ ] Standing conditions: the code is in `frob-foundry`; its `CONTRACT.md` covers this module in the ten sections (purpose, inputs, outputs, invariants, refusals, determinism class, evidence class of outputs, validity domain, oracles, known gaps); known answers run against the hand derivations, never against stored outputs of this code; every output carries its claim product and the no-authorization flags; the computation is CPU-only and claims no device path; each measured gate value is logged against its threshold with commit, hardware fingerprint and seed, in the receipt from which N31a-54 records the gate row.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry topo`:
- `two_bar_closed_form`: P = 1,000 N, L = 0.1 m, σ_allow = 100 MPa, E = 69 GPa: minimum volume 2·P·L/σ_allow = 2.0 × 10⁻⁶ m³; compliance at that volume 4·P²·L²/(E·V) = 0.2899 N·m.
- `two_bar_volume_against_angle`: volume ∝ 1/(sin θ·cos θ): 2.000 at 45°, 2.309 at 30° and at 60°.
- `compliance_sensitivity_two_springs`: springs in series with k_e = ρ_e³·k₀, k₀ = 1,000 N/m, F = 10 N, ρ₁ = 0.5: ∂c/∂ρ₁ = −3·F²/(ρ₁⁴·k₀) = −4.8, equal to the finite difference to 10⁻⁶.
- `region_empty_refuses_164`; `ports_disconnected_refuses_164`; `bosses_frozen_solid`.
- `bisection_returns_lightest_feasible`: synthetic monotone feasibility with threshold 0.37 on [0.10, 1.00] returns a fraction in [0.37, 0.38] after at most 7 solves.
- `rung_choice`: `--rung beam` → beam; no flag with KILL-TOPOPT set → beam with reason `kill-topopt`; no flag with the feature off → beam with reason `feature-absent`; `--rung topology` with the feature off → FROB-REF-790.
- `predicate_foundry_rung_topology`: the predicate is registered in `frob_verbs::admission::predicates` and the verb row passes `needs-groups-known`; it holds for a request with `--rung topology` and not for one with `--rung beam` or no `--rung`; a scratch registry whose row names an unregistered predicate `foundry-rung-topo` fails the lint.
- `ceiling_line_first`: the output's first result line is the ceiling line and the saving's baseline is a beam-rung record.
- `mirror_class_is_one_problem`; `class_load_set_is_union`.
- `smoothing_volume_limit`: 0.9% accepted, 1.1% reduced.
- `unqualified_refused_166`; `exploratory_is_labelled`.
- `timing_is_reported`.
- `d10_oracle_agreement` (the compendium oracle library of N12-50, never regenerated here): the sensitivity this bead computes on the two-spring fixture equals the D.10 oracle's −3/(ρ₁⁴·k₁) (times F²) to 10⁻¹² relative, and the two-bar volume ratio between 30° and 45° equals the oracle's 2/√3 = 1.1547 to 10⁻⁹.
- Planted defects, each caught: `swap-support-and-load`, `optimizer-checks-itself` (the relaxed stress measure used as the verdict lets the planted overstress through), `unfrozen-boss`.

### End-to-end script and logging
Every `frob foundry optimize` call below is a job-submitting `run` verb and carries its own idempotency key
`--key "$(foundry_key <label>)"`, one label per call (steps 3 and 4 make several calls, which must not share a
key; omitted here for brevity; a call without one is refused with FROB-REF-805, and a re-run
with the same key and content returns the stored envelope instead of repeating the work).
`scripts/e2e/foundry_optimize.sh`: (1) `frob foundry optimize --fixture twobar --rung topology --mode coarse
--exploratory --json` (the fixture name is N31a-18's), assert compliance and angle; (2) the swapped-port
fixture, assert the failure; (3) the 20 extraction fixtures, assert 20 of 20 mesh audits; (4) an unqualified
design, assert exit 3 and FROB-REF-166, then `--exploratory` and assert the label; (5) a region with a keep-out
through the load path, assert FROB-REF-164; (6) the same finalist with `--rung beam`, assert `rung: beam` and
a beam-rung record per class; (7) on the topology result of step (4), assert that the ceiling line precedes
the saving in the transcript and that `saving.robot_mass_fraction ≤ ceiling.upper`, logged as a `metric` line
with both values. Logs per STD-E2E to
`target/e2e-logs/foundry_optimize/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block; metrics
`compliance_rel_err` (0.10), `bar_angle_deg` (45 ± 5), `mesh_audits_passed` (20 of 20), `coarse_wall_s` (120),
`volume_change_smoothing` (0.01), each with unit and threshold; geometry and mesh digests per fixture; on
failure the first failing fixture, its measured and expected values and the geometry and mesh paths to
reproduce. The summary line gives assertions passed and failed and names GATE-FOUNDRY-04. The gate row is
recorded by N31a-54 from the receipt of its script `scripts/e2e/foundry_structure_loop.sh`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-739 (two-bar optimum: bars at 45°; compliance 4·P²·L²/(E·V); minimum volume 2·P·L/σ_allow; control:
swapping the support and the load port is caught). The construction-level two-bar row (volume ∝
1/(sin θ·cos θ), least at 45°). The planted overstressed link (utilization 1.25) as the control for the rule
that the optimizer does not check itself. Metamorphic: scaling all loads by k scales the minimum volume by k
at fixed allowable stress; mirroring the region and loads mirrors the result. Determinism: identical twice on
one machine and across worker counts for one resolution.

## Considerations

- KILL-TOPOPT (decided at M5): GATE-FOUNDRY-05 fails (saving under 7%, no convergence, or over budget). Fallback: beam-rung sizing supplies mass and inertia feedback; three-dimensional optimization becomes an offline option or is dropped; the structural-margin analysis is unaffected.
- OPEN-FOUNDRY-1 blocks GATE-FOUNDRY-04's meshing clause: whether `fs-mesh` conforms to an arbitrary optimized surface is unmeasured. Default: a second cut-cell solve at another resolution and offset, labelled `check: same-family`.
- The three-dimensional lane is feature-gated and tagged Frontier upstream; a change there is absorbed at the airlock, pinned by commit.
- The mass spiral can multiply a structural saving by 1/(1 − κ) only if actuators are then re-selected, which is a separate outer decision with full revalidation (ADR-FOUNDRY-07); this bead claims no such multiplier.
- A structural result holds for the load envelope it was given; an edit that changes the envelope invalidates it.
