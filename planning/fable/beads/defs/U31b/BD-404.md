## Background

Foundry's structure loop does not stop at an optimized part. A lighter or heavier link changes the robot's
mass and inertia, which changes the loads the robot generates, which changes the link that is needed. This
bead closes that loop: it writes the checked geometry's mass properties back into the design as a typed edit,
re-runs the load campaign on the new design, and decides with an explicit test whether the iteration has
converged, is diverging, or is cycling.

Why it can fail to converge. Structural mass of a link sized to a stress cap grows with the loads, and the
loads grow with total mass. In the linear model, link mass S and other mass M₀ satisfy S = A + λ·(M₀ + S), with
fixed point S* = (A + λ·M₀)/(1 − λ) and an error that contracts by λ per iteration. The actuator mass spiral
(actuator mass grows with required torque, which grows with total mass) has the same form with loop gain κ. If
both run in one loop the gain is about κ + λ: at κ = 0.59 and λ = 0.15 it is 0.74, five iterations contract the
first step only to 0.74⁴ = 0.30 of itself, and the fixed point amplifies a structural change by 3.8 against
2.4 for the actuator spiral alone. That is the reason for ADR-FOUNDRY-07: actuator cards are held fixed inside
the structure loop; re-selecting an actuator is an outer step with full revalidation, and combining the two
loops raises the gain and can cycle between catalogue sizes.

Rejected alternatives: iterating a fixed number of times without a test (hides divergence); declaring
convergence on mass alone (inertia and the load envelope can still move); fresh random worlds per iteration
(the sampling noise sets a floor above the 2% test, so the test cannot pass).

FACT (FrankenSim at commit `483d09c682`): `fs-mbd-material` (about 26,000 lines, depends on `fs-qty`) compiles
mass, centre of mass and spatial inertia from geometry volume moments and card density, with sources retained.
Geometry coverage and rigidity are the caller's obligations; material uncertainty is retained, not turned into
an inertia bound. Tier: Frontier [F].

## Technical approach

Modules (DESIGN): `crates/frob-foundry/src/loop_/{massprops,edit,driver,test,record}.rs`.

**1. Mass properties.** The checked geometry and its material card go through `fs-mbd-material`: mass m,
centre of mass c and inertia I_c, each with the uncertainty the card's density carries. An outward-rounded
volume enclosure (from `fs-geocon::volume_certified`) must contain the volume the mass was computed from.

**2. The typed edit.** The link's primitive Region is replaced by the optimized geometry through a typed edit
(DESIGN: `set-region` to a mesh Region addressed by the geometry's content hash, fill `solid`, the part's
material; where the model compiler cannot take volume moments of that Region, the fallback is `fill:
declared` with the three mass properties, provenance `foundry:<geometry hash>` and the Region kept as mass
support). A patch never edits a derived inertia directly. The edit yields a new immutable `DesignId` and
`ModelId` and an invalidation set. The edit and its inverse return the original `DesignId`.

**3. One iteration of the map G.** (i) optimize each link class under the current load set; (ii) independent
check; (iii) mass properties; (iv) typed edit → new design; (v) re-synthesize the baseline controller;
(vi) re-run the load campaign on the same world addresses; (vii) re-extract loads. Actuator cards do not
change. G is deterministic only because the campaign reuses its world addresses.

**4. The test.** Let x_k collect, after iteration k, every link-class mass and its three principal inertias,
each divided by its value at k = 0 (DESIGN: this makes x dimensionless), Δ_k = x_k − x_{k−1}, and ‖·‖ the
Euclidean norm.
- *Estimated contraction*: λ̂_k = ‖Δ_k‖/‖Δ_{k−1}‖, reported every iteration.
- *Converged* when all four hold (all TARGET): the relative change ‖Δ_k‖/‖x_k‖ is at most 2% for two
  successive iterations; the a-posteriori bound λ̂_k/(1 − λ̂_k)·‖Δ_k‖ is at most 2% of ‖x_k‖; the load
  envelope moved by at most 5% (largest relative change, over the envelope directions, of the maximum
  projection); and the final geometry passes the independent check under the loads it itself generates.
- *Budget*: five iterations. *Aitken extrapolation* x_k − Δ_k²/(Δ_k − Δ_{k−1}), applied componentwise, may
  propose the next iterate, which is then evaluated like any other (it is never accepted unevaluated).
- *Diverged* when λ̂ ≥ 1 on two successive iterations: refused with FROB-REF-260 and the beam rung as the
  repair.
- *Cycling* when Δ alternates in sign with λ̂ near 1 (DESIGN: within 0.05 of 1), which a discrete stock size
  produces: one under-relaxed step x ← x + ½·(G(x) − x) is tried, then refusal with reason `cycling`.
- *Budget exceeded*: five iterations without convergence: FROB-REF-260 with reason `budget`.
Proof obligation PO-FOUNDRY-03: for a contraction with factor λ, ‖x* − x_k‖ ≤ λ/(1 − λ)·‖Δ_k‖ (sum the
geometric series of later increments); the derivation is on file and the planted-gain fixture tests it.

**5. The record.** `LoopRecord { iterations: [{k, design, model, x, delta_norm, lambda_hat, bound,
envelope_change, check: pass|fail}], aitken_used, decision: Converged | Diverged | Cycling | Budget }`. It is
stored under `structure/` in the build packet, and the model identity of the final iteration is the one the
packet's evidence is computed with (INV-FOUNDRY-04).

**6. Surface.** `foundry.optimize --loop` (the verb of BD-403). The result carries the record, the saving
against the beam-rung baseline at convergence, and class Estimated. FROB-REF-260 payload: λ̂ per iteration,
the last iterates, the reason; repairs: the beam rung; holding the named stock size fixed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-06 (TARGET) on the planted-gain loop (M₀ = 12 kg, A = 0.6 kg, λ = 0.15, start 4.0 kg): the fixed point 2.8235 kg is reached within 10⁻⁶ relative, λ̂ is within 0.01 of 0.15, a planted λ = 1.1 is refused as divergent within three iterations, and a planted two-size cycle is detected and refused after one under-relaxed step.
- [ ] The a-posteriori bound holds on the planted-gain fixture at every iteration (PO-FOUNDRY-03).
- [ ] A loop run with fresh worlds per iteration cannot pass the test, and the driver refuses to start when the campaign definition does not pin world addresses.
- [ ] Actuator cards are identical in every iteration's design (checked by identity); a loop that would change one is a defect.
- [ ] The typed edit produces a new `DesignId`; applying its inverse restores the original; mass properties in the model equal those recomputed from the geometry within 10⁻⁶ relative.
- [ ] The final geometry passed the independent check under loads re-extracted from the final design.
- [ ] The loop record is written whole or not at all; cancelling the loop job mid-iteration leaves the last completed iteration as the stored state.
- [ ] Standing conditions: the code is in `frob-foundry`; its `CONTRACT.md` covers this module in the ten sections (purpose, inputs, outputs, invariants, refusals, determinism class, evidence class of outputs, validity domain, oracles, known gaps); known answers run against the hand derivations, never against stored outputs of this code; every output carries its claim product and the no-authorization flags; the computation is CPU-only and claims no device path; the gate row carries measured values with commit, hardware fingerprint and seed.

## Considerations

- The realized multiplier of a structural saving through the mass spiral is a HYPOTHESIS that this loop measures; no multiplier is claimed.
- The linear model is a description near the fixed point. Real maps have stock-size steps; cycling is the expected failure and has its own branch.
- Each iteration re-runs a campaign: the loop is the most expensive part of Foundry. Its cost is reported per iteration and is part of what the stage-2 comparison reads.
- KILL-TOPOPT fallback: the beam rung supplies mass and inertia feedback through the same typed edit and the same test, so this bead survives the kill gate.
- A converged loop licenses the packet's mass properties only for the model identity of the last iteration.
