## Background

An optimized link is a shape, not yet a part. A shape with a 0.3 mm web, an internal corner sharper than any
cutter, a pocket no tool can reach, or a ceiling a printer cannot bridge is not manufacturable by the process
it was meant for. This bead checks made parts against their declared process and reports each violation with
its location and a repair. It belongs to Foundry (structure, manufacture, lifecycle), in the manufacture half,
and feeds the build packet's `fab.json`.

Each made part declares a process: `cnc-3axis`, `cnc-5axis`, `sheet`, `fdm`, `sls` or `slm`. The sibling
library holds limits; this project measures geometry. FACT (FrankenSim at commit `483d09c682`): `fs-fab` (355
lines) has scalar limit constraints by process (overhang angle, minimum feature size, tool radius, draft
angle, and three structural-code and transport limits), each declaring its differentiability and certificate
availability; `check_all` with a repair target per violation; and parametric cost and embodied-carbon
estimates with a relative uncertainty. It compares a supplied number with a limit: it does not measure
geometry, and it has no process-capability data, no machine models and no prices. `fs-geocon` (about 1,300
lines) has soft minimum thickness, draft violations with undercuts flagged, keep-in and keep-out envelopes; its
thickness and envelope values carry estimate authority, and it has no overhang, tool-access or corner-radius
measure. So `frob-foundry` owns four geometric evaluators.

Design decisions. ADR-FOUNDRY-11: no gate depends on a reviewer or a machine shop; those are import lanes that
rest at `no-claim`. A mechanical manufacturability pass is therefore reported as a pass of the listed checks
and never as a shop's acceptance. Rejected alternative: making every process constraint active inside the
optimizer; only constraints with usable derivatives can be, so the rest are checked after extraction and fed
back by tightening the length scale. Tier: Frontier [F].

## Technical approach

Modules (the crate's module map, N31a-01): `crates/frob-foundry/src/fab/{process,dispatch,corner,voids,access,
overhang,inopt,cost}.rs`; thickness, draft and envelope are measured by the sibling calls the dispatcher makes,
and `fab.json` is assembled from the dispatcher's findings (N31a-26 step 6).

**1. Features, measures, limits and processes.**

| Feature | Measured by | Limit from | Process |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum wall and member thickness | `fs-geocon::min_thickness_soft` | `fs-fab::min_feature_size` | All |
| Internal corner radius | Curvature of the distance field (ours) | `fs-fab::cnc_tool_radius` | Milling |
| Tool access | Visibility of each surface point along the declared setup directions for the stated tool radius and reach (ours) | Setup list on the process card | Milling |
| Overhang angle | Angle between surface normal and build direction (ours) | `fs-fab::overhang_angle` | Additive |
| Draft and undercut | `fs-geocon::draft_violations` | `fs-fab::draft_angle` | Moulded |
| Enclosed voids | Connected components of the complement (ours) | None for milling; drain holes for powder processes | All |
| Keep-in and keep-out | `fs-geocon::envelope_violation` | The design region | All |

**2. The four own evaluators** (DESIGN; each returns a value, a location and its sampling resolution).
- *Internal corner radius.* On the signed distance field φ of the part (grid spacing h at most a quarter of the
  smallest tool radius or minimum feature size on the card), the principal curvatures κ₁ ≥ κ₂ at a surface
  point are the eigenvalues of the shape operator (the Hessian of φ projected on the tangent plane, divided by
  ‖∇φ‖), signed so that a convex sphere has κ = +1/R; where κ₂ < 0 the internal corner radius is
  r_int = −1/κ₂. A ball cutter is limited by both curvatures; a flat cutter only by the curvature of the level
  curve in the plane normal to each setup direction, best over setups, so the sharp floor edge a flat end mill
  leaves is not a violation. Radii below 2·h are reported as `r < 2h`; a tangent-ball test (a ball of the tool
  radius tangent outside the surface must not cut the part) cross-checks the violating region within 2·h
  (N31a-27 governs). Violation when r_int is below the tool radius; repair target: the tool radius;
  difference: tool radius minus measured.
- *Tool access.* For each surface sample x with outward normal n and each setup direction d of the process
  card: x is reachable from d when n·d ≥ 0 and the swept cylinder of the tool (radius r_tool, length up to the
  reach) placed tangent at x along d does not intersect the part. `cnc-3axis` uses the listed directions;
  `cnc-5axis` uses a cone of directions about each. A point reachable from no direction is a violation;
  repair: the nearest direction that would reach it, or the reach needed.
- *Overhang.* With build direction b, for downward-facing points (n·b < 0) the overhang angle from the
  vertical is ψ = arcsin(−n·b): 0° for a vertical wall, 90° for a flat ceiling. Violation when ψ exceeds the
  limit; repair target: the limit angle. The convention is unit-tested against the sibling's limit on a 30°
  and a 60° ramp, so that both sides read the angle the same way.
- *Enclosed voids.* Label the empty cells (φ > 0) of the same grid, padded by two cells; the component touching
  the padding is the outside, and every other empty component is an enclosed void, reported with its volume,
  centroid and bounding box. None is allowed for milling and `sheet`. For `sls` and `slm` a void needs a
  channel to the outside of at least the card's drain diameter (erode the empty space by half that diameter;
  a component cut off after erosion is `undrained`, with its widest opening measured). For `fdm` a void is
  reported as information (trapped support) and is not a violation.

**3. Inside and after the optimization.** Inside, only constraints with usable derivatives are active: length
scale, envelope, symmetry, and for additive parts an overhang filter. The others are checked after extraction.
A violation returns the repair from `check_all` (target value and difference) and triggers a re-run of the
optimization with the change N31a-29 chooses per finding (a tightened length scale L′ = max(1.25·L, the
repair target) for thickness and corners; first an alternative build direction for overhang; an added setup,
where the card allows one, for tool access; a keep-out channel to the nearest outside face for a void), at
most twice (DESIGN cap); findings that remain after the second re-run are returned with the part, not looped,
and the structure loop falls back to the last candidate that passes its process checks.

**4. Cost and embodied carbon.** Parametric estimates with a relative uncertainty, class Estimated, printed
only when a process card supplies the unit rates. With no rates the fields are absent, never zero.

**5. Report.** `fab.json` (DESIGN): per part `{ part, process, card, checks: [{feature, measured, limit, unit,
location, pass, authority, repair: {target, difference} | null}], voids, cost: {value, rel_uncertainty} |
absent, carbon: … | absent, class: "Estimated", review: "no-claim" }`. Every value from `fs-geocon` keeps its
estimate authority.

**6. The verb.** `foundry.check` (kind `run`, M5): independent stress, buckling, modal, fatigue and
manufacturability check of a geometry. Inputs: a geometry (content hash or part of a design), its material
and process cards (`--process <card>`), a load set for the structural half, `--structure`, `--fab` (both
halves when neither is given; `--fab` alone runs the manufacturability half, an option this bead defines; the
verb row VERBS-foundry carries both, with `--fixture <name>` for the cluster's named fixtures). Output: the
structure record and the fab report; exit 2 when there are findings; refusals FROB-REF-163 (a card lacks a
field a check needs) and FROB-REF-261 (an optimized part fails the independent check, when called by the
optimizer or the packet writer); the verb's registry row also carries FROB-REF-860, which belongs to the packet
mode of the verb (N31b-32), FROB-REF-169, which belongs to the structural half's load-set coverage rule
(BD-402; N31a-11 step 6), FROB-REF-113 under `--strict-data` (N31a-22, N31b-27) and the interim FROB-REF-890 of
`firmware-receipt-mismatch` with `--firmware-receipt` (N31a-62, row 9 of N31b-45). All these exit 3. The row's
`needs_groups = ["structure"]` (`fs-fab` and `fs-geocon` sit in that group) appends FROB-REF-790, exit 5, for a
binary without the group. Evidence: class Estimated.

**Upstream work item** FD-FSIM-29 (FrankenSim, `fs-fab`: geometric evaluators for overhang and tool access).
Interim posture: `frob-foundry` owns the evaluators and reports `review: no-claim`. Switch-over rule: a
differential test of the sibling's evaluators against ours on the gate fixtures, then deletion of the local
ones.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-07 (TARGET): planted violations of wall thickness, corner radius, tool access, overhang and undercut are each detected with location and repair, and the clean parts pass, on 20 of 20 fixtures.
- [ ] Each own evaluator reproduces its analytic fixture: a 2.0 mm fillet reads 2.0 mm ± h at h = 0.25 mm (the accuracy N31a-27 tests); a 60° ramp reads 60° ± 1°; a closed spherical cavity is one void and the same cavity with a drain hole is none; with a flat cutter the floor edge of a pocket is not flagged and with a ball cutter it is.
- [ ] Every violation carries a repair with target and difference from `check_all`; a violation triggers a re-run with the change N31a-29 chooses, at most two re-runs, after which the part is returned with its findings.
- [ ] Cost and carbon appear only when the process card supplies unit rates, with their relative uncertainty and class Estimated.
- [ ] `fab.json` validates against its schema and every number carries its authority and class.
- [ ] `foundry.check` is reachable through `frob --json`; a part with findings exits 2; a card without a needed limit exits 3 with FROB-REF-163.
- [ ] Standing conditions: the code is in `frob-foundry`; its `CONTRACT.md` covers this module in the ten sections (purpose, inputs, outputs, invariants, refusals, determinism class, evidence class of outputs, validity domain, oracles, known gaps); known answers run against the hand derivations, never against stored outputs of this code; every output carries its claim product and the no-authorization flags; the computation is CPU-only and claims no device path; each measured gate value is logged against its threshold with commit, hardware fingerprint and seed, in the receipt from which N31a-55 records the gate row (the seed's "KILL-FAB decided" is then N31b-17's, which reads that row).
- [ ] Cancellation of a running check (request, drain, finalize) leaves no partial report.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry fab`:
- `thickness_planted_thin_web`: a 0.6 mm web against a 0.8 mm limit: violation, repair target 0.8 mm, difference 0.2 mm.
- `corner_radius_fillet`: pocket fillet 2.0 mm read as 2.0 ± 0.25 mm at h = 0.25 mm; a 1.0 mm corner against a 1.5 mm tool: violation, difference 0.5 mm.
- `corner_radius_sphere_cavity`: concave sphere of radius 10 mm reads 10 mm within 3%.
- `flat_cutter_ignores_floor_edge`; `ball_cutter_flags_floor_edge`; `below_two_h_reported_unresolved` (a 0.3 mm corner at h = 0.25 mm reads `r < 2h` and is a violation against a 1.5 mm tool).
- `tool_access_open_pocket` passes from +z; `tool_access_pocket_under_ledge` fails from +z and passes when −x is added to the setup list; `tool_reach_exceeded`: a 60 mm deep pocket against a 40 mm reach.
- `overhang_ramps`: 30° passes and 60° fails at a 45° limit; a vertical wall reads 0°; a flat ceiling 90°.
- `draft_negative_is_undercut`: a moulded part with −1° draft is flagged as an undercut.
- `voids_hollow_sphere`: one void; with a 3 mm drain against a 2 mm requirement: none; with a 1 mm drain: violation (`undrained`); the same cavity under `fdm`: reported as information, no violation.
- `envelope_keep_out_intrusion`.
- `process_table_applicability`: milling checks are not applied to `sls`; overhang is not applied to `cnc-3axis`.
- `cost_absent_without_rates`; `cost_has_uncertainty`.
- `rerun_capped_at_two`: a thickness violation re-runs with L′ = max(1.25·L, target); a part still failing after two re-runs is returned with its findings and no third run starts.
- Planted defects, each caught: `skip-concavity-test` (convex edges reported as tight corners), `ignore-reach`, `overhang-from-horizontal` (angle convention swapped), `void-fill-from-inside`.

### End-to-end script and logging
Every `frob foundry check` call below is a job-submitting `run` verb and carries its own idempotency key from
the cluster preamble's `foundry_key <label>`, one label per call (N31a-51; a call without one is refused with FROB-REF-805, and a
re-run with the same key and content returns the stored envelope instead of repeating the work). `--fab` is
this bead's option and `--fixture` the cluster's named-fixture input (N31a-51); both are in VERBS-foundry's row.
`scripts/e2e/foundry_check_fab.sh`: runs `frob foundry check --fab --fixture <name> --process <p> --key "$(foundry_key fab-<name>)" --json`
over the 20 gate fixtures of the battery N31a-30 (fifteen planted, three per violation kind: wall, corner
radius, tool access, overhang, undercut; five clean), asserting for each planted fixture the feature, a
location inside the planted region dilated by two sampling steps, the measured value within the fixture's
tolerance and the repair's target and difference (the battery's matching rule), and for each clean fixture no
finding at all; then one real optimized link per process family. Logs per STD-E2E to
`target/e2e-logs/foundry_check_fab/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block; per
fixture the feature, planted and measured value, limit, unit, location and its distance from the planted
region, repair, `detected`; metrics `planted_detected` (15 of 15, and per kind 3 of 3), `clean_passed` (5 of
5), `sampling_resolution_mm`; on failure the first unmatched expectation (fixture, feature, expected and
measured) and the fixture and report paths to reproduce. The summary line gives assertions passed and failed
and names GATE-FOUNDRY-07; the gate row itself is recorded by N31a-55 from the receipt of its script
`scripts/e2e/foundry_check.sh`.

### Known answers and negative controls
The analytic fixtures above are the known answers (fillet radius, ramp angle, cavity count); the chapter lists
no numbered known answer for manufacturability. Negative controls: the fifteen planted parts; the four planted
defects; a report generator that prints `review: accept` after a mechanical pass (must be rejected by the
schema). Metamorphic: rigidly moving a part with its setup and build directions changes no finding; scaling a
part and its limits together changes no verdict; adding a setup direction never creates a violation.
Determinism: identical twice on one machine for a fixed sampling resolution.

## Considerations

- KILL-FAB (decided at M5): GATE-FOUNDRY-07 fails. Fallback: manufacturability findings become advisory lints, and a packet records no manufacturability pass.
- A per-part pass is not an assembly pass: a fastener enclosed by a previously mounted part passes every check here. That case belongs to the human rubric of packet review.
- The evaluators sample the surface; a feature smaller than the sampling resolution is reported as unresolved, not as passing.
- The chapter says `fs-geocon` measures two of the features while its table attributes three rows to it (thickness, draft, envelope); the table is followed.
- No pilot row exists for manufacturability alone; the build-packet pilot, which carries one hand-built part through the fab report, serves as its pilot.
