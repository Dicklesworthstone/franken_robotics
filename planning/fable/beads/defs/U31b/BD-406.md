## Background

Every dimension of a built robot deviates from its nominal. Tight tolerances are expensive and loose ones
break function. Foundry's tolerance step answers: given a functional requirement (a foot must land within so
many micrometres, a loop must close, a gear pair must keep its backlash), how loose may each feature be, and
which features must be tight? The answer uses what the model already knows: how much each feature moves the
quantity of interest. Tolerance is cheapest to loosen where sensitivity is low and cost is high, and the cube
root in the allocation law keeps the result from being extreme.

The mathematics (construction C-35). For a functional dimension f(x) with sensitivities aᵢ = ∂f/∂xᵢ, the
worst-case stack is Σ|aᵢ|·tᵢ and the statistical stack is (Σ aᵢ²·tᵢ²)^½. Minimizing the cost Σ kᵢ/tᵢ under a
stack limit gives tᵢ ∝ (kᵢ/aᵢ²)^⅓ for the statistical stack and tᵢ ∝ (kᵢ/|aᵢ|)^½ for the worst case.
Assumptions: nonzero first-order sensitivities and independent feature deviations.

FACT (FrankenSim at commit `483d09c682`): `fs-toleralloc` (about 6,500 lines) has cost-optimal allocation
t_i ∝ (c_i/s_i²)^(1/3) to a variance budget (`allocate`, `variance_budget`); a check of the linearization at
tolerance-band extremes (`robustness_check`); a report in which each tolerance carries its sensitivity and
that sensitivity's evidence colour (`gdt_report`); first-order correlated stacks aᵀ·C·a up to 128 terms
(`propagate_correlated_stack`) and grouped allocation (`allocate_grouped`); finite-population propagation
through piecewise-quadratic clearance laws with a gear-backlash consumer (`structured_propagation`,
`gear_backlash`). It has no datum reference frames or feature control frames: its report is a list of
suggestions, not a drawing annotation. Sensitivities and cost coefficients are inputs. Worst-case stacks are a
separate analysis. It requires positive sensitivities.

Design decisions. The allocation is a *suggestion with justification*: datum frames are declared by Foundry
in the packet, and no drawing annotation is produced. The cost model is an assumed 1/t law; any saving is
"under an assumed 1/t model only". Rejected alternatives: equal tolerances (19.9% dearer in the worked case);
worst-case boxes everywhere (kept as the fallback of KILL-TOLERANCE because it over-tightens); trusting a
first-order allocation without evaluating the true quantity at the band extremes. Tier: Frontier [F].

## Technical approach

Modules (the crate's module map, N31a-01): `crates/frob-foundry/src/tol/{qoi,sens,allocate,stack,robust,
structured,report}.rs`, with `tol/verify.rs` for the held-out coverage check of N31a-33.

**Quantities of interest.** Foot or tool position error; joint-axis offset and parallelism; closure error of
a loop; backlash; minimum clearance; wheel radius as an odometry scale.

**Sensitivities and class.** For kinematic quantities: exact derivatives of the product-of-exponentials map
with respect to geometric parameters, class Certified when evaluated in exact or interval arithmetic. For
simulated task metrics: the sensitivity analysis A-16 (automatic differentiation for static metrics, Morris
screening and smoothed gradient estimates for simulated ones), class Estimated. The allocation inherits the
weaker class of its inputs.

**Procedure.**
1. `variance_budget(spec_margin, target)` turns the specification into a variance budget B.
2. `allocate(features, budget, k)` returns t_i ∝ (c_i/s_i²)^(1/3), normalized to meet the budget. With
   σ_i = t_i/k and Σ s_i²·σ_i² = B the closed form is t_i = κ·(c_i/s_i²)^(1/3) with
   κ = k·√B / (Σ_j s_j^(2/3)·c_j^(2/3))^(1/2). Here c_i is the cost coefficient, s_i the sensitivity, and k the
   number of standard deviations a tolerance band represents.
3. Features that share a setup or a datum go through `propagate_correlated_stack` (variance aᵀ·C·a with C the
   covariance of the feature deviations) or `allocate_grouped`. The sibling's correlated stack takes at most
   128 terms: features sharing a setup are merged into groups first, and a stack that still exceeds 128 terms
   is verified by the worst-case stack Σ|sᵢ|·tᵢ instead, which needs no correlation model; the result says so
   (N31a-32 step 6). This is a fallback, not a refusal.
4. `robustness_check` evaluates the true quantity at the band extremes. Features that fail fall back to
   worst-case boxes by interval evaluation (`fs-uq::product_epistemic`), which is also the fallback of
   KILL-TOLERANCE.
5. Clearances and backlash, which switch modes, go through `structured_propagation` (and `gear_backlash`).
6. `gdt_report` is wrapped into the report object, schema `frob.foundry.tolerances/1` (N31a-33 step 8), which
   the verb's result carries under `report` and the packet assembler writes verbatim as `tolerances.json`. Per
   feature: the tolerance; `action` (`loosen`, `keep` or `tighten`, relative to the process default); `path`
   (`allocated`, `grouped`, `worst-case-box` or `refused`: how the tolerance was obtained); on a
   `worst-case-box` feature, `enclosure` (`product-epistemic`, or `local-bisection` when the sibling entry's
   admission failed, N33bx-06 step 5); `beyond_process_capability` (a boolean: the tolerance is tighter than the floor the process card declares
   in its optional `tolerance_floor_m` table, N31a-26 and N31a-32 step 8, flagged with the repair to
   redistribute or change process; a card without the table reads `process floor: not declared`); the
   sensitivity and its class; the governing quantity; and, for `worst-case-box` and `refused`, the reason
   (`zero-sensitivity`, `unknown-sensitivity` or `band-extreme-failed`). Then per quantity the stack results,
   the split φ where boxes were used, the robustness results (`corners: all` or `corners: sampled (2n + 2)`)
   and `band_extreme_failed_share`. Its header states that these are suggested tolerances with their
   justification, not a drawing annotation, and that datum frames are those declared in the packet.
7. The as-built audit compares each measured feature with its allocation.

**Robust path and refusal** (N31a-33 governs the details). A zero first-order sensitivity is not insensitivity:
for q(x) = x² at nominal zero the sensitivity is zero and a first-order allocator would loosen without limit,
while the deviation at tolerance t is t². A feature whose sensitivity enclosure contains zero, is below 10⁻⁹ of
the largest in the stack, or is `unknown` (standard error above half its magnitude) is refused by the
first-order allocator and routed to the robust path, as is a feature that fails the band-extreme check (true
change at its single-feature extreme differing from s_i·t_i by more than 10% of the margin T; or a corner of
the band box, all corners for n ≤ 12 features and the two linearly worst otherwise, whose discrepancy from the
linearization exceeds 0.1·T, failing the features whose return to nominal removes it, by the attribution rule
of N31a-33 step 2; DESIGN). The check tests the linearization, not the margin: a corner beyond the margin is
not a failure (the worked case's linearly worst corner, 41.4 µm against T = 30 µm, fails nothing). By
default the robust-path features receive worst-case boxes by interval evaluation and the result completes
with exit 2, naming them with their reason and labelling the quantity `mixed: statistical and worst-case`.
FROB-REF-165 (exit 3) is raised when the caller asked for a purely statistical allocation (`--method
statistical`) and a feature needs the robust path, when no box fits inside the margin above the process
floor, or when an unknown simulated sensitivity cannot be evaluated in interval arithmetic; repairs ranked:
worst-case boxes for the named features (the exact command), the extra episodes the sensitivity analysis
needs, a wider margin or another process. Worked fixture: q(x) = x²/L with L = 1 mm and margin 10 µm; the
band-extreme check at the 200 µm process default reads 40 µm against 10 µm and fails; the worst-case box is
√(10 µm × 1 mm) = 100 µm.

**The verb.** `foundry.tolerance` (kind `read`, M5; it returns within the call and creates no job). Inputs
(schema `frob.foundry.tolerance.in/1`): design (or `--fixture <name>`, the cluster's named-fixture input,
N31a-51), the quantities of interest (`--qoi`) with their specification margins and targets, cost coefficients
(default equal), `--k` (default 3), correlation groups, `--method auto|statistical|worst-case` (default
`auto`: the statistical allocation with worst-case boxes for the robust-path features; `statistical` refuses
rather than boxes; `worst-case` applies the worst-case closed form to every feature; N31a-32 step 10), and
`--verify monte-carlo [--samples N] --seed s` for the held-out coverage check of N31a-33. These options are
the component beads', and the verb row VERBS-foundry carries each of them. Refusals: FROB-REF-165 (above),
exit 3; the row's `needs_groups = ["structure"]` (`fs-toleralloc` sits in that group) appends FROB-REF-790,
exit 5, for a binary without the group. Output (schema `frob.foundry.tolerance.out/1`): the allocation record of N31a-32 with the
report object under `report` and the total cost under the 1/t model against the equal-tolerance baseline. A
`read` verb writes no file: the report reaches disk only inside a packet, through `foundry.export` and its
export door. (A first draft gave this verb an `--out` file option, which would have made a `read` verb write
outside the store; a script that wants the file extracts `report` from the envelope.)
Evidence: the weaker of the sensitivity classes; determinism D0 for kinematic sensitivities in exact
arithmetic; claims empty.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-08 (TARGET): the worked allocation (sensitivities 1 and 4, equal costs, k = 3, √B = 10 µm) gives 15.990412 µm and 6.345799 µm to 10⁻⁹ relative; the zero-sensitivity and quadratic cases are handled as specified (removed from the first-order allocation and boxed by interval evaluation with exit 2 by default, the quadratic fixture's box 100 µm ± 1%; FROB-REF-165 with exit 3 under `--method statistical`); a correlated stack equals its hand value (6.083 µm at correlation 0.5).
- [ ] Equal tolerances meeting the same budget are 7.276069 µm each and the allocation costs 19.9% less under the 1/t model (cost ratio 0.800812).
- [ ] The allocation's class is the weaker of its sensitivities' classes; a kinematic sensitivity computed in exact arithmetic is Certified and a simulated one is Estimated.
- [ ] Every allocated feature has a band-extreme result; a feature that fails it is boxed and labelled, not silently tightened.
- [ ] A mode-switching clearance goes through structured propagation and is never allocated by the smooth law.
- [ ] The report validates against `frob.foundry.tolerances/1` with `action`, `path`, `beyond_process_capability`, sensitivity and class per feature; the verb is reachable through `frob --json`; refusal 165 carries the feature, its sensitivity and the box as repair.
- [ ] A tolerance tighter than the process card's declared floor is flagged `beyond_process_capability` with the redistribution repair; a card without `tolerance_floor_m` reads `process floor: not declared`.
- [ ] A stack of more than 128 correlated terms after grouping is verified by the worst-case stack and says so; it is not refused.
- [ ] The gate receipt and the pilot's band-extreme failure share are handed to the kill-gate decision N31b-18 (the seed's "KILL-TOLERANCE decided").
- [ ] Standing conditions: the code is in `frob-foundry`; its `CONTRACT.md` covers this module in the ten sections (purpose, inputs, outputs, invariants, refusals, determinism class, evidence class of outputs, validity domain, oracles, known gaps); known answers run against the hand derivations, never against stored outputs of this code; every output carries its claim product and the no-authorization flags; the computation is CPU-only and claims no device path; the gate row carries measured values with commit, hardware fingerprint and seed.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-745 (16.0 µm and 6.35 µm; 19.9% cheaper; control: q = x² at nominal zero is refused, then fails the
band-extreme check). The construction-level row (cost weights 1 and 8: 0.0447 mm and 0.0894 mm).
Counterexample CX-340. Metamorphic: scaling all cost coefficients by a constant changes no tolerance; scaling
the budget √B by r scales every tolerance by r; permuting features permutes the result; adding a feature of
tiny sensitivity loosens it and barely changes the others. Determinism: exact arithmetic for the kinematic
path, identical everywhere.

## Considerations

- KILL-TOLERANCE (decided at M5): GATE-FOUNDRY-08 fails, or band-extreme checks fail on more than a fifth of features in the pilot. Fallback: worst-case boxes per interface.
- Independence of feature deviations is an assumption; shared setups and datums violate it, which is why step 3 exists.
- The 1/t cost law is assumed; no price data backs it, and no saving is stated in currency.
- First-order allocation is only as good as the linearization; the band-extreme check is the guard and is never skipped for speed.
- Sensitivities from simulated metrics are Estimated and may carry tier labels; the allocation prints them.
