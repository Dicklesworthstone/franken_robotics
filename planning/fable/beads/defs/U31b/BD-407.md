## Background

A build packet needs geometry that another tool can open. Foundry exports each made part's checked surface in
four formats: STL, 3MF and GLB for meshes, and STEP for interchange with design tools. Export is where silent
damage usually enters: an open shell, flipped triangles, a unit mix-up, a file too large for the reader, or a
round trip that moves coordinates. This bead makes export *bounded* (a stated limit, never a silent
truncation) and *replayable* (the STEP file decodes back to exactly the bits that went in), and proves that
the exported shapes have the mass properties the model used.

Decision ADR-FOUNDRY-08: exported STEP is a faceted solid and is described as interchange evidence; no
curved-surface re-fit is attempted, because that is what the sibling exporter emits and checks. Rejected
alternative: fitting spline surfaces to an optimized shape, which would create geometry that no check had
seen.

FACT (FrankenSim at commit `483d09c682`): `fs-io` (about 22,300 lines) provides bounded Part-21 parsing and
canonical writing at the syntax level (`step`); strict decoding of a triangle-only faceted solid
(`step_faceted`); deterministic re-emission of a decoded faceted solid with mandatory self-parse and bit-exact
replay (`step_faceted_export`); STL, OBJ, PLY, 3MF, GLB and VTK export; quarantine of every import. The
faceted exporter accepts only a solid produced by its own decoder: there is no entry point from a mesh. The
file has no curved surfaces, no units or product context and no application-protocol conformance claim.
Default limits admit 10⁶ instances per file.

Tier: Frontier [F]. Part of bet BET-29 through GATE-FOUNDRY-09: the bet is lost if the packet fails its
geometry round trip.

## Technical approach

Modules (the crate's module map, N31a-01): `crates/frob-foundry/src/export/{repair,mesh,step,decimate}.rs`
(validity and repairs, the three mesh writers, the STEP path with its bound, decimation); the geometry record
and the mass properties recomputed from each export sit in `packet/geometry.rs` and `packet/mass.rs` (N31a-40).

**1. Validity.** The extracted surface passes repair and validity checks through the quarantine path of
`fs-io`: watertight (every edge shared by exactly two triangles), manifold (no non-manifold vertex or edge),
oriented (consistent outward normals, positive signed volume). Three canonicalizing repairs are allowed, each
counted in the receipt (N31a-34 step 3): merging vertices with identical coordinates, removing triangles with
two identical vertices, and fixing orientation consistently outward, so a flipped triangle is turned and
counted, not refused. Anything else (a hole, a self-intersection, a non-manifold edge) is not repaired: it is
a refusal with the first offending edge or triangle and the remedy "re-extract at a finer grid"; nothing is
repaired silently.

**2. Frame and units.** Each part is exported in its part-local frame with the origin near its centroid
(DESIGN: this keeps single-precision formats inside the mass-identity tolerance). Coordinates are metres. The
faceted STEP file has no units entity; the manifest states metres, and the geometry record repeats the unit
for every file.

**3. Mesh formats.** STL, 3MF and GLB are written from the same triangle list, each followed by a read-back
that recomputes the triangle count and volume.

**4. STEP without an upstream change.** (i) Build a syntax-level `StepDocument` of the faceted-solid entity
chain from the mesh; (ii) write it with `write_step`; (iii) parse and decode it with
`decode_faceted_brep_with_limits`; (iv) re-emit it with `export_decoded_faceted_brep`, which refuses unless
every coordinate bit and triangle index replays. The file of step (iv) is the one shipped.

**5. The bound.** The default limit of 10⁶ instances admits about 285,000 triangles for a closed mesh: three
instances per triangle, about half a point per triangle (a closed triangle mesh of genus 0 has V = F/2 + 2
vertices), and two more for the solid. Instances = 3·F + V + 2 = 3.5·F + 4, so F ≤ 285,713 (FACT, arithmetic;
a tetrahedron has 4 × 3 + 4 + 2 = 18). A closed triangle mesh has an even number of faces, so the largest count
that can occur is 285,712 (999,996 instances), which is the bound the refusal prints (N31a-35). A larger
part is decimated under a stated Hausdorff tolerance and
re-checked, or refused with FROB-REF-824 and the bound stated.

**6. Decimation is a new geometry, not a view** (DESIGN, to keep the cross-export identity true). A decimated
mesh has a new content hash; it goes back through the independent check and the mass feedback before it can
be exported, so that all four files and the model describe the same triangles. `foundry.export` therefore
refuses an over-bound part and offers decimation as the repair. The repair is the option `--decimate
<tolerance>` specified in N31a-35: it never writes a decimated file beside the checked geometry; it produces
the new geometry, sends it back through validation, the independent check, mass properties and the feedback
edit, and exports all four files from it only if that re-check passes.

**7. Geometry record** (one per part, consumed by the packet): `{ part, geometry: Blake3, files: [{format,
blake3, triangles, unit: "m"}], step_instances, datums: [{name, kind, frame}], fiducials: [{name, position}],
hausdorff_tolerance | null }`. Datums and at least three non-collinear fiducials per part are placed at
design time so that an as-built scan can be registered with known correspondences; fewer than three, or
collinear ones, is a refusal at export.

**8. Mass properties from exported geometry.** For each file, mass, centre of mass and inertia are recomputed
from the triangles read back and the card density. They must equal the model's values within 10⁻⁶ relative
(TARGET): the cross-export identity (INV-FOUNDRY-04).

**9. Surface.** Library entry `export_geometry(part) → GeometryRecord`, and `foundry.export --geometry-only`
(kind `run`, M5). Refusal FROB-REF-824: a STEP export above its bounds or failing bit-exact replay; repair:
decimate under a stated tolerance. Loose files leave the store through the one export door of N37-18: the
writer takes an `ExportGrant` from `frob_verbs::export::admit`, whose checks run in order: flags present
(FROB-REF-816, exit 3: a loose file is handed over only with its sidecar `<file>.flags.json` carrying the four
false flags, N31a-34 step 7, which is the flags carrier the door reads), privacy (FROB-REF-731, exit 5),
licence (FROB-REF-733, exit 5), the row's `export:packet` scope, and the path jail (FROB-REF-734, exit 5:
an output path outside the session's export root). N37-18's nine export kinds (`report`, `pack`, `run`, `replay`,
`policy`, `bundle`, `packet`, `design`, `diagnostic`) name none for loose geometry files; the kind `geometry`, with the sidecar
as its flags and notices carrier (for an `attribution` licence set the notices go into the sidecar), is
requested of N37-18, and until it exists the writer uses `packet`. Evidence: class Estimated (the card
density); determinism: file bytes identical twice on one machine and across machines (the writers are
deterministic).

**Upstream work item** FD-FSIM-26 (FrankenSim, `fs-io`: a mesh entry point to the faceted STEP exporter).
Interim posture: the four-step path above under the stated triangle bound. Switch-over rule: re-export the
packet fixtures through the new entry point and compare hashes with the interim files; adopt only if equal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-09 (TARGET): the tetrahedron round trip is bit-exact with 18 instances; an open shell (the tetrahedron with one face removed) is refused with FROB-REF-824 and the sibling decoder's reason, its open edges listed (N31a-35); a part above the bound is refused with the bound stated; mass properties recomputed from each exported file are within 10⁻⁶ relative of the model's.
- [ ] The tube fixture (ρ = 2,700 kg/m³, radii 15 and 13 mm, length 0.20 m) reads 0.0950 kg from each of the four exports within 10⁻⁶ of the model value for the same faceting.
- [ ] A mesh of 285,712 triangles exports (999,996 instances); one of 285,714 triangles is refused with FROB-REF-824 and the numbers 1,000,003 and 1,000,000.
- [ ] A flipped triangle is canonicalized (oriented outward) and the receipt counts one orientation repair; a hole, a self-intersection and a non-manifold edge are each refused with the offending element and the re-extraction remedy.
- [ ] A loose export is written only under an `ExportGrant`: an output path outside the export root is refused with FROB-REF-734 (exit 5) and writes nothing; a loose file without its sidecar fails the door's flags check (FROB-REF-816).
- [ ] Every geometry record states metres for every file, the triangle counts and at least three non-collinear fiducials; two fiducials are refused.
- [ ] File bytes are identical across two runs and across the two reference machines.
- [ ] The receipt is available to the KILL-PACKET decision (N31b-19).
- [ ] Standing conditions: the code is in `frob-foundry`; its `CONTRACT.md` covers this module in the ten sections (purpose, inputs, outputs, invariants, refusals, determinism class, evidence class of outputs, validity domain, oracles, known gaps); known answers run against the hand derivations, never against stored outputs of this code; every output carries its claim product and the no-authorization flags; the computation is CPU-only and claims no device path; the gate row carries measured values with commit, hardware fingerprint and seed.
- [ ] Cancellation of an export job leaves no partial geometry record and no stray file.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry export`:
- `tetrahedron_step_18_instances`: vertices (0,0,0), (0.01,0,0), (0,0.01,0), (0,0,0.01) m; 18 instances; decode and re-emit reproduce every coordinate bit and index; volume 1.6667 × 10⁻⁷ m³.
- `instance_count_formula`: 3·F + V + 2 on a cube of 12 triangles and 8 vertices gives 46; on a torus mesh V = F/2 (genus 1).
- `bound_edges`: 285,712 triangles accepted; 285,714 refused with the bound in the payload.
- `open_shell_refused`; `flipped_triangle_canonicalized_and_counted` (one triangle reversed: exported with outward orientation, receipt `orientation_repairs: 1`); `self_intersection_refused`; `nonmanifold_edge_refused`.
- `loose_export_requires_grant` (the writer cannot be reached without an `ExportGrant`); `loose_export_path_jail_734`; `loose_export_without_sidecar_816`.
- `replay_detects_one_bit`: flipping the last mantissa bit of one coordinate after step (ii) makes step (iv) refuse.
- `stl_3mf_glb_readback_counts`.
- `mass_identity_tube`: mass 0.0950 kg, axial inertia 1.87 × 10⁻⁵ kg·m², transverse 3.26 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m² for the exact tube; the 256-sided faceted tube agrees with its own model value within 10⁻⁶ from every format.
- `single_precision_formats_stay_inside_tolerance`: a 0.2 m part exported in its centroid frame passes; the same part with a 100 m offset fails and is reported (the reason for the part-local frame).
- `unit_error_ratio`: a file written in millimetres and read as metres has 10⁹ times the volume.
- `fiducials_three_noncollinear`: two refused; three collinear refused.
- `decimation_yields_new_hash_and_requires_recheck`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `truncate-over-bound` (silently drops triangles), `skip-replay`, `export-in-world-frame`, `write-millimetres`.

### End-to-end script and logging
Every `frob foundry export` call below is a job-submitting `run` verb and carries its own idempotency key
`--key "$(foundry_key <step>)"` from the cluster preamble (N31a-51; a call without one is refused with
FROB-REF-805, and a re-run with the same key and content returns the stored envelope instead of repeating the
work); `--fixture` is the cluster's named-fixture input (N31a-51).
`scripts/e2e/foundry_export_geometry.sh`: (1) `frob foundry export --geometry-only --fixture tetrahedron
--format step --key "$(foundry_key g1)" --json`, assert 18 instances and replay; (2) `--fixture open-shell`,
assert exit 3, FROB-REF-824 and the open edges; (3) `--fixture over-bound`, assert exit 3 and FROB-REF-824 with
the bound; (4) `--fixture tube`, assert the mass identity for all four files; (5) `--fixture flipped-triangle`,
assert exit 0 and `orientation_repairs: 1` in the receipt; (6) an output path outside the session's export
root (`--out <dir>`, the loose export's target of N31a-34 step 7, in VERBS-foundry's row), assert exit 5, FROB-REF-734 and no file written; (7) run step 4 twice under two distinct keys and
compare BLAKE3 of all files (one key would replay the stored envelope and prove nothing). Logs per STD-E2E to
`target/e2e-logs/foundry_export_geometry/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence
block; per file its format, unit, digest and triangle count; metrics `step_instances` (18),
`mass_rel_diff_max` (1e-6), `com_rel_diff_max`, `inertia_rel_diff_max`, each with unit and threshold; digests
of first and second run; on failure the first differing byte offset or the first non-replaying coordinate
(entity, index, both bit patterns) and the paths of both files. The summary line gives assertions passed and
failed and names GATE-FOUNDRY-09; the gate row itself is written by `scripts/e2e/foundry_export_packet.sh`
(N31a-57).

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-748 (STEP round trip of a tetrahedron: 18 instances, bit-exact replay; controls: an open shell is refused;
a part above the bound is refused with the bound stated). KA-744 (tube mass properties). The unit-error ratio
10⁹. Metamorphic: permuting triangle order changes the file but not the recomputed mass properties; rigidly

Foundry).

## Considerations

- KILL-PACKET (decided at M5): this gate or the packet gate fails after one repair cycle. Fallback: parts list, mass properties and STL only; STEP and the wiring harness are deferred.
- The faceted STEP file is interchange evidence. It carries no curved surfaces, no units entity and no application-protocol claim; nothing downstream may describe it as a design-intent model.
- Binary STL and GLB carry single-precision coordinates: at 10⁻⁶ relative the margin is real but not large, which is why parts are exported about their centroids.
- Reading "decimate and re-check" as a new geometry that re-enters the loop is a DESIGN choice of this bead; the alternative (a decimated view shipped beside full-resolution meshes) would break the 10⁻⁶ identity among the four files.
- Geometry of a private scene never appears here: parts belong to the design, not to a scanned place.
