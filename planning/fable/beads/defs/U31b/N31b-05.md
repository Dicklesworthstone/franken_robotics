## Background

The Gauntlet is the program's verification harness: six tiers of tests owned by `frob-conformance`, run as
test targets and never through an agent verb, because a harness driven through the surface it judges is a
path to self-certification. A number in the plan is a target until a Gauntlet run records it. The tiers are:
G0 laws and self-tests; G1 known answers; G2 parity against a pinned oracle; G3 metamorphic relations; G4
chaos and planted failures; G5 determinism.

For Foundry the Gauntlet's subject table assigns: G0, dimension typing of every structural quantity; G1, the
cantilever, the two-bar optimum, the fatigue block, the rainflow sequence and the tolerance split; G2,
`fs-solid` through the airlock against the hand derivations; G3, rainflow in windows with carried residues
equals the whole-history count; G4, an open shell, an oversize part, a divergent loads–mass loop; G5, the STEP
round trip replays bit for bit.

Each mechanism bead of Foundry tests its own known answers beside its code. This bead is the independent
battery: the same rows wired into the conformance registry, run against the released `frob` binary and the
library entry points exactly as an agent would call them, with every negative control proven to fail its row.
It is what the gate rows cite. The Moonshot rows (KA-749 to KA-753) are wired by the gate beads of their
epics and are listed here only as reserved.

## Scope

In: registry rows in `known_answers.toml` for KA-737 to KA-748 and KA-754; fixtures; the tier assignments;
the controls from the counterexample registry; receipts; the Foundry line of each tier runner.
Out: derivation files (N31b-04); mechanism code; the Moonshot rows KA-749 to KA-753 (N31b-20, N31b-22); the
late gates of the three added subsections (their own gate beads).

## Technical approach

**1. Registry rows.** For each known answer one record in `frob-conformance/registry/known_answers.toml`:
`id`, `section`, `fixture` (path under `frob-conformance/fixtures/foundry/`), `truth` (value and unit),
`tolerance` with its reason, `tier`, `site`, `derivation` (file), `controls` (identifiers in
`controls.toml`), `gate`. A row whose tolerance is `to set` or whose control is `none stated` is loaded as
not gate-eligible until the proposal of the derivations bead is registered.

**2. The battery by tier.**

| Tier · site | Content | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| G0 · host | Dimension typing: every structural quantity in Foundry's public signatures is a typed quantity; a `compile_fail` corpus shows that millimetres cannot be passed where metres are expected, a force where a moment is expected, or a stress where a stress range is expected | Control of KA-737 |
| G1 · host | Cantilever (1.159 mm; 30 MPa), planted overstress (300 MPa; 1.25; root), two-bar (45°; 4·P²·L²/(E·V); 2·P·L/σ_allow), assembled peaks (P·L/6; P·L/3 or 0), fatigue block (0.116; 8.62), rainflow sequence (counts; 8.752 × 10⁻⁶), loads–mass loop (2.8235 kg; λ̂ 0.15), tube mass properties (0.0950 kg; 1.87 × 10⁻⁵; 3.26 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m²), tolerance allocation (16.0 and 6.35 µm; 19.9%), voltage drop (0.0688 Ω; 0.688 V; 6.9 W; 20 A/mm²), cable slack (36 mm), quasi-static limit (1.042) | KA-737 to KA-747, KA-754 |
| G2 · referee and host | Parity: `fs-solid` through the airlock against the hand derivations for the cantilever and the planted overstress (2% deflection, 5% stress after refinement); the rainflow counter of the sibling against the derivation's counts | KA-737, KA-738, KA-741, KA-742 |
| G3 · host | Metamorphic: rainflow in windows with carried residues equals the whole-history count (MR-34), on the reference sequence and on 1,000 random integer sequences split at random points | KA-742 |
| G4 · host | Planted failures: an open shell (refused), an oversize part (refused with the bound stated), a divergent loads–mass loop (refused within three iterations), each through the verb | KA-748, KA-743 |
| G5 · host | Determinism: the STEP round trip of the tetrahedron replays bit for bit (18 instances), twice on one machine and on both reference machines | KA-748 |

**3. Controls.** Every row's negative control is run and must fail the row: halved load (must halve, so a
fixed expected value fails); the unthinned beam (must pass, so a check that always reports 1.25 fails);
swapped ports; the assembling mutation; unsigned stress; the window split (7.816 × 10⁻⁶, 10.7% low); λ = 1.1
and the two-size cycle; a volume enclosure that excludes the exact volume; the quadratic feature; halved
conductor area; the open shell and the over-bound part.

**4. Gates cited.** The battery writes one receipt per gate clause it measures and the recording session
enters it in `registries/gates.toml`: GATE-FOUNDRY-01 (cantilever 2% and 5%; overstress 1.25 ± 5%), -02
(assembled-peak and equal-limb mutations refused; superposition and residual are measured by the loads bead's
script and cited here), -03 (10⁻¹²; split reproduces 10.7%), -04 (two-bar 10% and 5°), -06 (10⁻⁶; 0.01), -08
(10⁻⁹), -09 (bit-exact; refusals; 10⁻⁶), -10 (the packet clauses that are known answers: voltage drop and
slack inside a schema-valid packet).

**5. Independence.** The battery links `frob-conformance` and calls the released verbs; it shares no helper
with the mechanism crates for computing expected values, which come from the fixture files generated by the
derivations bead's recomputation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Thirteen registry rows (KA-737 to KA-748, KA-754) load, each with fixture, truth, tolerance, tier, site, derivation and at least one control; rows awaiting registration of a tolerance or a control are flagged not gate-eligible.
- [ ] Every row passes at its tier and site, and every control fails its row; both facts are in the receipts.
- [ ] The G0 `compile_fail` corpus has at least the three unit confusions named above.
- [ ] MR-34 holds on the reference sequence and on 1,000 random splits; the `finish-per-window` mutation breaks it.
- [ ] The STEP round trip is bit-exact on both reference machines.
- [ ] Each of the eight gates above has a receipt with measured values against thresholds, written by a session that did not implement the mechanism.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1 -- foundry` (and `g0`, `g2`, `g3`, `g4`, `g5` likewise): tests named
by row, for example `ka_737_cantilever_host`, `ka_737_cantilever_referee_parity`, `ka_742_rainflow_counts`,
`mr_34_window_residues`, `ka_748_step_roundtrip_bits`, `g4_divergent_loop_refused`. Expected values are those
of the table in section 2; tolerances are the gate's.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_known_answers.sh`: runs the six tier targets filtered to Foundry, then replays three rows
from the user's side (`frob foundry size --fixture cantilever --json`; `frob foundry check --structure
--fixture rainflow-sequence --json`; `frob foundry export --geometry-only --fixture tetrahedron --json`) and
compares with the registry's truths. Logs per STD-E2E to `target/e2e-logs/foundry_known_answers/`: per row
`event: assert` with the identifier, tier, site, truth, measured value, tolerance, and per control `caught`;
the summary counts rows passed, controls caught and lists the eight gates.

### Known answers and negative controls
All values in section 2, with their registry numbers. Negative controls: section 3, each traced to a record
in the negative-control registry (counterexamples CX-336 to CX-341 where they apply). Determinism lanes:
twice on one machine; across worker counts; debug and release (exact rows must be identical; finite-element
rows inside their tolerance); across the two reference machines for G5.

## Considerations

- A row that shares a fixture with a mechanism's unit test is still run here from outside: the point is a second, independent path to the same number.
- The parity tier compares a sibling library with hand derivations; the oracle is the derivation, never a stored output of the candidate.
- If a kill gate fires (loads, topology, tolerance, packet), the affected rows remain as regression tests of the fallback where they still apply (the cantilever, the fatigue block, the tolerance boxes) and are otherwise marked `not applicable: fallback in force`.
