## Background

GATE-FOUNDRY-05 is Foundry's one design-comparison gate and the test of the generative-structure bet: links
optimized under simulated load histories are lighter than engineered parts at equal margin. The bet is lost
if, on the reference links, the optimized part is less than 7% lighter than the engineered part at equal
stiffness and fatigue margin. The gate (TARGET, M5): on the stage-2 fixture the saving is at least 7% (the
kill threshold) with 15% the target; the convergence test of the loads–mass loop passes within five
iterations; the coarse solve takes at most 120 s per link class. If it fails, KILL-TOPOPT fires and the beam
rung supplies mass and inertia feedback.

The stage-2 pilot (N31b-07) ran this comparison by hand with scripts. This bead repeats it through the
released verbs, as a registered method experiment, and writes the gate row. A design comparison must have the
six parts of a method experiment or its result cannot be cited by a gate: a ceiling computed first; competing
arms including the strongest conventional alternative and the mechanism's own fallback; a potency control and
an A/A arm; a paired lower bound; a pre-declared fallback; a reachability pre-analysis.

Ceiling, written before the run: link structure is a fraction f_L of robot mass and the saving is s, so the
effect on the robot is at most f_L·s ≤ 0.27 × 0.30 ≈ 0.08; the gate concerns link mass only and makes no
claim about decision time.

## Scope

In: the registered protocol (the six parts of a method experiment); the script `gate_foundry_05.sh`; the
measurement of the three clauses over five paired campaigns; the controls; the gate row of GATE-FOUNDRY-05 with
its three kill-trigger fields. This is the only bead that writes that row.
Out: the mechanisms (BD-400 to BD-404); the end-to-end script of the verb `foundry.optimize`, which measures
GATE-FOUNDRY-04 and -06 and runs this comparison once on one campaign as a functional test (N31a-54, on which
this bead depends: the loop must run, pair its campaigns and report against the right baseline before five
campaigns are spent on it); the baseline and the equal-margin rule themselves (N31a-61); the kill decision
(N31b-15), which reads this bead's receipt.

## Technical approach

**1. Fixture.** The lower-leg link class of the reference quadruped, its wrought-alloy material card, and the
hand-sized thin-walled tube of the pilot as the engineered baseline (same ports, same peak load, sized by the
beam rung's closed forms; its derivation on file).

**2. Arms.** (a) The optimized link from `foundry.optimize --loop`; (b) the engineered tube; (c) the
mechanism's fallback: the beam rung's lightest passing stock section from `foundry.size`. Arms (b) and (c)
may coincide; both are reported.

**3. Pairs.** Five load campaigns on disjoint, registered world-address sets (the qualified task set plus
push-recovery and drop tasks, control-rate telemetry on every world). Within a pair, all arms are sized or
optimized under the same campaign. The loop's iterations reuse their campaign's addresses.

**4. Equal margin.** The rule is the one the baseline bead N31a-61 fixes, so that the loop and the gate count
the same thing: the reference mass is the tube's continuous-wall mass (never heavier than a stock section);
reference and optimized part go through the same independent check on a body-fitted mesh; the optimized mass
is read only when f₁,opt ≥ 0.98·f₁,ref (2% is the comparison tolerance of the two modal solves) and its
fatigue utilization is at most the reference's. A candidate that misses either is re-run once with a tighter
constraint and otherwise counts as saving zero.

**5. Quantities.**
- Saving per pair sᵢ = (m_tube,i − m_opt,i)/m_tube,i. Reported: the mean, and the one-sided 95% lower
  confidence bound on the mean over the five pairs from the harness's paired routine. Clause 1 passes when
  the bound is at least 0.07; the mean is reported against the 0.15 target.
- Convergence: in every pair the loop's test passes within five iterations (relative change at most 2% twice
  running; a-posteriori bound at most 2%; envelope movement at most 5%; the final geometry passes the
  independent check under its own loads). Clause 2 passes when all five do.
- Budget: wall time of the coarse solve per link class on the CPU cores of the M5 Ultra reference machine, largest over
  pairs and iterations. Clause 3 passes at 120 s or less.

**6. Controls.** Potency: an arm (b′) with the tube's wall doubled must show a saving above 0.40 (the harness
sees a planted effect). A/A: arm (c) against itself must give a saving of exactly 0 and a ratio that does not
clear 1.02. Independence: the planted overstressed link must be rejected by the check inside the pipeline
(utilization 1.25), so a saving cannot be bought by violating the margin. Address pairing: re-sampling
addresses between iterations in a test build must be flagged by the digest check.

**7. Reachability pre-analysis.** Registered before the run: with five pairs and the pilot's spread of
savings, the smallest true mean saving at which the lower bound reaches 0.07 with 80% probability. If the
pilot's spread makes 0.07 unreachable at five pairs, the number of pairs is raised before the run, never
after.

**8. Record.** The gate row for GATE-FOUNDRY-05: registration hash; per pair m_tube, m_opt, f₁, fatigue
utilization, iterations, λ̂ series, coarse-solve times; the bound; the three clause results; control results;
commit, hardware fingerprint and seeds; and one field for each of the three kill triggers, derived from the
clauses: `saving_under_7_percent` (the lower bound is below 0.07), `no_convergence` (fewer than five of five
pairs converge within five iterations), `over_budget` (the largest coarse-solve time exceeds 120 s). Status
`pass`, or `fail` handed to the kill decision with the triggers that are set.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The six parts are registered in the ledger and their sequence number precedes the first campaign.
- [ ] Five pairs are measured on disjoint registered address sets; every optimized mass is read at equal first bending frequency and equal fatigue utilization.
- [ ] The gate row holds the lower bound against 0.07, the mean against 0.15, convergence in five of five pairs within five iterations, and the largest coarse-solve time against 120 s.
- [ ] The potency control is detected (saving above 0.40), the A/A arm returns 0, the planted overstress is rejected, and the address-pairing violation is flagged.
- [ ] The script exits zero when the measurement is complete, whatever the outcome, and non-zero only when the measurement could not be completed.
- [ ] The receipt is recorded by a session that did not implement the mechanisms, with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-FOUNDRY-05 --receipt <hash>` (N38b-19); the row cannot be set to `pass` unless it stores the hash of the registered six-part form (`form_hash`, checked by the registry loader of N31b-12).
- [ ] Every `run` call carries a key from `foundry_key <label>` (STD-E2E's `e2e_key`), one label per call; no literal key is used, and the five pairs and the control arms carry distinct labels.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_foundry_05.rs`: `saving_definition` (0.20 kg against 0.17 kg → 0.15);
`paired_bound_constant` (five pairs of saving 0.12 → bound 0.12); `bound_below_threshold_fails` (savings 0.09,
0.05, 0.08, 0.06, 0.07 → mean 0.07, bound below 0.07 → clause 1 fails); `unequal_margin_counts_zero`;
`clause_table` (pass only if all three clauses pass); `registration_precedes_run`;
`kill_trigger_fields_derived_correctly` (a bound of 0.069 sets `saving_under_7_percent`; 0.070 does not).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gate_foundry_05.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper): for each of five address sets i runs `frob
foundry loads quad-ref --campaign <set-i> --key "$(foundry_key g05-<i>-loads)" --json` (its result names the
campaign identity `<camp-i>`), `frob foundry size quad-ref --links lower-leg --loads <camp-i> --json` (a `read`
verb: no key), `frob foundry optimize quad-ref --campaign <camp-i> --rung topology --links lower-leg --mode
coarse --loop --key "$(foundry_key g05-<i>-opt)" --json` and `frob foundry check <design-i> --link lower-leg
--campaign <camp-i> --key "$(foundry_key g05-<i>-check)" --json`, where `<design-i>` is the loop's final design
identity from the optimize result; then the control arms, each with its own key; then computes the bound.
Every arm of pair i names the same campaign, so the arms are sized under one load set, and the rung is named
explicitly: a defaulted rung falls back to `beam` with the reason `feature-absent` in a binary without the
`structure` group or with the KILL-TOPOPT switch set (VERBS-foundry), which would measure the fallback arm
against itself, while the explicit `--rung topology` is refused there with FROB-REF-790 (exit 5) and the script
stops with the measurement incomplete. The link-class flag is the verb registry's `--links` (settled with the verb's owner,
BD-403; the earlier reconciliation note on `--class` is closed by it). Every `run` verb call (loads, optimize,
check) carries its own idempotency key `--key "$(foundry_key <label>)"` from the cluster preamble (N31a-51),
whose `foundry_key <label>` is STD-E2E's `e2e_key <label>`: the label names the call and carries the pair
index (`g05-<i>-loads`, `g05-<i>-opt`, `g05-<i>-check`) or the control arm (`g05-potency-…`, `g05-aa-…`,
`g05-overstress-…`, `g05-resample-…`), so no two calls share a key; a label issued twice in a run is the
harness error, and every run of the script has its own state root, so no key meets a stored receipt of
another run or another build. Logs to
`target/e2e-logs/gate_foundry_05/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead` (`N31b-13`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; on a failed step the
first differing value, the pair, and the job and design identities to repeat it. Per pair the campaign identity and address digest,
`m_tube_kg`, `m_beam_kg`, `m_opt_kg`, `saving`, `f1_hz` of both, `u_fatigue` of both, `iterations`,
`lambda_hat`, `coarse_wall_s`; then `saving_lower_bound` (threshold 0.07), `saving_mean` (target 0.15),
`converged_pairs` (5 of 5), `coarse_wall_s_max` (120), control results, the evidence blocks (class Estimated),
and a `summary` naming GATE-FOUNDRY-05 with the three clause results.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers for the harness: constant-saving pairs return the saving; the planted-gain loop converges at
iteration four. Negative controls: section 6. Metamorphic: permuting the pairs changes nothing; scaling all
loads of a campaign by k leaves the saving approximately unchanged for stress-governed designs and is
reported, not asserted. Determinism: each pair re-run on the same machine gives the same masses; wall times
are reported with the machine fingerprint.

## Considerations

- The pilot's falsifier used 5%; the gate and the bet use 7%. A pilot that passed at 5% does not imply the gate.
- OPEN-FOUNDRY-4 blocks the fatigue leg for printed fixtures only: the gate fixture is wrought alloy. A printed fixture may be run beside it with `fatigue: no-claim` and is not part of the gate.
- Load cases here come from a fast tier and are provisional; the gate is about mass at equal computed margin, and a packet built from these designs still needs referee re-extraction.
- A saving on one link class is not a robot-level saving; the row prints the link mass share so that a reader can form f_L·s.
