## Background

North-Star scenario NS-20, "a build packet", is what Foundry owes the program at milestone M5. Before: the
design loop ends with a scored design. After: from a qualified design and task, Foundry emits a `.fbuild`
packet: a bill of materials against a pinned catalogue, faceted solid geometry, a tolerance stack that
closes, fatigue margins computed from the simulated load histories, and one link re-optimized to be lighter at
equal margin (the scenario's illustration says 18%). The packet's claim product is Estimated, because its
material cards are, and every file in it carries the three general no-authorization flags and the packet flag
`fabrication_released`, all false.

The program's definition of done has a clause for it: "the foundry is real" when a qualified design yields a
packet whose hashes match, whose geometry round trips, whose tolerance stack closes, and whose four flags are
present and false; or the reduced packet of the packet kill gate is in force. The milestone's demonstration is
the chain `frob foundry loads`, `size`, `optimize`, `check`, `tolerance` and `export`, ending in a packet
whose four flags are false.

Each verb has its own script in its own bead. This bead is the chain: one script, run from the user's side
with nothing but the released binary and a qualified design, that goes from telemetry to a verified packet and
shows that the pieces fit: the load cases the optimizer used are the ones the check used, the model identity
the evidence was computed with is the one in the manifest, and the packet survives the auditor.

## Scope

In: the pipeline script over the six verbs; the assertions that tie the chain together; the demonstration
manifest for NS-20; the variants under each kill gate's fallback.
Out: each verb's own end-to-end script and the gate measurements they own, on which this bead depends and
whose receipts it reads (`foundry.loads`: N31a-53; `foundry.optimize`: N31a-54, with the registered
GATE-FOUNDRY-05 measurement in N31b-13; `foundry.check`: N31a-55; `foundry.tolerance`: N31a-56;
`foundry.export` with GATE-FOUNDRY-09 and -10 and the packet-content record: N31a-57); the auditor's mutation
set (N31b-44); hidden-brief runs (N31b-34). This script re-measures only what the chain itself establishes.

## Technical approach

**Preconditions.** A qualified finalist of the reference quadruped (`DesignId`, `ModelId`, verdict
identity), its task set, a material card and a process card per made part, and seeds. All are fixtures with
recorded identities.

**Steps** (each `frob … --json`; long steps return a job handle with exit 8 and are awaited with `frob job
wait`):
1. `frob foundry loads <design> --campaign ns20 --variants stiffness,law` → envelope cases and spectra per
   link class; assert the free-body residual bound, the dropped fraction, and that every case has a world
   address, an episode and a tick.
2. Referee re-extraction (the default behaviour of step 1 when a packet is intended): assert every envelope
   case is `established` or `tier-sensitive` with both tier values and a replay address, and none is
   `provisional`.
3. `frob foundry size <design> --loads <campaign>` → the beam-rung section and structural margin per link.
4. `frob foundry optimize <design> --links lower-leg --loop` → the optimized link, the loop record
   (converged within five iterations), the new `DesignId` and `ModelId`, the saving against the beam rung.
5. `frob foundry check <design'>` → the independent structural check with its mesh hash, and the
   manufacturability report.
6. `frob foundry tolerance <design'> --qoi foot-position` → the allocation; assert the stack closes (the
   variance budget is met and every band-extreme result is inside the specification or boxed).
7. `frob foundry export <design'>` → the packet address.
8. `frob-verify --packet <packet>` → accept; then a copy with one flag flipped → reject.
9. `frob foundry check <packet>` → the mechanical pre-review items M1 to M6 all pass; `review` reads
   `no-claim`.

**Assertions that tie the chain together.** The campaign identity in the packet's `structure/` equals step
1's. The `ModelId` in the manifest equals step 4's final model (mass properties equal within 10⁻⁶). Every
geometry in the packet cites the mesh hash of step 5. The qualification verdict in the manifest is for the
exported `DesignId` and `ModelId` (re-qualified after the edit) and not for the pre-optimization design. The
envelope's `evidence.class` is `Estimated` at every step and `reality` is `no-claim`. The four flags are
`false` in the manifest, in the envelope of step 7, and in each generated view header. The parts list has
card references and quantities; prices appear only if a pinned, licensed catalogue is registered, otherwise
the script asserts their absence.

**Variants under fallbacks.** The script reads the kill-gate rows first and adapts: if KILL-TOPOPT fired,
step 4 uses the beam rung for feedback and the "one link lighter" assertion is replaced by "beam-rung section
recorded"; if KILL-LOADS fired, step 1 takes hand-declared cases and fatigue reads `no-claim`; if KILL-FAB
fired, the fab report is advisory and the packet records no manufacturability pass; if KILL-TOLERANCE fired,
step 6 returns worst-case boxes; if KILL-PACKET fired, the packet holds the parts list, mass properties and
STL only. In each case the scenario is recorded as demonstrated under the fallback's name.

**Demonstration manifest.** `docs/demos/ns-20.toml` (path DESIGN): the scenario's claims (packet exists and
verifies; geometry round trips; stack closes; fatigue utilizations present with their law; saving recorded;
flags false), each with a command that runs in under 60 s against the stored packet, and the claim
identifiers the gate rows rest on.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script runs from a clean store to a verified packet with exit 0 on the reference quadruped, using only released verbs.
- [ ] All chain assertions hold: campaign identity, model identity with mass equality at 10⁻⁶, mesh hashes cited, verdict for the exported design, class Estimated, `reality: no-claim`, four flags false everywhere.
- [ ] No envelope case in the packet is `provisional`.
- [ ] The saving of the re-optimized link against the engineered baseline is recorded in the log with its equal-margin conditions (no threshold is asserted here; the comparison gate owns it).
- [ ] Under each fired kill gate the corresponding variant runs and the scenario is recorded under the fallback's name.
- [ ] The NS-20 demonstration manifest exists and each of its commands completes in under 60 s.
- [ ] The run is repeated by the M5 exit review in a fresh session.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The script's helper functions have shell-level self-tests under `scripts/e2e/tests/`: `flags_all_false`
(accepts four `false`; rejects a missing flag and a `true`); `model_identity_matches`;
`kill_row_selects_variant` (a fixture registry with KILL-TOPOPT fired selects the beam-rung variant).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_pipeline_ns20.sh`. It sources the shared helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/foundry_pipeline_ns20/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript: for each step an `event:
cmd` line with the exact command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier,
determinism, identities, seed); `event: metric` lines for `free_body_residual_max`, `cases_established`,
`cases_tier_sensitive`, `loop_iterations`, `saving`, `u_peak`, `buckling_factor_min`,
`fatigue_utilization_max`, `stack_budget_met`, `mass_rel_diff_max` (1e-6), `flags_false` (4 of 4);
`event: assert` lines for each chain assertion with digests where identities are compared; on failure the
first differing value and the artifact paths; a `summary` with assertion counts, the variant in force and the
gates touched. No private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: four flags; tolerance 10⁻⁶ on mass properties; five iterations at most. Negative controls,
each run by the script against a copy: a manifest citing the pre-optimization verdict (the verdict assertion
must fail); a geometry swapped after the check (the mesh-hash assertion must fail); a flipped flag (the
auditor must reject); a provisional case injected into the request (the export must refuse). Determinism: two
runs from the same fixtures and seeds give the same packet address.

## Considerations

- The 18% of the scenario text is an illustration; the measured saving is whatever the fixture gives, and the comparison gate decides whether the topology rung survives.
- "A bill of materials against a pinned catalogue" depends on the owner's decision on catalogue and licence; without it the parts list has card references and no prices, and the script asserts that.
- The pipeline is CPU-hours when the topology rung runs; the script has a `--coarse` mode for routine runs and the full mode for the milestone record.
- Re-qualification after the mass edit is part of the chain: a packet that cites the old verdict is a defect the pre-review exists to catch.
