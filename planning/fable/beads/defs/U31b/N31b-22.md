## Background

Lifecycle compression (a Moonshot of Foundry, crate `frob-lifecycle`, feature `lifecycle`) advances a robot's
slow states (backlash per joint from wear, fatigue damage per gauge, winding insulation, cell capacity and
resistance) by large macro steps, estimating their averaged rates from short windows of full simulation at
fixed slow state, and returning to full resolution when the gait is about to change character. Every output
is class Estimated, and the compression it reports is a ratio of simulated steps, never of wall time or
field time.

GATE-FOUNDRY-12 (TARGET): a 100-hour reference (1.8 × 10⁸ steps at 500 Hz) compressed at least 50-fold in
steps with at most 5% error on every slow state; the planted backlash-induced gait collapse caught. The
chapter labelled the row "Performance of the method"; the gate register reads it as a Fidelity claim: a
compressed run agrees with a 100-hour reference within a stated error. The register places it at M6 with the
lifecycle epic. If it fails, KILL-LIFECYCLE fires and the fallback is aged model variants at hand-specified
wear states, with no time axis and no compression claim.

This bead is the end-to-end measurement of that gate through the released verb. It runs only if the pilot
was positive and the epic was funded.

## Scope

In: the reference run; the compressed run; the planted-collapse variant; the comparison; the known answers of
the method on a robot-free fixture; the gate row.
Out: the mechanism; the pilot (N31b-11); coefficient data (N31b-28); the kill decision (N31b-23).

## Technical approach

**1. Fixture.** The reference quadruped trotting on flat ground under its baseline controller, with a duty
profile registered in advance (DESIGN: continuous trot with a registered payload schedule). Cards for wear,
insulation and cell fade are *synthetic* cards registered for the gate, with coefficients scaled so that each
slow state moves by a measurable fraction (at least 10% of its threshold) within 100 hours; they are labelled
`synthetic` and every result of this bead prints that label. Slow states compared: backlash β of each
actuated joint; damage at each fatigue gauge; consumed insulation life per winding; capacity fade f_Q and
resistance growth f_R.

**2. Reference.** Full simulation of 100 hours: 1.8 × 10⁸ steps, with every slow law integrated at full
resolution (wear volume advanced from the recorded sliding distance and load each step; rainflow fed every
sample with residues carried; insulation rate evaluated at each step's winding temperature; cell states
advanced from current), recorded hourly. It runs once per backend used for the gate and is stored by
identity.

**3. Compressed run.** `frob foundry lifecycle <design> --duty <profile> --hours 100 --json` on the same
backend and profile. The envelope reports every slow state hourly, the macro steps taken and rejected, the
micro windows with their settling residuals, every return to full resolution with its trigger, and the total
of simulated fast steps (all of them: settling, averaging, rejected steps, full-resolution stretches).

**4. Clauses.**
- *Compression*: reference steps divided by compressed steps is at least 50 (at most 3.6 × 10⁶ fast steps).
- *Error*: for every slow state, the relative difference from the reference at every hourly mark is at most
  5% (relative to the reference's change from its initial value, with an absolute floor registered in
  advance for states that barely move).
- *Collapse caught*: in the planted variant, the wear coefficient of one knee is raised so that backlash
  reaches a registered value β* at which the trot's orbit loses stability (a Floquet multiplier reaches the
  unit circle) inside the 100 hours. The compressed run must return to full resolution before β* is reached
  and must report the collapse (the gait's failure and its time) within 5% of the reference's time. A run
  that macro-steps across β* fails the clause even if its final slow states are within tolerance.

**5. Known answers of the method** (robot-free fixtures run through the same verb with `--fixture`):
averaged growth ż = ε·a·x² with x = sin ωt gives z(T) = ε·a·T/2 over whole periods; wear feedback
β̇ = ε·(c₀ + c₁·β) gives β(t) = (β₀ + c₀/c₁)·exp(ε·c₁·t) − c₀/c₁; rate averaging with life halving per 10 K,
half the time at T_ref and half at T_ref + 20 K, gives 2.5/τ_ref and not 2.0/τ_ref; cell fade of 1% per
√month calendar and 0.5% per 100 cycles over 9 months and 300 cycles gives 4.5%.

**6. Controls.** `average-input-then-law` (must miss the insulation state by 20%: 2.0 against 2.5);
`finish-per-window` on the fatigue state (must lose the half cycles that span windows); `no-floquet-trigger`
(must step across β*); a potency run at 500-fold compression (must exceed 5% or refuse its step size).

**7. Record.** The gate row for GATE-FOUNDRY-12: steps of both runs, the compression, the largest error per
slow-state family, the collapse times, control results, the cards' `synthetic` label, commit, backend,
hardware fingerprint and seeds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The reference of 1.8 × 10⁸ steps exists with hourly slow states and is stored by identity.
- [ ] The compressed run uses at most 3.6 × 10⁶ fast steps (all kinds counted) and every slow state is within 5% at every hourly mark.
- [ ] In the planted variant the run returns to full resolution before β* and reports the collapse time within 5% of the reference.
- [ ] The four known answers pass and the four controls fail as stated.
- [ ] Every output is class Estimated, carries the `synthetic` card label, and prints the compression as a ratio of simulated steps; a law evaluated outside its card's domain refuses with FROB-REF-831.
- [ ] The gate row holds measured values; the script exits zero when the measurement is complete, whatever the outcome.

results, the evidence block with class Estimated; the summary names GATE-FOUNDRY-12.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-750 (averaged growth; 5% at a compression of 50), KA-751 (wear feedback; control: a planted gait collapse
at β* must force full resolution before β*), KA-752 (rate averaging; control: averaging the input is caught),
KA-753 (cell fade 4.5%). Counterexample: the law of the average is not the average of the law. Negative
controls: section 6. Determinism: reference and compressed runs are each identical twice on one backend
(class D2); across backends the comparison is a tolerance comparison and is reported separately.

## Considerations

- The gate uses synthetic coefficients because cards with provenance do not exist yet (open question on wear, insulation and cell-fade data). Passing the gate says the *method* reproduces a reference under given laws; it says nothing about real service duration.
- The reference is the most expensive single run in Foundry; it is run once per backend and reused.
- If the actuator chapter's ageing kill gate fired, the cell states are user-set constants: the cell family is then excluded from the error clause and the row says so.
- Averaging can fail quietly near a gait bifurcation; the collapse clause is the gate's guard against a result that is accurate on average and wrong where it matters.
