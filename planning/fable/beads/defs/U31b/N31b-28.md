## Background

Lifecycle compression (a Moonshot of Foundry) advances four slow states by empirical laws whose coefficients
come from cards: backlash per joint from Archard wear volume k·F_n·s/H mapped to recession by a card geometry
factor; fatigue damage per gauge; winding insulation by an Arrhenius rate in winding temperature; cell
capacity and resistance by calendar fade in √t plus fade per unit of charge throughput. The method can be
tested on synthetic coefficients. A statement about a real robot's service duration cannot be made without
coefficients that have provenance.

FACT: the sibling libraries have Archard wear volume from caller-supplied sliding distance and load with no
recession geometry and no map from volume to backlash, a battery without capacity fade or resistance growth,
and no cell-ageing law and no insulation-life law anywhere in the workspace. So the coefficients do not exist
in any dependency; they must come from data sheets, literature or measurement.

OPEN-FOUNDRY-5. Question: wear, insulation and cell-fade coefficients with provenance. Fail-closed default,
in force until decided: generic cards marked `assumed`, which `--strict-data` refuses. Owner: the Sinew lead.
It blocks GATE-FOUNDRY-12. The actuator chapter's kill gate KILL-SINEW-AGE has a matching clause: it fires if
no card with ageing coefficients better than `assumed` exists when the lifecycle work is funded, and then
capacity fade and resistance growth are user-set constants per scenario with no evolution law.

## Scope

In: the card fields per law with their validity domains; provenance classes; the options; how the gate may
proceed under the default; the decision record.
Out: the laws' implementation and the card reader (N31a-48, whose step 7 carries the coefficients and the
`assumed` marking) and the verb `foundry.lifecycle` (N31a-50): both are dependencies, because this bead's tests
and script drive that code with the field list and rules fixed below; fatigue stress-life curves (the
printed-material data question); producing wear or ageing measurements.

## Technical approach

1. **Card fields per law**, the fields the laws of N31a-48 read (that bead, a dependency, writes the reader;
   this bead fixes the provenance each field must carry and checks it), each with a provenance class, the
   card classes of N31a-22 (`measured` with dataset, `datasheet` with its source cited, `generic`, `assumed`),
   and a validity domain outside which the law refuses (FROB-REF-831):
   - *Wear*: the coefficient c_w (rad of backlash per joule of transmitted work, the one N31a-48 reads), with
     its constituents recorded beside it so its provenance can be checked (DESIGN): wear coefficient k
     (dimensionless), hardness H, the geometry factor γ from worn volume to backlash angle, the slip fraction σ,
     with c_w = γ·k·σ/H; the contact pair it applies to; domain: contact pressure range, sliding speed range,
     lubrication state, temperature.
   - *Insulation*: reference life τ_ref at T_ref, the temperature increment for halving ΔT_half (10 K in the
     common form) or an activation energy; domain: temperature range, insulation class.
   - *Cell*: the battery card's ageing group, defined once by N15b-17 in `frob-actuate` (k_cal per √time,
     k_cyc per ampere-hour of throughput, r_cal and r_cyc for resistance growth; validity group: temperature,
     `depth_of_discharge`, `current_rate`, `f_q_max`); this bead declares no second cell field and only
     requires the provenance class on each.
2. **Rules.** A lifecycle output carries the weakest provenance class among the coefficients it used. With
   any `assumed` coefficient the output is labelled `assumed` and `--strict-data` refuses it. An output is at
   most class Estimated whatever the provenance. A law never runs outside its domain: it refuses, naming the
   domain and the card needed.
3. **How the gate proceeds under the default.** GATE-FOUNDRY-12 is a Fidelity claim about the *method*
   (compressed against a full-resolution reference under the same laws). It may be measured with synthetic
   cards registered for the gate and labelled `synthetic`. What stays blocked until this question closes is
   any lifecycle output about a real design that is offered without the `assumed` label. The gate row
   therefore records `cards: synthetic`, and the row's status may be `pass` for the method while this
   question remains open; the release wording must then say that no service-duration figure is supported.
   The owner confirms or overrides this reading (the question's table entry says it blocks the gate).
4. **Options put to the owner.**
   - A. *Keep the default*: generic cards marked `assumed`; lifecycle answers are comparative only.
   - B. *Cited coefficients* for the reference robots' actuator, bearing and cell types, one source each.
   - C. *Coefficients through the calibration lane*: measured wear and fade imported as datasets; the card is
     updated as a new revision with fitted values.
   - D. *Close by kill*: if the actuator chapter's ageing kill gate fires and no wear data exists, the
     lifecycle epic's time axis has no data to stand on and the owner may recommend its fallback.
5. **Record.** `docs/adr/OPEN-FOUNDRY-5.md` with the option, sources or datasets, the reading of "blocks
   GATE-FOUNDRY-12" adopted, and the Sinew lead's signature; the register of open questions updated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every coefficient the laws of N31a-48 read (wear and insulation fields above, the cell group of N15b-17) carries a provenance class and a validity domain in the card schema; a coefficient without a class is a card load error.
- [ ] A lifecycle run with an `assumed` coefficient is labelled `assumed` and is refused under `--strict-data`; a law outside its domain refuses with FROB-REF-831.
- [ ] The decision is recorded and signed by the Sinew lead, including the adopted reading of what the question blocks; or the default is recorded as still in force at the M6 exit.
- [ ] The gate row for GATE-FOUNDRY-12 states the provenance of the cards it used.
- [ ] No lifecycle output about a real design appears without its provenance label on any branch.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-lifecycle cards`: `weakest_provenance_wins` (measured wear + assumed cell → `assumed`);
`strict_data_refuses_assumed`; `law_outside_domain_refuses_831` (winding at 190 °C against a card valid to
155 °C); `arrhenius_halving_form` (life consumed at 2ⁿ/τ_ref for T_ref + 10·n K: 1, 2, 4 for n = 0, 1, 2);
`cell_fade_form` (1% per √month and 0.5% per 100 cycles over 9 months and 300 cycles: 4.5%);
`archard_volume` (k = 10⁻⁵, F_n = 100 N, s = 1,000 m, H = 2 GPa: 5 × 10⁻¹⁰ m³, that is 0.5 mm³);
`wear_coefficient_from_constituents` (γ = 2 × 10⁶ rad/m³, k = 10⁻⁵, σ = 0.05, H = 2 GPa: c_w = 5 × 10⁻¹⁰ rad/J;
a card whose stored c_w differs from its constituents by more than 1% is a load error naming both);
`coefficient_without_class_refused`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_lifecycle_cards.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N31b-28 milestone=M6
labels=foundry,moonshot,owner-decision gates=GATE-FOUNDRY-12 seed=0 needs=cpu
plants=label-dropped,domain-extrapolated`; sources the STD-E2E helper and the cluster preamble of N31a-51;
`foundry.lifecycle` is a `run` verb, so each call carries its own idempotency key `$(foundry_key <step>)`, from
step and input digest, cards included): runs `frob foundry lifecycle --fixture slow-wear --key $(foundry_key
flc-1) --json` (`slow-wear` is this bead's fixture, an entry of kind `lifecycle` it adds to the fixture index of
N31a-51: a wear law and an insulation law on generic cards marked `assumed`, a short duty with the winding
inside the insulation card's domain), asserting the `assumed` label; with `--strict-data` (`$(foundry_key
flc-2)`), asserting `FROB-REF-113`, exit 3, naming the card and field; with the insulation card replaced by one
whose temperature domain ends below the fixture's winding temperature (`$(foundry_key flc-3)`), asserting
FROB-REF-831 with the domain and the card needed. Logs to `target/e2e-logs/foundry_lifecycle_cards/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a
transcript: every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31b-28`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit
code and wall time; per law the card identity, provenance class and domain check; the evidence block (class
Estimated); on a failed assertion the field, expected and observed values and the card path. The summary
counts labelled outputs and refusals.

### Known answers and negative controls
Cell fade (4.5%); rate averaging (2.5/τ_ref against 2.0/τ_ref). Negative controls: a run that drops the
provenance label when one of several coefficients is `assumed`; a law extrapolated outside its domain
without refusing.

## Considerations

- Accelerated estimates can change the failure mechanism; a validity domain per law is the guard, and it is the reason a law refuses instead of extrapolating.
- Wear coefficients vary by orders of magnitude with lubrication; a generic value supports a comparison between two designs under the same assumption and nothing more.
- This question and the actuator chapter's ageing question share an owner on purpose: the cell coefficients are the same data.
