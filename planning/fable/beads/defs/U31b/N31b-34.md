## Background

GATE-FOUNDRY-13 is the gate of the packet-review protocol. It covers the mechanical half only; the human
half is a recorded count that no gate depends on. The gate (Known answer, TARGET, M5): on the 12 hidden
briefs every outcome is a packet that passes pre-review items M1 to M6 or a typed refusal naming a binding
constraint (12 of 12); no cross-export difference exceeds 10⁻⁶; the planted unit error and the planted stale
verdict are each caught in 100 of 100 runs; importing a record leaves the packet digest unchanged; a record
with a wrong packet digest is refused. The fail-closed default without a reviewer is `review: no-claim`, and
the packet kill gate is unchanged by this subsection.

The parts exist in other beads: the sealed briefs with their commit-before-freeze protocol; the unattended
runner; the six-item pre-review; the review-record import. This bead is the one run that spends the real
hidden briefs. It can be run once per brief set: a brief is used once and then retired. So the script is
rehearsed on synthetic briefs until it is trusted, and the real run is made by a session that did not
implement the pipeline.

## Scope

In: the rehearsal on synthetic briefs; the single real run; the five clauses' measurement; the gate row; the
retirement of the briefs; the recorded (ungated) human count if records exist.
Out: building any of the parts; the kill-gate decision on packets, which reads other gates.

## Technical approach

**1. Preconditions checked by the script before any brief is opened.** The ledger holds the brief-set commit
record and, after it, the pipeline-freeze record; the running binary's digest equals the frozen one; no brief
of the set has a use record; the rehearsal on synthetic briefs passed with this binary.

**2. Clause A: twelve outcomes.** For each brief the unattended runner produces a run record. The outcome is
valid if it is (a) a packet on which `frob foundry check <packet>` passes M1 to M6, or (b) a typed refusal
whose binding constraint resolves to an item of the brief with evidence. Pass: 12 valid of 12. An `invalid`
outcome (crash, untyped error, unaccounted input, a packet failing any item) fails the clause.

**3. Clause B: cross-export identity.** Over all packets produced: the largest relative difference of mass,
centre of mass and inertia among the model, `mass.json`, each geometry export and the URDF and MJCF exports.
Pass: at most 10⁻⁶.

**4. Clause C: planted errors, 100 of 100 each.** On copies of the produced packets (never the originals),
with placements drawn from a registered seed: 100 runs in which one surface file of a randomly chosen part
and format is rewritten in millimetres (item M2 must fail, report the ratio 10⁹ and name the file); 100 runs
in which the manifest's verdict is replaced by the verdict of the design's pre-snap or pre-optimization
ancestor (item M5 must fail). If fewer than two packets were produced, the placements use the rehearsal's
synthetic packets and the row says so.

**5. Clause D: import leaves the digest unchanged.** For each produced packet a schema-valid review record is
imported (a real one if an independent reviewer supplied it; otherwise a record written for the test and
labelled `synthetic`, which is deleted afterwards and never counted). The packet's file hashes and address
are compared before and after.

**6. Clause E: wrong digest refused.** For each packet, a record naming another packet's digest is offered
and must be refused, with nothing stored: exit 3 with `detail: "review-record-packet-mismatch"` (N31b-33;
row 4 of N31b-45, whose number the allocation ledger has not yet assigned, so the clause reads the detail and
the exit, and the code once it exists).

**7. After the run.** Every brief gets its use record and is retired to the development corpus. The gate row
(the GATE-FOUNDRY-13 row of the Foundry gate registry, N31b-12) is written through the gate recorder N38b-19,
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-FOUNDRY-13 --receipt <hash>` (the hash of
the `--real` run's receipt), which turns the receipt into the row and requires the run's negative controls to
have been caught in the same run, with: set digest; frozen pipeline identity; per brief the outcome kind, the packet address or the
refusal code and binding constraint; clause results A to E with their numbers; the wall time per brief as a
recorded fact; commit, hardware fingerprint and seed.

**8. The recorded human half.** If independent review records were imported, the row's notes carry the count
of `accept` and `accept-with-changes` out of the packets produced against the target of 11 of 12, total
reviewer minutes and the rubric failures. If none, the notes read `review count: no-claim`. Neither affects
the gate's status.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script refuses to open any brief unless the commit-before-freeze order and the binary identity check pass.
- [ ] The rehearsal on synthetic briefs passes before the real run, and the real run is made once.
- [ ] The gate row holds: valid outcomes (12 of 12 required), largest cross-export difference (at most 10⁻⁶), unit error caught (100 of 100), stale verdict caught (100 of 100), digests unchanged by import (all packets), wrong-digest records refused (all packets).
- [ ] Every brief of the set is retired after the run and cannot be opened again.
- [ ] The row's notes carry the human count or `no-claim`, and the README status generated from the registry does not present that count as a gate.
- [ ] No brief content and no private data appear in the logs.
- [ ] The run is recorded by a fresh-context session that did not implement the pipeline.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_foundry_13.rs`: `clause_table` (the gate passes only if A to E all
pass); `eleven_of_twelve_valid_fails_clause_a`; `refusal_without_binding_constraint_is_invalid`;
`placements_are_reproducible_from_seed` (the 100 placements are the same for the same seed and packet set);
`synthetic_review_records_are_never_counted`; `preconditions_block_early_open`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gate_foundry_13.sh` with two modes: `--rehearsal` (synthetic briefs; may be run any number of
times) and `--real` (the sealed set; refuses a second run). It drives the runner, `frob foundry check
<packet> --key $(foundry_key g13-<brief>-<step>) --json` (a `run` verb: every call, including each planted
copy's and each record import's, carries its own idempotency key from the cluster preamble of N31a-51, whose
input digest includes the frozen binary's identity, so a rehearsal on a new binary is measured afresh rather
than answered from a stored envelope of the last one), the planted copies and the record imports. It sources the
STD-E2E helper and logs to `target/e2e-logs/gate_foundry_13/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: every
line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31b-34`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time;
the set digest and pipeline identity; per brief an identifier and digest (never content), the verb sequence
with exit codes, the outcome and, for packets, the six item results; `valid_outcomes` (12 of 12),
`mass_rel_diff_max` (1e-6), `unit_error_caught` (100 of 100), `stale_verdict_caught` (100 of 100),
`digest_unchanged` (n of n), `wrong_digest_refused` (n of n); the evidence blocks; on a clause failure the
first failing brief or placement with the expected and observed values and the path of the packet copy to
repeat it; a `summary` naming GATE-FOUNDRY-13 with the five clause results. Exit zero when the measurement is
complete, whatever the result.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: the tube part reproduced at 0.0950 kg from each export within 10⁻⁶; the millimetre file's
ratio 10⁹; the pre-snap verdict failing M5. Negative controls, run in rehearsal mode and again inside the
`--real` run on synthetic briefs and packet copies (so that no hidden brief is spent and the receipt the
recorder reads holds them `caught` in the same run, N38b-19): a runner that emits a packet for an infeasible
brief (clause A must fail on the pre-review); an importer that rewrites the manifest (clause D must fail); a
pre-review with item M2 disabled (clause C must fail at 0 of 100). A second `--real` run must be refused; that
is checked after the recording and logged, since it cannot sit in the receipt it would repeat. Determinism: with the frozen binary and registered seeds the rehearsal gives identical
outcomes twice.

## Considerations

- The real run consumes the brief set. A failure followed by a repair needs a new set; this is the price of a test that cannot be tuned to.
- The packet kill gate reads the geometry and packet gates, not this one. A failure here blocks the M5 exit for Foundry as a failed convergence gate with no kill gate of its own, until repaired and re-measured on a new set.
- If a packet-related kill gate has fired, the outcomes are reduced packets and the pre-review runs in its reduced form; the row records that.
- Wall time per brief is recorded and compared with nothing; the only place a time from brief to accepted packet is measured is the ten-fold protocol.
