## Background

Foundry extracts envelope load cases for each link from a load campaign: many episodes with control-rate
traces on every world. Such a campaign runs on a fast tier, because it needs every world traced and many
episodes. Peak contact and joint wrenches are exactly the quantities a compliant fast-tier contact model gets
wrong, and the existing rule on impact loads (re-extract with contact stiffness halved and doubled and under
the alternative contact law) bounds only the part of the error that moves with contact stiffness.

The program already has a rule for results computed on a fast tier, the trust ladder: such a result is
`provisional` and becomes `established` only after re-evaluation on a higher tier with the difference inside
a frozen envelope; a result that fails is `tier-sensitive` and is reported with both numbers. This bead
applies that rule to load cases: **load cases are promoted like any other result**. It adds a seventh rule to
load extraction:

7. *Envelope cases are re-extracted on the referee before they enter a packet.*

Why a small tier bias matters here more than in a task score: cumulative damage scales as stress range to the
power m of the material card's stress-life law. A load that is 10% high gives 1.1⁵ = 1.61 times the damage at
m = 5 and 1.1¹⁰ = 2.59 times at m = 10. An error that would be negligible in a task score changes a predicted
life by a factor of two.

Rejected alternatives: running the whole campaign on the referee (it cannot trace every world of a large
campaign in reasonable time); sampling a few cases for audit (every case that sizes a part is consequential,
and there are few of them, so all are re-extracted). No new kill gate: the rule is a use of promotion. Tier:
Solid [S].

## Scope

In: the replay window; reading the referee's own maximum; the comparison against the frozen envelope; the
three labels; the re-search of a direction when a case is tier-sensitive; adjudication by the strict CPU
profile when the referee is unavailable; the re-extraction record; the cost report; the fields `foundry.loads`
returns.
Out: spectra and the packet-side refusal (N31b-36); the gate's planted artifact and counts (N31b-37); the
extraction itself (BD-400).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs per envelope case.** The case's sample reference (world address, episode, tick t*), its
direction d in scaled load space, its fast-tier coordinates L_fast and projection p_fast = d·(L_fast/s), and
the campaign's identities.

**2. Replay.** The world and instant that produced the case are replayed on the referee from the nearest
snapshot (the teleport mechanism) over a window of 0.5 s on either side of t* (DESIGN). The snapshot is a
fast-tier snapshot restored under the referee's profile, so this is a comparison replay, not a continuation;
the record says so. The same scene draw, disturbances, randomization and controller are used (the world
address fixes them).

**3. Read.** Inside the window the referee's port wrenches and body field are computed at every control
tick; the instant t_ref of the referee's own maximum of d·(L/s) is found; the coordinates L_ref at t_ref are
the referee's value for the case. All ports and the body field come from that one referee instant, so the
case stays time-coherent. The first 0.05 s of the window (DESIGN) is a settling stretch after the restore and
is excluded from the maximum search (see Considerations); the record keeps the window and the excluded span.

**4. Compare and label.** With the ledger's frozen envelope ε for port-wrench projection on this stratum
(N16bx-04's quantity, read by `EnvelopeId`). The disagreement δ = |p_ref − p_fast|/|p_ref|, the ± 0.5 s window
maximum and the floor are N16bx-04's one exported function in `frob-dyn`, and the projection p = d·(L ⊘ s) is
N31a-10's function; this bead calls both and re-implements neither, so the ledger's measurement and the
consumer compute the same number:
- `established` when |p_ref − p_fast| ≤ ε·|p_ref| (the two agree inside the fidelity envelope for wrenches).
  The referee's value is used.
- `tier-sensitive` when they do not. The referee's value replaces the fast one, and the direction is
  searched again among the neighbouring fast-tier samples: the next candidates for direction d in the
  campaign (DESIGN: the five samples with the largest fast-tier projection that lie outside the window, in
  other episodes or worlds) are each re-extracted the same way; the case becomes the candidate with the
  largest *referee* projection among those examined, and every candidate examined is recorded.
- `provisional: referee-unavailable` when the referee cannot complete the scenario (it reports its own
  coverage limit, for example an island above its contact cap). The `strict` profile of `batch-cpu` then
  adjudicates by the same replay and comparison, and the label names it:
  `provisional: referee-unavailable (adjudicated: batch-cpu:strict)`. If the strict profile cannot complete
  the window either, the label is `provisional: referee-unavailable (unadjudicated)`.
- `provisional: below-envelope-floor` when |p_ref| < p_floor = 0.05 (the envelope's stated domain, N16bx-04
  item 8: a projection under 5% of the campaign scale is not an envelope candidate and δ is ill-conditioned
  there). The referee's value is still the one reported.
An envelope that is not frozen in the ledger is not used: the re-extraction refuses with the trust ladder's
"envelope not frozen" refusal (FROB-REF-821; repair: freeze the envelope first).

**5. The record.** Each case then carries both values, the tier pair and its label:
`ReExtraction { case, world_addr, episode, tick_fast, tick_adjudicated, window_s: [−0.5, 0.5], snapshot,
replay: "comparison", tier_pair: [fast, adjudicator], contact_variant, fast: { coords, projection },
adjudicated: { coords, projection }, ratio: p_ref/p_fast, envelope: { id, epsilon, ledger hash }, label,
neighbours_examined: [ … ], referee_seconds }`.

**6. What `foundry.loads` returns.** Each envelope case with both values, its label and the replay address
(world address, episode, snapshot identity and tick), so that a caller can reproduce the window with `frob
sim replay`. Cases not yet re-extracted are returned with label `provisional`. Re-extraction runs by default
when the caller declares a packet as the purpose (`--for packet`) and on request otherwise.

**7. Cost.** One referee window per envelope case. A two-port link has 18 load coordinates, so a direction
set of 2 × 18 axis directions gives at most 36 windows of 1 s per link class, and three to five classes on a
symmetric quadruped give 108 to 180 referee-seconds of simulated time per iteration of the structure loop
(DESIGN arithmetic). The verb reports referee-seconds per link class and in total; tier-sensitive re-searches
add up to five windows per affected case.

**8. Class and evidence.** A re-extracted case is still class Estimated. Its tier is the adjudicator's; its
determinism class is the weakest of the two runs.

**9. The planted artifact this bead writes.** The counterexample registry (N31-CX, late entry L31-2: "a tier
bias that is negligible in a task score is negligible in a damage sum") names this bead as the planting bead
of its control, kind `planted-world`, plant `N31b-35:fast-tier-stiffness-spike`. The plant is a fixture, not a
Cargo feature: `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/foundry/fast-tier-stiffness-spike.toml`, with the `synthetic`
kind header, holding one registered placement on the reference quadruped's campaign (a foot contact, an
episode, a tick interval of 20 ms inside a stance phase) and the plant file `frob.conformance-plant/1` for it:
a `stiffness` section with one `StiffnessPlant` (world address, contact selector, ticks, factor) under
N18bx-03's rules (the fast tier's `lockstep` profile only; factor in [1/16, 16]; a snapshot at least 0.5 s
before the interval), the factor tuned once so that the peak of the governing direction is doubled on the fast
tier (ratio fast to referee between 1.9 and 2.1). It enters a run by one route only, the conformance build's
`--conformance-plant <file>` input of `sim.run` (N29a-25), and the referee replay carries no plant. Its
`removed_check` names this bead's re-extraction (`frob_foundry::loads::reextract`). Expected: with the
re-extraction in place the spiked case is `tier-sensitive`, the referee's value replaces the fast one and the
neighbour search is recorded; with it removed the doubled peak enters the packet unrelabelled. The seeding
tool (N38b-05) issues the control record from N31-CX's entry, and the gate's run (N31b-37, which draws its 100
placements with the same plant form) must show it caught; this bead writes the fixture and no control record.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every envelope case returned with `--for packet` has a re-extraction record with both tier values, the tier pair, a label and a replay address; the comparison calls N16bx-04's exported disagreement function and N31a-10's projection, and a source check finds no second implementation of either in `frob-foundry`.
- [ ] A case whose referee projection is under the floor 0.05 is labelled `provisional: below-envelope-floor` and carries the referee's value.
- [ ] On a fixture where the tiers agree, cases are `established` and carry the referee's value; on the planted fast-tier artifact (a stiffness spike that doubles one peak on the fast tier only) the case is `tier-sensitive`, the referee's value replaces the fast one, and the neighbour search is recorded.
- [ ] The fixture `fast-tier-stiffness-spike.toml` exists with the `synthetic` header, its placement, its plant file and its `removed_check`; run through `--conformance-plant` on the conformance build it doubles the governing peak on the fast tier only (ratio between 1.9 and 2.1), and a release binary has no such input.
- [ ] With the referee made unavailable for a scenario, the strict CPU profile adjudicates and the label names it; with both unavailable the label is `unadjudicated`.
- [ ] The adjudicated values of a case come from a single adjudicator instant (checked by replay).
- [ ] An unfrozen envelope refuses with FROB-REF-821; after the freeze every record carries the envelope's `EnvelopeId` and the ledger hash (the ordering N16bx-04 guards).
- [ ] The cost report equals the number of windows times 1 s: at most 36 referee-seconds per two-port link class before re-searches.
- [ ] Re-running the re-extraction gives identical records (the replay is deterministic by address).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry reextract`: `window_is_one_second_centred`; `referee_maximum_inside_window_is_used`
(a synthetic referee trace peaking 12 ticks after t* yields t_ref = t* + 12); `label_established_inside_envelope`
(ε = 0.10: p_fast = 105, p_ref = 100 → established); `label_tier_sensitive_outside_envelope` (p_fast = 200,
p_ref = 100 → tier-sensitive, ratio 0.5); `neighbour_search_takes_largest_referee_projection` (candidates
with referee projections 100, 118, 97 → the 118 candidate becomes the case); `referee_unavailable_uses_strict`;
`both_unavailable_is_unadjudicated`; `below_floor_is_provisional` (p_ref = 0.04 → `provisional:
below-envelope-floor`, referee value reported); `calls_ledger_delta_and_projection` (the comparison goes
through N16bx-04's function and N31a-10's projection: p_fast = 0.80, p_ref = 1.00 gives δ = 0.20);
`unfrozen_envelope_refused_821`; `settling_stretch_excluded` (a
synthetic referee trace whose largest value lies in the first 0.05 s of the window, from the restore
transient, is not taken; the next largest after 0.05 s is); `window_count_arithmetic` (18
coordinates → 36 directions → 36 s; 3 to 5 classes → 108 to 180 s); `damage_sensitivity_arithmetic` (1.1⁵ =
1.61051; 1.1¹⁰ = 2.59374).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_loads_reextract.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; `foundry.loads` and `sim.replay` are
`run` verbs, so each call carries its own idempotency key, here `--key "$(foundry_key <label>)"` from the
cluster preamble of N31a-51, which is the harness's `e2e_key` under the cluster's name: one label per call and
no digest of the call's inputs; written `<k>` below).
(1) *Before the freeze*, in a scratch store whose ledger has no frozen `port-wrench-projection` envelope for
the campaign's stratum: `frob fidelity ledger --class <c> --tier-pair <fast>,referee --quantity
port-wrench-projection --json` prints `envelope: null`; `frob foundry loads quad-ref --campaign gate-foundry-14
--for packet --key <k> --json` exits 3 with FROB-REF-821 and the repair "freeze the envelope first", and writes
no re-extraction record. (2) *The freeze*, in the same scratch store, through N16bx-04's example target on its
measurement record: `cargo run -p frob-foundry --example port_wrench_ledger -- --record <measurement record>
--freeze`, which writes the pair rows and calls the freeze routine of N16bp1-01
(`frob_chronicle::fidelity::freeze`) for each complete stratum. The surface is not `eval.run
--freeze-envelopes`: that option freezes the strata of a whole battery run and cannot select this committed
slice (N16bx-04 item 5). The ledger then prints the envelope with its `EnvelopeId`. (3) *After the freeze*: the
same `foundry loads … --for packet` request under its own label (a second call, so a second key) completes; assert a record per case, each carrying the
`EnvelopeId` and the ledger hash, and that the freeze's sequence number is lower than every record's.
(4) Replays three cases with `frob sim replay --run <r> --world <w> --episode <e> --from-snapshot <hash> --to
<tick> --tier referee --comparison --capture t2 --key <k> --json` (the replay address step 6 returns, world
address, episode, snapshot identity and tick, maps onto these fields of the `sim.replay` row of VERBS-sim; the
row's window start is either `--from <tick>` or `--from-snapshot <hash>`, so the replay starts at the
snapshot and runs to t* + 0.5 s; `--comparison` because a fast-tier snapshot is restored under the referee's
profile, which the row refuses as a continuation with FROB-REF-894), and compares the adjudicated wrenches
with the records. (5) Runs the fixture with the referee disabled for one scenario class (its own key). (6) *The
planted artifact*, on the conformance build only (the step is a logged skip, `e2e_skip`, on a release binary,
which has no such input): the fixture campaign's fast-tier run with `--conformance-plant <the plant file of
item 9>` on `sim.run`, then `foundry loads … --for packet` (its own label): the spiked case is
`tier-sensitive`, its adjudicated value is the referee's, `neighbours_examined` is not empty, and the ratio
fast to referee of the governing peak lies between 1.9 and 2.1. Logs to
`target/e2e-logs/foundry_loads_reextract/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`N31b-35`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; per case its
direction, `p_fast`, `p_adjudicated`, `ratio`, the envelope and its hash, the label, the tier pair,
`referee_seconds`; totals `cases`, `established`, `tier_sensitive`, `referee_unavailable`,
`referee_seconds_total`; the evidence block; on a replay that disagrees with its record the case, the first
differing wrench component with both values and the replay address. The summary names GATE-FOUNDRY-14.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: the cost arithmetic (36 windows; 108 to 180 referee-seconds); the damage factors 1.61 and
2.59; on a rigid, contact-free fixture (a link swung by a prescribed joint motion) fast and referee wrenches
agree to the fast tier's rounding and every case is `established`. Negative controls: a build that labels by
comparing with the fast value re-read from telemetry instead of a referee replay (the planted artifact must
expose it); a build that reads the referee at t* instead of at its own maximum (a fixture with a 12-tick
shift must expose it); a build that assembles the adjudicated case from two referee instants (refused by the
time-coherence check). Determinism: records identical twice on one machine; the referee's class governs
across machines.

## Considerations

- A window of ± 0.5 s assumes the referee and the fast tier have not diverged in the half second before the peak; where the trajectories part earlier (contact chaos), the referee's maximum may be a different event. The record keeps both ticks and the ratio so a reader can see that.
- Restoring a fast-tier snapshot on the referee starts the referee's contact solve without matching contact memory; the first ticks of the window are a settling stretch and are excluded from the maximum search (DESIGN: the first 0.05 s).
- DESIGN reading of the third label: a case adjudicated by the strict profile keeps the word `provisional` in its label and is admissible in a packet with the label printed; an unadjudicated case is not. The packet-side rule is in the companion bead.
- The fidelity envelope for wrenches must exist as a ledger stratum: the quantity `port-wrench projection`, its battery measurement and its frozen envelope are N16bx-04 (a dependency); until that envelope is frozen, re-extraction refuses with FROB-REF-821.
