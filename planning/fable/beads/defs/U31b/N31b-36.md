## Background

Foundry treats load cases as fast-tier results under the trust ladder: an envelope case is `provisional`
until it has been re-extracted on the referee (the companion bead does that for envelope cases). Two things
remain, and they are this bead.

First, the consequence. A label nobody enforces is decoration. The rule is: a build-packet request that
contains a `provisional` envelope case is refused; an exploratory structure run may use provisional cases and
is labelled, as the chapter's first decision already requires (an exploratory run is allowed, is labelled,
and cannot enter a build packet). The fallback when neither the referee nor the strict CPU profile can
adjudicate a case is the existing one: no packet.

Second, fatigue. Envelope cases size a part; spectra decide its fatigue utilization, and damage scales as
stress range to the power m of the stress-life law, so a load 10% high gives 1.61 times the damage at m = 5
and 2.59 times at m = 10. Spectra are long histories and cannot be re-run on the referee in full. So they are
checked the same way on a keyed sample of windows: the ratio of referee to fast-tier stress range at each
gauge is recorded, and a spectrum whose ratio leaves the band declared in the packet is rescaled to the
referee's range before damage is summed. Tier: Solid [S].

## Scope

In: the keyed sample of windows; the per-gauge range ratio; the declared band; rescaling and its effect on
damage; the audit record; the packet-side admission rule and its refusal; the exploratory label.
Out: re-extraction of envelope cases (N31b-35); cycle counting (BD-402); the gate's counts (N31b-37).

## Technical approach

**1. Sample of windows.** The campaign is cut into windows of 1 s (DESIGN, matching the envelope replay
window). A window is identified by (world address, episode, window index). The sample is the k windows with
the smallest value of a counter-based hash keyed by the campaign seed, with counter (world, episode, window)
and its own stream identifier `foundry-spectrum-audit` (DESIGN: k = 32 per link class). Because the sample is
a pure function of addresses, it needs no record to reproduce and is the same across loop iterations and
across designs compared on the same addresses. In addition (DESIGN), for each gauge the window containing the
largest counted range is always included, because rare large cycles dominate damage and a random sample may
miss them.

**2. Per-window measurement.** Each sampled window is replayed on the referee from the nearest snapshot (a
comparison replay, as for envelope cases, with the same settling exclusion). Referee load coordinates are
turned into gauge stresses by the same influence matrix. For each gauge g: the stress range in the window on
the fast tier, R_fast, and on the referee, R_ref.

**3. Ratio and band.** Per gauge, the pooled ratio r_g = Σ_windows R_ref / Σ_windows R_fast, with its
smallest and largest per-window values. The packet declares a band (DESIGN default [0.95, 1.05]; a packet may
declare a narrower one). If r_g lies inside the band the spectrum is used as counted. If r_g leaves the band,
the gauge's spectrum is rescaled to the referee's range before damage is summed: every counted range (and
amplitude) is multiplied by r_g. For a stress-life law N(S) = (S/S₀)⁻ᵐ this multiplies the gauge's damage by
r_gᵐ. A ratio above 1 raises damage; a ratio below 1 lowers it; both are applied, and both are printed.

**4. Audit record** (stored with the structural evidence and shipped under `structure/` in the packet):
`SpectrumAudit { gauge, windows: [{ addr, episode, index, R_fast, R_ref }], ratio, ratio_min, ratio_max,
band, action: "none" | "rescaled", factor, damage_before, damage_after, law_exponent, referee_seconds }`. If
the referee cannot complete a sampled window, the strict CPU profile adjudicates it and the record names the
adjudicator; if no adjudicator completes at least half the sampled windows of a gauge, the gauge's fatigue
result is `fatigue: no-claim (spectrum unaudited)` for packet purposes.

**5. Packet-side admission.** One function, `admit_for_packet(load_set)`, exposed as `frob foundry loads …
--admit packet` (a read that reports admissibility) and called by `foundry.export` before it writes anything.
It inspects the load set that the packet's structural evidence cites:
- every envelope case must carry a re-extraction record with label `established`, `tier-sensitive`,
  `provisional: referee-unavailable (adjudicated: batch-cpu:strict)`, or `provisional: below-envelope-floor`
  (N31b-35's label for a referee projection under the envelope's floor of 0.05, N16bx-04; DESIGN: admitted
  with its label printed, because its value is the referee's and the envelope makes no statement there);
- a case with label `provisional` (never re-extracted) or `provisional: referee-unavailable (unadjudicated)`
  refuses the request (exit 3). Refusal `provisional-load-case-in-packet` (the chapter gives no code): row 3
  of the decision bead N31b-45, an evidence-and-protocol (`8xx`) request `held` under the owner decision on
  that full family (N35x-01); until the ledger assigns its number it is raised under the interim code, the
  trust ladder's label-misuse code FROB-REF-820, with `detail: "provisional-load-case-in-packet"`, behind one
  named constant, with the pointer at the case, `observed` its label, `required` an adjudicated label; repairs,
  ranked: (1) `frob foundry loads … --for packet` to re-extract; (2) re-run the structure step as
  exploratory, which is labelled and cannot enter a packet;
- every gauge that contributes a fatigue utilization must have a spectrum audit record, or read
  `fatigue: no-claim`;
- the load-case labels and the audit records are copied into the packet and printed beside the margins they
  govern.

**6. Exploratory runs.** `foundry.optimize --exploratory` and `foundry.check --exploratory` accept
provisional cases; every result carries `exploratory: true`, the count of provisional cases used, and is
rejected as an input by the packet writer.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The sample of windows is reproducible from addresses alone: two runs, and a run on another machine, select the same windows.
- [ ] For a gauge whose pooled ratio is inside the band the damage is unchanged; for a planted ratio of 1.10 with exponent 5 the damage after rescaling is 1.61051 times the damage before, and the record says `rescaled` with factor 1.10.
- [ ] A build-packet request holding a provisional envelope case is refused in every trial (the gate requires 100 of 100), with the pointer at the case and the two repairs in order.
- [ ] A request whose cases are all adjudicated proceeds, and the packet contains a re-extraction record per case and an audit record per fatigue gauge.
- [ ] An exploratory run with provisional cases succeeds, is labelled, and its output is rejected by the packet writer.
- [ ] With both adjudicators unavailable for a case, no packet is written.
- [ ] The referee cost of the audit is reported per link class.

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry -- spectrum_audit packet_admission` (cargo takes one filter before `--`; two go
after it): `sample_is_pure_function_of_addresses`;
`largest_range_window_always_included`; `pooled_ratio` (windows with (R_fast, R_ref) = (100, 104), (50, 53),
(200, 206) → 363/350 = 1.0371: inside [0.95, 1.05]); `rescale_multiplies_damage_by_r_to_m` (r = 1.10, m = 5
→ 1.61051; m = 10 → 2.59374; r = 0.90, m = 5 → 0.59049); `band_edge_is_inside` (r = 1.05 exactly: no
rescale); `unaudited_gauge_is_no_claim`; `provisional_case_refuses_packet`; `adjudicated_by_strict_is_admitted`;
`unadjudicated_refuses_packet`; `below_floor_case_is_admitted_with_label`; `exploratory_accepts_provisional_and_is_labelled`;
`packet_writer_rejects_exploratory_input`.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: 1.1⁵ = 1.61 and 1.1¹⁰ = 2.59; the pooled-ratio example. Negative controls: a build that
rescales stress but sums damage from the unscaled spectrum (the damage factor test fails); a build that
samples windows by wall-clock order instead of by address (the reproducibility test fails); a packet writer
that warns about a provisional case and proceeds (the refusal test fails). Metamorphic: scaling the
fast-tier forces of the whole campaign by c = 1.2, which takes every gauge out of the band, leaves the sample
of windows unchanged and makes each gauge's audited damage equal r_gᵐ times the baseline's counted damage,
r_g being the baseline's pooled ratio (the referee's ranges are what is summed); for a gauge the baseline
rescaled, that is the baseline's audited damage unchanged. Determinism: exact selection; records identical
twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- A sample of 32 windows estimates a ratio; it does not bound it. The record prints the smallest and largest per-window ratios, and a wide spread is a finding in itself.
- Rescaling by one factor per gauge assumes the tier bias is roughly proportional across the gauge's cycles. Where the per-window ratios differ strongly between small and large cycles the gauge is flagged `ratio not uniform` and its fatigue result reads `no-claim` for packet purposes.
- The interim refusal code is the trust ladder's; the code of Foundry's own is the ledger request filed for row 3 of N31b-45 (by N35x-02), held with the rest of the `8xx` family under N35x-01, and the mechanism changes only the constant's number when it arrives.
- The flags this bead and N31b-35 use (`--for packet` and `--admit packet` on `foundry.loads`, `--for packet` and `--exploratory` on `foundry.check`) and the codes 821 and 820 on `foundry.loads` are in VERBS-foundry's rows, which name these two beads as the extensions; the refusal table N31-REF lists the codes.
- The band is declared in the packet so that a reader knows what "agreement" meant for this packet.
