## Background

Foundry's load cases come from campaigns run on a fast simulation tier, and a fast tier's compliant contact
can produce force peaks that the reference tier (the referee) does not reproduce. The rule that guards a
build packet against such peaks: envelope cases are re-extracted on the referee before they enter a packet;
each case carries both tier values and a label (`established`, `tier-sensitive`, or `provisional:
referee-unavailable`); a packet request that holds a provisional case is refused; fatigue spectra are checked
on a keyed sample of windows and rescaled to the referee's range when their ratio leaves the declared band.

GATE-FOUNDRY-14 (Known answer, TARGET, M5) measures that the rule works on a running system: every envelope
case in a packet has a re-extraction record; a planted fast-tier artifact (a stiffness spike that doubles one
peak on the fast tier only) is relabelled `tier-sensitive` and replaced in 100 of 100 runs; a packet request
holding a provisional case is refused in 100 of 100; the referee cost per packet is reported. There is no
kill gate for this rule: it is a use of promotion, and the fallback when the referee and the strict CPU
profile are both unavailable is the existing one, no packet.

The planted artifact is the gate's negative control and its reason for existing: without it, a
re-extraction step that merely copied the fast-tier value into both columns would pass every other check.

## Scope

In: the fixtures; the planted artifact and its 100 randomized placements; the 100 refusal trials; the packet
inspection; the cost report; the gate row.
Out: the mechanisms (N31b-35, N31b-36, BD-408).

## Technical approach

**1. Fixture.** The reference quadruped's qualified finalist with a registered load campaign
(`gate-foundry-14`: the qualified task set plus push-recovery and drop tasks at fixed world addresses on a
fast tier, control-rate traces on every world, snapshots at the registered interval).

**2. Clause A: every envelope case in a packet has a re-extraction record.** Run the chain `foundry.loads
--for packet` → `foundry.size`/`optimize` → `foundry.check` → `foundry.export`; open the packet; for every
envelope case under `structure/` assert a record with both tier values, the tier pair, a label from the
admitted set, the envelope's ledger hash and a replay address; assert the count of records equals the count
of cases; replay three cases chosen by a registered seed and compare the adjudicated wrenches.

**3. Clause B: the planted artifact, 100 of 100.** One run = one placement, drawn from a registered seed: a
foot contact, an episode, a tick interval of 20 ms inside a stance phase. In the fast-tier campaign only, the
contact stiffness of that foot is multiplied during the interval by a factor tuned once (before the 100
runs, on a separate calibration placement) so that the affected peak of the governing direction is doubled
(ratio fast/referee between 1.9 and 2.1 on the calibration placement). The referee replay uses the unmodified
contact. For each run assert: the affected envelope case is labelled `tier-sensitive`; its value in the load
set is the referee's (within the envelope of the unspiked fast value, not the doubled one); the neighbour
search was performed and recorded; no `established` label rests on the spiked sample. A run in which the
spiked sample is not selected as an envelope case at all (the spike did not make it extreme) is redrawn and
counted separately; the 100 are runs in which it was selected.

**4. Clause C: provisional cases refused, 100 of 100.** One trial = a packet request whose load set has one
envelope case, chosen by seed, left `provisional` (re-extraction skipped for that case by a test-only
selector), or left `unadjudicated` (both adjudicators disabled for that case's scenario). Assert exit 3, the
refusal with the pointer at that case and the repairs in order, and that no packet directory and no temporary
directory remains. Half the trials use each kind.

**5. Clause D: the referee cost per packet is reported.** The packet's structural evidence and the envelope
of `foundry.loads` state referee-seconds of simulated time per link class and in total, for envelope cases
and for the spectrum audit. Assert presence, and assert consistency with the count of windows (each envelope
window 1 s; at most 36 per two-port link class before re-searches; three to five classes on the quadruped
give at most 108 to 180 referee-seconds per loop iteration for envelope cases).

**6. Record.** The gate row for GATE-FOUNDRY-14: cases and records counted; the 100 placements' seed and the
count relabelled and replaced; redraws; the 100 refusal trials' count; referee-seconds; commit, backends,
hardware fingerprint and seeds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Clause A: records equal cases in the packet, each complete; the three replays match their records.
- [ ] Clause B: 100 of 100 selected placements are relabelled `tier-sensitive` and replaced by the referee's value; the calibration placement's ratio is between 1.9 and 2.1 and is recorded; redraws are reported.
- [ ] Clause C: 100 of 100 requests are refused with the case named, and no packet or partial directory exists after any of them.
- [ ] Clause D: the referee cost is present in the packet and the envelope, and is consistent with the window counts.
- [ ] The control "copy the fast value into both columns" fails clause B at 0 of 100; the control "warn and proceed" fails clause C.
- [ ] The gate row holds measured values and is recorded by a session that did not implement the mechanisms; the script exits zero when the measurement is complete, whatever the result.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_foundry_14.rs`: `placements_reproducible_from_seed`;
`calibration_ratio_window` (1.89 rejected; 2.00 accepted; 2.11 rejected); `unselected_spike_is_redrawn_not_counted`;
`clause_table` (pass only if A, B, C and D pass); `cost_consistency` (36 windows → 36 s; a report of 30 s for
36 windows fails); `no_directory_left_after_refusal`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gate_foundry_14.sh`: runs the clean chain and inspects the packet (clause A); calibrates the
spike, then loops 100 placements: `frob sim run` of the affected worlds with the test-only stiffness
override, `frob foundry loads --for packet --json`, assertions (clause B); loops 100 refusal trials with
`frob foundry export … --json` (clause C); reads the cost fields (clause D). Logs per STD-E2E to
`target/e2e-logs/gate_foundry_14/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`: `cases`, `records`; per placement the foot,
episode, interval, `p_fast`, `p_referee`, `ratio`, the label, `replaced`; `relabelled` (100 of 100),
`redraws`; per refusal trial the kind, exit code, refusal code and pointer; `refused` (100 of 100);
`referee_seconds_envelope`, `referee_seconds_spectra`; the evidence blocks; a `summary` naming
GATE-FOUNDRY-14 with the four clause results.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answers: the doubling (ratio 2.0 ± 0.1 by calibration); the cost arithmetic (2 × 18 directions → 36
windows of 1 s per two-port link class; 108 to 180 referee-seconds for three to five classes); the damage
consequence the rule exists for (a load 10% high is 1.61 times the damage at exponent 5 and 2.59 times at
exponent 10). Negative controls: the two named in the acceptance criteria; a re-extraction that reads the
referee at the fast tier's tick when the referee's peak is shifted (must lower the replaced value and be
caught by the replay comparison of clause A). Determinism lanes: the clean chain twice on one machine gives
the same packet address; placements and trials are functions of the registered seeds.

## Considerations

- The spike is planted through a test-only override that exists in no release build; a build-graph test asserts that.
- "Doubles one peak" is achieved by calibration, not by formula: contact force is not linear in stiffness. The calibration happens once, before the counted runs, and its placement is excluded from them.
- If the load-extraction kill gate fired (hand-declared cases), there is nothing to re-extract: the gate is recorded `not applicable: loads declared`, and packets state `loads: declared`.
- The gate has no kill gate. A failure blocks packets (the fallback is "no packet") until repaired and re-measured.
