## Background

The preflight check of member sizing uses a bounded load set from statics: the robot's weight times a class
load factor at each foot, wheel or end-effector port. It runs before any rollout and catches links that
could not even stand. It cannot know what a gait, a push or a step-down does to a link. Once rollouts exist,
the simulator has that information, and the same closed-form sizing can be run against it.

The plan's rule, *after the first rollouts*: the same check under the peak port wrenches of event-level (T1)
telemetry, taken time-coherently as in load extraction. A design that is `under-built` under measured loads
keeps its scores with the label `structure-unchecked`. It cannot be promoted until it is re-run with the
sized section, or until a typed edit changes the declared one. Promotion of a design with a link that is
`under-built` under measured loads is refused, with the link, the ratio and the sized section as a typed
repair. `frob-arena` applies the label.

Why keep the scores and only label them: discarding the results would hide what the screening campaign
found and waste its compute; using them unlabelled would let an unbuildable design win. A label that blocks
promotion does neither. Promotion is the program's step from `provisional` to `established`, so blocking it
is exactly the right strength: the design can be discussed and compared, and cannot be relied on. Tier:
Solid [S].

## Scope

In: the measured load cases from event-level telemetry; the check; the label and how it travels; the
promotion refusal and its typed repair; the two ways the label is cleared; the fail-closed rule for designs
never checked under measured loads.
Out: the sizing kernel (N31b-38); the static check (N31b-39); the sized fill (N31b-41); full load campaigns
with control-rate traces, envelope cases and spectra, which belong to M5.

## Technical approach

**1. Measured cases, time-coherent.** For each checked link and each episode the telemetry holds one sample:
all of the link's port wrenches in the link frame and its body twist at the tick where the bending moment at
the link's proximal joint was largest in that episode. A case is that one sample; port values from different
ticks are never combined (the assembled case can be unsafe: a span carrying P at one third and later at two
thirds has mid-span moment P·L/6 each time, and assembling both gives P·L/3 or, with opposite signs, zero).
Over a batch of rollouts the load set of a link is the set of its per-episode samples, reduced to the samples
that are extreme in bending moment, axial force and torque (DESIGN: the top three by each, by exact
comparison, ties to the smallest world address).

**2. The check.** The sizing kernel is run on those cases through the same trait the preflight uses.
`under-built (measured)` when the declared mass is below the floor under measured loads; the finding carries
the link, the ratio m_floor/m_decl, the governing sample's world address, episode and tick, and the sized
section. The check runs automatically when a batch of rollouts of a design with shell or lattice links
completes, and costs microseconds per link.

**3. The label.** A design with at least one link `under-built (measured)` keeps every score of those
rollouts, and each scorecard and each result derived from them carries the label `structure-unchecked` with
the list of links. `frob-arena` applies it when it writes scorecards; in the claim product it is a condition
flag joined by union, so a comparison, a ranking or a report that consumes a labelled result is labelled.
Reports print: "structure-unchecked: link ⟨name⟩ is under-built by ⟨ratio⟩ under measured loads".

**4. Promotion.** `eval.promote` refuses a design that carries the label: typed refusal
`under-built-promotion` (no code in the plan; row 2 of the decision bead N31b-45, a design-and-model (`1xx`)
request in the allocation ledger, which assigns it the number it proposed, FROB-REF-171, and whose row N31b-45
loads), exit 3, `pointer` the link, `observed` the declared mass, `required` the floor, and one ranked repair
as a typed edit: `set-fill` to the sized stock section, with the predicted mass and inertia. A promotion of a
comparison in which either arm is labelled is refused the same way, naming the arm.

**5. Clearing the label.** Exactly two ways. (a) The design is re-run with the sized section: the typed edit
creates a new design version; its rollouts are new; its check passes or produces its own finding. The old
version keeps its label for ever. (b) A typed edit changes the declared section (a thicker wall, a different
radius, another material) so that the floor is met; again a new version with new rollouts. No flag,
override or configuration clears the label on an existing version.

**6. Fail-closed for unchecked designs** (DESIGN). A design that has shell or lattice links and whose
rollouts carry no measured sample for them (telemetry capture off, or a tier that does not record it) is
labelled `structure-unchecked (no measured loads)` and is refused promotion with the repair "re-run with
event-level capture" (the same FROB-REF-171, whose situation is a design labelled `structure-unchecked`
offered to promotion, with `detail: "no-measured-loads"`); a design with an outstanding static `under-built` finding is treated the same way
until a measured check has passed or its fill has changed.

**7. Evidence.** The measured check inherits the tier and determinism class of the rollouts; the label is
independent of tier (a link under-built on a fast tier is labelled; the sized re-run may later be promoted
like any result).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After the first rollouts of the planted under-built design, its scorecards carry `structure-unchecked` with the link and ratio, and its scores are unchanged from an unlabelled run.
- [ ] `frob eval promote` for that design is refused with the typed refusal, the link, the ratio and the sized section as a typed repair; a comparison with that design as one arm is refused naming the arm.
- [ ] Applying the repair creates a new design version; after its rollouts the check passes and promotion proceeds by the ordinary rule; the old version still carries its label.
- [ ] Every measured case is one sample: its port wrenches share a world address, an episode and a tick (checked against the telemetry).
- [ ] A design without measured samples for its shell links is refused promotion under the fail-closed rule.
- [ ] The label survives every consumer: a ranking, a report and a comparison built on labelled scores are labelled (property test over the claim product).
- [ ] No switch clears the label on an existing design version.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena structure_label` and `cargo test -p frob-analyze measured_member_sizing`:
- `case_is_one_sample`; `assembled_case_rejected` (the span fixture: P·L/6 per instant; the assembled P·L/3 case cannot be constructed through the API).
- `extreme_sample_selection_exact` (top three by bending moment; ties to the smallest address).
- `under_built_measured`: a measured tip force of 600 N on the 0.3 m tube against a declared 0.05 kg: floor 0.141 kg, ratio 2.8.
- `static_pass_but_measured_fail`: a link sized for 588.6 N that sees 900 N in a push: t_req rises from 1.81 mm to 2.77 mm; a declared 2.0 mm wall (0.153 kg) is under-built by 1.38.
- `label_applied_and_scores_unchanged`; `label_union_through_comparison`; `label_cannot_be_dropped`.
- `promotion_refused_with_typed_repair`; `comparison_arm_labelled_refused`.
- `repair_creates_new_version_old_keeps_label`.
- `no_measured_samples_fail_closed`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `peak-per-channel` (assembles peaks of different ticks), `label-on-report-only` (scorecard unlabelled), `promote-with-warning` (refusal downgraded).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/structure_unchecked_promotion.sh` (sources the STD-E2E helper; `sim.run`, `eval.promote` and
`design.edit` carry their own idempotency keys, `eval.scorecard` is a `read` verb): (1) `frob sim run
quad-thin-shank --scene <short gait scene> --worlds 64 --seconds 10 --tier <fast tier> --peak-capture
link-bending-proximal --key suc-1 --json`, a short batch of the planted design with the time-coherent peak
capture of N29ax-02 (its default entity set, the links with shell or lattice fill); (2) `frob eval scorecard
quad-thin-shank --json`, asserting the label and unchanged scores against a stored unlabelled reference; (3)
`frob eval promote <run> --tier referee --key suc-3 --json`, asserting exit 3, `FROB-REF-171` (row 2 of
N31b-45, a dependency, which has loaded it), pointer, observed, required and the typed repair; (4) the
repair as `next_actions` gives it (`frob design edit quad-thin-shank set-fill <link> <stock wall>
--expected-parent <DesignId> --key suc-4 --json`), rollouts of the new version (`--key suc-4-run`), `eval
promote` (`--key suc-4-pro`), asserting the ordinary promotion path; (5) a batch without `--peak-capture`
(`--key suc-5`) and its promotion (`--key suc-5-pro`), asserting the fail-closed refusal with the repair
"re-run with event-level capture"; (6) the three planted defects through the negative-control
runner. Logs to `target/e2e-logs/structure_unchecked_promotion/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript:
every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N31b-40`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall
time; per link `m_declared_kg`, `m_floor_measured_kg`, `ratio`, the governing sample's address, episode and
tick; `event: refusal` lines with code, `detail` and pointer; both design identities; the evidence blocks with
the label in their conditions; per control its id and outcome; on a failed assertion the first differing
score or field with expected and observed values and the run address. The summary names GATE-FOUNDRY-15 (its
promotion clause).

### Known answers and negative controls
The tube case (0.141 kg floor; 2.8). The assembled-peaks case. The 900 N case above (derived here: t_req =
270/(138 × 10⁶ × π × 0.015²) = 2.768 mm; floor 0.2113 kg; ratio 0.2113/0.1527 = 1.38). Negative controls:
the three planted defects; a design whose label is cleared by re-running the same version (must not happen).
Metamorphic: adding episodes can only raise a link's measured floor; permuting episodes changes nothing.
Determinism: selection and decisions are exact given the telemetry; the telemetry inherits the rollouts'
class.

## Considerations

- The plan says "peak port wrenches of T1 telemetry". Event-level telemetry holds events, not traces, so a coherent peak sample must be captured as an event on the device; that capture is N29ax-02's `--peak-capture link-bending-proximal` (a dependency: one sample per link per episode at the running maximum of the proximal bending moment, with the port wrenches and body twist as payload), which this bead reads and does not re-implement.
- One sample per link per episode at the peak of one scalar can miss a case governed by another criterion (a torsion peak at a different tick). The M5 load campaign with control-rate traces is the complete answer; this check is a guard, not a structural analysis.
- A design labelled on a fast tier may be under-built only because of a fast-tier force artifact. The label still blocks promotion; the remedy is the sized re-run, which is cheap, not an argument about the tier.
- The refusal has no code in the plan. Its number, FROB-REF-171, comes from the allocation ledger through N31b-45 (a dependency, which loads the row before this bead starts), behind one named constant in `frob-arena`. `eval.promote`'s row in VERBS-eval lists 171 (`under-built-promotion`, this bead's) among its chapter refusals.
