## Background

Member sizing as a validity check in screening is the one piece of Foundry that lands at M3, with the
screening machinery it protects. It has four parts: a closed-form sizing kernel; a preflight check under a
bounded statics load set that flags `under-built` links before any rollout; a check under measured peak
loads after the first rollouts that labels scores `structure-unchecked` and blocks promotion; and the fill
`{shell: sized}`, which lets a link be sized instead of declaring a wall. An experiment on the design corpus
measures how often enforcing sized members changes a campaign's winner or its actuator selection.

GATE-FOUNDRY-15 (TARGET, M3) has four clauses: the tube case returns t = 1.85 mm and 0.141 kg within 1%; a
planted under-built link is flagged before the first rollout and blocks promotion; the check adds at most
50 ms to `design.check` on the reference robots; the corpus comparison is run and its share recorded, and if
the winner or its actuator selection changes in fewer than 1 campaign in 15 the check stays as a promotion
guard and `{shell: sized}` is not made the template default. The gate was printed with two claims; its
governing part is the known-answer part (the first three clauses), and the fourth is a recorded figure that
decides a default.

This bead measures the first three clauses on the running system, reads the fourth from its experiment's
record, and writes the gate row.

## Scope

In: the script; the fixtures; the three measured clauses; reading the experiment's record; the gate row.
Out: the mechanisms and the experiment (N31b-38 to N31b-42).

## Technical approach

**1. Clause A: the tube case.** Through the released surface: `frob design check` on the fixture design
`tube-shank` (a single limb segment modelled as a cantilevered tube 0.3 m long, mean radius 15 mm, aluminium
alloy with S_y = 276 MPa and density 2,700 kg/m³, safety factor 2, loaded by 600 N across its tip through a
port whose registered load is exactly 600 N). Assert the reported floor: t = 1.85 mm and mass 0.141 kg, each
within 1% (accepted ranges 1.8315 to 1.8685 mm and 0.13959 to 0.14241 kg), and the inertia about the root
4.2 × 10⁻³ kg·m² within 1% of 4.226 × 10⁻³.

**2. Clause B: flagged before the first rollout, and blocks promotion.** On the planted design
`quad-thin-shank` (a reference quadruped whose lower-leg segments declare a shell giving 0.05 kg where the
floor is 0.141 kg):
1. From a clean store with no rollouts of this design, `frob design check` returns exit 2 with the finding
   `under-built` on each lower-leg link, ratio 2.8 within 2%, and the typed repairs. The script asserts that
   the ledger holds no episode of this design at that moment.
2. A short batch of rollouts is run; the scorecards carry `structure-unchecked` and the scores equal those
   of a stored reference run of the same design made with the label logic disabled.
3. `frob eval promote` is refused with the typed refusal naming the link, the ratio and the sized section.
4. The first repair is applied (`set-fill` to the sized section); the new version's check passes; its
   rollouts run; promotion proceeds by the ordinary rule (it may still fail promotion for ordinary reasons;
   the assertion is that the structural refusal is gone).
5. The sized twin `quad-sized-shank` shows no finding at any step (the control that the check can pass).

**3. Clause C: at most 50 ms.** For each reference robot (the archetype reference designs available at M3),
`frob design check --stages 1-6` is run 50 times with the member-sizing check enabled and 50 times with it
disabled by a test switch, interleaved, on the reference host with an empty memo graph each time. The added
time is the difference of medians and of 95th percentiles of stage 6; pass when both are at most 50 ms on
every reference robot. The absolute stage-6 and total times are reported against their own budgets (600 ms;
2 s for stages 1 to 6) but belong to the preflight gate.

**4. Clause D: the corpus comparison.** Read the experiment's record: registered six-part form present;
fifteen campaigns in two arms; controls valid; the share recorded with its interval; the decision applied by
the pre-declared rule. The clause passes when the comparison *was run and its share recorded* with valid
controls, whatever the share. The row's notes state the share and whether `{shell: sized}` became a template
default.

**5. Record.** The gate row for GATE-FOUNDRY-15: clause A values; clause B step results with the two design
identities; clause C added medians and 95th percentiles per robot; clause D share and decision; commit,
hardware fingerprint, seeds. Recorded by a session that did not implement the check.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Clause A: t and mass within 1% of 1.85 mm and 0.141 kg through `frob design check --json`.
- [ ] Clause B: the finding exists before any episode of the planted design exists; scores are labelled and numerically unchanged; promotion is refused with the typed repair; after the repair the structural refusal is gone; the sized twin shows no finding.
- [ ] Clause C: added median and added 95th percentile at most 50 ms on every reference robot, with the measured values in the row.
- [ ] Clause D: the experiment's record is complete and valid, and its share and decision are in the row's notes.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: with the check's load factor forced to zero the planted design is not flagged and clause B fails; with the promotion refusal downgraded to a warning clause B fails at step 3.
- [ ] The gate row is `pass` only if clauses A to D pass; the script exits zero when the measurement is complete, whatever the result.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_foundry_15.rs`: `one_percent_windows` (1.8315 to 1.8685 mm; 0.13959 to
0.14241 kg); `added_time_is_difference_of_interleaved_runs`; `clause_d_passes_on_any_share_with_valid_controls`;
`clause_d_fails_on_void_experiment`; `clause_table`; `no_episode_before_finding_check` (a fixture ledger
with one prior episode of the design makes step 1 fail).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gate_foundry_15.sh`: runs clauses A to C as above with `frob design check`, `frob sim run`,
`frob eval scorecard`, `frob eval promote` and `frob design edit`, then reads the experiment record. Logs per
STD-E2E to `target/e2e-logs/gate_foundry_15/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`: `t_required_mm` (1.85 ± 1%),
`m_floor_kg` (0.141 ± 1%), `inertia_root_kgm2`; for clause B each step's command, exit code and the
envelope's evidence block (tier `static` and determinism `exact` for the check; the rollouts' tier and class
for the scores), the finding, `ratio`, the label, the refusal with pointer, observed and required, both
design identities; for clause C per robot `added_ms_p50` and `added_ms_p95` (threshold 50) with the host
fingerprint; for clause D `changed_campaigns` of 15 and the decision; a `summary` naming GATE-FOUNDRY-15
with the four clause results.

### Known answers and negative controls
The tube case: root moment 600 N × 0.3 m = 180 N·m; allowable 276/2 = 138 MPa; t ≥ 180/(138 × 10⁶ × π ×
0.015²) = 1.85 mm; mass 2π·r·t·L·ρ = 0.141 kg; inertia about the root m·L²/3 = 4.2 × 10⁻³ kg·m²; a 0.05 kg
declaration is under-built by a factor of 2.8 and its swing inertia is understated by the same factor.
Negative controls: the two in the acceptance criteria; a sizing build with the solid-rod section modulus
(clause A must fail: it returns a different wall). Metamorphic: doubling the tip load doubles the required
wall of the yield-governed tube (3.69 mm) and the script checks it. Determinism: clauses A and B's check
results are exact and identical on every machine; clause C is a timing measurement and is reported with the
host fingerprint and repeated on the second reference machine for record.

## Considerations

- Clause B's first step is about order: the flag must exist before any rollout. The script proves it against the ledger instead of trusting the order of its own commands.
- 50 ms is a budget on added time, not on the check alone; interleaving the enabled and disabled runs removes drift of the host from the difference.
- If the corpus experiment is void (a control failed) and its one repetition is also void, clause D fails and the gate fails; there is no kill gate, and the remedy is to repair the experiment.
- The M5 beam rung repeats the tube case through `foundry.size`; the two must agree, and the M5 bead's tests assert it.
