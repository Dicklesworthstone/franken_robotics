## Background

Once many agents share the devices of FrankenRobotics through the daemon `frobd`, the order in which work runs
decides how long each agent's turn blocks. Agents mostly ask short questions and sometimes start long ones. The
plan's queueing arithmetic (an M/G/1 model with Poisson arrivals; a model, not a measurement) shows why the
obvious remedy fails. One device, 90% of jobs 10 s and 10% of jobs 30 min (E[S] = 189 s, E[S²] = 324,090 s²):

| Utilization ρ | First-come wait | Short-class wait, priority only | Short-class wait, preempt-resume | Slowdown of long jobs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 857 s | 439 s | 0.12 s + quantum | 2.4% |
| 0.7 | 2,001 s | 621 s | 0.17 s + quantum | 3.4% |
| 0.8 | 3,430 s | 713 s | 0.20 s + quantum | 4.0% |
| 0.9 | 7,716 s | 806 s | 0.22 s + quantum | 4.5% |

The wait under priority is the remainder of the long job already on the device; **only preemption removes it**
(counterexample CX-351: "priority without preemption protects interactive jobs"). The result flips if resume
costs several seconds or a device is reserved for interactive work: then run to completion.

This bead builds the scheduler that the arithmetic calls for, as the decision core DC-09 ("which job runs when on
a device"; tier Solid): size classes taken from the system's own estimate, fair ordering across agents, shortest
estimated work within an agent, ageing against starvation, preempt-resume at snapshot boundaries, and an
admission guard. Decision ADR-TETHER-08 bounds all of it: the scheduler decides when work runs and never what is
computed; no wall-clock quantity enters a result. Alternatives excluded by the plan: bandit allocation on
dispatch; mechanism design for the queue (agents would report their own job sizes; here estimates come from the
system); a device reserved for interactive work as the default (it idles the device and is kept only as the case
in which the conclusion flips).

## Scope

## Technical approach

**Size classes** come from the system's own estimate, never from the agent (TARGET boundaries):

| Class | Rule | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `interactive` | Point estimate at most 30 s of device time | May use the slot reserve; may trigger preemption of batch work |
| `batch` | Above 30 s and inside the machine envelope | Runs in non-reserve slots; may be parked |
| `campaign` | Above the machine envelope (default 16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour, one device, until the owner approves a compute class) | Refused with `FROB-REF-704` (exit 4) until an approved compute envelope is attached to the session |

A job whose actual work exceeds twice its estimate is reclassified at its next boundary and counted by the
estimate-reliability monitor (of the last 50 jobs, 20% over twice their estimate triggers the fallback).

**Order within a class.** Deficit round-robin across agents, weighted by remaining budget; within an agent,
shortest estimated remaining work first; a waiting job's effective work decays linearly to zero over 600 s
(TARGET), so no job waits behind an unbounded stream of shorter ones. Deficit round-robin (DESIGN detail): each
agent a with waiting work has a deficit counter D_a; each round D_a is increased by q₀·w_a, where w_a is the
agent's remaining budget divided by the mean remaining budget of agents with waiting work, clamped to
[0.25, 4]; the agent may start episodes while their estimated work is at most D_a, and D_a is reduced by the
work started; an agent with nothing waiting has D_a reset to zero.

**Preempt-resume.** A batch-class job can be parked at a snapshot boundary (quantum at most 5 s, TARGET) and
resumed later with identical digests. Steps: (1) the guard table emits `Preempt(job)` (the batch job with the
most remaining work, when the reserve is full and an interactive job waits); (2) at the job's next snapshot
boundary its worlds are snapshotted: state, solver warm starts, packet queues and stream counters; (3) the job's
state becomes `preempted`, with a checkpoint event carrying the per-world digests; its slots are freed at the
boundary; (4) on resume the snapshot is restored into any free slots, the digests are recomputed and compared
with the checkpoint's, and the state returns to `running`; (5) a difference fails the job as indeterminate,
raises the alarm and trips the fallback. Limits: at most one preemption per batch job per 30 s, and the batch
class keeps at least 30% of device time over a sliding window (TARGET; window 600 s, DESIGN); when the share
would fall below it, interactive work waits for the reserve.

**Admission guard.** Utilization is held under 0.8 per device (TARGET): busy slot-seconds over capacity
slot-seconds on a sliding window (60 s, DESIGN). Beyond it, or when the per-environment job cap is reached, new
work is refused with `FROB-REF-703` (exit 4) and `retry: backoff`, with `poll_after_ms` from the capacity table.

**Wiring the decision model.** Each quantum the dispatcher reads integer state from the ledger, calls the pure
guard-table function, executes the returned actions (co-batch at the next reset boundary; use the reserve;
preempt; open a second environment after the memory preflight; serve other agents first; fall back), and appends
the decision record. The table and its parameters are in the daemon's configuration digest.

**The capacity table the daemon publishes.** Predicted wait by class against utilization, from the closed forms
scaled by Kingman's correction (c_a² + c_s²) ÷ (1 + c_s²), with the squared coefficients of variation of
inter-arrival and service times measured from the daemon's own trace; labelled `model`. Refreshed on a schedule,
never on the dispatch path.

**Measurements for the gate.**
- *The replayed mix.* 90% interactive jobs by count, offered load ρ = 0.7 on one device. Lane A: the real
  dispatcher run inside the queue simulator on the full mix in virtual time, including the model's case (10 s
  and 30 min jobs). Lane B (the gate's device lane): the mix replayed against `frobd` on a device within the
  machine envelope of one hour, with real small jobs as the interactive class and real batch jobs of a few
  minutes (DESIGN; a longer replay needs an approved compute class).
- *Thresholds.* Interactive wait at the 95th percentile at most 6 s; batch slowdown at most 10% (time from first
  start to completion over the job's device time alone, minus one, averaged over batch jobs); no job beyond the
  ageing bound.
- *Evidence untouched.* The M3 comparison battery is run once undisturbed and once with random preemption and
  reordering injected by a test hook at every boundary; every per-world digest and every study verdict must be
  identical.

Determinism: scheduling uses the clock; results do not (INV-TETHER-09). Refusals: `FROB-REF-703`, `-704`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-TETHER-10 (TARGET): on the replayed mix (90% interactive) at utilization 0.7: interactive wait at the 95th percentile at most 6 s; batch slowdown at most 10%; no job beyond the ageing bound; random preemption and reordering leave every digest and verdict identical. Measured values are recorded per device family with the configuration digest.
- [ ] The measurements that decide KILL-PREEMPT (resumed digests; measured resume cost against the quantum) are recorded and handed to the decision bead, which closes the seed's condition "KILL-PREEMPT decided".
- [ ] Size classes are assigned from the system's estimate; a caller-supplied hint cannot change a class (there is no such parameter).
- [ ] A campaign-class request is refused with `FROB-REF-704`; a request beyond the guard with `FROB-REF-703` and `retry: backoff`.
- [ ] With two agents of equal budget submitting continuously, device time splits within 10% of evenly; with budgets 3:1 the split follows the weights within the clamp.
- [ ] A 30 min batch job that has waited 600 s starts before any interactive job admitted after that moment in its class ordering (the ageing bound holds on the device, not only in the model).
- [ ] At most one preemption per batch job per 30 s; the batch class receives at least 30% of device time in every window of the replay.
- [ ] Run inside the simulator, the real dispatcher reproduces the preempt-resume column of the model within 5% plus the boundary delay (the known answer of the queueing model, now for the real policy).
- [ ] The DC-09 tables (guard table, capacity table) regenerate bit-identically, and each fallback is demonstrated by forcing its trigger.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-verbs sched`, `cargo test -p frob-cli dispatcher_sched`)
- `size_class_boundaries`: estimates of 30 s → `interactive`; 30.001 s → `batch`; above the envelope → `campaign`, 704.
- `class_from_estimate_only`: the request schema has no size or priority field (schema test).
- `drr_equal_budgets_alternate`; `drr_weighted_3_to_1`; `drr_weight_clamped`: a 100:1 budget ratio is served at most 4:0.25.
- `srpt_within_agent`: remaining estimates 5 s, 60 s, 20 s → order 5, 20, 60.
- `ageing_reorders`: the 60 s job after 600 s waiting precedes a new 5 s job.
- `preempt_at_next_boundary_only`: no preemption between boundaries.
- `resume_digest_equal` on `batch-cpu`: park and resume 100 jobs at random boundaries; per-world digests equal an uninterrupted run.
- `resume_digest_mismatch_trips_fallback` (planted: a snapshot that omits the packet queue): the job fails, the alarm fires, the dispatcher enters run-to-completion and per-job dispatch.
- `preemption_rate_limit_30s`; `batch_share_at_least_30_percent`.
- `guard_refuses_703_above_0_8`: utilization 0.81 → 703 with `retry: backoff`; 0.79 → admitted.
- `reclassify_after_twice_estimate`.
- `capacity_table_kingman`: generated from a recorded trace summary; equals the generator's output for the same inputs.
- `dispatcher_in_simulator_matches_model`: ρ = 0.7 → short-class wait within 5% of 0.17 s plus the mean boundary delay.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/scheduling_mix.sh --lane sim|batch-cpu|cuda|metal --seed <s>`: generates the mix; lane `sim` runs it
in virtual time; device lanes replay it against `frobd` through real `frob` clients from several sessions for at
most one hour; then runs the comparison battery with and without the random-preemption hook. It sources the
shared helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/scheduling_mix/<utc>.ndjson` plus a transcript. Every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`BD-296`), `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence`
block; `metric` lines: interactive wait p50, p95 (threshold 6 s), p99; batch slowdown (≤ 10%); jobs beyond the
ageing bound (0); preemptions per job per 30 s (≤ 1); batch share (≥ 30%); utilization; resume cost (snapshot
plus restore, against the quantum); digest and verdict differences under injected preemption (0); refusals 703
and 704 seen; with units, the disclosure block and the configuration digest. On failure: the job, the boundary
and the first differing digest. The `summary` names GATE-TETHER-10 and KILL-PREEMPT.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-758 (5% at 10⁶ simulated jobs, TARGET), re-run for the real dispatcher in the simulator: the sixteen cells of the table above.
- CX-351 (negative control): priority without preemption. With preemption disabled by a test flag, the interactive p95 on the replayed mix must exceed 6 s and the gate must fail.
- Further negative controls: a snapshot without stream counters (resumed digests differ); a dispatcher that reads estimates supplied by the caller (a job labelled small by its submitter jumps the queue; the fairness test must fail); ageing disabled (a starved job appears).
- Metamorphic relations: injected preemption and reordering change no digest and no verdict; relabelling agents permutes outcomes only.
- Determinism lanes: the digest-equality campaign twice on one machine, across worker counts, debug and release on `batch-cpu`; on each device family against its class.

## Considerations

- KILL-PREEMPT. Trigger: a resumed job's digest differs, or measured resume cost exceeds the quantum. Fallback: run to completion with the slot reserve only. With the fallback, interactive latency depends on the reserve and on batch jobs' natural boundaries; the gate's 6 s target is then re-read and may not hold, which the record must say.
- OPEN-TETHER-4 (reserve, job cap, quantum, ageing period; defaults 5%, 64, 5 s, 600 s) supplies the parameters; they are configuration.
- If KILL-SLOT fired, preemption parks whole environments instead of freeing slots, and the reserve has no meaning; this bead's ordering and guard still apply.
- The device lane's mix differs from the model's 30 min case because of the one-hour envelope; the model case is covered in the simulator lane. A full-scale replay is a named gate measurement needing the owner's compute approval.
- The 30 s interactive boundary interacts with the bounded wait's 30,000 ms maximum: an interactive job normally completes inside one maximum wait.
