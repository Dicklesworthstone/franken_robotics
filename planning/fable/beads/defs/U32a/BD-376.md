## Background

Milestone M4 adds amortized control, co-design and mission queries at scale to FrankenRobotics: family policies
and inheritance, the evaluation ladder and the six-stage cascade, gait orbits with continuation, skill artifacts
and runtime shields, optimization with lineage, registered predictions, experiment suggestion, wheels, tracks
and soil. This bead registers their verbs: every verb whose first milestone is M4 becomes an entry of
`registries/verbs.toml` (compiled into the static table of the crate `frob-verbs`, from which the CLI, the MCP
tools, the schemas and the agent guide are generated) with an input schema, an output schema, at least one
runnable example and a refusal list, and the surface parity gate is recorded for the M4 set.

As before this is integration work: each verb's computation is built by its subsystem's bead (this bead's
dependencies), and gets here its thin binding, registry entry, schemas, golden envelope and honest status. Two
things are particular to M4. Many M4 verbs are tier Frontier, so their status depends on a pilot and a
kill-gate decision being on record (a Frontier capability is "implemented" only then, and one that fell back at
its kill gate is described by the name of its fallback). And one verb defined by the surface chapter has no
other home and is built here: `result.show`.

GATE-TETHER-02 (Known answer, TARGET): the CLI and MCP surfaces are generated from the registry and their parity
diff is empty; every verb has a schema, an example and a refusal list; the served `tools/list` equals the
registry under the name map (`.` and `-` replaced by `_`).

## Scope

In: registry entries, schemas, examples, refusal lists, task-cluster assignments, bindings, golden envelopes and
status for the M4 set; the handler of `result.show`; tier and fallback status for Frontier verbs; the M4
additions to the freeze; the M4 parity record. Out: each other verb's computation; `solve.run` itself (BD-372);
the surface A/B and the mission-session gate.

## Technical approach

**The checklist per verb** (unchanged): a complete entry; two schemas whose integers are only `int53`, `u64str`
or `i64str`, with units on quantities and the untrusted mark on fields that echo imported text; a runnable
example; a refusal list (the handler's codes plus the common codes of its kind); a golden envelope; a binding
with no computation; a truthful `capabilities` row.

**`result.show` (read; cluster `result`; tier Solid).** Purpose: a stored result with its claim product and
dependencies, so that an agent can see what an answer rests on before citing it.

| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Input | `result` (the identifier of a stored result: a run summary, a study answer, a mission answer, a promotion, a campaign result); optional `depth` (default 1) for the dependency listing |
| Output | The result as stored (the same bytes its producing verb returned); its claim product in full (kind, class, the weakest input that set the class, tier, determinism, reality scope, conditions); `inputs`: every consumed artifact with its own claim product, paged; `standing`: `current`, or `invalidated` with the change that invalidated it and the cost of the rerun, as the revalidation engine reports it |
| Refusals | 802; 720 (scope `<cluster>:read` of the result's producing cluster); the sealed-read refusal for a result of the blinded lane beyond its verdict and receipt; 806 and 807 for the paged inputs |
| Evidence | The stored result's own; `result.show` adds none |
| Property | `result.show` never recomputes: a test asserts no job is created and the returned result bytes equal the stored bytes |

**Frontier status.** For each Frontier verb of the set the `capabilities` row states one of: `implemented`
(pilot and kill decision on record, all eight conditions hold), `fallback: <name>` (its kill gate fired), or
`not implemented` with the missing conditions. The registry's `tier` field is checked against the mechanism's
tier in its bead.

**`solve.run` and the freeze.** The M4 entries are added to the freeze file at the end of this bead. If the
kill decision on `solve.run` has fired by then, its entry is absent and the `search` task cluster's seventh
tool is reassigned (DESIGN: `opt_cascade`); if the decision is still pending, the entry is frozen as Frontier
and its later removal is a recorded change.

**Task clusters.** `search` (`opt_start`, `opt_status`, `opt_archive`, `design_bisect`, `design_blame`,
`solve_run`, `experiment_suggest`); `compare` gains `experiment_suggest` and `predict_register`; `gaits`,
`shield`, `inherit`, `ladder` and `families` fill as assigned.

**Gate run.** `scripts/e2e/tether_parity.sh --through M4`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-TETHER-02 for the M4 verbs: empty parity diff; a schema, an example and a refusal list for every M4 verb; `tools/list` equals the registry under the name map; measured counts recorded.
- [ ] `result.show` returns the stored bytes, the full claim product and the paged inputs; it creates no job; its `standing` agrees with `result.revalidate` on a planted change.
- [ ] Every Frontier verb's `capabilities` row is one of the three forms above and agrees with the pilot and kill-gate records.
- [ ] Every example of every implemented M4 verb runs on the fixtures and returns a schema-valid envelope with a claim product; declared verbs answer `FROB-REF-724`.
- [ ] `mission.synthesize --strategy <s>` accepts exactly the registered strategies; an unknown strategy is 802.
- [ ] `predict.register` requires an idempotency key and binds the prediction before the run it predicts (a registration after the run is refused by its engine; the refusal is in the list).
- [ ] The freeze file includes the M4 entries; the state of `solve.run` matches the kill decision or is recorded as pending.
- [ ] Every M4 verb sits in a task cluster of at most seven.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-verbs handlers_m4 result_show`)
- `every_m4_entry_complete` (table-driven checklist).
- `result_show_bytes_equal_stored`; `result_show_creates_no_job`; `result_show_inputs_paged`.
- `result_show_standing_after_edit`: a result that depends on a design edited afterwards shows `invalidated` with the edit's identifier.
- `result_show_sealed`: a qualification result returns its verdict and receipt only.
- `claim_product_fields_complete`: kind, class, weakest, tier, determinism, reality, conditions all present.
- `frontier_status_three_forms`: a synthetic Frontier verb with no pilot record is `not implemented`.
- `strategy_enum`: `codesign`, `linkage`, `refine` accepted; `continuation` is declared for a later milestone and answers 724; `foo` is 802.
- `search_cluster_follows_kill_decision`.
- `declared_verbs_724`: `foundry.loads`, `ground.excite`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/m4_verbs.sh` (seed-explicit; `frobd` running with tokens): `frob predict register --metric success
--interval 0.6 0.9 --confidence 0.8 --for next --key p1 --json`; `frob opt start … --key o1 --sync-wait-ms 0
--json` (exit 8); `frob opt status <campaign> --json`; `frob job wait`; `frob opt archive <campaign> --json`;
`frob experiment suggest … --json`; `frob result show <result> --json` before and after a `design edit`;
`frob result revalidate <design> --since <version> --key rv1 --json`; `frob predict report --json`; then the
parity script. It sources the shared helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/m4_verbs/<utc>.ndjson` plus a
transcript: `cmd` lines with exit code and wall time; the envelope's `evidence` block per call; `assert` lines
for expected exits, for `result.show` byte equality and standing, for job-handle continuity; `metric` lines for
estimate against actual; a `summary` naming GATE-TETHER-02 (M4).

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: empty diff with one exemption; registry, CLI and MCP counts equal for the M4 set.
- Negative controls (each must be caught): a `result.show` that recomputes a summary (the no-job assertion and the byte-equality assertion); a Frontier verb reported implemented without a pilot record; a `predict.register` registered as `read`; an M4 verb in no task cluster.
- Determinism lanes: generated documentation, the freeze file and golden envelopes identical twice on one machine, in debug and release, on both hosts.

## Considerations

- No seed bead names `result.show`; the verb appendix attributes its definition to the surface chapter, so its handler is carried here as a pure read over the result store.
- OPEN-ATEL-4 was decided at the M3 freeze (default: `result.revalidate` and `mission.revalidate` are two verbs over one engine); the M4 grades extend the engine, not the surface.
- Verbs first due at M6 and M8 (`control.lift`, `foundry.lifecycle`, `mission.transport`, `skill.transport`) have no verb-set bead of their own; they are registered as declared by the M5 verb-set bead so that the registry is complete, and their handlers come with their epics.
- With two dozen dependencies, entries are registered as declared early and flipped as handlers land; the gate is recorded when the last M4 handler lands.
