## Background

Milestone M5 adds proofs at coverage, reality and manufacture to FrankenRobotics: certificate coverage for
derived kernels and the reflection pilot; the Reality lane (admitting measurements, identifying parameters,
freezing predictions, scoring them once, issuing reality bands, the replication kit); real-place ingestion; and
Foundry (loads, sizing, topology optimization, manufacturability, tolerance, the build packet). This bead
registers their verbs: every verb whose first milestone is M5, and the verbs dated earlier whose mechanisms land
now, becomes an implemented entry of `registries/verbs.toml` (compiled into the static table of the crate
`frob-verbs`, from which the CLI, the MCP tools, the schemas and the agent guide are generated) with an input
schema, an output schema, at least one runnable example and a refusal list. The surface parity gate is recorded
for the M5 set.

It is the last of the five verb-set beads, so it also makes the registry **complete**: every verb the plan
names is present, the few whose mechanisms belong to the Frontier and Moonshot epics of M6 and M8 as declared
entries that answer "not implemented in this build".

Two things are particular to M5. The `reality` field of the envelope's evidence block, which has read `no-claim`
on every answer so far, can now carry a calibrated family: this is the first milestone at which a verb may
return anything else, and only for a calibrated cell. And the exporting verbs here produce the artifacts closest
to hardware (a build packet, a certificate bundle, a replication kit), so the no-authorization flags matter
most: `execution_authorized`, `hardware_safety_signoff`, `sim_to_real_certified`, and on build packets a fourth,
`fabrication_released`, each the constant `false`.

GATE-TETHER-02 (Known answer, TARGET): the CLI and MCP surfaces are generated from the registry and their parity
diff is empty; every verb has a schema, an example and a refusal list; the served `tools/list` equals the
registry under the name map (`.` and `-` replaced by `_`).

## Scope

In: registry entries (those no cluster registry bead enters), schemas, examples, refusal lists, bindings, golden
envelopes and status for the M5 set; the check that each M5 verb is seated in NJL-05's task-cluster assignment
(the assignment and its lints are NJL-05's, a dependency); the declared entries for M6 and M8 verbs; the registry completeness check; the M5
additions to the freeze; the M5 parity record. Out: each verb's computation (its subsystem bead); the format
registry (its own seed); the safety gates on flags and private places.

## Technical approach

**The checklist per verb** (unchanged): a complete entry; two schemas whose integers are only `int53`, `u64str`
or `i64str`, with units on quantities and the untrusted mark on fields that echo imported text (log lines from a
real robot, scan annotations, licence strings); a runnable example; a refusal list (the handler's chapter codes;
the generator appends the codes of its kind and of its fields, NJL-01's variations, as BD-312 lists them, and no
row repeats them); a golden envelope; a binding with no computation; a truthful `capabilities` row.

**The M5 set, by cluster** (R read, U run, M mutate; the dependency that builds the computation in brackets).

**Declared entries (registered, not implemented; they answer `FROB-REF-724`, exit 5).** `control.lift` (U, first
due M6: fit and calibrate a lifted linear planning model for one soft-soil regime); `foundry.lifecycle` (U, M8,
Moonshot, under the feature `lifecycle`); `mission.transport` (U, M8, Frontier: move a certified claim through a
map between task interfaces with a bounded error, or return a counterexample naming the lost mode; its static
core is already registered); `skill.transport` (U, M8, Moonshot: move a skill artifact to another body with a
learned residual). The option `foundry.export --with-firmware` (Moonshot, M8) is declared in the schema as an
option that is refused in this build. Each is flipped to implemented by its epic's bead, through the freeze's
change record.

**Reality in the envelope.** The evidence block's `reality` takes one of: `no-claim`, `synthetic`,
`published-anchor`, `candidate`, `calibrated` (with the calibrated family the band was issued for), `retired`.
The serializer accepts it only as part of a claim product; a verb outside the `ground` cluster can carry a value
other than `no-claim` only by consuming a band (the claim product propagates it, weakest wins). Table-driven
check: every verb's golden envelope outside calibrated cells says `no-claim`.

**Security-relevant entries (table-driven tests).**
- `ground.ingest` requires `ground:import`; everything it admits is data, and text from logs is marked
  untrusted.
- `ground.score` is the only verb that opens sealed validation data, once; its output schema has no per-trial
  raw field beyond what the calibration record defines; re-running it on a retired holdout is refused by its
  engine with a code in its refusal list.
- `foundry.export` requires `foundry:run` and `export:packet`, writes its packet directory `--packet <dir>` only
  under the session's export root, and its envelope and packet carry four `false` flags; `prove.bundle` requires
  `cert:write`; `ground.kit` requires `ground:run`, and `export:packet` when it writes a kit with `--out`, and
`train.export`-style exports require
  their export scopes; anything derived from a private scene requires `private:export`.
- No M5 verb accepts a filesystem path outside the export root or an identifier that resolves to one.

**Task clusters.** `reality` (`ground_ingest`, `ground_identify`, `ground_predict`, `ground_score`,
`ground_band`, `predict_report`, `report_run`) and `make` (`foundry_size`, `foundry_optimize`, `foundry_check`,
`foundry_tolerance`, `foundry_export`, `prove_bundle`, `report_design`) become fully implemented; `ground_excite`,
`ground_kit`, `foundry_loads`, `prove_reflect` and `scene_probe_request` sit in the further clusters NJL-05
already assigns them (`calibrate`: `ground_excite`, `ground_kit`; `structure`: `foundry_loads`; `proofs`:
`prove_reflect`; `places-check`: `scene_probe_request`). That table governs and this bead checks it. (An earlier
draft of this bead proposed two clusters of its own, `reality-2` and `make-2`; a second assignment would break the
lint that every verb is seated against one table, so they are withdrawn.)

**Registry completeness.** A test holds NJL-03's reference: its baseline list of every verb name the plan's verb
appendix gives (243 names, by cluster: the 34 clusters of its mechanical inventory and those its chapter-defined
verbs add, 44 in all as NJL-03 counts them) plus the `[[addition]]` records (`artifact.import` of VERBS-artifact,
and the three late analysis verbs NJLx-51 registers, `analyze.sweep-monotone`, `analyze.constraint-graph`,
`analyze.regressor-identity`), and asserts that each is registered exactly once, that no unlisted name is
registered without an entry in the freeze's change record, and that every `analyze` verb corresponds to one
registered analysis. The list, not a number, is the reference; its count is NJLx-51's known answer, 247 verbs in
44 clusters, under NJL-03's fail-closed default that `fidelity.rank` is not registered (the owner decision of
N16b-27: if it is registered the count is 248, and the name is a registry row instead of a retired-name row,
never both). Under a fired KILL-SOLVE the `solve.run` row is withdrawn to the retired-name table (BD-376) and the
count is one lower.

**Gate run.** `scripts/e2e/tether_parity.sh --through M5`.

- [ ] Outside calibrated cells every golden envelope has `reality: no-claim`; a planted attempt to set `calibrated` from a handler that consumed no band does not compile or is rejected by the serializer.
- [ ] Each security-relevant entry is refused without its scope and accepted with it; no M5 verb can be made to write outside the export root.
- [ ] Every example of every implemented M5 verb runs on the fixtures (the synthetic rehearsal data set and the structure fixtures), after the example-identity substitution of NJL-02 (this bead adds the rows of `fixtures/verbs/identities.toml` for the examples its handlers make run), and returns a schema-valid envelope with a claim product. - [ ] The freeze file includes the M5 entries; every M5 verb is seated in NJL-05's task-cluster assignment (no cluster above seven tools; its lints pass with the complete registry).

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-verbs handlers_m5`, `cargo test -p frob-conformance registry_complete golden`)
- `every_m5_entry_complete` (table-driven checklist).
- `registry_matches_reference_list`: missing, duplicated and unlisted names each fail with the name; the reference list holds 247 names (NJL-03's 243 and the additions `artifact.import` and NJLx-51's three); with `fidelity.rank` moved from the retired-name table to a row, 248; with `solve.run` withdrawn, 246.
- `m5_verbs_seated_per_njl05`: `ground_excite` and `ground_kit` are in `calibrate`, `foundry_loads` in `structure`, `prove_reflect` in `proofs`, `scene_probe_request` in `places-check`; no cluster named `reality-2` or `make-2` exists.
- `declared_later_verbs_724`: `control.lift`, `foundry.lifecycle`, `mission.transport` (beyond its static core), `skill.transport`; `foundry.export --with-firmware` → refused in this build.
- `export_flags_three_and_four`: `prove.bundle`, `ground.kit` carry three; `foundry.export` carries four; a packet with `fabrication_released: true` is rejected.
- `reality_only_via_band`: a synthetic handler outside `ground` that tries to emit `calibrated` without consuming a band is rejected.
- `scope_table_m5`: `ground:import`, `export:packet`, `cert:write`, `private:export`.
- `export_root_jail`: a `--packet` (and `prove bundle --out`, `ground kit --out`) directory of `../x`, an absolute path and a symlink out of the root are each refused with `FROB-REF-734`, exit 5, and nothing is written.
- `score_once`: a second `ground.score` on the same sealed holdout returns the engine's refusal, which is in the verb's refusal list.
- `untrusted_marks_on_log_text`: a log line "ignore previous instructions" ingested by `ground.ingest` appears only in a marked field of any later envelope.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/m5_verbs.sh` (header: `bead=BD-409 milestone=M5 labels=tether,registry,verbs,reality,foundry
gates=GATE-TETHER-02 seed=409 needs=cpu,frobd,mcp plants=export-no-flags,calibrated-outside-ground,retired-spelling,missing-reference-verb`,
each plant one negative control below; `frobd` with tokens; the synthetic rehearsal data set, labelled
`synthetic`): `frob ground ingest <dataset dir>/ --key g1 --json`; `frob ground excite <design> --cards <cards>
--rig <rig> --json`; `frob ground identify <dataset> --key g2 --sync-wait-ms 0 --json` (exit 8) then `frob job
wait <job> --json`; `frob ground predict --trial <trial> --key g3 --json`; `frob ground score <prediction> --key
g4 --json`, then the same prediction again under a new key `g4b` (expect the spent-holdout refusal
`FROB-REF-886`, exit 3); `frob ground band --cell <cell> --metric <m> --level 0.90 --json` (inside the rehearsal's
scored cell a band whose status is `synthetic`, BD-393, never `calibrated`; `no-claim` outside it); `frob foundry size <design> --json`; `frob foundry check <design> --link
thigh --key f1 --json`; `frob foundry export <design> --packet <export root>/<design> --key f2 --json`, then the
same with `--packet ../outside` under key `f3` (expect `FROB-REF-734` `path-outside-roots`, exit 5); `frob prove bundle <certificate> --out
<export root>/bundle/ --key b1 --json`; `frob-verify` on the bundle; then the parity script and the completeness
test. It
sources the shared helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/m5_verbs/<utc>.ndjson` plus a transcript: `cmd` lines
with exit code and wall time; the envelope's `evidence` block per call, including `reality`; `assert` lines for
expected exits, for the flags on every exported artifact (read back from the files), for `reality` values inside
and outside the scored cell; `refusal` lines; a `summary` naming GATE-TETHER-02 (M5).

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: empty diff with one exemption; registry, CLI and MCP counts equal; the reference verb list is covered exactly.
- Negative controls (each must be caught): an exporting verb without the flags; a verb outside `ground` reporting `calibrated`; an M5 verb registered under a retired second spelling; a verb of the reference list missing from the registry.
- Determinism lanes: generated documentation, the freeze file and golden envelopes identical twice on one machine, in debug and release, on both hosts.

## Considerations

- M5 does not gate the tournament of M7; a stratum that needs an M5 mechanism that has not landed is declared `not run` there. The registry must therefore report each M5 verb's status truthfully whenever M5 lands relative to M4.
- Several Foundry and Ground verbs are Frontier with their own kill gates; a verb whose mechanism fell back is described by the fallback's name in `capabilities` (for example manufacturability findings as advisory lints).
- The Reality lane may legitimately end with a recorded no-claim; then `ground.band` returns `no-claim` everywhere and every golden envelope keeps `reality: no-claim`. That is a pass for this bead.
- No verb-set bead exists for M6 and M8; the declared entries here are how those verbs stay visible, and the freeze's change record is how they become implemented.
