## Background

The terminal dashboard `frob-tui` (core in N32b-06: a pure model updated from verb envelopes and the daemon's
event stream, with a source pointer on every displayed number) shows six panes and a gate view. This bead builds
the three panes that concern evidence and money and the gate view; N32b-08 builds the three that concern the
machine. All are read-only views. The rule for every pane (decision ADR-TETHER-10): each value shown is
obtainable by a named verb, and the pane definition names that verb.

What the three panes are for. *Races*: a person watching a campaign wants to see which candidates are still
alive and how strong the evidence is. *Budgets*: who has spent what, and how much of the qualification error
budget is gone. *Evidence*: what has been promoted, what is still provisional, and whether any monitor that
guards the statistics has raised an alarm. The gate view answers "where is the project" from the gate register
and nothing else.

Vocabulary used on screen (fixed by the comparison machinery, not by the dashboard). A paired comparison returns
per metric a verdict `settled`, `tied` or `inconclusive`, or `incomparable` or `infeasible` where it could not be
scored. An anytime-valid comparison reports an e-value (evidence against "no difference"; larger is stronger) and
pairs used out of its cap. A result is `provisional` until promoted to the referee tier, then `established`.
The blinded qualification lane spends an error budget α across submissions: submission i receives level
αᵢ = α / [i·(i + 1)], and after n submissions the sum spent is α·n/(n + 1), which stays below α for every n.

## Scope

In: the pane definitions (sources, layout, cells, derived cells) for Races, Budgets and Evidence; the gate-status
view; their empty, stale, unimplemented and refused states; their dumps for tests.
Out: the core (N32b-06); Devices, Queue and Failures (N32b-08); snapshot and reproduction tests on the recorded
trace (N32b-09); the verbs themselves.

## Technical approach

| Pane | Shows | Source verb(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Races | Survival brackets of each race and each ladder rung; per pair the e-value, pairs used of the cap, and the verdict; cancelled candidates | `opt.status`, `study.status` |
| Budgets | Per agent: GPU seconds, CPU core-seconds and disk spent and left; the qualification error budget with each submission's αᵢ and the sum spent | `session.show` (with `all`), `eval.scorecard` |
| Evidence | Promotions; results still provisional; alarms from the fidelity ledger and the exchangeability monitor | `eval.scorecard`, `telemetry.summary` |
| Gates (view) | One row per gate: identifier, claim kind, milestone, status, measured value | `capabilities` (its gate section, generated from `registries/gates.toml`) |

**Races.** A `Tree`: study or race → rung → comparison. A comparison row shows candidate A and B by short content
address and name, metric, `e = <value>`, a `ProgressBar` "pairs <used> / <cap>" (always with its denominator),
verdict, tier, and a `provisional` marker. A fixed-n study shows complete pairs of n and its reachable verdicts
instead of an e-value. Cancelled candidates are listed dimmed with the reason (stopped by the rule, budget,
cancelled by the agent). The five pair counts shown under a comparison (A only, B only, both, neither, unscored)
carry a derived cell "sum" whose inputs are the five source cells; the pane shows the planned count beside it and
highlights a mismatch instead of hiding it.

**Budgets.** A `Table` with one row per agent namespace: for GPU seconds, CPU core-seconds and disk bytes the
columns `cap`, `spent`, `reserved`, `left` with units, and derived sessions indented under their parent; a last
row for the daemon's own overhead line (idle reserve), which is never charged to an agent. Below it, one block
per open qualification campaign: total α, a row per submission (index i, αᵢ as the exact rational the verb
returns, for example `0.05/56`, and its decimal), and "sum spent" as returned by the verb. The pane displays the
verb's values; it does not compute the schedule.

**Evidence.** Three lists: promotions (result, from tier to tier, the correction applied, time); provisional
results older than a configurable age (default 24 h of daemon time), which are the ones most likely to be cited
too early; alarms, each with its source (fidelity ledger envelope exceeded; exchangeability monitor), the stratum
and the first offending record. An alarm is never auto-dismissed; it leaves the list when the source verb stops
reporting it.

**Gate view.** Columns: identifier, claim (one of the seven kinds), milestone, status (`not-run`, `pass`, `fail`,
`killed`, `no-claim`, `superseded`), measured value, date. Filters by milestone and status. The data comes only
from the gate register through the `capabilities` verb; the dashboard holds no copy of thresholds. A `pass` row
shows the number of controls caught with it.

**States common to all panes.** Empty (no data yet: says so, with the source verb's name); stale (daemon
unreachable: last values with their daemon instant); unimplemented (`FROB-REF-724` shown with the verb's
`first_milestone`); refused (`FROB-REF-720` shown with the missing scope, typically `session:read`).

**Dump format for tests.** `--dump` writes `<pane>.txt` (the frame) and `<pane>.cells.json`: a list of
`{row, column, text, value, unit, src: {verb, args_digest, pointer}, derived_from: [...]}`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the four views renders from a recorded trace and its dump lists every numeric cell with a resolving
      source pointer or a `derived_from` list of such cells.
- [ ] The Races pane shows, for a planted anytime-valid comparison, the e-value, pairs used of the cap and the
      verdict exactly as `study.status` returns them, and the five pair counts with their planned total.
- [ ] The Budgets pane shows the identity cap = spent + reserved + left + delegated per row as returned, and the
      overhead line separately.
- [ ] The α block shows, for α = 0.05, levels 0.025, 0.0083333…, 0.0041667, 0.0025 for submissions 1 to 4 and a
      sum spent of 0.04, when the verb returns that schedule.
- [ ] With `opt.status` not implemented in the build, the Races pane still shows studies and names the missing
      verb with `FROB-REF-724`.
- [ ] The gate view's rows equal the `capabilities` gate section row for row.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-tui/tests/panes_evidence.rs` (headless harness; envelopes are real outputs captured from the verbs
on fixtures, stored under `crates/frob-tui/fixtures/`):
- `races_rows_from_study_status`: a comparison at e = 310 with 96 of 96 pairs, verdict `settled`, renders those
  three values; cells point to `/result/comparisons/0/e_value`, `/pairs_used`, `/pairs_cap`, `/verdict`.
- `races_pair_counts_and_sum`: counts 19, 2, 40, 3, 0 with planned 64: the derived sum cell shows 64 and no
  highlight; with planned 65 the mismatch is highlighted.
- `races_fixed_n_shows_reachable_verdicts`.
- `budgets_rows_and_children_indented`; `budgets_overhead_line_separate`.
- `alpha_block_values`: the four levels and the sum 0.04 above; rational text preserved.
- `evidence_alarm_persists_until_source_clears`.
- `gate_view_equals_capabilities`.
- `pane_states`: empty, stale, unimplemented, refused each render their message.

### End-to-end script and logging
Steps `evidence-*` of `scripts/e2e/tui_panes.sh` (N32b-09): a seeded daemon run with one anytime-valid study, one
fixed-n study, two agents with budgets and a planted qualification campaign of four submissions; dump the four
views; re-issue each source verb with `frob --json` and compare every cell by pointer. Log lines carry the
command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block of each source envelope, cells compared and mismatched
(threshold 0), and the first mismatching cell with both values.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-760: Σ_{i=1..n} α/[i·(i + 1)] = α·n/(n + 1) < α; for α = 0.05 and n = 4 the sum is 0.04.
- KA-757: a_only + b_only + both + neither + unscored = planned (19 + 2 + 40 + 3 + 0 = 64).
- Negative control: a Budgets pane that sums job charges itself; when the daemon's `spent` includes a charge the
  pane has not seen, the reproduction test must fail.
- Negative control: an α block that computes α/i²: `alpha_block_values` must fail at i = 1 (0.05 against 0.025).
- Negative control: a Races pane that shows a percentage without its denominator fails the cell schema (a
  progress cell requires `value` and `total`).

## Considerations

- Showing the e-value invites reading it as a probability. The pane labels it "e-value (evidence; not a
  probability)" and links the verdict, which is what the rule decided.
- A leaderboard-like ordering of agents by spend is deliberately absent: the pane is for accounting, not ranking.
- `session.show --all` needs the scope `session:read`; a dashboard token without it shows only its own session
  and says why.
- If KILL-TUI fires, these definitions remain useful as the specification of which verb fields a person reads.
