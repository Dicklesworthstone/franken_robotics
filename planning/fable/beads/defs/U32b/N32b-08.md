## Background

The terminal dashboard `frob-tui` (core in N32b-06) shows six read-only panes. This bead builds the three that
show the machine at work: what each device is doing, what is waiting and why, and what has just failed. As with
every pane, each value is obtainable by a named verb (decision ADR-TETHER-10) and carries a pointer to the field
of the envelope it came from.

Context a reader of these panes needs. The daemon `frobd` keeps one persistent simulation environment per device
and shape class (a size bucket of robot models); an environment has a fixed number of world slots (the reference
batch is B = 8,192). When a slot's episode ends, the dispatcher resets that slot with the next admitted episode
from any job of the same shape class, so many small jobs share one batch. A share of slots (5%, TARGET; 410 of
8,192) is reserved for interactive work. Jobs fall into size classes by the system's own estimate: `interactive`
(point estimate at most 30 s of device time), `batch`, and `campaign` (above the machine envelope; refused without
an approved envelope). A batch job can be preempted at a snapshot boundary and resumed with identical digests.
Every estimate comes with an interval; a stratum (verb, tier, device family, shape class) is called calibrated
once it has 19 finished jobs, and `uncalibrated` before.

## Scope

In: pane definitions (sources, layout, cells) for Devices, Queue and Failures; the slot-strip compression rule;
their empty, stale, unimplemented and refused states; their dumps.
Out: the core (N32b-06); the other panes (N32b-07); the conformance run (N32b-09); the verbs and the dispatcher.

## Technical approach

| Pane | Shows | Source verb(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Devices | Per device: a slot strip coloured by job and agent, the reserve, environments by shape class, arena and kernel-cache use, the requested and actual placement of each stage, temperature on the Mac | `spark.devices`, `job.list` |
| Queue | Jobs by size class with wait, estimate against actual, the calibration of the estimate interval, preemptions | `job.list` |
| Failures | Latest autopsies with their first cause and stability; new minimal counterexample worlds | `autopsy.show`, `nemesis.hardset` |

**Devices.** One block per device (the two CUDA cards on the Linux host, the Metal device on the Mac, and
`batch-cpu`). For each environment on it (one per shape class):

- *Slot strip.* B slots drawn in W terminal cells. Cell k covers slots [⌊k·B/W⌋, ⌊(k + 1)·B/W⌋). Its colour is the
  job occupying the most slots of the cell (ties: the lowest job identifier); a cell entirely in the reserve is
  drawn in the reserve style; a free cell is blank. Colours come from a fixed palette indexed by a hash of the job
  identifier, with the agent shown by a second attribute (underline pattern), so the strip is a pure function of
  the occupancy list. Beneath the strip an exact legend: per job, slots occupied of B and the share as a
  percentage with one decimal; the reserve as "reserve 410 / 8,192"; free slots. Exact counts are always in the
  legend, because the strip is lossy by construction.
- *Environment line.* Shape class identifier, capacity B, jobs in the environment out of the cap (64, TARGET).
- *Memory.* Device arena bytes used of total; binary kernel-cache entries and bytes.
- *Placement.* A small table per stage of the step: requested placement, actual placement, and a marker where
  they differ (from the placement receipts).
- *Temperature.* On the Mac only, the value the device verb reports, with its unit; elsewhere the field is absent
  and the line is not drawn.

**Queue.** A `Tabs` widget with one tab per size class and a combined view. Per job: identifier, agent (or
"other" with class only, when the dashboard's token may not see names), state, wait so far (daemon instants),
estimate with its interval, actual so far, the ratio actual ÷ estimate when finished, preemption count. A header
per stratum shows calibration: `calibrated (n = <count>), 90% interval covered <k> of <m> finished` or
`uncalibrated (n = <count> < 19)`. Jobs over twice their estimate are marked: when 20% of the last 50 jobs are so
marked the dispatcher falls back to round-robin, and the pane shows that state from the verb's `dispatch_mode`
field. Refused admissions (`FROB-REF-703`, the admission guard) appear in the event log with `retry: backoff`.

**Failures.** Two lists. Latest autopsies: run, world address, the first cause (the top factor and its contrast),
and stability (the flip rate under perturbation, and a `stable` or `unstable` label as the verb gives it). New
minimal counterexample worlds from the hard-world set: world identifier, the number of edits from nominal, the
design it defeats, when added. Selecting a row shows the handle to pass to `frob autopsy show` or `frob nemesis
hardset`, so the person can continue on the command line.

**Slot arithmetic shown in the legend (and used as known answers).** A paired comparison of 96 pairs occupies 192
slots, 192 ÷ 8,192 = 2.34% of the batch; a 16-world mission probe 16 slots (0.2%); a 30-pair fixed-n study 60
slots (0.7%); a 2,048-world run 25%.

**States.** As for all panes: empty, stale, unimplemented (`FROB-REF-724`), refused (`FROB-REF-720`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three panes render from a recorded trace; every numeric cell has a resolving source pointer.
- [ ] For a planted occupancy (jobs of 192, 60, 16 and 2,048 slots in B = 8,192 with a 410-slot reserve) the legend
      shows 192 (2.3%), 60 (0.7%), 16 (0.2%), 2,048 (25.0%), reserve 410, and free 5,466; the strip at W = 64
      matches the stored snapshot.
- [ ] The strip is identical for two occupancy lists that differ only in order.
- [ ] The Queue pane shows `uncalibrated` for a stratum with 18 finished jobs and `calibrated` at 19.
- [ ] Other principals' jobs appear with class and state only when the token lacks operator read.
- [ ] The Failures pane shows first cause and stability exactly as `autopsy.show` returns them.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`crates/frob-tui/tests/panes_machine.rs` (headless harness; captured real envelopes):
- `slot_strip_cell_bounds`: B = 8,192, W = 64: cell k covers 128 slots; B = 8,192, W = 100: cells cover 81 or 82
  slots and partition [0, 8,192).
- `slot_strip_majority_and_ties`.
- `slot_strip_order_independent`.
- `legend_exact_counts`: the acceptance occupancy; free = 8,192 − 410 − (192 + 60 + 16 + 2,048) = 5,466.
- `placement_difference_marked`: a stage requested on the device and placed on the host is marked.
- `temperature_absent_off_mac`.
- `queue_calibration_boundary`: n = 18 and n = 19.
- `queue_over_estimate_marker`: actual 21 s against estimate 10 s is marked; 19 s is not.
- `foreign_jobs_class_only`.
- `failures_rows_from_autopsy_show`: first cause "strip friction", contrast and flip rate rendered from the fields.

### End-to-end script and logging
Steps `machine-*` of `scripts/e2e/tui_panes.sh` (N32b-09): on `batch-cpu` with a small environment (B = 512, so
the test needs no GPU), submit jobs of 12, 60 and 128 worlds from two agents plus one long batch job, dump the
panes, re-issue `frob spark devices --json` and `frob job list --json`, and compare cells by pointer. A second
lane on a GPU host, when present, repeats it at B = 8,192. Log lines carry the command, exit code, wall time, the
`evidence` block, slot counts with thresholds (sum of occupied + reserve + free = B), cells mismatched (threshold
0) and the first mismatch.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-761: 192 ÷ 8,192 = 2.34%.
- Negative control: a strip that colours a cell by its first slot instead of the majority; the snapshot of a cell
  split 10/118 between two jobs must differ.
- Negative control: a legend derived from the strip (counting coloured cells × B/W) instead of from the verb; the
  exact-count test must fail (192 is not a multiple of 128).
- Negative control: a Queue pane that calls a stratum calibrated at n = 9; the boundary test must fail.

## Considerations

- The strip compresses 8,192 slots into tens of cells; small jobs vanish in it. That is why the legend is the
  authority and the tests check the legend, not colours.
- Temperature is advisory and device-specific; it never feeds the dispatcher from the dashboard.
- The Failures pane shows causes as Autopsy labels them, including `Estimated`; it adds no interpretation.
- The reserve, job cap and class boundaries are constants under an open question (OPEN-TETHER-4, bead N32b-35);
  the panes read them from the verbs, never from their own source.
