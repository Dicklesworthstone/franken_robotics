## Background

The viewer draws physical overlays on recorded episodes (page: N32b-10; overlay interface and the first five
overlays: N32b-11). The rule is the same for every overlay: a named telemetry channel, its unit, a numeric
legend, the evidence class of the run, and purity (a function of the snapshot; nothing estimated). This bead
builds the remaining five overlays of the table, which depend on third-milestone mechanisms of other subsystems
(the film-friction law, clearance complexes, forks and their classification, minimal counterexample worlds) and
on one fifth-milestone mechanism (calibrated reality bands), plus two scene map layers the sensing chapter lists
among what the viewer draws.

Three of these overlays show comparisons, and comparisons are where a picture most easily overstates. The ghost
shows a counterfactual fork over its parent; the world difference shows what separates a failing world from its
nominal one; the reality band shows how far simulation may be trusted against measurement, which for almost
every run is not at all. Each therefore carries its label on screen: the fork's class, the attribution's
`Estimated` standing, and for the band either a calibrated family or the words "no claim".

## Scope

In: overlays `film`, `clearance`, `ghost`, `world-diff`, `reality-band`; the map layers `deviation` and `unknown`;
their legends, probes and absent-data behaviour.
Out: the interface (N32b-11); fork data inside replay packets and the packet tables panel (N32b-41, N32b-42);
computing any fork, band, complex or edit list (their own subsystems).

## Technical approach

All seven implement the `Overlay` record of N32b-11 (`requires`, `elements`, `legend`, `probe`); each element
carries the stored quantized value it shows.

| Overlay id | Quantity | Elements and encoding | Data |
|---|---|---|---|
| `film` | Film thickness and effective friction on floors, including film the robot has moved | A floor-aligned texture over each patch: thickness on a sequential scale (unit m, shown in µm or mm with the unit), or effective friction coefficient on a second scale, switched by a legend toggle; patch outlines; where the film state is per-world and time-varying, the texture is that of the drawn tick | Floor fields `film.thickness` (m), `film.mu_eff` (1) |
| `clearance` | Doorways and passages of a clearance complex, passable or not, with the bottleneck named | Each doorway or passage cell outlined green (passable for this robot's envelope) or red (not); the bottleneck of the binding cut highlighted, with a label holding its name and its clearance margin in metres; a route witness as a polyline when one exists | The clearance complex of the scene and design: cells, flags, the cut, the witness path |
| `ghost` | A fork drawn over its parent, diverging from the first different event | The fork's bodies drawn translucent over the parent from the fork tick; before the fork tick nothing extra is drawn (the prefix is the parent's); a marker at the first different event with its tick and stage; a per-body deviation readout (distance between fork and parent body origins, in metres, from the two stored rows) | Parent and fork frames; fork class and label; first-divergence record |
| `world-diff` | The edits that separate a minimal counterexample world from its nominal world | One marker per edit at its location: the edited property, nominal value → edited value with unit (for example "counter lip 18 → 22 mm", "wet strip 40 mm before the lip"); a list panel with the same rows in edit order | The edit list of the `.fmcw` |
| `reality-band` | The calibrated band around a predicted trace, or the words "no claim" | A strip in one of two forms, chosen by what the band is a statement about. *Scalar form* (a metric that is one number per episode, which is what `ground.band` issues): the metric's axis with the run's value as a marker and the interval as a bar from the stored `lower` to the stored `upper` (no upper end for a one-sided band), placed at the tick where the metric is attained when the run records one. *Trace form* (a metric recorded as a time series with a band per sample): the predicted trace with the band's lower and upper curves. In both: the nominal coverage (level), the calibration count n and the band's status in the legend; when no band exists for the design and family, the strip shows the value or trace and the words "no claim" in place of a band | The snapshot's `realityBand` block for the metric (kind `band`, `no-claim` with its code and counts, or `unread`), which is the `ground.band` envelope copied field for field; absent that block, the header's reality field |
| `deviation` (map layer) | Collision-to-visual deviation | A heat map on the visual mesh of collidable regions: bounded deviation (a guaranteed bound) and sampled deviation (a sample maximum) on separate scales, never mixed; regions over the pack threshold outlined | The scene pack's deviation record per region |
| `unknown` (map layer) | Unknown regions of a scanned or truncated scene | A hatch over regions the scene pack marks unknown (outside the truncation range, or unobserved in a scan) | The pack's `unknown` map (N19a-04, from M2: regions labelled `unknown` in generated and truncated scenes); for ingested scenes the same map carries the three-valued occupancy mask (N19c-03, M5) |

**Rules specific to the ghost.** (1) A null fork (the empty intervention) drawn as a ghost coincides with its
parent: the rows are bit-equal, so the deviation readout is exactly zero in every frame; any non-zero value is a
defect upstream and is shown, not suppressed. (2) The ghost's legend carries the fork's class (continuation,
exogenous intervention, contact-affecting intervention, information intervention, or structural or
design-parameter intervention) and its label; a structural fork has no shared prefix and is drawn from tick zero
as a second run, labelled so. (3) The overlay is offered only for runs whose determinism class licenses
counterfactual forks (class D2 with the batch-composition invariance gate current); otherwise the legend says
why it is unavailable. (4) Deviation is the Euclidean distance between the two stored translations after
anchoring (cell difference × 8 m plus offset difference), computed in double precision in the page for display;
it is a display of two stored values, and the probe returns both stored rows.

**Rules specific to the band.** Source: before the fifth milestone no band can exist (`ground.band` is BD-393,
M5), so the overlay reads the snapshot header's reality field, which says `no-claim` for every run. From M5 the
band arrives as the `ground.band` envelope, whose output schema the verb registry declares from M1 (VERBS-ground),
passed through N32b-10's `snapshotFromEnvelopes`; this bead codes the band path against that registry schema and
tests it on a synthetic fixture that validates against it. The overlay shows the claim it is given and nothing
more. "No claim" is the
normal state before imported real-robot data exists; the legend then reads `reality: no-claim`. A band is drawn
only with its family identifier, nominal coverage and calibration count. The page never draws a band from a
default width, and it does no arithmetic on a band: the bar's ends are the stored `lower` and `upper`, never a
value plus or minus a half-width formed in the page. The block's three kinds map to three displays: `band`
draws the marker and bar (or the curves); `no-claim` draws no band element and the legend reads "no claim"
with the refusal's code and its observed and required counts (for example "8 calibration points, 19 needed");
`unread` (the band could not be read: a missing scope, or the verb absent from the build) draws no band
element, says "band not read" with the code, and leaves the header's own `reality:` line as it is. A band
whose status is `synthetic` (issued on rehearsal data) is labelled as such on the bar ("synthetic rehearsal,
not a measurement") and the legend reads `reality: synthetic [dataset=<prefix>]`; the word `calibrated` is
printed only when the block's status says it. The block's fields are this overlay's input contract (DESIGN;
at the third milestone they exist only in hand-built fixtures labelled `synthetic`): kind `band` has `cell`,
`metric`, `unit`, `sided` (`two` or `lower`), `n`, `level`, `index`, `qHat`, `value`, `lower`, `upper`
(absent when one-sided), `status` (`calibrated` or `synthetic`) and `dataset`; kind `no-claim` has `code`,
`message`, `observed`, `required` and `repair`; kind `unread` has `code` and `message`. Filling the block
from the real verb's envelope, the live source's `ground_band` tool and the first test on a band the real
verb issued are N32bp2-44 (M5), which depends on this bead, uses these field names and changes none of its
drawing code.

**Rules specific to clearance.** Passable and impassable are properties of (scene, robot envelope); the legend
names both identities. An impassable verdict backed by a certificate is marked as such; one that is only
unresolved is drawn in a third style (hatched), not red.

**Absent data.** As for every overlay: nothing is drawn and the legend names what is missing. In particular
`film` on a scene with no film channel and `ghost` on a run with no forks draw nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the five overlays and both map layers renders on its fixture with a legend holding the quantity's
      name, unit, numeric range and the run's evidence class.
- [ ] A null fork as a ghost shows a deviation of exactly 0 in every frame of the fixture.
- [ ] A ghost of an exogenous-intervention fork draws nothing before the fork tick and marks the first different
      event at the tick `telemetry.divergence` reports.
- [ ] `world-diff` on the two-edit counterexample fixture lists exactly the two edits with nominal and edited
      values and units.
- [ ] `reality-band` with no calibration shows "no claim" and draws no band curves; a block for a scalar metric
      draws one marker and one bar at the stored bounds; a `synthetic` band is labelled synthetic and never
      prints `calibrated`.
- [ ] `clearance` on the door fixture names the bottleneck and its margin; an unresolved passage is not drawn as
      impassable.
- [ ] `deviation` never mixes bounded and sampled values in one scale.
- [ ] All elements pass the purity and probe checks of the interface.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`viewer/tests/overlays_b.test.mjs` (pinned headless browser of N32b-44; recorded fixtures of real runs made
with N32b-10's `record-snapshot.mjs` where the mechanism exists, hand-built and labelled `synthetic` otherwise; the
synthetic band fixture validates against the registry's `ground.band` output schema):
- `film_patch_values`: a patch with stored thickness 0.2 mm and effective friction 0.25 beside dry floor at 0.8:
  probes inside read 0.0002 m and 0.25; outside, no film element and floor friction 0.8.
- `clearance_door_fixture`: a doorway 0.76 m wide and a loaded envelope 0.774 m wide: drawn impassable, bottleneck
  label "door", margin −0.014 m; with the envelope at 0.746 m: passable, margin +0.014 m.
- `clearance_unresolved_is_hatched`.
- `ghost_null_fork_zero`: 150 frames, 13 bodies: maximum deviation 0, exactly.
- `ghost_starts_at_fork_tick`: fork at tick 90: element list empty for ticks below 90.
- `ghost_first_divergence_marker`: marker tick equals the stored first-divergence tick.
- `ghost_deviation_anchored`: parent at cell 3 offset 3.9, fork at cell 4 offset −3.9: deviation
  8·(4 − 3) + (−3.9 − 3.9) = 0.2 m.
- `ghost_unlicensed_class_unavailable`: a run of tolerance class: no elements, legend states the reason.
- `world_diff_two_edits`: "counter lip 18 → 22 mm" and "wet strip 40 mm before the lip".
- `reality_band_no_claim`; `reality_band_drawn_with_family_and_coverage` (synthetic band).
- `reality_band_scalar_marker_and_bar`: a `synthetic` block of kind `band` for a scalar metric (value 4.20 s,
  lower 4.01 s, upper 4.39 s, n = 20, level 0.90): the element list holds exactly one marker at 4.20 s and one
  bar from 4.01 s to 4.39 s, and the probe returns the three stored numbers with the unit; changing the
  block's half-width field alone leaves the bar unchanged (no arithmetic in the page); a one-sided block has
  a bar with no upper end.
- `reality_band_kinds_and_status`: kind `no-claim` with code FROB-REF-889, observed 8 and required 19 → zero
  band elements and the legend "no claim … 8 calibration points, 19 needed"; kind `unread` → zero band
  elements, "band not read", the header's `reality:` line unchanged; status `synthetic` → the bar's label and
  the legend `reality: synthetic [dataset=…]`, and the string `calibrated` appears nowhere on the page.
- `deviation_two_scales`.
- `unknown_mask_hatched`: a generated scene with one region labelled `unknown`: the hatch covers exactly that
  region; a scene with no `unknown` map draws nothing and the legend says so.

### End-to-end script and logging
Steps `overlay-b-*` of `scripts/e2e/viewer_overlays.sh` (N32b-14): a run on a floor with a film patch; a scene
with the door fixture; a run with a null fork and one exogenous fork made by `frob run fork`; a shrunk
counterexample world from `frob nemesis shrink`; each loaded in the headless page through the live source
(N32bp1-01; under its fail-closed default, as a replay packet) and probed; values compared with
`frob telemetry query`, `frob telemetry divergence`, `frob scene show` and the `.fmcw` listing. Lines log the
command, exit code, wall time, the `evidence` block, per probe the page value, the verb value and the quantum,
the maximum null-fork deviation against 0, and the first mismatch.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answer: the null-fork ghost deviates by exactly zero.
- Known answer: the anchored deviation 0.2 m above; the door margins ±0.014 m.
- Negative control: a ghost that draws the fork's interpolated poses at the parent's frame times; the null-fork
  deviation becomes non-zero wherever frames do not align, and the test fails.
- Negative control: a `reality-band` that draws a ±10% band when none exists; the no-claim test fails.
- Negative control: a `clearance` that colours unresolved passages red; the hatch test fails.
- Negative control: a deviation computed from offsets alone (ignoring the cell difference) reports 7.8 m; the
  anchored test fails.

## Considerations

- The reality band's data does not exist before the fifth milestone; the overlay ships at the third with its
  no-claim state fully working, and the band path is exercised on a synthetic fixture until then. Wiring the live
  source and the snapshot conversion to carry a real band is N32bp2-44 (M5, after BD-393, where the band first
  exists); whether a replay packet carries the band envelope of its run is an open point of the packet page
  (N32b-40), and until it does a packet page shows the header's `reality:` line and no band.
- The `unknown` layer shows only generated and truncated scenes' unknown regions until ingested scenes arrive at
  the fifth milestone (N19c-03); the layer's code does not change then, only its data.
- The ghost is persuasive. A fork that rescues a run is evidence about the simulator's model under one
  intervention; the legend repeats the fork's label, and attribution text shown beside it is the text the
  attribution verbs returned, marked `Estimated`.
- Film textures of large floors can be heavy; the layer is drawn at the stored field resolution and is not
  resampled in a way that changes probed values (the probe reads the stored cell).
