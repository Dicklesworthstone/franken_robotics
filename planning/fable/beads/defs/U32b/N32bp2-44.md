## Background

The viewer is the browser page that shows a recorded or running simulation. It computes no physics: every number
it shows is read from a `read` verb and can be probed back to the stored value. One of its overlays, `reality-band`
(N32b-12, third milestone), shows how far a simulated metric may be from the measured one. Until the fifth
milestone no band can exist, so that overlay has only ever shown the words "no claim" and has been tested on a
hand-built band labelled `synthetic`.

A reality band is issued by the calibration subsystem's verb `ground.band` (BD-393, fifth milestone): inside one
cell (a design, its physical units, controller, firmware, surface class, task family and an envelope of recorded
conditions), for one scalar metric, at a level 1 − α, the half-width q̂ is the ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉-th smallest
calibration residual of n pairs, and a simulated value ŷ is then reported as [ŷ − q̂, ŷ + q̂]. Outside a
calibrated cell the verb refuses, and the honest display is "no claim" with the reason.

Two pieces of the viewer were built before the verb existed and cannot reach it: the live source's list of tools
(N32bp1-01, a module constant of four `read` tools) does not contain `ground_band`, and the one conversion from
verb envelopes to the page's immutable snapshot, `snapshotFromEnvelopes` (N32b-10), does not accept a
`ground.band` envelope. Neither bead can gain the change, because both land before the verb. This bead is that
change, with the first test of the overlay on a band the real verb issued.

Rejected alternatives: letting the page compute a band from residuals it reads (the viewer computes nothing, and a
band's authorization order is the calibration subsystem's); drawing a default-width band when none exists (a
reader would take it for evidence); leaving the overlay on its synthetic fixture (the path from the verb to the
pixels would then never be exercised).

Tier: Frontier, because the band is. The overlay stays Solid: with this bead absent it still shows "no claim".

## Scope

In: `ground_band` (read forms only) in the live source's tool list; the band envelope and its refusals through
`snapshotFromEnvelopes` into a frozen `realityBand` block; the same envelope in the recorded-fixture tool; the
legend rules for a band on synthetic data and for each refusal; the scalar form of the strip; the script
`scripts/e2e/viewer_reality_band.sh`.

Out, with the bead that has it: issuing, refusing and authorizing bands (BD-393, N30a-36 to N30a-38); the overlay's
drawing code, its purity and probe interface (N32b-12); the live source itself, its token handling and polling
(N32bp1-01); the snapshot model (N32b-10); the retire action of `ground.band` (N30a-43), which the viewer never
calls; a band inside an offline replay packet (the packer of N32b-40 decides what a packet carries; until it
carries the band envelope, a packet page shows the header's `reality:` line and no band).

## Technical approach

1. **Tool list.** The constant of `VerbSource` in `viewer/src/source.js` (N32bp1-01) becomes `run_show`, `telemetry_query`,
   `artifact_read`, `telemetry_stills`, `ground_band`. `ground.band` has kind `read` in the verb registry; its one
   state-changing form is the action `retire`, sent as the input property `action: "retire"`, which needs the
   capability `ground:retire`. The source never sets `action`: the request builder for `ground_band` takes
   `{for, metric, level, sided}` or `{list: true}` and has no parameter that could carry it.
2. **Request.** When the user turns the overlay on for a metric, `VerbSource.realityBand(metric, level, sided)`
   calls `ground_band` with `for` = the run's identifier (the verb takes the design, model identity, target unit
   and recorded conditions from the stored result), `metric` named with its unit (for example `crossing_time_s`),
   `level` as an exact decimal string (`"0.90"`), and `sided` (`two` or `lower`). The metric choices offered are
   the scalar metrics in the `run.show` header; the level choices are `0.90` and `0.95` (DESIGN; a level whose
   quantile index exceeds n comes back as a refusal and is shown as such). One request per (run, metric, level,
   sided); the answer is cached in the snapshot, which is immutable.
3. **Conversion.** `snapshotFromEnvelopes` accepts an optional envelope with output schema
   `frob.ground.band.out/1` and adds one frozen block per request to the snapshot:
   - a band: `{ kind: "band", cell, metric, unit, sided, n, level, index, qHat, value, lower, upper, status,
     dataset }`, copied field for field from the envelope (`value` is ŷ, `lower` and `upper` the interval the
     verb returned; `upper` is absent for `sided = lower`); `status` is the status word of the envelope's
     `evidence.reality` (`calibrated` or `synthetic`);
   - a refusal: `{ kind: "no-claim", code, message, observed, required, repair }` from the refusal object, for
     `FROB-REF-889` (uncalibrated cell or too few points), `-895` (a design never built), `-897` (a retired
     calibration) and `-814` (pooled data);
   - not readable: `{ kind: "unread", code, message }` for `FROB-REF-720` (the token lacks `ground:read`) and
     `-724` (the verb is not in this build).
   An envelope whose schema name it does not know, or a band envelope missing `n`, `level` or the unit, is refused
   by the conversion (`viewer/src/snapshot.js`) with the field named, as for every other envelope. The page performs no arithmetic on the
   block: it does not form ŷ ± q̂ itself.
4. **What the overlay shows** (the drawing is N32b-12's; these are the rules for real data).
   - A band is a statement about one scalar metric of an episode. The strip therefore shows the metric's axis
     with the run's value as a marker and the interval as a bar from `lower` to `upper` (the scalar form of
     N32b-12's element list, which that bead admits beside the trace with two curves; for a metric that is one
     number per episode the bar is the faithful form, and it is drawn at the tick where the metric is attained
     when the run records one). The legend carries `n`, the level and the verb's coverage sentence.
   - `status = "synthetic"`: the legend reads `reality: synthetic [dataset=<prefix>]` and the bar is labelled
     "synthetic rehearsal, not a measurement". The word `calibrated` is printed only when the envelope says it.
     (Data of origin `synthetic` never yields another status; the page repeats the status, it does not judge it.)
   - `kind = "no-claim"`: no bar; the legend reads "no claim" with the code and the observed and required values
     (for example "8 calibration points, 19 needed") and the first repair.
   - `kind = "unread"`: no bar; the legend reads "band not read" with the code, and the header's own
     `reality:` line, which every run carries, is shown unchanged.
5. **Recorded fixtures.** `viewer/build/record-snapshot.mjs` gains `--band <metric>:<level>`: it issues
   `frob ground band --for <run> --metric <m> --level <l> --json` and stores the raw envelope (a band or a
   refusal) with the others. The test-only `FixtureSource` passes it through the same conversion, so the path is
   tested with networking off and under the fail-closed default of live mode.
6. **Scopes.** The live page's token needs `ground:read` beside the record's read scopes. Nothing else changes in
   the token handling of N32bp1-01.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The live source's tool list is the five names above, each of kind `read` in the registry, and no request it
      can build contains the property `action`.
- [ ] On a cell of the synthetic rehearsal with 20 scored pairs at level 0.90, the page's probe of the overlay
      returns the same `qHat`, unit, `n` = 20, `level` = 0.90, `index` = 19, `lower` and `upper` as
      `frob ground band --for <run> --metric <m> --level 0.90 --json`, to the last digit, and the legend reads
      `reality: synthetic [dataset=…]`; the string `calibrated` appears nowhere in the page.
- [ ] The short cell (8 pairs) shows "no claim" with `FROB-REF-889`, 8 and 19, and zero band elements; the design
      with no unit shows "no claim" with `FROB-REF-895`.
- [ ] With a token lacking `ground:read` the overlay shows "band not read" with `FROB-REF-720` and the header's
      `reality:` line; no band element is drawn.
- [ ] The store's digest is equal before and after the page's session (the page reads only).
- [ ] The recorded fixture made with `--band` gives the same snapshot block as the live source for the same run.
- [ ] A still of the run with `--overlay reality-band --band <result>` probes to the same `value`, `lower`,
      `upper`, n, level and status as the verb's envelope, and the short cell's still reads "no claim".
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/viewer_reality_band.sh` passes with the logging below, and exits non-zero under each of its
      three control builds.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`viewer/tests/reality_band.test.mjs` (the pinned headless browser of N32b-44; envelopes recorded from the real
verb with `record-snapshot.mjs --band`, on a temporary state root with the rehearsal ingested; no mocks; a
hand-built envelope is labelled `synthetic` and is used only where the mechanism cannot produce the case):
- `tool_list_has_ground_band_and_is_read_only`: the constant equals the five names; each is `read` in the registry.
- `band_request_has_no_action`: for every argument combination the request object's keys are a subset of
  `for`, `metric`, `level`, `sided`, `list`.
- `band_block_copies_envelope`: an envelope with ŷ = 4.20 s, q̂ = 0.19 s, n = 20, level 0.90, index 19 gives a
  block with `lower` = 4.01 s and `upper` = 4.39 s exactly as the envelope states them; changing `qHat` in the
  envelope without changing `lower` and `upper` leaves the drawn bar unchanged (the page does no arithmetic).
- `lower_sided_has_no_upper`.
- `refusal_889_is_no_claim_with_counts` (8 and 19); `refusal_895_is_no_claim`; `refusal_897_names_the_reason`
  (recorded once BD-394 has landed; until then a `synthetic` envelope that validates against the refusal schema);
  `refusal_720_is_unread_and_header_line_stays`; `refusal_724_is_unread`.
- `synthetic_status_never_prints_calibrated`.
- `conversion_refuses_band_without_unit`: the conversion names the missing field.
- `block_is_frozen`: a write to the block throws in strict mode.
- `scalar_metric_is_marker_and_bar`: the element list holds one marker and one bar, at the tick the metric names.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/viewer_reality_band.sh [--seed S]` (header `bead=N32bp2-44 milestone=M5 labels=tether,ground`;
sources the helper of `STD-E2E`; default seed 5). Steps:
1. `setup`: a temporary state root, whose `safety.toml` the script writes with one `principal.<id>` table
   granting the scopes of the sessions below and a small CPU budget (a root token is minted only from a
   principal's grant, N32b-01); `frobd --mcp` on loopback; two root sessions of that principal, each opened
   with no token in the environment. The working session, under which steps 2 to 4, 9 and 9b run, holds what
   their verbs require: `frob session open --scopes
   ground:import,ground:write,ground:run,ground:read,sim:run,run:read,telemetry:read,artifact:read --key vb-0
   --json` (`ground.ingest` needs `ground:import`, `ground.predict` `ground:write`, `ground.score` `ground:run`,
   `sim.run` `sim:run`). The page's session is read-only and its token is the only one the page is given:
   `frob session open --scopes run:read,telemetry:read,artifact:read,ground:read --key vb-1 --json`. Then
   `node viewer/build/pack.mjs --variant live`.
2. `rehearsal`: generate the band fixtures of the synthetic rehearsal (R-08 and R-09 of BD-388) with the
   generator's entry point; for one cell of R-08: `frob ground ingest`, `frob ground predict` and
   `frob ground score` for its 20 trials; the same for the 8-pair cell of R-09 (every call of a `run` or
   `mutate` verb here carries its own key, `vb-<verb>-<cell>-<trial>`, so no trial is answered from another's
   receipt).
3. `run`: `frob sim run` of the cell's design on its task (`--seed S --key vb-run-1`), the run the page will show.
4. `band-cli`: `frob ground band --for <run> --metric <m> --level 0.90 --json`: assert exit 0 and keep the envelope.
5. `band-page`: in both pinned browsers, attach the live page to the run, turn the overlay on for the metric at
   0.90, and read the overlay's probe through `window.__frobViewerTest`: assert every field equal to step 4's
   envelope, the legend text, and the absence of the word `calibrated`.
6. `no-claim`: the 8-pair cell and the unbuilt design of R-09: assert the legend, the code and zero band elements.
7. `scope`: a page session without `ground:read` (`frob session open --scopes
   run:read,telemetry:read,artifact:read --key vb-2 --json`): assert "band not read", `FROB-REF-720`, the header
   line.
8. `read-only`: the daemon's request log for the page's session holds only the five tools and no `action`
   property; the store digest before step 5 equals the digest after step 7.
9. `fixture`: `record-snapshot.mjs --band <m>:0.90` on the same run, loaded with networking off: the probe dump
   equals step 5's.
9b. `still`: the same band on a still, the first run of the stills overlay on a band the real verb issued
   (N24ap2-60 built it at the third milestone on a synthetic envelope): `frob telemetry stills <run> --world
   <w> --overlay reality-band --band <the result handle of step 4> --json`, then `frob telemetry inspect-pixel
   <still_id> --pixel <a pixel of the bar> --overlay reality-band --json`: assert `value`, `lower`, `upper`,
   unit, n, level and the status `synthetic` equal step 4's envelope, and that the still's caption does not
   contain `calibrated`; the 8-pair cell's still reads "no claim" with `FROB-REF-889` and has no bar.
10. `controls`: three builds on which the script must exit non-zero: a page that draws a bar of ±10% of the value
    when the block is `no-claim`; a page that prints `calibrated` for status `synthetic`; a live source whose band
    request carries `action: "retire"` (the server refuses it for the missing capability and step 8 fails).
11. `determinism`: steps 5 and 6 twice: identical probe dumps.
Under the fail-closed default of live mode (N32bp1-01), steps 5 to 8 are logged `not run` with the reason and
step 9 carries the assertions of steps 5 and 6.
Log `target/e2e-logs/viewer_reality_band/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it: per command a `cmd` line (the
exact command with the token redacted to its `tk:` identifier, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence
block: claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed, and the `reality` text); per probe a `metric` line with
the page value, the verb value, the unit and the threshold (equal); per refusal a `refusal` line with code,
observed, required and first repair; `assert` lines for the legend texts and the digests; on failure the first
differing field with the paths of both envelopes and the page dump; a final `summary` with assertions passed and
failed, steps `not run` and gates touched (none). No residual or sample value of any dataset enters the log.

### Known answers and negative controls

The index and half-width of the rehearsal's band (n = 20, level 0.90: index ⌈21·0.9⌉ = 19, the 19th smallest
residual) are the calibration subsystem's known answer KA-728, read here and not recomputed. Negative controls:
the three builds of step 10, registered with `STD-TEST`. Counterexample CX-334 (recovering planted parameters from
a simulator is not a Reality result) is what the `synthetic` legend rule protects. Determinism: the block is a
copy; two loads give byte-identical probe dumps in both browsers.

## Considerations

- The first real band is expected to be a `synthetic` one for a long time: no measured dataset exists, and none
  may ever be admitted. The page's wording for that state matters more than its wording for `calibrated`.
- If the kill gate on bands fires (bands withdrawn; reports print `reality: no-claim` with the raw residual table),
  `ground_band` answers with refusals only and the overlay shows "no claim": nothing in this bead changes.
- A drift monitor may retire a calibration between two polls of a running page. The block of the older snapshot is
  frozen and stays as it was read; the next request returns `FROB-REF-897`, and the newer snapshot says "no claim"
  with the retirement reason. The page never extrapolates a band across snapshots.
- Privacy: the envelope carries a cell identifier (a hash) and a dataset hash prefix, never unit serials or
  samples; a private dataset's band needs the private read scope like any other read of it.
- Open point for the packer (N32b-40): whether a replay packet should carry the band envelope of its run. Default
  until decided: it does not, and a packet page shows the header's `reality:` line only.
