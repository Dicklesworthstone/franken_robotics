## Background

Atelier is the design-studio subsystem of FrankenRobotics. Its static analyses answer questions about a design
without simulating it, each returning an evidence class, the model identity and, when it finds a problem,
ranked repairs as typed edits. Two of them are kinematic:

- **A-05 Workspace**: can the feet or the hand reach what the tasks require (a step height, a shelf height)?
- **A-06 Singularities**: where are the singular configurations, and how close does a nominal gait come?

Both rest on construction C-11, *kinematics is algebraic geometry*: forward kinematics is a product of
exponentials, a polynomial map in (cᵢ, sᵢ) with cᵢ² + sᵢ² = 1, so inverse-kinematics solution sets, workspace
boundaries and singular loci (det J = 0) are real algebraic varieties. What this replaces is numerical inverse
kinematics with no statement of how many solutions exist. The Solid part of the payoff is this bead:
closed-form inverse kinematics for three-joint legs, derived once by elimination and emitted as a kernel, and
singular loci for leg Jacobians in closed form, giving the distance to singularity along a nominal gait.
Certified inverse kinematics by interval methods is Frontier and is not here; general six-joint inverse
kinematics is not promised (FACT: FrankenSymPy has Buchberger with a cap of 1,024 basis polynomials, univariate
resultants only, and a real solver capped at 8 variables and zero-dimensional ideals).

The derived kernel is produced by the build-time annex `derive/frob-derive` with FrankenSymPy, through the
sparse-Jacobian route admitted by gate INT-FSYM-02 (first recorded at M0 on a planar two-link arm; an admission
is never inherited, so this bead re-runs it at its own pin on the leg). The kernel serves the legged baseline
controller (swing by Cartesian position control through the leg inverse-kinematics kernel) as well as the
analyses.

Seed done-condition: a 3R leg has four solutions and the singular locus matches the closed form; INT-FSYM-02.

## Scope

In: the three-joint leg fixture and its closed forms; the derived kernels `K-ik-leg3r` and `K-jac-leg3r`
(emitted into `frob-kernels`); the
analyses A-05 and A-06 in `frob-analyze` for legs of this class; the verbs `analyze.workspace` and
`analyze.singularities`; the INT-FSYM-02 record on the leg; the end-to-end script.
Out: arms and chains outside the three-joint leg class (they report `unsupported` with the reason; numerical
inverse kinematics with multistart, labelled incomplete, is the later fallback); certified inverse kinematics
(Frontier); stage wiring in `design.check` (BD-110, BD-244); the full-range sweep over a scene (stage 7).

## Technical approach

**The leg class.** Hip abduction about the body x axis (q₁), hip pitch (q₂) and knee pitch (q₃) about parallel
axes, thigh length a, shank length b, lateral hip offset d. Sagittal coordinates of the foot:
x_s = a·sin q₂ + b·sin(q₂ + q₃), z_s = −(a·cos q₂ + b·cos(q₂ + q₃)). Foot position in the hip frame:

  p = (x_s, d·cos q₁ − z_s·sin q₁, d·sin q₁ + z_s·cos q₁).

**Inverse kinematics in closed form, with unit complex outputs (no trigonometric function in the kernel).**
Given p = (p_x, p_y, p_z) and two branch signs σ_abd, σ_knee ∈ {+1, −1}:
1. r₁ = p_y² + p_z² − d². If r₁ < 0 the point is unreachable (inside the offset cylinder). z_s = σ_abd·√r₁.
2. c₁ = (d·p_y + z_s·p_z)/(p_y² + p_z²), s₁ = (d·p_z − z_s·p_y)/(p_y² + p_z²).
3. ρ² = p_x² + r₁. c₃ = (ρ² − a² − b²)/(2·a·b). r₃ = 1 − c₃². If r₃ < 0 the point is out of reach (too far or
   too close). s₃ = σ_knee·√r₃.
4. k₁ = a + b·c₃, k₂ = b·s₃, u = −z_s. c₂ = (k₁·u + k₂·p_x)/ρ², s₂ = (k₁·p_x − k₂·u)/ρ².
The four sign choices give up to four solutions. Outputs: (cᵢ, sᵢ) for i = 1..3, the radicands r₁ and r₃ (so a
caller sees its margin to the workspace boundary), and a validity mask. An invalid lane is produced by a
select, never by clamping a radicand to zero, and is never reported as a solution.

**Jacobian and singular locus in closed form.** With columns ∂p/∂qᵢ:
det J = a·b·sin q₃·z_s, where z_s = −(a·cos q₂ + b·cos(q₂ + q₃)). Derivation: ∂p/∂q₁ = R_x(q₁)·(0, −z_s, d);
the other two columns are R_x(q₁) applied to vectors in the x–z plane, whose cross product is along y with
magnitude a·b·sin q₃; the triple product gives the result, independent of d. The singular locus is the union
of two surfaces: **sin q₃ = 0** (knee straight or folded: the workspace boundary in reach) and **z_s = 0** (the
foot in the plane through the abduction axis; with d = 0 it is on the axis). On the first, the two knee
branches merge (r₃ = 0); on the second, the two abduction branches merge (r₁ = 0).

**Derived kernels (through `derive/frob-derive` and `frob-kir`).** `K-ik-leg3r`: steps 1 to 4 as a
straight-line program with selects, two square roots, inputs (p, a, b, d, σ_abd, σ_knee), dimension-checked
(lengths in metres; cᵢ, sᵢ dimensionless). `K-jac-leg3r`: the nine entries of J and det J from (cᵢ, sᵢ), by
the sparse-Jacobian route. Both are emitted as Rust (and dialect text for later device use) into the kernel
library `frob-kernels` (layer 3), with digests, and regenerate bit for bit. They live there, not in
`frob-analyze`, because the legged baseline controller consumes them: `frob-control` (N22a-10: swing inverse
kinematics and the footstep clamp) calls them in `frob-kernels` and may not depend on `frob-analyze`, which
ranks after it in layer 4 (that edge would point upward, rule L-1). A-05 and A-06 below are the design-check
views of the same kernels. Identities checked by FrankenSymPy at build time: det J − a·b·s₃·z_s ≡ 0 modulo the
circle relations; and forward kinematics composed with the inverse returns p (modulo t₁² = r₁, t₃² = r₃ for the
square-root witnesses).

**A-05 Workspace (`frob_analyze::a05`, verb `analyze.workspace`).** Input: a compiled model, a leg (or `all`),
and targets (points in the body frame, or task-level heights such as a step height at a stance width).
Algorithm per target: transform to the hip frame; run `K-ik-leg3r` for the four branches; discard branches
outside the joint ranges of the design (compared in exact unit-complex form against the range's end points);
report `reachable` with the surviving branches, or `unreachable` with the binding reason (inside the offset
cylinder, beyond full extension, inside the folded radius, or all branches outside joint ranges, naming the
joint). Margins reported in metres: extension margin (a + b) − ρ, fold margin ρ − |a − b|, and cylinder margin
√(p_y² + p_z²) − |d|. The result is typed `exact` for this leg class (a bound says which side it is on), with
evidence class `Tested` and determinism class D0, under one guard: the radicands r₁ and r₃ and the joint-range
comparisons are evaluated in floating point, so each is computed with a forward error bound (DESIGN: the
kernel's rounding-error constant, derived with the kernel and stated in `CONTRACT.md`, times the sum of the
magnitudes of the terms), and a target whose deciding quantity lies within its bound of zero is reported
`unresolved` with its margins, never `reachable` or `unreachable`, so `exact` is never claimed on a verdict that
rounding decided. On `unreachable`, ranked repairs as typed edits: lengthen the shank or thigh by the shortfall;
widen the named joint range; move the hip. Both analyses implement the analysis contract (N28a-04) and their
verbs are generated from their registrations by N28a-05.

**A-06 Singularities (`frob_analyze::a06`, verb `analyze.singularities`).** Input: a compiled model, a leg, and
either a pose or a trajectory of joint coordinates (the reference pose, or a nominal gait's swing and stance
trajectory). Output per sample: det J, its two factors a·b·sin q₃ and z_s, and the smallest singular value of
J in metres per radian; over a trajectory: the minimum of each with the sample where it occurs, and which
factor is responsible. A sample is `singular` when the smallest singular value is below a threshold given in
the request (default 1% of a + b per radian, DESIGN). Repairs: bend the nominal knee angle; change the stance
height; change the hip offset.

**Analyses as memo nodes.** Key: the model identity restricted to the fields read (the leg's lengths, offset,
joint ranges and frames), the analysis version and the parameters. An edit outside those fields recomputes
nothing.

**INT-FSYM-02 on the leg.** The admission record at this bead's pin: the leg Jacobian compiles through the
sparse-Jacobian route and agrees with the closed form at a regular and at two singular configurations (one on
each surface).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The 3R leg fixture has exactly four inverse-kinematics solutions at the regular target below, each reproducing the target to 10⁻¹² m, and the solution set equals the closed form's (seed done-condition; KA-074).
- [ ] The singular locus matches the closed form (seed done-condition): det J from `K-jac-leg3r` equals a·b·sin q₃·z_s to 10⁻¹² relative at 10³ seeded configurations, is zero on both singular surfaces, and the number of real solutions drops from four to two exactly on each surface.
- [ ] INT-FSYM-02 is recorded at this pin on the leg Jacobian, at one regular and two singular configurations.
- [ ] An out-of-reach target yields no solution lanes and the verdict `unreachable` with the binding reason and a repair; no radicand is clamped.
- [ ] `frob analyze workspace --json` and `frob analyze singularities --json` return envelopes with the claim product (class `Tested`, tier `static`, determinism `exact`), the model identity and the payload schemas the verb registry rows declare (`frob.analyze.workspace.out/1` and `frob.analyze.singularities.out/1`, VERBS-analyze), each validated against its schema file; exit code 0, or 2 with findings.
- [ ] Both kernels regenerate bit for bit and pass the dimension checker; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-analyze` documents A-05 and A-06 with their validity domain (the three-joint leg class) and known gaps.
- [ ] A design whose leg is outside the class returns `unsupported` with the reason, not a wrong answer.
- [ ] Cancellation is tested: a trajectory analysis over 10⁶ samples observes a cancel request between sample blocks and returns a refusal-free cancelled outcome with no partial result published as a result.

- `leg3r_det_closed_form`: at q = (0.2, 0.4, −0.9): z_s = −0.44966088897331447 m, det J = 0.0625 × sin(−0.9) × z_s = 0.022014467158738 m³; the four solutions have det J = ±0.022014467158738.
- `leg3r_knee_singularity`: q₃ = 0 → det J = 0; target at ρ = a + b = 0.5 m has r₃ = 0 and the two knee branches coincide.
- `leg3r_abduction_singularity`: target with p_y² + p_z² = d² → r₁ = 0, z_s = 0, det J = 0, two abduction branches coincide.
- `leg3r_unreachable_far`: |p| = 0.6 m → r₃ < 0, no valid lane, verdict `unreachable: beyond full extension`, shortfall ρ − (a + b) = √(0.6² − 0.08²) − 0.5 ≈ 0.0946427 m reported (ρ² = |p|² − d² = 0.3536 m², the negated extension margin; the repair lengthens the shank or thigh by this amount).
- `leg3r_unreachable_inside_cylinder`: p_y² + p_z² < d² → `unreachable: inside the offset cylinder`.
- `leg3r_unit_complex_outputs`: cᵢ² + sᵢ² = 1 to 4 units in the last place on 10⁴ seeded reachable targets.
- `leg3r_roundtrip_property`: for 10⁴ seeded joint triples away from the singular surfaces, forward then inverse returns the original triple among the four solutions.
- `a05_joint_range_filter`: with q₃ restricted to [−2.5, −0.1] rad only the two solutions with q₃ = −0.9 survive; with q₁ also restricted to [−0.8, 0.8] one survives.
- `a06_gait_distance`: along a synthetic swing trajectory that straightens the knee to q₃ = −0.02 rad, the minimum is reported at that sample with the factor `knee` named.
- `a05_a06_memo_key_live_dependency`: perturbing a thigh length changes the key; perturbing an unrelated arm link does not.
- `outside_class_unsupported`: a four-joint leg returns `unsupported`.
- `a05_boundary_unresolved`: a target placed at full extension (ρ = a + b = 0.5 m in floating point) and one on
  the offset cylinder (p_y² + p_z² = d²) are reported `unresolved` with margins of zero to within the bound,
  never `exact`; moving each target 10⁻⁶ m into the workspace (toward the hip for the first, away from the
  abduction axis for the second) gives `reachable`, and 10⁻⁶ m out of it gives `unreachable`.

`scripts/e2e/analyze_workspace_singularities.sh`: on a committed synthetic quadruped whose legs are the 3R
fixture above (`crates/frob-analyze/fixtures/a05/quad-3r.fbot`, labelled `synthetic`, shaped like the

shank until the step is unreachable (expects findings and a repair whose application restores `reachable`),
and regenerates the two kernels with `xtask check-generated`. Logging per `STD-E2E`: every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block
(claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); `assert` lines for solution counts, margins in metres and
det J in m³ against their expected values and tolerances; digests of regenerated kernels with the first
differing byte on failure; a final `summary` with assertion counts and the gate INT-FSYM-02.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-074: a spatial 3R positioning chain has up to four inverse-kinematics solutions, and the solver finds exactly the solutions the closed form gives; control: a missed or a spurious solution against the closed form.
- Closed forms: det J = a·b·sin q₃·z_s; the two singular surfaces; the numbers of the fixture above.
- Negative controls: (1) a kernel that returns only the knee-down branch (planted) must fail `leg3r_four_solutions`; (2) a kernel that clamps r₃ to zero for an unreachable target (planted) reports a spurious solution at full extension and must fail `leg3r_unreachable_far`; (3) a determinant with the factor z_s dropped (planted) must fail the abduction-singularity test; (4) the sign-flipped Jacobian entry of the INT-FSYM-02 control.
- Metamorphic relations: mirroring the leg (d → −d, p_y → −p_y) mirrors the solution set (q₁ → −q₁); scaling a, b, d and p by k leaves the angles unchanged and scales det J by k³.
- Determinism lanes: analysis results are bit-identical twice, across worker counts, in debug and release and on both hosts (class D0 claims are checked as such).

## Considerations

- The analyses are exact only for the leg class above; that is their stated validity domain. Legs with a knee offset or non-parallel pitch axes need another derivation and report `unsupported` until one exists.
- Near a singular surface the inverse map is ill-conditioned: a foot-position error of ε maps to joint error of order ε divided by the smallest singular value. A-06 reports that value so the controller's contract can refuse a target inside the threshold.
- The reachability predicates r₁ ≥ 0 and r₃ ≥ 0 are evaluated in floating point; a target within rounding of the boundary could be classified either way, which is why A-05 reports it `unresolved` (the guard above). The kernel returns the radicands so that a caller needing a strict decision can use an exact or guarded predicate.
- Performance: both analyses are a few hundred operations per target; they sit well inside the two-second budget of `design.check` for a template-sized design (TARGET).
- If the derivation toolchain is killed at M2 (KILL-KIR), the same closed forms are hand-written in Rust and the dialect and bound by these same tests.
