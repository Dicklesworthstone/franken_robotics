## Background

Lemma is the proof subsystem of FrankenRobotics. Most of what it certifies goes the **algebraic route**:
polynomial and rational identities checked by FrankenSymPy at build time and replayed by the project's own
standard-library auditor. Lean enters as a **second opinion, not a prover the project drives**: a small number
of general statements (theorem cards) are written in stock Lean 4 with Mathlib as a development-time tool, and
**FrankenLean**, the owner's independent Lean kernel and checker, re-checks the compiled result and seals it
into a capsule. Neither tool is in any production dependency graph; build outputs are not committed.

FrankenLean is foundation row `FD-FLN`: posture Subprocess, confined to tooling under `lean/`, admission gates
INT-FLN-01 and INT-FLN-02, fallback "stock Lean alone, recorded as a single-kernel check". FACT (from the
audit of `franken_lean` at `4426f8ea7`, read 2026-11-21; re-read at the pin): its command-line tool `fln`
offers `check-olean`, `seal-capsule` and `verify-capsule --require-semantic`; it re-checks compiled `.olean`
files with an independent kernel (Lean standard library 2,433 of 2,433 modules; Mathlib 9,131 of 11,083
accepted, 0 failed); it does not elaborate Lean source with Mathlib tactics (the elaboration monads are not
wired); accepted Mathlib modules a robotics proof would want include `Algebra.Lie.Basic`,
`Algebra.Quaternion`, permutation groups and `Order.FixedPoints`, while `Geometry.Manifold.Algebra.LieGroup`,
`Analysis.Normed.Algebra.MatrixExponential`, `Analysis.Quaternion` and all of representation theory are
blocked; its pins are governed by its own `SUITE.lock`, with Lean v4.32.0; its suite lock certifies only
`x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`, and the project's Mac has no Lean 4.32.0 toolchain installed, so **Lean work runs
on a Linux host**. A full Mathlib re-check peaked at 92.8 GiB; a single theorem's closure is far smaller, and
its measured size is recorded before any claim (open question OPEN-LEMMA-1, risk RISK-16).

The two gates:
- **INT-FLN-01**: `fln` builds on a Linux host, re-checks and seals one trivial theorem from `lean/`, and the
  capsule checks from an empty environment.
- **INT-FLN-02**: the checker admits a true port identity and rejects its sign-mutated twin; axioms and
  coverage are listed. (Source anchors: `fln-checker/src/admit.rs`, 10,040 lines, and `policy.rs`;
  `fln-kernel/src/lib.rs`; what is reused is an isolated checker for a small formal profile.)

The "port identity" is theorem card T-15, the rational gear law: from f₂ = r·f₁ and e₁ = −r·e₂ it follows that
e₁·f₁ + e₂·f₂ = 0 (an ideal gear exchanges effort and flow without creating power). Its sign-mutated twin
replaces e₁ = −r·e₂ by e₁ = +r·e₂, which gives e₁·f₁ + e₂·f₂ = 2·r·e₂·f₁, not zero. A checker that admits both
is checking nothing.

Tier: Frontier. The whole Lean lane is off every critical path (M1 to M4 and the tournament do not need it).

## Scope

In: the Lake project skeleton under `lean/` pinned to FrankenLean's Lean and Mathlib pins; the build of `fln`
on a Linux host; the trivial theorem; the port identity and its twins; sealing, verification from an empty
environment; the axiom and coverage report; the closure-size measurement; two admission records.
Out: theorem cards as sealed capsules in certificate bundles (BD-381); the reflection pilot (BD-382);
certificate coverage for derived kernels (BD-379); any change to FrankenLean (upstream work in its repository).

## Technical approach

**Layout.** `lean/lakefile.toml` (or the Lake file form the pinned Lean uses), `lean/lean-toolchain` naming the
Lean version of FrankenLean's `SUITE.lock`, `lean/lake-manifest.json` pinning Mathlib to the commit of the same
lock, one Lake library `Frob` with its sources under `lean/Frob/`: this bead's admission theorems in
`lean/Frob/Admission/`, the registered statements later in `lean/Frob/Statements/` (N12-24) and the proofs in
`lean/Frob/Cards/` (BD-381). DESIGN: one library root, so that the cards, the statements they are bound to and
these admission files share one build configuration and one set of pins. `lean/` is not Rust and keeps its own
toolchain; `.lake/` build outputs are ignored by version control, and capsules are written under
`target/lean/` (never committed). The axiom whitelist `lean/axioms.whitelist` is created here with its initial
content `propext`, `Quot.sound`, `Classical.choice` (DESIGN: the three axioms of ordinary Mathlib proofs;
`Lean.ofReduceBool` and `Lean.trustCompiler` are deliberately absent), and BD-381 seals the cards against the
same file. A script `lean/tools/pins.sh` prints the three pins (Lean, Mathlib, FrankenLean revision) and fails
if they disagree with `SUITE.lock` at the FrankenLean pin of `franken-stack.lock`.

**Host.** An x86-64 Linux host (the project's Linux machine, or a build worker of that triple). Every run
states its memory and time and stays inside the machine envelope (16 GiB, 8 cores, one hour) unless the owner
has approved more in writing; the closure measurement of step 4 decides whether that is ever needed.

**Procedure for INT-FLN-01.**
1. Build `fln` from the FrankenLean checkout at its pin, on the Linux host, through the build-offload shim;
   record the binary's hash.
2. Write `lean/Frob/Admission/Trivial.lean`: `theorem frob_trivial : (2 : Nat) + 2 = 4 := rfl` (no Mathlib
   import, so its closure is the Lean core only). Compile with stock Lean (`lake build Frob.Admission.Trivial`).
3. `fln check-olean` on the compiled module; then `fln seal-capsule` to produce
   `target/lean/admission/trivial.flncap`.
4. Record wall time and peak memory of the re-check, and the number of declarations and modules in the closure.
5. **Empty environment.** Copy only the `fln` binary and the capsule into a fresh directory inside a clean
   container or a new user account with no Lean toolchain, no Mathlib cache and networking disabled; run `fln
   verify-capsule --require-semantic trivial.flncap`. It must accept.
6. Negative control: flip one byte in the capsule's proof payload; verification must reject.

**Procedure for INT-FLN-02.**
1. `lean/Frob/Admission/Port.lean` (statement fixes intent; the proof is written and compiled in stock Lean with
   Mathlib):

```lean
/-- Theorem card T-15, rational gear law: with f₂ = r·f₁ and e₁ = −r·e₂ the port powers sum to zero. -/
theorem gear_power_balance {K : Type} [CommRing K] (r e₂ f₁ : K) :
    (-(r * e₂)) * f₁ + e₂ * (r * f₁) = 0 := by ring
```

2. Compile, re-check with `fln check-olean`, seal, and verify from the empty environment as above. Expected:
   admitted.
3. The sign-mutated twin, in three forms, each of which must be **rejected**, with the reason recorded:
   a. *Statement mutated, proof by `sorry`*: `(r * e₂) * f₁ + e₂ * (r * f₁) = 0 := by sorry`. Rejected by policy
      (a sealed capsule has no `sorry`).
   b. *Statement mutated, asserted by a new axiom*. Rejected by policy (no axiom outside the pinned
      whitelist).
   c. *Tampered binary*: the compiled true theorem with its stated type edited to the mutated statement while
      the proof term is kept. Rejected by the kernel re-check (the proof term does not have the stated type).
   A fourth form, `native_decide` on a decidable instance over a finite ring, is also rejected by policy.
4. **Axioms and coverage.** From the checker's report for the true identity, record: the axioms the proof
   depends on (each compared with the pinned whitelist), the modules and declarations in the closure, how many
   were re-checked, and whether any module of the closure is in FrankenLean's blocked set (expected: none, since
   the statement needs only commutative-ring algebra).
5. Closure cost for OPEN-LEMMA-1: wall time and peak memory of the re-check of this one theorem's closure.

**Capsule invariant (INV-LEMMA-06).** A sealed capsule has no `sorry`, no axiom outside the whitelist and no
`native_decide`, and it proves the statement it names, not a weakened one: the theorem's constants are mapped
to the exact symbolic form of the artifact it is about, and the hashes of translation, environment, kernel
binary, definitions and proof are kept in the capsule. The check is `fln verify-capsule --require-semantic`.

**Admission records.** One per gate, with the three pins, the `fln` binary hash, the commands and outputs, the
measured time and memory, the axiom list, the coverage counts and each control's outcome. `covers.tool` names
`fln` (a Subprocess foundation has no crate in the lockfile; the record pins the tool's repository revision
and binary hash instead). Both are written through N33a-07's writer. The script never writes the gate register:
each gate's row in `registries/gates.toml` is entered from the receipt of the gate's tier run
(`FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test <tier> --features gauntlet-mutations -- INT-FLN-0n`,
`<tier>` being the test target the gate record's `tier` field names; the tier test reads the script's results,
so the twins, the `native_decide` form and the byte-flipped capsule appear in that run as the gate's registered
controls) with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate INT-FLN-0n
--receipt <hash>`, by this bead, the one that measures both gates (one recorder per gate). The same receipt's
hash is the record's `harness_receipt`, so the record and the row cite one run.

Evidence class: a capsule that verifies supports class `Proved` for its statement, with the trusted base
stated (the FrankenLean kernel, plus stock Lean as the producing compiler). Determinism: verification is a
pure function of the capsule and the binary.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] INT-FLN-01: `fln` builds on a Linux host; the trivial theorem is re-checked and sealed; the capsule verifies from an empty environment with networking disabled; the byte-flipped capsule is rejected.
- [ ] INT-FLN-02: the true port identity is admitted and its sign-mutated twin is rejected (seed done-condition) in all three forms, each with its recorded reason (policy: `sorry`; policy: axiom; kernel: type mismatch); the fourth form, the mutated statement proved by `native_decide` over a finite ring, is rejected with the reason policy: `native_decide`.
- [ ] The axiom list and coverage of the true identity are recorded; no axiom is outside the whitelist; no blocked module is in the closure.
- [ ] Time and peak memory of re-checking each theorem's closure are recorded (the measurement OPEN-LEMMA-1 asks for), with the host named.
- [ ] `lean/` builds with stock Lean at the pinned version; its pins equal FrankenLean's `SUITE.lock` at the stack-lock pin; no build output is committed.
- [ ] Neither Lean nor `fln` appears in any Cargo manifest of this repository (`check-deps`).
- [ ] Two admission records exist and validate, each citing the tool revision and binary hash and the harness receipt of its gate's tier run; each gate's row was entered with `record_gate` from that receipt, never by the script.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
There is no Rust unit under test; the checks are Lean files under `lean/Frob/Admission/` (the twins under
`lean/tests/admission/`, outside the library so that `lake build Frob` never compiles them) and tool
invocations, run by the script:
- `Trivial.lean` compiles and verifies.
- `Port.lean` compiles and verifies; `PortTwinSorry.lean` and `PortTwinAxiom.lean` compile under stock Lean (they are legal Lean) and are rejected at sealing or verification; `port_twin_tampered.olean` is rejected by `fln check-olean`.
- `whitelist_initial_content`: `lean/axioms.whitelist` holds exactly `propext`, `Quot.sound`, `Classical.choice`.
- `PortTwinNativeDecide.lean`: the mutated statement specialised to `ZMod 2`, where it holds (the left side is 2·r·e₂·f₁), stated as `∀ r e₂ f₁ : ZMod 2, (r * e₂) * f₁ + e₂ * (r * f₁) = 0 := by native_decide`; it compiles under stock Lean and is rejected at sealing with the reason policy: `native_decide`, so a weakened statement proved by evaluation cannot pass as the port identity.
- `pins_agree`: the pins script exits 0, and exits non-zero when one pin is edited.
- `capsule_tamper_rejected`.
- A Rust-side test in `frob-conformance` (`lean_capsule_records`) parses the two admission records and asserts the fields above are present, so that a missing measurement cannot pass review.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/lean_admission.sh`, run on the Linux host: builds `fln`, builds `lean/`, runs the two procedures
and the empty-environment verification, then runs each gate's tier on those results, writes the two admission
records citing the tier's receipt, and logs the receipt hash for `record_gate` (the script itself writes no gate
row). Logging per `STD-E2E` (NDJSON under
`target/e2e-logs/lean_admission/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it): every line with `ts`,
`script`, `bead` (`BD-380`), `step` (`INT-FLN-01/<stage>` or `INT-FLN-02/<form>`), `event`, the exact command,
exit code and wall time, the Lean, Mathlib and FrankenLean pins, and the evidence block (claim Proof, class,
checker identity: the `fln` binary hash); `metric` lines for peak memory (threshold 16 GiB) and time (threshold
one hour) of each re-check, declarations and modules in each closure, axioms found against the whitelist;
`assert` lines for admitted (2 expected: the trivial theorem and the port identity) and rejected (5 expected:
the three twins, the `native_decide` form and the byte-flipped capsule) with the reason text of each rejection;
the hashes of the `fln` binary and of each capsule; on a failure the `fln` diagnostic and the paths of the Lean
source and capsule needed to reproduce; a `summary` naming INT-FLN-01 and INT-FLN-02.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answer: e₁·f₁ + e₂·f₂ = (−r·e₂)·f₁ + e₂·(r·f₁) = 0 identically; the twin gives 2·r·e₂·f₁ (for r = 3, e₂ = 2, f₁ = 5: 60, not 0).
- Negative controls (registered for INT-FLN-02): the three twins; the `native_decide` form; (for INT-FLN-01) the byte-flipped capsule; a capsule sealed against a different Mathlib commit than the pin (must be reported as an environment mismatch).
- Determinism lanes: sealing the same compiled module twice yields the same capsule hash; verification gives the same verdict on a second Linux host.

## Considerations

- RISK-16 (Lean work needs a large-memory Linux host): the leading indicator is the closure size measured here. If one theorem's closure exceeds the envelope, later Lean beads need the owner's approval before they run, and specifications are restated in elementary form to shrink closures (the default of OPEN-LEMMA-2).
- Fallback if FrankenLean cannot be admitted: stock Lean alone, recorded as a single-kernel check; capsules are then not produced and the `lean-capsule` proof kind stays unused. KILL-LEMMA (decided at M5: certificate coverage below 50% after one repair cycle) and KILL-REFLECT are decided by later beads.
- FD-FLN-02 (P3) would let this run on the Mac; nothing waits on it.
- Mathlib is a development-time tool with a large supply chain; it never enters a shipped artifact, and a capsule is checked by `fln` alone.
- The `ring` proof pulls a Mathlib closure; if that closure touches a blocked module at the pin, the identity is restated over the integers with an explicit calculation, and the record says so.
