## Background

Habitat is the scene subsystem of FrankenRobotics. Its generated scenes answer "does this body work in places
like these"; **ingestion** answers "does it work in this place": a particular home, kitchen or plant, captured
by a walkthrough video or a drone and turned into a scene pack. Ingestion has no effect on decision time; it
lets a candidate be evaluated in one particular place, which is the precondition for any Reality claim about
that place.

Two capture pipelines exist in the owner's projects (FACT, read 2026-11-21). (1) A route from walkthrough
video to an agent-built Blender twin with stable feature identifiers and a GLB export, with an accepted plan
for a **twin package** that links visual, geometric and traversability representations
(`franken_surveillance_system/BLENDER_TWIN_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md`; foundation row `FD-FSS`, Pattern). (2)
`franken_drone_geometry_reconstruction` (foundation row `FD-FDGR`, Pattern and Subprocess, Frontier): its
status file (snapshot 2026-09-04) describes an evidence path from recorded-media custody to an audited
relative bundle problem, and says it does not yet optimize cameras, emit metres or publish geometry; its
crates hold about 27 thousand lines. The schemas for what it will publish exist:
`schemas/geometry_generation.schema.json` requires `coordinate_frame`, `scale_status`, `representations`,
`uncertainty_root`, `coverage_root` and `publication_stage`, and `schemas/scale_model.schema.json` records a
scale authority with residuals in parts per million. Therefore `scene.import` consumes the twin package first
and the drone route when that project publishes geometry.

The design rule that makes ingestion honest: an imported place arrives with **what is not known** attached. A
scale label says how far its metres can be trusted; a three-valued occupancy says where nothing was observed;
materials are never inferred from appearance; and where a decision cannot be settled, the output is a request
for a measurement, not an assumption. Counterexample to the shortcut "import the mesh and trust it": a
corridor whose far half was never observed appears as empty space in a naive import, and a traversal
certificate over it would be false; with the coverage mask the same query returns unknown.

Tier: Frontier. Pilot (bead N33a-25, a dependency): the schema fixtures and the refusal cases, which need no
geometry, plus the inspection of one existing twin package. Kill gate **KILL-INGEST**, decided at M5.

## Scope

In: the real-place lane of `scene.import`; four-point registration; the scale label; the unknown mask and its
treatment by each consumer; the scan-import schema adapter and INT-FDGR-01; assumed interface and optical
cards; `scene.fit` on an ingested place with registration uncertainty; probe requests emitted from it; privacy
inheritance; GATE-HAB-03; the record of KILL-INGEST.
Out: the collision compile and scene acceptance themselves (BD-163, BD-228); the clearance complex and
`scene.fit` on generated scenes (BD-233); the probe-request verb (BD-291); the private mark and `scene.purge`
(BD-305); asset-populated interiors (BD-399); any reconstruction (done by the sibling pipelines).

## Technical approach

**Verb `scene.import` (run class; real places at M5).** Input: a source (a twin package directory, or a
published geometry generation of the drone project, or a bare mesh or point cloud with an operator
declaration), an import profile with decode limits, landmark correspondences, and the private scope. Output: a
scene pack with a **loss report** (what the source had that the pack does not), a **scale label**, an **unknown
mask**, acceptance results, and the list of `assumed` cards. The pack is private, is stored outside the
repository, and everything derived from it inherits the mark.

**Steps.**
1. *Decode under limits.* Through the hardened readers behind the airlock (`MeshExchange`); a limit exceeded
   or an unresolved reference is `FROB-REF-303`; an oversize input is quarantined, not parsed.
2. *Registration: four points, because three accept a reflection.* An import whose format or pipeline admits
   a parity change or a general affine map requires four non-coplanar correspondences. With four, the affine
   map is determined; check that its linear part L satisfies LᵀL = c²·I within the registration tolerance and
   det L > 0, and that the scale c matches the scale witness. The tetrahedron of the four landmarks must have a
   volume of at least 1% (TARGET) of the cube of their largest separation. Failure is `FROB-REF-302`
   (transform class not established; repairs: supply a fourth non-coplanar landmark; declare the source
   handedness; fix winding). Smallest case: landmarks A = (0, 0, 0), B = (1, 0, 0), C = (0, 1, 0); the map
   (x, y, z) ↦ (x, y, −z) fixes all three, so a mirrored room registers perfectly on three points; a fourth
   landmark D = (0, 0, 1) maps to (0, 0, −1) and shows a residual of 2 m. A gravity witness does not replace
   the fourth point. The registration residual becomes the pack's registration uncertainty u_reg.
3. *Scale label.* One of four values ordered by authority, matching the reconstruction project's vocabulary:

| Label | Meaning | What a task may do with it |
|---|---|---|
| `relative-only` | Shape without a metric scale | Viewing only; every metric task is refused (`FROB-REF-304`) |
| `estimated` | Scale inferred from priors such as typical door heights | Exploratory runs; clearance verdicts are `unresolved` within the stated scale interval |
| `witnessed` | Scale fixed by an object of known size or a measured baseline in the capture | Metric tasks, with the witness's uncertainty in u_reg |
| `surveyed` | Scale and registration from an independent survey | Metric tasks at survey accuracy |

4. *Unknown mask.* Occupancy is three-valued: free, occupied, unknown. Coverage or confidence per region from
   the source becomes the mask; an unseen underside stays unknown; a hole closed by repair is marked and its
   neighbourhood stays unknown. Each consumer's treatment is fixed:

| Consumer | Treatment of `unknown` |
|---|---|
| Spawn and goal placement | Not allowed in or adjacent to unknown space |
| Clearance complex | Blocked for witnesses, free for cuts |
| Contact | Solid keep-out; a contact with an unknown surface marks the world `Degraded: contact-with-unknown` and the episode is excluded from metrics with a count |
| Ray kernels | A ray that enters unknown space returns `invalid: unknown-region` |
| Tasks | May require known traversability; a required route through unknown space is `FROB-REF-305` |

5. *Collision compile and acceptance* of the scene subsystem, on the registered geometry.
6. *Cards.* An unprobed surface gets an interface card of class `assumed` with a wide friction interval, which
   the adversarial subsystem searches. No friction, compliance or optical value is estimated from an image.
   Fixture articulation is added only where a hinge axis and limits are supplied.
7. *Write the pack* with the loss report, the label, the mask, u_reg, and the private mark.

**The scan-import adapter and INT-FDGR-01 (`frob_scene::import::fdgr`; a schema adapter with no crate
dependency on the sibling).** Input: a `geometry_generation` document and its `scale_model`. Mapping:
`coordinate_frame` → the pack's frame declaration; `scale_status` → the scale label (`relative_only`,
`estimated`, `witnessed`, `surveyed`); `representations` → the mesh or point-cloud sources; `uncertainty_root`
→ u_reg and per-region uncertainty; `coverage_root` → the unknown mask; `publication_stage` → must be a
published stage. The gate: (a) the schema fixtures validate; (b) a geometry generation whose scale status is
not metric is refused for a physics scene (`FROB-REF-304`), because a relative scale cannot enter a physics
scene; (c) coverage masks become the scene's unknown-space label, so a clearance certificate treats an
unscanned region as unknown and never as free; (d) the privacy inheritance applies to the result. Fallback
when the sibling has published nothing: mesh and point-cloud import through `fs-io` with the scale declared by
the operator and `reality: no-claim`.

**`scene.fit` on an ingested place.** The clearance complex subdivides the footprint's planar configuration
space into cells with centre q₀, translation edge Δ and heading width δ; a cell stores one-sided clearance
values c_lo = c(q₀) − e and c_hi = c(q₀) + e with

  e = (Δ/2)·√2 + ρ·(δ/2) + ε_field + u_reg,

where ρ is the footprint's reference radius, ε_field the scene's field bound and u_reg the registration
uncertainty of the ingested scene. Three answers: a path of cells with c_lo above the margin is a feasibility
witness; start and goal in different components of the cells with c_hi above the margin is an impossibility
cut; otherwise `unresolved`. For an ingested place: unknown cells are blocked for witnesses and free for cuts;
under an `estimated` label verdicts inside the scale interval are `unresolved`; and the resolution floor is
2·(ε_field + u_reg), tens of millimetres in a scan, where the correct output is a measurement request. When a
verdict is `unresolved` because of registration or scale uncertainty, or because of an unknown region,
`scene.fit` emits a probe request naming the box to rescan or the surface to probe, the quantity and the
accuracy that would settle it (for example "the clear width of door D3 to ±5 mm").

**GATE-HAB-03 (Reality).** One real place ingested end to end, privately, with its scale label and unknown
regions respected, and at least one probe request emitted for it. A place for which no capture exists is
recorded as `no-claim`, not as a pass.

**KILL-INGEST (decided here, M5).** Trigger: GATE-HAB-03 cannot be passed with a scale label of `witnessed` or
better after one repair cycle. Fallback: the place is authored in the interior grammar from a tape-measure
survey, with every surveyed dimension recorded as `measured`, and the pack says it is a proxy.

Determinism: import is a pure function of the source bytes, the profile and the correspondences (class D0 for
the pack identity). Evidence: geometry `Tested` against acceptance; cards `assumed`; every report on the place
carries `reality: no-claim` until the Reality lane supplies data.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-HAB-03: one real place is ingested end to end, privately, its scale label and unknown regions are respected by every consumer of the table, and at least one probe request is emitted; or, if no capture exists, the gate is recorded `no-claim`.
- [ ] INT-FDGR-01: the schema fixtures validate; a non-metric scale status is refused with `FROB-REF-304`; a coverage mask becomes the unknown label and a clearance query over an unscanned half-corridor returns `unresolved` (not a witness); the result carries the private mark.
- [ ] KILL-INGEST is decided and recorded with the best scale label achieved; if it fired, the place exists as a surveyed proxy and its pack says so.
- [ ] Registration: the mirrored-room fixture is rejected with four points (residual 2 m at the off-plane marker) and the planted three-point check accepts it; a nearly coplanar fourth landmark (tetrahedron volume below 1% of the cube of the largest separation) is refused.
- [ ] A metric task on a `relative-only` pack is refused (`FROB-REF-304`); a route through unknown space is refused (`FROB-REF-305`) with a probe request for the named box as the first repair.
- [ ] No friction, compliance or optical value in the pack has a provenance other than `assumed` or `measured`.
- [ ] Cancellation is tested: a cancelled `scene.import` drains and finalizes with no partial pack on disk and no ledger row for a pack; the import path has a CPU-only implementation (it uses no device), and `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-scene` documents the real-place lane with its ten sections.
- [ ] `frob scene import --json` and `frob scene fit --json` return envelopes with the claim product, `reality: no-claim`, the private flag, documented schemas and exit codes; the pack is written outside the repository and no log line contains scene content.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-scene import`)
- `registration_four_points_proper`: the identity correspondence on A, B, C, D → c = 1, det L = +1, residual 0.
- `registration_rejects_reflection`: the room mirrored in z → det L = −1 → `FROB-REF-302`; with only A, B, C supplied the request is refused for lack of a fourth point when the format admits a parity change.
- `registration_scale_mismatch`: correspondences scaled by 1.02 against a witness of 1.00 with tolerance 0.5% → refused.
- `registration_tetrahedron_volume`: A, B, C, D unit landmarks have volume 1/6 against a largest separation of √2, a ratio of 0.0589 (accepted); D = (0, 0, 0.01) gives 0.00059 (refused).
- `scale_label_ordering_and_gating`: each label against a metric task, an exploratory task and a viewing request.
- `unknown_treatment_per_consumer`: five sub-tests, one per row of the table.
- `fdgr_schema_fixtures_validate`, `fdgr_non_metric_refused`, `fdgr_unpublished_stage_refused`, `fdgr_coverage_becomes_unknown`.
- `fit_error_term`: Δ = 5 cm, 32 headings, ρ = 0.40 m, ε_field = 1 mm, u_reg = 10 mm → e = 35.36 + 39.27 + 1 + 10 = 85.6 mm.
- `fit_half_scanned_corridor_unresolved`: a 0.90 m corridor whose far half is unknown and a 0.60 m footprint → no witness, no cut, `unresolved`, one probe request naming the unknown box.
- `fit_estimated_scale_unresolved_inside_interval`.
- `cards_are_assumed`, `private_mark_inherited_by_complex_and_report`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/scene_import_real_place.sh`: with a synthetic "captured place" fixture (a generated apartment
exported as a twin package with four landmarks, a scale witness and a coverage map that leaves one room
unobserved; labelled `synthetic`) it runs `frob scene import`, `frob scene accept`, `frob scene fit` for two
routes (one through observed space, one through the unobserved room), `frob scene probe-request`, a metric task
on a `relative-only` copy, and the scan-adapter fixtures. On the owner's machine with a real capture present it
runs the same steps on the private place for GATE-HAB-03. Logging per `STD-E2E`: every line with `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the command, exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block
(including `reality` and the private flag); `assert` lines for the scale label, u_reg in millimetres, unknown
cell counts, verdicts per route, refusal codes; `metric` lines for registration residual and the error term e;
a `summary` naming GATE-HAB-03, INT-FDGR-01 and KILL-INGEST. For a private place the log holds identifiers and
numbers only: no geometry, no images, no file names from the capture.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the reflected-room residual of 2 m; the error-term arithmetic (35.4 mm + 39.3 mm = 74.6 mm before field and registration error at the default lattice); the half-scanned corridor returning unknown.
- Negative controls: the three-point registration check (accepts the mirrored room; must be caught); a naive import that maps unknown to free (planted) must produce a false witness in the corridor fixture and fail the test; a friction value inferred from texture colour (planted) must be rejected by the provenance check; a `relative_only` generation passed as metric must be refused.
- Counterexamples protected: three labelled landmarks and a known length establish registration (false); import the mesh and trust it (false).
- Metamorphic relations: applying a proper rigid motion to the capture and to the correspondences leaves every verdict unchanged; mirroring the capture without mirroring the correspondences must be refused.
- Determinism lanes: the pack identity is identical twice, across worker counts, in debug and release and on both hosts.

## Considerations

- If the inspection pilot finds that the twin package lacks four non-coplanar landmarks, a scale witness or coverage per region, the first deliverable is the upstream work item FD-FSS-01 and not an importer; the interim is a tape-measure survey authored in the interior grammar.
- No milestone waits for the drone project to publish geometry; if it has not by the M5 entry review, that lane stays a schema adapter.
- Privacy and licence: a real place is someone's home or workplace. The pack never leaves the machine without the owner's export decision; purge removes it and everything derived.
- OPEN-HAB-6 (contact with unknown space) takes its default here: degrade, exclude from metrics, count; decided with the first real place.
- An ingested place is one sample; a result on it is not a claim about a class of places.
