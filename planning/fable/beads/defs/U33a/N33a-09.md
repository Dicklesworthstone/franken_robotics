## Background

The design of FrankenRobotics rests on dozens of statements about sibling code: a constant here, a line count
there, a documented limit, a missing feature. Every one was read on 2026-11-21 at named commits and is a FACT
with its path. The rule that governs them: **a sibling fact goes stale when the sibling moves, so a fact that a
gate depends on is re-read by that gate's admission test and is never copied from the design into a record.**
FrankenSim's multibody crate alone saw 409 commits in thirty days, so "the fact as read" has a short life.

This bead is the mechanism that does the re-reading: a registry of facts with the value read at planning time,
and a probe that reads each one again at the pinned commit, reports what it finds, and attaches the observed
value to the admission record of every gate that cites it. A changed fact is a finding, not a failure: it may
be good news (a limit lifted upstream) or bad (a new hazard). Either way the gates that cite it are marked stale
until they are rerun, and interim postures tied to the fact are reviewed.

Why a registry and not ad hoc greps inside tests: (a) the same fact is cited by several gates (the 21-contact
capacity is cited by the airlock's capacity field, by the referee's coverage figure and by the contact
battery); (b) the exit review needs one list of "what we believed about siblings and what is true at the pin";
(c) a probe that is data can be rerun at every pin move by the compatibility train at no engineering cost.

## Scope

This bead is the one registry of sibling facts and the one runner that re-reads them: the file, its schema,
every probe kind, the result vocabulary, the report, the `stale-fact` marking and the rule for a pin move are
specified here and nowhere else. Other beads add rows.

In: the fact registry, the probe kinds, the probe runner, the report, the `stale-fact` marking, the
disposition rule at a pin move, and the facts stated in the chapter's opening, the FrankenSim decision, the
airlock and the foundation ledger (listed below).
Out: behavioural admission tests (a probe reads source and metadata; it never runs sibling physics; the tests
that execute sibling code are BD-032 to BD-035 and their kin); the rows of the thesis chapter's foundation fact
sheet (about forty facts: what FrankenSim contains and lacks, the GPU pattern, the MCP substrate, the limits of
the siblings, the eleven behaviour facts linked to their admission tests), with the wrapper script
`tools/oracle/recheck_foundation_facts.sh` and the list of consequences to watch: N00-16, which depends on
this bead and enters its rows here in this schema, under `sheet = "foundation"`; facts of other subsections of
the chapter (toolchain table, group closures of the extended airlock list), which their owners add as rows of
the same registry.

## Technical approach

**Registry (DESIGN, `crates/frob-conformance/registry/sibling_facts.toml`, schema `frob.sibling-facts/1`).**

```toml
[[fact]]
id       = "SF-FSIM-007"                 # stable, append-only
repo     = "frankensim"
kind     = "constant"                    # constant | attribute | line-count | git-log-count | file-exists |
                                         # manifest-field | closure | lock-entry | text-present | text-absent |
                                         # symbol-exists | behaviour
path     = "crates/fs-contact/src/sustained/solve.rs"
pattern  = "pub const MAX_CONTACTS: usize = (\\d+)"
read     = { value = "21", date = "2026-11-21", rev = "483d09c682f2" }
cited_by = ["INT-FSIM-04", "INT-FSIM-07"]
posture  = "over-capacity contact sets refuse by name; capacity is a field of RefereeContact"
sheet    = "suite"                       # which list the row belongs to: "suite" (this bead), "foundation" (N00-16), …
label    = "FACT"                        # FACT | FACT-to-recheck (reported, not yet reproduced at a pin)
consequence = "the referee-coverage bet and the capacity field of the airlock lean on this value"
# optional: tolerance = 0.05             # line-count and closure only; the share of change that flags `changed`
# optional: admission = "INT-FSIM-05"    # behaviour only: the admission test whose record gives the status
```

Fields beyond the first seven (DESIGN: one schema for every sheet, so that the foundation fact sheet of N00-16
is rows of this file and not a second registry): `sheet`; `label`; `consequence`, the design decision that
leans on the fact, required and non-empty on every row; `tolerance`; `admission`. Identifiers are
`SF-<REPO>-<nnn>` for every sheet.

**Probe kinds.** `constant` and `attribute`: a regular expression with one capture over one file. `line-count`:
lines of Rust under a directory (two variants: `src` only; the whole crate directory). `git-log-count`: commits
and distinct days touching a path in a window ending at the pin's commit date. `file-exists` and
`text-present`. `manifest-field`: a key of a `Cargo.toml` (description, a dependency's version requirement, a
feature's contents). `closure`: the number of other sibling crates, and the lines of library source, that a
non-optional path dependency on a named crate set compiles, computed from the manifests. `lock-entry`: the
version and source of a package in a lockfile. `text-absent`: a pattern that must not occur under a path (a
fact about what a sibling lacks; the weakest kind, reported only as "not found at this commit").
`symbol-exists`: a named item is defined at the path; if it is no longer there but is defined elsewhere in the
repository the result is `moved` and the new path is recorded. `behaviour`: a fact about what sibling code
does when run; it is not probed, because the probe never executes sibling code: the row names its admission
test in `admission`, and the report prints that test's latest status, `defect-present`, `defect-fixed` or
`not-run`, beside the row.

**Reading at the pin.** For a sibling that is clean and on its pin, the probe reads the working tree. Otherwise
it reads through `git show <pin>:<path>` and `git ls-tree`, so the answer describes the pinned commit and not
whatever is checked out. It never writes to a sibling. This is the one rule for every caller (DESIGN): a
checkout whose head differs from the stack lock is not a reason to refuse, because other agents work in the
siblings and their heads move; the runner reads the pinned commit through git and says in the report that it
did. A pin that names a commit the repository does not have is the only refusal.

**Runner.** `frob_conformance::sibling_facts::probe_all(receipt) -> FactReport`: for each fact, the observed
value, `unchanged | changed | moved | unreadable` (and for a `behaviour` row the status of its admission
test), and the pin it was read at. One vocabulary for every sheet: `unchanged` is what a fact sheet calls
"holds". Counts and line counts compare exactly for `constant`, `lock-entry` and `file-exists`; `line-count`
and `closure` are always recorded and are flagged `changed` when they differ by more than the row's
`tolerance`, 5% unless the row declares another (they drift with every commit; the flag is for a change of
scale). `probe_all` takes an optional sheet filter, so that a wrapper can print one sheet; the report always
lives in one place, and a filtered run writes a view of it, never a second report format.

**Consequences of a change.**
1. Every gate in `cited_by` whose newest admission record was made with the old value is marked `stale-fact` in
   the generated admission file until rerun.
2. A fact with a `posture` (an interim behaviour of this project) is listed in the report under "postures to
   review". A probe that now shows a limit lifted is recorded as "fixed upstream at <rev>", and the posture is
   reviewed, not silently dropped.
3. Nothing is auto-edited: the design text, the registry's `read` value and the adapters change by a person's
   or an agent's commit that cites the report.
4. At a pin move the compatibility train runs the probe before and after. Every fact that is `changed` or
   `moved` between the two runs is listed with its `consequence` and the beads that field names, and the pin
   move is not complete until each has a recorded disposition: design unaffected, design updated, or fallback
   adopted.

**Attachment to records.** The admission-record writer (bead N33a-07) takes the subset of the report cited by
its gate and stores it as `facts_reread`.

Determinism: D0 for a fixed pin. The probe runs no sibling code and needs no build.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The registry holds one row per fact in the table above (grouped facts may be several rows), each with a stable identifier, a read value, a date, a revision, a label, a non-empty consequence and at least one citing gate or decision.
- [ ] The schema, the runner and the report serve every sheet: the rows that N00-16 adds under `sheet = "foundation"` load with no change to this bead's code, and no second registry file or report format for sibling facts exists in the repository.
- [ ] `probe_all` on the pinned FrankenSim returns a value for every FrankenSim fact, reading through `git show` when the checkout is off-pin or dirty.
- [ ] A planted change in a copy (the capacity constant edited to 24) is reported `changed`, marks INT-FSIM-04 and INT-FSIM-07 `stale-fact`, and lists the capacity posture for review.
- [ ] A fact whose file no longer exists is `unreadable`, not `unchanged`.
- [ ] The INT-FSIM-01 admission record contains `facts_reread` entries for the facts that cite it.
- [ ] The probe leaves every sibling byte-identical (run against read-only copies).
- [ ] The fact report is reachable as JSON (schema `frob.sibling-facts.report/1`) from the conformance harness.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-conformance sibling_facts`; synthetic mini-repositories)
- `probe_constant_reads_capture`: a file with `pub const MAX_CONTACTS: usize = 21;` → `"21"`.
- `probe_constant_changed`: the same with 24 → `changed`, observed `"24"`.
- `probe_reads_at_pin_not_worktree`: the working tree says 24, the pinned commit says 21 → observed `"21"`.
- `probe_git_log_count`: a fixture history with 5 commits on 3 days in the window → (5, 3).
- `probe_closure_counts`: a fixture of four manifests with a known dependency graph → the exact crate count and line total.
- `probe_lock_entry`: a fixture lockfile → version and source of a named package; two versions of one name → both reported.
- `probe_unreadable_on_missing_file`.
- `line_count_flag_threshold`: 1,000 → 1,040 is `unchanged` with the new value recorded; 1,000 → 1,060 is `changed`; with `tolerance = 0.10` on the row, 1,000 → 1,060 is `unchanged` and 1,000 → 1,110 is `changed`.
- `symbol_moved_is_reported_with_new_path`: an item defined in `a.rs` at the read revision and in `b.rs` at the pin → `moved`, new path `b.rs`.
- `text_absent_fact_fails_when_text_appears`: a `text-absent` row for subset-simulation code; a file named `subset_sim.rs` appears → `changed`.
- `behaviour_fact_links_admission_record`: a `behaviour` row is never probed; its status is the latest record of the test named in `admission`, or `not-run`.
- `every_fact_has_consequence`: a row with an empty `consequence` fails the load, on every sheet.
- `off_pin_checkout_is_read_at_the_pin_not_refused`; `unknown_pin_commit_refused`.
- `disposition_required_at_pin_move`: a train with a `changed` fact and no disposition is not complete.
- `stale_marking`: a changed fact marks exactly the gates in its `cited_by`.
- `registry_ids_append_only`: a planted renumbering fails.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/admission_m0.sh` (bead N33a-28), step `sibling_facts`: runs the probe against the real siblings at
the pins of the newest receipt and logs one `metric` line per fact (identifier, read value, observed value,
status, pin). The step asserts only that every fact is readable and that the report is attached to the records;
it does not assert that facts are unchanged, because a changed fact is information.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Negative controls: the planted capacity change; a planted deletion; a working tree that disagrees with its pin. Each must be reported as stated.
- Live-dependency test: perturbing the pinned revision in the receipt must change which commit the probe reads.
- Determinism lanes: the report is byte-identical twice on one machine and across platforms for the same pins.

## Considerations

- The probe reads text. A fact about behaviour ("returns zero translation near zero curvature") is not a probe; it is an admission test that executes the code. The registry links the two by gate identifier.
- Regular expressions over source are brittle by design: when a sibling refactors, the probe goes `unreadable`, which is the correct signal that the fact needs a human re-reading.
- The ±5% flag on size facts is a DESIGN choice to avoid noise from daily commits; the exact values are always recorded. A row may widen it with `tolerance` (the foundation sheet's row for the size of a tactic crate that is expected to grow declares 10%); no row may narrow the exact comparison of constants.
- RISK-01 (API churn) and BET-04's breakage metric use the churn fact as context; the breakage share itself is a different measurement (bead N33a-10).
