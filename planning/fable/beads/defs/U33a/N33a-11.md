## Background

`frob-fsim` is the **airlock**: the only crate of FrankenRobotics that may name types from FrankenSim's
volatile crates (workspace rule L-2). It exposes FrankenRobotics-native traits and adapts them to FrankenSim. A
FrankenSim API change then costs one crate. That matters because FrankenSim moves daily (FACT: 409 commits
touched its multibody crate in thirty days) and its component contract is not frozen
(`COMPONENT_CONTRACT_VERSION` is 0). The airlock stays whatever lands upstream.

DESIGN: the traits are grouped, and **each group is a Cargo feature of `frob-fsim`**, so that a crate above the
airlock compiles only the closure it uses. The groups serve two purposes of unequal weight. They bound what a
crate above the airlock may name, always. They save compile time only for the last two (`structure` and
`multiphysics`), because the other optional groups are small beside what M1 already compiles.

FACT (computed from FrankenSim's manifests on 2026-11-21; re-read by the fact probes of bead N33a-09): the M1
set (`referee`, `geometry`, `ledger`) compiles 65 FrankenSim crates and about 2.06 million lines of library
source. Beyond it: `spectral` adds nothing (its crate is already in that closure); `optimize` adds 12 crates
and about 84 thousand lines; `stats` 10 crates and about 113 thousand; `actuation` 2 crates and about 151
thousand; `structure` 13 crates and about 412 thousand; `multiphysics` 19 crates and about 2.31 million.

Rejected alternatives: (a) one airlock crate per FrankenSim crate: dozens of crates, and the volatile types
would be nameable from many places; (b) no features, always compile everything: the `multiphysics` closure
alone more than doubles the build; (c) letting each subsystem depend on the FrankenSim crate it wants: this is
exactly what L-2 forbids, since an upstream rename would then touch every subsystem.

The crate sits at layer 0 of the workspace (beside `frob-core`), is Solid tier, and has a scope cap of 20
thousand lines of Rust including tests.

## Scope

In: the manifest with its ten features and optional path dependencies; the module layout; the group registry
as data; the trait names and intents of every group (names fix intent, not signatures); the built-with set and
the refusal `FROB-REF-790`; the `harness` group; `CONTRACT.md` and `contract.toml`.
Out: adapter bodies: `RefereeDynamics` (BD-007), `RefereeContact` (N33a-14), `Integrator` and `PortSystem`
(N33a-15), the geometry group (N33a-16), `Ledger` (BD-009), `Session` and `Recompute` (N33a-17), the `optimize`
group (N33a-18), the `actuation` group (N33a-19); the `spectral`, `stats`, `structure` and `multiphysics`
adapters with their group gates (another unit); refusal conversion (N33a-13); the `remote` mode (BD-038).

The last column is this bead's initial reading of which subsystems reach which FrankenSim crates; it is data,
and a change is an ADR. The consumer lists for `referee`, `geometry`, `optimize` and `actuation` are DESIGN
derived from the subsystems' own statements; those for `stats`, `spectral`, `structure`, `ledger` and
`multiphysics` follow the design's "reached by" table.

**Trait intents (one line each; signatures are decided by the adapter beads).** `RefereeDynamics`: build an
articulated model and evaluate forward dynamics and steps in `f64`. `RefereeContact`: a contact step over a
caller-supplied contact set, with its capacity as data. `Integrator`: structure-preserving steps on groups and
swept motion charts. `PortSystem`: composition of components through typed effort and flow ports.
`GeometryKernel`: signed distance, convex support and separation queries. `SceneQuery`: role-tagged support and
keep-out regions of referee scenes. `MeshExchange`: hardened mesh read and write with content hashes.
`Ledger`: the transactional metadata store on FrankenSQLite. `Session`: packaged, replayable sessions.
`Recompute`: a content-addressed graph of results with skip certificates and invalidation. `Optimizer`:
derivative-free and Bayesian search. `Archive`: quality-diversity archives. `Racing`: anytime-valid races and
e-processes. `Uncertainty`: sampling, multilevel estimation, importance weights, sensitivity effects.
`ValueOfInformation`: ranking-flip probability and expected opportunity loss. `Surrogate`: reduced models
admitted inside validity bands. `Assimilation`: checked belief updates from observations. `Spectral`: spectral
admission and Floquet analysis of a supplied monodromy. `Magnetics`, `ThermalNetwork`, `CurrentLoop`,
`PortHamiltonian`: actuator physics at the fidelity levels of the actuator subsystem. `StructuralOpt`,
`ToleranceAllocation`, `AsBuilt`, `FabricationRules`: structure and manufacture. `FluidIsland`,
`CoupledIsland`: whole-field exchange with resolved matter at declared instants, evaluation tier only.

**Manifest (shape).** Every FrankenSim crate is an `optional = true` path dependency with default features off;
a feature lists the `dep:` entries of its group; `default = ["referee", "geometry", "ledger", "mode-inproc"]`.
`[package.metadata.frob] layer = 0, rank = 2`. The crate denies unsafe code. A mode feature pair `mode-inproc`
and `mode-remote` is declared here and left empty for BD-038.

**Module layout.** `src/lib.rs` (re-exports, `groups`, `refuse`), `src/groups.rs` (the registry and the
built-with set), one module per group (`referee/`, `geometry/`, `ledger/`, …) each behind `#[cfg(feature =
"…")]`, and `src/convert/` for vocabulary conversions. No public item outside a group module mentions an `fs-*`
type, and no public signature of any group exposes one: FrankenSim types never cross the airlock's public face,
except those of the bedrock-direct crates (bead N33a-12).

**Group registry and the built-with set.**

```rust
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq)]
pub enum Group { Referee, Geometry, Ledger, Optimize, Stats, Spectral, Actuation, Structure, Multiphysics }
pub const BUILT_WITH: &[Group] = &[ /* one cfg-gated entry per enabled feature */ ];
pub fn require(groups: &[Group]) -> Result<(), Refusal>;      // FROB-REF-790 on the first absent group
```

The same table is published as data in `crates/frob-fsim/groups.toml` (name, traits, crates, default, allowed
consumers, first milestone, admission gate) for `check-deps` and for `frob capabilities`.

**Refusal `FROB-REF-790` (`foundation-group-absent`).** Raised when a verb needs an airlock feature group that
this binary was not built with. The verb dispatcher calls `require(verb.groups)` before any work, so the
refusal costs nothing and charges no budget. Fields: `pointer` the group name, `observed` the built-with set,
`required` the verb's set, `retry: after-change`. Ranked repairs: (1) use a binary built with the group (the
build profile that enables it is named); (2) for an optional analysis, run the verb's reference path that does
not need the group, if its registry row declares one; (3) none for `multiphysics` on a throughput tier, which
is refused by design. Exit code 5 (capability not implemented or not admitted) at the command line.

**Rule: a group is populated only after its gate.** A group module may contain trait declarations at any time.
An adapter body that calls FrankenSim lands only with the admission record of the gate that covers it; the
never-inherited check of bead N33a-07 reports a group that is enabled by a manifest without such a record.

Determinism and evidence: this bead adds no computation. The contract records, per group, the determinism
class its adapters may claim (the referee claims same-platform determinism only, because FrankenSim uses
platform square root and trigonometric functions) and that outputs carry the evidence class FrankenSim's own
contracts give them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `cargo check -p frob-fsim` passes with default features, with `--no-default-features`, and with each optional feature alone (each group compiles independently).
- [ ] `cargo tree -p frob-fsim --no-default-features` shows no volatile `fs-*` crate; with `--features spectral` it shows `fs-spectral` and not `fs-lbm`.
- [ ] `BUILT_WITH` equals the enabled features in each of those builds (a test per build configuration).
- [ ] `require(&[Group::Stats])` in a default build returns `FROB-REF-790` naming `stats`, with exit code 5 through `frob --json`.
- [ ] No public signature of `frob-fsim` contains a type from a volatile FrankenSim crate (a test parses the public API listing and matches against the group crate list).
- [ ] `groups.toml` lists ten groups and agrees with the manifest's feature table; `check-deps` refuses a planted manifest in which `frob-dyn` enables `structure`.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` with its ten sections and `contract.toml` exist; the crate's line count is reported against its 20-thousand-line cap.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-fsim groups`, run under several feature sets)
- `built_with_default_is_three`: `[Referee, Geometry, Ledger]`.
- `built_with_none_is_empty` (`--no-default-features`).
- `require_present_ok`, `require_absent_refuses_790` (code, pointer, observed, required and the three repairs asserted).
- `require_reports_first_absent_in_declared_order`: requiring `[Stats, Spectral]` in a default build names `stats`.
- `groups_toml_matches_manifest`: the feature table parsed from `Cargo.toml` equals `groups.toml`.
- `public_api_names_no_volatile_type`: the generated API listing contains none of the group crate names.
- `multiphysics_not_in_any_default`: no workspace manifest enables `multiphysics` without an explicit feature of its own that is off by default.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/airlock_refusals.sh` (bead N33a-29), step `group_absent`: builds a `frob` binary without the
`referee` group, runs `frob sim run g1 --tier referee --seconds 0.01 --json`, and asserts exit code 5 and
`refusal.code = "FROB-REF-790"` with `pointer = "referee"`; then the default binary, which must not refuse. The
log carries both command lines, exit codes, the refusal object and the built-with sets.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Negative control: a binary in which `require` is stubbed to always succeed must be caught (the verb then fails later with an unhelpful error; the test asserts the refusal code, so the stub fails it).
- Negative control for the confinement rule: the planted `frob-dyn` → `structure` edge.
- Sibling facts re-read here: the six group closure costs quoted above.
- Determinism lanes: `BUILT_WITH` is identical in debug and release for the same features.

## Considerations

- The design's group table places `fs-io` in `geometry` (default on) while its admission known answer (a mesh written through it reads back with the same hash; an oversized STEP file is quarantined) is listed under the `structure` group gate at M5. Resolution adopted: `MeshExchange` is admitted by its own record at first use with the mesh round-trip half; the bounded STEP exchange is admitted with the structure gate. The never-inherited rule makes this safe either way.
- FrankenSim crates deliberately not behind any feature: `fs-conform` (a conformance kit for chart converters, not conformal prediction), `fs-truss` (brings franken_networkx), `fs-g1-train` (reported defects in its PPO update). `fs-la` is an oracle only, `fs-checker`, `fs-tilelang` and `fs-soa` are patterns.
- Feature unification: Cargo unions features across a workspace build, so "this crate did not ask for `structure`" does not mean "this binary lacks it". That is why `BUILT_WITH` is a property of the binary and why the consumer rule is enforced on manifests by `check-deps`, not by the type system.
- RISK-01 (API churn) is absorbed here: the count of adapter changes per pin move is the leading indicator.
- If KILL-FSIM fires, the groups keep their meaning: in `remote` mode a group names which job kinds the worker serves.
