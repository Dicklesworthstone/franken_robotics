## Background

The airlock crate `frob-fsim` is the only crate that may name types from FrankenSim's volatile crates. One
exception exists. A short **bedrock-direct** list may be used anywhere in FrankenRobotics, because the types of
those crates are the vocabulary of the mathematics: a motor of projective geometric algebra, an interval with
outward rounding, a counter-based random stream, a content hash, an evidence wrapper, a cancellation context.
Routing those through adapter types would put a conversion on every line of every subsystem and would buy
nothing, since the crates are small and stable in shape.

FACT (sizes counted on 2026-11-21 as lines of Rust under each crate's `src` and under the whole crate
directory; re-read by the fact probes of bead N33a-09):

| Crate | Library lines | With tests | Depends on `fs-qty` | Leaves FrankenSim | What it provides |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fs-ga` | 4,078 | 4,883 | no | no | Motors, screws and meets in projective geometric algebra; Lie façades in `src/lie.rs` |
| `fs-math` | 5,063 | 7,633 | no | no | Deterministic elementary functions with error budgets in units in the last place |
| `fs-qty` | 8,950 | 9,947 | it is `fs-qty` | no | Compile-time dimensional analysis; runtime-checked quantities; SI parsing |
| `fs-rand` | 7,356 | 12,053 | no | no | Counter-based Philox streams keyed by logical identity; Sobol and Owen sequences |
| `fs-blake3` | 7,304 | 13,757 | no | no | BLAKE3 in safe Rust; the 32-byte content hash |
| `fs-ivl` | 5,956 | 11,295 | yes, through `fs-evidence` | no | Outward-rounded intervals, affine forms, Taylor models, exact predicates |
| `fs-simd` | 3,331 | 4,242 | no | no | SIMD tiers behind safe façades |
| `fs-exec` | 33,870 | 38,543 | no | asupersync, by path | Two-lane executor and the `Cx` contract |
| `fs-evidence` | 37,120 | 57,107 | yes, directly | no | `Evidence<T>` and `Certified<T>` with conservative composition |
| `fs-ad` | 2,917 | 4,062 | no | asupersync through `fs-la`; FrankenTorch crates if a feature is on | Forward-mode duals and the `Real` scalar contract |

Eight of the ten are under nine thousand lines. `fs-exec` and `fs-evidence` are larger and are admitted direct
because `Cx` and `Evidence<T>` appear in signatures throughout. The closure of the list is 16 crates and about
161 thousand lines of library source. Everything else goes through the airlock.

Three properties of the list need care and are why this is a bead and not a comment: `fs-qty` enables an
incomplete nightly compiler feature (`generic_const_exprs`), so it must be reachable through exactly one
re-export; `fs-ad` pulls FrankenTorch crates if one of its features is on, which the core graph forbids; and a
"bedrock" crate that silently grows a dependency on a volatile crate would turn the exception into a hole in
rule L-2. The audit below catches the third.

## Scope

In: the list as data; the per-crate rules; an admission smoke test with one known answer and one negative
control per crate; the closure audit; the dependency manifest lines other crates copy.
Out: the `frob_core::qty` module itself (BD-005); the toolchain matrix row "bedrock-direct list" (BD-031 fills
it from this bead's build target); deeper admission of `fs-ga`'s Lie façades (INT-FSIM-02, another unit).

## Technical approach

**The list as data.** A `[bedrock_direct]` table in `crates/frob-fsim/contract.toml`: `crates = ["fs-ga",
"fs-math", "fs-qty", "fs-rand", "fs-blake3", "fs-ivl", "fs-simd", "fs-exec", "fs-evidence", "fs-ad"]`, plus per
crate `features_allowed` (default features off; a non-default feature is listed here or it is refused) and
`import_rule`.

**Per-crate rules (DESIGN).**
1. `fs-qty`: importable by name only in `frob-core` and `frob-fsim`. Every other crate writes `frob_core::qty::…`.
   This keeps the escape hatch cheap: if the units crate fails on the chosen toolchain, the re-export switches
   to a local crate and the change is one module plus the conversions in the airlock.
2. `fs-ad`: the feature that pulls FrankenTorch crates is forbidden in every core manifest.
3. `fs-exec`: brings asupersync by path; the graph must still show exactly one asupersync package.
4. `fs-evidence`, `fs-ivl`: depend on `fs-qty`; they are bedrock, but their quantity-typed items are used
   through `frob_core::qty` types.
5. All ten: path dependencies on the FrankenSim checkout at the pin of `franken-stack.lock`, default features
   off.

**Admission smoke test (`crates/frob-fsim/tests/bedrock_admission.rs`; one known answer and one control per
crate, through each crate's public API as found at the pin).**

| Crate | Known answer | Negative control (must fail) |
|---|---|---|
| `fs-blake3` | The hash of the empty input equals the published BLAKE3 vector `af1349b9f5f9a1a6a0404dea36dcc9499bcb25c9adc112b7cc9a93cae41f3262` | One flipped input bit gives a different digest |
| `fs-rand` | Two streams with the same key and counter give identical draws; the stream is a pure function of (key, counter) | A different logical key gives a different first draw |
| `fs-math` | The deterministic sine of 0 is exactly 0, and its value at a tabulated point is inside the crate's stated error budget against a high-precision reference stored in the test | A reference perturbed by 100 units in the last place is outside the budget |
| `fs-qty` | A length times a length has the dimension of area; metres plus metres compiles | Adding a length to a time fails to compile (a compile-fail test) |
| `fs-ivl` | The interval [1, 2] + [0.1, 0.2] contains 1.1 and 2.2 (outward rounding); the enclosure of x·x on [−1, 2] contains [0, 4] | An interval built with inward rounding (a planted mutant helper) fails the containment check |
| `fs-simd` | A façade sum over 1,024 values equals the scalar loop under the crate's stated ordering | A permuted input with a non-associative pattern differs when the ordering contract is violated by the planted helper |
| `fs-exec` | A task observes a cancellation request at its next checkpoint and returns a cancelled outcome | A task that ignores the context (planted) does not stop, and the test's timeout assertion catches it |
| `fs-evidence` | Composing two evidence values yields the weaker class (conservative composition) | A planted composition that returns the stronger class is rejected by the test |
| `fs-ad` | The forward-mode derivative of x ↦ x² at x = 3 is 6, and of x·y at (2, 5) with respect to x is 5 | The derivative of x² reported as 3 (a planted missing factor) fails |
| `fs-ga` | The identity motor composed with a motor m gives m; a rotation motor applied twice equals the motor of twice the angle | A reversed composition order on two non-commuting motors differs |

The exact item names are read at the pin and listed in the admission record; where a crate's public API does
not offer the operation in the simple form above, the test uses the nearest public operation and the record
says which.

**Closure audit (`closure_of_bedrock`).** From `cargo metadata` of a scratch crate that depends on the ten and
nothing else: (1) list every FrankenSim crate in the resolved graph; (2) assert that none belongs to a volatile
group of the airlock (`fs-mbd`, `fs-contact`, …); (3) record the count and the lines of library source and
compare with 16 crates and about 161 thousand lines (a change of more than 5% is reported as a finding); (4)
assert exactly one asupersync package and no FrankenTorch crate.

**Admission.** `fs-ga` is covered by the INT-FSIM-01 record of BD-006. The other nine are admitted by one record
of this bead's smoke test under the same gate, INT-FSIM-01 ("frankensim: bedrock crates by path" in the gate
table), a second record of that gate beside BD-006's in `receipts/admissions/INT-FSIM-01/`, written through the
writer of N33a-07 with `covers.crates` naming all ten and citing the same stack receipt; under the
never-inherited rule a bedrock crate is not usable until that record exists. If KILL-FSIM fires, bedrock crates
that still pass this test stay path dependencies; those that do not are lifted first, since they are small.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The `[bedrock_direct]` table of `contract.toml` holds the ten crates; `check-deps` allows a planted edge `frob-lie → fs-ga` and refuses `frob-lie → fs-mbd` and a direct `use fs_qty::…` in `frob-lie`.
- [ ] The smoke test passes for all ten crates at the pin and each negative control fails as stated.
- [ ] The closure audit records the crate count and line total, finds no volatile crate, exactly one asupersync package and no FrankenTorch crate.
- [ ] A manifest that enables `fs-ad`'s FrankenTorch feature is refused.
- [ ] An admission record covering the ten crates exists and validates.
- [ ] The dependency lines for the ten crates are documented once in `frob-fsim`'s `CONTRACT.md` (path, default features off, allowed features).

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-fsim --test bedrock_admission`, `cargo test -p xtask deps::bedrock`)
- Ten tests `bedrock_<crate>_known_answer` with the inputs and expected values of the table; ten `bedrock_<crate>_control` tests.
- `fs_qty_length_plus_time_fails` (trybuild compile-fail).
- `closure_has_no_volatile_crate`, `closure_one_asupersync`, `closure_no_frankentorch`, `closure_size_recorded`.
- `bedrock_list_is_ten`: the data file's list equals the ten names exactly; an eleventh name fails until an ADR is cited in the file.
- `fs_qty_import_rule`: a fixture crate with `use fs_qty::Length;` outside the two allowed crates → finding.
- `bedrock_edge_rules`: planted manifests: `frob-lie → fs-ga` (default features off) → allowed; `frob-lie → fs-mbd` → refused as a volatile edge; `frob-lie → fs-qty` → refused by the import rule; `frob-core → fs-qty` and `frob-fsim → fs-qty` → allowed.
- `bedrock_feature_rules`: `fs-ad` with its FrankenTorch feature enabled in any core manifest → refused; a bedrock crate with default features on, or with a non-default feature absent from its `features_allowed` → refused; a listed feature → allowed.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/admission_m0.sh` (bead N33a-28), step `bedrock_direct`: runs the smoke test and the closure audit
through the offload shim, writes the admission record, and logs per crate an `assert` line (known answer,
control caught) and for the closure the crate count, line total, asupersync package count and the delta from
the read values.

### Known answers and negative controls
- The published BLAKE3 empty-input vector; d(x²)/dx = 6 at 3; [1, 2] + [0.1, 0.2] ⊇ [1.1, 2.2]; the weaker-class composition rule.
- Negative controls: the ten of the table, the volatile edge, the forbidden feature, the direct `fs_qty` import.
- Counterexample kept in view: "a dimension vector is all the typing a quantity needs" is false, because a torque and an energy share the vector (kilogram 1, metre 2, second −2); quantities are distinct aliases, which is why `fs-qty` items are reached through one re-export that can enforce the alias set.
- Determinism lanes: the known answers are bit-identical in debug and release and on both platforms (`fs-math` is deterministic by contract; the test records its value's bits).

## Considerations

- The list is a privilege. A crate is added only by ADR, with its size, its `fs-qty` coupling and what leaves FrankenSim through it stated in the same columns as above.
- `fs-exec` and `fs-evidence` are the two large members (about 34 and 37 thousand lines). They are the likeliest to change shape; the adapter-free use of `Cx` and `Evidence<T>` is a deliberate bet that their public faces are stable, and the churn fact probe watches them.
- OPEN-SUITE-6 (whether computational crates take asupersync's `Cx` or a capability-free token from `frob-core`): default asupersync's `Cx` wherever cancellation is observed; the token only if the one-asupersync gate fails after the alignment work and a fallback splits the graph.
- The units-crate coupling is a named risk: of 22 FrankenSim crates coupled to `fs-qty`, 12 are reported to depend on it directly and 10 through another crate, and removing it from `fs-evidence` alone would free 8 (FACT to recheck; recorded by the toolchain gate).
