## Background

The airlock crate `frob-fsim` exposes FrankenSim to FrankenRobotics through traits grouped into Cargo features.
The `referee` group (on by default) holds four traits: `RefereeDynamics` (bead BD-007), `RefereeContact` (bead
N33a-14), and the two of this bead:

- **`Integrator`**: structure-preserving time steps on Lie groups and swept motion charts, over FrankenSim's
  `fs-time` and `fs-motion`.
- **`PortSystem`**: composition of components through typed effort and flow ports, over `fs-ports` and
  `fs-system`.

The group's crate list also names `fs-mbd-material` and `fs-tribo`; their vocabulary conversions (material
handles, friction-law selections) are needed by both contact and ports and are placed here.

FACT (read 2026-11-21; re-read at the pin): `fs-time` has a discrete Euler–Poincaré step on SE(3) with a
momentum adjoint (`dep_momentum_adjoint`) and a RATTLE projection hook; `fs-mbd`'s own stepping is a
second-order Lie midpoint step that its contract calls neither energy-exact nor variational; `fs-motion`
provides swept charts used to bound a link's motion over a joint range; FrankenSim's composition contract is
not frozen (`COMPONENT_CONTRACT_VERSION` is 0 at `fs-ports/src/component.rs:52`), which is hazard 2 of the
decision to consume FrankenSim and the subject of upstream work item FD-FSIM-08 (P1; interim posture: the
airlock adapts contract version 0; switch-over rule: re-run the functor laws).

Why these two sit in the airlock instead of being called directly: both crates are volatile (an unfrozen
contract; integrator signatures still moving), and both are used by several subsystems (dynamics, actuators,
analyses). One adapter per trait means one place to change at a pin move. The mathematics they serve is this
project's: time stepping stays on the group (construction C-05: an update g₊ = g·τ(h·ξ) for a retraction τ), and
a robot is a composition over typed ports whose wiring conserves power (construction C-12).

Admission is never inherited: `fs-time` is covered by the INT-FSIM-01 record and `fs-tribo` by INT-FSIM-09, but
`fs-motion`, `fs-ports`, `fs-system` and `fs-mbd-material` are first used here and are admitted here, by their
own record, at the pin of that day, in the form of INT-FSIM-01 applied to a crate: it compiles from this
repository behind its feature, one known answer passes through the airlock trait, one negative control fails.

## Scope

In: the two traits with in-process adapters; conversions for material and friction-law vocabulary; the
admission record for the four crates; known answers and controls.

## Technical approach

**Traits (DESIGN: intent, not signatures).**

```rust
pub trait Integrator {
    /// One discrete Euler–Poincaré step of a free rigid body on SE(3): pose g, body momentum μ, step h.
    fn dep_step_se3(&self, body: &RigidBodySpec, g: &Pose, mu: &Momentum, wrench: &BodyWrench, h: Rational)
        -> Result<(Pose, Momentum), RefereeRefusal>;
    /// A conservative enclosure of the region a body-fixed shape sweeps while one joint coordinate
    /// moves over [q_lo, q_hi].
    fn swept_chart(&self, link: &LinkShape, joint: &JointAxis, q_lo: f64, q_hi: f64)
        -> Result<SweptEnclosure, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn facts(&self) -> IntegratorFacts;   // order, what is conserved and what is not, as data
}
pub trait PortSystem {
    fn compose(&self, parts: &[ComponentHandle], wiring: &Wiring) -> Result<ComposedSystem, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn port_powers(&self, sys: &ComposedSystem, state: &PortState) -> Result<Vec<Power>, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn contract_version(&self) -> u32;    // FrankenSim's component contract version at the pin (0 at reading)
}
```

Poses, twists, wrenches and momenta cross the trait in the bedrock geometric-algebra types (`fs-ga` is on the
bedrock-direct list) or in this project's frame-typed wrappers; no `fs-time`, `fs-motion`, `fs-ports` or
`fs-system` type appears in a public signature.

**Rules for `Integrator`.**
1. `facts()` reports, read from the upstream contract text at the pin and stored as data: the order of the
   step; that spatial momentum of a free body is conserved by the discrete equations; that energy is bounded,
   not conserved; that no claim is made for an articulated tree. A caller that asks for more than `facts()`
   offers is refused by the caller's own contract, not here.
2. The rational step `h` is converted to `f64` exactly once, at the boundary, and the conversion error is zero
   for the dyadic steps the referee uses; a non-dyadic `h` is passed as the nearest double and flagged in the
   step record.
3. `swept_chart` returns an **outer** enclosure. The adapter states the enclosure's kind as data (the chart
   type FrankenSim offers at the pin) and never shrinks it.

**Rules for `PortSystem`.**
1. `contract_version()` is checked on construction against the version this adapter was written for; a
   different upstream version is a refusal at start-up naming both numbers, never a silent best effort.
2. Wiring is validated before the upstream call: every port connected at most once per direction, effort and
   flow types matching, no algebraic loop. A violation is this project's own design refusal for port errors,
   not an upstream one.
3. `port_powers` returns one signed power per port, positive into the component.

**Vocabulary conversions (`frob_fsim::convert::material`).** Material handles and friction-law selections are
converted between FrankenRobotics interface cards and FrankenSim's `fs-mbd-material` and `fs-tribo` types by
total functions; a law this project names that FrankenSim lacks is a typed refusal with the law's name, and a
missing material pair is a refusal, never a default coefficient.

**Admission record.** Written through the record writer of bead N33a-07 with `covers.crates = ["fs-motion",
"fs-ports", "fs-system", "fs-mbd-material"]`, citing the stack receipt of the day, the known answers below and
the caught controls.

Determinism class: same platform, same binary. Evidence: outputs carry the class FrankenSim's contracts give.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both traits compile under the `referee` feature with no volatile type in their public face.
- [ ] Free-body momentum: for inertia diag(1, 2, 3) kg·m², mass 1 kg, initial body angular velocity (0.1, 2.0, 0.1) rad/s, no wrench, h = 1/480 s, 10⁴ steps, the spatial angular momentum vector R·(I·ω) is constant to 10⁻¹² relative in each component (TARGET; the discrete equations conserve it exactly up to the implicit solve's tolerance).
- [ ] Group membership over the same run: ‖RᵀR − I‖_max ≤ 10⁻¹² (TARGET).
- [ ] Energy over the same run is bounded: the largest deviation of kinetic energy in the second half of the run is at most twice the largest in the first half (no secular drift), and the measured deviation is recorded.
- [ ] Swept chart: for a point at radius 1 m rotating by 90° about a fixed axis, the enclosure contains both endpoints and the arc midpoint.
- [ ] Port power: two components joined by an ideal gear of ratio r = 3 (flows f₂ = r·f₁, efforts e₁ = −r·e₂) give port powers that sum to zero to 10⁻¹² relative for 10³ random (e₂, f₁).
- [ ] A reversed port sign, an algebraic loop and a contract-version mismatch are each refused.
- [ ] The admission record for the four crates exists and validates; `check-stack`'s never-inherited check is clean for the `referee` group.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-fsim referee::integrator referee::ports convert::material`)
- `dep_free_body_momentum_conserved`, `dep_group_membership`, `dep_energy_bounded`: the three runs above with the stated inputs and thresholds.
- `dep_step_time_reversible`: stepping forward with h then with −h returns the initial pose and momentum to 10⁻¹² (TARGET).
- `swept_chart_contains_arc`: endpoints (1, 0, 0) and (0, 1, 0) m and midpoint (√2/2, √2/2, 0) m are inside the enclosure.
- `gear_port_power_sums_to_zero`: e₁·f₁ + e₂·f₂ = (−r·e₂)·f₁ + e₂·(r·f₁) = 0 identically (the rational gear law, theorem card T-15); measured residual ≤ 10⁻¹² relative.
- `reversed_port_sign_refused`: with e₁ = +r·e₂ the sum is 2·r·e₂·f₁ ≠ 0; the wiring validator refuses the composition.
- `algebraic_loop_refused`: two ideal elements wired in a causal cycle.
- `contract_version_mismatch_refused`: a test build that expects version 1 against the pin's 0.
- `missing_material_pair_refused`, `unknown_friction_law_refused`.
- Property test `material_conversion_roundtrip`: every interface-card material and law that converts to FrankenSim converts back to itself.

### End-to-end script and logging
No script of its own. The capability is observed through the referee rollouts of the dynamics subsystem; the
admission record is produced by the `referee_group_admission` step added to `scripts/e2e/airlock_groups.sh`
(bead N33a-34), which runs the tests above through the offload shim, writes the record and logs each known
answer's measured value with unit and threshold and each control's `caught` flag.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: constant spatial momentum of a free body (theorem card T-06 states the property for the Cayley discrete Euler–Poincaré step); the gear power identity (T-15); arc containment. Midpoint-miss size for the control below: the chord of a 90° arc at radius 1 m misses the arc midpoint by 1 − cos 45° = 0.2929 m.
- Negative controls: a chart built from the two endpoints only (the chord's bounding box inflated by zero) must fail the midpoint test; the reversed gear sign; a planted additive (non-group) pose update must fail group membership after 10⁴ steps.
- Metamorphic relation: rotating the whole free-body problem by a fixed rotation rotates the momentum vector by the same rotation (equivariance), to 10⁻¹².
- Determinism lanes: each run bit-identical twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- When FrankenSim freezes its component contract as version 1 (FD-FSIM-08), the adapter's expected version changes in one place and the functor-law tests of the design subsystem are rerun.
- The tolerances above are TARGETs until recorded; if FrankenSim's implicit solve tolerance is looser than 10⁻¹², the recorded value becomes the threshold with the upstream constant cited.
- `fs-motion`'s chart kinds may change; the adapter exposes the kind as data so that analysis A-13 can state which enclosure its verdict used.
- No symplectic or energy claim is made for an articulated tree, whose mass matrix depends on configuration.
