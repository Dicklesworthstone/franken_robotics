## Background

The airlock crate `frob-fsim` exposes FrankenSim to FrankenRobotics through traits grouped into Cargo features.
The `optimize` group is off by default and holds three traits, `Optimizer`, `Archive` and `Racing`, over the
FrankenSim crates `fs-dfo`, `fs-bo`, `fs-ascent`, `fs-archive`, `fs-opt`, `fs-sos`, `fs-race`, `fs-eproc`,
`fs-tropical` and `fs-symmetry`. Enabling it adds 12 crates and about 84 thousand lines beyond what M1 already
compiles (FACT, computed from manifests; re-read at the pin).

No named admission gate exists for this group as a whole. It is governed by the standing rule: **an admission
is never inherited; a crate first used at a milestone is admitted at that milestone, by its own record, at the
pin of that day**, in the form of the FrankenSim path gate applied to a group (the group compiles from this
repository behind its feature, one known answer passes through the airlock trait, one negative control fails).
The group is first needed at M1, by the two study types of the evaluation subsystem, which use FrankenSim's
anytime-valid statistics ("the statistics the two study types use, admitted when first used"). This bead
therefore admits the statistical half at M1: the trait `Racing` over `fs-eproc` (e-processes and confidence
sequences) and `fs-race` (races over several candidates). The optimizer half is bead N33a-31 at M3.

FACT about the two crates (read 2026-11-21; re-read at the pin):
- `fs-eproc` requires an explicit `LossSpan` for a betting e-process and states that it "must not be estimated
  from the race data"; it provides mixture confidence sequences, a likelihood-mixture confidence sequence for a
  Bernoulli rate (`bernoulli.rs`, a Beta(½, ½) mixture), `combine_average` (the average of e-values is an
  e-value under arbitrary dependence), `combine_product` (valid only for independent comparisons) and
  `e_benjamini_hochberg` (false discovery rate at most α under arbitrary dependence). Its Bernoulli sequence has
  a sample cap of 10⁶.
- `fs-race::race_field` uses a mixture construction: rounds are the only clock, every survivor consumes one
  observation per round in index order, a candidate's elimination evidence is the average of the pairwise
  e-processes against all original opponents, and an elimination needs that mixture to reach at least 1/α, so
  the true best is eliminated with probability at most α (`fs-race/CONTRACT.md`). `successive_halving` returns
  a bracket ledger whose kills are rank-based.

The mathematics served is construction C-15, evidence as a test martingale: a nonnegative process with
expectation at most 1 under the null, so that rejecting when it exceeds 1/α has error at most α at any stopping
time. That "any stopping time" is what lets a comparison stop early without inflating its error.

Three thin entries of the same feature have no trait in the group table and are owned by the beads that admit
them: `fs-sos` (probed for mission work; no value from it enters a certificate), `fs-symmetry` (cyclic
reference cases, admission gate INT-FSIM-10) and `fs-tropical` (max-plus cycle means for gait timing).

## Technical approach

**Trait (DESIGN: intent, not signatures; module `frob_fsim::optimize::racing`, feature `optimize`).**

```rust
pub trait Racing {
    fn eprocess(&self, span: LossSpan, alpha: Rational, null: NullSpec) -> Result<EProcess, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn observe(&self, e: &mut EProcess, paired_difference: f64) -> Result<EState, RefereeRefusal>;  // in address order
    fn bernoulli_sequence(&self, alpha: Rational) -> Result<BernoulliSeq, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn observe_bernoulli(&self, s: &mut BernoulliSeq, success: bool) -> Result<Interval01, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn combine_average(&self, evalues: &[f64]) -> f64;
    fn e_benjamini_hochberg(&self, evalues: &[f64], alpha: Rational) -> Vec<bool>;
    fn race_field(&self, candidates: u32, span: LossSpan, alpha: Rational) -> Result<Race, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn race_round(&self, r: &mut Race, observations: &[f64]) -> Result<RoundOutcome, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn limits(&self) -> RacingLimits;      // sample caps read at the pin (10⁶ for the Bernoulli sequence at reading)
}
pub struct EState { pub e_value: f64, pub n: u64, pub crossed: bool, pub conf_seq: (f64, f64) }
```

**Rules the airlock adds (DESIGN).**
1. *The loss span is an input, fixed before data.* `eprocess` and `race_field` take the span as an argument
   that the caller derives from the task definition; there is no entry point that estimates it. A span of zero,
   a non-finite span or an observation outside the span is a typed refusal.
2. *Observations arrive in address order.* The adapter does not sort or buffer; the caller's contract (a
   study's statistic is a pure function of per-address outcomes taken in address order) is what makes the
   e-value independent of completion order. The adapter records the count it has consumed so that a caller can
   prove it fed a contiguous prefix.
3. *Caps are data.* `limits()` returns the sample caps read from the sibling at the pin. A request that would
   exceed a cap is refused by name before the upstream call, with the cap as `required`; the caller then uses
   this project's own exact bound for larger counts. No silent restart of a sequence at the cap.
4. *`combine_product` is not exposed.* The product of e-values is valid only for independent comparisons, and
   comparisons on shared worlds are not independent; only the average and the e-value Benjamini–Hochberg rule
   are offered.
5. *State is serializable.* `EProcess`, `BernoulliSeq` and `Race` round-trip through canonical bytes owned by
   the airlock, so that a preempted study resumes with a bit-identical state.
6. Every upstream error goes through the conversion of bead N33a-13.

7. *One trait.* `Racing` is the only airlock surface for these two crates, in module
   `frob_fsim::optimize::racing`; the admission test of N33b-12 reaches `fs-eproc` through it. The null
   specification carries the margin and the identifier of the betting rule, and a process cannot be constructed
   without a span (there is no default), which N33b-12 checks with a compile-fail test.

**Admission record (M1).** `covers.groups = ["optimize"]`, `covers.crates = ["fs-race"]` (an added M1 row of INT-FSIM-14; the `fs-eproc` row is written by N33b-12), with the
known answers and the controls below, citing the stack receipt of the day, written by this bead with N33a-07's
command (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example admission -- record --gate INT-FSIM-14 …`) from the seeded
run of the tests below; the step `optimize_racing_admission` of N33a-34 re-runs them and records again at its
own pin. This bead writes no row of
`registries/gates.toml`: the M1 part of INT-FSIM-14 is entered by N33b-12 with `record_gate` and the M3 part by
N33b-13 (one recorder per gate part); the record here is an admission record of N33a-07's form. The `optimize`
feature is enabled only by the crates listed for it in the airlock's group registry.

Determinism class: D0 given the observation sequence (the statistics are deterministic functions of their
inputs). Evidence: an e-value is a statistic; the claim it supports is made by the study, not by the airlock.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `Racing` compiles under the `optimize` feature with no volatile type in its public face; a default build has no `fs-eproc` in its graph.
- [ ] Null calibration: over 10⁴ simulated studies with paired differences drawn symmetric about zero inside the span, each stopped at the first crossing or at 2,000 pairs, the share that cross 1/α for α = 0.05 is at most 0.05 plus a binomial acceptance margin (TARGET: at most 0.0569, the exact one-sided 99.9% limit: for X ~ Binomial(10⁴, 0.05), P(X > 569) = 0.00088 ≤ 0.001 < P(X > 568), so up to 569 crossings are accepted; the normal approximation 0.05 + 3.09·√(0.05·0.95/10⁴) = 0.0567 is slightly lower because the binomial is skewed to the right; the test computes the exact limit).
- [ ] Power sanity: with a true mean difference of 0.2 of the span and standard deviation 0.25 of the span, at least 95% of 10³ studies cross within 2,000 pairs.
- [ ] `combine_average` of (4, 0.5, 1.5) returns 2; the Benjamini–Hochberg e-value rule at α = 0.05 on (50, 30, 1) rejects the first two and not the third.
- [ ] A null race of 81 candidates, each replay run for 2,000 rounds or until one candidate remains, eliminates the designated best in at most 42 of 512 replays at α = 0.05 (the exact one-sided limit at test level 0.001: for X ~ Binomial(512, 0.05), P(X > 42) = 0.00076 ≤ 0.001 < P(X > 41) = 0.0014; the test computes it).
- [ ] A span estimated from the data cannot be passed (no such entry point); an observation outside the span is refused; a request past the Bernoulli cap is refused with the cap named.
- [ ] A preempted and resumed e-process ends with the bit-identical e-value of an uninterrupted one.
- [ ] Each of the three planted controls is caught by its named test.
- [ ] The M1 admission record for `fs-race` (gate INT-FSIM-14) is written by this bead and validates; the `fs-eproc` record is N33b-12's and is not written twice.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-fsim --features optimize optimize::racing`)
- `combine_average_known`: (4 + 0.5 + 1.5)/3 = 2.
- `e_bh_known`: m = 3, α = 0.05: sorted e-values 50, 30, 1; the rule rejects the k largest where k is the largest index with e₍ₖ₎ ≥ m/(α·k): thresholds 60, 30, 20 → k = 2 (30 ≥ 30), so two rejections.
- `null_calibration_10k` (seeded, runs under the gate command): crossings ≤ 569 of 10⁴ (share ≤ 0.0569, the exact binomial limit computed in the test, not copied).
- `power_sanity_1k`.
- `race_null_81_over_512`: 81 candidates with identically distributed observations, 2,000 rounds per replay or until one remains, 512 seeded replays; the designated best eliminated in at most 42 (the exact limit computed in the test).
- `planted_halved_span_caught`, `planted_single_crossing_caught`, `planted_fixed_sample_caught`: the three negative controls below, each run through the same harness as the known answer it breaks, must break it.
- `span_outside_refused`, `span_zero_refused`, `cap_refused_by_name`.
- `no_product_combination_exposed`: the public API listing contains no product combiner.
- `resume_bit_identical`: serialize after 137 observations, deserialize, continue; final state equals the uninterrupted run bit for bit.
- `order_matters_is_documented`: feeding the same 100 observations in two orders gives different intermediate e-values and the adapter's consumed-prefix counter identifies the order used (guards the address-order contract).
- `bernoulli_sequence_covers`: for p = 0.1 over 10³ seeded sequences of length 10⁴, the running interval contains 0.1 at every step in at least 95% of sequences at α = 0.05.

### End-to-end script and logging
No script of its own; the capability is observed through the study verbs (`study.plan`, `study.submit`,
`study.status`, on BD-124's engines) whose script `scripts/e2e/study_two_types.sh` belongs to bead N33b-19. The
admission step `optimize_racing_admission` of
`scripts/e2e/airlock_groups.sh` (bead N33a-34) runs the calibration tests with explicit seeds, writes the
record and logs the crossing share against 0.0569, the power share against 0.95, the race elimination count
against its bound and each control's outcome.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the arithmetic of the two combiners; null calibration; the null race.
- Negative controls, planted adapters in `frob_fsim::conformance` (N33a-11's module, gated on `optimize`): (1) `planted::halved-span`, an e-process whose span is halved after the fact (a wrapper that rescales observations), must exceed the null crossing bound of `null_calibration_10k`; (2) `planted::single-crossing`, an elimination rule that fires on any single pairwise crossing, must eliminate the true best more than 42 times in `race_null_81_over_512`; (3) `planted::fixed-sample`, optional stopping with a fixed-sample test substituted for the e-process, must exceed the crossing bound under the null.
- Counterexample protected: an elimination on any single pairwise crossing runs each comparison at α/(N − 1), not α.
- Determinism lanes: all seeded statistics are bit-identical twice on one machine; across platforms they are identical if the sibling's arithmetic is (recorded, not required).
