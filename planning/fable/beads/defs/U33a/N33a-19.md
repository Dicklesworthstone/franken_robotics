## Background

The airlock crate `frob-fsim` exposes FrankenSim to FrankenRobotics through traits grouped into Cargo features.
The `actuation` group is off by default and holds four traits, `Magnetics`, `ThermalNetwork`, `CurrentLoop`
and `PortHamiltonian`, over the FrankenSim crates `fs-em`, `fs-thermal`, `fs-control`, `fs-phs` and `fs-matdb`.
Enabling it adds 2 crates and about 151 thousand lines beyond the M1 set (FACT; re-read at the pin). It is
enabled only by the actuator crate `frob-actuate`.

What it serves. Actuators in FrankenRobotics are port-Hamiltonian components with a thermal node (construction
C-38). A component with stored energy H(x) obeys ẋ = (J(x) − R(x))·∇H(x) + G(x)·u and y = G(x)ᵀ·∇H(x), with J
skew-symmetric and R positive semidefinite, so Ḣ = uᵀy − ∇HᵀR∇H: stored energy changes by port power minus
dissipation. A motor axis has flux linkage φ = L·i and rotor momentum p = J_r·ω as states, energy
H = φ²/(2L) + p²/(2J_r), a gyrator of modulus k between them (torque constant and back-EMF constant are equal
in SI units) and dissipation R_w·i² + b·ω²: L·di/dt = V − R_w·i − k·ω and J_r·ω̇ = k·i − b·ω − τ_load. The
thermal node has temperature T_w and heat capacity C with C·Ṫ_w = P_loss − G_th·(T_w − T_amb), where P_loss
includes the copper loss i²·R₀·(1 + α·(T_w − T₀)).

FACT about the siblings (read 2026-11-21; re-read at the pin): `fs-phs` (66,221 lines of library source, no
dependency on the units crate) offers port-Hamiltonian composition and discrete-gradient stepping; its
constrained interconnections are deferred upstream, so closed-loop mechanisms do not rely on it; it has two
transformer entry points, `transformer` and `interconnect_mapped` (`fs-phs/src/interconnection.rs:91`), with
**opposite signs of the driven direction**, both conserving power (reported rates (12, −8) against (−12, 8)).
`fs-control` covers sampled motor current loops only (its manifest description). `fs-thermal` and `fs-matdb`
exist; `fs-matdb` returns material data with provenance.

Design consequences: only `interconnect_mapped` is reached, with the authored sign passed explicitly, because
a sign that conserves power but drives the wrong way is invisible to an energy check; and `fs-control` is
wrapped as a current loop and nothing more, since this project's controllers are its own.

Admission is never inherited. `fs-phs` and `fs-matdb` are admitted by INT-FSIM-09 (the seed BD-068, through
its own `PortHamiltonian` trait and its friction-law and material-data adapters, none of which is defined
here). `fs-em`, `fs-thermal` (as a directly used crate) and `fs-control` have no named gate and
are admitted here by their own record at first use, in the form of the FrankenSim path gate applied to a
group: the group compiles from this repository behind its feature, one known answer passes through each
airlock trait, one negative control fails.

## Scope
In: the three traits `Magnetics`, `ThermalNetwork` and `CurrentLoop` with in-process adapters over `fs-em`,
`fs-thermal` and `fs-control`; the estimate-class rule and the entropy check; the admission record for those
three crates; closed-form known answers and controls.
Out: `PortHamiltonian` over `fs-phs`, with the single-entry-point sign rule, the step audit and the refusal of
constrained interconnections, and the friction-law and material-data adapters (the seed BD-068, which owns them
together with the gate INT-FSIM-09; the fourth trait of this draft, its known answers and its tests are folded
there; BD-068 is a dependency, because the adapters here read a material only through its `MaterialData` and
never reach `fs-matdb` themselves, which would be an inherited admission); the M0 probe that fixes the sign
rule and writes the one transformer adapter (N33c-43, a dependency of BD-068: no trait of this bead joins
two ports, so this bead adds no transformer entry); actuator, battery and transmission cards and the
actuator levels (the actuator subsystem); the energy ledger; the batched actuator kernels, which are this
project's own code and never call FrankenSim.

## Technical approach

**Traits (DESIGN: intent, not signatures; module `frob_fsim::actuation`, feature `actuation`).**

```rust
// `PortHamiltonian` over fs-phs is specified by BD-068, the seed that owns INT-FSIM-09.
pub trait CurrentLoop {
    fn sampled_loop(&self, motor: &MotorCardView, period: Rational, law: LoopLaw) -> Result<LoopHandle, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn tick(&self, l: &mut LoopHandle, i_meas: f64, i_ref: f64, omega: f64) -> Result<f64 /* volts */, RefereeRefusal>;
}
pub trait ThermalNetwork {
    fn network(&self, nodes: &[ThermalNode], links: &[ThermalLink]) -> Result<ThermalHandle, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn step(&self, n: &mut ThermalHandle, losses_w: &[f64], t_amb_k: f64, h: Rational) -> Result<Vec<f64>, RefereeRefusal>;
}
pub trait Magnetics {
    fn torque_constant(&self, geom: &MotorGeometryView) -> Result<Estimated<f64>, RefereeRefusal>;   // N·m/A, with its evidence class
}
```

**Rules.**
1. to 3. The rules of `PortHamiltonian` (one transformer entry point with an explicit authored sign; energy
   bookkeeping returned by the step; constrained interconnections refused) are specified and tested in BD-068.
4. *`Magnetics` returns an estimate with its class.* A value derived from geometry is `Estimated`; a card value
   measured on a bench outranks it. The adapter never upgrades a class.
5. *Thermal steps use the node model above.* Temperature is the state; the adapter reports per step the heat
   leaving to ambient so that the entropy production σ = P_loss/T_w + G_th·(T_w − T_amb)²/(T_w·T_amb) can be
   checked nonnegative by the caller, and so that the node's first law can be checked: over any run,
   C·(T_w(end) − T_w(start)) = Σ (P_loss − Q̇_ambient)·h.
6. Every upstream error goes through the conversion of bead N33a-13. A material is read only through
   BD-068's `MaterialData` adapter; a missing material or pair is that adapter's refusal, passed on unchanged
   and never replaced by a default.

**Admission record (M1).** `covers.groups = ["actuation"]`, `covers.crates = ["fs-em", "fs-thermal",
"fs-control"]`, with the known answers and controls below, citing the stack receipt of the day.

Determinism class: same platform, same binary. Evidence: outputs carry the class FrankenSim's contracts give;
`Magnetics` outputs are `Estimated`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three traits compile under the `actuation` feature with no volatile type in their public face; a default build has no `fs-em`, `fs-thermal` or `fs-control` in its graph.
- [ ] Thermal steady state: constant loss 20 W, G_th = 0.5 W/K, T_amb = 293.15 K → T_w tends to 293.15 + 40 = 333.15 K; with C = 100 J/K the time constant is C/G_th = 200 s and T_w(200 s) = 293.15 + 40·(1 − e⁻¹) = 318.43 K to 10⁻³ relative.
- [ ] The thermal node's first law closes through the trait: stored heat C·ΔT_w equals supplied loss minus the reported heat to ambient to 10⁻⁹ relative, and the double-count control (2) is caught by `thermal_node_first_law`.
- [ ] Runaway threshold (closed form checked through the trait with temperature-dependent copper loss): R₀ = 0.1 Ω, α = 0.0039 K⁻¹, G_th = 0.5 W/K gives the critical current √(G_th/(R₀·α)) = √(0.5/0.00039) = 35.81 A; at 30 A the node settles and at 40 A it grows.
- [ ] `CurrentLoop` reproduces the independently computed five-tick sequence of the fixture loop; `Magnetics` returns `Estimated` values that scale linearly with the turn count and never displaces a bench-measured card value.
- [ ] The admission record for `fs-em`, `fs-thermal` and `fs-control` exists and validates, with one known answer and one caught control per trait.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-fsim --features actuation actuation`)
- `thermal_steady_state` and `thermal_time_constant`: 333.15 K and 318.43 K.
- `thermal_runaway_threshold`: with a = (i²·R₀·α − G_th)/C, a < 0 at 30 A (0.351 − 0.5 < 0) and a > 0 at 40 A (0.624 − 0.5 > 0); the simulated trend has the sign of a.
- `entropy_production_nonnegative`: σ ≥ 0 at every step of the thermal fixture.
- `thermal_node_first_law`: on the steady-state fixture (20 W, G_th = 0.5 W/K, C = 100 J/K, from 293.15 K)
  over 200 s the stored heat C·ΔT_w equals the summed (P_loss − Q̇_ambient)·h reported by the adapter, the two
  simulated sums agreeing to 10⁻⁹ relative. Closed-form values, met to 10⁻³: stored 100·25.285 = 2,528.5 J; of
  the 4,000 J supplied, 1,471.5 J has left to ambient. Control (2) below fails it by the stored 2,528.5 J.
- `current_loop_tick_matches_reference`: a proportional-integral loop with the gains of the fixture card reproduces a hand-stepped sequence of five ticks stored with the test (the reference is computed by an independent script from the loop's difference equation).
- `magnetics_class_is_estimated`: `torque_constant` on the fixture geometry returns a value whose evidence class is `Estimated`; a motor card carrying a bench-measured constant keeps its measured value and class when the estimate is requested beside it.
- `magnetics_turns_scaling`: doubling the turn count of the fixture winding, all else equal, doubles the torque constant to 10⁻¹² relative (k is proportional to turns), and the value is finite and positive.
- `missing_material_pair_refused`: a thermal link or winding material absent from `fs-matdb`, asked for through BD-068's `MaterialData`, is a typed refusal naming the pair, never a default.

### End-to-end script and logging
No script of its own; the capability is observed through the actuator verbs of the actuator subsystem. The
admission step `actuation_group_admission` of `scripts/e2e/airlock_groups.sh` (bead N33a-34) runs the tests,
writes the record and logs each closed-form value with its measured value, unit, tolerance and control result.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Closed forms: the thermal node's steady state and time constant; the critical current 35.81 A.
- Negative controls: (1) a regenerative mode with a negated copper loss gives σ < 0 and must be rejected; (2) a ledger that books heat entering the node as both stored and lost shows a deficit equal to that heat and must be flagged (planted here as an adapter that also reports the stored heat as heat to ambient; `thermal_node_first_law` fails by the stored 2,528.5 J); (3) a `Magnetics` adapter that labels a geometry-derived value `Measured` (planted class upgrade) must fail `magnetics_class_is_estimated`; (4) a current loop with its integral term dropped (planted) must fail `current_loop_tick_matches_reference` from the second tick on. (The reversed-sign direction control and the explicit-Euler control of `PortHamiltonian` moved with that trait to BD-068.)
- Metamorphic relation: scaling voltage by s scales the locked-rotor current by s and the loss by s².
- Determinism lanes: bit-identical twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- Lumped thermal parameters are card values, `Estimated` unless calibrated; the second-law check has content only as a check on the sign of each assigned loss.
- The batched tiers never call these traits; they are the referee-side reference against which the actuator kernels are checked.
- Tolerances are TARGETs until recorded; the scheme's order and tolerance are read from the sibling's contract at the pin.
