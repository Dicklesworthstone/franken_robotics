## Background

The airlock crate `frob-fsim` exposes FrankenSim to FrankenRobotics through traits grouped into Cargo features.
The `actuation` group is off by default and holds four traits, `Magnetics`, `ThermalNetwork`, `CurrentLoop`
and `PortHamiltonian`, over the FrankenSim crates `fs-em`, `fs-thermal`, `fs-control`, `fs-phs` and `fs-matdb`.
Enabling it adds 2 crates and about 151 thousand lines beyond the M1 set (FACT; re-read at the pin). It is
enabled only by the actuator crate `frob-actuate`.

What it serves. Actuators in FrankenRobotics are port-Hamiltonian components with a thermal node (construction
C-38). A component with stored energy H(x) obeys ẋ = (J(x) − R(x))·∇H(x) + G(x)·u and y = G(x)ᵀ·∇H(x), with J
skew-symmetric and R positive semidefinite, so Ḣ = uᵀy − ∇HᵀR∇H: stored energy changes by port power minus
dissipation. A motor axis has flux linkage φ = L·i and rotor momentum p = J_r·ω as states, energy
H = φ²/(2L) + p²/(2J_r), a gyrator of modulus k between them (torque constant and back-EMF constant are equal
in SI units) and dissipation R_w·i² + b·ω²: L·di/dt = V − R_w·i − k·ω and J_r·ω̇ = k·i − b·ω − τ_load. The
thermal node has temperature T_w and heat capacity C with C·Ṫ_w = P_loss − G_th·(T_w − T_amb), where P_loss
includes the copper loss i²·R₀·(1 + α·(T_w − T₀)).

FACT about the siblings (read 2026-11-21; re-read at the pin): `fs-phs` (66,221 lines of library source, no
dependency on the units crate) offers port-Hamiltonian composition and discrete-gradient stepping; its
constrained interconnections are deferred upstream, so closed-loop mechanisms do not rely on it; it has two
transformer entry points, `transformer` and `interconnect_mapped` (`fs-phs/src/interconnection.rs:91`), with
**opposite signs of the driven direction**, both conserving power (reported rates (12, −8) against (−12, 8)).
`fs-control` covers sampled motor current loops only (its manifest description). `fs-thermal` and `fs-matdb`
exist; `fs-matdb` returns material data with provenance.

Design consequences: only `interconnect_mapped` is reached, with the authored sign passed explicitly, because
a sign that conserves power but drives the wrong way is invisible to an energy check; and `fs-control` is
wrapped as a current loop and nothing more, since this project's controllers are its own.

Admission is never inherited. `fs-phs` and `fs-matdb` are admitted by INT-FSIM-09 (another bead, through the
traits defined here). `fs-em`, `fs-thermal` (as a directly used crate) and `fs-control` have no named gate and
are admitted here by their own record at first use, in the form of the FrankenSim path gate applied to a
group: the group compiles from this repository behind its feature, one known answer passes through each
airlock trait, one negative control fails.

## Scope

In: the four traits with in-process adapters; the single-entry-point sign rule; the admission record for
`fs-em`, `fs-thermal` and `fs-control`; closed-form known answers and controls.
Out: actuator, battery and transmission cards and the actuator levels (the actuator subsystem); the energy
ledger; INT-FSIM-09 itself (BD-068); the signed-motion witness gate (another unit); the batched actuator
kernels, which are this project's own code and never call FrankenSim.

## Technical approach

**Traits (DESIGN: intent, not signatures; module `frob_fsim::actuation`, feature `actuation`).**

```rust
pub trait PortHamiltonian {
    fn component(&self, spec: &PhsSpec) -> Result<PhsHandle, RefereeRefusal>;          // H, J, R, G as data
    fn interconnect(&self, a: &PhsHandle, b: &PhsHandle, map: &PortMap, sign: AuthoredSign) -> Result<PhsHandle, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn step_discrete_gradient(&self, sys: &PhsHandle, x: &[f64], u: &[f64], h: Rational) -> Result<PhsStep, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn energy(&self, sys: &PhsHandle, x: &[f64]) -> Result<f64, RefereeRefusal>;        // joules
}
pub enum AuthoredSign { Same, Reversed }      // the driven direction as the design states it; never defaulted
pub struct PhsStep { pub x_next: Vec<f64>, pub y: Vec<f64>, pub dissipated: f64, pub port_power: f64 }

pub trait CurrentLoop {
    fn sampled_loop(&self, motor: &MotorCardView, period: Rational, law: LoopLaw) -> Result<LoopHandle, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn tick(&self, l: &mut LoopHandle, i_meas: f64, i_ref: f64, omega: f64) -> Result<f64 /* volts */, RefereeRefusal>;
}
pub trait ThermalNetwork {
    fn network(&self, nodes: &[ThermalNode], links: &[ThermalLink]) -> Result<ThermalHandle, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn step(&self, n: &mut ThermalHandle, losses_w: &[f64], t_amb_k: f64, h: Rational) -> Result<Vec<f64>, RefereeRefusal>;
}
pub trait Magnetics {
    fn torque_constant(&self, geom: &MotorGeometryView) -> Result<Estimated<f64>, RefereeRefusal>;   // N·m/A, with its evidence class
}
```

**Rules.**
1. *One transformer entry point.* The adapter calls `interconnect_mapped` and never `transformer`. `sign` has
   no default: a call site must state the authored direction, and the adapter passes it explicitly.
2. *Energy bookkeeping is returned, not inferred.* `PhsStep` carries the dissipated energy and the port power
   of the step as reported by the discrete-gradient scheme, so that the caller's ledger closes from data:
   H(x_next) − H(x) = h·port_power − dissipated, to the scheme's stated tolerance.
3. *Constrained interconnections are refused.* A `PortMap` that implies an algebraic constraint is a typed
   refusal naming the constraint (the foundation defers them); closed-loop mechanisms use this project's own
   rows.
4. *`Magnetics` returns an estimate with its class.* A value derived from geometry is `Estimated`; a card value
   measured on a bench outranks it. The adapter never upgrades a class.
5. *Thermal steps use the node model above.* Temperature is the state; the adapter reports per step the heat
   leaving to ambient so that the entropy production σ = P_loss/T_w + G_th·(T_w − T_amb)²/(T_w·T_amb) can be
   checked nonnegative by the caller.
6. Every upstream error goes through the conversion of bead N33a-13; a missing material pair is a refusal,
   never a default.

**Admission record (M1).** `covers.groups = ["actuation"]`, `covers.crates = ["fs-em", "fs-thermal",
"fs-control"]`, with the known answers and controls below, citing the stack receipt of the day.

Determinism class: same platform, same binary. Evidence: outputs carry the class FrankenSim's contracts give;
`Magnetics` outputs are `Estimated`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four traits compile under the `actuation` feature with no volatile type in their public face; a default build has no `fs-phs` or `fs-em` in its graph.
- [ ] Electrical step: a locked rotor with R_w = 1 Ω, L = 10 mH and V = 12 V has current (V/R_w)·(1 − e^(−t/τ)) with τ = L/R_w = 10 ms; at t = 10 ms the current is 12·(1 − e⁻¹) = 7.5854 A, matched to 10⁻³ relative at h = 1/48,000 s (TARGET) and converging at the scheme's order under step halving.
- [ ] Energy balance per step: H(x_next) − H(x) − h·port_power + dissipated is zero to 10⁻¹² relative of the energy scale, over 10⁴ steps of the motor fixture.
- [ ] Thermal steady state: constant loss 20 W, G_th = 0.5 W/K, T_amb = 293.15 K → T_w tends to 293.15 + 40 = 333.15 K; with C = 100 J/K the time constant is C/G_th = 200 s and T_w(200 s) = 293.15 + 40·(1 − e⁻¹) = 318.43 K to 10⁻³ relative.
- [ ] Runaway threshold (closed form checked through the trait with temperature-dependent copper loss): R₀ = 0.1 Ω, α = 0.0039 K⁻¹, G_th = 0.5 W/K gives the critical current √(G_th/(R₀·α)) = √(0.5/0.00039) = 35.81 A; at 30 A the node settles and at 40 A it grows.
- [ ] Sign rule: interconnecting with `AuthoredSign::Reversed` drives the opposite direction and conserves power in both cases; a build that calls the legacy `transformer` entry point is rejected by a source scan.
- [ ] A constrained interconnection and a missing material pair are each refused by name.
- [ ] The admission record for `fs-em`, `fs-thermal` and `fs-control` exists and validates.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-fsim --features actuation actuation`)
- `rl_step_response`: i(10 ms) = 7.5854 A (12 × 0.63212); i(50 ms) = 12·(1 − e⁻⁵) = 11.919 A.
- `rl_step_order`: halving h reduces the error at t = 10 ms by the factor the scheme's order predicts (recorded; at least 3.5 for a second-order scheme).
- `phs_energy_balance_closes`: residual ≤ 10⁻¹² relative over 10⁴ steps with a time-varying voltage.
- `phs_passive_when_unforced`: with u = 0 the stored energy never increases over 10⁴ steps.
- `thermal_steady_state` and `thermal_time_constant`: 333.15 K and 318.43 K.
- `thermal_runaway_threshold`: with a = (i²·R₀·α − G_th)/C, a < 0 at 30 A (0.351 − 0.5 < 0) and a > 0 at 40 A (0.624 − 0.5 > 0); the simulated trend has the sign of a.
- `entropy_production_nonnegative`: σ ≥ 0 at every step of the thermal fixture.
- `sign_reversed_drives_opposite`: a gear of ratio 3 driven at flow f₁ = 4: `Same` gives f₂ = 12, `Reversed` gives f₂ = −12; in both the port powers sum to zero to 10⁻¹².
- `legacy_transformer_not_called` (source scan).
- `constrained_interconnection_refused`, `missing_material_pair_refused`.
- `current_loop_tick_matches_reference`: a proportional-integral loop with the gains of the fixture card reproduces a hand-stepped sequence of five ticks stored with the test (the reference is computed by an independent script from the loop's difference equation).

### End-to-end script and logging
No script of its own; the capability is observed through the actuator verbs of the actuator subsystem. The
admission step `actuation_group_admission` of `scripts/e2e/airlock_groups.sh` (bead N33a-34) runs the tests,
writes the record and logs each closed-form value with its measured value, unit, tolerance and control result.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Closed forms: the RL step (τ = 10 ms); the thermal node's steady state and time constant; the critical current 35.81 A.
- Negative controls: (1) a regenerative mode with a negated copper loss gives σ < 0 and must be rejected; (2) a ledger that books heat entering the node as both stored and lost shows a deficit equal to that heat and must be flagged; (3) the reversed sign passed where `Same` was authored must fail the direction test although power still balances; (4) an explicit-Euler replacement of the discrete-gradient step (planted) must fail `phs_passive_when_unforced` on a stiff fixture.
- Metamorphic relation: scaling voltage by s scales the locked-rotor current by s and the loss by s².
- Determinism lanes: bit-identical twice on one machine.
