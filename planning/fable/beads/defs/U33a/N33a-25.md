## Background

FrankenRobotics will ingest real places (a home, a kitchen, a plant) as scene packs, from two capture
pipelines of the owner: a walkthrough-video route that yields a **twin package** (a Blender twin with stable
feature identifiers, a GLB export and a plan to link visual, geometric and traversability representations;
`franken_surveillance_system`), and a drone-video route (`franken_drone_geometry_reconstruction`) that today
stops at an audited relative bundle problem and does not yet optimize cameras, emit metres or publish
geometry (FACT, its status file, snapshot 2026-09-04), although the schemas for what it will publish exist
(`schemas/geometry_generation.schema.json` with the required fields `coordinate_frame`, `scale_status`,
`representations`, `uncertainty_root`, `coverage_root` and `publication_stage`;
`schemas/scale_model.schema.json` with a scale authority and residuals in parts per million).

Ingestion is a Frontier mechanism, so it has a pilot with a falsifier that runs before the importer is funded.
The design names two paper-scale checks, and neither needs any geometry:

1. *The scan-import lane* (foundation row `FD-FDGR`): "the schema fixtures and the refusal cases". This is
   also the M3 part of the admission gate INT-FDGR-01, which the gate register dates "M3 for the schema
   fixtures; M5 for scan import".
2. *The twin-package lane*: "take one existing twin package and check by inspection that it exports what
   registration and unknown regions need (four non-coplanar landmarks, a scale witness, coverage or
   confidence per region); if it does not, the first deliverable is an upstream work item (FD-FSS-01) and not
   an importer."

**Ceiling, computed before the test:** ingestion has no effect on decision time. Its whole value is that a
candidate can be evaluated in one particular place, the precondition for a Reality claim about that place. It
is one import lane among several, and no milestone waits for a sibling to publish geometry. If both lanes fail
their pilots, the fallback already exists: a place authored in the interior grammar from a tape-measure
survey, labelled a proxy.

## Scope

In: the frozen protocol for both checks; the fixture documents; a throwaway validator and mapping table; the
inspection checklist and its result; the pilot record; the M3 admission record for INT-FDGR-01's schema part;
the recommendation for the importer and for FD-FSS-01.
Out: the importer and the schema adapter in `frob-scene` (BD-398, which depends on this pilot); any
reconstruction; any change in a sibling.

## Technical approach

**Pilot record (written to the pilot register before execution).** Mechanism: real-place ingestion, Frontier.
Tools and pins: the drone project's schemas at its revision of the day (head `db45703afc5e` on the day of
reading); one twin package produced by the walkthrough route, with its producing revision. Cost: at most one
agent-day, no device. Kill gate: KILL-INGEST (decided at M5 with the importer). Fallback: a surveyed proxy in
the interior grammar; for the scan lane, mesh and point-cloud import with the scale declared by the operator
and `reality: no-claim`.

**Part A: schema fixtures and refusal cases (frozen protocol).**
1. Copy the two schema files from the sibling at its pin into a scratch area with their hashes recorded.
2. Author six fixture documents under `tests/fixtures/fdgr/` (they describe no real place; labelled
   `synthetic`):
   - A1 valid, `scale_status = surveyed`, coverage and uncertainty present, a published stage;
   - A2 valid, `witnessed`;
   - A3 schema-valid, `estimated`;
   - A4 schema-valid, `relative_only`;
   - A5 schema-invalid: `coverage_root` missing;
   - A6 schema-valid with a `publication_stage` that is not a published stage.
3. Validate each against the schemas with an independent JSON-schema validator.
4. Apply the mapping table (the adapter's contract, here executed by a throwaway script):
   `scale_status` → scale label; a label below metric (`relative_only`) is **refused for a physics scene**
   (`FROB-REF-304`: scale authority is below what the task requires); `estimated` is admitted for exploratory
   runs only; `coverage_root` → the unknown mask, with every region lacking coverage labelled **unknown, never
   free**; `uncertainty_root` → registration uncertainty; an unpublished stage → refused; the result always
   carries the private mark.
5. Coverage semantics check on A1: its coverage map leaves half of a corridor unobserved; the mapped mask must
   mark that half unknown, and a clearance query phrased over it must come back `unresolved` in the script's
   three-valued logic (blocked for a witness, free for a cut).
Expected outcomes: A1 and A2 import as metric; A3 imports as exploratory; A4 is refused with `FROB-REF-304`;
A5 fails validation; A6 is refused as unpublished.

**Part B: twin-package inspection (frozen checklist).** For one existing twin package, answer from its files
alone: (1) does it carry at least four landmarks with positions, and are four of them non-coplanar (the
tetrahedron's volume at least 1% of the cube of the largest separation)? (2) does it carry a scale witness (an
object of known size or a measured baseline) with its uncertainty? (3) does it carry coverage or confidence
per region, so that unobserved space can be told from free space? (4) is the handedness and up axis of its
export stated (a glTF camera looks along local −Z with +Y up; a parity change must be excludable)? Each answer
is `present`, `absent` or `ambiguous`, with the file and field.

**Falsifiers.** Part A: any expected outcome above does not hold with the sibling's schemas as they are (for
example the schema cannot express per-region coverage, or admits a document without a scale status). Then the
scan lane is not built as designed and the finding is attached to FD-FDGR-01. Part B: any of items 1 to 3 is
`absent`. Then the first deliverable is the upstream work item FD-FSS-01 (four non-coplanar landmarks, a scale
witness, coverage or confidence per region in the twin package), not an importer, and the interim is the
surveyed proxy.

**Result record and admission record.** The pilot record gets the outcomes of both parts and a
recommendation per lane: `fund the importer on this lane`, or `upstream first`. The M3 admission record for
INT-FDGR-01 (schema part) cites the schema hashes, the six outcomes and the caught controls; `covers.tool`
names the sibling's schemas at the pinned revision.

Evidence class: `Tested` on synthetic fixtures; nothing here is a Reality claim.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot record exists with protocol, falsifiers, ceiling, fallback, cost, tools and pins written before the run, and both results after it.
- [ ] Part A: the six fixtures give exactly the expected outcomes; the half-corridor of A1 maps to unknown and the query returns `unresolved`.
- [ ] Part B: the four checklist items are answered for one real twin package, with file and field for each.
- [ ] An M3 admission record for the schema part of INT-FDGR-01 exists and validates.
- [ ] A recommendation per lane is recorded; BD-398 starts on a lane only with `fund the importer on this lane`, and otherwise its first deliverable is the upstream item.
- [ ] The inspected twin package is treated as private: the record holds answers and field names, no geometry and no images.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (the throwaway mapping script's own assertions, `scripts/pilots/fdgr_fixtures_test.py` or equivalent, with no project code under test)
- `a1_a2_metric`, `a3_exploratory`, `a4_refused_304`, `a5_schema_invalid`, `a6_unpublished_refused`.
- `coverage_gap_is_unknown`: no region absent from the coverage map is labelled free.
- `three_valued_query`: over the half-observed corridor the witness search fails and the cut search fails, giving `unresolved`.
- `tetrahedron_rule`: landmarks (0,0,0), (1,0,0), (0,1,0), (0,0,1) pass (ratio 0.0589); with the fourth at (0,0,0.01) they fail (ratio 0.00059).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/pilots/ingestion_pilot.sh` (kept with the pilot record): runs Part A end to end and prints the Part B
checklist for the operator to fill from the package. NDJSON lines carry the command, exit code, the schema
hashes, per fixture the validation result and mapped label, the refusal codes, and the checklist answers; a
final line with the two recommendations. No scene content is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the expected outcome table; unobserved space is unknown.
- Negative controls (registered for INT-FDGR-01): a mapping that treats a missing coverage entry as free (planted) must produce a false witness in the corridor case; a mapping that accepts `relative_only` as metric must be caught by A4; a validator that ignores `required` must be caught by A5.
- Counterexample protected: "import the mesh and trust it" (a corridor whose far half was never observed appears as empty space in a naive import, and a traversal certificate over it would be false).
- Determinism lanes: the script's outcomes are identical on both hosts.

## Considerations

- The drone project's kill condition for this lane is fixed in advance: no published geometry by the M5 entry review, after which the lane stays a schema adapter.
- Schema validity is not truth: a document can validate and still describe a badly scaled place. The scale label and its witness are what a task is allowed to rely on, and the label's authority is checked again at import.
- The twin package inspected may belong to a private place; the pilot is run on the owner's machine and its record is sanitized.
- If the sibling's schema changes between M3 and M5, the gate's M5 part reruns the fixtures at the new pin (an admission is never inherited).
