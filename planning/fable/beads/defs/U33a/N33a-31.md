## Background

The airlock crate `frob-fsim` exposes FrankenSim to FrankenRobotics through traits grouped into Cargo features.
The `optimize` group (off by default; 12 crates and about 84 thousand lines beyond the M1 set, FACT) holds
`Racing` (bead N33a-18, admitted at M1 for the study types) and the two traits of this bead, first needed at
M3 by the adversarial subsystem and by the studio's comparison and search, and used more heavily at M4:

- **`Optimizer`** over `fs-dfo`, `fs-bo`, `fs-ascent` and `fs-opt`. FACT (read 2026-11-21; re-read at the pin):
  FrankenSim has derivative-free optimizers with restart schedules (`fs-dfo`), Bayesian optimization with
  trust-region and multi-fidelity modules (`fs-bo`: `turbo`, `mf`), sequential quadratic programming, an
  augmented Lagrangian and Riemannian quasi-Newton for static objectives on manifold-valued parameters
  (`fs-ascent`, `fs-ascent::riemann`).
- **`Archive`** over `fs-archive`: a quality-diversity (MAP-Elites) archive over behaviour descriptors such as
  mass, speed, energy per metre and footprint. FACT: `fs-archive::MapElites::add` replaces an occupant only by
  a better candidate, which is what makes merges order-independent.

The dependency firewall lists FrankenSim's "optimizers, racing, statistics, spectral" as needed at M3 and
"optimizers, archive, surrogates" at M4. No named admission gate exists for these crates; the standing rule
applies: **an admission is never inherited; a crate first used at a milestone is admitted there, by its own
record, at the pin of that day**, in the form of the FrankenSim path gate applied to a group (it compiles from
this repository behind its feature, one known answer passes through the airlock trait, one negative control
fails). If a crate of the group fails admission, the method is implemented locally against the same known
answers (the default of open question OPEN-ATEL-3).

Why adapters instead of direct calls: every optimizer in FrankenRobotics must be replayable (random streams
keyed by address, never by wall clock), must respect budgets and cancellation supplied by the job context, and
must never see a result whose evidence class it could misuse (a rank-based search may use screening results; a
comparison may not). Those rules are enforced at one boundary.

## Scope

In: the two traits with in-process adapters; determinism, budget and cancellation rules; the M3 admission
record for the five crates; known answers and controls.
Out: the search strategies built on them (adversarial search, the studio's optimization, co-design, trajectory
optimization); racing (N33a-18); surrogates and value of information (the `stats` group, another unit).

## Technical approach

**Traits (DESIGN: intent, not signatures; module `frob_fsim::optimize`, feature `optimize`).**

```rust
pub trait Optimizer {
    fn start(&self, spec: &SearchSpec, seed: StreamAddress, budget: EvalBudget) -> Result<SearchState, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn ask(&self, s: &mut SearchState, n: u32) -> Result<Vec<Candidate>, RefereeRefusal>;       // points to evaluate
    fn tell(&self, s: &mut SearchState, results: &[(CandidateId, Objective)]) -> Result<Progress, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn best(&self, s: &SearchState) -> Option<(Candidate, Objective)>;
    fn methods(&self) -> Vec<MethodInfo>;        // which methods the pin offers, with their domains
}
pub enum Method { DerivativeFree { restarts: RestartSchedule }, BayesTrustRegion, BayesMultiFidelity,
                  Sqp, AugmentedLagrangian, RiemannQuasiNewton }
pub trait Archive {
    fn new_grid(&self, descriptors: &[DescriptorAxis]) -> Result<ArchiveState, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn add(&self, a: &mut ArchiveState, c: &Candidate, quality: f64, behaviour: &[f64]) -> Result<AddOutcome, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn merge(&self, a: &mut ArchiveState, other: &ArchiveState) -> Result<(), RefereeRefusal>;
    fn cells(&self, a: &ArchiveState) -> Vec<Elite>;
}
```

The interface is ask-and-tell on purpose: the adapter never calls a simulator. Evaluations are jobs of the
caller, so they obey the caller's budgets, memoization and determinism class.

**Rules.**
1. *Randomness by address.* Every stochastic choice inside an adapter draws from a counter-based stream keyed
   by `seed` and the ask index (the bedrock stream crate), never from a global generator or the clock. Two runs
   with the same seed and the same told results produce the same asks, bit for bit.
2. *Tell order does not matter.* `tell` accepts results in any order and applies them in candidate-identifier
   order, so preemption and out-of-order completion leave the search trajectory unchanged.
3. *Budgets and cancellation.* `EvalBudget` bounds asks; an ask beyond it is a typed refusal with the budget
   named. The job context's cancellation is observed between `ask` and `tell`; state is serializable to
   canonical bytes so a preempted search resumes exactly.
4. *Domains are data.* `methods()` states for each method whether it handles box bounds, general constraints,
   manifold-valued parameters and noisy objectives. A request outside a method's domain is refused by name; the
   adapter does not silently swap methods.
5. *Archive merges are order-independent.* `merge` is commutative, associative and idempotent (an invariant of
   the studio's lineage and archive: merges do not depend on who synchronized first). Ties in quality are
   broken by candidate identifier, never by arrival.
6. *Evidence discipline.* An `Objective` carries its evidence label (`screening`, `tested`, …). The adapter
   passes it through; an optimizer may rank with any label, and nothing the adapter returns is itself a claim.
7. Every sibling error passes through the conversion of bead N33a-13.

**Admission record (M3).** `covers.crates = ["fs-dfo", "fs-bo", "fs-ascent", "fs-opt", "fs-archive"]` (only
those a workspace crate enables at M3 are required to pass; the others are recorded `not used`), the known
answers below, the caught controls and the stack receipt of the day.

Determinism class: D0 given the told results. Evidence: search outputs are proposals; class is set by the
evaluations.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both traits compile under the `optimize` feature with no volatile type in their public face.
- [ ] Quadratic bowl: minimizing f(x) = Σᵢ (xᵢ − i)² for i = 1..4 from the origin with box [−10, 10]⁴, the derivative-free method reaches f ≤ 10⁻⁸ within 2,000 evaluations (TARGET) and reports x within 10⁻⁴ of (1, 2, 3, 4).
- [ ] Rosenbrock in two dimensions from (−1.2, 1): the sequential quadratic method with exact gradients supplied reaches (1, 1) to 10⁻⁶ within 200 iterations (TARGET).
- [ ] Constrained case: minimize x + y subject to x² + y² ≤ 1 gives (−√½, −√½) with value −√2 to 10⁻⁶.
- [ ] Replay: the same seed and told results give bit-identical asks across two runs and after a serialize, kill, restore cycle at a random point.
- [ ] Tell-order invariance: telling a batch in two different orders yields identical subsequent asks.
- [ ] Archive laws on 10³ random candidate sets: merge(a, b) = merge(b, a); merge(merge(a, b), c) = merge(a, merge(b, c)); merge(a, a) = a; all compared by canonical bytes.
- [ ] Controls caught: an objective with a flipped sign is not reported as converged to the true minimizer; an archive that breaks ties by arrival order fails the commutativity law; an ask past the budget is refused.
- [ ] The M3 admission record exists and validates; a default build has none of the five crates in its graph.
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/airlock_groups.sh --milestone M3` runs the step `optimize_search_admission` unskipped and it passes.

## Test plan

### Unit tests (`cargo test -p frob-fsim --features optimize optimize::search optimize::archive`)
- `dfo_quadratic_bowl`, `sqp_rosenbrock`, `auglag_disc_constraint` (expected minimizer (−0.7071067812, −0.7071067812), value −1.4142135624).
- `bo_one_dimensional`: minimizing (x − 0.3)² on [0, 1] with 30 evaluations finds x within 0.02 of 0.3 (TARGET; seeded).
- `asks_replay_bit_identical`, `resume_after_serialize_bit_identical`, `tell_order_invariant`.
- `budget_refused_by_name`, `method_outside_domain_refused` (a manifold parameter given to a box-only method).
- `cancellation_between_ask_and_tell`: with the job context cancelled after a `tell`, the next `ask` returns the typed cancellation and touches no stream; the state serialized at that point and restored under a fresh context produces the asks an uncancelled run produces, bit for bit.
- `objective_label_passed_through`: results told with the label `screening` come back from `best()` with `screening`; no value the adapter returns has a claim constructor (a compile-fail test).
- `archive_add_keeps_better`: cell empty → added; worse candidate → kept; better → replaced; equal quality → lower identifier wins.
- `archive_merge_commutative`, `archive_merge_associative`, `archive_merge_idempotent`.
- `no_global_rng_or_clock` (source scan of the adapter module).
- `flipped_objective_control`: the search on −f reaches the box boundary, and the test asserts it is not within 10⁻² of (1, 2, 3, 4).

### End-to-end script and logging
No script of its own: the traits have no verb before the adversarial search and optimization verbs of their
units, which observe them. This bead writes the step `optimize_search_admission` of
`scripts/e2e/airlock_groups.sh` (N33a-34, a dependency, provides the script, its step table and the skip logic;
the step is reported `skipped: not yet due (M3)` while `--milestone` selects an earlier one). The step, in the
form of the path gate applied to a group: (1) `cargo build -p frob-fsim --features optimize` from this
repository; (2) `cargo test -p frob-fsim --features optimize optimize::search optimize::archive` with explicit
seeds and structured output, one `assert` event per known answer and law; (3) the planted adapters (flipped
objective, arrival-order ties, past-budget ask, reset stream counter) declared as plants in the script header,
each logged as caught; (4) the M3 admission record written (the script's `--record` mode) and validated by
`cargo xtask check-stack` (no `unadmitted-use` or `admission-stale` finding), and the default build graph
holding none of the five crates. Logged per STD-E2E: `ts`, `script`, `bead` (`N33a-31`),
`step`, `event`, the exact command lines, exit codes, wall time; per case the evaluations used, the best value
against its threshold (10⁻⁸ for the bowl, 10⁻⁶ for Rosenbrock and the disc) and the minimizer error; replay
digests of the asks; per control its id and outcome; the record's crates and stack receipt; on failure the first
differing ask with the seed and the paths to replay it.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Closed forms: the bowl's minimizer; Rosenbrock's (1, 1); the disc-constrained linear objective at (−√½, −√½).
- Negative controls: the three of the acceptance list; a search state restored with its stream counter reset (planted) must fail the resume test.
- Metamorphic relations: translating the objective's argument by a vector translates the reported minimizer by the same vector (for the bowl, exactly in distribution under the same seed; checked on the final value); scaling the objective by a positive constant leaves a rank-based method's asks unchanged.
- Determinism lanes: asks identical twice, across worker counts, in debug and release; across platforms as recorded.

## Considerations

- Thresholds (evaluation counts, tolerances) are TARGETs for the pin's methods; the recorded values replace them, and a method that misses is recorded as failing admission for that method, not for the group.
- Bayesian optimization over typed designs of varying topology is not offered: no validated kernel exists for it; continuous sub-problems use the trust-region module.
- FrankenSim does not provide a hybrid multiple-shooting transcription for articulated models (FACT to recheck at the pin), so trajectory optimization with design variables builds its own transcription above this trait.
- A search is a consumer of budget, not a decision rule: stopping, promotion and discarding are decided by the studio's decision cores from ledger state, never inside the adapter.
