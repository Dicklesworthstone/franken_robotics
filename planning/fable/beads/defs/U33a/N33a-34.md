## Background

FrankenRobotics reaches FrankenSim through the airlock crate `frob-fsim`, whose traits are grouped into Cargo
features: `referee`, `geometry` and `ledger` (on by default), and `optimize`, `stats`, `spectral`,
`actuation`, `structure` and `multiphysics` (off by default). Beside the airlock sit a few Oracle-posture
foundations used only by the test harness (frankenscipy, franken-kernel's identifier forms, franken_numpy's
linear algebra, FrankenTorch's CPU crates behind a feature).

One rule governs when any of them may be used: **an admission is never inherited. A crate first used at a
milestone is admitted at that milestone, by its own record, at the pin of that day.** The same rule is stated
in the gate register (a foundation first used at a later milestone is admitted then) and in the milestone exit
rules (a foundation or an airlock group first used at a milestone is admitted at that milestone). A group gate
has a fixed form: the group compiles from this repository behind its feature, one known answer passes through
the airlock trait, and one negative control fails.

Each group and oracle has its own bead with its own tests. This bead is the one script that, for a user or an
exit reviewer, answers: "which groups does this tree use, is each admitted at the current pin, and do their
known answers and controls still hold?" It also demonstrates the never-inherited check end to end: enabling a
group without a record, or moving a pin without rerunning admission, is caught.

## Scope

In: the script and its steps for the M1 admissions of this unit (the rest of the `referee` group, the first
half of `geometry`, the `ledger` group's `Session` and `Recompute`, the statistical half of `optimize`, the
`actuation` group, and the two M1 oracles); the never-inherited scenarios; a table of group status.
Out: the mechanisms and their unit tests (their own beads); steps for later milestones, which their beads add
to this script (`fnp_linalg_admission` and `frankentorch_cpu_admission` and `geometry_mesh_admission` at M2;
`optimize_search_admission` at M3) and which are reported `skipped: not yet due` until then; the group gates
owned by other units.

## Technical approach

**Form.** `scripts/e2e/airlock_groups.sh`: POSIX shell with `set -eu`, sourcing the shared helper of
`STD-E2E`; idempotent; seed-explicit (`--seed`, default 1, passed to every seeded statistical test);
`--record` writes admission records (refused by the guard on a dirty stack); `--milestone M1|M2|…` selects
which steps are due. Cargo runs through the offload shim; each step runs under a timeout inside the machine
envelope.

**Steps due at M1.**
1. `groups_in_use`: from `cargo metadata` of every workspace, list which airlock features each crate enables
   and which FrankenSim crates are in each lockfile; print the table (group, enabled by, crates, record,
   record's pin, current pin).
2. `referee_group_admission`: free-body momentum over 10⁴ steps (spatial angular momentum constant to 10⁻¹²
   relative); group membership; bounded energy; the swept chart containing an arc's midpoint; the gear power
   identity; the reversed-sign, algebraic-loop and contract-version refusals. Record covers `fs-motion`,
   `fs-ports`, `fs-system`, `fs-mbd-material`.
3. `geometry_group_admission`: box support 0.1 m and 0.2121320344 m; sphere–plane gap 0.5 m; sphere–sphere
   gaps 0.5 m and −0.1 m; the thin-plate sweep detected; the unsupported pair and missing role refused. Record
   covers `fs-geom`, `fs-scene`.
4. `ledger_group_admission`: hit, miss and stale counts on the diamond graph; tolerance reuse only with a
   recorded achieved error; the determinism trip-wire; package round trip and tamper detection. Record covers
   `fs-session`, `fs-package`.
5. `optimize_racing_admission`: null calibration at α = 0.05 (crossing share at most 0.0568 over 10⁴ studies);
   power sanity; the null race of 81 candidates over 512 replays; the span, cap and product-combiner controls.
   Record covers `fs-eproc`, `fs-race`.
6. `actuation_group_admission`: the locked-rotor current 7.5854 A at 10 ms; the per-step energy balance; the
   thermal node at 333.15 K steady and 318.43 K at one time constant; the critical current 35.81 A; the sign
   rule; the constrained-interconnection and missing-pair refusals. Record covers `fs-em`, `fs-thermal`,
   `fs-control`.
7. `oracle_admissions`: the harmonic oscillator over one period; the 90° rotation about z with the
   component-order control; the quadratic bowl; the identifier round trip in both directions.
8. `never_inherited`: in a scratch copy, (a) enable the `stats` feature from a crate with no `stats` record:
   `xtask check-stack` must report `unadmitted-use: stats`; (b) change the FrankenSim pin in the lock without
   new records: it must report `admission-stale` for every FrankenSim record; (c) present a record for the
   Metal gateway as covering `ft-nn`: the validator must refuse the widening.
9. `foreign_gates_present`: for INT-FSIM-08, -09 and -16 (due by M1 in the register), log whether a valid
   record exists; under `--strict` a missing one fails the step.
10. `status_table`: print and log the final table; every group in use at this milestone must show a valid
    record at the current pin.

**Logging (per the shared standard).** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/airlock_groups/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript. Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (the key of the bead whose admission the step runs), `step`,
`event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; `metric` lines with each known answer's measured
value, unit, expected value and tolerance; `assert` lines for every control with `caught: true|false`;
record and receipt hashes; for refusals the code and upstream reason; on failure the first differing value
and the fixture path. A final `summary` gives assertions passed and failed, the groups admitted, and the
steps skipped as not yet due.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At M1 the script passes steps 1 to 10 from a clean checkout and, with `--record` on a clean stack, writes valid records for the crates named in steps 2 to 7.
- [ ] The status table shows every airlock group enabled by any crate with a valid record at the current pin, and no group enabled by a crate outside its allowed list.
- [ ] The three never-inherited scenarios are caught with the stated findings.
- [ ] Every negative control of steps 2 to 7 is caught; an uncaught control fails the script.
- [ ] Steps for later milestones report `skipped: not yet due` at M1 and run when their milestone is selected.
- [ ] Under `--strict` a missing record for a gate due by the selected milestone fails the script, naming the gate.
- [ ] The log validates against the shared schema and ends with a `summary`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The script is the test; `--self-test` runs each assertion helper on a passing and a failing input. A dry-run
mode (`--plan`) prints the steps for the selected milestone. A shell lint runs in the formatting lane.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. It is run at the M1 exit review (with `--milestone M1 --strict`), at every later exit
with that milestone selected, and by the compatibility train after every pin move, where its
`never_inherited` logic is the mechanism that forces admissions to be rerun.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers asserted (each defined in its mechanism bead and repeated in the script's expectations): spatial momentum of a free body conserved; e₁·f₁ + e₂·f₂ = 0 for an ideal gear; box support h(d) = a·|d_x| + b·|d_y| + c·|d_z|; the RL step with time constant L/R; the thermal node's steady state T_amb + P/G_th; (cos 2π, −sin 2π) = (1, 0); the average of e-values (4, 0.5, 1.5) is 2.
- Negative controls exercised: an endpoint-only swept chart; a reversed gear sign; an endpoint-only collision check on the thin plate; a tolerance-reused episode; an e-process with its span halved after the fact; a negated copper loss; a quaternion read in the wrong component order; the three never-inherited plants.
- Mutation check of the script: in self-test, with the record validator stubbed to accept everything, the never-inherited step must fail.
- Determinism lanes: all seeded statistics and all digests are identical on a rerun with the same seed and tree; the script is run on both hosts at an exit review.

## Considerations

- The statistical steps are the slow ones (10⁴ simulated studies); they are seeded, run under a timeout, and their counts are logged so a shortened developer run (`--quick`, 10³ studies, wider acceptance band) cannot be mistaken for the gate run.
- A changed sibling fact (for example a raised contact capacity or a renamed upstream error variant) usually shows here as a failed admission at the new pin; that is the purpose of rerunning at every pin move.
- If a group fails admission, its consumers fall back as their own beads state (a local implementation against the same known answers, or the capability is described by its fallback's name); the script then shows the group as `not admitted` and passes only without `--strict`.
- No private data: fixtures are synthetic.
