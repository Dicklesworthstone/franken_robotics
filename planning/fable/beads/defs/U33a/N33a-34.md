## Background

FrankenRobotics reaches FrankenSim through the airlock crate `frob-fsim`, whose traits are grouped into Cargo
features: `referee`, `geometry` and `ledger` (on by default), and `optimize`, `stats`, `spectral`,
`actuation`, `structure` and `multiphysics` (off by default). Beside the airlock sit a few Oracle-posture
foundations used only by the test harness (frankenscipy, franken-kernel's identifier forms, franken_numpy's
linear algebra, FrankenTorch's CPU crates behind a feature).

One rule governs when any of them may be used: **an admission is never inherited. A crate first used at a
milestone is admitted at that milestone, by its own record, at the pin of that day.** The same rule is stated
in the gate register (a foundation first used at a later milestone is admitted then) and in the milestone exit
rules (a foundation or an airlock group first used at a milestone is admitted at that milestone). A group gate
has a fixed form: the group compiles from this repository behind its feature, one known answer passes through
the airlock trait, and one negative control fails.

Each group and oracle has its own bead with its own tests. This bead is the one script that, for a user or an
exit reviewer, answers: "which groups does this tree use, is each admitted at the current pin, and do their
known answers and controls still hold?" It also demonstrates the never-inherited check end to end: enabling a
group without a record, or moving a pin without rerunning admission, is caught.

## Scope
In: the script and its steps for the M1 admissions of this unit (the rest of the `referee` group, both halves
of `geometry` that are first used at M1, the `ledger` group's `Session` and `Recompute`, the `fs-race` row of
the statistical half of `optimize`, the `actuation` group's three traits of N33a-19, and the frankenscipy
oracle); the never-inherited scenarios; the presence check of the M1 records written by other beads (the
`fs-eproc` row of INT-FSIM-14, N33b-12; `PortHamiltonian` under INT-FSIM-09, BD-068; the franken-kernel oracle
record, N33c-27, whose round trips are logged by its own step in `suite_deps_rules.sh`); a table of group
status.
Out: the mechanisms and their unit tests (their own beads); steps for later milestones, which their beads add
to this script and which are reported `skipped: not yet due` until then: `fnp_linalg_admission` (N33a-21) and
`frankentorch_cpu_admission` (N33a-22) at M2; `optimize_search_admission` (N33a-31) and
`tri_wheel_oracle_admission` (N33b-22) at M3; `tropical_admission` (N33bx-03) and `fs_mesh_admission`
(N33bx-04) at M4; `mass_properties_admission` (N33bx-05) and `epistemic_admission` (N33bx-06) at M5;
`tracer_admission` (N33bx-07) at M6; the group gates owned by other units.
After integration: this script covers the first-use admission records of the M1 groups and oracles and the
never-inherited scenarios; the group gates seen from the user's side, with the two-binary build matrix and
`FROB-REF-790` for an absent group, are the script of N33b-21 (`suite_airlock_groups.sh`).
The step `geometry_mesh_admission` (N33a-30) is an M1 step of this script, since the mesh half of `geometry` is
first used at M1.

## Technical approach

**Form.** `scripts/e2e/airlock_groups.sh`: POSIX shell with `set -eu`, sourcing the shared helper of
`STD-E2E`; idempotent; seed-explicit (`--seed`, default 1, passed to every seeded statistical test);
`--record` writes admission records (refused by the guard on a dirty stack); `--milestone M1|M2|…` selects
which steps are due. Cargo runs through the offload shim; each step runs under a timeout inside the machine
envelope.

**Steps due at M1.**
1. `groups_in_use`: from `cargo metadata` of every workspace, list which airlock features each crate enables
   and which FrankenSim crates are in each lockfile; print the table (group, enabled by, crates, record,
   record's pin, current pin).
2. `referee_group_admission`: free-body momentum over 10⁴ steps (spatial angular momentum constant to 10⁻¹²
   relative); group membership; bounded energy; the swept chart containing an arc's midpoint; the gear power
   identity; the reversed-sign, algebraic-loop and contract-version refusals. Record covers `fs-motion`,
   `fs-ports`, `fs-system`, `fs-mbd-material`.
3. `geometry_group_admission`: box support 0.1 m and 0.2121320344 m; sphere–plane gap 0.5 m; sphere–sphere
   gaps 0.5 m and −0.1 m; the thin-plate sweep detected; the unsupported pair and missing role refused. Record
   covers `fs-geom`, `fs-scene`.
4. `geometry_mesh_admission` (N33a-30): the unit cube's round trip with the same content hash in each mesh
   format the pin supports; its facts (volume 1 m³, area 6 m², Euler characteristic 2, Betti numbers (1, 0, 1));
   the 16 × 16 torus (Euler characteristic 0, Betti numbers (1, 2, 1)); the open box with one boundary loop of 4
   edges and nothing repaired; the flipped triangle reported; an input one byte over the bound quarantined
   unparsed with its receipt; the unit sphere's field enclosing 1.0 at (2, 0, 0) and −1.0 at the origin. Record
   covers `fs-io`, `fs-rep-mesh`, `fs-rep-sdf`, `fs-rep-frep`, `fs-topo`.
5. `ledger_group_admission` (N33a-17, `Session`): package round trip through `pack` and `check`; one changed
   blob byte, a missing blob, an authorization flag set to `true` or absent, a machine path and an unknown schema
   major each rejected with the reason named; the stack receipt carried in the manifest. Record covers
   `fs-session`, `fs-package`. (`Recompute`, with its hit, miss and stale counts on the diamond graph, tolerance
   reuse only with a recorded achieved error and the determinism trip-wire, is N33b-15's under INT-FSIM-16, run
   by N33b-21's step `ledger_recompute` and checked for presence in step 10.)
6. `optimize_racing_admission`: null calibration at α = 0.05 (crossing share at most 0.0569 over 10⁴ studies,
   the exact binomial limit of N33a-18); power sanity; the null race of 81 candidates over 512 replays; the
   span, cap and product-combiner controls. Record covers `fs-race` (the `fs-eproc` row is N33b-12's and is
   checked in step 10).
7. `actuation_group_admission` (N33a-19): the thermal node at 333.15 K steady and 318.43 K at one time
   constant; the runaway threshold (critical current 35.81 A: the node settles at 30 A and grows at 40 A);
   entropy production nonnegative; the current loop's five-tick sequence; `Magnetics` values `Estimated` and
   linear in the turn count; the missing-material-pair refusal. Record covers `fs-em`, `fs-thermal`,
   `fs-control`. (`PortHamiltonian`, with the locked-rotor step, the sign rule and the constrained-
   interconnection refusal, is BD-068's under INT-FSIM-09, checked in step 10.)
8. `oracle_admissions` (N33a-20): the harmonic oscillator over one period with the sign-flipped control; the
   90° rotation about z with the component-order control; the quadratic bowl with the unbounded control; the
   dev-only edges and the single `serde_json` version. Record covers the frankenscipy packages used.
9. `never_inherited`: in a scratch copy, (a) enable the `stats` feature from a crate with no `stats` record:
   `xtask check-stack` must report the finding `stack.admission` of kind `unadmitted-use` naming `stats`; (b)
   change the FrankenSim pin in the lock without new records: it must report `stack.admission` of kind
   `admission-stale` for every FrankenSim record; (c) present a record for the Metal gateway as covering
   `ft-nn`: the validator must refuse the widening.
10. `foreign_gates_present`: for INT-FSIM-08 (BD-079), INT-FSIM-09 (BD-068), INT-FSIM-14's `fs-eproc` row
    (N33b-12) and INT-FSIM-16 (N33b-15) (due by M1 in the register), and for the franken-kernel oracle record (N33c-27),
    log whether a valid record exists at the current pin; under `--strict` a missing one fails the step.
11. `status_table`: print and log the final table; every group in use at this milestone must show a valid
    record at the current pin.

**Steps added later by other beads.** Each is registered in the script's step table with its milestone, is
reported `skipped: not yet due` (with that milestone) while `--milestone` selects an earlier one, and runs from
the selected milestone on: `fnp_linalg_admission` (N33a-21, M2), `frankentorch_cpu_admission` (N33a-22, M2), `optimize_search_admission` (N33a-31, M3), `tri_wheel_oracle_admission` (N33b-22, M3),
`tropical_admission` (N33bx-03, M4), `fs_mesh_admission` (N33bx-04, M4), `mass_properties_admission`
(N33bx-05, M5), `epistemic_admission` (N33bx-06, M5), `tracer_admission` (N33bx-07, M6). This bead provides the
table and the skip logic; each adding bead writes its step.

**Plants.** The script header declares every step's planted adapters (the negative controls of steps 2 to 8 now,
and the `planted::` adapters each later step brings) as plants of the STD-E2E harness, each naming its step;
under `run-all.sh --verify-plants` each plant must turn its own step red. A plant whose step is not yet due is
listed and skipped with the step.

**Logging (per the shared standard).** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/airlock_groups/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript. Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead` (the key of the bead whose admission the step runs), `step`,
`event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; `metric` lines with each known answer's measured
value, unit, expected value and tolerance; `assert` lines for every control with `caught: true|false`;
record and receipt hashes; for refusals the code and upstream reason; on failure the first differing value
and the fixture path. A final `summary` gives assertions passed and failed, the groups admitted, and the
steps skipped as not yet due.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At M1 the script passes steps 1 to 11 from a clean checkout and, with `--record` on a clean stack, writes valid records for the crates named in steps 2 to 8.
- [ ] The status table shows every airlock group enabled by any crate with a valid record at the current pin, and no group enabled by a crate outside its allowed list.
- [ ] The three never-inherited scenarios are caught with the stated findings.
- [ ] Every negative control of steps 2 to 8 is caught; an uncaught control fails the script.
- [ ] Steps for later milestones (the nine listed above) report `skipped: not yet due` at M1 and run when their milestone is selected.
- [ ] Every plant declared in the script header turns its own step red under `run-all.sh --verify-plants`; the plants of steps not yet due are listed as skipped.
- [ ] Under `--strict` a missing record for a gate due by the selected milestone fails the script, naming the gate.
- [ ] The log validates against the shared schema and ends with a `summary`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
The script is the test; `--self-test` runs each assertion helper on a passing and a failing input. A dry-run
mode (`--plan`) prints the steps for the selected milestone. A shell lint runs in the formatting lane.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script. It is run at the M1 exit review (with `--milestone M1 --strict`), at every later exit
with that milestone selected, and by the compatibility train after every pin move, where its
`never_inherited` logic is the mechanism that forces admissions to be rerun.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers asserted (each defined in its mechanism bead and repeated in the script's expectations): spatial momentum of a free body conserved; e₁·f₁ + e₂·f₂ = 0 for an ideal gear; box support h(d) = a·|d_x| + b·|d_y| + c·|d_z|; the cube's and the torus's Euler characteristics 8 − 18 + 12 = 2 and 256 − 768 + 512 = 0; the thermal node's steady state T_amb + P/G_th and its value 318.43 K at one time constant C/G_th; the critical current √(G_th/(R₀·α)) = 35.81 A; (cos 2π, −sin 2π) = (1, 0); the average of e-values (4, 0.5, 1.5) is 2.
- Negative controls exercised: an endpoint-only swept chart; a reversed gear sign; an endpoint-only collision check on the thin plate; an adapter that welds the open box closed; a hash of file bytes instead of content; one changed blob byte in a package; an e-process with its span halved after the fact; a negated copper loss; a geometry-derived torque constant labelled `Measured`; a current loop with its integral term dropped; a quaternion read in the wrong component order; the three never-inherited plants.
- Mutation check of the script: in self-test, with the record validator stubbed to accept everything, the never-inherited step must fail.
- Determinism lanes: all seeded statistics and all digests are identical on a rerun with the same seed and tree; the script is run on both hosts at an exit review.

## Considerations

- The statistical steps are the slow ones (10⁴ simulated studies); they are seeded, run under a timeout, and their counts are logged so a shortened developer run (`--quick`, 10³ studies, wider acceptance band) cannot be mistaken for the gate run.
- A changed sibling fact (for example a raised contact capacity or a renamed upstream error variant) usually shows here as a failed admission at the new pin; that is the purpose of rerunning at every pin move.
- If a group fails admission, its consumers fall back as their own beads state (a local implementation against the same known answers, or the capability is described by its fallback's name); the script then shows the group as `not admitted` and passes only without `--strict`.
- No private data: fixtures are synthetic.
