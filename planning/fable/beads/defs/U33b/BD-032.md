## Background

FrankenRobotics consumes its sibling FrankenSim by path dependency, confined behind the airlock crate `frob-fsim`.
FrankenSim is the **referee** (the most trusted tier: `f64`, event-located, used to adjudicate the fast batched
tiers) and the source of the geometric algebra the whole project is written in. A crate that compiles is not yet
admitted: for each source file this project leans on, a named first test must pass before any code above the
airlock calls it, and the result is written to `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md` with the command, its output, the commit
and the resolution receipt hash. An admission is never inherited: it is re-run at every pin move.

This bead is the first admission test of two file groups (FACT, line counts at FrankenSim head `483d09c682f2`):

| Gate | Source anchor (lines) | What is reused |
|---|---|---|
| INT-FSIM-02 | `fs-ga/src/lie.rs` (1,735), `lie_differential.rs` (155), `lie_opt.rs` (311) | Group façades over motors; twist and wrench conventions |
| INT-FSIM-03 | `fs-mbd/src/articulated.rs` (3,428), `articulated/generalized.rs` (377), `articulated/point_motion.rs` (200), `point_motion.rs` (204) | Tree kinematics, recursive Newton–Euler, the articulated-body solve, floating-base maps, rigid-point motion |

Why these two first. `fs-ga` is on the bedrock-direct list (its types are the vocabulary of the mathematics and may
be named anywhere), so a convention error in it propagates into every crate. The errors that matter are a sign, a
transpose or an inverse: a wrench moved with the twist map loses its moment arm and nothing faults. `fs-mbd` is the
referee's dynamics; if its accelerations differ from the equations of motion, every parity and fidelity gate is
measured against a wrong reference. The design decision behind testing instead of trusting: 409 commits touched
`fs-mbd` in thirty days, so a fact read once goes stale, and a gate that depends on a sibling fact re-reads it.

Conventions under test (normative for this project). A pose g = (R, p) named `a_from_b` acts as x_a = R·x_b + p.
A twist is ξ = (ω, v), angular part first, as `fs_ga::Twist::new(angular, linear)`. A wrench is (τ, f), torque
first, as `fs_ga::Wrench::new(torque, force)`. The pairing ⟨w, ξ⟩ = τ·ω + f·v is power in watts. With p̂ the
cross-product matrix of p:

```text
Ad_g    = [[ R, 0 ], [ p̂·R, R ]]      (ω, v) ↦ (R·ω, R·v + p × R·ω)          twists, b to a
Ad_g⁻ᵀ  = [[ R, p̂·R ], [ 0, R ]]      (τ, f) ↦ (R·τ + p × R·f, R·f)          wrenches, b to a
⟨Ad_g⁻ᵀ w, Ad_g ξ⟩ = ⟨w, ξ⟩            power does not depend on the frame it is written in
```

## Technical approach

Tests live in `crates/frob-fsim/tests/admission/int_fsim_02.rs` and `int_fsim_03.rs`. Random inputs come from the
counter-based streams of `fs-rand`, keyed by `(seed, case_index)`, so any case replays alone. All arithmetic is
`f64`. Tolerances are TARGETs until the gate records measured values.

**INT-FSIM-02, five fixtures.**
- *F1, the hand wrench.* R = I, p = (1, 0, 0) m. The wrench (τ, f) = (0, (0, 0, 1) N) in b pushes forward to
  torque p × f = (0, −1, 0) N·m and the same force. Asserted through `fs_ga::Se3::coadjoint` (FACT: it documents
  the group coadjoint as the inverse transpose).
- *F2, the hand twist.* Same g. The twist (ω, v) = ((0, 0, 1) rad/s, 0) in b maps to ((0, 0, 1), p × ω) =
  ((0, 0, 1), (0, −1, 0) m/s): the origin of a sits at −1 m along b's x axis and is carried by the rotation at
  ω × r = (0, 0, 1) × (−1, 0, 0) = (0, −1, 0).
- *F3, a moving link, 1,000 seeded cases.* Draw g (rotation vector of norm at most 3 rad, translation of norm at
  most 10 m), a body twist ξ_b with components in [−5, 5] and a wrench w_b with components in [−100, 100]. Compute
  P_b = ⟨w_b, ξ_b⟩ and P_a = ⟨Ad_g⁻ᵀ w_b, Ad_g ξ_b⟩ through the façade. Pass: abs(P_a − P_b) ≤ 10⁻¹²·S, with scale
  S = ‖τ‖·‖ω‖ + ‖f‖·‖v‖ evaluated in frame a (the scale prevents a near-zero power from inflating the relative
  error). A boundary set is appended: identity, rotation by π about each axis, a rotation of 10⁻⁸ rad, and a
  translation of 30 m.
- *F4, composition.* For seeded g, h: Ad(g·h)·ξ = Ad(g)·(Ad(h)·ξ) to 10⁻¹² relative in each component block.
- *F5, the analytic Jacobian, 1,000 seeded configurations.* For x in the algebra (rotation part of norm at most
  3 rad, translation part at most 10 m) the left Jacobian J_l(x) of the exponential from `lie.rs`
  (`Se3::left_jacobian`) is compared column by column with the central difference
  [log(exp(x + h·e_k)·exp(x)⁻¹) − log(exp(x − h·e_k)·exp(x)⁻¹)]/(2h), h = 10⁻⁵ (truncation of order 10⁻¹⁰,
  rounding of order 10⁻¹¹). Pass: ‖J_analytic − J_fd‖_F ≤ 10⁻⁷·‖J_analytic‖_F. The right Jacobian is tested the
  same way with the perturbation on the right. Configurations stay inside the façade's stated domain; a refusal
  inside the sampled domain is a failure of the gate, and the observed refusal domain is recorded.
- *Negative control, the reversed adjoint.* A test-only wrapper passes `b_from_a` where `a_from_b` is meant (the
  inverse transform). It must fail at least three fixtures. Expected: F1 returns torque (0, +1, 0); F2 returns
  linear part (0, +1, 0); F3 violates the bound when the twist is moved correctly and the wrench with the
  reversed map; F4 fails whenever g and h do not commute. A second control moves the wrench of F1 with the twist
  map (Ad_g in place of Ad_g⁻ᵀ): the torque comes out zero, the moment arm is lost, and F1 fails.
- *Recorded.* The argument order of `Twist::new` and `Wrench::new` (probed by constructing ((1, 2, 3), (4, 5, 6))
  and reading the angular or torque part back), and which façade entry points were exercised.

**INT-FSIM-03, three fixtures, through the airlock trait `RefereeDynamics`.**
- *A1, the two-joint arm against independently differentiated energy.* A planar arm with two revolute joints
  about z, gravity g along −y, link lengths l₁, l₂, centre-of-mass distances c₁, c₂, masses m₁, m₂ and central
  inertias I₁, I₂; q₁ is measured from the x axis and q₂ is relative. The independent side is written in the test,
  shares no code with `fs-mbd`, and is the Euler–Lagrange equations of T − V:

  ```text
  M11 = I₁ + I₂ + m₁·c₁² + m₂·(l₁² + c₂² + 2·l₁·c₂·cos q₂)      M12 = I₂ + m₂·(c₂² + l₁·c₂·cos q₂)
  M22 = I₂ + m₂·c₂²                                             h   = m₂·l₁·c₂·sin q₂
  τ₁ = M11·q̈₁ + M12·q̈₂ − h·(2·q̇₁·q̇₂ + q̇₂²) + (m₁·c₁ + m₂·l₁)·g·cos q₁ + m₂·c₂·g·cos(q₁ + q₂)
  τ₂ = M12·q̈₁ + M22·q̈₂ + h·q̇₁²                + m₂·c₂·g·cos(q₁ + q₂)
  ```

  The same accelerations are also obtained by differentiating the coded energy function with `fs-ad` forward
  duals, as a check on the closed form. Hand case: two uniform rods (m = 1 kg, l = 1 m, c = 0.5 m, I = 1/12
  kg·m²), horizontal (q = 0), at rest, no torque: M = [[8/3, 5/6], [5/6, 1/3]], gravity terms (2g, g/2), so
  q̈ = (−9g/7, +12g/7) = (−12.612857, +16.817143) rad/s² at g = 9.81 m/s². Then 1,000 seeded states (q in [−π, π],
  q̇ in [−5, 5] rad/s, τ in [−20, 20] N·m, parameters in physical ranges). Pass:
  abs(q̈_fsim − q̈_energy) ≤ 10⁻¹⁰·max(1, ‖q̈‖) per joint.
- *A2, the offset inertial sensor.* A point at body offset r on a link whose origin has linear acceleration a₀,
  angular velocity ω and angular acceleration α accelerates at a_p = a₀ + α × r + ω × (ω × r). Hand case: a₀ = 0,
  ω = (0, 0, 2) rad/s, α = (0, 0, 3) rad/s², r = (0.5, 0, 0) m gives (−2, 1.5, 0) m/s². Then the second link of
  A1 with a sensor at a seeded offset over 1,000 states: the point acceleration from `point_motion` equals the
  formula evaluated from the link's (a₀, ω, α), to 10⁻¹⁰ relative.
- *A3, floating base.* The same arm on a free base with no external force: the acceleration of the system centre
  of mass is exactly gravity, to 10⁻¹⁰ m/s².
- *Negative controls.* (i) The independent side with the velocity term h dropped disagrees by more than 10⁻⁶ on
  every case with nonzero rates; (ii) the model handed to the airlock with I₂ omitted disagrees on the hand case;
  (iii) A2 with the centripetal term dropped returns (0, 1.5, 0). Each must be caught.
- *Recorded facts (re-read at the pin before any gate cites them).* Whether `fs-mbd` is generic over a
  differentiation scalar (reported: plain `f64`); whether it emits articulated adjoints (reported: none); whether
  its contact step computes or receives the contact set (reported: the caller supplies it); whether it offers
  batched environments (reported: none); which joint kinds exist (reported: fixed, revolute, prismatic, helical).
  Each is written as `confirmed` or `changed`, with file and line.

**Records.** Two entries in `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md`, each with the commit whose tracked `Cargo.lock` contains the
crate, the command, the output hash, the receipt hash, the worst observed error per fixture and the case index at
which it occurred. Claim: Known answer; class `Tested`; tier `referee`; determinism D2 on one platform.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Power ⟨wrench, twist⟩ is frame-invariant to 10⁻¹² relative on the 1,000 seeded moving-link cases and the boundary set (seed done-condition).
- [ ] The analytic Jacobian agrees with central differences to 10⁻⁷ relative at 1,000 seeded configurations, left and right.
- [ ] The deliberately reversed adjoint fails at least three fixtures (F1, F2, F3 and, for non-commuting pairs, F4), and the twist-map-for-wrench control fails F1 (seed done-condition).
- [ ] The two-joint arm's accelerations match the independently differentiated energy to 10⁻¹⁰ on the hand case (−9g/7, +12g/7) and on 1,000 seeded states (seed done-condition).
- [ ] The offset sensor's acceleration equals the rigid-point formula on the hand case (−2, 1.5, 0) m/s² and on 1,000 seeded states.
- [ ] The three INT-FSIM-03 negative controls are caught and registered.
- [ ] Scalar genericity, adjoint support, contact inputs, batching and joint kinds are recorded at the pin with file and line.
- [ ] Both gates have records in `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md` citing a receipt hash, and rows in `registries/gates.toml` with measured worst errors; no PASS is recorded on a dirty sibling tree.

`cargo test -p frob-fsim --test admission`:

- `fsim02_power_invariant_1000` (bound 10⁻¹²·S; reports worst case and its index); `fsim02_boundary_set`.
- `fsim02_adjoint_composition`; `fsim02_left_jacobian_fd_1000`, `fsim02_right_jacobian_fd_1000` (bound 10⁻⁷).
- `fsim02_reversed_adjoint_fails_three` (asserts the count of failing fixtures is at least 3); `fsim02_twist_map_on_wrench_fails`.
- `fsim02_argument_order_recorded`.
- `fsim03_two_rods_from_horizontal` (expects (−12.612857, +16.817143) rad/s² to 10⁻¹⁰); `fsim03_arm_vs_energy_1000`; `fsim03_energy_closed_form_equals_dual_derivative` (the independent side agrees with itself two ways).
- `fsim03_point_motion_hand_case`, `fsim03_point_motion_1000`, `fsim03_floating_base_com_falls_at_g`.
- `fsim03_dropped_coriolis_caught`, `fsim03_missing_inertia_caught`, `fsim03_dropped_centripetal_caught`.
- `fsim03_facts_recorded`: writes the fact table and fails if a fact cannot be determined.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/suite_admission_m0.sh` (bead N33b-20), steps `int_fsim_02` and `int_fsim_03`: runs the admission lane
and `frob doctor --json`, and asserts both gates appear under FD-FSIM with records. Logged per STD-E2E: command,
exit code, wall time, evidence block, metrics `power_rel_err_max` (threshold 10⁻¹²), `jacobian_rel_err_max`
(10⁻⁷), `reversed_adjoint_failures` (at least 3), `arm_accel_err_max` (10⁻¹⁰), `point_accel_err_max` (10⁻¹⁰), the
seed, and on failure the case index and the first differing component.

### Known answers and negative controls
Hand cases as stated: the wrench (0, −1, 0) N·m; the twist (0, −1, 0) m/s; the rods (−9g/7, +12g/7); the sensor
(−2, 1.5, 0) m/s². Negative controls: the reversed adjoint, the wrong map on a wrench, and the three dynamics
mutants. Metamorphic: rotating the world frame by a seeded rotation leaves every power and every joint
acceleration unchanged; scaling all lengths by s, masses by s³ and central inertias by s⁵ scales q̈ of the released
arm by 1/s.
Determinism: twice on one machine with one seed gives identical worst-case indices and bit-identical errors; debug
and release agree inside class D2; cross-platform equality is not claimed (the sibling uses platform `sqrt`, `sin`
and `cos`).

## Considerations

- The series behind `Se3::left_jacobian` refuses when the infinity norm of ad exceeds 32 (FACT, `lie.rs`), so a pure translation of 33 m is refused although the pose is regular. F5 samples inside the domain; the limit itself has its own admission test in the later foundation-fact gate, and `frob-lie` owns closed-form differentials.
- `fs-mbd::Wrench` mixes frames (`force_world` with `torque_body`), unlike `fs_ga::Wrench`. This bead applies no external wrench through `fs-mbd`, so it does not exercise that hazard; the mixed-wrench probe is a separate gate.
- The four reported limits of `fs-mbd` shape the referee's design (lowered joints, caller-supplied contact sets, no adjoints: upstream work items FD-FSIM-01, -03, -05). If the re-read finds one changed, the bead that relies on it is told through the record; nothing is assumed from this page.
- Vector vocabulary differs between `fs-mbd` and `fs-ga` (upstream work item FD-FSIM-06); the airlock converts, and A1 passes through that conversion on purpose.
- Runtime is seconds. The tests run in the default lane.
