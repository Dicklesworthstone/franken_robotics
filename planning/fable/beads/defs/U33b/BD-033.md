## Background

Wheeled and tracked robots need a closed-form answer for "where does a base end up when its wheels turn at given
speeds for a given time". That answer is the oracle behind the wheeled known answers of the contact subsystem and
the nonholonomic constructions. The sibling FrankenSim has one: `fs-mbd::planar_drive`
(`frankensim/crates/fs-mbd/src/planar_drive.rs`, 265 lines), a prescribed-speed planar drive. The plan intended to
reuse it "as a known-answer source", together with the G1 and iiwa7 catalog builders of `robot_models.rs`
(1,488 lines).

A defect was reported in it and must be treated as a fact to recheck, not as a finding: **near-straight motion at
a large heading returns zero translation.** What a reading of the source on 2026-11-21 confirms (lines 117–133) is
the mechanism; the run itself was not repeated. The step takes its straight branch only when abs(ω) ≤ 10⁻¹² rad/s.
Otherwise it forms θ′ = θ + ω·Δt and returns the displacement R·(sin θ′ − sin θ) and −R·(cos θ′ − cos θ), with
R = v/ω the turn radius.

The arithmetic that breaks it. Take heading θ = 32 rad, wheel speeds 1 and 1 + 6·10⁻¹³ m/s, track 0.5 m and
Δt = 1/400 s. Then ω = 6·10⁻¹³/0.5 = 1.2·10⁻¹² rad/s, just above the threshold, so the curved branch runs, and
ω·Δt = 3·10⁻¹⁵ rad. Doubles between 32 and 64 are spaced 2⁻⁴⁷ apart, so half the spacing at 32 is
2⁻⁴⁸ ≈ 3.55·10⁻¹⁵. The increment is below it, θ′ rounds to θ, and both differences are exactly zero. Both wheels
advance and the chassis does not move. Over 400 steps (one second) the true displacement is one metre along the
heading, about (0.83422336, 0.55142668) m.

The shortcut this refutes (counterexample CX-352): "a branch on abs(ω) ≤ 10⁻¹² rad/s separates straight from curved
motion". A fixed threshold on the rate ignores the size of the heading the increment is added to.

Decision (DESIGN). A defect found in a sibling becomes an admission test here and an upstream work item there, in
that order. The admission test INT-FSIM-05 records what the pinned commit does. The upstream work item FD-FSIM-11
(P2) changes `planar_drive` in FrankenSim's repository: choose the straight branch on the heading increment
relative to the heading's rounding unit, or use the half-angle chord form. **Until INT-FSIM-05 passes,
`planar_drive` is not an oracle**, and every known answer for a wheeled base uses an independent formula owned by
`frob-conformance`. This also serves bet BET-04's control: the admission tests for the reported defects must fail
on the pinned commit if the defects are real, and pass after the upstream fix.

## Technical approach

**The independent formula.** For a differential drive with wheel speeds v_l and v_r and track b, over a step Δt
with constant speeds: v = (v_l + v_r)/2, ω = (v_r − v_l)/b, δ = ω·Δt, and

```text
Δp = v·Δt·sinc(δ/2)·( cos(θ + δ/2), sin(θ + δ/2) ),     θ ← θ + δ,     sinc(x) = sin(x)/x,  sinc(0) = 1
```

This is the chord of the arc: its length is v·Δt·sinc(δ/2) and it points along the mean heading. It has no branch
on ω and no difference of nearly equal sines, so the near-straight case loses nothing.

Module `crates/frob-conformance/src/oracles/planar_chord.rs` (DESIGN):
1. `sinc(x)`: for abs(x) ≤ 2⁻¹³ return 1 − x²/6 (the dropped term x⁴/120 is below half a unit in the last place
   there); otherwise sin(x)/x with the deterministic sine of `fs-math`.
2. `chord_step(pose, v, ω, Δt) -> pose` implements the formula. The heading is carried as a unit complex number
   (c, s) with an integer turn counter, the project's convention for a revolute coordinate, and is advanced by
   multiplying by (cos δ, sin δ). No raw angle is accumulated, so a tiny δ is never absorbed by a large θ.
3. `chord(pose, v, ω, T) -> pose` is the one-shot form with δ = ω·T.
4. Inputs and outputs are `frob_core::qty` quantities (metres, seconds, radians per second); the module depends
   on bedrock-direct crates only and shares no code with `fs-mbd`.

**INT-FSIM-05, four families, tolerance 10⁻¹² m (TARGET).** Each family is run (a) on the independent formula
against a hand value or against itself in another form, and (b) on `fs-mbd::planar_drive` through a test-only
adapter in `crates/frob-fsim/tests/admission/int_fsim_05.rs`, against the independent formula.
1. *Exact straight travel*: ω = 0 at headings 0, π/2, 32 and 10⁶ rad; Δp = v·T·(cos θ, sin θ).
2. *Near-zero curvature*: the case above, 400 steps of 1/400 s. Expected: within 10⁻¹² m of one metre along the
   heading, about (0.83422336, 0.55142668) m (the total heading change is 1.2·10⁻¹² rad, so the lateral offset is
   about 6·10⁻¹³ m, inside the tolerance).
3. *Wrapped against unwrapped headings*: θ = 32 rad and θ = 32 − 10π rad with the same wheel speeds give the same
   displacement.
4. *Incremental against one-shot*: v = 1 m/s, ω = 0.5 rad/s, T = 2 s in 800 steps equals the one-shot chord; and
   the hand case, unit speed through a half turn from θ = 0 in one second (δ = π): the chord is
   (0, 2/π) = (0, 0.636620) m.

**What is recorded for the sibling.** For each family: the sibling's displacement, the independent value, the
difference, and for family 2 the field `zero_translation_reproduces: true | false` with the returned vector. The
gate status for `planar_drive` as an oracle is `pass` only if all four families agree to 10⁻¹²; otherwise it is
`fail` with the interim posture adopted, and the conformance oracle registry keeps `fs-mbd::planar_drive` marked
`not-an-oracle`. A `fail` here is a result, not a blocker: the posture (the independent formula) is the fallback
named in advance.

**Planted negative control.** `frob-conformance` holds a twelve-line copy of the reported logic (threshold branch,
difference of sines) under the name `planted::threshold_branch_drive`, never linked outside tests. On family 2 it
must return exactly (0, 0); the test asserts that the harness flags it. A second planted variant replaces sinc by
1 and must fail the half-turn case with (0, 1) m.

**Catalog smoke.** The G1 and iiwa7 builders of `robot_models.rs` are loaded through the airlock and their link
and joint counts are recorded (reported for G1: 30 links kept). No parity claim is made from them here.

**Record.** `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md` entry with commit, command, output hash, receipt hash, the four families'
worst differences, the reproduction flag, and the state of FD-FSIM-11. Claim: Known answer; class `Tested`; tier
host and referee; determinism D0 for the independent formula on one platform's deterministic functions.

- [ ] The planted `threshold_branch_drive` returns exactly zero translation on the near-straight case and is caught; the sinc-free variant fails the half-turn case.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-conformance oracles::planar_chord`:
- `chord_half_turn_is_two_over_pi`: expects (0, 0.6366197723675814) to 10⁻¹².
- `chord_straight_at_headings`: θ ∈ {0, π/2, 32, 10⁶}; expects v·T·(cos θ, sin θ) to 10⁻¹².
- `chord_near_zero_curvature_400_steps`: expects distance from (cos 32, sin 32) ≤ 10⁻¹² m and norm within 10⁻¹² of 1 + 3·10⁻¹³ m.
- `chord_wrapped_equals_unwrapped`; `chord_incremental_equals_one_shot` (800 steps against one).
- `sinc_series_matches_quotient_at_switch`: both forms agree to one unit in the last place at abs(x) = 2⁻¹³.
- `heading_increment_not_absorbed`: after 400 steps of δ = 3·10⁻¹⁵ at θ = 32 the heading has advanced by 1.2·10⁻¹² rad (to 10⁻¹⁴ rad; each complex multiply rounds at about 10⁻¹⁶), where a raw `f64` angle would show an advance of exactly zero.
- `planted_threshold_branch_returns_zero` and `planted_no_sinc_fails_half_turn`: the planted defects produce their wrong values and the comparison harness reports failure.
`cargo test -p frob-fsim --test admission int_fsim_05`:
- `sibling_planar_drive_four_families`: computes and writes the per-family differences; never panics on a mismatch, records it.
- `sibling_zero_translation_flag_recorded`: asserts the flag is present in the record, whichever value it has.
- `catalog_builders_load`: G1 and iiwa7 load; counts recorded.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/suite_admission_m0.sh` (bead N33b-20), step `int_fsim_05`: runs both test targets and
`frob doctor --json`; asserts the gate appears under FD-FSIM with its status and that the oracle registry mark
matches the status. Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, metrics
`chord_half_turn_err_m` (threshold 10⁻¹²), `near_straight_err_m` (10⁻¹²), `sibling_near_straight_norm_m`,
`zero_translation_reproduces`, `families_passed_by_sibling` (of 4), and on failure the family, the step index and
both vectors.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-776: the chord (0, 2/π) m. KA-775 and KA-003: the near-straight case, one metre, about (0.83422336, 0.55142668)
m, against which the reported branch returns exactly zero. CX-352 and CX-182: the threshold branch as a planted
defect. Metamorphic: rotating the initial heading by φ rotates Δp by φ; scaling both wheel speeds and 1/Δt by k
leaves Δp unchanged; reversing both wheel speeds returns the base to its start after the same duration.
Determinism: two runs give bit-identical results; debug and release agree; across platforms the oracle agrees
inside the error budget of the deterministic elementary functions.

## Considerations

- A reported number stays a report until this test records it. If the defect does not reproduce at the pin (the sibling may have changed), the record says so, the four families decide admission, and FD-FSIM-11 is closed or re-scoped with that evidence.
- Switch-over rule of FD-FSIM-11: re-run INT-FSIM-05 by a compatibility train; only then may `planar_drive` be admitted as an oracle. The independent formula stays regardless, as the differential partner.
- The formula assumes ideal rolling. It is retired for an operating region where the drive slips: there the frictional model answers and the kinematic prediction is withdrawn.
- Risk row "a foundation defect surfaced late": its signal is a known answer that cites a module before its admission test has passed; the registry test in the acceptance list is the guard.
- Large headings such as 10⁶ rad test the turn-counter representation; a heading stored as a raw angle would lose increments below 2⁻³³ there.
