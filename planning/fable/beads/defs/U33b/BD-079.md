## Background

The contact subsystem ("Traction", crate `frob-contact`, layer 3) represents geometry in two ways, by decision:
**everything static is a distance field** and **everything that moves is a union of convex primitives**: spheres,
capsules, boxes, cylinders and rounded boxes (convex hulls are Frontier and are not in this bead). A robot's
collision primitives are fitted from its link Regions by the model compiler with a recorded fitting error, or are
authored. Fixed primitive kinds are what make pair tests analytic and array shapes fixed, which thousands of
batched worlds need. The alternative, general meshes for moving bodies, was rejected for the fast tiers: traversal
breaks fixed shapes, and it stays a CPU-tier path for certificates and audits.

The referee tier steps contact with FrankenSim's solver, and FrankenSim's contact step takes its contact set from
the caller. FACT: FrankenSim's narrow phase is convex against convex for primitives, through a certified
Frank–Wolfe separation in `fs-query` (`fs-query/src/convex.rs`, 616 lines), and it refuses signed-distance pairs,
heightfields and mesh manifolds as a missing capability. It has no batched path. So contact generation is this
project's work in `frob-contact`, and `fs-query` is the reference it is checked against where both apply
(upstream work item FD-FSIM-03 adds to the sibling support maps for capsules and hulls and for pair gaps beyond
endpoint checks).

This bead delivers the CPU `f64` primitives and pair routines that every later tier is compared with, the
production airlock adapter for convex queries, and the function that turns a posed set of primitives into a
referee contact set. Its gate is GATE-TRAC-02 (claim: Parity; TARGET): **primitive pair distances and normals
agree with `fs-query` within 10⁻⁹ m in `f64` on 10⁶ random pairs.** It also re-runs the admission gate INT-FSIM-08
at the M1 pin through the production adapter (the M0 first test is bead N33b-10): a box-on-plane support query
equals the closed form, and a mesh-backed interior is not admitted by that test.

## Scope

In: primitive types and their support functions; analytic pair routines returning gap, normal and witness points
with an error bound; the half-space pair; the use of the `SceneQuery` and `GeometryKernel` adapters of N33a-16 (a dependency:
the traits and their in-process adapters are that bead's) for convex separation and support queries; `contact_candidates` for a posed primitive set on the referee; the 10⁶-pair
parity run and its record; INT-FSIM-08 at the M1 pin.

Out: sample spheres against distance fields (N18a-10); the CPU broad phase and self-collision filter (N18a-11);
the swept interface and `NarrowHit` (N18a-13); per-step contact-set generation inside the referee step and
compliant feet (BD-080); the batched kernels `K-np-prim`, `K-bp-sort`, `K-bp-pairs` (BD-158), which must match
these routines; fitting primitives to Regions (BD-062); convex hulls, meshes and thin primitives (other Traction
beads); self-collision analysis A-13 (BD-104).

## Technical approach

Modules (in the module tree of the crate charter N18a-01: primitives under `geom/`, detection under `detect/`):
`crates/frob-contact/src/geom/prim/{types,support,pairs,halfspace}.rs`, `src/detect/referee_sets.rs`; airlock side
`crates/frob-fsim/src/geometry/query.rs`.

```rust
// DESIGN: intent, not signatures. Lengths are frob_core::qty metres; poses are frob-lie SE(3) elements.
pub enum Primitive {
    Sphere     { r: Metres },
    Capsule    { r: Metres, half_len: Metres },             // axis along local z
    Box        { half: [Metres; 3] },
    Cylinder   { r: Metres, half_len: Metres },             // axis along local z
    RoundedBox { half: [Metres; 3], r: Metres },            // a box inflated by r
}
pub struct PairResult {
    pub gap: Metres,                 // signed: negative means penetration depth along `normal`
    pub normal: UnitVec3<World>,     // from body A towards body B
    pub witness_a: Point3<World>, pub witness_b: Point3<World>,
    pub eps: Metres,                 // bound on the error of `gap` (rounding and iteration)
    pub degenerate: Option<Degeneracy>,   // ParallelFaces | Concentric | ParallelAxes: witness not unique
}
```

**Support functions** (unit direction d in the primitive's frame; h(d) is the support value): sphere r; capsule
r + half_len·abs(d_z); box Σ half_k·abs(d_k); cylinder half_len·abs(d_z) + r·√(d_x² + d_y²); rounded box
Σ half_k·abs(d_k) + r. In world coordinates h_W(d) = c·d + h(Rᵀ·d) for centre c and rotation R.

**Pair routines.** Closed forms where they exist, a certified iteration elsewhere:
1. Sphere against anything: the distance from the sphere centre to the other shape, minus r. Point–segment for a
   capsule, point–box by clamping, point–cylinder by clamping radius and height.
2. Capsule–capsule: the closest points of two segments (the standard clamped solution of the 2×2 system, with the
   parallel case handled by a fixed tie rule), minus both radii.
3. Capsule–box, box–box and rounded-box pairs: separating-axis test over face normals and edge cross products for
   overlap and depth; closest features for the separated case; a rounded box is its core box with r subtracted
   from the gap.
4. Cylinder pairs other than with a sphere: a deterministic convex separation on the support functions (a
   Frank–Wolfe iteration with a fixed cap of 64 iterations, DESIGN), returning a certified lower and upper bound
   on the distance; `eps` is half their difference. A pair whose bound does not close to 10⁻¹⁰ m within the cap is
   reported with its `eps`, never silently accepted.
5. Any primitive A against a solid half-space B = {x : n·x ≤ o} with unit outward normal n: the lowest point of
   the primitive along n is c·n − h(−Rᵀ·n), so gap = (c·n − o) − h(−Rᵀ·n); the result's normal (from A towards B)
   is −n, `witness_a` is the primitive's support point in direction −n and `witness_b` is its projection onto
   the plane.
6. Degenerate cases never return an arbitrary answer: the normal follows a documented tie rule (lowest axis
   index, then positive sign), the witness is the midpoint of the contact feature, and `degenerate` is set.

Every routine is a pure function of its inputs, uses the deterministic elementary functions of `fs-math`, and
allocates nothing. The order of the pair (A, B) is canonical (lower primitive kind first, then lower body index);
swapping the arguments negates the normal and exchanges the witnesses exactly.

**Airlock adapters (feature `geometry`).** `SceneQuery::convex_separation(a, b)` and
`GeometryKernel::support(shape, d)` wrap `fs-query`'s convex routines; `SceneQuery::support_box` and
`keep_out_box` wrap `fs-scene`'s role-tagged boxes. A sibling refusal (a mesh interior, a signed-distance pair, a
heightfield) becomes a typed `RefereeRefusal` carrying the upstream reason and is never retried with a simplified
shape. Memoized query results carry the stack-lock digest in their key.

**Referee contact candidates.** `contact_candidates(poses, prims, halfspaces, margin) -> Vec<PairResult>`:
1. enumerate candidate pairs in canonical order from the pair filter (adjacency exclusions are the model's);
2. evaluate each pair with the routine above and, where `fs-query` applies, also through the airlock;
3. on the referee the airlock value is authoritative; the local value is kept beside it and a difference above
   10⁻⁹ m is a recorded parity defect for that pair, not a silent choice;
4. return pairs with gap below `margin`, in canonical order, each with its material pair identifiers.

**GATE-TRAC-02, the parity run.** 10⁶ pairs addressed by `(seed, index)`: kinds drawn uniformly from the pair
matrix; sizes in [5 mm, 0.5 m]; relative rotations uniform; centre distances from 20% overlap to two sizes apart,
with one pair in ten drawn in the near-touching band abs(gap) < 1 mm. For each pair where `fs-query` returns a
result: abs(gap − gap_ref) ≤ 10⁻⁹ m; for non-degenerate pairs also ‖witness − witness_ref‖ ≤ 10⁻⁹ m on both bodies
and ‖normal − normal_ref‖ ≤ 10⁻⁹. Pairs the sibling refuses (reported per kind) are `not-comparable` and are
counted, never dropped from the total; a kind with zero comparable pairs is a finding. Degenerate pairs are
compared on gap and normal only.

**INT-FSIM-08 at the M1 pin.** The box-on-plane closed form g = c_z − Σ half_k·abs(r_k,z) (hand cases 0.01 m for an
axis-aligned cube of half-extent 0.5 m centred at z = 0.51 m; 0.292893 m for the cube rotated 45° about x and
centred at z = 1 m) and the seeded family of N33b-10 (1,000 boxes, half-extents in [0.01, 1] m, uniform random
rotation, centre height in [0, 3] m, drawn from `fs-rand` keyed by `(seed, case_index)`; gap within
10⁻¹²·max(1, extent) m and normal within 10⁻¹² of (0, 0, 1)) through the production adapter, and the typed refusal
of a mesh-backed interior. A new admission record is written at the M1 pin (the production admission: an
admission is per use and per pin, and N40-07's exit check reads it); the M0 record stays as history.

**Recording (N33a-07's form; one recorder per gate part, N38b-19).** GATE-TRAC-02 has one part (M1) and this bead
is its recorder; the Traction chapter has no register bead, so this bead fills the row's `tier` (`g2_parity`) and
`controls` (the four planted defects). The parity run above writes the harness receipt, and the row is entered
from it with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TRAC-02 --receipt <hash>` by a
session that did not implement this bead. INT-FSIM-08's one register part (M0) is N33b-10's row: this bead writes
no INT-FSIM-08 gate row. Its M1 admission record is written in N33a-07's form: the two hand cases, the seeded
family and the mesh refusal are bound as a second entry of `g1_known_answers` naming INT-FSIM-08 (milestone M1,
its own id beside N33b-10's M0 entry, since the table rejects a duplicate id), wrapping the airlock
lane `cargo test -p frob-fsim fsim08_production_adapter`, with N33b-10's `full-extent-as-half` and
`unrotated-support` re-run against the production adapter and the bounding-sphere substitution as its controls;
`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations --
INT-FSIM-08` writes the harness receipt; the admission record, written by a session that did not implement the
adapter, cites it as `harness_receipt`.

Evidence of outputs: claim Parity (the gate) and Known answer (the admission); class `Tested`; tier `referee` and
host; determinism D0 for the closed-form routines and D2 for the iterative one on one platform.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-TRAC-02: on 10⁶ seeded random pairs, distances and normals agree with `fs-query` within 10⁻⁹ m in `f64` wherever the sibling answers; the comparable and not-comparable counts per pair kind are reported (the seed done-condition).
- [ ] Every pair routine returns gap, normal, both witnesses and `eps`; the iterative routine's `eps` is at most 10⁻¹⁰ m on the gate's pairs or the pair is reported.
- [ ] Swapping the pair negates the normal and exchanges witnesses exactly; degenerate pairs set `degenerate` and follow the tie rule.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-08 passes at the M1 pin through the production adapter (both hand gaps and 1,000 seeded boxes to 10⁻¹² m), and a mesh-backed interior is refused by type and recorded as not admitted.
- [ ] `contact_candidates` returns pairs in canonical order, independent of input order and of worker count.
- [ ] Negative controls are caught: a box routine that uses full extents as half-extents; a capsule routine that omits one radius; a normal returned from B towards A; an adapter that substitutes a bounding sphere when the sibling refuses.
- [ ] `frob-contact` names no `fs-query` type; all sibling calls pass through `frob-fsim` (`xtask check-deps`).
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-contact` gains the primitive and pair sections (types, determinism class, error bounds, refusals); the parity run is the Gauntlet's cargo form (ADR-GAUNT-01, BD-025: there is no `frob conformance` command) `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g2_parity -- GATE-TRAC-02` (KA-425 is a G2 row), with `--features gauntlet-mutations` when its controls run, and it writes a hash-named receipt; a long parity run honours cancellation at pair-batch boundaries (a cancelled run returns a typed `Cancelled` outcome and writes no receipt and no gate row). (No registered verb takes a raw primitive pair: `contact.probe`, N18b-29, evaluates laws at a contact state, and the user-side path to these routines is the referee step of BD-080; this bead adds no verb.)
- [ ] GATE-TRAC-02 is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with measured worst differences, the seed and the receipt hash, entered from the parity run's receipt with `record_gate --gate GATE-TRAC-02 --receipt <hash>` by a session that did not implement this bead, the four controls caught in that run. INT-FSIM-08's M1 admission record cites the harness receipt of its `g1_known_answers` entry; this bead writes no INT-FSIM-08 gate row (its one part, M0, is N33b-10's).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-contact prim`:
- `sphere_sphere_hand`: radii 0.1 and 0.2 m, centres 0.5 m apart on x: gap 0.2 m, normal (1, 0, 0), witnesses at x = 0.1 and x = 0.3.
- `capsule_capsule_crossed`: two capsules of radius 0.05 m, axes along x and y, offset 0.3 m in z: gap 0.2 m, normal (0, 0, 1).
- `capsule_capsule_parallel_tie_rule`: parallel axes; `degenerate == ParallelAxes`, gap exact, witness at the overlap midpoint.
- `box_box_face_gap` (two unit cubes, centres 1.25 m apart on x: gap 0.25 m) and `box_box_penetration_depth` (centres 0.9 m apart: gap −0.1 m).
- `box_box_edge_edge`: one cube rotated 45° about z and 45° about x; compared with a brute-force minimum over a 10⁶-point surface sampling to 10⁻⁴ m and with `fs-query` to 10⁻⁹ m.
- `rounded_box_is_box_minus_r`; `cylinder_plane_tilted` (radius 0.1 m, half-length 0.2 m, tilted 30°, centre height 0.5 m: gap = 0.5 − (0.2·cos 30° + 0.1·sin 30°) = 0.276795 m).
- `halfspace_matches_support_function_1000`; `pair_swap_antisymmetry_1000`; `canonical_order_independent_of_input_order`.
- `iterative_eps_bound_holds`: the certified interval contains the closed-form distance for sphere-inflated test shapes.
- `parity_fs_query_1e4` (default lane; the 10⁶ run is the long lane): asserts the 10⁻⁹ m bounds and reports the worst pair.
- `parity_run_cancelled_at_batch_boundary`: the parity runner's context is cancelled after the first batch of 10⁴ pairs; the outcome is `Cancelled` with 10⁴ pairs done, and no receipt or gate row is written.
- `mutants_caught`: the four planted defects.
- Airlock: `geometry_mesh_interior_refused_typed`, `geometry_memo_key_has_stack_digest`, `fsim08_production_adapter_hand_cases`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/traction_primitives_parity.sh`: runs the hand-case lane (`cargo test -p frob-contact prim -- --exact`
on `sphere_sphere_hand`, `box_box_face_gap`, `cylinder_plane_tilted`, with `--format json`) and checks the logged
gap, normal and witnesses against the stated values; runs the INT-FSIM-08 lane through the production adapter and
validates its new admission record; then runs the long parity lane
(`FROB_TEST_SEED=<s> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g2_parity -- GATE-TRAC-02`, the ADR-GAUNT-01 form; the
script never writes the literal `frob conformance`, which BD-025's `no_conformance_command` lint refuses) under a
timeout, reads its receipt, and once more interrupted (SIGINT) after the log reports the first pair batch,
asserting the cancelled outcome in the log and no receipt and no gate row written; the run with
`--features gauntlet-mutations` must fail on each of the four planted defects. Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit
code, wall time, evidence block, metrics `pairs_total` (10⁶), `pairs_comparable`, `gap_diff_max_m` (threshold
10⁻⁹), `normal_diff_max` (10⁻⁹), `witness_diff_max_m` (10⁻⁹), `not_comparable_by_kind`, the digest of the result
table, and on failure the `(seed, index)` of the worst pair with both results. Summary names GATE-TRAC-02 and
INT-FSIM-08.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-425 (GATE-TRAC-02: agreement within 10⁻⁹ m in `f64` on 10⁶ random pairs). Hand cases as listed. Negative
controls: the four planted defects; the M0 probe's `full-extent-as-half` and `unrotated-support` mutants re-run
against the production adapter. Metamorphic: a rigid transform applied to both primitives leaves the gap unchanged
and transforms normal and witnesses; uniform scaling by s scales the gap by s; inflating both shapes by δ lowers
the gap by 2δ. Determinism: the 10⁶-pair result digest is identical twice on one machine, across worker counts,
and in debug and release; cross-platform equality is claimed for the closed-form routines only.

## Considerations

- Parity is asserted only where both sides apply. If the sibling refuses a pair kind entirely, that kind has no parity evidence and its batched kernel is checked against these CPU routines alone; the gate record says so per kind.
- The fitting error of a primitive to its Region is not part of this gate: it enters the gap error bound ε_g, and a clearance claim uses outer and inner enclosures, never a fitted primitive's nominal gap.
- Self-collision pairs come from a precomputed filter list; excluded pairs stay inspectable. That list is the model compiler's and the broad phase's concern, and `contact_candidates` takes it as input.
- Upstream item FD-FSIM-03 (P1): when support maps for capsules and hulls and pair gaps beyond endpoint checks land in FrankenSim, the switch-over rule is to re-run GATE-TRAC-02; the local routines stay as the reference for the batched kernels.
- Performance is not a goal here; these routines are the reference. The envelope: the 10⁶-pair lane runs in minutes on eight cores and far under 16 GiB.
- The half-space pair is the only static shape in this bead. Distance fields, heightfields and thin primitives have their own beads and their own error bounds.
