## Background

The **referee** tier advances each world by one **transaction per accepted physics interval**: a transaction
either commits a complete, checked interval or leaves no trace. Its contact dynamics are FrankenSim's, reached
through the airlock `frob-fsim`. FrankenSim's sustained contact step keeps **fixed material witnesses and a fixed
contact frame**: the two points at which a contact acts (one on each body, in body coordinates) and the normal
and tangents are those the step was given, and it refuses tangentially separating witnesses. That limit is
declared upstream and enforced by typed refusal; bead BD-035 recorded the refusal at the pin (the first half of
admission gate INT-FSIM-07).

Real contacts move their witnesses: a wheel rolling over a sharp lip goes from the flat, to the edge, to the top;
a sole slides; a foot rocks heel to toe. A contact also carries **tangential memory**: the stick anchor of a
regularized friction law, the accumulated tangential displacement and the warm-start impulse. If that memory is
kept on a contact whose feature changed, the anchor points along a direction that is no longer in the tangent
plane and a couple appears that the contact forces do not explain. The invariant (INV-TRAC-06): tangential memory
is transported or reset at every witness change, each reset is an event, and memory is never kept silently on a
changed feature.

Decision (DESIGN): until the sibling gains a moving-surface owner (upstream work item FD-FSIM-12, P1), witnesses
are **migrated above the airlock, between calls**. The transaction cuts time so that each call to the sibling's
sustained step sees an interval short enough that its witnesses are fixed inside FrankenSim's own gate, and
between calls `frob-contact` re-localizes the witnesses and transports or resets the memory. If the sibling still
refuses, the refusal is surfaced by name and the `strict` profile (the same transaction on this project's own
kernels, due at M2) adjudicates. Rejected: retrying the sibling with a relaxed tangential tolerance (the airlock
never weakens inputs); dropping the contact and re-creating it (that discards memory silently and biases friction
low at every edge); waiting for FD-FSIM-12 (no milestone waits on an upstream change).

This decides what M1 may claim: referee rollouts over planes and primitive obstacles are admitted, and a wheel
climbing a riser is admitted only through this event-located step. The gate this bead completes is INT-FSIM-07 in
full: the flat-to-edge-to-top transition returns the typed refusal through the airlock at the pin (done in
BD-035), **and the same transition completes under witness migration with the energy ledger closed to its stated
residual**.

## Technical approach

The transaction, for reference, with the steps this bead touches marked `*`:

```text
T1  S ← checkpoint(X_committed)                    * contact memory is in the restore set
T2  for attempt in 0 ..= 3:                          attempt selects a numerical plan only
T3    X ← restore(S); work ← [(t0, t1)]
T4    while work is not empty: (a, b) ← work.pop_front()
T4.2    broad phase on swept bounds; narrow phase returns features, normal, witnesses, material pair, gap, ε_g
T4.3    t* ← earliest guard change in (a, b]         * adds the witness-change guard
T4.4    split at t* on the dyadic grid; refuse FROB-REF-220 past L_max = 16 or E_max = 64
T4.5    advance sustained-contact dynamics from a to b with the active set held fixed   * the sibling call
T4.6    at an event: coupled impulse once per island
T4.7    invalidate affected witnesses, redetect, migrate tangential memory              * this bead
T5    checks, including the energy-ledger residual  * migration terms are booked before the check
T6    COMMIT, or T7 after the last attempt: restore(S), mark Faulted (FROB-REF-221)
```

**1. Contact memory in the restore set.** The checkpoint of T1 contains, per contact: the stable contact
identity, witness pair, feature pair, contact frame, stick anchor, accumulated tangential displacement, warm-start
impulse and the active-set flag. A retry restores all of it. Omitting any field makes a retried interval differ
from a first attempt, which INV-KIN-02 forbids.

**2. The witness-change guard (T4.3).** For each active contact the narrow phase reports the closest feature pair
at a. A guard function is positive while the closest feature of each body is unchanged and changes sign when the
contact point reaches a feature boundary (the lip's edge line, the end of an edge, a face boundary of a box). Its
earliest crossing in (a, b] is bracketed like any other guard: conservative advancement on the distance to the
boundary divided by a bound on the contact point's tangential speed, then bisection on the dyadic grid
t0 + ℓ₀·k/2ᴸ, L ≤ 16. A crossing is an event of kind `witness-change`, so the sub-interval handed to the sibling
in T4.5 ends there and its witnesses are fixed throughout.

**3. Migration at T4.7.** After every event, for the islands the event touched:
1. invalidate the witnesses of the affected contacts;
2. redetect: run the narrow phase at b;
3. call the migration function (from the algorithm bead) for each matched contact. It re-localizes the witnesses
   by closest features with one consistent world contact point at touching, and classifies the change as
   `same-feature`, `adjacent-feature` (face to edge to face around a lip) or `new-contact`;
4. `same-feature`: memory is kept. `adjacent-feature` with the world contact point moved continuously (inside
   the tolerance δ_w): memory is **transported**: rotated by the minimal rotation that takes the old normal to
   the new one and projected onto the new tangent plane. `new-contact`, or a jump of the contact point: memory is
   **reset** to zero and a `witness-reset` event is emitted with the contact identity, both feature pairs and the
   reason. A law with no anchor (exact Coulomb) transports warm starts only;
5. the new witnesses, frame and memory become the inputs of the next sibling call. The event list of the
   interval records every `witness-change` and `witness-reset` with its rational time.

**4. Energy bookkeeping (DESIGN; the reason is ledger closure).** A regularized law stores tangential elastic
energy E_t = ½·k_t·‖anchor displacement‖² per contact. Transport by rotation leaves the magnitude unchanged; the
projection onto the new tangent plane can shorten it; a reset removes it. The migration call returns
ΔE = E_t(after) − E_t(before) ≤ 0 for each contact, and the transaction debits −ΔE to the ledger's dissipation
account under the cause `witness-migration` at the event's instant. It is never credited to mechanical energy.
With that entry the ledger identity holds across the event: the change of kinetic, potential and stored energy
equals port work minus dissipation, to the ledger's residual.

**5. A refusal that persists.** If the sibling returns its fixed-witness refusal for a sub-interval although the
guard located no change (the two detectors disagree at the tolerance), attempt 1 of the retry ladder halves the
largest sub-interval and forces bisection. If the refusal remains at the finest dyadic cell, the transaction ends
with `FROB-REF-721` (referee capacity or fixed-witness gate), carrying the upstream reason verbatim, the contact
identity and the time. Nothing from the failed interval is published. The refusal is counted in the referee's
coverage report under `fixed-witness-gate`; its ranked repairs are a documented patch reduction and
`--profile strict`. The sibling is never called with weakened inputs.

**6. INT-FSIM-07 in full.** Fixture `wheel-over-lip`, defined in `frob-conformance` by BD-086 with the geometry of BD-035's probe: wheel radius R = 0.10 m, mass 5 kg,
sill height s = 0.03 m with a sharp 90° lip, friction μ = 0.8, constant axle torque τ = 5 N·m, base interval
h = 2 ms. The wheel can pivot about the lip because τ exceeds m·g·√(2·R·s − s²) = 5 × 9.81 × 0.071414 = 3.503 N·m.
The run completes when the wheel centre is above the top surface and has advanced one radius beyond the lip. Pass:
(a) the run commits every interval with no refusal; (b) the environment-side feature sequence of the wheel's
contact is `flat`, `edge`, `top`; (c) the energy ledger closes over the whole run and across each `witness-change`
event with relative residual at most 10⁻⁹ (the referee's stated residual); (d) every reset appears as an event.
A hand check on the ledger: when the centre has risen by s the potential account has gained
m·g·s = 5 × 9.81 × 0.03 = 1.4715 J, and the axle work is τ times the wheel's rotation angle.

What happens at each transition on this fixture (geometry, not a tolerance choice). The wheel first touches the
lip while it still rests on the flat: with the centre at height R, the lip point is at distance R from the centre
when the horizontal offset is √(2·R·s − s²) = 0.071414 m, so the edge contact appears as a **`new-contact`** (zero
memory, one `witness-reset` event) beside the active flat contact, the two are resolved in one island at T4.6, and
the flat contact then separates as the wheel pivots. The edge contact keeps its feature while the normal turns
about the lip; it reaches the top face when the centre is directly above the lip, where the edge normal is
vertical and equal to the top face's normal, so the edge-to-top change is **`adjacent-feature`** with a continuous
contact point and a normal rotation of zero up to the located event cell, and its memory is **transported**.

**7. The diagnostic switch for part 1 (DESIGN).** `frob sim run … --tier referee --no-migration` turns off the
witness-change guard and T4.7, so that the sibling's own fixed-witness gate is reached from the user's side. It is
accepted only with `--tier referee`, it sets `diagnostic: true` in the envelope's evidence, and a run made with it
can enter no claim, gate record or comparison (the evidence check refuses it). It exists so that part 1 of
INT-FSIM-07 can be shown through the verb, and is the flag the defect report FDEF-04 uses.

Outputs: claim Known answer; class `Tested`; tier `referee`; determinism D2 per platform. The interval record
stores the event list (kind, rational time, feature identifiers) and the attempt index that committed; the run's
telemetry carries the same events as the event channels `witness-change` and `witness-reset` of the `contact`
stream (event kind, never decimated), which this bead declares.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] INT-FSIM-07 in full: the flat-to-edge-to-top transition completes under witness migration with the energy ledger closed to a relative residual of at most 10⁻⁹ (the seed done-condition), and BD-035's refusal record is cited as part 1.
- [ ] The environment-side feature sequence is `flat`, `edge`, `top`: the edge contact starts as `new-contact` (one `witness-reset` event) while the flat contact is still active, the flat contact then separates, and the edge-to-top change is `adjacent-feature` with transported memory and a normal rotation below 1° at the located event.
- [ ] `--no-migration` is accepted only with `--tier referee`, marks the evidence `diagnostic: true`, and a run made with it is refused as input to any claim or gate record.
- [ ] Each sibling call spans an interval with fixed witnesses: the count of fixed-witness refusals during the run is zero, and the count of `witness-change` events is at least two.
- [ ] Every transport and reset books its ΔE to the dissipation account `witness-migration`; with the booking disabled (mutant) the ledger check fails.
- [ ] A forced retry of any interval that contains a witness change reproduces the first attempt's digest (INV-KIN-02): contact memory is in the restore set.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: `keep-memory` (memory copied unchanged across a feature change), `silent-reset` (a reset with no event and no ledger entry), `restore-omits-contact-memory`, `weaken-and-retry`.
- [ ] A persisting sibling refusal ends the transaction with `FROB-REF-721`, the upstream reason, no published state, and one count in the coverage report.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-07's M1 part is recorded in `registries/gates.toml` with the measured residual, the commit, the receipt hash and the seed, so that with BD-035's M0 part the row reads passed in both parts; the capability is reachable through `frob sim run --tier referee --json`, and cancellation at an interval boundary leaves committed state only.
- [ ] The M1 part is measured and recorded in N33a-07's form (one recorder per part): this bead binds KA-778's migration half and KA-008 at `@referee` in `g1_known_answers` as entries naming INT-FSIM-07 that wrap `wheel_over_lip_completes` and read its residual and event sequence, with the four mutants as the gate's controls; `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features gauntlet-mutations -- INT-FSIM-07` writes the harness receipt; the M1 admission record cites it as `harness_receipt`; and the part is entered from the same receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate INT-FSIM-07 --receipt <hash> --part M1` by a session that did not implement the migration. A `--no-migration` run is never cited.

## Considerations

- OPEN-TRAC-5 fixes the migration tolerances (continuity tolerance δ_w, default the sample radius; the largest normal rotation transported, default 30°). On this fixture neither limit is approached at a change: the normal's turn from arcsin(0.071414/0.10) ≈ 45.6° off vertical at first touch to vertical happens continuously while the contact stays on the edge feature (same-feature, memory kept), the flat-to-edge change is a new contact, and the edge-to-top change has zero rotation. A design whose change exceeds a limit falls back to reset with an event, which is the fail-closed default; the record states the class of every change.
- More than 64 events in one base interval, or an event that cannot be bracketed within 16 dyadic levels, ends the transaction with `FROB-REF-220`. A wheel chattering on an edge can reach that; it is a refusal, never an approximation.
- RISK-31 and bet BET-34: the referee must complete the scenes where decisions are made. This bead removes the fixed-witness cause of non-completion for rolling and rocking contacts; the 21-contact capacity remains (FD-FSIM-13).
- Switch-over rule of FD-FSIM-12: when the sibling owns moving surfaces, the fidelity ledger is re-baselined once and this wiring becomes a thin pass-through; the fixture and the gate stay.
- The lockstep tiers do not locate events and have no witnesses to migrate in this sense; their protection is the speculative margin, and what they miss is measured by the referee's audit.
- Cost: redetection after each event is a narrow-phase call on the affected islands only. No performance claim is made for the referee.
