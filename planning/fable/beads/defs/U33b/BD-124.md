## Background

A **study** is a registered plan to answer one comparative or absolute question about designs: "is B better than
A on this task", "does this candidate meet the requirement". Every comparison in the studio pairs episodes by
world address, so both designs meet the same scenes, disturbances and random draws. The statistics that turn
paired outcomes into a verdict come in two kinds, and the plan's decision is that a study **declares its type when
it is created and keeps it for its whole life** (invariant INV-ARENA-05):

| | Anytime-valid study | Fixed-n exact study |
|---|---|---|
| Inference | A betting e-process or a confidence sequence, valid at every stopping time | An exact interval or test at a sample size fixed in advance |
| Stopping | When the evidence crosses its threshold or the budget ends; unfinished episodes are cancelled | At n complete units, never earlier |
| Before the first episode | Null hypothesis, effect margin, bound on outcomes, betting rule, level, budget | n, the rule, the level, and a pre-analysis: which verdicts are reachable at that n, and the power at declared alternatives |
| Reports | The e-value, the confidence sequence, pairs used, the stopping reason | Counts, the interval, the verdict, and the pre-analysis hash |
| Interim information | Any; validity does not depend on it | Progress only. The verdict is computed once, at n |
| Typical use | Screening, racing, promotion between tiers, bisection | Registered A/B questions, equivalence claims, qualification of a finalist |

Why the types are never mixed. A fixed-n interval computed at the moment an anytime-valid study stops has no
coverage guarantee, and a fixed-n study that stops when its running p-value first looks good loses its level.
Counterexample (CX-266): an exact one-sided sign test at nominal level 5% on pairs that favour either design with
probability ½, looked at after every pair. After 8 looks the chance of a false rejection is 13/256 = 5.08%; after
16 it is 8.76%, after 32, 12.9%, and after 64, 17.4%.

Why a fixed-n study needs a pre-analysis. A frozen rule may be unable to return some of its verdicts at its n
(CX-265). In the first fixture's rule at n = 64 no outcome is a practical tie, because perfect concordance gives
the interval [−0.0662, +0.0662], wider than the ±0.05 band; a tie first becomes reachable at n = 86.

This bead builds both engines on the CPU (referee tier, determinism class D2), with their refusals. Its gates:
GATE-ARENA-05 (`eval.preanalyze` reproduces the fixture tables exactly and refuses the unreachable verdict),
GATE-ATEL-06 (the fixed-n planner reproduces the enumeration at n = 64 and n = 86 exactly; five comparison
refusals each fire on their planted violation, 100 of 100), and the M1 part of the statistics admission
INT-FSIM-14 (bead N33b-12). Devices, races over many designs and the qualification lane come later (BD-262,
BD-259).

## Technical approach

Crate `frob-arena`, module `study/{types,registry,anytime,fixed_n,pairing,refusal}.rs`.

```rust
// DESIGN. One type for a study's whole life (INV-ARENA-05); the two are never mixed inside one study.
pub enum Study { Anytime(AnytimeStudy), FixedN(FixedNStudy) }
pub struct AnytimeStudy { null: Hypothesis, margin: Qty,
    span: LossSpan,                 // a bound on the paired difference, fixed before the first observation
    betting_rule: RuleId, level: Ratio, budget: Budget }
pub struct FixedNStudy { n: u32, rule: RuleId, level: Ratio, margin: Qty,
    preanalysis: PreAnalysis }      // computed before the first episode; a study without one is refused
pub struct PreAnalysis { outcomes: u64, reachable: Set<StudyVerdict>, power: Vec<(Alternative, Ratio)>, hash: Blake3 }
pub enum StudyVerdict { Promote, Inferior, PracticalTie, Inconclusive }
pub struct PairCounts { a_only: u32, b_only: u32, both: u32, neither: u32, unscored: u32, planned: u32 }
// identity: a_only + b_only + both + neither + unscored = planned
```

**Pairing rules (requirements on every paired study; a violation is a refusal, not a warning).**
1. Pair by (world, replicate). A counter that depends on training progress is not part of an address
   (counterexample: seeds keyed by (world, epoch) on a 64-wide batch pair only morphologies whose indices agree
   modulo 64).
2. Exogenous fields are indexed by physical coordinate, not by event order: two robots on different paths read
   one space-indexed material field at their own contact points; pairing "the k-th friction draw" pairs nothing.
3. Each design is settled separately; a design that starts in penetration makes the pair `unscored`; a buildable
   design that stalls is a failed trial, not a missing one.
4. Pairing imposes no minimum number of episodes per design.
5. The accounting identity holds with integers, and the verdict states its denominator.
6. Both arms run at class D2 or better on one backend (at M1: the referee). A D4 run never enters a comparison.
When several episodes share a cluster (one building), the cluster is the unit of inference. A comparison of
designs declares its controller arm: a shared controller, controllers retuned with equal resources, or a
candidate that inherits weights (which declares its warm start and receives a matched transfer baseline).

**The anytime engine.**
1. For each metric the task must declare a span s: a bound on the paired difference d = (candidate − baseline)
   fixed before the first observation (FACT: the sibling `fs-eproc` requires an explicit `LossSpan`, which "must
   not be estimated from the race data"). A metric without one is refused for this study type.
2. The null is H₀: E[d] ≤ m for margin m ≥ 0. A betting e-process is E_k = Π_{i ≤ k} (1 + λ_i·(d_i − m)/(s + m)),
   with each bet λ_i in [0, 1) chosen from earlier observations only. Each factor is nonnegative because
   d_i − m ≥ −(s + m). Under H₀ the process is a nonnegative supermartingale started at 1, so the probability
   that it ever reaches 1/α is at most α (Ville's inequality). The engine calls the admitted sibling e-process
   through the airlock; the local reference rule, used for known answers and as the fallback, is the fixed bet
   λ = ½.
3. Episodes are consumed **in address order, never in completion order**, so preemption and reordering by a
   scheduler leave the e-value unchanged (INV-ATEL-02: the statistic is a pure function of per-address outcomes
   in address order). Completed episodes beyond the next unconsumed address wait.
4. Stop when E_k ≥ 1/α for the tested direction (verdict `settled`), when the confidence sequence (the set of
   margins not rejected at level α) lies inside [−m, +m] (verdict `tied`), or when the budget ends
   (`inconclusive`). On stopping, unfinished episodes are cancelled and counted as `unscored`.
5. Several metrics: the average of the per-metric e-values is an e-value under arbitrary dependence and tests the
   global null (FACT: `fs-eproc::combine_average`; the product is valid only for independent comparisons). A
   claim about one named metric uses that metric's own e-process, with the e-value form of the
   Benjamini–Hochberg rule when several are claimed (FACT: `fs-eproc::e_benjamini_hochberg`).
6. A race over N candidates at this milestone spends α/(N − 1) per pairwise comparison, so that a null race keeps
   its best candidate with probability at least 1 − α.

**The fixed-n engine.**
1. The rule is frozen before sampling: n pairs; b = pairs where only the candidate succeeds, a = pairs where only
   the baseline succeeds; effect (b − a)/n. Form two-sided 97.5% exact Clopper–Pearson intervals [L_b, U_b] and
   [L_a, U_a] for the two discordance probabilities: for x discordant pairs of n, the lower endpoint is the p at
   which P(Bin(n, p) ≥ x) = 0.0125 (0 when x = 0) and the upper endpoint the p at which P(Bin(n, p) ≤ x) = 0.0125
   (1 when x = n). The interval [L_b − U_a, U_b − L_a] covers the difference with probability at least 95%.
2. Verdicts, with margin 0.05 in the first fixture: `Promote` if L_b − U_a > +margin and the candidate violates no
   hard limit in a condition where the baseline does not; `Inferior` if U_b − L_a < −margin; `PracticalTie` if
   the interval lies inside [−margin, +margin]; otherwise `Inconclusive`.
3. Endpoints are computed with outward rounding and each comparison against the margin is decided exactly; an
   endpoint within rounding of the margin is resolved by exact rational evaluation of the binomial tail.
4. The verdict is computed once, at n complete pairs. Progress (counts so far) may be read at any time.
5. **Exact curtailment.** With k pairs resolved at counts (a_k, b_k) and r = n − k outstanding, the reachable final
   counts are {(a_k + i, b_k + j) : i, j ≥ 0, i + j ≤ r}. The study may stop early only if the frozen rule gives
   the same verdict for every one of them. No e-process may stop it.
6. A study whose declared verdict set contains a verdict the pre-analysis shows unreachable is refused unless the
   request acknowledges it by removing that verdict from the set.

**Refusals (families `5xx`: input or question refused, exit code 3).**

| Code | Raised when | Ranked repairs |
|---|---|---|
| `FROB-REF-552` | A study request mixes study types | Declare one type |
| `FROB-REF-532` | A fixed-n study has no pre-analysis, or names a verdict it cannot reach | Run the pre-analysis; raise n to the first value at which the verdict is reachable |
| `FROB-REF-533` | Paired statistics requested under an uncontrolled determinism class | Run both arms on a controlled profile |
| `FROB-REF-553` | A comparison mixes study types, or re-reads fixed-n data as a sequential study | Declare one type; open a new study |
| `FROB-REF-555` | A fixed-n comparison's declared verdict set contains an unreachable verdict | Raise n to the first reachable value (reported), or acknowledge |
| `FROB-REF-542` | A paired study under an uncontrolled determinism class, or with arms in different classes | Rerun both arms on a D2 profile |
| `FROB-REF-543` | An `anytime` study on a metric with no declared span | Declare the span in the task |
| `FROB-REF-556` | A pairing keyed by event order or by a training counter | Space-indexed fields; (world, replicate) addresses |

The first three are raised when a study is created; the last five when a comparison is requested or evaluated.

**What a study returns.** The verdict in the comparison vocabulary (`settled`, `tied`, `inconclusive`,
`incomparable`, `infeasible`, all legitimate dispositions); the statistic of its type; the pair counts with the
identity; the tier; each design's absolute hard-limit verdict beside the paired verdict (a relative result does
not establish that either design meets the mission); a rank-robustness field that reads `unchecked` at M1. A
verdict from this bead is a development result on discovery worlds; a claim goes through the qualification lane.
Evidence: claim Design comparison; class `Tested`; tier `referee`; determinism D2. The registry record, every
consumed outcome and the stopping reason go to the ledger.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A study that declares both types is refused with `FROB-REF-552` (the seed done-condition), and no API can change a study's type after creation (compile-time: the enum is fixed in the registry record; runtime: a type change is a new study).
- [ ] GATE-ARENA-05: the pre-analysis attached by the fixed-n engine equals the fixture tables exactly (2,145 / 521 / 521 / 0 / 1,103 at n = 64; 3,828 / 1,031 / 1,031 / 1 / 1,765 at n = 86; 8,385 / 2,517 / 2,517 / 4 / 3,347 at n = 128; 33,153 / 11,384 / 11,384 / 39 / 10,346 at n = 256), and a request naming `PracticalTie` at n = 64 is refused with `FROB-REF-532`.
- [ ] GATE-ATEL-06: the planner reproduces the enumeration at n = 64 and n = 86 exactly; `FROB-REF-553`, `-555`, `-542`, `-543` and `-556` each fire on their planted violation, 100 of 100.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-14, M1 part: the anytime engine runs on the admitted sibling e-process (or, if admission failed, on the local reference with that fact in every result's evidence).
- [ ] The e-value is identical under any completion order, preemption or worker count for a fixed set of outcomes.
- [ ] Exact curtailment stops only when every completion gives one verdict; on 10⁴ seeded studies the curtailed verdict equals the verdict at n in every case.
- [ ] The accounting identity holds in every result; a stopped anytime study counts cancelled episodes as `unscored`.
- [ ] The peeking control is caught: a fixed-n study polled for a verdict before n returns progress only.
- [ ] `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-arena` documents both types, the refusals, the determinism class and the evidence class; the gates are recorded with measured values.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-arena study`:
- `clopper_pearson_zero_of_64_upper`: expects 1 − 0.0125^(1/64) = 0.0662 (to four decimals) and the perfect-concordance interval [−0.0662, +0.0662].
- `fixed_n_promote_threshold_at_64`: with a = 0, b = 14 is `Inconclusive` and b = 15 is `Promote`; with a = 1, 2, 3, 4 the least promoting b is 17, 19, 21, 23.
- `preanalysis_counts_64_86_128_256`: the four rows above, exact; `tie_first_reachable_at_86_only_for_0_0`.
- `preanalysis_power_table`: P(prefer B) at discordance (0.17, 0), (0.25, 0.02), (0.40, 0.02) is 0.117, 0.406, 0.962 at n = 64 and 0.775, 0.906, above 0.999 at n = 128; P(tie) at (0.02, 0.02) is 0, 0.072, 0.427 at n = 64, 128, 256.
- `curtailment_equals_verdict_at_n_1e4`; `curtailment_never_stops_when_two_verdicts_reachable`.
- `eprocess_reference_hand_case`: s = 1, m = 0, λ = ½, differences (+1, +1, −1, +1): E = 1.5 × 1.5 × 0.5 × 1.5 = 1.6875.
- `eprocess_stops_at_pair_8`: all differences +1 at α = 0.05: 1.5⁷ = 17.09 < 20 ≤ 1.5⁸ = 25.63, so the study stops with `settled` at pair 8 and cancels the rest.
- `eprocess_address_order_invariance`: 1,000 seeded permutations of completion order give one e-value.
- `eprocess_null_crossing_rate`: 10⁴ seeded null sequences of ±1 with span 1; the count that ever reaches 20 is at most 565 (5% plus three standard deviations).
- `average_of_evalues`: (2, 4, 6) combine to 4; `product_refused_for_dependent_metrics`.
- `accounting_identity_always`: property test over random outcome tables.
- `refusal_552_532_533_at_creation`, `refusal_553_555_542_543_556_100_of_100` (each planted violation repeated over 100 seeded requests).
- `peeking_inflation_enumerated`: the exact false-rejection probability of the peeking sign test is 13/256 after 8 looks; the engine exposes no path that reproduces it (`verdict_before_n_is_progress_only`).

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-612 (the enumeration at n = 64, 86, 128, 256; 15 B-only pairs needed with no A-only pair, then 17, 19, 21, 23;
the chance of the single tie outcome at n = 86 under 5% discordance each way is 0.9⁸⁶ = 0.0116%). KA-613 (the
power table). KA-090 (perfect concordance gives [−0.0662, +0.0662]). Counterexamples CX-265 and CX-266. Negative
controls: the mixed-type request; the unreachable verdict; the peeking path; a pairing keyed by a training
counter; an e-process fed a span estimated from its own data. Metamorphic: exchanging the two arms exchanges
`Promote` and `Inferior` counts and leaves ties and inconclusives unchanged (the table is symmetric: 521 and 521);
relabelling world addresses consistently in both arms changes nothing. Determinism: every statistic is identical
twice on one machine, across worker counts, and in debug and release.

## Considerations

- The verb registry lists `study.plan`, `study.submit` and `study.status` at M2 and `eval.compare` at M3, while this seed work item delivers them on the CPU at M1; the engines here are complete at M1 and the registry rows for devices follow at their milestones.
- The refusal registry has two overlapping sets (552, 532, 533 from the task-and-study chapter; 553, 555, 542 from the comparison chapter). Both are kept, mapped to creation time and comparison time as in the table; the unreachable-verdict wording differs between chapters ("refused" and "refused unless acknowledged") and acknowledgement is implemented as removing the verdict from the declared set.
- One comparison is valid under optional stopping. It does nothing about an agent that submits fifty candidates to the same held-out worlds until one passes; that is the qualification lane's job, with fresh sealed clusters and the error budget α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)].
- If the sibling e-process is not admitted, the local reference rule is used and labelled. Its power is lower than an adaptive rule's; its validity is the same.
- Memory and time are negligible beside the episodes themselves. The fixed-n pre-analysis at n = 256 classifies 33,153 count pairs.
- OPEN question inherited from the comparison chapter: whether the race and value-of-information crates pass admission on the toolchain T; the default is local implementations against the same known answers.
