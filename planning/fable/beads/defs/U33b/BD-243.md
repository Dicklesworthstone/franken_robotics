## Background

The studio's static analyses A-01 to A-26 answer design questions without batch simulation. The seed work item
bundles three of them with one admission gate. This bead carries **A-09, connection curvature**, and the shared
wiring; A-14 (sensor coverage) and A-16 (sensitivity) are split into beads N33b-17 and N33b-18 because each is
several days of work in its own right, and all three register in `frob-analyze` (layer 4) as memo nodes.

**A-09 answers: how far does a kinematic locomotor move per cycle of shape change?** A snake-like crawler with
passive wheels, a kinematic wheeled base, or a robot reorienting in free fall moves by changing its shape. The
mathematics is construction C-08, "locomotion is holonomy". The configuration space Q is a principal bundle over
shape space Q/G, with G the group of body placements. A **connection** 𝔸 splits motion into shape change and
group motion:

```text
ξ = −𝔸(s)·ṡ + 𝕀(s)⁻¹·p        ξ: body velocity of the base;  s: shape;  p: momentum (zero in the cases below)
𝔸 = 𝕀⁻¹·𝕀_BS                  from the block form of the kinetic energy (zero-momentum flight), or
𝔸 = W_g⁻¹·W_s                 from Pfaffian constraints W_g·ξ + W_s·ṡ = 0 when rank W_g = dim G (ideal rolling)
```

For a closed loop in shape space (one gait cycle) the net displacement is the **holonomy**: the right-ordered
path exponential of −∮𝔸·ds, which to leading order in loop size is minus the integral of the **curvature** over
the enclosed surface:

```text
(D𝔸)ᵢⱼ = ∂ᵢ𝔸ⱼ − ∂ⱼ𝔸ᵢ − [𝔸ᵢ, 𝔸ⱼ]
g(loop) ≈ exp( −(D𝔸)ᵢⱼ · da )       small loop of area da in the (i, j) plane, traversed from i towards j
```

The payoff is a design oracle with no rollouts: curvature integrated over reachable shape space ranks two
morphologies by displacement per cycle before anything is simulated. The **sign of the bracket term** is the
classic mistake and is fixed by a hand fixture: for the constant connection 𝔸₁ = e_x, 𝔸₂ = e_y on SO(3), where
the bracket is the cross product, the curl vanishes and (D𝔸)₁₂ = −e_z, so a small loop of area da rotates the
body by +da about z. The same follows directly from exp(−a·e_x)·exp(−b·e_y)·exp(a·e_x)·exp(b·e_y) ≈
exp(a·b·e_z). A formula with +[𝔸ᵢ, 𝔸ⱼ] predicts −da and is wrong by twice the answer (theorem card T-20).

Honest scope (DESIGN). The analysis is exact for principally kinematic systems (kinematic wheeled bases,
snake-like crawlers, zero-momentum free flight). For dynamic walking it is a diagnostic, not a predictor. A
connection belongs to one contact stratum: a loop that crosses slip or lift-off cannot borrow its old connection.
Curvature computed in poor coordinates misleads, so minimum-perturbation coordinates are required. And it is
**not a wheel-layout test**: a mecanum base with misaligned rollers still has spanning curvature, as a
differential drive does; that defect is a rank drop in the connection, which the growth vector of A-07 reports
(the withdrawn use is recorded so nobody re-adds it). Tier: Frontier, among the first items to be cut if its
payoff is not measured; fallback: direct collocation over the same loops.

The admission gate INT-FSIM-10 covers what these analyses take from FrankenSim: forward-mode duals and
implicit-function sensitivities of `fs-ad` (`ift.rs`, `gradcheck.rs`) and the cyclic reference cases of
`fs-symmetry`. Its first test ran at M0 (bead N33b-10); this bead re-runs it at the M3 pin through production
use, with the recorded scope: cyclic groups only, no contact-event derivatives.

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-analyze/src/curvature/{connection,coords,curvature,holonomy}.rs`. The analysis is a memo
node whose field mask is: the kinematic tree, link geometry and inertias (flight case), wheel and contact
constraint declarations (rolling case), and the joint set chosen as shape variables.

1. **Stratum and shape variables.** The request names the contact stratum (which wheels roll without lateral
   slip; or `flight`) and two or more shape joints. The analysis refuses a request whose constraint rows do not
   give rank W_g = dim G, reporting the rank and the missing direction, or whose rows over-determine the group
   motion for generic shapes (it names the redundant rows).
2. **Connection.** Rolling case: assemble W_g (dim G × dim G) and W_s (dim G × number of shape variables) from
   the constraint one-forms at shape s, and solve 𝔸(s) = W_g⁻¹·W_s. Flight case: 𝔸(s) = 𝕀(s)⁻¹·𝕀_BS(s) from the
   locked inertia 𝕀 and the base-shape coupling block of the mass matrix. Each column 𝔸ᵢ is an element of the
   Lie algebra of G (se(2) for planar systems), typed by `frob-lie`.
3. **Minimum-perturbation coordinates.** The body frame is re-chosen as g′ = g·β(s), with β selected to
   minimize the squared norm of the transformed connection over the sampled shape domain. For planar systems the
   rotational component is an exact linear least-squares problem on the shape grid, because planar rotations
   commute; the translational components are then minimized in the rotated frame. The chosen β is part of the
   result, so that a displacement can be mapped back to the authored frame.
4. **Curvature.** ∂ᵢ𝔸ⱼ is computed with `fs-ad` forward duals through steps 2 and 3 (one dual pass per shape
   variable). (D𝔸)ᵢⱼ = ∂ᵢ𝔸ⱼ − ∂ⱼ𝔸ᵢ − [𝔸ᵢ, 𝔸ⱼ], with the bracket of the group's algebra. It is evaluated on a grid
   over the joint ranges (DESIGN default 33 × 33 per pair of shape variables).
5. **Displacement per cycle.** For a requested loop, the leading-order estimate is exp(−∬ D𝔸) over the enclosed
   region in the optimized coordinates. The analysis also integrates the ordered product of exp(−𝔸·Δs) along the
   loop (1,000 segments by default) and reports both and their difference. Large loops keep the ordered product
   and do not use the area formula; the estimate is labelled `leading-order` and carries the difference as its
   error indicator.
6. **Result.** Curvature component fields with their extrema; for each named loop the predicted displacement
   (x, y, yaw for planar systems) by both methods; the stratum; the coordinate change; evidence class
   `Estimated`, determinism D0 on one platform's deterministic functions; the note `kinematic only`. When the
   reference displacement the task class needs (a net translation per cycle) is unreachable at leading order on
   every admissible loop, the finding feeds preflight stage 4's refusal `FROB-REF-118` (the base cannot produce
   a motion the task class requires) with the missing direction and the joint edit that restores it.
7. **Registry wiring for the three analyses.** Each registers an identifier (A-09, A-14, A-16), a version, its
   field mask, its tier ([F]; [S]; [S] with a Frontier part), its preflight stage (4 for kinematic
   shape-changers; 7 and 8; none: a separate verb) and its verb (`analyze.curvature`, `analyze.coverage`,
   `analyze.sensitivity`, kind `read`). Results carry an evidence class, the model identity and, when a problem
   is found, ranked repairs as typed edits.
8. **INT-FSIM-10 at the M3 pin.** The implicit sensitivity of the 2×2 fixed point x = A(θ)·x + b with
   A = [[θ, 0.1], [0.2, 0.3]], b = (1, 2) at θ = 0.5 equals (700/121, 200/121) = (5.785124, 1.652893); the
   recorded scope is cyclic groups only and no contact-event derivatives. A new record is written.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot recorded its verdict before the Frontier analysis was built.
- [ ] On the `snake-crawler` archetype the curvature has the sign fixed by the convention: for a small rectangle loop the leading-order displacement agrees with the ordered product in sign and to first order in loop area, in all three planar components (the seed done-condition).
- [ ] A planted sign flip of the bracket term is caught: on the SO(3) constant-connection fixture the mutant predicts −da where +da is correct, and on the snake crawler it disagrees with the ordered product by an error of order one (the seed done-condition).
- [ ] Elroy's beanie: two coaxial bodies with inertias I₁ and I₂ at zero momentum; a relative rotation Δψ rotates body 1 by −I₂/(I₁ + I₂)·Δψ, to 10⁻¹² (for I₁ = 2, I₂ = 1 kg·m² and Δψ = 0.3 rad: −0.1 rad).
- [ ] The planar three-link floating body: net rotation per cycle against direct integration, with the loop doubled (displacement doubles to leading order), reversed (sign flips) and enlarged until the area formula's error indicator exceeds its bound (the estimate is then withdrawn in favour of the ordered product).
- [ ] Chart control: the same physical gait reconstructed in another body frame predicts the same motion after mapping back.
- [ ] A mecanum base with misaligned rollers is not judged by this analysis: stage 4 routes it to the growth vector, and a direct `analyze.curvature` call on it returns the result with the note that curvature does not test roller alignment.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-10 passes at the M3 pin through production use and is recorded (the seed done-condition); A-09, A-14 and A-16 are registered with masks, verbs and memo keys; GATE-ATEL-02's rows for A-09 and A-14 have known-answer records.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-analyze curvature`:
- `so3_constant_connection_sign`: 𝔸₁ = e_x, 𝔸₂ = e_y; (D𝔸)₁₂ = −e_z; a loop of area 10⁻⁴ gives a rotation of +10⁻⁴ rad about z to 10⁻⁷; the ordered product of four exponentials agrees.
- `bracket_sign_mutant_caught`: with +[𝔸ᵢ, 𝔸ⱼ] the same loop gives −10⁻⁴ and the test fails.
- `elroys_beanie_closed_form` (−0.1 rad for the stated values); `beanie_any_loop_shape`: the result depends only on Δψ.
- `three_link_doubling_and_reversal`; `three_link_area_formula_breaks_gracefully`.
- `snake_crawler_sign_and_first_order`: loops of side 0.02, 0.04, 0.08 rad; the difference between the two methods shrinks faster than the area.
- `min_perturbation_reduces_norm`: the optimized connection's norm is not larger than the authored frame's; `chart_control_same_motion`.
- `rank_deficient_constraints_refused`: two parallel wheel constraints; expects the rank and missing direction.
- `duals_match_gradcheck`: ∂ᵢ𝔸ⱼ from forward duals against central differences to 10⁻⁷ relative.
- `fsim10_fixed_point_sensitivity` ((5.785124, 1.652893) to 10⁻¹²); `fsim10_scope_recorded`.
- `analysis_registry_has_a09_a14_a16`: identifiers, masks, stages and verbs present; the live-dependency test of the memo graph passes for A-09.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/analyze_curvature.sh`: `frob design new --template snake-crawler`, then
`frob analyze curvature <design> --shape joint1,joint2 --loop rect:0.05,0.05 --json`; assert the displacement by
both methods and their agreement; run the planted-mutant build and assert disagreement; run
`frob design check <design> --json` and assert stage 4 lists A-09 with its timing against the 200 ms stage
budget. Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block, metrics `holonomy_yaw_area_formula`,
`holonomy_yaw_ordered`, `rel_diff` with its threshold, `stage4_ms` (budget 200), `mutant_caught`, the digest of
the curvature field, and on failure the loop and both displacement vectors. Summary names INT-FSIM-10.

### Known answers and negative controls
The SO(3) sign fixture (+da about z); Elroy's beanie; the three-link body against direct integration; the
known answer shared with A-07 (a mecanum base with misaligned rollers has full-rank curvature and a rank drop in
the growth vector; negative control: a curvature-only check passes the defective base). Negative controls: the
bracket-sign mutant; a connection used across a stratum change (a wheel declared slipping mid-loop must make the
analysis refuse the loop). Metamorphic: reversing a loop negates the leading-order displacement; scaling all
lengths by k scales translational displacement by k and leaves rotation unchanged. Determinism: identical fields
twice on one machine and in debug and release.

## Considerations

- KILL-AUGUR-PHASE governs the inverse use (M6): if no proposed gait is admitted by a full rollout, or trajectory optimization finds every proposed gait at lower total cost, A-09 stays a forward diagnostic and the atlas is parked. Nothing in this bead depends on that branch.
- Retirement rule: where the drive slips outside ideal rolling, the kinematic prediction is withdrawn for that region and the frictional model answers; the result's `kinematic only` note is how a caller learns this.
- `fs-symmetry` covers cyclic groups only (FACT, about 480 lines); the head-to-tail reversal symmetry of the snake crawler is a two-element cyclic group and is within that scope, but the project's own group code is what the analyses use.
- Budget: stage 4 has 200 ms; a 33 × 33 grid with one dual pass per shape variable fits for two shape variables. More shape variables are analysed pairwise on request, outside the preflight.
- Splitting the seed item into three beads keeps its key, milestone and dependencies on this bead; the other two depend on nothing later than M3.
