## Background

The gait atlas (analysis A-04, bead BD-332) lists the gaits a body can have from its symmetry group: trot, pace,
bound, the tripod gait of a hexapod. It does not say whether a listed gait exists for this body under this
controller, or whether it is stable. `frob-gait` (layer 4, a Frontier crate) answers that: it computes whether a
listed gait exists as a **periodic orbit of the closed loop**, whether it is stable, and (in later beads) how it
moves when the design changes.

What the result is worth, stated before any test (FACT, source register): published full-order orbit solves take
about 4 to 260 s of CPU at 20 to 68 states with initial guesses supplied, about 1% to 63% of a 410 s incumbent
quadruped training run. An orbit is not an accepted controller: published hardware gaits needed added heuristics
to stabilize. Floquet multipliers describe the chosen feedback law on the chosen tier, and local stability only;
a locally stable orbit on flat ground supplies no estimate of recovery on a staircase. Every result of this bead
is therefore labelled **`screening: gait-orbit`** (invariant INV-GAIT-01): it may discard a design under the
evaluation ladder's rules and may never establish a comparison. Bet BET-26 (gait orbits as screening) is decided
by GATE-GAIT-03 and kill gate KILL-GAIT; if that fires, orbits serve stability diagnostics and warm starts only.

The one rule this bead exists to enforce: **stability is computed over the full reduced state, never over the
symmetric subspace alone.** Counterexample (CX-237): ẋ = −x, ẏ = +y with the symmetry y ↦ −y. On the fixed
subspace {y = 0} every orbit decays with multiplier e^(−T); the full-space multiplier in the antisymmetric block
is e^(+T) > 1. A restricted computation reports a stable gait for a robot that falls over sideways.

Foundations (FACT): FrankenSim has shooting, pseudo-arclength continuation and monodromy (`fs-orbit`), with a
shooting module that disclaims hybrid saltations, a separate saltation correction, and no hybrid
multiple-shooting solver on articulated models and no derivatives of the articulated dynamics. The
compliant-tier route is therefore this project's and comes first; the referee route waits on upstream work item
FD-FSIM-20. The spectrum of the monodromy goes to FrankenSim's Floquet analysis through the airlock (group
`spectral`, gate INT-FSIM-13, bead N33b-11), which takes a supplied monodromy and also reports non-normal
transient growth.

## Scope

simultaneous impacts; the `GaitOrbit` record; the verb `gait.orbit`; the fixtures of GATE-GAIT-01; the re-run of
INT-FSIM-13 at the M4 pin through production use.

form (N22c-36); stability over admitted stride sequences (N22c-41); the referee route (waits on FD-FSIM-20).

## Technical approach

Crate `frob-gait`, modules `map.rs`, `shoot.rs`, `monodromy.rs`, `saltation.rs`, `section.rs`, `simultaneous.rs`,
`record.rs`. All of it is behind the crate's Frontier status; no default build of a mixed crate enables it.

**1. The map.** The state is x = (q, v, a, c): configuration, velocity, actuator and thermal states, and
controller state (the gait clock, and the filter state when the loop runs on estimated state). One control period
on a batched tier is a map F_θ: x ↦ x⁺, with θ the design parameters. The baseline's gait clock forces the loop
periodically, so a stride of N control periods is the stroboscopic map P_θ = F_θ^N; the stride period is a
rational number in the clock contract. On flat ground under a body-frame velocity command the closed loop is
equivariant under horizontal translation, and under yaw when heading is not tracked. Those coordinates are
removed and **listed in the result**; x̄ is the state without them. A gait is a fixed point P̄_θ(x̄*) = x̄*, a
relative periodic orbit of the full state whose drift is the stride displacement.

**2. Shooting with symmetry.** For a gait with spatio-temporal group H and spatial group K (from the atlas), H/K
is cyclic of order m with generator σ, and the orbit satisfies the twisted condition
F̄^(N/m)(x̄*) = ρ(σ)·x̄*, with ρ the signed permutation by which σ acts on the state. Solve
r(x̄) = ρ(σ)⁻¹·F̄^(N/m)(x̄) − x̄ = 0 for x̄ in Fix(K):
1. seed x̄₀ from the atlas's phase lags and a settled stance;
2. Newton iteration with the Jacobian of r restricted to Fix(K), built by tangent worlds (step 3 of this list);
3. a backtracking line search on ‖r‖; on failure halve the step up to 8 times;
4. stop when ‖r‖ is below tolerance (DESIGN default 10⁻⁸ in the scaled state norm) with outcome `converged`, or
   with `diverged` after step halving fails, or `budget` when the budget ends. Nothing partial is published as
   an orbit.
The problem is m times shorter and of lower dimension than the full stride.

**3. The monodromy, in every direction** (proof obligation PO-GAIT-01). M = D P̄_θ(x̄*) =
(ρ(σ)⁻¹·D F̄^(N/m)(x̄*))^m with the Jacobian taken over the whole reduced state, not over Fix(K). Each column is a
**tangent world** rolled for N/m periods beside the nominal one: n tangent worlds for a reduced state of
dimension n (DESIGN estimate: n is 40 to about 130 for the robots in scope). The first implementation uses
central differences in `f64` on the `strict` profile of `batch-cpu` at two step sizes with a Richardson check: a
column whose two estimates differ beyond tolerance is flagged and its label becomes `finite-difference` with the
discrepancy recorded. PO-GAIT-01 is tested by also computing the direct Jacobian of the full stride F̄^N and
requiring a relative difference below 10⁻⁸.

**4. Blocks and spectrum.** M is block-diagonalized by the isotypic projectors of the group, so each multiplier is
attributed to a symmetry type. A multiplier outside the unit circle in a non-trivial block is a
symmetry-breaking instability named by its block. Each block is passed to the airlock's `Spectral` trait, which
returns multipliers, the spectral radius, transient growth and a verdict. If the computation was restricted to
Fix(K) for any reason (a budget stop before the transverse columns), the result is marked `incomplete` and
carries no stability verdict.

**5. Saltation on event-located flows.** Through a transverse event with guard g(x) = 0, reset map R and vector
fields f⁻ before and f⁺ after, a perturbation is carried by the saltation matrix

```text
Ξ = D R + (f⁺ − D R·f⁻)·D g / (D g·f⁻)
```

Each event records its chart, its guard margin, its identity and the denominator D g·f⁻. Near grazing, where
abs(D g·f⁻) falls below its threshold, an ordinary derivative is refused (`FROB-REF-431`: a gait derivative was
requested at grazing or at a fold; repairs: use the one-sided estimates returned, move the parameter, or use
derivative-free search), and the offending state, the perturbation directions and the one-sided estimates are
returned.

**6. The return map on an event section.** Where the stride has a repeatable transverse event with guard g, the
orbit is a fixed point of the return map P_Σ on Σ = {g = 0}, of dimension one less than the state, with
D P_Σ = (I − f⁺·∇gᵀ/(∇gᵀ·f⁺))·M restricted to the tangent space of Σ. Its multipliers are those of M without the
trivial one. This is the form in which the rimless wheel and the compass gait are solved.

**7. Derivative labels.** Every derivative carries one label of the project's fixed set: `smooth` (no event in
the stride), `one-sided` (an estimate on one side of a guard), `generalized` (a set-valued result carrying
several selection Jacobians), `smoothed` (the derivative of a declared smoothed surrogate), `discrete-step`
(compliant-tier Jacobians with an unchanged active set), `saltation` (event-located), `finite-difference`, or
`refused`.

**8. Simultaneous impacts are handled, not refused.** Trot, bound and pronk put two or four feet down at one
instant by symmetry.
- On the compliant tiers contact force is a continuous function of state, so the stride map is continuous and
  piecewise smooth. One extra check runs: the touchdown times of each simultaneous group are split by ±δ and the
  one-sided Jacobians are compared; if they differ beyond tolerance the result carries both as selection
  Jacobians with the label `generalized`.
- On an event-located flow an exactly simultaneous group is one coupled impact solve per island. The analysis
  computes the outcome and the saltation product for every ordering of a group of up to four feet (24 orderings)
  and for the coupled solve, and reports the **order sensitivity**: the largest difference between outgoing
  velocities in the mass norm, and the off-diagonal contact-space term J₁·M⁻¹·J₂ᵀ for each pair.
- The verdict is `stable` only if all selections agree within tolerance, or if one quadratic norm contracts under
  every selection (a common Lyapunov matrix from a small semidefinite program). Otherwise it is
  `order-sensitive`, reported with the range of spectral radii and a finite-perturbation recovery test over
  sampled disturbances, class `Tested`.

**9. The record and the verb.** `GaitOrbit` holds the `ControllerId`, the tier, (H, K, σ), the stride length N,
x̄*, the residual, the removed coordinates, the multipliers with their block labels, transient growth, order
sensitivity, the derivative labels, the metrics (cost of transport, peak torque margin, friction utilization)
and the standing `screening`. Verb `gait.orbit` (kind `run`, first milestone M4): input a design, a controller,
a gait from the atlas (or (H, K, σ) and N), the tier, a budget and tolerances; output the record or a typed
outcome (`diverged`, `budget`); long solves return a job handle (exit 8). Evidence: class Estimated with standing
`screening: gait-orbit`; determinism D2 on one backend; the claim list of the envelope is empty.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot recorded its verdict before this bead started (funding rule for Frontier work).
- [ ] GATE-GAIT-01, rimless wheel: with c = cos 2α and b = (4g/l)·sin α·sin γ, the fixed point ω*² = c²·b/(1 − c²) and the multiplier c² are recovered within 10⁻⁶ (for eight spokes, l = 1 m, γ = 0.1 rad: ω*² = 1.49915 rad²/s², multiplier ½); an uphill slope returns no fixed point.
- [ ] GATE-GAIT-01, compass gait: multipliers within 10⁻⁶ of an independent high-precision computation.
- [ ] GATE-GAIT-01, the planted transverse instability is found: on the fixture with a decaying symmetric block and a growing antisymmetric block the result is unstable and names the block; a run restricted to Fix(K) is marked `incomplete` and gives no verdict.
- [ ] PO-GAIT-01: the monodromy assembled from the twisted factor equals the direct Jacobian of the full stride to 10⁻⁸ relative in `f64`.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-13 is re-run at the M4 pin through this bead's use of the `Spectral` trait and recorded (the seed done-condition, with GATE-GAIT-01).
- [ ] Every result carries `ControllerId`, tier, residual, the full-space spectrum, derivative labels, removed coordinates and `screening` (INV-GAIT-01); a result without them fails schema validation.
- [ ] A simultaneous group is never a refusal; the bar fixture returns rest for the coupled solve and v/4 for one-support-first, with the coupling term's sign.
- [ ] `gait.orbit` is registered with schema, example and refusal list (`FROB-REF-431`, `FROB-REF-790`), answers through `frob --json`, honours cancellation at stride boundaries, and has a CPU reference path; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-gait` exists.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-gait`:
- `saltation_bouncing_ball`: state (y, v), flow (v, −g), guard y = 0, reset (y, −r·v), impact speed v⁻ < 0: Ξ = [[−r, 0], [−(1 + r)·g/v⁻, −r]], determinant r². The planted mutant that uses the reset Jacobian diag(1, −r) as the event derivative has determinant −r and is caught.
- `rimless_wheel_return_map`: the map on the post-impact rate is ω₊² ↦ cos²(2α)·(ω₊² + (4g/l)·sin α·sin γ); for eight spokes the multiplier is exactly ½; fixed point 1.49915 rad²/s² at l = 1 m, γ = 0.1 rad, g = 9.81 m/s².
- `rimless_wheel_uphill_no_fixed_point` (b < 0).
- `section_projection_removes_trivial_multiplier`: on the planar loop ṙ = r·(μ − r), φ̇ = 1 the time-2π monodromy has multipliers 1 and e^(−2πμ); the section map has the single multiplier e^(−2πμ).
- `full_space_instability_found`: ẋ = −x, ẏ = +y, T = 1: multipliers e^(−1) = 0.367879 in the symmetric block and e^(+1) = 2.718282 in the antisymmetric block; verdict unstable, block named.
- `restricted_run_marked_incomplete`.
- `twisted_factor_equals_full_stride` (PO-GAIT-01, bound 10⁻⁸) on a seeded linear map with a C₂ symmetry and on the quadruped fixture.
- `richardson_flags_bad_column`: a deliberately noisy column is flagged and labelled.
- `bar_fixture_orderings`: a uniform bar on supports at ±l/3 (β = 1/3): coupled solve gives rest; one support first leaves the first end lifting at v/4 with 1/16 of the kinetic energy; coupling term (1 − β)/m is positive. For β ≥ 1 every ordering gives rest.
- `grazing_refused_431_with_one_sided_estimates`.
- `record_schema_requires_screening_and_spectrum`.
- `shooting_outcomes_typed`: forced divergence returns `diverged` and publishes no orbit.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/gait_orbit.sh`: (1) `frob gait orbit --fixture rimless-wheel --spokes 8 --slope 0.1 --json` and assert
the fixed point and multiplier; (2) the compass gait against the pinned high-precision oracle capture; (3) the
planted-instability fixture, asserting `unstable` and the block; (4) the reference quadruped's trot from
`frob gait atlas`, asserting a record with residual below tolerance or a typed outcome; (5) the same in a binary
built without the `spectral` group, asserting exit 5 and `FROB-REF-790`. Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code,
wall time, evidence block with `screening`, metrics `fixed_point_err` and `multiplier_err` (threshold 10⁻⁶),
`po_gait_01_rel_diff` (10⁻⁸), `residual`, `tangent_worlds`, `solve_cpu_s` (recorded beside the published 4 to
260 s), the multiplier list digest, and on failure the first differing multiplier and the run's artifact paths.
Summary names GATE-GAIT-01 and INT-FSIM-13.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-560 and KA-093 (rimless wheel: ω*² = c²·b/(1 − c²), multiplier c², exactly ½ for eight spokes; an uphill slope
has no fixed point). KA-561 (compass-gait multipliers against an independent high-precision computation). KA-092
(the bouncing-ball saltation matrix). CX-237 (stability on the symmetric subspace only). Negative controls: the
restricted computation; the reset-Jacobian mutant; a monodromy built from Fix(K) columns only; an ordering-blind
impact analysis on the bar. Metamorphic: relabelling legs by a group element permutes blocks and leaves the
multiplier set unchanged; doubling N (two strides) squares every multiplier; the removed translation changes no
multiplier. Determinism: the same seed, design and controller give identical records twice on one machine and
across worker counts on the `strict` profile.

## Considerations

- KILL-GAIT: trigger, GATE-GAIT-03 fails (continuation succeeds on fewer than 50% of 10% design perturbations, or the median solve exceeds 10 minutes of CPU, or Kendall τ between orbit cost of transport and specialists is below 0.5). Fallback, named in advance: orbits serve stability diagnostics and warm starts only; the atlas keeps supplying clocks and reward references; no design is ranked by an orbit. This bead's record format and solver stay useful on that branch.
- OPEN-GAIT-1: when the referee route for orbits is built (owner: Kinesis lead with upstream; blocks nothing here). OPEN-GAIT-2: the norm and tolerance for order sensitivity (owner: Reflex lead; blocks GATE-GAIT-02, so the bar fixture's tolerance is provisional here).
- Cost: n tangent worlds per Jacobian, n between 40 and about 130, each one stride long, on `batch-cpu`. The measured solve cost is recorded beside the published range; a run starts under a timeout and inside the machine envelope.
- The gate register dates INT-FSIM-13 at M3 and this consumer at M4; the M3 record is bead N33b-11 and this bead re-runs the gate at its own pin.
- Autonomous loops (a passive walker, an event-reset controller) have a multiplier exactly 1 for every design; judging them by the stroboscopic equations is refused elsewhere, and the two autonomous fixtures of this gate go through the section form with the trivial multiplier removed and listed.
- A second derivative route through the kernel derivative programs is differentially tested against the central differences of this bead when it arrives; until then every Jacobian is `finite-difference` or `discrete-step`.
