## Background

Foundry is the subsystem that makes a design buildable: it extracts load cases from telemetry, sizes links,
checks stress and fatigue, feeds mass and inertia back into the model, and writes a build packet. It makes the
answer better, not sooner: no part of the ten-fold thesis rests on it. The ceiling is stated before any test:
with link structure a fraction f_L of robot mass and a saving s, the direct ceiling is f_L·s ≤ 0.27 × 0.30 ≈ 0.08
of robot mass (reported savings against engineered parts are roughly 7 to 30% of link mass, and in the one
quadruped with a published mass breakdown the legs are about 27% of the robot).

The structure loop has two rungs by decision (ADR-FOUNDRY-02): **closed-form beam sizing by default and as
fallback; three-dimensional topology optimization for finalists.** The beam rung costs milliseconds, is the
engineered baseline every optimized part is judged against, is the fallback if topology optimization is killed
(KILL-TOPOPT), and answers analysis **A-15, structural margin**: *would a link yield under the peak loads seen or
bounded?* A-15's mathematics is beam theory, and finite elements through FrankenSim's `fs-solid` for the full
form. This bead builds the beam rung, the A-15 margin and the verb `foundry.size`.

Decisions that bind it. A load case is one instant of one episode; peaks from different instants are never
assembled (ADR-FOUNDRY-03). Counterexample: a simply supported beam of span L carries P at L/3 at one instant and
P at 2L/3 at another; the mid-span moment is P·L/6 each time; assembling both peaks gives P·L/3, a twofold
over-design, and with opposite signs the assembled case gives zero while each real case gives ±P·L/6, which is
unsafe. Foundry results are at most class Estimated (INV-FOUNDRY-06). Fatigue is reported as utilization under a
named empirical law; the word "life" is not used for a result (ADR-FOUNDRY-06). The optimizer never checks its
own result (ADR-FOUNDRY-05): here that means the finite-element form of A-15 runs on a body-fitted mesh that no
optimizer produced.

Gate (TARGET): GATE-FOUNDRY-01, claim Known answer: cantilever deflection within 2% and stress within 5% after
refinement; the planted overstress located with utilization 1.25 ± 5%. The gate register allows it to be
recorded at M4 and requires it by M5. The seed done-condition adds the group gate INT-FSIM-15 (bead N33b-14),
re-run here at this bead's pin through production use.

## Scope
The beam rung is written by four beads. This seed is the one that carries the verb, the structural margin and
gate GATE-FOUNDRY-01; it does not repeat what the other three specify.

- **N31b-38** (M3, a dependency of this bead) holds the closed-form kernel in
  `beam/{section,criteria,stock,size}.rs`: the idealization of a link as a tube or a rectangle loaded by one
  wrench at its distal port, the section properties, the four static criteria (yield, column buckling, local
  wall buckling, first bending frequency), the continuous mass floor, the lightest passing stock section with
  its tie rule, inertia about the proximal joint, the exact decisions and the record `MemberSizing`. It was
  delivered early for the screening check of GATE-FOUNDRY-15. This bead calls it and extends the same modules;
  it writes no second kernel and restates none of its formulas.
- **N31a-60** (M5, depends on this bead) holds the load model of a full envelope case: the section forces
  along a member from every port wrench and the body acceleration field of one sample, the station set, the
  checks of a rectangle in torsion and biaxial bending, and the option `--diagram` of this bead's verb. It
  replaces the reduction of step 2 below.
- **N31a-61** (M5, depends on this bead and on N31a-60) holds the fatigue criterion in beam form (gauges at
  declared notches, counting, utilization per trial section), stock sizing under campaign loads and fatigue,
  the baseline record `frob.foundry.beam/1`, the saving and the fallback mode of `foundry.optimize`. This bead
  holds only the place of the fatigue criterion in its result.
- **N33b-14** records the group gate INT-FSIM-15; this bead re-runs it at its pin.

In: the verb `foundry.size` with its inputs, outputs, labels and refusals; the load-set reader (envelope cases
of a campaign, or `statics` with a load factor) and its refusal of a case assembled from two instants; the
reduction of a case to the kernel's load case; the call of the kernel's stock search over every case and the
governing criterion and case; the fatigue field of the result and its `no-claim` outcome; the A-15 margin in
beam form and in finite-element form with refinement; the known answers of GATE-FOUNDRY-01 (the cantilever and
the planted overstress) through the verb; the re-run of INT-FSIM-15; the admission predicate `foundry-size-fe`;
the default load set of the verb; the registration of A-15 as an analysis of `frob-analyze`, from which the row
`analyze.structure` is generated (step 7).

Out: the kernel's formulas and its unit tests (N31b-38); section forces along a member and `--diagram`
(N31a-60); fatigue in beam form, the baseline and the saving (N31a-61); load campaigns, envelope cases and
spectra (BD-400, `foundry.loads`); the independent check of optimized geometry, modes and cumulative fatigue
damage (BD-402); topology optimization (BD-403); inertia feedback and the convergence test (BD-404); the
screening-time validity check and its promotion rule (the Foundry unit's beads N31b-39 to N31b-41, which call
the kernel); manufacturability, tolerances, export and the packet (BD-405 to BD-408).

## Technical approach

Crate `frob-foundry` (layer 5), modules `beam/{section,criteria,stock,size}.rs` and `margin.rs`.

**1. What the kernel supplies (N31b-38; used here, not specified again).** A link idealized between its ports
as a thin-walled tube or a rectangular section of length L; the section properties (A, I, Z and, for the tube,
J); four static criteria, each a utilization u that passes when u ≤ 1: yield, u_y, on the von Mises
combination of the axial-plus-bending stress and the tube's torsional shear against S_y/SF, SF the declared
safety factor; column buckling, u_b, against the Euler load with the end-condition factor K of the port
pattern (1 for pinned ends, 2 for a cantilever); local wall buckling of a tube, u_l, against the classical
thin-cylinder stress times the card's knock-down factor; first bending frequency with end masses, u_f, against
a supplied floor. Then the lightest stock entry whose every utilization is at most 1 in every case (ties:
smaller outer dimension, then catalogue order, so the choice is deterministic), the outcome `no-stock-passes`
with the nearest entry and its governing utilization, the continuous wall t_required and mass floor
m_floor = ρ·A(t_required)·L, the stock mass m_stock, inertia about the proximal joint for both, and exact
decisions: rationals compared in exact arithmetic (class D0) wherever a criterion is rational in its inputs,
outward-rounded intervals where a square root or π enters, a decision inside the rounding interval reported
as `at-limit` and counted as not passing. The plan's m_min (§31.23: the mass the check compares with the
declared one) is the kernel's m_floor in the known answer (1.85 mm, 0.141 kg) and its m_stock for the sized
section; this bead reports both under the kernel's names.

**2. Load sets, cases and the reduction to the kernel's load case.** A load set is the identifier of a load
campaign's envelope cases (BD-400), or `statics` with a load factor. An envelope case is one real instant of
one episode, with all port wrenches and the body acceleration field from the same sample; the reader checks
that every quantity of a case carries one sample identifier and refuses a case assembled from two instants with
`FROB-REF-167` (exit 3; the offending samples are returned). For a link class with several instances (mirror or
symmetric limbs) the reader also requires the load set's `instances_covered` to equal the class membership
computed from the design (the equal-limb rule of N31a-11, step 6): a set built from a strict subset of the
instances is refused with `FROB-REF-169` (telemetry without the samples a load metric needs; exit 3; the payload
names the missing limbs, and the repair is to read those limbs' traces). A `statics` set is generated for every
instance and passes by construction.
Material comes from the link's card (E, S_y, density ρ, Poisson ratio ν, a wall-buckling knock-down factor,
each with provenance), and the stock catalogue of the link's section family from its stock card (radii and
walls, or widths and depths, with provenance). In this bead a case reaches the kernel in the kernel's form:
the wrench at the distal port, evaluated at the root as axial force N, bending moment M (the vector sum of the
two transverse components), torque T and shear V (DESIGN: the reduction that needs nothing beyond the kernel;
it is exact for the gate's cantilever fixtures and for the bounded statics set). How N, V, M and T follow
along the whole member from every port wrench and the body acceleration field of the sample is N31a-60's,
which replaces this reduction for campaign load sets.

**3. What this bead supplies to the criteria.**
- *The frequency floor.* The kernel takes f_floor as an input. `foundry.size` supplies the actuator bandwidth
  requirement in the form N31a-60 and the modal check use, f_floor = max(2 × actuator bandwidth, 5 × f₉₉), f₉₉
  being the highest frequency that carries 99% of the load power of the campaign; with a `statics` load set
  there are no spectra and the floor is 2 × actuator bandwidth (DESIGN: one floor for the beam rung and the
  independent check, so that a section cannot pass one and fail the other on the definition alone). The
  criterion is the kernel's, u_f = 1.015·f_floor/f₁, the factor 1.015 covering the one-mode value's upward
  bias (DESIGN, stated with its reason in N31b-38 and N31a-60).
- *Fatigue at declared notches.* It is the fifth criterion of the plan's sizing rule. Its beam form (gauge
  rows, counting per trial section, the choice among passing sections) is N31a-61's. This bead defines its
  place in the result: a field `fatigue` that holds the utilization for a notch under the campaign's spectra
  when a valid stress-life curve exists, and `fatigue: no-claim` with its reason otherwise (no valid curve; a
  declared load set without spectra; the beam form not yet present). In the finite-element form the
  utilization at a declared notch is the one the fatigue observer of the independent check supplies (BD-402,
  which depends on this bead); until BD-402 fills it, the finite-element form reports `fatigue: no-claim`
  under the third reason.
- *The governing criterion and case* are those with the largest u over all criteria and cases.

**4. Sizing through the verb.** Per link: read the cases (step 2), call the kernel's stock search with all of
them, and return its record with the governing case's world address and instant added.

**5. A-15, the structural margin.** Beam form: margin = 1/u_max − 1 per link, with the governing case, location
and criterion. Full form (finite elements, through the airlock's `StructuralOpt` solve on a body-fitted
tetrahedral mesh): per envelope case, utilization u = σ_vm/(S_y/SF) per element; the mesh is refined until the
peak moves by under 5% between refinements; a peak at a re-entrant corner that does not converge is reported as
`singular: fillet required` with the radius as the repair. The margin inherits the weaker evidence class of its
inputs and the label `model-conditional` when the governing load case carries it (a peak contact force that
moves by more than 20% under halved and doubled contact stiffness). Class: Estimated.

**6. The verb.** `foundry.size` (kind `read`; registry first milestone M4, delivered with Foundry at M5). Input:
a design, a link selector, a load set (the identifier of a load campaign's envelope cases, or `statics` with a
load factor), the section family, safety factors, optional `--fe` for the full form. Output per link: the
kernel's record (the chosen stock section and m_stock, t_required and m_floor, inertia about the proximal joint
for both), utilizations by criterion, the `fatigue` field, the governing case with its world address and
instant, the A-15 margin, labels. The option `--diagram` is added by N31a-60. Refusals (the registry row in
VERBS-foundry): `FROB-REF-163`, a check whose material card lacks the field or provenance it needs (repair: the
missing field; `fatigue: no-claim` for the fatigue criterion alone); `FROB-REF-167`, a load set containing a case
that is not one instant (step 2); `FROB-REF-169`, a class load set that does not cover every instance of the
class, naming the missing limbs (step 2). `foundry.size` needs no qualification verdict: it runs on any design,
at preflight inside `design.check` (§31.23, N31b-39) as well as on finalists, and FROB-REF-166 is not on its
list (ADR-FOUNDRY-01 bars only optimization and packet export; N31a-03). Its result carries the `exploratory`
label only when an input (a load set from an exploratory structure run) carries it.
The full form needs the airlock's `structure` group; without it the beam form still answers and `--fe` returns
`FROB-REF-790`. The registry row states that as `needs_groups = [{ groups = ["structure"], when =
"foundry-size-fe" }]`; this bead registers the predicate `foundry-size-fe` in
`frob_verbs::admission::predicates` (true exactly when the resolved request has `fe` set), which the lint
`needs-groups-known` of N33c-32 requires, so that N33c-32's admission check raises 790 before the handler runs
and a request without `--fe` is never refused for the group. Envelope evidence: claim list empty (a structural
margin enters a claim product as its weakest input); class Estimated; determinism D0 for the beam form.

*The load set's spelling and its default (DESIGN).* `--loads <campaign id | file>` names envelope cases;
`--loads statics` is the bounded statics set, and `--loads statics:<factor>` the same set with one load factor
for every port. With no `--loads` (the registry example `frob foundry size quad-a --json` gives none) the load
set is `statics` with the class load factors of the design's archetype card: the bounded set of the stage-6
member-sizing check (N31b-39: each foot, wheel or end-effector port carries the robot's weight times its class
factor, 3 for legged feet, 2 for wheels, 1.5 for manipulator ports with the rated payload added, in the worst
direction the port admits, each factor overridable per design with provenance). The result is labelled
`load set: bounded-statics` with the factors used. Reason: the example then runs, and the verb with no load
argument returns for a link what `design.check` reported for it at stage 6; a schema refusal would make the
simplest call the one that fails. A fixture brings its own declared case, so `--fixture` needs no `--loads`.

**7. A-15 as a registered analysis (the row `analyze.structure`).** The margin of step 5 in beam form is
registered in `frob-analyze` as the `Analysis` A-15 under the analysis contract of N28a-04
(`crates/frob-analyze/src/analyses/a15_structure.rs`), and N28a-05's generator emits the row
`analyze.structure` from it (kind `read`, tier F, registry first milestone M4, capability `analyze:read`,
schemas `frob.analyze.structure.in/1` and `.out/1`, owner `frob-analyze`, example `frob analyze structure quad-a
--json`). `frob-analyze` is layer 4 and `frob-foundry` layer 5, so the view reaches the margin through the
inversion N31b-39 put in place and adds no second implementation: `frob-analyze` owns the trait `MemberSizer`,
`frob-foundry` implements it over its kernel, and `frob-verbs` passes the implementation in. This bead adds
one method to that trait, `margin(&self, req: &MemberSizingRequest) -> StructuralMarginAnswer` (per link: u_max,
margin = 1/u_max − 1, the governing case, location and criterion, the load-set label), implemented over
`margin.rs`; no new inversion row is needed. The registration (DESIGN where the row leaves it open):
- `ID = "A-15"`, `VERSION = 1`, `tier()` Frontier, `screening()` false.
- Inputs: the design and `--loads` with the spellings and the default of step 6 ("load cases or a load
  campaign" in the row).
- `reads()`: link geometry between ports, fills, the material and stock cards the links name with the fields
  the criteria use, the port pattern of each link, the masses distal to each link and the archetype card's
  class load factors. A campaign load set enters the key by its identifier as a parameter.
- `run`: `output` = per link the margin, the governing case and location, the criterion and the load-set
  label, in beam form only (the finite-element form stays `foundry.size --fe`: it is a job, and an analysis
  verb is a static read). Evidence: *analysis*, class Estimated, determinism D0. With no sizer in the build the
  output reads `not-run` with reason `no-sizer`, never a pass.
- Findings: `A-15.<link>.under-built` (High), the kind and policy row N31b-39 already registered, when a link's
  declared section has u_max > 1 under the load set (equivalently its declared mass is below the floor), with
  the observed u_max, the required 1, and N31b-39's ranked repairs as typed edits. Exit 2 with findings; an
  analysis verb never refuses on one.
- Declared refusals: `FROB-REF-163` (the row's code), and `FROB-REF-167` and `FROB-REF-169`, which the load-set
  reader of step 2 raises for a campaign set whatever verb calls it; the generator appends `FROB-REF-551`. The
  row on file lists 163 only, so the two reader codes are requested of VERBS-analyze, as VERBS-foundry added
  them to `foundry.size`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry beam margin`. The kernel's own tests (section properties, with this seed's second
row r = 14 mm, t = 2 mm; Euler buckling 8,558 N; the von Mises combination 132.29 MPa; `at-limit`; the tie
break; `no-stock-passes`) are N31b-38's and run in the same target; this bead adds:
- `cantilever_closed_forms`: deflection P·L³/(3·E·I) = 1.159 mm with I = b·h³/12 = 1.6667 × 10⁻⁹ m⁴; root stress 6·P·L/(b·h²) = 30 MPa; halving the load halves both.
- `planted_overstress_utilization_1_25`: 6 × 80 × 0.2/(0.02 × 0.004²) = 300 MPa against 240 MPa; location `root`.
- `tube_sizing_hand_case_through_verb`: the verb's record for the tube case equals the kernel's: required t = 180/(138 × 10⁶ × π × 0.015²) = 1.845 mm; chosen stock 2.0 mm; with the exact 1.85 mm wall the mass is 0.141 kg.
- `case_reduces_to_distal_wrench`: a case with a 600 N tip force on a 0.3 m member reaches the kernel as N = 0, V = 600 N, M = 180 N·m, T = 0.
- `frequency_floor_from_bandwidth_and_spectra`: bandwidth 20 Hz and f₉₉ = 12 Hz give a floor of 60 Hz; with a `statics` load set, 40 Hz.
- `fatigue_field_no_claim_reasons`: each of the three reasons of step 3 yields `fatigue: no-claim` with that reason, and the static criteria still answer.
- `assembled_case_refused_167`: the simply supported span with P at L/3 and at 2L/3 in two samples; each real case gives P·L/6 at mid-span, and a case built from both samples is refused with `FROB-REF-167` naming both sample identifiers; `governing_case_replays_to_sample`.
- `card_missing_yield_refused_163`; `fatigue_no_claim_without_curve`.
- `partial_class_load_set_refused_169`: a lower-leg class of four instances with cases from `FL.shank` only is refused, the payload naming `FR.shank`, `HL.shank`, `HR.shank`; the full set passes; a singleton class is unaffected.
- `unqualified_design_sized_without_166`: a design with no verdict is sized and its result carries no `exploratory` label.
- `default_load_set_is_bounded_statics`: with no load set the request resolves to `statics` with the archetype's class factors (3 on a legged foot of the quadruped fixture) and the result equals N31b-39's stage-6 answer for the same link; `statics:2` overrides every port's factor with 2.
- `fe_predicate_registered`: `foundry-size-fe` is in `frob_verbs::admission::predicates`, is true for a request with `fe` set and false without; on a scratch registry a row naming an unregistered predicate fails `needs-groups-known`.
- `a15_view_equals_foundry_margin` (in `frob-analyze`, with the Foundry sizer passed in): margins, governing cases and criteria equal `foundry.size`'s on the tube case and the quadruped fixture; with the null sizer the output is `not-run: no-sizer`; `a15_under_built_finding`: the thinned cantilever yields `A-15.<link>.under-built` with observed 1.25 and required 1; `a15_generates_row`: the generated `analyze.structure` row has kind `read`, tier F, the example on file and the declared refusals.
- `unit_error_caught_by_types`: passing millimetres where metres are expected does not compile (dimension typing).
- `fe_cantilever_within_2_and_5_percent` (feature `structure`); `fe_refinement_stops_under_5_percent`; `fe_reentrant_corner_reports_fillet`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `sum-of-peaks` (assembles cases), caught by this bead's tests; and, as mutations of the kernel caught through the verb's known answers, `solid-rod-modulus` (uses π·r³/4 for the tube; the kernel's name for it, which this seed first called `section-modulus-of-solid-rod`) and `ignore-torsion` (drops τ from the yield check on a torsion-dominated case).

### Known answers and negative controls
The cantilever (1.159 mm; 30 MPa; shear adds about 0.2%; negative control: halving the load halves both, and a
millimetre-for-metre unit error is caught by dimension typing). The planted overstressed link (300 MPa,
utilization 1.25, at the root; control: the unthinned beam passes). The assembled-peaks case (P·L/6 per instant;
assembled P·L/3 or 0; control: the assembling mutation is refused). The tube sizing case (1.85 mm, 0.141 kg,
4.2 × 10⁻³ kg·m²; a design declaring that segment at 0.05 kg is under-built by a factor of 2.8). Metamorphic:
scaling every load by k scales every stress utilization by k and leaves the frequency criterion unchanged;
scaling all lengths by s at fixed load scales bending stress by 1/s²; rotating the load about the tube axis
changes nothing for a tube. Determinism: the beam form is identical everywhere; the finite-element form is
identical twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- KILL-TOPOPT (decided at M5): if the stage-2 pilot saves under 7%, does not converge or exceeds its budget, beam-rung sizing supplies mass and inertia feedback and three-dimensional optimization becomes an offline option or is dropped; A-15 is unaffected. KILL-LOADS (decided at M5, may be recorded at M4): if the load-extraction gate fails after one repair cycle, load cases are declared by hand from statics with a stated dynamic factor and spectra read `no-claim`; this bead's `statics` load set is that path.
- The beam rung is a floor on mass, not a design: no joints, no bolted interfaces, no local effects within one boss diameter of a port (`no-claim` there), and impact enters only through the load set.
- Actuator cards are held fixed inside the structure loop; re-selecting an actuator after a mass change is an outer step with full revalidation (ADR-FOUNDRY-07).
- Hypothesis to be tested by the screening experiment (N31b-42), not here: enforcing sized members changes the winning actuator card or gear ratio in a non-trivial share of campaigns.
- The registry dates `foundry.size` at M4 and the gate may be recorded at M4; the seed places the work at M5 with Foundry. The closed-form routine has no sibling dependency, which is why it is delivered at M3 by N31b-38 for the stage-6 validity check; this seed starts from that code.
- Until N31a-60 lands, a campaign load set is checked at the root from the distal-port wrench only: a link loaded mostly by its own acceleration, or carrying a third port part-way along, is not fully represented. Both beads are M5 work of one package, and Foundry's exit needs both.
- Budget: milliseconds per link for the beam form; the finite-element form is a whole job of seconds to minutes and suits the worker process.
