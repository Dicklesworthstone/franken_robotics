## Background

Foundry is the subsystem that makes a design buildable: it extracts load cases from telemetry, sizes links,
checks stress and fatigue, feeds mass and inertia back into the model, and writes a build packet. It makes the
answer better, not sooner: no part of the ten-fold thesis rests on it. The ceiling is stated before any test:
with link structure a fraction f_L of robot mass and a saving s, the direct ceiling is f_L·s ≤ 0.27 × 0.30 ≈ 0.08
of robot mass (reported savings against engineered parts are roughly 7 to 30% of link mass, and in the one
quadruped with a published mass breakdown the legs are about 27% of the robot).

The structure loop has two rungs by decision (ADR-FOUNDRY-02): **closed-form beam sizing by default and as
fallback; three-dimensional topology optimization for finalists.** The beam rung costs milliseconds, is the
engineered baseline every optimized part is judged against, is the fallback if topology optimization is killed
(KILL-TOPOPT), and answers analysis **A-15, structural margin**: *would a link yield under the peak loads seen or
bounded?* A-15's mathematics is beam theory, and finite elements through FrankenSim's `fs-solid` for the full
form. This bead builds the beam rung, the A-15 margin and the verb `foundry.size`.

Decisions that bind it. A load case is one instant of one episode; peaks from different instants are never
assembled (ADR-FOUNDRY-03). Counterexample: a simply supported beam of span L carries P at L/3 at one instant and
P at 2L/3 at another; the mid-span moment is P·L/6 each time; assembling both peaks gives P·L/3, a twofold
over-design, and with opposite signs the assembled case gives zero while each real case gives ±P·L/6, which is
unsafe. Foundry results are at most class Estimated (INV-FOUNDRY-06). Fatigue is reported as utilization under a
named empirical law; the word "life" is not used for a result (ADR-FOUNDRY-06). The optimizer never checks its
own result (ADR-FOUNDRY-05): here that means the finite-element form of A-15 runs on a body-fitted mesh that no
optimizer produced.

Gate (TARGET): GATE-FOUNDRY-01, claim Known answer: cantilever deflection within 2% and stress within 5% after
refinement; the planted overstress located with utilization 1.25 ± 5%. The gate register allows it to be
recorded at M4 and requires it by M5. The seed done-condition adds the group gate INT-FSIM-15 (bead N33b-14),
re-run here at this bead's pin through production use.

## Scope

In: section idealization of a link between its ports; the closed-form criteria; stock selection; exact
comparison for rational inputs; the A-15 margin in beam form and in finite-element form with refinement; the
result record; the verb `foundry.size` and its refusal; known answers and the planted overstress.

Out: load campaigns, envelope cases and spectra (BD-400, `foundry.loads`); the independent check of optimized
geometry, modes and cumulative fatigue damage (BD-402); topology optimization (BD-403); inertia feedback and the
convergence test (BD-404); the screening-time validity check and its promotion rule (the Foundry unit's bead for
that subsection, which calls this routine); manufacturability, tolerances, export and the packet (BD-405 to
BD-408).

## Technical approach

Crate `frob-foundry` (layer 5), modules `beam/{section,criteria,stock,size}.rs` and `margin.rs`.

**1. Idealization.** Each link is idealized between its ports as a thin-walled tube or a rectangular section of
length L, material from its card (E, S_y, density ρ, Poisson ratio ν, a wall-buckling knock-down factor, each
with provenance). Internal loads at a section (axial force N, bending moment M as the vector sum of the two
transverse components, torque T, shear V) are taken from an envelope case: one real instant, with all port
wrenches and the body acceleration field from the same sample.

**2. Section properties.** Thin-walled tube of mean radius r and wall t: area A = 2π·r·t; second moment
I = π·r³·t; section modulus Z = π·r²·t; torsion constant J = 2π·r³·t. Rectangle of width b and depth h:
A = b·h; I = b·h³/12; Z = b·h²/6.

**3. Criteria, each with a utilization u (pass when u ≤ 1).**
- *Yield*: σ = abs(N)/A + M/Z; τ = T/(2π·r²·t) for the tube; u_y = √(σ² + 3·τ²)/(S_y/SF), SF the declared safety
  factor.
- *Column buckling*: P_cr = π²·E·I/(K·L)² with K the end-condition factor of the port pattern (1 for pinned
  ends, 2 for a cantilever); u_b = SF_b·N_compressive/P_cr.
- *Local wall buckling* (tube): the classical thin-cylinder stress E·t/(r·√(3·(1 − ν²))) multiplied by the card's
  knock-down factor (DESIGN: the plan fixes "a card knock-down" and this formula is the value it scales);
  u_l = σ_compressive/σ_local.
- *First bending frequency*: the beam formula with end masses; for a cantilever with tip mass m_tip and beam
  mass m_b, f₁ = (1/2π)·√(3·E·I/((m_tip + 0.2357·m_b)·L³)) (DESIGN: the Rayleigh one-mode value); u_f = f_floor/f₁,
  the floor being the actuator bandwidth requirement.
- *Fatigue at declared notches*: the utilization supplied by the fatigue observer for that notch under the
  campaign's spectra, when a valid stress-life curve exists; otherwise `fatigue: no-claim`.
The governing criterion and case are those with the largest u.

**4. Sizing.** From the stock catalogue of the link's section family (a card listing radii and walls, or widths
and depths, with provenance), pick the lightest size whose every utilization is at most 1 in every envelope
case. Ties are broken by smaller outer dimension, then by catalogue order, so the choice is deterministic. The
result gives the chosen size, m_min = ρ·A·L and its inertia about the proximal joint. If no stock size passes,
the result is `no-stock-passes` with the nearest size and its governing utilization.

**5. Exactness.** Card values and loads that are rationals are compared in exact arithmetic (class D0): the
decision "u ≤ 1" is made on rational expressions wherever the criterion is rational in its inputs (yield squared,
buckling), and with outward-rounded intervals where a square root or π enters; a decision inside the rounding
interval is reported as `at-limit` and counted as not passing.

**6. A-15, the structural margin.** Beam form: margin = 1/u_max − 1 per link, with the governing case, location
and criterion. Full form (finite elements, through the airlock's `StructuralOpt` solve on a body-fitted
tetrahedral mesh): per envelope case, utilization u = σ_vm/(S_y/SF) per element; the mesh is refined until the
peak moves by under 5% between refinements; a peak at a re-entrant corner that does not converge is reported as
`singular: fillet required` with the radius as the repair. The margin inherits the weaker evidence class of its
inputs and the label `model-conditional` when the governing load case carries it (a peak contact force that
moves by more than 20% under halved and doubled contact stiffness). Class: Estimated.

**7. The verb.** `foundry.size` (kind `read`; registry first milestone M4, delivered with Foundry at M5). Input:
a design, a link selector, a load set (the identifier of a load campaign's envelope cases, or `statics` with a
load factor), the section family, safety factors, optional `--fe` for the full form. Output per link: the chosen
section, m_min and inertia, utilizations by criterion, the governing case with its world address and instant,
the A-15 margin, labels. Refusal `FROB-REF-163`: a check whose material card lacks the field or provenance it
needs (repair: the missing field; `fatigue: no-claim` for the fatigue criterion alone). A design with no
qualification verdict is sized only with `--exploratory`, and that result is labelled and cannot enter a packet.
The full form needs the airlock's `structure` group; without it the beam form still answers and `--fe` returns
`FROB-REF-790`. Envelope evidence: claim list empty (a structural margin enters a claim product as its weakest
input); class Estimated; determinism D0 for the beam form.

- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-01, cantilever: L = 0.20 m, section 20 × 10 mm, E = 69 GPa, tip load 50 N: beam form gives tip deflection 1.159 mm and root stress 30 MPa exactly as closed forms; the finite-element form is within 2% on deflection and 5% on stress after refinement.
- [ ] GATE-FOUNDRY-01, planted overstress: the same beam with a root section 4 mm deep under 80 N with yield 240 MPa gives stress 300 MPa and utilization 1.25 ± 5%, located at the root; the unthinned beam passes with utilization 0.20.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-foundry beam margin`:
- `tube_section_properties`: r = 14 mm, t = 2 mm: A = 1.7593 × 10⁻⁴ m², I = 1.7241 × 10⁻⁸ m⁴, Z = 1.2315 × 10⁻⁶ m³, J = 3.4482 × 10⁻⁸ m⁴.
- `cantilever_closed_forms`: deflection P·L³/(3·E·I) = 1.159 mm with I = b·h³/12 = 1.6667 × 10⁻⁹ m⁴; root stress 6·P·L/(b·h²) = 30 MPa; halving the load halves both.
- `planted_overstress_utilization_1_25`: 6 × 80 × 0.2/(0.02 × 0.004²) = 300 MPa against 240 MPa; location `root`.
- `tube_sizing_hand_case`: required t = 180/(138 × 10⁶ × π × 0.015²) = 1.845 mm; chosen stock 2.0 mm; with the exact 1.85 mm wall the mass is 0.141 kg.
- `euler_buckling_pinned`: a pinned tube r = 10 mm, t = 1 mm, L = 0.5 m, E = 69 GPa: I = 3.1416 × 10⁻⁹ m⁴, P_cr = π²·E·I/L² = 8,558 N.
- `von_mises_combination`: σ = 100 MPa, τ = 50 MPa gives 132.29 MPa.
- `exact_decision_at_limit`: rational inputs that put u exactly at 1 are reported `at-limit`; one unit in the last place below passes.
- `deterministic_tie_break`; `no_stock_passes_reports_nearest`.
- `assembled_case_refused`; `governing_case_replays_to_sample`.
- `card_missing_yield_refused_163`; `fatigue_no_claim_without_curve`.
- `unit_error_caught_by_types`: passing millimetres where metres are expected does not compile (dimension typing).
- `fe_cantilever_within_2_and_5_percent` (feature `structure`); `fe_refinement_stops_under_5_percent`; `fe_reentrant_corner_reports_fillet`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `section-modulus-of-solid-rod` (uses π·r³/4 for the tube), `sum-of-peaks` (assembles cases), `ignore-torsion` (drops τ from the yield check on a torsion-dominated case).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/foundry_size.sh`: (1) `frob foundry size --fixture cantilever --json`; assert 1.159 mm and 30 MPa;
(2) `--fixture cantilever-thinned --load 80` and assert utilization 1.25 at the root and exit code 2 (completed
with findings); (3) `--fixture cantilever --fe` and assert the 2% and 5% bounds with the refinement history;
(4) `frob foundry size <qualified design> --loads <campaign id> --json` and assert a section per link, a
governing case with a world address, and class Estimated in the evidence block; (5) the same with a card
stripped of its yield field, asserting exit 3 and `FROB-REF-163`; (6) step 3 in a binary without the `structure`
group, asserting exit 5 and `FROB-REF-790`. Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code, wall time, evidence block,
metrics `tip_deflection_mm` (1.159, tolerance 2% for the finite-element form), `root_stress_mpa` (30, 5%),
`utilization_planted` (1.25 ± 5%), `refinement_levels`, `peak_change_last` (below 5%), and on failure the link,
criterion and both values. Summary names GATE-FOUNDRY-01 and INT-FSIM-15.

### Known answers and negative controls
The cantilever (1.159 mm; 30 MPa; shear adds about 0.2%; negative control: halving the load halves both, and a
millimetre-for-metre unit error is caught by dimension typing). The planted overstressed link (300 MPa,
utilization 1.25, at the root; control: the unthinned beam passes). The assembled-peaks case (P·L/6 per instant;
assembled P·L/3 or 0; control: the assembling mutation is refused). The tube sizing case (1.85 mm, 0.141 kg,
4.2 × 10⁻³ kg·m²; a design declaring that segment at 0.05 kg is under-built by a factor of 2.8). Metamorphic:
scaling every load by k scales every stress utilization by k and leaves the frequency criterion unchanged;
scaling all lengths by s at fixed load scales bending stress by 1/s²; rotating the load about the tube axis
changes nothing for a tube. Determinism: the beam form is identical everywhere; the finite-element form is
identical twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- KILL-TOPOPT (decided at M5): if the stage-2 pilot saves under 7%, does not converge or exceeds its budget, beam-rung sizing supplies mass and inertia feedback and three-dimensional optimization becomes an offline option or is dropped; A-15 is unaffected. KILL-LOADS (M4): if the load-extraction gate fails after one repair cycle, load cases are declared by hand from statics with a stated dynamic factor and spectra read `no-claim`; this bead's `statics` load set is that path.
- The beam rung is a floor on mass, not a design: no joints, no bolted interfaces, no local effects within one boss diameter of a port (`no-claim` there), and impact enters only through the load set.
- Actuator cards are held fixed inside the structure loop; re-selecting an actuator after a mass change is an outer step with full revalidation (ADR-FOUNDRY-07).
- Hypothesis to be tested by the screening bead, not here: enforcing sized members changes the winning actuator card or gear ratio in a non-trivial share of campaigns.
- The registry dates `foundry.size` at M4 and the gate may be recorded at M4; the seed places the work at M5 with Foundry. The closed-form routine has no sibling dependency, so an earlier consumer (the stage-6 validity check, M3) can call it as soon as this module exists.
- Budget: milliseconds per link for the beam form; the finite-element form is a whole job of seconds to minutes and suits the worker process.
