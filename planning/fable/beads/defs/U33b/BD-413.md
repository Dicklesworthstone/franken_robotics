## Background

A place changes because a robot is in it. A wheel drags oil out of a patch; a robot that crossed a spill slips on
its own second pass over a floor that was dry. A scene whose only state is rigid geometry and a friction map
cannot produce that failure. The Solid answer, already built by the seed work item this epic depends on, is the
**film-friction patch**: a static, inexhaustible wetted region where the friction offered to the contact cone
depends on the film thickness h under each contact sample, which drains by the squeeze-film relation
d(h⁻²)/dt = 4·F/(3π·η·R⁴) for a pad of radius R under load F on a fluid of viscosity η. In that tier the robot
neither removes nor carries film.

This epic is the Frontier step: the film becomes a **per-world field** h(x, y, t) on a two-dimensional lattice
that the robot spreads and carries. It is a coverage feature. Its ceiling is stated before any test: it adds cost
and contributes nothing to throughput; its whole benefit is the number of failure classes it produces that a
static patch cannot, of which the clearest is tracked film on a dry floor.

It stays inside the determinism classes because it is an integer-indexed field with a conservative update, not a
fluid solver in the batch. The fluid solvers of the sibling FrankenSim are reached only through the gated
multiphysics bridge, as an evaluation-tier reference, never from a batched kernel.

## Technical approach

The short plan the seed asks for, in the order the children run.

1. **Pilot first.** With a carried film of 20 µm, a transfer fraction of one half per wheel revolution and a
   roughness of 5 µm (all assumed), the deposited film stays at or above film ratio Λ = h/σ = 1 for two
   revolutions (20 → 10 → 5 µm), which is 1.26 m for a wheel of radius 0.1 m. The pilot repeats this arithmetic
   over the admitted ranges of the three parameters. Falsifier: if the tracked low-friction length is under one
   wheel circumference across those ranges, advection changes no outcome and the work is not funded.
2. **The field.** Cell edge Δx (default 2 cm); sparse tiles of 16 × 16 cells allocated from a per-device pool
   when first wetted; each contact body carries a reservoir volume with a capacity. Owner: `frob-scene` for the
   field and its update kernel, `frob-contact` for the friction it feeds. Cost: a tile is 1,024 bytes of
   thickness; counting whole tiles of 0.32 m, a 6 m × 6 m floor fully wet is 19 × 19 = 361 tiles, about 370 kB
   per world; a 1 m × 1 m spill is 16 tiles. An exhausted pool marks the world `Degraded: film-pool`.
3. **The update, finite volume and conservative.** For each cell, h ← h − (Δt/A)·Σ_faces F_f with face flux
   F_f = [1_f·h_up·(v̄·n)_f − (g/(3ν))·h_f³·(∇h·n)_f]·L_f. The first term is transport by the sliding contact (1_f
   marks faces under a contact patch, h_up is the upwind thickness, v̄ the mean tangential velocity of the
   patch); the second is gravity spreading in the lubrication limit, with face mobility the arithmetic mean of
   the cubes, h_f³ = ½·(h_L³ + h_R³), so that a wet cell beside a dry one has a nonzero flux and the front
   advances. Pickup moves volume from the cells under a contact into that body's reservoir and redeposition moves
   it back; each transfer is one paired statement, so what leaves one store enters the other.
4. **Stability and refusal.** Gravity spreading is a nonlinear diffusion with coefficient D = g·h³/(3ν); the
   explicit update is stable for Δt ≤ Δx²/(4·D), which bounds the thickness at
   h_max = (3ν·Δx²/(4·g·Δt))^(1/3): 2.5 mm for water at Δx = 2 cm and Δt = 2 ms, 10 mm for a 65 mm²/s oil. A deeper
   liquid is not a film; it is refused or sub-cycled at admission (`FROB-REF-307`).
5. **The gate, then the kill decision.** GATE-HAB-07 is measured on every tier; KILL-ADVECT is decided with it.
6. **The sibling reference.** Through the gated bridge (INT-FSIM-17): the sibling's film module conserves mass to
   10⁻¹² relative over 10⁴ steps and its channel flow matches the Poiseuille parabola. The in-batch field's
   spreading is compared with that evaluation-tier reference on the spill fixtures; the comparison is labelled
   evaluation tier and enters no throughput claim.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
Owned by the children. The ones this epic requires to exist: the three-cell conservation case; the stability
bound; pool exhaustion marking `Degraded: film-pool`; reservoir pairing (the sum of cell volume and reservoir
volume is unchanged by a pickup or a redeposition).

### End-to-end script and logging
The children's gate script (the `scripts/e2e/` entry of N19b-10, the GATE-HAB-07 bead) drives `frob sim run` on the spill
fixtures on each tier and logs per STD-E2E the volume residual, the fitted spreading exponent, the leak count
and the added step cost with their thresholds; the bridge's script step is in bead N33b-21. This epic adds no
script of its own; its closing check reads both summaries.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-442: total volume, cells plus reservoirs, is constant (invariant INV-HAB-07), with no trace in another world.
KA-443: a constant volume released on a flat floor spreads with radius proportional to t^(1/8) in the
axisymmetric case and front position proportional to t^(1/5) in the planar case; removing the h³ factor changes
the exponent. Counterexample CX-191, the seed of the negative control: writing transport as 1·∇·(h·v̄) with the
contact indicator outside the divergence creates or destroys film at the patch boundary; with three cells
h = (0, 1, 0), the indicator on the middle cell only and v̄ = +1, the masked update removes Δt/Δx from the middle
cell and adds it nowhere, while the face-flux form moves the same amount into the right-hand cell. KA-772 for the
bridge.

## Considerations

- KILL-ADVECT (decided at M6 with GATE-HAB-07). Trigger: the advected film fails its mass gate after one repair cycle, or costs more than 15% of the step, or design rankings on the spill battery agree with static patches at a rank correlation of 0.95 or above. Fallback: static film-friction patches under the Solid law; advection is not built. If the film-friction law's own kill gate fires (KILL-FILM, decided by N18a-34), both fall to static condition cards.
- Evidence class of everything this mechanism outputs is Estimated; the validity of the drainage law for treaded soles is a hypothesis with no data yet, and the Reality cell is `no-claim`.
- A harmonic or geometric mean for the face mobility gives zero flux at a wet-dry front and the spill never spreads; the arithmetic mean of cubes is a requirement, not a tuning choice.
- Memory is small (about 370 kB per fully wet 6 m floor) but the pool is per device and shared by all worlds; pool sizing enters the admission estimate.
- At most four Moonshot items are active in a milestone; this epic is Frontier and does not count against that, but its bridge shares build cost with the coupled-islands Moonshot.
