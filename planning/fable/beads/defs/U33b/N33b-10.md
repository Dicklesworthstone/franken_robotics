## Background

FrankenRobotics reaches FrankenSim only through the airlock crate `frob-fsim`. A crate that compiles is not yet
admitted: for each sibling source file the project leans on there is a named first test that must pass before any
code above the airlock calls it. The gate register and the M0 milestone both require the file-by-file tests
INT-FSIM-02 to -11 to have a record at the M0 exit. Three of them concern crates whose production consumers
arrive later: collision primitives at M1, actuator ports and materials at M1, sensitivities at M3. The seed work
items that build those consumers (BD-079, BD-068, BD-243) sit at their own milestones.

This bead closes the gap (DESIGN): it runs the three first tests at M0, through test-only adapters, and writes
their records. The later beads then build production adapters on admitted ground and re-run the same gates at the
pin of their own day, because an admission is never inherited. The reason to do it at M0 and not wait: M0 exists
to find out, with the smallest amount of code, whether the foundations can be consumed from outside their trees;
a file whose first test fails at M0 changes the M1 plan while it is still cheap to change.

## Scope

In: one probe file per gate under `crates/frob-fsim/tests/admission/`, each with hand cases, a seeded family and
negative controls; the compile-cost record of the two non-default groups; three admission records.

Out: `frob-contact`'s primitives and their parity gate (BD-079); the production `PortHamiltonian`, friction and
material adapters used by the actuator subsystem (BD-068); the analyses that consume sensitivities (BD-243 and its
split beads); the sign hazard between `fs_phs::transformer` and `interconnect_mapped`, which has its own later
gate; mesh-backed scene interiors (scene cooking, the Habitat units).

## Technical approach

Lane: `cargo test -p frob-fsim --features actuation,optimize --test admission`. The two extra features are enabled
by this lane only; no default build compiles them (FACT, computed from the manifests on 2026-11-21: beyond the M1
closure `actuation` adds 2 crates and about 151 thousand lines, `optimize` 12 crates and about 84 thousand). Cold
and warm check times and peak memory of the lane are recorded and must fit the machine envelope (one hour, 16 GiB,
8 cores). Random inputs are drawn from `fs-rand` streams keyed by `(seed, case_index)`.

**INT-FSIM-08: a box-on-plane support query equals the closed form.**
- Closed form. A box with half-extents (a, b, c), centre c₀ and rotation R with columns r₁, r₂, r₃ has support
  function h(d) = c₀·d + a·abs(r₁·d) + b·abs(r₂·d) + c·abs(r₃·d) in unit direction d. Against the half-space
  z ≤ 0 the signed gap is g = c₀_z − (a·abs(r₁_z) + b·abs(r₂_z) + c·abs(r₃_z)) and the normal is (0, 0, 1).
- Hand cases. An axis-aligned cube of half-extent 0.5 m centred at z = 0.51 m: g = 0.01 m. The same cube rotated
  45° about x and centred at z = 1 m: g = 1 − 0.5·(cos 45° + sin 45°) = 1 − 0.707107 = 0.292893 m, with the
  witness on the lower edge.
- Seeded family: 1,000 boxes (half-extents in [0.01, 1] m, uniform random rotation, centre height in [0, 3] m).
  Pass: abs(g_query − g_closed) ≤ 10⁻¹² m·max(1, extent) and the normal within 10⁻¹² of (0, 0, 1); a penetrating
  box reports a negative gap of the right magnitude.
- Keep-out and support boxes of `fs-scene` are constructed with their role tags and read back unchanged.
- Recorded limit: a request for a mesh-backed interior, a signed-distance pair or a heightfield is refused by the
  sibling as a missing capability (FACT as read). The probe asserts the refusal arrives as a typed airlock
  refusal carrying the upstream reason, and the record states that such interiors are **not admitted by this
  test**.
- Negative controls: `full-extent-as-half` (extents passed without halving; the first hand case returns
  g = −0.49 m) and `unrotated-support` (the rotation ignored; the second hand case returns 0.5 m). Both must fail.

**INT-FSIM-09: power, provenance, and a missing pair.**
- Power across an interconnection. Two port-Hamiltonian elements are joined through `interconnect_mapped` (the
  only entry point this project reaches; the authored sign is passed explicitly). Fixture: the brushed motor.
  Winding state φ = L·i, rotor state p = J_r·ω, joined by a gyrator of modulus k with τ = k·i and e = k·ω. Power
  into the gyrator on the electrical side is e·i = k·ω·i and power out on the shaft side is τ·ω = k·i·ω: the sum
  is zero. Second fixture: a transformer of modulus N (a gear): ω₂ = ω₁/N and τ₂ = N·τ₁, so τ₁·ω₁ − τ₂·ω₂ = 0.
  Over 1,000 seeded port states and moduli in [0.1, 100]: abs(Σ_j e_j·f_j) ≤ 10⁻¹²·Σ_j abs(e_j·f_j), with e the
  efforts and f the flows at the junction's ports.
- Hand case: k = 0.05 N·m/A, i = 4 A, ω = 100 rad/s: e = 5 V, τ = 0.2 N·m, powers 20 W in and 20 W out.
- Material data. The probe enumerates the material pairs `fs-matdb` lists at the pin and, for each, asks
  `fs-tribo`'s law selection for its parameters. Pass: every returned parameter carries a provenance record
  (source and class); a parameter without one fails the clause. The count of pairs and parameters is recorded.
- A missing pair. A query for the pair (`frob-test:unobtainium`, `frob-test:void`) must return a typed refusal,
  never a default coefficient.
- Negative controls: `non-conserving-junction` (the modulus applied to effort and flow in the same direction, so
  the power sum is (1 − N²)·τ₁·ω₁); `default-friction` (an adapter that returns μ = 0.5 for the missing pair);
  `stripped-provenance` (an adapter that drops the provenance record). Each must fail.

**INT-FSIM-10: the implicit sensitivity of a 2×2 fixed point equals its closed form.**
- Closed form. For a fixed point x = F(x, θ) with I − ∂F/∂x nonsingular, dx*/dθ = (I − ∂F/∂x)⁻¹·∂F/∂θ.
- Hand case. F(x, θ) = A(θ)·x + b with A = [[θ, 0.1], [0.2, 0.3]] and b = (1, 2), at θ = 0.5. Then
  I − A = [[0.5, −0.1], [−0.2, 0.7]], its determinant is 0.33, x* = (30/11, 40/11) = (2.727273, 3.636364),
  ∂F/∂θ = (x₁*, 0), and dx*/dθ = (700/121, 200/121) = (5.785124, 1.652893).
- The probe evaluates the sensitivity with `fs-ad`'s implicit-function routine and compares it with the closed
  form to 10⁻¹² relative. A nonlinear contraction (x₁ = θ·cos x₂, x₂ = ½·sin x₁) is added and checked against
  `gradcheck` central differences to 10⁻⁷ relative, at 100 seeded θ in [0.1, 0.9].
- Cyclic cases. For n ∈ {2, 3, 4, 6} the generator of the cyclic group applied n times is the identity and the
  group has n elements; any orbit or averaging routine the crate exposes is compared with a brute-force
  enumeration written in the test.
- Recorded scope, stated in the record as limits and not as defects: `fs-symmetry` covers cyclic groups only;
  `fs-ad` offers no derivative through a contact event.
- Negative controls: `naive-partial` (returns ∂F/∂θ = (2.727273, 0) without the inverse) and `wrong-sign`
  (returns −(I − ∂F/∂x)⁻¹·∂F/∂θ). Both must fail the hand case.

**Records.** Three entries in `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md`, each with the commit whose tracked lockfile contains the
crates, the command, its output hash, the receipt hash, the worst measured error with its case index, the recorded
limits and the compile cost. Each row is marked `first test at M0; re-run at first production use` and names the
bead that re-runs it (BD-079, BD-068, BD-243). Claim: Known answer; class `Tested`; tier host or referee;
determinism D2 on one platform.

### Known answers and negative controls
Hand values as stated in the approach. Negative controls: `full-extent-as-half`, `unrotated-support`,
`non-conserving-junction`, `default-friction`, `stripped-provenance`, `naive-partial`, `wrong-sign`. Metamorphic:
translating a box horizontally leaves its gap unchanged; scaling efforts by k and flows by 1/k leaves the power
sum zero; the sensitivity of the linear case to b₁ equals the first column of (I − A)⁻¹. Determinism: fixed seeds
give identical worst-case indices twice on one machine and in debug and release.

## Considerations

- The gate register places these three gates at M0 while the seed work items that carry their names sit at M1 and M3. This bead is the reconciliation: the M0 record exists, and the later beads own production use and the re-run.
- `fs-phs` defers constrained interconnections upstream, so closed-loop mechanisms do not rely on it; the probe uses unconstrained junctions only. A second polarization pair for its battery is upstream item FD-FSIM-21.
- `fs_phs::transformer` and `interconnect_mapped` drive opposite directions while both conserve power, so power conservation alone cannot detect a swapped sign. That is why only `interconnect_mapped` is reached and why the signed motion witness is a separate gate.
- If a group fails to compile from outside the tree at the pin, the gate is recorded `fail` with the reason, and the consumer beads fall back as their own plans state (local implementations against the same known answers).
- Convex support queries are admitted for primitives only. Contact generation beyond that is this project's work, checked against `fs-query` where both apply (upstream item FD-FSIM-03).
