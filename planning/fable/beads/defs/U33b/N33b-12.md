## Background

The two study types of the design studio (bead BD-124) arrive at M1, and one of them, the anytime-valid study, is
built on an **e-process**: a nonnegative statistic that starts at 1, is expected not to grow when the null
hypothesis is true, and may be watched continuously, because the chance that it ever reaches 1/α under the null
is at most α. FrankenSim already implements this machinery (FACT, to be re-read here): crate `fs-eproc` has
betting e-processes and a mixture confidence sequence, requires an explicit `LossSpan` (a bound on the observed
difference that "must not be estimated from the race data"), provides `combine_average` (the average of e-values
is an e-value under arbitrary dependence), `combine_product` (valid only for independent comparisons) and
`e_benjamini_hochberg` (false discovery rate at most α under arbitrary dependence), and has a Bernoulli
confidence sequence with a cap of 10⁶ samples.

The dependency firewall says what M1 takes from FrankenSim: the referee, geometry, the ledger, the memo store,
and "the statistics the two study types use (admitted when first used)". An admission is never inherited and
never anticipated: a crate first used at M1 is admitted at M1 by its own record. The group gate for statistics is
INT-FSIM-14, whose full form (the `stats` group: uncertainty quantification, value of information, surrogates)
is due at M3 and is bead N33b-13. This bead is its **M1 part**: the one crate the M1 study engine calls.

A bookkeeping fact to state plainly: in the airlock's feature table `fs-eproc` and `fs-race` sit in the group
`optimize`, not in `stats`, and the plan defines no group gate named for `optimize`. DESIGN: their first-use
admission is recorded as added rows of INT-FSIM-14, in the same form as every group gate (the group compiles
from this repository behind its feature, one known answer passes through the airlock trait, one negative control
fails). `fs-race` is reached through the same trait; its row is written with the `Racing` adapter (N33a-18) and
re-run at the pin of each consumer (BD-262, BD-341).

## Scope
In: the M1 part of INT-FSIM-14 for `fs-eproc`: the admission test with hand-checkable known answers and negative
controls, run through the trait `Racing` of N33a-18; the local reference rule used as fallback and oracle; the
record.

Out: the trait `Racing`, its adapter and the `fs-race` row (N33a-18, a dependency of this bead); the study
engines (BD-124); the full `stats` group and its gate at M3 (N33b-13); rare-event confidence sequences above 10⁶
samples (upstream work item FD-FSIM-18).

## Technical approach

The airlock surface is the trait `Racing` of N33a-18, in `crates/frob-fsim/src/optimize/racing.rs` (feature
`optimize`, off by default; enabled only by the crates on its consumer allow-list in the feature records of
N33a-11 as N33b-09 extends them; at M1 the study engine in `frob-arena` is its only production consumer, and
`frob-conformance` runs this lane).

```rust
// What this admission requires of `Racing` (N33a-18), stated as intent. It is not a second trait: `start` is
// `Racing::eprocess`; `observe`, `e_value` and `confidence_sequence` are read from the `EState` that
// `Racing::observe` returns; the two free functions are `Racing::combine_average` and
// `Racing::e_benjamini_hochberg`.
pub struct Span(pub Qty);                                   // required; no default; never estimated from data
pub trait EProcess {
    fn start(&self, null: &Hypothesis, margin: Qty, span: Span, rule: RuleId, level: Ratio) -> Result<EState, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn observe(&self, st: &mut EState, paired_difference: Qty) -> Result<(), RefereeRefusal>;
    fn e_value(&self, st: &EState) -> Ratio;
    fn confidence_sequence(&self, st: &EState) -> (Qty, Qty);
}
pub fn combine_average(es: &[Ratio]) -> Ratio;              // valid under arbitrary dependence
pub fn e_bh(es: &[Ratio], alpha: Ratio) -> Vec<usize>;      // indices rejected
```

Adapter rules: convert and never reinterpret (a sibling refusal is carried with its reason); an observation
outside the declared span is a refusal, not a clamp; state is a value with no hidden global, so a study can be
checkpointed and resumed; results carry the stack-lock digest.

**Local reference (fallback and oracle).** `frob_arena::study::reference_eprocess`: the fixed-fraction betting
rule E_k = Π_{i ≤ k} (1 + λ·(d_i − m)/(s + m)) with λ = ½, in exact rational arithmetic. It is what the study
engine uses if this admission fails, and what the sibling is compared with where the sibling exposes a
fixed-fraction rule.

**The admission test** (`crates/frob-fsim/tests/admission/int_fsim_14_m1.rs`):
1. *Known answer A, combination.* `combine_average` of (2, 4, 6) is 4, exactly.
2. *Known answer B, the e-value Benjamini–Hochberg rule.* For e-values (100, 40, 1), α = 0.05 and three
   hypotheses, the k-th largest must reach 3/(0.05·k) = 60, 30, 20 for k = 1, 2, 3. The largest k that qualifies
   is 2 (100 ≥ 60 and 40 ≥ 30; 1 < 20), so the first two hypotheses are rejected.
3. *Known answer C, validity under the null.* 10⁴ seeded sequences of fair ±1 differences of length 1,000 with
   span 1 and margin 0; the number of sequences whose e-value ever reaches 20 must not exceed 565 (5% of 10⁴ plus
   three binomial standard deviations).
4. *Known answer D, power on a separated pair.* Differences all +1: the sibling's e-value must reach 20 within
   50 observations (the fixed-fraction reference reaches it at observation 8, since 1.5⁷ = 17.09 and
   1.5⁸ = 25.63). The count at which it crosses is recorded.
5. *Differential.* Where the sibling exposes a fixed-fraction rule, its e-value on the sequence (+1, +1, −1, +1)
   equals the reference's 1.6875; otherwise the record names the rule the sibling uses and skips this row as
   `not-comparable`.
6. *Negative control 1, the span.* A process cannot be started without a `Span` (compile-fail on the airlock
   type), and an adapter that estimates the span from the first observations (`planted::estimated-span`) is
   caught by a test that feeds one observation of ten times the estimated span and expects a refusal.
7. *Negative control 2, the product on dependent metrics.* Two copies of one metric with e-value 5 each: the
   average is 5 and does not reject at α = 0.05; the product is 25 and does. An adapter that combines by product
   (`planted::product-on-dependent`) must be caught.
8. *Recorded.* The betting rules and confidence sequences the crate exposes; its sample caps; the compile cost of
   enabling `optimize` (FACT, computed from the manifests on 2026-11-21: 12 crates and about 84 thousand lines
   beyond the M1 closure) with measured cold and warm check times.

**Record.** `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md` entry titled `INT-FSIM-14 (M1 part: fs-eproc)`: commit, command, output
hash, receipt hash, the four known answers with measured values, both controls, and the outcome `admitted` or
`local reference in force`. Claim: Known answer; class `Tested`; tier host; determinism D0 for exact rows, a
seeded statistical bound for row 3.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four known answers pass through the airlock: 4; rejections {1, 2}; at most 565 null crossings in 10⁴; a crossing within 50 observations on the separated pair.
- [ ] Both negative controls are caught and registered.
- [ ] The local reference passes rows 1 to 4 on its own, so the fallback is exercised regardless of the outcome.
- [ ] `optimize` is off in every default build; only the allow-listed crates enable it (`xtask check-deps`); the lane fits the machine envelope.
- [ ] The record exists with a receipt hash and the outcome, and the `registries/gates.toml` row of INT-FSIM-14 shows its M1 part with measured values; the M3 part stays `not run` until N33b-13.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `eproc_average_2_4_6_is_4`; `eproc_ebh_100_40_1_rejects_two`; `eproc_null_crossings_at_most_565`; `eproc_separated_pair_crosses_within_50`.
- `eproc_differential_fixed_fraction` (expects 1.6875 or `not-comparable`).
- `span_is_required` (compile-fail); `planted_estimated_span_caught`; `planted_product_on_dependent_caught`.
- `out_of_span_observation_refused`: a difference of 1.5 under span 1 is refused with the upstream reason.
- `state_checkpoint_resume_equal`: an e-process serialized after 100 observations and resumed gives the same value at 200 as an uninterrupted one.
- `reference_passes_rows_1_to_4`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/suite_airlock_groups.sh` (bead N33b-21), step `stats_m1`: runs the lane with `--features optimize`
under a timeout and reads `frob doctor --json` for the gate row. Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code, wall
time, evidence block, metrics `null_crossings` (threshold 565 of 10⁴), `separated_cross_at` (threshold 50),
`ebh_rejections` (expected 2), `outcome`, `cold_check_s`, and on failure the seed and sequence index.

### Known answers and negative controls
As listed in the approach. The group gate's own known answers (the closed-form ranking-flip probability and the
multilevel estimate of E[x²]) and its control (importance weights that trip the effective-sample-size floor)
belong to the M3 part and are in N33b-13 (KA-769). Metamorphic: scaling every difference, the margin and the span by
one factor leaves the e-value unchanged; permuting hypotheses permutes the e-BH rejections. Determinism: exact rows
are identical everywhere; row 3 is a pure function of its seed.

## Considerations

- OPEN-ATEL-3 applies: if the crate does not pass on the toolchain T, the default is the local implementation against the same known answers, and every anytime study's evidence names it.
- The plan's group table and its gate table disagree about where sequential statistics belong (`optimize` or `stats`). This bead records the admission under the gate the seed work item names and keeps the feature where the airlock table puts it; the coverage file reports the mismatch.
- `fs-eproc`'s Bernoulli sequence caps at 10⁶ samples. Studies at M1 are far below it; claims that need more are a later concern (upstream item FD-FSIM-18).
- An e-process is only as valid as its span. The span comes from the task definition, is part of the study's registered plan, and is in the pre-registration hash.
