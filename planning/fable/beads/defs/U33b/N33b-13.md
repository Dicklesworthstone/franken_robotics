## Background

From M3 the studio needs statistics beyond a paired comparison: rare-failure estimation for the adversarial-world
subsystem, value-of-information ranking for experiment suggestion, uncertainty propagation for the qualification
lane, and later reduced-order surrogates and belief updates from measurements. FrankenSim has crates for these.
They are reached only through the airlock crate `frob-fsim`, behind the Cargo feature group **`stats`**, which is
off by default so that only the milestone that needs it compiles its closure.

the number of other FrankenSim crates a non-optional path dependency compiles):

| Crate | Library lines (with tests) | Closure | Needs `fs-qty` | What it provides | Reached by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fs-uq` | 15,887 (23,672) | 37 | yes | Karhunen–Loève fields, polynomial chaos, quasi-Monte Carlo, multilevel Monte Carlo, control variates, importance sampling, Shapley effects | `frob-nemesis`, `frob-arena`, `frob-ground` |
| `fs-voi` | 1,044 (1,715) | 5 | yes | Ranking-flip probability, an expected-value-of-information surrogate, expected opportunity loss | `frob-opt`, `frob-arena`, `frob-mission` |
| `fs-surrogate` | 6,663 (8,841) | 0 by default | no | Reduced-order models (POD, DMD, polynomial EDMD) admitted only inside validity bands, with escalation | `frob-opt`, `frob-mission` |
| `fs-assimilate` | 13,024 (20,534) | 9 | yes, directly | Checked linear-Gaussian belief updates from instrument observations | `frob-ground` |

Beyond the M1 closure the group adds 10 crates and about 113 thousand lines of library source (FACT, computed
from the manifests on 2026-11-21). One crate that looks as if it belonged here does not: `fs-conform` is a
conformance kit for chart-to-chart converters, not conformal prediction, despite the name; it is not admitted, and
conformal bands come from `fs-surrogate` or are written in this project.

The group gate is INT-FSIM-14 (first needed at M3; thresholds are TARGETs). Like every group gate it is the
path-consumption gate applied to one group at the milestone that first uses it: the group compiles from this
repository behind its feature, one known answer passes through the airlock trait, and one negative control fails.

| Known answer through the airlock | Negative control |
|---|---|
| A two-design comparison with mean gap Δ and standard deviations s₁, s₂ returns the closed-form ranking-flip probability Φ(−Δ/√(s₁² + s₂²)) to 10⁻¹²; a multilevel estimate of E[x²] under a standard normal is 1 within its own stated interval | Importance weights on a proposal that misses the failure region trip the effective-sample-size floor and the estimate is refused |

The M1 part of this gate (the sequential-statistics crate used by the study types) was recorded by bead N33b-12.

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-fsim/src/stats/{uq,voi,surrogate,assimilate,regime,robust}.rs`, all behind feature `stats`.

```rust
// DESIGN: trait names fix intent, not signatures.
pub trait ValueOfInformation {
    fn ranking_flip_probability(&self, gap: Qty, s1: Qty, s2: Qty) -> Result<Ratio, RefereeRefusal>;

pub trait Surrogate   { /* fit inside a validity band; a query outside it escalates or refuses */ }
```

Adapter rules (the airlock's three, applied here): a sibling refusal is converted with its reason and never
retried with weakened inputs (an effective-sample-size refusal is **not** answered by lowering the floor); no
result is cached across pins; limits are data (the sibling's sample caps and the size limits of its drivers are
fields a caller can read before submitting). A verb that needs the group in a binary built without it returns
`FROB-REF-790` (`foundation-group-absent`).

**INT-FSIM-14, the test** (`crates/frob-fsim/tests/admission/int_fsim_14.rs`, lane `--features stats`).
1. *Ranking flip, closed form.* With independent normal estimates of two designs, the probability that the
   observed order is wrong is Φ(−Δ/√(s₁² + s₂²)), Φ the standard normal distribution function. Hand cases:
   Δ = 1, s₁ = s₂ = 1/√2 gives Φ(−1) = 0.158655253931; Δ = 0 gives 0.5; Δ = 3, s₁ = 3, s₂ = 4 gives
   Φ(−0.6) = 0.274253117750; and KA-769's printed case, Δ = 1, s₁ = s₂ = 1, gives Φ(−1/√2) =
   0.23975006109347673. Then 1,000 seeded triples. Pass: agreement to 10⁻¹² with an independent evaluation
   through the deterministic error function of `fs-math`.
2. *Multilevel estimate.* E[x²] for x standard normal is 1. A three-level plan whose levels are the same
   integrand at increasing sample resolution is run through the sibling's allocation with seeded streams; pass
   when 1 lies inside the interval the estimate states, and when the stated interval's half-width is below 0.05.
   Repeated over 200 seeds, the truth lies inside the stated 95% interval in at least 180 runs (a coverage check
   on the interval itself).
3. *Negative control, the effective-sample-size floor.* Target: x standard normal; failure region x > 4
   (probability 3.167 × 10⁻⁵); proposal centred at −2 with unit variance, which puts about 10⁻⁹ of its mass in
   the region. The floor (100, DESIGN default for the test) applies to the effective sample size of the
   weighted indicator, (Σ w·f)² / Σ (w·f)² with w the likelihood ratio and f the indicator of the region, taken
   as 0 when no draw hits. With n = 10⁴ draws from the −2 proposal no draw hits (the expected count is about
   10⁻⁵), the statistic is 0, and the estimate must be **refused**. Hand anchors for the statistic: weights
   (1, 0, 0, 0) give an effective sample size of 1; four equal weights give 4. A second run with the proposal
   centred at +4 gives a statistic of about 1,800 (expected n·(E[w·f])²/E[(w·f)²] = 10⁴ × 0.181), passes the
   floor and returns an interval that contains 3.167 × 10⁻⁵. The statistic on the raw weights, (Σ w)² / Σ w²,
   would get both runs wrong: about 10⁴/e⁴ ≈ 180 for the −2 proposal (above the floor) and about 10⁴/e¹⁶,
   under 1 in expectation and about 5 to 20 on seeded runs, for the +4 proposal (below it). If the sibling's
   statistic is the raw-weight one, the adapter computes the weighted-indicator statistic from the returned
   weights and the record states it under the recorded limits (step 5).
4. *Planted adapter* `planted::lower-the-floor`: on refusal it retries with floor 1 and returns the estimate. The
   test asserts that a returned estimate for case 3 is a failure.
5. *Recorded limits.* The sample caps (reported: 10⁶ in the importance execution), the form of the multilevel
   driver (reported: a per-call closure, so batched estimation is this project's), Gaussian germs only for
   importance sampling.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The group compiles from this repository behind feature `stats`, off by default, inside the machine envelope; only allow-listed crates enable it.
- [ ] The ranking-flip probability equals Φ(−Δ/√(s₁² + s₂²)) to 10⁻¹² on the four hand cases (KA-769's Φ(−1/√2) = 0.23975006109347673 among them) and 1,000 seeded triples.
- [ ] The multilevel estimate of E[x²] contains 1 inside its stated interval, and the interval covers the truth in at least 180 of 200 seeded runs.
- [ ] The missed-region proposal trips the effective-sample-size floor and is refused; the centred proposal is accepted with an interval containing 3.167 × 10⁻⁵.
- [ ] The planted `lower-the-floor` adapter is caught; no adapter path retries a refusal with weaker inputs.
- [ ] Built without the feature, the airlock's group-absent path for `stats` returns the refusal that maps to `FROB-REF-790` naming the group (exit 5 for a verb whose registry row names `stats` in `needs_groups`, through N33c-32's admission check). No row names the group at M3; the beads of its consumers declare it with their verbs and check it through the binary in their own scripts.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-14 is recorded in full in `registries/gates.toml` with measured values, entered with `record_gate` from the harness receipt of the `g1_known_answers` run of the Record paragraph, which the admission record cites as `harness_receipt`; each later first-use probe is listed with its due milestone and owning consumer.
- [ ] The effective-sample-size floor is applied to the weighted indicator: the −2 proposal (no hit in 10⁴ draws, statistic 0) is refused and the +4 proposal (statistic about 1,800) is accepted; a planted check on the raw weights, which accepts the first and refuses the second, is caught.
- [ ] The `fs-voi` probe passes or fails on record (the hand cases, the worked menu 0.00131 and 0.00550 within 1%, the zero-value stop), and the `fs-voi` row of `docs/adr/OPEN-ATEL-3.md` no longer reads `deferred`: it names the branch, the reason and this record's receipt hash.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-769: the ranking-flip probability Φ(−Δ/√(s₁² + s₂²)) to 10⁻¹², and the multilevel estimate of E[x²] equal to 1
within its own stated interval; its negative control is the effective-sample-size refusal. Additional control:
the planted adapter. Metamorphic: scaling Δ, s₁ and s₂ by one positive factor leaves the flip probability
unchanged; shifting the target and the proposal of case 3 by the same amount leaves the refusal unchanged.
Determinism: seeded streams keyed by logical identity give identical estimates twice on one machine and across
worker counts; cross-machine comparison of ensemble results is not claimed (upstream item FD-FSIM-24).

## Considerations

- The gate register dates INT-FSIM-14 at M3 and the dependency firewall lists "statistics" for FrankenSim at M3; the study types at M1 use only the sequential-statistics crate, admitted separately.
- OPEN-ATEL-3: if `fs-voi` does not pass on the toolchain T, the closed-form value is implemented locally against the same known answers (one line of arithmetic and an error function).
- Upstream items that change what the group can do: a batched entry point for multilevel estimation and importance sampling beyond Gaussian germs (FD-FSIM-17); lifting the 10⁶ sample caps (FD-FSIM-18); a splitting estimator (FD-FSIM-19); windowed assimilation beyond 256 rows and a cross-machine statement for its ensemble lane (FD-FSIM-23, -24). Interim postures: this project's own estimators; an exact binomial bound for larger counts; splitting implemented here; windows of summary features.
- A closure of 37 crates for `fs-uq` is the reason the group is a feature. If the cold check of the group does not fit the envelope, the group is consumed through the worker process and the adapters speak the job protocol.
- An estimate refused for effective sample size is information: it tells the caller that its proposal does not reach the failure region. The refusal carries the measured value so that the caller can choose a better proposal.
