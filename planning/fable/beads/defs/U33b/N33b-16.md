## Background

Some failures of a robot in a real place involve matter that is not rigid: a spill it tracks across a floor,
liquid sloshing in a carried pot, a rug it pushes into a fold. The plan refuses the strong response, fluid, cloth
and cable solvers inside every training world, by arithmetic (a 10 m × 10 m × 3 m room at 1 cm lattice spacing is
3.0 × 10⁸ cells and 45.6 GB for one world; a film 0.1 to 1 mm thick needs cells of 0.25 mm; a coupled world may
cost at most 181 rigid worlds if 5% of worlds are coupled and the batch keeps a tenth of its throughput, and the
sibling solvers miss that by orders of magnitude). It admits living matter in tiers, and only the cheapest are in
batches. FrankenSim's fluid and coupling crates have exactly two jobs here: an **evaluation-tier reference** for
the in-batch film mechanisms, and the Moonshot **coupled islands** that run beside one referee world for
finalists.

FACT (FrankenSim head `483d09c682f2`): `fs-lbm` is 348,054 library lines (517,241 with tests), closure 39, needs
`fs-qty` directly, and provides lattice Boltzmann solvers with `film`, `freesurface` and `d3q19` modules; it
declares `MACH_LIMIT = 0.3` for its low-Mach regime. `fs-couple` is 1,042,487 library lines (1,839,169 with
tests), closure 65, needs `fs-qty` directly, and provides multiphysics composition through typed port relations
with measured energy audits. Together their closure is 66 crates and about 4.1 million lines of library source,
of which 19 crates and about 2.3 million lines lie outside what M1 compiles.

That size is why they sit behind **the gated bridge** (DESIGN): they are reached only through the `multiphysics`
feature of `frob-fsim`, which is off in every default build, is compiled only by the M6 lanes, and exposes two
traits that **exchange whole fields at declared instants**. No kernel of the batched step calls across the
bridge. A result that crossed it is labelled as evaluation tier and cannot enter a throughput claim (invariant
INV-SUITE-05: a result that crossed the multiphysics bridge is never merged into a throughput-tier or same-stack
claim).

Group gate INT-FSIM-17 (first needed at M6; thresholds are TARGETs):

| Known answer through the airlock | Negative control |
|---|---|
| Poiseuille flow between plates matches the parabola to the crate's own stated tolerance; total film mass is conserved to 10⁻¹² relative over 10⁴ steps | The bridge refuses when the feature is off, and refuses a throughput-tier request |

If INT-FSIM-17 fails, the fluid film stays the Solid film-friction law of the contact subsystem and coupled
islands are dropped. That fallback is named in advance and is not a failure of the program.

## Scope

In: feature `multiphysics`; the two traits with their whole-field exchange contract; the tier label and the two
refusals of the bridge; the INT-FSIM-17 test; build isolation of the group; the record.

Out: the in-batch advected film field and its conservative update (the Habitat beads under epic BD-413); the
film-friction law (BD-230); island declaration, scheduling beside a referee world and the proxy-adequacy pilot
(the Habitat beads under epic BD-416); cloth through franken_textiles (gate INT-FTX-01, another unit); dynamic
rods and batched cloth upstream (FD-FSIM-22).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-fsim/src/multiphysics/{fluid,coupled,bridge}.rs`, all behind feature `multiphysics`. A stub
module compiled when the feature is off holds only the refusal path, so that callers link in every build.

```rust
// DESIGN: trait names fix intent, not signatures.
pub struct FieldSnapshot { pub instant: RationalTime, pub grid: GridSpec, pub channels: Vec<Channel>, pub digest: Blake3 }
pub struct BridgeRequest { pub tier: Tier, pub purpose: Purpose /* Oracle | Island */, pub stack_receipt: Blake3 }
pub trait FluidIsland {
    fn open(&self, req: &BridgeRequest, spec: &FluidSpec) -> Result<IslandHandle, BridgeRefusal>;
    fn exchange(&self, h: &IslandHandle, at: RationalTime, boundary: &FieldSnapshot, cx: &Cx)
        -> Result<Evaluation<FieldSnapshot>, BridgeRefusal>;        // advance to `at`, return the whole field
    fn audit(&self, h: &IslandHandle) -> Result<MassEnergyAudit, BridgeRefusal>;
}
pub trait CoupledIsland { /* the same shape for a composed island: ports, declared exchange instants, energy audit */ }
pub struct Evaluation<T>(T);   // a value whose evidence tier is fixed to `evaluation`; no conversion to a throughput tier exists
pub enum BridgeRefusal {
    GroupAbsent,                                   // -> FROB-REF-790 foundation-group-absent
    ThroughputTier { requested: Tier },            // refused by rule: the bridge serves evaluation only
    OutsideRegime { quantity: &'static str, observed: f64, limit: f64 },   // e.g. lattice Mach number above 0.3
    Upstream { upstream_reason: String },
}
```

Rules of the bridge:
1. **Whole fields at declared instants.** `exchange` takes and returns complete snapshots at instants named in
   the island's declaration. There is no per-cell, per-contact or per-tick call; the solver and its working set
   stay on one side.
2. **Evaluation tier, by type.** Everything returned is wrapped in `Evaluation<T>`. The claim-product type of
   `frob-core` has no path from it to a throughput or same-stack claim; an attempt is a compile error where the
   types are visible, and where evidence is merged at run time the claim product refuses the merge with the
   tier-violation refusal the evidence types define. A result produced under another stack receipt is the
   separate case `FROB-REF-890` (`stack-mismatch`).
3. **Refuse a throughput-tier request.** A `BridgeRequest` whose tier is a lockstep tier (`batch-cpu`,
   `gpu-cuda`, `gpu-metal`) is refused before any sibling code runs.
4. **Refuse outside the regime.** The lattice Mach number Ma = √3·u·Δt/Δx is computed from the request and must
   be at most the sibling's limit 0.3; the relaxation time τ = 3·ν·Δt/Δx² + ½ must clear ½ by the sibling's own
   margin. A request outside either is refused with the numbers (stepping a fluid once per 50 Hz control tick
   with u = 1 m/s gives Ma = 3.5 at 1 cm cells: refused).
5. **Convert, never reinterpret; no cache across pins.** A sibling refusal is carried with its reason. Snapshots
   and audits carry the stack receipt.
6. **Build isolation.** Only the M6 lanes enable the feature. `xtask check-deps` fails any default-feature path
   that reaches `fs-lbm` or `fs-couple`, and any crate outside the allow-list (`frob-scene` for living worlds at
   the evaluation tier, `frob-conformance`) that enables the group.

**INT-FSIM-17, the test** (`crates/frob-fsim/tests/admission/int_fsim_17.rs`, lane `--features multiphysics`,
run through the worker process if the cold build of the group does not fit the machine envelope).
1. *Poiseuille flow.* Fluid between two parallel plates a distance H apart, driven by a body force g_x per unit
   mass, with kinematic viscosity ν: the steady profile is u(y) = (g_x/(2·ν))·y·(H − y), with maximum
   g_x·H²/(8·ν) at mid-gap. Fixture in lattice units chosen inside the regime (Ma at most 0.1). Pass: the
   computed profile matches the parabola at every lattice node to the tolerance the crate itself states for this
   scheme and resolution (the stated tolerance is read from the crate and recorded, not invented here), and the
   error falls at the crate's stated order when the resolution doubles.
2. *Film mass.* A film of fixed total volume on a closed domain, advanced 10⁴ steps with the `film` module: the
   total mass (the sum over cells) changes by at most 10⁻¹² relative.
3. *Negative control: feature off.* In a build without `multiphysics`, `open` returns `GroupAbsent`, surfaced as
   `FROB-REF-790`, and the binary contains no symbol from `fs-lbm` (checked from `cargo tree`).
4. *Negative control: throughput tier.* `open` with tier `batch-cpu` returns `ThroughputTier` before any sibling
   call (a call counter on the adapter stays at zero).
5. *Type-level control.* A compile-fail test tries to add an `Evaluation<Metric>` into a throughput-tier claim
   product and must not compile.
6. *Planted adapters.* `planted::strip-evaluation-label` unwraps the result into a plain value;
   `planted::per-tick-coupling` calls `exchange` once per physics tick with single-cell snapshots (it must be
   refused by the instant check: the instants are not in the island's declaration). Both must be caught.

**Record.** `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md` entry for INT-FSIM-17 at the M6 pin: commit, command, output hash, receipt
hash, the Poiseuille error and the crate's stated tolerance with file and line, the mass drift, the two
refusals, and the compile cost of the group (cold and warm check times, peak memory; whether it fit one hour,
16 GiB and 8 cores or ran through the worker). Claim: Known answer; class `Tested`; tier `referee`, labelled
evaluation; determinism D2 on one platform.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Poiseuille flow between plates matches the parabola to the crate's own stated tolerance, which is recorded with its source.
- [ ] Total film mass is conserved to 10⁻¹² relative over 10⁴ steps.
- [ ] The bridge refuses when the feature is off (`FROB-REF-790`), and refuses a throughput-tier request before any sibling call.
- [ ] No default build compiles `fs-lbm` or `fs-couple`: `cargo tree` on the default feature set of every workspace shows neither, and `check-deps` fails a planted manifest that enables the group from a crate off the allow-list.
- [ ] A value that crossed the bridge cannot enter a throughput or same-stack claim: the compile-fail test holds and the run-time merge is refused (INV-SUITE-05).
- [ ] Both planted adapters are caught; a request outside the lattice regime is refused with its Mach number.
- [ ] INT-FSIM-17 has an admission record citing a receipt hash and a row in `registries/gates.toml`; if it fails, the record states the adopted fallback (the Solid film-friction law; coupled islands dropped) and the epics that depended on it are told.

- `film_mass_conserved_1e4_steps` (bound 10⁻¹² relative).
- `feature_off_refuses_790`; `throughput_tier_refused_before_sibling_call` (call counter 0).
- `evaluation_cannot_enter_throughput_claim` (compile-fail); `runtime_merge_refused`.

- `planted_strip_label_caught`; `planted_per_tick_coupling_caught`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/suite_airlock_groups.sh` (owned by N33b-21, a dependency: an M1 bead whose script holds a stage per
group, skipped until the group's bead lands), step `multiphysics`, which this bead enables. In the default build
it runs `feature_off_refuses_790`, checks from `cargo tree` that neither fluid crate is in the build, and checks
that `frob capabilities --json` does not list the group. In the M6 lane build it runs the admission lane under a
timeout and inside the envelope (or through `frob-fsim-worker`), which includes
`throughput_tier_refused_before_sibling_call` (a request with tier `batch-cpu` refused before any sibling call).
The same two refusals through a verb (`frob sim run` on a scene that declares a coupled island exiting 5 with
`FROB-REF-790` in the default build, by the predicate `scene-has-coupled-island` of `sim.run`'s row, and an
evaluation request with `--tier batch-cpu` refused as throughput tier) need the island declarations of N19b-21,
which depends on this bead: its script `island_oracle.sh` asserts them, and this stage does not wait for it. The
stage logs the hashes of the harness receipt and the admission record and writes no gate record. Logged per STD-E2E to
`target/e2e-logs/suite_airlock_groups/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`
(`N33b-16` for this step), `step` (`multiphysics`), `event`, the command, exit code, wall time, evidence block (tier
shown as evaluation), the `refusal` events, metrics `poiseuille_err`,
`poiseuille_tol_stated`, `film_mass_rel_drift` (threshold 10⁻¹²), `default_build_has_fs_lbm` (false),
`cold_check_s`, `peak_rss_gib`, the stack-receipt hash, and on failure the node or step at which the bound was
exceeded; the `summary` names `gates: ["INT-FSIM-17"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-772: Poiseuille flow matches the parabola to the crate's stated tolerance; film mass conserved to 10⁻¹²
relative over 10⁴ steps; negative control: the bridge refuses when the feature is off and refuses a
throughput-tier request. The arithmetic of the naive versions (Ma = 0.35, 3.5 and 6.9 at 10 cm, 1 cm and 5 mm
cells for u = 1 m/s at 50 Hz; τ − ½ = 6 × 10⁻⁶) is used as refusal fixtures. Metamorphic: reversing the body
force reverses the profile; doubling the plate gap at fixed force quadruples the maximum velocity. Determinism:
the same request gives the same snapshot digests twice on one machine.

## Considerations
- The fallback is part of the plan: on failure of this gate the fluid film stays the Solid film-friction law and coupled islands are dropped; the advected-film epic keeps its own in-batch mechanism, which does not need the bridge, and loses only its sibling oracle.
- The group's closure is 19 crates and about 2.3 million lines beyond M1. If the cold check exceeds one hour, 16 GiB or 8 cores it does not get an exception by default: the worker process builds it once per pin and the core speaks the job protocol to it; anything larger is filed as a `[[request]]` row of `registries/compute_envelope.toml` (N44-01's rule for adding a row) and needs the owner's recorded approval before it starts.
- Coupled islands are Moonshot work for finalists on the CPU referee, behind a flag and after M6; at most four Moonshot items are active in any milestone, and nothing depends on this one.
- A single-relaxation lattice scheme is unstable with the relaxation time that close to one half; the regime refusal encodes that, so an agent cannot obtain a meaningless fluid result by asking politely.
- A worker at a different receipt than the job names refuses (`FROB-REF-791`); a result from another stack is never offered as same-stack evidence (`FROB-REF-890`).
- The bridge is a child of the advected-films epic and is built only after that epic's paper-scale pilot (N19b-07,
  a dependency) has a record with a result.
