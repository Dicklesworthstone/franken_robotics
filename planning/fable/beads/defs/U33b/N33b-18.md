## Background

Analysis **A-16, sensitivity** answers: *which parameters matter most for a chosen metric?* Its consumers are
three. `explain` turns it into a ranked list of typed edits predicted to help, printed as the envelope's
`next_actions`. The actuator subsystem uses it to label a verdict **card-conditional**: every tier reads the same
card, so if a card field is wrong the referee and the fast tiers are wrong together, and a verdict that changes
sign inside the card's stated uncertainty band must say so and name the field that decides it. And tolerance
allocation takes its sensitivities of simulated task metrics, class Estimated.

The plan's method statement: automatic differentiation for static metrics; Morris screening and smoothed gradient
estimates for simulated ones. Tier: Solid for the first two, Frontier for smoothed gradients. It is a separate
verb, not part of the preflight, because it costs more and needs a choice of metric from the caller.

Two facts shape the design. First, a **static metric** (a mass, a stability margin, an actuator envelope margin,
a reach) is a composition of the analyses' own arithmetic, so its derivative is exact to rounding with forward
duals; where the metric is defined by a fixed point (the mass spiral of co-design closure, a thermal steady
state), the derivative comes from the implicit-function theorem, dx*/dθ = (I − ∂F/∂x)⁻¹·∂F/∂θ, which is what
FrankenSim's `fs-ad` provides (`ift.rs`; admitted by gate INT-FSIM-10). Second, a **simulated metric** (success
rate, cost of transport over episodes) is not differentiable through contact events: FACT, `fs-ad` offers no
derivative through a contact event, and that is the recorded scope of the admission. For simulated metrics the
Solid tool is a screening design that needs no derivative.

Counterexample kept in view (from tolerance allocation): a zero first-order sensitivity is not insensitivity.
For q(x) = x² at nominal zero the sensitivity is zero while the deviation at tolerance t is t². A ranking by
first-order terms alone would call the parameter irrelevant.

## Scope

In: the parameter set and its scaling; the static mode (forward duals and implicit-function sensitivities); the
simulated mode by Morris elementary effects on paired worlds; the band-extreme check that guards against the
zero-sensitivity trap; the card-conditional label; ranked typed edits; the verb `analyze.sensitivity`; the hook
for the Frontier smoothed-gradient mode.

Out: smoothed and paired finite-difference design gradients themselves (BD-353); tolerance allocation (BD-406);
the explain reports (BD-112); Shapley-style attribution of failures (the Autopsy beads); gradients through
contact (a Frontier epic).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-analyze/src/sensitivity/{params,static_mode,morris,label,report}.rs`.

1. **Parameters.** The caller names a metric and a parameter set: design levers (each with bounds), card fields
   (each with its uncertainty band), or task parameters. Each parameter p_i gets a scale Δ_i: half the lever
   range, or half the card band. All reported sensitivities are **scaled**: S_i = (∂m/∂p_i)·Δ_i, in the metric's
   own unit, so that parameters with different units can be ranked.
2. **Static mode.** The metric function is evaluated on `fs-ad` forward duals, one pass per parameter (or
   vector duals for small sets). Where the analysis solves a fixed point x = F(x, θ), the dual pass is replaced
   by the implicit-function solve through the airlock. Each derivative is checked once per metric kind against
   central differences (`gradcheck`), and the result records the check's relative error. Evidence: the class of
   the underlying analysis; determinism D0.
3. **Band-extreme check.** For every parameter the metric is also evaluated at p_i ± Δ_i. If the larger of the
   two changes exceeds twice abs(S_i) or S_i is zero while a change is nonzero, the parameter is flagged
   `nonlinear-in-band` and ranked by the band-extreme change instead of by S_i.
4. **Simulated mode: Morris elementary effects.** For k parameters scaled to [0, 1], p grid levels (DESIGN
   default p = 4) and jump δ = p/(2·(p − 1)) = 2/3: draw r trajectories (default r = 10), each a random start on
   the grid followed by k one-at-a-time moves of ±δ in random order, r·(k + 1) evaluations in all. The
   elementary effect of parameter i on trajectory t is EE_i,t = [m(x + δ·e_i) − m(x)]/δ. Report
   μ*_i = mean over t of abs(EE_i,t) (importance) and σ_i = the standard deviation of EE_i,t (nonlinearity or
   interaction). Every evaluation of m is a batch of episodes on **the same world addresses**, so the difference
   inside an elementary effect is a paired difference; the study machinery supplies the pairing and an interval
   on each μ*_i from the per-address differences. A parameter whose interval contains zero is listed as
   `not-separated` and is not ranked above a separated one. Evidence: class Estimated; determinism that of the
   tier the episodes ran on; the claim list is empty (a screening result).
5. **The card-conditional label.** For a verdict with signed margin m (positive passes) and each card field in
   its mask: if abs(S_i) ≥ abs(m), or the margin changes sign at a band extreme, the verdict is labelled
   `card-conditional` and names the field with the largest abs(S_i) among those that can flip it, with the value
   at which the sign changes (by bisection on the band). Under `--strict-data` a verdict that rests on a field of
   provenance class `assumed` is refused by the card rule (`FROB-REF-113`); the label is what a caller sees
   without that flag.
6. **Ranked typed edits.** The parameters are ordered by abs(S_i) (or band-extreme change, or μ*_i), sign
   attached, and each lever becomes a typed edit moving it in the helpful direction by a step inside its bounds,
   with the predicted change of the metric and the analyses that must be re-run after it. Locked fields are
   listed and not offered.
7. **Frontier hook.** Mode `smoothed-gradient` is declared in the verb's schema and returns `FROB-REF-724` (not
   implemented in this build) until the paired finite-difference pilot has passed; if its kill gate fires, the
   mode instead returns the finite-difference stencil as a report with the label `finite-difference`.
8. **Verb.** `analyze.sensitivity`: input a design or run, a metric, a parameter set and a mode (`static`,
   `morris`, `smoothed-gradient`). The static mode is a read; the Morris mode submits episodes and may return a
   job handle (exit 8). The analysis is a memo node keyed by the metric, the parameter set, the mode and the
   masked model identity.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Static mode, mass roll-up: the sensitivity of total mass to one link's mass is exactly 1 (scaled: Δ_i); to a link's length under a tube fill it equals the tube's linear density, to 10⁻¹² relative.
- [ ] Static mode, implicit: the loads–mass fixed point m = A + λ·(M₀ + m) with M₀ = 12 kg, A = 0.6 kg, λ = 0.15 (m* = 2.8235 kg) has dm*/dA = 1/(1 − λ) = 1.176471 and dm*/dM₀ = λ/(1 − λ) = 0.176471, and the 2×2 fixed-point case returns (5.785124, 1.652893); all to 10⁻¹².
- [ ] The band-extreme check flags q(x) = x² at nominal zero as `nonlinear-in-band` and ranks it by its band change, not by its zero first-order term.
- [ ] Morris mode on the linear metric m = 3·x₁ − 2·x₂ + 0·x₃ returns μ* = (3, 2, 0) and σ = (0, 0, 0) exactly; on m = x₁ + x₂·x₃ it returns σ₁ = 0 and σ₂, σ₃ > 0.
- [ ] Morris mode on episodes uses identical world addresses for both ends of every elementary effect (asserted from the run manifests), and reports `not-separated` for a planted inert parameter.
- [ ] The card-conditional label: a margin of +0.5 with scaled card sensitivity 0.8 is labelled and names the field and the flip value; with sensitivity 0.1 and no sign change at the band extremes it is not labelled.
- [ ] Ranked edits respect lever bounds and locks; applying the first edit moves the metric in the predicted direction on the fixtures.
- [ ] `analyze.sensitivity` is registered with schema, example and refusals; the `smoothed-gradient` mode returns `FROB-REF-724` in this build; `CONTRACT.md` records classes and determinism.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-analyze sensitivity`:
- `static_mass_wrt_link_mass_is_one`; `static_mass_wrt_tube_length_is_linear_density` (ρ = 2,700 kg/m³, radii 15 and 13 mm: 2700·π·(0.015² − 0.013²) = 0.4750 kg/m).
- `implicit_mass_spiral_derivative` (m* = 2.8235 kg; dm*/dA = 1.176471; dm*/dM₀ = 0.176471); `implicit_two_by_two_fixed_point`.
- `gradcheck_agrees_with_duals` (relative error below 10⁻⁷, recorded).
- `band_extreme_flags_square` (q = x², band ±0.1: first-order 0, band change 0.01, flagged).
- `morris_linear_exact`: μ* = (3, 2, 0), σ = 0, for any seed; `morris_interaction_has_sigma`; `morris_evaluation_count` (r·(k + 1) = 40 for r = 10, k = 3).
- `morris_pairs_world_addresses`; `morris_inert_parameter_not_separated`.
- `label_card_conditional_and_flip_value`: margin m(p) = 0.5 − 1.6·(p − p₀) with half-band 0.5: scaled sensitivity 0.8, flip at p₀ + 0.3125.
- `label_absent_when_band_cannot_flip`.
- `ranked_edits_respect_bounds_and_locks`.
- `smoothed_gradient_mode_returns_724`.
- Planted defects, each caught: `unscaled-ranking` (ranks by raw ∂m/∂p across units, which puts a length in metres above a mass in kilograms on the fixture), `independent-worlds-morris` (fresh world addresses at each end of an elementary effect; the interval on μ* widens and the inert parameter appears separated in a seeded case), `first-order-only` (skips the band-extreme check).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/analyze_sensitivity.sh`: (1) `frob analyze sensitivity quad-a --metric mass.total --mode static
--json`, asserting unit sensitivities to link masses; (2) `--metric stability.margin` with card fields, asserting
the label on a design placed near its margin; (3) `--mode morris --metric eval.success --params
knee.gear,foot.mu,payload.mass --json` on the referee with a small budget, waiting on the job, asserting 40
evaluation batches on shared addresses and a ranked list; (4) `frob explain <run> --json`, asserting that
`next_actions` are the same edits in the same order. Logged per STD-E2E: command, exit code, wall time, evidence
block, metrics `gradcheck_rel_err`, `morris_batches` (expected r·(k + 1)), `shared_address_fraction` (threshold
1.0), `separated_params`, the ranking digest, and on failure the first parameter whose rank or sign differs.

### Known answers and negative controls
The 2×2 fixed-point sensitivity (700/121, 200/121) shared with gate INT-FSIM-10; the mass-spiral derivatives
1/(1 − λ) and λ/(1 − λ); the exact Morris values on linear metrics; the x² counterexample. Negative controls: the three planted
defects. Metamorphic: rescaling a parameter's unit leaves its scaled sensitivity unchanged; adding a constant to
the metric changes no sensitivity; duplicating a parameter (two levers tied to one field) gives equal
sensitivities. Determinism: static mode is identical everywhere; Morris mode is a pure function of its seed and
the world addresses on a D2 tier.

## Considerations

- KILL-FDGRAD (decided at M4, pilot before funding): if the paired finite-difference pilot's falsifier fires (variance ratio below 2 at every horizon of 50 steps or more, or sign agreement below 70%), design gradients fall back to rank-based search and the stencil is kept as a sensitivity report for this analysis. The hook in step 7 is that report's home.
- Sensitivities of simulated metrics are class Estimated, and anything that consumes them (a tolerance allocation) inherits the weaker class.
- Morris cost is r·(k + 1) batches of episodes; at k = 20 and r = 10 that is 210 batches, which is a campaign-sized request on the referee. The verb returns its estimate before running and refuses on budget.
- A ranked edit is a prediction, not a result: the edit's effect is established by running the comparison, and `explain` says so beside the list.
- Sensitivity to a field of class `assumed` is still reported; it is exactly the information that tells an agent which measurement to ask for.
