## Background

The airlock crate `frob-fsim` groups its adapters to FrankenSim into Cargo features: three compiled by default
(`referee`, `geometry`, `ledger`) and six that only the milestone needing them compiles (`optimize`, `stats`,
`spectral`, `actuation`, `structure`, `multiphysics`). Each group is admitted by a group gate: the group compiles
from this repository behind its feature, one known answer passes through the airlock trait, and one negative
control fails. An admission is never inherited: a group first used at M4 is admitted at M4, by its own record, at
the pin of that day.

For a user or an agent the groups surface in exactly one way: a binary either has a capability or it does not,
and when it does not, the verb that needs it must say so by name. That is refusal `FROB-REF-790`,
`foundation-group-absent`: a verb needs an airlock feature group that this binary was not built with. A binary
that silently substituted a fallback, or crashed, would make results from two builds incomparable without anyone
knowing why.

Each group has its own bead with unit tests. This bead is the end-to-end script for the capability "optional
foundation groups": one script, one step per group, each step run against **two real binaries**, one built with
the group and one without. It also carries the dependency-rule step that proves `xtask check-deps` enforces the
group allow-lists.

## Scope
In: `scripts/e2e/suite_airlock_groups.sh` with its steps, the two-binary build matrix, the assertions and the
structured log; the planted failures of the script header.

Out: the adapters and gates themselves; the M0 file-by-file battery (N33b-20); the worker-process posture
(N33b-06).
After integration: the first-use admission records of the M1 groups and oracles and the never-inherited scenarios
are the script of N33a-34 (`airlock_groups.sh`); the M0 refusal scenarios on one group are N33a-29's.

## Technical approach

Plain shell on the STD-E2E helper; idempotent (scratch target directories under the offload base, scratch
stores), seed-explicit, every build and lane under a timeout and inside the machine envelope. Binaries are built
by `cargo build -p frob-cli` with explicit feature lists; the script never edits a manifest.

**Build matrix.** For each group G that exists at the current milestone: binary `with-G` (default groups plus G)
and binary `without-G` (default groups only). Where the cold build of G does not fit the envelope (expected for
`multiphysics`), `with-G` is the worker-process build and the step says so.

**Stages and the beads that enable them.** A stage runs only what its group's bead and this bead's dependencies
deliver; no stage calls a verb of a bead that comes later. The M1 stages are built here. The stages `spectral`,
`stats`, `structure` and `multiphysics` exist from the start, log `skipped: capability absent`, and are enabled
by N33b-11, N33b-13, N33b-14 and N33b-16, which depend on this bead. The check of a group through the verb that
consumes it belongs to that verb's bead and its own script, named in the table. No stage writes a gate record:
each logs the hashes of the harness receipt and the admission record, and the group's bead records its gate.

One later step is added by another bead: `amendments` (N33bx-02, M4) extends `rules` with the passing and
failing cases of the ADR-SUITE-03 rows. The first-use admissions of single crates and modules inside a group
(N33b-22 at M3; N33bx-03 and N33bx-04 at M4; N33bx-05 and N33bx-06 at M5; N33bx-07 at M6) are steps of N33a-34's
`airlock_groups.sh`, not of this script.

For every "without" case the script asserts that `frob capabilities --json` of that binary does not list the
group, and it reads from the registry the verbs whose rows name the group in `needs_groups`, logging their count.
For each call of such a verb it asserts: exactly one JSON envelope; `ok: false`; `refusal.code ==
"FROB-REF-790"`; `refusal.pointer` or message names the group; at least one repair; exit code 5; no artifact
written. For every "with" case it asserts that `frob capabilities --json` lists the group and that the
envelope's evidence carries the stack receipt.

**Cross-binary check.** Results that do not involve the group (the digest of `frob sim run g1 --tier referee
--seconds 2 --key ag-x-<binary> --json`, one key per binary so that neither call returns the other's stored
receipt, and `frob design check` on one archetype) are computed with both binaries and must be byte-identical:
enabling a group changes capabilities, not physics.

**Planted failures (the STD-E2E plant mechanism; no separate self-test script).** The header declares
`plants=silent-fallback,absent-envelope-property`. Under `silent-fallback` the script builds `without-G` with a
test-only build flag that makes one adapter fall back silently when its group is absent, and the step must fail:
with "expected FROB-REF-790, got a result" for a verb whose row names the group, and at M1, where the case is
the anytime comparison of `stats_m1`, with "local reference without its label". Under `absent-envelope-property` the step's checker is fed synthetic
envelopes (labelled `synthetic`), each violating one of the "without" properties above, and must fail on each.
`scripts/e2e/run-all.sh --verify-plants --script suite_airlock_groups` runs both.

**Logging (STD-E2E).** Newline-delimited JSON to `target/e2e-logs/suite_airlock_groups/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`
with a transcript beside it; each line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line and
feature list, exit code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block, measured values with units and thresholds
(`power_sum_rel_max`, `digest_mismatches`, `control_caught_at_hit`, `null_crossings`, `multiplier_err_max`,
`flip_abs_err_max`, `tolerance_rel_err_max`, `film_mass_rel_drift`, `cold_check_s`, `peak_rss_gib`), the digests
compared across binaries, the `refusal` events with code and group, and on failure the first differing value and
artifact paths. The `summary` line counts assertions, lists skipped steps with reasons, and names the gates
touched at this milestone.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At M1 the steps `rules`, `actuation`, `ledger_recompute` and `stats_m1` run and pass; the later steps are logged `skipped: capability absent` and are not counted as passes.
- [ ] For each group that exists, the binary without it does not list the group in `frob capabilities --json`, the airlock's group-absent test passes in that build, and every verb whose registry row names the group in `needs_groups` returns `FROB-REF-790` with exit code 5 naming it (the count of such rows is logged, and where it is zero no verb is called); the binary with it passes the group's known answer and catches its negative control.
- [ ] In the binary without `optimize` the anytime comparison of `stats_m1` completes on the local reference with `local reference in force` in its evidence; the same result without the label fails the step.
- [ ] No stage calls a verb that is not delivered by this bead's dependencies or by the bead that enables the stage, and no stage writes a gate record.
- [ ] Results that do not involve a group are byte-identical between the two binaries.
- [ ] The four planted dependency violations fail with their rule names.
- [ ] Under `run-all.sh --verify-plants` the silent-fallback build and each synthetic envelope that violates one "without" property are caught.
- [ ] At each later milestone the corresponding step is enabled by the group's bead and its gate appears in the summary with measured values; the script itself needs no structural change.
- [ ] Two consecutive runs on an unchanged tree differ only in timestamps and wall times.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
An e2e bead's checks run through the STD-E2E tool, not a second test runner:
- `frob-e2e lint` on the run's log: schema-valid lines; summary counts equal the `assert` events; a `skipped` step
  is never counted as passed.
- Idempotence: two runs in one scratch directory succeed and change no tracked file (the script's last step, from
  `git status --porcelain`).
- Plant `absent-envelope-property`: one synthetic envelope per "without" property (one envelope, `ok: false`,
  code 790, group named, a repair, exit 5, no artifact, group absent from `frob capabilities`), each caught.
- Plant `silent-fallback`: the silent-fallback build caught.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead is the script; commands, assertions and logging as specified above.

### Known answers and negative controls
Per group, from the group's own gate: KA-768 (spectral: both multipliers 1; control: modulus 1.01 unstable);
KA-769 (statistics: Φ(−Δ/√(s₁² + s₂²)) and E[x²] = 1; control: the effective-sample-size refusal); KA-770
(structure: the variance split and the mesh hash; control: the quarantined STEP file); KA-771 (recompute:
incremental equals full on 10⁴ sequences; control: the missing input caught within 64 hits); KA-772
(multiphysics: Poiseuille and film mass; control: feature off and throughput tier both refused). The script's own
negative controls: the planted dependency violations and the silent-fallback build. Determinism lanes: twice on
one machine; the cross-binary equality is itself a check that feature selection does not perturb results.

## Considerations
- The script builds two binaries per group, which is expensive for the large groups. It runs at a group's admission, at every pin move (called by the compatibility train) and at milestone exits. `spectral` adds no crate, so its two builds differ only in the feature flag.
- The registry does not yet state the exit code of the `79x` codes explicitly; the script expects 5 (capability not admitted) and reads the expected value from `registries/refusals.toml`, so it follows the registry if the mapping is fixed differently.
- Cargo unifies features within one build, so "without G" means no crate in that binary's graph requested G. The script builds the two binaries in separate target directories to keep them from sharing a unified feature set.
- The gate register dates INT-FSIM-09 at M0, INT-FSIM-13 and -14 at M3, -16 at M1, -15 at M5 and -17 at M6. The script's step table follows the register for when a step must be green and the seed work items for when its consumer verb exists.
- The `gates` field of this bead names the three gates it measures at M1 (INT-FSIM-09, the M1 part of
  INT-FSIM-14, INT-FSIM-16). The steps for INT-FSIM-13, -15 and -17 exist in the script from the start, log
  `skipped: capability absent`, and are measured when N33b-11 (M3), N33b-14 (M5) and N33b-16 (M6) land; those
  beads carry the gates.
