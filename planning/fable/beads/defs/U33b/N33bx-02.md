## Background

FrankenRobotics consumes FrankenSim through one airlock crate, `frob-fsim`, whose adapters are grouped into
Cargo features (`referee`, `geometry`, `ledger`, `optimize`, `stats`, `spectral`, `actuation`, `structure`,
`multiphysics`, and the test-only `harness`). Each group has a list of FrankenSim crates behind it and a list
of crates above the airlock that may request it. The lists are data in the feature records of
`crates/frob-fsim/contract.toml`, and `xtask check-deps` enforces them: a crate that requests a group it is not
listed for fails the build check (rules L-2 and L-4, invariant INV-SUITE-01). The bead that wrote the lists
says what they are: an initial reading of which subsystem reaches which FrankenSim crate, where "a change is an
ADR".

Later chapters name consumers, and one crate, that the initial reading does not have. None of them asks for
anything the suite chapter forbids; each is a statement elsewhere in the design that a subsystem reaches a
FrankenSim crate "through the airlock". Left as they are, the lists would make `check-deps` fail the first
build of each of these subsystems, and each bead would be tempted to edit the table for itself. This bead is
the one ADR and the one edit. Tier: Solid; it is data and tests, no adapter.

It also records one amendment that was asked for and is **not** made. The trajectory-optimization lane of
`frob-synth` was reported to need the implicit-function module of `fs-adjoint` under `optimize` at M4. The
design places `fs-adjoint` in group `structure`, for structural sensitivities, reached by `frob-foundry`. The
lane does not need it: its gradients with respect to design parameters come from this project's derived
kernels, and the sensitivity of the optimal cost is the envelope derivative dJ*/dθ = ∂L/∂θ at the solution,
one gradient evaluation. If a lane ever needs an implicit-function sensitivity, `fs-ad` is on the
bedrock-direct list and its `ift` module is admitted by INT-FSIM-10. So `fs-adjoint` stays where it is, and
what the lane really lacks is row 1 below.

## Technical approach

**1. The amendments (ADR-SUITE-03, `docs/adr/ADR-SUITE-03-airlock-consumers.md`; DESIGN, each with its reason).**

| # | Feature | Amendment | Why the design needs it | First use | Adapter and admission |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `optimize` | consumer added: `frob-synth` | The trajectory-optimization lane builds its own transcription and solves it with the sequential-quadratic-programming and augmented-Lagrangian solvers of `fs-ascent`, accepting a result only on a recomputed KKT residual | M4 | `Optimizer` (N33a-31, admitted at M3); BD-362 re-runs that admission at its own pin |
| 2 | `optimize` | consumers added: `frob-algebra`, `frob-gait` | Max-plus matrices and the stride period of a gait schedule are taken from `fs-tropical` (`max_cycle_mean`, `critical_circuit`) | M4 | N33bx-03 |
| 3 | `structure` | crate added: `fs-mesh`; consumers unchanged | The independent structural check runs on a body-fitted tetrahedral mesh of the extracted surface, a different discretization from the optimizer's | M4 (the conformity experiment), then M5 | N33bx-04 |
| 4 | `referee` | consumer added: `frob-foundry` | Mass, centre of mass and inertia of checked and exported geometry are computed by `fs-mbd-material` | M5 | N33bx-05 |
| 5 | `stats` | consumer added: `frob-foundry` | Features that fail the band-extreme check of tolerance allocation fall back to worst-case boxes by `fs-uq::product_epistemic` | M5 | N33bx-06 |
| 6 | `tracer` (new; Frontier; off by default) | crate `fs-render` built with its upstream feature `tracer`; consumer `frob-sense`; test consumer `frob-conformance` | Hero renders for people come from FrankenSim's spectral path tracer, offline only | M6 | N33bx-07 |

Not amended, by decision: `structure` gains no consumer `frob-synth` (reason in the Background).

**2. What changes in the records.** For rows 1, 2, 4 and 5 one string is appended to `consumers` of the
feature record. For row 3 `fs-mesh` is appended to `crates` of `[features.structure]`; it is already in the
closure of the default build through `fs-io` and of `structure` through `fs-solid` (FACT, read 2026-11-22 at
FrankenSim head `95a7fba8e8a7`: both manifests list it), so the amendment adds a nameable crate and no compile
cost. Row 6 adds a record:

```toml
[features.tracer]
default = false
tier = "frontier"
crates = ["fs-render"]            # with the sibling feature "tracer"
gate = "first-use admission, M6"  # no row in the gate register; recorded in DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md
consumers = ["frob-sense"]
test_consumers = ["frob-conformance"]
```

and the value `Group::Tracer` in the group registry of `frob-fsim`, with its stub module returning
`GroupAbsent { group }` when the feature is off. `fs-render` stays in the dev-only `harness` group with its
default features; only the new feature requests the sibling's `tracer` feature. The skeleton's M0 statement
"ten groups" becomes eleven in the same change: the test that compares records with the manifest stays an
equality, and the literal count in its acceptance text is the M0 state.

**3. Rules that keep the amendments honest.**
- A consumer requests an optional group through a feature of its own that is off by default (for example
  `frob-algebra` feature `airlock-tropical` enables `frob-fsim/optimize`); no default feature set enables an
  optional group.
- A row is data and may name a crate that does not exist yet; that is already true of the first lists.
- An amendment admits nothing. An adapter body lands only with its admission record, and the never-inherited
  check reports a manifest that enables a group without one.
- Any later change to these lists is another ADR; `check-contracts` fails a record that differs from the
  generated table in `CONTRACT.md`.

**4. Tests added to `check-deps`** (fixture workspaces with real `cargo metadata`): for each row a passing
case with the amendment and a failing case without it; for the decision not made, `frob-synth` requesting
`structure` fails; for row 6, a default feature set of `frob-fsim` that enables `tracer` fails. Each failing case
names the finding that owns it: a consumer off a list is `fsim.group-not-allowed`, an optional group in the
default set is `fsim.default-set` (both N34-03), and a direct edge to `fs-mesh` from outside the airlock is
`fsim.volatile-edge` (BD-003's rule 1, with N33c-29's exceptions, neither of which covers `frob-foundry`). This
bead adds no rule; it adds data and the cases that pin the data. The first rule of section 3 (a consumer reaches
an optional group only through an off-by-default feature of its own) is enforced by N34-03's finding
`fsim.optional-default-on` (a dependency of this bead: in the default-feature resolution, `frob-fsim`'s resolved
features contain a group whose record says off, naming the member and how: a plain declaration listing the
group, or a feature of the member's `default` list that enables it); the test
`consumer_request_is_off_by_default` below stays as the pin of this bead's data, and its failing cases now name
that finding.

Determinism: the checks are pure functions of manifests and records, class D0.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: the real tree passes with six amendments and eleven records. Negative controls: the planted
violations of the unit tests, one per row and one for the decision not made; a record edited by hand so that it
differs from `CONTRACT.md` fails `check-contracts`. Metamorphic: removing one amendment turns exactly the tests
of that row from pass to fail and no other. Lanes: identical verdicts on both platforms.

## Considerations

- Cargo unifies features across a build, so the lists bound who may request a group, which is what bounds the
  closure of each binary; they do not make a group invisible to a crate that did not ask.
- `optimize` adds 12 crates and about 84 thousand lines beyond the M1 closure (FACT of the first group table,
  re-read by the facts script of N33b-09). That is the price `frob-algebra` and `frob-gait` pay for a crate of
  about 700 lines with no dependencies; a sub-feature was rejected because the groups are the unit the
  registry, the refusal and the capability report all speak in, and the saving is small beside what M1 compiles.
- The closure of `fs-render` with `tracer` is not counted here; N33bx-07 records it at admission and decides
  whether hero renders run in process or through the worker.
- If a row's subsystem is cut by its kill gate, the row stays (it costs nothing) and the ADR notes it.
