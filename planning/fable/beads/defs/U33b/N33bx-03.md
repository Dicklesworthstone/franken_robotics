## Background

A periodic gait can be written as a max-plus linear system. In the max-plus algebra a ⊕ b = max(a, b),
a ⊗ b = a + b and ε = −∞ is the zero; a footfall schedule is x(k + 1) = A ⊗ x(k), where x(k) collects the
touchdown times of stride k. For an irreducible A the eigenproblem A ⊗ x = λ ⊗ x has one eigenvalue λ, the
**maximum cycle mean** of A (the largest average weight of a circuit in the precedence graph). λ is the stride
period, and a circuit that attains it, a **critical circuit**, names the constraints that set the pace. This
is construction C-10; the gait atlas (`gait.atlas`, analysis A-04) reports λ, phase offsets and the transient
between gaits.

The design says where the max-plus kernel comes from: the algebra crate `frob-algebra` owns "max-plus
matrices, taken from `fs-tropical` through the airlock", and the airlock's `optimize` feature group lists
`fs-tropical`. The bead that builds the first part of that group calls it one of three thin entries that have
no trait in the group table and are "owned by the beads that admit them". No bead admitted it. The rule that
applies is that **an admission is never inherited**: a crate first used at M4 is admitted at M4, by its own
record, at the pin of that day, in the form of a file-level admission test (it compiles from this repository
behind its feature, one known answer passes through the airlock, one negative control fails). Until that
record exists the atlas has only its own exact routine, and its law test "agrees with `fs-tropical`" cannot run.

FACT (read 2026-11-22 at FrankenSim head `95a7fba8e8a7`; re-read at the pin by this bead's lane):
`frankensim/crates/fs-tropical/src/lib.rs` is 721 lines, layer L1, with no dependencies. `max_cycle_mean(matrix:
&[Vec<f64>]) -> Result<Option<f64>, TropicalError>` is Karp's algorithm in `f64`, with `matrix[u][v]` the weight
of the edge u → v and −∞ for an absent edge; it returns `Ok(None)` when there is no circuit and admits at most
256 nodes (`MAX_TROPICAL_MATRIX_NODES`). `critical_circuit(matrix) -> Result<Option<Vec<usize>>, TropicalError>`
enumerates simple cycles by brute force and admits at most 10 nodes (`MAX_BRUTE_FORCE_NODES`). The rest of the
crate is task-graph timing for scheduling and is not used here.

Two things follow. The sibling's arithmetic is floating point, while the atlas certifies λ in exact rationals,
so the sibling is a second implementation to agree with, not the source of the certified value. And the
sibling's matrix is in edge form (row to column) while a schedule matrix is in successor form (A[i][j] is the
weight from x_j(k) to x_i(k + 1)): the two are transposes. λ does not notice; a critical circuit comes back
reversed if the transpose is forgotten. Tier: Solid. Rejected: using the entry on the strength of the `optimize` group's M1 record (an
inherited admission); linking `fs-tropical` directly from `frob-algebra` because it is small and has no
dependencies (rule L-2: only the airlock names a FrankenSim crate outside the bedrock-direct list, and that
list is closed).

## Technical approach

**1. The entry (DESIGN; module `frob_fsim::optimize::tropical`, feature `optimize`).**

```rust
pub struct MaxPlusMatrix { n: usize, w: Vec<Option<f64>> }   // successor form: w[i·n + j] is the weight from j to i; None is ε
pub struct TropicalLimits { pub max_nodes: usize, pub max_circuit_nodes: usize }   // read from the sibling at the pin
pub fn limits() -> TropicalLimits;                                                  // 256 and 10 on the day of reading
pub fn max_cycle_mean(a: &MaxPlusMatrix) -> Result<Option<f64>, RefereeRefusal>;    // None: no circuit
pub fn critical_circuit(a: &MaxPlusMatrix) -> Result<Option<Vec<usize>>, RefereeRefusal>;  // nodes in successor order
```

**2. Rules.**
1. *One convention at the boundary.* Callers use successor form. The adapter passes the transpose to the
   sibling, so that a returned circuit i₀ → i₁ → … → i₀ reads in the direction of constraint ("the event at
   i₀ constrains the event at i₁"), and returns it unchanged.
2. *Capacities are data.* A matrix above `max_nodes`, or a circuit request above `max_circuit_nodes`, is a
   refusal by name carrying the limit; nothing is truncated and no larger method is substituted in silence.
3. *It converts and never reinterprets.* A sibling error becomes a typed refusal carrying the upstream reason;
   a NaN or +∞ weight is refused, not cleaned. ε is the only non-finite value a caller can express.
4. *The result is a floating-point value of class `Estimated`* (N00-26's creation rule: the entry is neither a
   checker nor the harness; what is `Tested` is the admission lane's record below). A certified stride period is
   the exact routine's.
   The atlas compares the two and reports `cross_check: agree | disagree | absent`; `disagree` withholds the
   schedule block of that entry and names both values.

**3. Admission lane** (`crates/frob-fsim/tests/admission/tropical.rs`, lane `--features optimize`).
1. Facts re-read at the pin and recorded: the two signatures, the edge convention, both node limits, the
   return for an acyclic matrix, and the crate's dependency list (expected empty).
2. *Trot schedule (KA-073).* Flight 1/5 s, double stance 1/20 s, minimum stance 3/10 s give, in successor
   form, A = [[1/2, 1/4], [3/4, 1/2]]. Circuits: the two self-loops with mean 1/2 and the two-cycle with mean
   (1/4 + 3/4)/2 = 1/2. λ = 1/2 exactly (every entry is dyadic, so the floating-point result is exact).
3. *A unique critical circuit.* B = [[3/10, 9/10], [1/2, 1/5]] (in either form: the two-cycle is the same):
   self-loops 0.3 and 0.2, two-cycle (0.9 + 0.5)/2 = 0.7. λ = 0.7 to 10⁻¹²; the critical circuit is {0, 1}.
4. *Orientation.* The graph with edges 0 → 1 of weight 3, 1 → 2 of weight 1, 2 → 0 of weight 2, every reverse
   edge of weight 0 and no self-loops: the three-cycle has mean (3 + 1 + 2)/3 = 2, the two-cycles 1.5, 0.5 and
   1, the reverse three-cycle 0. In successor form this graph is A with A[1][0] = 3, A[2][1] = 1, A[0][2] = 2
   and zeros in the three reverse places. The entry returns λ = 2 and the circuit 0 → 1 → 2 → 0, checked edge by
   edge. The same array read as edge form is the reversed graph, whose critical circuit is 0 → 2 → 1 → 0: that
   is what an adapter without the transpose returns.
5. *No circuit.* One edge 0 → 1 of weight 5: `None`.
6. *Capacity.* A 257-node matrix and an 11-node circuit request are refused with the limit named.
7. *Seeded family.* 1,000 matrices addressed by `(seed, index)`, 2 ≤ n ≤ 8, entries multiples of 1/64 in
   [−4, 4] or ε with probability 1/4: the entry's λ equals an independent exact computation (all simple cycles
   enumerated in rationals in `frob-conformance`) to 10⁻¹² (TARGET), and the returned circuit attains λ.

**4. Record.** `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md` entry `fs-tropical (first use, M4)` under foundation `FD-FSIM`:
`covers.crates = ["fs-tropical"]`, commit, command, output hash, receipt hash, the facts of step 1, measured
values of steps 2 to 7, the controls caught, status. The gate register has no row for it; `frob doctor` lists
it, and it blocks only its consumers. Claim: Known answer; class `Tested`; tier host; determinism D0 for
dyadic inputs, same-platform otherwise.

**5. If admission fails.** The atlas keeps its exact routine as the only path, reports `cross_check: absent`
with the reason, and the defect goes to the upstream register through BD-037's procedure with the failing case
as fixture. No milestone waits.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-fsim --features optimize --test admission tropical`:
- `facts_at_pin` (signatures resolve; limits 256 and 10; empty dependency list).
- `trot_lambda_is_one_half`; `unique_circuit_lambda_0_7`; `orientation_circuit_in_direction_of_constraint`; `acyclic_is_none`.
- `over_capacity_refused_by_name` (257 nodes; 11-node circuit); `nan_weight_refused`; `plus_infinity_refused`.
- `seeded_family_1000`.
- `epsilon_is_absorbing`: a matrix whose only circuit passes through an ε entry has no circuit.
- Property test `lambda_invariant_under_transpose` and `lambda_shifts_with_uniform_offset`: adding c to every finite entry adds c to λ.
`cargo test -p frob-conformance oracle::max_plus`: `exact_enumeration_matches_hand_cases`.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead adds the step `tropical_admission` (due at M4) to `scripts/e2e/airlock_groups.sh`, the script of
N33a-34 that covers every first-use admission and to which later beads add their steps (N33b-21's script is the
two-binary group matrix). The step runs the lane, writes the record under `--record`, then asserts through
`frob doctor --json` that the record is listed with its receipt hash. The four planted adapters below are plants
of the script header, each turning the step red under `run-all.sh --verify-plants`.
Logged with the shared helper: `cmd` events with exit code and wall time; `metric` events `lambda_trot` (0.5),
`lambda_b` (0.7), `lambda_orientation` (2), `worst_error` (threshold 10⁻¹²), `cases` (1,000); one `assert`
event per control with its `caught` flag; on a mismatch the case address `(seed, index)`, the matrix, both
values and the circuit. Before M4 the step logs `skipped: not yet due`. The user-side observation is the
`cross_check` field of `frob --json gait atlas`, asserted in the atlas's own script.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-073 (the trot schedule: λ = 1/2 with x = (0, 1/4) the atlas's eigenvector); the closed form of the atlas's
schedule family, λ = max(τ_g + τ_f, n·(τ_f + τ_Δ)), which gives max(1/2, 2·(1/4)) = 1/2 for the trot. Negative
controls (planted adapters, each must fail a test): λ taken as the largest entry (3/4 on the trot schedule,
0.9 on B); the transpose omitted (the orientation case returns the reversed circuit); an over-capacity matrix
truncated to its first 256 nodes; a NaN replaced by ε. Counterexample kept in view: a schedule whose critical
graph is not strongly connected has a non-unique eigenvector, so agreement on λ says nothing about offsets.
Lanes: identical results twice on one machine, in debug and release, on both platforms.

## Considerations

- Requesting `optimize` costs `frob-algebra` and `frob-gait` the group's closure (12 crates, about 84 thousand
  lines beyond M1) for a crate of 721 lines. The request sits behind a feature of each consumer that is off by
  default, so only binaries that serve the gait verbs pay it.
- The ten-node limit on critical circuits is enough for gait schedules of the eighteen archetypes (at most
  six phase groups); a larger schedule gets λ from the entry and its circuit from the exact routine.
- A pin move invalidates the record; the compatibility train re-runs this lane with the other admissions.
- Agreement of two implementations on λ is evidence against an arithmetic slip in either. It is not a
  certificate; the certified value stays the exact one.
