## Background

Foundry (crate `frob-foundry`, tier Frontier, milestone M5) closes a loop: a link is optimized and checked,
and then its real mass, centre of mass and inertia replace the primitive Region the design started with, by a
typed edit that creates a new design identity. The loads are re-extracted with the new inertia and the loop
runs until it converges. The same numbers are written to `mass.json` in the build packet, where the mass
properties of the exported geometry must agree with the model to 10⁻⁶. The design says where the numbers come
from: "the checked geometry and its material card go through `fs-mbd-material`", FrankenSim's crate that
compiles mass, centre of mass and spatial inertia from geometry volume moments and card density, with sources
retained, and which is "reached through the airlock".

The airlock crate `frob-fsim` lists `fs-mbd-material` in its default-on `referee` group. It was admitted at M1
for one purpose, the conversion of material handles between this project's cards and the sibling's types, and
the group's consumer list did not name `frob-foundry`. ADR-SUITE-03 (N33bx-02) adds the consumer. This bead
builds the surface Foundry needs, the mass properties of a solid given as tetrahedra or as a primitive, and
admits it: a use first made at M5 is admitted at M5, by its own record, at the pin of that day. An admission
is never inherited, and the M1 record says nothing about volume moments.

FACT (read 2026-11-22 at FrankenSim head `95a7fba8e8a7`; re-read at the pin by this bead's lane):
`frankensim/crates/fs-mbd-material` is layer L3, about 26,000 lines of library source, and depends on
`fs-qty`. `src/geometry.rs` has `VolumeMoments { volume_m3, centroid_m, inertia_m5 }` with constructors
`sphere`, `ellipsoid`, `cuboid`, `cylinder`, `hollow_cylinder` and `tetrahedron(vertices_m: [Vec3; 4])`, and
`transformed(body_from_region: Se3)`, which rotates the tensor and moves the centroid and adds no
parallel-axis term to a tensor that is still centroidal. The tetrahedron's volume is the **absolute value** of
the triple product over 6 ("vertex order does not choose mass sign"), its centroid the mean of the vertices,
and its central second moment (V/20)·Σᵢ of the inertia-form of (vᵢ − c). Density is applied inside the crate
when a body is resolved against material bindings (`resolve_body` over `RegionBinding`), not by a public
multiplication. There is no hollow cuboid, capsule or mesh shell. Four optional features exist
(`hydrostatic-equilibrium`, `dissipative-coupling`, `electrothermal-coupling`, `thermoelastic-coupling`); two
of them pull the solver crate and one pulls the lattice-Boltzmann crate.

Two of those facts are hazards. The absolute value means a folded mesh (an element of negative orientation
overlapping its neighbours) gains mass instead of being caught. And the optional features would drag the
multiphysics closure into the default build if the adapter enabled them. Rejected: computing the moments in
`frob-foundry` (a second implementation of what the referee's inertias already come from, and the design names
the sibling); reaching the sibling only through `frob-model` (its inertia functor serves the Regions of a
design, primitives and shells with one material each; a tetrahedral mesh with several materials is not a
Region, and widening the functor would put Foundry's mesh type into the design compiler).

## Technical approach

**1. Trait (DESIGN: intent, not signatures; module `frob_fsim::referee::mass`, feature `referee`).**

```rust
pub trait MassProperties {
    fn of_tetrahedra(&self, mesh: &TetArrays, materials: &[RegionMaterial], body_from_mesh: &Pose)
        -> Result<MassReport, RefereeRefusal>;
    fn of_primitive(&self, solid: &PrimitiveSolid, material: &MaterialRef, body_from_solid: &Pose)
        -> Result<MassReport, RefereeRefusal>;
}
pub struct MassReport {
    pub mass_kg: f64, pub volume_m3: f64,
    pub com_m: [f64; 3],                 // in the body frame
    pub inertia_com_kg_m2: [f64; 6],     // xx, yy, zz, xy, xz, yz about the centre of mass, body axes; products as tensor entries (I_xy = −∫ρ·x·y dV)
    pub per_region: Vec<RegionMass>, pub sources: Vec<SourceRef>,   // card identity, version, density with its provenance
}
```

`TetArrays` is the export of `VolumeMesher`; `PrimitiveSolid` covers exactly the sibling's constructors.

**2. Rules.**
1. *Orientation is checked here.* Before any tetrahedron reaches the sibling the adapter computes its signed
   volume under the mesh's stated orientation and refuses the whole mesh, naming the first element index, if
   one is zero or negative. The sibling's absolute value is never relied on.
2. *Metres, kilograms, body frame.* Inputs are typed quantities; the composite is assembled as Σ of regional
   moments with the parallel-axis terms, and the report is about the composite's centre of mass.
3. *No default material.* A region with no material, or a card without a density, is a refusal that names
   the region; a non-positive density is the sibling's refusal carried verbatim.
4. *Sources are retained.* Every regional result carries its card identity, version and the density's
   provenance; the report's class is the weakest of geometry and card (a floating-point mesh makes it
   `Estimated`; the sibling says analytic integration over floating-point geometry is not certified exact).
5. *No optional sibling feature is enabled* by the `referee` group.
6. *Capacities are data*: a mesh above the configured element count is refused by name.

**3. Admission lane** (`crates/frob-fsim/tests/admission/mass_properties.rs`, default features).
1. Facts re-read at the pin and recorded: the constructor list, the absolute value in `tetrahedron`, the
   behaviour of `transformed`, how density is applied, the feature list and what each pulls.
2. *Right tetrahedron* with vertices (0,0,0), (1,0,0), (0,1,0), (0,0,1) m at 1,000 kg/m³: volume 1/6 m³, mass
   166.6667 kg, centre of mass (1/4, 1/4, 1/4) m, I_xx = I_yy = I_zz = 1000/80 = 12.5 kg·m² and
   I_xy = I_xz = I_yz = 1000/480 = 2.083333 kg·m² about the centre of mass.
3. *Cube of side 0.1 m* as six tetrahedra at 2,700 kg/m³: mass 2.7 kg, centre (0.05, 0.05, 0.05) m, inertia
   m·a²/6 = 0.0045 kg·m² on the diagonal and 0 off it; equal to `of_primitive` for the cuboid to 10⁻¹² relative.
4. *Two materials.* A box 0.2 × 0.1 × 0.1 m, the half x < 0.1 m at 1,000 kg/m³ and the other at 3,000 kg/m³:
   masses 1 kg and 3 kg; centre of mass x = (1 × 0.05 + 3 × 0.15)/4 = 0.125 m; I_xx = 4 × 0.01/6 = 0.0066667
   kg·m²; I_yy = I_zz = (1 × 0.01/6 + 1 × 0.075²) + (3 × 0.01/6 + 3 × 0.025²) = 0.0072917 + 0.006875 =
   0.0141667 kg·m².
5. *Rigid transform.* The cube of case 3 placed by a rotation of 90° about z and a translation (1, 2, 3) m:
   mass unchanged, centre of mass (0.95, 2.05, 3.05) m, inertia about the centre of mass unchanged.
6. *Tube* (hollow cylinder, radii 15 mm and 13 mm, length 0.20 m, 2,700 kg/m³): mass 0.0950 kg, axial inertia
   ½·m·(R² + r²) = 1.87 × 10⁻⁵ kg·m², transverse m/12·(3·(R² + r²) + L²) = 3.26 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m².
7. *Parity with the design's own moments.* For the cube and the tube the report equals the Region moments of
   `frob-model` to 10⁻¹² relative.
8. *Refusals.* The folded pair below; a region without a material; a density of 0.

**4. Record.** `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md` entry `fs-mbd-material: volume moments (first use by Foundry, M5)`
under `FD-FSIM`: `covers.crates = ["fs-mbd-material"]`, commit, command, output hash, receipt hash, the facts,
the measured values of cases 2 to 7, the controls caught, status. No row in the gate register; it is written

element order).

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the tetrahedron (1/6 m³; 1/80 and 1/480 m⁵ per unit density); the cube (m·a²/6); the
two-material box (0.125 m; 0.0141667 kg·m²); the tube (0.0950 kg; 1.87 × 10⁻⁵ and 3.26 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m²).
Negative controls (planted adapters, each must fail a test): `planted::abs-volume` passes the folded pair to
the sibling and reports 250 kg at 1,000 kg/m³ where the signed volume gives 83.33 kg; `planted::mm-as-m`
passes millimetres as metres (the cube's mass becomes 2.7 × 10⁹ kg); `planted::inertia-about-origin` omits
the shift to the centre of mass (case 5 changes under translation); `planted::default-density` fills a
missing material with 1,000 kg/m³; `planted::product-sign` returns +∫ρ·x·y dV for I_xy (case 2 gives −2.0833).
Metamorphic: scaling lengths by s scales mass by s³ and inertia by s⁵; scaling density by k scales both by k;
a rotation R maps the tensor to R·I·Rᵀ. Lanes: twice on one machine, debug and release, both platforms.

## Considerations

- Material uncertainty is retained and not turned into an inertia bound. A consumer that needs a bound
  propagates the card's interval itself and says so; this adapter never widens or narrows a number.
- The sign convention of products of inertia is the commonest silent error in mass-property exchange, which is
  why the report states it in the type and case 2 has non-zero products.
- The loads–mass loop needs the same world addresses and the same element order on every iteration for its
  map to be deterministic; the fixed-order sum here is part of that.
- A pin move invalidates the record; the compatibility train re-runs this lane with the other admissions.
