## Background

FrankenRobotics does not build a photorealistic renderer; that is a stated non-goal. Sensors are ray queries
against the collision field, and the stills an agent looks at are drawn from the same field, so that they show
what physics has. For the one remaining need, a picture of a design or a run good enough to show a person, the
design names the source: **hero renders** come from FrankenSim's spectral path tracer, **offline only**. The
sensing subsystem's bead for this route (N20ap2-01, the option `--quality hero` of `telemetry.stills`, split
from N20a-28 so that hero renders do not depend on the RGB epic) sends a recorded snapshot "through the airlock" when the
workspace is built with a tracer feature, and refuses with "capability not built" otherwise. It is Frontier
work: nothing before M6 needs it, and nothing on a critical path ever does.

The airlock crate `frob-fsim` had no such feature. `fs-render` appears only in its `harness` group, which is
test support and dev-only, and no admission record covers the tracer. ADR-SUITE-03 (N33bx-02) adds an eleventh
feature group, `tracer`, off by default, with `frob-sense` as its consumer. This bead builds the entry and
admits it. An admission is never inherited: a crate feature first used at M6 is admitted at M6, by its own
record, at the pin of that day. Because the route is Frontier, the admission is also its paper-scale pilot:
the wiring in N20ap2-01 is funded only if this passes.

FACT (read 2026-11-22 at FrankenSim head `95a7fba8e8a7`; re-read at the pin by this bead's lane):
`frankensim/crates/fs-render` is layer L5 and describes itself as an unbiased spectral path-tracing core that
is CPU-native, deterministic and replayable, with a Lambertian furnace test and multiple-importance-sampling
weight audits. Its default feature `chart-backends` brings certified sphere tracing and a scalar bounding-volume
hierarchy (dependencies `fs-blake3`, `fs-evidence`, `fs-exec`, `fs-fft`, `fs-geom`, `fs-ivl`, `fs-rep-nurbs`).
The feature `tracer` is tagged Frontier upstream and off by default: "the spectral path tracer v1,
hero-wavelength MIS over the chart backends, EXR out"; it adds `fs-alloc`, `fs-rand`, `fs-img` and
`fs-substrate`. Eight further features build on it and pull heavy crates: `photometry`, `volumes`,
`material-state`, `optical-heating`, `crystal-emission`, `free-surface` (the lattice-Boltzmann crate),
`material-geometry` (the solid-mechanics crate) and `differentiable`. The crate's source is about 398 thousand
lines with every feature.

Rejected: an in-house renderer (the non-goal); using the viewer's rasterizer for hero images (it draws for
inspection, with no light transport); reaching `fs-render` from `frob-sense` directly (rule L-2); enabling the
physically richer sibling features for nicer pictures (each drags a solver closure into a presentation path).

## Scope

In: the trait and adapter; the whole-job rule; the type-level separation from observations; capacities,
cancellation and privacy; the admission lane with analytic fixtures and controls; the measured closure cost;
the record; the pilot's falsifier and the fallback.
Out: the feature record and consumer row (N33bx-02); the quality option of the stills verb, the presentation
artifact with its flags and refusals `FROB-REF-790` and `FROB-REF-442` (N20ap2-01); stills from the collision
field (N20a-33, BD-191); RGB cameras for policies (N20a-28) and their epic (BD-420); any use of a rendered image as a sensor (forbidden).

## Technical approach

**1. Trait (DESIGN: intent, not signatures; module `frob_fsim::tracer`, feature `tracer`).**

```rust
pub trait HeroRender {
    fn limits(&self) -> RenderLimits;      // largest image, samples per pixel and primitive count admitted; data
    fn render(&self, scene: &HeroScene, camera: &HeroCamera, opts: &HeroOptions, cx: &Cx)
        -> Result<HeroImage, RefereeRefusal>;
}
pub struct HeroScene { pub bodies: Vec<(MeshHandle, Pose, SurfaceLook)>, pub lights: Vec<HeroLight>,
                       pub environment: Option<UniformEnvironment> }     // one frozen snapshot; no time, no state handle
pub struct HeroOptions { pub width: u32, pub height: u32, pub samples_per_pixel: u32, pub max_bounces: u32, pub seed: u64 }
pub struct HeroImage { pub exr: Vec<u8>, pub width: u32, pub height: u32, pub identity: RenderIdentity }
```

`RenderIdentity` holds the snapshot hash, camera, options, seed, the sibling revision and the stack-receipt
hash. Camera convention of the airlock (the adapter converts to the sibling's): right-handed, looking down
−z, +x right, +y up; horizontal field of view; row 0 is the top of the image.

**2. Rules.**
1. *Whole jobs.* A snapshot goes in and an image comes out. The tracer calls nothing in this project, and no
   kernel of the batched step, no sensor and no requirement can call it.
2. *Not an observation.* `HeroImage` has no conversion to an observation sample in any crate; a compile-fail
   test holds the line here, and the runtime refusal for a binding attempt is N20ap2-01's.
3. *Only the sibling feature `tracer` is enabled*, never the eight that build on it.
4. *Deterministic.* Equal snapshot, camera, options and seed give equal image bytes; the class observed across
   worker counts and platforms is recorded and is the only determinism claimed.
5. *Capacities are data*; over-limit requests are refused by name. A cancelled `Cx` returns a typed
   cancellation and no image.
6. *Privacy.* An image of a private scene is private; logs and records carry hashes, never pixels.
7. *It converts and never reinterprets*: sibling errors become typed refusals with the upstream reason.

**3. Admission lane and pilot** (`crates/frob-fsim/tests/admission/tracer.rs`, lane `--features tracer`).
1. Facts re-read at the pin and recorded: the feature table of the sibling manifest and what each feature
   pulls; the tracer's entry points, its image format, its bounce-count convention, its camera convention.
2. *Closure cost.* Crates and lines the feature adds beyond the M1 set, cold and warm `cargo check` times and
   peak memory, against the build envelope (one hour, 16 GiB, 8 cores).
3. *Furnace.* A Lambertian sphere of albedo 0.5 at every wavelength inside a uniform environment of radiance
   1, 64 × 64 pixels, 16 samples. A convex Lambertian body under uniform illumination has radiance ρ·L:
   irradiance π·L, radiosity ρ·π·L, radiance ρ·L. Every sphere pixel reads 0.5 and every background pixel 1,
   to 10⁻⁶ relative. With albedo 1 the sphere vanishes: every pixel reads 1.
4. *Bounce series.* A camera inside a closed box whose walls emit radiance 1 and reflect 0.5 diffusely. With
   k reflections allowed the radiance is Σ_{j=0..k} 0.5ʲ = 2·(1 − 0.5^(k+1)): 1.5, 1.75, 1.9375, 1.99609375
   for k = 1, 2, 4, 8, and 2 in the limit. For `max_bounces` = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 the image equals the series at
   k = `max_bounces` + c with one integer offset c in {−1, 0, 1} for all five; c is recorded as the convention.
5. *Camera.* A unit-radiance emissive square [0, 1] × [0, 1] at z = −2 in a black world, horizontal field of
   view 90°, 64 × 64 pixels: at distance 2 the image half-width is 2, so the square covers 0 < x, y < ½ in
   normalized coordinates: 16 × 16 = 256 lit pixels, columns 32 to 47 and rows 16 to 31 (row 0 at the top), all
   others 0. A mirrored or flipped convention lights another quadrant.
6. *Determinism.* The same request twice, and with 1 and 8 workers: byte-identical images, or the observed
   class is recorded and rule 4 is narrowed to it.
7. *Refusals.* A request above `limits()`; a mesh that is not closed (returned with its topology report, not
   healed).

**4. Falsifier of the pilot (TARGET).** The route is dropped if the furnace or the bounce series misses its
value by more than 10⁻³ relative, if the image is not reproducible on one machine, or if the cold check of the
feature exceeds the build envelope in process **and** the worker-process mode cannot serve it. If only the
in-process closure is too large, hero renders are served by the airlock's worker process (a whole-job exchange
suits it) and the feature stays out of every in-process binary.

**5. Record.** `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md` entry `fs-render[tracer] (first use, M6)` under `FD-FSIM`: the crates
the feature adds, commit, command, output hash, receipt hash, the facts, the closure cost, the measured values
of steps 3 to 6, the controls caught, the pilot verdict (`funded: in-process`, `funded: worker` or
`dropped`), status. No row in the gate register; it is written with N33a-07's writer under `--gate INT-FSIM-01`,
FrankenSim's admission gate, with `covers` naming this use (the form N33a-21 and N33a-22 use for a later use of an
admitted foundation); `frob doctor` lists it. Claim: Known answer; class `Tested`;
tier host.

**6. Fallback, named in advance.** No hero renders. Stills from the collision field and the viewer remain,
and `frob telemetry stills` refuses the hero quality option with "capability not built" (exit code 5).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The entry compiles from this repository behind `tracer`; a default build's `cargo tree` shows no crate added by the feature; with the feature on, none of the lattice-Boltzmann, solid-mechanics or thermal crates appears because of it.
- [ ] The facts of step 1 and the closure cost of step 2 are recorded at the pin.
- [ ] Furnace: sphere pixels 0.5 and background pixels 1 to 10⁻⁶ relative; with albedo 1 every pixel is 1.
- [ ] Bounce series: the five images match 2·(1 − 0.5^(k+1)) at one recorded offset c.
- [ ] Camera: exactly the 256 pixels of columns 32 to 47 and rows 16 to 31 are lit.
- [ ] The same request gives byte-identical images twice on one machine; the class across worker counts is recorded.
- [ ] `HeroImage` cannot be converted to an observation (compile-fail test); an over-limit request and an open mesh are refused for the stated reasons; a cancelled context returns no image.
- [ ] Each negative control below is caught and registered; the admission record with the pilot verdict validates and cites a receipt hash; `frob doctor` lists it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-fsim --features tracer --test admission tracer`:
- `facts_at_pin`; `closure_cost_recorded`; `only_tracer_feature_enabled` (the resolved feature set of `fs-render` is `chart-backends` and `tracer`).
- `furnace_albedo_half`; `furnace_albedo_one_vanishes`.
- `bounce_series_one_offset` (1.5, 1.75, 1.9375, 1.99609375 and the k = 16 value 2 − 2⁻¹⁶ at the recorded c).
- `camera_quadrant_256_pixels`.
- `same_request_same_bytes`; `worker_counts_class_recorded`.
- `over_limit_refused_by_name`; `open_mesh_refused_with_report`; `cancelled_returns_no_image`.
- Compile-fail test `hero_image_is_not_an_observation`.
- Property test `furnace_holds_for_seeded_convex_bodies`: 20 seeded convex polyhedra of albedo ρ ∈ {0.2, 0.5, 0.8} read ρ on every body pixel.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead adds the step `tracer_admission` (due at M6) to `scripts/e2e/airlock_groups.sh`, the script of
N33a-34 that covers every first-use admission and to which later beads add their steps. The step builds with
the feature, runs the lane, writes the record under `--record`, asserts through `frob doctor --json` that it is
listed with its receipt hash and pilot verdict, and asserts that `frob capabilities --json` lists `tracer` for
the binary built with it and not for a default binary. The user-side refusal (`frob telemetry stills <run>
--quality hero --json` exiting 5 with `FROB-REF-790` naming `tracer` on a default binary) belongs to the stills
verb's hero option, N20ap2-01, which depends on this bead and asserts it in its own script. The five `planted::`
controls below are plants of the script header, each turning the step red under `run-all.sh --verify-plants`. Logged with the shared helper: `cmd` events with exit
code and wall time; `metric` events `cold_check_s`, `warm_check_s`, `peak_rss_gib`, `crates_added`,
`furnace_sphere`, `furnace_background`, `bounce_offset_c`, `lit_pixels` (256), `image_blake3`; one `assert`
event per refusal and per control with its `caught` flag; on a mismatch the fixture, the first differing pixel
and both values. Image bytes are written under the test's scratch directory and never into the log. Before M6
the step logs `skipped: not yet due`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: ρ·L for a convex Lambertian body in a furnace (0.5 and 1); the geometric series
1.5, 1.75, 1.9375, 1.99609375; the 256-pixel quadrant. Negative controls (planted adapters or manifests, each
must fail a test): `planted::srgb-albedo` passes 0.5 through a display transfer curve (the furnace reads about
0.214); `planted::flip-rows` writes row 0 at the bottom (the lit block moves to rows 32 to 47);
`planted::mirror-x`; `planted::free-surface-feature` enables a sibling feature beyond `tracer` (the
lattice-Boltzmann crate appears in the tree); `planted::partial-on-cancel`. Metamorphic: scaling every
radiance by s scales every pixel by s; doubling the samples changes no pixel of the furnace. Lanes: twice on
one machine, worker counts 1 and 8, debug and release, both platforms, each against the recorded class.

## Considerations

- A hero render shows visual meshes and looks, which may differ from the collision field that physics uses.
  The deviation report of the scene subsystem bounds that difference, and the artifact N20ap2-01 writes says
  which geometry it shows; this adapter carries what it is given.
- The tracer is tagged Frontier upstream and its surface may move. The airlock absorbs that; a pin move
  invalidates the record and the compatibility train re-runs this lane.
- Render time is unbounded in principle. `HeroOptions` is the budget, the job is cancellable, and the stills
  verb hands hero requests to the job queue; none of that is a throughput claim.
- Cost of the feature is paid only by a binary built with it. No default build, no test lane outside this
  one and no tournament binary enables it.
