## Background

A build of FrankenRobotics may contain exactly one asupersync package (asupersync is the suite's structured
concurrency runtime; two versions in one binary would give two incompatible cancellation contexts). The siblings
do not all agree: frankentui pins `=0.5.0` behind an optional feature `asupersync-executor`, franken_threed pins
`=0.5.0`, and frankenjax requires `0.5.0` optionally and uses the third-party `egg` crate, while the consumed
siblings resolve 0.6.2 (FACT, read 2026-11-21). The design consequence: one asupersync version, and 0.5.0 never
enters a graph. FrankenSim's constellation, FrankenTorch and fastmcp_rust are aligned on one published version by
changes in those repositories (FD-ASUP-01, gate INT-ASUP-01); frankentui's executor feature is forbidden; and
frankenjax and franken_threed are never Cargo dependencies. `xtask check-deps` enforces all three.

Several other siblings are named by the design and deliberately kept out of the dependency graph. Each has a
posture: **Pattern** (the design is copied, not the code), **Oracle** (development and test only, never in a
production graph), **Subprocess** (a binary this project invokes; no crate in the build graph), or **not
admitted**. A posture that lives only in prose is forgotten the day someone adds a convenient dependency. This
bead writes the postures as data and makes `check-deps` refuse what they forbid.

## Scope

In: the suite deny rows of `check-deps` and their finding identifiers (`asup.v0_5_0`, `ftui.executor-feature`,
`suite.forbidden-package`, `suite.egg`); the plant of the dev-only edge to `franken-kernel`, which is decided by
`L-4` on the FD-FKER row (BD-003); the posture register `registries/siblings.toml`
(posture columns); their plants and tests. This bead is the only definition of these rows: the 0.5.0 ban, the
frankentui executor feature and the never-dependency siblings were also drafted in the workspace unit's rule
bead (N34-03) and in the gate bead (BD-008), and both now point here.

Out, with the bead that has it: the `check-deps` engine and its layer rules (BD-003); the
one-asupersync-per-binary rule `asup.duplicate`, the airlock feature-group rule, the tier-feature rule and the
resolution with every feature enabled (N34-03); the invariant battery of INV-SUITE-01 and -02 (N33c-29); the
comparison of the resolved asupersync version with the stack lock, and the gate INT-ASUP-01 (BD-008); the
`serde_json` single-version rule (N33c-38); the confinement of `fs-qty` (N33c-03).

## Technical approach

**The register (DESIGN).** `registries/siblings.toml` (schema `frob.siblings/1`) is one file shared with the
suite survey (N33c-07, on which this bead depends): the survey creates it and owns the columns it reads (`path`,
`survey`, `toolchain_file`, the asupersync key, the watched keys). This bead owns the policy columns of every row:
`posture` (a list of the posture text forms of N33a-01, `consume`, `consume-path`, `subprocess`, `lift`,
`oracle`, `pattern`, `build-time`, because a posture belongs to a use and one sibling may be consumed for one
purpose and be an oracle for another; an empty list for a sibling that is not admitted), `cargo` (`allowed`,
`dev-only`, `never`), `allowed_in` (crate names), `forbidden_features`, `reason`, `fallback`. A foundation that
has a row in `franken-stack.lock` keeps its posture there, and the posture-to-graph rules of N33a-01 govern it;
for such a sibling this file's policy columns may stay empty, and where they are filled a disagreement with the
lock is a load error. Rows this bead fills, adding with `survey = false` the four siblings the survey does not
read and a row for franken-kernel (a package of the asupersync repository, `path = "../asupersync"`, which the
survey reads through the asupersync row) (FACT columns were checked in code on 2026-11-21 and are re-read by the
suite survey):

**The deny rows (read by `check-deps` over `cargo metadata` of every workspace: core, both GPU annexes, `apps/`,
`derive/`, `bindings/`, `worker/`).**

1. *No asupersync 0.5.0 anywhere* (finding `asup.v0_5_0`). Any package `asupersync` of version 0.5.0 in any
   resolved graph of any workspace, or in any committed lockfile, fails, naming the path of dependents that
   brought it. It fails even when no binary links the package, and even when it is the only asupersync package
   of the workspace (the ban is on the version, not on a duplicate). DESIGN: the match is on the whole 0.5 line
   (`0.5.*`). The design's wording is "0.5.0", the only 0.5 version any sibling pins; matching the line means a
   patch release of the old line cannot pass the row, and no sibling this project consumes needs one.
2. *The frankentui executor* (finding `ftui.executor-feature`). If `ftui-runtime` (or any other frankentui
   package) is in a graph, its resolved feature set must not contain `asupersync-executor`, in any resolution;
   frankentui crates may be direct dependencies of `frob-tui` only.
3. *Never-dependency siblings* (finding `suite.forbidden-package`). No package whose manifest path lies under
   `../frankenjax`, `../franken_threed`, `../franken_textiles`, `../franken_firmware`, `../franken_networkx`,
   `../franken_markdown`, `../frankenmermaid` or `../franken_surveillance_system` may appear in any resolved
   graph of the workspaces above, at any depth, for any target or feature combination. The check resolves with
   every optional feature group enabled, because a build that never enables a feature resolves cleanly and the
   conflict appears only when the flag is first turned on. One exception, fail-closed (DESIGN, reconciling the
   two drafts of this row): a workspace may carry a package of one of these siblings only if a foundation row
   of `franken-stack.lock` lists that workspace for that sibling and names its admission gate. No such row
   exists initially, so the rule refuses everywhere. Reason: the design names franken_threed and frankenjax in
   two ways (never Cargo dependencies; used behind a feature or in the derivation annex if admitted), and an
   admission must be a recorded row, never a silent edit of this table. The admission-probe workspace under
   `tests/admission/` is not one of the workspaces this check reads, which is why the probe of the register's
   franken_threed row is possible without an exception.
4. *Dev-only siblings* (no row of this table). `franken-kernel` has the FD-FKER row of the stack lock (Pattern;
   Oracle confined to `frob-conformance`), so its edges are decided by BD-003's `L-4` through N33a-01's oracle
   rule: it may appear only as a dev-dependency of `frob-conformance`; a normal edge from any crate, or any edge
   from `frob-core`, fails as `L-4.foundation-confined`. DESIGN: this row was drafted here as a separate finding
   `suite.dev-only`, a second rule for an edge the foundation row already decides; the narrowing that
   FD-FKER-01's case 1 may adopt (one normal edge from `frob-core`) is then a change of the FD-FKER row's uses
   by ADR, in one place. This bead keeps the plant.
5. *Third-party carried by a forbidden sibling* (finding `suite.egg`). The crate `egg` in any graph fails, with
   the hint that it arrives through frankenjax.

Each failure prints the finding identifier, the offending package and one dependency path, in the report format
of the check's other rules (N34-03). `check-deps` itself stays inside the six-subcommand limit of `xtask`: these
are rows of its table, not a new check. A second asupersync package is not a finding of these rows: it is
`asup.duplicate` of N34-03, and a planted second package at 0.5.0 trips both findings, in this one check.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `registries/siblings.toml` validates against its schema and contains one row per sibling of the table above (ten rows: franken_markdown and frankenmermaid share a table line and are two rows) with posture, cargo policy, reason and fallback.
- [ ] Five plants on scratch copies each fail `cargo xtask check-deps` with the named rule: (1) a path dependency that resolves asupersync 0.5.0; (2) `frob-tui` enabling `ftui-runtime/asupersync-executor`; (3) a `frob-design` dependency on `fnx-algorithms`; (4) a normal dependency of `frob-core` on `franken-kernel` (`L-4.foundation-confined`); (5) a `derive/` dependency on `fj-egraph`.
- [ ] The clean tree passes with every optional feature group enabled.
- [ ] A plant behind an optional feature that no default build enables (a feature `cloth-oracle` adding `ftx-drape`) is still caught.
- [ ] A lockfile containing `asupersync 0.5.0` fails with `asup.v0_5_0` even when no binary links it, and a workspace whose only asupersync package is 0.5.0 fails with the same finding.
- [ ] Adding an `f3d-*` package or `frankenjax` to a core crate fails with `suite.forbidden-package`; the same package in a workspace that a stack-lock foundation row lists for that sibling, with an admission gate, passes; no such row exists in the committed stack lock.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`xtask` tests (`cargo test -p xtask check_deps_suite`), each on a scratch copy of the real tree with one planted
manifest edit and real `cargo metadata`:
- `denies_asupersync_0_5_0`, `denies_ftui_executor_feature`, `denies_never_dependency_sibling` (parameterized over the eight directories; the franken_threed case adds an `f3d-*` package and the frankenjax case `fj-egraph`), `denies_franken_kernel_normal_edge` (reported as `L-4.foundation-confined` on the FD-FKER row, and as nothing else), `allows_franken_kernel_dev_edge_in_conformance`, `denies_egg`, `catches_plant_behind_optional_feature`.
- `denies_asupersync_0_5_0_in_lock_unlinked`: a lockfile entry for 0.5.0 that no binary links still fails. `denies_asupersync_0_5_0_when_unique`: a workspace whose only asupersync is 0.5.0 fails (no duplicate is needed).
- `forbidden_package_admitted_only_by_stack_lock_row`: with a scratch stack lock whose foundation row lists the workspace and an admission gate, the package passes in that workspace and still fails in every other.
- `siblings_registry_schema`: a row with an unknown posture string or a missing `reason` is a load error; a franken_networkx row with `posture = []` and `cargo = "never"` loads (not admitted).
- `siblings_policy_agrees_with_stack_lock`: a row whose posture disagrees with the posture of the same foundation's row in `franken-stack.lock` (frankentui `pattern` here, `consume` there) is a load error naming both files.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/suite_deps_rules.sh` (this bead creates it; N33c-29 and N33c-38 add their plants to it): for each
plant, copies the tree, applies the edit, runs `cargo xtask check-deps`, and asserts a non-zero exit and the
rule name in the output; then runs the clean tree with all features. It sources the STD-E2E helper and writes
`target/e2e-logs/suite_deps_rules/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`
(the bead that owns the plant), `step` and `event`. It logs one `cmd` event per invocation (exact command line,
exit code, wall time), one `assert` event per plant (rule expected, rule reported, exit code, the dependency path
the check printed, the BLAKE3 of the planted manifest), and on a missed plant the scratch copy's path, the planted
diff and the full check output's path. The `summary` gives plants caught out of plants planted and the gates
touched (this bead claims none; INT-ASUP-01 of BD-008 cites the clean-tree result). This bead depends on
`check-deps` alone, not on the assembly of the six checks (BD-004): that no other of the six reports these
plants is asserted by N34-14's `scripts/e2e/workspace_checks.sh`, which carries the plants of acceptance items 2,
4, 5 and 6 as rows of its extra-plants table (contributing bead N33c-09, owning check `check-deps`, the finding
identifiers above) and runs them against all six checks.

### Known answers and negative controls

The plants above are the negative controls; a deny rule that no plant can trip validates nothing. Known answer:
on the clean tree `cargo tree -d` lists no duplicate `asupersync`. Counterexample to the shortcut "a passing build
means the pins are compatible": a build that never enables the oracle's feature flag resolves, and the conflict
appears only when the flag is first turned on at the oracle's milestone; hence resolution with every optional
feature group enabled. Lanes: both machines (the Metal annex resolves only on the Mac, the CUDA annex on `trj`).

## Considerations

- RISK-03: two asupersync versions in one build; leading indicator `cargo tree -d` at INT-ASUP-01.
- RISK-19: the viewer drifts outside franken_threed's admitted subset; plain Three.js is the fallback and is
  always built.
- Path-based rule 3 depends on siblings living beside this repository under `~/projects`; a sibling consumed from
  a registry instead would be matched by package name, which the register also lists.
- If frankentui's executor is ever wanted, the upstream work item FD-FTUI-01 (relax the exact 0.5.0 pin) must
  land first; until then the feature is forbidden and nothing here needs it.
