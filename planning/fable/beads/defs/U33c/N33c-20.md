## Background

The memo store evicts with a three-queue S3-FIFO policy written in `frob-verbs` after the design of FrankenSQLite's
pager (`frankensqlite/crates/fsqlite-pager/src/s3_fifo.rs`, 3,058 lines). The sibling's state machine is keyed on
`fsqlite_types::PageNumber` and cannot be instantiated over 32-byte content hashes, so the policy is a Pattern
(the design is copied, not the code) and the sibling module is an Oracle: it is linked into the conformance
harness only, never into a production graph.

A re-implementation of an eviction policy is easy to get subtly wrong: an off-by-one in the promotion threshold
or a ghost queue fed from the wrong queue still "works" and silently loses hit ratio. The admission gate removes
the doubt by comparing decisions, not outcomes.

**INT-FSQL-02** (TARGET). Under a bijection from keys to page numbers, our eviction sequence equals the sibling's
on 10 recorded traces of at least 10⁵ accesses, and on the edit-loop trace the hit ratio is not below LRU's.
The gate register places the gate at M1, where the memo store first serves analyses; the seed bead for memoized
episodes (BD-220, M2) cites it again when episodes enter the store, and by the rule that an admission is never
inherited it is re-run there at the pin of that day.

## Scope

In: the trace corpus; the oracle adapter over the sibling module; the differential comparison; the LRU hit-ratio
comparison; the record. Out: the policy (N33c-18); bytes, cost admission and pins (N33c-19), which are excluded
from the differential comparison on purpose and tested in their own bead.

## Technical approach

1. **Oracle adapter (DESIGN).** In `frob-conformance` (dev and test only): a path dev-dependency on
   `fsqlite-pager` at the pinned frankensqlite revision. The adapter builds the bijection on the fly: the i-th
   distinct key of a trace maps to `PageNumber(i + 1)` (page numbers are positive). It drives the sibling's state
   machine with the mapped access sequence at the same capacity and the same parameters, and records the sequence
   of evicted page numbers, mapped back to keys. The sibling's rollout gate is set to "policy active". Whatever
   configuration surface the sibling exposes (capacity in pages, small share bounds 5% and 30%, adaptation) is
   read at the pin and mirrored into the test's parameter record.
2. **Our side.** The bare state machine of N33c-18 in unit-size mode, with cost admission off and no pins, driven
   by the same trace.
3. **Comparison.** The two eviction logs must be equal as sequences of (access index, key). On the first
   difference the test reports the access index, the two victims, and both state machines' queue summaries
   (sizes of small, main and ghost, the small share) at that index.
4. **The trace corpus: ten traces, each at least 10⁵ accesses, stored under `tests/fixtures/memo-traces/` with a
   manifest (name, source, capacity, accesses, distinct keys, BLAKE3).** Recorded traces come from real verb runs
   with `--memo-trace`; a trace that cannot be recorded yet at M1 is generated, labelled `synthetic`, and replaced
   by a recorded one when its verb exists.
   1. `edit-loop`: `frob design check` after each of at least 2,000 one-parameter edits of the reference
      quadruped, with the analyses' memo nodes (recorded).
   2. `edit-loop-two-designs`: the same, alternating between two designs (recorded).
   3. `scan-once`: one pass of `design check` over 10⁵ generated designs, each key touched once (recorded or
      `synthetic`).
   4. `scan-with-hot-set`: trace 3 interleaved with a 100-key hot set every tenth access (`synthetic`).
   5. `campaign-compare`: a comparison of two design versions over 2,048 worlds reusing the first version's
      episodes (recorded when episodes are memoized; `synthetic` at M1).
   6. `zipf-0.8` and 7. `zipf-1.2`: Zipf-distributed keys over 10⁴ distinct keys (`synthetic`).
   8. `loop-larger-than-cache`: a cyclic sweep over 1.5 × capacity keys (`synthetic`; the classic bad case for
      FIFO-family policies).
   9. `phase-change`: three phases with disjoint hot sets (`synthetic`).
   10. `adversarial-ghost`: keys re-requested exactly at the ghost window's edge (`synthetic`).
   Capacities: each trace is run at capacities of 1%, 10% and 50% of its distinct-key count, so thirty
   comparisons in all; all thirty must agree.
5. **Hit ratio against LRU.** On `edit-loop` (and reported for all ten), the S3-FIFO hit ratio is not below the
   hit ratio of a plain LRU of the same capacity. Both ratios are recorded per capacity.
6. **Record.** Admission record: frankensqlite revision, the receipt hash, the parameter record read from the
   sibling, the thirty comparisons with their result, the hit ratios, which traces were `synthetic`. A row in
   `registries/gates.toml`. No PASS on a dirty frankensqlite tree.
7. **If it fails after one repair cycle.** The fallback comes into force: LRU with admission weighted by
   recompute cost (N33c-19), never an unbounded store; the S3-FIFO module stays in shadow; the gate is recorded
   as failed with the fallback named.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On all ten traces at all three capacities the eviction sequence equals the sibling's, under the bijection.
- [ ] Each trace has at least 10⁵ accesses and a manifest row with its hash and its `recorded` or `synthetic` label; at least `edit-loop` is recorded from a real run.
- [ ] On `edit-loop` the S3-FIFO hit ratio is at least LRU's at each capacity; both numbers are in the record.
- [ ] The oracle dependency is a dev-dependency of `frob-conformance` only; `xtask check-deps` confirms `fsqlite-pager` is in no production graph through this edge.
- [ ] The admission record and the gate row exist and cite the receipt; the negative controls below were caught.
- [ ] Re-running the gate on an unchanged tree reproduces every eviction log hash.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/int_fsql_02.rs`:
- `bijection_is_injective_and_positive`: 10⁵ random keys map to distinct page numbers starting at 1.
- `oracle_adapter_smoke`: a 20-access trace at capacity 4 produces an eviction log from the sibling.
- `differential_small` (inside `cargo test`): the thirteen-access hand trace of N33c-18 and a 10⁴-access Zipf trace agree with the sibling.
- `lru_reference_hand_case`: capacity 2, accesses A, B, A, C, B → LRU evicts B at C and A at B; hit count 1.
- `manifest_matches_files`: every trace file hashes to its manifest row.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/suite_memo_eviction.sh`:
1. Record `edit-loop` live: `frob design check <fixture> --memo-trace <file> --json` in a loop of typed edits,
   through the real binary.
2. Run the thirty differential comparisons.
3. Run the LRU comparison.
4. Run the three policy settings of N33c-19 on the live loop and compare result digests.
The script sources the STD-E2E helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/suite_memo_eviction/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript. Per comparison it logs a `metric` event with `trace`, `capacity`, `accesses`, `evictions_ours`,
`evictions_oracle`, `first_difference` (−1 when equal), `hit_ratio_s3fifo`, `hit_ratio_lru`; `cmd` events carry
each `frob` command with exit code, wall time and the envelope's `evidence` block; on a difference, the access
index, both victims and the paths of both logs. The `summary` names `gates: [INT-FSQL-02]` and counts comparisons
equal out of thirty. Logs carry key hashes only.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the hand trace; the LRU hand case. Negative controls, each a planted mutant of our policy that the
differential test must catch on at least one trace: promotion threshold off by one; main-queue evictions pushed
to the ghost queue; small share fixed at 10% when the sibling adapts; reinsertion unbounded. A control for the
harness itself: comparing the sibling against itself under two different bijections must report equality (the
result may not depend on the mapping). Lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; both machines.

## Considerations

- The gate proves equality with the sibling at one revision. A pin move of frankensqlite re-runs it; if the
  sibling changes its policy, the local copy follows or the difference is recorded as a deliberate fork with its
  own hit-ratio evidence.
- Equality is demanded of the unit-size state machine only. Production behaviour (bytes, cost admission, pins)
  is deliberately different and is covered by its own tests.
- The upstream work item FD-FSQL-01 (make the sibling's state machine generic over its key type, priority P3)
  ends the duplication; its switch-over rule is "consume it; delete the local copy", after this oracle passes
  against the generic version.
- Memo traces of private scenes stay outside the repository; the corpus uses the reference robots only.
