## Background

Consuming FrankenSim by path means compiling its closure. FACT (computed from the manifests under
`frankensim/crates` on 2026-11-21): a path dependency on the multibody crate `fs-mbd` compiles 42 other FrankenSim
crates and about 1.57 million lines of library source, because `fs-contact` depends on `fs-material` (633
thousand lines), `fs-thermal`, `fs-thermochem` and `fs-feec`. The crate set milestone M1 needs (the airlock groups
`referee`, `geometry`, `ledger`) compiles 65 crates and about 2.06 million lines. FrankenSim's lockfile holds 479
packages, of which 262 come from the registry.

Every run in this project obeys the machine envelope unless the owner has approved more in writing before it
starts: no more than 16 GiB of memory, 8 cores or one hour of wall time, builds and tests included. The admission
gate INT-FSIM-01 requires its cold check to fit that envelope and records cold and warm check times, peak memory
and package count. If the cold check exceeds the envelope, the kill gate KILL-FSIM fires and the referee moves
behind a worker process.

**OPEN-SUITE-5.** What is the budget for the cold build of the M1 closure on the build workers? Owner: the
project owner. Decided at INT-FSIM-01; wanted as owner input at M0. Fail-closed default: the machine envelope
(one hour, 16 GiB, 8 cores). Consequence of the default: KILL-FSIM fires if the cold check exceeds the envelope.

It is an owner decision because raising the budget spends shared build capacity, and because the alternative (the
worker fallback) changes how the whole referee tier is built. The register row (N41-OPEN7) names INT-FSIM-01 as
what decides it, and the register's loader sets such a row `decided` only once that gate's record exists (rule
4 of N41-03); BD-006 and BD-007 write that record, so this bead follows both.

## Scope

In: the measurements that inform the decision; the options; the record; the configured limits of build recipes.
Out: INT-FSIM-01 itself (BD-006); the worker fallback (BD-038 and its mechanism beads); the envelope classes
(the performance chapter's decision OPEN-SPARK-2).

## Technical approach

1. **Measurements to bring to the owner (taken from the INT-FSIM-01 record and the toolchain matrix).** For the
   cold `cargo check --all-targets` and for a cold `cargo build --release` of each of: the bedrock-direct list
   alone (its closure: 16 crates, about 161 thousand lines); the M1 closure; the M1 closure plus each optional group
   (`optimize` +12 crates, about 84 thousand lines; `stats` +10, about 113 thousand; `actuation` +2, about 151
   thousand; `structure` +13, about 412 thousand; `multiphysics` +19, about 2.31 million; `spectral` adds
   nothing): wall time at 8 cores, peak resident memory, and package count; the warm check time after a
   one-line change in `frob-fsim`; the same for the `worker/` workspace. Each on both machines through the
   offload shim, each with the receipt hash.
2. **Derived figures.** The headroom of each build against the envelope (time and memory); the frequency at
   which a cold build is needed (every pin move; every toolchain train; a new worker; a cache eviction on the
   build workers) estimated from FrankenSim's churn; the cost of the same builds in mode `remote` (the core
   without the closure, the worker with it).
3. **Options for the owner.**
   - **A (the default).** The envelope: one hour, 16 GiB, 8 cores. A cold build that does not fit fires
     KILL-FSIM; the referee moves to the worker process, whose own cold build must still fit.
   - **B (a named larger budget for cold builds only).** For example two hours, 32 GiB, 16 cores, for the cold
     build of a pinned closure, approved once and recorded as an envelope class for that build recipe; warm
     builds stay inside the default.
   - **C (reduce the closure).** Keep the default and cut what M1 compiles: move a group behind its feature
     earlier, or prioritize the upstream change that removes a heavy edge (for example the dependency of
     `fs-contact` on `fs-material`), as an upstream work item with its own priority.
   - **D (prebuilt artifacts).** Cache the compiled closure per receipt on the build workers so that a cold
     build happens once per pin; the first build per pin still needs A or B.
4. **Recommendation carried to the owner.** Decide from the measured use of the envelope by the cold check of
   the M1 closure (the larger of the time and memory fractions, on the worse machine): at most 50%, take A and
   record the headroom; above 50% and at most 80%, take A and record the revisit trigger of item 5 as armed;
   above 80% and still inside the envelope (headroom below 20%), take A with D; outside the envelope, the choice
   is between B and the worker fallback, and the record prices both.
5. **Record.** The decision record `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-SUITE-5.md` (named by the question, so that the M0 suite
   decisions do not race for numbers in the ADR-SUITE series): the measurement table, the option chosen, the
   limits now configured in the build recipes (`-j`, the timeout, the memory limit), and the trigger for
   revisiting (the closure grows by more than 20% in lines at a pin move, or a cold build comes within 10% of its
   budget). The row OPEN-SUITE-5 of `registries/open.toml` gets `status` (`decided`, or `defaulted` with the
   reason in the record) and `adr = "docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-SUITE-5.md"`. Under option B the approval carries the
   envelope id that a run above one hour cites with `--approved-envelope <id>` (STD-E2E), for example the
   pin-move train of N33bp2-01.
6. **Configured limits.** Build recipes (`scripts/build/*.sh`, among them the worker recipe of N33c-12, and the
   matrix harness of N33c-01) read their limits from one file, `registries/build-budget.toml`, which holds the
   decided values and the record's path; a recipe never hard-codes a larger number.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The measurement table exists for both machines with wall time, peak memory and package count for each build set, each with a receipt hash.
- [ ] The decision record lists options A to D, the recommendation rule and the owner's choice, or "default taken" with the date.
- [ ] `registries/build-budget.toml` holds the decided limits; every build recipe reads them; a recipe run with a limit above the file's value refuses to start.
- [ ] The row OPEN-SUITE-5 of `registries/open.toml` has `status` `decided` (or `defaulted`) and `adr = "docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-SUITE-5.md"`, and the loader of N41-03 accepts it.
- [ ] If the cold check of the M1 closure exceeded the default envelope, the record states whether KILL-FSIM fired or a larger budget was approved before the next run.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Script tests under `scripts/build/tests/`:
- `budget_file_parsed`: limits read as 3,600 s, 16 GiB, 8 cores from the default file.
- `recipe_refuses_above_budget`: invoking a recipe with `-j 16` under the default file exits non-zero before any cargo command.
- `timeout_enforced`: a `synthetic` build step that sleeps past a 5 s test budget is stopped and reported as `timeout`.
- `headroom_arithmetic`: a measured 1,500 s under a 3,600 s budget is headroom 58% (option A); 2,500 s is 31% (A, trigger armed); 3,300 s is 8% (A with D) and triggers the "within 10%" note; a 9 GiB peak under 16 GiB with 1,500 s takes the larger fraction, 56%.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/suite_build_budget.sh` (owned by this bead; header `bead=N33c-34 milestone=M0
labels=suite,workspace plants=over-budget-record,recipe-ignores-budget`), on the STD-E2E helper: runs the script
tests above; runs N33c-01's harness for the M1-closure build sets (resuming finished cells, under the limits of
`registries/build-budget.toml`); computes headroom against that file and logs `metric` events `cold_check_s`,
`peak_rss_bytes`, `packages` and `headroom_fraction` per build set and machine with their thresholds and receipt
hashes, and an `assert` event that no recorded cell exceeded the budget in force; asserts that the open.toml row
points at an existing record once it is decided (before that, logged `not-run` with this bead's key, never as
passed). Under `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE=<plant>` the named negative control below is planted and must be caught. This
bead's script, not a step of BD-031's `suite_toolchain_matrix.sh`, which neither depends on this bead nor is
depended on by it.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the arithmetic cases above. Negative control: a planted measurement record with a cold check of
4,000 s under the default budget must be reported as exceeding the envelope and must trip the KILL-FSIM trigger
note; a recipe that ignores the budget file must be caught by `recipe_refuses_above_budget`. Lanes: both
machines.

## Considerations

- Fail-closed default until decided: the machine envelope.
- RISK-22: the FrankenSim closure makes builds slow and makes the lift expensive; leading indicator, cold check
  time and peak memory at INT-FSIM-01 against the envelope; mitigations, feature groups in the airlock and the
  worker process before any lift.
- Builds run through the build-offload shim, which queues for a worker and never compiles locally; "the build
  workers" of the question are those workers, and the measurement must be taken there, not on an idle
  workstation.
- A disk preflight precedes heavy lanes; a cold build of the closure in two modes and three workspaces is one.
- When an agent stops, its jobs stop: a cold build started under a larger approved budget is still cancelled
  with its agent.
