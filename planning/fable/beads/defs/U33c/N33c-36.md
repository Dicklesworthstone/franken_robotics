## Background

FrankenRobotics writes a wrench as (τ, f), torque first, both in one frame, and moves it between frames with one
of three typed maps so that a twist map can never be applied to a wrench by mistake. FrankenSim's multibody crate
uses a different convention for the external wrench of its rigid-body step, and the two meet at the airlock.

FACT (confirmed by reading on 2026-11-21 at FrankenSim head `483d09c682f2`; re-read at the pin by this bead's
probe): `fs-mbd::Wrench` (`frankensim/crates/fs-mbd/src/lib.rs:1695–1701`) is `force_world` with `torque_body`:
two frames in one value, unlike `fs_ga::Wrench` and unlike this project's convention. The rigid-body type
requires the body origin at the centre of mass (`lib.rs:582–595`) and applies the angular impulse as
Δt·R̄·`torque_body` (`:1913`), R̄ being the step's rotation.

This is a convention hazard, not a defect. Its danger is that nothing faults. The hand case: let the body frame
be rotated by 90° about z, so that body x is world y. A caller holding a body-frame wrench with force (1, 0, 0) N
means a push along world y. Copied unconverted into `force_world` it pushes along world x: the same magnitude,
the same energy, a direction wrong by 90°, and no error anywhere.

DESIGN: `frob-fsim` defines `MixedWrench { force_world, torque_body }` beside the three frame maps. Its only
constructors are from a body wrench at the centre of mass and from a world wrench at the centre of mass. A wrench
at any other reference point is first moved by the pushforward. No other crate names the type. The upstream work
item FD-FSIM-33 (priority P2) documents the mixed frames at the type or adds named constructors, in FrankenSim's repository;
its switch-over rule: replace the local type and re-run this probe.

## Scope

In: the type, its two constructors, the conversion into `fs_mbd::Wrench` inside the airlock, the admission probe
through the airlock, and the unconverted mutant. Out: the three frame maps as public types (BD-047); the
articulated step's external forces beyond a single free body; the upstream change (FD-FSIM-33).

## Technical approach

1. **Type (DESIGN; `frob_fsim::wrench`, crate-private fields, not re-exported from the crate root's prelude).**
   ```rust
   pub struct MixedWrench { force_world: [f64; 3], torque_body: [f64; 3] }   // N, N·m
   impl MixedWrench {
       /// From a wrench expressed in the body frame, reference point at the centre of mass.
       pub fn from_body_at_com(r: &Rotation, torque_body: Vec3, force_body: Vec3) -> Self;
       /// From a wrench expressed in the world frame, reference point at the centre of mass.
       pub fn from_world_at_com(r: &Rotation, torque_world: Vec3, force_world: Vec3) -> Self;
   }
   ```
   with R = `world_from_body` (x_world = R·x_body):
   - `from_body_at_com`: f_world = R·f_body; torque unchanged (τ_body).
   - `from_world_at_com`: τ_body = Rᵀ·τ_world; force unchanged (f_world).
   There is no `new`, no public field, no `From` impl, and no `Default`. The only consumer is the airlock's own
   conversion `fn into_fs(self) -> fs_mbd::Wrench`, private to `frob-fsim`.
2. **Other reference points.** A wrench (τ, f) given at a point offset p from the centre of mass, in a frame b
   with pose g = (R, p) relative to the frame a at the centre of mass, is first moved by the wrench pushforward
   Ad_g⁻ᵀ: (τ, f) ↦ (R·τ + p × R·f, R·f). Only then is a constructor called. A helper
   `from_body_at_point(r, p_body, τ, f)` composes the two steps and is the only other entry.
3. **Confinement.** `xtask check-deps` already confines `fs-mbd` to `frob-fsim`. In addition, `MixedWrench` does
   not appear in `frob-fsim`'s public trait signatures. At M0 the airlock's entry for an external wrench on a
   free body is the crate-private adapter function `apply_external_wrench(body, frame: Body | World, τ, f)`,
   which takes this project's wrench (torque first; the se(3) dual of BD-010) with its frame named, builds the
   `MixedWrench` from the body's current rotation, and is what the probe drives; from M1 the public trait method
   takes the frame-typed wrench of BD-047 and calls the same adapter. The public-API test of the airlock
   (`crates/frob-fsim/tests/public_api.rs`, N33c-29) fails if the type leaks.
4. **The probe (through the airlock, at the pin).** A single free rigid body, no gravity, no contact: mass
   m = 2 kg, body origin at the centre of mass, inertia diag(0.02, 0.03, 0.04) kg·m² in the body frame,
   orientation R = R_z(90°) (R maps body x to world y: R = [[0, −1, 0], [1, 0, 0], [0, 0, 1]]), at rest, one step
   of Δt = 1/1000 s. Four cases, each with exact expected momentum changes (torques are about principal axes and
   the body starts at rest, so R̄·e = R·e for the axis e of the applied torque and the expectation has no
   integrator error):
   | Case | Caller's wrench | Constructor | Expected Δp_world (kg·m/s) | Expected ΔL_world (kg·m²/s) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | A | world force (1, 0, 0) N | `from_world_at_com` | (0.001, 0, 0) | 0 |
   | B | body torque (1, 0, 0) N·m | `from_body_at_com` | 0 | (0, 0.001, 0) |
   | C | body force (1, 0, 0) N | `from_body_at_com` | (0, 0.001, 0) | 0 |
   | D | world torque (1, 0, 0) N·m | `from_world_at_com` | 0 | (0.001, 0, 0) |
   Pass: each component within 10⁻¹² relative to 0.001 (absolute 10⁻¹⁵) of the expectation; the components
   expected to be zero are at most 10⁻¹⁵ in magnitude. Cases A and B are the gate's stated pair (a pure world
   force and a pure body torque); C and D are the crossings a caller is most likely to get wrong.
5. **The mutants.** A test-only constructor `unconverted(torque, force)` that copies its arguments into the two
   fields. Cases C and D run through it must fail: case C then gives Δp = (0.001, 0, 0) instead of (0, 0.001, 0);
   case D gives ΔL = (0, 0.001, 0) instead of (0.001, 0, 0). A second, `transposed`, builds the torque with R in
   place of Rᵀ; case D through it gives τ_body = R·(1, 0, 0) = (0, 1, 0) and ΔL_world = R·(0, 1, 0)·Δt =
   (−0.001, 0, 0) instead of (0.001, 0, 0), and it fails
   `from_world_rotates_torque_only`. Both mutants, and the probe entry that runs the four cases on a free body,
   live in the module `frob_fsim::conformance`, compiled only under `#[cfg(any(test, feature = "conformance"))]`:
   the unit tests reach them under `cfg(test)`, and the conformance driver reaches them through the
   `conformance` feature, which no product binary enables (a `cfg(test)`-only mutant could not be run by another
   crate's driver). The driver is the example target `crates/frob-conformance/examples/foundation_probe.rs`
   (BD-025: there is no `frob-conformance` binary; an example is a thin argument parser over library functions,
   printing one JSON object and exiting non-zero when its assertions fail), whose subcommands are the
   foundation probes of GATE-WS-06 and GATE-WS-09, one per row: the first probe bead to land creates the file
   and its `[[example]]` entry with `required-features = ["gauntlet-mutations"]`, and each adds its own
   subcommand (the pattern of `atelier_harness`). This bead adds `mixed-wrench`, run as `cargo run -p
   frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --example foundation_probe -- mixed-wrench --json` (by
   N33c-40's script); it reaches the four cases and both mutants through `frob-fsim`'s `conformance` feature,
   which the umbrella `gauntlet-mutations` enables (BD-025; the umbrella does not compile under the release
   profile).
6. **Record.** The probe's four results at the pin, with the FrankenSim revision and the receipt hash, go into
   the GATE-WS-06 record. If FrankenSim has changed the type (FD-FSIM-33 landed), the probe is recorded as
   "fixed upstream", the local type is replaced by the named constructors, and the probe is re-run.

Determinism: exact arithmetic of one step; the probe is a known answer of tier G1 on the referee.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-fsim wrench::`:
- `from_body_rotates_force_only`: R = R_z(90°), body wrench (τ = (0, 0, 0), f = (1, 0, 0)) → `force_world` = (0, 1, 0), `torque_body` = 0.
- `from_world_rotates_torque_only`: world torque (1, 0, 0) → `torque_body` = Rᵀ·(1, 0, 0) = (0, −1, 0); force unchanged.
- `constructors_agree_on_same_physical_wrench`: for 10⁴ seeded rotations and wrenches, building from the body form and from the world form of the same physical wrench gives equal fields to 10⁻¹⁵.
- `power_is_frame_invariant`: for seeded twists, f_world·v_world + τ_body·ω_body equals the body-frame pairing τ·ω + f·v to 10⁻¹² relative.
- `pushforward_hand_case` (the check above).
- `probe_case_{a,b,c,d}` (through `RefereeDynamics` on the free body, the wrench applied by `apply_external_wrench`).
- `mutant_fails_c_and_d`; `transposed_fails_d`: ΔL = (−0.001, 0, 0) where (0.001, 0, 0) is expected.
- `compile_fail/mixed_wrench_literal.rs`, `compile_fail/mixed_wrench_default.rs`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/suite_ws06.sh` (N33c-40) runs the four cases and the mutant through the conformance driver
(`cargo run -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --example foundation_probe -- mixed-wrench
--json`) and logs,
per case, a `metric` event with the six measured momentum components, the expected values, the tolerance 10⁻¹²
and the unit, and `assert` events `mutant_fails` and `transposed_fails` with each mutant's observed vectors.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the table of four cases; the hand case (a body-frame force (1, 0, 0) N at R_z(90°) is a push along
world y). Negative controls: the unconverted mutant and the transposed mutant, both registered in the
negative-control registry under GATE-WS-06.
A second control guards the probe's sensitivity: with R = I all four cases pass through the mutant too, so a
probe written at the identity orientation would validate nothing; the test asserts that the probe's orientation
is not the identity. Lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; both machines.

## Considerations

- The expectation for torque cases relies on a principal-axis torque from rest. For a general torque the body's
  rotation during the step enters at second order in Δt and the comparison would need the integrator's own R̄;
  the probe avoids that on purpose.
- FrankenSim's rigid-body type requires the origin at the centre of mass; a model whose link frame is elsewhere
  is shifted by the airlock, and the shift is the pushforward, never a field copy.
- One vector vocabulary across `fs-mbd` and `fs-ga` is a separate upstream work item (the airlock converts until
  it lands); this bead covers only the wrench of the step.
