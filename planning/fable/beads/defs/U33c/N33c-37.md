## Background

The suite's only reinforcement-learning trainer lives in a FrankenSim crate that this project does not link. Its
action sampler has a defect that plumbing tests cannot see. FACT (mechanism confirmed by reading on 2026-11-21 at
FrankenSim head `483d09c682f2`; the fraction below is arithmetic on the two constants in the source and was not
executed): `fs_g1_train::gaussian_action` (`frankensim/crates/fs-g1-train/src/ppo.rs:172–189`) draws each action
channel from `gaussian_sample`, a Box–Muller cosine over a counter advanced by the constant 0x9E3779B97F4A7C15,
with the phase uniform taken from the top 53 bits of the counter times 0x94D049BB133111EB. The phase is therefore
a linear function of the counter modulo 1, and consecutive draws differ in phase by the fixed fraction 0.167245
of a turn, an angle of 0.334490·π. For a uniform angle θ, cos θ and cos(θ + φ) have the same sign with
probability 1 − φ/π, so two adjacent channels agree in sign with probability 0.665510 where independence gives
0.5. Reported counts in 100,000 two-channel calls per seed are 66,552, 66,552 and 66,551, while each channel
alone is positive half the time: the marginals are correct and the joint law is wrong.

Why it matters: a policy-gradient trainer scores sampled actions with a product of marginal densities. With
correlated channels its likelihood ratio is computed for a random variable that was not the one sampled, and
exploration is correlated across joints. This project's rule P-12 therefore requires that the joint law of the
sampled action equal the law the likelihood computation assumes, across the channels of one decision, across
consecutive decisions of one world, and across neighbouring worlds; marginal checks do not establish it.

Interim posture toward the sibling's sampler: not linked; a planted copy is the negative control. The upstream
work item FD-FSIM-32 (priority P2) corrects the sampler in FrankenSim; its switch-over rule: re-run the joint-law
battery against the corrected sampler, and only then may it serve as a comparison baseline. The admission test of
this foundation fact is the joint-law battery itself; the workspace gate GATE-WS-06 requires that the planted
sampler is rejected by rule P-12 (the sampler row of that gate is due at M2).

## Technical approach

1. **The planted sampler (DESIGN; `frob_conformance::plants::linear_phase_sampler`, file
   `crates/frob-conformance/src/plants/linear_phase_sampler.rs`, selectable in conformance builds as
   `@fixture:planted-linear-phase`; this is the only location, and the battery bead N23b-30, which had drafted a
   second copy under `fixtures/cortex/planted_sampler/`, now uses this one).** For a decision with K
   channels, starting counter c₀ (derived from the world address and decision index so that runs are
   reproducible):
   1. For channel j = 0 … K − 1: c_j = c₀ + (j + 1)·A mod 2⁶⁴, with A = 0x9E3779B97F4A7C15.
   2. Phase uniform: u_j = ((c_j · B) mod 2⁶⁴ >> 11) / 2⁵³, with B = 0x94D049BB133111EB.
   3. Radius uniform: v_j, an independent well-mixed uniform in (0, 1] (a Philox draw keyed by the address, the
      decision and the channel), so that the marginal is exactly standard normal and only the joint law is
      defective.
   4. z_j = √(−2·ln v_j) · cos(2π·u_j). The action is mean + σ·z_j per channel.
   The plant declares, in its sampler manifest, the product-Gaussian law that it does not have.
2. **What the plant must exhibit (known answers, from the arithmetic f = (A·B mod 2⁶⁴)/2⁶⁴ = 0.16724512).** The
   sign-agreement probability of channels at lag k is 1 − 2·min(f_k, 1 − f_k) with f_k = frac(k·f):

   | Lag k | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | Agreement | 0.66551 | 0.33102 | 0.00347 | 0.33796 | 0.67245 | 0.99306 |

   At N = 10⁵ decisions the standard deviation of an agreement frequency under independence is ½/√N = 0.00158,
   so lag 1 lies about 105 standard deviations from ½, and lag 3 and lag 6 are almost perfectly anticorrelated
   and correlated. Each channel alone is positive with frequency ½ ± 0.005.
3. **Across decisions and worlds.** The plant's c₀ for decision t of world w is c₀(w, t) = hash(w) + t·K·A, so
   consecutive decisions continue the same linear sequence (channel 0 of decision t + 1 is at lag K from channel
   0 of decision t), which makes the consecutive-decision agreement equal the lag-K value of the table's
   formula: 0.33102 for K = 2, 0.98612 for K = 12 and 0.70022 for K = 29, the three action dimensions of
   GATE-CORTEX-18; adjacent worlds use independent hashes and agree at ½. The fixture documents these so the
   battery's three kinds of statistic each have a known expected value on the plant.
4. **What this bead checks without the battery.** The fixture's tests count sign agreements directly (a loop
   over N = 10⁵ decisions; no statistic of the battery is used): for action dimensions 2, 12 and 29 the
   adjacent-channel count lies outside ½ ± 0.005, at 0.6655 ± 0.005, and it does so on each of 100 seeds
   (seeds 1 to 100, K = 2), so the registered control is known to raise its signal before any gate fires it.
   The rejection by the joint-law battery itself (dimensions 2, 12 and 29, N = 10⁵ decisions and 64 worlds, the
   rejection record naming the pair, the statistic and the observed value) and the refusal of the plant by the
   admission check of `train.start` (the refusal for an action sampler not qualified against its likelihood;
   exercised in process, since the `frob` binary cannot link this crate) are measured by
   N23b-30, which depends on this bead: the battery cannot be accepted without its negative control, so the
   control is finished first. DESIGN, with the rejected alternative: letting this bead wait for the battery
   would make each bead need the other.
5. **Re-read of the sibling (when its checkout is present).** A test reads the two constants from the sibling's
   source text at the pinned revision and compares them with A and B; a difference means the sibling changed
   its sampler: the plant stays as it is (it is a fixed negative control), the fact is recorded as changed, and
   FD-FSIM-32's state is checked. Nothing of the sibling is compiled or linked.
6. **Registration.** One control, one writer: the control record is written by the seeding tool of the
   negative-control registry from the Cortex catalogue's late entry on the joint law (N23-CX, tag `CX-23-L1` in
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/counterexamples.toml` until N38a-40 issues its number), whose `plant` is
   this fixture and whose rule identifiers are rule P-12, GATE-CORTEX-18, GATE-WS-06 and GATE-WS-07. This bead
   supplies the fixture file with its `synthetic` and `planted` header and its recorded hash, and writes no
   control row by hand. The late risk row "a sampler has correct marginals and a wrong joint law" (whose
   planted instance must raise its signal in 100 of 100 runs) names that control. The suite catalogue's entry
   `suite-sampler-marginals` (N33-CX) uses the same fixture for its own rule; whichever of the two is numbered
   later carries `same_as` the earlier (N33-CX's rule).
7. **Re-run of the FACT entry (the receipt that sets `rerun = true`).** N23-CX registers CX-23-L1 with
   `label = "FACT"` (the sibling's sampler at a pinned commit) and `rerun = false`, so the seeding tool writes
   its control as an `obligation`, runnable for no gate, until the case is reproduced here; N33-CX does the same
   for `suite-sampler-marginals`. This bead is the re-run for both:
   1. The G1 entry `cx:CX-23-L1@host` (`tests/g1_known_answers.rs`, from the `fn` item
      `plants::linear_phase_sampler::rerun_here`; gates GATE-CORTEX-18 and GATE-WS-06; milestone M2; it catches
      no control, it measures the plant) reproduces, in one run: the two constants re-read from the sibling's
      source at the pinned revision equal A and B (the entry is `Skipped` with the reason if the checkout is
      absent, and a skip reproduces nothing); f = (A·B mod 2⁶⁴)/2⁶⁴ = 0.16724512 as an exact integer
      comparison; 1 − 2f = 0.665510; and the adjacent-channel agreement for K = 2, N = 10⁵ on seeds 1, 2 and 3
      inside 66,551 ± 500 with each marginal inside 50,000 ± 500. The reported counts 66,552, 66,552 and 66,551
      come from the sibling's own counter seeding, which the plant does not copy; the receipt records the
      frequency band as what is reproduced, never the three counts.
   2. It is run as `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test
      g1_known_answers -- GATE-CORTEX-18` (before N23b-30 lands this selects this entry and N23b-29's; the
      gate's own line is not eligible then, which does not matter here), and the run writes its hash-named
      receipt.
   3. The recording session, which did not implement the plant, sets `rerun = true` and `rerun_receipt =
      "<receipt hash>"` on CX-23-L1 and on `suite-sampler-marginals`, completes each entry's `plant` with the
      code path `frob_conformance::plants::linear_phase_sampler` if it is not already there, changes no other
      field, and re-runs N38b-05's seeding tool (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test seed_controls --
      --ignored seed`), which makes both controls runnable under their existing `NC-` numbers.
   4. A reproduction outside the bands, or constants that differ from A and B, leaves `rerun = false`, is
      reported with the measured values, and FD-FSIM-32's state is checked (item 5); the gates that need the
      control cannot record a pass until the catalogue and this bead agree.

Determinism: exact (counter arithmetic and addressed draws). The plant is labelled `synthetic` and `planted` and
cannot be selected outside conformance builds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The plant's marginal sign frequency per channel is ½ ± 0.005 at N = 10⁵, and its standardized marginal passes the battery's marginal checks (mean 0 ± 0.01, variance 1 ± 0.02).
- [ ] The plant's adjacent-channel sign agreement is 0.6655 ± 0.005 at N = 10⁵ for dimensions 2, 12 and 29; lags 2, 3 and 6 match the table within 0.005.
- [ ] The consecutive-decision agreement of channel 0 is within 0.005 of 0.33102, 0.98612 and 0.70022 for dimensions 2, 12 and 29, and the adjacent-world agreement is ½ ± 0.005.
- [ ] The direct count of adjacent-channel agreements lies outside ½ ± 0.005 on 100 of 100 seeds (K = 2, N = 10⁵) and the test names the adjacent pair. (The battery's rejection of the plant, the `train.start` refusal and the 100-seed battery row are acceptance lines of N23b-30 and N41-38.)
- [ ] The plant is absent from every binary: it lives in `frob-conformance`, which no workspace manifest depends on (`cargo tree` of `frob` shows no path to it), and a search of the workspace finds the two constants A and B together in this one file only.
- [ ] Exactly one control of the negative-control registry has this fixture as its plant: the one seeded from the Cortex catalogue's late entry (N23-CX, CX-23-L1), under rule P-12, GATE-CORTEX-18, GATE-WS-06 and GATE-WS-07; the fixture's header carries the revision of the sibling whose constants were re-read; N23b-30 completes row 6 of GATE-WS-06 (due M2) from it. (N33-CX's `suite-sampler-marginals` seeds its own control from the same fixture against its own rule, as N33-CX states.)
- [ ] CX-23-L1 and `suite-sampler-marginals` carry `rerun = true` with `rerun_receipt` equal to the hash of a receipt on file in which the entry `cx:CX-23-L1@host` passed (constants equal at the pinned revision, f exact, the three adjacent counts and the marginals inside their bands); after the re-seeding both controls are runnable under unchanged `NC-` numbers, and a second seeding run changes no byte.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance plants::linear_phase_sampler`:
- `phase_step_fraction`: (A·B mod 2⁶⁴)/2⁶⁴ equals 0.16724512 to eight decimals (integer arithmetic; exact value compared as a 64-bit integer).
- `marginal_sign_half`: K = 2, N = 10⁵; each channel positive in 50,000 ± 500 draws.
- `adjacent_agreement_0_6655`: K = 2, N = 10⁵; count in 66,551 ± 500 (the reported counts are 66,552, 66,552 and 66,551 for three seeds).
- `lag_table`: K = 12, N = 10⁵; lags 1 to 6 within 0.005 of the table.
- `consecutive_decisions_follow_lag_K`: K = 2, 12 and 29, N = 10⁵; channel 0 at decisions t and t + 1 agrees in sign with frequency within 0.005 of 0.33102, 0.98612 and 0.70022.
- `adjacent_worlds_independent`: agreement ½ ± 0.005.
- `direct_counts_outside_band_dims_2_12_29`: for K = 2, 12 and 29 the adjacent-channel agreement, counted in the test itself, is 0.6655 ± 0.005 (the battery's own rejection test is `planted_rejected_all_dims` of N23b-30).
- `direct_count_fires_100_seeds`: seeds 1 to 100, K = 2, N = 10⁵; every count lies in 66,551 ± 500.
- `single_location`: the constant pair A, B occurs in one source file of the workspace.
- `constants_match_sibling_source` (skipped with a logged reason if the sibling is absent).
- `rerun_here_reproduces_fact`: the function behind `cx:CX-23-L1@host`, run on the fixture with a scratch copy of the sibling's source lines: passes with the real constants; with B altered by one bit in the scratch copy it fails naming the constant; with the copy absent it returns `Skipped`, never `Pass`.
- `rerun_receipt_shows_entry_passed` (the real catalogue): when CX-23-L1 or `suite-sampler-marginals` carries `rerun = true`, its `rerun_receipt` resolves to a receipt on file whose BLAKE3 matches and in which `cx:CX-23-L1@host` is `Pass`; a scratch copy whose receipt shows the entry `Skipped` fails the test.

### End-to-end script and logging

The plant has no surface of its own in the `frob` binary; it is reached from the user's side in two scripts
that other beads own, and this bead supplies the fixture both resolve. `scripts/e2e/sampler_joint_law.sh`
(N23b-30) runs the battery on the plant and on the qualified sampler and reads, from the gate run's receipt,
the admission refusal of the plant (the refusal an unqualified sampler meets in `train.start`). `scripts/e2e/suite_ws06.sh` (N33c-40) has the M2 mode
`--sampler-row`, run by N23b-30 to complete row 6: the same battery commands and refusal, logging `metric`
events per dimension with every pair's agreement frequency, the threshold ½ ± 0.005, the number of pairs tested
(so the family-wise rate is stated), the planted value 0.6655 ± 0.005, and a `refusal` event for the admission check;
its `summary` names `gates: [GATE-WS-06 (sampler row), GATE-CORTEX-18]`. This bead's own contribution to the
default mode of `suite_ws06.sh` is one step, `cargo test -p frob-conformance plants::linear_phase_sampler`,
followed by the re-run of item 7 (the tier command of item 7.2), logged with the receipt hash, the entry's
outcome and measured values, and an `assert` event that the catalogue's two `rerun_receipt` fields equal that
hash once the recording session has set them. At
M0, before this M2 bead lands, the step is logged `not-run: N33c-37` and row 6 stays `due M2`. Once the bead has
landed the step runs and is logged with the command, exit code, wall time and `metric` events for the phase
step, the marginal sign frequency and the adjacent-channel count on three seeds. The gate record attaches these
to row 6 as preliminary evidence only; the row is completed by the battery (N23b-30), never by these unit tests
(N33c-40's `row6_cannot_pass_without_battery`).

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: f = 0.16724512; agreement 0.66551 at lag 1 (reported counts 66,552, 66,552, 66,551 of 100,000);
the lag table. This bead is itself a negative control; its own control is the reverse case: the qualified
sampler (independent addressed draws per channel) must pass with every agreement inside ½ ± 0.005, so that the
battery is shown to separate the two. A second control: the same plant with A replaced by a constant whose
product with B has fraction near ½ per step would show adjacent agreement near 0; the test computes the expected
value from the formula and checks it, proving the fixture's arithmetic and not just one number. Lanes: twice on
one machine; both machines; `f64` uniforms only (the plant is host code).

## Considerations

- The defect is easy to write twice: any sampler that takes several uniforms from one linear congruence has a
  lattice structure across channels. Rule P-12 exists so that this project's own device sampler is tested the
  same way.
- The trainer's three other reported defects (the clip branch for negative advantage, the critic gradient from
  the stored value, two reset episodes forwarded as one sequence) are separate facts with their own probes; the
  count in the plan is five defects or limits plus one convention hazard, of which this is the fifth defect.
- The plant must never be reachable from a training run that is not a conformance test; the refusal in
  `train.start` is the second fence after the feature gate.
