## Background

FrankenSim ships catalog builders for reference robots; the airlock uses the G1 humanoid builder for the first
referee rollouts and digest tests. A late reading found that the builder is a reduced model.

FACT (the omission is stated in the builder's own list, read on 2026-11-21 at FrankenSim head `483d09c682f2`,
`frankensim/crates/fs-mbd/src/robot_models.rs:398–403`): the G1 catalog builder keeps 30 links and omits fixed
attachments with nonzero mass (fixed rubber hands, head, pelvis contour, logo, waist supports and sensor
attachments), and states that "their nonzero masses are not lumped into retained links". The 30 retained masses
sum to 31.96314202 kg (computed from the source table). Reported and not re-read from the primary source: four
welded links (the head, the logo and the two hands) add 1.377 kg, for 33.34014202 kg over 38 links.

Why it matters: a mass that is silently missing changes every load-bearing verdict. At 9.81 m/s² the reduced
model weighs 313.5584 N and the source assembly 327.0668 N. A static support rated at 320 N passes on the
reduction and fails on the robot.

Admission test: the mass roll-up through the airlock equals 31.96314202 kg, and the reduction lists its
omissions. Interim posture: the builder is used as the reduced profile `g1-reduced-30`, never as the import-parity
reference; import parity of the humanoid archetype is judged against the 38-link source. Upstream work item
(added to the plan's register by description; numbered `FD-FSIM-34` by N33c-47 and carried by NF-07): fold or
declare the omitted masses of the G1 catalog. This is row 9 of the
foundation-fact table and one of the six probes of the workspace gate GATE-WS-09.

## Scope

In: the roll-up probe through the airlock; the omission list attached to the airlock's G1 model as an M0
annotation; the 320 N fixture as a demonstration; the record of the probe for GATE-WS-09 (this bead is the one
holder of that admission record). Out, with the bead that has each: the `reduced` block of a design as a
schema, the refusal of mass dropped at import, the profile file `assets/profiles/g1-reduced-30.toml` and the
reduced-model clauses of GATE-SKEL-11 at M1 (N14b-34, which depends on this bead); the asset directory
`assets/robots/fsim-g1/` with its manifest and validation, which cites this bead's record (ROBOT-fsim-g1); the
general mass roll-up analysis and the fold of fixed joints (the design chapter's beads); importing the 38-link
source (the import beads); the upstream change (NF-07).

## Technical approach

1. **Probe.** Through `frob-fsim`'s referee trait, build the catalog G1 model at the pin and read the per-link
   masses the builder produced. Compute the total in two ways: a compensated floating-point sum in `f64`, and an
   exact sum of the decimal strings of the masses as rationals (the masses are read back as shortest
   round-tripping decimals). Expected: 30 links; total 31.96314202 kg. Pass: the rational sum equals
   31.96314202 exactly if the builder's table is given to that precision, and the `f64` sum is within 5·10⁻⁹ kg
   (half a unit of the last printed digit).
2. **The reduction record.** The airlock's G1 model carries `reduced { profile: "g1-reduced-30", omitted:
   [ {id: "head", mass: unknown}, {id: "logo", …}, {id: "left hand", …}, {id: "right hand", …},
   {id: "pelvis contour", …}, {id: "waist supports", …}, {id: "sensor attachments", …} ],
   omitted_mass: unknown | value, builder: "fs-mbd robot_models at <revision>" }`. The names are taken from the
   builder's own list at the pin (re-read, not from this bead). `omitted_mass` is `unknown` until the primary
   source is re-read; the reported 1.377 kg for the four welded links is recorded as `reported`, with its
   provenance, and is never used in a computation that a gate cites.
   DESIGN (one record, stated with N14b-34): the field names `profile`, `omitted` (entries with `id` and
   `mass`) and `omitted_mass` are those of the design block that N14b-34 defines at M1, so that the M0
   annotation and the M1 block are one record at two stages and not two schemas. Two differences are stated:
   at M0 the masses are `unknown`, because the 38-link source is not on file until the reference asset exists
   (at M1 they are exact and `omitted_mass` is their sum, 1.377 kg); and the block's `source` field, the hash
   of the full source document, is absent at M0 for the same reason, which is why the builder's revision is
   carried under its own name `builder` (an earlier draft called it `source`, with another meaning than the
   block's field, and listed bare names).
3. **Every result cites the reduction.** A rollout, digest or analysis on this model prints the profile name
   `g1-reduced-30` and the reduction record in its evidence block, as `evidence.reduced = { profile,
   omitted_mass }` (the field N14b-34 requires of every mass-dependent envelope at M1). The model's record lists
   its admitted uses, `admitted_uses = ["smoke", "digest", "referee-rollout"]`: the reduced profile is admitted
   for dynamics smoke tests, digests and referee rollouts, and never for import parity or a load verdict on the
   real assembly. At M0 the airlock enforces this at its own boundary: asking it for the catalog G1 with the use
   `import-parity-reference` or `load-verdict` returns a typed error naming the profile and the admitted uses.
   The user-facing refusal of an import-parity request (the code N14b-34 assigns) and the import-parity rule
   itself are N14b-34's at M1.
4. **Demonstration fixture.** A static support check with a rating of 320 N: on `g1-reduced-30` the weight is
   31.96314202 × 9.81 = 313.5584 N (passes); on a `synthetic` stand-in for the 38-link source assembly, built as
   the 30 retained links plus the four reported welded masses (34 bodies; the description's other links carry no
   reported mass and are not modelled), total 33.34014202 kg and labelled as reported, the weight is 327.0668 N
   (fails).
   This shows the decision reversing, and is the reason the profile carries its label.
5. **Record.** The probe's result at the pin (link count, total, the omission list as read) with the FrankenSim
   revision and receipt hash, for the GATE-WS-09 record. If the builder has changed (masses folded or declared
   upstream), the row is recorded `fixed upstream at <revision>` and the profile is reviewed.
6. **Re-run receipt of the Suite catalogue's FACT entry `suite-reduced-catalog`** (N33-CX, a dependency; entry in
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/counterexamples.toml`). N33-CX labels the entry FACT (the 30 links summing
   to 31.96314202 kg, computed from the sibling's table and not run here) with `rerun = false`, so the seeding
   tool writes its control as an `obligation`, runnable for no gate, and names this bead's probe record for
   GATE-WS-09 at the pin as the re-run. The receipt hash that the record of item 5 carries is that re-run
   receipt:
   1. When the record reproduces the number (30 links; rational sum exactly 31.96314202 kg) and the entry carries
      `rerun = false`, the recording session (which did not implement the probe) sets `rerun = true` and
      `rerun_receipt = "<receipt hash>"` on that entry, changes no other field, and re-runs N38b-05's seeding tool
      (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test seed_controls -- --ignored seed`), which makes the control runnable
      under its existing `NC-` number.
   2. A reproduction that measures another count or total leaves `rerun = false`, flags the fact `changed` in the
      record with the measured values, and the entry's case is revised by its catalogue owner (N33-CX).

Determinism: exact (a sum). Evidence: a known answer of tier G1 on the host.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Through the airlock the catalog G1 has 30 links and a total mass of 31.96314202 kg (rational sum exact; `f64` sum within 5·10⁻⁹ kg), at the pinned commit.
- [ ] The model carries the reduction record with the omitted attachments as listed by the builder at the pin.
- [ ] A rollout on the model prints `g1-reduced-30` and the reduction in its evidence block.
- [ ] The 320 N fixture passes on the reduction (313.5584 N) and fails on the source-assembly stand-in (327.0668 N).
- [ ] Asking the airlock for the catalog G1 as an import-parity reference or for a load verdict returns the typed error naming `g1-reduced-30` and its admitted uses; it is never silently accepted.
- [ ] The probe's result (link count, total, the omission list as read) is written with the FrankenSim revision and the receipt hash as this bead's admission record, in the form N33c-48's GATE-WS-09 record cites (the gate row is N33c-48's, which depends on this bead); `cargo run -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --example foundation_probe -- g1-mass --json` prints it.
- [ ] When the record reproduces 30 links and 31.96314202 kg, `suite-reduced-catalog` carries `rerun = true` with `rerun_receipt` equal to the record's receipt hash and no other field changed, and its control is runnable after the seeding tool's re-run; a record with another total leaves the entry byte-identical and flags the fact `changed`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-fsim catalog_g1`:
- `g1_catalog_has_30_links`.
- `g1_catalog_mass_rollup`: 31.96314202 kg, both sums.
- `g1_reduction_lists_omissions`: the record is non-empty and equals the builder's list read at the pin.
- `weight_fixture_reverses`: 313.5584 N < 320 N < 327.0668 N, each to 10⁻⁴ N.
- `import_parity_request_refused_for_reduced_profile`: the uses `import-parity-reference` and `load-verdict` return the typed error; `smoke`, `digest` and `referee-rollout` succeed.
- `evidence_block_cites_reduction`.
- `reduced_catalog_rerun_sets_flag_on_scratch_catalogue` (in `frob-conformance`): a scratch `counterexamples.toml` with a `synthetic` FACT entry `suite-reduced-catalog` (`rerun = false`) and a record of 30 links and 31.96314202 kg: the entry gains `rerun = true` and `rerun_receipt` equal to the record's receipt hash and no other field changes; a record of 31.5 kg leaves the entry byte-identical; a second seeding run changes no byte.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/suite_ws09.sh` (N33c-48): `cargo run -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --example
foundation_probe -- g1-mass --json` (there is no `frob-conformance` binary, BD-025; this bead adds the subcommand
`g1-mass` to the example declared in N33c-36, creating the file if it lands first, a thin parser over the probe
functions of `frob-fsim` that prints one JSON object and exits non-zero when its assertions fail) and `frob sim
run g1 --tier referee --seconds 0.1 --key ws09-g1-<run> --json` (a run verb: the key is required, and `<run>` is
the script's run identifier so that a repeated run measures again); logs a `metric` event `g1_total_mass_kg` (expected 31.96314202, tolerance
5·10⁻⁹), `g1_links` (expected 30), and an `assert` event that the envelope's evidence block names the reduced
profile.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 31.96314202 kg over 30 links; 313.5584 N and 327.0668 N at 9.81 m/s². Negative controls: a
roll-up that lumps a `synthetic` 0.5 kg attachment into a retained link must change the total and fail the probe
(showing the probe would notice a silent fold); a model with the reduction record stripped must fail
`evidence_block_cites_reduction`; the 320 N fixture evaluated without the profile label must be rejected by the
verdict's own check. Lanes: twice on one machine; both machines; debug and release.

## Considerations

- The reported 38-link figures come from a description of the upstream robot and must be re-read from the
  primary source before any gate cites them; until then only the 30-link sum, which was computed from the
  sibling's own table, is a known answer.
- An import that drops a body of nonzero mass without declaring a reduction is refused by the importer; the
  catalog builder declares its omission in a comment, which the airlock turns into data.
- The digest test of the airlock (equality with the digest FrankenSim computes for itself) is unaffected: both
  sides use the same reduced model.
