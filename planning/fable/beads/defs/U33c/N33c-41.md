## Background

FrankenSim ships catalog builders for reference robots; the airlock uses the G1 humanoid builder for the first
referee rollouts and digest tests. A late reading found that the builder is a reduced model.

FACT (the omission is stated in the builder's own list, read on 2026-11-21 at FrankenSim head `483d09c682f2`,
`frankensim/crates/fs-mbd/src/robot_models.rs:398–403`): the G1 catalog builder keeps 30 links and omits fixed
attachments with nonzero mass (fixed rubber hands, head, pelvis contour, logo, waist supports and sensor
attachments), and states that "their nonzero masses are not lumped into retained links". The 30 retained masses
sum to 31.96314202 kg (computed from the source table). Reported and not re-read from the primary source: four
welded links (the head, the logo and the two hands) add 1.377 kg, for 33.34014202 kg over 38 links.

Why it matters: a mass that is silently missing changes every load-bearing verdict. At 9.81 m/s² the reduced
model weighs 313.5584 N and the source assembly 327.0668 N. A static support rated at 320 N passes on the
reduction and fails on the robot.

Admission test: the mass roll-up through the airlock equals 31.96314202 kg, and the reduction lists its
omissions. Interim posture: the builder is used as the reduced profile `g1-reduced-30`, never as the import-parity
reference; import parity of the humanoid archetype is judged against the 38-link source. Upstream work item
(described, not yet numbered): fold or declare the omitted masses of the G1 catalog. This is row 9 of the
foundation-fact table and one of the six probes of the workspace gate GATE-WS-09.

## Scope

In: the roll-up probe through the airlock; the omission list attached to the airlock's G1 model; the 320 N
fixture as a demonstration; the record. Out: the general mass roll-up analysis and the fold of fixed joints (the
design chapter's beads); importing the 38-link source (the import beads); the upstream change.

## Technical approach

1. **Probe.** Through `frob-fsim`'s referee trait, build the catalog G1 model at the pin and read the per-link
   masses the builder produced. Compute the total in two ways: a compensated floating-point sum in `f64`, and an
   exact sum of the decimal strings of the masses as rationals (the masses are read back as shortest
   round-tripping decimals). Expected: 30 links; total 31.96314202 kg. Pass: the rational sum equals
   31.96314202 exactly if the builder's table is given to that precision, and the `f64` sum is within 5·10⁻⁹ kg
   (half a unit of the last printed digit).
2. **The reduction record.** The airlock's G1 model carries `reduced { omitted: ["head", "logo", "left hand",
   "right hand", "pelvis contour", "waist supports", "sensor attachments"], omitted_mass: unknown | value,
   source: "fs-mbd robot_models at <revision>" }`. The names are taken from the builder's own list at the pin
   (re-read, not from this bead). `omitted_mass` is `unknown` until the primary source is re-read; the reported
   1.377 kg for the four welded links is recorded as `reported`, with its provenance, and is never used in a
   computation that a gate cites.
3. **Every result cites the reduction.** A rollout, digest or analysis on this model prints the profile name
   `g1-reduced-30` and the reduction record in its evidence block. A request that asks for the G1 as an
   import-parity reference, or as "the G1" in a verdict about the physical robot, is refused or labelled: the
   reduced profile is admitted for dynamics smoke tests, digests and referee rollouts, and never for import
   parity or a load verdict on the real assembly.
4. **Demonstration fixture.** A static support check with a rating of 320 N: on `g1-reduced-30` the weight is
   31.96314202 × 9.81 = 313.5584 N (passes); on a 38-link fixture with total mass 33.34014202 kg (a `synthetic`
   stand-in that adds the four reported welded masses, labelled as reported) the weight is 327.0668 N (fails).
   This shows the decision reversing, and is the reason the profile carries its label.
5. **Record.** The probe's result at the pin (link count, total, the omission list as read) with the FrankenSim
   revision and receipt hash, for the GATE-WS-09 record. If the builder has changed (masses folded or declared
   upstream), the row is recorded `fixed upstream at <revision>` and the profile is reviewed.

Determinism: exact (a sum). Evidence: a known answer of tier G1 on the host.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Through the airlock the catalog G1 has 30 links and a total mass of 31.96314202 kg (rational sum exact; `f64` sum within 5·10⁻⁹ kg), at the pinned commit.
- [ ] The model carries the reduction record with the omitted attachments as listed by the builder at the pin.
- [ ] A rollout on the model prints `g1-reduced-30` and the reduction in its evidence block.
- [ ] The 320 N fixture passes on the reduction (313.5584 N) and fails on the 38-link stand-in (327.0668 N).
- [ ] A request to use the catalog G1 as the import-parity reference is refused or relabelled, never silently accepted.
- [ ] The result is in the GATE-WS-09 record with revision and receipt.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-fsim catalog_g1`:
- `g1_catalog_has_30_links`.
- `g1_catalog_mass_rollup`: 31.96314202 kg, both sums.
- `g1_reduction_lists_omissions`: the record is non-empty and equals the builder's list read at the pin.
- `weight_fixture_reverses`: 313.5584 N < 320 N < 327.0668 N, each to 10⁻⁴ N.
- `import_parity_request_refused_for_reduced_profile`.
- `evidence_block_cites_reduction`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/suite_ws09.sh` (N33c-48): `frob-conformance probe g1-mass --json` and `frob sim run g1 --tier
referee --seconds 0.1 --json`; logs a `metric` event `g1_total_mass_kg` (expected 31.96314202, tolerance
5·10⁻⁹), `g1_links` (expected 30), and an `assert` event that the envelope's evidence block names the reduced
profile.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 31.96314202 kg over 30 links; 313.5584 N and 327.0668 N at 9.81 m/s². Negative controls: a
roll-up that lumps a `synthetic` 0.5 kg attachment into a retained link must change the total and fail the probe
(showing the probe would notice a silent fold); a model with the reduction record stripped must fail
`evidence_block_cites_reduction`; the 320 N fixture evaluated without the profile label must be rejected by the
verdict's own check. Lanes: twice on one machine; both machines; debug and release.

## Considerations

- The reported 38-link figures come from a description of the upstream robot and must be re-read from the
  primary source before any gate cites them; until then only the 30-link sum, which was computed from the
  sibling's own table, is a known answer.
- An import that drops a body of nonzero mass without declaring a reduction is refused by the importer; the
  catalog builder declares its omission in a comment, which the airlock turns into data.
- The digest test of the airlock (equality with the digest FrankenSim computes for itself) is unaffected: both
  sides use the same reduced model.
