## Background

`fs-ga` is FrankenSim's geometric-algebra crate and one of the bedrock-direct crates any FrankenRobotics crate may
use: motors, screws, and Lie façades for SO(3) and SE(3). The Lie layer of this project (`frob-lie`) is built over
it. A late reading found a limit in one of its public paths.

FACT (confirmed by reading on 2026-11-21 at FrankenSim head `483d09c682f2`): `fs_ga::Se3::left_jacobian` and
`right_jacobian` (`fs-ga/src/lie.rs:46`, `:1044–1049`) evaluate a series and refuse when the infinity norm of ad
exceeds 32 (`SE3_SERIES_MAX_AD_NORM = 32.0`); the refusal precedes the series. A pure translation of 33 m
therefore returns `IllConditioned`, for a pose that `Se3::exp` accepts (`:766–776`, which has no such limit) and
that is perfectly regular.

Why it matters: a warehouse aisle is longer than 32 m. The project's anchored coordinates keep translations
inside a cell small (cells of side 8 m), but a relative pose between two bodies in one aisle is not anchored. A
refusal labelled "ill-conditioned" there would read as a singular pose and would exclude ordinary configurations
from analyses and gradients.

**The closed form.** Here ad_ξ = [[ω̂, 0], [v̂, ω̂]] for a twist ξ = (ω, v), angular part first. For a pure
translation the twist has zero angular part, ad² = 0, and the left and right differentials of the exponential are
exactly J_l = I + ad/2 and J_r = I − ad/2, both with determinant one. Hand case: for a translation of 33 m along
+x and a unit rotation rate about +z (η = (ω = (0, 0, 1), v = 0)), ad_ξ η = (0, v × η_ω) = (0, (0, −33, 0)), so
the linear y component of the result is −16.5 m in J_l·η and +16.5 m in J_r·η.

Admission test: the public paths at translations of 1 m and 33 m, against the closed form. Interim posture:
`frob-lie` owns the differentials in closed form; a limit of an owner is reported as `unsupported`, never as a
singular pose. Upstream work item (added to the plan's register by description; numbered `FD-FSIM-35` by N33c-47
and carried by NF-08): a closed-form differential for small rotation and large translation in `fs-ga`. This is row 10 of the foundation-fact table and a probe of GATE-WS-09.

## Scope

In: the probe of the sibling's two public functions at the pin; the closed-form check in `frob-lie`; the mapping
of the sibling's refusal to `unsupported` at the airlock and in `frob-lie`'s own error type; the record. Out: the
retractions and their derivatives in general (BD-011); the refusal conversion table itself (N33a-13); the upstream
change (NF-08).

## Technical approach

1. **Probe of the sibling (a conformance test that names `fs-ga` directly, which the bedrock-direct rule
   allows).** For the twist ξ_t = (ω = 0, v = (t, 0, 0)):
   - t = 1 m: `left_jacobian` and `right_jacobian` return matrices equal to I + ad/2 and I − ad/2 to 10⁻¹²
     (entry-wise absolute, the entries being of order 1); applied to η = ((0, 0, 1), 0) the linear y components
     are −0.5 and +0.5.
   - t = 32 m: accepted (the infinity norm of ad for this twist is t, and the limit is exceeded only above 32);
     the result equals the closed form to 10⁻¹² relative.
   - t = 33 m: both return the `IllConditioned` refusal. `Se3::exp(ξ_33)` succeeds and returns the translation
     (33, 0, 0) m with identity rotation.
   - The value of the constant is read back where the crate exposes it and recorded (expected 32.0).
2. **The closed form in `frob-lie`.** `frob_lie::se3::dexp_left(ξ)` and `dexp_right(ξ)` must not refuse on
   translation magnitude. Their implementation (owned by BD-011) is the closed form in terms of the rotation
   angle θ = ‖ω‖ with the small-angle branch evaluated by its own series in θ only; the translation enters
   linearly. This bead adds the tests that pin the behaviour:
   - pure translations of 1, 33, 10³ and 10⁶ m: exactly I ± ad/2 (bitwise equal to the expression evaluated in
     `f64`), determinant 1;
   - the hand case: ∓16.5 m;
   - small rotation with large translation, θ = 10⁻⁸ rad about z with 33 m along x: equal to a 40-digit
     reference (computed offline in exact rationals from the series I + ad/2 + ad²/6 + ad³/24 + …, truncated
     with a remainder bound, and stored as a fixture) to 10⁻¹² relative.
3. **The mapping.** When code above `fs-ga` calls a sibling function that refuses for a limit of that
   implementation, the refusal that reaches the caller is `unsupported { owner: "fs-ga", limit:
   "SE3_SERIES_MAX_AD_NORM", observed: 33.0, bound: 32.0 }`. Concretely, in two places: in `frob-lie`'s own error
   type as `LieError::Unsupported { owner, limit, observed, bound }`; and, for a call that crosses the airlock,
   through the refusal conversion of N33a-13, which classes an upstream numerical limit as `OwnerLimit` and
   raises `FROB-REF-792` with `kind: owner-limit`, pointer `referee/fs-ga/IllConditioned`, `observed` 33.0 and
   `required` 32.0 (this bead adds the 33 m case to that conversion's table tests; it defines no new code). It is
   never mapped to the singular-pose or ill-conditioned refusals of this project (`FROB-REF-248` is for a solve
   that is ill-conditioned beyond its precision profile, which this is not). In `frob-lie` the sibling's
   Jacobian paths are not called at all for SE(3) differentials; the mapping exists for any other caller and is
   tested with the 33 m case.
4. **Record.** The three sibling outcomes (agreement at 1 m, acceptance at 32 m, refusal at 33 m), the constant,
   and the closed form's ∓16.5 m, with the FrankenSim revision and receipt hash, for GATE-WS-09. If the sibling
   now accepts 33 m and agrees with the closed form, the row is `fixed upstream at <revision>` and the mapping is
   kept as dead-code-free documentation of the old limit only if another limit remains.

Determinism: exact arithmetic on small matrices; tier G1 known answer on the host.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At the pin, `fs_ga::Se3::left_jacobian` and `right_jacobian` agree with I ± ad/2 at 1 m to 10⁻¹² and refuse at 33 m; `Se3::exp` accepts the 33 m twist.
- [ ] `frob-lie`'s closed-form differentials return ∓16.5 m for the hand case, have determinant 1 for pure translations, and do not refuse at 33, 10³ or 10⁶ m.
- [ ] The small-rotation, large-translation case matches the stored reference to 10⁻¹² relative.
- [ ] A caller that receives the sibling's refusal sees `unsupported` with the owner and the limit, never a singular-pose or ill-conditioned refusal.
- [ ] `frob-lie` contains no call to the sibling's SE(3) Jacobian series (a text and symbol check).
- [ ] The results (the three sibling outcomes, the constant, the closed form's ∓16.5 m) are written with the FrankenSim revision and the receipt hash in the form N33c-48's GATE-WS-09 record cites (the gate row is N33c-48's, which depends on this bead); `cargo run -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --example foundation_probe -- se3-dexp --json` prints them.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance probe_se3_dexp` and `cargo test -p frob-lie se3::dexp`:
- `sibling_agrees_at_1m`, `sibling_accepts_at_32m`, `sibling_refuses_at_33m`, `sibling_exp_accepts_33m`.
- `closed_form_pure_translation`: t ∈ {1, 33, 10³, 10⁶} m; J_l = I + ad/2 and J_r = I − ad/2 exactly; det = 1.
- `hand_case_16_5`: J_l·η has linear part (0, −16.5, 0) and J_r·η has (0, +16.5, 0).
- `left_right_relation`: J_r(ξ) = J_l(−ξ) for 10⁴ seeded twists, to 10⁻¹² relative.
- `small_rotation_large_translation_reference`.
- `derivative_matches_dual_numbers`: the closed form equals the forward-mode derivative of exp at 10³ seeded twists with translations up to 100 m, to 10⁻⁹ relative.
- `refusal_maps_to_unsupported`: the 33 m refusal becomes `LieError::Unsupported` with owner, limit, 33.0 and 32.0; through the airlock's conversion it becomes `FROB-REF-792`, `kind: owner-limit`, never `FROB-REF-248`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/suite_ws09.sh` (N33c-48): `cargo run -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --example
foundation_probe -- se3-dexp --json` (there is no `frob-conformance` binary, BD-025; this bead adds the
subcommand `se3-dexp` to the example declared in N33c-36, creating the file if it lands first, a thin parser
over the `probe_se3_dexp` functions that prints one JSON object and exits non-zero when its assertions fail);
logs `metric` events
`dexp_left_y_at_33m` (expected −16.5, tolerance 10⁻¹²) and `dexp_right_y_at_33m` (expected +16.5),
`sibling_max_abs_error_at_1m` (threshold 10⁻¹²), and `assert` events `sibling_refuses_at_33m` and
`refusal_mapped_unsupported` with the constant read.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: ∓16.5 m at 33 m; ∓0.5 m at 1 m; determinant 1. Negative controls: a closed form with the sign of
ad/2 swapped between left and right must fail the hand case; a `frob-lie` variant that delegates to the sibling's
series must fail `closed_form_pure_translation` at 33 m (it refuses); a mapping that returns the ill-conditioned
refusal must fail `refusal_maps_to_unsupported`. Lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; both machines
(the closed form is exact in `f64` for these inputs, so the lanes must agree bitwise).

## Considerations

- "Exceeds" is read as strict (33 refused, 32 accepted). If the sibling refuses at exactly 32, the probe records
  it and nothing else changes.
- The limit applies to the series, so a twist with a large rotation part can trip it at smaller translations;
  the probe adds one such case, θ = 3 rad about +x with 30 m along +x (a translation under 32 m whose ad has a
  maximum row sum of 33), and records the outcome without asserting it, since the sibling's choice of norm
  decides it.
- GPU kernels do not call these functions; kernel arithmetic for the same differentials is derived and
  certified separately.
- Tolerances carry dimensions: the linear part of the differential applied to a unit rotation rate is in metres
  per radian, and the 10⁻¹² tolerance is relative to 16.5.
