## Background

The broad phase of collision detection discards pairs of shapes that cannot touch during a step, using a bound on
where each shape can be. A pair discarded there never reaches the narrow phase, so an undersized bound is a
missed contact with no later chance of detection. FrankenSim's contact crate provides a helper for a moving
sphere, and a late reading found that it covers less than its name suggests.

FACT (a documented scope limit, read on 2026-11-21 at FrankenSim head `483d09c682f2`,
`frankensim/crates/fs-contact/src/broad_phase.rs:146–156`): `swept_sphere_aabb` documents that it "encloses
straight centre motion only" and leaves curved trajectories to the caller. It is right for prismatic and
ballistic segments and wrong for a sample on a rotating link.

The counterexample (the arc fixture): a 1 m arm turns from −60° to +60° about a fixed pivot at the origin. Its
tip is a sphere of radius 5 cm, and an obstacle sits at (1, 0) m. The tip's end positions are (0.5, ∓0.866) m,
their chord lies on x = 0.5 m, and the chord padded by the radius reaches x = 0.55 m. The tip passes through
(1, 0). A bound built from the end positions rejects the pair, and the arm goes through the obstacle.

Admission test: the arc fixture: the helper's bound misses the obstacle, and the arc-aware bound contains it.
Interim posture: `frob-contact` builds its own bounds for rotating links. Upstream work item (described, not yet
numbered): an arc-aware swept bound in `fs-contact`. This is row 12 of the foundation-fact table; in the
workspace gate GATE-WS-09 its row is due at M2.

## Technical approach

1. **The fixture, with its numbers.** Pivot at the origin; orbital radius R = 1 m; sweep from θ₀ = −60° to
   θ₁ = +60° (Δθ = 120° = 2π/3 rad); sample sphere radius r = 0.05 m; obstacle a point (or a sphere of radius
   1 mm) at (1, 0, 0) m. End positions A = (0.5, −0.8660254, 0) and B = (0.5, +0.8660254, 0) m; chord length
   ‖B − A‖ = √3 ≈ 1.7320508 m.
2. **The sibling helper at the pin.** Call `swept_sphere_aabb(A, B, r)` (through a conformance probe target in
   `frob-fsim`; the function is in the `referee` group's crate). Expected box: x ∈ [0.45, 0.55], y ∈ [−0.9160254,
   0.9160254], z ∈ [−0.05, 0.05] m. Assert that the obstacle's x = 1 lies outside the box: the helper's bound
   misses the obstacle. Also assert that for a straight motion from A to B the helper's box does contain every
   point of the segment dilated by r (the helper is correct for what it documents).
3. **The arc-aware bounds of this project (DESIGN, from the contact chapter; evaluated here on the fixture).**
   - *One revolute joint.* For a rotation by an angle Δθ of magnitude at most π at orbital radius R, the bound is
     the segment AB dilated by the sagitta R·(1 − cos(Δθ/2)) plus r. Here the sagitta is 1·(1 − cos 60°) = 0.5 m,
     the dilation is 0.55 m, and the bound reaches x = 0.5 + 0.55 = 1.05 m: it contains the obstacle.
   - *Any admitted motion.* With L an upper bound on the path length of the sample, every point X of the path
     satisfies ‖X − A‖ + ‖X − B‖ ≤ L, so the path lies in the segment AB dilated by ½·√(L² − ‖B − A‖²), plus r.
     Here L = R·Δθ = 2π/3 ≈ 2.0943951 m and the dilation is ½·√(4.3864908 − 3) ≈ 0.5887467 m (0.589 m), which
     contains the sagitta bound; with r the bound reaches x ≈ 1.139 m.
   - Where a speed limit is missing, the bound cannot be formed and the model is refused at admission.
4. **The usage rule.** In `frob-fsim` and `frob-contact`, `swept_sphere_aabb` may be called only for samples
   whose centre motion over the sub-interval is a straight segment (a prismatic joint; a ballistic segment; a
   base translation with no rotation of any ancestor). The call site carries a typed marker
   `StraightCentreMotion` that only those three constructors produce; a sample on a link with a rotating
   ancestor cannot construct the marker, so the misuse does not compile.
5. **Record.** For GATE-WS-09 (row 12, M2): the helper's box, the obstacle outside it, the two arc-aware bounds
   containing it, the FrankenSim revision and receipt hash. If the sibling has gained an arc-aware bound, the
   row is `fixed upstream at <revision>` and the local bounds are compared with it on the fixture before any
   switch.

Determinism: exact geometry in `f64`; tier G1 known answer.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At the pin, the sibling helper's box for the arc fixture reaches x = 0.55 m and excludes the obstacle at x = 1 m.
- [ ] The sagitta bound reaches x = 1.05 m and contains the obstacle; the path-length bound has dilation 0.5887 m (to 10⁻⁶) and contains the sagitta bound.
- [ ] The helper's box contains the straight segment AB dilated by r (its documented scope holds).
- [ ] The helper cannot be called for a sample with a rotating ancestor: the marker type has no constructor for that case (a compile-fail test).
- [ ] The tunnelling audit on the fixture reports the contact when the arc-aware bound is used and reports a missed contact attributed to broad-phase rejection when the chord-only mutant is used.
- [ ] The results are in the GATE-WS-09 record (row 12, M2) with revision and receipt.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-contact swept::arc_fixture` and `cargo test -p frob-fsim probe_swept_sphere`:
- `end_positions`: A and B as above to 10⁻¹².
- `sibling_box_misses_obstacle`: box bounds as above; obstacle outside.
- `sibling_box_contains_straight_segment`: 1,000 points of the segment, dilated by r.
- `sagitta_bound_contains_arc`: 1,000 points of the arc lie inside the bound; the bound reaches x = 1.05 m.
- `path_length_bound_contains_sagitta_bound`: dilation 0.5887467 m ≥ 0.5 m.
- `compile_fail/straight_marker_on_rotating_link.rs`.
- Property test: for 1,000 seeded variants (radius 0.2 to 2 m, angle up to π, sphere radius 1 to 10 cm, an obstacle on the arc and outside the padded chord) the helper's box excludes the obstacle and both arc-aware bounds contain it.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/suite_ws09.sh --m2-rows` (N33c-48): `frob-conformance probe swept-arc --json` and `frob contact
audit` on the fixture scene; logs `metric` events `helper_box_xmax_m` (expected 0.55), `sagitta_bound_xmax_m`
(expected 1.05), `path_bound_dilation_m` (expected 0.5887), and `assert` events `helper_misses_obstacle`,
`arc_bound_contains_obstacle` and `audit_no_broad_phase_miss`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: chord at x = 0.5 m; padded chord to 0.55 m; sagitta 0.5 m; bound to 1.05 m; L = 2π/3 m; dilation
0.589 m. Negative control: the chord-only mutant (the project's bound replaced by the sibling helper on a
rotating link) must fail the fixture and the 1,000-variant property; it is the registered mutation of the
contact chapter's gate. A control on the probe: with the sweep reduced to ±1° the helper's box contains the
obstacle moved onto that short arc, so the probe asserts that its sweep is large enough for the sagitta (0.5 m)
to exceed r (0.05 m). Lanes: twice on one machine; debug and release; `f64` on the host.
