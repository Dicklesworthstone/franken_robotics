## Background

The suite chapter keeps a table of facts about sibling components that could mislead this project if they were
relied on unexamined: defects, limits and convention hazards. Each row has what was read in the source, an
admission test that settles it at the pinned commit, an interim posture, and (where a change in the sibling
would help) an upstream work item. Nothing in the table was executed when the plan was written: each mechanism
was read in source on 2026-11-21, and reported values are marked as reported.

Six rows were added last, numbered 9 to 14. Rows 9, 10, 12 and 13 are limits or defects; rows 11 and 14 are
convention hazards. They are counted separately from the five defects or limits and the one convention hazard of
the earlier rows.

**GATE-WS-09** (claim type: known answer). Each of the six admission tests is recorded at the pin with its
result: the roll-up of 31.96314202 kg; the refusal at 33 m and the agreement at 1 m to 10⁻¹², with the closed
form returning ∓16.5 m; the signed motion witness for both transformer entry points; the arc fixture; the two
`fs-sos` probes compared with exact evaluation; the two-plane ray fixture. A probe that now passes upstream is
recorded as fixed and its interim posture is reviewed. Milestone M0, with rows 12 and 14 at M2.

The gate does not require the siblings to be correct. It requires that what each sibling does at the pin is
measured, written down, and matched by an interim posture that makes the fact harmless.

## Scope

In: the end-to-end script in its M0 and M2 modes; the gate record with six rows; the fixed-upstream review
procedure; the closing conditions. Out: the six probes (N33c-41 row 9; N33c-42 row 10; N33c-43 row 11; N33c-44
row 12; N33c-45 row 13; N33c-46 row 14); the registration of the upstream work items (N33c-47).

## Technical approach

1. **Preconditions.** `cargo xtask check-stack` passes on clean sibling trees and a receipt is written;
   FrankenSim is at the stack lock's revision; for row 14, franken_threed's revision is the one in the suite
   survey.
2. **M0 mode (rows 9, 10, 11, 13).** Run through the conformance driver, the example target `foundation_probe`
   of `frob-conformance` declared in N33c-36 (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations
   --example foundation_probe -- <probe> --json`; there is no `frob-conformance` binary, BD-025), each
   producing one JSON envelope:
   - Row 9: 30 links; total 31.96314202 kg (rational sum exact; `f64` within 5·10⁻⁹ kg); the omission list.
   - Row 10: the sibling agrees with I ± ad/2 at 1 m to 10⁻¹², accepts at 32 m and refuses at 33 m; the closed
     form in `frob-lie` returns −16.5 m (left) and +16.5 m (right) for a 33 m translation along +x applied to a
     unit rotation rate about +z; the refusal is mapped to `unsupported`.
   - Row 11: rates (12, −8) and (−12, 8) for efforts (2, 3) and coefficient 4, each with zero power residual to
     10⁻¹²; the witness returns the authored sign through `interconnect_mapped` and the opposite sign through the
     legacy entry point; which entry point gave which vector is recorded.
   - Row 13: for 1 − 2⁻⁵⁰·x on abs(x) ≤ 2²⁸, the returned value against the exact minimum 1 − 2⁻²²; for
     1 − x/2 on abs(x) ≤ 2⁻⁵⁵, the returned value against 1 − 2⁻⁵⁶; verdicts; the rechecked bounds 1 − 2⁻²² and
     1 − 2⁻⁵³.
3. **M2 mode (`--m2-rows`: rows 12 and 14).** This bead writes the mode at M0; its steps log `not-run: N33c-44`
   and `not-run: N33c-46` until those M2 beads have landed, and the recording of each row at M2 is the
   acceptance of its probe bead, run through this mode.
   - Row 12: for the 1 m arm from −60° to +60° with a 5 cm tip sphere and an obstacle at (1, 0) m, the sibling
     helper's box reaches x = 0.55 m and misses the obstacle; the sagitta bound reaches 1.05 m and the
     path-length bound (dilation 0.589 m) contains it.
   - Row 14: planes at 2 m (scale 1/2, local parameter 4) and 3 m (scale 3, local parameter 1): the adapter
     returns the foreground at 2 m; the local-sort mutant returns the background.
4. **Row states.** Each row is one of: `recorded` (the fact holds at the pin and the interim posture's test
   passes); `fixed upstream at <revision>` (the sibling no longer shows the fact); `changed` (the sibling behaves
   differently from both the fact and the fix, with the observation attached); `due M2`; `not run` with a
   reason. The gate is `pass` at M0 when rows 9, 10, 11 and 13 are `recorded` or `fixed upstream` and rows 12
   and 14 are `due M2`; it is complete at M2 when those two are likewise recorded.
5. **The fixed-upstream review (procedure).** When a probe reports that the sibling now passes:
   1. Record the row as `fixed upstream at <revision>` with the probe's output.
   2. Find the upstream work item by the probe's entry in the link table of N33c-47
      (`crates/frob-conformance/src/upstream/probe_links.toml`); run its switch-over rule (for example
      "re-run the 1 m and 33 m probe; then `frob-lie` may delegate or keep its own, by ADR").
   3. The owner of the interim posture decides, in a short ADR: keep it (with the reason: an explicit direction
      argument costs nothing; a closed form is still preferable), or remove it (then the removal lands with the
      probe turned into a regression test that the sibling stays fixed).
   4. The negative control of the row stays registered either way: the mutant must still fail.
   A row is never deleted; `fixed upstream` is a state.
6. **Record.** One row in `registries/gates.toml` for GATE-WS-09 with six sub-results (measured values, the
   negative control and whether it was caught, the state), the receipt hash and the sibling revisions; an entry
   in `DEPENDENCY_ADMISSION.md`. The script never writes the gate register (one recorder per gate). The gate is
   measured by G1 entries registered under GATE-WS-09 in `crates/frob-conformance/tests/g1_known_answers.rs`
   (N38a-02's `Entry`, whose `gates` and `controls` fields name the gate and each row's `NC-` control): rows 9,
   10, 11 and 13 by this bead, each calling its probe's functions in process (the same functions the
   `foundation_probe` subcommands drive); rows 12 and 14 at M2 by N33c-44 and N33c-46 (row 14's entry reads the
   record that the `f3d-ray-probe` runner writes in its own workspace, since the core cannot link `f3d-math`).
   The recording session enters the M0 part from the receipt of `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p
   frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test g1_known_answers -- GATE-WS-09` with `cargo run -p
   frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-WS-09 --receipt <hash> --part M0`; at M2 the part
   `--part M2` is entered the same way once both M2 rows' entries exist, by whichever of N33c-44 and N33c-46
   lands second (until then the part stays open, and the first bead's row is evidenced by the receipt of its
   own tier run, which its script logs). No PASS on a
   dirty sibling tree: the recorder refuses a receipt whose `dirty` flag is set, and such a run writes a
   development record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The unit tests of the six probe beads are prerequisites and are run by the script. This bead adds
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/gate_ws09.rs`:
- `gate_record_has_six_rows`: rows 9 to 14, each with a state from the allowed set.
- `m0_pass_requires_four_rows`: with row 13 missing the gate is `incomplete`.
- `m2_rows_cannot_be_recorded_without_their_probe_output`.
- `fixed_upstream_requires_revision_switch_over_and_adr`.
- `row_never_deleted`: a registry in which a previously present row is absent fails.
- `state_machine`: allowed transitions are `due M2 → recorded`, `recorded → fixed upstream`, `recorded → changed`; `fixed upstream → recorded` only with a new observation (a regression).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/suite_ws09.sh` (default: M0 rows; `--m2-rows`: rows 12 and 14):
1. `cargo xtask check-stack`.
2. `cargo run -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --example foundation_probe -- g1-mass --json`,
   then the same command with `se3-dexp`, `transformer-sign` and `sos-bounds` in place of `g1-mass`; and
   `frob sim run g1 --tier referee --seconds 0.1 --key ws09-g1-<run> --json` for row 9's evidence block (N33c-41).
3. With `--m2-rows` (each step `not-run: <bead>` until N33c-44 or N33c-46 has landed): the same example with
   `swept-arc`; the arc runs and their tunnelling audits named in N33c-44; build the `f3d-math` probe workspace
   and run its tests and its runner `f3d-ray-probe --json` (in that workspace, since the core's driver cannot
   link the sibling's math crate); the viewer's headless pick.
4. The upstream-register consistency test (N33c-47), with the probes it reports linked or `probe pending`.
5. The gate's tier run (item 6; with `--m2-rows`, after the M2 entries exist); the script logs the receipt hash
   and the gate line's status and stops there: the row is entered by the recording session with `record_gate`,
   never by the script.
The script sources the STD-E2E helper and writes `target/e2e-logs/suite_ws09/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript.
Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead: N33c-48`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines carry the exact command, exit code
and wall time; `metric` lines carry each measured value with its unit, expected value and tolerance
(`g1_total_mass_kg` 31.96314202 ± 5·10⁻⁹; `dexp_left_y_at_33m` −16.5 and `dexp_right_y_at_33m` +16.5 at 10⁻¹²
relative; the two rate vectors; `helper_box_xmax_m` 0.55 and `sagitta_bound_xmax_m` 1.05; the two `fs-sos`
returned values in hex with their exact minima; the two world ray parameters 2 m and 3 m); `assert` lines carry
each negative control's outcome and each row's state; on failure the first value outside tolerance and the path
of the probe's envelope. The `summary` gives assertions passed and failed, the state of all six rows, and
`gates: [GATE-WS-09]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers, all in one place: 31.96314202 kg over 30 links; ∓16.5 m at 33 m and agreement at 1 m to 10⁻¹²;
rates (12, −8) and (−12, 8); chord padded to 0.55 m, sagitta bound to 1.05 m; minima 1 − 2⁻²² and 1 − 2⁻⁵⁶;
local parameters 4 and 1 mapping to 2 m and 3 m. Negative controls: one per row, as in the acceptance criteria,
each registered in the negative-control registry under this gate. A control on the gate: the script run against a
dirty FrankenSim tree must write a development record and no PASS. Lanes: `trj` and the Mac; twice on one
machine with identical records apart from timestamps; debug and release for the probes.

## Considerations

- Three numbers in the table are reported and not yet reproduced (the 38-link mass of 33.34014202 kg and the
  1.377 kg of four welded links; the transformer's rates; the two `fs-sos` returned values). The gate records
  what the pin does; where a reported number is not reproduced, the measured one replaces it in the record and
  the report is marked superseded.
- Pre-mortem row 24 ("a foundation defect surfaced late") has as its last line "unreported defects exist". This
  gate and its two predecessors close the reported ones; the standing defence is the rule that an admission is
  never inherited and that a crate that compiles is not yet admitted.
- The two M2 rows depend on mechanisms of M1 and M2 (the arc-aware bounds; the ray kernels and viewer picking);
  the M0 exit review must read the gate as `pass (rows 9, 10, 11, 13); rows 12, 14 due M2`.
- The gate register lists this gate among those defined in subsections added by the final audit; no earlier
  milestone list names it, so the exit reviews take it from the register.
