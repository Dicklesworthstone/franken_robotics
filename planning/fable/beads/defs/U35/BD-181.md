## Background

**Statement (INV-NUM-03).** Given a policy (weights and normalizers), a world's trajectory is a function of
its address, design, scene and task alone. It is the same bits when the world runs alone, in any batch, at any
slot, beside any co-tenants, in any bucket whose capacities it fits, and on any number of shards of one device
family. Decision ADR-NUM-02 makes this part of determinism class D2: incumbents document replay of a batch,
not of a world taken out of its batch, and the studio forks and teleports single worlds. FACT (source
register): one incumbent that documents bitwise replay of a batch holds a world equal to its isolated run only
to 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻⁴ in its own tests; the property is worth having only if it is exact.

What rests on it: slot dispatch in the daemon (a finished world's slot is refilled by an unrelated job),
teleport (one world re-run alone on the CPU), counterfactual forks, memoized episodes, and paired comparison
(two arms of a pair run in different batches). The licence table grants bit-exact pairing, attribution and
forks only with this gate *current* for the device family.

**GATE-SPARK-15** (claim: Known answer; TARGET; earliest M2). For 1,000 episodes of each reference workload,
per-world digests are identical alone, in 100 random co-tenancies, under slot permutation, at two bucket
capacities and on one and two shards, on `batch-cpu` and on each device family; draws depend on address alone;
both negative controls of the chapter's known-answer table are caught.

**GATE-KIN-05** (claim: Known answer; earliest M2), the dynamics chapter's statement of the same property for
the batched step (INV-KIN-04: a world's digests depend on its address and identities only, not on its slot,
its co-tenants, the capacity of its buffers or the world count): 1,000 episodes with identical digests solo
and in 100 random co-tenancies, capacities and slot assignments, per back end; the path-table Jacobian gives
the same digests as the dense one.

This seed bead is the test as a running capability and both gates' measurement on every backend. The design
conditions that make the property true and their audit are N35-13 and N35-14; the mutants that prove the test
can fail are N35-15; the draw-table clause is N35-16. Tier: Solid. BET-02 (one source, three backends, inside
declared determinism classes) names a failure of this test as one of its falsifiers.

## Scope

In: the frozen world set; the arrangement matrix at gate size; the command; digest comparison by address;
first-divergence localization on failure; the composition evidence the classifier reads, with currency; the
path-table clause; the gate records. Out: the fast sweeps inside every `spark check` (the Spark unit); the
conditions and their audit; the mutants; draw tables; the end-to-end script (N35-17).

## Technical approach

**Frozen world set (per reference workload; content-hashed).** Workload Q: the reference quadruped (12 joints,
18 degrees of freedom, contact capacity 32, row capacity 160, step 2 ms, decimation 10). Workload H: the
reference humanoid (29 joints, 35 degrees of freedom, contact capacity 64, row capacity 320).

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Tested worlds | 1,000 world addresses per workload (one episode each: until termination or 500 control intervals), plus the four fixture worlds of N35-15 (`neg-zero-sum`, `equal-keys`, `two-observers`, `mixed-magnitude-sum`) |
| Tasks and scenes | Flat velocity tracking and rough-terrain traversal for Q; standing and walking for H; resets enabled, so reset draws are exercised |
| Policies (frozen: weights and normalizers, by content hash) | The baseline controller; one trained policy checkpoint for Q |
| Filler worlds | A pool of 8,192 other addresses with other parameter variants, and for mixed buckets other body plans, used only as co-tenants |

**Arrangements (each is compared with the reference arrangement `alone`).**

| Axis | Arrangement | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `alone` | Each tested world as a batch of one, in its own shape class | 1,000 runs per workload and backend |
| `cotenancy` | 100 seeded random co-tenancies: all tested worlds plus a random number of fillers (0 to 7,192) in random slots, batch sizes drawn from {1,024, 4,096, 8,192} (the largest where memory allows) | 100 batches |
| `slot` | The tested worlds in identity order, reversed, and three seeded permutations | 5 batches |
| `capacity` | The world's own bucket and the next larger registered one (Q: 18 and 24 degrees of freedom; H: 36 and 48); contact buffer capacities 32, 64 and 128 | 2 × 3 batches |
| `shard` | One device; two shards with the tested worlds split at random (two cards on the Linux host; two environments on one device on the Mac and on `batch-cpu`) | 2 arrangements |
| `jacobian` | The dense contact Jacobian against the path table (each contact row touches only the degrees of freedom on its support path) | 2 step plans |

**Comparison.** Per world address: the chain of in-kernel state digests (four `u32` words per committed chunk,
each chain value covering the whole prefix) and the BLAKE3 hash of the final committed snapshot. Equality must
be exact for every tested world in every arrangement: the threshold is zero mismatches. Rows are matched by
address, never by slot.

**On a mismatch.** Binary search over the chunk chain (valid because each chain value covers the prefix), then
the first unequal step, then a re-run of that step with per-stage digests, then the first kernel whose outputs
differ, then the element. The record (arrangement, world address, step, stage, kernel, quantity, both bit
patterns) goes to the divergence ledger, and the gate is recorded as failed for that device family with the
record attached.

**Command.** `frob spark check --invariance --workload <Q|H> [--axes all|…] [--episodes 1000]
[--cotenancies 100]`. It reuses the sweep machinery of the check at gate size. Exit 0 when every axis has zero
mismatches; exit 2 (findings) otherwise.

**Draws.** The clause "draws depend on address alone" is satisfied by the draw-table record of N35-16 for the
same device family and fingerprint; the command reads it and refuses to report the gate complete without it.

**Controls.** The mutants of N35-15 are run through the axes they are keyed on in the same campaign. The
gate's "both negative controls" are read as: every control that the known-answer table and the metamorphic
register name for this property (`batch-wide-reduction`, `pad-plus-zero`, `normalizer-in-segment`,
`capacity-shaped-tree`) must be caught, and so must `neighbour-row-read`, which is one of BET-02's two
controls.

**Evidence for the classifier.** One `composition` record per device family, fingerprint digest, step plan and
profile: axes run, worlds, episodes, mismatches, the audit report's identifier (N35-14), the draw-table
record's identifier, receipt hash. The record is *current* only while the fingerprint (hardware, driver,
compiler), the kernel content hashes and the step plan are unchanged; after a change, the fast sweeps of
`spark check` restore currency for day-to-day use and this full matrix is re-run for the gate record.

**Gate records.** Rows of `registries/gates.toml` for GATE-SPARK-15 (per backend and workload: episodes,
co-tenancies, axes, mismatches = 0, controls caught, draw clause, commit, fingerprint, seed, receipt hash) and
for the device rows of GATE-KIN-05 (the same measurement, plus the `jacobian` axis); the `batch-cpu` row of
GATE-KIN-05 is shared with the dynamics unit's bead and recorded once. Entered by a session that did not
implement the work.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SPARK-15 on `batch-cpu`, `gpu-cuda` and `gpu-metal`: for 1,000 episodes of Q and of H, zero digest mismatches between `alone` and each of 100 random co-tenancies, five slot orders, two bucket capacities and one against two shards.
- [ ] GATE-KIN-05 on each backend: the same, including contact buffer capacities 32, 64 and 128 and slot assignments; the path-table Jacobian gives the same digests as the dense one.
- [ ] The draw-table record for each device family is current and shows zero mismatches (draws depend on address alone).
- [ ] `batch-wide-reduction`, `pad-plus-zero`, `normalizer-in-segment`, `capacity-shaped-tree` and `neighbour-row-read` are each caught on every backend, with the first divergence localized to the planted kernel.
- [ ] The four fixture worlds are in the tested set and pass every axis without a mutant.
- [ ] A composition record is written per device family with currency keys; the classifier attests class D2 for the family only with it.
- [ ] A mismatch produces a first-divergence record and a failed gate row; nothing is averaged or tolerated.
- [ ] The command is reachable through `frob --json`; cancelling it mid-matrix leaves no partial record marked complete and no leaked device resources.
- [ ] If a device family fails after two repair cycles, the recorded outcome is the weaker class for that family (D3 against its own isolated runs, tolerance replay), and the stop decision of M2 is evaluated: if no device family passes, M2 falls back to its no-GPU variant.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `world_set_hash_frozen`: the registered hash equals the content hash; the four fixture worlds are present.
- `cotenancy_plan_is_seeded`: the 100 co-tenancies (fillers, slots, batch sizes) are a pure function of the seed; a different seed gives different plans.
- `match_by_address`: two arrangements with different slot orders and equal per-address digests compare equal; a planted swap of two worlds' digests is reported with both addresses.
- `short_matrix_cpu`: 16 worlds of Q, 50 control intervals, 5 co-tenancies, all axes, on `batch-cpu` `f32` and `f64`: zero mismatches.
- `short_matrix_mutants`: the same with each of the five named mutants: at least one mismatch on a keyed axis, localized to the planted kernel.
- `jacobian_variants_equal_short`.
- `composition_record_currency`: changing the fingerprint digest, a kernel hash or the step plan makes the record stale.
- `gate_incomplete_without_draw_record`.
- `capacity_axis_uses_registered_buckets`: Q → (18, 24), H → (36, 48).

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/num_batch_invariance.sh` is bead N35-17. It runs this command at gate size on each backend
present, the conditions audit, the draw tables and the controls, then
`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g5 -- GATE-SPARK-15 GATE-KIN-05`, logging through the STD-E2E helper
every `cmd` with exact command line, exit code and wall time, the evidence block (claim known-answer, class,
tier, determinism D2, identities with fingerprint digest, seed), `metric` lines per axis (`worlds`,
`episodes`, `arrangements`, `digest_mismatches`, threshold 0) and, on failure, the arrangement, the world
address, the step, stage, kernel and quantity with both bit patterns and the paths of both digest chains.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-785 (one world alone, in 100 random co-tenancies, under slot permutation, at two bucket capacities and on
  one and two shards: identical per-world digests); KA-786 (−0.0 at two capacities); KA-787 (two worlds
  observing 1 and 3); KA-795 (draws). The dynamics chapter's known answer for padding and co-tenancy (one
  world at buffer capacities 32, 64 and 128, solo and in 100 random co-tenancies: identical digests over
  1,000 episodes per back end; negative control: a reduction tree shaped by capacity).
- CX-367, CX-368; the grouping case with 10⁸.
- Metamorphic: MR-01 (batch-composition invariance; mutations: a reduction tree shaped by capacity, a
  batch-wide normalizer); permuting world indices permutes results and changes nothing else; adding inactive
  worlds or unused capacity changes no active world's digest.
- Determinism lanes: `alone` twice (identical, the run-to-run half of class D2); the matrix on `batch-cpu`
  across worker counts; each device family against its own class. No cross-backend equality is asserted here.

## Considerations

- Cost. `alone` is 1,000 batch-of-one runs of 5,000 world-steps each, dominated by launch overhead on devices
  (of the order of tens of minutes per workload and backend); the co-tenancy axis is about 100 × 8,192 × 5,000
  = 4·10⁹ world-steps per workload and backend. This exceeds the default compute envelope's hour on some
  backends and needs the gate-measurement class; without approval the axes are run at reduced counts and the
  gate is recorded `not run` (a reduced run is a rehearsal, not a pass).
- Two shards on the Mac are two environments on one device; the two-card form runs only on the Linux host.
  Both are recorded with what they were.
- Kill gate KILL-D1 does not touch this gate: it concerns equality between a device and the CPU. A family can
  pass this gate (class D2 for itself) and fail D1.
- The test covers the frozen workloads. A kernel that only another task exercises (a sensor, a film law) is
  covered by the per-kernel sweeps of the check and by the audit of N35-14; a new stage added to the step plan
  makes the composition record stale by changing the plan identity.
- RISK-04's mitigation states that the property claimed is batch-composition invariance, with bit equality
  only where class D1 is attained; this gate is that claim.
