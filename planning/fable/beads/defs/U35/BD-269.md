## Background

Every result carries a determinism class (D0 exact; D1 bitwise across backends; D2 bitwise per backend and
independent of the batch; D3 inside a tolerance with agreeing task decisions; D4 nothing), and decision
ADR-NUM-01 requires every verb to declare the minimum class of its inputs and to refuse below it. The licence
table says what each class licenses: bit-exact pairing needs both arms at D2 on one device family with a
current batch-independence record, or D1 across families; a D3 pair is allowed only as `paired-by-address`,
labelled, with its realized pair correlation; any arm of class D4 or of unrecorded class is refused
(`FROB-REF-561`); attribution and counterfactual forks need D2 with a current record and are otherwise refused
(`FROB-REF-562`); promotion needs D3 with a ledger envelope and the decision-level test; training is D2 by
default and D4 only as the labelled `fast-training` profile, whose numbers are never evidence
(`FROB-REF-861`) and whose weights are cited only through a re-evaluation under `device-replay` or stricter.

The reasons are in the arithmetic of pairing and replay: seeding a generator fixes the draws, not the order
of a reduction; a difference of one unit in the last place reaches order one after about 16.6/λ seconds at
divergence rate λ; the variance of a paired difference scales with 1 − ρ, so a fall in pair correlation from
0.95 to 0.50 costs ten times the episodes; and under class D4 nothing about an arm can be replayed, so its
verdict could not be reproduced, promoted or attributed. The refusal is by code, not by convention.

The mechanisms exist by M3: the class type and classifier, the licence check (M2), the `fast-training`
profile, the comparison engine on devices. This seed bead joins them at the verbs where they bite and
measures two gates.

**GATE-SPARK-17** (Known answer; TARGET; earliest M3): each licence refusal of the table fires on its planted
case (a D4 arm in a paired comparison; attribution below D2; a cited training return; a cross-family pair
demanded as paired), and a policy trained under `fast-training` re-evaluates to the same digest twice.

**GATE-SAFE-09** (Known answer; M3): a result produced under the nondeterministic training profile is refused
on each of the five paths of invariant INV-SAFE-07 (no D4 result is consumed by paired statistics, promotion,
qualification, a certificate or a build packet); the same policy re-evaluated under D2 is accepted. Its known
answer's negative control is the D4 result offered unlabelled.

Tier: Solid. It serves GOAL-20 (state determinism as a class on every result, and test it) and closes the
program's negative-control register row for the determinism classifier: a planted nondeterministic reduction
is classified below its claimed class; a D4 result offered unlabelled is refused on every path.

## Scope

In: wiring the licence check and the citation check into the M3 verbs; the planted-case corpus for both
gates; the acceptance controls (the same requests at an admissible class succeed); the five paths offered
through the real verbs; the unlabelled and misclassified plants offered through the verbs; the sweep over
every result-consuming verb; both gate rows, GATE-SPARK-17 and GATE-SAFE-09, each entered once and here.
Out: the check itself (N35-07); the profile and everything a `fast-training` job writes (N35-06, with the
trainer's half in N23a-18); the classifier (N35-02); the pairing report (N35-08); the evidence boundary's
refusal `FROB-REF-825`, its reading "absent means D4", the per-path tests of the boundary function, the
INV-SAFE-07 property test and the boundary mutants (N37-28, which enters no gate row: this bead runs its
tests as the boundary-function part of GATE-SAFE-09 and cites their output in the row); the safety unit's
other gates; the end-to-end script (N35-32).

This seed bead is the capstone of GATE-SPARK-17 and GATE-SAFE-09: it measures, and its components build. It
depends on the licence check (N35-07), the profile (N35-06), the pairing report (N35-08), the classifier
(N35-02), the trainer's half of the labelled profile (N23a-18) and the evidence boundary's refusal of a D4
result on the five paths (N37-28), and on the verbs it wires and tests at M3: comparison (BD-262), promotion
(BD-263), qualification (BD-259) and the certificate bundle writer (BD-133). No component depends on this
bead; a bead elsewhere that needs only the check or only the profile depends on N35-07 or N35-06. Three
higher-numbered seed beads are connected and do not block it: the attribution verbs (BD-279, BD-280), whose
planted cases 4 and 4′ are run when those verbs exist and are recorded `not run` with the reason before
that; the claim product at the boundary (BD-308); and the build-packet writer (BD-408, M5), for which the
fifth path is measured through the boundary function as stated below.

## Technical approach

**Wiring (each admitting verb calls the check before it draws an episode or reads a result).**

| Verb | Call | Outcome fields |
|---|---|---|
| `eval.compare`, `study.compare` | `licence::check(PairedComparison, arms, pair, asked)`, after the comparison path's `pairing::admit` (BD-262, N28a-18), which runs first and answers a D4 arm, arms of different classes and a D3 pair without a decision-equivalence record with `FROB-REF-542` (the layer rule of N28-REF and the pair table of N35x-02: one situation, one code, one verb); the check keeps `FROB-REF-561` for what 542 does not cover (an arm of unrecorded class; a cross-family pair, or a D3 pair, demanded `exact`) | `pairing: paired-exact \| paired-by-address \| unpaired`; under `paired-by-address` the pair correlation is mandatory |
| `run.fork`, `autopsy.intervene`, `autopsy.attribute`, `autopsy.rerun` | `check(Fork \| Attribution, [source run])` | Admitted, or `FROB-REF-562` |
| `eval.promote` | `check(Promotion, [fast-tier result], pair)` | Admitted with the pair's decision-equivalence verdict cited, or refused |
| Any verb that attaches a number to a gate record, scorecard, comparison, promotion, report or claim | The citation check of the `fast-training` profile, and `check(CiteDeviceResult, …)` | `FROB-REF-861`, `FROB-REF-864`, or admitted |
| `eval.qualify`, certificate writing, build-packet writing | The evidence boundary (claim product: weakest determinism wins) | `FROB-REF-825` for a D4 input |

**Planted cases for GATE-SPARK-17 (the corpus `frob-conformance/corpus/licences/`; each case is a request file
plus its fixture runs).**

| # | Planted case | Request | Must return |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A D4 arm in a paired comparison | `eval compare A B` where arm B's episodes were collected during a `fast-training` job; and the same two arms given to `licence::check(PairedComparison, …)` directly (KA-802, the daemon site) | At the verb `FROB-REF-542` (the comparison path's code for this condition), naming arm B and its profile, before any episode; at the check `FROB-REF-561`, naming arm B and its profile. Both are recorded |
| 2 | An arm of unrecorded class | The same with arm B's determinism field absent | `FROB-REF-561`, class `unrecorded` |
| 3 | A cross-family pair demanded as paired | Arm A on `gpu-cuda`, arm B on `gpu-metal` (or `batch-cpu`), no class D1 between them, default pairing | `FROB-REF-561`, both families named |
| 4 | Attribution below D2 | `autopsy attribute` on a source run of class D3 | `FROB-REF-562` |
| 5 | A fork on a stale record | `run fork` on a D2 run whose batch-independence record is stale | `FROB-REF-562` |
| 6 | A cited training return | The final return of a `fast-training` job attached to a scorecard | `FROB-REF-861`, first repair the re-evaluation command |
| 7 | Re-evaluation determinism | The exported policy evaluated twice under `device-replay` on 256 world addresses | Identical digests; the evaluation is admitted wherever case 6 was refused |

**Acceptance controls (each planted refusal has a twin that must succeed, so that the gate cannot be passed by
refusing everything).** 1′ and 2′: both arms evaluated under `device-replay` on one family with a current
record → `paired-exact`. 3′: the same cross-family request with `--pairing unpaired` → admitted and labelled
`unpaired`; and with `--pairing by-address` where a decision-equivalence verdict exists for the task and the
placement pair → `paired-by-address` with a pair correlation. 4′ and 5′: the same request after the world is
teleported to `batch-cpu`, or after the record is renewed → admitted. 6′: the re-evaluation's success rate
attached to the scorecard → admitted.

**The five paths for GATE-SAFE-09.** One D4 result R (the training-time evaluation summary of the
`fast-training` job) and its policy P are offered on each path:

| Path | Offer | Must return | Twin that must be accepted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paired statistics | R as an arm of `eval compare`; R inside a stored study given to `study compare <study>` (the verb takes a stored study; since `study plan` refuses a D4 arm with `FROB-REF-533`, the Arena study admission's code, the study is written by `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example licence_harness -- plant-study --arm <E_base> --arm <R>`, declared below, which N37-29 also uses) | `FROB-REF-542` at comparison admission (`pairing::admit`); `FROB-REF-825` at the stored-study boundary | P re-evaluated under `device-replay` as the arm |
| Promotion | R to `eval promote` | `FROB-REF-825` | The re-evaluation of P, with the pair's decision verdict |
| Qualification | R cited in an `eval qualify` request | `FROB-REF-825` | A qualification request on P, whose worlds are evaluated under a D2 profile by the lane itself |
| Certificate | R as an input of a certificate bundle | `FROB-REF-825` | The re-evaluation as the input |
| Build packet | R as evidence in a build-packet request | `FROB-REF-825` | The re-evaluation as the evidence |

At M3 the build-packet writer does not exist yet; the fifth path is exercised through the evidence boundary's
function with a conformance packet request, recorded as such, and re-measured with the real verb when it
lands.

**The example target `licence_harness` (DESIGN, declared by this bead; `crates/frob-conformance/examples/
licence_harness.rs`).** There is no binary named `frob-conformance` (BD-025), so the corpus's drivers are
subcommands of one example: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example licence_harness -- plant-study --arm
<result> --arm <result>` writes a stored study holding exactly the given arm results through the conformance
build's store doorway (it stands for a study that holds a D4 arm by another route, such as an imported study
record) and prints one JSON object with the study identifier; `… -- check --case <n>` runs planted case n against
`licence::check` directly and prints the code returned. It is no verb, links only in a build with
`gauntlet-mutations`, writes no gate row, and exits non-zero when its own assertions fail.

**Unlabelled and misclassified plants.**
- *Unlabelled D4.* A copy of R with its determinism field and its profile removed. Every path must still
  refuse: an absent class reads as D4 (never as a stronger class), and the artifact graph shows the producing
  job's profile.
- *Planted nondeterministic reduction.* A conformance kernel with a scheduling-dependent floating reduction in
  a run that claims `device-replay`. The classifier's repeat evidence fails, the run is classified below its
  claimed class, and a paired comparison or a fork on it is refused.

**Gate records.** Rows of `registries/gates.toml`: GATE-SPARK-17 (cases 1 to 7 with code returned, twins
accepted, both digests of case 7) and GATE-SAFE-09 (the five paths with code returned and twins accepted; the
unlabelled plant; the note on the fifth path), with commit, fingerprint digests and receipt hashes, entered
from the gate run's receipt with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate <id> --receipt
<hash>` (the one writer of gate rows, N38b-19) by a session that did not implement the work. The GATE-SAFE-09
row is entered once, here; it cites the output of N37-28's boundary tests (per-path lines, the property test,
the three mutants caught). The two scripts that walk the paths (`num_licences_d4.sh`, N35-32, and `safe_d4.sh`,
N37-29) depend on this bead and so cannot be cited when it closes: the rows are entered with this bead's own
measured outcomes and N37-28's output. A script writes no row: each produces a receipt, and when it has run a
recording session records the gate again from that receipt with the same `record_gate` command (N35-32's for
GATE-SPARK-17, N37-29's for GATE-SAFE-09), which adds the receipt to the row's measured history.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the seven planted cases returns the stated code with exit 3 and ranked repairs (seed done-condition for GATE-SPARK-17); each twin is admitted with the stated label.
- [ ] A policy trained under `fast-training` and evaluated twice under `device-replay` gives identical digests on each device family present and on `batch-cpu`.
- [ ] A D4 result is refused on each of the five paths, and the same policy's re-evaluation under D2 is accepted on each (seed done-condition for GATE-SAFE-09); the fifth path's measurement is labelled `boundary function` until the build-packet verb exists.
- [ ] The unlabelled D4 copy is refused on all five paths; the planted nondeterministic reduction is classified below D2 and refused for pairing and for forks.
- [ ] Every `eval.compare` result carries `pairing`; under `paired-by-address` it carries the realized pair correlation; a cross-family comparison is never labelled `paired-exact` without class D1.
- [ ] No verb in the registry that consumes results bypasses the check: the registry lint on `uses` passes, and a sweep that offers the D4 result to every result-consuming verb finds no acceptance.
- [ ] Both gates are recorded with measured outcomes per case; a case that could not be run is `not run` with its reason, never passed.
- [ ] The refusals are reachable identically through `frob --json` and the MCP tools.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `licence_corpus_cases` (seven cases and their twins against the real check with fixture runs; case 1 returns 561 at the check and 542 through `eval.compare`, cases 2 and 3 return 561 at both).
- `plant_study_holds_given_arms`: `licence_harness plant-study` with two fixture results writes a study whose arm results are exactly those two, by identifier.
- `five_paths_refuse_d4_at_verbs` and `five_paths_accept_reevaluation_at_verbs`: R, then the re-evaluation
  of P, offered through each consuming verb's admission step with the codes of the table (542 at
  `eval.compare`, 825 elsewhere; a build in which `eval.compare` answers 561 for the D4 arm fails). The same five uses against the boundary function alone are N37-28's
  `five_paths_refuse_825` and `five_paths_accept_d2_reeval`; the gate test invokes them and does not copy
  them.
- `unlabelled_d4_refused_at_verbs`: the stripped artifact offered through each verb is refused (that an
  absent class combines to D4 is N37-28's `unrecorded_is_d4`).
- `misclassified_run_demoted`: with repeat evidence `fail`, a run declared `device-replay` is not D2.
- `compare_label_matrix`: (same family D2 current) → `paired-exact`; (cross-family, D1) → `paired-exact`; (cross-family, no D1, default) → 561; (cross-family, `unpaired`) → `unpaired`; (D3 with verdict, `by-address`) → `paired-by-address`.
- `by_address_requires_correlation_field`.
- `every_consumer_calls_check`: for each verb with `uses`, a conformance hook counts calls to the check during one request; zero calls fails.
- `gate_recorder_rejects_missing_twin`: a GATE-SPARK-17 record without its acceptance controls is not accepted.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/num_licences_d4.sh` is bead N35-32. It trains the small `fast-training` policy, provokes the
seven cases and their twins and the five paths through the real `frob` binary and `frob-mcp`, compares the
two re-evaluation digests, and runs `FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
g5_determinism -- GATE-SPARK-17 GATE-SAFE-09` (the long target name; `--test g5` is not a target).
It logs through the STD-E2E helper every `cmd` with exact command line, exit code and wall time, the evidence
block (the training lines show determinism D4; the evaluations D2), `refusal` events with code, `pointer`,
`observed`, `required` and first repair, `metric` lines for the two digests and for the pair correlation
where printed, and on failure the request file and the artifact identifiers involved.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-801 (a `fast-training` policy evaluated twice under `device-replay`: identical digests; control: citing
  its training return is refused); KA-802 (the licence refusals on planted cases: 561 twice, 562, 861);
  KA-834, the safety chapter's known answer for the D4 gate (refused on each of the five paths of INV-SAFE-07;
  the same policy re-evaluated under D2 accepted; exact verdict; tier G5; control: the D4 result offered
  unlabelled); KA-803 for the pairing arithmetic the labels report ((1 − 0.50)/(1 − 0.95) = 10 times the
  episodes; ln(2²⁴)/λ ≈ 16.6/λ seconds).
- CX-366 (seeding the generator does not make two arms a replayable pair).
- Negative controls: the unlabelled D4 result; the planted nondeterministic reduction; a build with the
  comparison's admission step removed (case 1 is then accepted and the gate test fails); a build in which
  everything is refused (the twins fail).
- Determinism lanes: case 7 on each device family and `batch-cpu`, twice each; the refusals are exact
  decisions (class D0) and identical on every platform.

## Considerations

- Kill gate KILL-D1 (per device family, decided at M2). If a family cannot hold bit equality with `batch-cpu`,
  case 3 is the permanent state for pairs that include it, and twin 3′ (`unpaired`, or `paired-by-address`
  with a verdict) is the only way to compare across it.
- Kill gate KILL-SHARD (decided at M2). If it fired, `fast-training` has no asynchronous knob; the gates still
  need a D4 result, which any knob the pilot funded, or analytic-gradient training, provides. If no knob at
  all is funded, case 7 and the five paths use a conformance D4 job (a training job with the arrival-order
  learner reduction enabled only in conformance builds), and the records say so.
- Overlapping codes from several chapters (561, 542 and 533; 561 and 825; 861 and 867; 562 and 584) are
  resolved by raising site in the refusal registry beads (N35-REF, N28-REF, N37-REF, N35x-02's pair table); this gate's corpus states the code expected at each site, so a disagreement
  between units shows up as a failed case and not as a silent double standard.
- Promotion's twin needs the placement pair's decision-equivalence verdict from the M2 gate; where a pair has
  none, promotion is refused for that pair and the case is recorded against the pair, not against this gate.
