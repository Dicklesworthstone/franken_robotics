## Background

Comparing two designs on common world addresses (a world address is `(run_seed, world, episode)` and fixes
every random draw a world sees) is the program's way of getting a verdict from few episodes: both arms meet the
same scenes and disturbances, so world-to-world variance cancels. How much cancels is measured by the
correlation ρ between the arms' outcomes. For equal marginal variances σ², the variance of the paired
difference is Var(D) = 2σ²·(1 − ρ). If uncontrolled arithmetic lowers the correlation from 0.95 to 0.50, a
comparison needs (1 − 0.50)/(1 − 0.95) = 10 times the episodes (illustrative values, not a prediction).

Arithmetic erodes pairing because contact-rich closed-loop dynamics amplify rounding. A difference of one unit
in the last place grows like e^(λt) and reaches order one after about ln(2²⁴)/λ ≈ 16.6/λ seconds, for a largest
Lyapunov exponent λ that is measured per task. FACT (source register): learned continuous-control policies in
closed loop have measured maximal Lyapunov exponents of 0.03 to 0.16 per control step on two benchmark tasks,
which at that benchmark's 25 ms control period is 1.2 to 6.4 s⁻¹, a horizon of about 13.9 s down to 2.6 s.

The licence table therefore allows a paired comparison whose arms agree only inside a tolerance (class D3)
only as `paired-by-address`, labelled, **with the realized pair correlation reported beside the verdict**. A
paired test under class D3 remains valid as statistics; what the reader must be told is how much pairing was
actually left. This bead computes the two numbers the rule needs: the realized correlation per metric, and the
divergence rate per task with the horizon it implies. Tier: Solid.

Alternative rejected: reporting only the label. A label says pairing may have decayed; the correlation says by
how much, and lets a planner size the next study.

## Scope

In: the estimator of the realized pair correlation and its interval; the episode multiplier; where the fields
appear; the twin-run measurement of λ̂ per task and tier; the horizon; the record in the fidelity ledger. Out:
the licence that decides the label (N35-07); the comparison engine (BD-262); the decision-level test (N35-09,
N35-10); attribution's use of the horizon to bound fork leads (the attribution unit).

## Technical approach

**Realized pair correlation (per metric of a comparison).** Given the n scored pairs (aᵢ, bᵢ) in address
order:

  ρ̂ = Σ (aᵢ − ā)(bᵢ − b̄) / √( Σ (aᵢ − ā)² · Σ (bᵢ − b̄)² ).

For a binary outcome this is the phi coefficient of the 2 × 2 table. Rules:
1. If either arm has zero variance, the field is `undefined: zero-variance`, never 0 and never 1.
2. Interval: with z = atanh(ρ̂) and standard error 1/√(n − 3), the 95% interval is tanh(z ± 1.96/√(n − 3));
   omitted, with the reason, for n < 10.
3. `var_ratio = Var(a − b) / (Var(a) + Var(b))`, computed directly from the pairs (it equals 1 − ρ̂ when the
   two variances are equal).
4. `episode_multiplier = (1 − ρ̂) / (1 − ρ_ref)`, where ρ_ref is the realized correlation on record for the
   same task, metric and design class under `paired-exact` (class D2 on one device family); omitted with the
   reason `no-reference` when none is on record.
5. Unscored pairs are excluded from the estimate and counted (`pairs_used`, `pairs_unscored`), per the rule
   that a summary states its exclusions.
The arithmetic is double precision on the host over outcomes consumed in address order, so the estimate does
not depend on completion order.

**Where it appears.** `eval.compare` and `study.compare` envelopes gain, per metric,
`pair_correlation: { rho, interval, n, var_ratio, episode_multiplier, reference }`. It is mandatory when
`pairing` is `paired-by-address` (the envelope schema rejects the label without it) and is also printed for
`paired-exact`, where it populates ρ_ref.

**Divergence rate per task (the twin-run measurement).**
1. Take K = 64 world addresses of the task's development battery, a design, a controller or policy, and a
   tier (the measurement is per tier; `batch-cpu` in double precision is the reference).
2. For each address run the world to its nominal horizon, and run a twin forked at tick t₀ (after the settling
   interval) whose state differs in one component (the base's forward velocity v) by one unit in the last place
   of v in single precision, u(v) = 2^(e−23) for |v| in [2^e, 2^(e+1)): a relative perturbation between 2⁻²⁴
   and 2⁻²³ (not exactly 2⁻²⁴), applied as v + u(v) in the tier's width (in `f32` this is the next float above
   v, exactly); where v = 0 it is 2⁻²⁴ m/s. The realized δ₀ = |v′ − v| is computed in double precision and
   recorded with the twin, and the fit uses it. The twin is a declared intervention through the fork mechanism,
   so both runs share every exogenous draw.
3. At every control interval record the separation δ(t): the largest physically scaled state difference (the
   fidelity ledger's scaling per quantity: lengths by the robot's characteristic length, velocities by its
   characteristic speed, angles in radians).
4. For each world fit the slope of ln δ(t) against t by least squares over the window where
   10·δ(t₀) ≤ δ(t) ≤ 10⁻² (after the transient, before saturation). Worlds whose separation never leaves the
   floor are counted `non-divergent`.
5. λ̂ = the median slope over divergent worlds, reported with its interquartile range and the count of
   non-divergent worlds. The horizon is t* = ln(2²⁴)/λ̂ = 16.64/λ̂ seconds.
6. Store one row per (task, design class, controller or policy identity, tier, kernel versions) in the
   fidelity ledger: λ̂, interquartile range, t*, K, non-divergent count, the fork receipts.

**One estimator, two users (DESIGN, the same statement in N27b-22).** λ̂ names this estimator and no other: the
median over divergent twins of the least-squares slope of ln δ(t) over the window 10·δ(t₀) ≤ δ(t) ≤ 10⁻². Each
row gives it in s⁻¹ and, beside it, per control step (λ̂ times the task's control period), the unit of the
published 0.03 to 0.16. The attribution chapter's pilot of placebo-separation growth (N27b-22, at M2, on a
development oracle or `batch-cpu`, with no product dependency) uses the same definition in its own code and
reports its worst-fork figure ln G(W)/steps under another name (`lambda_envelope`), because a peak ratio over
the worst fork is an upper envelope and not this rate. Its value is preliminary; the rows written here, on our
tiers, are the measurement of record for OPEN-AUTOPSY-3. The slope does not depend on the size of the
perturbation or on a constant scaling of the coordinates, so the pilot's placebo of 10⁻⁷ and this bead's
one-ulp twin estimate the same number.

**How the two numbers are used.** `eval.compare` under `paired-by-address` prints t* beside the episode length:
an episode much longer than t* is expected to have lost pairing in its late part, and the report says so. The
attribution chapter bounds fork leads with the same λ̂ (its default until measured: leads of at most 2 s).
Neither number is a gate; both are evidence of class Estimated with tier and determinism recorded.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For a comparison labelled `paired-by-address`, the envelope carries `pair_correlation` for every metric; removing the field makes schema validation fail.
- [ ] ρ̂ equals the hand value on fixtures: perfectly concordant pairs → 1; pairs (0,1), (1,0) alternating → −1; one arm constant → `undefined: zero-variance`.
- [ ] The estimate is unchanged when the pairs are delivered in a different completion order.
- [ ] `episode_multiplier` reproduces the known answer (ρ̂ = 0.50 against ρ_ref = 0.95 → 10.0) and is omitted with `no-reference` when no `paired-exact` record exists.
- [ ] The twin-run procedure on a planted linear system with known growth rate recovers it: for ẋ = λx with λ = 2.0 s⁻¹ integrated exactly, λ̂ is within 2% of 2.0 and t* within 2% of 8.32 s.
- [ ] On a contact-free, asymptotically stable fixture (a damped pendulum) every world is `non-divergent` and no λ̂ is reported; the report says so and prints no horizon.
- [ ] λ̂ rows are written to the fidelity ledger with their fork receipts, and `frob fidelity ledger` lists them.
- [ ] Twin forks are admitted only on a source run of class D2 with a current batch-independence record (the licence check); on a lesser run the measurement refuses with `FROB-REF-562`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `rho_hand_values`: pairs {(1,1),(2,2),(3,3)} → 1; {(1,3),(2,2),(3,1)} → −1; {(1,2),(2,1),(3,4),(4,3)} → 0.6.
- `rho_binary_is_phi`: table a = 40 (both succeed), b = 10, c = 10, d = 40 → φ = (40·40 − 10·10)/√(50·50·50·50) = 0.6.
- `rho_zero_variance_is_undefined`.
- `rho_interval_fisher`: ρ̂ = 0.6, n = 103 → z = 0.6931, half-width 0.196 → interval (0.460, 0.711).
- `rho_order_invariant` (property): any permutation of the pairs gives the same ρ̂ to the last bit (the sums are taken in address order after sorting).
- `var_ratio_equals_one_minus_rho_for_equal_variances`.
- `episode_multiplier_known_answer`: (1 − 0.50)/(1 − 0.95) = 10.
- `horizon_known_answer`: ln(2²⁴) = 16.6355; λ = 1.2, 3.2 and 6.4 s⁻¹ → 13.86 s, 5.20 s and 2.60 s.
- `lambda_fit_linear_system`: synthetic separation δ(t) = 2⁻²⁴·e^(2t) sampled every 20 ms (labelled `synthetic`) → slope 2.000.
- `lambda_per_step_known_answer`: the map x ← 1.1735·x at a 25 ms control period → 0.16 per step and 6.4 s⁻¹ (the known answer the pilot N27b-22 shares).
- `lambda_window_excludes_saturation`: a series that saturates at 0.3 gives the same slope as its unsaturated prefix.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/num_pairing_report.sh [--tier T] [--seed N]`, every run verb under its own key (the two
comparisons are compared, so their keys differ): (1) `frob --json eval compare A B --task <t> --backend <T>
--key npr-1` with both arms on device family T under its default profile `device-replay` (expect `paired-exact`
and a correlation, which becomes ρ_ref); (2) the same comparison under key `npr-2` with arm B placed on
`batch-cpu` through `eval.compare`'s one placement input, `--backend 1=<T> --backend 2=batch-cpu`, and
`--pairing by-address` (expect `paired-by-address`, a correlation, and a multiplier against step 1, where a
decision-equivalence verdict exists for the task and that placement pair; otherwise `unpaired` with the finding
that pairing was not available, logged as such); (2b) the same arms under key `npr-3` with `--pairing exact`
on a family without class D1 against `batch-cpu` (expect `FROB-REF-561`, exit 3, before any episode: the
licence's code on `eval.compare` for a cross-family pair demanded exact, N35-07); (3) the twin-run measurement
through `frob --json run fork <run> --world <w> --at <t₀> --change '<base forward velocity> := v + u(v)' --key
npr-fk-<w>` with the one-ulp perturbation on 64 addresses and `frob --json fidelity ledger --class <c>
--tier-pair <T>,<T>` (the tier's intrinsic rows) to read the row; the same fork on a source run of class below
D2 under key `npr-fk-low` (expect `FROB-REF-562`); (4) the damped-pendulum fixture (expect `non-divergent`, no
horizon printed). On a machine with no device family, steps 1, 2 and 2b are logged `skipped: no device family`
and T is `batch-cpu` for step 3. Logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/num_pairing_report/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`: `cmd` lines with exact command line, exit code,
wall time; the evidence block (claim design-comparison or fidelity, class, tier, determinism, identities,
seed); `metric` lines `rho`, `rho_interval`, `var_ratio`, `episode_multiplier`, `lambda_hat` (s⁻¹ and per
control step), `t_star` (s), `non_divergent`, with the fork receipts' digests; `assert` lines for label and
field presence; `refusal` lines with code and repairs; on failure the first differing value and the run and
fork identities to reproduce it; a `summary` line.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-803: a fall in pair correlation from 0.95 to 0.50 needs 10 times the episodes; one unit in the last
  place reaches order one after about 16.64/λ seconds.
- CX-366: equal seeds do not make a replayable pair (a twin run with identical draws and a one-ulp state
  difference separates at rate λ̂).
- Negative controls: a comparison engine that reports ρ̂ from pairs sorted by outcome (inflates the
  correlation to nearly 1; caught by `rho_hand_values`); a twin that receives fresh random draws (its
  separation jumps to order one at the first draw; the fit's window test rejects it and the script fails).
- Determinism lanes: the estimator twice and across worker counts (identical bits); the twin runs under
  `reference-replay` on `batch-cpu`.

## Considerations

- λ̂ depends on the policy as much as on the physics; a retrained policy needs a new row. The record is keyed
  by the policy identity for that reason.
- The measurement costs 2·K episodes per row and fits the default compute envelope for the reference tasks on
  `batch-cpu`.
- OPEN-AUTOPSY-3 (the Lyapunov exponents of our own controllers and policies on our tiers; default until
  measured: fork leads of at most 2 s; owner: Cortex lead) is answered by the rows this bead writes.
- The keyed random streams and exposure domains raise ρ for a given class; this bead reports what was realized
  and makes no claim about which mechanism produced it.
