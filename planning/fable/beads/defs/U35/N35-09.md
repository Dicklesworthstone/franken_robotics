## Background

Two placements of the same worlds (two backends, or two tiers) that do not agree bit for bit are compared
under determinism class D3: agreement inside a recorded envelope **and** agreement of task decisions. Decision
ADR-NUM-03: under class D3, agreement of task decisions is tested beside agreement of states, because a state
tolerance neither implies nor is implied by agreement on a verdict.

The smallest case: a success predicate x ≥ 1 m, one placement ending at 1.0000001 m and the other at
0.9999999 m. The state error is 2·10⁻⁷ m, inside any envelope; the verdicts differ. Conversely two placements
may end a metre apart and agree that the robot fell. A check that compares states only passes the first case
and fails the second, and is wrong both times about what a user will conclude from the runs.

DESIGN. For a pair of placements claimed at class D3, on a frozen ensemble of world addresses with frozen
policies, the CPU-versus-device check (`spark check`) and the promotion verb (`eval.promote`) compute per
world:

1. state error in physically scaled units at checkpoints, until the first divergence beyond the envelope;
2. impulse and work residuals;
3. event times (first contact, termination);
4. **task decisions**: termination cause, each success predicate, each requirement verdict, the winner of
   each design comparison in the ensemble, and the pass or fail of each gate metric;
5. the margin of each decision, that is, the distance of its deciding quantity from its boundary.

Discordant worlds are listed with their margins. A decision whose margin is smaller than the divergence
envelope is reported as `boundary-sensitive` on both placements, whichever way it fell. The first divergence
of each discordant world (step, kernel, quantity) goes to the divergence ledger.

This bead builds that per-world record and the per-ensemble decisions; the statistics that turn a set of
records into "decision-equivalent or not" are N35-10. The same record is what teleport prints for a tolerance
replay and what attribution uses before it trusts a double-precision replay of a single-precision failure
(the same verdict, failure class and binding atom, with the failure time inside the recorded envelope). Tier:
Solid.

## Scope

In: the record types; alignment of two episode records; the five computations; margins per decision kind; the
`boundary-sensitive` rule; discordance direction; the ensemble-level decisions; the hand-off of first
divergences to the divergence ledger. Out: the statistics and the verdict (N35-10); thresholds (N35-11); the
gate (BD-177); the envelopes themselves (BD-212); the first-divergence search (BD-206); how the two runs are
produced (`spark check`, teleport, promotion).

## Technical approach

**Types (DESIGN; module `frob_chronicle::equiv`, schema `frob.equiv.world/1`).**

```rust
pub struct WorldCmp {
    addr: WorldAddr,
    state: StateCmp,          // checkpoints: Vec<(Tick, QuantityId, f64 /*scaled error*/)>, first_divergence: Option<Divergence>
    residuals: Residuals,     // impulse: (p, q, scaled diff); work: (p, q, scaled diff)
    events: EventTimes,       // first_contact: (Tick, Tick); termination: (Tick, Tick)
    decisions: Vec<DecisionCmp>,
    continuous: Vec<(MetricId, f64, f64)>,   // return, energy per metre, time to failure, on each placement
    discordant: bool, direction: Direction,  // None | PFavoured | QFavoured | Mixed
}
pub struct DecisionCmp {
    kind: DecisionKind,       // TerminationCause | Success | Requirement | GateMetric
    id: Ident, value_p: DecisionValue, value_q: DecisionValue, concordant: bool,
    margin_p: f64, margin_q: f64, unit: Unit, envelope: f64,
    boundary_sensitive: bool,
}
pub struct Divergence { tick: Tick, stage: Option<StageId>, kernel: Option<KernelId>, quantity: QuantityId,
                        value_p: f64, value_q: f64, scaled_error: f64 }
pub struct EnsembleCmp { comparisons: Vec<(ComparisonId, Winner, Winner, f64, f64)>,  // winner and statistic margin per placement
                         ranking_p: Vec<DesignId>, ranking_q: Vec<DesignId> }
```

**Procedure `compare_world(run_p, run_q, envelopes)` (numbered).**
1. *Align.* Committed ticks and events of both runs; canonical variables by stable identifier (link, joint,
   contact feature pair, channel), never by slot. The two runs must share world address, design, scene, task
   and policy identities; otherwise the comparison is refused as not a pair.
2. *States.* At each checkpoint tick in order, for each compared quantity of the fidelity ledger's quantity
   registry that is a state at a tick (base displacement, yaw, stance impulse, slip distance), compute the
   scaled error |p − q| / s, where s is the quantity's scale constant (its floor) in that registry (N16b-02:
   1 cm, 1 N·s, 1 mm; yaw, whose disagreement there is absolute, in rad with s = 1 rad). Joint positions and
   velocities have no row in that registry, so no scale and no envelope (DESIGN): they are recorded at each
   checkpoint as raw differences in their own units (rad or m; rad/s or m/s) for the divergence ledger and
   never end the walk. Stop the state walk at the first tick where a registry quantity's scaled error exceeds
   its envelope; record that tick, quantity and both values as `first_divergence`. States after that tick are not compared (the worlds are then different worlds), but
   steps 3 to 6 still run on the whole episode.
3. *Residuals.* Impulse residual: the scaled difference of the per-episode sums of contact impulse magnitudes.
   Work residual: the scaled difference of the energy ledger's actuator work and contact dissipation totals.
4. *Event times.* Tick of first contact and tick of termination on each placement.
5. *Decisions.* Read the decision records of both runs and pair them by identifier. `concordant` is equality
   of the decision values (a termination cause is a category; the other kinds are pass or fail).
6. *Margins.* Per kind:
   - success predicate and gate metric `g ⋈ c`: margin = g − c for ≥ and >, c − g for ≤ and <, in the
     predicate's unit; positive means satisfied;
   - requirement verdict: the quantitative margin of the monitored formula as the requirement language
     defines it (the signed distance of the binding atom, after unit normalization), with the binding atom's
     identifier;
   - termination cause: the margin of the termination condition that fired, and of the nearest condition that
     did not, at the terminating tick; the smaller magnitude is recorded.
7. *Boundary sensitivity.* `boundary_sensitive = min(|margin_p|, |margin_q|) < envelope`, where `envelope` is
   the ledger's divergence envelope for the decision's deciding quantity on this tier pair, converted to the
   margin's unit. It is set on both placements whichever way the decision fell, and also for concordant
   decisions.
8. *Discordance.* The world is `discordant` if any decision is not concordant. `direction`: `PFavoured` if
   every non-concordant pass-or-fail decision passes on P and fails on Q; `QFavoured` for the reverse; `Mixed`
   if both occur or only a category differs.
9. *First divergence of a discordant world.* If step 2 found none inside the envelope (the smallest case
   above), run the first-divergence search in tolerance mode with the decision's deciding quantity and a zero
   envelope to find where the two placements first differ at all, and record step, stage, kernel and quantity.
   Append the record to the divergence ledger keyed by world address and placement pair.

**Ensemble-level decisions.** For each design comparison defined on the ensemble, evaluate the comparison's
frozen rule on each placement's outcomes and record the winner (or `tied`, `inconclusive`) and the margin of
its statistic from its threshold. For the designs in the ensemble, record each placement's ranking by the
task's primary score.

**Evidence and determinism.** The record is computed in double precision on the host from stored episode
records; given the two records it is a pure function (class D0 for the decision fields, D2 for the floating
fields). It is of evidence class Tested and carries both runs' identities.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The smallest case is reproduced: with P ending at 1.0000001 m and Q at 0.9999999 m under the predicate x ≥ 1 m, the record shows state error 2.0·10⁻⁷ m with no first divergence at an envelope of 10⁻⁴ m, `concordant = false`, margins +1.0·10⁻⁷ and −1.0·10⁻⁷ m, `boundary_sensitive = true` on both, `discordant = true`, `direction = PFavoured`.
- [ ] The converse case is reproduced: two placements whose final base positions differ by 1 m and which both terminate with cause `fell`: a first divergence is recorded, every decision is concordant, `discordant = false`.
- [ ] A concordant decision with |margin| below the envelope is flagged `boundary_sensitive` on both placements.
- [ ] All five item groups are present for every world; a world for which a decision record is missing on either placement makes the comparison fail with the missing identifier, never a default of "concordant".
- [ ] Every discordant world has a first-divergence record (step, stage or kernel, quantity) appended to the divergence ledger, including worlds whose state error stayed inside the envelope.
- [ ] Two runs that are not a pair (different address, design, scene, task or policy identity) are refused.
- [ ] The record is identical when either run's worlds are stored in a different slot order.
- [ ] `EnsembleCmp` reports, per comparison, both winners and both statistic margins, and both rankings.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead's own lane is `scripts/e2e/equiv_world.sh --seed <s>` (the verb that exposes the comparison, `spark
check --decisions`, is BD-177's, which depends on this bead). Steps: `pair` (two placements of the same 64
world addresses, design, scene, task and policy: `frob sim run --workload Q --worlds 64 --seconds 5 --tier
referee --seed <s> --key ew-p-<run-id> --json` in `f64`, and the same on `--tier batch-cpu --precision f32`
with `--key ew-q-<run-id>`, so the pair is a real D3 pair); `compare` (`cargo run -p frob-chronicle --release
--example equiv_world -- --p <run p> --q <run q>`: one `frob.equiv.world/1` record per world from the stored
episode records, with the five item groups present for every world); `first_divergence` (for each discordant
world, `frob telemetry divergence <run p> <run q> --mode tolerance --json` agrees with the step and quantity the
record handed to the divergence ledger); `self` (run p compared with itself: zero errors, all concordant, no
flags); `fixtures` (the boundary and converse cases as stored `synthetic` episode records through the same
example: discordant and `boundary-sensitive` on both sides; concordant with a first divergence). Logs through
the STD-E2E helper to `target/e2e-logs/equiv_world/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript: `cmd` with exit code and
wall time, the evidence block, per discordant world a `metric` line with the world address, the decision
identifier, both values, both margins with unit, the envelope and the first-divergence step and kernel; worlds
compared, discordant and flagged; on failure the paths of both episode records; a `summary` line. The later
lane is `scripts/e2e/num_decision_equivalence.sh` (N35-12), which reaches the same records through `frob spark
check --decisions`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-788: the predicate x ≥ 1 m at 1.0000001 m against 0.9999999 m is reported as discordant and
  `boundary-sensitive` on both placements. Negative control: a check that compares states only (a conformance
  variant of this comparator with steps 5 to 8 removed) reports the pair as agreeing and must be caught by
  `decision_differs_inside_envelope`. The variant is built under N35-CX's plant identifier for CX-364's
  control, `states-only-comparator` (kind `mutation`; the Cargo feature `states-only-comparator` of
  `frob-chronicle`, conformance builds only), so that the control N38b-05's seeding tool derives from CX-364
  finds its plant; BD-177's gate test and N35-30's row 4 load the same variant. This bead writes no row of
  `controls.toml`.
- CX-364: a state tolerance does not imply agreement on the verdict, in both directions.
- Metamorphic: swapping P and Q swaps `PFavoured` and `QFavoured` and changes nothing else; comparing a run
  with itself gives zero errors, all concordant, no flags.
- Determinism lanes: the comparator twice, across worker counts, debug and release: identical records.

## Considerations

- Margins need quantitative decision records at every tier. A tier that writes only pass or fail cannot be
  compared under class D3; the comparison then fails with the missing field, which is the fail-closed outcome.
- The envelope used for the boundary flag is the ledger's frozen one. Before the ledger is frozen the flag is
  computed with the provisional envelope and marked `provisional`.
- Locating the kernel of a first divergence costs a re-run of one step with per-stage digests; it is done for
  discordant worlds only (at most a few tens per ensemble if the pair is near the thresholds).
- Private scenes: the record carries world addresses and decision identifiers, never scene geometry; logs
  follow the rule that no private-scene data is written.
