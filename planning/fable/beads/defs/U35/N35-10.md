## Background

Under determinism class D3 two placements of the same worlds are claimed to agree inside a tolerance and to
lead to the same task decisions. The per-world comparison (N35-09) yields, for each world address of a frozen
ensemble, whether any decision differs, in which direction, the paired continuous outcomes and, across the
ensemble, each placement's ranking of designs. This bead turns those records into one verdict.

The pair is decision-equivalent when all of the following hold (the thresholds are TARGETs; open question
OPEN-NUM-2 owns them, default as stated, frozen once at M2):

- the one-sided 95% upper bound (exact binomial) on the rate of discordant decisions is at most 1%. With 4,096
  worlds that admits roughly thirty discordant worlds; with zero discordant worlds the bound is about 3/n, so
  at least 300 worlds are needed to show 1% at all;
- discordances are not one-sided: the bound on the net rate (one placement passing where the other fails,
  minus the reverse) is at most 0.5%;
- continuous outcomes (return, energy per metre, time to failure) are equivalent by two one-sided tests inside
  the margins of the fidelity ledger;
- the ranking of the designs in the ensemble agrees at the top, by the rank-agreement measure the program uses
  between evaluation rungs.

Why an upper bound and not a count. Zero discordances among 100 worlds is compatible with a true rate of 2.95%;
only a bound says what the data exclude. Why the net rate as well: thirty discordances split evenly are noise
near boundaries; thirty all in one direction are a bias that moves every success rate computed on the weaker
placement. Why continuous outcomes too: a return can drift without any decision flipping. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the four criteria as exact, platform-independent computations; the verdict and its record; the
`insufficient-ensemble` outcome; the list of discordant worlds; the operating-characteristic helper used to
set thresholds. Out: per-world records (N35-09); freezing the thresholds (N35-11); running the test on real
placements and the gate (BD-177); the end-to-end script (N35-12).

## Technical approach

**Inputs.** n world records (the unit is the world: a world is *discordant* if any of its decisions differs),
of which k are discordant, b are P-favoured (every differing pass-or-fail decision passes on P and fails on
Q), c are Q-favoured and k − b − c are mixed. Thresholds `p_max = 1/100`, `net_max = 1/200`, `n_min_gate =
4096`, confidence 95%, read from the frozen threshold record.

**1. Discordance bound (exact binomial, Clopper–Pearson).** The one-sided 95% upper bound U(k, n) is the p
that solves Σ_{i=0..k} C(n, i)·pⁱ·(1 − p)^(n−i) = 0.05. For k = 0 it is 1 − 0.05^(1/n). The *criterion*
U(k, n) ≤ p_max is equivalent to F(k; n, p_max) ≤ 0.05, where F is the binomial distribution function, and is
decided in exact integer arithmetic: Σ_{i=0..k} C(n, i)·99^(n−i) · 20 ≤ 100ⁿ. The verdict is therefore class
D0. The printed value of U is obtained by bisection over dyadic rationals j/2⁴⁰ with F evaluated exactly, so it
is identical on every platform; it is printed with six significant digits and named "upper bound".

**2. Net one-sided bound (DESIGN estimator; the criterion is the program's, the estimator is frozen with
OPEN-NUM-2).** With U₉₇.₅ and L₉₇.₅ the one-sided 97.5% Clopper–Pearson upper and lower bounds (L₉₇.₅(0, n) =
0):

  U_net = max( U₉₇.₅(b, n) − L₉₇.₅(c, n),  U₉₇.₅(c, n) − L₉₇.₅(b, n) ).

Opposite endpoints of two 97.5% bounds give at least 95% simultaneous coverage for the difference of the two
directional rates (the same construction the fixed-n study type uses). Criterion: U_net ≤ net_max. Mixed
worlds count in k and in neither b nor c.

**3. Continuous outcomes (two one-sided tests).** For each metric with a ledger margin Δ > 0, over the worlds
scored on both placements, with paired differences dᵢ, mean d̄, sample standard deviation s and t the 95th
percentile of Student's distribution with n′ − 1 degrees of freedom (n′ the scored worlds; t = 1.6452 at
n′ = 4,096): equivalent iff d̄ − t·s/√n′ > −Δ and d̄ + t·s/√n′ < +Δ. Worlds not scored on both (for time to
failure: worlds in which either placement did not fail) are excluded and counted in the record.

**4. Rank agreement at the top.** If the ensemble holds at least two designs: Kendall τ between the two
placements' rankings restricted to the top band (the top m of either ranking, m = max(10, 3·k_keep), k_keep
the number of designs the ensemble's campaign keeps), with a bootstrap interval over world addresses (1,000
resamples drawn from an address-keyed stream, so the interval is reproducible), and top-k recall. Criterion
(DESIGN default, frozen with the thresholds): the lower bootstrap bound of τ is at least 0.7 and the two
placements name the same top design. Designs whose primary scores differ by less than the ledger's
equivalence margin for that score on *both* placements are treated as tied before ranking, so that a flip
between two designs neither placement can separate is not counted as a rank disagreement. With one design the
criterion is `not-applicable: single-design`; with fewer than five designs τ takes few values and the
criterion amounts to an identical order of the untied designs.

**Verdict.**
1. If n < n_min(p_max) = the smallest n with 1 − 0.05^(1/n) ≤ p_max (299 for 1%), or n is below the ensemble
   size the caller requires (4,096 for evidence at the gate): `insufficient-ensemble`. It is not a pass.
2. Otherwise `equivalent` if criteria 1 to 4 all hold (4 where applicable); else `not-equivalent`, listing the
   failing criteria.

**Record (`frob.equiv.verdict/1`).** n, k, U; b, c, mixed, U_net; per metric: n′, excluded with reasons, d̄,
s, the two one-sided bounds, Δ, pass or fail; τ with interval, recall, top-design agreement; the count of
`boundary-sensitive` decisions; the discordant worlds in address order, each with its decisions, margins and
first-divergence record identifier; the threshold record's identifier; the identities of both placements,
their profiles and fingerprints. Evidence class Tested; determinism D0 for the verdict and counts, D2 for the
floating summaries.

**Operating characteristic (helper for N35-11).** `oc(n, p_true, split)` returns the exact probability that
criteria 1 and 2 pass when each world is independently discordant with probability p_true and a discordant
world is P-favoured with probability `split`. It is a finite sum over (k, b) and is exact.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Criterion 1 is decided in exact integer arithmetic and agrees with the table: at n = 4,096, k = 30 passes and k = 31 fails; at n = 299, k = 0 passes; at n = 298, k = 0 fails.
- [ ] The printed bounds match the table to four significant digits and are bit-identical on aarch64 and x86-64.
- [ ] Criterion 2 agrees with the table: (11, 0) passes and (12, 0) fails; (15, 15) passes; (20, 10) fails.
- [ ] A planted bias of one decision in fifty on 4,096 worlds (82 one-sided discordances) returns `not-equivalent` with both criteria 1 and 2 listed as failing.
- [ ] n below 299, or below the caller's required ensemble size, returns `insufficient-ensemble` even with zero discordances.
- [ ] The two one-sided tests reproduce the hand cases in the unit tests, and exclusions are counted per metric with their reason.
- [ ] The rank criterion returns `not-applicable: single-design` for a one-design ensemble and never counts as a failure there.
- [ ] The record lists every discordant world in address order with margins and a first-divergence identifier, and names the threshold record it used; with no frozen threshold record the function refuses to return `equivalent` and returns the verdict labelled `provisional-thresholds`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `cp_upper_bound_table`: every U value of the table.
- `cp_criterion_is_exact_integer`: the inequality Σ C(n, i)·99^(n−i)·20 ≤ 100ⁿ is evaluated with big integers for (n, k) = (4096, 30) true and (4096, 31) false.
- `zero_discordant_small_n_is_insufficient`: n = 100, 200, 298 → `insufficient-ensemble`; n = 299 with the caller's minimum set to 299 → criterion 1 passes.
- `net_bound_table`: every U_net value of the table.
- `planted_one_in_fifty_fails`: k = b = 82, c = 0, n = 4,096 → `not-equivalent`.
- `mixed_worlds_count_in_k_only`: k = 5, b = 1, c = 1, three mixed → U uses 5; U_net uses (1, 1).
- `tost_cases`: n′ = 4,096, s = Δ: d̄ = 0.2·Δ → bounds (0.1743·Δ, 0.2257·Δ), equivalent; d̄ = 0.99·Δ → upper 1.0157·Δ, not equivalent; d̄ = −1.2·Δ → not equivalent.
- `tost_exclusions_counted`: 100 of 4,096 worlds without a failure on one placement → time-to-failure uses n′ = 3,996 and reports 100 excluded.
- `rank_single_design_not_applicable`; `rank_top_band_tau`: two rankings of 12 designs identical in the top band → τ = 1; one adjacent swap among 10 → τ = 1 − 2·1/45 = 0.9556.
- `oc_is_a_probability` (property): 0 ≤ oc ≤ 1; oc is non-increasing in p_true for fixed n and split = ½.
- `bound_monotone` (property): U(k, n) increases with k and decreases with n.
- `coverage_simulation`: 10⁴ simulated ensembles of n = 4,096 at true p = 1% (address-keyed draws): the fraction with U < 1% is at most 5% plus three binomial standard errors.

### End-to-end script and logging
Driven by `scripts/e2e/num_decision_equivalence.sh` (bead N35-12), which runs the verdict on real placement
pairs and on the planted bias and logs, through the STD-E2E helper, `metric` lines `n`, `k`, `U` against 1%,
`b`, `c`, `U_net` against 0.5%, each metric's two one-sided bounds against ±Δ, τ with its interval, and the
verdict; on `not-equivalent` the failing criteria and the first three discordant world addresses with their
margins and record paths.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-789: the bound must be at most 1%; zero discordant worlds give about 3/n (exactly 299 worlds are the
  minimum; the round figure is 300); 4,096 worlds admit thirty; net rate at most 0.5%. Negative control: the
  planted bias of one decision in fifty must fail.
- CX-365: zero discordant decisions on a small ensemble do not show equivalence.
- Negative controls of this bead: a variant that uses the point estimate k/n in place of the bound (it passes
  k = 40 at n = 4,096, since 40/4,096 = 0.98%; `cp_criterion_is_exact_integer` and the table catch it); a
  variant that tests only criterion 1 (it passes (30, 0); `net_bound_table` catches it).
- Determinism lanes: all criteria twice, across worker counts, debug and release, and across the two CPU
  architectures: identical records.

## Considerations

- The estimator for the net bound is conservative: thirty discordances split 20 to 10 fail it. That is
  intended at the default thresholds, and it is the estimator, not the criterion, that OPEN-NUM-2's decision
  may change; the operating-characteristic helper shows the consequence of any choice before it is frozen.
- Decisions inside one world are not independent; taking the world as the unit avoids pretending they are.
  Worlds are independent given their addresses.
- A pair that is `not-equivalent` is not a defect report by itself: under kill gate KILL-D1's fallback a
  family may legitimately be class D3 against others. The consequence is that results from the weaker
  placement cannot be promoted or paired against the other, which the licence table enforces.
