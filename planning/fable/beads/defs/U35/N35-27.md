## Background

**A number never claims more than its certificate.** A simulated quantity (a final position, a success rate, a
peak torque) is only as good as the numerical conditions under which it was computed: did the solver converge,
how badly were constraints violated, how ill-conditioned was the contact system, how often did a guarded
comparison have to fall back to exact arithmetic, how close did buffers come to overflowing, and on which
machine and under which profile did it run. The program's rule for every capability is that no naked number
reaches an agent: outputs carry the kind of claim, an evidence class, a tier, a determinism class, identities,
a seed and the no-authorization flags. This chapter adds the numerical side of that record.

Every numerical result records what bounds it: solver gradient norm and iteration count, maximum constraint
and closure violation, group-membership error, the energy-ledger residual, the number of worlds marked
`Degraded` or `Faulted`. A summary statistic over worlds states how many worlds it excludes and why (N35-28).

Added to the record of every step and every result (DESIGN):

- the conditioning lower bound κ̂ and κ̂·u, the precision profile and the refinement steps taken;
- the fallback fraction of each guarded predicate;
- capacity headroom: the largest active contact and row counts against their capacities;
- the determinism class, the profile, the placement receipt and the fingerprint digest;
- for a decision, its margin.

(κ̂ = (max L̂_ii / min L̂_ii)² is read off the Cholesky factor of the power-of-two-scaled contact matrix and is a
lower bound on its 2-norm condition number; u = 2⁻²⁴ is the single-precision unit round-off. A guarded
predicate decides a strict comparison from an `f32` residual with a proved error bound and defers to exact
integer arithmetic when the residual is inside the bound. Headroom is the gap between what a padded buffer
held at most and its capacity.)

Each of these quantities is produced by some other component. This bead defines the one block that carries
them, the order-independent way they are reduced from worlds to a run, and the checks that make the block
mandatory. Without it the failure-mode indicators the program watches (the share of worlds marked
`Degraded: ill-conditioned`; the guarded-predicate fallback rate) would have no home. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the block's schema at step (chunk) level and at result level; reduction from per-world values; where the
block is attached; the registry lint that makes it mandatory; the rule linking reported numbers to their
bounds. Out: producing each raw quantity (owners below); summary exclusions and the wording rules (N35-28);
the performance disclosure block, which quotes some of these fields (the performance harness).

## Technical approach

**Schema (DESIGN; `frob.numcert/1`; the `numerics` object of a chunk receipt and of a result envelope).**

```text
solver        grad_norm_max        largest scaled gradient norm at exit, over worlds and steps (dimensionless, scaled variables)
              iterations           the shape class's constant iteration count; refinement_steps (0 or 1)
violations    closure_max          largest loop-closure residual (m)
              penetration_max      largest penetration beyond the contact law's compliance (m)
              complementarity_max  largest complementarity residual, as a fraction of the normal impulse
              constraint_max       largest violation of any other equality constraint, with its unit
group         membership_ulp_max   largest | ‖q‖² − 1 | (c² + s² − 1 for a unit complex joint coordinate) in units in the last place at 1
energy        rho_max              largest per-world relative ledger residual ρ = |R| / W over the horizon (R: the summed
                                   step residual; W: the summed absolute work exchanged; N21bp2-01's measure)
              zero_work_worlds     count of worlds with W = 0, excluded from rho_max and always printed
worlds        total; degraded { ill-conditioned, capacity-contacts, capacity-rows, … : count }; faulted { cause : count }
conditioning  kappa_lower_bound_max; kappa_u_max; kappa_u_hist (integer histogram of κ̂·u over worlds and steps)
              precision_profile    default | stiff;  width  f32 | f64 (the element type of the step, N35-04)
guarded       [ { predicate, evaluations, fallbacks, fallback_fraction } ]      one entry per guarded predicate in the plan
headroom      contacts { max_active, capacity }; rows { max_active, capacity }
determinism   class; profile; pins_digest; placement_receipt; fingerprint_digest
decisions     [ { id, value, margin, unit } ]                                    present when the result contains decisions
```

**Producers (owner of each raw quantity).** Gradient norm and iterations: the solve stage (BD-156). Violations
and group error: the batched step (BD-159). Energy residual: the batched energy ledger (BD-208). Status words:
the batched step. κ̂ and κ̂·u: the conditioning record of the solve stage. Guard evaluations and fallbacks: the
guarded kernels (BD-182). Active counts: the compaction and row kernels. Class, profile and pins: N35-01,
N35-04. Placement receipt and fingerprint: the placement planner (BD-178). Margins: decision records.

**Reduction (must not depend on slot, batch, shard or arrival).**
1. Per world and step, kernels write the scalars into the telemetry slab of the lowest telemetry level, which
   is always on.
2. Per chunk, the host reduces over worlds and steps with operations that are exact and order-independent:
   maxima by `det_max` (no NaN can be present: a NaN faults the world and is counted there), integer counts
   by addition, histograms by integer bin addition. No floating sum is used anywhere in the block, so the
   block is identical however the worlds were arranged. The energy residual is the ledger's own figure, which
   is accumulated in double-float pairs in a fixed order.
3. Per result, chunk blocks are merged by the same operations. Shards merge exactly.
4. Per-world values remain queryable at the telemetry level that stores them; the block is the always-present
   summary.

**Attachment.** Every chunk receipt carries the block for its chunk. Every result envelope of a verb that
ran physics (`sim.run`, `sim.replay`, `eval.run`, `train.start` status and final receipt, `run.fork`, the
check verbs) carries the merged block. A verb whose output is derived from runs (a comparison, a scorecard)
carries the block's `worlds`, `conditioning.kappa_u_max`, `guarded` and `determinism` parts merged over its
inputs, and links to each input's full block.

**Mandatory by construction.** A registry lint over `registries/verbs.toml` and the generated schemas fails
any verb whose output schema contains a floating-point result field and no `numerics` reference. At run time
the envelope writer refuses to serialize such a result without the block (an internal integrity fault, since
it can only be a programming error).

**Linking numbers to bounds.** Each floating output field's schema names the parts of the block that bound it
(`bounded_by: ["violations", "conditioning", "determinism"]`); `frob explain` style renderers print the bound
beside the number. A lower bound is called a lower bound in the field name (`kappa_lower_bound_max`); wording
is enforced by N35-28.

**Evidence and class.** The block's integers are class D0; its maxima are class D2 and, being maxima of
kernel outputs, are bit-identical across arrangements of one device family. The block is evidence of class
Tested about the run that produced it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob.numcert/1` is registered; every chunk receipt and every physics-running verb's envelope contains the block with every field populated or explicitly `not-applicable` with a reason (for example `guarded: []` for a plan with no guarded predicate).
- [ ] The block of a run is bit-identical when the same worlds are run in permuted slots, in batches of different size, at two capacities and on one or two shards (the invariance arrangements), on `batch-cpu` and on each device family.
- [ ] A run in which one world's κ̂·u exceeds the limit reports that world under `degraded.ill-conditioned`, `kappa_u_max` above the limit, and the precision profile and refinement steps in force.
- [ ] A guarded predicate's `fallback_fraction` equals fallbacks/evaluations computed from integer counts, and matches an independent host recount on a fixture of 65,536 rows.
- [ ] Headroom reports the largest active contact and row counts with their capacities; on a fixture built to fill 31 of 32 contact slots it reports 31 and 32.
- [ ] A planted verb whose output schema has a floating result and no `numerics` reference fails the registry lint; a planted envelope without the block fails serialization.
- [ ] A decision output carries its margin with a unit.
- [ ] The block carries, as running maxima over a run's worlds and steps, each measure the precision battery (BD-179) and the energy measurement (N21bp2-01) read: `membership_ulp_max`, `closure_max`, `penetration_max` and `complementarity_max` as separate fields, and `rho_max` with `zero_work_worlds`; a world with W = 0 is excluded from `rho_max` and counted, never dropped silently.
- [ ] The block is reachable through `frob --json` and the generated MCP tools, with the same schema.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
- `numcert_schema_fields`: the nine groups are present and typed; `kappa_lower_bound_max` is the only name under which κ̂ appears.
- `reduce_is_order_independent` (property, 10⁴ cases): per-world records in random order, split into 1 to 8 parts and merged in random order, give an identical block.
- `reduce_uses_no_float_sum`: the reduction's code path contains only maxima, integer adds and histogram merges (asserted by a small audit of the function's operations in the kernel IR form, and by the property test above on adversarial magnitudes 10⁸, −10⁸, 1).
- `fallback_fraction_from_counts`: 7,282 fallbacks in 65,536 evaluations → 0.1111 (and the complementary 58,254 decided on the device).
- `headroom_fixture`: 31 of 32.
- `energy_rho_excludes_zero_work`: three worlds with (R, W) = (10⁻⁶ J, 1 J), (0, 0) and (2·10⁻⁵ J, 0.5 J) give `rho_max` = 4·10⁻⁵ and `zero_work_worlds` = 1; a conformance variant that counts the W = 0 world as ρ = 0 without counting it fails.
- `contact_violations_separate`: a synthetic slab with penetration beyond compliance 3·2⁻¹⁸ m and complementarity residual 2⁻¹⁷ of the normal impulse in different worlds reports both maxima in their own fields, neither folded into `constraint_max`.
- `degraded_counts_by_reason`: a synthetic status slab (labelled `synthetic`) with 3 ill-conditioned, 2 capacity-contacts and 1 faulted world.
- `registry_lint_requires_numerics`; `envelope_writer_requires_block`.
- `derived_verb_merges_parts`: a comparison over two runs carries the merged `worlds`, `kappa_u_max`, `guarded` and the weakest determinism class.

### End-to-end script and logging
This bead's own lane is `scripts/e2e/num_certificate.sh [--tier batch-cpu|gpu-cuda|gpu-metal|all] --seed <s>`:
(1) `arrangements`: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<s> cargo test -p frob-chronicle --release --test numcert_e2e`, with
`FROB_TEST_DEVICE=<id>` on each device present: the batched step of workload Q on 256 world addresses as one
batch, in three seeded slot permutations, inside a batch of 1,024 beside seeded co-tenants, at two capacities
and on one and two shards, asserting the 256 worlds' merged blocks equal field for field; the ill-conditioned
fixture, the 31-of-32 headroom fixture and the 65,536-row guarded fixture with its independent host recount;
(2) `envelope`: `frob sim run <fixture> --tier <t> --worlds 256 --seconds 2 --seed <s> --key
nc-run-<t>-<run-id> --json` and `frob sim replay --run <r> --world 0 --key nc-replay-<t>-<run-id> --json`,
asserting each envelope carries `numerics` with all nine groups populated or `not-applicable` with a reason, and
that each chunk receipt carries its chunk's block; (3) `degraded`: the ill-conditioned fixture through `frob sim
run <fixture> --tier <t> --on-degraded continue-flagged --seed <s> --key nc-degraded-<t>-<run-id> --json`,
asserting the `degraded.ill-conditioned` count, `kappa_u_max` above the frozen limit and the precision profile
and refinement steps in force; (4) `lint`: `cargo test -p frob-verbs --release -- registry_lint_requires_numerics
envelope_writer_requires_block`. It logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/num_certificate/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`; `cmd` lines with exit code, wall time and the evidence block; the full block as `metric` lines (each
field with its unit and, where one exists, its threshold: group error against 4 ulp, `rho_max` against 10⁻⁴
with `zero_work_worlds` printed, κ̂·u against the frozen limit); `assert` lines for block equality across arrangements with the differing
field on failure; the chunk receipt paths; a `skip` event for an absent device, never counted as passed; a
`summary` line. The later lane is `scripts/e2e/num_profiles.sh` (N35-31), step `certificate`.

### Known answers and negative controls
- KA-799 (κ̂ is a lower bound and is named one); the guarded-kernel pilot's figure (58,254 of 65,536 rows of
  the widely spaced grid decided on the device, the rest deferred to exact arithmetic, zero final errors).
- Negative controls: a reduction that averages κ̂ over worlds with a floating sum (the block then changes
  with slot order; `reduce_is_order_independent` fails); an envelope writer that fills a missing field with
  zero (a zero energy residual where the ledger did not run; the `not-applicable` rule and its test catch it).
- Metamorphic: permuting worlds, adding inactive worlds or unused capacity changes no field of the block.
- Determinism lanes: the block twice on one machine; across worker counts on `batch-cpu`; per device family
  under the invariance arrangements.

## Considerations

- Cost: the block adds a few scalars per world and step to the always-on telemetry level; the performance
  harness measures that level's cost, and the fields were chosen to be available from values the kernels
  already compute.
- The block reports maxima, which hide distributions; the histogram of κ̂·u is included because the share of
  ill-conditioned worlds is a watched indicator, and other distributions are available through telemetry
  histograms.
- OPEN-NUM-4 (the limit for `Degraded: ill-conditioned`; default κ̂·u above 0.1) is read from its frozen
  record; the block reports the measured maximum regardless of the limit.
- A referee sampler computes the true condition number in double precision for sampled worlds; the ledger
  holds both figures, and this block carries only the lower bound that is available for every world.
