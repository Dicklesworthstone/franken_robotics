## Background

The program's thesis is a ratio, and a ratio needs a measured denominator. The table from which the plan argues
composes **published** figures from different incumbent stacks; it is not a measurement of any loop. So the first
performance work of the project measures the incumbents themselves, on our own hardware, by phase, starting with
the phases that need no agent. This is method rule "measure the incumbent first", and it is an M0 exit condition:
with no incumbent phase measurement on record nothing is confirmable, the thesis arithmetic stays a HYPOTHESIS and
no work package is funded as a speed lever.

This bead is the M0 half: the rows on `trj` (the Linux host with two RTX 4090 cards of 24 GB, no link between
them, often heavily loaded). The Mac rows and the native-settings rows are the M1 half (BD-142). Nothing of our
own engine is measured here; no FrankenRobotics simulation code is needed, only the out-of-process incumbent
harness, the unit and disclosure types and the registries.

Terms. *Workloads*: Q is the reference quadruped (18 degrees of freedom, 13 bodies, step h = 2 ms, decimation
d = 10, that is 50 Hz control); H is the reference humanoid (35 degrees of freedom, 30 bodies, same h and d).
*World-step*: one physics step of one world. *Env-step*: one control tick of one world, d world-steps plus
observation, reward and reset. *Matched settings*: the incumbent run at our h, d and iteration count where its
solver allows. *Cold* and *warm*: with an empty compile cache, and with its caches populated. *Phase ledger*: a
list of timed intervals, each assigned to a phase (`compile`, `train`, `wait`, …), that sums to the clock.

## Scope

In: the M0 campaign on `trj`, its repeats and order, the reproduction judgement for each published figure, the
written explanations, the first phase ledger, the updated target register. Out: the incumbent harness and pins
(BD-039, N36-25); the script (N36-29); the Mac and native-settings rows (BD-142); the agent-driven design round
(BD-143); any measurement of our engine (M2).

## Technical approach

**Rows measured (all on `trj`, each under a complete disclosure block, each with the comparator's frozen strongest
warm configuration unless the row is a cold row).**

| Row | What is timed | Repeats and statistics | Published context (FACT, to re-read) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold start | Process start to the first completed device step, with an empty cache, then with a warm cache; reported separately | 5 cold, 10 warm; median and range | One stack: just-in-time compilation "can be slow (1-3 minutes)"; a second compiles at first launch, "can take a minute", and caches on disk; a third starts in 4.4 to 6.3 s with cached shaders and 263 s without. MuJoCo Warp's cold kernel compilation takes about 96 s on an RTX 6000 Ada and its cached start about 0.9 s |
| Edit to first step, parameter edit | Acceptance of a one-parameter model edit (a link length or a mass) to completion of the first device step of the edited model | 20 edits; 50th and 95th percentiles | None published. An incumbent model edit needs a host compile, a model upload and a new graph capture |
| Edit to first step, topology edit | The same for an edit that adds or removes a link | 20 edits; 50th and 95th percentiles | None published |
| Physics, Q and H | World-steps/s of physics only, one RTX 4090, at 1, 8, 64, 256 worlds and upward to 8,192, matched settings | 10 interleaved repeats per size; median, 99th-percentile step time, peak memory | 4.8 M world-steps/s for a quadruped at d = 4 on an RTX 5090 at 4,096 worlds; 2.55 M humanoid world-steps/s at 8,192 worlds on an RTX 6000 Ada, 1.68 M on a heightfield; 88.4 kB per humanoid world |
| Full step, Q and H | Env-steps/s of the full environment step without policy, same hardware and sizes | As above | 347 k env-steps/s against 1.20 M for physics (71% overhead) on the RTX 5090 benchmark |
| Training rate, Q | Env-steps/s of step, step with inference, step with inference and training | 5 repeats | 94 k, 88 k and 82 k for a humanoid rough task at 4,096 worlds on one RTX 4090 (another stack); 417 k quadruped training on an A100 at d = 5 |
| Time to threshold, one card | Wall-clock from cold start to the return threshold of the incumbent's flat velocity task, compilation included | 5 runs; median and range; compile region reported | About 410 s (read by eye from a figure, ± 20 s), of which about 45 s is compilation |
| Time to threshold, two cards | The same job on both cards | 5 runs | About 325 s; ratio 1.26 |

**Procedure, numbered.**
1. Freeze first: the comparator registry is decided and frozen (N36-25); the harness identity is printed.
2. Admission: each run passes the compute-envelope check. Rows that fit the default class (one device, one hour)
   run first; the two-card row needs the gate-measurement class.
3. Run `scripts/e2e/perf_parity_baseline.sh --milestone m0 --machine trj --seed <s>`. Order within a row is
   randomized; host load is recorded for every repeat (`trj` is often loaded; the load is recorded, never
   corrected for).
4. Timers cover completion, never enqueue: a rate is completed steps over wall-clock from submission to
   completion, and steps of failed or invalid worlds are not in the numerator.
5. Phase ledger: for each timed training run, intervals for `compile`, `train` (with the learner's share where the
   stack exposes it) and `wait`; exclusive phases must sum to the clock within 2%.
6. Reproduction judgement, per published figure: reproduced if measured ÷ published is inside [0.8, 1.2].
   Otherwise an explanation is written in `docs/perf/explanations/<figure>.md` stating the cause (different card,
   different world count, different decimation, different revision, host load) with the evidence, and the
   measured figure replaces the published one as the planning value in the target register. Figures published on
   hardware we do not have (an RTX 5090, an RTX 6000 Ada, an A100) need an explanation by construction unless
   they happen to fall inside the band.
7. Derived outputs recorded with the baseline: the incumbent's own two-card strong scaling (time on one card ÷
   time on two) with its spread; the step's share of training from the nested rates; the seven Amdahl limits on
   measured inputs; the family-batch bound from the measured switch cost (the edit-to-first-step rows).
8. Record: the M0 part of the GATE-SPARK-21 record with measured values; the ten-fold test's parity-baseline
   gate reads the same rows.

**Evidence.** Claim kind Parity, class `Tested`, for "published figure reproduced on our hardware"; the rates
themselves are Performance figures of the incumbents under their disclosure blocks.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The `trj` rows of GATE-SPARK-21 are recorded under the disclosure block (seed done-condition): cold start, both edit rows, physics and full step for Q and H at matched settings across sizes, time to threshold on one card and, where the envelope admits it, on two.
- [ ] Each published figure of the target table that these rows touch is reproduced within 20% or the difference is explained in writing (seed done-condition), and the target register carries the measured value.
- [ ] Every row has a closed phase ledger or is flagged `ledger-open`; cold and warm are never mixed in one row.
- [ ] The incumbent's two-card ratio is on record with its spread, and the pilot of the sharding kill gate can read it.
- [ ] The Amdahl limits and the family-batch bound are recomputed from measured inputs and printed beside the published ones; any single-phase limit that reaches 10 is flagged.
- [ ] A row not run for lack of an approved envelope class is recorded `not run` with the reason; nothing is omitted.
- [ ] The M0 stop rule can be evaluated from the record: if the measured ledger puts every ceiling of the thesis under 3 for every mission class, the thesis arithmetic is rewritten before any work is funded against it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance perf::baseline_m0`:
- `reproduction_judgement`: (published 410, measured 470) → inside (1.146); (410, 500) → outside (1.22), explanation required.
- `two_card_ratio`: 410 ÷ 325 = 1.26; a measured pair (430, 340) → 1.26.
- `compile_share`: 45 ÷ 410 = 0.11.
- `step_share_from_measured_triple`: uses the calculator of N36-04 on a recorded triple.
- `explanation_required_for_other_hardware`: a figure whose published hardware differs and whose ratio is outside the band fails without an explanation file.
- `planning_value_replaced`: after recording, the target register returns the measured value with status `tolerance`.
- `size_sweep_complete`: a physics row missing the 64-world point fails the completeness check; a size that does not fit in memory is present and marked.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/perf_parity_baseline.sh --milestone m0 --machine trj` (N36-29). It logs one `metric` line per
repeat with comparator and revision, settings, world count, the rate with unit and decimation or the latency
percentiles, host load, the disclosure block identifier, and the published value with the ratio; and a `summary`
with rows measured, rows `not run` and `gates: ["GATE-SPARK-21"]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Arithmetic known answers on the published fixture: 1.26; 0.711 overhead (1 − 347/1,200); 0.872 step share; KA-818 to KA-820.
- Negative controls of the gate (registered by N36-29): a row without its block; a figure 1.5 times off with no explanation; a revision changed under an unchanged identity; cold merged into warm.
- Sanity control on the measurement itself: the same incumbent configuration measured twice, back to back (an A/A pair), must agree within the spread recorded for the row; a larger gap marks the row `noisy` and it is repeated.

## Considerations

- RISK-25 (the denominator is wrong: incumbents are faster, or their loop is shorter, than the published composite; likelihood high). This bead is where that is found out; the finding changes the ranking of levers, the funding of work packages and the expected ranges, and never the estimand or the outcome rules of the ten-fold test.
- Pre-mortem entries served: "nothing confirmable existed" (early sign: no incumbent phase measurement on record at M0 exit) and "the ten-fold claim failed on quadruped missions because incumbents already train in minutes" (early sign: time to threshold near the published 410 s).
- RISK-09 (`trj` loaded) and RISK-10 (no envelope approved): single-card rows fit the default; the two-card row waits for the gate-measurement class.
- This measurement closes another chapter's open question (whether the incumbent wall-clock used by the training-time parity gate is re-measured on our card before M2): after this bead it is.
- Published time-to-threshold figures were read by eye from a figure (± 20 s); a 20% band is wider than that reading error.
