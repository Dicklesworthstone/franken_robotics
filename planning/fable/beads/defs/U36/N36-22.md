## Background

**ADR-PERF-03**: every target is a tolerance or an ambition and says which. A tolerance is binding: leaving it has
a consequence. Most rows of the target table are carried by a gate that measures them (the throughput gates, the
training-time parity gate, the scaling gate, the variants gate, the sampled-referee gate). Three rows have a
tolerance and no gate of their own. A tolerance nobody measures is not a tolerance, so this bead measures them
under the performance harness and records each with its verdict in the target register.

| # | Workload, tier and hardware | Unit | Tolerance (binding) | Ambition | Incumbent reference (FACT, to re-read) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Reference humanoid, time to the return threshold of a first-stage flat-ground task, two RTX 4090 | Minutes of wall-clock; decimation 10 | At most 1.5 times the incumbent reproduced on `trj`; planning value 45 min | At most 30 min | MuJoCo Playground: under 30 minutes on two RTX 4090 for two humanoids (under 15 minutes for a third). Isaac Lab's default rough-terrain humanoid run is about 1 h on one RTX 4090 (derived from its published rate) |
| 16 | Referee, humanoid, one world, one CPU core | Multiple of real time; no decimation applies | At least 0.2 | At least 1.0 | — |
| 18 | Height scan, 187 rays per world at 50 Hz, any device | Added cost per env-step at decimation 10 | At most 15% | At most 8% | — |

Why each matters. Row 9 is the humanoid counterpart of the quadruped training-time row: the dominant phase of a
humanoid design loop is training (over 90% of a composite iteration of about 31 minutes), so the engine must not
make it worse than 1.5 times the incumbent. Row 16 sets how much of a device run the trusted tier can adjudicate:
at 0.2 times real time per core a referee on 24 cores covers about 0.14% of a humanoid device run, at 1.0 about
0.7%, and the ambition of shadowing 1% needs 1.0 on all 36 cores. Row 18 bounds the cost of the most common
exteroceptive observation in legged locomotion; if a height scan costs more than 15% of an env-step, rough-terrain
training rows inherit a penalty no kernel work on the step can remove.

Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the three campaigns, their comparators, verdicts and records. Out: the humanoid task definition, the referee
and the ray kernels (their own beads); the quadruped training-time gate and the scaling gate; row 17 (the
sampled-referee gate) and row 19 (N36-23).

## Technical approach

**Row 9, numbered.**
1. Comparator: the incumbent's humanoid first-stage flat-ground recipe on both cards of `trj`, re-measured in the
   parity baseline (time to its threshold, compile region reported). If the baseline could not run it, the row is
   judged against the planning value of 45 minutes with status `interim-floor`, never as a tolerance.
2. Our arm: PPO on the reference humanoid's first-stage flat-ground task from a cold start, two cards
   (synchronous shards under the global normalization rule, or one card if the sharding kill gate fired; the
   record states which and the row's hardware column is then "one RTX 4090" with its own incumbent comparator).
3. Times are read under the attainment rule (held and confirmed by evaluation), with equal task quality judged by
   re-evaluation of the final policies.
4. Ratio = incumbent time ÷ our time, at least ten pairs where the compute envelope allows; verdict on the paired
   lower bound against 1/1.5 = 0.667. Each pair costs about (30 + 45) minutes × 2 devices = 2.5 device-hours, so
   ten pairs exceed the 12 device-hours of one gate measurement; the campaign is declared with five pairs and the
   record marks the row `not judged (fewer than ten pairs)` with the five ratios printed, unless a larger budget
   is approved for it.

**Row 16.** `frob sim run --tier referee --workload H --worlds 1 --seconds 20 --seed <s> --json` pinned to one
core, on the Mac after the thermal soak and on `trj`: real-time multiple = simulated seconds ÷ wall-clock seconds
from submission to completion. Scenarios: standing, walking under the baseline controller, and a push recovery
(contact-rich), ten repeats each; the figure is the median per scenario and the row's value is the minimum over
the three scenarios (the referee is event-located, so contact-rich motion is slower). Verdict against 0.2 and 1.0.
A scenario the referee refuses (its contact cap) is recorded as refused, not as zero.

**Row 18.** Paired campaign on each device and on `batch-cpu`: arm A, the quadruped full environment step with the
187-ray height scan at 50 Hz; arm B, the same with proprioception only; 8,192 worlds, at least ten pairs. Added
cost = (time per env-step of A) ÷ (time per env-step of B) − 1; verdict on the one-sided upper bound of the cost
(the paired lower bound of B's time over A's, inverted) against 0.15 and 0.08. Run on flat ground and on rough
terrain, since ray traversal cost depends on the scene; the row's value is the larger.

**Records.** Each row gets a report under the harness (complete disclosure blocks, pairs record where a ratio is
stated) and a line in the target register: measured value, verdict (`inside tolerance`, `outside tolerance`,
`ambition met`, `not judged`, `not run` with reason), and the consequence for an outside-tolerance row: a recorded
defect with an owner and one optimization pass, under the same ledger as any other optimization.

**Evidence.** Performance claims of class `Tested` for our figures; row 9's ratio additionally needs the
incumbent's disclosure block.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Row 16 is measured on both machines for the three scenarios with its verdict against 0.2 and 1.0, and the value is the minimum over scenarios.
- [ ] Row 18 is measured on each available tier, flat and rough, with the upper bound of the added cost against 0.15 and 0.08.
- [ ] Row 9 is measured with the configuration stated (two cards or the fallback), under attainment and equal quality, against the re-measured incumbent; with fewer than ten pairs it is recorded `not judged` with its ratios; under the default envelope it is `not run` with the reason.
- [ ] Each row's result is written to the target register with its status; no row of the table is left without a carrier and a record.
- [ ] An outside-tolerance result opens a recorded defect that names the row, the bound and the next action.
- [ ] Every figure has a complete disclosure block; a rate or cost stated without one is refused.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance perf::other_rows`:
- `real_time_multiple`: 20 simulated seconds in 80 s of wall-clock → 0.25 (inside tolerance, ambition missed).
- `row16_min_over_scenarios`: (0.9, 0.5, 0.15) → 0.15, outside tolerance.
- `refused_scenario_is_not_zero`.
- `height_scan_cost`: env-step 3.4 µs with the scan against 3.0 µs without → 13.3%; upper bound 16% → outside tolerance even though the mean is inside.
- `row9_orientation`: incumbent 28 min, ours 40 min → 0.70 ≥ 0.667 inside; ours 45 min → 0.622 outside.
- `row9_requires_ten_pairs_to_judge`.
- `row9_configuration_recorded`: two cards or one, and the comparator matches it.
- `interim_floor_is_not_tolerance`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Three steps are added to `scripts/e2e/spark_parity_rows.sh` (owned by the throughput-rows bead): `row_16`,
`row_18` and `row_9`, each admitted through the compute-envelope check. Logged per repeat or pair: scenario or
arm, the figure with its unit, the pair's order and host state, block identifiers; per row the bound, the
thresholds and the verdict; rows not run are listed with reasons in the `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Arithmetic checks: 24 × 0.2 ÷ 3,400 = 0.14% and 36 × 1.0 ÷ 3,400 = 1.06% (the coverage consequences of row 16).
- Negative controls: a referee timing that excludes model loading (the rate must be submission to completion); a height-scan comparison whose two arms differ in world count (rejected by the block diff); a row 9 time read at first crossing.
- A/A control for row 18: the proprioception-only arm against itself must not show a cost whose upper bound exceeds 2%.

## Considerations

- Row 9 is the costliest row of the table to measure and the first to be cut by a tight compute envelope; `not judged` and `not run` are honest outcomes and are listed in the exit review.
- A humanoid first-stage flat-ground task may not exist at M2 (the M2 task families are quadruped velocity tracking and terrain traversal). If it does not, row 9 is recorded `not run: task not available` at M2 and measured at the first milestone that has the task; the bead is not closed on the M2 record alone.
- An open question of the verification chapter (whether 10⁴ scenarios per metamorphic relation per tier per release is affordable for relations that need the referee) is answered by row 16's measured speed: at 0.2 times real time a 10-second scenario costs 50 core-seconds, so 10⁴ scenarios cost about 139 core-hours per relation.
- Row 18 at 187 rays is one layout; denser scans and depth images scale with ray count and are covered by the sensing chapter's own gates.
