## Background

The studio's promise to an agent is a short design cycle: check a design, synthesize a controller without
training, simulate, explain, edit. The plan's arithmetic for that cycle (a design-check-simulate-explain-edit
cycle of 120 s with no training in it is 3.0 to 3.5 times faster than an incumbent quadruped cycle of 360 to
420 s) holds only if the CPU-side steps of the cycle are themselves fast. Row 19 of the target table states what
"fast" means for three of them:

| # | Operation, on CPU | Unit | Tolerance (binding) | Ambition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 19a | `design check` | Seconds; no decimation applies | Under 2 s at the 95th percentile | After a one-parameter edit, with the memo graph: 0.2, 0.5 and 1.0 s at the 50th, 95th and 99th percentiles |
| 19b | `control synth` | Seconds | Under 5 s | — |
| 19c | Interior scene generate and compile | Seconds | Under 60 s | — |

These have no gate in the performance chapter, and ADR-PERF-03 says a tolerance must be measured. This bead
measures them under the performance harness when all three exist (M3: `design check` and `control synth` exist
from M1, interior generation from M3), records the verdicts in the target register and adds them to the
regression lane. It is CPU work that fits the default compute envelope (8 cores, 16 GiB, under an hour).

Why the percentiles matter: an agent's turn is bounded by the slowest step it waits for, and a check that is
usually 0.3 s and sometimes 8 s is felt as 8 s. Tier: Solid.

## Scope

In: the three latency campaigns, their workloads, verdicts, records and smoke rows. Out: making any of the three
faster (their own crates); the device-side edit-to-first-step row (N36-21); the full design-cycle time of the
ten-fold test.

## Technical approach

**Timing rule.** Latency = wall-clock from submission of the verb to receipt of its envelope (completion, never
enqueue), measured by the harness driving the real binary: `frob design check <design> --json`, `frob control
synth <design> --task <task> --json`, `frob scene gen <archetype> --seed <s> --json` followed by `frob scene
compile <scene> --json`. Each run records cold or warm (process start and caches), threads used, and the memo
store's state; cold and warm are never mixed in one percentile.

**19a, `design check`, numbered.**
1. Workload: 200 designs from the design corpus, covering the archetypes that exist (quadruped, humanoid, wheeled
   bases, mobile manipulator), each checked once from a warm process with an empty memo store ("full check").
2. Then for each design a sequence of 20 one-parameter edits (a link length, a gear ratio, a mass), each followed
   by a re-check with the memo graph populated ("incremental check").
3. Report the 50th, 95th and 99th percentiles of both distributions. Verdicts: full check, 95th percentile under
   2 s (tolerance); incremental check, 0.2, 0.5 and 1.0 s at the three percentiles (ambition).
4. The record states the fraction of the check's stages that were served from the memo graph on incremental
   checks; an incremental check that recomputes everything is flagged, since its time is then a full check's.

**19b, `control synth`.** Workload: the baseline controller of each archetype that has one, for each of 50 corpus
designs of that archetype, warm process. Report percentiles; verdict: 95th percentile under 5 s (the row states
"under 5 s"; the percentile is this bead's reading, chosen to match 19a, and the maximum is reported beside it).

**19c, interior scene generate and compile.** Workload: each of the seven interior archetypes at its default size
(a home of about 300 m³ at 2 cm spacing as the reference point), 10 seeds each, warm process, the scene compiler
on 8 threads. Latency = generation plus compilation to a scene pack ready for upload. Verdict: 95th percentile
under 60 s; larger scenes (warehouse, building) are timed and reported with their volume and spacing, not judged
against 60 s.

**Scaling with size.** For each operation the latency is also recorded against a size measure (links for 19a and
19b, voxels for 19c), so that a regression can be told from a larger input.

**Records and regression.** Each sub-row gets a harness report with a disclosure block (the fields that do not
apply to a CPU verb are marked not applicable with the reason; hardware, threads, cold or warm, cache state, host
load, percentiles and peak memory are filled) and a line in the target register. The three operations join the
regression lane as smoke rows (a paired before-and-after on a fixed subset of 20 designs and 3 scenes).

**Evidence.** Performance claims of class `Tested` on named hardware; percentiles read from recorded samples are
reported with their sample counts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `design check` full and incremental latencies are recorded at the 50th, 95th and 99th percentiles on both machines, with the memo hit fraction, and judged against 2 s (tolerance) and 0.2, 0.5, 1.0 s (ambition).
- [ ] `control synth` latency is recorded per archetype and judged against 5 s at the 95th percentile, with the maximum shown.
- [ ] Interior generate-and-compile latency is recorded for the seven archetypes and judged against 60 s at the 95th percentile; larger scenes are reported with size and not judged.
- [ ] Cold and warm runs are separate distributions; every figure carries a disclosure block.
- [ ] The three sub-rows are in the target register with verdicts, and in the regression lane as smoke rows.
- [ ] The whole campaign runs inside the default compute envelope and states its measured wall time.
- [ ] An outside-tolerance sub-row opens a recorded defect naming the operation, the percentile and the dominant stage of the slow samples.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance perf::cpu_rows`:
- `percentiles_from_samples`: a fixture of 200 latencies with known order statistics returns the 100th, 190th and 198th smallest for the 50th, 95th and 99th percentiles (nearest-rank rule, stated).
- `cold_and_warm_are_separate`.
- `incremental_requires_memo_hits`: an incremental sample with a memo hit fraction of zero is flagged.
- `verdict_19a`: 95th percentile 1.8 s → inside tolerance; 2.1 s → outside; incremental (0.25, 0.45, 0.9) → ambition missed at the 50th only.
- `verdict_19c_only_for_reference_size`: a warehouse at 2 cm is reported, not judged.
- `latency_is_submission_to_envelope`.
- `na_fields_allowed_for_cpu_verbs`: the disclosure applicability table accepts the CPU-verb form and rejects a missing `threads`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/perf_cpu_rows.sh` (owned by this bead; sources the shared end-to-end helper; seed-explicit;
idempotent; runs on either machine inside the default envelope): steps `design_check_full`,
`design_check_incremental`, `control_synth`, `scene_gen_compile`, `verdicts`. Each NDJSON line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time and the evidence block; `metric` lines carry
the design or scene identity (public corpus items and generated scenes only), size measure, latency in
milliseconds, cold or warm, memo hit fraction; `assert` lines the percentiles against their thresholds; a
`summary` line counts assertions and lists the sub-rows with verdicts.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The nearest-rank percentile fixture above.
- Negative controls: a latency measured from inside the process (excluding start-up and envelope writing) is rejected by `latency_is_submission_to_envelope`; an incremental check that silently bypasses the memo graph is flagged; a campaign that mixes cold and warm samples fails.
- A/A: the same subset timed twice back to back agrees at the 95th percentile within the recorded spread.

## Considerations

- The arithmetic of the design cycle rests on a HYPOTHESIS outside this bead (a baseline controller can stand in for a trained policy when designs are ranked); a fast `control synth` is necessary for that cycle and does not validate it.
- `design check` latency depends on which analyses are enabled; the campaign uses the default preflight set and records it, and a different set is a different row.
- Interior generation at the reference size is the judged case because the 60 s figure was stated for an interior; a warehouse at 2 cm is about 64 times the voxels and belongs on the Mac.
- The target row prints `control synth` as "under 5 s" and scene generation as "under 60 s" without a percentile; the reading used here is recorded in this unit's coverage file.
