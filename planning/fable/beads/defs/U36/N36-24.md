## Background

**The question.** OPEN-PERF-1: the tolerance ratio of 0.67 for the physics rows of the target table, and the band
below it. Owner: the performance lead. Default: as stated; below 0.33 the parity kill gate fires. It blocks
GATE-SPARK-03, the gate that judges the CUDA throughput rows against their tolerances.

**What the numbers mean.** A physics row is inside tolerance when the paired lower bound of (our rate ÷ the
incumbent's rate, matched settings, same card) is at least 0.67: a step at most 1.5 times slower than the
incumbent's. Between 0.33 and 0.67 the row is a recorded defect: one further optimization pass, and a disclosure
on every Performance claim until it closes. Below 0.33 (half the tolerance) the parity kill gate fires: feature
work on the device tier stops until the row is inside tolerance.

**Why 0.67.** Engine throughput is a parity requirement, and the cost of being slower is paid in training time.
If the environment step is a share s of training wall-clock and our step runs at ratio ρ of the incumbent's
speed, training lengthens by 1 + s·(1/ρ − 1). On incumbent stacks s is between 0.29 and 0.87, so at ρ = 0.67 the
factor is between 1.15 and 1.44, which the plan's other levers must absorb and which the ten-fold test charges in
full. Beyond that the engine is a liability.

**Status of the default.** The gate register confirms this default; it is open only to new evidence. The new
evidence that could move it is the measured value of s on our own hardware (from the incumbent baseline) and on
our own engine (from the M2 profile). Fail-closed default at the deadline: 0.67, 0.33 and the defect band stand
as written and are recorded as an ADR.

Rejected alternatives recorded with the question: an absolute throughput target with no comparator (hides a gap;
absolute targets can sit below published incumbent figures unnoticed); a tolerance of 1.0 (makes parity itself the
gate and turns every small deficit into a failed milestone, while the plan's argument tolerates a bounded
deficit); no lower band (a row at 0.4 would be indistinguishable from one at 0.66).

## Scope

In: the decision packet; the decision record and its timing; the table of consequences for candidate values; the
propagation of a change into the target register and the kill gate's band. Out: measuring the rows (the
throughput-rows bead); the kill-gate decision itself (N36-27).

## Technical approach

1. **Evidence gathered before the decision.** From the completed incumbent baseline: the environment step's share
   of training on `trj` for the reference quadruped and humanoid tasks (replacing the published 0.29 to 0.87),
   and the physics share of the environment step. From the Amdahl calculator: the penalty table below at the
   measured shares.

   | ρ (our speed ÷ incumbent's) | Training lengthening at s = 0.29 | at s = 0.87 | Reading |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | Parity |
   | 0.80 | 1.07 | 1.22 | A tighter tolerance |
   | 0.67 | 1.14 | 1.43 | The default (the plan prints 1.15 to 1.44 from "1.5 times slower") |
   | 0.50 | 1.29 | 1.87 | A looser tolerance |
   | 0.33 | 1.59 | 2.77 | The kill threshold |

2. **Decision packet** for the performance lead, copied to the owner: the measured shares; the penalty table at
   those shares; the default; and the one question: does the measured share change the judgement that a 1.15 to
   1.44 penalty is absorbable. If the measured s is at the high end for the tasks the product leads with, a
   tighter tolerance is the conservative amendment; nothing in the evidence can justify a looser one without the
   ten-fold arithmetic being redone with it.
3. **Timing.** The decision is recorded **before** the first paired throughput campaign of GATE-SPARK-03 runs. A
   threshold changes only by a dated amendment to the gate register, with its reason, made before the measurement
   it governs; after the first pair exists the tolerance is frozen for that gate.
4. **Record.** An ADR (`docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-PERF-1.md`) with the measured shares, the table, the decision
   (`confirmed` or `amended to ⟨value⟩`), the band (kill threshold = half the tolerance unless stated otherwise)
   and the date. The target register's rows 1, 7 and 8 and the kill gate's reading function take their numbers
   from this record; no other file states them.
5. **If amended.** The gate register receives the dated amendment; the register rows are regenerated; the
   interim floor of row 1 (2.0 M world-steps/s, used only until an incumbent measurement exists) is unaffected;
   the training-time tolerance (1.25 times the incumbent's time) is a separate number and is not changed by this
   decision.
6. **If undecided at the deadline.** The default is recorded as the ADR and the exit review says the tolerance
   was taken at its default.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision packet exists with the measured step shares on `trj` and the penalty table evaluated at them, dated before the first paired campaign of GATE-SPARK-03.
- [ ] The ADR is on record (`confirmed`, `amended`, or default at deadline) and the gate register row of GATE-SPARK-03 cites it.
- [ ] The target register reads the tolerance and the kill threshold from the decision record; changing the record changes both and nothing else.
- [ ] An attempt to amend the tolerance after a paired campaign for the gate exists is rejected by the register's lint.
- [ ] The band is consistent: kill threshold below the tolerance, the defect band between them, and the three readings partition the positive ratios with no gap.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance perf::tolerance`:
- `penalty_table`: 1 + s·(1/ρ − 1) for (0.29, 0.67) = 1.143; (0.87, 0.67) = 1.429; (0.29, 0.8) = 1.073; (0.87, 0.8) = 1.218; (0.29, 0.5) = 1.29; (0.87, 0.5) = 1.87; (0.29, 0.33) = 1.589; (0.87, 0.33) = 2.766; and with ρ = 1/1.5 exactly: 1.145 and 1.435.
- `band_partition`: bounds 0.32, 0.33, 0.50, 0.669, 0.67, 0.80 → {kill, defect, defect, defect, inside, inside} (0.33 itself is not "below 0.33"; 0.67 itself is inside).
- `amendment_before_measurement_only`.
- `kill_threshold_is_half_tolerance_by_default`: tolerance 0.80 → 0.40 unless the record states another value.
- `register_reads_decision_record`.

### End-to-end script and logging

No script of its own. The throughput-rows script (owned by BD-217) prints, in its preflight, the tolerance, the
kill threshold, the decision's state and ADR reference, and refuses to start a paired campaign for GATE-SPARK-03
if no decision record (confirmed, amended or default-at-deadline) is present. Logged: the numbers, the state, the
ADR path.

### Known answers and negative controls

- The penalty arithmetic (KA-818's last clause: a step 1.5 times slower lengthens training by 1.15 to 1.44 for a step share of 0.29 to 0.87).
- Negative control: a register in which the tolerance was edited after a campaign record exists must fail the lint.
- Negative control: a reading function that judges the mean ratio instead of the paired lower bound is rejected by the harness's ratio type.

## Considerations

- RISK-30 (engine throughput or training time lands below the parity band; likelihood high; early signs: a paired lower bound under 0.67 of the incumbent, or training time above 1.25 times the incumbent's). The band is the plan's graded response to it.
- The tolerance is charged in full by the ten-fold test: a slower step is not forgiven there, so loosening this number does not make any program-level claim easier.
- The Metal row has its own threshold (0.25 of one RTX 4090, a bet with its own kill gate) and the CPU rows theirs (0.5 of CPU MuJoCo on the same machine); this decision covers the CUDA physics rows 1, 7 and 8.
- If the default physics step moves from 2 ms, the rows are restated with their new decimation (a separate decision); the ratio 0.67 is unit-free and unaffected.
