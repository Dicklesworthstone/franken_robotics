## Background

**The question.** OPEN-PERF-2: which incumbent revisions and configurations are the comparators of the performance
program. Owner: the Gauntlet lead; the project owner's input is asked for at M0 (it is on the M0 list of questions
put to the owner). It blocks GATE-SPARK-21, the parity-baseline gate, whose `trj` rows are an M0 exit condition.

**One decision under two identifiers.** The verification chapter asks the same thing as the second half of its
question OPEN-GAUNT-4 ("which releases and container digests form the comparator set"; owner there: the rival
audit; it blocks the oracle-hygiene gate GATE-GAUNT-05). There is one comparator set, one registry and one
decision record: this bead makes it, and its ADR is filed under both identifiers. The first half of OPEN-GAUNT-4,
which incumbent release is the development oracle for pilots that must run before the CPU referee exists, is a
different choice and is bead N38b-35's, which cites this bead's ADR for the comparator half.

**Why it must be decided first.** Every parity target of the project is a ratio to an incumbent measured on our
own hardware. A ratio whose denominator can be chosen after the numerator is known is worthless, so incumbents are
frozen first: no paired run of any kind happens before every arm in it has a published freeze record. The
comparator of each row must also be the incumbent's **strongest warm or cached configuration**, frozen after
tuning on development fixtures only; comparing against a default or cold configuration would flatter us.

**Fail-closed default.** The revisions in the source register, frozen before any paired run. Those are the
releases read for the plan (FACT; each to be re-pinned by exact revision): MuJoCo and MuJoCo Warp 4.0.1, Warp
1.19.0, Newton 1.8.0, Isaac Lab 3.0.0, Genesis 1.4.7, Drake 1.58.0, ManiSkill 3.0.1, with mjlab and MuJoCo
Playground at the revisions their published benchmarks used. Until this decision is recorded, GATE-SPARK-21 has
the status `blocked: OPEN-PERF-2` and no incumbent figure may be cited as a comparator; published figures remain
`published-context`.

Rejected alternatives: "latest at the time of each run" (the denominator drifts between runs and between
milestones); one incumbent for every row (no single incumbent publishes every reference figure, and the strongest
differs by row); freezing only versions and not configurations (the published two-orders-of-magnitude dispute over
one simulator's headline number was about configuration).

## Scope

In: the decision packet for the comparator set; the row-to-comparator table; the selection rules; the decision
fields this decision writes into the rows of the comparator registry (`uses`, `rows`, `configurations.matched`,
`configurations.native`, `tuned_on`, `frozen_at`, `state`, `reference`); the default as a tested state; the freeze
rule and the rule for changing a pin later; the ADR, filed under OPEN-PERF-2 and under the comparator half of
OPEN-GAUNT-4.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- The registry file `crates/frob-conformance/registry/comparators.toml`, its row schema, its loader (exact pins,
  no floating reference), the `[[pin_move]]` records and the check that refuses a pin move during an open study:
  BD-039. This bead defines no file and no schema; it depends on BD-039 and fills fields of rows that exist.
- Installing, pinning and running incumbents, their recorded environments and the tuning logs behind each warm
  configuration: BD-039. Measuring: BD-040, BD-142.
- The choice of the development oracle and its record in `registry/oracles.toml`: N38b-35. The oracle register
  itself: N38a-04.
- The rival arms of the ten-fold tournament, which reuse these pins but are frozen by their own manifests (each
  adds its study identifier to the row's `frozen_for`).

## Technical approach

1. **Decision packet** (`docs/decisions/OPEN-PERF-2.md`, a short document put to the Gauntlet lead and the
   owner, dated; `docs/decisions/OPEN-GAUNT-4.md` points to it for its comparator half): the table below, and for
   each candidate incumbent its exact release tag and commit, its container image digest (or the lockfile digest
   of its environment), its licence, what it documents that it can do (its capability table), which reference
   robots and tasks it runs unmodified, the host and device it was audited on, known defects relevant to the
   rows, and for each row the candidate configurations (starting from the strongest warm configuration its own
   documentation recommends) with the reason one is believed strongest.

   Selection rules (DESIGN; fixed before the choice): (a) per comparison (throughput, training time, mixed
   batches, each tournament stratum) the set contains the strongest composed rival at a pinned version, in its
   strongest warm configuration: a weaker comparator measures the comparator, not us; (b) each entry is admitted
   like a dependency: a pin, an admission test (it reproduces its own published or documented figure on the
   audit host within the stated tolerance) and a negative control (a deliberately wrong pin is detected by
   digest); (c) nothing chosen is linked into any production binary, and the dependency firewall checks it.

   | Target rows | Comparator (default) | Configuration to freeze |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1, 2 (Q physics and full step, one RTX 4090) | MuJoCo Warp under mjlab, quadruped velocity task | Matched: our h = 2 ms, d = 10, 4 iterations where its solver allows. Native: its own step and decimation (published at d = 4) |
   | 3 (Q training rate) | Isaac Lab with its own PPO recipe; MuJoCo Playground | Native recipe, warm compile cache, graph capture on |
   | 4, 5 (Q time to threshold, one and two cards) | MuJoCo Playground, flat joystick task | Its published recipe; cold start included; the two-card run as published |
   | 7, 8 (H physics, flat and heightfield) | MuJoCo Warp nightly-benchmark configuration, humanoid | 8,192 worlds; matched and native |
   | 9 (H time to threshold, two cards) | MuJoCo Playground humanoid, first-stage flat-ground task | Its published recipe |
   | 10 (mixed batch of three body plans) | Newton with its mixed-topology solver | Three-, four- and five-legged bodies in one batch |
   | 11 (parameter variants) | MuJoCo Warp batched model fields | Per-world model fields on, 8,192 worlds |
   | 12 (Metal) | Genesis on its Metal path, on the Mac | Its own recommended settings; reported beside ours |
   | 13, 14 (CPU) | CPU MuJoCo in `f64` on the Mac | 1, 24 and 36 threads; h = 2 ms |
   | 20 (edit to first step) | Each GPU comparator above | A parameter edit and a topology edit, warm cache and cold |

2. **Entries in the comparator registry.** The registry is `crates/frob-conformance/registry/comparators.toml`.
   Its file, its row schema and its loader belong to the incumbent harness bead BD-039; the path is the one the
   verification chapter fixes, and no file `registries/comparators.toml` exists. Each row already holds, from
   BD-039, the pin and the audit facts: `name`, `role`, `release`, `source_revision` (a full commit; a release
   tag alone or a floating reference is refused by the loader), `container_digest`, `runtime`, `licence`,
   `recipe`, `lock`, `warm_config`, `capabilities[]`, `pinned_at`, `pinned_by`. This decision fills the decision
   fields of each row: `uses[]` (`throughput`, `training-time`, `mixed-batch`, `proxy-adequacy`), `rows[]` (the
   target rows of the table above), `configurations.matched` and `configurations.native` (each the content hash
   of a configuration file under `tools/oracle/incumbents/<name>/`; `native` is the strongest warm configuration
   in the incumbent's own step, decimation and recipe, `matched` the same with our settings where its solver
   allows), `tuned_on` (reference tasks and development fixtures only), `frozen_at`, `state` (`default`,
   `decided`, `amended`) and `reference` (the ADR or the owner's note). The registry's digest is part of the
   incumbent harness identity: a changed revision changes the harness identity and invalidates every record
   made under the old one.

   DESIGN (correction of this bead's first draft, which put a second registry at `registries/comparators.toml`
   with a `revision` field that also accepted a release tag): one file at the path above, the field names of
   BD-039, and exact commits only. Reason: three drafts defined this file differently; the seed's harness
   already keys its identity and run records on its own field names, and "exact releases and container digests"
   rules out a tag.
3. **Freeze rule.** A comparator's configuration may be improved in any way a competent engineer would (warm
   caches, graph capture, recommended solver settings) until it is frozen; after `frozen_at` nothing changes. A
   defect found in a frozen comparator voids the affected rows, which are re-measured at the next gate; it is
   never patched mid-gate. The rule is code of this bead, in `frob_conformance::perf::comparators` (DESIGN
   names): `require_frozen(registry, name)` refuses a row with `state = "default"` or an empty `frozen_at`
   (`ComparatorError::Unfrozen`), the check BD-039's schema promises for an empty `frozen_at` ("every paired run
   against the row is refused"), which BD-039's launcher does not itself make; and `freeze_order(registry,
   runs)` fails a row whose `frozen_at` is later than the start of a run record of BD-039's launcher against it
   (`ComparatorError::FrozenAfterRun`). Callers that start a comparator arm call `require_frozen` first.
4. **Change rule after the decision.** A pin changes only by a dated amendment, made before the measurement it
   governs, with its reason; rows measured under the old pin stay on record under the old harness identity and are
   not mixed with new ones in one ratio.
5. **Outcome.** Recorded as one ADR at M0, filed under OPEN-PERF-2 and under the comparator half of OPEN-GAUNT-4.
   If the question reaches its deadline undecided, the default is recorded as the ADR and the exit review
   reports that the comparators are the source-register releases. While no ADR is on record the rows stay at
   `state = "default"` with `frozen_at` empty, every paired run against them is refused, and an arm with no
   frozen comparator reports `not run`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision packet exists, dated, with the table, licences and capability tables, and has been put to the Gauntlet lead and the owner.
- [ ] `crates/frob-conformance/registry/comparators.toml` (BD-039's file and schema) holds every comparator of the table with an exact commit, a container digest, both configurations by content hash, `tuned_on` and a freeze date; the registry lint passes, and this bead has added no schema field and no second file.
- [ ] The ADR names both identifiers, OPEN-PERF-2 and OPEN-GAUNT-4 (comparator set), and each frozen row's `reference` points to it.
- [ ] No chosen tool appears in any production dependency graph.
- [ ] With `state = "default"` the registry lists the source-register releases and the gate status tool reports GATE-SPARK-21 as `blocked: OPEN-PERF-2` until an ADR reference is present.
- [ ] Changing one revision changes the harness identity (checked by the incumbent harness's identity test).
- [ ] `require_frozen` refuses a comparator with `state = "default"` or an empty `frozen_at`, and `freeze_order` fails a row frozen after a run against it.
- [ ] The ADR is on record before the first paired run of any kind.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance perf::comparators`:
- `default_lists_source_register_releases`: seven release lines (MuJoCo with MuJoCo Warp, Warp, Newton, Isaac Lab, Genesis, Drake, ManiSkill), each with a `source_revision` that is not empty.
- `frozen_requires_configurations_and_tuned_on`.
- `identity_changes_with_revision` (one changed character changes the registry digest).
- `amendment_requires_date_and_reason`.
- `unfrozen_comparator_refuses_pairing` (a row with `state = "default"` and empty `frozen_at`: `require_frozen` returns `Unfrozen`; the same row decided and frozen passes).
- `pin_move_after_freeze_detected`: a frozen row whose commit differs from the ADR's with no `[[pin_move]]` record fails (the check is BD-039's registry lint, run here on this decision's rows).
- `every_ratio_row_has_a_comparator`: joins this registry's `rows[]` with the target register (N36-01) and fails on a row of kind `ratio_to_incumbent` with no comparator; a `ratio_to_row` row (row 6, weak scaling, ratio to row 1) needs none and is not checked.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/perf_parity_baseline.sh` (N36-29), step `pins`: prints the registry digest, each comparator's
revision and configuration hashes and the decision state, and asserts that every comparator it is about to run is
frozen. Logged: `comparators_digest`, `state`, the ADR reference or the `blocked` status.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Negative control: a scratch registry with one comparator's `frozen_at` after the start of a run record of BD-039's launcher against it must fail `freeze_order` (`FrozenAfterRun`).
- Negative control: a comparator recorded with a cold-cache configuration for a warm row is rejected (cold and warm runs are never mixed in one row).
- Negative controls of the entries (caught by BD-039's loader): a floating tag in place of a commit; a digest that does not match the pulled image.

## Considerations

- RISK-25 (the denominator of the ten-fold ratio is wrong; likelihood high): pinned comparator revisions are one of its three mitigations, with the M0 phase ledger and the ceiling-first rule.
- The published reference figures are on other cards (an RTX 5090, an RTX 6000 Ada, an A100); the pins fix the software, and the hardware difference is what the 20% reproduction rule and its written explanations absorb.
- Licences: incumbents run out of process as comparison instruments and never enter a production dependency graph; a comparator whose licence forbids benchmarking or redistribution of results is replaced before the freeze, and the packet says so.
- The same pins serve two other instruments: the incumbent arm of the mixed-batch head-to-head and the proxy-adequacy pilot of the scene chapter.
- An incumbent release that appears after the freeze is not adopted mid-gate; it is a candidate for the next gate's freeze.
