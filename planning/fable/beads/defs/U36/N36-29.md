## Background

GATE-SPARK-21 is the parity-baseline gate (claim: Parity). Its criterion: incumbents are measured on `trj` (the
Linux host with two RTX 4090) and on the Mac (the M5 Ultra) under the disclosure block: physics and full step for
the reference quadruped workload Q and humanoid workload H at matched and native settings; time to threshold on
one and two cards; cold start; edit to first step for a parameter edit and a topology edit; CPU MuJoCo on the Mac
at 1, 24 and 36 threads; an incumbent with a Metal path on the Mac. Each published figure of the target table is
reproduced within 20% or the difference is explained in writing. The gate is split across two milestones: the
`trj` rows that need no agent are due at M0 (they are an M0 exit condition and the input of the ten-fold test's
first phase ledger); the whole gate closes at M1.

This bead is the script that runs the baseline from the user's side and writes the structured log and the gate
receipt. The measuring work and its judgement (which runs, how many repeats, what counts as an explanation) are
the two seed beads that use it: the `trj` rows at M0 and the completion at M1. The script exists at M0 with both
modes; the M1 mode's rows are filled in by the M1 bead.

Method rule this script embodies: **measure the incumbent first**, by phase, starting with what needs no agent,
with the harness as an out-of-process tool and the comparator revisions frozen beforehand.

## Scope

In: the script, its steps, assertions, logging and receipts; the registered negative controls of the gate. Out:
the incumbent harness (BD-039); the campaign definition and written explanations (BD-040, BD-142); the
agent-driven design-loop ledger (BD-143).

## Technical approach

**Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/perf_parity_baseline.sh --milestone m0|m1 --machine trj|mac [--rows <list>]
[--repeats <n>] --seed <s>`. Plain shell; sources the shared helper of the end-to-end standard; idempotent (a row
already on record under the same harness identity, seed and repeat index is skipped and logged as `cached`);
exits non-zero on a failed assertion, a missing measurement that was admitted, or broken accounting. A row that
could not be admitted under the compute envelope in force is recorded `not run` with the reason and does not fail
the script.

**Steps.**
1. `preflight`: `frob doctor --json` (stack receipt); `frob spark fingerprint --json` and assert the fingerprint
   is current for this machine; print the envelope class in force; record host load (`trj`) or run the 20-minute
   sustained load and record the thermal state (Mac); disk preflight.
2. `pins`: print the comparator registry digest and assert every comparator to be run is frozen; print the
   incumbent harness identity.
3. `workloads`: print the Q and H records with their identities.
4. `rows` (per row: the envelope admission check, then the incumbent harness command, then the adapter that
   converts its output into a report row):
   - M0, `trj`: `cold_start` (cold cache and warm cache, separately); `edit_param` and `edit_topology` (edit to
     first device step, at least 20 edits each, 50th and 95th percentiles); `physics_Q`, `physics_H`,
     `fullstep_Q`, `fullstep_H` at matched settings, at 1, 8, 64, 256 worlds and upward to 8,192; `train_1card`;
     `train_2card`.
   - M1 adds, `trj`: the same physics and full-step rows at native settings; and on the Mac: `cpu_mujoco_H` and
     `cpu_mujoco_Q` at 1, 24 and 36 threads; `metal_incumbent_Q`.
5. `disclosure`: validate every row's disclosure block; then delete one field at a time from a copy and assert
   refusal `FROB-REF-862` with the right pointer.
6. `units`: assert every step rate carries unit, decimation, step and scope; run the ordering check on each
   (machine, workload, settings) group.
7. `reproduce`: for each published figure in the target register, log published, measured and their ratio;
   assert that the ratio is inside [0.8, 1.2] or that a written explanation file for that figure exists and is
   non-empty (`docs/perf/explanations/<figure>.md`), and that the measured value has replaced the planning value.
8. `limits` and `family_batch_bound`: run the Amdahl calculator and the family-batch bound on the recorded rows.
9. `ledger`: emit the phase-ledger rows (compile, train with the learner's share, wait) for the timed runs and
   assert each closes: phases sum to the clock within 2%, else the row is flagged `ledger-open`.
10. `gate`: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g2 -- GATE-SPARK-21 --milestone <m>` assembles the gate record
    (commit, command, output, receipt hash) from the rows; at M0 the record's status is `partial: trj rows` and at
    M1 `pass`, `fail` or `not run` per row.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/perf_parity_baseline/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it.
Every line: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the
exact command line, exit code, wall time, the evidence block (claim `Parity`, class `Tested`, tier, determinism,
identities, seed). `metric` lines add: the row, comparator and revision, settings (matched or native), world
count, the rate with unit and decimation, percentiles for latencies, repeat index and order, host load and thermal
state, the disclosure block identifier, the published value and the ratio against the 20% band. On failure: the
first missing field or differing value and the artifact paths needed to reproduce. A final `summary` line gives
assertions passed and failed, rows measured, rows `not run` with reasons, and `gates: ["GATE-SPARK-21"]`. Logs
never contain private-scene data (the workloads are public reference robots on generated ground).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists with both modes and exits 0 on a machine where the admitted rows were measured; it exits non-zero when an admitted row has no record, a block is incomplete, a step rate lacks its decimation, or a published figure is outside 20% with no explanation file.
- [ ] Every step and assertion above writes its NDJSON lines with all required fields, and the `summary` line names GATE-SPARK-21.
- [ ] Rows that need an unapproved envelope class are logged `not run` with the reason and listed in the summary.
- [ ] Re-running with the same seed and harness identity is a no-op for recorded rows; changing a comparator revision invalidates them and they are re-measured.
- [ ] The gate test writes a receipt that names the commit, the fingerprint digest of each machine and the seed, with measured values (not targets).
- [ ] The four registered negative controls are caught.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance perf::incumbent_adapter`:
- `adapter_requires_unit_and_decimation`: an incumbent output with a bare "steps/s" is rejected.
- `adapter_keeps_cold_and_warm_apart`: one row cannot contain both.
- `reproduction_band`: 1.19 inside; 1.21 outside; 0.80 inside; 0.79 outside.
- `not_run_is_not_fail`.
- `ledger_closure`: phases summing to 97.9% of the clock flag `ledger-open`; 98.1% close.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; the two paragraphs above define what it runs, asserts and logs.

### Known answers and negative controls

Registered against GATE-SPARK-21 before the passing run, each a planted defect the script must catch:
1. A row recorded without its disclosure block (refused, `FROB-REF-862`).
2. A published figure reproduced at 1.5 times with no explanation file (the `reproduce` assertion fails).
3. A changed comparator revision under an unchanged harness identity (the `pins` assertion fails).
4. A cold-start time merged into a warm row (rejected by the adapter).
Known values checked along the way: the reproduction band arithmetic; the Amdahl and family-batch known answers
(KA-818 to KA-820, KA-810) on the published fixture, so that a change in the calculators is not mistaken for a
change in the measurements. Determinism lanes do not apply to incumbent timings (they are measurements with
recorded spread); the script records the spread of repeats per row.

## Considerations

- RISK-09: `trj` is often heavily loaded, so measurements are noisy and compiles slow; the script records load per repeat and interleaves rows; it never discards a repeat silently.
- OPEN-SPARK-2: at the default class E0 the two-card training row and the 24- and 36-thread CPU rows are `not run`; the M0 exit then has the single-card and eight-core rows only, and the exit review says so.
- The incumbent harness's interface is another unit's; this script isolates it behind one adapter so that a change there does not ripple into the steps.
- Time-to-threshold runs are minutes long; under E1 the gate's budget of 12 device-hours bounds the number of repeats, and the script prints the remaining budget before each long row.
