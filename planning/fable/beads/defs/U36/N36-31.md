## Background

GATE-SPARK-20 (claim: Performance; earliest milestone M2; TARGET): weak scaling at least 1.8 and strong scaling at
least 1.25 at equal task quality, both as paired lower bounds; the gradient known answer of the distributed
normalization rule holds and its control is caught.

The gate is measured on a running system: two physical RTX 4090 cards on `trj` (no link between them), one
training job split into two shards, and the same job on one card. This bead is the end-to-end script that drives
it from the user's side, records every pair with its disclosure blocks, and writes the gate receipt. The campaign
design and the judgement of results are the seed bead that uses the script; the mechanisms are the shard runtime
and the paired-bound estimator.

Terms. *Weak scaling*: each card carries the full single-card load; the ratio is the sum of both cards' rates to
one card's rate. *Strong scaling*: the same training job to the same return threshold; the ratio is the one-card
time to the two-card time. *Equal task quality*: both arms attain the threshold (held and confirmed, not merely
crossed) and the two-card policy's evaluation return is not below the one-card policy's by more than a margin
fixed before the campaign. *Gradient known answer*: per-sample gradients (1, 0, 0, 0) reduce to 0.25 on one shard
and on two, however the four are split, and with an empty shard; the mean of shard means gives 0.5.

## Scope

In: the script, its steps, assertions, logging; the registered negative controls of the gate. Out: the runtime
(N36-08), the estimator (N36-06), the campaign judgement and gate records (BD-199), the kill-gate ADR (N36-10).

## Technical approach

**Invocation.** `scripts/e2e/perf_shard_scaling.sh --machine trj [--phase pre|post] [--pairs 10] --seed <s>`.
Plain shell; sources the shared end-to-end helper; seed-explicit; idempotent (pairs are content-addressed by
campaign, pair index and seed; a completed pair is not re-run); exits non-zero on a failed assertion or broken
accounting. A measured gate that fails its threshold does not fail the script: the script's job is a correct
record. If the compute envelope in force does not admit two devices, the script logs every device step as
`not run` with the reason, runs the two-shard fixtures on `batch-cpu`, and exits 0.

**Envelope.** An arm that holds both cards is above E0 (the plan counts the weak- and the strong-scaling rows
among the measurements the default class cannot run): the `--shards 2` jobs of steps 3 and 6, and the two-card
arm of step 5, whose two per-card commands run at the same time. Such an arm runs under row R2 of the envelope
register (N44-01: training to threshold on two cards and the scaling rows, class E1, scope
`gate:GATE-SPARK-20`). A run verb has no scope input and, started directly, is admitted under `adhoc`, class E0
(N36-20), so the arm is started as the child of `frob-launch --scope gate:GATE-SPARK-20 --request R2 --devices 2
--device-ids 0,1 --cores 8 --mem-gib <g> --wall-s <the arm's upper wall estimate, at most 7200> --json -- <the
arm's command>` (for step 5, a wrapper that starts the two per-card commands together; both inherit the token).
The verbs adopt the launcher's token; the launcher's meter row, one per arm, is cited in the pairs record and
counted against the gate's 12 device-hours. A launcher refusal (`FROB-REF-761`, exit 4) is the `not run` case
above. The one-card arms fit E0 and are started directly.

**Steps.**
1. `preflight`: `frob doctor --json`; `frob spark devices --json` (assert two CUDA devices); `frob spark
   fingerprint --json` (assert current; print each card's free memory and the `peer` field); the envelope class
   in force; host load.
2. `gradient_known_answer`: the registry row KA-813 on the two cards, `FROB_TEST_SEED=<s> cargo test -p
   frob-conformance --features cuda,gauntlet-mutations --test g5_determinism -- KA-813` (the Gauntlet has no
   `frob` command): from the receipt, assert 0.25 for the splits {1 | 0,0,0}, {1,0 | 0,0}, {1,0,0,0 | ∅},
   {∅ | 1,0,0,0}, and for the registered control (the mean-of-shard-means reducer, applied by the control runner
   in the same run) assert the value 0.5 on the first split and the line `caught`.
3. `shard_equivalence`: a short training job (`frob train start <reference quadruped> --task <reference flat
   velocity> --worlds 8192 --budget env_steps=2000000 --seed <s> --backend gpu-cuda:0,1 --shards 2 --key
   equiv-2-<s> --json`) beside the same job with `--backend gpu-cuda:0 --shards 1 --key equiv-1-<s>`; assert per-update reduced-gradient agreement inside the recorded D3
   envelope, bit-identical repeats for the fixed layout, and equal collection digests per world on either card.
4. `ceiling`: print the pilot's prediction record and assert it predates this script's first two-card pair.
5. `weak`: at least ten pairs of {both cards at 8,192 worlds each, simultaneously; one card at 8,192 worlds with
   the other idle}, physics only, `frob sim run --workload Q --tier gpu-cuda:<card> --worlds 8192 --seconds 20
   --seed <s_i> --key weak-<arm>-<card>-<i> --json` per card; arm order randomized by (seed, pair index). Compute the paired lower bound of (sum of two rates) ÷
   (single rate); log it against 1.8 and 1.95.
6. `strong`: at least ten pairs of {one card; two cards with `--shards 2` at the same global world count}, each a
   `frob train start` job as in step 3 (keys `strong-<arm>-<i>`) from a cold start, configured to stop at attainment of the return threshold
   (the stop condition the training-time parity measurement uses), the same seed within a pair. Compute the
   paired lower bound of (one-card time) ÷ (two-card time); log it against 1.25 and 1.6 and beside the pilot's
   predicted ceiling S_max (N36-09), so that the pilot is scored (a bound above S_max falsifies the pilot).
7. `quality`: for every pair, `frob eval run <reference quadruped> --policy <final policy> --battery <the
   evaluation battery of the reference training gate> --key quality-<arm>-<i> --json` on both final policies
   under the deterministic replay profile (the form the reference training bead BD-198 uses for its evaluation);
   assert attainment in both arms and non-inferiority against the pre-declared margin; a failing pair voids the
   campaign and the script reports which.
8. `aa_control`: one campaign in which the "two-card" arm is the one-card job repeated; assert its bound does not
   clear 1.02.
9. `optimize` (phase `post` only): run the optimization-ledger landing check for the sharding levers recorded
   since the `pre` phase.
10. `gate`: `FROB_TEST_SEED=<s> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features cuda,gauntlet-mutations --test
    g2_parity -- GATE-SPARK-20` (the tier target named by the gate record's `tier` field) assembles the gate's receipt (commit, command, output, both fingerprint digests, seed) from the
    campaigns of this phase, with the three-way reading of the strong bound for the kill gate; the script prints
    the receipt hash, and the status is entered by the recording session of BD-199 with `cargo run -p
    frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-SPARK-20 --receipt <hash>`.

**Logging.** NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/perf_shard_scaling/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` and a transcript. Every line:
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event` (`start`, `cmd`, `assert`, `metric`, `refusal`, `end`), the exact command
line, exit code, wall time, and the envelope's evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed).
`metric` lines add: campaign, pair index, arm order, per-card rates with unit and decimation (weak) or times to
attainment in seconds (strong), evaluation returns and margin, transfer bytes and synchronization counts per
card, host load, the two disclosure block identifiers, and for a two-card arm the launcher's meter row (token id,
scope, device-seconds, outcome); per campaign n, ℓ̄, s, t and the lower bound against its
thresholds. On failure: the first pair and field that broke an assertion and the paths of the pairs record and
both receipts. Final `summary`: assertions passed and failed, both bounds, the A/A bound, the three-way reading,
`gates: ["GATE-SPARK-20", "GATE-CORTEX-08"]`. The task uses a public reference robot on generated ground; no
private data is logged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exists, runs on `trj` under the gate-measurement class and writes the GATE-SPARK-20 receipt with measured bounds (not targets) for the phase it ran.
- [ ] Under the default envelope it exits 0 with every two-device step logged `not run` and the `batch-cpu` shard fixtures passed.
- [ ] The gradient known answer and its control are both shown on real cards.
- [ ] Each pair's two disclosure blocks are complete and differ only in the fields the comparison allows (device count, shard layout).
- [ ] A planted doubled-batch two-card arm presented as constant workload is rejected by the block diff; a strong-scaling time read at first crossing instead of attainment is rejected by the quality step.
- [ ] The A/A control does not clear 1.02.
- [ ] Every NDJSON line has the required fields and the `summary` names the gates.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

None of its own beyond a shell lint and a dry-run mode (`--plan`); the runtime, estimator and campaign logic have
their unit tests in their beads.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script; steps, assertions and log fields are defined above.

### Known answers and negative controls

- KA-813 (0.25 on one and two shards and with an empty shard; control 0.5). KA-807 guards the estimator inside the same run (the A/A control).
- Registered controls for GATE-SPARK-20: the mean-of-shard-means reducer; the doubled-batch arm; the crossing-time reading; a ratio reported as a mean without a bound.
- Determinism lanes: step 3 (fixed layout twice, bitwise; one device against two shards, class D3 with the envelope printed).

## Considerations

- OPEN-SPARK-2 and RISK-10: two devices exceed the default envelope; the script's `not run` path is the default behaviour until the class is approved.
- RISK-09: `trj` is often loaded; a strong-scaling pair takes over ten minutes of wall-clock, so host load can drift inside a pair; both arms' load samples are logged and a drifting pair is flagged, not dropped.
- The `pre` and `post` phases are the measurements before and after the single optimization pass; the kill gate reads `post`.
- Ten strong pairs cost about three device-hours; the script prints the gate's remaining device-hour budget before it starts each campaign.
