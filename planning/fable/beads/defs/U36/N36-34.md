## Background

Target row 9 of the performance program is binding and has no gate: the reference humanoid (workload H: 29
actuated joints, 35 degrees of freedom, 30 bodies, contact capacity 64, row capacity 320, physics step 2 ms,
four warm-started Newton iterations, decimation 10, that is 50 Hz control) must reach the return threshold of a
**first-stage flat-ground task** on two RTX 4090 in at most 1.5 times the incumbent's time reproduced on `trj`
(planning value 45 minutes; ambition 30 minutes). The incumbent reference is a FACT to be re-read from its
source: a published GPU learning stack trains two humanoids in under 30 minutes each, and a third in under 15,
on two RTX 4090, in what it calls first-stage flat-ground training: velocity tracking on flat ground, before any
rough-terrain stage. Humanoid briefs are where training dominates the design loop (over 90% of a composite
iteration of about 31 minutes), so this is the row that says whether the engine makes the dominant phase worse.

The row can only be measured if the task exists. The M2 task families are velocity tracking and terrain
traversal, written for the legged and wheeled reference classes, and the reference training gate trains the
quadruped only. No other piece of work defines the humanoid task, its threshold or its training recipe, and the
bead that measures row 9 (N36-22) records `not run: task not available` without it. This bead supplies them.

Terms. A *return threshold* is the mean episodic return on a fixed development stream that corresponds to the
task's requirement being met. *Attainment* is reaching a target with error control over every evaluation look;
a first crossing of the curve is descriptive only. *Equal task quality* between two stacks cannot be read from
returns, because their reward definitions differ; it is read from one requirement evaluated on both final
policies.

Tier: Solid. Rejected alternatives: timing the rough-terrain humanoid task (the published under-30-minute figure
is not for it; its own published context is about an hour on one card); taking the incumbent's return value as
our threshold (returns of different reward definitions are not comparable); letting the measuring bead define
the task (the denominator and the numerator of a ratio would then be chosen by the bead that reports it).

## Scope

In: the task file; its requirement, used both for the threshold and for the equal-quality check; the threshold's
registration procedure; the training recipe; the negative controls that show the task is learned and not
plumbing; the comparator setting records for row 9; a smoke run that fits the default machine envelope; one
confirming training on one card. Out: measuring row 9 and its verdict (N36-22); the two-card runtime (N36-08);
the trainer and its rules (the PPO seed bead of the learning unit); the training verbs; the attainment procedure
and the evaluation report (the learning unit's beads, used here); the task terms and the velocity-tracking
family this task instantiates (the task units' beads); the incumbent's recipe and pin (N36-25 and the incumbent
harness); the humanoid archetype and reference-robot assets.

## Technical approach

**1. The task file** (`humanoid-flat-velocity.ftask`, beside the quadruped reference task; an instance of the
velocity-tracking family on flat ground, written with the family's task terms). Design: the model that workload
H names. Scene: the flat ground of workload H. Engine settings come from the workload record and are not
restated in the task. Control: joint position targets for the 29 actuated joints about the nominal standing
pose, through the actuator driver loop, at 50 Hz. Episode: 20 s; reset from the settled-pose cache;
termination on a fall (any contact of a body other than a foot with the ground, or pelvis height below half its
nominal value).

**2. Command.** A planar velocity command (forward, lateral, yaw rate), redrawn at the family's physical-time
switch instants from a stream keyed by world address. DESIGN default ranges, replaced by the frozen incumbent
recipe's ranges where they differ, so that both arms face the same commands: forward −1.0 to 1.0 m/s, lateral
−0.5 to 0.5 m/s, yaw rate −1.0 to 1.0 rad/s, with a share of zero commands (standing) of 0.1.

**3. Observations and actions (DESIGN defaults, all in the task file).** Actor: base angular velocity (3),
projected gravity (3), command (3), joint positions relative to nominal (29), joint velocities (29), previous
action (29): 96 values, with sensor noise from the sensor cards. Critic-only: base linear velocity (3), foot
contact flags (2), applied joint torques (29). Action: 29 position offsets, scaled by 0.25 rad.

**4. Requirement (what "the task is solved" means; DESIGN scales).** Over the last 10 s of an episode: mean
planar velocity error at most 0.25 m/s, mean yaw-rate error at most 0.25 rad/s, and no fall. Target fraction:
at least 0.9 of episodes on the development stream. The requirement is written in the task's requirement
grammar and is the only definition of quality used by this bead and by row 9.

**5. Registering the target, before any timed run.** The development stream is fixed first: held-out seeds and
command schedules, disjoint from training worlds, five seeds. Then, in order of preference:
   1. *Return target.* If a baseline controller for the humanoid passes the requirement on the development
      stream, the return threshold is a stated fraction (DESIGN 0.9) of that controller's mean return, recorded
      with the controller's identity. This is the procedure of the quadruped reference task.
   2. *Success target.* Biped balance baselines are a Frontier route at M2 and may not pass. Then the target is
      registered as a success target: requirement pass rate at least 0.9 on the development stream, under the
      success-target form of the attainment procedure. The row is then "time to the requirement threshold", and
      every record says so.
   The registration (target kind, value, stream identity, evaluation cadence) is committed before the first
   gated or timed training; a target chosen after looking at a learning curve voids the run.

**6. Training recipe (DESIGN defaults; every value is in the run manifest; tuned on development seeds only and
frozen before row 9's first pair).** PPO under the trainer's rules; network `mlp:512,256,128`, ELU, asymmetric
critic; physical discount and trace rates equal to the quadruped task's (0.99 and 0.95 at the 20 ms interval);
clip 0.2; five epochs, four minibatches; 24 control intervals per collection; KL-adaptive step size; 4,096
worlds per card (8,192 in total on two cards under the global normalization rule, 4,096 on one); explicit
resets; profile `device-replay` (class D2). Command: `frob train start <workload H's model> --task
humanoid-flat-velocity --network mlp:512,256,128 --backend gpu-cuda [--shards 2] --worlds 4096 --seed <s> --key
<k> --json` (the design positional and `--key`, as the `train.start` row spells them; `--shards` is N23a-14's
pass-through to the two-shard update).

**7. Comparator settings for row 9.** The setting records of workload H gain the row-9 entries: `native` (the
incumbent's own first-stage flat-ground recipe for its closest humanoid, its step and decimation, with the
source) and, where its solver allows, `matched` (our step, decimation and iteration budget). Which incumbent,
revision and configuration is the comparator is the comparator decision's; this bead supplies the task-side
facts it needs (robot, ground, command ranges, episode length).

**8. Equal-quality check (used by N36-22).** `quality(policy, stack)`: the requirement of item 4 evaluated on
the final policy, in the stack that trained it, over the same number of episodes and the same command
schedules; reported as a pass rate with an exact binomial interval. Two arms have equal task quality when
neither pass rate's lower bound is below 0.9, or both are below it and their intervals overlap; a row-9 pair in
which only our arm fails is `outside tolerance` whatever the times.

**9. Evidence and determinism.** Training under `device-replay` is class D2 on one device family. Returns and
pass rates are class `Tested` on a reference fixture; nothing here is a claim about a physical robot.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob task check humanoid-flat-velocity --json` passes; the task identity changes when any field of items 1 to 4 changes; the task names workload H's model and restates no engine setting.
- [ ] The target registration (kind, value, development stream identity, cadence) is committed before the first timed training, and the record states which of the two procedures of item 5 was used and why.
- [ ] Smoke (default machine envelope): 64 worlds on `batch-cpu` for a fixed number of updates give a return that exceeds the zero-weight control's return by a margin declared in advance, twice with identical weights digests.
- [ ] Confirming run: on one RTX 4090, at least one seed of the frozen recipe attains the registered target, with time to attainment, environment steps, both throughput units and the learner's share recorded under a complete disclosure block; if the run needs more than the envelope class in force allows it is recorded `not run` with the reason and this bead stays open.
- [ ] Negative controls on the development stream: the zero-weight policy and a random untrained policy both miss the target; with the command channel zeroed the trained policy's return changes.
- [ ] The row-9 setting records exist for workload H with a source for the native record.
- [ ] `quality` returns the pass rate with its interval for a policy of ours, and its three verdict cases are unit-tested.
- [ ] The recipe file is frozen (content hash recorded) before N36-22's first pair.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-learn reference_humanoid` and `cargo test -p frob-conformance perf::row9_task`:
- `task_parses_and_has_29_actions`; `actor_observation_is_96_values`; `critic_only_channels_not_in_actor`.
- `requirement_boundary_cases`: velocity error 0.24 m/s with no fall passes; 0.26 fails; a fall at 19.9 s fails; a fall at 9.9 s (before the dwell) fails, since a fall terminates the episode.
- `command_schedule_identical_across_designs` (the same world address gives the same command schedule for two models).
- `registration_precedes_runs` (commit order); `success_target_form_when_no_baseline_passes`.
- `quality_verdicts`: (0.95 with bound 0.91, 0.94 with bound 0.90) → equal; (0.80, 0.82) with overlapping intervals → equal and both flagged below target; (0.70 ours, 0.95 incumbent) → not equal.
- `zero_weight_policy_outputs_nominal_pose`; `recipe_hash_frozen`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/perf_humanoid_task.sh` (owned by this bead; sources the shared end-to-end helper; seed-explicit;
idempotent). Steps: `check` (`frob task check humanoid-flat-velocity --json`); `smoke` (`frob train start <H>
--task humanoid-flat-velocity --backend batch-cpu --worlds 64 --steps <fixed small budget> --seed 7 --key
hft-smoke-7-a --json`, `frob job wait`, `frob train status <job> --json`, then the same under `hft-smoke-7-b` so
that the second run computes, asserting equal weights digests; export; `frob eval run <H> --policy <smoke id>
--task humanoid-flat-velocity --stream dev --key hft-ev-smoke --json`); `controls` (`frob eval run <H> --policy
zero:<smoke id> --task humanoid-flat-velocity --stream dev --key hft-ev-zero --json` and the same with `--policy
random:<smoke id>:<seed>` under `hft-ev-random`, N23b-15's forms, where the id names the trained policy whose
network and normalizer the control copies; the smoke margin over the zero-weight return is asserted); `confirm`
(the one-card training `frob train start <H> --task humanoid-flat-velocity --backend gpu-cuda --devices 0 --seed
<s> --key hft-confirm-<s> --json`, admitted through the compute-envelope check, then `frob train export <job>
--to store --json`, `frob eval run <H> --policy <id> --task humanoid-flat-velocity --stream dev --key
hft-ev-confirm-<s> --json`, the same two controls against it, and the command-channel-zeroed evaluation (`--policy masked:<id>:<command
role>`, N23b-15's form, key `hft-ev-masked-<s>`, asserting a changed return);
recorded `not run` with the reason when the envelope does not admit it). Each NDJSON line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit
code, wall time, the envelope's evidence block (class, tier, determinism, identities, seed), `metric` lines for
return, pass rate with its interval, steps and seconds to attainment, env-steps/s and world-steps/s at
decimation 10, and the learner's share; on failure the first failing assertion and the run identifier. A
`summary` line counts assertions and lists steps `not run` with reasons.

### Known answers and negative controls

- Arithmetic checks: 24 intervals × 8,192 worlds = 196,608 samples per update on two cards; at the planning value of 45 minutes and 100 k env-steps/s the run is 2.7·10⁸ env-steps.
- Negative controls: a threshold lowered until the random policy passes (the control then passes and the registration is rejected); a time read at first crossing; a quality check that compares returns across stacks instead of the requirement.
- Determinism lanes: the smoke run twice on one machine and across worker counts on `batch-cpu`.

## Considerations

- OPEN-SPARK-2: the confirming run may exceed one hour on one card and row 9 needs two cards; both need the gate-measurement class. Under the default class the smoke run is the only training that runs, and the bead is not closed on it alone.
- If the sharding kill gate fires, row 9 is measured on one card against the incumbent's one-card time; this task and recipe are unchanged, and the record says which configuration ran.
- The DESIGN scales of the requirement are the weakest part of this bead. They are fixed before any timed run and changed only by a dated amendment that also voids earlier times.
- A humanoid that learns to stand and not to walk can score well on a return; the requirement's tracking terms and the zero-command share of 0.1 are what prevent a standing policy from attaining the target.
- The incumbent's humanoid differs from ours in mass and joint count; the pair is a parity measurement on closest-available models, and the difference is part of the row's written disclosure.
