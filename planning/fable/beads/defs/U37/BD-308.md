## Background

Every number that crosses the agent boundary carries a claim product: the meet, over all of its inputs, of
proof class (Proved, Certified, Tested, Estimated), tier, determinism class, and the reality and
model-conditional labels. The weakest input wins by construction.

- The envelope serializer accepts evidence only as a claim-product value. The type has no constructor from
  literals outside its owning crate and no conversion toward a stronger class, so nothing can be converted
  into a proof.
- An envelope names which of the seven claims its result supports and lists explicit non-claims, at least
  `reality: no-claim` wherever the calibration lane has supplied no data.
- A certificate bundle stores the product together with its inputs, and `frob-verify` recomputes it.
- Citing a result above its product is refused (`FROB-REF-819`): for example, offering a provisional
  fast-tier result where an established one is required.

Smallest case that breaks the obvious alternatives: design A was evaluated on the referee and design B on a
fast tier, still provisional. A "majority" or "best input" rule labels their comparison established. The
product labels it provisional, which is all the evidence supports. A longer worked case: a comparison of two
designs consumes a kernel whose group-membership identity is Certified, a friction coefficient from an
interface-card field marked `assumed` (Estimated), and a paired study on a GPU tier under determinism class D2
(Tested). The product is a Design comparison of class Estimated, its weakest input is the friction field of
the card, its tier is the GPU tier and so it is provisional until promoted, and its reality scope is
`no-claim`; the report prints that in one line and offers, as its first next action, the measurement that
would raise the weakest input.

Why a type and not a convention: an optimizing agent will find any path by which a statistical result can be
reported as a proof, and a rule that lives in a style guide is such a path. Invariant **INV-SAFE-06**: the
evidence in an envelope equals the claim product of its inputs. Gate **GATE-SAFE-08** (Known answer, TARGET):
over random sets of input evidence the envelope's class equals the meet; no public function returns a
stronger class than its inputs. Its first record is taken at M1 (N37-25). This seed bead makes the rule hold
on **every verb of the M2 and M3 surface**, where evidence starts to flow through stores, caches, aggregates
and devices, and re-records the gate. Seed done-condition: GATE-SAFE-08.

## Scope

In: evidence propagation through stored results, the memo store, aggregates, jobs and progress frames; the
`claims` and `non_claims` members on every verb; inputs stored with the product; the "first next action
raises the weakest input" rule; the handler lint; the bundle-side check through the auditor; the M3 random-set
campaign on real tiers; the API scan over the M3 workspace; the gate record. Out: the type (foundation
crate); the M1 measurement harness (N37-25); the refusal of citations above the product (N37-26); the D4
refusals (N37-28); the auditor's implementation (certificate chapter).

## Technical approach

1. **Evidence is carried, never rebuilt.** Every stored result row (run, episode set, study, comparison,
   verdict, analysis result, memo node, scorecard row, mission cell) has a `claim_product` column holding the
   canonical serialization of its product and an `inputs_root` (the Merkle root over the content hashes of
   its inputs, each of which has its own product). A handler obtains a product in exactly three ways: from a
   leaf constructor it is permitted to call; from the typed loader of a stored result; from `combine` over
   products it holds. It never parses one from JSON and never edits one.
2. **Handler lint (DESIGN).** Each verb's registry row lists `consumes = [...]`, the kinds of stored objects
   and leaf sources its result may depend on. A test harness runs each verb on fixtures with **taint**: every
   input fixture's product carries a unique condition flag `taint:<n>` (a test-only condition, joined by
   union like every other); the envelope's `conditions` must contain the taint of every input the registry
   lists and that the request named. A handler that forgot to combine one of its inputs is caught because its
   taint is missing. This is the live-dependency proof for evidence: perturb an input's evidence and the
   output's evidence must change.
3. **Aggregates and caches.** A scorecard, a study, a comparison, a cascade stage and a report combine the
   products of all rows they aggregate, including rows that failed or were excluded with a reason (a failure
   is a failed cell, never an excluded cell). The memo store returns `(value, product)` and a memo hit is a
   stored result like any other; a hit never returns a value without its product. Under shared batches the
   product of a world's result does not depend on its co-tenants.
4. **Jobs and progress.** A job's terminal receipt carries the product of what was published. Progress frames
   carry no evidence block (they are not results). After a budget stop or a cancellation nothing partial is
   published as a result, so there is no partial product either.
5. **Claims and non-claims.** For every verb, the output schema requires `evidence.claims` (which of the
   seven claims the result may be cited for; empty for most studio answers) and `evidence.non_claims`
   (explicit; always `reality: no-claim` unless the calibration lane supplied a band for a calibrated family
   that every input shares; plus, where they apply, `fidelity: not-established` for an unpromoted fast-tier
   result and `performance: no-claim` for a result with no disclosure block). The registry row
   `claim_kinds = [...]` bounds what a verb may ever put in `claims`; the serializer refuses to write a kind
   outside it (an internal fault, exit 9, not a user refusal). Reports use only the permitted wording of each
   claim kind, and the word "verified" describes no result.
6. **Next action raises the weakest input.** When the product's class or standing is below the best its kind
   allows, the first entry of `next_actions` is the action registered for the weakest input's source kind:
   an `assumed` card field → the measurement or card update that would replace it; a provisional fast-tier
   result → `eval promote`; an unknown or D3 placement → the device check. The mapping is a registry table
   (`registries/raise-actions.toml`), and commands are built from identifiers only.
7. **Bundles.** A certificate bundle and a report manifest store the product and the list of inputs with
   their own products. The auditor recomputes the product from the listed inputs with its own code and
   rejects a bundle whose stored product is stronger in any member than the recomputation, or whose
   assumptions are missing. This bead adds the M3 bundle kinds to the conformance corpus with two mutants
   each: the stored class raised by one step; one input removed with hashes made consistent.
8. **The M3 campaign.** The random-set test of the M1 harness is repeated with leaves that are real results
   of the M3 surface: referee runs, `batch-cpu` runs, device runs where a device is present, analyses,
   certified kernel identities, assumed and measured card fields, studies and promoted results; sets of 2 to
   16; N = 10⁴ (TARGET). Combination is done by the real aggregating verbs (`eval.compare`, `study.compare`,
   `eval.scorecard`, `explain.compare`, `report.run`) and by N37-25's test-support verb `testprobe.combine`
   (present only in builds with `frob-verbs`' `conformance` feature; there is no `conformance` verb); each
   envelope is compared with the independent oracle.
9. **API scan at M3.** The scan of public functions with evidence in and out is re-run over the M3
   workspace; every such function has a property; leaf constructors equal the doorway list.
10. **Record.** Verbs covered by the taint lint (n of n), sets run and mismatches (0), bundle mutants
    rejected, functions scanned and missing properties (0), controls caught.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SAFE-08 on the M3 surface (TARGET): over 10⁴ random sets of real input evidence the envelope's
      class equals the meet, and no public function returns a stronger class than its inputs. Recorded with
      counts (seed done-condition).
- [ ] The taint lint passes for every verb that consumes stored results; a planted handler that ignores one
      input fails it.
- [ ] A memo hit returns the same product as the original computation; a scorecard over 100 runs with one
      Estimated input is Estimated.
- [ ] Every M2 and M3 envelope has `claims` inside its verb's `claim_kinds` and `non_claims` containing
      `reality: no-claim`.
- [ ] For a provisional comparison the first next action is `eval promote` on the weakest input; for an
      assumed card field it is the registered measurement action.
- [ ] Both bundle mutants of each M3 bundle kind are rejected by `frob-verify`.
- [ ] The worked case reproduces (KA-027, N00-KA; this bead restates its fixture and adds no second row):
      {Certified kernel identity, Estimated card field, Tested D2 GPU study} as a Design comparison → class
      Estimated, weakest = the card field, standing provisional, reality no-claim.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-829 (the product of {Tested on the referee, Tested and provisional on a fast tier} is provisional; the
envelope's class equals the meet over random sets; exact; control: a public function that returns a stronger
class than its inputs). KA-027 (N00-KA; the worked claim-product case of the Background: a design comparison
over a Certified kernel identity, an `assumed` friction field (Estimated) and a Tested D2 GPU study is a Design
comparison of class Estimated, weakest input the friction field, provisional until promoted, reality
`no-claim`; exact; tier G0; control: a product reported at the class of its strongest input), exercised by
`worked_case_estimated_weakest_card_field`. Also the class rule's own known answer: an exact argument over an
Estimated material card is Estimated. Negative controls registered against GATE-SAFE-08: a `combine` that takes the maximum; a
"best input" standing; a handler that drops one input; a memo store that returns values without products; a
planted class-raising public function. Metamorphic relations: permuting inputs changes nothing; replacing an
input by a weaker one never strengthens any member. Determinism lanes: the campaign's results are identical
for the same seed twice on one machine, across worker counts, in debug and release and across platforms;
device leaves are compared within their own determinism class.

## Considerations

- Carrying the product costs one small column per stored result and one meet per aggregation; neither is in
  a hot loop.
- The taint lint is the strongest check here: random sets test the rule, taint tests that each handler
  actually applies it to all of its inputs.
- A proved kernel fed an assumed friction coefficient produces an estimate; that is the intended outcome, and
  the report's first next action is how the agent learns what would raise it.
- Conditions travel: `tier-sensitive`, `controller-sensitive` and `model-conditional` are joined by union and
  cannot be dropped by a consumer; a model-conditional result may not spend prototype budget (a refusal owned
  by the learning chapter).
- The seed lists this bead at M3 while the gate's first date is M1; the M1 record is N37-25's.
