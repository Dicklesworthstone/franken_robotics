## Background

The privacy mark and the licence set of an artifact are the joins of those of its lineage: a still of a
replay of a policy evaluated in a private scene is private without anyone marking it. One class of artifact
makes that rule expensive: **policy weights**. Weights trained in a scene built from a scan of the owner's
home are, by the rule, private; weights trained on a scene containing a research-only asset are, by the rule,
research-only. They then cannot be exported, cannot cross to the other machine without the private export
scope and flag, vanish with `scene.purge`, and pass their marks on to every evaluation of the policy anywhere.

**OPEN-SAFE-2.** *Whether policy weights trained in a private or research-only scene inherit its mark and
licence set.* Owner: the project owner. Blocks: GATE-SAFE-04 (privacy) and GATE-SAFE-05 (licences), because
both gates list "each derived artifact kind" and weights are one. **Fail-closed default: they inherit both.**
The safety chapter itself notes that the default may be stricter than the owner wants.

Why the default is inheritance. A trained network can memorize its training environment: a policy that has
learned the layout of one kitchen carries information about that kitchen, and the risk register lists exactly
this path (RISK-12: a scan of a private place leaks through a still, a fixture or policy weights). For
licences the question is contractual, not technical: many non-commercial dataset terms restrict derived
models. In both cases the tool cannot tell a harmless set of weights from a harmful one, so it refuses on
doubt.

## Scope

In: the options; what each changes in the mark's parents table, the purge closure, exports and the two gates;
the record of the decision; the default that holds meanwhile. Out: the mark lattice and join (N37-17); the
export rules (BD-305, N37-18); purge (N37-19).

## Technical approach

1. **Options.**
   | Option | Privacy mark of weights | Licence set of weights | Consequence |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | A (default) | Inherited from every training scene | Inherited | Weights from a private scene stay on the machine unless exported with the private scope and flag; purge removes them; research-only assets block export of the policy |
   | B | Inherited | Not inherited (weights carry only the licences of their initial weights and donors) | Policies trained on research-only scenes can be exported; the owner accepts the contractual risk per dataset |
   | C | Not inherited when the training set is "wide" (at least N scenes, of which private ones are a small share, with a stated threshold) | As A or B | Needs a memorization argument the project does not have; N and the share would be assumptions |
   | D | Never inherited | Never inherited | Fastest workflow; RISK-12's weight path is open |
   Option C is recorded because it is the one an owner is most likely to ask for; the fail-closed reading is
   that without measured evidence about memorization it is option D with extra steps, and it is not offered
   as a default.
2. **Where the decision lands.** One key in the owner-held safety configuration,
   `private.weights_inherit` (default `true`), and one, `licence.weights_inherit` (default `true`; DESIGN:
   a separate key so option B is expressible). The mark's parents table has the row "policy weights,
   checkpoint → the training run (hence its scenes), the initial weights, the donor"; with a key set to
   `false`, the training run is omitted as a parent for that component of the mark only. The configuration
   digest is recorded in every job row, so a later reader can tell under which rule a checkpoint was marked.
   Marks already computed are not lowered when a key changes: the new rule applies to checkpoints written
   afterwards, and a deliberate relabel of an existing checkpoint is an explicit, logged owner act.
3. **Effects that tests must show for the chosen option.**
   - Gate rows: the artifact-kind sweep of the privacy gate and the laundering battery of the licence gate
     each have a "trained weights" row whose expected result depends on the option; the expected value is
     read from the configuration, so the gates stay meaningful under any option.
   - Purge: under A the weights are members of the closure; under D they are not, and the dry run says so.
   - Cross-machine transfer of weights: under A, weights from a private scene are a private export.
   - Evaluation hygiene is unaffected by the option: the manifest's `trained_on` still names the scenes, so an
     evaluation that reuses a training scene is still refused.
4. **Record.** An ADR for OPEN-SAFE-2 signed by the owner: the option, the reason, the datasets or places it
   was decided for, and the rejected options; the two configuration keys with their values; the open-question
   register updated. Deadline: before the M3 exit review measures GATE-SAFE-04 and GATE-SAFE-05. If
   undecided, "default adopted (option A)" is recorded and both gates are measured under A.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An ADR for OPEN-SAFE-2 exists, or the M3 exit record states "default adopted: weights inherit mark and
      licence set".
- [ ] Under the default, weights trained in a private, research-only scene are private and research-only;
      `train.export` of them is refused; they are in the purge closure.
- [ ] With `licence.weights_inherit = false` (option B, in a test configuration), the same weights are
      private and their licence class does not include the scene's research-only entry.
- [ ] Changing a key does not lower the mark of an existing checkpoint.
- [ ] `frob doctor --json` prints both keys and whether each is a default.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-chronicle --test weights_inherit` (a `synthetic` private, research-only scene; a short
`batch-cpu` training run):
- `default_weights_inherit_both`.
- `option_b_drops_licence_only`.
- `option_d_drops_both` (test configuration only).
- `existing_marks_not_lowered_on_config_change`.
- `purge_closure_follows_option`.
- `trained_on_still_names_scenes_under_every_option`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/safe_private_licence.sh` (N37-21) reads the two keys from `frob doctor --json`, logs them as a
`metric` line (`weights_inherit`, with the ADR identifier or `default`), and selects the expected result of
its "trained weights" rows accordingly.

### Known answers and negative controls
Known answer under the default: the weights' mark equals the scene's mark joined with the initial weights'
mark. Negative control: a `mutants` build in which checkpoints are written as root objects (no parents) must
fail `default_weights_inherit_both`.

## Considerations

- Option A makes a policy trained on a real kitchen hard to move to the training machine. The intended
  workflow keeps a job that needs a private scan on the machine that holds it; training at scale on private
  scenes is therefore limited to that machine, which is a cost the owner should weigh.
- Option B is a contractual judgement per dataset, not a blanket one; the ADR should name the datasets.
- Nothing in this decision affects evaluation claims: a policy's results are established by evaluation, and
  the evaluation's own scenes mark its outputs.
- If measured evidence about memorization becomes available (for example a membership-style test on scene
  layouts), option C can be revisited with numbers; until then it stays unoffered.
