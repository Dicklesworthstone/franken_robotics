## Background

Every result records a determinism class: D0 exact; D1 bitwise across backends; D2 bitwise per backend and
independent of batch composition; D3 agreement inside a recorded tolerance with agreement of task decisions;
**D4 unspecified, which guarantees nothing**. Training may run under a profile that gives up run-to-run
reproducibility for throughput (the profile `fast-training`: asynchronous delivery of collected segments and
gradient sums between shards, a relaxed reduction order and matrix-product path in the learner, minibatches
composed by arrival). It is allowed on one condition: **it is labeled, and the label has consequences.**

- Every result, checkpoint and policy produced under it carries determinism class D4 and the profile's name.
- A D4 result is never evidence. Paired statistics, promotion, qualification, certificates and build packets
  refuse it (`FROB-REF-825`).
- Weights trained under it are admissible as data, like any other policy file. Every claim about them comes
  from re-evaluation under D2 or stricter, and that evaluation, not the training run, is what is cited.
- The profile relaxes ordering and placement only. The bans on fast-math relaxation, fused multiply-add,
  built-in transcendentals in batched kernels and floating-point atomics hold under it.

Why the refusal is by code and not by convention: under class D4 nothing about an arm can be replayed, so its
verdict could not be reproduced, promoted or attributed. An agent that trains fast and then cites the training
curve as a comparison is the threat "a result cited as more than it is". The profile's own ceiling is small
(inference and learning are about 13% of training wall-clock on one measured incumbent stack, so relaxing them
gains at most 1.15 on one device; its real use is the asynchronous two-device variant), which is one more
reason nothing may lean on its numbers.

Invariant **INV-SAFE-07**: no D4 result is consumed by paired statistics, promotion, qualification, a
certificate or a build packet. Gate **GATE-SAFE-09** (Known answer, TARGET, M3): a result produced under the
nondeterministic training profile is refused on each of the five paths of INV-SAFE-07; the same policy
re-evaluated under D2 is accepted. The Gauntlet's certifier table adds: a D4 result offered unlabeled is
refused on every path.

The profile, the class licences and their own refusals belong to the numerics chapter's bead. This bead is the
safety-side enforcement: the label cannot be lost, the five consumers refuse, and weights pass as data.

## Scope

## Technical approach

1. **What carries the label.** A job started with `train.start --profile fast-training` writes: the job row
   and receipt (`determinism: D4`, `profile: fast-training`); every training-time result (learning curves,
   returns, in-training evaluations, `train.status` envelopes), whose claim product has determinism D4 and an
   empty `claims` list; every checkpoint and exported policy, whose manifest carries
   `trained_on.determinism = "D4"` and `trained_on.profile = "fast-training"`. Determinism is a member of the
   claim product and combines by "weakest wins", so anything computed from a D4 result is D4.
2. **Absent means D4.** A stored result with no recorded determinism class, or a result presented from
   outside the store with no product, is treated as D4 for every check below (the same fail-closed reading as
   a missing authorization flag). This is what defeats "the D4 result offered unlabeled": stripping the label
   does not produce a stronger class, because class is never inferred upward.
3. **Results against weights (DESIGN).** A *result* is a number with a claim product. A *weights artifact* is
   data with an identity. When weights are an input to an evaluation they contribute their content hash to
   the evaluation's inputs and **no determinism class**: the evaluation's class is that of its own kernels,
   placement and profile. So a policy trained under D4 and evaluated under `device-replay` yields a D2 result
   (deterministic given the weights), and that result is what may be cited. The manifest's `trained_on` block
   stays as provenance and is printed in reports ("trained under fast-training (D4); evaluated under
   device-replay (D2)").
4. **The five paths.** Each consumer takes stored results through the common admission function
   `admit(product, requirement)`, whose first check is determinism: D4 or unrecorded → `FROB-REF-825`.
   | Path | Verb and argument | What is offered in the gate's planted case |
   |---|---|---|
   | Paired statistics | `study.compare` and `eval.compare` reading stored arm results | The in-training evaluation of the D4 run as one arm |
   | Promotion | `eval.promote <result>` | The D4 run's final training return |
   | Qualification | `eval.qualify` (a baseline or candidate standing, or a request whose tier plan names the D4 profile) | The D4 result as the candidate's supporting evidence; a tier plan with `fast-training` |
   | Certificate | `prove.bundle` with a numeric input or a cited result | The D4 result as a cited input of the bundle |
   | Build packet | The build packet's evidence argument | The D4 result as cited evidence (at M3 through the admission function with the build-packet requirement; with the real verb at M5) |
   The check runs in the dispatcher before the handler, so a refused request spends nothing.
5. **The refusal.** `FROB-REF-825`, "a result of class D4 offered as evidence". Exit 3; `retry:
   after-change`. Fields: `pointer` (the argument and the result), `observed` (`D4` or `unrecorded`, and the
   profile name when known), `required` (the minimum class of the path), `affected_claims`. Ranked repairs
   (DESIGN): (1) re-evaluate the policy under `device-replay` (D2) or stricter and offer that result: the
   refusal carries the ready `eval run` command built from identifiers; (2) re-run the training under the
   default deterministic profile if the training-time number itself is what is needed; (3) none other: a
   class is never raised by relabeling, only by running again under a stricter profile.
6. **Neighbouring codes (so that each request gets exactly one).** `FROB-REF-561`: a paired statistic is
   *requested to be run* with an arm whose profile is D4 or unrecorded (a property of the execution being
   planned; raised at study planning). `FROB-REF-861`: a number from a `fast-training` run is *cited* in a
   report or a claim, or a policy trained under it is offered for promotion or qualification with no stricter
   re-evaluation on record. `FROB-REF-825`: a stored D4 *result* is offered to one of the five consumers.
   On the five paths of the gate the expected code is 825.
7. **Re-evaluation is accepted.** `eval run --policy <the D4-trained weights> --profile device-replay` twice
   gives identical digests; its result is D2 and passes the determinism check of each of the five paths (other
   requirements of a path, such as standing, apply as usual).
8. **The bans hold.** The kernel lint that rejects fast-math relaxation, fused multiply-add, built-in
   transcendentals and floating-point atomics in batched kernels runs on every kernel regardless of profile.
   The profile's knob set is closed (shard delivery order, learner reduction order, matrix-product path,
   minibatch composition by arrival, the declared bound on version lag); any other key in a profile request
   is `FROB-REF-802`. The physics step stays class D2 under the profile; a test compares the per-world state
   digests of collection under `fast-training` with a replay of the same addresses under `device-replay`.
9. **Per job only.** The profile is enabled by an argument of one job; no key of the owner-held
   configuration and no session scope makes it a default. The envelope of a job that used it says so in
   `evidence` and repeats, in `non_claims`, `training-numbers: not-evidence`.
10. **Record.** For each path: the planted D4 result refused with 825; the unlabeled variant refused; the D2
    re-evaluation accepted; plus the digest equality of two re-evaluations and the bans check.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] GATE-SAFE-09 (TARGET): a result produced under `fast-training` is refused with `FROB-REF-825` on each of
      the five paths; the same policy re-evaluated under D2 is accepted on each. Recorded per path.
- [ ] The same result with its determinism member removed (unrecorded) is refused on each path.
- [ ] Every artifact written by a `fast-training` job carries D4 and the profile name: job row, receipt,
      `train.status` envelopes, checkpoints, the exported policy's manifest.
- [ ] Two evaluations of the D4-trained policy under `device-replay` have identical digests, and the
      evaluation's `evidence.determinism` is D2.
- [ ] Citing the training return in a report is refused (the numerics chapter's `FROB-REF-861`), and planning
      a paired study with a D4 arm is refused (`FROB-REF-561`): one code per request.
- [ ] A `fast-training` request with an extra knob is `FROB-REF-802`; the ban lint passes on the profile's
      kernels and fails on a planted fused multiply-add; collection's per-world state digests equal the D2
      replay's.
- [ ] No configuration key or scope enables the profile by default.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-core --test admit_d4 -p frob-verbs --test d4_paths -p frob-learn --test d4_labels` (a
short `batch-cpu` training job under the profile; no mocks):
- `d4_first_in_precedence`: a product that is D4, Estimated and provisional → `FROB-REF-825`.
- `unrecorded_is_d4`.
- `five_paths_refuse_825`: one case per path with the planted result of the table.
- `five_paths_accept_d2_reeval`: the determinism check passes on each path for the re-evaluated result.
- `labels_on_every_artifact`: job row, receipt, status envelope, checkpoint manifest, exported manifest.
- `weights_contribute_identity_not_class`: an evaluation whose policy was trained under D4 has
  `determinism` equal to the evaluation profile's class and lists the policy's hash among its inputs.
- `reeval_twice_identical_digests`.
- `derived_from_d4_is_d4`: a scorecard that includes one D4 result is D4.
- `planned_d4_arm_is_561`; `cited_training_return_is_861`.
- `profile_knobs_closed`; `physics_digests_equal_under_profile`; `ban_lint_independent_of_profile`.
- `no_default_enable_key` (the owner-held configuration schema has no such key).
- Property test (INV-SAFE-07): for 10⁴ random products with determinism D4 or unrecorded and any other
  members, `admit` fails for each of the five uses.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/safe_d4.sh` (N37-29) runs the gate through the real `frob` binary and daemon with the structured
logging of STD-E2E: per path the command line, exit code, refusal code, `observed`, `required`, the envelope's
`evidence` block and the repair command.

### Known answers and negative controls
KA-834 (a result produced under the nondeterministic training profile is refused on each of the five paths of
INV-SAFE-07; the same policy re-evaluated under D2 is accepted; exact, verdict; tier G5, host; control: the
D4 result offered unlabeled). Known answer adopted from the numerics chapter: a `fast-training` policy
evaluated twice under `device-replay` gives identical digests, and citing its training return is refused.
Negative controls registered against GATE-SAFE-09: a `mutants` build in which unrecorded determinism is read
as D2 (the unlabeled result is accepted; the test must fail); one in which the promotion consumer skips
`admit`; one in which the weights' training class is propagated into the evaluation (the re-evaluation is
then refused and the gate's second clause fails). Determinism lanes: the refusals are D0; the re-evaluation is
run twice on one machine and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- Qualification has no natural "D4 result" input, since a request names a candidate and worlds are run by the
  lane; the planted cases are a supporting result and a tier plan naming the profile. If the qualification
  bead narrows what a request may carry, the planted case follows it.
- The fifth path uses the admission function directly until the build packet exists; its bead re-runs the
  planted case with the real verb and appends to this gate's record.
- Asynchronous two-device training (the profile's real use) is Frontier under its own kill gate; if it is
  killed the profile may never be used, and this bead's refusals cost nothing to keep.
- A D3 result is not D4: it is admissible where its licence allows (for example as `paired-by-address`,
  labelled); only D4 and unrecorded are refused outright here.
