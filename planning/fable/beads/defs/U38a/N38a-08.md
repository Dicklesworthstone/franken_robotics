## Background

The method-experiment register of FrankenRobotics (N38a-07) requires four computed quantities before and after
an experiment: the ceiling of the effect, the paired lower bound, the detection of a planted effect, and the
silence of an A/A arm. This bead is the small library that computes them, inside the Gauntlet's crate
`frob-conformance`, and the proof that it is exact where it must be. A harness that mis-states a bound makes
every kill rule built on it wrong, which is why the harness has its own proof obligation: PO-GAUNT-01, "the
paired lower bound of the harness is exact on constructed inputs: ten pairs of constant ratio r return r; an A/A
campaign does not clear 1.02".

DESIGN: a lower confidence bound on a paired ratio, never a ratio of means and never a point estimate. Reason:
pairing by world address and seed removes world-to-world variance, and a kill rule must read a bound. Rejected:
ratio of means (dominated by the fastest worlds, hides the world an arm never finishes); unpaired tests (need
many times the episodes when arms are correlated).

## Scope

In: the ceiling formula; the paired lower bound in ratio and difference form; the potency and A/A procedures;
the Student quantile table; exact arithmetic for the constructed cases. Out: the register and its refusals
(N38a-07); e-processes and exact fixed-n tests (the study-types bead BD-124); the disclosure block.

## Technical approach

**1. Ceiling from a phase breakdown.** Measure the baseline arm's wall time by phase (for example parse, assemble,
solve, check, serialize, harness). Let f be the fraction of baseline time the mechanism can improve, r the factor
by which it improves that fraction, and h the overhead the mechanism adds as a fraction of baseline time. Then

  speedup ≤ 1 / (1 − f + f/r + h),  and the ceiling is the limit r → ∞:  ceiling = 1 / (1 − f + h).

Equivalently, with T_base the baseline's total and T_untouched the part of the mechanism arm's time that the
mechanism does not touch, ceiling = T_base ÷ T_untouched. Worked values (known answers): infinite acceleration of
85% of the time gives at most 6.67; f = 0.97, r = 40, h = 0.02 give 13.47; with h = 0.02 no r reaches ten-fold
unless f ≥ 0.92. The planning pilot (FACT): specialized full batch 610.1 µs, specialized solve only 34.4 µs,
composed full batch 842.8 µs; untouched residual 610.1 − 34.4 = 575.7 µs; ceiling 842.8 ÷ 575.7 = 1.464; measured
1.345 warm and 1.088 cold. Three rules of use: f, r and h are measured on the strong baseline's critical path (a
comparator that also caches); a pruning ratio is never multiplied by a device speedup measured on work already
removed (100 candidates of 10 s; pruning removes 90; the ten hard survivors accelerate by 2, not the 5 measured
on the full set: 1,000 ÷ 50 = 20, not 50); queue time for proofs and transfers counts.

**2. Paired lower bound, ratio form.** For n ≥ 10 pairs (aᵢ, bᵢ) of positive measurements, the two arms of a pair
run back to back in randomized order on the same host state, workload and world addresses:
ℓᵢ = ln(aᵢ/bᵢ); ℓ̄ = (1/n)·Σ ℓᵢ; s² = (1/(n − 1))·Σ (ℓᵢ − ℓ̄)²; bound = exp(ℓ̄ − t·s/√n), where t is the 95th
percentile of Student's distribution with n − 1 degrees of freedom. Fewer than ten pairs is refused. A gate
threshold applies to the bound, never to the mean.

**3. Paired lower bound, difference form.** dᵢ = aᵢ − bᵢ; bound = d̄ − t·s_d/√n, same t.

**4. Quantile table (one-sided 95%).** Degrees of freedom → t: 9 → 1.833; 10 → 1.812; 11 → 1.796; 12 → 1.782;
13 → 1.771; 14 → 1.761; 15 → 1.753; 16 → 1.746; 17 → 1.740; 18 → 1.734; 19 → 1.729; 20 → 1.725; 24 → 1.711;
29 → 1.699; 30 → 1.697; 40 → 1.684; 60 → 1.671; 120 → 1.658; ∞ → 1.645. Between tabulated values the next
smaller tabulated degrees of freedom is used (the larger t), so the bound errs low.

**5. Exactness on constructed inputs.** When every ℓᵢ is equal, s is exactly 0 in the implementation (the sum of
squared deviations is computed from differences to the first element, not from Σℓ² − n·ℓ̄²), and the bound is
returned as the input ratio itself, carried as a rational when the inputs are rationals. Ten pairs (3, 2) return
exactly 3/2.

**6. Potency control.** Before the real arms run, the harness runs the experiment's pipeline on a planted effect
of the declared size (for a speed claim: a baseline arm with a deterministic delay removed, of known true ratio
ρ; for an analysis or a screen: the design corpus with planted defects and planted winners). Pass: the lower
bound exceeds 1 and the upper bound covers ρ. An experiment whose pipeline does not detect its planted effect is
`not-potent` and is not run.

**7. A/A arm.** Both arms are the same build and configuration. Pass: the lower bound does not exceed 1.02. An
A/A campaign that clears 1.02 means the harness is broken (ordering effects, warm caches, host drift), and the
experiment's results are void until it is fixed. (The performance program separately requires that of 100 A/A
campaigns at most 9 clear 1.02.)

**API (DESIGN sketch).** `ceiling(phases, touched) -> Ceiling`; `paired_ratio_lower_bound(pairs) -> Bound`;
`paired_difference_lower_bound(pairs) -> Bound`; `potency(run, planted) -> Potency`; `aa(run) -> AaResult`.
Outputs are evidence of class `Tested` and carry the pairing unit and the determinism class of the arms.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] PO-GAUNT-01: ten pairs of constant ratio r return exactly r, for r in {1, 3/2, 10}; an A/A campaign on the
      harness's own fixture does not clear 1.02.
- [ ] The ceiling formula reproduces 6.67, 13.47, the f ≥ 0.92 condition and 1.464.
- [ ] Fewer than ten pairs is refused; a non-positive measurement in ratio form is refused.
- [ ] A planted effect of ratio 1.25 is detected by the potency procedure; with the effect removed it is not.
- [ ] The quantile lookup never returns a t smaller than the exact value for the requested degrees of freedom.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/experiment_stats.rs`:
- `po_gaunt_01_constant_ratio`: ten pairs (3, 2) → 3/2 exactly; ten pairs (10, 1) → 10; ten pairs (1, 1) → 1.
- `ratio_bound_known_value`: ℓ = (0.10, 0.20, 0.10, 0.20, 0.10, 0.20, 0.10, 0.20, 0.10, 0.20): ℓ̄ = 0.15,
  s = 0.052705, bound = exp(0.15 − 1.833 × 0.052705 ÷ √10) = exp(0.119450) = 1.1269 (to four decimals).
- `difference_bound_known_value`: d = (1, 2, 1, 2, 1, 2, 1, 2, 1, 2): d̄ = 1.5, s = 0.52705, bound = 1.1945.
- `fewer_than_ten_refused`; `nonpositive_refused`.
- `ceiling_amdahl_values`: (f = 0.85, h = 0) → 6.667; (0.97, r = 40, 0.02) → 13.468; residual form
  842.8 ÷ 575.7 → 1.4640.
- `pruning_times_device_counterexample`: 1,000 ÷ 50 = 20.
- `quantile_conservative`: df = 22 uses the entry for 20 (1.725).
- `aa_does_not_clear`: 20 pairs of the same deterministic workload → bound ≤ 1.02.
- `potency_detects_planted`: planted ratio 1.25 → lower bound > 1 and interval covers 1.25.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/experiment_stats.sh` (header: `bead=N38a-08 milestone=M1 labels=gauntlet gates=GATE-GAUNT-09
plants=ratio-of-means`). It runs the statistics test target, then an A/A campaign and a potency campaign on a
small real workload (the `frob` binary's own `doctor` verb timed twenty times per arm, with a planted sleep in
the potency arm), then plants `ratio-of-means` (the bound replaced by the ratio of arm means) and requires the
constant-ratio test with one outlier pair to fail. Log at `target/e2e-logs/experiment_stats/<utc>.ndjson`: the
commands, the bounds as `metric` lines with thresholds (A/A `<= 1.02`; potency `> 1`), the host load, the
negative-control assertion, and the `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-807 (ten pairs each with ratio exactly 1.5 give a lower bound of 1.5; its control is the A/A campaign that
must not clear 1.02). The pusher ceiling 1.464 and the three Amdahl values. Negative controls: the ratio of
means; a bound computed with the normal quantile 1.645 at n = 10 (too high by exp((1.833 − 1.645)·s/√n)); a
variance computed as Σℓ² − n·ℓ̄² that returns a tiny negative number on constant input. Metamorphic relation:
multiplying every aᵢ and bᵢ by a common positive constant leaves the bound unchanged; swapping the arms inverts
the point estimate. Determinism lane: the arithmetic is class D0 on rational inputs.

## Considerations

- Timing measurements are host-dependent; on the Mac a sustained load precedes measurement and on the Linux host
  the load is recorded. The harness reports, never hides, the spread.
- The bound assumes log-ratios that are roughly symmetric; heavy tails are handled by pre-declared trimming only
  if the experiment's record states it before the run.
- This library is shared with the ten-fold test's harness, which adds restricted mean times and exact guards.
