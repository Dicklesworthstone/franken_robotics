## Background

The method-experiment register of FrankenRobotics (N38a-07) requires four computed quantities before and after
an experiment: the ceiling of the effect, the paired lower bound, the detection of a planted effect, and the
silence of an A/A arm. This bead is the small library that computes them, inside the Gauntlet's crate
`frob-conformance`, and the proof that it is exact where it must be. A harness that mis-states a bound makes
every kill rule built on it wrong, which is why the harness has its own proof obligation: PO-GAUNT-01, "the
paired lower bound of the harness is exact on constructed inputs: ten pairs of constant ratio r return r; an A/A
campaign does not clear 1.02".

DESIGN: a lower confidence bound on a paired ratio, never a ratio of means and never a point estimate. Reason:
pairing by world address and seed removes world-to-world variance, and a kill rule must read a bound. Rejected:
ratio of means (dominated by the fastest worlds, hides the world an arm never finishes); unpaired tests (need
many times the episodes when arms are correlated).

## Scope

In: the ceiling of a method experiment computed from a measured phase table (`ceiling_from_phases`, a wrapper
over the one bound); the paired lower bound, which this bead implements once for the whole program, in ratio
and in difference form, with its result type `PairedBound`, its errors and the Student quantile; exact
arithmetic for the constructed cases; the potency procedure; the A/A arm of a single experiment.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- the speedup bound itself, `ceiling(f, r, h)` = 1/(1 − f + f/r + h) and `min_fraction_for`, its known answers
  (13.47; f ≥ 0.92 for ten-fold; the family batch), the three rules of use with the pruning-times-device-speedup
  composition (20, not 50), the ceiling record `frob.perf.ceiling/1` and the refusal `FROB-REF-866`: N36-05
  (M0). This bead calls `ceiling` and adds only the derivation of f and h from phases;
- what the performance harness builds on the estimator: the type `PerfRatio` that cannot exist without a
  `PairedBound`, the campaign protocol (randomized arm order, host-state rules, no pair discarded, the timed
  interval), the orientation rule, the campaign record `frob.perf.pairs/1`, the refusal `FROB-REF-863`, and the
  calibration of a machine by 100 A/A campaigns (at most 9 clear 1.02) for GATE-SPARK-19: N36-06 (M2), which
  calls this bead's estimator and quantile and implements neither again;
- the register and its refusals (N38a-07); e-processes and exact fixed-n tests (the study-types bead BD-124);
  the disclosure block.

## Technical approach

**1. Ceiling from a phase breakdown.** Measure the baseline arm's wall time by phase (for example parse, assemble,
solve, check, serialize, harness). Let f be the fraction of baseline time the mechanism can improve, r the factor
by which it improves that fraction, and h the overhead the mechanism adds as a fraction of baseline time. The
bound speedup ≤ 1/(1 − f + f/r + h) is implemented once, as N00-17's `thesis::general_bound`; N36-05 exposes it
to the performance program as `frob_conformance::perf::bound::ceiling(f, r, h)`, an adapter that holds no copy of
the arithmetic (N00-17's single-implementation acceptance forbids one in `frob-core`); this bead calls that
adapter and does not implement the bound again. What this bead adds is the step before it:

`ceiling_from_phases(phases, touched, overhead) -> Ceiling`, where `phases` is the baseline arm's phase table
(name, seconds), `touched` the set of phase names the mechanism can improve, and `overhead` the seconds the
mechanism adds. It computes T_base = Σ phases, f = Σ touched ÷ T_base, h = overhead ÷ T_base, calls
`ceiling(f, ∞, h)`, and returns the ceiling with f, h, T_base and T_untouched = T_base·(1 − f + h). The ceiling of
an experiment is the limit r → ∞, 1/(1 − f + h), which is the same number as T_base ÷ T_untouched. A touched
phase that is not in the table, an empty table or a negative time is an error.

Worked value (known answer of the wrapper): the planning pilot (FACT): specialized full batch 610.1 µs, of which
the specialized solve is 34.4 µs; composed full batch 842.8 µs; untouched residual 610.1 − 34.4 = 575.7 µs;
ceiling 842.8 ÷ 575.7 = 1.464; measured 1.345 warm and 1.088 cold. As a phase table: solve 267.1 µs touched and
575.7 µs untouched, no overhead, f = 0.3169. A table with 85% of its time touched gives 6.67. The bound's other
known answers, its three rules of use and its refusal are N36-05's; an experiment's ceiling is filed as that
bead's record `frob.perf.ceiling/1`.

**2. Paired lower bound, ratio form (the program's one estimator).** For n ≥ 10 pairs (aᵢ, bᵢ) of positive
measurements, the two arms of a pair run back to back in randomized order on the same host state, workload and
world addresses: ℓᵢ = ln(aᵢ/bᵢ); ℓ̄ = (1/n)·Σ ℓᵢ; s² = (1/(n − 1))·Σ (ℓᵢ − ℓ̄)²; bound L = exp(ℓ̄ − t·s/√n), where t
is the 95th percentile of Student's distribution with n − 1 degrees of freedom. Fewer than ten pairs is refused
(`PairError::TooFewPairs`); a non-positive measurement is refused (`PairError::NonPositive`). A gate threshold
applies to the bound, never to the mean.

Where it lives (DESIGN). The pure arithmetic is in `frob_core::perf::paired`, so that any crate can state a
ratio only through it and the performance harness's `PerfRatio` (N36-06) is built on the same function:
`PairedBound { n, mean_ln, sd_ln, t, lower_bound, level: 0.95, campaign: Option<CampaignId> }`,
`paired_lower_bound(pairs) -> Result<PairedBound, PairError>` (ratio form),
`paired_difference_lower_bound(pairs) -> Result<PairedDifference, PairError>`, and `student_t95(df) -> f64`.
`frob-conformance` re-exports them as `experiment::stats`. Reason: `frob-core` cannot depend on
`frob-conformance`, and two implementations of one bound are how two results come to disagree.

**3. Paired lower bound, difference form.** dᵢ = aᵢ − bᵢ; bound = d̄ − t·s_d/√n, same t.

**4. The Student quantile (one-sided 95%).** `student_t95(df)` is computed for the exact degrees of freedom
n − 1 (by inverting the regularized incomplete beta function, bisection to 10⁻⁹) and the result is rounded
upward, so the value used is never below the exact quantile and the bound never errs high. Table values are the
function's tests, not its implementation: degrees of freedom → t: 9 → 1.833; 10 → 1.812; 11 → 1.796;
12 → 1.782; 13 → 1.771; 14 → 1.761; 15 → 1.753; 16 → 1.746; 17 → 1.740; 18 → 1.734; 19 → 1.729; 20 → 1.725;
22 → 1.717; 24 → 1.711; 29 → 1.699; 30 → 1.697; 40 → 1.684; 60 → 1.671; 99 → 1.660; 120 → 1.658; ∞ → 1.645.
DESIGN, replacing an earlier rule of this bead: a draft looked the quantile up in the table and, between
tabulated values, took the next smaller tabulated degrees of freedom (1.725 at 22). The plan's rule is the 95th
percentile at n − 1 degrees of freedom, and the performance harness computed it, so the same 23 pairs would
have had two bounds. One function, exact and rounded upward, serves both; it is still never smaller than the
exact value.

**5. Exactness on constructed inputs.** When every ℓᵢ is equal, s is exactly 0 in the implementation (the sum of
squared deviations is computed from differences to the first element, not from Σℓ² − n·ℓ̄²), and the bound is
returned as the input ratio itself, carried as a rational when the inputs are rationals. Ten pairs (3, 2) return
exactly 3/2.

**6. Potency control.** Before the real arms run, the harness runs the experiment's pipeline on a planted effect
of the declared size (for a speed claim: a baseline arm with a deterministic delay removed, of known true ratio
ρ; for an analysis or a screen: the design corpus with planted defects and planted winners). Pass: the lower
bound L exceeds 1 and the interval [L, U] covers ρ, where U = exp(ℓ̄ + t·s/√n) is the matching one-sided upper
bound with the same t (difference form: U = d̄ + t·s_d/√n). An experiment whose pipeline does not detect its planted effect is
`not-potent` and is not run.

**7. A/A arm.** Both arms are the same build and configuration. Pass: the lower bound does not exceed 1.02. An
A/A campaign that clears 1.02 means the harness is broken (ordering effects, warm caches, host drift), and the
experiment's results are void until it is fixed. This is the check of one experiment's own A/A arm. The
calibration of a machine is a different procedure with a different pass rule and belongs to N36-06: 100 A/A
campaigns, of which at most 9 may clear 1.02 (a true ratio of 1 clears 1.0 in 5% of campaigns by construction,
so a single campaign that clears is weak evidence and a count over 100 is the calibrated test). N36-06 runs
`aa` 100 times and counts; it does not define a second A/A procedure.

**API (DESIGN sketch).** In `frob_core::perf`: `paired_lower_bound(pairs) -> Result<PairedBound, PairError>`;
`paired_difference_lower_bound(pairs) -> Result<PairedDifference, PairError>`; `student_t95(df)`. In
`frob_conformance::experiment::stats`: `ceiling_from_phases(phases, touched, overhead) -> Ceiling`;
`potency(run, planted) -> Potency`; `aa(run) -> AaResult`. Outputs are evidence of class `Tested` and carry the
pairing unit and the determinism class of the arms.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] PO-GAUNT-01: ten pairs of constant ratio r return exactly r, for r in {1, 3/2, 10}; an A/A campaign on the
      harness's own fixture does not clear 1.02.
- [ ] `ceiling_from_phases` reproduces 1.464 from the pilot's phase table and 6.67 from a table with 85% touched,
      and its result equals `frob_conformance::perf::bound::ceiling(f, ∞, h)` (N36-05's adapter over N00-17's
      `thesis::general_bound`) bit for bit; the bound's other known
      answers (13.47; f ≥ 0.92) are tested once, in N36-05.
- [ ] Fewer than ten pairs is refused; a non-positive measurement in ratio form is refused.
- [ ] Ten ratios with ℓ̄ = 0.2 and s = 0.1 give exp(0.2 − 1.833 × 0.1/√10) = 1.153.
- [ ] A planted effect of ratio 1.25 is detected by the potency procedure; with the effect removed it is not.
- [ ] `student_t95` matches the table values to 10⁻³ for degrees of freedom 9 to 99 and never returns a t
      smaller than the exact value for the requested degrees of freedom (22 → 1.717, not the table's 20-row
      value 1.725 and not less than 1.7171).
- [ ] The estimator exists once: `frob_core::perf::paired` holds it and `frob-conformance` re-exports it, with
      no second implementation in the tree (the performance harness's `PerfRatio`, N36-06 at M2, which depends
      on this bead, is built on `PairedBound` and states that in its own acceptance).
- [ ] `scripts/e2e/experiment_stats.sh` passes, and GATE-GAUNT-09 is recorded by this bead from its tier receipt.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/experiment_stats.rs`:
- `po_gaunt_01_constant_ratio`: ten pairs (3, 2) → 3/2 exactly; ten pairs (10, 1) → 10; ten pairs (1, 1) → 1.
- `ratio_bound_known_value`: ℓ = (0.10, 0.20, 0.10, 0.20, 0.10, 0.20, 0.10, 0.20, 0.10, 0.20): ℓ̄ = 0.15,
  s = 0.052705, bound = exp(0.15 − 1.833 × 0.052705 ÷ √10) = exp(0.119450) = 1.1269 (to four decimals).
- `difference_bound_known_value`: d = (1, 2, 1, 2, 1, 2, 1, 2, 1, 2): d̄ = 1.5, s = 0.52705, bound = 1.1945.
- `fewer_than_ten_refused` (n = 9 → `PairError::TooFewPairs`); `nonpositive_refused`.
- `formula_fixture`: a pattern of ten log-ratios with mean 0.2 and sample standard deviation exactly 0.1 →
  L = 1.1526.
- `known_answer_all_equal` (KA-807): [1.5; 10] → L = 1.5, s = 0.
- `lower_bound_below_geometric_mean` (property): L ≤ exp(ℓ̄), with equality only when s = 0.
- `scale_invariance` (property): multiplying every aᵢ by k multiplies L by k.
- `permutation_invariance`: permuting the pairs leaves L unchanged; `swap_is_not_inverse`: swapping the arms
  negates ℓ̄ and does not give 1/L (the bound is one-sided), asserted so that nobody inverts a bound.
- `ceiling_from_phases_values`: the pilot's table (solve 267.1 µs touched, 575.7 µs untouched) → f = 0.3169,
  ceiling 1.4640 = 842.8 ÷ 575.7; a table with 85% touched and no overhead → 6.667; equality with
  `frob_conformance::perf::bound::ceiling` on 100 random tables, bit for bit.
- `ceiling_from_phases_errors`: a touched phase absent from the table; an empty table; a negative time.
- `student_quantiles`: 9 → 1.833; 10 → 1.812; 14 → 1.761; 19 → 1.729; 22 → 1.717; 29 → 1.699; 99 → 1.660.
- `quantile_exact_and_never_low`: for every df from 9 to 120 the returned t is at least the exact quantile
  (checked against a 30-digit reference table in the test) and exceeds it by less than 10⁻⁶; df = 22 returns
  1.7171, not 1.725.
- `aa_does_not_clear`: 20 pairs of the same deterministic workload → bound ≤ 1.02.
- `potency_detects_planted`: planted ratio 1.25 → lower bound > 1 and interval covers 1.25.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/experiment_stats.sh` (header: `bead=N38a-08 milestone=M1 labels=gauntlet gates=GATE-GAUNT-09
plants=ratio-of-means`). It runs the statistics test target, then an A/A campaign and a potency campaign on a
small real workload (the `frob` binary's own `doctor` verb timed twenty times per arm, with a planted sleep in
the potency arm), then plants `ratio-of-means` (the bound replaced by the ratio of arm means, in a build with
`--features gauntlet-mutations`) and requires the constant-ratio test with one outlier pair to fail; then the
receipt step `e2e_tier g0_laws GATE-GAUNT-09 --mutations` runs the gate's rows (N38a-07's admission rules and
this bead's PO-GAUNT-01 and A/A rows) with their controls. The script records nothing. This bead, which
depends on N38a-07, is the one recorder of GATE-GAUNT-09: `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example record_gate
-- --gate GATE-GAUNT-09 --receipt <hash>`, from that receipt, by a session that did not implement N38a-07 or this
bead; the record cites both. Log at `target/e2e-logs/experiment_stats/<utc>.ndjson`: the commands, the bounds as
`metric` lines with thresholds (A/A `<= 1.02`; potency `> 1`), the host load, the negative-control assertion,
the receipt hash, and the `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-807 (ten pairs each with ratio exactly 1.5 give a lower bound of 1.5; its control is the A/A campaign that
must not clear 1.02). The pusher ceiling 1.464 through the phase wrapper (the bound's own known answers KA-809
to KA-812, the three Amdahl values and the pruning composition among them, are N36-05's). Negative controls:
the ratio of means; an estimator that reports exp(ℓ̄), the mean, as the bound (fails
`lower_bound_below_geometric_mean` on a noisy fixture); a bound computed with the normal quantile 1.645 at
n = 10 (too high by exp((1.833 − 1.645)·s/√n)); a quantile taken from a table row of larger degrees of freedom
(too small a t); a variance computed as Σℓ² − n·ℓ̄² that returns a tiny negative number on constant input.
Metamorphic relations: multiplying every aᵢ and bᵢ by a common positive constant leaves the bound unchanged;
multiplying every aᵢ alone by k multiplies it by k; permuting the pairs leaves it unchanged; swapping the arms
inverts the point estimate and does not give 1/L. Determinism lane: the arithmetic is class D0 on rational
inputs.

## Considerations

- Timing measurements are host-dependent; on the Mac a sustained load precedes measurement and on the Linux host
  the load is recorded. The harness reports, never hides, the spread.
- The bound assumes log-ratios that are roughly symmetric; heavy tails are handled by pre-declared trimming only
  if the experiment's record states it before the run.
- This library is shared with the ten-fold test's harness, which adds restricted mean times and exact guards,
  and with the performance harness (N36-06), whose every ratio row, optimization, scaling row and A/A
  calibration reads this estimator.
- The log-ratio model assumes multiplicative noise. Ten pairs is a floor: the bound tightens as s/√n, and a
  quantity near its threshold needs more pairs.
