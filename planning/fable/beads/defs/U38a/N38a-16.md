## Background

The Gauntlet (the verification subsystem of FrankenRobotics) registers metamorphic relations, properties that
hold between runs without knowing the answer, each with the mutation that must break it. Forty-two relations
were added by the constructions of the present design. This bead registers the first fourteen. They protect the
promises that distinguish the system: a world's trajectory does not depend on the batch it ran in; a draw of
randomness is a function of an address; a design evaluated through a symmetric copy gives the transported
result; power-of-two rescaling changes no bit; a fork replays; a verdict survives exactly the edits its
polarity allows; and the blinded qualification lane keeps its error rate under adaptive retries.

Terms. *Digest*: a hash over declared state bits. *World address*: the identity from which a world's seeds,
resets and task are derived. *Orbit*: the set of designs related by a symmetry group; the *observation-preserving
subgroup* is the part of the mechanical symmetry group that the sensors do not break. *Polarity*: whether a
verdict (an inclusion or an exclusion) survives a given change of world set, policy class or assumptions.
*α*: the campaign-wide false-promotion level of the qualification lane, spent as α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)] on the i-th
request.

## Scope

In: fourteen registry records (statement, source, sites, comparison, mutation), their binding check, the mutation
records in the control registry (twelve; the batch-wide normalizer of MR-01 and the planted defects of MR-03,
MR-07 and MR-09 are catalogue controls, see below), and the unit tests below on the pure-arithmetic cases. Out: the harness
(N38a-14); the measurement of GATE-GAUNT-03 (N38a-19). Each relation's transform and comparison code on the
dynamics, device and daemon sites, and the `cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch of each mutation registered here
at the mutated site (NC-nnnn being the control record this bead registers with `seed = "MR-nn"`; the suite's one
mutation mechanism, N38a-14), are written by the owning
subsystem's bead, which binds the runner entry `mr:<id>@<site>` and depends on this registry bead (registries
come first; DESIGN, the same for all four relation beads): this bead does not wait for those mechanisms, and a
relation that is due and unbound is reported `due-unbound` by the binding check and fails the gate in N38a-19.

## Technical approach

Sites are DESIGN assignments; `bits` means identical digests or bytes.

| MR-03 | A draw depends on (address, step, stream) only; adding a stream changes no other stream | INV-NUM-04, randomness by address | host, lockstep, cuda, metal | `bits` | Sequential draws from one generator |
| MR-04 | **Orbit representatives.** Evaluating any member of an orbit under the observation-preserving subgroup and transporting the result equals evaluating the representative; g·d in world w behaves as d in g⁻¹·w | C-22, family quotienting | strict, lockstep | `bits` for signed-permutation elements, else `bound` | Deduplication under the mechanical group when a sensor breaks it |

| MR-13 | **Qualification under adaptive retries.** On no-signal worlds the campaign-wide false-promotion rate stays at most α for any number of retries and any adaptive strategy; the decision of request i is unchanged by later requests | The blinded qualification lane, GATE-QUAL-01 | host, daemon | `bound`: exact binomial upper bound on the rate at most α; `exact` for the second clause | Reused clusters at a constant level |
| MR-14 | An anytime-valid study's level holds at every stopping time; a fixed-n study's verdict is computed once | C-15, the two study types | host | `bound`: false-rejection rate at most the level over 10⁴ null campaigns with adversarial stopping | Peeking: 13/256 after 8 looks |

**Scenario classes (DESIGN).** MR-01, MR-02: 1,000 random worlds per reference workload, each run alone and in
100 random co-tenancies with random slots, at capacities 32, 64 and 128 and on one and two shards. MR-03: 10⁴
random (address, step, stream) triples with and without an added stream. MR-06, MR-07: random symmetric
positive-definite systems of size 6 to 64 and random rollouts of 64 ticks. MR-08, MR-09: random rollouts with a
random fork tick and, for MR-09, forced rejections at random sub-intervals. MR-10 to MR-12: random typed edit
sequences of length 1 to 8 on designs of the open modular class and on mission cells. MR-13: 2,000 no-signal
campaigns with 500 adaptive retries by a scripted adversary that sees every receipt and all discovery data, on
the no-signal world `absolute-above` of N24b-18 (see below). MR-14: sign-test streams with success probability
½.

**Mutations that are catalogue controls (one plant, one record: BD-025's reuse-first rule, N38a-14 step 5).**
Four of the sixteen planted defects are stated by counterexample entries, whose records N38b-05's seeding tool
derives; this bead writes no control record and no `nc-<nnnn>` feature for them, and the relation's `mutation`
entry names the seeded record (`control`) and its feature exactly as the record writes it (`feature`):
- MR-01, the batch-wide normalizer: CX-368's plant `normalizer-in-segment` (built by N35-15; N35-CX).
- MR-03, sequential draws from one generator: CX-371's plant `sequential-stream` (draws taken in call order from
  one sequential stream, in the stream keying of `frob-core`; N35-CX).
- MR-07, a scaling by 10 instead of a power of two: CX-372's plant `chol-scale-by-ten` (in the diagonal scaling
  `K-scale`; N35-CX).
- MR-09, redraw on rejection: CX-144's plant `BD-083:cx-144` (a rejected step redraws its random input; BD-083
  builds it and tests it on the fair-sign fixture, P(positive) = ¾; N16-CX), feature `cx-144`.
Each of the four records lists GATE-GAUNT-03 (and, for MR-01's, GATE-KIN-05, the gate of MR-01's source; for
MR-09's, GATE-GAUNT-06, the chaos tier's transaction-fault control, N38a-24) beside its own gates: the plants of
CX-368, CX-371, CX-372 (N35-CX) and CX-144 (N16-CX) name them, and the seeding tool, the only writer of a seeded
record, adds them (gates are only ever added). This bead depends on N35-CX and N16-CX because it names the
records seeded from their entries.

MR-01's other planted defect, the reduction tree shaped by capacity, is the plant `capacity-shaped-tree` that
N35-15 builds for GATE-SPARK-15. No catalogue entry states it, and its record is registered here, with
`seed = "MR-01"`, its `nc-<nnnn>` feature and `gate = ["GATE-GAUNT-03", "GATE-KIN-05", "GATE-SPARK-15"]`; N35-15
lists that record for its gate and writes no second one, and builds the branch under that feature. Rejected:
this bead naming a record of N35-15, which would make this registry wait on N35-15's kernel work (and lift it,
N38a-19 and SPEC-C-29 from levels 15, 16 and 24 of the dependency graph to beyond 100).

So the control runner runs each of the five when GATE-GAUNT-03 runs, and the G3 runner's `mutant:<id>` builds
each with `--features gauntlet-mutations,<crate>/<feature>` as its record writes it.

**MR-13's binding and mutation.** The entry `mr:MR-13@host` is bound by N24b-18 (the qualification lane's
adversarial campaign simulator) and `mr:MR-13@daemon` by N24a-34. Its mutation is one `nc-<nnnn>` feature in
`frob-arena` with two branches, written by N24b-18: the cluster derivation of N24b-17 ignores the request index
(clusters are reused) and the level of N24b-15 is held constant instead of α_i. The gate's count and its control
are run on `absolute-above` (every candidate's requirement fails with probability 0.055, no baseline arm), where
the test is near its level and both can fail: under the mutation the chance of at least one false promotion in
500 requests is 1 − (1 − 0.0132)⁵⁰⁰ = 0.9987. On N24b-18's two paired no-signal worlds the mutant reaches only
0.034 (`paired-equal`) and 0.126 (`paired-boundary`) at n = 64; those are reported beside the gate's figure and
are not held to it.

**Binding check.** As for the carried relations: records present, entries per site, a control record and its
feature per mutation (two each for MR-01 and MR-08, which name two planted defects; `nc-<nnnn>` for the controls
registered here, the seeded records' own features for the four catalogue controls), due milestones
respected (MR-01 to MR-09 at M2; MR-10 to MR-12 with the memo graph's invalidation
contract, GATE-PHYLUM-05 at M3; MR-13 at M3; MR-14 at M1, when the study-type engine exists; a relation whose
due milestone precedes the harness's own M2 is registered and run from M2).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Fourteen records registered with the fields above, each of the sixteen mutations a `kind = "mutation"`
      control of GATE-GAUNT-03: twelve registered here with `seed` their MR id and their own `nc-<nnnn>`
      feature (MR-01's `capacity-shaped-tree` record also naming GATE-KIN-05 and GATE-SPARK-15), and four
      naming seeded records with their own features (MR-01's CX-368, `normalizer-in-segment`; MR-03's CX-371;
      MR-07's CX-372; MR-09's CX-144, plant `BD-083:cx-144`); no second record exists for any plant, and each
      of the four seeded records lists GATE-GAUNT-03.
- [ ] The binding check passes when every due relation is bound and reports `due-unbound` naming the relation
      and site otherwise.
- [ ] Each bound due relation holds at the release count on its sites; MR-01 holds on 10⁴ worlds per device
      family (the determinism gate's clause); the full measurement over every due relation is N38a-19's.
- [ ] Each bound mutation breaks its relation.
- [ ] MR-09's mutation shows the bias it plants: the share of positive accepted draws is ¾ within sampling
      error where ½ is true.
- [ ] On the world `absolute-above`, MR-13's mutation gives a false promotion in more than 99% of campaigns
      (0.9987 expected), and the correct lane in at most 123 of 2,000; the paired worlds' mutant fractions
      (0.034 and 0.126 expected) are reported, not held to 99%.
- [ ] MR-14's mutation reproduces 13/256.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `mr_03_stream_independence`: draw (address 42, step 7, stream 2) is unchanged when stream 5 is added.
- `mr_06_power_of_two_bits`: 0.3 × 4 has the significand of 0.3; 0.3 × 10 does not.
- `mr_07_cholesky_scaling`: H = [[4, 2], [2, 3]] has factor L = [[2, 0], [1, √2]]; with D = diag(2, ¼) the
  factor of D·H·D equals D·L bit for bit; with D = diag(10, ¼) it does not.
- `mr_09_redraw_bias`: redrawing a standard normal draw once when it is negative gives P(positive) = ¾.
- `mr_11_polarity_docking`: the inclusion at (U, ε) = (¾, ¼) survives ε falling to 1/8 and U rising to 1; the
  exclusion at (¾, 3/8) survives ε rising and U falling; each opposite move requires a recheck.
- `mr_13_level_series`: Σ over i = 1…m of α/[i·(i + 1)] = α·m/(m + 1) < α for α = 1/20 and m up to 500.
- `mr_13_mutant_rate_absolute_above`: 1 − (1 − 0.0132)⁵⁰⁰ = 0.9987 to four decimals, above the 0.99 the
  acceptance asks.
- `mr_14_peeking_rate`: the exact one-sided sign test at nominal 5% rejects falsely with probability 13/256
  after 8 looks.

### End-to-end script and logging

Run by `scripts/e2e/metamorphic_g3.sh` (N38a-19) with the filter `MR-01..MR-14`; one `metric` per relation and
site (scenarios, violations against zero) and one negative-control `assert` per mutation.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the Cholesky example; ¾; 13/256 = 5.08%; α·m/(m + 1); the docking polarity cases (the family
ε < (U + ½)/4). Counterexamples carried: seeding a generator fixes the draws, not the order of a reduction;
a hash match identifies an artifact and only an implication licenses reuse; a rejected step may not redraw its
random input (CX-144, whose control is MR-09's); draws in call order (CX-371, MR-03's); a Cholesky scaling by 10
(CX-372, MR-07's). Negative controls: the fourteen relations' sixteen planted defects (MR-01 and MR-08 name two
each), twelve registered here and four seeded from the catalogue. Determinism lanes: every `bits`
relation twice on one machine, across worker counts and across shards.

## Considerations

- MR-01 is also the last clause of the determinism gate; its count there is 10⁴ worlds per device family.
- MR-13 and MR-14 are statistical: their thresholds are exact binomial bounds computed before the run, and their
  campaigns are no-signal by construction (`synthetic`).
- Where a device family attains only class D3, `bits` relations are not claimed on it and the account says so.
- MR-09's mutation (redraw on rejection, CX-144's record) is also the transaction-fault control of the chaos
  tier (N38a-24) and BD-083's GATE-KIN-04 control: the record lists GATE-GAUNT-06 and GATE-GAUNT-03 beside
  GATE-KIN-04, so one branch serves the three gates.
