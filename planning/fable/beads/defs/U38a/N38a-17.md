## Background

The Gauntlet (the verification subsystem of FrankenRobotics) registers metamorphic relations, properties that
hold between runs without knowing the answer, each with the mutation that must break it. This bead registers
relations MR-15 to MR-28 of the forty-two added by the present design. They cover time and information (events
fall on a rational schedule whatever the physics step; a checkpoint at any instant loses nothing; nothing
undelivered can influence a command), the requirement language (unit changes and streaming evaluation), reward
and energy accounting, the invariances of family policies, and three contact-level laws.

Terms. *Clock contract*: every sensor, controller and reward clock is a rational period with an integer phase
accumulator; nominal event k is at phase + k·period. *Packet*: a delivered observation with capture and arrival
instants. *Normalized robustness*: the margin of a requirement atom divided by its declared scale, so that atoms
in different units compare. *G over [0, T]* ("always") and *H over [0, T]* ("historically") are the future-time
and past-time forms of the same bounded requirement. *Family policy*: one policy for a class of robot
morphologies, addressed by tokens matched to parts. *Film law*: the friction law of a thin fluid layer between
pad and floor.

## Scope

In: fourteen registry records and their binding check. Out: the harness (N38a-14) and each relation's transform
code (owning subsystems, under `needs`).

**Scenario classes (DESIGN).** MR-15: periods drawn from {1/30, 1/50, 1/100, 1/400, 1/1000} s and their
rational multiples, physics steps of 1/2000 to 1/250 s, horizons of 20 s; the registered fixture has common
quantum count 1,200 per second with decisions at boundaries 0, 14, 27, 40. MR-16: checkpoint instants drawn
uniformly and at the three named adversarial instants. MR-17: pairs of independent events (two sensors with
disjoint state) at one instant, in both orders. MR-18: world pairs differing in a hidden parameter first
observable at a random delivery instant. MR-19, MR-20: 10⁴ random traces per operator. MR-21: constant and
piecewise-constant reward rates at control rates of 50, 100 and 200 Hz over 10 s. MR-23 to MR-25: random
morphologies of one class with random re-descriptions. MR-26: pad radii r and 2r at film thickness 0 and at
10 random thicknesses. MR-27: random load-weighted quadrature sets. MR-28: the split-grip course and its mirror.

**Binding check.** Records present; entries per site; a `mut-mr-<nn>` feature per mutation; due milestones
respected (MR-15 to MR-18 with the clock contract at M1 on CPU sites and M2 on devices; MR-19, MR-20, MR-27 at
M1; MR-28 at M2; MR-26 at M3; MR-21 to MR-25 at M4).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `mr_15_integer_equals_rational`: for a 30 Hz clock on a 400 Hz physics tick, event k occurs at k/30 s exactly;
  the first four events fall at quanta 0, 40, 80, 120 of a 1,200-per-second lattice; the every-thirteenth-step
  mutant gives 616 events in [0, 20) s.
- `mr_17_same_instant_commute`: two independent events applied as (a, b) and (b, a) give one digest.
- `mr_19_unit_change_binding_atom`: margins 0.02 m at scale 0.1 m and 3 N at scale 20 N have normalized
  robustness 0.20 and 0.15; in millimetres and kilonewtons the values and the binding atom (the force) are
  unchanged; composing raw margins picks 0.02 as binding in SI and 0.003 in the other units.
- `mr_20_g_equals_h`: on the trace (0.3, −0.1, 0.4, 0.2), G over the window evaluated at 0 and H evaluated at
  the end both give −0.1.
- `mr_21_rate_total`: a penalty rate of 100 per second over 10 s totals 1,000 at 50 Hz and at 100 Hz; a
  per-step penalty of 1 totals 500 and 1,000.
- `mr_26_radius_power_four`: squeeze time proportional to R⁴ gives a ratio of 16 for a doubled radius.
- `mr_27_duplicate_point`: weights (1, 1) at speeds (2, 4) and weights (1, ½, ½) at (2, 4, 4) give the same
  weighted mean 3.
- `mr_28_axial_polar`: under a mirror in the plane y = 0 a force (1, 2, 3) maps to (1, −2, 3) and a moment
  (1, 2, 3) maps to (−1, 2, −3).

### End-to-end script and logging

Run by `scripts/e2e/metamorphic_g3.sh` (N38a-19) with the filter `MR-15..MR-28`; one `metric` per relation and
site (scenarios, violations against zero) and one negative-control `assert` per mutation.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 30.77 Hz and 616 against 600; 500 against 1,000; the factor 16; the mirror rules for polar and
axial vectors. Counterexamples carried: scheduling a 30 Hz policy every thirteenth step of 400 Hz physics; a
reward counted per step; a policy that peeks at truth behind an undelivered packet. Negative controls: the
fourteen mutations. Determinism lanes: every `bits` relation twice on one machine and across worker counts.

## Considerations

- MR-18 needs worlds that differ only in undelivered information; constructing them requires the causal typing
  of observations, so the relation is due with that mechanism, on CPU sites first.
- MR-21's second clause holds to first order only; its test checks the leading coefficient and that the residual
  shrinks with the square of the interval.
- MR-23 to MR-25 are due at M4 and exist only if family policies are funded; if their kill gate fires the three
  records are marked `superseded` with the decision's identifier, not deleted.
