## Background

The Gauntlet (the verification subsystem of FrankenRobotics) registers metamorphic relations, properties that
hold between runs without knowing the answer, each with the mutation that must break it. This bead registers the
last fourteen of the forty-two relations added by the present design. They reach beyond dynamics into the parts
of the system where silent drift is easiest: geometric enclosures, generated scenes and data splits, telemetry
sketches, a variance-reduced estimator, a fatigue counter, canonical bytes and identities, the job protocol, the
kernel toolchain, rankings under model-form change, the claim product, a geometric-phase law and exact position
arithmetic.

Terms. *Clearance complex*: a pair of cell complexes bracketing the free space of a scene for a body footprint
(inner ⊆ free ⊆ outer). *SceneId*: the hash identity of a scene manifest. *Split group*: the set of scenes that
must fall on the same side of a train and held-out split. *Tier-corrected estimate*: a fast-tier mean corrected
by audited referee samples with recorded inclusion probabilities. *Class-E rewrite*: a kernel rewrite declared
exact (no change of any bit). *Model-form variants*: the contact-law variants CL-1 and CL-2, a cone against a
pyramid, stiffness × ½ and × 2, step h against h/2. *Claim product*: the type that gives a result the weakest
evidence class among its inputs. *Anchored position*: a position stored as an integer cell index plus an offset
within a cell of side 8 m.

## Scope

In: fourteen registry records and their binding check. Out: the harness (N38a-14) and each relation's transform
code (owning subsystems, under `needs`).

**Scenario classes (DESIGN).** MR-29: 10⁴ generated scenes of at most 40 cells with rectangular footprints at
grid sizes g and g/2 and margins m and 2m. MR-30, MR-31: each scene generator at 100 random seeds, with its
mirror, a clutter variant and a retrofit variant. MR-32: 10⁴ random event streams of integer values, permuted,
split over 1 to 4 shards and reassigned to slots. MR-33: synthetic populations of 10³ addresses with planted
tier bias and audit probabilities of 0.01 to 1. MR-34: random load histories of 10³ reversals cut into 1 to 16
windows. MR-35: every registered format with 10⁴ seeded round trips. MR-36: 10³ submissions with duplicate keys
and concurrent appends under the simulation runtime. MR-37: the built-in reference model stepped for 2 s.
MR-38: every registered class-E rewrite and every specialized kernel on 10⁴ random inputs. MR-39: design
ensembles of 16 with k = 4. MR-40: random sets of input evidence of size 1 to 8 in random orders. MR-41:
rectangular shape loops of side 0.01 to 0.1 on the reference kinematic systems. MR-42: 10⁶ random anchored
positions shifted by ±1 cell.

**Binding check.** Records present; entries per site; a `mut-mr-<nn>` feature per mutation; due milestones
respected (MR-35, MR-37, MR-40, MR-42 at M0 to M1; MR-32, MR-36, MR-38 at M2; MR-29 to MR-31, MR-33, MR-39 at
M3; MR-41 at M4; MR-34 at M5).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

- `mr_29_scalar_radius_fails`: a 0.6 m by 0.3 m footprint in a 0.5 m doorway passes lengthwise and is blocked
  crosswise; a scalar radius of 0.15 m admits both orientations and one of 0.3 m rejects both, so neither
  brackets the free set.
- `mr_32_histogram_permutation`: counts of the stream (3, 1, 3, 2) and of any permutation or shard split are
  {1: 1, 2: 1, 3: 2}.
- `mr_34_windows_equal_whole`: the reversal sequence (0, 5, 1, 4, 2, 6, 0) counted whole and in windows of 3
  with carried residues gives the same cycle multiset; calling `finish` per window does not.
- `mr_35_key_order`: `{"b":1,"a":2}` and `{ "a": 2, "b": 1 }` have one identity; with key sorting disabled, two.
- `mr_40_meet_is_order_free`: the classes (Certified, Estimated, Tested) combine to Estimated in all six orders.
- `mr_41_loop_laws`: for a loop of area A the displacement is c·A to leading order; doubled 2·c·A; reversed
  −c·A; traversed twice as fast, unchanged; with the commutator's sign flipped the reversed loop fails.
- `mr_42_cell_shift_exact`: in `f32`, an offset that has drifted to 4.25 m re-anchors as (cell + 1, −3.75 m) and
  one at −4.5 m as (cell − 1, 3.5 m), both exactly (the subtraction of 8 from a value in [4, 8] loses no bit),
  and shifting back recovers the original bits; under the range [0, 8) an offset of −0.3 m re-anchors to
  −0.3 + 8, which is not representable, so the round trip changes bits.

### End-to-end script and logging

Run by `scripts/e2e/metamorphic_g3.sh` (N38a-19) with the filter `MR-29..MR-42`; one `metric` per relation and
site (scenarios, violations against zero) and one negative-control `assert` per mutation.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the doorway footprint case; the histogram; the class meet; the anchored-cell constants (side 8 m,
offsets in [−4, 4) m). Counterexamples carried: a digest over library-map bytes changes when an unrelated crate
enables insertion-order maps; offset pagination repeats or skips rows under concurrent appends; a finalist that
wins only on a pyramidal cone. Negative controls: the fourteen mutations (sixteen planted defects, since MR-32
and MR-36 name two each). Determinism lanes: every `bits` relation twice on one machine, across worker counts,
and for MR-30 and MR-35 across the two platforms.

## Considerations

- MR-33's first clause is statistical; its tolerance is the exact standard error of the synthetic population,
  fixed before the run, and its data are labelled `synthetic`.
- MR-37 depends on the worker process being built at the same commit; the relation is the test that detects a
  stale worker.
- MR-41 holds to leading order in loop size; the test checks the scaling of the residual with the cube of the
  loop side.
- MR-34 exists only if the structure subsystem's fatigue lane is funded; otherwise its record is `superseded`.
