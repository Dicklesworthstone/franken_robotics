## Background

This is the pad mechanism of the epic "tactile sensing and compliant pads" in FrankenRobotics: a fingertip pad
modelled as a grid of micro-patch contacts, which a tactile-array sensor then reads. It is Moonshot-tier,
lives behind the feature `moonshot-tactile` of `frob-contact` (off by default) and is started only if its pilot
(N18b-35, which superseded the draft N38a-30) rejected its falsifier and the epic's kill gate is registered
(N38a-37). If the pilot's falsifier holds, this bead is not started and closes `withdrawn` with a pointer to
N38a-39, which records KILL-TACTILE (N41-17: it delivers nothing and never counts as work done). The pilot's statement: a fingertip pad
as 2 × 2 and 4 × 4 four-dimensional contacts feeding the sensors, on the `strict` profile; falsifier: no change
in grasp-slip outcomes against one patch; fallback: one patch per pad.

The sensor that reads the pad (tactile arrays with finite taxel footprints and normal and shear saturation,
whose first check is the resultant load against the sum over taxels) is a separate mechanism of a different
tier: Frontier, in `frob-sense`, specified once by N20a-19. An earlier draft of this bead specified that sensor
a second time; that part now lives only there, and this bead depends on it. What the pad adds for the sensor is
contact samples that each carry the area they represent, so that load is distributed over taxels by overlap
and not assigned to one point.

Rejected alternatives: a finite-element pad (out of reach at batch speed and without data to validate it); a
single patch per pad with a pressure profile assumed afterwards (the sensor would report a distribution the
dynamics never computed).

## Scope

In: the pad as micro-patches in the contact model; the pad compliance card; capacity accounting and the refusal;
the grasp-slip comparison on the pilot's cases; the step cost of the pads; the grasp end-to-end script, which
reads the tactile sensor on the pads.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- the tactile-array sensor in `frob-sense`: its card, the map from contact samples to taxels by area overlap,
  saturation and flags, noise, quantization and packets under the clock contract, the conservation check
  (KA-523) with its tolerance (10⁻¹² relative in double precision, 10⁻⁴ in single) and its registered control
  (the total written into every taxel), the checkpoint-and-restore test of its packets, the kernel, the
  sensor's own cost fraction and its mask-only fallback, and its unit tests (`ka_523_resultant_equals_sum`,
  `overlap_weights_sum_to_one`, `impulse_counted_once`, `normal_saturation`,
  `shear_saturation_scales_vector`, `nc_total_in_every_taxel`, `checkpoint_restores_tactile_packets`): N20a-19;
- the pilot (N18b-35); learning from tactile input; micro-slip precursors (killed; reopenable only with
  imported data); device tiers unless cost and capacity allow (decided by the kill gate's clauses).

## Technical approach

**Pad (in `frob-contact`).** A pad is declared on a link by a rectangle (centre, axes, sides a × b), a grid
N_u × N_v (1 × 1, 2 × 2 or 4 × 4) and a compliance card (normal stiffness per area k_n in N/m³, damping ratio,
friction pair). Each grid cell becomes one four-dimensional contact sample at the cell centre with area
A_c = a·b/(N_u·N_v), normal stiffness k_n·A_c, and an effective torsional radius set by the pilot's matching
construction (N18b-35, DESIGN): r_cell = (0.3826 − ℓ̄)·s for a square pad of side s, where ℓ̄ is the mean
distance of the cell centres from the pad centre in units of s (0 at 1 × 1, 0.3536 at 2 × 2, 0.3744 at 4 × 4),
that is 0.3826·s, 0.0290·s and 0.0082·s, so that Σ Nᵢ·(‖pᵢ − c‖ + r_cell) at uniform load equals the single
patch's N·0.3826·s (times μ, its torsional capacity) at every resolution and a difference in outcome comes from the distribution,
not from a different torsional budget. (An earlier draft gave each cell the mean lever of its own cell, 0.3826
times the cell side; in that sum it raises the twist capacity by 42% at 2 × 2 and 23% at 4 × 4, and the pads
could not reproduce the pilot's arms.) A rectangular pad uses the same construction with the exact lever of its
rectangle. The pad's samples count toward contact capacity; a pad
that would exceed the admitted capacity of the tier is refused at model compile, or reduced by the documented
patch reduction with the label `capacity-biased`; contacts are never dropped silently.

**What the pad hands to the sensor.** For each micro-patch cell and each committed step: the cell's rectangle in
pad coordinates (its area element), its normal force and its two tangential components in the pad frame, and
its contact impulses, so that the sensor (N20a-19) can accumulate over its exposure window and distribute each
cell's load onto taxels by the overlap of the cell with each taxel's footprint. The pad exposes these through
the contact telemetry every sensor reads; it adds no private channel.

**Evidence and labels.** Tier and determinism class of the contact step; class `Tested` for known answers; the
compliance card's provenance (`generic`, `assumed`) is carried into the claim product of everything computed
with the pad, the sensor's readings included.

**Verbs.** No new verb: the pad is part of the design (`design.check` reports its contact count and capacity);
the sensor's clock and cost are reported by the sensing verbs, as N20a-19 states.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pad of 2 × 2 and of 4 × 4 micro-patches compiles, counts toward capacity and is refused by name where
      the tier cannot admit it.
- [ ] Every micro-patch sample carries its cell's area element, and the sum of the cells' normal forces equals
      the pad's total contact force of the step exactly as summed by the contact telemetry.
- [ ] On the grasp fixture the tactile sensor's unsaturated resultant on the pad equals the pad's total contact
      force over the exposure within the sensor's tolerance (KA-523, set by N20a-19: 10⁻¹² relative in double
      precision), at the three pad resolutions.
- [ ] Grasp-slip outcomes on the pilot's 75 cases reproduce the pilot's record on `strict`.
- [ ] Step cost of a 4 × 4 pad pair against one patch per pad is reported with paired bounds; with the lower
      bound of the paired ratio above 1.25 the kill gate's clause is recorded (the reading KILL-TACTILE's clause
      2 and N38a-39 use; bounds that straddle 1.25 are recorded and do not fire).
- [ ] The feature is off by default; the contract of `frob-contact` states the mechanism and its gaps.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-contact/tests/pad.rs`:
- `pad_cells_and_areas`: a 20 mm × 20 mm pad at 4 × 4 has 16 samples of area 25 mm² at the cell centres; at
  2 × 2, four of 100 mm².
- `cell_stiffness_scales_with_area`: k_n·A_c per cell; the cells' stiffnesses sum to k_n·a·b.
- `cell_radius_matches_pilot`: r_cell = 0.0290·s at 2 × 2 and 0.0082·s at 4 × 4 (0.3826·s at 1 × 1); the
  summed capacity at uniform load equals 0.3826·N·s within 10⁻⁹ relative at the three resolutions, the same
  value as N18b-35's `capacity_matching`.
- `capacity_refusal_two_4x4_pads_referee`: 32 contacts against a limit of 21 are refused at compile on the
  referee.
- `capacity_reduction_is_labelled`: the documented patch reduction applied to a pad sets `capacity-biased`.
- `cell_forces_sum_to_pad_force`: the cells' forces and the pad's telemetry total agree on a pressed pad.

The sensor's unit tests are in `crates/frob-sense/tests/tactile.rs` and belong to N20a-19.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tactile_pad_grasp.sh` (header: `bead=N38a-31 milestone=M6 labels=sensorium,traction,tactile
plants=total-in-every-taxel,drop-contacts`), on a build with `moonshot-tactile` and `frontier-tactile`. For each
resolution R (`1x1`, `2x2`, `4x4`) it runs the pilot's pinch fixture with the product pads,
`frob sim run fixtures/tactile/pinch-pad-<R>.fbot --task <the pilot's 75-case grid task> --tier batch-cpu
--profile strict --worlds 75 --seconds 2 --seed 35 --key tpg-<R> --json` (the seed and world addresses of
N18b-35's grid, so the outcomes are comparable with its record), reads tactile observations with
`frob telemetry query <run> --stream sensing --json` and the contact telemetry of the same run with
`--stream contact`, and checks the resultant against it; the live-dependency proof re-runs the 4 × 4 pad with
the grip force changed, `--key tpg-4x4-grip`, and asserts that the readings change; the cost pair runs the 4 × 4
pads and one patch per pad on the cost workload with keys `tpg-cost-4x4` and `tpg-cost-1x1`; the two plants run
as builds with their registered features (`--features gauntlet-mutations,<crate>/nc-<nnnn>`; `gauntlet-mutations`
alone plants nothing), with keys `tpg-plant-<name>`. Every run has its own key, so none returns
another's stored receipt. If the sensor was not built (N20a-19 closed `withdrawn`) or fell back to the mask
(KILL-TAXEL fired, N38ap3-01), the resultant row is recorded `not run: array not built` or `not run: mask-only`,
the reason KILL-TACTILE's decision (N38a-39) reads, and the pads are checked against the contact telemetry
only. Log at
`target/e2e-logs/tactile_pad_grasp/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`
("N38a-31"), `step`, `event`; commands with exit codes and wall time, evidence blocks with card provenance, `metric` lines for the resultant error
(`<= 1e-12` relative), differing slip outcomes, cost ratio (`<= 1.25`), refusals met, both negative controls, each an `assert` with `caught`, and
the `summary` with assertions passed and failed.

### Known answers and negative controls

KA-523 on the pads, through the sensor of N20a-19 (the tolerance and the registered control, the
total-in-every-taxel mutation, are that bead's; this script plants the mutation once more on the grasp fixture,
through that control's own `nc-<nnnn>` feature in `frob-sense`, to show the end-to-end check is live). The mean
lever about the pad centre, in units of the pad side: 0.3826
for a continuous square, (√2 + ln(1 + √2))/6, the value of a single patch; 0.3536 (√2/4) for the four cell
centres at 2 × 2; 0.3744 for the sixteen cell centres at 4 × 4 (four at √2/8, eight at √10/8, four at √18/8). Negative
controls of this bead: contacts dropped silently at capacity (the capacity audit must catch it), a `mutation`
record of `registry/controls.toml` that this bead registers under the capacity rule's gate (seed and gate
GATE-GAUNT-06: "the typed refusal or the `capacity-biased` label, never a silent truncation"), planted as a
`cfg(feature = "nc-<nnnn>")` branch in the pad compiler of `frob-contact`. Metamorphic
relation: the mirrored hand on the mirrored object returns mirrored readings. Determinism lanes: twice; worker
counts; each tier against its class.

## Considerations

- Kill gate named in advance: the epic's (trigger: the pilot's falsifier, more than 25% step cost, an
  inadmissible contact count, or a failed resultant; fallback: one patch per pad).
- Without imported tactile measurements nothing here is a reality claim; every reading carries its card's
  provenance.
- Hands with many pads multiply contacts quickly; `design.check` reports the pad contact count beside the
  tier's capacity so an agent sees the refusal before it runs.
- Order of work: the sensor is Frontier and reads any contact set, so it can exist with one patch per pad; this
  Moonshot bead follows it. If the sensor fell back to the contact mask per taxel, the grasp script checks the
  mask against the cells in contact and the resultant row is recorded `not run`.
