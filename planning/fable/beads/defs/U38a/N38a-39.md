## Background

A kill gate is decided on recorded values at its milestone, against a trigger and a fallback registered before
the measurement, and the outcome is written as an architecture decision record (ADR) whichever way it goes.

KILL-TACTILE is the kill gate of the pads of the Moonshot epic "tactile sensing and compliant pads" (BD-418). The
epic has two parts: a fingertip pad represented as an N × N grid of micro-patches (N = 2 or 4), each a
four-dimensional contact (one normal, two tangential and one spin component under one friction cone), in place
of one patch (Moonshot, N38a-31); and a tactile array with finite taxel footprints and saturation in normal and
shear load, fed by those contacts (Frontier, N20a-19). This gate decides the pads. The array has its own gate,
KILL-TAXEL (fallback: the contact mask per taxel), decided by N38ap3-01, because it reads any contact set, down
to one point sample per pad, and can be kept or dropped without the pads; this gate reads the array only through
the resultant check on the pads (clause 4). The ceiling was stated first: realism of tactile sensing, no speed
benefit, and a cost that rises with the contact count (a 4 × 4 pad is sixteen contacts where there was one). The
row is registered by N38a-37; this bead evaluates it.

Trigger as registered (TARGET numbers). Any one clause fires the gate:
1. the pilot's verdict is negative, by N18b-35's registered rule (as N38a-37 enters the row): zero differing
   grasp-slip outcomes of 75 for both the 2 × 2 and the 4 × 4 pad against one patch with no paired difference on
   the off-centre and twist trials; a difference on one reading only; or a failed per-element determinacy check;
2. a 4 × 4 pad pair adds more than 25% to step cost on the grasp workload (lower bound of the paired ratio above
   1.25);
3. the pad's contact count cannot be admitted on any tier without removing contacts (two 4 × 4 pads are 32
   contacts; the referee admits at most 21 in one solve);
4. the taxel resultant on the pads fails its known answer: the resultant load of the array equals the sum over
   its taxels.

Fallback: one patch per pad, with the sensor reading the single patch's wrench; the "dexterous hands" variant of
the design language is then described by that fallback. Decided at M6. A stronger idea stays killed whatever the
outcome: micro-slip precursors cannot be validated without tactile-array data and may be reopened only behind
the data-import lane.

## Scope

In: evaluating the four clauses on recorded receipts; the decision record and the row's decision entries;
applying the fallback if the gate fires; what changes downstream. Out: registering the row (N38a-37); the pilot (N18b-35, which superseded the
draft N38a-30); the mechanism and its
measurement script (N38a-31); the tactile-array model and its own gate KILL-TAXEL (N20a-19; N38ap3-01); the
four-dimensional cone and the rolling and torsional rows it builds on.

## Technical approach

**Inputs (by receipt hash).** The pilot record of N18b-35 (`PILOT-TRAC-<nn>` in the pilot register): its
verdict (`falsifier-holds`, `falsifier-rejected`, `void` or `withdrawn`), differing cases of 75 for each grid,
the paired differences, the determinacy result, onset-load differences and the cost ratio. Clause 1 reads the
recorded verdict and does not recount the cases. From `scripts/e2e/tactile_pad_grasp.sh` of N38a-31: differing slip
outcomes, the taxel resultant error (threshold 10⁻¹² relative), the paired bounds of the cost ratio, and for each
tier whether a 2 × 2 and a 4 × 4 pad pair is admitted, refused by name, or reduced under the label
`capacity-biased`.

**Evaluation.**
1. Pilot verdict `falsifier-holds`: clause 1 fires, outcome `fired`. Pilot record `withdrawn` (the owner did not
   select the epic under the cap of four Moonshot items, so the pilot was not run): outcome `not-reached`. In
   both cases N38a-31 is not started and has closed `withdrawn` with a pointer to this bead (N41-17), which is how
   this bead, which depends on it, becomes ready. A `void` record decides nothing: the pilot is repaired and
   re-run first.
2. Otherwise: clause 2 fires when the lower bound of the cost ratio exceeds 1.25; clause 3 when no tier admits
   the pad at full contact count (a tier that admits it only by the documented patch reduction counts as *not*
   admitting it, because contacts were removed); clause 4 when the resultant error exceeds its threshold on any
   tier claimed. Clause 4 is `not-applicable` when N38a-31's receipt records the resultant row `not run` because
   the array was not built (N20a-19 closed `withdrawn`) or fell back to the contact mask (KILL-TAXEL fired): the
   pads are then judged on clauses 1 to 3, and the ADR cites that reason. Any other missing measurement makes
   its clause `not-run`, and `not-fired` cannot be recorded for a resolution while one of its clauses is
   `not-run`.
3. The row is decided per resolution (`scope_kind = "resolution"`, the kind N40-03's table lists for
   KILL-TACTILE): the decision is an array with one entry per grid, `scope = "resolution:2x2"` and `scope =
   "resolution:4x4"`. A partial outcome is allowed and named: `kept at 2 × 2` when the 4 × 4 pad fires clause 2
   or 3 and the 2 × 2 pad does not, recorded as `fired` for `resolution:4x4` and `not-fired` for
   `resolution:2x2`, never as one `not-fired`. An outcome that is the same for both grids (the cases of step 1)
   is written on both entries.
4. One repair cycle is permitted for clauses 2 to 4, in N40-03's form (opened by the measuring bead's
   `repair-cycle` note line, closed by one re-measurement on the same fixture and thresholds); the first
   receipts stay on record, and a second cycle is a `deferred` decision with `deferred_to` and `reason`.
5. Write the decision ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TACTILE-M6.md` with the fields N40-03 requires (the one directory
   for decision records), one section per resolution: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered
   with its amendments; each value with its receipt hash, commit, fingerprint and seeds beside the trigger's
   number; the repair taken; the clause-by-clause verdict; the outcome (`not-fired`, `fired`, `not-reached` or
   `deferred`); the fallback in force and the name the capability carries; the beads re-read (each "stands on
   the fallback", "superseded by this ADR" or "withdrawn before this decision"); what changes downstream; and
   the recording session, which is not the implementing one. That session writes the row's entries in
   `registries/gates.toml`, each `{ exit = "M6", scope, outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }`, with at least
   one receipt hash under `receipts/gates/` for `not-fired`; the row gains no measured value, and the load
   refuses an entry whose `session` implemented the mechanism (N40-03's check, not the gate recorder of
   N38b-19, which never records a kill decision).

**Downstream.** *Kept* (or kept at 2 × 2): the pad compliance card is admitted at the resolution kept (the
tactile-array card's admission follows KILL-TAXEL); every tactile result is labelled with its card's provenance
(`generic` or `assumed`), since no tactile-array measurement has been imported. *Fired*: pads compile as one patch; the tactile sensor reads that
patch's wrench; card kinds added for the mechanism stay parseable and are refused at admission with the
fallback's name; the epic BD-418 closes `fallback` on "one patch per pad", citing the ADR; N38a-31 (a dependency
of this bead, closed on its recorded receipts) is re-read in the ADR. *Not reached*: one patch per pad is in
force without a measurement; BD-418 closes `fallback` citing the ADR.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TACTILE-M6.md` exists with every field N40-03 requires, cites the registered trigger and
      one receipt hash per measured value, and states per resolution `not-fired`, `fired`, `not-reached` or
      `deferred` (`kept at 2 × 2` being `fired` for 4 × 4 and `not-fired` for 2 × 2), with the clause that fired.
- [ ] The row KILL-TACTILE in `registries/gates.toml` carries one decision entry per resolution, `{ exit, scope =
      "resolution:<grid>", outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }`, written by a session other than the
      implementing one (an entry by an implementing session fails the load); with any clause `not-run` this
      bead's evaluation refuses to produce `not-fired`, and a `not-fired` entry with no receipt fails the load;
      clause 4 recorded `not-applicable` with its reason does not block.
- [ ] If fired: capability listings describe the hand variant as "one patch per pad", BD-418 closes `fallback`
      citing the ADR and N38a-31 is re-read in it. If the pilot's verdict was negative or its record `withdrawn`:
      N38a-31 is closed `withdrawn` with a pointer to this bead.
- [ ] If not fired: the admitted tiers are listed with their contact counts, and no tier is listed on which
      contacts were removed.
- [ ] The M6 exit review can read the outcome from the register without opening this bead.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_tactile_decision.rs`, on scratch receipts (labelled `synthetic`):
- `negative_verdict_fires_clause_1`: a record with verdict `falsifier-holds` (zero of 75 for both grids, or a
  split result, or a failed determinacy check) fires clause 1; `falsifier-rejected` does not; the evaluation
  reads the verdict and does not recount cases.
- `withdrawn_pilot_is_not_reached`: a `withdrawn` record gives outcome `not-reached` with no measurement read.
- `cost_bound_1_26_fires_clause_2`.
- `reduced_pad_counts_as_not_admitted`: a tier that admits two 4 × 4 pads only under `capacity-biased` reduction
  → clause 3 fires for 4 × 4.
- `thirty_two_contacts_refused_on_referee`: 32 > 21 is a refusal by name, never a silent drop.
- `partial_outcome_named`: 4 × 4 fires, 2 × 2 passes → `kept at 2 × 2`, two entries with scopes
  `resolution:4x4` (`fired`) and `resolution:2x2` (`not-fired`); an entry with no `scope` fails the load
  (`decision-scope-invalid`).
- `resultant_not_applicable_without_array`: a grasp receipt whose resultant row is `not run: array not built`, or
  `not run: mask-only`, gives clause 4 `not-applicable` and leaves `not-fired` reachable; a receipt with the row
  absent gives `not-run` and a refused `not-fired`.
- `resultant_error_fires_clause_4`: a planted array that reports the pad's total load in every taxel (resultant
  wrong by the taxel count) fires clause 4.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kill_tactile_decision.sh` (header: `bead=N38a-39 milestone=M6 labels=kill-gate,sensorium
gates=KILL-TACTILE plants=missing-receipt,self-recorded`). It collects the pilot record and the receipts of
`tactile_pad_grasp.sh`, evaluates the clauses (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_tactile_decision`,
then the evaluation on the real receipts), writes the ADR draft and the decision entries into a scratch copy of
the register and validates it (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example validate_register -- --file
<scratch>/gates.toml`); the plants remove one receipt (expects `not-run` and a refused `not-fired`) and write
the entry from the implementing session (expects the load refusal). The entries are committed to
`registries/gates.toml` by the recording session, not by the script. Log at
`target/e2e-logs/kill_tactile_decision/<utc>.ndjson` with a
transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N38a-39"), `step`, `event`; each clause as an
`assert` with value, unit and threshold (differing cases `> 0`, the cost ratio's lower bound `<= 1.25` with its upper bound logged, resultant error
`<= 1e-12`), the admission table per tier, receipt digests, both refusals, and the `summary` with assertions passed and
failed, naming KILL-TACTILE, the outcome and the ADR path.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers the measurements rest on: the torsional lever of a uniformly loaded square pad of side s about its
centre is 0.3826·s; a 2 × 2 grid of point contacts gives 0.3536·s and a 4 × 4 grid 0.3744·s; translational
capacity is μ·N at every resolution; the resultant of a tactile array equals the sum over its taxels (KA-523).
Negative controls: the all-taxels-report-the-total array; contacts dropped silently at capacity; a decision
recorded with a missing receipt. Determinism: the evaluation is a pure function of the receipts; identical twice.

## Considerations

- Seventy-five cases are a paper-scale probe; clause 1 asks only whether the mechanism can matter.
- A kept gate makes no reality claim about any tactile sensor.
- The pad pilot was drafted twice and is one bead, N18b-35 (N38a-30 superseded); the tactile array is N20a-19,
  which this decision reads through N38a-31's script. The two gates are independent: pads can be kept on the
  contact mask, and the array can be kept on one patch per pad.
- At most four Moonshot items are active at once; this epic is fourth in the default order of funding.
