## Background

A kill gate is decided on recorded values at its milestone, against a trigger and a fallback registered before the
measurement, and the outcome is written as an architecture decision record (ADR) whichever way it goes. A gate
that fires is not a project failure; it replaces a capability by a cheaper one that the design already contains.

KILL-TAXEL is the kill gate of the *tactile-array sensor model* of FrankenRobotics: a sensor of `frob-sense`
(Frontier tier, behind the feature `frontier-tactile`, off by default; mechanism N20a-19) that reads the contact
set of a pad through an array of small sensing elements (taxels) with finite footprints and saturation in normal
and shear load. Its first check is conservation: the resultant load of the array equals the sum over its taxels.
No task before M6 needs it.

The sensing chapter gives every Frontier sensor model that is on no task's path before M5 (tactile arrays, event
cameras, radiometric RGB, retroreflector bloom, backscatter from optical volumes) one rule: it is funded only
when a registered task needs it, names one analytic fixture as its pilot, has no speed benefit to claim, and
falls back to the mask-only model under the cost rule of exposure integration, which is more than 25% added cost
of the stage it joins. For the tactile array the pilot is N20a-18 (a sphere pressed on a flat elastic pad, a
Hertz contact) and the mask-only model is a *contact mask per taxel*: each taxel reports only whether it is in
contact, from the contact set's footprint, with no pressure or shear value.

The gate register as compiled had no row for this rule, so the pilot's record could not name a kill gate and
could not fund the mechanism. N38a-37 enters the row (the name KILL-TAXEL is DESIGN); this bead evaluates it.
DESIGN: the array has a row and a decision of its own, separate from the pads' gate KILL-TACTILE (N38a-39),
because the two differ in tier (Frontier against Moonshot), pilot (N20a-18 against N18b-35) and fallback (the
contact mask against one patch per pad), and because either can be kept without the other: the array reads any
contact set, down to one point sample per pad. Rejected: a clause of KILL-TACTILE (a kill row has one fallback
and that row is decided per pad resolution, a scope the array does not have).

Trigger as registered (TARGET numbers). Either clause fires the gate:
1. the pilot's falsifier holds, by N20a-18's frozen rule: (a) on the registered task's decision (which the task
   names when it registers, for example "is the object centred on the pad within 2 mm") the taxel readings at
   the candidate pitch give no better decision accuracy than the six-axis resultant alone on the fixture's sweep
   of offsets; or (b) the estimated added cost exceeds 25% of the contact-and-sensing stage for the task's pad
   sizes;
2. the built model's measured added cost exceeds 25% of the contact-and-sensing stage on the registered task
   (N20a-19's cost measurement).

Fallback: the mask-only model; the capability is then described as "contact mask per taxel". Decided at M6.

## Scope

In: evaluating the two clauses on the pilot's record and the mechanism's cost receipt; the decision record and
the row's decision entry; what changes downstream. Out: registering the row (N38a-37); the pilot and its verdict
(N20a-18); the sensor and its cost measurement (N20a-19); the pads and their gate (N38a-31, N38a-39); the
kill-row schema, the decision entry's checks and `apply_decisions` (N40-03).

## Technical approach

**Inputs (by receipt hash, none retyped).** From the pilot register `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`:
the record of N20a-18 (`PILOT-SENSE-<nn>`) with its status and verdict, the decision-accuracy pair (taxel
readings against the six-axis resultant) and the cost estimate. From N20a-19: the receipt of its cost
measurement, the added cost of the kernel `K-sense-tactile` as a fraction of the contact-and-sensing stage on
the registered task, with its bounds where the receipt carries them.

**Evaluation.**
1. Read the pilot record.
   - Status `withdrawn` with the reason "not due: no registered task" (no registered task named tactile sensing
     by the M6 exit candidate, so the pilot was not run): outcome `not-reached`. The mask-only model is in force
     without a measurement.
   - Verdict `falsifier-holds`: clause 1 fires, outcome `fired`.
   - In both cases N20a-19 was not started and has closed `withdrawn` (N41-17: it delivers nothing and never
     counts as work done), which is how this bead, which depends on it, becomes ready. Go to step 4.
   - A `void` or `inconclusive` record decides nothing: the pilot is repaired and re-run under its rules first;
     if the M6 exit arrives before that, the outcome is `deferred` with `deferred_to` (a later exit) and the
     `reason`, and the mechanism stays unfunded.
2. Verdict `falsifier-rejected`: read the cost receipt. Clause 2 fires when the recorded fraction exceeds 0.25.
   Where the receipt carries paired lower and upper bounds of the fraction, the lower bound is the figure
   compared (DESIGN: the reading of the cost clauses of KILL-RUT and KILL-TACTILE, so that a kill rule reads a
   bound and never a point estimate); a fraction whose bounds straddle 0.25 is recorded with both bounds and
   does not fire. A missing cost receipt makes clause 2 `not-run`, and `not-fired` cannot be recorded while a
   clause is `not-run`.
3. One repair cycle is permitted for clause 2, in N40-03's form: N20a-19 opens it with a `repair-cycle` note line
   of its log naming the failed receipt and the hypothesis, makes one bounded change to the mechanism and closes
   it with one re-measurement on the same task and threshold; the first receipt stays on record. A second cycle
   is a `deferred` decision. The pilot's verdict is never repaired here.
4. Write the decision ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TAXEL-M6.md` (the one directory for decision records) with the
   fields N40-03 requires: the kill gate; the milestone; the trigger as registered and the amendments applied;
   each measured value with its receipt hash beside the trigger's number (the accuracy pair, the estimated
   fraction, the measured fraction with its bounds); the repair taken; the clause-by-clause verdict; the outcome
   (`not-fired`, `fired`, `not-reached` or `deferred`); the fallback in force and the name the capability
   carries; the beads re-read, each "stands on the fallback", "superseded by this ADR" or "withdrawn before this
   decision" (N20a-19; N38a-31, whose grasp script reads the sensor); what changes downstream; and the
   recording session, which must not be a session that implemented the sensor.
5. That session writes the row's decision in `registries/gates.toml`: `decision = { outcome, adr =
   "docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TAXEL-M6.md", date, session, receipts = [<hashes>] }`. The row has no `scope_kind`, so the
   decision is one table and carries no `scope`. `receipts` lists the hashes the outcome rests on under
   `receipts/gates/`, at least one for `not-fired`; the row gains no measured value. The load refuses an entry
   whose `session` implemented the mechanism and a `not-fired` entry with no receipt (N40-03's checks).

**Downstream.** *Not fired*: the tactile-array card is admitted behind `frontier-tactile`; every reading is
labelled with its card's provenance (`generic` or `assumed`), since no tactile-array measurement has been
imported, and no reality claim follows. *Fired*: the tactile sensor reports the contact mask per taxel; the
generated views and the capability listing print the fallback's name (`apply_decisions` of N40-03); the pads'
grasp script (N38a-31) checks the mask against the cells in contact and records its resultant row `not run:
mask-only`, which the pads' decision (N38a-39) reads as not applicable. *Not reached*: the mask-only model is in
force without a measurement; if a task registers after the M6 exit, the pilot runs under a new record and the
decision is taken again at that exit as a new entry (entries are appended, the latest is in force; a killed or
unreached capability is never enabled by a feature flag alone).

Determinism: the evaluation is a pure function of the record and the receipts (class D0). Evidence class of the
decision's inputs: `Tested`, labelled `synthetic` for the pilot's analytic fixture.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TAXEL-M6.md` exists with every field N40-03 requires, cites the registered trigger and
      one receipt hash per measured value, and states `not-fired`, `fired`, `not-reached` or `deferred` with the
      clause that fired.
- [ ] The row KILL-TAXEL in `registries/gates.toml` carries `decision = { outcome, adr, date, session, receipts }`,
      written by a session other than the implementing one; an entry by an implementing session, a `not-fired`
      entry with no receipt and an entry carrying a `scope` each fail the load.
- [ ] A `withdrawn` pilot record gives `not-reached` and a `falsifier-holds` verdict gives `fired` without
      reading a cost receipt; in both cases N20a-19 is closed `withdrawn`.
- [ ] A measured fraction above 0.25 (its lower bound, where bounds are recorded) gives `fired`; one at or below
      it gives `not-fired`; straddling bounds are recorded and do not fire; a missing cost receipt blocks
      `not-fired`.
- [ ] If fired or not reached: the capability is listed as "contact mask per taxel" and N20a-19 is re-read in
      the ADR. If not fired: the measured fraction and its receipt are in the ADR.
- [ ] The M6 exit review can read the outcome from the register without opening this bead.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/kill_taxel_decision.rs`, on scratch records and receipts (labelled `synthetic`):
- `falsifier_holds_fires_clause_1`: a record with verdict `falsifier-holds` (accuracy 0.80 with taxels against
  0.80 with the resultant, or an estimated fraction of 0.26) gives `fired`; the cost receipt is not read.
- `withdrawn_not_due_is_not_reached`: status `withdrawn`, reason "not due: no registered task" → `not-reached`.
- `void_record_decides_nothing`: a `void` record returns no outcome; at the exit it yields `deferred` only with
  `deferred_to` later than M6 and a non-empty `reason`.
- `cost_fraction_0_26_fires_clause_2`; `cost_fraction_0_25_does_not` (the trigger is "exceeds"); with bounds
  (0.23, 0.27) the outcome is `not-fired` and both bounds are in the ADR draft; with bounds (0.26, 0.31) `fired`.
- `missing_cost_receipt_blocks_not_fired`: verdict `falsifier-rejected` and no cost receipt → clause 2 `not-run`
  and a refused `not-fired`.
- `decision_entry_form`: the entry loads as one table; the same entry with `scope = "resolution:4x4"` fails
  (`decision-scope-invalid`); with `receipts = []` and outcome `not-fired` it fails; with the implementing
  session it fails.
- `decision_cites_receipts`: an ADR with a retyped fraction and no receipt hash is rejected.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/kill_taxel_decision.sh` (header: `bead=N38ap3-01 milestone=M6 labels=kill-gate,sensorium
gates=KILL-TAXEL plants=missing-receipt,self-recorded`). Steps: read the funding state with `cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example pilots -- fundable --bead N20a-19 --json` (read-only) and the pilot's record; collect
the cost receipt of N20a-19; run `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_taxel_decision`, then the evaluation
on the real record and receipt; write the ADR draft and the decision entry into a scratch copy of the register
and validate it (`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example validate_register -- --file <scratch>/gates.toml`);
apply the two plants (one receipt removed: `not-run` and a refused `not-fired`; the entry written from the
implementing session: the load refusal). The entry is committed to `registries/gates.toml` by the recording
session, not by the script, and the script writes no gate record. Log at
`target/e2e-logs/kill_taxel_decision/<utc>.ndjson` through the shared helper, with a transcript: every line
carries `ts`, `script`, `bead` ("N38ap3-01"), `step`, `event`; each command with its exact line, exit code and
wall time; the evidence block `{claim: known-answer, class: Tested, tier: G0:host, determinism: D0}`; each clause
as an `assert` with the measured value, its unit and its threshold (accuracy with taxels `>` accuracy with the
resultant; estimated fraction `<= 0.25`; measured fraction, lower bound, `<= 0.25` with the upper bound beside
it); the digests of the record, the receipts and the scratch register; both refusals with their reasons; on
failure the first differing field and the scratch path; and the `summary` with assertions passed and failed,
naming KILL-TAXEL, the outcome and the ADR path.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers the decision rests on: the cost bound 25% (a fraction of 0.25, the same number as KILL-EXPOSURE's
rule for exposure integration); the conservation check KA-523 of the sensor (the resultant load equals the sum
over taxels; tolerance 10⁻¹² relative in double precision and 10⁻⁴ in single, set by N20a-19), which the
mechanism must pass before its cost is worth reading. Negative controls: a decision recorded with a missing
receipt; a decision written by the implementing session; a `not-fired` entry with no receipt; a
`falsifier-holds` record offered together with a passing cost receipt (the outcome must stay `fired`: clause 1
is read from the pilot's verdict, which N20a-18's own rule table produced, and the cost receipt of an unfunded
mechanism is not read). Determinism lane: identical output twice on one machine.

## Considerations

- The gate governs a Frontier model and is outside the cap of four active Moonshot items.
- A kept gate makes no reality claim about any tactile sensor; without imported tactile-array measurements every
  reading carries its card's provenance.
- The plan funds the model only for a registered task. With none by the M6 exit candidate the pilot is recorded
  `withdrawn`, this decision is `not-reached`, and the M6 exit review does not wait on a task that does not
  exist.
- The pads (N38a-31) follow this sensor in order of work and are decided separately: KILL-TACTILE's resultant
  clause is not applicable when this gate has fired or was not reached.
- Open, with its fail-closed default: whether N20a-19's cost receipt carries paired bounds; until it does, the
  recorded fraction itself is compared with 0.25.
