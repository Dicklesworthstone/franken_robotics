## Background

FrankenRobotics is delivered in milestones. M1 is the "referee studio": a design studio on the most trusted
dynamics tier (the *referee*, double precision on the CPU) plus the first exact mission queries. The
verification subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) must be able to say, at the M1 exit, that every
gate that passed did so against a planted defect it catches (GATE-GAUNT-01), that the known answers due were
recovered (GATE-GAUNT-02), and that every oracle fixture is pinned and was never regenerated from the candidate
(GATE-GAUNT-05). This bead assembles the conformance content of M1 and measures GATE-GAUNT-01 for M1.

Terms. A *known answer* (KA-nnn) is an analytic or synthetic truth with a derivation on file, a tolerance, a
tier and a negative control. An *oracle* is a pinned reference tool whose output is captured once by script; it
is a comparison instrument, never a dependency (ADR-GAUNT-04), and agreement with it is Parity, never Reality.
A *counterexample* (CX-nnn) is the smallest case that breaks a tempting shortcut; each seeds at least one
*negative control* (NC-nnnn). A *battery* is a named, versioned set of scenarios.

Why at M1: the first user-visible release (the studio preview) rests on these gates, and a gate recorded without
a caught control at M1 would be inherited by everything built on the referee.

## Scope

In: (1) the G1 rows due at M1 running against their derivations and, where a row names one, a pinned oracle;
(2) the controls seeded from the counterexample catalogue made runnable for every M1 gate; (3) the `smoke` and
`fixtures` batteries registered with their identities and wired into the per-change run; (4) the M1 measurement
of GATE-GAUNT-01. Out: the mechanisms themselves (registry N38a-12, G1 run N38a-13, oracle register N38a-04,
control runner N38b-04, seeding N38b-05, all-controls script N38b-09); the battery manifests `frob.battery/1`,
their identities and membership events, and the definitions of `smoke` and `fixtures` (N24b-01); the
path-to-trigger map that decides when `fixtures` runs (N38a-22, which reads its paths from N24b-01's trigger
table, so that there is one map); the run schedule with its four triggers and budgets (N38b-25); each subsystem's
own tests. This seed is the M1 capstone of the conformance work: it depends on those mechanism beads, on
every chapter's registry bead of M0 and M1, and on the M0 and M1 beads that make rows countable for
GATE-GAUNT-02 (a row with tolerance `to set` or control `none stated` is never counted): the controls of the
seven thesis rows and the four tolerances of N00-KA's rows printed `to set` (N00-33), the controls of the seven
North-Star fixture rows (N00p1-13), the tolerance decision
for rows printed `to set` (N38b-27), and the controls owed by rows printed without one that are due at M1
(N38b-29, N38b-30, N38b-31, N38b-33). The M2 closures (N38b-32, N38b-34) are BD-223's.

Ordering (DESIGN). Because this bead depends on every M0 and M1 registry and mechanism bead, it is among the
last beads of M1 to become ready, and no component may depend on it: a registry or mechanism bead that needs the
control registry, the tiers or the G1 run depends on BD-025, N38a-02, N38b-04 or N38a-13 instead (requests filed
in polishing round 1 for the beads that depended on this capstone). The measurement is taken on the M1 exit
candidate; a gate whose test is not yet built shows `not-eligible` in the matrix and keeps this bead open; it is
never counted as caught.

## Technical approach

**1. Known answers against pinned oracles.** Oracles in use at M1, each with pin, capture date and capture script
under `tools/oracle/`, and each admitted by its own test and negative control before use:

| Oracle | Pin | Use at M1 |
|---|---|---|
| FrankenSim `fs-mbd`, `fs-contact`, `fs-query` | The commit in `franken-stack.lock` | The referee itself; parity for kinematics, dynamics, primitive distances |
| MuJoCo C, double precision | An exact release recorded with the fixture | Contact-free dynamics of the reference robots (the fixtures of BD-067, the parity bead BD-089); import fixtures |
| FrankenSciPy `solve_ivp` (DOP853) | The pinned version | Reference trajectories for smooth known answers (the dynamics battery BD-088) |
| FrankenSymPy evaluation | The pinned commit | Reference values for derived arithmetic; second checker of rational and interval certificates, never linked into the auditor |
| FrankenNumPy `fnp-linalg` | The pinned commit | Double-precision oracle for small-matrix kernels |
| Analytic derivations | `docs/derivations/<fixture>.md` | Every known-answer row |

Held out of every oracle role until their admission tests pass: the sibling PPO update (it fails three of the
learning rules it would be an oracle for) and the sibling `planar_drive` routine (zero translation on a
near-straight case). Two oracles that disagree are a filed finding with both pins; the candidate is tuned
toward neither until a derivation file explains the difference.

**2. Rows due at M1.** Select `due ≤ M1` from `registry/known_answers.toml`; run each on the weakest site that
decides it (`host`, then `auditor`, then `referee`); count as passing only rows that are eligible (derivation
on file, tolerance set, control registered). The count of ineligible rows due is printed, not hidden.

**3. Catalogue seeds made runnable.** For every gate with a part due at M1 (the acceptance list of the
milestone: the Marrow, Skeleton, Sinew, Kinesis, Traction, Sensorium, Reflex, Atelier, Chronicle, Arena, Augur,
Lemma, Ground, Tether, safety, workspace and program gates named there, the admission gate of the agent
surface, the completed parity baseline, and the Gauntlet's own GATE-GAUNT-01, -02, -04 for the certificate
checker, -05, -09 and -10): at least one control in `registry/controls.toml` with state runnable, its mutation
feature or fixture present in the owning crate, and a `caught` outcome in the M1 run.

**4. Batteries.** The manifests and identities are N24b-01's and the trigger map is N38a-22's; this bead runs
both batteries at M1 as entries of the tier targets, checks that the identity in the receipt equals the committed
manifest hash, and wires them into the per-change run. Their M1 content, as N24b-01 defines it:
- `smoke`: minutes of wall time; every design template and reference robot that exists at M1 (`quad-mammal`,
  `humanoid`, `diff-drive`, `mecanum`, the cart of fixture 1) on a handful of scenarios on the referee; fixed
  seeds; runs on every change. Its manifest lists (robot, scenario, seed) triples and has an identity (hash of
  the manifest); a silently shortened battery changes identity.
- `fixtures`: the analytic layer of `fixture:cart-sill-dock` (a loaded cart crossing a sill and docking) and the
  known answers of the requirement monitors; runs on every change that touches contact, actuators, clocks or
  the requirement language. The second fixture joins at M2.
Both run as entries of the tier targets (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- battery:smoke`).

**5. Measurement.** `scripts/e2e/gauntlet_controls.sh M1` produces the control × gate matrix; a fresh-context
session records GATE-GAUNT-01 for M1 from its receipt.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: GATE-GAUNT-01 for M1 — every M1 gate has a registered negative control that it
      catches; the matrix shows every (control, gate) pair `caught` and no `pass` row without a caught control.
- [ ] Every oracle fixture used has a pin, a date and a capture script; a planted fixture regenerated from the
      candidate fails CI (INV-GAUNT-01); no foreign tool is in a production dependency graph.
- [ ] Every known-answer row due at M1 either passes on its site inside its tolerance with a derivation file or
      is printed as an obligation; none with tolerance `to set` or control `none stated` is counted.
- [ ] `smoke` and `fixtures` exist with identities, run on their triggers, and a shortened manifest changes
      identity.
- [ ] The PPO update and `planar_drive` appear in no oracle role without a passing admission record.
- [ ] The gate is recorded by a session that implemented none of the M1 capabilities.
- [ ] No registry or mechanism bead depends on this bead; a gate whose test is not built reads `not-eligible`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/m1_content.rs`:
- `m1_gates_have_runnable_controls`: join of the M1 exit list with `controls.toml`; zero gates without one.
- `oracle_fixtures_have_pins`: every fixture header has kind `oracle`, pin, date, script.
- `held_out_oracles_not_used`: no row names the PPO update or `planar_drive` as its oracle.
- `batteries_run_with_committed_identity`: the M1 run's receipt names `smoke` and `fixtures` with the identities
  of N24b-01's committed manifests; a scratch manifest with one triple removed gives a different identity and the
  check fails (the hash and trigger unit tests themselves are N24b-01's `smoke_manifest_identity` and N38a-22's
  trigger tests, not repeated here).
- `fixtures_selected_on_trigger`: through N38a-22's map, a scratch change under the contact crate puts
  `fixtures` in the per-change selection of the M1 run; a change under the surface crate does not.
- `ka_due_m1_accounting`: eligible + obligations = rows due; obligations listed by KA number.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/conformance_m1.sh` (header: `bead=BD-146 milestone=M1 labels=gauntlet,exit
gates=GATE-GAUNT-01,GATE-GAUNT-02,GATE-GAUNT-05 plants=regenerated-fixture,detach-control`). It runs
`frob sim run diff-drive --profile referee --seed 0 --key conformance-m1-oracle --json` (one reference robot,
compared with its oracle fixture), the `smoke` and `fixtures` batteries, the G1
rows due, then `gauntlet_controls.sh M1`; it plants a fixture regenerated from the candidate (must fail) and a
detached control (must fail). Log at `target/e2e-logs/conformance_m1/<utc>.ndjson`: commands, exit codes, wall
time; per-battery scenario counts and identities; rows passed, failed and owed; the matrix triple; the evidence
block of each verb envelope (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed); digests of compared states;
the `summary` naming the three gates.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers cited as fixtures of the run: the docking grid of fixture 1 (344 cells admitted, 217 excluded, 9
on the boundary of 561; KA-501); the wheel-loop spectral radius 0.9207 against the mutant's 1.497 (CX-262).
Negative controls: the regenerated fixture; the detached control; a row counted while its tolerance is `to
set`. Determinism lanes: `twice` and `profiles` on the referee rows, class D0 for exact rows.

## Considerations

- A gate may pass before M1; none may move later without a recorded decision.
- The first reading of the kill gate on the development oracle (KILL-DEVORACLE) is taken at this exit
  (N38b-24): results obtained on an incumbent simulator before the referee existed support no gate.
- Open: the runtime budget per change is undecided; the fail-closed default is exactly these two batteries per
  change and everything else nightly.
- Risk: a reported foundation defect is relied on before it is rechecked; signal: a known answer or oracle that
  cites one of the four reported modules; mitigation: the admission records above.
