## Background

M2 of FrankenRobotics puts a batched engine under the studio: one model on the CPU batch tier (`batch-cpu`), on
CUDA and on Metal, with many designs in one batch. Three kinds of conformance content become due, and the
verification subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) must have them wired before the M2 exit:

- the *standard battery*: for each robot class about 200 scenarios (TARGET) with fixed seeds, the one battery
  the Gauntlet owns. The fidelity ledger (fast tier against referee), the divergence ledger, the tier-correction
  ledger and the exploitation detectors all read it, so a change to it is a recorded event with a new battery
  identity, and a silently shortened battery changes identity;
- *metamorphic relations*: relations that hold without knowing the answer (53 are registered: 11 carried and
  42 added, MR-01 to MR-42), each with the mutation that must break it;
- *chaos tests*: injected faults that must end in a typed refusal or an accepted state with no leaked resource.

This bead wires the three on devices and measures GATE-GAUNT-01 for M2. It is the M2 link of the chain of
per-milestone conformance beads; the mechanisms are separate beads and are cited by key.

## Scope

In: binding the standard battery to its four readers and to the device sites; the relation runs due at M2 on
`strict`, `lockstep`, `cuda` and `metal`; the kernel-side chaos families on devices; the M2 control matrix. Out:
the battery's definition and freeze (N38a-20, seed BD-212); the relation registry and records (N38a-14 to -18);
the chaos framework and families (N38a-23, N38a-24); the determinism tier (N38a-35).

## Technical approach

**1. Standard battery wiring.**
1. The battery is frozen by its owner bead; this bead registers its identity (hash of the manifest) in
   `registry/batteries.toml` with the list of readers: `fidelity.ledger`, the divergence ledger of `spark.check`,
   the tier-correction ledger, `eval.detect`.
2. Each reader's output records the battery identity it read; a conformance test fails when a reader's recorded
   identity differs from the frozen one, or when a reader ran on a subset without printing the subset.
3. Per robot class the scenario rows are: stand, push recovery, each atlas gait at three speeds, turning, stairs
   up and down, a slope, a low-friction patch, a payload and a drop, crossed with randomized parameters under
   fixed seeds; wheeled, tracked and wheel-legged classes replace the gait rows with straight travel, turning
   in place, a sill, a ramp and a split-grip strip. A class whose list is not frozen has no fidelity claim.
4. The battery runs on `referee` (sampled), `strict`, `lockstep`, `cuda` and `metal`; column sums (scenarios run
   per site) are printed and must equal the manifest counts.

**2. Metamorphic relations due at M2.** Those whose subsystems exist: batch-composition invariance (MR-01: a
world's digests identical alone, in any batch, slot, co-tenancy, bucket capacity 32, 64 or 128 and shard count
of one device family), variants against homogeneous runs (MR-02), address-keyed draws (MR-03), similarity
rescaling by powers of two (MR-06), scaled Cholesky (MR-07), fork and replay (MR-08), transactional retry
(MR-09), clock re-phasing and checkpoint relations (MR-15 to MR-18), specialized against generic kernels
(MR-38), anchored cell shifts (MR-42), and the eleven carried relations. Each runs at 10⁴ random scenarios per
site per release (TARGET; 10³ on the referee by default, with the reduced count printed), and each registered
mutation must break its relation. Relations whose subsystems land later keep their later due milestone.

**3. Chaos on devices.** The kernel-side families: cancellation at every kernel-batch boundary under a
deterministic schedule (the receipt reports the last committed tick per world; a dropped handle is not a
cancellation receipt); device loss mid-step, a kernel cache entry with one flipped byte, an arena lease
revoked, a strict-backend mismatch before scoring, out-of-memory at admission (a chunk replayed from the
committed slab gives the unfaulted digests; the flipped entry is refused); 10⁵ faults between the checks and
the commit of a transactional step (TARGET) with zero uncommitted reads; contact rows, islands, packet queues
and buckets forced past capacity (the typed refusal or the `capacity-biased` label, never a silent truncation).
GATE-GAUNT-06 is due at M2 for kernels and at M3 for the daemon.

**4. The no-GPU variant.** If no device family is admitted, every device entry is `Skipped` with the reason,
device gates are recorded `not run`, and the wiring is exercised on `batch-cpu` alone; nothing is counted as
passed for a site that did not run.

**5. Measurement.** `scripts/e2e/gauntlet_controls.sh M2`; a fresh-context session records GATE-GAUNT-01 for M2.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: GATE-GAUNT-01 for M2 — every gate with a part due at M2 has a registered negative
      control caught in the M2 run.
- [ ] The four readers record the frozen battery identity; a planted shortened battery changes identity and
      fails the binding test.
- [ ] Every relation due at M2 holds on its sites at the stated count and is broken by its mutation
      (GATE-GAUNT-03); MR-01 holds on 10⁴ worlds per device family (GATE-GAUNT-07).
- [ ] Every kernel-side chaos case ends in its typed refusal or an accepted state with no leaked device
      resource (GATE-GAUNT-06, kernel part).
- [ ] Skipped device sites are counted and named; a skip never contributes to a pass.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/m2_content.rs`:
- `battery_identity_bound_to_readers`: four readers, one identity; a fifth reader without a recorded identity
  fails.
- `battery_column_sums`: scenarios run per site equal manifest counts (a fixture of 3 classes × 8 scenarios).
- `relations_due_m2_registered`: every relation listed above has entries for each of its sites and a mutation
  feature.
- `chaos_families_due_m2_registered`: four kernel-side families, each with schedule files and an oracle.
- `m2_gates_have_runnable_controls`.
- `no_gpu_variant_counts_skips`: with `E2E_DEVICES=cpu`, device entries are `Skipped`, device gates not passed.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/conformance_m2.sh` (header: `bead=BD-223 milestone=M2 labels=gauntlet,exit
gates=GATE-GAUNT-01,GATE-GAUNT-03,GATE-GAUNT-06,GATE-GAUNT-07 plants=short-battery,reduction-by-capacity`).
It runs `frob fidelity ledger` on the standard battery, `frob spark check`, the relation script and the chaos
script for M2, the determinism lanes, then `gauntlet_controls.sh M2`. Plants: a shortened battery manifest (the
identity check must fail) and a reduction tree shaped by bucket capacity (MR-01's mutation; the digests at
capacities 32 and 64 must differ). Log at `target/e2e-logs/conformance_m2/<utc>.ndjson`: commands, exit codes,
wall time; battery identity and per-site column sums; per-relation scenario counts and violations (threshold
0); schedules run and leaks (threshold 0); world digests compared; the matrix triple; evidence blocks with
tier, determinism class, identities and seed; the `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: digests at capacities 32, 64 and 128 are equal for one world (MR-01); a retry of a transactional
step equals a clean run (redraw on rejection would give P(positive) = ¾ instead of ½ on the noise-refinement
fixture, MR-09). Negative controls: the shortened battery; the capacity-shaped reduction; a batch-wide
normalizer; packet queues left out of a checkpoint. Determinism lanes: all five, each against the declared
class; D2 is not a cross-backend claim.

## Considerations

- Kill gates decided at M2 (the kernel toolchain, the contact law, the Metal tier, mixed batches, cross-backend
  bit equality) change which sites exist; the wiring reads the admitted sites from the gate file and adapts.
- The scenario lists for wheeled, tracked, wheel-legged and manipulating classes are an open question
  (N38b-37); until frozen, those classes have no fidelity claim.
- Whether 10⁴ scenarios per relation is affordable on the referee is open (N38b-38); default 10³, printed.
- Runs above the default machine envelope name their budget and wait for the owner's approval.
