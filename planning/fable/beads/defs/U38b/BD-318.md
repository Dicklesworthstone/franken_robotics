## Background

M3 of FrankenRobotics adds places (seven interior archetypes), tasks, adversarial search for failures
(the subsystem called Nemesis), counterfactual debugging of failures (Autopsy) and the blinded qualification
lane. The verification subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) needs three kinds of content before
the M3 exit, each of which is a store of planted things with known answers:

- two batteries: `hard` (for each task family and robot class, the versioned list of minimal counterexample
  worlds Nemesis keeps) and `covering` (a scene set chosen by submodular coverage selection, with one slot in
  five drawn at random from held-out scenes; its fallback is the standard battery);
- the planted-defect registries that prove Nemesis and Autopsy find what was planted and nothing else;
- the hostile-input corpora that prove the safety rules hold against crafted input.

This bead assembles them, binds each planted item to a negative-control record, and measures GATE-GAUNT-01 for
M3. A planted registry is the potency control of a method: a search that cannot find a planted trap is silent
about real ones, and a debugger that names a cause for a chaotic failure is inventing one.

## Scope

In: the conformance-side manifests, labels and control records for the items below; the M3 control matrix. For
Nemesis and for Autopsy this bead writes one index file each and the negative-control records, and no planted
case.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- the planted cases of Nemesis, their planting procedures and truth checks: the 100 illegal edits, 100
  impossible traps, 100 solvable traps of known nominal probability and the rare-event fixtures (probabilities
  10⁻⁴ and 10⁻⁵, one with two separate modes) in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/planted/nemesis/`: N26a-04.
  The planted simulator artifacts and planted genuine failures in `registry/planted/nemesis_guard/`: N26a-15.
  The planted causes of sizes 1, 2 and 3 for shrinking in `registry/planted/nemesis_shrink/`: N26a-34. The
  biased and unbiased planted screens in `registry/planted/nemesis_audit/`: N26b-06, at M4, read by BD-377.
  This bead depends on the three M3 beads, lists their files by path and hash, and never copies or regenerates
  a case. (An earlier form of this bead kept the Nemesis plants in its own file `registry/planted/nemesis.toml`;
  that file is not built.)
- the planted cases of Autopsy, their planting procedures, truth checks and case files. The 200 planted single
  causes with their ten-candidate lists, the 20 out-of-list controls, the single-cause check and the manifest
  `crates/frob-conformance/registry/autopsy_planted.toml`: N27b-08. The 100 joint and alternative cases with
  four-corner truth tables, the three host tables and the registry
  `crates/frob-conformance/registry/autopsy-plants-coalitions.toml`: N27a-12. The 100 chaotic and 100 no-cause
  cases, the knife-edge fixture, the 30 controls and the registry
  `crates/frob-conformance/registry/autopsy-plants-abstention.toml`: N27a-14. This bead depends on all three,
  lists their files by path and hash, and never copies or regenerates a case;
- the batteries' selection logic and the hard-world set itself (seeds BD-275, BD-278; reading rules N38a-21);
  the search, oracle, attribution and importers under test; the fuzz and firewall chaos families (N38a-26); the
  design corpus (N38a-36).
- the catalogue entries and known-answer rows of the chapters due at M3 (Nemesis N26-CX and N26-KA, Autopsy
  N27-CX, the ten-fold harness N39-CX and N39-KA). This bead depends on them, as BD-146 does on the M0 and M1
  registries: the M3 matrix shows a caught control for an M3 gate only after its chapter's entries are entered
  and seeded. No component depends on this bead.

## Technical approach

**1. Batteries (`registry/batteries.toml` gains two identities).**
- `hard`: read under three constraints, each an existing refusal. It is discovery data and never a confirmation
  sample (FROB-REF-567: a hard-world or search-selected world offered as a confirmation sample). It is never
  the nominal draw of a rare-event bound (FROB-REF-560). It is never the only content of a battery, since a
  battery made of past failures overfits them. A world enters only with a `solvable-causal` or
  `plant-feasible` witness and after promotion on the referee. A re-run after a repair is labelled a
  regression result, not evidence of robustness.
- `covering`: the manifest records the selection's inputs, the certificate of coverage and the one-in-five
  random held-out slots with their seed; the identity changes when any of them changes.
- `standard`: the fourth reader of the standard battery, `eval.detect` (BD-267, M3), listed by BD-223 with
  `due = "M3"`, is bound here: its output records the battery identity it read, and the binding test of BD-223
  fails when that identity differs from the frozen one or a subset is read without printing it.

**2. Planted-defect registry of Nemesis: an index (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/planted/nemesis.index.toml`;
labels frozen before any tuning).** DESIGN, for the reason given for Autopsy in section 3: one index over the
case directories that the Nemesis beads write, holding no case. Per row: the row's name, the case directory's
path, the BLAKE3 hash of its manifest as committed by the owning bead, the counts it must contain, the expected
outcome, the gate, and the identifiers of the negative-control records bound to it.

| Planted item | Case directory and owner | Counts the index checks | Expected | Control that must be caught |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Illegal edits | `registry/planted/nemesis/` (N26a-04) | 100 | 100 of 100 refused with the bound named | A legality profile with the bound removed admits them |
| Impossible traps (for example a doorway narrower than the body) | `registry/planted/nemesis/` (N26a-04) | 100 | 0 of 100 counted as counterexamples; `infeasible-for-candidate` with the bottleneck | A solvability oracle that checks a point robot calls the doorway solvable |
| Solvable traps of known nominal probability | `registry/planted/nemesis/` (N26a-04) | 100 | At least 95 of 100 receive a causal witness | Card-uniform randomization at equal budget, as the comparison arm |
| Artifacts: box-cone traction; energy-pumping penetration recovery | `registry/planted/nemesis_guard/` (N26a-15) | 2 kinds, with the planted genuine failures | `artifact-candidate`, with the model variant that removed each | A genuine planted failure survives every variant |
| Shrinking: planted causes of size 1, 2 and 3 among 8 to 32 edits | `registry/planted/nemesis_shrink/` (N26a-34) | per size | The planted set exactly | The order-dependence case: {c, d, e} before the pair check, {a, b} after |
| Planted failures of probability 10⁻⁴ and 10⁻⁵, one with two separate modes | `registry/planted/nemesis/` (N26a-04) | 30 repetitions | Exploration reaches failures with at least 10 times fewer episodes than crude sampling at 10⁻⁵; both modes found in at least 80% | A fork with no fresh randomness is refused |
| Planted winner removed by a biased screen | `registry/planted/nemesis_audit/` (N26b-06), due M4 | per campaign | The removal is reported | An unbiased screen passes |

The last row is due at M4, with the screening audit lane (§26.10): at M3 the index lists it as `not-due`, and it
is checked by BD-377. The index is checked, not trusted: `nemesis_index_matches_case_files` recomputes each
manifest's hash and counts and fails on any difference, and `labels_frozen_before_tuning` applies to these
directories as to the Autopsy files.

**3. Planted-defect registry of Autopsy: an index (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/planted/autopsy.toml`).**
DESIGN: the registry of Autopsy's plants is one index file over the case files that the Autopsy beads write, and
holds no case itself. Reason: each case file is produced by a planting procedure that establishes the truth of
every case by direct simulation and freezes it by hash before any attribution code sees it; a second file with
the same cases would be a second copy of labels that could drift from the first, and the plan gives no path for
either. Per row the index records: the row's name, the case file's path, its BLAKE3 hash as committed by the
owning bead, the counts it must contain, the expected outcome, the gate, and the identifiers of the
negative-control records bound to it.

| Planted item | Case file and owner | Counts the index checks | Expected | Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 failures in five classes (friction drop, torque cap, payload shift, latency, terrain step), ten candidate factors each | `registry/autopsy_planted.toml` (N27b-08) | 200 admitted, 40 per class, ten candidates with exactly one rescuer; 20 out-of-list controls | The planted factor ranks first with label `but-for` in at least 90% (GATE-AUTOPSY-03) | A failure planted through a factor outside the list names no factor |
| Joint and alternative pairs | `registry/autopsy-plants-coalitions.toml` (N27a-12) | 100 simulated cases (50 joint, 50 alternative; 10 per pair of classes); 3 host tables; 10 worlds with no adverse factor | Labels `joint` and `alternative` in at least 95 of 100 (GATE-AUTOPSY-04); Shapley values (½, ½) in both; repair sets {1, 2}, and {1} or {2} | A summary that reports only Shapley values cannot tell them apart |
| Chaotic placebo: a doubling map with failure on its fortieth iterate; failures with no cause in the list | `registry/autopsy-plants-abstention.toml` (N27a-14) | 100 chaotic (50 host, 50 simulated); the knife-edge fixture; 100 no-cause (20 per class); 30 controls | A 10⁻¹² placebo is amplified by 2⁴⁰ ≈ 1.1 × 10¹²; about half of placebo forks flip; label `chaotic: unattributable`; no factor named in 100 of 100 of each kind (GATE-AUTOPSY-05) | The same map over five iterates attributes normally |
| Null fork | The fork fixtures of the Autopsy crate (no planted case file) | — | Identical digests | A checkpoint with its packet queue stripped is refused |
| First divergence | The divergence fixtures of the Autopsy crate (no planted case file) | — | Tick 1 for traces differing at ticks 1 and 5 | A pointwise midpoint search reports tick 5 |

The index is checked, not trusted: `autopsy_index_matches_case_files` recomputes each file's hash and counts and
fails on any difference, and a case file whose hash was committed after the first tuning commit of the method it
tests fails `labels_frozen_before_tuning`. The paths are relative to `crates/frob-conformance/`.

**4. Hostile-input corpora (`crates/frob-conformance/corpora/`, every file headed `synthetic`).**

| Corpus | Size (TARGET) | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| Hostile strings through every verb that echoes imported text | At least 200 | None appears outside an untrusted-marked field, in a tool description, a prompt or a suggested next command |
| Read paths against a confirmation run by a session holding every discovery scope | At least 25 enumerated | All return FROB-REF-840; a test build that leaks per-world outcomes is detected by the adaptive-retry test |
| Traversal, absolute-path and swapped-link attempts on every path argument | The enumerated corpus | All refused (FROB-REF-734) |
| Crafted files that reach each importer limit; fuzz seeds per importer | 10⁷ executions per importer | No panic; each limit refused (FROB-REF-832) |
| Fuzzed read attempts against a sealed campaign | 10⁴ | No sealed field returned; a planted leak path found by the audit |
| Results of class D4 offered on each of five evidence paths | 5 paths | Each refused (FROB-REF-825); the same policy re-evaluated under D2 accepted |
| Supply-chain plants: a kernel cache entry with one flipped byte; a second async-runtime package | 2 | Refused; reported |

**5. Binding and measurement.** Every row above is one or more NC records with the gates of its rule; the M3
matrix is produced by `scripts/e2e/gauntlet_controls.sh M3` and recorded by a fresh-context session.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: GATE-GAUNT-01 for M3 — every gate with a part due at M3 has a caught control.
- [ ] `hard` is refused as a confirmation sample and as a nominal draw, and a battery made only of hard worlds
      is rejected; a regression result carries its label.
- [ ] `eval.detect` records the frozen standard battery identity, completing the four readers bound by BD-223.
- [ ] The Nemesis and Autopsy registries exist with frozen labels, counts as tabulated, and one NC record per
      row; label files predate any tuning commit of the method they test.
- [ ] The Nemesis registry is the index of section 2: each row names a directory written by N26a-04, N26a-15,
      N26a-34 or (at M4) N26b-06, the recorded hash equals the manifest's hash and the hash that bead committed,
      the counts match, and no planted case of Nemesis exists in any other file of the conformance registry; the
      biased-screen row reads `not-due` at M3.
- [ ] The Autopsy registry is the index of section 3: each of its three case-file rows names a file written by
      N27b-08, N27a-12 or N27a-14, the recorded hash equals the file's hash and the hash that bead committed,
      the counts match, and no planted case of Autopsy exists in any other file of the conformance registry.
- [ ] Every corpus exists at its size; each expected outcome holds; each corpus has a planted product defect
      (the limit or mark removed) that makes its test fail.
- [ ] No corpus file or log line contains private-scene data.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/m3_content.rs`: `hard_refused_as_confirmation` (FROB-REF-567);
`hard_refused_as_nominal_draw` (FROB-REF-560); `battery_of_only_hard_worlds_rejected`;
`covering_identity_changes_with_random_slots`; `standard_battery_bound_to_eval_detect` (the four readers, one
identity; an `eval.detect` result naming another battery identity fails); `nemesis_registry_counts` (100, 100, 100, read through the index);
`nemesis_index_matches_case_files` (a manifest with one plant removed, or with its hash changed after the
recorded commit, fails); `nemesis_audit_row_not_due_at_m3`;
`autopsy_registry_counts` (read through the index: 200 in five classes of 40 and 20 out-of-list controls; 100
coalition cases, 50 and 50, with 3 host tables; 100 chaotic, 100 no-cause and 30 controls);
`autopsy_index_matches_case_files` (hashes and counts recomputed; a case file with one label edited fails);
`labels_frozen_before_tuning` (commit order);
`corpus_sizes` (≥ 200, ≥ 25, 10⁴); `each_planted_row_has_control`; `m3_gates_have_runnable_controls`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/conformance_m3.sh` (header: `bead=BD-318 milestone=M3 labels=gauntlet,exit
gates=GATE-GAUNT-01,GATE-GAUNT-04,GATE-GAUNT-06 plants=point-robot-oracle,mark-removed`). It runs
`frob nemesis oracle <candidate> --task <t> --world <w> --legality <profile> --key m3-oracle-<i> --json` on each
trap of the registry, `frob autopsy attribute <fork> --json` (a read verb; its positional fork is required) on
the forks of ten planted causes, an import of ten hostile strings through
`frob design import <file> --name <n> --key m3-hostile-<i> --json`, a sealed-read attempt through `frob eval
ledger <campaign> --json` (a read verb; its positional campaign is required) on a sealed campaign, then
`gauntlet_controls.sh M3`. Plants: the point-robot oracle (the doorway must be called solvable by it and the
test must fail) and a build with the untrusted mark removed. Log at
`target/e2e-logs/conformance_m3/<utc>.ndjson`: commands, exit codes, wall time; counts per registry row against
thresholds (100/100, 0/100, ≥ 95/100, ≥ 90%); refusal events with their codes; the matrix triple; evidence
blocks; the `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 2⁴⁰ ≈ 1.1 × 10¹² amplification of the placebo; Shapley values (½, ½); zero-failure counts 29,956,
299,572 and 2,995,731 at δ = 0.05. Negative controls: the point-robot oracle; the bound-removed legality
profile; the mark-removed build; a leaking test build. Determinism: replay of a stored counterexample world to
its state hash, bit for bit on the tier that found it.

## Considerations

- A certified failure rate of 10⁻⁶ needs a compute class above the default; the default certifies to 10⁻⁵ and
  reports 10⁻⁶ as `Estimated`.
- Corpora grow: a hostile input found in use is appended and becomes a permanent control.
- The Autopsy case files are read again at M4 (the single-precision lane and the comparison of agents with and
  without Autopsy); a change to any of them is a new suite identity and a new index hash, never an edit in place.
- The daemon half of the chaos gate closes here with the job and chunk protocol families.
