## Background

M4 of FrankenRobotics is the design-science milestone: cheap evaluation rungs screen many designs, a six-stage
co-design cascade runs campaigns over families, and verdicts are carried across edits by revalidation instead
of being recomputed. Each of these can produce confident wrong answers at scale, so the verification subsystem
(the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) holds three frozen stores against which they are judged, and measures
GATE-GAUNT-01 (every passing gate has a caught negative control) for M4:

- *calibration sets*: small sets of designs with trusted scores, used to decide whether a cheap rung may select
  or discard;
- *the frozen 30-amendment campaign*: thirty edits to designs, worlds and missions, fixed before any
  implementation sees them, on which every reused verdict is compared with a fresh recomputation;
- *the seeded cascade population*: a population of designs generated from a seed, with planted winners and
  planted defects, on which the cascade is run end to end.

The common rule is the potency control of a method experiment: the labels are fixed before any analysis is
tuned, and the method must both find what was planted and leave alone what was not.

## Scope

In: the three stores as conformance data (manifests, frozen labels, identities), their binding to gates and
negative controls, the M4 control matrix. Out: the rank-agreement experiment, the revalidation engine, the
cascade and the decision cores themselves; the certifier self-tests of the rank-agreement ledger and screening
audits (N38b-13; this bead runs CERT-10's M4 part, section 5); the campaign protocol, its authoring and truths
(N25a-17); the planted screens and their gate run (N26b-06, N26b-07).

## Technical approach

**1. Calibration sets (`registry/calibration/<class>.<family>.toml`).**

| Set | Size | Used by | Thresholds fixed before data |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rank agreement between rungs, per morphology class | 40 designs | The rank-agreement ledger | Kendall τ on the top band, at the lower bootstrap bound: at least 0.7 lets a rung select (`selective`); at least 0.5 lets it discard the bottom half (`discard-only`); below 0.5 the rung is `uninformative`. Episode-level correlation ρ_ep at least 0.7 |
| Baseline rung against trained specialists, per class and task family | 30 to 40 designs | Baseline controller synthesis | The lower 95% bound of τ at least 0.7 to select, at least 0.5 to discard |
| Gait orbits | The class's set | Orbit screening | τ at least 0.5 between orbit cost of transport and specialists |
| Shield margins | n calibration episodes | Runtime shields | The margin is the ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉-th smallest score |

Rules enforced by a loader: a set is frozen (hash recorded in the ledger) before the first scored run; its
designs never appear in the population it calibrates; calibration from survivors of a screen is refused
(FROB-REF-546); pooling across two model identities is refused (FROB-REF-548); an unmeasured stratum is
`uninformative` (no licence to eliminate: the stricter of the two readings in the source, the one the self-test
N38b-13 checks) and a rung without a record is refused (FROB-REF-537).

**2. The frozen 30-amendment campaign (`registry/amendments/campaign-30.toml`).** The campaign's protocol is
N25a-17's, and this bead is the campaign's home in the conformance suite at M4 that N25a-17 names; it authors no
amendment and states no truth. What it takes from N25a-17: the roles (a campaign author who implements neither
the revalidation engine nor the incumbent); the composition, ten categories with their counts (cosmetic and
visual-only 3; telemetry-only 2; camera and packet timing 5, including the apparently cosmetic change that
advances a packet by one control tick; sole and contact geometry 3; floor and material law 3; torque and actuator
limits 4; shared noise 2; load authority 2; facility and world 4; hypothesis support 2); the amendment record
format; truths recomputed from scratch by the author; and sealing by hash in `campaign30.freeze.json` before
either the engine or its comparison arm sees the set. Because a campaign is revealed after the gate run it
served, the M4 run uses a fresh campaign authored and sealed under that protocol for M4 (N25a-17's rule that a
new one is authored for the next milestone's run); `campaign-30.toml` is the conformance-side manifest of that
sealed set (its freeze hash, categories and counts) and the fixture's identity. Procedure per run:
1. Start fresh solving, dependency grades 1 and 2, and grade 3 from the same cache state and dirty fraction.
2. Replay every decision independently (a full recomputation is the oracle).
3. Record every incorrectly inherited success and every unnecessary invalidation; an unsafe reuse counts as a
   false accept even if a later amendment happens to restore feasibility.
The campaign is one fixture run once per milestone and read by two gates, each against its own threshold:
revalidation in the studio loop (every decision equals full recomputation; zero unsafe reuse; the stale-key
mutant is caught; unnecessary invalidations at most 20% of unaffected verdicts, counted and reported) and
revalidation of mission cells (zero incorrectly inherited verdicts; unnecessary invalidations at most 20%; on
the cost-9 and cost-5 fixture the witness is kept and the minimum withdrawn). Kill gate named in advance:
reuse at grade 3 is dropped (grades 1 and 2 only) on any unsafe reuse, or if grade 3 saves less than a fifth
of the recheck cost that grade 2 leaves (TARGET).

**3. The seeded cascade population (`registry/populations/cascade-seeded.toml`).** A population generated from
a recorded generator identity and seed, containing planted winners (designs known to be best under a full
evaluation, whose rank is on record) and planted defects of known kind. The cascade is run end to end on it;
the conformance checks are: every stage's control ran; the report prints every mode of every rung, every
fallback and the phase ledger; a planted winner removed by a stage is reported by the screening audit; merging
two agents' archives in both orders gives identical bytes; a stage with no audit record has its rung set to
`uninformative` and its results labelled `screen-biased`.

**4. Binding and measurement.** Each store has an identity in `registry/batteries.toml` style records; each
planted item is an NC record (kinds `planted-design`, `planted-input`, `a-a`). The M4 matrix comes from
`scripts/e2e/gauntlet_controls.sh M4`, recorded by a fresh-context session from the run at the M4 exit candidate
commit: GATE-GAUNT-01 is a recurring gate, and a recurring part is fresh only if its receipt's commit is the exit
candidate's (N40-04), so the exit review re-runs the script there (N40-05 step 4, BD-378). The run cannot be at
threshold earlier: four gates with a part due at M4 are measured on these stores by beads that depend on this one,
and have no tier entry until it is finished (GATE-ATEL-08, N28b-19; GATE-ATEL-09, N28b-31; the M4 part of
GATE-ATEL-11, N28b-35; GATE-ATEL-16, N28b-28). This bead supplies the content the exit run needs and runs the
script once at its own commit, read as the first acceptance criterion says.

**5. The M4 rows of the planted registries and of the certifier table.** At M4 the screening audit lane exists
(§26.10, `nemesis.audit`), so two rows left `not-due` at M3 are checked here: the biased-screen row of the
Nemesis planted index (BD-318 section 2), against the planted screens of N26b-06 by hash and count; and the
audit part of certifier row CERT-10 (N38b-13), by running `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M4` (N38b-17), which reads
the record of GATE-NEMESIS-07 written by N26b-07 and requires N26b-06's mutant `nemesis-audit-mut-post-score-draw`
to be caught in it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: GATE-GAUNT-01 for M4 — every gate with a part due at M4 has a caught control. The
      recorded M4 part is the run of `gauntlet_controls.sh M4` at the exit candidate commit (section 4). This bead
      closes on its own run of the script, in whose matrix `target/gauntlet/controls/M4.json` every (control,
      gate) pair of a gate that has a tier entry at that commit is `caught`, or `not-yet-due` and listed apart
      (none `missed`, none `stale`), and the pairs with no outcome belong only to gates that have no tier entry
      yet, each listed with the bead that measures it; the four gates of section 4 are on that list, and no gate
      measured by one of this bead's dependencies is.
- [ ] Each calibration set is frozen before its first scored run and disjoint from the population it
      calibrates; the three refusals above fire on their planted requests.
- [ ] The 30 amendments of the M4 campaign are sealed under N25a-17's protocol (freeze record with digests, date
      and author predating the engine's first commit that reads them), in the ten categories with the counts
      above; the manifest's freeze hash equals `campaign30.freeze.json`; the stale-key mutant yields at least one
      unsafe reuse and is caught.
- [ ] `certifiers.sh M4` reports CERT-10's audit part equal to N26b-07's record, the post-score-draw mutant
      caught; the Nemesis index's biased-screen row matches N26b-06's manifest.
- [ ] The seeded population regenerates to the same identity from its seed on both machines; each planted
      winner's full-evaluation rank is on record (else FROB-REF-566).
- [ ] The archive merge is byte-identical in both orders.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/m4_content.rs`: `calibration_set_frozen_first` (commit order);
`calibration_disjoint_from_population`; `refuse_calibration_from_survivors` (FROB-REF-546);
`refuse_pooling_across_models` (FROB-REF-548); `rung_without_record_refused` (FROB-REF-537);
`campaign_has_30_amendments_in_10_categories` (counts 3, 2, 5, 3, 3, 4, 2, 2, 4, 2 in N25a-17's order);
`campaign_manifest_matches_freeze` (the manifest's hash equals the freeze record; an amendment edited after the
freeze fails); `campaign_authored_by_non_implementer` (the freeze record's author is in neither implementer set);
`unsafe_reuse_counts_even_if_restored`; `nemesis_audit_row_at_m4`;
`population_identity_from_seed`; `planted_winner_rank_on_record`; `archive_merge_commutes`;
`m4_gates_have_runnable_controls` (a registry check, so it passes before the gates' entries exist: every gate
with a part due at M4 lists at least one control that is not an obligation, and a planted gate row whose only
control is an obligation fails it).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/conformance_m4.sh` (header: `bead=BD-377 milestone=M4 labels=gauntlet,exit gates=GATE-GAUNT-01
plants=stale-key,biased-screen`). It runs `frob result revalidate` over the 30 amendments and compares each
decision with a fresh `frob mission synthesize` or `frob eval run`; runs `frob opt cascade` on the seeded
population and `frob report campaign`; merges two archives in both orders; runs `certifiers.sh M4`; then
`gauntlet_controls.sh M4`, whose exit status it logs without asserting it (that script exits non-zero while a
gate due at M4 has no tier entry) and whose matrix it reads for the first acceptance criterion: an assertion
that no pair is `missed` or `stale`, and one that every pair with no outcome belongs to a gate with no entry.
Plants: a memo key that omits delivery time (must produce an inherited stale verdict that the comparison
catches) and a deliberately biased screen (the planted winner's removal must be reported). Log at
`target/e2e-logs/conformance_m4/<utc>.ndjson`: commands, exit codes, wall time; per amendment the reused and
fresh verdicts and their digests; counts of unsafe reuse (threshold 0) and unnecessary invalidations (threshold
20% of unaffected); archive digests in both orders; the matrix triple; evidence blocks; the `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: with 30 clean audited discards the upper bound on the miss rate is 1 − 0.05^(1/30) = 9.5%;
thresholds 0.7 and 0.5. Negative controls: the stale-key mutant; the biased screen; calibration from
survivors; a set changed after its first run (identity mismatch). Metamorphic relation: an edit followed by its
inverse restores every verdict with zero evaluations. Determinism: identical bundle and archive bytes on a
second machine.

## Considerations

- If the family-policy bet was killed, M4 exits in its variant with specialists and inheritance; the stores
  are unchanged and the rungs that remain are calibrated the same way.
- Campaign runs need a compute class above the default; without approval they are sized to the default with
  fewer briefs and wider intervals, and the reduction is printed.
- Revalidation is an evidence rule and a hygiene measure; it is not counted as a source of speed.
