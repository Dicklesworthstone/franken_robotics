## Background

M5 of FrankenRobotics is the evidence milestone: certificates for derived kernels and Lean theorem capsules,
the first calibration against reality (or a recorded `no-claim`), and a build packet for manufacture. The
verification subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) holds the three stores that judge them and
measures GATE-GAUNT-01 (every passing gate has a caught negative control) for M5:

- *certificate mutation corpora*: for every kind of proof object, valid specimens and a large set of mutated
  ones that the independent checker must reject. "A false certificate is worse than an ordinary wrong answer"
  (FACT, a sibling's agent instructions), so the checker is the first thing the Gauntlet certifies;
- *the synthetic rehearsal*: the calibration machinery tested without real data, on logs produced by a truth
  simulator with planted parameters. Passing it shows a bounded loop is sound and nothing more; every result is
  labelled `synthetic`, is never promoted, and the Reality claim stays `no-claim`;
- *the structure fixtures*: hand-derived cases for structural sizing, fatigue, tolerance and export.

## Scope

In: the corpora at their M5 sizes for every proof kind in use; the rehearsal rows and structure rows as
conformance entries with their controls; the Reality lane's rehearsal check (check 3 of N38b-01, registered
there `not-due`) and step 6 of `scripts/e2e/reality_lane.sh`; the M5 run of `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh` for the
product binding of the conformal-band row CERT-15; the M5 control matrix. Out: the checker (`frob-cert`, binary
`frob-verify`) and its M1 corpus mechanism (N38b-11); the proof-kind registry `frob.cert.kinds/1` (N12-02); the
rows' truths, tolerances and stated controls (N30-KA for KA-721 to KA-734, N31-KA for the structure rows); the
Foundry catalogue entries CX-336 to CX-344 and their late cases (N31-CX, the one registry bead due at M5 that
the M5 matrix needs and no other dependency brings); the calibration and structure mechanisms; the Reality lane's other duties (N38b-01, N38bp1-01) and the dataset
decision (N38b-26).

## Technical approach

**1. Certificate mutation corpora (`crates/frob-conformance/corpora/certs/<kind>/`).** The corpus covers every
kind marked active in the proof-kind registry `frob.cert.kinds/1` (N12-02; 27 kinds at plan time) and in use by
M5; a kind added to the registry without a corpus fails `corpus_covers_every_active_kind`. The kinds in use by
M5 include the M1 kinds (`poly-identity`, `rational-ldlt`, `lp-primal`, `lp-farkas`, `finite-game`,
`alias-pair`, `partition`, `interval-box`, `trace-witness`), then `alpha-root`, `lean-capsule`,
`sample-coverage` and the energy-budget kind. Per kind:
- valid specimens, each accepted, each re-serialized by the checker to identical bytes;
- at least 1,000 mutants (TARGET) generated deterministically from the specimens by seven mutation classes:
  a changed coefficient; reordered quantifiers; a missing side condition; a wrong frame or unit; a fabricated
  kind with consistent hashes; a flag set to true; a boundary opened or closed. Zero may be accepted;
- verdicts from the standalone binary and from the in-daemon call must be identical on the whole corpus; the
  checker's dependency count outside the standard library is zero.
Additional M5 mutants: a qualification campaign replayed by the checker with a reordered index, a changed
request and a reused cluster (each rejected); a Lean capsule containing `sorry`, an axiom outside the
whitelist or `native_decide` (rejected); a reflected Farkas or rational LDLᵀ instance whose witness was
perturbed (rejected by kernel evaluation).

**4. Binding and measurement.** Each control cell is an NC record, the DESIGN controls that N30-KA and N31-KA
register for rows whose plan text states none included (an earlier form of this bead listed three rehearsal rows
as "control owed"; their controls now exist). `scripts/e2e/gauntlet_controls.sh M5` yields the matrix; a fresh
session records it.

**5. The Reality lane at M5 (check 3 of N38b-01; step 6 of `reality_lane.sh`).** The rehearsal check
`rehearsal_is_synthetic`, registered by N38b-01 as `not-due` until M5, is carried here: every output of the
rehearsal rows KA-721 to KA-734 carries `origin = "synthetic"` and the line `reality: synthetic` or `no-claim`;
no gate of kind `reality` cites a rehearsal artifact (planted violation: a gate row of kind `reality` citing a
rehearsal receipt). Step 6 (`synthetic`) of `scripts/e2e/reality_lane.sh` is added to the script's step table
(N38b-02): `frob ground identify` and `frob ground band` on the rehearsal dataset; assert every envelope has
`origin: synthetic` in its evidence and the line does not read `calibrated`; plant `promote-synthetic`, a
request to promote the synthetic comparison, refused with FROB-REF-814 (pooling or promoting synthetic with real
data). A design that was never built receives no band (asserted). If a real dataset is admitted (N38b-26),
step 6 gains a `calibrated` branch for its cell and keeps the `no-claim` assertion everywhere else; the step
logs the decision identifier of OPEN-GAUNT-1 in its `summary`.

**6. The conformal-band certifier at M5.** `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M5` (N38b-17) runs the product binding of
row CERT-15 (N38b-15): `frob ground band` on the rehearsal cell, mean coverage within 0.01 of 19/21 and the
pooled shifted regime flagged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: GATE-GAUNT-01 for M5 — every gate with a part due at M5 has a caught control.
- [ ] Every proof kind in use has at least 1,000 mutants with zero accepted and all specimens accepted and
      byte-stable; standalone and in-daemon verdicts are identical (GATE-GAUNT-04's corpus clause).
- [ ] Every rehearsal row reproduces; every output carries `synthetic`; a planted promotion of a synthetic
      comparison to Reality is refused.
- [ ] Every structure row reproduces at its tolerance and its control fails.
- [ ] Every rehearsal row (KA-721 to KA-734) and every structure row (KA-737 to KA-748 and KA-754) has a caught
      control, the DESIGN controls of N30-KA and N31-KA included; none is counted as an obligation.
- [ ] The corpus covers every active kind of `frob.cert.kinds/1` in use by M5.
- [ ] `rehearsal_is_synthetic` passes and its planted violation is caught; the M5 run of the Reality-lane script
      runs steps 1 to 6 and refuses `promote-synthetic` with FROB-REF-814.
- [ ] The M5 run of the certifier script measures CERT-15's product binding: mean coverage in [0.895, 0.915];
      the pooled shifted regime flagged.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/m5_content.rs`: `corpus_size_per_kind` (≥ 1,000); `seven_mutation_classes_present`;
`corpus_covers_every_active_kind` (against N12-02's registry; a fixture registry with one more active kind and
no corpus fails); `specimens_reserialize_identically`; `standalone_equals_daemon_verdicts`;
`rehearsal_rows_registered` (14: KA-721 to KA-734); `rehearsal_design_controls_caught` (KA-727, KA-729, KA-731
with e = 25, KA-734); `rehearsal_is_synthetic` (with its planted `reality` gate row);
`rehearsal_outputs_are_synthetic`; `synthetic_never_promoted`;
`cantilever_deflection` (1.159 mm; 30 MPa); `fatigue_block_damage` (0.116; 8.62);
`rainflow_counts_and_window_mutant` (8.752 × 10⁻⁶ against 7.816 × 10⁻⁶); `assembled_peaks` (P·L/6 per instant;
P·L/3 or 0); `tube_mass_properties` (0.0950 kg; 1.87 × 10⁻⁵ and 3.26 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m²); `voltage_drop` (0.0688 Ω,
0.688 V, 6.9 W); `cable_slack_and_unit_mutants` (36 mm; 2,063 mm and 0.628 mm fail); `quasi_static_limit`
(25/24 = 1.042; 1.061 and 1.125 fail); `structure_rows_registered` (13); `m5_gates_have_runnable_controls`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/conformance_m5.sh` (header: `bead=BD-411 milestone=M5 labels=gauntlet,exit
gates=GATE-GAUNT-01,GATE-GAUNT-04 plants=accepting-checker,promote-synthetic`). It runs `frob-verify` over a
sample of each corpus, `frob prove verify` on the same bundles in the daemon, `frob ground identify
<rehearsal dataset> --key m5-identify --json` and `frob ground band --cell <rehearsal cell> --json` on the
rehearsal dataset, `frob foundry size` on the cantilever, `reality_lane.sh M5` (with
step 6), `certifiers.sh M5`, then `gauntlet_controls.sh M5`. Plants: a checker build that skips the quantifier-prefix comparison (mutants of that
class must then be accepted and the corpus test must fail) and a request to record a synthetic comparison as
Reality (refused). Log at `target/e2e-logs/conformance_m5/<utc>.ndjson`: commands, exit codes, wall time;
mutants per kind and accepted count (threshold 0); rehearsal and structure values against truths with units;
evidence blocks including `reality: no-claim` or `synthetic`; the matrix triple; the `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

As tabulated. Further negative controls: the accepting checker; the promoted synthetic result; a corpus with a
duplicated mutant counted twice. Determinism: corpus generation is a pure function of specimens and seed;
`frob-verify` verdicts are class D0 on both platforms.

## Considerations

- If no real dataset is admitted, the Reality lane's first record is a recorded `no-claim`; the rehearsal is
  not a substitute and is never labelled as one.
- If the reflection or Lean kill gates fire, their corpora are dropped with the mechanism and the instance
  certificates stay `Certified`.
- Corpus generation at 1,000 mutants per kind is cheap; checking is linear in corpus size.
