## Background

FrankenRobotics distinguishes seven kinds of claim. *Reality* (the simulation agrees with physical measurement)
is the one the first release mostly cannot make: nothing in the program has been compared with a physical
robot, and "more numerical permutations cannot supply those observations" (FACT, a sibling project's record of
the same limit). The lane where real data lands belongs to the calibration subsystem (called Ground): admission
of data, the cell that scopes a result, clock fitting, identifiability, frozen predictions on sealed trials,
conformal bands per calibrated cell, drift retirement and the replication kit. The verification subsystem (the
Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) keeps four duties toward that lane, each a conformance check with a planted
violation. The checks fall due at different milestones (M0, M1, M3, M5); this bead carries the conformance
target and the parts whose mechanisms exist by M1, and the later parts are carried at their own milestones
(split in polishing round 1): the M3 anchor run and the share of guesses by N38bp1-01, the M5 rehearsal check by
BD-411.

1. **The line.** Every report prints `reality: <status> […]`; with no dataset the status is `no-claim`, which is
   a legitimate terminal state, not a failure.
2. **The label.** A comparison with a published figure for the same robot is scheduled by the Gauntlet at M3 and
   recorded as Parity with status `published-anchor`, never as Reality. A comparison with data from a simulator
   standing in for reality is `synthetic` and is never promoted (invariant INV-GROUND-06).
3. **The rehearsal.** The calibration machinery is tested without real data on a synthetic rehearsal; passing it
   shows a bounded loop is sound and nothing more.
4. **The share of guesses.** A report that supports a benchmark claim prints the share of card fields whose
   provenance is `generic`, `assumed` or `synthetic`, because agreement between tiers cannot reveal a wrong card: every tier
   consumes the same card.

A fifth rule closes the lane: no exported artifact carries `sim_to_real_certified = true`; the flag has no
setter. Rejected alternative: treating agreement with a second simulator, or with an incumbent that was itself
compared with hardware, as partial Reality (its agreement with reality is its authors' claim under their
conditions, and agreement with it transfers none of that).

## Scope

In: the conformance target `reality_lane.rs` and its five check names; check 1 (the line) in full; check 2's
type and loader half (a parity record has no Reality constructor; a planted anchor offered as `reality` and a
`synthetic` comparison edited to `calibrated` are refused); check 5 (the flag) in full; the format and loader of
the anchor schedule `registry/anchors.toml`; planted violations as negative controls. Checks 3 and 4 and the run
half of check 2 are registered here as `not-due` entries and pass only through the beads that carry them.

Out, with the bead that has it: the status type and line grammar (N30a-02) and the line on every report with
its 50-misuse battery (BD-139); the flag type (BD-005); the M3 run of the anchor schedule as Parity and check 4,
the share of guessed card fields (N38bp1-01, on the anchor comparison BD-311 and the share computation
N30a-03); check 3, the rehearsal (BD-411, on the rehearsal of BD-388); the first real dataset (N38b-26).

## Technical approach

**Statuses and line (owned by the calibration subsystem; restated because the checks read them).** `no-claim`
(default); `synthetic`; `published-anchor`; `candidate(cell)`; `calibrated(cell, n, level)`; `retired(cell,
reason)`. Line: `reality: <status> [cell=<id> n=<n> level=<1−α> band=<q̂ with unit> dataset=<hash prefix>]`.

**Checks (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/reality_lane.rs`).**
1. `line_on_every_report` (due M1): enumerate the verb registry for verbs of kind report or explanation and
   every verb whose envelope has an `evidence` block; run each on its smallest fixture; assert the envelope's
   `evidence.reality` exists and every rendered report has exactly one `reality:` line; with no admitted
   dataset the value is `no-claim`. Planted violation: a report template with the line removed.
2. `anchor_is_parity`, type and loader half (due M1, here): the record type of a parity result has no
   constructor for another kind; a result record loaded from `registry/anchors.toml` (below) must carry claim
   kind `parity`, status `published-anchor`, its source and stated conditions. Planted violation: a
   hand-written result record that offers the anchor as `reality` (must be refused by the loader); a
   `synthetic` comparison whose status is edited to `calibrated` (must be refused: never promoted). The run
   half (due M3: every scheduled anchor run and its stored result checked) is N38bp1-01's.
3. `rehearsal_is_synthetic` (due M5; registered here `not-due`, carried by BD-411): every output of the
   rehearsal rows carries `origin = "synthetic"` and the line `reality: synthetic` or `no-claim`; no gate of
   kind `reality` cites a rehearsal artifact. Planted violation: a gate row of kind `reality` citing a
   rehearsal receipt.
4. `share_of_guesses_printed` (due M3; registered here `not-due`, carried by N38bp1-01): every report that
   supports a benchmark claim prints, directly under its `reality:` line, the line of N30a-03 (printed by
   BD-139): `cards: guessed=<g>/<N> (<pct>%) [generic=<a> assumed=<b> synthetic=<f> datasheet=<c>
   measured=<d> candidate=<e>]`, with g = a + b + f over every field with a provenance of every card bound
   into the model identity (the nine card kinds: actuator, transmission, battery, interface, material, soil,
   optical, sensor, payload), `<pct>` with one decimal, and the six columns summing to N. Planted violation: a
   benchmark report with the line omitted; a card whose `assumed` field is relabelled `measured` without a
   dataset (the provenance check must refuse it).
5. `no_setter_for_sim_to_real` (due M0): compile-fail tests show the flag type has one inhabitant; every schema
   declares `sim_to_real_certified` as the constant `false`; a reader that meets `true`, or one of our
   artifacts without the field, refuses it (existing refusal FROB-REF-816). Planted violation: an artifact
   edited to `true`, and one with the field removed.

**The M3 schedule (`registry/anchors.toml`; one `[[anchor]]` each).** `id`, `robot` (a reference robot with a
public model), `quantity` (for example a published walking speed, a cost of transport, a payload), `published`
(value, unit, source, date read, conditions as stated by the source), `fixture` (the scenario that reproduces
the stated conditions), `tolerance`, `due = "M3"`, `result` (filled by the comparison). The Gauntlet owns the
schedule and the label; the comparison is run by the calibration subsystem's bead (BD-311). This bead fixes the
format and the loader (a record without source or conditions is rejected); N38bp1-01 enters the M3 records and
checks their results. Optional fields of the comparison bead BD-311 (which depends on this bead):
`robot.model`, `quantity.metric`, `published.spread`, `published.locator`, `read_level`,
`conditions_unstated`, `licence`, `captured_by`. This loader admits them from the start as optional fields and
BD-311 adds their checks at M3; any other unknown field is a load error naming the record (DESIGN: one loader,
so that the schedule has one reader).

**A check not yet due** reports `not-due` with the milestone and the carrying bead, never `pass`; the suite
fails if a `not-due` entry is still `not-due` after its milestone's exit.

**Gate statuses.** A gate of kind `reality` may record the terminal status `no-claim`; the status views print
it as the state, not as a pass.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every report-producing verb prints the line; with no dataset it reads `reality: no-claim`.
- [ ] No stored result with status `published-anchor` or `synthetic` has claim kind `reality`; each planted
      promotion is refused by the loader.
- [ ] `registry/anchors.toml` validates; an anchor without source or conditions is rejected.
- [ ] `sim_to_real_certified` has no setter; both planted artifacts are refused.
- [ ] Checks 3 and 4 and the run half of check 2 report `not-due` with their milestone and carrying bead
      (BD-411, N38bp1-01) and are never reported as passing.
- [ ] Each of the checks carried here has a registered negative control that it catches; the controls of the
      later checks are registered here with their due milestone.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In `reality_lane.rs`, on real verbs and fixtures: checks 1, 2 (type and loader half) and 5 above, plus
- `line_grammar`: `reality: no-claim` and `reality: calibrated cell=c1 n=20 level=0.90 band=0.031 m
  dataset=9f2c` parse; `reality: verified` does not.
- `anchor_record_requires_source`: an anchor without source or conditions is rejected.
- `anchor_optional_fields_admitted`: a record carrying BD-311's eight optional fields loads; a record with a
  field outside the schema (`verified = true`) is refused naming the record.
- `parity_type_has_no_reality_constructor` (compile-fail).
- `planted_anchor_as_reality_refused`; `synthetic_edited_to_calibrated_refused`.
- `later_checks_not_due`: before M3 the entries of checks 2 (run half) and 4, and before M5 check 3, report
  `not-due` naming N38bp1-01 or BD-411; a fixture registry past M3 with check 4 still `not-due` fails the suite.
The share arithmetic test (`share_arithmetic`, `guessed=14/20 (70.0%)`) moves with check 4 to N38bp1-01.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reality_lane.sh` (bead N38b-02, steps 1 to 3 at M1; extended by N38bp1-01 at M3 and BD-411 at M5)
drives the checks from the user's side and logs them.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the line for an empty lane; the grammar examples. Negative controls: the planted violations of
checks 1, 2 and 5 (the removed line; the anchor offered as reality; the promoted synthetic comparison; the
artifact edited to `true`; the artifact without the field); the negative battery of 50 planted misuses of the
reality line owned by the line's own gate (BD-139; all refused). Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- Which real-robot dataset, if any, is admitted first is an owner decision (N38b-26); the fail-closed default
  is `reality: no-claim` everywhere, and a recorded `no-claim` satisfies the program's definition of done.
- Published test conditions are loose; an anchor comparison is reported with the conditions as the source
  states them and with nothing inferred.
- Through M4 the card catalogue holds generic classes only, so the printed share will be high; that is the
  honest number.
