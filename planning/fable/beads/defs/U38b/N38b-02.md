## Background

FrankenRobotics prints one line on every report, `reality: <status> […]`, stating whether anything measured on
a physical robot supports the result. With no admitted dataset the line reads `reality: no-claim`. The
verification subsystem (the Gauntlet) has four duties toward the Reality lane: the line is present; a
comparison with a published figure is labelled Parity (`published-anchor`) and a comparison with a stand-in
simulator is labelled `synthetic` and never promoted; the calibration machinery is rehearsed on synthetic data
only; and reports that support a benchmark claim print the share of card fields that were guessed (`generic`,
`assumed` or `synthetic` provenance). In addition no exported artifact may carry `sim_to_real_certified = true`.

This bead is the end-to-end script that shows these behaviours from the user's side, with planted misuses. It
creates the script at M1 with the three steps whose mechanisms exist at M1; the later steps are added to the same
script at their own milestones (split in polishing round 1, because an M1 bead cannot depend on the M3 and M5
mechanisms): steps 4 and 5 (anchor, share) by N38bp1-01 at M3, step 6 (synthetic rehearsal) by BD-411 at M5.

## Scope

In: the script `scripts/e2e/reality_lane.sh` with its milestone argument and step table, steps 1 to 3, their
fixtures and the log format. Out: the checks' unit tests (N38b-01); steps 4 and 5 (N38bp1-01); step 6 (BD-411);
the mechanisms (BD-139 for the line; BD-024 for `sim.run`, BD-112 for `explain.run`, N29a-02 for
`run.export`, N29a-03 for the offline auditor `frob-verify`).

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/reality_lane.sh <M1|M3|M5>` (header: `bead=N38b-02 milestone=M1 labels=gauntlet,ground
gates=GATE-GROUND-01 plants=strip-line,flag-true,flag-removed`), seed 0, scratch store. These are the three
plants run at M1; N38bp1-01 adds its anchor plant and BD-411 adds `promote-synthetic` when they fill their steps.

Steps.
1. `line` (M1): `frob sim run g1 --tier referee --seconds 2 --seed 0 --key rl-run-1 --json` then
   `frob explain run <run> --json`;
   assert `evidence.reality == "no-claim"` in both envelopes and exactly one line `reality: no-claim` in the
   rendered explanation. Repeat for every verb the registry marks as producing a report.
2. `flag` (M1): `frob run export <run> --out <scratch>/exports/ --key rx-1 --json` (the registry form of the
   export verb); assert `authorization` holds the three no-authorization fields `execution_authorized`,
   `hardware_safety_signoff` and `sim_to_real_certified`, all `false`.
   Plant: edit the exported bundle's `sim_to_real_certified` to `true` and read it back with the offline
   auditor, `frob-verify <scratch>/exports/<bundle>.frun --json` (N29a-03, the reader a user has for an
   exported bundle at M1); expect exit 2 naming the failed check `authorization-flag`. Plant: remove the field;
   expect the same (absence is not read as false). The in-process reader's refusal of the same two bundles,
   FROB-REF-816, is check 5 of N38b-01.
3. `strip-line` (M1): render a report with a test template that omits the line; expect the report generator's
   guard to fail and the conformance check `line_on_every_report` to exit non-zero.
4. `anchor` (M3) and 5. `share` (M3): slots in the step table, filled by N38bp1-01.
6. `synthetic` (M5): slot in the step table, filled by BD-411.
7. `end`: summary.

A step whose milestone is later than the argument, or whose carrying bead has not yet landed, is logged
`not-due` with its milestone and carrying bead and is never counted as passed. The plant list of the header
grows with the steps (`promote-synthetic` arrives with step 6); a plant whose step is `not-due` is not listed.

**Logging.** `target/e2e-logs/reality_lane/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. `cmd` events: exact command, exit
code, wall time. `assert` events: the reality value of each envelope; the count of reports checked and of lines
found (threshold: equal); the share as printed against the share as recomputed. `refusal` events: the code
(or, for `frob-verify`, the failed check) and message for each plant. The evidence block of each envelope (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed,
reality). `summary`: assertions passed and failed; GATE-GROUND-01. The log never contains scene data from a
private place; the fixtures are public reference robots and generated scenes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At M1, steps 1 to 3 pass and all three plants (stripped line, flag set to true, flag removed) are refused
      or caught.
- [ ] The export of step 2 uses the registry form of the verb and names the three flags it checks.
- [ ] Steps 4 to 6 are logged `not-due` with their milestone and carrying bead (N38bp1-01, BD-411), never as
      passed; adding a step is a row of the step table, not a second script.
- [ ] The script is idempotent and leaves the repository unchanged.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The checks are N38b-01's. The harness self-test adds `reality_lane_step_table`: the script's step table lists
six steps with milestones M1, M1, M1, M3, M3, M5 and carrying beads N38b-02, N38bp1-01, BD-411; with the argument
`M1` steps 4 to 6 are `not-due`; a planted step table that marks step 6 passed without running it is flagged.

### End-to-end script and logging

As above; selected by `scripts/e2e/run-all.sh --label ground --milestone <M>` and at each milestone exit,
because the reality line's gate is re-run at every exit after its first.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: with no dataset, every line reads `reality: no-claim`. Negative controls: the stripped line; the
flag set to true; the flag removed (the anchor offered as Reality is N38bp1-01's plant; the promoted synthetic
comparison is BD-411's). Determinism: outcomes identical twice on one machine and on both platforms.

## Considerations

- If a real dataset is admitted, step 6 (BD-411) gains a `calibrated` branch for its cell and keeps the
  `no-claim` assertion everywhere else.
- A design that was never built never receives a band; step 6 asserts the refusal when the band verb exists.
