## Background

"A false certificate is worse than an ordinary wrong answer" (FACT, the agent instructions of the sibling
simulation library). The first duty of FrankenRobotics' verification subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate
`frob-conformance`) is therefore to the things that issue certificates and verdicts: the certificate checker,
the qualification lane, the solvability oracle, the rank-agreement ledger, the requirement monitors and the
statistics. A *certifier* here is any component whose output other components trust as a verdict. Each one gets
a self-test in tier G0 (laws and self-tests), and the self-tests together are gate GATE-GAUNT-04 (claim kind:
known answer; TARGET: every certifier row meets its threshold, and the certificate mutation corpus holds at
least 1,000 mutants per proof kind with zero accepted; due at M1 for the checker and at M3 for the lanes).

DESIGN: a self-test has two halves. The certifier must give the right verdict on inputs whose truth is known,
and a deliberately *broken certifier* (a planted variant that accepts what it should reject) must fail the same
self-test. Reason: a self-test that a broken certifier passes certifies nothing. Rejected: trusting a
certifier because its unit tests pass (those are written by its implementer against its own understanding).

This bead creates the register and the rule, and carries the four self-tests inherited from the earlier design.

## Scope

In: the certifier register; the two-halves rule as a meta-test; the four carried self-tests. Out: the fifteen
added certifier rows (N38b-11 to N38b-16, and N38bp1-03 for the rare-event bounds, whose estimators land at M3);
the end-to-end measurement (N38bp1-02 at M1, which creates `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh` with the eight M1 rows;
N38b-17 from M3); the certifiers themselves.

## Technical approach

**Register (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/certifiers.toml`; one `[[certifier]]` each).** Fields: `id`
(`CERT-01` …, append-only), `name`, `owner_crate`, `owner_gates` (the owning subsystem's gates), `test` (the G0
entry identifier), `threshold` (text and machine-checkable numbers), `broken` (the NC identifier of the planted
broken certifier), `due` (M1 or M3; a row whose binding to its product lands later lists both milestones, the
arithmetic part first: CERT-10 screening audits M3 and M4, CERT-15 conformal bands M3 and M5), `site`.

**Meta-test (`tests/certifier_register.rs`).** Every record has a G0 entry, a threshold with numbers, and a
`broken` control of kind `mutation` or `a-a` that exists in the control registry and names GATE-GAUNT-04; a
record without one makes the gate `not-eligible`.

**The nineteen rows.** Four carried (below) and fifteen added: certificate checker; checker before an answer;
qualification lane; solvability oracle; rank-agreement ledger; screening audits; requirement monitors; fixed-n
pre-analysis; rare-event bounds; attribution; conformal bands; claim product; determinism classifier; energy
audit; the Gauntlet's own harness.

**Carried self-tests.**

| Id | Certifier | Self-test | Threshold | Broken certifier that must fail it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CERT-01 | Certificate verifier | Mutated certificates are rejected | Every mutant rejected, every specimen accepted (detail and corpus: N38b-11) | A verifier that skips the quantifier-prefix comparison |
| CERT-02 | Dimension checker of kernel programs | Seeded dimension violations are rejected | 100% of a seeded set rejected: a length added to a time; a force assigned where a torque is declared; a millimetre literal in a metre field; a missing power of the time step; every clean kernel accepted | A checker that treats all quantities as dimensionless when one operand is a literal |
| CERT-03 | The e-process of an anytime-valid study | Error rate under the null with optional stopping | Over 10⁴ null campaigns with an adversary that stops at the first crossing, the number of false rejections at α = 0.05 is at most 551 (an exact one-sided binomial test of a rate at or below 0.05 rejects at the 1% level from 552) | A fixed-n test peeked at: the exact one-sided sign test at nominal 5%, looked at after every pair of 64 and stopped at its first rejection. Its false-rejection chance is exactly 13/256 = 5.08% after 8 looks, 8.76% after 16, 12.9% after 32 and 17.4% after 64, so it rejects in about 1,742 of 10⁴ campaigns |
| CERT-04 | Physical-consistency test of inertias | A matrix one eigenvalue below zero is rejected | A 4×4 pseudo-inertia [[Σ, h], [hᵀ, m]] (Σ the second moment of mass, h the first moment, m the mass) with smallest eigenvalue −10⁻⁹ is rejected and the eigenvalue is reported; the same matrix shifted to +10⁻⁹ is accepted; principal moments (1, 1, 2.1) violating the triangle inequality are rejected | A test that checks only the mass and the diagonal of Σ for positivity |

**CERT-03 procedure.** (1) Draw paired outcomes with no true difference, keyed by address, so runs are
reproducible. (2) Feed the e-process one pair at a time up to its cap. (3) The adversary reads the e-value
after every pair and stops at the first time it reaches 1/α. (4) Count campaigns that rejected. (5) Repeat with
the broken certifier in the e-process's place, on the same draws and under the same adversary, with 64 pairs per
campaign: its count must exceed 551, and lies within 1,742 ± 190 (the rate 0.17418; five standard deviations of
37.9), which shows the harness can see a broken procedure. Eight looks would not show it: at 13/256 the expected
count is 508 and stays at or below 551 in 97.6% of runs, so a control with eight looks would pass the threshold
it is meant to fail. The eight-look value is therefore checked exactly, by enumerating the 256 sign sequences of
eight pairs, of which 13 reject at some look.

**CERT-04 procedure.** Construct the matrices in exact rationals from a chosen eigenbasis, evaluate the check in
double precision and by exact rational LDLᵀ, and require both to agree on the verdict.

**Evidence.** Class `Tested`, kind known answer, tier G0, site `host`; determinism D0 for CERT-02 and CERT-04,
D0 given the seed for CERT-03.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The register holds nineteen records, each with a G0 entry, a numeric threshold and a `broken` control.
- [ ] Each carried self-test passes on the real certifier and fails on its broken variant.
- [ ] CERT-03 reports the count of false rejections and the threshold 551 of 10⁴; the peeking control's count
      at 64 looks is above 551 and is reported against its expected 1,742; the eight-look chance is exactly
      13/256 by enumeration.
- [ ] CERT-04 reports the smallest eigenvalue it rejected and agrees between double precision and exact LDLᵀ.
- [ ] A register record with no `broken` control makes GATE-GAUNT-04 `not-eligible`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/g0_laws.rs` entries and `tests/certifier_register.rs`:
- `cert_register_complete`: nineteen records; none without a control.
- `cert_02_seeded_violations_rejected`: four seeded kernels rejected, four clean kernels accepted.
- `cert_02_broken_checker_fails`: under the planted checker at least one seeded kernel is accepted.
- `cert_03_null_error_rate`: 10⁴ campaigns, seed 0; false rejections ≤ 551.
- `cert_03_peeking_control`: enumerating the 256 sign sequences of eight pairs gives 13 that reject at some look
  (13/256 = 0.0508, exact); the broken certifier at 64 looks, 10⁴ campaigns, seed 0, gives a count in
  [1,552, 1,932], which is above 551, so the self-test fails on it.
- `cert_04_negative_eigenvalue_rejected`: λ_min = −10⁻⁹ rejected; +10⁻⁹ accepted; (1, 1, 2.1) rejected.
- `cert_04_broken_test_fails`: the diagonal-only test accepts the negative-eigenvalue matrix.
- `threshold_552_is_exact`: P(Binomial(10⁴, 0.05) ≥ 552) ≤ 0.01 < P(≥ 551), computed in the test.

### End-to-end script and logging

Measured at M1 by `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M1` (bead N38bp1-02, which drives `frob spark check`, `frob study
submit` and `frob design check` for CERT-02 to CERT-04) and re-measured at every later exit through the same
script (extended by N38b-17). This bead's rows log, per certifier, the measured count or value, its threshold
and the outcome of the broken-certifier control.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 552 as the first rejecting count at 10⁴ campaigns (P(X ≥ 552) = 0.00983, P(X ≥ 551) = 0.01106
for X ~ Binomial(10⁴, 0.05)); 13/256 for eight looks, exact, and 0.17418 for 64; the verdicts of CERT-04's three
matrices. Negative controls: the four broken certifiers. Determinism lanes: `twice` and
`platforms`, class D0.

## Considerations

- CERT-03 at 10⁴ campaigns fits the default machine envelope on the host; a larger count names its budget.
- A broken certifier is a mutation branch in the owning crate under the `gauntlet-mutations` feature; it cannot
  be compiled into a product build.
- The physical-consistency refusal in the product is the existing code for an inconsistent inertia
  (FROB-REF-104, which reports the smallest eigenvalue).
