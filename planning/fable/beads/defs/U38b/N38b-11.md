## Background

FrankenRobotics answers some design questions with *certificates*: proof objects shipped in a `.frobcert`
bundle and re-checked by an independent offline auditor, the binary `frob-verify` built from the crate
`frob-cert`, which depends on the Rust standard library and nothing else. A producer never calls the auditor's
decision code to construct a witness, so the two share a format and nothing else. Because "a false certificate
is worse than an ordinary wrong answer" (FACT, a sibling's agent instructions), the verification subsystem (the
Gauntlet) certifies this certifier first. Two rows of its certifier table are carried here:

1. *The certificate checker* (register row CERT-05; the carried row CERT-01 of N38b-10, "mutated certificates
   are rejected", is measured on the same corpus): a mutation corpus per proof kind. Threshold (TARGET): every mutant rejected and
   every specimen accepted, with identical bytes on re-serialization; standalone and in-daemon verdicts
   identical on the whole corpus; dependency count zero. GATE-GAUNT-04 adds: at least 1,000 mutants per proof
   kind with zero accepted. Due at M1.
2. *The checker before an answer* (register row CERT-06): the verb layer runs the checker before a mission answer returns; when a
   producer emits a false bundle because of a planted lowering defect, the answer is withheld and the cell is
   downgraded to `Tested` or left unresolved.

Rejected alternatives: sampling a few hand-written mutants per kind (too few to exercise field interactions);
letting the producer's own test suite stand in for the corpus (shared understanding, shared errors).

## Scope

In: the Gauntlet's run of the certificate mutation corpus for GATE-GAUNT-04: at least 1,000 confirmed-false
mutants per proof kind with at least 50 per applicable class of the seven below, zero accepted; the named
mutants that must be present; the corpus checks on that run; the checker-before-answer test with its planted
defect; the two broken-certifier controls; the gate record.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- The mutation generator (the operators per kind, their classes, the generic operators, hash recomputation,
  the address-keyed draws, the labelling oracle that confirms a mutant is false, the stop rule) is BD-136, the
  seed bead of the mission slice, which runs it at 200 per kind for GATE-AUGUR-06 together with the 65 fixed
  mission cases of GATE-AUGUR-01 and the checker-independence checks. There is one generator: this bead depends
  on BD-136 and calls it with `per_kind = 1000` and `min_per_class = 50`; it adds no operator of its own, and
  a class that lacks an operator for a kind is repaired in BD-136's operator table.
- The accepted specimens and the hand-pinned rejecting mutations per kind
  (`crates/frob-cert/tests/specimens/<kind>/`, owned by the checker beads) and the end-to-end run over them
  for GATE-LEMMA-03, with the build-alone, empty-environment, re-serialization and verdict-agreement steps, are
  N12-34. This bead keeps no specimen set of its own: its bases are those pinned `accept-*` files, and its
  script runs N12-34's step functions over the generated corpus. It depends on N12-34.
- The checker and the bundle writer (BD-132, BD-027); the verb layer that runs the checker (BD-133); the M5
  growth of the corpus to later proof kinds (BD-411); the repetition of the independence campaign at M3
  (BD-294).

## Technical approach

**Corpus layout.** The accepted specimens are the pinned files
`crates/frob-cert/tests/specimens/<kind>/accept-*.frobcert` (one set, shared with N12-34 and with BD-136's
generator; an earlier sketch of a second specimen directory under `crates/frob-conformance/corpora/certs/` is
not built, DESIGN: one accepted specimen per kind must not exist in two places that can drift). This bead
commits only `crates/frob-conformance/corpora/certs/<kind>/mutants.manifest` (seed, class counts, generator
version, and the hash of each mutant's canonical bytes for replay). Mutants are generated on demand from
specimens and seed by BD-136's generator, not committed, so that 1,000 per kind costs no repository space.

**Seven mutation classes (the coverage requirement of this gate; each applies wherever the kind has the
field; the operators that realise them are BD-136's, tagged `coefficient`, `quantifier`, `side-condition`,
`frame-unit`, `kind`, `flag`, `boundary`).** Refusal codes in the table are those the auditor reports on
`frob-verify` and `prove.verify`; a mission verb reports the same rejection under its own code, by the mapping
of the verb layer (BD-133), given in brackets where it differs.

| Class | What changes | Example that must be rejected |
|---|---|---|
| Changed coefficient | One rational coefficient, multiplier or bound perturbed | The dual witness of infeasibility at half-width b = 1/5 m with one multiplier perturbed |
| Reordered quantifiers | The quantifier prefix or its domain | The docking cell (U, ε) = (¾, ¼) offered with "for every world there is a command" in place of "one command for every world" (refusal FROB-REF-826) [mission verb: FROB-REF-881] |
| Missing side condition | A side condition of a proof rule removed | An energy budget without its gravity term over the stroke (FROB-REF-829) |
| Wrong frame or unit | A frame, origin, sign, port order or unit | (60, 30) N·m passed as the support torque where (30, −60, 0) is required; a swapped foot order with unchanged forces (FROB-REF-829) [mission verb: FROB-REF-850] |
| Fabricated kind | An unknown proof kind with consistent hashes | Rejected with FROB-REF-823 although every hash recomputes [mission verb: FROB-REF-848] |
| A flag set to true | Any of `execution_authorized`, `hardware_safety_signoff`, `sim_to_real_certified` | Rejected with FROB-REF-833; a missing flag is rejected too |
| Boundary opened or closed | A strict inequality made non-strict or the reverse; an interval end moved by one unit in the last place | A cell boundary point admitted by a closed bound that must be open |

Named mutants that must be present: the four of the stance specimen (swapped foot order with unchanged forces;
an unreported positive force floor; a passive-authority tag; (60, 30) passed as the torque), which are the
pinned files `reject-lp-primal.port-order`, `reject-side.force-floor`, `reject-field.authority` and
`reject-field.type-torque` that N12-34 asserts by name and BD-136 lists as cases A1 to A4 (one file each, not
copied here); a rewrite trace with a reassociation filed as a bit-preserving rewrite; one changed coefficient in
the Cayley-kernel identity. The last two are not in either of those lists: this bead adds them as pinned
`reject-*` files in the specimen directories of their kinds and asserts them by name.

**Generation (BD-136's generator, called with this gate's parameters).** `per_kind = 1000`,
`min_per_class = 50`, a seed recorded in `mutants.manifest`. What the generator guarantees, restated so that
the checks below can be read: applicable fields are enumerated in canonical order per specimen and class; the
field and the perturbation are drawn from the address-keyed generator (seed, kind, class, index); every mutant
is re-serialized canonically with every hash recomputed, so that none fails on a stale hash; falsity is
confirmed independently by the second checker out of process, and a mutant that is still true (an equivalent
mutant) is dropped and counted as `equivalent`; the run stops at 1,000 confirmed-false mutants per kind with at
least 50 per applicable class, and reports a kind that cannot reach them. Because draws are address-keyed, the
first 200 mutants of this run are the mutants of BD-136's campaign for the same seed.

**Corpus checks.** (a) `frob-verify` returns `accept` for every specimen and `reject`, naming the first failing
rule, for every mutant; `unsupported` counts as a failure of the corpus for a kind in use. (b) Each specimen
re-serialized by the checker gives identical bytes. (c) The same corpus through the in-daemon call
(`prove.verify`) gives verdict-for-verdict equality with the standalone binary. (d) `cargo tree -p frob-cert`
lists no dependency. Checks (b), (c) and (d), the build of the auditor alone and its run in an empty
environment are the step functions of N12-34's script (which applies them to the pinned corpus for
GATE-LEMMA-03); this bead's script calls the same functions with the generated corpus directory and its
manifest, and implements none of them a second time.

**Checker before an answer.** Fixture: the stance family query with a planted lowering defect in the producer
(the force floor dropped when the query is lowered to a linear program), which makes the producer emit an
inclusion bundle for a cell that is false. Expected through the public verb `mission.synthesize`: the checker
rejects the bundle, the answer for that cell is withheld, and the cell is returned as `Tested` (if sampling
evidence exists) or in the unresolved remainder; never as `Certified`.

**Broken certifiers (controls of GATE-GAUNT-04).** (i) A checker build that skips the quantifier-prefix
comparison: mutants of class 2 are accepted and check (a) fails. (ii) A verb-layer build that returns the
producer's bundle without running the checker: the false cell is returned `Certified` and the test fails.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least 1,000 confirmed-false mutants per proof kind in use at M1, at least 50 per applicable class,
      zero accepted; every specimen accepted and byte-stable.
- [ ] The named mutants are each rejected with the stated refusal.
- [ ] Standalone and in-daemon verdicts are identical on the whole corpus.
- [ ] The dependency count of the checker outside the standard library is zero.
- [ ] With the planted lowering defect, no false cell is returned `Certified`.
- [ ] Both broken certifiers fail the self-test.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cert_corpus.rs`:
- `stance_specimen_accepts`: the bundle at b = 3/14 m (forces (160, 130, 110, 0) N) is accepted.
- `stance_four_mutants_reject`; `dual_witness_perturbed_rejects` (b = 1/5 m, f₊₊ − f₋₋ = 165 N against a 160 N
  cap); `docking_quantifiers_reordered_rejects`; `unknown_kind_rejects`; `flag_true_rejects`; `flag_missing_rejects`.
- `corpus_counts`: ≥ 1,000 per kind, ≥ 50 per class, `equivalent` reported.
- `mutants_have_consistent_hashes`: no mutant is rejected for a hash mismatch.
- `reserialize_identity`; `standalone_equals_daemon`; `checker_has_no_dependencies`.
- `answer_withheld_on_false_bundle`; `broken_checker_fails_corpus`; `unchecked_answer_fails`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/cert_corpus.sh` (header: `bead=N38b-11 milestone=M1 labels=gauntlet,lemma gates=GATE-GAUNT-04
plants=skip-quantifiers,skip-checker`). It builds `frob-verify` in an empty environment, runs it over the
corpus, runs `frob prove verify` over the same bundles, runs `frob mission synthesize` on the stance fixture
with the planted defect, then both plants. Log at `target/e2e-logs/cert_corpus/<utc>.ndjson`: commands, exit
codes, wall time; per kind the mutants generated, equivalent, rejected and accepted (threshold 0); verdict
digests of both paths; the refusal code of each named mutant; the evidence block of the mission envelope
(class must not be `Certified` for the false cell); the `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: stance half-width 3/14 m; the 165 N dual; the docking cell (¾, ¼). Negative controls: the two
broken certifiers; a mutant with a stale hash (must be excluded by the generator, checked by a planted one).
Determinism: corpus bytes are a pure function of specimens, seed and generator version; verdicts are class D0.

## Considerations

- One accepted false certificate fails the owning gate and, from M1 on, removes its proof kind if the rule is
  at fault; the false bundle becomes a permanent control.
- The second checker confirms falsity only; it is never linked into the auditor and never decides acceptance.
- Corpus size per kind is a TARGET; the generator reports when a kind has too few applicable fields to reach it.
