## Background

Two components of FrankenRobotics issue verdicts that later work treats as settled, and the verification
subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) self-tests each as a *certifier* in tier G0 under gate
GATE-GAUNT-04 (due at M3 for these lanes).

*The qualification lane.* A design or policy is "qualified" only by a confirmatory test on sealed world
clusters that discovery work cannot read. An agent may resubmit after every rejection, so the level spent by
request i is α_i = α/[i·(i + 1)]; the levels of the first m requests sum to α·m/(m + 1), below α for every m.
Each request is committed before it is evaluated, uses fresh clusters, and returns a receipt carrying only the
decision, the index, the level spent, the units used and a hash (never which worlds failed: an agent that
learned them could patch for those worlds and resubmit, and the schedule would then bound the error of a
procedure no longer being run).

*The solvability oracle.* The adversarial search may report a world as a counterexample only if the task is
solvable there by some admissible controller; a world that no robot of that body could pass is not a failure
of the design. The oracle classifies planted worlds, and the legality set refuses edits outside declared
bounds.

## Scope

In: the two rows of the certifier register, CERT-07 and CERT-08, with their thresholds and their
broken-certifier controls, each bound to the run that measures it; for CERT-07 the binding to the gate run of
the qualification lane and to the broken-lane run, and one control this bead runs itself (the leaking build);
for CERT-08 the planted trap sets as inputs and the oracle's self-test in full.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- for CERT-07, the instruments and the run. The no-signal and planted-gain world families, the scripted
  adversary with its full strategy set, the campaign driver under the daemon's simulation runtime and the
  broken lane (the mutant `reuse-clusters-constant-level`): N24a-34, on the instrument of N24b-18. The run of
  2,000 campaigns of 500 adaptive retries with its statistic, the power arm and the truncation replay: BD-260,
  the bead of GATE-QUAL-01. This bead depends on both and runs none of those campaigns a second time. It
  first specified an adversary script of its own (resubmit unchanged; the best of ten variants scored on
  discovery worlds; the metric's tie-break altered); that script is withdrawn, and the two strategies the full
  set lacked are now rows of the strategy table of N24a-34;
- the lane and the oracle themselves; the planted trap sets and edit sets themselves (N26a-04, indexed at M3 by
  BD-318); replay of a campaign by the offline auditor (M5).

## Technical approach

**CERT-07 Qualification lane (register row; G0 entry `cert:qualification@host`, simulated campaigns).** The row
states the self-test and its thresholds; each value is read from the record of the run that measured it, and
the row is `not run` until that record exists.

| Clause of the row | Threshold (TARGET) | Measured by |
|---|---|---|
| No-signal campaigns: 2,000 campaigns at α = 0.05, up to 500 requests each, request i tested at level α_i = 0.05/[i·(i + 1)] on fresh sealed clusters, against the scripted adversary that sees every receipt and all discovery data | A false promotion in at most 123 campaigns (an exact one-sided binomial test of a rate at or below 0.05 rejects at the 1% level from 124) | The record of GATE-QUAL-01 (BD-260), run with the adversary and driver of N24a-34 |
| Power: a planted true gain of the declared size | Promotion within 10 requests in at least 80% of campaigns | The same record, its planted-gain arm |
| Order independence: the decision of request i does not depend on requests after it | Recomputing the first k decisions from a truncated campaign gives identical receipts | The same record, its truncation replay (metamorphic relation MR-13) |
| Broken certifier (control): the same adversary against reused clusters at a constant level α | A false promotion in more than 99% of campaigns on the absolute no-signal world `absolute-above` (its exact per-request size at level 0.05 is 1.319 × 10⁻², n = 200 with at most 4 failures, N24b-18; with 500 retries 1 − (1 − 0.01319)⁵⁰⁰ = 0.9987), so the self-test bound to that lane reports FAIL | The broken-lane run of N24a-34 (2,000 campaigns), as BD-260's arm C |
| Second control: a test build of the lane that leaks per-world outcomes in its receipts | The adversary, given the leaked outcomes, exceeds 123 false promotions | This bead, with the adversary and driver of N24a-34 |

The adversary of every clause is the one N24a-34 specifies (the three statistical strategies of the
instrument, the two carried over from this bead's first script, and the six protocol attacks); this bead
defines none.

**CERT-08 Solvability oracle (G0 entry `cert:solvability@referee`).** Inputs: 100 planted illegal edits, 100
planted impossible traps (for example a doorway narrower than the body), 100 planted solvable traps of known
nominal probability. Thresholds (TARGET): 100 of 100 illegal edits refused with the violated bound named; 0 of
100 impossible traps counted as counterexamples (each labelled infeasible for the candidate, with the
bottleneck); at least 95 of 100 solvable traps receive a witness. Broken certifier (control): an oracle that
checks a point robot calls the narrow doorway solvable and is caught on that fixture; a legality profile with
the bound removed admits the illegal edits.

**Firewall clause used by CERT-07.** 10⁴ fuzzed read attempts against a sealed campaign return no sealed field
(existing refusal FROB-REF-840), and a planted leak path is found by the audit; a test build that leaks
per-world outcomes lets the adversary exceed the 123 threshold, which is how a leak would show.

**Evidence.** Class `Tested`, kind known answer; the campaigns are `synthetic`; determinism D0 given the seed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] CERT-07: the row's three measured clauses equal the record of GATE-QUAL-01 value for value (at most 123
      false promotions in 2,000 no-signal campaigns of 500 adaptive retries; at least 80% of planted-gain
      campaigns promoted within 10 requests; request i unchanged by later requests), and the row is `not run`
      while that record is missing.
- [ ] The reused-cluster control gives more than 99% false promotions in the broken-lane run of N24a-34, and
      the self-test bound to that lane reports FAIL.
- [ ] The leaking build lets the adversary exceed 123 false promotions.
- [ ] CERT-08: 100/100, 0/100 and at least 95/100 on the planted sets; the point-robot oracle is caught.
- [ ] Receipts contain only decision, index, level spent, units used, hash and the no-authorization flags
      (the field list of N24a-34).
- [ ] Both rows are in the certifier register with their controls and are read by GATE-GAUNT-04.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cert_lanes.rs`:
- The campaigns themselves and their arithmetic are tested where they are run: the level series
  (`level_series_exact`), the determinism of the adversary and the broken lane in N24a-34; the threshold 124,
  the no-signal count, the power arm and the truncation replay in BD-260. Here:
- `cert07_row_equals_gate_record`: with a fixture gate record (118 false promotions, power 0.86, replay
  equal) the row reads the same three values and `pass`; with the record absent it reads `not run`.
- `cert07_threshold_restated`: the row's threshold 123 equals the smallest F with
  P(Binomial(2,000, 0.05) ≥ F + 1) ≤ 0.01, computed in exact rational arithmetic (P(X ≥ 124) = 0.00953,
  P(X ≥ 123) = 0.01228), recomputed here so that a changed gate threshold cannot go unseen. (Every F above 123
  also satisfies the inequality, so "largest" would give 2,000.)
- `cert07_bound_to_broken_lane_fails`: bound to the broken-lane run (more than 99% false promotions) the
  self-test reports FAIL.
- `leaking_build_exceeds_123`: the leaking test build against the adversary of N24a-34.
- `receipt_fields_closed`: a receipt with a per-world field is rejected by the schema.
- `oracle_planted_sets`: 100/100 refused, 0/100 counted, ≥ 95/100 witnessed.
- `point_robot_oracle_caught`: the doorway narrower than the body is called solvable by the planted oracle.

### End-to-end script and logging

Measured by `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M3` (bead N38b-17), which for CERT-07 reads the record of GATE-QUAL-01
and the broken-lane run (it starts no campaign of its own beyond the leaking-build control, and a short
liveness check of `frob eval qualify` and `frob eval ledger` on a sealed synthetic family), and for CERT-08
calls `frob nemesis oracle` on the planted sets. Logged values: false promotions of
2,000 (threshold 123), planted-gain share (threshold 0.80), control share (threshold above 0.99), the three
oracle counts, refusal events with codes.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 124 as the first rejecting count; the level series; 1 − (1 − 0.01319)⁵⁰⁰ = 0.9987 on
`absolute-above`. Negative controls: reused
clusters at a constant level; a leaking build; the point-robot oracle; the bound-removed legality profile.
Metamorphic relation: the decision of request i is unchanged by later requests (MR-13). Determinism: identical
counts twice on one machine for a fixed seed.

## Considerations

- 2,000 × 500 simulated requests are cheap when campaigns are simulated at the level of test statistics; when
  run through the real verb on real clusters the count needs a compute class above the default and is run
  nightly at a reduced, printed count. That is a property of the gate run (BD-260), which this row reads; the
  row prints the count the record was measured at.
- Who holds the administrative scope of the lane and how large sealed families are is an owner decision
  (default: the owner alone opens and closes campaigns; one sealed family per task family, sized for 20
  requests at 256 clusters). Until a family exists nothing is called qualified.
