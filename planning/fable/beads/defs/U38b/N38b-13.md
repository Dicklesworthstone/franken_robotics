## Background

FrankenRobotics screens many robot designs with cheap evaluation *rungs* (a static check, a baseline
controller, a family policy, a short horizon) before spending trained, referee-promoted evaluation on the
survivors. A cheap rung may eliminate designs only if it orders them as the expensive rung does. Two components
decide that, and the verification subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) self-tests each as a
*certifier* under gate GATE-GAUNT-04 (lanes due at M3):

- *The rank-agreement ledger* measures, per stratum (morphology class and task family), Kendall's τ between
  rungs on the top band (the top m designs of either rung) with a bootstrap interval, and assigns a mode:
  `selective` if the lower bound of τ is at least 0.7, `discard-only` if it is at least 0.5 and below 0.7,
  `uninformative` below 0.5. Thresholds are fixed before data.
- *Screening audits* make a screen's losses observable: a stratified, blinded sample of what it discarded, and
  a planted winner (a design known to belong in the top k, inserted in a form the screen represents poorly).

Why self-test them: a ledger that reports high agreement on a population where there is none licenses the
screen to throw away the winner, and nothing downstream will notice, because a comparison among survivors says
nothing about a design discarded at the first rung.

## Scope

In: planted populations with known rank correlation; the two self-tests with thresholds; the audit bound
arithmetic; the broken-certifier control of the ledger; the CERT-10 register row with its two parts (arithmetic
at M3; the binding to the audit's planted-screen record at M4). Out: the ledger, the audit and the cascade
themselves; the real rank-agreement experiment on the engine (M4); the calibration sets' storage (BD-377); the
biased and unbiased planted screens, the 1,000 synthetic campaigns and the audit's registered mutants (N26b-06,
M4) and their run for GATE-NEMESIS-07 (N26b-07); the M4 run of `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M4`, which reads that
record (BD-377).

## Technical approach

**CERT-09 Rank-agreement ledger.**
The ledger's rule, restated from its owner (BD-265) because every expected value below follows from it:
Kendall τ-b on the top band (the designs in the top m of either rung, m = max(10, 3k), k = 10, so m = 30);
2,000 seeded resamples of designs; a 95% percentile interval; every threshold applied to the **lower** bound
(the 2.5th percentile); `selective` also needs top-10 recall at least 0.8 and a valid calibration (at least 19
designs; exchangeability martingale below 20).
1. *Planted populations.* Synthetic populations of n = 40 designs (the ledger's set size). Rung scores are the
   planted ranks (expensive rung 40 − i for design i; cheap rung 40 − π(i)), and π reverses consecutive blocks
   of s designs inside the top band of 30 and leaves the 10 designs below it in the same order on both rungs.
   A block reversal gives d = 30·(s − 1)/2 discordant pairs, so τ = 1 − 4·d/(m·(m − 1)) = 1 − 2·(s − 1)/29
   exactly, and every design of the band has exactly s − 1 discordant partners. DESIGN, reason: the
   construction must leave the interval as narrow as 30 designs permit. Discordance concentrated in a few
   designs (any permutation with d inversions) widens it: at a planted τ of 0.80 a random such permutation
   gives a lower bound near 0.60, and the population would then test its construction, not the ledger.

   | Population | s | Planted τ | 95% interval (seed 0, approximate) | Mode the rule prescribes |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | A, above 0.7 | 3 | 25/29 = 0.8621 | [0.80, 0.92] | `selective` (top-10 recall 9/10) |
   | B, below 0.7 and above 0.5 | 6 | 19/29 = 0.6552 | [0.56, 0.79] | `discard-only` |
   | C, below 0.5 | 10 | 11/29 = 0.3793 | [0.27, 0.60] | `uninformative` |
   | D, above 0.7 by less than the interval | 5 | 21/29 = 0.7241 | [0.64, 0.83] | `discard-only` |

   Population B serves both thresholds (below 0.7, above 0.5). Population D is the control for a ledger that
   applies the thresholds to the point estimate: such a ledger assigns `selective` there. The interval column
   is a guide for the fixture's author; the test asserts coverage and the mode, not the printed ends, and the
   fixture is rejected if any lower bound lies within 0.03 of a threshold (it then tests the seed, not the rule)
   or if population A's martingale value is not below 20.
2. *Planted winner.* One further population (population A's ranks) contains a design that the expensive rung
   ranks first and the cheap rung ranks in its bottom half.
3. *Run* the ledger on each population through its public verb, in the verb's fixture form (N23b-35): each
   population, and the control population below, is a registered synthetic score table, run as `frob eval
   rank-agreement --fixture <population> --key <k> --json` (tier `static`, every output labelled `synthetic`).
4. *Thresholds (TARGET).* The measured top-band τ equals the planted value and lies inside its bootstrap
   interval; the mode assigned is the one the thresholds prescribe for the measured lower bound (the table's
   last column); the lost planted winner stops the rung from discarding in that stratum (its mode there becomes
   `uninformative`); an unmeasured stratum does not license elimination.
Broken certifier (control): a ledger that computes τ over the whole population instead of the top band. Its
population: n = 120, the top band of 30 reversed in blocks of 10 (top-band τ = 11/29 = 0.379, correct mode
`uninformative`) and the 90 designs below in the same order on both rungs, so the whole-population τ is
1 − 4·135/(120·119) = 0.962 and the broken ledger assigns `selective`; the self-test must fail.
Kill gate named in advance by the owning subsystem: if top-weighted τ is below 0.5 in more than half the strata
for the reference classes, tier pairs promote individual results only and no population is ordered across
tiers.

**CERT-10 Screening audits (two parts).** The screening audit lane (`nemesis.audit`, §26.10) lands at M4, one
milestone after the M3 part of GATE-GAUNT-04 that names it; the row therefore has an arithmetic part due at M3
and a measured part due at M4, as CERT-15 has for its product binding.
1. *Arithmetic (M3, this bead).* With no true top-k design among m audited discards the upper bound on the rate
   of discarded winners is 1 − δ^(1/m) at confidence 1 − δ, so 30 clean audited discards bound the rate at 9.5%
   and 59 at 5.0% (δ = 0.05); the approximation ln(1/δ)/m, which prints 10.0% at m = 30, is not the rule.
2. *Thresholds of the measured part (TARGET; the row's statement).* A deliberately biased screen (it discards
   every design of one morphology feature, which the planted winner has) and an unbiased screen run on the same
   seeded population: the planted winner's removal by the biased screen is reported and its results are
   labelled `screen-biased`; the unbiased screen passes; the 30-discard bound prints 9.5%.
3. *Audit hygiene stated in the row:* an audit design and a survivor are the same job type with the same budget;
   no audit episode is cancelled or re-weighted; audit bits are fixed before outcomes.
4. *Measured by (M4).* The record of GATE-NEMESIS-07 written by N26b-07 on the planted screens of N26b-06 (biased
   and unbiased screens, 100 + 100 runs and 1,000 synthetic campaigns). The row reads that record value for
   value and is `not run` until it exists. The broken certifier is N26b-06's registered mutant
   `nemesis-audit-mut-post-score-draw` (the audit sample drawn after scores, avoiding the discards near the cut,
   so a clean audit is reported where the winner was lost): its commitment check and coverage row must fail in
   that record. This bead first specified a biased screen and a post-hoc audit sample of its own; they are
   N26b-06's screens and mutant, and no second set is built.

**Evidence.** Class `Tested`, kind known answer; populations are `synthetic`; determinism D0 given the seed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the four planted populations the measured τ equals the planted value, the interval covers it, and the
      assigned mode is the one the rule prescribes for the lower bound (`selective` at 25/29; `discard-only` at
      19/29 and at 21/29; `uninformative` at 11/29); a ledger that reads the point estimate fails on 21/29.
- [ ] The lost planted winner makes the stratum `uninformative` and re-opens earlier eliminations.
- [ ] A stratum with no record yields no licence to eliminate.
- [ ] CERT-10 at M3: the bound for 30 clean discards prints 9.5% and for 59 prints 5.0%, computed exactly.
- [ ] CERT-10 at M4: the row equals N26b-07's record of GATE-NEMESIS-07 value for value (the biased screen's
      removal of the planted winner reported, the unbiased screen passing, the post-score-draw mutant caught),
      and reads `not run` while that record is missing.
- [ ] The whole-population τ ledger fails CERT-09's self-test.
- [ ] Both rows are in the certifier register and read by GATE-GAUNT-04; CERT-10 carries `due` M3 and M4.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cert_lanes.rs`:
- `tau_from_discordant_pairs`: m = 10, d = 9 gives τ = 1 − 36/90 = 0.6 exactly; a band of 30 reversed in
  blocks of 3, 5, 6 and 10 gives 25/29, 21/29, 19/29 and 11/29 exactly.
- `block_reversal_balanced`: every design of each planted band has exactly s − 1 discordant partners.
- `ledger_covers_planted_tau`: four populations, seed 0; each lower bound is at least 0.03 from 0.5 and 0.7.
- `mode_by_lower_bound`: 25/29 → `selective`; 19/29 → `discard-only`; 11/29 → `uninformative`;
  21/29 → `discard-only`.
- `point_estimate_ledger_caught`: a test build that applies the thresholds to the point estimate assigns
  `selective` to population D and fails the self-test.
- `lost_winner_blocks_discard`.
- `unmeasured_stratum_gives_no_licence`.
- `audit_bound`: 1 − 0.05^(1/30) = 0.0950 to four decimals; m = 59 gives 0.0495.
- `audit_bound_not_approximation`: ln(20)/30 = 0.0999 is rejected as the bound at m = 30.
- `cert10_row_equals_gate_record`: a fixture record of GATE-NEMESIS-07 (biased screen reported 100 of 100;
  unbiased screen passes; post-score-draw mutant caught) makes the M4 part `pass`; the same record with the
  mutant `missed` makes it `fail`; an absent record leaves it `not run`.
- `cert10_due_parts`: the register row lists M3 and M4; at an M3 fixture exit only the arithmetic part is due.
- `whole_population_tau_control`: on the 120-design control population the broken ledger computes 0.962 and
  assigns `selective`; the self-test fails.

### End-to-end script and logging

Measured by `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M3` (bead N38b-17), which runs `frob eval rank-agreement` on the planted
populations and checks the audit bound; and at M4 by `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M4` (run by BD-377), which reads
N26b-07's record of `frob nemesis audit` on the two screens. Logged: planted and measured τ with intervals, the
mode per population, the audit bound, the planted winner's fate, control outcomes.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the τ formula; the planted values 25/29, 21/29, 19/29 and 11/29; the control's 0.962; 9.5% at
m = 30; 5.0% at m = 59. Negative controls: whole-population τ; thresholds applied to the point estimate; the
post-score-draw audit (N26b-06's mutant, read at M4);
a ledger fed two model identities pooled (existing refusal FROB-REF-548). Metamorphic relation: relabelling
designs changes no τ. Determinism: bootstrap resamples are address-keyed; results identical twice.

## Considerations

- The Gauntlet's certifier table prints "an unmeasured stratum is `discard-only`", while the ledger's own rule
  and the fail-closed default say an unmeasured stratum is `uninformative` and a rung without a record is
  refused (existing refusal FROB-REF-537). This bead tests the stricter rule: no licence to eliminate. The
  difference is recorded for the lead.
- The real experiment (about forty per-design trainings in one class) needs approval above the default
  envelope; until it runs, every cheap rung is `uninformative` and neither selects nor discards.
- Screening audits at M3 exercise the arithmetic only, because the audit lane does not exist before M4; the
  planted-winner path is measured on the real lane at M4, and the cascade applies the audit at M4. The plan's
  GATE-GAUNT-04 lists the audit among the M3 lanes; the split is recorded for the lead.
