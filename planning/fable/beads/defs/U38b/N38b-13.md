## Background

FrankenRobotics screens many robot designs with cheap evaluation *rungs* (a static check, a baseline
controller, a family policy, a short horizon) before spending trained, referee-promoted evaluation on the
survivors. A cheap rung may eliminate designs only if it orders them as the expensive rung does. Two components
decide that, and the verification subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) self-tests each as a
*certifier* under gate GATE-GAUNT-04 (lanes due at M3):

- *The rank-agreement ledger* measures, per stratum (morphology class and task family), Kendall's τ between
  rungs on the top band (the top m designs of either rung) with a bootstrap interval, and assigns a mode:
  `selective` if the lower bound of τ is at least 0.7, `discard-only` if it is at least 0.5 and below 0.7,
  `uninformative` below 0.5. Thresholds are fixed before data.
- *Screening audits* make a screen's losses observable: a stratified, blinded sample of what it discarded, and
  a planted winner (a design known to belong in the top k, inserted in a form the screen represents poorly).

Why self-test them: a ledger that reports high agreement on a population where there is none licenses the
screen to throw away the winner, and nothing downstream will notice, because a comparison among survivors says
nothing about a design discarded at the first rung.

## Scope

In: planted populations with known rank correlation; the two self-tests with thresholds; the audit bound
arithmetic; the broken-certifier controls. Out: the ledger, the audit and the cascade themselves; the real
rank-agreement experiment on the engine (M4); the calibration sets' storage (BD-377).

## Technical approach

**CERT-09 Rank-agreement ledger.**
1. *Planted populations.* For each threshold in force (0.7 and 0.5) build two synthetic populations of 40
   designs whose two rung scores have a known top-band τ on each side of the threshold (TARGET values: 0.80 and
   0.60 around 0.7; 0.60 and 0.40 around 0.5). Construction: start from identical orderings and apply a fixed
   number of adjacent transpositions inside the top band; τ = 1 − 4·d/(m·(m − 1)) for d discordant pairs among
   m designs, so d is chosen exactly.
2. *Planted winner.* One population contains a design that the expensive rung ranks first and the cheap rung
   ranks in its bottom half.
3. *Run* the ledger on each population through its public verb.
4. *Thresholds (TARGET).* The measured top-band τ covers the planted value inside its bootstrap interval; the
   mode assigned is the one the thresholds prescribe for the planted value; the lost planted winner stops the
   rung from discarding in that stratum (its mode there becomes `uninformative`); an unmeasured stratum does
   not license elimination.
Broken certifier (control): a ledger that computes τ over the whole population instead of the top band reports
high agreement on a population whose top band is shuffled and assigns `selective`; the self-test must fail.
Kill gate named in advance by the owning subsystem: if top-weighted τ is below 0.5 in more than half the strata
for the reference classes, tier pairs promote individual results only and no population is ordered across
tiers.

**CERT-10 Screening audits.**
1. A deliberately biased screen (it discards every design of one morphology feature, which the planted winner
   has) and an unbiased screen run on the same seeded population.
2. Thresholds (TARGET): the planted winner's removal by the biased screen is reported and its results are
   labelled as screen-biased; the unbiased screen passes; with no true top-k design among m audited discards
   the upper bound on the rate of discarded winners is 1 − δ^(1/m) at confidence 1 − δ, so 30 clean audited
   discards bound the rate at 9.5% (δ = 0.05).
3. Audit hygiene: an audit design and a survivor are the same job type with the same budget; no audit episode
   is cancelled or re-weighted; audit bits are fixed before outcomes.
Broken certifier (control): an audit whose sample is drawn after the outcomes are known (it avoids the
discarded stratum that holds the winner) reports a clean audit; the self-test must fail.

**Evidence.** Class `Tested`, kind known answer; populations are `synthetic`; determinism D0 given the seed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the four planted populations the interval covers the planted τ and the assigned mode matches
      (`selective` at 0.80; `discard-only` at 0.60; `uninformative` at 0.40).
- [ ] The lost planted winner makes the stratum `uninformative` and re-opens earlier eliminations.
- [ ] A stratum with no record yields no licence to eliminate.
- [ ] The biased screen's removal of the planted winner is reported; the unbiased screen passes; the bound for
      30 clean discards prints 9.5%.
- [ ] Both broken certifiers fail their self-tests.
- [ ] Both rows are in the certifier register and read by GATE-GAUNT-04.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cert_lanes.rs`:
- `tau_from_discordant_pairs`: m = 10, d = 9 gives τ = 1 − 36/90 = 0.6 exactly.
- `ledger_covers_planted_tau`: four populations, seed 0.
- `mode_by_threshold`: 0.80 → `selective`; 0.60 → `discard-only`; 0.40 → `uninformative`.
- `lost_winner_blocks_discard`.
- `unmeasured_stratum_gives_no_licence`.
- `audit_bound`: 1 − 0.05^(1/30) = 0.0950 to four decimals; m = 59 gives 0.0495.
- `biased_screen_reported`, `unbiased_screen_passes`.
- `whole_population_tau_control`, `post_hoc_audit_control`: each broken certifier fails.

### End-to-end script and logging

Measured by `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M3` (bead N38b-17), which runs `frob eval rank-agreement` on the planted
populations and `frob nemesis audit` on the two screens. Logged: planted and measured τ with intervals, the
mode per population, the audit bound, the planted winner's fate, control outcomes.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the τ formula; 9.5% at m = 30. Negative controls: whole-population τ; the post-hoc audit sample;
a ledger fed two model identities pooled (existing refusal FROB-REF-548). Metamorphic relation: relabelling
designs changes no τ. Determinism: bootstrap resamples are address-keyed; results identical twice.

## Considerations

- The Gauntlet's certifier table prints "an unmeasured stratum is `discard-only`", while the ledger's own rule
  and the fail-closed default say an unmeasured stratum is `uninformative` and a rung without a record is
  refused (existing refusal FROB-REF-537). This bead tests the stricter rule: no licence to eliminate. The
  difference is recorded for the lead.
- The real experiment (about forty per-design trainings in one class) needs approval above the default
  envelope; until it runs, every cheap rung is `uninformative` and neither selects nor discards.
- Screening audits at M3 exercise the arithmetic and the planted-winner path; the cascade applies them at M4.
