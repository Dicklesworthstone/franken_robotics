## Background

Three components of FrankenRobotics turn raw episodes into verdicts, and the verification subsystem (the
Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) self-tests each as a *certifier* in tier G0 under gate GATE-GAUNT-04:

- *Requirement monitors.* A task's requirement is a formula over signals with temporal operators; its verdict
  and margin are computed by three independent evaluators of the requirement language (among them a streaming
  past-time monitor and an offline evaluator). If they disagree, a verdict depends on which code path ran.
- *The fixed-n pre-analysis.* A study with a fixed number of pairs uses a frozen decision rule. Before sampling,
  every verdict the rule can return at the planned n is enumerated; a verdict that cannot be reached must not
  be promised. A planning pilot found that at 64 pairs the frozen rule can return three of its four verdicts
  and that a tie is first reachable at 86 pairs (FACT, to be re-run here).
- *Rare-event bounds.* A claimed failure rate such as 10⁻⁴ is supported by a confidence sequence from weighted
  sampling; weights are bounded by 1/ε through a defensive mixture so that one sample cannot dominate.

## Scope

In: the three self-tests with thresholds, inputs and broken-certifier controls. Out: the monitors, the study
runner and the estimators; the monitors' refusal battery; attribution and conformal bands (N38b-15).

## Technical approach

**CERT-11 Requirement monitors.** For each operator of the language (the Boolean connectives, the bounded
past-time and future-time windows, `until`, and the unit-normalized atoms), draw 10⁶ random traces (address-keyed
generator; lengths 1 to 4,096 samples; multi-rate signals; window bounds that need the declared rounding to a
clock period) and evaluate verdict and margin with all three evaluators. Threshold: agreement bit for bit on
every trace, for verdict and for margin. Relations checked on the way: streaming past-time robustness equals
offline evaluation; "globally over [0, T]" evaluated at 0 equals "historically over [0, T]" evaluated at T.
Broken certifier (control): a monitor that pads a short window with the neutral element instead of refusing or
shortening as declared; it disagrees with the other two on traces shorter than the window.

**CERT-12 Fixed-n pre-analysis.** The frozen rule: for paired binary outcomes with a counts of A-only successes
and b counts of B-only successes among n pairs, form two-sided 97.5% exact (Clopper–Pearson) intervals and
classify with a ±5 point indifference band into *prefer B*, *B inferior*, *practical tie* or *inconclusive*.
Regenerate, by exact enumeration of all (a, b) with a + b ≤ n (there are (n + 1)(n + 2)/2 such pairs):

| n | Count pairs | Prefer B | B inferior | Practical tie | Inconclusive |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 64 | 2,145 | 521 | 521 | 0 | 1,103 |
| 86 | 3,828 | 1,031 | 1,031 | 1 | 1,765 |
| 128 | 8,385 | 2,517 | 2,517 | 4 | 3,347 |
| 256 | 33,153 | 11,384 | 11,384 | 39 | 10,346 |

Further exact facts to reproduce: perfect concordance at n = 64 gives the interval [−0.0662, +0.0662]
(1 − 0.0125^(1/64) = 0.0662), so no outcome returns a tie; a tie is first reachable at n = 86 and only for
(0, 0), with chance 0.9⁸⁶ = 0.0116% at 5% discordance each way; with no A-only pair a preference needs 15
B-only pairs of 64, and 17, 19, 21, 23 with one to four A-only pairs. Threshold: exact equality with the table,
and a study that names the tie verdict at n = 64 is refused at admission (existing refusal FROB-REF-532).
Broken certifier (control): a pre-analysis that uses a normal approximation reports a reachable tie at n = 64.

**CERT-13 Rare-event bounds (due with the estimators, M3).**
- Gaussian tail by defensive mixture: P(z > 4) = 3.167 × 10⁻⁵ for a standard normal; proposal shifted by 4;
  mixture share ε = 0.1 of the nominal law, so every weight is at most 1/ε = 10.
- Splitting on a biased random walk: the probability of reaching level 20 before level 0 from level 1 with
  up-probability 0.4 is 0.5/(1.5²⁰ − 1) = 1.504 × 10⁻⁴.
- Threshold (TARGET): over repeated studies with known p, the truth lies inside the confidence sequence in at
  least the stated share of repetitions (the 95% upper bound covers p in at least 95% of 10⁴ synthetic
  studies), and every weight is at most 1/ε.
Broken certifiers (controls): a mixture without its nominal share on a mis-centred proposal (weights unbounded,
coverage fails); a walk with no noise after the fork (identical children), which must be refused (existing
refusal FROB-REF-569), and a zero-failure episode count one short of the exact rule (N38b-57).

**Evidence.** Class `Tested`, kind known answer; tier G0, site `host`; D0 for CERT-12, D0 given the seed for the
others.

### End-to-end script and logging

Measured by `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh` (bead N38b-17): `frob task verdict` on recorded traces through each
evaluator, `frob eval preanalyze --n 64`, `frob study plan` with a tie verdict (refused), and at M3
`frob nemesis bound` on the two synthetic laws. Logged: disagreements (threshold 0), the table as measured, the
refusal code, coverage share and largest weight with thresholds.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the enumeration table (KA-612); the reachable-verdict fact (KA-002); the tail and walk values.
Negative controls: the padding monitor; the normal-approximation pre-analysis; the mixture without nominal
share; the noiseless fork. Metamorphic relation: a change of units leaves normalized margins unchanged.
Determinism: enumeration is exact (D0); trace generation is address-keyed.

## Considerations

- The interval [−0.0662, +0.0662] reconciles only with two-sided 97.5% intervals; at 95% the bound is 0.0560
  and the first tie is at n = 72. The level is part of the rule and must be stated wherever the numbers are
  (N38b-59).
- 10⁶ traces per operator on three evaluators is a nightly run inside the default envelope when traces are
  short; the count is printed if reduced.
- A certified failure rate of 10⁻⁶ needs a compute class above the default; the default certifies to 10⁻⁵.
