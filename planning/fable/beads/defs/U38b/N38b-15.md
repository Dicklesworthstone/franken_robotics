## Background

Two components of FrankenRobotics say *why* or *how well* and are trusted as verdicts; the verification
subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) self-tests each as a *certifier* under gate GATE-GAUNT-04.

- *Attribution* (the subsystem called Autopsy) explains a failed episode by re-running it from a snapshot with
  one factor changed (a counterfactual fork) and reporting which factor's removal prevents the failure
  (`but-for`), with coalition tables and Shapley values when several factors interact. It must also abstain: on
  a chaotic episode, an infinitesimal placebo change flips the outcome as often as a real change, and any named
  cause is invented.
- *Conformal bands* (the calibration subsystem, Ground) wrap a prediction for a calibrated cell in a band with
  a coverage guarantee that holds for draws exchangeable with the calibration draws, and only for those.

## Scope

In: the two self-tests with inputs, thresholds and broken-certifier controls; the coverage arithmetic. Out: the
attribution engine and the band mechanism; storage of the planted registry (BD-318); the single-precision
attribution lane.

## Technical approach

**CERT-14 Attribution.**
1. *Planted single causes.* 200 failures in five classes of 40 (friction drop, torque cap, payload shift,
   latency, terrain step), each with ten candidate factors, one of which was planted as the cause. Run
   attribution in double precision. Threshold (TARGET): the planted factor ranks first with label `but-for` in
   at least 90% of the 200; the top factor changes in at most 30% of cases under a 10⁻⁷ perturbation.
2. *Joint and alternative pairs.* Two coalition tables over two factors: (a) value of the pair 1 and of each
   single 0; (b) value 1 for either single and for the pair. Expected labels `joint` and `alternative`; Shapley
   values (½, ½) in both; repair sets {1, 2} for (a) and {1} or {2} for (b).
3. *Efficiency law.* Shapley values sum to v(N) − v(∅) exactly in rationals; for v(∅) = 0, v({1}) = 1,
   v({2}) = 0, v({1, 2}) = 0 the values are (½, −½), summing to 0.
4. *Exact against budgeted.* With ten factors, exact Shapley over all 2¹⁰ = 1,024 coalitions is compared with
   the budgeted summary; the budgeted values lie inside their stated intervals.
5. *Chaotic placebo.* The doubling map x ↦ 2x mod 1 with failure defined on its fortieth iterate: a placebo
   perturbation of 10⁻¹² is amplified by 2⁴⁰ ≈ 1.1 × 10¹², about half of placebo forks flip the outcome, and the
   label must be `chaotic: unattributable`. The same map over five iterates attributes normally.
Broken certifier (control): an attribution that ranks one-at-a-time contrasts without the placebo fork names a
cause on the doubling map; a summary that reports only Shapley values cannot tell cases (a) and (b) apart.

**CERT-15 Conformal bands.**
1. Draw n = 20 exchangeable calibration residuals and one test residual from the same law; target coverage
   1 − α = 0.90. The band's margin is the k-th smallest calibration score with
   k = ⌈(n + 1)·(1 − α)⌉ = ⌈18.9⌉ = 19.
2. Over 1,000 resamples (and 10⁴ for the gate run): marginal coverage is k/(n + 1) = 19/21 = 0.905;
   conditional on the calibration set, coverage is distributed Beta(n + 1 − l, l) with l = ⌊(n + 1)·α⌋ = 2, so
   the fraction of calibration sets whose coverage is below 0.80 is P(Beta(19, 2) < 0.80) = 0.069.
3. Thresholds (TARGET): mean coverage within 0.01 of 19/21; the fraction below 0.80 within 0.03 of 0.069; a
   pooled shifted regime is flagged (pooling a shifted regime with the calibrated one yields 16 of 20 covered
   in the shifted regime, and the exchangeability alarm must fire); zero bands are issued for a shifted cell, a
   cell with 8 points, or a design that was never built.
Broken certifier (control): a band that uses the ⌈n·(1 − α)⌉-th score (18th of 20) has mean coverage 18/21 =
0.857 and fails the 0.01 tolerance; a band issued for a pooled shifted regime fails the flag check.

**Evidence.** Class `Tested`, kind known answer; all inputs `synthetic`; attribution runs under determinism
class D2 or stricter (attribution below D2 is refused by an existing rule); the band arithmetic is D0 given the
seed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] CERT-14: at least 180 of 200 planted factors ranked first with `but-for`; both pair tables labelled
      correctly with Shapley (½, ½); the negative-value table gives (½, −½); the doubling map at forty iterates
      is `chaotic: unattributable` and at five iterates attributes normally.
- [ ] CERT-15: mean coverage in [0.895, 0.915]; fraction below 0.80 in [0.039, 0.099]; the shifted regime is
      flagged; no band for the three refused cases.
- [ ] Each broken certifier fails its self-test.
- [ ] Both rows are in the certifier register with their controls; the band row carries `due = "M5"` for the
      product binding and `M3` for the arithmetic.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cert_evaluators.rs`:
- `shapley_efficiency_exact`: three coalition tables in rationals; sums equal v(N) − v(∅).
- `joint_vs_alternative_labels`; `negative_shapley_value` ((½, −½)).
- `exact_vs_budgeted_1024`: ten factors, 1,024 coalitions.
- `doubling_map_forty`: amplification 2⁴⁰ = 1,099,511,627,776; flip share in [0.4, 0.6] over 1,000 placebo
  forks; label `chaotic: unattributable`. `doubling_map_five`: attributes normally.
- `planted_causes_rank_first`: ≥ 90% of 200 (seed 0).
- `conformal_index`: n = 20, α = 0.1 gives k = 19; n = 8 gives k = 9 > n (no finite margin: refused).
- `conformal_mean_coverage`: within 0.01 of 0.905 over 10⁴ resamples.
- `conformal_low_coverage_fraction`: Beta(19, 2) mass below 0.80 equals 0.0692 analytically and within 0.03
  empirically.
- `pooled_shift_flagged`; `off_by_one_index_control` (0.857); `no_placebo_control`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Measured by `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M3` (bead N38b-17): `frob autopsy attribute` on ten planted failures and
on the doubling-map fixture; at M5 `frob ground band` on the rehearsal cell. Logged: rank-first share
(threshold 0.90), labels, flip share, mean coverage and low-coverage fraction with thresholds, refusals.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 19/21; 0.069; 2⁴⁰; the Shapley tables (KA-673, KA-674). Negative controls: no placebo fork;
Shapley-only summary; the off-by-one conformal index; the pooled shifted regime. Metamorphic relations: the
null fork is the original; a placebo flips nothing on a non-chaotic case. Determinism: attribution in double
precision; a marginal failure that flips under 10⁻⁷ and not under 10⁻¹² is labelled `precision-sensitive`.

## Considerations

- A contrast between forks is evidence inside the simulation; citing it as an established cause, or as a cause
  in the physical robot, is refused by an existing rule (FROB-REF-880).
- No band is ever issued for a design that was never built; coverage is a statement about exchangeable draws.
- 200 planted failures at ten forks each fit the default envelope on the CPU tiers.
