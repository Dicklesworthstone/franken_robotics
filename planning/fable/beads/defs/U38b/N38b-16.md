## Background

Four components of FrankenRobotics label evidence rather than produce it, and a wrong label silently upgrades
everything downstream. The verification subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) self-tests each as a
*certifier* in tier G0 under gate GATE-GAUNT-04.

- *The claim product.* Every result carries a typed summary of what it asserts and how strongly: one of seven
  claim kinds, an evidence class (Estimated < Tested < Certified < Proved), a tier, a determinism class, a
  reality scope and condition flags. Combination takes the weakest class, tier and determinism, the
  intersection of scopes and the union of flags; there is no setter for the class.
- *The determinism classifier.* Each kernel and result has a class: D0 exact; D1 bitwise across backends; D2
  bitwise per backend and independent of batch composition; D3 inside a tolerance with agreement of task
  decisions; D4 unspecified, never evidence. A result of class D4 is refused wherever evidence is consumed.
- *The energy audit.* The energy ledger closes per step; energy that no actuator supplied (a contact model
  handing a policy free energy) raises the flag `energy-created`.
- *The Gauntlet's own harness.* Its paired lower bound is what every method experiment's kill rule reads.

## Scope

In: the four self-tests with thresholds and broken-certifier controls. Out: the types and mechanisms
themselves; the harness statistics (N38a-08 implements them; this bead registers the row); the determinism
lanes (N38a-35).

## Technical approach

**CERT-16 Claim product.** Over random sets of input evidence (10⁵ sets of 1 to 12 inputs, each with a random
class, tier, determinism class, reality scope and flag set): the envelope's class equals the meet (minimum) of
the inputs' classes; tier and determinism equal the weakest; flags equal the union; the product is independent
of input order; no public function of the owning crate returns a class stronger than its inputs. Worked known
answer: a kernel identity that is Certified, a friction coefficient from a card field marked `assumed`
(Estimated) and a paired study on a GPU tier under D2 (Tested) combine to a design comparison of class
Estimated whose weakest input is the friction field. Second known answer: {Tested on the referee, Tested and
provisional on a fast tier} is provisional. Compile-fail tests pin three absences: a conversion from a claim
product into a Proved value; a setter for the class; a public constructor taking a class.
Broken certifier (control): a combine function that returns the strongest class ("best input"), or the
majority class, labels the provisional comparison established; the self-test must fail.

**CERT-17 Determinism classifier.**
1. A planted nondeterministic reduction (a sum taken in arrival order across workers) inside a kernel declared
   D2: the classifier must place it below its claimed class (D3 if inside the envelope with agreeing decisions,
   else D4), detected by the lanes "twice on one machine" and "across worker counts".
2. A result of class D4 offered unlabeled (its class field removed or relabelled D2) on each of the five
   evidence paths (paired statistics, promotion, qualification, a certificate, a build packet): refused on
   every path (existing refusal FROB-REF-825); the same policy re-evaluated under D2 is accepted.
3. A class is never raised by relabelling, only by running again under a stricter profile.
Broken certifier (control): a classifier that trusts the declared class without running the lanes passes the
planted reduction as D2.

**CERT-18 Energy audit.**
1. Residual per step: r = Δ kinetic + Δ potential − (actuator work + external work − all dissipation); created
   energy is Σ max(0, r) over a window. Flag threshold (TARGET): above 1% of the absolute work exchanged in any
   0.5 s window on a fast tier; above 10⁻⁶ relative on the referee.
2. Fixture A, the stiff penalty bounce: a body bouncing on a stiff penalty contact at a step near the stability
   limit gains height; the audit must raise `energy-created` (and the contact-passivity flag). The same drop
   under the convex contact law at the default step must raise neither.
3. Fixture B, a sign error at one port: a damped pendulum's ledger with the sign of one port's power flipped;
   the audit must localize the error to that port (residual attributed to the port, below 10⁻⁹ relative
   elsewhere on the referee).
Broken certifier (control): the audit switched off, under which the bouncing policy wins a comparison; an audit
that sums signed residuals over the window (created and lost energy cancel) misses fixture A.

**CERT-19 The Gauntlet's own harness (proof obligation PO-GAUNT-01).** Ten pairs of constant ratio r must
return the paired lower bound r exactly; an A/A campaign (both arms identical) must not clear 1.02. Broken
certifier (control): a harness that reports a ratio of means instead of a paired bound; a harness whose arms
are paired by run order instead of by world address and seed.

**Evidence.** Class `Tested`, kind known answer, site `host` (CERT-16, -19), `strict` and device sites
(CERT-17), `referee` and fast tiers (CERT-18).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] CERT-16: meet, weakest, union and order-independence hold on 10⁵ random sets; both worked answers
      reproduce; the three compile-fail tests fail to compile; the "best input" variant is caught.
- [ ] CERT-17: the planted reduction is classified below D2; D4 is refused on all five paths and accepted
      after re-evaluation under D2; the trusting classifier is caught.
- [ ] CERT-18: fixture A raises `energy-created` and the convex-law drop does not; fixture B names the port;
      both broken audits are caught.
- [ ] CERT-19: bound equals r for r in {0.5, 1, 1.345, 10}; A/A does not clear 1.02; both broken harnesses are
      caught.
- [ ] The four rows are in the certifier register with their controls and are read by GATE-GAUNT-04.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cert_evidence.rs`:
- `claim_product_meet_random` (10⁵ sets; property test with shrinking); `claim_product_order_independent`.
- `claim_product_worked_case`: {Certified, Estimated, Tested} → Estimated, weakest = the friction field.
- `claim_product_provisional`: {Tested referee, Tested provisional fast} → provisional.
- `no_class_setter`, `no_into_proved`, `no_class_constructor` (compile-fail).
- `planted_reduction_classified_below`: worker counts 1 and 8 give different digests → not D2.
- `d4_refused_five_paths`; `d4_reevaluated_accepted`; `relabel_does_not_raise`.
- `energy_created_on_penalty_bounce`; `no_flag_on_convex_law`; `port_sign_error_localized`.
- `paired_bound_constant_ratio`; `a_a_does_not_clear`.
- one `*_control` test per broken certifier (six in all).

### End-to-end script and logging

Measured by `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M3` (bead N38b-17): a `frob eval compare` whose arms differ in tier (the
envelope must print the weakest class), a D4 training result offered to `frob eval promote` (refused), `frob
fidelity energy` on the bounce and pendulum fixtures. Logged: the evidence block of each envelope, refusal
codes, created energy against its threshold, the port named, the paired bound against r.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the two worked claim products (CX-014); the paired bound r; the flags of fixtures A and B.
Negative controls: best-input and majority combine rules; the trusting classifier; the audit switched off; the
signed-sum audit; ratio of means; order pairing. Metamorphic relation: the claim product is order-independent
and never raises a class (MR-40). Determinism lanes: `twice` and `workers` are the instruments of CERT-17.

## Considerations

- The claim-product type lives in the core crate; the self-test is in the conformance crate so that the type's
  own tests are not its only judge.
- An A/A threshold of 1.02 is the strict form for the Gauntlet's fixture; the performance program's own gate
  uses a frequency form over many A/A campaigns.
- A flagged episode keeps its result and gains the flag; a comparison whose advantage coincides with flagged
  episodes is typed model-conditional by the owning analysis.
