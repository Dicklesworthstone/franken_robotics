## Background

The verification subsystem of FrankenRobotics (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) judges every other
subsystem's gates, and it has gates of its own. Its decision ADR-GAUNT-03 says every gate is registered with its
failing control before its passing test is written: the control (a planted defect the gate's test must catch)
is the specification of what the gate detects. This bead applies that rule to the Gauntlet itself. It enters
the Gauntlet's eleven gates, the adopted known-answer gate that its chapter defines for another family
(GATE-FID-09) and its kill gate into `registries/gates.toml`, each with claim kind, threshold, earliest
milestone, and a non-empty list of registered negative controls, before any of them is measured.

All thresholds are TARGETs until a gate records a measured value. All eleven Gauntlet gates and GATE-FID-09 are
of claim kind *known answer*: they show the harness behaves on planted inputs.

## Scope

In: the thirteen rows; for each the controls that must be registered first; the binding to the beads that
measure them. Out: the measurements; the record schema (N38b-18); the kill decision (N38b-24).

**Procedure.**
1. Write the control records (NC identifiers, kinds, targets, defects, expectations) for every planted failure
   in the fourth column into `registry/controls.toml`, each naming its gate.
2. Write the thirteen gate records with `status = "not-run"` and `controls` filled; GATE-GAUNT-01 is marked
   recurring; gates that close in parts get one part per milestone listed.
3. Run the registry check; none of the twelve convergence rows may be `not-eligible`.
4. A meta-test compares this table with the file (identifier, claim kind, milestone list, control count).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Thirteen records exist; twelve have non-empty control lists whose records exist and name the gate; the
      kill gate has trigger, fallback and decision milestone.
- [ ] In the commit history each gate's first control is no later than its first passing receipt.
- [ ] The meta-test fails when a threshold, a milestone or a control is removed from the file.
- [ ] Every row's claim kind is `known-answer` (the kill gate has none).
- [ ] Each gate is cited by the bead that measures it, and each measuring bead cites the gate.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: the table itself. Negative control: the dropped control list; a gate registered after its first
receipt (history fixture). Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- Where a subsystem's text and the register differ on a gate's identity, claim, milestone or number, the
  register governs; the threshold column is a brief and the full criterion is in the measuring bead.
- GATE-FID-09 carries a fidelity-family name and a known-answer claim; it is entered once, under the Gauntlet
  chapter as owner.
- The kill gate's trigger, threshold or fallback changes only by a dated amendment made before the measurement
  it governs.
