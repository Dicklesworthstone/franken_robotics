## Background

The verification subsystem of FrankenRobotics (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) runs six tiers of tests
(G0 laws and self-tests, G1 known answers, G2 parity, G3 metamorphic relations, G4 chaos, G5 determinism). They
cannot all run on every change: some counts are 10⁴ scenarios per relation per tier, 10⁵ seeded fault
schedules and 10⁷ fuzz executions per importer, and every run on any machine is bound by a default envelope of
16 GiB of memory, 8 cores and one hour unless the owner approved more in writing beforehand. Several of the
Gauntlet's gates are stated with a cadence: "every milestone exit", "M0, then every exit", "per release", "at
each funding decision". The runtime budget itself is an open question (OPEN-GAUNT-5) with a fail-closed
default: the `smoke` and `fixtures` batteries per change; everything else nightly.

This bead turns the cadences into data and a runner, so that what runs when is not left to each session.
Rejected alternative: one always-everything test command (it would exceed the envelope and so would never be
run in full, which is worse than a declared schedule).

## Scope

In: the schedule file; the four triggers and their selections; path-based selection for the per-change run;
the list of recurring gates; reduced-count reporting; the runner script. Out: measuring the budgets
(N38b-36); the tiers and their receipts (N38a-02); the exit review procedure (`needs`).

## Technical approach

**Schedule (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/schedule.toml`; DESIGN).** One `[[trigger]]` per cadence, each a
list of selections `{ target, filter, count, budget }`.

| Trigger | When | Selections (fail-closed default) |
|---|---|---|
| `change` | Every change before commit | Tier G0 in full; the `smoke` battery (every template and reference robot on a handful of scenarios, minutes); the `fixtures` battery when the change touches contact, actuators, clocks or the requirement language; the invariant suite; the registry checks; the controls of gates whose crates the change touches; relations at a smoke count of 32 scenarios per site |
| `nightly` | Once per night per machine | Tier G1 for rows due; G2 parity; G3 at 10³ scenarios per site; the kernel-side chaos families at 10³ schedules; G5 lanes `twice`, `workers`, `profiles`; all controls of gates due |
| `release` | Before any release | G3 at 10⁴ scenarios per site (10³ on the referee by default, printed); 10⁵ seeded chaos schedules with every await point of the publish and submit paths hit; 10⁷ fuzz executions per importer and per format reader; G5 lanes `platforms` and `backends`; the certifier self-tests at full counts |
| `exit` | At each milestone exit, by a fresh-context session | Everything in `release` for the gates due at the milestone, plus: the all-controls run (GATE-GAUNT-01); the known-answer accounting with obligations counted (GATE-GAUNT-02); oracle hygiene (GATE-GAUNT-05); status accounting (GATE-GAUNT-10); the close audit; the suspect-control review; the pilot register check at each funding decision (GATE-GAUNT-08) |

**Recurring gates.** Four gates are re-run at every exit after their first: the reality line's gate, the
no-authorization flags' gate, GATE-GAUNT-01 and the program gate that enforces ceiling-before-speed. The `exit`
trigger includes them unconditionally. The Gauntlet's own "then every exit" gates (GATE-GAUNT-02, -03 from M2,
-05, -10) are included from their first milestone on.

**Per-change path selection.** `scripts/e2e/gauntlet_run.sh change` reads the changed paths (`git diff
--name-only` against the merge base) and maps crates to selections through `registry/schedule_paths.toml`: a
change under the contact, actuator, sensor-clock or requirement crates selects `fixtures`; a change under a
crate selects the controls of every gate whose measuring entries live in that crate; a change to any registry
file selects the invariant suite and the registry diff. An unmapped path selects the conservative default (G0,
`smoke`, invariants).

**Budgets and reduced counts.** Each selection carries a `budget` class: `default` (inside the envelope) or a
named larger class that waits for the owner's approval. A selection run at a reduced count prints
`reduced: <count> of <target>` in its receipt and can never be recorded as a pass at the full count. A
selection that would exceed its budget is split by crate or by site and resumes from the last finished part.

**Runner.** `scripts/e2e/gauntlet_run.sh <change|nightly|release|exit> [milestone]` executes the selections in
order, stops a selection at its timeout, and writes one summary receipt listing every tier receipt it produced.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The schedule file validates; every tier target and every gate of the Gauntlet appears in at least one
      trigger; no selection lacks a budget class.
- [ ] `change` finishes inside the per-change budget on the reference tree (minutes), runs `fixtures` exactly
      when a triggering path changed, and runs the controls of touched gates.
- [ ] `exit M<k>` runs the recurring gates and the Gauntlet's "every exit" gates and fails if any is missing a
      receipt.
- [ ] A reduced-count run is printed as reduced and refused by the recorder as evidence for the full-count
      threshold.
- [ ] A selection above the default envelope without a recorded approval does not start.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/schedule.rs`:
- `schedule_covers_all_targets`: six tier targets, the invariant suite, the registry checks.
- `fixtures_trigger_paths`: a change under the contact crate selects `fixtures`; a change under the surface
  crate does not.
- `touched_gate_controls_selected`.
- `unmapped_path_is_conservative`.
- `exit_includes_recurring`: the four recurring gates and GATE-GAUNT-02, -05, -10 are in `exit M1`; GATE-GAUNT-03
  joins at `exit M2`.
- `reduced_count_not_recordable`: a receipt with `reduced` set is refused for a full-count threshold.
- `over_envelope_needs_approval`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/gauntlet_run.sh` is the script (header: `bead=N38b-25 milestone=M1 labels=gauntlet
gates=GATE-GAUNT-01,GATE-GAUNT-02,GATE-GAUNT-05,GATE-GAUNT-10 plants=skip-recurring`). Its self-check plants
an `exit` schedule with one recurring gate removed and expects the exit selection to fail. Log at
`target/e2e-logs/gauntlet_run/<utc>.ndjson`: one `cmd` event per selection (command, exit code, wall time,
budget class), receipts' hashes, counts run against targets with any `reduced` flag, the list of gates touched,
and the `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: on a fixture change set (one file under the contact crate, one registry file) the `change`
trigger selects exactly G0, `smoke`, `fixtures`, the invariant suite, the registry diff and the controls of
the contact gates. Negative controls: the removed recurring gate; a hidden reduced count. Determinism: the
selection is a pure function of the schedule and the changed paths.

## Considerations

- The default is conservative on purpose; the measured budgets (N38b-36) may move selections between triggers
  by a recorded change to the schedule file, never silently.
- On a heavily loaded machine nightly runs are queued through the build-offload shim; when an agent stops, its
  jobs stop.
- A gate may pass before its milestone; the schedule never delays a measurement that is ready.
