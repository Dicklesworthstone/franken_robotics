## Background

FrankenRobotics separates seven kinds of claim; *Reality* (the simulation agrees with physical measurement) is
established only by imported real-robot measurements with their own provenance, predictions frozen before
comparison, and a band for a calibrated cell. Nothing in the program has been compared with a physical robot.
Until a dataset is admitted, every report prints `reality: no-claim`, which is a legitimate terminal state.

OPEN-GAUNT-1 is the open question, carried from the earliest version of the design: **which real-robot dataset,
if any, is admitted to the Reality lane first, with what licence and provenance.** It is one of the questions
that no measurement inside the program can answer; the owner decides it.

- Owner: the project owner (the calibration subsystem holds the engineering half).
- Blocks: any Reality gate; in particular the import-lane gate of the calibration subsystem, which is tied to
  no milestone and is recorded when data is admitted.
- Fail-closed default: `reality: no-claim` everywhere; a second-simulator stand-in is labelled `synthetic`.
- What the default costs: no Reality claim; the import lane stays empty.
- Needed by: M5 (the latest milestone at which an answer still changes the outcome).

The rule for every open question applies: a default removes a claim or a capability and never adds one; nothing
is decided by silence; at its deadline an undecided question takes its default, recorded as an architecture
decision record; a closed question is tombstoned, not reused.

## Scope

owner's answer may take and the conditions it must satisfy; the decision record
`docs/adr/ADR-PROG-06-reality-dataset.md` and the register row OPEN-GAUNT-1, under an answer or under the
default; the status of the Reality gates and the reality line of the reports under each outcome; the tombstone.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- the candidate list with the facts the owner needs (the survey, its one table of thirteen fields per
  candidate, the admission checklist, the class of support each candidate could give, and the Ground lead's
  finding on OPEN-GROUND-1, the engineering half): N30b-25, which this bead depends on. The facts this bead
  first listed for its own packet (the robot and whether a public model of it exists; what was measured and
  at what rates; licence and redistribution; provenance; clock alignment; whether a held-out portion can be
  sealed; the cell it would calibrate; what could not be learned) are fields of that table and are not kept

- the page `docs/decisions/OPEN-GAUNT-1.md` that puts that table to the owner one milestone before M5 (at the

## Technical approach

**What the decision reads.** The candidate table of the survey (N30b-25) as it stands at its re-run date, with
each candidate's checklist result and class of support, and the Ground lead's finding: a named dataset
recommended, none, or a bench package recommended instead. The owner is not bound by the recommendation, but
cannot admit a candidate that failed the checklist: the admission path would refuse it.

**Admission conditions the answer must satisfy (existing rules of the calibration subsystem).** A dataset
lacking licence, provenance, rights or measurement uncertainty is refused (existing refusal FROB-REF-873,
repair: supply the missing fields); a prediction is frozen before its trial data is readable; a scored holdout
is retired; a band belongs to exactly one cell and is never issued for a design that was never built.

**Forms of the answer.** The owner chooses one dataset, several in a stated order, or none. A decision that
names a dataset states its licence class, its provenance and the cell it is admitted for.

**Recording** (DESIGN: the record is `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-06-reality-dataset.md`, the program's consecutive
number for this owner question, so that one identifier names it in every bead; the page
`docs/decisions/OPEN-GAUNT-1.md` of N41-10 is its supporting file; the register row OPEN-GAUNT-1 moves to
`decided` or `defaulted` and names the ADR).
1. If the owner names a dataset: record the decision, the licence and the cell; the import-lane gate becomes
   eligible; the reality line of results inside the calibrated cell changes only after a frozen prediction is
   scored on sealed data; nothing outside the admitted cells changes its line.
2. If the owner declines or does not answer by the M5 exit: record the default as a decision; every gate of
   claim kind `reality` takes the terminal status `no-claim` (the gate register's status for a Reality gate
   with nothing measured: a state, shown as such, never as a pass), with the reason `no-dataset-admitted`,
   the reason code of the Reality lane's record; the Reality lane's first record is that `no-claim` (form B
   of the record of BD-397, which writes the row of GATE-GROUND-10 and cites this ADR).
3. Until either is recorded, GATE-GROUND-10 reads `blocked` with `detail = "OPEN-GROUND-1"` (the engineering
   half is named because it closes first; both halves are closed at the M5 exit).
4. In both cases the question is tombstoned with the decision's identifier.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision cites the survey record of N30b-25 at its re-run date (the table of every candidate, or
      the statement that none was found) and the dated page of N41-10 on which it was put to the owner one
      milestone before M5.
- [ ] At the M5 exit the decision record `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-06-reality-dataset.md` exists: a named dataset
      (or several in an order) with licence, provenance and cell, or the default; the register row names it.
- [ ] A decision that names a candidate whose checklist result in the survey is a failure is rejected.
- [ ] Under the default, every Reality gate reads `no-claim` and every report prints `reality: no-claim`; the
      status views show it as the state, not as a pass.
- [ ] Under an admitted dataset, no report outside the calibrated cell changes its line.
- [ ] No synthetic or published-anchor comparison is relabelled by this decision.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/open_questions.rs`: `open_gaunt_1_default_applied` (with no decision record at
the M5 exit fixture, Reality gates load as `no-claim` and the exit query accepts them);
`open_gaunt_1_dataset_needs_licence` (a decision naming a dataset without a licence field is rejected);
`open_gaunt_1_rejects_failed_candidate` (a decision naming a candidate whose survey row fails the checklist
is rejected, with the failed item named); `open_gaunt_1_default_reason_code` (the default's reason is
`no-dataset-admitted`, the code of the lane record's form B); `tombstone_keeps_identifier`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Covered by `scripts/e2e/reality_lane.sh M5` (steps 1 to 3 by N38b-02, steps 4 and 5 by N38bp1-01, the M5
rehearsal step by BD-411, which depends on this bead), which asserts the line under whichever outcome is
recorded and logs the decision's identifier in its `summary`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Negative controls: a Reality gate set to `pass` with no dataset (refused); a `no-claim` recorded on a
non-Reality gate (refused); a dataset decision without provenance (refused). Determinism: not applicable (a
recorded decision).

## Considerations

- A private repository cannot be independently confirmed; if the first dataset is the owner's own, the
  replication kit and the offline auditor are the artifacts prepared for a third party.
- Bench packages are optional, packaged separately and block no milestone; sourcing may start at M0.
- No calibration against hardware is required for the program to be done: a recorded `no-claim` satisfies it.
