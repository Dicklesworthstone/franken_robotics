## Background

The known-answer suite of FrankenRobotics holds 154 rows stated by its forty mathematical *constructions* (the
contracts behind the subsystems: Lie-group kinematics, cones, symmetry, design functors, statistics, mission
polarity, numerical similarity, quotienting, tier correction, rare events, forks, requirements, certificates,
structure, variational steps, contact cones, actuators, value of information). 72 of those rows state no
negative control (FACT, counted). A row without a control cannot support a gate, because a test that nothing
can fail validates nothing; this is open question OPEN-GAUNT-3 with the fail-closed default "the row supports
no gate".

Each of the 72 rows has its registered control set by the bead that owns its construction: the registry bead
N10-KA1 for the 27 rows of constructions C-01 to C-15, and the specification beads of C-16 to C-39 (the
`SPEC-C-nn` beads and their parts N10b-nn and N10c-nn) for the other 45, each of which writes "registered here"
or a named mutant in its known-answer table. Those are the rows' controls. This bead does three things on top
of them: it checks, row by row, that the owner's control is present as an NC record with `seed = "KA-nnn"`,
obeys the rule and is caught; it offers one alternate per row, the shortcut somebody else would plausibly take,
written so that the row then reports a value outside its tolerance, as a second control for the owner to accept
or decline; and it keeps the count of the 72 in the burn-down until every one is closed. Every alternate is
DESIGN; the binding rule is the one every control must satisfy (stated in full in N38b-28): a defect and not a
boundary; one of five kinds; a stated wrong outcome outside tolerance and on the unsafe side; a named gate; run
whenever that gate runs.

## Scope

In, for all 72 rows: the check that the owner's control is registered as an NC record with its gate, obeys
the rule and is caught from the row's due milestone on; one alternate per row with its disposition (an
alternate the owner accepts is registered as a further NC record with the same `seed` and gate, with the wrong
value added to the derivation file under "what the control gives" and the run that shows the row fails with it
planted; one the owner declines is dropped with a dated note); the count of the 72 in the burn-down of N38b-49.

Out, with the bead that has it: the rows themselves (N10-KA1, N10-KA2, N10-KA3). The registered controls: for
the 27 rows of C-01 to C-15 they are set in N10-KA1 in agreement with `SPEC-C-01` to `SPEC-C-15`; for the 45
rows of C-16 to C-39 (N10-KA2 and N10-KA3 carry them as printed, control cell open) they are set by the
specification beads listed under "Status of the 45 rows" below. This bead does not set them a second time and
does not replace them. Tolerances still to set for construction rows, by rule (N38b-27; the 23 of C-01 to C-15
are set in N10-KA1); truths named and not written (N38b-47); the rule every control obeys and the registry
mechanics (N38b-28, N38b-04); the construction audits that require every row's control (N10a-22, N10b-19,
N10c-10).

- [ ] The check of the owners' controls runs over all 72 rows: every row whose owner has landed (N10-KA1 and
      the M1 owners at this bead's close) has its owner's control as the `registered` NC record with
      `seed = "KA-nnn"`; the others are listed `open: owner <key>` with the owner's milestone. No row has two
      different controls both claiming to be the one the registry counts; an accepted alternate is a further
      record.

      recorded disposition: registered as a further control, or declined with a dated note; an owner not yet
      landed is asked when it lands, and the open dispositions are listed.

      none is `cannot-fail` or `boundary-not-control`; rows due later are run by the control runner from their
      due milestone (logged `not-due` until then, never as a pass).

- `open_row_fails_m3_rule`: a scratch registry with KA-096 open and due at M2 fails the burn-down check when the
  milestone is set to M3 and passes (printing the row) at M1.
Per row, a test `nc_<id>_caught` in the owning crate's conformance target (written by the owner for the
registered control, by this bead for an accepted alternate).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ka_controls_constructions.sh` (header: `bead=N38b-29 milestone=M1 labels=gauntlet,registry
gates=GATE-GAUNT-01,GATE-GAUNT-02 plants=unregistered-row,second-registered,coinciding-alternate`; sources the
STD-E2E helper; works in a scratch copy of the registries). Steps:
1. `check`: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test control_obligations -- constructions`; one `assert` per row
   (registered record present, owner, rule passed) or `skip` with `open: owner <key>`.
2. `run`: for every row due by the current milestone, the control runner plants the registered control (and
   any accepted alternate) through the G0 and G1 tier targets
   (`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers -- ka:<id>` and the owning crate's law target with
   `--features gauntlet-mutations,<crate>/nc-<nnnn>`); per row an `e2e_expect_caught` line with the truth, the
   tolerance, the control's value and `caught`.
3. `burn-down`: the constructions' row of `target/gauntlet/obligations.json` as a `metric`
   (`rows_without_control`, unit `rows`, threshold `== 0` for rows due by the current milestone from M3 on).
4. Plants: `unregistered-row` (the registered record of KA-150 removed: must be reported open and, with the
   milestone set to M3, fail), `second-registered` (an alternate given the `registered` mark beside the
   owner's: rejected), `coinciding-alternate` (KA-176's alternate registered as a second record with the
   owner's defect: rejected as a duplicate).
Log at `target/e2e-logs/ka_controls_constructions/<utc>.ndjson` with the transcript beside it; every line has
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines carry the exact command, exit code and wall time; failed
assertions carry the first differing KA or NC identifier with observed and expected values and the scratch
paths; the `summary` gives counts of assertions, controls caught and missed, rows open by owner, and names
GATE-GAUNT-01 and GATE-GAUNT-02.

### Known answers and negative controls

The 72 registered controls (the owners') are the rows' negative controls; the 51 differing alternates are
offered as second controls. Known answers used to check them: the closed-form wrong values listed above, and
KA-072's 8 and 26 against 6 and 23. Negative controls of this bead's own check: an unregistered row, a second
record marked registered, a coinciding alternate registered twice. Determinism: D0 for host rows; referee rows
compare under their tolerance.

## Considerations

- Several proposals coincide with mutations registered elsewhere (backlash as a dead zone; the stripped packet
  queue; the point-robot oracle); the registry then links the row to the existing control instead of making a
  second one.
- A law row (tier G0) whose property test already runs against a mutated implementation needs only the
  registration.
- Mutant builds are grouped by crate; 72 controls cost a handful of rebuilds, not 72.
