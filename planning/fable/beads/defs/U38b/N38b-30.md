## Background

In FrankenRobotics a known-answer row cannot support a gate until a *negative control* is registered for it:
the mutation or planted failure that must make the row fail. Of the 835 rows of the suite, 325 state none
(open question OPEN-GAUNT-3; fail-closed default: the row supports no gate). This bead covers 25 of them, from
four chapters: the conventions shared by every subsystem (frames, inertia parameters, clocks, sensing windows;
6 rows), the proof subsystem called Lemma (6), the algebraic core called Marrow (6) and the design language
called Skeleton (7). Most of these rows are *laws* (tier G0) or round trips; their tempting shortcuts are
dropped factors, wrong orders and omitted canonicalization.

Each proposal below is DESIGN, confirmed by the row's owner. The binding rule (stated in full in N38b-28): a
control is a defect and not a boundary; it has one of five kinds; it states a wrong outcome that lies outside
the row's tolerance and on the unsafe side; it names the row's gate and runs whenever that gate runs.

## Scope

In: 25 proposed controls, registered as NC records with `seed = "KA-nnn"`; the wrong outcome recorded in each
derivation file; the run showing each row fails with its control planted. Out: the rows; tolerances to set
(N38b-27); the balancer pole's closed form (N38b-47).

**Registration.** For each row: an NC record (`seed`, `gate` = the row's owning gate, `kind`, `target`,
`defect`, `expect`), a feature branch in the owning crate for kind M or a fixture for kind I, and a line "what
the control gives" in the derivation file.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the 25 rows has a registered control obeying the rule, confirmed or replaced by its owner.
- [ ] With its control planted each row fails for the stated reason.
- [ ] The obligations meta-test reports zero rows of these four chapters without a control at the milestone
      where each row is due, and zero for rows due by M3 at the M3 exit.
- [ ] KA-269's control stays an obligation until its closed form is on file.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/control_obligations.rs`: `conventions_lemma_marrow_skeleton_25_listed`;
`wrong_values_outside_tolerance` for the closed-form rows (202: one short; 226: α·π²/6 = 1.645·α; 241: x/2;
262: 1.59 against 2.44). Per row, `nc_<id>_caught` in the owning crate's conformance target.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ka_obligations.sh` (shared; header in N38b-27) runs these rows with controls planted through the
G0, G1 and G3 tier targets and logs per row the truth, the control's outcome and `caught`, then the burn-down
counts for the four chapters.

### Known answers and negative controls

The 25 proposals are the negative controls. Several coincide with mutations already registered for
metamorphic relations (packet queues left out of a checkpoint; key-order canonicalization disabled; an input
missing from a key; a two-statement idempotency check); the registry links the row to the existing control.
Determinism: D0 on the host and the auditor.

## Considerations

- Law rows whose property tests run 10⁶ to 10⁹ cases need the control to be caught at the per-change count as
  well, so that a regression is seen before the nightly run.
- KA-263 is a refusal row: its control is the build with the check removed, never the refusal itself.
