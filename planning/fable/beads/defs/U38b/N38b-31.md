## Background

A known-answer row of FrankenRobotics supports a gate only when a *negative control* is registered for it: the
mutation or planted failure that must make the row fail. 325 of the suite's 835 rows state none (open question
OPEN-GAUNT-3; fail-closed default: the row supports no gate). This bead covers the 69 such rows of the body's
physics: actuators, transmissions and power (the subsystem called Sinew, 25 rows), dynamics on three tiers
(Kinesis, 11), families of bodies in one batch (Phylum, 9) and collision, contact, wheels, tracks and terrain
(Traction, 24). Their tempting shortcuts are the textbook simplifications: a constant resistance, a dead zone
for backlash, viscous friction for stiction, a pyramid for a cone, the wrong mean, a dropped lever arm.

Each proposal is DESIGN, confirmed by the row's owner. The binding rule (in full in N38b-28): a control is a
defect, not a boundary; one of five kinds; a stated wrong outcome outside the row's tolerance and on the
unsafe side; the row's gate named; run whenever that gate runs.

## Scope

In: 69 proposed controls registered as NC records with `seed = "KA-nnn"`; each wrong outcome in its derivation
file; the run showing each row fails. Out: the rows; tolerances (N38b-27); closed forms not yet written for
three rows (N38b-47); the two rows that share a formula with different cards (N38b-48).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/control_obligations.rs`: `sinew_kinesis_phylum_traction_69_listed`;
`wrong_values_outside_tolerance` for closed forms (314: 27.7 against 33.9 V; 315: 3,394 against 3,151; 318:
2,424 against 5,657; 348: 17 against 27 kB; 362: 144 against 576; 368: 1.74 against 1.58; 374: 4.9 against
1.25; 383: 1,344 against 2,624; 397: 0.102 against 0.153 s). Per row, `nc_<id>_caught` in the owning crate.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ka_obligations.sh` (shared; header in N38b-27) runs these rows with controls planted through the
tier targets on their sites and logs per row the truth, tolerance, control value and `caught`, then the
burn-down counts for the four chapters.

### Known answers and negative controls

The 69 proposals are the negative controls; several are the registered mutations of metamorphic relations
(scaling by 10; a slot-dependent digest; unweighted point speeds) and link to them. Determinism: host rows D0;
tier rows compare under their tolerance and class.

## Considerations

- Rows on batched tiers need the mutation in the kernel source as well as in the CPU reference, so that the
  control is caught on every site the row names.
- Illustrative cards differ between chapters for some formulas; a control's numbers are computed from the
  row's own card.
