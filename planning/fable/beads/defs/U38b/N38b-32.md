## Background

A known-answer row of FrankenRobotics supports a gate only when a *negative control* is registered for it: the
mutation or planted failure that must make the row fail. 325 of the suite's 835 rows state none (open question
OPEN-GAUNT-3; fail-closed default: the row supports no gate). This bead covers 70 of them: scenes, living
worlds, clearance and retrofits (the subsystem called Habitat, 21 rows); sensors, clocks and observability
(Sensorium, 35); GPU backends and the kernel toolchain (Spark, 14). The tempting shortcuts here are of three
families: a missing dimension in a size or error calculation, a sensor that ignores time or its mount, and a
device path that quietly differs from its CPU reference.

Each proposal is DESIGN, confirmed by the row's owner. The binding rule (in full in N38b-28): a control is a
defect, not a boundary; one of five kinds; a stated wrong outcome outside the row's tolerance and on the
unsafe side; the row's gate named; run whenever that gate runs.

## Scope

In: 70 proposed controls registered as NC records with `seed = "KA-nnn"` (first or second, by the owners' rule
below); the check of every control of these rows, the owners' included, against the binding rule; each wrong
outcome in its derivation file; the run showing each row fails. Out: the rows (N19-KA, N20-KA, N21-KA, on which
this bead depends); the controls those registries or their mechanism beads already name, which stay first;
tolerances (N38b-27); the reconciliation of two rows' printed arithmetic (KA-445, N38b-53; KA-460, N38b-54),
whose controls are nevertheless proposed here.

## Technical approach

All controls are of kind `mutation` unless marked I (`planted-input`) or W (`planted-world`). "→" introduces
what the row reports with the defect planted.

**Owners' controls.** Some owners already name a control or the defect a row guards: N19-KA for KA-427 (the
withdrawn quantization over ±10 m, 20 m/65,536 ≈ 0.31 mm, about 30 N per step); N20-KA for KA-488 (a smaller
null space), KA-500 (the requirement chapter's non-strict comparator, BD-190), KA-506 (the planted leaks of
KA-497), KA-509 (a build that derives friction from `r0`), KA-519 (a model that reports the true distance
inside the blind zone, N20a-20) and KA-520 (quantization by truncation, N20a-20); the Spark mechanism beads named in N21-KA for
KA-531 and KA-540 to KA-552. The rule is the one of the sibling beads, applied mechanically from the control
registry: where an NC record with `seed = "KA-nnn"` from the owner exists, it is the row's first control, and
this bead's proposal is linked to it when it is the same defect (488, 500, 509, 519, 520 by the examples above) or
registered as a second record when it differs (427: 8-bit storage is a different defect from the ±10 m range);
where no owner's record exists when the row falls due, the proposal is the first control. The check prints,
per row, which record is first and from which bead.

**Registration.** One NC record per row; for a kernel mutation the feature branch is in the dialect source so
that the defect exists on the CPU batch tier and on each device backend; "what the control gives" is added to
the derivation file.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the 70 rows has a registered control obeying the rule, confirmed or replaced by its owner; the
      check prints the first record and its bead, and no row has two records both marked first.
- [ ] With its control planted each row due by the current milestone fails for the stated reason on every
      site the row names; rows due later are run by the control runner from their due milestone and logged
      `not-due` before it.
- [ ] The burn-down (N38b-49) reports zero rows of these three chapters without a control at the milestone
      where each is due; the M3 rule is enforced by a planted open row due at M2 in a run set to M3.
- [ ] Controls of device-only rows (540 to 546) are caught on each admitted device family; a skipped family is
      named and not counted.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/control_obligations.rs` (section for these three chapters):
- `habitat_sensorium_spark_70_listed`: the 70 numbers (21, 35, 14 by chapter).
- `first_record_is_the_owners`: for every row with an owner's record, that record is first and this bead's
  proposal is linked or second; a planted second record marked first is rejected.
- `wrong_values_outside_tolerance` for closed forms: 427: 0.16 m/255 = 0.63 mm against 2.4 µm; 449: 35.4 mm
  against 35.4 + 39.3 = 74.7 mm for the first two terms; 450: 38,400 against 1,228,800; 454: 60,880 against 10,700; 459: 1.5 MB against 75 MB; 460:
  counts × 1/0.85 = 1.18 against 1; 461: 75 MB against 614 GB; 462: 4 against 6 bytes; 480: 0 against 0.5 m;
  520: 4.10 against 4.11 m;
  547: 3.16 against 2.96; 549: 4.9 against 1.25; 550: 0.25 against 0.125; 551: about 37.5 M against 75,411
  slots; 552: 972 against 1,944.
Per row, `nc_<id>_caught` in the owning crate.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ka_controls_scene_sense_spark.sh` (header: `bead=N38b-32 milestone=M2 labels=gauntlet,registry
gates=GATE-GAUNT-01,GATE-GAUNT-02 needs=cpu plants=two-first-records,uncaught-control`; sources the STD-E2E
helper; scratch copy of the registries). Steps: (1) `check`: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
control_obligations -- habitat_sensorium_spark`, one `assert` per row naming its first record and its bead;
(2) `run`: for each row due by the current milestone, every record planted on every site the row names
(`--features gauntlet-mutations,<crate>/<feature>`,
the record's `feature` field as written; kernel mutations on `strict`, `lockstep` and each device in
`E2E_DEVICES`), an `e2e_expect_caught` line per record and site with the truth, tolerance, control value,
site and `caught`; an absent device family logs `skip` with its name and is not counted; rows due later log
`skip` with `not-due: <milestone>`; (3) `burn-down`: one `metric` per chapter (`rows_without_control`, unit
`rows`, threshold `== 0` for rows due by the current milestone from M3 on); (4) plants: `two-first-records` (a
second record for KA-509 marked first: rejected) and `uncaught-control` (KA-552's mutant built without its
feature: the gate line must fail). Log at `target/e2e-logs/ka_controls_scene_sense_spark/<utc>.ndjson` with the
transcript; every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines the exact command, exit code
and wall time; failed assertions the first differing KA or NC identifier with observed and expected values,
the site and backend, and the scratch paths; the `summary` the counts, device families skipped, and gates
GATE-GAUNT-01 and GATE-GAUNT-02.

### Known answers and negative controls

The 70 rows' first controls (the owners' where they exist, this bead's proposals otherwise) and the second
records are the negative controls; several are registered mutations of metamorphic relations (a batch-wide
normalizer; packet queues; a policy reading undelivered truth; a generator reading an unseeded source) and
link to them. Negative controls of this bead's own check: two records marked first; an uncaught control.
Determinism: host rows D0; device rows compare against their class.

## Considerations

- The four noninterference rows (505 to 509) are relations; their controls are also the relations' mutations,
  registered once and cited twice.
- Device controls need a device; in the variant without devices they stay registered and `not-run`, and the
  rows they guard are not counted.
