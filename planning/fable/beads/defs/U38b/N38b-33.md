## Background

A known-answer row of FrankenRobotics supports a gate only when a *negative control* is registered for it: the
mutation or planted failure that must make the row fail. 325 of the suite's 835 rows state none (open question
OPEN-GAUNT-3; fail-closed default: the row supports no gate). This bead covers 29 of them, in the subsystems
that decide rather than simulate: estimation, control, gaits and shields (Reflex, 4 rows); learning (Cortex,
1); tasks, fixtures and qualification (Arena, 11); mission queries and inverse design (Augur, 8);
counterfactual debugging (Autopsy, 2); the studio loop and decision cores (Atelier, 3). Their tempting
shortcuts are statistical (a bound rounded the wrong way, a constant test level) and structural (a second
wheel forgotten, a small-angle formula, a causal rule ignored).

Each proposal is DESIGN, confirmed by the row's owner. The binding rule (in full in N38b-28): a control is a
defect, not a boundary; one of five kinds; a stated wrong outcome outside the row's tolerance and on the
unsafe side; the row's gate named; run whenever that gate runs. Two rows here count episodes for a
zero-failure claim: for them "unsafe side" means *fewer* episodes or a *smaller* bound than the truth.

## Scope

In: 29 proposed controls registered as NC records with `seed = "KA-nnn"`; each wrong outcome in its derivation
file; the run showing each row fails. Out: the rows; tolerances (N38b-27); the control of the zero-failure
row that errs on the safe side (N38b-57).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the 29 rows has a registered control obeying the rule, confirmed or replaced by its owner.
- [ ] With its control planted each row fails for the stated reason.
- [ ] For the counting rows (559, 614, 694, 706) the control's value is on the unsafe side: fewer episodes or
      a smaller bound than the truth.
- [ ] The obligations meta-test reports zero rows of these six chapters without a control at the milestone
      where each is due, and zero for rows due by M3 at the M3 exit.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/control_obligations.rs`: `reflex_to_atelier_29_listed`;
`wrong_values_outside_tolerance` (559: 298 against 299 with 0.99²⁹⁸ > 0.05; 569: 1 against 50 kg; 589: 527
against 585; 603: 20 against 40 N; 605: 37.95 against 36.00 mm; 614 and 694: 0.0450 against 0.0457; 653:
(2/3, 2/3) against (1, 1); 674: sum ½ against 0; 706: 0.033 against 0.095). Per row, `nc_<id>_caught` in the
owning crate.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ka_obligations.sh` (shared; header in N38b-27) runs these rows with controls planted and logs per
row the truth, tolerance, control value and `caught`, then the burn-down counts for the six chapters.

### Known answers and negative controls

The 29 proposals are the negative controls. Known answers used to check them: the closed-form wrong values
above. Determinism: D0 on the host; referee rows under their tolerance.

## Considerations

- Rows 636 and 645 are verdict rows about causality and labels; their controls change the information
  structure, not a number, and are caught when the verdict changes.
- Rows quoting FACTs (655) keep "to be re-read" beside them; their control can be registered before the
  re-read, but the row counts only after it.
