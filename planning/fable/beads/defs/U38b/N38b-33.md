## Background

A known-answer row of FrankenRobotics supports a gate only when a *negative control* is registered for it: the
mutation or planted failure that must make the row fail. 325 of the suite's 835 rows state none (open question
OPEN-GAUNT-3; fail-closed default: the row supports no gate). This bead covers 29 of them, in the subsystems
that decide rather than simulate: estimation, control, gaits and shields (Reflex, 4 rows); learning (Cortex,
1); tasks, fixtures and qualification (Arena, 11); mission queries and inverse design (Augur, 8);
counterfactual debugging (Autopsy, 2); the studio loop and decision cores (Atelier, 3). Their tempting
shortcuts are statistical (a bound rounded the wrong way, a constant test level) and structural (a second
wheel forgotten, a small-angle formula, a causal rule ignored).

Each proposal is DESIGN, confirmed by the row's owner. The binding rule (in full in N38b-28): a control is a
defect, not a boundary; one of five kinds; a stated wrong outcome outside the row's tolerance and on the
unsafe side; the row's gate named; run whenever that gate runs. Four rows here are counting rows of a
zero-failure claim (KA-559, an episode count; KA-614, KA-694 and KA-706, upper bounds on a failure or
false-discard rate): for them "unsafe side" means *fewer* episodes or a *smaller* bound than the truth.

## Scope

In: 29 proposed controls registered as NC records with `seed = "KA-nnn"` (first, second or linked, by the
owners' rule below); the check of every control of these rows, the owners' included, against the binding rule;
each wrong outcome in its derivation file; the run showing each row fails. Out: the rows (N22-KA, N24-KA,
N25-KA and N28-KA, on which this bead depends; the Cortex row KA-589 in N23-KA and the Autopsy rows KA-674 and
KA-676 in N27-KA, both M2 registries: their records are registered when those rows land and are reported
`open: row not registered` until then); the controls owners already register, which stay first; tolerances
(N38b-27); the control of the zero-failure row that errs on the safe side (N38b-57).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the 29 rows has a registered control obeying the rule, confirmed or replaced by its owner; the
      eleven rows of the owners' table have the owner's record first (five linked, six with a second record
      from this bead); no row has two records both marked first; KA-589, KA-674 and KA-676 are reported
      `open: row not registered` until their M2 registries land.
- [ ] With its control planted each row due by the current milestone fails for the stated reason; rows due
      later are run by the control runner from their due milestone and logged `not-due` before it.
- [ ] For the counting rows (559, 614, 694, 706) the control's value is on the unsafe side: fewer episodes or
      a smaller bound than the truth.
- [ ] The burn-down (N38b-49) reports zero rows of these six chapters without a control at the milestone where
      each is due; the M3 rule is enforced by a planted open row due at M2 in a run set to M3.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/control_obligations.rs` (section for these six chapters):
- `reflex_to_atelier_29_listed`: the 29 numbers (4, 1, 11, 8, 2, 3 by chapter).
- `owners_first_records`: the eleven rows of the owners' table have the owner's record first; the five linked
  rows have one record; a planted second record marked first is rejected.
- `wrong_values_outside_tolerance`: 559: 298 against 299 with 0.99²⁹⁸ = 0.05003 > 0.05; 569: 1 against 50 kg;
  589: 527 against 585 (216,000/369 = 585.4, printed "about 590"); 602: 8.100 kg and ratio 1.25 against
  8.225 kg and 1.5625; 603: 20 against 40 N; 605: 37.95 against 36.00 mm; 614 and 694: 0.04504 against 0.04573;
  649: 0 against 4w·(1 − cos θ); 653: (2/3, 2/3) against (1, 1); 674: sum ½ against 0; 703: 2,621 s against
  0.2 s for the short job; 706: 0.033 against 0.095.
- `unsafe_side_for_counting_rows`: for 559, 614, 694 and 706 the control's value is fewer episodes or a smaller
  bound than the truth; a planted control of 300 episodes for 559 is rejected `cannot-fail`.
Per row, `nc_<id>_caught` in the owning crate.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ka_controls_decide.sh` (header: `bead=N38b-33 milestone=M1 labels=gauntlet,registry
gates=GATE-GAUNT-01,GATE-GAUNT-02 plants=safe-side-count,two-first-records`; sources the STD-E2E helper;
scratch copy of the registries). Steps: (1) `check`: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
control_obligations -- reflex_to_atelier`, one `assert` per row naming its first record and its bead, or
`skip` with `open: row not registered`; (2) `run`: for each row due by the current milestone, every record
planted through its tier target (`--features gauntlet-mutations,<crate>/<feature>`,
the record's `feature` field as written, or the planted fixture),
an `e2e_expect_caught` line per record with the truth, tolerance, control value and `caught`; rows due later
logged `skip` with `not-due: <milestone>`; (3) `burn-down`: one `metric` per chapter (`rows_without_control`,
unit `rows`, threshold `== 0` for rows due by the current milestone from M3 on); (4) plants: `safe-side-count`
(KA-559's control replaced by 300 episodes: rejected `cannot-fail`) and `two-first-records` (a second record for
KA-653 marked first: rejected). Log at `target/e2e-logs/ka_controls_decide/<utc>.ndjson` with the transcript;
every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines the exact command, exit code and wall time;
failed assertions the first differing KA or NC identifier with observed and expected values and the scratch
paths; the `summary` the counts and gates GATE-GAUNT-01 and GATE-GAUNT-02.

### Known answers and negative controls

The 29 rows' first controls (the owners' where they exist, this bead's proposals otherwise) and the second
records are the negative controls. Known answers used to check them: the closed-form wrong values above.
Negative controls of this bead's own check: the safe-side count; two records marked first. Determinism: D0 on

## Considerations

- Rows 636 and 645 are verdict rows about causality and labels; their controls change the information
  structure, not a number, and are caught when the verdict changes.
- Rows quoting FACTs (655) keep "to be re-read" beside them; their control can be registered before the
  re-read, but the row counts only after it.
