## Background

The *standard battery* is the one battery owned by FrankenRobotics' verification subsystem (the Gauntlet): for
each robot class about 200 scenarios (TARGET) under fixed seeds, frozen at M2. The fidelity ledger (does a fast
tier track the trusted referee tier), the divergence ledger, the tier-correction ledger and the exploitation
detectors all read it, so its content decides what "fidelity" means for a class. For legged robots the design
states the rows: stand, push recovery, each atlas gait at three speeds, turning, stairs up and down, a slope, a
low-friction patch, a payload and a drop, crossed with randomized parameters. For wheeled, tracked and
wheel-legged classes it states only that the gait rows are replaced by straight travel, turning in place, a
sill, a ramp and a split-grip strip; for manipulating classes it states nothing.

OPEN-GAUNT-7 is therefore open: **the scenario lists of the standard battery for wheeled, tracked,
wheel-legged and manipulating classes.**

- Owner: the evaluation subsystem (Arena) with the dynamics subsystem (Kinesis).
- Blocks: the fidelity gate GATE-FID-01 for those classes.
- Fail-closed default: the class has no fidelity claim until its battery is frozen.

A default removes a claim and never adds one: a class without a frozen list cannot have results promoted from a
fast tier, and its fast-tier results stay provisional.

## Scope

In: a proposed list per class with the reasoning; the decision record; the freeze of each list as a battery
identity; the default. Out: the battery mechanism, identity and change events (N38a-20, seed BD-212); the
contact laws and task families themselves.

## Technical approach

**Design rules for a class's list (DESIGN).**
1. Every row exercises a distinct mechanism of the tier difference: sustained contact, impact, stick-slip
   transition, actuator saturation, load transfer, capacity (many contacts), long-horizon drift.
2. Rows are tasks the class is built for, at three intensities where a speed or load applies.
3. Each row has a decision the tiers can agree or disagree on (a success predicate), not only a state error.
4. About 200 scenarios per class after crossing with randomized parameters under fixed seeds; the list is
   frozen with its seeds and gets a battery identity (hash of the manifest).
5. No row is taken from the hard-world set: a battery made of past failures overfits them.

**Proposed rows (to be confirmed or amended by the owners before the freeze).**

| Class | Rows replacing the gait rows | Rows kept from the common list |
|---|---|---|
| Wheeled (differential, steered, omnidirectional, mecanum) | Straight travel at three speeds; turning in place; an arc at two radii; a sill at three heights; a ramp up and down; a split-grip strip; braking to a stop | Stand (parked on a slope), push recovery (lateral shove), a slope, a low-friction patch, a payload, a drop off a low kerb |
| Tracked and skid-steer | Straight travel at three speeds; turning in place with scrub; a sill; a ramp; a split-grip strip; a step climb with flippers where fitted | The common rows above; a soft-ground patch once the terrain law exists (M4) |
| Wheel-legged and hybrid | The wheeled rows in wheel mode; a mode change (wheel to step) at a kerb; stairs up and down in leg mode | Push recovery, a slope, a low-friction patch, a payload, a drop |
| Manipulating (arm on a base; humanoid arms) | Reach to three poses; pick and place of a payload at three masses; carry while the base moves; contact with a fixed surface at a commanded force; a door or drawer pull | Stand under a payload, push recovery while holding, a low-friction patch under the base, a drop of the payload |

**Decision procedure.** (1) The owners review the proposal against the task families that exist at M2 and
strike rows whose mechanism has no model yet (those rows carry a later due milestone). (2) Each list is run
once on the referee to confirm every row terminates with a decision. (3) The lists are frozen with seeds; the
identities are recorded in the ledger. (4) The decision is recorded; classes whose list is not frozen are
listed with "no fidelity claim".

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A decision record exists at the M2 exit naming, per class, the frozen list and its battery identity, or
      the default "no fidelity claim" with the reason.
- [ ] Every frozen row terminates on the referee with a decision and states which tier-difference mechanism it
      exercises.
- [ ] No class without a frozen list has a fidelity gate row other than `not-run`, and its fast-tier results
      print as provisional.
- [ ] A later change to a list is a recorded event with a new identity.
- [ ] Rows deferred to a later milestone are named with the mechanism they wait for.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/open_questions.rs`: `class_without_frozen_battery_has_no_fidelity_claim`;
`frozen_list_has_identity_and_seeds`; `rows_name_a_mechanism`; `no_row_from_hard_world_set`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Covered by the standard-battery script of the battery's own bead and by `scripts/e2e/conformance_m2.sh`
(BD-223), which print per class the battery identity or "no fidelity claim" and the column sums.

### Known answers and negative controls

Negative controls: a class given a fidelity pass with no frozen list (refused); a list shortened without a new
identity (identity mismatch); a row copied from the hard-world set (refused). Determinism: the manifest and its
identity are D0.

## Considerations

- Tracked and hybrid archetypes receive their contact laws at M3 and M4; their lists can be frozen at M2 only
  for rows whose laws exist, and the fidelity gate for those classes is then recorded in parts.
- About 200 scenarios per class times several classes times five sites is a nightly cost that the budget
  measurement must include.
- If the owners prefer fewer classes at M2, the others simply keep the default.
