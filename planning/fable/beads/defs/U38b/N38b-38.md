## Background

FrankenRobotics' verification subsystem (the Gauntlet) tests *metamorphic relations*: relations that must hold
without knowing the answer (permuting worlds permutes results; a retry equals a clean run; rescaling units by
powers of two reproduces every bit). Its gate GATE-GAUNT-03 asks that each relation hold on 10⁴ random
scenarios per tier it names per release (TARGET), and that each registered mutation break its relation. Some
relations must run on the *referee*, the trusted double-precision CPU tier, which is slow: a planning estimate
puts it at 0.2 to 1.0 times real time per core. The question OPEN-GAUNT-8 is whether the count is affordable
there:

- Owner: the performance program.
- Blocks: GATE-GAUNT-03.
- Fail-closed default: referee relations run at 10³ scenarios and print the reduced count.

This bead measures the cost, decides the count per relation and site, and records it. Evidence decides where
evidence exists.

## Scope

In: the cost model, the measurement, the per-relation decision (full count, reduced count with its printed
value, or a cheaper sufficient site), the record; and, because the device sites arrive between the two
experiments, the M2 timing of the sites that N38b-36 recorded as `not measured`, with its script, entered in the
OPEN-GAUNT-5 record as a dated amendment. Out: the relations and their runner (N38a-14); the global budget and
its decision (N38b-36).

## Technical approach

**Cost model.** For a relation r on site s: cost(r, s) = N · (runs per scenario) · (simulated seconds per run)
/ (site speed in simulated seconds per wall second per core) / cores. With N = 10⁴, two runs per scenario
(base and follow-up) and a 2-second scenario, the referee at 0.2 to 1.0 times real time per core on 8 cores
needs between 10⁴·2·2/(1.0·8) = 5,000 s (about 1.4 hours) and 10⁴·2·2/(0.2·8) = 25,000 s (about 6.9 hours) per
relation: above the one-hour default envelope at the full count, inside it at 10³ (500 s to 2,500 s).
The model is a HYPOTHESIS until the referee's speed is measured on the relation scenarios.

**Measurement.**
1. For each relation whose sites include `referee`, run 100 scenarios, three times interleaved, and record
   wall time per scenario with its range and the disclosure block.
2. Extrapolate to 10³ and 10⁴; verify the extrapolation by one run at 10³ for the three costliest relations.
3. For each such relation examine whether the referee is needed: a relation whose comparison is exact
   arithmetic or bit equality within one tier can be decided on `host` or `strict` at the full count, with the
   referee run kept at a reduced count as a cross-check.

**Decision table (one row per relation and site).** `count` (10⁴, 10³ or another printed value), `budget`
class, `reason`. Rules: a count below 10⁴ is always printed in the account and in the gate's measured value
("held on 10³ of the 10⁴ target on the referee"); a reduced-count result is recorded against the threshold as
measured and is not rounded up; a relation is never dropped from a site to fit a budget without a recorded
decision.

**Detection power at a reduced count (stated so that the decision is informed).** If a relation is violated
on a fraction q of scenarios, the chance that N scenarios show no violation is (1 − q)^N: with N = 10³ a
violation rate of 0.3% is missed with probability 5%, and with N = 10⁴ a rate of 0.03% is missed with the same
probability (ln 0.05 = −3.0, so q ≈ 3/N).

**Device sites for the budget record.** `scripts/e2e/gauntlet_budget.sh` (N38b-36) is run on the sites its
M1 table lists as `not measured`; the rows replace those entries, each dated, and the OPEN-GAUNT-5 record gains
an amendment line with the adopted budget for those selections, or the statement that the default holds for
them.

**Record.** The measured costs and the decision table are the file `docs/decisions/OPEN-GAUNT-8.md`. The
decision itself is the record `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-GAUNT-8.md` (the one directory for decision records), which
cites that file; the row OPEN-GAUNT-8 of `registries/open.toml` (N41-OPEN8; decided by measurement, N41-03 rule
4) moves to `decided` with `adr` naming it, or, under the default below, to `defaulted` with the record written
from the register's default template (N41-03). A row left `open` past its M2 deadline would hold the M2 exit.

**Default if undecided at the M2 exit.** 10³ on the referee, printed; the gate records the reduced count.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Measured wall time per scenario, with range and disclosure block, for every relation that names the
      referee.
- [ ] A decision row per relation and site with count, budget class and reason.
- [ ] The relation runner prints the reduced count wherever it applies, and the gate's measured value carries
      it.
- [ ] No referee relation runs above the default envelope without a recorded approval.
- [ ] The decision record states the smallest violation rate each chosen count detects with 95% probability.
- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-GAUNT-8.md` exists at the M2 exit and the register row OPEN-GAUNT-8 names it, with
      status `decided`, or `defaulted` if undecided (the default recorded as the decision).
- [ ] The OPEN-GAUNT-5 record has no `not measured` row left for a site available at M2; each replaced row and
      the amendment carry a date.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/open_questions.rs`: `detection_rate_rule` (N = 1,000 → q = 0.2991% for a 5%
miss; N = 10,000 → 0.02995%, from 1 − 0.05^(1/N)); `reduced_count_printed_in_account` (the account reads
"1,000 of 10,000" on the referee); `reduced_count_not_recorded_as_full` (a measured value of 10⁴ written for a
10³ run is rejected); `cost_model_arithmetic` (5,000 s and 25,000 s for the stated inputs; 500 s and 2,500 s at
10³); `extrapolation_checked` (100 scenarios at 1.2 s each extrapolate to 1,200 s at 10³; a check run of 1,500 s
lies outside the series' range and is reported, not averaged in); `undecided_relation_dropped_rejected` (a
relation removed from the referee site with no decision row is rejected); `budget_amendment_dated` (an
OPEN-GAUNT-5 row replacing `not measured` without a date is rejected).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/relation_cost.sh` (header: `bead=N38b-38 milestone=M2 labels=gauntlet,performance
gates=GATE-GAUNT-03 plants=hidden-reduced-count,dropped-relation`; sources the STD-E2E helper). Steps: (1)
`time`: 100 scenarios of each relation whose sites include `referee`, through the G3 target
(`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g3_metamorphic -- <id>@referee --count 100`, the
form of N38a-19, with the script's seed), three times interleaved; (2) `extrapolate`: costs at
10³ and 10⁴ against the default envelope, and one run at 10³ for the three costliest relations; (3) `decide`:
the decision table written and checked by `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test open_questions`, with an
assertion that `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-GAUNT-8.md` exists and that the row OPEN-GAUNT-8 of `registries/open.toml`
reads `decided` or `defaulted` and names it; (4)
`budget-devices`: `scripts/e2e/gauntlet_budget.sh` on the sites N38b-36 left `not measured`, its log path
recorded; (5) plants: a run whose account omits its reduced count (the account check must fail) and a relation
dropped from the referee site without a decision row (rejected). Log at
`target/e2e-logs/relation_cost/<utc>.ndjson` with the transcript; every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`; `cmd` lines the exact command, exit code and wall time; one `metric` per relation for seconds
per scenario (with range) and for the extrapolated cost at 10³ and 10⁴ (threshold: the one-hour envelope), the
decided count and the detectable violation rate; the disclosure block; failed assertions the relation, observed
and expected values and the paths of the run's artifacts; the `summary` with counts at full, reduced and moved,
and GATE-GAUNT-03.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the detection-rate values; the cost-model arithmetic. Negative control: the hidden reduced
count. A/A check: two interleaved timing series of one relation agree within their range. Determinism: the
relation outcomes are deterministic for a fixed seed; timings are distributions.

## Considerations

- A relation that needs the referee for its meaning (for example model-form robustness of a ranking) keeps the
  referee even at a reduced count; a relation that only needs exactness can move its full count to a cheaper
  site.
- The per-release count is per release, not per change; per change the smoke count is 32 scenarios per site.
- If the owner approves a larger compute class for release runs, the full count is restored by an edit of the
  decision table, dated.
