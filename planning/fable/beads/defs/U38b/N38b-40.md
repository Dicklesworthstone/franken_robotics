## Background

The verification subsystem of FrankenRobotics (the Gauntlet) adopts fourteen known answers that are the
smallest cases separating a mechanism from a tempting shortcut. They are owned by the conformance crate and
run under the verbs of their subsystems. Four of them land at M1, the milestone of the CPU design studio:
three under gate GATE-FID-09 (corrected fixtures: times to 0.1 s, counts exact, admissions exact, every
negative control failing) and one under GATE-GAUNT-11 (late fixtures: rational rows exact). This bead writes
their tests through the public verbs and plants their controls.

Why these four matter at M1: the studio's preflight (`design check`) decides whether an actuator overheats and
whether a wheel can climb a sill, the symmetry machinery decides which contact states are "the same", and the
study runner decides which of two designs is preferred. A wrong shortcut in any of them gives a confident
wrong design decision.

## Scope

In: four rows through verbs on the host and the referee tier; four mutants or planted inputs as controls. Out:
the registry records and derivations (N38-KA); the mechanisms (the actuator thermal model, the orbit counter,
fixture 1, the study runner).

## Technical approach

**Row 1, thermal runaway, second card (KA-836; verb `actuator.thermal`, and `design.check` stage 6).**
Inputs: constant current 25.4 A; winding resistance 0.54 Ω referenced to 20 °C; temperature coefficient
0.00393 K⁻¹; heat capacity 140 J/K; conductance to ambient 1/2.2 W/K; ambient and initial temperature 25 °C;
limit 120 °C. Expected: loss 348.4 W at 20 °C and 485.3 W at 120 °C; slope s = I²·R₀·α = 1.369 W/K exceeds the
conductance, so no equilibrium exists (the verb must say so; existing refusal for "no stable thermal
equilibrium at a required operating point" where the task requires one); growth rate 0.006533 s⁻¹; limit
reached at 33.49 s. Regeared from ratio 12 to 16 at equal output torque: loss down 43.75%, s = 0.770 W/K,
still no equilibrium, rate 0.002254 s⁻¹. Ratio 22: s = 0.407 W/K, rate −0.000337 s⁻¹, equilibrium rise
2,240 K, 58.7 °C after 45 s, limit after 128.6 s: a stable sign with an equilibrium far above the limit, so
the verdict depends on the duty time. Tolerance 0.1 s, 0.1 K, 0.1 W. Controls (`mutation`): (a) the reference
temperature of R₀ read as 25 °C → 34.08 s; (b) constant resistance → 39.92 s; each outside 0.1 s.

**Row 3, contact strata under the observation subgroup (KA-838; verb `algebra.orbits`).** Four feet, each open,
stick or slip: 81 states, 648 directed one-foot transitions. Under the four-element leg-permutation group:
27 strata, 162 transitions. Under the two-element subgroup left by a forward-facing sensor: 45 strata, 324
transitions. The test enumerates orbits explicitly and checks the orbit-counting formula
(81 + 9 + 9 + 9)/4 = 27 and (81 + 9)/2 = 45. Control (`mutation`): the quotient taken under the mechanical
group where the observation subgroup is required → 27 where 45 is true. The verb must also refuse a quotient
requested under a group that moves a sensor (existing refusal for a group that is not observation-preserving).

**Row 5, a single wheel at a sharp lip (KA-840; verb `task.fixture` on the cart fixture's analytic layer).**
Axle load 25 N, lip 12 mm, pure drive couple. With a = √(2·r·h − h²) and b = r − h: lift-off torque W·a and
friction demand a/b. r = 60 mm: 0.900 N·m and 0.7500. r = 75 mm: 1.017 N·m and 0.6459. Admission table:
(μ = 0.70, cap 1.2 N·m) admits only the 75 mm wheel; (μ = 0.80, cap 0.95 N·m) admits only the 60 mm wheel.
General corner rule checked on ten seeded (P, M) pairs: lift-off b·P + M = W·a; corner reaction
a·P + b·W ≥ 0; friction |M| ≤ μ·(a·P + b·W). Control (`planted-design`, with a `mutation` of the admission
check): a check of torque alone admits both wheels in the first condition.

**Row 7 of the late table, unresolved outcomes in a comparison (KA-842; verbs `study.plan`, `study.submit` and
`study.status` on a fixed-n study, in the CPU form that answers at M1, N33b-19; their registry rows keep M2).** Baseline 60 successes in 100 valid trials; candidate 2 successes in 2
valid trials and 98 unresolved (the solver gave up; `unknown` is a verdict and never a failure or a success).
Expected report: complete-case rate 100%; success rate in [2%, 100%]; unresolved counts per arm; verdict "no
preference"; the unresolved worlds are listed for a re-run on the referee before any ranking that depends on
them is promoted. Control (`planted-input` with a `mutation` of the ranking rule): a ranking on complete cases
prefers the candidate.

**Execution.** Each row is a tier-G1 entry on `host` (closed form or enumeration) and, for rows 1 and 5, on
`referee` (the simulated winding temperature crossing the limit; the simulated wheel lifting or not at the
four admission corners, within the fixture's creep protocol). Each control is an NC record with a feature in
the owning crate, seeded from the Gauntlet's counterexample entries (N38-CX, registered at M0, a dependency, as
N38-KA is for the rows): CX-385 for row 1 (the catalogue's seeded record is the 25 °C reference; the
constant-resistance reading is a further record with the same seed), CX-387 for row 3, CX-389 for row 5, each
naming GATE-FID-09, and CX-391 for the late row, naming GATE-GAUNT-11.

**Plant names.** N38-CX names these plants `N38b-40:cx-385`, `N38b-40:cx-387`, `N38b-40:cx-389` and
`N38b-40:cx-391`, and the seeding tool writes the name after the colon into the control (N38b-05 step 2), so
this bead declares exactly those names: the Cargo features `cx-385` (R₀ referenced to 25 °C) in `frob-actuate`
and `cx-387` (the quotient by the mechanical group) in `frob-algebra`, each implying its crate's `conformance`
feature (N38b-04's mutation guard); the fixtures `cx-389` (the lip design with the 60 mm wheel under the first
admission condition, μ = 0.70 and cap 1.2 N·m, which the torque-only check admits) and `cx-391` (the late row's
study, baseline 60 of 100 against candidate 2 of 2 with 98 unresolved, ranked on complete cases), each beginning
with the `synthetic` header, in `frob-arena`. The constant-resistance record, which this bead registers itself
with seed CX-385, takes the feature `nc-<nnnn>` after its own NC number in `frob-actuate`. Under any other name
the seeded control has no plant: `feature-missing` or `fixture-missing` at load once its gate is past `not-run`
(BD-025 rule 3).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/adopted_known_answers.sh M1` (bead N38b-46, which depends on this bead): `frob actuator thermal
<second card> --current 25.4A --json` (the row's operating point; without `--current` or `--torque` the verb
has none), `frob design check <design carrying the card> --json`, `frob algebra orbits <group> --set feet:4
--transitions --json` (without `--transitions` the 162 and 324 are not reported), `frob task fixture fixture:cart-sill-dock --layer analytic --key aka-840-<n> --json`, then
`frob study plan <manifest> --json`, `frob study submit <plan> --key aka-842-<n> --json` and `frob study status
<study> --json` (each run-kind call with its own key, the planted re-runs included). It logs, with the
fields of STD-E2E (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`; exact command, exit code, wall time; the envelope's
evidence block), each measured value with unit, truth and tolerance, the refusal codes where a refusal is the
expected outcome, each control's outcome with the shortcut's observed value, and on failure the first differing
value and the feature flags and fixture paths to reproduce it.

### Known answers and negative controls

As above (KA-836, -838, -840, -842; controls 34.08 s, 39.92 s, 27, both wheels admitted, candidate preferred).
Metamorphic relations: scaling the thermal card's capacity, conductance and winding resistance by 2 (so that
every power term doubles with them) leaves the runaway time unchanged, while scaling capacity and conductance
alone does not (the rate becomes (s − 2G)/2C); relabelling feet changes no orbit count; mirroring the lip fixture changes no admission.
Determinism lanes: `twice` and `profiles`; D0 for counts and admissions.

## Considerations

- The thermal row uses an illustrative card; another illustrative card of the same formula elsewhere has a
  different critical current, and the registry keeps the two as separate fixtures.
- The referee's refusal rate on the sill fixture is an open question of the dynamics subsystem; if the lip
  cases do not complete on the referee, the row is recorded on the host and the referee part stays `not-run`.
