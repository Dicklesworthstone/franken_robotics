## Background

Two of the fourteen known answers adopted by FrankenRobotics' verification subsystem (the Gauntlet) belong to
the contact subsystem's M4 work: soil mechanics for wheels and tracks, and the *response batch* (many
right-hand sides solved against one factored contact system, each column accepted only with an error bound).

*Plate sinkage* (gate GATE-FID-09, corrected fixtures; tolerance: sinkage to 10⁻⁵ m in double precision). A
loaded plate sinks into soft ground by a power law: pressure p = (k_c/b + k_φ)·zⁿ, with b the plate width, z
the sinkage, k_c and k_φ soil moduli and n the exponent. The fixture's first printed values were inconsistent
with its exponent, and an exponent copied from a neighbouring row passes every test that does not check
numbers; the corrected row is the tested one.

*Residual bound* (gate GATE-GAUNT-11, late fixtures; single-precision rows bit-exact on every backend). A
solve's error is bounded by the residual divided by the smallest eigenvalue only if the rounding error of
forming the residual is added. Without that margin the expression is not a bound: in single precision a
residual can be computed as exactly zero while the answer is wrong in the last place.

## Scope

In: both rows through their verbs and on the host; two controls. Out: the registry records (N38-KA); the soil
law, rut state, and the response batch themselves.

## Technical approach

**Row 2 of the corrected table, plate sinkage with n = 0.5 (KA-837; verb `contact.soil`).** Constants:
k_c = 0.41 kN/m^1.5; k_φ = 21.6 kN/m^2.5; two tracks of length 0.55 m and width b; g = 9.81 m/s². Static
pressure p = m·g/(2·0.55·b); sinkage z = (p/(k_c/b + k_φ))^(1/n) with pressures in kPa.

| Load and track width | p (kPa) | k_c/b + k_φ | z |
|---|---|---|---|
| 45 kg on 8 cm | 5.0165 | 26.725 | 0.03523 m (3.52 cm) |
| 75 kg on 8 cm | 8.3608 | 26.725 | 0.09787 m (9.79 cm), more than an 8 cm belly clearance |
| 75 kg on 12 cm | 5.5739 | 25.017 | 0.04964 m (4.96 cm) |

The verb must also report the consequence: the 75 kg machine on 8 cm tracks bottoms out (sinkage above belly
clearance) and the 12 cm tracks do not. Control (`mutation` of the card reader or a `planted-input` card): the
same constants with n = 0.7 give 0.0917, 0.1901 and 0.1171 m; each differs from the truth by far more than
10⁻⁵ m. Sites: `host` (closed form) and `strict` (a plate pressed quasi-statically on the terrain law).

**Row 7 of the late table, residual bound in single precision (KA-847; verb `contact.response`).** One row,
H = 3, b = 1, unit roundoff u = 2⁻²⁴.
1. x̂ = fl(1/3) = 0.33333334 (bit pattern 0x3EAAAAAB).
2. Computed residual r̂ = fl(b − fl(H·x̂)): H·x̂ = 1.0000000298…, which rounds to 1, so r̂ = 0 exactly.
3. True error |x̂ − 1/3| = 9.93 × 10⁻⁹.
4. Rounding margin ρ = γ·(|H|·|x̂| + |b|) with γ = (n + 1)·u/(1 − (n + 1)·u) and row length n = 1:
   γ = 1.192 × 10⁻⁷, |H|·|x̂| + |b| = 2.0000000, ρ = 2.384 × 10⁻⁷ (printed 2.4 × 10⁻⁷).
5. Bound (‖r̂‖ + ρ)/λ_lo with λ_lo = 3: 7.947 × 10⁻⁸ (printed 7.9 × 10⁻⁸), which contains the true error.
Expected from the verb: the column is accepted with the bound 7.9 × 10⁻⁸ attached. Control (`mutation` of the
acceptance rule): the bound without ρ reports 0, claiming zero error; the test asserts bound ≥ true error and
fails under the mutant. Sites: `strict`, `cuda`, `metal`, bit-exact for x̂ and r̂.

**Property behind the row.** For 10⁴ seeded single-precision systems of size 1 to 8 with known exact solutions
(rational inputs solved in exact arithmetic), the bound with ρ contains the true error in every case; without
ρ it fails in a measurable fraction. The property test reports that fraction as the control's potency.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Row 2: the three sinkages reproduce to 10⁻⁵ m on host and within the tier envelope on `strict`; the verb
      reports the belly-clearance consequence; the exponent mutant gives 9.17, 19.01 and 11.71 cm and is caught.
- [ ] Row 7: x̂ and r̂ bit-exact on every admitted backend; ρ = 2.384 × 10⁻⁷; bound 7.947 × 10⁻⁸ ≥ 9.934 × 10⁻⁹;
      the no-margin mutant claims zero and is caught.
- [ ] The property test shows the bound with ρ never fails on 10⁴ systems.
- [ ] The rows are recorded as the M4 parts of GATE-FID-09 and GATE-GAUNT-11 by a session that did not
      implement the mechanisms.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Conformance entries `ka:KA-837@host|strict`, `ka:KA-847@strict|cuda|metal`, and
`crates/frob-conformance/tests/adopted_rows_m4.rs`:
- `ka_837_sinkage_three_cases`; `ka_837_bottoming_reported`; `ka_837_units` (kPa and metres; a card in Pa with
  the same digits is rejected by dimension typing).
- `ka_847_bits`: 0x3EAAAAAB; residual 0x00000000.
- `ka_847_margin_and_bound`: ρ and the bound to four significant figures.
- `po_residual_bound_contains_error`: 10⁴ systems (property test).
- `nc_exponent_0_7_caught`; `nc_bound_without_rho_caught`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/adopted_known_answers.sh M4` (bead N38b-46): `frob --json contact soil` for the three cases and
`frob --json contact response` on the one-row system per backend. It logs each sinkage with unit, truth and
tolerance, the bit patterns per backend, ρ and the bound against the true error, and each control's outcome.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 3.52, 9.79, 4.96 cm (KA-837); x̂ = 0.33333334, residual 0, bound 7.9 × 10⁻⁸ (KA-847). Negative
controls: exponent 0.7; bound without ρ. Metamorphic relations: doubling both track widths and the load leaves
p and the k_φ term's share consistent with the formula; scaling H and b by a power of two leaves x̂'s bits
unchanged and scales ρ accordingly. Determinism lanes: `twice`, `backends` (class D1 for row 7).

## Considerations

- Soil constants are illustrative DESIGN values for a fixture; they describe no real soil, and the row makes
  no Reality claim.
- A column that fails the cone or sign tests of the response batch is rerouted to the full solve; this row
  concerns only the error bound attached to an accepted column.
