## Background

A Frontier mechanism of FrankenRobotics lets a thin liquid film on a floor spread and be carried by a robot's
wheels or feet. The film is a thickness h per cell of a floor grid; each face between two cells carries a flux
with two terms: transport by a sliding contact, and gravity spreading in the lubrication limit,
−(g/(3ν))·h_f³·(∇h·n) per unit face length, where g is gravity, ν the kinematic viscosity and h_f³ the *face
mobility* built from the two neighbouring thicknesses.

How h_f³ is formed decides whether a spill spreads at all. The design takes the arithmetic mean of the cubes,
h_f³ = ½·(h_L³ + h_R³), so that a wet cell beside a dry one has a nonzero flux and the front advances. A
harmonic or geometric mean, natural choices for a face coefficient in other diffusion problems, gives zero
whenever one neighbour is dry: the spill never reaches a dry cell, mass is still conserved, and nothing
crashes. The verification subsystem (the Gauntlet) adopts the two-cell case as a known answer under gate
GATE-GAUNT-11 (known answer; every negative control fails), landing at M6 with the mechanism.

## Scope

In: the two-cell mobility row; the front-exponent check; the mutant. Out: the registry record (N38-KA); the
film mechanism, its stability limit and its storage; the film friction law of M3.

## Technical approach

**The row (KA-848).** Two cells with h = (1 mm, 0).
1. Face mobility: ½·((1 mm)³ + 0³) = 0.5 mm³. Exact.
2. Flux sign: ∇h points from the dry to the wet cell, so the gravity term moves volume from wet to dry;
   after one explicit step the dry cell's thickness is strictly positive and the pair's total volume is
   unchanged (what leaves one cell enters the other, one paired statement per face).
3. Front law: on a line of cells with a fixed volume released at one end, the planar front position follows
   t^(1/5) (the classical viscous gravity-current exponent in one dimension; the axisymmetric radius follows
   t^(1/8)). The fitted exponent over the self-similar range must be within 2% of 1/5.

**Tests.**
1. *Host, exact*: the mobility function on (1 mm, 0) in rationals returns 1/2 mm³; on (h, h) it returns h³
   (consistency); it is symmetric in its arguments.
2. *One step on the `strict` tier*: after one update at a step inside the stability bound
   Δt ≤ Δx²/(4·D) with D = g·h³/(3ν), the dry cell holds positive volume; total volume conserved to rounding.
3. *Front exponent on `strict`*: 256 cells, a fixed volume 1 mm deep in the first 8 against a no-flux wall at
   x = 0, water (ν = 1 mm²/s), cell 2 cm. The front is measured by the root-mean-square distance from the wall,
   X_rms(t) = √(Σ hᵢ·xᵢ² / Σ hᵢ) with xᵢ the cell centre (DESIGN, the measure of the film gate's own spreading
   test, N19b-10): for a self-similar profile it is a fixed multiple of the front position and needs no wet–dry
   threshold. The outermost cell with h > 0 is not a front: an explicit update puts some film in every dry cell
   beside a wet one at each step, whatever the law, so the support says nothing about the spreading rate. Fit log X_rms against log t by least squares over a window fixed before the run (DESIGN: 512 s to
   16,384 s, a factor 32 in time over which the front grows by 32^(1/5) = 2; a planning computation of this
   fixture with the explicit update gives X_rms from 0.27 to 0.55 m, the outermost cell above 10⁻³ of the
   peak near 1.1 m, inside the 5.12 m line, and a fitted exponent of 0.199; HYPOTHESIS until the product's
   run records it). Exponent within 2% of 0.2, that is in [0.196, 0.204].

**Negative control (seed CX-397, the catalogue entry on harmonic or geometric face means, N38-CX, M0; kind
`mutation`; gate GATE-GAUNT-11).** A feature in the scene crate replaces the arithmetic mean of cubes by the
harmonic mean (2·h_L³·h_R³/(h_L³ + h_R³), defined as 0 when either is 0): the record CX-397 seeds. A second
variant, the geometric mean (√(h_L³·h_R³)), is a further record with the same seed and its own feature. Under either: test 1 returns 0; in test 2 the dry cell stays exactly dry; in test 3 the front
never moves. `caught` requires the zero flux at the dry face.

**Evidence.** Known answer, class `Tested`, tier G1, sites `host` and `strict`; the mobility value is D0.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: 0.5 mm³ and exponent 1/5 (KA-848; the neighbouring registry row on viscous spreading gives the
exponents 1/8 and 1/5). Negative controls: harmonic mean; geometric mean. Metamorphic relations: a film law at
zero thickness equals the dry case bit for bit and is continuous in thickness; doubling the cell size and
quadrupling time under the diffusion scaling leaves the similarity profile unchanged. Determinism lanes:
`twice`; the film update is class D2 on the batch tiers.
