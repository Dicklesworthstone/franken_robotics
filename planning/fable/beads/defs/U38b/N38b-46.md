## Background

FrankenRobotics' verification subsystem (the Gauntlet) adopts fourteen known answers, each the smallest case
that separates a mechanism from a tempting shortcut, and measures them under two gates of claim kind known
answer:

- **GATE-FID-09** (five corrected fixtures). Every row is reproduced at its tolerance (times to 0.1 s; sinkage
  to 10⁻⁵ m in double precision; counts exact; admissions exact) and every negative control fails. Rows 1, 3
  and 5 at M1; row 2 at M4; row 4 with the continuation lane.
- **GATE-GAUNT-11** (nine late fixtures). Every row is reproduced at its tolerance (single-precision rows
  bit-exact on every backend; rational rows exact; the root row to four significant figures in exact rational
  evaluation of α and γ) and every negative control fails. Each row lands at the milestone of its owner
  section.

The rows are owned by the conformance crate and run under the verbs of their subsystems. This bead is the one
end-to-end script that drives all fourteen from the user's side, by milestone, with each row's planted
shortcut, and produces the receipts from which both gates are recorded in parts.

## Scope

In: the script, its row table, its measured values and log. Out: the row tests and mutants (N38b-39 to
N38b-45); the registry records (N38-KA).

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/adopted_known_answers.sh <M0|M1|M2|M3|M4|M6|M8>` (header: `bead=N38b-46 milestone=M1
labels=gauntlet,gate gates=GATE-FID-09,GATE-GAUNT-11 plants=one-shortcut-per-row`). The argument selects rows
whose milestone is at or before it; rows not yet due are logged `not-due`.

**Steps per due row.** (1) Run the command; parse the envelope. (2) Compare each measured value with its truth
under the row's tolerance (exact, bit pattern, absolute or significant figures). (3) Re-run with the row's
control planted (`--features gauntlet-mutations,<crate>/nc-<id>` for a mutation; the fixture for a planted
input or design) and require the comparison to fail with the shortcut's value. (4) Emit the events below.

**Measured value of each gate part.** (rows reproduced, rows due, controls caught); threshold (due, due, due).
A fresh-context session records the part from the receipt: GATE-FID-09 at M1 (three rows), M4 (one) and M8
(one); GATE-GAUNT-11 at M0 (one), M1 (one), M2 (two), M3 (one), M4 (one), M6 (one), M8 (two).

**Logging.** `target/e2e-logs/adopted_known_answers/<utc>.ndjson` and a transcript. Per row: `cmd` (exact
command, exit code, wall time); `metric` per value (value, unit, truth, tolerance; bit patterns as hex for
single-precision rows, with one line per backend); `assert` for the row and for its control (`kind:
negative-control`, the shortcut's observed value, `caught`); `refusal` where the expected outcome is a
refusal (the view write; the thermal operating point); the envelope's evidence block (claim known-answer,
class, tier, backend, determinism class, identities, seed). The `summary` lists rows passed, failed and
not-due, controls caught, and both gates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With argument M1 the script measures five rows (one of M0, four of M1) and their controls; it exits 0
      only if all are reproduced and all shortcuts fail.
- [ ] Each later argument adds exactly the rows of its milestone; skipped backends are named and never
      counted.
- [ ] A regression in any single row (any of the fourteen shortcuts landing in the product) makes the script
      exit non-zero and names the row and the shortcut's value.
- [ ] The two gates' parts are recorded from this script's receipts by a session that implemented none of the
      mechanisms; no part is recorded for a milestone whose rows did not all run.
- [ ] The log validates against the end-to-end log schema.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

One meta-test, `adopted_rows_table_matches_registry` (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/adopted_rows_host.rs`):
the script's row table, the fourteen registry records and the two gate rows agree on identifiers, gates and
due milestones.

### End-to-end script and logging

As above; selected by `scripts/e2e/run-all.sh --label gate --milestone <M>` and at each milestone exit.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the fourteen rows with the values tabulated. Negative controls: the fourteen shortcuts (sixteen
planted variants, counting both thermal and both film variants). A vacuous variant of the script that skips
step 3 is planted in the harness self-test and must be flagged. Determinism: exact and bit-pattern rows are
compared as such; outcomes are identical twice on one machine and across platforms and backends for the rows
that claim it.

## Considerations

- Two rows (KA-839, KA-841) quote FACTs from the literature and from a recorded planning pilot; the script
  prints "to be re-read" or "re-run on <date>" beside them until that is done, and the recorder refuses the
  gate part while the note is outstanding.
- Rows of Frontier and Moonshot mechanisms are measured only if their mechanisms are funded; otherwise the
  gate part reads `killed` or `not-run` with the reason, and the remaining rows are unaffected.
