## Background

The known-answer suite of FrankenRobotics follows a decision of its verification subsystem (ADR-GAUNT-06):
one fixture identity per physical case. A case stated by a mathematical construction and again by the
subsystem that implements it is one fixture with two rows, because two copies of a fixture's constants are two
chances to disagree. The loader therefore reports rows whose constants are equal and whose fixture names
differ.

The suite's own account of its gaps records the opposite situation, twice: two rows serve *one formula* with
*different illustrative cards*. Both pairs are internally consistent, and the registry keeps both fixtures
under separate identities. This bead makes that explicit so that nobody later "fixes" one row to match the
other, merges the two fixtures, or marks them as the same case.

## Scope

In: the two pairs, their constants and values; registry fields that link rows by formula without merging
fixtures; tests that each card gives its own row's values and that swapping cards fails both rows. Out: the
rows' mechanisms and their own tests; the adopted thermal row of the corrected fixtures (a third card, bead
N38b-40).

## Technical approach

**Pair 1: inelastic closure of a backlash gap.** Formula: a rotor of reflected inertia J₁ = N²·J_r closes the
gap at relative speed Δω (measured at the output) onto a link of inertia J₂ at rest, with no restitution;
common speed after closure J₁·Δω/(J₁ + J₂); energy lost ½·(J₁·J₂/(J₁ + J₂))·Δω²; time to cross a gap β at
rotor speed ω₀ is β·N/ω₀.

| Row | Card | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Actuator chapter (KA-302) | N = 9, J_r = 10⁻⁴ kg·m², J₂ = 0.02 kg·m², Δω = 2 rad/s | J₁ = 0.0081 kg·m²; common speed 0.577 rad/s; energy lost 0.0115 J |
| Contact chapter (KA-403) | Rotor inertia 10⁻⁴ kg·m² at ω₀ = 5 rad/s behind N = 10; gap β = 0.5°; output inertia 0.05 kg·m² | Crossing time 0.5°·(π/180)·10/5 = 17.5 ms; J₁ = 0.01 kg·m², Δω = ω₀/N = 0.5 rad/s; output speed 0.083 rad/s |

**Pair 2: critical current of thermal runaway.** Formula: with winding resistance R₀·(1 + α·(T − T₀)) and
thermal conductance G, no equilibrium exists when I²·R₀·α ≥ G; critical current I_crit = √(G/(R₀·α)).

| Row | Card | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Actuator chapter (KA-308) | G = 0.80 W/K, R₀ = 0.20 Ω, α = 0.00393 K⁻¹ | √(0.80/(0.20·0.00393)) = 31.9 A |
| Actuator construction (KA-185) | G = 0.5 W/K, R₀ = 0.1 Ω, α = 0.0039 K⁻¹ | √(0.5/0.00039) = 35.8 A |

A third card of the same formula is the adopted corrected fixture (25.4 A, 0.54 Ω at 20 °C, 0.00393 K⁻¹,
1/2.2 W/K: s = 1.369 W/K above G, critical current 14.6 A); it has its own identity as well.

**Registry rules (DESIGN).**
1. Each row keeps its own `fixture` name (`backlash-closure/actuator-card`, `backlash-closure/contact-card`,
   `thermal-runaway/chapter-card`, `thermal-runaway/construction-card`) and its own constants block.
2. A new optional field `formula = "inelastic-closure"` or `"thermal-critical-current"` links rows that share a
   formula; the derivation of the formula is one file (`docs/derivations/formulas/<formula>.md`) cited by
   each fixture's own derivation file, which holds only the card and the arithmetic.
3. `same_as` stays empty for these rows: they are not the same physical case and carry no "same fixture" mark.
4. The loader's check for equal constants under different names does not fire (the constants differ); a new
   check `formula_rows_have_distinct_cards` fails if two rows with the same `formula` and different `fixture`
   names have identical constants (then they should be one fixture).
5. Tolerances stay the rows' own (1% for the backlash rows; 0.5% for the chapter's critical current; the
   construction's row has a tolerance still to set).

**Tests.** One formula function per formula, evaluated on each card; a swap test evaluates each row's check
with the other row's card and must fail by more than the tolerance (0.577 against 0.083 rad/s; 31.9 against
35.8 A, a 12% difference).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Four rows carry distinct fixture names, their own constants and a shared `formula` field per pair.
- [ ] Each card reproduces its row's values: 0.577 rad/s and 0.0115 J; 17.5 ms and 0.083 rad/s; 31.9 A;
      35.8 A.
- [ ] Swapping the cards fails each row; merging the fixtures (one constants block for both rows) is rejected
      by the loader's printout check because a row's printed value would change.
- [ ] No row of either pair carries a same-fixture mark, and the count of such marks in the suite stays 14.
- [ ] The formula derivation files exist and are cited by all the cards that use them.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/illustrative_cards.rs`:
- `backlash_card_actuator`: J₁ = 0.0081; 0.0081·2/0.0281 = 0.5765; ½·(0.0081·0.02/0.0281)·4 = 0.01153.
- `backlash_card_contact`: 0.0087266·10/5 = 0.017453 s; 0.01·0.5/0.06 = 0.08333.
- `critical_current_cards`: 31.90 A and 35.81 A; the adopted card gives 14.63 A.
- `swap_fails_rows`: KA-302's check on the contact card gives 0.0833 against 0.577 rad/s; KA-403's check on
  the actuator card gives 0.577 against 0.0833 rad/s; KA-308's check on the construction card gives 35.81
  against 31.90 A (12%, outside 0.5%); KA-185's on the chapter card gives 31.90 against 35.81 A.
- `merged_fixture_rejected`: one constants block for KA-302 and KA-403 changes a printed value and is rejected
  by the printout check.
- `formula_rows_have_distinct_cards`: a planted duplicate card under two names is reported.
- `formula_rows_listed`: on the real registry, `inelastic-closure` lists exactly KA-302 and KA-403 and
  `thermal-critical-current` exactly KA-308 and KA-185, each with its fixture name, values and derivation path
  (the listing the end-to-end script reads).
- `same_fixture_marks_count_is_14`.

### End-to-end script and logging

The registry's own script (`scripts/e2e/known_answer_registry.sh`, N38a-12) validates the loader as a whole;
this bead adds `scripts/e2e/illustrative_cards.sh` (header: `bead=N38b-48 milestone=M1 labels=gauntlet,registry
gates=GATE-GAUNT-02 plants=swapped-cards,merged-fixture,same-fixture-mark`; sources the STD-E2E helper; scratch
copy of the registry). Steps: (1) `cards`: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test illustrative_cards`, one
`metric` per card value (value, unit, truth, tolerance); (2) `formulas`: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test
illustrative_cards -- formula_rows_listed --nocapture` (there is no `frob conformance` command, KNOWN_DRIFTS 1),
which loads `registry/known_answers.toml` and prints, per `formula`, the fixtures that use it with their values
and derivation paths as one JSON line each; one `assert` that
each pair has two distinct `fixture` names and an empty `same_as`, and a `metric` for the suite's count of
same-fixture marks (threshold `== 14`); (3) plants, each expected to fail: the two cards of each pair swapped
(`e2e_expect_caught` per row, with the observed and expected values); one constants block for the backlash pair
(rejected by the printout check); a same-fixture mark added between KA-308 and KA-185 (rejected, the count
would read 15). Log at `target/e2e-logs/illustrative_cards/<utc>.ndjson` with the transcript; every line has
`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines the exact command, exit code and wall time; failed
assertions the row, observed and expected values and the scratch paths; the `summary` with rows checked,
plants caught and GATE-GAUNT-02.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the four rows' values. Negative controls: the swapped cards; a merge of the two fixtures; a
same-fixture mark added between the rows. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- Illustrative cards are DESIGN choices, not measurements of any motor; that is why two can coexist.
- If a later revision deliberately unifies the cards, that is a recorded change: one fixture, two rows, a
  same-fixture mark, and the retired fixture name tombstoned.
