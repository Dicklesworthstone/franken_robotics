## Background

The known-answer suite (KA-001 to KA-835) and the counterexample catalogue (CX-001 to CX-384) of
FrankenRobotics are registries; their printed forms are generated. The suite closes with a table of *coverage
and gaps*: per chapter, how many rows, how many state no negative control, how many have a tolerance still to
set, and how many counterexamples. Those counts are facts about the registry at plan time, and three of them
are obligations: 325 rows with no stated control, 133 with a tolerance to set, and the rows whose truths are
named and not written. Gate GATE-GAUNT-02 requires that the count of rows that are obligations be printed at
every milestone exit and be zero by M3 for rows due by M3.

This bead turns the table into data with assertions, and into a burn-down report that every exit prints. The
point is that a gap cannot close silently (a count changing without a named row) or stay open silently (no
one looking at the count).

## Scope

In: the per-chapter table as a committed expectation file; the meta-tests; the burn-down report and its
milestone thresholds; the list of chapters without rows; the burn-down script `scripts/e2e/ka_obligations.sh`,
whose header this bead owns. Out: closing the gaps and the checks that feed each column of the report: the
"tolerance" column (N38b-27, with its own script), the "control" column (N38b-28, which adds the `controls-*`
steps to this script, and N38b-29 to N38b-34 with their own scripts), the "derivation" column (N38b-47), the
"unreconciled" column (the reconciliation register, N38b-50, and the values N38b-50 to N38b-60); the registry
loader and its totals (N38a-12).

**Chapters without rows.** Three chapters of the design state no known answers (the principles, the reader
table and the goals). DESIGN: this is recorded as a fact with a check, not as a gap to fill by force: the
meta-test asserts that every chapter either has rows or appears in `chapters_without_rows` with a one-line
reason ("states no computable case"). A chapter that later gains a computable statement appends rows.

**Rows adopted after plan time.** The fourteen rows adopted by the verification chapter (reserved KA-836 to
KA-849) and the sixteen catalogue entries it states (reserved CX-385 to CX-400) are recorded as the first
dated addition, each with a control and a tolerance from the start, so they add nothing to the obligations.

**Meta-tests (`tests/coverage_table.rs`).**
1. `per_chapter_counts`: recount rows, `none stated` and `to-set` per chapter from the registry and compare
   with the expectation file; print every differing chapter with the rows that changed.
2. `totals`: 835, 325, 133, 384; tier counts; 14, 13 and 17.
3. `ranges_contiguous`: each chapter's numbers form one contiguous block and blocks do not overlap.
4. `changes_are_dated`: a count may change only together with a new dated line in the expectation file that
   names the rows (an obligation met, a row appended).

**Burn-down report (`target/gauntlet/obligations.json` and a table in every G1 account).** Per chapter and
per due milestone: rows without a control, rows with tolerance to set, rows with a derivation owed, rows
flagged unreconciled. Thresholds: for rows due at or before M3, all four counts are zero at the M3 exit; at
earlier exits the counts are printed and compared with the previous exit (a count that rose without appended
rows fails). The report is computed from the registries alone, so it exists from M0 although most of the beads
that empty its columns land at M1 and M2; each row's entry names the bead that owns its obligation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The expectation file holds the table above and the meta-tests pass on the seeded registries.
- [ ] A planted change of one row from `none stated` to a control without a dated line fails `changes_are_dated`
      and names the row.
- [ ] The burn-down report is produced by the G1 run and by the all-controls run, and is attached to each
      milestone-exit record.
- [ ] At the M3 exit the report shows zero obligations among rows due by M3, or the exit fails GATE-GAUNT-02.
- [ ] Every chapter either has rows or is listed with its reason.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/coverage_table.rs`: the four meta-tests, plus `column_sums` (the 35 chapter
rows sum to 835, 325, 133 and 384; tiers 135, 591, 16, 36, 19, 38); `shared_fixture_marks_are_14` (the fourteen
rows listed above); `chapters_without_rows` (the three listed with a reason; a chapter neither with rows nor
listed is reported); `burn_down_monotone` (a fixture history of three exits with control-column counts 325,
310, 312 and no appended row is rejected at the third; with two appended rows it is accepted);
`zero_by_m3_enforced` (one open row due at M2 in a run set to M3 fails, the same row due at M4 passes);
`obligation_not_counted_as_pass` (a row with a tolerance `to set` offered toward a gate is `not-eligible`);
`adopted_rows_add_no_obligation` (KA-836 to KA-849 each carry a control and a tolerance); `burn_down_report`
(computes the four columns per chapter and due milestone from the registries, writes
`target/gauntlet/obligations.json` with one entry per obligation row naming the bead that owns it, and checks
that each column's total equals the coverage count less the closures named by dated lines; the G1 run and the
all-controls run produce the file through the same function, and N38b-47 and the exit record read it).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/ka_obligations.sh <milestone>` (header: `bead=N38b-49 milestone=M0 labels=gauntlet,registry
gates=GATE-GAUNT-02,GATE-GAUNT-01 plants=undated-count-change,obligation-counted-as-pass,dropped-chapter,boundary-only-control,safe-side-control,control-row-counted`;
the last three plants belong to N38b-28's `controls-*` steps; sources the STD-E2E helper; works on a scratch copy
of the registries). This bead's steps:
1. `coverage`: `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test coverage_table`; one `assert` per chapter (rows, no stated
   control, tolerance to set, CX entries) with observed and expected values, and `metric` lines for the totals
   (835, 325, 133, 384) and tier counts.
2. `burn-down`: the report regenerated by `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test coverage_table --
   burn_down_report` (the test that computes it from the registries and writes
   `target/gauntlet/obligations.json`; there is no `frob conformance` command, KNOWN_DRIFTS 1); one `metric` line
   per chapter and column (`tolerance`,
   `control`, `derivation`, `unreconciled`; unit `rows`) with the threshold for the given milestone (`== 0` for
   rows due by it from M3 on; compared with the previous exit's file before M3, which the log names with its
   digest); the file's digest as a `digest` line.
3. The `controls-*` steps of N38b-28.
4. Plants, each through `e2e_expect_caught`: `undated-count-change` (one row changed from `none stated` to a
   control with no dated line: `changes_are_dated` fails naming it); `obligation-counted-as-pass` (a `to set`
   row offered toward a gate: refused `not-eligible`); `dropped-chapter` (the Spark chapter removed from the
   expectation file: `per_chapter_counts` fails naming it).
Log at `target/e2e-logs/ka_obligations/<utc>.ndjson` with the transcript; every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines the exact command, exit code and wall time; failed assertions the chapter,
column, observed and expected counts, the rows that differ and the scratch paths; the `summary` with the four
column totals, the counts due by the milestone, plants caught, and GATE-GAUNT-01 and GATE-GAUNT-02. Selected at
every milestone exit by `scripts/e2e/run-all.sh --label registry --milestone <M>`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the table and its totals. Negative controls: an undated count change; a row counted as passing
while an obligation; a chapter silently dropped from the table. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- The table describes the registry at plan time; it is not a target. Counts are expected to change, always
  with a dated, row-naming line.
- "Zero by M3 for rows due by M3" is strict: an obligation that cannot be met must be turned into a decision
  (the row withdrawn with a reason, or its due milestone moved by a recorded decision), never left to lapse.
