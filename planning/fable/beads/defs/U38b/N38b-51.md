## Background

FrankenRobotics bounds what a faster physics engine can buy with Amdahl's rule: if the environment step is a
share s of training wall-clock time, an infinitely fast step speeds training by at most 1/(1 − s). The design
quotes an incumbent stack whose step share on a rough-terrain humanoid task is 87% and first printed the
ceiling as "about 7.8×". Recomputed from the stated share, 1/(1 − 0.87) = 7.69. The 7.8 had come from the
unrounded share: nested rates of 94, 88 and 82 thousand environment steps per second give a share of 82/94
and a ceiling of 94/12 = 7.83. The text was corrected to "about 7.7 (1/(1 − 0.87))".

This is one of eleven values that did not reconcile with their own formulas when the known-answer suite was
compiled (register record REC-02; status corrected). It matters because the ceiling is quoted wherever the
program argues that engine speed alone cannot deliver a ten-fold result.

## Scope

In: the register record REC-02 with its stale entry; the derivation file; the truth, tolerance and derivation
reference of the two known-answer rows that carry the figure (KA-010, KA-818); the arithmetic tests on the
record.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- the register mechanism and the scan engine: N38b-50;
- the print-consistency rule for derived figures, the ceiling formatter `format_ceiling` that cannot return
  a string mixing the rounded share with the unrounded ceiling, and the report lint that rejects
  "1/(1 − 0.87) = 7.8": NEG-05, which resolves item E7-2. That bead keeps the mixed line from being printed
  again; this one settles what the rows hold. Item E7-2 of the worked cards' discrepancy register (NEG-03) and
  the pin DX-02 of NAB-41 record the same correction and name REC-02 in their `same_as` fields;
- re-reading the incumbent's rates (the performance program).

## Technical approach

**Formula.** ceiling(s) = 1/(1 − s), s the step share of training wall-clock time.

**Both values.** From the rounded share: s = 0.87 → 100/13 = 7.692. From the raw rates (FACT to re-read):
s = 82/94 = 0.8723 → 94/12 = 7.833. The other figures of the same rows are unaffected: 6.7 at 85%
(1/0.15 = 6.67), 1.9 at 46% (1/0.54 = 1.85), about 1.4 from physics at 29% of the step (1/0.71 = 1.41), and
at most 1.15 from inference and learning at 13% (1/0.87 = 1.149).

**Settlement.**
1. The rows (KA-010 in the thesis chapter; KA-818 in the performance chapter) state their input as s = 87% and
   their truth as the exact rational 100/13, printed "about 7.7", tolerance half a unit of the last printed
   digit (0.05). 7.8 is retired for this input.
2. The derivation file `docs/derivations/amdahl-step-share.md` records both computations and names the primary
   input: the measured rates. When the incumbent's rates are re-read or re-measured on the program's own host,
   the row's input becomes the measured share as an exact ratio and the printed value is regenerated from it;
   until then the row is labelled FACT to re-read.
3. A different figure that happens to be printed "7.8" stays: the cap (410 + t)/t at an agent latency of 60 s
   is 470/60 = 7.83. The retired-value scan is therefore scoped to the step-share context: the record's
   entry, in the scan's common form, is `retired = [{ pattern = "7\\.8", scope = "step share|Amdahl|87 ?%" }]`.
   The figure "7.8 (from the unrounded share 82/94 = 0.872)", which the formatter of NEG-05 prints, is the
   legitimate second value and sits on lines its report marks `stale-as-control: REC-02`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/reconciliations.rs`:
- `rec_02_from_rounded_share`: 1/(1 − 87/100) = 100/13 = 7.6923.
- `rec_02_from_raw_rates`: 1/(1 − 82/94) = 94/12 = 7.8333.
- `rec_02_printed_value_checks`: |7.7 − 7.6923| ≤ 0.05; |7.8 − 7.6923| > 0.05.
- `rec_02_other_figures`: 6.67, 1.85, 1.41, 1.149.
- `rec_02_unrelated_7_8_kept`: (410 + 60)/60 = 7.8333 is not flagged.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reconciliations.sh` (bead N38b-50, on which this bead depends) carries REC-02 once it is settled:
the `register` step logs its `metric` line (printed 7.8, recomputed 7.69, settled 100/13, status corrected); the
`rows` step runs KA-010 and KA-818 with truth 100/13 and tolerance 0.05 (`assert` lines with observed and
expected values); the `scan` step reports zero hits for REC-02's entry and resolves the links from E7-2 and DX-02
when those registers are present (absent ones logged as skipped sources). Its `retired-string` plant is run
for REC-02 as well: "a ceiling of about 7.8 at a step share of 87%" written into a generated page must give one
hit naming REC-02 (and E7-2 and DX-02 where loaded). No script of its own.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 100/13 and 94/12. Negative control: the ceiling computed as 1/s (1.15), which is KA-010's
registered control (N00-33) and KA-818's proposed control (N38b-34) and must fail both rows; for the scan, the
stale "7.8" beside the 87% share. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- Both values are right for their inputs; the defect was a value printed beside an input it was not computed
  from. The settlement pins the input.
- If the re-measured share differs materially from 87%, every argument that cites the ceiling is re-read, not
  only the row.
- The same correction is item E7-2 of the worked cards' register (NEG-03; resolved by NEG-05) and pin DX-02 of
  the killed-idea register (NAB-41). All three state 100/13 for the share 0.87 and 47/6 = 94/12 for the share 82/94; the
  rows named here hold the value and the other two point at REC-02.
