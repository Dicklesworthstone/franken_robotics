## Background

FrankenRobotics bounds what a faster physics engine can buy with Amdahl's rule: if the environment step is a
share s of training wall-clock time, an infinitely fast step speeds training by at most 1/(1 − s). The design
quotes an incumbent stack whose step share on a rough-terrain humanoid task is 87% and first printed the
ceiling as "about 7.8×". Recomputed from the stated share, 1/(1 − 0.87) = 7.69. The 7.8 had come from the
unrounded share: nested rates of 94, 88 and 82 thousand environment steps per second give a share of 82/94
and a ceiling of 94/12 = 7.83. The text was corrected to "about 7.7 (1/(1 − 0.87))".

This is one of eleven values that did not reconcile with their own formulas when the known-answer suite was
compiled (register record REC-02; status corrected). It matters because the ceiling is quoted wherever the
program argues that engine speed alone cannot deliver a ten-fold result.

## Scope

In: the record, the derivation file, the registry values of the two rows that carry the figure, a test. Out:
the register mechanism (N38b-50); re-reading the incumbent's rates (the performance program).

## Technical approach

**Formula.** ceiling(s) = 1/(1 − s), s the step share of training wall-clock time.

**Both values.** From the rounded share: s = 0.87 → 100/13 = 7.692. From the raw rates (FACT to re-read):
s = 82/94 = 0.8723 → 94/12 = 7.833. The other figures of the same rows are unaffected: 6.7 at 85%
(1/0.15 = 6.67), 1.9 at 46% (1/0.54 = 1.85), about 1.4 from physics at 29% of the step (1/0.71 = 1.41), and
at most 1.15 from inference and learning at 13% (1/0.87 = 1.149).

**Settlement.**
1. The rows (KA-010 in the thesis chapter; KA-818 in the performance chapter) state their input as s = 87% and
   their truth as the exact rational 100/13, printed "about 7.7", tolerance half a unit of the last printed
   digit (0.05). 7.8 is retired for this input.
2. The derivation file `docs/derivations/amdahl-step-share.md` records both computations and names the primary
   input: the measured rates. When the incumbent's rates are re-read or re-measured on the program's own host,
   the row's input becomes the measured share as an exact ratio and the printed value is regenerated from it;
   until then the row is labelled FACT to re-read.
3. A different figure that happens to be printed "7.8" stays: the cap (410 + t)/t at an agent latency of 60 s
   is 470/60 = 7.83. The retired-value scan is therefore scoped to the step-share context.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] REC-02 is settled in the register with printed 7.8, recomputed 7.69 and settled 100/13.
- [ ] KA-010 and KA-818 carry the exact rational, the tolerance 0.05 and the derivation file; the tolerance
      column no longer reads `to set` for the ceiling of KA-818.
- [ ] "7.8" does not appear as the ceiling at an 87% share in any generated text.
- [ ] The derivation file states which input is primary and what happens when it is re-read.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/reconciliations.rs`:
- `rec_02_from_rounded_share`: 1/(1 − 87/100) = 100/13 = 7.6923.
- `rec_02_from_raw_rates`: 1/(1 − 82/94) = 94/12 = 7.8333.
- `rec_02_printed_value_checks`: |7.7 − 7.6923| ≤ 0.05; |7.8 − 7.6923| > 0.05.
- `rec_02_other_figures`: 6.67, 1.85, 1.41, 1.149.
- `rec_02_unrelated_7_8_kept`: (410 + 60)/60 = 7.8333 is not flagged.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reconciliations.sh` (bead N38b-50) prints REC-02 with its three values and runs the two rows; no
script of its own.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 100/13 and 94/12. Negative control: the ceiling computed as 1/s (1.15), which is the proposed
control of both rows and must fail them. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- Both values are right for their inputs; the defect was a value printed beside an input it was not computed
  from. The settlement pins the input.
- If the re-measured share differs materially from 87%, every argument that cites the ceiling is re-read, not
  only the row.
