## Background

FrankenRobotics sets a training-time target for its reference quadruped task relative to an incumbent: at most
1.25 times the incumbent's time to the return threshold, cold start, on one RTX 4090. An incumbent stack
publishes about 410 s for that task (a FACT read by eye from a published curve, to be re-measured on the
program's own host). The planning value was first printed as 515 s "as 1.25 times the published 410 s".
Recomputed, 1.25 × 410 = 512.5 s. The text was corrected to 513 s.

This is one of eleven values that did not reconcile with their own formulas when the known-answer suite was
compiled (register record REC-03; status corrected). It is a planning value, not a gate threshold: the binding
gate is the ratio 1.25 against the incumbent re-measured on the same hardware, whatever number of seconds
that gives.

## Scope

In: the record REC-03 with its retired entry, a derivation note, the test that binds the record to the
performance table's row. Out: the register and the scan (N38b-50); the performance targets registry
`registries/perf_targets.toml`, its row 4 and its consistency lint `planning_values_consistent`, which already
checks 1.25 × 410 s = 512.5 s printed 513 s (N36-01, on which this bead depends); the re-measurement decision
(N23b-28) and the gate GATE-CORTEX-05 (BD-218).

## Technical approach

**Formula.** planning_value = ratio × incumbent_time, with ratio = 5/4 and incumbent_time = 410 s (published,
to be re-measured).

**Both values.** Printed first: 515 s. Recomputed: 512.5 s, printed as 513 s (rounded half up to a whole
second). 515 s corresponds to a ratio of 515/410 = 1.256, which is not the stated ratio.

**Settlement.**
1. The performance targets registry (N36-01) holds row 4's ratio 5/4, the incumbent reference (410 s, its
   source and the status "published; to be re-measured") and the planning value 513 s, and its consistency lint
   recomputes the value from the two inputs. DESIGN (correction of this bead's first draft, which proposed a
   second mechanism that generates the value): the registry's lint is the one mechanism; the value is stored
   and checked, and a typed value that disagrees with ratio × time fails the lint.
2. The generated text reads "at most 1.25 times the incumbent reproduced on the program's host; planning value
   513 s (1.25 × 410 s)".
3. When the incumbent is re-measured, the planning value is regenerated from the measured time and the
   published figure is kept as context with its source.
4. No known-answer row is created for it: a planning value is not a truth. The register record keeps 515 as
   retired in this context: `retired = [{ pattern = "515 ?s", scope = "1\\.25|410 ?s|planning value" }]`.

## Acceptance criteria

- `rec_03_row_matches_register`: row 4 of the seeded `perf_targets.toml` gives 513 = settled; a fixture copy

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reconciliations.sh` (bead N38b-50, on which this bead depends) carries REC-03: the `register`

naming REC-03). No script of its own.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: 512.5 s. Negative control: the typed 515. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- Rounding half up loosens a limit by half a second; that is immaterial for a planning value and is why the
  gate reads the ratio.
- The published 410 s was read by eye from a curve; its own uncertainty is larger than the discrepancy. The
  settlement is about consistency, not accuracy.
