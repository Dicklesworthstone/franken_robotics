## Background

A Frontier mechanism of FrankenRobotics stores a liquid film on a floor in tiles of 16 × 16 cells; a tile
holds 1,024 bytes of thickness. One paragraph of the design sized the store with two rounding rules at once:
"352 film tiles for a 6 m × 6 m floor, and 16 tiles (about 16 kB) for 1 m²". Recomputed at a 2 cm cell (tile
side 0.32 m, tile area 0.1024 m²): area over tile area gives 351.6 and 9.8 tiles; counting whole tiles along
each axis gives 19 × 19 = 361 and 4 × 4 = 16. So the first figure used the area ratio and the second used
whole tiles.

This is one of eleven values that did not reconcile with their own formulas when the known-answer suite was
compiled (register record REC-04; status remark: "two rounding rules in one paragraph"). The body text has
since been written with whole tiles (361 tiles, about 370 kB; 16 tiles, about 16 kB), while the known-answer
row for film tile storage still prints 352 tiles and about 360 kB. The row and the text therefore disagree
until this is settled.

## Scope

In: one counting rule; the record; the truth, tolerance and derivation the row takes from the record; a
test. Out: the register (N38b-50); the row itself, which N19-KA enters at M2 from this record (its text keeps it
`to set` until this settlement exists); the film mechanism; the row's negative control (proposed in N38b-32).

## Technical approach

**Formula (one rule: whole tiles).** For an axis-aligned rectangle of sides a and b on a tile grid of side
s = 16·Δx: tiles = ⌈a/s⌉·⌈b/s⌉ when the rectangle is aligned with the grid, and at most
(⌈a/s⌉ + 1)·(⌈b/s⌉ + 1) when it is not. Bytes = 1,024 × tiles.

**Both values.** Area ratio: 36 m²/0.1024 m² = 351.6 (printed 352), 360,448 bytes for 352 tiles; 1 m²/0.1024 m²
= 9.8. Whole tiles: ⌈6/0.32⌉ = 19, so 361 tiles and 369,664 bytes (about 370 kB); ⌈1/0.32⌉ = 4, so 16 tiles
and 16,384 bytes (about 16 kB). Misaligned worst case: 20 × 20 = 400 tiles (409,600 bytes) and 5 × 5 = 25
tiles.

**Settlement.**
1. The row's truth becomes: tile bytes 1,024 (exact); a 6 m × 6 m floor at 2 cm is 361 tiles when aligned and
   at most 400 otherwise; a 1 m × 1 m spill is 16 tiles when aligned and at most 25 otherwise. Tolerance
   `exact` (integers), replacing `to set`.
2. The row's `printed` field keeps "352 tiles, about 360 kB"; 352 is retired as a tile count in this context.
   The area ratio 351.6 is kept in the derivation file as the lower bound on any tiling, not as a count.
3. The derivation file `docs/derivations/film-tile-count.md` states the rule, both computations and the
   worst case; the pool-exhaustion behaviour (a world marked degraded for film when the pool runs out) is
   sized from the worst case.
4. The film store (N19b-08, M6) already counts whole tiles and asserts 361 and 16 in its own test
   (`tile_bytes_and_counts`); the derivation file is the reference both tests cite, so the product and the
   conformance row cannot drift to two rules again. The retired entries are
   `retired = [{ pattern = "352 ?(film )?tiles", scope = "6 ?m|film|floor" }, { pattern = "360 ?kB", all_of = ["tile"] }]`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/reconciliations.rs`:
- `rec_04_area_ratio`: 36/0.1024 = 351.5625; 1/0.1024 = 9.765625.
- `rec_04_whole_tiles`: ⌈18.75⌉² = 361; ⌈3.125⌉² = 16.
- `rec_04_bytes`: 361 × 1,024 = 369,664; 352 × 1,024 = 360,448; 16 × 1,024 = 16,384.
- `rec_04_worst_case`: 400 and 25; a property test over 10³ random offsets of the rectangle on the grid never
  exceeds the worst case and never goes below the area ratio.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reconciliations.sh` (bead N38b-50, on which this bead depends) carries REC-04: the `register`
step logs its `metric` line (printed 352 and 16, recomputed 351.6 and 9.8 by area and 361 and 16 by whole
tiles, settled 361 and 16 aligned and 400 and 25 worst case, status remark); the `rows` step runs KA-445 with
its exact truths (`assert` lines with observed and expected counts and bytes; until N19-KA has entered the row
at M2, an `e2e_skip` naming N19-KA, never a pass); the `scan` step
reports zero hits for REC-04's entries, and the `retired-string` plant is run for it ("352 film tiles for a
6 m × 6 m floor" in a generated page gives one hit naming REC-04). No script of its own.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 361 and 16. Negative controls: a tile read as 8 × 8 cells (four times the count); the area
ratio used as a count for a misaligned floor (it undercounts). Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- The mechanism is Frontier and may be killed at its pilot; the arithmetic is settled regardless, so that the
  pilot's own memory estimate uses one rule.
- Wet area is rarely the whole floor; the count is an upper sizing figure, not a typical one.
