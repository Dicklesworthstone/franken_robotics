## Background

FrankenRobotics enumerates the verdicts a frozen fixed-n decision rule can return before any sampling. For the
rule of its first fixture, with paired binary outcomes and a ±5 point indifference band, the construction that
defines the statistics states: perfect concordance at n = 64 gives the interval [−0.0662, +0.0662], so no
outcome can return a tie, and a tie is first reachable at n = 86.

Recomputed, those numbers reconcile only with two-sided 97.5% exact intervals: 1 − 0.0125^(1/64) = 0.0662. At
the more usual 95% the bound is 1 − 0.025^(1/64) = 0.0560 and the first reachable tie is at n = 72. This is
not an error in the numbers: the construction omits the level that the fixture's own section states. It is
one of eleven values recorded as not reconciling when the known-answer suite was compiled (register record
REC-10; status remark). It matters because "a tie is unreachable at 64 pairs" is the program's standing
example of why reachability must be enumerated, and a reader who assumes 95% gets a different table.

## Scope

In: the record; the level made explicit wherever the numbers appear; the pre-analysis taking the level as a
parameter; tests at both levels. Out: the register (N38b-50); the enumeration tables' self-test (N38b-14).

## Technical approach

**Formula.** With zero discordant pairs among n, the exact (Clopper–Pearson) upper limit of each one-sided
discordance rate at tail probability t is 1 − t^(1/n). A two-sided interval at confidence 1 − γ uses t = γ/2.
The difference of the two rates then lies in [−(1 − t^(1/n)), +(1 − t^(1/n))]. A tie verdict requires that
interval inside [−0.05, +0.05], that is n ≥ ln t/ln 0.95.

**Both readings.**

| Interval level | t | Bound at n = 64 | Least n with a reachable tie |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two-sided 97.5% (as the fixture states) | 0.0125 | 0.0662 | ⌈85.43⌉ = 86 |
| Two-sided 95% (the tempting default) | 0.025 | 0.0560 | ⌈71.92⌉ = 72 |

**Settlement.**
1. The construction's row and specification gain the level in their case text: "two-sided 97.5% exact
   intervals for each rate (tail probability 0.0125), ±5 point band". No number changes.
2. `eval.preanalyze` (N24b-06, the verb's owner) takes the level as `--level α`, an exact rational with
   default 0.05, and uses tail t = α/4, so that each rate's interval is two-sided 1 − α/2: α = 0.05 is the
   fixture's 97.5% (t = 0.0125) and α = 0.1 is the tempting 95% (t = 0.025). Its record `frob.preanalysis/1`
   carries α (an output field `level` beside `concordance_interval`, so that the record's hash covers it; the
   schema is N24b-06's and the field is asked of it), and every bound or reachability count it prints carries
   the per-interval level beside it; a
   frozen study plan carries the pre-analysis record, so the level is part of the frozen rule. DESIGN
   (correction of this bead's first draft, which asked for a required parameter in confidence form,
   `--level 0.975` or `0.95`, with no default): the verb is N24b-06's, and two forms would make `--level 0.95`
   mean different things in two beads; what the first draft wanted, that no number appears without its level,
   is kept by the printing rule and by item 3.
3. The registry rows (the construction's row on reachability at n = 64; the fixture's enumeration row) carry
   `level = "two-sided 97.5%"` in their case, so that the truth [−0.0662, +0.0662] and "first tie at 86" are
   functions of stated inputs.
4. The derivation file `docs/derivations/fixed-n-reachability.md` shows both readings, so that a reader who
   recomputes at 95% finds the explanation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] REC-10 is settled in the register as a remark with both readings and the statement that the level is
      now explicit.
- [ ] Both rows state the level; the specification of the statistics construction states it.
- [ ] `frob eval preanalyze --rule paired-clopper-pearson/1 --level 0.05 --margin 0.05 --json` reports no
      reachable `practical-tie` at n = 64 and at n = 85 and a reachable one at n = 86; with `--level 0.1` the
      tie is unreachable at n = 71 and reachable at n = 72; from n = 64, `first_reachable` gives 86 under
      `practical-tie` at level 0.05 and 72 at level 0.1; each record carries its α and the printed bounds
      (0.0662 and 0.0560 at n = 64) carry the per-interval level.
- [ ] No generated text prints 0.0662 or "86 pairs" without the level beside it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/reconciliations.rs`:
- `rec_10_bounds`: 1 − 0.0125^(1/64) = 0.06618; 1 − 0.025^(1/64) = 0.05601.
- `rec_10_first_tie`: ln 0.0125/ln 0.95 = 85.43 → 86; ln 0.025/ln 0.95 = 71.92 → 72.
- `rec_10_tie_at_86_only_for_zero_zero`: at n = 86 and 97.5% the only count pair returning a tie is (0, 0),
  whose chance at 5% discordance each way is 0.9⁸⁶ = 1.16 × 10⁻⁴.
- `rec_10_alpha_to_tail`: α = 1/20 gives t = 1/80 = 0.0125; α = 1/10 gives t = 1/40 = 0.025 (N24b-06's t = α/4).
- `preanalysis_records_level`: a `frob.preanalysis/1` record without α is rejected; the printout of a bound
  without its level beside it fails the print check.
- `rec_10_level_printed`: in generated text, every occurrence of 0.0662, 0.0560, "86 pairs" or "72 pairs" in a
  tie or concordance context has "97.5%", "95%" or "α =" within the 200-character window of N38b-50's engine
  (the same collapsed-text window, applied as a required context rather than a retired value); a fixture page
  without it is reported naming REC-10, one with it passes.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reconciliations.sh` (bead N38b-50, on which this bead depends) carries REC-10: the `register`
step logs its `metric` line (printed 0.0662 and 86 without a level, recomputed 0.0662 and 86 at two-sided 97.5%
and 0.0560 and 72 at 95%, status remark); the `rows` step runs KA-090 and KA-612 with their levels and calls
`frob eval preanalyze --rule paired-clopper-pearson/1 --n <n> --level <α> --margin 0.05 --json` at (α, n) =
(0.05, 64), (0.05, 85), (0.05, 86), (0.1, 71) and (0.1, 72), logging per call the exact command, exit code,
wall time, the envelope's evidence block and the reachable verdict set, with an `assert` on whether
`practical-tie` is in it. Nothing is retired by REC-10 (both readings are right for their levels), so the
stale-value scan carries no entry for it; instead the step runs `rec_10_level_printed` over the generated text
and, as a plant, writes "a tie is first reachable at 86 pairs" with no level within 200 characters into a
generated page, which must be reported naming REC-10. No script of its own.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 0.0662 and 86; 0.0560 and 72. Negative controls: a request for a tie verdict at n = 64 (must be
refused); a pre-analysis by normal approximation (reports a reachable tie at 64). Determinism: D0 (exact
enumeration).

## Considerations

- Why 97.5%: two rates are bounded at once, and the fixture chose to spend the 5% across both intervals; the
  choice is the fixture's and is now printed with it.
- Any later change of level is a change of the frozen rule, which is a new study, never an edit.
