## Background

The known-answer suite of FrankenRobotics carries each value as its chapter prints it; values are not improved
in transit. While the suite was compiled every hand-checkable value was recomputed, and eleven did not
reconcile with their own formulas. The suite lists them in a reconciliation table. This is open question
OPEN-GAUNT-6 of the verification subsystem (the Gauntlet): **reconciliation of the values listed in that
table.** Owner: the lead, with the owning chapters. Blocks: any gate that cites one of the values. Fail-closed
default: the row keeps the printed value and is flagged; the gate cites the derivation file.

Seven of the eleven have been corrected in the design (rows 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 9) and four stand as remarks
(rows 4, 8, 10 and 11). None changes a verdict of a fixture. Each has its own bead that states both values,
the formula and the settlement. This bead is the decision that closes the question: it checks that all eleven
are settled in the register, that every affected row is unflagged with a derivation file, and that the retired
values are gone from generated text; then it records the closure.

## Scope

In: the closure checklist; the summary table; the decision record; the standing rule for future
discrepancies. Out: the eleven settlements (N38b-50 to N38b-60); tolerances and controls owed by other rows.

**Closure checklist (run by `tests/reconciliations.rs -- closure`).**
1. Every register record has `status` in {corrected, remark}, a filled `settled` block and, where it names
   known-answer rows, a derivation file that lists them.
2. No known-answer row is `flagged: unreconciled`.
3. The retired-value scan finds no retired string outside the generated reconciliation table.
4. Every gate that cites an affected row cites its derivation file.
5. Each owning chapter's confirmation is recorded in the register (`confirmed_by`, a session that did not write
   the settlement).

**Decision record.** `docs/decisions/OPEN-GAUNT-6.md`: the table above with the register digests, the
checklist's output, and the closing statement. The question is then tombstoned with the decision's identifier
and is not reused.

**Standing rule for later discrepancies (DESIGN).** A recomputation that disagrees with a printed value never
edits the value in place. It appends a register record (`status = "open"`), flags the rows, and blocks the
gates that cite them until a settlement bead closes it. The printed reconciliation table is regenerated from
the register, so the history of every corrected number stays visible.

**Default if not closed by the M1 exit.** Rows of unsettled records keep their printed values and stay
flagged; they are not counted by GATE-GAUNT-02; the gates that cite them read the derivation file where one
exists and are otherwise `blocked` on this question.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All eleven records are settled and confirmed; the closure checklist passes.
- [ ] No row is flagged unreconciled; the count of flagged rows printed by the known-answer account is zero.
- [ ] The decision record exists and the question is tombstoned.
- [ ] A planted new discrepancy (a row's printed value changed without a record) is caught and reopens the
      checklist.
- [ ] If the question is not closed at the M1 exit, the default is recorded and the affected gates show
      `blocked: OPEN-GAUNT-6`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/reconciliations.rs`: `closure_all_settled`; `closure_no_flagged_rows`;
`closure_retired_scan_clean`; `closure_gates_cite_derivations`; `closure_confirmed_by_other_session`;
`new_discrepancy_reopens` (a fixture registry with a twelfth, open record fails closure).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/reconciliations.sh closure` (the script of bead N38b-50 with the closure argument): runs every
record's tests, the scan and the checklist, and prints the summary table. Log: one `metric` line per record
(status, settled value, confirmed), the count of flagged rows (threshold 0), the checklist assertions, and the
`summary` naming GATE-GAUNT-02 and the decision identifier.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the eleven settled values (1.46; 7.7; 513 s; 361 and 16; 188.7 GiB; 616; 76,959; 29,955 as the
control; 1 to 3%; 0.0662 at 97.5%; 0.64 for 13 bisections). Negative controls: a retired value reintroduced
into a generated page; a record marked settled without a derivation file; a settlement confirmed by its own
author. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- The seven corrections are already in the design's text; the work here is making the registry, the
  derivation files and the generated documents agree, and keeping the first printed values on record.
- A twelfth discrepancy found during implementation follows the standing rule; it is not a reason to reopen the
  eleven.
- Several corrected inputs are FACTs to re-read or re-measure (the incumbent's rates, the published training
  time, the working-set limit); re-reading them may move a settled value again, each time by a dated record.
