## Background

FrankenRobotics distinguishes seven kinds of claim. *Reality* (the simulation agrees with physical measurement)
is established only by imported real-robot data, which the program does not yet have. The verification
subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`) keeps four duties toward the Reality lane, which belongs to
the calibration subsystem (Ground). Two of them fall due at M3:

- **The label.** A comparison with a published figure for the same robot (a published walking speed, a cost of
  transport, a payload) is scheduled by the Gauntlet at M3 and recorded as Parity with status
  `published-anchor`, never as Reality: the published figure is its authors' measurement under their stated
  conditions, and agreeing with it transfers no physical evidence.
- **The share of guesses.** A report that supports a benchmark claim prints the share of card fields whose
  provenance is `generic`, `assumed` or `synthetic`, because agreement between tiers cannot reveal a wrong card:
  every tier consumes the same card.

These checks were first carried by N38b-01 (M1) and their end-to-end steps by N38b-02 (M1), which could not
depend on the M3 mechanisms (the anchor comparison BD-311, the share computation N30a-03). This bead carries
them at M3 (split in polishing round 1), on the conformance target, the anchor schedule format and the script
that N38b-01 and N38b-02 created.

## Scope

In: the M3 records of the anchor schedule `registry/anchors.toml` (at least one per reference robot that has a
published figure); the run half of check 2, `anchor_is_parity`; check 4, `share_of_guesses_printed`, with its
planted violations; steps 4 (`anchor`) and 5 (`share`) of `scripts/e2e/reality_lane.sh`; their negative
controls and log lines.

Out, with the bead that has it: the conformance target, the schedule's format and loader, the type and loader
half of check 2, the line and the flag checks (N38b-01); the script's first form and steps 1 to 3 (N38b-02); the
comparison itself (BD-311); the share computation and the provenance check (N30a-03); the rehearsal check and
step 6 (BD-411); the first real dataset (N38b-26).

## Technical approach

**Anchor records.** One `[[anchor]]` per scheduled comparison in `registry/anchors.toml`, in N38b-01's format:
`id`, `robot` (a reference robot with a public model), `quantity`, `published` (value, unit, source, date read,
conditions as stated by the source), `fixture` (the scenario that reproduces the stated conditions),
`tolerance`, `due = "M3"`, `result` (filled by BD-311's comparison: the simulated interval with its median over
the sweep, the published value, the signed gap, the conditions matched and assumed, and the outcome label
`inside-sweep` when |gap| ≤ `tolerance`, otherwise `outside-sweep(gap)`, a finding and never a gate verdict).
Every reference robot with a published figure gets at least one record; a reference robot without one is listed
with the reason, never silently skipped.

**Check 2, run half (`anchor_is_parity`, due M3).** For every record the stored result has claim kind `parity`,
status `published-anchor`, evidence class `Estimated`, its source, its stated conditions and its outcome label;
every report statement that cites it prints `reality: published-anchor [source=<anchor key>]` followed by the one
sentence BD-311 generates from the record ("Compared with a published figure (<citation>: <published value>
<unit>); simulated <min> to <max> <unit> over <k> assumed conditions; <outcome>; published conditions are
incomplete; this is a Parity result and says nothing about a band.", with <outcome> `inside-sweep` or
`outside-sweep by <gap> <unit>`). The check asserts that form and that no fixed wording such as "matches
<source>" appears, which would be false on `outside-sweep`. Planted violation: a hand-written result offering
the anchor as Reality (refused by the loader of N38b-01).

**Check 4 (`share_of_guesses_printed`, due M3).** Every report that supports a benchmark claim prints, directly
under its `reality:` line, the line of N30a-03 (printed by BD-139): `cards: guessed=<g>/<N> (<pct>%)
[generic=<a> assumed=<b> synthetic=<f> datasheet=<c> measured=<d> candidate=<e>]`, with g = a + b + f over every
field with a provenance of every card bound into the model identity (the nine card kinds: actuator,
transmission, battery, interface, material, soil, optical, sensor, payload), `<pct>` with one decimal, and the
six columns summing to N (the form N38b-01's check 4 registers). The check enumerates the report verbs that
support a benchmark claim, runs each on its smallest benchmark fixture, and recomputes every column from the
cards' provenance fields. Planted violations: a benchmark report with the line omitted; a card whose `assumed`
field is relabelled `measured` without a dataset (the provenance check of N30a-03 must refuse it).

**Script steps (added to the step table of `scripts/e2e/reality_lane.sh`).**
4. `anchor` (M3): run the published-anchor comparison for each scheduled record through BD-311's entry points
   (`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g2_parity -- anchor`, and per record
   `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example anchors -- run <id> --seed <seed> --json`); assert the stored result as in
   check 2, its outcome label, and BD-311's generated sentence under the `reality: published-anchor` line.
   Plant: submit a hand-written result offering the anchor as Reality; expect refusal by the loader.
5. `share` (M3): `frob report design <design> --json` on a benchmark design; assert the line `cards:
   guessed=<g>/<N> (<pct>%) [generic=… assumed=… synthetic=… datasheet=… measured=… candidate=…]` under the
   `reality:` line and recompute every column from the cards' provenance fields. Plant: the report template
   with the line omitted; expect the check to exit non-zero.

**Logging** (the script's log `target/e2e-logs/reality_lane/<utc>.ndjson`, through the shared helper). `cmd`
events with exact command, exit code and wall time; per anchor an `assert` event with the stored claim kind,
status, source, pin, tolerance and the measured difference; per report the share as printed against the share as
recomputed (threshold: equal); `refusal` events with code and message for each plant; the evidence block of each
envelope (claim, class, tier, determinism, identities, seed, reality); the `summary` naming GATE-GROUND-01 and
GATE-GAUNT-05.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At M3 the schedule holds at least one anchor per reference robot that has a published figure, each run and
      recorded as Parity with its source and conditions; robots without one are listed with the reason.
- [ ] No stored anchor result has claim kind `reality`; the hand-written Reality record is refused.
- [ ] Every benchmark report prints the share of guessed card fields, and the count recomputes from the cards.
- [ ] The omitted share and the relabelled `assumed` field are each caught.
- [ ] `reality_lane.sh M3` runs steps 1 to 5 and reports step 6 `not-due`.
- [ ] Each check carried here has a registered negative control that it catches.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

In `crates/frob-conformance/tests/reality_lane.rs`, on real verbs and fixtures:
- `anchor_is_parity_run`: every M3 record's stored result is `parity`, `published-anchor`, with source and
  conditions.
- `anchors_cover_reference_robots`: each reference robot with a published figure has a record; one without is
  listed with its reason.
- `anchor_sentence_printed`: the report carries BD-311's sentence with citation, published value, simulated
  interval, count of assumed conditions and outcome; on an `outside-sweep` record it reads `outside-sweep by
  <gap> <unit>`, and a template printing "matches <source>" is rejected.
- `share_of_guesses_printed`: every benchmark report verb prints the line under its `reality:` line.
- `share_arithmetic`: a result binding 12 card fields, 5 `generic`, 2 `assumed`, 3 `datasheet` and 2
  `measured`, prints `cards: guessed=7/12 (58.3%) [generic=5 assumed=2 synthetic=0 datasheet=3 measured=2
  candidate=0]`; with one `datasheet` field replaced by a `synthetic` one it prints `guessed=8/12 (66.7%)`.
- `share_recomputes_from_cards`: every printed column equals a recount over the nine card kinds bound into the
  model identity.
- `share_omitted_caught`; `assumed_relabelled_measured_refused`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Steps 4 and 5 of `scripts/e2e/reality_lane.sh` (header plants gain `anchor-as-reality,omit-share`), run with
`M3` and at every later exit; logged as above.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the share arithmetic (`guessed=7/12 (58.3%)`, and `8/12 (66.7%)` with a synthetic field); each
anchor's published value with its unit. Negative
controls: the anchor offered as Reality; the omitted share; the `assumed` field relabelled `measured`.
Live-dependency proof: changing one card field's provenance from `assumed` to `measured` (with a dataset)
changes the printed share. Determinism: D0 for the checks; anchor runs at the comparison's declared class.

## Considerations

- Published test conditions are loose; an anchor comparison is reported with the conditions as the source states
  them and with nothing inferred.
- Through M4 the card catalogue holds generic classes only, so the printed share will be high; that is the
  honest number.
- An anchor that misses its tolerance is a recorded Parity result, not a defect of the check.
