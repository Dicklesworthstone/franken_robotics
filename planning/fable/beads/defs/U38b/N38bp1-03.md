## Background

FrankenRobotics supports a claimed failure rate such as 10⁻⁴ with a confidence sequence computed from weighted
sampling (the certification lane of the adversarial-search subsystem, Nemesis, verb `nemesis.bound`) and
explores rare failures by multilevel splitting on forked episodes (its exploration lane). Weights are bounded by
1/ε through a defensive mixture, so that one sample cannot dominate the estimate. These estimators issue
verdicts that later work cites, so the verification subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`)
self-tests them as a *certifier* in tier G0 under gate GATE-GAUNT-04 (part due at M3 for the lanes): the
estimator must recover truths known in closed form, and deliberately broken variants must fail the same test.

This row was first carried by N38b-14, an M1 bead that could not depend on the M3 estimators (BD-276, BD-277);
it is split out here at the milestone of its mechanism, with its content unchanged.

## Scope

In: the register row CERT-13 with its threshold and broken-certifier controls; the two closed-form truths; the
coverage study; the three broken variants. Out: the estimators themselves (BD-276 certification lane, BD-277

## Technical approach

**CERT-13 Rare-event bounds (G0 entry `cert:rare-event@host`).**
- Gaussian tail by defensive mixture: P(z > 4) = 3.167 × 10⁻⁵ for a standard normal (½·erfc(4/√2)); proposal
  shifted by 4; mixture share ε = 0.1 of the nominal law, so every weight is at most 1/ε = 10.
- Splitting on a biased random walk: the probability of reaching level 20 before level 0 from level 1 with
  up-probability 0.4 is (1.5 − 1)/(1.5²⁰ − 1) = 0.5/(1.5²⁰ − 1) = 1.504 × 10⁻⁴ (ratio q/p = 0.6/0.4 = 1.5).
- Threshold (TARGET): over repeated studies with known p, the truth lies inside the confidence sequence in at
  least the stated share of repetitions (the 95% upper bound covers p in at least 95% of 10⁴ synthetic
  studies), and every weight is at most 1/ε.

**Broken certifiers (controls).**
1. A mixture without its nominal share on a mis-centred proposal: weights unbounded, coverage fails.
2. A walk with no noise after the fork (identical children): must be refused (existing refusal FROB-REF-569).
3. A zero-failure episode count one short of the exact rule of N38b-57: the bound it reports is caught as below
   the stated confidence.

**Register row.** `id = "CERT-13"`, owner crate `frob-nemesis`, owner gates the GATE-NEMESIS gates of §26.15
(GATE-NEMESIS-04 among them), `due = "M3"`, site `host`, `broken` naming the three controls above (each an NC
record of kind `mutation` naming GATE-GAUNT-04).

**Evidence.** Class `Tested`, kind known answer; inputs `synthetic`; tier G0, site `host`; determinism D0 given
the seed (address-keyed draws).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both truths lie inside their confidence sequences at the stated share over 10⁴ synthetic studies.
- [ ] The largest weight observed is at most 10.
- [ ] The three broken variants are caught or refused (the noiseless fork with FROB-REF-569).
- [ ] The row is in the certifier register with its controls, `due = "M3"`, and is read by GATE-GAUNT-04.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cert_evaluators.rs`:
- `gaussian_tail_value`: ½·erfc(4/√2) = 3.167 × 10⁻⁵ to four significant figures.
- `walk_value`: 0.5/(1.5²⁰ − 1) = 1.504 × 10⁻⁴, with 1.5²⁰ = 3,325.26 computed in the test.
- `weights_bounded_by_ten`: every weight of the mixture run ≤ 1/ε = 10.
- `coverage_over_studies`: 10⁴ studies, seed 0; the coverage share is reported against the stated share.
- `mis_centred_mixture_control`: without the nominal share the largest weight exceeds 10 and coverage fails.
- `noiseless_fork_refused`: identical children are refused with FROB-REF-569.
- `zero_failure_one_short_control`: the count one short of N38b-57's exact rule is caught.

### End-to-end script and logging

Measured by `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M3` (bead N38b-17): `frob nemesis bound` on the Gaussian law, and the
exploration lane's splitting run (BD-277) on the walk. Logged per law: the true value, the bound or interval,
coverage share against its threshold, the largest weight against 10, each broken variant's outcome and the
refusal code FROB-REF-569, the evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, seed).

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 3.167 × 10⁻⁵; 1.504 × 10⁻⁴. Negative controls: the mixture without nominal share; the noiseless
fork; the zero-failure count one short. Metamorphic relations: none registered for this row (the known answers
are closed-form). Determinism: identical counts twice on one machine for a fixed seed.

## Considerations

- A certified failure rate of 10⁻⁶ needs a compute class above the default; the default certifies to 10⁻⁵.
- 10⁴ studies of the closed-form laws are cheap at the level of test statistics; when run through the real verb
  the count may be reduced at night and is printed.
