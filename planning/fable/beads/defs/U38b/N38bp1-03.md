## Background

FrankenRobotics supports a claimed failure rate such as 10⁻⁴ with a confidence sequence computed from weighted
sampling (the certification lane of the adversarial-search subsystem, Nemesis, verb `nemesis.bound`) and
explores rare failures by multilevel splitting on forked episodes (its exploration lane). Weights are bounded by
1/ε through a defensive mixture, so that one sample cannot dominate the estimate. These estimators issue
verdicts that later work cites, so the verification subsystem (the Gauntlet, crate `frob-conformance`)
self-tests them as a *certifier* in tier G0 under gate GATE-GAUNT-04 (part due at M3 for the lanes): the
estimator must recover truths known in closed form, and deliberately broken variants must fail the same test.

This row was first carried by N38b-14, an M1 bead that could not depend on the M3 estimators (BD-276, BD-277);
it is split out here at the milestone of its mechanism, with its content unchanged.

## Scope

In: the register row CERT-13 with its threshold and broken-certifier controls; the two closed-form truths; the
coverage study; the one broken variant that has no catalogue plant. Out: the estimators themselves (BD-276
certification lane, BD-277 exploration lane); the two broken variants that are catalogue plants (CX-291 and
CX-292, N26-CX, whose seeded records are reused); the exact zero-failure rule (N38b-56's reference, N26a-21's
product function) and the undercount controls of KA-664 (N38b-57); the register and the two-halves rule
(N38b-10); the end-to-end run (N38b-17).

## Technical approach

**CERT-13 Rare-event bounds (G0 entry `cert:rare-event@host`).**
- Gaussian tail by defensive mixture: P(z > 4) = 3.167 × 10⁻⁵ for a standard normal (½·erfc(4/√2)); proposal
  shifted by 4; mixture share ε = 0.1 of the nominal law, so every weight is at most 1/ε = 10.
- Splitting on a biased random walk: the probability of reaching level 20 before level 0 from level 1 with
  up-probability 0.4 is (1.5 − 1)/(1.5²⁰ − 1) = 0.5/(1.5²⁰ − 1) = 1.504 × 10⁻⁴ (ratio q/p = 0.6/0.4 = 1.5).
- Threshold (TARGET): over repeated studies with known p, the truth lies inside the confidence sequence in at
  least the stated share of repetitions, tested as N38b-10 tests CERT-03 so that an exactly calibrated bound
  does not fail by sampling noise: the 95% upper bound misses p in at most 551 of 10⁴ synthetic studies (an
  exact one-sided binomial test of a miss rate at or below 5% rejects at the 1% level from 552); and every
  weight is at most 1/ε.

**Broken certifiers (controls).** One plant, one record (BD-025): a defect the catalogue already plants is
named by its seeded record, not registered again.
1. A mixture without its nominal share on a mis-centred proposal (ε = 0, centred at −4 instead of +4): weights
   unbounded, coverage fails. CX-291's plant; its seeded record is reused.
2. Forked children keyed by the parent's address, so that they replay the parent identically (build feature
   `nemesis-fork-same-key`): the splitting estimate on the walk misses 1.504 × 10⁻⁴. CX-292's plant; its seeded
   record is reused. A request in which no randomness enters after the fork is refused with FROB-REF-569 on a
   correct build; that refusal is a known answer, not a control, since it passes on a correct build (N38b-28,
   `boundary-not-control`).
3. A zero-failure episode count one short of the exact rule in the product function `required` of N26a-21
   (29,955 at δ = 0.05, p⋆ = 10⁻⁴): the bound it reports is caught as below the stated confidence. No catalogue
   plant targets the product's count (N38b-57's plants are in the conformance reference), so this bead
   registers it: seed `CERT-13`, kind `mutation`, feature `nc-<nnnn>` of `frob-nemesis`, `due` M3.

**Register row.** `id = "CERT-13"`, owner crate `frob-nemesis`, owner gates the GATE-NEMESIS gates of §26.15
(GATE-NEMESIS-04 among them), `due = "M3"`, site `host`, `broken` naming the three records above, each of kind
`mutation` and naming GATE-GAUNT-04 (the two seeded ones get that gate from their plants' gates in N26-CX,
since the seeding tool only adds gates).

**Evidence.** Class `Tested`, kind known answer; inputs `synthetic`; tier G0, site `host`; determinism D0 given
the seed (address-keyed draws).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both truths lie inside their confidence sequences at the stated share over 10⁴ synthetic studies.
- [ ] The largest weight observed is at most 10.
- [ ] The three broken variants are caught; a request with no randomness after the fork is refused with
      FROB-REF-569 on a correct build.
- [ ] The row is in the certifier register with its controls, `due = "M3"`, and is read by GATE-GAUNT-04.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`crates/frob-conformance/tests/cert_evaluators.rs`:
- `gaussian_tail_value`: ½·erfc(4/√2) = 3.167 × 10⁻⁵ to four significant figures.
- `walk_value`: 0.5/(1.5²⁰ − 1) = 1.504 × 10⁻⁴, with 1.5²⁰ = 3,325.26 computed in the test.
- `weights_bounded_by_ten`: every weight of the mixture run ≤ 1/ε = 10.
- `coverage_over_studies`: 10⁴ studies, seed 0; misses ≤ 551, reported with the coverage share.
- `mis_centred_mixture_control`: without the nominal share the largest weight exceeds 10 and coverage fails.
- `fork_same_key_control` (built with `nemesis-fork-same-key`): identical children, and the walk's estimate
  misses 1.504 × 10⁻⁴.
- `noiseless_fork_refused` (known answer, correct build): a request with no randomness after the fork is
  refused with FROB-REF-569.
- `zero_failure_one_short_control`: the product's count one short of the exact rule (29,955) is caught.

### End-to-end script and logging

Measured by `scripts/e2e/certifiers.sh M3` (bead N38b-17): `frob nemesis bound` on the Gaussian law, and the
exploration lane's splitting run (BD-277) on the walk. Logged per law: the true value, the bound or interval,
coverage share against its threshold, the largest weight against 10, each broken variant's outcome and the
refusal code FROB-REF-569, the evidence block (claim, class, tier, determinism, seed).

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 3.167 × 10⁻⁵; 1.504 × 10⁻⁴; FROB-REF-569 for a fork with no randomness after it. Negative
controls: the mixture without nominal share (CX-291); children keyed by the parent's address (CX-292); the
product's zero-failure count one short. Metamorphic relations: none registered for this row (the known answers
are closed-form). Determinism: identical counts twice on one machine for a fixed seed.

## Considerations

- A certified failure rate of 10⁻⁶ needs a compute class above the default; the default certifies to 10⁻⁵.
- 10⁴ studies of the closed-form laws are cheap at the level of test statistics; when run through the real verb
  the count may be reduced at night and is printed.
