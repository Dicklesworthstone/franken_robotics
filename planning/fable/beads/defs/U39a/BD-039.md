## Background

FrankenRobotics claims that the time from a robot-design question to an independently accepted answer is ten
times shorter than with the strongest composed rival. The rival is built from incumbent simulators and learning
stacks. A hygiene rule of the program says: **the incumbent is measured before a mechanism is funded against
it**. Versions are pinned, the strongest warm configuration is recorded, and the phase ledger of the incumbent
loop is taken on the program's Linux host `trj` (two RTX 4090 with 24 GB each and no link between them) before
any ratio is written. The reason is that no end-to-end phase ledger of an agent-driven design loop on the current
incumbent stacks has been published (FACT about the sources read), and a ratio without a measured denominator is
not a ratio. The program's risk register names the failure this prevents: the denominator of the ten-fold ratio
is wrong, because incumbents are faster, or their loop is shorter, than a composite of published figures.

This bead builds the instrument for that measurement: a harness that installs, pins and launches the incumbents
**out of process**. DESIGN (ADR-GAUNT-04): foreign tools are comparison instruments, never dependencies and never
baselines of record; they run out of process, under a pin, on fixtures captured by script. Reasons: the
foundation library's adapter rule, and the finding that agreement with an oracle is Parity and never Reality.
Rejected: linking an incumbent as a library (it would enter the product's dependency graph and its licence);
quoting published figures instead of measuring (they come from different stacks and hardware; absolute
throughput on an RTX 4090 was not found in the sources, whose figures are for other cards).

The first use is the **rival audit** of the parity-baseline gate GATE-TENFOLD-01 at M0: pin every incumbent by
revision, record what each one documents that it can do, and freeze a warm configuration for each after tuning
on reference tasks only. The same pins later serve the rival arms of the ten-fold test, the incumbent arms of
the mixed-batch head-to-head experiment, the proxy-adequacy pilot of the scene chapter, and the neutral
adjudicating simulator.

## Scope

In:
- **The comparator registry**: the file `crates/frob-conformance/registry/comparators.toml`, its row schema, its
  loader and its freeze check. This bead is the only definition of that file. The decision beads write rows and
  decision fields into it and define no schema of their own.
- Recorded environments; the launcher; the harness identity; warm-configuration records with their tuning logs;
  the licence record.
- **Filling the disclosure block for incumbent runs**: the adapter from this harness's run record to a block
  `frob.perf.disclosure/1` with `subject: "incumbent"`.
- Pin-move records for comparators, and the refusal of a pin move while a study that froze the pin is open.

Out, with the bead that has it:
- The disclosure block's schema, field list, applicability table, completeness check, content identity and the
  refusal `FROB-REF-862`: N36-13 (frob-core, M0). This bead calls that check and restates none of it.
- The decision which revisions and configurations are the comparators of each target row, the row-to-comparator
  table, the freeze and change rules and the ADR (OPEN-PERF-2, which is also the comparator half of OPEN-GAUNT-4):
  N36-25. The choice of the development oracle (the other half of OPEN-GAUNT-4): N38b-35.
- The oracle register `registry/oracles.toml`, the fixture store, the capture-script convention and the format
  of the pin-move log: N38a-04. Its row "incumbent batched simulators" points at this bead's registry.
- The timed measurements (seeds BD-040 on `trj`, BD-142 on the Mac, BD-143 for the agent-driven loop); phase
  tagging of launched processes (N39a-25); the capability register's content (N00-13, a dependency: each
  comparator row's `capabilities` cites its `IA-nn` ids); composing arm B for a stratum (N39a-20).

## Technical approach

**1. Candidates (FACT about the releases read for the plan; each is re-read and re-pinned by revision here).**
MuJoCo and MuJoCo Warp 4.0.1 (with the MuJoCo Playground and mjlab training stacks), Newton 1.8.0, Isaac Lab
3.0.0 (on Newton or PhysX), Genesis 1.4.7, Drake 1.58.0, ManiSkill 3.0.1. The source register's release list also
names Warp 1.19.0 (the kernel compiler and runtime MuJoCo Warp and Newton run on) and Isaac Sim 6.2.0 (under
Isaac Lab); both are pinned here as well, Warp with a row of its own. That gives seven release lines (MuJoCo with
MuJoCo Warp, Warp, Newton, Isaac Lab, Genesis, Drake, ManiSkill), and these seven are the default comparator set
of the decision N36-25. Published context to be re-measured:
about 2.55 million humanoid world-steps per second for one stack on an RTX 6000 Ada; a flat-ground quadruped
policy trained in about 410 s on one RTX 4090 and about 325 s on two, with a compile region of about 45 s inside
those times.

**2. Comparator registry (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/comparators.toml`).** The path is the one the
verification chapter fixes for the frozen comparator set (`frob-conformance/registry/comparators.toml`, beside
`oracles.toml`, `pilots.toml` and `controls.toml`); the top-level `registries/` directory holds the product's
registries and is not used for it. One file, one schema, defined here:
```toml
[[comparator]]
name = "…"; role = "engine" | "learner" | "stack" | "solver" | "adjudicator-candidate"
release = "…"; source_revision = "<commit>"; container_digest = "sha256:…"
runtime = { python = "…", cuda = "…", driver = "…" }
licence = { spdx = "…", permits_publishing_comparison = "yes" | "no" | "unknown", source = "…", read_at = "…" }
recipe = "b3:…"; lock = "b3:…"; warm_config = "b3:…"; capabilities = ["<register row ids>"]
pinned_at = "…"; pinned_by = "…"
# decision fields: empty until a decision bead fills them (N36-25 for every row)
uses = ["throughput", "training-time", "mixed-batch", "proxy-adequacy", "tournament:<stratum>"]
rows = [1, 2]                                  # target rows of the performance program this comparator serves
configurations = { native = "b3:…", matched = "b3:…" }
tuned_on = ["<reference task or development fixture>"]
frozen_at = ""                                 # empty = not frozen: every paired run against the row is refused
frozen_for = ["<study id>"]                    # studies that froze this pin and are still open
state = "default" | "decided" | "amended"; reference = "<ADR or owner's note>"
```
Loader rules: a row with a release tag but no commit and no container digest is refused; a floating reference
(`latest`, `main`, any branch name) in `release` or `source_revision` is refused, and a `source_revision` is a
full 40-hex commit; an unknown field is a load error; `frozen_at` non-empty requires `configurations`, `tuned_on`
and `reference`. The file exists and is schema-valid from the first commit of this bead, with the seven default
rows in `state = "default"` and `frozen_at` empty.

DESIGN (one schema, chosen here because three drafts of this file named the same things differently; the
verification chapter fixes only the path and "exact releases and container digests"). The seed's field names
stand: `name`, `source_revision`, `container_digest`. The drafts' `id`, `revision`, `commit` and `digest` are
these fields and are not separate ones. `role` says what kind of thing the comparator is; `uses` says which
comparisons it serves (a second meaning of `role` in one draft). `configurations.native` is the strongest warm
configuration of item 6 in the incumbent's own step, decimation and recipe, and equals `warm_config`;
`configurations.matched` is the same record with our time step, decimation and iteration count where the
incumbent's solver allows them, for rows compared at matched settings. Both take their step settings from the
`[[setting]]` records of the workload registry `registries/workloads.toml` (N36-02, a dependency: matched with
its written difference where the solver forbids our values, native with its source) and record those settings'
hashes, so the step settings are stated once. `frozen_at` is the freeze date (one
draft's `frozen`); `frozen_for` lists the open studies (one draft's `study`). A row one draft called
`provisional` is a row with `state = "default"` and an empty `frozen_at`. Reason for keeping the seed's names:
the launcher, the harness identity and the run records already use them.

**3. Recorded environments (`tools/oracle/incumbents/<name>/`).** `recipe` (base image by digest, system
packages by version), `lock` (every package with its hash), `build.sh`, `run.sh`, `smoke.sh`. An environment is
built once on `trj`, stored by container digest, and run with no network. `frob-tenfold incumbent build <name>`
records the digest; a rebuild that yields a different digest is a new pin.

**4. The launcher (`frob-tenfold incumbent run <name> --config <hash> -- <task args>`).** The `incumbent`
subcommands live in the harness operator tool `frob-tenfold` whose shell, record rules and store the harness
skeleton (N39a-01) provides; they are never `frob` verbs. Numbered behaviour:
(1) resolve the registry row; (2) verify the container digest and the warm configuration's hash, refusing on a
mismatch before anything is started, with the integrity reason `incumbent-pin-drift` of the ten-fold refusal
registry (N39-REF, a dependency: exit 9; repairs: restore the pinned container, or re-pin through the audit,
which changes the harness identity); (3) apply the device, core and memory limits of the request; (4) start the container out of process
with the task; (5) capture standard output and error, exit status and wall time; (6) write a run record with the
harness identity, the host's hardware fingerprint digest, the host load at start, and the disclosure block.
No product crate links, imports or vendors any incumbent package; the workspace dependency check must stay
green with this bead's code present.

**5. The harness identity.** `harness_id = BLAKE3(canonical(sorted comparator rows) ‖ launcher source hash ‖
recipe and lock hashes)`. It appears on every run record. **A changed revision changes the harness identity**:
moving any pin, rebuilding any environment or changing any warm configuration yields a new identity, and
measurements under different identities are never pooled. Moving a pin is a deliberate event with its own
record: a `[[pin_move]]` entry appended to `comparators.toml`, in the format the oracle register defines for
every pin (N38a-04): `comparator` (the row's `name`, where the oracle register writes `oracle`), `from`, `to`,
`date`, `reason`, `by`, `fixtures_recaptured`. A comparator pin does not move between an arm's freeze and the
end of the study that uses it: **the freeze check** refuses a `[[pin_move]]`, and any edit of `release`,
`source_revision`, `container_digest`, `recipe`, `lock` or `configurations`, on a row whose `frozen_for` lists a
study that is still open. A pin changed after `frozen_at` with no `[[pin_move]]` record fails the registry lint
in CI. The registry's digest is part of `harness_id`, so every record made under the old pin stays under the old
identity.

**6. Strongest warm configurations.** For each incumbent, a configuration record chosen after tuning on
**reference tasks only** (the incumbent's own home tasks, for example a flat-ground quadruped velocity task and a
humanoid task; never a mission of the ten-fold suite):
`{world_count, time_step, substeps, control_decimation, solver: {iterations, tolerance}, contact_capacity,
precision, compile_cache: "warm", graph_capture, learner: {recipe, hyperparameters}, selection: "shortest time
to the task's return threshold" | "highest throughput at matched settings", tuning_log: "b3:…"}`.
"Warm" means caches populated (kernels compiled and cached on disk, shaders cached); cold start is measured
separately. The tuning log lists every run with its settings, device-seconds and result, so the choice is
auditable.

**7. The disclosure block on every figure.** Any Performance figure produced through the harness carries a
complete disclosure block `frob.perf.disclosure/1` with `subject: "incumbent"`. The block's field list (25 field
groups: hardware and driver through peak memory, and the added groups from rates with their decimation to
energy), its applicability table, its completeness check `frob_core::perf::disclosure::check`, its content
identity `block_id` and the refusal `FROB-REF-862` ("a performance figure without a complete disclosure block")
are N36-13's; this bead depends on it and holds no second list. What this bead adds is the adapter
`disclosure_from_run(run_record, fingerprint) -> Disclosure`:
1. Every field is read from what the run itself recorded: the container's reported versions, the warm
   configuration record (world count, time step, substeps, control decimation, solver iterations and tolerance,
   contact capacity, precision, cache state, graph capture), the launcher's measurements (warm-up, duration,
   wall time, peak memory, host load, thermal state) and the incumbent's own output (median and 99th-percentile
   step time, mean active contacts, rates with their units).
2. `comparator = {name, revision, container_digest, configuration}` is copied from the registry row and the
   configuration hash the launcher verified; N36-13 requires this group for `subject: "incumbent"`.
3. A field the incumbent has no notion of (`placement`, `kernels`, `row_capacity`, `contact_capacity` where its
   solver has no fixed capacity, `bytes_touched_per_world_step`) is written as `{"na": "<reason>"}`, which
   N36-13's applicability table allows for this subject and nowhere else. A field the run could not supply is
   left absent, so that the check fails; no default is invented.
4. `energy` is a measured value with its source or a named gap, never zero.
5. The block is passed to N36-13's check before the run record is written; a failing block makes the run record
   a failed record with the refusal's pointer, and the figure is not a Performance claim.

**8. Capability and licence records.** Each registry row links to the rows of the incumbent capability register
(what the incumbent *documents* it can do, with the source and pin: per-world model parameters in one batch,
state-preserving model edits, opt-in run-to-run determinism, mixed topologies in one batch, electrical and
thermal actuator models, sampling control without training, interior generators, agent access over MCP). The
licence record states whether publishing a comparison is permitted. Default while the owner's decision is open:
revisions and fetch instructions only are distributed; an incumbent whose licence forbids publication is replaced
by the next strongest, and the replacement is printed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed done-condition: the pinned incumbents run on `trj` from a recorded environment (each `smoke.sh` passes
      from its stored container digest with the network disabled), and a changed revision changes the harness
      identity.
- [ ] `crates/frob-conformance/registry/comparators.toml` exists and is schema-valid; no other bead defines a
      schema for it, and no file `registries/comparators.toml` exists.
- [ ] Every candidate has a registry row with a commit, a container digest, a licence record and a warm
      configuration with its tuning log; rows without a commit or digest are refused, and so is a floating
      reference (`latest`, a branch name).
- [ ] A pin move on a row whose `frozen_for` lists an open study is refused; a pin changed after `frozen_at`
      without a `[[pin_move]]` record fails the registry lint.
- [ ] A launch with a mismatching digest or configuration hash is refused before the container starts.
- [ ] Every run record carries the harness identity, the fingerprint digest, host load and a disclosure block
      `frob.perf.disclosure/1` (`subject: "incumbent"`, with the `comparator` group) that passes N36-13's
      completeness check; a planted record with one field removed is refused with `FROB-REF-862`.
- [ ] No incumbent package appears in any manifest or lockfile of the workspace.
- [ ] The rival-audit part of GATE-TENFOLD-01 is recordable: every incumbent pinned with its capability rows and
      licence, warm configurations frozen (the timed rows are the parity-baseline bead's).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- incumbent::`
- `identity_changes_with_revision`: changing one row's `source_revision` changes `harness_id`; reordering rows
  does not.
- `identity_changes_with_warm_config`.
- `registry_requires_pin`: a row with only a release tag → refused.
- `floating_reference_refused`: `release = "latest"` or `source_revision = "main"` → refused; a 40-hex commit
  with a digest → accepted.
- `decision_fields_schema`: `frozen_at` set without `configurations`, `tuned_on` or `reference` → load error;
  an unknown field (`revision`, `digest`, `study`) → load error.
- `frozen_pin_cannot_move`: a `[[pin_move]]` on a row with an open study in `frozen_for` → refused; the same
  move after the study is closed → accepted and the harness identity changes.
- `digest_mismatch_refused`: launcher with a wrong digest, or a wrong configuration hash → refusal
  `incumbent-pin-drift` with exit 9 before the container starts, and no run record is written.
- `disclosure_from_run_passes_check`: the adapter's block for a recorded smoke run passes N36-13's check
  (the exhaustive single-field deletion test is N36-13's and is not repeated here).
- `disclosure_missing_measurement_fails`: a run record with no peak-memory measurement yields a block that the
  check refuses with `FROB-REF-862` and pointer `peak_memory_bytes`; a zero is never written.
- `disclosure_na_only_where_allowed`: the adapter writes `na` for `placement` and never for `worlds`.
- `disclosure_carries_comparator`: the block's `comparator` group equals the registry row and the verified
  configuration hash.
- `pin_move_record_required`: a registry diff that changes a frozen row's pin with no `[[pin_move]]` record →
  the registry lint fails.
- `capabilities_resolve`: every id in a row's `capabilities` resolves to a record `IA-nn` of the capability
  register; an unknown id is a load error.
- `no_foreign_dependency`: the workspace metadata contains no package from the candidate list.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/incumbent_harness.sh` (header: `bead=BD-039 milestone=M0 gates=GATE-TENFOLD-01 needs=cuda`; skips
with code 77 off `trj`). Steps: print the registry and the harness identity; for each pinned incumbent run its
smoke task (a ten-second batched step of its home robot) from the stored environment with the network disabled;
verify the run record's disclosure block; copy the registry to the scratch directory, change one revision and
assert that the identity changes; attempt a launch with a planted digest and assert the refusal. The script
sources the shared helper `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes NDJSON lines to
`target/e2e-logs/incumbent_harness/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it. Logs per line: the command, exit
code, wall time, comparator name and pin, container digest, harness identity, host load, the disclosure block's
digest, each refusal with its code and pointer, on a failed assertion the first differing value with the registry
copy and run-record paths needed to reproduce it, and a summary naming GATE-TENFOLD-01 as touched (not passed:
the timed rows belong to the parity baseline). Throughput numbers printed by smoke tasks are logged as metrics without
thresholds.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: none of physics; the harness's own invariants (identity is a function of the pins). Published
figures the later measurements must reproduce within 20% or explain in writing: about 410 s (one RTX 4090) and
about 325 s (two) to train the flat-ground quadruped policy; a compile region of about 45 s. Negative controls:
an incumbent run from a mutable environment (an unpinned package upgrade changes the digest; the launcher
refuses); a figure with its decimation omitted (the disclosure check refuses); a silent pin move (the identity
changes and the pin-move check fails); an incumbent imported into a product crate (the dependency check fails).
Determinism: registry hashing is D0; incumbent runs are measurements under the incumbent's own determinism
settings, which the disclosure block records.

## Considerations

- OPEN (project lead): licences; blocks GATE-TENFOLD-01; default as stated. OPEN: which revisions are the
  comparators (OPEN-PERF-2, and the comparator half of OPEN-GAUNT-4); default the revisions read for the plan,
  pinned here and frozen by the decision bead N36-25, which writes the decision fields of the registry rows.
- Compute: smoke tasks fit the default envelope (one device, one hour, 8 cores, 16 GiB). The parity
  measurements on both cards and all cores need the owner's approval of the gate-measurement class; without it
  only single-card, eight-core rows are recorded and the others are `not run`.
- `trj` is often heavily loaded (FACT); the harness records load and does not wait for quiet.
- Incumbent environments are large (tens of gigabytes each); they are stored by digest outside the repository.
- The harness never treats an incumbent's output as truth: agreement with it is Parity.
- Revisit when an incumbent ships a major release during the program: a new pin is a new identity and the M7
  tournament refreshes the audit before its freeze.
