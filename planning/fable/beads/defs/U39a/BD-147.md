## Background

An **exit review** records that a milestone's demonstrations ran and its gates have values. It is performed by a
**fresh-context session**: one that implemented none of the milestone's work. That session regenerates the gate
report from a clean checkout, reads every receipt, checks that every gate has a measured value and a caught
negative control, records a disposition for each, and records every kill decision due as an architecture
decision record (ADR). It implements nothing and repairs nothing. Reason: the sibling projects of this program
documented how agent swarms produce the appearance of progress (87 work items closed within 12 seconds with one
shared close reason); a gate's PASS is therefore never recorded by the session that built the capability.

Milestone M1 is the referee studio and first mission slice, on the CPU only: a typed design language and model
compiler, static analyses, a transactional referee step, baseline controllers, structured telemetry, both study
types, and the first mission queries answered over families of designs with certificates that an independent
auditor re-checks. It is the smallest product that is already useful and already honest, and it needs only three
foundations. Two program-level questions are also answered at this exit.

- **Is the rival's loop what the thesis assumed?** GATE-TENFOLD-02: the incumbent design loop with the common
  agent, end to end, on at least 12 development missions of the `legged-quad` and `wheeled` strata, with closed
  phase ledgers; the table of published phase figures is replaced by measured rows; a removable share and a
  ceiling are recorded for every lever of the thesis. And GATE-TENFOLD-09, ceiling before speed, runs for the
  first time here and at every exit after.
- **Is narrow purity affordable?** The schedule gate of BET-12 (the bet that a core built only on the program's
  own sibling libraries, with GPU annexes, reaches M2 without a foreign physics engine).

## Scope

In: the M1 exit record; a disposition for every gate part due; the kill decisions due; the schedule gate's
evaluation; the records of GATE-TENFOLD-02 and GATE-TENFOLD-09 and of KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER on the rival's ledger;
the risks and open questions due; the stop rules. Out: any repair or measurement; the shared procedure and the
register query (see `needs`); the gates' own beads.

## Technical approach

**1. Procedure (in brief; the shared runner implements it).** (1) Start a session with no context of M1 whose
identity appears as implementer of no due row. (2) Clean clone at the exit candidate commit; build on the pinned
toolchain. (3) Run every M1 demonstration through `frob --json` and, where the registry exposes the verb to
agents, through `frob-mcp`. (4) Run the recurring gates and the full negative-control sweep at this commit.
(5) Regenerate the gate report and the README status table; both must equal the committed files. (6) List kill
decisions with and without ADRs, shared close reasons, controls that have never failed, and parts that are
`not-run`, `blocked` or `fail`. (7) Record a disposition per part from its receipt: `accepted-pass`,
`accepted-kill: <fallback>`, `recorded-fail: blocks`, `not-run: <reason>`, `rejected: <reason>`. (8) Check each
kill decision against its registered trigger. (9) Read the risk register: per risk, whether its earliest signal
fired. (10) Answer or default the open questions due. (11) Demonstrate the exit line and apply the stop rules.
(12) Write `docs/delivery/exits/M1/EXIT.md`.

**2. The five exit conditions.** Every gate of the milestone has a recorded value (a failing value is a record; a
kill gate that fired is an ADR with the fallback in force). Every demonstration runs from a clean checkout.
GATE-GAUNT-01 holds: every gate that passed has a registered negative control that it catches. Every report
prints its `reality:` line (GATE-GROUND-01, from M1 onward). The risk register has been read and the open
questions due have been answered or have taken their defaults. A gate may pass before its milestone; it may not
move later without an ADR; where a list and the register differ, the register governs.

**3. Gate parts due (identifiers; criteria are in the milestone epic and the generated report).**
Admission: INT-FSIM-07, INT-FSIM-16, INT-FSQL-02, INT-FMCP-01 for the M1 verbs (an admission is never inherited:
each is recorded at this milestone, at the pin of that day). Marrow: GATE-MARROW-02. Skeleton: GATE-SKEL-01 to
-08, with -03 for the first archetypes and -04 for preflight stages 1 to 6. Sinew: GATE-SINEW-01, -02, -04 to -08
on the referee. Kinesis and Traction: GATE-KIN-01, -02; GATE-TRAC-02; the referee rows of GATE-KIN-04,
GATE-TRAC-01 and GATE-TRAC-06. Sensorium: the CPU rows of GATE-SENS-04, -05, -12 and the docking rows of -08, -09.
Reflex: GATE-REFLEX-01 (referee rows), -03. Atelier: GATE-ATEL-02 for the analyses that exist, -06, the analysis
parts of -05 and -12, the dependency grades of -11 on mission cells. Chronicle: GATE-CHRON-03 (referee), -04
(CPU), -10, the CPU part of -11. Arena: GATE-ARENA-03, -04 (referee rows), -05. Augur and Lemma: GATE-AUGUR-01 to
-07; GATE-LEMMA-03, -04. Ground: GATE-GROUND-01. Tether and safety: GATE-TETHER-02, -07; GATE-SAFE-01, -08, -10;
GATE-WS-03, and GATE-WS-02 for the formats that exist. Parity baseline: GATE-SPARK-21 complete. Late parts: the
twelve listed in `needs`. Recurring from here: GATE-GROUND-01, GATE-SAFE-01, GATE-TENFOLD-09, GATE-GAUNT-01.
Counts to expect: 4 admission parts, 59 convergence parts, 12 late parts.

**4. Program gates read here.**
- *GATE-TENFOLD-02.* The review checks: at least 12 development missions of `legged-quad` and `wheeled` were run
  by the composed rival with the common agent; every run has a ledger and the share of `ledger-open` runs is
  reported; the published-figure table has a measured column for every phase; every one of the eight levers has a
  removable share and a ceiling computed from closed ledgers; a repeat of the round reproduces each phase's share
  inside its stated interval. If unmet: M1 does not exit, the arithmetic of the thesis stays a HYPOTHESIS, no work
  package is funded as a speed lever, and the table of published figures may not be cited as a denominator.
- *GATE-TENFOLD-09.* First run: every work package funded as a speed lever cites a removable share of at least
  10% on a `ledger-closed` run of the strong baseline; every registered speed comparison records its ceiling and
  is refused when the ceiling is below the ratio it sets out to show. The review re-runs the check and records
  the two clause values. If unmet: the comparison is refused and the package loses its speed justification.
- *KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER on the rival's ledger.* Eight decision records, one per lever: fired where the removable
  share is under 10% of the measured loop; a struck lever's work packages carry a non-speed reason and no speed
  figure before M2 begins.
- *The schedule gate (BET-12).* Measurement: the elapsed time of M0 plus M1 against the planning estimate (2 + 5
  = 7 weeks), with the cause of any slip attributed in this review. Not met if the two milestones took more than
  1.5 times their estimate (more than 10.5 weeks) *because* a sibling library lacked a primitive, its upstream
  work item had not landed and no interim posture applied. Consequence: the program pauses for a written re-plan
  before M2 starts. A slip above 10.5 weeks for other causes is recorded with its causes and does not trip the
  gate. The estimates exist to make the bet testable; they are not acceptance evidence.

**6. Demonstrations (the exit line: NS-01, NS-07 and the docking scenario demonstrated; the schedule gate met).**
NS-01, check before you simulate: `frob design check` returns in under two seconds with the mass roll-up, the
actuator rating exceeded, the symmetry groups and three ranked repairs as typed edits. NS-07, an import that
tells the truth: importing a public humanoid description yields a loss report. NS-22, the docking fixture: a
cart with unknown gain must dock in three one-second ticks, and the mission query returns its four certified
facts (docking tolerates sensor error ε exactly when ε < (U + ½)/4; with the packet late no causal controller
docks in both worlds). NS-13 on the CPU: the stance family with boundary exactly 3/14 m, re-checked by the
offline auditor.

**7. Open questions due, with defaults.** Approval of compute for the rank-agreement experiment (about forty
per-design trainings; about 5 device-hours with reused specialists, about 45 otherwise): default not run above
the default envelope, every cheap rung `uninformative`. The owner's reading of the measured ledger against the
expected range per mission class: recorded as read, or as pending.

**8. Stop rules to apply.** The schedule gate. Re-plan the mission chapter before adding physics if a fresh agent
cannot complete the docking and stance amendments of GATE-AUGUR-07 after one repair cycle. Re-plan the referee if
its refusal rate on the M1 fixtures leaves the sill fixture without a completed run.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every gate of M1 has a recorded value and a caught negative control (the seed's done-condition).
- [ ] NS-01, NS-07 and NS-22 are demonstrated from a clean checkout through the CLI and through MCP.
- [ ] The schedule gate is evaluated: elapsed weeks, the 10.5-week threshold, and the cause attribution are in
      the exit record; if tripped, the pause is recorded and M2 does not start.
- [ ] GATE-TENFOLD-02 and GATE-TENFOLD-09 are recorded with their measured values; KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER has eight
      decision records.
- [ ] KILL-SKEL-SEXPR, KILL-SKEL-SUBST, KILL-DEVORACLE and the M1 re-read of KILL-MCP have ADRs;
      KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT is registered as standing.
- [ ] Every report prints its `reality:` line and every exported artifact carries the three no-authorization
      flags, all false.
- [ ] The regenerated gate report equals the committed one and is `exit-ready`; `docs/delivery/exits/M1/EXIT.md`
      is written by the fresh-context session; no close reason is shared between beads.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance exit::m1::`: `due_counts` (4 admission, 59 convergence, 12 late parts; 5 kill
decisions plus the eight lever records); `exit_line_needs_demonstrations` (a missing NS-22 receipt → `not-ready`);
`schedule_gate_threshold` (the rule is "more than 1.5 times": 10.5 weeks with the named cause → not tripped; 10.6
weeks with the named cause → tripped; 11 weeks with another cause → not tripped); `tenfold_02_blocks_exit` (no
closed rival ledger → `not-ready`); `lever_records_required` (seven of eight → `not-ready`);
`recurring_start_here` (GATE-GROUND-01, GATE-SAFE-01 and GATE-TENFOLD-09 due from M1 on);
`self_certified_pass_rejected` (a PASS recorded by an implementer's session → `rejected`).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/exit_m1.sh` wraps the shared runner `scripts/exit/review.sh M1`: the four demonstrations as
scripted sessions through the CLI and MCP; the ceiling check re-run on the rival's ledgers of record; the lever
shares recomputed from the ledgers and compared with the decision records; the negative-control sweep; the ADR
listing; the schedule computation from the milestone registry's dates. Logs: each command, exit code, wall time,
the envelope's evidence block, each recomputed share and ceiling beside the recorded one, elapsed weeks beside
10.5, each disposition, and on failure the first differing value with artifact paths; the summary names the
gates touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the M1 load (4, 59, 12); 7 weeks and 10.5 weeks; 1/(1 − 0.10) = 1.11 for the lever rule; the
docking tolerance ε < (U + ½)/4 and the stance boundary 3/14 m shown by the demonstrations. Controls the review
must catch: a gate with a value and no caught control; a kill ADR that cites no receipt; a close reason shared by
several beads; a speed-lever work package with no cited share; a rival ledger that is `ledger-open` used for a
ceiling; a report without its `reality:` line; a PASS recorded by the implementing session.

## Considerations

- If the rival loop was not measured (compute not approved, or an incumbent could not be installed), M1 does not
  exit; the review records that state and stays open. It does not substitute published figures.
- The schedule gate asks about a cause, not only a duration; attributing blocked time to upstream work items is
  the review's most judgement-laden step, and the upstream work-item records are its evidence.
- No single failed bet blocks this exit: a symbolic, proof or GPU foundation that failed admission leaves the
  same M1; a conventional program that answers the docking query as well ships behind the same verbs.
- Risks whose earliest signals are read here include "the denominator of the ten-fold ratio is wrong" (signal:
  the measured phase ledger).
