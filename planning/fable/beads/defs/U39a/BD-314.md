## Background

At milestone M3 the program makes its first measured comparison with a rival: the **partial ten-fold gate**. It
runs the classes that the M3 product can run, with three arms (A, FrankenRobotics; B, the strongest composed
rival; C, a short robust program over the rival's engine), on sealed missions that no arm has seen, scored by a
neutral adjudicator. Its result is recorded whatever the ratio; a measured ratio under 3 on a class withdraws the
speed claim for that class.

A comparison like this is only as good as what was fixed before it ran. This bead is the **protocol**: the
document and the committed objects that exist before either arm runs. The seed's done-condition: the gates
GATE-TENFOLD-03, -04, -05, -10 and -11 are satisfied for the parts a protocol can satisfy; the protocol is
committed before either arm runs; and a stratum whose computed ceiling is below its claimed ratio is refused.

What arm A has at M3 (and what it lacks) shapes the scope: the daemon with slot dispatch, mixed batches, the
baseline controllers and the sampling fallback, the ladder with fixed allocation, the qualification lane, the
adversarial search and the autopsy tools, the memo graph, and the mission queries on the CPU. It does not yet
have family policies, inheritance or rank-agreement allocation, which land at M4. The partial gate therefore
measures the product without the "train few" lever. HYPOTHESIS: on `legged-quad` the ratio at M3 lies near the
bottom of the expected range of 3 to 10. The outcome rules apply all the same, and a class withdrawn at M3 stays
withdrawn until M7 measures it on fresh families.

DESIGN reason for a pre-registration: thresholds, cells, deadlines and the rule itself are fixed when the first
suite hash is committed, and none is changed after any sealed run. Rejected: "decide the analysis when the data
is in" (every forking path favours the party choosing).

(publication of class descriptions and the development suite; commitment of the suite and protocol hashes); the
ceiling pre-check per stratum and its refusal; assembling the pre-opening evidence that belongs to the protocol.

## Technical approach

**1. Scope of the partial gate.** Cells: (`legged-quad`, cold) and (`wheeled`, cold) as confirmatory cells;
(`envelope`, warm) and (`sensing`, cold) as confirmatory cells if their pre-analyses allow it, and as descriptive
cells otherwise. Tracked and hybrid briefs wait for M4 and are first measured at M7. Arms A, B and C all run.
At most four cells are confirmatory.

**2. The pre-registration (`docs/delivery/tenfold/M3-preregistration.md` plus `tenfold/M3-partial/prereg.json`,
hashed into sealing step 2).** Built by `frob-tenfold prereg build --gate M3-partial` from the registries and the
owner decision records, and checked by `frob-tenfold prereg check <prereg.json>`, which exits 3 naming the first
missing or paraphrased section (DESIGN: the pre-registration is a document of the gate store, not one of the five
harness records, so `frob-tenfold record check` does not read it). The three subcommands `prereg build`, `prereg
check` and `prereg precheck` (step 3) are this bead's, in `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/prereg.rs`. The
check refuses with the reason `prereg-section-missing` (a section of the twelve is absent or empty) or
`prereg-rule-paraphrased` (a section's rule text differs from the text of the bead that owns the rule), each with
the section number as its pointer and the repairs "regenerate the document with `prereg build`" and "quote the
owning rule text". Neither reason is yet a row of the ten-fold refusal registry (N39-REF): both are requested of
it (exit 3, no product code) and are entered there before this bead's code lands, because the registry's two-way
consistency test admits no unregistered reason. Sections, each quoting the rule in full:
1. *Estimand.* R_s = E[min(T_B, H_s)] ÷ E[min(T_A, H_s)], pooled as a ratio of sums over paired runs; cold and warm
   never pooled; the word governed by the ratio against the stronger of arm B and arm C.
2. *Cells and sizes.* Per stratum: 12 public development missions; 24 sealed mission families by default (never
   below 12); c = 1 campaign per family (2 where fewer than 24 families exist); the result type of each cell
   (finite-suite below 32 families; population needs at least 32, and 46 where four cells share the level).
3. *Deadlines.* H per stratum, from the adjudicator, or the default: three times the frozen rival's median
   development time, rounded up to half an hour, raised until H ÷ RMT_A is at least 10.
4. *Acceptance.* The five answer kinds; the neutral adjudicating simulator per stratum or the cross-home mode
   decided for it; the submission protocol with levels α_i = 0.05/[i·(i + 1)].
5. *The decision rule.* The five verdicts in order (`disqualified`, `descriptive`, `withdrawn` below 3 or on a
   futility stop, `ten-fold` at R̂ ≥ 10 with the exact guard, non-inferior completion and coverage, no tie and the
   adjudication condition, otherwise `measured`); the exact guard (families with ratio above 5 against a
   binomial(m, ½) tail; 17 of 24 at 0.05, 18 of 24 with four cells under Holm); the bootstrap interval as
   descriptive; the non-inferiority mode chosen by the owner or the default as written.
6. *Reachable verdicts.* The pre-analysis procedure and the four reachability conditions; the list of cells
   proposed as confirmatory (finalized at sealing step 5).
7. *The monitor.* The futility e-process (λ = ½, threshold 20), armed at this gate.
8. *Thresholds.* 3 and 10, the guard floor 5, the tie factor 1.5, the margin 0.05, at most four confirmatory
   cells, the 2% ledger closure, the 5% exclusion limit: ratified values or defaults as written.
9. *Outcome rules and disqualification.* Quoted from their owners; each outcome becomes an ADR at M3.
10. *Equal terms, caps, schedule seed commitment, price table.*
11. *What is decided on the gate's record besides the ratio.* Whether a short robust program over a conventional
    solver ties on the family strata (KILL-AUGUR-TIE) and whether any family certificate decided a cell the
    generic comparator left undecided (KILL-FAMILYCERT), on `envelope` and `sensing`; whether the
    Gymnasium-compatible adapter passed its gate (KILL-GYMADAPT); the incumbent arm of the agent-structure bet;
    and whether the `train` phase still holds a removable share of at least 10% for each class, which decides how
    much of the M4 work on the "train few" lever is funded as a speed lever.
12. *Cost.* A cell costs m·c·Σ over arms of E[min(T, H)] device-hours: with 24 families, three arms and a 4 h
    deadline at most 288 device-hours per cell, reached only if every run is censored; HYPOTHESIS about 70
    device-hours per design cell at restricted mean times near 1.5 h, 1.0 h and 0.4 h for arms B, C and A. This is
    above the default compute envelope, so the gate needs the owner's approval of a campaign class for each run,
    and of the pretraining class where an arm builds a standing asset.

**3. The ceiling pre-check (the seed's refusal).** For each stratum proposed for a confirmatory cell, compute
from the measured rival ledger of the M1 baseline (and arm A's development ledgers, which exist at M3) the
ceilings that bound the cell's ratio: the unremovable floor (R ≤ RMT_B ÷ u), the completion ceiling (R ≤ 1/q on
development completion), and the horizon (R ≤ H ÷ RMT_A). The calculators are N39a-37's (`frob-tenfold-score
ceiling --cell …` writes the cell's ceiling record with its binding ceiling); `frob-tenfold prereg precheck --cell
<cell-dir> --claim <ratio>` compares that binding ceiling with the claim and exits 0 when the ceiling is at least
the claim, 3 with `ceiling-below-goal` otherwise. The cell's *claimed ratio* is 10 for a cell that seeks
the word, and 3 for a cell registered only against withdrawal (DESIGN: this bead's reading of "claimed ratio";
the protocol's thresholds are the only ratios a cell can claim). **A stratum whose computed ceiling is below its
claimed ratio is refused** as a confirmatory cell for that claim (`ceiling-below-goal`): it is opened as
descriptive for the word, and the report says so before any sealed run. The check and its numbers are part of
the pre-registration.

**4. Sealing steps 1 and 2 for this gate.** Step 1: publish the class descriptions (strata and grid for the two
M3 rows and the two family strata), the mission grammar and schemas, the development suite with public control
flags, and the role registry. Step 2: the adjudicator commits the sealed suite (cards, families, controls,
deadlines, caps), the referee protocol and acceptance tree, the per-stratum adjudication records, the thresholds,
the pre-registration's hash and the price table. From this instant the comment channel is closed.

**5. Protocol-side evidence for the pre-opening gates.**
- GATE-TENFOLD-03: the rehearsal pipeline (N39a-41) reproduces every known answer of the harness's set and
  catches the planted wrong scorers; the recorded gate with all seven negative controls is N39b-22's and is
  attached.
- GATE-TENFOLD-04: steps 1 and 2 recorded in order with the checker of N39a-40; the rest is measured with the
  same checker after the arms freeze.
- GATE-TENFOLD-05: adjudicator acceptance recorded per stratum (N39a-10), or the cross-home decision (N39a-11).
- GATE-TENFOLD-10: the record of N39b-36 is attached.
- GATE-TENFOLD-11: the audit register and pack of N39b-37 are attached; the reproduction check of each frozen
  arm is attached when BD-315 freezes it. The protocol lists both gates as preconditions of `open`.
The gate's PASS is recorded when the last bead that cites it closes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pre-registration exists with the twelve sections, each stating its rule in full, and its hash is in the
      sealing ledger's step-2 entry, dated before any arm's freeze manifest and before any sealed run.
- [ ] Seed done-condition: the protocol is committed before either arm runs (ledger order), and a stratum whose
      computed ceiling is below its claimed ratio is refused (demonstrated on a planted stratum whose horizon cap
      is 8 against a claim of 10).
- [ ] The four cells are listed with their status and result type; `tracked` and `hybrid` are listed
      `not run: first measured at M7`.
- [ ] Every threshold appears with its ratified value or `default as written`; the non-inferiority decision is
      recorded or `default-taken` is flagged.
- [ ] The protocol-side evidence of step 5 exists for GATE-TENFOLD-03, -04 (steps 1 and 2) and -05, and the
      records of GATE-TENFOLD-10 and of the pre-freeze part of GATE-TENFOLD-11 are attached.
- [ ] The owner's approval of the campaign compute class is on record, or the gate is recorded `not run` and M3
      does not exit.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- prereg::`
- `sections_complete`: a pre-registration record missing any of the twelve sections is refused
  (`prereg-section-missing`, pointer the section number, exit 3); one whose section 5 rewords the verdict rule is
  refused (`prereg-rule-paraphrased`); the record built by `prereg build` passes.
- `ceiling_precheck`: horizon cap 8 against a claim of 10 → refused; unremovable floor giving 8.571 against 10 →
  refused; the same stratum against a claim of 3 → admitted.
- `prereg_before_freeze`: a freeze manifest dated before the pre-registration hash → refused by the sealing
  ledger.
- `thresholds_from_prereg`: the scorer reads thresholds from the committed record only.
- `four_cells_scope`: a fifth confirmatory cell → refused; `tracked` proposed at M3 → `not run`.
- `cost_bound`: 24 × 3 × 4 = 288 device-hours.
- `precheck_agrees_with_d9_oracles`: every ceiling the pre-check computes is compared with the compendium's D.9
  oracle library (N12-49), which the pre-check never regenerates: the completion cap for q = 1/8 is 8 and for
  q = 0.12 is 25/3; the restricted-time ratio of the 25-mission case is 10.081 (and the rate ratio 9.677 is
  reported under its own name, never as R); the family counts that section 2 quotes for a population result are
  the oracle's 32 at α = 0.05, 46 at 0.0125 and 49 at 0.01. A planted pre-check that caps by the rate ratio, or a
  section 2 that quotes 24 as a population count, fails.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_m3_protocol.sh` sources the STD-E2E helper (`scripts/e2e/lib.sh`). On a rehearsal store
(gate `rehearsal-m3-protocol`, no sealed material) it: (1) generates the pre-registration with `frob-tenfold
prereg build` and checks it with `frob-tenfold prereg check` (a scratch copy missing one of the twelve sections is
refused); (2) computes the ceilings with `frob-tenfold-score ceiling --cell …` on two `synthetic` strata and runs
`frob-tenfold prereg precheck --cell … --claim 10` on each: the stratum with a horizon cap of 8 must be refused
(`tenfold.ceiling-below-goal`, FROB-REF-866, exit 3) and admitted with `--claim 3`; (3) performs sealing steps 1
and 2 with `frob-tenfold seal step …` and checks them with
`frob-tenfold seal verify`; (4) edits a threshold in a scratch copy after step 2 and asserts that `seal verify`
detects the change; (5) prints the preconditions of `open` with their state. It writes
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_m3_protocol/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript; every line has `ts`, `script`,
`bead` (`BD-314`), `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; `metric` lines carry the
pre-registration hash, the step-2 commitment and, per stratum, the three ceilings (unremovable floor,
completion, horizon) beside the claimed ratio; `refusal` lines carry the reason; on failure the first differing
value and the store path needed to reproduce it; the `summary` line has assertion counts and names the six gates
of this bead (GATE-TENFOLD-03, -04, -05, -09, -10, -11) as touched, not passed. On the real gate instance the
same script runs only the read-only checks and writes nothing sealed to its logs.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the cost bound 288 and the estimate 69.6 device-hours; 17 of 24 and 18 of 24; the worked ceilings
8.571 and 3.75. Negative controls: a protocol committed after an arm ran (ledger order refutes it); a threshold
edited after step 2; a stratum admitted for the word with a ceiling of 8; a pre-registration that paraphrases a
rule instead of quoting it (a diff against the rule texts of the owning beads fails).

## Considerations

- OPEN questions that block this bead's commitment, with fail-closed defaults: the adjudicator and simulator (a
  pinned incumbent CPU engine no arm trains in, run by a fresh-context session; ratios `self-adjudicated`; the
  partial gate is then a rehearsal for the wording); the advocates (agent sessions with no product authorship; 40
  device-hours per stratum); the envelope (nothing above the default class runs; the gate is `not run`; M3 does
  not exit); deadlines; the agent; the thresholds (as written).
- The rule of the ten-fold bet is applied per mission class here: a measured ratio under 3 withdraws the speed
  claim for that class; under 3 on every class, speed work is no longer funded and the product is positioned on
  replay, qualification, certificates and the evidence ledger.
- With the completion non-inferiority condition as written, no 24-family cell can carry the word (N39a-31); the
  pre-registration must show the owner's choice.
- The partial gate passes when its record exists and the outcome rules have been applied, whatever the ratio.
