## Background

The partial ten-fold gate at milestone M3 is the program's first measured comparison of the time to an
independently accepted answer. Its numerator is the time of the rival. This bead builds and runs the rival side
of that gate: **arm B, the strongest composed rival** (a named incumbent engine, per-design reinforcement
learning, a standard outer-loop optimizer, analytic bounds and a verifier where the stratum needs them, and the
same agent that drives FrankenRobotics), and with it **arm C**, the ablation arm (at most 2,000 lines of program
text over arm B's engine, implementing any mechanism of the program's thesis that needs no engine internals).

The rival is not built by the builders of FrankenRobotics. It is built and tuned by **rival advocates** who have
written no FrankenRobotics code, on public development missions only, with a budget at least equal to the
builders' own, and then frozen and published before any sealed mission is opened. The seed's done-condition:
restricted mean times to an independently accepted answer are recorded per brief with the phase ledger, cold and
warm runs separated.

"Driven through its own tools and the adapter" has two halves. The rival's agent uses the incumbents' own tools
(their command-line programs, Python entry points and, where an incumbent offers one, its MCP server) through a
tool layer the advocates write; nothing of FrankenRobotics is on that path. Separately, the
**Gymnasium-compatible adapter** of the product lets the rival's learning stack train in the product's
environment for the learner-swap diagnostic, which splits a difference in the `train` phase into an engine part
and a learner part and never enters the ratio.

Reason for giving the rival this much: a ratio against a weak rival is worthless, and the program's bet names
the arm as the strongest composed rival, improved by a competent baseline engineer on development fixtures and
then frozen. Rejected: the incumbent "as commonly run" as the only rival (it is reported too, through the
recovery ratio, but it is not the arm the word is judged against).

## Scope

In: composing arms B and C for the strata of the partial gate; their tool layers, wrappers, tag maps and agent
guides; tuning on development missions; the freeze packs and manifests; running the arms' campaigns under the
harness on the sealed missions when the gate opens; the per-brief records (restricted times, ledgers, cost
columns), cold and warm separated; the learner-swap diagnostic runs. Out: the protocol (BD-314); arm A (BD-316);
adjudication, scoring and the gate's record (BD-317); the mechanisms this bead uses (the N39a beads it depends
on); the composed incumbent W beyond arm C's limit and its one-receipt pipeline (see `needs`).

## Technical approach

**1. Strata and configurations at M3.** `legged-quad` and `wheeled` (cold): MuJoCo Warp under MuJoCo Playground or
mjlab, or Isaac Lab on Newton or PhysX, each with its own PPO recipe and one policy per design, or a published
shared design-conditioned policy where one fits; outer loop CMA-ES or Bayesian optimization over the design
parameters with successive halving; the engine's per-world model fields for parametric variants; warm compilation
caches; sampling-based control with no training where it helps. `envelope` (warm) and `sensing` (cold): an
expert's program over Drake, a linear or mixed-integer solver, an SMT or interval tool; a scenario language for
world generation; system identification from the same calibration data. The advocates run the candidates on each
stratum's 12 development missions and freeze, per stratum, the one configuration with the smallest restricted
mean time (never a per-mission choice). The releases are those pinned by the incumbent harness.

**2. What the advocates build (the pack, per stratum).**
1. *Tool layer.* Shims around the incumbents' tools, every one launched through the harness wrapper so that each
   process is tagged with a phase; an MCP or command-line surface the common agent can call; no call to any
   FrankenRobotics binary; no reference to the adjudicating simulator.
2. *Tag map.* Rules assigning each tool to one of the thirteen phases, with the first-device-step marker on
   simulation and training tools; no catch-all rule.
3. *Agent guide.* A written guide for the common agent, no longer (in tokens) than the product's generated
   documentation, plus a local mirror of the incumbents' own documentation at the pinned revisions.
4. *Outer-loop scripts, reward and curriculum libraries, warm caches and policies*, each content-addressed.
5. *Standing assets*, each with its measured build cost (wall and device seconds) and build ledger.
6. *Arm C's program* (at most 2,000 counted lines) with its per-mechanism checklist.
7. *The capability checklist* for the chosen engine: every documented capability used, or the reason it is not.

**3. Tuning (development missions only).** Budget per arm and stratum: max(40 device-hours, the compute the
builders logged on the same development suite). Everything a competent engineer would do is allowed
(hyperparameters, reward shaping, curricula, warm caches, policy reuse, batching, analytic pruning). The tuning
log records every run. Before the freeze the builders may object in writing that the rival is under-built and the
adjudicator rules; and the rival must win at least one easy-win development family per stratum or its freeze is
refused.

**4. Freeze.** One manifest per arm and stratum (engine and learner revisions, outer-loop script, tool layer,
agent guide, the common agent's pin and caps, cache state, standing assets, tag map, tuning log, development
scores, the advocate's signed statement with what more budget would have added, the hardware fingerprint), hashed
and published after the suite and protocol hashes are committed and before the suite is opened. The frozen rival
reproduces its home-task training time within 20% on `trj`, and its own development-fixture figure within 10%
when re-run on the gate's hardware.

**5. Running the sealed campaigns (when the gate is open).** For every cell in scope, every sealed family and
campaign index: the harness creates the arm's workspace from the pack, starts a fresh agent session through the
agent proxy, delivers the mission card (clock start), enforces the caps, and records intervals until an accepted
receipt or the deadline. Order and device assignment come from the gate's randomized, counterbalanced,
interleaved schedule. Cold runs start from the card and nothing mission-specific. Warm campaigns (`envelope`)
run the card's amendment sequence on an accepted cold answer with the equal cache budget; a failed amendment
stays in the sequence and costs its deadline. The rival's agent submits answers through the same submission
protocol as arm A and receives the same receipts.

**6. What is recorded per brief (the seed's observable).** For each mission (brief) and arm: the campaign record
(outcome, clock, censoring, submissions); the phase ledger (thirteen exclusive phases summing to the clock, the
resource table, turns and tokens, designs decided, trainings, closure status); the cost columns; the receipts.
Per cell and arm: the restricted mean time RMT = Σ min(T, H) ÷ n with its completion count, cold and warm in
separate records, never pooled; for warm cells the pooled per-amendment time and the standing-asset cost.

**7. The learner-swap runs.** For each design stratum: the rival's frozen learner trains the stratum's reference
task in the product's environment through the adapter (5 seeds), beside the rival's own engine with its own
learner (5 seeds); the product's own training is arm A's contribution. If the adapter failed its gate, the rival
learner drives whole rollouts through the product's job interface and the added latency is recorded.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Arms B and C have published freeze manifests for `legged-quad`, `wheeled`, `envelope` and `sensing`, each
      dated after the suite commitment and before the suite opening, with packs in which every hash resolves.
- [ ] Each frozen rival wins at least one easy-win development family in its stratum, has no unexplained unused
      capability, and reproduces its home-task training time within 20% on `trj`.
- [ ] Tuning stayed within max(40 device-hours, the builders' logged compute) per arm and stratum, on development
      missions only; the adjudicating simulator appears in no log.
- [ ] Seed done-condition: for every brief of every cell in scope, restricted times to an independently accepted
      answer are recorded with a phase ledger, cold and warm in separate records.
- [ ] At least 90% of the rival's sealed runs are `ledger-closed` (TARGET: the rival's ledger is the input of the
      ceilings); every run carries its cost columns and freeze hash.
- [ ] Arm C's counted program text is at most 2,000 lines by the adjudicator's count.
- [ ] No rival campaign started before every arm of its pair was frozen, and no pack byte changed afterwards.
- [ ] The `pre-open` part of the freeze-and-seal gate record for this gate instance (written with the checker of
      N39a-40 once arms A, B and C are frozen) is `pass` before the first sealed campaign of any arm.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- arm_b_run::`
- `pack_resolves`: every reference of a manifest resolves in the pack; a missing file fails.
- `no_product_on_rival_path`: the tool layer and arm C's pack contain no product binary, crate or schema writer.
- `per_brief_record_complete`: a campaign without a ledger, cost block or receipt list is rejected by the cell
  scorer's input validation.
- `cold_warm_separate`: the per-arm summary has two records for a stratum with both regimes and no pooled field.
- `rmt_from_briefs`: restricted times (9,000, 6,300, 14,400 censored, 5,400, 11,700, 7,200) s → RMT = 9,000 s,
  completion 5 of 6.
- `closed_share`: 22 closed of 24 → 91.7%, above the target; 21 of 24 → 87.5%, flagged.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_m3_rival_arm.sh` (`needs=cuda`; skips with code 77 without an installed incumbent): on two
public development missions of `legged-quad` (never sealed ones), builds a reduced pack for arm B from the pinned
incumbent, runs the selection on two candidate configurations at reduced budgets, freezes on a rehearsal gate,
runs one cold campaign per mission with the common agent under caps, and builds the ledgers and the per-brief
records. Asserts that each record is complete, each ledger sums to its clock, the tag map covered every launched
process, and the freeze drift check holds. Logs each command, exit code, wall time, the manifest hash, the clock
and outcome per brief, the exclusive table, the unattributed share against 2%, device-hours against the budget,
the disclosure block of each training figure, and the summary naming GATE-TENFOLD-06 and GATE-TENFOLD-11 as
touched. With `E2E_SMALL=1` the log says `gates_claimable: false`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the RMT example above; 1.2 × 410 s = 492 s for the home-task limit; the budget floor of 40
device-hours. Negative controls: a rival pack changed after its freeze (the campaign is refused and the arm is
flagged); a rival tuned on a sealed mission (impossible by construction before the opening; the test asserts the
sealed store is unreadable to advocate identities); a rival whose training is tagged `screen` (the resource
table shows learner seconds in a non-training phase and the pre-freeze review flags it); a per-brief record that
omits a censored run (the scorer's `missing-run` refusal).

## Considerations

- Cost: HYPOTHESIS about 70 device-hours per design cell for three arms at restricted mean times near 1.5 h, 1.0 h
  and 0.4 h for arms B, C and A; worst case 288. Above the default envelope; needs the owner's approval of a
  campaign class per run. Without it the gate is `not run` and M3 does not exit.
- Under the default, the advocates are agent sessions with no product authorship and every result prints
  `rival: internally advocated`.
- FACT about the incumbent documents read: run-to-run determinism, heterogeneous worlds and the fastest solver
  sit on different solver paths; the advocates record which property binds when they are demanded together.
- If the adapter's gate fails, the diagnostic uses the job interface and records the added latency; the arms
  themselves are unaffected, because the rival's own loop never uses the product.
- A defect found in the frozen rival after the suite opens voids the affected cells; it is not patched mid-gate.
