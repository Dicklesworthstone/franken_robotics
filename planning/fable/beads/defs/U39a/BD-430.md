## Background

The ten-fold tournament at milestone M7 compares FrankenRobotics with rival arms built on incumbent simulators.
Two rival arms run on every stratum, on the same sealed missions, acceptance protocol, hardware and resource
caps as the product:

- **the declared incumbent**: a leading incumbent simulator with per-design reinforcement learning and a standard
  outer-loop optimizer, frozen by its own advocates on development fixtures;
- **the composed incumbent**: the same engine or engines with every portable method layered on (native state
  restore and forks, per-topology buckets or the engine's own heterogeneous solver, a sampling controller or a
  multi-embodiment prior with checkpoint resume, racing and stopping, importance-weighted failure search, a
  second engine as referee), built by a competent baseline engineer. Its short form is the ablation arm of at
  most 2,000 lines over the incumbent engine.

The word "ten-fold" is governed by the product against the *stronger* of the two, never against the declared
incumbent alone. If the composed incumbent ties, the plan's answer is written in advance: say so, and ship the
layer over that engine for that stratum (BET-14).

This epic is split into tasks when M7 opens. Seed done-condition: a short plan exists and its pilot has run; the
rival's phase ledger is recorded on `trj` under the disclosure block. The mechanisms (the pinned incumbent
harness, the wrapper and tag map, arm B's selection and tuning protocol, the ablation arm's line count, freeze
manifests, equal terms) exist from the partial gate at M3.

## Scope

In: the short plan; the refresh pilot; the rival audit refreshed at current releases; arms for every stratum of
the tournament; tuning by advocates; freezes; running cold and warm campaigns; the rival's ledgers under the
disclosure block. Out: the suite (BD-428); the decision rule (BD-429); arm A (BD-431); adjudication (BD-432);
recovery ratios and ablations as computations (see `needs`).

## Technical approach

**The short plan (to `docs/delivery/epics/BD-430.md` when M7 opens) covers the following contract.**

1. **Refresh the rival audit.** Re-read and re-pin every incumbent at its then-current release (incumbents
   release often, and a rival frozen at stale pins is not the strongest): revision, container digest, licence,
   documented capabilities, strongest warm configuration tuned on reference tasks only. A new pin is a new
   harness identity. The frozen rival must reproduce its home-task training time within 20% on the Linux host.
2. **Arms per stratum.** Locomotion strata (`legged-quad`, `legged-biped`, `wheeled`, `tracked`, `hybrid`):
   MuJoCo Warp under MuJoCo Playground or mjlab, or Isaac Lab on Newton or PhysX, each with its own PPO recipe and
   one policy per design, or a published shared design-conditioned policy where one fits; for bipeds and
   humanoids the incumbent's humanoid training recipe; outer loop CMA-ES or Bayesian optimization with successive
   halving; per-world model fields; warm compilation caches; sampling control with no training where it helps.
   `throughput`: Newton with a solver that batches different topologies, or ManiSkill3, with mixed-topology
   batches. `envelope`, `sensing`: an expert's program over Drake, a linear or mixed-integer solver, an SMT or
   interval tool; a scenario language; system identification from the same calibration data. `rebody`,
   `failure`, `facility`: the locomotion configuration with retraining from a warm start, uniform randomization
   with the same monitors, an incremental mixed-integer program and a standard motion planner. One frozen
   configuration per stratum and arm, chosen as the fastest on the development suite.
3. **Everything documented is available and expected to be used.** Per-world model parameters in one batch,
   state-preserving model edits, opt-in run-to-run determinism, mixed topologies in one batch, electrical and
   thermal actuator models, sampling control without training, interior generators, agent access over MCP. An
   unused capability is explained in writing before the freeze.
4. **Advocates and budget.** Advocates with no FrankenRobotics authorship; per arm and stratum a tuning budget of
   max(40 device-hours, the compute the builders logged on the same development suite); development missions
   only; the builders' written under-built objection and the adjudicator's ruling; at least one easy-win
   development family won per stratum, or the freeze is refused.
5. **Equal terms.** The same agent model at one pinned revision, the same token and tool-call caps, a fresh
   context per campaign, no human in the loop; matched devices (one RTX 4090 of `trj` and the same number of CPU
   cores per campaign), order and card assignment randomized and counterbalanced, arms interleaved in time; the
   same calibration data, prior answers and proof problems; an advocate-written agent guide no longer than the
   product's documentation. A best-supported-platform result may be added and is never the ratio; a platform an
   arm cannot use prints `unavailable`.
6. **Freeze and publication.** Content-addressed manifests with the advocate's signed statement and the list of
   what more budget would have added, published after the suite commitment and before the opening; nothing
   changes afterwards; a defect voids the affected cells, re-run at a later gate. Each frozen configuration
   reproduces its development-fixture figure within 10% on the tournament hardware.
7. **Cold and warm.** Cold campaigns start from the card and nothing mission-specific; warm campaigns run the
   declared amendment sequences with equal cache budgets (a fair rival also caches graphs, factors, compiled
   kernels and policies); standing assets are declared with their measured build cost.
8. **The record.** For every campaign: outcome and clock; the phase ledger in thirteen exclusive phases that sum
   to the clock, with the resource table and the learner's share of training; cost columns; receipts; all on
   `trj` under the disclosure block (hardware and driver, versions, world count, step and decimation, solver
   settings, contacts, sensors, cold or warm, host load, and the rest of the block). The ceilings of the test are
   evaluated on this ledger.
9. **Optional arm D**: the product's own layer over the incumbent engine through the same verbs, built only if
   the ablation arm tied on a stratum at M3 or the owner decides so; reported when run, never in the ratio.

**The pilot.** *Refresh pilot.* After re-pinning, run each M3-frozen configuration unchanged on its M3
development fixtures at the new pins, and run the home task; for each new stratum (`legged-biped`, `tracked`,
`hybrid`) run two development missions with the default recipe. *Falsifier:* any M3 configuration's
development-fixture figure moves by more than 10% at the new pins (re-tuning is then budgeted at the full rule
for that stratum), or a new stratum has no incumbent that can represent its missions (the stratum's rival cells
print `unavailable` and the stratum is `descriptive`). The result is recorded either way.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The short plan exists with the nine parts; the refresh pilot has run and is recorded.
- [ ] Seed done-condition: the rival's phase ledgers for the tournament are recorded on `trj`, each with a
      resolving disclosure block and freeze manifest.
- [ ] Every stratum run has frozen, published manifests for the declared and the composed incumbent, dated after
      the suite commitment and before the opening; no pack byte changed afterwards.
- [ ] Each frozen arm reproduces its development-fixture figure within 10% on the tournament hardware and wins at
      least one easy-win development family per stratum.
- [ ] Cold and warm records are separate; warm records carry standing-asset costs.
- [ ] The ablation arm's counted program text is at most 2,000 lines where it is labelled as such; the unbounded
      composed incumbent prints its own line count.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Carried by the mechanism beads. The epic adds `epic_bd430_ledgers_under_disclosure`: every rival ledger of the
tournament resolves a complete disclosure block and the harness identity of the refreshed pins; and
`epic_bd430_both_rivals_present`: every cell run has ledgers for both rival arms or an `unavailable` record.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_m7_rival_arms.sh` (`needs=cuda`): on development missions, exercises the refreshed pins end
to end for one locomotion stratum and one family stratum: pack check, freeze on a rehearsal gate, one cold and
one warm campaign per arm, ledger build, disclosure check. Logs each command, exit code, wall time, pins and
harness identity, manifest hashes, clocks, exclusive tables, the unattributed share against 2%, and device-hours
against the budget.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 1.2 × the published home-task time as the reproduction limit (492 s for 410 s); the 10% band on
development-fixture figures; 2,000 lines. Negative controls: a rival frozen at the M3 pins without the refresh
(its harness identity differs from the registry's); a configuration switched per mission; a rival that leaves a
documented capability unused without a reason; a ledger with no disclosure block (refused as a Performance
figure).

## Considerations

- KILL-TENFOLD-TIE (M7): the ablation arm within a factor of 1.5 of the product's restricted mean time at the same
  accepted scope, or a recovery ratio of at least 0.8 on time and on compute, means the layer ships over that
  engine for the stratum; engine work then continues only for engine-internal gaps.
- Under the default, advocates are agent sessions with no product authorship and results print
  `rival: internally advocated`; at M7 external advocates are strongly preferable and the owner decides.
- Licences decide what may be published; an incumbent whose licence forbids publication is replaced by the next
  strongest and the replacement is printed.
- Every pin is frozen for the length of the tournament; a pin move voids the runs it spans.
