## Background

The ten-fold tournament at milestone M7 exists to produce one public artifact: the measured ratio of restricted
mean times to an independently accepted answer, per stratum, between FrankenRobotics and the strongest composed
rival, **published whatever it is**. The gate of the tournament (GATE-TENFOLD-07) passes when the report is
published with its ledgers and its kit, whatever it says; if it is not, M7 does not exit and no comparative claim
is made. The program committed in advance to what the report will say in each outcome, including the ones it
hopes not to see: a ratio under 3 withdraws the speed claim for the class, and a tie with a short robust program
over an incumbent engine is reported as a tie, with the layer shipped over that engine (BET-14).

This epic is split into tasks when M7 opens. Seed done-condition: a short plan exists and its pilot has run; the
measured ratio is published with its phase ledger whatever it is, and a tie with a short robust program over an
incumbent is reported as a tie.

Reason for an epic of its own: publication is where a careful measurement is most easily softened. Wording
written before the data exists, a report generated from records, and a kit that lets a stranger recompute every
number are the three protections.

## Scope

In: the short plan; the publication pilot; the report's content and generation; the wording per outcome; the
replication kit's content and the two replication levels; the limits statement; what changes in the program's own
documents after publication. Out: the report record's type and tables (N39a-36); verdicts (BD-429 and the scorer
beads); adjudication (BD-432); the kit's gate record (see `needs`).

## Technical approach

**The short plan (to `docs/delivery/epics/BD-433.md` when M7 opens) covers the following contract.**

1. **The report.** One table per cell (a stratum and a regime): the pooled ratio R̂; the one-sided 95% bounds
   [L, U]; the verdict; completion and coverage for each arm; the tie test against the ablation arm; the crossover
   reuse count N* and the standing-asset cost S for warm cells; the adjudication label. One phase ledger per arm
   per cell, with the ceilings evaluated on the rival's ledger. The recovery ratios with their intervals; the
   count of certificates that changed a decision; every stratum listed as run or `not run` with its reason; every
   voided cell and disqualification with its evidence; the pre-run notice (which cells were confirmatory, decided
   before any sealed run) reproduced first. Every ratio carries its regime, family count, pair count, deadline,
   interval, adjudication label and freeze hashes. The report is generated from the report record; nobody types a
   number.
2. **What the report says, by outcome on a confirmatory cell (fixed before any measurement).**

| Outcome | What the report says | What the program does |
|---|---|---|
| `withdrawn`: R̂ below 3 | "Measured R̂ with [L, U] on ⟨stratum, regime⟩. The speed claim for this class is withdrawn." | The class leaves the speed claim in the thesis, the README and every report; work packages whose only justification is speed for that class are re-planned |
| `measured`: R̂ from 3 up to 10, or 10 and above without the guard | "⟨R̂⟩ times shorter, and at least ⟨L⟩ with 95% confidence, on ⟨stratum, regime⟩." The word "ten-fold" is not used for the class anywhere | The ledger names the binding ceiling; lever work for the class continues only on phases whose removable share is at least 10% |
| `ten-fold` | "Ten-fold on ⟨stratum, regime⟩: measured R̂ with [L, U]", with S and N* for a warm cell; on fewer than 32 families, "ten-fold on the sealed suite" and no population wording | The ten-fold bet is met for that stratum and for no other |
| Tie with the ablation arm: it reaches the same accepted scope and RMT_C ÷ RMT_A is at most 1.5; or the recovery ratio is at least 0.8 on elapsed time and on compute | "A short program over ⟨engine⟩ reached the same accepted answers within a factor of 1.5", or "a layer over ⟨engine⟩ recovers ⟨r_T⟩ of the saving in time and ⟨r_C⟩ in compute." | Say so. Ship the layer over that engine for that stratum, behind the same verbs, schemas and checker. Keep engine work only for the gaps internal to an engine (two documented: a mixed-topology batch with run-to-run replay, and the Metal tier; two unverified and checked before they are relied on: replay of one world outside its batch, and solver-internal certificates). The ratio against the declared rival is then the layer's ratio, and no claim is made that the mechanisms need this engine |
| Engine advantage not established: RMT_C ÷ RMT_A above 1.5 with its lower bound at or below 1.5 | "The advantage of the engine over a layer was not established." | The layer is offered beside the engine for that stratum |
| The rival is faster: R̂ below 1 | The measured ratio | As `withdrawn`; where licences allow, the rival's method is adopted behind the verbs |
| `descriptive` | The numbers, with the reason no verdict was possible | Nothing is cited as passing or failing the bet |
| `disqualified` | The event, its scope and its evidence | Nothing is awarded to the other arm |

   Across strata: each stratum is its own result; an equal-weight portfolio ratio over the strata run is reported
   as descriptive; the bet is met when at least one named stratum has the verdict `ten-fold`.
3. **Limits printed on the report.** `reality: no-claim` (acceptance happens in simulation; the adjudicating
   simulator is a third model, not the world). The clock stops at the first accepted answer, so quality beyond
   the acceptance threshold is not scored. Both arms are driven by one agent; the ratio is conditional on it;
   human engineering time is outside the test. Cost columns are reported and no cost claim is made. Nothing is
   inferred about systems, versions or configurations that were not run, or about missions outside the sealed
   population; manipulation with hands, flight and soft bodies are outside the suite. Standing assets are
   charged; the engineering that produced either system is not. Step rate, rendering and safety are outside
   every claim here.
4. **The replication kit, published with the report.** Contents: the pre-registration and the pre-analysis; the
   mission cards, the world generators and the revealed keys; the referee protocol and the adjudicator's
   acceptance code; every freeze manifest with the scripts, wrappers, tag maps and agent guides it names; every
   ledger, receipt and agent transcript; the scorer, which depends on nothing outside the standard library;
   hardware fingerprints and disclosure blocks; a manifest of hashes over all of it. *Level 1, recompute:* from
   ledgers and receipts alone, the scorer reproduces every published ratio, interval, count and verdict, bit for
   bit, on a clean machine. *Level 2, re-run:* from the frozen manifests, on hardware with a comparable
   fingerprint, one sampled pair per stratum is run again for every arm (TARGET: the same acceptance outcome and
   a time within a factor of 1.5 in at least 90% of the sampled pairs). Limits: incumbent code is not
   redistributed (the kit pins revisions and gives fetch instructions); a kit built from a private scene or
   dataset is refused; every exported artifact carries the no-authorization flags, all false.
5. **External replication.** An external party re-runs the kit. Adjudication at M7 is external, or the word is
   not used until the external replication has passed; a kit that fails either level leaves the result labelled
   `unreplicated`.
6. **After publication.** The table of expected ranges in the program's thesis is replaced by the measured
   table in the next version of the design document; the README's status and every report follow the wording
   above; each outcome is recorded as an ADR; a kill outcome is not re-tested on the same suite. If the speed
   claim is withdrawn, the product is described by the claims that never depended on speed: any world can be
   re-run alone on the CPU from its address; comparisons carry error control that survives an agent's retries;
   family answers are checked by an auditor that shares no code with their producer; failures arrive as minimal
   replayable worlds with an interventional explanation; every result names its claim, its class and its
   evidence; and the energy ledger closes.

**The pilot.** *Publication dry run on the partial gate's record.* From the revealed data of the M3 partial gate,
build a kit and a report with the M7 generator; run level 1 on a clean machine with no network; give the kit to a
fresh-context reader session with ten factual questions (the ratio of a named cell, its interval, why a cell was
descriptive, which arm was the denominator, the binding ceiling, and so on). *Falsifier:* level 1 does not
reproduce bit for bit, or the kit needs anything outside itself, or the reader answers fewer than nine of ten
from the kit alone. The result is recorded either way and the generator is fixed before the tournament's freeze.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The short plan exists with the six parts; the dry run has run and its result is recorded.
- [ ] Seed done-condition: the report is published with one phase ledger per arm per cell, whatever the ratios
      are; a rehearsal cell constructed as a tie is reported with the tie sentence, not as a win.
- [ ] Every sentence about an outcome is one of the fixed forms above, generated from the verdict record.
- [ ] The kit is published with the report, and level 1 reproduces every published number bit for bit on a clean
      machine.
- [ ] The limits of part 3 appear on the report; the three no-authorization flags are false on every artifact.
- [ ] After publication the thesis table, the README status and the ADRs are updated as part 6 states.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Carried by the report-record and scorer beads. The epic adds `epic_bd433_wording_closed_set`: every outcome
sentence in a generated report matches one of the eight forms; `epic_bd433_tie_is_reported`: a verdict record
with a tie renders the tie sentence and the program action, never "ten-fold"; `epic_bd433_kit_manifest_complete`:
every hash named by the report resolves inside the kit.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_m7_publication.sh`: builds a report and a kit from the rehearsal store of the scorer's
end-to-end fixtures, runs level 1 in an empty environment (the scorer built from the kit's own sources), compares
digests, and checks the wording set. Logs each command, exit code, wall time, the report and kit hashes, the count
of numbers recomputed and matched, each sentence's form, and on a mismatch the first differing value with the
ledger it came from.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the rehearsal cells' verdicts and ratios (for example R̂ = 45/17 = 2.647, `withdrawn`). Negative
controls: a report edited by hand (its digest differs from the regeneration); a report that omits a withdrawn
cell; a tie printed as `measured` without the tie sentence; a kit with a private input (refused); a level-1 run
that needs the network.

## Considerations

- Repository visibility and the licence are the owner's decision; under the default (private) the kit and the
  offline auditor are the only artifacts prepared for a third party, and a private repository cannot be
  independently confirmed, which the report states.
- Under self-adjudication the report prints ratios and withholds the words "independently accepted" and
  "ten-fold" until an external re-run of the kit has passed.
- Incumbent licences decide what the kit may carry; the default is revisions and fetch instructions only.
- Publishing a negative or middling result is the point of the exercise; the exit review checks that the
  published text is the generated text.
