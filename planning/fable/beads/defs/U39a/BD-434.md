## Background

An **exit review** records that a milestone's demonstrations ran and its gates have values. A **fresh-context
session**, one that implemented none of the milestone's work, regenerates the gate report from a clean checkout,
reads every receipt, checks that every gate has a measured value and a caught negative control, records a
disposition for each, and records every decision due as an architecture decision record (ADR). It implements
nothing.

Milestone M7 is the ten-fold tournament: the thesis of FrankenRobotics is measured against the strongest composed
rival on a sealed suite, with neutral adjudication, and the measured result is published whatever it is. The
exit line is **the publication of the measured ratio with its phase ledger** (GATE-TENFOLD-07). No mechanism is
killed at M7. Three written rules are applied: the decision rule of the ten-fold bet (BET-13) and its wording
for each range of the ratio; the tie rule (BET-14); and disqualification of an arm from the scoped claim by one
false certificate.

The seed's done-condition: every gate of the ten-fold test due at M7 (GATE-TENFOLD-03 to -05 repeated, -07, -08,
-11, -12, -13) has a recorded value and a caught negative control, and the measured ratio is published with its
phase ledger.

## Scope

In: the M7 exit record; dispositions for the eight program gates due and the recurring gates; verification that
the written rules were applied per cell; the kill rules of the ten-fold test as applied; protocol-breach checks;
risks and open questions due; the positioning that follows; the M7 sweep of the decision records (NAB-30), read as
a part of the exit record and checked with `decision-sweep check --exit M7`. The review waits on the M4 exit
(BD-378), not on M5 or M6: M7 needs M4 only (plan §43.1, §43.7, §43.9), so the M6 review BD-427 is related, not a
prerequisite. Out: any measurement or repair; the shared review
procedure, exit record and runner (N40-05) and the exit as a query over the gate register (N40-04); the gate
beads whose records it reads (register rows N40-14, N40-16, N40-17, N40-20, N40-21, N40-22 and the fallback
ledger of the seven ten-fold kill gates N40-28); the tournament's own epics.

## Technical approach

**1. Procedure (in brief).** A session with no context of M7 and no implementer identity on any due row; a clean
clone at the exit candidate commit; the demonstration of the sealed-tournament scenario (a rehearsal on unsealed
development missions, and the checks run on the published report); the recurring gates and the negative-control
sweep; regeneration of the gate report and README table, equal to the committed files; a disposition per part
from its receipt (`accepted-pass`, `accepted-kill: <fallback>`, `recorded-fail: blocks`, `not-run: <reason>`,
`rejected: <reason>`); the risk register and open questions; the exit record `docs/delivery/exits/M7/EXIT.md`.

**2. Program gates due, with what the review checks for each.**

Recurring, each re-run at the exit candidate's commit (a recurring part whose receipt names an older commit is
not fresh; the due set of the exit query, N40-04 rule 2): GATE-TENFOLD-09 (ceiling before speed), GATE-GROUND-01,
GATE-SAFE-01, GATE-GAUNT-01, and of the later subsections GATE-GAUNT-02 (every known-answer row due by M7 passes
on its tier with a derivation file), GATE-GAUNT-03 (every exit from M2), GATE-GAUNT-05 (oracle hygiene) and
GATE-GAUNT-10 (the status table equals a fresh generation from the gate register): eight recurring parts, the
same set the M4 exit lists.

**3. Order checks from the ledger's hashes and times.** No sealed mission was opened before GATE-TENFOLD-03, -04,
-05 and -11 had records for *this* suite's hash (a pass at M3 does not carry); the suite commitment precedes
every freeze; every freeze precedes the opening; every card was shown at its arm's clock start; no family of the
tournament served the partial gate; receipts were released only after every arm had closed.

**4. Written rules the review verifies were applied, per confirmatory cell.** `withdrawn` (R̂ below 3: at the
tournament the futility stop is disabled and every cell runs to n): the speed claim for the class is withdrawn
from the thesis, the README and every report (KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED). `measured` (from 3 up to 10, or 10 and above
without the guard): the word is not used for the class. `ten-fold`: only with R̂ at least 10 against the stronger
rival, the exact guard, non-inferior completion and coverage, no tie, and the adjudication condition; on fewer
than 32 families only "ten-fold on the sealed suite". Tie (the ablation arm within a factor of 1.5 at the same
accepted scope, or a recovery ratio of at least 0.8 on time and on compute): reported as a tie, and the layer
ships over that engine for the stratum (KILL-TENFOLD-TIE). `descriptive`: a cell whose pre-analysis could not
reach its verdicts (KILL-TENFOLD-REACH), or with too few pairs or too many exclusions. `disqualified`: one false
certificate (KILL-TENFOLD-CERT) or a leak from sealed worlds (KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK); nothing is awarded to the
other arm. Adjudication: neutral per stratum, or cross-home and labelled, or descriptive (KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ).
Each outcome is an ADR. The review also checks the adjudication label: if adjudication was not external, the
word is absent from the published text until an external re-run of the kit is on record.

**5. Protocol-breach checks (the only grounds on which a tournament is voided and re-run).** A pin moved during
the freeze (the stack lock hash differs between the first and last campaign records); sealed material read by an
arm (the leak audit's record; canary values in transcripts); an arm changed after its freeze (a workspace hash
differing from its manifest). A tournament is not stopped or voided by an interim number.

**6. Open questions due, with defaults.** The neutral party and the adjudicating simulator (default: a pinned
incumbent CPU engine that neither arm trains in, run by a fresh-context session; ratios `self-adjudicated`).
Repository visibility and outside replication (default: private; the kit and the offline auditor are the
artifacts prepared for a third party). The tournament's compute envelope (default: fewer strata, each `not run`
with the reason).

**7. After publication.** The positioning follows the bet: the product is described by its measured ratio per
stratum, and by auditability (replay, qualification, certificates, the evidence ledger) where the ratio is under
3. The table of expected ranges in the thesis is replaced by the measured table.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance exit::m7::`: `due_gates` (the eight program gates plus the eight recurring parts:
GATE-TENFOLD-09, GATE-GROUND-01, GATE-SAFE-01, GATE-GAUNT-01, -02, -03, -05 and -10; a GATE-GAUNT-02 receipt at
the M4 exit commit → `not fresh`);
`exit_line_needs_publication` (no published report → `not-ready` even with every other part passed);
`m3_pass_does_not_carry` (pre-opening records for the M3 suite hash offered for the tournament → rejected);
`sealing_order_checked` (a ledger in which the opening precedes a freeze → rejected); `wording_matches_verdict`
(a report whose sentence for a `measured` cell contains the word → rejected); `stop_disabled_at_tournament` (a
cell ended early by a monitor at M7 → rejected as a protocol breach); `bets_due_m7` (the due set is exactly
{BET-13, BET-14}; BET-13 with the verdict `Falsified` on a scratch record whose every stratum is `withdrawn`
and no KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED decision on record → `open` → `not-ready`; a stratum `measured` between 3 and 10 is
neither `held` nor `falsified` for BET-13, as N00-28's reading states).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/exit_m7.sh` wraps the shared runner `scripts/exit/review.sh M7`: the tournament's numbers recomputed
by the standalone scorer from the kit's ledgers and receipts and compared bit for bit with the published ones;
the published text compared with the regenerated text; the order checks; the control sweep; the ADR listing;
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --bin decision-sweep -- check --exit M7` on NAB-30's record; and `FROB_TEST_SEED=0
cargo test -p frob-conformance exit::m7::`. It sources the shared helper `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and
writes `target/e2e-logs/exit_m7/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every log line carries `ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`.
Logs: each command, exit code, wall time, each recomputed ratio and interval beside the published one, the
sealing steps with hashes and times, kit digests, each recurring part with its receipt commit, each disposition,
and on failure the first differing value with artifact paths; the summary counts dispositions by kind and names
the gates touched. No sealed content is logged before the reveal.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the harness's own set as listed for GATE-TENFOLD-03. Controls the review must catch: a published
number that the scorer does not reproduce; a freeze manifest dated after the suite opening; a canary value from
a sealed world in an arm's transcript; a planted false certificate; a tie printed as a win; a self-certified
PASS; a pin moved between two campaigns.

## Considerations

- If the owner has not approved the tournament's compute, strata are `not run` with the reason; if nothing runs,
  GATE-TENFOLD-07 is unmet and M7 does not exit. The review does not substitute a smaller experiment.
- M7 needs M4, not M5 or M6; a stratum that needs a later mechanism which has not landed is `not run`.
- With the completion non-inferiority condition as written, a cell needs at least 59 families to reach the word;
  the review reads the owner's decision on that rule from the pre-registration.
- The review of a measured ratio under 3 on every class is still an exit: the publication is the observable.
