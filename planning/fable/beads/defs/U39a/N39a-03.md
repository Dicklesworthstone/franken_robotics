## Background

The quantity the ten-fold test measures is a time. DESIGN: the unit of success is an *accepted answer*, not a
simulation step and not a score. Three FACTs about published incumbent stacks settle the choice. (1) An
infinitely fast environment step shortens training by at most about 7.7 times (1/(1 − 0.87)) on one incumbent
stack and about 1.4 times on another, because the step is 87% and 29% of the respective loops. (2) An incumbent
already measures about 2.55 million humanoid world-steps per second. (3) In the one published agent-driven design
loop with a time breakdown, a round takes about an hour and is dominated by policy training, which no step rate
describes. A step rate also says nothing about whether the answer it produced is right. Steps per second are
therefore measured elsewhere as a parity requirement, and enter this test only through the phases they shorten.

**The clock.** T is the wall-clock time from the moment the agent receives the mission card and its permitted
inputs to the moment the adjudicator accepts the first correct answer with all requested scope and precision.
Unsuccessful runs, training, tuning, compilation, device transfer, queueing, investigation of unknowns, certificate
generation and checking, repair, review and every rejected submission are inside it. Time the harness itself
spends between an arm's request and its service is measured and charged to the phase `wait`; it is not subtracted.

**The restricted time.** Each stratum s (a set of mission families that share a robot class or an answer kind, and
a deadline) has a common deadline H_s fixed before the suite is sealed. A run not accepted by H_s is *censored*
and contributes H_s. The restricted mean time of an arm on a stratum is RMT = E[min(T, H_s)] over the sealed
missions and campaign seeds. No run is dropped: a crash of the arm's own software is time passing, and a run that
never finishes costs H_s.

This bead is the stopwatch: one definition of when T starts and stops, applied identically to every arm by a
process no arm controls. It is needed first at M1, when the rival loop is timed with the common agent on
development missions, and it is the input of every statistic of the test.

## Scope

In: clock start and stop events; the outcome classes of a campaign; censoring at H; resource-cap breach handling;
the campaign record; the override when an accepted answer is later shown wrong. Out: where the time went (the
phase ledger, N39a-23 and N39a-24); cost columns (N39a-04); ratios (N39a-27); the acceptance decision itself
(N39a-06, N39a-07, N39a-09).

## Technical approach

**1. One timekeeper.** A single harness process, started for each campaign by `frob-tenfold campaign start` (this
bead's subcommand of the operator tool of N39a-01), stamps every event of every arm with its host's monotonic clock
(for durations) and UTC (for the record). Arms never self-report a time. Card delivery and receipt issue pass
through this process, so both ends of T are stamped by it.

**1a. Start preconditions (DESIGN).** The timekeeper keeps a precondition registry for a campaign start. A bead
that owns a condition a campaign must meet before its clock starts registers a function that returns `ok` or a
refusal with its N39-REF reason and exit code. `campaign start` runs every registered precondition in registration
order before it stamps `card_delivered`; the first refusal refuses the start, printed in N39-REF's refusal shape
with its exit code: nothing is stamped, no campaign record is written and no card is delivered. What a refusal
means for the scheduled campaign (an exclusion, a void or a later start) is the registering bead's rule and is
recorded by it, so that no scheduled campaign is left without a record (the scorer's `missing-run`). This bead
registers none. The conditions are registered by the beads that own them (among them the workspace drift and
pair-freeze checks of N39a-18, the terms and agent-revision checks of N39a-19, the cold-start check of N39a-16,
the futility-stop check of N39a-35 and the tournament watcher of N39bp3-01); the card delivery of N39a-05 is the
atomic step that follows them. Reason: one entry point and one ordered list let every start check be audited in
one place, and no check can run after the clock has started.

**2. Events (DESIGN).** `card_delivered` (t₀: the mission card's public part and the permitted inputs are made
readable to the arm's agent in one atomic step); `submission_committed(i)`; `receipt_issued(i, decision)`;
`cap_breach(kind)`; `arm_exit(status)`; `harness_fault(kind)`; `deadline` (t₀ + H_s).

**3. Stop rule.** Let t_acc be the stamp of the first `receipt_issued` whose decision is `accepted`.
- If t_acc − t₀ ≤ H_s: outcome `accepted`, T = t_acc − t₀, `censored: false`.
- Otherwise the campaign is censored at H_s: the harness terminates the arm's process tree at the `deadline` event,
  and records `clock_s = H_s`, `censored: true`, `accepted: false`. An adjudication still in flight at the deadline
  finishes for the record (`late_decision` is kept as a descriptive field) and does not change the outcome: a
  correct answer, or a correct refusal, that arrives after the deadline is a missed deadline.
- A truthful `unknown` stops the clock only on missions whose question is whether the evidence identifies the
  choice; everywhere else it is not an answer and the clock keeps running.

**4. Outcome classes.**

| Outcome | Condition | Charged time | In the ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| `accepted` | accepted receipt by H_s | T | yes |
| `censored-deadline` | no accepted receipt by H_s | H_s | yes |
| `censored-cap` | a resource cap (devices, cores, memory, tokens, tool calls) was breached | H_s; the breach instant is recorded | yes; a breach is not a disqualification |
| `censored-exit` | the arm's own software exited or crashed and did not resume before H_s | H_s | yes; a crash is time passing |
| `overturned` | an answer was accepted and later shown wrong within its scope | H_s for the arm that submitted it | yes |
| `harness-fault` | a fault that is not the arm's own (harness, adjudicator service, host) | none | the pair is re-run once under the next campaign index (N39a-27) |

An arm's own crash never becomes a `harness-fault`. The classification of a fault as the harness's is made from
the harness's own health log before the pair's times are compared, and the record states the evidence.

**5. Campaign record (`campaign.json`, harness record; fields are DESIGN).**
```
{ "gate": "…", "arm": "A", "stratum": "legged-quad", "mission": "b3:…", "family": "f:…", "campaign": 7,
  "regime": "cold", "deadline_s": "14400",
  "seeds": { "agent_sampling": "…" | "unavailable", "optimizer": ["…"], "arm_world": ["…"] },
  "events": [ { "seq": 0, "kind": "card_delivered", "mono_ms": "0", "utc": "…" }, … ],
  "outcome": "accepted", "clock_s": "1500", "accepted": true, "censored": false, "submissions": 1,
  "late_decision": null, "freeze": "b3:…", "adjudication": "self-adjudicated", "hash": "b3:…" }
```
A *campaign* is one arm's attempt at one mission with one campaign seed; the seed fixes the agent's sampling seed
where the provider allows it, the optimizer seeds and the arm's own world seeds. `restricted_ms =
min(clock_ms, deadline_ms)` is computed by the scorer, never stored twice.

**6. Harness service time.** For every arm request the harness serves (card delivery, artifact hand-off, queueing
for a device the schedule assigned, submission intake), it records an interval from request to service. Those
intervals are handed to the phase ledger as `wait` and are inside T for the arm that waited.

**7. Rules that protect the bar (enforced here).** (a) The time score is never improved by lowering what counts as
accepted: the record stores the hash of the mission card, and a receipt that cites a different card is refused.
(b) `overturned` is appended as a new record that supersedes the outcome; the original is kept. (c) A late
correct refusal is `censored-deadline`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] T equals the difference of two stamps made by the harness process; no arm-supplied time enters any record.
- [ ] A campaign with no accepted receipt by H_s records exactly H_s and `censored: true`, and its process tree is
      gone within the configured grace period.
- [ ] A cap breach yields `censored-cap` with the breach instant; a planted arm crash yields `censored-exit`; a
      planted harness fault yields `harness-fault` and no charged time.
- [ ] `unknown` submitted on a mission of another kind does not stop the clock.
- [ ] An overturned acceptance scores H_s and keeps the original record.
- [ ] No code path deletes or omits a campaign record once `card_delivered` exists (INV-TENFOLD-05, first half).
- [ ] `frob-tenfold campaign start` runs the registered start preconditions in order before `card_delivered`; a
      refusing precondition leaves no stamped event and no campaign record, and returns its reason and exit code.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- clock::`
- `accept_before_deadline`: t₀ = 0, accepted receipt at 1,500,000 ms, H = 14,400 s → `accepted`, clock `"1500"`.
- `accept_after_deadline`: accepted receipt at 14,400,001 ms → `censored-deadline`, clock `"14400"`,
  `late_decision` set.
- `accept_exactly_at_deadline`: 14,400,000 ms → `accepted` (the rule is ≤).
- `second_submission_stops`: rejected at 600 s, accepted at 900 s → T = 900 s, `submissions: 2`.
- `cap_breach_censors`: breach at 3,000 s → `censored-cap`, clock `"14400"`, breach instant 3,000 s recorded.
- `crash_is_time`: arm exit at 100 s, no resume → `censored-exit`, charged H.
- `unknown_not_an_answer`: `unknown` on a qualified-design mission → clock continues.
- `overturn_supersedes`: outcome becomes `overturned`, charged H; the first record still parses and hashes.
- `no_run_dropped_property` (INV-TENFOLD-05): for 10³ random event sequences that begin with `card_delivered`,
  exactly one final outcome record exists and its charged time is T or H or it is `harness-fault`.
- `start_preconditions_in_order`: two registered test preconditions, the second refusing with exit 3 → the start
  is refused with that reason and exit code, the first ran, no event is stamped and no `campaign.json` exists;
  with the first refusing, the second is not called.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_clock.sh`: runs four synthetic arms on a rehearsal mission with H = 20 s (a script that
submits a planted correct answer after 3 s; one that never submits; one that exceeds a memory cap; one that
exits). Decisions reach the clock through the timekeeper's real receipt intake, issued by a rehearsal
adjudicator session that accepts the planted answer (the decision is planted and labelled `synthetic`; no
component of the clock is replaced). Asserts outcome, charged time and censor flag for each, and that the process
tree of the censored arm is gone. The script sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_clock/<utc>.ndjson`. Logs each command, exit code, wall time, the measured T beside the
expected value with a tolerance of 0.5 s, H, the outcome, the campaign record's hash, and on failure the first
differing field, the event list and the campaign directory. Fixtures are labelled `synthetic`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: min(T, H) for the six-family card of the scorer tests: rival times 9,000, 6,300, 14,400 (censored),
5,400, 11,700, 7,200 s and candidate times 1,500, 900, 2,100, 700, 14,400 (censored), 800 s at H = 14,400 s.
Negative controls: a planted timekeeper that subtracts harness service time (T shrinks; the test compares against
the stamp difference and fails it); a planted build that drops a crashed run (the property test fails); a planted
build that lets a late acceptance count. Determinism: outcome classification is a pure function of the event list
(D0); stamps are measurements.

## Considerations

- OPEN (OPEN-TENFOLD-5, owner the adjudicator, decided by N39b-30): H per stratum is fixed before the suite hash
  is committed, by the default rule (three times the frozen rival's median development time, rounded up to half
  an hour, and raised until the horizon cap H ÷ RMT_A on the development suite is at least 10) or a rule the
  adjudicator records in its place. H is part of the suite commitment and never changes after it. Development
  runs come before H exists and are censored at the provisional development deadline the card carries: 14,400 s
  at M1 (BD-143), and from M3 the stratum record's `development_deadline_s` (N39a-12, 14,400 s by default).
- An agent provider that does not expose a sampling seed makes campaigns non-repeatable; the record says
  `unavailable` and the result is stated as conditional on the pinned agent.
- Charging adjudication latency to the arm is deliberate (both arms pay it equally), so the adjudication service
  must be scheduled symmetrically (N39a-19); the harness measures its own service time to make asymmetry visible.
- A host suspend or clock step would corrupt durations: the timekeeper refuses to run when the monotonic source is
  not steady, and records the host's uptime at start and stop.
