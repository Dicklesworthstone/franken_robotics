## Background

An arm of the ten-fold test may submit an answer, be rejected, repair it and submit again; rejected submissions
and their repair are inside its clock. Unconstrained retrying would let an arm buy acceptance by repetition: with
independent attempts at level 5% each, fourteen submissions of a design with no margin give a 51% chance of at
least one false acceptance. And a rejection that reveals which worlds failed would teach the arm the sealed
worlds.

DESIGN: acceptance uses the protocol of the product's blinded qualification lane, run by the adjudicator on the
adjudicator's own sealed key. A submission is committed before any world is drawn. Each submission draws fresh
world clusters. The i-th submission on a mission is tested at level α_i = α / [i·(i + 1)] with α = 0.05. The arm
receives a **receipt** holding only the decision, the index, the level spent, the units used and a hash. A
rejected submission returns no per-world outcome. Retrying is allowed, costs clock time, and cannot buy acceptance
by repetition. Every arm is under the same schedule. Where the submission format allows it, the adjudicator scores
an answer without being told which arm produced it, and the paired results are released only after every arm of
the gate has closed.

Why the error rate holds: 1/[i·(i + 1)] = 1/i − 1/(i + 1), so the levels spent after m submissions sum to
α·m/(m + 1) and to α in the limit. Submission i is fixed before its worlds exist and is tested on clusters
independent of everything the arm has seen, so conditional on the past its chance of a false acceptance is at
most α_i; a union bound gives at most α for the whole mission, for any number of retries and any adaptive
strategy. The argument needs three things, and this bead enforces exactly those: the submission is fixed before
the draw, the clusters are fresh, and the level is spent whether or not the arm likes the answer.

Rejected: a cap on the number of submissions (arbitrary, and it rewards guessing the cap); returning failing
worlds to help repair (a leak of sealed content); the product's own lane service as the adjudicator's
implementation of record (the builders wrote it; the adjudicator runs the *protocol* with its own key and code).

## Scope

In: the commit step; the index and level ledger per (mission, arm, campaign); cluster-key derivation inputs; blind
scoring; the receipt record; release at close; the three rules that protect the bar as they bear on receipts.
Out: how a committed submission is decided (N39a-06, N39a-07); the clock events (N39a-03); leak detection.

## Technical approach

**1. Protocol for submission i of an arm's campaign on mission M (numbered steps).**
1. *Commit.* The arm calls `frob-tenfold submit` with the answer payload. The harness computes the payload hash,
   assigns `index = i` (1 + the number of earlier committed submissions of this campaign), and appends
   `{mission, campaign, arm_token, index, payload_hash, committed_at}` to the gate ledger **before any world for
   this index exists**. The clock event `submission_committed(i)` is stamped. A payload that differs from its
   commitment is refused; a commitment is never withdrawn.
2. *Spend.* α_i = 0.05 / [i·(i + 1)] is recorded as spent. The index advances for every committed submission,
   including ones later found malformed.
3. *Draw.* The adjudicator derives the units for this index from the sealed key: for battery kinds, the n_i world
   clusters; for family kinds, the probe points assigned to this index. The key is (sealed key, mission, campaign
   index, submission index, counter). The arm identity is not in the key, so the two arms of a pair are scored on
   the same units at equal index.
4. *Decide.* The acceptance procedure for the card's kind runs at level α_i.
5. *Answer.* The receipt is written, the clock event `receipt_issued(i, decision)` is stamped, and the receipt is
   delivered to the arm. Nothing else is delivered.
6. *Close.* When every arm of the gate has closed, the sealed key is revealed and per-unit outcomes, times and
   transcripts are released together.

**2. The receipt (DESIGN).**
```
{ "schema": "frob.tenfold.receipt/1", "gate": "M3-partial", "mission": "b3:…", "campaign": 7,
  "arm_token": "t:…", "index": 2, "alpha_total": "0.05", "alpha_i": "0.05/6", "alpha_spent": "0.05*2/3",
  "decision": "accepted" | "rejected" | "malformed", "units_used": 46, "answer_kind": "qualified-design",
  "payload": "b3:…", "simulator": "b3:…" | null, "adjudication": "external" | "self-adjudicated" | "cross-home",
  "sealed_until_close": ["world addresses", "per-unit outcomes", "probe points", "telemetry"],
  "authorization": {"execution_authorized": false, "hardware_safety_signoff": false, "sim_to_real_certified": false},
  "reality": "no-claim", "receipt_hash": "b3:…" }
```
Levels are exact rationals in strings. `receipt_hash` covers the record with that field blanked and is signed
under the adjudicator's key; a receipt that does not verify under the committed key is not a receipt.

**3. Blind scoring.** The decision procedure receives the payload under an opaque `arm_token` (a random token
mapped to the arm only in the sealed area). Where the payload format itself reveals the arm (a product bundle
against a rival's plain obligation file), the record says `blind: false` for that submission; blinding protects
judgement calls, and the battery and exact evaluations have none.

**4. What a rejection reveals.** Exactly one bit beyond the index and the units used. No per-world outcome, no
failing cluster, no probe point, no margin. A `malformed` decision (the payload does not parse, names the wrong
card, or violates the interface) is distinguishable from `rejected` so that an arm can fix a format error; it
still spends its level.

**5. Rules that protect the bar.**
- The time score is never improved by lowering what counts as accepted: thresholds, battery rule and level
  schedule are in the suite commitment; a receipt computed under any other rule fails verification.
- An accepted answer later shown wrong within its scope: the adjudicator appends an `overturn` record that cites
  the receipt and the evidence; the mission is scored unresolved at the deadline for the arm that submitted it.
- A correct refusal, or a correct answer, whose receipt is stamped after the deadline is a missed deadline.

**6. Equality across arms.** Same α, same schedule, same decision code and same simulator pin for all arms of a
gate; the scorer refuses a cell in which two arms' receipts cite different rule hashes (`acceptance-rule-differs`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For every receipt, the ledger holds a commitment with an earlier timestamp and the same payload hash; a
      planted draw-before-commit is detected and the submission is void.
- [ ] Levels: α₁ = 0.025, α₂ = 0.05/6, α₃ = 0.05/12; after m submissions the spent total is 0.05·m/(m + 1).
- [ ] The units of index i are disjoint from those of every other index and mission; both arms of a pair get the
      same units at equal index.
- [ ] A rejected receipt contains none of the sealed fields; a fuzzed reader of 10⁴ read attempts through the
      arm-side interface returns no sealed field before close.
- [ ] In 2,000 simulated no-margin missions with 500 adaptive retries each, a false acceptance occurs in at most
      123 (TARGET; the same bound the product's qualification lane must meet in its own no-signal test).
- [ ] An arm-issued "receipt" not signed under the adjudicator's key is refused; an overturn rescored at H.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- submission::`
- `level_series`: α_i for i = 1…5 equals 1/40, 1/120, 1/240, 1/400, 1/600 exactly; Σ_{i≤m} α_i = (1/20)·m/(m + 1)
  for m = 1…1,000 in exact rationals (the telescoping law).
- `commit_precedes_draw`: the draw function refuses an index with no commitment.
- `index_advances_on_malformed`: malformed at i = 1 → next submission has i = 2 and level 1/120.
- `receipt_has_no_sealed_field`: schema check against an allowlist of fields.
- `receipt_signature`: tampered `decision` → verification fails.
- `same_units_across_arms`: units(M, campaign 7, index 2) identical for arm tokens of A and B; different for
  index 3 and for campaign 8.
- `late_receipt_is_missed_deadline`: commit at H − 1 s, receipt at H + 30 s → the campaign is censored.
- `overturn_rescoring`: accepted then overturned → restricted time H.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_submissions.sh`: a scripted adversary arm submits a no-margin `synthetic` design 50 times on
a development mission with a stub decision procedure, reading every receipt; then attempts to read sealed fields
through every arm-side path. Asserts indices, levels, units used per index, the absence of sealed fields, and the
refusal of a payload that differs from its commitment. Logs each command, exit code, wall time, index, α_i as a
rational, units, decision, the receipt hash, each refusal with its reason, and the summary.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the level series and its sum α·m/(m + 1); relative battery cost of a retry (units at index 1, 10,
100, 500 are about 1.23, 2.57, 4.08 and 5.15 times a single test at α = 0.05). Negative controls: reused clusters
at a constant level (false acceptance in more than 99% of 500-retry campaigns); a receipt that carries a failing
world address; a draw before commit; an index reset by reopening the campaign (refused: the index is keyed by
mission and campaign, and a campaign cannot be reopened). Metamorphic: the decision of submission i does not
depend on submissions after it.

## Considerations

- The paired design means the rival's i-th submission and the product's i-th submission see the same sealed
  units. That is safe only because nothing per-unit is returned before close; any change that returns more than
  the receipt breaks the pairing's safety.
- Under the fail-closed adjudicator default (an agent session under a named human) receipts carry
  `self-adjudicated`; the signing key is then the operator's.
- Adjudication latency is charged to the arm's clock; the service must not favour an arm (N39a-19 interleaves).
- A leak of the sealed key voids every receipt drawn from it; detection and the consequence are the other unit's.
