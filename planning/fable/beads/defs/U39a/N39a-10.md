## Background

The ten-fold test rests on one sentence: an answer counts when a neutral adjudicator accepts it. If the
adjudicator's simulator is unsound, or its acceptance procedures accept wrong answers or reject right ones, every
time measured to "acceptance" is meaningless, and a sealed suite scored by it is spent. The program therefore puts
a gate in front of every sealed suite.

**GATE-TENFOLD-05 (claim kind: Known answer; TARGET; due at M3 before the partial gate's suite is opened, and
repeated at M7 before the tournament's).** Adjudicator acceptance:

1. the adjudicating simulator passes the known answers of the verification program that it can express, inside
   its own stated tolerances;
2. of 20 planted answers per stratum (10 that must pass, 10 that must fail) it classifies 20 correctly;
3. no arm's logs show the adjudicating simulator in training or tuning;
4. on a 5% sample of accepted answers the discordance with each arm's home referee is reported.

If the gate is not met, the suite is not opened. If no neutral simulator passes for a stratum, the kill rule
KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ decides the fallback (N39a-11).

This bead measures the gate: it is the acceptance test *of the acceptor*. It is separate from the mechanism beads
because the plan's rule is that a gate is registered with its failing control before its passing test is written,
and recorded by a session that did not implement the mechanisms.

## Scope

In: the planted-answer sets and how they are built; the four measurements; the gate record with measured values;
the negative controls of the gate; the end-to-end script. Out: the mechanisms under test (N39a-06 to N39a-09);
the program-gate register rows of GATE-TENFOLD-05 (an M3 part and an M7 part) and the `may_open` conjunction
with the other pre-opening gates GATE-TENFOLD-03 (N39b-22), -04 (N39a-40), -10 and -11 (N40-14, which depends
on this bead, reads this bead's records for the strata in scope of the suite and writes no second record of the
gate); the decision KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ (N39a-11); the M7 dry run of adjudication with a planted false acceptance
(the adjudication epic's pilot).

## Technical approach

**1. Clause 1: known answers the simulator can express.** From the known-answer registry (the rows of N38a-12,
read through its one loader, with their truths, tolerances and derivation files) select rows whose
physical case the adjudicating simulator represents natively (free flight and impact times, pendulum and top
invariants, a block on an incline below and above the friction angle, a rolling wheel's no-slip speed, a
quadruped's static load split on flat ground). For each: build the case through the neutral exchange description,
run it, and compare with the registered truth inside the simulator's **own stated tolerance** (its documented
integrator and contact tolerances at the pinned settings, written into the adjudication record before the run).
A row the simulator cannot express is listed `not expressible` with the reason; it is not a failure. The gate
needs every expressible row to pass, and at least one row per phenomenon the stratum's cards rely on
(`coverage: {contact, friction, actuation limits, terrain, clocks}`); a stratum with an uncovered phenomenon fails
clause 1 for that stratum.

**2. Clause 2: twenty planted answers per stratum.** Built by the adjudicator from development missions (never
sealed ones), by answer kind of the stratum:

| Stratum kind | 10 that must pass | 10 that must fail |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified design | Designs whose true pass rate in the adjudicating simulator is 1.0 by construction (a wide stability margin, verified by 1,000 development clusters) | 4 with true pass rate 0.5; 2 that pass on a fast tier and fail in the adjudicating simulator (tier traps); 2 whose controller misses its per-tick deadline; 1 whose export has a loss on a requirement term; 1 with a privileged-observation controller |
| Family answer, amendment verdict | Correct answers from the exactly solved fixtures (stance, docking, quantifier order), in both the bundle format and the plain obligation format | 2 false certificates with consistent hashes; 2 with a boundary endpoint flipped; 2 vacuous enclosures; 1 swapped quantifier prefix; 1 missed withdrawal; 1 narrower scope than asked; 1 band above the maximum |
| Truthful unknown | 10 unidentifiable cases with valid witness pairs | 5 `unknown` on identifiable cases; 3 witness worlds outside the family; 2 witnesses whose best choices coincide |
| Counterexample and repair | 10 planted failures with working repairs | 4 failures that do not reproduce; 3 illegal worlds; 3 repairs that still fail the submitted world |

Each planted answer is submitted through the real submission protocol at index 1. Threshold: **20 of 20** per
stratum. For the stochastic entries (true pass rate 0.5 against a threshold of 0.9 at 36 clusters) the chance of a
wrong classification is below 10⁻⁹, so 20 of 20 is the expected outcome of a correct adjudicator and not a matter
of luck.

**3. Clause 3: not used by any arm.** Run the condition-1 search of the adapter (N39a-08) over the freeze
manifests (`frob.tenfold.freeze/1`, N39a-18), tuning logs and process tag maps of every arm and stratum; the measured value is the count of hits, which must be 0. This
clause can only be fully recorded after the arms are frozen; before that the bead records it on the rehearsal
arms and the gate's row stays `pending: freeze` for this clause.

**4. Clause 4: discordance on a 5% sample.** After acceptance decisions exist (development phase: on development
submissions; gate phase: after close, on accepted sealed answers), sample 5% of accepted answers per arm and
stratum (at least 5; seeded from the gate's seed) and re-run each in that arm's home referee (the product's CPU
referee tier for arm A; the rival's CPU reference for B and C). Report, per arm and stratum, the share whose home
verdict differs and the per-cluster disagreement rate. The gate requires the number to be **reported**; it sets
no threshold, because the gap between a home simulator and the adjudicator is that arm's risk.

**5. The gate record (one per stratum and per gate instance, M3 and M7).**
```
{ gate: "GATE-TENFOLD-05", instance: "M3-partial", stratum, sim: SimPin, mode,
  ka: {expressible, passed, not_expressible: [...], coverage: {...}},
  planted: {must_pass: {n: 10, accepted}, must_fail: {n: 10, rejected}, misclassified: [...]},
  arm_usage_hits, discordance: {A: {...}, B: {...}, C: {...}} | "pending: after-close",
  controls: [{id, caught}], verdict: "pass" | "fail" | "pending: <clause>", recorded_by, hash }
```
A pass at M3 does not carry to M7: the simulator pin, the strata and the planted sets are re-run.

Verdict and the opening (DESIGN, one rule shared with the pre-opening verdict of N40-14). The `verdict` is
`pass` when clauses 1 to 3 are met on the frozen arms and the discordance table of the development phase is
present. The discordance on accepted sealed answers cannot exist before the suite is opened, so at the opening
it is `pending: after-close` inside a record whose verdict is already `pass`; it is appended after close and a
record that never receives it is reported as incomplete in the gate's report. `pending: freeze` (clause 3 only
measured on rehearsal arms) is not a pass and blocks the opening. `may_open` also requires the record's `sim`
pin to equal the adjudicator pin committed in sealing step 2.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For `legged-quad` and `wheeled` (and `envelope`, `sensing` where their cells are confirmatory), every
      expressible known answer passes inside the stated tolerance, with coverage of every phenomenon the cards use.
- [ ] 20 of 20 planted answers are classified correctly per stratum; any misclassification is listed with its
      decision record.
- [ ] Arm-usage hits are 0 for every frozen arm; a planted hit is detected.
- [ ] The discordance table exists for the sampled accepted answers, per arm and stratum.
- [ ] Every negative control below is caught, and the record names each with `caught: true`.
- [ ] The record is written by a session that implemented none of N39a-06 to N39a-09.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- gate05::`
- `planted_set_shape`: each stratum's set has exactly 10 and 10, built only from development missions.
- `misclassification_probability`: P(Binomial(36, 0.5) ≥ 36) = 2⁻³⁶ < 10⁻⁹ (the 0.5-rate plants cannot pass).
- `sample_is_five_percent_seeded`: 200 accepted answers → 10 sampled; the same seed gives the same sample.
- `coverage_required`: a stratum whose cards use films, with no film known answer expressible → clause 1 fails.
- `pending_states`: before freeze the verdict is `pending: freeze`, never `pass`; after freeze, with clauses 1
  to 3 met and the development-phase discordance present, the verdict is `pass` while the sealed-answer
  discordance reads `pending: after-close`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_gate05_adjudicator.sh` (header: `bead=N39a-10 milestone=M3 gates=GATE-TENFOLD-05
needs=cpu`). Steps: (1) print the simulator pin and the stated tolerances; (2) run the expressible known answers;
(3) submit and adjudicate the 20 planted answers of each stratum in scope; (4) run the arm-usage search on the
rehearsal arms; (5) sample and re-run the discordance set; (6) replay the negative controls through the control
runner of N38b-04 (`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test
<tier named by the gate's record> -- GATE-TENFOLD-05`), which writes each control's line (`caught`, `missed` or
`stale`) in the run receipt (a control's `last_seen` is written later, by the gate recorder, N38b-19); (7) write
the per-stratum gate records with the controls read from that receipt. The script writes no row of
`registries/gates.toml` (convention 9): the records and the run receipt are committed byte-identical under
`receipts/gates/` (N40-01), and the gate's part for the instance is entered from the receipt with `cargo run -p
frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TENFOLD-05 --receipt <hash> --part <M3|M7>`, citing the
per-stratum records by hash, by a session that implemented none of N39a-06 to N39a-09; this bead is the gate's
one recorder, as N40-14 names it. It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_gate05_adjudicator/<utc>.ndjson`. Each known answer is logged beside its registered truth
and tolerance; each planted answer with its
expected and observed decision, level and units; the hit count beside 0; the discordance shares with their sample
sizes. On failure the first differing value and the decision record path are logged. Nothing sealed is read.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 20 of 20; the stance boundary 3/14 m accepted with a closed endpoint and rejected with it opened;
battery sizes 36 (threshold 0.9) and 368 (threshold 0.99) at index 1. Negative controls, each of which the gate
must catch: (a) an adjudicator build that scores in the arm's home simulator (the tier-trap plants are then
accepted: 18 of 20); (b) an acceptance build that trusts certificate replay alone (the false-certificate plants
are accepted); (c) a simulator pin moved after acceptance (the record's `SimPin` no longer matches; the gate is
void); (d) an arm whose tuning log names the adjudicating engine (the registry entry TF-CX-15 of N39-CX, whose
planted input `judge_in_tuning_logs` is written by N39a-08, run in step 6 through the control runner); (e) a
planted set drawn from sealed missions (refused at construction). Determinism: the planted decisions are identical on a second run with the same seed.

## Considerations

- OPEN: which simulator adjudicates each stratum; fail-closed default a pinned incumbent CPU engine that no arm
  trains in, and cross-home adjudication, labelled, where it cannot represent the mission.
- Twenty planted answers per stratum is a small sample for an error rate; the gate is a known-answer check on the
  machinery, not a certification of the simulator's physics. Its physical adequacy is bounded only by clause 1.
- Planted answers come from development missions, which the arms have seen; that is harmless because the plants
  test the adjudicator, not the arms.
- Compute: clause 2 for a design stratum is about 20 × 36 episodes plus 1,000 clusters per must-pass design at
  construction; within the default envelope on a CPU.
