## Background

Answers in the ten-fold test are accepted in a **neutral adjudicating simulator**: one that neither arm used for
training, tuning or screening on the stratum, whose contact model was derived independently of both arms, that
runs deterministically on a CPU, and that is pinned by revision. Whether such a simulator exists for a given
stratum is not known in advance. Legged briefs on rigid terrain are within reach of a mature CPU multibody
engine; briefs with liquid films, tracks with grousers, soft ground or moving surfaces may not be. The plan
therefore writes the outcome of that uncertainty before it is measured.

**KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ.** *Trigger:* no neutral simulator passes GATE-TENFOLD-05 for a stratum. (GATE-TENFOLD-05 is
adjudicator acceptance: the simulator passes the known answers it can express inside its own stated tolerances;
of 20 planted answers per stratum it classifies 20 correctly; no arm's logs show it in training or tuning; the
discordance with each arm's home referee on a 5% sample is reported.) *Fallback, named in advance:* **cross-home
adjudication**, labelled: an answer must pass in the highest-trust tier of both arms (the product's CPU referee
and the rival's CPU reference), run by the adjudicator. If neither home referee can run the other arm's answers,
the stratum is `descriptive`: its numbers are reported and nothing is cited as passing or failing the ten-fold
bet. *Decided:* at M3 for the strata of the partial gate, and again at M7 for every stratum of the tournament.

This is a decision bead: it applies a rule that was fixed before any measurement, records the outcome either way
as an architecture decision record, and states what changes downstream.

## Scope

In: evaluating the trigger per stratum from the recorded values of GATE-TENFOLD-05; trying substitutes in a fixed
order; the decision record; the downstream changes. Out: the gate's measurement (N39a-10); the cross-home
mechanism (N39a-08); choosing candidate engines (owner decision in `needs`).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs.** For each stratum in the gate's scope: the list of candidate engines in the adjudicator's order of
preference (default candidate first; substitutes that meet the four conditions next), and one GATE-TENFOLD-05
record per (stratum, candidate) that was tried.

**2. Decision procedure (per stratum; numbered).**
1. Take the candidates in order. The first whose GATE-TENFOLD-05 record is `pass` on clauses 1 and 2 (known
   answers with full coverage; 20 of 20 planted answers) and has zero arm-usage hits is the stratum's adjudicating
   simulator: `mode: neutral`. The kill gate **does not fire** for this stratum.
2. If a candidate fails only because an arm uses it as a tool on this stratum, it is skipped for the stratum
   (that is substitution, not failure of the engine).
3. If the list is exhausted, the kill gate **fires** for the stratum. Run the cross-home feasibility check: each
   arm's accepted development answers (at least 5 per arm) are exported through the neutral exchange description
   and run in the *other* arm's home referee. Feasible iff both directions run every answer to a verdict (not iff
   the verdicts agree).
4. Feasible → `mode: cross-home`. Acceptance for the stratum is the conjunction of the two home verdicts at the
   submission's level; every record and report line of the stratum prints `adjudication: cross-home`.
5. Not feasible → `mode: descriptive`. The cell is opened as descriptive and says so before any sealed run; it is
   enlarged or re-examined for the next gate.

**3. The decision record (an ADR in the milestone in which it occurs).**
```
{ kill_gate: "KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ", instance: "M3-partial", stratum, fired: bool,
  candidates: [{sim: SimPin, gate05: "pass" | "fail: <clause>" | "skipped: used-by-arm <X>"}],
  cross_home: {feasible: bool, evidence: [...]} | null,
  outcome: "neutral: <sim>" | "cross-home" | "descriptive",
  trigger_text, fallback_text, decided_at, decided_by (adjudicator role), hash }
```
The record states the measured values beside the trigger, whichever way it went. It is written before the suite
hash is committed, because the adjudication mode of each stratum is part of the commitment.

**4. What changes downstream if it fires.**
- The per-stratum adjudication record (N39a-08) carries the mode; the acceptance procedures (N39a-06) run two
  simulators and take the conjunction.
- The verdict rule's "adjudication condition" (N39a-32): a `cross-home` stratum may carry a verdict but its report
  line always shows the label; a `descriptive` stratum can carry no verdict.
- The pre-analysis (N39a-33) is recomputed for a cross-home stratum, because requiring acceptance in two
  simulators lowers both arms' completion rates and may move the cell below its reachability conditions.
- The rival's home referee becomes part of the acceptance path, so its revision is frozen with the suite, not only
  with the arm.
- A kill outcome is not re-tested on the same suite; the stratum may get a neutral simulator at the next gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every stratum in the gate's scope has a decision record with the candidates tried and their gate values.
- [ ] The procedure is a pure function of its inputs: the same records give the same outcome and the same hash.
- [ ] A planted stratum whose only candidate misclassifies 2 of 20 planted answers yields `fired: true` and, with
      a feasible cross-home check, `cross-home`; with an infeasible one, `descriptive`.
- [ ] A candidate used by an arm on the stratum is recorded as `skipped`, and the next candidate is tried.
- [ ] The records exist before sealing step 2 of the gate, and the suite commitment includes their hashes.
- [ ] The decision is recorded as an ADR whether or not the gate fired.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- kill_adj::`
- `first_passing_candidate_wins`: [fail, pass, pass] → the second candidate; `fired: false`.
- `skip_when_used_by_arm`: [used-by-arm B, pass] → second candidate; `fired: false`.
- `fires_to_cross_home`: [fail, fail], feasible → `cross-home`.
- `fires_to_descriptive`: [fail], infeasible (one direction cannot import the other's answers) → `descriptive`.
- `feasible_means_runs_not_agrees`: both directions run with opposite verdicts on 2 of 5 answers → still feasible.
- `record_before_seal`: a decision record dated after step 2 of the same gate is refused by the sealing ledger.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_kill_adj.sh`: on a rehearsal gate with two synthetic strata, feeds one stratum a passing
candidate and the other a candidate planted to misclassify, runs the feasibility check with the product's referee
and a stand-in rival reference (labelled `synthetic`), and writes both decision records. Logs each command, exit
code, wall time, per-candidate gate values (known answers passed of expressible, planted answers classified of
20, arm-usage hits), the feasibility evidence, the outcome and the record hash; the summary names
KILL-TENFOLD-ADJ as decided for both strata.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the three outcomes on the three constructed inputs above. Negative controls: a decision procedure
that falls back to the product's own referee alone when no neutral engine passes (the test for the rejected
alternative: the outcome must be `cross-home` or `descriptive`, never `neutral: <product referee>`); a record
written after sealing; a stratum silently dropped from the scope instead of being recorded as `descriptive`.

## Considerations

- Fail-closed default while the owner's choice of simulator is open: a pinned incumbent CPU engine that no arm
  trains in; where it cannot represent the mission, cross-home adjudication, labelled.
- Cross-home adjudication is weaker than neutral adjudication in one specific way: an error shared by both home
  simulators (for example a common friction card) passes both. The label exists so that a reader weighs the
  stratum accordingly.
- Expect the trigger to be live for `tracked`, `hybrid` and any stratum with films at M7; expect it not to fire
  for `legged-quad` at M3 (HYPOTHESIS, settled by the gate's measurement).
- If every stratum of a gate ends `descriptive`, the gate records its ratios and no verdict; the milestone still
  exits on the record, and the adjudication question returns to the owner.
