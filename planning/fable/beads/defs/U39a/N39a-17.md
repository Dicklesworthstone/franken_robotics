## Background

A warm comparison in the ten-fold test measures how fast each arm brings an accepted answer up to date after an
amendment. An arm can make that fast by paying in advance: pretraining a policy for a whole class of robots,
compiling a bank of scenes, building a kernel cache, tuning a reward library. If that advance payment is left out,
the arm with the largest hidden investment wins. If it is charged in full against a single amendment, no
investment ever looks worthwhile.

DESIGN: a **standing asset** is anything an arm built for a mission class before a clock started. Each arm
declares its standing assets in its freeze manifest with their measured build cost S. Let ā and b̄ be the pooled
restricted mean times per amendment of arms A and B. The warm ratio at reuse count N is

    R_warm(N) = (S_B + N·b̄) ÷ (S_A + N·ā),

reported at N = 1, 10 and 100 and in the limit b̄ ÷ ā. The **crossover reuse count** N* is the smallest N at which
S_A + N·ā ≤ S_B + N·b̄: the number of amendments after which arm A's advance payment has paid for itself. The warm
verdict is evaluated at N = 10. Reason for 10: a class that needs a hundred reuses to pay for itself is reported
as such and is not summarized by its limit. A warm result is never quoted without S and N*.

Rejected: quoting the limit b̄ ÷ ā as "the warm ratio" (ignores S entirely); amortizing S over the amendments
that happened to be in the suite (makes the ratio depend on the suite's length); excluding assets the builders
call "part of the product" (a pretrained family policy is exactly what a rival would also have to build).

## Scope

In: the standing-asset declaration as the scorer reads it; S per arm and stratum; ā and b̄; R_warm(N) at 1, 10,
100 and the limit; N*; the undefined and degenerate cases; the fields the report must carry. Out: how warm
campaigns are run (N39a-16); the freeze manifest that carries the declaration (N39a-18); the interval and verdict
machinery applied to the warm cell at N = 10 (N39a-28, N39a-32).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs.**
- From each arm's freeze manifest for the stratum: `standing_assets: [{name, build_wall_s, build_device_s, hash}]`.
  S (the quantity in the formula) is wall time: S = Σ `build_wall_s` over the arm's assets for the stratum's
  mission class (DESIGN: the test's primary claim is wall time; `build_device_s` is reported beside it and enters
  the cost columns). An asset shared by several strata is charged to each stratum that uses it, in full (DESIGN:
  conservative; the report notes the sharing).
- From the warm ledgers: for each family i and amendment k, the restricted time min(T, H_warm) of each arm.

**2. Pooled per-amendment times.** ā = Σ_{i,k} min(T_A,i,k, H_warm) ÷ (number of amendment units); b̄ likewise.
The sum runs over every amendment of every family in the cell, failed amendments included at H_warm, and
amendments of a campaign with no accepted cold base at H_warm each. Both are exact rationals in milliseconds.

**3. The ratio and the crossover (exact arithmetic).**
1. R_warm(N) = (S_B + N·b̄) ÷ (S_A + N·ā) for N ∈ {1, 10, 100}; `limit` = b̄ ÷ ā.
2. N* = the smallest integer N ≥ 1 with S_A + N·ā ≤ S_B + N·b̄.
   - If ā < b̄: N* = max(1, ⌈(S_A − S_B) ÷ (b̄ − ā)⌉).
   - If ā = b̄: N* = 1 when S_A ≤ S_B (arm A is never behind), otherwise `never`.
   - If ā > b̄ (arm A is slower per amendment): the inequality holds exactly for N ≤ N_max =
     ⌊(S_B − S_A) ÷ (ā − b̄)⌋. If N_max ≥ 1, report N* = 1 and `overtaken_at` = N_max + 1, the first reuse count
     at which arm B is ahead for good; if N_max < 1, N* = `never`.
3. The warm cell's point estimate for the verdict rule is R̂ = R_warm(10). The per-family warm ratio used by the
   exact guard and the monitor is ρ_i = (S_B + 10·b̄_i) ÷ (S_A + 10·ā_i), with ā_i and b̄_i the family's own
   per-amendment means (DESIGN: the family is the unit, and each family is charged the full class-level S, which
   is what a single user of that family would face).

**4. Report fields (INV-TENFOLD-08, warm part: a warm ratio carries S and N*).**
`{regime: "warm", n_eval: 10, S_A_s, S_B_s, a_bar_s, b_bar_s, r_warm: {"1": …, "10": …, "100": …, "limit": …},
n_star: <integer> | "never", overtaken_at: … | null, assets: {A: [...], B: [...]}}`. The report writer refuses to
print any warm ratio whose record lacks `S_*` or `n_star` (`warm-without-assets`), and never prints `limit` in the
position of the ratio.

**5. Auditing S.** S is self-declared by the party that built the asset, which is the party with an interest in
understating it. Three checks: (a) `build_wall_s` and `build_device_s` must be backed by a build ledger (intervals
with resources) hashed in the manifest; (b) the adjudicator re-runs a declared fraction of the build where it is
cheap, or checks the asset's training-step count against the arm's measured step rate (wall ≥ steps ÷ rate);
(c) an asset present in the workspace at clock start and absent from the declaration is a foreign file under the
cold-start manifest check and refuses the campaign.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The warm worked card is reproduced exactly: S_A = 14,400 s, S_B = 0, ā = 470 s, b̄ = 4,050 s give
      R_warm(1) = 4,050/14,870 = 0.272, R_warm(10) = 40,500/19,100 = 2.120, R_warm(100) = 405,000/61,400 = 6.596,
      limit 8.617 and N* = 5.
- [ ] N* is `never` when S_A > S_B and ā ≥ b̄; N* = 1 when S_A ≤ S_B and ā ≤ b̄.
- [ ] A warm ratio without S and N* cannot be printed; the limit cannot be printed as the ratio.
- [ ] The warm verdict input is R_warm(10), not the limit and not R_warm(100).
- [ ] An undeclared asset in a workspace refuses the campaign; a declared S below steps ÷ rate is flagged.
- [ ] Results are exact rationals with the stated renderings, identical on both platforms.

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- warm::`

  renderings `0.272`, `2.120`, `6.596`, `8.617`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer (the warm card of the harness's own known-answer set): R_warm at 1, 10, 100: 0.272, 2.120, 6.596;
limit 8.617; N* = 5. It guards against quoting the limit as the warm ratio and against omitting a standing
asset. Negative controls, each a planted wrong scorer the test must catch: (a) one that reports the limit 8.617
as the warm ratio; (b) one that drops S_A and reports 8.617 at every N; (c) one that evaluates the verdict at
N = 100 (6.596) instead of N = 10 (2.120). Reading of the card: this class measures 2.12 after ten reuses, below
3, and above 6 only after a hundred. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- HYPOTHESIS: for family queries with amendments the expected ratio is 1 to 3 per query, more only through reuse
  across amendments, and cold may be below 1; N* is the number that tells a reader which regime they are in.
- Pretraining a family policy exceeds the default compute envelope; where an arm builds such an asset the gate
  needs the owner's approval of the pretraining class, and without it arm A runs with no family policy and S_A
  is correspondingly small.
- Charging the full S to every stratum that shares an asset overstates cost for a multi-stratum user; the report
  prints the shared assets so the reader can redo the arithmetic.
- S for arm B's incumbents includes only what the advocates built for the class (a tuned reward library, a warm
  compile cache), not the engineering that produced the incumbent, just as arm A's S excludes the engineering
  that produced FrankenRobotics.
