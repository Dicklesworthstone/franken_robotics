## Background

The phase ledger of the ten-fold test records, for every run of every arm, where the clock went. Its *exclusive*
table (one phase per instant; N39a-23) answers "what was the agent waiting for". Two things are still missing
before a ledger can be trusted and used.

First, the exclusive table hides work that ran in the background of something else: a compile that finished in
the shadow of a training job, device time burned while the agent was composing its next request. The **resource
table** gives device-seconds and core-seconds per phase, including that background work, so that compute cost is
not mistaken for zero.

Second, an instrumented timeline has holes. DESIGN, **closure**: the exclusive phases sum to T within 2%. Any
remainder is recorded as `unattributed`. A run with more than 2% unattributed keeps its place in the ratio, is
flagged `ledger-open`, and is excluded from ceiling arithmetic (**INV-TENFOLD-04**: exclusive phases sum to the
clock within 2%, or the run is flagged `ledger-open` and leaves ceiling arithmetic). Reason for both halves: a
run must never be dropped from the ratio because its instrumentation was poor (that would let an arm shed slow
runs by breaking its own tagging), and a ceiling computed from a table with a large unexplained hole is
arithmetic on a guess.

For rival arms the advocate's wrapper tags every process it launches, and the tag map is part of the freeze
manifest; this bead consumes the intervals that produces.

## Scope

In: the resource table and its rules; the learner's share of `train`; the closure computation and the
`ledger-open` flag; the ledger record with every field; the builder and checker commands. Out: exclusive
attribution (N39a-23); capture of intervals (N39a-25); cost columns derived from the resource table (N39a-04);
the readers (N39a-26, N39a-37).

## Technical approach

**1. Resource table (`phase/resource.rs`).** From every `work` interval of the raw record, whether or not it was
on the critical path:
- `device_s[phase]` += (end − start) × number of devices the interval held;
- `core_s[phase]` += measured CPU time of the interval's process tree (`cpu_ms`), or (end − start) × pinned cores
  where no measurement exists, with the record saying which;
- `learner_s[phase]` += the part of the interval spent in the learner (inference, storage, optimization), as
  reported by the training loop's own timers through the wrapper; it is populated for `train` and lets an engine
  difference and a learner difference be told apart.
`turn` intervals contribute nothing here (model latency is not local compute); `harness` and `adjudication`
intervals contribute to a separate `service` block that is reported and never charged to the arm's compute.
The table is not required to sum to anything: device-seconds can exceed the clock (two devices, concurrent jobs)
or fall far below it.

**2. Closure (`phase/closure.rs`).** With E the exclusive table and T the charged clock in milliseconds:
1. `unattributed` = T − Σ_{13 phases} E[phase] (non-negative by construction of the attribution).
2. `open` ⇔ unattributed × 100 > 2 × T (strictly more than 2%; exactly 2% is closed).
3. A `ledger-open` run: stays in every time ratio (pooled and per-family); is listed in the report's ledger
   status table; is excluded from ceiling arithmetic, from the per-phase ratio tables and from lever attribution;
   and counts toward the monitor's operational alarm when more than 10% of a cell's runs are open (DESIGN alarm
   level).
4. A cap-breach remainder flagged `censored-remainder` is not unattributed time; it is excluded from ceiling
   arithmetic by its own flag.

**3. The ledger record (one per campaign; sample values are those of the protocol's worked example).**
```json
{ "schema": "frob.tenfold.ledger/1", "gate": "M3-partial", "arm": "A", "stratum": "legged-quad",
  "mission": "b3:…", "family": "f:…", "campaign": 7, "regime": "cold", "deadline_s": "14400",
  "clock_s": "1500", "accepted": true, "censored": false, "submissions": 1,
  "exclusive_s": {"author": "600", "propose": "360", "check": "20", "synthesize": "0", "compile": "12",
                  "screen": "96", "train": "150", "evaluate": "60", "qualify": "40", "certify": "0",
                  "diagnose": "30", "wait": "42", "adjudicate": "90", "unattributed": "0"},
  "resource": {"device_s": {"screen": "96", "train": "150", "evaluate": "60"}, "core_s": {"check": "160"}},
  "agent": {"turns": 4, "tokens_in": "…", "tokens_out": "…"}, "designs_decided": 128, "trainings": 1,
  "disclosure_block": "b3:…", "freeze": "b3:…", "receipts": ["b3:…"] }
```
Fields beyond the sample that the record also carries (DESIGN): `status` (`ledger-closed` | `ledger-open`),
`resource.learner_s`, `resource.core_s_source` (`measured` | `pinned-cores`), `flags` (`censored-remainder`,
`human-intervened`), `intervals` (the hash of the raw interval file), `tag_map` (the hash of the tag map in
force), `amendment_seq` for warm records, and `hash`.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `clock_s`, `accepted`, `censored`, `submissions` | Copied from the campaign record; `clock_s` is the charged time (T, or H when censored) |
| `exclusive_s` | The thirteen phases and `unattributed`; sums to `clock_s` exactly |
| `resource` | Device-seconds, core-seconds and learner-seconds per phase; sparse (absent means zero) |
| `agent` | Turn count and token counts from the agent proxy |
| `designs_decided` | Distinct designs on which the arm recorded a keep-or-discard decision with evidence |
| `trainings` | Number of policy trainings or fine-tunes started |
| `disclosure_block` | Address of the disclosure block for the run's performance-relevant settings |
| `freeze` | The arm's freeze manifest for this stratum |
| `receipts` | The receipts of this campaign's submissions, in index order |

**4. Commands.** `frob-tenfold ledger build <campaign-dir>` reads `campaign.json` and `intervals.ndjson`, runs the
attribution and the two computations above, and writes `ledger.json`. `frob-tenfold ledger check <ledger.json>`
recomputes everything from the raw files and fails on any difference, on a sum that does not equal the clock, or
on a missing disclosure block or freeze reference. The reasons are rows of the ten-fold refusal registry N39-REF
(a dependency): an edited ledger or a raw-interval hash that does not verify is `record-hash-mismatch` (exit 9);
a reference that does not resolve is `ledger-disclosure-missing` (exit 3, mapped to FROB-REF-862). The scorer
re-runs the check on every ledger it reads; an edited ledger is a disqualifying event, detected here by the hash
of the raw intervals.

**4a. Input selection (`phase/inputs.rs`).** The two halves of INV-TENFOLD-04 are enforced by two selection
functions that every later reader calls instead of filtering on its own: `inputs::for_ratio(ledgers)` returns every
ledger of the cell whatever its status, and `inputs::for_ceilings(ledgers)` returns only `ledger-closed` ledgers
without the `censored-remainder` flag. `frob-tenfold ledger select --for ratio|ceilings <dir>` prints the selected
list. The readers built later (the ceiling arithmetic N39a-26 and N39a-37, the scorer N39a-27) depend on this bead
and test, in their own suites, that they read through these functions. Because scorer files call them,
`phase/inputs.rs` is inside the scorer boundary of the harness skeleton (N39a-01): it imports only `std`,
`frob_cert` and the boundary's own modules (`ids.rs`, `records/`), holds no file or TOML reading (the command
reads the directory and passes the parsed ledgers in), and this bead enters it in `BOUNDARY_FILES` and as a
`#[path]` module of `kit/scorer/`, so that the kit's scorer selects ledgers with the same code.

**5. The baseline uses of the record (M0 and M1).** The incumbent phases that need no agent (cold start; a
parameter edit and a topology edit to the first device step; training to the return threshold on one card and on
two) are written as ledgers with `gate: "M0-baseline"`, a single phase each (`compile` for the edit rows, `train`
with its `compile` sub-interval for the training rows) and `agent.turns: 0`. The agent-driven rival loop of M1
writes full ledgers with `gate: "M1-baseline"`. Both use this record unchanged, so the first ledgers on file are
the rival's. No freeze manifest exists before M3 (the first is written by N39a-18), so a baseline ledger's
`freeze` field holds the content address of the frozen comparator-registry row the run used (BD-039's registry,
frozen before the campaign by N36-25), and `ledger check` resolves it as it resolves a manifest; a baseline
ledger with no such reference fails the check like any other (`ledger-disclosure-missing`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `ledger build` on the worked attribution fixture gives the exclusive table of that fixture, `device_s`
      {compile 12, screen 18, train 48} and `status: ledger-open` (10% unattributed).
- [ ] The sample record parses; its exclusive values sum to 1,500 s; `ledger check` passes on a record built from
      matching raw files and fails when any exclusive value is edited.
- [ ] Closure boundary: 2.0% unattributed → `ledger-closed`; 2.1% → `ledger-open`; 3% → `ledger-open` and excluded
      from ceilings.
- [ ] A `ledger-open` run is returned by `inputs::for_ratio` and not by `inputs::for_ceilings`; a closed run with
      `censored-remainder` is returned by the first only.
- [ ] `phase/inputs.rs` is listed in `BOUNDARY_FILES`, passes the scorer boundary lint of N39a-01, and builds in
      `kit/scorer/` offline.
- [ ] Every ledger names a freeze manifest and a disclosure block that resolve; a missing one fails the check.
- [ ] INV-TENFOLD-04 holds over a gate's store as a query: no ledger with more than 2% unattributed is marked
      closed, and none marked open is among the inputs of any ceiling record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- phase::ledger::`
- `sample_record_sums`: 600 + 360 + 20 + 0 + 12 + 96 + 150 + 60 + 40 + 0 + 30 + 42 + 90 + 0 = 1,500.
- `resource_includes_background`: the worked fixture → `device_s.compile` = 12 although `exclusive_s.compile` = 0.
- `two_devices_double`: a 100 s training interval holding two devices → `device_s.train` = 200.
- `learner_share`: `train` interval 410 s with `learner_ms` 53,300 → `learner_s.train` = 53.3 (13%).
- `closure_boundary`: T = 10,000 s; unattributed 200 s → closed; 201 s → open.
- `open_stays_in_ratio`: over {closed, open, closed with `censored-remainder`}, `for_ratio` returns all three and
  `for_ceilings` returns the first only.
- `check_detects_edit`: change `train` from 150 to 140 → `ledger check` fails naming the field.
- `inv_tenfold_04_query`.
- `inputs_inside_boundary`: the boundary lint of N39a-01 passes with `tenfold/phase/inputs.rs` listed; a planted
  `use toml;` in that file fails it naming the line; `cargo build --offline` of `kit/scorer/` succeeds with the
  file included.
- `baseline_single_phase`: an M0 edit-to-first-step ledger of 45 s is `compile` 45, turns 0, closed; its `freeze`
  field names a scratch comparator-registry row and `ledger check` passes; with the field empty it fails with
  `ledger-disclosure-missing`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_ledger_closure.sh` (`bead=N39a-24 milestone=M0 needs=cpu`): builds ledgers with
`frob-tenfold ledger build` from three synthetic campaign directories (fully tagged; 3% of the clock untagged;
one whose `ledger.json` is then edited), runs `ledger check` on each, then runs `ledger select --for ratio` and
`--for ceilings` over the three and asserts the two lists (all three, of which the edited one fails its check
first; only the fully tagged one). Feeding the same directories to the scorer and the ceiling report is the
end-to-end script of each of those readers. It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_ledger_closure/<utc>.ndjson`. Logs each command, exit code, wall time, the exclusive
table, the sum beside the clock, the unattributed share against 2%, the status, the two selected lists, for the
edited ledger the first differing field with both values and the ledger path, and the summary.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the sample record; the worked attribution fixture. Negative control from the harness's own list:
**a ledger with 3% unattributed is flagged and excluded from ceilings** (and still counted in the ratio). It is
entry TF-CX-13 of the harness's counterexample registry (N39-CX; gates GATE-TENFOLD-03 and GATE-TENFOLD-02), and
this bead writes its planted input under the registry's plant name, as the fixture directory
`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/tenfold/controls/ledger_three_percent_open/` (the campaign directory with 3% of
the clock untagged that the script above builds from); the defect it plants is a build that marks that run
`ledger-closed` or hands it to `for_ceilings`. Further
controls: a builder that rescales phases to hide a hole (the check recomputes from raw intervals and differs); a
ledger whose resource table omits background work (the fixture's 12 s of compile); an edited receipt list (the
receipts are verified against the adjudicator's signatures).

## Considerations

- The 2% threshold is a DESIGN number; it is part of the pre-registration.
- A rival arm with poor tagging produces `ledger-open` runs that are useless for ceilings; because ceilings are
  computed on the rival's ledger before any speed test, the builders have an interest in the rival's wrapper
  being well tagged, and the tag map is reviewed before the freeze.
- Core-seconds from pinned cores overstate CPU use for idle processes; the record says which source was used,
  and the cost columns print it.
- The record carries no private-scene data; mission cards with private inputs cannot enter the suite at all.
