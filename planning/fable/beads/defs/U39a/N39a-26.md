## Background

The phase ledger of the ten-fold test records where each arm's clock went, in thirteen phases. Three consumers
read it, and this bead builds the reading side for two of them (the third, the ceilings, is N39a-37).

**Lever attribution.** The program's thesis is that no single mechanism delivers a ten-fold shortening; eight
levers each attack a different part of the loop. The ledger says which part each one could touch (DESIGN):

| # | Lever | Phases it acts on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decide many designs per turn (a family of designs per dispatch; a ranked family per agent turn) | `propose`, through the number of turns; `screen` |
| 2 | Train few (family policies per morphology class; inheritance along design edits) | `train` |
| 3 | Spend where it matters (a ladder of rungs; anytime-valid stopping; paired tier correction) | `screen`, `evaluate` |
| 4 | Find failures instead of waiting for them (adversarial search; minimal counterexample worlds) | `diagnose` |
| 5 | Answer over families instead of points (mission queries with inner and outer sets; region certificates) | `certify`, `propose` |
| 6 | Do not recompute or re-decide (memo graph; dependency-directed revalidation; campaign quotienting) | `check`, `compile`, `evaluate` |
| 7 | Explain by intervention (counterfactual forks with typed interventions) | `diagnose` |
| 8 | Short setup (kernels derived at build time and cached; topology as data; warm device state) | `compile`, `author` |

**The end-to-end rows of the performance program.** Three quantities that the performance program lists and this
test defines: row 20, *edit to first step*, is the `compile` phase of one edit, measured for the incumbent at the
M0 parity baseline and for both arms in every run; row 21, *designs decided per hour*, is `designs_decided` over
the clock; row 22 is the restricted mean time itself. The learner's share of `train` is recorded in the resource
table, so that an engine difference and a learner difference can be told apart.

**Lost factors.** Three kill gates elsewhere in the program promise that, if they fire, the factor the program
loses is recorded here: KILL-RANK, KILL-PARITY and KILL-GYMADAPT.

Reason for a mechanical reading: the funding rule of the test (a mechanism whose removable cost is under 10% of
the measured loop is not funded as a speed lever) is applied to the rival's ledger at M1; it must be computed the
same way every time, from closed ledgers only, by code the advocates can also run.

## Scope

In: pooled phase shares of a cell; the removable-share bound per lever; the per-phase saving table between two
arms and its lever view; rows 20 to 22; the lost-factor record. Out: ceilings and the refusal of speed
comparisons (N39a-37, N39a-38); the funding decision per lever (N39a-39); the ledger itself (N39a-24).

## Technical approach

**1. Pooled shares (`phase/levers.rs`).** For an arm and a cell, over its `ledger-closed` runs only (open runs
and cap-breach remainders are excluded): share(phase) = Σ_runs exclusive[phase] ÷ Σ_runs clock. Exact rationals.
The record lists the runs used and the runs excluded with their reasons.

**2. Removable share per lever.** For lever ℓ with phase set P(ℓ) from the table:
`p_max(ℓ) = Σ_{phase ∈ P(ℓ)} share_rival(phase)`, computed on the **rival's** ledger (arm B; or arm C where it is
the stronger rival). A registered removable share p(ℓ) for a work package must satisfy p(ℓ) ≤ p_max(ℓ); a claim
above the bound is refused (`lever-share-above-ledger`). Because levers share phases (`propose`: 1 and 5;
`screen`: 1 and 3; `evaluate`: 3 and 6; `compile`: 6 and 8; `diagnose`: 4 and 7), the bound for a *set* of levers
is the share of the union of their phases, each phase counted once; adding p_max over levers double-counts and is
refused in any product of lever effects.

**3. The saving table between two arms (how a measured ratio is attributed).** For arms X (rival) and A on one
cell: saved(phase) = Σ exclusive_X[phase] − Σ exclusive_A[phase] over the cell's pairs with both ledgers closed;
`saving_share(phase) = saved(phase) ÷ Σ_phases saved(phase)`. The per-phase column sums to exactly 1. The lever
view lists, for each lever, Σ over its phases, with shared phases marked; lever rows are not additive and the
table says so. Phases outside every lever (`synthesize`, `qualify`, `wait`, `adjudicate`) are listed as "no
lever". Also printed: the per-phase ratio Σ exclusive_X[phase] ÷ Σ exclusive_A[phase] (`n/a` where the divisor is
zero).

**4. Worked example (the single-pair card; unit-test fixture).** Arm B's exclusive seconds: `author` 900,
`propose` 1,080, `check` 240, `compile` 540, `screen` 0, `train` 4,920, `evaluate` 360, `qualify` 0, `diagnose`
600, `wait` 180, `adjudicate` 180 (clock 9,000). Arm A's: 600, 360, 20, 12, 96, 150, 60, 40, 30, 42, 90 (clock
1,500).
- Rival shares: `author` 10%, `propose` 12%, `check` 2.67%, `compile` 6%, `train` 54.67%, `evaluate` 4%,
  `diagnose` 6.67%, `wait` 2%, `adjudicate` 2%.
- p_max per lever: 1 → 12%; 2 → 54.67%; 3 → 4%; 4 → 6.67%; 5 → 12%; 6 → 12.67%; 7 → 6.67%; 8 → 16%. Under the
  10% funding rule levers 3, 4 and 7 could not be funded as speed levers on this (illustrative) ledger.
- Savings (total 7,500 s): `author` 300, `propose` 720, `check` 220, `compile` 528, `screen` −96, `train` 4,770,
  `evaluate` 300, `qualify` −40, `diagnose` 570, `wait` 138, `adjudicate` 90; `train` is 63.6% of the saving.

**5. Rows 20 to 22.**
- *Row 20, edit to first step.* From the raw intervals: for each design edit, the `compile` interval between the
  acceptance of the edit and the first device step of the edited design. Reported per arm as the 50th and 95th
  percentiles over edits, split by edit class where the arm reports it (a parameter edit inside a shape class; a
  topology edit inside an existing bucket; an edit that needs a new shape class), cold and warm. The performance
  program's TARGETs for arm A at the 95th percentile are 0.5 s, 2 s and 5 s (warm cache) for the three classes,
  with ambitions 0.1 s and 0.5 s for the first two; those are its thresholds, and this bead only supplies the
  measurement. For the incumbent the figure is unpublished (FACT about the sources read: an incumbent model edit
  needs a host compile, a model upload and a new graph capture) and is first measured at the M0 baseline.
- *Row 21, designs decided per hour.* `designs_decided` ÷ (clock ÷ 3600) per campaign; pooled per cell as
  Σ designs ÷ Σ hours.
- *Row 22, time to a qualified design.* The restricted mean time of the cell (the scorer's own quantity); printed
  here beside rows 20 and 21 so the three appear together.
- *Learner share.* `learner_s.train ÷ exclusive.train` per arm, pooled; printed with row 22.

**6. Lost-factor records (`tenfold/<gate>/lost_factors.json`).** One entry per kill gate that fired:
`{kill_gate, decided_at, fallback, phases, before, after, factor, basis: "measured" | "estimated", method, runs}`.
- *KILL-RANK.* Phases `train` and `screen`. `after` = arm A's pooled clock on the development suite with the
  cheap rungs in the fallback mode (they neither select nor discard, so more designs reach training); `before` =
  the same missions and seeds with selective rungs, where that configuration can be run; otherwise an estimate
  from the ledger (clock + extra trainings × mean exclusive time per training), labelled `estimated`.
  factor = after ÷ before.
- *KILL-PARITY.* Phases whose step share is known (`screen`, `train`, `evaluate`). With a physics step k times
  slower than the incumbent's and step share s of a phase, the phase lengthens by 1 − s + s·k. factor = pooled
  clock with each affected phase scaled ÷ pooled clock unscaled; k and s are measured values.
- *KILL-GYMADAPT.* Phase `wait`: the added interface latency per rollout when a rival learner drives whole
  rollouts through the job interface; factor from the learner-swap diagnostic (N39a-22).
A fired kill gate without its entry blocks the gate's report (`lost-factor-missing`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the worked card the shares, the eight p_max values and the eleven savings equal the figures of step 4.
- [ ] Shares use closed ledgers only; an open ledger changes no share and is listed as excluded.
- [ ] A registered removable share above p_max is refused; a product of lever effects that counts a shared phase
      twice is refused.
- [ ] The per-phase saving column sums to 1 exactly; the lever view is marked non-additive.
- [ ] Row 20 percentiles, row 21 and the learner share are printed for both arms of a cell; with 12 compile
      intervals of 45 s the rival's row 20 is 45 s at both percentiles.
- [ ] A fired kill gate with no lost-factor entry blocks the report.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- levers::`
- `worked_card_shares`: 4,920/9,000 = 41/75 (54.67%), 1,080/9,000 = 3/25, 240/9,000 = 2/75.
- `p_max_table`: the eight values above as exact rationals (lever 6: (240 + 540 + 360)/9,000 = 19/150).
- `union_not_sum`: levers {1, 5} → 12%, not 24%; levers {6, 8} → (240 + 540 + 360 + 900)/9,000 = 17/75.
- `saving_table`: the eleven savings; Σ = 7,500; `train` share 4,770/7,500 = 0.636.
- `negative_saving_kept`: `screen` −96 and `qualify` −40 are printed with their signs.
- `row20_percentiles`: edits (45 × 12) s → p50 = p95 = 45 s; arm A edits 1 s × 12 → 1 s.
- `row21`: 128 designs in 1,500 s → 307.2 per hour.
- `parity_factor`: s = 0.87, k = 1.5 → 1.435; s = 0.29, k = 1.5 → 1.145.
- `lost_factor_required`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_ledger_readers.sh`: writes the worked card as two `synthetic` closed ledgers and a third
open one, runs `frob-tenfold ledger shares`, `ledger levers`, `ledger rows` and a planted fired kill gate with
and without its entry. Logs each command, exit code, wall time, every share, bound and saving beside its expected
value (tolerance: exact), the runs used and excluded, and the summary naming GATE-TENFOLD-02 as touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the worked card; a step 1.5 times slower lengthens training by 1.15 to 1.44 for a step share of
0.29 to 0.87. Negative controls: shares computed on the wrong arm's ledger (lever 2 would read 10% from arm A's
`train` share instead of 54.67%); p_max summed across levers that share a phase; an open ledger silently included;
row 21 computed from designs *evaluated* instead of decided.

## Considerations

- The lever-to-phase table is a DESIGN mapping; a mechanism can act outside its listed phases (a memo graph also
  shortens `screen`). The table bounds what may be *claimed* for funding; the saving table shows what happened.
- Phase shares on the rival's ledger depend on how well the rival's tag map separates phases; the M1 baseline
  repeats a round to check that each share reproduces inside its stated interval.
- All figures on the worked card are illustrative arithmetic, not forecasts.
- OPEN (Lemma lead, due at M7): whether certificates change any design decision is counted from this ledger
  (the number of accepted answers whose decision changed after a `certify` phase); if the count is zero, proof
  work is evidence quality only and stays off the critical path.
