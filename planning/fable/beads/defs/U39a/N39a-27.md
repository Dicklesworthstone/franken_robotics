## Background

The word "ten-fold" in this program attaches to exactly one quantity. For arms A (FrankenRobotics) and B (the
strongest composed rival) on a stratum s with common deadline H_s,

    R_s = RMT_B ÷ RMT_A = E[min(T_B, H_s)] ÷ E[min(T_A, H_s)],

where T is the wall-clock time from the mission card to the adjudicator's acceptance and a run not accepted by
H_s is censored and contributes H_s. It is estimated by a **pooled ratio**: a ratio of sums over paired runs, not
an average of per-mission ratios.

DESIGN reason: an average of ratios is dominated by the missions where arm A is fastest and hides the mission it
never finishes; a ratio of sums charges that mission its full deadline. On the worked six-family card of this
bead, five of six per-family ratios lie between 6 and 9 and the pooled ratio is 2.65, because the one mission arm
A never finished costs it 14,400 s, more than the other five together.

The rule was also re-specified for power. The first draft required, on at least four of six briefs including both
held-out ones, a ratio of medians of at least 10 with a lower bound above 5, over five seeds. A Monte Carlo of
that rule confirmed a true ratio of 10 in at most 18% of campaigns (FACT about the planning computation), because
every brief needed its own point estimate above 10, which a true 10 delivers half the time, and the requirement
was multiplied across briefs. The pooled rule has one estimate per cell.

Where the composed incumbent (arm C, a short program over the incumbent engine) has a smaller restricted mean
time than arm B, the word is governed by the ratio against it: the word is never awarded on arm B alone.

## Technical approach

**1. Vocabulary (DESIGN).** A **cell** is a stratum and a regime (`cold`, or `warm`); regimes are never pooled.
A **campaign** is one arm's attempt at one mission with one campaign seed. A **pair** is the two campaigns of
arms A and B on the same mission with the same campaign index, scored on the same sealed acceptance worlds; with
arm C it is a triple, and "pair" below means the whole set. A cell has m mission families and c campaigns per
family: n = m·c pairs. Default m = 24 and c = 1; c = 2 where fewer than 24 families exist. The **family** is the
unit of inference. No verdict of any kind is issued on fewer than ten paired campaigns per arm (n ≥ 10); ten is a
floor for reporting anything, not a licence for a population claim.

**2. Input validation (`score/ratio.rs`; each failure is a refusal that names the pair).**
1. Every scheduled (family, campaign index, arm) has exactly one final campaign record and ledger, or a recorded
   supersession by a re-run, or a recorded exclusion (`missing-run` otherwise: no run is silently absent). The
   ledgers of a cell are taken through `inputs::for_ratio` of the ledger bead (N39a-24), so a `ledger-open` run is
   counted in every sum like any other.
2. Both ledgers of a pair cite the same mission, campaign index and deadline, and each cites the freeze manifest
   published for its arm and stratum, read from the step-4 entries of the sealing ledger (N39a-15, N39a-18)
   (`pair-mismatch`). Their campaigns ran under the same caps, checked by the cap comparison of the cost bead
   (`cost::caps_equal`, N39a-04: `caps-differ`), and their receipts cite the same acceptance-rule hash
   (`acceptance-rule-differs`, the rule of N39a-09). These are three rows' ids in the refusal registry, one per
   condition; regime and platform are check 4.
3. Every ledger passes its own check against its raw intervals; every receipt verifies against the receipt format
   and signature of N39a-09.
4. Regimes are not mixed in a cell (`regime-mixed`), and neither are platforms: a campaign record marked
   `platform: best-supported` (written by the equal-terms bead N39a-19) is scored only in its own labelled cell
   (`platform-mixed`).

**3. The pooled ratio (cold cell).** With x_j = min(T_X,j, H) in integer milliseconds, summed over all pairs j of
the cell that are not excluded:

    R̂ = Σ_j min(T_B,j, H) ÷ Σ_j min(T_A,j, H),

an exact reduced rational. The per-family ratio ρ_i is the same expression restricted to the pairs of family i.
For a warm cell R̂ is the warm ratio at ten reuses with pooled per-amendment times (N39a-17).

**4. The censoring rule.**
(a) A campaign not accepted by H contributes H.
(b) A pair in which both arms are censored stays in both sums (it contributes H to each; ρ = 1 for a family of
    such pairs).
(c) A harness fault that is not the arm's own is repaired by re-running the whole pair once under the next
    campaign index; a second harness fault excludes the pair for every arm, and it is counted. A cell with more
    than 5% of pairs excluded is descriptive only.
(d) When arm B is censored, ρ_i is a lower bound on that family's ratio, which is conservative for arm A; when
    arm A is censored, ρ_i is at most 1 when c = 1. The scorer labels each ρ_i, and every family gets exactly one
    label: `exact` (nothing censored), `lower-bound` (some B campaign censored, no A campaign censored),
    `at-most-1` (an A campaign censored, no B campaign censored, c = 1), `upper-bound` (an A campaign censored, no
    B campaign censored, c = 2: the family's ratio may exceed 1 through its other pair, and censoring can only
    have lowered A's sum), `both-censored` (every censored campaign is in a pair where both arms are censored), and
    `mixed` (A and B campaigns censored in different pairs of the family: no direction is known).
(e) A run is never dropped because its outcome is inconvenient, and an exclusion is decided before the pair's
    times are compared.
**INV-TENFOLD-05**: no run is dropped; a censored run contributes H; an exclusion is a harness fault recorded
before times are compared. Enforcement of the last clause: an exclusion or supersession entry must have been
appended to the gate ledger at fault time, with the harness's own health evidence, and its sequence number must
precede the gate's close entry (when times are released); an exclusion appended after the close is refused
(`exclusion-after-reveal`). The `exclusion` and `supersession` entry kinds are registered by this bead in the
sealing ledger's kind registry (N39a-15), with their payload schema and the roles allowed to write them.

**5. Excluded share.** `excluded_share = excluded pairs ÷ scheduled pairs`. The cell is `descriptive` when
excluded × 100 > 5 × scheduled. With 24 pairs one exclusion (4.17%) is tolerated and two (8.33%) are not.

**6. The stronger rival.** Compute Σ_B and Σ_C over the same pairs. The cell's denominator arm is
X = B if Σ_B ≤ Σ_C, else C, and R̂ = Σ_X ÷ Σ_A; the per-family ratios used by the guard and the monitor are
against the same X (DESIGN: one rival per cell, chosen by pooled time, so that the estimand stays a ratio between
two frozen arms). Both R̂_AB and R̂_AC are always printed. A per-mission best-of-rivals ensemble,
Σ_j min(x_B,j, x_C,j) ÷ Σ_A, may be printed as an extra lower bound and is labelled `ensemble: lower bound only`;
it never feeds a verdict.

**7. Output (per cell).**
```
{ cell: {stratum, regime}, m, c, n, deadline_s, rival: "B" | "C",
  sums_s: {A, B, C}, r_hat: {"rational": "45/17", "decimal": "2.647"}, r_hat_ab, r_hat_ac,
  per_family: [{family, rho: {"rational", "decimal"}, label}], censored: {A: k, B: k, C: k},
  excluded: [{family, campaign, reason, ledger_seq}], excluded_share, superseded: [...],
  ensemble_lower_bound, inputs_hash }
```

**8. The worked card (the six-family card; exact).** H = 14,400 s; "c" marks a censored run, charged H.

| Family | T_B | T_A | ρ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9,000 | 1,500 | 6.00 |
| 2 | 6,300 | 900 | 7.00 |
| 3 | 14,400 c | 2,100 | 6.857, a lower bound |
| 4 | 5,400 | 700 | 7.714 |
| 5 | 11,700 | 14,400 c | 0.8125 |
| 6 | 7,200 | 800 | 9.00 |
| Sum | 54,000 | 20,400 | R̂ = 54,000 ÷ 20,400 = 45/17 = 2.647 |

With one miss in six the completion ceiling is 54,000 ÷ 14,400 = 3.75 on these rival times and at most 1/q = 6 on
any. With six families there is no verdict (n is below 10).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The worked card gives R̂ = 45/17 (rendered 2.647), per-family ratios 6, 7, 48/7 (6.857, `lower-bound`),
      54/7 (7.714), 13/16 (0.8125, `at-most-1`), 9, and no verdict.
- [ ] The two planted wrong scorers are caught: averaging the per-family ratios (6.23) and dropping the censored
      family 5 (42,300 ÷ 6,000 = 7.05).
- [ ] A cell with a scheduled campaign that has no record is refused; an exclusion added after the close is refused.
- [ ] One harness fault leads to one re-run under the next campaign index, with the first attempt kept and marked
      superseded; two faults exclude the pair for all arms.
- [ ] 2 of 24 pairs excluded → `descriptive`; 1 of 24 → not.
- [ ] Where Σ_C < Σ_B the denominator is arm C and both ratios are printed.
- [ ] All arithmetic is in integers and rationals; output bytes are identical across platforms and build modes.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- score::ratio::`
- `six_family_card`: as above, exact.
- `wrong_scorer_average`: mean of ρ_i = 6.2307 ≠ 45/17 (the test asserts the scorer's value and that the mutant
  differs).
- `wrong_scorer_drop_censored`: without family 5 → 7.05.
- `both_censored_stays`: a pair (H, H) adds H to both sums; ρ = 1, label `both-censored`.
- `c_equals_two`: a family with pairs (9,000, 1,500) and (6,000, 3,000) → ρ = 15,000/4,500 = 10/3.
- `labels_total_with_c_two`: H = 14,400; pairs (9,000, 1,500) and (10,000, 14,400 c) → ρ = 19,000/15,900 =
  190/159 (1.195), label `upper-bound` (not `at-most-1`); pairs (14,400 c, 1,500) and (10,000, 14,400 c) →
  `mixed`; every generated family of 10³ random censoring patterns with c ∈ {1, 2} receives exactly one label.
- `harness_fault_once`: fault then clean re-run → the re-run's times are used; `superseded` has one entry.
- `harness_fault_twice`: excluded for A, B and C; `excluded_share` = 1/24.
- `excluded_share_boundary`: 1/24 → verdict-eligible; 2/24 → descriptive; 1/20 (exactly 5%) → eligible.
- `stronger_rival`: Σ_B = 54,000, Σ_C = 36,000, Σ_A = 20,400 → rival C, R̂ = 30/17.
- `missing_run_refused`, `pair_mismatch_refused` (different deadlines), `caps_differ_refused` (8 and 16 cores →
  `caps-differ`, not `pair-mismatch`), `acceptance_rule_differs_refused` (two receipts citing different rule
  hashes), `regime_mixed_refused`, `platform_mixed_refused` (a primary and a `best-supported` campaign in one
  pair).
- `open_ledger_counted`: a pair whose A ledger is `ledger-open` contributes its time to both sums exactly as a
  closed one would; R̂ is unchanged by the status.
- `freeze_hash_must_be_published`: a ledger citing a manifest hash absent from the step-4 entries →
  `pair-mismatch` naming the arm.
- `inv_tenfold_05_property`: for 10³ random gate stores, every scheduled campaign contributes T, H, or appears
  in `excluded` with a pre-close ledger entry.
- `n_floor`: n = 9 → the cell output carries `eligible: false (n < 10)`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_pooled_ratio.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N39a-27 milestone=M3 gates=GATE-TENFOLD-03 seed=39
needs=cpu`) is this bead's own script, built only on its dependencies. On a rehearsal store labelled `synthetic`
(three synthetic arms with published freeze manifests and step-4 entries, written with the commands of N39a-15
and N39a-18) it:
1. writes the six-family card as campaign records and ledgers, runs `frob-tenfold-score cell`, and asserts the
   sums 54,000 and 20,400, R̂ = 45/17, each ρ_i with its label, the censored counts and `eligible: false (n < 10)`;
2. adds arm C's campaigns with Σ_C = 36,000 and asserts rival C, R̂ = 30/17, and both ratios printed;
3. on scratch copies provokes each refusal of the validation step (`tenfold.missing-run`,
   `tenfold.pair-mismatch`, `tenfold.caps-differ`, `tenfold.acceptance-rule-differs`, `tenfold.regime-mixed`,
   `tenfold.platform-mixed`, `tenfold.exclusion-after-reveal`) and asserts the reason, the pair it names and
   exit code 3;
4. on a 24-pair cell records one harness fault, then two, and asserts the supersession, the exclusion for every
   arm and the excluded share beside 5%;
5. repeats step 1 under each of the two planted scorers through `e2e_expect_caught`: `avg_ratios` (6.23) and
   `drop_censored` (7.05) against 2.647.

It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_pooled_ratio/<utc>.ndjson`
with a transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines carry the exact
command line, exit code and wall time; `assert` lines carry the sums, R̂ as a rational and a decimal, each ρ_i with
its label, and each planted scorer's output beside the correct one with `caught`; `refusal` lines carry the
reason and the pair; on failure the first differing value and the record path; the `summary` line counts
assertions and names GATE-TENFOLD-03 as touched. The rehearsal `scripts/e2e/tenfold_rehearsal.sh` (N39a-41, which
depends on this bead) then runs the same scorer inside the whole pipeline; the regime and platform refusals are
also exercised end to end by the scripts of N39a-16 and N39a-19.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer (the six-family card of the harness's known-answer set): R̂ = 2.647; the completion ceiling 3.75;
per-family ratios 6.00, 7.00, 6.857, 7.714, 0.8125, 9.00. It guards against averaging ratios (6.23) and dropping a
censored run (7.05), both of which are planted wrong scorers in the harness tests. They are entries TF-CX-01 and
TF-CX-02 of the harness's counterexample registry (N39-CX) and are built here under the registry's plant names,
as `cfg` branches in `score/ratio.rs`, each behind its control's own mutation feature (never part of the
`gauntlet-mutations` umbrella): `avg_ratios` returns the mean of the per-family ratios (4,187/672 = 6.2307 on the
card) and `drop_censored` leaves out every pair with a censored arm-A run (42,300 ÷ 6,000 = 141/20 = 7.05).
`six_family_card` fails under each. Related registry rows: the
completion cap R ≤ 1/q with 25/3 at 12% unfinished (KA-009); the counterexamples "declare ten-fold when the point
estimate is at least 10 on every brief" (CX-003) and "report speed on the missions that finished" (CX-008).
Metamorphic relations: permuting families or pairs does not change R̂; scaling every time and H by a constant
does not change any ratio; adding a both-censored pair moves R̂ toward 1. Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- A ratio of sums weights long missions more. That is the intent (it is the ratio of total time a user would
  spend on the suite), and it means one very long mission can dominate a cell; the per-family table shows it.
- R ≤ H ÷ RMT_A whatever the rival does (the rival's restricted time cannot exceed H), so the deadline caps the
  measurable ratio; the pre-analysis requires that cap to be at least 10 before a cell may be confirmatory.
- Choosing the rival by pooled time after the fact introduces a small selection effect in favour of the rival
  (the smaller of two noisy sums); it is conservative for arm A and is accepted for that reason.
- Risk named in the program register: the tournament cannot confirm a true ratio of ten, for lack of power or
  because unfinished missions cap the ratio at 1/q; the per-cell completion counts printed here are its first
  signal.
