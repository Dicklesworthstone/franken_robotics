## Background

A system can be fast on what it finishes and finish less. The ten-fold test charges an unfinished mission its
full deadline, which already caps the ratio (if arm A misses the share q of deadlines the ratio cannot exceed
1/q; with 12% unfinished it cannot exceed 25/3 ≈ 8.33). The protocol adds a direct condition for the word
"ten-fold": the candidate must not be buying speed with lower completion or thinner answers than the rival.

DESIGN: for the word, arm A's **completion** rate by the deadline H, and on family strata its **coverage** (its
share of mandatory obligations discharged), must be non-inferior to arm B's: the exact one-sided 95% lower bound
on the difference A minus B is above −0.05 (a DESIGN margin, frozen before sealing). And rare failures get an
interval that is valid at zero counts: zero misses in 24 independent families still leaves a one-sided 95% upper
bound of 1 − 0.05^(1/24) = 11.7% on the miss rate, where a bootstrap of 24 zeros would report zero width.

The second sentence constrains the first. Any *exact* 95% lower bound on a difference of completion rates, on a
suite of m families in which both arms completed everything, can be no better than −(1 − 0.05^(1/m)): the
all-complete outcome has probability at least 5% when arm A's true miss rate is that large. So the condition
"above −0.05" is attainable only when m ≥ 59 (0.95⁵⁹ = 0.0485 < 0.05 ≤ 0.95⁵⁸ = 0.0510). With the default 24
families the best attainable bound is −0.1174. This bead implements the rule exactly as written and makes that
arithmetic visible; whether the margin, the level or the cell size should change is an owner decision (N39a-31).

Rejected: an asymptotic interval for the difference of proportions (zero width at zero counts, the defect the
plan names); a bootstrap of completion indicators (the same defect); pooling missions within families as if
independent.

## Scope

In: the family-level statistics for completion and for coverage; the exact lower bound on A minus B; the
non-inferiority decision at a margin; the zero-count upper bound; the table of smallest m at which the condition
is attainable; reporting. Out: the reachability decision (N39a-31); the verdict rule that consumes the result
(N39a-32); the bounded paired contrast and the restatement of these rules for population results (N39b-35, which
plugs its paired-difference form into this bead's bound).

## Technical approach

**1. Per-family values.** For family i with c campaigns per arm:
- completion: u_X,i = (number of arm X's campaigns in family i accepted by H) ÷ c;
- coverage (family strata only): v_X,i = (mandatory obligations discharged by arm X's accepted answers in family
  i) ÷ (mandatory obligations on the family's cards); a censored campaign discharges none.
The parameter of interest is Δ = E[u_A − u_B] (and likewise for v) over the population of families.

**2. The exact bound (DESIGN; `score/noninferiority.rs`).** Let W = #{families i in which arm A's value is
strictly lower than arm B's}. For every family, u_A,i − u_B,i ≥ −1 when A is worse and ≥ 0 otherwise, so
Δ ≥ −P(A worse). With families independent, W ~ Binomial(m, P(A worse)), and the Clopper–Pearson upper bound
p_U(W; m, α) on P(A worse) gives

    L_NI = −p_U(W; m, α),   p_U = the p solving Σ_{k=0}^{W} C(m, k)·p^k·(1 − p)^{m−k} = α,

an exact, distribution-free one-sided (1 − α) lower bound on Δ, with α = 0.05. For W = 0, p_U = 1 − α^(1/m).
It is conservative when arm A is often better (it never exceeds 0), which costs nothing here because the
question is only whether L_NI > −δ.

**3. The decision without root finding.** Non-inferior at margin δ (δ = 1/20) iff p_U < δ iff
BinCDF(W; m, δ) < α, that is

    Σ_{k=0}^{W} C(m, k)·1^k·19^{m−k} ÷ 20^m < 1/20,

evaluated in exact integer arithmetic (arbitrary precision from the auditor's crate; 20^m exceeds 128 bits for
m ≥ 30). The reported value of L_NI is found by bisection on a 10⁻⁶ grid with the same exact tail and rounded
outward (down).

**4. The same construction for coverage.** W_cov = #{families with v_A,i < v_B,i}; L_NI,cov = −p_U(W_cov; m, α).
On strata whose answers are qualified designs there are no obligations and only completion is tested.

**5. Zero-count bound for any rare failure.** For any per-family binary event observed 0 times in m independent
families (a missed deadline, a missed withdrawal, a false acceptance), the one-sided (1 − α) upper bound on its
rate is 1 − α^(1/m): 0.1174 at m = 24, 0.0894 at m = 32, 0.0631 at m = 46 (printed as upper bounds, rounded up).
For k > 0 events the bound is p_U(k; m, α). The report prints this bound wherever it prints a zero count; a
zero-width interval from zeros is never printed.

**6. Attainability table (printed by `frob-tenfold-score noninferiority --table` and used by the pre-analysis).**
The smallest m at which non-inferiority at margin 0.05 and level 0.05 can be concluded when arm A is worse in W
families:

| W | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| smallest m | 59 | 93 | 124 | 153 |

and, for a given m with W = 0, the bound −L_NI that a margin would have to exceed: 0.1174 (m = 24), 0.0894 (32),
0.0631 (46), 0.0496 (59), each rounded up.

**7. Output (per cell).** `{completion: {A: {accepted, of}, B: {...}, per_family: [...], W, L_ni, margin, level,
non_inferior: bool, attainable_at_this_m: bool}, coverage: {... | "not-applicable"}, zero_count_bounds: [...]}`.
`attainable_at_this_m` is false when even W = 0 cannot pass; the verdict rule and the pre-analysis read it.
The output is printed by this bead's subcommand of the scorer binary (N39a-01), `frob-tenfold-score
noninferiority <cell> [--json]`, which reads the cell's campaign records through the cell computation of N39a-27;
`frob-tenfold-score noninferiority --table` prints the table of step 6.

- [ ] p_U(0; 24, 0.05) = 1 − 0.05^(1/24) = 0.11734615…, rendered 0.1174; L_NI = −0.1174; not non-inferior at 0.05.
- [ ] The attainability table is reproduced exactly: 59, 93, 124, 153.
- [ ] m = 58, W = 0 → not non-inferior (0.95⁵⁸ = 0.05105); m = 59, W = 0 → non-inferior (0.95⁵⁹ = 0.04849).
- [ ] A cell where A is worse in 3 of 60 families → not non-inferior (BinCDF(3; 60, 0.05) = 0.647).
- [ ] Coverage uses the same routine with obligation shares; qualified-design strata print `not-applicable`.
- [ ] Every zero count in the report is accompanied by its upper bound; no interval of zero width is emitted.
- [ ] All decisions are exact (integer arithmetic) and identical across platforms.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- score::noninferiority::`
- `zero_count_bound`: m = 24 → 0.117346 (upper, rendered 0.1174); m = 32 → 0.089368; m = 46 → 0.063049.
- `attainable_m`: W = 0, 1, 2, 3 → 59, 93, 124, 153; and one less than each fails.
- `decision_exact`: 10³ random (m ≤ 200, W) against a big-rational reference of BinCDF at 1/20.
- `strictly_lower_counts`: families with equal values do not count toward W; a family where A is higher does not
  reduce W.
- `c_two_fractional`: c = 2, u_A = ½, u_B = 1 → the family counts toward W.
- `coverage_share`: obligations (6 of 6) for A against (5 of 6) for B → not worse; (4 of 6) against (5 of 6) → worse.
- `null_coverage`: 10⁵ simulated cells with P(A worse) = 0.05 exactly and m = 59 → the bound L_NI is above −0.05
  in at most 5% of cells (the error rate of the exact bound at the boundary).
- `bisection_outward`: the reported L_NI is never above the true bound.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_noninferiority.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N39a-30 milestone=M3 gates=GATE-TENFOLD-03 seed=39
needs=cpu`) is this bead's own script, built only on its dependencies. On `synthetic` cells it:
1. prints the table with `frob-tenfold-score noninferiority --table` and compares it, as exact values, with step
   6 (59, 93, 124, 153; 0.1174, 0.0894, 0.0631, 0.0496);
2. runs `frob-tenfold-score noninferiority` on cells of 24 and 59 families with full completion in both arms
   (not non-inferior with `attainable_at_this_m: false`; non-inferior), on a 59-family cell with one family where
   arm A missed (not non-inferior) and on a 60-family cell with W = 3 (BinCDF 0.647: not non-inferior);
3. on the 24-family cell asserts the zero-count bound 0.1174 beside its zero count;
4. repeats step 3 under the mutation `bootstrap_zero_counts` (below) through `e2e_expect_caught`.

It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_noninferiority/<utc>.ndjson`: each command with exit code and wall time; per cell m, W,
the exact tail against 1/20, L_NI against the margin, the decision and `attainable_at_this_m`; the plant's line
with its value beside 0.1174 and `caught`; on failure the first differing value with the output path; and the
summary naming GATE-TENFOLD-03 as touched. The rehearsal `scripts/e2e/tenfold_rehearsal.sh` (N39a-41, which
depends on this bead) runs the same table inside the whole pipeline.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer (the zero-count bound of the harness's known-answer set): 1 − 0.05^(1/24) = 0.1174; it guards
against a zero-width interval from 24 zeros. (0.11734615… rounds to 0.1173 to the nearest; the printed 0.1174 is
the outward-rounded upper bound, which is the rule for bounds in every harness record.) Related: the completion
cap R ≤ 1/q, 25/3 at 12% unfinished (KA-009). The bootstrap of 24 zeros is entry TF-CX-08 of the harness's
counterexample registry (N39-CX; GATE-TENFOLD-03) and is built here under the registry's plant name, the mutation
`bootstrap_zero_counts`: a `cfg` branch in `score/noninferiority.rs`, behind the control's own mutation feature
and never part of the `gauntlet-mutations` umbrella, that computes the zero-count bound as a percentile bootstrap
of the per-family indicators, so that 24 zeros give an interval of zero width (upper bound 0); `zero_count_bound`
fails under it. Further negative controls: a Wald interval for the difference (reports
[0, 0] for two arms that both completed 24 of 24, and would declare non-inferiority at any margin; the test
asserts the scorer does not); a bootstrap of indicators; a routine that counts missions instead of families on a
fixture of 24 missions on one site (it would report m = 24 where m = 1).

## Considerations

- As specified, a default cell of 24 families cannot satisfy the completion condition even with perfect
  completion in both arms; the pre-analysis must therefore report P(`ten-fold`) = 0 for such a cell unless the
  owner's decision changes the margin, the level, the cell size or the construction (N39a-31). Until that
  decision the fail-closed default is the rule as written.
- The bound ignores how much better arm A is where it is better; a sharper exact bound exists in principle
  (inverting a test over the full trinomial of better, equal, worse) but cannot beat −(1 − α^(1/m)) at the
  all-equal outcome, so it would not change attainability.
- Coverage shares depend on the adjudicator's obligation lists; they are fixed on the cards before sealing.
- The margin 0.05 is among the thresholds fixed when the first suite hash is committed.
