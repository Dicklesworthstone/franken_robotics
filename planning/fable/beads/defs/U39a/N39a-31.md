## Background

The ten-fold test awards the word "ten-fold" to a cell (a stratum and a regime) only when five conditions hold
together: the pooled ratio of restricted times is at least 10 against the stronger rival; an exact guard on
per-family ratios rejects; **arm A's completion and coverage are non-inferior to arm B's**; the ablation arm has
not tied; and the adjudication condition holds. The third condition is specified as: the exact one-sided 95%
lower bound on the difference (arm A minus arm B) in completion rate by the deadline, and on family strata in
the share of mandatory obligations discharged, is above −0.05, a margin frozen before sealing.

The same protocol fixes the default cell at 24 mission families (the family being the independent unit), states
that a population result needs at least 32, and warns that zero misses in 24 independent families still leaves a
one-sided 95% upper bound of 1 − 0.05^(1/24) = 11.7% on the miss rate.

Those statements cannot all hold for the word at the default size. This bead records the conflict, which was
found while the scorer was being specified, and asks the owner to resolve it before any suite is committed. It
follows the program's own hygiene rule: a frozen decision rule is enumerated before sampling, and a verdict that
cannot be reached must not be promised.

## Scope

In: the arithmetic that shows the conflict; the options with their consequences; the fail-closed default that
holds until the owner decides; what each option changes downstream; the record of the decision. Out: the
implementation of the bound (N39a-30), which is parameterized by margin and level and does not depend on the
outcome, and whose attainability table is the measurement this decision rests on; the general ratification of
thresholds (OPEN-TENFOLD-8, the owner-decision bead N39b-33, which depends on this bead and records the option
chosen here inside the ratified block). Also out, as the branch beads that depend on this one and test the
chosen mode in their own suites: the verdict rule's use of the parameter set (N39a-32), the pre-analysis'
reachable-verdict sets and P(`ten-fold`) under each option (N39a-33), and the routing of unreachable cells by
KILL-TENFOLD-REACH (N39a-34). This bead's own artifacts are the parameter-set record, its loader, the step-2
hook that writes the default, and the decision record.

## Technical approach

**1. The arithmetic (hand-checkable).**
- Let q be arm A's true probability of doing worse than arm B on a random family (for completion: missing a
  deadline the rival met). If arm B completes everything, the difference in completion rates is −q.
- On a suite of m independent families, the outcome "arm A is worse on no family" has probability (1 − q)^m.
- A procedure that reports a lower bound above −δ on that outcome must be wrong with probability at most 0.05
  whenever the truth is below −δ, in particular when q is just above δ. So it needs (1 − δ)^m ≤ 0.05.
- At δ = 0.05: 0.95^m ≤ 0.05 requires m ≥ 59 (0.95⁵⁸ = 0.05105; 0.95⁵⁹ = 0.04849).
- Therefore, with **any** exact 95% bound, a cell of m families in which both arms complete every mission cannot
  meet the margin 0.05 unless m ≥ 59. The best attainable bound is −(1 − 0.05^(1/m)): −0.1174 at m = 24, −0.0894
  at m = 32, −0.0631 at m = 46, −0.0496 at m = 59.
- If arm A is worse on one family, m ≥ 93 is needed; on two, 124; on three, 153.
Consequence under the rule as written: P(`ten-fold`) = 0 for every cell of fewer than 59 families, whatever the
true ratio. The planning table of the protocol (for example P(`ten-fold`) = 0.94 at a true ratio of 12 with 24
families) was computed with no censoring and without this condition, so it does not show the effect.

**2. Options for the owner (each is a complete, implementable rule).**

| Option | Change | Word reachable at m = 24? | Cost or risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. As written | None | No. Cells under 59 families are opened as `descriptive` for the word by the reachability kill rule; ratios and intervals are still reported | BET-13 cannot be met at the partial gate or at a 24-family tournament cell |
| B. Larger cells | Confirmatory cells have at least 59 families (c = 1) | Only at m ≥ 59, and only if arm A is worse on no family | About 2.5 times the sealed families and device-hours per confirmatory cell (a 24-family cell with three arms and a 4 h deadline is at most 288 device-hours; 59 families is at most 708) |
| C. Wider margin | Margin δ set so that it is attainable at the registered m with W = 0, for example δ = 0.12 at m = 24 or δ = 0.09 at m = 32 | Yes, when arm A is worse on no family | A weaker protection: the word could be awarded with arm A's true completion up to 12 points below the rival's |
| D. Point condition plus printed bound | Replace the inferential condition by: arm A's observed completion and coverage are not below arm B's on the sealed suite (a finite-suite statement), and the zero-count or exact upper bound on arm A's miss rate is printed beside the word | Yes | No error control on the population difference; consistent with a finite-suite result, not with population wording |
| E. Two tiers | Option D for finite-suite results (cells under the population size); option A or B for population wording | Yes for "ten-fold on the sealed suite"; population wording needs ≥ 59 | Two rules to explain; matches the protocol's existing split between finite-suite and population results |

This bead does not choose. It notes that option E is the only one that leaves both the default cell size and the
written margin intact, by reading the margin as a requirement of population wording.

**3. Fail-closed default until decided.** Option A: the rule as written. The scorer computes the exact bound; the
pre-analysis includes the condition in its enumeration of reachable verdicts; a cell whose registered m cannot
meet it is reported as failing a reachability condition and is opened as `descriptive` for the word, and the
report says so before any sealed run.

**4. What changes downstream, by option.** The verdict rule (N39a-32) takes the condition as a parameter set
`{mode: "exact-bound" | "finite-suite-point", margin, level}`. The pre-analysis (N39a-33) recomputes
P(`ten-fold`) with the chosen mode and with censoring from the development suite. The family registry (N39a-13)
changes its size table under option B. The pre-registration (the protocol bead of the partial gate) records the
chosen option with the owner's identity and date; the choice is inside the suite commitment and is never changed
after a sealed run.

**5. The decision's files.** The supporting file `docs/delivery/decisions/tenfold-noninferiority.md` (the request
and its analysis; the decision record of authority is the ADR under `docs/adr/` written by N39b-33, as the last
paragraph of this step states, so there is one place to look for the decision) and an entry in the gate's
pre-registration: the option, the parameters, the reasoning, the date, and the statement that it was taken
before the first suite hash. The parameter set is a record `tenfold/<gate>/noninferiority.json`
`{option: "A" | "B" | "C" | "D" | "E", mode, margin, level, population_mode | null, decided_by, decided_at |
"default-taken", hash}` with a loader that the verdict rule and the pre-analysis call. This bead registers a
step-2 precondition with the sealing ledger (N39a-15): if no decision record exists when step 2 is attempted, the
default of step 3 is written into the commitment automatically and flagged `default-taken`; a record dated after
the step-2 entry is refused.

One source per number (DESIGN, the program's rule that a decision has one record of authority). This question is
part of OPEN-TENFOLD-8, whose record of authority is the ADR and ratified threshold block written by the
owner-decision bead N39b-33 (which depends on this one): the block holds `noninferiority_margin` and `level` and
names the option chosen here. The decision file above is the supporting analysis that ADR cites by path and
digest. This bead's parameter-set record adds only what the block does not hold (`option`, `mode`,
`population_mode`); its `margin` and `level` fields are copies of the block's values, written from the same
decision, and N39b-33 refuses a block whose margin, level or named option differs from this record. Options C
and E that move the margin are therefore amendments of the block as well, never a second value.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- decision_ni::`
- `attainability_arithmetic`: 0.95⁵⁸ > 0.05 ≥ 0.95⁵⁹; best bounds −0.1174, −0.0894, −0.0631, −0.0496 at m = 24,
  32, 46, 59.
- `option_a_unattainable_at_24`: through the loader, option A's parameter set makes the N39a-30 routine report
  `attainable_at_this_m: false` at m = 24 (best bound −0.1174 against −0.05) and true at m = 59.
- `option_e_parameters`: option E's record gives mode `finite-suite-point` below the population size and
  `exact-bound` at margin 0.05 for population wording; a record with option E and no `population_mode` is
  refused. (The reachable-verdict sets under A and E are asserted by the pre-analysis bead N39a-33.)
- `record_after_step_two_refused`: a decision record dated after the step-2 entry is refused.
- `default_taken_flag`: sealing step 2 without a decision record writes option A and the flag.
- `mode_from_commitment`: a scorer run whose commitment says option C with margin 0.12 passes a 24-family
  all-complete cell on this condition; with 0.05 it does not.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_ni_decision.sh`: on a rehearsal gate, writes the parameter-set record of each of the five
options, runs `frob-tenfold-score noninferiority --table` and the routine on all-complete `synthetic` cells of 24
and 59 families under each, and attempts sealing step 2 with and without a decision record and with one dated
after the step. (Running the pre-analysis under the five options, with the reachable verdicts and P(`ten-fold`)
at a true ratio of 15, is a step of the pre-analysis bead's script, N39a-33, which depends on this bead.) It
sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_ni_decision/<utc>.ndjson`.
Logs each command, exit code, wall time, the option, m, the best attainable bound against the margin, the
decision, the record hash and whether it is inside the commitment, the `default-taken` flag, and the summary
naming KILL-TENFOLD-REACH as touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the table of step 1, which is registry row TF-27 of N39-KA (59, 93, 124, 153; the best bound
−0.1174 at m = 24), bound to the attainability table of N39a-30 and to `attainability_arithmetic` here.
Negative controls: a scorer with the margin hard-coded (the
`mode_from_commitment` test fails it); a decision recorded after the suite hash (refused by the sealing ledger);
a pre-analysis that omits the non-inferiority condition (it reports P(`ten-fold`) = 0.94 at a true ratio of 12
for m = 24 under option A, where the full rule gives 0; asserted by the pre-analysis bead N39a-33).

## Considerations

- This is a finding about the plan's internal consistency, not a change to it. It is raised because the
  alternative, discovering at the partial gate that no cell could have carried the word, would spend a sealed
  suite to learn something computable today.
- Censoring makes the picture worse, not better: any family on which arm A is worse raises the required m (93 for
  one such family at margin 0.05).
- The same arithmetic applies to coverage on family strata.
- Priority is one step above the milestone's because the decision blocks the first suite commitment.
