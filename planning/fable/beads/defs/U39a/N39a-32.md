## Background

The ten-fold test ends, for each cell (a stratum and a regime), in one word chosen from five. The rule that
chooses it was written before any measurement exists, and it has a history: a first draft, which required a
point estimate of at least 10 on each of several briefs, confirmed a true ratio of 10 in at most 18% of simulated
campaigns. The rule below has one estimate per cell. No honest rule can confirm a true ratio of exactly 10 more
than about half the time, because a fair measurement of 10 falls below 10 half the time; this rule reaches that
half, and reaches 80% at a true ratio near 11 to 12.

**Two studies, never mixed.** The verdict of a gate is a *fixed-n study*: its sample is fixed before the suite
opens, its rule is fixed, it has a pre-analysis of which verdicts are reachable, and it is computed once, at n. A
separate anytime-valid study monitors the incoming runs for operational decisions. **INV-TENFOLD-07**: no output
of a monitor enters the fixed-n verdict, and the verdict is computed once, at n.

**The verdict rule (DESIGN).** The rows are tested in order, and the first that applies is the verdict.

| Verdict | Condition |
|---|---|
| `disqualified` | A ground of disqualification applies to arm A in this cell |
| `descriptive` | n < 10, or more than 5% of pairs excluded, or the cell was not named confirmatory, or the pre-analysis found the cell underpowered |
| `withdrawn` | R̂ < 3; or, at the partial gate, the futility monitor stopped the cell |
| `ten-fold` | R̂ ≥ 10 against the stronger of arm B and the composed incumbent (arm C); and the exact guard rejects at the cell's adjusted level; and arm A's completion and coverage are non-inferior to arm B's; and the ablation arm has not tied; and the adjudication condition holds |
| `measured` | Every other case: the report states R̂ with [L, U] and does not use the word |

The threshold of 3 is a convention; it sits at the bottom of the range expected for quadruped briefs, so
withdrawal of that class at the partial gate is a live outcome and not a remote one. The thresholds 3 and 10, the
guard floor 5, the tie factor 1.5 and the margin 0.05 are fixed when the first suite hash is committed, and none
is changed after any sealed run.

On a cell of fewer than 32 independent families the verdict `ten-fold` is a finite-suite result: the report says
"ten-fold on the sealed suite" and uses no population wording. Population wording needs, in addition, a cell of
at least the family count its registered bound requires and a nonnegative lower bound on the bounded paired
contrast.

## Scope

In: the verdict function and its inputs; the once-at-n mechanism; the combiner that adds the monitor's futility
stop without letting it into the fixed-n computation; the wording state; the verdict record. Out: each input's
computation (pooled ratio N39a-27; interval N39a-28; guard N39a-29; non-inferiority N39a-30 in the mode of the
owner decision N39a-31; warm ratio N39a-17; monitor N39a-35); the disqualification grounds and their records
(the engine N39b-01); the bounded paired contrast behind population wording (N39b-35); the tie test and the
recovery ratios (N39b-43); report wording and program action per outcome (the outcome-rule engine N39b-05 and the
wording lint N39b-15); the external re-run of the kit that unlocks the wording (level 2, N39b-20, and its gate
record N39b-21); the pre-analysis record (N39a-33).

## Technical approach

**1. Inputs of the fixed-n function (`score/verdict.rs`; all from records, none from a monitor).**
`gate` (partial or tournament), cell identity, `named_confirmatory` and `underpowered` (from the pre-analysis
record), n, excluded share, R̂ against the cell's denominator arm, the guard's Holm decision, the non-inferiority
result for completion and (on family strata) coverage in the mode the commitment registers, the tie record,
the adjudication label and the replication record, the disqualification records for arms A, B and C, the family
count m and the cell's registered result type, and the thresholds read from the suite commitment. Three of these
records are written by beads that depend on this one, because each applies this function: the pre-analysis
(N39a-33, which simulates gates through it), the tie record (N39b-43) and the replication record (N39b-20). For
those three this bead defines the input view it reads (`PreanalysisView {named_confirmatory, underpowered,
hash}`, `TieView {tied: bool, against, hash}`, `ReplicationView {external_rerun_passed: bool, hash}`); the
producing beads write records that satisfy the views and test that in their own suites, and this bead's tests
build the views directly.

**2. The function (numbered; the first return wins).**
1. If arm A is disqualified in this cell: `disqualified` (the first row of the rule; for arm A the suspension of
   the proof kind follows from it). If arm B is disqualified as well, the verdict is still `disqualified` and
   `reasons` also carries `no-denominator`, so the cell is re-run at the next gate.
2. If arm B is disqualified in this cell and arm A is not: return `no-denominator` (not a verdict: the cell has
   no ratio, awards nothing to anyone, and is re-run at the next gate).
3. If n < 10, or excluded × 100 > 5 × scheduled, or not `named_confirmatory`, or `underpowered`: `descriptive`,
   with the reason.
4. If R̂ < 3 (exact rational comparison): `withdrawn`.
5. If all of: R̂ ≥ 10; the guard rejects; completion non-inferior; coverage non-inferior or not applicable; the
   tie record exists and says not tied; arm C is not disqualified (otherwise the comparison against the stronger
   rival and the tie test are unavailable): the *statistical verdict* is `ten-fold`.
6. Otherwise the statistical verdict is `measured`.
A missing input (no tie record, no pre-analysis record, thresholds absent from the commitment) is a refusal
(`verdict-input-missing`), never a default.

**3. The adjudication condition and the wording state.** The condition holds when the cell's adjudication is
`external` (neutral or cross-home, run by an external adjudicator), or when it was `self-adjudicated` and an
external party's re-run of the replication kit is on record. The verdict record carries both:
- `statistical_verdict`: the result of step 2, computed once;
- `wording`: `open`, or `locked-pending-replication` when the statistical verdict is `ten-fold` and the condition
  does not hold;
- `verdict`: equal to the statistical verdict, except that a locked `ten-fold` is published as `measured` with
  the note "all statistical conditions for the word are met; wording locked pending external replication".
When the replication record arrives, an `unlock` entry is appended and `verdict` becomes `ten-fold`; the
statistical computation is **not** run again.

**4. Result type.** `result_type` is `finite-suite` when m is below the population count registered for the cell
(at least 32 at α = 0.05; 46 where four confirmatory cells share the level) or when the cell declared it; then
the only permitted phrase is "ten-fold on the sealed suite". `population` wording additionally requires the
contrast record with a nonnegative lower bound; without it the record says `population-wording: not earned`.

**5. Once, at n (INV-TENFOLD-07).**
1. `frob-tenfold-score verdict <cell>` requires a `cell-closed` entry in the gate ledger: every scheduled pair is
   final (accepted, censored, superseded-and-re-run, or excluded) and the gate has closed for that cell.
2. It writes the verdict record with `inputs_hash` (the hash of every input record) and appends a
   `verdict-computed` ledger entry.
3. A second call with the same `inputs_hash` returns the stored record (a replay). A call with different inputs
   is refused (`verdict-already-computed`); a correction requires voiding the cell, not recomputing it.
4. A call before `cell-closed`, including at a monitor's stopping time, is refused (`verdict-before-n`): the same
   refusal the product gives a request that re-reads fixed-n data as a sequential study.
5. The function's signature has no parameter through which a monitor output could pass; a type-level test
   asserts that the input struct contains no field sourced from the monitor.

**6. The futility stop (partial gate only), outside the fixed-n function.** If the anytime-valid monitor stopped
the cell, the cell never reaches n and step 5 can never run for it. The *cell outcome combiner* then writes
`{verdict: "withdrawn", source: "monitor", level: "0.05", e_value, families_seen}` on the monitor's own authority.
Order of precedence in the combiner: disqualification of arm A, then a monitor stop, then the fixed-n verdict. At
the tournament the stop is disabled and the combiner only ever sees fixed-n verdicts: a monitor stop offered to
it for a cell of a gate of type tournament is refused with `monitor-stop-disarmed` (exit 3, a row of N39-REF) and
the cell continues to n.

**7. The verdict record.**
```
{ gate, cell: {stratum, regime}, m, c, n, deadline_s, rival, r_hat, L, U, guard, noninferiority, tie,
  adjudication: {label, condition_holds}, disqualification, preanalysis: "b3:…", result_type,
  statistical_verdict, wording, verdict, source: "fixed-n" | "monitor", reasons: ["…"],
  warm: {S_A, S_B, n_star} | null, freezes: {A, B, C}, thresholds: "b3:…", inputs_hash, computed_at, hash }
```
`reasons` lists, for `measured`, each condition of the word that failed, so that the report can name the binding
one.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision table below is reproduced case by case.
- [ ] A verdict requested before the cell is closed, or at a monitor's stopping time, is refused; a second
      computation with changed inputs is refused; a replay returns identical bytes.
- [ ] The fixed-n input type has no monitor-sourced field (compile-time or reflection test).
- [ ] A self-adjudicated cell whose statistical verdict is `ten-fold` is published as `measured` with the lock
      note, and becomes `ten-fold` on the unlock entry without recomputation.
- [ ] A 24-family `ten-fold` carries `result_type: finite-suite` and the only phrase offered is "ten-fold on the
      sealed suite".
- [ ] Thresholds are read from the commitment; a scorer started with other thresholds refuses.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_verdict.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N39a-32 milestone=M3 gates=GATE-TENFOLD-03 seed=39
needs=cpu`) is this bead's own script, built only on its dependencies. On a rehearsal store of `synthetic` closed
cells, whose pre-analysis, tie and replication inputs are fixture records written directly to the three views of
step 1 (the beads that produce those records come later), it:
1. runs `frob-tenfold-score verdict` on cells constructed for cases 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 9, 9a and 10 of the table and
   asserts verdict, reasons, result type and wording;
2. replays case 1 and asserts identical bytes; changes one input and asserts `tenfold.verdict-already-computed`;
   removes the tie record and asserts `tenfold.verdict-input-missing`;
3. requests the verdict of an unclosed cell, and of the monitor's planted input `peek_verdict` (TF-CX-09, the
   fixture of N39a-35: a cell stopped by the monitor), and asserts `tenfold.verdict-before-n` with exit 3, the
   second through `e2e_expect_caught`;
4. runs the combiner on the stopped cell and asserts `withdrawn`, source `monitor`; with the gate type set to
   tournament, asserts that the combiner refuses a monitor stop with `tenfold.monitor-stop-disarmed`;
5. appends the unlock entry to case 10 and asserts `ten-fold` with an unchanged `inputs_hash`.

It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes NDJSON to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_verdict/<utc>.ndjson` with
a transcript beside it. Every line has `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`; `cmd` lines carry the exact
command line, exit code and wall time; `assert` lines carry every input of the rule beside its threshold, the
verdict, the reasons and the inputs hash; `refusal` lines carry the reason and its first repair; on failure the
first differing field and the cell's store path; the `summary` line counts assertions and names GATE-TENFOLD-03
and KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED as touched. The rehearsal `scripts/e2e/tenfold_rehearsal.sh` (N39a-41) then runs the same
function inside the whole pipeline, with the tie record computed by the three-arm step instead of written by
hand.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the decision table; the six-family proportions give `withdrawn`. Negative control from the
harness's own list: **a request for the fixed-n verdict at a monitor's stopping time is refused** (TF-CX-09 of
the registry N39-CX; its planted input `peek_verdict` is the monitor bead's fixture, and step 3 of the script
above is where it is caught). Further
controls: a verdict function that awards the word on L ≥ 10 or on R̂ ≥ 10 alone (cases 2 and 16 fail it); one that
tests `withdrawn` before `descriptive` (case 7); one that compares R̂ in binary floating point at the boundary
(cases 5 and 6 with 3 and 10 represented as sums of milliseconds); one that recomputes on unlock with new data.
Determinism: D0.

## Considerations

- KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED is the kill gate this verdict feeds: on a confirmatory cell R̂ below 3, or a futility stop at
  the partial gate, withdraws the speed claim for that class. This bead computes the trigger; the decision and
  its consequences are the outcome rules'.
- Between true ratios of 7 and 10 the word can be awarded by chance, in up to a fifth of gates at 8.5; that is
  the price of a threshold on a measured quantity, and it is why the interval is printed beside the word.
- With the non-inferiority condition as written, a 24-family cell cannot reach `ten-fold` (owner decision
  N39a-31); the function takes the registered mode as an input and is unaffected by which option is chosen.
- `no-denominator` is this bead's name for the stated rule that a cell whose rival is disqualified has no
  denominator; it is deliberately not one of the five verdicts.
