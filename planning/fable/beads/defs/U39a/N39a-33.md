## Background

A frozen decision rule can be unable to return one of its verdicts. The program learned this from one of its own
planning pilots: a paired rule at 64 pairs could return three of its four verdicts, and the fourth was first
reachable at 86 pairs. The first draft of the ten-fold rule had a related defect: it confirmed a true ratio of 10
in at most 18% of simulated campaigns. The hygiene rule that followed is that every fixed-n rule is enumerated
before sampling, and a verdict that cannot be reached is not promised.

For the ten-fold test that rule becomes the **pre-analysis**: a computation, recorded before the sealed suite is
opened (step 5 of the sealing order), of how likely each verdict is at each true ratio, for each cell (a stratum
and a regime) at its planned size. A cell is **confirmatory** only if its recorded pre-analysis shows:

1. P(`ten-fold`) at least 0.8 at a true ratio of 15;
2. P(`withdrawn`) at most 0.05 at a true ratio of 5;
3. the guard reachable at its adjusted level;
4. the horizon cap H ÷ RMT_A on the development suite at least 10, since R ≤ H ÷ RMT_A whatever the rival does.

A cell that fails any of these is opened as `descriptive`, and the report says so before any sealed run (the kill
rule KILL-TENFOLD-REACH, decision bead N39a-34).

The protocol prints an illustrative planning table (not a result) under a stated model: lognormal times, a
per-family difficulty spread of 0.5 in the logarithm, per-family log-ratio spread s, no censoring, one
confirmatory cell, 20,000 simulated gates per entry. HYPOTHESIS: s lies between 0.5 and 0.8 on the design strata.
The binding pre-analysis is recomputed from the development suite's measured times, with censoring at H and the
multiplicity adjustment.

## Scope

In: the planning model and its table as a regression target; the binding pre-analysis from development data; the
four conditions; the choice of final m and of H under the default rule; the pre-analysis record. Out: the verdict
rule it simulates (N39a-32); the kill decision (N39a-34); the extension of the product verb `eval.preanalyze` to
ten-fold study plans with the population-claim counts and its refusal (N39b-34); the pilot that runs this
pre-analysis on the tournament's planned cells before M7 sampling (N39b-16).

## Technical approach

**1. The planning model (made precise so that the table can be regenerated).** For a cell of m families and true
ratio R, each simulated gate draws, independently per family i: a shared difficulty d_i ~ N(0, 0.5²) and two arm
noises e_A,i, e_B,i ~ N(0, s²/2); T_A,i = exp(d_i + e_A,i) and T_B,i = R·exp(d_i + e_B,i). The per-family
log-ratio ln(T_B,i ÷ T_A,i) then has mean ln R and spread s, and E[T_B] ÷ E[T_A] = R exactly. No censoring,
c = 1, one confirmatory cell (guard at level 0.05). A gate is `ten-fold` when R̂ ≥ 10 and the guard rejects
(X ≥ crit(m, 0.05)); `withdrawn` when R̂ < 3. 20,000 gates per entry.

| Families m | Spread s | Guard needs | P(`ten-fold`) at a true ratio of 5 / 7 / 8.5 / 10 / 12 / 15 / 20 | P(`withdrawn`) at 2 / 3 / 5 | True ratio at which P(`ten-fold`) reaches 0.8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | 0.5 | 17 of 24 | 0.000 / 0.002 / 0.08 / 0.50 / 0.94 / 1.00 / 1.00 | 1.00 / 0.50 / 0.000 | 11.25 |
| 24 | 0.8 | 17 of 24 | 0.000 / 0.03 / 0.20 / 0.49 / 0.83 / 0.98 / 1.00 | 0.98 / 0.50 / 0.005 | 12.0 |
| 12 | 0.8 | 10 of 12 | 0.002 / 0.05 / 0.19 / 0.39 / 0.65 / 0.89 / 0.98 | 0.93 / 0.50 / 0.03 | 13.75 |
| 6 | 0.8 | 6 of 6 | 0.006 / 0.05 / 0.12 / 0.23 / 0.38 / 0.57 / 0.77 | 0.86 / 0.49 / 0.08 | above 20 |
| 4 | any | unreachable | 0 at every ratio | 0.81 / 0.50 / 0.13 | never |

FACT (a recomputation made while this bead was drafted, with the model exactly as stated and 20,000 gates per
entry): every entry is reproduced within 0.015 (most within 0.005), and the last column exactly on a grid of
0.25. Two readings of the table: between true ratios of 7 and 10 the word can be awarded by chance, in up to a
fifth of gates at 8.5; and six families give a rule no better than the one that was rejected.

**2. The binding pre-analysis (`score/preanalysis.rs`; `frob-tenfold preanalyze --cell <stratum>/<regime>`).**
1. *Inputs.* The development ledgers of arms A, B and C for the stratum at the frozen configurations; the planned
   m and c; the stratum's H; the number k of confirmatory cells named for the gate; the thresholds and the
   non-inferiority mode from the commitment.
2. *Fit.* From the development families: μ_A = mean of ln T_A; σ_d = spread of the per-family mean log time across
   arms; s = spread of ln(T_B ÷ T_A) across families, with its small-sample interval; each arm's completion rate
   at H. With 12 development missions these are rough; the record prints the fitted values and runs the
   simulation at s and at the upper end of its interval.
3. *Simulated gate.* Draw families from the fitted lognormal model as in step 1; for a hypothesised true
   restricted-time ratio R, scale arm B's times by the factor κ(R) for which E[min(κ·T_B, H)] ÷ E[min(T_A, H)] = R
   (bisection on a fixed common sample of 10⁵ families); if no κ reaches R, the ratio is above the horizon cap and
   every probability at that R is reported with P(`ten-fold`) = 0. Apply censoring at H to both arms. Compute R̂,
   the guard at the first Holm level 0.05 ÷ k, completion non-inferiority in the registered mode, the excluded
   share (zero in simulation) and the verdict with the real verdict function. The tie test and the adjudication
   condition are assumed favourable and the record says so.
4. *Grid.* True ratios 2, 3, 5, 7, 8.5, 10, 12, 15, 20; 20,000 gates each; seed derived from the suite commitment.
   Normal variates come from the scorer's own inverse normal function applied to the scorer's generator, and the
   exponential from the scorer's own routine, so the table is bit-reproducible.
5. *Conditions.* (i) P(`ten-fold` | R = 15) ≥ 0.8; (ii) P(`withdrawn` | R = 5) ≤ 0.05; (iii) guard reachable:
   m ≥ ⌈log₂(k ÷ 0.05)⌉ (5 for one cell, 7 for four); (iv) H ÷ RMT_A ≥ 10 with RMT_A arm A's restricted mean time
   on the development suite. Also reported, because they can make the word unreachable: whether the
   non-inferiority condition is attainable at this m, and the reachable-verdict set (a verdict whose probability
   is 0 at every grid point and which an argument shows cannot occur is listed as unreachable).
6. *Decision per cell.* All four conditions hold → `confirmatory: eligible`. Otherwise `descriptive`, with the
   failing conditions. At most four eligible cells may be *named* confirmatory.
7. *Sizes.* The final sealed m is fixed here: the smallest m ≥ the planned m for which the conditions hold, if
   that many families exist; never below 12.

**3. The deadline under the default rule.** If the adjudicator has not set H (OPEN-TENFOLD-5, decided in
N39b-30; this is its fail-closed default): H = 3 × (the frozen rival's median
development time), rounded up to a multiple of 1,800 s, then raised in steps of 1,800 s until H ÷ RMT_A ≥ 10.
Example: rival median 5,400 s → 16,200 s; with RMT_A = 1,500 s the cap is 10.8 and H stands; with RMT_A = 1,800 s
the cap is 9 and H rises to 18,000 s.

**4. The record (`preanalysis.json`, hashed into sealing step 5).**
`{gate, cells: [{stratum, regime, m, c, k, H, fit: {mu_a, sigma_d, s, s_upper, completion}, table: {R → {p_tenfold,
p_withdrawn, p_measured, p_descriptive}}, conditions: [4 results], ni_attainable, reachable_verdicts, eligible,
named_confirmatory, result_type}], model: "lognormal/1", gates_per_point: 20000, seed, scorer_build, hash}`.
The verdict function reads `named_confirmatory` and `underpowered` (= named but not eligible, which the sealing
step refuses) from this record and from nowhere else, through the `PreanalysisView` that the verdict bead
(N39a-32) defines; this bead's record satisfies it, and `record_satisfies_verdict_view` tests that.

**5. Refusals.** Naming a cell confirmatory that is not eligible (`cell-not-reachable`); naming a fifth cell
(`too-many-confirmatory-cells`); running sealed campaigns for a cell with no pre-analysis entry
(`preanalysis-missing`); a pre-analysis recorded after the suite was opened (refused by the sealing order).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The planning table is regenerated from the model of step 1: each probability within 0.015 of the printed
      value (Monte Carlo tolerance at 20,000 gates), and the last column equal on a grid of 0.25.
- [ ] A four-family cell reports `ten-fold` unreachable and fails condition (iii); a six-family cell with four
      named cells fails condition (iii).
- [ ] With development data implying H ÷ RMT_A = 9.6 (H = 14,400 s, RMT_A = 1,500 s) condition (iv) fails; the
      default rule would raise H to 16,200 s, where the cap is 10.8.
- [ ] Under the non-inferiority rule as written, a 24-family cell reports `ten-fold` unreachable and is
      `descriptive`; under a registered finite-suite mode it is evaluated on the four conditions.
- [ ] Two runs with the same commitment give byte-identical records; the record's hash is in sealing step 5.
- [ ] The verdict function refuses a confirmatory verdict for a cell absent from the record.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- score::preanalysis::`
- `planning_table_24_05`, `planning_table_24_08`, `planning_table_12_08`, `planning_table_6_08`,
  `planning_table_4`: each row within tolerance.
- `true_ratio_is_ratio_of_means`: in the model, the sample ratio of means over 10⁶ families is within 0.5% of R.
- `power_crossing`: the smallest grid ratio with P(`ten-fold`) ≥ 0.8 is 11.25, 12.0, 13.75 for the first three
  rows and none up to 20 for the fourth.
- `holm_first_step_effect`: m = 24, s = 0.8, k = 4 (guard needs 18) → P(`ten-fold`) at 12 is 0.81 ± 0.015
  (against 0.83 at k = 1).
- `horizon_cap`: H = 14,400, RMT_A = 1,500 → 9.6, fails; default-rule H for a rival median of 5,400 s is 16,200.
- `censoring_caps_ratio`: with H ÷ RMT_A = 8 every probability of `ten-fold` is 0 and R = 10 is reported as
  unattainable.
- `conditions_to_eligibility`: each single failing condition yields `descriptive` with that condition named.
- `deterministic_record`.
- `record_satisfies_verdict_view`: the record of a two-cell gate converts to N39a-32's `PreanalysisView` for each
  cell with the same `named_confirmatory`, `underpowered` and hash; a cell absent from the record gives no view.
- `noninferiority_option_a_at_24` (from the owner-decision bead N39a-31): under the parameter set of option A
  (`exact-bound`, margin 0.05, level 0.05) a 24-family cell's reachable-verdict set excludes `ten-fold` and
  P(`ten-fold`) = 0 at every grid ratio; the same simulation with the condition omitted gives about 0.94 at a true
  ratio of 12 with s = 0.5 (the negative control of N39a-31), and the test asserts the full-rule value 0.
- `noninferiority_option_e_at_24`: under option E (`finite-suite-point` below the population size) the set
  includes `ten-fold`, and population wording is refused below the population size.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_cell_preanalysis.sh` (`bead=N39a-33 milestone=M3`; named apart from `tenfold_preanalysis.sh`,
which is the GATE-TENFOLD-10 script of N39b-36): regenerates the planning table (in `E2E_SMALL=1` with 2,000 gates per
entry and a tolerance of 0.04, marked not claimable), runs the binding pre-analysis on `synthetic` development
ledgers for a 24-family and a four-family cell, names cells, and attempts the three refusals. A further step
(moved here from N39a-31, whose script cannot call this bead) runs the binding pre-analysis for a 24-family and a
59-family cell under the parameter set of each of the five non-inferiority options and prints the reachable
verdicts and P(`ten-fold`) at a true ratio of 15. It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_cell_preanalysis/<utc>.ndjson`. Logs each command, exit code, wall time, every table entry
beside its printed value and tolerance, the fitted s with its interval, the four conditions with measured values
and thresholds, eligibility, the option and reachable-verdict set per cell, each refusal
(`tenfold.cell-not-reachable`, `tenfold.too-many-confirmatory-cells`, `tenfold.preanalysis-missing`), the record
hash, on a table entry outside tolerance its row, column and both values, and the summary naming GATE-TENFOLD-04
and KILL-TENFOLD-REACH as touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the planning table; the critical counts 17 of 24, 10 of 12, 6 of 6 and none at 4; the first Holm
step 18 of 24 with four cells. Negative controls: a draft rule "point estimate at least 10 on every one of six
briefs" simulated in the same model confirms a true ratio of 10 in at most 18% of gates (CX-003; the test
asserts the pooled rule's 0.50 and the draft's low figure); a pre-analysis without censoring on data whose
horizon cap is 8 (it would report high power; the censored one reports 0); a model with the median per-family
ratio set to R instead of the ratio of means (P(`withdrawn`) at 3 comes out near 0.07 at m = 24, s = 0.8, not
0.50; the regression catches it). A confirmatory cell of four families is entry TF-CX-07 of the harness's
counterexample registry (N39-CX; gates GATE-TENFOLD-03 and GATE-TENFOLD-04), and this bead writes its planted
input under the registry's plant name, as the `synthetic` fixture directory
`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/tenfold/controls/four_family_cell/`: a rehearsal gate store with development
ledgers of arms A, B and C for one stratum and a pre-analysis request that names a cold cell of m = 4 families
confirmatory. It is caught when `frob-tenfold preanalyze` records condition (iii) failed (4 of 4 has tail 1/16 =
0.0625 > 0.05), lists `ten-fold` as unreachable, and refuses the naming with `cell-not-reachable` (exit 3); the
decision that opens the cell `descriptive` is KILL-TENFOLD-REACH's (N39a-34), and the control runner of
GATE-TENFOLD-04 (N39a-40) reads the same directory.

## Considerations

- Twelve development missions estimate s poorly. The record runs the conditions at the upper end of the interval
  for s as well, and a cell that passes only at the point estimate is flagged `fragile`.
- The lognormal model is a planning device. It has no slow tail: a population in which 1% of families take a
  thousand times longer would pass this pre-analysis and still mislead a resampling interval, which is why
  population wording rests on a separate bounded contrast and at least 32 families.
- The pre-analysis is also where the cost of a cell is estimated: m·c·Σ over arms of E[min(T, H)] device-hours,
  at most 288 for 24 families, three arms and a 4 h deadline.
- Risk named in the program register: the tournament cannot confirm a true ratio of ten for lack of power; this
  record is its earliest signal.
