## Background

A sealed suite of missions can be opened once. Before that happens the ten-fold protocol requires proof that the
arms were frozen before the missions could be written against them, that the missions were fixed before the arms
could be tuned against them, that the rival is a real rival, and that the decision rule can return its verdicts
on cells of the planned size.

**GATE-TENFOLD-04 (claim kind: Known answer; TARGET; due at M3 before the partial gate's suite is opened, and
repeated at M7 before the tournament's).** Freeze and seal:

1. the six steps in order (class descriptions and the development suite published; the adjudicator commits the
   hash of the sealed suite and of the referee protocol; arms tuned on the development suite only; each arm's
   freeze manifest published; the pre-analysis recorded; the suite opened to the harness, a mission card shown to
   an arm only when that arm's clock starts);
2. a published manifest and advocate statement for every arm;
3. the frozen rival reproduces its home-task training time within 20% on the Linux host `trj` and wins at least
   one easy-win development family per stratum;
4. the pre-analysis recorded, with every confirmatory cell meeting the reachability conditions (P(`ten-fold`) at
   least 0.8 at a true ratio of 15; P(`withdrawn`) at most 0.05 at a true ratio of 5; the guard reachable at its
   adjusted level; the horizon cap H ÷ RMT_A on the development suite at least 10).

If the gate is not met, the suite is not opened. This bead measures the gate on the real arms and the real suite
of a gate instance; the mechanisms it reads are built by the beads it depends on.

## Scope

In: the four measurements as a checker; the registration of the owner-input and adjudication preconditions on
the freeze-and-seal steps (step 0 below); the gate record with measured values; the negative controls; the
end-to-end script (which runs the same checks on a rehearsal gate so that the gate itself can fail). Out: the
owner-inputs check and its configuration record (N41-15) and the adjudication record with its check (N41-06),
which this bead calls; the sealing ledger (N39a-15), freeze manifests (N39a-18), equal terms (N39a-19), arm B's protocol (N39a-20), the pre-analysis
(N39a-33) and the reachability decisions with the pre-run notice (N39a-34), which clause 4 reads; the other
pre-opening gates (GATE-TENFOLD-03: N39b-22; GATE-TENFOLD-05: N39a-10; GATE-TENFOLD-10: the script N39b-36 with its
register row N40-19; GATE-TENFOLD-11: the rival audit N39b-37 with its register row N40-20); the program-gate
register rows of GATE-TENFOLD-04 (an M3 part and an M7 part) and the pre-opening conjunction `may_open` of the five
gates (N40-14, which depends on this bead, reads the `pre-open` part of this bead's record for the suite's hash and
writes no second record of the gate).

Order of work: this bead is a component of the protocol capstone (seed BD-314, whose done-condition names
GATE-TENFOLD-04): BD-314 depends on it, and it does not depend on BD-314. It closes when the checker, the record
format and the script exist and every control is caught on the rehearsal gate. The record of a real gate instance is produced with the same checker at the moment
the arms of that instance are frozen, which happens inside the arm beads of the gate (seeds BD-315 and BD-316 at
M3; the arm epics at M7); those beads cannot start a sealed campaign until the `pre-open` part of the record is
`pass`. The gate's PASS is therefore recorded when the last bead that cites it closes.

## Technical approach

**0. Owner-input and adjudication preconditions (registered by this bead; checked before anything is measured).**
Every freeze-and-seal step of a gate instance first calls the owner-inputs check of N41-15,
`frob_conformance::tenfold::owner_inputs::require_frozen_inputs()`: the four owner rows (OPEN-TENFOLD-3, -4, -6,
-8) are `decided` or `defaulted` and the configuration record `registries/tenfold/owner-inputs.toml` equals its
generation from the ADRs. Every step that seals a family (the step-2 `commit-suite` entry, which binds the sealed
families into the suite commitment) also first calls the adjudication check of N41-06,
`frob_conformance::tenfold::adjudication::require_record(gate)` with `gate` = `partial` at M3 and `tournament` at
M7 (the record present; at M7 a confirmation dated after the page regenerated at the M6 exit). Either failure
refuses the step (exit 3, naming each open row, the mismatching table or the missing half, as those beads
specify) and appends nothing. This bead registers the first check as a precondition of each of the six sealing
steps with the sealing ledger (N39a-15) and through the freeze-check hook of `freeze write` (N39a-18), and the
second as a precondition of step 2; the two command forms of those checks (`require-owner-inputs --json` and
`require-adjudication --gate <partial|tournament> --json`, subcommands of the operator binary `frob-tenfold`,
N39a-01) are what the script below runs. The gate record carries the configuration digest and the adjudication
record digest returned by the checks. Clause 1 additionally lists as a violation any family of the sealed pool
(N39a-13) whose creation time precedes the adjudication record's date, so a family sealed before its sealer
existed is visible even if it was created outside the sealing steps.

**1. Clause 1: the six steps (measured by `frob-tenfold seal verify`).** Replays the sealing ledger's hash chain;
checks step order, completeness of each step, monotone evidence-ledger sequence numbers and wall-clock times,
that each arm's `frozen_at` follows the suite commitment and precedes `open`, that the suite files still open the
step-2 commitment, and that the role registry is unchanged since step 1. Value: the six entry hashes and times;
`violations: []`.

**2. Clause 2: manifests and statements.** For every arm (A, B, C; D if run) and every stratum in scope: a
published freeze manifest whose hash verifies and whose references all resolve in the published pack; an advocate
statement (the builders' statement for arm A) that verifies under the registered identity and lists what more
budget would have added; the manifest agrees with the gate's equal-terms record. Value: a table arm × stratum of
manifest hashes; `missing: []`.

**3. Clause 3: the rival is real.**
- *Home-task reproduction.* For arm B's frozen engine and learner: train the incumbent's own home task to its own
  return threshold on `trj` (one RTX 4090), three runs, through the wrapper. Value: the median time and its ratio
  to the incumbent's published figure for that task (about 410 s on one RTX 4090 for the flat-ground quadruped
  policy of the stack read for the plan; the figure and its source are re-read and recorded). Threshold: within
  20% (TARGET), or the difference explained in writing with the measured figure replacing the published one.
- *Easy wins.* From `development_scores` in arm B's manifest and arm A's logged development times: per stratum,
  the number of development easy-win families on which arm B's restricted time is strictly below arm A's.
  Threshold: at least 1 per stratum; zero refuses the freeze.
- Also recorded (not thresholds of this gate): the capability checklist with no unexplained row; the tuning
  budget spent against max(40 device-hours, the builders' logged compute) per stratum.

**4. Clause 4: pre-analysis and reachability.** The pre-analysis record exists with a hash in sealing step 5; at
most four cells are named confirmatory; for each named cell the four condition values are read from the record
and compared with their thresholds; every proposed cell has its reachability decision record and the public
pre-run notice lists each cell's status. Value: a table of cells with the four values.

**5. The gate record (per gate instance).**
```
{ gate: "GATE-TENFOLD-04", instance: "M3-partial", suite: "b3:…", protocol: "b3:…",
  owner_inputs: "b3:…", adjudication_record: "b3:…",
  steps: [{step, entry_hash, utc, ledger_seq}], violations: [],
  manifests: {…table…}, statements: {…}, terms: "b3:…",
  rival: [{stratum, home_task: {published_s, measured_s, ratio, explained: null | "b3:…"}, easy_wins: n}],
  preanalysis: "b3:…", cells: [{cell, named, conditions: [4 values with thresholds]}], notice: "b3:…",
  controls: [{id, caught}], verdict: "pass" | "fail: <clause>", recorded_by, hash }
```
A pass at M3 does not carry to M7: the tournament has a new suite, new freezes and a new record.

**6. Order relative to the other pre-opening gates.** `open` (step 6) is itself the last entry this gate checks,
so the record is completed in two parts: `pre-open` (clauses 2 to 4 and steps 1 to 5; required before `open`) and
`post-open` (step 6's entry and the first `card-shown` entries, confirming that no card was shown before its
arm's clock start). The pre-opening conjunction (`may_open`, N40-14) reads the `pre-open` part: it requires
`verdict: "pass"` on that part, `violations: []`, every control caught, and the record's `suite` equal to the
hash of the suite being opened. The `post-open` part is completed and checked here, after the opening.

## Acceptance criteria

Delivered by this bead (on the rehearsal gate):
- [ ] The checker produces a record with all four clauses from a gate store; the record format round-trips.
- [ ] Each negative control below is caught on the rehearsal gate.
- [ ] `open` is impossible while the `pre-open` part is not `pass`.
- [ ] No sealing step or freeze is appended while an owner row is `open` or the configuration record differs from
      its generation; no step 2 is appended without the adjudication record (at M7, without the post-M6
      confirmation).

Required of every real gate instance, checked with this bead's checker and written by a session that implemented
none of the mechanisms:
- [ ] Six steps in order with hashes and times, no violation; one manifest and one verifying statement per arm
      and stratum.
- [ ] Arm B's home-task median is within 20% of the recorded published figure on `trj`, or a written explanation
      is attached and the measured figure replaces it.
- [ ] Arm B wins at least one easy-win development family in every stratum in scope.
- [ ] Every named confirmatory cell meets the four reachability conditions with measured values on record; at most
      four are named; every other cell is `descriptive` in the pre-run notice.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold -- gate04::`
- `record_two_parts`: `pre-open` without step 6 is complete; `post-open` requires `card-shown` entries each later
  than its arm's freeze.
- `home_task_ratio`: published 410 s, measured (395, 402, 431) s → median 402, ratio 0.98, inside; measured median
  500 s → 1.22, outside unless explained.
- `easy_win_count`: rival times below arm A's on 0, 1 and 2 of 2 easy-win families → fail, pass, pass.
- `four_cells_max`: five named → fail.
- `conditions_table`: a named cell with horizon cap 9.6 → fail on clause 4; with horizon cap 9.9 → fail; a cell
  with P(`ten-fold` | true ratio 15) = 0.79 → fail on clause 4 if named (it must be `descriptive`); 0.80 →
  passes as confirmatory.
- `sealing_order`: of the 720 permutations of the six steps exactly one is accepted by the clause-1 check; a
  ledger with step 4 after step 6, and one with step 2 after step 4, are among the 719 refused.
- `holm_first_step`: four named cells of 24 families → the guard's first Holm step requires 18 of 24 (level
  0.0125); the pre-analysis record must state that count.
- `pass_does_not_carry`: an M3 record offered for an M7 suite hash → refused.
- `owner_inputs_precede_every_step`: with OPEN-TENFOLD-6 `open` in a `synthetic` configuration record, each of
  the six sealing steps and `freeze write` exits 3 naming that row and appends no entry; with all four rows
  `defaulted` they proceed; a configuration record edited by hand (not equal to its generation) → refused naming
  the table.
- `adjudication_precedes_commit_suite`: step 2 with no adjudication record → refused naming both halves; with the
  default record → accepted; at M7 (`tournament`) with a confirmation dated before the M6 page → refused, after
  it → accepted.
- `family_sealed_before_record`: a sealed-pool family created before the adjudication record's date → listed in
  clause 1's `violations`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_gate04_freeze_seal.sh` (header: `bead=N39a-40 milestone=M3 gates=GATE-TENFOLD-04`). On a
rehearsal gate with development-grade missions, three `synthetic` arms and stand-in times: (1) roles and step 1;
(2) suite and protocol commitment; (3) tuning windows; (4) freeze and publish three arms; (5) pre-analysis,
reachability decisions and the notice; (6) `open` and two campaign starts; then `seal verify` and the gate
record. Before step (1) it runs `frob-tenfold require-owner-inputs --json` and, before step (2),
`frob-tenfold require-adjudication --gate partial --json`, logging each exit code and digest. Then nine replays
with one planted defect each, asserting the refusal or the `fail`. Last, the controls that the control registry
holds for GATE-TENFOLD-04 (those seeded from the counterexample entries TF-CX-14, the freeze after the opening,
and TF-CX-07, the four-family confirmatory cell, of N39-CX) are run through the control runner of N38b-04
(`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance --features gauntlet-mutations --test <tier named by the
gate's record> -- GATE-TENFOLD-04`), and each control's outcome is copied from the run receipt into the record's
`controls`; one reported `missed` or `stale` makes the `pre-open` part fail. The arms and their
times are labelled `synthetic`; the home-task measurement of clause 3 on a real rival is the real instance's (and
N39a-20's script runs it on `trj`). The script writes the gate record and no row of `registries/gates.toml`
(convention 9): the record and the control run's receipt are committed byte-identical under `receipts/gates/`
(N40-01), and the gate's part for the instance is entered from the receipt of the `pre-open` run with `cargo run
-p frob-conformance --example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TENFOLD-04 --receipt <hash> --part <M3|M7>`, citing the
record by hash, by a session that implemented none of the mechanisms; this bead is the gate's one recorder, as
N40-14 names it. It sources `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E) and writes NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_gate04_freeze_seal/<utc>.ndjson`. Logged per step: the command, exit code, wall time, the
entry hash and time, the commitment, each manifest hash, the home-task ratio beside 1.2, easy-win counts beside 1,
the four condition values beside their thresholds, each refusal with its reason, for each planted defect the
control id and whether it was caught, and a summary naming GATE-TENFOLD-04. On the real gate instance the same
script is run in a mode that reads the gate's store read-only and writes the record.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: one accepted ordering of the six steps; 1.2 × 410 s = 492 s as the home-task limit; the first Holm
step 18 of 24 when four cells are named. Negative controls, each of which the gate must catch ((a) and (f) are
the registry entries TF-CX-14 and TF-CX-07, whose planted inputs `freeze_after_open` and `four_family_cell` are
written by N39a-18 and N39a-33; the others are this bead's own replays): (a) a manifest
whose freeze time follows the suite opening (the run is refused); (b) a suite hash committed after an arm's
freeze; (c) an arm with no advocate statement; (d) a rival that wins no easy-win family (a strawman); (e) a rival
whose home-task time is 30% above its published figure with no explanation (a mis-installed rival); (f) a
confirmatory cell of four families (refused as unreachable); (g) a card shown to an arm before its clock start;
(h) a freeze attempted while an owner row is `open` (refused by the owner-inputs precondition); (i) a suite
commitment attempted with no adjudication record (refused by the adjudication precondition).
Determinism: `seal verify` is D0; the home-task time is a measurement with its disclosure block.

## Considerations

- OPEN questions that block this gate, with defaults: who the rival advocates are and their budget
  (OPEN-TENFOLD-3, N39b-28: agent sessions that have contributed nothing to FrankenRobotics; 40 device-hours per
  stratum; label `rival: internally advocated`); the agent model (OPEN-TENFOLD-6, N39b-31: the agent the project is
  developed with at the gate's date, pinned); the deadline per stratum (OPEN-TENFOLD-5, N39b-30: three times the
  frozen rival's median development time, rounded up to half an hour, raised until the horizon cap is at least
  10); ratification of the thresholds (OPEN-TENFOLD-8, N39b-33: as written).
- With the completion non-inferiority condition as written, no 24-family cell can be named confirmatory
  (N39a-31); the gate then passes with zero confirmatory cells only if every cell is declared `descriptive` in
  the notice, and the report says the gate could not confirm or withdraw the claim.
- The home-task check needs the campaign compute class for its three training runs when the task is longer than
  the default one-hour envelope allows.
- A pass says the comparison may run. It says nothing about its result.
