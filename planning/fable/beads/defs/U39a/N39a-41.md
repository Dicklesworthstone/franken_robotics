## Background

The ten-fold test is scored by a small program, the scorer: from phase ledgers, campaign records and receipts it
computes pooled ratios of restricted times, intervals, the exact guard, completion bounds, warm ratios, ceilings,
and one verdict per cell. A wrong scorer is the cheapest way to get a wrong headline: averaging per-family ratios
instead of pooling turns 2.65 into 6.23; dropping one censored run turns it into 7.05. Both mistakes look
reasonable in code review.

The protocol's defence is a set of worked cards with hand-checkable answers, and planted wrong scorers that the
tests must catch (the program's rule: a gate that nothing can fail validates nothing). Each statistic has unit
tests in its own bead. What no unit test shows is the *pipeline*: that records written by the harness are the
records the scorer reads, that a verdict is reachable only through the once-at-n path, and that the report built
from them carries every ratio with its context. This bead is that end-to-end script, run entirely on synthetic
campaigns so that nothing sealed is needed and so that it can run on every commit.

It is also the executable form of the parts of GATE-TENFOLD-03 that concern the statistics: "the harness
reproduces every known answer of its set exactly".

## Scope

In: the script `scripts/e2e/tenfold_rehearsal.sh`, which takes synthetic campaign stores through ledgers, the
scorer, the verdict rule, the monitor, the ceilings and the report; its assertions; how the planted wrong scorers
are injected; the cross-platform digest lane. The worked-card fixtures and the planted scorers are held once, as
rows and files of the chapter's registries (known answers N39-KA, negative controls N39-CX); this script builds
campaign stores from them and adds none of its own under another name. Out: the mechanisms (their own beads); the
recorded gate GATE-TENFOLD-03 with its test target, its receipt and all seven negative controls, including the
three that need disqualification machinery (N39b-22); the rehearsal of the gate's procedure from sealing to
outcome rules, with the disqualification and leak drills (N39b-12); the freeze-and-seal path (N39a-40) and the
adjudicator's acceptance (N39a-10). Three scripts therefore touch the statistics, each for a different purpose:
this one rehearses the scoring pipeline, N39b-22 records the gate, N39b-12 rehearses the whole partial gate.

## Technical approach

**1. Fixtures (`crates/frob-conformance/tests/fixtures/tenfold/`, all labelled `synthetic`).** Each is a complete
rehearsal gate store: role registry, a minimal sealing ledger, strata and family registries, freeze manifests for
three synthetic arms, campaign records, raw intervals, receipts signed by a rehearsal adjudicator key, and a
pre-analysis record. They are generated by a checked-in generator from small tables, so the tables are the
source of truth.

| Fixture | Content | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| `single_pair` | One `legged-quad` cold pair, H = 14,400 s. Rival exclusive seconds: `author` 900, `propose` 1,080, `check` 240, `compile` 540, `train` 4,920, `evaluate` 360, `diagnose` 600, `wait` 180, `adjudicate` 180. Arm A: 600, 360, 20, 12, `screen` 96, 150, 60, `qualify` 40, 30, 42, 90 | Clocks 9,000 and 1,500; pair ratio 6.0; removing training 2.206; unremovable floor 8.571; `check` 1.03 |
| `six_family` | Six families: rival 9,000, 6,300, 14,400 c, 5,400, 11,700, 7,200; arm A 1,500, 900, 2,100, 700, 14,400 c, 800 | R̂ = 45/17 = 2.647; ρ = 6.00, 7.00, 6.857 (lower bound), 7.714, 0.8125, 9.00; completion ceiling 3.75 and 1/q = 6; no verdict (n < 10) |
| `warm_card` | One amendment: rival 4,050 s, arm A 470 s; S_A = 14,400 s, S_B = 0 | R_warm(1, 10, 100) = 0.272, 2.120, 6.596; limit 8.617; N* = 5 |
| `cell_24_tenfold` | 24 families constructed with R̂ = 12 and 18 families above 5, full completion, one named cell, external rehearsal adjudication, finite-suite mode registered | `ten-fold`, finite-suite |
| `cell_24_measured` | As above with 16 families above 5 | `measured`, reason guard (16 < 17) |
| `cell_24_withdrawn` | The six-family proportions repeated four times (24 families) | R̂ = 45/17; `withdrawn` |
| `cell_4` | Four families | Refused as a confirmatory cell; `descriptive` |
| `cell_monitor` | A partial-gate cell whose first eight families (in schedule order) are below 3 | Futility stop after the eighth; `withdrawn`, source monitor |
| `ledger_open` | One run with 3% unattributed | Flagged `ledger-open`; in the ratio; excluded from ceilings |

Every 24-family and four-family cell also holds arm C's campaigns on the same missions, with RMT_C ÷ RMT_A = 15
and the same accepted scope as arm A, so arm B (R̂ = 12 in `cell_24_tenfold`) is the stronger rival and the tie
test reads *engine advantage established*. The verdict rule refuses a cell without a tie record
(`verdict-input-missing`), so the tie record is not hand-written into the fixture: the scorer's three-arm step
(N39b-43) computes it from these campaigns, which is the pipeline this script rehearses.

**2. The script (`scripts/e2e/tenfold_rehearsal.sh`; header `# e2e: bead=N39a-41 milestone=M3
gates=GATE-TENFOLD-03 seed=39 needs=cpu plants=avg_ratios,drop_censored,limit_as_warm,wrong_arm_ceiling,
normal_guard,peek_verdict`).** Steps:
1. Generate the fixtures; `frob-tenfold record check` on every record.
2. `frob-tenfold ledger build` for every campaign; assert each exclusive table sums to its clock; assert the
   `ledger_open` flag.
3. `frob-tenfold-score cell` on `six_family`; assert R̂, every ρ with its label, the censored counts.
4. `frob-tenfold-score bootstrap` on `six_family` with seed 39: assert p₀ = 9,569/20,000, indices 398 and 9,380,
   L = 612/469, U = 126/17.
5. `frob-tenfold-score guard --table`: assert 5 of 5, 6 of 6, 7 of 8, 10 of 12, 12 of 16, 17 of 24, 22 of 32 at
   0.05, 18 of 24 at 0.0125, none at 4.
6. `frob-tenfold-score noninferiority --table`: assert the zero-count bound 0.1174 at 24 and the smallest family
   counts 59, 93, 124, 153.
7. `frob-tenfold-score warm` on `warm_card`: assert the five values; offer the planted input
   `standing_asset_omitted` to `frob-tenfold campaign start` and assert its refusal (step 3 below).
8. `frob-tenfold-score ceiling` on `single_pair` and `six_family`: assert 2.206, 8.571, 1.03, 3.75 and that the
   open ledger was excluded.
9. Monitor on `cell_monitor`: assert E = 2,187/128 after seven (no stop) and 6,561/256 after eight (stop).
10. Close each cell; compute its three-arm object and tie record and assert q̂ = 15 and `tied = false`;
    `frob-tenfold-score verdict` on the three 24-family cells and the four-family cell; assert verdicts and
    reasons; request a verdict for `cell_monitor` and for an unclosed copy and assert both refusals; replay one
    verdict and assert identical bytes.
11. `frob-tenfold report build` (the gate report generator, N39b-14, which this bead depends on for that command);
    validate; assert every ratio object is complete, the `not_run` list names the
    strata the rehearsal omits, and the rendering regenerates byte-identically.
12. Planted wrong scorers (step 3 below).
13. Print the digest of the report record; when `FROB_E2E_SECOND_HOST` is set, run steps 1 to 11 there and
    compare digests.

**3. Planted wrong scorers.** The scorer is built with a test-only feature that selects one mutation at a time
(`E2E_PLANT_FAILURE=<name>`); the script runs the relevant step under each plant and requires the assertion to
fail (the helper's negative-control form: the check must be caught). Each plant is an entry of the chapter's
negative-control registry (N39-CX), which holds its record, and is built by the bead that owns its target:
`avg_ratios` is TF-CX-01 and `drop_censored` TF-CX-02 (both N39a-27), `wrong_arm_ceiling` TF-CX-03 (N39a-37),
`limit_as_warm` TF-CX-04 (N39a-17), `normal_guard` TF-CX-06 (N39a-29), `peek_verdict` TF-CX-09 (N39a-35). The
first five are mutations of the scorer; `peek_verdict` is registered as a planted input (the request for the
fixed-n verdict of a cell the monitor stopped), and its plant here is the build in which that refusal is
removed. One control per entry needs one plant per entry, so TF-CX-05 (leaving out a standing asset) is not
`limit_as_warm`: it has its own planted input, `standing_asset_omitted` (N39a-17's fixture: the warm card with
S_A = 0 and the asset in the workspace), which step 7 offers to `frob-tenfold campaign start` and which must be
refused with `tenfold.cold-start-foreign-file`; scored without that check it would give 8.617 at every N and
N* = 1.

| Plant | Mutation | What it reports | Caught by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `avg_ratios` | Mean of per-family ratios instead of the ratio of sums | 6.23 on `six_family` | Step 3 |
| `drop_censored` | Drops pairs with a censored arm-A run | 42,300 ÷ 6,000 = 7.05 | Step 3 |
| `limit_as_warm` | Reports the limit b̄ ÷ ā in the position of the warm ratio | 8.617 at N = 10, where the card gives 2.120 | Step 7 |
| `wrong_arm_ceiling` | Phase shares from arm A's ledger | "Training removed" 1.11 instead of 2.206 | Step 8 |
| `normal_guard` | Normal approximation to the binomial tail | Rejects at 21 of 32 | Step 5 (extended table) |
| `peek_verdict` | Allows the verdict before the cell is closed | A verdict at the monitor's stopping time | Step 10 |

**4. Logging.** Through the shared helper `scripts/e2e/lib.sh` (STD-E2E), as NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_rehearsal/<utc>.ndjson`: every command with exit code and wall time; every asserted value
as `observed` beside `expected` with the tolerance (`exact` for all statistics); on a mismatch the first differing
value with the fixture and record it came from; the known-answer tag of the row it belongs to; each plant with
`caught: true|false`; the report digest; and a summary with assertion counts and `gates: ["GATE-TENFOLD-03"]`. The evidence fields of each line carry the harness record's labels (`reality:
no-claim`, `synthetic`, class `Tested`, determinism D0, the rehearsal suite hash, the seed).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every expected value of the fixture table is asserted and passes on a clean checkout.
- [ ] Each of the six plants is caught, and the planted input `standing_asset_omitted` (TF-CX-05) is refused; a
      plant or planted input that is not caught fails the script.
- [ ] The script is idempotent, uses only its scratch directory, takes its seed from the header and exits 0 only
      with at least one assertion per step.
- [ ] The report digest is identical on arm64 macOS and x86-64 Linux, in debug and release builds.
- [ ] Run time is under five minutes on eight cores (TARGET), so that it runs on every change to the harness.
- [ ] No sealed material and no private data appear in fixtures or logs.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The statistics are unit-tested in their own beads. This bead adds `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test tenfold
-- fixtures::`:
- `generator_is_deterministic`: fixtures regenerate byte-identically.
- `tables_match_fixtures`: each fixture's ledgers reproduce the numbers of its source table (sums, clocks).
- `cell_24_tenfold_construction`: R̂ = 12 exactly, exactly 18 families with ρ > 5, and RMT_C ÷ RMT_A = 15 exactly.
- `plants_change_output`: each mutation changes the corresponding output on its fixture (a plant that changes
  nothing is not a control).

### End-to-end script and logging

As specified in steps 2 and 4 above: `scripts/e2e/tenfold_rehearsal.sh`, NDJSON logs under
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_rehearsal/`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers (the harness's own set, all asserted here): the six-family card (R̂ = 2.647; completion ceiling
3.75; per-family ratios 6.00, 7.00, 6.857, 7.714, 0.8125, 9.00); the single-pair ledger (6.0; training removed
2.206; unremovable floor 8.571); the warm card (0.272, 2.120, 6.596; limit 8.617; N* = 5); the exact guard
(critical counts as in step 5); the zero-count bound (1 − 0.05^(1/24) = 0.1174); the completion ceiling (12%
unfinished caps R at 25/3); the monitor (stops at eight consecutive families below 3, 25.6, and not at seven,
17.1). Negative controls exercised here from the harness's list: a ledger with 3% unattributed (flagged, and
excluded from ceilings); a request for a confirmatory cell with four families (refused as unreachable); a request
for the fixed-n verdict at a monitor's stopping time (refused); plus the six plants of step 3 and the planted
input of TF-CX-05. Determinism
lanes: twice on one machine; one and eight worker threads; debug and release; both platforms: class D0 for every
scorer output.

## Considerations

- Synthetic campaigns test arithmetic and plumbing, not physics or agents; every output of this script prints
  `synthetic`, and none can be cited for a gate other than the known-answer gate of the harness.
- The fixtures double as the replication kit's level-1 self-test: a third party who builds the scorer can run
  them before trusting it on the published ledgers.
- When a statistic's specification changes before the first suite hash, its fixture table changes in the same
  commit; after the first suite hash the method identifiers are frozen and a change needs a new identifier.
