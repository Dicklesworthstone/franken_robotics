## Background

The ten-fold tournament at M7 runs every stratum that has a product. Each stratum needs 12 public development
missions before its sealed suite can be committed: arms are tuned on the development suite only, a stratum's
deadline is derived from the rival's median development time on it, the rival's freeze is refused if it wins
none of its development easy cases, and the freeze and dress rehearsal of the tournament runs on the
development pool. Publishing the pool is the first of the six ordered sealing steps, and the epic of the sealed
suite says so in one line ("publish the enlarged development pool"). The pool has two sources, and neither has
a task:

1. **Families retired from the partial gate.** A sealed family serves one gate. When the partial gate at M3
   closes, its missions, worlds, keys and receipts are revealed and the family enters the development pool; it
   never serves as a sealed family again.
2. **New development missions for the strata first run at M7:** `legged-biped`, `tracked`, `hybrid`, `rebody`,
   `failure`, `throughput`, and `facility` (a Moonshot stratum, run only if a pass of the facility co-design
   gate GATE-AUGUR-24 is on record, as the strata registry N39a-12 checks; otherwise `not run`).

The pool tooling, the published form of a development card and the publication package exist from the partial
gate (N39ax-01), and so does the witness rule for design missions (N39ax-02). This bead applies them at the
tournament's scope. Tier: Solid. It is a child of the sealed-suite epic and, like that epic, is split per
stratum when M7 opens; the split rule is stated below so that the split loses nothing.

## Scope

In: folding the revealed M3 families into the pool with their rotation records; up to 84 new development
missions in seven strata; the tournament's publication package and its hash; the order in which strata are
authored and dropped. Out: the pool checks and package builder (N39ax-01); the witness rule (N39ax-02); sealed
mission templates for rebody, facility and throughput and the warm-campaign rules (N39b-41); the sealed suite
and its commitment (the epic's sealed-suite tasks); deadlines (N39b-30); the rehearsal (N39b-17).

## Technical approach

**1. Retired families.** For every family of the partial gate with status `revealed`: its registry row moves to
`active(development)` in the tournament's `families.json` with `served_gate = "M3-partial"`; its cards, revealed
parts, worlds and receipts are copied into the tournament's package by content address; its keys stay in the
connectivity graph, so that no sealed family of the tournament may share a generator draw, a site, a material
batch or a parent design with it. A family retired early because of a leak is included with its reason. The
count rule of the development profile is per stratum: at least 12 development missions in at least 6 families,
counting those authored for an earlier gate and those retired from one. The four strata of the partial gate
therefore need no new authoring: each carries its 12 missions of M3 and the families it retired.

**2. New missions (DESIGN allocation; the adjudicator authors, as at M3).**

| Stratum | 12 development missions | Answer kind | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| `legged-biped` | Biped and humanoid briefs across the legged row of the grid, every world type covered | Qualified design | Solvability witness, at least 4 not produced by the product |
| `tracked` | The tracked row: stairs, rubble and soft ground; a stair flight with a carried load; turning in place on a sill; scrub energy on carpet; ramp traction with payload; floor grates and cable covers; stopping distance in an aisle; a low-traction turn | Qualified design | Witness |
| `hybrid` | The hybrid row: curbs and stairs on wheels; switching between rolling and stepping; a lift sill; thresholds between carpet and tile; a ramp and a dock plate; cable covers and grates; a curb at an entrance; drains and mats | Qualified design | Witness |
| `rebody` | An accepted body changed under a frozen task interface (a gearbox, a wheel, a lower limb), then latency, friction or energy reserve perturbed; warm amendment sequences | Amendment verdict | The exact verdict list: which verdicts are withdrawn, which stand |
| `failure` | A planted failure inside the legality bounds to be found and repaired | Counterexample and repair | The planted world and one accepted repair |
| `throughput` | A heterogeneous family at scale: fixed and changed topology, grazing contacts, dense depth sensors, memory pressure; the whole loop timed | Qualified design over a family | Witness on a reduced family, with the full-scale cost estimate |
| `facility` [M] | Recommission a facility: move a door or a counter, perturb scan uncertainty, reduce a shared resource, alter a shift | Amendment verdict | The exact verdict list; authored only if the facility gate has passed |

Controls per stratum as in the development profile: at least 2 easy wins and 1 unresolved answer per 12, all
four mutation kinds, a tier trap in each design stratum (`legged-biped`, `tracked`, `hybrid`, `throughput`),
and in every warm card exactly one consequential edit among at least two inconsequential ones.

**3. Order and budget.** Authoring follows the hours-per-family figure measured by the suite-authoring pilot
(N39ax-04). Strata are authored in the reverse of the drop order the epic names, so that what is most likely to
run is written first: `legged-biped`, `tracked`, `hybrid`, `failure`, `rebody`, `throughput`, `facility`. A
stratum whose development missions are not complete when the tournament's freeze window opens is declared `not
run` in the report with that reason; a stratum is never run on a partial development suite, because its
deadline and its rival's audit would then rest on fewer missions than every other stratum's.

**4. Split rule when M7 opens.** One task per stratum, each with this bead's checks for its stratum, plus one
task for the retired families and the package. The package task depends on the stratum tasks that are run.

**5. Package.** `tenfold/M7-tournament/development/` (the gate's store directory carries the gate name of the
harness records, as `tenfold/M3-partial/development/` does) with the same layout as at M3 and an `INDEX.json` whose hash is
recorded by sealing step 1 of the tournament. Checks and package are class D0; witness batteries class D2.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every family of the partial gate with status `revealed` is in the tournament's development pool with its
      rotation record, and no family appears in both pools of any gate.
- [ ] `frob-tenfold suite check --development` passes for every stratum that is run; every stratum is listed as
      run or `not run` with its reason, and `facility` is `not run` unless its gate has passed.
- [ ] Every design mission has a witness that passes its battery in the adjudicating simulator; every other
      mission has a reference answer the acceptance code accepts and mutants it rejects.
- [ ] The package hash is byte-identical across two builds and equals the value in the tournament's
      `publish-development` ledger entry, which precedes the suite commitment and every freeze entry.
- [ ] No sealed family of the tournament is connected to any mission of this pool (the commitment check's
      output is attached).

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::development_m7` (real registries; plants are scratch copies labelled
`synthetic`):
- `retired_families_enter_pool`: a revealed M3 family missing from the pool fails naming it.
- `no_family_in_two_pools`: a family listed as development and sealed fails.
- `revealed_to_sealed_refused`: moving a retired family back to the sealed pool is a forbidden transition.
- `count_rule_with_retired`: a partial-gate stratum with its 12 missions of M3 and its retired families passes
  with nothing new authored; a stratum first run at M7 with 11 authored missions fails.
- `partial_stratum_is_not_run`: a stratum with 10 missions at the freeze window is reported `not run`.
- `facility_conditional`: with no GATE-AUGUR-24 pass on record, a `facility` card in the package fails; with a
  pass on record it is admitted.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_development_m7.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N39ax-03 milestone=M7 labels=tenfold,gauntlet
seed=0 needs=cpu plants=retired-omitted,two-pools,partial-as-run,shared-site`; the header's seed is a decimal
number, and the seed in force, `FROB_E2E_SEED`, is logged): (0) `FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo
test -p frob-conformance tenfold::development_m7`; (1) `frob-tenfold suite check --development --stratum <s>
--json` for each stratum of the tournament (exit 0 for a run stratum; a stratum declared `not run` is listed
with its reason, and `facility` without a GATE-AUGUR-24 pass must be `not run`); (2) the connectivity check of
the union of both pools and of every earlier gate's revealed families (`frob-tenfold suite check --commitment
--json` on the tournament's draft commitment); (3) for two missions per stratum chosen by the run's seed, the
reference answer or witness battery re-run through the harness's acceptance command, mutants included; (4)
scratch plants (a retired family omitted; a family in two pools; a partial stratum presented as run; a sealed
family sharing a site with a retired M3 family), each asserted by reason; (5) the package built twice and its
hash compared with the `publish-development` ledger entry. It logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_development_m7/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`: `cmd` lines with exact command line, exit code
and wall time; the evidence block of acceptance envelopes; `metric` lines per stratum (authored, retired,
families, controls, status); one `assert` per check and plant; the package hash; a `summary` line. Everything
read is public or already revealed; nothing sealed and no private-scene data is logged.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: seven strata first run at M7, six without `facility`; 12 missions each; four design strata with
  tier traps; the drop order of the epic reversed as the authoring order.
- Negative controls: the three plants; a tournament sealed family sharing a site with a retired M3 family (must
  be refused by the commitment check).
- Determinism lanes: checks and package twice on one machine and on both platforms (class D0); witness
  batteries twice on one backend (class D2).

## Considerations

- Size: up to 84 missions, 36 or more with witnesses. This is weeks of adjudicator time, not days; the split
  per stratum at M7's opening is part of the plan for this bead, and the pilot's figure decides how many strata
  are attempted.
- `tracked`, `hybrid` and film-dependent cells are the likeliest to need cross-home adjudication; their
  witnesses name both homes.
- The pool only grows. After the tournament its sealed families are revealed too and join it for any later
  re-run of the kit.
