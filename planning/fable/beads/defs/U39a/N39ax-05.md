## Background

The ten-fold test is a ratio against the **strongest composed rival**: incumbent simulators, their own training
recipes, an outer-loop optimizer and, where a mission calls for it, an expert's program over a solver, each at a
pinned version, tuned by advocates who did not write this system, and frozen in a content-addressed manifest
before the sealed suite is opened. The rival was first built, audited and frozen for the partial gate at M3. The
tournament at M7 comes several milestones later. Incumbents release often, so the tournament re-reads and
re-pins every incumbent at its then-current release (a rival frozen at stale pins is not the strongest), and a
new pin is a new harness identity. Each frozen configuration must reproduce its own development-fixture figure
within 10% on the tournament hardware (the rival-audit gate), and the frozen rival must reproduce its home-task
training time within 20% on the Linux host.

The seed bead of the incumbent arms is an epic whose done-condition begins "a short plan exists and its pilot
has run". The epic names the pilot, the **refresh pilot**, and no task carried it. Its question is narrow and
decides a budget: after re-pinning, do the configurations frozen at M3 still perform as they did, so that
re-tuning can be light, or have the new releases moved them, so that each stratum's rival must be re-tuned at
the full rule (per arm and stratum, the larger of 40 device-hours and the compute the builders logged on the
same development suite)? And for the three locomotion strata that did not exist at M3, is there an incumbent
that can represent their missions at all? Tier: Solid.

Rejected: re-tuning everything at the full rule without looking (it spends the advocates' budget where nothing
moved and hides which release changed what); carrying the M3 freeze into the tournament unchanged (a stale
rival is weaker than the one a competent team would field, and the result would be discounted for it).

## Scope

In: the registered protocol; the re-runs at the new pins; the probes of the new strata; the verdict per stratum
against the falsifier; the record and what it changes in the tournament's tuning budget. Out: the audit itself
and its experiment pack (N39b-37); the arms, their tuning and their freeze manifests (the epic's tasks, with
N39a-20 and N39a-18); the pin freeze for the length of the tournament (N33b-03); the development missions used
(N39ax-01, N39ax-02, N39ax-03); licences for publishing a comparison (N39b-32).

## Technical approach

**1. Registration.** A row in `crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` before any run: mechanism "rival
refresh at tournament pins"; the protocol below by hash; the falsifier; the ceiling (none: this pilot sizes a
budget and claims no speed); the fallback per branch; the cost cap (the runs fit the compute class the owner
approved for the audit, or the pilot waits for that approval); the session identities.

**2. Protocol.**
1. *Re-pin.* For every incumbent of the M3 rival: the then-current release, with revision, container digest,
   licence and documented capabilities recorded through the incumbent harness. Each new pin gets a new harness
   identity; the M3 identities stay on file.
2. *Unchanged re-run.* Each configuration frozen at M3 (one per stratum and arm: `legged-quad`, `wheeled`,
   `envelope`, `sensing`; the composed rival and the ablation arm) runs **without any change of settings** on
   its M3 development fixtures at the new pins, on the tournament hardware (one RTX 4090 of the Linux host and
   the M3 core count), three seeds each, under the disclosure block.
3. *Home task.* The incumbent's own reference training task at its published settings, at the new pins, once
   per incumbent.
4. *New strata.* For `legged-biped`, `tracked` and `hybrid`: two development missions each with the
   incumbent's default recipe for that robot class, to a fixed budget of the stratum's provisional development
   deadline, recording whether the mission can be expressed (robot class, terrain or interior, contact model)
   and whether training starts and progresses.

**3. Measurements and falsifier.**

| Quantity | Computation | Falsifier | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drift of an M3 configuration | For each development fixture, the ratio of its figure at the new pins (median of three seeds) to the figure recorded in the M3 freeze manifest; the stratum's drift is the largest absolute deviation from 1 | More than 10% for any M3 configuration | Re-tuning of that stratum is budgeted at the full rule; otherwise a light re-tune of at most a quarter of the full rule (DESIGN) and a new freeze |
| Home task | Training time at the new pins against the incumbent's published time | Outside 20% | The pin is investigated before any tuning: an installation or driver defect is fixed and the run repeated once; a real regression is recorded and the faster of the old and new releases is the pin |
| Representability of a new stratum | Both probe missions expressible and training progressing | No incumbent can represent the stratum's missions | The stratum's rival cells print `unavailable` and the stratum is `descriptive`; it earns no word |

A figure that **improves** by more than 10% also trips the first falsifier: the rival got stronger, and a
stronger rival is re-tuned and re-frozen exactly as a weaker one would be.

**4. Record.** The pilot's row gains the result per stratum, with the ledgers and disclosure blocks of every
run, including a negative result. The epic's short plan cites it for the tuning budget of each stratum. Runs
are class D2 at best on a device and are reported as medians with the three values; the comparison arithmetic
is exact on the recorded figures.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot's row exists in the pilot register, registered before the first run, with protocol hash,
      falsifier and fallback per branch.
- [ ] Every M3 frozen configuration has three re-runs at the new pins on the tournament hardware, each with a
      resolving disclosure block and a closed phase ledger; no setting differs from the M3 freeze manifest (the
      manifest diff is empty apart from the pins).
- [ ] The drift per stratum is recorded with the three values behind each median, and each stratum is marked
      `light re-tune` or `full re-tune` by the stated rule.
- [ ] The home task of each incumbent is recorded against its published time, inside or outside 20%.
- [ ] Each of `legged-biped`, `tracked` and `hybrid` is marked representable or `unavailable`, with the two
      probe records.
- [ ] The result is recorded whatever it is, and the epic's short plan cites it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests
`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::refresh_pilot`:
- `drift_rule`: figures (100, 104, 97) against a frozen 100 → median 100, drift 0%, light; (100, 112, 115)
  → median 112, drift 12%, full; (85, 88, 86) → median 86, drift 14%, full (an improvement also trips it).
- `drift_boundary`: exactly 10% is light; 10.1% is full.
- `home_task_band`: 119% of the published time is inside; 121% is outside.
- `manifest_diff_only_pins`: a re-run whose manifest differs from the M3 freeze in a solver setting fails the
  record check naming the field.
- `unrepresentable_stratum_is_descriptive`: a stratum with no expressible probe mission is marked
  `unavailable` and `descriptive`.

### End-to-end script and logging
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_refresh_pilot.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N39ax-05 milestone=M7 labels=tenfold,gauntlet,pilot
seed=0 needs=cpu plants=changed-setting,hidden-drift,foreign-hardware`): (0) `FROB_TEST_SEED=0 cargo test -p
frob-conformance tenfold::refresh_pilot`; (1) reads the registration row of
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` read-only and verifies that its protocol hash and timestamp precede
the first re-run's ledger; (2) for each recorded re-run,
verifies that its ledger closes, its disclosure block resolves and its manifest differs from the M3 freeze
only in pins; (3) recomputes drift, the home-task ratios and the per-stratum marks from the recorded figures;
(4) on a scratch copy, plants a changed solver setting, a figure edited to hide a 12% drift, and a re-run from
other hardware under the tournament's hardware key, asserting all three are caught (the second by
recomputation from the ledgers, the third by the disclosure block's hardware fields). The incumbent runs themselves are launched by the
incumbent harness out of process, under the machine-envelope guard or the approved compute class; the script
does not start them. It logs through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_refresh_pilot/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson`: `cmd` lines with exact command line, exit code
and wall time; `metric` lines per configuration (frozen figure, three new figures, median, drift against 10%),
per incumbent (home-task ratio against 20%) and per new stratum (representable or not); one `assert` per
check; a `summary` line naming GATE-GAUNT-08. No sealed content and no private-scene data.

### Known answers and negative controls
- Known answers: the drift table of the unit test; thresholds 10% and 20%; four strata frozen at M3 and three
  new locomotion strata probed.
- Negative controls: the changed setting; the edited figure; a re-run on hardware other than the tournament's
  presented under the tournament's hardware key (the disclosure block's hardware fields disagree).
- Determinism: the arithmetic is class D0 on recorded figures; the runs carry their own class and are compared
  as medians of three, never as single values.

## Considerations

- Compute: three seeds of every M3 configuration plus home tasks and six probes is above the default machine
  envelope; it runs under the compute class approved for the rival audit, and without that approval the pilot
  is recorded `not run` and every stratum is budgeted at the full rule, which is the safe side.
- A licence that changed between releases can forbid publishing a comparison; the re-pin step records licences
  first, and a forbidden release keeps the last pin whose licence allows publication, with the reason.
- The pilot is run by the rival's advocates or by a session with no authorship in this system; its figures
  feed the tuning budget and never the ratio.
