## (e) Contradictions, gaps and what was done

1. **The completion non-inferiority condition cannot be met at the default cell size.** §39.7 requires, for the
   word, an exact one-sided 95% lower bound above −0.05 on the completion (and coverage) difference A minus B, and
   in the next sentence notes that zero misses in 24 families leaves an upper bound of 11.7% on the miss rate. Any
   exact bound is then at best −0.1174 at 24 families; the margin is attainable only from 59 families (0.95⁵⁹ <
   0.05 ≤ 0.95⁵⁸). As written, no 24-family (or 32- or 46-family) cell can return `ten-fold`, and the planning
   table of §39.7 does not show this because it omits the condition. Done: the bound is implemented as written
   (N39a-30); the pre-analysis includes it in reachability (N39a-33); an owner-decision bead states the arithmetic,
   five options and the fail-closed default "as written" (N39a-31); BD-314 depends on that decision.
2. **Ordering between seed beads and the pre-opening gates.** The seed graph has BD-314 → BD-315, BD-316 →
   BD-317, but GATE-TENFOLD-04 can only be recorded after the arms are frozen (inside BD-315 and BD-316) and
   before any sealed run (also inside them). Done: N39a-40 delivers the checker and closes on a rehearsal gate; the
   record of the real instance is written in the flow of the arm beads; BD-315 depends on N39a-40. The same holds
   for the reachability decisions (N39a-34) and for clause 3 of GATE-TENFOLD-05 (N39a-10 records it `pending`
   until the freeze). The dependencies the lead should add are in (d).
3. **"Fresh world clusters per submission" (§39.3) against "a pair is scored on the same sealed acceptance
   worlds" (§39.7).** Reconciled in N39a-06 and N39a-09: clusters are derived from the sealed key, the mission, the
   campaign index and the submission index, not from the arm, so they are fresh per submission and shared by the
   arms of a pair at equal index.
4. **"Bias-corrected bootstrap … on ln R̂" (§39.7).** Read literally as the bias-corrected percentile bootstrap
   without acceleration; such bounds are unchanged by an increasing transformation, so N39a-28 orders exact
   rational replicates and evaluates no logarithm, which is what makes the kit's bit-for-bit recomputation
   (§39.14) possible. The accelerated variant is recorded as rejected unless ratified before the first suite hash.
5. **The printed zero-count bound 0.1174 (§39.17)** is 0.11734615… rounded up, not to nearest (0.1173). Kept as
   printed, under one rendering rule for all harness records: bounds are rounded outward (N39a-01, N39a-30).
6. **The planning model of the pre-analysis table is not fully specified in §39.7.** The model stated in N39a-33
   (a shared family difficulty of spread 0.5 and independent arm noises of variance s²/2, so that the true ratio
   is the ratio of means) was found by recomputation at drafting time to reproduce every printed entry within
   0.015 and the last column exactly; two other natural readings do not (at 24 families and spread 0.8 they give
   P(`withdrawn`) at a true ratio of 3 near 0.56 or 0.07 instead of 0.50).
7. **The chapter states refusals without codes, and Appendix O has no ten-fold codes.** No product code was
   invented. N39a-42 registers harness refusal reasons and maps them to existing product codes where the meaning
   coincides; whether any deserves a product code is left to the registry owner.
8. **No type sketches (Appendix I) and no verbs (Appendix J) exist for the harness.** Record layouts, module
   layout and the operator tool `frob-tenfold` and scorer `frob-tenfold-score` are DESIGN in these beads; the two
   records the chapter prints (freeze, ledger) are carried verbatim (N39a-18, N39a-24) and the other three
   (mission, receipt, report) are designed in N39a-05, N39a-09 and N39a-36.
9. **"The scorer depends on nothing outside the standard library" (§39.14)** is read to allow the auditor's
   crate `frob-cert`, whose own dependency table is empty (N39a-01); if that reading is rejected the scorer
   vendors the four routines it uses.
