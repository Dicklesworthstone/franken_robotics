## Background

The program's headline claim is a ratio of times: the restricted mean time to an independently accepted answer of
the strongest composed rival, divided by ours. A ratio without a measured denominator is not a ratio. No end-to-end
phase ledger of an agent-driven design loop on current incumbent stacks has been published (FACT, source register);
the table the thesis uses composes published figures from different stacks (about 410 s to train a flat-ground
quadruped policy on one RTX 4090 with about 45 s of compilation inside it; about an hour per round of 16 candidates
in the one published agent loop; agent latency assumed 60 to 120 s per turn) and is explicitly not a measurement.
This bead measures the loop: the composed rival, driven by the common agent, end to end, with every second of the
clock assigned to a phase. It closes the program gate GATE-TENFOLD-02 (claim kind: Performance; M1): closed ledgers
on at least 12 development missions of the strata `legged-quad` and `wheeled`, the composite table replaced by
measured rows, and a removable share and a ceiling recorded for every lever of the thesis. If the gate is not met,
M1 does not exit, the thesis arithmetic stays a HYPOTHESIS, no work package is funded as a speed lever, and the
composite table may not be cited as a denominator.

Terms. *`trj`*: the Linux workstation with two RTX 4090 cards. *Composed rival (arm B)*: a named incumbent engine,
per-design reinforcement learning, a standard outer-loop optimizer and the same agent, assembled and tuned by
people or sessions who wrote no FrankenRobotics code. *Clock T*: wall time from the agent receiving the mission
card to acceptance of the first correct answer; everything in between is inside it. *Phase ledger*: for one run,
the list of timed intervals and the two tables derived from it. *Exclusive table*: every instant of the clock is
assigned to exactly one of the thirteen phases `author`, `propose`, `check`, `synthesize`, `compile`, `screen`,
`train`, `evaluate`, `qualify`, `certify`, `diagnose`, `wait`, `adjudicate` (remainder: `unattributed`). *Closed*:
the exclusive phases sum to T within 2%. *Cold*: the run starts from the card and nothing mission-specific. *Warm*:
a declared sequence of amendments to an accepted cold answer, with declared reusable inputs. *Disclosure block*:
the mandatory list of conditions on any performance figure (hardware and driver, backend and versions, world
count, step and decimation, solver settings, contact capacity, sensors, whether policy and learning are included,
scene, determinism class, warm-up and duration, cold or warm with compile times and cache state, thermal state and
host load, comparator revision and configuration, energy or a named gap).

Rejected alternatives. Using the composite table as the denominator (rejected: it joins stacks and is not a loop).
Timing only training (rejected: the one published loop is dominated by evaluation rounds, and diagnosis time is
unpublished). Letting the builders run and tune the rival (rejected: an under-built rival measures the builder).

## Scope

In: the measurement and everything computed from it: the runs, their ledgers, the pooled phase-share table with
intervals, the measured replacements for the composite rows, the recomputed ceilings with their worked values, the
removable share of each of the eight levers with its interval and ceiling (each referred to the original loop and
recorded with its time base, so that the order-rule check can be applied to any product formed from them later),
the repeat, the order of record, and the measurement record of GATE-TENFOLD-02 (the committed ledgers, tables and
disclosure blocks with their digest: what the gate reads).

Out, with the bead that has it: the program gate itself (N40-13, which depends on this bead): the check
`tenfold02(records)` that the measurement record is complete (at least 12 missions with closed ledgers, every cell
of the regenerated table resolving to a ledger, a share and a ceiling for every lever, no run dropped), the row
GATE-TENFOLD-02 in the gate register written by a session that did not run the measurement, and the consequence
if the gate is not met. That bead computes no share and no ceiling. The status of each lever (`funded-as-speed`,
`struck`, `marginal`) and the funding decision that reads the shares (N39a-39, the one decision of
KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER at M1). Also out: the phases that need no agent and the rival audit (BD-040, N39b-08); the Mac
rows (BD-142); the document that prints published beside measured figures (N00-18); the rival arm of the partial
gate (BD-315). The mechanisms this bead runs on measured data are not rebuilt here: the campaign clock and
censoring (N39a-03), the ledger and closure rule (N39a-24), the wrapper and tag map (N39a-25), pooled shares and
the per-lever removable-share bound (N39a-26), the ceiling calculators (N39a-37, which call the exact formulas of
N00-17), and the order rule and registration of speed comparisons (N39a-38).

## Technical approach

1. **Preconditions.** Incumbent pins, licences and frozen warm configurations exist (N39b-08, BD-039). The agent
   model, revision and caps are pinned (N39b-31). The rival configuration per stratum is the one that finishes
   the development missions soonest among: MuJoCo Warp under MuJoCo Playground or mjlab, or Isaac Lab on Newton
   or PhysX, each with its own PPO recipe and one policy per design; an outer loop of CMA-ES or Bayesian
   optimization with successive halving; the engine's per-world model fields for parametric variants; warm
   compilation caches. One frozen configuration per stratum, never a per-mission choice.
2. **Missions.** At least 12 development missions, at least six per stratum, drawn from the public pool. If the
   pool does not exist at M1, this bead authors the cards (they are public by definition and join the pool):
   legged briefs over stairs, gaps, stepping stones and rubble; wheeled briefs over slopes, curbs and a threshold
   delivery with a loaded cart; each with robot family and bounds, world generator parameters, the requirement,
   the acceptance battery (n, threshold, tier), resource caps and permitted inputs.
3. **Deadline and acceptance at M1 (DESIGN, provisional).** H = 14,400 s per mission until the adjudicator sets
   the stratum deadlines. Acceptance runs in the pinned adjudicating simulator if one has been named; otherwise
   in the rival's own CPU reference tier, operated by a session that is not the arm's agent, and every record
   then carries `adjudication: provisional-home`. Reason: the gate needs clocks that stop at an acceptance, and
   the neutral simulator's own acceptance gate is not due until M3.
4. **A run.** Fresh agent context; the card and permitted inputs are delivered and the clock starts; the agent
   works through the rival's own tools and documentation plus the advocate's guide; each submission goes to
   acceptance; the run ends at the first acceptance or at H (censored, contributes H). No human in the loop; any
   intervention is logged and voids the run for ceiling arithmetic.
5. **Instrumentation.** The wrapper tags every process it launches with a phase; the raw ledger is a list of
   intervals (start, end, phase, resource, and for agent turns token counts). Derivation of the exclusive table:
   an agent turn in progress owns its instants; while the agent is blocked on jobs, the job that finishes last
   among those awaited owns them; while blocked on the harness or the adjudicator, `wait` or `adjudicate` owns
   them. The resource table gives device-seconds and core-seconds per phase, including background work, and the
   learner's share of `train`. A run with more than 2% `unattributed` is flagged `ledger-open`, kept, and
   excluded from every ceiling and share below.
6. **Cold and warm, never pooled.** Every mission is run cold. For each accepted cold answer a predeclared
   sequence of three amendments is run warm (a parametric edit, a single-limb or wheel edit, a friction or
   latency perturbation; DESIGN), with the rival's caches and policies kept as a fair rival would keep them. A
   failed amendment stays in the sequence at H. Standing assets are listed with their measured build cost S.
7. **Shares.** For regime g and phase φ, the pooled share is p_φ = Σ_runs seconds_φ ÷ Σ_runs min(T, H) over closed
   runs. Its stated interval is the 2.5% and 97.5% percentiles of 10,000 bootstrap replicates that resample
   missions with replacement (seeded; the seed is in the record). Also recorded: agent turn latency t (median,
   90th percentile), turns per mission, trainings per mission, cost per trained candidate C in device-seconds,
   edit-to-first-step per edit, the environment step's share s of training, completion by H.
8. **Measured rows.** Each composite row gets its measured value and disclosure block: the agent's turn; design
   realization; compile and first step; training per candidate; step share of training; evaluation; confirmation
   retrainings; trainings per campaign; compute per campaign; candidates failing trivially; diagnosis time. A
   published figure reproduced within 20% is marked reproduced; otherwise the difference is explained in writing
   and the measured figure replaces it.
9. **Ceilings recomputed** on the pooled cold ledger (and separately warm), each with its inputs:
   (a) removing a phase with share p: at most 1/(1 − p); (b) improving a share f by factor r with overhead h:
   at most 1/(1 − f + f/r + h); (c) an infinitely fast environment step: at most 1/(1 − p_train·s) on the loop;
   (d) one design per agent turn: at most (C + t)/t; (e) compute per decision: C ÷ (c + c_T·f) tabulated for
   the planning values of our cheap rung c, training rung c_T and trained fraction f, with the largest f that
   still gives ten; (f) unremovable cost u (the rival's own `author`, the common agent's turns at the smallest
   plausible turn count, `adjudicate`): R ≤ RMT_B ÷ u; (g) the horizon: R ≤ H ÷ RMT_A, evaluated for RMT_A at
   the planning values; (h) completion: R ≤ 1/q, tabulated for q; (i) the design cycle alone: the measured
   incumbent cycle (training per candidate with its `compile`, plus one agent turn) ÷ our planning cycle of 120 s
   with no training in it, valid only if a controller that needs no per-design training can stand in for a trained
   policy when designs are ranked (untested; the premise is printed with the value). Every formula is evaluated by
   the exact functions of `frob_conformance::thesis` (N00-17): (a), (b), (d), (f), (g) and (h) through the per-cell
   calculators of N39a-37, and the thesis's nine ceilings (with (c), (e) and (i)) by running N00-17's declared
   example `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example thesis -- ceilings --ledger <pooled cold ledger> --json`, which
   labels each value `measured` and prints it beside the published one. Each ceiling exists because a tempting
   comparison ignores it, and each is arithmetic on the rival's ledger, never on ours. Worked values the
   recomputation must reproduce, with RMT the restricted mean time (time capped at H) and R the ratio rival ÷
   ours: (a) removing half the loop gives 2, removing 85% gives 6.7, ten needs p ≥ 0.9, and a phase holding 10%
   gives 1/0.9 = 1.11; (b) f = 0.8, r = 4, h = 0.05 gives 1/(0.2 + 0.2 + 0.05) = 2.222, with f, r and h all
   measured on the rival's critical path; (d) C = 420 s and t = 60 s give 8; (f) when u exceeds one tenth of the
   rival's time a ratio of ten needs another stage changed or the claim changed, since our arm's floor (authoring,
   the common agent's turns, adjudication, checking, serialization) is removed by no mechanism; (g) H = 4 h and
   RMT_A = 0.5 h give 8, because the rival's restricted time cannot exceed H; (h) with q the share of deadlines our
   arm misses, RMT_A ≥ q·H and RMT_B ≤ H, so with 12% unfinished the ratio cannot exceed 25/3 ≈ 8.33; (i) an
   incumbent cycle of 360 s gives 3 and one of 420 s gives 3.5.
10. **Lever shares.** Lever 1 (many designs per turn) acts on `propose` through the number of turns and on
    `screen`; lever 2 (train few) on `train`; lever 3 (spend where it matters) on `screen` and `evaluate`;
    levers 4 and 7 (find failures; explain by intervention) on `diagnose`; lever 5 (answers over families) on
    `certify` and `propose`; lever 6 (do not recompute) on `check`, `compile` and `evaluate`; lever 8 (short
    setup) on `compile` and `author`. For each lever the record states its removable share p (the pooled share
    of the phases it acts on that it could remove, with the argument), the interval, and the ceiling 1/(1 − p).
    A phase share is never counted twice across levers when a product of levers is formed: each factor is
    measured on the time the earlier factors left, and a pruning ratio is never multiplied by a speed-up measured
    on work the pruning already removed (the order rule). This record forms no product: each lever's share is
    recorded with `time_base: "original-loop"` and its phase set, and the order-rule check of N39a-38 (which reads
    this record after it is committed) rejects any later product whose second factor was measured on the full
    ledger, with the harness reason `tenfold.order-rule-violation`, mapped to FROB-REF-866 in the ten-fold refusal
    registry N39-REF. This record states shares; it assigns no status to a lever.
    Whether a lever with a share under 0.10 is struck from the speed case is decided by N39a-39 on these shares
    and their repeat ranges.
11. **What the baseline may change, and may not.** May: the ranking of levers, work-package funding, the
    deadlines H, the expected ranges by mission class. May not: the estimand or the outcome rules. The record
    carries the digest of the estimand and outcome-rule parameter block; a change of that digest in the same
    commit as a baseline record is refused.
12. **Repeat.** The whole pass is repeated under the next campaign index. Each phase's pooled share from the
    repeat must lie inside the first pass's stated interval.
13. **Order of record.** The ledgers, the share table and the disclosure blocks are committed and their digest
    entered in the project ledger before any ratio against an incumbent is written anywhere. The same commit
    appends to `incumbent_audit.toml` the second record of `NF-08` (no published end-to-end phase ledger of a
    design loop; N00-14), linking these ledgers, which closes that not-found item by measurement.
14. **Envelope.** Twelve missions at H = 4 h are 48 device-hours in the worst case, before warm sequences and
    the repeat, which is above the default class (16 GiB, 8 cores, 1 hour, one device). The run needs an
    approved campaign class. Fail-closed default: each run is cut at 3,600 s on one device, recorded
    `deadline: envelope-limited`, and the gate record says so.

Determinism: the measurement is wall-clock and is not reproducible bit for bit; the derived tables are a pure
function of the raw intervals (class D0 for the derivation). Evidence: every figure is `Tested`, claim kind
Performance or Parity, `reality: no-claim`.

      with warm sequences separate; no run was dropped; censored runs contribute H.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::baseline`: `exclusive_table_last_finisher_owns_blocked_time` (two jobs
ending at 40 s and 70 s under one blocked interval of 70 s: the second owns it); `closure_flag_at_two_percent`
(2.0% closed, 2.1% open); `pooled_share_is_ratio_of_sums` (two runs, train 100 of 200 and 900 of 1,000: share
1,000/1,200 = 0.8333, not the mean 0.70); `single_pair_ceilings` (rival column `author` 900, `propose` 1,080,
`check` 240, `compile` 540, `screen` 0, `train` 4,920, `evaluate` 360, `qualify` 0, `diagnose` 600, `wait` 180,
`adjudicate` 180, `synthesize` and `certify` 0, clock 9,000: train share 54.67%, ceiling 9,000/4,080 = 2.206;
`check` 2.67% gives 1.027); `ceiling_design_cycle` (incumbent cycle 360 s and 420 s against 120 s → 3 and 7/2);
`lever_share_never_double_counted`; `ratio_without_baseline_digest_refused`; `baseline_cannot_touch_estimand`;
`ceiling_remove_phase` (p = 0.5 → 2; p = 0.85 → 6.667; p = 0.9 → 10; p = 0.10 → 1.111); `ceiling_improve_part`
(f = 0.8, r = 4, h = 0.05 → 2.222); `ceiling_completion` (q = 0.12 → 25/3); `ceiling_common_latency` (C = 420 s,
t = 60 s → 8); `ceiling_horizon` (H = 4 h, RMT_A = 0.5 h → 8); `lever_shares_carry_time_base` (every lever row
has `time_base: "original-loop"` and its phase set, and the record holds no product of lever factors; the order-rule
tests themselves are N39a-38's `order_rule_pruning` and `order_rule_sequence`);
`ceiling_uses_rival_ledger` (a ceiling computed from our arm's ledger is refused: the single-pair ledger gives
2.206 on the rival's column and a different value on ours).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_incumbent_loop.sh` (bead BD-143; needs `cuda`, an approved envelope). Steps: verify pins and
the agent pin; run the missions; derive tables; compute shares, ceilings (the per-cell calculators, then
`cargo run -p frob-conformance --example thesis -- ceilings --ledger <pooled cold ledger> --json`), lever shares; run the repeat; assert
closure, intervals and the order of record. With `E2E_SMALL=1` it replays a recorded synthetic interval trace
(labelled `synthetic`, gates not claimable). Logs through the shared helper of STD-E2E to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_incumbent_loop/<utc>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line carries `ts`,
`script`, `bead: "BD-143"`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall time; each `cmd` line
carries the envelope's evidence block (claim Performance or Parity, class `Tested`, tier, determinism, the
incumbent and agent pins as identities, the bootstrap seed); each share is a `metric` with its unit, its interval
and the 10% funding line; each ceiling a `metric` with its inputs and the worked value it must reproduce; host
load and thermal state per run; digests of every ledger and of the estimand and outcome-rule block; on failure
the first phase whose share left its interval, the run identifiers and the ledger paths needed to replay it. The
final `summary` line counts assertions passed and failed, runs launched, censored and `ledger-open`, and names
GATE-TENFOLD-02.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the single-pair ledger (6.0; 2.206; floor 8.571); completion cap 1/q (KA-009); the general bound
13.5 at f = 0.97, r = 40, h = 0.02 (KA-014). Negative controls: 3% unattributed; a planted mis-tag (training
tagged `wait`) must move the `train` share outside its interval (live-dependency proof); a planted 25% slowdown of
training in the repeat must fail the repeat check; an A/A repeat must pass.

## Considerations

- Risk (register): the denominator is wrong because incumbents are faster or their loop is shorter than the
  composite. This bead is the earliest signal; a quadruped ceiling under ten is a live outcome and is recorded.
- `trj` is often heavily loaded (FACT): host load and thermal state are recorded per run; arms of later gates
  are interleaved for this reason.
- The agent's sampling is seeded only where the provider allows; the result is conditional on the pinned agent.
- Incumbents run out of process, are never linked and are never a truth; their agreement with anything is Parity.
- If a physics row later falls below 0.33 of the incumbent (KILL-PARITY), the arithmetic is redone (N39b-10).
- Revisit if: the provisional acceptance path and the neutral simulator disagree on which runs were accepted.
