## Background

The ten-fold test compares wall-clock time from a mission card to an independently accepted answer between
FrankenRobotics (arm A), the strongest composed rival (arm B: an incumbent engine with per-design reinforcement
learning, an outer-loop optimizer and the same agent) and an ablation arm (arm C: a program of at most 2,000 lines
over arm B's engine that implements the portable mechanisms; it is the first form of the composed incumbent W).
The partial gate at M3 is the first time the comparison is made on sealed missions. It exists so that the speed
claim meets a measurement while the program can still change course: a class whose ratio is under 3 leaves the
speed claim, and a tie with the ablation arm changes what is shipped. The gate GATE-TENFOLD-06 (claim kind:
Performance) passes when the record exists and the outcome rules have been applied, whatever the ratio. M3 does
not exit without it. This bead is the closing half of the gate: the protocol (BD-314), the rival arm (BD-315) and
our arm (BD-316) come first; here the gate is closed, audited, scored, judged by rules written in advance, and
recorded. The closing sequence itself is one operator command, `frob-tenfold gate close` (N39bp4-01), which the
rehearsal (N39b-12) runs on synthetic arms before any sealed family is opened; this bead runs the same command,
once, on the sealed suite, and owns what only the real gate has: the adjudicating session, the hypothesis
written before scoring, the outcome ADRs, the retirement of the sealed families and the hand-off of the record.

Terms. *Stratum*: a set of mission families sharing a robot class or answer kind, and a deadline H. *Regime*:
cold or warm. *Cell*: a stratum and a regime. *Family*: the independent unit; missions sharing a generator draw,
a site, a material batch or a parent design are one family. *Pair*: the campaigns of two arms on the same mission
and campaign index, scored on the same sealed acceptance worlds. *RMT*: restricted mean time E[min(T, H)].
*Pooled ratio*: R̂ = Σ_j min(T_B,j, H) ÷ Σ_j min(T_A,j, H) over the pairs of the cell (a ratio of sums, never a mean
of ratios). *Confirmatory cell*: one of at most four named before sealing whose verdict can carry the word.

## Scope

In: closing the gate; the post-close audits; the reveal; scoring every cell in scope; the tie test and recovery
ratios; the outcome rules; the record of GATE-TENFOLD-06 and of the M3 part of GATE-TENFOLD-13; the ADRs; family
retirement. Out: the closing command, its module `tenfold::close`, the layout of the gate record and of the
decision inputs, and their unit tests and script (N39bp4-01, a dependency; split from this bead in polishing
round 4, because this bead depends on its rehearsal N39b-12 and the rehearsal runs that code); the run-time
acceptance service and the sealed suite (BD-314, the adjudication beads); the arms
(BD-315, BD-316); the kill-gate decision records themselves (N39b-06, N39b-07, N39b-03, N39b-04, N39a-11,
N39a-34); the decisions that other subsystems read on this record (N39b-11, N12-30, N25a-18).

Also out: the program-level reading of this record (N40-15, which depends on this bead). The division with it
is exact. This bead applies the outcome rules (through the engine of N39b-05), writes the one ADR per outcome
and produces the gate record: the report, its digest, the per-cell results and the ADR identifiers. N40-15
applies no rule and writes no outcome ADR. It runs the check `tenfold06` on this record (every field present,
every verdict the one the table assigns to the recorded numbers, an ADR for every outcome, the pre-opening
gates recorded before the first sealed mission was opened), and its fresh-context session enters the row of
GATE-TENFOLD-06 in the gate register, citing this record. It also carries the consequences read at program level
(the measured table replacing the expected ranges, the funding of M4).

## Technical approach

**Cells in scope.** (`legged-quad`, cold) and (`wheeled`, cold) are confirmatory. (`envelope`, warm) and
(`sensing`, cold) are confirmatory if their recorded pre-analyses meet the reachability conditions, and
descriptive otherwise. Tracked and hybrid briefs wait for M4 and are first measured at M7. Arms A, B and C all
run. Default design: m = 24 sealed families per cell and c = 1 campaign per family (c = 2 where fewer than 24
families exist); no verdict on fewer than ten paired campaigns per arm.

**What arm A has at M3, stated in the record.** The daemon with slot dispatch, mixed batches, the baseline
controllers and the sampling fallback, the ladder with fixed allocation, the qualification lane, adversarial
search and intervention-based attribution, the memo graph, and mission queries on the CPU. It does not have
family policies, inheritance or rank-agreement allocation (M4). The gate therefore measures the product without
the lever "train few". HYPOTHESIS, written into the record before scoring: on `legged-quad` the ratio lies near
the bottom of the expected range of 3 to 10. The rules apply all the same, and a class withdrawn at M3 stays
withdrawn until M7 measures it on fresh families.

**Procedure (each step is an entry in the gate's event log with a hash and a time).** The adjudicating session
runs `frob-tenfold gate close --gate M3-partial --json` (N39bp4-01); the fourteen steps below are what its
twelve stages do (steps 7 to 10 are its stage `score`, step 12 its stages `report` and `record`), stated here so
that the gate can be read from this bead alone. A stage that refuses is repaired as its reason says and the
command is called again; it resumes after the last completed stage.

1. *Close.* Confirm every planned campaign of every arm has ended (accepted, censored at H, or censored at a
   resource-cap breach at the instant of the breach). A harness fault that is not the arm's own was repaired by
   re-running the whole pair once under the next campaign index; a second fault excludes the pair for every arm,
   is counted, and a cell with more than 5% of pairs excluded is descriptive only. Exclusions are fixed before
   any times of the pair are compared.
2. *Integrity audit* (N39b-01, N39b-23). Roles disjoint; sealing steps in order; every arm frozen and published
   before its first paired run and unchanged since; ledgers and receipts unedited; no arm's logs show the
   adjudicating simulator in development or tuning. Each failure is a disqualification ground, not a warning.
3. *Certificate audit.* Every accepted family answer is re-evaluated by the adjudicator's direct methods (exact
   rational evaluation at the sealed probe points, an SMT or interval tool on the raw mission statement, dense
   sampling in the adjudicating simulator); every submitted certificate is replayed by the offline auditor. A
   certificate its declared checker accepted and that is shown false within its stated scope is a false
   certificate: KILL-TENFOLD-CERT (N39b-03). A truthful `unknown`, a refused certificate or a narrower scope
   costs coverage and time and is not a false certificate.
4. *Leak audit* (N39b-02). Canary scan of every transcript, firewall audit, access logs. A leak voids the
   touched cells for that arm and retires the families; if the extent cannot be bounded the gate is void and is
   re-run on a rotated suite: KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK (N39b-04).
5. *Late-error rule.* An answer accepted and since shown wrong within its scope scores that mission as
   unresolved at H for the arm that submitted it.
6. *Reveal.* Paired times, per-world outcomes and transcripts are released; the sealed keys are revealed.
7. *Score* (the scorer of N39b-19, once, at n; no monitor output enters). Per cell: R̂; the one-sided 95% bounds
   L and U from the bias-corrected family bootstrap (10,000 replicates on ln R̂, families resampled with their
   pairs kept together; descriptive, never the basis of the word); the per-family ratios and the exact guard count; completion by H and, on family strata, the share
   of mandatory obligations discharged, for each arm, with the non-inferiority test at margin 0.05; for warm
   cells R_warm at N = 1, 10, 100 and the limit, the standing-asset costs S and the crossover count N*; the
   bounded paired contrast where a population result was declared (N39b-35); every cost column.
8. *Three arms* (N39b-43). The tie test RMT_C ÷ RMT_A with its lower bound and the accepted-scope comparison;
   the recovery ratios r_T and r_C with 95% intervals for every stratum that has a ratio; the three phase
   ablations on both briefs with their bounds (N39b-45); the one-receipt pipeline's binding property (N39b-44).
9. *Ceilings.* The six ceilings evaluated on the rival's closed ledger per cell; the binding one is named.
10. *Verdict*, first row that applies: `disqualified`; `descriptive` (n < 10, more than 5% excluded, not named
    confirmatory, or underpowered); `withdrawn` (R̂ < 3, or the futility monitor stopped the cell, which it may
    do at this gate: with Y_i = 1 when ρ_i ≥ 3, E_n = Π (1 + ½·(1 − 2·Y_i)) over families in randomized run
    order reached 20; the shortest stop is eight consecutive families below 3, 1.5⁸ = 25.6); `ten-fold` (R̂ ≥ 10 against the stronger of B and the
    composed incumbent, the guard rejects at the cell's Holm-adjusted level, completion and coverage
    non-inferior, the ablation arm has not tied, the adjudication condition holds); `measured` otherwise.
11. *Outcome rules* (N39b-05). For each cell the report sentence and the program action are produced from the
    table fixed before sealing; each outcome is recorded as an ADR in M3, written by this bead's session under
    `docs/adr/` (the one directory for decision records) from the engine's draft; a kill outcome is not
    re-tested on the same suite.
12. *Report and record* (N39b-14, N39b-15). One table per cell, one phase ledger per arm per cell, the labels in
    force (`self-adjudicated`, `rival: internally advocated`, `adjudication: cross-home`, `reality: no-claim`),
    the limits block. The record of GATE-TENFOLD-06 cites the report digest; the M3 row of GATE-TENFOLD-13 cites
    r_T, r_C, the ablations and the one-receipt record.
13. *Decision inputs for other subsystems*, computed once, by the stage `decision-inputs` of the closing command
    (`decision_inputs.json`, N39bp4-01), and digested in the record. The per-cell table
    of family answers decided by a family certificate and by the generic comparator at equal cost (read by the
    decision on specialized projections, KILL-FAMILYCERT, N12-30); the accepted scope and turnaround per
    amendment of the short robust program on the family strata against arm A's (read by the mission subsystem's
    tie rule, KILL-AUGUR-TIE, N25a-18); the added latency per rollout (median, 95th percentile, total per
    campaign) if rival learners drove whole rollouts through the job interface; the pooled `train` share of arm
    B per design class with its family bootstrap interval. N39b-11 takes the lever-2 funding, BET-10-arm and
    adapter decisions on these inputs and runs the learner-swap diagnostic; it produces no second version of
    any table.
14. *Rotate.* Missions, worlds and receipts are revealed and every sealed family of this gate is retired into
    the development pool; none may serve as a sealed family again.

If the compute class was not approved (N39b-29 at its default), only campaigns that fit the default class run:
the CPU strata are recorded and the device strata are `not run` with the reason; if no stratum has a record the
gate is `not run` and M3 does not exit (the rule of N39b-29, step 6, and of the M3 exit check, N41-15). The record
directory follows the replication-kit layout so that the kit builder can package this gate later.

**The two standing kill gates at this gate.** Steps 3 and 4 run the procedures of KILL-TENFOLD-CERT (N39b-03) and
KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK (N39b-04) on every allegation and every evidence item, and leave each procedure's ADR draft
and decision-entry fragment in the gate directory, `not-fired` when the audits found nothing. The session that
enters the row of GATE-TENFOLD-06 (N40-15) commits the two ADRs and enters the two M3 decision entries, citing
that row's receipt; this bead enters neither.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every cell in scope has R̂, [L, U], verdict, completion and coverage per arm, the tie test, and one closed
      phase ledger per arm; warm cells carry S and N*; every ratio carries regime, m, n, H, the adjudication
      label and the freeze hashes (INV-TENFOLD-08).
- [ ] The outcome rules were applied and each outcome is an ADR dated in M3 (seed: recorded whatever the ratio,
      under the rule of BET-13).
- [ ] r_T and r_C with 95% intervals are on record for every stratum that has a ratio; the three ablations ran on
      both briefs inside their bounds or the stratum is voided as an accounting error (GATE-TENFOLD-13, M3 part).
- [ ] The inputs for KILL-FAMILYCERT and KILL-AUGUR-TIE are on the record and their triggers are evaluated on
      measured values, so that their decision records can close (seed: decided).
- [ ] The scorer recomputes every published number from ledgers and receipts alone, bit for bit.
- [ ] The gate directory holds `gate_record.json` and `decision_inputs.json` as N39bp4-01 defines them, both
      passing `frob-tenfold gate check --gate M3-partial`, the passing `post-report` audit, and the ADR drafts
      and decision-entry fragments of KILL-TENFOLD-CERT and KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK for this gate.
- [ ] All seven harness negative controls were caught in the rehearsal run before the suite opened.
- [ ] Sealed families of the gate are retired; a second use is refused.
- [ ] The gate record of GATE-TENFOLD-06 (report digest, per-cell results, ADR identifiers) and the M3 part of
      the record of GATE-TENFOLD-13 are complete and are handed to the program-gate beads (N40-15 for
      GATE-TENFOLD-06, N40-22 for GATE-TENFOLD-13), whose fresh-context sessions, which implemented none of
      this, enter the rows in the gate register. This bead enters no row itself, so that the session that scored
      the gate is not the one that records it.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The closing module's tests are N39bp4-01's (`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::close`:
`close_refuses_open_campaign`, `exclusion_fixed_before_times`, `second_fault_excludes_pair_for_all_arms`,
`five_percent_exclusions_make_cell_descriptive`, `late_error_scores_h`, `verdict_first_row_wins`,
`record_requires_all_inv08_fields`, `retired_family_cannot_be_sealed_again` and the others listed there); they
were this bead's before the split and must pass at the gate's frozen harness commit. This bead adds one test of
what it writes, `cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::gate06`: `outcome_adrs_cover_outcomes` (on the
rehearsal's kept gate directory, every outcome row of `gate_record.json` has an ADR file under a scratch
`docs/adr/` whose evidence hashes equal the draft's; a record with one outcome lacking its ADR fails, naming the
cell and the row).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh` (owned by N39b-12, a dependency) rehearses this whole procedure on synthetic
arms and a development-pool suite, with the closing command of N39bp4-01, whose own script
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_gate_close.sh` it runs as a stage. The real gate is run by the adjudicator's session with the
same commands; its transcript and NDJSON log (the STD-E2E fields: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the
exact command line, exit code, wall time) record every step, the digests of the event log before and after, each
measured value against its threshold, each refusal, and a final `summary` naming GATE-TENFOLD-06 and
GATE-TENFOLD-13 as touched.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers used as fixtures: the six-family card (R̂ = 2.647, would be `withdrawn` in a registered cell); the
warm card (R_warm(10) = 2.120, N* = 5); critical counts 17 of 24, and 18 of 24 at the first Holm step with four
cells. Negative controls that must be caught: a planted false certificate; a canary in a transcript; a ledger
with 3% unattributed; a freeze manifest dated after the suite opened; a four-family confirmatory cell; a request
for the fixed-n verdict at a monitor's stopping time; an arm that touched the adjudicating simulator in tuning.

## Considerations

- Cost: a cell costs m·c·Σ_arms E[min(T, H)] device-hours; 288 device-hours worst case at 24 families, three arms
  and H = 4 h; about 70 expected (HYPOTHESIS). This exceeds the default compute class (N39b-29).
- Without an external adjudicator every ratio is `self-adjudicated`; the gate still passes, and the words
  "independently accepted" and "ten-fold" wait for an external re-run of the replication kit.
- A disqualification awards nothing to the other arm; a cell whose rival is disqualified has no denominator and
  is re-run at the next gate.
- A defect found in a frozen arm after the suite opens voids the affected cells; it is never patched mid-gate.
- Risks: a composed layer ties (the layer then ships for the stratum); no neutral party is named.
