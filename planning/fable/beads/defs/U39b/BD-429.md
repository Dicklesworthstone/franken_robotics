## Background

At M7 the program measures its thesis on a sealed mission suite against the strongest composed rival and
publishes the result whatever it is. The word "ten-fold" is a verdict of a rule, and that rule is fixed before any
sealed run. This epic owns the rule for the tournament: the estimator, the sample (how many independent mission
families per cell), the analysis that shows which verdicts the rule can return at that sample, and the kill rules
that say what happens for each verdict. As an epic of M7 it is split into tasks when the milestone opens; part of
its done-condition is that a short plan exists and its pilot has run.

Why a pooled rule. An earlier draft required, on at least four of six briefs, a ratio of medians of at least 10
with a lower bound above 5, over five seeds. Simulation of that rule confirmed a true ratio of 10 in at most 18%
of campaigns (FACT, plan-time pilot; re-run in N39b-16), because every brief needed its own estimate above 10,
which a true 10 delivers half the time, and the requirement was multiplied across briefs. The pooled rule has one
estimate per cell; it confirms a true 10 about half the time (no honest threshold rule can do better) and reaches
80% near a true ratio of 11 to 12. Why a ratio of sums. An average of per-mission ratios is dominated by the
missions where arm A is fastest and hides the mission it never finishes; a ratio of sums charges that mission its
full deadline. Why two result types. Resampling cannot show a slow tail the sample does not contain, so a claim
about the population of missions needs more families than a statement about the sealed suite.

## Scope

In: the plan document for the rule at the tournament; the choice of result type and family count per cell; the
binding pre-analysis and the naming of at most four confirmatory cells; the enumeration of reachable verdicts;
the written outcome and kill rules as they apply at M7. Out: the estimator code (the harness statistics beads);
the suite (BD-428); the arms (BD-430, BD-431); adjudication (BD-432); publication (BD-433); the run order and
breach rules of the tournament (N39b-13).

## Technical approach

The short plan (`docs/delivery/epics/BD-429.md` when M7 opens) fixes the following contract.

1. **Estimand and estimator.** For arms A (this system) and B (the rival), stratum deadline H and pairs j of a
   cell: R̂ = Σ_j min(T_B,j, H) ÷ Σ_j min(T_A,j, H). A campaign not accepted by H contributes H; a pair with both
   arms censored stays in both sums; no run is dropped. For a warm cell R̂ is R_warm(10) = (S_B + 10·b̄) ÷
   (S_A + 10·ā), with ā, b̄ the pooled restricted mean times per amendment and S the standing-asset build costs.
   Where the composed incumbent W has a smaller restricted mean time than B, the word is governed by the ratio
   against W. Cold and warm are never pooled.
2. **Sample.** A cell has m mission families and c campaigns per family. Default m = 24, c = 1 (finite-suite
   result: a statement about the sealed suite). A population result needs at least 32 independent families at
   α = 0.05 and 46 where four confirmatory cells share the level, because a distribution-free procedure can
   certify R ≥ 10 only if (10/11)ⁿ ≤ α; the bound actually registered (the bounded paired contrast, N39b-35)
   needs 182 at α = 0.05 even on the best possible sample. Each cell declares its result type before sealing.
   Ten paired campaigns per arm is the floor for any verdict. The final sealed count is never below 12.
3. **Guard.** X = number of families with per-family ratio ρ_i > 5; reject "the median per-family ratio is at
   most 5" when the binomial(m, ½) tail P(X ≥ x) ≤ α. Critical counts at 0.05: 5 of 5, 6 of 6, 7 of 8, 10 of 12,
   12 of 16, 17 of 24, 22 of 32. At m = 4 the guard cannot be passed (1/16 = 0.0625). Across at most four
   confirmatory cells Holm's step-down procedure holds the gate-wide level at 0.05; with four cells the first
   step needs 18 of 24.
4. **Verdict, first row that applies.** `disqualified`; `descriptive` (n < 10, more than 5% of pairs excluded,
   not named confirmatory, or underpowered); `withdrawn` (R̂ < 3); `ten-fold` (R̂ ≥ 10 against the stronger of B
   and W, guard rejects at the adjusted level, completion and coverage of A non-inferior to B at margin 0.05,
   the ablation arm has not tied, adjudication condition holds); `measured` otherwise. On fewer than 32 families
   `ten-fold` reads "ten-fold on the sealed suite"; population wording also needs a nonnegative registered
   lower bound on the paired contrast.
5. **Pre-analysis, binding.** Recomputed from the development suite's measured times with censoring at H and
   the multiplicity adjustment, recorded before the suite opens (part of GATE-TENFOLD-04). It lists every
   verdict each cell can return at its n and level and the power at declared true ratios (5, 7, 8.5, 10, 12,
   15, 20) and the withdrawal probability at 2, 3 and 5. A cell is confirmatory only if: P(`ten-fold`) ≥ 0.8 at
   a true ratio of 15; P(`withdrawn`) ≤ 0.05 at a true ratio of 5; the guard is reachable at its adjusted
   level; and the horizon cap H ÷ RMT_A on the development suite is at least 10. A cell failing any condition
   opens as `descriptive` and says so before any sealed run (KILL-TENFOLD-REACH, N39b-25).
6. **Rejecting an unreachable rule.** `eval.preanalyze` on the ten-fold study plan (N39b-34) enumerates the
   reachable verdicts; a rule that names a verdict it cannot return at the planned n is rejected (the refusal
   for a fixed-n study that names an unreachable verdict, FROB-REF-532), and a population result below the
   necessary family count is refused.
7. **The monitor at the tournament.** One anytime-valid monitor per cell reports; its futility stop is disabled:
   every cell runs to n, and no monitor output enters the fixed-n verdict, which is computed once.
8. **Written kill rules.** The thresholds 3 and 10, the guard floor 5, the tie factor 1.5, the margin 0.05 and
   the limit of four cells are fixed when the first suite hash is committed (N39b-33) and never changed after a
   sealed run. `withdrawn` fires KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED for the class; a tie with arm C within 1.5, or a recovery
   ratio of at least 0.8 on time and on compute, fires KILL-TENFOLD-TIE; a false certificate or a leak
   disqualifies (KILL-TENFOLD-CERT, -LEAK). BET-13 is met when at least one named stratum is `ten-fold`.
   A kill outcome is not re-tested on the same suite. No mechanism is killed at M7; the rules decide wording
   and what ships.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The short plan exists and states parts 1 to 8 with the cells, their result types, m, c, H and levels.
- [ ] The pilot N39b-16 has run and its result is recorded (seed done-condition).
- [ ] The reachable verdicts of every cell are enumerated and recorded before sampling; a planted rule with an
      unreachable verdict (a four-family confirmatory cell) is rejected (seed done-condition).
- [ ] At most four confirmatory cells are named; each meets the four reachability conditions or is opened as
      `descriptive` with that statement published before any sealed run.
- [ ] The parameter block's digest equals the one ratified before the first suite hash.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

Carried by the statistics and pre-analysis beads. The epic adds `epic_bd429_preanalysis_precedes_open`: a query
over the gate's event log that the pre-analysis entry precedes the suite-open entry and that the named cells
match the report's confirmatory cells.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_decision_rule_pilot.sh` (owned by N39b-16) runs the pre-analysis on development-suite times
and logs, per cell, each reachable verdict, each power figure against its threshold, and each refusal.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the critical counts above; P(X ≥ 17) = 0.032 and P(X ≥ 16) = 0.076 at m = 24; the family counts
32, 46 and 49; the illustrative power row for m = 24 and log-ratio spread 0.5 (0.50 at a true ratio of 10, 0.94
at 12, 0.8 reached at 11.25) as a planning value to be replaced. Negative controls: the draft per-brief rule
(must show at most 18% power at a true ratio of 10); a four-family confirmatory cell (refused); a fixed-n verdict
requested at a monitor's stopping time (refused); a percentile bootstrap offered as a population certificate on
the rare-tail population (must be rejected by the registered contrast test).

## Considerations

- Risk (register): the tournament cannot confirm a true ratio of ten for lack of power, or because unfinished
  missions cap the ratio at 1/q. Countermeasure: this pre-analysis, and completion treated as a first-order goal.
- Between true ratios of 7 and 10 the word can be awarded by chance (up to a fifth of gates at 8.5 in the
  illustrative table); this is why the interval is printed beside the word. Six families give a rule no better
  than the rejected draft.
- Compute: 24 families per cell by default, at least 32 for a population cell, on both machines, needs an
  approved campaign class per run and a pretraining class where an arm builds a standing asset.
- HYPOTHESIS: the per-family log-ratio spread lies between 0.5 and 0.8 on the design strata; the binding
  pre-analysis replaces it with the measured spread.
