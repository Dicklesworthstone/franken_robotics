## Background

In the ten-fold test correctness is a gate and not a term in a score. If wrong answers merely cost points, an
arm could trade correctness for speed and the time ratio would reward it. So certain events remove an arm from a
claim altogether, and they apply to every arm alike: this system (arm A), the composed rival (arm B) and the
ablation arm (arm C). This bead is the mechanism that turns such an event into a recorded disqualification with
a computed scope, and the detectors for the three grounds that are matters of record integrity. Leak detection
has its own bead (N39b-02); the decisions that follow a false certificate or a leak are N39b-03 and N39b-04.

Terms. *Cell*: a stratum (a set of mission families with one deadline H) and a regime (cold or warm). *Frozen
arm*: an arm whose freeze manifest was published before any paired run; nothing in it may change afterwards.
*Adjudicating simulator*: the neutral simulator in which acceptance batteries run; no arm may use it for
training, tuning or screening on that stratum. *Receipt*: the adjudicator's record of a submission's decision.
*Proof kind*: the registered type of a certificate (for example a linear-program dual witness).

Design decisions. A disqualification never awards anything to the other arm (reason: a ratio needs both a
numerator and a denominator measured under the protocol; a forfeit is not a measurement). A breach of a resource
cap is not a disqualification (reason: running out of budget is slowness, which the clock already prices): the
run is censored at the instant of the breach and costs H. A leak is never repaired by excluding the affected
runs and keeping the rest of the cell (reason: the selection of what to exclude would itself be informed).

## Scope

In: the ground taxonomy; scope computation; the event record; the effect on verdicts; detectors for a changed
frozen arm, for use of the adjudicating simulator in development or tuning, and for edited ledgers or receipts;
cap-breach censoring. Out: canaries and leak detection (N39b-02); the adjudicator's direct evaluation that finds
a false certificate (the adjudication beads); the arm-A procedure after a false certificate, which this engine
invokes with the disqualification record (N25b-03: report, disablement of the proof kind and checker version,
withdrawal sweep, downgrade to `Tested`, permanent fixture), and the decision whether the rule or its
implementation was at fault (N25b-04, KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/dq.rs` (DESIGN). Rust sketch:

```rust
pub enum Ground { FalseCertificate { proof_kind: String }, Leak, ArmChangedAfterFreeze,
                  AdjudicatorUsedInDevelopment, RecordEdited }
pub struct Disqualification { pub gate: String, pub arm: Arm, pub ground: Ground,
    pub cells: Vec<CellId>, pub families_retired: Vec<FamilyId>, pub gate_void: bool,
    pub evidence: Vec<Hash>, pub decided_by: SessionId, pub at: Timestamp }
```

**Grounds, scope and effect.**

| Ground | Definition | Scope | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| One false certificate | A certificate the arm submitted, that its declared checker accepted, and that the adjudicator's direct evaluation or any later audit shows false within its stated scope | Every cell of the gate in which that arm used that proof kind | Those cells have no ratio for that arm; the cell's verdict is `disqualified`. For arm A also: the proof kind is suspended, its cells fall to evidence class `Tested`, and the decision KILL-AUGUR-FALSECERT determines whether the rule or its implementation was at fault |
| Leak from sealed confirmation worlds | Any read, by an arm's agent, tools or developers, of sealed content before the gate closes: a mission card before its clock starts, a sealed world address, a per-world outcome, a probe point, another arm's transcript | The cells touched | Void for that arm; the families are retired at once; if the extent cannot be bounded the whole gate is void and is re-run on a rotated suite |
| Change to a frozen arm | Any difference between the arm as run and its published freeze manifest | The arm's cells run after the change; all its cells if the time of change is unknown | Void for that arm; re-run at the next gate |
| Adjudicating simulator in development or tuning | The arm's logs, environments or tag maps show that simulator before the gate closed | The stratum | The arm is disqualified on the stratum |
| Edited ledger or receipt | A record whose bytes no longer match the hash entered in the event log at creation | The arm's cells that depend on the record; all cells after a broken chain link | Disqualified in those cells |

Not false certificates: a truthful `unknown`, a refused certificate, a certificate with a narrower scope than
was asked. They cost coverage and time.

**Scope computation.**
1. False certificate: from the submission index, C = {cells where arm X has any submission citing proof kind k};
   record ground, C, the certificate hash and the contradicting evaluation.
2. Leak: map each leaked object (card, world address, outcome, probe point, transcript) to its family and cell.
   If any evidence item does not resolve to an enumerable object set (a directory-level read, an exposed key,
   an unlogged interval), set `gate_void`.
3. Changed arm, edited record: as in the table, from the first failing check.

**Effect on scoring.** Arm A disqualified in a cell: verdict `disqualified` (the first row of the verdict rule).
Arm B disqualified: the cell has no denominator; the verdict rule (N39a-32) returns `no-denominator`, which is
not a verdict and awards nothing to anyone; the cell is reported as such and re-run at the next gate. Arm C disqualified: the tie test of that stratum is recorded `not available`
and the engine-necessity bet is neither met nor failed there (DESIGN, by the same reasoning as arm B).

**Detectors.**
- *Changed arm.* Before every campaign start and again at close, recompute the content address of the manifest
  closure (engine, learner, outer-loop script, tool layer, agent guide, agent model revision and caps, cache
  list, standing-asset hashes, phase-tag map, hardware fingerprint) and compare with the published
  `manifest_hash`. A mismatch refuses the run (`tenfold.frozen-arm-changed`, exit 9; `arm-changed` is its
  alias and is never emitted) and records the ground.
- *Adjudicator in development.* The adjudicating simulator's identity (name, revision, binary and container
  digests) is searched for in every arm's tuning log, process tag map, environment lock and container digest
  list, and, for arm A, in the project's own run ledger for the stratum's development period.
- *Edited records.* Each ledger record and receipt is entered into the event log by hash when created; the log
  is a hash chain (each entry carries the hash of its predecessor). At close every record is re-hashed.
- *Cap breach.* Watchers on the caps of the mission card (tokens, tool calls, device-seconds, cores, memory) end
  the run at the breach; the ledger gets `censored: true`, `cap_breach: <which>`, and the run contributes H.

Outputs are records in the gate directory, evidence class `Tested`; nothing here is a product verb.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each ground produces a record with the scope of the table; the scope tests below pass.
- [ ] A disqualification changes no other arm's numbers and awards nothing.
- [ ] A cell whose rival is disqualified is reported "no denominator" and scheduled for the next gate.
- [ ] The three integrity detectors each catch their planted defect; a cap breach censors and does not disqualify.
- [ ] Planted negative controls caught: a false certificate (arm disqualified in exactly the cells using that
      proof kind); an arm that touched the adjudicating simulator during tuning; a manifest changed after freeze.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::dq`: `false_cert_scope_is_cells_using_proof_kind` (arm A used
`lp-farkas` in `envelope` warm and `sensing` cold, not in `legged-quad`: two cells disqualified, one untouched);
`narrow_scope_is_not_false`; `truthful_unknown_is_not_false`; `leak_unbounded_voids_gate`;
`rival_dq_gives_no_denominator`; `dq_awards_nothing` (arm A's sums unchanged when arm B is disqualified);
`manifest_drift_detected` (one byte of the agent guide changed); `hash_chain_break_scopes_forward`;
`cap_breach_censors_at_instant` (breach at 5,000 s with H = 14,400 s contributes 14,400 s, flagged).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_disqualification.sh`, owned by this bead and built only on its dependencies (header
`bead=N39b-01 milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e gates=GATE-TENFOLD-03,KILL-TENFOLD-CERT,KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK
seed=390101 needs=cpu plants=false-certificate,unbounded-leak,arm-changed,adjudicator-in-tuning,record-edited,cap-breach`),
on a synthetic gate directory of three cells (`envelope` warm, `sensing` cold, `legged-quad` cold) with two
synthetic arms, labelled `synthetic` and never claimable: (1) `FROB_TEST_SEED=390101 cargo test -p
frob-conformance tenfold::dq`; (2) plants each ground in turn through `e2e_plant` and asserts its record: a
false certificate of `lp-farkas` used in two of the three cells (exactly those two disqualified, the third
untouched); an unbounded leak (the gate void); one byte of the agent guide changed after the freeze (the next
campaign start refused with `tenfold.frozen-arm-changed`, exit 9); the adjudicating simulator's digest in an arm's
tuning log (`tenfold.adjudicator-in-tuning`, that stratum); a ledger record edited after entry (the close re-hash
refuses with `tenfold.record-hash-mismatch`, exit 9, scoped forward from the break); (3) a cap breach at 5,000 s
with H = 14,400 s, asserted censored at H with `cap_breach` set and no disqualification; (4) for a disqualified
rival, the cell reported "no denominator" and arm A's sums unchanged. Logged as NDJSON through the STD-E2E helper
(`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, seed): the event-log digest
before and after each plant, each record's ground, scope and cells, each refusal slug with its exit code, each
plant as a `negative-control` assertion with `caught`; on failure the first differing record and the gate
directory's path; a final `summary` with assertion counts and controls caught. The partial-gate script
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh` (N39b-12, which depends on this bead) runs this script as a stage and then
plants the grounds again in its rehearsal.

### Known answers and negative controls

Controls TF-CX-11, -15 and -24 of the harness registry. Metamorphic relation: permuting the order in which
grounds are recorded does not change the final set of disqualified cells. Determinism: scope computation is a
pure function of the event log (twice on one machine; debug and release).

## Considerations

- The builders wrote the certificate checker, so its acceptance is not the last word; the false-certificate
  ground depends on the adjudicator's independent evaluation.
- A defect found in a frozen arm after the suite opens voids the affected cells; it is never patched mid-gate.
- Detecting the adjudicating simulator by identity can miss a renamed build; the adjudicator may add a behavioural
  probe (a known answer only that simulator reproduces to its last digit). Recorded as a known gap.
- Risk: certificates issued for the wrong statement; the scope of a certificate is read from its embedded
  statement, never from the submission's description.
