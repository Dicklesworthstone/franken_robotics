## Background

KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK is a standing kill gate of the ten-fold test, in force from the partial gate at M3. Trigger:
any leak from sealed confirmation worlds, meaning any read, by an arm's agent, tools or developers, of sealed
content before the gate closes (a mission card before its clock starts, a sealed world address, a per-world
outcome, a probe point, another arm's transcript). Fallback, named in advance: the affected cells are void for
that arm and their families are retired; if the extent cannot be bounded the whole gate is void and is re-run on
a rotated suite. A kill gate's decision is recorded as an ADR in the milestone in which it occurs.

Why so strict. The test's claim is about missions an arm has not seen. After a leak nobody can show which of the
arm's choices were informed by it. Excluding "the affected runs" and keeping the rest would require deciding
which runs were affected with knowledge of their times, which is a selection on outcome. So a leak is never
repaired by exclusion inside a cell.

## Scope

In: the decision procedure, the bounded-or-unbounded determination, the record, the re-run rule, and what
changes downstream. Out: detection (N39b-02); the disqualification event and cell effects (N39b-01); authoring a
rotated suite (the suite beads).

## Technical approach

The procedure is the module `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/killleak.rs` behind two subcommands of the
operator binary (DESIGN), adjudicator role only: `frob-tenfold kill-leak decide --gate <id> [--rulings <file>]
[--json]` (steps 1 to 7: it reads the evidence file `leak_evidence.json` that the audit of N39b-02 wrote and
the adjudicator's rulings on alarms; exit 0 when a decision is recorded or nothing was found, 2 while an alarm
has no ruling and its cells stay `under audit`, 3 with `role-not-permitted`) and `frob-tenfold kill-leak record
--gate <id> --exit <Mn> [--json]` (steps 8 and 9).

1. **Evidence file.** From the detectors: canary hits (arm, transcript location, object), access-log entries
   (session, role, object, time), firewall attempts, and the access log's continuity result.
2. **Confirmation.** A string-canary hit or a logged read by a non-adjudicator session is confirmed evidence. A
   numeric-canary hit alone, or a refused read attempt, is an alarm: the adjudicator examines it and records
   either confirmation with the supporting log entry or dismissal with the reason.
3. **Extent.** Bounded if every confirmed item resolves to an enumerable set of sealed objects and the access log
   is continuous for the whole gate; unbounded otherwise.
4. **Decision, bounded.** For the arm concerned: each cell containing a touched family is void (no ratio, no
   verdict for that cell; it is reported as void with the event). The touched families are retired at once for
   every arm, since they can no longer be sealed, and are replaced only at the next gate. Other cells stand.
   If the voided cell was confirmatory it is not replaced within the gate, and the multiplicity adjustment of
   the remaining confirmatory cells is unchanged (DESIGN: the adjusted levels were fixed before sealing and are
   not relaxed after an event).
5. **Decision, unbounded.** The gate is void: no ratio of the gate is published as a result; the record states
   the event; the gate is re-run on a rotated suite of families that no earlier gate used, with the same frozen
   parameter block. At the tournament this is one of the three breaches that void and re-run it.
6. **Which arm.** The rule applies to every arm alike. A leak to arm B voids arm B's cells: those cells then
   have no denominator and are re-run at the next gate. It awards nothing to arm A.
7. **Source closure.** Before any re-run the path of the leak is identified and closed (a mis-scoped log, a
   shared cache, a credential), and the closure is tested by re-running the positive controls of N39b-02.
8. **ADR content.** Evidence, confirmation notes, bounded or unbounded with the argument, the cells voided, the
   families retired, the source and its closure, the re-run plan, and the downstream list: report wording
   ("void: leak", never a number), the schedule effect on the milestone exit (M3 does not exit without the
   partial-gate record; a void gate is re-run before exit), and the cost of the re-run in device-hours.

9. **Record, in the one form of kill-gate decisions (N40-03).** The row KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK (`mode = "standing"`,
   from M3, no `scope_kind`: decided whole, N40-28) carries one decision entry per reading, `{exit, outcome,
   adr, date, session, receipts}`: `fired` when at least one evidence item of the reading was confirmed (bounded
   or unbounded; which cells are void and which families retired is the ADR's content, not register state),
   `not-fired` when the audits found nothing or every alarm was dismissed with its reason. The ADR of a reading
   is `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK-<milestone>.md` with the content of step 8, or the statement that the
   gate's leak audit found no evidence. `kill-leak record` writes the ADR draft and the decision-entry fragment
   into the gate directory (`tenfold/<gate>/kill/KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK/`) when the gate's leak audit closes. The
   session that enters the gate's row (N40-15 for the partial gate, N40-16 for the tournament) commits the ADR
   and enters the entry, citing in `receipts` the receipt of that row's part under `receipts/gates/` (a
   `not-fired` entry needs one); a void gate has no such part, and its `fired` entry cites the receipt of the
   pre-opening record of GATE-TENFOLD-04, whose sealing and access record this gate reads (N40-28). The row is
   standing, so an entry is due at every later exit as well, with its own ADR: at an exit in whose milestone no
   gate of the test ran it is `not-fired`, cites the latest gate's receipt and states that no evidence was
   filed since. Evidence confirmed inside a milestone whose exit is already decided adds an entry with `reading
   = "reopened"` and the ADR suffix `-reopened` (N40-03, step 6). This bead also writes the gate's owner
   declaration, which N40-30 compares with the ledger, in the `contract.toml` of `frob-conformance`: `[[gate]]`
   with `role = "owner"` and the ledger's current `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied,
   not restated; the `decision` block is added with each entry. Without the declaration `cargo test -p
   frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` fails `owner-declaration-missing` from the M3 exit on.

Fail-closed posture while an alarm is unexamined: the cell prints `under audit`; no verdict is issued for it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the synthetic gate of this bead's script, a planted canary in one arm's transcript voids exactly the
      cells containing the touched family for that arm, retires the family, and leaves the other arm's sums
      unchanged.
- [ ] A planted access-log gap yields `unbounded` and a void gate with a re-run plan on a rotated suite.
- [ ] A numeric-canary alarm with no supporting log entry is dismissible only with a recorded reason.
- [ ] No output of the harness prints a ratio for a void cell.
- [ ] `kill-leak record` writes `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK-<milestone>.md` as a draft and a decision-entry
      fragment that loads on a scratch copy of the register: `fired` for the planted leak, `not-fired` with a
      receipt for a clean audit; a `not-fired` fragment with no receipt is refused.
- [ ] The owner declaration of KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK is in the `contract.toml` of `frob-conformance` with the
      ledger's text, and `cargo test -p frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` finds exactly one.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::killleak`: `bounded_leak_voids_only_touched_cells`;
`unbounded_leak_voids_gate`; `void_cell_has_no_ratio_in_report`; `holm_levels_unchanged_after_void`;
`leak_to_rival_gives_no_denominator`; `exclusion_inside_cell_is_refused` (a request to drop only the affected
pairs and score the remainder is refused with `tenfold.exclusion-not-harness-fault`, exit 3: an exclusion is only
ever a harness fault recorded before times are compared); `numeric_alarm_needs_ruling` (a numeric-canary hit
with no ruling leaves the cell `under audit` and writes no entry; dismissed with a reason it is `not-fired`);
`reading_outcome` (`fired` for a bounded and for an unbounded confirmed leak, `not-fired` for none).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_kill_leak.sh`, owned by this bead and built only on its dependencies (header `bead=N39b-04
milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e,kill-gate gates=KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK seed=390404 needs=cpu
plants=exclude-and-keep,undismissed-alarm,gap-called-bounded`), on the synthetic gate directories of N39b-02's
script (the clean one, the planted input `canary_in_transcript` of TF-CX-12 and the one with a heartbeat gap):
(1) `FROB_TEST_SEED=390404 cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::killleak`; (2) `frob-tenfold leak audit`
(N39b-02) then `frob-tenfold kill-leak decide --gate <id> --json` on each: nothing found; a bounded leak (the
cell void for that arm only, the family retired, the other arm's sums unchanged, the Holm levels of the other
confirmatory cells unchanged); an unbounded leak (the gate void, a re-run plan on a rotated suite); (3)
`frob-tenfold kill-leak record --gate <id> --exit M3 --json` on each, the fragment appended to a scratch copy of
the register under `work/` and loaded with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example validate_register -- --file
work/gates.toml` (N40-01); (4) the plants: a build under `--features gauntlet-mutations` that excludes the
affected pairs and scores the rest of the cell (caught by `tenfold.exclusion-not-harness-fault`, exit 3), a
numeric-canary alarm with no ruling offered as decided, and an access-log gap decided `bounded`. Logged as NDJSON
through the STD-E2E helper (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time,
seed): evidence counts, the extent decision, cells before and after, the retired-family list, the digests of
the ADR draft and of the fragment, each refusal with its reason and exit code, each plant as a
`negative-control` assertion with `caught`; on failure the first differing value and the gate directory's path;
a final `summary` with `gates: [{id: "KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK", measured_ok, controls_caught}]` and `gates_claimable:
false`. The partial-gate script `scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh` (N39b-12, which depends on this bead) runs
this script as a stage and repeats both variants on its rehearsal gate (its sub-step `leak-drill`).

### Known answers and negative controls

Control TF-CX-12. A mutant decision that excludes the affected pairs and reports the rest of the cell must be
caught by `exclusion_inside_cell_is_refused`.

## Considerations

- Cost of an unbounded leak at M3: a full re-run, up to 288 device-hours per cell in the worst case, and fresh
  sealed families; this is why access logs must be continuous and why the positive controls run before opening.
- A leak by a person through an unlogged channel is outside detection; role disjointness and the published
  protocol are the only defences, and the report says so.
- Retired families improve the development pool; nothing is lost but the sealed status.
