## Background

The ten-fold test can end in several ways, and each has consequences for what the program says and builds. If
those consequences were chosen after the numbers were known, the choice would be shaped by the numbers. The
outcome rules are therefore written before any measurement and applied mechanically. This bead implements them:
a pure function from a cell's scored result to the sentence the report prints, the actions the program takes,
and a draft of the architecture decision record (ADR) that records the outcome in the milestone in which it
occurs. The partial gate at M3 "passes" exactly when this function has been applied to every cell in scope.

Terms. *Cell*: a stratum and a regime (cold, or warm at N amendments). *R̂*: the pooled ratio of restricted times,
rival over ours. *[L, U]*: its one-sided 95% bounds. *Guard*: the exact test that the median per-family ratio
exceeds 5. *Arm C*: the ablation arm, a short program over the rival's engine. *RMT*: restricted mean time.
*Recovery ratios* r_T, r_C: the share of our saving over the declared incumbent that the composed incumbent
recovers, on elapsed time and on compute. *BET-13*: ten-fold on at least one named stratum. *BET-14*: the
mechanisms need this engine.

## Scope

In: the frozen parameter block and its binding; the outcome table; sentence templates; action records; ADR
drafts; the across-strata rules; the positioning text for a withdrawn class; the no-retest rule. Out: computing
verdicts (statistics beads); the tie test and recovery ratios (N39b-43); executing re-plans and shipping
decisions (the kill-gate decision beads N39b-06, N39b-07); the report layout (N39b-14).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/outcome.rs` (DESIGN).

**Parameter block (DESIGN of the record; the values are the plan's; the file is written and ratified by
OPEN-TENFOLD-8, N39b-33, and loaded here).** `withdraw_below = 3`, `word_at = 10`,
`guard_floor = 5`, `tie_factor = 3/2`, `noninferiority_margin = 1/20`, `max_confirmatory_cells = 4`,
`recovery_layer_enough = 4/5`, `recovery_engine_source = 1/2`, `warm_reuse_count = 10`, `alpha = 1/20`, stored
as exact rationals in canonical JSON. Its digest is entered in the event log in the same entry that commits the
first suite hash. After that entry any change is refused (`tenfold.parameters-frozen`); none is changed after
any sealed run. The threshold 3 is a convention at the bottom of the range expected for quadruped briefs, so
withdrawal at the partial gate is a live outcome; the guard floor 5 keeps the property that a true ratio of 5 is
called ten-fold with probability near zero.

**The table.** For a confirmatory cell, evaluated in this order; several rows can apply (a verdict row and a
three-arm row), and each that applies emits its sentence and actions.

| Outcome | Condition | Sentence (placeholders in ⟨⟩) | Program action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `withdrawn` | R̂ < 3, or a futility stop at the partial gate | "Measured ⟨R̂⟩ with [⟨L⟩, ⟨U⟩] on ⟨stratum, regime⟩. The speed claim for this class is withdrawn." | KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED: the class leaves the speed claim in the thesis, the README and every report; work packages for the class whose only justification is speed are re-planned before the next milestone starts; the class may be measured again at M7 on fresh sealed families |
| `measured` | 3 ≤ R̂ < 10, or R̂ ≥ 10 without every condition of `ten-fold` | "⟨R̂⟩ times shorter, and at least ⟨L⟩ with 95% confidence, on ⟨stratum, regime⟩." The word "ten-fold" is not used for the class anywhere | The ledger names the binding ceiling; lever work for the class continues only on phases whose removable share is at least 10% |
| `ten-fold` | The verdict rule's row | "Ten-fold on ⟨stratum, regime⟩: measured ⟨R̂⟩ with [⟨L⟩, ⟨U⟩]", with S and N* for a warm cell; on fewer than 32 families "ten-fold on the sealed suite" | BET-13 is met for that stratum and for no other |
| Tie with the ablation arm | Arm C reaches the same accepted scope and RMT_C ÷ RMT_A ≤ 1.5; or r_T ≥ 0.8 and r_C ≥ 0.8 | "A short program over ⟨engine⟩ reached the same accepted answers within a factor of 1.5", or "a layer over ⟨engine⟩ recovers ⟨r_T⟩ of the saving in time and ⟨r_C⟩ in compute." | KILL-TENFOLD-TIE: say so; ship the layer over that engine for that stratum behind the same verbs, schemas and checker; keep engine work only for gaps internal to an engine (documented: a mixed-topology batch with run-to-run replay; the Metal tier; unverified and checked before being relied on: replay of one world outside its batch; solver-internal certificates); spend research budget on the first mission the program cannot resolve. The ratio against arm B is then the layer's ratio, and no claim is made that the mechanisms need this engine |
| Engine advantage not established | RMT_C ÷ RMT_A > 1.5 with its lower bound ≤ 1.5 | "The advantage of the engine over a layer was not established." | The layer is offered beside the engine for that stratum; BET-14 is neither met nor failed there and is re-tested at the next gate |
| The rival is faster | R̂ < 1 | The measured ratio | As `withdrawn`; where licences allow, the rival's method is adopted behind the verbs |
| `descriptive` | The verdict rule's row | The numbers, with the reason no verdict was possible | Nothing is cited as passing or failing BET-13; the cell is enlarged for the next gate |
| `disqualified` | The verdict rule's row | The event, its scope and its evidence | The disqualification rules |

**Across strata.** Each stratum is its own result; a result on one stratum earns that stratum alone. BET-13 is
met when at least one named stratum has the verdict `ten-fold`. A portfolio ratio with equal weights over the
strata run is printed and labelled `descriptive`; DESIGN: R_port = Σ_s RMT_B,s ÷ Σ_s RMT_A,s over strata s, each
stratum entering by its restricted mean time per pair so that its pair count does not weight it. Whatever the
verdicts, the action "replace the table of expected ranges by mission class with the measured table in the next
version of the thesis" is emitted.

**What remains if the speed claim is withdrawn.** For a `withdrawn` class the engine emits the positioning text:
the product is described by its measured ratio and by the claims that never depended on speed: any world can be
re-run alone on the CPU from its address; comparisons carry error control that survives an agent's retries;
family answers are checked by an auditor that shares no code with their producer; failures arrive as minimal
replayable worlds with an interventional explanation; every result names its claim, its class and its evidence;
and the energy ledger closes. The comparison with incumbents is stated as parity of speed with a different kind
of evidence. If every class at the partial gate is under 3, the action list adds: speed work is no longer
funded, and the family-policy spend of M4 is decided again.

**No re-test.** A kill outcome is not re-tested on the same suite: a second scoring request for a cell with a
recorded `withdrawn`, tie or `disqualified` outcome on the same suite hash is refused
(`tenfold.retest-on-same-suite`). A class withdrawn at M3 stays withdrawn until M7 measures it on fresh families.

**Command (DESIGN).** `frob-tenfold outcomes apply --gate <id> [--json]`: reads the scorer's output of the gate
directory (`score.json`, N39b-19) and the parameter block bound in the event log, calls `apply` on every
confirmatory cell and writes `outcomes.json` and the ADR drafts beside it; exit 0, or 3 with the refusal
(`verdict-input-missing` for a cell without its verdict record; `tenfold.parameters-frozen`;
`tenfold.retest-on-same-suite`). It is what the closing command's stage `outcomes` runs (N39bp4-01).

**API.** `apply(cell: &CellResult, three: Option<&ThreeArm>, params: &Params) -> Vec<Outcome>`; each `Outcome`
has `row`, `sentence`, `actions: Vec<Action>` (typed: `WithdrawSpeedClaim`, `ReplanSpeedOnlyPackages`,
`ShipLayer{engine}`, `KeepEngineWorkFor{gaps}`, `OfferLayerBeside`, `EnlargeCell`, `AdoptRivalMethod`,
`ReplaceExpectedRanges`, `MarkBetMet{bet, stratum}`) and `adr: AdrDraft {id, milestone, context, decision,
consequences, evidence_hashes}`. Output bytes are deterministic (class D0).

**One definition of the table.** The table above is this module's data, exported as `outcome_table() ->
&'static [OutcomeRow]` with its digest. Every other reader of the outcome rules uses it and does not restate it:
the gate record (BD-317) applies it; the program-gate check `tenfold06` (N40-15) verifies that each recorded
cell carries the row this function assigns to its recorded numbers by calling `apply` on the record, and checks
the table digest equal to the one bound with the parameter block; the kill-gate decisions (N39b-06, N39b-07) and
the wording lint (N39b-15) read the sentences from it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The parameter digest is bound to the first suite hash and a later change is refused.
- [ ] Every row of the table is produced by a test input, with the exact sentence text.
- [ ] `ten-fold` is emitted only when the verdict is `ten-fold`; a tie row suppresses nothing silently: the
      report carries both the measured sentence and the tie sentence.
- [ ] An ADR draft exists for every outcome of every confirmatory cell, dated in the gate's milestone.
- [ ] A re-test on the same suite is refused; the portfolio ratio is labelled `descriptive`.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::outcome`: `withdrawn_at_2_647` (the six-family proportions in a
registered cell: sentence contains "2.65" and "withdrawn"; action `WithdrawSpeedClaim`); `measured_at_6`
(R̂ = 6.0, L = 4.2: sentence "6.0 times shorter, and at least 4.2 with 95% confidence"; no "ten-fold" substring);
`ten_or_more_without_guard_is_measured` (R̂ = 12 with 16 of 24: `measured`); `ten_fold_with_guard` (R̂ = 12, 18 of
24 under four cells, non-inferior, no tie); `finite_suite_wording_below_32`; `tie_within_1_5` (RMT_C ÷ RMT_A =
1.4, same scope); `tie_by_recovery` (r_T = 0.85, r_C = 0.81); `engine_advantage_not_established` (1.8 with lower
bound 1.3); `rival_faster` (R̂ = 0.8); `portfolio_is_ratio_of_sums_of_rmt`; `params_frozen_after_hash`;
`amendment_between_gates_accepted` (a dated amendment block of OPEN-TENFOLD-8, N39b-33, loaded after the partial
gate closes and before the tournament's suite hash governs the tournament; the partial gate's record keeps its
own block's digest);
`retest_refused`; `all_under_three_reopens_family_policy_spend`; `outcome_table_digest_bound` (the exported
table's digest is in the parameter-block entry; one edited sentence changes it and is refused after the hash).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_outcomes.sh`, owned by this bead and built only on its dependencies (header `bead=N39b-05
milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e gates=GATE-TENFOLD-06,KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED,KILL-TENFOLD-TIE seed=390505 needs=cpu
plants=word-for-measured,block-changed-after-hash,retest-same-suite`), on scored synthetic gate directories
written by this bead (labelled `synthetic`; one per row of the table: the six-family proportions in a
24-family cell; R̂ = 6 with L = 4.2; R̂ = 12 with 16 of 24; R̂ = 12 with 18 of 24 under four cells; a tie within
1.5; a tie by recovery at 0.85 and 0.81; 1.8 with lower bound 1.3; R̂ = 0.8; a descriptive and a disqualified
cell; the warm card): (1) `FROB_TEST_SEED=390505 cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::outcome`; (2)
`frob-tenfold outcomes apply --gate <id> --json` on each, asserting the rows, the sentences byte for byte
against golden files under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/tenfold/outcomes/`, the typed actions, one ADR
draft per outcome dated in the gate's milestone, and the warm sentence with S = 14,400 s and N* = 5; (3) the
same command twice, asserting equal bytes; (4) the plants: under `--features gauntlet-mutations` a build that
prints "ten-fold" for `measured` with R̂ ≥ 10 (caught by the golden sentence), a parameter block changed after
the entry that commits the first suite hash (`tenfold.parameters-frozen`, exit 3), and a second scoring request
for the withdrawn cell on the same suite hash (`tenfold.retest-on-same-suite`, exit 3). Logged as NDJSON
through the STD-E2E helper (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time,
seed): per cell the verdict, the rows, R̂ as a `metric` against 3 and 10, the tie statistic against 1.5, r_T
and r_C against 0.8, the action list, the digests of `outcomes.json`, of each ADR draft and of the exported
table, each refusal with its reason and exit code, each plant as a `negative-control` assertion with `caught`;
on failure the first differing sentence and the paths of the gate directory and the golden file; a final
`summary` with assertion counts and `gates_claimable: false`. The partial-gate script
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh` (N39b-12, which depends on this bead) runs this script as a stage; its
step `outcomes` then applies the engine to the rehearsal's cells.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: thresholds 3, 10, 5, 1.5, 0.05; the warm sentence carries S = 14,400 s and N* = 5 on the warm
card. Negative controls: a mutant that prints "ten-fold" for `measured` with R̂ ≥ 10 must be caught by
`ten_or_more_without_guard_is_measured`; a mutant that lets the block change after the hash must be caught.

## Considerations

- The sentences are the only wording a report may use for a cell's result; the wording lint (N39b-15) checks
  documents against them.
- Ratification of the parameter values is an owner decision due before the first suite hash (N39b-33).
- The portfolio formula is this bead's DESIGN choice where the source text gives none; it carries no verdict.
