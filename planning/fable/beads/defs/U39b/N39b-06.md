## Background

A kill gate names its trigger and its fallback in advance and is decided on measured values; the outcome, either
way, is recorded as an architecture decision record in the milestone named. KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED is the kill gate
of the program's headline bet (BET-13: on at least one named stratum the restricted mean time to an
independently accepted answer is ten times shorter than the strongest composed rival's). Its early falsifier is
deliberately far from ten: a measured ratio under 3 on a mission class at the partial gate.

Trigger: on a confirmatory cell, R̂ (the pooled ratio of restricted times, rival over ours) is below 3; or, at the
partial gate, the futility monitor stops the cell. The monitor is an anytime-valid test of "the median
per-family ratio is at least 3": with Y_i = 1 when family i's ratio is at least 3, E_n = Π(1 + ½·(1 − 2·Y_i)),
stopping at E_n ≥ 20; eight consecutive families below 3 stop a cell (1.5⁸ = 25.6), seven do not (17.1). Its stop
yields `withdrawn` on the monitor's own authority at level 0.05. At the tournament the stop is disabled and only
R̂ < 3 at n triggers.

Fallback, named in advance: the speed claim for that class is withdrawn from the thesis, the README and every
report; speed-only work packages for the class are re-planned; the product is described for that class by the
capabilities that do not depend on speed, and by its measured ratio. Decided: M3 for the classes run; M7.

Why 3. It is a convention placed at the bottom of the range the thesis expects for quadruped briefs (3 to 10
against per-design training), so withdrawal at M3 is a live outcome and not a remote one. Why act at M3: the
family-policy spend of M4 and the engine work after it are the largest costs justified by speed.

## Scope

In: reading the trigger on each confirmatory cell of the partial-gate record; the ADR; the downstream change
list and its execution order; the same reading at M7 (performed under this procedure by N39b-13). Out: scoring
(BD-317); sentence text (N39b-05); the tie rule (N39b-07).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** For each confirmatory cell of the gate record: R̂, [L, U], the verdict, whether the monitor
   stopped it and at which family, completion by H for each arm, the binding ceiling from the rival's ledger,
   and the phase ledgers.
2. **Reading.** Per cell: fired if verdict is `withdrawn` (which includes R̂ < 1, the rival being faster); not
   fired if `measured` or `ten-fold`; not decidable if `descriptive` or `disqualified` (recorded as such; the
   cell is enlarged or re-run at the next gate and the class keeps its prior status).
3. **Status.** For each class that fired the entry's outcome is `fired` (printed "killed: fallback adopted");
   for `measured` and `ten-fold` it is `not-fired`, the gate records the measured ratio and stays armed for M7;
   a cell that is not decidable is `deferred` to M7 with its reason (step 9).
4. **Fallback execution, per fired class, in order.**
   a. Wording: the class's speed claim is struck in the claims registry; the README status table and every
      report template print the measured sentence ("Measured R̂ with [L, U] on ⟨stratum, regime⟩. The speed
      claim for this class is withdrawn."). The wording lint then refuses the word for that class.
   b. Positioning text for the class: replay of any world alone on the CPU from its address; error control
      that survives retries; family answers checked by an independent auditor; failures as minimal replayable
      worlds with interventional explanation; claim, class and evidence on every result; a closing energy
      ledger; the incumbent comparison stated as parity of speed with a different kind of evidence.
   c. Re-plan: list every open work package and bead whose funding reason for that class is "speed lever"
      only; each is either re-justified in writing for trust, coverage or correctness (with no speed figure) or
      closed as not built. The re-plan is complete before the next milestone starts.
   d. The ledger attribution is recorded: which ceiling bound (completion 1/q, unremovable cost, the
      one-design-per-turn cap, a missing lever such as "train few" at M3) and which phases held the time.
5. **If every class run is under 3** (the M3 stop decision): speed work is no longer funded; the product is
   positioned on replay, qualification, certificates and the evidence ledger; the family-policy spend of M4 is
   put to the owner again before M4 starts.
6. **If the rival is faster (R̂ < 1).** As above, and where licences allow the rival's method is adopted behind
   the same verbs; the adoption is opened as new work with its own pilot.
7. **Persistence.** A class withdrawn at M3 stays withdrawn until M7 measures it on fresh sealed families. A kill
   outcome is not re-tested on the same suite.
8. **ADR content.** The cells, their numbers, the monitor trace if it stopped, the reading, the fallback steps
   taken with links to the changed registries, the re-plan list, and the bound that held.

9. **Record, in the one form of kill-gate decisions (N40-03).** The ADR is
   `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED-M3.md`, one section per confirmatory cell. The gate's row in
   `registries/gates.toml` (decided per cell: `scope_kind = "cell"`, `decided = "M3"`, `reread = ["M7"]`,
   N40-28) receives one decision entry per cell, `{exit = "M3", scope = "cell:<stratum>", outcome, adr, date,
   session, receipts}`: `fired` for `withdrawn`; `not-fired` for `measured` and `ten-fold`; `deferred` with
   `deferred_to = "M7"` and its reason for a cell that is `descriptive`, `disqualified`, void or not run. Every
   entry cites in `receipts` the receipt of the M3 part of GATE-TENFOLD-06 under `receipts/gates/`, which the
   program-gate bead N40-15 (a dependency) commits when it enters that row; a `not-fired` entry without it is
   refused by the register. The M7 entries, with `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED-M7.md`, are written by the
   tournament bead (N39b-13), which repeats this procedure on the sealed suite with the monitor's stop
   disabled; the M3 entries are kept, never edited. The recording session also writes the gate's owner
   declaration, which N40-30 compares with the ledger, in the `contract.toml` of `frob-conformance`: `[[gate]]`
   with `role = "owner"` and the ledger's current `trigger`, `fallback`, `decided`, `mode` and `reads` copied,
   not restated, and the `decision` block with each measured R̂ citing that receipt; without it `cargo test -p
   frob-conformance --test kill_ledger` fails `owner-declaration-missing` from the M3 exit on.

Fail-closed posture until the decision is recorded: no document uses the word "ten-fold" or a speed ratio for
any class of the gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every confirmatory cell of the partial gate has a reading (fired, not fired, not decidable) in
      `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED-M3.md`, and the gate's row holds one M3 decision entry per cell with
      `scope = "cell:<stratum>"`, the outcome of N40-03's list that matches the reading, and the receipt of
      GATE-TENFOLD-06's M3 part; the owner declaration is in the `contract.toml` of `frob-conformance`.
- [ ] For each fired class: the claims registry, README table and report templates are changed; the lint
      refuses the word for the class; the re-plan list is complete and every item is re-justified or closed.
- [ ] The all-classes-under-3 branch produces an owner decision request on the M4 family-policy spend.
- [ ] The decision is dated in M3 and recorded by a session that did not implement the scored mechanisms.
- [ ] The M7 reading uses the same procedure with the monitor's stop disabled.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The reading is a pure function that lives beside the outcome engine (N39b-05), so its tests exist before the gate
runs and are re-run here on the real record. `cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::killspeed`: `fires_below_three` (R̂ = 2.647); `does_not_fire_at_three`
(R̂ = 3.0 exactly is `measured`); `futility_stop_fires_at_partial_gate_only`; `descriptive_is_not_decidable`;
`all_under_three_emits_owner_request`; `rival_faster_adds_adoption_action`; `reading_maps_to_outcome`
(`withdrawn` gives `fired`, `measured` and `ten-fold` give `not-fired`, `descriptive` and `disqualified` give
`deferred` with `deferred_to = "M7"`).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_kill_speed.sh` (this bead; header `bead=N39b-06 milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e,kill-gate
gates=KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED seed=390606 plants=upper-bound-reader,retest-same-suite`). It cannot be a step of the
rehearsal script N39b-12, which comes before the gate record this bead depends on; it runs that script in
replay mode with `--keep-gate` and acts on the kept directory. Steps: `record` (the rehearsal cell with the
six-family proportions repeated to 24 families, R̂ = 45/17, outcome row `withdrawn`); `apply` (the reading
fires; the claims registry of a scratch project is changed; the re-plan list is produced and each item carries
re-justify or close; the ADR draft and one decision-entry fragment per cell are written, appended to a scratch
copy of the register under `work/` and loaded with `cargo run -p frob-conformance --example validate_register
-- --file work/gates.toml`, asserting `fired` with `scope = "cell:legged-quad"` and a `synthetic` receipt placed
under the scratch root's `receipts/gates/`, and that the same fragment with `scope = "backend:gpu-cuda"` is
refused `decision-scope-invalid`); `lint` (the wording lint run afterwards refuses a planted sentence "ten-fold on
legged-quad"); `all-under-three` (a second kept record with every class under 3 yields the owner request on the
M4 family-policy spend); `plants` (a mutant reading the threshold on U instead of R̂ survives a cell with
R̂ = 2.6 and U = 3.4 and must be caught; a second scoring request on the same suite hash is refused with
`tenfold.retest-on-same-suite`). Logged as NDJSON through the shared helper (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`, exact command, exit code, wall time): R̂ as an exact rational and as a `metric` against 3; the
monitor's E_n trace against 20; each claims-registry change with digests before and after; each re-plan item;
the ADR draft digest; on failure the first differing value and the paths of the gate directory and golden file;
the `summary` with `gates: [{id: "KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED", measured_ok, controls_caught}]` and `gates_claimable:
false`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 1.5⁸ = 25.63 and 1.5⁷ = 17.09; R̂ = 2.647 on the card proportions. Negative control: a mutant that
reads the threshold on the upper bound U instead of R̂ (so that a cell with R̂ = 2.6 and U = 3.4 survives) must
be caught.

## Considerations

- Between 3 and 10 the gate does not fire; the class is reported by its ratio and the word is not used.
- The partial gate measures the product without the lever "train few"; a low M3 ratio on `legged-quad` is the
  expected reading (HYPOTHESIS: near the bottom of 3 to 10) and still triggers the rule if under 3.
- Risk: incumbents already train quadruped policies in minutes, so the class's ceiling may be under ten whatever
  is built; the denominator measured at M0 and M1 is the earliest signal.
- No milestone is blocked by this bet failing: M3 exits with the record and the withdrawal.
