## Background

Almost every mechanism of this system can in principle be hosted by an incumbent simulator with a layer on top.
The bet BET-14 says that is not enough: a short robust program over an incumbent engine cannot reach the same
accepted answers, with the same checking scope, in comparable time. KILL-TENFOLD-TIE is the kill gate that makes
the bet losable. Every plan-time pilot's strong baseline tied or won, so a tie is a live outcome.

Trigger (either prong, per stratum): (1) arm C (the ablation arm: at most 2,000 lines of program text over the
rival's engine, written by people who have read the design) reaches the same accepted scope with a restricted
mean time within a factor of 1.5 of arm A's, RMT_C ÷ RMT_A ≤ 1.5; or (2) the composed incumbent's recovery ratio
is at least 0.8 on elapsed time and on compute: r_T = (T_B − T_W)/(T_B − T_F) ≥ 0.8 and
r_C = (C_B − C_W)/(C_B − C_F) ≥ 0.8, where B is the declared incumbent, W the composed incumbent (the same engine
with every portable method layered on), F this system, T restricted mean time and C GPU-hours.

Fallback, named in advance: say so; ship the layer over that engine for that stratum behind the same verbs,
schemas and checker; keep engine work only for the gaps internal to an engine; spend the research budget on the
first mission the program cannot resolve. Decided at M3 and M7. A kill outcome is not re-tested on the same suite.

## Scope

In: the reading per stratum on the partial-gate record; the intermediate outcome "engine advantage not
established"; the ADR; the downstream change list, including the decision on arm D. Out: computing the tie test
and recovery ratios (N39b-43); the family-strata tie rule of the mission subsystem (N25a-18) and the decision on
specialized projections (N12-30), which read the same record.

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs per stratum.** RMT_A, RMT_B, RMT_C with the bootstrap lower bound of RMT_C ÷ RMT_A; the
   accepted-scope comparison of arms A and C (same missions accepted, same answer kinds, same mandatory
   obligations discharged); r_T and r_C with 95% intervals and their case (defined; "no saving to recover" when
   T_F ≥ T_B; a loss to the composed incumbent when T_W < T_F); the three phase ablations; the binding property
   recorded by the one-receipt pipeline for arm W.
2. **Reading.**
   - *Tie (fired):* same accepted scope and RMT_C ÷ RMT_A ≤ 1.5; or r_T ≥ 0.8 and r_C ≥ 0.8.
   - *Engine advantage not established:* RMT_C ÷ RMT_A > 1.5 with its lower bound ≤ 1.5. Not fired; the layer is
     offered beside the engine for that stratum; BET-14 is neither met nor failed there and is re-tested at the
     next gate.
   - *Engine is a source of the gain:* r_T < 0.5 and r_C < 0.5 and no tie. Not fired.
   - *Mixed:* anything else; reported with the phase the layer could not recover, named from the ledger.
3. **Fallback execution for a fired stratum.**
   a. The report says so, with the tie sentence; the ratio against arm B is then the layer's ratio and no claim
      is made that the mechanisms need this engine.
   b. The layer ships over that engine for that stratum: opened as new work (an adapter behind the verb
      registry that runs the stratum's verbs on the incumbent engine out of process, with the same schemas and
      the same offline checker), with its own plan, licences check and acceptance on the stratum's development
      missions.
   c. Engine work for the stratum is kept only for engine-internal gaps. Two are documented in incumbents' own
      documentation: a mixed-topology batch with run-to-run replay, and the Metal tier. Two are unverified and
      are checked on the installed incumbents before anything relies on them: replay of one world outside its
      batch, and solver-internal certificates. Each open engine bead for the stratum is re-read: kept if it
      serves one of the confirmed gaps, re-justified for another stratum, or closed.
   d. Research budget moves to the first mission the program cannot resolve.
4. **Arm D** (the product's own layer over an incumbent engine through the same verbs). Open question, owner:
   the lead of the agent surface; it blocks nothing; default: not built unless arm C ties on a stratum. If this
   gate fires on any stratum, the ADR opens arm D as work and it is run and reported at the next gate.
5. **Interaction with the word.** A tie removes the verdict `ten-fold` for the cell (the verdict rule requires
   that the ablation arm has not tied), whatever R̂ against arm B is.
6. **ADR content.** Per stratum: the numbers, the reading, the fallback steps, the engine beads re-read with
   their disposition, the result of checking the two unverified gaps, and the arm D decision.

Fail-closed posture until the decision is recorded: no document claims that the mechanisms need this engine.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every stratum with a ratio has one of the four readings in the ADR, with r_T, r_C and their intervals
      printed whatever their values.
- [ ] For a fired stratum: the tie sentence is in the report; the layer work is opened; each engine bead for the
      stratum has a recorded disposition; the two unverified gaps have a recorded check result.
- [ ] "Engine advantage not established" produces the offer of the layer and a re-test entry for the next gate.
- [ ] The arm D question is decided in the ADR on each firing.
- [ ] The decision is dated in M3; the M7 reading repeats the procedure on the sealed suite (N39b-13).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The reading is a pure function that lives beside the outcome engine (N39b-05), so its tests exist before the gate
runs and are re-run here on the real record. `cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::killtie`: `tie_at_factor_1_5_inclusive` (1.5 fires, 1.51 does not by
prong 1); `scope_mismatch_blocks_prong_one` (arm C accepted on 22 of 24 where arm A on 24: no tie by prong 1);
`recovery_prong_needs_both` (r_T = 0.9, r_C = 0.7: not fired); `not_established_when_lower_bound_at_or_below_1_5`;
`both_below_half_is_engine_source`; `tie_removes_ten_fold_verdict`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Step `kill-tie` of `scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh` (N39b-12) on a rehearsal stratum built to tie
(T_B = 100, T_W = 28, T_F = 10 in arbitrary units: r_T = 0.8 exactly) and one built not to (T_W = 60:
r_T = 0.444). Logged: each prong's value against its threshold, the reading, the ADR draft digest.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: if T_F = 0.10·T_B then r_T ≥ 0.8 exactly when T_W ≤ 0.28·T_B. Negative controls: a mutant that
fires on r_T alone; a mutant that reads the tie on the point estimate when the accepted scopes differ.

## Considerations

- Risk (register): a short robust program over an incumbent engine reaches the same accepted answers in
  comparable time; ties are already on record from plan-time pilots. The consequence is that the engine's share
  of the product shrinks; it is not a failure of the milestone.
- One expensive incompatibility can sink a layer; several rarely exercised gaps may leave it competitive.
  Feature counts decide neither, which is why the reading is on measured ratios.
- Licences of incumbents decide whether a layer can ship; an incumbent whose licence forbids it is replaced by
  the next strongest and the replacement is printed.
