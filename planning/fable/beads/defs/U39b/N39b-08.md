## Background

The program's thesis is a ratio of times against the strongest composed rival, and the rule of the program is
that the incumbent is measured before any mechanism is funded against it: versions pinned, the strongest warm
configuration recorded, and the phases of the incumbent loop timed on the project's own hardware. Incumbents are
frozen first. GATE-TENFOLD-01 (the parity-baseline gate; claim kind: Parity; M0) is the first step: the rival
audit and the phases that need no agent. If it is not met, M0 does not exit, the thesis arithmetic stays a
HYPOTHESIS and no work package is funded as a speed lever. The agent-driven loop follows at M1 (BD-143).

Terms. *Incumbent*: an existing simulator or learning stack. *Pin*: an exact release and revision plus a
container digest. *Warm configuration*: the incumbent's fastest documented settings with caches populated.
*`trj`*: the Linux workstation with two RTX 4090 cards. *Reference tasks*: the incumbents' own published home
tasks (for example a flat-ground quadruped velocity policy), as opposed to missions of the test. *Return
threshold*: the task return at which the incumbent's published training-time figure is read.

Division of labour with neighbouring beads: BD-039 builds the out-of-process incumbent harness (recorded
environments; a changed revision changes the harness identity); BD-040 times the `trj` rows of the incumbent
parity table under the disclosure block; this bead performs the audit that fixes what is pinned and frozen,
reads the no-agent rows into the first phase ledger of the ten-fold test, checks the published figures the
thesis uses, and records the gate.

## Scope

In: the audit record per incumbent (pin, licence, frozen warm configuration, and the capability table as a
per-incumbent view over the capability register); the first phase ledger; the 20% reproduction check, which
this bead implements once; the measured record of GATE-TENFOLD-01 (every measured value, with the receipt the
program-gate row cites).

Out, with the bead that has it:
- the capability register itself: the 21 rows of what incumbents document, with quotes, sources and versions
  (`incumbent_audit.toml`, records `IA-01` to `IA-21`: N00-13), and the twelve documented absences and eleven
  not-found items with their re-audit by execution on `trj` (records `DA-01` to `DA-12` and `NF-01` to `NF-11`,
  each with an `m0_record`: N00-14). This bead depends on both, cites their records by identifier in its
  capability table and transcribes no quote or source a second time;
- the row of GATE-TENFOLD-01 in the program-gate register, the completeness verdict `tenfold01(records)`, the
  M0 exit consequence (M0 does not exit; speed justifications marked `unfunded: no measured denominator`), the
  rules on what the baseline may and may not change, and the regeneration of the thesis table from the measured
  rows: N40-12, which depends on this bead, reads this bead's record and runs no measurement of its own;
- the harness (BD-039); the raw timing runs (BD-040); the Mac rows (BD-142); the agent-driven loop (BD-143); the
  pre-sealing audit of frozen arms at M3 and M7 (N39b-37).

## Technical approach

1. **Candidates to audit** (releases as read at planning time, FACT from the source register; each re-pinned by
   revision here): MuJoCo and MuJoCo Warp 4.0.1 (under MuJoCo Playground or mjlab), Newton 1.8.0, Isaac Lab
   3.0.0 (on Newton or PhysX), Genesis 1.4.7, Drake 1.58.0, ManiSkill 3.0.1. An incumbent whose licence forbids
   publishing a comparison is replaced by the next strongest and the replacement is printed (N39b-32).
2. **Audit record per incumbent**: one row of `crates/frob-conformance/registry/comparators.toml` in the schema
   of BD-039, which is the only definition of that file (its loader refuses an unknown field). The audit fills
   `name`, `release`, `source_revision` (a full commit), `container_digest`, `licence` (with
   `permits_publishing_comparison` from the licence reading of N39b-32), `capabilities` (the `IA`, `DA` and `NF`
   identifiers of step 3) and `warm_config` (a content hash of the configuration files and cache state). The
   primary-source re-read of the incumbent's documentation is a record of the source register (NAB-12, re-read
   by N00-34) reached through those capability records; it is not a field of this file.
3. **Capability table.** For each incumbent, what its own documentation says it can do, each row `documented`,
   `absent` or `not found`, with the source: per-world model parameters in one batch; state-preserving model
   edits; opt-in run-to-run determinism; mixed topologies in one batch; electrical and thermal actuator models;
   sampling control without training; interior generators; agent access over MCP. Every documented capability
   is available to the rival arm and is expected to be used where it helps; a capability left unused on
   development missions is questioned before a freeze.
   The table is a view, not a second transcription (DESIGN; reason: one fact about an incumbent must have one
   quoted source, or two files drift). Each cell holds a status and the identifiers of the register records
   that carry the quote, the source and the version read: `IA-nn` (N00-13) behind a `documented` cell, `DA-nn`
   (N00-14) behind an `absent` cell, `NF-nn` (N00-14) behind a `not found` cell. A cell with a status and no
   record behind it fails the audit; where the register has no row for one of the eight capabilities and an
   incumbent, the row is added to the register first. After the re-audit by execution, a cell follows the
   executed result: a `DA` record that N00-14 marked `withdrawn` (the incumbent does the thing) turns the cell
   to `documented` with the execution record as its source, and an `NF` item that was found does the same.
   Three further columns are read from the same register for every incumbent, because the program-gate row
   asks for them: restorable state and forks (the register's forking-state row), a Metal path (its Apple GPU
   row; `DA-06`, `NF-11`), and the fastest solver path, which is not a register row but the solver named in
   the frozen warm configuration of step 4.
4. **Freeze a warm configuration** for each incumbent after tuning on reference tasks only (never on missions of
   the test): solver and step settings, world counts, learner recipe, cache state. The configuration's hash is
   the identity later freeze manifests cite.
5. **No-agent phases on `trj`** (taken from BD-040's runs, each under a complete disclosure block). Five rows,
   lettered as the program-gate row letters them (N40-12):
   (a) cold start: process launch to the first device step, with and without caches;
   (b) a parameter edit to the first device step, and (c) a topology edit to the first device step (the
   incumbent's edit-to-first-step latency: a host compile, a model upload and a new graph capture);
   (d) training to the return threshold of the home quadruped task on one card; (e) the same on two cards.
6. **Read into the first phase ledger.** One `frob.tenfold.ledger/1` record per measurement with `arm: "B"`,
   `regime` cold or warm, and exclusive seconds assigned: launch and compilation to `compile`; learner and
   environment stepping during training to `train`, with the learner's share in the resource table; idle and
   transfer to `wait`. Each record must close within 2%.
7. **Published figures the thesis uses, reproduced or replaced.** For each figure: measured value, published
   value, relative difference. Within 20%: `reproduced`. Otherwise a written explanation and the measured figure
   replaces the published one. Figures: about 410 s to the threshold on one RTX 4090 (read by eye from a
   published figure, ± 20 s) and about 325 s on two (a strong-scaling ratio of 1.26), with a compile region of
   about 45 s inside those times; first-launch kernel compilation of about a minute, cached thereafter; cached
   and uncached start times where the stack is one of the audited ones.
8. **Other users of the pins.** The same pins serve the incumbent arms of the mixed-batch head-to-head
   experiment and the proxy-adequacy pilot of the scene subsystem; the audit record lists these consumers so
   that a pin move is seen by all of them. A comparator pin does not move between a freeze and the end of the
   study that uses it.
9. **Gate record.** GATE-TENFOLD-01 passes when: every incumbent is pinned with its capability table and
   licence; warm configurations are frozen; (a) to (e) are timed with closed ledgers; and every figure of step 7
   is reproduced within 20% or explained in writing. The rival audit is complete only when the re-audit of
   N00-14 is: no `DA` or `NF` record lacks its `m0_record`, and every `m0_record` was executed at the release
   pin and container digest that `comparators.toml` holds for that incumbent (a record at another pin is stale
   and its audit script is re-run before the gate is recorded).
   This bead writes the measured record (DESIGN name `frob.tenfold.gate01/1`, stored in the ledger): per
   incumbent the entry's identity, pin, licence and warm-configuration hash; the capability table's hash and
   the counts of `stands`, `withdrawn` and `not-executable` re-audit records; per row (a) to (e) the timing
   with unit, the ledger's identity and closure residual, the disclosure block's identifier and the harness
   identity; per published figure the measured value, the published value, the relative difference and the
   status `reproduced`, `explained` (with the explanation's identity) or `unexplained`; the controls with
   `caught`. One record, written here; the register row of the program gate (N40-12) cites it by hash and its
   verdict function reads it. There is no second measurement and no second script that times the incumbents.

Envelope: measurement on both cards exceeds the default compute class; with only the default class the two-card
row (e) is recorded `not run` and the gate record says so. A record with a row `not run` is incomplete: the
gate cannot pass on it (the plan's row requires training timed on one card and on two), and the M0 exit report
names the missing row. Evidence class `Tested`; determinism: timings are not bit-reproducible; `reality:
no-claim`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `comparators.toml` has one complete entry per audited incumbent; a changed revision changes the harness
      identity (BD-039's seed condition) and invalidates the frozen configuration hash.
- [ ] The capability table has a source for every row; `not found` is never recorded as `absent`. Every cell
      names at least one register record (`IA`, `DA` or `NF`) and no quote or source text is stored in
      `comparators.toml`; a cell whose `DA` record was withdrawn by the re-audit reads `documented`.
- [ ] The measured record `frob.tenfold.gate01/1` exists with every field of step 9; with row (e) `not run`,
      or with any re-audit record missing or executed at a stale pin, its status is incomplete and names what
      is missing.
- [ ] Ledger records exist and close for cold start, both edits and both training runs; each has a complete
      disclosure block.
- [ ] Each published figure is `reproduced` (within 20%) or carries a written explanation and replacement.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: a figure with an incomplete block is refused; a configuration tuned on a
      development mission (not a reference task) is refused by the audit's provenance check.
- [ ] The gate is recorded with measured values by a session that did not run the measurement.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::audit0`: `within_twenty_percent` (410 against 480 is 17.1%: reproduced;
410 against 500 is 22.0%: needs explanation; published 410 s and measured 470 s is 14.6%: reproduced; measured
520 s is 26.8%: `unexplained` with no explanation, `explained` with one, and the measured figure then replaces
the published one); `strong_scaling_ratio` (410/325 = 1.262);
`capability_not_found_is_not_absent`; `revision_change_changes_identity`; `ledger_from_training_run_closes`
(410 s with 45 s `compile`, 360 s `train`, 5 s `wait`); `licence_forbidding_publication_triggers_replacement`;
`capability_cell_needs_register_record` (a `documented` cell with no `IA` identifier fails; a cell citing a
withdrawn `DA` record must read `documented`); `stale_reaudit_pin_blocks_record` (an `m0_record` whose container
digest differs from the registry's makes the record incomplete); `two_card_row_not_run_is_incomplete`;
`warm_config_tuned_on_mission_refused` (a warm configuration whose tuning log names a development mission of the
test instead of a reference task is refused, `tenfold.warm-config-provenance`); `pin_drift_stops_before_timing`
(a container digest differing from `comparators.toml` refuses the run with `tenfold.incumbent-pin-drift`, exit 9,
before any timing starts; control TF-CX-27).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_parity_baseline.sh` (bead N39b-08; header `bead=N39b-08 milestone=M0 labels=tenfold,e2e
gates=GATE-TENFOLD-01 needs=cuda plants=pin-drift,incomplete-disclosure`): verifies pins against container
digests (a planted pin drift stops the script before any timing); runs step `licences` by running
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_licences.sh` (N39b-32's script: every pinned incumbent has a current licence reading whose
text digest matches the fetched licence, and is `cleared`, or its replacement is printed) as a stage; runs (a) to
(e) through the incumbent harness (`frob-tenfold incumbent build <name>` and `frob-tenfold incumbent run <name>
--config <hash> -- …`, BD-039); derives ledgers with `frob-tenfold ledger build` and checks their closure with
`frob-tenfold ledger check`; runs the 20% check, checks the re-audit records of N00-14 against the registry's
pins, writes the measured record `frob.tenfold.gate01/1` and prints the gate table. `E2E_SMALL=1` replays
recorded synthetic timings (labelled `synthetic`; gates not claimable). Logged as NDJSON through the STD-E2E
helper to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_parity_baseline/<utc>.ndjson` (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the
exact command line, exit code, wall time, and the evidence block of each measurement): each measured figure as a
`metric` with its published value and the 20% band, each ledger's digest and closure residual beside 2%, the
harness identity and pins, host load and thermal state, each plant as a `negative-control` assertion with
`caught` (the incomplete disclosure block refused with FROB-REF-862); on failure the first differing value
(figure, measured, published) and the paths of the ledgers; a final `summary` with assertion counts, the measured
record's hash and `gates: [{id: "GATE-TENFOLD-01", measured_ok, controls_caught}]`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the 20% band arithmetic; the scaling ratio 1.26; closure at 2%. Negative controls: an incomplete
disclosure block (FROB-REF-862); a planted pin drift (the container digest differs from the registry) must stop
the script before any timing.

## Considerations

- Risk (register): the denominator is wrong because incumbents are faster than the composite of published
  figures; this gate is its earliest signal. A time to threshold near the published 410 s confirms that the
  quadruped class has a ceiling near ten or under.
- Absolute incumbent throughput on an RTX 4090 was not verified at planning time (published figures are for
  other cards); nothing here may assume it.
- `trj` is often loaded; every run records host load, and timing runs are repeated when load exceeds the
  disclosure block's stated bound.
- Which incumbent revisions are the comparators is an open question whose default is the revisions in the
  source register, frozen here.
