## Background

The program's thesis is a ratio of times against the strongest composed rival, and the rule of the program is
that the incumbent is measured before any mechanism is funded against it: versions pinned, the strongest warm
configuration recorded, and the phases of the incumbent loop timed on the project's own hardware. Incumbents are
frozen first. GATE-TENFOLD-01 (the parity-baseline gate; claim kind: Parity; M0) is the first step: the rival
audit and the phases that need no agent. If it is not met, M0 does not exit, the thesis arithmetic stays a
HYPOTHESIS and no work package is funded as a speed lever. The agent-driven loop follows at M1 (BD-143).

Terms. *Incumbent*: an existing simulator or learning stack. *Pin*: an exact release and revision plus a
container digest. *Warm configuration*: the incumbent's fastest documented settings with caches populated.
*`trj`*: the Linux workstation with two RTX 4090 cards. *Reference tasks*: the incumbents' own published home
tasks (for example a flat-ground quadruped velocity policy), as opposed to missions of the test. *Return
threshold*: the task return at which the incumbent's published training-time figure is read.

Division of labour with neighbouring beads: BD-039 builds the out-of-process incumbent harness (recorded
environments; a changed revision changes the harness identity); BD-040 times the `trj` rows of the incumbent
parity table under the disclosure block; this bead performs the audit that fixes what is pinned and frozen,
reads the no-agent rows into the first phase ledger of the ten-fold test, checks the published figures the
thesis uses, and records the gate.

## Scope

In: the audit record per incumbent (pin, capability table, licence, frozen warm configuration); the first phase
ledger; the 20% reproduction check; the gate record. Out: the harness (BD-039); the raw timing runs (BD-040); the
Mac rows (BD-142); the agent-driven loop (BD-143); the pre-sealing audit of frozen arms at M3 and M7 (N39b-37).

## Technical approach

1. **Candidates to audit** (releases as read at planning time, FACT from the source register; each re-pinned by
   revision here): MuJoCo and MuJoCo Warp 4.0.1 (under MuJoCo Playground or mjlab), Newton 1.8.0, Isaac Lab
   3.0.0 (on Newton or PhysX), Genesis 1.4.7, Drake 1.58.0, ManiSkill 3.0.1. An incumbent whose licence forbids
   publishing a comparison is replaced by the next strongest and the replacement is printed (N39b-32).
2. **Audit record per incumbent** (one entry of `comparators.toml`, DESIGN): `name`, `release`, `revision`,
   `container_digest`, `licence`, `publication_allowed`, `source_reread` (the primary source and date),
   `capabilities` and `warm_config` (a content hash of the configuration files and cache state).
3. **Capability table.** For each incumbent, what its own documentation says it can do, each row `documented`,
   `absent` or `not found`, with the source: per-world model parameters in one batch; state-preserving model
   edits; opt-in run-to-run determinism; mixed topologies in one batch; electrical and thermal actuator models;
   sampling control without training; interior generators; agent access over MCP. Every documented capability
   is available to the rival arm and is expected to be used where it helps; a capability left unused on
   development missions is questioned before a freeze.
4. **Freeze a warm configuration** for each incumbent after tuning on reference tasks only (never on missions of
   the test): solver and step settings, world counts, learner recipe, cache state. The configuration's hash is
   the identity later freeze manifests cite.
5. **No-agent phases on `trj`** (taken from BD-040's runs, each under a complete disclosure block):
   (a) cold start: process launch to the first device step, with and without caches;
   (b) a parameter edit to the first device step, and (c) a topology edit to the first device step (the
   incumbent's edit-to-first-step latency: a host compile, a model upload and a new graph capture);
   (d) training to the return threshold of the home quadruped task on one card and on two.
6. **Read into the first phase ledger.** One `frob.tenfold.ledger/1` record per measurement with `arm: "B"`,
   `regime` cold or warm, and exclusive seconds assigned: launch and compilation to `compile`; learner and
   environment stepping during training to `train`, with the learner's share in the resource table; idle and
   transfer to `wait`. Each record must close within 2%.
7. **Published figures the thesis uses, reproduced or replaced.** For each figure: measured value, published
   value, relative difference. Within 20%: `reproduced`. Otherwise a written explanation and the measured figure
   replaces the published one. Figures: about 410 s to the threshold on one RTX 4090 (read by eye from a
   published figure, ± 20 s) and about 325 s on two (a strong-scaling ratio of 1.26), with a compile region of
   about 45 s inside those times; first-launch kernel compilation of about a minute, cached thereafter; cached
   and uncached start times where the stack is one of the audited ones.
8. **Other users of the pins.** The same pins serve the incumbent arms of the mixed-batch head-to-head
   experiment and the proxy-adequacy pilot of the scene subsystem; the audit record lists these consumers so
   that a pin move is seen by all of them. A comparator pin does not move between a freeze and the end of the
   study that uses it.
9. **Gate record.** GATE-TENFOLD-01 passes when: every incumbent is pinned with its capability table and
   licence; warm configurations are frozen; (a) to (d) are timed with closed ledgers; and every figure of step 7
   is reproduced within 20% or explained in writing.

Envelope: measurement on both cards exceeds the default compute class; with only the default class the two-card
row is recorded `not run` and the gate record says so. Evidence class `Tested`; determinism: timings are not
bit-reproducible; `reality: no-claim`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `comparators.toml` has one complete entry per audited incumbent; a changed revision changes the harness
      identity (BD-039's seed condition) and invalidates the frozen configuration hash.
- [ ] The capability table has a source for every row; `not found` is never recorded as `absent`.
- [ ] Ledger records exist and close for cold start, both edits and both training runs; each has a complete
      disclosure block.
- [ ] Each published figure is `reproduced` (within 20%) or carries a written explanation and replacement.
- [ ] Negative controls caught: a figure with an incomplete block is refused; a configuration tuned on a
      development mission (not a reference task) is refused by the audit's provenance check.
- [ ] The gate is recorded with measured values by a session that did not run the measurement.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::audit0`: `within_twenty_percent` (410 against 480 is 17.1%: reproduced;
410 against 500 is 22.0%: needs explanation); `strong_scaling_ratio` (410/325 = 1.262);
`capability_not_found_is_not_absent`; `revision_change_changes_identity`; `ledger_from_training_run_closes`
(410 s with 45 s `compile`, 360 s `train`, 5 s `wait`); `licence_forbidding_publication_triggers_replacement`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_parity_baseline.sh` (bead N39b-08; needs `cuda`): verifies pins against container digests,
runs (a) to (d) through the incumbent harness, derives ledgers, runs the 20% check and prints the gate table.
`E2E_SMALL=1` replays recorded synthetic timings (labelled `synthetic`; gates not claimable). Logged: each
command, wall time, each measured figure as a `metric` with its published value and the 20% band, the ledger
digests, host load and thermal state.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the 20% band arithmetic; the scaling ratio 1.26; closure at 2%. Negative controls: an incomplete
disclosure block (FROB-REF-862); a planted pin drift (the container digest differs from the registry) must stop
the script before any timing.

## Considerations

- Risk (register): the denominator is wrong because incumbents are faster than the composite of published
  figures; this gate is its earliest signal. A time to threshold near the published 410 s confirms that the
  quadruped class has a ceiling near ten or under.
- Absolute incumbent throughput on an RTX 4090 was not verified at planning time (published figures are for
  other cards); nothing here may assume it.
- `trj` is often loaded; every run records host load, and timing runs are repeated when load exceeds the
  disclosure block's stated bound.
- Which incumbent revisions are the comparators is an open question whose default is the revisions in the
  source register, frozen here.
