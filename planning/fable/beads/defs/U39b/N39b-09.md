## Background

The thesis of the program is a conjunction of eight levers, each attacking a different part of the time an
agent-driven design loop spends: (1) decide many designs per agent turn; (2) train few; (3) spend evaluation
where it matters; (4) find failures instead of waiting for them; (5) answer over families instead of points;
(6) do not recompute or re-decide; (7) explain by intervention; (8) short setup. A lever can only save time that
is there. Ceiling 1 of the test: if a phase holds the share p of the rival's restricted time and a mechanism
removes it entirely, the speed-up is at most 1/(1 − p). At p = 0.10 that is 1/0.9 = 1.11. KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER
applies this to funding. Trigger: a mechanism's removable cost is under 10% of the measured loop. Fallback,
named in advance: it is not funded as a speed lever; it may still be funded for what it does to trust, coverage
or correctness, with that reason written in its work package and no speed figure attached. Decided at M1 on the
rival's ledger, and again at M2 on our own profile (N39b-10). A lever with p under 0.10 is struck from the speed
case before M2 begins.

This is the funding half of the program rule "ceiling before speed" (GATE-TENFOLD-09: every work package funded
as a speed lever cites a removable share of at least 10% on a `ledger-closed` run of the strong baseline, and
every registered speed comparison records its ceiling and is refused when the ceiling is below the ratio it sets
out to show). Its origin is a planning pilot in which a specialized solver was measured at 1.345 times a
composed one on a test whose ceiling, computed afterwards from a residual of 575.7 µs that the solver does not
touch, was about 1.46 (842.8 ÷ 575.7): the test could not have confirmed its claim.

## Scope

In: the reading of the lever-share table; the strike list; the funding-reason field of each affected work
package; the ADR; the first application of GATE-TENFOLD-09 to funding. Out: measuring the shares (BD-143); the
re-reading at M2 (N39b-10); the refusal of individual speed comparisons (the ceiling beads).

## Technical approach

1. **Input.** The lever-share table of the incumbent baseline: for each lever, each stratum class (`legged-quad`,
   `wheeled`) and each regime (cold, warm), the removable share p with its interval, on closed ledgers only.
   The phases a lever acts on: lever 1 on `propose` (through the number of turns) and `screen`; lever 2 on
   `train`; lever 3 on `screen` and `evaluate`; levers 4 and 7 on `diagnose`; lever 5 on `certify` and `propose`;
   lever 6 on `check`, `compile` and `evaluate`; lever 8 on `compile` and `author`.
2. **Reading rule.** Per lever and class: `funded-as-speed` if p ≥ 0.10; `struck` if p < 0.10; `marginal` if the
   interval of p contains 0.10 (DESIGN: a marginal lever keeps its speed funding until the M2 re-reading and is
   flagged in every work package that cites it). The reading is on the rival's ledger: time the rival does not
   spend cannot be saved.
3. **Order rule.** A pruning ratio is never multiplied by a speed-up measured on work the pruning already
   removed. In any product of levers each factor is measured on the time the earlier factors left. The ADR
   therefore records, besides each lever's own share, the shares that overlap (for example `propose` under
   levers 1 and 5, `compile` under 6 and 8) and forbids adding them.
4. **Funding reasons.** Every open work package gets `funding_reason`: either `speed-lever` with the lever
   number, the cited p, the class and the ledger digest, or one of `trust`, `coverage`, `correctness` with a
   written reason and no speed figure. A package citing a struck lever loses `speed-lever` for that class. A
   package with no reason is not funded.
5. **What the baseline may change here:** the ranking of the levers, the funding of work packages, the
   deadlines H, and the expected ranges by mission class. It may not change the estimand or the outcome rules.
6. **ADR content.** The table (p, interval, ceiling 1/(1 − p), status) per lever and class; the strike list; the
   packages whose reason changed; the re-ranked lever order; the binding ceiling per class (for example the
   one-design-per-turn cap (C + t)/t with measured C and t); and the list of speed comparisons that are now
   refused for lack of a ceiling above their goal.
7. **Downstream.** Struck levers stay in the plan for their other value. Their beads drop any speed threshold
   from their acceptance criteria; a later request to register a speed comparison for them is refused. If lever
   2 is struck for a class, the M4 work on family policies is funded for that class on other grounds or not at
   all. If no lever reaches 0.10 for a class, the class's expected range is restated and the partial gate still
   measures it.

Worked reading on the illustrative single-pair ledger (rival seconds: `author` 900, `propose` 1,080, `check` 240,
`compile` 540, `screen` 0, `train` 4,920, `evaluate` 360, `diagnose` 600, `wait` 180, `adjudicate` 180; clock
9,000): lever 1: 12.0%; lever 2: 54.67%; lever 3: 4.0%, struck; levers 4 and 7: 6.67%, struck; lever 5: 12.0%;
lever 6: 12.67%; lever 8: 16.0%. These numbers are illustrative and are not a forecast.

Fail-closed posture until this decision is recorded: no work package is funded as a speed lever.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR exists, dated in M1, with a status for each of the eight levers per class and regime.
- [ ] Every open work package has a `funding_reason`; none cites a struck lever for speed.
- [ ] A planted work package citing a 4% share as its speed justification is rejected.
- [ ] A planted product of levers that adds overlapping shares is flagged by the order rule.
- [ ] GATE-TENFOLD-09's funding half is recorded at M1 with the measured shares.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::lever`: `worked_card_readings` (the seven shares above; struck set
{3, 4, 7}); `ten_percent_is_funded` (p = 0.10 exactly is funded; 0.0999 struck); `marginal_when_interval_straddles`;
`ceiling_of_ten_percent_is_ten_ninths`; `overlap_is_not_additive` (levers 1 and 5 together remove at most
`propose` + `screen` + `certify`, not twice `propose`); `package_without_reason_unfunded`.

### End-to-end script and logging

Step `lever-reading` of `scripts/e2e/tenfold_incumbent_loop.sh` (BD-143): reads the share table, prints the
status per lever, and asserts the strike list against the registry of work packages in the scratch project.
Logged: each p as a `metric` with threshold 0.10 and its interval, the ADR draft digest.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 1/0.9 = 1.11; the pilot ceiling 842.8 ÷ 575.7 = 1.464 against measured 1.345 and 1.088; the worked
card readings. Negative controls: a share computed on our own ledger instead of the rival's; a speed comparison
registered with a ceiling below its goal (must be refused).

## Considerations

- The 10% line is a rule about speed funding only. Certificates, adversarial search and attribution may each
  hold little removable time and remain funded for what they do to trust and coverage.
- The shares are measured on development missions of two strata; other classes are read when first measured.
- Risk: agent turn latency and rival training time are both shorter than assumed, which lowers the ceilings of
  levers 1 and 2; the ADR states the measured values beside the planning values (60 to 120 s; 300 to 410 s).
