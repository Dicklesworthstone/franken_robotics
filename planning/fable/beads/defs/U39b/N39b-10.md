## Background

At M1 the kill gate KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER was read on the rival's ledger: a lever whose phases hold under 10% of
the rival's measured loop cannot be funded as a speed lever (N39a-39). That reading says how much time is there
to be removed. It does not say how much of it our mechanism actually removes, or what the mechanism costs. At M2
the batched engine, the device backends, mixed batches, the learner and the cache exist and have a measured
profile, so the gate is read again with our own numbers (the milestone's acceptance list says: "KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER
is re-read on our own profile"). Ceiling 2 of the test supplies the arithmetic: if the share f of the rival's
time is improved by the factor r and the mechanism adds overhead h (as a fraction of the rival's time), the
speed-up is at most 1/(1 − f + f/r + h). The three terms are measured on the rival's critical path.

The same milestone decides the engine's own kill gate, KILL-PARITY: after one optimization pass guided by the
profile, if the paired lower bound of a physics row is below 0.33 of the incumbent on the same card, feature work
on the device tier stops, and the arithmetic of the thesis and of the ten-fold test is redone with measured
rates before the partial gate; whether to ship the layer over an incumbent engine is then decided at that gate
under its own outcome rules, not at M2. This bead performs that redo when it is triggered, and performs a
lighter restatement whenever a physics row lies in the recorded-defect band between 0.33 and 0.67.

## Scope

In: the M2 reading per lever; the net removable share; the redo of the thesis arithmetic with measured engine
rates; the consequences for deadlines and reachability at the partial gate; the ADR. Out: the engine profile and
parity rows themselves (the performance beads); the M1 reading (N39a-39); the partial gate (BD-317).

## Technical approach

1. **Inputs.** From M1: f per lever, class and regime (the rival's shares, closed ledgers). From our M2 profile,
   each under a complete disclosure block: edit-to-first-step for a parameter edit and for a topology edit;
   mixed-batch rate over the harmonic mean of homogeneous rates; physics, full-step and training throughput
   against the incumbent on the same card as paired lower bounds; time to the return threshold; cache hit
   rates; the measured cost of our cheap rung per design.
2. **Net removable share.** For lever ℓ with measured improvement factor r_ℓ on its share f_ℓ and overhead h_ℓ:
   p_net = f_ℓ·(1 − 1/r_ℓ) − h_ℓ, and its ceiling is 1/(1 − p_net) = 1/(1 − f_ℓ + f_ℓ/r_ℓ + h_ℓ). Reading:
   `funded-as-speed` if p_net ≥ 0.10; `struck` if below; `unmeasured` if r_ℓ or h_ℓ has no measurement yet (the
   lever keeps its M1 status and is listed for the next exit).
3. **Which levers can be read at M2.** Lever 8 (short setup): r from the rival's and our edit-to-first-step; h
   from the cache's own cost. Lever 1 (many designs per turn), in part: r for switching designs from the
   mixed-batch measurement; h from padding. Lever 2's training rung at parity: our time to threshold over the
   incumbent's (above 1.25 it is a cost, entered as negative saving). Lever 6: measured memo hit rates on
   analyses. Levers 3, 4, 5 and 7 are `unmeasured` until M3 and M4.
4. **Order rule.** Each lever's r is measured on the time the earlier levers left; a pruning ratio is never
   multiplied by a device speed-up measured on work the pruning removed. Smallest case: 100 candidates of 10 s
   each; pruning removes 90; the ten survivors are the hard ones and accelerate by 2, not by the 5 measured on
   the full set: the result is 1,000/50 = 20, not 10 × 5 = 50.
5. **Parity restatement.** With ρ the measured ratio of our rate to the incumbent's on a physics row, every
   planning value of arm A's `screen`, `train` and `evaluate` time is multiplied by 1/ρ for the share of that
   phase that is stepping. A step 1.5 times slower (ρ = 0.67) lengthens training by a factor between 1.15 and
   1.44, since the environment step is between 0.29 and 0.87 of training wall-clock on incumbent stacks.
6. **Redo when KILL-PARITY fires (ρ < 0.33 on row 1, 7 or 8).** Recompute, per class: arm A's planned restricted
   mean time; the unremovable-cost cap RMT_B ÷ u; the horizon cap H ÷ RMT_A; the compute-per-decision ratio
   C ÷ (c + c_T·φ) with the measured c and c_T; and the expected range. Symbols: RMT_A and RMT_B are the
   restricted mean times to an accepted answer of arm A (ours) and arm B (the rival), restricted at the horizon
   H (the mission deadline), so RMT_B ≤ H; u is the floor of arm A's time that no mechanism removes (authoring,
   the common agent's turns, adjudication, checking, serialization); C is the rival's device-seconds per trained
   candidate (planning value about 410); c is our screening cost per design and c_T our training cost per trained
   candidate, both in device-seconds; φ is the fraction of screened designs that go on to training (written f in
   the thesis chapter, renamed here so as not to collide with a lever's share f_ℓ). Ten-fold on compute needs
   c + c_T·φ ≤ C/10 = 41 device-seconds. Consequences written into the ADR: a cell
   whose horizon cap falls below 10 has its deadline raised by the adjudicator's rule or is opened as
   `descriptive`; the class's expected range is restated; the question "ship the layer over an incumbent
   engine" is put on the agenda of the partial gate, to be decided there by the tie rule.
7. **ADR content.** The table per lever (f, r, h, p_net, ceiling, status); the parity ratios; the restated
   planning values; the cells affected; the list of work packages whose `funding_reason` changes.
8. **Record, in the one form of kill-gate decisions (N40-03).** This is the M2 re-read of a gate decided at M1
   (N40-28: `decided = "M1"`, `reread = ["M2"]`, no `scope_kind`). The ADR is
   `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER-M2.md`, a new record beside the M1 one of N39a-39, which is kept and not
   edited. The gate's row in `registries/gates.toml` receives one entry appended to its `decision` array,
   `{exit = "M2", outcome, adr, date, session, receipts}`: `fired` when at least one lever is struck for at least
   one class (the ADR names which; the fallback applies to those levers only); `not-fired` when every measured
   lever has p_net ≥ 0.10, citing at least one receipt under `receipts/gates/` (N40-01) of the records the
   shares rest on. Because the row has no `scope_kind`, the per-lever and per-class statuses, including the
   levers still `unmeasured` with the exit at which each is read, are in the ADR and not in separate entries.

Fail-closed posture: a lever with no M2 measurement keeps no speed figure in any document beyond its M1 share.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ADR `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER-M2.md` exists, dated in M2, with a status for each lever and a
      restated planning table per class, and the gate's row holds its M2 decision entry beside the M1 entry.
- [ ] Every work package still funded as a speed lever cites p_net ≥ 0.10 or is marked `unmeasured` with the
      exit at which it will be read.
- [ ] If any physics row is below 0.33 of the incumbent, the redo of step 6 is on record before the partial
      gate's protocol is committed; if between 0.33 and 0.67, the restatement of step 5 is on record.
- [ ] The order-rule known answer (20, not 50) is reproduced and its mutant is caught.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::lever_m2`: `net_share_formula` (f = 0.16, r = 20, h = 0.01: p_net =
0.142, ceiling 1.166); `overhead_can_strike` (f = 0.12, r = 4, h = 0.02: p_net = 0.07, struck);
`general_bound_known_values` (f = 0.97, r = 40, h = 0.02 gives 13.5; f = 0.85 with r → ∞ and h = 0 gives 6.67);
`pruning_order_rule` (20, not 50); `parity_restatement` (ρ = 2/3 with step share 0.87 gives 1 + 0.87·0.5 =
1.435; with 0.29 gives 1.145); `horizon_cap_drop_opens_descriptive`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_lever_m2.sh` (owned by this bead; sources the shared end-to-end helper; deterministic over
recorded inputs; runs nothing on a device). Steps: `inputs` (load the M1 share table of N39a-39 and the M2
records: the step profiles, the parity rows' receipts of BD-217 and the KILL-PARITY decision of N36-27; assert
each carries a complete disclosure block and each ratio a paired lower bound, else stop with the refusal);
`levers` (compute f, r, h, p_net and the ceiling per lever and assert each status against 0.10, `unmeasured`
for levers 3, 4, 5 and 7); `order_rule` (the 100-candidate case: assert 20, and assert that the planted product
10 × 5 = 50 is rejected); `parity` (the restatement of step 5 for each physics row in the defect band);
`redo` (only when KILL-PARITY fired; otherwise asserted absent); `controls` (with
`E2E_PLANT_FAILURE=parity-below-third`, assert that the redo is produced and the affected cell is flagged; a
profile figure with its block removed is refused with `FROB-REF-862`; a ratio given as a mean is refused with
`FROB-REF-863`); `adr` (write the draft and log its digest; write the M2 decision entry to a scratch copy of the
register and assert that it is appended after the M1 entry, which is unchanged, with the outcome the lever
statuses imply and at least one receipt for `not-fired`). NDJSON to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_lever_m2/<utc-timestamp>.ndjson` with a transcript beside it; every line has `ts`,
`script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time and the evidence block of the record
read; `metric` lines carry f, r, h and p_net per lever against 0.10, the parity ratios against 0.33 and 0.67, the
restated planning values with units; on failure the lever or row, the first differing value and the record
paths; a final `summary` counts assertions, lists each lever's status and names `KILL-TENFOLD-LEVER`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the general bound (13.5; 6.7; no r reaches ten at h = 0.02 unless f ≥ 0.92); the order rule.
Negative controls: a mutant that multiplies a pruning ratio by a full-set speed-up; a profile figure without a
disclosure block (refused, FROB-REF-862); a ratio without a paired lower bound (refused, FROB-REF-863).

## Considerations

- A lever struck at M2 for overhead is a design finding about our mechanism, not about the rival; the ADR
  records which overhead term bound it.
- A measured parity ratio between 0.33 and 0.67 is a recorded defect: one further optimization pass, and a
  disclosure on every performance claim until it closes.
- Revisit when the default physics step of the reference workloads changes: every row is restated first.
