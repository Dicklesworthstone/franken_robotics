## Background

The partial ten-fold gate at M3 is the first measured comparison of this system (arm A), the strongest composed
rival (arm B) and a short program over the rival's engine (arm C) on sealed missions. Its record is expensive
(up to 288 device-hours per cell in the worst case) and is read by more than the speed claim. Five other
decisions are taken on it, so that no second campaign is needed: two kill gates of the mission and proof
subsystems (KILL-AUGUR-TIE and KILL-FAMILYCERT, on the `envelope` and `sensing` strata); the kill gate of the
adapter through which rival learning stacks drive our environment (KILL-GYMADAPT); the incumbent arm that the bet
on agent surfaces (BET-10) names; and whether the `train` phase still holds a removable share of at least 10%
for each class, which decides how much of the M4 work on the lever "train few" (family policies and inheritance)
is funded as a speed lever. The gate record (BD-317) carries every input; this bead reads and checks them, takes
the three decisions that belong to the ten-fold test, and confirms that the other two owners have complete inputs.

Terms. *Phase ledger*: per run, the clock assigned to thirteen phases, one of which is `train` (policy training,
fine-tuning and distillation, learner time included). *Removable share*: the fraction of the rival's restricted
time a lever could remove; under 10% the lever's whole effect is at most 1/0.9 = 1.11. *Family certificate*:
checked evidence about a cell of a design family from a specialized projection of the mission. *Generic
comparator*: the conventional method a specialized one must beat (a lifted robust linear program, a scenario
tree, a satisfiability or interval tool).

## Scope

In: the decisions on lever-2 funding, on the BET-10 arm and on the adapter-latency record, with their ADRs; the
learner-swap runs of step 3; the check that the five decision inputs are on the gate record, complete and
digested, and the `decisions.json` that cites them. Out: computing the measured inputs themselves, which BD-317
records as step 13 of closing the gate (the `train` share per class with its interval, the family-certificate
table, arm C's turnaround on the family strata, the adapter's added latency): this bead recomputes the `train`
share from the ledgers only as a check that must agree exactly, and never produces a second version of any
table; the ADRs of KILL-FAMILYCERT (N12-30) and KILL-AUGUR-TIE (N25a-18), which read BD-317's record directly;
the gate record itself (BD-317); the program-level tie rule (N39b-07); the listing of these decisions at the M3
exit by the program-gate check of GATE-TENFOLD-06 (N40-15, step 4), which cites this bead's `decisions.json` and
decides nothing.

## Technical approach

1. **Lever-2 funding (decided here).** From arm B's closed ledgers on the sealed missions of each design class
   (`legged-quad`, `wheeled`), cold: the pooled `train` share p = Σ train seconds ÷ Σ min(T, H) with its family
   bootstrap interval, as BD-317 recorded it (recomputed here from the same ledgers; a difference in any digit
   stops the decision and is reported against BD-317's record). Reading: p ≥ 0.10: the M4 work on family policies and inheritance is funded as a speed
   lever for that class, citing p and the ledger digest; p < 0.10: it is not funded as a speed lever for that
   class (it may be funded for coverage or correctness with a written reason and no speed figure); interval
   straddling 0.10: `marginal`, funded, re-read at the M4 exit. Also recorded: arm A's own `train` share at M3
   (the product without that lever), and the ceiling 1/(1 − p) of removing rival training entirely.
2. **The incumbent arm of BET-10 (decided here).** The bet compares agents on the full studio surface against
   agents given only `sim run` and a scalar score; a third reference is an incumbent stack. The record names
   that arm: per design stratum, the frozen arm-B configuration of the partial gate, by its freeze-manifest
   hash, with its restricted mean time per development brief of the design benchmark. No new tuning is allowed
   between this naming and the A/B run; a configuration change is a new freeze.
3. **Adapter latency (recorded here).** If the adapter's gate passed: the learner-swap diagnostic is reported.
   With E_o, E_r the engines and L_o, L_r the learners, three combinations are run: (E_o, L_o), (E_r, L_r),
   (E_o, L_r). The difference in the `train` phase between arms splits into an engine part
   train(E_r, L_r) − train(E_o, L_r) and a learner part train(E_o, L_r) − train(E_o, L_o). The diagnostic never
   enters the ratio R. If KILL-GYMADAPT fired: rival learners drove whole rollouts through the job interface;
   the added latency per rollout (median and 95th percentile) and its total per campaign are recorded in the
   ledger's resource table and stated in the report as a lost factor, so that the comparison is not flattered
   by a slow access path.
4. **Inputs for KILL-FAMILYCERT (checked and cited; produced by BD-317 step 13).** For every cell of every
   family answer submitted on `envelope` and `sensing`: the verdict carried by a family certificate (inner, excluded, unresolved) with producer wall
   time, compute seconds and checker seconds; and the generic comparator's verdict at a budget equal to that
   cost. The table and the count of cells decided by the certificate and left undecided by the comparator go to
   the decision record of that gate. Trigger there: the count is zero.
5. **Inputs for KILL-AUGUR-TIE (checked and cited; produced by BD-317 step 13).** On `envelope` and `sensing`: arm C's accepted scope and
   restricted mean time against arm A's, the turnaround per amendment, and the scope comparison (same checked
   obligations). Trigger there: a short robust program over a conventional solver reaches the same checked
   scope at comparable turnaround; fallback there: that program ships behind the same verbs, schema and checker.
6. **Record.** One document `decisions.md` in the gate directory plus a machine-readable `decisions.json`, each
   item with its inputs' digests; three ADRs (lever-2 funding per class; BET-10 arm; adapter latency or
   diagnostic). Lost factors promised to this ledger by other kill gates (low rank agreement between evaluation
   rungs; engine throughput below parity; the adapter) are listed with their measured size or `not fired`.

Fail-closed defaults until recorded: lever 2 is not funded as a speed lever at M4; BET-10's incumbent arm is
`not named` and its A/B reports that arm `not run`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `train` share with interval is recorded per design class from arm B's sealed-suite ledgers, and the
      funding status of the M4 lever-2 work is recorded per class.
- [ ] The BET-10 incumbent arm is named by manifest hash per stratum.
- [ ] Either the learner-swap diagnostic (three combinations, two parts) or the fallback latency is on record.
- [ ] The two hand-off tables exist with digests and are cited by the two kill-gate decision records.
- [ ] A planted `train` share of 8% yields "not funded as a speed lever"; a planted missing combination in the
      diagnostic is reported as `incomplete`, not as zero.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::sidedecisions`: `train_share_pooled` (ratio of sums over runs);
`funding_threshold_inclusive` (0.10 funded); `learner_swap_split_sums` (engine part + learner part equals the
total difference); `fallback_latency_recorded_when_adapter_killed`; `familycert_count_zero_is_reported_as_zero`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_side_decisions.sh` (this bead; header `bead=N39b-11 milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e
gates=GATE-TENFOLD-06 seed=390611 plants=train-share-from-arm-a,diagnostic-into-r,missing-combination,
share-mismatch`). It cannot be a step of the rehearsal script N39b-12, which comes before the gate record this
bead depends on; it runs that script in replay mode with `--keep-gate` and acts on the kept directory, whose
step-13 inputs N39b-12 has already asserted. Steps: `check-inputs` (the five inputs present, complete and
digested; the `train` share recomputed from the ledgers equals BD-317's to the last digit); `decide` (lever-2
funding per class, the BET-10 arm named by manifest hash, the adapter record or the learner-swap diagnostic);
`write` (`decisions.md`, `decisions.json`, three ADR drafts); `plants` (a share read from arm A's ledger; a
diagnostic value offered to the scorer, refused by its input schema; a missing learner-swap combination,
reported `incomplete`, never zero; a share differing from BD-317's in one digit, which stops the decision).
Logged as NDJSON through the shared helper (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code,
wall time): each share as a `metric` against 0.10 with its interval and the funding status; the diagnostic's two
parts in seconds and their sum against the total difference; each input's digest as recorded by BD-317 and as
read; the digests of `decisions.json` and of each ADR draft; on failure the first differing value and the gate
directory path; the `summary` with the plants caught and `gates_claimable: false`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: on the illustrative single-pair ledger the rival's `train` share is 54.67% (ceiling 2.206) and arm
A's is 10%. Negative controls: a `train` share read from arm A's ledger instead of arm B's; a diagnostic value
entered into R (must be refused by the scorer's input schema).

## Considerations

- The partial gate measures arm A without lever 2, so the reading is about the rival's time available to be
  removed, not about what we removed.
- Each decision taken here is conditional on the pinned agent and on `self-adjudicated` status where it applies.
- If the gate was recorded `not run` (no compute class approved), all five decisions stay at their fail-closed
  defaults and are listed as open at the M3 exit.
