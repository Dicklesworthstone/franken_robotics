## Background

The partial ten-fold gate is run once, on sealed mission families that can never be reused, at a cost of tens to
hundreds of device-hours. A defect in the gate's own machinery (a sealing step out of order, a scorer that drops
a censored run, a leak detector that looks at the wrong files) would either void the gate or, worse, publish a
wrong number. This bead is the end-to-end script that rehearses the whole gate before any sealed family is
opened: it drives the real harness through every step of the real procedure on a rehearsal suite and synthetic
arms, and plants each failure the harness must catch. It claims no gate: its log says `gates_claimable: false`
for GATE-TENFOLD-06. What it proves is that the pipeline which will produce that gate's record works from the
operator's side and fails when it should.

Terms. *Arm*: one competitor (A: this system; B: the composed rival; C: a short program over the rival's
engine). *Synthetic arm*: a scripted stand-in that answers rehearsal missions with prepared answers after
programmed delays; it is an input fixture of the harness under test and is labelled `synthetic` everywhere.
*Cell*: a stratum and a regime. *H*: the deadline; a run not accepted by H is censored and contributes H.

## Scope

In: the script, its synthetic arms and rehearsal suite, its golden files, its planted failures, its log. Out: the
mechanisms it exercises (their beads); the harness known-answer battery (N39b-22); the rehearsal of the scoring
pipeline alone, with planted wrong scorers (N39a-41); the M7 dress rehearsal (N39b-17); the post-gate decisions
of N39b-06, N39b-07 and N39b-11, whose own scripts run on this script's kept replay record.

## Technical approach

**Files.** `scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh`; fixtures under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/tenfold/
rehearsal/` (stratum one: 24 rehearsal families, the six-family card repeated four times; stratum two: the
three-arm variants of step 9; synthetic arm programs, golden sentences and report).
Header: `bead=N39b-12 milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e gates=GATE-TENFOLD-06 seed=390612 timeout_s=1800
plants=false-cert,canary,ledger-open,late-freeze,four-family,monitor-verdict,adjudicator-in-tuning,arm-changed,
record-edited,log-gap`.

**Time scale.** The live mode scales the illustrative six-family card by 1/720: H = 20 s; the six families'
(T_B, T_A) become (12.5, 2.083), (8.75, 1.25), (censored, 2.917), (7.5, 0.972), (16.25, censored), (10, 1.111)
seconds; repeated four times they give a 24-family cell. A replay mode feeds the harness recorded ledgers with
the exact card values so that sums are asserted exactly (Σ T_B = 4 × 54,000, Σ T_A = 4 × 20,400, R̂ = 45/17).

**Stages.** Before step 1 the script runs, as stages, the own scripts of the mechanism beads it depends on that
this rehearsal exercises, each of which must pass: `scripts/e2e/tenfold_disqualification.sh` (N39b-01),
`tenfold_wording_lint.sh` (N39b-15), `tenfold_scorer.sh` (N39b-19), `tenfold_three_arm.sh` (N39b-43) and
`tenfold_params_block.sh` (N39b-33). The steps below then exercise those mechanisms together on the rehearsal
gate; a stage's failure fails this script and is named in its summary.

**Steps** (each `e2e_step`; every command through `e2e_cmd`, every expected refusal through
`e2e_expect_refusal` or a slug assertion, every planted failure through `e2e_expect_caught`).

1. `roles`: create three sessions (builder, advocate, adjudicator) with disjoint identities; assert a session
   holding two roles is refused. First run `scripts/e2e/tenfold_adjudicator_decision.sh` (N39b-27's M2 script) as
   a stage. Then the adjudicating session is set up as the fail-closed default of OPEN-TENFOLD-2 (N39b-27):
   create it, assert its access set, attempt a write to each protected target class (an arm's directory, the
   repository, the sealed store) and log each attempt with its target path class and the expected and observed
   refusal, enter the session's action log in the gate's event log, and assert that `self-adjudicated` is derived
   from the role registry and carried on every ratio of the rehearsal report. The advocate sessions follow
   OPEN-TENFOLD-3 (N39b-28): assert eligibility (a planted identity with one commit to this system is refused),
   the derived label `rival: internally advocated`, and that a tuning log exceeding its stratum's budget is
   flagged.
2. `seal`: run the six sealing steps in order on the rehearsal suite (publish class descriptions and
   development suite; commit suite and protocol hashes with the parameter block's digest; tune; publish each
   arm's freeze manifest; record the pre-analysis; open). Assert each event-log entry has a hash and a time;
   that H per stratum follows the rule of OPEN-TENFOLD-5 (N39b-30) from the synthetic arms' development times
   (t_med, H₀, H, horizon cap at least 10); and that the parameter block's digest (N39b-33) is in the suite-hash
   entry and a later change to the block is refused.
3. `envelope`: on a second scratch registration, so that the main rehearsal keeps its full scope, plant an
   approval of half the rehearsal's estimated cost (OPEN-TENFOLD-4, N39b-29): assert that scheduling stops at
   the cap, that the excess campaign is refused with FROB-REF-761 before it starts, and that the report lists
   the omitted cell as `not run` with the reason.
4. `controls-before-open`: run the canary positive controls; assert all found and no decoy hit.
5. `run`: interleave the three synthetic arms over 24 families, c = 1, in the counterbalanced run plan of the
   protocol controls (N39b-38) generated from the seed; assert the plan's balance (each arm first equally often
   within a block, arms interleaved in time), that the executed order equals the plan (from the event log), and
   transcript isolation (no arm's workspace can read another's); cards are shown to an arm only at its clock
   start (asserted from the event log); the agent caps are equal across arms and a campaign under a planted
   agent-revision mismatch is refused (OPEN-TENFOLD-6, N39b-31).
6. `close`: close the gate; assert that an open campaign blocks closing, and that no paired time, per-world
   outcome or transcript was released to any arm before every arm closed (blind release of N39b-38).
7. `audit`: integrity audit and invariant auditor; assert pass on the clean run.
8. `score`: run the scorer once; assert (replay mode, exact) R̂ = 45/17, the completion-ceiling value 3.75 per
   block, 20 of 24 families above 5, the verdict `withdrawn` (R̂ < 3 precedes the guard); (live mode) R̂ within
   2% of 2.647. Assert the scorer run twice gives identical bytes.
9. `three-arm`: stratum two in three replay variants, each with T_B = 100, T_F = 10 time units and C_B = 50,
   C_F = 5 GPU-hours (arms A, B and C over the same families). (a) *tie*: T_W = 28, C_W = 14, arm C with
   RMT_C ÷ RMT_A = 3: assert r_T = 4/5, r_C = 4/5, the tie reading by the recovery prong and a tie record with
   `tied = true`. (b) *near miss*: T_W = 28, C_W = 20, and arm C accepted on 22 of the 24 missions arm A
   accepted with RMT_C ÷ RMT_A = 7/5: assert r_T = 4/5, r_C = 2/3, *scope differs* on the ablation prong, the
   reading `mixed` and `tied = false` (this variant is what catches a reader that fires on r_T alone or that
   reads the tie on the point estimate when scopes differ). (c) *no tie*: T_W = 60, C_W = 30: assert
   r_T = r_C = 4/9, the reading "engine is a source of the gain" and `tied = false`.
10. `outcomes`: apply the outcome engine; compare sentences with golden files byte for byte; assert an ADR draft
    per confirmatory cell, and that the outcome rows assigned are `withdrawn` on stratum one and the tie row on
    variant (a) of stratum two, with no tie row on variants (b) and (c) (the readings of N39b-05 on which the
    kill gates act).
11. `report`: generate the report; run the wording lint on it (pass) and on a planted sentence "ten-fold on
    legged-quad" (refused).
12. `drills`: for each planted failure re-run the affected steps and assert the outcome. The sub-step
    `false-cert-drill` (plant `false-cert`; N39b-03): arm disqualified in exactly the cells using the proof kind.
    The sub-step `leak-drill` (N39b-02, N39b-04) in its bounded variant (plant `canary`: cell void, family
    retired, other arm unchanged) and its unbounded variant (plant `log-gap`: extent unbounded, gate void). Then
    `ledger-open` (3% unattributed: flagged, kept in the ratio, excluded from ceilings); `late-freeze` (run
    refused); `four-family` (confirmatory cell refused as unreachable); `monitor-verdict` (fixed-n verdict at a
    stopping time refused); `adjudicator-in-tuning` (disqualified); `arm-changed` and `record-edited` (detected,
    scoped).
13. `rotate`: retire the rehearsal families; assert a second sealing of one is refused.

**What runs after this script, on its record.** The kill-gate decisions KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED (N39b-06) and
KILL-TENFOLD-TIE (N39b-07) and the side decisions of N39b-11 depend on the gate record bead BD-317, which
depends on this bead, so they cannot be steps of this script. Each has its own script (`tenfold_kill_speed.sh`,
`tenfold_kill_tie.sh`, `tenfold_side_decisions.sh`) that runs this script in replay mode with `--keep-gate
<dir>` and acts on the kept gate directory: the re-plan and claims-registry changes, the ADRs, `decisions.json`.
The decision inputs that closing the gate writes for other subsystems (BD-317 step 13: arm B's `train` share per
class with its interval, the family-certificate table, arm C's turnaround on the family strata, the adapter
latency or `not fired`) are BD-317's, so they are asserted present, complete and digested by the `check-inputs`
step of `tenfold_side_decisions.sh` (N39b-11) on the kept gate directory, not by a step of this script.

**Logging.** Every line through the shared helper: `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command,
exit code, wall time, the seed; measured values with units and thresholds as `metric` (R̂ against 3 and 10, guard
count against 17, r_T against 0.8, unattributed share against 2%, H per stratum against the horizon cap 10,
advocate budget and spend per stratum against the floor of 40 device-hours scaled to the rehearsal, estimated,
approved and spent device-seconds in `envelope`, plan balance metrics against their bounds); the event-log
digest before and after each
step; record digests wherever state is compared; on failure the first differing value and the paths of the gate
directory and golden file. The final `summary` gives assertions passed and failed, controls caught and missed,
and `gates: [{id: "GATE-TENFOLD-06", measured_ok: …, controls_caught: …}]` with `gates_claimable: false`.
Rehearsal content is not private, so nothing is redacted; canary values are still never logged.

**Idempotence.** The script works only in its scratch directory with its own gate directory and keys derived
from the seed; a second run with the same seed produces the same assertions (replay mode: the same bytes). With
`--keep-gate <dir>` (replay mode only) the closed, scored rehearsal gate directory is copied to `<dir>` for the
post-gate scripts named above and for the kit builder's `tenfold_kit_build.sh` (N39b-18, M7); nothing else is
kept.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script exits 0 on a clean tree in replay mode and in live mode, and exits non-zero when any single
      mechanism bead's behaviour is broken (checked with one mutant per step).
- [ ] All ten plants are caught; a plant not caught fails the script and is named in the summary.
- [ ] Exact assertions hold in replay mode; live mode stays within its 2% tolerance on three consecutive runs.
- [ ] The log passes the harness's log linter; the summary lists GATE-TENFOLD-06 as touched and not claimable.
- [ ] Running time under 30 minutes within the default compute class (one core group, no device).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The fixtures have their own checks in `cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::rehearsal_fixtures`:
`card_scaled_by_720_preserves_ratios`; `synthetic_arms_are_labelled`; `golden_sentences_match_parameter_block`.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. It is run by `scripts/e2e/run-all.sh --label tenfold --milestone M3`, and again by the
adjudicating session immediately before the real suite is opened.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers asserted: R̂ = 2.647; per-family ratios 6, 7, 6.857, 7.714, 0.8125, 9; 20 of 24 above 5; r_T = 0.8
at T_W = 0.28·T_B; r_C = 2/3 at C_W = 20 of C_B = 50 with C_F = 5; 4/9 at T_W = 60. Negative controls: the ten plants, which include the seven the harness must always catch.
Metamorphic relations: permuting family order leaves R̂ and the verdict unchanged; scaling every time and H by
the same factor leaves R̂, the per-family ratios and the verdict unchanged (the 1/720 scaling is one instance).
Determinism lanes: replay mode twice on one machine with equal bytes; debug and release builds.

## Considerations

- Live-mode timing on a loaded host can exceed the 2% tolerance; the script records host load and retries the
  timing steps once before failing, logging both attempts.
- Synthetic arms cannot rehearse agent behaviour or incumbent tooling; those are rehearsed by the arm beads on
  development missions.
- The rehearsal suite's families are development-pool families and are never candidates for sealing.
