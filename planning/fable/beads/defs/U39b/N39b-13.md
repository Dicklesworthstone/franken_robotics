## Background

The tournament is the measurement the whole program points at: every stratum of the sealed mission suite that
has a product to run, on sealed families that no earlier gate used, against the strongest composed rival, with
the result published whatever it is. The gate GATE-TENFOLD-07 (claim kind: Performance; M7) passes when the
report is published with its ledgers and its replication kit, whatever it says; if it is not met, M7 does not
exit and no comparative claim is made. This bead is the conduct of the tournament: the fixed order of work, the
freeze of every pin for its length, the rules that void it, and the record. No mechanism is killed at M7; three
written rules are applied (the decision rule and its wording per range of the ratio, the tie rule, and
disqualification by one false certificate).

Why every pin is frozen. A ratio of times measured across a move of a pinned dependency is not one measurement:
the arms would have run on different software. So every pin of every arm and of every sibling library, and the
toolchain, are frozen for the tournament's length.

Why it is never stopped on an interim number. The published record is worth its compute, and a tournament
stopped when an interim ratio looks decided is a fixed-n study that peeked. The per-cell monitor reports and
raises operational alarms (ledger closure, excluded pairs, a leak signal); its futility stop is disabled.

## Scope

In: the conduct of the real tournament: performing the ten steps in order on the sealed suite, from a passing
go or no-go checklist of the rehearsal (N39b-17); writing the tournament's freeze record at step 5 and running
the watcher before every campaign; the run schedule's constraints; re-running what a breach voids;
application of the outcome rules at M7 to produce each cell's verdict, and the M7 decision entries of the two
per-cell kill gates read here; what the row of GATE-TENFOLD-07 cites (the event log's digest, the freeze record,
the report digest and the kit digest); the M7 row of GATE-TENFOLD-13.

Out, with the bead that has it:

- the mechanisms of conduct: the enforcement of the ten-step order, the freeze record's content and its
  watcher, the classification of breaches by extent with the void records (`tenfold.protocol-breach`), and the
  refusal of a stop request: N39bp3-01, on which this bead and the rehearsal both depend;

- the freeze of the stack itself: the `[freeze]` record of `franken-stack.lock`, `check-stack` in frozen mode,
  the trains' refusal while frozen, the harness's refusal of a run built under another receipt (`FROB-REF-890`),
  pooling by receipt, and the opening and closing of the freeze as recorded acts: N33b-03. This bead's freeze
  record cites that record and adds the items that are not the stack; its watcher calls `check-stack` for the
  stack and holds no second comparison of pins;
- the program gate GATE-TENFOLD-07: the check that the published report, the kit and the ledger are complete,
  that the freeze window was clean and that each cell's verdict equals the written rule's assignment, the entry
  of the gate's row, and the positioning that follows: N40-16, which depends on this bead. This bead enters no
  row of that gate;
- the suite (BD-428), the rule (BD-429), the arms (BD-430, BD-431), adjudication (BD-432), publication mechanics
  (BD-433), the kit (N39b-18, N39b-20), the two-week dress rehearsal (N39b-17).

## Technical approach

**Start.** The tournament starts only from the rehearsal's `go-no-go.json` (N39b-17) with every item `pass`;
step 1 is refused otherwise, naming the failed items.

**Order of work.** Each step is an entry in the gate's event log with a hash and a time, entered with
`frob-tenfold tournament step` (N39bp3-01); a step cannot be entered before its predecessor.

1. *Refresh the rival audit.* Re-pin every incumbent by revision, re-read its documented capabilities and
   licence, re-freeze warm configurations (the M0 procedure repeated at the tournament's date).
2. *Publish the enlarged development pool*, which now holds the families the partial gate retired.
3. *Commit the suite and protocol hashes* (with the parameter block's digest).
4. *Tune*: arms are tuned on the development pool only; advocates get at least the compute the builders logged
   on that pool and never under 40 device-hours per stratum.
5. *Freeze and publish every arm* (content-addressed manifests, the advocate's signed statement, the list of
   what more budget would have added); complete the pre-sealing audit of frozen arms (N39b-37).
6. *Record the pre-analysis and name at most four confirmatory cells* (BD-429); each cell declares a
   finite-suite or population result.
7. *Run with the arms interleaved*: every stratum that has a product to run (`facility` only if its own gate
   passed, otherwise `not run`); matched devices; card assignment and run order randomized and counterbalanced;
   a mission card shown to an arm only when its clock starts.
8. *Close and reveal*: audits (integrity, certificates, leaks), then release of paired results and keys.
9. *Publish the report and the kit*: one table per cell (R̂, [L, U], verdict, completion and coverage per arm,
   the tie test against arm C, N* and S for warm cells, the adjudication label) and one phase ledger per arm per
   cell, with the six ceilings evaluated on the rival's ledger; the recovery ratios r_T and r_C with 95%
   intervals for every stratum that has a ratio; the count of certificates that changed a decision.
10. *External re-run of the kit* (level 1 recompute and level 2 sampled re-run).

**Adjudication rule (DESIGN of the plan).** Adjudication at M7 is external, or the word "ten-fold" is not used
until the external replication has passed; until then every ratio is labelled `self-adjudicated`, and a result
whose kit has not been re-run is labelled `unreplicated`.

**The freeze record.** At step 5, with the stack freeze opened by N33b-03 at this step, this bead writes the
tournament's freeze record with `frob-tenfold tournament freeze-record` (N39bp3-01: the stack's campaign and
frozen receipt exactly as the `[freeze]` table holds them, the stack lock's digest, every arm's manifest hash,
the comparator registry's digest, the agent model revision and caps, the hardware fingerprints), and the watcher
`frob-tenfold tournament watch` verifies it before every campaign start and at close (`check-stack` in frozen
mode for the stack; digests for the rest).

**Breach rules.** A tournament is voided and re-run only for a breach of protocol: (a) a pin moved during the
freeze; (b) sealed material read by an arm; (c) an arm changed after its freeze. Each is measured by its extent
and recorded as `tenfold.protocol-breach` by N39bp3-01's rules (the spanned runs and their cells for a pin
moved with the freeze open; the touched cells for a bounded leak; the changed arm's later cells; the whole
tournament only for an aborted freeze, an unattributable run or an unbounded leak). This bead re-runs what is
void, a cell or the tournament, on a rotated suite of fresh families. Nothing else voids it: not a low ratio,
not a disqualification, not a harness fault.

**Rules applied at M7.** The outcome engine is applied to every confirmatory cell (N39b-05); KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED
and KILL-TENFOLD-TIE are read per stratum by the procedures of N39b-06 and N39b-07 with the monitor's stop
disabled; a class withdrawn at M3 is measured here on fresh families and its status replaced by the M7 reading;
BET-13 is met if at least one named stratum is `ten-fold`. After publication the product is described by its
measured ratio per stratum, and by auditability where the ratio is under 3.

**The M7 decisions, in the one form of N40-03.** KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED and KILL-TENFOLD-TIE are decided per cell
with `reread = ["M7"]` (N40-28). This bead writes `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED-M7.md` and
`docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-TIE-M7.md`, one section per stratum, and appends to each row's `decision` array in
`registries/gates.toml` one entry per stratum `{exit = "M7", scope = "cell:<stratum>", outcome, adr, date,
session, receipts}` with the outcome the procedure of N39b-06 or N39b-07 gives, a `not-fired` entry citing at
least one receipt under `receipts/gates/`; a stratum declared `not run` gets `not-reached` when its capability
was not funded (`facility` without its own gate), otherwise `deferred` with `deferred_to` and its reason. The M3
entries are kept and never edited; the latest entry per cell is in force.

**Record.** This bead closes with four things on file for the gate: the event log's digest, the freeze record,
the report digest and the kit digest. GATE-TENFOLD-07's row cites them; the row is entered once, by the gate
check of N40-16 run from a fresh-context session, not by this bead. GATE-TENFOLD-13's M7 row cites r_T, r_C and
the ablations on the sealed suite.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Step 1 was entered only after the rehearsal's `go-no-go.json` had every item `pass`.
- [ ] The ten steps appear in the event log in order, each with a hash and a time.
- [ ] No pin, toolchain or manifest digest differs between step 5 and close, as the watcher's record of every
      campaign start shows; any breach is recorded with its extent by N39bp3-01's rules, and what it voided was
      re-run on fresh families.
- [ ] The freeze record's stack entries equal the `[freeze]` table of the stack lock (campaign, receipt), and the
      watcher's stack verification is `check-stack` in frozen mode (N33b-03).
- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED-M7.md` and `docs/adr/ADR-KILL-TENFOLD-TIE-M7.md` exist, and each row holds
      one M7 entry per stratum with `scope = "cell:<stratum>"`, the M3 entries unchanged.
- [ ] No cell was stopped on an interim number; no monitor output entered a verdict.
- [ ] The report has, for every stratum run, the per-cell table and one closed phase ledger per arm, and is
      published with its kit whatever the ratios are.
- [ ] No sealed family of the tournament was used by the partial gate; all are retired afterwards.
- [ ] The word "ten-fold" appears only for cells with that verdict and only under external adjudication or
      after an external replication.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The tests of the conduct mechanisms (step order, freeze record, watcher, breach extents, stop refusal) are
N39bp3-01's. `cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::tournament_m7`: `go_no_go_required` (a checklist with one
item `fail` or one item missing → step 1 refused, naming it); `m3_family_cannot_be_sealed_at_m7`;
`withdrawn_class_is_remeasured` (a class `withdrawn` at M3 is run on fresh families and its status is the M7
reading); `m7_decision_entries_appended` (an M7 entry per stratum is appended to each kill-gate row with its
scope and outcome, the M3 entry byte-identical afterwards; a `not-fired` entry without a receipt is refused).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_tournament.sh`, owned by this bead (header `bead=N39b-13 milestone=M7 labels=tenfold,e2e
gates=GATE-TENFOLD-07,GATE-TENFOLD-13 needs=cuda,frobd,mcp,second-host plants=go-no-go-fail,m3-family`, with
`--approved-envelope`). Before the tournament it runs `scripts/e2e/tenfold_tournament_conduct.sh` (N39bp3-01) as
a stage and asserts the two plants on scratch copies (a `go-no-go.json` with one failed item: step 1 refused,
naming it; a family retired by the partial gate offered for sealing: `tenfold.family-reused`). It then reads the
rehearsal's `go-no-go.json` (N39b-17, which walked all ten steps on the development pool with each breach
planted) and drives the real tournament with the same commands: each step entered with `frob-tenfold tournament
step`, the freeze record at step 5, the watcher before every campaign start and at close, the outcome engine
and the M7 decision entries, the report and the kit. Logged as NDJSON through the STD-E2E helper to
`target/e2e-logs/tenfold_tournament/<utc>.ndjson` (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line,
exit code, wall time, and the evidence block of every `frob` call by arm A): each step's event-log entry hash,
each watcher run and what it compared, every refusal and every `tenfold.protocol-breach` with its extent, each
cell's verdict and each decision entry, the digests of the event log, freeze record, report and kit; on failure
the first differing value and the artifact paths; a final `summary` naming GATE-TENFOLD-07 and -13 with the four
digests the gate's row cites (entered by N40-16, never by this script). Logs carry no sealed card content and no
canary values.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: at most four confirmatory cells; 18 of 24 at the first Holm step with four cells; 24 families per
cell by default, 32 or more for a population cell. Negative controls: a pin moved during the freeze, an arm
changed after its freeze and a request to stop a cell on a monitor value are planted and caught by N39bp3-01's
script and again in the rehearsal (N39b-17); here, a family reused from M3 and a go-or-no-go checklist with a
failed item.

## Considerations

- Schedule: the milestone is planned at six weeks including a two-week freeze and rehearsal.
- Compute: 24 families per cell (32 or more for a population cell) on both machines needs an approved campaign
  class per run and a pretraining class where an arm builds a standing asset; without it, fewer strata run and
  each omitted stratum is declared `not run` with the reason.
- Risks: the tournament cannot confirm a true ratio of ten for lack of power or because unfinished missions cap
  the ratio at 1/q; no neutral party is named, so nobody outside can confirm the result.
- A defect found in a frozen arm mid-tournament voids the affected cells, which are re-run at a later gate.
