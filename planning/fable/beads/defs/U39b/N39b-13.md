## Background

The tournament is the measurement the whole program points at: every stratum of the sealed mission suite that
has a product to run, on sealed families that no earlier gate used, against the strongest composed rival, with
the result published whatever it is. The gate GATE-TENFOLD-07 (claim kind: Performance; M7) passes when the
report is published with its ledgers and its replication kit, whatever it says; if it is not met, M7 does not
exit and no comparative claim is made. This bead is the conduct of the tournament: the fixed order of work, the
freeze of every pin for its length, the rules that void it, and the record. No mechanism is killed at M7; three
written rules are applied (the decision rule and its wording per range of the ratio, the tie rule, and
disqualification by one false certificate).

Why every pin is frozen. A ratio of times measured across a move of a pinned dependency is not one measurement:
the arms would have run on different software. So every pin of every arm and of every sibling library, and the
toolchain, are frozen for the tournament's length.

Why it is never stopped on an interim number. The published record is worth its compute, and a tournament
stopped when an interim ratio looks decided is a fixed-n study that peeked. The per-cell monitor reports and
raises operational alarms (ledger closure, excluded pairs, a leak signal); its futility stop is disabled.

## Scope

In: the ten-step order; the freeze record and its verification; the run schedule's constraints; breach
detection and the void-and-re-run rule; application of the outcome rules at M7; the gate record; the M7 row of
GATE-TENFOLD-13. Out: the suite (BD-428), the rule (BD-429), the arms (BD-430, BD-431), adjudication (BD-432),
publication mechanics (BD-433), the kit (N39b-18, N39b-20), the two-week dress rehearsal (N39b-17).

## Technical approach

**Order of work.** Each step is an entry in the gate's event log with a hash and a time; a step cannot be
entered before its predecessor.

1. *Refresh the rival audit.* Re-pin every incumbent by revision, re-read its documented capabilities and
   licence, re-freeze warm configurations (the M0 procedure repeated at the tournament's date).
2. *Publish the enlarged development pool*, which now holds the families the partial gate retired.
3. *Commit the suite and protocol hashes* (with the parameter block's digest).
4. *Tune*: arms are tuned on the development pool only; advocates get at least the compute the builders logged
   on that pool and never under 40 device-hours per stratum.
5. *Freeze and publish every arm* (content-addressed manifests, the advocate's signed statement, the list of
   what more budget would have added); complete the pre-sealing audit of frozen arms (N39b-37).
6. *Record the pre-analysis and name at most four confirmatory cells* (BD-429); each cell declares a
   finite-suite or population result.
7. *Run with the arms interleaved*: every stratum that has a product to run (`facility` only if its own gate
   passed, otherwise `not run`); matched devices; card assignment and run order randomized and counterbalanced;
   a mission card shown to an arm only when its clock starts.
8. *Close and reveal*: audits (integrity, certificates, leaks), then release of paired results and keys.
9. *Publish the report and the kit*: one table per cell (R̂, [L, U], verdict, completion and coverage per arm,
   the tie test against arm C, N* and S for warm cells, the adjudication label) and one phase ledger per arm per
   cell, with the six ceilings evaluated on the rival's ledger; the recovery ratios r_T and r_C with 95%
   intervals for every stratum that has a ratio; the count of certificates that changed a decision.
10. *External re-run of the kit* (level 1 recompute and level 2 sampled re-run).

**Adjudication rule (DESIGN of the plan).** Adjudication at M7 is external, or the word "ten-fold" is not used
until the external replication has passed; until then every ratio is labelled `self-adjudicated`, and a result
whose kit has not been re-run is labelled `unreplicated`.

**The freeze record.** At step 5 the record lists: the stack lock's digest (every sibling commit and the
toolchain), every arm's manifest hash, the comparator registry's digest, the agent model revision and caps, and
the hardware fingerprints. A watcher verifies all of them before every campaign start and at close.

**Breach rules.** A tournament is voided and re-run only for a breach of protocol: (a) a pin moved during the
freeze; (b) sealed material read by an arm; (c) an arm changed after its freeze. (b) with a bounded extent voids
only the touched cells under the leak rule; an unbounded extent voids the tournament. A void is recorded with
its evidence (`tenfold.protocol-breach`), and the re-run uses a rotated suite of fresh families. Nothing else
voids it: not a low ratio, not a disqualification (which removes one arm from scoped cells), not a harness fault
(repaired by re-running the pair once; a second fault excludes the pair, counted).

**Rules applied at M7.** The outcome engine is applied to every confirmatory cell (N39b-05); KILL-TENFOLD-SPEED
and KILL-TENFOLD-TIE are read per stratum by the procedures of N39b-06 and N39b-07 with the monitor's stop
disabled; a class withdrawn at M3 is measured here on fresh families and its status replaced by the M7 reading;
BET-13 is met if at least one named stratum is `ten-fold`. After publication the product is described by its
measured ratio per stratum, and by auditability where the ratio is under 3.

**Record.** GATE-TENFOLD-07's row cites the report digest, the kit digest and the freeze record; it is entered
by a fresh-context session. GATE-TENFOLD-13's M7 row cites r_T, r_C and the ablations on the sealed suite.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ten steps appear in the event log in order, each with a hash and a time.
- [ ] No pin, toolchain or manifest digest differs between step 5 and close; a planted pin move is detected
      before the next campaign starts and voids the tournament.
- [ ] No cell was stopped on an interim number; no monitor output entered a verdict.
- [ ] The report has, for every stratum run, the per-cell table and one closed phase ledger per arm, and is
      published with its kit whatever the ratios are.
- [ ] No sealed family of the tournament was used by the partial gate; all are retired afterwards.
- [ ] The word "ten-fold" appears only for cells with that verdict and only under external adjudication or
      after an external replication.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::tournament`: `steps_enforce_order` (step 6 before step 5 refused);
`pin_move_voids` (one sibling commit changed in the stack lock); `interim_stop_is_disabled`;
`disqualification_does_not_void`; `m3_family_cannot_be_sealed_at_m7`; `withdrawn_class_is_remeasured`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_tournament_rehearsal.sh` (N39b-17) walks all ten steps on the development pool with planted
breaches. The real tournament is driven by the same commands; its log records each step's event-log digest, the
freeze verification before each campaign, and every refusal.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: at most four confirmatory cells; 18 of 24 at the first Holm step with four cells; 24 families per
cell by default, 32 or more for a population cell. Negative controls: a pin moved during the freeze; an arm
changed after its freeze; a request to stop a cell on a monitor value; a family reused from M3.

## Considerations

- Schedule: the milestone is planned at six weeks including a two-week freeze and rehearsal.
- Compute: 24 families per cell (32 or more for a population cell) on both machines needs an approved campaign
  class per run and a pretraining class where an arm builds a standing asset; without it, fewer strata run and
  each omitted stratum is declared `not run` with the reason.
- Risks: the tournament cannot confirm a true ratio of ten for lack of power or because unfinished missions cap
  the ratio at 1/q; no neutral party is named, so nobody outside can confirm the result.
- A defect found in a frozen arm mid-tournament voids the affected cells, which are re-run at a later gate.
