## Background

The ten-fold test produces verdicts, and each verdict licenses exact words. `measured` licenses a ratio with its
interval and forbids the word "ten-fold" for that class anywhere. `ten-fold` on fewer than 32 independent
mission families licenses "ten-fold on the sealed suite" and no wording about missions in general. A ratio
adjudicated by the project's own session is `self-adjudicated`, and the words "independently accepted" and
"ten-fold" wait until an external party has re-run the replication kit. These rules only bind if something
enforces them on every document the project publishes. This bead is that enforcement, and the fixed block of
statements about what the test does not measure, which every report of the test prints.

Why a lint and not a style guide: the project is written largely by agents, and the easiest sentence to write
after a good number is a stronger one than the number supports. A refusal is cheaper than a retraction.

## Scope

In: the limits block; the wording rules; the lint tool and its registration in the claims-wording registry; the
citation syntax that ties a sentence to a report cell. Out: the sentences themselves (N39b-05); the report
(N39b-14); the general claims registry (its own bead).

## Technical approach

**The limits block** (printed verbatim in every report of the test; DESIGN: stored once in
`crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/limits.md` and included by the report generator).

1. *Reality.* Acceptance happens in simulation. The adjudicating simulator is a third model, not the world.
   Every report prints `reality: no-claim` unless a measured reality band covers the mission.
2. *How good the best design is.* The clock stops at the first accepted answer. Quality beyond the acceptance
   threshold is not scored, except where a mission states an optimality gap.
3. *People.* Both arms are driven by one agent. Human engineering time, learning curves and usability for a
   person are outside the test, and the ratio is conditional on the pinned agent.
4. *Money.* Cost columns are reported and no cost claim is made.
5. *Systems that were not run.* Nothing is inferred about an incumbent, a version or a configuration outside the
   frozen arms, or about missions outside the sealed population. Manipulation with hands, flight and soft bodies
   are outside the suite.
6. *The cost of building either system.* Standing assets for a mission class are charged; the engineering that
   produced this system or an incumbent is not.
7. *Step rate, rendering and safety.* Throughput belongs to the performance program. Rendering is outside every
   claim here. Nothing in this test is a safety statement about a machine.

**Wording rules.** A *claim sentence* is a sentence outside a block marked as protocol text that contains a
trigger term and names this system, a stratum or a mission class.

| Rule | Trigger terms | Allowed only when |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | "ten-fold", "tenfold", "10x", "10×", "ten times", "order of magnitude" | The cited cell's verdict is `ten-fold`, and adjudication was external or an external replication of the kit has passed |
| W2 | Population wording with W1's terms ("on quadruped missions", "for robot design", any wording not limited to the sealed suite) | The cell declared a population result, has at least the family count its registered bound requires, and the lower bound on the paired contrast is nonnegative; otherwise the sentence must read "ten-fold on the sealed suite" |
| W3 | "independently accepted", "independently adjudicated", "neutral" | Adjudication was external; under `self-adjudicated` the words wait for an external re-run of the kit |
| W4 | Any ratio of times against a rival | The sentence carries the regime ("cold", or "warm at N amendments") and the interval; a warm ratio carries S and N*; the warm limit is never quoted as the ratio |
| W5 | Any speed claim for a class | The class is not `withdrawn`; for a withdrawn class only the measured sentence and the non-speed capabilities may be printed |
| W6 | "decisions per hour", "designs per hour" | The sentence cites the rate ratio, says it is a different estimand, and does not use W1's terms |
| W7 | W1's terms against "the incumbent" | The ratio cited is against the stronger of the declared incumbent and the composed incumbent |
| W8 | A comparative naming a system, version or configuration | That system is a frozen arm of the cited gate |
| W9 | "cheaper", "less compute" as a claim; "safe", "certified for hardware"; "matches reality" | Never (cost is reported, not claimed; no safety statement; `reality: no-claim`) |
| W10 | W1's terms | Not under the label `unreplicated` |

**Citation.** A claim sentence must carry a reference `[tenfold:<gate>/<stratum>/<regime>]` (DESIGN). The lint
resolves it to the cell of the published report whose digest is recorded in the gate's row and checks the rules
against that cell's verdict, result type, labels and numbers. A claim sentence with no reference is refused.

**The lint.** `frob-tenfold lint-wording <paths…>` (DESIGN): segments Markdown, HTML and plain text into
sentences; skips fenced blocks and regions between `<!-- protocol -->` markers (so that the rules themselves can
be written down); applies the table; exits 3 with one refusal record per violation (`tenfold.word-not-earned`,
with the file, line, rule, the cell's actual verdict and the sentence the outcome rule prescribes as the first
repair). It runs on the README, `docs/`, the changelog, release notes and every generated report, and is
registered as a rule set of the claims-wording registry so that the generated status table is checked too.

**Fail-closed default.** Before any gate has a published report, every claim sentence with a trigger term is
refused: the word cannot appear as a claim before it is earned.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every report of the test contains the seven statements verbatim and the `reality:` line.
- [ ] Each rule W1 to W10 has a passing sentence and a refused sentence in the test corpus.
- [ ] The rules' own text and this bead's examples pass when inside protocol markers and are refused outside.
- [ ] On the rehearsal gate (verdict `withdrawn`) the planted sentence "ten-fold on legged-quad" is refused with
      the prescribed sentence as its first repair.
- [ ] The lint is part of the documentation check run at every milestone exit from M3.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::wording`: `w1_requires_verdict_and_external_or_replicated`;
`w2_finite_suite_wording` ("ten-fold on quadruped missions" refused for a 24-family cell; "ten-fold on the
sealed suite" accepted); `w3_self_adjudicated_blocks_independent`; `w4_ratio_needs_regime_and_interval`;
`w4_warm_limit_refused` ("8.6 times faster when warm" refused; "2.12 at 10 amendments, S = 4 h, N* = 5"
accepted); `w5_withdrawn_class`; `w6_rate_ratio_wording`; `w7_stronger_of_b_and_w`; `w8_unrun_system`;
`w9_never_terms`; `w10_unreplicated`; `protocol_markers_exempt`; `missing_reference_refused`;
`limits_block_verbatim` (digest of the included block equals the stored one).

### End-to-end script and logging

Step `report` of `scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh` (N39b-12) runs the lint on the generated report (pass) and
on a planted document with one violation per rule (ten refusals expected, in order). Logged: files scanned,
claim sentences found, refusals with rule ids, the first repair of each.

### Known answers and negative controls

Negative controls: a lint that matches only the literal "ten-fold" (must miss "10×" and fail); a lint that
ignores sentences without a reference (must fail `missing_reference_refused`); a report generated without the
limits block (must fail).

## Considerations

- Trigger lists will have false positives in prose about other subjects (for example a mechanism that is "ten
  times" slower); such a sentence either is not a claim sentence (it names no stratum or class) or gets a
  protocol marker with a reviewer's note. The cost of a false positive is a marker; the cost of a false
  negative is a public claim the record does not support.
- The lint reads published reports; it never decides a verdict.
- Under a ratio below 3 the product is positioned on replay, qualification, certificates and the evidence
  ledger; the lint then also refuses older speed sentences left in documents.
