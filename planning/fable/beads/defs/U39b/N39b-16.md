## Background

The epic that owns the tournament's decision rule (BD-429) is done only when a short plan exists and its pilot
has run: the reachable verdicts are enumerated before sampling, and a rule that cannot return one of its
verdicts at the planned sample is rejected. This bead is that pilot. It is a simulation on a CPU, cheap, with a
falsifier, and it has three parts. First, it re-runs the calculation that caused the rule to be rewritten, so
that the number is this repository's own: an earlier draft rule confirmed a true ratio of 10 in at most 18% of
simulated campaigns. Second, it reproduces the illustrative power table of the pooled rule under its stated
model. Third, it computes the binding pre-analysis for the tournament from measured development-pool times, with
censoring at the deadline and the multiplicity adjustment, which is what the freeze-and-seal gate requires to be
recorded before the suite opens.

Terms. *Pooled ratio* R̂ = Σ min(T_B, H) ÷ Σ min(T_A, H) over the pairs of a cell (B the rival, A this system, H
the deadline). *Per-family ratio* ρ_i: the same expression on family i's pairs. *Guard*: X = #{i : ρ_i > 5}
against a binomial(m, ½) tail at the cell's adjusted level. *Verdict*: `withdrawn` if R̂ < 3; `ten-fold` if
R̂ ≥ 10 and the guard rejects (with the other conditions, held true in this simulation); `measured` otherwise.
*True ratio*: E[min(T_B, H)] ÷ E[min(T_A, H)] of the simulated population.

## Scope

In: Part 2 (the power table of the pooled rule) and Part 3 (the binding pre-analysis) as simulations of this
bead; Part 1 as a re-execution, at the gate's commit, of the draft-rule simulation that already exists; the
pilot's record in the pilot register; the reachability check per planned cell; the script.

Out, with the bead that has it: the draft-rule simulation itself, that is its definition of the draft rule,
its model, its grid, its module `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/draft_rule.rs` and the M0 record of row 14
of the plan-time pilot ledger (N38a-11, a dependency of this bead; no second implementation is written here);
the estimator code (N39a-27 to N39a-32) and the pre-analysis machinery this pilot runs (N39a-33); the rare-tail
simulation of the population test (N39b-36); the decision to open a cell as descriptive (N39a-34).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/pilot_rule.rs` (DESIGN); seeded, counter-based random numbers so
that results are identical across thread counts.

**Part 1: the draft rule (re-executed, not re-implemented).** The draft rule is: on at least four of six
briefs, including both held-out ones, a ratio of medians of at least 10 with a lower bound above 5, over five
seeds per brief. Its simulation (lognormal run times; k = 5, 10, 20 seeds per arm and brief; true ratio R from
5 to 20; the lower bound's construction; 10⁵ campaigns per cell) is specified and implemented once, by N38a-11
at M0, as the module `tenfold/draft_rule.rs`, and its result is row 14 of the plan-time pilot ledger. Expected
(FACT, plan-time record, reproduced there): at R = 10 the rule confirms in at most 18% of campaigns, and in 0
to 11% with five seeds; it reaches 80% only near true ratios of 15 to 20. The cause: each brief needs its own
point estimate above 10, which a true 10 delivers half the time, and the requirement compounds across briefs
(¼ · 11/16 = 17.2% before the lower-bound condition; the unit test of that arithmetic is N38a-11's).

What this bead does with it: (1) calls that module from `pilot_rule.rs` at the gate's commit with the recorded
seed and asserts that the counts equal the row-14 record (cited by hash), so that the figure the epic's plan
cites is this repository's own and current; (2) feeds the draft rule through the reachability conditions of
Part 3 as a negative control, where it must fail "P(`ten-fold`) ≥ 0.8 at R = 15" for five seeds.

**Part 2: the illustrative table of the pooled rule.** Model (stated with the table): lognormal times, a
per-family difficulty spread of 0.5 in the logarithm, per-family log-ratio spread s, no censoring, one
confirmatory cell, 20,000 simulated gates per entry. Parametrization used here (DESIGN): for family i, log
difficulty d_i ~ N(0, 0.5²); each arm's own log noise ε_A,i, ε_B,i independent N(0, s²/2); T_A,i = exp(d_i +
ε_A,i) and T_B,i = R·exp(d_i + ε_B,i). Then ln ρ_i = ln R + ε_B,i − ε_A,i ~ N(ln R, s²), the population ratio of
means E[T_B] ÷ E[T_A] is R, and because the two arms' noise is exchangeable, ln(R̂ ÷ R) is symmetric about 0, so
the median of R̂ is R. Expected entries, tolerance ± 0.015 on probabilities:

| m | s | Guard needs | P(`ten-fold`) at R = 5 / 7 / 8.5 / 10 / 12 / 15 / 20 | P(`withdrawn`) at 2 / 3 / 5 | R at which P(`ten-fold`) = 0.8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | 0.5 | 17 of 24 | 0.000 / 0.002 / 0.08 / 0.50 / 0.94 / 1.00 / 1.00 | 1.00 / 0.50 / 0.000 | 11.25 |
| 24 | 0.8 | 17 of 24 | 0.000 / 0.03 / 0.20 / 0.49 / 0.83 / 0.98 / 1.00 | 0.98 / 0.50 / 0.005 | 12.0 |
| 12 | 0.8 | 10 of 12 | 0.002 / 0.05 / 0.19 / 0.39 / 0.65 / 0.89 / 0.98 | 0.93 / 0.50 / 0.03 | 13.75 |
| 6 | 0.8 | 6 of 6 | 0.006 / 0.05 / 0.12 / 0.23 / 0.38 / 0.57 / 0.77 | 0.86 / 0.49 / 0.08 | above 20 |
| 4 | any | unreachable | 0 at every R | 0.81 / 0.50 / 0.13 | never |

The parametrization matters. Under the one above, a simulation of 20,000 gates per entry reproduces every
probability of the table within the tolerance (for example 0.497 and 0.937 at R = 10 and 12 for m = 24, s = 0.5;
0.498 and 0.819 for s = 0.8; 0.816, 0.496 and 0.13 for m = 4), and the last column is the first ratio on a grid of
0.25 at which the power reaches 0.8. Putting the whole spread on arm B with a drift that keeps the ratio of means
at R (T_B,i = T_A,i·exp(ℓ_i), ℓ_i ~ N(ln R − s²/2, s²)) does not reproduce it (about 0.29 at R = 10 for m = 24,
s = 0.8, and P(`withdrawn`) above 0.5 at R = 3), because it puts the median per-family ratio at R·exp(−s²/2) and
the median of R̂ below R; that parametrization is a negative control of the unit tests. If the table does not
reproduce within tolerance, the discrepancy is recorded with both sets of values and the parametrization is
corrected before Part 3 is trusted; the table is a planning computation, not a result. Two readings the pilot
must confirm: between true ratios of 7 and 10 the word can be awarded by chance (up to a fifth of gates at 8.5),
and six families give a rule no better than the rejected one.

**Part 3: the binding pre-analysis.** For each planned cell of the tournament:
1. Fit, from development-pool times (including the partial gate's revealed families): the per-family log
   difficulty spread, the per-family log-ratio spread s, each arm's censoring rate at H, and the within-family
   campaign spread. HYPOTHESIS to check: s lies between 0.5 and 0.8 on the design strata.
2. Simulate 20,000 gates per true ratio at the planned m, c and H with censoring, the Holm-adjusted level for
   the planned number of confirmatory cells (with four cells the first step needs 18 of 24), and the completion
   non-inferiority condition.
3. Enumerate the reachable verdicts (a verdict is reachable if some outcome of the data yields it; the guard at
   m = 4 is not) and tabulate P(`ten-fold`) at R = 5, 7, 8.5, 10, 12, 15, 20 and P(`withdrawn`) at 2, 3, 5.
4. Check the four reachability conditions: P(`ten-fold`) ≥ 0.8 at R = 15; P(`withdrawn`) ≤ 0.05 at R = 5; the
   guard reachable at its adjusted level; the horizon cap H ÷ RMT_A on the development pool at least 10 (since
   R ≤ H ÷ RMT_A whatever the rival does).
5. Emit the pre-analysis record with its hash, for entry in the gate's event log before the suite opens.

**Falsifier of the pilot.** The planned design fails if, with the measured spread and censoring, any named
confirmatory cell misses a reachability condition. Fallback, named in advance: the cell is enlarged for this
gate if families exist, otherwise opened as `descriptive` and the report says so before any sealed run
(KILL-TENFOLD-REACH). Computed ceiling of the benefit: none on speed; the pilot's value is refusing a test that
cannot confirm its claim. Cost: CPU minutes within the default compute class. Tool: this crate at the gate's
commit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Part 1: the draft-rule module of N38a-11, called at the gate's commit with the recorded seed, gives the
      counts of the row-14 record (at most 18% at R = 10, and 0 to 11% with five seeds, or the difference that
      record states); a mismatch with the record, or a record whose status is `differs`, is written in the
      review log before the epic's plan cites the figure. No second simulation of the draft rule exists in
      this crate.
- [ ] Part 2 reproduces the table within tolerance or records and resolves the discrepancy.
- [ ] Part 3 produces, per planned cell, the reachable verdicts, the power figures and a pass or fail for each
      of the four conditions, and its record is hashed.
- [ ] The pilot's record is in the pilot register (`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml`, schema
      `frob.gauntlet.pilot/1`) with protocol, falsifier, fallback (`fallback.ships`: the cell enlarged or opened
      `descriptive`; `fallback.kill_gate`: KILL-TENFOLD-REACH), cost and result, its verdict one of
      `falsifier-holds`, `falsifier-rejected`, `inconclusive` or `void`, entered before the suite hash is
      committed.
- [ ] A planted four-family confirmatory cell is rejected as unreachable.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::pilot_rule`: `draft_rule_matches_row_14_record` (the module of
N38a-11 with the recorded seed reproduces the recorded counts; its own unit tests, among them
`draft_rule_binomial_core` with ¼ · 11/16 = 0.1719, live with that module);
`simulation_is_seed_and_thread_stable`; `guard_unreachable_at_four`; `withdrawal_probability_half_at_three`
(by the symmetry of ln(R̂ ÷ R) under exchangeable arm noise, within ± 0.015, at every m of the table);
`no_spread_is_exact` (s = 0 and no censoring: R̂ = R and every ρ_i = R in every gate, so P(`withdrawn`) is 1 at
R = 2 and 0 at R = 4, and P(`ten-fold`) is 1 at R = 12 and 0 at R = 8 for m ≥ 5; ratios away from 3 and 10, which
rounding could cross); `drifted_parametrization_misses_table` (the one-arm drifted parametrization above gives
P(`ten-fold`) below 0.40 at R = 10 for m = 24, s = 0.8, and P(`withdrawn`) above 0.53 at R = 3: the comparison
with the table must flag it); `horizon_cap_condition`
(H = 14,400 s, RMT_A = 1,800 s: cap 8, fails; RMT_A = 1,200 s: cap 12, passes);
`censoring_lowers_power` (a monotonicity check: more censoring of arm A never raises P(`ten-fold`)).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_decision_rule_pilot.sh` (header `# e2e: bead=N39b-16 milestone=M7
labels=tenfold,gauntlet,pilot gates=GATE-TENFOLD-04,KILL-TENFOLD-REACH,GATE-GAUNT-08 seed=<recorded> needs=cpu
plants=drop-censored,four-family-cell,draft-rule`): reads the pilot's registration row in
`crates/frob-conformance/registry/pilots.toml` read-only (registered before the suite hash); runs
`FROB_TEST_SEED=<seed> cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::pilot_rule` and the three parts; then calls `frob
eval preanalyze` (a read verb) on each planned cell's study plan in the verb's ten-fold plan mode (N39b-34; the
plan file carries the cell's registered rule, m and c, the margin 0.05 and the adjusted level: `frob eval
preanalyze --plan <cell plan.json> --json`, never the fixed-n flags, whose verdict tokens are not the ten-fold
verdicts) and
asserts agreement on the reachable verdicts; the three negative controls below run as plants, each asserted by
reason (the four-family cell by `FROB-REF-532`). Logged through the STD-E2E helper (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`, the exact command line, exit code, wall time): every table entry as a `metric` with its expected value and
tolerance and, for Part 2, the recorded discrepancy where there is one; each condition as an assertion with its
threshold; the fitted spreads with intervals; the record's hash; and the envelope's evidence block for the `frob`
call.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the table above; critical counts 17 of 24 and 18 of 24; the draft rule's 18%. Negative controls:
the draft rule itself (must fail the 0.8-at-15 condition for five seeds); a four-family cell; a mutant
simulation that drops censored campaigns (must raise P(`ten-fold`) and be caught by the monotonicity test).

## Considerations

- Every simulated figure depends on the lognormal model; the binding pre-analysis therefore also reports a
  bootstrap of the measured development-pool times themselves (families resampled) beside the model.
- No honest rule can confirm a true ratio of exactly 10 more than about half the time, because a fair
  measurement of 10 falls below 10 half the time; the pilot documents this rather than tuning it away.
- The pilot uses development data only; it never reads a sealed family.
