## Background

The tournament at M7 consumes sealed mission families that can never be sealed again and a compute budget that
needs the owner's approval. It is voided and re-run for any breach of protocol. The milestone's schedule
therefore includes a two-week freeze and rehearsal: with every pin frozen, the whole order of work is walked on
the enlarged development pool, with the real arms on real development missions at reduced size, the real
adjudication path and the real kit, and with each breach planted once. This bead is the script and its
procedure. It claims no gate (`gates_claimable: false`); it repeats the harness known-answer gate
(GATE-TENFOLD-03 is due again at M7 before the suite opens) by calling that battery as its first step.

How it differs from the M3 rehearsal (N39b-12): that script uses synthetic arms and second-scale deadlines to
test the harness; this one uses the frozen arms, the frozen pins, development missions at their real deadlines
(a reduced count), all three roles as separate sessions, and the publication path through the kit.

## Scope

In: the script; the reduced rehearsal design; the planted breaches; the rehearsal report; the go or no-go
checklist handed to the tournament. Out: the tournament itself (N39b-13); the mechanisms rehearsed.

## Technical approach

**Design of the rehearsal (DESIGN).** Per stratum that will run: 3 development families, c = 1, all arms,
interleaved, at the stratum's real H (so at most 3 × arms × H device-hours per stratum; with H = 4 h and three
arms, 36 device-hours in the worst case, inside an approved campaign class). The rehearsal's cells are
`descriptive` by construction (n < 10) and its report is labelled `synthetic: rehearsal`.

**Steps** (`scripts/e2e/tenfold_tournament_rehearsal.sh`; header `bead=N39b-17 milestone=M7 gates=GATE-TENFOLD-07
needs=cuda,frobd,mcp,second-host timeout_s=…` with `--approved-envelope`).

1. `known-answers`: run the harness known-answer and negative-control battery; stop on any failure.
2. `freeze-record`: write the freeze record (stack-lock digest, toolchain, arm manifests, comparator registry,
   agent pin, hardware fingerprints); assert the watcher verifies it.
3. `audit-refresh`: re-run the rival audit checks; each frozen arm reproduces its development-fixture figure
   within 10%.
4. `seal-rehearsal-suite`: the six sealing steps in order on rehearsal families drawn from the development pool,
   marked `rehearsal` so that they can never be confused with sealed families.
5. `preanalysis`: record a pre-analysis for the rehearsal cells; assert they are opened `descriptive` and that
   the report will say so before any run.
6. `run`: the interleaved, randomized, counterbalanced schedule; cards shown at clock start; fresh agent
   contexts; no human in the loop; the monitor in report-only mode.
7. `adjudicate`: acceptance by the adjudicating session through the real path; the adjudication dry run's
   planted false acceptance must be caught.
8. `close-reveal`: audits (integrity, certificates, canaries, access logs), then reveal.
9. `score-report`: scorer, three-arm statistics, outcome engine, report, wording lint.
10. `kit`: build the kit; level 1 on a clean second host reproduces every number bit for bit; level 2 re-runs
    one pair per stratum there and compares outcome and time (factor 1.5).
11. `breaches`: with `E2E_PLANT_FAILURE` in turn: `pin-moved` (a sibling commit changed in the stack lock:
    detected before the next campaign, rehearsal voided); `sealed-read` (an arm session reads a rehearsal card
    before its clock: leak recorded); `arm-changed` (one byte of an agent guide: detected, arm void);
    `interim-stop` (a request to stop a cell on the monitor's value: refused); `m3-family` (a family retired by
    the partial gate offered for sealing: refused); `private-kit-input` (refused).
12. `checklist`: emit `go-no-go.json`: each precondition of the tournament with pass or fail and its evidence.

**Go or no-go checklist (all must pass).** Known answers and controls; freeze record verified; audit figures
within 10%; six sealing steps in order; every campaign's ledger closed within 2% (or the count of `ledger-open`
runs is under 10% of runs, with the cause written); adjudication path caught its planted answer; level 1 bit
exact; level 2 within the factor in the sampled pairs; all six breaches caught; wording lint clean; compute
class approved for the tournament's estimated cost (the rehearsal's measured restricted mean times × the planned
family count).

**Logging.** Through the shared helper: every command with exit code and wall time; the envelope's evidence
block for every `frob` call made by arm A; measured values against thresholds (closure against 2%, audit
figures against 10%, level-2 time ratio against 1.5); digests of the event log, freeze record, report and kit;
on failure the first differing value and artifact paths. Logs carry no private-scene data and no canary values.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The script completes on the frozen tree with every step passing and writes the checklist.
- [ ] Each of the six planted breaches is caught by the mechanism that will guard the tournament.
- [ ] The rehearsal report is labelled as a rehearsal, carries no verdict other than `descriptive`, and passes
      the wording lint.
- [ ] The tournament's cost estimate is derived from rehearsal measurements and recorded with its interval.
- [ ] The rehearsal families are marked so that sealing one is refused afterwards.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::rehearsal`: `rehearsal_families_never_sealable`;
`checklist_fails_on_any_missing_item`; `cost_estimate_from_rmt` (RMT of 1.5 h, 1.0 h and 0.4 h at 24 families
gives 69.6 device-hours per cell).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script, run once at the start of the freeze and once at its end; both logs are kept with the
tournament's record.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the cost arithmetic (288 device-hours worst case, 69.6 at the stated times); the audit and kit
thresholds (10%; factor 1.5 in at least 90% of sampled pairs). Negative controls: the six breaches; the planted
false acceptance in adjudication; a checklist item deleted from the record (the checklist must fail).

## Considerations

- A rehearsal on three families says nothing about ratios; its numbers are never quoted as results.
- The freeze must hold between the two runs; a necessary fix found by the first run is applied before the
  freeze restarts, never during it.
- If the adjudicator is external, the rehearsal is also their acceptance test of the protocol document.
