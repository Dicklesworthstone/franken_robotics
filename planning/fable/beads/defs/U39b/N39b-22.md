## Background

GATE-TENFOLD-03 (claim kind: Known answer) is the precondition for opening any sealed suite: the harness
reproduces every known answer of the test exactly and catches every negative control. It is due at M3 before
the partial gate's suite opens and is repeated at M7 before the tournament's. If it is not met, the suite is not
opened. This bead is the test target and the script that measure it. The rows and the controls themselves are
data in two registries (N39-KA with labels TF-01 to TF-22 for the harness battery, plus the mechanism rows
TF-23 to TF-30, which their own beads assert and which are not part of this gate's battery; N39-CX with labels
TF-CX-01 to TF-CX-27); this bead runs them against the real harness and records the gate. A gate run in which any control was not caught is a
failure of the gate whatever the passing rows say.

## Scope

In: the module `tenfold_ka` of the known-answer target `g1_known_answers`, the script, the mapping from rows and
controls to harness commands, the gate receipt. Out: the registries' content; the pre-analysis rows that belong to the estimation gate (measured in
N39b-36, though this target also runs them); the mechanisms; the end-to-end rehearsal of the scoring pipeline on
synthetic campaign stores with the planted wrong scorers (N39a-41, which uses the same registry fixtures and
records no gate); the rehearsal of the gate's procedure (N39b-12); the program-gate register rows of
GATE-TENFOLD-03 (an M3 part and an M7 part) and the pre-opening conjunction `may_open` with GATE-TENFOLD-04,
-05, -10 and -11 (N40-14, which depends on this bead, reads this bead's receipt and neither re-runs a row nor
writes a second record of the gate). This bead is the component of the protocol capstone (seed BD-314) for
GATE-TENFOLD-03: BD-314 depends on it, and it does not depend on BD-314.

## Technical approach

**Test target.** `FROB_TEST_SEED=390322 cargo test -p frob-conformance --test g1_known_answers --features
gauntlet-mutations -- GATE-TENFOLD-03` (convention 1: the harness has no agent verb and no build-tool
subcommand; its tiers are test targets, and the known-answer tier is `g1_known_answers`, N38a-02). This bead
writes the module `tenfold_ka` (`rows::*`, `controls::*`, `meta::*`) that supplies the functions the registry
rows name; there is no separate `tenfold_ka` target. With the gate filter the target builds its table from
`known_answers.toml` and `controls.toml`, runs every row of GATE-TENFOLD-03 at tier `G1 · host` and every control
registered on the gate in the same run, and writes one receipt (`frob.gauntlet.receipt/1` under
`target/gauntlet/receipts/`, named by its BLAKE3) naming the commit, the hardware fingerprint, the seed and, per
row, observed and expected values, and per control `caught`. A single row or control is run by its own filter.

**Rows (exact unless a tolerance is given).** Each is computed by the component that will compute it in a gate:

| Rows | Computed by | Check |
|---|---|---|
| The six-family card: R̂ = 2.647 (45/17); per-family ratios 6.00, 7.00, 6.857, 7.714, 0.8125, 9.00; completion ceiling 3.75 | The scorer on fixture B | Rational equality |
| The single-pair ledger: 6.0; training removed 2.206 (75/34); unremovable floor 8.571 (60/7) | The scorer and the ceiling calculators on fixture A, the rival's column | Rational equality |
| The warm card: R_warm at 1, 10, 100 = 0.272, 2.120, 6.596; limit 8.617; N* = 5 | The scorer on fixture C | Rational equality; decimals to three places |
| Exact guard: 5 of 5, 6 of 6, 7 of 8, 10 of 12, 12 of 16, 17 of 24, 22 of 32 at 0.05; 18 of 24 at 0.0125; no count at m = 4 | The guard routine | Integer equality |
| Zero-count bound 1 − 0.05^(1/24) = 0.1174 | The rare-failure interval routine | 10⁻⁴ |
| Completion ceiling: 12% unfinished caps R at 25/3 | The ceiling calculator | Rational equality |
| Monitor: stops at eight consecutive families below 3 (25.6) and not at seven (17.1) | The monitor on two planted sequences | Stop index equality |

**Controls (each must be caught).**

| Control | How it is planted | Caught when |
|---|---|---|
| A planted false certificate | A synthetic arm submits a certificate its declared (weakened) checker accepts; direct evaluation at a probe point contradicts it | The arm is disqualified in the cells using that proof kind |
| A canary value from a sealed world in a transcript | Injected into a rehearsal transcript | The cell is void and the family retired |
| A ledger with 3% unattributed | Fixture ledger | Flagged `ledger-open`, kept in the ratio, excluded from ceilings |
| A manifest whose freeze time follows the suite opening | Event log fixture | The run is refused |
| A confirmatory cell with four families | Registration fixture | Refused as unreachable |
| The fixed-n verdict at a monitor's stopping time | Scorer invoked on a cell with open campaigns after a stop | Refused |
| An arm that touched the adjudicating simulator during tuning | Tuning-log fixture naming the simulator's identity | The arm is disqualified on the stratum |
| Wrong scorers: average of ratios (6.23); dropping the censored run (7.05); ceiling on the wrong arm's ledger (1.11 instead of 2.206); the warm limit quoted as the ratio (8.617 instead of 2.120); an omitted standing asset (N* = 1); the uncorrected normal guard (9 of 12, 21 of 32 and 4 of 4 at 0.05; 6 of 6
and 10 of 12 at 0.0125); a bootstrap of 24 zeros (width 0); a monitor that stops at seven | Mutant builds of the scoring module | The row they correspond to fails under the mutant |

**Script.** `scripts/e2e/tenfold_known_answers.sh` (header `bead=N39b-22 milestone=M3 gates=GATE-TENFOLD-03
seed=390322`): first runs the known-answer registry's own script `scripts/e2e/ka_tenfold_registry.sh` (N39-KA)
as a stage; builds the harness and the standalone scorer; runs the test target with the gate filter above; then,
as information only, with the filters `GATE-TENFOLD-10` and `GATE-TENFOLD-13` for rows TF-17 to TF-22 (whose
gates are recorded by N39b-36 and N39b-47, not here); then repeats the seven
operator-visible controls through the harness driver from the command line (so that the refusal records and
slugs are checked as an operator would see them); then runs each mutant build and expects its row to fail;
then runs the refusal registry's `scripts/e2e/tenfold_refusals.sh` (N39-REF) and fails if any registry row
whose raising mechanism is due by this gate instance's milestone (M3 for `M3-partial`; every row for
`M7-tournament`) is still `pending` or is not produced with its registered reason, exit code and first repair.

**Logging.** Through the STD-E2E helper, to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_known_answers/<utc>.ndjson` (`ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, seed). One `assert` line per row with its
number from N39-KA's block of N38a-40 as `ka` and the local label (`TF-01` …) as `slug`, observed and expected as
exact strings and the tolerance; one `assert` of kind `negative-control` per control with its issued number from
N39-CX's block of 27 (the controls seeded into `controls.toml` by N38b-05) as `cx` and the tag (`TF-CX-01` …) as
`slug`, and `caught: true|false`; until a block is issued a line carries the label alone, marked `provisional`;
every refusal with its code or slug and ranked repairs; the receipt's hash; the final `summary` with
`gates: [{id: "GATE-TENFOLD-03", measured_ok, controls_caught}]`. A control that is not caught fails the script.

**Recording.** The receipt is committed byte-identical at `receipts/gates/<blake3>.json` (N40-01; a named or
target copy is never cited), and the gate's part is recorded from it with `cargo run -p frob-conformance
--example record_gate -- --gate GATE-TENFOLD-03 --receipt <hash>` by a fresh-context session that did not
implement the harness (conventions 1 and 9; this bead is the gate's one recorder, as N40-14 names it); `pass` is
refused when the control list is empty or any control was not caught in the same run.
The receipt is the one measured record of GATE-TENFOLD-03. The pre-opening verdict (N40-14) binds it to a gate
instance by its commit: the receipt counts for the instance (`M3-partial` or `M7-tournament`) whose frozen
harness commit it names, so the target is re-run at each instance's frozen commit and an M3 receipt does not
serve M7.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every row reproduces exactly (or within its stated tolerance) and every control is caught, in one run.
- [ ] Each mutant scorer fails exactly the row it is aimed at and no other.
- [ ] The receipt names commit, fingerprint and seed; the gate row cites it; the session differs from the
      implementer's.
- [ ] The target is run again at M7 before the tournament's suite opens, at the tournament's frozen commit.
- [ ] Removing any control from the registry makes the gate unable to record a pass.
- [ ] No refusal-registry row of a mechanism due by the instance's milestone is `pending`; at M7 none is.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

The target is the test: `tenfold_ka::rows::*` (one per row label), `tenfold_ka::controls::*` (one per control
label), `tenfold_ka::meta::every_row_has_control`, `tenfold_ka::meta::every_control_last_seen_caught`.

### End-to-end script and logging

As above. Runs inside the default compute class in minutes; no device needed.

### Known answers and negative controls

All of N39-KA rows TF-01 to TF-16, and every N39-CX control attached to GATE-TENFOLD-03 (TF-CX-01 to -15 and
-24), run by their issued numbers with the tags as slugs; each control's plant is built by the bead N39-CX names
for it under its plant identifier (for this gate's controls N39a-07, -08, -17, -18, -24, -27, -29, -30, -33, -35,
-37 and N39b-01, -02), each a dependency of this bead directly or through another; rows TF-17 to TF-22 are also run here and are the gate content of N39b-36 and N39b-47; the controls
attached only to other gates (TF-CX-16 to -23, -25 to -27) are run by those gates' beads. Determinism lanes: the target twice on one machine with equal receipts apart
from time fields; debug and release; arm64 macOS and x86-64 Linux.

## Considerations

- A control that has never failed its gate in any recorded run is itself suspect and is reviewed at each
  milestone exit: either the defect cannot be planted as described or the gate is not looking.
- Any false certificate, leak or withdrawn result found in real use is added here permanently.
