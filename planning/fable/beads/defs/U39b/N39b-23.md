## Background

The ten-fold test rests on eight invariants about how a gate is conducted. Each is enforced where it arises (a
run is refused if an arm is not frozen; a report is refused if a ratio lacks its interval). Enforcement at the
point of action is not proof that the finished record is clean: a refusal path can be bypassed, a record can be
added by hand, a step can be recorded out of order by a faulty tool. This bead is the second line: an auditor
that reads a gate's entire record after the fact and checks every invariant as a property of the record. It runs
before any verdict is computed and again after the report is generated, and a gate whose audit fails records no
pass. It is also the property-test home of the invariants: each has a generator of valid and invalid records.

## Scope

In: the auditor command, one check per invariant, the property tests, the audit record. Out: the point-of-action
enforcement (the mechanisms' beads); disqualification scope (N39b-01).

## Technical approach

Command (DESIGN): `frob-tenfold audit --gate <id> [--phase pre-score|post-report]` writing `audit.json`; module
`crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/audit.rs`. Exit 0 when every check passes, 1 otherwise, with one finding per
violation (invariant, record, expected, observed); 1 is the check-mode code the refusal registry N39-REF
reserves for the scorer's `--check` and this auditor; a record that cannot be read at all (an unverifiable hash
chain) exits 9. The auditor reads; it never repairs. A finding names the harness reason of the registry where
one describes the violation, so that an audit finding and the point-of-action refusal carry one name: INV-01
`role-conflict` (two roles) and `role-authorship`; INV-02 `seal-out-of-order`; INV-03 `freeze-after-open` (a run
before a publication) and `frozen-arm-changed` (a manifest that differs from the published hash); INV-05
`missing-run` and `exclusion-after-reveal`; INV-06 `family-reused`; INV-07 `verdict-before-n` and
`verdict-already-computed` (the harness forms of FROB-REF-552 and -553); INV-08 `ratio-incomplete` and
`warm-without-assets`; INV-04 has no reason and prints the residual. For `role-conflict` and `family-reused` the
registry names this bead as the raising bead, and the tests `inv01_actor_with_two_roles` and
`inv06_family_in_two_gates` are their producing tests. The report it reads in phase `post-report` is the record
`frob.tenfold.report/1` of N39a-36.

| ID | Invariant | Check over the record |
|---|---|---|
| INV-TENFOLD-01 | The builder, rival-advocate and adjudicator roles are disjoint for the life of a gate, for people and for agent sessions | Every event's actor maps to exactly one role in the gate's role registry; no actor identity appears under two roles; the action matrix holds (suite and protocol commits, receipts, exclusions and reveals by the adjudicator only; freeze manifests of arms B and C by advocates; arm A's by builders); no advocate identity is an author in this repository's history; no adjudicator identity is an author in any arm |
| INV-TENFOLD-02 | The six sealing steps appear in the ledger in order, each with a hash and a time | Entries exist for: (1) class descriptions and development suite published; (2) hash of the sealed suite and of the referee protocol committed; (3) tuning on the development suite only; (4) each arm's freeze manifest published; (5) the pre-analysis recorded; (6) the suite opened to the harness. Times and sequence numbers are increasing; step 2 precedes every step-4 entry (no mission written against a frozen arm); every step-4 entry precedes step 6 (no arm tuned against a mission); every card-delivery entry follows step 6 and equals that arm's clock start |
| INV-TENFOLD-03 | No paired run exists whose arms were not all frozen and published before it started; no arm changes after its freeze | For every campaign start, each arm of its pair has a publication entry earlier in the log; every manifest verification entry (before each campaign and at close) equals the published hash |
| INV-TENFOLD-04 | Exclusive phases sum to the clock within 2%, or the run is flagged `ledger-open` and leaves ceiling arithmetic | For every ledger, |Σ exclusive − clock| ≤ 0.02·clock or `ledger_open` is set; no ceiling or share computation lists a `ledger-open` run among its inputs; every such run is present in the ratio's inputs |
| INV-TENFOLD-05 | No run is dropped; a censored run contributes H; an exclusion is a harness fault recorded before times are compared | The set of planned campaigns (cell × family × campaign index × arm) equals ledgers plus exclusions; every censored ledger enters the sums at H; every exclusion entry names a harness fault, covers the pair for every arm, and precedes the reveal entry of that pair's times; an exclusion that follows a reveal is a finding |
| INV-TENFOLD-06 | A sealed family serves one gate and is then retired | No family identifier appears in the sealed suites of two gates; every family of a closed gate has a retirement entry; a family touched by a leak was retired at the leak's entry |
| INV-TENFOLD-07 | No output of a monitor enters the fixed-n verdict, and the verdict is computed once, at n | The scorer's recorded input digests contain no monitor record; exactly one verdict-producing scorer entry exists per cell and it follows the close of every planned campaign of the cell (the monitor's own `withdrawn` at the partial gate is a separate entry on the monitor's authority) |
| INV-TENFOLD-08 | Every reported ratio carries its regime, m, n, H, [L, U], the adjudication label and the freeze hashes; a warm ratio also carries S and N* | Every ratio object of the report has all fields; every ratio that appears in rendered text resolves to such an object |

**Property tests.** A generator builds random valid gate records (roles, sealing order, 2 to 4 cells, 4 to 32
families, random censoring and exclusions) and the auditor must pass them; for each invariant a mutator applies
one violation (swap two sealing steps; give one actor two roles; start a campaign before a manifest; set
unattributed to 2.5% without the flag; delete one ledger; move an exclusion after a reveal; reuse a family;
insert a monitor digest among scorer inputs; score a cell twice; strip one field of a ratio) and the auditor
must report exactly that invariant.

**Use.** Phase `pre-score` checks INV-01 to -06 and is step 2 of the gate's closing procedure; phase
`post-report` checks all eight. `audit.json` is part of the gate record and of the kit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each invariant has a check, a valid-record generator and at least one mutator; 10⁴ random valid records
      pass and every mutated record yields a finding naming the right invariant.
- [ ] On the clean synthetic gate of this bead's script the auditor passes in both phases; with each plant it
      fails with the right finding and reason.
- [ ] The auditor never writes to the gate directory except `audit.json`.
- [ ] `audit.json` carries the phase, the event-log head and the report digest it read, the count of checks
      and the findings, so that a reader can tell which record was audited: the gate record cites its digest
      (N39bp4-01), and the program-gate checks refuse to enter a row whose record lacks a passing `post-report`
      audit of the same report digest (N40-15 for GATE-TENFOLD-06; N40-16 for GATE-TENFOLD-07).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::audit`: `inv01_actor_with_two_roles`; `inv01_advocate_is_repo_author`;
`inv02_step2_after_step4`; `inv02_card_before_clock`; `inv03_run_before_freeze`; `inv03_manifest_drift`;
`inv04_two_percent_boundary` (2.0% passes, 2.01% needs the flag); `inv04_open_run_in_ceiling_inputs`;
`inv05_missing_ledger`; `inv05_exclusion_after_reveal`; `inv05_censored_enters_at_h`; `inv06_family_in_two_gates`;
`inv07_monitor_digest_in_inputs`; `inv07_two_verdicts`; `inv08_each_missing_field`;
`findings_name_registered_reasons` (each test above asserts its finding's reason as listed; a finding with a
reason that is not a row of the registry fails the registry's consistency test); plus the property tests
`valid_records_pass` and `single_violation_single_finding` (seeded, 10⁴ cases each, with shrinking).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_audit.sh`, owned by this bead and built only on its dependencies (header `bead=N39b-23
milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e gates=GATE-TENFOLD-03,GATE-TENFOLD-06,GATE-TENFOLD-07 seed=392323 needs=cpu
plants=two-roles,steps-swapped,run-before-freeze,arm-patched,three-percent-open,dropped-run,
exclusion-after-reveal,family-reused,monitor-in-inputs,scored-twice,field-stripped`), on a synthetic closed gate
record written by this bead's generator (labelled `synthetic`; two cells of 24 families, three arms, a report
record in N39a-36's form): (1) `FROB_TEST_SEED=392323 cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::audit`; (2)
`frob-tenfold audit --gate <id> --phase pre-score --json` and `--phase post-report --json` on the clean record
(exit 0, no finding, `audit.json` written and nothing else changed, asserted by a digest of the directory
before and after); (3) each plant through `e2e_plant` on a fresh copy, asserting exit 1 and exactly one
finding with its invariant and reason: the eleven plants above are one mutator per invariant clause, among
them the planted inputs of the harness registry that this auditor catches (TF-CX-02 a dropped run, TF-CX-09 a
monitor record among the scorer's inputs, TF-CX-13 a ledger with 3% unattributed and no flag, TF-CX-14 a
freeze after the opening, TF-CX-24 an arm patched after the opening; the fixtures of the last three are
N39a-24's and N39a-18's, under `crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/tenfold/controls/`); (4) a record whose event
log does not verify (exit 9). Logged as NDJSON through the STD-E2E helper (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`,
`event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, seed): the audit digest, the phase, the count of checks,
each finding with its invariant id, reason, record, expected and observed, each plant as a `negative-control`
assertion with `caught`; on failure the first plant not caught and the gate directory's path; a final
`summary` with assertion counts and controls caught. The partial-gate script
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh` (N39b-12, which depends on this bead) runs this script as a stage and
runs the auditor again on its rehearsal record (steps `audit` and `drills`).

### Known answers and negative controls

Controls TF-CX-13 (3% unattributed), TF-CX-14 (freeze after opening), TF-CX-09 (monitor), TF-CX-02 (dropped run) and
TF-CX-24 (an arm patched after the suite opened: the manifest verification entry differs from the published hash, an
INV-03 finding). Metamorphic relation: reordering independent entries that the invariants do not order (two arms'
manifest publications) leaves the audit result unchanged.

## Considerations

- INV-01 for people depends on honest identity in the role registry; the auditor checks consistency, not
  identity. Under the fail-closed adjudicator (a project session under a named human) the audit passes and the
  label `self-adjudicated` is what tells the reader.
- The repository-authorship check needs the list of advocate and adjudicator identities used in version
  control; a missing mapping is a finding, not a pass.
- The audit is cheap (seconds) and is re-run by the kit's verifier.
