## Background

The ten-fold test has three parties, and a person or an agent session holds exactly one role for the life of a
gate: the *builders* wrote this system; the *rival advocates* build and tune the rival arms and have written
none of this system; the *adjudicator* writes the sealed missions and the referee protocol, runs acceptance and
releases receipts, and belongs to neither group. The design intends the adjudicator to be an external party: a
person or a team with no authorship in any arm. Who that is, is an open question owned by the project owner. It
must be decided before any sealed world family exists, because the adjudicator writes them; the program's
open-question register dates the need at M2 and lists the partial gate at M3 and the tournament at M7 as what
it blocks.

Fail-closed default, if no external party can be engaged: a fresh-context agent session with no write access to
any arm, operating from the protocol document alone under a named human, with every action logged. Every ratio
measured that way is labelled `self-adjudicated`. Under that label a ratio may be reported, and the words
"independently accepted" and "ten-fold" wait until an external party has re-run the replication kit.

## Scope

In: the decision OPEN-TENFOLD-2 itself: the options, what an external adjudicator needs, the role statement, the
access set-up that follows from either branch, the party-kind vocabulary and the values of the function that
derives the label from it, and the party's part of the one decision record.

What this bead can show at M2, and what waits for M3 (DESIGN). At M2 there is no gate, no arm and no sealed
store, so the bead's own tests work on the decision record and on the configuration record generated from it.
The enforcement at a gate is built with the gate: the role registry `tenfold/<gate>/roles.json`, its authorship
and session-lineage checks, the permission matrix, the comment channel and the wording lock are N39a-02 (M3),
which reuses this bead's vocabulary and the label function; the default session is created and its access
asserted by the rehearsal step `roles` of N39b-12 (M3); the conditioned wording rows are N41-06's.

Out, with the bead that has it: the simulator choice (N39b-26, the other half of the same owner question); the
owner packet of OPEN-PROG-2 (N41-06, a dependency of this bead): the page on which these options are put to the
owner at the M1 exit, the register rows of OPEN-PROG-2 and its two aliases, the configuration record
`registries/tenfold/adjudication.toml` that projects this decision, its validator, the refusal of the family
writer while no record exists and the second confirmation before the M7 suite is sealed; the rival advocates
(N39b-28); the acceptance service (the adjudication beads).

## Technical approach

1. **Options put to the owner.** (a) An external person or team engaged for both gates. (b) External for the
   tournament only; the default session for the partial gate, which is then a rehearsal in the register's
   words. (c) The default session for both, with an external re-run of the kit sought after publication.
2. **What an external adjudicator needs** (so the choice is informed): the public class descriptions, the
   mission grammar and the schemas; the development missions; the blinded-qualification protocol; a machine or
   account to hold the sealed suite and key; compute for acceptance batteries (the `adjudicate` phase); and
   (HYPOTHESIS, to be confirmed with the party) about two weeks of effort before the partial gate to write 24
   sealed families per cell and the referee protocol.
   They receive no acceptance code from the builders.
3. **Role statement** (signed by whoever is named): no authorship in any arm; holds no other role in the gate;
   comments from builders and advocates on protocol drafts come through one logged channel until the suite hash
   is committed, and none after.
4. **Set-up under the default.** A dedicated agent session with: no write access to the repository or to any
   arm's directories; read access to the protocol document, schemas and public descriptions only; its own
   store and key for the sealed suite; a named human who answers for it; an action log entered in the gate's
   event log. The session is fresh for each gate and never one that implemented or tuned anything.
5. **Label.** The party kind is one of `external` and `internal-session` (the default session). The label is
   a function of the kind: `internal-session` gives `self-adjudicated`, `external` gives `external`. The
   function, `frob_conformance::tenfold::adjudication::adjudication_label(kind)`, is defined with the
   configuration record in N41-06, which needs it to write the default record before this bead runs; this
   bead fixes its two values as part of the decision and tests them. It is never typed by hand: the configuration record's
   `label` field and, at M3, the role registry's `adjudication` field are written by calling it, and a record
   whose label disagrees with its kind is refused. The label prints on every ratio of the gate. The wording
   lint refuses "independently accepted" and a stated ten-fold result under `self-adjudicated` until an
   external replication of the kit is recorded (the two rows N41-06 enters in the claims-wording registry).
6. **Record.** Part 1 ("the party") of the one decision record of the owner question,
   `docs/adr/ADR-PROG-02-adjudication.md`: the option chosen, the named party, the human responsible, the role
   statement, the access list, the date, and the gates it covers; the role registry of each gate cites it.
   Part 2 of the same file is the simulator per stratum, written by N39b-26. DESIGN: one file, because the
   program's register carries the two questions as one (OPEN-PROG-2) and its rows move together; an earlier
   draft of this bead wrote `decisions/OPEN-TENFOLD-2.md`, which is not created. The configuration record of
   N41-06 is generated from this file and never edited by hand: the machine-read content of part 1 is one
   fenced `toml` block whose first line is `# part 1: party`, with one table per gate holding the fields of
   that record (`option`, `kind`, `name`, `human`, `statement`, `access`); under option (b) the two gates
   carry different kinds. After writing it this bead runs `frob-tenfold adjudication generate` and asserts
   that the record's party tables equal the block.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision is recorded before any sealed world family is written, or the default is recorded as in
      force with the named human.
- [ ] Part 1 of the record names, per gate, the party, its kind and, under the default, the named human;
      `frob-tenfold require-adjudication --gate partial --json` prints that kind and the label derived from it,
      and a record whose label was typed differently is refused.
- [ ] Under the default, part 1 carries the named human and the access list of step 4 (read access to the
      protocol document, schemas and public descriptions only; its own sealed store; no write scope on the
      repository or on any arm), in the form the M3 rehearsal asserts when it creates the session (N39b-12,
      step `roles`, which refuses and logs an attempted write to an arm's directory and enters the session's
      action log in the gate's event log).
- [ ] The record carries the one line the M3 exit review prints among the limits of the gate's record
      (`adjudication: external` or `self-adjudicated`, with the party and the date).

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::party`, on the decision record and the configuration record, with no
gate needed: `adjudicator_kind_sets_label` (`internal-session` gives `self-adjudicated`);
`external_kind_clears_label` (`external` gives `external`); `label_not_typed_by_hand` (a configuration record,
or a report fixture, whose label disagrees with the derivation from the party kind is refused);
`default_requires_named_human` (part 1 with kind `internal-session` and an empty `human` is refused);
`option_b_has_two_kinds` (option (b): the partial gate reads `internal-session`, the tournament `external`);
`access_list_complete_under_default` (a default part 1 whose `access` grants a write scope on the repository
or on an arm, or omits the session's own sealed store, is refused); `upgrade_within_gate_refused` (on a `synthetic` gate record that carries a
suite-hash date, changing the party kind is refused; with no suite hash, and between gates, it is allowed).
Tested where they are enforced, and not repeated here: an adjudicator with authorship in an arm
(`party_in_arm_authorship_rejected` of N41-06 on the record; the registration checks of N39a-02 at the gate);
a comment after the suite hash (`channel_closes_at_step_2` of N39a-02); the wording under the default
(`conditioned_phrases_follow_record` of N41-06; `wording_lock` of N39a-02).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_adjudicator_decision.sh` (this bead, runnable at M2; sources the STD-E2E helper): on a
scratch root, for each of the options (a), (b) and (c), writes part 1 of a scratch ADR, runs
`frob-tenfold adjudication generate` and `frob-tenfold require-adjudication --gate partial --json` and
`--gate tournament --json`, and asserts the party kind, the derived label and, under the default, the named
human and the access list; produces a `synthetic` ratio table per option and asserts that `self-adjudicated`
is on every line exactly when the gate's kind is `internal-session`. Declared plants, each through
`e2e_expect_caught`: `default-without-human` (the generator must refuse), `label-typed-by-hand` (a record
edited to `label = "external"` under kind `internal-session` must be refused), `default-with-write-scope`.
Every line is NDJSON through the shared helper to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_adjudicator_decision/<utc>.ndjson`
with a transcript (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command, exit code, wall time): the
option, the party kind and the digests of the ADR and of the record; `refusal` lines with reason and exit code;
one `assert` per expected field with expected and observed values; the label observed on every line of the
ratio table; on failure the first field or line whose observed value differs, with the scratch paths; a
`summary` with assertions passed and failed and the plants caught, naming GATE-TENFOLD-05 as read, not
measured. At M3 the step `roles` of `scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh` (N39b-12) exercises the set-up
itself: it creates the adjudicating session, asserts its access set, logs each attempted write with its target
path class (arm, repository, sealed store) and the expected and observed refusal, and asserts the label on
every ratio of the rehearsal report.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the two kinds and their labels; three options, of which (b) gives the two gates different kinds.
Negative controls of this bead: the three plants above. Negative controls enforced at the gate and owned
there: a session holding both builder and adjudicator roles (refused by the role registry, N39a-02); a report
under the default that prints "independently accepted" (refused by the wording rows of N41-06 from M2 and by
the rule set of N39b-15 from M3).

## Considerations

- Risk (register): no neutral party is named, or the repository stays private, so nobody outside can confirm
  the result. Countermeasures: the replication kit, the offline auditor as a standalone binary, and the label.
- No milestone is blocked: without a neutral party M3 exits with a partial gate labelled `self-adjudicated`.
- An external adjudicator who later contributes code to an arm cannot adjudicate a subsequent gate.
- The decision can be upgraded (default to external) between gates, never within one.
