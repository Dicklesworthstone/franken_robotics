## Background

Every arm of the ten-fold test is driven by the same AI agent: the same model at one pinned revision for the
whole gate, the same token and tool-call caps per campaign, a fresh context per campaign, and no human in the
loop. The agent's latency per turn is common to both arms and bounds what any system can gain per turn (with
latency t and a rival cost C per decision, deciding one design per turn gives at most (C + t)/t). Which agent,
at which revision and with which caps, is an open question owned by the project owner. It blocks every freeze,
and the first use is at M1: the incumbent design loop is measured "with the common agent" (GATE-TENFOLD-02).

Fail-closed default: the agent the project itself is developed with at the gate's date, pinned; one agent only,
with the result stated as conditional on it.

## Scope

In: the content of the decision OPEN-TENFOLD-6 and its record (the ADR `ADR-PROG-17` and its supporting file
`decisions/OPEN-TENFOLD-6.md`); the pin and caps and how the caps are chosen; the equality of caps across arms;
the seed policy; the revision check and the retirement risk; how the choice is stated in results. Also in: the
small module `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/agentpin.rs` (DESIGN) that loads the record and answers two
questions for every campaign driver from M1 on (the incumbent loop BD-143 first): the caps in force, and whether
a reported revision equals the pin. It is built on the harness skeleton (N39a-01) and hands cap breaches to the
campaign clock (N39a-03), which censors them.

Out, and where it is: the one-page request that puts this question to the owner again at the M1 exit together
with the three other owner inputs of the ten-fold test, the generated configuration record
`registries/tenfold/owner-inputs.toml` whose `[agent]` table is a projection of this decision, and the freeze
precondition that all four owner rows are `decided` or `defaulted` (N41-15, a later bead that depends on this
one); the agent documentation of this system and the advocates' guide (their beads); the register row of
OPEN-TENFOLD-6 itself (N41-OPEN8, a dependency: this bead writes the ADR its `adr` field cites); the checks at
freeze time that every arm's manifest carries the same agent revision and caps (`freeze-agent-differs`) and the
equal-terms record (N39a-19, M3), which is also why no cell can mix agents.

## Technical approach

1. **What is decided.** (a) The model and its exact revision identifier as the provider publishes it. (b) The
   token cap and the tool-call cap per campaign, the same for every arm. (c) The per-turn output limit and
   context limit, if the provider exposes them. (d) Whether the provider allows a sampling seed; if it does,
   the campaign seed fixes it; if not, the record says so and agent sampling is an unpaired source of variance.
   (e) Parallel workers per campaign (the same cap on model calls and parallel workers for every arm).
2. **How caps are chosen (DESIGN).** The caps are set at twice the 90th percentile of tokens and of tool calls
   that the rival's agent used on development missions that reached acceptance, so that a cap rarely binds a run
   that would have finished; a cap breach is not a disqualification, the run is censored at the breach and costs
   H. Because that rule needs measured runs and the M1 baseline (BD-143) needs caps before it runs, the caps
   recorded at M1 are `provisional`: the provider's per-campaign maximum where it publishes one, otherwise a
   recorded placeholder, the same for every arm. BD-143's measured runs then give the percentiles, and the caps
   are re-derived by the rule and recorded as `final` before the partial gate's freeze (GATE-TENFOLD-04); a
   freeze against `provisional` caps is refused. A baseline run that breached a provisional cap is reported with
   the breach and is not used for the percentile (it would bias the cap down). Caps are revisited before each
   gate's freeze and never during a gate.
3. **One agent only.** The primary result uses one agent. A second agent may be run as a separate, labelled
   result; ratios are never pooled across agents. Every reported ratio is conditional on the pinned agent, and
   the limits block of every report says so.
4. **Equal documentation.** Arm A's agent reads this system's agent documentation; the agents of arms B and C
   read the incumbents' own documentation and an advocate-written guide no longer than that documentation,
   frozen with the arm. The decision records the length reference (in tokens of the pinned model).
5. **Revision drift.** Before every campaign the harness records the revision the provider reports; a campaign
   whose reported revision differs from the pin is refused (`tenfold.agent-revision-differs`, registry N39-REF). If the provider retires the revision during a gate,
   the gate is suspended, not continued on another revision; cells not yet complete are void and re-run at the
   next gate. The kit's README states the known retirement date, since level 2 of replication depends on it.
6. **Record.** `decisions/OPEN-TENFOLD-6.md`: the fields of step 1, the cap derivation with the measured
   percentiles, the length reference, and the date; cited by every freeze manifest's `agent` block.
   One decision has one record of authority (DESIGN, the same statement in N41-15 and in the four
   decision beads N39b-28, N39b-29, N39b-31 and N39b-33): the ADR that the open-question register requires, which
   the register row's `adr` field cites. The ADRs are `ADR-PROG-15` (OPEN-TENFOLD-3, advocates), `ADR-PROG-16`
   (OPEN-TENFOLD-4, envelope), `ADR-PROG-17` (OPEN-TENFOLD-6, agent) and `ADR-PROG-18` (OPEN-TENFOLD-8, thresholds),
   under `docs/adr/`. The file `decisions/OPEN-TENFOLD-<n>.md` is the ADR's supporting file: the request, the
   options, the figures and the evidence. The ADR cites it by path and digest, and it states no decision that the
   ADR lacks. Reason: the register's rule is that a decision or a default is an ADR, while the request and its
   arithmetic are too long for one and change until the decision is taken.
   This bead writes `ADR-PROG-17` when the owner decides; if the first use at M1 arrives first, the register's
   default writer writes it with the default above.
7. **The caps, by name.** The freeze manifest's `agent` block has four fields: `model`, `revision`, `token_cap`
   and `tool_call_cap`. The two caps are per campaign and the same for every arm, and they are the only caps the
   configuration record of N41-15 carries in its `[agent]` table (`token_cap`, `tool_call_cap`), with their status
(`caps_status`: `provisional` or `final`, step 2). The per-turn
   output limit, the context limit and the cap on parallel workers of step 1 are recorded in this decision's
   supporting file where the provider exposes them. No cap on turns and no wall-clock cap is an owner input: the
   wall-clock limit of a campaign is the deadline H of its stratum.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The decision or the default is recorded before the M1 incumbent loop runs; the baseline's records carry
      the agent pin.
- [ ] Caps are identical across arms in every freeze manifest of a gate; a manifest with different caps is
      refused at publication.
- [ ] A campaign started under a different reported revision is refused.
- [ ] The caps are recorded `final`, derived by the twice-p90 rule from the baseline's measured runs, before the
      partial gate's freeze; a freeze against `provisional` caps is refused.
- [ ] Reports state that ratios are conditional on the pinned agent.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::agentpin`: `record_has_one_cap_per_kind` (a record with a per-arm cap
field is refused, so caps cannot differ by arm); `revision_mismatch_refuses_campaign`
(`tenfold.agent-revision-differs`, before the clock starts); `cap_rule_twice_p90` (p90 of 180,000 tokens gives a
cap of 360,000; p90 of 41 tool calls gives 82); `p90_excludes_breached_and_unaccepted_runs`;
`provisional_caps_refused_at_freeze` (`tenfold.agent-caps-provisional`; `final` accepted); `cap_breach_censors` (handed to the
campaign clock, the run contributes H, flagged `cap_breach: tokens`); `second_agent_is_separate_label` (a record
naming a second agent marks it `secondary`, and its results carry their own label); `seed_policy_recorded` (a
provider without a sampling seed records `seed: unavailable`, never a made-up value). The freeze-time equality of
caps and revision across manifests is tested where it is enforced (N39a-19, `terms_fieldwise` and
`freeze_hook_checks`).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_agent_pin.sh` (this bead; header `bead=N39b-31 milestone=M1 labels=tenfold,e2e
gates=GATE-TENFOLD-02 seed=390631 plants=revision-mismatch,per-arm-cap,provisional-at-freeze`): loads the
recorded decision (or the default), starts one short synthetic campaign under the pinned revision (accepted) and
one under a planted different reported revision (refused before its clock starts), drives a synthetic run past
the token cap (censored at the breach instant, contributing H), re-derives the caps from a fixture of baseline
runs and asserts the `final` values, and asserts that a freeze against `provisional` caps is refused. Logged as
NDJSON through the shared helper (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command, exit code, wall time):
the model and revision as identities, the record's digest and status, each cap with its derivation (p90 and
twice it) as a `metric`, tokens and tool calls per campaign against the caps, each refusal with its reason, on
failure the first differing value. Later, step `run` of `scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh` (N39b-12) repeats
the revision check on the synthetic arms, and `scripts/e2e/tenfold_incumbent_loop.sh` (BD-143) logs the agent
pin, tokens and tool calls per campaign against the caps on real runs.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: the latency ceiling (C + t)/t, for example C = 410 s and t = 90 s gives 5.56. Negative controls: a
manifest with a larger token cap for one arm; a silent revision change.

## Considerations

- A stronger agent may shrink the ratio (it compensates for a weaker tool surface) or grow it; the test makes
  no claim about agents that were not run.
- Provider-side nondeterminism cannot be removed; it is why times replicate only within a factor (1.5 in the
  kit's second level) and why the family, not the campaign, is the unit of inference.
- Token cost is reported in the cost columns at declared prices; no cost claim is made.
