## Background

The ten-fold test runs incumbent simulators and learning stacks as rival arms and publishes times measured with
them. Some software licences restrict publishing benchmark comparisons, redistributing code or binaries, or
commercial use. Whether each incumbent's licence permits publishing a comparison, and what the replication kit
may carry, is an open question owned by the project lead. It blocks the parity-baseline gate (GATE-TENFOLD-01),
whose rival audit must record a licence for every pinned incumbent. Fail-closed default: the kit carries
revisions and fetch instructions only; an incumbent whose licence forbids publication is replaced by the next
strongest, and the replacement is printed.

## Scope

In: the licence reading per incumbent; the three determinations (run, publish, redistribute); the replacement
rule; the record; the admissibility check `licences::cleared(incumbent, revision)` (DESIGN) that answers, from
the record, whether an incumbent at a pin may enter an arm, refusing with `tenfold.incumbent-licence-not-cleared`
(registry N39-REF). Out: the rival audit that consumes the result (N39b-08); the freeze of an arm, which calls
the check (N39a-18, M3); the kit builder's enforcement (N39b-18); the register row of OPEN-TENFOLD-7
(N41-OPEN8, a dependency: this bead's ADR `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-TENFOLD-7.md` is the record its `adr` field cites).

## Technical approach

1. **Incumbents to read** (the candidates of the rival arms and of adjudication, each at the release pinned by
   the audit): MuJoCo and MuJoCo Warp, MuJoCo Playground and mjlab, Newton, Isaac Lab and the physics engine
   under it, Genesis, Drake, ManiSkill; the learners and optimizers their recipes use; any scenario language,
   solver (linear, mixed-integer, satisfiability, interval) or planner an advocate proposes for `envelope`,
   `sensing`, `rebody`, `failure` or `facility`.
2. **For each, from the primary licence text at the pinned revision** (never from a summary): the licence
   identifier; then three determinations, each `yes`, `no` or `unclear` with the clause quoted:
   (a) *run*: may it be executed for this internal evaluation on the project's machines;
   (b) *publish*: may results of a comparison that names it be published;
   (c) *redistribute*: may its code, binaries or containers be included in a kit.
   Also noted: obligations on publication (attribution, notice), and terms attached to assets or models the
   recipes download (reference robots, scenes, pretrained weights).
3. **Rules.**
   - (a) `no` or `unclear`: the incumbent is not run.
   - (b) `no`: the incumbent is replaced on its strata by the next strongest candidate (by development-suite
     time), and every report prints the replacement ("the strongest rival whose licence permits publication").
     `unclear`: treated as `no` until the owner rules.
   - (c) is never relied on: the kit pins revisions and gives fetch instructions for every incumbent, whatever
     its licence says. A `yes` on (c) changes nothing in the default.
   - A layer shipped over an incumbent engine (the fallback when the ablation arm ties) needs (a) for users and
     the licence's terms for derived or linked use; that reading is added to the record if the tie rule fires.
4. **Record.** One `[[reading]]` per incumbent and pinned revision in
   `crates/frob-conformance/registry/licence_readings.toml` (this bead's file; DESIGN): `name`,
   `source_revision`, `spdx`, `text_digest` (of the licence text read at that revision), `run`, `publish`,
   `redistribute` (each `{value = "yes" | "no" | "unclear", clause = "<quoted>"}`), `obligations`, `asset_terms`,
   `read_at`, `reader`. The decision is recorded in `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-TENFOLD-7.md`, the one record of
   authority, under `docs/adr/` where every decision record lives: the rule of step 3 as decided, the
   replacements, and the readings file cited by path and digest (a decision record outside `docs/adr/`, such as
   a summary under `decisions/`, is not written). `cleared` reads the readings file. The command
   `frob-tenfold licences check [<name>@<revision>…] --json` (DESIGN, a subcommand of the operator binary of
   N39a-01; without arguments, every pinned incumbent of `comparators.toml`) answers `cleared` from the file:
   exit 0 when every named incumbent is cleared, printing each replacement with the incumbent it replaced; exit
   3 with `tenfold.incumbent-licence-not-cleared` and its ranked repairs, naming each incumbent that is not
   cleared and the cause (a *run* or *publish* reading other than `yes`, a reading whose `text_digest` differs
   from the licence text at the pin, or a comparator row whose projection differs from the reading, with the
   two differing values). The comparator row of the same incumbent (BD-039's schema, the only definition of `comparators.toml`,
   whose loader refuses unknown fields) receives the projection its `licence` table holds: `spdx`,
   `permits_publishing_comparison` (the *publish* value, with `unclear` written `unknown`), `source` (the
   reading's `text_digest`) and `read_at`; the run and redistribute determinations and the clauses stay in the
   reading. The reading is repeated whenever a pin moves and at the tournament's audit refresh.
5. **This is not legal advice inside the repository.** The record is an engineering reading with quoted
   clauses; where a determination is `unclear`, the owner decides whether to seek counsel, and until then the
   fail-closed rule applies.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every audited incumbent has the three determinations with quoted clauses, from the licence text at its
      pinned revision.
- [ ] No incumbent with (a) or (b) other than `yes` appears in any arm's freeze manifest; each replacement is
      printed in the audit record.
- [ ] The kit builder refuses an incumbent artifact regardless of determination (c).
- [ ] A pin move invalidates the reading for that incumbent until it is repeated.
- [ ] `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-TENFOLD-7.md` records the decision with the replacements and the readings file's digest,
      and the register row of OPEN-TENFOLD-7 cites it in its `adr` field.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::licences`: `unclear_is_treated_as_no`; `publish_no_triggers_replacement`
(the next candidate by development time is selected and the replacement text is produced);
`pin_move_invalidates_reading`; `unpublishable_engine_not_cleared` (`cleared` refuses an incumbent whose *publish*
reading is `no` or `unclear`, with `tenfold.incumbent-licence-not-cleared` and its ranked repairs);
`run_no_is_never_run`; `clauses_quoted_from_pinned_text` (a determination without a quoted clause, or quoted from
a text whose digest is not the pinned revision's, is refused); `redistribute_yes_changes_nothing` (the kit's
contents for that incumbent are still pins and fetch instructions only); `projection_matches_comparator_row` (the
comparator row's `licence` table equals the projection of its reading, `unclear` written `unknown`, and loads
under BD-039's schema).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_licences.sh`, owned by this bead and built only on its dependencies (header `bead=N39b-32
milestone=M0 labels=tenfold,e2e gates=GATE-TENFOLD-01 seed=393201 needs=cpu
plants=stale-reading,publish-unclear,projection-mismatch,unquoted-clause`): (1) `FROB_TEST_SEED=393201 cargo test
-p frob-conformance tenfold::licences`; (2) fetches the licence text of every pinned incumbent at its pinned
revision by the pin's fetch instructions and asserts that each reading's `text_digest` equals the fetched text's;
(3) `frob-tenfold licences check --json`: every pinned incumbent cleared or its replacement printed; (4) asserts
that `docs/adr/ADR-OPEN-TENFOLD-7.md` exists, cites the readings file at its current digest, and is the record
the register row's `adr` field names; (5) the plants, each through `e2e_expect_refusal` or `e2e_expect_caught` on
a scratch copy of the readings file: a reading whose `text_digest` does not match the fetched text, a planted
entry with `publish = unclear`, a comparator row whose `permits_publishing_comparison` differs from its reading
(each refused, exit 3, `tenfold.incumbent-licence-not-cleared` with its cause), and a determination without a
quoted clause (refused by the loader). Logged as NDJSON through the STD-E2E helper (`ts`, `script`, `bead`,
`step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, seed): per incumbent its pin, the three
determinations, the digest of the licence text read and of the registry's copy, the `cleared` answer, each
replacement with the incumbent it replaced, each plant as a `negative-control` assertion with `caught`; on
failure the incumbent and the two differing digests; a final `summary` with assertion counts. Step `licences` of
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_parity_baseline.sh` (N39b-08, which depends on this bead) runs this script as a stage.

### Known answers and negative controls

Negative controls: a reading whose `text_digest` does not match the fetched text (stale reading); a
`cleared` query for an engine whose reading has `publish = unclear` (refused); a comparator row whose
`permits_publishing_comparison` differs from its reading's *publish* value (the projection check fails). The same refusal at freeze time is
tested by the freeze manifest's bead (N39a-18).

## Considerations

- Replacing the strongest rival for licence reasons weakens the comparison; the report must say which rival was
  excluded and why, so that a reader can discount accordingly.
- Asset licences (robot models, scenes) can bind even when the engine's licence does not; development and
  sealed missions use assets whose terms allow publication of derived results.
- Repository visibility is a separate owner decision; a private repository does not relax any licence term.
