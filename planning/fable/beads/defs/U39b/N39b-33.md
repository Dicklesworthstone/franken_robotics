## Background

The outcome rules of the ten-fold test are written before any measurement. They rest on a handful of numbers:
the withdrawal threshold 3, the word threshold 10, the guard floor 5, the tie factor 1.5, the non-inferiority
margin 0.05 and the limit of four confirmatory cells per gate. They are fixed when the first suite hash is
committed, and none is changed after any sealed run. Because they decide what the program will say and build,
the project owner ratifies them. This is an open question that blocks the first suite hash. Fail-closed default:
as written.

## Scope

In: the content of the decision OPEN-TENFOLD-8 and its record (the ADR `ADR-PROG-18` and its supporting file
`decisions/OPEN-TENFOLD-8.md`); a one-page statement of what each number does and what moving it would change; the
ratified block itself as a canonical JSON file of exact rationals, written with the harness's canonical writer,
with its digest. Out: the binding of that digest into the event-log entry that commits the first suite hash and
the refusal of a later change (`tenfold.parameters-frozen`), which the outcome engine N39b-05 implements,
reading this block (it depends on this bead; one implementation, not two); the schedule on
which the question is put to the owner (one page with the three other owner inputs at the M1 exit), the generated
configuration record `registries/tenfold/owner-inputs.toml` whose `[thresholds]` table is a projection of the
ratified block, and the freeze precondition that all four owner rows are `decided` or `defaulted` (N41-15, on
which this bead depends for the record format); the engine that applies the numbers (N39b-05); the analysis of one part of
this question, which this bead depends on and whose outcome it records with the block: at the margin 0.05 the
completion and coverage non-inferiority condition cannot be met below 59 families, so that no cell of the default
size can return `ten-fold` as written; the arithmetic, the five options and the fail-closed default ("as
written") are in the owner-decision bead N39a-31, and the ratified block names the option chosen.

## Technical approach

1. **The block to ratify**, with the reason each number has its value:

| Parameter | Value | What it does | Reason, and what a change would do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Withdrawal threshold | R̂ < 3 | A confirmatory cell below it withdraws the speed claim for the class | A convention at the bottom of the range expected for quadruped briefs (3 to 10), so withdrawal at the partial gate is a live outcome. Lower: the claim survives weaker evidence. Higher: classes expected near 3 to 5 are withdrawn even when the thesis's own range holds |
| Word threshold | R̂ ≥ 10 | Needed for the verdict `ten-fold` | The claim's own number. No honest threshold rule can confirm a true ratio of exactly 10 more than about half the time; the pooled rule reaches that half and 80% at a true ratio near 11 to 12 |
| Guard floor | Median per-family ratio above 5, by an exact binomial test | Stops a few extreme families from carrying the word | Inherited from the first draft, whose lower bound had to exceed 5 and which never confirmed a true ratio of 5; under the pooled rule a true ratio of 5 is called ten-fold with probability near zero |
| Tie factor | RMT_C ÷ RMT_A ≤ 1.5 | A short program over the rival's engine within this factor is a tie | "Comparable time" made precise; a larger factor makes ties (and shipping the layer) more frequent |
| Non-inferiority margin | 0.05 | Arm A's completion and coverage may trail arm B's by less than this for the word | Frozen before sealing; a larger margin lets a faster arm finish fewer missions |
| Confirmatory cells | At most 4 | Holm's procedure holds the gate-wide error at 5% across them | With four cells the first step needs 18 of 24; more cells need larger suites |
| Recovery thresholds | r_T and r_C ≥ 0.8: a layer is enough; both < 0.5: the engine is a source of the gain | Decide the engine-necessity bet per stratum | TARGET thresholds registered before the sealed suite opens |
| Warm reuse count | N = 10 | The warm verdict is evaluated there | A class that needs a hundred reuses to pay for itself is reported as such and not summarized by its limit |
| Level | α = 0.05 | One-sided bounds and tests | Convention |

2. **Evidence shown to the owner.** The illustrative power table (for 24 families and a per-family log-ratio
   spread of 0.5: P(`ten-fold`) = 0.08 at a true ratio of 8.5, 0.50 at 10, 0.94 at 12; P(`withdrawn`) = 0.50 at
   a true ratio of 3 and 0.000 at 5); the measured lever shares and ceilings from the incumbent baseline; the
   statement that between true ratios of 7 and 10 the word can be awarded by chance in up to a fifth of gates,
   which is why the interval is printed beside the word.
3. **Decision forms.** Ratify as written; or amend, with each amendment dated and reasoned; an amendment is
   possible only before the first suite hash of a gate and applies to that gate and later ones.
4. **Binding.** The ratified block is serialized as exact rationals in canonical JSON; its digest goes into the
   event-log entry that commits the first suite hash. After that entry a change is refused
   (`tenfold.parameters-frozen`). The serialization and the file are this bead's; the binding and the refusal
   are performed by the outcome engine (N39b-05), which loads this file. The same block governs the partial gate
   and the tournament unless amended between them, before the tournament's suite hash; an amendment is a new
   block file with its own digest, date and reason, and the earlier one is kept.
5. **Record.** `decisions/OPEN-TENFOLD-8.md` with the table, the evidence digests, the decision, the block's
   digest and the date.
   One decision has one record of authority (DESIGN, the same statement in N41-15 and in the four
   decision beads N39b-28, N39b-29, N39b-31 and N39b-33): the ADR that the open-question register requires, which
   the register row's `adr` field cites. The ADRs are `ADR-PROG-15` (OPEN-TENFOLD-3, advocates), `ADR-PROG-16`
   (OPEN-TENFOLD-4, envelope), `ADR-PROG-17` (OPEN-TENFOLD-6, agent) and `ADR-PROG-18` (OPEN-TENFOLD-8, thresholds),
   under `docs/adr/`. The file `decisions/OPEN-TENFOLD-<n>.md` is the ADR's supporting file: the request, the
   options, the figures and the evidence. The ADR cites it by path and digest, and it states no decision that the
   ADR lacks. Reason: the register's rule is that a decision or a default is an ADR, while the request and its
   arithmetic are too long for one and change until the decision is taken.
   This bead writes `ADR-PROG-18` when the owner ratifies or amends; if the first suite hash is due first, the
   register's default writer writes it with the default "as written".
6. **The block and the configuration record.** The canonical JSON block of step 4 is the only source of the
   parameters. The `[thresholds]` table of `registries/tenfold/owner-inputs.toml` (N41-15) is generated from it:
   it repeats the six constants of the question as exact rational strings (`withdraw_below = "3"`,
   `confirm_at = "10"`, `guard_floor = "5"`, `tie_factor = "3/2"`, `margin = "1/20"`,
   `confirmatory_cells_max = 4`) and names the block by `block_digest`; the recovery thresholds, the warm reuse
   count and the level are read from the block itself. The block's field names are the outcome engine's
   (N39b-05) and the table's are the configuration record's (N41-15); the projection is fixed: `withdraw_below`
   ← `withdraw_below`, `confirm_at` ← `word_at`, `guard_floor` ← `guard_floor`, `tie_factor` ← `tie_factor`,
   `margin` ← `noninferiority_margin`, `confirmatory_cells_max` ← `max_confirmatory_cells`; the comparison below
   uses this map and no other. A configuration record whose values differ from the block,
   or that holds a threshold as a binary floating-point literal, is rejected. The block's digest is bound in the
   event-log entry that commits the first suite hash; the configuration record's digest is embedded in the suite
   manifest at each freeze. These are two digests of two objects, and the first governs the freeze of the
   parameters.
7. **The non-inferiority record agrees with the block.** N39a-31's parameter-set record
   `tenfold/<gate>/noninferiority.json` holds only what the block does not (`option`, `mode`, `population_mode`)
   and copies `margin` and `level` from the ratified block. The block's loader refuses a block whose
   `noninferiority_margin` differs from that record's `margin`, whose `level` differs from its `level`, or whose
   named non-inferiority option differs from its `option`, naming the field and both values; an amendment that
   moves the margin (options C and E of N39a-31) is therefore an amendment of the block and of the record together,
   never of one alone.
8. **Check command (DESIGN).** `frob-tenfold params check <block.json> [--config <owner-inputs.toml>]
   [--noninferiority <noninferiority.json>] --json`, a subcommand of the operator binary (N39a-01), runs the
   loader: it refuses a binary floating-point literal, recomputes the block's digest, compares the generated
   `[thresholds]` table by the projection of step 6 and the non-inferiority record by step 7, and exits 0 with
   the block's digest, or 3 naming each differing field with both values.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ratification (or the default "as written") is recorded with the block's digest before the first suite
      hash is committed.
- [ ] The digest in the suite-hash entry equals the ratified block's digest.
- [ ] An attempted change after that entry is refused; an amendment between gates is accepted with its date.
- [ ] The one-page statement is attached to the decision and cites measured baseline figures, not only planning
      values.
- [ ] A block whose margin, level or named non-inferiority option differs from N39a-31's record
      `tenfold/<gate>/noninferiority.json` is refused, naming the field and both values.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::params`: `block_serializes_exact_rationals` (3/2 and 1/20, never 1.5
or 0.05 as binary floats); `digest_stable_across_field_order`; `default_equals_written_values` (the ten values
of the block equal the plan's: 3, 10, 5, 3/2, 1/20, 4, 4/5, 1/2, 10, 1/20); `amendment_needs_date_and_reason`
(an amendment block without its date, its reason or the evidence that prompted it is refused, and the earlier
block is kept); `config_table_matches_block` (a generated `[thresholds]` table whose six values differ from the
block's, or that holds a float literal, is rejected); `noninferiority_record_matches_block` (a record with margin
1/10 against the block's 1/20, a level of 1/40 against 1/20, or option C against the block's option A is refused
naming the field; the record written from the same decision loads); `one_page_cites_measured_figures` (the
statement refuses to render with planning values only once the baseline record exists). The freeze after the first suite hash and the
acceptance of an amendment between gates are tested where they are enforced (N39b-05, `params_frozen_after_hash`)
and end to end below.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_params_block.sh`, owned by this bead and built only on its dependencies (header `bead=N39b-33
milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e gates=GATE-TENFOLD-04 seed=393301 needs=cpu
plants=float-literal,table-mismatch,noninferiority-mismatch,undated-amendment`): (1) `FROB_TEST_SEED=393301 cargo
test -p frob-conformance tenfold::params`; (2) writes the default block ("as written") with the canonical writer,
generates the `[thresholds]` table of a scratch configuration record and N39a-31's default non-inferiority record,
and runs `frob-tenfold params check` on the three (exit 0, the digest equal across two writes and across field
order); (3) `frob-tenfold require-owner-inputs --json` (N41-15) on the scratch configuration with the four rows
`defaulted` (exit 0 with the configuration digest); (4) the plants, each through `e2e_expect_refusal`: a table
with `tie_factor = 1.5` as a float literal and a table whose `margin` differs from the block (exit 3 from both
commands), a non-inferiority record with margin 1/10 (exit 3 naming `margin`), an amendment block without its
date and reason (refused, the earlier block kept). Logged as NDJSON through the STD-E2E helper (`ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, seed): the block's ten values as exact strings,
the block's digest and the configuration record's digest, each refusal with its field and both values, each plant
as a `negative-control` assertion with `caught`; on failure the first differing field; a final `summary` with
assertion counts. The binding of the digest into the suite-hash entry and the refusal of a later change
(`tenfold.parameters-frozen`) are the outcome engine's (N39b-05) and are asserted in step `seal` of
`scripts/e2e/tenfold_partial_gate.sh` (N39b-12), which runs this script as a stage.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: 18 of 24 at the first Holm step with four cells; the power figures quoted above as planning
values. Negative control: a build that reads thresholds from a mutable configuration file after sealing.

## Considerations

- These numbers are DESIGN choices and TARGETs, not measurements; the record must say so.
- Changing a threshold after seeing even development-suite ratios invites tuning the rule to the expected
  result; amendments therefore state what evidence prompted them.
