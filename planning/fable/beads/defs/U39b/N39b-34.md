## Background

`eval.preanalyze` is the product verb that, for a study with a sample size fixed in advance, lists the verdicts
its rule can return at that size and level and the power at declared alternatives; a fixed-sample study without
such a pre-analysis is not admitted. The ten-fold test is a fixed-sample study with an unusual question: when
may a ratio measured on a sealed suite be stated about the population of missions? This bead extends the verb
to accept a ten-fold study plan and to answer that before any suite is sealed. Owner crate: `frob-arena` (the
evaluation subsystem); the harness that calls it is in `frob-conformance`.

The mathematics (DESIGN, derived; hand-checkable). The claim is R_time ≥ 10, where
R_time = Σᵢ min(T_rival,i, H) ÷ Σᵢ min(T_frob,i, H). Form the bounded paired contrast per independent family
Xᵢ = min(T_rival,i, H) − 10·min(T_frob,i, H), which lies in [−10·H, H]; the claim is E[X] ≥ 0. Suppose a fraction
p of families has X = −10·H (we time out, the rival is instant) and the rest have X = H. Then E[X] = H·(1 − 11p),
negative for p > 1/11, and a suite of n families shows no slow family with probability (1 − p)ⁿ. Any procedure
with one-sided error α that reports a nonnegative bound on the best possible sample must therefore have
(10/11)ⁿ ≤ α, so n ≥ ln(1/α)/ln(1.1): 32 families at α = 0.05, 46 at 0.0125 (four strata sharing the level),
49 at 0.01. The registered distribution-free bound X̄ − 11·H·√(ln(1/α)/(2n)) needs n ≥ 121·ln(1/α)/2, which is 182
at α = 0.05, even on that best sample. With 24 families, zero slow missions is compatible at the 5% level with a
slow-family rate of 1 − 0.05^(1/24) = 11.7%, and 11 × 0.117 exceeds 1. Hence two result types: a *population
result* (enough independent families for its registered bound) and a *finite-suite result* (an exact statement
about the sealed missions, with no population wording).

## Technical approach

**Input (DESIGN, a variant of the study-plan record; exact numbers are strings).**

```json
{ "study": "tenfold", "claim_ratio": "10", "cell": {"stratum": "legged-quad", "regime": "cold"},
  "result_type": "population", "alpha": "0.05", "confirmatory_cells": 1, "deadline_s": "14400",
  "families": 24, "campaigns_per_family": 1,
  "clusters": [{"family": "f:…", "site": "s:…", "material_batch": "b:…", "missions": 1}],
  "bound": "bounded-paired-contrast-hoeffding/1",
  "alternatives": [{"name": "rare-tail", "rival_s": "20", "fast_s": "1", "slow_s": "999", "slow_fraction": "0.01"}] }
```

**Computation.**
1. *Unit of inference.* Missions that share a family, an originating site or a material batch form one cluster;
   n is the number of connected components of that relation, not the number of rows. 24 rows from one site are
   one replicate (n = 1).
2. *Level.* α_cell = alpha ÷ confirmatory_cells for the necessary-count check (a Bonferroni split; the guard
   itself uses Holm's step-down and is reported with its first-step count).
3. *Necessary count.* n_min = ⌈ln(1/α_cell)/ln(1.1)⌉. *Count required by the registered bound.* For
   `bounded-paired-contrast-hoeffding/1`: n_bound = ⌈121·ln(1/α_cell)/2⌉. Both are computed with outward-rounded
   interval arithmetic on the logarithms so that the ceiling is certain; if the interval straddles an integer
   the larger count is returned.
4. *Reachable verdicts.* Always `disqualified`, `descriptive`. `withdrawn` and `measured` when n ≥ 10. `ten-fold`
   (finite-suite wording) when n ≥ 10 and the exact guard is reachable at the cell's adjusted level (critical
   count returned: for example 17 of 24 at 0.05, 18 of 24 at 0.0125; none at n = 4). `ten-fold` with population
   wording only when `result_type` is `population` and n ≥ max(n_min, n_bound).
5. *Zero-count bound.* For the plan's n: the one-sided upper bound 1 − α^(1/n) on a rate observed at zero counts.
6. *Alternatives.* For each declared two-point population: the population ratio
   rival ÷ (fast·(1 − p) + slow·p) with each time restricted at H; the probability (1 − p)ⁿ that the suite shows
   no slow family; E[X] ÷ H = (rival − 10·(fast·(1 − p) + slow·p)) ÷ H; and the statement of what a
   percentile bootstrap would report on an all-fast sample (rival ÷ fast).
7. *Estimand reminder.* The output names both estimands and states that the word attaches to R_time only.

**Output (envelope `result`).** `n_effective`, `alpha_cell`, `n_min`, `n_bound`, `guard: {critical, tail}`,
`reachable_verdicts`, `zero_count_upper`, `alternatives[…]`, `result_type_admitted`, `preanalysis_hash`.
Evidence: exact arithmetic on the plan; determinism class D0; claim kind known-answer; the no-authorization
flags as on every envelope.

**Refusals.**
- `suite-too-small-for-population-claim` (new code in the study block; exit 3): `result_type` is `population`
  and n_effective < max(n_min, n_bound). `observed`: n_effective; `required`: both counts. Ranked repairs: declare
  a finite-suite result; enlarge the cell to the required count; name fewer confirmatory cells; register a
  bound with a smaller required count together with its proof.
- FROB-REF-532: the plan names a verdict it cannot reach (for example a confirmatory cell of four families).
- The usual schema refusals for unknown fields, a number that is not an exact string, or a missing unit.

**Surface.** `frob eval preanalyze --plan plan.json --json`; the same verb through the MCP server; `study.plan`
calls it for ten-fold studies as it does for other fixed-sample studies.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For α = 0.05, 0.0125 and 0.01 the verb returns n_min = 32, 46, 49 and, for the registered bound, 182 at
      0.05.
- [ ] A population plan with 24 families is refused with the population refusal and the four repairs in order;
      the same plan as `finite-suite` is admitted and lists `ten-fold` with finite-suite wording as reachable.
- [ ] A plan of 24 missions on one site reports n_effective = 1 and reaches only `disqualified` and
      `descriptive`.
- [ ] The rare-tail alternative returns the population ratio 1.8215 and the probability 0.78568 at n = 24.
- [ ] The verb is reachable through `frob --json` and the MCP server with a documented schema, examples and
      refusal list; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-arena` is updated in its ten sections.
- [ ] Cancellation leaves no state (the verb is a pure read).

## Considerations

- 182 families per population cell is far beyond the default suite (24); in practice population wording will
  be rare unless a tighter valid bound is registered with its proof. That is the intended reading of the rule.
- The Bonferroni split is used for the necessary count because it is what the derivation assumes; it is
  slightly conservative relative to Holm.
- A plan's `alternatives` are illustrations for the reader of the pre-analysis; they are not hypotheses tested.
