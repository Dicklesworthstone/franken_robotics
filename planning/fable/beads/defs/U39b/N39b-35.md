## Background

The ten-fold test reports a ratio measured on a finite sealed suite. Three inference questions were fixed before
any suite is opened, because each has a tempting wrong answer. Which quantity is "the ratio"? When does a ratio
on the suite say something about missions in general? And how is a rare failure reported when none was seen?
This bead implements the answers in the harness: two estimands that are never interchanged; a test for the
population claim that stays valid when the sample happens to contain no slow mission; the declaration of each
stratum as a population result or a finite-suite result; and intervals that are valid at zero counts.

The procedure that is invalid here, and why. Let H = 1,000 s, the rival always take 20 s, and the candidate take
1 s on 99% of families and 999 s on 1%. Both always complete. The population ratio is 20/10.98 = 1.8215. A suite
of 24 families contains only fast missions with probability 0.99²⁴ = 0.78568, and then every percentile-bootstrap
resample reports 20. Resampling cannot show a tail that the sample does not contain.

## Scope

In: R_time and R_rate; makespan rules; the cluster structure as the resampling unit; the bounded paired contrast
and its bound; result types; paired differences for completion and coverage; zero-count intervals. Out: the
pre-analysis verb (N39b-34); the bootstrap interval, guard and verdict rule (statistics beads; the scorer
N39b-19 calls this module); the simulation gate (N39b-36).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/score/contrast.rs` and `estimands.rs` (standard library only, part
of the scorer).

1. **Two estimands.** R_time = Σᵢ min(T_rival,i, H) ÷ Σᵢ min(T_frob,i, H). R_rate = (accepted_frob ÷ makespan_frob)
   ÷ (accepted_rival ÷ makespan_rival). The word ten-fold attaches to R_time only; a statement about decisions
   per hour cites R_rate. Both are always computed and printed with their names.
2. **Makespan.** For serial campaigns the makespan of an arm is the sum of its restricted campaign times. With
   parallel jobs it is the measured campaign makespan under matched resource caps (first start to last end of
   the arm's campaigns in the cell, from the event log). Each arm's independent acceptance is timestamped
   before the joint reveal, and those timestamps are the ones used.
3. **Unit of inference.** The independent mission family. Missions that share a family, an originating site or a
   material batch form one cluster; connected groups stay together across the development and sealed splits
   (a cluster with a member in the development pool cannot have a member in the sealed suite: checked). The
   resampling unit, H and the cluster structure are read from the suite's registration, never inferred from data.
4. **Bounded paired contrast.** For each cluster i, Xᵢ = (1/kᵢ)·Σ over its kᵢ pairs of
   [min(T_rival, H) − 10·min(T_frob, H)], which lies in [−10·H, H]. The claim R_time ≥ 10 is E[X] ≥ 0. With n
   clusters, mean X̄ and level α, the distribution-free one-sided lower bound (Hoeffding, range 11·H) is
   LB = X̄ − 11·H·√(ln(1/α)/(2n)). The population claim is made only when a registered valid bound is
   nonnegative. The bound's identifier (`bounded-paired-contrast-hoeffding/1`) is part of the registration; a
   different bound may be registered before sealing with its proof and its required count.
   Evaluation is exact where possible: X̄ is a rational; the square root and logarithm are enclosed by
   outward-rounded intervals and LB ≥ 0 is decided on the lower end, so that a rounding error can never grant
   the claim.
5. **Result types.** Each stratum declares one before sealing. *Population result*: n at least the count its
   registered bound requires and never fewer than ⌈ln(1/α)/ln(1.1)⌉; reported with LB; population wording
   allowed only if LB ≥ 0 and the cell's verdict is `ten-fold`. *Finite-suite result*: the ratio on the sealed
   missions as an exact fact about that suite, with its completion table and phase ledger, and no population
   wording. The scorer refuses to compute a population statement for a cell registered as finite-suite.
6. **Completion and useful coverage** are tested as paired differences against non-inferiority margins frozen
   with the suite: per cluster, dᵢ = (share of arm A's campaigns accepted by H) − (arm B's share); the
   non-inferiority test of the statistics module is applied to the dᵢ with margin 0.05; on family-answer strata
   the same for the share of mandatory obligations discharged.
7. **Rare binary failures** (a missed withdrawal, a false positive, a missed deadline at zero count): 0 of n
   gives the one-sided upper bound 1 − α^(1/n) (n = 24: 11.7%); k of n gives the exact Clopper–Pearson upper
   bound. A bootstrap of n zeros has zero width and is never used; the scorer has no code path that bootstraps
   a binary rate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the 25-mission case (rival 50 s each; candidate 24 at 1 s and one timeout at H = 100 s) the module
      returns R_time = 10.081 and R_rate = 9.677, each labelled.
- [ ] On an all-fast sample of 24 clusters from the rare-tail population (every X = 20 − 10·1 = 10 s, H = 1,000 s)
      the contrast bound is negative: LB = 10 − 11,000·√(ln 20/48) = 10 − 2,748 s < 0; no population claim is made.
- [ ] On the best possible sample (every X = H) LB is nonnegative at n = 182 and negative at n = 181 (the
      bound's own required count).
- [ ] A cluster split across development and sealed sets is refused at registration check.
- [ ] 0 of 24 returns 0.1174; no binary rate is ever given a bootstrap interval.
- [ ] A population statement requested for a finite-suite cell is refused.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: rows TF-14 and TF-17 to TF-19 of the harness registry. Negative controls: the percentile

leaves the sign of LB unchanged.

## Considerations

- The Hoeffding bound is deliberately crude; it buys validity without assumptions at the price of 182 families.
  A sharper valid bound (for example one using the empirical variance) may be registered before sealing with
  its proof; the necessary count 32 at α = 0.05 remains a floor for any such bound.
- With a finite-suite result the report says "ten-fold on the sealed suite"; the wording lint enforces it.
- Risk (pre-mortem): a rare slow mission is absent from the sealed sample and a resampling interval certifies a
  ratio the population does not have; this module is the countermeasure.
