## Background

The sealed suite of the tournament adds three mission types to the three available at the partial gate: changing
an accepted carrier or walker (`rebody`), recommissioning a facility (`facility`, a Moonshot stratum that runs
only if its own gate has passed and is otherwise `not run`), and a heterogeneous high-throughput family
(`throughput`). As in the first set, each has private changes the adjudicator applies when deriving sealed
variants and a strongest conventional control built into the rival arm. Two of the three are *amendment*
missions: the arm holds an accepted answer and must say, after a change, which earlier verdicts survive, which
are withdrawn, and what the repaired answer is. This bead supplies the operators and controls for the three
types and the rules for warm campaigns, in which a declared sequence of amendments is applied to an accepted
cold answer.

## Scope

In: operators and controls for the three types; amendment-sequence generation; the adjudicator's ground truth
for withdrawals; the warm-campaign accounting rules as checks. Out: the first three types and the mutation
classes (N39b-40); the scorer's warm formulas (N39b-19); the hygiene measurement (N39b-42).

## Technical approach

Modules under `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/suitegen/` (adjudicator side only).

| Sealed mission type | Private changes (operators) | Strongest control in the rival arm |
|---|---|---|
| Changing an accepted carrier or walker (`rebody`) | Replace a gearbox, a wheel or a lower limb; perturb latency, friction or energy reserve | Retraining; a shared conditioned actor; warm-started control; ordinary graph caching |
| Recommissioning a facility (`facility`) | Move a door or a counter; perturb scan uncertainty; reduce a shared resource; alter a shift | Recomputation; incremental mixed-integer programming; standard motion planning; selective replay with cached bounds |
| A heterogeneous high-throughput family (`throughput`) | Mix fixed and changed topology; grazing contacts; dense depth sensors; pressure to spill to the host | An optimized batched incumbent; this engine with family sharing switched off |

**Notes per type.**
- *Rebody*: the task interface is frozen (the same observation and action interface and requirement); only the
  body or a plant parameter changes. The answer kind is an amendment verdict. The reference solution gives, for
  each earlier verdict, whether it survives.
- *Facility*: only if the facility gate has passed; otherwise the stratum is declared `not run` with that reason
  before sealing. The control arm must be given the same scan data and resource model.
- *Throughput*: the complete loop is timed, including certificates, divergence handling, cold compilation and
  the learner; the answer kind is a qualified design over a family. One control is this system itself with
  family sharing switched off, which separates the batch mechanism from everything else.

**Amendment sequences (warm campaigns).**
1. *Predeclared.* The sequence of amendments for a warm mission is fixed in the sealed card before the clock
   starts; its length (TARGET: 10 amendments, so that the warm verdict at N = 10 is measured and not
   extrapolated) and its operator draws are sealed.
2. *The consequential edit is concealed.* Each sequence mixes edits that change at least one earlier verdict
   with edits that change none; the card does not say which is which. Ground truth for every earlier verdict
   after every amendment is computed by the adjudicator's reference solution and sealed.
3. *Failed amendments stay.* An amendment whose repaired answer is not accepted by its deadline stays in the
   sequence and costs its deadline; later amendments still run.
4. *Both error kinds are counted.* A *missed withdrawal* (the arm keeps a verdict the ground truth withdraws)
   fails the mission. An *unnecessary invalidation* (the arm withdraws a verdict that survives) is counted and
   reported per arm; it costs time, not acceptance.
5. *Identical reusable inputs and equal cache budgets* for every arm; a fair rival also caches graphs, factors,
   compiled kernels and policies.
6. *One-time authoring is amortized over 1, 10 and 100 amendments.* With S an arm's standing-asset and one-time
   authoring cost and ā, b̄ the pooled restricted mean times per amendment, R_warm(N) = (S_B + N·b̄) ÷ (S_A + N·ā)
   is reported at N = 1, 10, 100 and in the limit b̄ ÷ ā, with the crossover count N*. Accepted count, total time
   and time per acceptance are reported separately. A warm result is never quoted without S and N*.

**Generator output.** Per family: the cold card; the sealed amendment sequence; the ground-truth table
(verdict × amendment → survives or withdrawn); the reference repaired answers; the strongest-control declaration.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Operators for `rebody` and `throughput` exist with admissible ranges and stay inside the public class
      descriptions; `facility` operators exist behind the condition on its gate.
- [ ] Each generated amendment sequence contains at least one consequential and one inconsequential edit, and
      the arm-visible card reveals neither which nor how many.
- [ ] The ground-truth table is produced by a reference solution independent of every arm and verified on
      development families.
- [ ] A planted arm that keeps a withdrawn verdict fails the mission; one that withdraws a surviving verdict
      passes with one unnecessary invalidation counted.
- [ ] A failed amendment remains in the sequence at its deadline in the warm totals.
- [ ] `facility` is registered `not run` with the reason when its gate has not passed.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::suitegen2`: `gearbox_swap_withdraws_torque_verdicts` (live dependency:
a changed reduction ratio withdraws the verdicts that depend on output torque and keeps the geometric ones);
`friction_perturbation_inconsequential_case`; `sequence_has_both_kinds`; `card_hides_consequence`;
`missed_withdrawal_fails`; `unnecessary_invalidation_counts`; `failed_amendment_costs_deadline`;
`throughput_control_sharing_off_is_same_engine`; `facility_not_run_without_gate`;
`warm_card_amortization` (S_A = 14,400 s, ā = 470 s, b̄ = 4,050 s: 0.272, 2.120, 6.596, limit 8.617, N* = 5).

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_suitegen.sh` (extended from N39b-40) generates development-pool families of the two types
with sequences of ten amendments, runs a reference arm and two planted arms through them, and asserts the
amendment tables. Logged: per sequence the counts of consequential edits, missed withdrawals and unnecessary
invalidations, and the warm ratios with S and N*.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: the warm card. Plan-time record (FACT to be re-run): on a four-state edit fixture a correctly
keyed dependency graph ties a semantic receipt (3 pair evaluations and 2 reductions each), and omitting one
dependency inherits a stale promotion, which is the missed-withdrawal control here. Negative controls: TF-CX-21
(a kept verdict the truth withdraws); a sequence generator that labels the consequential edit in the public
card.

## Considerations

- A hundred amendments are not run; R_warm(100) and the limit are computed from ā and b̄ measured over the
  declared sequence, and the report says so.
- The `rebody` controls include ordinary graph caching, which tied the semantic receipt in the pilot; reuse is
  expected to be hygiene, not a source of the ratio.
- `throughput` under memory pressure must record rows that did not fit as rows, marked, not drop them.
