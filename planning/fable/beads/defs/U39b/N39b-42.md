## Background

GATE-TENFOLD-12 (claim kind: Known answer; M7) is the gate of suite hygiene: every stratum holds at least one
routine easy win and one unresolved answer; the checker rejects 100% of the planted unit, contact, clock and
passive-load mutations; in the amendment sequences an arm keeps its scoped claim only with zero missed
withdrawals, and unnecessary invalidations are reported per arm. If the gate is not met, the affected stratum is
`descriptive`. The reasoning behind each clause: a suite with no mission a conventional method wins cannot show
that the rival was given a fair chance; a suite with no mission whose right answer is `unknown` rewards arms
that always answer; an acceptance procedure that lets a unit error through will accept wrong answers quickly,
which is exactly what a time score would reward; and an arm that keeps verdicts the truth withdraws is fast
because it is wrong. This bead is the script that measures the gate, in two parts: before the suite opens
(composition and mutation rejection) and after the gate closes (withdrawals).

## Scope

In: the script and its two phases; the counting rules (module `tenfold::hygiene::measure` of
`frob-conformance`); the per-stratum table with a count for every property of the gate; the two phase
receipts; the registration, before the suite opens, of the strata that fail composition or mutation
rejection.

Out, with the bead that has it: generating missions and mutants (N39b-40, N39b-41); the acceptance procedure;
scoring; the gate itself as a program gate (N40-21, which depends on this bead): the function `tenfold12`
that turns this script's two receipts and the run record into one verdict per stratum, the one row of
GATE-TENFOLD-12 in the gate register, the consequence as printed in the tournament report, and the test of
that function on a synthetic tournament. This bead measures and N40-21 judges and records (DESIGN: both beads
had measured the gate, applied its consequence and recorded it). The one consequence this bead applies itself
is the one that cannot wait for the tournament: a stratum that fails composition or mutation rejection is
registered `descriptive` before the suite-open entry, and N40-21's verdict must agree with that registration.

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_suite_hygiene.sh`; header `bead=N39b-42 milestone=M7 labels=tenfold,e2e
gates=GATE-TENFOLD-12 seed=391221 plants=no-easy-win,no-unresolved,unit-blind-checker,missed-withdrawal,
site-as-families`. The script runs under the adjudicator role, because it reads sealed registration data; its
log contains counts and identifiers of strata only, never sealed content.

**Phase `pre-open` (before the suite-open entry of the event log).**
1. `composition`: for every stratum, from the sealed registration: the number of independent families (clusters,
   not rows); the number flagged easy win and flagged unresolved; the design-stratum tier traps. Assert at least
   one easy win and at least one unresolved answer per stratum (the suite's own quotas, one family in six and
   one in twelve, are reported beside the gate's minimum). Assert at least two sealed families per cell of the
   coverage grid or a written `not applicable`. Also counted here, for the gate's other properties: that every
   easy-win and unresolved tag was committed before the suite hash (a tag dated after it is not a tag); and,
   per design stratum, the number of worlds that come from an evaluator independent of the proposing engine.
2. `easy-win-check`: on the development pool, the frozen rival wins or ties at least one easy-win family per
   stratum (otherwise the rival is presumed under-built; reported to the freeze gate).
3. `mutations`: for every sealed family, the four planted mutant answers (unit, contact order, causal clock,
   passive load) are submitted to the acceptance procedure by the adjudicator's own session, outside any arm's
   clock. Assert rejected = planted, per class and per stratum (100%). A mutant that is accepted is listed by
   stratum and class.
4. `independence`: assert that no family of the sealed suite shares a generator draw, a site, a material batch
   or a parent design with another family counted as independent, and none served an earlier gate.
5. Decision: a stratum failing step 1 or step 3 is registered `descriptive` before the suite opens, and the
   report's protocol section says so.

**Phase `post-close` (after reveal).**
6. `withdrawals`: for every amendment sequence and arm, compare the arm's amendment verdicts with the sealed
   ground truth: count missed withdrawals and unnecessary invalidations. Assert that every arm whose cell
   carries a scoped claim (a verdict other than `descriptive` or `disqualified` on an amendment stratum) has
   zero missed withdrawals in it; an arm with any loses the scoped claim on that stratum (its missions with a
   missed withdrawal are failed missions, charged the deadline, and the stratum's amendment claim is reported
   as not kept). Counted with it, for every warm cell: that the amortization rows at 1, 10 and 100 amendments
   are present, and that failed amendments are still in the sequence.
7. `tag-outcomes`: per stratum, the number of pre-tagged easy-win families that a rival arm solved, and the
   number of pre-tagged unresolved families that received no accepted verdict from any arm (with the arms
   that answered them `unknown`, which costs coverage only). The tags are never edited after sealing: an
   easy win that the rival lost is reported as lost, not re-tagged.
8. `report-table`: per stratum and arm: families, easy wins (tagged; solved by the rival), unresolved answers
   (tagged; left unresolved), external worlds, mutants planted and rejected by class, missed withdrawals,
   unnecessary invalidations, amortization rows present.

**What the counts mean for a stratum (DESIGN, stated identically in N40-21, whose function applies it; the
plan's gate text gives the three clauses and the register gives "the affected stratum is `descriptive`").**
- Easy win and unresolved answer: the stratum must hold at least one of each twice over, as tagged before
  sealing (step 1) and as it turned out (step 7: at least one tagged easy win solved by a rival arm, at least
  one tagged unresolved family left without an accepted verdict). Failing either makes the stratum
  `descriptive`. The plan's clause is "holds at least one routine easy win and one unresolved answer"; a
  stratum whose only easy wins the rival lost does not, in the event, show that the rival was allowed to win.
- Mutations: one accepted mutant in a stratum makes the stratum `descriptive`.
- Withdrawals: the consequence falls on the arm. An arm with a missed withdrawal loses its scoped claim on
  that stratum and its missions with a missed withdrawal are failed missions charged the deadline. If that
  arm is the candidate, no verdict of the ten-fold bet is cited for the stratum, which is then `descriptive`;
  if it is a rival arm, the stratum is not `descriptive` and the rival's charged failures stand in the score.
  Unnecessary invalidations are reported per arm and forfeit nothing.

**Commands.** Each phase first runs `FROB_TEST_SEED=391221 cargo test -p frob-conformance
tenfold::hygiene::measure`; steps 1 and 4 read the registered suite through `frob-tenfold suite check --controls
--json` and `frob-tenfold suite check --families --json` (registered harness commands; this script adds no
option to them) and compute the counts with the module above; step 3 submits the mutant answers through the
acceptance procedures of N39a-06 and N39a-07, each submission with its own key (`hyg-mut-<family>-<class>`).

**Logging.** Through the shared STD-E2E helper, to `target/e2e-logs/tenfold_suite_hygiene/<utc>.ndjson` with its
transcript; every line carries `ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, the exact command line, exit code and wall
time. Each count as a `metric` with its threshold (easy wins ≥ 1; unresolved ≥ 1; rejected == planted; missed
withdrawals == 0 for a kept claim), the gate tag on each, the registration digest, the phase, and for failures
the stratum and class (never the mission content); each plant as a `negative-control` assertion with `caught`
(verified by `run-all.sh --verify-plants`). Summary with
`gates: [{id: "GATE-TENFOLD-12", measured_ok, controls_caught}]`.

**Plants** (run on the development-pool copy of the suite, never on sealed data): `no-easy-win` (a stratum with
its easy-win flags removed: step 1 fails and the stratum is marked `descriptive`); `no-unresolved`;
`unit-blind-checker` (an acceptance build that ignores units: the unit mutants are accepted and step 3 fails);
`missed-withdrawal` (a planted arm keeping a withdrawn verdict: step 6 removes its claim); `site-as-families`
(24 missions of one site registered as 24 families: step 4 fails).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Phase `pre-open` is recorded before the suite-open entry; every stratum has its composition row.
- [ ] Mutants rejected equals mutants planted in every stratum and class; any accepted mutant makes the stratum
      `descriptive` and is reported.
- [ ] Phase `post-close` reports missed withdrawals and unnecessary invalidations per arm and stratum; no arm
      keeps a scoped amendment claim with a missed withdrawal.
- [ ] All five plants are caught on the development-pool copy.
- [ ] Both phases write a receipt holding, per stratum and arm, every count of the report table, the
      registration digest and the phase, and store it byte-identical at `receipts/gates/<blake3>.json` under
      its own BLAKE3 hash (N40-01, as N32a-18 does); the named copies
      `receipts/gates/GATE-TENFOLD-12/pre-open-<suite digest>.json` and `post-close-<suite digest>.json` (DESIGN
      paths) are convenience copies and are never cited. The log's summary prints both hashes; the gate's row
      is written from those hashes by N40-21 (`record_gate --receipt <hash>`), in a fresh-context session, and
      this bead writes no row in the gate register.
- [ ] Phase `post-close` reports, per stratum, tagged easy wins solved by a rival arm and tagged unresolved
      families left without an accepted verdict, and per warm cell the presence of the amortization rows and
      of failed amendments in the sequence.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::hygiene::measure`: `composition_counts_clusters_not_rows`;
`stratum_without_easy_win_is_descriptive` and `mutant_accepted_fails_stratum` (the two pre-open
registrations); `zero_missed_withdrawals_required`; `unnecessary_invalidations_reported_not_failing`;
`family_reuse_across_gates_detected`; `tag_after_suite_hash_not_counted`; `tag_outcomes_counted` (a stratum
with two tagged easy wins of which the rival solved one reports 2 and 1). The rules that turn the counts of
both receipts into a verdict per stratum are tested with the gate function (N40-21, `tenfold::hygiene::gate`).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Phase `pre-open` takes minutes plus the acceptance batteries for the mutants (four per
family); phase `post-close` is a table computation.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: with 24 families per stratum the suite quotas imply at least 4 easy wins and 2 unresolved
answers; 4 mutants per family give 96 per 24-family stratum, all rejected. Negative controls: the five plants;
controls TF-CX-18 and TF-CX-21 of the harness registry.

## Considerations

- Mutant submissions consume acceptance worlds; they are drawn from a separate sealed stream so that no arm's
  submission ever shares worlds with a mutant.
- "Routine easy win" is judged on the development pool before sealing; on the sealed suite the outcome is a
  measurement, and an easy win that the rival loses there is reported, not corrected.
- A stratum made `descriptive` here still runs and is reported; it cannot carry the word.
