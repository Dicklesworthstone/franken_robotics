## Background

GATE-TENFOLD-12 (claim kind: Known answer; M7) is the gate of suite hygiene: every stratum holds at least one
routine easy win and one unresolved answer; the checker rejects 100% of the planted unit, contact, clock and
passive-load mutations; in the amendment sequences an arm keeps its scoped claim only with zero missed
withdrawals, and unnecessary invalidations are reported per arm. If the gate is not met, the affected stratum is
`descriptive`. The reasoning behind each clause: a suite with no mission a conventional method wins cannot show
that the rival was given a fair chance; a suite with no mission whose right answer is `unknown` rewards arms
that always answer; an acceptance procedure that lets a unit error through will accept wrong answers quickly,
which is exactly what a time score would reward; and an arm that keeps verdicts the truth withdraws is fast
because it is wrong. This bead is the script that measures the gate, in two parts: before the suite opens
(composition and mutation rejection) and after the gate closes (withdrawals).

## Scope

In: the script and its two phases; the per-stratum table; the gate receipt; the effect on strata that fail. Out:
generating missions and mutants (N39b-40, N39b-41); the acceptance procedure; scoring.

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_suite_hygiene.sh`; header `bead=N39b-42 milestone=M7 labels=tenfold,e2e
gates=GATE-TENFOLD-12 seed=391221 plants=no-easy-win,no-unresolved,unit-blind-checker,missed-withdrawal,
site-as-families`. The script runs under the adjudicator role, because it reads sealed registration data; its
log contains counts and identifiers of strata only, never sealed content.

**Phase `pre-open` (before the suite-open entry of the event log).**
1. `composition`: for every stratum, from the sealed registration: the number of independent families (clusters,
   not rows); the number flagged easy win and flagged unresolved; the design-stratum tier traps. Assert at least
   one easy win and at least one unresolved answer per stratum (the suite's own quotas, one family in six and
   one in twelve, are reported beside the gate's minimum). Assert at least two sealed families per cell of the
   coverage grid or a written `not applicable`.
2. `easy-win-check`: on the development pool, the frozen rival wins or ties at least one easy-win family per
   stratum (otherwise the rival is presumed under-built; reported to the freeze gate).
3. `mutations`: for every sealed family, the four planted mutant answers (unit, contact order, causal clock,
   passive load) are submitted to the acceptance procedure by the adjudicator's own session, outside any arm's
   clock. Assert rejected = planted, per class and per stratum (100%). A mutant that is accepted is listed by
   stratum and class.
4. `independence`: assert that no family of the sealed suite shares a generator draw, a site, a material batch
   or a parent design with another family counted as independent, and none served an earlier gate.
5. Decision: a stratum failing step 1 or step 3 is registered `descriptive` before the suite opens, and the
   report's protocol section says so.

**Phase `post-close` (after reveal).**
6. `withdrawals`: for every amendment sequence and arm, compare the arm's amendment verdicts with the sealed
   ground truth: count missed withdrawals and unnecessary invalidations. Assert that every arm whose cell
   carries a scoped claim (a verdict other than `descriptive` or `disqualified` on an amendment stratum) has
   zero missed withdrawals in it; an arm with any loses the scoped claim on that stratum (its missions with a
   missed withdrawal are failed missions, charged the deadline, and the stratum's amendment claim is reported
   as not kept).
7. `report-table`: per stratum and arm: families, easy wins, unresolved answers, mutants planted and rejected by
   class, missed withdrawals, unnecessary invalidations.

**Logging.** Through the shared helper: each count as a `metric` with its threshold (easy wins ≥ 1; unresolved
≥ 1; rejected == planted; missed withdrawals == 0 for a kept claim), the gate tag on each, the registration
digest, the phase, and for failures the stratum and class (never the mission content). Summary with
`gates: [{id: "GATE-TENFOLD-12", measured_ok, controls_caught}]`.

**Plants** (run on the development-pool copy of the suite, never on sealed data): `no-easy-win` (a stratum with
its easy-win flags removed: step 1 fails and the stratum is marked `descriptive`); `no-unresolved`;
`unit-blind-checker` (an acceptance build that ignores units: the unit mutants are accepted and step 3 fails);
`missed-withdrawal` (a planted arm keeping a withdrawn verdict: step 6 removes its claim); `site-as-families`
(24 missions of one site registered as 24 families: step 4 fails).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Phase `pre-open` is recorded before the suite-open entry; every stratum has its composition row.
- [ ] Mutants rejected equals mutants planted in every stratum and class; any accepted mutant makes the stratum
      `descriptive` and is reported.
- [ ] Phase `post-close` reports missed withdrawals and unnecessary invalidations per arm and stratum; no arm
      keeps a scoped amendment claim with a missed withdrawal.
- [ ] All five plants are caught on the development-pool copy.
- [ ] The gate's row cites both phases' receipts and is recorded by a fresh-context session.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::hygiene`: `composition_counts_clusters_not_rows`;
`stratum_without_easy_win_is_descriptive`; `mutant_accepted_fails_stratum`; `zero_missed_withdrawals_required`;
`unnecessary_invalidations_reported_not_failing`; `family_reuse_across_gates_detected`.

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. Phase `pre-open` takes minutes plus the acceptance batteries for the mutants (four per
family); phase `post-close` is a table computation.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: with 24 families per stratum the suite quotas imply at least 4 easy wins and 2 unresolved
answers; 4 mutants per family give 96 per 24-family stratum, all rejected. Negative controls: the five plants;
controls TF-CX-18 and TF-CX-21 of the harness registry.

## Considerations

- Mutant submissions consume acceptance worlds; they are drawn from a separate sealed stream so that no arm's
  submission ever shares worlds with a mutant.
- "Routine easy win" is judged on the development pool before sealing; on the sealed suite the outcome is a
  measurement, and an easy win that the rival loses there is reported, not corrected.
- A stratum made `descriptive` here still runs and is reported; it cannot carry the word.
