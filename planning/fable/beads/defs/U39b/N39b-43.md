## Background

One ratio cannot answer the two questions the ten-fold test is asked: how far this system is ahead of the
incumbent pipeline as it is commonly run, and how much of that lead an incumbent engine recovers once every
portable method is layered over it. Every stratum is therefore run with three arms on the same sealed missions,
acceptance protocol, hardware and resource caps:

| Arm | What it is | Frozen by |
|---|---|---|
| B, the declared incumbent | A leading incumbent simulator with per-design reinforcement learning and a standard outer-loop optimizer | Its own advocates, on development fixtures |
| W, the composed incumbent | The same engine or engines with every portable method layered on: native state restore and forks, per-topology buckets or the engine's own heterogeneous solver, a sampling controller or a multi-embodiment prior with checkpoint resume, racing and stopping, importance-weighted failure search, a second engine as referee | A competent baseline engineer, on development fixtures |
| F, this system | The release under test | The release |

Naming: elsewhere in the protocol F is called arm A, and the first form of W is arm C, a program of at most
2,000 lines over arm B's engine; W is built as one pipeline before F exists and is not held to 2,000 lines where
a portable method needs more. The tie test and the recovery ratio are both computed on that arm. This bead
computes the three-arm statistics; the decisions that read them are N39b-07 (the tie kill gate) and the
outcome engine (N39b-05).

## Scope

In: the tie test with its lower bound and scope comparison; r_T and r_C with 95% intervals; the three fixed
cases; the reading (layer enough, engine a source, mixed with the unrecovered phase named); the rule for which
rival governs the word. Out: the ablations (N39b-45); the verb that prints these (N39b-46); decisions.

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/score/threearm.rs` (standard library only; part of the scorer).

**Quantities per stratum and regime.** T_X = the restricted mean time of arm X: (1/n)·Σ_j min(T_X,j, H) over the
stratum's campaigns; a mission unresolved at the deadline H costs H in every arm. C_X = the arm's GPU-hours on
the stratum: the sum over its campaigns of device-seconds in the resource table, divided by 3,600 (a censored
campaign contributes what it actually used; standing-asset build cost is reported beside it, not inside it).

**Tie test (ablation arm).**
1. Ratio q̂ = Σ_j min(T_C,j, H) ÷ Σ_j min(T_A,j, H) over the triples of the cell (equal to T_C ÷ T_A).
2. One-sided 95% lower bound q_L by the same bias-corrected family bootstrap as the main ratio (10,000
   replicates; families resampled with all three arms' campaigns kept together; the same seed stream).
3. Accepted scope (DESIGN, stated so that it is fixed before sealing): arm C "reaches the same accepted scope"
   when, on every mission where arm A has an accepted answer by H, arm C also has an accepted answer by H of the
   same answer kind that discharges the same mandatory obligations; the count of missions where this fails is
   printed.
4. Reading: *tie* if same scope and q̂ ≤ 1.5; *engine advantage not established* if q̂ > 1.5 and q_L ≤ 1.5;
   *engine advantage established* if q_L > 1.5; *scope differs* otherwise (no tie by this prong; the differing
   missions are listed).

**Recovery ratios.**

    r_T = (T_B − T_W) ÷ (T_B − T_F)        r_C = (C_B − C_W) ÷ (C_B − C_F)

defined when F beats B on that measure. A value of 1 means the layer recovers the whole saving and the engine
adds nothing on that stratum; 0 means the layer recovers nothing. Three cases are fixed before any measurement:
- If T_F ≥ T_B (or C_F ≥ C_B): the stratum is reported as "no saving to recover" on that measure and r is
  undefined (never computed as a quotient of non-positive numbers).
- If T_W < T_F: r exceeds 1 and the stratum is a loss to the composed incumbent.
- Otherwise 0 ≤ r ≤ 1 up to noise; a negative r (W slower than B) is printed as measured.
Intervals: two-sided 95% (the 2.5% and 97.5% bias-corrected percentiles) from the family bootstrap of the
triple (T_B, T_W, T_F), and likewise for C. Replicates in which the denominator is not positive are counted; if
they exceed 2.5% of replicates the interval is printed as "saving not established in resampling" with the
count, and no bound is given (DESIGN: a ratio with an unstable denominator has no honest finite interval).

**Reading (TARGET thresholds, registered before the sealed suite opens).**
- r_T ≥ 0.8 and r_C ≥ 0.8: a thin layer over an incumbent is enough on that stratum; the report says so and the
  fallback of the engine-necessity bet applies there.
- Both below 0.5: the engine is a source of the gain on that stratum.
- Otherwise: mixed, with the phase the layer could not recover named from the ledger: the phase with the
  largest positive difference between W's and F's pooled exclusive time.

**Which rival governs the word.** R̂_B = Σ min(T_B, H) ÷ Σ min(T_F, H) and R̂_W likewise. The governing ratio is the
smaller of the two (the stronger rival has the smaller restricted time). The word "ten-fold" is governed by F
against the stronger of B and W, never by F against B alone; both ratios are printed with the label of which
governs.

**Output.** A `three_arm` object per cell in the scorer's output: T and C per arm, q̂, q_L, scope counts, the tie
reading, r_T and r_C with intervals and case, the reading, the unrecovered phase, both ratios and the governing
one. Exact rationals for point values; class D0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With T_F = 0.10·T_B, r_T ≥ 0.8 exactly when T_W ≤ 0.28·T_B (r_T = 0.8 at 0.28; 0.79 at 0.289).
- [ ] The three fixed cases produce their labels and never a division by a non-positive saving.
- [ ] r_T and r_C are printed with 95% intervals for every stratum that has a ratio, whatever their values.
- [ ] The tie reading matches the four-way rule on constructed inputs, including a scope mismatch.
- [ ] The governing ratio is the smaller of R̂_B and R̂_W, and the report says which rival governs.
- [ ] Outputs are bit-equal across runs and platforms.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answer: row TF-21 of the harness registry. Negative controls: TF-CX-23 (the word read on F against B alone
when W is the stronger rival); a mutant that computes r with F and W exchanged; a mutant that divides when
T_F ≥ T_B. Metamorphic relations: scaling all times by a constant leaves q̂ and r_T unchanged; exchanging the
labels B and W where T_W = T_B gives r_T = 0.

## Considerations

- The recovery ratio decides by measurement what a count of feature gaps cannot: one expensive incompatibility
  can sink the layer, and several rarely exercised gaps may leave it competitive.
- Arm C under its 2,000-line cap is a lower bound on what W can do; where both are run, the stronger (smaller
  restricted time) is the W of the formulas and the other is printed beside it.
- Against a composed rival that hosts every portable lever, the expected ratio on fixed-topology missions is
  near one unless an engine-internal property binds; this module is where that expectation meets data.
