## Background

When this system finishes a design mission sooner than a rival, there are three different accounts of why. The
gain may come from *information* (deciding more per agent turn and filtering candidates cheaply), from
*controller acquisition* (not training a policy per design), or from *stepping* (a faster simulator). The
accounts imply different products, and the evidence so far is that no instrumented design session exists: where
every candidate is trained, training is about 96% of an illustrative campaign and information features that do
not remove training cap near 1.04; once controller acquisition is cheap, agent time becomes the larger share.
The phase ledger separates the three accounts if each is changed alone. This bead runs three single-change
replays on the rival's own engine, on two briefs, against a traced baseline, and checks each result against a
bound computed from the baseline's trace. It is part of GATE-TENFOLD-13: the three ablations are run on both
briefs, and a ratio above its bound voids the stratum as an accounting error.

Terms. *Traced baseline*: arm B's run of a brief with its phase ledger and its trace (the sequence of
candidates proposed, the controller obtained for each, and the steps simulated). *Replay*: a re-run that keeps
the trace fixed except for the one change. *Critical path*: the exclusive phase time that determines the clock.

## Scope

In: the two briefs; the traced baseline; the three replays; the touched share; the two bounds and the void rule;
the regime statement. Out: the recovery ratios (N39b-43); the verb that prints an ablation (N39b-46); the gate
script (N39b-47).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/ablate.rs` (DESIGN) plus wrapper scripts on arm B's engine, run out
of process by sessions in the rival-advocate role.

1. **Briefs.** One narrow wheeled brief (a differential-drive base on slopes and a curb with a loaded cart) and
   one contact-rich legged brief (a quadruped over stairs and rubble), both from the development pool.
2. **Traced baseline.** Arm B at its frozen configuration runs each brief with the common agent; the harness
   records the ledger (which must close within 2%) and the trace: for each agent turn the candidates proposed;
   for each candidate the controller acquisition (a training run with its seed and budget) and the evaluation
   steps. Baseline clock T₀; training share s₀ = `train` seconds ÷ T₀.
3. **Replays, each changing one thing.**
   a. *Information only.* Batch proposals (the agent proposes a family per turn) and a sound cheap diagnostic
      filter (a static check that rejects only candidates the baseline's own evaluation also rejected; soundness
      is verified against the trace) with the same controller acquisition and the same solver. Candidates that
      pass the filter are trained exactly as in the baseline.
   b. *Controller acquisition only.* The same candidates in the same order; per-design training is replaced by a
      qualified cheaper controller: a prior with fine-tuning, inheritance from the parent design, or a delivered
      sampling controller. "Qualified" means the acceptance outcome of each candidate is unchanged on the
      brief's evaluation battery (a candidate whose outcome flips is listed and its time is charged as in the
      baseline).
   c. *Stepping only.* The same candidates and the same controllers on a faster solver at matched fidelity (the
      engine's faster path, or more devices, with the evaluation battery's outcomes unchanged).
   Each replay returns its ratio ρ = T₀ ÷ T_replay and the share τ of the baseline's critical path that it touched
   (the baseline exclusive seconds of the phases it changed, divided by T₀).
4. **Bounds from the baseline trace.** Let s be the share of elapsed time that is training an intervention does
   not remove. Then that intervention's ratio is at most 1/s (1.43 at s = 0.7). If an intervention removes that
   share entirely its ratio is at most 1/(1 − s) (3.33). Applied: replay (a) leaves training of the surviving
   candidates, s_a = remaining `train` ÷ T₀, bound 1/s_a; replay (b) removes training, bound 1/(1 − s₀) and, with
   the controller's own cost c added back, 1/(1 − s₀ + c/T₀); replay (c) with stepping share u of T₀ sped up by k
   is bounded by 1/(1 − u + u/k) and by 1/(1 − u). In general no replay may exceed 1/(1 − τ).
5. **Void rule.** A replay whose ρ exceeds its bound is an accounting error (time was removed that the change
   did not touch, or the trace was not held fixed): the stratum's ablations are void
   (`tenfold.ablation-above-bound`), nothing from them is reported as a result, and the trace is re-taken.
6. **Regime statement.** Per brief the record states which regime the stratum is in, as a measurement:
   training-dominated (s₀ near 1: information alone is capped near 1/s₀) or turn-dominated (after cheap
   acquisition, agent time is the larger share and information becomes the larger lever).
7. **Record.** `ablations.json` per brief: T₀, the baseline ledger digest, and for each replay ρ, τ, its bound,
   the list of flipped candidates, the replay ledger digest, and a complete disclosure block. It is written by
   `frob-tenfold ablate check <brief-dir> --json` (DESIGN, a subcommand of the operator binary of N39a-01), which
   reads the traced baseline and the three replays with their ledgers and traces, checks that each replay
   changed exactly one thing, and computes ρ, τ, the bound and the status of each replay: exit 0 when every
   replay is within its bound; exit 3 with `tenfold.ablation-above-bound` (N39-REF) when one is above it, the
   stratum's ablations void; a replay whose trace changed more than its one thing is refused naming the first
   differing candidate, and a filter that rejected a candidate the baseline accepted is reported unsound with
   its time saving disallowed.

Illustration (arithmetic, not a forecast): 50 trained designs at 35 minutes each with 1.5 minutes of agent time
per design make training 1,750 of 1,825 minutes, 95.9%, and cap information alone at 1.04. With controller
acquisition at 45 s per design, agent time is 75 of 112.5 minutes, two thirds, and information becomes the
larger lever.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both briefs have a traced baseline with a closed ledger and three replays, each with ρ, τ and its bound.
- [ ] Each replay changed exactly one thing: the trace comparison shows identical candidates (for b and c) and
      identical controller acquisition and solver (for a).
- [ ] No reported ρ exceeds its bound; a planted replay reporting 1.6 where unremoved training is 70% voids the
      stratum.
- [ ] Flipped candidates in (b) and (c) are listed and charged baseline time.
- [ ] The regime statement is recorded per brief with the measured shares.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::ablate`: `bound_one_over_s` (s = 0.7: 10/7 = 1.4286);
`bound_one_over_one_minus_s` (10/3 = 3.333); `illustration_training_share` (1,750/1,825 = 0.9589; cap
1,825/1,750 = 1.043); `illustration_cheap_acquisition` (75/112.5 = 2/3); `above_bound_voids`;
`touched_share_bounds_ratio` (τ = 0.3: at most 1.4286); `trace_must_match_for_single_change`;
`flipped_candidate_charged_baseline`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_ablation.sh`, owned by this bead and built only on its dependencies (header `bead=N39b-45
milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e gates=GATE-TENFOLD-13 seed=394501 plants=above-bound,trace-mismatch,unsound-filter`).
Two modes: `fixture` (default, `needs=cpu`; recorded synthetic traces written by this bead, labelled `synthetic`:
the illustration's 50 designs with 1,750 of 1,825 minutes of training, and a stepping trace with stepping share
0.5) and `record` (`--brief-dir <dir>`; the two briefs' traced baselines and replays on arm B's engine, run out
of process through `frob-tenfold incumbent run <name> --config <hash> -- …`, BD-039; `needs=cuda`). Steps: (1)
`FROB_TEST_SEED=394501 cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::ablate`; (2) `frob-tenfold ablate check` on each
brief directory; in `fixture` mode assert the information-only bound 1,825/1,750 = 1.043 with a replay at 1.03
`within-bound`, the controller-only bound 1/(1 − 0.9589) = 24.3 with a replay at 16.2 (112.5 minutes) within
it, and the stepping bound 1/(1 − 0.5 + 0.25) = 1.333 with a replay at 1.30 within it; in `record` mode assert
for both briefs a closed baseline ledger, three replays each with ρ, τ, its bound and its status, and the regime
statement; (3) the plants: an information-only replay at 1.6 where unremoved training is 70% (TF-CX-22: exit 3,
`tenfold.ablation-above-bound`, the stratum void), a replay that silently changes the candidate set (refused,
first differing candidate named), and an unsound filter (reported, saving disallowed). Logged as NDJSON through
the STD-E2E helper (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, seed, and
the disclosure block's identifier for every timed figure): T₀ and each replay's clock, ρ against its bound as a
`metric` with threshold, τ, trace-equality assertions, flipped candidates, ledger digests, each plant as a
`negative-control` assertion with `caught`; on failure the first differing value and the trace paths; a final
`summary` with assertion counts and controls caught. The gate script `scripts/e2e/tenfold_recovery_ablation.sh`
(N39b-47, which depends on this bead) runs this script as a stage before its `baseline` and `ablate-*` steps.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: row TF-22 of the harness registry. Negative controls: TF-CX-22 (an ablation above its bound); a
replay that silently changes the candidate set (trace mismatch must be caught); a filter that is not sound
(rejects a candidate the baseline accepted) must be reported and its time saving disallowed.

## Considerations

- The ablations are on the rival's engine, so they say what an incumbent layer could gain from each account;
  they do not measure this system.
- "Matched fidelity" for the stepping replay is judged by unchanged evaluation outcomes, not by equal settings.
- Two briefs cannot represent a stratum; the result is reported as two cases and never as a stratum average.
