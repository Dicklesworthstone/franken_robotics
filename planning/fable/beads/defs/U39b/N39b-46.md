## Background

The three-arm comparison of the ten-fold test (the declared incumbent B, the composed incumbent W, this system
F) and its three phase ablations are protocol of the test, but their numbers come from phase ledgers, and the
phase ledger is owned by the evidence subsystem (`frob-chronicle`). The user-visible way to read a campaign's
ledger is the verb `report.campaign`. This bead extends that verb with two options so that an agent or an
engineer can ask the product itself for the recovery ratios and for an ablation with its bound, with the claim
and evidence of every figure attached, as for any other result:

- `report.campaign --arms B,W,F` prints T (restricted mean time), C (GPU-hours), r_T and r_C with intervals.
- `report.campaign --ablate {information, controller, stepping}` prints an ablation with its bound.

DESIGN decision: the verb's computation lives in `frob-chronicle` and is written independently of the
standalone scorer of the harness. Reason: nothing may depend on the verification crate, and two independent
implementations that must agree bit for bit on fixtures are a check on both. Rejected: calling the scorer from
the product (a dependency on the judge); having the harness call the verb for its published numbers (the judged
surface would compute its own verdict inputs).

## Scope

In: the two options, their inputs, outputs, schema, refusals and determinism; the differential test against the
scorer. Out: the definitions' own bead (N39b-43, N39b-45); the verb's base behaviour (its seed bead); verdicts
and wording (the verb prints numbers and readings, never the word).

## Technical approach

**Inputs.**
- `frob report campaign <comparison-id> --arms B,W,F [--stratum s] [--regime cold|warm] --json`. A *comparison* is
  an artifact-graph node that groups, per arm, the ledgers of the campaigns of one stratum and regime; arm F's
  are this system's own campaign records; arms B and W's are `frob.tenfold.ledger/1` records imported from the
  harness with their freeze hashes, marked as foreign and untrusted text-wise, with tier `foreign`.
  Registration of a comparison is `frob report campaign --register-comparison <manifest.json>` (read-only on
  ledgers; it writes one graph node).
- `frob report campaign <comparison-id> --ablate information|controller|stepping --json` reads the traced
  baseline's ledger and the named replay's ledger and trace summary from the same node.

**Computation for `--arms`.** With H the stratum's deadline: T_X = mean over the arm's campaigns of min(T, H);
C_X = Σ device-seconds ÷ 3,600; r_T = (T_B − T_W) ÷ (T_B − T_F); r_C = (C_B − C_W) ÷ (C_B − C_F); cases "no saving to
recover" (T_F ≥ T_B or C_F ≥ C_B: r undefined) and "loss to the composed incumbent" (T_W < T_F: r > 1); two-sided
95% intervals by the family bootstrap (families resampled, the three arms' campaigns kept together, 10,000
replicates, seed in the output; interval withheld when more than 2.5% of replicates have a non-positive
denominator); the reading (layer enough at r_T ≥ 0.8 and r_C ≥ 0.8; engine a source when both are below 0.5;
otherwise mixed, naming the phase with the largest positive W − F exclusive time); both ratios R̂_B and R̂_W with
the governing (smaller) one. Exact rationals for point values; decimal strings in the output.

**Computation for `--ablate`.** ρ = T₀ ÷ T_replay; τ = the baseline exclusive seconds of the phases the replay
changed ÷ T₀; s = the baseline training share the replay does not remove; bounds: 1/s for a replay that leaves
training, 1/(1 − s₀) for one that removes it, and 1/(1 − τ) in general; status `within-bound` or
`void: accounting-error` when ρ exceeds its bound.

**Output (`result`).** For `--arms`: `arms: {B, W, F: {T_s, C_gpu_h, campaigns, censored, ledger_open}}`,
`r_T`, `r_T_interval`, `r_C`, `r_C_interval`, `case`, `reading`, `unrecovered_phase`, `ratio_vs_B`,
`ratio_vs_W`, `governing`, `seed`, `deadline_s`, `freeze`. For `--ablate`: `kind`, `rho`, `tau`, `s`, `bound`,
`status`, `flipped_candidates`. The envelope's evidence block: claim kinds `design-comparison` and
`performance`; class `Tested` (the weakest input); tier `foreign` where a foreign ledger enters; determinism D0
for the derived figures; `reality: no-claim`; the no-authorization flags. A human-readable table is printed
without `--json`.

**Refusals.** A missing arm (no ledgers for B, W or F): the verb's missing-input refusal, naming the arm. A
performance figure whose ledgers lack a complete disclosure block: FROB-REF-862. A ratio requested without its
interval (`--no-interval` does not exist; a comparison with fewer than two families cannot give one):
FROB-REF-863. `ledger-open` runs are kept in T and excluded from phase shares, and the count is printed
(omitting it would be FROB-REF-865). An ablation above its bound is not refused: it is printed as void.

**Surfaces.** The command line and the MCP server through the verb registry; schema, examples and the refusal
list are added to the M3 verb documentation; `CONTRACT.md` of `frob-chronicle` is updated. The verb is a pure
read apart from comparison registration; cancellation leaves no state. The row's `first_milestone` is M4
(VERBS-report); the M3 handler of this bead is a reduced form of `report.campaign` that answers registered
comparisons with these options, and `frob capabilities --json` lists the verb as implemented with that form
named (VERBS-report convention 4), never as absent.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On a constructed comparison with T_B = 100, T_W = 28, T_F = 10 (units of H/100) the verb prints r_T = 0.8
      with its interval and the reading for the time measure; with T_F = 110 it prints "no saving to recover".
- [ ] `--ablate information` on the illustrative trace (training 1,750 of 1,825 minutes kept) prints the bound
      1.043; a replay reporting above its bound prints `void: accounting-error`.
- [ ] Output bytes are identical on repeated calls and equal the standalone scorer's three-arm output on the
      shared fixtures (differential test), on two platforms.
- [ ] Every figure carries the evidence block; foreign ledgers are marked; no output contains the word
      "ten-fold".
- [ ] The options are reachable through `frob --json` and MCP with documented schema and exit codes.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`cargo test -p frob-chronicle report::arms` and `report::ablate`: `recovery_from_three_ledgers`;
`gpu_hours_from_device_seconds` (7,200 device-seconds give 2 GPU-hours); `no_saving_case`; `loss_case`;
`interval_withheld_on_unstable_denominator`; `governing_is_smaller_ratio`; `ablation_bounds` (s = 0.7: 1.43 and
3.33); `void_above_bound`; `ledger_open_counted_and_excluded_from_shares`; `missing_arm_refused`;
`never_prints_the_word`. In `frob-conformance`: `differential_verb_vs_scorer` on five fixtures.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/report_campaign_arms.sh`, owned by this bead and built only on its dependencies (header
`bead=N39b-46 milestone=M3 labels=chronicle,e2e,verb:report.campaign gates=GATE-TENFOLD-13 seed=394601 needs=cpu
plants=mean-of-ratios,ledger-open-in-shares`), on constructed comparisons written by this bead (labelled
`synthetic`): (1) `FROB_TEST_SEED=394601 cargo test -p frob-chronicle report::arms`, then the same with
`report::ablate`; (2) `frob
report campaign --register-comparison <manifest.json> --json`, asserting one graph node written; (3) `frob report
campaign <id> --arms B,W,F --json` on T_B = 100, T_W = 28, T_F = 10 (r_T = 0.8 with its interval and reading),
on T_F = 110 ("no saving to recover") and on T_W = 8 (loss to the composed incumbent), asserting the envelope's
evidence block (claims `design-comparison` and `performance`, class `Tested`, tier `foreign`, `reality:
no-claim`, the no-authorization flags) and that no output contains the word "ten-fold"; (4) `--ablate
information` on the illustrative trace (bound 1.043) and on a replay above its bound (`void: accounting-error`,
printed, not refused); (5) the same calls through the MCP tool `report_campaign`, asserting equal `result`
bytes, and each call repeated with equal bytes; (6) refusals through `e2e_expect_refusal`: a missing arm (named),
a ledger without a complete disclosure block (`FROB-REF-862`), a one-family comparison (`FROB-REF-863`), and a
`ledger-open` run counted in T and excluded from phase shares with its count printed; (7) the differential test
against the scorer (`cargo test -p frob-conformance differential_verb_vs_scorer`) and the two planted builds
(per-family recovery ratios averaged; `ledger-open` runs in phase shares), each caught. Logged as NDJSON through
the STD-E2E helper (`ts`, `script`, `bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, seed): each
envelope's evidence block, r_T and r_C with intervals as `metric` lines, each ablation's ρ against its bound,
each refusal with its code and first repair, each plant as a `negative-control` assertion with `caught`; on
failure the first differing field between the two surfaces or between verb and scorer; a final `summary` with
assertion counts. The gate script `scripts/e2e/tenfold_recovery_ablation.sh` (N39b-47, which depends on this
bead) runs this script as a stage before its `register` step.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: rows TF-21 and TF-22 of the harness registry. Negative controls: a verb build that averages
per-family recovery ratios instead of forming the ratio of means (the differential test must fail); a build
that includes `ledger-open` runs in phase shares.

## Considerations

- The verb reads whatever comparison is registered; it is the harness, not the verb, that guarantees a
  comparison came from a frozen, sealed gate. A comparison registered from development runs prints the same
  numbers and is labelled by its provenance.
- Imported ledgers may carry free text from foreign tools; it is echoed only with the untrusted mark.
