## Background

GATE-TENFOLD-13 (claim kinds: Design comparison and Performance; M3, and M7 for the sealed suite) is the gate of
the three-arm comparison. Its threshold: r_T and r_C are published with 95% intervals for every stratum that has
a ratio, whatever their values; the three ablations are run on both briefs, and a ratio above its 1/s or
1/(1 − s) bound voids the stratum as an accounting error; and the one-receipt pipeline for arm W is built and
its binding property recorded before any engine-internal work package beyond M2 is funded. If the gate is not
met, no engine-internal work package beyond M2 is funded. Like the two program gates of the test it passes on
publication, not on a favourable value: a recovery ratio of 0.9 (a thin layer is enough) passes it as well as
one of 0.1.

Terms. B: the declared incumbent. W: the composed incumbent (the same engine with every portable method
layered on). F: this system. T_X: an arm's restricted mean time on a stratum; C_X: its GPU-hours.
r_T = (T_B − T_W) ÷ (T_B − T_F); r_C likewise on compute.

This bead is the end-to-end script that exercises the user-visible capability (the two options of
`report.campaign`) and the harness pipeline behind the gate, on fixtures before the gate and on the real record
at the gate, and the receipt the gate's row cites.

## Scope

This bead is the one end-to-end script of GATE-TENFOLD-13 and the one completeness check of its record. The
program-gate bead N40-22, which depends on this bead, adds no second script and no second check: it applies
this check at M3 and at M7, decides per stratum from the published ratios, and enforces the funding
consequence.

In: the script; its fixtures; the three checks of the gate, as the function
`frob_conformance::tenfold::gate13::tenfold13(record) -> Verdict` that the script's `record` mode calls
(r_T and r_C with 95% intervals, or the fixed case label, for every stratum that has a ratio; the three
ablations on both briefs, each with its touched share and its bound and each at or below its bound; the
one-receipt record for arm W with its binding property, dated before any engine-internal work package beyond
M2 was funded); the plants; the receipt. Out: the statistics, the ablations, the pipeline and the verb
(N39b-43, N39b-45, N39b-44, N39b-46); the partial gate's record (BD-317), which cites this script's receipt for
the M3 row; the M7 row on the sealed suite (N39b-13); and, in N40-22: the register row of the gate with its two
parts, the decision rule per stratum (layer enough at 0.8 and above on both ratios, engine a source below 0.5 on
both, mixed otherwise) with its ADR, the rule for the word "ten-fold", and the refusal to start an
engine-internal work package beyond M2 while the row has no M3 record.

## Technical approach

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_recovery_ablation.sh`; header `bead=N39b-47 milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e
gates=GATE-TENFOLD-13 seed=392213 needs=cpu plants=ablation-above-bound,missing-interval,b-only-word,
divide-by-nonpositive,stitched-receipt,dropped-unresolved`. Two modes: `fixture` (default; synthetic ledgers,
labelled `synthetic`, gate not claimable; the three-arm record is this bead's own fixture under
`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/tenfold/three_arm/`, the five strata below with their traced ablation
baselines, written here; the pooled-ratio script `tenfold_06.sh` of the later program-gate bead N40-15, which
depends on this bead through BD-317, may reuse it, and this script takes nothing from that one) and `record`
(`--gate-dir <dir>`; reads a closed
gate's ledgers). No other script measures this gate: a `tenfold_13.sh` does not exist beside this one.

Steps:

0. `stages`: run, as stages, the own scripts of the three mechanism beads this gate reads, each in its `fixture`
   or default mode: `scripts/e2e/tenfold_three_arm.sh` (N39b-43), `tenfold_ablation.sh` (N39b-45) and
   `report_campaign_arms.sh` (N39b-46); a stage's failure fails this script and is named in the summary. The
   steps below then exercise the gate's pipeline on this bead's own fixture and, in `record` mode, on the record.
1. `register`: build a comparison from three arms' ledgers and register it with
   `frob report campaign --register-comparison`.
2. `arms`: `frob report campaign <id> --arms B,W,F --json`. Fixture assertions, with (T_B, T_W, T_F) in time
   units and (C_B, C_W, C_F) in GPU-hours: stratum S1 (100, 28, 10) and (50, 14, 5): r_T = r_C = 4/5, reading
   "layer enough"; S2 (100, 60, 10) and (50, 30, 5): r_T = r_C = 4/9, reading "engine a source of the gain"; S3
   (90, 50, 100) and (45, 25, 50): "no saving to recover" on both measures; S4 (100, 8, 10) and (50, 4, 5):
   r_T = r_C = 46/45, "loss to the composed incumbent"; S5 (100, 28, 10) and (50, 20, 5): r_T = 4/5, r_C = 2/3,
   reading "mixed", with the unrecovered phase (the largest positive W − F exclusive time in the fixture ledgers,
   `train`) named. Assert intervals on both measures for S1, S2, S4 and S5 and none for S3. Assert the
   envelope's evidence block (class `Tested`, tier `foreign`, `reality: no-claim`).
3. `scorer-agreement`: run the standalone scorer on the same ledgers; assert its three-arm output equals the
   verb's figures byte for byte.
4. `mcp`: repeat step 2 through the MCP server; assert equal `result`.
5. `baseline` and three `ablate-*` steps: `frob report campaign <id> --ablate information|controller|stepping`
   on the fixture trace (baseline 1,825 minutes with 1,750 of training). Assert: information-only bound 1.043
   and status `within-bound` for a replay at 1.03; controller-only bound 1/(1 − 0.9589) = 24.3 and a replay at
   16.2 (112.5 minutes) within it; stepping-only with stepping share 0.5 sped up 2 times: bound 1.333, replay at
   1.30 within it.
6. `one-receipt`: assert that the one-receipt record exists in the project ledger with a named binding property
   per brief (or the recorded falsifier), that its two repeat digests are equal, and that its date precedes the
   funding entry of any engine-internal work package beyond M2.
7. `gate-table` (`record` mode): for every stratum of the gate that has a ratio: r_T and r_C with intervals
   present; both briefs have three ablations each with status; print the table. The assertions of steps 6 and
   7 are one function, `tenfold13(record) -> Verdict`, which the script calls and whose verdict it prints; the
   program-gate bead (N40-22) calls the same function when it applies the gate.
8. `plants`: `ablation-above-bound` (an information-only replay at 1.6 where unremoved training is 70%: the
   verb prints `void: accounting-error` and the gate table marks the stratum void); `missing-interval` (a
   comparison of one family: the ratio request is refused, FROB-REF-863); `b-only-word` (a report draft that
   awards the word on F against B while W governs: the wording lint of N39b-15, `frob-tenfold lint-wording`,
refuses it by its rule W7); `divide-by-nonpositive` (a
   mutant that prints a number for S3: caught); `stitched-receipt` (a one-receipt record assembled from two
   runs, or arm W assembled from three separate pipelines whose receipts are combined: rejected);
   `dropped-unresolved` (a recovery ratio computed with arm B's unresolved missions dropped instead of costed
   at the deadline H in every arm: refused).

**Logging.** Through the shared helper: each `frob` command with its exact command line, exit code, wall time
and evidence block; r_T and r_C as `metric` lines with their intervals, the thresholds 0.8 and 0.5 and the gate
tag; each ablation's ratio against its bound; refusals with code and first repair; plants as negative-control
assertions; digests of the comparison node and of the one-receipt record; on failure the first differing value
between verb and scorer. Summary with `gates: [{id: "GATE-TENFOLD-13", measured_ok, controls_caught}]`.

**Recording.** In `record` mode at the partial gate the receipt is cited by the M3 row of the gate; at the
tournament the script is run again on the sealed suite's record for the M7 row. The receipt is committed
byte-identical at `receipts/gates/<blake3>.json` and cited by that hash (N40-01); a named or target copy is never
cited. Rows are entered by a fresh-context session (N40-22).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In `fixture` mode the script exits 0 with the five strata's readings and the three ablations as stated,
      and all six plants caught.
- [ ] In `record` mode it fails if any stratum with a ratio lacks r_T, r_C or an interval, if either brief lacks
      an ablation, or if the one-receipt record is missing or postdates a funded engine-internal package.
- [ ] Verb and scorer agree byte for byte on every fixture.
- [ ] The gate passes with any values of r_T and r_C; a planted "fail because r_T is high" rule is rejected in
      review of the script (the script asserts presence and bounds, never favourable values).

### End-to-end script and logging

This bead is the script. `fixture` mode runs in minutes on CPU inside the default compute class.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: rows TF-21 and TF-22 of the harness registry; the fixture values above. Negative controls: the
six plants (including TF-CX-22 and TF-CX-23). Determinism lanes: twice on one machine with equal result bytes;
debug and release; command line against MCP; verb against scorer.

## Considerations

- A pass says the comparison was published honestly; what it says about the engine is read by the tie kill gate
  and the engine-necessity bet, per stratum.
- At M3 arm W may exist only in its first form (arm C, under 2,000 lines); the table then labels the arm used.
- If the partial gate is recorded `not run`, this gate's M3 row is `not run` as well, and the funding
  consequence (no engine-internal package beyond M2) holds.
