## Background

The ten-fold test compares wall-clock time from a mission card to an independently accepted answer between this
system (arm A), the strongest composed rival (arm B) and a short program over the rival's engine (arm C), on sealed
missions. A gate of the test ends with a fixed sequence that turns closed campaigns into an audited, scored and
recorded gate: confirm that every campaign ended, audit integrity, certificates and leaks, apply the late-error
rule, reveal, score once, apply the outcome rules, write the report and the record, and retire the sealed
families. Everything read afterwards (the outcome ADRs, the kill-gate decisions, the program gate's check, the
replication kit) is read from what this sequence leaves in the gate directory.

This bead is that sequence as code: one operator command with resumable stages. It exists as a bead of its own
(split from the gate bead BD-317 in polishing round 4) because the sequence must be the same code in the
rehearsal and at the sealed gate, and the rehearsal (N39b-12) comes before the gate: BD-317 depends on its
rehearsal, so the code the rehearsal runs cannot be BD-317's. Rejected: keeping the closing code in BD-317 and
letting the rehearsal close its gate by hand-written steps (the rehearsal would then rehearse something else).

Terms. *Gate directory*: `tenfold/<gate>/`, the store of one gate. *Event log*: the hash-chained ledger of the
gate (N39a-15), which continues after `open` with the run and with close and reveal. *Cell*: a stratum and a
regime. *Pair*: the campaigns of the arms on one mission and campaign index. *H*: the stratum's deadline; a run
not accepted by H is censored and contributes H.

## Scope

In: the subcommand `frob-tenfold gate close` and its module; the rules that belong to closing and to no other
bead (the closing check, the point after which no exclusion is accepted, the late-error rule, the order of the
stages); the gate record `gate_record.json`; the decision inputs `decision_inputs.json`; the retirement call at
the end; this bead's script.

Out, with the bead that has it: every mechanism a stage calls (the event log N39a-15; pairs, sums and exclusions
N39a-27; the family bootstrap N39a-28; the verdict rule N39a-32; the standalone scorer N39b-19 with the
three-arm object of N39b-43; the gate auditor N39b-23; the integrity detectors and disqualification records
N39b-01; the leak audit N39b-02 and the two standing decisions N39b-03 and N39b-04; direct evaluation of family
answers N39a-07; the outcome engine N39b-05; the report N39b-14 and the wording lint N39b-15; the family
lifecycle N39a-13); running the command on the sealed suite, the outcome ADRs and the hand-off of the record
(BD-317); the rehearsal that runs it on synthetic arms (N39b-12); the tournament's conduct, whose step 8 is this
command (N39b-13, N39bp3-01); the decisions read on the record (N39b-06, N39b-07, N39b-11, N12-30, N25a-18).

## Technical approach

Module `crates/frob-conformance/src/tenfold/close.rs`; command (DESIGN) `frob-tenfold gate close --gate <id>
[--through <stage>] [--json]`, adjudicator role only (`role-not-permitted` otherwise). It runs the stages below
in order, starting after the last stage whose `close-stage` entry is in the event log, and stops after `<stage>`
when `--through` is given; a completed stage is never run again, so a second call resumes and an operator cannot
enter a stage out of order. Each stage ends by appending one `close-stage` entry (`{stage, outputs}`, the digests
of what it wrote; hash, time and actor as every entry), after the stage's own entries. A stage interrupted before
its `close-stage` entry is run again from its start and skips what its own entries already record (a family
already retired, a cell already closed), so a crash never leaves a stage that cannot be resumed. This bead
registers the kinds it writes that the ledger does not yet know (`close-stage`, `gate-closed`, `cell-closed`,
`late-error`, `gate-record`, `decision-inputs`) in the kind registry of N39a-15, adjudicator only; without a
registered kind the ledger refuses the append. Exit codes are those of the harness (N39-REF): 0 completed,
including a gate or cell recorded `void`, `disqualified` or `descriptive` (a void or a disqualification that
follows a detection is a recorded event, and the command that records it exits 0); 2 completed with flags (the
scorer reported one: a `ledger-open` run or an `index_tie`, N39b-19); 3 refused, with the reason; 9 an event log
or record that does not verify.

A second subcommand, `frob-tenfold gate check --gate <id> [--json]` (DESIGN), checks the two files this bead
writes. They are not harness records (N39a-01's five schemas; `frob-tenfold record check` refuses them with
`record-unknown-schema`), so this bead checks them itself with N39a-01's common rules: canonical bytes and
strict reading under their schemas (a field outside the schema, a missing field, a duplicate key or a quantity
not written canonically is refused with the same `record-*` reason and exit code as in a harness record); each
file's content address recomputed and compared with the digest of its `gate-record` or `decision-inputs` entry
(`record-hash-mismatch`, exit 9); every ratio of the record carrying the fields of INV-TENFOLD-08
(`ratio-incomplete`; `warm-without-assets` for S or N*). Exit 0 when both files pass.

| Stage | What it does | Mechanism called |
|---|---|---|
| `close` | Every scheduled campaign of every arm has a final record (accepted, censored at H, or censored at a resource-cap breach at the instant of the breach), a supersession or a recorded exclusion; otherwise refused with `missing-run`, naming the campaign. A campaign of a cell whose ledger holds a `futility-stop` entry (N39a-35) that never started because the stop cancelled it counts as final; one in flight at the stop needs its final record like any other. Appends `gate-closed`, then one `cell-closed` entry per cell whose scheduled pairs are all final, the entry without which the verdict of N39a-32 is refused (`verdict-before-n`); a cell stopped by the futility monitor gets none (N39a-35). From `gate-closed` on an `exclusion` or `supersession` entry is refused (`exclusion-after-reveal`): exclusions are fixed before any times of a pair are compared. A cell with excluded × 100 > 5 × scheduled pairs (integers) is marked `descriptive` | N39a-15, N39a-27, N39a-35 |
| `integrity` | `frob-tenfold audit --gate <id> --phase pre-score` and the integrity detectors; each failure is a disqualification record with its scope, never a warning and never a refusal of this command | N39b-23, N39b-01 |
| `certificates` | Every accepted family answer is re-evaluated by the adjudicator's direct methods and every submitted certificate replayed by `frob-verify`; a contradiction is filed as an allegation and decided by the procedure of KILL-TENFOLD-CERT | N39a-07, N39b-03 |
| `leaks` | The leak audit; its evidence is decided by the procedure of KILL-TENFOLD-LEAK. A bounded leak voids the touched cells for that arm; an unbounded one makes the gate `void`: of the later stages the command then runs only `record` (a record with no ratio, carrying the void event) and `rotate`, enters each stage between as a `close-stage` entry marked `skipped: gate void`, and exits 0 | N39b-02, N39b-04 |
| `late-errors` | The late-error rule (below) | this bead |
| `reveal` | The sealed key is revealed and paired times, per-world outcomes and transcripts are released; the `reveal` entry | N39a-09 |
| `score` | The scorer runs once, at n: `frob-tenfold-score --gate-dir <dir> --out score.json`; per cell the pooled ratio, the bounds, the guard, completion and coverage, the warm quantities, the three-arm object, the ceilings on the rival's ledger and the fixed-n verdict of every cell with a `cell-closed` entry. No monitor output is among its inputs. This command invokes the scorer once per gate; a direct second verdict-producing invocation with other inputs is refused by the scorer (`verdict-already-computed`), and one with the same inputs returns the stored record. The stage then runs the cell outcome combiner of N39a-32 (step 6) on every cell: a disqualification of arm A first, then a futility stop (`withdrawn`, source `monitor`, for a cell with a `futility-stop` entry and no fixed-n verdict), then the fixed-n verdict | N39b-19, N39b-43, N39a-32 |
| `outcomes` | `apply` of the outcome engine on every confirmatory cell: the outcome rows, sentences, actions and ADR drafts, in `outcomes.json` | N39b-05 |
| `report` | `frob-tenfold report build --gate <id>`, the wording lint on the rendered report, then `frob-tenfold audit --gate <id> --phase post-report` | N39b-14, N39b-15, N39b-23 |
| `record` | Writes the gate record (below); appends `gate-record` with its digest | this bead |
| `decision-inputs` | Writes the decision inputs (below); appends `decision-inputs` with its digest | this bead |
| `rotate` | Missions, worlds and receipts are revealed and every sealed family of the gate is moved to `revealed` and retired into the development pool through the family lifecycle, one `family-retired` entry each; a retired family offered for sealing again is refused (`family-reused`) | N39a-13 |

**The late-error rule.** An answer that was accepted and has since been shown wrong within its scope (the
adjudicator records a `late-error` entry naming the mission, the arm, the receipt and the contradicting evidence)
scores that mission as unresolved at H for the arm that submitted it. The campaign's ledger and receipt are
kept unchanged; the scorer's input view carries the campaign as censored at H with the flag `late_error`, so the
pair stays in both sums and the report prints the event. It is not a false certificate unless a certificate
its checker accepted is contradicted (that is the `certificates` stage). A late error recorded after the
`score` stage, including after the report is published, is recorded the same way but cannot change a verdict
already computed (N39a-32: the verdict is computed once, and a correction voids the cell rather than recomputing
it): it voids the cell, the `late-error` entry being the void event, and the report is built again under a new
digest; the earlier report stays retrievable.

**The gate record** `tenfold/<gate>/gate_record.json` (schema `frob.tenfold.gaterecord/1`, DESIGN; canonical
bytes as every harness record, N39a-01): the gate, the suite hash, the parameter block's digest, the event-log
head; per cell the stratum, regime, status (`confirmatory`, `descriptive` with its reason, `void` with its event,
`not run` with its reason), m, n, H, R̂ as an exact rational, [L, U], the verdict, completion and coverage per
arm, the tie test, the digest of the three-arm object, one closed phase-ledger digest per arm, the adjudication
label, the freeze hashes, and for a warm cell S and N*; the outcome rows with their ADR draft identifiers; the
report digest; the digests of the two audits with their phases and finding counts; the disqualification and
void records. The stage refuses to write a record in which a ratio lacks a field of INV-TENFOLD-08
(`ratio-incomplete`; `warm-without-assets` for S or N*), naming the cell and the field.

**The decision inputs** `tenfold/<gate>/decision_inputs.json` (schema `frob.tenfold.decisioninputs/1`, DESIGN),
computed once, here, each table with the digests of the ledgers, receipts and submissions it was computed from:
`train_share` (per design class the pooled `train` share of arm B, p = Σ train seconds ÷ Σ min(T, H) over its
cold campaigns as a ratio of sums, with its family bootstrap interval, and arm A's own share beside it);
`familycert` (per cell of every family answer submitted on `envelope` and `sensing`: the verdict a family
certificate carried, with producer wall time, compute seconds and checker seconds, and the generic comparator's
verdict at a budget equal to that cost; and the count of cells the certificate decided and the comparator left
undecided); `augur_tie` (on the same strata arm C's accepted scope and restricted mean time against arm A's,
the turnaround per amendment, and the comparison of discharged obligations); `adapter` (the added latency per
rollout, median, 95th percentile and total per campaign, where a ledger's resource table carries rollouts
driven through the job interface; `not fired` where none does). No other bead computes a second version of any
of the four tables.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `frob-tenfold gate close` on a complete synthetic gate directory runs the twelve stages in order, leaves
      one `close-stage` entry per stage, and a second call does nothing and exits 0; with `--through score` it
      stops after `score` and a later call resumes at `outcomes`.
- [ ] An open campaign refuses `close` with `missing-run`; an exclusion offered after `gate-closed` is refused
      with `exclusion-after-reveal`; 2 of 24 pairs excluded makes the cell `descriptive` and 1 of 24 does not.
- [ ] Every cell whose pairs are final has its `cell-closed` entry before `score`; a cell stopped by the
      futility monitor has none, its cancelled campaigns do not refuse `close`, and its recorded outcome is
      `withdrawn`, source `monitor`.
- [ ] A late error scores the mission at H for the submitting arm only, with the original records unchanged.
- [ ] The scorer is invoked exactly once per gate by this command; a direct second invocation after a
      `late-error` entry has changed one campaign of the cell's input view is refused with
      `verdict-already-computed`, one with the same inputs returns the stored record byte for byte, and the
      late error voids the cell.
- [ ] `frob-tenfold gate check` passes `gate_record.json` and `decision_inputs.json`, and the record carries
      every field above; removing one INV-TENFOLD-08 field from the scorer's output refuses the `record` stage
      and names the field; one edited byte in either file after its entry fails `gate check` with
      `record-hash-mismatch` (exit 9).
- [ ] `decision_inputs.json` holds the four tables with input digests; the `train` share equals the hand values
      (4,920 ÷ 9,000 on the single-pair ledger alone; 4,920 ÷ 12,000 with the second campaign of the unit test).
- [ ] After `rotate` every sealed family of the gate is retired and a second sealing of one is refused.
- [ ] An unbounded leak leaves a `void` record with no ratio, retires the families and exits 0.

## Test plan

### Unit tests

`FROB_TEST_SEED=394001 cargo test -p frob-conformance tenfold::close` (real records in a temporary gate directory,
labelled `synthetic`; no mocks): `close_refuses_open_campaign` (`missing-run`); `exclusion_fixed_before_times` (an
exclusion entered after `gate-closed` is refused); `second_fault_excludes_pair_for_all_arms`;
`five_percent_exclusions_make_cell_descriptive` (2 of 24 is 8.3%: descriptive; 1 of 24 is 4.2%: not; compared in
integers); `late_error_scores_h` (T_A = 1,500 s accepted, then a late error: the pair contributes 14,400 s for arm A
and arm B's 9,000 s is unchanged); `stages_resume_in_order` (interrupt after each stage in turn: the next call starts
at the following stage; and inside `rotate` after half the families: the rerun retires the rest and no family twice);
`cell_closed_written_per_final_cell` (a cell with one open pair has no `cell-closed` and the scorer's verdict on it is
refused with `verdict-before-n`); `futility_stopped_cell_closes_without_verdict` (a cell with a `futility-stop` entry
after 8 of 24 families: `close` passes, no `cell-closed`, outcome `withdrawn`, source `monitor`); `score_runs_once`;
`gate_check_rejects_edited_record`; `verdict_first_row_wins` (a disqualified cell with R̂ = 12 is recorded
`disqualified`, the verdict being N39a-32's); `record_requires_all_inv08_fields` (eight variants);
`void_gate_has_no_ratio`; `train_share_is_ratio_of_sums` (two campaigns with train 4,920 of 9,000 and 0 of 3,000:
0.41, not the mean of shares 0.273); `familycert_count_zero_is_recorded_as_zero`;
`adapter_not_fired_without_rollout_rows`; `retired_family_cannot_be_sealed_again`.

### End-to-end script and logging

`scripts/e2e/tenfold_gate_close.sh`, owned by this bead and built only on its dependencies (header `bead=N39bp4-01
milestone=M3 labels=tenfold,e2e gates=GATE-TENFOLD-06 seed=394001 needs=cpu timeout_s=900
plants=open-campaign,late-exclusion,late-error-ignored,stripped-field,score-twice,family-resealed`). On a synthetic
gate directory written by this bead (`crates/frob-conformance/fixtures/tenfold/close/`: one cold cell of 24 families
registered confirmatory, with its registration, pre-analysis record and adjudication label, the records the verdict
reads; the six-family card four times; three synthetic arms; every campaign closed; public keys): (1) the unit tests;
(2) `frob-tenfold gate close --gate <id> --through score --json`, asserting the stage entries, the `cell-closed`
entry, R̂ = 45/17 and the verdict `withdrawn`; (3) the same command with no `--through`, asserting the resume at
`outcomes`, the report digest, `frob-tenfold gate check --gate <id> --json` (exit 0), the two audit digests and the
retired families; (4) the same command again, asserting no new entry; (5) each plant through `e2e_plant` on a fresh
copy: a campaign left open (`missing-run`, exit 3), an exclusion after `gate-closed` (`exclusion-after-reveal`), a
build under `--features gauntlet-mutations` that ignores late errors (the planted late error must change arm A's sum
by 12,900 s; the mutant leaves it and is caught), a stripped `H` in the scorer output (`ratio-incomplete`), a second
scorer invocation after a late error entered on one accepted campaign of the cell (`verdict-already-computed`) and a
retired family offered for sealing (`family-reused`). Logged as NDJSON through the STD-E2E helper (`ts`, `script`,
`bead`, `step`, `event`, exact command line, exit code, wall time, seed): the event-log digest before and after each
stage, each stage's entry hash, R̂ as a `metric` against 3 and 10, the excluded share against 5%, the record and
decision-input digests, each refusal with its reason and exit code, each plant as a `negative-control` assertion with
`caught`; on failure the first differing field and the gate directory's path; a final `summary` with assertion counts,
controls caught and `gates_claimable: false`.

### Known answers and negative controls

Known answers: R̂ = 45/17 on the card proportions; 8.3% and 4.2%; the `train` share 4,920 ÷ 9,000 = 54.67%; a
late error on family 1 moves Σ T_A of one block from 20,400 s to 33,300 s. Negative controls: the six plants.
Metamorphic relation: interrupting and resuming after any stage leaves the same record bytes as an
uninterrupted run. Determinism: the record and the decision inputs are pure functions of the gate directory
(twice on one machine; debug and release).

## Considerations

- The command never repairs: a refused stage leaves the event log as it was, and the operator's repair is the
  one the reason names.
- A defect found in a frozen arm after the suite opened voids the affected cells; it is recorded by the
  integrity stage and is never patched during closing.
- The stage list is closed; a gate that needs a further stage changes this bead, so that rehearsal and gate
  cannot drift.
- Privacy: transcripts are released at `reveal` into the gate directory only; logs carry digests, never
  transcript text or canary values.
